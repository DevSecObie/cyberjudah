## Introduction

*[[0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgSQcRI9-24&t=32s)]* Contradict This on temptation: James 1:13 says God tempts no man, yet Genesis 22:1 says God did tempt Abraham. It was the angel of the Lord who called to Abraham, and in 2 Chronicles 18 the Lord counsels with the host of heaven, his angels, and sends a spirit to entice Ahab. The Lord is in control of everything and has angels to do his work.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Psalms 14:1](/bible/psalms/14#v1)**  *[[0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgSQcRI9-24&t=32s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/14#v1)</sup> The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God. They are corrupt, they have done abominable works, there is none that doeth good.

- For the unbelievers who don't believe in the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob: the fool hath said in his heart, there is no God. The Lord said he'd give us a mouth none of our enemies can gainsay. The atheists say we're stupid for believing the Bible, yet they can't get a thousand black men together to organize something and build the community. The wisdom God blessed us with is what will build the people. They are corrupt, eating unclean food and doing all kinds of abominations, and the Lord looks down and sees it.

**[James 1:13](/bible/james/1#v13)**  *[[4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgSQcRI9-24&t=249s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/james/1#v13)</sup> Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God: for God cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man:

- God cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man. So does this contradict Genesis 22:1?


**[Genesis 22:1](/bible/genesis/22#v1)**  *[[4:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgSQcRI9-24&t=249s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/22#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after these things, that God did tempt Abraham, and said unto him, Abraham: and he said, Behold, here I am.

- God did tempt Abraham. Did they get us this time? No: you just don't understand.


**[Genesis 22:11](/bible/genesis/22#v11)**  *[[5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgSQcRI9-24&t=309s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/22#v11)</sup> And the angel of the Lord called unto him out of heaven, and said, Abraham, Abraham: and he said, Here am I.

- The angel of the Lord called to him, like we covered in Seeing God. The angel was testing whether Abraham would withhold anything from God: thou hast not withheld thy son from me.

**[2 Chronicles 18:18](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v18)**  *[[7:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pgSQcRI9-24&t=433s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v18)</sup> Again he said, Therefore hear the word of the Lord; I saw the Lord sitting upon his throne, and all the host of heaven standing on his right hand and on his left.

- The Lord is in control of everything. The host of heaven on his right hand and left are his angels, Psalm 148:2. He asks them who shall entice Ahab, one says this and another that, and a spirit says I will go and be a lying spirit in the mouth of all his prophets: go out and do even so. The Lord has angels to do his work. James 1:13 does not contradict Genesis 22.
