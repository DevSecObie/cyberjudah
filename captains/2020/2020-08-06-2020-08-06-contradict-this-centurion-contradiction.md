## Introduction

*[[0:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1irnX2t7Xw&t=40s)]* Contradict This on the centurion: Matthew 8 says the centurion came to Christ himself, while Luke 7 says he sent the elders of the Jews and then his friends. The answer is in the passage: what one does by another, one does by oneself. The friends he sent are the servants he tells do this, and he doeth it, just as the Lord saved Israel by the hand of David, who had soldiers under him.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 8:5](/bible/matthew/8#v5)**  *[[1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1irnX2t7Xw&t=78s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/8#v5)</sup> And when Jesus was entered into Capernaum, there came unto him a centurion, beseeching him,

- There's no contradictions, just confusion and a lack of studying. Here the centurion comes to Christ beseeching him. Centurions were in charge of a hundred soldiers. Christ said, I have not found so great faith, no, not in Israel.


**[Luke 7:1](/bible/luke/7#v1)**  *[[2:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1irnX2t7Xw&t=178s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/luke/7#v1)</sup> Now when he had ended all his sayings in the audience of the people, he entered into Capernaum.

- People find the stupidest things to say the scriptures ain't real, but they forgot about Deuteronomy 28, Luke 21 and Joel 3 that came to pass. Here the centurion sent the elders of the Jews; he loved the nation and built them a synagogue, because he had money. Then he sent friends: the same servants he tells do this, and he doeth it. The answer is right there in the passage.

**[Matthew 8:6](/bible/matthew/8#v6)**  *[[6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1irnX2t7Xw&t=370s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/8#v6)</sup> And saying, Lord, my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, grievously tormented.

- Matthew says the same thing, you just got to put the precepts together. There's an old Quaker English saying: what one does by another, one does by themselves. If I tell the officer to get me a bottle of water and he sends a brother, the brother might say Captain Isaac came and got some water. Because you hate law, order, the Bible and God, there's a contradiction, when the contradiction is in your mind.


**[2 Samuel 3:18](/bible/2-samuel/3#v18)**  *[[7:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w1irnX2t7Xw&t=464s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-samuel/3#v18)</sup> Now then do it: for the Lord hath spoken of David, saying, By the hand of my servant David I will save my people Israel out of the hand of the Philistines, and out of the hand of all their enemies.

- By the hand of my servant David I will save my people Israel out of the hand of the Philistines. Did David kill all the Philistines by himself? No, he had soldiers under him, just like the centurion. It's saying the same exact thing as Matthew and Luke.
