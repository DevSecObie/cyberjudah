## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Isu40345R0I&t=14s)]* Contradict This: has anybody seen God and lived? Genesis 32:30 says Jacob saw God face to face; John 1:18 says no man hath seen God at any time. Hosea 12 shows Jacob wrestled with the angel, and lord and god are titles: Esdras calls the angel Uriel both my lord and my God.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**  *[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Isu40345R0I&t=14s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

- A lot of y'all don't understand the Bible, so things look like contradictions. A good understanding have all they that do his commandments; some things you won't understand if you're not doing them.


**[Genesis 32:30](/bible/genesis/32#v30)**  *[[2:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Isu40345R0I&t=137s)]*

> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/32#v30)</sup> And Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved.

- I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved; yet John 1:18 says no man hath seen God at any time.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 1:18](/bible/john/1#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/john/1#v18)</sup> No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.

    No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.


**[Hosea 12:3](/bible/hosea/12#v3)**  *[[2:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Isu40345R0I&t=137s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/12#v3)</sup> He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God:

- He had power over the angel and prevailed: that's who Jacob wrestled. When he said he saw God face to face, he saw that angel face to face.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 12:4](/bible/hosea/12#v4)**

**[1 Corinthians 8:5](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v5)**  *[[3:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Isu40345R0I&t=234s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v5)</sup> For though there be that are called gods, whether in heaven or in earth, (as there be gods many, and lords many,)

- There are things called gods in heaven and in earth, gods many and lords many: it's a title, like a lord or a sir in England.
