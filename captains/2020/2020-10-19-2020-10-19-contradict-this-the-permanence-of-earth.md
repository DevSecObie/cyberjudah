## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=14s)]* Contradict This on the permanence of the earth: Ecclesiastes 1:4 says the earth abideth for ever, and 2 Peter 3:10 says the earth and the works therein shall be burned up. The old world perished in Noah's flood but it was the same earth; the new heavens and new earth wherein dwelleth righteousness are the earth refreshed, as 2 Esdras 11:46 says.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Ecclesiastes 1:4](/bible/ecclesiastes/1#v4)**  *[[1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=72s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/1#v4)</sup> One generation passeth away, and another generation cometh: but the earth abideth for ever.

- One generation passeth away and another cometh, going into regeneration: a man dies, a man comes back. But the earth abideth for ever; it has been here since God created it.


**[2 Peter 3:10](/bible/2-peter/3#v10)**  *[[1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=72s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-peter/3#v10)</sup> But the day of the Lord will come as a thief in the night; in the which the heavens shall pass away with a great noise, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat, the earth also and the works that are therein shall be burned up.

- A lot of y'all think the day of the Lord ain't coming, but it will come like a thief in the night, and there will be fire on earth in those times. The first time the Lord wiped man off the face of the earth he used water; the second time he will use fire. Read to verse 13: new heavens and a new earth wherein dwelleth righteousness. That proves it's talking about the earth being refreshed, not destroyed: no pedophilia, homosexuality, transgenderism, abortion, murder, rape or robbery, but people keeping the commandments of God.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Peter 3:13](/bible/2-peter/3#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-peter/3#v13)</sup> Nevertheless we, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness.

    Nevertheless we, according to his promise, look for new heavens and a new earth, wherein dwelleth righteousness.


**[2 Peter 3:5](/bible/2-peter/3#v5)**  *[[4:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=241s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-peter/3#v5)</sup> For this they willingly are ignorant of, that by the word of God the heavens were of old, and the earth standing out of the water and in the water:

- The heavens were of old: that goes into the time of Noah. After the flood the earth was refreshed, and we're living in a new heaven and new earth. It got old because the wickedness of man increased, so now we look for new heavens and a new earth where righteousness dwells.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Peter 3:6](/bible/2-peter/3#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-peter/3#v6)</sup> Whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished:

    Whereby the world that then was, being overflowed with water, perished:


**[Genesis 6:13](/bible/genesis/6#v13)**  *[[4:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=283s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/6#v13)</sup> And God said unto Noah, The end of all flesh is come before me; for the earth is filled with violence through them; and, behold, I will destroy them with the earth.

- The earth was filled with violence, and he destroyed them with the earth, but saved Noah and his family, eight of them total.


**[2 Peter 2:5](/bible/2-peter/2#v5)**  *[[6:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=369s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-peter/2#v5)</sup> And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;

- Spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness. The old world, the new world: but it's the same earth. The earth abideth for ever.


**[2 Esdras 11:40](/bible/2-esdras/11#v40)**  *[[6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rKPPUaHJIiA&t=415s)]*

> <sup>[40](/bible/2-esdras/11#v40)</sup> And the fourth came, and overcame all the beasts that were past, and had power over the world with great fearfulness, and over the whole compass of the earth with much wicked oppression; and so long time dwelt he upon the earth with deceit.

- Why does the earth need to be refreshed? The fourth kingdom had power over the world with great fearfulness: this is America, with over 800 military bases worldwide, ruling with much wicked oppression. If you don't follow their laws and politics, they put up blockades and starve your people out. The earth must be refreshed and delivered from that violence. That's what 2 Peter 3:10 is talking about, and the earth will abide for ever as King Solomon said. No contradiction.

    That all the earth may be refreshed, and may return, being delivered from thy violence, and that she may hope for the judgment and mercy of him that made her.
