## Introduction

*[[0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=36s)]* Contradict This on the fighting men of Israel: 2 Samuel 24:9 counts 800,000 valiant men and 1 Chronicles 21:5 a thousand thousand and an hundred thousand. Joab began to number but finished not; David's own count of the twelve monthly courses was 288,000, and together they make about 1.1 million, with men always left behind in Jerusalem.

## Scriptures Opened

**[2 Peter 3:3](/bible/2-peter/3#v3)**  *[[0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=36s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-peter/3#v3)</sup> Knowing this first, that there shall come in the last days scoffers, walking after their own lusts,

- In the last days there shall come scoffers, people that hate the Bible and hate God and say there are plenty of contradictions, walking after their own lusts: fornicators, whoremongers, those that love celebrating Christmas and Easter, which are not of God.


**[2 Samuel 24:9](/bible/2-samuel/24#v9)**  *[[1:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=76s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-samuel/24#v9)</sup> And Joab gave up the sum of the number of the people unto the king: and there were in Israel eight hundred thousand valiant men that drew the sword; and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand men.

- Joab gave up the sum of the number of the people unto the king: 800,000 valiant men in Israel. 1 Chronicles 21:5 says a thousand thousand and an hundred thousand men that drew sword, about 1.1 million. It ain't no contradiction.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Chronicles 21:5](/bible/1-chronicles/21#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/21#v5)</sup> And Joab gave the sum of the number of the people unto David. And all they of Israel were a thousand thousand and an hundred thousand men that drew sword: and Judah was four hundred threescore and ten thousand men that drew sword.

    And Joab gave the sum of the number of the people unto David. And all they of Israel were a thousand thousand and an hundred thousand men that drew sword: and Judah was four hundred threescore and ten thousand men that drew sword.


**[1 Chronicles 27:23](/bible/1-chronicles/27#v23)**  *[[2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=126s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/27#v23)</sup> But David took not the number of them from twenty years old and under: because the Lord had said he would increase Israel like to the stars of the heavens.

- How Joab got his 1,100,000: David took not the number of them from twenty years old and under, because the Lord said he would increase Israel like the stars of the heavens.


**[1 Chronicles 27:1](/bible/1-chronicles/27#v1)**  *[[2:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=176s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/27#v1)</sup> Now the children of Israel after their number, to wit, the chief fathers and captains of thousands and hundreds, and their officers that served the king in any matter of the courses, which came in and went out month by month throughout all the months of the year, of every course were twenty and four thousand.

- David had a count: twelve captains, one for each month of the year, each over 24,000 men, down to the twelfth captain for the twelfth month in verse 15. 24,000 times 12 is 288,000.


**[1 Chronicles 27:24](/bible/1-chronicles/27#v24)**  *[[3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=224s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/27#v24)</sup> Joab the son of Zeruiah began to number, but he finished not, because there fell wrath for it against Israel; neither was the number put in the account of the chronicles of king David.

- Joab began to number, but he finished not. 1 Chronicles 21:5 is adding David's count to Joab's: Joab's 800,000 plus David's 288,000 makes 1,088,000. Round that off and you get one million one hundred thousand.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Samuel 24:9](/bible/2-samuel/24#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-samuel/24#v9)</sup> And Joab gave up the sum of the number of the people unto the king: and there were in Israel eight hundred thousand valiant men that drew the sword; and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand men.

    And Joab gave up the sum of the number of the people unto the king: and there were in Israel eight hundred thousand valiant men that drew the sword; and the men of Judah were five hundred thousand men.


**[2 Chronicles 1:14](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v14)**  *[[5:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZJ--V1ZUiyg&t=332s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v14)</sup> And Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen: and he had a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, which he placed in the chariot cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.

- For the unbelievers: there were always men of the army left behind to protect Jerusalem. David had men stay back, just like his son Solomon, who placed horsemen in the chariot cities and with the king at Jerusalem. Read 1 Chronicles 27:16-22 and 26-34 and there were princes and men that weren't counted. One million one hundred thousand.
