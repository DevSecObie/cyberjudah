## Introduction

*[[0:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=0s)]* This is 15 Minutes with the Captains, and today's class is titled How to Prove. Many brothers and sisters in Israel want to get married. You might be proving right now, or have a mind to prove in the near future, but not really know what that looks like. We do not have anything in society that prepares us for how to prove thus saith the Lord, so this class gives insight and guidance on how to prove in the spirit. Before you even start proving, you have to make sure the foundation is right.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Hebrews 13:4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)**  *[[0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=35s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)</sup> Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.

- If you want to get married, the Bible says marriage is honourable. It is noble; it is a great thing to get married. When a man finds a wife he finds a good thing. It is nation building.

- What y'all do in that bed is undefiled. It is no sin.

- What you don't want to be is a whoremonger and adulterer. No sneaking around, no sneaky stuff.


**[1 Corinthians 14:40](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v40)**  *[[1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=78s)]*

> <sup>[40](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v40)</sup> Let all things be done decently and in order.

- What you don't want to do is do things the wrong way and stain your name.

- Decently means not in that sister's DM, not in that brother's DM. Sneaking around is not decent; it is not just, it is not holy.

- In order means stay in the spirit and do things according to protocol. No backdoor marriages. Do not build your marriage upon an evil foundation.


**[Proverbs 20:21](/bible/proverbs/20#v21)**  *[[2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=121s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/20#v21)</sup> An inheritance may be gotten hastily at the beginning; but the end thereof shall not be blessed.

- A disclaimer before getting into the class, for those who want to be simple and do things the wrong way.

- When you get a wife, the Bible says that is your inheritance: she takes on your last name.

- For you backdoor brothers: I know a lot of y'all burning, but if you do that thing hastily and try to back door it, the end thereof will not be blessed. Be very mindful.


**[Sirach 4:26](/bible/sirach/4#v26)**  *[[2:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=166s)]*

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/4#v26)</sup> Be not ashamed to confess thy sins; and force not the course of the river.

- Don't be ashamed to confess your sins, and don't force the course of the river.

- A brother sees a sister that is attractive, with good qualities, and bypasses the leadership table and the sister's parents. He wants to talk to the sister in the corner, sneaky, down low. That is forcing the course of the river. Don't do that.

- If she's for you, brother, nobody can stop it. Sister, if he's for you, no sister can stop it.


**[Tobit 6:17](/bible/tobit/6#v17)**  *[[3:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=224s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/tobit/6#v17)</sup> And the devil shall smell it, and flee away, and never come again any more: but when thou shalt come to her, rise up both of you, and pray to God which is merciful, who will have pity on you, and save you: fear not, for she is appointed unto thee from the beginning; and thou shalt preserve her, and she shall go with thee. Moreover I suppose that she shall bear thee children. Now when Tobias had heard these things, he loved her, and his heart was effectually joined to her.

- You might think: I want nobody else to get him, I want to hurry up, grab him and put my stamp on him. If it is ordained of God, you don't have to do that.

- This is the history of Tobias, Tobit's son, when he was getting his wife Sarah. She was appointed unto him from the beginning.

- The marriages God sets up were ordained from the beginning. That backdoor stuff is not ordained by God. If that brother is for you, sister, it is ordained from the beginning, and hell and hot water can't stop it. Stay in the spirit; in your patience possess ye your souls.


**[Genesis 24:3-4](/bible/genesis/24#v3)**  *[[5:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=317s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/24#v3)</sup> And I will make thee swear by the Lord, the God of heaven, and the God of the earth, that thou shalt not take a wife unto my son of the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I dwell:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/24#v4)</sup> But thou shalt go unto my country, and to my kindred, and take a wife unto my son Isaac.

- The first thing you do if you believe the scriptures: if that person's father or mother is in the truth, they are involved in the marriage. We have to come back to the way things are done in our heritage. You go to that sister's father, or her mother, and you deal with leadership.

- Parents are involved in the Israelite marriage if they are in the truth. This is our heritage; this is our custom.

- It must be of your own people. That should be a given, but somebody new may be watching: we prove with our own people and we marry our own people.

- There is no such thing as an independent woman in Israel. Both sides must get counsel, but when dealing with the woman you deal with her father if he is present, and if a father is not present you deal with leadership.


**[Numbers 30:16](/bible/numbers/30#v16)**  *[[6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=403s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/30#v16)</sup> These are the statutes, which the Lord commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, between the father and his daughter, being yet in her youth in her father’s house.

- We have to undo the filth and foolishness we learned in the world, the whoredom and dating culture. It is madness; it is folly.

- The Lord God set it up where the husband is over the wife, and before she is married the father is over the daughter.

- In our culture the daughter does not go get her own apartment if her father and mother are in the truth. She abides in her parents' household until she is of marriageable age. Her father is part of the picking process for the man she proves, and she is handed off with the father's permission to her husband to be.

- If the father is not present and it is just the mother, the mother and the leadership of whatever city you dwell in are part of that process, to make sure that sister is treated properly and dealt with uprightly.


**[Hebrews 13:7](/bible/hebrews/13#v7)**  *[[8:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=487s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/13#v7)</sup> Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation.

- Some of y'all say: my father is not in the truth, or my mother is not in the truth. What is the next step?

- Remember the men who taught you, whom God set up as the government in your city. Follow their faith, their example and their counsel, understanding that God set them up and they have your best interest.


**[Hebrews 13:17](/bible/hebrews/13#v17)**  *[[8:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=521s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/13#v17)</sup> Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account, that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you.

- Submit yourself to your leadership the same way you would submit to your parents if they were in the truth, because they have your best interest and are watching for your soul. They must give an account for you.

- When is it with grief? When you are being sneaky, being slick, in somebody's DM, entertaining lust. That is when we have to give an account for grief, and then you will find yourself outside these doors. That is not what we want. We want to build our nation, but it must be built on godly principles and the order of the Bible.

- So it is vital to deal with your leadership so we can assess and discuss potential suitors for you. Sisters can get carnal, brothers can be carnal, so you need somebody outside of yourself to help with that process.


**[Sirach 7:24-25](/bible/sirach/7#v24)**  *[[10:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=604s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/7#v24)</sup> Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/7#v25)</sup> Marry thy daughter, and so shalt thou have performed a weighty matter: but give her to a man of understanding.

- This is talking to the parents. Do you have daughters? Have a care for their bodies: consider how they dress, control how they dress, make sure they dress modestly.

- That daughter is not your friend. Many parents want to be friends with their children. You can be their friend once they are out of your house, married with their own family. Right now you are their caretaker.

- Marriage is a weighty matter: one of the most important decisions you will ever make in your life.

- Give her to a man of understanding. Sisters, all y'all had a type the whole time you were in the world. Your type never worked. We don't give a damn who your type is. That does not mean we are going to get you a busted Joe from around the corner; the understanding comes first. You need somebody who is going to walk you into the gates of the kingdom and guide you in the spirit of Christ. We have to stop being carnal.


**[1 Samuel 16:7](/bible/1-samuel/16#v7)**  *[[11:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=700s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/16#v7)</sup> But the Lord said unto Samuel, Look not on his countenance, or on the height of his stature; because I have refused him: for the Lord seeth not as man seeth; for man looketh on the outward appearance, but the Lord looketh on the heart.

- Don't look on his countenance, his outward appearance. Sister wants somebody six feet with a six-pack; God says don't look on the height of his stature. Everybody needs help sometime.

- Many times our decision-making is skewed because we are looking on the outward appearance.

- Your father in the truth, your mother in the truth, assisted by the leadership if your father is not in the picture, are going to look at that brother: make sure he is not full of crap, not gaming you, that he is diligent. We are privy to things you don't know about that brother.

- He may not be ready, and that is okay. It may not be today or next week, and it does not mean he is off limits forever, but some brothers need space to get themselves right.


**[John 7:24](/bible/john/7#v24)**  *[[13:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=784s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/john/7#v24)</sup> Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.

- Did anything change in the New Testament? Don't be deceived by the outward appearance.

- Judging by appearance is simple, carnal, worldly. That is why sisters have got threefold baby daddies: because he had hazel eyes, he was tall, he had big feet.

- Judge righteous judgment. You are going to need some help with that, and we are here for you.


**[Proverbs 11:14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)**  *[[13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQeCG3N9PZQ&t=823s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

- Where no counsel is you are going to make bad decisions, and then you have to live with them. You make that bed, you lay in it; you lay down with a dog, you get up with fleas.

- The multitude of counsellors is the leadership table, your father if he is in this truth, your mother if she is in this truth. In that there is safety.

- If you truly care about yourself and your future, you will do things the right way and not rush into any situation that can cause you harm.
