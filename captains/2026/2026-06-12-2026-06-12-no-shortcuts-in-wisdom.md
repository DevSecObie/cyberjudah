## Introduction

*[[0:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=12s)]* Today we're going to talk about no shortcuts in wisdom. Wisdom is the principal thing, and wisdom comes through experience. It's not that you just open up the Bible and the Holy Spirit is going to come down upon you and dial on you all manner of wisdom. You need experience in this truth, you need time, and you got to strive lawfully. Ain't no shortcuts.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 4:7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)**  *[[0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=22s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)</sup> Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

- Wisdom is the principal thing: we all should be striving for wisdom first and foremost. Therefore get wisdom.

- And with all thy getting get understanding: you got to get understanding of how wisdom works, how the Lord gives wisdom. Wisdom comes through experience.


**[Sirach 34:9-10](/bible/sirach/34#v9)**  *[[0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=54s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/34#v9)</sup> A man that hath travelled knoweth many things; and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/34#v10)</sup> He that hath no experience knoweth little: but he that hath travelled is full of prudence.

- A man that hath travelled knoweth many things; and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom: wisdom comes with experience. It's not something that you just walk in the body, open up the Bible, and feel like the Holy Spirit is going to come down upon you. You need to go through different things in this truth. That's how you start to accumulate more understanding, more wisdom.

- He that hath no experience knoweth little: if you a novice in this truth, you knoweth little. That's what separates the wisdom of somebody that's one or two years in the truth from somebody 30 years in this truth: the experience they went through over the duration of their walk.


**[Hebrews 5:14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)**  *[[2:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=134s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)</sup> But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.

- Strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil: through experience, you start to have your senses exercised. You start to see the good and evil, first and foremost in yourself, then in the brothers around. Wisdom is going to start to deal with you, and you going to start to get understanding in the scriptures.

- It ain't no shortcuts in that. You need experience. You need time, brothers and sisters. I don't see it too much on the sister side, but brothers, y'all got to be patient and accumulate experience in this truth.


**[Proverbs 4:7-8](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)**  *[[3:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=196s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)</sup> Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/4#v8)</sup> Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.

- And with all thy getting get understanding: wisdom, knowledge, it could come quick, but understanding comes over time. With all the classes that come out, it's easy to accumulate wisdom. I remember the leadership said a long time ago, brothers got good memories. It's not that you learning. Your memory game is on point, so you memorize this precept, that precept. And those be the ones that be struggling in the truth, from what I seen, because the knowledge came so quickly, but you don't have the experience, the substance behind it.

- Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her. That's where the honor comes in, when you actually embrace wisdom, and that takes time.

- Remember Samuel was doing the work of the Lord in the temple as a child, but it took time. The Lord was calling him, and he didn't even know. It's the same thing with us. When you coming to this walk, you got to be patient and understand it's no shortcuts.


**[Sirach 24:28](/bible/sirach/24#v28)**  *[[5:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=323s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/24#v28)</sup> The first man knew her not perfectly: no more shall the last find her out.

- The first man knew her not perfectly: no more shall the last find her out. Who's the her? Verse one.


**[Sirach 24:1](/bible/sirach/24#v1)**  *[[5:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=341s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/24#v1)</sup> Wisdom shall praise herself, and shall glory in the midst of her people.

- Wisdom shall praise herself: so if the first man didn't know wisdom perfectly, and the scriptures are telling you no more the last man, you should really take your time. You ain't going to know this thing perfectly, and some brothers be striving for that: I got to know this, I got to know that. Listen, bro, be patient in the walk.


**[Sirach 24:28-29](/bible/sirach/24#v28)**  *[[6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=372s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/24#v28)</sup> The first man knew her not perfectly: no more shall the last find her out.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/24#v29)</sup> For her thoughts are more than the sea, and her counsels profounder than the great deep.

- For her thoughts are more than the sea, and her counsels profounder than the great deep: you got the rest of your life that you have allotted on this earth to study, gain experience, get exercised in wisdom. Don't try to rush it. Don't be a novice. That's how much wisdom and understanding is out here.


**[2 Esdras 9:18-20](/bible/2-esdras/9#v18)**  *[[7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=428s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-esdras/9#v18)</sup> And now when I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live, no man spake against me.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-esdras/9#v19)</sup> For then every one obeyed: but now the manners of them which are created in this world that is made are corrupted by a perpetual seed, and by a law which is unsearchable rid themselves.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-esdras/9#v20)</sup> So I considered the world, and, behold, there was peril because of the devices that were come into it.

- When I prepared the world, which was not yet made, no man spake against me. For then every one obeyed: before he created the earth for the Israelites, we all obeyed.

- But now the manners of them which are created in this world that is made are corrupted by a perpetual seed: we come down here and now we're corrupted.

- And by a law which is unsearchable: the first man didn't know it perfectly. How much more you think you going to know? You're ever learning in this truth. There's always something to learn. You never want to be puffed up.

- Rid themselves: we think we know it all, and we pull ourselves from this truth. You get comfortable, and the word has become a burden to you. Because you rushing wisdom, now you repeating the damn lessons. The Lord putting things in front of you to grow and accumulate experience, and you keep falling in the same trap.

- But your Bible is a New York subway station, graffiti. Brothers' Bibles inked all the way up. Then all of a sudden correction come, boom, they out. Offense come, boom, you out. Trial come, boom, you out. Well damn, what was you writing? Experience graduates you to the next level. You can't rid yourself from an unsearchable law.


**[Sirach 20:32](/bible/sirach/20#v32)**  *[[9:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=589s)]*

> <sup>[32](/bible/sirach/20#v32)</sup> Necessary patience in seeking the Lord is better than he that leadeth his life without a guide.

- God grows the spirit in stages. Ain't no shortcuts.

- Necessary patience in seeking the Lord is better than he that leadeth his life without a guide: our brothers and sisters in the world don't have no guide, no shepherd. When you come in here, it's necessary that you be patient when you seek the Lord. Be patient with yourself.


**[Sirach 21:14](/bible/sirach/21#v14)**  *[[10:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=635s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/21#v14)</sup> The inner parts of a fool are like a broken vessel, and he will hold no knowledge as long as he liveth.

- This is why the Lord is counseling us that it's necessary to be patient when you seek him, because he already understands this.

- The inner parts of a fool are like a broken vessel: the Lord already know, when we come in, ain't we sick? Ain't our vessels damaged? Don't we come in with burdens?

- And he will hold no knowledge as long as he liveth: you're not going to hold knowledge in that state. You got to fix your vessel. You got to get your spirit right. That's why it says the fear of the Lord is the beginning of understanding. A lot of brothers and sisters don't fear God, and this is why you find yourself in folly.


**[Sirach 21:13](/bible/sirach/21#v13)**  *[[11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=697s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/21#v13)</sup> The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood: and his counsel is like a pure fountain of life.

- The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood: once you start to get your spirit together, your vessel together, the experiences are graduating you to the next level of exercising between good and evil, and now it's abounding with you. Now you're able to help yourself and help brothers and sisters in the walk.

- And his counsel is like a pure fountain of life: the counsel that you give is like that clean glass of water. How you going to be able to give a counsel with no experience? You ain't been through nothing. You're a novice. The Lord got this thing set up perfectly how he wants it.


**[Sirach 40:20](/bible/sirach/40#v20)**  *[[12:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=749s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/40#v20)</sup> Wine and musick rejoice the heart: but the love of wisdom is above them both.

- Remember the Lord said, I give doctrine in weight. Why? Cuz he understands that vessel. It takes time for us to get our vessels together to be able to hold the wisdom. I'm not going to drown you and flood you out.

- Wine and musick rejoice the heart: this is what a lot of brothers and sisters know about.

- But the love of wisdom is above them both: do you embrace wisdom with that same fortitude? When you're reading your four chapters a day, do you open your Bible with that same zeal and excitement as when you're at the wine and you got music playing, feeling good? Some brothers can't put the bottle down. That's how you got to look at wisdom.


**[2 Timothy 2:5](/bible/2-timothy/2#v5)**  *[[14:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=855s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-timothy/2#v5)</sup> And if a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, except he strive lawfully.

- If a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, except he strive lawfully: to master our vessels, to master this wisdom, you got to strive lawfully. You got to keep the law, keep the commandments. You can't take shortcuts. Shortcuts have you quoting scriptures based off memory, because you're not striving lawfully. And what's the result?

>

>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/4#v5)</sup> But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.

- But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled: based off memory, cuz you ain't striving lawfully, you was able to strengthen the minds of the feeble with your words. But then when it comes to you, you faint. You're not going to be crowned in that day, because behind the scenes you're not striving lawfully. You got to have integrity. It was something behind the scenes nobody knew but you, and you thought it was a shortcut to this understanding.


**[2 Timothy 2:5](/bible/2-timothy/2#v5)**  *[[16:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=1009s)]*

- Except he strive lawfully: the only way you're going to obtain your crown is to be keeping the law. When nobody else is watching, you got to be striving lawfully with your brothers, with your sisters.


**[Jeremiah 9:23-24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v23)**  *[[17:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=1051s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/9#v23)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, neither let the mighty man glory in his might, let not the rich man glory in his riches:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v24)</sup> But let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me, that I am the Lord which exercise lovingkindness, judgment, and righteousness, in the earth: for in these things I delight, saith the Lord.

- But let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me: don't glory in having memorized all the precepts. Glory in the fact that you know Christ, the understanding of Christ, who you are, who your people are, and what's your lot: to restore the 12 tribes.


**[Sirach 3:23](/bible/sirach/3#v23)**  *[[18:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=1134s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/3#v23)</sup> Be not curious in unnecessary matters: for more things are shewed unto thee than men understand.

- Be not curious in unnecessary matters: for more things are shewed unto thee than men understand. This is what you glory in, that you understand Christ. You know how many people don't understand Christ? Just his color, who he came for. That's something right there to glory in.


**[Jeremiah 9:24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v24)**  *[[19:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zU-jK6_LGRQ&t=1165s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v24)</sup> But let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me, that I am the Lord which exercise lovingkindness, judgment, and righteousness, in the earth: for in these things I delight, saith the Lord.

We got to be exercising lovingkindness, judgment and righteousness. What is that? Experience. You got to be going through some things. Judgment: knowing how to judge matters. That's what you glory in in these last days. Get that experience.

- For in these things I delight, saith the LORD.
