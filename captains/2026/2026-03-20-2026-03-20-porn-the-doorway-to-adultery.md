## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 5:27-28](/bible/matthew/5#v27)**  *[[0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=47s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/5#v27)</sup> Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not commit adultery:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/5#v28)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.

- So Christ is magnifying the law here. You have heard of old time, thou shalt not commit adultery.

- Whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart: that's what you're doing when you look at pornography, when you lust after pornography, when you look at these different Instagram models and all these different TikTok videos, cuz that's where it starts. That's why I said it's a doorway to adultery. Satan always puts that little bit in front of you, and it's a doorway that leads to another doorway, to now you're in full-blown sin.

- When you looking upon that woman, what are you thinking about in your mind? You ain't thinking about, oh man, that's a nice woman right there, she speaks real well. No, you looking at breast, behind, and imagining everything in your mind that you would want to do with her lasciviously.


**[Job 31:1-6](/bible/job/31#v1)**  *[[2:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=148s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/31#v1)</sup> I made a covenant with mine eyes; why then should I think upon a maid?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/31#v2)</sup> For what portion of God is there from above? and what inheritance of the Almighty from on high?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/31#v3)</sup> Is not destruction to the wicked? and a strange punishment to the workers of iniquity?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/31#v4)</sup> Doth not he see my ways, and count all my steps?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/31#v5)</sup> If I have walked with vanity, or if my foot hath hasted to deceit;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/31#v6)</sup> Let me be weighed in an even balance, that God may know mine integrity.

- Job said something similar as well. I made a covenant with mine eyes: and that's what all of us have to do. That covenant is with this Bible, that Christ said if I look upon a maid to lust after her, that's adultery. Why should I then look upon a maid, to lust after her?

- Is not destruction to the wicked? He starts by saying I made a covenant with my eyes, and then it leads to: is not destruction for the wicked?

- And a strange punishment to the workers of iniquity: cuz ultimately that's what you're doing when you do that. You're working iniquity.

- Doth not he see my ways, and count all my steps? A lot of times we got to take into consideration God sees all our ways. He counts all our steps. You might think it's 3:00 a.m., and you under the covers, you hiding in the dark, and you sitting there with the phone in your face, scrolling through all manner of filth like don't nobody see you. No, God sees you.

- Let me be weighed in an even balance, that God may know mine integrity: we all want to be able to say this right here.


**[Job 31:9-10](/bible/job/31#v9)**  *[[4:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=264s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/job/31#v9)</sup> If mine heart have been deceived by a woman, or if I have laid wait at my neighbour’s door;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/31#v10)</sup> Then let my wife grind unto another, and let others bow down upon her.

- If mine heart have been deceived by a woman: ultimately, that's what porn does. It deceives your mind.

- Or if I have laid wait at my neighbour's door: that's why I said it's a doorway. Because it might start with the porn, it might start with some little softcore, you might see something on there that entices you, and then next thing you know, you down that rabbit hole of pornography. Then, you know what? Pornography, it ain't fixing it, it ain't giving me that dopamine hit no more, I ain't getting that fix, I need the real thing. And that's what it leads to, cuz now you done rubbed it raw, or whatever the case is, now you want the real thing.

- What does it mean to lay wait at your neighbor's door? You trying to commit adultery.

- Then let my wife grind unto another: if I've been deceived by a woman and laid wait at my neighbor's door to go off and commit adultery, let another grind my wife.


**[Matthew 5:28-29](/bible/matthew/5#v28)**  *[[5:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=350s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/5#v28)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/5#v29)</sup> And if thy right eye offend thee, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: for it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not that thy whole body should be cast into hell.

- That's why we must make a covenant with our eyes to not look upon a maid to lust after, just like Christ said: if you lust after a woman, you've committed adultery already in your heart with her. Same thing Job said: don't be deceived by a woman. And you sisters, some of you sisters be hard up and get sucked into lasciviousness and pornography as well.

- Watch what Christ says the very next verse. There's a reason why he goes from saying, if you look upon a woman to lust, you've committed adultery with her already, to this next verse.

- If thy right eye offend thee, pluck it out: now you might think, damn, okay, Christ just went into another topic. No, he's still on the same vein, giving you solutions to deal with what he said in verse 28.

- It is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish: what are those members?


**[Colossians 3:5-10](/bible/colossians/3#v5)**  *[[7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=450s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/colossians/3#v6)</sup> For which things’ sake the wrath of God cometh on the children of disobedience:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/colossians/3#v7)</sup> In the which ye also walked some time, when ye lived in them.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/3#v8)</sup> But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/colossians/3#v9)</sup> Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/colossians/3#v10)</sup> And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him:

- Mortify therefore your members: what does it mean to mortify? Means to kill or to cut off, like we just read in Matthew.

- Evil concupiscence: that's pornography right there, that evil sexual lust.

- And covetousness, which is idolatry: all these things, you see he ends it with which is idolatry. What you don't realize, you can make your rod an idol. Looking at pornography, you are so hard up that you will listen to Peter, and I ain't talking about the apostle, over the Bible. And we ain't talking about the book of Peter.

- So just like Paul is explaining in Colossians, we must cut off or kill those members, just like Christ said: if your right eye offend thee, cut it out. It is better for your member to perish, which is fornication, adulteries, evil concupiscence.

- For which things' sake the wrath of God cometh on the children of disobedience: you want to know why a lot of people have problems and issues in their marriages? Cuz you're watching too much porn. You're watching too much lascivious activity, and you're trying to bring that now into the bedroom, and wondering why your wife ain't swinging from a chandelier like the chick you just spent 6 hours watching. It don't work like that.

- That industry, they are set up like that for a reason. They know what they're doing. They know it's a dopamine hit. They know there's levels and stages in this. Their job is to get you addicted to it, just like many of these industries. It's our job as Israelites that come into this truth and understand this Bible to mortify our members. To make a covenant with our eyes.

- In the which ye also walked some time: cuz we used to walk like that. Many of us come from the world, it was okay. That was normal, a normal lifestyle, to watch that type of stuff. No, not in this truth.

- But now ye also put off all these: the fornication, the uncleanness, the inordinate affections, the evil concupiscence, and the covetousness, which is idolatry. Paul mentions all these things cuz they all go hand in hand. You're watching pornography. It leads to fornication. It leads to covetousness, cuz you now start looking at other people's wives and other women, cuz you want the real thing now, because you tired of just looking at a screen. And it all is idolatry, cuz now you have made something else your idol. Something else has more control over you than the Bible.

- Seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds: all that is the old man and his deeds.

- And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge: we have to be renewed in knowledge. And what is the knowledge? What we read in Matthew 5. What we read in Job 31. Christ said if thy right eye offend thee, cut it off. We all know the trigger. You know where things started to where you fell into that lust.

- A lot of times many brothers and sisters will say, yeah, man, I'm battling. No, you're not battling. You are in the midst of. Battling is the thought comes and you don't give in to it. In the midst of: the thought comes and you give in to it. Or you put yourself in the same situations that cause you to fall and stumble.

- After the image of him that created him: we got to walk just like Christ walked, cuz he didn't give in to the flesh. He didn't give in to the evil thoughts that might have came to his mind to not complete the mission. That's how we must walk.

- Whatever makes you susceptible to that: for example, you might be a brother or sister that can't be on social media, cuz you know, okay, my spirit can't handle that, cuz for some reason every time I get on there, I always end up at the same place. Christ said, if thy right eye offend thee, cut it off. You have to know yourself.


**[Romans 7:22-23](/bible/romans/7#v22)**  *[[14:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=856s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/7#v22)</sup> For I delight in the law of God after the inward man:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/7#v23)</sup> But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members.

- For I delight in the law of God after the inward man: you must delight in the law of God. Your meditation must be in the laws of God. Think about it. Ain't nobody ever in the history been sitting down, reading the Bible, and praying, and all of a sudden now they want to go watch porn. It don't happen. You ain't never been around brothers talking about scriptures and then just said, you know what, I want to look at this flick. That's how we have to renew our minds and train our minds.

- But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind: that spirit is always there warring against us. That's the fight. That's the battle. That's why we must give our earnest heed to make our calling and election sure. We must apply the things given and written, so we don't fall susceptible to that.


**[2 Corinthians 13:5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)**  *[[15:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=926s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)</sup> Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?

- Examine yourselves: we all must examine ourselves.

- Prove your own selves: how do you prove yourselves? You take inventory of yourself. You know what leads you to that gateway of porn. You know what caused you to go down that road. You know the steps that you take in your life that put you in those situations. It's up to you to be real with yourself and fix it.

- Except ye be reprobates: anytime you keep doing the same thing over and over and over, looking for a different result, they call that insanity. The Bible also talks about you being reprobate, cuz you're not actually really examining yourself and fixing the problems that you have within you that keep causing you to go down that lascivious path.


**[Sirach 17:26](/bible/sirach/17#v26)**  *[[16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZVUPd0ig-y8&t=996s)]*

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/17#v26)</sup> Turn again to the most High, and turn away from iniquity: for he will lead thee out of darkness into the light of health, and hate thou abomination vehemently.

- Turn again to the most High, and turn away from iniquity: so that's what we must all do. We all have a choice. That's why Moses said, choose ye this day.

- And hate thou abomination vehemently: that's how we must all get to that point, where we hate abomination vehemently. We hate sin. We hate to look at it. We hate to see it. We hate it, just like the Bible says.
