## Introduction

*[[0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=15s)]* Today, 15 Minutes with the Captains, we're going to go into the gift of exhortation. The gift of exhortation: what is it? Do you have it? And can it be beneficial to the body?

## Scriptures Opened

**[Romans 12:5-8](/bible/romans/12#v5)**  *[[0:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=38s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/romans/12#v5)</sup> So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/12#v6)</sup> Having then gifts differing according to the grace that is given to us, whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/12#v7)</sup> Or ministry, let us wait on our ministering: or he that teacheth, on teaching;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/12#v8)</sup> Or he that exhorteth, on exhortation: he that giveth, let him do it with simplicity; he that ruleth, with diligence; he that sheweth mercy, with cheerfulness.

- We being many in the body are one in Christ. So all of the gifts that are placed within us is for the edifying of the entire body of Christ. It serves his purpose.

- One of the gifts given to us is prophecy. We got leadership: they could go within the prophecies, take the news, put it together, and show what happened and what's going to happen. That is a gift.

- Then there's another type of teaching. It may be classroom setting, it may be camp setting, it may be sisters dealing with the kids, to get people engaged to understand. That is a gift.

- The definition of exhortation is read: "A strong, earnest speech or piece of writing that urges, advises, or encourages someone to take a specific action."

- It's a gift from God to be able to speak in such a way where you can urge someone, advise someone, or encourage someone to a specific action. Whatever the mission is that we're about to undertake, whatever the thought is that we need to have the body all included on, there's one person, or a handful of brothers and or sisters, that have a gift, a spirit from God, where they can speak and people move.

- Exhortation is a very special gift, and those that have it serve a very special purpose in the body of Christ.


**[1 Corinthians 12:4-6](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v4)**  *[[3:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=184s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v4)</sup> Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v5)</sup> And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v6)</sup> And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all.

- If you prophesy, if you minister where you help people, if you teach people, if you exhort people, all of those gifts are diverse in the way they're executed, but they all have one spirit. And whose spirit is that? It's Christ.

- Differences of administrations: you have the stewardship, brothers in the civilian side of the army of the living God. Then you have all types of things in the military: the camp equipment, camp locations. All these things make the nation move, but it's the same Lord. We all serve God.

- If you're exhorting a brother or a sister, it's not for your own benefit. It's for God's benefit. It's for the nation's benefit.

- In the picture on screen, up in the upper left corner you have the classroom teaching. Right next to him you have the brother exhorting on the podcast, and then you have them exhorting, making content. These are the different ways: whatever we got to do to make them move, we make them move.


**[Hebrews 3:13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)**  *[[4:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=299s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)</sup> But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.

- What are the different ways you can exhort people? You can first exhort a brother or a sister to righteousness.

- Exhort one another daily while it is called today. That's why in Malachi 3:16 the scripture say those that feared the Lord spoke often one to another. In that exhortation you can lift a brother or a sister up, where you get them out of their own head. They've been mulling about, musing on many things, and then you call: "What's going on? You ready for Tabernacles? How was your Day of Atonement? How does it feel having a clean slate?" And you lift up their spirit and build up their spirit.

- Sin is very sneaky. Sin is very deceitful. Sin will have you thinking that you don't need anybody, that you're good by yourself. No, we need the exhortation of a brother or a sister to righteousness.


**[Jude 1:3](/bible/jude/1#v3)**  *[[6:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=379s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/jude/1#v3)</sup> Beloved, when I gave all diligence to write unto you of the common salvation, it was needful for me to write unto you, and exhort you that ye should earnestly contend for the faith which was once delivered unto the saints.

- Exhort you: to advise you, to counsel you, to urge you.

- He had to urge them to contend for the faith. He had to urge them to get in the fight, to do something different than what they were doing. That is a gift from the Lord. And if you want that gift, pray for it.


**[Acts 14:21-22](/bible/acts/14#v21)**  *[[7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=428s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/acts/14#v21)</sup> And when they had preached the gospel to that city, and had taught many, they returned again to Lystra, and to Iconium, and Antioch,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/14#v22)</sup> Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

- When leadership goes and visits the different schools that they set up, they're confirming the souls of the saints: yes, you are the children of Israel. In these countries that we go to, they don't know that. They have hundreds of years, thousands of years, of doing it the wrong way. And now we come and confirm them in the scriptures through prophecy, that is also a gift, that they are the children of Israel, and exhort them to continue in the faith.

- We know according to Sirach 2 it's going to get challenging. It's going to get difficult.

- Through much tribulation: that's a part that you going to have to encourage some brothers and sisters about. They might get fearful. They might see the ChatGPT images of us hit upside the head and blood everywhere, and then they might see the real video of Kenya getting hit upside the head, and there's blood everywhere. You got to exhort them: don't be shaken by that. Continue in the faith.


**[1 Maccabees 2:49-52,62-64](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v49)**  *[[8:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=528s)]*

> <sup>[49](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v49)</sup> Now when the time drew near that Mattathias should die, he said unto his sons, Now hath pride and rebuke gotten strength, and the time of destruction, and the wrath of indignation:
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v50)</sup> Now therefore, my sons, be ye zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of your fathers.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v51)</sup> Call to remembrance what acts our fathers did in their time; so shall ye receive great honour and an everlasting name.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v52)</sup> Was not Abraham found faithful in temptation, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness?
>
> <sup>[62](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v62)</sup> Fear not then the words of a sinful man: for his glory shall be dung and worms.
>
> <sup>[63](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v63)</sup> To day he shall be lifted up, and to morrow he shall not be found, because he is returned into his dust, and his thought is come to nothing.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v64)</sup> Wherefore, ye my sons, be valiant, and shew yourselves men in the behalf of the law; for by it shall ye obtain glory.

- You can also exhort to courage. You can exhort a brother, a sister, your children to courage.

- He gathered his sons around and said: I'm about to die, but the times are evil and they only going to get worse.

- Be ye zealous for the laws of God. Be on fire for these commandments.

- Give your lives for the covenant of your fathers: you think he whispered that to them? No, he said it with his last breaths. Be willing to give your life for the covenant of your fathers. Exhorting them to be courageous and to fight.

- He starts at Abraham and goes down the list of forefathers, exhorting them in their own history, in their bloodline, reminding them who they come from.

- Don't fear the mouths of them sinful men. Their glory is dung and worms, death and destruction and decay. This is the fate of the so-called white man, the so-called Chinese man, Arab man. This is exhortation right here. They might be in a high state right now, but their glory is dung, worms, and decay. Right now he's up; tomorrow he will be no longer.


**[1 Maccabees 3:18-22](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v18)**  *[[11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=697s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v18)</sup> Unto whom Judas answered, It is no hard matter for many to be shut up in the hands of a few; and with the God of heaven it is all one, to deliver with a great multitude, or a small company:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v19)</sup> For the victory of battle standeth not in the multitude of an host; but strength cometh from heaven.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v20)</sup> They come against us in much pride and iniquity to destroy us, and our wives and children, and to spoil us:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v21)</sup> But we fight for our lives and our laws.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v22)</sup> Wherefore the Lord himself will overthrow them before our face: and as for you, be ye not afraid of them.

- What happened to his son? Judas is exhorting the small army that had to go fight against the larger army. He's exhorting them to courage the way his father did to him.

- Where did he get that from? He got that from his father, who got that from his fathers. That's a gift of God, to be able to urge and compel a man to go out there and risk his life for his family, his laws, and the covenant of his fathers.

- They come to spoil our nation, our people. We can't have that.

- The exhortation can move an army, can move men to action, to change their mindset from fear to courage, from doubt to conviction.


**[Proverbs 15:23](/bible/proverbs/15#v23)**  *[[13:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=787s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/15#v23)</sup> A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth: and a word spoken in due season, how good is it!

- Never underestimate the exhortation that you could provide for a brother. You can help build him up in his confidence, in his identity of who he is as a soldier of the Lord.

- Sometimes that word spoken is, "Hey, that was excellent teaching." Sometimes it's, "Keep up the good work. I see what you're doing. You're in the spirit." That's a good word. It could build somebody up. It could provide joy. It can change their spirit and encourage them to endure longer in this truth.


**[Proverbs 25:11](/bible/proverbs/25#v11)**  *[[14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=852s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/25#v11)</sup> A word fitly spoken is like apples of gold in pictures of silver.

- Fitly spoken: it is right on time. It's like the missing puzzle piece. That's a beautiful sight.

- The correct exhortation in the right moment at the right time can do wonders for a family, for a household, for a brother, for a sister. We have to be mindful of the words that we speak, and we also have to exhort one another daily while it's called today, lest we be hardened by the deceitfulness of sin.

- The exhortation that we provide can help keep a brother out of sin. He might think that all he was was just a no good Negro. But you told him: "No, I see the spirit of God in you. I see Christ in you. I see the man that you're becoming." I see the sister that you're becoming, the good mother, the good wife. Those words can go a long way.


**[Sirach 18:16](/bible/sirach/18#v16)**  *[[15:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0UYq-4vLsH4&t=919s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/18#v16)</sup> Shall not the dew asswage the heat? so is a word better than a gift.

- When the dew comes from heaven and it's hot, it makes everything cool. It makes everything better. It provides relief.

- So is a word better than a gift: that's how powerful the words of exhortation can be, for a brother or for a sister, for a nation, for a community, for a body.

- Leaders, make sure you're exhorting the men under you. Don't always be breaking brothers down, chopping brothers down, breaking sisters down. Make sure that the same way you're plucking down, you're building up at the same time.
