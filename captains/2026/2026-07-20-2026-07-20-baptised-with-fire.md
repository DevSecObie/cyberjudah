## Introduction

*[[0:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=19s)]* Today's topic is baptized with fire. John the Baptist said he that cometh after me shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire. The Holy Ghost is the spirit of Christ dwelling in you. The fire has two meanings: judgment upon the wicked, the chaff burned with unquenchable fire, and the fire of adversity that those who follow Christ must go through to be purified as gold. Prepare for your adversity.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 3:11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)**  *[[0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=26s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)</sup> I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:

- This is John the Baptist speaking. He that cometh after me is mightier than I: referring to Jesus the Christ.

- He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire. So let's get the Holy Ghost first.


**[John 14:16-18](/bible/john/14#v16)**  *[[1:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=64s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/14#v16)</sup> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/john/14#v17)</sup> Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/john/14#v18)</sup> I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.

- Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive: we always say we're in the truth. The spirit of truth is the understanding of who you are, and what you must do to please God, in the faith of Jesus Christ.


**[John 14:26](/bible/john/14#v26)**  *[[1:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=109s)]*

> <sup>[26](/bible/john/14#v26)</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

- The Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, is the spirit of Christ.

- He shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance. So the Holy Spirit is literally the spirit of Christ dwelling in you. When you have the Holy Spirit, the spirit of Christ is dwelling in you: you have the understanding, you have the spirit of truth, you are obedient unto the commandments.


**[1 Peter 1:10-11](/bible/1-peter/1#v10)**  *[[2:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=146s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-peter/1#v10)</sup> Of which salvation the prophets have enquired and searched diligently, who prophesied of the grace that should come unto you:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-peter/1#v11)</sup> Searching what, or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ which was in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow.

- The Spirit of Christ which was in them: so the spirit of Christ, which is the spirit of truth, which is the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, was in the prophets.

- When it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ: it testified beforehand because it had the understanding, the spirit of truth, the spirit of Christ.


**[Matthew 3:11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)**  *[[3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=198s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)</sup> I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:

- John said, I baptized you with water unto repentance, but he that cometh after me is going to do a different baptism. He's not going to baptize you with water.

- He shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost: he's going to baptize you in the understanding, with the spirit of Christ, with the spirit of truth. And with fire.


**[Job 11:6](/bible/job/11#v6)**  *[[4:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=246s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/job/11#v6)</sup> And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.

- This fire is twofold. When we read the Bible, the Bible has duality in its meanings. It could be meaning something in context for that time period, but also be prophetically talking about another time. It also could be referring to two separate things: the fire could represent one thing and also another thing, which we're going to go into the scriptures and explain.

- The secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is: the secrets of wisdom have double meanings, and through the precepts, through line upon line, we can get the understanding of what those meanings are.


**[Matthew 3:12](/bible/matthew/3#v12)**  *[[5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=322s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/3#v12)</sup> Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.

- So what does it mean, with fire? The first meaning of fire is literally judgment.

- He will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire. What is that chaff? This is clearly describing a judgment, a punishment that's coming upon the wicked.


**[Psalms 1:4](/bible/psalms/1#v4)**  *[[5:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=353s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/1#v4)</sup> The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

- The ungodly are like the chaff: the wicked is the chaff. Christ said they're going to be burned up with an unquenchable fire.

- So that's the first meaning. Those who are disobedient unto the word of God, you're going to be baptized with fire. Those Israelites who decide to reject the covenant, to reject the law, statutes and commandments and the faith of Christ, you're going to be burned with fire.


**[Malachi 4:1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)**  *[[6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=403s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)</sup> For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.

- The day cometh, that shall burn as an oven: the day is going to come where the earth is going to be so hot it's going to feel like an oven. People are going to be destroyed through this heat.

- All that do wickedly, shall be stubble: going into the chaff that's going to be burned with an unquenchable fire.

- It shall leave them neither root nor branch: meaning their descendants are going to be destroyed as well. They won't continue to replenish the earth with their seed.


**[Sirach 2:5](/bible/sirach/2#v5)**  *[[7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=462s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/2#v5)</sup> For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.

- What I really wanted to get into is the second meaning, the duality meaning of fire, which is adversity.

- Gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity: men who become acceptable have to go through the furnace of adversity. That means you have to go through different trials to prove who you are, to refine who you are, to develop who you are.

- Character is built through a series of trials to prove who you are and what you're going to do when you're faced with different situations. How are you going to respond, react, deal, handle yourself?


**[Job 23:10](/bible/job/23#v10)**  *[[9:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=544s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/job/23#v10)</sup> But he knoweth the way that I take: when he hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold.

- These trials, this adversity you're going to go through, are not meant to destroy you. This adversity is meant to purify you. It's a purification process, a weeding out process, because some people are going to come and endure for a while, like it says in Mark 4. But once the adversity comes, once the trial comes, once the persecution comes, they endure but for a season and then they're gone with the wind. They're back in the world, back celebrating Christmas, back doing Easter egg hunting, dressing up in matching pajamas with their unbelieving family. We've seen it time and time again.

- When he hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold: that's the confidence and the mindset we've got to have. No matter what is thrown our way, we've still got to endure and continue pushing forward in this truth.


**[1 Peter 4:12-13](/bible/1-peter/4#v12)**  *[[10:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=615s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-peter/4#v12)</sup> Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened unto you:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-peter/4#v13)</sup> But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s sufferings; that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.

- Think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you: John already knew through the spirit that those that follow Christ are going to have to be baptized with the fire of adversity. You're going to have to go through some very challenging times, some very difficult things. You're not just going to get the kingdom for free. You're going to have to be made worthy for the kingdom.

- As though some strange thing happened unto you: when things start happening in your life, you're like, why is this happening? It's not some strange thing that's happening to you. These are things that were ordained to purify you.

- Partakers of Christ's sufferings: that also happened in the scriptures with the sons of Zebedee, the sons of thunder.


**[Matthew 20:20-23](/bible/matthew/20#v20)**  *[[11:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=695s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/20#v20)</sup> Then came to him the mother of Zebedee’s children with her sons, worshipping him, and desiring a certain thing of him.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/20#v21)</sup> And he said unto her, What wilt thou? She saith unto him, Grant that these my two sons may sit, the one on thy right hand, and the other on the left, in thy kingdom.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/20#v22)</sup> But Jesus answered and said, Ye know not what ye ask. Are ye able to drink of the cup that I shall drink of, and to be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with? They say unto him, We are able.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/20#v23)</sup> And he saith unto them, Ye shall drink indeed of my cup, and be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with: but to sit on my right hand, and on my left, is not mine to give, but it shall be given to them for whom it is prepared of my Father.

- We think that if we're going to be glorified with Christ, we don't have to go through the same thing that Christ went through. Oh, we're going to go through the sufferings of Christ. We're going to have to endure some hard times.

- A lot of times brothers think that once you give your life to the Lord and dedicate your life to furthering this truth, it's going to be a bed of roses, a cakewalk. No. You're going to be tried, to see whether this is what you really want to do. What are you really made out of? Gold, or tin, or wood? The adversity is what's going to show and prove what you're made out of.

- Ye know not what ye ask: the mother asks, can my two sons receive this great blessing? And Jesus said, listen, you don't know the things your sons are going to have to endure in order to receive that glory.

- Are ye able to drink of the cup that I shall drink of? Are you able to go through the adversity that I've got to go through? Are you willing to be ridiculed of men, disrespected, disregarded, killed, lose your life, lose everything you have?

- And to be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with: that's that baptism of fire, that baptism of adversity that we must go through.


**[Acts 12:1-3](/bible/acts/12#v1)**  *[[14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=844s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/12#v1)</sup> Now about that time Herod the king stretched forth his hands to vex certain of the church.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/acts/12#v2)</sup> And he killed James the brother of John with the sword.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/12#v3)</sup> And because he saw it pleased the Jews, he proceeded further to take Peter also. (Then were the days of unleavened bread.)

- He killed James the brother of John with the sword: these are the brothers referenced in Matthew that we just read, who said we can drink of that cup. And surely he drank of that cup. He murdered him.

- He proceeded further to take Peter also. This is the plight that we have to endure as followers of Christ. We have to prepare our minds and our spirits to be able to endure these things. These things must happen.


**[Matthew 19:27-29](/bible/matthew/19#v27)**  *[[15:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=907s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/19#v27)</sup> Then answered Peter and said unto him, Behold, we have forsaken all, and followed thee; what shall we have therefore?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/19#v28)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, Verily I say unto you, That ye which have followed me, in the regeneration when the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his glory, ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/19#v29)</sup> And every one that hath forsaken houses, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my name’s sake, shall receive an hundredfold, and shall inherit everlasting life.

- Behold, we have forsaken all, and followed thee: some of y'all don't want to forsake all when y'all come to follow the Lord. You want to hold on to certain things, people. That's going to hinder your walk with the Lord.

- Every one that hath forsaken houses: something's going to happen where you're going to lose a home, take a major loss. Or brethren, or sisters: you're going to lose family.

- Or father, or mother: some of y'all don't want to do that, especially you sisters. Sisters don't want to let go of a wicked mother. Listen, she's not going to be in the world to come. She don't want to change, don't want to repent, don't want to follow the Lord. Her mind is on evil, on BS. And guess what you do? You love your mama. You follow your mama.

- Or wife: sometimes brothers come into this truth and their wife don't believe, and they've got to say goodbye. Or some brothers will be married and she'll step out on them and deal with another man. That's to try your faith, brother. Are you going to simp out, or are you going to put her away like the scriptures say? This is your trial, your adversity that you've got to go through so that you may be fine gold. Some of y'all are going to lose your wife whom you love oh so much.

- Or children: your kids grow up to be demons, and you want to hold on to them, trim your ways for those kids. Or lands, for my name's sake: you're going to lose those things because you chose to follow Christ. Now you've got to take what comes with it. You've got to be baptized with that fire.

- Shall receive an hundredfold, and shall inherit everlasting life: that's the faith you've got to have, because everlasting life don't come right away. It don't come right after you put away that wicked woman who cheated on you, or after you get rid of those wicked kids who refuse to keep God's commandments, who sneak their boyfriend or girlfriend into your house behind your back, who go to school and take the dress off and put on pants or shorts. We've seen it all.

- You still have to deal with the loss of those kids, grieve and get over it. It don't happen right away. And some of y'all will initially do it and then go backwards: you start to trim your ways, to backtrack. You cannot be that way.


**[Wisdom of Solomon 3:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/3#v5)**  *[[18:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oWPP4-UMaOk&t=1135s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/3#v5)</sup> And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly rewarded: for God proved them, and found them worthy for himself.

- Having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly rewarded: God calls the adversity we're going to go through, even if you lose your life, a little chastisement, because it is nothing compared to the reward we're going to receive: life everlasting and abundant.
