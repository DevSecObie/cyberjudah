## Introduction

*[[0:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=1s)]* Today's topic is entitled wherever you go, there you are, meaning you can't escape yourself. No matter where you move, travel, relocate or try to start over, you still bring your thoughts, habits, personalities, traumas, weaknesses and mindset with you. This saying challenges the idea that changing your location automatically changes your life or your situation. Family, listen: you can't outrun yourself. We've got to repent.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Jeremiah 17:9](/bible/jeremiah/17#v9)**  *[[0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=35s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/17#v9)</sup> The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?

- The heart is deceitful above all things. Meaning what? You might be tricked into believing, let me relocate to Atlanta, my life will be better; let me relocate to Memphis, and my life might be better. No.

- And desperately wicked: that's like a cornered rat, just desperate to escape. That's how our minds are, family.

- Who can know it? Who can know the thoughts of the mind?


**[Proverbs 4:23](/bible/proverbs/4#v23)**  *[[1:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=85s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/4#v23)</sup> Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.

- Keep thy heart with all diligence: you've got to keep your mind with all diligence. That's why Bishop put out study, pray and apply.

- For out of it are the issues of life: whatever's going on in your mind is going to come out of your mouth or be on display through your actions.

- So remember the class topic, wherever you go, there you are: your mindset determines everything, no matter where you are located.


**[Exodus 16:1-3](/bible/exodus/16#v1)**  *[[2:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=125s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/16#v1)</sup> And they took their journey from Elim, and all the congregation of the children of Israel came unto the wilderness of Sin, which is between Elim and Sinai, on the fifteenth day of the second month after their departing out of the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/16#v2)</sup> And the whole congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/16#v3)</sup> And the children of Israel said unto them, Would to God we had died by the hand of the Lord in the land of Egypt, when we sat by the flesh pots, and when we did eat bread to the full; for ye have brought us forth into this wilderness, to kill this whole assembly with hunger.

- Let's get an example out of the Bible. We just left Egypt, where we were held as slaves. Now we're in the wilderness, and we got to complaining.

- Would to God we had died by the hand of the LORD in the land of Egypt: were they not just slaves in Egypt? We just got delivered with a deliverance nobody's ever seen. We're in a whole new area, but guess what? The slave mindset traveled with them out there into the wilderness where they were free. They may have relocated, but the mindset was still back in Egypt as a slave.

- When we sat by the flesh pots: they were slaves, but they were reminiscing on the comforts that they had.

- That right there lets you know the mindset of our people. We would choose comfort over freedom. We were slaves in Egypt, and we brought the same slave mindset out there.


**[Numbers 14:4](/bible/numbers/14#v4)**  *[[3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=233s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/14#v4)</sup> And they said one to another, Let us make a captain, and let us return into Egypt.

- Let us make a captain, and let us return into Egypt. Listen, family: they changed their location, but the mindset was still the same.

- A lot of us really believe that if we change our location, our circumstances are going to improve. It's not like that, family. We've got to change our mind.


**[Jonah 1:1-5](/bible/jonah/1#v1)**  *[[4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=266s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/jonah/1#v1)</sup> Now the word of the Lord came unto Jonah the son of Amittai, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jonah/1#v2)</sup> Arise, go to Nineveh, that great city, and cry against it; for their wickedness is come up before me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jonah/1#v3)</sup> But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish from the presence of the Lord, and went down to Joppa; and he found a ship going to Tarshish: so he paid the fare thereof, and went down into it, to go with them unto Tarshish from the presence of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jonah/1#v4)</sup> But the Lord sent out a great wind into the sea, and there was a mighty tempest in the sea, so that the ship was like to be broken.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jonah/1#v5)</sup> Then the mariners were afraid, and cried every man unto his god, and cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten it of them. But Jonah was gone down into the sides of the ship; and he lay, and was fast asleep.

- This is a classic example. The Lord said, listen, Jonah, I need you to go down there and prophesy against Nineveh. But Jonah rose up to flee unto Tarshish: he decided to do his own thing. I'm not going to go there; I think I need to go to Tarshish.

- The LORD sent out a great wind into the sea: that wind, that storm, represents troubles. He relocated to an area he wasn't supposed to go to, and the same troubles followed him. That's what you've got to see today. If you've got a ninja mindset up here, or wherever you're at, and you go to a different country, a different city, you're just going to be a ninja wherever you relocate. Them same ninja problems are going to follow you.

- So that the ship was like to be broken: just like your life. Damn, you know what, man, we up here in DC, I've got a broken life, I'm going to relocate. You go to another area, and your life is still broken.

- And cast forth the wares that were in the ship into the sea, to lighten it: they're trying to figure out what to do. That's what you might do: maybe if I get a new job, a new car, go to a different church. You're trying to unload all these burdens, and the storm's still going on, because you didn't unload the main burden, which is your thoughts, which govern everything you think, do and say.


**[Jonah 1:10-12](/bible/jonah/1#v10)**  *[[6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=415s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/jonah/1#v10)</sup> Then were the men exceedingly afraid, and said unto him, Why hast thou done this? For the men knew that he fled from the presence of the Lord, because he had told them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jonah/1#v11)</sup> Then said they unto him, What shall we do unto thee, that the sea may be calm unto us? for the sea wrought, and was tempestuous.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jonah/1#v12)</sup> And he said unto them, Take me up, and cast me forth into the sea; so shall the sea be calm unto you: for I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you.

- Then were the men exceedingly afraid, because of the storm. Why hast thou done this?

- How can we relate this today? You go down there and call one of your family members, and they're like, hold on, weren't you just in the same trouble back up here? You got into the same stuff down there? Bro, what is you doing? It's you. It's not where you are. It's you, brother.

- You might move somewhere with your cousin and bring your same problems down there, and he's going to be like, yo, bro, you've got to get out. I didn't have them problems till you came down here. I opened up my door, and you did nothing but trouble my house since you've been here. You see them Facebook posts like that: yeah, I'm moving this, that and the third. You get down there, you bunk up, you room with somebody, and they throw you out because you're bringing your troubles and drama down there.

- So shall the sea be calm unto you: man, my life was good before you got here. I've got to throw you out and go back to that calm life I had before you got here.

- For I know that for my sake this great tempest is upon you: that's what they'll say, brother. You brought all this drama to my life. Listen, family, we've got to get ourselves together. You can't escape your problems.


**[Luke 15:11-19](/bible/luke/15#v11)**  *[[8:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=528s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/luke/15#v11)</sup> And he said, A certain man had two sons:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/luke/15#v12)</sup> And the younger of them said to his father, Father, give me the portion of goods that falleth to me. And he divided unto them his living.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/15#v13)</sup> And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/15#v14)</sup> And when he had spent all, there arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be in want.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/luke/15#v15)</sup> And he went and joined himself to a citizen of that country; and he sent him into his fields to feed swine.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/luke/15#v16)</sup> And he would fain have filled his belly with the husks that the swine did eat: and no man gave unto him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/luke/15#v17)</sup> And when he came to himself, he said, How many hired servants of my father’s have bread enough and to spare, and I perish with hunger!
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/15#v18)</sup> I will arise and go to my father, and will say unto him, Father, I have sinned against heaven, and before thee,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/luke/15#v19)</sup> And am no more worthy to be called thy son: make me as one of thy hired servants.

- We know this is a parable talking about the northern kingdom.

- Took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living: he took his money and moved somewhere else, living just a riotous living. He didn't change anything. He just changed his location, but he was still the same person.

- There arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be in want: the famine hit. Damn, I'm lacking these cheap things that I had.

- He sent him into his fields to feed swine: he may have left the area where he had a good job and relocated, and now he's down there digging ditches or something.

- He would fain have filled his belly with the husks that the swine did eat: he was barely making it. He relocated to a whole new area without counsel and got a job where he can barely feed himself. We even had that happen in the body.

- When he came to himself: hold on, man, what am I doing? I need to return where I came from, because I was actually doing better up there than when I relocated down here. I can't even afford to keep a roof over my head.

- This is an example: the prodigal son left without counsel, moved to another area, engaged in riotous living, had to take a job where he couldn't even feed himself or keep a roof over his head, and had to go back. Meaning we carry our problems with us no matter where we go.


**[Mark 7:21-23](/bible/mark/7#v21)**  *[[11:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=688s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/mark/7#v21)</sup> For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/mark/7#v22)</sup> Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/mark/7#v23)</sup> All these evil things come from within, and defile the man.

- Out of the heart of men proceed evil thoughts: if I'm evil here and move over there, my thoughts haven't changed. Look at these things that come out of the mind. All these things are within the man.

- An evil eye: meaning you think it's everybody around you. Man, they're not in the spirit up there, I've got to move. They're tripping on my job, I've got to get a new job. It's like, brother, sister, you're getting in trouble no matter where you're at.

- All these evil things come from within, and defile the man: that's how we get defiled.


**[Matthew 23:25-26](/bible/matthew/23#v25)**  *[[12:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=752s)]*

> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/23#v25)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/23#v26)</sup> Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also.

- Let's get a perfect example of men that were defiled inwardly. Ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter: they may have been at the top of society, the leaders, but he said y'all are clean on the outside.

- But within they are full of extortion and excess: no matter if they were the chiefs in the synagogue in Jerusalem, no matter where they were, Christ is saying, listen, y'all are filthy on the inside.

- Cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter: meaning get your mind right. You could be at the top of society, but if your mind is broken, it doesn't matter if you're at the top or the bottom. What is Christ instructing? Clean up your mind. It's not about where you are; it's where you're at mentally.


**[Romans 7:18](/bible/romans/7#v18)**  *[[13:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=829s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/7#v18)</sup> For I know that in me (that is, in my flesh,) dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not.

- In me, that is, in my flesh, dwelleth no good thing: it doesn't matter where you go. If you know yourself, there's nothing good in us.

- How to perform that which is good I find not: we've got to be guided by the scriptures. The will to do right is there, but we can't find it on the inside. We only find it in the scriptures.


**[Sirach 32:17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)**  *[[14:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=863s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)</sup> A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.

- A sinful man will not be reproved: if you're always making excuses, man, the brothers ain't in the spirit over here, the sisters ain't in the spirit, you move over there and they're not in the spirit over there either. Bro, you've got to examine your spirit. Sister, you've got to examine your spirit. You went halfway around the country and you're still in the same drama.

- If you think it's about your location, the Bible is telling you a sinful man or woman cannot be changed or corrected.

- But findeth an excuse according to his will: you're going to look for every other excuse but the real reason. Get your mind right.


**[2 Corinthians 13:5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)**  *[[15:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=915s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)</sup> Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?

- So what must we do? Examine yourselves: is it about my location, or my current mindset?

- Whether ye be in the faith: Paul is saying, brother, it's about your mindset. You've got to examine yourself to see if you're in the faith.

- Prove your own selves: am I living my life according to the scriptures?

- Except ye be reprobates: if you don't really know yourself, you're reprobate. You've got to know the things you're dealing with.


**[Psalms 19:7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)**  *[[16:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRZ_BZQvJrA&t=964s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

- So how do we change this? What's the real solution? Is it me relocating to another city?

- The law of the LORD is perfect: not where you live, brother. Not where you live, sister. Converting the soul: that's what changes our mind. Changing the zip code does not change your mind.

- The testimony of the LORD is sure, making wise the simple. So family, let's be wise. Let's examine ourselves and understand: it's not about where we currently are, but about our mindset. We've got to change our mind.
