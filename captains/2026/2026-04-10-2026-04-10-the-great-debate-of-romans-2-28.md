*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=14s)]* Today we going to do a quick topic on what many Christians get wrong in the Bible, cuz they don't read it in context. ## Scriptures Opened

**[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**  *[[1:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=72s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

- For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning: for us to learn from, to follow, to understand, to know how to proceed with our lives according to God.

- That we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope: hope in our lives, how to navigate and get through this world, how to understand what's going on.


**[Romans 2:28-29](/bible/romans/2#v28)**  *[[2:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=131s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/romans/2#v28)</sup> For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/romans/2#v29)</sup> But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter; whose praise is not of men, but of God.

- Was this for us to learn from about race or about something else?

- Christians pull that and they say all races, all nations. It's not about what you do on the outside, it's what you do on the inside. They try to say any race can be saved, any nation, you could be a Jew just by how you feel on the inside or your relationship with Christ, even though you can't find the word relationship in the Bible.

- He talks about circumcision outwardly and inwardly. You have to understand the history: who was the circumcision and who was the uncircumcision. When you read back in Kings with the split, the circumcision was the Jews, mainly the southern kingdom, Judah, Benjamin and Levi.


**[Romans 2:1](/bible/romans/2#v1)**  *[[3:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=232s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/2#v1)</sup> Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things.

- Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for thou that judgest doest the same things. He's talking to the judges: you're condemning yourself because you're trying to judge somebody else on a matter and you're doing the same sin.

- So the context so far has nothing to do with race. It's talking about judging, being a hypocrite, as many Christians do today: saying they love God and not doing none of the commandments.


**[Romans 2:21-24](/bible/romans/2#v21)**  *[[4:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=293s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/2#v21)</sup> Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/2#v22)</sup> Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/2#v23)</sup> Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/2#v24)</sup> For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written.

- Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? Whatever sins you're telling a person to stop, you at the same time should stop doing it. I can't be smoking cigarettes and then tell somebody else you got to stop smoking cigarettes.

- Thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal? Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? Same thing that's going on in these churches today: talking about the love of God, meanwhile these pastors in adultery and fornication, being total hypocrites.

- Thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege? In this time they're getting on the northern kingdom, the so-called Gentiles, who were the Israelites, for worshipping idols, while some of the southern kingdom are doing the same thing. Paul is letting them know you guys are being hypocrites.

- Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God? Boasting about the law while breaking the law. Nothing to do with race so far.

- For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you: who are the Gentiles he's talking about? The northern kingdom, the Israelites. When they went to idolatry, they were called Gentiles. They were called the uncircumcision. The southern kingdom, Judah, Benjamin and Levi, were called the circumcision.


**[Romans 2:25-27](/bible/romans/2#v25)**  *[[7:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=450s)]*

> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/2#v25)</sup> For circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law: but if thou be a breaker of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/romans/2#v26)</sup> Therefore if the uncircumcision keep the righteousness of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision?
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/romans/2#v27)</sup> And shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law?

- For circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law: circumcision only profits if you're keeping the law. These southern kingdom Jews were circumcised but still breaking the law, as all the Pharisees did in Christ's time.

- But if thou be a breaker of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision: just getting circumcised doesn't mean anything if you're breaking the law. Is this talking about anything to do with race? No. Not at all.

- Therefore if the uncircumcision keep the righteousness of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision? The northern kingdom, the uncircumcision, trying to keep the law. Yes, eventually they will have to get circumcised, but they have to start keeping the laws first.

- Judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law: you guys are circumcised, but you are still transgressing the law. Some of the southern kingdom were still doing the idolatry, the stealing, everything he named up top.


**[Romans 2:28-29](/bible/romans/2#v28)**  *[[9:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=573s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/romans/2#v28)</sup> For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/romans/2#v29)</sup> But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter; whose praise is not of men, but of God.

- The great debate scripture: Christians say anybody can be a Jew, if it's not outwardly, it's inwardly. Paul's not talking about that at all. Reading through the chapter, Paul has not said anything to do with any type of nation. This is talking about being a hypocrite and keeping the commandments.

- For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh: the outward appearance is the circumcision, the broad phylacteries, the fringes. Just wearing those things does not make you a Jew.

- But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly: keeping all the commandments when no one's watching him, not just outwardly in front of men. That's what makes you a real Jew when you're an Israelite.

- Whose praise is not of men, but of God: they were putting on a show for the people while committing all these sins. Where do you see anything about race? Nowhere. You Christians are lies. You don't read the Bible. You don't know how to rightly divide the word of truth.


**[Romans 3:1-2](/bible/romans/3#v1)**  *[[11:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=709s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/3#v1)</sup> What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/3#v2)</sup> Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.

- These are letters. They were not broken up like this beforetime. This is a long letter that Paul is writing. Paul's going to explain how the southern kingdom was supposed to be teaching the laws to all the other tribes, because they were the ones that kept the commandments. But Paul's getting on them saying you guys are being hypocrites.

- What advantage then hath the Jew? What advantage being southern kingdom, being Judah, Benjamin and Levi? Or what profit is there of circumcision?

- Chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God: the oracles of God were committed to the southern kingdom, the law givers. They're the ones that stayed with the right path of what God said to do. A remnant of the northern kingdom still kept the commandments, but the majority went into idolatry and had to be brought back.


**[Matthew 23:1-3](/bible/matthew/23#v1)**  *[[13:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=823s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/23#v1)</sup> Then spake Jesus to the multitude, and to his disciples,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/23#v2)</sup> Saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/23#v3)</sup> All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.

- Where's Paul getting this from? Christ taught Paul. That's where he's getting his understanding about them being hypocrites.

- The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses' seat: all therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do. Whatever they tell you to do by the law, you should do it.

- But do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not. They were saying to do these laws, but not doing them themselves. Just like the story of the woman caught in adultery: he that is without sin, let him first cast a stone. They was doing the sin too. That's why they all walked away. That's what Paul's getting at in Romans chapter two.


**[Matthew 23:23](/bible/matthew/23#v23)**  *[[15:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=901s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/23#v23)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.

- Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these scribes and Pharisees were being straight hypocrites, just like Paul's getting on the people in Romans.


**[Matthew 23:27](/bible/matthew/23#v27)**  *[[15:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=939s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/23#v27)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.

- Ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men's bones: just like Paul said, not a Jew outwardly, but one inwardly, keeping the commandments when the people are not watching. The Pharisees were righteous on the outside. Straight hypocrites, like these pastors today.


**[Romans 2:28](/bible/romans/2#v28)**  *[[16:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Za7Vkz8Or24&t=976s)]*
