## Introduction

*[[0:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=26s)]* Today's 15 Minutes with the Captains is called weapons of mass distraction: social media. A couple months back I did video games; it's a series to make sure we all stay focused on what God really needs from us. They call it doom scrolling: your mind gets so warped you don't even know what time of day it is. Even when some of y'all reading the Bible, your phone pings and you stop reading to start scrolling. Social media has its purpose, but we should be addicted to the ministry of the saints.

## Scriptures Opened

**[1 Corinthians 16:15](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v15)**  *[[1:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=110s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v15)</sup> I beseech you, brethren, (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints,)

- They have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints: that means you have to let those things go, because they're going to distract you. We should be addicted to this Bible, to the things our forefathers did, the Acts of the Apostles: what we need to do to be saved, who we need to go to, how to break this down. They're going to ask you about Paul's letters, which is a hard thing to explain. You can't go there looking dumb. You got to addict yourself to the reading and the understanding of God's word.


**[Ecclesiastes 3:1](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v1)**  *[[3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=181s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v1)</sup> To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven:

- We understand social media has its purpose. I'm not saying be a hermit crab and don't use social media.

- To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven: there's a time and a purpose for social media, to further the gospel. If you're saving photos, those photos should help you with your radio show, your classes, brothers trying to lose weight. How do I use this to better this truth? How do I use this to teach somebody something?

**[Sirach 38:24](/bible/sirach/38#v24)**  *[[7:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=455s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/38#v24)</sup> The wisdom of a learned man cometh by opportunity of leisure: and he that hath little business shall become wise.

- The wisdom of a learned man cometh by opportunity of leisure: being addicted to the scriptures and the understanding of God's word comes from an opportunity of leisure.

- And he that hath little business shall become wise: you got to get off of social media. You got to stop working so damn much. You might work to take care of your family, I get it, but you have to sit down and force yourself to do the work of the Lord, to be addicted to seeking the wisdom in God's law.


**[Sirach 39:1-2](/bible/sirach/39#v1)**  *[[8:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=511s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/39#v1)</sup> But he that giveth his mind to the law of the most High, and is occupied in the meditation thereof, will seek out the wisdom of all the ancient, and be occupied in prophecies.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/39#v2)</sup> He will keep the sayings of the renowned men: and where subtil parables are, he will be there also.

- This is the order in how you should start to read the Bible.

- But he that giveth his mind to the law of the most High: first, brothers and sisters, get off them social media and addict yourself to the law. Zakai say it all the time: you cannot understand the Bible if you don't understand where they getting it from. Why Christ said this, where did he get it from? The law. Why Paul says there's neither Jew nor Greek, or the law is done away with: he's clearly not saying you can commit adultery or do whatever you want with animals. But you don't understand what he's saying because you haven't gone first to the law.

- And is occupied in the meditation thereof: then you must be occupied in that law. Why is the calf not supposed to be seethed in his mother's milk?

- Will seek out the wisdom of all the ancient: once you understand the law, then you seek out the wisdom of Solomon, the proverbs. You don't go to the wisdom scriptures first, cuz you're going to be like, this damn thing don't make no sense, because you ain't read the law.

- And be occupied in prophecies: those are the most difficult ones. When Deacon Malachi and the Bishop start rattling off prophecies, you be like, golly. Why? Because they started with the law first, the milk first. If you've been in this truth long enough, you hear how brothers used to teach, then they're getting better, then all of a sudden fire mode. You cannot skip the process. If you got social media on your brain, you going to do the bare minimum just to get by.

- And where subtle parables are, he will be there also: where brothers are talking about scriptures, sharpening your sword with the brother. There's brothers and sisters in some schools that when scriptures are going out, they're not at no leadership table asking questions or even listening. They all the way in the back dancing, outside giggling. There's brothers with high ranks that never is in the war room trying to listen to the parables. It baffles me.


**[1 Corinthians 15:33](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v33)**  *[[12:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=770s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v33)</sup> Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.

- Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners. Constantly on social media doom scrolling, your communication is getting corrupt, cuz all type of evil is on social media. Your mind, your spirit is getting corrupt.


**[Mark 4:24](/bible/mark/4#v24)**  *[[13:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=798s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/mark/4#v24)</sup> And he said unto them, Take heed what ye hear: with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you: and unto you that hear shall more be given.

- Take heed what ye hear: be careful what you let get into your ears or your eyes.

- With what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you: whatever you put into your spirit, and how much of it, that's what's going to be in your mind, on your spirit, out of your mouth and in your actions.

- And unto you that hear shall more be given: if you're putting a lot of social media in your mind, you're going to have a warped sense of reality, cuz you're not going to be living in the reality of God.


**[1 Corinthians 10:23](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v23)**  *[[14:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_B1XxQh7A0&t=861s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v23)</sup> All things are lawful for me, but all things are not expedient: all things are lawful for me, but all things edify not.

- All things are lawful for me, but all things are not expedient: you can be on social media, but it might not be expedient for you, because whatever measure you mete in this social media is what's going to be in your spirit.

- But all things edify not: social media does not edify all the time.
