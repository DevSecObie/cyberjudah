## Introduction

*[[0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kD5tKkLirNo&t=5s)]* Today's topic is idle words, from IUIC Tupelo. Christ said every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment. A lot of us love to talk reckless, to speak evil of the leadership, of your brother, of your sister. You got to watch your mouth. Death and life are in the power of the tongue, and a backbiting tongue can put a congregation in disarray.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 12:36](/bible/matthew/12#v36)**  *[[0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kD5tKkLirNo&t=25s)]*

> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/12#v36)</sup> But I say unto you, That every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment.
