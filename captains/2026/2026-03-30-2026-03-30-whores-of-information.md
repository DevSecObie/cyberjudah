## Introduction

*[[0:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=57s)]* The episode opens with a short skit of two sisters gossiping ("Sis, I got something spicy to tell you... That new sister got beef with sis, you know who... What other tea you got for me? I love this drama, sis."). Today's class, we're going to go over gossip: whores of information. Are you a whore of information? In today's time, that's all you see on social media. Everybody got something to say. Everybody got gossip. Everybody got the tea. Everybody got the scoop. But what does God feel about that?

## Scriptures Opened

**[Deuteronomy 5:1](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v1)**  *[[1:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=92s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v1)</sup> And Moses called all Israel, and said unto them, Hear, O Israel, the statutes and judgments which I speak in your ears this day, that ye may learn them, and keep, and do them.

- The statutes and judgments: that's what you got to understand. The Lord want us to adhere to the statutes and judgments.

- That ye may learn them, and keep, and do them.


**[Deuteronomy 4:9-10](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v9)**  *[[1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=118s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v9)</sup> Only take heed to thyself, and keep thy soul diligently, lest thou forget the things which thine eyes have seen, and lest they depart from thy heart all the days of thy life: but teach them thy sons, and thy sons’ sons;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v10)</sup> Specially the day that thou stoodest before the Lord thy God in Horeb, when the Lord said unto me, Gather me the people together, and I will make them hear my words, that they may learn to fear me all the days that they shall live upon the earth, and that they may teach their children.

- But teach them thy sons: them is the statutes, the commandments.

- That they may learn to fear me all the days that they shall live upon the earth: so you want to live upon the earth, you must learn how to fear God. You must learn to keep his statutes. Learn his commandments. Learn what pleases your heavenly father.


**[Psalms 119:73](/bible/psalms/119#v73)**  *[[2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=179s)]*

> <sup>[73](/bible/psalms/119#v73)</sup> Thy hands have made me and fashioned me: give me understanding, that I may learn thy commandments.

- Give me understanding, that I may learn thy commandments: that's what we must do. And that's why these classes, these 15 Minutes with the Captains, these In the Classroom classes, they're meant for you to learn God's commandments. Learn what pleases him and learn what makes him angry. Because gossip does not please God.

- The definition of gossip is read: "Noun. Rumors or information about the behavior or personal lives of other people, specifically information about the behavior or lives of famous people: writes a gossip column. A chatty talk. The subject matter of gossip. A person who habitually reveals personal or sensational facts about others: the worst gossip in town."

- That's going on right here in the body. That's going on in the world. In your high schools, in your middle schools. A lot of us grew up hearing our parents, our aunties, our uncles gossip. We grew up around gossip. We grew up around whores of information: those that just want to know everybody's business, to spread rumors, to spread gossip.

- The definition of whore is read: "Three. A mercenary or unscrupulous person; a person who is willing to compromise their integrity or principles for personal gain or in pursuit of something. Four. A person who gets an unusual amount of pleasure from or has an unusual amount of interest in something."

- A person is willing to compromise their integrity. Willing to compromise keeping God's commandments. Willing to compromise friendships. Willing to compromise trust. You're a whore of information. You're a whore for gossip. And this something that you having an unusual amount of interest in is gossip.


**[Proverbs 6:16-19](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)**  *[[5:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=351s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/6#v17)</sup> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/6#v18)</sup> An heart that deviseth wicked imaginations, feet that be swift in running to mischief,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/6#v19)</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

- These six things doth the Lord hate: the Lord hate these things.

- A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren. Gossip sows discord. Here's the proof.


**[Sirach 28:13-14](/bible/sirach/28#v13)**  *[[6:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=391s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/28#v13)</sup> Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/28#v14)</sup> A backbiting tongue hath disquieted many, and driven them from nation to nation: strong cities hath it pulled down, and overthrown the houses of great men.

- Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: you whisper, girl, sis, you know that sister... That's what you're doing. Curse the whisperer.

- For such have destroyed many that were at peace: you at peace. You don't know what the hell going on. You at peace, somebody bring you some crap they heard from three other sisters about you. You sowing discord. Gossip sows discord.

- A backbiting tongue hath disquieted many: a backbiting tongue has caused issues with those that didn't even know they had issues. Didn't even know their name was being spread around like a virus. But you did that.


**[Proverbs 11:13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)**  *[[7:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=461s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)</sup> A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.

- A talebearer: you do nothing but telling everybody's secrets. Lying, discord, gossiping, rumors.

- Revealeth secrets: a talebearer will destroy the trust from someone that entrusted their secrets, entrusted their life, entrusted their personal matters. Those secrets ain't talking about sin. It's talking about personal matters.

- But he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter: you ain't going to be a blabbermouth. Girl, you know sister so-and-so just lost her job the other day. Girl, you know brother so-and-so, her husband, her kid... Shut up. Be quiet. Damn.


**[Proverbs 18:8](/bible/proverbs/18#v8)**  *[[8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=533s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/18#v8)</sup> The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly.

- Gossip is poisonous. Rumors are poisonous. It can destroy someone inside of a body that don't even know that they're being destroyed. Don't even know that everybody looking at them based off of something that ain't even true. Gossip destroys friendships. It destroys trust. And the Lord will deal with those that do that.

- The words of a talebearer are as wounds: you know what they say? Sticks and stones may break my bones, but words will never hurt me. That's a lie. Not according to the Bible. The words are as wounds. And they go down to the soul. You feel it in your bones. It impacts your very life. It impacts the way you feel. It impacts your happiness. It impacts everything.


**[Proverbs 17:4](/bible/proverbs/17#v4)**  *[[10:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=601s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/17#v4)</sup> A wicked doer giveth heed to false lips; and a liar giveth ear to a naughty tongue.

- Cuz somebody said, I don't gossip. But watch this. A wicked doer giveth heed to false lips: God says you wicked, you give heed to false lips.

- And a liar giveth ear to a naughty tongue.

- A video is played: "Unfortunately we live in a space where it's very much encouraged, cuz back in the day gossip used to look like sitting around a table and having lunch and then bringing somebody up and talking about them. Today what it looks like is you create YouTube videos and you make hour-long videos just bashing somebody, whether it's a pastor or someone of influence... and then you're obviously getting paid, especially if you're a YouTuber or any sort of influencer at that level who is making money from this. You're getting paid to gossip about someone. So this is where we are in our society today."

- So that's where we are at right now. We are in the midst of gossip culture. You see hundreds and hundreds of videos of so-called Israelites calling themselves followers of Christ. Their whole scheme, their whole message, their whole doctrine is to bash IUIC. Anything with IUIC name on it, that's where they get the views from. They are whores of information.

- Sisters do the same thing. Brothers do the same thing. You just bash people to other people. You spread rumors. You talk crap. You talk crap about your leadership. You talk crap about your husband. You talk crap about everything, with no solution. My dear, you're a whore of information.


**[Proverbs 18:15](/bible/proverbs/18#v15)**  *[[12:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=742s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/18#v15)</sup> The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge; and the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge.

- The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge: the prudent, the wise gets knowledge.

- And the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge: not seeketh rumors, not seeketh gossip. If you the wise, you going to seek knowledge. You going to hear knowledge. You going to hear the laws. You going to hear prophecies. You want to hear good things. You ain't going to put up with somebody trying to gossip to you. You ain't going to put up with anybody spreading rumors amongst the body.


**[Ephesians 4:29](/bible/ephesians/4#v29)**  *[[13:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=803s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/ephesians/4#v29)</sup> Let no corrupt communication proceed out of your mouth, but that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers.

- Let no corrupt communication: evil, corrupt communication.

- But that which is good to the use of edifying, that it may minister grace unto the hearers: you should be spreading how great your sisters are. You should be spreading how good your husband is. You should be spreading how smart your children are. What are you good at? What are your sisters good at? That is what you should be spreading amongst the body. Not evil rumors.


**[Psalms 101:5](/bible/psalms/101#v5)**  *[[14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=844s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/101#v5)</sup> Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer.

- Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour: with his gossip.

- Him will I cut off: the Lord said he going to kill you. The Lord said he going to put you to death. Don't be no whore of information in the midst of this gossip culture.

- Gossiping is sin. Listening to gossip without checking it is sin. Spreading rumors is sin. God judges both the speaker and hearer. Silent restraint and truth mark righteousness. God judges the gossiper and the receiver of gossip the same.


**[Proverbs 12:18](/bible/proverbs/12#v18)**  *[[14:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qPvmN33DYZw&t=886s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/12#v18)</sup> There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise is health.

- There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: that's going into that gossip. That's going into those inappropriate words. That's going into evil communication. Spreading those rumors, you're like a sword.

- But the tongue of the wise is health. So don't be no whore of information. Speak health to your brothers and sisters. Stay away from gossip culture.
