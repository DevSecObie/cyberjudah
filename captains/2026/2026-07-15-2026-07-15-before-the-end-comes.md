## Introduction

*[[0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=11s)]* Today's class is entitled before the end comes. As soon as stuff starts to pop off throughout the earth, international conflicts and wars, you might run into a Christian who will tell you Jesus can come back any day now. But we know Christ is not coming back any day now, because there's a lot of prophecy that still must be fulfilled: the fourth, fifth and sixth seals, the great tribulation, the time of Jacob's trouble.

## Scriptures Opened

**[1 Thessalonians 5:4](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v4)**  *[[0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=44s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v4)</sup> But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.

- That day is talking about the day of the Lord, the second coming of Christ. It's not going to overtake us as a thief. Christ is coming back as a thief in the night, but we're not in darkness, meaning we're not confused; we don't lack understanding.

- We will know the events that lead up to his return. We don't know the day or the hour, but we know the events that lead up to the day and the hour. Why? Because Christ told us.


**[Matthew 24:6-8](/bible/matthew/24#v6)**  *[[1:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=81s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/24#v6)</sup> And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/24#v7)</sup> For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/24#v8)</sup> All these are the beginning of sorrows.

- Ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: that's what we're hearing about right now. Russia, Ukraine, China, Taiwan, America and Israel versus Iran.

- See that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.

- Famines, and pestilences: sicknesses. And earthquakes, in divers places: all over the world.

- All these are the beginning of sorrows: this is the beginning of the end, not the end of the end. What Christ is describing in Matthew 24 are the events that lead up to his return. The wars, rumours of wars and pestilence all go into the fourth seal.


**[Revelation 6:7-8](/bible/revelation/6#v7)**  *[[2:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=143s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/revelation/6#v7)</sup> And when he had opened the fourth seal, I heard the voice of the fourth beast say, Come and see.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/6#v8)</sup> And I looked, and behold a pale horse: and his name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him. And power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth, to kill with sword, and with hunger, and with death, and with the beasts of the earth.

- Behold a pale horse: that pale horse is going to the nation of Edom, the so-called white man.

- His name that sat on him was Death, and Hell followed with him: wherever the so-called white man shows up, death and hell follow this man. Once he shows up with his political systems, the people of that land get enslaved, and they have everlasting debt to America.

- Power was given unto them over the fourth part of the earth: that's the Americas. To kill with sword: with the wars and rumours of wars we read about in Matthew 24. And with hunger: that's famine. And with death: because the white man is a murderer. He will kill you.

- And with the beasts of the earth: that goes into the pestilence, the sickness, the disease, the viruses. Why is it the beasts of the earth? Because they use DNA from animals to genetically create these diseases going throughout the earth, like COVID, new forms, new strains, the flu. All of this stuff is manufactured in the lab: zoonosis, gain of function research. What we're reading about is the fourth seal.


**[Matthew 24:9](/bible/matthew/24#v9)**  *[[3:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=235s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/24#v9)</sup> Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted, and shall kill you: and ye shall be hated of all nations for my name’s sake.

- Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted: after the wars, rumours of wars and pestilence, the Bible says the Israelites must be delivered up to be afflicted. They must be persecuted. And shall kill you.

- Ye shall be hated of all nations for my name's sake: wherever we are on the earth, we will be hated.


**[Matthew 24:21-22](/bible/matthew/24#v21)**  *[[4:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=266s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/24#v21)</sup> For then shall be great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/24#v22)</sup> And except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved: but for the elect’s sake those days shall be shortened.

- Then shall be great tribulation: that's what Christ tells us we must go through. It will come upon our people.

- Such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be: Christ tells us in red letters there's going to be a time on earth worse than any time in the past. The affliction we went through in Assyria, the oppression of the Egyptians, the way they hunted us down during the time of the Greeks, the way they lynched our brothers in the Roman captivity like they did Christ, the time of slavery: it's going to be worse than that.

- But for the elect's sake: and Israel is the elect. Those days shall be shortened: it's going to be so bad on the earth that the Lord is going to have to intervene to shorten the days. So if we think ICE is bad, ICE is just the tip of the iceberg.

- What Christ is describing from verse nine down to verse 22 is the fifth seal.


**[Revelation 6:9-11](/bible/revelation/6#v9)**  *[[5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=343s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/6#v9)</sup> And when he had opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of them that were slain for the word of God, and for the testimony which they held:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/6#v10)</sup> And they cried with a loud voice, saying, How long, O Lord, holy and true, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood on them that dwell on the earth?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/6#v11)</sup> And white robes were given unto every one of them; and it was said unto them, that they should rest yet for a little season, until their fellowservants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled.

- The souls of them that were slain for the word of God: brothers and sisters that were martyred, killed for the word of God. These days have not come upon the earth as yet.

- How long, O Lord, dost thou not judge and avenge our blood: those that were martyred are asking the Lord, when are you going to avenge us? They did us wrong. When are you going to go back and get our brothers so we can rebuild our kingdom?

- White robes were given unto every one of them: white robes are our glorified angelic bodies. That they should rest yet for a little season: they're going to stay in paradise for a little bit of time.

- Until their fellow servants also and their brethren, that should be killed as they were, should be fulfilled: there's a certain number of men and women that must be killed for the word of God. These days have not happened yet. But Moses told us about those days.


**[Deuteronomy 4:30](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v30)**  *[[7:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=421s)]*

> <sup>[30](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v30)</sup> When thou art in tribulation, and all these things are come upon thee, even in the latter days, if thou turn to the Lord thy God, and shalt be obedient unto his voice;

- When thou art in tribulation: what tribulation? The great tribulation that we just read about during the time of Christ in Matthew 24. And all these things are come upon thee: all these bad things.

- Even in the latter days, if thou turn to the LORD thy God, and shalt be obedient unto his voice: that's what we must do. The Lord told us through the hand of Moses that we will go into great tribulation in the latter days, the days leading up to the return of Christ, and we must return back to our Lord and be obedient to him.

- Christianity don't teach us to be obedient to God. They teach us to say to hell with God and all of his rules; you can do whatever the white man tells you you can do.


**[Jeremiah 30:7](/bible/jeremiah/30#v7)**  *[[8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=481s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/30#v7)</sup> Alas! for that day is great, so that none is like it: it is even the time of Jacob’s trouble; but he shall be saved out of it.

- That day is great, so that none is like it: a time on earth that's worse than any time in the past.

- It is even the time of Jacob's trouble: Jeremiah is telling us about the same thing Moses told us about, the time of great tribulation.

- But he shall be saved out of it: when our oppression is worse than ever before, then is our salvation near and the end close.


**[Daniel 12:1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)**  *[[8:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=519s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)</sup> And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.

- Daniel told us about the same thing. We've been told time and time again that Christ will not return until the Israelites are afflicted worse than they've ever been afflicted before.

- At that time shall Michael stand up: what time? The time of Jacob's trouble, the time of great tribulation. A time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation: from the time the earth was created, there's never been a time so bad as what's going to come on this earth for the Israelites.

- At that time thy people shall be delivered: that is the time frame that leads to the return of Jesus Christ, the black Messiah. Every one that shall be found written in the book: your name has got to be written in the book if you're going to be saved out of that time.


**[Matthew 24:29](/bible/matthew/24#v29)**  *[[9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=577s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/24#v29)</sup> Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken:

- Immediately after the tribulation of those days: meaning we must go through tribulation. The only people who will not go through tribulation are those that die in the Lord before the tribulation comes. If you're here on this earth, you will go through tribulation before Christ returns. This is how we know the end is not yet. It's only the beginning of the end.

- Shall the sun be darkened: darkened from what? From bombs. Bomb clouds going up to where you can't see the sun anymore. And the moon shall not give her light: the exact same thing at night, bombs dropped at night and the clouds going up to block the moon.

- The stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken: all the empires on this earth will be shaken from the stars that fall from heaven. Those stars are bombs dropping from the sky. What is Christ describing? The sixth seal.


**[Revelation 6:12-14](/bible/revelation/6#v12)**  *[[10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dOgX2d7Zncw&t=648s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/6#v12)</sup> And I beheld when he had opened the sixth seal, and, lo, there was a great earthquake; and the sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/6#v13)</sup> And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth, even as a fig tree casteth her untimely figs, when she is shaken of a mighty wind.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/6#v14)</sup> And the heaven departed as a scroll when it is rolled together; and every mountain and island were moved out of their places.

- There was a great earthquake: an earthquake from what? From bombs on the earth.

- The sun became black as sackcloth of hair, and the moon became as blood: the same thing Christ just described, issues with the sun and the moon, from bombs dropping all throughout the earth. And the stars of heaven fell unto the earth: bombs falling on the earth.

- The heaven departed as a scroll: going in the twofold, the heaven is talking about the people in rulership. What's going to happen to the people in rulership? Bombs will drop, those mushroom clouds will come up, and these empires will be destroyed.

- These are descriptions of modern warfare through ancient eyes. This is the sixth seal.

The Lord will remove the oppression that's on the Israelites and redirect the attention to the international conflict.
