## Introduction

*[[0:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=19s)]* Today's topic is the cares of the world. When worldly concerns, riches, desires, your career, different things start to attract our attention, they can deter us or prevent us from fulfilling God's word and fulfilling the will of God. We're all called into this truth to bring forth fruit of some kind or nature, and the cares of this world will hinder you.

## Scriptures Opened
**[Mark 4:18-19](/bible/mark/4#v18)**  *[[0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=54s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/mark/4#v18)</sup> And these are they which are sown among thorns; such as hear the word,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/mark/4#v19)</sup> And the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becometh unfruitful.

- What becomes unfruitful? You. You're the one that becomes unfruitful. Because the cares of this world, the deceitfulness of riches, your pursuit of riches, the lust of other things enter in and choke the word: your pursuit of these different things cannot coincide with the spirit of God.

- The spirit of God must come first. God is not taking a backseat to anything in your life.

- Sometimes we get in the mindset of, I got to do this, I got to take care of this, and we push God to the back burner. Then whatever we're trying to accomplish ends up failing anyway, because except the Lord build the house, it shall not stand.


**[Matthew 6:24](/bible/matthew/6#v24)**  *[[2:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=126s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/6#v24)</sup> No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.

- In this class, the two masters are the Lord and the cares of this world. When the cares of this world start competing with the Lord, that's when you have a major problem.

- The Lord is not saying that you can't take care of your worldly business; you have to take care of your worldly business. But these two cannot be operating on the same level. One has to take the front seat while the other is in the back seat. There has to be priorities in place, a pecking order of things in your life.

- One is going to suffer when you put the other in front of it. You cannot put the Lord in the back seat and have your job in the front seat. When brothers and sisters live like that, over the process of time the spirit of God starts to leave them, and they end up disgruntled about position, disgruntled about correction, disgruntled about minor things in the congregation, which causes them to start watching from home.

- Or they start congregating less and less. We go from seeing them every Sabbath, to two Sabbaths, to one Sabbath out the month, and then we don't see them anymore. We run into them in the grocery store and they don't have fringes on. Why? Because the spirit has left the building.

- Ye cannot serve God and mammon. Mammon is money: that's the deceitfulness of riches.


**[Matthew 6:31-33](/bible/matthew/6#v31)**  *[[4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=276s)]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/6#v31)</sup> Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/6#v32)</sup> (For after all these things do the Gentiles seek:) for your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/6#v33)</sup> But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

- After all these things do the Gentiles seek, because they don't have the Lord to provide for them. They got to go out there and figure it out. We have a God that's going to make sure we're taken care of as long as we put him first. King David said, "I never seen the righteous forsaken or begging bread."

- A lot of us don't believe that. We go after the sight of our own eyes: this bill and this bill are stacking up, not knowing that the Lord can make something happen overnight and everything be taken care of. That's the way you've been living your whole life and you've never gone without, so why panic now? But that's what we do. We get into a panic, a frenzy.

- We can't see that the Lord is watching us and providing for us, so we tend to forget that that's what's happening.

- Seek ye first the kingdom of God and his righteousness, and all these things shall be added unto you: the food, the drink and the clothing of verse 31. As long as we put the Lord first, he'll make sure the necessities of life are provided for us. Does that mean he's going to make you rich? No. It means all the things you need to survive, the bare necessities, will be provided.


**[Luke 10:38-42](/bible/luke/10#v38)**  *[[7:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=422s)]*

> <sup>[38](/bible/luke/10#v38)</sup> Now it came to pass, as they went, that he entered into a certain village: and a certain woman named Martha received him into her house.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/luke/10#v39)</sup> And she had a sister called Mary, which also sat at Jesus’ feet, and heard his word.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/luke/10#v40)</sup> But Martha was cumbered about much serving, and came to him, and said, Lord, dost thou not care that my sister hath left me to serve alone? bid her therefore that she help me.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/luke/10#v41)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto her, Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things:
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/luke/10#v42)</sup> But one thing is needful: and Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her.

- Martha received Christ into her house. Mary is sitting at the feet of Christ learning the Bible, learning the words of God. She's locked in to the word.

- The definition of cumbered is shown: "burdened, weighed down or hindered by too many tasks, objects or worries." This is how a lot of people are: they come into the body and bring all of their cumberings in with them. When you come into this truth you can't be cumbered with many things; you got to come in and let some of that stuff go. You got all these tasks undone in your mind, consumed with the cares of the world to the point where you can't even clear your mind enough to take in the word of God that's being preached to you.

- Martha is concerned that Mary is sitting there learning from Christ: Lord, tell her to get up and come help me with all these things I got to do. Christ said, you are worrying about too many different things.

- Mary has chosen the thing you should be concerned with: studying and learning the word of God. That's the posture we should take when we come to serve the Lord. We have to learn how to push those worldly cares to the side, because they aren't important in the grand scheme of things.

- When you're old and gray and your last day on earth is upon thee, all those meaningless tasks, serving at your job, trying to get this promotion, trying to please the white man, ain't going to matter. What's going to matter is where your soul salvation lies: did you do the things that would make the Lord say, "Well done, my good and faithful servant."

- Which shall not be taken away from her: when you seek the Lord first in his righteousness, none of that will be taken away from you. That job can be taken from you. That car you love so much, that house you put so much investment into, them wicked family members you invest so much time with, they can be taken from you. We have to understand where our faith, our time and our investment should lie.


**[Luke 21:34-36](/bible/luke/21#v34)**  *[[11:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=698s)]*

> <sup>[34](/bible/luke/21#v34)</sup> And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/luke/21#v35)</sup> For as a snare shall it come on all them that dwell on the face of the whole earth.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/luke/21#v36)</sup> Watch ye therefore, and pray always, that ye may be accounted worthy to escape all these things that shall come to pass, and to stand before the Son of man.

- Your hearts be overcharged: that's your mind. Your mind is overcharged, consumed with surfeiting, drunkenness and the cares of this life. That encompasses a lot: you got bills coming up, a graduation party to go to, there's a funeral, I'm behind in my career and I got to catch up. All these different things are overcharging thy mind.

- The day that comes upon you unawares is the day of Christ. It will catch you off guard because you ain't paying attention. It's not on your priority list, not on your vision board. What's on there is all manner of cares of this world. When Christ returns you're going to be caught off guard, overly stimulated and occupied in the anxieties and the cares of this world.

- Everybody on the face of the earth that's following after the cares of this world is going to be caught off guard. Watch and pray: you got to be locked in, pay attention, be focused.

- If your job is getting the best version of you, all your time, the best of your energy, all of your mental fortitude and intellect, how are you a benefit to this truth? How are you going to bring forth fruits in this truth? You have to be counted worthy to escape the destruction that's going to come. We know the prophecies of the scriptures talk about how this world is going to be destroyed, in a perilous place.

- How do we prepare ourselves to be worthy to stand before Christ? That's what we got to be preparing our minds for. No, you're preparing your mind for that promotion, that new car you always dreamed of, the two-story five-bedroom house with the big yard. That's what you're fighting for.


**[1 Corinthians 7:29](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v29)**  *[[15:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=912s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v29)</sup> But this I say, brethren, the time is short: it remaineth, that both they that have wives be as though they had none;

- Is Paul saying to neglect your wife? No. Is Paul saying act like she don't exist? No. What he's saying is that your priority should be the ministry of the Lord.


**[1 Corinthians 7:34-35](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v34)**  *[[15:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=953s)]*

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v34)</sup> There is difference also between a wife and a virgin. The unmarried woman careth for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and in spirit: but she that is married careth for the things of the world, how she may please her husband.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v35)</sup> And this I speak for your own profit; not that I may cast a snare upon you, but for that which is comely, and that ye may attend upon the Lord without distraction.

- When a woman is single in this truth, her time should be dedicated to assisting in the ministry.

- The married woman is concerned with the things of the world, how she shall please her husband; her primary focus is not on the ministry. But Paul just told us, let those that have wives be as though they have none. What if the woman had that same mindset toward the ministry as the husband? A lot of women's complaint is that the man doesn't give me enough time, he's not intentional with me, he's not focused on me. How about both of you focus on the Lord?

- He's not trying to cast a stumbling block before you. We have to learn how to attend upon the Lord without distraction. Sometimes the marriage can become a distraction to this truth: the wife may want this, that and the third, the husband may want this, that and the third, and it becomes a hindrance to the ministry.

## In Closing

*[[17:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=23WCQ5nnv6k&t=1056s)]* We have to learn how to serve the Lord without distraction. And with that, that's been my time. Shalom.
