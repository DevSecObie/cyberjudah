## Introduction

*[[0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=16s)]* This is another 15 minutes with your captain. Today's class is about friendship. We're going to see what the Bible says about friendship. How do you get a friend? How do you prove a friend? Let's see what the Bible says.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Sirach 6:14](/bible/sirach/6#v14)**  *[[0:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=29s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/6#v14)</sup> A faithful friend is a strong defence: and he that hath found such an one hath found a treasure.

- A faithful friend is a strong defense: now, the Bible says if you got a faithful friend, that's going to be a strong defense for you.

- And he that hath found such an one: and if you can find a faithful friend in this truth, hath found a treasure. You found a treasure. You found a gold mine. So the Bible says a faithful friend is a treasure, but you have to find that faithful friend.


**[Matthew 10:22](/bible/matthew/10#v22)**  *[[1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=66s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/10#v22)</sup> And ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.

- And ye shall be hated of all men: the Bible says in these last days, those that are following after Christ are going to be hated of all men. For my name's sake: for Christ's name's sake. For this truth's sake.

- But he that endureth to the end: the Bible says that we have to endure to the end. What's going to help you endure to the end? Having faithful brothers and sisters around you. Shall be saved. Gather my saints together unto me, those that are in heaven and on the earth living.


**[Proverbs 17:17](/bible/proverbs/17#v17)**  *[[1:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=111s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/17#v17)</sup> A friend loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.

- A friend loveth at all times: says a faithful friend is going to love at all times. A friend is not seasonal. Meaning what? When you're going through things, that brother or sister is not around. That's a seasonal brother or sister. The Bible says a friend loveth at all times.

- And a brother is born for adversity: that faithful friend, that gold mine, is there for adversity. When you're going through things, that faithful brother or sister is going to be there to help you, to build you up. When you're going through loss, when you're going through pressures that this world has, cuz remember the Bible says you have to endure to the end. So you're going to need faithful brothers and sisters around you that are not seasonal brothers and sisters. Meaning that they're only there for time.


**[Sirach 7:35](/bible/sirach/7#v35)**  *[[2:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=175s)]*

> <sup>[35](/bible/sirach/7#v35)</sup> Be not slow to visit the sick: for that shall make thee to be beloved.

- Be not slow to visit the sick: the Bible says don't be slow to visit the sick. So when you're going through things, whether it's physical or spiritually you're down, that brother and sister is not going to be slow to visit you while you're going through things, while you're sick.

- For that shall make thee to be beloved: when you're going through hard times, hardship, sickness, spiritual sickness, you want faithful brothers and sisters around you that's going to help build you up. That's what's going to build bonds between you and brothers and sisters. When they there with you and striving with you while you're going through hard times, through bondage.


**[Proverbs 27:6](/bible/proverbs/27#v6)**  *[[3:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=228s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/27#v6)</sup> Faithful are the wounds of a friend; but the kisses of an enemy are deceitful.

- Faithful are the wounds of a friend: because sometimes correction is not comely. But you want brothers and sisters around you that's going to correct you. That's going to show you when you're in the errors of your way. They're not going to sugarcoat things.

- But the kisses of an enemy are deceitful: your enemy is going to fill you with flowers. They're going to say nice things when things are not going nice, when things are not well for you. They're going to fill you with flowery words. They're not going to speak the truth to you.

- There's deceitful brothers and sisters around. So you need to make sure that you're examining what's going on. Take the Bible as an example for yourself.


**[Matthew 16:21-23](/bible/matthew/16#v21)**  *[[4:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=292s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/16#v21)</sup> From that time forth began Jesus to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Jerusalem, and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/16#v22)</sup> Then Peter took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Lord: this shall not be unto thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/16#v23)</sup> But he turned, and said unto Peter, Get thee behind me, Satan: thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the things that be of God, but those that be of men.

- From that time forth began Jesus to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Jerusalem, and suffer many things of the elders: so Christ is telling these disciples that he must go through things, cuz it's what? Prophesied of Christ to suffer.

- And be killed, and be raised again the third day: because it's prophesied for Christ to die for what? For the nation of Israel. To redeem Israel back to the Most High, Christ had to come, live that perfect life, keep the commandments, and ultimately he had to die. So he was pre-warning the disciples what was going to happen through prophecy.

- Then Peter took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Lord: this shall not be unto thee: he didn't want it to happen. He didn't want nothing to happen to Christ. But it's prophesied for something to happen to Christ. Prophecy had to be fulfilled. So you might look at this and think, well, Peter was trying to do a good thing. But Peter was coming with his own doctrine. He was coming with his own feelings and emotions.

- Get thee behind me, Satan: Christ called Peter Satan. Thou art an offence unto me: you're an offense unto me.

- For thou savourest not the things that be of God: he said you're not savoring the things of the Most High. You don't want prophecy to come forth. But those that be of men: you are doing things off of your own back, of your own will, your own free will, your own emotions. You're making an emotional decision.

- Was Christ hateful towards Peter? Did Christ dislike Peter? No. Christ rebuked him sharply.


**[Proverbs 27:6](/bible/proverbs/27#v6)**  *[[7:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=439s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/27#v6)</sup> Faithful are the wounds of a friend; but the kisses of an enemy are deceitful.

- Faithful are the wounds of a friend: so Peter was wounded with those words. Christ called him Satan. But did Christ hate him? No, he didn't hate him. But prophecy had to be fulfilled. Peter was going outside of prophecy, coming up with his own thing.

- You want people around you that's going to tell you how it is, regardless of how you may feel about it. They're going to tell you the truth. But the kisses of an enemy are deceitful.


**[Proverbs 18:24](/bible/proverbs/18#v24)**  *[[8:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=481s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/18#v24)</sup> A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: and there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother.

- So you want people around you that's going to tell you how it is. They're not going to sugarcoat things for you. They're just going to tell you it plain. That's the faithful friends that you want around you.

- A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: first you got to show yourself friendly in order to gain friends. So if you want friends, you got to show yourself friendly first.

- And there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother: in other words, who you are is who your friends are going to be. Do you understand? That's who you're going to attract. So if you got the fruits of the spirit and you're showing forth fruits of the spirit, you're going to get brothers and sisters around you that show forth the same thing. If you have fruits of the flesh, then you're going to have brothers and sisters that have fruits of what? Of the flesh around them.


**[Sirach 13:15](/bible/sirach/13#v15)**  *[[9:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=550s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/13#v15)</sup> Every beast loveth his like, and every man loveth his neighbor.

- Every beast loveth his like: every beast loves its like. And every man his neighbour: every man loves his neighbor.

- So who you are, that's who your friends are going to be. That's who you're going to attract around you.


**[Sirach 6:7](/bible/sirach/6#v7)**  *[[9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=583s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/6#v7)</sup> If thou wouldest get a friend, prove him first and be not hasty to credit him.

- If thou wouldest get a friend: now if you want a friend, prove him first. The Bible says you have to prove that brother or sister first. You have to examine that brother or sister first. Examine their patterns. Examine their movement, how they move in the congregation. Watch how they handle correction. How they go through hardship. Is the Bible a comfort to them when you're trying to bring scriptures out to them to correct them? Prove them first.

- And be not hasty to credit him: the Bible says do not be hasty to credit any brother or sister in this truth in your walk. You have to prove them first. Examine them according to the Bible.

- Prosperity and adversity reveals character. Through prosperity and through adversity, you're going to see who a brother or sister is.


**[Sirach 12:8](/bible/sirach/12#v8)**  *[[10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=652s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/12#v8)</sup> A friend cannot be known in prosperity: and an enemy cannot be hidden in adversity.

- A friend cannot be known in prosperity: a friend not really going to be known in prosperity. Because everything's good. When you're going through good times, everything's good. What's there to examine? Everyone's happy.

- And an enemy cannot be hidden in adversity: in adversity, that's when you're really going to see who's got your back. Who's really down for you. Who's really going to strive and suffer for you.


**[Ruth 1:15-17](/bible/ruth/1#v15)**  *[[11:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=701s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/ruth/1#v15)</sup> And she said, Behold, thy sister in law is gone back unto her people, and unto her gods: return thou after thy sister in law.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ruth/1#v16)</sup> And Ruth said, Intreat me not to leave thee, or to return from following after thee: for whither thou goest, I will go; and where thou lodgest, I will lodge: thy people shall be my people, and thy God my God:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ruth/1#v17)</sup> Where thou diest, will I die, and there will I be buried: the Lord do so to me, and more also, if ought but death part thee and me.

- When things are going wrong, do they stay with you? Do they stay by you? Or do they start to speak against you? Through adversity.

- For whither thou goest, I will go: Ruth said, wherever you go, I will go. And where thou lodgest, I will lodge: wherever you lodge, I'm going to lodge also.

- Where thou diest, will I die: I will die also. Whatever you're going through, I'm going to be there. I'm going to go through it with you.

- The LORD do so to me, and more also, if ought but death part thee and me: she's saying that nothing but death is going to part me from you. Whatever you're going through, I'm going to go through it as well. I'm going to suffer with you also. Those are the people that you call your friend. When you're going through trials and tribulations, have they got your back?

- It's not easy to know who's your friend in prosperity. It's when you're going through things. That's when you're going to see who's your real friend.


**[Sirach 37:1](/bible/sirach/37#v1)**  *[[13:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=808s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/37#v1)</sup> Every friend saith, I am his friend also: but there is a friend, which is only a friend in name.

- But there is a friend, which is only a friend in name: there's a friend that's only a friend in name. Do you have people around you that's only friends in name?

- The Bible says actions are weighed. What are their patterns when you're going through adversity? Are they there for you? These are the things that you're supposed to be looking at when you get yourself a friend or if you're looking for a friend.


**[1 Corinthians 15:33](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v33)**  *[[14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=845s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v33)</sup> Be not deceived: evil communications corrupt good manners.

- Evil communications corrupt good manners: so in your examining of a brother or sister, what is their communication like? What is their speech like? Because the Bible says evil communication will corrupt good manners. They will lead you astray. You shouldn't want someone around you that doesn't show forth the fruits of the spirit. Whose communication is corruptive. That's going to lead you astray. A brother or sister that's a murmurer, a complainer.


**[Philippians 2:14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)**  *[[14:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=887s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)</sup> Do all things without murmurings and disputings:

- You want to watch the communication of the people that you have around you.

- Do all things without murmurings and disputings: the Bible says don't have people around you that's always murmuring, always got a issue. There's always a dispute going on. You don't want brothers and sisters like that around you. Evil communication eventually is going to corrupt you.


**[Sirach 28:13](/bible/sirach/28#v13)**  *[[15:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=921s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/28#v13)</sup> Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.

- Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: curse the whisperer and the double tongue.

- For such have destroyed many that were at peace: you have brothers and sisters that's just trying to get on in this truth. But you got brothers and sisters that are whisperers, murmurers, and complainers. Is that the people that's in your circle? Do leadership talk about people in your circle? Do leadership exhort people that's in your circle? Or are you always seeing the leadership exhort brothers and sisters that are not in the circle that you're in?

- These are the things that you need to examine to know who you have around you and who you are aligning yourself with. Every beast love its own kind.


**[Sirach 6:16-17](/bible/sirach/6#v16)**  *[[16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbj7BWf8pfM&t=970s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/6#v16)</sup> A faithful friend is the medicine of life; and they that fear the Lord shall find him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/6#v17)</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord shall direct his friendship aright: for as he is, so shall his neighbour be also.

- Read verse 17 first. Whoso feareth the Lord shall direct his friendship aright: so if you fear the Most High, you're going to direct your friendship aright. You're going to know who you have around you.

- For as he is, so shall his neighbour be also: that's who your company's going to be also.

- Then verse 16. A faithful friend is the medicine of life; and they that fear the Lord shall find him: if you fear the Most High, you're going to find him. That faithful friend. That strong defense. That gold mine.
