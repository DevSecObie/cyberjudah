## Introduction

*[[0:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=12s)]* Today's topic is called the rapture. This was common back in the Christian church, but the word rapture is not in the Bible. The concept is, but the word is not. The same people that gave us the word rapture are the same people that gave us white Jesus, and they got that wrong. We going to get the proper understanding: who is the rapture for, and when is it going to happen?

## Scriptures Opened

**[1 Thessalonians 4:16](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v16)**  *[[0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=53s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v16)</sup> For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of the archangel, and with the trump of God: and the dead in Christ shall rise first:

- For the Lord himself shall descend from heaven with a shout: the Lord is going to make his advent, his second coming.

- And the dead in Christ shall rise first: all the cemeteries throughout the earth aren't gonna be rattling and shaking and dead bones coming out, looking like Halloween. That's not what it's talking about. Those that died in Christ in the past will return with Christ, according to Jude verse 14.


**[Jude 1:14](/bible/jude/1#v14)**  *[[1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=104s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/jude/1#v14)</sup> And Enoch also, the seventh from Adam, prophesied of these, saying, Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints,

- Behold, the Lord cometh with ten thousands of his saints: when the Lord returns for his second coming, he's coming back with ten thousands of his saints. Now, who are the saints? Are the saints all them people up in the Christian church? No, they're not, not according to the Bible.


**[Psalms 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**  *[[2:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=136s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

- The praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel: so who are the Lord's saints? The children of Israel. Not your grandmama in the Christian church. It don't matter what they called her, your uncle, your grandpa. The saints are the Israelites according to the Bible.

- A people near unto him: the only people that are near unto the Lord are the Israelites.


**[1 Thessalonians 4:17](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v17)**  *[[3:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=181s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v17)</sup> Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord.

- Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds: that's where the concept of the word rapture comes from. Being caught up in the air with Christ and the saints, with Christ and the dead Israelites.

- So the question is, who is the we that will be caught up? Who is the we that Paul was talking to?


**[Acts 17:1](/bible/acts/17#v1)**  *[[3:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=227s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/17#v1)</sup> Now when they had passed through Amphipolis and Apollonia, they came to Thessalonica, where was a synagogue of the Jews:

- They came to Thessalonica, where was a synagogue of the Jews: so who were the Thessalonians? The Thessalonians were the Jews. The we that will be caught up in 1 Thessalonians 4:17 are the Jews, the Israelites from the 12 tribes of Israel.


**[1 Thessalonians 4:17](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v17)**  *[[4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=276s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v17)</sup> Then we which are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds, to meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord.

- Then we which are alive: the Thessalonians, the Jews. To meet the Lord in the air: and so shall we ever be with the Lord.


**[Daniel 12:1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)**  *[[5:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=301s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)</sup> And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.

- Maybe you say Acts 17:1 is not enough, that's the New Testament. Let's see what Daniel said about the exact same thing in the Old Testament.

- At that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: there's going to be a time on the earth in the future where Michael will stand up.

- At that time thy people shall be delivered: the people that will be delivered, that will be caught up, that will be raptured, are Daniel's people. Only Daniel's people are going to be raptured. But you think Daniel was a Christian? There was no religion called Christianity during Daniel's time. Daniel was an Israelite.


**[Daniel 1:6](/bible/daniel/1#v6)**  *[[6:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=376s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/1#v6)</sup> Now among these were of the children of Judah, Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:

- Now among these were of the children of Judah, Daniel: he was an Israelite from the tribe of Judah. So who's going to be delivered in these end times? The Israelites. Not just from the tribe of Judah, but from all 12 tribes.


**[Matthew 24:31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)**  *[[6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=403s)]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)</sup> And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.

- He shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet: we just read about that trumpet in 1 Thessalonians 4:16-17.

- And they shall gather together his elect from the four winds: Christ says in red letters, the angels are only going to gather the elect. You don't get to sign up to become the elect. There's no vote you get to cast, no application you get to fill out. The Bible tells us who the elect is.


**[Isaiah 45:4](/bible/isaiah/45#v4)**  *[[7:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=462s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/45#v4)</sup> For Jacob my servant’s sake, and Israel mine elect, I have even called thee by thy name: I have surnamed thee, though thou hast not known me.

- For Jacob my servant's sake, and Israel mine elect: so who are the angels going to the four corners of the earth to gather? The Israelites.


**[Ezekiel 28:25](/bible/ezekiel/28#v25)**  *[[8:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=490s)]*

> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/28#v25)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; When I shall have gathered the house of Israel from the people among whom they are scattered, and shall be sanctified in them in the sight of the heathen, then shall they dwell in their land that I have given to my servant Jacob.

- When I shall have gathered the house of Israel from the people among whom they are scattered: who is the Lord going to gather? The house of Israel, scattered to the four corners of the earth.

- Then shall they dwell in their land that I have given to my servant Jacob: after we get gathered together by the angels, a period of time will happen and eventually we'll go back to our land.


**[Matthew 24:29-31](/bible/matthew/24#v29)**  *[[9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=551s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/24#v29)</sup> Immediately after the tribulation of those days shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light, and the stars shall fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens shall be shaken:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/24#v30)</sup> And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)</sup> And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.

- The next question about the so-called rapture: when is it going to happen? There's a lot of controversy. Is it going to be pre-tribulation, mid-tribulation, post-tribulation? Out of Christ's own mouth, he gives us a timeline.

- Verse 31 is the so-called rapture, when the Israelites are caught up into the sky with Christ and the dead Israelites. What happened before that? Verse 29.

- Immediately after the tribulation of those days: there should be no confusion, no multiple doctrines about pre, post, mid. Christ out of his own mouth says after the tribulation of those days. Shall the sun be darkened, and the moon shall not give her light: all these things lead up to verse 31, the angels gathering the elect from the four corners of the earth.


**[Psalms 27:5](/bible/psalms/27#v5)**  *[[10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=631s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/27#v5)</sup> For in the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion: in the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me; he shall set me up upon a rock.

- For in the time of trouble: meaning a period of time will go past, a time of trouble. Jacob's trouble will happen first.

- He shall hide me in his pavilion: he will deliver me from where I'm at, and I will be caught up into the chariots with the angels. He shall set me up upon a rock: we'll be in the chariots in the tabernacle with the Lord while the tribulation has just happened amongst our people.


**[Revelation 3:10](/bible/revelation/3#v10)**  *[[11:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=680s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/3#v10)</sup> Because thou hast kept the word of my patience, I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation, which shall come upon all the world, to try them that dwell upon the earth.

- So why do they say pre-tribulation rapture? Because of a misunderstanding of scriptures.

- I also will keep thee from the hour of temptation: the confusion comes when the Lord told the church of Philadelphia, which we read about in verse 7, he will keep them from the hour of temptation. So somebody comes up with a doctrine: oh, pre-tribulation rapture. No. What the Lord is telling the brothers and sisters in the church in Philadelphia specifically is they won't be here during the time of great tribulation. This particular church would have died already before the earth gets too bad.


**[Isaiah 57:1](/bible/isaiah/57#v1)**  *[[12:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=756s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/57#v1)</sup> The righteous perisheth, and no man layeth it to heart: and merciful men are taken away, none considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come.

- The righteous perisheth: the righteous die. And no man layeth it to heart: nobody understands why. And merciful men are taken away: just like the church in Philadelphia, according to Revelation 3:7-10.

- None considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come: that's Revelation 3:10, when it says he will keep them from the hour of trouble.


**[Revelation 11:11-12](/bible/revelation/11#v11)**  *[[13:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=792s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)</sup> And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/11#v12)</sup> And they heard a great voice from heaven saying unto them, Come up hither. And they ascended up to heaven in a cloud; and their enemies beheld them.

- And after three days and an half: after about 350 years. The Spirit of life from God entered into them: the Israelites started to wake up to their true nationality, to understand who they are and what they must do to get right with God.

- And they stood upon their feet: they began to organize themselves. And great fear fell upon them which saw them: as they organized themselves, great fear fell upon our enemies. That fear brings forth a time of great tribulation.

- And they heard a great voice from heaven saying unto them, Come up hither: that's when the rapture happens. After the period of tribulation, when great fear fell upon the nations and they began to oppress us and established the time of Jacob's trouble, the Lord returned and gathered his people from the four corners of the earth.

- And they ascended up to heaven in a cloud; and their enemies beheld them: we went up into the heavens in a cloud, meaning a chariot, and all of our enemies saw that we were delivered. The strangeness of our salvation was beheld.


**[Isaiah 26:19](/bible/isaiah/26#v19)**  *[[14:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=880s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/26#v19)</sup> Thy dead men shall live, together with my dead body shall they arise. Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: for thy dew is as the dew of herbs, and the earth shall cast out the dead.

- Thy dead men shall live: where did Paul get his understanding from? He got it from Isaiah. All the prophets are saying the same thing, whether in the Old Testament or the New Testament. The dead Israelites will return back with Christ.

- Together with my dead body shall they arise: they will come back with the same kind of body that Christ has.

- Awake and sing, ye that dwell in dust: those that have died and been buried in the earth, rejoice, because you're coming back with an angelic body.


**[Ezekiel 34:30](/bible/ezekiel/34#v30)**  *[[15:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=948s)]*

> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/34#v30)</sup> Thus shall they know that I the Lord their God am with them, and that they, even the house of Israel, are my people, saith the Lord God.

- Who is the Lord going to say come up hither to? My people. Who is the Lord's people?

- They, even the house of Israel, are my people: so when Christ says come my people, who is he speaking to? The Israelites.


**[Isaiah 26:20](/bible/isaiah/26#v20)**  *[[16:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHerw53xCO4&t=979s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/26#v20)</sup> Come, my people, enter thou into thy chambers, and shut thy doors about thee: hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast.

- Come, my people: the Israelites. Enter thou into thy chambers: go into the chariots into the sky. And shut thy doors about thee: stay safe in the chariots.

- Hide thyself as it were for a little moment, until the indignation be overpast: just stay up there for a little bit, until the Lord finishes doing a whole lot of killing.

- So who is the rapture for? The rapture is for the Israelites. And when is it going to happen? After the time of Jacob's trouble.
