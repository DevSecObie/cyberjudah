## Introduction

*[[0:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=21s)]* Today's topic is the hot seat. A lot of people don't like to be in a hot seat, but the hot seat is good for you. It's needed for you. It's there to make you better. You got the hot seat so that you can get purged from all of those little things that you can't see in yourself, but leadership can. The hot seat is made to reprove and correct so that you can be better.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Psalms 141:5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)**  *[[0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=58s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)</sup> Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities.

- When the righteous smite you, meaning correct you. To get smited mean to get hit. It's not a good feeling at all times to get hit.

- But when you do get hit, it's a kindness. It's to get you back on track, like you do your kids. When your kids start wandering off and you hit them in the back of their head, it ain't going to feel good to them. They going to be embarrassed, but they going to be on track.

- It shall be an excellent oil: when you have that excellent oil, it's anointed. That correction sanctifies you. When the righteous smite you, it makes you holy.

- Which shall not break my head: it's not going to break you. You're not going to die because of correction.

- A lot of people come into Israel and they think that this is the kingdom: you ain't going to have no problems, everything going to be hunky dory until Christ come back with the chariots. No, you're going to have issues. You're going to have problems with your brothers and your sisters, and you're going to have to correct them.


**[Isaiah 1:4-5](/bible/isaiah/1#v4)**  *[[2:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=173s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/1#v4)</sup> Ah sinful nation, a people laden with iniquity, a seed of evildoers, children that are corrupters: they have forsaken the Lord, they have provoked the Holy One of Israel unto anger, they are gone away backward.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/1#v5)</sup> Why should ye be stricken any more? ye will revolt more and more: the whole head is sick, and the whole heart faint.

- We are a sinful nation. That's why this Bible was written. We wasn't always doing the right thing, so we had to get smitten a lot of times.

- A people laden with iniquity: that's why we're repenting. Not repented, meaning it already happened, it's done. Now we're repenting. That's why Paul said, "I die daily." The righteous got to smite you at all times to keep you on track. That's what leadership is for.

- They have forsaken the Lord: a lot of us forsook the Lord for pork, for Sunday worship, by saying the color of Christ don't matter, going on a campaign trying to make people Democrats and Republicans.

- Why did we provoke the Holy One? Because we wanted to be like the other nations so bad we forsook the creator of heaven and earth.

- Why should we get hit anymore? The scriptures say that a man often reprove hardened his neck. After a while we started to get hardened to all of that correction.

- Revolt more and more: that's what we did. Captivity after captivity, we revolt. Still to this day. Even in the body, people will revolt. The whole head is sick. We are sick. That's why you need the healing of the hot seat, to make you better.


**[2 Timothy 3:16-17](/bible/2-timothy/3#v16)**  *[[5:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=320s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-timothy/3#v16)</sup> All scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-timothy/3#v17)</sup> That the man of God may be perfect, throughly furnished unto all good works.

- When you in the hot seat, those of y'all who have been around leadership, scriptures is going to come out, and some of them might pop you upside your head.

- All of those scriptures from Genesis to Revelation is profitable for doctrine: to teach you to order your life for right.

- Reproof and correction: he said it twice. Do it again and get corrected.

- For instruction in righteousness: that's what the hot seat is for, to instruct you in righteousness.

- A lot of y'all say you can't be perfect, but the scriptures, through reproof, correction, and instruction, will make you perfect.

- To thoroughly furnish something means you put it in fire, you take it out, you check it. If it has any more impurities, you put it back in the fire. So sometimes you got to get in the hot seat and get corrected: "All right, I'mma fix it." You leave, you come back, you got to get back in the hot seat. It is what it is. That's what the hot seat is for: it's for correction.


**[Hebrews 12:6-9](/bible/hebrews/12#v6)**  *[[7:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=432s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/12#v6)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)</sup> If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/12#v8)</sup> But if ye be without chastisement, whereof all are partakers, then are ye bastards, and not sons.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/12#v9)</sup> Furthermore we have had fathers of our flesh which corrected us, and we gave them reverence: shall we not much rather be in subjection unto the Father of spirits, and live?

- If the Lord loves you, you going to find yourself in a hot seat a lot, or under the attention of leadership.

- You may not be in a hot seat all the time because you did something wrong; you may be getting tried. You may get asked certain scriptures, asked to break certain things down, to check your understanding. They may see good things inside of you that they want to see exercised throughout the nation, and they want to be sure that you ready for that next step. So those of y'all that don't go around leadership, y'all tripping.

- If you endure that hot seat, you are a son of God. Look at the seat that Christ had to sit in. He had to get killed, put on a cross, capital punishment, and he was innocent. All because of our mess ups, our disobedience.

- Every father chastises their son. That's why fathers ain't liked; that's why all the kids run to mama for safety, because daddy come with instruction and correction. You ain't going to do what you want to do all of the time when you got a father in your life.

- Everybody is a partaker of chastisement. You think you different? You think you're not going to have problems with people in the body, not going to get corrected, not going to be under the eye of leadership, to make sure you know your scriptures, that you're around constantly, that your life is in order? These things have to happen.

- Our fathers corrected us, and most of the time it wasn't even scriptural. They corrected you based on how they felt, and you still gave them reverence. But now leadership corrects you, and before, they were all wise men, the best men on earth; soon as they correct you, now they just Negroes. It should be the same feeling. They're not doing it out of their own emotions or feelings. They're doing it out of the word of God.

- We got to be in subjection to the Father of spirits, because he created us. He created everything. He created Christ, and Christ created us. And we have to be in subjection to the men that he put in place, and sometimes those men are going to correct you.


**[Proverbs 27:2](/bible/proverbs/27#v2)**  *[[10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=648s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/27#v2)</sup> Let another man praise thee, and not thine own mouth; a stranger, and not thine own lips.

- If you say, "I'm the best reader in Israel," who going to believe that? But if you got five, six different people saying that brother is one of the best readers in Israel, that report is more likely to be believed. Somebody else has to praise you in order for it to be valid. Don't you think somebody else has to correct you in order for it to be valid?

- You're supposed to examine yourself, but there are little nuances about you that you need to fix that you don't readily identify, because it's normal to you. Then you get around leadership and they're like, "Hey, bro, why you doing that? Stop doing that. Move like this." This is why you have to be around. But some people don't come around because of this reason.


**[John 3:19-20](/bible/john/3#v19)**  *[[12:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=731s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/john/3#v19)</sup> And this is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/john/3#v20)</sup> For every one that doeth evil hateth the light, neither cometh to the light, lest his deeds should be reproved.

- It ain't verse 16. Light is Christ. Light also is the leadership that's set over you. Remember Matthew 5 say ye are the light of the world.

- Men love darkness: they love sin. They loved Rome at that time. Some of y'all love Babylon in this time rather than Christ the Black Messiah. A lot of y'all think that Jesus Christ is a Caucasian man, and that's wrong. He's a Black man. He look just like us. He a lot darker than me. He is a very dark man.

- You hate light and you love darkness because your deeds are evil. You think nobody see what I'm doing. If I go around leadership, that sword might flash over toward me, I might get called into the hot seat, because you know that you're not right.

- Everyone that does evil, they hate being around leadership. Just like those people that left Christ, they ain't want to be around Christ because they hate the light. You don't want to be around leadership because they hold the words of Christ.

- Lest his deeds should be reproved: didn't the scripture say all scripture is given by inspiration of God for reproof? You don't want your deeds to be reproved, therefore you're not around, therefore you ain't never in a hot seat. How can you ever get better if you're never in a hot seat? You're never shown what you got to fix, what you got to work on, so that you can become a better prophet, a better husband, a better brother, a better teacher, a better leader.


**[Sirach 6:22,32-36](/bible/sirach/6#v22)**  *[[15:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k_d619OCDa4&t=915s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/6#v22)</sup> For wisdom is according to her name, and she is not manifest unto many.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/sirach/6#v32)</sup> My son, if thou wilt, thou shalt be taught: and if thou wilt apply thy mind, thou shalt be prudent.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/6#v33)</sup> If thou love to hear, thou shalt receive understanding: and if thou bow thine ear, thou shalt be wise,
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/sirach/6#v34)</sup> Stand in the multitude of the elders; and cleave unto him that is wise.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/sirach/6#v35)</sup> Be willing to hear every godly discourse; and let not the parables of understanding escape thee.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/sirach/6#v36)</sup> And if thou seest a man of understanding, get thee betimes unto him, and let thy foot wear the steps of his door.

- The hot seat never is a bad thing. It's not always correction to the point where you're going to walk away moping. Sometimes it's necessary for you to get better and get to that next level. Leadership may see something in you that you don't see in yourself, and there are certain things you got to stop before you get to that next level, because those problems become magnified.

- Wisdom is not manifest to many. Not everybody has wisdom.

- If you apply wisdom, you will be taught. Sometimes being in a hot seat is a lesson to learn. If you're there, you're there to learn something. If you're around leadership, you're there to learn something. That's why scriptures is always coming out.

- If you're not afraid of that hot seat, you're going to receive understanding. If you bow thine ear, if you humble yourself: "Yes, sir, I'mma fix it." Not make an excuse, not come up with a reason why you had to do the dumb stuff that you did. Accept the correction. Move on.

- Why did it say the elders right after it started talking about wisdom? Because that's where wisdom is going to be: among the elders.

- Be willing to hear every godly discourse, whether it's for you to correct yourself or for you to learn something. The scriptures is coming out, and you believe that these men are men of God. Listen.

- Don't let their parables escape you. Understanding doesn't come all the time by a straight up reading of the scriptures. I had to learn that the iron was hot by touching it. I was a baby; I touched that thing. I guarantee you right now as an adult, I know that the iron is hot. Sometimes it take a little heat for you to understand something.

- Let thy foot wear the steps of his door: you better be aggravating for wisdom's sake. Be invited to be inside with the leadership. Be the one that they look to: "You, come here. Sit down here and learn something." That's what the hot seat is for. It's for correction, for reproof, for instruction in righteousness from the elders.
