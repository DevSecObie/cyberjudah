## Introduction

*[[0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=7s)]* Today we are going to talk about the book of life. You may have heard about it growing up in Christianity or in some commentaries, but we're going to go inside the Bible and see who's written in the book of life and who is not. Your name is already written in the book. You would have to do the work to get your name blotted out, and that's why we must endure and overcome.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Luke 10:20](/bible/luke/10#v20)**  *[[0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=37s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/10#v20)</sup> Notwithstanding in this rejoice not, that the spirits are subject unto you; but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.

- Rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven: the Bible says their names were written in heaven. What is their name written in? Let's get Exodus chapter 32.


**[Exodus 32:32](/bible/exodus/32#v32)**  *[[1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=74s)]*

> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/32#v32)</sup> Yet now, if thou wilt forgive their sin—; and if not, blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book which thou hast written.

- If not, blot me, I pray thee, out of thy book which thou hast written: Moses is interceding for the nation of Israel, because they had committed the sin of idolatry against the Lord. Lord, please blot me out of thy book. Which means Moses's name was already written in the book of life, and so were the children of Israel.


**[Exodus 32:13](/bible/exodus/32#v13)**  *[[2:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=124s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/32#v13)</sup> Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, thy servants, to whom thou swarest by thine own self, and saidst unto them, I will multiply your seed as the stars of heaven, and all this land that I have spoken of will I give unto your seed, and they shall inherit it for ever.

- Remember Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, thy servants: so Moses is asking the Lord, please remember what you said about them. Remember the promise. And I'm even willing that you blot my name out of the book of life.


**[Exodus 32:33](/bible/exodus/32#v33)**  *[[2:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=156s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/32#v33)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Whosoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book.

- Whosoever hath sinned against me, him will I blot out of my book: whosoever of the children of Israel have sinned. You can only blot out somebody's name if their name is already in it.

- So what is sin? How do you get blotted out? We're seeing that this has been an Israelite conversation thus far.


**[1 John 3:4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)**  *[[3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=195s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)</sup> Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.

- For sin is the transgression of the law: so sin, breaking God's commandments, is what will get your name excommunicado out of the book of life.


**[Psalms 69:28](/bible/psalms/69#v28)**  *[[3:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=215s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/69#v28)</sup> Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.

- Let them be blotted out of the book of the living: evil, wicked Israelites. And not be written with the righteous: let's see who we're talking about.


**[Psalms 69:35](/bible/psalms/69#v35)**  *[[4:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=243s)]*

> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/69#v35)</sup> For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may dwell there, and have it in possession.

- For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: who's the righteous written in the book of life? Remember he said, let them be blotted out of the book of the living and not be written with the righteous. Let's see who the righteous be.


**[Isaiah 60:21](/bible/isaiah/60#v21)**  *[[4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=279s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/60#v21)</sup> Thy people also shall be all righteous: they shall inherit the land for ever, the branch of my planting, the work of my hands, that I may be glorified.

- Thy people also shall be all righteous: they shall inherit the land for ever. So the children of Israel are the righteous that are going to inherit the land forever. Why? Cuz their names are written in the book of life.


**[Isaiah 4:3](/bible/isaiah/4#v3)**  *[[5:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=312s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/4#v3)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that he that is left in Zion, and he that remaineth in Jerusalem, shall be called holy, even every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem:

- He that is left in Zion, and he that remaineth in Jerusalem, shall be called holy: because they were keeping God's commandments.

- Even every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem: written where? In the book of life.


**[Daniel 12:1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)**  *[[5:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=346s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)</sup> And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.

- The great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: Daniel was an Israelite. So when it says thy people, it's not talking about the Babylonians that we were subject to, the Assyrians, the Amalekites. Even in the heavens, Michael only stands for the Israelites.

- At that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book: it don't get no more simpler or plainer than that. I have not heard or read anywhere that all nations or all people are in the book of life.


**[Hebrews 12:23](/bible/hebrews/12#v23)**  *[[7:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=469s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/hebrews/12#v23)</sup> To the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven, and to God the Judge of all, and to the spirits of just men made perfect,

- Let's jump over to the New Testament, cuz they don't contradict.

- To the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven: let's deal with the church first. Is it the Ebenezer Baptist? Is it the megachurch where Joel Osteen hiding money in the walls? Let's see.


**[Acts 7:38](/bible/acts/7#v38)**  *[[8:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=533s)]*

> <sup>[38](/bible/acts/7#v38)</sup> This is he, that was in the church in the wilderness with the angel which spake to him in the mount Sina, and with our fathers: who received the lively oracles to give unto us:

- This is he, that was in the church in the wilderness: the children of Israel was the church in the wilderness. They're the ones whose name was written in heaven. Now it says the church of the firstborn.


**[Exodus 4:22](/bible/exodus/4#v22)**  *[[9:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=555s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/4#v22)</sup> And thou shalt say unto Pharaoh, Thus saith the Lord, Israel is my son, even my firstborn:

- Israel is my son, even my firstborn: so whose names was written in the book of life in heaven? The church, which is the children of Israel, of the firstborn, which is the children of Israel. Which means the New Testament audience has been and always will be the children of Israel.

- It's very simple. It's very plain. But if you have not been taught correctly, it's a deep mystery. That's why we here.


**[Revelation 3:5](/bible/revelation/3#v5)**  *[[10:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=600s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/3#v5)</sup> He that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life, but I will confess his name before my Father, and before his angels.

- He that overcometh, the same shall be clothed in white raiment; and I will not blot out his name out of the book of life: so if you are keeping God's commandments, I have excellent news for you. This is part of the gospel. Your name is already written in the book.

- You would have to do the work to get your name blotted out, erased. That's why we must endure. We must overcome. We're not trying to get our name inscribed. It's already there.

- But I will confess his name before my Father, and before his angels: that's what we want right there.


**[Revelation 13:8](/bible/revelation/13#v8)**  *[[10:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=648s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/13#v8)</sup> And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.

- All that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life: so if you are worshipping white Jesus, your name is not written in the book of life. Your name's written in the book of death. Your name is blotted out and eliminated if you leave the truth and go back to white Jesus, if you take your hand off the plow.


**[Revelation 17:8](/bible/revelation/17#v8)**  *[[11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=690s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/17#v8)</sup> The beast that thou sawest was, and is not; and shall ascend out of the bottomless pit, and go into perdition: and they that dwell on the earth shall wonder, whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world, when they behold the beast that was, and is not, and yet is.

- Whose names were not written in the book of life from the foundation of the world: how are their names written in the book of life? From the foundation, meaning the very beginning, the inception of the world.


**[2 Esdras 9:18](/bible/2-esdras/9#v18)**  *[[12:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=722s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-esdras/9#v18)</sup> And now when I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live, no man spake against me.

- When I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live: from the foundation of the world.

- No man spake against me: no man spake against God, because our names were written already in the book of life. We was already sealed. We was already chosen. We are the elect, the one-third. We were already holy, already righteous. We just had to come down here and complete our missions. So brothers and sisters, endure. Your name is written in the book of life already.


**[Revelation 20:12-15](/bible/revelation/20#v12)**  *[[12:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=768s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)</sup> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/20#v13)</sup> And the sea gave up the dead which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/20#v14)</sup> And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/20#v15)</sup> And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

- And the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life. The books of your life, the books of the law and the book of life are all before the Lord, and everybody's being judged out of those things. Whether you did good or whether you did evil, is your name blotted out or is it still there?

- And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire: that's all nations, and those wicked Israelites that do not keep God's laws. And I'mma prove it.


**[Jeremiah 30:11](/bible/jeremiah/30#v11)**  *[[14:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=847s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/30#v11)</sup> For I am with thee, saith the Lord, to save thee: though I make a full end of all nations whither I have scattered thee, yet will I not make a full end of thee: but I will correct thee in measure, and will not leave thee altogether unpunished.

- Though I make a full end of all nations: why is the Lord completely annihilating all nations? Because their names are not written in the book of life.

- Yet will I not make a full end of thee: why? Because we have an opportunity to repent, to come back, and to keep our names written in the Lamb's book of life.

- But I will correct thee in measure, and will not leave thee altogether unpunished: so we going through our punishment now. But as we're repenting, guess what is written in the best heavenly calligraphy? Your name, your signature.


**[Revelation 21:8](/bible/revelation/21#v8)**  *[[15:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=917s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/21#v8)</sup> But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: which is the second death.

- But the fearful, and unbelieving, and the abominable, and murderers, and whoremongers, and sorcerers, and idolaters, and all liars, shall have their part in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone: so don't think just because you're an Israelite, you good. If you fall in any of these sinful categories, they're taking a big heavenly spiritual eraser and blotting your name out.


**[Revelation 21:12](/bible/revelation/21#v12)**  *[[15:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=956s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/21#v12)</sup> And had a wall great and high, and had twelve gates, and at the gates twelve angels, and names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel:

- Let's talk about the kingdom. Names written thereon, which are the names of the twelve tribes of the children of Israel: that's how you know that from the foundation of the world, the kingdom was here. It was brought back up, but it's going to be brought back down, and on it are the 12 tribes of the children of Israel. And who's going into them gates? The people whose names are written in the book of life.


**[Revelation 21:27](/bible/revelation/21#v27)**  *[[16:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bZikND66wKw&t=996s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/revelation/21#v27)</sup> And there shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth, neither whatsoever worketh abomination, or maketh a lie: but they which are written in the Lamb’s book of life.

- There shall in no wise enter into it any thing that defileth: the other nations, and those Israelites that commit sin. But they which are written in the Lamb's book of life.

- Brothers and sisters, endure to the end. Overcome your sins. Study. Pray. Apply. Stay in the spirit. And keep your name in the book of life.
