## Introduction

Shalom Israel. Most High and Christ bless. It is Captain Yahoshua with another edition of 15 Minutes with the Captains.

**Today's lesson is entitled: I need more confidence.**

This life that we live, it makes us feel like nothing. It devalues us. It takes all of our worth away. It lowers our value. It lowers our self-esteem. But this truth that we are in right now, it gives us our purpose. It gives us our heritage. It gives us confidence.

So if you need more confidence, which we all do, the first thing we need to do is find scriptures that will boost us up. Find scriptures that will excite you.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Deuteronomy 7:6](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v6)**  *[[1:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=67s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v6)</sup> For thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God: the Lord thy God hath chosen thee to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth.

- My favourite scripture that boosts me up, which is my favourite scripture overall.

- For thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God: the Lord thy God hath chosen thee to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth. So out of all the races on earth, out of all the people on earth, God chose my race. God chose me. That makes me feel special right there. And when I feel special, what does that do for my self-esteem? It boosts my self-esteem. It boosts my confidence.

**[Amos 3:2](/bible/amos/3#v2)**  *[[1:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=113s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/3#v2)</sup> You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.

- You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities. Now yes, we have been punished by God, but we have only been punished by God because he loves us.

- And I would much rather be in a position where my God loves me than, say, the white man, who God does not love at all. So although we are getting punished, this guy is running through the earth, running amok. He is bombing little girls' schools and he is not being punished at all. Why? Because God does not love him. So it boosts my confidence to know that I have a God, and my God loves me.

**[Joel 2:27](/bible/joel/2#v27)**  *[[2:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=164s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/joel/2#v27)</sup> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.

- And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else. So God is not dealing with any other race of people. So regardless of what we are going through while we are in this truth, or where we came from before we came into the truth, God chose us. God is only the God of the nation of Israel, and that has to boost your confidence.

**[Isaiah 14:21](/bible/isaiah/14#v21)**  *[[3:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=211s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/14#v21)</sup> Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities.

- But let us say you are at the job and maybe that Edomite is giving you hell on the job. Maybe you might pick a scripture like this to help boost you up and excite you.

- Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities. Now that is some good stuff right there. That is the type of stuff that will make your teeth white. You are going to be all kinds of excited. That is going to boost you up.

**[Matthew 7:28-29](/bible/matthew/7#v28)**  *[[4:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=260s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/7#v28)</sup> And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these sayings, the people were astonished at his doctrine:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/7#v29)</sup> For he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes.

- So what we have got to understand is that confidence is a sign of Christ's spirit being inside of us. When we read the word of God we are going to understand how Christ got down. And when his spirit is in us, we will be acting the exact same way.

- And it came to pass, when Jesus had ended these sayings, the people were astonished at his doctrine. They were astonished at his what? At his doctrine.

- For he taught them as one having authority, and not as the scribes. So Christ taught with authority. He taught with power. He did not teach like the scribes taught. He was not teaching it like the people in the Christian church teach, all the hooping and hollering. He was not teaching soft. Christ was a man's man. He was aggressive. The Bible says he was an austere man. Christ was bold. Christ was full of confidence. So if we claim to be disciples of Christ, if we claim to follow Christ, if we claim to have the spirit of Christ, we have got to roll how Christ rolled.

**[Acts 4:13](/bible/acts/4#v13)**  *[[5:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=345s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/acts/4#v13)</sup> Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men, they marvelled; and they took knowledge of them, that they had been with Jesus.

- Now that is something that we understand about the apostles. The way the apostles moved, everybody understood that they rode with Christ. Let us find out what characteristic the apostles had that said, these men rode with Jesus the Christ.

- Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John. They saw the boldness of Peter. And John. So Peter was not the only one rolling like Christ. Peter and John.

- And perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men, they marvelled; and they took knowledge of them, that they had been with Jesus. So they took knowledge. They understood that these men were rolling with Jesus the Christ. Why? Because they were bold. Because they were full of confidence.

- So we have got to understand and desire that. Christ was bold. Christ taught with authority. Christ spoke with conviction. So I want to speak that exact same way. I cannot have low self-esteem. I cannot have low confidence, because the person whose spirit I claim to have did not have low self-esteem or low confidence. They were bold.

**[Acts 13:46](/bible/acts/13#v46)**  *[[6:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=417s)]*

> <sup>[46](/bible/acts/13#v46)</sup> Then Paul and Barnabas waxed bold, and said, It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you: but seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the Gentiles.

- Then Paul and Barnabas waxed bold. They waxed bold. Why were they waxing bold? Because they had the spirit of Christ inside of them.

- And said, It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you: but seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the Gentiles. So Paul and Barnabas had a clear understanding of the word of God, a clear understanding of the prophecy of Zechariah 12:7, that said that the gospel would come to the southern kingdom, or the Jews, first. But since some of them rejected it, the religious leaders at the time, now the gospel is being brought to the Gentiles, which is the northern kingdom according to the Bible.

- And how did they preach? They preached with boldness. They preached with confidence. So you and I must be the exact same way. We have got to roll with a spirit of confidence.

**[Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)**  *[[9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=549s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

- Now let us say you lack confidence, or you need more confidence. Here are some practical things. And when I say practical, that means you must put these things into practice. You must do them, to help develop and boost your confidence. Confidence is not something that you have naturally. It is something that has to be developed over a pattern of good works. Over a period of time you will start to develop a thought process that exudes confidence.

- **Number one: read your Bible every day.** I am going to say that again. I cannot stress that enough. If you want confidence, the number one thing you must do is read your Bible every day. If Jesus Christ was the word of God that became flesh, and that flesh walked with confidence, and we want that same confidence, but he is not here any more, how do we tap into that spirit? We must tap into the word of God.

- This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth. So it shall not depart out of your mouth. If anything is in your mouth, it has to be in your mind first. The only way the word of God is in your mouth is if it is in your mind. Meaning you are reading it, you are studying it, and then your conversation will reflect the word of God.

- But thou shalt meditate therein day and night. Often, frequently, regularly.

- That thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein. Daily.

- For then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success. So after you meditate in the word, converse about the word, the Bible says then you will make your way prosperous and have good success. The people that we associate with prosperity and success, they all have something in common. And you know what that is? Confidence. The word of God will bring you confidence.

- Now there is a key point that we just heard right here in Joshua 1:8, and it did not use this word, but I am going to outline the word for you. Discipline. Reading your Bible every day is a discipline. Discipline builds confidence. Why? Because discipline is a consistent pattern of success. If you want to build confidence, you have to put yourself continually in a position of being successful. So when you say you are going to read your Bible every day and you do it, that means you were successful every day. I said I am going to read four chapters today. I did it. I was successful. I said I was going to read four chapters tomorrow. I was successful. Continuing to do that day in and day out develops a conditioning in your mind of being successful.

- You want to add these disciplines to your life, starting with the word of God. Even something as small as what time you wake up. If you say you are going to wake up at eight, it is best to wake up at eight. What was that? That was success. But you keep hitting the snooze to eight ten and eight twenty and eight thirty. You know what happens every time you hit the snooze? You produce failure, and you accept failure. So what would be better is for you just to set your alarm at eight thirty and get up at eight thirty, than to set your alarm at eight and keep hitting the snooze. Because every time you hit the snooze, you are conditioning yourself subconsciously to fail. I failed to get up at eight. I failed to get up at eight ten. I failed to get up at eight twenty. And now I am up at eight thirty. Get rid of the failure out of your life. Do what you say you are going to do. That is going to build your confidence over a period of time.

**[Sirach 37:12](/bible/sirach/37#v12)**  *[[12:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=744s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/37#v12)</sup> But be continually with a godly man, whom thou knowest to keep the commandments of the Lord, whose, mind is according to thy mind, and will sorrow with thee, if thou shalt miscarry.

- **Number two: surround yourself with people who believe in you.**

- But be continually with a godly man. The Bible tells us to be continually with godly men.

- Whom thou knowest to keep the commandments of the Lord. And that is how we identify the godly men, or the godly women: they keep the commandments of God.

- Whose mind is according to thy mind. If my mind is to meditate therein day and night, to read my four chapters, to have a spirit of confidence, now I am going to be surrounded by other men that are confident too.

- And will sorrow with thee, if thou shalt miscarry. Now, misery loves company. That is not what the Bible is telling us. So if I am dealing with a godly man that keeps the commandments, which breeds confidence in me and in him, when I fall on hard times, how is he going to sorrow with me? He is going to build me up. My brother will help build me up in my time of sorrow. And those are the type of people that we need to be surrounded by. Brothers that build you up, brothers that believe in you.

**[Proverbs 18:21](/bible/proverbs/18#v21)**  *[[13:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=828s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/18#v21)</sup> Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.

- **Number three: do not speak negative about yourself.**

- Death and life are in the power of the tongue. Now we all heard this scripture growing up. Death and life are in the power of the tongue. What is that talking about? Your speech. Your speech is very, very powerful, and you need to control it.

- And they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof. The Bible tells us you will eat the fruit of your speech. So if you speak negative about yourself, how can you ever develop confidence? Too often people become their own obstacle by saying, I cannot do it, it is too much, it is too heavy. Speak life into yourself instead of accepting failure.

- And instead of accepting failure, develop delayed success. Perhaps you have got a large goal, and maybe it is not even realistic for you to meet that goal this month. Well, do not set the large goal if it is unrealistic for this month. The way you develop your confidence goes back to our earlier points: develop a pattern of success. Set smaller goals. Maybe instead of one big goal, you can set ten smaller goals that lead you towards the success of your overall goal. But you have got to make sure that your goals are realistic, because you may have a hundred things on your list of things to do for today, but it is not realistic for you to get all those things done. And if you get ninety-nine things done, and you do not get that one thing done, that still makes you a failure. So instead of creating scenarios and circumstances that produce failure, you have got to create systems and scenarios that develop success. So instead of the hundred-item list, set the goal for sixty items. And then if you get ninety-nine, now you are a superhero.

**[Sirach 30:25](/bible/sirach/30#v25)**  *[[16:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=975s)]*

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/30#v25)</sup> A cheerful and good heart will have a care of his meat and diet.

- **Number four: diet and exercise.** Diet and exercise will help boost your confidence.

- A cheerful and good heart will have a care of his meat and diet. A cheerful and good heart, or a cheerful and good mind, will have a care of his meat and diet. That person will have a care for what they do with their body. The Bible says someone with a cheerful and good heart is a person with high self-esteem, a high standard for themselves.

- Someone with a high standard for themselves. You know where that ultimately comes from? Deuteronomy 7:6 tells us that God set us above everybody. So that means our standard has to be above everybody's standard, to be consistent with the word of God. So when we have a high standard for ourselves we are not just going to eat anything. We are not just going to drink anything. We are not just going to let bad things happen to our body and not care about it. We are not going to overeat. Why? Because we have a cheerful and good heart. And that is the fruit of good confidence.

- So if you are lacking in confidence, look at your diet, look at your exercise, and see what are some things that you can change to raise your standard. And what will happen is that over a period of time you will look around your life and see where you elevated your standard in many different areas, and now your confidence is higher.

**[Sirach 19:29-30](/bible/sirach/19#v29)**  *[[17:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CFuOYZ2TYeA&t=1070s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/19#v29)</sup> A man may be known by his look, and one that hath understanding by his countenance, when thou meetest him.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/19#v30)</sup> A man’s attire, and excessive laughter, and gait, shew what he is.

- **Number five: dress nice and fix your posture.**

- A man may be known by his look. So the Bible tells us that you will know a man based on what he looks like, based on how he presents himself.

- And one that hath understanding by his countenance, when thou meetest him.

- A man's attire. His clothing. And excessive laughter. The way he behaves. And gait. And the way he walks. Shew what he is. It will show what is deep down inside of your subconscious. It will show how you feel about yourself.

- So let your actions reflect your mind. You have got to think different about yourself, and then your actions have to follow as well. So when you look good you are going to feel good. When you feel good you are going to perform good. That is a recipe for good confidence.
