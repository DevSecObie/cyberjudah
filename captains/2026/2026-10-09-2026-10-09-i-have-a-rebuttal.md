## Introduction

*[[0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=15s)]* I have a rebuttal. You see it all the time in Israel: every day sisters get counseled, brothers get corrected, and everybody has got to come back with a rebuttal. We are going into the scriptures to see how God feels about a person that always has a rebuttal, those Israelites that every time something comes up it is never their fault. One of the strong signs of maturity is the ability to hear correction without immediately reaching for a rebuttal. The Bible does not view this spirit as wisdom: God desires his people to be teachable, humble, and willing to receive instruction, and the first thing those people battle is a prideful spirit.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 13:10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)**  *[[3:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=206s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)</sup> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.

- We have got to always examine ourselves to see: is it me that has that prideful spirit?

- If you are always in some type of contention, the scriptures say only by pride comes contention. Somebody has got some pride.

- If every time you look up you are into it with Sister Sarah, into it with Brother Kenneth, every week into it with somebody, that is pride.

- With the well advised it will be wisdom.


**[Sirach 10:7](/bible/sirach/10#v7)**  *[[4:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=245s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/10#v7)</sup> Pride is hateful before God and man: and by both doth one commit iniquity.

- Pride is the beginning of sin. When you read Proverbs 6, God says he hates a proud look. We have to fix that.

- Pride is hateful before God, and man: nobody likes a proud spirit.

- Even in the business world, if I am a manager and I have an employee I cannot tell nothing, who always has a rebuttal, always wants a raise, always has a problem, I am going to put them in the basement, because they are too hard to deal with.


**[Sirach 10:13](/bible/sirach/10#v13)**  *[[5:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=309s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/10#v13)</sup> For pride is the beginning of sin, and he that hath it shall pour out abomination: and therefore the Lord brought upon them strange calamities, and overthrew them utterly.

- Pride is the beginning of sin, and he that has pride is going to pour out abomination.

- God wants us to humble our spirit. You do not have to rebuttal every time you hear something, every time an order comes out or a policy changes. God does not like that.

- Those rebuttal spirits will not take counsel.


**[Proverbs 12:15](/bible/proverbs/12#v15)**  *[[5:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=354s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/12#v15)</sup> The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise.

- If you have one of those rebuttal spirits you are not going to take counsel, and the Lord says we must take counsel.

- You see what God calls you? A fool. If nobody can tell you nothing, you are like Kanye West: you can't tell me nothing. God calls that spirit a fool.

- If you always think you are right, you are a fool: the way of a fool is right in his own eyes.

- But he that hearkens unto counsel is wise.


**[Proverbs 11:14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)**  *[[6:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=407s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

- The person with a rebuttal spirit often believes they are already right. When you say something to them they are not listening, because they are already thinking of a rebuttal. You cannot even finish your sentence; their mouth is already open.

- Where no counsel is, the people fall. Most of those spirits I have seen over the years don't make it. They are out of here, because they cannot control that spirit of rebuttal, that spirit of pride, that spirit of being difficult to deal with. God is going to cast it away.

- In the multitude of counsellors there is safety. If you keep hearing, bro, you are hard to deal with, you have got pride on you, you have got to examine yourself and ask the Lord to remove that spirit from you. You have got to change.

- A lot of us reject counsel because we feel like we know better.


**[Proverbs 18:13](/bible/proverbs/18#v13)**  *[[8:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=522s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/18#v13)</sup> He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him.

- God commands us to hear before speaking.

- He that answers a matter before he even hears it, it is folly and shame unto him.

- Many people formulate a response before hearing the entire matter. God wants you to hear the entire matter before you start a rebuttal.

- Sometimes you talk to people and they feel like they are smarter than you, they know more than you, they don't have to listen to you. God can't use a spirit like that.


**[Proverbs 15:12](/bible/proverbs/15#v12)**  *[[9:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=584s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/15#v12)</sup> A scorner loveth not one that reproveth him: neither will he go unto the wise.

- If you have that I have a rebuttal spirit, you are a scorner and you don't want nobody to reprove you.

- He won't even go and sit amongst the leaders, because he is a scorner. The same way amongst sisters: you never hear from them, they never call you for counsel, because they have that prideful, rebuttal spirit and don't feel like they need counsel from you.

- A scorner dislikes correction. Instead of accepting reproof, he argues against it.


**[Proverbs 9:8](/bible/proverbs/9#v8)**  *[[11:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=661s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/9#v8)</sup> Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.

- If you rebuke a scorner he is going to hate you. But if you correct a wise man he is going to love you, because he knows what the scriptures say.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 141:5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)</sup> Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities.

    A wise man understands that God says go and get the correction: it shall be an excellent oil which shall not break my head. Correction is going to help you; take it and move on.


**[James 1:19](/bible/james/1#v19)**  *[[12:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=732s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/james/1#v19)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:

- Be swift to hear, slow to speak. Don't be making up a rebuttal in your head before the person can get through counselling you, correcting you about an issue, or giving you some advice.

- A lot of times the person is coming to you sincerely, wanting to help you, but after a while they are going to say, I am not going to try to help this person again; they don't want to hear it.

- The rebuttal spirit reverses God's order: they are quick to speak and slow to listen, and most of the time quick to wrath too.


**[Proverbs 10:19](/bible/proverbs/10#v19)**  *[[13:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=817s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/10#v19)</sup> In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin: but he that refraineth his lips is wise.

- A foolish man cannot stop talking. If you can't control your talking, you are a foolish man according to the Bible.

- He that refraineth his lips is wise. Sometimes wisdom is simply just being quiet. You don't have to always give a rebuttal.

- The most effective people in a workplace most of the time don't talk a whole lot. There is a time to talk, but when it is time to get the work done, it is time to get the work done.

- Not every correction requires an argument.


**[Proverbs 21:24](/bible/proverbs/21#v24)**  *[[14:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=885s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/21#v24)</sup> Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

- The proud refuse instruction; they do not like instruction. Proud and haughty scorner is his name.


**[Sirach 3:24](/bible/sirach/3#v24)**  *[[15:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=914s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/3#v24)</sup> For many are deceived by their own vain opinion; and an evil suspicion hath overthrown their judgment.

- A lot of us get deceived by our own vain opinion.

- The Lord will put that man of wisdom or that woman of experience in front of you and you won't even see it, because you are so prideful, so haughty. When the instruction comes out that is coming from the Lord, you don't hear it, because you have got a rebuttal.


**[Proverbs 19:20](/bible/proverbs/19#v20)**  *[[16:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=969s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/19#v20)</sup> Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.

- Hear counsel and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.

- The rebuttal spirit never thinks about the latter end. All they want to do is win the conversation, win the argument. Some of y'all need to be a lawyer, because you love to argue your point. God does not like that.


**[Proverbs 3:7](/bible/proverbs/3#v7)**  *[[16:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=1018s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/3#v7)</sup> Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the Lord, and depart from evil.

- Be not wise in your own eyes. Fear the Lord.

- When you are dealing with people with more experience than you, the Lord has probably put those people in your life to help you in this walk. Don't listen to yourself.


**[John 8:28](/bible/john/8#v28)**  *[[17:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=1049s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/john/8#v28)</sup> Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things.

- Even Christ took instruction: I do nothing of myself, but as my Father hath taught me. That is how we should be moving.


**[Proverbs 29:1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)**  *[[18:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=1089s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)</sup> He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.

- Rebellion is eventually going to lead you to total destruction.

- If you are constantly corrected because you are making the same mistakes, going against counsel, constantly got a rebuttal, you are going to eventually harden your neck.

- You are going to be destroyed, and that without remedy. You are not going to listen to nothing, the Lord is going to stop dealing with you, and you are going to be destroyed.


**[Sirach 32:9](/bible/sirach/32#v9)**  *[[19:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=1150s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/32#v9)</sup> If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.

- Don't be rebuttling. You take the counsel, you sit in the room, you listen to the wise, the men and women before you.

- Use not many words. I have seen, brothers and sisters, when ancient men, people with more experience, give them some advice, they always have a rebuttal. That is not a good spirit.


## In Closing

*[[20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cKTFRrlHSkA&t=1212s)]* Lord's will our people come out of that spirit and listen: listen to the people with more experience that came in before you. A rebuttal spirit thinks, what can I say back? I'm gonna hit him with this. But a humble spirit asks, what can I learn? The servant of God grows when they stop defending every mistake and start receiving instruction in meekness.
