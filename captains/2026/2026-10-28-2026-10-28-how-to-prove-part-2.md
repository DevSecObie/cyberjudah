## Scriptures Opened

**[Sirach 6:7](/bible/sirach/6#v7)**  *[[0:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMhqoBRB6Qc&t=19s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/6#v7)</sup> If thou wouldest get a friend, prove him first, and be not hasty to credit him.

- If thou wouldest get a friend: understand husband, wife, best friend, whatever you want to call it. All these things are simplified under friend, which means companion. And the most important companion you're going to ever have is the one you choose to marry.

- Many of you think that in this truth you prove and you fall into the same snares of the world. You're on the phone discussing sexual things, you want to FaceTime. That is out of order. That is sin. That is not your spouse. Y'all are not betrothed, y'all are not married, y'all are not proving. Y'all are lusting and dating. That is worldly, and you cannot build nothing on that foundation.

- The King James definition of prove is read: "To try to ascertain some unknown quality or truth by an experiment or by a test or standard. Thus, we prove the strength of gunpowder by experiment."

- From the sister's point of view, you need a protector. You need the attributes of a provider, of a priest who can guide you in the scriptures in the household and is going to be steadfast in his walk with Christ. As you're discussing and convening with chaperones, you're looking for these attributes that show he can do this for the long term, and then raise up children and replicate those same behaviors till death comes or Christ comes.

- On the brother's point of view, you're looking for the qualities of a soft-spoken woman, a submissive woman, a humble woman, a nurturer. Because she got to nurture you. You might come home from a 12-hour shift; you need somebody who can understand you, who can speak life and godly affirmations into you. And then she got to raise children. You're not trying to see her shape through her dress. That's not proving. That's lusting.

- "To evince truth by argument." She say she a Titus two sister, a Proverbs 31 sister. When you talk to her, sometimes y'all might argue. You got to figure out if she really about it. You got to ask hard questions. Queen Sheba came to Solomon and wanted to prove him with hard questions; she had to test the validity of what she heard about him.

- "Induction or reasoning." You can't believe what everybody say. You got to reason, and test the validity of what they say.

- "To experience." People might say what they want, but when you prove with them, you want to experience that that's who they truly are. Not just their words. You watching their character, how they move. You talking to the brothers that they know, the sisters that she might know, the local leadership, so you can get a full spectrum of this sister's character, because that is a weighty matter.

- If you 400 lb, you can't just sit in any chair. You got to inspect that chair, put some pressure on that chair: is this German steel, is this bamboo, is this made with straw? That's a commitment, and it may cause you harm if you move too hasty. You got to prove it first.

- Be not hasty to credit him: don't be hasty to credit that brother, don't be hasty to credit that sister.


**[Sirach 19:4](/bible/sirach/19#v4)**  *[[5:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMhqoBRB6Qc&t=327s)]*

**[Proverbs 20:5-6](/bible/proverbs/20#v5)**  *[[6:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMhqoBRB6Qc&t=384s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/20#v5)</sup> Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water; but a man of understanding will draw it out.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/20#v6)</sup> Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?

- Counsel in the heart of man: a man's thoughts, intentions. It's like deep water. It's hard to really get somebody to show you who they really are.

- A man of understanding will draw it out. The counsel is a person's plans, their real motives. People hide those things under the surface. You got to be diligent to really get down to the bottom of what somebody is trying to hide from you. Ask the right questions, talk to people who know them, meet their parents, look at their parents' relationship.

- Be diligent or you going to end up with a dragon, or a ferocious lion, and you'll be miserable. And then you'll start to hate the truth, all because you were hasty and light-minded.

- Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: when you prove them, they're going to say all the right things. You got to trip them up, ask the right questions, really see if this person is giving you their representative or is this the real them.

- It takes time. That's why we recommend proving for 2 years. In those 2 years you're going to see their reputation, their conversation, their works. Are they growing in the truth? Are they getting demoted? Are they even thrown out? You get a nice wide range of events to make a good assessment of this person.

- A faithful man: that's what all sisters want. Brothers want a faithful sister. They hard to find.
