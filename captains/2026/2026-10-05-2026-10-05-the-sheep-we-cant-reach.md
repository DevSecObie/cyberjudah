## Introduction

*[[0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=15s)]* Captain Emmanuel of IUIC Arkansas, and today the topic is the sheep we can't reach. Christ revealed that we are not going to reach every city where our people are by the time he comes back, but the Lord has a plan for the Israelites the prophets are not able to reach.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 10:23](/bible/matthew/10#v23)**  *[[0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=25s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/10#v23)</sup> But when they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another: for verily I say unto you, Ye shall not have gone over the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be come.

- The Lord is saying that we are not going to reach the cities where all of our people are by the time he comes back.

- Some people have the thought from Matthew 24, where it says the gospel shall be preached throughout all the world and then shall the end come. It is not saying we are going to reach every single soul. The 144,000 is going to be sealed.

- But the Lord is telling us right here we are not going to be able to reach every city where our people are at.


**[Genesis 28:14](/bible/genesis/28#v14)**  *[[1:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=77s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/28#v14)</sup> And thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth, and thou shalt spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south: and in thee and in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.

- This is the Lord's blessing upon Jacob, whose name was changed to Israel.

- Thou shalt spread abroad to the west: the Israelites will be in the western hemisphere. And to the east, in the eastern hemisphere; to the north and to the south.

- In thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed: blessed with the opportunity to repent through Christ.


**[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**  *[[2:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=120s)]*

> <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

- How would Jacob's seed be spread to the four corners of the earth? The Lord shall scatter thee among all people, all nations, from the east to the west.

- In those other nations you are going to serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, because they will be imposed upon you by those nations where you were scattered.


**[Tobit 13:5](/bible/tobit/13#v5)**  *[[2:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=159s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/tobit/13#v5)</sup> And he will scourge us for our iniquities, and will have mercy again, and will gather us out of all nations, among whom he hath scattered us.

- The curse in Deuteronomy 28 is a judgment for our sin. Yet God said there is a blessing at the end of that: I will have mercy, and I will gather my people out of all nations among whom he has scattered us.

- We have that promise as God's chosen people: he will gather us out of all nations.


**[2 Esdras 15:17](/bible/2-esdras/15#v17)**  *[[3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=198s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-esdras/15#v17)</sup> A man shall desire to go into a city, and shall not be able.

- Some of us may think that means the prophets have to set foot in every crack and crevice of the earth where the Israelites are.

- The prophecy is that you are going to desire to go into the city and not be able. As the times become more perilous (2 Timothy 3:1), this is going to be magnified on a different level, but there is a level in which it applies right now. There are certain places where we can't go.


<div class="reading">

Reading from an on-screen list of countries closed to Americans  *[[4:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=278s)]*

> Countries Americans are legally banned from visiting. These are the strictest cases. US law itself blocks travel. North Korea.

- So North Korea: if you are an American, your passport will not get you there.

> Examples reported in 2026: Niger has suspended entry visas for US citizens. Mali imposed reciprocal restrictions on Americans. Burkina Faso, reported in some sources, may restrict visas entry. In these places, Americans may be denied visas or turned away at the border.

- These are countries that currently block or severely restrict Americans, meaning if you take your American ass on a plane and you land there, you are going to have a rude awakening.

- Niger, Mali and Burkina Faso are West African countries. We know our people are there. Right now, we can't go.

</div>


**[Romans 9:15](/bible/romans/9#v15)**  *[[5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=358s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/9#v15)</sup> For he saith to Moses, I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion.

- For our brothers and sisters that won't be able to hear the good news from a far country in the form of the prophets preaching it, what does the Lord have in store for them?

- The Lord has mercy and compassion upon whom he chooses.


**[Jeremiah 31:10](/bible/jeremiah/31#v10)**  *[[6:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=387s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/31#v10)</sup> Hear the word of the Lord, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off, and say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.

- This is what needs to be declared to the nations and the isles afar off. Leadership broke ground in Papua New Guinea and the Solomon Islands, Malaita, places like that. You know how many islands are over there? We are not going to be able to hit every single last one.

- But the Lord said declare to those isles afar off: he that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him as a shepherd doth his flock. That is God's promise.

- There is going to be mercy and compassion upon those that the prophets are not able to reach.


**[Isaiah 43:3-6](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)**  *[[7:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=456s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)</sup> For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour: I gave Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/43#v4)</sup> Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/43#v5)</sup> Fear not: for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the east, and gather thee from the west;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/43#v6)</sup> I will say to the north, Give up; and to the south, Keep not back: bring my sons from far, and my daughters from the ends of the earth;

- Notice how God is speaking in these precepts: I will. I will bring thy seed from the east and gather thee from the west.

- All of this goes back to Genesis 28:14. He told Jacob your seed is going to be spread abroad to the east, the west, the north and the south, and they are going to be blessed among all these nations.

- This mercy and compassion is going to come through Christ, and the Lord is going to gather them: bring my sons from far, and my daughters from the ends of the earth.


**[Isaiah 60:4-5](/bible/isaiah/60#v4)**  *[[9:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=557s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/60#v4)</sup> Lift up thine eyes round about, and see: all they gather themselves together, they come to thee: thy sons shall come from far, and thy daughters shall be nursed at thy side.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/60#v5)</sup> Then thou shalt see, and flow together, and thine heart shall fear, and be enlarged; because the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee.

- Thy sons shall come from far: that is what we just read in chapter 43.

- The sons and daughters are going to be nursed.

- The abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, and the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee.


**[Isaiah 60:9](/bible/isaiah/60#v9)**  *[[10:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=607s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/60#v9)</sup> Surely the isles shall wait for me, and the ships of Tarshish first, to bring thy sons from far, their silver and their gold with them, unto the name of the Lord thy God, and to the Holy One of Israel, because he hath glorified thee.

- Surely the isles shall wait for me: we read in Jeremiah 31, declare to the isles afar off, I am going to gather my people.

- Remember verse 5, the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee. It says the ships of Tarshish first, to bring thy sons from far. There are going to be ships in this day that are going to transport these lost Israelites from far.

- Their silver and their gold with them: that is the forces of the Gentiles at the bottom of verse 5.


**[Isaiah 66:18-20](/bible/isaiah/66#v18)**  *[[11:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=664s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/66#v18)</sup> For I know their works and their thoughts: it shall come, that I will gather all nations and tongues; and they shall come, and see my glory.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/66#v19)</sup> And I will set a sign among them, and I will send those that escape of them unto the nations, to Tarshish, Pul, and Lud, that draw the bow, to Tubal, and Javan, to the isles afar off, that have not heard my fame, neither have seen my glory; and they shall declare my glory among the Gentiles.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/66#v20)</sup> And they shall bring all your brethren for an offering unto the Lord out of all nations upon horses, and in chariots, and in litters, and upon mules, and upon swift beasts, to my holy mountain Jerusalem, saith the Lord, as the children of Israel bring an offering in a clean vessel into the house of the Lord.

- I will send those that escape of them unto the nations, to Tarshish: the same thing we read in chapter 60 and verse 9, the ships of Tarshish. Tarshish is modern-day Spain.

- Pul is Libya, and Lud is Lydia; Tubal is Turkey; and Javan is Greece; and to the isles afar off.

- That have not heard my fame: they didn't get to hear the prophets, they didn't get to hear the gospel.

- They shall bring all your brethren for an offering unto the Lord out of all nations. Our lost people that are not going to be able to hear the gospel, the Lord has a plan for them: the nations are going to bring them as offerings to the Lord.


**[Isaiah 49:13](/bible/isaiah/49#v13)**  *[[13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=780s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/49#v13)</sup> Sing, O heavens; and be joyful, O earth; and break forth into singing, O mountains: for the Lord hath comforted his people, and will have mercy upon his afflicted.

- The Lord hath comforted his people and is going to have mercy upon his afflicted.


**[Isaiah 49:19-23](/bible/isaiah/49#v19)**  *[[13:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=806s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/49#v19)</sup> For thy waste and thy desolate places, and the land of thy destruction, shall even now be too narrow by reason of the inhabitants, and they that swallowed thee up shall be far away.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/49#v20)</sup> The children which thou shalt have, after thou hast lost the other, shall say again in thine ears, The place is too strait for me: give place to me that I may dwell.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/49#v21)</sup> Then shalt thou say in thine heart, Who hath begotten me these, seeing I have lost my children, and am desolate, a captive, and removing to and fro? and who hath brought up these? Behold, I was left alone; these, where had they been?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/49#v22)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God, Behold, I will lift up mine hand to the Gentiles, and set up my standard to the people: and they shall bring thy sons in their arms, and thy daughters shall be carried upon their shoulders.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/49#v23)</sup> And kings shall be thy nursing fathers, and their queens thy nursing mothers: they shall bow down to thee with their face toward the earth, and lick up the dust of thy feet; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord: for they shall not be ashamed that wait for me.

- Right now Israel is the land of our destruction, trodden down by the Gentiles. But when the times of refreshing come from Christ, it shall even now be too narrow by reason of the inhabitants.

- They that swallowed thee up shall be far away: the Gentiles, the other nations that swallowed us up, are going to be far away. It is not talking about it being packed and crowded because we have got all the damn races. They are going to be far away, but the land is going to be too narrow by reason of the inhabitants.

- The children which thou shalt have after thou hast lost the other: after we have lost two-thirds of wicked Jacob.

- Who hath begotten me these? This is what we are going to say. We have been teaching and trying to gather and scrape up all the Israelites we can. Where did these Israelites come from? Where has this great multitude of Israelites been?

- They shall bring thy sons in their arms: that is what we read in chapter 66, they are going to bring our brethren for an offering to the Lord. Kings shall be thy nursing fathers goes back to chapter 60, your daughters are going to be nursed. And bowing down with their face toward the earth goes back to Revelation 3:9.

- They shall not be ashamed that wait for me. We have got millions of Israelites waiting on the Lord's mercy.


## In Closing

*[[16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmoY8T8qb1k&t=968s)]* Don't worry about the sheep that we can't reach. The Lord got them.
