## Introduction

*[[0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lopRSOMRwfU&t=16s)]* Today's topic is Israel's rebellion doesn't stop the promise. A lot of times we hear Christian apologetics and false Christians try to point the blame and say everybody could be saved because Israel was rebellious. Israel was rebellious, but that don't stop the promise that he gave to our fathers. Just like you may have bad kids at home and you still love them, you ain't adopt no other kids. You going to work with them. That's what the Lord did with us.
