## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=14s)]* Today's topic is the power struggle at home: when a wife competes for authority instead of supporting her husband. A lot of homes today are struggling because of that power struggle, that battle for authority. Instead of working together in God's order, many wives compete against their husband for control, because that's what society teaches. But the scripture says otherwise.

## Scriptures Opened

**[1 Corinthians 14:33](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v33)**  *[[1:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=94s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v33)</sup> For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace, as in all churches of the saints.

- God is not the author of confusion. He let it be known in 1 Corinthians 11, the order. He didn't put a man and a woman in the house for a woman to struggle and compete for authority or for domination in the house. That's black people stuff that we learned in America. That stuff is out of order.

- That's not to say that women in other places are not evil too or don't compete for authority. But a lot of it is learned in America: the Willie Lynch letter, the making of a slave, has some stuff in it that shows you how the so-called white man divided the household and allowed the woman to have a mind of her own, and once she thinks she has that mind of her own, now she wants to compete with the man.

- God made Adam first. Eve was created to assist Adam's mission: a helper. That's what she is.

- A lot of times sisters say, are we not valuable? Sisters are valuable. A wife is valuable. A helper is valuable, because a helper is needed. But a helper is not the leader. Plain and simple.


**[1 Corinthians 11:3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)**  *[[3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=225s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)</sup> But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.

- The head of every man is Christ, and the head of the woman is the man. This is God's chain of command, plain and simple. Christ follows the Father, man follows Christ, and a woman follows her husband.

- How hard is that for a black woman, a Latino woman, a Native American woman, the women in the diaspora, to understand? It's not complicated.

- The order doesn't mean the woman is inferior. No, their role is valuable, but they're not the leader in the house.


**[Ephesians 5:22-23](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)**  *[[4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=276s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.

- Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands. Sisters have got to understand: submitting yourself to your own husband is obedience to God's structure.

- As unto the Lord: you won't talk back to the Lord, so don't talk back to your husband. Don't do things to him that you won't do to the Lord. How hard is that to understand?

- The husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: the same thing we just read in Corinthians 11.

- He is the saviour of the body: the husband is the saviour of that wife. But she's got to listen. It's very important. If you're not listening, how the hell are you going to be saved? How the hell are you going to be built up? Or maybe you know better, right? No, you've got to keep quiet and listen. There's no power struggle in no house. Just one driver, not two drivers.


**[Genesis 3:16](/bible/genesis/3#v16)**  *[[6:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=371s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/3#v16)</sup> Unto the woman he said, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception; in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children; and thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee.

- And thy desire shall be to thy husband. This order came after the sin was committed. That's when the Lord gave this order.

- Eve was the starting of the transgression; that's why the scripture says of the woman came the beginning of sin. So after Eve sinned, the Lord gave her the order.

- God is reaffirming that the husband must rule the wife. That's what God is doing. Because after she sinned, after she went into that mess and pulled her husband into it, the Lord had to reaffirm it again, refocus her mind: that man is your head.


**[Proverbs 21:9](/bible/proverbs/21#v9)**  *[[7:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=469s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/21#v9)</sup> It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.

- The Lord don't want constant arguments, refusing instruction, wanting the final decision, undermining her husband's leadership before the children. The Lord don't want that mess at all.

- It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman: because constant arguing, arguing, arguing. Why? A power struggle. Tussling for power. That's all it is. Wanting to lead, wanting to have the last word, wanting to control the man, wanting to have that dominion over the man.

- In a wide house: a contentious wife destroys peace. She makes the house uncomfortable. That's what she does.


**[Proverbs 19:13](/bible/proverbs/19#v13)**  *[[9:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=552s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/19#v13)</sup> A foolish son is the calamity of his father: and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping.

- The contentions of a wife are a continual dropping: every day becomes warfare, every single day. That's why it says continual dropping. It's annoying. It is annoying.

**[Ecclesiastes 10:4](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v4)**  *[[11:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=710s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v4)</sup> If the spirit of the ruler rise up against thee, leave not thy place; for yielding pacifieth great offences.

- If the spirit of the ruler rise up against thee: he's mad. The leader is mad, or maybe your husband, your leader, is mad at you.

- Leave not thy place: don't leave your place. Stand and listen to what he is saying. Learn.

- For yielding pacifieth great offences: by yielding, by submitting yourself unto him, you pacify the great offence that you did, because you're going to learn something when you keep quiet. I hope the brothers and sisters listened to the video that I just played.


**[Colossians 3:18](/bible/colossians/3#v18)**  *[[12:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=767s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/3#v18)</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as it is fit in the Lord.

- Power tussle: the husband said one, the wife said two. Wives can't make this nonsense up.

- Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands. Submission is an act of faith. That means you believe. So when you're not in submission, you don't believe at all. That's what sisters have got to understand. It is an act of faith when you are submissive.


**[Sirach 26:2](/bible/sirach/26#v2)**  *[[13:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=806s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/26#v2)</sup> A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband, and he shall fulfil the years of his life in peace.

- A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband, and he shall fulfil the years of his life in peace. That's a righteous wife. She encourages him. She supports him. She respects him in public and privately.

- A lot of sisters respect in public and disrespect privately. Can't make it up.


**[Titus 2:3-4](/bible/titus/2#v3)**  *[[14:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=864s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/titus/2#v3)</sup> The aged women likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/titus/2#v4)</sup> That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,

- A lot of sisters have got Titus 2 meetings, but they don't be taking nothing from the Titus 2 meeting, and those things are a disgrace. You portray yourself as somebody, but you don't apply the scripture. You dishonour your husband. You fight for power, struggle for authority in the house. You say one, he says one, you say two. That's out of order. That shows that you don't believe.

- The aged women, that they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands: a lot of sisters do not love their husband. Plain and simple. If you're going back and forth with him, that's not an act of faith. That's not an act of reverence. That's an act of disrespect.

- To love their children: a lot of sisters do not love their children either. If a black woman naturally loved her husband and her children, it wouldn't be in this Bible. But it's in here because black women do not naturally love their children. They have got to be taught. That's what the Bible is saying.


**[2 Esdras 2:15](/bible/2-esdras/2#v15)**  *[[16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=968s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-esdras/2#v15)</sup> Mother, embrace thy children, and bring them up with gladness, make their feet as fast as a pillar: for I have chosen thee, saith the Lord.

- About loving their husbands and loving their children. Mother, embrace thy children: this scripture also goes to the individual household, the wife, as well.

- And bring them up with gladness: a lot of sisters don't even like taking care of their kids. It's a struggle with them. Be glad in raising your children.

- For I have chosen thee, saith the Lord. So wives have got to learn to bring their children up in gladness and to embrace their children. Not no ostrich behaviour, not no ostrich characteristics.


**[Titus 2:4](/bible/titus/2#v4)**  *[[17:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=1043s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/titus/2#v4)</sup> That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,

- God expects women to be taught these things, because society teaches the opposite. That's why sisters have got Titus 2 classes. But a lot of sisters do not apply what they are taught.

- The greater battle in a lot of houses is not financial battles. The greater battle in people's houses, that they keep quiet, is the battle that a wife fights against authority. She wants that authority in the house because she wants to challenge God's order, and she allows Satan to gain access to the home through her, just like her foremother Eve. Same exact thing.

- But when both people play their role, when she's listening and the man plays the man's role, there comes peace, structure and blessing within the house.


**[Sirach 25:1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)**  *[[18:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=1129s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/25#v1)</sup> In three things I was beautified, and stood up beautiful both before God and men: the unity of brethren, the love of neighbours, a man and a wife that agree together.

- We have got to understand: the wife was never created to compete with her husband, but to complete that man. That's what she was created for, to complete that man instead of competing with him. A husband must lead in righteousness, and a wife must support him in respect. That's how it's got to be.

- A man and a wife that agree together: when God's order is honoured in the house, the home becomes a place of peace instead of a battlefield. That's what God called us to do: a pillar of rest, and a leader that has dominion, which is the man.


## In Closing

*[[19:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5fpjPosGgaE&t=1197s)]* I pray you glean some from this lesson. Sisters, you're not to power struggle. You're not to want to have dominion over your husband. The Lord didn't make you out of Adam to do such thing. If you respect and reverence, you will learn something from that man. And by doing that, you will be saved. And with that I say shalom.
