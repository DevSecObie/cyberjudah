## Introduction

*[[0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=47s)]* This is Captain Lemuel with 15 minutes with the captains. We going to touch on honoring our elders. For so long amongst blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans, especially black folks, we are not used to honoring our elders. We so used to the drunk uncle, the crazy granddaddy, that when you see men of honor, men of renown, men of fame, wise men, a lot of times you don't know how to act. You don't know how to conduct yourself. These are things that you have to learn, and these are things that you are going to be taught today. The Lord in these last days is re-establishing a nation, the government of Israel in the earth. Within that, he's re-establishing order, righteous acts. We are rehearsing those acts today, because they will be required of us in the kingdom.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Sirach 7:29-31](/bible/sirach/7#v29)**  *[[2:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=127s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/7#v29)</sup> Fear the Lord with all thy soul, and reverence his priests.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/7#v30)</sup> Love him that made thee with all thy strength, and forsake not his ministers.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/7#v31)</sup> Fear the Lord, and honour the priest; and give him his portion, as it is commanded thee; the firstfruits, and the trespass offering, and the gift of the shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things.

- And reverence his priests: because at that time, when the temple was still standing, we gave sacrifices to the priests. So the Lord said, honor those men.

- And forsake not his ministers: don't forsake his ministers that go before the Lord on your behalf.

- So why would you bring that, Cap, cuz there's no priest today, there's no temple, there's no sacrifices? I get that. I full well understand that there's no temple. So who are these priests that are being referenced today? Remember, God's judgments are like a ring. There's nothing new under the sun.


**[Revelation 1:5-6](/bible/revelation/1#v5)**  *[[3:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=192s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/1#v5)</sup> And from Jesus Christ, who is the faithful witness, and the first begotten of the dead, and the prince of the kings of the earth. Unto him that loved us, and washed us from our sins in his own blood,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/1#v6)</sup> And hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father; to him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

- And hath made us kings and priests: or teachers, prophets, sacrificing our lives. Sacrifice of service for the nation of Israel. That's what you have in our bishops. That's what you have in our deacons. They have sacrificed their lives on behalf of the nation of Israel.

- Christ has made them kings and priests, and the Lord said to reverence them, honor them. So what does that look like today?


**[Leviticus 19:32](/bible/leviticus/19#v32)**  *[[4:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=279s)]*

> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/19#v32)</sup> Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the Lord.

- There's certain things that we do that you may have seen before, like in the court system, but you don't know where it came from. You think we just out here honoring white folks, or honoring a judge whenever we stand up. No. Israel is the forerunner of all honor and respect and dignity and reverence.

- Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head: stand up before the hoary head. That's why, if you've seen older courts or older court shows, the judges have those white or gray hair wigs on, and when they come in the courtroom, what does the court do? They rise. Where you think they got that from?

- So what do we do? We rehearse that, and we keep that commandment. Bishop on deck, and then we rise for the bishops. Deacons on deck, and we rise for the deacons. Because that's how we reverence the priests as our God commanded us. Because he made them kings and priests. Because of their sacrifice of service, because of their wisdom, because of their labor in this truth for years.

- When your leadership walks in the room, your elders, you stand at attention. Hey, shalom, Bishop, Most High Christ bless. Hey, shalom, Deacon, Most High Christ bless. And: hey, what up, Bishop? Hey, what up, Deacon? Hey, what it do? Bro, what the hell wrong with you? That's not reverence. That's not honor. That's not respect. These are elders. These are dignitaries. They would not allow you to do that to the president. They would not allow you to do that to the governors. Hey, what up, guv?

- Or some of y'all are weird enough to be in the presence of the bishop and don't say nothing. You won't even salute the bishop. Sometimes, if you're in proximity, a smaller circle, you can come up: hey, shalom, Bishop, Most High Christ bless, and salute. If it's a larger crowd, or it require some distance: hey, shalom, Bishop. We call it Wi-Fi, where you don't physically salute. This is how you honor your elders.

- These are customs in the world that are kept that they got from our records. So if the heathens are requiring these things of their dignitaries, how much more the dignitaries of the earth, the dignitaries of the planet, the dignitaries of the universe?

- Cuz you don't stand up for just anybody. When it says gray hair, does that mean that you stand up for any gray-haired brother that walk through the door? No. The officer 10 may be 60 years old, 50 years old, head full of gray hair, beard full of gray hair. Does that mean you stand at attention for him? No.


**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:8-9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v8)**  *[[9:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=563s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v8)</sup> For honourable age is not that which standeth in length of time, nor that is measured by number of years.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v9)</sup> But wisdom is the gray hair unto men, and an unspotted life is old age.

- For honourable age is not that which standeth in length of time: being reverenced as an elder is not just because you're 60 years old. You 60 years old, so now you an elder? No. Cuz that's what you got with some other camps. He's 60 years old, so he an elder. He got a head and a beard full of gray, so he an elder. No.

- Nor that is measured by number of years: not measured by your age.

- But wisdom is the gray hair unto men, and an unspotted life is old age: as they have gained experience and years in this truth, laboring in this truth, traveling in this truth, they have gained the title of elders. Not just because they've been on the earth for 40, 50, 60 years. Because they have gained wisdom and experience in their time in the truth.

- That's why we rise up for them. Because they are the ones that are tirelessly laboring, traveling from country to country, gaining experience and raising up the 12 tribes of Israel. Going in the countries and territories and islands that have not been touched before with this truth.


**[1 Timothy 5:1-2](/bible/1-timothy/5#v1)**  *[[11:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=673s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/5#v1)</sup> Rebuke not an elder, but intreat him as a father; and the younger men as brethren;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/5#v2)</sup> The elder women as mothers; the younger as sisters, with all purity.

- Let's get some more attributes of the elders. Rebuke not an elder: or disrespect an elder.

- But intreat him as a father: hey, what it do, pops? You don't do that to your earthly father. At least you shouldn't. So why do it in this truth, when you are supposed to be entreating them as fathers? Who are laying down their lives for their children day in and day out. Losing sleep for their children day in and day out. Losing time with their blood families for the family of Israel, day in and day out.

- The elder women as mothers: you honor them as well, for their labor, their sacrifices in this truth. Shalom, Mama, Most High Christ bless. That's how you show reverence, respect for the elder women. Hey, what up, moms? What it do, moms? What up, my dukes? What up, G Mama? No. That's not how you show respect or honor.

- Now here's the thing. Sometimes the bishops or the deacons, as you rising up and you trying to show respect and honor, they say, hey, sit down now, you good. Guess what you do? You don't say, no, the scripture say I got to reverence the priests, the scripture say I got to stand up before the hoary head. What the hell wrong with you? The scripture just said rebuke not an elder. They said, we good, sit down. So what do you do? Sit your behind down. This stuff is simple.


**[1 Timothy 5:17](/bible/1-timothy/5#v17)**  *[[14:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=if3t9f3KWWg&t=847s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-timothy/5#v17)</sup> Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in the word and doctrine.

- So what is Paul doing? Paul is teaching Timothy, and teaching the church, respect and honor for your elders. Rebuilding the spiritual house, the spiritual family.

- Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour: this isn't just the bishops and the deacons. This is the senior men that have been laboring in this truth. So that means you show the captains reverence and honor. Now, don't take it too far. You don't need to give me the captain on deck. No. Hey, shalom, Cap, Most High Christ bless. All praise to the Most High. That's it. You show respect for the men that have been laboring in this truth. But for the bishops and the deacons, it's that next level, because they are the elder elders. They're the fathers.

- That ain't talking about the brother that's been in the truth 5 years. He's known he's Israel for 5 years, now he's an elder. No.

- Especially they who labour in the word and doctrine: that's time. You been in the truth 5 years. You're a 5-year-old. You're a child. You're not an elder. You got the bishops collectively have been laboring in the word and doctrine for 100 years. You just came out your mama's behind 5 years ago, talking about you an elder. Stop it. You're not an elder. The hell is this?
