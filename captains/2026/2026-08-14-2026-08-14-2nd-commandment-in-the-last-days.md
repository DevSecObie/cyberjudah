The definition on the screen:

> Religious syncretism is the blending of religious belief systems into a new system, or the incorporation of other beliefs into an existing religious tradition. This can occur for many reasons, where religious traditions exist in proximity to each other, or when a culture is conquered and the conquerors bring their religious beliefs with them, but do not succeed in eradicating older beliefs and practices. It frequently involves melding church and state power. Many religions have syncretic elements, but adherents often frown upon the application of the label, especially those who belong to revealed religions such as the Abrahamic religions, or any system with an exclusivist approach, seeing syncretism as corrupting the original religion. Non-exclusivist systems of belief, on the other hand, feel more free to incorporate other traditions into their own.

Any system with an exclusivist approach — like the Israelites. They say we have that type of approach because we say that Christ only came for the Israelites. But that is what the Bible says.

Non-exclusivist systems of belief, on the other hand, feel more free to incorporate other traditions into their own. To incorporate other traditions into their own, including now the worship of what? A white image of Christ.
