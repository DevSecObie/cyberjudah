## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 13:4](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)**  *[[0:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6huD-Z1Mrw&t=24s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)</sup> The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.

- The soul of the sluggard desireth: all of us have desires, but the Bible is talking about the sluggard. A sluggard is someone that's slow to do or to achieve any goals that they have set, or have supposed to set, or even responsibilities and tasks. They desire to achieve this and obtain this.

- And hath nothing: but they never achieve. They never obtain. They always come short.

- But the soul of the diligent shall be made fat: those that are diligent, they do achieve. They do obtain.


**[Proverbs 14:23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)**  *[[1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6huD-Z1Mrw&t=87s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)</sup> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

- With that soul of the sluggard that desires, oftentimes those desires are verbalized, and they will have a full-fledged conversation with many people about their desires. Always talking about, oh, I want to do this. Oh, I want to start a food truck. Oh, I want to start a clothing line. Oh, I want to invest in crypto or forex. The hell is that? I want to do this. I want to do that. But they never end up doing it. Hey, how did that go? We talked for hours about that. How did that go? Man...

- In all labour there is profit: whatever you do that's working towards something, there's always going to be a profit to it.

- But the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury: you just talking about it. Penury is another word for poverty, meaning lack. You don't obtain. You don't have that which you talking about. That's all that talk gets you: absolutely nothing. You have to do the work.


**[Sirach 4:29](/bible/sirach/4#v29)**  *[[2:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6huD-Z1Mrw&t=177s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/4#v29)</sup> Be not hasty in thy tongue, and in thy deeds slack and remiss.

- It's easy to sit around and talk about the things that you want, the things that you want to do, the things you want to learn. But if there's no action behind it, it's literally unprofitable. It's pointless.

- Be not hasty in thy tongue: you quick to say, oh yeah, I want to get into that. Oh yeah, I'm going to get me a trade too. Oh yeah, I'm going to go back to school. You better shut your black lips.

- And in thy deeds slack and remiss: but in your deeds, when it's time to enroll, when it's time to save up and invest, when it's time to take that course, no. Damn, I done missed registration, man. Damn, I forgot, man. I missed the damn auction, man.

- All of that is in the realm of chronic procrastination. The definition is read: "Chronic procrastination is a persistent pattern of delaying tasks despite knowing that the delay will likely lead to negative consequences: stress, missed opportunities, damaged confidence, or poorer outcomes. Unlike occasional procrastination, which everyone experiences, chronic procrastination becomes a default behavioral style."

- Everybody deals with procrastination to some extent. Some worse than others. It'll happen. But chronic procrastination is a pattern, meaning that's how you is. These are your tendencies. You know that it's going to lead to poverty, to penury, to nothing, to lack. You know this because you done been through it too many times. That becomes you, who you are.

- Key characteristics are read: "Task avoidance becomes habitual, not occasional. Short-term relief outweighs long-term goals in decision-making. Follow-through is consistently difficult, even when the person genuinely wants to change. The delay is often emotional, not logical. Tasks are postponed because of discomfort, not because they aren't important."

- Why it happens: we always must get to the why. Why is this happening? Why are you like this? Why am I like this? "Common underlying drivers. Chronic procrastination isn't laziness." Well, according to the Bible it is. "It's usually rooted in psychological or emotional processes such as..." That's the thing: it's deeper than you just, you know, Bruno Mars, today I don't feel like doing anything. It's a psychological defect within you.

- "Emotional avoidance: avoiding tasks that trigger anxiety, boredom, self-doubt, or perfectionism. Perfectionism: fear of making mistakes or producing something not good enough." Excuses. Those two are pretty much grouped together. Perfectionism is an element of emotional avoidance. That's just your fancy way of avoiding the doing of something. Oh, I didn't want to mess up.

- "Overwhelm: difficulty breaking tasks into manageable steps." Oh, it's too much. "Executive function challenges: difficulty initiating tasks, organizing, prioritizing, or sustaining attention." I tried. I was trying to... "Immediate gratification bias: the brain favors short-term reward over long-term benefit." I was finna do it, but it was going to take 18 months. Like, damn. I need some right now.


**[Matthew 25:24-28](/bible/matthew/25#v24)**  *[[8:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y6huD-Z1Mrw&t=489s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/25#v24)</sup> Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/25#v25)</sup> And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth: lo, there thou hast that is thine.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/25#v26)</sup> His lord answered and said unto him, Thou wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I have not strawed:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/25#v27)</sup> Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming I should have received mine own with usury.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/25#v28)</sup> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents.

- Emotional avoidance or perfectionism. Let's see what the Lord said to the perfectionist.

- Then he which had received the one talent came: so the Lord gave him a talent for him to do something with it. By the time he returned, it needed to be something done with that talent.

- Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man: so what is he doing? He coming with emotions. I felt like you hard. Like, you all stern. You don't play, Lord.

- And I was afraid: that's how you see it's emotional. I was scared. I was afraid.

- And went and hid thy talent in the earth: I ain't want to mess it up. I ain't want to make a mistake. I ain't want to ruin the talent. So I made sure I hid it so that nothing happened to it. That's perfectionism. But it's really emotional avoidance. I was afraid.

- Thou wicked and slothful servant: he don't give a damn about your emotions. Hell no. The Lord don't give a damn about your perfectionism. The hell, you wicked and you slothful.

- Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers: so if you was afraid of how hard I am, for real for real, you would have at least put my money or my talent somewhere where it would have built interest. You won't just let it sit under a rock.

- Take therefore the talent from him: so the talent was taken away from him, and that could be you, if you keep playing with chronic procrastination.
