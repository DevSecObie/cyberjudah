## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=14s)]* Today's lesson is called Menstruation: Don't Touch Her Yet. This topic is to bring us to awareness of laws that some of our people break because of impatience, and because of them following their own mind and their own thoughts. The law of the woman's menstruation is to teach patience, to teach respect, and to teach honor within marriage. Everything the Lord does is for a reason with us.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**  *[[0:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=44s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

- Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

- Once she is on her period, it teaches you patience. It teaches you to respect that time and to honor the marriage. That is what it does.


**[Leviticus 15:19](/bible/leviticus/15#v19)**  *[[1:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=102s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/15#v19)</sup> And if a woman have an issue, and her issue in her flesh be blood, she shall be put apart seven days: and whosoever toucheth her shall be unclean until the even.

- If her issue in her flesh be blood, she shall be put apart seven days.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 1:5](/bible/genesis/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/1#v5)</sup> And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day.

    How do we count the days? The evening and the morning: the evening begins the day. Whenever the evening is coming, a new day is coming.


**[Leviticus 15:24-28](/bible/leviticus/15#v24)**  *[[3:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=207s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/15#v24)</sup> And if any man lie with her at all, and her flowers be upon him, he shall be unclean seven days; and all the bed whereon he lieth shall be unclean.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/15#v25)</sup> And if a woman have an issue of her blood many days out of the time of her separation, or if it run beyond the time of her separation; all the days of the issue of her uncleanness shall be as the days of her separation: she shall be unclean.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/15#v26)</sup> Every bed whereon she lieth all the days of her issue shall be unto her as the bed of her separation: and whatsoever she sitteth upon shall be unclean, as the uncleanness of her separation.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/15#v27)</sup> And whosoever toucheth those things shall be unclean, and shall wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/15#v28)</sup> But if she be cleansed of her issue, then she shall number to herself seven days, and after that she shall be clean.

- You cannot have sex with that woman that is put apart, that has an issue of her blood. No sexual intercourse or sexual stuff at all. That is what the Bible is saying.

- All this is to show you that we are a special people. God set her apart; God set us apart. He set a specific time frame, seven days, for physical separation during her cycle, a specific time every month, and we have to honor that.

- Her separation is seven days. When the seventh day expires, on her eighth day she is clean. But if she is spotting again, if it runs beyond the time of her separation, she shall be unclean, so you cannot touch her.

- If she is cleansed of her issue she numbers to herself seven days. For example, she is supposed to be clean on the eighth day and she sees a spot: she has got to count seven more days until she sees nothing. That is how it has got to be.


**[Leviticus 18:19](/bible/leviticus/18#v19)**  *[[5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=337s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/18#v19)</sup> Also thou shalt not approach unto a woman to uncover her nakedness, as long as she is put apart for her uncleanness.

- That is why I said, don't touch her yet. Don't approach her as long as she is put apart. No sexual intercourse or whatever.

- Leave her. Don't touch her. Honor that time. Learn patience and honor that marriage.


**[Leviticus 20:18](/bible/leviticus/20#v18)**  *[[6:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=396s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/20#v18)</sup> And if a man shall lie with a woman having her sickness, and shall uncover her nakedness; he hath discovered her fountain, and she hath uncovered the fountain of her blood: and both of them shall be cut off from among their people.

- He shall discover her fountain, and she hath uncovered the fountain of her blood, and both of them shall be cut off from among their people. So you cannot do that.

- It is very important. The Lord lets us know: you have got to wait.

- These are the laws that we have got to learn. This law teaches us patience, it teaches us respect, and it teaches us honor within the marriage.


**[2 Samuel 11:4](/bible/2-samuel/11#v4)**  *[[7:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=436s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-samuel/11#v4)</sup> And David sent messengers, and took her; and she came in unto him, and he lay with her; for she was purified from her uncleanness: and she returned unto her house.

- She was purified from her uncleanness after her cycle. So no intercourse until her uncleanness is finished. Plain and simple.

- We cannot be confused on this. Your wife has got to have a calendar and track all those things. You cannot be impatient: since she don't see nothing, come on now, let's go. No. That is out of order, and that is against God's law.

- As men, we must understand these things.


**[Isaiah 30:22](/bible/isaiah/30#v22)**  *[[9:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=540s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/30#v22)</sup> Ye shall defile also the covering of thy graven images of silver, and the ornament of thy molten images of gold: thou shalt cast them away as a menstruous cloth; thou shalt say unto it, Get thee hence.

- Menstruous cloth: if the woman is on her menstruation, that is a period of her being defiled. You cannot go to her and lie with her.

- The Lord puts us in a bigger picture: you shall cast it away as a menstruous cloth. Same way when she is on her cycle, don't go there, bro. Wait until she is clean.

- The Lord uses menstruation imagery to describe the casting away, the setting apart, because you are not clean. The woman is not clean when she is on her menstruation. You have got to see that imagery the Lord is painting for us.


**[Lamentations 1:17](/bible/lamentations/1#v17)**  *[[10:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=636s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/lamentations/1#v17)</sup> Zion spreadeth forth her hands, and there is none to comfort her: the Lord hath commanded concerning Jacob, that his adversaries should be round about him: Jerusalem is as a menstruous woman among them.

- The Lord described Jerusalem as a menstruous woman, in her disgrace and in her exile. That is because of our sin.

- Menstruation is always used in the scripture as separation, as the time where you are away by yourself because you are not clean yet.

- Why am I bringing all this scripture out? To show you the imagery of how the Lord uses menstruation to separate: we are not clean as a nation. And when the woman is on her menstrual cycle as well, she is set apart. She is not to be dealt with sexually. That is what the law says.


## In Closing

*[[12:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9I8dWuSZNFg&t=729s)]* The law is teaching us patience, waiting on God's timing, not just personal desire. It teaches us honor as well: treating a wife's body with respect during a vulnerable time. It also teaches us communication: husband and wife being honest about the cycle, about when it came, and about when she is actually clean. Because some wives will say, I ain't seen it no more, when the time has not expired yet. So it is a trial as well, a test of our faith, to see if we are going to be honest with each other, honest with the Lord, and have that patience. I pray you glean something from the lesson.
