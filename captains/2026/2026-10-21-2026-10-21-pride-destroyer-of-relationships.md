## Introduction

*[[0:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=19s)]* Today the topic is pride, the destroyer of relationships. What is very important is the unity of the body: the relationship we have with our brothers, with the sisters, with our wives, and the wife with the husband, is very important to the unification of our nation. Anytime we allow pride to come in, it disrupts that unity. Pride turns disagreements into division, because it makes being right more important than the peace and the bond of that relationship. You want to be vindicated, your ego gets in the way, and we have to be mindful that the scripture says blessed be the peacemakers.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 13:10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)**  *[[1:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=74s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)</sup> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.

- Pride does not allow you to take the low road. Pride does not allow you to say, you know what, I was wrong, that is on me, I will take that L.

- Wisdom will tell you that whatever caused the friction between me and my brother, me and my wife, or me and my sister, is not worth the disruption of our bond.

- So the wise person will take the low. He will fall back, she will fall back, and say: I was wrong, that is me. That is what wisdom teaches you.

- At the end of the day it really does not matter what the issue is. What matters is that we get back on the same page.


**[Proverbs 28:25](/bible/proverbs/28#v25)**  *[[2:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=152s)]*

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/28#v25)</sup> He that is of a proud heart stirreth up strife: but he that putteth his trust in the Lord shall be made fat.

- Oftentimes strife comes when brothers or sisters get corrected, and they do not like the way the correction came, or they do not like who it came from.

- Then they end up bucking up against it: getting loud, rejecting it, murmuring. They want to be respected the way they feel they should be respected, and their pride will not allow them to be corrected or to feel disrespected.

- Correction is not disrespect. Ask yourself: do I want the correction, or do I want to be highly respected? Is my correction embarrassing me to the point where my ego cannot take it?

- When you put your trust in the Lord, you will be blessed.


**[Proverbs 17:14](/bible/proverbs/17#v14)**  *[[3:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=237s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/17#v14)</sup> The beginning of strife is as when one letteth out water: therefore leave off contention, before it be meddled with.

- When you have a water leak, it is something small that caused it at the beginning. If you do not address it and fix it, it turns into something beyond your control.

- Think of a dam: the leak is not patched, the leak becomes bigger, and now there is a flood of water coming that cannot be stopped, and you cannot even identify where the original leak came from.

- Ever been into it with somebody so long you forgot how it started? This happens in marriages a lot. You and your wife go a couple of days without talking and you cannot even remember why y'all are not talking.

- You let that little hole come through, and your pride and your frustration start seeping out little by little.

- That leak is just a little contention. You cannot respond with silence, you cannot respond with agreement; you have to have some type of rebuttal, the last word. No matter who it is or what the situation is, you have to be right. Leave off contention.


**[Proverbs 9:7-9](/bible/proverbs/9#v7)**  *[[5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=358s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/9#v7)</sup> He that reproveth a scorner getteth to himself shame: and he that rebuketh a wicked man getteth himself a blot.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/9#v8)</sup> Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/9#v9)</sup> Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be yet wiser: teach a just man, and he will increase in learning.

- Do not reprove a scorner: this is the type of person that turns a correction into disrespect in their mind. You tell them according to the scriptures they should not be doing this, that and the third, and they feel disrespected.

- You can correct in your window, in the class, without using their name, and they are calling you after the class: why you talking about me in the class? I was not even talking about you. But a hit dog will holler, and now they are even more mad. Something in your conscience can make you say, Is it me, Lord? It is you.

- Bring correction to a wise man and he will thank you, even when you gave it to him rough: thank you for bringing that to my attention so I can get myself right with the Lord.

- The scorner that hates you for correcting him does not care about getting right with the Lord. All he cares about is how he is viewed in man's eyes.

- What happens to that spirit when it is not being corrected? The pride grows, the foolishness festers, and they become reprobate concerning the faith, because nobody wants to deal with you anymore. Every correction becomes a Matthew 18 situation. It is not a Matthew 18 situation because you got corrected. Fix yourself. Humble yourself.

- Reprove a scorner and he comes back at you with a rebuttal. Now you are going back and forth with this idiot, nobody knows who the fool is in the room, and you have put a blot on your name because you argued with him.

- Some people the Bible tells you to let go headlong in their folly: after the first and second admonition, reject. How many times do we have to talk to you about how rude you are, about being late? Eventually that is going to be your ruin and your downfall.

- That is the goal of reproof and correction: we want to see you grow in the spirit and multiply in fruits, the unity of the brotherhood, the congregation growing.

- When people who hate correction dwell amongst the congregation, that spirit grows and festers, and you find yourself in 35 councils every week, because somebody cannot take correction, somebody is offended, somebody's ego is damaged.


**[Sirach 21:6](/bible/sirach/21#v6)**  *[[10:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=637s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/21#v6)</sup> He that hateth to be reproved is in the way of sinners: but he that feareth the Lord will repent from his heart.

- He that fears God will hear the correction and repent from his heart.

- Why does it say from the heart, from the mind? Some people get corrected and say, understood, thank you for correcting me, and then go do the same thing over and over again. They never really change; their spirit never changed. They are still that same old negro, Latino or Native American.


**[Matthew 5:23-24](/bible/matthew/5#v23)**  *[[11:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=690s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/5#v23)</sup> Therefore if thou bring thy gift to the altar, and there rememberest that thy brother hath ought against thee;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/5#v24)</sup> Leave there thy gift before the altar, and go thy way; first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift.

- You come before the Lord and remember that your brother has an issue with you. You know you were rude; you know you did something.

- When it was brought to you, you did not correct it, you did not apologize, you did not humble down. This is where the relationship starts to grow sideways, where it starts to be broken and destroyed.

- The priority is reconciling with your brother. Do not go to the Lord until you clear up the issues amongst ourselves. That is what is most important.


**[Philippians 2:3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)**  *[[12:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=776s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)</sup> Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.

- Lowliness of mind means humility.

- We just restructured the congregation and numerous brothers were upset, because they could not esteem the other better than himself. Certain brothers immediately started to look for fault in other men.

- That is how we do: we look at other people and highlight the negative things in their spirit instead of the positive. That is not a Christ-like spirit.

- Look at your brother and your sister and esteem them higher than yourself, because we all have a couple of things wrong with us that we are still working on purging out. Highlight the good; focus on the good.


**[James 4:1-3](/bible/james/4#v1)**  *[[14:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=871s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/james/4#v1)</sup> From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/james/4#v2)</sup> Ye lust, and have not: ye kill, and desire to have, and cannot obtain: ye fight and war, yet ye have not, because ye ask not.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/james/4#v3)</sup> Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts.

- Where comes the drama, the contention, from among us? We have things within us that push us to go after certain things, and it causes problems amongst brothers and amongst sisters.

- That is how we deal in our relationships in the congregation: what advantage can I get from this brother or this sister, how can I fulfill my lust with them? You may want money, you may want status, sometimes it is sex.

- Deal that way and you destroy relationships and you destroy your spirit, because the relationship was never genuine from the start. You had an ulterior motive.

## In Closing

*[[16:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HJmeeAfj_WI&t=968s)]* Whatever motives and lust you have, you have to learn how to mortify those members, as it says in Colossians 3:5. Learn how to deaden those members and come to the congregation with a pure, sincere heart and a pure, sincere spirit. That is my time. I'm Captain Obadiah. Shalom.
