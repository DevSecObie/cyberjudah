## Introduction

*[[0:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P31yRDxT9oA&t=2s)]* Today we're going over don't be a yes man, and in parentheses, bashfulness. Don't be a yes man. Don't be bashful. Because you reverence a man, because you see a man in high esteem, you go along with him even when he's wrong according to the scriptures. Sometimes leaders be wrong, and a real leader wants men around him that will correct him. You can correct people respectfully, but the correction still got to go out.
