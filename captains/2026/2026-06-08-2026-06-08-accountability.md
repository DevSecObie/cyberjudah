## Introduction

*[[0:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=19s)]* We're going to be going over accountability. Every man shall bear his own burden: when you do wrong, you got to deal with that. It's not somebody else's job to clean up your mess or take the blame for whatever you do. When we come to the Lord we have to be sincere. If you hide your sins or make excuses for them, you block your spiritual growth, and you will give an account of yourself to God.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Galatians 6:5](/bible/galatians/6#v5)**  *[[0:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=25s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/galatians/6#v5)</sup> For every man shall bear his own burden.

- For every man shall bear his own burden: when you do wrong, you got to deal with that. It's not somebody else's job to come and clean up your mess, to accept fault for what you've done, to take the blame for whatever you do.


**[Proverbs 28:13](/bible/proverbs/28#v13)**  *[[1:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=66s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/28#v13)</sup> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.

- When we come to the Lord, we have to be sincere. If you're not sincere, you're not going to last.

- He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: you either hide them, or once you get caught, you got excuses for them. Oh, the reason why I did this is because... No. The reason you did it cuz you're evil. You got to repent, brother. You can't blame nobody else. You can't blame your circumstances. Circumstances don't determine your situation. You determine what happens.

- But whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy: if you don't take full accountability, you're never going to forsake them, because you're going to constantly point the finger at other things. Your character will never change because it was never your fault, right sisters? It was him. It was her. It was never you that was wicked.

- When you hide your sins, you block your spiritual growth. You ever wonder why people could be in the truth for years and never grow past a certain point? That brother that's been with us for years, never had rank, never aspired to go out and teach or help around the school. Why are you here, brother?


**[Sirach 32:17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)**  *[[3:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=204s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)</sup> A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.

- These are all scriptures that we know, but at some point we must start applying these things. If you make excuses and cover your sins, you're not going to grow spiritually. You're going to be stunted. Some of our people have a spiritual block.

- A sinful man will not be reproved: a wicked person will never accept accountability, never accept correction. He's always going to have a reason why this happened and why that happened.

- But findeth an excuse according to his will: there's contributing factors and determining factors. You may not have a car; that may contribute to your situation. But the determining factor is you got to go to work anyway, because you got kids to take care of and rent to pay. You going to get on the bus, get an Uber, ride a bike or walk. You let your contributing factors dictate your character and how you move according to the Lord. The Lord don't care about no contributing factors. Did you keep the commandments or not?

- The Lord don't care about your excuse. Did you do it, yes or no? When you go to Esau's court of law, they don't care about the reason. You broke statute such and such. Here's the penalty, here's the fine, here's the jail time.


**[Romans 14:12](/bible/romans/14#v12)**  *[[5:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=358s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/14#v12)</sup> So then every one of us shall give account of himself to God.

- If you don't hold yourself accountable here and purge out your sins now, you going to get to the judgment seat of Christ and be condemned, because you're still holding on to grudges, hatred, wicked family members, a wicked spouse, and making excuses for them.

- So then every one of us shall give account of himself to God: you ain't got to worry about me. I don't have eyes 10,000 times brighter than the sun. I ain't watching you at home or at work. That's the Lord that's watching you. When we get wind of things, we'll judge as we see, but there's so many things we don't see, and a lot of people think they're going to get away with those things. The Lord going to catch up with you. So why not just hold yourself accountable now? Confess and forsake, like Proverbs 28:13.


**[1 Samuel 15:1-3](/bible/1-samuel/15#v1)**  *[[7:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=454s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-samuel/15#v1)</sup> Samuel also said unto Saul, The Lord sent me to anoint thee to be king over his people, over Israel: now therefore hearken thou unto the voice of the words of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-samuel/15#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I remember that which Amalek did to Israel, how he laid wait for him in the way, when he came up from Egypt.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/15#v3)</sup> Now go and smite Amalek, and utterly destroy all that they have, and spare them not; but slay both man and woman, infant and suckling, ox and sheep, camel and ass.

- Let's get an example: King Saul.

- Now go and smite Amalek, and utterly destroy all that they have, and spare them not: these are very specific instructions. Very clear, very to the point. There's no ambiguity, no confusion. Go and kill everything moving among the Amalekites: man, beast, woman, infant, everything.


**[1 Samuel 15:4](/bible/1-samuel/15#v4)**  *[[8:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=530s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-samuel/15#v4)</sup> And Saul gathered the people together, and numbered them in Telaim, two hundred thousand footmen, and ten thousand men of Judah.

- And Saul gathered the people together, and numbered them in Telaim, two hundred thousand footmen, and ten thousand men of Judah.


**[1 Samuel 15:7-11](/bible/1-samuel/15#v7)**  *[[9:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=543s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/15#v7)</sup> And Saul smote the Amalekites from Havilah until thou comest to Shur, that is over against Egypt.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-samuel/15#v8)</sup> And he took Agag the king of the Amalekites alive, and utterly destroyed all the people with the edge of the sword.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/15#v9)</sup> But Saul and the people spared Agag, and the best of the sheep, and of the oxen, and of the fatlings, and the lambs, and all that was good, and would not utterly destroy them: but every thing that was vile and refuse, that they destroyed utterly.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-samuel/15#v10)</sup> Then came the word of the Lord unto Samuel, saying,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-samuel/15#v11)</sup> It repenteth me that I have set up Saul to be king: for he is turned back from following me, and hath not performed my commandments. And it grieved Samuel; and he cried unto the Lord all night.

- And he took Agag the king of the Amalekites alive: was that the order the Lord gave? Absolutely not.

- But Saul and the people spared Agag, and the best of the sheep, and of the oxen: and would not utterly destroy them. They disobeyed the order of the Lord.

- But every thing that was vile and refuse, that they destroyed utterly: the stuff they didn't want, they destroyed. But oh, these are some good oxen, some good sheep. Man, it's going to make some good lamb for Passover. The Lord said destroy it. But you know how Israelites are, hard-headed to the core, stiff-necked and rebellious.

- It repenteth me that I have set up Saul to be king: imagine the Lord put you in a position to be over people, like many brothers in the body are now, and he say, I wish I never put him over the people. There are brothers like that in the body who shouldn't be over people, because you don't know how to follow order. The orders come from the Lord on down, but you got to add your own seasoning, your own interpretation. Just do what the scriptures say.

- For he is turned back from following me, and hath not performed my commandments.


**[1 Samuel 15:15](/bible/1-samuel/15#v15)**  *[[11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=707s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-samuel/15#v15)</sup> And Saul said, They have brought them from the Amalekites: for the people spared the best of the sheep and of the oxen, to sacrifice unto the Lord thy God; and the rest we have utterly destroyed.

- For the people spared the best of the sheep and of the oxen, to sacrifice unto the LORD thy God: that's the excuse right there. Did God say go kill everything moving, but save the best and sacrifice it unto me? That wasn't the order. This is the king over all Israel, the man supposed to be leading us. Can't take accountability.

- The people spared: who's over the people? Saul. So why would you blame the people for your iniquity? You command the people. He shift the blame to the people.

- A lot of times brothers be dealing with that with their wife. You confront your wife about something, she want to point the finger and blame everybody in the world except herself. Brothers in the body go off, you try to check them, they got 45 different excuses on why they did what they did. Bro, we don't care about the 45 excuses. Stop doing that.


**[1 Samuel 15:19-23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v19)**  *[[14:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=852s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-samuel/15#v19)</sup> Wherefore then didst thou not obey the voice of the Lord, but didst fly upon the spoil, and didst evil in the sight of the Lord?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-samuel/15#v20)</sup> And Saul said unto Samuel, Yea, I have obeyed the voice of the Lord, and have gone the way which the Lord sent me, and have brought Agag the king of Amalek, and have utterly destroyed the Amalekites.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-samuel/15#v21)</sup> But the people took of the spoil, sheep and oxen, the chief of the things which should have been utterly destroyed, to sacrifice unto the Lord thy God in Gilgal.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)</sup> And Samuel said, Hath the Lord as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the Lord? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)</sup> For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

- Wherefore then didst thou not obey the voice of the LORD, but didst fly upon the spoil?

- Yea, I have obeyed the voice of the LORD: this dude is delusional. Now he arguing with the prophet sent by God. The scriptures that were written aforetime were written for our learning: we got to learn from the mistakes of our forefathers. We can't repeat those mistakes. But yet brothers still do that. Things go left and they point the finger.

- But the people took of the spoil: he's pointing the finger at the people as if he's not the one in charge. You going to be held accountable for what the men under you do. Brothers want to be leaders, but don't want to take accountability for the things that happen under their leadership. Your men unruly and out of order, that's your fault.

- Hath the LORD as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the LORD? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice: that's been the mindset of Israel since we got the sacrificial law. Just bring the lamb, the oxen, the turtledoves to absolve ourselves of any wrongdoing. But the Lord said he'd rather you obey than give sacrifice.

- For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry: when you disobey the Lord and you stubborn against the Lord, that's idolatry. That's death.

- Because thou hast rejected the word of the LORD, he hath also rejected thee from being king: when we reject the word of the Lord, the Lord is going to reject you. God's rejection ain't like the rejection of man. It comes with pain after death, with eternal hellfire.


**[2 Corinthians 5:10](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v10)**  *[[18:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e1Shwh_rPNA&t=1081s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v10)</sup> For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body, according to that he hath done, whether it be good or bad.

- For we must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ; that every one may receive the things done in his body: everything you did in your body, you're going to be held accountable for, whether it be good or bad.

- So you can take accountability now and repent, and the Lord will forgive those things, or you got to deal with it face to face with the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ in that day. Which one you want? You decide.
