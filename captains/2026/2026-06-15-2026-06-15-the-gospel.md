## Introduction

*[[0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=5s)]* Today we going to talk about the gospel, the glorious gospel of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. Many of our people are confused at what the gospel is. We've been taught lies concerning the gospel through Christianity, and today we going to destroy all them lies and philosophies. The gospel means good news, good tidings: that Christ came to save us from our enemies.

## Scriptures Opened

**[2 Corinthians 11:4](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v4)**  *[[0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=37s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v4)</sup> For if he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or if ye receive another spirit, which ye have not received, or another gospel, which ye have not accepted, ye might well bear with him.

- This is Paul giving the church of Corinth a warning, and this same warning applies to us today. If he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or if ye receive another spirit, which ye have not received: from the apostles and Paul.

- Or another gospel: Paul is warning us about another gospel being preached, which the apostles didn't receive, which Paul didn't receive, which our forefathers didn't receive.

- Which ye have not accepted: that's the keyword I want to talk about right there when it comes to the gospel.


**[Luke 1:68-71](/bible/luke/1#v68)**  *[[1:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=114s)]*

> <sup>[68](/bible/luke/1#v68)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people,
>
> <sup>[69](/bible/luke/1#v69)</sup> And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David;
>
> <sup>[70](/bible/luke/1#v70)</sup> As he spake by the mouth of his holy prophets, which have been since the world began:
>
> <sup>[71](/bible/luke/1#v71)</sup> That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us;

- Christianity has taught us that the gospel came to save all nations, and as long as you believe and accept Jesus Christ, everyone will be saved, and you'll be dancing with Rolls-Royces and friendship bracelets into the kingdom. Contrary to popular belief, that is not the gospel according to Jesus Christ.

- Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people.

- And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David: that horn of salvation was Jesus the Christ. He came from the house of David, out of the lineage and loins of David.

- As he spake by the mouth of his holy prophets: Isaiah, Jeremiah, Daniel.

- That we should be saved from our enemies: the gospel, which is the good news, that's what the word gospel means, good news or good tidings. The gospel is that Christ came to save us from our enemies. We are an impoverished people. We on the bottom of society. That right there should be some good news to our people. And from the hand of all that hate us.


**[Luke 4:18](/bible/luke/4#v18)**  *[[3:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=230s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/4#v18)</sup> The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,

- Luke 1 was Zacharias prophesying in the spirit, speaking in the Holy Ghost, that Christ would come and save us from our enemies. Let's see if Christ said anything different.

- He hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor: he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives. That's saved from your enemies. That's redeemed.

- We as a nation of people are the captives. We are on the bottom of society. Everywhere you see blacks, Hispanics and Native Americans, the Israelites scattered across the earth, we are the poor and the captives, whether we're in America, Africa, India or Australia.


**[Ephesians 1:12-13](/bible/ephesians/1#v12)**  *[[5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=343s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/1#v12)</sup> That we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted in Christ.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ephesians/1#v13)</sup> In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise,

- After that ye heard the word of truth: the commandments of God. The gospel of your salvation: whose salvation? The Israelites, the 12 tribes of Israel scattered abroad. This is the good news. This is the glad tidings.

- According to the Bible, it ain't that all nations can be saved as long as you believe on that white image of the beast, Caesar Borgia. No, it's the salvation of the Israelites, like we read in Luke 1:68.

- That's why I'm reading 2 Corinthians 11 and 4: if they preach another gospel which you have not accepted. What our people have to come to the realization and understand is, with our salvation comes the destruction of our enemies, the destruction of those that have put us in captivity. That's the gospel.


**[Jeremiah 30:16-17](/bible/jeremiah/30#v16)**  *[[7:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=459s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/30#v16)</sup> Therefore all they that devour thee shall be devoured; and all thine adversaries, every one of them, shall go into captivity; and they that spoil thee shall be a spoil, and all that prey upon thee will I give for a prey.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/30#v17)</sup> For I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds, saith the Lord; because they called thee an Outcast, saying, This is Zion, whom no man seeketh after.

- This is what has to happen for us to be saved.

- All they that devour thee shall be devoured; and all thine adversaries, every one of them, shall go into captivity: we as a nation, that's good news to us. You mean to tell me the same people that put me in slavery, stole my heritage, stole my land, stole my nationality, Christ came to redeem me and pull me out of that, and then they going to go into captivity? That's some good news right there to our people.

- For I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds: because they called thee an Outcast. They call us an outcast, you no-good Negroes, every name under the sun.


**[Revelation 13:9-10](/bible/revelation/13#v9)**  *[[9:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=546s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/13#v9)</sup> If any man have an ear, let him hear.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)</sup> He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.

- You might have thought it's changed. You read the same thing in the New Testament.

- He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: those that led us into captivity shall go into captivity. He that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword: the same thing we read in Jeremiah. Those that devoured us shall be devoured.

- Here is the patience and the faith of the saints: this is what we waiting on. This is the gospel of our salvation.


**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)**  *[[9:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=596s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)</sup> So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies.

- Contrary to popular belief, your pastor don't know the true gospel according to the Bible. He's teaching the gospel according to his oppressor.

- So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies: that's what we have to come to the understanding and acknowledgement of.


**[2 Corinthians 11:4](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v4)**  *[[10:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=644s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v4)</sup> For if he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or if ye receive another spirit, which ye have not received, or another gospel, which ye have not accepted, ye might well bear with him.

- Or another gospel, which ye have not accepted: our forefathers understood what the true gospel was, and they accepted the true gospel: that the righteous would be saved, and it would be destruction to our enemies. For Christ to come back and save us and redeem us, our enemies have to be destroyed.


**[Isaiah 43:3-4](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)**  *[[11:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=689s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)</sup> For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour: I gave Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/43#v4)</sup> Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life.

- I gave Egypt for thy ransom: when he delivered the children of Israel out of Egypt during the time of Moses, Egypt had to be destroyed. Ethiopia and Seba for thee: this is an example of what we read in Wisdom of Solomon 18:7.

- Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life. That's some good news right there. We got a God that love us so much he would destroy nations for us. And that same thing's going to happen during this time.


**[Jeremiah 16:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/16#v14)**  *[[13:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=780s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/16#v14)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that it shall no more be said, The Lord liveth, that brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/16#v15)</sup> But, The Lord liveth, that brought up the children of Israel from the land of the north, and from all the lands whither he had driven them: and I will bring them again into their land that I gave unto their fathers.

North America, Babylon the Great. This place is going to be destroyed for our deliverance.


**[Isaiah 52:6-10](/bible/isaiah/52#v6)**  *[[13:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=819s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/52#v6)</sup> Therefore my people shall know my name: therefore they shall know in that day that I am he that doth speak: behold, it is I.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/52#v7)</sup> How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/52#v8)</sup> Thy watchmen shall lift up the voice; with the voice together shall they sing: for they shall see eye to eye, when the Lord shall bring again Zion.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/52#v9)</sup> Break forth into joy, sing together, ye waste places of Jerusalem: for the Lord hath comforted his people, he hath redeemed Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/52#v10)</sup> The Lord hath made bare his holy arm in the eyes of all the nations; and all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God.

- You read the gospel in the Old Testament just like you do the New Testament. It's just worded a little differently.

- They shall know in that day that I am he that doth speak: in what day? When Christ returns.

- How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings: what is the good tidings? The gospel. All these scriptures that we just went through, the destruction of our enemies, the salvation of the righteous, that's good tidings. That's gospel. That publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth.

- When the LORD shall bring again Zion: the same thing we read in Luke 1, Luke 4, Ephesians 1.

- Break forth into joy, sing together, ye waste places of Jerusalem: you supposed to bring forth in joy, knowing that Christ is coming to redeem you and save you out of the poverty that we live in, as long as we keep God's commandments.

- He hath redeemed Jerusalem: how did he redeem Jerusalem? We read it in Luke 1:68. He sent his son to die on the cross for our sins, that we can have a right to the tree of life.

- And all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God: and what is that salvation? That he going to deliver us from our enemies.


**[Romans 10:15](/bible/romans/10#v15)**  *[[15:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=951s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/10#v15)</sup> And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!

- How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace: did we not just read that in Isaiah 52? Isaiah 52 said good tidings. Romans said the gospel of peace. It's talking about the same thing, that glorious gospel, like y'all like to say in the Christian church.

- And bring glad tidings of good things: what is those good things? That we going to be saved, and our enemies are going to be destroyed.


**[Mark 1:14-15](/bible/mark/1#v14)**  *[[16:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgl18iH99aY&t=1005s)]*
