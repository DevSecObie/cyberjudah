## Introduction

*[[0:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PC8DUOYH-Es&t=12s)]* Today's title is three persons, one God? We're going to go into that unholy Trinity doctrine. Nowhere in the Bible can you read about the Trinity, the triune, coexisting, co-eternal doctrine substantiated within the scriptures. Captain Micah shows from the scriptures that Christ is the word, the physical embodiment of the word of God the Father, that the Father and the Son are two separate beings, and that the Holy Ghost is the spirit of Christ through the keeping of the commandments.
