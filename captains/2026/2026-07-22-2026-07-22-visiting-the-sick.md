## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=14s)]* Today's class is called visiting the sick. When we are gathered together as a nation, we cannot forget our brothers and sisters that are battling in their body. Sickness is one of the most vulnerable times in this walk: when you're strong, your mind is clear and you're getting through it, but it's another thing when you're sick with cancer or MS. We see a lot of brothers and sisters pass in their sickness, and along the way, we as men of the Lord, as sisters of the Lord, as leaders, must visit the sick.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Zephaniah 2:1](/bible/zephaniah/2#v1)**  *[[1:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=73s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/zephaniah/2#v1)</sup> Gather yourselves together, yea, gather together, O nation not desired;

- Gather yourselves together: gathering together don't just mean coming on a Sabbath, going to blitzes or going to somebody's house. It also means to gather together even in sickness, even in time of trial, like you read in Sirach 2:1. Gathering together encompasses all of those things.


**[Romans 12:10](/bible/romans/12#v10)**  *[[1:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=118s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/12#v10)</sup> Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another;

- Because of gathering together, what happens in your mind? You end up preferring your brothers. When your family members in the world go to the hospital, you're the first one there with the balloons and the candy. But when it comes to your brothers and sisters in this truth, you have to care for them more, because that's how you seek the Lord: you prefer your brother ten times more.

- Be kindly affectionate one to another: meaning that you love your brother. Truly love him, not trying to get something from him.

- With brotherly love: that means you put aside all the foolishness. You understand that we're in this fight together, going through the same challenges, through sickness, through ups and downs. Like Paul said, I've learned to abound and I've learned to be abased. That's brotherly love: going through it together.

- In honour preferring one another: unlike Christianity or other religions, where it's all about them. Christianity is one of the most, if not the most, selfish religions on the planet. Why? Because it's all about your blessing: I'm going to God for me a blessing. But God never said that. He said be kindly affectionate one to another. That's why Christ said if a brother trespass against you, forgive him seventy times seven.

- All the baby mamas out there busting it wide open on a stripper pole are Christians. Busting windows, scratching up cars, with Christian crosses around their neck: Christians. God says, prefer one another.


**[James 5:14-16](/bible/james/5#v14)**  *[[4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=263s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/james/5#v14)</sup> Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/james/5#v15)</sup> And the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up; and if he have committed sins, they shall be forgiven him.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/james/5#v16)</sup> Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

- When we are preferring one another, we're gathering together. I'm going to be straight: a lot of times in this truth, or just in life, people are prideful in their sicknesses, or they don't want to divulge what's going on with them, and they don't tell the leaders. But the Lord says for us to tell the leaders when we're sick. We cannot hide our condition.

- Is any sick among you? Let him call for the elders of the church: if you've got sickness, tell the elders. Tell your leaders: the bishops, the deacons, the captains. If you've got officers over your schools, you must tell them.

- And let them pray over him: tell the elders so they can pray to the Lord that you have long life, or that the sickness goes away, if it be within God's will. But if you keep it to yourself, no one knows what to pray for.

- The prayer of faith shall save the sick: that leader over your congregation must pray to the Lord, and that prayer of faith, believing on God for you to get better, will save the sick, if it's God's will and you're not in the midst of sin. There's a caveat to that: you have to be keeping God's commandments and the faith in his son.

- If he have committed sins, they shall be forgiven him: so we know sickness is connected to sin in most cases. Not all the time, but in most cases. That's why you go to the leaders, they pray over you, anoint you with oil, and pray to the Lord that you may be healed.

- In order for the sickness to go away, your sins have got to be forgiven. You have to let the leadership know, and be willing to go to God and say, Lord, please forgive me. You have to put away your sins so the Lord may hear your prayers. But if you're in the midst of active foolishness, sin, to be direct, the prayer is being blocked somewhere.

- Confess your faults one to another: that's how you are forgiven for your sins. Confess your faults to the leaders, your counsellor amongst a thousand, brothers and/or sisters. Confess when you have gone off. And pray one for another: that they get over the sin so they can be healed from their sickness.

- The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much: the man or woman that is not committing sin. Availeth means profits.

- Sickness is directly connected with sin in most cases. I say most cases because you read about the brother who was born blind, and they asked who did sin, him or his parents, and it was said nobody did; he was just born that way so the Lord can show his power. So sickness is not always connected to sin, but in most cases it is.


**[Sirach 7:34-36](/bible/sirach/7#v34)**  *[[9:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=549s)]*

> <sup>[34](/bible/sirach/7#v34)</sup> Fail not to be with them that weep, and mourn with them that mourn.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/sirach/7#v35)</sup> Be not slow to visit the sick: for that shall make thee to be beloved.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/sirach/7#v36)</sup> Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss.

- We went over sickness and visiting, getting rid of sin and anointing with oil. Another part of that is when you visit your brother and/or sister: this is how you should come.

- Fail not to be with them that weep, and mourn with them that mourn. Be not slow to visit the sick: it's spoken in reverse. Be quick to visit the sick.

- For that shall make thee to be beloved: when you visit the sick, you can see the smile on their face. They were in pain, doubting themselves and going through stuff, but when you visit them, it'll make thee beloved.

- You're not looking for, oh, Captain such and such, Officer such and such. No, you're looking for that brother or sister to perk up. Now they're sitting up: oh, I've got to get myself together. That creates something in their spirit; they're willing to fight through it. They might have been sitting there, Lord, please just take me. But you come visit them, you and a couple of other brothers and sisters, and their spirits just go right up.

- Whatsoever thou takest in hand: why does the Lord say that? Do not go visit the sick empty-handed. If you're going to visit your brother or sister that is sick, don't just show up. Yes, that's going to help perk up that spirit. But go to the store and get them something, even if they say, nah, I'm good.

- Some food, a case of water, a case of anything. Go to Publix; you'll figure out what to bring. Come with something in your hand. Don't just show up, because you value that brother, you value that sister.


**[Matthew 25:35-40](/bible/matthew/25#v35)**  *[[12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=725s)]*

> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/25#v35)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/25#v36)</sup> Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/25#v37)</sup> Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/25#v38)</sup> When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/25#v39)</sup> Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/25#v40)</sup> And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.

- I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: charity. I'm reading this because when you're visiting them, bringing them gifts, praying for them, anointing them, remember you're not just doing it to them. Yes, that's your brother, that's your sister. But who is the father of us? The Lord feels these things. When that brother perks up, when that sister perks up, the Lord feels it, just like a good father.

- Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me: whatever you do to your brother, you do it to Christ. These Christians say, I've got a personal relationship. No, this is how you get a personal relationship with Christ: you do it to your brother.

- I put a post online today about some damn stupid Easter, and a Christian comes on my post with the foolishness: yes, the Lord does say Passover, but I prayed to Christ, and Christ told me that Passover is not really... Oh yeah? What did Christ tell you, that you don't got to keep the Passover? You Christians are crazy, man.

- That's how I know you don't give a damn about the sick, because Christ says, when you've done it to the least of these my brethren, you have done it unto me, and it's in red. Christians are the farthest thing from what God wants in his kingdom, because everything he says, you do the opposite.


**[Philippians 2:12-15](/bible/philippians/2#v12)**  *[[14:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=887s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/philippians/2#v13)</sup> For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)</sup> Do all things without murmurings and disputings:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/philippians/2#v15)</sup> That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world;

- Do all things without murmurings and disputings: so visiting the sick, do it without murmuring.

- That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world.


## In Closing

*[[15:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PCuZbE8w-N4&t=954s)]* So to be a light in this world, do what God says, what Christ says: visit the sick when they're sick. Make sure you bring a gift. Make sure you anoint their head. Make sure you pray for them, call them and visit them, so their spirits can go up. And when you get there and talk to them, if they're in the midst of sin, tell them to confess their faults one to another, that their prayers might be heard.
