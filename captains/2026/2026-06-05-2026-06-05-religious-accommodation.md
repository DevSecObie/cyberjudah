## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JMcCF9Sr6pY&t=14s)]* Today we going to talk about religious accommodations, from IUIC Arkansas. God calls us a peculiar people, and what sets us apart is the peculiar commandments: laws contrary to all nations. All nations want to shop on Saturday, but God's people have a covenant to rest on the Sabbath. Employers make religious accommodations for Sabbaths, head coverings and fringes. If you have not even attempted to get one, check your spirit.
