## Introduction

*[[0:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=16s)]* Today's topic is when the world loves its own. A lot of times what we are doing, the world hates it. A lot of you new spirits might come in and say, I'm getting hated from this side and that side, my family don't like me, my friends say I'm in a cult. But just know this: you are on the right track, you are in the right place. The world will always love who they are. If you are who the world is, the world is going to love you; but when you are not of the world, the world is going to hate you, and Christ told us that.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Amos 3:1-2](/bible/amos/3#v1)**  *[[0:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=47s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/3#v1)</sup> Hear this word that the Lord hath spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/3#v2)</sup> You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.

- Why do you think you were the one that got caught out of everybody, all of the evil you were doing with your friends? Because God is going to punish us for our iniquities: his chosen, his elect, that remnant.

- We can't get away with nothing. We are the ones that go to jail when the dumb stuff happens, the ones that get caught in a lie, the ones that can't cheat out of work, can't cheat in school. Why? Because we are not of this world.

- The world will love its own. The world will say, well, he is one of mine, so I'mma let him go ahead and get past. But you ain't one of theirs.


**[John 15:18-20](/bible/john/15#v18)**  *[[2:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=142s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/john/15#v18)</sup> If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before it hated you.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/john/15#v19)</sup> If ye were of the world, the world would love his own: but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hateth you.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/john/15#v20)</sup> Remember the word that I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also.

- If you were of the world, the world would love you. You would be accepted, in the good part of society, the one fitting in with everybody, the cool guy.

- My pops always told me, never be the cool guy, because the cool guy knows everybody, is cool with everybody, friends with everybody, but at the end of the day he ain't got nothing. What we have got is the kingdom and rulership coming to us. Don't worry about being cool with the world.

- The world is going to hate us because we were chosen out of it. Why do you think they give y'all sisters a hard time for dressing modestly? Brothers, you don't go to the club, you're not smoking weed with everybody else, and they talk bad about you, because you are different. You are an example of what they are not, sometimes of something they could never attain to. Half of it is jealousy and envy, and the other half is what Christ just said.

- If the world hates you, just know that it hated Christ first. You are not alone: the Lord of lords and King of kings was hated before us.

- The servant is not greater than his lord. Christ put his life on the line for the nation of Israel; you think we are not going to have to do that? That is what is wrong with Christianity: y'all say Christ lived a perfect life so that we won't have to. If we have got to go through what he went through, why not attain to the same perfection that he did? Why not give it an effort?

- If they persecuted Christ for speaking against all the evil our people were doing, what makes you think they ain't going to persecute you too? You are supposed to speak up against the evil our people do and the evil that is in the world.

- If they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also: those that are going to listen will listen to you just like they listened to Christ, because you are speaking the words of Christ.


**[Luke 16:15](/bible/luke/16#v15)**  *[[6:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=361s)]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/luke/16#v15)</sup> And he said unto them, Ye are they which justify yourselves before men; but God knoweth your hearts: for that which is highly esteemed among men is abomination in the sight of God.

- Ye are they which justify yourselves before men. That is what they do. Tell them Christmas is a pagan holiday: no, that is just a day me and my family get together. Thanksgiving is a celebration of the slaughter and murder of your people: no, that's just a day me and family get together. But you still have a Christmas tree, you decorate it and you still give gifts. You still do the custom, but you try to justify it in your mind. You are of the world.

- But as soon as you say we keep Passover, the Feast of Dedication, the Sabbath day, the new moon, the feast days: oh no, you ain't supposed to be doing all of that. I could celebrate pagan holidays, but if I celebrate God's holidays that are written in the Bible, I'm wrong. Y'all make no sense, and the people are going to wake up and realize that.

- That which is highly esteemed among men is abomination in the sight of God. What is highly esteemed among men? Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Year's, birthdays; the way our women dress out of order; prostituting your daughters.

- Yes, you are prostituting your daughters: giving them boyfriends, giving your daughter over to a man that is not her husband, allowing a young man with no plan in life to deal with your daughter, knowing that boys only want one thing.

- Y'all let them go to prom, pay for the out of order dresses, slits all the way up to the hip, dressed tight as hell, let these young boys pick them up, pay for the limo, and some of y'all even give them hotel rooms. Don't pimps do the same thing? Buy the women their clothes, their food, and when the johns come, buy the hotel room. That is prostitution, and you allow your daughters to do that because it is highly esteemed among men. Shame on y'all.


**[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**  *[[9:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=577s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

- We are supposed to be transformed by the renewing of our mind. Our minds are supposed to be renewed, changed, born again.

- When we reform our mind, it proves that the will of God is good and acceptable and perfect.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 40:8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)</sup> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.

    Thy law is the will. When we do the will of God, his laws, we prove that it is good and perfect and acceptable, because the law of God is going to change our mind, renew our mind.


**[1 Peter 4:3-4](/bible/1-peter/4#v3)**  *[[10:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=658s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-peter/4#v3)</sup> For the time past of our life may suffice us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles, when we walked in lasciviousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-peter/4#v4)</sup> Wherein they think it strange that ye run not with them to the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you:

- When you renew your mind you let it be known that you are different from everybody. You renew your dress code, your dietary code, the way you talk, the way you solve issues, the way you raise your kids, the way you act. You are no longer the person that holds on to grudges or walks around with the mean stink face all the time. You are actually friendly, more patient. All of that shows that the will of God is good, acceptable and perfect.

- Before this truth we used to work the will of the Gentiles: whatever was popular, we went along with it. Those of y'all making music made music right along with what they made; those in sports played just like they played, all the locker room games included. When you finally found a good job that paid well, you did exactly what Negroes do when they get a good job: nothing but wickedness, tricking off your money, buying women, cheating on your wife, getting a whole bunch of women pregnant. Why? Because that is what is highly esteemed.

- Lasciviousness is evil sexual lust. Revellings, that is clubbing. Those of y'all around my age, in your 40s, know the snap era. We were in the club, listening to Three 6 Mafia, UGK, Yung Joc and all of them, even Biggie and Nas, talking about killing your own people and drug dealing. We were all a part of that.

- They think it strange that ye run not with them: now that you stopped doing all of that, you are strange, because you are no longer doing what the cool kids do, what is socially normal.

- Speaking evil of you: now you are in a cult because you cover your body up, because you stopped going to the club, because you stopped worshipping false gods on these wicked holidays. And when you can prove it out of the Bible, they tell you close the Bible, and these are people that were raised in the church.

- What is a real Christian? Why have we never seen the real Christians? Because the Israelites are the real Christians. We are the ones that are going to defend this gospel, speak boldly in the face of everybody, and make sure this gospel is taught to the four corners of the earth.


**[Ephesians 5:11](/bible/ephesians/5#v11)**  *[[15:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=922s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/5#v11)</sup> And have no fellowship with the unfruitful works of darkness, but rather reprove them.

- We are supposed to reprove the unfruitful works of darkness, and we will continue to reprove the unfruitful works of darkness.

- Have no fellowship with them. Don't worry about what everybody else is doing, because that which is highly esteemed among men is abomination to God.

- Don't worry about not fitting in. You will fit in with the elect, with the remnant, with the one-third, with the 144,000. That is who you fit in with.


## In Closing

*[[16:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZlcWAylH4UA&t=970s)]* So stay encouraged. Don't worry about what everybody else is doing. The Bible already said it, so take hold to it, take heed to it, and never give up. Shalom.
