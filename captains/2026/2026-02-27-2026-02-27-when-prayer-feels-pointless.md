## Introduction

*[[0:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9L4-Sdtalio&t=53s)]* Shalom, most high, Christ bless. Your brother Captain Gad. You are tuned in to 15 minutes with the captains. Today's class is called when prayer feels pointless. You know, it's a time of this walk where you feel like you've been hit on all four sides. You praying to the Lord. You asking the Lord for healing. You asking the Lord to take these things from you. And through your infirmities, you feel like God ain't listening to me. Prayers are pointless. So we're going to go over that today.

## Scriptures Opened

**[1 Corinthians 10:13](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v13)**  *[[1:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9L4-Sdtalio&t=84s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v13)</sup> There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it.

- There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: first of all, you got to understand that what you going through, everybody's going through the same exact thing. You ain't the only one that's going through what you're going through. You are not alone.

- But God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able: nothing you go through is new. Nothing you go through is alone.


**[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**  *[[2:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9L4-Sdtalio&t=121s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

- We're going to prove it. We're going to prove that what you feel, our forefathers felt the same thing, and what they did to get over that.

- For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning: the things that were written is for us to learn from. Why? Because we would go through the same exact situations, the same exact emotions, the same exact feelings, the same exact turmoil, the same exact afflictions our forefathers went through, we will go through in these last days. So in order to learn how to get over those things, we must read the scriptures.

- That we through patience: it teaches us patience when we read. And comfort: and it give us comfort to know our forefathers went through it and they overcame it. If they can do it, we can do it.

- Of the scriptures might have hope: so our forefathers went through the same things we go through today. And at times felt the same way. We say our prayer is pointless, God ain't hearing us. Well, some of our forefathers felt that same way.

You know if you only in here just to cause issues amongst the body. You know that. You know if you don't believe. You know if you're not teaching your kids God's commandments. You know that.

You really don't believe. God ain't hearing you because you still dealing with old things that you says you put away, or you ain't put away yet.
