## Introduction

*[[0:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrz788lI8t0&t=30s)]* Captain Karataza out of Houston, Texas. This is 15 minutes with the captains. Today we're going to get into chastening. What is chastening? Chastening is basically to chastise. The definition read in class: "Chastise: rebuke or reprimand severely." And: "What does it mean to chastise someone? To chastise someone means to criticize them harshly or punish them severely for doing something wrong, often with the intent to discipline or correct their behavior." So basically chastising is correction. And the reason why correction is given is to keep you on the right path. A lot of times while we're in this truth, in this walk, we go down the wrong path, and we have our spiritual leaders there to chastise us or correct us.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 14:12](/bible/proverbs/14#v12)**  *[[1:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrz788lI8t0&t=105s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/14#v12)</sup> There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.

- There is a way which seemeth right unto a man: meaning the ways that we think are right, the ways that we think is okay, the way that the world tells us go. We think that's going to get us the righteousness, the prosperity, the eternal life, the kingdom of heaven. Like you see in modern-day Christianity. No.

- But the end thereof are the ways of death: meaning destruction in the end of things. You thought you were going to get eternal life, but you didn't follow the counsel. You didn't receive the correction. You didn't receive the chastening.


**[Proverbs 3:11-12](/bible/proverbs/3#v11)**  *[[2:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrz788lI8t0&t=157s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/3#v11)</sup> My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; neither be weary of his correction:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/3#v12)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

- My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord: don't hate the chastening or the correction from the Lord.

- Neither be weary of his correction: remember, you're being chastised so you can be put on the right path.

- For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth: those that the Lord are dealing with, and he loves you, he is going to correct you. You're in this truth, you're in this walk, you will receive some correction. Whether it be from your immediate leaders or our leadership, deacons, bishops, captains, officers, or it be your spiritual father. There will be correction.

- A clip is played of a mother and her little girl, who has found something in her sister's backpack and is blowing it up like a balloon. Sounds like there's about to be some chastening going on. When the mother comes, the girl bounces back off the recliner to give herself instant access to run. And you already know the words coming out of the mother's mouth: don't you run from me. A lot of y'all can relate to that, whether you was the parent doing the chastening or you was the child receiving the chastening.

- Chastening comes when we're young, when we're old, when we're children, when we're adults. Everybody needs to be chastened at some point in time. There has to be some correction given.


**[Hebrews 12:6-8](/bible/hebrews/12#v6)**  *[[5:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrz788lI8t0&t=348s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/12#v6)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)</sup> If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/12#v8)</sup> But if ye be without chastisement, whereof all are partakers, then are ye bastards, and not sons.

- It says the exact same thing. For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth: he corrects.

- And scourgeth every son whom he receiveth: son or daughter.

- For what son is he whom the father chasteneth not? So if you're not receiving correction, does God really love you? If everything is going on in your life, you don't have no issues, no problems, no trials, no tribulations, nobody corrects you when you're doing wrong, you might want to check your spirit, or you might want to check your lineage.

- What child is not chastened by the parent, or the fathers, or the mothers, or the spiritual leaders, the spiritual fathers? What one is loved that they don't receive correction?

- A second clip is played of a father correcting his grown daughter: "Did I not raise you for better? How many times have I told you you have to be what? Twice as good as them to get half of what they have." Notice the father chastised the daughter for her actions. If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons: or in this case, a daughter. So what type of child are you if you're not receiving correction? The father, or the spiritual fathers, our leaders, they're going to take time to make sure that you're on the right path, a righteous path, to gain eternal life.

- Then are ye bastards, and not sons: if there's no correction coming to you, then you might be fatherless. You might be parentless.

- At the end of the clip he's touching her cheek. He chastened her for her actions, but at the same time, at the end of it, he still showed that I love you, that I still care for you. And that's the same thing that the Most High does for us. That's the same thing that our leaders do for us as well. They get on us when we are off the beaten path, when we're not keeping the commandments, when we're not trying to obtain eternal life. But they do it because they love us. Because they want to see us get the kingdom.


**[Hebrews 12:11](/bible/hebrews/12#v11)**  *[[10:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zrz788lI8t0&t=603s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/12#v11)</sup> Now no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous: nevertheless afterward it yieldeth the peaceable fruit of righteousness unto them which are exercised thereby.

- Now no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous: correction is designed to make you have godly sorrow. So no, it's not going to feel good at the present time.

- Nevertheless afterward it yieldeth the peaceable fruit of righteousness: even though it's grievous unto us, even though it doesn't feel good, by us being able to accept that correction, being able to accept that chastening, it's going to put us on a righteous path to where we can keep God's commandments.

- A last clip is played of a judge sentencing a man to the State Department of Corrections. If you don't receive correction, then you're going to end up on this path right here. So that correction is given to us to keep us away from situations like that. Where you throw your life away because you refuse to humble down and accept correction. Because you refuse to humble down and pay attention to the scriptures. Or because you refuse to humble down and listen to your leadership that's in front of you.

- So remember: whom the Lord loveth, he chasteneth. Count it a joyous thing when you receive that chastening.
