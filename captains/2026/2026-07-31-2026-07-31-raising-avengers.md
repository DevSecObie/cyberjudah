## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ig7WijWybfM&t=14s)]* Captain Emmanuel of IUIC Arkansas, with the topic for the day: raising avengers. Sirach 30 says the father that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy, and when he died he left behind him an avenger against his enemies. That is the goal for each and every father in this truth. We search our forefathers, Abraham, David, Tobit and Mattathias, to see how they charged their sons, and what scripture says about discipline, nurture and labour.

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/30#v1)</sup> He that loveth his son causeth him oft to feel the rod, that he may have joy of him in the end.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/30#v2)</sup> He that chastiseth his son shall have joy in him, and shall rejoice of him among his acquaintance.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/30#v3)</sup> He that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy: and before his friends he shall rejoice of him.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/30#v4)</sup> Though his father die, yet he is as though he were not dead: for he hath left one behind him that is like himself.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/30#v5)</sup> While he lived, he saw and rejoiced in him: and when he died, he was not sorrowful.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/30#v6)</sup> He left behind him an avenger against his enemies, and one that shall requite kindness to his friends.

- He that loveth his son causeth him oft to feel the rod: going into discipline. You're going to discipline your son often.

- That he may have joy of him in the end: because the end of discipline is good results in behaviour and conduct.

- He that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy: when you teach your son his true nationality, his true identity, what God requires of him and how to apply these things, you're grieving the enemy, because the enemy works hard to destroy our sons from the womb. From the food to the evil communications, the TV, the media, the music, everything is meant to destroy our sons. So when you teach them exactly what they have been untaught and mistaught, the things they've hidden from our people, you're going to grieve the enemy.

- Though his father die, yet he is as though he were not dead, for he hath left one behind him that is like himself: you're essentially replicating yourself with your son. So we have got a heavy responsibility as fathers to lead by example.

- He left behind him an avenger against his enemies. That is the goal for each and every father in this truth. You want to leave behind an avenger. That's how you want your son to be.
