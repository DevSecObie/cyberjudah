## Introduction

*[[0:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=2s)]* Today we are going to go over an ungodly father. Say your father was ungodly: is that an excuse for you to remain ungodly? We are going to talk about it. God has given us the power to change our minds, to change our trajectory and to break generational curses.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Ezekiel 18:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/18#v1)**  *[[0:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=22s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/18#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord came unto me again, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/18#v2)</sup> What mean ye, that ye use this proverb concerning the land of Israel, saying, The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children’s teeth are set on edge?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/18#v3)</sup> As I live, saith the Lord God, ye shall not have occasion any more to use this proverb in Israel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/18#v4)</sup> Behold, all souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is mine: the soul that sinneth, it shall die.

- The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge: meaning the fathers made mistakes and the children suffer for it. The fathers were in sin, and so now the children have to feel that sin. That happened in time past.

- Ye shall not have occasion any more to use this proverb in Israel: the Lord said, I am no longer going to let you use this proverb in Israel. You can't make an excuse that because your father was wicked, you have to be wicked.

- All souls are mine; the soul that sinneth, it shall die. The Lord said, no, you are now in charge of your own salvation. You can either continue in the ways of your father, or you can repent and turn back to God.


**[Ezekiel 18:5-13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)**  *[[1:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=104s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)</sup> But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/18#v6)</sup> And hath not eaten upon the mountains, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, neither hath defiled his neighbour’s wife, neither hath come near to a menstruous woman,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/18#v7)</sup> And hath not oppressed any, but hath restored to the debtor his pledge, hath spoiled none by violence, hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/18#v8)</sup> He that hath not given forth upon usury, neither hath taken any increase, that hath withdrawn his hand from iniquity, hath executed true judgment between man and man,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/18#v9)</sup> Hath walked in my statutes, and hath kept my judgments, to deal truly; he is just, he shall surely live, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/18#v10)</sup> If he beget a son that is a robber, a shedder of blood, and that doeth the like to any one of these things,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/18#v11)</sup> And that doeth not any of those duties, but even hath eaten upon the mountains, and defiled his neighbour’s wife,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/18#v12)</sup> Hath oppressed the poor and needy, hath spoiled by violence, hath not restored the pledge, and hath lifted up his eyes to the idols, hath committed abomination,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v13)</sup> Hath given forth upon usury, and hath taken increase: shall he then live? he shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him.

- Hath not lifted up his eyes to the idols: idolatry. Neither hath defiled his neighbour's wife: adultery. Neither hath come near to a menstruous woman: broken the laws of sex. Hath not oppressed any: oppressed no man.

- So this man right here is walking righteously before God. He is just; he shall surely live, saith the Lord God.

- If he beget a son that is a robber: this man did all these righteous things, but his son is unrighteous. Shall that man live, after his father taught him the righteous way but he didn't live righteously? The Lord said, hell no, he ain't going to live. His blood shall be upon him.

- All souls are the Lord's. The souls of the righteous shall live, and the souls of the unrighteous shall die. So just because you grew up in a bad neighborhood, around gang violence, around drugs, around homosexuality, around molestation, all these things in your past, the trauma from your past, that does not give you license to remain that way. God says you can change.


**[Ezekiel 18:14](/bible/ezekiel/18#v14)**  *[[3:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=236s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/18#v14)</sup> Now, lo, if he beget a son, that seeth all his father’s sins which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not such like,

- If he beget a son that seeth all his father's sins, and considereth, and doeth not such like: you don't do like your father.


**[Ezekiel 18:19](/bible/ezekiel/18#v19)**  *[[4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=251s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/18#v19)</sup> Yet say ye, Why? doth not the son bear the iniquity of the father? When the son hath done that which is lawful and right, and hath kept all my statutes, and hath done them, he shall surely live.

- When the son hath done that which is lawful and right, and hath kept all my statutes, he shall surely live. So the Lord don't give us the excuse of saying, my father was ungodly, therefore I'm going to live ungodly. No, the Lord has given us the power to change our minds and to change our trajectory, to break generational curses.


**[Sirach 41:5-7](/bible/sirach/41#v5)**  *[[4:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=297s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/41#v5)</sup> The children of sinners are abominable children, and they that are conversant in the dwelling of the ungodly.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/41#v6)</sup> The inheritance of sinners’ children shall perish, and their posterity shall have a perpetual reproach.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/41#v7)</sup> The children will complain of an ungodly father, because they shall be reproached for his sake.

- The children of sinners are abominable children. Why? Because the children of sinners learn the ways of their father, and they become conversant in the dwelling of the ungodly.

- This is an example to us now as fathers: do not be a sinner, do not teach our children sin, because you make it hard for them. Yes, repentance is open unto them, but they have got an uphill climb because of the climate that you as a father established for them. This is why it's not good to sleep around, why it's good for marriage, why it's good to learn God's commandments and apply them, so that you can do the best by your children.

- The children will complain of an ungodly father, because they shall be reproached for his sake. Now your children have an uphill climb because of the evil you perpetuated and taught them; now they have got to break all these generational curses, break through all that trauma. Don't put your children in that position.

- But if you grew up like that, if your father was ungodly, if you have been reproached for his sake, God says there is still a way of repentance. You can now repent.


**[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**  *[[6:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=390s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

- There is a neurological term called neuroplasticity, where you can change your mind through repetition, through thinking the right thoughts, through applying God's word.

- Be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind. Brothers and sisters, change your mind.

- The Lord don't give you an excuse; he takes away all excuses. I have given you Christ, I have given my son, he died for you. Now you have got a chance to repent.


**[2 Corinthians 12:9](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v9)**  *[[7:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=432s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v9)</sup> And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.

- The Lord told Paul the same thing: no more excuses.

- That the power of Christ may rest upon me: meaning in Christ I have the power to change. I can no longer use my past as an excuse.

- No longer am I going to let what my father did, what my mother did, continue to be an excuse for me. Yes, they raised me up in a bad situation, in the ghetto, to learn evil. But I can't use that as an excuse no more. I have got to push forward.


**[Romans 6:4-6](/bible/romans/6#v4)**  *[[7:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=469s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/6#v4)</sup> Therefore we are buried with him by baptism into death: that like as Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, even so we also should walk in newness of life.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/romans/6#v5)</sup> For if we have been planted together in the likeness of his death, we shall be also in the likeness of his resurrection:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/6#v6)</sup> Knowing this, that our old man is crucified with him, that the body of sin might be destroyed, that henceforth we should not serve sin.

- We are buried with Christ by baptism into death, and just like Christ was raised up from the dead by the glory of the Father, we now can walk in newness of life.

- Christ's death, burial and resurrection is symbolic of us: the old man dying, us burying him, and now we are resurrected as a new man. So we cannot let our past sins plague us or hold us down. We have got to press on toward the mark. Yeah, our fathers were wicked; yeah, our mothers didn't teach us right. But that ain't an excuse to stay in this way. Change, brothers and sisters.

- Our old man is crucified with him, that henceforth we should not serve sin: from henceforth we should not serve our trauma, our old man, the things we have been through. None of that. We are breaking all those walls down; all those things that were once holding us back, we are destroying them one by one.


**[Sirach 17:24-28](/bible/sirach/17#v24)**  *[[9:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0Pt6DW5yMI&t=576s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/17#v24)</sup> But unto them that repent, he granted them return, and comforted those that failed in patience.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/17#v25)</sup> Return unto the Lord, and forsake thy sins, make thy prayer before his face, and offend less.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/17#v26)</sup> Turn again to the most High, and turn away from iniquity: for he will lead thee out of darkness into the light of health, and hate thou abomination vehemently.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/17#v27)</sup> Who shall praise the most High in the grave, instead of them which live and give thanks?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/17#v28)</sup> Thanksgiving perisheth from the dead, as from one that is not: the living and sound in heart shall praise the Lord.

- That is why when people see you now in the truth, they say, I don't know you no more. Because I'm breaking down my trauma, destroying the old ways, the old man.

- Unto them that repent he granted them return: when you repent, the Lord allows you to return. And he comforts you when you fail in patience.

- Make thy prayer before his face: go to God. And offend less: stop offending the Lord. Change your diet. Change that lust. Stop watching porn. Stop having hatred for your brother.

- Ask yourself why you have hatred for the man. I have got hatred because he was ranked over me, because he got the test before I got the test. Why don't I want to see other people do well? Damn, that's rooted in my trauma from my past, because I was the only child and I'm used to getting my way, used to being the center of attention. Well, the Bible says esteem others better than yourself. So I have got to change.

- I have got to humble myself under the mighty hand of God, knowing that God is the one that raises up. When you submit yourself to the Lord in his word, you are able to shed off all these demons. Satan tries to come up, but he can't work, because he has nothing to work with: you are removing these things.

- Hate thou abomination vehemently. The living and sound in heart shall praise the Lord: you give all glory and praise to the Lord, because he has given you an opportunity to return even though you were raised up under an ungodly father.
