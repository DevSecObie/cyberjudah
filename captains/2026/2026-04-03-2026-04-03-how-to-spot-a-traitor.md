## Introduction

*[[0:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=34s)]* We got a topic for you today. It's called how to spot a traitor, cuz they will be amongst us, so we need to spot it. Betrayal happens after trust has been established, but that person normally shows signs, and we just ignore them, because we have an emotional attachment to a brother or a sister, or there's some type of benefit that this person is adding to our lives. Three things to look out for: somebody that's always talking about money or work; a brother or a sister that has a weak spot for their spouse and children; and someone who lacks discipline.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 24:10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)**  *[[0:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=54s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)</sup> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.

- So the Bible says, Christ says in red letters, that we will have betrayers amongst us.

- Now the thing about a betrayal: a betrayal can't happen from your enemy. A betrayal has to happen from somebody that's close to you, somebody that you trust.


**[Psalms 41:9](/bible/psalms/41#v9)**  *[[1:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=86s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/41#v9)</sup> Yea, mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted, which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me.

- Mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted: we had a good relationship. We was tight.

- Which did eat of my bread: we ate together, we broke bread together.

- Hath lifted up his heel against me: you betrayed me. You turned your back on me. Now this is prophecy of Judas and Christ. But Christ told us the same thing that happened to him is going to happen to us.

- It's an old saying that says, when somebody shows you their ass, take a picture. The writing was already on the wall. So when that betrayal happened, you'll look back and say, damn, they used to always do this. They always act funny around this. They always handled themselves strange when it came to this situation. So as we start to grow close with each other and develop relationships, there's certain characteristics that we want to make sure that we look out for.


**[Sirach 38:25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)**  *[[2:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=179s)]*

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)</sup> How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough, and that glorieth in the goad, that driveth oxen, and is occupied in their labours, and whose talk is of bullocks?

- The first thing that we want to look out for is somebody that's always talking about money or work.

- How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough: so the Bible says there's going to be a conflict. You're trying to get wisdom, but you always at work.

- And that glorieth in the goad: not only do you always be at work, but you glory in that thing. Your mind is always wrapped around the job.

- And is occupied in their labours: your profession is your job. Your occupation is your job, but our occupation is the word of the Most High. Going to the blitzes, waking up the 12 tribes of Israel.

- And whose talk is of bullocks: whose conversation is always about work, it's always about money.


**[Sirach 38:33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)**  *[[4:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=256s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)</sup> They shall not be sought for in publick counsel, nor sit high in the congregation: they shall not sit on the judges’ seat, nor understand the sentence of judgment: they cannot declare justice and judgment; and they shall not be found where parables are spoken.

- The Bible says that these type of men will not sit in the judge's seat. Not only will they not sit in the judge's seat, the scripture says they won't even understand judgment. They always talking about money, they always talking about work. The Lord says they not going to have the spirit of wisdom on them.


**[Proverbs 28:5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)**  *[[5:02](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=302s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)</sup> Evil men understand not judgment: but they that seek the Lord understand all things.

- Let's find out what kind of men do not understand judgment. Evil men understand not judgment.

- So we got to draw the correlation here. The scripture tells us somebody that's always occupied in their job, always talking about work, will not understand judgment, and the Bible says evil men don't understand judgment. You not look at this brother that's always talking about work as an evil brother. But the scriptures is subtly telling us there's some form of evil in this brother or sister if all they talking about is work, work, work, money, money, money.


**[Sirach 38:33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)**  *[[6:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=360s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)</sup> They shall not be sought for in publick counsel, nor sit high in the congregation: they shall not sit on the judges’ seat, nor understand the sentence of judgment: they cannot declare justice and judgment; and they shall not be found where parables are spoken.

- Nor sit high in the congregation: they won't be leaders amongst the nation.

- And they shall not be found where parables are spoken: they're not going to be around the leadership either. Why? They was at work.

- This is the type of brother or sister that when the job calls and says, hey, Mr. Such and such or Mrs. Such and such, we need you to come in on Saturday this week, they're going to look to the left, they're going to look to the right, and they're going to say, yes sir, boss, I'll be there.

- So these people who glorify their job, they glorify money, they will be willing to compromise for the right dollar amount. They will betray you if the price is right. They're subtly telling you, by always talking about money, always talking about their job, that they have a price where they can be bought. They will betray Christ. They will betray the laws of God. They will break the Sabbath, and they will betray you too.

- That brother or sister that says, well, I got to do what I got to do to provide for my family: what's the difference between them and the drug dealer? Don't drug dealers say the same thing? Can you trust a drug dealer? No. So you can't trust a brother or sister that will idolize their money above the laws of God.


**[Sirach 33:19](/bible/sirach/33#v19)**  *[[7:49](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=469s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/33#v19)</sup> Give not thy son and wife, thy brother and friend, power over thee while thou livest, and give not thy goods to another: lest it repent thee, and thou intreat for the same again.

- The next thing that we're going to look out for is a brother or a sister that has a weak spot for their spouse and children.

- The Bible says not to give our power away to our spouse, to our wife or our children.

- So this person right here, they will sell you out for their wife. They will sell you out for their kids. How do you know? Well, can't nobody correct their wife. Can't nobody correct their kids. If you go to correct their wife for being out of order, they come to the rescue. Little Johnny, little Susie, their kid get into some trouble. As soon as that kid is getting corrected, they come to the rescue.

- Your little 5-year-old, 9-year-old, 6-year-old, they get into an issue with another child. This type of person wants to go confront the other child. That is a weak spirit right there. The scripture say foolishness is bound in the heart of a child. It's our job as parents to coach the children, build them up, show them the scriptures. Oh, this is what happened, son? Let's go to the scriptures. You see this Matthew chapter 18? If you got an issue, you need to go to that brother. Next Sabbath, I want you to go talk to that brother, tell him what happened, and try to gain your brother. But the weak brother that got a weak spot for his children, he want to jump in the middle of that, he want to try to handle that for his child. We can't have spirits like that.


**[Sirach 30:7](/bible/sirach/30#v7)**  *[[9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=593s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/30#v7)</sup> He that maketh too much of his son shall bind up his wounds; and his bowels will be troubled at every cry.

- He that maketh too much of his son: that's who we just read about in Sirach chapter 33. You gave your power over to your children. You idolized your children. You made too much of your children.

- And his bowels will be troubled at every cry: as soon as something happen, oh, what's wrong? What happened? What's going on?


**[Sirach 30:9](/bible/sirach/30#v9)**  *[[10:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=629s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/30#v9)</sup> Cocker thy child, and he shall make thee afraid: play with him, and he will bring thee to heaviness.

- Cocker thy child: meaning to coddle your child.

- If you got that soft weak spot for your kids, it's going to make you weak. And it will be ammunition for Satan to use them to cause you to betray. You will betray whoever you're close to for your children. You will betray whoever your loved ones are, whoever you claim to look up to, you will betray them for your spouse.

- So as soon as Satan can identify that in you, he making notes. All I got to do is put some pressure on his spouse and he going to crack. All I got to do is send some adversity toward his child and he going to crack.

- So when Jacob's trouble comes and brothers being tortured and kidnapped, you know how they going to get the information about the leaders in the congregation? All they got to do is put pressure on your child. All they got to do is threaten your spouse. And you going to give up any information and all information that you got.


**[Matthew 10:37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)**  *[[11:47](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=707s)]*

> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)</sup> He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me: and he that loveth son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.

- Christ wouldn't have to say this if it wasn't true, if it wasn't facts, if it wasn't going to be a reality at some point.

- He that loveth father or mother more than me: there are brothers and sisters that love their parents more than they love the Lord. Is not worthy of me: the Lord says you're not worthy of him.

- And he that loveth son or daughter more than me: if you got a weak spot for your kids, you're not worthy of Christ.

- What the Lord is showing us is that you'll turn on everyone in your life for whoever you hold as number one in your life. That's why Christ says nobody can be greater than me in your life. We sacrificed our entire lives for Christ. We turned on our old spouses that don't believe. We turned on our family members that don't believe. We quit jobs because of how high we hold Christ in our lives. He's the number one spot.

- So Christ is saying if you can put anybody else in that spot, you'll betray everybody for that person. For that dollar amount. For that brother. For that sister. That mother, father, husband, wife, child. There's nobody that can hold that position other than Christ. We cannot have a weak spot for spouses and children.


**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)**  *[[13:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=815s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)</sup> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

- The next thing that we're going to look out for is someone who lacks discipline. Someone who lacks discipline will betray you.

- For the holy spirit of discipline: the Holy Spirit, the spirit that comes from Christ, is a spirit of discipline. If you don't have discipline, you're not dealing with the spirit of Christ.

- And will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in: so if you lack discipline, it's because there's some form of unrighteousness in you. If you lack discipline, there's some form of deceit that is inside of you.

- Lack of discipline is an outward show of deeply rooted character flaws. Someone who lacks discipline, they lack a standard. If you lack discipline, you lack commitment. If pressure is applied, your yea will turn into a nay. Your nay will turn into a yea. You won't take accountability for your actions, and you will find some way to blame everybody except for yourself.


**[Proverbs 24:21](/bible/proverbs/24#v21)**  *[[15:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oh0jav3N2mw&t=908s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/24#v21)</sup> My son, fear thou the Lord and the king: and meddle not with them that are given to change:

- My son, fear thou the Lord and the king: you must understand where your loyalty lies. We show respect toward the powers that be, but our loyalty lies with the Lord. So we'll fear both, but our commitment and our loyalty is only to one.

- And meddle not with them that are given to change: stay far away from people who can't discern where their loyalty lies. People that lack discipline will lack loyalty, and they will betray you.

- So the question you got to ask yourself: are you a traitor? Do you have potential to be a traitor? Are the people that are in your inner circle showing patterns to show you that they have potential to betray you?
