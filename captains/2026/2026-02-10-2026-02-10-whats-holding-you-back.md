## Scriptures Opened

**[Isaiah 34:16-17](/bible/isaiah/34#v16)**  *[[0:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=28s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/34#v16)</sup> Seek ye out of the book of the Lord, and read: no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate: for my mouth it hath commanded, and his spirit it hath gathered them.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/34#v17)</sup> And he hath cast the lot for them, and his hand hath divided it unto them by line: they shall possess it for ever, from generation to generation shall they dwell therein.

- Seek ye out of the book of the LORD, and read: so we supposed to seek out of the book of the Lord, because it's the book of life. That's what it is. Every situation that we have ever went through is already written in here in some form. We just have to seek out the book of the Lord and find it in the scriptures.

- They shall possess it for ever, from generation to generation shall they dwell therein: so the Lord gave us this book to dwell in from generation to generation, because it will answer all of our questions that may come up in any situation that we need guidance or wisdom of God of how to move in it. God said, I'm going to make sure you have this from generation to generation, no matter where you are on the earth.


**[Proverbs 22:20](/bible/proverbs/22#v20)**  *[[1:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=93s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/22#v20)</sup> Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge,

- Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge: that's why we have to seek out the book of the Lord, because it's excellent things that were written to us by God's hand. He used the spirit of the prophets to write down these things. Our ancestors, and they wrote down the road map of how to get back to him, how to handle things, how to counsel, when to counsel, what is good counsel, what is not good counsel.


**[Psalms 33:11](/bible/psalms/33#v11)**  *[[2:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=132s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/33#v11)</sup> The counsel of the Lord standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations.

- The counsel of the LORD standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations: that's what the Bible is. It's the counsel of the Lord, the mind of the Lord. And it said it stands forever to all generations. To generations of who? To generations of Israelites that are never ending, that are like the sand of the sea, that are scattered throughout the four corners of the earth. He gave us a way to get back. His thoughts of how to please him, how to get back to him, every single generation.


**[1 Peter 2:9](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)**  *[[2:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=171s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)</sup> But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:

- That ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light: so what's holding us back? When God called us out of the darkness into his marvelous light, God knew where we was and what situation we were in. When he decided to reveal himself to us in this generation, he revealed himself to you knowing that you might have been in lust. You might have been in adultery. You might have been in fornication. He revealed himself to you to call you out of that darkness. Not to know of him and remain in darkness, but to come out of that darkness.


**[Joshua 1:9](/bible/joshua/1#v9)**  *[[3:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=225s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/1#v9)</sup> Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the Lord thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

- Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest: so now that we've been called out of that darkness into his marvelous light, and commanded to repent and be better than we was, there shouldn't be anything that holds us back. Especially fear. Because God said, when I called you, I knew you was in darkness. I knew the situation you was in. I called you out into this marvelous light, and I am with you from here on going forward. No matter where you are, God is always with us. So there should be nothing holding us back, especially fear.


**[2 Chronicles 15:1-2](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v1)**  *[[4:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=280s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v1)</sup> And the Spirit of God came upon Azariah the son of Oded:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v2)</sup> And he went out to meet Asa, and said unto him, Hear ye me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin; The Lord is with you, while ye be with him; and if ye seek him, he will be found of you; but if ye forsake him, he will forsake you.

- The LORD is with you, while ye be with him: we just read that in Joshua. He said, fear not, I am with you. But he said, I am with you while you are with him.

- And if ye seek him, he will be found of you; but if ye forsake him, he will forsake you: so he is with those that are seeking him, that are seeking him in the book of the Lord, that are praying unto him, that are trying to come out of that darkness and walk in his marvelous light. He said, as long as you are seeking me, I am with you.


**[Hebrews 11:1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)**  *[[5:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=333s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)</sup> Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

- Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen: so what do you have to do? You have to replace that fear with faith. Replace fear with faith. That's what God wants. Because you're coming out of darkness. You're walking in his marvelous light. And you may not know how to walk uprightly, perfectly as he commands you, but he wants you to walk in faith, not in fear.


**[Hebrews 11:6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)**  *[[6:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=385s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)</sup> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

- But without faith it is impossible to please him: without that faith, we cannot please him. We can't please God walking in fear and saying that we still have a relationship with him. He's not pleased by those things. It has to be faith in him, and walking in that, to please him.

- For he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him: now you have to believe that, in seeking the Lord, you are coming out of that darkness, that there is a reward coming to you from God. And God's not a man that you should lie. He said if you diligently seek him, then he will give you that reward. He deliver you from that darkness that you lived your entire life in, that held you back. You don't need to stay in the darkness. Come out of it. Walk in that light in faith without fear, and it'll please God when you seek him.


**[Sirach 2:10-11](/bible/sirach/2#v10)**  *[[7:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=447s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/2#v10)</sup> Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/2#v11)</sup> For the Lord is full of compassion and mercy, longsuffering, and very pitiful, and forgiveth sins, and saveth in time of affliction.

- Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded: that's why history is so important, and that's why the world tries to separate us, blacks and Hispanics and Native Americans as well, from our history. Because when you know your history, you can look back on it and you can gain strength from what God has did for your ancestors, and you can apply that to yourself and walk in that faith and not fear.

- For the Lord is full of compassion and mercy, longsuffering, and very pitiful, and forgiveth sins, and saveth in time of affliction: so the Lord is compassionate upon those that are trying to come out of sin, that are looking at the generations of old and saying, I can be like Abraham. I can be like Paul. I can be like the disciples. I can roll in the spirit of Jeremiah. I can build up my people like Nehemiah. God has compassion for those people that are walking after the spirit of their ancestors, in that faith that they had.

- That's what Hebrews 11 is all about. It gives you examples of the faith that your ancestors had that you should walk in. And when you walk in that faith, God says, I have compassion on those, and mercy, and I forgive sins of those that are afflicted by the darkness they walked in.


**[Lamentations 3:22-25](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)**  *[[9:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=551s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)</sup> It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/lamentations/3#v23)</sup> They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/lamentations/3#v24)</sup> The Lord is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/lamentations/3#v25)</sup> The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.

- It is of the LORD's mercies that we are not consumed: it's nothing that we've done ourselves. It's nothing that our parents did. It's nothing that this world has done for us. It is by the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed by the darkness that was holding us back.

- They are new every morning: every morning we awake, we have a chance to walk in that marvelous light that God has called us in, and not stay back in the darkness that Satan wants to keep us trapped in.

- To the soul that seeketh him: God is compassionate every morning to the soul that is seeking him and not letting things hold him back. It's new every morning. That's why we have to repent daily, because as this walk of us coming out of darkness and in this light, we will stumble as newborn babes. But God said he has compassion and mercy because you're seeking him with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.


**[Deuteronomy 13:6-8](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v6)**  *[[10:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=631s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v6)</sup> If thy brother, the son of thy mother, or thy son, or thy daughter, or the wife of thy bosom, or thy friend, which is as thine own soul, entice thee secretly, saying, Let us go and serve other gods, which thou hast not known, thou, nor thy fathers;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v7)</sup> Namely, of the gods of the people which are round about you, nigh unto thee, or far off from thee, from the one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v8)</sup> Thou shalt not consent unto him, nor hearken unto him; neither shall thine eye pity him, neither shalt thou spare, neither shalt thou conceal him:

- Namely, of the gods of the people which are round about you: that's going into Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, witchcraft, whatever it may be.

- Thou shalt not consent unto him, nor hearken unto him: you shouldn't let any relationship in your life hold you back. It doesn't matter if it's your father, your mother, your brother, cousins, family member, friends. God says nobody should hold you back from serving me. And don't consent to what they saying. No matter how good it may sound, if it's not thus saith the Lord.

- Neither shalt thou conceal him: it says don't conceal them. Tell them, hey, leave me alone. I'm seeking the Lord. I'm seeking thus saith the Lord as it is written, his laws, his commandments, and I'm walking in that faith. Don't let anybody hold you back from serving the Lord.


**[Hebrews 12:1](/bible/hebrews/12#v1)**  *[[11:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=713s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/12#v1)</sup> Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which doth so easily beset us, and let us run with patience the race that is set before us,

- Seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses: that's what you read in Hebrews 11.

- Let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which doth so easily beset us: don't even let sin hold you back. That means what? You got to overcome yourself. If it's not written, thus saith the Lord that thou shalt do, you have to remove that. Overcome that sin. Overcome yourself. Whether it be drugs, whether it be fornication, whether it be stealing, whether it be robbing or scamming, whatever it may be, overcome that sin. Don't even let sin hold you back from serving God.


**[Sirach 17:24-26](/bible/sirach/17#v24)**  *[[12:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JLLb4q16a5o&t=760s)]*

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/17#v24)</sup> But unto them that repent, he granted them return, and comforted those that failed in patience.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/17#v25)</sup> Return unto the Lord, and forsake thy sins, make thy prayer before his face, and offend less.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/17#v26)</sup> Turn again to the most High, and turn away from iniquity: for he will lead thee out of darkness into the light of health, and hate thou abomination vehemently.

- But unto them that repent, he granted them return: God grants return to those that repent, that turn from them sins that are holding them back.

- Return unto the Lord, and forsake thy sins, make thy prayer before his face, and offend less: get better and better and better. Leave off from those sins. Now that you are walking in this light, don't let those sins from the past creep back up on you and hold you back. Overcome them. Get better and better. Offend less.

- For he will lead thee out of darkness into the light of health: there's a better life waiting on you if you don't let these sins and the family members and friends hold you back from serving God. There's a better life beyond that.

- And hate thou abomination vehemently: and when you leave that, and when you have overcome those things and they're not holding you back, now you despise those sins, and you go out and teach others how not to be held back.
