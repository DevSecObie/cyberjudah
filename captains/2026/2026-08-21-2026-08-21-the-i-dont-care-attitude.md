## Introduction

Shalom Israel. Most High Christ bless. Welcome to another edition of 15 Minutes with the Captains. My name is Captain Benaiah, IUIC Tupelo, Mississippi council.

**And today's class is called the I don't care attitude.**

That I don't care attitude. You will see that a lot of times in the truth. And I am telling you what it is: our people do not want to change. You come into the truth, you learn who you are, you know you are an Israelite, you know you must keep the commandments of God. But in order to grow, you have to change.
