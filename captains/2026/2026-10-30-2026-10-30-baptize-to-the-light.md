## Introduction

*[[0:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VL6wmZ1I3Ew&t=58s)]* Welcome to 15 Minutes with the Captains. The title is Baptize to the Light. We want to talk about repentance. We want to talk about baptism. A lot of the world, the churches, they say that you must be dipped in water, washed in the blood of the lamb in order to receive salvation. Along with fasting, then you will start to offend less. Just because you got dumped in that water, you are not offending less. Your mindset has not changed.

The scriptures is showing you it's not getting dumped in the water that changes things. It's being in the word of God that changes things. Then and only then will you be led out of darkness into that light.

- The light of health: then you have a good conscience toward God, when you are applying the scriptures, applying the word of God.

- Hate thou abomination vehemently: hate sin vehemently. That's the mindset we got to have. You know you deal with addiction, you know you deal with lust, you got to start to hate that thing, so that you will flee from sin like the face of a serpent.
