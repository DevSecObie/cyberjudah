## Introduction

*[[0:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BECx_FiADmE&t=11s)]* We're going to go real quick over charity: charity, the royal law. The royal law is thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, and charity is part of loving thy neighbour as thyself. We examine two eloquent men in the Bible that did not have charity, Ahithophel and Absalom, who kept secret hatred for King David until it turned to grudges and a desire to kill him.
