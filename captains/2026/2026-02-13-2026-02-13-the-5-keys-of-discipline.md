## Scriptures Opened

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v17)**  *[[0:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRZrok8BO_Q&t=32s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v17)</sup> For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love;

- For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline: to her, there is wisdom. And when you read verse 20, it says wisdom brings you to a kingdom. That's what we all want, right? So the beginning of that is the desire of discipline. Discipline is something that we all should not even should, but need to desire.

- And the care of discipline is love.

- So let's deal with five keys of discipline. We're going to deal with consistency, self-control, clarity, accountability, and structure.

- So with consistency, consistency in terms of what we're going to talk about is showing up and doing the work even when you don't feel like it. That's true consistency.


**[Proverbs 8:34](/bible/proverbs/8#v34)**  *[[1:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRZrok8BO_Q&t=99s)]*

> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/8#v34)</sup> Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates, waiting at the posts of my doors.

- So the emphasis is of course spiritual, because that's where everything starts, and that's the standard that the Lord has for us in the scriptures.

- Blessed is the man that heareth me, watching daily at my gates: and this is wisdom speaking in first person. Blessed is the man that heareth wisdom and watcheth daily at my gates. A lot of us, we have good days. We have hot streaks. Like, damn, that day that brother was on point. Them couple days, he was in the spirit. She was on a roll. But the Bible sets the standard of a daily thing dealing with wisdom.

- Studying, praying and applying: there is literally a numerical standard that's established for the nation. Studying four chapters a day. Pray three times a day is Psalms 55:17. Daniel did it in Daniel chapter 6. It's written for your learning. In application, that's why the Bible say, don't forsake the assembly thereof. Exhort one another daily. Like in Acts two, they gathered together daily from house to house. You understand? And through that consistency, what happened? The Lord added souls to the church. That's what the Lord wants to see from us spiritually. That is the standard. He wants to see a pattern of good works.

- Even when you don't feel like it. Guess what? The Lord don't give a damn about our feelings. And if you know, you know this is true: when you don't feel like doing something, but you do it anyway, a lot of times that be the most rewarding feeling after you have done it. You'll be like, damn, I'm so glad I did. I'm so glad I ain't listened to my wicked self, that inner B within. I ain't let them win. I got my ass up and got it in. You understand? Spirit for the win. And that's what the Lord looking for. Consistency. That's the first key of discipline.

- So the next key of discipline, self-control. Self-control going into what? Resisting impulses and staying focused on long-term goals.


**[Sirach 7:36](/bible/sirach/7#v36)**  *[[4:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRZrok8BO_Q&t=276s)]*

> <sup>[36](/bible/sirach/7#v36)</sup> Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss.

- This is a heavy one right here.

- Whatsoever thou takest in hand: whatever you take in your hand. What's where we taking in our hand the most every damn day? This damn device right here. You understand? Along with many other things.

- Remember the end: so whether it's a responsibility, a task, a book, or a recreational device or activity, whatever it is, remember the end. What's going to be the benefit from it? If you read, you build your spirit. You're watering the root of Christ within you to bear forth fruits of the spirit. It's going to contribute to godly conversation, which is second Peter. He said that that's better be all we involved in, for when Christ come back, to get out that fire. All manner of godly conversation.

- There is so many benefits to the end result of you choosing to read the Bible. You choosing to pick up the phone and call that brother and sister to get in the scriptures. You calling your officer, your leadership, or whoever, to get more involved, to find an office, to put your hand to the plow. But if you take your phone in your hand and you be like, let me scroll on Instagram, for a lot of us, that usually ends up, you looking at some profiles and clicking some links, and now your ass watching porn. You understand? Your ass watching dumb rap videos. You're coveting the wicked. You're envying the life of people who not keeping God commandments, and you find yourself depressed and confused. You don't know where you want to be in life. You don't know if you want to be somewhere with money bag yo and little baby or whoever, or if you want to be at the leadership table. You don't know what your goals is. The hell is this?

- And thou shalt never do amiss: if you stop to consider what's going to be the end result of me doing this, that's going to stop you from doing the dumb that always hinder you and that you always end up in a slump about. If you was to stop and consider before you chose the foolishness, you would never choose the foolishness, and you would always choose to do things that would somehow profit you in the end.

- That is true self-control. The ability to tell yourself no, and the capability of doing something productive. That's a major key of discipline.

- The next one, clarity. Clarity in this context is knowing exactly what you want and why you're doing it.


**[Psalms 112:7-8](/bible/psalms/112#v7)**  *[[8:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRZrok8BO_Q&t=517s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/112#v7)</sup> He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: his heart is fixed, trusting in the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/112#v8)</sup> His heart is established, he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies.

- A lot of time, brothers and sisters just be in limbo. You don't know what the hell you want to do. Your goals ain't clear. You don't have a true plan in sight. Your vision is not established. But the good man, according to the Bible in Psalms 112, is who it's talking about. Let's see how the good man or the good woman thinks.

- He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: you're not going to be afraid. Cuz every time you trying to do something good, it's always going to be some type of resistance. Somebody telling you, nah, I did that before, bro. I tried that. That ain't going to work. Nah, bro, that ain't the way to go. You can actually do this instead. They ain't even allowing that no more. You ain't going to be worried about that. Why?

- His heart is fixed: you know what that mean? That's like fixed income. Somebody on fixed income, that amount that they get every month is the amount that they get every month. It's never changing. When your heart is fixed, that mean your mind is made up. You determine, what the hell nobody talking about, this is what the hell I'm going to do. Period. And I'm not taking no for an answer.

- Trusting in the LORD: because you trust in the Lord.

- His heart is established: and that be a lot of our problem. I think I kind of want to do this, and I kind of, but yesterday I really, but now I... Your heart not established. The Lord said a good man heart is established.

- He shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies: that go back to that determination. I'm not letting up until the mission is complete. And you know exactly what that mission is. That way every move can be more and more intentional. With intentional moves come accomplishments and milestones. That's a major key of discipline. You need that clarity. Stop being in limbo, you don't know what the hell you really got going on.

- Next one, accountability. Shout out to my sisters. But this is for all of us. Accountability, we talking about tracking your actions and owning your results.


**[Haggai 1:4-7](/bible/haggai/1#v4)**  *[[11:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRZrok8BO_Q&t=705s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/haggai/1#v4)</sup> Is it time for you, O ye, to dwell in your cieled houses, and this house lie waste?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/haggai/1#v5)</sup> Now therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts; Consider your ways.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/haggai/1#v6)</sup> Ye have sown much, and bring in little; ye eat, but ye have not enough; ye drink, but ye are not filled with drink; ye clothe you, but there is none warm; and he that earneth wages earneth wages to put it into a bag with holes.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/haggai/1#v7)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Consider your ways.

- Is it time for you, O ye, to dwell in your cieled houses, and this house lie waste: this house is talking about, in context, the temple of the Lord that was supposed to be being rebuilt by the remnant that was allowed to return to rebuild it. But what was that remnant doing? They ass was on vacation. They were chilling in the crib.

- Consider your ways: cuz they had excuses. Well, a couple years ago, Darius, and a couple years ago they made a law that we couldn't, they stopped the funding. No. What about right now, today? Lord said, consider your ways.

- Ye have sown much, and bring in little: you work, but it ain't enough. You ain't bringing in enough. Ye eat, but ye have not enough: you not full, you should be. Ye clothe you, but there is none warm: you don't have enough clothing.

- And he that earneth wages earneth wages to put it into a bag with holes: you get money, but it's like it just go in one pocket and never come back out the pocket. It go through the pocket. The hell is this? What's going on? The bag never increase.

- So you hear that verse, your first thought is excuses, man. Cuz I ain't get this raise, and I tried, and I was applying, and the prices went up on this and that, and I got this ticket that came out of nowhere. What is God reply to all of that? Thus saith the LORD of hosts; Consider your ways. That's God response to you, black man and black woman.

- Consider your way. Track your actions and own your results. It's you. You the reason why you at the dead end job. You the reason why you still in an apartment and out the house. Take accountability. Consider your ways. What are you doing or not doing that can change your results? That's accountability, brothers and sisters. Major key of discipline.

- And the final one we're going to deal with is structure. Structure in terms of discipline is creating routines and environments that support your goals.


**[2 Esdras 13:55](/bible/2-esdras/13#v55)**  *[[14:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IRZrok8BO_Q&t=895s)]*

> <sup>[55](/bible/2-esdras/13#v55)</sup> Thy life hast thou ordered in wisdom, and hast called understanding thy mother.

- Look at what the Lord said to Israel.

- Thy life hast thou ordered in wisdom: a lot of our lives, they have no order. You take it one day at a time, just like you ain't got no alarm clock. It is what it is. I went to sleep about 10:42, so usually my body wake me up about 7. Cut it out.

- Ordering your life in wisdom is dealing with what? Prioritizing, making routines, creating a schedule. Having increments of time where only this is allowed. Making sure you get this in and you get that in, and so on and so forth. Not, I know I'mma do it because I said I gotta do it. No, this time is allotted for this and that. I put this in place for this and for that. I have objects and tools to remind me. I'm not just counting on my memory. There's order and structure to your life in wisdom. That's how we ought to be.

- And hast called understanding thy mother: so that's what we got to do. We got to have structure. We got to order our lives in wisdom. Prioritize wisely. Set clear goals. Have that clarity. Take accountability. Make sure we keep track of what we doing and why certain stuff ain't increasing. We got to take stock of those things. We got to control, like, we can't just, tonight it's a cheat day. You got a cheat day every week. You ain't got no damn diet. The hell is this? You got to binge on Netflix night every week, and you ain't serious.

- Inconsistency. When you find what works, do what works, and wax old in your work. Trust me, you going to reap the rewards.
