## Scriptures Opened

**[Acts 2:17](/bible/acts/2#v17)**  *[[0:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IAB-Axx4XiY&t=37s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/acts/2#v17)</sup> And it shall come to pass in the last days, saith God, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream dreams:

- and your old men shall dream dreams: so there's two parts to this misunderstood scripture. First, we going to deal with the top part, about pouring out the spirit upon all flesh. A lot of people don't understand that the word all actually has context. Even though it's talking about a sum of people, it still has context when it's being written down. You got to pay attention to that. So Peter ain't getting this from nowhere. This is actually according to what was written in the old covenant, to be fulfilled with the Israelites in the last days.

>

>
> <sup>[29](/bible/joel/2#v29)</sup> And also upon the servants and upon the handmaids in those days will I pour out my spirit.

- in those days will I pour out my spirit: now who is the subject matter? We just read it in verse 27. The children of Israel. That is the all people that he will pour out his spirit upon.

- Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you: that's that word of God again.

When the spirit of God comes upon you, you don't make excuses to try to justify the things of the world. You come out from the things of the world.
