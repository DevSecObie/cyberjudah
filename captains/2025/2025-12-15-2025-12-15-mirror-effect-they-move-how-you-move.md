## Scriptures Opened

**[1 Timothy 4:12](/bible/1-timothy/4#v12)**  *[[1:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vZJBiOWt9g&t=91s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/4#v12)</sup> Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

- Let no man despise thy youth: so we're not supposed to let anybody despise how young we may be, cuz some of us, we are in the truth young. It is what it is. But we supposed to be that right example as young men.

- but be thou an example of the believers: so no matter what your age, no matter what your background, you have to be an example to the believers. That's our job, to be an example to the believers. You understand?

- in word, in conversation: don't let your conversation be the same thing as people in the world. Don't let your conversation be about just football, basketball, and a bunch of women, cuz that's a lot of things that our people talk about. That's really the basis of what our people talk about. When you can't have a conversation about sports with us, you can't talk about different women with us, the room really get quiet, because they have nothing else to talk about. When we start talking about real life issues, they get uncomfortable. But we supposed to be an example in conversation, an example in word.

- in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity: we supposed to be an example in all of those things. You understand?


**[Philippians 3:17](/bible/philippians/3#v17)**  *[[3:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vZJBiOWt9g&t=197s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/philippians/3#v17)</sup> Brethren, be followers together of me, and mark them which walk so as ye have us for an ensample.

- Brethren, be followers together of me: that's what Paul is saying. We have to say, be followers together of us.

- and mark them which walk so as ye have us for an ensample: mark them that walk just like how we walk, them that walk as the right example. Why should you mark them? So that you can follow them. Somebody got to set the example, and somebody's gonna follow. There's always an example set, whether it be good or whether it be bad. You understand?


**[James 5:10-11](/bible/james/5#v10)**  *[[4:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vZJBiOWt9g&t=251s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/james/5#v10)</sup> Take, my brethren, the prophets, who have spoken in the name of the Lord, for an example of suffering affliction, and of patience.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/james/5#v11)</sup> Behold, we count them happy which endure. Ye have heard of the patience of Job, and have seen the end of the Lord; that the Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.

- for an example of suffering affliction, and of patience: there's always an example to be set. Every time we walk out the door, every time we go to the store, every time we teach, every time we go anywhere, there's an example set. How we dress, how we talk, how we walk, our conversation, all of that stuff people listen to and people watch for. That's why we have to be an example to them. That's why the scriptures say that which was written before time was written for our learning. Because when we look, all right, I may be going through it in my marriage. Okay, Abraham went through it in his marriage. They got a bunch of people that went through it in their marriage. Jacob went through it in his marriage. There's always lessons and understanding that we can pull out of the scriptures to show, damn, our forefathers went through the same thing I'm going through right now. That should give us comfort.

- Ye have heard of the patience of Job: they said you saw the patience of Job. We're supposed to look at what Job went through and learn from it, and understand, well, man, maybe my situation ain't all that bad. Look at what Job had to go through. We just have to believe. A lot of times we look at the Bible like it's a fairy tale book. It's not. It's real live stories, real live accounts of our people, our forefathers, our ancestors, and how they got through their trials and their tribulations.


**[Proverbs 22:6](/bible/proverbs/22#v6)**  *[[6:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vZJBiOWt9g&t=367s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/22#v6)</sup> Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

- Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it. Whether it be good or whether it be bad, somebody is going to follow the example that you set. Why? Because a child is going to follow you whether you're actually doing the right thing or whether you're doing the wrong thing.


<div class="reading">

Reading from a definition of vicarious modeling  *[[6:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vZJBiOWt9g&t=403s)]*

> Vicarious modeling, also known as vicarious learning, is a learning method that involves observing and imitating the behaviors of others. It's a type of observational learning that can take place in educational or workplace settings.

- This is called vicarious modeling. You can learn vicariously, whether in education, whether at your job, whether the people that you're around. That's why a lot of our people, they talk a certain way. They learned that vicariously. A lot of our women got vicarious trauma. They never had anything happen to them really, but because they heard it, they often imitate their behaviors. Just like when we talk about women that kill their babies, they unalive their babies. They say, Oh, well, what if a woman gets raped? They always use that example. Always. But that's not a primary reason for why they do that. Then when you say the man is the head of the household, the woman got to be subject unto the man, she got to submit to the man, they automatically think about domestic abuse. Why? Vicarious learning. When you have movies that show the man beating the woman, when you have people that constantly tell stories about, well, back in the day the man used to beat the woman, she was barefoot and pregnant, they learn that vicariously. Then they internalize it and they make it real to themselves. That's why we have to be a right example to our people.

**[Ezekiel 16:44](/bible/ezekiel/16#v44)**  *[[8:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vZJBiOWt9g&t=507s)]*

> <sup>[44](/bible/ezekiel/16#v44)</sup> Behold, every one that useth proverbs shall use this proverb against thee, saying, As is the mother, so is her daughter.

- As is the mother, so is her daughter: one of the scriptures actually tells us that a young woman will follow in the footsteps of her mother. If you use a proverb against somebody, that's going into a negative connotation. It said, As is the mother, so is the daughter. Wives raise up other wives. Single mothers raise up other single mothers. You ever heard the phrase hurt people hurt people? Because that's what they do. They imitate the hurt behavior. So a lot of women grow up by a broken woman, they grow up broken. A lot of men grow up by a broken woman, they grow up broken. That's why it's important for us to have marriages. That's why it's important for us to have a right example of who to follow and who not to follow, because the child is going to follow somebody, whether it be good or whether it be bad.

So a lot of times you need that correction. You understand? But through vicarious learning, a lot of even our people now see disciplining a child as abuse, as something that you're doing that, oh, that's too far. No, don't put your hands on my baby. No. The scriptures say spare the rod, spoil the child. Discipline is supposed to happen in a child's life. That's why the mothers often take the children away, especially the sons. Take the children away from their fathers, and then when they get too bad to handle, they try to send them back to their daddy. Ah, he too much for me. You take him now. What you mean? We tried to discipline him early on. You said that was too much. You got to set the right example from the jump.

- He that chastiseth his son shall have joy in him: when you chastise your son, you're going to have joy in him. Why? Because he's going to learn from your correction, adjust his behavior, and your friends gonna be like, Man, your children are really well behaved. You be like, Uh-huh. All praise to the Most High. Yep. You know why they well behaved. They know why they well behaved. Cuz you ain't playing that.

- He that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy: he that teacheth his son. When your son vicariously learns from you, that grieves the enemy. Why?

- for he hath left one behind him that is like himself: so if you were a prophet of God, and you was causing hell, you was prophesying the death and destruction of this country, the death and destruction of all the wicked, you was bringing out prophecies, you was hitting the streets, and your son turn right around and do the same thing you doing, that's going to grieve the enemy. The enemy is going to hate that. Like, nah, bro, we got to do something about this. His father just did it, and now he's following behind his father's footsteps. Why? That grieves the enemy because your son learned from you. And guess what? It'll grieve the enemy when your daughters learn from you as well. Well, she's not walking around in tight pants and revealing clothes. She's actually covering her body up. She's still a virgin. What the hell is going on? How the hell we going to have Planned Parenthood, nobody having sex? How we going to have an STD clinic and nobody's having sex? What's up with all of these families being started? These black families, I can't take it. Because we're teaching our children. They're learning vicariously through us. You understand?

She's going to be exactly how you are, whether you righteous or whether you wicked. Your children are going to be exactly how you are, whether you're righteous or whether you're wicked. Scriptures say that a man will be known in his children. Children are honest. You can't hide that.
