## Scriptures Opened

**[Leviticus 25:18-19](/bible/leviticus/25#v18)**  *[[0:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=36s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/25#v18)</sup> Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/25#v19)</sup> And the land shall yield her fruit, and ye shall eat your fill, and dwell therein in safety.

- Wherefore ye shall do my statutes: so the Lord said we must do his statutes.

- and keep my judgments, and do them: and keep his judgments, and do them.

- and ye shall dwell in the land in safety: so that was our key to dwelling or living in our own land in safety. But we didn't do the statutes. We didn't do the judgments. And what happened? We got kicked out.

- And the land shall yield her fruit: so if we kept those commandments, those statutes, those judgments, then the land would have been fruitful, right?

- and dwell therein in safety: so we would have been in safety, and the land would have been fruitful. Let's see what some of those commandments were that would have made the land fruitful, because we wanted the land to be fruitful, but we're not in the land no more. How will we become fruitful as the sons and daughters of God?


**[Leviticus 25:2-3](/bible/leviticus/25#v2)**  *[[1:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=100s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/25#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land which I give you, then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/25#v3)</sup> Six years thou shalt sow thy field, and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard, and gather in the fruit thereof;

- Speak unto the children of Israel: so these are the instructions that the Lord told Moses to give to us, the children of Israel, so-called blacks, Hispanics, natives here in America and scattered to the four corners of the earth. That's right.

- When ye come into the land which I give you: so this was to prepare us before we went into the land, so we didn't get caught off guard. Before we went in the land, Moses said, All right, do this, do that, don't do this, don't do that, and you're going to be good.

- then shall the land keep a sabbath unto the Lord: so the land had to keep a Sabbath. That's part of the land being fruitful. The land had to keep a Sabbath.

- Six years thou shalt sow thy field: so one of the things that we had to do, for 6 years we had to sow continuously.

- and six years thou shalt prune thy vineyard: and six years we also had to do what? We had to prune. So we can't just let everything grow on its own, get all wild. No, we got to prune it. We got to dress it. Got to keep it neat. Something start getting out of order, you got to chop that limb off. You got something that's growing and it's dead, the leaves is dying, you got to prune it back so that the nutrients coming up from the roots aren't going to places that are becoming unfruitful.

- and gather in the fruit thereof: so if we sow, and if we then prune, then we can gather. So the same way that applied in our land in times past, that also applies now in our spirits. So if we are to get the fruit of correction, then we must sow things in our lives in order to get that fruit. What is the thing that we must sow?


**[Mark 4:14](/bible/mark/4#v14)**  *[[3:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=226s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/mark/4#v14)</sup> The sower soweth the word.

- The sower soweth the word: so the sower is Christ. What is the word? The word is the word of God. How do we sow the word of God in our lives? Well, we got to pray at least three times a day. Scriptures say pray without ceasing, but there are three times a day, evening, morning, and noon, that we must pray. We must read our four chapters minimum per day. We got to keep the word of God constantly nourishing our spirits. We should be watching classes like this one, 15 minutes with the captains. If you miss the classes on the Sabbath, you should be going back throughout the week and watching those classes, reviewing your notes. We must constantly keep the word of God in our spirits. That's the first thing. Remember, it said you sow, then you prune, then you gather. Let's talk about the pruning, because the pruning brings us to our topic for today, the fruit of correction.


**[John 15:2](/bible/john/15#v2)**  *[[4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=290s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/john/15#v2)</sup> Every branch in me that beareth not fruit he taketh away: and every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit.

- Every branch in me that beareth not fruit he taketh away: so if you are unfruitful, it means you don't believe, or you a heathen. One of the two. The Lord is going to take you. He's going to get rid of you. You're only going to be here for a short period of time.

- and every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it: but if you bear fruit, what's going to happen? He purgeth it. What is the purging? It's the same that we read about in Leviticus 25. It's the pruning. It's the things that are done to us that may not feel good, but they're done for our benefit. They are done to make us better.

- that it may bring forth more fruit: so the purging is the correction, that it may bring forth more fruit. So when we are corrected, it's because there's certain things that are off in our spirit that we must get right. So the Lord is going to send men to correct you with the scriptures, dealing in the spirit of the Lord, to make you more fruitful. If you not getting any type of correction, you got to say, Damn, maybe I ain't a true Jew. I must not be one of God's sons. I must be a bastard. Because bastard children don't have a father there to correct them. Damn. Raised by their mother, doing whatever they want to do, not getting corrected. But if you really want to be a son or a daughter of the living God, you know what comes along with that? Correction. And a lot of times we get corrected, we get in our feelings. We get in our emotions. But we got to remember what the scripture says. The scripture says that the correction is intended to make us more fruitful. It's never meant to destroy you. It's only meant to make you better.


**[Hebrews 12:11](/bible/hebrews/12#v11)**  *[[7:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=424s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/12#v11)</sup> Now no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous: nevertheless afterward it yieldeth the peaceable fruit of righteousness unto them which are exercised thereby.

- Now no chastening for the present: correction is intended to make us better. But the only way you get better is when you're receiving the correction. You can't be emotional, and you got to understand the purpose of the correction. It says no chastening. What's another word for chastening? Purging. What's another word for purging that we read in Leviticus 25? Pruning. Getting rid of all the stuff that's no good for you.

- seemeth to be joyous: it don't feel good. Ain't nobody likes getting corrected. You don't like being told that this is wrong with you or that's wrong with you, or you did this wrong or you did that bad. That's not joyous.

- but grievous: it's grievous. Like, damn, I thought I had this under control. I didn't even realize I was dealing with that spirit.

- nevertheless afterward it yieldeth the peaceable fruit: but if you're open-minded and you're not emotional when you receive the correction, it yieldeth peaceable fruit. It makes you more fruitful. It makes you closer to being perfect. We want Christ to look in the mirror and see himself, and then look over at you and see a reflection of himself. But that does not happen without correction.

- of righteousness: peaceable fruit of righteousness. So that's what you got to understand, that correction is coming from a righteous place. It's coming from a brother or sister that loves you enough to tell you what you're doing wrong. Loves you enough to see an error in you and want you to correct yourself, fix yourself, so that you can be better. Cuz if you better, we better, cuz we all one body.

- unto them which are exercised thereby: and that's what you got to do. You got to be exercised in the correction. Y'all know how it is. If you don't go to the gym, that first time you go to the gym, you ain't been there in two years, you go do them squats, you going to have them noodle legs, man. Them legs is going to be sore. So we can't allow that to be the case for correction with us. We have to be exercised thereby. Remember, bodily exercise profiteth little. But the exercise that's going to bear more fruit is the chastening, the correction. And that's going to come from being around your leadership. Women, you got to be with your husbands. Husbands, you got to deal with your wives, right? Brothers, you need to be dealing with your leadership. You're not going to get that chastening from your peers. You're not going to get that chastening from the men that you set over. You going to get that from the higher ranking officers, the captains, the deacons, the bishops. But a lot of times brothers run from the correction because they running from their emotions. You in your feelings. You haven't been exercised thereby, so it's like foreign to you. That's why you're not in the gym, cuz you don't want your legs to be sore.


**[Psalms 118:18](/bible/psalms/118#v18)**  *[[10:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=633s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/118#v18)</sup> The Lord hath chastened me sore: but he hath not given me over unto death.

- The Lord hath chastened me sore: the fruit of correction comes through the exercise thereof. You got to go into it with the right spirit. You got to expect it. You got to get comfortable with it. You got to feel uncomfortable if you're not getting corrected. He what? The Lord chastened me sore. Why? Because that's the exercise. What happen when you exercise and you get sore? What happens when you get chastised and it ain't familiar to you? You sore. Your behind is sore because you didn't get that spanking. You didn't get that spiritual spanking to get your spirit right.

- but he hath not given me over unto death: that's what you got to realize. You ain't die. Ain't nobody about to kill you. We're not under the old covenant. The mistake that you made ain't going to cost you your life. God forbid. Lord willing, he spare your life. But the correction is just intended to make you better.


**[Psalms 141:5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)**  *[[11:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=708s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/141#v5)</sup> Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities.

- Let the righteous smite me: it don't necessarily going to feel good, but you got to know that it's for your good. This is what King David said. He said, Let the righteous smite me. Let the righteous correct me. Who was the righteous that corrected King David? The prophet Nathan corrected King David. Imagine if the prophet Nathan never corrected King David. We probably wouldn't be reading about him right now. Being able to take correction and improve yourself is what's going to separate the men from the boys. That's going to show between the great men and the mediocre men. King David was a great man, not only because of the great things that he did, but because he was able to take correction and fix himself when he went off.

- it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me. So the scripture says that correction shall be a kindness. The words may not have been kind. The words may not have been nice. They may not have felt good, but the peaceable fruit that came from it, it's like, damn, you know, that brother really loved me. That sister really loved me enough to tell me what's wrong when I didn't see it myself.

- which shall not break my head: it's not going to kill you. It's not going to bring death upon you. Correction is not the end of the world.


**[Sirach 20:1](/bible/sirach/20#v1)**  *[[13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=811s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/20#v1)</sup> There is a reproof that is not comely: again, some man holdeth his tongue, and he is wise.

- There is a reproof that is not comely: there's a correction that is not comely, that don't feel good. Remember, Hebrews told us that it was grievous. Reproof don't feel good.


**[Proverbs 15:10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)**  *[[13:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=838s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)</sup> Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die.

- Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: if you don't want correction, if you're not exercised in correction, the Bible tells you something that's in your spirit. That's the only person that don't want to be corrected. That's the brother that's emotional and don't want to apply the correction. Somebody that has forsaken the way. Don't let that be you, brother. Don't let that be you, sister. Take the correction, get better, cuz that's the reason that it was given. So remember, for the land to be fruitful, it must have been sown. It must have been pruned, and then it can be gathered. If we want to be gathered, the scripture says we must sow. Allow the sower to sow the word, which is the word of God. But we must be pruned, meaning all the bad, the evil that's in us, we must accept the correction so that we can become more fruitful. And if we want to be gathered, we must apply the correction and the counsel.


**[Matthew 13:30](/bible/matthew/13#v30)**  *[[15:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4XZbXWL7w0&t=913s)]*

> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/13#v30)</sup> Let both grow together until the harvest: and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather ye together first the tares, and bind them in bundles to burn them: but gather the wheat into my barn.

- Let both grow together until the harvest: this is what the gathering looks like if we accept the correction. The harvest is the end of the world. Well, what led up to the harvest? Well, in Leviticus 25, there was six years of sowing. There was six years of pruning. And then at the end of that, there's a harvest. What is the harvest for us? It's the kingdom of heaven. It's when the angels come to gather us up. They take us to the wilderness. What happens after the wilderness? We go and our kingdom is rebuilt for us. That's what we want. But we can't get to the harvest if sowing didn't happen, if pruning didn't happen. The correction had to happen to get us to the harvest.

- and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers: the reapers are the angels. They going to come in the midst of World War II, when that last trumpet blow.

- Gather ye together first the tares: when it's time for the harvest, what's going to happen? First the tares. You don't want to be a tare. A tare is one of two categories. Number one, you a heathen and we thought you was Israel. Or number two, you Israel and you didn't want to take the correction, so you got choked out by the tares and you turned into a tare.

- and bind them in bundles to burn them: brothers and sisters, don't let that be you. You cannot correct the tare, because either it's of a heathen nation or you got an evil spirit on you.

- but gather the wheat into my barn: that's the righteous brothers and sisters that allowed the word of God to be sown in them. And they allowed the righteous to smite them with correction. They allowed the Lord to chastise them, and they knew that it wouldn't break their head. It would only yield peaceable fruit that would lead us to the kingdom of heaven.
