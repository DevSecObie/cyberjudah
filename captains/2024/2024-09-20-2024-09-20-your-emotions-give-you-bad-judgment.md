## Introduction

*[[0:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hf0KDz7Iuac&t=15s)]* Hey, shalom Israel. Most High, Christ bless. This Captain Lemuel. This is 15 minutes with the captains. I'm touching on: never let your emotions cloud your judgment. And we going to key off of Ephesians 4:26. The point is, never allow your emotions to get the best of you, to the point where you start to make life-altering decisions. A lot of what we've seen is men let their emotions get the best of them, and what do they do? They start to beat their kids. They start to beat their wife. They get in fights with the men around them. Sisters start to put their hands on their husbands. So we have to understand that in the process of repentance, we can never let our emotions cloud our judgment.

- which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts: Which is what? Which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts. You change your mind, you change your speech. You reform your heart, which is your mind, you will reform your mouth. Reform your mind, reform your mouth.

- And be renewed in the spirit of your mind: See that? When you reform your mind, you'll reform your mouth. 'Cause remember what James said in James chapter 3, when he talked about the mouth being a fire, a world of iniquity. That's why Paul said when you change your mind, you'll change your mouth, or change your speech, your conversation. Because a lot of arguments start off where verbally we start to verbally abuse each other. We start to, evil for evil, railing for railing. These verbal conflicts start, then they turn physical. That's why Paul said you must reform your mind and you'll reform your mouth, or your speech.

- And that ye put on the new man: When you reform your mind, you start to put on the new man.

- which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness: Which is created in righteousness and true holiness.

- Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour: Notice how this is going into your speech, your former conversation. It says what? Speak every man truth to his neighbor. It says, wherefore put away lying. Lying happens through what? Your mouth. Your speech. That's why Paul said you must conform your conversation. Change your conversation. It says what? Wherefore put away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbor. You must speak truth to your neighbor.

- for we are members one of another: Because we're members one of another. You're my brother. You're my sister. We're members one of another. So me lying to you gets us nowhere. That doesn't get us to the end result. That doesn't get us to the next step. That doesn't get us to the end goal.

- Be ye angry, and sin not: So now here's the point. Be ye angry and sin not. This is the point of the title: never let your emotions cloud your judgment. Through emotion, our judgment, our right judgment, gets clouded, gets unclear. So you can be angry. A brother or sister lies to you, your wife lies to you, your husband lies to you, just dealing with the scripture above. Okay, you can be angry. You can be mad. Does that mean you go and punch him in the face? No. It says what? Be ye angry and sin not. Does that mean you go and punch him in the mouth? You push him? You put your hands on them? No. It says what? Be ye angry and sin not. Your child lies to you. Does that mean you go and you put black marks on their back, red marks and black bruises on their back? No. It says what? Be ye angry and sin not.

- let not the sun go down upon your wrath: Let's see what that part means. Let not the sun go down upon your wrath. It don't mean don't go to bed angry. Don't let the sun go down on your wrath, meaning before the sun goes down, make sure that you get over whatever it is. No, that's not what it's talking about. Let's see what that sun is.
