# 1 Corinthians 12

1 Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant. ^v1

2 Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led. ^v2

3 Wherefore I give you to understand, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed: and that no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost. ^v3

4 Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit. ^v4

5 And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord. ^v5

6 And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all. ^v6

7 But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal. ^v7

8 For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; ^v8

9 To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit; ^v9

10 To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues: ^v10

11 But all these worketh that one and the selfsame Spirit, dividing to every man severally as he will. ^v11

12 For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ. ^v12

13 For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit. ^v13

14 For the body is not one member, but many. ^v14

15 If the foot shall say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body? ^v15

16 And if the ear shall say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body? ^v16

17 If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smelling? ^v17

18 But now hath God set the members every one of them in the body, as it hath pleased him. ^v18

19 And if they were all one member, where were the body? ^v19

20 But now are they many members, yet but one body. ^v20

21 And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you. ^v21

22 Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary: ^v22

23 And those members of the body, which we think to be less honourable, upon these we bestow more abundant honour; and our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness. ^v23

24 For our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked: ^v24

25 That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another. ^v25

26 And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it. ^v26

27 Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular. ^v27

28 And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues. ^v28

29 Are all apostles? are all prophets? are all teachers? are all workers of miracles? ^v29

30 Have all the gifts of healing? do all speak with tongues? do all interpret? ^v30

31 But covet earnestly the best gifts: and yet shew I unto you a more excellent way. ^v31
