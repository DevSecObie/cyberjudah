# Numbers 28

1 And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying, ^v1

2 Command the children of Israel, and say unto them, My offering, and my bread for my sacrifices made by fire, for a sweet savour unto me, shall ye observe to offer unto me in their due season. ^v2

3 And thou shalt say unto them, This is the offering made by fire which ye shall offer unto the Lord; two lambs of the first year without spot day by day, for a continual burnt offering. ^v3

4 The one lamb shalt thou offer in the morning, and the other lamb shalt thou offer at even; ^v4

5 And a tenth part of an ephah of flour for a meat offering, mingled with the fourth part of an hin of beaten oil. ^v5

6 It is a continual burnt offering, which was ordained in mount Sinai for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord. ^v6

7 And the drink offering thereof shall be the fourth part of an hin for the one lamb: in the holy place shalt thou cause the strong wine to be poured unto the Lord for a drink offering. ^v7

8 And the other lamb shalt thou offer at even: as the meat offering of the morning, and as the drink offering thereof, thou shalt offer it, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord. ^v8

9 And on the sabbath day two lambs of the first year without spot, and two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, and the drink offering thereof: ^v9

10 This is the burnt offering of every sabbath, beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering. ^v10

11 And in the beginnings of your months ye shall offer a burnt offering unto the Lord; two young bullocks, and one ram, seven lambs of the first year without spot; ^v11

12 And three tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, for one bullock; and two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, for one ram; ^v12

13 And a several tenth deal of flour mingled with oil for a meat offering unto one lamb; for a burnt offering of a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord. ^v13

14 And their drink offerings shall be half an hin of wine unto a bullock, and the third part of an hin unto a ram, and a fourth part of an hin unto a lamb: this is the burnt offering of every month throughout the months of the year. ^v14

15 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering unto the Lord shall be offered, beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering. ^v15

16 And in the fourteenth day of the first month is the passover of the Lord. ^v16

17 And in the fifteenth day of this month is the feast: seven days shall unleavened bread be eaten. ^v17

18 In the first day shall be an holy convocation; ye shall do no manner of servile work therein: ^v18

19 But ye shall offer a sacrifice made by fire for a burnt offering unto the Lord; two young bullocks, and one ram, and seven lambs of the first year: they shall be unto you without blemish: ^v19

20 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil: three tenth deals shall ye offer for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram; ^v20

21 A several tenth deal shalt thou offer for every lamb, throughout the seven lambs: ^v21

22 And one goat for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you. ^v22

23 Ye shall offer these beside the burnt offering in the morning, which is for a continual burnt offering. ^v23

24 After this manner ye shall offer daily, throughout the seven days, the meat of the sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord: it shall be offered beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering. ^v24

25 And on the seventh day ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work. ^v25

26 Also in the day of the firstfruits, when ye bring a new meat offering unto the Lord, after your weeks be out, ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work: ^v26

27 But ye shall offer the burnt offering for a sweet savour unto the Lord; two young bullocks, one ram, seven lambs of the first year; ^v27

28 And their meat offering of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals unto one bullock, two tenth deals unto one ram, ^v28

29 A several tenth deal unto one lamb, throughout the seven lambs; ^v29

30 And one kid of the goats, to make an atonement for you. ^v30

31 Ye shall offer them beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, (they shall be unto you without blemish) and their drink offerings. ^v31
