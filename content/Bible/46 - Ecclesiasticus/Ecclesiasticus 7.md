# Ecclesiasticus 7

1 Do no evil, so shall no harm come unto thee. ^v1

2 Depart from the unjust, and iniquity shall turn away from thee. ^v2

3 My son, sow not upon the furrows of unrighteousness, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold. ^v3

4 Seek not of the Lord preeminence, neither of the king the seat of honour. ^v4

5 justify not thyself before the Lord; and boast not of thy wisdom before the king. ^v5

6 Seek not to be judge, being not able to take away iniquity; lest at any time thou fear the person of the mighty, an stumblingblock in the way of thy uprightness. ^v6

7 Offend not against the multitude of a city, and then thou shalt not cast thyself down among the people. ^v7

8 Bind not one sin upon another; for in one thou shalt not be unpunished. ^v8

9 Say not, God will look upon the multitude of my oblations, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept it. ^v9

10 Be not fainthearted when thou makest thy prayer, and neglect not to give alms. ^v10

11 Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one which humbleth and exalteth. ^v11

12 Devise not a lie against thy brother; neither do the like to thy friend. ^v12

13 Use not to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good. ^v13

14 Use not many words in a multitude of elders, and make not much babbling when thou prayest. ^v14

15 Hate not laborious work, neither husbandry, which the most High hath ordained. ^v15

16 Number not thyself among the multitude of sinners, but remember that wrath will not tarry long. ^v16

17 Humble thyself greatly: for the vengeance of the ungodly is fire and worms. ^v17

18 Change not a friend for any good by no means; neither a faithful brother for the gold of Ophir. ^v18

19 Forego not a wise and good woman: for her grace is above gold. ^v19

20 Whereas thy servant worketh truly, entreat him not evil. nor the hireling that bestoweth himself wholly for thee. ^v20

21 Let thy soul love a good servant, and defraud him not of liberty. ^v21

22 Hast thou cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for thy profit, keep them with thee. ^v22

23 Hast thou children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their youth. ^v23

24 Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them. ^v24

25 Marry thy daughter, and so shalt thou have performed a weighty matter: but give her to a man of understanding. ^v25

26 Hast thou a wife after thy mind? forsake her not: but give not thyself over to a light woman. ^v26

27 Honour thy father with thy whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of thy mother. ^v27

28 Remember that thou wast begotten of them; and how canst thou recompense them the things that they have done for thee? ^v28

29 Fear the Lord with all thy soul, and reverence his priests. ^v29

30 Love him that made thee with all thy strength, and forsake not his ministers. ^v30

31 Fear the Lord, and honor the priest; and give him his portion, as it is commanded thee; the firstfruits, and the trespass offering, and the gift of the shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things. ^v31

32 And stretch thine hand unto the poor, that thy blessing may be perfected. ^v32

33 A gift hath grace in the sight of every man living; and for the dead detain it not. ^v33

34 Fail not to be with them that weep, and mourn with them that mourn. ^v34

35 Be not slow to visit the sick: for that shall make thee to be beloved. ^v35

36 Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss. ^v36
