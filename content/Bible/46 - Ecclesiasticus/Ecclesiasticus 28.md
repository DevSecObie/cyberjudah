# Ecclesiasticus 28

1 He that revengeth shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance. ^v1

2 Forgive thy neighbour the hurt that he hath done unto thee, so shall thy sins also be forgiven when thou prayest. ^v2

3 One man beareth hatred against another, and doth he seek pardon from the Lord? ^v3

4 He sheweth no mercy to a man, which is like himself: and doth he ask forgiveness of his own sins? ^v4

5 If he that is but flesh nourish hatred, who will intreat for pardon of his sins? ^v5

6 Remember thy end, and let enmity cease; remember corruption and death, and abide in the commandments. ^v6

7 Remember the commandments, and bear no malice to thy neighbour: remember the covenant of the Highest, and wink at ignorance. ^v7

8 Abstain from strife, and thou shalt diminish thy sins: for a furious man will kindle strife, ^v8

9 A sinful man disquieteth friends, and maketh debate among them that be at peace. ^v9

10 As the matter of the fire is, so it burneth: and as a man’s strength is, so is his wrath; and according to his riches his anger riseth; and the stronger they are which contend, the more they will be inflamed. ^v10

11 An hasty contention kindleth a fire: and an hasty fighting sheddeth blood. ^v11

12 If thou blow the spark, it shall burn: if thou spit upon it, it shall be quenched: and both these come out of thy mouth. ^v12

13 Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace. ^v13

14 A backbiting tongue hath disquieted many, and driven them from nation to nation: strong cities hath it pulled down, and overthrown the houses of great men. ^v14

15 A backbiting tongue hath cast out virtuous women, and deprived them of their labours. ^v15

16 Whoso hearkeneth unto it shall never find rest, and never dwell quietly. ^v16

17 The stroke of the whip maketh marks in the flesh: but the stroke of the tongue breaketh the bones. ^v17

18 Many have fallen by the edge of the sword: but not so many as have fallen by the tongue. ^v18

19 Well is he that is defended through the venom thereof; who hath not drawn the yoke thereof, nor hath been bound in her bands. ^v19

20 For the yoke thereof is a yoke of iron, and the bands thereof are bands of brass. ^v20

21 The death thereof is an evil death, the grave were better than it. ^v21

22 It shall not have rule over them that fear God, neither shall they be burned with the flame thereof. ^v22

23 Such as forsake the Lord shall fall into it; and it shall burn in them, and not be quenched; it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and devour them as a leopard. ^v23

24 Look that thou hedge thy possession about with thorns, and bind up thy silver and gold, ^v24

25 And weigh thy words in a balance, and make a door and bar for thy mouth. ^v25

26 Beware thou slide not by it, lest thou fall before him that lieth in wait. ^v26
