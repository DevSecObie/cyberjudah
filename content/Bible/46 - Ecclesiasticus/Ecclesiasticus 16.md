# Ecclesiasticus 16

1 Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons. ^v1

2 Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them. ^v2

3 Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly. ^v3

4 For by one that hath understanding shall the city be replenished: but the kindred of the wicked shall speedily become desolate. ^v4

5 Many such things have I seen with mine eyes, and mine ear hath heard greater things than these. ^v5

6 In the congregation of the ungodly shall a fire be kindled; and in a rebellious nation wrath is set on fire. ^v6

7 He was not pacified toward the old giants, who fell away in the strength of their foolishness. ^v7

8 Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for their pride. ^v8

9 He pitied not the people of perdition, who were taken away in their sins: ^v9

10 Nor the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts. ^v10

11 And if there be one stiffnecked among the people, it is marvel if he escape unpunished: for mercy and wrath are with him; he is mighty to forgive, and to pour out displeasure. ^v11

12 As his mercy is great, so is his correction also: he judgeth a man according to his works ^v12

13 The sinner shall not escape with his spoils: and the patience of the godly shall not be frustrate. ^v13

14 Make way for every work of mercy: for every man shall find according to his works. ^v14

15 The Lord hardened Pharaoh, that he should not know him, that his powerful works might be known to the world. ^v15

16 His mercy is manifest to every creature; and he hath separated his light from the darkness with an adamant. ^v16

17 Say not thou, I will hide myself from the Lord: shall any remember me from above? I shall not be remembered among so many people: for what is my soul among such an infinite number of creatures? ^v17

18 Behold, the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, and all that therein is, shall be moved when he shall visit. ^v18

19 The mountains also and foundations of the earth be shaken with trembling, when the Lord looketh upon them. ^v19

20 No heart can think upon these things worthily: and who is able to conceive his ways? ^v20

21 It is a tempest which no man can see: for the most part of his works are hid. ^v21

22 Who can declare the works of his justice? or who can endure them? for his covenant is afar off, and the trial of all things is in the end. ^v22

23 He that wanteth understanding will think upon vain things: and a foolish man erring imagineth follies. ^v23

24 by son, hearken unto me, and learn knowledge, and mark my words with thy heart. ^v24

25 I will shew forth doctrine in weight, and declare his knowledge exactly. ^v25

26 The works of the Lord are done in judgment from the beginning: and from the time he made them he disposed the parts thereof. ^v26

27 He garnished his works for ever, and in his hand are the chief of them unto all generations: they neither labour, nor are weary, nor cease from their works. ^v27

28 None of them hindereth another, and they shall never disobey his word. ^v28

29 After this the Lord looked upon the earth, and filled it with his blessings. ^v29

30 With all manner of living things hath he covered the face thereof; and they shall return into it again. ^v30
