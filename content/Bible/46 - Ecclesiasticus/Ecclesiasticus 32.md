# Ecclesiasticus 32

1 If thou be made the master of a feast, lift not thyself up, but be among them as one of the rest; take diligent care for them, and so sit down. ^v1

2 And when thou hast done all thy office, take thy place, that thou mayest be merry with them, and receive a crown for thy well ordering of the feast. ^v2

3 Speak, thou that art the elder, for it becometh thee, but with sound judgment; and hinder not musick. ^v3

4 Pour not out words where there is a musician, and shew not forth wisdom out of time. ^v4

5 A concert of musick in a banquet of wine is as a signet of carbuncle set in gold. ^v5

6 As a signet of an emerald set in a work of gold, so is the melody of musick with pleasant wine. ^v6

7 Speak, young man, if there be need of thee: and yet scarcely when thou art twice asked. ^v7

8 Let thy speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one that knoweth and yet holdeth his tongue. ^v8

9 If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words. ^v9

10 Before the thunder goeth lightning; and before a shamefaced man shall go favour. ^v10

11 Rise up betimes, and be not the last; but get thee home without delay. ^v11

12 There take thy pastime, and do what thou wilt: but sin not by proud speech. ^v12

13 And for these things bless him that made thee, and hath replenished thee with his good things. ^v13

14 Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favour. ^v14

15 He that seeketh the law shall be filled therewith: but the hypocrite will be offended thereat. ^v15

16 They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light. ^v16

17 A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will. ^v17

18 A man of counsel will be considerate; but a strange and proud man is not daunted with fear, even when of himself he hath done without counsel. ^v18

19 Do nothing without advice; and when thou hast once done, repent not. ^v19

20 Go not in a way wherein thou mayest fall, and stumble not among the stones. ^v20

21 Be not confident in a plain way. ^v21

22 And beware of thine own children. ^v22

23 In every good work trust thy own soul; for this is the keeping of the commandments. ^v23

24 He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse. ^v24
