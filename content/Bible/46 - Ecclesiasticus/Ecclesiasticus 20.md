# Ecclesiasticus 20

1 There is a reproof that is not comely: again, some man holdeth his tongue, and he is wise. ^v1

2 It is much better to reprove, than to be angry secretly: and he that confesseth his fault shall be preserved from hurt. ^v2

3 How good is it, when thou art reproved, to shew repentance! for so shalt thou escape wilful sin. ^v3

4 As is the lust of an eunuch to deflower a virgin; so is he that executeth judgment with violence. ^v4

5 There is one that keepeth silence, and is found wise: and another by much babbling becometh hateful. ^v5

6 Some man holdeth his tongue, because he hath not to answer: and some keepeth silence, knowing his time. ^v6

7 A wise man will hold his tongue till he see opportunity: but a babbler and a fool will regard no time. ^v7

8 He that useth many words shall be abhorred; and he that taketh to himself authority therein shall be hated. ^v8

9 There is a sinner that hath good success in evil things; and there is a gain that turneth to loss. ^v9

10 There is a gift that shall not profit thee; and there is a gift whose recompence is double. ^v10

11 There is an abasement because of glory; and there is that lifteth up his head from a low estate. ^v11

12 There is that buyeth much for a little, and repayeth it sevenfold. ^v12

13 A wise man by his words maketh him beloved: but the graces of fools shall be poured out. ^v13

14 The gift of a fool shall do thee no good when thou hast it; neither yet of the envious for his necessity: for he looketh to receive many things for one. ^v14

15 He giveth little, and upbraideth much; he openeth his mouth like a crier; to day he lendeth, and to morrow will he ask it again: such an one is to be hated of God and man. ^v15

16 The fool saith, I have no friends, I have no thank for all my good deeds, and they that eat my bread speak evil of me. ^v16

17 How oft, and of how many shall he be laughed to scorn! for he knoweth not aright what it is to have; and it is all one unto him as if he had it not. ^v17

18 To slip upon a pavement is better than to slip with the tongue: so the fall of the wicked shall come speedily. ^v18

19 An unseasonable tale will always be in the mouth of the unwise. ^v19

20 A wise sentence shall be rejected when it cometh out of a fool’s mouth; for he will not speak it in due season. ^v20

21 There is that is hindered from sinning through want: and when he taketh rest, he shall not be troubled. ^v21

22 There is that destroyeth his own soul through bashfulness, and by accepting of persons overthroweth himself. ^v22

23 There is that for bashfulness promiseth to his friend, and maketh him his enemy for nothing. ^v23

24 A lie is a foul blot in a man, yet it is continually in the mouth of the untaught. ^v24

25 A thief is better than a man that is accustomed to lie: but they both shall have destruction to heritage. ^v25

26 The disposition of a liar is dishonourable, and his shame is ever with him. ^v26

27 A wise man shall promote himself to honour with his words: and he that hath understanding will please great men. ^v27

28 He that tilleth his land shall increase his heap: and he that pleaseth great men shall get pardon for iniquity. ^v28

29 Presents and gifts blind the eyes of the wise, and stop up his mouth that he cannot reprove. ^v29

30 Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is hoarded up, what profit is in them both? ^v30

31 Better is he that hideth his folly than a man that hideth his wisdom. ^v31

32 Necessary patience in seeking the Lord is better than he that leadeth his life without a guide. ^v32
