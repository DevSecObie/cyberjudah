# Acts 2

1 And when the day of Pentecost was fully come, they were all with one accord in one place. ^v1

2 And suddenly there came a sound from heaven as of a rushing mighty wind, and it filled all the house where they were sitting. ^v2

3 And there appeared unto them cloven tongues like as of fire, and it sat upon each of them. ^v3

4 And they were all filled with the Holy Ghost, and began to speak with other tongues, as the Spirit gave them utterance. ^v4

5 And there were dwelling at Jerusalem Jews, devout men, out of every nation under heaven. ^v5

6 Now when this was noised abroad, the multitude came together, and were confounded, because that every man heard them speak in his own language. ^v6

7 And they were all amazed and marvelled, saying one to another, Behold, are not all these which speak Galileans? ^v7

8 And how hear we every man in our own tongue, wherein we were born? ^v8

9 Parthians, and Medes, and Elamites, and the dwellers in Mesopotamia, and in Judea, and Cappadocia, in Pontus, and Asia, ^v9

10 Phrygia, and Pamphylia, in Egypt, and in the parts of Libya about Cyrene, and strangers of Rome, Jews and proselytes, ^v10

11 Cretes and Arabians, we do hear them speak in our tongues the wonderful works of God. ^v11

12 And they were all amazed, and were in doubt, saying one to another, What meaneth this? ^v12

13 Others mocking said, These men are full of new wine. ^v13

14 But Peter, standing up with the eleven, lifted up his voice, and said unto them, Ye men of Judea, and all ye that dwell at Jerusalem, be this known unto you, and hearken to my words: ^v14

15 For these are not drunken, as ye suppose, seeing it is but the third hour of the day. ^v15

16 But this is that which was spoken by the prophet Joel; ^v16

17 And it shall come to pass in the last days, saith God, I will pour out of my Spirit upon all flesh: and your sons and your daughters shall prophesy, and your young men shall see visions, and your old men shall dream dreams: ^v17

18 And on my servants and on my handmaidens I will pour out in those days of my Spirit; and they shall prophesy: ^v18

19 And I will shew wonders in heaven above, and signs in the earth beneath; blood, and fire, and vapour of smoke: ^v19

20 The sun shall be turned into darkness, and the moon into blood, before that great and notable day of the Lord come: ^v20

21 And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved. ^v21

22 Ye men of Israel, hear these words; Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs, which God did by him in the midst of you, as ye yourselves also know: ^v22

23 Him, being delivered by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of God, ye have taken, and by wicked hands have crucified and slain: ^v23

24 Whom God hath raised up, having loosed the pains of death: because it was not possible that he should be holden of it. ^v24

25 For David speaketh concerning him, I foresaw the Lord always before my face, for he is on my right hand, that I should not be moved: ^v25

26 Therefore did my heart rejoice, and my tongue was glad; moreover also my flesh shall rest in hope: ^v26

27 Because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption. ^v27

28 Thou hast made known to me the ways of life; thou shalt make me full of joy with thy countenance. ^v28

29 Men and brethren, let me freely speak unto you of the patriarch David, that he is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day. ^v29

30 Therefore being a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him, that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne; ^v30

31 He seeing this before spake of the resurrection of Christ, that his soul was not left in hell, neither his flesh did see corruption. ^v31

32 This Jesus hath God raised up, whereof we all are witnesses. ^v32

33 Therefore being by the right hand of God exalted, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Ghost, he hath shed forth this, which ye now see and hear. ^v33

34 For David is not ascended into the heavens: but he saith himself, The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, ^v34

35 Until I make thy foes thy footstool. ^v35

36 Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ. ^v36

37 Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men and brethren, what shall we do? ^v37

38 Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost. ^v38

39 For the promise is unto you, and to your children, and to all that are afar off, even as many as the Lord our God shall call. ^v39

40 And with many other words did he testify and exhort, saying, Save yourselves from this untoward generation. ^v40

41 Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls. ^v41

42 And they continued stedfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers. ^v42

43 And fear came upon every soul: and many wonders and signs were done by the apostles. ^v43

44 And all that believed were together, and had all things common; ^v44

45 And sold their possessions and goods, and parted them to all men, as every man had need. ^v45

46 And they, continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, did eat their meat with gladness and singleness of heart, ^v46

47 Praising God, and having favour with all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily such as should be saved. ^v47
