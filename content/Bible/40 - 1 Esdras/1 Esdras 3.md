# 1 Esdras 3

1 Now when Darius reigned, he made a great feast unto all his subjects, and unto all his household, and unto all the princes of Media and Persia, ^v1

2 And to all the governors and captains and lieutenants that were under him, from India unto Ethiopia, of an hundred twenty and seven provinces. ^v2

3 And when they had eaten and drunken, and being satisfied were gone home, then Darius the king went into his bedchamber, and slept, and soon after awaked. ^v3

4 Then three young men, that were of the guard that kept the king’s body, spake one to another; ^v4

5 Let every one of us speak a sentence: he that shall overcome, and whose sentence shall seem wiser than the others, unto him shall the king Darius give great gifts, and great things in token of victory: ^v5

6 As, to be clothed in purple, to drink in gold, and to sleep upon gold, and a chariot with bridles of gold, and an headtire of fine linen, and a chain about his neck: ^v6

7 And he shall sit next to Darius because of his wisdom, and shall be called Darius his cousin. ^v7

8 And then every one wrote his sentence, sealed it, and laid it under king Darius his pillow; ^v8

9 And said that, when the king is risen, some will give him the writings; and of whose side the king and the three princes of Persia shall judge that his sentence is the wisest, to him shall the victory be given, as was appointed. ^v9

10 The first wrote, Wine is the strongest. ^v10

11 The second wrote, The king is strongest. ^v11

12 The third wrote, Women are strongest: but above all things Truth beareth away the victory. ^v12

13 Now when the king was risen up, they took their writings, and delivered them unto him, and so he read them: ^v13

14 And sending forth he called all the princes of Persia and Media, and the governors, and the captains, and the lieutenants, and the chief officers; ^v14

15 And sat him down in the royal seat of judgment; and the writings were read before them. ^v15

16 And he said, Call the young men, and they shall declare their own sentences. So they were called, and came in. ^v16

17 And he said unto them, Declare unto us your mind concerning the writings. Then began the first, who had spoken of the strength of wine; ^v17

18 And he said thus, O ye men, how exceeding strong is wine! it causeth all men to err that drink it: ^v18

19 It maketh the mind of the king and of the fatherless child to be all one; of the bondman and of the freeman, of the poor man and of the rich: ^v19

20 It turneth also every thought into jollity and mirth, so that a man remembereth neither sorrow nor debt: ^v20

21 And it maketh every heart rich, so that a man remembereth neither king nor governor; and it maketh to speak all things by talents: ^v21

22 And when they are in their cups, they forget their love both to friends and brethren, and a little after draw out swords: ^v22

23 But when they are from the wine, they remember not what they have done. ^v23

24 O ye men, is not wine the strongest, that enforceth to do thus? And when he had so spoken, he held his peace. ^v24
