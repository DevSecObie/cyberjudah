# Wisdom of Solomon 1

1 Love righteousness, ye that be judges of the earth: think of the Lord with a good (heart,) and in simplicity of heart seek him. ^v1

2 For he will be found of them that tempt him not; and sheweth himself unto such as do not distrust him. ^v2

3 For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise. ^v3

4 For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; nor dwell in the body that is subject unto sin. ^v4

5 For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in. ^v5

6 For wisdom is a loving spirit; and will not acquit a blasphemer of his words: for God is witness of his reins, and a true beholder of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue. ^v6

7 For the Spirit of the Lord filleth the world: and that which containeth all things hath knowledge of the voice. ^v7

8 Therefore he that speaketh unrighteous things cannot be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punisheth, pass by him. ^v8

9 For inquisition shall be made into the counsels of the ungodly: and the sound of his words shall come unto the Lord for the manifestation of his wicked deeds. ^v9

10 For the ear of jealousy heareth all things: and the noise of murmurings is not hid. ^v10

11 Therefore beware of murmuring, which is unprofitable; and refrain your tongue from backbiting: for there is no word so secret, that shall go for nought: and the mouth that belieth slayeth the soul. ^v11

12 Seek not death in the error of your life: and pull not upon yourselves destruction with the works of your hands. ^v12

13 For God made not death: neither hath he pleasure in the destruction of the living. ^v13

14 For he created all things, that they might have their being: and the generations of the world were healthful; and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor the kingdom of death upon the earth: ^v14

15 (For righteousness is immortal:) ^v15

16 But ungodly men with their works and words called it to them: for when they thought to have it their friend, they consumed to nought, and made a covenant with it, because they are worthy to take part with it. ^v16
