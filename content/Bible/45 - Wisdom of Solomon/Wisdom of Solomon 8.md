# Wisdom of Solomon 8

1 Wisdom reacheth from one end to another mightily: and sweetly doth she order all things. ^v1

2 I loved her, and sought her out from my youth, I desired to make her my spouse, and I was a lover of her beauty. ^v2

3 In that she is conversant with God, she magnifieth her nobility: yea, the Lord of all things himself loved her. ^v3

4 For she is privy to the mysteries of the knowledge of God, and a lover of his works. ^v4

5 If riches be a possession to be desired in this life; what is richer than wisdom, that worketh all things? ^v5

6 And if prudence work; who of all that are is a more cunning workman than she? ^v6

7 And if a man love righteousness her labours are virtues: for she teacheth temperance and prudence, justice and fortitude: which are such things, as men can have nothing more profitable in their life. ^v7

8 If a man desire much experience, she knoweth things of old, and conjectureth aright what is to come: she knoweth the subtilties of speeches, and can expound dark sentences: she foreseeth signs and wonders, and the events of seasons and times. ^v8

9 Therefore I purposed to take her to me to live with me, knowing that she would be a counsellor of good things, and a comfort in cares and grief. ^v9

10 For her sake I shall have estimation among the multitude, and honour with the elders, though I be young. ^v10

11 I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgment, and shall be admired in the sight of great men. ^v11

12 When I hold my tongue, they shall bide my leisure, and when I speak, they shall give good ear unto me: if I talk much, they shall lay their hands upon their mouth. ^v12

13 Moreover by the means of her I shall obtain immortality, and leave behind me an everlasting memorial to them that come after me. ^v13

14 I shall set the people in order, and the nations shall be subject unto me. ^v14

15 Horrible tyrants shall be afraid, when they do but hear of me; I shall be found good among the multitude, and valiant in war. ^v15

16 After I am come into mine house, I will repose myself with her: for her conversation hath no bitterness; and to live with her hath no sorrow, but mirth and joy. ^v16

17 Now when I considered these things in myself, and pondered them in my heart, how that to be allied unto wisdom is immortality; ^v17

18 And great pleasure it is to have her friendship; and in the works of her hands are infinite riches; and in the exercise of conference with her, prudence; and in talking with her, a good report; I went about seeking how to take her to me. ^v18

19 For I was a witty child, and had a good spirit. ^v19

20 Yea rather, being good, I came into a body undefiled. ^v20
