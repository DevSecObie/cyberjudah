# Wisdom of Solomon 6

1 Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth. ^v1

2 Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations. ^v2

3 For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels. ^v3

4 Because, being ministers of his kingdom, ye have not judged aright, nor kept the law, nor walked after the counsel of God; ^v4

5 Horribly and speedily shall he come upon you: for a sharp judgment shall be to them that be in high places. ^v5

6 For mercy will soon pardon the meanest: but mighty men shall be mightily tormented. ^v6

7 For he which is Lord over all shall fear no man’s person, neither shall he stand in awe of any man’s greatness: for he hath made the small and great, and careth for all alike. ^v7

8 But a sore trial shall come upon the mighty. ^v8

9 Unto you therefore, O kings, do I speak, that ye may learn wisdom, and not fall away. ^v9

10 For they that keep holiness holily shall be judged holy: and they that have learned such things shall find what to answer. ^v10

11 Wherefore set your affection upon my words; desire them, and ye shall be instructed. ^v11

12 Wisdom is glorious, and never fadeth away: yea, she is easily seen of them that love her, and found of such as seek her. ^v12

13 She preventeth them that desire her, in making herself first known unto them. ^v13

14 Whoso seeketh her early shall have no great travail: for he shall find her sitting at his doors. ^v14

15 To think therefore upon her is perfection of wisdom: and whoso watcheth for her shall quickly be without care. ^v15

16 For she goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her, sheweth herself favourably unto them in the ways, and meeteth them in every thought. ^v16

17 For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love; ^v17

18 And love is the keeping of her laws; and the giving heed unto her laws is the assurance of incorruption; ^v18

19 And incorruption maketh us near unto God: ^v19

20 Therefore the desire of wisdom bringeth to a kingdom. ^v20

21 If your delight be then in thrones and sceptres, O ye kings of the people, honour wisdom, that ye may reign for evermore. ^v21

22 As for wisdom, what she is, and how she came up, I will tell you, and will not hide mysteries from you: but will seek her out from the beginning of her nativity, and bring the knowledge of her into light, and will not pass over the truth. ^v22

23 Neither will I go with consuming envy; for such a man shall have no fellowship with wisdom. ^v23

24 But the multitude of the wise is the welfare of the world: and a wise king is the upholding of the people. ^v24

25 Receive therefore instruction through my words, and it shall do you good. ^v25
