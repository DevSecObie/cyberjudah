# Proverbs 9

1 Wisdom hath builded her house, she hath hewn out her seven pillars: ^v1

2 She hath killed her beasts; she hath mingled her wine; she hath also furnished her table. ^v2

3 She hath sent forth her maidens: she crieth upon the highest places of the city, ^v3

4 Whoso is simple, let him turn in hither: as for him that wanteth understanding, she saith to him, ^v4

5 Come, eat of my bread, and drink of the wine which I have mingled. ^v5

6 Forsake the foolish, and live; and go in the way of understanding. ^v6

7 He that reproveth a scorner getteth to himself shame: and he that rebuketh a wicked man getteth himself a blot. ^v7

8 Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee. ^v8

9 Give instruction to a wise man, and he will be yet wiser: teach a just man, and he will increase in learning. ^v9

10 The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: and the knowledge of the holy is understanding. ^v10

11 For by me thy days shall be multiplied, and the years of thy life shall be increased. ^v11

12 If thou be wise, thou shalt be wise for thyself: but if thou scornest, thou alone shalt bear it. ^v12

13 A foolish woman is clamorous: she is simple, and knoweth nothing. ^v13

14 For she sitteth at the door of her house, on a seat in the high places of the city, ^v14

15 To call passengers who go right on their ways: ^v15

16 Whoso is simple, let him turn in hither: and as for him that wanteth understanding, she saith to him, ^v16

17 Stolen waters are sweet, and bread eaten in secret is pleasant. ^v17

18 But he knoweth not that the dead are there; and that her guests are in the depths of hell. ^v18
