# Proverbs 22

1 A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold. ^v1

2 The rich and poor meet together: the Lord is the maker of them all. ^v2

3 A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished. ^v3

4 By humility and the fear of the Lord are riches, and honour, and life. ^v4

5 Thorns and snares are in the way of the froward: he that doth keep his soul shall be far from them. ^v5

6 Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it. ^v6

7 The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender. ^v7

8 He that soweth iniquity shall reap vanity: and the rod of his anger shall fail. ^v8

9 He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor. ^v9

10 Cast out the scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease. ^v10

11 He that loveth pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king shall be his friend. ^v11

12 The eyes of the Lord preserve knowledge, and he overthroweth the words of the transgressor. ^v12

13 The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets. ^v13

14 The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: he that is abhorred of the Lord shall fall therein. ^v14

15 Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him. ^v15

16 He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches, and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want. ^v16

17 Bow down thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thine heart unto my knowledge. ^v17

18 For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee; they shall withal be fitted in thy lips. ^v18

19 That thy trust may be in the Lord, I have made known to thee this day, even to thee. ^v19

20 Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge, ^v20

21 That I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send unto thee? ^v21

22 Rob not the poor, because he is poor: neither oppress the afflicted in the gate: ^v22

23 For the Lord will plead their cause, and spoil the soul of those that spoiled them. ^v23

24 Make no friendship with an angry man; and with a furious man thou shalt not go: ^v24

25 Lest thou learn his ways, and get a snare to thy soul. ^v25

26 Be not thou one of them that strike hands, or of them that are sureties for debts. ^v26

27 If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee? ^v27

28 Remove not the ancient landmark, which thy fathers have set. ^v28

29 Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men. ^v29
