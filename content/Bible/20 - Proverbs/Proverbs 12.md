# Proverbs 12

1 Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is brutish. ^v1

2 A good man obtaineth favour of the Lord: but a man of wicked devices will he condemn. ^v2

3 A man shall not be established by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall not be moved. ^v3

4 A virtuous woman is a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed is as rottenness in his bones. ^v4

5 The thoughts of the righteous are right: but the counsels of the wicked are deceit. ^v5

6 The words of the wicked are to lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them. ^v6

7 The wicked are overthrown, and are not: but the house of the righteous shall stand. ^v7

8 A man shall be commended according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised. ^v8

9 He that is despised, and hath a servant, is better than he that honoureth himself, and lacketh bread. ^v9

10 A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel. ^v10

11 He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain persons is void of understanding. ^v11

12 The wicked desireth the net of evil men: but the root of the righteous yieldeth fruit. ^v12

13 The wicked is snared by the transgression of his lips: but the just shall come out of trouble. ^v13

14 A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of his mouth: and the recompence of a man’s hands shall be rendered unto him. ^v14

15 The way of a fool is right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel is wise. ^v15

16 A fool’s wrath is presently known: but a prudent man covereth shame. ^v16

17 He that speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit. ^v17

18 There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise is health. ^v18

19 The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment. ^v19

20 Deceit is in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors of peace is joy. ^v20

21 There shall no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief. ^v21

22 Lying lips are abomination to the Lord: but they that deal truly are his delight. ^v22

23 A prudent man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness. ^v23

24 The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute. ^v24

25 Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad. ^v25

26 The righteous is more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked seduceth them. ^v26

27 The slothful man roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man is precious. ^v27

28 In the way of righteousness is life; and in the pathway thereof there is no death. ^v28
