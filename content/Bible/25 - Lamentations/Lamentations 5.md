# Lamentations 5

1 Remember, O Lord, what is come upon us: consider, and behold our reproach. ^v1

2 Our inheritance is turned to strangers, our houses to aliens. ^v2

3 We are orphans and fatherless, our mothers are as widows. ^v3

4 We have drunken our water for money; our wood is sold unto us. ^v4

5 Our necks are under persecution: we labour, and have no rest. ^v5

6 We have given the hand to the Egyptians, and to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread. ^v6

7 Our fathers have sinned, and are not; and we have borne their iniquities. ^v7

8 Servants have ruled over us: there is none that doth deliver us out of their hand. ^v8

9 We gat our bread with the peril of our lives because of the sword of the wilderness. ^v9

10 Our skin was black like an oven because of the terrible famine. ^v10

11 They ravished the women in Zion, and the maids in the cities of Judah. ^v11

12 Princes are hanged up by their hand: the faces of elders were not honoured. ^v12

13 They took the young men to grind, and the children fell under the wood. ^v13

14 The elders have ceased from the gate, the young men from their musick. ^v14

15 The joy of our heart is ceased; our dance is turned into mourning. ^v15

16 The crown is fallen from our head: woe unto us, that we have sinned! ^v16

17 For this our heart is faint; for these things our eyes are dim. ^v17

18 Because of the mountain of Zion, which is desolate, the foxes walk upon it. ^v18

19 Thou, O Lord, remainest for ever; thy throne from generation to generation. ^v19

20 Wherefore dost thou forget us for ever, and forsake us so long time? ^v20

21 Turn thou us unto thee, O Lord, and we shall be turned; renew our days as of old. ^v21

22 But thou hast utterly rejected us; thou art very wroth against us. ^v22
