# Job 16

1 Then Job answered and said, ^v1

2 I have heard many such things: miserable comforters are ye all. ^v2

3 Shall vain words have an end? or what emboldeneth thee that thou answerest? ^v3

4 I also could speak as ye do: if your soul were in my soul’s stead, I could heap up words against you, and shake mine head at you. ^v4

5 But I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips should asswage your grief. ^v5

6 Though I speak, my grief is not asswaged: and though I forbear, what am I eased? ^v6

7 But now he hath made me weary: thou hast made desolate all my company. ^v7

8 And thou hast filled me with wrinkles, which is a witness against me: and my leanness rising up in me beareth witness to my face. ^v8

9 He teareth me in his wrath, who hateth me: he gnasheth upon me with his teeth; mine enemy sharpeneth his eyes upon me. ^v9

10 They have gaped upon me with their mouth; they have smitten me upon the cheek reproachfully; they have gathered themselves together against me. ^v10

11 God hath delivered me to the ungodly, and turned me over into the hands of the wicked. ^v11

12 I was at ease, but he hath broken me asunder: he hath also taken me by my neck, and shaken me to pieces, and set me up for his mark. ^v12

13 His archers compass me round about, he cleaveth my reins asunder, and doth not spare; he poureth out my gall upon the ground. ^v13

14 He breaketh me with breach upon breach, he runneth upon me like a giant. ^v14

15 I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and defiled my horn in the dust. ^v15

16 My face is foul with weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death; ^v16

17 Not for any injustice in mine hands: also my prayer is pure. ^v17

18 O earth, cover not thou my blood, and let my cry have no place. ^v18

19 Also now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and my record is on high. ^v19

20 My friends scorn me: but mine eye poureth out tears unto God. ^v20

21 O that one might plead for a man with God, as a man pleadeth for his neighbour! ^v21

22 When a few years are come, then I shall go the way whence I shall not return. ^v22
