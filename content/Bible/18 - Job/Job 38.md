# Job 38

1 Then the Lord answered Job out of the whirlwind, and said, ^v1

2 Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge? ^v2

3 Gird up now thy loins like a man; for I will demand of thee, and answer thou me. ^v3

4 Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding. ^v4

5 Who hath laid the measures thereof, if thou knowest? or who hath stretched the line upon it? ^v5

6 Whereupon are the foundations thereof fastened? or who laid the corner stone thereof; ^v6

7 When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy? ^v7

8 Or who shut up the sea with doors, when it brake forth, as if it had issued out of the womb? ^v8

9 When I made the cloud the garment thereof, and thick darkness a swaddlingband for it, ^v9

10 And brake up for it my decreed place, and set bars and doors, ^v10

11 And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further: and here shall thy proud waves be stayed? ^v11

12 Hast thou commanded the morning since thy days; and caused the dayspring to know his place; ^v12

13 That it might take hold of the ends of the earth, that the wicked might be shaken out of it? ^v13

14 It is turned as clay to the seal; and they stand as a garment. ^v14

15 And from the wicked their light is withholden, and the high arm shall be broken. ^v15

16 Hast thou entered into the springs of the sea? or hast thou walked in the search of the depth? ^v16

17 Have the gates of death been opened unto thee? or hast thou seen the doors of the shadow of death? ^v17

18 Hast thou perceived the breadth of the earth? declare if thou knowest it all. ^v18

19 Where is the way where light dwelleth? and as for darkness, where is the place thereof, ^v19

20 That thou shouldest take it to the bound thereof, and that thou shouldest know the paths to the house thereof? ^v20

21 Knowest thou it, because thou wast then born? or because the number of thy days is great? ^v21

22 Hast thou entered into the treasures of the snow? or hast thou seen the treasures of the hail, ^v22

23 Which I have reserved against the time of trouble, against the day of battle and war? ^v23

24 By what way is the light parted, which scattereth the east wind upon the earth? ^v24

25 Who hath divided a watercourse for the overflowing of waters, or a way for the lightning of thunder; ^v25

26 To cause it to rain on the earth, where no man is; on the wilderness, wherein there is no man; ^v26

27 To satisfy the desolate and waste ground; and to cause the bud of the tender herb to spring forth? ^v27

28 Hath the rain a father? or who hath begotten the drops of dew? ^v28

29 Out of whose womb came the ice? and the hoary frost of heaven, who hath gendered it? ^v29

30 The waters are hid as with a stone, and the face of the deep is frozen. ^v30

31 Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion? ^v31

32 Canst thou bring forth Mazzaroth in his season? or canst thou guide Arcturus with his sons? ^v32

33 Knowest thou the ordinances of heaven? canst thou set the dominion thereof in the earth? ^v33

34 Canst thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, that abundance of waters may cover thee? ^v34

35 Canst thou send lightnings, that they may go, and say unto thee, Here we are? ^v35

36 Who hath put wisdom in the inward parts? or who hath given understanding to the heart? ^v36

37 Who can number the clouds in wisdom? or who can stay the bottles of heaven, ^v37

38 When the dust groweth into hardness, and the clods cleave fast together? ^v38

39 Wilt thou hunt the prey for the lion? or fill the appetite of the young lions, ^v39

40 When they couch in their dens, and abide in the covert to lie in wait? ^v40

41 Who provideth for the raven his food? when his young ones cry unto God, they wander for lack of meat. ^v41
