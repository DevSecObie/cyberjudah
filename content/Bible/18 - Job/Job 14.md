# Job 14

1 Man that is born of a woman is of few days, and full of trouble. ^v1

2 He cometh forth like a flower, and is cut down: he fleeth also as a shadow, and continueth not. ^v2

3 And dost thou open thine eyes upon such an one, and bringest me into judgment with thee? ^v3

4 Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? not one. ^v4

5 Seeing his days are determined, the number of his months are with thee, thou hast appointed his bounds that he cannot pass; ^v5

6 Turn from him, that he may rest, till he shall accomplish, as an hireling, his day. ^v6

7 For there is hope of a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that the tender branch thereof will not cease. ^v7

8 Though the root thereof wax old in the earth, and the stock thereof die in the ground; ^v8

9 Yet through the scent of water it will bud, and bring forth boughs like a plant. ^v9

10 But man dieth, and wasteth away: yea, man giveth up the ghost, and where is he? ^v10

11 As the waters fail from the sea, and the flood decayeth and drieth up: ^v11

12 So man lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep. ^v12

13 O that thou wouldest hide me in the grave, that thou wouldest keep me secret, until thy wrath be past, that thou wouldest appoint me a set time, and remember me! ^v13

14 If a man die, shall he live again? all the days of my appointed time will I wait, till my change come. ^v14

15 Thou shalt call, and I will answer thee: thou wilt have a desire to the work of thine hands. ^v15

16 For now thou numberest my steps: dost thou not watch over my sin? ^v16

17 My transgression is sealed up in a bag, and thou sewest up mine iniquity. ^v17

18 And surely the mountain falling cometh to nought, and the rock is removed out of his place. ^v18

19 The waters wear the stones: thou washest away the things which grow out of the dust of the earth; and thou destroyest the hope of man. ^v19

20 Thou prevailest for ever against him, and he passeth: thou changest his countenance, and sendest him away. ^v20

21 His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not; and they are brought low, but he perceiveth it not of them. ^v21

22 But his flesh upon him shall have pain, and his soul within him shall mourn. ^v22
