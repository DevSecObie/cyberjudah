# Job 35

1 Elihu spake moreover, and said, ^v1

2 Thinkest thou this to be right, that thou saidst, My righteousness is more than God’s? ^v2

3 For thou saidst, What advantage will it be unto thee? and, What profit shall I have, if I be cleansed from my sin? ^v3

4 I will answer thee, and thy companions with thee. ^v4

5 Look unto the heavens, and see; and behold the clouds which are higher than thou. ^v5

6 If thou sinnest, what doest thou against him? or if thy transgressions be multiplied, what doest thou unto him? ^v6

7 If thou be righteous, what givest thou him? or what receiveth he of thine hand? ^v7

8 Thy wickedness may hurt a man as thou art; and thy righteousness may profit the son of man. ^v8

9 By reason of the multitude of oppressions they make the oppressed to cry: they cry out by reason of the arm of the mighty. ^v9

10 But none saith, Where is God my maker, who giveth songs in the night; ^v10

11 Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and maketh us wiser than the fowls of heaven? ^v11

12 There they cry, but none giveth answer, because of the pride of evil men. ^v12

13 Surely God will not hear vanity, neither will the Almighty regard it. ^v13

14 Although thou sayest thou shalt not see him, yet judgment is before him; therefore trust thou in him. ^v14

15 But now, because it is not so, he hath visited in his anger; yet he knoweth it not in great extremity: ^v15

16 Therefore doth Job open his mouth in vain; he multiplieth words without knowledge. ^v16
