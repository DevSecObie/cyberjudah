# Job 30

1 But now they that are younger than I have me in derision, whose fathers I would have disdained to have set with the dogs of my flock. ^v1

2 Yea, whereto might the strength of their hands profit me, in whom old age was perished? ^v2

3 For want and famine they were solitary; fleeing into the wilderness in former time desolate and waste. ^v3

4 Who cut up mallows by the bushes, and juniper roots for their meat. ^v4

5 They were driven forth from among men, (they cried after them as after a thief;) ^v5

6 To dwell in the clifts of the valleys, in caves of the earth, and in the rocks. ^v6

7 Among the bushes they brayed; under the nettles they were gathered together. ^v7

8 They were children of fools, yea, children of base men: they were viler than the earth. ^v8

9 And now am I their song, yea, I am their byword. ^v9

10 They abhor me, they flee far from me, and spare not to spit in my face. ^v10

11 Because he hath loosed my cord, and afflicted me, they have also let loose the bridle before me. ^v11

12 Upon my right hand rise the youth; they push away my feet, and they raise up against me the ways of their destruction. ^v12

13 They mar my path, they set forward my calamity, they have no helper. ^v13

14 They came upon me as a wide breaking in of waters: in the desolation they rolled themselves upon me. ^v14

15 Terrors are turned upon me: they pursue my soul as the wind: and my welfare passeth away as a cloud. ^v15

16 And now my soul is poured out upon me; the days of affliction have taken hold upon me. ^v16

17 My bones are pierced in me in the night season: and my sinews take no rest. ^v17

18 By the great force of my disease is my garment changed: it bindeth me about as the collar of my coat. ^v18

19 He hath cast me into the mire, and I am become like dust and ashes. ^v19

20 I cry unto thee, and thou dost not hear me: I stand up, and thou regardest me not. ^v20

21 Thou art become cruel to me: with thy strong hand thou opposest thyself against me. ^v21

22 Thou liftest me up to the wind; thou causest me to ride upon it, and dissolvest my substance. ^v22

23 For I know that thou wilt bring me to death, and to the house appointed for all living. ^v23

24 Howbeit he will not stretch out his hand to the grave, though they cry in his destruction. ^v24

25 Did not I weep for him that was in trouble? was not my soul grieved for the poor? ^v25

26 When I looked for good, then evil came unto me: and when I waited for light, there came darkness. ^v26

27 My bowels boiled, and rested not: the days of affliction prevented me. ^v27

28 I went mourning without the sun: I stood up, and I cried in the congregation. ^v28

29 I am a brother to dragons, and a companion to owls. ^v29

30 My skin is black upon me, and my bones are burned with heat. ^v30

31 My harp also is turned to mourning, and my organ into the voice of them that weep. ^v31
