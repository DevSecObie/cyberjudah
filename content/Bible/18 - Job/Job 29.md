# Job 29

1 Moreover Job continued his parable, and said, ^v1

2 Oh that I were as in months past, as in the days when God preserved me; ^v2

3 When his candle shined upon my head, and when by his light I walked through darkness; ^v3

4 As I was in the days of my youth, when the secret of God was upon my tabernacle; ^v4

5 When the Almighty was yet with me, when my children were about me; ^v5

6 When I washed my steps with butter, and the rock poured me out rivers of oil; ^v6

7 When I went out to the gate through the city, when I prepared my seat in the street! ^v7

8 The young men saw me, and hid themselves: and the aged arose, and stood up. ^v8

9 The princes refrained talking, and laid their hand on their mouth. ^v9

10 The nobles held their peace, and their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth. ^v10

11 When the ear heard me, then it blessed me; and when the eye saw me, it gave witness to me: ^v11

12 Because I delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, and him that had none to help him. ^v12

13 The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me: and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy. ^v13

14 I put on righteousness, and it clothed me: my judgment was as a robe and a diadem. ^v14

15 I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame. ^v15

16 I was a father to the poor: and the cause which I knew not I searched out. ^v16

17 And I brake the jaws of the wicked, and plucked the spoil out of his teeth. ^v17

18 Then I said, I shall die in my nest, and I shall multiply my days as the sand. ^v18

19 My root was spread out by the waters, and the dew lay all night upon my branch. ^v19

20 My glory was fresh in me, and my bow was renewed in my hand. ^v20

21 Unto me men gave ear, and waited, and kept silence at my counsel. ^v21

22 After my words they spake not again; and my speech dropped upon them. ^v22

23 And they waited for me as for the rain; and they opened their mouth wide as for the latter rain. ^v23

24 If I laughed on them, they believed it not; and the light of my countenance they cast not down. ^v24

25 I chose out their way, and sat chief, and dwelt as a king in the army, as one that comforteth the mourners. ^v25
