# Job 12

1 And Job answered and said, ^v1

2 No doubt but ye are the people, and wisdom shall die with you. ^v2

3 But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you: yea, who knoweth not such things as these? ^v3

4 I am as one mocked of his neighbour, who calleth upon God, and he answereth him: the just upright man is laughed to scorn. ^v4

5 He that is ready to slip with his feet is as a lamp despised in the thought of him that is at ease. ^v5

6 The tabernacles of robbers prosper, and they that provoke God are secure; into whose hand God bringeth abundantly. ^v6

7 But ask now the beasts, and they shall teach thee; and the fowls of the air, and they shall tell thee: ^v7

8 Or speak to the earth, and it shall teach thee: and the fishes of the sea shall declare unto thee. ^v8

9 Who knoweth not in all these that the hand of the Lord hath wrought this? ^v9

10 In whose hand is the soul of every living thing, and the breath of all mankind. ^v10

11 Doth not the ear try words? and the mouth taste his meat? ^v11

12 With the ancient is wisdom; and in length of days understanding. ^v12

13 With him is wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding. ^v13

14 Behold, he breaketh down, and it cannot be built again: he shutteth up a man, and there can be no opening. ^v14

15 Behold, he withholdeth the waters, and they dry up: also he sendeth them out, and they overturn the earth. ^v15

16 With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his. ^v16

17 He leadeth counsellors away spoiled, and maketh the judges fools. ^v17

18 He looseth the bond of kings, and girdeth their loins with a girdle. ^v18

19 He leadeth princes away spoiled, and overthroweth the mighty. ^v19

20 He removeth away the speech of the trusty, and taketh away the understanding of the aged. ^v20

21 He poureth contempt upon princes, and weakeneth the strength of the mighty. ^v21

22 He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth out to light the shadow of death. ^v22

23 He increaseth the nations, and destroyeth them: he enlargeth the nations, and straiteneth them again. ^v23

24 He taketh away the heart of the chief of the people of the earth, and causeth them to wander in a wilderness where there is no way. ^v24

25 They grope in the dark without light, and he maketh them to stagger like a drunken man. ^v25
