# Ezra 2

1 Now these are the children of the province that went up out of the captivity, of those which had been carried away, whom Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon had carried away unto Babylon, and came again unto Jerusalem and Judah, every one unto his city; ^v1

2 Which came with Zerubbabel: Jeshua, Nehemiah, Seraiah, Reelaiah, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispar, Bigvai, Rehum, Baanah. The number of the men of the people of Israel: ^v2

3 The children of Parosh, two thousand an hundred seventy and two. ^v3

4 The children of Shephatiah, three hundred seventy and two. ^v4

5 The children of Arah, seven hundred seventy and five. ^v5

6 The children of Pahath–moab, of the children of Jeshua and Joab, two thousand eight hundred and twelve. ^v6

7 The children of Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty and four. ^v7

8 The children of Zattu, nine hundred forty and five. ^v8

9 The children of Zaccai, seven hundred and threescore. ^v9

10 The children of Bani, six hundred forty and two. ^v10

11 The children of Bebai, six hundred twenty and three. ^v11

12 The children of Azgad, a thousand two hundred twenty and two. ^v12

13 The children of Adonikam, six hundred sixty and six. ^v13

14 The children of Bigvai, two thousand fifty and six. ^v14

15 The children of Adin, four hundred fifty and four. ^v15

16 The children of Ater of Hezekiah, ninety and eight. ^v16

17 The children of Bezai, three hundred twenty and three. ^v17

18 The children of Jorah, an hundred and twelve. ^v18

19 The children of Hashum, two hundred twenty and three. ^v19

20 The children of Gibbar, ninety and five. ^v20

21 The children of Beth–lehem, an hundred twenty and three. ^v21

22 The men of Netophah, fifty and six. ^v22

23 The men of Anathoth, an hundred twenty and eight. ^v23

24 The children of Azmaveth, forty and two. ^v24

25 The children of Kirjath–arim, Chephirah, and Beeroth, seven hundred and forty and three. ^v25

26 The children of Ramah and Gaba, six hundred twenty and one. ^v26

27 The men of Michmas, an hundred twenty and two. ^v27

28 The men of Beth–el and Ai, two hundred twenty and three. ^v28

29 The children of Nebo, fifty and two. ^v29

30 The children of Magbish, an hundred fifty and six. ^v30

31 The children of the other Elam, a thousand two hundred fifty and four. ^v31

32 The children of Harim, three hundred and twenty. ^v32

33 The children of Lod, Hadid, and Ono, seven hundred twenty and five. ^v33

34 The children of Jericho, three hundred forty and five. ^v34

35 The children of Senaah, three thousand and six hundred and thirty. ^v35

36 The priests: the children of Jedaiah, of the house of Jeshua, nine hundred seventy and three. ^v36

37 The children of Immer, a thousand fifty and two. ^v37

38 The children of Pashur, a thousand two hundred forty and seven. ^v38

39 The children of Harim, a thousand and seventeen. ^v39

40 The Levites: the children of Jeshua and Kadmiel, of the children of Hodaviah, seventy and four. ^v40

41 The singers: the children of Asaph, an hundred twenty and eight. ^v41

42 The children of the porters: the children of Shallum, the children of Ater, the children of Talmon, the children of Akkub, the children of Hatita, the children of Shobai, in all an hundred thirty and nine. ^v42

43 The Nethinims: the children of Ziha, the children of Hasupha, the children of Tabbaoth, ^v43

44 The children of Keros, the children of Siaha, the children of Padon, ^v44

45 The children of Lebanah, the children of Hagabah, the children of Akkub, ^v45

46 The children of Hagab, the children of Shalmai, the children of Hanan, ^v46

47 The children of Giddel, the children of Gahar, the children of Reaiah, ^v47

48 The children of Rezin, the children of Nekoda, the children of Gazzam, ^v48

49 The children of Uzza, the children of Paseah, the children of Besai, ^v49

50 The children of Asnah, the children of Mehunim, the children of Nephusim, ^v50

51 The children of Bakbuk, the children of Hakupha, the children of Harhur, ^v51

52 The children of Bazluth, the children of Mehida, the children of Harsha, ^v52

53 The children of Barkos, the children of Sisera, the children of Thamah, ^v53

54 The children of Neziah, the children of Hatipha. ^v54

55 The children of Solomon’s servants: the children of Sotai, the children of Sophereth, the children of Peruda, ^v55

56 The children of Jaalah, the children of Darkon, the children of Giddel, ^v56

57 The children of Shephatiah, the children of Hattil, the children of Pochereth of Zebaim, the children of Ami. ^v57

58 All the Nethinims, and the children of Solomon’s servants, were three hundred ninety and two. ^v58

59 And these were they which went up from Tel–melah, Tel–harsa, Cherub, Addan, and Immer: but they could not shew their father’s house, and their seed, whether they were of Israel: ^v59

60 The children of Delaiah, the children of Tobiah, the children of Nekoda, six hundred fifty and two. ^v60

61 And of the children of the priests: the children of Habaiah, the children of Koz, the children of Barzillai; which took a wife of the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite, and was called after their name: ^v61

62 These sought their register among those that were reckoned by genealogy, but they were not found: therefore were they, as polluted, put from the priesthood. ^v62

63 And the Tirshatha said unto them, that they should not eat of the most holy things, till there stood up a priest with Urim and with Thummim. ^v63

64 The whole congregation together was forty and two thousand three hundred and threescore, ^v64

65 Beside their servants and their maids, of whom there were seven thousand three hundred thirty and seven: and there were among them two hundred singing men and singing women. ^v65

66 Their horses were seven hundred thirty and six; their mules, two hundred forty and five; ^v66

67 Their camels, four hundred thirty and five; their asses, six thousand seven hundred and twenty. ^v67

68 And some of the chief of the fathers, when they came to the house of the Lord which is at Jerusalem, offered freely for the house of God to set it up in his place: ^v68

69 They gave after their ability unto the treasure of the work threescore and one thousand drams of gold, and five thousand pound of silver, and one hundred priests’ garments. ^v69

70 So the priests, and the Levites, and some of the people, and the singers, and the porters, and the Nethinims, dwelt in their cities, and all Israel in their cities. ^v70
