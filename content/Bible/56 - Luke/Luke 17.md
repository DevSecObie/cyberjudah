# Luke 17

1 Then said he unto the disciples, It is impossible but that offences will come: but woe unto him, through whom they come! ^v1

2 It were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he cast into the sea, than that he should offend one of these little ones. ^v2

3 Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him. ^v3

4 And if he trespass against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to thee, saying, I repent; thou shalt forgive him. ^v4

5 And the apostles said unto the Lord, Increase our faith. ^v5

6 And the Lord said, If ye had faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye might say unto this sycamine tree, Be thou plucked up by the root, and be thou planted in the sea; and it should obey you. ^v6

7 But which of you, having a servant plowing or feeding cattle, will say unto him by and by, when he is come from the field, Go and sit down to meat? ^v7

8 And will not rather say unto him, Make ready wherewith I may sup, and gird thyself, and serve me, till I have eaten and drunken; and afterward thou shalt eat and drink? ^v8

9 Doth he thank that servant because he did the things that were commanded him? I trow not. ^v9

10 So likewise ye, when ye shall have done all those things which are commanded you, say, We are unprofitable servants: we have done that which was our duty to do. ^v10

11 And it came to pass, as he went to Jerusalem, that he passed through the midst of Samaria and Galilee. ^v11

12 And as he entered into a certain village, there met him ten men that were lepers, which stood afar off: ^v12

13 And they lifted up their voices, and said, Jesus, Master, have mercy on us. ^v13

14 And when he saw them, he said unto them, Go shew yourselves unto the priests. And it came to pass, that, as they went, they were cleansed. ^v14

15 And one of them, when he saw that he was healed, turned back, and with a loud voice glorified God, ^v15

16 And fell down on his face at his feet, giving him thanks: and he was a Samaritan. ^v16

17 And Jesus answering said, Were there not ten cleansed? but where are the nine? ^v17

18 There are not found that returned to give glory to God, save this stranger. ^v18

19 And he said unto him, Arise, go thy way: thy faith hath made thee whole. ^v19

20 And when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come, he answered them and said, The kingdom of God cometh not with observation: ^v20

21 Neither shall they say, Lo here! or, lo there! for, behold, the kingdom of God is within you. ^v21

22 And he said unto the disciples, The days will come, when ye shall desire to see one of the days of the Son of man, and ye shall not see it. ^v22

23 And they shall say to you, See here; or, see there: go not after them, nor follow them. ^v23

24 For as the lightning, that lighteneth out of the one part under heaven, shineth unto the other part under heaven; so shall also the Son of man be in his day. ^v24

25 But first must he suffer many things, and be rejected of this generation. ^v25

26 And as it was in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man. ^v26

27 They did eat, they drank, they married wives, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all. ^v27

28 Likewise also as it was in the days of Lot; they did eat, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded; ^v28

29 But the same day that Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all. ^v29

30 Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed. ^v30

31 In that day, he which shall be upon the housetop, and his stuff in the house, let him not come down to take it away: and he that is in the field, let him likewise not return back. ^v31

32 Remember Lot’s wife. ^v32

33 Whosoever shall seek to save his life shall lose it; and whosoever shall lose his life shall preserve it. ^v33

34 I tell you, in that night there shall be two men in one bed; the one shall be taken, and the other shall be left. ^v34

35 Two women shall be grinding together; the one shall be taken, and the other left. ^v35

36 Two men shall be in the field; the one shall be taken, and the other left. ^v36

37 And they answered and said unto him, Where, Lord? And he said unto them, Wheresoever the body is, thither will the eagles be gathered together. ^v37
