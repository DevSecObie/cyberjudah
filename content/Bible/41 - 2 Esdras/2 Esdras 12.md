# 2 Esdras 12

1 And it came to pass, whiles the lion spake these words unto the eagle, I saw, ^v1

2 And, behold, the head that remained and the four wings appeared no more, and the two went unto it and set themselves up to reign, and their kingdom was small, and fill of uproar. ^v2

3 And I saw, and, behold, they appeared no more, and the whole body of the eagle was burnt so that the earth was in great fear: then awaked I out of the trouble and trance of my mind, and from great fear, and said unto my spirit, ^v3

4 Lo, this hast thou done unto me, in that thou searchest out the ways of the Highest. ^v4

5 Lo, yet am I weary in my mind, and very weak in my spirit; and little strength is there in me, for the great fear wherewith I was afflicted this night. ^v5

6 Therefore will I now beseech the Highest, that he will comfort me unto the end. ^v6

7 And I said, Lord that bearest rule, if I have found grace before thy sight, and if I am justified with thee before many others, and if my prayer indeed be come up before thy face; ^v7

8 Comfort me then, and shew me thy servant the interpretation and plain difference of this fearful vision, that thou mayest perfectly comfort my soul. ^v8

9 For thou hast judged me worthy to shew me the last times. ^v9

10 And he said unto me, This is the interpretation of the vision: ^v10

11 The eagle, whom thou sawest come up from the sea, is the kingdom which was seen in the vision of thy brother Daniel. ^v11

12 But it was not expounded unto him, therefore now I declare it unto thee. ^v12

13 Behold, the days will come, that there shall rise up a kingdom upon earth, and it shall be feared above all the kingdoms that were before it. ^v13

14 In the same shall twelve kings reign, one after another: ^v14

15 Whereof the second shall begin to reign, and shall have more time than any of the twelve. ^v15

16 And this do the twelve wings signify, which thou sawest. ^v16

17 As for the voice which thou heardest speak, and that thou sawest not to go out from the heads but from the midst of the body thereof, this is the interpretation: ^v17

18 That after the time of that kingdom there shall arise great strivings, and it shall stand in peril of failing: nevertheless it shall not then fall, but shall be restored again to his beginning. ^v18

19 And whereas thou sawest the eight small under feathers sticking to her wings, this is the interpretation: ^v19

20 That in him there shall arise eight kings, whose times shall be but small, and their years swift. ^v20

21 And two of them shall perish, the middle time approaching: four shall be kept until their end begin to approach: but two shall be kept unto the end. ^v21

22 And whereas thou sawest three heads resting, this is the interpretation: ^v22

23 In his last days shall the most High raise up three kingdoms, and renew many things therein, and they shall have the dominion of the earth, ^v23

24 And of those that dwell therein, with much oppression, above all those that were before them: therefore are they called the heads of the eagle. ^v24

25 For these are they that shall accomplish his wickedness, and that shall finish his last end. ^v25

26 And whereas thou sawest that the great head appeared no more, it signifieth that one of them shall die upon his bed, and yet with pain. ^v26

27 For the two that remain shall be slain with the sword. ^v27

28 For the sword of the one shall devour the other: but at the last shall he fall through the sword himself. ^v28

29 And whereas thou sawest two feathers under the wings passing over the head that is on the right side; ^v29

30 It signifieth that these are they, whom the Highest hath kept unto their end: this is the small kingdom and full of trouble, as thou sawest. ^v30

31 And the lion, whom thou sawest rising up out of the wood, and roaring, and speaking to the eagle, and rebuking her for her unrighteousness with all the words which thou hast heard; ^v31

32 This is the anointed, which the Highest hath kept for them and for their wickedness unto the end: he shall reprove them, and shall upbraid them with their cruelty. ^v32

33 For he shall set them before him alive in judgment, and shall rebuke them, and correct them. ^v33

34 For the rest of my people shall he deliver with mercy, those that have been pressed upon my borders, and he shall make them joyful until the coming of the day of judgment, whereof I have spoken unto thee from the the beginning. ^v34

35 This is the dream that thou sawest, and these are the interpretations. ^v35

36 Thou only hast been meet to know this secret of the Highest. ^v36

37 Therefore write all these things that thou hast seen in a book, and hide them: ^v37

38 And teach them to the wise of the people, whose hearts thou knowest may comprehend and keep these secrets. ^v38

39 But wait thou here thyself yet seven days more, that it may be shewed thee, whatsoever it pleaseth the Highest to declare unto thee. And with that he went his way. ^v39

40 And it came to pass, when all the people saw that the seven days were past, and I not come again into the city, they gathered them all together, from the least unto the greatest, and came unto me, and said, ^v40

41 What have we offended thee? and what evil have we done against thee, that thou forsakest us, and sittest here in this place? ^v41

42 For of all the prophets thou only art left us, as a cluster of the vintage, and as a candle in a dark place, and as a haven or ship preserved from the tempest. ^v42

43 Are not the evils which are come to us sufficient? ^v43

44 If thou shalt forsake us, how much better had it been for us, if we also had been burned in the midst of Sion? ^v44

45 For we are not better than they that died there. And they wept with a loud voice. Then answered I them, and said, ^v45

46 Be of good comfort, O Israel; and be not heavy, thou house of Jacob: ^v46

47 For the Highest hath you in remembrance, and the Mighty hath not forgotten you in temptation. ^v47

48 As for me, I have not forsaken you, neither am I departed from you: but am come into this place, to pray for the desolation of Sion, and that I might seek mercy for the low estate of your sanctuary. ^v48

49 And now go your way home every man, and after these days will I come unto you. ^v49

50 So the people went their way into the city, like as I commanded them: ^v50

51 But I remained still in the field seven days, as the angel commanded me; and did eat only in those days of the flowers of the field, and had my meat of the herbs ^v51
