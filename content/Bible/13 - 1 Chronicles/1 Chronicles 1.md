# 1 Chronicles 1

1 Adam, Sheth, Enosh, ^v1

2 Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jered, ^v2

3 Henoch, Methuselah, Lamech, ^v3

4 Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^v4

5 The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras. ^v5

6 And the sons of Gomer; Ashchenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah. ^v6

7 And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. ^v7

8 The sons of Ham; Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan. ^v8

9 And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raamah, and Sabtecha. And the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan. ^v9

10 And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be mighty upon the earth. ^v10

11 And Mizraim begat Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim, ^v11

12 And Pathrusim, and Casluhim, (of whom came the Philistines,) and Caphthorim. ^v12

13 And Canaan begat Zidon his firstborn, and Heth, ^v13

14 The Jebusite also, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite, ^v14

15 And the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite, ^v15

16 And the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite. ^v16

17 The sons of Shem; Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech. ^v17

18 And Arphaxad begat Shelah, and Shelah begat Eber. ^v18

19 And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg; because in his days the earth was divided: and his brother’s name was Joktan. ^v19

20 And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah, ^v20

21 Hadoram also, and Uzal, and Diklah, ^v21

22 And Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba, ^v22

23 And Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan. ^v23

24 Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah, ^v24

25 Eber, Peleg, Reu, ^v25

26 Serug, Nahor, Terah, ^v26

27 Abram; the same is Abraham. ^v27

28 The sons of Abraham; Isaac, and Ishmael. ^v28

29 These are their generations: The firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam, ^v29

30 Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema, ^v30

31 Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael. ^v31

32 Now the sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan; Sheba, and Dedan. ^v32

33 And the sons of Midian; Ephah, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaah. All these are the sons of Keturah. ^v33

34 And Abraham begat Isaac. The sons of Isaac; Esau and Israel. ^v34

35 The sons of Esau; Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah. ^v35

36 The sons of Eliphaz; Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek. ^v36

37 The sons of Reuel; Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah. ^v37

38 And the sons of Seir; Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan. ^v38

39 And the sons of Lotan; Hori, and Homam: and Timna was Lotan’s sister. ^v39

40 The sons of Shobal; Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. And the sons of Zibeon; Aiah, and Anah. ^v40

41 The sons of Anah; Dishon. And the sons of Dishon; Amram, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran. ^v41

42 The sons of Ezer; Bilhan, and Zavan, and Jakan. The sons of Dishan; Uz, and Aran. ^v42

43 Now these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the children of Israel; Bela the son of Beor: and the name of his city was Dinhabah. ^v43

44 And when Bela was dead, Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead. ^v44

45 And when Jobab was dead, Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his stead. ^v45

46 And when Husham was dead, Hadad the son of Bedad, which smote Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Avith. ^v46

47 And when Hadad was dead, Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead. ^v47

48 And when Samlah was dead, Shaul of Rehoboth by the river reigned in his stead. ^v48

49 And when Shaul was dead, Baal–hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead. ^v49

50 And when Baal–hanan was dead, Hadad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Pai; and his wife’s name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab. ^v50

51 Hadad died also. And the dukes of Edom were; duke Timnah, duke Aliah, duke Jetheth, ^v51

52 Duke Aholibamah, duke Elah, duke Pinon, ^v52

53 Duke Kenaz, duke Teman, duke Mibzar, ^v53

54 Duke Magdiel, duke Iram. These are the dukes of Edom. ^v54
