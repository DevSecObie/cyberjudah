# 1 Chronicles 2

1 These are the sons of Israel; Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun, ^v1

2 Dan, Joseph, and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher. ^v2

3 The sons of Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah: which three were born unto him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the sight of the Lord; and he slew him. ^v3

4 And Tamar his daughter in law bare him Pharez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five. ^v4

5 The sons of Pharez; Hezron, and Hamul. ^v5

6 And the sons of Zerah; Zimri, and Ethan, and Heman, and Calcol, and Dara: five of them in all. ^v6

7 And the sons of Carmi; Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the thing accursed. ^v7

8 And the sons of Ethan; Azariah. ^v8

9 The sons also of Hezron, that were born unto him; Jerahmeel, and Ram, and Chelubai. ^v9

10 And Ram begat Amminadab; and Amminadab begat Nahshon, prince of the children of Judah; ^v10

11 And Nahshon begat Salma, and Salma begat Boaz, ^v11

12 And Boaz begat Obed, and Obed begat Jesse, ^v12

13 And Jesse begat his firstborn Eliab, and Abinadab the second, and Shimma the third, ^v13

14 Nethaneel the fourth, Raddai the fifth, ^v14

15 Ozem the sixth, David the seventh: ^v15

16 Whose sisters were Zeruiah, and Abigail. And the sons of Zeruiah; Abishai, and Joab, and Asahel, three. ^v16

17 And Abigail bare Amasa: and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmeelite. ^v17

18 And Caleb the son of Hezron begat children of Azubah his wife, and of Jerioth: her sons are these; Jesher, and Shobab, and Ardon. ^v18

19 And when Azubah was dead, Caleb took unto him Ephrath, which bare him Hur. ^v19

20 And Hur begat Uri, and Uri begat Bezaleel. ^v20

21 And afterward Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead, whom he married when he was threescore years old; and she bare him Segub. ^v21

22 And Segub begat Jair, who had three and twenty cities in the land of Gilead. ^v22

23 And he took Geshur, and Aram, with the towns of Jair, from them, with Kenath, and the towns thereof, even threescore cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir the father of Gilead. ^v23

24 And after that Hezron was dead in Caleb–ephratah, then Abiah Hezron’s wife bare him Ashur the father of Tekoa. ^v24

25 And the sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron were, Ram the firstborn, and Bunah, and Oren, and Ozem, and Ahijah. ^v25

26 Jerahmeel had also another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam. ^v26

27 And the sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerahmeel were, Maaz, and Jamin, and Eker. ^v27

28 And the sons of Onam were, Shammai, and Jada. And the sons of Shammai; Nadab, and Abishur. ^v28

29 And the name of the wife of Abishur was Abihail, and she bare him Ahban, and Molid. ^v29

30 And the sons of Nadab; Seled, and Appaim: but Seled died without children. ^v30

31 And the sons of Appaim; Ishi. And the sons of Ishi; Sheshan. And the children of Sheshan; Ahlai. ^v31

32 And the sons of Jada the brother of Shammai; Jether, and Jonathan: and Jether died without children. ^v32

33 And the sons of Jonathan; Peleth, and Zaza. These were the sons of Jerahmeel. ^v33

34 Now Sheshan had no sons, but daughters. And Sheshan had a servant, an Egyptian, whose name was Jarha. ^v34

35 And Sheshan gave his daughter to Jarha his servant to wife; and she bare him Attai. ^v35

36 And Attai begat Nathan, and Nathan begat Zabad, ^v36

37 And Zabad begat Ephlal, and Ephlal begat Obed, ^v37

38 And Obed begat Jehu, and Jehu begat Azariah, ^v38

39 And Azariah begat Helez, and Helez begat Eleasah, ^v39

40 And Eleasah begat Sisamai, and Sisamai begat Shallum, ^v40

41 And Shallum begat Jekamiah, and Jekamiah begat Elishama. ^v41

42 Now the sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel were, Mesha his firstborn, which was the father of Ziph; and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron. ^v42

43 And the sons of Hebron; Korah, and Tappuah, and Rekem, and Shema. ^v43

44 And Shema begat Raham, the father of Jorkoam: and Rekem begat Shammai. ^v44

45 And the son of Shammai was Maon: and Maon was the father of Beth–zur. ^v45

46 And Ephah, Caleb’s concubine, bare Haran, and Moza, and Gazez: and Haran begat Gazez. ^v46

47 And the sons of Jahdai; Regem, and Jotham, and Geshan, and Pelet, and Ephah, and Shaaph. ^v47

48 Maachah, Caleb’s concubine, bare Sheber, and Tirhanah. ^v48

49 She bare also Shaaph the father of Madmannah, Sheva the father of Machbenah, and the father of Gibea: and the daughter of Caleb was Achsah. ^v49

50 These were the sons of Caleb the son of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah; Shobal the father of Kirjath–jearim, ^v50

51 Salma the father of Beth–lehem, Hareph the father of Beth–gader. ^v51

52 And Shobal the father of Kirjath–jearim had sons; Haroeh, and half of the Manahethites. ^v52

53 And the families of Kirjath–jearim; the Ithrites, and the Puhites, and the Shumathites, and the Mishraites; of them came the Zareathites, and the Eshtaulites. ^v53

54 The sons of Salma; Beth–lehem, and the Netophathites, Ataroth, the house of Joab, and half of the Manahethites, the Zorites. ^v54

55 And the families of the scribes which dwelt at Jabez; the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and Suchathites. These are the Kenites that came of Hemath, the father of the house of Rechab. ^v55
