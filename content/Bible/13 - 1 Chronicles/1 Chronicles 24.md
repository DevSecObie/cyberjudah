# 1 Chronicles 24

1 Now these are the divisions of the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron; Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. ^v1

2 But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest’s office. ^v2

3 And David distributed them, both Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their offices in their service. ^v3

4 And there were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar; and thus were they divided. Among the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen chief men of the house of their fathers, and eight among the sons of Ithamar according to the house of their fathers. ^v4

5 Thus were they divided by lot, one sort with another; for the governors of the sanctuary, and governors of the house of God, were of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar. ^v5

6 And Shemaiah the son of Nethaneel the scribe, one of the Levites, wrote them before the king, and the princes, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and before the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites: one principal household being taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar. ^v6

7 Now the first lot came forth to Jehoiarib, the second to Jedaiah, ^v7

8 The third to Harim, the fourth to Seorim, ^v8

9 The fifth to Malchijah, the sixth to Mijamin, ^v9

10 The seventh to Hakkoz, the eighth to Abijah, ^v10

11 The ninth to Jeshua, the tenth to Shecaniah, ^v11

12 The eleventh to Eliashib, the twelfth to Jakim, ^v12

13 The thirteenth to Huppah, the fourteenth to Jeshebeab, ^v13

14 The fifteenth to Bilgah, the sixteenth to Immer, ^v14

15 The seventeenth to Hezir, the eighteenth to Aphses, ^v15

16 The nineteenth to Pethahiah, the twentieth to Jehezekel, ^v16

17 The one and twentieth to Jachin, the two and twentieth to Gamul, ^v17

18 The three and twentieth to Delaiah, the four and twentieth to Maaziah. ^v18

19 These were the orderings of them in their service to come into the house of the Lord, according to their manner, under Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel had commanded him. ^v19

20 And the rest of the sons of Levi were these: Of the sons of Amram; Shubael: of the sons of Shubael; Jehdeiah. ^v20

21 Concerning Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, the first was Isshiah. ^v21

22 Of the Izharites; Shelomoth: of the sons of Shelomoth; Jahath. ^v22

23 And the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth. ^v23

24 Of the sons of Uzziel; Michah: of the sons of Michah; Shamir. ^v24

25 The brother of Michah was Isshiah: of the sons of Isshiah; Zechariah. ^v25

26 The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi: the sons of Jaaziah; Beno. ^v26

27 The sons of Merari by Jaaziah; Beno, and Shoham, and Zaccur, and Ibri. ^v27

28 Of Mahli came Eleazar, who had no sons. ^v28

29 Concerning Kish: the son of Kish was Jerahmeel. ^v29

30 The sons also of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites after the house of their fathers. ^v30

31 These likewise cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron in the presence of David the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites, even the principal fathers over against their younger brethren. ^v31
