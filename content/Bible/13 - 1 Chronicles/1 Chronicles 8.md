# 1 Chronicles 8

1 Now Benjamin begat Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, and Aharah the third, ^v1

2 Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth. ^v2

3 And the sons of Bela were, Addar, and Gera, and Abihud, ^v3

4 And Abishua, and Naaman, and Ahoah, ^v4

5 And Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram. ^v5

6 And these are the sons of Ehud: these are the heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Geba, and they removed them to Manahath: ^v6

7 And Naaman, and Ahiah, and Gera, he removed them, and begat Uzza, and Ahihud. ^v7

8 And Shaharaim begat children in the country of Moab, after he had sent them away; Hushim and Baara were his wives. ^v8

9 And he begat of Hodesh his wife, Jobab, and Zibia, and Mesha, and Malcham, ^v9

10 And Jeuz, and Shachia, and Mirma. These were his sons, heads of the fathers. ^v10

11 And of Hushim he begat Abitub, and Elpaal. ^v11

12 The sons of Elpaal; Eber, and Misham, and Shamed, who built Ono, and Lod, with the towns thereof: ^v12

13 Beriah also, and Shema, who were heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who drove away the inhabitants of Gath: ^v13

14 And Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth, ^v14

15 And Zebadiah, and Arad, and Ader, ^v15

16 And Michael, and Ispah, and Joha, the sons of Beriah; ^v16

17 And Zebadiah, and Meshullam, and Hezeki, and Heber, ^v17

18 Ishmerai also, and Jezliah, and Jobab, the sons of Elpaal; ^v18

19 And Jakim, and Zichri, and Zabdi, ^v19

20 And Elienai, and Zilthai, and Eliel, ^v20

21 And Adaiah, and Beraiah, and Shimrath, the sons of Shimhi; ^v21

22 And Ishpan, and Heber, and Eliel, ^v22

23 And Abdon, and Zichri, and Hanan, ^v23

24 And Hananiah, and Elam, and Antothijah, ^v24

25 And Iphedeiah, and Penuel, the sons of Shashak; ^v25

26 And Shamsherai, and Shehariah, and Athaliah, ^v26

27 And Jaresiah, and Eliah, and Zichri, the sons of Jeroham. ^v27

28 These were heads of the fathers, by their generations, chief men. These dwelt in Jerusalem. ^v28

29 And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon; whose wife’s name was Maachah: ^v29

30 And his firstborn son Abdon, and Zur, and Kish, and Baal, and Nadab, ^v30

31 And Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher. ^v31

32 And Mikloth begat Shimeah. And these also dwelt with their brethren in Jerusalem, over against them. ^v32

33 And Ner begat Kish, and Kish begat Saul, and Saul begat Jonathan, and Malchi–shua, and Abinadab, and Esh–baal. ^v33

34 And the son of Jonathan was Merib–baal; and Merib–baal begat Micah. ^v34

35 And the sons of Micah were, Pithon, and Melech, and Tarea, and Ahaz. ^v35

36 And Ahaz begat Jehoadah; and Jehoadah begat Alemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri begat Moza, ^v36

37 And Moza begat Binea: Rapha was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son: ^v37

38 And Azel had six sons, whose names are these, Azrikam, Bocheru, and Ishmael, and Sheariah, and Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel. ^v38

39 And the sons of Eshek his brother were, Ulam his firstborn, Jehush the second, and Eliphelet the third. ^v39

40 And the sons of Ulam were mighty men of valour, archers, and had many sons, and sons’ sons, an hundred and fifty. All these are of the sons of Benjamin. ^v40
