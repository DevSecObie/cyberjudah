# Tobit 9

1 Then Tobias called Raphael, and said unto him, ^v1

2 Brother Azarias, take with thee a servant, and two camels, and go to Rages of Media to Gabael, and bring me the money, and bring him to the wedding. ^v2

3 For Raguel hath sworn that I shall not depart. ^v3

4 But my father counteth the days; and if I tarry long, he will be very sorry. ^v4

5 So Raphael went out, and lodged with Gabael, and gave him the handwriting: who brought forth bags which were sealed up, and gave them to him. ^v5

6 And early in the morning they went forth both together, and came to the wedding: and Tobias blessed his wife. ^v6
