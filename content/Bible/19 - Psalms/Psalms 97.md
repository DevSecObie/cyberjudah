# Psalms 97

1 The Lord reigneth; let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of isles be glad thereof. ^v1

2 Clouds and darkness are round about him: righteousness and judgment are the habitation of his throne. ^v2

3 A fire goeth before him, and burneth up his enemies round about. ^v3

4 His lightnings enlightened the world: the earth saw, and trembled. ^v4

5 The hills melted like wax at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth. ^v5

6 The heavens declare his righteousness, and all the people see his glory. ^v6

7 Confounded be all they that serve graven images, that boast themselves of idols: worship him, all ye gods. ^v7

8 Zion heard, and was glad; and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of thy judgments, O Lord. ^v8

9 For thou, Lord, art high above all the earth: thou art exalted far above all gods. ^v9

10 Ye that love the Lord, hate evil: he preserveth the souls of his saints; he delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked. ^v10

11 Light is sown for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart. ^v11

12 Rejoice in the Lord, ye righteous; and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness. ^v12
