# Psalms 33

1 Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous: for praise is comely for the upright. ^v1

2 Praise the Lord with harp: sing unto him with the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings. ^v2

3 Sing unto him a new song; play skilfully with a loud noise. ^v3

4 For the word of the Lord is right; and all his works are done in truth. ^v4

5 He loveth righteousness and judgment: the earth is full of the goodness of the Lord. ^v5

6 By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth. ^v6

7 He gathereth the waters of the sea together as an heap: he layeth up the depth in storehouses. ^v7

8 Let all the earth fear the Lord: let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him. ^v8

9 For he spake, and it was done; he commanded, and it stood fast. ^v9

10 The Lord bringeth the counsel of the heathen to nought: he maketh the devices of the people of none effect. ^v10

11 The counsel of the Lord standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations. ^v11

12 Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord; and the people whom he hath chosen for his own inheritance. ^v12

13 The Lord looketh from heaven; he beholdeth all the sons of men. ^v13

14 From the place of his habitation he looketh upon all the inhabitants of the earth. ^v14

15 He fashioneth their hearts alike; he considereth all their works. ^v15

16 There is no king saved by the multitude of an host: a mighty man is not delivered by much strength. ^v16

17 An horse is a vain thing for safety: neither shall he deliver any by his great strength. ^v17

18 Behold, the eye of the Lord is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy; ^v18

19 To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine. ^v19

20 Our soul waiteth for the Lord: he is our help and our shield. ^v20

21 For our heart shall rejoice in him, because we have trusted in his holy name. ^v21

22 Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, according as we hope in thee. ^v22
