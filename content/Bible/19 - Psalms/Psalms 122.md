# Psalms 122

1 I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord. ^v1

2 Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem. ^v2

3 Jerusalem is builded as a city that is compact together: ^v3

4 Whither the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord, unto the testimony of Israel, to give thanks unto the name of the Lord. ^v4

5 For there are set thrones of judgment, the thrones of the house of David. ^v5

6 Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee. ^v6

7 Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces. ^v7

8 For my brethren and companions’ sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee. ^v8

9 Because of the house of the Lord our God I will seek thy good. ^v9
