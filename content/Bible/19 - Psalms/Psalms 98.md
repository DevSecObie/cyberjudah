# Psalms 98

1 O sing unto the Lord a new song; for he hath done marvellous things: his right hand, and his holy arm, hath gotten him the victory. ^v1

2 The Lord hath made known his salvation: his righteousness hath he openly shewed in the sight of the heathen. ^v2

3 He hath remembered his mercy and his truth toward the house of Israel: all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God. ^v3

4 Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all the earth: make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise. ^v4

5 Sing unto the Lord with the harp; with the harp, and the voice of a psalm. ^v5

6 With trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the Lord, the King. ^v6

7 Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein. ^v7

8 Let the floods clap their hands: let the hills be joyful together ^v8

9 Before the Lord; for he cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall he judge the world, and the people with equity. ^v9
