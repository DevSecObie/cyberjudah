# Psalms 69

1 Save me, O God; for the waters are come in unto my soul. ^v1

2 I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing: I am come into deep waters, where the floods overflow me. ^v2

3 I am weary of my crying: my throat is dried: mine eyes fail while I wait for my God. ^v3

4 They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of mine head: they that would destroy me, being mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away. ^v4

5 O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee. ^v5

6 Let not them that wait on thee, O Lord God of hosts, be ashamed for my sake: let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake, O God of Israel. ^v6

7 Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face. ^v7

8 I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother’s children. ^v8

9 For the zeal of thine house hath eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee are fallen upon me. ^v9

10 When I wept, and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach. ^v10

11 I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb to them. ^v11

12 They that sit in the gate speak against me; and I was the song of the drunkards. ^v12

13 But as for me, my prayer is unto thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation. ^v13

14 Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters. ^v14

15 Let not the waterflood overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me. ^v15

16 Hear me, O Lord; for thy lovingkindness is good: turn unto me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies. ^v16

17 And hide not thy face from thy servant; for I am in trouble: hear me speedily. ^v17

18 Draw nigh unto my soul, and redeem it: deliver me because of mine enemies. ^v18

19 Thou hast known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour: mine adversaries are all before thee. ^v19

20 Reproach hath broken my heart; and I am full of heaviness: and I looked for some to take pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none. ^v20

21 They gave me also gall for my meat; and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. ^v21

22 Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap. ^v22

23 Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to shake. ^v23

24 Pour out thine indignation upon them, and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them. ^v24

25 Let their habitation be desolate; and let none dwell in their tents. ^v25

26 For they persecute him whom thou hast smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded. ^v26

27 Add iniquity unto their iniquity: and let them not come into thy righteousness. ^v27

28 Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous. ^v28

29 But I am poor and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O God, set me up on high. ^v29

30 I will praise the name of God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving. ^v30

31 This also shall please the Lord better than an ox or bullock that hath horns and hoofs. ^v31

32 The humble shall see this, and be glad: and your heart shall live that seek God. ^v32

33 For the Lord heareth the poor, and despiseth not his prisoners. ^v33

34 Let the heaven and earth praise him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein. ^v34

35 For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may dwell there, and have it in possession. ^v35

36 The seed also of his servants shall inherit it: and they that love his name shall dwell therein. ^v36
