# Psalms 22

1 My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring? ^v1

2 O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent. ^v2

3 But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel. ^v3

4 Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them. ^v4

5 They cried unto thee, and were delivered: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded. ^v5

6 But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people. ^v6

7 All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying, ^v7

8 He trusted on the Lord that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him. ^v8

9 But thou art he that took me out of the womb: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother’s breasts. ^v9

10 I was cast upon thee from the womb: thou art my God from my mother’s belly. ^v10

11 Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help. ^v11

12 Many bulls have compassed me: strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round. ^v12

13 They gaped upon me with their mouths, as a ravening and a roaring lion. ^v13

14 I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint: my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels. ^v14

15 My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death. ^v15

16 For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet. ^v16

17 I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me. ^v17

18 They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture. ^v18

19 But be not thou far from me, O Lord: O my strength, haste thee to help me. ^v19

20 Deliver my soul from the sword; my darling from the power of the dog. ^v20

21 Save me from the lion’s mouth: for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns. ^v21

22 I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee. ^v22

23 Ye that fear the Lord, praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel. ^v23

24 For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard. ^v24

25 My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation: I will pay my vows before them that fear him. ^v25

26 The meek shall eat and be satisfied: they shall praise the Lord that seek him: your heart shall live for ever. ^v26

27 All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the Lord: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee. ^v27

28 For the kingdom is the Lord’s: and he is the governor among the nations. ^v28

29 All they that be fat upon earth shall eat and worship: all they that go down to the dust shall bow before him: and none can keep alive his own soul. ^v29

30 A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation. ^v30

31 They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this. ^v31
