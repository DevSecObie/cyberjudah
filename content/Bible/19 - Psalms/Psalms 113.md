# Psalms 113

1 Praise ye the Lord. Praise, O ye servants of the Lord, praise the name of the Lord. ^v1

2 Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and for evermore. ^v2

3 From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same the Lord’s name is to be praised. ^v3

4 The Lord is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens. ^v4

5 Who is like unto the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high, ^v5

6 Who humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven, and in the earth! ^v6

7 He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill; ^v7

8 That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people. ^v8

9 He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise ye the Lord. ^v9
