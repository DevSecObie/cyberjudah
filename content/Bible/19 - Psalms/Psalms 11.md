# Psalms 11

1 In the Lord put I my trust: how say ye to my soul, Flee as a bird to your mountain? ^v1

2 For, lo, the wicked bend their bow, they make ready their arrow upon the string, that they may privily shoot at the upright in heart. ^v2

3 If the foundations be destroyed, what can the righteous do? ^v3

4 The Lord is in his holy temple, the Lord’s throne is in heaven: his eyes behold, his eyelids try, the children of men. ^v4

5 The Lord trieth the righteous: but the wicked and him that loveth violence his soul hateth. ^v5

6 Upon the wicked he shall rain snares, fire and brimstone, and an horrible tempest: this shall be the portion of their cup. ^v6

7 For the righteous Lord loveth righteousness; his countenance doth behold the upright. ^v7
