# Psalms 38

1 O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure. ^v1

2 For thine arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand presseth me sore. ^v2

3 There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine anger; neither is there any rest in my bones because of my sin. ^v3

4 For mine iniquities are gone over mine head: as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me. ^v4

5 My wounds stink and are corrupt because of my foolishness. ^v5

6 I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long. ^v6

7 For my loins are filled with a loathsome disease: and there is no soundness in my flesh. ^v7

8 I am feeble and sore broken: I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart. ^v8

9 Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hid from thee. ^v9

10 My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me. ^v10

11 My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off. ^v11

12 They also that seek after my life lay snares for me: and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long. ^v12

13 But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth. ^v13

14 Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs. ^v14

15 For in thee, O Lord, do I hope: thou wilt hear, O Lord my God. ^v15

16 For I said, Hear me, lest otherwise they should rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me. ^v16

17 For I am ready to halt, and my sorrow is continually before me. ^v17

18 For I will declare mine iniquity; I will be sorry for my sin. ^v18

19 But mine enemies are lively, and they are strong: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied. ^v19

20 They also that render evil for good are mine adversaries; because I follow the thing that good is. ^v20

21 Forsake me not, O Lord: O my God, be not far from me. ^v21

22 Make haste to help me, O Lord my salvation. ^v22
