# Psalms 129

1 Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, may Israel now say: ^v1

2 Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth: yet they have not prevailed against me. ^v2

3 The plowers plowed upon my back: they made long their furrows. ^v3

4 The Lord is righteous: he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked. ^v4

5 Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Zion. ^v5

6 Let them be as the grass upon the housetops, which withereth afore it groweth up: ^v6

7 Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand; nor he that bindeth sheaves his bosom. ^v7

8 Neither do they which go by say, The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we bless you in the name of the Lord. ^v8
