# Psalms 101

1 I will sing of mercy and judgment: unto thee, O Lord, will I sing. ^v1

2 I will behave myself wisely in a perfect way. O when wilt thou come unto me? I will walk within my house with a perfect heart. ^v2

3 I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that turn aside; it shall not cleave to me. ^v3

4 A froward heart shall depart from me: I will not know a wicked person. ^v4

5 Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer. ^v5

6 Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walketh in a perfect way, he shall serve me. ^v6

7 He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight. ^v7

8 I will early destroy all the wicked of the land; that I may cut off all wicked doers from the city of the Lord. ^v8
