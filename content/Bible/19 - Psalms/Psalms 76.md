# Psalms 76

1 In Judah is God known: his name is great in Israel. ^v1

2 In Salem also is his tabernacle, and his dwelling place in Zion. ^v2

3 There brake he the arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the battle. Selah. ^v3

4 Thou art more glorious and excellent than the mountains of prey. ^v4

5 The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep: and none of the men of might have found their hands. ^v5

6 At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep. ^v6

7 Thou, even thou, art to be feared: and who may stand in thy sight when once thou art angry? ^v7

8 Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven; the earth feared, and was still, ^v8

9 When God arose to judgment, to save all the meek of the earth. Selah. ^v9

10 Surely the wrath of man shall praise thee: the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain. ^v10

11 Vow, and pay unto the Lord your God: let all that be round about him bring presents unto him that ought to be feared. ^v11

12 He shall cut off the spirit of princes: he is terrible to the kings of the earth. ^v12
