# Psalms 105

1 O give thanks unto the Lord; call upon his name: make known his deeds among the people. ^v1

2 Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him: talk ye of all his wondrous works. ^v2

3 Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord. ^v3

4 Seek the Lord, and his strength: seek his face evermore. ^v4

5 Remember his marvellous works that he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth; ^v5

6 O ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye children of Jacob his chosen. ^v6

7 He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth. ^v7

8 He hath remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations. ^v8

9 Which covenant he made with Abraham, and his oath unto Isaac; ^v9

10 And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant: ^v10

11 Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance: ^v11

12 When they were but a few men in number; yea, very few, and strangers in it. ^v12

13 When they went from one nation to another, from one kingdom to another people; ^v13

14 He suffered no man to do them wrong: yea, he reproved kings for their sakes; ^v14

15 Saying, Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm. ^v15

16 Moreover he called for a famine upon the land: he brake the whole staff of bread. ^v16

17 He sent a man before them, even Joseph, who was sold for a servant: ^v17

18 Whose feet they hurt with fetters: he was laid in iron: ^v18

19 Until the time that his word came: the word of the Lord tried him. ^v19

20 The king sent and loosed him; even the ruler of the people, and let him go free. ^v20

21 He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his substance: ^v21

22 To bind his princes at his pleasure; and teach his senators wisdom. ^v22

23 Israel also came into Egypt; and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham. ^v23

24 And he increased his people greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies. ^v24

25 He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal subtilly with his servants. ^v25

26 He sent Moses his servant; and Aaron whom he had chosen. ^v26

27 They shewed his signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham. ^v27

28 He sent darkness, and made it dark; and they rebelled not against his word. ^v28

29 He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish. ^v29

30 Their land brought forth frogs in abundance, in the chambers of their kings. ^v30

31 He spake, and there came divers sorts of flies, and lice in all their coasts. ^v31

32 He gave them hail for rain, and flaming fire in their land. ^v32

33 He smote their vines also and their fig trees; and brake the trees of their coasts. ^v33

34 He spake, and the locusts came, and caterpillers, and that without number, ^v34

35 And did eat up all the herbs in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground. ^v35

36 He smote also all the firstborn in their land, the chief of all their strength. ^v36

37 He brought them forth also with silver and gold: and there was not one feeble person among their tribes. ^v37

38 Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them fell upon them. ^v38

39 He spread a cloud for a covering; and fire to give light in the night. ^v39

40 The people asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of heaven. ^v40

41 He opened the rock, and the waters gushed out; they ran in the dry places like a river. ^v41

42 For he remembered his holy promise, and Abraham his servant. ^v42

43 And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness: ^v43

44 And gave them the lands of the heathen: and they inherited the labour of the people; ^v44

45 That they might observe his statutes, and keep his laws. Praise ye the Lord. ^v45
