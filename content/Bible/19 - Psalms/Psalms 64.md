# Psalms 64

1 Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy. ^v1

2 Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity: ^v2

3 Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words: ^v3

4 That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not. ^v4

5 They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them? ^v5

6 They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep. ^v6

7 But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded. ^v7

8 So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away. ^v8

9 And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing. ^v9

10 The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory. ^v10
