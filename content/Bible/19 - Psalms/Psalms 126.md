# Psalms 126

1 When the Lord turned again the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream. ^v1

2 Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing: then said they among the heathen, The Lord hath done great things for them. ^v2

3 The Lord hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad. ^v3

4 Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as the streams in the south. ^v4

5 They that sow in tears shall reap in joy. ^v5

6 He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him. ^v6
