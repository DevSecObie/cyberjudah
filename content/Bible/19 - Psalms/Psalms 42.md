# Psalms 42

1 As the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God. ^v1

2 My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God: when shall I come and appear before God? ^v2

3 My tears have been my meat day and night, while they continually say unto me, Where is thy God? ^v3

4 When I remember these things, I pour out my soul in me: for I had gone with the multitude, I went with them to the house of God, with the voice of joy and praise, with a multitude that kept holyday. ^v4

5 Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted in me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him for the help of his countenance. ^v5

6 O my God, my soul is cast down within me: therefore will I remember thee from the land of Jordan, and of the Hermonites, from the hill Mizar. ^v6

7 Deep calleth unto deep at the noise of thy waterspouts: all thy waves and thy billows are gone over me. ^v7

8 Yet the Lord will command his lovingkindness in the daytime, and in the night his song shall be with me, and my prayer unto the God of my life. ^v8

9 I will say unto God my rock, Why hast thou forgotten me? why go I mourning because of the oppression of the enemy? ^v9

10 As with a sword in my bones, mine enemies reproach me; while they say daily unto me, Where is thy God? ^v10

11 Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God. ^v11
