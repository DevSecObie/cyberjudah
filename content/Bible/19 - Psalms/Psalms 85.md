# Psalms 85

1 Lord, thou hast been favourable unto thy land: thou hast brought back the captivity of Jacob. ^v1

2 Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of thy people, thou hast covered all their sin. Selah. ^v2

3 Thou hast taken away all thy wrath: thou hast turned thyself from the fierceness of thine anger. ^v3

4 Turn us, O God of our salvation, and cause thine anger toward us to cease. ^v4

5 Wilt thou be angry with us for ever? wilt thou draw out thine anger to all generations? ^v5

6 Wilt thou not revive us again: that thy people may rejoice in thee? ^v6

7 Shew us thy mercy, O Lord, and grant us thy salvation. ^v7

8 I will hear what God the Lord will speak: for he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: but let them not turn again to folly. ^v8

9 Surely his salvation is nigh them that fear him; that glory may dwell in our land. ^v9

10 Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other. ^v10

11 Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven. ^v11

12 Yea, the Lord shall give that which is good; and our land shall yield her increase. ^v12

13 Righteousness shall go before him; and shall set us in the way of his steps. ^v13
