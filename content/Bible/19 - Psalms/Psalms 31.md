# Psalms 31

1 In thee, O Lord, do I put my trust; let me never be ashamed: deliver me in thy righteousness. ^v1

2 Bow down thine ear to me; deliver me speedily: be thou my strong rock, for an house of defence to save me. ^v2

3 For thou art my rock and my fortress; therefore for thy name’s sake lead me, and guide me. ^v3

4 Pull me out of the net that they have laid privily for me: for thou art my strength. ^v4

5 Into thine hand I commit my spirit: thou hast redeemed me, O Lord God of truth. ^v5

6 I have hated them that regard lying vanities: but I trust in the Lord. ^v6

7 I will be glad and rejoice in thy mercy: for thou hast considered my trouble; thou hast known my soul in adversities; ^v7

8 And hast not shut me up into the hand of the enemy: thou hast set my feet in a large room. ^v8

9 Have mercy upon me, O Lord, for I am in trouble: mine eye is consumed with grief, yea, my soul and my belly. ^v9

10 For my life is spent with grief, and my years with sighing: my strength faileth because of mine iniquity, and my bones are consumed. ^v10

11 I was a reproach among all mine enemies, but especially among my neighbours, and a fear to mine acquaintance: they that did see me without fled from me. ^v11

12 I am forgotten as a dead man out of mind: I am like a broken vessel. ^v12

13 For I have heard the slander of many: fear was on every side: while they took counsel together against me, they devised to take away my life. ^v13

14 But I trusted in thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my God. ^v14

15 My times are in thy hand: deliver me from the hand of mine enemies, and from them that persecute me. ^v15

16 Make thy face to shine upon thy servant: save me for thy mercies’ sake. ^v16

17 Let me not be ashamed, O Lord; for I have called upon thee: let the wicked be ashamed, and let them be silent in the grave. ^v17

18 Let the lying lips be put to silence; which speak grievous things proudly and contemptuously against the righteous. ^v18

19 Oh how great is thy goodness, which thou hast laid up for them that fear thee; which thou hast wrought for them that trust in thee before the sons of men! ^v19

20 Thou shalt hide them in the secret of thy presence from the pride of man: thou shalt keep them secretly in a pavilion from the strife of tongues. ^v20

21 Blessed be the Lord: for he hath shewed me his marvellous kindness in a strong city. ^v21

22 For I said in my haste, I am cut off from before thine eyes: nevertheless thou heardest the voice of my supplications when I cried unto thee. ^v22

23 O love the Lord, all ye his saints: for the Lord preserveth the faithful, and plentifully rewardeth the proud doer. ^v23

24 Be of good courage, and he shall strengthen your heart, all ye that hope in the Lord. ^v24
