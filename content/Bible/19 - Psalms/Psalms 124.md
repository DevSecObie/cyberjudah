# Psalms 124

1 If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, now may Israel say; ^v1

2 If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, when men rose up against us: ^v2

3 Then they had swallowed us up quick, when their wrath was kindled against us: ^v3

4 Then the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul: ^v4

5 Then the proud waters had gone over our soul. ^v5

6 Blessed be the Lord, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth. ^v6

7 Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped. ^v7

8 Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth. ^v8
