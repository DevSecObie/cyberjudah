# Psalms 70

1 Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord. ^v1

2 Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt. ^v2

3 Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha. ^v3

4 Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified. ^v4

5 But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying. ^v5
