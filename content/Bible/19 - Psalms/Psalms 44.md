# Psalms 44

1 We have heard with our ears, O God, our fathers have told us, what work thou didst in their days, in the times of old. ^v1

2 How thou didst drive out the heathen with thy hand, and plantedst them; how thou didst afflict the people, and cast them out. ^v2

3 For they got not the land in possession by their own sword, neither did their own arm save them: but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou hadst a favour unto them. ^v3

4 Thou art my King, O God: command deliverances for Jacob. ^v4

5 Through thee will we push down our enemies: through thy name will we tread them under that rise up against us. ^v5

6 For I will not trust in my bow, neither shall my sword save me. ^v6

7 But thou hast saved us from our enemies, and hast put them to shame that hated us. ^v7

8 In God we boast all the day long, and praise thy name for ever. Selah. ^v8

9 But thou hast cast off, and put us to shame; and goest not forth with our armies. ^v9

10 Thou makest us to turn back from the enemy: and they which hate us spoil for themselves. ^v10

11 Thou hast given us like sheep appointed for meat; and hast scattered us among the heathen. ^v11

12 Thou sellest thy people for nought, and dost not increase thy wealth by their price. ^v12

13 Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and a derision to them that are round about us. ^v13

14 Thou makest us a byword among the heathen, a shaking of the head among the people. ^v14

15 My confusion is continually before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me, ^v15

16 For the voice of him that reproacheth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and avenger. ^v16

17 All this is come upon us; yet have we not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt falsely in thy covenant. ^v17

18 Our heart is not turned back, neither have our steps declined from thy way; ^v18

19 Though thou hast sore broken us in the place of dragons, and covered us with the shadow of death. ^v19

20 If we have forgotten the name of our God, or stretched out our hands to a strange god; ^v20

21 Shall not God search this out? for he knoweth the secrets of the heart. ^v21

22 Yea, for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter. ^v22

23 Awake, why sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, cast us not off for ever. ^v23

24 Wherefore hidest thou thy face, and forgettest our affliction and our oppression? ^v24

25 For our soul is bowed down to the dust: our belly cleaveth unto the earth. ^v25

26 Arise for our help, and redeem us for thy mercies’ sake. ^v26
