# Psalms 123

1 Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens. ^v1

2 Behold, as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress; so our eyes wait upon the Lord our God, until that he have mercy upon us. ^v2

3 Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt. ^v3

4 Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud. ^v4
