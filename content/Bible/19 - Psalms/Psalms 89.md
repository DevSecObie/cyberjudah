# Psalms 89

1 I will sing of the mercies of the Lord for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations. ^v1

2 For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very heavens. ^v2

3 I have made a covenant with my chosen, I have sworn unto David my servant, ^v3

4 Thy seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Selah. ^v4

5 And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O Lord: thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints. ^v5

6 For who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord? who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto the Lord? ^v6

7 God is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence of all them that are about him. ^v7

8 O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee? ^v8

9 Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them. ^v9

10 Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy strong arm. ^v10

11 The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: as for the world and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them. ^v11

12 The north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name. ^v12

13 Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand. ^v13

14 Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face. ^v14

15 Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance. ^v15

16 In thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted. ^v16

17 For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy favour our horn shall be exalted. ^v17

18 For the Lord is our defence; and the Holy One of Israel is our king. ^v18

19 Then thou spakest in vision to thy holy one, and saidst, I have laid help upon one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen out of the people. ^v19

20 I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him: ^v20

21 With whom my hand shall be established: mine arm also shall strengthen him. ^v21

22 The enemy shall not exact upon him; nor the son of wickedness afflict him. ^v22

23 And I will beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him. ^v23

24 But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted. ^v24

25 I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers. ^v25

26 He shall cry unto me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation. ^v26

27 Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth. ^v27

28 My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him. ^v28

29 His seed also will I make to endure for ever, and his throne as the days of heaven. ^v29

30 If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments; ^v30

31 If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments; ^v31

32 Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes. ^v32

33 Nevertheless my lovingkindness will I not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail. ^v33

34 My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips. ^v34

35 Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie unto David. ^v35

36 His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me. ^v36

37 It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven. Selah. ^v37

38 But thou hast cast off and abhorred, thou hast been wroth with thine anointed. ^v38

39 Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his crown by casting it to the ground. ^v39

40 Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast brought his strong holds to ruin. ^v40

41 All that pass by the way spoil him: he is a reproach to his neighbours. ^v41

42 Thou hast set up the right hand of his adversaries; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice. ^v42

43 Thou hast also turned the edge of his sword, and hast not made him to stand in the battle. ^v43

44 Thou hast made his glory to cease, and cast his throne down to the ground. ^v44

45 The days of his youth hast thou shortened: thou hast covered him with shame. Selah. ^v45

46 How long, Lord? wilt thou hide thyself for ever? shall thy wrath burn like fire? ^v46

47 Remember how short my time is: wherefore hast thou made all men in vain? ^v47

48 What man is he that liveth, and shall not see death? shall he deliver his soul from the hand of the grave? Selah. ^v48

49 Lord, where are thy former lovingkindnesses, which thou swarest unto David in thy truth? ^v49

50 Remember, Lord, the reproach of thy servants; how I do bear in my bosom the reproach of all the mighty people; ^v50

51 Wherewith thine enemies have reproached, O Lord; wherewith they have reproached the footsteps of thine anointed. ^v51

52 Blessed be the Lord for evermore. Amen, and Amen. ^v52
