# Psalms 146

1 Praise ye the Lord. Praise the Lord, O my soul. ^v1

2 While I live will I praise the Lord: I will sing praises unto my God while I have any being. ^v2

3 Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help. ^v3

4 His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish. ^v4

5 Happy is he that hath the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the Lord his God: ^v5

6 Which made heaven, and earth, the sea, and all that therein is: which keepeth truth for ever: ^v6

7 Which executeth judgment for the oppressed: which giveth food to the hungry. The Lord looseth the prisoners: ^v7

8 The Lord openeth the eyes of the blind: the Lord raiseth them that are bowed down: the Lord loveth the righteous: ^v8

9 The Lord preserveth the strangers; he relieveth the fatherless and widow: but the way of the wicked he turneth upside down. ^v9

10 The Lord shall reign for ever, even thy God, O Zion, unto all generations. Praise ye the Lord. ^v10
