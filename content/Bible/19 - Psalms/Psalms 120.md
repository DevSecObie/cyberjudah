# Psalms 120

1 In my distress I cried unto the Lord, and he heard me. ^v1

2 Deliver my soul, O Lord, from lying lips, and from a deceitful tongue. ^v2

3 What shall be given unto thee? or what shall be done unto thee, thou false tongue? ^v3

4 Sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals of juniper. ^v4

5 Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech, that I dwell in the tents of Kedar! ^v5

6 My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace. ^v6

7 I am for peace: but when I speak, they are for war. ^v7
