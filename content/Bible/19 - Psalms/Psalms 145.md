# Psalms 145

1 I will extol thee, my God, O king; and I will bless thy name for ever and ever. ^v1

2 Every day will I bless thee; and I will praise thy name for ever and ever. ^v2

3 Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised; and his greatness is unsearchable. ^v3

4 One generation shall praise thy works to another, and shall declare thy mighty acts. ^v4

5 I will speak of the glorious honour of thy majesty, and of thy wondrous works. ^v5

6 And men shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and I will declare thy greatness. ^v6

7 They shall abundantly utter the memory of thy great goodness, and shall sing of thy righteousness. ^v7

8 The Lord is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy. ^v8

9 The Lord is good to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works. ^v9

10 All thy works shall praise thee, O Lord; and thy saints shall bless thee. ^v10

11 They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom, and talk of thy power; ^v11

12 To make known to the sons of men his mighty acts, and the glorious majesty of his kingdom. ^v12

13 Thy kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations. ^v13

14 The Lord upholdeth all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down. ^v14

15 The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest them their meat in due season. ^v15

16 Thou openest thine hand, and satisfiest the desire of every living thing. ^v16

17 The Lord is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works. ^v17

18 The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth. ^v18

19 He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him: he also will hear their cry, and will save them. ^v19

20 The Lord preserveth all them that love him: but all the wicked will he destroy. ^v20

21 My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever. ^v21
