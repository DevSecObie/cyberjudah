# Psalms 12

1 Help, Lord; for the godly man ceaseth; for the faithful fail from among the children of men. ^v1

2 They speak vanity every one with his neighbour: with flattering lips and with a double heart do they speak. ^v2

3 The Lord shall cut off all flattering lips, and the tongue that speaketh proud things: ^v3

4 Who have said, With our tongue will we prevail; our lips are our own: who is lord over us? ^v4

5 For the oppression of the poor, for the sighing of the needy, now will I arise, saith the Lord; I will set him in safety from him that puffeth at him. ^v5

6 The words of the Lord are pure words: as silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times. ^v6

7 Thou shalt keep them, O Lord, thou shalt preserve them from this generation for ever. ^v7

8 The wicked walk on every side, when the vilest men are exalted. ^v8
