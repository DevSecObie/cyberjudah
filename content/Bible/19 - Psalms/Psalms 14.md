# Psalms 14

1 The fool hath said in his heart, There is no God. They are corrupt, they have done abominable works, there is none that doeth good. ^v1

2 The Lord looked down from heaven upon the children of men, to see if there were any that did understand, and seek God. ^v2

3 They are all gone aside, they are all together become filthy: there is none that doeth good, no, not one. ^v3

4 Have all the workers of iniquity no knowledge? who eat up my people as they eat bread, and call not upon the Lord. ^v4

5 There were they in great fear: for God is in the generation of the righteous. ^v5

6 Ye have shamed the counsel of the poor, because the Lord is his refuge. ^v6

7 Oh that the salvation of Israel were come out of Zion! when the Lord bringeth back the captivity of his people, Jacob shall rejoice, and Israel shall be glad. ^v7
