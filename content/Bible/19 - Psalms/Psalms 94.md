# Psalms 94

1 O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth; O God, to whom vengeance belongeth, shew thyself. ^v1

2 Lift up thyself, thou judge of the earth: render a reward to the proud. ^v2

3 Lord, how long shall the wicked, how long shall the wicked triumph? ^v3

4 How long shall they utter and speak hard things? and all the workers of iniquity boast themselves? ^v4

5 They break in pieces thy people, O Lord, and afflict thine heritage. ^v5

6 They slay the widow and the stranger, and murder the fatherless. ^v6

7 Yet they say, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it. ^v7

8 Understand, ye brutish among the people: and ye fools, when will ye be wise? ^v8

9 He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? he that formed the eye, shall he not see? ^v9

10 He that chastiseth the heathen, shall not he correct? he that teacheth man knowledge, shall not he know? ^v10

11 The Lord knoweth the thoughts of man, that they are vanity. ^v11

12 Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Lord, and teachest him out of thy law; ^v12

13 That thou mayest give him rest from the days of adversity, until the pit be digged for the wicked. ^v13

14 For the Lord will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance. ^v14

15 But judgment shall return unto righteousness: and all the upright in heart shall follow it. ^v15

16 Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity? ^v16

17 Unless the Lord had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence. ^v17

18 When I said, My foot slippeth; thy mercy, O Lord, held me up. ^v18

19 In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul. ^v19

20 Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by a law? ^v20

21 They gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood. ^v21

22 But the Lord is my defence; and my God is the rock of my refuge. ^v22

23 And he shall bring upon them their own iniquity, and shall cut them off in their own wickedness; yea, the Lord our God shall cut them off. ^v23
