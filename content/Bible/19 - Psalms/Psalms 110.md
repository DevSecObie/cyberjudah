# Psalms 110

1 The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool. ^v1

2 The Lord shall send the rod of thy strength out of Zion: rule thou in the midst of thine enemies. ^v2

3 Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth. ^v3

4 The Lord hath sworn, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek. ^v4

5 The Lord at thy right hand shall strike through kings in the day of his wrath. ^v5

6 He shall judge among the heathen, he shall fill the places with the dead bodies; he shall wound the heads over many countries. ^v6

7 He shall drink of the brook in the way: therefore shall he lift up the head. ^v7
