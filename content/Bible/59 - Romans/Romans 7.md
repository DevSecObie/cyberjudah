# Romans 7

1 Know ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,) how that the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth? ^v1

2 For the woman which hath an husband is bound by the law to her husband so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband. ^v2

3 So then if, while her husband liveth, she be married to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if her husband be dead, she is free from that law; so that she is no adulteress, though she be married to another man. ^v3

4 Wherefore, my brethren, ye also are become dead to the law by the body of Christ; that ye should be married to another, even to him who is raised from the dead, that we should bring forth fruit unto God. ^v4

5 For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members to bring forth fruit unto death. ^v5

6 But now we are delivered from the law, that being dead wherein we were held; that we should serve in newness of spirit, and not in the oldness of the letter. ^v6

7 What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet. ^v7

8 But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law sin was dead. ^v8

9 For I was alive without the law once: but when the commandment came, sin revived, and I died. ^v9

10 And the commandment, which was ordained to life, I found to be unto death. ^v10

11 For sin, taking occasion by the commandment, deceived me, and by it slew me. ^v11

12 Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good. ^v12

13 Was then that which is good made death unto me? God forbid. But sin, that it might appear sin, working death in me by that which is good; that sin by the commandment might become exceeding sinful. ^v13

14 For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin. ^v14

15 For that which I do I allow not: for what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I. ^v15

16 If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that it is good. ^v16

17 Now then it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me. ^v17

18 For I know that in me (that is, in my flesh,) dwelleth no good thing: for to will is present with me; but how to perform that which is good I find not. ^v18

19 For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do. ^v19

20 Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me. ^v20

21 I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me. ^v21

22 For I delight in the law of God after the inward man: ^v22

23 But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members. ^v23

24 O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death? ^v24

25 I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God; but with the flesh the law of sin. ^v25
