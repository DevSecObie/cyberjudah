# Ephesians 6

1 Children, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is right. ^v1

2 Honour thy father and mother; (which is the first commandment with promise;) ^v2

3 That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth. ^v3

4 And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord. ^v4

5 Servants, be obedient to them that are your masters according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as unto Christ; ^v5

6 Not with eyeservice, as menpleasers; but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart; ^v6

7 With good will doing service, as to the Lord, and not to men: ^v7

8 Knowing that whatsoever good thing any man doeth, the same shall he receive of the Lord, whether he be bond or free. ^v8

9 And, ye masters, do the same things unto them, forbearing threatening: knowing that your Master also is in heaven; neither is there respect of persons with him. ^v9

10 Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. ^v10

11 Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. ^v11

12 For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places. ^v12

13 Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. ^v13

14 Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness; ^v14

15 And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace; ^v15

16 Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. ^v16

17 And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God: ^v17

18 Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints; ^v18

19 And for me, that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel, ^v19

20 For which I am an ambassador in bonds: that therein I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak. ^v20

21 But that ye also may know my affairs, and how I do, Tychicus, a beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, shall make known to you all things: ^v21

22 Whom I have sent unto you for the same purpose, that ye might know our affairs, and that he might comfort your hearts. ^v22

23 Peace be to the brethren, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ. ^v23

24 Grace be with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity. Amen.  Written from Rome unto the Ephesians by Tychicus. ^v24
