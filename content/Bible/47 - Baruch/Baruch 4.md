# Baruch 4

1 This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endureth for ever: all they that keep it shall come to life; but such as leave it shall die. ^v1

2 Turn thee, O Jacob, and take hold of it: walk in the presence of the light thereof, that thou mayest be illuminated. ^v2

3 Give not thine honour to another, nor the things that are profitable unto thee to a strange nation. ^v3

4 O Israel, happy are we: for things that are pleasing to God are made known unto us. ^v4

5 Be of good cheer, my people, the memorial of Israel. ^v5

6 Ye were sold to the nations, not for your destruction: but because ye moved God to wrath, ye were delivered unto the enemies. ^v6

7 For ye provoked him that made you by sacrificing unto devils, and not to God. ^v7

8 Ye have forgotten the everlasting God, that brought you up; and ye have grieved Jerusalem, that nursed you. ^v8

9 For when she saw the wrath of God coming upon you, she said, Hearken, O ye that dwell about Sion: God hath brought upon me great mourning; ^v9

10 For I saw the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting brought upon them. ^v10

11 With joy did I nourish them; but sent them away with weeping and mourning. ^v11

12 Let no man rejoice over me, a widow, and forsaken of many, who for the sins of my children am left desolate; because they departed from the law of God. ^v12

13 They knew not his statutes, nor walked in the ways of his commandments, nor trod in the paths of discipline in his righteousness. ^v13

14 Let them that dwell about Sion come, and remember ye the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting hath brought upon them. ^v14

15 For he hath brought a nation upon them from far, a shameless nation, and of a strange language, who neither reverenced old man, nor pitied child. ^v15

16 These have carried away the dear beloved children of the widow, and left her that was alone desolate without daughters. ^v16

17 But what can I help you? ^v17

18 For he that brought these plagues upon you will deliver you from the hands of your enemies. ^v18

19 Go your way, O my children, go your way: for I am left desolate. ^v19

20 I have put off the clothing of peace, and put upon me the sackcloth of my prayer: I will cry unto the Everlasting in my days. ^v20

21 Be of good cheer, O my children, cry unto the Lord, and he will deliver you from the power and hand of the enemies. ^v21

22 For my hope is in the Everlasting, that he will save you; and joy is come unto me from the Holy One, because of the mercy which shall soon come unto you from the Everlasting our Saviour. ^v22

23 For I sent you out with mourning and weeping: but God will give you to me again with joy and gladness for ever. ^v23

24 Like as now the neighbours of Sion have seen your captivity: so shall they see shortly your salvation from our God which shall come upon you with great glory, and brightness of the Everlasting. ^v24

25 My children, suffer patiently the wrath that is come upon you from God: for thine enemy hath persecuted thee; but shortly thou shalt see his destruction, and shalt tread upon his neck. ^v25

26 My delicate ones have gone rough ways, and were taken away as a flock caught of the enemies. ^v26

27 Be of good comfort, O my children, and cry unto God: for ye shall be remembered of him that brought these things upon you. ^v27

28 For as it was your mind to go astray from God: so, being returned, seek him ten times more. ^v28

29 For he that hath brought these plagues upon you shall bring you everlasting joy with your salvation. ^v29

30 Take a good heart, O Jerusalem: for he that gave thee that name will comfort thee. ^v30

31 Miserable are they that afflicted thee, and rejoiced at thy fall. ^v31

32 Miserable are the cities which thy children served: miserable is she that received thy sons. ^v32

33 For as she rejoiced at thy ruin, and was glad of thy fall: so shall she be grieved for her own desolation. ^v33

34 For I will take away the rejoicing of her great multitude, and her pride shall be turned into mourning. ^v34

35 For fire shall come upon her from the Everlasting, long to endure; and she shall be inhabited of devils for a great time. ^v35

36 O Jerusalem, look about thee toward the east, and behold the joy that cometh unto thee from God. ^v36

37 Lo, thy sons come, whom thou sentest away, they come gathered together from the east to the west by the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the glory of God. ^v37
