## Introduction

The name of the class: the bold and the bashful. This is a continuation of Deacon Malachi's class from two weeks ago on being just as a leader in Israel. Now we get the flip side: how you can fall as a leader that is bashful. Coming back from the overseas Passover trips, we have an issue, and it is not just overseas, it is here in America too, of brothers and leaders that are bashful. You do not want to rock the boat. You do not want to correct when you see something wrong. You turn a blind eye because he is your friend or she is your friend. So this class touches on what can possibly happen to you when you are a bashful leader.

Definitions read in class: bashfulness is the quality of being shy, easily embarrassed, or overly modest in social situations. It typically involves an acute sense of self-consciousness, a tendency to avoid the spotlight, and feelings of timidity around others. Key characteristics: social timidity, a hesitance to interact, often caused by a fear of negative judgment; modesty; physical cues like blushing, a lowered gaze, or awkwardness. Timidity is a lack of courage or confidence. In a social situation like class, bashful brothers want to hide amongst the flock. You do not want to be seen, you do not want to be heard, you do not want to stand out; you just want to blend in and fall to the wayside. Why do you think brothers do not come to the war room all the time? Because you are bashful. You do not want brothers to correct you on your sin and your bad ways, because you are comfortable in those sins. A lot of brothers and sisters have lack of confidence and low self-esteem, which goes into a lack of faith: not just a lack of faith in the Lord, a lack of faith in yourself. Whatever institution you grew up in, your peers, your teachers, your pastors, your churches, created what we have in you today.

## In Closing

Bashfulness goes into lack of confidence, low self-esteem, self-consciousness, timidity, and shyness. Because Saul was all those things, he was self-willed, he made excuses, he feared the people and was manipulated by them, and he lost the kingdom and the Lord departed from him. The Lord cannot use a bashful leader. But the righteous man stands in great boldness: faith brings confidence, faith comes by hearing the word of God, and this kind goes out by prayer and fasting. Be willing to be uncomfortable, break yourself of your weaknesses, stand up manfully, and be turned into another man.
