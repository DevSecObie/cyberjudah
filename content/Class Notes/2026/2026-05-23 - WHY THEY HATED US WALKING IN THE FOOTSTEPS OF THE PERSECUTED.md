## In Closing

Why they hated us? The answer was never a mystery: the nations were created with enmity toward Israel, and our own rebellion brought the curses on top of it. So stop asking the question and walk in the footsteps of the persecuted: the prophets, the apostles, and Christ himself were all hated before us. Do not seek love where there is no love; do not trade the eternal for the temporary. Rejoice, be glad, gather together, and ride this Israelite thing out, because the judgment on our enemies is prepared, our judgment is almost ended, and the kingdom is ours. 12 tribes, worldwide.
