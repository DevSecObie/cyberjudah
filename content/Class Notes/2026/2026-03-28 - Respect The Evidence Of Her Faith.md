## Introduction

The title of the class is Respect: The Evidence Of Her Faith. A lot of our relationships look beautiful in front of everybody, but when you go back home it is something else. A lot of our sisters do not really have a grasp on what their role and demeanor is supposed to be toward their husband, no matter how long they have been in the truth. We as the men of the Lord understand the fight in this world and how difficult it is for them, because we understand the original sin and where it all began; understanding that they are the weaker vessel, it is classes like this that constantly remind them why they feel the way they feel and how Satan is lurking. Sisters, pay attention; do not be scared.

- Slideshow read in class, ten signs she does not respect the Christ in you: she talks over you or interrupts you (respect listens; disrespect wants to prove a point); she publicly corrects or embarrasses you (a respectful woman covers her man, not exposes him); she ignores your instructions or decisions (selective obedience equals rebellion; if your husband gives you an order, that is law, and you should race to get it done like you race the sun before the Sabbath); she speaks to you with attitude or contempt (respect is heard in tone, not just words; speak to your husband the way you want your children to speak to you); she does not value your presence (when your husband comes through the door, be eager that he is home; adjust the house to what he likes, because that is your husband); she competes with you instead of supporting you (she was built to assist, not oppose; take what he wants and bring it to fruition, that is a team); she shares your business with others (if your life is not safe with her, respect is missing); she does not seek your counsel (respect consults; disrespect moves solo); she disrespects you in subtle ways (body language, eye rolls, dismissive looks: disrespect often whispers before it shouts); and she does not follow your lead spiritually (picks and chooses what to follow).
- Bonus sign: she disrespects other men. Some brothers are in denial: if other men are complaining about your wife's demeanor, she has normalized being disrespectful to you. The people around you will let you know.
