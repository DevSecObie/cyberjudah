## Introduction

Opening prayer: Lord, we come before you today coming into the last day of the feast of unleavened bread, which you have commanded us to keep throughout all our generations. This week we have kept thy feast, which is symbolic of getting rid of the leaven from our heart. Take away that old leaven, that sin, from our people. Help us to get rid of the malice, the envy, the bitterness, the strife. Watch over all the brothers traveling right now, waking Israel up throughout the four corners of the earth. Heal the sick amongst us, and send your son to deliver us out of these lands of our captivity, out of this spiritual Egypt, and judge these nations for all the evil that they have done to us, the same way you judged them in the past.

We are in the days of unleavened bread, the season where we were delivered from ancient Egypt. When the Most High delivered us from Egypt, he brought plagues on Egypt. The same way he showed signs and brought plagues on that people, it will be the same thing again in these last days when we are delivered from spiritual Egypt, which is America. At the beginning of the feast the lesson was Exodus From America, Modern Day Egypt: how the same curses written in Deuteronomy 28 that were on us in Egypt are on us here in America. Tonight we go into the plagues. The same plagues that fell on ancient Egypt are the same plagues God is going to bring on these Egyptians today, on modern day Egypt, which is America, but on another technological level. Write it down: the plagues coming to America.
