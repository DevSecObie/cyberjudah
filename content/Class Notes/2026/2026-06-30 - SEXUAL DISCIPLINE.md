## Introduction

Happy new moon, Israel. Tonight is a new moon feast, so we are going to touch a couple of scriptures concerning the new moon and then go into the lesson prepared for tonight. The topic is sexual discipline. The summer is here, and the job of a leader and a teacher is to know the state of the flock. A lot of brothers and sisters keep falling into sexual sins. This is going to be a two part lesson. Some of you young men are caught up with pornography; a lot of you are battling that right there, and this class goes into the scriptures you can use to help you overcome that demon. Not just you brothers, but you sisters too, because everyone has to learn sexual discipline; some sisters get caught up in fornication and some sisters watch porn too. Sometimes we forget to talk about that.

What I am hoping out of this lesson is to give you what you need to fight and to overcome the demons you are fighting. Our forefathers always exercised sexual discipline. In this society today it is hard, and it is hard for a reason. It is hard by design.
