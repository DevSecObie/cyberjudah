## Introduction

Thumbnail video: "I'm just not good enough. God ain't with me. There's no point in even trying."

Captain Gad teaching from the West Coast. Today's topic is going to be a topic that a lot of brothers and sisters don't really want to go over. Being here on what they call the rest coast, I'm hearing a lot of brothers saying they deal with low self-esteem, depression, anxiety. And a lot of times what they are doing, they are using alcohol as a coping mechanism. Instead of dealing with those issues, they turn to the bottle. So today's class we are going over the bottle, the battle, and the Bible.

The bottle: the coping mechanism. Alcoholism, addiction, escapism. The battle: the underlying struggle. Depression, shame, failure, hopelessness, trauma, sin, spiritual warfare. The Bible: the solution and source. Hope, truth, healing, transformation. All praise to the Most High for the hope and healing program; you brothers who know you deal with alcoholism need to be in that program.

I wanted to go over Juneteenth, but Juneteenth makes no sense. What is a black holiday when black people are still being murdered? What is a black holiday when babies are being murdered, and brothers are being thrown in prison for self-defense? You say no justice, no peace, but thank God for Juneteenth. That had to be said before we start.

Video played from the Soft White Underbelly interviews: a man about 50 years old recalls his mother pulling off in a truck and leaving him outside at night when he was three or four; a neighbor took him in, fed him, and bathed him. Pause it. That is what trauma sounds like. These are the events that a lot of brothers are using alcohol and drugs to cope with.
