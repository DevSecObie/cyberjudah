# Numbers 15: Offerings, Presumptuous Sin, and the Fringes

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 15]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Numbers 15|Numbers 15]] through 18: the offerings to be made in the land and the one law for Israel and the stranger, the difference between sin of ignorance and presumptuous sin with the man that gathered sticks on the sabbath, the rebellion of Korah, Aaron's rod that budded, and the charge and portion of the priests and the Levites. This was a reading session with brief comment on the verses; no questions were raised at the end.

**[[Numbers 15#^v1|Numbers 15:1-3]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v1]]
![[Numbers 15#^v2]]
![[Numbers 15#^v3]]

- When Israel came into the land of their habitations, they were to make an offering by fire unto the Lord: a burnt offering, a sacrifice in performing a vow, a freewill offering, or an offering in the solemn feasts, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
- These are all the accounts on which you would sacrifice to the Lord, taken of the herd or of the flock.

**[[Numbers 15#^v4|Numbers 15:4-10]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v4]]
![[Numbers 15#^v5]]
![[Numbers 15#^v6]]
![[Numbers 15#^v7]]
![[Numbers 15#^v8]]
![[Numbers 15#^v9]]
![[Numbers 15#^v10]]

- Every sacrifice came with its measured meat offering of flour mingled with oil and its drink offering of wine, and the measures scaled up with the animal: a lamb, a ram, and then a bullock.
- An hin is roughly a gallon to a gallon and a half, so the bullock, the greatest offering, carried half an hin of oil and half an hin of wine.

**[[Numbers 15#^v11|Numbers 15:11-13]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v11]]
![[Numbers 15#^v12]]
![[Numbers 15#^v13]]

- Thus it was done for one bullock, one ram, one lamb, or a kid; the kid is the goat. Everyone did according to the number they prepared.
- All that are born of the country did these things after this manner when offering an offering made by fire of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

**[[Numbers 15#^v14|Numbers 15:14-16]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v14]]
![[Numbers 15#^v15]]
![[Numbers 15#^v16]]

- The scripture separates the two: the children of Israel and the stranger that sojourns with them. If a stranger joined himself to Israel, he had to keep the law to journey with them in the land.
- One ordinance and one law stood both for the congregation and for the stranger that was joined with them, an ordinance for ever in their generations: as ye are, so shall the stranger be before the Lord.

**[[Numbers 15#^v17|Numbers 15:17-21]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v17]]
![[Numbers 15#^v18]]
![[Numbers 15#^v19]]
![[Numbers 15#^v20]]
![[Numbers 15#^v21]]

- When Israel ate of the bread of the land, they offered up a cake of the first of their dough as an heave offering unto the Lord, as they did the heave offering of the threshingfloor.
- The heave offering was a particular motion in presenting it: the offering was held up to the Most High and brought back down; that is why it was called the heave offering.

**[[Numbers 15#^v22|Numbers 15:22-26]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v22]]
![[Numbers 15#^v23]]
![[Numbers 15#^v24]]
![[Numbers 15#^v25]]
![[Numbers 15#^v26]]

- If the whole congregation sinned through ignorance, without the knowledge of what they were doing, and the priest came behind them and corrected them on it, then all the congregation offered a young bullock for a burnt offering and a kid of the goats for a sin offering.
- The priest made an atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it was forgiven them, for it was ignorance; the stranger among them was included in that forgiveness.

**[[Numbers 15#^v27|Numbers 15:27-29]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v27]]
![[Numbers 15#^v28]]
![[Numbers 15#^v29]]

- If any single soul sinned through ignorance, he brought a she goat of the first year for a sin offering, and the priest made an atonement for him, and it was forgiven him.
- One law covered him that sinned through ignorance, both for him born among the children of Israel and for the stranger that sojourned among them.

**[[Numbers 15#^v30|Numbers 15:30-31]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v30]]
![[Numbers 15#^v31]]

- But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously sinned willfully; he thought about what he was doing before he did it. Whether born in the land or a stranger, that soul reproached the Lord and was cut off from among his people.
- Because he despised the word of the Lord and broke his commandment, that soul was utterly cut off, meaning put to death, and his iniquity was upon him.

**[[Numbers 15#^v32|Numbers 15:32-36]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v32]]
![[Numbers 15#^v33]]
![[Numbers 15#^v34]]
![[Numbers 15#^v35]]
![[Numbers 15#^v36]]

- The man found gathering sticks on the sabbath day is the example of the presumptuous sin just described: someone who thought about the sin before he did it. He was not picking up sticks just to pick up sticks; he was gathering them to kindle a fire and cook on the sabbath.
- They put him in ward, meaning they locked him up, because it was not declared what should be done to him; then the Lord commanded Moses that the man be surely put to death, and all the congregation stoned him with stones without the camp.
- He did not even get to the act itself; he had the intention, and for that willful intent he was put to death.

**[[Numbers 15#^v37|Numbers 15:37-41]]**

![[Numbers 15#^v37]]
![[Numbers 15#^v38]]
![[Numbers 15#^v39]]
![[Numbers 15#^v40]]
![[Numbers 15#^v41]]

- Because of that act, the Most High gave Israel the commandment to make fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and to put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue.
- The fringe is to look upon and remember all the commandments of the Lord and do them, and not to seek after your own heart and your own eyes; so that when we think of sin, we look at the fringes and remember the laws of the Most High instead of going and picking up sticks.
- I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God.

# Numbers 16: The Rebellion of Korah

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 16]]

**[[Numbers 16#^v1|Numbers 16:1-3]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v1]]
![[Numbers 16#^v2]]
![[Numbers 16#^v3]]

- Korah was of Levi, and Dathan, Abiram, and On were of Reuben. With them rose up two hundred and fifty princes of the assembly, famous in the congregation, men of renown; that is two hundred and fifty three in all against Moses and Aaron.
- These were known and powerful men among the congregation, and their influence, because of who they were, is how they were able to persuade people.
- They charged Moses and Aaron with taking too much upon themselves and lifting themselves above the congregation. They thought Moses and Aaron carried themselves as better than the rest, but the Most High is the one who chose them as leaders and was dealing with them; the accusers were jealous of them.

**[[Numbers 16#^v4|Numbers 16:4-7]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v4]]
![[Numbers 16#^v5]]
![[Numbers 16#^v6]]
![[Numbers 16#^v7]]

- When Moses heard it, he fell upon his face, and told Korah and all his company: even to morrow the Lord will shew who are his, and who is holy; the Most High himself would show whom he chose and whom he was dealing with.
- The test was censers: take you censers, Korah and all his company, put fire therein and incense before the Lord, and the man whom the Lord doth choose, he shall be holy. Ye take too much upon you, ye sons of Levi.

**[[Numbers 16#^v8|Numbers 16:8-11]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v8]]
![[Numbers 16#^v9]]
![[Numbers 16#^v10]]
![[Numbers 16#^v11]]

- Moses exhorted Korah: seemeth it but a small thing that the God of Israel hath separated you from the congregation to bring you near to himself, to do the service of the tabernacle? Only Levi could do the service of the tabernacle, and that was a heavy job in itself.
- You already stand before the congregation and minister unto them; the Most High is still dealing with you. But they sought the priesthood also, and for that cause Korah and all his company were gathered together against the Lord: and what is Aaron, that ye murmur against him?

**[[Numbers 16#^v12|Numbers 16:12-14]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v12]]
![[Numbers 16#^v13]]
![[Numbers 16#^v14]]

- Dathan and Abiram would not come up when Moses called them. They were being rebellious against Moses because they wanted his spot, accusing him of bringing them out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill them in the wilderness and of making himself a prince over them.
- Those same spirits are around today; the same thing that went on in the old time goes on now.

**[[Numbers 16#^v15|Numbers 16:15-19]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v15]]
![[Numbers 16#^v16]]
![[Numbers 16#^v17]]
![[Numbers 16#^v18]]
![[Numbers 16#^v19]]

- Moses was very wroth and said unto the Lord, respect not thou their offering: I have not taken one ass from them, neither have I hurt one of them.
- Every man took his censer, two hundred and fifty censers, with Aaron and Korah also, put fire and incense in them, and stood in the door of the tabernacle of the congregation; and Korah gathered all the congregation against Moses and Aaron, and the glory of the Lord appeared unto all the congregation.

**[[Numbers 16#^v20|Numbers 16:20-22]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v20]]
![[Numbers 16#^v21]]
![[Numbers 16#^v22]]

- The Lord told Moses and Aaron to separate themselves from among the congregation, that he might consume them in a moment: get away from them, because judgment is about to come.
- Moses and Aaron fell upon their faces and pleaded: O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh, shall one man sin, and wilt thou be wroth with all the congregation? They still had that love for their people and pleaded with the Lord not to deal with the whole congregation over one man's sin.

**[[Numbers 16#^v23|Numbers 16:23-27]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v23]]
![[Numbers 16#^v24]]
![[Numbers 16#^v25]]
![[Numbers 16#^v26]]
![[Numbers 16#^v27]]

- The Lord commanded the congregation to get up from about the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram; Moses warned them to depart from the tents of these wicked men and touch nothing of theirs, lest they be consumed in all their sins and put to death in all that wickedness.
- The congregation got up from the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram on every side; and Dathan and Abiram came out and stood in the door of their tents with their wives, their sons, and their little children.

**[[Numbers 16#^v28|Numbers 16:28-30]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v28]]
![[Numbers 16#^v29]]
![[Numbers 16#^v30]]

- Moses put the proof on the Most High: hereby ye shall know that the Lord hath sent me to do all these works, for I have not done them of mine own mind. If these men die the common death of all men, then the Lord hath not sent me; if the Most High does not do something out of the ordinary to put them to death, then the Lord had not sent him.
- But if the Lord make a new thing, and the earth open her mouth and swallow them up, and they go down quick into the pit, then ye shall understand that these men have provoked the Lord.

**[[Numbers 16#^v31|Numbers 16:31-34]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v31]]
![[Numbers 16#^v32]]
![[Numbers 16#^v33]]
![[Numbers 16#^v34]]

- As he made an end of speaking, the ground clave asunder under them, and the earth opened her mouth and swallowed them up, their houses, all the men that appertained unto Korah, and all their goods; anybody that had something to do with them went down, with all they owned.
- The earth just opened and closed upon them, and they perished from among the congregation; all Israel round about fled at the cry of them, for you could hear them crying as they were swallowed up, and they said, lest the earth swallow us up also.

**[[Numbers 16#^v35|Numbers 16:35]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v35]]

- The other two hundred and fifty men that followed them and offered incense did not escape either: there came out a fire from the Lord and consumed them.

**[[Numbers 16#^v36|Numbers 16:36-40]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v36]]
![[Numbers 16#^v37]]
![[Numbers 16#^v38]]
![[Numbers 16#^v39]]
![[Numbers 16#^v40]]

- The Lord told Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest to take up the censers out of the burning, for they are hallowed, and scatter the fire yonder; the censers of these sinners against their own souls were made broad plates for a covering of the altar, because they were offered before the Lord.
- The plates were a memorial and a sign unto the children of Israel, that no stranger which is not of the seed of Aaron come near to offer incense before the Lord, that he be not as Korah and as his company.

**[[Numbers 16#^v41|Numbers 16:41-45]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v41]]
![[Numbers 16#^v42]]
![[Numbers 16#^v43]]
![[Numbers 16#^v44]]
![[Numbers 16#^v45]]

- On the morrow, the very next day, all the congregation murmured against Moses and Aaron, saying, ye have killed the people of the Lord. After they had just seen the earth open up and swallow men, they still would not let it go and gathered against Moses again; that is just how rebellious we are.
- The Most High saw what was going on, and the cloud covered the tabernacle and the glory of the Lord appeared; Moses and Aaron came before the tabernacle because they knew the Most High was ready to deal with them.
- The Lord came down ready to put them to death again, saying get you up from among this congregation, that I may consume them as in a moment; and Moses and Aaron fell upon their faces, still pleading for their people because they loved them so much.

**[[Numbers 16#^v46|Numbers 16:46-48]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v46]]
![[Numbers 16#^v47]]
![[Numbers 16#^v48]]

- Moses told Aaron to take a censer with fire from off the altar and incense, and go quickly unto the congregation and make an atonement for them, for wrath had gone out from the Lord and the plague was begun; the Most High was already putting them to death.
- Aaron ran into the midst of the congregation, put on incense, made an atonement for the people, and stood between the dead and the living; and the plague was stayed.

**[[Numbers 16#^v49|Numbers 16:49-50]]**

![[Numbers 16#^v49]]
![[Numbers 16#^v50]]

- They that died in the plague were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, beside them that died about the matter of Korah; so beside the two hundred and fifty three that died with Korah, fourteen thousand seven hundred more were put to death.

# Numbers 17: Aaron's Rod That Budded

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 17]]

**[[Numbers 17#^v1|Numbers 17:1-5]]**

![[Numbers 17#^v1]]
![[Numbers 17#^v2]]
![[Numbers 17#^v3]]
![[Numbers 17#^v4]]
![[Numbers 17#^v5]]

- Every tribe took a rod, a stick or staff, according to the house of their fathers, twelve rods, and the prince of every tribe wrote his name upon his rod; Aaron's name was written upon the rod of Levi.
- The rods were laid up in the tabernacle of the congregation before the testimony, the ark of the covenant, where the Lord met with them; the man's rod whom the Lord chose would blossom and grow.
- By this the Most High would make the murmurings of the children of Israel to cease from him, showing them that he was dealing with Moses and Aaron and them alone.

**[[Numbers 17#^v6|Numbers 17:6-9]]**

![[Numbers 17#^v6]]
![[Numbers 17#^v7]]
![[Numbers 17#^v8]]
![[Numbers 17#^v9]]

- Every prince gave Moses a rod, one for each according to their fathers' houses, twelve rods, and the rod of Aaron was among them, and Moses laid them up before the Lord in the tabernacle of witness.
- On the morrow, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi was budded, and brought forth buds, and bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds; and every man took back his own rod.

**[[Numbers 17#^v10|Numbers 17:10-11]]**

![[Numbers 17#^v10]]
![[Numbers 17#^v11]]

- The Lord commanded Aaron's rod to be brought again before the testimony, to be kept for a token against the rebels, to take away their murmurings from the Lord, that they die not; so he would not have to put them to death again for murmuring.
- And Moses did as the Lord commanded him.

**[[Numbers 17#^v12|Numbers 17:12-13]]**

![[Numbers 17#^v12]]
![[Numbers 17#^v13]]

- The children of Israel cried, behold, we die, we perish, we all perish: whosoever cometh any thing near unto the tabernacle of the Lord shall die.
- You had to be of Levi to go near the tabernacle; that had to be your office. The next chapter explains who was to minister there.

# Numbers 18: The Charge and Portion of the Priests and Levites

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 18]]

**[[Numbers 18#^v1|Numbers 18:1-7]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v1]]
![[Numbers 18#^v2]]
![[Numbers 18#^v3]]
![[Numbers 18#^v4]]
![[Numbers 18#^v5]]
![[Numbers 18#^v6]]
![[Numbers 18#^v7]]

- The Lord told Aaron that he and his sons and his father's house would bear the iniquity of the sanctuary and of their priesthood; that was their job. This explains who should minister in the tabernacle after Israel's cry in the last chapter.
- The tribe of Levi was joined unto Aaron to minister unto him and keep the charge of the tabernacle, their jobs; only they were not to come nigh the vessels of the sanctuary and the altar, the ark of the covenant and the different vessels, that neither they nor the priests die.
- The Levites were given to Aaron as a gift for the Lord, to do the service of the tabernacle; Aaron and his sons kept the priest's office for every thing of the altar and within the vail. The stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death: an Israelite not of Levi could not come near the tabernacle.

**[[Numbers 18#^v8|Numbers 18:8-11]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v8]]
![[Numbers 18#^v9]]
![[Numbers 18#^v10]]
![[Numbers 18#^v11]]

- The Lord gave Aaron the charge of the heave offerings of all the hallowed things of the children of Israel, by reason of the anointing, to him and his sons by an ordinance for ever.
- Of the most holy things reserved from the fire, every oblation, meat offering, sin offering, and trespass offering was most holy for Aaron and his sons; the priests kept a portion of it and ate it in the most holy place.
- The heave offering of their gift, with all the wave offerings of the children of Israel, was given to Aaron and his sons and his daughters by a statute for ever; every one that is clean in the house could eat of it. This is what the children of Israel gave for the Levites.

**[[Numbers 18#^v12|Numbers 18:12-14]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v12]]
![[Numbers 18#^v13]]
![[Numbers 18#^v14]]

- All the best of the oil, the best of the wine, and the wheat, the firstfruits which they offered unto the Lord, were given to the priests; whatsoever is first ripe in the land was theirs, and every one that was clean in the house could eat of it.
- Every thing devoted in Israel was theirs.

**[[Numbers 18#^v15|Numbers 18:15-17]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v15]]
![[Numbers 18#^v16]]
![[Numbers 18#^v17]]

- Every thing that openeth the matrix in all flesh, whether of men or beasts, was the Lord's; the firstborn was to be offered. Nevertheless the firstborn of man was surely redeemed, because you would not sacrifice the firstborn of our people, and the firstling of unclean beasts was redeemed also.
- Verse 16 explains how you redeem them: those to be redeemed from a month old were redeemed for the money of five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs; twenty gerahs equal one shekel.
- But the firstling of a cow, a sheep, or a goat was not redeemed, because those you could sacrifice: they are holy; their blood was sprinkled upon the altar and their fat burnt for an offering made by fire, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.

**[[Numbers 18#^v18|Numbers 18:18-19]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v18]]
![[Numbers 18#^v19]]

- The flesh of them was the priest's, as the wave breast and as the right shoulder; all the heave offerings of the holy things which the children of Israel offered unto the Lord were given to Aaron and his sons and his daughters by a statute for ever, to the lineage of the Levites.
- [[Exodus 29|Exodus 29]] shows how the wave offering and the heave offering were done. The wave offering was put in the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved, meaning shaken, before the Lord; then it was received of their hands and burnt upon the altar. Each had a different way it was waved or lifted up to the Most High before it was actually burnt and offered to the Lord, and the breast of the ram of consecration was waved and became the priest's part.
- It is a covenant of salt for ever before the Lord, unto Aaron and to his seed with him, the children that come after them.

**[[Numbers 18#^v20|Numbers 18:20-24]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v20]]
![[Numbers 18#^v21]]
![[Numbers 18#^v22]]
![[Numbers 18#^v23]]
![[Numbers 18#^v24]]

- The Lord told Aaron: thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither any part among them; I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel. The Levites had no inheritance of land to pass down; the Most High said, I will be taking care of you.
- The Lord gave the children of Levi all the tenth in Israel for an inheritance. This is the tithe: the tenth part was given to the Levites for their service which they served, the service of the tabernacle of the congregation, their sacrificing and teaching of the laws.
- That tenth went to the children of Levi, and it was done away with when Christ came and died and took that priesthood; we no longer give that tenth to Levi.
- The children of Israel were not to come nigh the tabernacle of the congregation, lest they bear sin and die; it was a sin for the other tribes of Israel to come near the tabernacle, on pain of death. All the work to be done in the tabernacle was committed unto the Levites, and they bore their iniquity, a statute for ever.

**[[Numbers 18#^v25|Numbers 18:25-32]]**

![[Numbers 18#^v25]]
![[Numbers 18#^v26]]
![[Numbers 18#^v27]]
![[Numbers 18#^v28]]
![[Numbers 18#^v29]]
![[Numbers 18#^v30]]
![[Numbers 18#^v31]]
![[Numbers 18#^v32]]

- When the Levites took of the children of Israel the tithes given them for their inheritance, they offered up of it an heave offering for the Lord, even a tenth part of the tithe: after the Levites got their tenth, the Most High wanted his tenth part of that, which they had to offer up.
- Their heave offering was reckoned unto them as though it were the corn of the threshingfloor and the fulness of the winepress, and the Lord's heave offering out of all their tithes was given to Aaron the priest; out of all their gifts they offered every heave offering of the Lord, of all the best thereof, even the hallowed part.
- When they had heaved the best thereof from it, they could eat it in every place, they and their households, for it was their reward for their service in the tabernacle of the congregation; and they bore no sin by reason of it when they heaved the best of it, neither polluted the holy things of the children of Israel, lest they die.
