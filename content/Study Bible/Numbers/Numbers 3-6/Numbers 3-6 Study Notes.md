# Numbers 3: The Levites Taken Instead of the Firstborn

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 3]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Numbers 3|Numbers 3]] through 6: the Levites taken for the Most High instead of all the firstborn of Israel, the service and burdens of the sons of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari, the law of putting the defiled out of the camp and the law of jealousies, and the law of the Nazarite with the blessing of the children of Israel. The session picks up at [[Numbers 3#^v11|Numbers 3:11]] after the reading of the earlier verses was interrupted; the recap of the stranger teaching from that portion is kept under [[Numbers 3#^v10|Numbers 3:10]]. The word to remember through the whole reading is order: everything in the camp of Israel was set in order, and the Most High required it on pain of death.

# Numbers 4: The Service of the Tabernacle Done in Order

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 4]]

![[Numbers 4#^v1]]
![[Numbers 4#^v2]]

- The sons of Kohath were numbered from thirty years old and upward even unto fifty years old, all that enter into the host to do the work in the tabernacle of the congregation. You took on that responsibility at thirty years old.
- Fifty was basically the age of retirement from the service; the law elsewhere shows the elder then sat down to be a judge amongst his brethren.

![[Numbers 4#^v4]]

- The design of the ark and the tabernacle, the pure gold dishes, spoons, and vessels, was laid out back in [[Exodus 35|Exodus 35]] and 36; review it there.

**[[Numbers 4#^v7|Numbers 4:7-14]]**

![[Numbers 4#^v7]]
![[Numbers 4#^v8]]

- The table of shewbread, the candlestick, the golden altar, and all the instruments of ministry were each wrapped in cloths of blue, scarlet, or purple and covered with badgers' skins before they were put on the staves and bars.
- Badger skin is something we would consider unclean today, yet the Lord had his holy things covered in it. Under the law of sacrifice, defilement came by touching unclean things to eat them; touching them to make something for the tabernacle still left you unclean until sundown, unable to sacrifice, but the skins themselves served the sanctuary.

**[[Numbers 4#^v15|Numbers 4:15]]**

![[Numbers 4#^v15]]

- After Aaron and his sons made an end of covering the sanctuary, the sons of Kohath came to bear it, but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die. The Most High is very particular about the most holy things.
- The Lord referred to all the tribes together as the camp. If you are not in a camp you are out of order; people avoid camps because a camp carries a certain level of order and subjection, and they want to do their own thing, which is to sit in the midst of sin.
- All twelve tribes are not gathered together right now, but we are still commanded to rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, and that takes gathering; the body must move in one unison, in one direction, as a camp.

# Numbers 5: Put Out of the Camp, and the Law of Jealousies

- Every leper, every one that hath an issue, and whosoever is defiled by the dead, male and female, was put out without the camp, that they defile not their camps in the midst whereof the Most High dwelt. And the children of Israel did so.
- Israel is referred to as a camp all through these chapters. The Lord is gathering his camp back together again to come and deliver us; if you are not in a camp, get in a camp.
- Inside the camp we consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works; the stronger in faith exhort the weaker to keep striving and endure to the end. If you are not gathered you cannot do that, and you do a disservice to the Lord and to your people.
- Separating yourself after receiving the knowledge of the truth is willful sin, and there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins, but a fearful looking for of judgment; that is doing despite unto the spirit of grace that woke us up in these last days.

- Christ is the advocate and the propitiation, the mercy, for our sins; there is no more Levite priest sacrifice, for he is the mediator between us and the Most High now. The whole world in that verse is the whole world of the nation of Israel.

# Numbers 6: The Law of the Nazarite and the Blessing of Israel

- When a man or woman vowed the vow of a Nazarite, to separate themselves unto the Lord, he separated himself from wine and strong drink, drank no vinegar of wine nor liquor of grapes, and ate nothing made of the vine tree, from the kernels even to the husk.
- John the Baptist fell under that Nazarite vow: the angel told Zacharias his son shall drink neither wine nor strong drink, and shall be filled with the Holy Ghost even from his mother's womb.
- John was filled with the Holy Spirit before he was born, and Elizabeth did not sleep with an angel; Zacharias was her husband. The same way Christ was filled with the Holy Spirit before birth; the immaculate conception is a Catholic doctrine, not the scriptures.

- Paul partook in that Nazarite purification. Unto the Jews he became a Jew; he brought himself down to their level of understanding to win them over, not by breaking the law but by walking orderly and keeping it.
- The elders at Jerusalem told Paul to purify himself with four men which had a vow on them and be at charges with them, that all may know thou thyself walkest orderly and keepest the law. And note it well: the thousands there that believed were Jews, all zealous of the law, and the Gentiles Paul taught among were Jews scattered among the Gentiles.

In our understanding today you cannot keep the vow of a Nazarite under Christ: you can let your hair grow as long as you want, but when we break bread and take the cup in remembrance of Christ, renewing that covenant, you have to drink that wine to be in covenant with him.

- On this wise Aaron and his sons blessed the children of Israel: the Lord bless thee and keep thee, the Lord make his face shine upon thee and be gracious unto thee, the Lord lift up his countenance upon thee and give thee peace; and they shall put my name upon the children of Israel, and I will bless them.
- All of that is a stipulation, a contract; it is not unconditional love with the Most High. Keep my commandments and live.
- When the southern kingdom asked wherefore the Lord pronounced evil against them, the answer was because your fathers forsook me and walked after other gods, and ye have done worse than your fathers. Bring it up to today: Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, every denomination walking after the imagination of its own evil heart, cast out into a land we knew not, serving gods where the Lord shows no favor. That is why our people have no peace and no blessing.
- The Most High has taken that blessing from us, and we have to work to get it back by repentance and enduring to the end. But it will happen again; the Lord is pulling his spirit back out on us, and the remnant is waking up.
