# Genesis 24: A Wife From the Kindred

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 24]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Genesis 24|Genesis 24]] through 27: the marriage of Isaac, the two nations in Rebekah's womb, the wells of Isaac, and the blessing that passed to Jacob.

**[[Genesis 24#^v3|Genesis 24:3-4]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v3]]
![[Genesis 24#^v4]]

- The Most High does not deal with marriage into the other nations among His chosen. Abraham made his servant swear not to take a wife for Isaac of the daughters of the Canaanites; these laws were in place long before Moses reiterated them.
- The reason is given in the law itself: they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve other gods. Solomon is the prime example; he loved women of the other nations, went into their gods, and had to repent and bring it back.
- The forefathers arranged marriages. The building block of a strong nation is a strong family, and a strong family comes from marriage, not from young men and women left to their own accord.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 7#^v3|Deuteronomy 7:3-4]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 7#^v3]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 7#^v4]]
    Make no marriages with the nations, for they will turn away thy son from following the Most High to serve other gods.

- Abraham took Keturah and had more sons, but Abraham gave all that he had unto Isaac; to the sons of the concubines he gave gifts and sent them away eastward from Isaac. The inheritance followed the child of the promise.

**[[Genesis 25#^v12|Genesis 25:12-18]]**

![[Genesis 25#^v12]]

![[Genesis 25#^v15]]

- The generations of Ishmael list twelve princes according to their nations, exactly as the Most High promised in [[Genesis 17#^v20|Genesis 17:20]]. Ishmael lived an hundred and thirty and seven years and died in the presence of all his brethren.

**[[Genesis 25#^v20|Genesis 25:20-21]]**

![[Genesis 25#^v20]]
![[Genesis 25#^v21]]

- Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah to wife. Brothers waiting on a wife, be patient and stay virtuous; Isaac waited forty years, and the Most High had the right sister appointed.
- Isaac intreated the Lord for his wife because she was barren, and the Lord was intreated of him; Rebekah conceived.

**[[Genesis 25#^v22|Genesis 25:22-23]]**

![[Genesis 25#^v22]]
![[Genesis 25#^v23]]

- The children struggled together within her, and the Lord gave Rebekah the prophecy: two nations are in thy womb, one people stronger than the other, and the elder shall serve the younger.
- The whole scripture is good set against evil: the godly against the sinner, Jacob against Esau, righteousness against sin. Two powerful forces were being born into the earth, and the struggle began in the womb.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v14|Ecclesiasticus 33:14-15]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v14]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v15]]
    Good is set against evil and the godly against the sinner; all the works of the Most High are two and two, one against another.

**[[Genesis 25#^v24|Genesis 25:24-26]]**

![[Genesis 25#^v24]]
![[Genesis 25#^v25]]
![[Genesis 25#^v26]]

- The first came out red, all over like an hairy garment, and they called his name Esau. The scriptures describe the man because a nation comes from him; this teaching identifies Esau's seed with the red, hairy, melanin-lacking people of the world today.
- Jacob's hand took hold on Esau's heel, and that is prophecy: Esau is the end of the world, and Jacob is the beginning of it that followeth. When Esau's rulership is done, Jacob comes into power; that is how we know the times.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Esdras 6#^v7|2 Esdras 6:7-9]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 6#^v7]]
    ![[2 Esdras 6#^v8]]
    ![[2 Esdras 6#^v9]]
    Jacob's hand held first the heel of Esau; Esau is the end of the world and Jacob the beginning of it that followeth.

**[[Genesis 25#^v27|Genesis 25:27-28]]**

![[Genesis 25#^v27]]
![[Genesis 25#^v28]]

- Esau was a cunning hunter, a man of the field; Jacob was a plain man, dwelling in tents. The Most High gives you the characteristics of the man so the nation can be known.
- Isaac loved Esau because he did eat of his venison, but Rebekah loved Jacob, because the Lord had told her the prophecy that the younger was the chosen.

**[[Genesis 25#^v29|Genesis 25:29-34]]**

![[Genesis 25#^v29]]
![[Genesis 25#^v30]]
![[Genesis 25#^v31]]
![[Genesis 25#^v32]]
![[Genesis 25#^v33]]
![[Genesis 25#^v34]]

- Esau sold his birthright for red pottage; therefore was his name called Edom, red. He said, What profit shall this birthright do to me, and sware it away for a meal.
- Thus Esau despised his birthright. Jacob is called a supplanter, but the supplanting was of the Holy Ghost; Esau gave the birthright away himself.

# Genesis 26: The Wells and the Envy of the Philistines

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 26]]

**[[Genesis 26#^v1|Genesis 26:1-5]]**

![[Genesis 26#^v1]]
![[Genesis 26#^v2]]
![[Genesis 26#^v3]]
![[Genesis 26#^v4]]
![[Genesis 26#^v5]]

- In the famine Isaac was told, Go not down into Egypt; the oath sworn to Abraham was performed unto Isaac: seed as the stars, all these countries, and in thy seed shall all nations of the earth be blessed.
- In thy seed shall all nations be blessed does not put everybody in the covenant. The seed is Christ, and the children of the prophets and of the covenant are Israel, scattered into every nation; through Christ scattered Israel in all kindreds of the earth receives repentance and comes back to the Father.
- The reason the oath stood: because Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Galatians 3#^v16|Galatians 3:16]]**
    ![[Galatians 3#^v16]]
    The promise was to Abraham and his seed, which is Christ.
  - **[[Acts 3#^v25|Acts 3:25-26]]**
    ![[Acts 3#^v25]]
    ![[Acts 3#^v26]]
    Ye are the children of the prophets and of the covenant; unto you first God sent His Son to bless you in turning every one from his iniquities.
  - **[[Amos 2#^v11|Amos 2:11]]**
    ![[Amos 2#^v11]]
    The Most High raised up prophets of the sons of Israel; the children of the prophets are Israel.

- Take the lesson from the sporting: love your wife, play with your wife, keep the flirting and the freshness in the marriage. Give her love, attention, and affection all the days.

- The nations envy the prosperity of Israel. It is not for anything done to them; the blessing itself is what is hated.

- Isaac was not simple; he separated from the people that hated him rather than assimilating among them, and he dealt with their covenant with open eyes. The words can be like butter while war is in the heart.

![[Genesis 26#^v35]]

- Esau at forty took wives of the Hittites, and they were a grief of mind unto Isaac and to Rebekah. He went straight against the fathers' law of marrying within the kindred; that is the nature of the vessel.

# Genesis 27: The Blessing of Jacob

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 27]]

**[[Genesis 27#^v1|Genesis 27:1-4]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v1]]
![[Genesis 27#^v2]]
![[Genesis 27#^v3]]
![[Genesis 27#^v4]]

- Isaac's eyes were dim so that he could not see, and the Lord allowed that blindness for a purpose: so that the blessing could pass to Jacob. What looks like evil can be for good, as Joseph's slavery in Egypt was for good.

**[[Genesis 27#^v5|Genesis 27:5-13]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v5]]
![[Genesis 27#^v6]]
![[Genesis 27#^v7]]
![[Genesis 27#^v8]]
![[Genesis 27#^v9]]
![[Genesis 27#^v10]]
![[Genesis 27#^v11]]
![[Genesis 27#^v12]]
![[Genesis 27#^v13]]

- Rebekah heard and moved for Jacob because she carried the word from the Lord: the elder shall serve the younger. She knew the blessing belonged to Jacob by prophecy, and she took the curse upon herself: Upon me be thy curse, my son; only obey my voice.

**[[Genesis 27#^v14|Genesis 27:14]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v14]]

- His mother made savoury meat, such as his father loved. Rebekah knew exactly how her husband loved his food and made it to that standard; sisters, learn to prepare it such as he loveth.

**[[Genesis 27#^v15|Genesis 27:15-23]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v15]]
![[Genesis 27#^v16]]
![[Genesis 27#^v17]]
![[Genesis 27#^v18]]
![[Genesis 27#^v19]]
![[Genesis 27#^v20]]
![[Genesis 27#^v21]]

![[Genesis 27#^v23]]

- Rebekah put Esau's raiment on Jacob and the skins of the kids of the goats upon his hands and neck, and Isaac discerned him not: the voice is Jacob's voice, but the hands are the hands of Esau.

**[[Genesis 27#^v28|Genesis 27:28-29]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v28]]
![[Genesis 27#^v29]]

- The blessing is rulership of the earth: the dew of heaven, the fatness of the earth, plenty of corn and wine; let people serve thee, and nations bow down to thee; be lord over thy brethren; cursed be every one that curseth thee, and blessed be he that blesseth thee.

**[[Genesis 27#^v34|Genesis 27:34-38]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v34]]
![[Genesis 27#^v35]]
![[Genesis 27#^v36]]
![[Genesis 27#^v37]]
![[Genesis 27#^v38]]

- Esau cried with a great and exceeding bitter cry and begged, Bless me, even me also, O my father. But he had already sold the birthright and never dealt rightly with Jacob about it; he wanted the blessing anyway.
- Esau found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears. He that despised the birthright for one morsel of meat was rejected when he would have inherited the blessing.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Hebrews 12#^v16|Hebrews 12:16-17]]**
    ![[Hebrews 12#^v16]]
    ![[Hebrews 12#^v17]]
    Esau, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright, was rejected and found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears.

**[[Genesis 27#^v39|Genesis 27:39-40]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v39]]
![[Genesis 27#^v40]]

- Esau's portion: the fatness of the earth and the dew of heaven from above, and by thy sword shalt thou live. Esau's kingdoms have gotten what they have through war; his living is by the sword, by ever more advanced weaponry, and the nations are overcome and awed by it.
- And it shall come to pass when thou shalt have the dominion, that thou shalt break his yoke from off thy neck: this is the time period now, where Esau has the dominion and rules first, and when his rule is done, Jacob comes into power.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Revelation 13#^v13|Revelation 13:13-14]]**
    ![[Revelation 13#^v13]]
    ![[Revelation 13#^v14]]
    He maketh fire come down from heaven in the sight of men and deceiveth them that dwell on the earth by those wonders; the sword Esau lives by.

**[[Genesis 27#^v41|Genesis 27:41-46]]**

![[Genesis 27#^v41]]
![[Genesis 27#^v42]]
![[Genesis 27#^v43]]
![[Genesis 27#^v44]]
![[Genesis 27#^v45]]
![[Genesis 27#^v46]]

- Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and purposed in his heart to slay him, the same hatred Cain had for Abel. Good against evil runs from the beginning of the book to the end.
- Rebekah sent Jacob to Laban her brother until Esau's fury turned away, and said she was weary of her life because of the daughters of Heth: if Jacob take a wife of the daughters of the land, what good shall my life do me? Marrying the sons and daughters within the kindred is a weighty matter to the mothers of Israel.
