# Joshua 21: The Cities of the Levites

Read the chapter → [[Joshua 21]]

## Introduction

This reading closes out the book of Joshua, covering chapters 21 through 24: the cities and suburbs given to the Levites out of every tribe, the altar of witness built by the two and a half tribes on the other side Jordan, Joshua's farewell charge to cleave unto the Most High, and the covenant made at Shechem where all Israel chose to serve the Lord. The book of Joshua, like the books before it, is the history of the tribes; it goes further into how Israel got the land, and precept upon precept some things are more detailed in Numbers while Joshua carries the same history further. The Q&A after the reading is folded into the verses it belongs to.

- Their reasoning was national: if ye rebel today against the Lord, tomorrow he will be wroth with the whole congregation of Israel. Judgment does not fall on pieces of the nation; [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]] happened to all of us, and they understood that, so they showed up quick, fast, and in a hurry rather than sit around and let it happen.

- They cited the proof from their own history: did not Achan the son of Zerah commit a trespass in the accursed thing, and wrath fell on all the congregation of Israel, and that man perished not alone in his iniquity. One man trespassed and the whole nation felt it.

- They feared that generations later the children on one side of Jordan would tell the children on the other side that they were not the children of Israel, shutting them out from fearing the Lord, from the temple, the feast days, and the sacrifices.

- If anyone ever said to their generations, Ye have no part in the Lord, the answer would stand built in stone: behold the pattern of the altar of the LORD, which our fathers made, not for burnt offerings nor for sacrifices, but it is a witness between us and you.
- The same thing is happening today with the northern kingdom. Our people left their own witnesses over here: the writings on the Los Lunas stone identify with the northern kingdom, and the traditions, artifacts, practices, laws, and books that are being brought out in the classes all prove our people were here long ago and that they are the children of Israel. Yet with all that proof out there, people still deny it, the very thing the two and a half tribes feared.
- Nobody else could have built that altar to the pattern of the altar of the Lord, because no other nation was keeping the laws; only Israel keeps the precepts, not the heathen. That is why the memorial could stand as identification.

- This is the same thing that happened at Baalpeor, when Israel went and mingled with the women of Moab and then worshipped their gods. The warning here is do not do that thing.

Many come out of Christianity being told God is good all the time, yet the scripture says plainly that the Most High will destroy you when you go off, because that also is what he promised.
- When ye have transgressed the covenant and served other gods, ye shall perish quickly from off the good land. That is exactly why Israel is not at home right now; we did exactly that. And that is what the waking up is for today: the classes going out all day, the teaching in the streets, trying to bring our people back to the laws of God.

![[Joshua 24#^v15]]

- Now therefore fear the LORD, and serve him in sincerity and in truth, and put away the gods which your fathers served on the other side of the flood, and in Egypt.
- Choose you this day whom ye will serve; but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD. A lot of people hang that quote up in the house and do not keep one law; everybody reads this verse and reads nothing before it about being put to death for going off. Lord willing they have space and time to repent.
- Moses said the same thing: he set life and death, blessing and cursing before Israel and said choose life. [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]] laid it out, blessings in verses 1 through 14 if Israel obeyed, to be above all people and rule; curses from 15 down to 68 if not. Israel chose the cursing and the death, and that is what we are in now, trying to come back and get out of it.

**[[Joshua 24#^v19|Joshua 24:19-20]]**

![[Joshua 24#^v19]]
![[Joshua 24#^v20]]

- Joshua said unto the people, Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God; he is a jealous God; he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins.
- If ye forsake the LORD, and serve strange gods, then he will turn and do you hurt, and consume you, after that he hath done you good. Everything he promised for good had come to pass and Israel was on top in the land; all they had to do was keep the commandments. Going off meant being taken out of that land into captivity, and because that is what Israel decided to do, that is the predicament we are in today.
