# Numbers 31: Vengeance on the Midianites

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 31]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Numbers 31|Numbers 31]] through 34: the war of vengeance against Midian and the dividing of the prey, Reuben and Gad receiving their inheritance on this side Jordan, the journeys of Israel from Egypt to the plains of Moab, and the borders and dividers of the land of Canaan. This session is mostly reading with brief exposition; the counsel through the chapters of journeys and borders is to follow along with the maps in the back of the Bible, so the lands and cities being read take on life instead of putting the reader to sleep.

**[[Numbers 31#^v1|Numbers 31:1-2]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v1]]
![[Numbers 31#^v2]]

- The Most High told Israel to avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites. The reason for this vengeance is the history of [[Numbers 25|Numbers 25]]; go back and read that chapter to see what Midian did.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Numbers 25#^v1|Numbers 25:1-3]]**
    ![[Numbers 25#^v1]]
    ![[Numbers 25#^v2]]
    ![[Numbers 25#^v3]]
    The people began whoredom with the daughters of Moab and were joined unto Baal-peor; that history is the reason the Lord commanded the vengeance.

**[[Numbers 31#^v3|Numbers 31:3-6]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v3]]
![[Numbers 31#^v4]]
![[Numbers 31#^v5]]
![[Numbers 31#^v6]]

- A thousand of every tribe, twelve thousand armed for war, were sent with Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, with the holy instruments and the trumpets to blow.
- Phinehas is the one who killed the man of Israel that was fornicating with the woman of the other nation, in the history of [[Numbers 25|Numbers 25]].

  Precepts:
  - **[[Numbers 25#^v7|Numbers 25:7-8]]**
    ![[Numbers 25#^v7]]
    ![[Numbers 25#^v8]]
    Phinehas took a javelin and thrust the man of Israel and the Midianitish woman through, and the plague was stayed.

**[[Numbers 31#^v7|Numbers 31:7-8]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v7]]
![[Numbers 31#^v8]]

- They warred against the Midianites as the Lord commanded Moses, slew all the males, and slew the five kings of Midian: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba.
- Balaam also the son of Beor they slew with the sword. Balaam was a soothsayer, a diviner; he was a witch, so to speak.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Joshua 13#^v22|Joshua 13:22]]**
    ![[Joshua 13#^v22]]
    Balaam the son of Beor, the soothsayer, did the children of Israel slay with the sword among them that were slain by them.

**[[Numbers 31#^v9|Numbers 31:9-12]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v9]]
![[Numbers 31#^v10]]
![[Numbers 31#^v11]]
![[Numbers 31#^v12]]

- Israel took the women of Midian captives with their little ones, took the spoil of all their cattle, flocks, and goods, and burnt all their cities and all their goodly castles with fire.
- Midian comes from the lineage of Ishmael, the people known today as the Arab man. This shows Esau was not the one who first came up with castles; the castles seen in the movies were conquered from other nations.
- They took all the spoil and all the prey, both of men and of beasts: the Midianites had captives of their own, and Israel took over both the men and the beasts when they overcame them.

**[[Numbers 31#^v13|Numbers 31:13-18]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v13]]
![[Numbers 31#^v14]]
![[Numbers 31#^v15]]
![[Numbers 31#^v16]]
![[Numbers 31#^v17]]
![[Numbers 31#^v18]]

- Moses was wroth with the officers of the host, the leaders of the army of Israel, because they saved all the women alive.
- These women caused the children of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to commit trespass against the Lord in the matter of Peor. Balaam took it upon himself to send the women of the other nations to Israel, and it caused the people to go into fornication, and the Lord began putting them to death, over twenty and four thousand, in the history of [[Numbers 25|Numbers 25]].
- Therefore every male among the little ones was to be killed, and every woman that had known man by lying with him; but the women children that had not known a man they kept alive for themselves.
- Moses allowed the men to keep those women so they would not commit sin and go raping the women, being out at war. That does not mean it is done today; it was a time that the Most High allowed it so the men would not be in the midst of sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Numbers 25#^v9|Numbers 25:9]]**
    ![[Numbers 25#^v9]]
    Those that died in the plague were twenty and four thousand; that plague in the matter of Peor is why the women who had known man could not be kept.

**[[Numbers 31#^v19|Numbers 31:19-24]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v19]]
![[Numbers 31#^v20]]
![[Numbers 31#^v21]]
![[Numbers 31#^v22]]
![[Numbers 31#^v23]]
![[Numbers 31#^v24]]

- Whoever killed any person or touched any slain had to abide without the camp seven days and purify himself and his captives on the third day and on the seventh day. That is the law going back to [[Numbers 5#^v2|Numbers 5:2]], putting the defiled outside the camp.
- Purifying the raiment, all that is made of skins, all work of goats' hair, and all things made of wood goes back to the law of purification in [[Numbers 19|Numbers 19]].
- Eleazar the priest gave the ordinance: gold, silver, brass, iron, tin, and lead, everything that may abide the fire, goes through the fire and is purified with the water of separation; whatever abides not the fire goes through the water; and on the seventh day they wash their clothes, are clean, and come into the camp.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Numbers 5#^v2|Numbers 5:2]]**
    ![[Numbers 5#^v2]]
    Put out of the camp every one defiled by the dead; the seven days without the camp keep that law.
  - **[[Numbers 19#^v11|Numbers 19:11-12]]**
    ![[Numbers 19#^v11]]
    ![[Numbers 19#^v12]]
    He that touches the dead purifies himself on the third day and on the seventh day; the men of war purified themselves by that law.

**[[Numbers 31#^v25|Numbers 31:25-30]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v25]]
![[Numbers 31#^v26]]
![[Numbers 31#^v27]]
![[Numbers 31#^v28]]
![[Numbers 31#^v29]]
![[Numbers 31#^v30]]

- Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the chief fathers of the congregation took the sum of the prey, both of man and of beast, adding up all that was taken from the Midianites.
- The prey was divided into two parts: between the army who went out to battle and took the prey, and all the congregation that did not go to war. The nation looked out for each other.
- A tribute was levied unto the Lord: of the men of war, one soul of five hundred, of persons, beeves, asses, and sheep, given to Eleazar the priest for a heave offering, because he was the priest; and of the children of Israel's half, one portion of fifty, given to the Levites which kept the charge of the tabernacle.

**[[Numbers 31#^v41|Numbers 31:41]]**

![[Numbers 31#^v41]]

- Moses gave the tribute, the Lord's heave offering, unto Eleazar the priest, as the Lord commanded Moses. The heave offerings belong to the priesthood by statute forever, a covenant of salt, because Aaron and Levi have no inheritance in the land; the Most High is their part and their inheritance.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Numbers 18#^v19|Numbers 18:19]]**
    ![[Numbers 18#^v19]]
    All the heave offerings of the holy things are given to the priest and his sons by a statute for ever; it is a covenant of salt before the Lord.
  - **[[Numbers 18#^v20|Numbers 18:20]]**
    ![[Numbers 18#^v20]]
    Aaron has no inheritance in their land; the Lord is his part and his inheritance among the children of Israel.
  - **[[Numbers 18#^v21|Numbers 18:21]]**
    ![[Numbers 18#^v21]]
    The children of Levi are given all the tenth in Israel for an inheritance, for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.

# Numbers 32: The Inheritance of Reuben and Gad on This Side Jordan

![[Numbers 32#^v1]]
![[Numbers 32#^v2]]

- The children of Reuben and Gad had a very great multitude of cattle, and when they saw that Jazer and Gilead was a place for cattle, they asked Moses for that land and said, bring us not over Jordan.
- They did not want to go forward any more; they were ready to set up shop before they crossed over Jordan, and they had not even finished the mission.

**[[Numbers 32#^v6|Numbers 32:6-9]]**

![[Numbers 32#^v6]]
![[Numbers 32#^v7]]

- Moses said unto them, shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here and do nothing?
- Wherefore discourage ye the heart of the children of Israel from going over into the land which the Lord hath given them: thus did your fathers when they were sent from Kadesh-barnea to see the land. That goes back into the history of the spies in [[Numbers 13|Numbers 13]]; the fathers were not even in the land yet and were already getting comfortable.

  - **[[Numbers 13#^v31|Numbers 13:31-33]]**
    ![[Numbers 13#^v31]]
    ![[Numbers 13#^v32]]
    ![[Numbers 13#^v33]]

- He made Israel wander in the wilderness forty years, until all the generation that had done evil in the sight of the Lord was consumed. Moses is giving Reuben and Gad the history of everything so they see what their request repeats.

- They vowed not to return to their houses until the children of Israel had inherited every man his inheritance, since their own inheritance had fallen to them on this side Jordan eastward.

- But if ye will not do so, behold, ye have sinned against the Lord: and be sure your sin will find you out.
- Build you cities for your little ones and folds for your sheep, and do that which hath proceeded out of your mouth. Do not think only about yourself; look out for your children, the nation coming after you.

# Numbers 33: The Journeys of Israel from Egypt to the Plains of Moab

Read the chapter → [[Numbers 33]]

**[[Numbers 33#^v1|Numbers 33:1-4]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v1]]
![[Numbers 33#^v2]]
![[Numbers 33#^v3]]
![[Numbers 33#^v4]]

- These are the journeys of the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt, and Moses wrote their goings out by the commandment of the Lord.
- They departed from Rameses on the fifteenth day of the first month, on the morrow after the passover, with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians, while the Egyptians buried all their firstborn, which the Lord had smitten among them: upon their gods also the Lord executed judgments; he put them to death.

**[[Numbers 33#^v8|Numbers 33:8-11]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v8]]
![[Numbers 33#^v9]]
![[Numbers 33#^v10]]
![[Numbers 33#^v11]]

- They passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness, pitched in Marah, came to Elim where there were twelve fountains of water and threescore and ten palm trees, encamped by the Red sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.
- Follow these journeys with the maps in the back of the Bible; most Bibles have them. Seeing the wilderness of Sin, the Red sea, and the route they were taking gives a better understanding of the lands and cities being read.

**[[Numbers 33#^v16|Numbers 33:16-17]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v16]]
![[Numbers 33#^v17]]

- They would walk a little and then set up camp wherever the Lord wanted them to stop. They were led by the cloud by day and the light by night; the light gave them sight at night, and when they saw it stop, they knew to pitch camp right there.

**[[Numbers 33#^v37|Numbers 33:37-39]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v37]]
![[Numbers 33#^v38]]
![[Numbers 33#^v39]]

- They pitched in mount Hor, in the edge of the land of Edom; the map shows the land of Edom there.
- Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of the Lord and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the first day of the fifth month; and Aaron was an hundred and twenty and three years old when he died. The number is the fortieth year; read it carefully.

**[[Numbers 33#^v44|Numbers 33:44-49]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v44]]
![[Numbers 33#^v45]]
![[Numbers 33#^v46]]
![[Numbers 33#^v47]]
![[Numbers 33#^v48]]
![[Numbers 33#^v49]]

- They pitched in Ije-abarim, in the border of Moab; on the map Moab sits right above the land of Edom.
- They departed from the mountains of Abarim and pitched in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho; so they ended in the midst of the land of Moab, where the camp now sits.

**[[Numbers 33#^v50|Numbers 33:50-53]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v50]]
![[Numbers 33#^v51]]
![[Numbers 33#^v52]]
![[Numbers 33#^v53]]

- When ye are passed over Jordan into the land of Canaan, ye shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, destroy all their pictures and all their molten images, and quite pluck down all their high places: all the idols go.
- Dispossess the inhabitants of the land and dwell therein, for the Most High has given the land to possess it.

**[[Numbers 33#^v54|Numbers 33:54]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v54]]

- The land was to be divided by lot for an inheritance among the families, to the more the more inheritance and to the fewer the less, every man's inheritance in the place where his lot falleth, according to the tribes of the fathers. The map of the tribal lands, Simeon, Judah, Benjamin, Reuben and the rest with their cities, is the product of the tribes following what the Most High said to do.

**[[Numbers 33#^v55|Numbers 33:55-56]]**

![[Numbers 33#^v55]]
![[Numbers 33#^v56]]

- But if ye will not drive out the inhabitants of the land, those which ye let remain shall be pricks in your eyes and thorns in your sides, and shall vex you in the land wherein ye dwell.
- They are going to be problems, because they are not following the Most High as Israel follows; if you allow them to stay in your land they will always cause problems.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Joshua 23#^v13|Joshua 23:13]]**
    ![[Joshua 23#^v13]]
    The nations left remaining become snares and traps, scourges in your sides and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off the good land.

# Numbers 34: The Borders of the Land of Canaan

**[[Numbers 34#^v1|Numbers 34:1-5]]**

![[Numbers 34#^v1]]
![[Numbers 34#^v2]]
![[Numbers 34#^v3]]

- This is the land of Canaan that falls to Israel for inheritance, with the coasts thereof; the south quarter runs from the wilderness of Zin along by the coast of Edom, the south border from the outmost coast of the salt sea eastward, fetching a compass unto the river of Egypt and out at the sea.
- This is going into how far the land stretched. When reading these borders, look at the map and visualize how the land is stretched, border by border.

- Levi did not get an inheritance of land, because Levi was scattered throughout all the other tribes' lands to do the service of the Lord.
