# Exodus 26: The Pattern Shown in the Mount

Read the chapter → [[Exodus 26]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Exodus 26|Exodus 26]] through 29: the curtains, boards, and veil of the tabernacle; the brasen altar, the court, and the oil for the lamp; the holy garments of Aaron the high priest; and the consecration of Aaron and his sons in the priest's office. This is a reading session; the chapters are read straight through with teaching at the key points, and the first two chapters move quickly since the tabernacle furniture was covered in the previous session on chapter 25. The recording begins in the middle of [[Exodus 26|Exodus 26]], at the comment on the cubit in verse 2, so the opening of the session is not on the tape.

- Eleven curtains of goats' hair cover the tabernacle, with a covering of rams' skins dyed red above, and a covering above that of badgers' skins.
- The badgers' skins settle a doctrine question. [[Leviticus 11|Leviticus 11]] says not to touch the carcase of the unclean beast, and the badger goes on paws, yet the tabernacle had to be built with badger skins by hand. The law goes into sacrificing the unclean thing and eating it, touching it with your mouth; it is not saying a dead animal in your path cannot be moved out of the way with your hands.

- The Most High commanded holy garments to be made for Aaron for glory and for beauty. Garments were ordained for the men of Israel; when the world hears [[Deuteronomy 22#^v5|Deuteronomy 22:5]] and asks why the men wear garments, the answer is here: the most holy and regarded men of the nation, the priests, were arrayed in long garments down to the foot.

The hundred forty and four thousand of [[Revelation 7|Revelation 7]] that must be sealed before destruction comes are read of again in 2 Esdras: they departed from the shadow of the world, leaving spiritual Egypt and the ways Babylon taught behind, and have received glorious garments of the Lord. In the kingdom of heaven the Most High will clothe His men in glorious garments as in ancient times, on another level than anything worn today.

- Urim and Thummim means lights and perfections: objects not specifically described in the scriptures, perhaps stones since they went in the breastplate, by which the high priest ascertained the will of the Most High in important matters affecting the nation. To ascertain is to find out for certain, a divine discerning beyond what a man can determine by his own thinking.

The better theory: they served as a symbol of the high priest's authority to seek counsel of the Most High, His will being revealed to him through inner illumination.

- Joshua likewise was to stand before Eleazar the priest, who shall ask counsel for him after the judgment of Urim before the Lord; the Most High gave revelation through it.

- And shalt wash them with water: cleanliness and spotlessness were very important to the Most High in everything dealing with the temple, and He is real specific and detailed about how clean and sanctified a man must be before he enters the holy place. This thread runs all through the first five books, Leviticus especially.

The priest's office shall be theirs for a perpetual statute: thou shalt consecrate Aaron and his sons.
- Ecclesiasticus records the same consecration: Moses anointed him with holy oil, an everlasting covenant to Aaron and to his seed so long as the heavens should remain, to minister unto Him, execute the office of the priesthood, offer the sacrifices, and teach Jacob the testimonies and inform Israel in His laws. The priest's job was the offerings and teaching the nation the law, statutes, and commandments.
- What happened to that covenant is answered in Malachi, where the commandment is spoken to the priests: the covenant with Levi was of life and peace, the priest's lips should keep knowledge and they should seek the law at his mouth, for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts; but ye are departed out of the way, ye have caused many to stumble at the law, ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi. The priests were finessing the offerings, keeping the best animals for themselves and sacrificing the blind and the broken, and they perverted the teachings of the law for their own benefit.
- That is why leadership today is not walking around requiring Levite lineage of Aaron. Christ is the high priest now, and the nation is a whole nation of kings and priests; Malachi and Hebrews are two good books to study on the changing of the priesthood, against the doctrine that the Aaronic covenant could never change.

- The second ram is the ram of consecration: its blood is put upon the tip of the right ear of Aaron and of his sons, upon the thumb of their right hand and the great toe of their right foot, and sprinkled upon the altar round about; the blood and the anointing oil are sprinkled upon Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments, and he shall be hallowed.
- The bread and the parts of the ram are put in the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved for a wave offering before the Lord, then burnt upon the altar; and the breast of the wave offering and the shoulder of the heave offering are sanctified for Aaron and his sons by a statute for ever. There go the wave offering and the heave offering, which you will read about throughout the first five books, Leviticus predominantly.
- These ordinances belonged to the Levitical priesthood, and the doctrines that would put men back under pieces of that office today do not even keep what they claim; they sound right because they use scriptures strongly and confidently, but they are in error. Be not tossed to and fro with every wind of doctrine, nor carried about with strange doctrines; do not ever fall away from the Messiah.

- The tabernacle shall be sanctified by His glory, and Aaron and his sons sanctified to minister in the priest's office; for the Most High to enter and deal with Israel, everything had to be sanctified. That was serious.
- I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God. He was in no other nation's tabernacle, temple, or synagogue, and though there is no tabernacle today it is still the same case: He is in the midst of Israel and none else, which excludes everybody else from the benefits of having the Most High as their God. There is nothing to be ashamed of in the fact that He chose Israel only, and nothing changed across testaments: blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for he hath visited and redeemed his people, and that is the purpose of Christ, to redeem His people.
- The other nations knew it. When the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin heard the temple was being rebuilt they came saying, Let us build with you, for we seek your God; and Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the chief of the fathers answered, Ye have nothing to do with us to build an house unto our God, but we ourselves together will build unto the Lord God of Israel. That is the mindset for these last days: Israel does not need the other nations' assistance, but rebuilds one another spiritually as a nation while awaiting Christ's return to restore the kingdom.
- He brought them forth out of the land of Egypt that He may dwell among them: I am the Lord their God. Israel should walk confident, special, and chosen above all nations of people, for the Most High is faithful and belongs to no one else; repent from spiritual fornication and return to the Most High.
