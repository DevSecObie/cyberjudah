# Idolatry

The golden calf, the groves, Baalim, and every warning against serving other gods.

- Precept **[[Deuteronomy 7#^v3|Deuteronomy 7:3-4]]**: Make no marriages with the nations, for they will turn away thy son from following the Most High to serve other gods.

**[[Genesis 31#^v30|Genesis 31:30-32]]**  taught in [[Genesis 28-31 Study Notes|Genesis 28-31]]

That is why the Most High told Abram to get out from his country and from his kindred: he separated Abram because of what he had set up for Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and for us, that we not be idolaters.
- Precept **[[Joshua 24#^v2|Joshua 24:2]]**: Your fathers dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time, even Terah the father of Abraham and the father of Nachor, and they served other gods; Laban came of that idolatrous house.

- Precept **[[Genesis 31#^v19|Genesis 31:19]]**: Rachel had stolen the images that were her father's; that is where the strange gods in the household came from.

## Exodus

**[[Exodus 3#^v19|Exodus 3:19-20]]**  taught in [[Exodus 2-5 Study Notes|Exodus 2-5]]

- I am sure that the king of Egypt will not let you go, no, not by a mighty hand; and I will stretch out my hand, and smite Egypt with all my wonders. The Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart so that the deliverance would come with wonders, destroying the idols of Egypt in the sight of Israel and of Egypt; the Most High is not one to be played with.

**[[Exodus 4#^v24|Exodus 4:24-26]]**  taught in [[Exodus 2-5 Study Notes|Exodus 2-5]]

- By the way in the inn, the LORD met him, and sought to kill him, because Moses had not circumcised his son. Zipporah took a sharp stone, cut off the foreskin of her son, and said, Surely a bloody husband art thou to me: bloody because of the circumcision she had to perform herself.
- This shows the laws did not begin at Sinai. Circumcision was the covenant given to Abraham for every man child in his generations, on the eighth day, and the uncircumcised man child whose flesh is not circumcised, that soul shall be cut off from his people: he hath broken my covenant. That is why Moses was worthy of death.
- The covenant still stands. A son of Israel must be circumcised; and a man that comes to the truth grown must still be circumcised, even at forty, fifty, or sixty years, as Abraham himself was circumcised at ninety and nine, and as Paul circumcised Timothy, whose father was a Greek. Nothing that defileth enters the kingdom.
- The Egyptians did not practice circumcision; they were a nation of uncleanness, and the covenant was between the Lord and Israel: after the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do.
- [[Romans 2|Romans 2]] does not do away with circumcision of the flesh. Circumcision verily profiteth if thou keep the law: the Jews who grew up circumcised and keeping the law, yet broke it, made their circumcision uncircumcision; and the uncircumcised of the cast-off northern kingdom who keep the righteousness of the law are counted as circumcised. Circumcision of the heart is putting away the evil of the doings, as the prophets say, take away the foreskins of your heart; the scribes and Pharisees were clean on the outside of the cup but full of iniquity within.
- Precept **[[Colossians 3#^v5|Colossians 3:5-6]]**: Mortify therefore your members, fornication, uncleanness; for which things' sake the wrath of God cometh on the children of disobedience.

**[[Exodus 7#^v19|Exodus 7:19-22]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

- With this first plague the Most High was mocking Sobek, the Egyptian idol god of the rivers, showing that he could not defend his own waters.
- Many Egyptologists claim this never happened, because to admit it happened is to admit that the idols they claim to be gods and goddesses were powerless before our God, and that those idols do not exist at all.

**[[Exodus 8#^v5|Exodus 8:5-8]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

**[[Exodus 8#^v16|Exodus 8:16-19]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

- With the third and fourth plagues, the lice and the swarms of flies, the Most High was mocking Beelzebub, the idol lord of flies and uncleanness.

**[[Exodus 9#^v1|Exodus 9:1-7]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

- The Egyptians could not pray to their idols to help them, because the Lord was mocking them, showing them that Hathor does not exist, Sobek does not exist, Heqet does not exist, and Beelzebub does not exist.

**[[Exodus 9#^v8|Exodus 9:8-12]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

**[[Exodus 9#^v18|Exodus 9:18-21]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

- He that regarded not the word of the Lord left his servants and his cattle in the field. Many in Egypt held certain idols in more favor than others, and these trusted that Nut, the idol goddess of the sky, would keep them safe.

**[[Exodus 9#^v22|Exodus 9:22-26]]**  taught in [[Exodus 6-9 Study Notes|Exodus 6-9]]

- With this seventh plague the Most High was mocking Nut and Geb, the Egyptian idol gods of the sky and the earth, striking from the sky and burning along the ground they were supposed to rule.

**[[Exodus 12#^v29|Exodus 12:29]]**  taught in [[Exodus 10-13 Study Notes|Exodus 10-13]]

- At midnight the Lord smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, from the firstborn of Pharaoh that sat on his throne unto the firstborn of the captive in the dungeon, and all the firstborn of cattle. Even the prisoners' firstborn died, and Pharaoh's own son died.
- In this Christ showed that Osiris and Isis, the supposed Egyptian gods of life and death, control neither life nor death. Unto God the Lord belong the issues from death; the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob is the God of salvation, and he alone brings forth death.
- Precept **[[Psalms 68#^v20|Psalm 68:20]]**: He that is our God is the God of salvation; and unto God the Lord belong the issues from death, not to the idols of Egypt.

**[[Exodus 14#^v1|Exodus 14:1-5]]**  taught in [[Exodus 14-17 Study Notes|Exodus 14-17]]

- The heart of Pharaoh and of his servants turned as soon as the people were gone: why have we done this, that we have let Israel go from serving us? The oppressor does not release his slaves gladly.

**[[Exodus 18#^v9|Exodus 18:9-11]]**  taught in [[Exodus 18-21 Study Notes|Exodus 18-21]]

- Other non-Israelites came to that same knowledge. All the gods of the nations are idols, but the Lord made the heavens; give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name.
- Naaman the Syrian, healed of his leprosy in Jordan, confessed that there is no God in all the earth but in Israel. His faith was so strong he vowed to offer neither burnt offering nor sacrifice unto other gods, and asked pardon for even bowing in the house of Rimmon with his master.
- Nebuchadnezzar came to praise and extol and honour the King of heaven, for those that walk in pride he is able to abase. The heathen understand that the Most High is greater than all other gods; it is our people that want a god that does not look like them.
- Precept **[[Psalms 96#^v5|Psalms 96:5-8]]**: All the gods of the nations are idols, but the Lord made the heavens; give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name.
- Precept **[[2 Kings 5#^v14|2 Kings 5:14-18]]**: Naaman dipped seven times in Jordan and was clean, and confessed there is no God in all the earth but in Israel; a heathen vowed to sacrifice to no other god but the Lord.

**[[Exodus 20#^v4|Exodus 20:4-5]]**  taught in [[Exodus 18-21 Study Notes|Exodus 18-21]]

- Visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation: Israel is yet this day in captivity, scattered for a reproach and a curse, according to all the iniquities of the fathers which departed from the Lord. Right now Israel is paying for the sins of the fathers; that is why the people are scattered to the four corners of the earth.

**[[Exodus 20#^v6|Exodus 20:6]]**  taught in [[Exodus 18-21 Study Notes|Exodus 18-21]]

- Precept **[[Judith 8#^v18|Judith 8:18-20]]**: The fathers were given to the sword for idolatry, but a generation that knows none other god can trust he will not despise the nation.

**[[Exodus 20#^v8|Exodus 20:8-11]]**  taught in [[Exodus 18-21 Study Notes|Exodus 18-21]]

- Precept **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v64|Deuteronomy 28:64]]**: Scattered among all people, there shalt thou serve other gods, wood and stone; that is how the Sabbath was forgotten.

**[[Exodus 23#^v13|Exodus 23:13]]**  taught in [[Exodus 22-25 Study Notes|Exodus 22-25]]

- Joshua and Deuteronomy double it: neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow yourselves unto them; and if thy brother or son or wife entice thee secretly, saying, Let us go and serve other gods, thou shalt not consent. Wherever you are, do not go trying to find out how the nations worship their gods.
- Precept **[[Deuteronomy 13#^v6|Deuteronomy 13:6-8]]**: If thy nearest kin entice thee secretly, saying, Let us go and serve other gods, thou shalt not consent unto him nor hearken unto him.

- Precept **[[Exodus 20#^v1|Exodus 20:1-5]]**: I am the LORD thy God; thou shalt have no other gods before me, nor make any graven image, nor bow down to them, for the iniquity of the fathers is visited upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate him; the calf broke these first commandments.
