# Numbers 29

1 And in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work: it is a day of blowing the trumpets unto you. ^v1

2 And ye shall offer a burnt offering for a sweet savour unto the Lord; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year without blemish: ^v2

3 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram, ^v3

4 And one tenth deal for one lamb, throughout the seven lambs: ^v4

5 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you: ^v5

6 Beside the burnt offering of the month, and his meat offering, and the daily burnt offering, and his meat offering, and their drink offerings, according unto their manner, for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord. ^v6

7 And ye shall have on the tenth day of this seventh month an holy convocation; and ye shall afflict your souls: ye shall not do any work therein: ^v7

8 But ye shall offer a burnt offering unto the Lord for a sweet savour; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year; they shall be unto you without blemish: ^v8

9 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals to a bullock, and two tenth deals to one ram, ^v9

10 A several tenth deal for one lamb, throughout the seven lambs: ^v10

11 One kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering of it, and their drink offerings. ^v11

12 And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work, and ye shall keep a feast unto the Lord seven days: ^v12

13 And ye shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord; thirteen young bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year; they shall be without blemish: ^v13

14 And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals unto every bullock of the thirteen bullocks, two tenth deals to each ram of the two rams, ^v14

15 And a several tenth deal to each lamb of the fourteen lambs: ^v15

16 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v16

17 And on the second day ye shall offer twelve young bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without spot: ^v17

18 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v18

19 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering thereof, and their drink offerings. ^v19

20 And on the third day eleven bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish; ^v20

21 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v21

22 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v22

23 And on the fourth day ten bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish: ^v23

24 Their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v24

25 And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v25

26 And on the fifth day nine bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without spot: ^v26

27 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v27

28 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v28

29 And on the sixth day eight bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish: ^v29

30 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v30

31 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v31

32 And on the seventh day seven bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish: ^v32

33 And their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullocks, for the rams, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v33

34 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v34

35 On the eighth day ye shall have a solemn assembly: ye shall do no servile work therein: ^v35

36 But ye shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord: one bullock, one ram, seven lambs of the first year without blemish: ^v36

37 Their meat offering and their drink offerings for the bullock, for the ram, and for the lambs, shall be according to their number, after the manner: ^v37

38 And one goat for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, and his drink offering. ^v38

39 These things ye shall do unto the Lord in your set feasts, beside your vows, and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, and for your meat offerings, and for your drink offerings, and for your peace offerings. ^v39

40 And Moses told the children of Israel according to all that the Lord commanded Moses. ^v40
