# Numbers 33

1 These are the journeys of the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt with their armies under the hand of Moses and Aaron. ^v1

2 And Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the Lord: and these are their journeys according to their goings out. ^v2

3 And they departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the children of Israel went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians. ^v3

4 For the Egyptians buried all their firstborn, which the Lord had smitten among them: upon their gods also the Lord executed judgments. ^v4

5 And the children of Israel removed from Rameses, and pitched in Succoth. ^v5

6 And they departed from Succoth, and pitched in Etham, which is in the edge of the wilderness. ^v6

7 And they removed from Etham, and turned again unto Pi–hahiroth, which is before Baal–zephon: and they pitched before Migdol. ^v7

8 And they departed from before Pi–hahiroth, and passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness, and went three days’ journey in the wilderness of Etham, and pitched in Marah. ^v8

9 And they removed from Marah, and came unto Elim: and in Elim were twelve fountains of water, and threescore and ten palm trees; and they pitched there. ^v9

10 And they removed from Elim, and encamped by the Red sea. ^v10

11 And they removed from the Red sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin. ^v11

12 And they took their journey out of the wilderness of Sin, and encamped in Dophkah. ^v12

13 And they departed from Dophkah, and encamped in Alush. ^v13

14 And they removed from Alush, and encamped at Rephidim, where was no water for the people to drink. ^v14

15 And they departed from Rephidim, and pitched in the wilderness of Sinai. ^v15

16 And they removed from the desert of Sinai, and pitched at Kibroth–hattaavah. ^v16

17 And they departed from Kibroth–hattaavah, and encamped at Hazeroth. ^v17

18 And they departed from Hazeroth, and pitched in Rithmah. ^v18

19 And they departed from Rithmah, and pitched at Rimmon–parez. ^v19

20 And they departed from Rimmon–parez, and pitched in Libnah. ^v20

21 And they removed from Libnah, and pitched at Rissah. ^v21

22 And they journeyed from Rissah, and pitched in Kehelathah. ^v22

23 And they went from Kehelathah, and pitched in mount Shapher. ^v23

24 And they removed from mount Shapher, and encamped in Haradah. ^v24

25 And they removed from Haradah, and pitched in Makheloth. ^v25

26 And they removed from Makheloth, and encamped at Tahath. ^v26

27 And they departed from Tahath, and pitched at Tarah. ^v27

28 And they removed from Tarah, and pitched in Mithcah. ^v28

29 And they went from Mithcah, and pitched in Hashmonah. ^v29

30 And they departed from Hashmonah, and encamped at Moseroth. ^v30

31 And they departed from Moseroth, and pitched in Bene–jaakan. ^v31

32 And they removed from Bene–jaakan, and encamped at Hor–hagidgad. ^v32

33 And they went from Hor–hagidgad, and pitched in Jotbathah. ^v33

34 And they removed from Jotbathah, and encamped at Ebronah. ^v34

35 And they departed from Ebronah, and encamped at Ezion–gaber. ^v35

36 And they removed from Ezion–gaber, and pitched in the wilderness of Zin, which is Kadesh. ^v36

37 And they removed from Kadesh, and pitched in mount Hor, in the edge of the land of Edom. ^v37

38 And Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of the Lord, and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the first day of the fifth month. ^v38

39 And Aaron was an hundred and twenty and three years old when he died in mount Hor. ^v39

40 And king Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt in the south in the land of Canaan, heard of the coming of the children of Israel. ^v40

41 And they departed from mount Hor, and pitched in Zalmonah. ^v41

42 And they departed from Zalmonah, and pitched in Punon. ^v42

43 And they departed from Punon, and pitched in Oboth. ^v43

44 And they departed from Oboth, and pitched in Ije–abarim, in the border of Moab. ^v44

45 And they departed from Iim, and pitched in Dibon–gad. ^v45

46 And they removed from Dibon–gad, and encamped in Almon–diblathaim. ^v46

47 And they removed from Almon–diblathaim, and pitched in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo. ^v47

48 And they departed from the mountains of Abarim, and pitched in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho. ^v48

49 And they pitched by Jordan, from Beth–jesimoth even unto Abel–shittim in the plains of Moab. ^v49

50 And the Lord spake unto Moses in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho, saying, ^v50

51 Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye are passed over Jordan into the land of Canaan; ^v51

52 Then ye shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, and destroy all their pictures, and destroy all their molten images, and quite pluck down all their high places: ^v52

53 And ye shall dispossess the inhabitants of the land, and dwell therein: for I have given you the land to possess it. ^v53

54 And ye shall divide the land by lot for an inheritance among your families: and to the more ye shall give the more inheritance, and to the fewer ye shall give the less inheritance: every man’s inheritance shall be in the place where his lot falleth; according to the tribes of your fathers ye shall inherit. ^v54

55 But if ye will not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you; then it shall come to pass, that those which ye let remain of them shall be pricks in your eyes, and thorns in your sides, and shall vex you in the land wherein ye dwell. ^v55

56 Moreover it shall come to pass, that I shall do unto you, as I thought to do unto them. ^v56
