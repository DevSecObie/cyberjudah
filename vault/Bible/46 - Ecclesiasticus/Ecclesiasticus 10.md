# Ecclesiasticus 10

1 A wise judge will instruct his people; and the government of a prudent man is well ordered. ^v1

2 As the judge of the people is himself, so are his officers; and what manner of man the ruler of the city is, such are all they that dwell therein. ^v2

3 An unwise king destroyeth his people; but through the prudence of them which are in authority the city shall be inhabited. ^v3

4 The power of the earth is in the hand of the Lord, and in due time he will set over it one that is profitable. ^v4

5 In the hand of God is the prosperity of man: and upon the person of the scribe shall he lay his honour. ^v5

6 Bear not hatred to thy neighbour for every wrong; and do nothing at all by injurious practices. ^v6

7 Pride is hateful before God and man: and by both doth one commit iniquity. ^v7

8 Because of unrighteous dealings, injuries, and riches got by deceit, the kingdom is translated from one people to another. ^v8

9 Why is earth and ashes proud? There is not a more wicked thing than a covetous man: for such an one setteth his own soul to sale; because while he liveth he casteth away his bowels. ^v9

10 The physician cutteth off a long disease; and he that is to day a king to morrow shall die. ^v10

11 For when a man is dead, he shall inherit creeping things, beasts, and worms. ^v11

12 The beginning of pride is when one departeth from God, and his heart is turned away from his Maker. ^v12

13 For pride is the beginning of sin, and he that hath it shall pour out abomination: and therefore the Lord brought upon them strange calamities, and overthrew them utterly. ^v13

14 The Lord hath cast down the thrones of proud princes, and set up the meek in their stead. ^v14

15 The Lord hath plucked up the roots of the proud nations, and planted the lowly in their place. ^v15

16 The Lord overthrew countries of the heathen, and destroyed them to the foundations of the earth. ^v16

17 He took some of them away, and destroyed them, and hath made their memorial to cease from the earth. ^v17

18 Pride was not made for men, nor furious anger for them that are born of a woman. ^v18

19 They that fear the Lord are a sure seed, and they that love him an honourable plant: they that regard not the law are a dishonourable seed; they that transgress the commandments are a deceivable seed. ^v19

20 Among brethren he that is chief is honorable; so are they that fear the Lord in his eyes. ^v20

21 The fear of the Lord goeth before the obtaining of authority: but roughness and pride is the losing thereof. ^v21

22 Whether he be rich, noble, or poor, their glory is the fear of the Lord. ^v22

23 It is not meet to despise the poor man that hath understanding; neither is it convenient to magnify a sinful man. ^v23

24 Great men, and judges, and potentates, shall be honoured; yet is there none of them greater than he that feareth the Lord. ^v24

25 Unto the servant that is wise shall they that are free do service: and he that hath knowledge will not grudge when he is reformed. ^v25

26 Be not overwise in doing thy business; and boast not thyself in the time of thy distress. ^v26

27 Better is he that laboureth, and aboundeth in all things, than he that boasteth himself, and wanteth bread. ^v27

28 My son, glorify thy soul in meekness, and give it honour according to the dignity thereof. ^v28

29 Who will justify him that sinneth against his own soul? and who will honour him that dishonoureth his own life? ^v29

30 The poor man is honoured for his skill, and the rich man is honoured for his riches. ^v30

31 He that is honoured in poverty, how much more in riches? and he that is dishonourable in riches, how much more in poverty? ^v31
