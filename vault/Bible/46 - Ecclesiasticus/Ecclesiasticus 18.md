# Ecclesiasticus 18

1 He that liveth for ever Hath created all things in general. ^v1

2 The Lord only is righteous, and there is none other but he, ^v2

3 Who governeth the world with the palm of his hand, and all things obey his will: for he is the King of all, by his power dividing holy things among them from profane. ^v3

4 To whom hath he given power to declare his works? and who shall find out his noble acts? ^v4

5 Who shall number the strength of his majesty? and who shall also tell out his mercies? ^v5

6 As for the wondrous works of the Lord, there may nothing be taken from them, neither may any thing be put unto them, neither can the ground of them be found out. ^v6

7 When a man hath done, then he beginneth; and when he leaveth off, then he shall be doubtful. ^v7

8 What is man, and whereto serveth he? what is his good, and what is his evil? ^v8

9 The number of a man’s days at the most are an hundred years. ^v9

10 As a drop of water unto the sea, and a gravelstone in comparison of the sand; so are a thousand years to the days of eternity. ^v10

11 Therefore is God patient with them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them. ^v11

12 He saw and perceived their end to be evil; therefore he multiplied his compassion. ^v12

13 The mercy of man is toward his neighbour; but the mercy of the Lord is upon all flesh: he reproveth, and nurtureth, and teacheth and bringeth again, as a shepherd his flock. ^v13

14 He hath mercy on them that receive discipline, and that diligently seek after his judgments. ^v14

15 My son, blemish not thy good deeds, neither use uncomfortable words when thou givest any thing. ^v15

16 Shall not the dew asswage the heat? so is a word better than a gift. ^v16

17 Lo, is not a word better than a gift? but both are with a gracious man. ^v17

18 A fool will upbraid churlishly, and a gift of the envious consumeth the eyes. ^v18

19 Learn before thou speak, and use physick or ever thou be sick. ^v19

20 Before judgment examine thyself, and in the day of visitation thou shalt find mercy. ^v20

21 Humble thyself before thou be sick, and in the time of sins shew repentance. ^v21

22 Let nothing hinder thee to pay thy vow in due time, and defer not until death to be justified. ^v22

23 Before thou prayest, prepare thyself; and be not as one that tempteth the Lord. ^v23

24 Think upon the wrath that shall be at the end, and the time of vengeance, when he shall turn away his face. ^v24

25 When thou hast enough, remember the time of hunger: and when thou art rich, think upon poverty and need. ^v25

26 From the morning until the evening the time is changed, and all things are soon done before the Lord. ^v26

27 A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the day of sinning he will beware of offence: but a fool will not observe time. ^v27

28 Every man of understanding knoweth wisdom, and will give praise unto him that found her. ^v28

29 They that were of understanding in sayings became also wise themselves, and poured forth exquisite parables. ^v29

30 Go not after thy lusts, but refrain thyself from thine appetites. ^v30

31 If thou givest thy soul the desires that please her, she will make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies that malign thee. ^v31

32 Take not pleasure in much good cheer, neither be tied to the expence thereof. ^v32

33 Be not made a beggar by banqueting upon borrowing, when thou hast nothing in thy purse: for thou shalt lie in wait for thine own life, and be talked on. ^v33
