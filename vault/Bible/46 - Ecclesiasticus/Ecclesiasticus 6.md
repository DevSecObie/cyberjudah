# Ecclesiasticus 6

1 Instead of a friend become not an enemy; for thereby thou shalt inherit an ill name, shame, and reproach: even so shall a sinner that hath a double tongue. ^v1

2 Extol not thyself in the counsel of thine own heart; that thy soul be not torn in pieces as a bull straying alone. ^v2

3 Thou shalt eat up thy leaves, and lose thy fruit, and leave thyself as a dry tree. ^v3

4 A wicked soul shall destroy him that hath it, and shall make him to be laughed to scorn of his enemies. ^v4

5 Sweet language will multiply friends: and a fairspeaking tongue will increase kind greetings. ^v5

6 Be in peace with many: nevertheless have but one counsellor of a thousand. ^v6

7 If thou wouldest get a friend, prove him first and be not hasty to credit him. ^v7

8 For some man is a friend for his own occasion, and will not abide in the day of thy trouble. ^v8

9 And there is a friend, who being turned to enmity, and strife will discover thy reproach. ^v9

10 Again, some friend is a companion at the table, and will not continue in the day of thy affliction. ^v10

11 But in thy prosperity he will be as thyself, and will be bold over thy servants. ^v11

12 If thou be brought low, he will be against thee, and will hide himself from thy face. ^v12

13 Separate thyself from thine enemies, and take heed of thy friends. ^v13

14 A faithfull friend is a strong defence: and he that hath found such an one hath found a treasure. ^v14

15 Nothing doth countervail a faithful friend, and his excellency is invaluable. ^v15

16 A faithful friend is the medicine of life; and they that fear the Lord shall find him. ^v16

17 Whoso feareth the Lord shall direct his friendship aright: for as he is, so shall his neighbour be also. ^v17

18 My son, gather instruction from thy youth up: so shalt thou find wisdom till thine old age. ^v18

19 Come unto her as one that ploweth and soweth, and wait for her good fruits: for thou shalt not toil much in labouring about her, but thou shalt eat of her fruits right soon. ^v19

20 She is very unpleasant to the unlearned: he that is without understanding will not remain with her. ^v20

21 She will lie upon him as a mighty stone of trial; and he will cast her from him ere it be long. ^v21

22 For wisdom is according to her name, and she is not manifest unto many. ^v22

23 Give ear, my son, receive my advice, and refuse not my counsel, ^v23

24 And put thy feet into her fetters, and thy neck into her chain. ^v24

25 Bow down thy shoulder, and bear her, and be not grieved with her bonds. ^v25

26 Come unto her with thy whole heart, and keep her ways with all thy power. ^v26

27 Search, and seek, and she shall be made known unto thee: and when thou hast got hold of her, let her not go. ^v27

28 For at the last thou shalt find her rest, and that shall be turned to thy joy. ^v28

29 Then shall her fetters be a strong defence for thee, and her chains a robe of glory. ^v29

30 For there is a golden ornament upon her, and her bands are purple lace. ^v30

31 Thou shalt put her on as a robe of honour, and shalt put her about thee as a crown of joy. ^v31

32 My son, if thou wilt, thou shalt be taught: and if thou wilt apply thy mind, thou shalt be prudent. ^v32

33 If thou love to hear, thou shalt receive understanding: and if thou bow thine ear, thou shalt be wise, ^v33

34 Stand in the multitude of the elders; and cleave unto him that is wise. ^v34

35 Be willing to hear every godly discourse; and let not the parables of understanding escape thee. ^v35

36 And if thou seest a man of understanding, get thee betimes unto him, and let thy foot wear the steps of his door. ^v36

37 Let thy mind be upon the ordinances of the Lord and meditate continually in his commandments: he shall establish thine heart, and give thee wisdom at thine owns desire. ^v37
