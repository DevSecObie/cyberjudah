# Ecclesiasticus 14

1 Blessed is the man that hath not slipped with his mouth, and is not pricked with the multitude of sins. ^v1

2 Blessed is he whose conscience hath not condemned him, and who is not fallen from his hope in the Lord. ^v2

3 Riches are not comely for a niggard: and what should an envious man do with money? ^v3

4 He that gathereth by defrauding his own soul gathereth for others, that shall spend his goods riotously. ^v4

5 He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? he shall not take pleasure in his goods. ^v5

6 There is none worse than he that envieth himself; and this is a recompence of his wickedness. ^v6

7 And if he doeth good, he doeth it unwillingly; and at the last he will declare his wickedness. ^v7

8 The envious man hath a wicked eye; he turneth away his face, and despiseth men. ^v8

9 A covetous man’s eye is not satisfied with his portion; and the iniquity of the wicked drieth up his soul. ^v9

10 A wicked eye envieth his bread, and he is a niggard at his table. ^v10

11 My son, according to thy ability do good to thyself, and give the Lord his due offering. ^v11

12 Remember that death will not be long in coming, and that the covenant of the grave is not shewed unto thee. ^v12

13 Do good unto thy friend before thou die, and according to thy ability stretch out thy hand and give to him. ^v13

14 Defraud not thyself of the good day, and let not the part of a good desire overpass thee. ^v14

15 Shalt thou not leave thy travails unto another? and thy labours to be divided by lot? ^v15

16 Give, and take, and sanctify thy soul; for there is no seeking of dainties in the grave. ^v16

17 All flesh waxeth old as a garment: for the covenant from the beginning is, Thou shalt die the death. ^v17

18 As of the green leaves on a thick tree, some fall, and some grow; so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born. ^v18

19 Every work rotteth and consumeth away, and the worker thereof shall go withal. ^v19

20 Blessed is the man that doth meditate good things in wisdom, and that reasoneth of holy things by his understanding. ^v20

21 He that considereth her ways in his heart shall also have understanding in her secrets. ^v21

22 Go after her as one that traceth, and lie in wait in her ways. ^v22

23 He that prieth in at her windows shall also hearken at her doors. ^v23

24 He that doth lodge near her house shall also fasten a pin in her walls. ^v24

25 He shall pitch his tent nigh unto her, and shall lodge in a lodging where good things are. ^v25

26 He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches. ^v26

27 By her he shall be covered from heat, and in her glory shall he dwell. ^v27
