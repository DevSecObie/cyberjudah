# Ecclesiasticus 21

1 My son, hast thou sinned? do so no more, but ask pardon for thy former sins. ^v1

2 Flee from sin as from the face of a serpent: for if thou comest too near it, it will bite thee: the teeth thereof are as the teeth of a lion, slaying the souls of men. ^v2

3 All iniquity is as a two edged sword, the wounds whereof cannot be healed. ^v3

4 To terrify and do wrong will waste riches: thus the house of proud men shall be made desolate. ^v4

5 A prayer out of a poor man’s mouth reacheth to the ears of God, and his judgment cometh speedily. ^v5

6 He that hateth to be reproved is in the way of sinners: but he that feareth the Lord will repent from his heart. ^v6

7 An eloquent man is known far and near; but a man of understanding knoweth when he slippeth. ^v7

8 He that buildeth his house with other men’s money is like one that gathereth himself stones for the tomb of his burial. ^v8

9 The congregation of the wicked is like tow wrapped together: and the end of them is a flame of fire to destroy them. ^v9

10 The way of sinners is made plain with stones, but at the end thereof is the pit of hell. ^v10

11 He that keepeth the law of the Lord getteth the understanding thereof: and the perfection of the fear of the Lord is wisdom. ^v11

12 He that is not wise will not be taught: but there is a wisdom which multiplieth bitterness. ^v12

13 The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood: and his counsel is like a pure fountain of life. ^v13

14 The inner parts of a fool are like a broken vessel, and he will hold no knowledge as long as he liveth. ^v14

15 If a skilful man hear a wise word, he will commend it, and add unto it: but as soon as one of no understanding heareth it, it displeaseth him, and he casteth it behind his back. ^v15

16 The talking of a fool is like a burden in the way: but grace shall be found in the lips of the wise. ^v16

17 They enquire at the mouth of the wise man in the congregation, and they shall ponder his words in their heart. ^v17

18 As is a house that is destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool: and the knowledge of the unwise is as talk without sense. ^v18

19 Doctrine unto fools is as fetters on the feet, and like manacles on the right hand. ^v19

20 A fool lifteth up his voice with laughter; but a wise man doth scarce smile a little. ^v20

21 Learning is unto a wise man as an ornament of gold, and like a bracelet upon his right arm. ^v21

22 A foolish man’s foot is soon in his neighbour’s house: but a man of experience is ashamed of him. ^v22

23 A fool will peep in at the door into the house: but he that is well nurtured will stand without. ^v23

24 It is the rudeness of a man to hearken at the door: but a wise man will be grieved with the disgrace. ^v24

25 The lips of talkers will be telling such things as pertain not unto them: but the words of such as have understanding are weighed in the balance. ^v25

26 The heart of fools is in their mouth: but the mouth of the wise is in their heart. ^v26

27 When the ungodly curseth Satan, he curseth his own soul. ^v27

28 A whisperer defileth his own soul, and is hated wheresoever he dwelleth. ^v28
