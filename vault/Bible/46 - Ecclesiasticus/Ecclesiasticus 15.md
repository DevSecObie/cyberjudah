# Ecclesiasticus 15

1 He that feareth the Lord will do good, and he that hath the knowledge of the law shall obtain her. ^v1

2 And as a mother shall she meet him, and receive him as a wife married of a virgin. ^v2

3 With the bread of understanding shall she feed him, and give him the water of wisdom to drink. ^v3

4 He shall be stayed upon her, and shall not be moved; and shall rely upon her, and shall not be confounded. ^v4

5 She shall exalt him above his neighbours, and in the midst of the congregation shall she open his mouth. ^v5

6 He shall find joy and a crown of gladness, and she shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name. ^v6

7 But foolish men shall not attain unto her, and sinners shall not see her. ^v7

8 For she is far from pride, and men that are liars cannot remember her. ^v8

9 Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent him of the Lord. ^v9

10 For praise shall be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it. ^v10

11 Say not thou, It is through the Lord that I fell away: for thou oughtest not to do the things that he hateth. ^v11

12 Say not thou, He hath caused me to err: for he hath no need of the sinful man. ^v12

13 The Lord hateth all abomination; and they that fear God love it not. ^v13

14 He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel; ^v14

15 If thou wilt, to keep the commandments, and to perform acceptable faithfulness. ^v15

16 He hath set fire and water before thee: stretch forth thy hand unto whether thou wilt. ^v16

17 Before man is life and death; and whether him liketh shall be given him. ^v17

18 For the wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholdeth all things: ^v18

19 And his eyes are upon them that fear him, and he knoweth every work of man. ^v19

20 He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin. ^v20
