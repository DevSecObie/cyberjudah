# Ecclesiasticus 8

1 Strive not with a mighty man, lest thou fall into his hands. ^v1

2 Be not at variance with a rich man, lest he overweigh thee: for gold hath destroyed many, and perverted the hearts of kings. ^v2

3 Strive not with a man that is full of tongue, and heap not wood upon his fire. ^v3

4 Jest not with a rude man, lest thy ancestors be disgraced. ^v4

5 Reproach not a man that turneth from sin, but remember that we are all worthy of punishment. ^v5

6 Dishonour not a man in his old age: for even some of us wax old. ^v6

7 Rejoice not over thy greatest enemy being dead, but remember that we die all. ^v7

8 Despise not the discourse of the wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs: for of them thou shalt learn instruction, and how to serve great men with ease. ^v8

9 Miss not the discourse of the elders: for they also learned of their fathers, and of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give answer as need requireth. ^v9

10 Kindle not the coals of a sinner, lest thou be burnt with the flame of his fire. ^v10

11 Rise not up in anger at the presence of an injurious person, lest he lie in wait to entrap thee in thy words ^v11

12 Lend not unto him that is mightier than thyself; for if thou lendest him, count it but lost. ^v12

13 Be not surety above thy power: for if thou be surety, take care to pay it. ^v13

14 Go not to law with a judge; for they will judge for him according to his honour. ^v14

15 Travel not by the way with a bold fellow, lest he become grievous unto thee: for he will do according to his own will, and thou shalt perish with him through his folly. ^v15

16 Strive not with an angry man, and go not with him into a solitary place: for blood is as nothing in his sight, and where there is no help, he will overthrow thee. ^v16

17 Consult not with a fool; for he cannot keep counsel. ^v17

18 Do no secret thing before a stranger; for thou knowest not what he will bring forth. ^v18

19 Open not thine heart to every man, lest he requite thee with a shrewd turn. ^v19
