# Proverbs 13

1 A wise son heareth his father’s instruction: but a scorner heareth not rebuke. ^v1

2 A man shall eat good by the fruit of his mouth: but the soul of the transgressors shall eat violence. ^v2

3 He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction. ^v3

4 The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat. ^v4

5 A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame. ^v5

6 Righteousness keepeth him that is upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner. ^v6

7 There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches. ^v7

8 The ransom of a man’s life are his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke. ^v8

9 The light of the righteous rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out. ^v9

10 Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom. ^v10

11 Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase. ^v11

12 Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life. ^v12

13 Whoso despiseth the word shall be destroyed: but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded. ^v13

14 The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death. ^v14

15 Good understanding giveth favour: but the way of transgressors is hard. ^v15

16 Every prudent man dealeth with knowledge: but a fool layeth open his folly. ^v16

17 A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health. ^v17

18 Poverty and shame shall be to him that refuseth instruction: but he that regardeth reproof shall be honoured. ^v18

19 The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but it is abomination to fools to depart from evil. ^v19

20 He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed. ^v20

21 Evil pursueth sinners: but to the righteous good shall be repayed. ^v21

22 A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children’s children: and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just. ^v22

23 Much food is in the tillage of the poor: but there is that is destroyed for want of judgment. ^v23

24 He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes. ^v24

25 The righteous eateth to the satisfying of his soul: but the belly of the wicked shall want. ^v25
