# Proverbs 5

1 My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding: ^v1

2 That thou mayest regard discretion, and that thy lips may keep knowledge. ^v2

3 For the lips of a strange woman drop as an honeycomb, and her mouth is smoother than oil: ^v3

4 But her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a twoedged sword. ^v4

5 Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on hell. ^v5

6 Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, that thou canst not know them. ^v6

7 Hear me now therefore, O ye children, and depart not from the words of my mouth. ^v7

8 Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house: ^v8

9 Lest thou give thine honour unto others, and thy years unto the cruel: ^v9

10 Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth; and thy labours be in the house of a stranger; ^v10

11 And thou mourn at the last, when thy flesh and thy body are consumed, ^v11

12 And say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof; ^v12

13 And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me! ^v13

14 I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly. ^v14

15 Drink waters out of thine own cistern, and running waters out of thine own well. ^v15

16 Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad, and rivers of waters in the streets. ^v16

17 Let them be only thine own, and not strangers’ with thee. ^v17

18 Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth. ^v18

19 Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe; let her breasts satisfy thee at all times; and be thou ravished always with her love. ^v19

20 And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger? ^v20

21 For the ways of man are before the eyes of the Lord, and he pondereth all his goings. ^v21

22 His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sins. ^v22

23 He shall die without instruction; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray. ^v23
