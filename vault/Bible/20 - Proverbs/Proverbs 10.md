# Proverbs 10

1 The proverbs of Solomon. A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish son is the heaviness of his mother. ^v1

2 Treasures of wickedness profit nothing: but righteousness delivereth from death. ^v2

3 The Lord will not suffer the soul of the righteous to famish: but he casteth away the substance of the wicked. ^v3

4 He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich. ^v4

5 He that gathereth in summer is a wise son: but he that sleepeth in harvest is a son that causeth shame. ^v5

6 Blessings are upon the head of the just: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked. ^v6

7 The memory of the just is blessed: but the name of the wicked shall rot. ^v7

8 The wise in heart will receive commandments: but a prating fool shall fall. ^v8

9 He that walketh uprightly walketh surely: but he that perverteth his ways shall be known. ^v9

10 He that winketh with the eye causeth sorrow: but a prating fool shall fall. ^v10

11 The mouth of a righteous man is a well of life: but violence covereth the mouth of the wicked. ^v11

12 Hatred stirreth up strifes: but love covereth all sins. ^v12

13 In the lips of him that hath understanding wisdom is found: but a rod is for the back of him that is void of understanding. ^v13

14 Wise men lay up knowledge: but the mouth of the foolish is near destruction. ^v14

15 The rich man’s wealth is his strong city: the destruction of the poor is their poverty. ^v15

16 The labour of the righteous tendeth to life: the fruit of the wicked to sin. ^v16

17 He is in the way of life that keepeth instruction: but he that refuseth reproof erreth. ^v17

18 He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool. ^v18

19 In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin: but he that refraineth his lips is wise. ^v19

20 The tongue of the just is as choice silver: the heart of the wicked is little worth. ^v20

21 The lips of the righteous feed many: but fools die for want of wisdom. ^v21

22 The blessing of the Lord, it maketh rich, and he addeth no sorrow with it. ^v22

23 It is as sport to a fool to do mischief: but a man of understanding hath wisdom. ^v23

24 The fear of the wicked, it shall come upon him: but the desire of the righteous shall be granted. ^v24

25 As the whirlwind passeth, so is the wicked no more: but the righteous is an everlasting foundation. ^v25

26 As vinegar to the teeth, and as smoke to the eyes, so is the sluggard to them that send him. ^v26

27 The fear of the Lord prolongeth days: but the years of the wicked shall be shortened. ^v27

28 The hope of the righteous shall be gladness: but the expectation of the wicked shall perish. ^v28

29 The way of the Lord is strength to the upright: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity. ^v29

30 The righteous shall never be removed: but the wicked shall not inhabit the earth. ^v30

31 The mouth of the just bringeth forth wisdom: but the froward tongue shall be cut out. ^v31

32 The lips of the righteous know what is acceptable: but the mouth of the wicked speaketh frowardness. ^v32
