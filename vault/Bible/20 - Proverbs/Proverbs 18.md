# Proverbs 18

1 Through desire a man, having separated himself, seeketh and intermeddleth with all wisdom. ^v1

2 A fool hath no delight in understanding, but that his heart may discover itself. ^v2

3 When the wicked cometh, then cometh also contempt, and with ignominy reproach. ^v3

4 The words of a man’s mouth are as deep waters, and the wellspring of wisdom as a flowing brook. ^v4

5 It is not good to accept the person of the wicked, to overthrow the righteous in judgment. ^v5

6 A fool’s lips enter into contention, and his mouth calleth for strokes. ^v6

7 A fool’s mouth is his destruction, and his lips are the snare of his soul. ^v7

8 The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly. ^v8

9 He also that is slothful in his work is brother to him that is a great waster. ^v9

10 The name of the Lord is a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe. ^v10

11 The rich man’s wealth is his strong city, and as an high wall in his own conceit. ^v11

12 Before destruction the heart of man is haughty, and before honour is humility. ^v12

13 He that answereth a matter before he heareth it, it is folly and shame unto him. ^v13

14 The spirit of a man will sustain his infirmity; but a wounded spirit who can bear? ^v14

15 The heart of the prudent getteth knowledge; and the ear of the wise seeketh knowledge. ^v15

16 A man’s gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men. ^v16

17 He that is first in his own cause seemeth just; but his neighbour cometh and searcheth him. ^v17

18 The lot causeth contentions to cease, and parteth between the mighty. ^v18

19 A brother offended is harder to be won than a strong city: and their contentions are like the bars of a castle. ^v19

20 A man’s belly shall be satisfied with the fruit of his mouth; and with the increase of his lips shall he be filled. ^v20

21 Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof. ^v21

22 Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the Lord. ^v22

23 The poor useth intreaties; but the rich answereth roughly. ^v23

24 A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: and there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother. ^v24
