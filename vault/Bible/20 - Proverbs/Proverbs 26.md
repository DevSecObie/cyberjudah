# Proverbs 26

1 As snow in summer, and as rain in harvest, so honour is not seemly for a fool. ^v1

2 As the bird by wandering, as the swallow by flying, so the curse causeless shall not come. ^v2

3 A whip for the horse, a bridle for the ass, and a rod for the fool’s back. ^v3

4 Answer not a fool according to his folly, lest thou also be like unto him. ^v4

5 Answer a fool according to his folly, lest he be wise in his own conceit. ^v5

6 He that sendeth a message by the hand of a fool cutteth off the feet, and drinketh damage. ^v6

7 The legs of the lame are not equal: so is a parable in the mouth of fools. ^v7

8 As he that bindeth a stone in a sling, so is he that giveth honour to a fool. ^v8

9 As a thorn goeth up into the hand of a drunkard, so is a parable in the mouth of fools. ^v9

10 The great God that formed all things both rewardeth the fool, and rewardeth transgressors. ^v10

11 As a dog returneth to his vomit, so a fool returneth to his folly. ^v11

12 Seest thou a man wise in his own conceit? there is more hope of a fool than of him. ^v12

13 The slothful man saith, There is a lion in the way; a lion is in the streets. ^v13

14 As the door turneth upon his hinges, so doth the slothful upon his bed. ^v14

15 The slothful hideth his hand in his bosom; it grieveth him to bring it again to his mouth. ^v15

16 The sluggard is wiser in his own conceit than seven men that can render a reason. ^v16

17 He that passeth by, and meddleth with strife belonging not to him, is like one that taketh a dog by the ears. ^v17

18 As a mad man who casteth firebrands, arrows, and death, ^v18

19 So is the man that deceiveth his neighbour, and saith, Am not I in sport? ^v19

20 Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth. ^v20

21 As coals are to burning coals, and wood to fire; so is a contentious man to kindle strife. ^v21

22 The words of a talebearer are as wounds, and they go down into the innermost parts of the belly. ^v22

23 Burning lips and a wicked heart are like a potsherd covered with silver dross. ^v23

24 He that hateth dissembleth with his lips, and layeth up deceit within him; ^v24

25 When he speaketh fair, believe him not: for there are seven abominations in his heart. ^v25

26 Whose hatred is covered by deceit, his wickedness shall be shewed before the whole congregation. ^v26

27 Whoso diggeth a pit shall fall therein: and he that rolleth a stone, it will return upon him. ^v27

28 A lying tongue hateth those that are afflicted by it; and a flattering mouth worketh ruin. ^v28
