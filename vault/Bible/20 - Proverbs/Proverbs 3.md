# Proverbs 3

1 My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments: ^v1

2 For length of days, and long life, and peace, shall they add to thee. ^v2

3 Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart: ^v3

4 So shalt thou find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man. ^v4

5 Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding. ^v5

6 In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths. ^v6

7 Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the Lord, and depart from evil. ^v7

8 It shall be health to thy navel, and marrow to thy bones. ^v8

9 Honour the Lord with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase: ^v9

10 So shall thy barns be filled with plenty, and thy presses shall burst out with new wine. ^v10

11 My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; neither be weary of his correction: ^v11

12 For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth. ^v12

13 Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding. ^v13

14 For the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold. ^v14

15 She is more precious than rubies: and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her. ^v15

16 Length of days is in her right hand; and in her left hand riches and honour. ^v16

17 Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace. ^v17

18 She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her. ^v18

19 The Lord by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding hath he established the heavens. ^v19

20 By his knowledge the depths are broken up, and the clouds drop down the dew. ^v20

21 My son, let not them depart from thine eyes: keep sound wisdom and discretion: ^v21

22 So shall they be life unto thy soul, and grace to thy neck. ^v22

23 Then shalt thou walk in thy way safely, and thy foot shall not stumble. ^v23

24 When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet. ^v24

25 Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh. ^v25

26 For the Lord shall be thy confidence, and shall keep thy foot from being taken. ^v26

27 Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it. ^v27

28 Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again, and to morrow I will give; when thou hast it by thee. ^v28

29 Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee. ^v29

30 Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm. ^v30

31 Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways. ^v31

32 For the froward is abomination to the Lord: but his secret is with the righteous. ^v32

33 The curse of the Lord is in the house of the wicked: but he blesseth the habitation of the just. ^v33

34 Surely he scorneth the scorners: but he giveth grace unto the lowly. ^v34

35 The wise shall inherit glory: but shame shall be the promotion of fools. ^v35
