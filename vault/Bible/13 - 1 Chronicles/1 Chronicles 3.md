# 1 Chronicles 3

1 Now these were the sons of David, which were born unto him in Hebron; the firstborn Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; the second Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess: ^v1

2 The third, Absalom the son of Maachah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur: the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith: ^v2

3 The fifth, Shephatiah of Abital: the sixth, Ithream by Eglah his wife. ^v3

4 These six were born unto him in Hebron; and there he reigned seven years and six months: and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty and three years. ^v4

5 And these were born unto him in Jerusalem; Shimea, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon, four, of Bath–shua the daughter of Ammiel: ^v5

6 Ibhar also, and Elishama, and Eliphelet, ^v6

7 And Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia, ^v7

8 And Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine. ^v8

9 These were all the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines, and Tamar their sister. ^v9

10 And Solomon’s son was Rehoboam, Abia his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son, ^v10

11 Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son, ^v11

12 Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son, ^v12

13 Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son, ^v13

14 Amon his son, Josiah his son. ^v14

15 And the sons of Josiah were, the firstborn Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum. ^v15

16 And the sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son. ^v16

17 And the sons of Jeconiah; Assir, Salathiel his son, ^v17

18 Malchiram also, and Pedaiah, and Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah. ^v18

19 And the sons of Pedaiah were, Zerubbabel, and Shimei: and the sons of Zerubbabel; Meshullam, and Hananiah, and Shelomith their sister: ^v19

20 And Hashubah, and Ohel, and Berechiah, and Hasadiah, Jushab–hesed, five. ^v20

21 And the sons of Hananiah; Pelatiah, and Jesaiah: the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shechaniah. ^v21

22 And the sons of Shechaniah; Shemaiah: and the sons of Shemaiah; Hattush, and Igeal, and Bariah, and Neariah, and Shaphat, six. ^v22

23 And the sons of Neariah; Elioenai, and Hezekiah, and Azrikam, three. ^v23

24 And the sons of Elioenai were, Hodaiah, and Eliashib, and Pelaiah, and Akkub, and Johanan, and Dalaiah, and Anani, seven. ^v24
