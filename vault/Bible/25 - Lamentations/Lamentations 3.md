# Lamentations 3

1 I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath. ^v1

2 He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light. ^v2

3 Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day. ^v3

4 My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones. ^v4

5 He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail. ^v5

6 He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old. ^v6

7 He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy. ^v7

8 Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer. ^v8

9 He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked. ^v9

10 He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places. ^v10

11 He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate. ^v11

12 He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow. ^v12

13 He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins. ^v13

14 I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day. ^v14

15 He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood. ^v15

16 He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes. ^v16

17 And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity. ^v17

18 And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the Lord: ^v18

19 Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall. ^v19

20 My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me. ^v20

21 This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope. ^v21

22 It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not. ^v22

23 They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness. ^v23

24 The Lord is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him. ^v24

25 The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him. ^v25

26 It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord. ^v26

27 It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth. ^v27

28 He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him. ^v28

29 He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope. ^v29

30 He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach. ^v30

31 For the Lord will not cast off for ever: ^v31

32 But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies. ^v32

33 For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men. ^v33

34 To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth, ^v34

35 To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High, ^v35

36 To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not. ^v36

37 Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not? ^v37

38 Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good? ^v38

39 Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins? ^v39

40 Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the Lord. ^v40

41 Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens. ^v41

42 We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned. ^v42

43 Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied. ^v43

44 Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that our prayer should not pass through. ^v44

45 Thou hast made us as the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people. ^v45

46 All our enemies have opened their mouths against us. ^v46

47 Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction. ^v47

48 Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water for the destruction of the daughter of my people. ^v48

49 Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission, ^v49

50 Till the Lord look down, and behold from heaven. ^v50

51 Mine eye affecteth mine heart because of all the daughters of my city. ^v51

52 Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause. ^v52

53 They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon me. ^v53

54 Waters flowed over mine head; then I said, I am cut off. ^v54

55 I called upon thy name, O Lord, out of the low dungeon. ^v55

56 Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry. ^v56

57 Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not. ^v57

58 O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life. ^v58

59 O Lord, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause. ^v59

60 Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me. ^v60

61 Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me; ^v61

62 The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day. ^v62

63 Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick. ^v63

64 Render unto them a recompence, O Lord, according to the work of their hands. ^v64

65 Give them sorrow of heart, thy curse unto them. ^v65

66 Persecute and destroy them in anger from under the heavens of the Lord. ^v66
