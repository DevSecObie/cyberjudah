# Book of Additions to Esther

Part of [[The Holy Bible|the Holy Bible (KJV with Apocrypha)]].

Cambridge-style numbering: the Additions continue the chapter count of the
Hebrew [[Book of Esther|Esther]] (1–9), running from 10:4 to 16:24.
Some editions renumber these seven chapters 1–7 (1=10, … 7=16).

The chapters are bound in the order Jerome appended them, not story order.
To read in narrative order, start with the dream of Mardocheus at
[[Additions to Esther 11#^v2|11:2]] (11:1 is a translator's colophon);
10:4–11:1 is actually the story's ending, where the dream is interpreted.
