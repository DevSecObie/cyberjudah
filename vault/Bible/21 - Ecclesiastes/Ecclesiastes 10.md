# Ecclesiastes 10

1 Dead flies cause the ointment of the apothecary to send forth a stinking savour: so doth a little folly him that is in reputation for wisdom and honour. ^v1

2 A wise man’s heart is at his right hand; but a fool’s heart at his left. ^v2

3 Yea also, when he that is a fool walketh by the way, his wisdom faileth him, and he saith to every one that he is a fool. ^v3

4 If the spirit of the ruler rise up against thee, leave not thy place; for yielding pacifieth great offences. ^v4

5 There is an evil which I have seen under the sun, as an error which proceedeth from the ruler: ^v5

6 Folly is set in great dignity, and the rich sit in low place. ^v6

7 I have seen servants upon horses, and princes walking as servants upon the earth. ^v7

8 He that diggeth a pit shall fall into it; and whoso breaketh an hedge, a serpent shall bite him. ^v8

9 Whoso removeth stones shall be hurt therewith; and he that cleaveth wood shall be endangered thereby. ^v9

10 If the iron be blunt, and he do not whet the edge, then must he put to more strength: but wisdom is profitable to direct. ^v10

11 Surely the serpent will bite without enchantment; and a babbler is no better. ^v11

12 The words of a wise man’s mouth are gracious; but the lips of a fool will swallow up himself. ^v12

13 The beginning of the words of his mouth is foolishness: and the end of his talk is mischievous madness. ^v13

14 A fool also is full of words: a man cannot tell what shall be; and what shall be after him, who can tell him? ^v14

15 The labour of the foolish wearieth every one of them, because he knoweth not how to go to the city. ^v15

16 Woe to thee, O land, when thy king is a child, and thy princes eat in the morning! ^v16

17 Blessed art thou, O land, when thy king is the son of nobles, and thy princes eat in due season, for strength, and not for drunkenness! ^v17

18 By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through. ^v18

19 A feast is made for laughter, and wine maketh merry: but money answereth all things. ^v19

20 Curse not the king, no not in thy thought; and curse not the rich in thy bedchamber: for a bird of the air shall carry the voice, and that which hath wings shall tell the matter. ^v20
