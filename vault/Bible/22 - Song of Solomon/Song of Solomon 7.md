# Song of Solomon 7

1 How beautiful are thy feet with shoes, O prince’s daughter! the joints of thy thighs are like jewels, the work of the hands of a cunning workman. ^v1

2 Thy navel is like a round goblet, which wanteth not liquor: thy belly is like an heap of wheat set about with lilies. ^v2

3 Thy two breasts are like two young roes that are twins. ^v3

4 Thy neck is as a tower of ivory; thine eyes like the fishpools in Heshbon, by the gate of Bath–rabbim: thy nose is as the tower of Lebanon which looketh toward Damascus. ^v4

5 Thine head upon thee is like Carmel, and the hair of thine head like purple; the king is held in the galleries. ^v5

6 How fair and how pleasant art thou, O love, for delights! ^v6

7 This thy stature is like to a palm tree, and thy breasts to clusters of grapes. ^v7

8 I said, I will go up to the palm tree, I will take hold of the boughs thereof: now also thy breasts shall be as clusters of the vine, and the smell of thy nose like apples; ^v8

9 And the roof of thy mouth like the best wine for my beloved, that goeth down sweetly, causing the lips of those that are asleep to speak. ^v9

10 I am my beloved’s, and his desire is toward me. ^v10

11 Come, my beloved, let us go forth into the field; let us lodge in the villages. ^v11

12 Let us get up early to the vineyards; let us see if the vine flourish, whether the tender grape appear, and the pomegranates bud forth: there will I give thee my loves. ^v12

13 The mandrakes give a smell, and at our gates are all manner of pleasant fruits, new and old, which I have laid up for thee, O my beloved. ^v13
