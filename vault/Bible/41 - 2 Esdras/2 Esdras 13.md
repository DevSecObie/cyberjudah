# 2 Esdras 13

1 And it came to pass after seven days, I dreamed a dream by night: ^v1

2 And, lo, there arose a wind from the sea, that it moved all the waves thereof. ^v2

3 And I beheld, and, lo, that man waxed strong with the thousands of heaven: and when he turned his countenance to look, all the things trembled that were seen under him. ^v3

4 And whensoever the voice went out of his mouth, all they burned that heard his voice, like as the earth faileth when it feeleth the fire. ^v4

5 And after this I beheld, and, lo, there was gathered together a multitude of men, out of number, from the four winds of the heaven, to subdue the man that came out of the sea ^v5

6 But I beheld, and, lo, he had graved himself a great mountain, and flew up upon it. ^v6

7 But I would have seen the region or place whereout the hill was graven, and I could not. ^v7

8 And after this I beheld, and, lo, all they which were gathered together to subdue him were sore afraid, and yet durst fight. ^v8

9 And, lo, as he saw the violence of the multitude that came, he neither lifted up his hand, nor held sword, nor any instrument of war: ^v9

10 But only I saw that he sent out of his mouth as it had been a blast of fire, and out of his lips a flaming breath, and out of his tongue he cast out sparks and tempests. ^v10

11 And they were all mixed together; the blast of fire, the flaming breath, and the great tempest; and fell with violence upon the multitude which was prepared to fight, and burned them up every one, so that upon a sudden of an innumerable multitude nothing was to be perceived, but only dust and smell of smoke: when I saw this I was afraid. ^v11

12 Afterward saw I the same man come down from the mountain, and call unto him another peaceable Multitude. ^v12

13 And there came much people unto him, whereof some were glad, some were sorry, and some of them were bound, and other some brought of them that were offered: then was I sick through great fear, and I awaked, and said, ^v13

14 Thou hast shewed thy servant these wonders from the beginning, and hast counted me worthy that thou shouldest receive my prayer: ^v14

15 Shew me now yet the interpretation of this dream. ^v15

16 For as I conceive in mine understanding, woe unto them that shall be left in those days and much more woe unto them that are not left behind! ^v16

17 For they that were not left were in heaviness. ^v17

18 Now understand I the things that are laid up in the latter days, which shall happen unto them, and to those that are left behind. ^v18

19 Therefore are they come into great perils and many necessities, like as these dreams declare. ^v19

20 Yet is it easier for him that is in danger to come into these things, than to pass away as a cloud out of the world, and not to see the things that happen in the last days. And he answered unto me, and said, ^v20

21 The interpretation of the vision shall I shew thee, and I will open unto thee the thing that thou hast required. ^v21

22 Whereas thou hast spoken of them that are left behind, this is the interpretation: ^v22

23 He that shall endure the peril in that time hath kept himself: they that be fallen into danger are such as have works, and faith toward the Almighty. ^v23

24 Know this therefore, that they which be left behind are more blessed than they that be dead. ^v24

25 This is the meaning of the vision: Whereas thou sawest a man coming up from the midst of the sea: ^v25

26 The same is he whom God the Highest hath kept a great season, which by his own self shall deliver his creature: and he shall order them that are left behind. ^v26

27 And whereas thou sawest, that out of his mouth there came as a blast of wind, and fire, and storm; ^v27

28 And that he held neither sword, nor any instrument of war, but that the rushing in of him destroyed the whole multitude that came to subdue him; this is the interpretation: ^v28

29 Behold, the days come, when the most High will begin to deliver them that are upon the earth. ^v29

30 And he shall come to the astonishment of them that dwell on the earth. ^v30

31 And one shall undertake to fight against another, one city against another, one place against another, one people against another, and one realm against another. ^v31

32 And the time shall be when these things shall come to pass, and the signs shall happen which I shewed thee before, and then shall my Son be declared, whom thou sawest as a man ascending. ^v32

33 And when all the people hear his voice, every man shall in their own land leave the battle they have one against another. ^v33

34 And an innumerable multitude shall be gathered together, as thou sawest them, willing to come, and to overcome him by fighting. ^v34

35 But he shall stand upon the top of the mount Sion. ^v35

36 And Sion shall come, and shall be shewed to all men, being prepared and builded, like as thou sawest the hill graven without hands. ^v36

37 And this my Son shall rebuke the wicked inventions of those nations, which for their wicked life are fallen into the tempest; ^v37

38 And shall lay before them their evil thoughts, and the torments wherewith they shall begin to be tormented, which are like unto a flame: and he shall destroy them without labour by the law which is like unto me. ^v38

39 And whereas thou sawest that he gathered another peaceable multitude unto him; ^v39

40 Those are the ten tribes, which were carried away prisoners out of their own land in the time of Osea the king, whom Salmanasar the king of Assyria led away captive, and he carried them over the waters, and so came they into another land. ^v40

41 But they took this counsel among themselves, that they would leave the multitude of the heathen, and go forth into a further country, where never mankind dwelt, ^v41

42 That they might there keep their statutes, which they never kept in their own land. ^v42

43 And they entered into Euphrates by the narrow places of the river. ^v43

44 For the most High then shewed signs for them, and held still the flood, till they were passed over. ^v44

45 For through that country there was a great way to go, namely, of a year and a half: and the same region is called Arsareth. ^v45

46 Then dwelt they there until the latter time; and now when they shall begin to come, ^v46

47 The Highest shall stay the springs of the stream again, that they may go through: therefore sawest thou the multitude with peace. ^v47

48 But those that be left behind of thy people are they that are found within my borders. ^v48

49 Now when he destroyeth the multitude of the nations that are gathered together, he shall defend his people that remain. ^v49

50 And then shall he shew them great wonders. ^v50

51 Then said I, O Lord that bearest rule, shew me this: Wherefore have I seen the man coming up from the midst of the sea? ^v51

52 And he said unto me, Like as thou canst neither seek out nor know the things that are in the deep of the sea: even so can no man upon earth see my Son, or those that be with him, but in the day time. ^v52

53 This is the interpretation of the dream which thou sawest, and whereby thou only art here lightened. ^v53

54 For thou hast forsaken thine own way, and applied thy diligence unto my law, and sought it. ^v54

55 Thy life hast thou ordered in wisdom, and hast called understanding thy mother. ^v55

56 And therefore have I shewed thee the treasures of the Highest: after other three days I will speak other things unto thee, and declare unto thee mighty and wondrous things. ^v56

57 Then went I forth into the field, giving praise and thanks greatly unto the most High because of his wonders which he did in time; ^v57

58 And because he governeth the same, and such things as fall in their seasons: and there I sat three days. ^v58
