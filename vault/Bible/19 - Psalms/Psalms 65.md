# Psalms 65

1 Praise waiteth for thee, O God, in Sion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed. ^v1

2 O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come. ^v2

3 Iniquities prevail against me: as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away. ^v3

4 Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple. ^v4

5 By terrible things in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation; who art the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are afar off upon the sea: ^v5

6 Which by his strength setteth fast the mountains; being girded with power: ^v6

7 Which stilleth the noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people. ^v7

8 They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid at thy tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice. ^v8

9 Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou greatly enrichest it with the river of God, which is full of water: thou preparest them corn, when thou hast so provided for it. ^v9

10 Thou waterest the ridges thereof abundantly: thou settlest the furrows thereof: thou makest it soft with showers: thou blessest the springing thereof. ^v10

11 Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness. ^v11

12 They drop upon the pastures of the wilderness: and the little hills rejoice on every side. ^v12

13 The pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing. ^v13
