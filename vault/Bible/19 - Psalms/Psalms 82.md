# Psalms 82

1 God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods. ^v1

2 How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the persons of the wicked? Selah. ^v2

3 Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy. ^v3

4 Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked. ^v4

5 They know not, neither will they understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth are out of course. ^v5

6 I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High. ^v6

7 But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes. ^v7

8 Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations. ^v8
