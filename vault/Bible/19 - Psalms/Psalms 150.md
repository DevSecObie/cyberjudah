# Psalms 150

1 Praise ye the Lord. Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the firmament of his power. ^v1

2 Praise him for his mighty acts: praise him according to his excellent greatness. ^v2

3 Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp. ^v3

4 Praise him with the timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs. ^v4

5 Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high sounding cymbals. ^v5

6 Let every thing that hath breath praise the Lord. Praise ye the Lord. ^v6
