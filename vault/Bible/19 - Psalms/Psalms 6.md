# Psalms 6

1 O Lord, rebuke me not in thine anger, neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure. ^v1

2 Have mercy upon me, O Lord; for I am weak: O Lord, heal me; for my bones are vexed. ^v2

3 My soul is also sore vexed: but thou, O Lord, how long? ^v3

4 Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: oh save me for thy mercies’ sake. ^v4

5 For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks? ^v5

6 I am weary with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears. ^v6

7 Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies. ^v7

8 Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping. ^v8

9 The Lord hath heard my supplication; the Lord will receive my prayer. ^v9

10 Let all mine enemies be ashamed and sore vexed: let them return and be ashamed suddenly. ^v10
