# Psalms 114

1 When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language; ^v1

2 Judah was his sanctuary, and Israel his dominion. ^v2

3 The sea saw it, and fled: Jordan was driven back. ^v3

4 The mountains skipped like rams, and the little hills like lambs. ^v4

5 What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou wast driven back? ^v5

6 Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams; and ye little hills, like lambs? ^v6

7 Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob; ^v7

8 Which turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters. ^v8
