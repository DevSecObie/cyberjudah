# Psalms 86

1 Bow down thine ear, O Lord, hear me: for I am poor and needy. ^v1

2 Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee. ^v2

3 Be merciful unto me, O Lord: for I cry unto thee daily. ^v3

4 Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul. ^v4

5 For thou, Lord, art good, and ready to forgive; and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon thee. ^v5

6 Give ear, O Lord, unto my prayer; and attend to the voice of my supplications. ^v6

7 In the day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me. ^v7

8 Among the gods there is none like unto thee, O Lord; neither are there any works like unto thy works. ^v8

9 All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name. ^v9

10 For thou art great, and doest wondrous things: thou art God alone. ^v10

11 Teach me thy way, O Lord; I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name. ^v11

12 I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with all my heart: and I will glorify thy name for evermore. ^v12

13 For great is thy mercy toward me: and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell. ^v13

14 O God, the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men have sought after my soul; and have not set thee before them. ^v14

15 But thou, O Lord, art a God full of compassion, and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth. ^v15

16 O turn unto me, and have mercy upon me; give thy strength unto thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid. ^v16

17 Shew me a token for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed: because thou, Lord, hast holpen me, and comforted me. ^v17
