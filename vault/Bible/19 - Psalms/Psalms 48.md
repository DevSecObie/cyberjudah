# Psalms 48

1 Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness. ^v1

2 Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King. ^v2

3 God is known in her palaces for a refuge. ^v3

4 For, lo, the kings were assembled, they passed by together. ^v4

5 They saw it, and so they marvelled; they were troubled, and hasted away. ^v5

6 Fear took hold upon them there, and pain, as of a woman in travail. ^v6

7 Thou breakest the ships of Tarshish with an east wind. ^v7

8 As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish it for ever. Selah. ^v8

9 We have thought of thy lovingkindness, O God, in the midst of thy temple. ^v9

10 According to thy name, O God, so is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of righteousness. ^v10

11 Let mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad, because of thy judgments. ^v11

12 Walk about Zion, and go round about her: tell the towers thereof. ^v12

13 Mark ye well her bulwarks, consider her palaces; that ye may tell it to the generation following. ^v13

14 For this God is our God for ever and ever: he will be our guide even unto death. ^v14
