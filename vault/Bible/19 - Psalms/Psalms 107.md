# Psalms 107

1 O give thanks unto the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v1

2 Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy; ^v2

3 And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south. ^v3

4 They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in. ^v4

5 Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them. ^v5

6 Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses. ^v6

7 And he led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation. ^v7

8 Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men! ^v8

9 For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness. ^v9

10 Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron; ^v10

11 Because they rebelled against the words of God, and contemned the counsel of the most High: ^v11

12 Therefore he brought down their heart with labour; they fell down, and there was none to help. ^v12

13 Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he saved them out of their distresses. ^v13

14 He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and brake their bands in sunder. ^v14

15 Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men! ^v15

16 For he hath broken the gates of brass, and cut the bars of iron in sunder. ^v16

17 Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted. ^v17

18 Their soul abhorreth all manner of meat; and they draw near unto the gates of death. ^v18

19 Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble, and he saveth them out of their distresses. ^v19

20 He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions. ^v20

21 Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men! ^v21

22 And let them sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works with rejoicing. ^v22

23 They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters; ^v23

24 These see the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep. ^v24

25 For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up the waves thereof. ^v25

26 They mount up to the heaven, they go down again to the depths: their soul is melted because of trouble. ^v26

27 They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and are at their wits’ end. ^v27

28 Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresses. ^v28

29 He maketh the storm a calm, so that the waves thereof are still. ^v29

30 Then are they glad because they be quiet; so he bringeth them unto their desired haven. ^v30

31 Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men! ^v31

32 Let them exalt him also in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the elders. ^v32

33 He turneth rivers into a wilderness, and the watersprings into dry ground; ^v33

34 A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein. ^v34

35 He turneth the wilderness into a standing water, and dry ground into watersprings. ^v35

36 And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, that they may prepare a city for habitation; ^v36

37 And sow the fields, and plant vineyards, which may yield fruits of increase. ^v37

38 He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied greatly; and suffereth not their cattle to decrease. ^v38

39 Again, they are minished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow. ^v39

40 He poureth contempt upon princes, and causeth them to wander in the wilderness, where there is no way. ^v40

41 Yet setteth he the poor on high from affliction, and maketh him families like a flock. ^v41

42 The righteous shall see it, and rejoice: and all iniquity shall stop her mouth. ^v42

43 Whoso is wise, and will observe these things, even they shall understand the lovingkindness of the Lord. ^v43
