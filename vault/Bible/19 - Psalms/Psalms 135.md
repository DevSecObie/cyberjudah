# Psalms 135

1 Praise ye the Lord. Praise ye the name of the Lord; praise him, O ye servants of the Lord. ^v1

2 Ye that stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God, ^v2

3 Praise the Lord; for the Lord is good: sing praises unto his name; for it is pleasant. ^v3

4 For the Lord hath chosen Jacob unto himself, and Israel for his peculiar treasure. ^v4

5 For I know that the Lord is great, and that our Lord is above all gods. ^v5

6 Whatsoever the Lord pleased, that did he in heaven, and in earth, in the seas, and all deep places. ^v6

7 He causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth; he maketh lightnings for the rain; he bringeth the wind out of his treasuries. ^v7

8 Who smote the firstborn of Egypt, both of man and beast. ^v8

9 Who sent tokens and wonders into the midst of thee, O Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants. ^v9

10 Who smote great nations, and slew mighty kings; ^v10

11 Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan: ^v11

12 And gave their land for an heritage, an heritage unto Israel his people. ^v12

13 Thy name, O Lord, endureth for ever; and thy memorial, O Lord, throughout all generations. ^v13

14 For the Lord will judge his people, and he will repent himself concerning his servants. ^v14

15 The idols of the heathen are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands. ^v15

16 They have mouths, but they speak not; eyes have they, but they see not; ^v16

17 They have ears, but they hear not; neither is there any breath in their mouths. ^v17

18 They that make them are like unto them: so is every one that trusteth in them. ^v18

19 Bless the Lord, O house of Israel: bless the Lord, O house of Aaron: ^v19

20 Bless the Lord, O house of Levi: ye that fear the Lord, bless the Lord. ^v20

21 Blessed be the Lord out of Zion, which dwelleth at Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord. ^v21
