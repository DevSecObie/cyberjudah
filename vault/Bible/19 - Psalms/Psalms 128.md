# Psalms 128

1 Blessed is every one that feareth the Lord; that walketh in his ways. ^v1

2 For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee. ^v2

3 Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine by the sides of thine house: thy children like olive plants round about thy table. ^v3

4 Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord. ^v4

5 The Lord shall bless thee out of Zion: and thou shalt see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life. ^v5

6 Yea, thou shalt see thy children’s children, and peace upon Israel. ^v6
