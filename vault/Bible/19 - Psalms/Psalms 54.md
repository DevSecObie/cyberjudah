# Psalms 54

1 Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me by thy strength. ^v1

2 Hear my prayer, O God; give ear to the words of my mouth. ^v2

3 For strangers are risen up against me, and oppressors seek after my soul: they have not set God before them. Selah. ^v3

4 Behold, God is mine helper: the Lord is with them that uphold my soul. ^v4

5 He shall reward evil unto mine enemies: cut them off in thy truth. ^v5

6 I will freely sacrifice unto thee: I will praise thy name, O Lord; for it is good. ^v6

7 For he hath delivered me out of all trouble: and mine eye hath seen his desire upon mine enemies. ^v7
