# Psalms 149

1 Praise ye the Lord. Sing unto the Lord a new song, and his praise in the congregation of saints. ^v1

2 Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: let the children of Zion be joyful in their King. ^v2

3 Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises unto him with the timbrel and harp. ^v3

4 For the Lord taketh pleasure in his people: he will beautify the meek with salvation. ^v4

5 Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds. ^v5

6 Let the high praises of God be in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand; ^v6

7 To execute vengeance upon the heathen, and punishments upon the people; ^v7

8 To bind their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron; ^v8

9 To execute upon them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints. Praise ye the Lord. ^v9
