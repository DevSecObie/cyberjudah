# Psalms 37

1 Fret not thyself because of evildoers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity. ^v1

2 For they shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither as the green herb. ^v2

3 Trust in the Lord, and do good; so shalt thou dwell in the land, and verily thou shalt be fed. ^v3

4 Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart. ^v4

5 Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass. ^v5

6 And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday. ^v6

7 Rest in the Lord, and wait patiently for him: fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass. ^v7

8 Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself in any wise to do evil. ^v8

9 For evildoers shall be cut off: but those that wait upon the Lord, they shall inherit the earth. ^v9

10 For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be. ^v10

11 But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace. ^v11

12 The wicked plotteth against the just, and gnasheth upon him with his teeth. ^v12

13 The Lord shall laugh at him: for he seeth that his day is coming. ^v13

14 The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, and to slay such as be of upright conversation. ^v14

15 Their sword shall enter into their own heart, and their bows shall be broken. ^v15

16 A little that a righteous man hath is better than the riches of many wicked. ^v16

17 For the arms of the wicked shall be broken: but the Lord upholdeth the righteous. ^v17

18 The Lord knoweth the days of the upright: and their inheritance shall be for ever. ^v18

19 They shall not be ashamed in the evil time: and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied. ^v19

20 But the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the Lord shall be as the fat of lambs: they shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away. ^v20

21 The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth. ^v21

22 For such as be blessed of him shall inherit the earth; and they that be cursed of him shall be cut off. ^v22

23 The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way. ^v23

24 Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the Lord upholdeth him with his hand. ^v24

25 I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread. ^v25

26 He is ever merciful, and lendeth; and his seed is blessed. ^v26

27 Depart from evil, and do good; and dwell for evermore. ^v27

28 For the Lord loveth judgment, and forsaketh not his saints; they are preserved for ever: but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off. ^v28

29 The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever. ^v29

30 The mouth of the righteous speaketh wisdom, and his tongue talketh of judgment. ^v30

31 The law of his God is in his heart; none of his steps shall slide. ^v31

32 The wicked watcheth the righteous, and seeketh to slay him. ^v32

33 The Lord will not leave him in his hand, nor condemn him when he is judged. ^v33

34 Wait on the Lord, and keep his way, and he shall exalt thee to inherit the land: when the wicked are cut off, thou shalt see it. ^v34

35 I have seen the wicked in great power, and spreading himself like a green bay tree. ^v35

36 Yet he passed away, and, lo, he was not: yea, I sought him, but he could not be found. ^v36

37 Mark the perfect man, and behold the upright: for the end of that man is peace. ^v37

38 But the transgressors shall be destroyed together: the end of the wicked shall be cut off. ^v38

39 But the salvation of the righteous is of the Lord: he is their strength in the time of trouble. ^v39

40 And the Lord shall help them, and deliver them: he shall deliver them from the wicked, and save them, because they trust in him. ^v40
