# Psalms 95

1 O come, let us sing unto the Lord: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation. ^v1

2 Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms. ^v2

3 For the Lord is a great God, and a great King above all gods. ^v3

4 In his hand are the deep places of the earth: the strength of the hills is his also. ^v4

5 The sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land. ^v5

6 O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the Lord our maker. ^v6

7 For he is our God; and we are the people of his pasture, and the sheep of his hand. To day if ye will hear his voice, ^v7

8 Harden not your heart, as in the provocation, and as in the day of temptation in the wilderness: ^v8

9 When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my work. ^v9

10 Forty years long was I grieved with this generation, and said, It is a people that do err in their heart, and they have not known my ways: ^v10

11 Unto whom I sware in my wrath that they should not enter into my rest. ^v11
