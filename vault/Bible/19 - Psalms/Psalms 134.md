# Psalms 134

1 Behold, bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord, which by night stand in the house of the Lord. ^v1

2 Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the Lord. ^v2

3 The Lord that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion. ^v3
