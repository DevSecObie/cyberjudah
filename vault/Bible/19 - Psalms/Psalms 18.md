# Psalms 18

1 I will love thee, O Lord, my strength. ^v1

2 The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower. ^v2

3 I will call upon the Lord, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies. ^v3

4 The sorrows of death compassed me, and the floods of ungodly men made me afraid. ^v4

5 The sorrows of hell compassed me about: the snares of death prevented me. ^v5

6 In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried unto my God: he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears. ^v6

7 Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were shaken, because he was wroth. ^v7

8 There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it. ^v8

9 He bowed the heavens also, and came down: and darkness was under his feet. ^v9

10 And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly: yea, he did fly upon the wings of the wind. ^v10

11 He made darkness his secret place; his pavilion round about him were dark waters and thick clouds of the skies. ^v11

12 At the brightness that was before him his thick clouds passed, hail stones and coals of fire. ^v12

13 The Lord also thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice; hail stones and coals of fire. ^v13

14 Yea, he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them. ^v14

15 Then the channels of waters were seen, and the foundations of the world were discovered at thy rebuke, O Lord, at the blast of the breath of thy nostrils. ^v15

16 He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of many waters. ^v16

17 He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them which hated me: for they were too strong for me. ^v17

18 They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the Lord was my stay. ^v18

19 He brought me forth also into a large place; he delivered me, because he delighted in me. ^v19

20 The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me. ^v20

21 For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God. ^v21

22 For all his judgments were before me, and I did not put away his statutes from me. ^v22

23 I was also upright before him, and I kept myself from mine iniquity. ^v23

24 Therefore hath the Lord recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his eyesight. ^v24

25 With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful; with an upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright; ^v25

26 With the pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt shew thyself froward. ^v26

27 For thou wilt save the afflicted people; but wilt bring down high looks. ^v27

28 For thou wilt light my candle: the Lord my God will enlighten my darkness. ^v28

29 For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall. ^v29

30 As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the Lord is tried: he is a buckler to all those that trust in him. ^v30

31 For who is God save the Lord? or who is a rock save our God? ^v31

32 It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect. ^v32

33 He maketh my feet like hinds’ feet, and setteth me upon my high places. ^v33

34 He teacheth my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms. ^v34

35 Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath holden me up, and thy gentleness hath made me great. ^v35

36 Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip. ^v36

37 I have pursued mine enemies, and overtaken them: neither did I turn again till they were consumed. ^v37

38 I have wounded them that they were not able to rise: they are fallen under my feet. ^v38

39 For thou hast girded me with strength unto the battle: thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me. ^v39

40 Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies; that I might destroy them that hate me. ^v40

41 They cried, but there was none to save them: even unto the Lord, but he answered them not. ^v41

42 Then did I beat them small as the dust before the wind: I did cast them out as the dirt in the streets. ^v42

43 Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; and thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve me. ^v43

44 As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me: the strangers shall submit themselves unto me. ^v44

45 The strangers shall fade away, and be afraid out of their close places. ^v45

46 The Lord liveth; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted. ^v46

47 It is God that avengeth me, and subdueth the people under me. ^v47

48 He delivereth me from mine enemies: yea, thou liftest me up above those that rise up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man. ^v48

49 Therefore will I give thanks unto thee, O Lord, among the heathen, and sing praises unto thy name. ^v49

50 Great deliverance giveth he to his king; and sheweth mercy to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for evermore. ^v50
