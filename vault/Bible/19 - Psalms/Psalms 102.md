# Psalms 102

1 Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come unto thee. ^v1

2 Hide not thy face from me in the day when I am in trouble; incline thine ear unto me: in the day when I call answer me speedily. ^v2

3 For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burned as an hearth. ^v3

4 My heart is smitten, and withered like grass; so that I forget to eat my bread. ^v4

5 By reason of the voice of my groaning my bones cleave to my skin. ^v5

6 I am like a pelican of the wilderness: I am like an owl of the desert. ^v6

7 I watch, and am as a sparrow alone upon the house top. ^v7

8 Mine enemies reproach me all the day; and they that are mad against me are sworn against me. ^v8

9 For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping, ^v9

10 Because of thine indignation and thy wrath: for thou hast lifted me up, and cast me down. ^v10

11 My days are like a shadow that declineth; and I am withered like grass. ^v11

12 But thou, O Lord, shalt endure for ever; and thy remembrance unto all generations. ^v12

13 Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Zion: for the time to favour her, yea, the set time, is come. ^v13

14 For thy servants take pleasure in her stones, and favour the dust thereof. ^v14

15 So the heathen shall fear the name of the Lord, and all the kings of the earth thy glory. ^v15

16 When the Lord shall build up Zion, he shall appear in his glory. ^v16

17 He will regard the prayer of the destitute, and not despise their prayer. ^v17

18 This shall be written for the generation to come: and the people which shall be created shall praise the Lord. ^v18

19 For he hath looked down from the height of his sanctuary; from heaven did the Lord behold the earth; ^v19

20 To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death; ^v20

21 To declare the name of the Lord in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem; ^v21

22 When the people are gathered together, and the kingdoms, to serve the Lord. ^v22

23 He weakened my strength in the way; he shortened my days. ^v23

24 I said, O my God, take me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are throughout all generations. ^v24

25 Of old hast thou laid the foundation of the earth: and the heavens are the work of thy hands. ^v25

26 They shall perish, but thou shalt endure: yea, all of them shall wax old like a garment; as a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed: ^v26

27 But thou art the same, and thy years shall have no end. ^v27

28 The children of thy servants shall continue, and their seed shall be established before thee. ^v28
