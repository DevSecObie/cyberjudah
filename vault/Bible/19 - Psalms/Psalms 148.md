# Psalms 148

1 Praise ye the Lord. Praise ye the Lord from the heavens: praise him in the heights. ^v1

2 Praise ye him, all his angels: praise ye him, all his hosts. ^v2

3 Praise ye him, sun and moon: praise him, all ye stars of light. ^v3

4 Praise him, ye heavens of heavens, and ye waters that be above the heavens. ^v4

5 Let them praise the name of the Lord: for he commanded, and they were created. ^v5

6 He hath also stablished them for ever and ever: he hath made a decree which shall not pass. ^v6

7 Praise the Lord from the earth, ye dragons, and all deeps: ^v7

8 Fire, and hail; snow, and vapour; stormy wind fulfilling his word: ^v8

9 Mountains, and all hills; fruitful trees, and all cedars: ^v9

10 Beasts, and all cattle; creeping things, and flying fowl: ^v10

11 Kings of the earth, and all people; princes, and all judges of the earth: ^v11

12 Both young men, and maidens; old men, and children: ^v12

13 Let them praise the name of the Lord: for his name alone is excellent; his glory is above the earth and heaven. ^v13

14 He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord. ^v14
