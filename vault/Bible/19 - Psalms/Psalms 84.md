# Psalms 84

1 How amiable are thy tabernacles, O Lord of hosts! ^v1

2 My soul longeth, yea, even fainteth for the courts of the Lord: my heart and my flesh crieth out for the living God. ^v2

3 Yea, the sparrow hath found an house, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, even thine altars, O Lord of hosts, my King, and my God. ^v3

4 Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: they will be still praising thee. Selah. ^v4

5 Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; in whose heart are the ways of them. ^v5

6 Who passing through the valley of Baca make it a well; the rain also filleth the pools. ^v6

7 They go from strength to strength, every one of them in Zion appeareth before God. ^v7

8 O Lord God of hosts, hear my prayer: give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah. ^v8

9 Behold, O God our shield, and look upon the face of thine anointed. ^v9

10 For a day in thy courts is better than a thousand. I had rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness. ^v10

11 For the Lord God is a sun and shield: the Lord will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly. ^v11

12 O Lord of hosts, blessed is the man that trusteth in thee. ^v12
