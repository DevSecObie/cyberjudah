# Psalms 136

1 O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v1

2 O give thanks unto the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v2

3 O give thanks to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v3

4 To him who alone doeth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v4

5 To him that by wisdom made the heavens: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v5

6 To him that stretched out the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v6

7 To him that made great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v7

8 The sun to rule by day: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v8

9 The moon and stars to rule by night: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v9

10 To him that smote Egypt in their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v10

11 And brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v11

12 With a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v12

13 To him which divided the Red sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v13

14 And made Israel to pass through the midst of it: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v14

15 But overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red sea: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v15

16 To him which led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v16

17 To him which smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v17

18 And slew famous kings: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v18

19 Sihon king of the Amorites: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v19

20 And Og the king of Bashan: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v20

21 And gave their land for an heritage: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v21

22 Even an heritage unto Israel his servant: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v22

23 Who remembered us in our low estate: for his mercy endureth for ever: ^v23

24 And hath redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v24

25 Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v25

26 O give thanks unto the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v26
