# Psalms 36

1 The transgression of the wicked saith within my heart, that there is no fear of God before his eyes. ^v1

2 For he flattereth himself in his own eyes, until his iniquity be found to be hateful. ^v2

3 The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit: he hath left off to be wise, and to do good. ^v3

4 He deviseth mischief upon his bed; he setteth himself in a way that is not good; he abhorreth not evil. ^v4

5 Thy mercy, O Lord, is in the heavens; and thy faithfulness reacheth unto the clouds. ^v5

6 Thy righteousness is like the great mountains; thy judgments are a great deep: O Lord, thou preservest man and beast. ^v6

7 How excellent is thy lovingkindness, O God! therefore the children of men put their trust under the shadow of thy wings. ^v7

8 They shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of thy house; and thou shalt make them drink of the river of thy pleasures. ^v8

9 For with thee is the fountain of life: in thy light shall we see light. ^v9

10 O continue thy lovingkindness unto them that know thee; and thy righteousness to the upright in heart. ^v10

11 Let not the foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of the wicked remove me. ^v11

12 There are the workers of iniquity fallen: they are cast down, and shall not be able to rise. ^v12
