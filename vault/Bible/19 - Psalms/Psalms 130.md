# Psalms 130

1 Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord. ^v1

2 Lord, hear my voice: let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications. ^v2

3 If thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? ^v3

4 But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared. ^v4

5 I wait for the Lord, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope. ^v5

6 My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning. ^v6

7 Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption. ^v7

8 And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities. ^v8
