# Psalms 20

1 The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee; ^v1

2 Send thee help from the sanctuary, and strengthen thee out of Zion; ^v2

3 Remember all thy offerings, and accept thy burnt sacrifice; Selah. ^v3

4 Grant thee according to thine own heart, and fulfil all thy counsel. ^v4

5 We will rejoice in thy salvation, and in the name of our God we will set up our banners: the Lord fulfil all thy petitions. ^v5

6 Now know I that the Lord saveth his anointed; he will hear him from his holy heaven with the saving strength of his right hand. ^v6

7 Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the Lord our God. ^v7

8 They are brought down and fallen: but we are risen, and stand upright. ^v8

9 Save, Lord: let the king hear us when we call. ^v9
