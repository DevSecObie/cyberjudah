# Psalms 121

1 I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help. ^v1

2 My help cometh from the Lord, which made heaven and earth. ^v2

3 He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber. ^v3

4 Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep. ^v4

5 The Lord is thy keeper: the Lord is thy shade upon thy right hand. ^v5

6 The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night. ^v6

7 The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul. ^v7

8 The Lord shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore. ^v8
