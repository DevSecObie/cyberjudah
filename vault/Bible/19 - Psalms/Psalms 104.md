# Psalms 104

1 Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honour and majesty. ^v1

2 Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain: ^v2

3 Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind: ^v3

4 Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire: ^v4

5 Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever. ^v5

6 Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains. ^v6

7 At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away. ^v7

8 They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys unto the place which thou hast founded for them. ^v8

9 Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth. ^v9

10 He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills. ^v10

11 They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst. ^v11

12 By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches. ^v12

13 He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works. ^v13

14 He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth; ^v14

15 And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man’s heart. ^v15

16 The trees of the Lord are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted; ^v16

17 Where the birds make their nests: as for the stork, the fir trees are her house. ^v17

18 The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies. ^v18

19 He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down. ^v19

20 Thou makest darkness, and it is night: wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth. ^v20

21 The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their meat from God. ^v21

22 The sun ariseth, they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens. ^v22

23 Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening. ^v23

24 O Lord, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches. ^v24

25 So is this great and wide sea, wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts. ^v25

26 There go the ships: there is that leviathan, whom thou hast made to play therein. ^v26

27 These wait all upon thee; that thou mayest give them their meat in due season. ^v27

28 That thou givest them they gather: thou openest thine hand, they are filled with good. ^v28

29 Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled: thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust. ^v29

30 Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created: and thou renewest the face of the earth. ^v30

31 The glory of the Lord shall endure for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works. ^v31

32 He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth: he toucheth the hills, and they smoke. ^v32

33 I will sing unto the Lord as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being. ^v33

34 My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the Lord. ^v34

35 Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the Lord, O my soul. Praise ye the Lord. ^v35
