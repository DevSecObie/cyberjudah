# Psalms 28

1 Unto thee will I cry, O Lord my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit. ^v1

2 Hear the voice of my supplications, when I cry unto thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy oracle. ^v2

3 Draw me not away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, which speak peace to their neighbours, but mischief is in their hearts. ^v3

4 Give them according to their deeds, and according to the wickedness of their endeavours: give them after the work of their hands; render to them their desert. ^v4

5 Because they regard not the works of the Lord, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them, and not build them up. ^v5

6 Blessed be the Lord, because he hath heard the voice of my supplications. ^v6

7 The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him. ^v7

8 The Lord is their strength, and he is the saving strength of his anointed. ^v8

9 Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: feed them also, and lift them up for ever. ^v9
