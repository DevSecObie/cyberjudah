# Psalms 26

1 Judge me, O Lord; for I have walked in mine integrity: I have trusted also in the Lord; therefore I shall not slide. ^v1

2 Examine me, O Lord, and prove me; try my reins and my heart. ^v2

3 For thy lovingkindness is before mine eyes: and I have walked in thy truth. ^v3

4 I have not sat with vain persons, neither will I go in with dissemblers. ^v4

5 I have hated the congregation of evil doers; and will not sit with the wicked. ^v5

6 I will wash mine hands in innocency: so will I compass thine altar, O Lord: ^v6

7 That I may publish with the voice of thanksgiving, and tell of all thy wondrous works. ^v7

8 Lord, I have loved the habitation of thy house, and the place where thine honour dwelleth. ^v8

9 Gather not my soul with sinners, nor my life with bloody men: ^v9

10 In whose hands is mischief, and their right hand is full of bribes. ^v10

11 But as for me, I will walk in mine integrity: redeem me, and be merciful unto me. ^v11

12 My foot standeth in an even place: in the congregations will I bless the Lord. ^v12
