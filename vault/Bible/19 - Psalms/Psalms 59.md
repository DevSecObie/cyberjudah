# Psalms 59

1 Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me. ^v1

2 Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men. ^v2

3 For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O Lord. ^v3

4 They run and prepare themselves without my fault: awake to help me, and behold. ^v4

5 Thou therefore, O Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, awake to visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any wicked transgressors. Selah. ^v5

6 They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city. ^v6

7 Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they, doth hear? ^v7

8 But thou, O Lord, shalt laugh at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision. ^v8

9 Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defence. ^v9

10 The God of my mercy shall prevent me: God shall let me see my desire upon mine enemies. ^v10

11 Slay them not, lest my people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord our shield. ^v11

12 For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride: and for cursing and lying which they speak. ^v12

13 Consume them in wrath, consume them, that they may not be: and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. Selah. ^v13

14 And at evening let them return; and let them make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city. ^v14

15 Let them wander up and down for meat, and grudge if they be not satisfied. ^v15

16 But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble. ^v16

17 Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing: for God is my defence, and the God of my mercy. ^v17
