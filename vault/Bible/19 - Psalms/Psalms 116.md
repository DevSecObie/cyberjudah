# Psalms 116

1 I love the Lord, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications. ^v1

2 Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, therefore will I call upon him as long as I live. ^v2

3 The sorrows of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow. ^v3

4 Then called I upon the name of the Lord; O Lord, I beseech thee, deliver my soul. ^v4

5 Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful. ^v5

6 The Lord preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he helped me. ^v6

7 Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord hath dealt bountifully with thee. ^v7

8 For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling. ^v8

9 I will walk before the Lord in the land of the living. ^v9

10 I believed, therefore have I spoken: I was greatly afflicted: ^v10

11 I said in my haste, All men are liars. ^v11

12 What shall I render unto the Lord for all his benefits toward me? ^v12

13 I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord. ^v13

14 I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people. ^v14

15 Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints. ^v15

16 O Lord, truly I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid: thou hast loosed my bonds. ^v16

17 I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call upon the name of the Lord. ^v17

18 I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people, ^v18

19 In the courts of the Lord’s house, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord. ^v19
