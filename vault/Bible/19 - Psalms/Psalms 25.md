# Psalms 25

1 Unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul. ^v1

2 O my God, I trust in thee: let me not be ashamed, let not mine enemies triumph over me. ^v2

3 Yea, let none that wait on thee be ashamed: let them be ashamed which transgress without cause. ^v3

4 Shew me thy ways, O Lord; teach me thy paths. ^v4

5 Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day. ^v5

6 Remember, O Lord, thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses; for they have been ever of old. ^v6

7 Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: according to thy mercy remember thou me for thy goodness’ sake, O Lord. ^v7

8 Good and upright is the Lord: therefore will he teach sinners in the way. ^v8

9 The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way. ^v9

10 All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth unto such as keep his covenant and his testimonies. ^v10

11 For thy name’s sake, O Lord, pardon mine iniquity; for it is great. ^v11

12 What man is he that feareth the Lord? him shall he teach in the way that he shall choose. ^v12

13 His soul shall dwell at ease; and his seed shall inherit the earth. ^v13

14 The secret of the Lord is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his covenant. ^v14

15 Mine eyes are ever toward the Lord; for he shall pluck my feet out of the net. ^v15

16 Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted. ^v16

17 The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses. ^v17

18 Look upon mine affliction and my pain; and forgive all my sins. ^v18

19 Consider mine enemies; for they are many; and they hate me with cruel hatred. ^v19

20 O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I put my trust in thee. ^v20

21 Let integrity and uprightness preserve me; for I wait on thee. ^v21

22 Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles. ^v22
