# Psalms 51

1 Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy lovingkindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions. ^v1

2 Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin. ^v2

3 For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me. ^v3

4 Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest. ^v4

5 Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me. ^v5

6 Behold, thou desirest truth in the inward parts: and in the hidden part thou shalt make me to know wisdom. ^v6

7 Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow. ^v7

8 Make me to hear joy and gladness; that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice. ^v8

9 Hide thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities. ^v9

10 Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me. ^v10

11 Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy holy spirit from me. ^v11

12 Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit. ^v12

13 Then will I teach transgressors thy ways; and sinners shall be converted unto thee. ^v13

14 Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, thou God of my salvation: and my tongue shall sing aloud of thy righteousness. ^v14

15 O Lord, open thou my lips; and my mouth shall shew forth thy praise. ^v15

16 For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou delightest not in burnt offering. ^v16

17 The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise. ^v17

18 Do good in thy good pleasure unto Zion: build thou the walls of Jerusalem. ^v18

19 Then shalt thou be pleased with the sacrifices of righteousness, with burnt offering and whole burnt offering: then shall they offer bullocks upon thine altar. ^v19
