# Psalms 115

1 Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us, but unto thy name give glory, for thy mercy, and for thy truth’s sake. ^v1

2 Wherefore should the heathen say, Where is now their God? ^v2

3 But our God is in the heavens: he hath done whatsoever he hath pleased. ^v3

4 Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands. ^v4

5 They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not: ^v5

6 They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not: ^v6

7 They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat. ^v7

8 They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them. ^v8

9 O Israel, trust thou in the Lord: he is their help and their shield. ^v9

10 O house of Aaron, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield. ^v10

11 Ye that fear the Lord, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield. ^v11

12 The Lord hath been mindful of us: he will bless us; he will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron. ^v12

13 He will bless them that fear the Lord, both small and great. ^v13

14 The Lord shall increase you more and more, you and your children. ^v14

15 Ye are blessed of the Lord which made heaven and earth. ^v15

16 The heaven, even the heavens, are the Lord’s: but the earth hath he given to the children of men. ^v16

17 The dead praise not the Lord, neither any that go down into silence. ^v17

18 But we will bless the Lord from this time forth and for evermore. Praise the Lord. ^v18
