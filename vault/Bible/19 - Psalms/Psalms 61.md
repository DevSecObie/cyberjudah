# Psalms 61

1 Hear my cry, O God; attend unto my prayer. ^v1

2 From the end of the earth will I cry unto thee, when my heart is overwhelmed: lead me to the rock that is higher than I. ^v2

3 For thou hast been a shelter for me, and a strong tower from the enemy. ^v3

4 I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings. Selah. ^v4

5 For thou, O God, hast heard my vows: thou hast given me the heritage of those that fear thy name. ^v5

6 Thou wilt prolong the king’s life: and his years as many generations. ^v6

7 He shall abide before God for ever: O prepare mercy and truth, which may preserve him. ^v7

8 So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows. ^v8
