# 1 Peter 5

1 The elders which are among you I exhort, who am also an elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed: ^v1

2 Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind; ^v2

3 Neither as being lords over God’s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock. ^v3

4 And when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away. ^v4

5 Likewise, ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder. Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble. ^v5

6 Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time: ^v6

7 Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you. ^v7

8 Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour: ^v8

9 Whom resist stedfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world. ^v9

10 But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you. ^v10

11 To him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen. ^v11

12 By Silvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I suppose, I have written briefly, exhorting, and testifying that this is the true grace of God wherein ye stand. ^v12

13 The church that is at Babylon, elected together with you, saluteth you; and so doth Marcus my son. ^v13

14 Greet ye one another with a kiss of charity. Peace be with you all that are in Christ Jesus. Amen. ^v14
