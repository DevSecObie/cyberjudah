# Job 36

1 Elihu also proceeded, and said, ^v1

2 Suffer me a little, and I will shew thee that I have yet to speak on God’s behalf. ^v2

3 I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Maker. ^v3

4 For truly my words shall not be false: he that is perfect in knowledge is with thee. ^v4

5 Behold, God is mighty, and despiseth not any: he is mighty in strength and wisdom. ^v5

6 He preserveth not the life of the wicked: but giveth right to the poor. ^v6

7 He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous: but with kings are they on the throne; yea, he doth establish them for ever, and they are exalted. ^v7

8 And if they be bound in fetters, and be holden in cords of affliction; ^v8

9 Then he sheweth them their work, and their transgressions that they have exceeded. ^v9

10 He openeth also their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity. ^v10

11 If they obey and serve him, they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures. ^v11

12 But if they obey not, they shall perish by the sword, and they shall die without knowledge. ^v12

13 But the hypocrites in heart heap up wrath: they cry not when he bindeth them. ^v13

14 They die in youth, and their life is among the unclean. ^v14

15 He delivereth the poor in his affliction, and openeth their ears in oppression. ^v15

16 Even so would he have removed thee out of the strait into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and that which should be set on thy table should be full of fatness. ^v16

17 But thou hast fulfilled the judgment of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold on thee. ^v17

18 Because there is wrath, beware lest he take thee away with his stroke: then a great ransom cannot deliver thee. ^v18

19 Will he esteem thy riches? no, not gold, nor all the forces of strength. ^v19

20 Desire not the night, when people are cut off in their place. ^v20

21 Take heed, regard not iniquity: for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction. ^v21

22 Behold, God exalteth by his power: who teacheth like him? ^v22

23 Who hath enjoined him his way? or who can say, Thou hast wrought iniquity? ^v23

24 Remember that thou magnify his work, which men behold. ^v24

25 Every man may see it; man may behold it afar off. ^v25

26 Behold, God is great, and we know him not, neither can the number of his years be searched out. ^v26

27 For he maketh small the drops of water: they pour down rain according to the vapour thereof: ^v27

28 Which the clouds do drop and distil upon man abundantly. ^v28

29 Also can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, or the noise of his tabernacle? ^v29

30 Behold, he spreadeth his light upon it, and covereth the bottom of the sea. ^v30

31 For by them judgeth he the people; he giveth meat in abundance. ^v31

32 With clouds he covereth the light; and commandeth it not to shine by the cloud that cometh betwixt. ^v32

33 The noise thereof sheweth concerning it, the cattle also concerning the vapour. ^v33
