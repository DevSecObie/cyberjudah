# Job 40

1 Moreover the Lord answered Job, and said, ^v1

2 Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him? he that reproveth God, let him answer it. ^v2

3 Then Job answered the Lord, and said, ^v3

4 Behold, I am vile; what shall I answer thee? I will lay mine hand upon my mouth. ^v4

5 Once have I spoken; but I will not answer: yea, twice; but I will proceed no further. ^v5

6 Then answered the Lord unto Job out of the whirlwind, and said, ^v6

7 Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me. ^v7

8 Wilt thou also disannul my judgment? wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayest be righteous? ^v8

9 Hast thou an arm like God? or canst thou thunder with a voice like him? ^v9

10 Deck thyself now with majesty and excellency; and array thyself with glory and beauty. ^v10

11 Cast abroad the rage of thy wrath: and behold every one that is proud, and abase him. ^v11

12 Look on every one that is proud, and bring him low; and tread down the wicked in their place. ^v12

13 Hide them in the dust together; and bind their faces in secret. ^v13

14 Then will I also confess unto thee that thine own right hand can save thee. ^v14

15 Behold now behemoth, which I made with thee; he eateth grass as an ox. ^v15

16 Lo now, his strength is in his loins, and his force is in the navel of his belly. ^v16

17 He moveth his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his stones are wrapped together. ^v17

18 His bones are as strong pieces of brass; his bones are like bars of iron. ^v18

19 He is the chief of the ways of God: he that made him can make his sword to approach unto him. ^v19

20 Surely the mountains bring him forth food, where all the beasts of the field play. ^v20

21 He lieth under the shady trees, in the covert of the reed, and fens. ^v21

22 The shady trees cover him with their shadow; the willows of the brook compass him about. ^v22

23 Behold, he drinketh up a river, and hasteth not: he trusteth that he can draw up Jordan into his mouth. ^v23

24 He taketh it with his eyes: his nose pierceth through snares. ^v24
