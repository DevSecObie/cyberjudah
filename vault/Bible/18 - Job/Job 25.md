# Job 25

1 Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said, ^v1

2 Dominion and fear are with him, he maketh peace in his high places. ^v2

3 Is there any number of his armies? and upon whom doth not his light arise? ^v3

4 How then can man be justified with God? or how can he be clean that is born of a woman? ^v4

5 Behold even to the moon, and it shineth not; yea, the stars are not pure in his sight. ^v5

6 How much less man, that is a worm? and the son of man, which is a worm? ^v6
