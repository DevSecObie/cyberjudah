# Job 22

1 Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said, ^v1

2 Can a man be profitable unto God, as he that is wise may be profitable unto himself? ^v2

3 Is it any pleasure to the Almighty, that thou art righteous? or is it gain to him, that thou makest thy ways perfect? ^v3

4 Will he reprove thee for fear of thee? will he enter with thee into judgment? ^v4

5 Is not thy wickedness great? and thine iniquities infinite? ^v5

6 For thou hast taken a pledge from thy brother for nought, and stripped the naked of their clothing. ^v6

7 Thou hast not given water to the weary to drink, and thou hast withholden bread from the hungry. ^v7

8 But as for the mighty man, he had the earth; and the honourable man dwelt in it. ^v8

9 Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken. ^v9

10 Therefore snares are round about thee, and sudden fear troubleth thee; ^v10

11 Or darkness, that thou canst not see; and abundance of waters cover thee. ^v11

12 Is not God in the height of heaven? and behold the height of the stars, how high they are! ^v12

13 And thou sayest, How doth God know? can he judge through the dark cloud? ^v13

14 Thick clouds are a covering to him, that he seeth not; and he walketh in the circuit of heaven. ^v14

15 Hast thou marked the old way which wicked men have trodden? ^v15

16 Which were cut down out of time, whose foundation was overflown with a flood: ^v16

17 Which said unto God, Depart from us: and what can the Almighty do for them? ^v17

18 Yet he filled their houses with good things: but the counsel of the wicked is far from me. ^v18

19 The righteous see it, and are glad: and the innocent laugh them to scorn. ^v19

20 Whereas our substance is not cut down, but the remnant of them the fire consumeth. ^v20

21 Acquaint now thyself with him, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee. ^v21

22 Receive, I pray thee, the law from his mouth, and lay up his words in thine heart. ^v22

23 If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, thou shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacles. ^v23

24 Then shalt thou lay up gold as dust, and the gold of Ophir as the stones of the brooks. ^v24

25 Yea, the Almighty shall be thy defence, and thou shalt have plenty of silver. ^v25

26 For then shalt thou have thy delight in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face unto God. ^v26

27 Thou shalt make thy prayer unto him, and he shall hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows. ^v27

28 Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee: and the light shall shine upon thy ways. ^v28

29 When men are cast down, then thou shalt say, There is lifting up; and he shall save the humble person. ^v29

30 He shall deliver the island of the innocent: and it is delivered by the pureness of thine hands. ^v30
