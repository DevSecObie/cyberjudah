# Job 10

1 My soul is weary of my life; I will leave my complaint upon myself; I will speak in the bitterness of my soul. ^v1

2 I will say unto God, Do not condemn me; shew me wherefore thou contendest with me. ^v2

3 Is it good unto thee that thou shouldest oppress, that thou shouldest despise the work of thine hands, and shine upon the counsel of the wicked? ^v3

4 Hast thou eyes of flesh? or seest thou as man seeth? ^v4

5 Are thy days as the days of man? are thy years as man’s days, ^v5

6 That thou enquirest after mine iniquity, and searchest after my sin? ^v6

7 Thou knowest that I am not wicked; and there is none that can deliver out of thine hand. ^v7

8 Thine hands have made me and fashioned me together round about; yet thou dost destroy me. ^v8

9 Remember, I beseech thee, that thou hast made me as the clay; and wilt thou bring me into dust again? ^v9

10 Hast thou not poured me out as milk, and curdled me like cheese? ^v10

11 Thou hast clothed me with skin and flesh, and hast fenced me with bones and sinews. ^v11

12 Thou hast granted me life and favour, and thy visitation hath preserved my spirit. ^v12

13 And these things hast thou hid in thine heart: I know that this is with thee. ^v13

14 If I sin, then thou markest me, and thou wilt not acquit me from mine iniquity. ^v14

15 If I be wicked, woe unto me; and if I be righteous, yet will I not lift up my head. I am full of confusion; therefore see thou mine affliction; ^v15

16 For it increaseth. Thou huntest me as a fierce lion: and again thou shewest thyself marvellous upon me. ^v16

17 Thou renewest thy witnesses against me, and increasest thine indignation upon me; changes and war are against me. ^v17

18 Wherefore then hast thou brought me forth out of the womb? Oh that I had given up the ghost, and no eye had seen me! ^v18

19 I should have been as though I had not been; I should have been carried from the womb to the grave. ^v19

20 Are not my days few? cease then, and let me alone, that I may take comfort a little, ^v20

21 Before I go whence I shall not return, even to the land of darkness and the shadow of death; ^v21

22 A land of darkness, as darkness itself; and of the shadow of death, without any order, and where the light is as darkness. ^v22
