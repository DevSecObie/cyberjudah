# Job 4

1 Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said, ^v1

2 If we assay to commune with thee, wilt thou be grieved? but who can withhold himself from speaking? ^v2

3 Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands. ^v3

4 Thy words have upholden him that was falling, and thou hast strengthened the feeble knees. ^v4

5 But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled. ^v5

6 Is not this thy fear, thy confidence, thy hope, and the uprightness of thy ways? ^v6

7 Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? or where were the righteous cut off? ^v7

8 Even as I have seen, they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same. ^v8

9 By the blast of God they perish, and by the breath of his nostrils are they consumed. ^v9

10 The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken. ^v10

11 The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the stout lion’s whelps are scattered abroad. ^v11

12 Now a thing was secretly brought to me, and mine ear received a little thereof. ^v12

13 In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men, ^v13

14 Fear came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones to shake. ^v14

15 Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up: ^v15

16 It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof: an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice, saying, ^v16

17 Shall mortal man be more just than God? shall a man be more pure than his maker? ^v17

18 Behold, he put no trust in his servants; and his angels he charged with folly: ^v18

19 How much less in them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, which are crushed before the moth? ^v19

20 They are destroyed from morning to evening: they perish for ever without any regarding it. ^v20

21 Doth not their excellency which is in them go away? they die, even without wisdom. ^v21
