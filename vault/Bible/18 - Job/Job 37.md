# Job 37

1 At this also my heart trembleth, and is moved out of his place. ^v1

2 Hear attentively the noise of his voice, and the sound that goeth out of his mouth. ^v2

3 He directeth it under the whole heaven, and his lightning unto the ends of the earth. ^v3

4 After it a voice roareth: he thundereth with the voice of his excellency; and he will not stay them when his voice is heard. ^v4

5 God thundereth marvellously with his voice; great things doeth he, which we cannot comprehend. ^v5

6 For he saith to the snow, Be thou on the earth; likewise to the small rain, and to the great rain of his strength. ^v6

7 He sealeth up the hand of every man; that all men may know his work. ^v7

8 Then the beasts go into dens, and remain in their places. ^v8

9 Out of the south cometh the whirlwind: and cold out of the north. ^v9

10 By the breath of God frost is given: and the breadth of the waters is straitened. ^v10

11 Also by watering he wearieth the thick cloud: he scattereth his bright cloud: ^v11

12 And it is turned round about by his counsels: that they may do whatsoever he commandeth them upon the face of the world in the earth. ^v12

13 He causeth it to come, whether for correction, or for his land, or for mercy. ^v13

14 Hearken unto this, O Job: stand still, and consider the wondrous works of God. ^v14

15 Dost thou know when God disposed them, and caused the light of his cloud to shine? ^v15

16 Dost thou know the balancings of the clouds, the wondrous works of him which is perfect in knowledge? ^v16

17 How thy garments are warm, when he quieteth the earth by the south wind? ^v17

18 Hast thou with him spread out the sky, which is strong, and as a molten looking glass? ^v18

19 Teach us what we shall say unto him; for we cannot order our speech by reason of darkness. ^v19

20 Shall it be told him that I speak? if a man speak, surely he shall be swallowed up. ^v20

21 And now men see not the bright light which is in the clouds: but the wind passeth, and cleanseth them. ^v21

22 Fair weather cometh out of the north: with God is terrible majesty. ^v22

23 Touching the Almighty, we cannot find him out: he is excellent in power, and in judgment, and in plenty of justice: he will not afflict. ^v23

24 Men do therefore fear him: he respecteth not any that are wise of heart. ^v24
