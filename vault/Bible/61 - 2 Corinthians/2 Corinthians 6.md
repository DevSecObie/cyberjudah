# 2 Corinthians 6

1 We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain. ^v1

2 (For he saith, I have heard thee in a time accepted, and in the day of salvation have I succoured thee: behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.) ^v2

3 Giving no offence in any thing, that the ministry be not blamed: ^v3

4 But in all things approving ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses, ^v4

5 In stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labours, in watchings, in fastings; ^v5

6 By pureness, by knowledge, by longsuffering, by kindness, by the Holy Ghost, by love unfeigned, ^v6

7 By the word of truth, by the power of God, by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left, ^v7

8 By honour and dishonour, by evil report and good report: as deceivers, and yet true; ^v8

9 As unknown, and yet well known; as dying, and, behold, we live; as chastened, and not killed; ^v9

10 As sorrowful, yet alway rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, and yet possessing all things. ^v10

11 O ye Corinthians, our mouth is open unto you, our heart is enlarged. ^v11

12 Ye are not straitened in us, but ye are straitened in your own bowels. ^v12

13 Now for a recompence in the same, (I speak as unto my children,) be ye also enlarged. ^v13

14 Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? and what communion hath light with darkness? ^v14

15 And what concord hath Christ with Belial? or what part hath he that believeth with an infidel? ^v15

16 And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? for ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said, I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people. ^v16

17 Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you, ^v17

18 And will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty. ^v18
