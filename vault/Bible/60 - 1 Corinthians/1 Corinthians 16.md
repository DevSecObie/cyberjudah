# 1 Corinthians 16

1 Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I have given order to the churches of Galatia, even so do ye. ^v1

2 Upon the first day of the week let every one of you lay by him in store, as God hath prospered him, that there be no gatherings when I come. ^v2

3 And when I come, whomsoever ye shall approve by your letters, them will I send to bring your liberality unto Jerusalem. ^v3

4 And if it be meet that I go also, they shall go with me. ^v4

5 Now I will come unto you, when I shall pass through Macedonia: for I do pass through Macedonia. ^v5

6 And it may be that I will abide, yea, and winter with you, that ye may bring me on my journey whithersoever I go. ^v6

7 For I will not see you now by the way; but I trust to tarry a while with you, if the Lord permit. ^v7

8 But I will tarry at Ephesus until Pentecost. ^v8

9 For a great door and effectual is opened unto me, and there are many adversaries. ^v9

10 Now if Timotheus come, see that he may be with you without fear: for he worketh the work of the Lord, as I also do. ^v10

11 Let no man therefore despise him: but conduct him forth in peace, that he may come unto me: for I look for him with the brethren. ^v11

12 As touching our brother Apollos, I greatly desired him to come unto you with the brethren: but his will was not at all to come at this time; but he will come when he shall have convenient time. ^v12

13 Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong. ^v13

14 Let all your things be done with charity. ^v14

15 I beseech you, brethren, (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints,) ^v15

16 That ye submit yourselves unto such, and to every one that helpeth with us, and laboureth. ^v16

17 I am glad of the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus: for that which was lacking on your part they have supplied. ^v17

18 For they have refreshed my spirit and yours: therefore acknowledge ye them that are such. ^v18

19 The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Priscilla salute you much in the Lord, with the church that is in their house. ^v19

20 All the brethren greet you. Greet ye one another with an holy kiss. ^v20

21 The salutation of me Paul with mine own hand. ^v21

22 If any man love not the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be Anathema Maran-atha. ^v22

23 The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. ^v23

24 My love be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen.  The first epistle to the Corinthians was written from Philippi by Stephanas, and Fortunatus, and Achaicus, and Timotheus. ^v24
