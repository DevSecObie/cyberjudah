# Prayer of Azariah 1

1 And they walked in the midst of the fire, praising God, and blessing the Lord. ^v1

2 Then Azarias stood up, and prayed on this manner; and opening his mouth in the midst of the fire said, ^v2

3 Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: thy name is worthy to be praised and glorified for evermore: ^v3

4 For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done to us: yea, true are all thy works, thy ways are right, and all thy judgments truth. ^v4

5 In all the things that thou hast brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, even Jerusalem, thou hast executed true judgment: for according to truth and judgment didst thou bring all these things upon us because of our sins. ^v5

6 For we have sinned and committed iniquity, departing from thee. ^v6

7 In all things have we trespassed, and not obeyed thy commandments, nor kept them, neither done as thou hast commanded us, that it might go well with us. ^v7

8 Wherefore all that thou hast brought upon us, and every thing that thou hast done to us, thou hast done in true judgment. ^v8

9 And thou didst deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies, most hateful forsakers of God, and to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world. ^v9

10 And now we cannot open our mouths, we are become a shame and reproach to thy servants; and to them that worship thee. ^v10

11 Yet deliver us not up wholly, for thy name’s sake, neither disannul thou thy covenant: ^v11

12 And cause not thy mercy to depart from us, for thy beloved Abraham’s sake, for thy servant Issac’s sake, and for thy holy Israel’s sake; ^v12

13 To whom thou hast spoken and promised, that thou wouldest multiply their seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that lieth upon the seashore. ^v13

14 For we, O Lord, are become less than any nation, and be kept under this day in all the world because of our sins. ^v14

15 Neither is there at this time prince, or prophet, or leader, or burnt offering, or sacrifice, or oblation, or incense, or place to sacrifice before thee, and to find mercy. ^v15

16 Nevertheless in a contrite heart and an humble spirit let us be accepted. ^v16

17 Like as in the burnt offerings of rams and bullocks, and like as in ten thousands of fat lambs: so let our sacrifice be in thy sight this day, and grant that we may wholly go after thee: for they shall not be confounded that put their trust in thee. ^v17

18 And now we follow thee with all our heart, we fear thee, and seek thy face. ^v18

19 Put us not to shame: but deal with us after thy lovingkindness, and according to the multitude of thy mercies. ^v19

20 Deliver us also according to thy marvellous works, and give glory to thy name, O Lord: and let all them that do thy servants hurt be ashamed; ^v20

21 And let them be confounded in all their power and might, and let their strength be broken; ^v21

22 And let them know that thou art God, the only God, and glorious over the whole world. ^v22

23 And the king’s servants, that put them in, ceased not to make the oven hot with rosin, pitch, tow, and small wood; ^v23

24 So that the flame streamed forth above the furnace forty and nine cubits. ^v24

25 And it passed through, and burned those Chaldeans it found about the furnace. ^v25

26 But the angel of the Lord came down into the oven together with Azarias and his fellows, and smote the flame of the fire out of the oven; ^v26

27 And made the midst of the furnace as it had been a moist whistling wind, so that the fire touched them not at all, neither hurt nor troubled them. ^v27

28 Then the three, as out of one mouth, praised, glorified, and blessed, God in the furnace, saying, ^v28

29 Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever. ^v29

30 And blessed is thy glorious and holy name: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever. ^v30

31 Blessed art thou in the temple of thine holy glory: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever. ^v31

32 Blessed art thou that beholdest the depths, and sittest upon the cherubims: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever. ^v32

33 Blessed art thou on the glorious throne of thy kingdom: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever. ^v33

34 Blessed art thou in the firmament of heaven: and above all to be praised and glorified for ever. ^v34

35 O all ye works of the Lord, bless ye the Lord : praise and exalt him above all for ever, ^v35

36 O ye heavens, bless ye the Lord : praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v36

37 O ye angels of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v37

38 O all ye waters that be above the heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v38

39 O all ye powers of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v39

40 O ye sun and moon, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v40

41 O ye stars of heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v41

42 O every shower and dew, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v42

43 O all ye winds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever, ^v43

44 O ye fire and heat, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v44

45 O ye winter and summer, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v45

46 O ye dews and storms of snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v46

47 O ye nights and days, bless ye the Lord: bless and exalt him above all for ever. ^v47

48 O ye light and darkness, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v48

49 O ye ice and cold, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v49

50 O ye frost and snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v50

51 O ye lightnings and clouds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v51

52 O let the earth bless the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v52

53 O ye mountains and little hills, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v53

54 O all ye things that grow in the earth, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v54

55 O ye mountains, bless ye the Lord: Praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v55

56 O ye seas and rivers, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v56

57 O ye whales, and all that move in the waters, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v57

58 O all ye fowls of the air, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v58

59 O all ye beasts and cattle, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v59

60 O ye children of men, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v60

61 O Israel, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v61

62 O ye priests of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v62

63 O ye servants of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v63

64 O ye spirits and souls of the righteous, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v64

65 O ye holy and humble men of heart, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever. ^v65

66 O Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever: far he hath delivered us from hell, and saved us from the hand of death, and delivered us out of the midst of the furnace and burning flame: even out of the midst of the fire hath he delivered us. ^v66

67 O give thanks unto the Lord, because he is gracious: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v67

68 O all ye that worship the Lord, bless the God of gods, praise him, and give him thanks: for his mercy endureth for ever. ^v68
