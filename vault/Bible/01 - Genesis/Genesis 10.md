# Genesis 10

1 Now these are the generations of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth: and unto them were sons born after the flood. ^v1

2 The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras. ^v2

3 And the sons of Gomer; Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah. ^v3

4 And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim. ^v4

5 By these were the isles of the Gentiles divided in their lands; every one after his tongue, after their families, in their nations. ^v5

6 And the sons of Ham; Cush, and Mizraim, and Phut, and Canaan. ^v6

7 And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabtah, and Raamah, and Sabtecha: and the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan. ^v7

8 And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be a mighty one in the earth. ^v8

9 He was a mighty hunter before the Lord: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the Lord. ^v9

10 And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar. ^v10

11 Out of that land went forth Asshur, and builded Nineveh, and the city Rehoboth, and Calah, ^v11

12 And Resen between Nineveh and Calah: the same is a great city. ^v12

13 And Mizraim begat Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim, ^v13

14 And Pathrusim, and Casluhim, (out of whom came Philistim,) and Caphtorim. ^v14

15 And Canaan begat Sidon his firstborn, and Heth, ^v15

16 And the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgasite, ^v16

17 And the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite, ^v17

18 And the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite: and afterward were the families of the Canaanites spread abroad. ^v18

19 And the border of the Canaanites was from Sidon, as thou comest to Gerar, unto Gaza; as thou goest, unto Sodom, and Gomorrah, and Admah, and Zeboim, even unto Lasha. ^v19

20 These are the sons of Ham, after their families, after their tongues, in their countries, and in their nations. ^v20

21 Unto Shem also, the father of all the children of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder, even to him were children born. ^v21

22 The children of Shem; Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram. ^v22

23 And the children of Aram; Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Mash. ^v23

24 And Arphaxad begat Salah; and Salah begat Eber. ^v24

25 And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of one was Peleg; for in his days was the earth divided; and his brother’s name was Joktan. ^v25

26 And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah, ^v26

27 And Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah, ^v27

28 And Obal, and Abimael, and Sheba, ^v28

29 And Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were the sons of Joktan. ^v29

30 And their dwelling was from Mesha, as thou goest unto Sephar a mount of the east. ^v30

31 These are the sons of Shem, after their families, after their tongues, in their lands, after their nations. ^v31

32 These are the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, in their nations: and by these were the nations divided in the earth after the flood. ^v32
