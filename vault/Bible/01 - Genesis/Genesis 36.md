# Genesis 36

1 Now these are the generations of Esau, who is Edom. ^v1

2 Esau took his wives of the daughters of Canaan; Adah the daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Aholibamah the daughter of Anah the daughter of Zibeon the Hivite; ^v2

3 And Bashemath Ishmael’s daughter, sister of Nebajoth. ^v3

4 And Adah bare to Esau Eliphaz; and Bashemath bare Reuel; ^v4

5 And Aholibamah bare Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah: these are the sons of Esau, which were born unto him in the land of Canaan. ^v5

6 And Esau took his wives, and his sons, and his daughters, and all the persons of his house, and his cattle, and all his beasts, and all his substance, which he had got in the land of Canaan; and went into the country from the face of his brother Jacob. ^v6

7 For their riches were more than that they might dwell together; and the land wherein they were strangers could not bear them because of their cattle. ^v7

8 Thus dwelt Esau in mount Seir: Esau is Edom. ^v8

9 And these are the generations of Esau the father of the Edomites in mount Seir: ^v9

10 These are the names of Esau’s sons; Eliphaz the son of Adah the wife of Esau, Reuel the son of Bashemath the wife of Esau. ^v10

11 And the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, and Gatam, and Kenaz. ^v11

12 And Timna was concubine to Eliphaz Esau’s son; and she bare to Eliphaz Amalek: these were the sons of Adah Esau’s wife. ^v12

13 And these are the sons of Reuel; Nahath, and Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah: these were the sons of Bashemath Esau’s wife. ^v13

14 And these were the sons of Aholibamah, the daughter of Anah the daughter of Zibeon, Esau’s wife: and she bare to Esau Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah. ^v14

15 These were dukes of the sons of Esau: the sons of Eliphaz the firstborn son of Esau; duke Teman, duke Omar, duke Zepho, duke Kenaz, ^v15

16 Duke Korah, duke Gatam, and duke Amalek: these are the dukes that came of Eliphaz in the land of Edom; these were the sons of Adah. ^v16

17 And these are the sons of Reuel Esau’s son; duke Nahath, duke Zerah, duke Shammah, duke Mizzah: these are the dukes that came of Reuel in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Bashemath Esau’s wife. ^v17

18 And these are the sons of Aholibamah Esau’s wife; duke Jeush, duke Jaalam, duke Korah: these were the dukes that came of Aholibamah the daughter of Anah, Esau’s wife. ^v18

19 These are the sons of Esau, who is Edom, and these are their dukes. ^v19

20 These are the sons of Seir the Horite, who inhabited the land; Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, ^v20

21 And Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan: these are the dukes of the Horites, the children of Seir in the land of Edom. ^v21

22 And the children of Lotan were Hori and Hemam; and Lotan’s sister was Timna. ^v22

23 And the children of Shobal were these; Alvan, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shepho, and Onam. ^v23

24 And these are the children of Zibeon; both Ajah, and Anah: this was that Anah that found the mules in the wilderness, as he fed the asses of Zibeon his father. ^v24

25 And the children of Anah were these; Dishon, and Aholibamah the daughter of Anah. ^v25

26 And these are the children of Dishon; Hemdan, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran. ^v26

27 The children of Ezer are these; Bilhan, and Zaavan, and Akan. ^v27

28 The children of Dishan are these; Uz, and Aran. ^v28

29 These are the dukes that came of the Horites; duke Lotan, duke Shobal, duke Zibeon, duke Anah, ^v29

30 Duke Dishon, duke Ezer, duke Dishan: these are the dukes that came of Hori, among their dukes in the land of Seir. ^v30

31 And these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom, before there reigned any king over the children of Israel. ^v31

32 And Bela the son of Beor reigned in Edom: and the name of his city was Dinhabah. ^v32

33 And Bela died, and Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead. ^v33

34 And Jobab died, and Husham of the land of Temani reigned in his stead. ^v34

35 And Husham died, and Hadad the son of Bedad, who smote Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Avith. ^v35

36 And Hadad died, and Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead. ^v36

37 And Samlah died, and Saul of Rehoboth by the river reigned in his stead. ^v37

38 And Saul died, and Baal–hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead. ^v38

39 And Baal–hanan the son of Achbor died, and Hadar reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Pau; and his wife’s name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab. ^v39

40 And these are the names of the dukes that came of Esau, according to their families, after their places, by their names; duke Timnah, duke Alvah, duke Jetheth, ^v40

41 Duke Aholibamah, duke Elah, duke Pinon, ^v41

42 Duke Kenaz, duke Teman, duke Mibzar, ^v42

43 Duke Magdiel, duke Iram: these be the dukes of Edom, according to their habitations in the land of their possession: he is Esau the father of the Edomites. ^v43
