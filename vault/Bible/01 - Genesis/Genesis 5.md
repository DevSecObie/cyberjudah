# Genesis 5

1 This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him; ^v1

2 Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created. ^v2

3 And Adam lived an hundred and thirty years, and begat a son in his own likeness, after his image; and called his name Seth: ^v3

4 And the days of Adam after he had begotten Seth were eight hundred years: and he begat sons and daughters: ^v4

5 And all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years: and he died. ^v5

6 And Seth lived an hundred and five years, and begat Enos: ^v6

7 And Seth lived after he begat Enos eight hundred and seven years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v7

8 And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years: and he died. ^v8

9 And Enos lived ninety years, and begat Cainan: ^v9

10 And Enos lived after he begat Cainan eight hundred and fifteen years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v10

11 And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years: and he died. ^v11

12 And Cainan lived seventy years, and begat Mahalaleel: ^v12

13 And Cainan lived after he begat Mahalaleel eight hundred and forty years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v13

14 And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years: and he died. ^v14

15 And Mahalaleel lived sixty and five years, and begat Jared: ^v15

16 And Mahalaleel lived after he begat Jared eight hundred and thirty years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v16

17 And all the days of Mahalaleel were eight hundred ninety and five years: and he died. ^v17

18 And Jared lived an hundred sixty and two years, and he begat Enoch: ^v18

19 And Jared lived after he begat Enoch eight hundred years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v19

20 And all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty and two years: and he died. ^v20

21 And Enoch lived sixty and five years, and begat Methuselah: ^v21

22 And Enoch walked with God after he begat Methuselah three hundred years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v22

23 And all the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty and five years: ^v23

24 And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him. ^v24

25 And Methuselah lived an hundred eighty and seven years, and begat Lamech: ^v25

26 And Methuselah lived after he begat Lamech seven hundred eighty and two years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v26

27 And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty and nine years: and he died. ^v27

28 And Lamech lived an hundred eighty and two years, and begat a son: ^v28

29 And he called his name Noah, saying, This same shall comfort us concerning our work and toil of our hands, because of the ground which the Lord hath cursed. ^v29

30 And Lamech lived after he begat Noah five hundred ninety and five years, and begat sons and daughters: ^v30

31 And all the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy and seven years: and he died. ^v31

32 And Noah was five hundred years old: and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth. ^v32
