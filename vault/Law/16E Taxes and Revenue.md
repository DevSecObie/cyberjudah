# 16E · Taxes and Revenue

[[Part 16 Economic and monetary laws|Part 16 · Economic and monetary laws]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 16E.1
Taxes are paid for the support of godly governments.

Scripture: [[Romans 13#^v6|Romans 13:6]]

#### 16E.2
A poll or head tax can be levied (which is to be of a small amount) according to a census taken on persons of a certain age.

Scripture: [[Exodus 30#^v12|Exodus 30:12-15]]; [[Leviticus 27#^v3|Leviticus 27:3-8]]; [[Numbers 3#^v40|Numbers 3:40-51]]

#### 16E.3
All men subject to a poll tax are to pay the same amount (the rich shall not pay more, and the poor less).

Scripture: [[Exodus 30#^v13|Exodus 30:13-15]]

#### 16E.4
Every man shall give as he is able, according to the blessings God has given him (advalorern tax).

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 16#^v17|Deuteronomy 16:17]]; [[1 Corinthians 16#^v1|1 Corinthians 16:1-2]]

#### 16E.5
Abusive and heavy taxation leads to rebellion by the people.

Scripture: [[1 Kings 12#^v13|1 Kings 12:13-19]]; [[2 Chronicles 10#^v13|2 Chronicles 10:13-19]]

#### 16E.6
Abusive and heavy taxation causes an impoverished nation and burdensome debts.

Scripture: [[Nehemiah 5#^v1|Nehemiah 5:1-5]]

#### 16E.7
Oppressive taxation is often due to the sins of the nation.

Scripture: [[Nehemiah 9#^v32|Nehemiah 9:32-37]]

#### 16E.8
Tithes and offerings collected as taxes are to be put into the treasury.

Scripture: [[Nehemiah 10#^v38|Nehemiah 10:38]]; [[Nehemiah 13#^v12|Nehemiah 13:12-13]]

#### 16E.9
It is unlawful to impose a toll, tribute, or custom tax upon any priest or ministerial aide.

Scripture: [[Ezra 7#^v24|Ezra 7:24]]

#### 16E.10
A tarriff or excise tax levied on the traffic of imported merchandise.

Scripture: [[1 Kings 10#^v15|1 Kings 10:15,28-29]]
