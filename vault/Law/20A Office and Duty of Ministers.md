# 20A · Office and Duty of Ministers

[[Part 20 Churches & ministers|Part 20 · Churches & ministers]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 20A.1
To possess the written law of God.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 31#^v9|Deuteronomy 31:9]]

#### 20A.2
Priests are to keep God's law and statutes in all of his assemblies.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 44#^v24|Ezekiel 44:24]]

#### 20A.3
To perform service at the alter in the sanctuary.

Scripture: [[Numbers 18#^v7|Numbers 18:7]]

#### 20A.4
Priests are to hallow God's Sabbaths.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 44#^v24|Ezekiel 44:24]]

#### 20A.5
The proper vestures to be worn by a priest.

Scripture: [[Exodus 28#^v39|Exodus 28:39-43]]; [[Leviticus 6#^v10|Leviticus 6:10-11]]; [[Leviticus 16#^v4|Leviticus 16:4,23-24]]; [[Ezekiel 44#^v17|Ezekiel 44:17-19]]

#### 20A.6
Priests are not to drink wine or intoxicating drink before entering into the tabernacle or inner court of the sanctuary to minister.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 10#^v9|Leviticus 10:9]]; [[Ezekiel 44#^v21|Ezekiel 44:21]]

#### 20A.7
A priest is to teach the people the difference between holy and profane and to discern between unclean and clean.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 10#^v10|Leviticus 10:10]]; [[Ezekiel 44#^v23|Ezekiel 44:23]]; [[1 Timothy 3#^v2|1 Timothy 3:2]]; [[2 Timothy 1#^v11|2 Timothy 1:11]]

#### 20A.8
New priests are to be anointed, consecrated and sanctified.

Scripture: [[Exodus 29#^v33|Exodus 29:33,35,37,44]]; [[Leviticus 8#^v12|Leviticus 8:12,33]]; [[Leviticus 21#^v8|Leviticus 21:8]]

#### 20A.9
Ministers are to teach the people all the laws and statutes of God.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 10#^v11|Leviticus 10:11]]; [[2 Chronicles 17#^v8|2 Chronicles 17:8-9]]; [[Nehemiah 8#^v1|Nehemiah 8:1-2]]; [[Matthew 28#^v20|Matthew 28:20]]

#### 20A.10
Priests are to bless the people.

Scripture: [[Numbers 6#^v22|Numbers 6:22-27]]

#### 20A.11
In a controversy between those of the church the priests shall stand in judgment, and shall judge it according to God's judgment.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 44#^v24|Ezekiel 44:24]]

#### 20A.12
Priests are not to teach or instruct for hire or for a price.

Scripture: [[Micah 3#^v10|Micah 3:10-11]]

#### 20A.13
Pastors, as messengers of God, are to feed the people with knowledge and understanding.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 3#^v15|Jeremiah 3:15]]; [[Malachi 2#^v7|Malachi 2:7]]

#### 20A.14
If anyone ministers, let him do it as with the ability which God supplies.

Scripture: [[1 Peter 4#^v10|1 Peter 4:10-11]]

#### 20A.15
Priests are to keep the charge of the sanctuary and tabernacle of the congregation and keep it supplied and in proper order.

Scripture: [[Exodus 27#^v21|Exodus 27:21]]; [[Leviticus 24#^v3|Leviticus 24:3-8]]; [[Numbers 3#^v38|Numbers 3:38]]; [[Numbers 18#^v5|Numbers 18:5]]

#### 20A.16
Priests are to wash themselves with water before they go into the tabernacle of the congregation or come to the alter to minister.

Scripture: [[Exodus 30#^v18|Exodus 30:18-21]]; [[Leviticus 16#^v4|Leviticus 16:4,24]]

#### 20A.17
Ministers should be preachers of righteousness.

Scripture: [[2 Peter 2#^v5|2 Peter 2:5]]

#### 20A.18
Ministers should give thanks to God for their appointment to the ministry.

Scripture: [[1 Timothy 1#^v12|1 Timothy 1:12]]

#### 20A.19
A priest shall not go near the holy things which have been sanctified or dedicated to the LORD while he has uncleanness upon him.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 22#^v3|Leviticus 22:3-8]]
