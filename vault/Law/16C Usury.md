# 16C · Usury

[[Part 16 Economic and monetary laws|Part 16 · Economic and monetary laws]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 16C.1
If you lend to any of your brethren in need, you are not to take or lay usury upon them.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v25|Exodus 22:25]]

#### 16C.2
No substance loaned or borrowed is to be repaid with increase or interest-no usury of money, or food, or of anything that is borrowed.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v37|Leviticus 25:37]]; [[Deuteronomy 23#^v19|Deuteronomy 23:19]]

#### 16C.3
You may charge usury to a foreigner.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 23#^v20|Deuteronomy 23:20]]

#### 16C.4
Usury and the usurer are condemned.

Scripture: [[Nehemiah 5#^v7|Nehemiah 5:7,10]]; [[Psalms 15#^v1|Psalms 15:1,5]]; [[Proverbs 28#^v8|Proverbs 28:8]]

#### 16C.5
If one of your brethren becomes poor, you shall exact no usury or interest from him; you shall not lend him your money for usury, nor lend him your food at a profit.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v36|Leviticus 25:36]]

#### 16C.6
Usury is regarded as dishonest profit.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 28#^v8|Proverbs 28:8]]; [[Ezekiel 22#^v12|Ezekiel 22:12-13]]

#### 16C.7
Usury is an iniquity, for the righteous man does not lend money on interest or take increase.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 18#^v8|Ezekiel 18:8-13]]

#### 16C.8
He who does not put out his money at usury may dwell in the LORD's holy nation.

Scripture: [[Psalms 15#^v1|Psalms 15:1,5]]

#### 16C.9
Sons are not to suffer punishment for usury exacted by their fathers.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 18#^v14|Ezekiel 18:14-17]]
