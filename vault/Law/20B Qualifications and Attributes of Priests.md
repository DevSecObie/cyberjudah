# 20B · Qualifications and Attributes of Priests

[[Part 20 Churches & ministers|Part 20 · Churches & ministers]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 20B.1
The office of priesthood is not to be filled by one who has recently become a Christian.

Scripture: [[1 Timothy 3#^v6|1 Timothy 3:6]]

#### 20B.2
Ministers are not to profane the name of their God.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 21#^v6|Leviticus 21:6]]

#### 20B.3
Priests are not to defile or profane themselves.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 21#^v1|Leviticus 21:1-4,15]]

#### 20B.4
Priests are to be males of at least thirty years of age.

Scripture: [[2 Chronicles 31#^v16|2 Chronicles 31:16]]

#### 20B.5
Priests are not to marry a whore, widow, divorced woman or profane woman, but shall marry a virgin of his own people. Lev, 21:7,13-14;

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 44#^v22|Ezekiel 44:22]]

#### 20B.6
A minister must be blameless, vigilant, sober, just, holy, self-control led, of good behavior, given to hospitality, and able to teach and exhort.

Scripture: [[1 Timothy 3#^v2|1 Timothy 3:2]]; [[Titus 1#^v7|Titus 1:7-9]]

#### 20B.7
A minister must not be addicted to alcohol, violent or quick-tempered, greedy for money, quarrelsome, or covetous.

Scripture: [[1 Timothy 3#^v3|1 Timothy 3:3]]; [[Titus 1#^v7|Titus 1:7-8]]

#### 20B.8
Must be of a good reputation outside the church.

Scripture: [[1 Timothy 3#^v7|1 Timothy 3:7]]

#### 20B.9
Priests are not to make themselves bald, shave off the corner of their beard or make any cuttings in their flesh.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 21#^v5|Leviticus 21:5]]

#### 20B.10
Priests are not to let their hair grow long.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 44#^v20|Ezekiel 44:20]]

#### 20B.11
A priest shall not profane his posterity among his people.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 21#^v15|Leviticus 21:15]]

#### 20B.12
A priest shall not eat anything that died of itself or has been torn in pieces.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 44#^v31|Ezekiel 44:31]]

#### 20B.13
Disabilities and deformities not allowed for the priesthood: No blemish, blind man, lame, disfigured face, deformed limb, having a broken foot or hand, a hunchback or dwarf, a blemish in his eye, having eczema, or scabs, or crushed testicles.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 21#^v16|Leviticus 21:16-23]]

#### 20B.14
Priests to be of an unpolluted genealogy.

Scripture: [[Ezra 2#^v61|Ezra 2:61-62]]

#### 20B.15
A stranger (alien) who comes near the priest's office or the tabernacle shall be put to death.

Scripture: [[Numbers 3#^v10|Numbers 3:10,38]]

#### 20B.16
Ministers should not use flattery.

Scripture: [[1 Thessalonians 2#^v5|1 Thessalonians 2:5]]

#### 20B.17
A minister must be one who rules well his own house.

Scripture: [[1 Timothy 3#^v4|1 Timothy 3:4-5]]

#### 20B.18
A priest's daughter that plays the whore is to be burnt with fire.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 21#^v9|Leviticus 21:9]]

#### 20B.19
Pastors should hold fast to sound words.

Scripture: [[2 Timothy 1#^v13|2 Timothy 1:13]]; [[Titus 1#^v9|Titus 1:9]]

#### 20B.20
Ministers should mourn for the release of heathen control and rule over the nation.

Scripture: [[Joel 2#^v17|Joel 2:17]]
