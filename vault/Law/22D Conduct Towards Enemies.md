# 22D · Conduct Towards Enemies

[[Part 22 Warfare and military laws|Part 22 · Warfare and military laws]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 22D.1
When faced with the alternative of battle we are not to be afraid of strong enemies.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v1|Deuteronomy 20:1]]; [[Joshua 11#^v6|Joshua 11:6]]; [[Psalms 27#^v3|Psalms 27:3]]

#### 22D.2
Enemies are to be given an opportunity to surrender in peace before engaging in battle.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v10|Deuteronomy 20:10]]

#### 22D.3
Enemies that surrender peaceable should be put under tribute but not killed.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v11|Deuteronomy 20:11]]

#### 22D.4
If an enemy city or territory will not surrender in peace, but wills to war against us, we are then to go to war against it and besieged the it.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v12|Deuteronomy 20:12]]; [[Joshua 11#^v19|Joshua 11:19-20]]

#### 22D.5
We are not to destroy enemies taken captive or conquered but rather provide for them.

Scripture: [[2 Kings 6#^v22|2 Kings 6:22-23]]

#### 22D.6
Heathen enemies within our own nation are to be totally destroyed.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 7#^v2|Deuteronomy 7:2]]; [[Deuteronomy 20#^v16|Deuteronomy 20:16-18]]; [[Joshua 6#^v21|Joshua 6:21]]; [[Joshua 8#^v24|Joshua 8:24-28]]; [[Joshua 11#^v1|Joshua 11:1-11]]

#### 22D.7
The kings or leaders of a conquered enemy are to be executed.

Scripture: [[Numbers 31#^v8|Numbers 31:8]]; [[Joshua 8#^v29|Joshua 8:29]]; [[Joshua 10#^v23|Joshua 10:23-26,28,30,39]]; [[Joshua 11#^v10|Joshua 11:10]]; [[Judges 7#^v25|Judges 7:25]]

#### 22D.8
We are not to have pity or mercy on heathen or ungodly enemies, but are to destroy all those who God shall deliver to us.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 7#^v2|Deuteronomy 7:2,16]]

#### 22D.9
A defeated enemy nation is subject to pay tribute taxes to the conquering nation.

Scripture: [[1 Kings 9#^v21|1 Kings 9:21]]; [[2 Kings 18#^v14|2 Kings 18:14]]; [[1 Chronicles 18#^v2|1 Chronicles 18:2]]; [[1 Chronicles 18#^v6|1 Chronicles 18:6]]; [[2 Chronicles 8#^v8|2 Chronicles 8:8]]; [[2 Chronicles 27#^v5|2 Chronicles 27:5]]; [[2 Chronicles 36#^v3|2 Chronicles 36:3]]

#### 22D.10
All males of heathen nations are to be killed.

Scripture: [[Numbers 31#^v7|Numbers 31:7,17]]; [[Deuteronomy 20#^v13|Deuteronomy 20:13]]; [[Joshua 11#^v14|Joshua 11:14]]

#### 22D.11
Prisoners of war, cattle, and goods of a defeated nation are to be taken as a spoil.

Scripture: [[Numbers 31#^v9|Numbers 31:9-18]]; [[Deuteronomy 20#^v14|Deuteronomy 20:14]]; [[Joshua 11#^v14|Joshua 11:14]]

#### 22D.12
We may take possession of the lands of defeated enemies.

Scripture: [[Numbers 21#^v24|Numbers 21:24-25,35]]
