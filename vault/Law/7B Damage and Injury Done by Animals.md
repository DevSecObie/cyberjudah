# 7B · Damage and Injury Done by Animals

[[Part 07 Animal regulations|Part 7 · Animal regulations]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 7B.1
If an animal kills a person the animal is to be put to death.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v28|Exodus 21:28-29]]

#### 7B.2
If an animal is proven to be aggressive, and its owner has been warned, yet he does not confine it, and it kills a man or woman, then both the animal and the owner are to be put to death. If there is a ransom of money demanded of the owner, it can be paid for the redemption of his life.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v29|Exodus 21:29-31]]

#### 7B.3
If an animal shall gore a servant, the owner shall give the servant's master a set amount of money and the animal is to be killed.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v32|Exodus 21:32]]

#### 7B.4
If a man's animal eats the crop of another man's field, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v5|Exodus 22:5]]

#### 7B.5
If one man's animal kills another's animal, then the live animal shall be sold and the money (and the dead animal if it be cattle) shall be divided between them.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v35|Exodus 21:35]]

#### 7B.6
If an animal which is known to be aggressive kills another animal yet its owner had not confined him, then he shall pay for the animal killed but can keep the dead animal.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v36|Exodus 21:36]]
