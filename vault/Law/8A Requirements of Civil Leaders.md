# 8A · Requirements of Civil Leaders

[[Part 08 Government & civil officers|Part 8 · Government & civil officers]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 8A.1
Leaders chosen must be wise and just.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 1#^v13|Deuteronomy 1:13]]; [[2 Samuel 23#^v3|2 Samuel 23:3]]; [[Psalms 2#^v10|Psalms 2:10]]; [[Proverbs 21#^v10|Proverbs 21:10]]; [[Proverbs 29#^v14|Proverbs 29:14]]; [[Ezekiel 45#^v9|Ezekiel 45:9]]

#### 8A.2
Civil leaders are to be diligent in governing.

Scripture: [[Romans 12#^v8|Romans 12:8]]

#### 8A.3
Civil leaders are to be men of truth, hating covetousness.

Scripture: [[Exodus 18#^v21|Exodus 18:21]]

#### 8A.4
Civil leaders, judges and officers are to fear God.

Scripture: [[Exodus 18#^v21|Exodus 18:21]]; [[Deuteronomy 17#^v19|Deuteronomy 17:19]]; [[2 Samuel 23#^v3|2 Samuel 23:3]]; [[2 Chronicles 19#^v7|2 Chronicles 19:7,9]]; [[Psalms 2#^v10|Psalms 2:10-11]]

#### 8A.5
Civil leaders are to be of God.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 17#^v15|Deuteronomy 17:15]]; [[Hosea 8#^v4|Hosea 8:4]]; [[Proverbs 8#^v15|Proverbs 8:15-16]]; [[Romans 13#^v1|Romans 13:1]]

#### 8A.6
Rulers are not to be a terror to good behavior, but rather to the wicked and evil doers.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 20#^v26|Proverbs 20:26]]; [[Romans 13#^v3|Romans 13:3-4]]

#### 8A.7
Civil rulers are not to use their office and position to bring gain to themselves.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 17#^v16|Deuteronomy 17:16-17]]

#### 8A.8
Civil rulers are ministers of God for good.

Scripture: [[Romans 13#^v4|Romans 13:4]]

#### 8A.9
Irresponsible civil leaders are they who are either blind, ignorant, lazy, greedy, self-centered, drunkards, or act for their own gain.

Scripture: [[Isaiah 56#^v10|Isaiah 56:10-12]]

#### 8A.10
Civil rulers are to record, preserve, study and proclaim the law of God at all times.

Scripture: [[Exodus 18#^v20|Exodus 18:20]]; [[Deuteronomy 17#^v18|Deuteronomy 17:18-20]]; [[Deuteronomy 31#^v9|Deuteronomy 31:9,24-26]]; [[Joshua 8#^v32|Joshua 8:32,34-35]]; [[2 Kings 22#^v8|2 Kings 22:8-20]]; [[2 Kings 23#^v1|2 Kings 23:1-3]]

#### 8A.11
If a ruler unintentionally or out of ignorance violates the law and the violation comes to his knowledge, he shall make restitution for it.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 4#^v22|Leviticus 4:22-23]]

#### 8A.12
Civil leaders and authorities are to be men not women.

Scripture: [[Exodus 18#^v21|Exodus 18:21]]; [[Deuteronomy 1#^v13|Deuteronomy 1:13,15]]; [[Isaiah 3#^v12|Isaiah 3:12]]

#### 8A.13
Government officers are to execute judgment and righteousness, and deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor; they are to do no wrong, do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, nor the widow, neither shed innocent blood in the land.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 22#^v3|Jeremiah 22:3]]

#### 8A.14
Government officers that engage in conspiracy, treachery and murder are to be put to death.

Scripture: [[Judges 9#^v1|Judges 9:1-57]]

#### 8A.15
The Law of God is to be read in public addresses by civil leaders to direct the course of society and government.

Scripture: [[Joshua 8#^v34|Joshua 8:34-35]]; [[2 Kings 23#^v1|2 Kings 23:1-3]]; [[2 Chronicles 17#^v7|2 Chronicles 17:7-9]]; [[2 Chronicles 34#^v30|2 Chronicles 34:30]]; [[Nehemiah 13#^v1|Nehemiah 13:1-3]]

#### 8A.16
A good civil leader or judge is from God, and keeps a people from rebelling against the ways of God.

Scripture: [[Judges 2#^v18|Judges 2:18-19]]; [[Judges 3#^v9|Judges 3:9-12]]

#### 8A.17
Civil authorities who are oppressive do not understand justice.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 28#^v5|Proverbs 28:5,16]]

#### 8A.18
Lying is unbecoming in rulers.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 17#^v7|Proverbs 17:7]]

#### 8A.19
If a ruler hearken to lies, all his aides and ministers become wicked.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 29#^v12|Proverbs 29:12]]

#### 8A.20
Those in government are not to consult mediums or spiritists for guidance but are to inquire of the LORD.

Scripture: [[1 Chronicles 10#^v13|1 Chronicles 10:13-14]]

#### 8A.21
Civil leaders are not to drink lest it causes them to forget the law and pervert justice.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 31#^v4|Proverbs 31:4-5]]

#### 8A.22
Civil rulers are to be chosen from among thy brethren or race, we are not to put a stranger (an alien or one of another race) over us in a position of authority.

Scripture: [[Exodus 18#^v21|Exodus 18:21]]; [[Deuteronomy 17#^v15|Deuteronomy 17:15]]; [[Jeremiah 30#^v21|Jeremiah 30:21]]

#### 8A.23
God will punish civil authorities who punish unjustly and contrary to the law.

Scripture: [[Acts 23#^v1|Acts 23:1-3]]

#### 8A.24
It is an abomination for a civil ruler to commit wickedness, for his office is established on righteousness.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 16#^v12|Proverbs 16:12]]
