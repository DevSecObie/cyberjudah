# 10I · Manslaughter

[[Part 10 Capital laws & offenses|Part 10 · Capital laws & offenses]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 10I.1
Manslaughter or accidental killing is not punished by death, but the one guilty is to be banished to a city of refuge.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v13|Exodus 21:13]]; [[Numbers 35#^v6|Numbers 35:6,11-15]]; [[Deuteronomy 4#^v41|Deuteronomy 4:41-42]]; [[Deuteronomy 19#^v4|Deuteronomy 19:4-5]]

#### 10I.2
If the manslayer at any time goes outside the city of refuge where he fled, he is subject to be killed by the avenger of blood.

Scripture: [[Numbers 35#^v26|Numbers 35:26-28]]

#### 10I.3
If a murder flees to a city of refuge, he is to be turned over to the hand of the executioner that he may die.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 19#^v12|Deuteronomy 19:12]]
