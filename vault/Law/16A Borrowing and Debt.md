# 16A · Borrowing and Debt

[[Part 16 Economic and monetary laws|Part 16 · Economic and monetary laws]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 16A.1
There shall be a cancellation of debt every seven years - a creditor can only hold a debt for 6 years and must release the obligation on the seventh year.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 15#^v1|Deuteronomy 15:1-2]]

#### 16A.2
Debts of foreigners may be continued beyond seven years.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 15#^v3|Deuteronomy 15:3]]

#### 16A.3
Those who need to borrow from you are not to be turned away.

Scripture: [[Matthew 5#^v42|Matthew 5:42]]

#### 16A.4
The borrower is servant to the lender.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 22#^v7|Proverbs 22:7]]

#### 16A.5
You shall not think evil and withhold loaning to your brother in need because the Seventh Year, the year of release, is at hand. You shall surely give to him and the LORD will bless you in all your works.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 15#^v9|Deuteronomy 15:9-10]]

#### 16A.6
It is wicked to borrow and not repay.

Scripture: [[Psalms 37#^v21|Psalms 37:21]]; [[Proverbs 3#^v27|Proverbs 3:27]]; [[Romans 13#^v8|Romans 13:8]]

#### 16A.7
Lend to the poor and needy what is sufficient for his needs.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 15#^v8|Deuteronomy 15:8]]

#### 16A.8
It is best to owe no man any thing.

Scripture: [[Romans 13#^v8|Romans 13:8]]

#### 16A.9
Items necessary to sustain a livelihood are not to be given as a pledge (security) for a debt.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 24#^v6|Deuteronomy 24:6]]; [[Job 24#^v3|Job 24:3]]

#### 16A.10
You shall not take a widow's garment as a pledge.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 24#^v17|Deuteronomy 24:17]]

#### 16A.11
Warnings against giving sureties for debts of others.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 6#^v1|Proverbs 6:1-3]]; [[Proverbs 17#^v18|Proverbs 17:18]]; [[Proverbs 22#^v26|Proverbs 22:26-27]]

#### 16A.12
He who is surety for a stranger will suffer for it, but one who hates being surety is secure.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 11#^v15|Proverbs 11:15]]

#### 16A.13
Pledges given by the poor for debt are not to be retained overnight.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v26|Exodus 22:26]]; [[Deuteronomy 24#^v12|Deuteronomy 24:12-13]]; [[Job 24#^v9|Job 24:9-10]]

#### 16A.14
When you lend your brother anything you shall not go into his house to take his pledge, but rather he shall bring the pledge out to you.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 24#^v10|Deuteronomy 24:10-11]]

#### 16A.15
The righteous man restores to the debtor his pledge, but the unrighteous does not restore a pledge.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 18#^v5|Ezekiel 18:5,7,12]]
