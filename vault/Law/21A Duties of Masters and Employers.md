# 21A · Duties of Masters and Employers

[[Part 21 Servants, employees, etc.|Part 21 · Servants, employees, etc.]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 21A.1
You are not to oppress the hired servant that is poor and needy, whether he be of thy brethren or a stranger, but shall give him his due wages.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 24#^v14|Deuteronomy 24:14-15]]; [[Jeremiah 22#^v13|Jeremiah 22:13]]

#### 21A.2
The laborer is worthy of his hire.

Scripture: [[Luke 10#^v7|Luke 10:7]]; [[1 Timothy 5#^v18|1 Timothy 5:18]]

#### 21A.3
Dutiful servants are to be honored.

Scripture: [[Proverbs 27#^v18|Proverbs 27:18]]

#### 21A.4
The wages of a hired man are not to be detained but paid to him the same day.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 19#^v13|Leviticus 19:13]]; [[Deuteronomy 24#^v15|Deuteronomy 24:15]]

#### 21A.5
Masters are to give to their servants that which is just and fair.

Scripture: [[Colossians 4#^v1|Colossians 4:1]]

#### 21A.6
Masters are to treat servants well and not to threaten them.

Scripture: [[Ephesians 6#^v9|Ephesians 6:9]]

#### 21A.7
God will avenge those who keep back the wages of their hirelings through fraud and exploitation.

Scripture: [[Malachi 3#^v5|Malachi 3:5]]; [[James 5#^v4|James 5:4]]

#### 21A.8
You shall not rule over a servant with rigor or severity, but you shall fear your God.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v43|Leviticus 25:43]]

#### 21A.9
When a servant departs from your service, you shall not let him go away emptyhanded, you shall supply him liberally from your estate.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 15#^v13|Deuteronomy 15:13-14]]
