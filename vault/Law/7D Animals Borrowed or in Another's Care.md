# 7D · Animals Borrowed or in Another's Care

[[Part 07 Animal regulations|Part 7 · Animal regulations]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 7D.1
If a man gives his neighbor his animal to keep for him and it dies or runs away, then an oath before the LORD shall be made between them, that he has not laid his hands on his neighbor's animal. He is not required to pay for it.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v10|Exodus 22:10-11]]

#### 7D.2
If a borrowed animal is stolen, the keeper shall make restitution to the owner of the animal.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v12|Exodus 22:12]]

#### 7D.3
If a borrowed animal is torn in pieces, the remains shall be brought as evidence, and no restitution shall be made.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v13|Exodus 22:13]]

#### 7D.4
If a man borrows any animal, and it is injured or dies while its owner is not with it, he shall make full restitution to the owner. But if the owner is with it, no restitution is to be made.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v14|Exodus 22:14-15]]
