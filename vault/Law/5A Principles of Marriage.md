[[Part 05 Marriage and Divorce|Part 5 · Marriage and Divorce]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 5A.1
Marriage between man and woman is instituted by God as a good and natural thing.

Scripture: [[Genesis 2#^v18|Genesis 2:18]]; [[Proverbs 18#^v22|Proverbs 18:22]]; [[Matthew 19#^v4|Matthew 19:4]]; [[Mark 10#^v6|Mark 10:6]]

#### 5A.2
Husband and wife are to remain together for life (marriage vows to be kept).

Scripture: [[Genesis 2#^v24|Genesis 2:24]]; [[Matthew 19#^v5|Matthew 19:5]]; [[Mark 10#^v7|Mark 10:7]]; [[1 Corinthians 7#^v10|1 Corinthians 7:10-11,27]]

#### 5A.3
A woman is bound by the law to her husband so long as he lives; but if the husband dies, she is released from the law concerning the husband.

Scripture: [[Romans 7#^v2|Romans 7:2-3]]

#### 5A.4
Husband and wife regarded as one flesh. Gen, 2:23-24;

Scripture: [[Matthew 19#^v5|Matthew 19:5]]; [[Mark 10#^v8|Mark 10:8]]; [[Ephesians 5#^v31|Ephesians 5:31]]

#### 5A.5
Marriage is honorable among all, and the bed undefiled.

Scripture: [[Hebrews 13#^v4|Hebrews 13:4]]

#### 5A.6
Husband and wife are to have children (be fruitful and multiply).

Scripture: [[Genesis 1#^v28|Genesis 1:28]]; [[Genesis 9#^v1|Genesis 9:1,7]]; [[Genesis 35#^v11|Genesis 35:11]]; [[Psalms 127#^v3|Psalms 127:3]]; [[Jeremiah 29#^v6|Jeremiah 29:6]]; [[1 Timothy 5#^v14|1 Timothy 5:14]]

#### 5A.7
Marriage bonds are of God and not to be put asunder by any man.

Scripture: [[Matthew 19#^v6|Matthew 19:6]]; [[Mark 10#^v9|Mark 10:9]]

#### 5A.8
Man is not independent of woman, nor is woman independent of man.
