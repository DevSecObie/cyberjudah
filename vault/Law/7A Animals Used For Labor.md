# 7A · Animals Used For Labor

[[Part 07 Animal regulations|Part 7 · Animal regulations]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 7A.1
You are not to work animals on the Sabbath.

Scripture: [[Exodus 20#^v10|Exodus 20:10]]; [[Exodus 23#^v12|Exodus 23:12]]; [[Deuteronomy 5#^v14|Deuteronomy 5:14]]

#### 7A.2
You are not to have two different kinds of animals work together; You shall not plow with an ox and an ass together.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 22#^v10|Deuteronomy 22:10]]

#### 7A.3
You shall not muzzle the mouth of the ox that is being used for work.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 25#^v4|Deuteronomy 25:4]]; [[1 Corinthians 9#^v9|1 Corinthians 9:9]]; [[1 Timothy 5#^v18|1 Timothy 5:18]]
