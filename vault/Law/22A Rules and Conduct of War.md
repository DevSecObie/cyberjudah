# 22A · Rules and Conduct of War

[[Part 22 Warfare and military laws|Part 22 · Warfare and military laws]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 22A.1
When an enemy nation attacks, plead to God that He will deliver the enemy into your hand.

Scripture: [[Numbers 21#^v1|Numbers 21:1-3]]

#### 22A.2
You are not to destroy the produce of a region besieged and occupied.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v19|Deuteronomy 20:19-20]]

#### 22A.3
Before a nation goes to battle the people should pray to God that He will maintain their cause.

Scripture: [[1 Kings 8#^v44|1 Kings 8:44-45]]

#### 22A.4
The use of spies towards enemy or hostile nations.

Scripture: [[Numbers 13#^v1|Numbers 13:1-21]]; [[Numbers 21#^v32|Numbers 21:32]]; [[Joshua 2#^v1|Joshua 2:1]]; [[Joshua 7#^v2|Joshua 7:2]]

#### 22A.5
Priests (chaplains) are to encourage the people before battle that God will fight for them.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v2|Deuteronomy 20:2-4]]

#### 22A.6
The LORD your God goes with you into battle, to fight for you against your enemies.

Scripture: [[Exodus 14#^v14|Exodus 14:14]]; [[Deuteronomy 20#^v1|Deuteronomy 20:1,4]]; [[2 Chronicles 13#^v12|2 Chronicles 13:12]]

#### 22A.7
We should inquire of the LORD as the whether or not we are to go to battle with another nation.

Scripture: [[Judges 6#^v33|Judges 6:33-40]]; [[1 Samuel 30#^v8|1 Samuel 30:8]]; [[2 Samuel 5#^v19|2 Samuel 5:19,23]]

#### 22A.8
Blowing of a trumpet before going into battle.

Scripture: [[Numbers 10#^v9|Numbers 10:9]]; [[Numbers 31#^v6|Numbers 31:6]]; [[Joshua 6#^v20|Joshua 6:20]]; [[Judges 7#^v16|Judges 7:16-22]]

#### 22A.9
The conquering nation may take as spoils of war: cattle, money, land, and goods of the defeated nation (to the victor belongs the spoils of war).

Scripture: [[Numbers 31#^v9|Numbers 31:9-11]]; [[Deuteronomy 2#^v35|Deuteronomy 2:35]]; [[Deuteronomy 3#^v7|Deuteronomy 3:7]]; [[Joshua 8#^v2|Joshua 8:2,27]]; [[1 Chronicles 20#^v2|1 Chronicles 20:2]]; [[1 Chronicles 26#^v27|1 Chronicles 26:27]]; [[Isaiah 10#^v6|Isaiah 10:6]]

#### 22A.11
You shall not take as a spoil those things accursed of God (pagan artifacts, idols, etc.).

Scripture: [[Joshua 7#^v1|Joshua 7:1-2,11-13,21-26]]

#### 22A.12
Abstain from every evil thing when at war against your enemies.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 23#^v9|Deuteronomy 23:9]]

#### 22A.13
You shall not unnecessarily oppress kindred nations you are at war with.

Scripture: [[2 Chronicles 28#^v8|2 Chronicles 28:8-15]]

#### 22A.14
The tabernacle of testimony is to be brought into the camp of the army.

Scripture: [[Numbers 1#^v50|Numbers 1:50-53]]

#### 22A.15
When encamped during war you are to cover up excrement outside of the camp.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 23#^v12|Deuteronomy 23:12-14]]

#### 22A.16
Cleanliness in the camp is to be maintained.

Scripture: [[Numbers 31#^v19|Numbers 31:19-24]]; [[Deuteronomy 23#^v9|Deuteronomy 23:9-11]]

#### 22A.17
The spoils of war are to be divided between those who served in battle, and between all the inhabitants of the nation.

Scripture: [[Numbers 31#^v27|Numbers 31:27]]; [[Joshua 22#^v8|Joshua 22:8]]

#### 22A.18
When you besiege a city you are to build bulwarks and fortified defenses within it until it be subdued.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 20#^v20|Deuteronomy 20:20]]

#### 22A.19
Do not plunder or fight any nation without just cause.

Scripture: [[Psalms 7#^v4|Psalms 7:4]]; [[Psalms 35#^v1|Psalms 35:1,7-8]]

#### 22A.20
When forces march out to battle, a detachment is to remain to watch over the camp.

Scripture: [[1 Samuel 30#^v24|1 Samuel 30:24]]

#### 22A.21
An army is divided into three divisions to divert the attention of the enemy and to conceal the attack.

Scripture: [[Judges 7#^v16|Judges 7:16]]; [[1 Samuel 11#^v11|1 Samuel 11:11]]; [[2 Samuel 18#^v2|2 Samuel 18:2]]
