# 20E · Tithes and Offerings

[[Part 20 Churches & ministers|Part 20 · Churches & ministers]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 20E.1
A tenth part of all increase (not all wealth owned) is to be given as a tithe.

Scripture: [[Genesis 14#^v20|Genesis 14:20]]; [[Genesis 28#^v22|Genesis 28:22]]; [[Numbers 18#^v21|Numbers 18:21]]; [[Hebrews 7#^v2|Hebrews 7:2,4]]

#### 20E.2
All offerings and things devoted to God as tithes are given to the priests and their aides.

Scripture: [[Genesis 14#^v18|Genesis 14:18-20]]; [[Numbers 18#^v8|Numbers 18:8-19,21,24]]; [[2 Chronicles 31#^v4|2 Chronicles 31:4-6,12-19]]; [[Nehemiah 10#^v37|Nehemiah 10:37]]; [[Hebrews 7#^v5|Hebrews 7:5]]

#### 20E.3
We are to honor God by bringing the choice first fruits of our land into the house of God, so He may bring prosperity to us.

Scripture: [[Exodus 23#^v19|Exodus 23:19]]; [[Malachi 3#^v10|Malachi 3:10-12]]; [[Proverbs 3#^v9|Proverbs 3:9-10]]

#### 20E.4
Withholding tithes and offerings to God is considered as robbing God.

Scripture: [[Malachi 3#^v8|Malachi 3:8]]

#### 20E.5
Do not try to purchase gifts of God with money and offerings.

Scripture: [[Acts 8#^v18|Acts 8:18-21]]

#### 20E.6
A tenth of all the produce of the land is to be tithed to God.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 27#^v30|Leviticus 27:30]]; [[Deuteronomy 14#^v22|Deuteronomy 14:22]]; [[2 Chronicles 31#^v5|2 Chronicles 31:5]]

#### 20E.7
A tenth of all cattle and farm animals are to be tithed to God.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 27#^v32|Leviticus 27:32]]; [[2 Chronicles 31#^v6|2 Chronicles 31:6]]

#### 20E.8
If a man wishes to redeem part of his tithe, he shall add to it one-fifth of it.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 27#^v31|Leviticus 27:31]]

#### 20E.9
You shall not bring the hire of a harlot or the wages of a dog (male prostitute) into the house of the LORD your God for any pledge, vow or tithe.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 23#^v18|Deuteronomy 23:18]]

#### 20E.10
Tithes of food and first fruits of the land are to be eaten by all the people, the foreigners, and the poor as a feast to God.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 12#^v6|Deuteronomy 12:6-7,11-12]]; [[Deuteronomy 14#^v23|Deuteronomy 14:23,28-29]]; [[Deuteronomy 26#^v10|Deuteronomy 26:10-11]]

#### 20E.11
Those that labor in preaching and teaching the word of God, or in doing his work, are entitled to a good wage for their work.

Scripture: [[2 Chronicles 34#^v9|2 Chronicles 34:9-12,17]]; [[1 Timothy 5#^v17|1 Timothy 5:17-18]]

#### 20E.12
Priests and ministerial aides are to give a tenth of the tithes they receive.

Scripture: [[Numbers 18#^v25|Numbers 18:25-30]]

#### 20E.13
Tithes are to be given to the priests and ministerial aides so that they may devote themselves to the law of God.

Scripture: [[Numbers 18#^v21|Numbers 18:21-24]]; [[2 Chronicles 31#^v4|2 Chronicles 31:4]]; [[Nehemiah 10#^v37|Nehemiah 10:37-39]]; [[Hebrews 7#^v5|Hebrews 7:5]]

#### 20E.14
At the end of three years, you shall give the tithe of your increase to the ministerial aides, the sojourners, the orphans and widows in the land that they may eat.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 26#^v12|Deuteronomy 26:12]]
