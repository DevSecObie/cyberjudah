#### 12D.1
Women shall not wear men's clothing, nor shall a man put on women's clothing, this is an abomination to God.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 22#^v5|Deuteronomy 22:5]]

#### 12D.2
We are not to wear a garment of mixed fabrics (e.g. wool and linen).

Scripture: [[Leviticus 19#^v19|Leviticus 19:19]]; [[Deuteronomy 22#^v11|Deuteronomy 22:11]]

#### 12D.3
Women are to wear modest apparel and should not have braided hair or adorn themselves with gold or fancy and costly array.

Scripture: [[Isaiah 3#^v16|Isaiah 3:16-24]]; [[1 Timothy 2#^v9|1 Timothy 2:9]]; [[1 Peter 3#^v3|1 Peter 3:3]]

#### 12D.4
Clothes are to be washed before appearing in the sight of God.

Scripture: [[Exodus 19#^v10|Exodus 19:10-11]]

#### 12D.5
Whoever handles the carcass of unclean animals is to wash their clothes.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 11#^v25|Leviticus 11:25]]

#### 12D.6

Scripture: [[1 Corinthians 11#^v14|1 Corinthians 11:14]]

#### 12D.7
