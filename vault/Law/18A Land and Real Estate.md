# 18A · Land and Real Estate

[[Part 18 Laws regarding property|Part 18 · Laws regarding property]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 18A.1
The earth and all realty and things on it belong to God.

Scripture: [[Genesis 14#^v19|Genesis 14:19]]; [[Genesis 14#^v22|Genesis 14:22]]; [[Exodus 19#^v5|Exodus 19:5]]; [[Leviticus 25#^v23|Leviticus 25:23]]; [[Deuteronomy 10#^v14|Deuteronomy 10:14]]; [[Psalms 24#^v1|Psalms 24:1]]; [[Psalms 50#^v10|Psalms 50:10-12]]; [[Psalms 89#^v11|Psalms 89:11]]; [[1 Corinthians 10#^v26|1 Corinthians 10:26,28]]

#### 18A.2
Land can be sold but not permanently. You shall grant a redemption for all the land in your possession (the original owner has the right to buy back land he sold).

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v23|Leviticus 25:23-24]]

#### 18A.3
In the year of the Jubilee (every fiftieth year) land is to be returned to the person, or his rightful heirs or family, that originally possessed the land.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v10|Leviticus 25:10,13-17,28,31,33-34]]

#### 18A.4
If a house in the city is sold, it can be redeemed (bought back) within a year. After that the house is unredeemable and shall belong permanently to him who bought it. It will not return to the seller in the Jubilee.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v29|Leviticus 25:29-30]]

#### 18A.5
Houses in the rural or country regions may be redeemed at any time, and they shall be released in the Jubilee.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v31|Leviticus 25:31]]

#### 18A.6
If one becomes poor and is unable to redeem his land, a kinsmen of his may redeem for him what he had sold.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v25|Leviticus 25:25]]; [[Ruth 4#^v3|Ruth 4:3-4]]; [[Jeremiah 32#^v7|Jeremiah 32:7]]

#### 18A.7
If one has no means by which to pay the purchaser of his property the redemption money, then what was sold shall remain in the hand of him who bought it until the Year of Jubilee.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 25#^v28|Leviticus 25:28]]

#### 18A.8
Transfer of land to be by signed deed in the presence of witnesses.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 32#^v8|Jeremiah 32:8-12]]

#### 18A.9
Deeds to land are to be preserved as a record.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 32#^v14|Jeremiah 32:14]]

#### 18A.10
Silver used to buy land.

Scripture: [[Genesis 23#^v13|Genesis 23:13-16]]; [[Joshua 24#^v32|Joshua 24:32]]; [[Jeremiah 32#^v9|Jeremiah 32:9]]

#### 18A.11
Do not to remove established landmarks and boundaries.

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 19#^v14|Deuteronomy 19:14]]; [[Deuteronomy 27#^v17|Deuteronomy 27:17]]; [[Job 24#^v2|Job 24:2]]; [[Proverbs 22#^v28|Proverbs 22:28]]; [[Proverbs 23#^v10|Proverbs 23:10]]

#### 18A.12
You should take appropriate precautions of any dangerous situation on your land which might cause damage or injury to other persons or their property.

Scripture: [[Exodus 21#^v33|Exodus 21:33-34]]; [[Deuteronomy 22#^v8|Deuteronomy 22:8]]

#### 18A.13
The mortgage of land and homes brings one into bondage.

Scripture: [[Nehemiah 5#^v1|Nehemiah 5:1-5]]

#### 18A.14
Restitution is required for damage done to one's land or crops.

Scripture: [[Exodus 22#^v5|Exodus 22:5-6]]
