# 14C · Plants and Herbs

[[Part 14 Food and health laws|Part 14 · Food and health laws]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 14C.1
Every green herb and herbs bearing seed have been given by God to be used for food.

Scripture: [[Genesis 1#^v29|Genesis 1:29]]; [[Genesis 3#^v18|Genesis 3:18]]

#### 14C.2
A newly planted tree producing edible food shall not be eaten for the first three years it produces. In the fourth year all its fruit shall be an offering to God. In the fifth year the fruit of the tree may be eaten.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 19#^v23|Leviticus 19:23-25]]

#### 14C.3
Every tree which bears fruit yielding seed was made by God to be eaten as food.

Scripture: [[Genesis 1#^v29|Genesis 1:29]]

#### 14C.4
You are not to sow your vineyards with divers seeds: least all the produce of the seed which you have sown, and the fruit of the vineyard become defiled (hybridized).

Scripture: [[Deuteronomy 22#^v9|Deuteronomy 22:9]]

#### 14C.5
You shall not sow your field with mingled (mixed) seed, for the different kinds created were to be kept separate.

Scripture: [[Leviticus 19#^v19|Leviticus 19:19]]

#### 14C.6
Green plants given by God for food.

Scripture: [[Genesis 9#^v3|Genesis 9:3]]
