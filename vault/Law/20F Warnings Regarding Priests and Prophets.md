# 20F · Warnings Regarding Priests and Prophets

[[Part 20 Churches & ministers|Part 20 · Churches & ministers]] · [[Law Index]] · [Home](/)

#### 20F.1
Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep's clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.

Scripture: [[Matthew 7#^v15|Matthew 7:15]]; [[Matthew 24#^v11|Matthew 24:11,24]]; [[Mark 13#^v22|Mark 13:22]]

#### 20F.2
Beware of those that honor Christ with words but teach as their doctrines the precepts and traditions of men.

Scripture: [[Matthew 15#^v8|Matthew 15:8-9]]

#### 20F.3
Bad pastors can cause the people to go astray and become devoured by their adversaries.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 23#^v1|Jeremiah 23:1-2]]; [[Jeremiah 50#^v6|Jeremiah 50:6-7]]

#### 20F.4
Lying pastors and those that withhold the truth of God's word are cursed for causing national apostasy.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 23#^v1|Jeremiah 23:1-2]]

#### 20F.5
Those that boast are false apostles of Christ.

Scripture: [[2 Corinthians 11#^v12|2 Corinthians 11:12-13]]

#### 20F.6
Preachers who preach to please men are not a servant of Christ.

Scripture: [[Galatians 1#^v10|Galatians 1:10]]

#### 20F.7
Beware lest any deceive you through philosophy, according to the traditions of men, and not according to Christ.

Scripture: [[Colossians 2#^v8|Colossians 2:8]]

#### 20F.8
Take heed that no preacher, theologian, or teacher deceive you; for many shall come in Christ's name and shall deceive many.

Scripture: [[Jeremiah 29#^v8|Jeremiah 29:8-9]]; [[Matthew 24#^v4|Matthew 24:4-5]]; [[Mark 13#^v5|Mark 13:5-6]]

#### 20F.9
Beware of pastors that feed themselves and become fat (wealthy), but do not feed (instruct) the people and will neglect their needs.

Scripture: [[Ezekiel 34#^v2|Ezekiel 34:2-4,10]]

#### 20F.10
Do not believe every spirit, but test the spirits, whether they are of God; for there are many false prophets.

Scripture: [[1 John 4#^v1|1 John 4:1]]
