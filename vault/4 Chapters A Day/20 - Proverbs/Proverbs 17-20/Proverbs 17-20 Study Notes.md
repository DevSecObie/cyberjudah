# Proverbs 17: Trials That Refine and the Weight of Words

Read the chapter → [[Proverbs 17]]

This session covers [[Proverbs 17|Proverbs 17]] through 20, wisdom Solomon received directly from the Most High God. Solomon ruled in a peaceable time because his father King David fought the wars and brought nations under subjection, so Solomon had leisure to gain wisdom. In this captivity most of us work long hours by design, because Esau does not want us to return to our rightful places, and that leaves little time to study. But wisdom is required of us, so we must put away distractions like television and apply our time to the word.

  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 38#^v24|Ecclesiasticus 38:24]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 38#^v24]]
    Little business makes a man wise, but this captivity steals our hours.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v64|Deuteronomy 28:64]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v64]]
    Scattered among the nations, our feet find no rest and we serve, which is why our time is consumed.
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 7#^v17|Wisdom of Solomon 7:17]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 7#^v17]]
    The Most High gave Solomon knowledge of how everything works.
  - **[[1 Kings 4#^v29|1 Kings 4:29]]**
    ![[1 Kings 4#^v29]]
    God gave Solomon wisdom exceeding all men, and he spake three thousand proverbs, some of which we now read.

- The fining pot and furnace remove impurities from silver and gold; likewise the Most High tries our hearts through trials to rid us of our impurities. We must go through trials and tribulations to gain the kingdom.
- This is why Christianity is false when it teaches you only have to accept Jesus in your heart to be saved forever. That is not what the scriptures say.
- Whoso mocks the poor reproaches his Maker, and gladness at another man's calamity does not go unpunished.
- Children's children are the crown of old men, and the glory of children are their fathers. Many of us grew up in fatherless homes, but we must not glory in a father's dishonor.
- Sisters should not speak evil of the man they chose to lie down with and bring in seed by, no matter how bad he is; children raised to despise their fathers must repent of it in this truth.
- Just as grandchildren crown the old, we glorify our Father in heaven and our forefathers by our repentance and return to righteousness.

- Whoso rewards evil for good, evil shall not depart from his house. That is everything right there.
- The beginning of strife is like letting out water, so leave off contention before it is meddled with; where envying and strife are, there is confusion and every evil work.

- He that justifies the wicked and he that condemns the just are both an abomination to the Most High. To justify the wicked is to wrest judgment and take a bribe.
- A bribe blinds the eyes of the wise and perverts the words of the righteous, and this perverting of judgment is something we suffer with as a people.
- Woe to those who call evil good and good evil, who justify the wicked for reward; the reward usually comes as a financial gift or a place of importance.
- There is no point in a fool holding a price to buy wisdom when he has no heart for it.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 16#^v19|Deuteronomy 16:19]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 16#^v19]]
    Thou shalt not wrest judgment, respect persons, nor take a gift, for a gift blinds the wise.
  - **[[Exodus 23#^v8|Exodus 23:8]]**
    ![[Exodus 23#^v8]]
    Thou shalt take no gift, for the gift blinds the wise and perverts the words of the righteous.
  - **[[Isaiah 5#^v20|Isaiah 5:20]]**
    ![[Isaiah 5#^v20]]
    Woe unto them that call evil good and good evil, that justify the wicked for reward.

- He that loves transgression loves strife, and he that exalts his gate seeks destruction; a froward heart finds no good and a perverse tongue falls into mischief.

- A merry heart does good like a medicine, but a broken spirit dries the bones.
- Wisdom is before him that has understanding, but a fool's eyes wander to the ends of the earth. Understanding comes only through keeping the commandments.
- This is why in Ecclesiastes Solomon gives the conclusion of the whole matter: keep the commandments, for that is the whole duty of man.

- He that has knowledge spares his words, and even a fool who holds his peace is counted wise. Talking too much, especially without the words of the Most High, marks a fool.
- Let thy speech be short, comprehending much in few words; speak only when there is need of thee and hold thy tongue among great men.

# Proverbs 18: The Power of the Tongue and Righteous Judgment

Read the chapter → [[Proverbs 18]]

**[[Proverbs 18#^v1|Proverbs 18:1-5]]**
![[Proverbs 18#^v1]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v2]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v3]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v4]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v5]]

- Through desire a man having separated himself seeks and intermeddles with all wisdom; this is the educated fool who studies everything yet says nothing anyone understands, stricken with madness in his own eyes.
- A fool has no delight in understanding but only that his heart may discover itself; when the wicked comes, contempt and reproach come also.
- It is not good to accept the persons of the wicked to overthrow righteous judgment, the very thing the Most High despises.
- We may not say the law is done away with, for if it were, righteous judgment would be done away with too, which makes no sense.
  - **[[Leviticus 19#^v15|Leviticus 19:15]]**
    ![[Leviticus 19#^v15]]
    Do no unrighteousness in judgment; respect not the poor nor honor the mighty, but judge thy neighbor in righteousness.
  - **[[James 2#^v1|James 2:1]]**
    ![[James 2#^v1]]
    Hold not the faith of Christ with respect of persons; favoring the rich man over the poor makes you a judge of evil thoughts.
  - **[[James 2#^v9|James 2:9]]**
    ![[James 2#^v9]]
    If ye respect persons, ye commit sin and are convinced of the law as transgressors.

**[[Proverbs 18#^v6|Proverbs 18:6-9]]**
![[Proverbs 18#^v6]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v7]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v8]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v9]]

- A fool's lips enter into contention and his mouth calls for strokes; a fool's mouth is his destruction and his lips the snare of his soul.
- The words of a talebearer are as wounds; when you repeat a matter told in confidence, you separate friends because they can no longer trust you. A gossiping, backbiting spirit must not be named among Israel, a holy people.
- He that is slothful in his work is brother to a great waster.

**[[Proverbs 18#^v10|Proverbs 18:10-14]]**
![[Proverbs 18#^v10]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v11]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v12]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v13]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v14]]

- The name of the Lord is a strong tower; the righteous run into it and are safe, while a rich man's wealth is only his strong city in his own conceit.
- Before destruction the heart is haughty, and before honor is humility.
- He that answers a matter before he hears it, it is folly and shame unto him; blame not before examining the truth and interrupt not men in the midst of their talk. That is wisdom and discipline.
- A wounded spirit no man can bear.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 11#^v7|Ecclesiasticus 11:7]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 11#^v7]]
    Hear the matter fully before answering; that is wisdom and discipline.

![[Proverbs 18#^v18]]
![[Proverbs 18#^v19]]

- The heart of the prudent gets knowledge and the ear of the wise seeks knowledge; a man's gift makes room for him before great men.
- A brother offended is harder to be won than a strong city. Once you offend someone it is very hard to win them back.
- If thy brother trespasses against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone; go straight to the source rather than becoming a talebearer.
- There is a difference in offense. If you offend a brother or cause harm, make it right; but if you bring out God's commandments, such as a sister wearing what belongs to a man or a brother refusing to grow his beard, and they are offended, then they are offended at Christ, not at you.
  - **[[Matthew 18#^v15|Matthew 18:15]]**
    ![[Matthew 18#^v15]]
    Go and tell thy brother his fault between thee and him alone; if he hears thee, thou hast gained thy brother.
  - **[[Matthew 11#^v6|Matthew 11:6]]**
    ![[Matthew 11#^v6]]
    Blessed is he whosoever shall not be offended in me.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 22#^v5|Deuteronomy 22:5]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 22#^v5]]
    A woman shall not wear that which pertains to a man, the commandment some are offended at.

**[[Proverbs 18#^v20|Proverbs 18:20-24]]**

- Death and life are in the power of the tongue; the things you say can build someone up or tear them down, and those that love the tongue eat its fruit.
- Whoso finds a wife finds a good thing and obtains favor of the Lord.
- The poor uses entreaties but the rich answers roughly; pride from riches makes a man deal harshly, and the proud among the nations speak roughly and spare no words.
- A man that has friends must show himself friendly, and there is a friend that sticks closer than a brother.
  - **[[James 3#^v10|James 3:10]]**
    ![[James 3#^v10]]
    Out of the same mouth proceed blessing and cursing; these things ought not so to be.

# Proverbs 19: Discipline, the Fear of the Lord, and Servitude

![[Proverbs 19#^v2]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v3]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v4]]

- Better is the poor that walks in his integrity than he that is perverse in his lips and is a fool.
- That the soul be without knowledge is not good, and he that hastes with his feet sins; the foolish man perverts his way then his heart frets against the Lord.
- Wealth makes many friends, but the poor is separated from his neighbor. When goods increase, so do those that eat them.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 5#^v11|Ecclesiastes 5:11]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 5#^v11]]
    When goods increase, they are increased that eat them; the owner only beholds them with his eyes.

![[Proverbs 19#^v6]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v7]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v8]]

- A false witness shall not be unpunished and he that speaks lies shall not escape.
- Every man is a friend to him that gives gifts, but all the brethren of the poor hate him and his friends go far from him.
- He that gets wisdom loves his own soul, and he that keeps understanding shall find good.

**[[Proverbs 19#^v10|Proverbs 19:10-14]]**
![[Proverbs 19#^v10]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v11]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v12]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v13]]

- Delight is not seemly for a fool, much less for a servant to have rule over princes; and right now our servants are having rule over us.
- The discretion of a man defers his anger, and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.
- A foolish son is the calamity of his father, and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping; Solomon knew this, and his own son Rehoboam was a foolish young man who lacked his father's wisdom.
- House and riches are the inheritance of fathers, but a prudent wife is from the Lord, called a gift.

**[[Proverbs 19#^v15|Proverbs 19:15-18]]**
![[Proverbs 19#^v15]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v16]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v17]]
![[Proverbs 19#^v18]]

- Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.
- He that keeps the commandments keeps his own soul, but he that despises his ways shall die.
- He that has pity on the poor lends unto the Lord, and the Lord will repay him.
- Chasten thy son while there is hope and let not thy soul spare for his crying. Esau has continually tried to take away our ability to rightfully punish our children and bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.
- Discipline must start in the youth. If you wait until twelve or thirteen to begin, it is over, or you will have a very difficult time.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 30#^v12|Ecclesiasticus 30:12]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 30#^v12]]
    Discipline must start in the youth.

![[Proverbs 19#^v24]]

- A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment; hear counsel and receive instruction that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.
- There are many devices in a man's heart, but the counsel of the Lord shall stand. This is why the heart is called deceitful above all things.
- The fear of the Lord tends to life, and he that has it shall abide satisfied and not be visited with evil.
  - **[[Jeremiah 17#^v9|Jeremiah 17:9]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 17#^v9]]

- Smite a scorner and the simple will beware; reprove one that has understanding and he will understand knowledge.
- He that wastes his father and chases away his mother is a son that causes shame and brings reproach.
- Judgments are prepared for scorners and stripes for the backs of fools. The Most High is preparing us for the kingdom; he will beat that foolishness out of us.
- Bread, correction, and work are for a servant. We have been brought down to the serving class because of sin, because of disobedience to God's commandments.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v24|Ecclesiasticus 33:24]]**

    Is Israel a servant? Is he a homeborn slave? Why is he spoiled? Because of disobedience.

# Proverbs 20: Integrity, Just Weights, and Waiting on the Lord

Read the chapter → [[Proverbs 20]]

**[[Proverbs 20#^v1|Proverbs 20:1-4]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v1]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v2]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v3]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v4]]

- Wine is a mocker and strong drink is raging; whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise.
- The fear of a king is as the roaring of a lion; whoso provokes him to anger sins against his own soul.
- It is an honor for a man to cease from strife, but every fool will be meddling.
- The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold, so he shall beg in harvest and have nothing; he is the same man who says there is a lion in the way.

**[[Proverbs 20#^v5|Proverbs 20:5-8]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v5]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v6]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v7]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v8]]

- Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water, but a man of understanding will draw it out.
- Most men proclaim their own goodness, but a faithful man who can find?
- The just man walks in his integrity, and his children are blessed after him. This is what we must seek in this truth and repentance, to walk circumspectly in our integrity.
- A king on the throne of judgment scatters away all evil with his eyes.

**[[Proverbs 20#^v9|Proverbs 20:9-12]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v9]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v10]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v11]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v12]]

- Who can say, I have made my heart clean, I am pure from sin? No man can, because man does not know his own ways or goings; that belongs to the Lord.
- Divers weights and divers measures are both alike an abomination unto the Lord, the very cheating Amos condemns.
- Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure and right.
- The hearing ear and the seeing eye, the Lord has made both. When you battle with someone who does not understand, it is not their time; the Lord dulls the ears and closes the spiritual eyes so they cannot see or understand.
  - **[[Amos 8#^v5|Amos 8:5]]**
    ![[Amos 8#^v5]]
    The cheaters ask when the new moon will be gone so they can make the ephah small and the shekel great and falsify the balances.
  - **[[Isaiah 6#^v9|Isaiah 6:9]]**
    ![[Isaiah 6#^v9]]
    Hear ye indeed but understand not; the Lord makes their heart fat and shuts their eyes lest they see and convert.

**[[Proverbs 20#^v13|Proverbs 20:13-17]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v13]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v14]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v15]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v16]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v17]]

- Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes and thou shalt be satisfied with bread.
- It is naught, says the buyer, but when he is gone his way then he boasts; that is trying to get over on people.
- Take the garment of him that is surety for a stranger. As with surety before, it is like being a co-signer, saying you will cover that person.
- Bread of deceit is sweet to a man, but afterward his mouth is filled with gravel. Getting over on somebody is sweet until you get caught.

**[[Proverbs 20#^v18|Proverbs 20:18-22]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v18]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v19]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v20]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v21]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v22]]

- Every purpose is established by counsel, and with good advice make war.
- He that goes about as a talebearer reveals secrets, so meddle not with him that flatters with his lips; the talebearer separates friends, and there are laws against it.
- Whoso curses father or mother, his lamp shall be put out in obscure darkness.
- An inheritance gotten hastily at the beginning shall not be blessed in the end.
- Say not thou I will recompense evil, but wait on the Lord and he shall save thee. When Absalom and Ahithophel conspired against David, he did not go physically to destroy them but prayed and fasted and waited for the Lord's answer.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 21#^v18|Deuteronomy 21:18]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 21#^v18]]
    The stubborn and rebellious son who will not obey father or mother is brought before the elders for judgment.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 27#^v16|Deuteronomy 27:16]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 27#^v16]]
    Cursed be he that sets light by his father or his mother.

**[[Proverbs 20#^v23|Proverbs 20:23-27]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v23]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v24]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v25]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v26]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v27]]

- Divers weights are an abomination unto the Lord, and a false balance is not good.
- Man's goings are of the Lord; how can a man then understand his own way?
- It is a snare to devour that which is holy and to make inquiry only after making vows.
- A wise king scatters the wicked and brings the wheel over them; the spirit of man is the candle of the Lord, searching all the inward parts of the belly.

**[[Proverbs 20#^v28|Proverbs 20:28-30]]**
![[Proverbs 20#^v28]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v29]]
![[Proverbs 20#^v30]]

- Mercy and truth preserve the king, and his throne is upheld by mercy.
- The glory of young men is their strength, and the beauty of old men is the gray head. Honorable age is not length of years but wisdom, the gray hair being an unspotted life and a good name kept blameless.
- The blueness of a wound cleanses away evil, and so do stripes the inward parts of the belly; the older generation knows well what that discipline meant.
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 4#^v8|Wisdom of Solomon 4:8]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 4#^v8]]
    The gray head is a good name kept blameless.
