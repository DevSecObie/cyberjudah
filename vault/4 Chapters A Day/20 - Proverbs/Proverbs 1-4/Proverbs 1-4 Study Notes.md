# Proverbs 1: The Fear of the Lord Is the Beginning of Knowledge

Read the chapter → [[Proverbs 1]]

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session moves through [[Proverbs 1|Proverbs 1]] to 4. Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel, gives understanding in these proverbs, and the whole thread is the fear of the Most High and the keeping of His wisdom. He warns against being enticed by sinners and against greediness of gain, and he holds up wisdom and the commandments as our life, far exceeding silver, gold, and rubies. Chapter 1 opens the book with the purpose of the proverbs and the first warning: consent not when sinners entice you.

- The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel. Solomon is giving understanding in this proverb, and from the very top he is basically talking about the fear of the Most High and understanding.
- These verses lay out why the proverbs are given: to know wisdom and instruction, to perceive the words of understanding, to receive the instruction of wisdom, justice, judgment, and equity, to give subtlety to the simple, and to the young man knowledge and discretion.
- A wise man will hear and increase learning, and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels, to understand the proverb and the interpretation, the words of the wise and their dark sayings.

- The fear of the Most High is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction. This is the key that runs through all four chapters.
- My son, hear the instruction of thy father and forsake not the law of thy mother, for they shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head and chains about thy neck.

- A key point is verse 10: my son, if sinners entice thee, consent thou not. If somebody who is committing sin entices you or tries to get you to go with them, do not go with them.
- To bring some life to it, look at something like a robbery. If they say come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause, that means you are doing evil to the innocent for no reason.
- Let us swallow them up alive as the grave, and we shall find all precious substance and fill our houses with spoil. Whatever you take from this person, you are going to fill your own pockets with their goods.
- Cast in thy lot among us, let us all have one purse. The sinner is enticing you, saying that if you consent, then they will share the spoils of what they get.
- My son, walk not thou in the way with them, refrain thy foot from their path. Solomon is giving wisdom: do not fall into the hands of sinners, do not be enticed by the things they want to do, and do not be enticed by their greediness.
- So are the ways of everyone that is greedy of gain, which taketh away the life of the owners thereof. If you are greedy of gain, you will be going with them, you will be enticed, and you will consent to them.

# Proverbs 2: The Benefits of Keeping the Wisdom of the Lord

Read the chapter → [[Proverbs 2]]

**[[Proverbs 2#^v1|Proverbs 2:1-5]]**

![[Proverbs 2#^v1]]
![[Proverbs 2#^v2]]

![[Proverbs 2#^v4]]

- My son, if thou receive my words and hide my commandments with thee, so that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom and apply thine heart to understanding.
- Verses 2 through 4 explain what that means: crying after knowledge, lifting up thy voice for understanding, and seeking her as silver and searching for her as for hid treasures.
- The reward of hiding the commandments with thee is in verse 5: then shalt thou understand the fear of the Most High and find the knowledge of God.

![[Proverbs 2#^v6]]

- For the Most High giveth wisdom; out of His mouth cometh knowledge and understanding. He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous and is a buckler to them that walk uprightly.
- He keepeth the paths of judgment and preserveth the way of His saints. The saints are the Israelites, as we read yesterday.
- Then shalt thou understand righteousness, judgment, and equity, yea every good path, when wisdom enters into thine heart and knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul; discretion shall preserve thee and understanding shall keep thee.

- Understanding delivers thee from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh forward things. Forward means things that are against the Most High, things that go contrary to the commandments.
- These evil men leave the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness, who rejoice to do evil and delight in the forwardness of the wicked, whose ways are crooked and who are forward in their paths.

- Understanding also delivers thee from the strange woman, from the stranger which flattereth with her words, which forsaketh the guide of her youth and forgetteth the covenant of her God.
- Her house inclineth unto death and her paths unto the dead; none that go unto her return again, neither take they hold of the paths of life. This goes for men as well, and it is vice versa, so it is something to meditate on.

**[[Proverbs 2#^v20|Proverbs 2:20-22]]**

![[Proverbs 2#^v20]]
![[Proverbs 2#^v21]]

- That thou mayest walk in the way of good men and keep the paths of the righteous. For the upright shall dwell in the land and the perfect shall remain in it.
- But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth and the transgressors shall be rooted out of it. The whole chapter is basically showing the benefits of keeping the wisdom of the Most High.

- Happy is the man that findeth wisdom and the man that getteth understanding, for the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver and the gain thereof than fine gold. - She is more precious than rubies, and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her. Nothing can be compared to the wisdom of the Most High.
- Length of days is in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and honor. Her ways are ways of pleasantness and all her paths are peace. She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her, and happy is everyone that retaineth her.

# Proverbs 4: Wisdom Is the Principal Thing

**[[Proverbs 4#^v1|Proverbs 4:1-4]]**

![[Proverbs 4#^v1]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v2]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v3]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v4]]

- Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding, for I give you good doctrine; forsake ye not my law.
- For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother; he taught me also and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words, keep my commandments and live.

**[[Proverbs 4#^v5|Proverbs 4:5-9]]**

![[Proverbs 4#^v5]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v6]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v7]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v8]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v9]]

- Get wisdom, get understanding; forget it not, neither decline from the words of my mouth. Forsake her not and she shall preserve thee; love her and she shall keep thee.
- Wisdom is the principal thing, therefore get wisdom, and with all thy getting get understanding. Exalt her and she shall promote thee; she shall bring thee to honor when thou dost embrace her.
- She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace and a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

**[[Proverbs 4#^v10|Proverbs 4:10-13]]**

![[Proverbs 4#^v10]]

- Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings, and the years of thy life shall be many. I have taught thee in the way of wisdom and led thee in right paths.
- When thou goest thy steps shall not be straitened, and when thou runnest thou shalt not stumble. Take fast hold of instruction, let her not go, keep her, for she is thy life. The commandments are our life.

![[Proverbs 4#^v14]]

- Enter not into the path of the wicked and go not in the way of evil men; avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away. This is the same thing we read in [[Proverbs 1#^v16|Proverbs 1:16]].
- For they sleep not except they have done mischief, and their sleep is taken away unless they cause some to fall, for they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence.
- The path of the just is as the shining light that shineth more and more unto the perfect day, but the way of the wicked is as darkness; they know not at what they stumble.
  - **[[Proverbs 1#^v16|Proverbs 1:16]]**
    ![[Proverbs 1#^v16]]
    For their feet run to evil and make haste to shed blood, the same wicked path Solomon says to avoid and turn from.

- My son, attend to my words and incline thine ear unto my sayings; let them not depart from thine eyes, keep them in the midst of thine heart, for they are life unto those that find them and health to their flesh.
- Keep thy heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. Put away from thee a forward mouth and perverse lips put far from thee. A forward mouth is a mouth that speaks things opposite of what the word of the Most High says.

**[[Proverbs 4#^v25|Proverbs 4:25-27]]**

![[Proverbs 4#^v25]]
![[Proverbs 4#^v26]]

- Let thine eyes look right on and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet and let all thy ways be established.
- Turn not to the right hand nor to the left; remove thy foot from evil. Across all four chapters Solomon says the same thing over and over, the same thing he says in his psalms: depart not from the commandments, keep them close to you, and depart from evil, all of it praising the Most High.
- On the question of the law and the commandments, they are exactly the same; the words are just used interchangeably. The commandments He gave are the laws, as in [[Exodus 20|Exodus 20]] where the commandments are given, thou shalt not kill, thou shalt not steal, the ten commandments plus many more that came with them.
