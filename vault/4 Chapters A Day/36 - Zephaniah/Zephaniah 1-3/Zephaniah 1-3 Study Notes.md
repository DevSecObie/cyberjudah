# Zephaniah 1: The Great Day of the Lord Is Near

Read the chapter → [[Zephaniah 1]]

## Introduction

This session reads Habakkuk 3 through Zephaniah 3; these notes cover Zephaniah, which closes the reading. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. Zephaniah comes in the days of Josiah king of Judah: the day of the Lord against Judah and Jerusalem, the charge against the nations round about, and then the turning of a pure language to the people, with the daughter of Zion told to sing because the King of Israel is in the midst of her.

- Woe to her that is filthy and polluted, to the oppressing city! She obeyed not the voice; she received not correction; she trusted not in the Lord; she drew not near to her God.
- Her princes within her are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves; her prophets are light and treacherous persons: her priests have polluted the sanctuary, they have done violence to the law.
- The just Lord is in the midst thereof; he will not do iniquity: every morning doth he bring his judgment to light, he faileth not; but the unjust knoweth no shame.
