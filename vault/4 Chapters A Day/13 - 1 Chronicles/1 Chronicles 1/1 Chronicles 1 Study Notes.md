# 1 Chronicles 1: The Generations from Adam to Edom

Read the chapter → [[1 Chronicles 1]]

After moving through the fall of Judah and the carrying away to Babylon in 2 Kings, the reading turns into [[1 Chronicles 1|1 Chronicles 1]]. This chapter is genealogy: it traces the line from Adam down through Noah and his sons, the nations that came from Japheth, Ham, and Shem, the line of Abraham through Isaac and Ishmael, and the sons of Esau and the dukes of Edom. The chapter was read as lineage with little added exposition, so the main entries below follow the verse order and hold the record of the generations as it stands.

- The line from the first man is set down in order: Adam, Seth, Enos, Cainan, Mahalaleel, Jered, Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, Noah, and the three sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
- This is the record of the generations from Adam to Noah, the same line preserved through the flood.

- The sons of Gomer and the sons of Javan are set down after him, tracing the nations that came out of Japheth.

- The chosen line is set down plainly: Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah, Eber, Peleg, Reu, Serug, Nahor, Terah, and Abram, who is Abraham.
- This narrows the whole record from Shem down to Abraham, the father of the promise.
