# Psalm 23: The Lord Is My Shepherd

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 23]]

This session is Scriptures 365, four chapters a day, reading [[Psalms 23|Psalm 23]] through [[Psalms 26|Psalm 26]], all Psalms of King David. Sisters cover your heads, brothers uncover, and we face Jerusalem, which from America is east, acknowledging that we are in captivity, that we are from Jerusalem, and that we will return there again to our homeland. [[Psalms 23|Psalm 23]] is a heavy chapter and gets extra time; the rest are read with the precepts brought out along the way. Everything written is for the Israelites, for our benefit, for us to study and understand.

**[[Psalms 23#^v1|Psalm 23:1]]**
![[Psalms 23#^v1]]
- The Lord is my shepherd, I shall not want. That word "want" means lack.

**[[Psalms 23#^v2|Psalm 23:2]]**
![[Psalms 23#^v2]]
- He maketh me to lie down in green pastures, He leadeth me beside the still waters. Let us look at what these green pastures are.
- The green pastures are peace and the beauty of the land: the grass, the cattle, the flowers of the field. It is going into the homeland, the glory of all lands, which is Jerusalem, Israel.
  - **[[Ezekiel 20#^v6|Ezekiel 20:6]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 20#^v6]]
    A land flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands, which is Jerusalem, our homeland; so laying in green pastures is going into peace.

**[[Psalms 23#^v3|Psalm 23:3]]**
![[Psalms 23#^v3]]
- He restoreth my soul and leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.

**[[Psalms 23#^v4|Psalm 23:4]]**
![[Psalms 23#^v4]]
- The valley of the shadow of death is talking about captivity. Though I walk through it I will fear no evil, for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff, they comfort me.
- The shadow of death is those bound in affliction and iron. So the valley of the shadow of death is America, the valley of dry bones, the Israelites waking up in the presence of their enemies.
  - **[[Psalms 107#^v10|Psalm 107:10]]**
    ![[Psalms 107#^v10]]
    Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron; that is the shadow of death, captivity.

**[[Psalms 23#^v5|Psalm 23:5]]**
![[Psalms 23#^v5]]
- Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies. Our enemies are the other nations, but more specifically the Edomite nations whose hands are still in America today.
- The table is the word of the Lord: His laws, statutes and commandments written in a book. It is our correction and our comfort in these last days.
- He prepares that table in the presence of the enemy by setting them in order before our eyes. The other nations do heinous things against us, they kill us, shoot us down, imprison us, put drugs in our neighborhoods and call it a "war on drugs" when they mean war on us, the Israelites.
- They claim to be a Christian nation but do the most killings; the Lord says He will reprove them and set them in order before our eyes, giving us the book, the correction and comfort, to rise up as a great army in the presence of our enemies.
  - **[[Isaiah 30#^v8|Isaiah 30:8]]**
    ![[Isaiah 30#^v8]]
    Now go, write it before them in a table and note it in a book; the "it" is the words of the Lord, His laws and commandments to get us right.
  - **[[Psalms 50#^v21|Psalm 50:21]]**
    ![[Psalms 50#^v21]]
    These things hast thou done and I kept silence; but I will reprove thee and set them in order before thine eyes, talking about the other nations.
  - **[[Revelation 11#^v11|Revelation 11:11]]**
    ![[Revelation 11#^v11]]
    After three days and a half the spirit of life from God entered into them and they stood upon their feet, and great fear fell upon them which saw them, in the presence of our enemies.

**[[Psalms 23#^v6|Psalm 23:6]]**
![[Psalms 23#^v6]]
- Thou anointest my head with oil, my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.
- We wake up through the words of life, not through the comedic community, not Egyptology, not Scientology, or whatever else is out there.

# Psalm 24: The King of Glory

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 24]]

**[[Psalms 24#^v1|Psalm 24:1]]**
![[Psalms 24#^v1]]
- The earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof, the world and they that dwell therein. This is not "the world is yours"; you barely have a pot to piss in and a window to throw it out of and brothers talk about the world is theirs. Sit down.
- You are not self-made. The scripture says the earth is the Lord's and the fullness thereof.

**[[Psalms 24#^v2|Psalm 24:2-3]]**
![[Psalms 24#^v2]]
![[Psalms 24#^v3]]
- For He hath founded it upon the seas and established it upon the floods. Who shall ascend into the hill of the Lord, and who shall stand in His holy place?

**[[Psalms 24#^v4|Psalm 24:4]]**
![[Psalms 24#^v4]]
- He that hath clean hands and a pure heart, who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity nor sworn deceitfully.
- Clean hands means he has not committed sins, or has repented from them. A pure heart is the mind: not pondering upon evil things, because out of the heart the mouth speaks, and once the mouth speaks it you give life to it and will eventually do it.
- Not lifting up the soul unto vanity nor swearing deceitfully means no lying, not telling a brother "I'ma pay you back" and never doing it.
  - **[[Mark 7#^v21|Mark 7:21]]**
    ![[Mark 7#^v21]]
    Out of the heart of man proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders, thefts and the rest; these come from within and defile the man, so a pure heart is not pondering these things.

**[[Psalms 24#^v5|Psalm 24:5-6]]**
![[Psalms 24#^v5]]
![[Psalms 24#^v6]]
- He shall receive the blessing from the Lord and righteousness from the God of his salvation. This is the generation of them that seek Him, that seek thy face, O Jacob.

**[[Psalms 24#^v7|Psalm 24:7-9]]**
![[Psalms 24#^v7]]
![[Psalms 24#^v8]]
![[Psalms 24#^v9]]
- Lift up your heads, O ye gates, and be ye lifted up, ye everlasting doors, and the King of glory shall come in.
- The gates are you leaders, you men of Israel, the men of the flock of His pasture. Open up yourselves to receive the Lord and repent; as scripture says, come nigh unto Me and I will come nigh unto you.

**[[Psalms 24#^v10|Psalm 24:10]]**
![[Psalms 24#^v10]]
- Who is this King of glory? The Lord of hosts, He is the King of glory. Selah.

# Psalm 25: Lift Up My Soul

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 25]]

**[[Psalms 25#^v1|Psalm 25:1-2]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v1]]
![[Psalms 25#^v2]]
- Unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul. O my God, I trust in thee; let me not be ashamed, let not mine enemies triumph over me.
- David was on another level. When we feel like our enemies are coming against us, our first move should be to lift up our soul to the Lord, but for a lot of us that is only the third or fourth step. First we get upset and offended; we run to the wife or the husband, we confront, we murmur and talk about the situation to a person we did not even have the situation with. David went straight to the Most High.

**[[Psalms 25#^v3|Psalm 25:3]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v3]]
- Let none that wait on thee be ashamed; let them be ashamed which transgress without cause.
- Some people just hate because they hate. It is not you they are hating on, it is the spirit within you; they cannot figure you out and cannot understand why you will not fall to their trickery, their words, their pulling and nagging.

**[[Psalms 25#^v4|Psalm 25:4-5]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v4]]
![[Psalms 25#^v5]]
- Show me thy ways, O Lord, teach me thy paths. Lead me in thy truth and teach me, for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day.

**[[Psalms 25#^v6|Psalm 25:6-7]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v6]]
![[Psalms 25#^v7]]
- Remember, O Lord, thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses, for they have been ever of old. Remember not the sins of my youth nor my transgressions.
- David was raised in this truth, yet he says forget the things I did when I was young in mind, young in spirit, young in stature. He is asking for tender mercy on the transgressions of his past.
- Just because we do not get judged immediately does not mean we will not eventually be judged for the sins we did when we were younger in the truth. David asks for mercy so that in his old age he can continue to trust the Lord and continue in this truth.

**[[Psalms 25#^v8|Psalm 25:8-9]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v8]]
![[Psalms 25#^v9]]
- Good and upright is the Lord, therefore will He teach sinners in the way; the way of righteousness, the way of correction. He teaches through judgment; when we break His commandment He spanks us with His correction.
- The meek will He guide in judgment and the meek will He teach His way, because the meek go into total submission to the laws of the Lord. If we get off track He corrects us and puts us back in the right path.
- Sometimes He cuts off the thing you love the most, the way you thought you were going to go, the power you thought you had, just so He can guide you, because we are a stiffnecked, hardheaded people.

**[[Psalms 25#^v10|Psalm 25:10]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v10]]
- All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth unto such as keep His covenant and His testimonies.
- A lot of people only know the Lord as judgment. A lot of us only want His mercy: "He's full of grace, He's full of mercy." But He is full of truth too. Repent or die; that is what He said.

**[[Psalms 25#^v11|Psalm 25:11-14]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v11]]
![[Psalms 25#^v12]]
![[Psalms 25#^v13]]
![[Psalms 25#^v14]]
- For thy name's sake, O Lord, pardon mine iniquity, for it is great. He recognizes his sins are great and asks forgiveness.
- What man is he that feareth the Lord? Him shall He teach in the way that he shall choose; his soul shall dwell at ease and his seed shall inherit the earth. The secret of the Lord is with them that fear Him, and He will show them His covenant.

**[[Psalms 25#^v15|Psalm 25:15]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v15]]
- Mine eyes are ever toward the Lord, for He shall pluck my feet out of the net.
- We get caught up in the system, the hustle and bustle, the day-to-day captivity: can't get off work, broken body. The Lord is not okay with us never keeping the Sabbath, never congregating, never seeing our families because of work.
- Keeping our eyes fixed on Him, keeping His commandments, praying and fasting that He make a way, He plucks our feet out of the net, out of the systems of America that try to entangle you in their philosophies and the upholding of their institutions.

**[[Psalms 25#^v16|Psalm 25:16-18]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v16]]
![[Psalms 25#^v17]]
![[Psalms 25#^v18]]
- Turn thou unto me and have mercy upon me, for I am desolate and afflicted. The troubles of my heart are enlarged; O bring thou me out of my distresses.
- The heart here is the mind. The troubles of the mind are enlarged: every time I take a breath, every time I walk outside, I think about something that troubles me. The brother could have gone into deep depression, but he prays to be brought out of his distresses.
- When we go through depression points, triggers in the marriage or triggers from when we were younger, things we have done and things done to us, he says look upon my affliction and my pain and forgive all my sins.

**[[Psalms 25#^v19|Psalm 25:19-21]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v19]]
![[Psalms 25#^v20]]
![[Psalms 25#^v21]]
- Consider mine enemies, for they are many, and they hate me with cruel hatred. O keep my soul and deliver me; let me not be ashamed, for I put my trust in thee.
- Let integrity and uprightness preserve me, for I wait on thee. Integrity is doing what you are supposed to do even when nobody is watching.

**[[Psalms 25#^v22|Psalm 25:22]]**
![[Psalms 25#^v22]]
- Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles. Not redeem the whole world, not just the Christianity faith; it says redeem Israel, save Israel out of all his troubles. That is Jacob's trouble, and that is the prayer for Israel.

# Psalm 26: I Have Walked in Mine Integrity

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 26]]

**[[Psalms 26#^v1|Psalm 26:1]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v1]]
- Judge me, O Lord, for I have walked in mine integrity; I have trusted also in the Lord, therefore I shall not slide. We slide back into the world when we do not trust the Most High.
- The employer asks you to work this Saturday. Are you going to keep the laws even when you know you need the money? Will you make the shekel great and the ephah small, or make the ephah, the laws of the Lord, great and the shekel small? Trust that the Lord is going to make a way.

**[[Psalms 26#^v2|Psalm 26:2]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v2]]
- Examine me, O Lord, and prove me; try my reins and my heart.
- When we examine ourselves we forget to ask the Lord to examine us. Lord, show me the things that are not right in me, especially going into a proving process or about to get married: show me what baggage I still have so I do not go into it with extra luggage.
- You are on Spirit Airlines trying to carry all this luggage to new heights, new levels, new destinations; guess what, you are going to pay for it, it is going to hit you if you are not ready.

**[[Psalms 26#^v3|Psalm 26:3-5]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v3]]
![[Psalms 26#^v4]]
![[Psalms 26#^v5]]
- For thy lovingkindness is before mine eyes and I have walked in thy truth. I have not sat with vain persons, neither will I go in with dissemblers.
- David did not hang around haters, complainers, or people always talking about other people. He did not hang around those complaining "this unleavened bread is not good, this Sabbath food is not good." He did not sit with vain persons who have no value to you in this truth, whether at the job, the gym, or even family members.
- I have hated the congregation of evildoers and will not sit with the wicked.

**[[Psalms 26#^v6|Psalm 26:6-7]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v6]]
![[Psalms 26#^v7]]
- I will wash mine hands in innocency, so will I compass thine altar, O Lord, that I may publish with the voice of thanksgiving and tell of all thy wondrous works.
- Washing your hands in innocency means you have nothing to do with it. A brother comes and says "she looking right, you think I should talk to her?" and you say, "I think you should keep the commandments, are you going to marry her?"
- I once told a brother straight up, "you a dog, bro, you dogging out your people, dogging out your sisters." He did not like it, stopped training with me, and I have not had a training partner in about three years, but I'll take it.

**[[Psalms 26#^v8|Psalm 26:8]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v8]]
- Lord, I have loved the habitation of thy house and the place where thine honour dwelleth. He is talking about Jerusalem, but we also have little sanctuaries: little congregations in Florida, Georgia, New York, Uganda, France, Russia.
- Honor is the keeping of the commandments of God, the love between a husband and wife, making marriages great again. David loved everything that came with the keeping of the commandments: congregating, seeing your brothers and sisters, going for counsel, getting corrected.
- A lot of us do not like going to the Sabbath, do not like congregating, hear the class and they are out. We have to love the habitation of His house; these are the things that continually build us up.
  - **[[Ezekiel 11#^v16|Ezekiel 11:16]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 11#^v16]]
    Although I have cast them far off among the heathen and scattered them among the countries, yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they shall come.

**[[Psalms 26#^v9|Psalm 26:9-11]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v9]]
![[Psalms 26#^v10]]
![[Psalms 26#^v11]]
- Gather not my soul with sinners, nor my life with bloody men, in whose hands is mischief and their right hand is full of bribes.
- But as for me, I will walk in mine integrity; redeem me and be merciful unto me.

**[[Psalms 26#^v12|Psalm 26:12]]**
![[Psalms 26#^v12]]
- My foot standeth in an even place; in the congregations will I bless the Lord.
- His foot standing in an even place means he is stable and steadfast in his truth, not tossed to and fro. There is no sleight of hand happening to him, no wind of doctrine throwing him around.
- The sleight of hand and cunning craftiness is the deception hitting our brothers and sisters: the sunrise sabbath, "you can eat whatever you want to." God is not about that.
- When he is able to congregate and be among his people, he blesses the Lord by keeping His laws, keeping His statutes, and going out declaring His glory to the children of Israel, showing them who they are, where they come from, and where they are going.
  - **[[Ephesians 4#^v12|Ephesians 4:12]]**
    ![[Ephesians 4#^v12]]
    The gifts are given for the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ, till we all come into the unity of the faith unto a perfect man.
  - **[[Ephesians 4#^v14|Ephesians 4:14]]**
    ![[Ephesians 4#^v14]]
    That we henceforth be no more children tossed to and fro and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the sleight of men and cunning craftiness whereby they lie in wait to deceive.
