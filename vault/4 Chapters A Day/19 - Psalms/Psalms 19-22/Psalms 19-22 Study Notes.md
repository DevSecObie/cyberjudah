# Psalm 19: The Perfect Law That Converts the Soul

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 19]]

This session covers [[Psalms 19|Psalms 19]] through 22, the last four chapters of the day's reading. It opens with the gospel going out to all the earth ([[Psalms 19|Psalm 19]]), digs deep into a basic precept about the law being perfect and converting the soul, moves through cleansing from secret and presumptuous sins, then into the Lord's deliverance and the destruction of the enemy (Psalms 20-21), and closes with [[Psalms 22|Psalm 22]], a prophecy fulfilled in Christ on the cross. We picked up out of Ezekiel where we left off before turning back to Psalms.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v2|Ezekiel 47:2]]**

![[Ezekiel 47#^v2]]

- The man with the line went forth eastward and measured, and the waters that ran out on the right side going out from the temple is the gospel going out, the man with the line measuring your understanding of the scriptures.
  - **[[Ezekiel 47#^v3|Ezekiel 47:3]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 47#^v3]]
    He measured a thousand cubits and brought me through the waters, and the waters were to the ankles; not only is the gospel going out, your understanding is being measured.

**[[Psalms 19#^v4|Psalm 19:4]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v4]]

- The line is gone out through all the earth, the gospel going out to the end of the world, and in them he set a tabernacle for the sun.
  - **[[Isaiah 62#^v5|Isaiah 62:5]]**
    ![[Isaiah 62#^v5]]
    As a young man marrieth a virgin so shall thy sons marry thee, and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride so shall thy God rejoice over thee; the gospel is about us being joined back to Christ.

**[[Psalms 19#^v6|Psalm 19:6]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v6]]

- His going forth is from the end of the heaven and his circuit unto the ends of it, and there is nothing hid from the heat thereof.

**[[Psalms 19#^v7|Psalm 19:7]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v7]]

- The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul; the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple. We hear this scripture all the time, so we are going to dig into it a little while we do these chapters.
- The law of the Lord is perfect means going into perfection with our lives.
- The law of the Lord converting the soul is what changes us. You do not just up and decide to follow the Bible; it takes conversion.
- The testimony of the Lord is sure because the prophecies in this Bible are sure; we have a more sure word of prophecy. Did we not go into slavery on ships like the Most High declared through Moses in [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]]? We are living proof that the Bible is real.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 34#^v8|Ecclesiasticus 34:8]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 34#^v8]]
    The law is perfect, taking us into perfection with our lives.
  - **[[Jeremiah 13#^v23|Jeremiah 13:23]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 13#^v23]]
    Can the Ethiopian change his skin or the leopard his spots? The Ethiopians are the dark nations of people; change does not just up and happen, so neither can you do good who are accustomed to do evil.
  - **[[2 Esdras 4#^v2|2 Esdras 4:2]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 4#^v2]]
    The angel tells Ezra his mind is gone too far in the world, so his thoughts are not our thoughts.
  - **[[Psalms 119#^v9|Psalm 119:9]]**
    ![[Psalms 119#^v9]]
    Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed thereto according to thy word; that is how we are converted and changed.
  - **[[Matthew 18#^v3|Matthew 18:3]]**
    ![[Matthew 18#^v3]]
    Except ye be converted and become as little children ye shall not enter the kingdom, because a child is teachable and will not argue back when it is time to learn.
  - **[[Matthew 18#^v4|Matthew 18:4]]**
    ![[Matthew 18#^v4]]
    Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven; we must humble ourselves as little children.

**[[Psalms 19#^v7|Psalm 19:7]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v7]]

- The testimony of the Lord is sure; the testimonies and prophecies in this Bible are sure.
  - **[[2 Peter 1#^v19|2 Peter 1:19]]**
    ![[2 Peter 1#^v19]]
    We have also a more sure word of prophecy; the testimony of the Lord is sure.
  - **[[Numbers 23#^v19|Numbers 23:19]]**
    ![[Numbers 23#^v19]]
    God is not a man that he should lie; hath he said, and shall he not do it? This is why the testimony is so sure, because he will perform his word.
  - **[[Isaiah 46#^v10|Isaiah 46:10]]**
    ![[Isaiah 46#^v10]]
    Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done; that is what prophecy is, and we have a sure word of it.

**[[Psalms 19#^v7|Psalm 19:7]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v7]]

- Making wise the simple: pay attention to who the simple is, the scorner, the foolish, the youth void of understanding. A lot of times we go out and think the scorner is somebody who does not want to hear, but it also goes into a youth who thinks he knows something but really does not.
  - **[[Proverbs 19#^v25|Proverbs 19:25]]**
    ![[Proverbs 19#^v25]]
    Smite a scorner and the simple will beware; the scorner is called simple, and these words are synonymous.
  - **[[Proverbs 21#^v11|Proverbs 21:11]]**
    ![[Proverbs 21#^v11]]
    When the scorner is punished the simple is made wise; that is making wise the simple.
  - **[[Proverbs 1#^v22|Proverbs 1:22]]**
    ![[Proverbs 1#^v22]]
    How long ye simple ones will ye love simplicity, and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge; simple, scorner and fool are synonyms.
  - **[[Proverbs 1#^v7|Proverbs 1:7]]**
    ![[Proverbs 1#^v7]]
    The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction.
  - **[[Proverbs 7#^v6|Proverbs 7:6-7]]**
    ![[Proverbs 7#^v6]]
    ![[Proverbs 7#^v7]]
    He looked through his casement and beheld among the simple ones a young man void of understanding; simple means you are void of understanding, the youth.
  - **[[Romans 2#^v20|Romans 2:20]]**
    ![[Romans 2#^v20]]
    An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes; there go the synonyms, foolish and babes and youth, so we must come back as a baby knowing we do not know anything.

**[[Psalms 19#^v8|Psalm 19:8]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v8]]

- The statutes of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the Lord is pure, enlightening the eyes. When the commandments enlighten your eyes you know where to walk, you will not stumble, you stay focused on keeping God's laws.
  - **[[Matthew 6#^v22|Matthew 6:22-23]]**
    ![[Matthew 6#^v22]]
    ![[Matthew 6#^v23]]
    The light of the body is the eye; if thine eye be single thy whole body is full of light, but if thine eye be evil thy body is full of darkness; keep the commandment as the apple of your eye.
  - **[[Proverbs 7#^v2|Proverbs 7:2]]**
    ![[Proverbs 7#^v2]]
    Keep my commandments and live, and my law as the apple of thine eye.
  - **[[Psalms 119#^v105|Psalm 119:105]]**
    ![[Psalms 119#^v105]]
    Thy word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path; the word enlightens your eyes and becomes a light to navigate life.

**[[Psalms 19#^v9|Psalm 19:9]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v9]]

- The fear of the Lord is clean, enduring for ever; the judgments of the Lord are true and righteous altogether. This whole Bible is the ways of the Lord, the judgments of the Lord, true and righteous altogether; if you want to know what righteousness is you have to learn and follow the Bible.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 32#^v4|Deuteronomy 32:4]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v4]]
    He is a rock, his work is perfect, for all his ways are judgment, a God of truth and without iniquity; all the ways of the Lord are judgment.
  - **[[Job 32#^v7|Job 32:7-9]]**
    ![[Job 32#^v7]]
    ![[Job 32#^v8]]
    ![[Job 32#^v9]]
    Days should speak and multitude of years should teach wisdom, but great men are not always wise, neither do the aged understand judgment; the multitude of years should have taught our people wisdom about the judgments, but they do not have it.

**[[Psalms 19#^v10|Psalm 19:10]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v10]]

- More to be desired are they than gold, yea than much fine gold, sweeter also than honey and the honeycomb; moreover by them is thy servant warned.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 12#^v12|Ecclesiastes 12:12]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 12#^v12]]
    By these, my son, be admonished; of making many books there is no end; you do not need to search out a lot of books thinking wisdom is missing, these books right here are what you need.
  - **[[Isaiah 34#^v16|Isaiah 34:16]]**
    ![[Isaiah 34#^v16]]
    Seek ye out of the book of the Lord and read; no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate, for his mouth it hath commanded; this Bible is a collection of our records.

**[[Psalms 19#^v12|Psalm 19:12]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v12]]

- Who can understand his errors? Cleanse thou me from secret faults. This should be your reason to be in this walk, not to appear righteous, but because God shall bring every work into judgment; work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.
  - **[[Psalms 139#^v23|Psalm 139:23-24]]**
    ![[Psalms 139#^v23]]
    ![[Psalms 139#^v24]]
    Search me O God and know my heart, try me and know my thoughts, and see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting; God knows your thoughts, so let him try you.
  - **[[Philippians 2#^v12|Philippians 2:12]]**
    ![[Philippians 2#^v12]]
    Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, now much more in his absence; this goes into cleansing you from secret faults.
  - **[[Psalms 4#^v4|Psalm 4:4]]**
    ![[Psalms 4#^v4]]
    Stand in awe and sin not, commune with your own heart upon your bed and be still; know yourself in your alone time and ask what you can do.
  - **[[1 Corinthians 16#^v15|1 Corinthians 16:15]]**
    ![[1 Corinthians 16#^v15]]
    The house of Stephanas addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints; studying in your alone time should become an addiction, a righteous addiction that replaces idleness.

**[[Psalms 19#^v13|Psalm 19:13]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v13]]

- Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me; then shall I be upright and innocent from the great transgression. A sin addiction has to be replaced with a righteous addiction, because change does not just up and happen when you are accustomed to doing evil.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v1|Ecclesiasticus 23:1-6]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v1]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v2]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v3]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v4]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v5]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 23#^v6]]
    A cry to have discipline set over your thoughts, lest ignorance increase to destruction and you commit that great transgression.

**[[Psalms 19#^v14|Psalm 19:14]]**

![[Psalms 19#^v14]]

- Let the words of my mouth and the meditation of my heart be acceptable in thy sight, O Lord, my strength and my redeemer.

# Psalm 20: The Lord Defends His Anointed

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 20]]

**[[Psalms 20#^v1|Psalm 20:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 20#^v1]]
![[Psalms 20#^v2]]

- The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee; send thee help from the sanctuary and strengthen thee out of Zion. When the time comes Christ is jumping down from heaven like he did in Egypt; we have to visualize this because it is a prophecy that will come true.
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v14|Wisdom of Solomon 18:14-16]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v14]]
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v15]]
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v16]]
    He is coming back with vengeance to defend us.

**[[Psalms 20#^v4|Psalm 20:4-5]]**

![[Psalms 20#^v4]]
![[Psalms 20#^v5]]

- Grant thee according to thine own heart and fulfil all thy counsel; we will rejoice in thy salvation and set up our banners; this goes into lifting up the banner on this mountain, the Bible.

**[[Psalms 20#^v6|Psalm 20:6]]**

![[Psalms 20#^v6]]

- Now know I that the Lord saveth his anointed; he will hear him from his holy heaven with the saving strength of his right hand. This is why we ask, to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed; his arm is not short, it can save.
  - **[[Isaiah 59#^v1|Isaiah 59:1-2]]**
    ![[Isaiah 59#^v1]]
    ![[Isaiah 59#^v2]]
    Behold the Lord's hand is not shortened that it cannot save, but your iniquities have separated between you and your God; that is the problem we face, not that his hand is short. Once we get right as a nation he will fight for us and get a name for himself.

**[[Psalms 20#^v7|Psalm 20:7]]**

![[Psalms 20#^v7]]

- Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we will remember the name of the Lord our God. Like today we trust in the shadow of Egypt still doing it; but we Israelites do not trust in oppression, we remember our God.
  - **[[Isaiah 31#^v1|Isaiah 31:1]]**
    ![[Isaiah 31#^v1]]
    Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help and stay on horses and trust in chariots, but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel; you trust and get comfortable in captivity, in America's army, thinking destruction is not coming to this place.
  - **[[Isaiah 30#^v12|Isaiah 30:12]]**
    ![[Isaiah 30#^v12]]
    Because ye despise this word and trust in oppression and perverseness and stay thereon; you trust in the white man's word that a woman can wear pants and be over the man, though the Most High never said it, and you trust in their perverseness.
  - **[[Isaiah 31#^v3|Isaiah 31:3]]**
    ![[Isaiah 31#^v3]]
    Now the Egyptians are men and not God, and their horses flesh and not spirit; the white man is just a man, he is not God, and their army has nothing on God's army.

**[[Psalms 20#^v8|Psalm 20:8-9]]**

![[Psalms 20#^v8]]
![[Psalms 20#^v9]]

- They are brought down and fallen, but we are risen and stand upright; save, Lord, let the king hear us when we call.

![[Psalms 21#^v1]]
![[Psalms 21#^v2]]
![[Psalms 21#^v3]]

- Be thou exalted, Lord, in thine own strength; so will we sing and praise thy power.

# Psalm 22: The Prophecy of Christ on the Cross

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 22]]

This chapter is a prophecy about Christ, familiar to many, so we touch on a few points. It is the last chapter of the four-chapter reading.

**[[Psalms 22#^v1|Psalm 22:1]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v1]]

- My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? Why art thou so far from helping me? This is what Christ said when he was on the cross; Christ did not come to do away with the law but to fulfil it.
  - **[[Luke 24#^v44|Luke 24:44]]**
    ![[Luke 24#^v44]]
    All things must be fulfilled which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the Psalms, concerning me; Christ had to fulfil the prophecies written about him.
  - **[[Matthew 27#^v45|Matthew 27:45-46]]**
    ![[Matthew 27#^v45]]
    ![[Matthew 27#^v46]]
    From the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land, and about the ninth hour Jesus cried, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani, that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me; this was fulfilled on the cross.

**[[Psalms 22#^v2|Psalm 22:2-5]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v2]]
![[Psalms 22#^v3]]
![[Psalms 22#^v4]]
![[Psalms 22#^v5]]

- I cry in the daytime but thou hearest not, but thou art holy; our fathers trusted in thee and thou didst deliver them; they cried unto thee and were delivered.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 2#^v10|Ecclesiasticus 2:10]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 2#^v10]]
    If you abide in the fear of God you will not be forsaken.

**[[Psalms 22#^v6|Psalm 22:6-8]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v6]]
![[Psalms 22#^v7]]
![[Psalms 22#^v8]]

- But I am a worm and no man, a reproach of men and despised of the people; all they that see me laugh me to scorn, saying, He trusted on the Lord that he would deliver him, let him deliver him. We read that same mockery in Matthew when they said, If he be the Son of God let God deliver him.

**[[Psalms 22#^v9|Psalm 22:9-11]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v9]]
![[Psalms 22#^v10]]
![[Psalms 22#^v11]]

- But thou art he that took me out of the womb; thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts. Christ was on his mother's breast drinking milk growing up until he was of age, and she taught him the commandments; even filled with the Holy Ghost, [[Deuteronomy 6#^v7|Deuteronomy 6:7]] still applied in their household, which is why at twelve he was in the temple.
  - **[[2 Esdras 8#^v8|2 Esdras 8:8-12]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 8#^v8]]
    ![[2 Esdras 8#^v9]]
    ![[2 Esdras 8#^v10]]
    ![[2 Esdras 8#^v11]]
    ![[2 Esdras 8#^v12]]
    You are fed milk until you can talk, then you start learning the commandments.

**[[Psalms 22#^v14|Psalm 22:14-17]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v14]]
![[Psalms 22#^v15]]
![[Psalms 22#^v16]]
![[Psalms 22#^v17]]

- I am poured out like water and all my bones are out of joint; my strength is dried up, they pierced my hands and my feet, I may tell all my bones. His soul was made a sin offering; they pierced his hands and feet as they did to Christ, and because he was already dead none of his bones were broken, so the prophecy that he could tell all his bones was fulfilled.
  - **[[Isaiah 53#^v10|Isaiah 53:10]]**
    ![[Isaiah 53#^v10]]
    Yet it pleased the Lord to bruise him, when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin; his soul was made a sin offering, and he shall see his seed and prolong his days.

**[[Psalms 22#^v18|Psalm 22:18]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v18]]

- They part my garments among them and cast lots upon my vesture; but be not thou far from me, O Lord.

**[[Psalms 22#^v22|Psalm 22:22-24]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v22]]
![[Psalms 22#^v23]]
![[Psalms 22#^v24]]

- I will declare thy name unto my brethren; Christ is the one who declared the word of God to us. He did not hold back; he came, preached, and laid down his life. He hath not despised the affliction of the afflicted; when he cried unto him he heard, so the Lord will hear our prayers.

**[[Psalms 22#^v26|Psalm 22:26-28]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v26]]
![[Psalms 22#^v27]]
![[Psalms 22#^v28]]

- The meek shall eat and be satisfied; all the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the Lord, for the kingdom is the Lord's and he is the governor among the nations; that is why the scripture says the government shall be upon his shoulder.

**[[Psalms 22#^v30|Psalm 22:30-31]]**

![[Psalms 22#^v30]]
![[Psalms 22#^v31]]

- A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation; they shall come and declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this. This answers the question Isaiah asked about who shall declare his generation.
  - **[[Isaiah 53#^v1|Isaiah 53:1-3]]**
    ![[Isaiah 53#^v1]]
    ![[Isaiah 53#^v2]]
    ![[Isaiah 53#^v3]]
    Who hath believed our report, and to whom is the arm of the Lord revealed? He is despised and rejected of men, a man of sorrows; the arm of the Lord is Christ, the power and salvation of the Lord.
  - **[[Isaiah 53#^v8|Isaiah 53:8]]**
    ![[Isaiah 53#^v8]]
    He was taken from prison and from judgment, and who shall declare his generation? For he was cut off out of the land of the living for the transgression of my people; the seed serving him and accounted for a generation answers that question.
