# Psalm 59: The Mind of a King Under Affliction

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 59]]

This session reads through [[Psalms 59|Psalms 59]] through 62, four chapters a day for the nation. The teacher notes up front that the chapters are extremely short, so the purpose is mostly to read and give as much explanation as needed without doing too much. [[Psalms 59|Psalm 59]] is set during the time when Saul sent men to watch David's house to kill him. The teaching holds this up as the mind we should have when we are going through affliction: with people watching us, nations watching us, the heathen raging, and our own people hating us, these are the prayers that need to come out of our spirit. Stay in the spirit.

**[[Psalms 59#^v1|Psalms 59:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 59#^v1]]
![[Psalms 59#^v2]]

- The superscription places this at the time Saul sent men who watched the house to kill David. Saul and his men were watching David, his ins and outs, to try to kill him, and this is what David was going through.
- David cries for the Most High to deliver him from his enemies and defend him from those that rise up against him, and to save him from the workers of iniquity and the bloody men.

**[[Psalms 59#^v3|Psalms 59:3-5]]**

![[Psalms 59#^v3]]
![[Psalms 59#^v4]]
![[Psalms 59#^v5]]

- The mighty are gathered against David, lying in wait for his soul, not for his transgression nor his sin. They run and prepare themselves without any fault of his.
- He calls on the Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, to awake to visit all the heathen and to be not merciful to any wicked transgressors.

**[[Psalms 59#^v6|Psalms 59:6-8]]**

![[Psalms 59#^v6]]
![[Psalms 59#^v7]]
![[Psalms 59#^v8]]

- The enemies return at evening, make a noise like a dog, and go around about the city; they belch out with their mouth, and swords are in their lips, thinking no one hears.
- But the Most High laughs at them and holds all the heathen in derision.

**[[Psalms 59#^v9|Psalms 59:9-11]]**

![[Psalms 59#^v9]]
![[Psalms 59#^v10]]
![[Psalms 59#^v11]]

- Because of the enemy's strength, David waits upon the Most High, who is his defense and the God of his mercy.
- He asks that they not be slain outright lest his people forget, but scattered by the power of the Most High and brought down.

**[[Psalms 59#^v12|Psalms 59:12-13]]**

![[Psalms 59#^v12]]
![[Psalms 59#^v13]]

- For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips, let them be taken in their pride, in their cursing and lying.
- The heavy point: let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. That is a heavy point.

**[[Psalms 59#^v14|Psalms 59:14-17]]**

![[Psalms 59#^v14]]
![[Psalms 59#^v15]]
![[Psalms 59#^v16]]
![[Psalms 59#^v17]]

- Again the enemies return at evening making a noise like a dog, wandering up and down for meat, grudging if they are not satisfied.
- But David will sing of the power of the Most High and sing aloud of His mercy in the morning, for He has been his defense and refuge in the day of trouble.
- This is the mind of King David, and the type of mind we should have when going through affliction. With people watching us, nations watching us, the heathen raging, and our own people hating us, these are the prayers that need to come out of our spirit. Stay in the spirit.

# Psalm 60: Through God We Shall Do Valiantly

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 60]]

[[Psalms 60|Psalm 60]] is a psalm of David written when he strove with Aram-naharaim and Aram-zobah, when Joab returned and smote twelve thousand of Edom in the valley of salt. The teacher points out that the brief synopsis at the top of the psalm in the King James gives you the understanding of why David is saying what he is saying, and during what time he is saying it; make sure you are reading those. The psalm moves from a nation cast off and scattered to the confidence that vain is the help of man, but through God we shall do valiantly.

**[[Psalms 60#^v1|Psalms 60:1-3]]**

![[Psalms 60#^v1]]
![[Psalms 60#^v2]]
![[Psalms 60#^v3]]

- David says the Most High has cast us off and scattered us, has been displeased, and asks Him to turn to us again.
- The Most High made the earth to tremble and broke it; David asks Him to heal the breaches, for it is shaken, and He has showed His people hard things and made them drink the wine of astonishment.

**[[Psalms 60#^v4|Psalms 60:4-6]]**

![[Psalms 60#^v4]]
![[Psalms 60#^v5]]
![[Psalms 60#^v6]]

- The Most High has given a banner to them that fear Him, that it may be displayed because of the truth, so that His beloved may be delivered by His right hand.
- God has spoken in His holiness that He will rejoice, divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

**[[Psalms 60#^v7|Psalms 60:7-9]]**

![[Psalms 60#^v7]]
![[Psalms 60#^v8]]
![[Psalms 60#^v9]]

- Gilead is His, Manasseh is His, Ephraim is the strength of His hand, and Judah is His lawgiver; Moab is His washpot, over Edom He casts out His shoe.
- He asks who will bring him into the strong city and who will lead him into Edom.

**[[Psalms 60#^v10|Psalms 60:10-12]]**

![[Psalms 60#^v10]]
![[Psalms 60#^v11]]
![[Psalms 60#^v12]]

- David asks whether it is not the Most High, who had cast them off and did not go out with their armies, who will give help from trouble, for vain is the help of man.
- Through God we shall do valiantly, for it is He that shall tread down our enemies.

# Psalm 61: Lead Me to the Rock That Is Higher Than I

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 61]]

[[Psalms 61|Psalm 61]] is a psalm of David for the chief musician. It is the cry of a man whose heart is overwhelmed, asking to be led to the rock that is higher than himself, to abide in the tabernacle of the Most High and trust in the covert of His wings. The teaching draws out what it means that David was a man after the Most High's own heart: he constantly prayed, constantly sought out the doings of the Most High, and kept repenting from the wrong that he did.

**[[Psalms 61#^v1|Psalms 61:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 61#^v1]]
![[Psalms 61#^v2]]
![[Psalms 61#^v3]]
![[Psalms 61#^v4]]

- David asks the Most High to hear his cry and attend to his prayer; from the end of the earth he will cry when his heart is overwhelmed, asking to be led to the rock that is higher than himself.
- The Most High has been a shelter for him and a strong tower from the enemy; he will abide in the tabernacle forever and trust in the covert of His wings.

**[[Psalms 61#^v5|Psalms 61:5-8]]**

![[Psalms 61#^v5]]
![[Psalms 61#^v6]]
![[Psalms 61#^v7]]
![[Psalms 61#^v8]]

- The Most High has heard David's vows and given him the heritage of those that fear His name, and will prolong the king's life as many generations.
- The king shall abide before God forever; so David will sing praise unto His name forever and daily perform his vows.
- This is what it means that David was a man after the Most High's own heart. He constantly prayed and constantly sought out the doings of the Most High. Was David free from any guilt? No. But he kept getting himself together, kept praying, and kept repenting from the wrong that he did. That is one thing that made him perfect.

# Psalm 62: Let Your Expectation Be of God

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 62]]

[[Psalms 62|Psalm 62]] is the last chapter of the session, a psalm of David to Jeduthun. The soul waits only upon God, who alone is the rock, the salvation, and the defense. The teacher lifts up verse 5 as the heavy point: a lot of us expect things from men and expect people not to let us down, but let your expectations be of God as David's expectations were of God. You cannot change anybody, but God can. Expect great things from the Most High and you do great things yourself.

**[[Psalms 62#^v1|Psalms 62:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 62#^v1]]
![[Psalms 62#^v2]]
![[Psalms 62#^v3]]
![[Psalms 62#^v4]]

- Truly David's soul waits upon God, from whom comes his salvation; He only is his rock and salvation and defense, so he shall not be greatly moved.
- He rebukes those who imagine mischief against a man, who shall be slain like a bowing wall and a tottering fence; they consult to cast him down from his excellency, delight in lies, and bless with their mouth but curse inwardly.

**[[Psalms 62#^v5|Psalms 62:5-8]]**

![[Psalms 62#^v5]]
![[Psalms 62#^v6]]
![[Psalms 62#^v7]]
![[Psalms 62#^v8]]

- David tells his own soul to wait only upon God, for his expectation is from Him; He only is his rock, salvation, defense, glory, and refuge.
- A lot of us expect things from men and expect people not to let us down. "I expected more of you," "I expected you to be this way," and that is your problem. Let your expectations be of God, as David's expectations were of God. You cannot change anybody, but God can.
- Trust in Him at all times, pour out your heart before Him, for God is a refuge for us.

**[[Psalms 62#^v9|Psalms 62:9-12]]**

![[Psalms 62#^v9]]
![[Psalms 62#^v10]]
![[Psalms 62#^v11]]
![[Psalms 62#^v12]]

- Men of low degree are vanity and men of high degree are a lie; laid in the balance they are altogether lighter than vanity. Trust not in oppression, become not vain in robbery, and if riches increase, set not your heart upon them.
- God has spoken once, twice have we heard this: that power belongs to God, and to Him belongs mercy, for He renders to every man according to his work.
- Expect great things from the Most High and you do great things yourself.
