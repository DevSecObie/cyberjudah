# Psalm 103: Bless the Lord for His Benefits to Israel

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 103]]

This session moves through [[Psalms 103|Psalms 103]] through 106, four psalms of remembrance and praise. It opens with David blessing the Most High for His mercy toward Israel, moves through the Most High's care over creation in [[Psalms 104|Psalm 104]], and then recounts the nation's history from Abraham through the wilderness in [[Psalms 105|Psalms 105]] and 106. The thread throughout is that the mercy, grace, and covenant of the Most High belong to Israel, and that this mercy is not a license to sin but a call to keep His commandments. Christianity is a curse ([[Deuteronomy 28#^v64|Deuteronomy 28:64]]); the grace of the Most High was given so that when the Holy Ghost reminds us of His commandments, we apply them to our lives.

- David charges his own soul and all that is within him to bless the holy name of the Most High and to forget not His benefits.
- Those benefits are named: He forgives all iniquities, heals diseases, redeems life from destruction, crowns with lovingkindness and tender mercies, and renews the youth like the eagle's.
- The Most High executes righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed. Hear that clearly: it does not say for all people, it says for all that are oppressed.
