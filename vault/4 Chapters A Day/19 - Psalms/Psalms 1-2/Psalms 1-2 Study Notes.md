# Psalm 1: Blessed Is the Man That Walketh Not in the Counsel of the Ungodly

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 1]]

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session moves from the close of Job into [[Psalms 1|Psalms 1]] and 2. [[Psalms 1|Psalm 1]] contrasts the blessed man who delights in the law of the Most High and meditates on it day and night with the ungodly who are like chaff the wind drives away. [[Psalms 2|Psalm 2]] turns to the heathen raging against the Most High and against His anointed, and to the promise that Christ, His anointed, will rule Israel and give His people the heathen for their inheritance and the uttermost parts of the earth for their possession.

**[[Psalms 1#^v1|Psalm 1:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 1#^v1]]
![[Psalms 1#^v2]]

- Basic reading shows there are only particular ways to be blessed. Blessed is the man that walks not in the counsel of the ungodly, meaning he does not walk in wicked ways such as eating pork, and in this season the counsel of the ungodly is Christmas, Thanksgiving, Valentine's Day, and New Year's, all the holidays coming up. The music has been playing in every store for months, so a man has to be very mindful of what he does around this season.
- Blessed is the man that walks not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor stands in the way of sinners, nor sits in the seat of the scornful. Instead his delight is in the law of the Most High, and in His law he meditates day and night.
- We have to get in the habit of meditating on the commandments day and night, not as a second thought but in and out all the time, and if you are not there, work on getting there by praying, fasting, and studying. The more you read the Bible, the easier it becomes to keep your mind in it.

**[[Psalms 1#^v3|Psalm 1:3-6]]**

![[Psalms 1#^v3]]
![[Psalms 1#^v4]]
![[Psalms 1#^v5]]
![[Psalms 1#^v6]]

- The blessed man shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water that brings forth his fruit in his season, his leaf shall not wither, and whatsoever he does shall prosper. You will be successful and prosper if you actually meditate in the commandments day and night and do them, not merely think about scriptures the way many of us did in Christianity.
- The ungodly are not so, meaning they will not prosper like the righteous, but are like the chaff which the wind drives away. Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.
- The Most High knows the way of the righteous, but the way of the ungodly shall perish. This is a reminder as we walk through these last days not to roll with ungodly people or anyone who is halfway in and halfway out, because the Most High deals only with those walking righteously. You cannot excuse a work Christmas party as just appeasing people, because the righteous do everything they can to walk in the way of the godly and make no excuses.
  - **[[Joshua 1#^v8|Joshua 1:8]]**
    ![[Joshua 1#^v8]]
    Meditate in the law day and night and observe to do it, and then your way shall be prosperous and you shall have good success, which is the same promise given to the blessed man.

# Psalm 2: Why Do the Heathen Rage

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 2]]

**[[Psalms 2#^v1|Psalm 2:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 2#^v1]]
![[Psalms 2#^v2]]
![[Psalms 2#^v3]]
![[Psalms 2#^v4]]

- Why do the heathen rage and the people imagine a vain thing? The heathen are the other nations, and they rage because they see our people waking up. Leadership has taught many classes on this chapter, because when the nations see us teaching they show a lot of hatred toward our people, even against the unrighteous among our people.
- The kings of the earth set themselves and the rulers take counsel together against the Most High and against His anointed, saying let us break their bands asunder and cast away their cords from us. These nations are looking for our destruction, wanting our people to stay on the bottom, and they keep us in a sleep state.
- He that sits in the heavens shall laugh and hold them in derision. Christmas, Thanksgiving, and all these holidays are another chance for the nations to put us to sleep, because their rage drives them to keep us the way we are, since they understand that if we get back in power everything they have done to us will be put back on them.

**[[Psalms 2#^v5|Psalm 2:5-6]]**

![[Psalms 2#^v5]]
![[Psalms 2#^v6]]

- Then shall He speak unto them in His wrath and vex them in His sore displeasure. The Most High is letting us know He will have the last laugh, so even though these nations are mocking and oppressing us and putting us through all types of hell, at the end of the day He will have the last laugh, even if you are killed here on this earth.
- Yet have I set my king upon my holy hill of Zion. Zion is another word for Israel, the term the Most High uses to refer to His people, and the king set upon the holy hill is Christ, set to be the ruler of His people Israel.
  - **[[Matthew 2#^v6|Matthew 2:6]]**
    ![[Matthew 2#^v6]]
    Out of Bethlehem shall come a Governor that shall rule the people Israel, which is Christ, the king the Most High set upon His holy hill.

**[[Psalms 2#^v7|Psalm 2:7-9]]**

![[Psalms 2#^v7]]
![[Psalms 2#^v8]]
![[Psalms 2#^v9]]

- The Most High said unto Him, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee, which gives more clues that this is talking about Christ. Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.
- This is the promise we get: just as Job received much more than he went into his trial with, our walk in this life is a trial, and we will receive much more, including the heathen for our inheritance and the earth for our possession, the same inheritance Christ receives He gives to us.
- Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron and dash them in pieces like a potter's vessel. Things are going back the way they were supposed to be in the old times, back when we had laws in Leviticus, Numbers, and Deuteronomy on having servants and dealing with these other nations. The heathen round about will be our plow men who build up the kingdom and the walls of Jerusalem.
  - **[[Leviticus 25#^v44|Leviticus 25:44]]**
    ![[Leviticus 25#^v44]]
    Both thy bondmen and thy bondmaids shall be of the heathen round about you, the same heathen that rage in Psalm 2:1.
  - **[[Leviticus 25#^v45|Leviticus 25:45]]**
    ![[Leviticus 25#^v45]]
    Of the children of the strangers that sojourn among you shall ye buy, and they shall be your possession.
  - **[[Leviticus 25#^v46|Leviticus 25:46]]**
    ![[Leviticus 25#^v46]]
    Ye shall take them as an inheritance for your children forever, so the promise stands even though it has been delayed because our people broke the commandments.

**[[Psalms 2#^v10|Psalm 2:10-12]]**

![[Psalms 2#^v10]]
![[Psalms 2#^v11]]
![[Psalms 2#^v12]]

- Be wise now therefore, O ye kings, be instructed ye judges of the earth. The Most High is being clear who His judges are, the judges of the earth. Serve the Most High with fear and rejoice with trembling.
- Kiss the Son lest He be angry and ye perish from the way when His wrath is kindled but a little, and blessed are all they that put their trust in Him. To kiss the Son means to worship the Son, who is Christ mentioned in verse seven, so if you worship the Son you are blessed, but if you do not you will perish.
  - **First Kings 19:18**  *18 Yet I have left me seven thousand in Israel, all the knees which have not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him.*  The Most High left seven thousand in Israel who had not bowed unto Baal, and every mouth which hath not kissed him, showing that to kiss means to worship.
  - **[[Acts 3#^v22|Acts 3:22-23]]**
    ![[Acts 3#^v22]]
    ![[Acts 3#^v23]]
    Moses said a Prophet shall the Most High raise up, and every soul that will not hear that Prophet shall be destroyed, which is Christ, the same warning as kissing the Son lest you perish.
