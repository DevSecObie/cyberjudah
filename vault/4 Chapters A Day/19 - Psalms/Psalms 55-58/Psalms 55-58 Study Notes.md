# Psalm 55: Cast Thy Burden Upon the Lord

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 55]]

This session moves through [[Psalms 55|Psalms 55]] through 58, read for the nation four chapters a day out of the Orlando camp. [[Psalms 55|Psalm 55]] is the cry of a man surrounded by the voice of the enemy and the oppression of the wicked, longing to fly away like a dove and be at rest, yet betrayed by his own acquaintance. The teaching drives home that we have to put ourselves in the scriptures and see our own condition in them: even when we come into keeping the commandments we still suffer the same oppression, the violence and strife in the city, the betrayal that King David and Christ both endured. The answer is not escape but endurance, casting the burden upon the Most High who will sustain the righteous.

![[Psalms 55#^v1]]
![[Psalms 55#^v2]]

- David asks the Most High to give ear to his prayer and not hide from his supplication. You have to put yourself in the Bible and see yourself in these scriptures, the way Elder Kena, Bishop, and the leadership men teach.
- He mourns and makes a noise because of the voice of the enemy and the oppression of the wicked, who cast iniquity upon him and hate him in wrath.
- The way to get the Most High to hear our prayers is righteousness. His eyes are over the righteous and His ears are open to their prayers, but His face is against them that do evil.

- The fearfulness and trembling and horror are written in the curses, the curses we did not learn in the Christian church. Many of the scriptures come back to these curses.

# Psalm 56: I Will Not Fear What Flesh Can Do Unto Me

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 56]]

[[Psalms 56|Psalm 56]] is the confidence of a man daily swallowed up and fought against by many, who resolves to trust the Most High and not fear what flesh can do. The teaching applies this to Christ, whom the scribes and Pharisees followed daily to catch Him in His words, and to the enemies of the truth today who come against IUIC. The answer is the same: put all your trust in the Most High, hold the line, and be a light to your brothers and sisters.

**[[Psalms 56#^v1|Psalms 56:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 56#^v1]]
![[Psalms 56#^v2]]
![[Psalms 56#^v3]]
![[Psalms 56#^v4]]

- He asks for mercy because man would swallow him up, fighting daily and oppressing him, for many fight against him. What time he is afraid, he will trust in the Most High.
- In God he praises His word and puts his trust, and will not fear what flesh can do. This does not mean you never experience fear, but you will not put the fear of man over the fear of God or let it stand between you and the mission.
  - **[[Matthew 10#^v28|Matthew 10:28]]**
    ![[Matthew 10#^v28]]
    Fear not them that kill the body but cannot kill the soul; rather fear Him who can destroy both soul and body in hell.

**[[Psalms 56#^v5|Psalms 56:5-9]]**

![[Psalms 56#^v5]]
![[Psalms 56#^v6]]
![[Psalms 56#^v7]]
![[Psalms 56#^v8]]
![[Psalms 56#^v9]]

- Every day they wrest his words and all their thoughts are against him for evil; they gather, hide, and mark his steps, waiting for his soul. This is David in the spirit foreseeing what would happen to Christ.
- He asks whether they shall escape by iniquity, and trusts that the Most High tells his wanderings and keeps his tears in a bottle, and that his enemies shall turn back because God is for him.
  - **[[Luke 11#^v53|Luke 11:53-54]]**
    ![[Luke 11#^v53]]
    ![[Luke 11#^v54]]
    The scribes and Pharisees provoked Christ, laying wait to catch something out of His mouth to accuse Him, wicked enough to provoke the Son of the living God.

**[[Psalms 56#^v10|Psalms 56:10-13]]**

![[Psalms 56#^v10]]
![[Psalms 56#^v11]]
![[Psalms 56#^v12]]
![[Psalms 56#^v13]]

- He praises the word of the Most High and puts his trust in Him, not afraid of what man can do, rendering praises for the vows upon him.
- The Most High delivered his soul from death and will deliver his feet from falling, that he may walk before God in the light of the living. Put all your trust in the Most High.
- People are always trying to come against IUIC, but you hold the line, continue to study, keep the commandments, and be a light unto your brothers even when they walk in wickedness.

# Psalm 57: My Heart Is Fixed

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 57]]

[[Psalms 57|Psalm 57]] makes the shadow of the Most High's wings a refuge until the calamities be overpast. The teaching locates that refuge in meditating on the scriptures day and night, in the secret place of the Most High, and lifts up the deliverance He sends from heaven, illustrated by the angels that scourged Heliodorus in 2 Maccabees. The heart that is fixed is the tree planted by the rivers of water that will not be swayed by doctrines, looks, or money, and no one takes its crown.

**[[Psalms 57#^v1|Psalms 57:1]]**

![[Psalms 57#^v1]]

- He asks for mercy, for his soul trusts in the Most High, and makes his refuge in the shadow of His wings until the calamities be overpast.
- Where should we make our refuge? It begins with meditating on the scriptures, which is why the four chapters a day has been set up.

    ![[Joshua 1#^v8]]

# Psalm 58: The Righteous Shall Rejoice When He Seeth the Vengeance

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 58]]

[[Psalms 58|Psalm 58]] calls out the wicked congregation that weighs violence in the earth, estranged from the womb and full of the poison of a serpent, and asks the Most High to break their teeth. The teaching lands on the vengeance to come: the righteous washing his feet in the blood of the wicked, tied to Isaiah's treader of the winepress and to Ezekiel's burial of Gog, when the house of Israel will spend seven months burying the multitude and know that there is a God that judges the earth.

**[[Psalms 58#^v1|Psalms 58:1-5]]**

![[Psalms 58#^v1]]
![[Psalms 58#^v2]]
![[Psalms 58#^v3]]
![[Psalms 58#^v4]]
![[Psalms 58#^v5]]

- He challenges the congregation whether they speak righteousness and judge uprightly, when in heart they work wickedness and weigh the violence of their hands in the earth.
- The wicked are estranged from the womb and go astray as soon as they are born, speaking lies; their poison is like a serpent's, like the deaf adder that will not hearken to the charmer.

**[[Psalms 58#^v6|Psalms 58:6-9]]**

![[Psalms 58#^v6]]
![[Psalms 58#^v7]]
![[Psalms 58#^v8]]
![[Psalms 58#^v9]]

- He asks the Most High to break their teeth and the great teeth of the young lions, to let them melt away as waters that run continually and pass away like a snail that melts.
- Before the pots can feel the thorns, He shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living and in His wrath.

**[[Psalms 58#^v10|Psalms 58:10-11]]**

![[Psalms 58#^v10]]
![[Psalms 58#^v11]]

- The righteous shall rejoice when he sees the vengeance and wash his feet in the blood of the wicked, so that a man shall say there is a reward for the righteous and a God that judges the earth.
- The Most High is going to lay His vengeance upon Edom by the hand of His people Israel, His battle axe, and there will be so much death that everyone has to pitch in and bury them for seven months. That is a beautiful thing.

  - **[[Ezekiel 25#^v14|Ezekiel 25:14]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 25#^v14]]
    The Most High will lay His vengeance upon Edom by the hand of His people Israel, and they shall know His vengeance.
  - **[[Ezekiel 39#^v1|Ezekiel 39:1,11-15]]**
