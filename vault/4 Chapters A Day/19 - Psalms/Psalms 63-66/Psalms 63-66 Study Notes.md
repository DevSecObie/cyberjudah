# Psalm 63: Early Will I Seek Thee

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 63]]

This session reads [[Psalms 63|Psalms 63]] through 66, four chapters a day, called relatively short chapters with a lot to go into. This is a reading recording with brief exposition: the summary given is that these psalms are David giving praise to the Most High and showing the workings of the wicked, which the bishop touches on all the time. [[Psalms 63|Psalm 63]] is the thirst of the soul for the Most High in a dry and thirsty land, the praise that is better than life, and the promise that those who seek the soul to destroy it go down into the lower parts of the earth.

**[[Psalms 63#^v1|Psalms 63:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 63#^v1]]
![[Psalms 63#^v2]]

- David seeks the Most High early; his soul thirsts and his flesh longs for Him in a dry and thirsty land where no water is.
- He longs to see the power and glory of the Most High as he has seen it in the sanctuary.

**[[Psalms 63#^v3|Psalms 63:3-5]]**

![[Psalms 63#^v3]]
![[Psalms 63#^v4]]
![[Psalms 63#^v5]]

- Because the loving kindness of the Most High is better than life, David's lips shall praise Him, and he will bless Him while he lives and lift up his hands in His name.
- His soul shall be satisfied with marrow and fatness, and his mouth shall praise Him with joyful lips.

**[[Psalms 63#^v6|Psalms 63:6-8]]**

![[Psalms 63#^v6]]
![[Psalms 63#^v7]]
![[Psalms 63#^v8]]

- When he remembers the Most High upon his bed and meditates on Him in the night watches, he rejoices in the shadow of His wings because He has been his help.
- His soul follows hard after the Most High, and His right hand upholds him.

**[[Psalms 63#^v9|Psalms 63:9-11]]**

![[Psalms 63#^v9]]
![[Psalms 63#^v10]]
![[Psalms 63#^v11]]

- Those that seek his soul to destroy it shall go down into the lower parts of the earth; they shall fall by the sword and be a portion for foxes.
- The king shall rejoice in the Most High, and everyone that swears by Him shall glory, but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.

# Psalm 64: Hide Me From the Secret Counsel of the Wicked

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 64]]

[[Psalms 64|Psalm 64]] is a prayer to be preserved from the fear of the enemy and hidden from the secret counsel of the wicked. It sets out the workings of the wicked who whet their tongue like a sword and shoot bitter words in secret, and it declares that the Most High shall shoot at them with an arrow so that their own tongue falls upon themselves.

**[[Psalms 64#^v1|Psalms 64:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 64#^v1]]
![[Psalms 64#^v2]]

- David asks the Most High to hear his voice in prayer and preserve his life from fear of the enemy.
- He asks to be hidden from the secret counsel of the wicked and from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity.

**[[Psalms 64#^v3|Psalms 64:3-6]]**

![[Psalms 64#^v3]]
![[Psalms 64#^v4]]
![[Psalms 64#^v5]]
![[Psalms 64#^v6]]

- The wicked whet their tongue like a sword and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words, that they may shoot in secret at the perfect and suddenly, without fear.
- They encourage themselves in an evil matter, commune of laying snares privily, and say who shall see them; they search out iniquities and accomplish a diligent search, and the inward thought and heart of every one of them is deep.

**[[Psalms 64#^v7|Psalms 64:7-10]]**

![[Psalms 64#^v7]]
![[Psalms 64#^v8]]
![[Psalms 64#^v9]]
![[Psalms 64#^v10]]

- But the Most High shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they shall be wounded, and their own tongue shall fall upon themselves, so all that see them flee away.
- All men shall declare the work of the Most High and wisely consider His doing; the righteous shall be glad in the Most High, trust in Him, and all the upright in heart shall glory.

# Psalm 65: Thou Crownest the Year With Thy Goodness

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 65]]

[[Psalms 65|Psalm 65]] opens with praise waiting for the Most High in Zion and the vow performed, then moves to the purging of transgressions and the blessedness of the man He chooses to approach Him. It closes with the Most High enriching the earth, watering the ridges and furrows, and crowning the year with His goodness so that the pastures and valleys shout for joy.

**[[Psalms 65#^v1|Psalms 65:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 65#^v1]]
![[Psalms 65#^v2]]
![[Psalms 65#^v3]]
![[Psalms 65#^v4]]

- Praise waits for the Most High in Zion and the vow shall be performed; He hears prayer and unto Him shall all flesh come.
- Iniquities prevail, but He purges away the transgressions; blessed is the man He chooses and causes to approach to dwell in His courts, satisfied with the goodness of His house and holy temple.

**[[Psalms 65#^v5|Psalms 65:5-8]]**

![[Psalms 65#^v5]]
![[Psalms 65#^v6]]
![[Psalms 65#^v7]]
![[Psalms 65#^v8]]

- By terrible things in righteousness He answers as the God of salvation, the confidence of all the ends of the earth and of them far off upon the sea.
- By His strength He sets the mountains and stills the noise of the seas and the tumult of the people; they that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid of His tokens, and He makes the outgoings of morning and evening rejoice.

**[[Psalms 65#^v9|Psalms 65:9-13]]**

![[Psalms 65#^v9]]
![[Psalms 65#^v10]]
![[Psalms 65#^v11]]
![[Psalms 65#^v12]]
![[Psalms 65#^v13]]

- He visits the earth and waters it, greatly enriching it with the river of God which is full of water, and He prepares corn, waters the ridges and settles the furrows.
- He crowns the year with His goodness and His paths drop fatness; the pastures are clothed with flocks, the valleys covered over with corn, and they shout for joy and sing.

# Psalm 66: Come and See the Works of God

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 66]]

[[Psalms 66|Psalm 66]] calls all lands to make a joyful noise unto the Most High and to see His works, terrible in His doing toward the children of men, turning the sea into dry land. It recounts how He proved and tried His people, brought them through fire and water, and brought them out into a wealthy place, closing with the vows and offerings paid to Him and the declaration of what He has done for the soul.

**[[Psalms 66#^v1|Psalms 66:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 66#^v1]]
![[Psalms 66#^v2]]
![[Psalms 66#^v3]]
![[Psalms 66#^v4]]

- Make a joyful noise unto the Most High, all lands; sing forth the honor of His name and make His praise glorious.
- Say unto the Most High how terrible He is in His works; through the greatness of His power His enemies submit themselves, and all the earth shall worship and sing unto Him.

**[[Psalms 66#^v5|Psalms 66:5-7]]**

![[Psalms 66#^v5]]
![[Psalms 66#^v6]]
![[Psalms 66#^v7]]

- Come and see the works of the Most High, terrible in His doing toward the children of men, who turned the sea into dry land so they went through the flood on foot.
- He rules by His power forever; His eyes behold the nations, and let not the rebellious exalt themselves.

**[[Psalms 66#^v8|Psalms 66:8-12]]**

![[Psalms 66#^v8]]
![[Psalms 66#^v9]]
![[Psalms 66#^v10]]
![[Psalms 66#^v11]]
![[Psalms 66#^v12]]

- Bless the Most High and make the voice of His praise heard, who holds the soul in life and does not suffer the feet to be moved.
- He proved and tried His people as silver is tried, brought them into the net, laid affliction upon their loins, and caused men to ride over their heads, but brought them out through fire and water into a wealthy place.

**[[Psalms 66#^v13|Psalms 66:13-15]]**

![[Psalms 66#^v13]]
![[Psalms 66#^v14]]
![[Psalms 66#^v15]]

- David will go into the house of the Most High with burnt offerings and pay the vows his lips uttered and his mouth spoke when he was in trouble.
- He will offer burnt sacrifices of fatlings with the incense of rams, and offer bullocks with goats.

**[[Psalms 66#^v16|Psalms 66:16-20]]**

![[Psalms 66#^v16]]
![[Psalms 66#^v17]]
![[Psalms 66#^v18]]
![[Psalms 66#^v19]]
![[Psalms 66#^v20]]

- Come and hear, all that fear the Most High, and he will declare what He has done for his soul; he cried unto Him with his mouth and He was extolled with his tongue.
- If he regarded iniquity in his heart the Most High would not hear him, but truly He heard and attended to the voice of his prayer; blessed be the Most High, who has not turned away his prayer nor His mercy from him.
