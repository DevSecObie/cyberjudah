# Psalm 51: Acknowledge, Repent, and Be Cleansed

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 51]]

This session covers [[Psalms 51|Psalms 51]] through 54, four chapters of David. [[Psalms 51|Psalm 51]] is David's prayer of repentance after Nathan the prophet confronted him over Bathsheba and the killing of Uriah. The teaching drives home that Israel must put itself in these scriptures: acknowledge our transgressions, understand what sin is, seek to be cleansed by the word, and know that the wages of sin is the death and captivity we are living in. [[Psalms 52|Psalms 52]] through 54 turn to the boasting of the wicked man, the fool who says there is no God, and David's cry to be saved from the strangers and oppressors who rise against him.

**[[Psalms 51#^v1|Psalms 51:1]]**
![[Psalms 51#^v1]]
- To the chief musician, a Psalm of David, written after Nathan the prophet came to him following his sin with Bathsheba, the wife of Uriah one of the soldiers in David's army.
- Nathan got on David about it and corrected him. Now David sends up this prayer, asking the Most High for forgiveness.
- David appeals to the loving kindness and tender mercies of the Most High, asking that his transgressions be blotted out.
