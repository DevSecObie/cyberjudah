# Psalm 123: Our Eyes Wait Upon the Most High

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 123]]

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session covers [[Psalms 123|Psalms 123]] through 126, four very short chapters that move quickly. The thread running through all of them is Israel being saved from the hand of their enemies and protected by the Most High. [[Psalms 123|Psalm 123]] lifts the eyes of the servant to the Most High for mercy. [[Psalms 124|Psalm 124]] declares that if it had not been for the Most High on Israel's side, the enemy would have swallowed them up. [[Psalms 125|Psalm 125]] promises that those who trust in the Most High shall be as mount Zion, that Israel will never be cast off, and that peace shall be upon Israel. [[Psalms 126|Psalm 126]] rejoices in the Most High turning again the captivity of Zion, sowing in tears now but reaping in joy.

- Unto Thee lift I mine eyes, O Thou that dwellest in the heavens. As the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress, so our eyes wait upon the Most High our God until He have mercy upon us.
- Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us, for we are exceedingly filled with contempt. Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease and with the contempt of the proud.

- If it had not been for the Most High who was on our side, now may Israel say, if it had not been for the Most High who was on our side when men rose up against us, then they had swallowed us up quick when their wrath was kindled against us.
- When the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul, then the proud waters had gone over our soul. Blessed be the Most High, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth.
- Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers, and the snare is broken and we are escaped. Our help is in the name of the Most High, who made heaven and earth.
- When Christians go to say that these psalms are not talking about Israel, these psalms are talking about Israel being saved from their enemies, being protected from our enemies, because our help only comes from the Most High God of Israel.

- They that trust in the Most High shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed but abideth forever. As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the Most High is round about His people from henceforth even forever, which goes and shows that Israel will never be cast off.
  - **[[Romans 11#^v1|Romans 11:1]]**
    ![[Romans 11#^v1]]
    Israel will never be cast off; the Most High has not cast away His people whom He foreknew.
- For the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous, lest the righteous put forth their hands unto iniquity. Do good, O Lord, unto those that be good and to them that are upright in their hearts.
- As for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, the Most High shall lead them forth with the workers of iniquity, but peace shall be upon Israel. Always remember, peace shall always be upon Israel.

# Psalm 126: They That Sow in Tears Shall Reap in Joy

![[Psalms 126#^v1]]
![[Psalms 126#^v2]]

- When the Most High turned again the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream. Then was our mouth filled with laughter and our tongue with singing. Then said they among the heathen, the Most High hath done great things for them.
- The Most High hath done great things for us, whereof we are glad. Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as the streams in the south.
- They that sow in tears shall reap in joy. He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him.
- This is all going into Israel being saved from the hand of their enemies. Pay attention, all through the psalms it was Israel, Israel, Israel, us, our, we, us. All of these are possessive words used to describe us in our condition. We are sowing in tears right now, but we shall reap in joy.
