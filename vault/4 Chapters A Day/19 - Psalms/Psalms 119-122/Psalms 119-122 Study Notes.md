# Psalm 119: Blessed Are the Undefiled in the Way

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 119]]

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session covers [[Psalms 119|Psalms 119]] through 122. [[Psalms 119|Psalm 119]] is the longest chapter in the Bible, a marathon of one hundred seventy six verses arranged as an acrostic that magnifies the law, the testimonies, the statutes, the precepts, the commandments, and the judgments of the Most High. The teaching drives home again and again that a precept is a law, a rule, a commandment, and that our whole duty upon this earth is to keep the commandments of the Most High until we die. [[Psalms 120|Psalms 120]] through 122 turn to the pilgrim songs of ascent, dwelling with them that hate peace in Babylon, the help that comes from the Most High, and the call to pray for the peace of Jerusalem.

- Blessed are the undefiled in the way who walk in the law of the Most High. We have to walk in the law, because without it we are defiled. Blessed are they that keep His testimonies and seek Him with the whole heart, the whole mind.
- Thou hast commanded us to keep Thy precepts diligently. When you look at the word precept it means law, it means commandment, it means rule. That is what a precept is, so precept upon precept is talking about the word of the Most High, the scriptures, the law.
- Once we depart from the precepts we are in the midst of sin. The prayer here is that our ways were directed to keep His statutes, so that we shall not be ashamed when we have respect unto all His commandments.
