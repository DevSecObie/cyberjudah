# Psalm 75: The Most High Is the Judge Who Puts Down One and Sets Up Another

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 75]]

This session is four chapters a day, Scripts 365, covering [[Psalms 75|Psalms 75]] through 78. The reading is relatively short today, though [[Psalms 78|Psalm 78]] is long. The thread running through these chapters is that nothing that happens is for no reason: the Most High is in control of everything, He sits one man up and sets another down, He judges the nations and spares His people, and He has done wonderful works for the children of Israel throughout their history even while they kept provoking Him. Before beginning, this is a Bible-based movement and not a hate group; it does not advocate or condone acts of violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender.

**[[Psalms 75#^v1|Psalm 75:1]]**
![[Psalms 75#^v1]]
- Give thanks unto God, for His name is near and His wondrous works declare that name.

**[[Psalms 75#^v2|Psalm 75:2-3]]**
![[Psalms 75#^v2]]
![[Psalms 75#^v3]]
- When the Most High receives the congregation He will judge uprightly. The earth and all the inhabitants are dissolved, but He bears up the pillars of it.

**[[Psalms 75#^v4|Psalm 75:4-5]]**
![[Psalms 75#^v4]]
![[Psalms 75#^v5]]
- He tells the fools not to deal foolishly and the wicked not to lift up the horn. Do not lift up your horn on high or speak with a stiff neck.

**[[Psalms 75#^v6|Psalm 75:6-7]]**
![[Psalms 75#^v6]]
![[Psalms 75#^v7]]
- Promotion comes neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south, but God is the judge. He puts down one and sets up another.
- Nothing that happens is just for any reason. The Lord is in control of everything; He sits up, He sits down. As one of the soldiers from Tampa put it, the Lord brought you in and He will take you out.

**[[Psalms 75#^v8|Psalm 75:8]]**
![[Psalms 75#^v8]]
- In the hand of the Lord there is a cup, and the wine is red and full of mixture. He pours it out, but the dregs the wicked of the earth wring out and drink.

**[[Psalms 75#^v9|Psalm 75:9-10]]**
![[Psalms 75#^v9]]
![[Psalms 75#^v10]]
- The psalmist declares he will sing praises to the God of Jacob forever. All the horns of the wicked will be cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.

# Psalm 76: God Is Known in Judah and Terrible to the Kings of the Earth

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 76]]

![[Psalms 76#^v1]]
![[Psalms 76#^v2]]
![[Psalms 76#^v3]]
- In Judah is God known, His name is great in Israel. In Salem is His tabernacle and in Zion His dwelling place. There He broke the arrows of the bow, the shield, the sword, and the battle.

**[[Psalms 76#^v4|Psalm 76:4-6]]**
![[Psalms 76#^v4]]
![[Psalms 76#^v5]]
![[Psalms 76#^v6]]
- The Most High is more glorious and excellent than the mountains of prey. The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep, and none of the men of might found their hands. At His rebuke both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep.

**[[Psalms 76#^v7|Psalm 76:7-9]]**
![[Psalms 76#^v7]]
![[Psalms 76#^v8]]
![[Psalms 76#^v9]]

**[[Psalms 76#^v10|Psalm 76:10]]**
![[Psalms 76#^v10]]

    ![[Exodus 9#^v5]]
    ![[Exodus 9#^v6]]

**[[Psalms 76#^v11|Psalm 76:11-12]]**
![[Psalms 76#^v11]]
![[Psalms 76#^v12]]
- Vow and pay unto the Lord your God; let all that are round about Him bring presents unto Him that ought to be feared. He shall cut off the spirit of princes and is terrible to the kings of the earth.

# Psalm 78: The History of Israel's Rebellion and the Choosing of Judah and David

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 78]]

**[[Psalms 78#^v1|Psalm 78:1-2]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v1]]
![[Psalms 78#^v2]]
- Give ear, O my people, to my law; incline your ears to the words of my mouth. I will open my mouth in a parable and utter dark sayings of old.
- When you look at the tribe of Judah, at Christ and his ministry, this is a prophecy of what Christ would do. The scriptures talk about how the spirit of Christ was in the prophets and he would speak through them.
- When Christ spoke to the apostles and disciples, he would give them the understanding of the parables and dark sayings in a separate setting than what he gave everyone else. This was so that everyone after Christ was gone could come to the disciples or apostles for the understanding, setting up the hierarchy of the church.

**[[Psalms 78#^v3|Psalm 78:3-4]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v3]]
![[Psalms 78#^v4]]
- What we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us, we will not hide from the children, but show to the generation to come the praises of the Lord and His strength and His wonderful works.
- That was the apostles bringing it to the children of Israel after they were taught by Christ.

**[[Psalms 78#^v5|Psalm 78:5-7]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v5]]
![[Psalms 78#^v6]]
![[Psalms 78#^v7]]
- He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to make known to their children, so the generation to come and the children yet to be born might know them, declare them to their children, set their hope in God, and keep His commandments.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 24#^v23|Ecclesiasticus 24:23]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 24#^v23]]
    A lot of people, meaning the other nations, think this Bible pertains to them, but it does not; you might find them in the Bible, but they are not of the Bible.

**[[Psalms 78#^v8|Psalm 78:8]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v8]]
- The purpose was that they not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation that set not their heart aright and whose spirit was not steadfast with God.

**[[Psalms 78#^v9|Psalm 78:9-11]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v9]]
![[Psalms 78#^v10]]
![[Psalms 78#^v11]]
- The children of Ephraim, being armed and carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle. They kept not the covenant of God, refused to walk in His law, and forgot His works and the wonders He had showed them.

**[[Psalms 78#^v12|Psalm 78:12-14]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v12]]
![[Psalms 78#^v13]]
![[Psalms 78#^v14]]
- He did marvelous things in the sight of their fathers in the land of Egypt, in the field of Zoan. He divided the sea and caused them to pass through, made the waters stand as a heap, led them with a cloud by day and with a light of fire all night.

**[[Psalms 78#^v15|Psalm 78:15-16]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v15]]
![[Psalms 78#^v16]]
- He clave the rocks in the wilderness and gave them drink as out of the great depths, bringing streams out of the rock and causing waters to run down like rivers.

**[[Psalms 78#^v17|Psalm 78:17-18]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v17]]
![[Psalms 78#^v18]]
- Yet they sinned more against Him, provoking the Most High in the wilderness and tempting God in their heart by asking meat for their lust.
- This is going into their covetousness. They were always looking back to Egypt, wanting to be like Egypt, wanting the leeks, the fish, the cucumbers, all the foods and benefits they got there. They wanted their cake and to eat it too in the wilderness, and that was the children of Israel, us.

**[[Psalms 78#^v19|Psalm 78:19-20]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v19]]
![[Psalms 78#^v20]]
- They spoke against God, saying, can God furnish a table in the wilderness? He smote the rock so the waters gushed out and streams overflowed, but they asked whether He could give bread and provide flesh for His people.

**[[Psalms 78#^v21|Psalm 78:21-22]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v21]]
![[Psalms 78#^v22]]
- The Lord heard this and was wroth; a fire was kindled against Jacob and anger came up against Israel, because they believed not in God and trusted not in His salvation.

**[[Psalms 78#^v23|Psalm 78:23-25]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v23]]
![[Psalms 78#^v24]]
![[Psalms 78#^v25]]
- Though He commanded the clouds above and opened the doors of heaven, rained down manna for them to eat and gave them the corn of heaven, so that man did eat angels' food, and He sent them meat to the full.

**[[Psalms 78#^v26|Psalm 78:26-28]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v26]]
![[Psalms 78#^v27]]
![[Psalms 78#^v28]]
- He caused an east wind to blow and by His power brought the south wind, and He rained flesh upon them as dust and feathered fowls like the sand of the sea, letting it fall in the midst of their camp round about their habitations.
- We read about the Lord getting angry with us in Numbers, how He gave us quail to eat, so much that it would come out of our nose, but still the children of Israel were covetous after our own lust; it was not good enough.
- You can use this scripture too, along with [[Numbers 11|Numbers 11]], to answer people who say you have to be a vegan or a vegetarian; this shows the Lord gave us meat in the wilderness and did it Himself.

**[[Psalms 78#^v29|Psalm 78:29-31]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v29]]
![[Psalms 78#^v30]]
![[Psalms 78#^v31]]

![[Psalms 78#^v32]]
![[Psalms 78#^v33]]

  - **[[Exodus 15#^v13|Exodus 15:13]]**
    ![[Exodus 15#^v13]]

![[Psalms 78#^v38]]

![[Psalms 78#^v43]]
![[Psalms 78#^v44]]
![[Psalms 78#^v45]]

- He is going back into the history of when the Lord did these works coming out of Egypt, and Israel acts like it never happened; the Lord gave us benefits and we say we will do one thing but do not do it.

![[Psalms 78#^v46]]
![[Psalms 78#^v47]]

**[[Psalms 78#^v49|Psalm 78:49-51]]**
![[Psalms 78#^v49]]
![[Psalms 78#^v50]]
![[Psalms 78#^v51]]
- He cast upon them the fierceness of His anger, wrath, and indignation and trouble, by sending evil angels among them. He made a way to His anger, spared not their soul from death, gave their life over to the pestilence, and smote all the firstborn in Egypt, the chief of their strength in the tabernacles of Ham.
- That is a heavy thing. The judgments of the lightning bolts, the fire, the locusts and caterpillars going on throughout Egypt, the Lord said these were His messengers, His evil angels sent to judge Egypt. Israel would look on these things and consider that the Lord does double to the nations of what He does to us.
- The pestilence going into the pandemics and things happening at that time as well.
