# Psalm 15: Who Shall Abide in the Tabernacle

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 15]]

This session moves through [[Psalms 15|Psalms 15]] through 18 in the "4 Chapters a Day" reading. The opening disclaimer is a reminder that Christ commanded us to watch and pray, and that this is not the time to revolt physically; the Most High has not given us that strength and power as of yet, so we stay vigilant and on guard. [[Psalms 15|Psalm 15]] sets the standard of the righteous man, [[Psalms 16|Psalm 16]] preserves the trust of the saints, [[Psalms 17|Psalm 17]] is a prayer for protection from the wicked, and [[Psalms 18|Psalm 18]] is prophesied to reach into the last days concerning the elect.

**[[Psalms 15#^v1|Psalms 15:1-2]]**
![[Psalms 15#^v1]]
![[Psalms 15#^v2]]

- The Psalm opens with a question: who shall abide in the Most High's tabernacle and dwell in his holy hill. The answer is the man that walketh uprightly and worketh righteousness and speaketh the truth in his heart.
- Righteousness is defined by keeping the commandments.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 6#^v25|Deuteronomy 6:25]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 6#^v25]]
    Righteousness is when we observe to do all these commandments.

**[[Psalms 15#^v3|Psalms 15:3-5]]**
![[Psalms 15#^v3]]
![[Psalms 15#^v4]]
![[Psalms 15#^v5]]

- The righteous man does no evil to his neighbor, does not take up a reproach against him, condemns the vile person but honors them that fear the Lord, and sweareth to his own hurt and changeth not.
- He puts not out his money to usury nor takes reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.
- These attributes are the same standard laid out in the law and in the description of the just, righteous man.
  - **[[Ezekiel 18#^v5|Ezekiel 18:5]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 18#^v5]]
    The just man that doeth that which is lawful and right is the picture of this righteous cell.

# Psalm 16: The Portion of Mine Inheritance

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 16]]

**[[Psalms 16#^v1|Psalms 16:1-3]]**
![[Psalms 16#^v1]]
![[Psalms 16#^v2]]
![[Psalms 16#^v3]]

- David asks the Most High to preserve him because he puts his trust in him, confessing that his goodness extends to the saints that are in the earth and to the excellent in whom is all his delight.
- The saints are defined by the scripture itself.
  - **[[Psalms 50#^v5|Psalms 50:5]]**
    ![[Psalms 50#^v5]]
    The saints are those that have made a covenant with the Most High by sacrifice.
  - **[[Psalms 148#^v14|Psalms 148:14]]**
    ![[Psalms 148#^v14]]
    The Most High exalted the horn of his people, his saints, even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him.

**[[Psalms 16#^v4|Psalms 16:4]]**
![[Psalms 16#^v4]]

- Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god. The pronoun "their" points back to his saints, warning that even his own people multiply sorrow when they chase after other gods.
- David refuses to offer their drink offerings of blood or take up their names into his lips.

**[[Psalms 16#^v5|Psalms 16:5-8]]**
![[Psalms 16#^v5]]
![[Psalms 16#^v6]]
![[Psalms 16#^v7]]
![[Psalms 16#^v8]]

- The Lord is the portion of his inheritance and of his cup; the lines are fallen unto him in pleasant places and he has a goodly heritage.
- He blesses the Most High who gives him counsel, and his reins instruct him in the night season.
- He sets the Lord always before him; because the Most High is at his right hand he shall not be moved. This is not a bumper-sticker slogan; it is the true standing of a man who keeps the Most High before his face.

**[[Psalms 16#^v9|Psalms 16:9-11]]**
![[Psalms 16#^v9]]
![[Psalms 16#^v10]]
![[Psalms 16#^v11]]

- His heart is glad and his flesh shall rest in hope, for the Most High will not leave his soul in hell nor suffer his Holy One to see corruption. This speaks of the resurrection on the third day.
- The Most High will show him the path of life; in his presence is fullness of joy, and at his right hand are pleasures forevermore.

# Psalm 17: Keep Me as the Apple of the Eye

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 17]]

**[[Psalms 17#^v1|Psalms 17:1-3]]**
![[Psalms 17#^v1]]
![[Psalms 17#^v2]]
![[Psalms 17#^v3]]

- David asks the Most High to hear the right, attend to his cry, and receive a prayer that goeth not out of feigned lips. Feigned means fake, so he prays from real and not fake lips.
- Let his sentence come forth from the Most High's presence; let his eyes behold the things that are equal.
- The Most High has proved and visited him and found nothing; David is purposed that his mouth shall not transgress.
  - **[[James 3#^v2|James 3:2]]**
    ![[James 3#^v2]]
    He that offends not in word is a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body; read this chapter if you struggle with a foul mouth or talking back.

**[[Psalms 17#^v4|Psalms 17:4-5]]**
![[Psalms 17#^v4]]
![[Psalms 17#^v5]]

- Concerning the works of men, by the word of the Most High's lips David has kept himself from the paths of the destroyer.
- He asks the Most High to hold up his goings in his paths so that his footsteps slip not. Our footsteps slip when we come off the path of righteousness, turning to the right hand or the left instead of following the good path that leads to the kingdom.
  - **[[Psalms 73#^v2|Psalms 73:2-3]]**
    ![[Psalms 73#^v2]]
    ![[Psalms 73#^v3]]
    His feet were almost gone when he was envious at the foolish and saw the prosperity of the wicked.
  - **[[Proverbs 3#^v31|Proverbs 3:31]]**
    ![[Proverbs 3#^v31]]
    Envy not the oppressor and choose none of his ways; envy is one thing that makes the feet slip, like sticking a Christmas tree in your house and following the ways of the wicked.
  - **[[Psalms 73#^v17|Psalms 73:17]]**
    ![[Psalms 73#^v17]]
    Not until he went into the sanctuary of the Most High did he understand their end; get knowledge and understanding built up first.

**[[Psalms 17#^v6|Psalms 17:6-9]]**
![[Psalms 17#^v6]]
![[Psalms 17#^v7]]
![[Psalms 17#^v8]]
![[Psalms 17#^v9]]

- David calls because he knows the Most High will hear him; he asks him to incline his ear and show his marvellous lovingkindness, saving by his right hand them that put their trust in him.
- Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings. This prayer asks to be kept safe from the wicked that oppress him and from his deadly enemies who compass him about.
  - **[[Malachi 1#^v4|Malachi 1:4]]**
    ![[Malachi 1#^v4]]
    Edom, the so-called white man or red man, says they will build the desolate places, but the Most High calls them the border of wickedness, the people against whom he has indignation forever.
  - **[[Genesis 25#^v25|Genesis 25:25]]**
    ![[Genesis 25#^v25]]
    Esau came out red all over, which identifies Edom.
  - **[[Job 9#^v24|Job 9:24]]**
    ![[Job 9#^v24]]
    The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; the example of that wicked ruler right now is the so-called white man, not the African or the Arab.

**[[Psalms 17#^v10|Psalms 17:10-12]]**
![[Psalms 17#^v10]]
![[Psalms 17#^v11]]
![[Psalms 17#^v12]]

- The wicked are enclosed in their own fat, meaning they have wealth, and with their mouth they speak proudly.
- They have compassed David in his steps, set their eyes bowing down to the earth, like a lion greedy of his prey and a young lion lurking in secret places. What happened in DC with the group calling themselves the Proud Boys shows the Bible is a true book, for they speak proudly with their mouth.

**[[Psalms 17#^v13|Psalms 17:13-14]]**
![[Psalms 17#^v13]]
![[Psalms 17#^v14]]

- Arise, O Lord, disappoint him, cast him down, deliver my soul from the wicked which is thy sword. The wicked here is the same deadly enemy and oppressor named in verse 9.
  - **[[Leviticus 26#^v3|Leviticus 26:3-7]]**
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v3]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v4]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v5]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v6]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v7]]
    If we keep his statutes and commandments he will give peace in the land, rid evil beasts, and the sword shall not go through our land; keeping the law is how the sword turns.
- David asks deliverance from men of the world which have their portion in this life, whose belly the Most High fills with hid treasure, who are full of children and leave their substance to their babes.
- This verse ties to the teaching in Bishop's Sabbath class going through [[Job 20|Job 20]].

# Psalm 18: The Song of the Elect in the Last Days

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 18]]

[[Psalms 18|Psalm 18]] is prophecy: David was speaking of the last days concerning the elect. The elect are Jacob, the Most High's chosen, the Israelites, and that identity has not changed from the Old Testament into the New.

  - **[[Isaiah 45#^v4|Isaiah 45:4]]**
    ![[Isaiah 45#^v4]]
    For Jacob my servant's sake and Israel mine elect, the Most High names his elect plainly.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v30|Matthew 24:30-31]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v30]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v31]]
    The Son of man will send his angels to gather his elect from the four winds; since they are gathered, they were first scattered.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v64|Deuteronomy 28:64]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v64]]
    The Lord shall scatter thee among all people from one end of the earth to the other, where thou shalt serve other gods of wood and stone; this is the scattering the gathering reverses.

**[[Psalms 18#^v1|Psalms 18:1-3]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v1]]
![[Psalms 18#^v2]]
![[Psalms 18#^v3]]

- David loves the Most High, his strength, his rock, fortress, deliverer, buckler, the horn of his salvation and his high tower.
- He calls upon the Lord who is worthy to be praised, and so is saved from his enemies.

**[[Psalms 18#^v4|Psalms 18:4-6]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v4]]
![[Psalms 18#^v5]]
![[Psalms 18#^v6]]

- The sorrows of death compassed him and the floods of ungodly men made him afraid; the sorrows of hell compassed him and the snares of death prevented him.
- The sorrows of death describe the curses of the scattering.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v65|Deuteronomy 28:65-66]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v65]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v66]]
    Among the nations we find no ease but a trembling heart, failing eyes, sorrow of mind, and life hanging in doubt with fear day and night; this includes depression that many of our people suffer, and the constant fear even a law-abiding person feels when police pull up behind them.
- In his distress David called upon the Lord, and the Most High heard his voice out of his temple.

**[[Psalms 18#^v7|Psalms 18:7-15]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v7]]
![[Psalms 18#^v8]]
![[Psalms 18#^v9]]
![[Psalms 18#^v10]]
![[Psalms 18#^v11]]
![[Psalms 18#^v12]]
![[Psalms 18#^v13]]
![[Psalms 18#^v14]]
![[Psalms 18#^v15]]

- The earth shook and the foundations of the hills moved and were shaken because the Most High was wroth. Smoke went out of his nostrils and fire out of his mouth devoured.
- He bowed the heavens and came down with darkness under his feet; he rode upon a cherub and did fly upon the wings of the wind.
- He made darkness his secret place, dark waters and thick clouds his pavilion; at his brightness the clouds passed with hailstones and coals of fire.
- The Lord thundered in the heavens, the Most High gave his voice, and sent out his arrows and lightnings to scatter and discomfit his enemies.
- The channels of waters were seen and the foundations of the world were discovered at his rebuke, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

**[[Psalms 18#^v16|Psalms 18:16-19]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v16]]
![[Psalms 18#^v17]]
![[Psalms 18#^v18]]
![[Psalms 18#^v19]]

- He sent from above and drew David out of many waters, delivering him from his strong enemy and from them which hated him, for they were too strong for him.
- We are still looking for that deliverance from our strong enemy and them that hate us.
  - **[[Leviticus 26#^v17|Leviticus 26:17]]**
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v17]]
    If we disobey, they that hate us shall reign over us and we shall be slain before our enemies; this is why the wicked oppressor rules right now, and why our people run when the police come even when innocent.
- They prevented him in the day of his calamity, but the Lord was his stay and brought him forth into a large place because he delighted in him.
  - **[[Ezekiel 20#^v35|Ezekiel 20:35]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 20#^v35]]
    The large place ties to being brought into the wilderness of the people, where the Most High delivers again.

**[[Psalms 18#^v20|Psalms 18:20-24]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v20]]
![[Psalms 18#^v21]]
![[Psalms 18#^v22]]
![[Psalms 18#^v23]]
![[Psalms 18#^v24]]

- The Lord rewarded David according to his righteousness and the cleanness of his hands, because he kept the ways of the Lord and did not wickedly depart from his God.
- We get clean by taking heed to the word and the commandments, not by water baptism.
  - **[[Psalms 119#^v9|Psalms 119:9]]**
    ![[Psalms 119#^v9]]
    A young man cleanses his way by taking heed according to the word.
  - **[[John 15#^v3|John 15:3]]**
    ![[John 15#^v3]]
    Ye are clean through the word which Christ has spoken; the cleansing did not change in the New Testament.
  - **[[Isaiah 8#^v20|Isaiah 8:20]]**
    ![[Isaiah 8#^v20]]
    To the law and to the testimony; if they speak not according to this word there is no light in them, so we learn and apply the law to clean ourselves up.
  - **[[Titus 2#^v11|Titus 2:11-12]]**
    ![[Titus 2#^v11]]
    ![[Titus 2#^v12]]
    The grace of God teaches us to deny ungodliness and worldly lusts and to live soberly and righteously, so grace itself teaches us to keep the commandments.
- David kept the Most High's judgments and statutes before him and kept himself from his iniquity. Iniquity is sin.
  - **[[Colossians 3#^v5|Colossians 3:5]]**
    ![[Colossians 3#^v5]]
    Mortify your members, fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness which is idolatry, for which the wrath of God comes on the disobedient.

**[[Psalms 18#^v25|Psalms 18:25-27]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v25]]
![[Psalms 18#^v26]]
![[Psalms 18#^v27]]

- With the merciful the Most High shows himself merciful, with the upright man upright, with the pure pure, and with the froward he shows himself froward.
- He will save the afflicted people but bring down high looks. Israel is the afflicted people the scripture is talking about.
  - **[[Daniel 9#^v12|Daniel 9:12]]**
    ![[Daniel 9#^v12]]
    He confirmed his words against us and our judges by bringing on us a great evil, not against all people but against us, as was done upon Jerusalem, who are the Israelites.

**[[Psalms 18#^v28|Psalms 18:28-33]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v28]]
![[Psalms 18#^v29]]
![[Psalms 18#^v30]]
![[Psalms 18#^v31]]
![[Psalms 18#^v32]]
![[Psalms 18#^v33]]

- The Most High lights David's candle and enlightens his darkness; by his God he has run through a troop and leaped over a wall.
- As for God, his way is perfect and the word of the Lord is tried; he is a buckler to all that trust in him.
  - **[[Leviticus 26#^v7|Leviticus 26:7-8]]**
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v7]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v8]]
    Ye shall chase your enemies and five shall chase a hundred and a hundred put ten thousand to flight; this is the power we are looking to receive.
- There is no God save the Lord and no rock save our God. It is God that girds David with strength, makes his way perfect, and makes his feet like hinds' feet.

**[[Psalms 18#^v34|Psalms 18:34-40]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v34]]
![[Psalms 18#^v35]]
![[Psalms 18#^v36]]
![[Psalms 18#^v37]]
![[Psalms 18#^v38]]
![[Psalms 18#^v39]]
![[Psalms 18#^v40]]

- He teaches David's hands to war so that a bow of steel is broken by his arms; he gives him the shield of his salvation, his right hand holds him up, and his gentleness makes him great.
- He enlarges David's steps under him so his feet do not slip; David pursues his enemies, overtakes them, and does not turn again till they are consumed.
  - **[[Micah 5#^v7|Micah 5:7-9]]**
    ![[Micah 5#^v7]]
    ![[Micah 5#^v8]]
    ![[Micah 5#^v9]]
    The remnant of Jacob shall be among the Gentiles as a lion among the beasts, treading down and tearing in pieces with none to deliver, and every enemy shall be cut off; another proof we were scattered everywhere like the dew and rain, and a picture of the strength the Most High returns to us.
- David wounds his enemies so they cannot rise; they fall under his feet, for the Most High girded him with strength for the battle and subdued under him those that rose up against him.

**[[Psalms 18#^v41|Psalms 18:41-45]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v41]]
![[Psalms 18#^v42]]
![[Psalms 18#^v43]]
![[Psalms 18#^v44]]
![[Psalms 18#^v45]]

- His enemies cry but there is none to save them, even unto the Lord, but he answers them not; David beats them small as the dust before the wind and casts them out as dirt in the streets.
- The Most High delivers him from the strivings of the people and makes him the head of the heathen; a people whom he has not known shall serve him.
  - **[[Isaiah 60#^v14|Isaiah 60:14]]**
    ![[Isaiah 60#^v14]]
    The sons of them that afflicted us shall come bending, showing the strangers submitting themselves.
- As soon as they hear of him the strangers submit and fade away, afraid out of their close places.

**[[Psalms 18#^v46|Psalms 18:46-48]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v46]]
![[Psalms 18#^v47]]
![[Psalms 18#^v48]]

- The Lord liveth, blessed be his rock, and let the God of his salvation be exalted; it is God that avenges David and subdues the people under him.
- He delivers David from his enemies and lifts him above those that rise against him, delivering him from the violent man. The enemies are not just going to and fro; there is a people who serve satan, the so-called white man, to make it plain.
  - **[[Matthew 11#^v12|Matthew 11:12]]**
    ![[Matthew 11#^v12]]
    The kingdom of heaven suffereth violence and the violent take it by force; the violent men of that time were Rome fighting against the kingdom, which is Israel.
  - **[[1 Chronicles 28#^v5|1 Chronicles 28:5]]**
    ![[1 Chronicles 28#^v5]]
    The Most High chose Solomon to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel, so the kingdom of God is Israel.

**[[Psalms 18#^v49|Psalms 18:49-50]]**
![[Psalms 18#^v49]]
![[Psalms 18#^v50]]

- Therefore David gives thanks among the heathen and sings praises to the Most High's name.
- Great deliverance he gives to his king and shows mercy to his anointed, to David and to his seed forevermore.
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v7|Wisdom of Solomon 18:7]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v7]]
    This great deliverance is the good news, for a remnant of Israel is fighting for our enemies and against the remnant of Jacob, but the righteous will be accepted.
  - **[[Jeremiah 16#^v14|Jeremiah 16:14-15]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 16#^v14]]
    ![[Jeremiah 16#^v15]]
    The days come that it shall no more be said the Lord liveth that brought Israel out of Egypt, but the Lord liveth that brought the children of Israel from the land of the north, which is North America, and from all the lands whither he had driven them; if the people in the land now were not Israel, why are they still here in New York, Hollywood, and Miami.
