# Psalm 147: The Most High Gathers the Outcasts of Israel

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 147]]

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session covers [[Psalms 147|Psalms 147]] through 150, the final psalms. All four chapters run on the same thread: praising the Most High and understanding who His people are. [[Psalms 147|Psalm 147]] shows the Most High building up Jerusalem and gathering the outcasts of Israel from the four corners of the earth, taking pleasure in them that fear Him, and giving His word and judgments to Israel alone. [[Psalms 148|Psalm 148]] calls all of creation to praise Him and exalts the horn of His people. [[Psalms 149|Psalm 149]] puts the high praises of the Most High in the mouth of the saints, the children of Israel, with a two-edged sword in their hand to execute vengeance. [[Psalms 150|Psalm 150]] closes with every living thing that hath breath praising the Most High.

- Praise ye the Most High, for it is good to sing praises unto our God, for it is pleasant and praise is comely. The Most High doth build up Jerusalem; He gathereth together the outcasts of Israel.
- In church they talk a lot about the outcasts of Israel being Gentiles and things of that nature, but this shows plainly that Israel are the people being gathered. Our people are going to be gathered from many different places, from the four corners of the earth, from Assyria, Hamath, Egypt, and Cush. Our people are in Israel, in India, in Arabia, and all over the world.
  - **[[Isaiah 11#^v11|Isaiah 11:11-12]]**
    ![[Isaiah 11#^v11]]
    ![[Isaiah 11#^v12]]
    The Most High sets His hand the second time to recover the remnant of His people from Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the islands of the sea, assembling the outcasts of Israel and the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.
- He healeth the broken in heart and bindeth up their wounds. He telleth the number of the stars; He calleth them all by their names. Great is our Lord and of great power; His understanding is infinite.
- The Most High lifteth up the meek; He casteth the wicked down to the ground. Sing unto the Most High with thanksgiving, sing praise upon the harp unto our God, who covereth the heaven with clouds, who prepareth rain for the earth, who maketh grass to grow upon the mountains.
- He giveth to the beast his food and to the young ravens which cry. He delighteth not in the strength of the horse; He taketh not pleasure in the legs of a man.
- The Most High taketh pleasure in them that fear Him and in those that hope in His mercy. We are still talking about Israel here. He takes pleasure in those that fear Him, meaning those that keep His commandments. The way we know what fear is comes through the precepts.
  - **[[Psalms 111#^v10|Psalm 111:10]]**
    ![[Psalms 111#^v10]]
    The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom; those that fear the Lord are those that do His commandments.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 15#^v1|Ecclesiasticus 15:1]]**  He that feareth the Lord will do good, and he that hath the knowledge of the law shall obtain her.  Those that fear the Lord will keep the commandments; that is what the fear of the Lord is.
- Those that hope in His mercy are going to keep His commandments. That is why faith without works is dead; you have to have faith and you have to have works. You hope in His mercy and you keep His commandments.

- Praise the Most High, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion. Zion is another word for Israel, for Jerusalem, for the people. For He hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; He hath blessed thy children within thee.
- He maketh peace in thy borders and filleth thee with the finest of the wheat. He sendeth forth His commandment upon earth; His word runneth very swiftly. He giveth snow like wool; He scattereth the hoarfrost like ashes.
- He casteth forth His ice like morsels; who can stand before His cold? He sendeth out His word and melteth them; He causeth His wind to blow, and the waters flow.
- He sheweth His word unto Jacob. Jacob is Israel; Jacob is a forefather whose name was changed from Jacob to Israel. When the scriptures refer to a group of people as Jacob, they are referring to the nation of Israel.
- He sheweth His word unto Jacob, His statutes and His judgments unto Israel. He hath not dealt so with any nation, and as for His judgments they have not known them. All the other nations outside of Israel have not known the judgments the Most High wrote in His Bible, and they do not know the promises that come with them.
- None of this belongs to the other nations. No nation can just get up, get this Bible, and understand the judgments in it, because it is not for them. He only gave that to the Israelites.

- Praise ye the Most High from the heavens; praise Him in the heights. Praise ye Him, all His angels; praise ye Him, all His hosts. Praise Him, sun and moon; praise Him, all ye stars of light.
- Praise Him, ye heavens of heavens, and ye waters that be above the heavens. The waters that be above the heavens go back to Genesis, when He divided the waters.
- Let them praise the name of the Most High, for He commanded and they were created. All these things He created obey Him. He hath also stablished them forever and ever; He hath made a decree which shall not pass.
  - **[[Jeremiah 31#^v35|Jeremiah 31:35-36]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 31#^v35]]
    ![[Jeremiah 31#^v36]]
    The decree that shall not pass: as long as the sun, moon, and stars keep their ordinances, the seed of Israel shall not cease from being a nation before Him forever.
- As long as the nation of Israel is on this earth, these ordinances will stay and do the jobs they were given, serving for a light by night and by day. Even the heavens, the sun, the moon, and the stars are not here for decoration; they are here for a purpose, used for signs and for seasons as we read in [[Ecclesiasticus 43|Ecclesiasticus 43]].
- Praise the Most High from the earth, ye dragons and all deeps, fire and hail, snow and vapours, stormy wind fulfilling His word.
- Mountains and all hills, fruitful trees and all cedars, beasts and all cattle, creeping things and flying fowl, kings of the earth and all people, princes and all judges of the earth, both young men and maidens, old men and children.
- Let them praise the name of the Most High, for His name alone is excellent; His glory is above the earth and heaven. He also exalteth the horn of His people, the praise of all His saints, even of the children of Israel, a people near unto Him.
- He lets you know who His people is. There is no other people He says is near unto Him but the children of Israel. The horn He exalteth is the horn of salvation, which is Christ.
  - **[[Luke 1#^v68|Luke 1:68-69]]**
    ![[Luke 1#^v68]]
    ![[Luke 1#^v69]]
    Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who raised up a horn of salvation for His people in the house of His servant David; that horn is Christ.
  - **[[Matthew 1#^v21|Matthew 1:21]]**
    ![[Matthew 1#^v21]]
    She shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call His name Jesus, for He shall save His people from their sins; salvation comes through Christ.
- This verse also lets you know that the children of Israel are the saints mentioned in the Bible. The saints are not Saint Nicholas or the different saints of the Christian church doctrine; they are no other nation but the children of Israel.

- Sing unto the Most High a new song, and His praise in the congregation of saints. Let Israel rejoice in Him that made him; let the children of Zion be joyful in their King.
- Zion is another word for Jerusalem or Israel. Jerusalem or Israel was a people before it was a place, so when scripture refers to Zion it points to the children of Israel.
  - **[[1 Kings 8#^v1|1 Kings 8:1]]**
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v1]]
    Solomon assembled the elders of Israel to bring up the ark out of the city of David, which is Zion; Zion is another word for Jerusalem or Israel.
- Let the children of Zion be joyful in their King. That King is the same horn of His people, the horn of salvation, which is Christ.
- Let them praise His name in the dance; let them sing praises unto Him with the timbrel and harp. For the Most High taketh pleasure in His people; He will beautify the meek with salvation.
- The word meek means submissive or obedient. He will beautify the meek, the obedient, with salvation. If you keep the commandments and are obedient to the Most High, as you read in [[Psalms 147|Psalm 147]], you will have salvation.
- Let the saints be joyful in glory; let them sing aloud upon their beds. Let the high praises of the Most High be in their mouth and a two-edged sword in their hand. This explains what happens once they get their salvation.
- To execute vengeance upon the heathen and punishments upon the people, to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with fetters of iron, to execute upon them the judgment written; this honour have all His saints.
- The honor and glory the saints, the children of Israel, receive when they overcome these people is the privilege of binding their kings with chains and their nobles with fetters of iron. If you do not understand that we are Israelites, you will read this and think it is talking about a time during David or during the kings, but it is talking about the future.

- Praise the Most High in His sanctuary; praise Him in the firmament of His power. Praise Him for His mighty acts; praise Him according to His excellent greatness.
- Praise Him with the sound of the trumpet, with the psaltery and harp, with the timbrel and dance, with stringed instruments and organs, upon the loud cymbals and the high sounding cymbals.
- Let every thing that hath breath praise the Most High. This is the same thing read in Psalm 148: all the living of the earth praise the Most High.
- When Christ returns and the kingdom is delivered unto the Israelites, then every living thing on this earth is going to praise the Most High, even the sun, the moon, and the stars, which will do the same thing.
- On how to properly read the Bible: precept upon precept, line upon line, here a little and there a little. You can read from the back to get the history, and then precept upon precept, where reading one part explains another. Different parts of the Bible explain different things.
  - **[[Isaiah 28#^v10|Isaiah 28:10]]**
    ![[Isaiah 28#^v10]]
    For precept must be upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little; this is how we read the Bible.
