# Psalm 91: Dwelling in the Secret Place of the Most High

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 91]]

This session covers [[Psalms 91|Psalms 91]] through 94, focusing on the reading with basic precepts along the way. The Most High is preparing His people that a time of destruction is coming upon the earth, and we are not to fear it because our trust is in Him. As the disclaimer reminds us, Israel United in Christ is a non-violent, Bible-based movement that does not advocate or condone violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender. With civil unrest jumping off in the world, this is exactly why it is necessary for us to be that light in the dark place and restore order among our people, praying that the 144 and the one third repents soon.

**[[Psalms 91#^v1|Psalms 91:1-4]]**
![[Psalms 91#^v1]]
![[Psalms 91#^v2]]
![[Psalms 91#^v3]]
![[Psalms 91#^v4]]
- Whoever dwells in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under His shadow. When we say the Most High is our refuge and fortress and put our trust in Him, He delivers us from the snare of the fowler and the noisome pestilence.
- His truth shall be our shield and buckler. The shield is what you use to protect yourself, so we have to understand what the truth is.
  - **[[Psalms 119#^v142|Psalms 119:142]]**
    ![[Psalms 119#^v142]]
    God's laws are the truth; His righteousness is an everlasting righteousness.

**[[Psalms 91#^v5|Psalms 91:5-7]]**
![[Psalms 91#^v5]]
![[Psalms 91#^v6]]
![[Psalms 91#^v7]]
- We are not to be afraid of the terror by night nor the arrow that flieth by day. The Most High is preparing us that a time of destruction is coming upon the earth, and we are not supposed to fear that time because our trust is in our God.
- Christ warned us about this. A thousand shall fall at thy side and ten thousand at thy right hand, but it shall not come nigh thee. That means we have to gird up our minds, because we are going to see death, more than the little jimmy or pookie getting shot in the streets that we have been seeing.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v4|Matthew 24:4-6]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v4]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v5]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v6]]
    Take heed no man deceive you; wars and rumors of wars must come, but see that ye be not troubled as long as your trust is in our God.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v9|Matthew 24:9-11]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v9]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v10]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v11]]
    They shall deliver you up to be afflicted and killed, and ye shall be hated of all nations for Christ's name's sake.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v12|Matthew 24:12]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v12]]
    Because iniquity, being sin, shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v13|Matthew 24:13]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v13]]
    He that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved; this is the point for us.

**[[Psalms 91#^v8|Psalms 91:8-10]]**
![[Psalms 91#^v8]]
![[Psalms 91#^v9]]
![[Psalms 91#^v10]]
- Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked. Because we have made the Most High our habitation, no evil shall befall us and no plague shall come nigh our dwelling.
- This does not mean some of us will not die, but the Most High promises He will protect us. We have to make sure we are grounded in this truth.

**[[Psalms 91#^v11|Psalms 91:11-16]]**
![[Psalms 91#^v11]]
![[Psalms 91#^v12]]
![[Psalms 91#^v13]]
![[Psalms 91#^v14]]
![[Psalms 91#^v15]]
![[Psalms 91#^v16]]
- He shall give His angels charge over thee to keep thee in all thy ways. Because he hath set his love upon the Most High and known His name, the Most High will deliver him and set him on high.
- His name being the commandments, the laws. The Most High promises to answer him, be with him in trouble, deliver and honor him, and satisfy him with long life.
- [[Psalms 91|Psalm 91]] is about deliverance when we put our faith and trust in the one true living God and in these commandments.

# Psalm 92: Planted in the House of the Lord

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 92]]

**[[Psalms 92#^v1|Psalms 92:1-6]]**
![[Psalms 92#^v1]]
![[Psalms 92#^v2]]
![[Psalms 92#^v3]]
![[Psalms 92#^v4]]
![[Psalms 92#^v5]]
![[Psalms 92#^v6]]
- It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Most High and to sing praises unto His name, to show forth His lovingkindness in the morning and His faithfulness every night.
- The Most High's works are great and His thoughts are very deep. A brutish man and a fool do not understand this.

**[[Psalms 92#^v7|Psalms 92:7-11]]**
![[Psalms 92#^v7]]
![[Psalms 92#^v8]]
![[Psalms 92#^v9]]
![[Psalms 92#^v10]]
![[Psalms 92#^v11]]
- When the wicked spring as the grass and the workers of iniquity flourish, it is that they shall be destroyed forever. The Most High's enemies shall perish and all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.
- My horn shalt thou exalt like the horn of a unicorn; I shall be anointed with fresh oil, and mine eyes shall see my desire on mine enemies.

**[[Psalms 92#^v12|Psalms 92:12-13]]**
![[Psalms 92#^v12]]
![[Psalms 92#^v13]]
- The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. Listen to the terminology: those that be planted take root, meaning you start to gain understanding because you are focused on the word and meditating on it.
- If you are planted in the house of the Lord, that means you are applying these things that keep you grounded in this truth.
  - **[[Joshua 1#^v8|Joshua 1:8]]**
    ![[Joshua 1#^v8]]
    Meditate on the book of the law day and night and observe to do all written in it, and thy way shall be prosperous and have good success.

**[[Psalms 92#^v14|Psalms 92:14-15]]**
![[Psalms 92#^v14]]
![[Psalms 92#^v15]]
- They shall still bring forth fruit in old age and shall be fat and flourishing, to show that the Most High is upright, my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.
- There is no sin in the Most High. Our people may struggle saying you cannot keep all the laws, but the Most High says His laws are not grievous and He did not give any man permission to sin.
  - **[[1 John 5#^v17|1 John 5:17]]**
    ![[1 John 5#^v17]]
    All unrighteousness is sin, and there is a sin not unto death.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 15#^v20|Ecclesiasticus 15:20]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 15#^v20]]
    He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath He given any man license to sin.

# Psalm 93: The Lord Reigneth and His Testimonies Are Sure

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 93]]

**[[Psalms 93#^v1|Psalms 93:1-4]]**
![[Psalms 93#^v1]]
![[Psalms 93#^v2]]
![[Psalms 93#^v3]]
![[Psalms 93#^v4]]
- The Most High reigneth, clothed with majesty and girded with strength. The world is established that it cannot be moved, and His throne is established of old; He is from everlasting.
- The floods have lifted up their voice and their waves, but the Most High on high is mightier than the noise of many waters.

**[[Psalms 93#^v5|Psalms 93:5]]**
![[Psalms 93#^v5]]
- His testimonies are very sure; holiness becometh His house forever.
- If each of us took a strong look at ourselves, we are more wise than before we came into the knowledge of the truth, because we should be growing in understanding as we continue to apply the commandments. The scriptures are spiritual; the law is spiritual.
  - **[[Psalms 19#^v7|Psalms 19:7]]**
    ![[Psalms 19#^v7]]
    The law of the Most High is perfect, converting the soul; the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.
  - **[[Revelation 19#^v10|Revelation 19:10]]**
    ![[Revelation 19#^v10]]
    The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy; He comes in the volume of the book.

# Psalm 94: The God of Vengeance and Defense of His People

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 94]]

**[[Psalms 94#^v1|Psalms 94:1-3]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v1]]
![[Psalms 94#^v2]]
![[Psalms 94#^v3]]
- O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth, show thyself. We really have to understand this in these times when a lot of our brothers and sisters want to take up arms against the oppressor, because vengeance belongeth to the Most High.
- We ask the question, how long shall the wicked triumph.
  - **[[Luke 18#^v2|Luke 18:2-8]]**
    ![[Luke 18#^v2]]
    ![[Luke 18#^v3]]
    ![[Luke 18#^v4]]
    ![[Luke 18#^v5]]
    ![[Luke 18#^v6]]
    ![[Luke 18#^v7]]
    ![[Luke 18#^v8]]
    The unjust judge avenged the widow for her continual coming; how much more shall God avenge His own elect speedily, yet shall He find faith on earth when the Son of man cometh.
  - **[[Luke 21#^v19|Luke 21:19]]**
    ![[Luke 21#^v19]]
    In your patience possess your souls; while we cry out how long, do not lose faith, Israel.

**[[Psalms 94#^v4|Psalms 94:4-7]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v4]]
![[Psalms 94#^v5]]
![[Psalms 94#^v6]]
![[Psalms 94#^v7]]
- The workers of iniquity utter and speak hard things and boast themselves, like Trump and the Proud Boys who can scale the walls and get into the White House, while a sister who did a U-turn out there got shot up. Do not think the Most High is negligent or does not see these things.
- They break the Most High's people in pieces and afflict His heritage; they slay the widow and the stranger and murder the fatherless. That is the curses we are up under.
- Yet they say the Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it. That is a heavy point, because it shows the people know who we are; it does not say the God of all people, it says the God of Jacob.
  - **[[Jeremiah 51#^v20|Jeremiah 51:20]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 51#^v20]]
    We are the Most High's battle axe and weapons of war, so we must prepare ourselves to be part of that army.

**[[Psalms 94#^v8|Psalms 94:8-11]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v8]]
![[Psalms 94#^v9]]
![[Psalms 94#^v10]]
![[Psalms 94#^v11]]
- Understand, ye brutish among the people, and ye fools, when will ye be wise. He that planted the ear shall hear, He that formed the eye shall see, and He that teacheth man knowledge shall know.
- The Most High knoweth the thoughts of man that they are vanity. He knows our thoughts as His heritage, and He knows the thoughts of the heathen because He created them.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 32#^v26|Deuteronomy 32:26-28]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v26]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v27]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v28]]
    The Most High scattered us into corners as a curse, but feared the wrath of the enemy lest they should say their hand is high; He knows the heathen are a nation void of counsel.

**[[Psalms 94#^v12|Psalms 94:12-15]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v12]]
![[Psalms 94#^v13]]
![[Psalms 94#^v14]]
![[Psalms 94#^v15]]
- Blessed is the man whom the Most High chasteneth and teacheth out of His law. We get chastened and taught out of that law; that is why in Revelation the books are opened and the book of your life is measured against the laws.
- The Most High will not cast off His people nor forsake His inheritance. People say black people are gentiles and nationality is not important, but this tells you nationality is extremely important; you must know who you are.
  - **[[Romans 11#^v1|Romans 11:1-3]]**
    ![[Romans 11#^v1]]
    ![[Romans 11#^v2]]
    ![[Romans 11#^v3]]
    Paul says God hath not cast away His people whom He foreknew, for Paul himself is an Israelite of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.

**[[Psalms 94#^v16|Psalms 94:16-19]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v16]]
![[Psalms 94#^v17]]
![[Psalms 94#^v18]]
![[Psalms 94#^v19]]
- Who will rise up for me against the evildoers and workers of iniquity, iniquity being sin. Unless the Most High had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence.
- When I said my foot slippeth, Thy mercy held me up; in the multitude of my thoughts within me, Thy comforts delight my soul.

**[[Psalms 94#^v20|Psalms 94:20]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v20]]
- Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by law. A throne is the image of power, so shall that power have fellowship with the Most High that frames mischief by law.
- The mischief framed by law is men marrying men, women marrying women, setting the new year in the midst of winter to have us worshiping another god, and coexist meaning you can serve any gods you want.
  - **[[Habakkuk 1#^v3|Habakkuk 1:3-4]]**
    ![[Habakkuk 1#^v3]]
    ![[Habakkuk 1#^v4]]
    Iniquity, spoiling, and violence are before us; the law is slack and judgment never goes forth, and wrong judgment proceeds so our oppressors are found not guilty.
  - **[[2 Corinthians 6#^v14|2 Corinthians 6:14-15]]**
    ![[2 Corinthians 6#^v14]]
    ![[2 Corinthians 6#^v15]]
    Be ye not unequally yoked with unbelievers, for there is no fellowship between righteousness and unrighteousness, nor concord between Christ and Belial.
  - **[[1 Timothy 5#^v8|1 Timothy 5:8]]**
    ![[1 Timothy 5#^v8]]
    He that provides not for his own house hath denied the faith and is worse than an infidel, so do not hang around a slothful man or woman.
  - **[[2 Corinthians 6#^v16|2 Corinthians 6:16-17]]**
    ![[2 Corinthians 6#^v16]]
    ![[2 Corinthians 6#^v17]]
    We are the temple of the living God; He does not say He is the God of all people, so come out from among them, be ye separate, and touch not the unclean thing.

**[[Psalms 94#^v21|Psalms 94:21-23]]**
![[Psalms 94#^v21]]
![[Psalms 94#^v22]]
![[Psalms 94#^v23]]
- They gather together against the soul of the righteous and condemn the innocent blood; this has been happening to us from the beginning, especially in Babylon.
- But the Most High is my defense and the rock of my refuge, and He shall bring upon them their own iniquity and cut them off in their own wickedness. The first verse says vengeance is His, and the last verse says He shall cut them off; we do not have the power ourselves.
  - **[[Romans 9#^v17|Romans 9:17]]**
    ![[Romans 9#^v17]]
    For the same purpose the Most High raised up Pharaoh, that He might show His power in him and that His name might be declared throughout all the earth.
