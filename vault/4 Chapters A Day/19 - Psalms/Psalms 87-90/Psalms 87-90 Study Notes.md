# Psalm 87: Glorious Things Are Spoken of Zion

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 87]]

This session reads through [[Psalms 87|Psalms 87]] through 90, four chapters a day for the nation. The teaching moves through the psalms mostly by reading, bringing out precepts along the way. [[Psalms 87|Psalm 87]] speaks of the Most High hallowing Jerusalem, the city of God, whose foundation is in the holy mountains and whose gates the Lord loveth more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

**[[Psalms 87#^v1|Psalms 87:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 87#^v1]]
![[Psalms 87#^v2]]
![[Psalms 87#^v3]]
![[Psalms 87#^v4]]

- The foundation of the city is in the holy mountains, and the Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. This is about how the Most High hallowed Him a city, Jerusalem.
- Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. The Most High makes mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know Him, and of Philistia and Tyre with Ethiopia, saying this man was born there.

**[[Psalms 87#^v5|Psalms 87:5-7]]**

![[Psalms 87#^v5]]
![[Psalms 87#^v6]]
![[Psalms 87#^v7]]

- Of Zion it shall be said that this and that man was born in her, and the Most High Himself shall establish her. The Lord shall count, when He writeth up the people, that this man was born there.
- As well the singers as the players of instruments shall be there; all the springs are in the Most High.

# Psalm 88: A Soul Full of Troubles

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 88]]

[[Psalms 88|Psalm 88]] is a cry of a soul full of troubles, whose life draws near unto the grave. Much of it lines up with what was prophesied in [[Psalms 22|Psalm 22]] concerning Christ, when the crucifixion was being foretold. The teaching shows this is prophecy of our forefathers and is relevant to us today.

**[[Psalms 88#^v1|Psalms 88:1-5]]**

![[Psalms 88#^v1]]
![[Psalms 88#^v2]]
![[Psalms 88#^v3]]
![[Psalms 88#^v4]]
![[Psalms 88#^v5]]

- The prayer cries day and night before the Most High, asking that his prayer come before Him and that He incline His ear unto his cry, for his soul is full of troubles and his life draws near unto the grave.
- He is counted with them that go down into the pit and is as a man that has no strength. This sounds like some of the same things said in [[Psalms 22|Psalm 22]], when Christ was prophesying about what happened to Christ, the cross being prophesied.

**[[Psalms 88#^v6|Psalms 88:6-9]]**

![[Psalms 88#^v6]]
![[Psalms 88#^v7]]
![[Psalms 88#^v8]]
![[Psalms 88#^v9]]

- The Most High has laid him in the lowest pit, in darkness, in the deep; His wrath lieth hard upon him and He has afflicted him with all His waves. He has put away his acquaintance far from him and made him an abomination unto them; he is shut up and cannot come forth.
- His eye mourneth by reason of affliction; he has called daily upon the Most High and stretched out his hands unto Him. When the Lord turns His face from us, a lot of this stuff is relevant to us today, like the dead bodies laying in the streets of the great city.
  - **[[Hosea 5#^v15|Hosea 5:15]]**
    ![[Hosea 5#^v15]]
    In our afflictions we will seek Him early, so we cry daily to the Lord in repentance until He hear us.
  - **[[1 Kings 8#^v47|1 Kings 8:47]]**
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v47]]
    We cry daily to the Lord keeping the commandments and the faith in Christ until He hear us.

**[[Psalms 88#^v10|Psalms 88:10-12]]**

![[Psalms 88#^v10]]
![[Psalms 88#^v11]]
![[Psalms 88#^v12]]

- He asks whether the Most High will show wonders to the dead, whether the dead shall arise and praise Him, whether His lovingkindness shall be declared in the grave or His faithfulness in destruction.
- He asks whether His wonders shall be known in the dark and His righteousness in the land of forgetfulness. You can't serve God when you're dead.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 9#^v10|Ecclesiastes 9:10]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 9#^v10]]
    There is no work and no devices in the grave; you cannot serve God when you are dead.

**[[Psalms 88#^v13|Psalms 88:13-18]]**

![[Psalms 88#^v13]]
![[Psalms 88#^v14]]
![[Psalms 88#^v15]]
![[Psalms 88#^v16]]
![[Psalms 88#^v17]]
![[Psalms 88#^v18]]

- He cries unto the Most High, and in the morning his prayer shall prevent Him; he asks why the Lord casts off his soul and hides His face from him. He is afflicted, like Christ said, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani, my God why are you so far from me.
- He is afflicted and ready to die from his youth up, suffering the terrors of the Most High; His fierce wrath goes over him and His terrors have cut him off, like Christ was cut off from the land of the living.
- They came round about him daily like water, dogs have compassed him, like it says in [[Psalms 22|Psalm 22]]; lover and friend are put far from him and his acquaintance into darkness. These are the prophecies of our forefathers, and this is the real black history, the Most High declaring the end from the beginning to strengthen our faith.
  - **[[Psalms 22#^v16|Psalms 22:16]]**
    ![[Psalms 22#^v16]]
    For dogs have compassed me; they compassed Christ about as prophesied here.
  - **[[Psalms 62#^v11|Psalms 62:11]]**
    ![[Psalms 62#^v11]]
    The Lord gave the word; the forefathers wrote down what the Lord said through them.

# Psalm 89: The Covenant Made with David

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 89]]

[[Psalms 89|Psalm 89]] sings of the mercies of the Lord forever and of the covenant the Most High made with David His servant, that his seed would be established forever and his throne built up to all generations. The teaching stresses that Christ is of the seed of David, and that the doctrines of a virgin birth denying this lineage are blasphemy against the Bible.

**[[Psalms 89#^v1|Psalms 89:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 89#^v1]]
![[Psalms 89#^v2]]
![[Psalms 89#^v3]]
![[Psalms 89#^v4]]

- He will sing of the mercies of the Lord forever, making known His faithfulness to all generations, for mercy shall be built up forever and His faithfulness established in the very heavens.
- The Most High has made a covenant with His chosen and sworn unto David His servant that his seed will be established forever and his throne built up to all generations. Because he was the seed of David, the doctrine of the virgin birth denying that lineage is blasphemy against the Bible, vain lies.
  - **[[Psalms 132#^v11|Psalms 132:11]]**
    ![[Psalms 132#^v11]]
    The Lord has sworn in truth unto David, that of the fruit of his body He would set upon his throne.

**[[Psalms 89#^v5|Psalms 89:5-8]]**

![[Psalms 89#^v5]]
![[Psalms 89#^v6]]
![[Psalms 89#^v7]]
![[Psalms 89#^v8]]

- The heavens shall praise His wonders and His faithfulness in the congregation of the saints, for who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord, who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto Him.
- The Most High is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints and to be had in reverence of all them about Him; O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto Him, or to His faithfulness round about Him.

- The Most High rules the raging of the sea; when the waves arise, He stills them, like when Christ was on the boat and they said Lord you don't care if we perish, and he rebuked the storm, and they said what manner of man is this that even the sea obeys him.
- He has broken Rahab in pieces as one that is slain and scattered His enemies with His strong arm; the heavens are His and the earth also, the world and the fullness thereof He has founded.
- The north and the south He has created; Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in His name; He has a mighty arm, strong is His hand and high is His right hand.

**[[Psalms 89#^v14|Psalms 89:14-18]]**

- Justice and judgment are the habitation of His throne; mercy and truth shall go before His face. That is the gospel, and it is a great thing to have a just God who has the final authority.
- We go through things, but this should build up your faith; it is why it is so important for things to be done decent and in order, the order that God established.
- Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound; they shall walk in the light of His countenance. We are going to walk in the laws and be before the Lord blameless like Abraham and our forefathers were.
  - **[[Revelation 19#^v11|Revelation 19:11]]**
    ![[Revelation 19#^v11]]
    In righteousness He doth judge and make war, for justice and judgment are His throne.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 5#^v8|Ecclesiastes 5:8]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 5#^v8]]
    See the violent perverting of judgment; marvel not, for there is one higher than they, over how they shoot us down in the streets.

- The Most High spoke in vision to His Holy One, saying He has laid help upon one that is mighty, exalted one chosen out of the people, found David His servant and anointed him with His holy oil, with whom His hand shall be established and His arm shall strengthen him.
- So the Most High made a way for salvation for His people through David, through the chosen lineage of David the son of Jesse. The enemy shall not exact upon him nor the son of wickedness afflict him, and He will beat down his foes before his face and plague them that hate him.

- His mercy the Most High will keep for him forevermore, and His covenant shall stand fast with him; his seed also He will make to endure forever and his throne as the days of heaven. That is why of his kingdom there shall be no end.

- He asks the Most High to remember the reproach of His servants, how they have reproached the footsteps of His anointed. They hated the very steps of Christ, and the Lord is going to remember that. Blessed be the Lord forevermore, amen and amen.

# Psalm 90: Teach Us to Number Our Days

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 90]]

[[Psalms 90|Psalm 90]] is a prayer of Moses, the man of God, on the eternity of the Most High and the shortness of man's life. The Lord has been our dwelling place in all generations; from everlasting to everlasting He is God, while man is turned to destruction. The teaching draws out how the Most High has cut man's days short down to the present day.

**[[Psalms 90#^v1|Psalms 90:1-6]]**

![[Psalms 90#^v1]]
![[Psalms 90#^v2]]
![[Psalms 90#^v3]]
![[Psalms 90#^v4]]
![[Psalms 90#^v5]]
![[Psalms 90#^v6]]

- The Lord has been our dwelling place in all generations; before the mountains were brought forth, or ever He had formed the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting He is God.
- He turns man to destruction and says return, ye children of men; this is the circle of life, He destroys us, puts us through the grave, and regenerates the souls. A thousand years in His sight are but as yesterday when it is past.
- We are like grass which groweth up; in the morning it flourisheth and groweth up, in the evening it is cut down and withereth. Our youth is like a blade of grass, green and healthy, but the glory of it fades away.
  - **[[Proverbs 8#^v22|Proverbs 8:22-25]]**
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v22]]
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v23]]
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v24]]
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v25]]
    The Lord possessed Christ in the beginning of His way, set up from everlasting, before the mountains were settled.

**[[Psalms 90#^v7|Psalms 90:7-10]]**

![[Psalms 90#^v7]]
![[Psalms 90#^v8]]
![[Psalms 90#^v9]]
![[Psalms 90#^v10]]

- We are consumed by His anger and troubled by His wrath; He has set our iniquities before Him, our secret sins in the light of His countenance. That is how the Lord can judge you; we don't get away.
- In the regeneration they shall live again, and then the works of the righteous and the works of the wicked shall be declared. All our days are passed away in His wrath; we spend our years as a tale that is told.
- The days of our years are threescore years and ten; a score is twenty, so three score and ten is seventy. At seventy you must give up the ghost, for the Lord's spirit will not always be striving with you.
  - **[[Genesis 6#^v3|Genesis 6:3]]**
    ![[Genesis 6#^v3]]
    My spirit shall not always strive with man; his days shall be a hundred and twenty years, as He constantly cuts our days short.

**[[Psalms 90#^v11|Psalms 90:11-13]]**

![[Psalms 90#^v11]]
![[Psalms 90#^v12]]
![[Psalms 90#^v13]]

- Who knoweth the power of His anger; even according to His fear, so is His wrath. He asks the Most High to teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
- Now we know we only have about seventy years, so we number our days and consider the end from the beginning, to be effective in our walk and use the time and gifts God gives us, because there are no devices in the grave.
- He asks the Most High to return, and how long, and to let it repent Him concerning His servants.

**[[Psalms 90#^v14|Psalms 90:14-17]]**

![[Psalms 90#^v14]]
![[Psalms 90#^v15]]
![[Psalms 90#^v16]]
![[Psalms 90#^v17]]

- He asks the Most High to satisfy us early with His mercy, that we may rejoice and be glad all our days, and to make us glad according to the days wherein He has afflicted us and the years wherein we have seen evil.
- He asks that His work appear unto His servants and His glory unto their children, that the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us, and that He establish the work of our hands. The Lord will give us according to the fruit of our doings.
  - **[[Psalms 51#^v8|Psalms 51:8]]**
    ![[Psalms 51#^v8]]
    Make me to hear joy and gladness, that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.
  - **[[Jeremiah 17#^v10|Jeremiah 17:10]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 17#^v10]]
    I the Lord search the heart, to give every man according to his ways and the fruit of his doings, so our doings must be the fruit of the spirit.
