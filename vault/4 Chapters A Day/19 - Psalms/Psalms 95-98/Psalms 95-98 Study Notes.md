# Psalm 95: The Rock of Our Salvation

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 95]]

This session covers [[Psalms 95|Psalms 95]] through 98, four psalms of praise. The whole point of these four chapters is giving praise to the Most High, and the teaching walks through precepts inside them to show why we give Him praise. We should always give praise to the Most High that you are an Israelite and you are the best people upon this earth above all nations.

**[[Psalms 95#^v1|Psalm 95:1]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v1]]
- Come let us sing unto the Lord and make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation. So we should always be giving praise that you are an Israelite, the best people upon this earth above all nations.
- Who is the rock of our salvation? That rock is Christ.
- Why should we make a joyful noise? Because at one time we thought Christ was white, we thought Christ was for everyone, and we were deceived by the nations. Knowing that Christ looked like us and He is a black man from the tribe of Judah is a joyful thing, and He is coming to save us, the so-called black, Hispanic, and native Indians. The rock, our foundation that we build this church and our faith upon, is Christ.
  - **[[1 Corinthians 10#^v4|1 Corinthians 10:4]]**
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v4]]
    That rock that followed them was Christ; the rock of our salvation is Christ.

**[[Psalms 95#^v2|Psalm 95:2]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v2]]
- Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving and make a joyful noise unto Him with psalms. We need to come with thanksgiving, joyful noise, and psalms.

**[[Psalms 95#^v3|Psalm 95:3]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v3]]
- The Lord is a great God and a great King above all gods. Our Lord is a great God above all gods upon the earth. We only have the one true power, the living power, and He chose us out of all the nations, so that is a joyful thing.

**[[Psalms 95#^v4|Psalm 95:4-5]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v4]]
![[Psalms 95#^v5]]
- In His hand are the deep places of the earth, and the strength of the hills is His. The strength here refers to the height of the hills.
- The sea is His, He made it, and His hands formed the dry land. Through the Lord's hand He made all things.
  - **[[Genesis 1#^v9|Genesis 1:9-10]]**
    ![[Genesis 1#^v9]]
    ![[Genesis 1#^v10]]
    God called the dry land earth; through the Lord's hand He made all things.

**[[Psalms 95#^v6|Psalm 95:6]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v6]]
- O come let us worship and bow down, let us kneel before the Lord our maker. Why? Because He made the earth and the seas. Who did He make it for? He made it for the Israelites, the saints. He made it for us to rule this planet earth and the nations, and that is a joyful thing.

**[[Psalms 95#^v7|Psalm 95:7]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v7]]
- For He is our God and we are the people of His pasture and the sheep of His hand. Today if you will hear His voice, that voice is this Bible.
- We are that flock, the lost sheep that needs to be found by the voice, this Bible. This Bible tells us from [[Deuteronomy 28#^v15|Deuteronomy 28:15]] through 68 who the Israelites are that have been scattered to the four corners of the earth. That is the gospel that needs to go out to give our people praise and uplift them in their spirits.
  - **[[Psalms 78#^v52|Psalm 78:52]]**
    ![[Psalms 78#^v52]]
    He made His own people go forth like sheep and guided them like a flock; we are that flock.
  - **[[Luke 12#^v32|Luke 12:32]]**
    ![[Luke 12#^v32]]
    Fear not little flock, it is the Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom; the kingdom is going to that flock, the Israelites.

**[[Psalms 95#^v8|Psalm 95:8-9]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v8]]
![[Psalms 95#^v9]]
- Harden not your heart as in the provocation. Your heart means your mind, and provocation means to provoke to sin. Do not harden your neck or your mind to sin.
- When your fathers tempted Me, proved Me, and saw My work. Now we are getting some history of our forefathers, but Paul told us the same thing in the New Testament: do not harden your heart, do not be like our forefathers.
  - **[[1 Corinthians 10#^v6|1 Corinthians 10:6-11]]**
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v6]]
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v7]]
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v8]]
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v9]]
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v10]]
    ![[1 Corinthians 10#^v11]]
    These things were examples so we should not lust, be idolaters, commit fornication, tempt Christ, or murmur as our forefathers did; they are written for our admonition upon whom the ends of the world are come.
  - **[[Romans 15#^v4|Romans 15:4]]**
    ![[Romans 15#^v4]]
    Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that through patience and comfort of the scriptures we might have hope.

**[[Psalms 95#^v10|Psalm 95:10-11]]**
![[Psalms 95#^v10]]
![[Psalms 95#^v11]]
- Forty years long was the Lord grieved with that generation, a people that do err in their heart and have not known His ways.
- He swore in His wrath that they should not enter into His rest. Our forefathers did not enter into the rest, meaning the promised land, at that time. It goes the same way today: we want the kingdom of heaven, to enter into that rest. These are examples we need to go by, for our learning, of what our forefathers did wrong.

# Psalm 96: Declare His Glory Among the Heathen

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 96]]

**[[Psalms 96#^v1|Psalm 96:1]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v1]]
- O sing unto the Lord a new song, sing unto the Lord all the earth. Singing a new song is joyfulness.

**[[Psalms 96#^v2|Psalm 96:2]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v2]]
- Sing unto the Lord, bless His name, show forth His salvation from day to day. Why show forth His salvation from day to day? Because salvation is only for the Israelites, but your mind has to be correct, your mind has to be in the right place.
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v7|Wisdom of Solomon 18:7]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 18#^v7]]
    You have to accept the salvation of the righteous, those keeping God's commandments, and the destruction of the enemy; if you do not, Christianity, religion, and "God loves everybody" is still in your mind.

**[[Psalms 96#^v3|Psalm 96:3]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v3]]
- Declare His glory among the heathen, His wonders among all people. What is God's glory? His glory goes into many things, His mercy and righteousness, and Israel itself.
- Israel is God's glory, so declaring His glory among the heathen means the so-called blacks, native Indians, and Hispanics are coming back to their glory, coming back to God's law, statutes, and commandments. God is putting us back in order among the heathen; we are coming back to our traditions and our heritage and leaving the heritage of the heathen.
  - **[[Baruch 5#^v9|Baruch 5:9]]**
    ![[Baruch 5#^v9]]
    God will lead Israel with joy; the mercy and righteousness that come from Him are His glory.
  - **[[Isaiah 46#^v13|Isaiah 46:13]]**
    ![[Isaiah 46#^v13]]
    He will place salvation in Zion for Israel His glory; Israel is God's glory.

**[[Psalms 96#^v4|Psalm 96:4-6]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v4]]
![[Psalms 96#^v5]]
![[Psalms 96#^v6]]
- The Lord is great and greatly to be praised, He is to be feared above all gods. All the gods of the nations are idols; the nations are the heathen, and their gods are idolatry, but the Lord made the heavens.
- Honour and majesty are before Him, strength and beauty are in His sanctuary.

**[[Psalms 96#^v7|Psalm 96:7-8]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v7]]
![[Psalms 96#^v8]]
- Give unto the Lord glory and strength, give unto the Lord the glory due unto His name. The glory due unto His name is His honour for the things He has done for Israel: He put us in slavery and now He is bringing us back to His glory, our glory, where we once were as a people before.
- Bring an offering and come into His courts. Do we sacrifice? No, we do not sacrifice anymore, so the offering we bring is keeping the commandments, doing God's commandments.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 35#^v1|Ecclesiasticus 35:1]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 35#^v1]]
    When you keep the law you bring offerings enough; keeping the commandment is the peace offering.

**[[Psalms 96#^v9|Psalm 96:9-10]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v9]]
![[Psalms 96#^v10]]
- O worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness, fear before Him all the earth.
- Say among the heathen that the Lord reigneth: the world also shall be established, that it shall not be moved. That is very heavy. The world referred to is the world of Israel, the world without end that shall not be moved.
- He shall judge the people righteously.
  - **[[2 Esdras 6#^v8|2 Esdras 6:8-9]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 6#^v8]]
    ![[2 Esdras 6#^v9]]
    Esau is the end of the world and Jacob is the beginning; Jacob is that world that will be established and not moved.

**[[Psalms 96#^v11|Psalm 96:11-12]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v11]]
![[Psalms 96#^v12]]
- Let the heavens rejoice and let the earth be glad, let the sea roar and the fulness thereof. When the heavens rejoice there will be no more holes in the ozone layer and no more toxic fumes going up to the heavens.
- Why will the earth be glad? No more GMOs, no more digging for oil. Why will the sea roar? No more oil spills.
- Let the field be joyful and all that is therein, then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice. The field being joyful is still about the GMOs in our foods and plants. The trees rejoicing means no more Christmas, because when Esau's world ends that world in verse 10 shall not be moved, so everything is going to rejoice.

**[[Psalms 96#^v13|Psalm 96:13]]**
![[Psalms 96#^v13]]
- He cometh to judge the earth: He shall judge the world with righteousness and the people with His truth.

# Psalm 97: A Fire Goeth Before Him

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 97]]

**[[Psalms 97#^v1|Psalm 97:1-2]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v1]]
![[Psalms 97#^v2]]
- The Lord reigneth, let the earth rejoice, let the multitude of isles be glad thereof.
- Clouds and darkness are round about Him; righteousness and judgment are the habitation of His throne.

**[[Psalms 97#^v3|Psalm 97:3]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v3]]
- A fire goeth before Him and burneth up His enemies round about. That is Christ coming back to destroy the nations.
  - **[[Habakkuk 3#^v4|Habakkuk 3:4-6]]**
    ![[Habakkuk 3#^v4]]
    ![[Habakkuk 3#^v5]]
    ![[Habakkuk 3#^v6]]
    His brightness was as the light, He had horns coming out of His hand where His power was hidden, pestilence and burning coals went before Him, and He beheld and drove asunder the nations; His ways are everlasting.

**[[Psalms 97#^v4|Psalm 97:4-6]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v4]]
![[Psalms 97#^v5]]
![[Psalms 97#^v6]]
- His lightnings enlightened the world; the earth saw and trembled. The hills melted like wax at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.
- The heavens declare His righteousness and all the people see His glory. How do all the people see His glory? Israel, the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and native Indians, are His glory.
  - **[[Psalms 19#^v1|Psalm 19:1-4]]**
    ![[Psalms 19#^v1]]
    ![[Psalms 19#^v2]]
    ![[Psalms 19#^v3]]
    ![[Psalms 19#^v4]]
    The heavens declare the glory of God; their line is gone out through all the earth, and that line is the gospel, the laws of God going out through the brothers teaching to the four corners of the earth.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v14|Matthew 24:14]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v14]]
    This gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness, and then the end comes; once the last person is sealed with God's laws, Christ comes back.
- You see Israel coming back to their nationality and seeing His glory. The men are pure gold, the leaders of the earth, the kings and priests of the earth.

**[[Psalms 97#^v7|Psalm 97:7-8]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v7]]
![[Psalms 97#^v8]]
- Confounded be all they that serve graven images and boast themselves of idols; worship Him all ye gods.
- Zion heard and was glad, and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of His judgments. The Israelites, Zion, are glad because they know salvation is for them and they understand why we lost our identity and why we are catching hell upon the earth.

**[[Psalms 97#^v9|Psalm 97:9]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v9]]
- For the Lord is high above all the earth; He is exalted far above all gods.

**[[Psalms 97#^v10|Psalm 97:10]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v10]]
- Ye that love the Lord hate evil. How do we love the Lord? We cannot hug Him, so we love Him by keeping His commandments. If you love God's commandments you hate sin, and if you keep His commandments you abstain from breaking His laws.
- He preserveth the souls of His saints and delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked.
  - **[[1 John 5#^v3|1 John 5:3]]**
    ![[1 John 5#^v3]]
    This is the love of God, that we keep His commandments, and His commandments are not grievous; that is how we love God.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v14|Ecclesiasticus 33:14-15]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v14]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 33#^v15]]
    Good is set against evil and the godly against the sinner; God's laws are contrary to sin.
  - **[[Proverbs 19#^v16|Proverbs 19:16]]**
    ![[Proverbs 19#^v16]]
    He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; that is how you preserve your soul and are delivered out of the hands of the wicked. He that despiseth His ways shall die, meaning you love evil and love sin and you will die.

**[[Psalms 97#^v11|Psalm 97:11-12]]**
![[Psalms 97#^v11]]
![[Psalms 97#^v12]]
- Light is sown for the righteous and gladness for the upright in heart. What is the light? The light is the commandments, and it gets sown in your mind by keeping it.
- We will be glad because we love the Lord with all our heart and soul and love our neighbor as ourself, bringing forth fruits of repentance like God wants us to be.
- Rejoice in the Lord ye righteous, and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness. He did not forget us; He chastened us for a little but He did not forget, and He is going to remember the covenant.
  - **[[Proverbs 6#^v23|Proverbs 6:23]]**
    ![[Proverbs 6#^v23]]
    The commandment is a lamp and the law is light; the commandments are the light.
  - **[[2 Esdras 9#^v30|2 Esdras 9:30-31]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 9#^v30]]
    ![[2 Esdras 9#^v31]]
    Hear O Israel, I sow My law in you and it shall bring forth fruit; the law has to be sown in our minds, which is why we are told to study, pray, and apply.

# Psalm 98: Sing Unto the Lord a New Song

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 98]]

**[[Psalms 98#^v1|Psalm 98:1]]**
![[Psalms 98#^v1]]
- O sing unto the Lord a new song, for He hath done marvellous things: His right hand and His holy arm have gotten Him the victory.
- This "sing a new song" is repeated in [[Psalms 95|Psalm 95]], [[Psalms 96|Psalm 96]], and [[Psalms 98|Psalm 98]]. All our lives we have been hearing the songs of fools, R&B, hip hop, and fake gospel. We never sang about the so-called black, Hispanic, and native Indian Israelites taking back over the earth; the Lord keeps reminding us to sing a new song unto Him.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 7#^v5|Ecclesiastes 7:5]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 7#^v5]]
    It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise than for a man to hear the song of fools; all our lives we heard the songs of fools.

**[[Psalms 98#^v2|Psalm 98:2-3]]**
![[Psalms 98#^v2]]
![[Psalms 98#^v3]]
- The Lord hath made known His salvation, His righteousness hath He openly showed in the sight of the heathen.
- He hath remembered His mercy and His truth toward the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God. The line, the gospel, is going out, so the nations understand that salvation is for the Israelites; we are coming back to our rightful position and our rightful name, and they are seeing it unfold in front of their eyes.
  - **[[2 Esdras 13#^v36|2 Esdras 13:36-39]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 13#^v36]]
    ![[2 Esdras 13#^v37]]
    ![[2 Esdras 13#^v38]]
    ![[2 Esdras 13#^v39]]
    Christ destroys the nations without labor, light work by the law, and gathers another peaceable multitude, the Israelites coming back to their heritage as upright people.

**[[Psalms 98#^v4|Psalm 98:4-6]]**
![[Psalms 98#^v4]]
![[Psalms 98#^v5]]
![[Psalms 98#^v6]]
- Make a joyful noise unto the Lord all the earth; make a loud noise, rejoice, and sing praise. We need to break forth and sing praise, saying man, I am an Israelite, God chose me. We should be very happy about that thing.
- Sing unto the Lord with the harp and the voice of a psalm, with trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the Lord the King.

**[[Psalms 98#^v7|Psalm 98:7-8]]**
![[Psalms 98#^v7]]
![[Psalms 98#^v8]]
- Let the sea roar and the fulness thereof, the world and they that dwell therein.
- Let the floods clap their hands, let the hills be joyful together before the Lord.

**[[Psalms 98#^v9|Psalm 98:9]]**
![[Psalms 98#^v9]]
- He cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall He judge the world and the people with equity. Righteousness means us keeping God's commandments, observing and doing them, and equity means justice, the practice of giving what is due. By His righteousness He is going to give the people the justice due to them.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 6#^v25|Deuteronomy 6:25]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 6#^v25]]
    It shall be our righteousness if we observe to do all these commandments; righteousness is keeping God's commandments.
  - **[[Psalms 119#^v142|Psalm 119:142]]**
    ![[Psalms 119#^v142]]
    Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness; the commandments are everlasting and are not done away with.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 32#^v16|Ecclesiasticus 32:16]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 32#^v16]]
    They that fear the Lord shall find judgment and kindle justice as a light.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 32#^v24|Ecclesiasticus 32:24]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 32#^v24]]
    He that believes in the Lord takes heed to the commandment and shall fare never the worse; that is how you receive the equity, the justice, due to you.
