# Psalm 115: Not Unto Us, But Unto Thy Name Give Glory

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 115]]

This session covers [[Psalms 115|Psalms 115]] through 118, moving through the reading with basic precepts along the way. The teaching turns on two threads: the vanity of idols made with men's hands versus the power of our God who does whatsoever He pleases, and the truth that we are not a cursed people but a people under the curses, blessed and remembered by the Most High even when we are cast out among our enemies. As the disclaimer reminds us, Israel United in Christ is a non-violent, Bible-based movement that does not advocate or condone violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender.

- Not unto us but unto His name give glory, for His mercy and His truth's sake. The heathen ask where is now their God, but our God is in the heavens and He has done whatsoever He has pleased.
- Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men's hands. They have mouths but speak not, eyes but see not, ears but hear not, and they that make them are like unto them. This is going into idols and idolatry, which is spoken of throughout the scriptures.
  - **[[Isaiah 42#^v8|Isaiah 42:8]]**
    ![[Isaiah 42#^v8]]
    I am the Lord, that is My name, and My glory will I not give to another; the graven images are the idols.
  - **[[Isaiah 40#^v18|Isaiah 40:18-23]]**
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v18]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v19]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v20]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v21]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v22]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v23]]
    To whom will you liken God? The workman melts a graven image, but it is the Most High that sits upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants are as grasshoppers; He brings the princes to nothing.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v64|Deuteronomy 28:64]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v64]]
    Because of these idols the Most High said He would scatter us among all people and there we would serve other gods of wood and stone.
  - **[[Habakkuk 2#^v18|Habakkuk 2:18]]**
    ![[Habakkuk 2#^v18]]
    The graven image is a teacher of lies, so the entertainers, athletes, and dope boys flossing with the jesus piece around their neck are carrying a teacher of lies.
  - **[[John 3#^v16|John 3:16]]**
    ![[John 3#^v16]]
    This is one of those lies learned under the false image, telling everybody on the earth that God so loved the whole world.

**[[Psalms 115#^v9|Psalms 115:9-12]]**
![[Psalms 115#^v9]]
![[Psalms 115#^v10]]
![[Psalms 115#^v11]]
![[Psalms 115#^v12]]
- O Israel, O house of Aaron, and ye that fear the Lord, trust in the Lord; He is their help and their shield. The Lord has been mindful of us and He will bless us.
- This is so important because we are not a cursed people; we are a people under the curses. Because the Lord has been mindful of us, let us see how He has been mindful.
  - **[[Leviticus 26#^v44|Leviticus 26:44]]**
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v44]]
    When we are in the land of our enemies the Most High will not cast us away nor destroy us utterly, but will remember the covenant of our ancestors.
  - **[[Romans 9#^v4|Romans 9:4]]**
    ![[Romans 9#^v4]]
    This is one of the promises that belong to us as Israelites, the covenants and the promises.

**[[Psalms 115#^v13|Psalms 115:13-16]]**
![[Psalms 115#^v13]]
![[Psalms 115#^v14]]
![[Psalms 115#^v15]]
![[Psalms 115#^v16]]
- He will bless them that fear the Lord, both small and great, and increase you more and more, you and your children. The scriptures say we are blessed, not cursed, and this speaks of us as a nation, not the brother or sister in the midst of sin claiming to be blessed and highly favored.
- The heavens are the Lord's, but the earth He has given to the children of men. The dead praise not the Lord, but we will bless the Lord from this time forth and forevermore.
  - **[[Numbers 22#^v12|Numbers 22:12]]**
    ![[Numbers 22#^v12]]
    The Most High told Balaam not to curse the people for they are blessed, refusing to let the king of Moab hire a curse upon Israel coming up out of Egypt.
  - **[[Isaiah 61#^v9|Isaiah 61:9]]**
    ![[Isaiah 61#^v9]]
    Our seed shall be known among the Gentiles, and all that see them shall acknowledge that they are the seed which the Lord has blessed.

# Psalm 116: I Love the Lord Because He Hath Heard My Voice

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 116]]

**[[Psalms 116#^v1|Psalms 116:1-5]]**
![[Psalms 116#^v1]]
![[Psalms 116#^v2]]
![[Psalms 116#^v3]]
![[Psalms 116#^v4]]
![[Psalms 116#^v5]]
- I love the Lord because He has heard my voice and my supplications; therefore will I call upon Him as long as I live. When the sorrows of death and the pains of hell got hold on me, I called upon the name of the Lord to deliver my soul.
- Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful. Our God is merciful, and this is why we are not consumed.
  - **[[Lamentations 3#^v22|Lamentations 3:22-23]]**
    ![[Lamentations 3#^v22]]
    ![[Lamentations 3#^v23]]
    It is of the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not; they are new every morning, great is Thy faithfulness.

**[[Psalms 116#^v6|Psalms 116:6-12]]**
![[Psalms 116#^v6]]
![[Psalms 116#^v7]]
![[Psalms 116#^v8]]
![[Psalms 116#^v9]]
![[Psalms 116#^v10]]
![[Psalms 116#^v11]]
![[Psalms 116#^v12]]
- The Most High preserves the simple; when I was brought low, He helped me. Return unto thy rest, O my soul, for the Lord has dealt bountifully with thee, delivering my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.
- What shall I render unto the Lord for all His benefits toward me? The apostle Paul answered that question.
  - **[[Romans 12#^v1|Romans 12:1]]**
    ![[Romans 12#^v1]]
    Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
  - **[[Ezra 9#^v13|Ezra 9:13]]**
    ![[Ezra 9#^v13]]
    One of His benefits is that the Most High has punished us less than our iniquities deserve and has given us such deliverance.

**[[Psalms 116#^v13|Psalms 116:13-19]]**
![[Psalms 116#^v13]]
![[Psalms 116#^v14]]
![[Psalms 116#^v15]]
![[Psalms 116#^v16]]
![[Psalms 116#^v17]]
![[Psalms 116#^v18]]
![[Psalms 116#^v19]]
- I will take the cup of salvation and call upon the name of the Lord, and pay my vows unto Him now in the presence of all His people. What we render is keeping the seventh-day Sabbath, the Passover, the new moons and the high holy days, observing the dietary laws, and learning to love our brethren as ourselves.
- Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints. The sisters are to come up out of the uncleanness they have been in, marry, and become righteous wives, offering the sacrifice of thanksgiving unto the Lord.

# Psalm 117: Praise the Lord, All Ye Nations

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 117]]

**[[Psalms 117#^v1|Psalms 117:1-2]]**
![[Psalms 117#^v1]]
![[Psalms 117#^v2]]
- O praise the Lord, all ye nations; praise Him, all ye people. His merciful kindness is great toward us and the truth of the Lord endures forever.
- These are the only two verses in this psalm, a short call to praise Him for His mercy and His enduring truth.

# Psalm 118: His Mercy Endureth For Ever

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 118]]

**[[Psalms 118#^v1|Psalms 118:1-6]]**
![[Psalms 118#^v1]]
![[Psalms 118#^v2]]
![[Psalms 118#^v3]]
![[Psalms 118#^v4]]
![[Psalms 118#^v5]]
![[Psalms 118#^v6]]
- O give thanks unto the Lord, for He is good, because His mercy endures forever. Let Israel, the house of Aaron, and them that fear the Lord all say that His mercy endures forever.
- I called upon the Lord in distress and He answered me and set me in a large place. The Lord is on my side; I will not fear what man can do unto me, but this does not mean fear will never come upon you at all.
  - **[[Matthew 10#^v28|Matthew 10:28]]**
    ![[Matthew 10#^v28]]
    Fear not them which kill the body but are not able to kill the soul, but rather fear Him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.
  - **[[2 Maccabees 7#^v11|2 Maccabees 7:11-14]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 7#^v11]]
    ![[2 Maccabees 7#^v12]]
    ![[2 Maccabees 7#^v13]]
    ![[2 Maccabees 7#^v14]]
    The young men despised the pains and died for the laws of the Most High, showing the very spirit of not fearing them that kill the body.

**[[Psalms 118#^v7|Psalms 118:7-14]]**
![[Psalms 118#^v7]]
![[Psalms 118#^v8]]
![[Psalms 118#^v9]]
![[Psalms 118#^v10]]
![[Psalms 118#^v11]]
![[Psalms 118#^v12]]
![[Psalms 118#^v13]]
![[Psalms 118#^v14]]
- The Lord takes my part with them that help me; it is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in man or in princes. All nations may compass me about, but in the name of the Lord will I destroy them; they are quenched as the fire of thorns.
- Thou hast thrust sore at me that I might fall, but the Lord helped me. The Lord is my strength and song, and is become my salvation.
- We learn to reach a level of faith like those young men by prayer, study, and application: pray and apply. When your life is clean, fasting and praying and overcoming your sins, the Most High will put a spirit of boldness upon you and you will start to believe the scriptures written about us. Sin causes fear and sin causes doubt.

- The voice of rejoicing and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous; the right hand of the Lord is exalted and does valiantly. I shall not die but live and declare the works of the Lord.
- The Lord has chastened me sore but has not given me over unto death. When you come to serve the Lord, understand that this walk brings chastising to try us.

    ![[2 Corinthians 6#^v7]]

    ![[2 Corinthians 6#^v9]]
    By the word of truth, by honor and dishonor, as chastened and not killed; the prophets and the apostle Paul agree with what we read in the psalm.

    ![[Hebrews 10#^v7]]
