# Psalm 47: God the Great King Over All the Earth

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 47]]

This session covers [[Psalms 47|Psalms 47]] through 50, the songs of King David, a man after the Most High's own heart. These chapters are basic but heavy, deep basics, dealing with the Most High as terrible king over all the earth, the subduing of the nations under Israel, the vanity of riches in the grave, and the judgment against the wicked who take the covenant into their mouths. King David wrote as a prophet in the spirit, and everything he opens here is redundant with the whole Bible: the redemption of Israel and the destruction of the nations that will not serve them.

- The Most High commands the people to clap and shout with the voice of triumph because Psalms is music. One day those who repent and keep the commandments will return to the land as the righteous rulers of the earth and praise the Most High's name.
- Every time commandment keepers gather, the Sabbath, the new moon, the feast days, camp 101, the [[Titus 2|Titus 2]] events, it is a time of triumph and rejoicing in the Most High in this wicked world.
- The Most High is terrible not in an ugly or scary sense but in a powerful sense; when the angel appeared to Samson's mother his countenance was very terrible, going into the power of the angels.
- Daniel saw the Ancient of Days, the Most High, seated with hair like pure wool, an afro, his throne like fiery flame, a fiery stream before him and millions of angels ministering unto him; that terrible glory is what David means.
- Verse 3 says he shall subdue the people under us and the nations under our feet, so all nations cannot be saved; a man after the Most High's own heart would not prophesy something the Most High is not okay with. King David was a black man, a prophet.
- The Most High chooses Israel's inheritance for them; it will not be up to you to say you do not want to rule the nations. The spirit will be put on you and you will be kings, your wives goddesses and queens, your sons princes, your daughters princesses, ruling the whole planet in righteousness.

# Psalm 49: The Vanity of Riches Against the Grave

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 49]]

**[[Psalms 49#^v1|Psalm 49:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 49#^v1]]
![[Psalms 49#^v2]]
![[Psalms 49#^v3]]
![[Psalms 49#^v4]]

- The Most High calls all people, low and high, rich and poor together, to hear; David's mouth speaks wisdom and the meditation of his heart is understanding.
- David inclines his ear to a parable and opens his dark sayings upon the harp; he is prophesying over music, which is why the sisters cover their heads when music comes out.
- He is not only making a melody but teaching and bringing out deep, dark parables in the Psalms.

**[[Psalms 49#^v5|Psalm 49:5-9]]**

![[Psalms 49#^v5]]
![[Psalms 49#^v6]]
![[Psalms 49#^v7]]
![[Psalms 49#^v8]]
![[Psalms 49#^v9]]

- Why should David fear in the days of evil when the iniquity of his heels compasses him about? Those who trust in wealth and boast in riches cannot by any means redeem a brother or give the Most High a ransom.
- Celebrities and rich people watch family members die and can do nothing; all the money for transplants, surgery and chemotherapy cannot save them from the grave if the Most High wants to take them.
- Many of Israel's own brothers and sisters trust in their riches; the charge is to be not high-minded nor trust in uncertain riches but in the living God.
- Riches are uncertain; money in an online bank cannot be reached when Wi-Fi and electricity go out, and then the rich man is in the same boat as the poor.
  - **[[1 Timothy 6#^v17|1 Timothy 6:17]]**
    ![[1 Timothy 6#^v17]]
    Charge them that are rich not to be high-minded nor trust in uncertain riches but in the living God who giveth us richly all things to enjoy.
  - **[[Proverbs 11#^v4|Proverbs 11:4]]**
    ![[Proverbs 11#^v4]]
    Riches profit not in the day of wrath, but righteousness delivereth from death.
  - **[[Zephaniah 1#^v18|Zephaniah 1:18]]**
    ![[Zephaniah 1#^v18]]
    Neither their silver nor their gold shall deliver them in the day of the Most High's wrath, for the whole land shall be devoured by the fire of his jealousy.

**[[Psalms 49#^v10|Psalm 49:10-12]]**

![[Psalms 49#^v10]]
![[Psalms 49#^v11]]
![[Psalms 49#^v12]]

- Both wise men and the foolish and brutish perish and leave their wealth to others; a rich man dying without a will leaves Esau to take his money and pay his own people.
- Their inward thought is that their houses endure forever, so they call their lands after their own names, as the so-called white man names America after Amerigo Vespucci and boulevards after dead men.
- They think it will always be here for their children, but the Most High will take it from them and everything returns to its natural name.
- Man being in honor abides not; he is like the beasts that perish, boasting himself and naming things after himself but destined to die.
  - **[[Luke 4#^v6|Luke 4:6]]**
    ![[Luke 4#^v6]]
    The devil told Christ all this power and glory is delivered unto him to give to whomsoever he will, so the wicked prospering without keeping the commandments shows whom they worship.
  - **[[Psalms 73#^v12|Psalm 73:12]]**
    ![[Psalms 73#^v12]]
    Behold, these are the ungodly who prosper in the world; they increase in riches.

**[[Psalms 49#^v13|Psalm 49:13-15]]**

![[Psalms 49#^v13]]
![[Psalms 49#^v14]]
![[Psalms 49#^v15]]

- Their way is their folly, yet their posterity approve their sayings; man's honor abides not and he will eventually die like the beasts.
- Like sheep they are laid in the grave, death feeds on them, and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning when the Most High resurrects the dead and they see Israel ruling.
- Those who pierced Christ will be regenerated on earth, look up, see him return and remember that they were the ones who did that to him.
- Israel's beauty consumes in the grave from their dwelling, but the Most High will redeem the soul from the power of the grave and receive it, raising Israel up as the dead in Christ rise first.

**[[Psalms 49#^v16|Psalm 49:16-20]]**

![[Psalms 49#^v16]]
![[Psalms 49#^v17]]
![[Psalms 49#^v18]]
![[Psalms 49#^v19]]
![[Psalms 49#^v20]]

- Be not afraid when one is made rich and the glory of his house increased, for when he dies he carries nothing away and his glory does not descend after him.
- Do not be troubled or vexed by people in the world prospering while wicked; the Most High says they will get their judgment.
- Though while he lives the rich man blesses his soul and men praise him when he does well to himself, he goes to the generation of his fathers and they never see light.
- A man in honor who understands not, whether a king, judge or governor with no understanding of the word, is like the beasts that perish; Kobe Bryant retired at thirty-seven and died at forty-one, and Chadwick Boseman died soon after Black Panther, and that is the Most High doing what he does.
- Kobe's wife is Northern Kingdom and his daughters are Judah, our sisters; the prayer is that she repents and finds the Most High or supports the truth secretly.

# Psalm 50: The Mighty God Judges His People and the Wicked

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 50]]

**[[Psalms 50#^v1|Psalm 50:1-3]]**

![[Psalms 50#^v1]]
![[Psalms 50#^v2]]
![[Psalms 50#^v3]]

- The mighty God, the Most High, has spoken and called the earth from the rising of the sun to its going down, from east to west.
- Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, the Most High has shined; Zion is the people, the land mass, the nation and the city, though right now it does not look like it.
- When the Most High brings destruction and gives redemption Israel will be beautiful, no more fat, diabetes, heart disease or cancer, but the perfection of beauty.
- The Most High shall come and not keep silence; a fire shall devour before him and it shall be very tempestuous round about him, as Christ comes back with fire.

**[[Psalms 50#^v4|Psalm 50:4-6]]**

![[Psalms 50#^v4]]
![[Psalms 50#^v5]]
![[Psalms 50#^v6]]

- The Most High calls the heavens above and the earth that he may judge his people, gathering his saints who made a covenant with him by sacrifice, which is the Israelites.
- He sends his angels with a great sound of a trumpet to gather his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other; this is what the world calls the rapture, and only the children of Israel are gathered.
- While Christ brings destruction on the nations himself, most of the angels are used to gather Israel up; the Most High destroys and the angels gather.
- The heavens declare his righteousness, for the Most High is judge himself.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v30|Matthew 24:30-31]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v30]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v31]]
    The sign of the Son of man appears, all tribes of the earth mourn, they see him coming in the clouds with power and great glory, and he sends his angels with a great sound of a trumpet to gather his elect from the four winds.

- The Most High testifies against Israel as their God but will not reprove them for their sacrifices or burnt offerings, for he takes no bullock or he goats out of their houses.
- Every beast of the forest and the cattle on a thousand hills are already his; the world and its fullness are his, and if he were hungry he would not tell.
- The Most High will not eat the flesh of bulls or drink the blood of goats; instead offer thanksgiving and pay thy vows unto the Most High, showing he was doing away with animal sacrifice.
- This is the mercy prophesied in the New Testament: the Most High will have mercy and not sacrifice, for the blood of bulls and goats cannot take away sin, which is why Christ had to die.
- Call upon the Most High in the day of trouble and he will deliver thee and thou shalt glorify him.

    ![[Hosea 6#^v6]]

  - **[[Hebrews 10#^v4|Hebrews 10:4]]**
    ![[Hebrews 10#^v4]]
    It is not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away sins.

- The wicked hate instruction and cast the words behind them; when they saw a thief they consented, and when Babylon came to raze Jerusalem Esau was there saying raze it, partaking with the thief against his brother Jacob.
