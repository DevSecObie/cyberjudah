# Psalm 67: The Nation Blessed in Rulership

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 67]]

This session moves through [[Psalms 67|Psalms 67]] through 70, read for the nation four chapters a day. [[Psalms 67|Psalm 67]] asks the Most High to be merciful and to bless us so that His way is known upon the earth and His saving health among all nations. The teaching ties the blessing of the psalm directly to Deuteronomy 28: the blessings are national, not the individual blessing that people today claim for themselves. Our forefathers understood that when we were blessed as a nation of people, we were the ruling class, and the earth yielding her increase is the very increase promised for keeping the commandments.

**[[Psalms 67#^v1|Psalms 67:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 67#^v1]]
![[Psalms 67#^v2]]

- The plea is for the Most High to be merciful, to bless us, and to cause His face to shine upon us.
- The purpose of the blessing is that His way may be known upon the earth and His saving health among all nations.

**[[Psalms 67#^v3|Psalms 67:3-5]]**

![[Psalms 67#^v3]]
![[Psalms 67#^v4]]
![[Psalms 67#^v5]]

- The call is for all the people to praise the Most High, for the nations to be glad and sing for joy.
- He shall judge the people righteously and govern the nations upon the earth. This is us going into rulership.
- The blessing our forefathers understood was that when we were blessed as a nation of people, we were the ruling class.

**[[Psalms 67#^v6|Psalms 67:6-7]]**

![[Psalms 67#^v6]]
![[Psalms 67#^v7]]

- Then the earth shall yield her increase and our own God shall bless us, and all the ends of the earth shall fear Him.
- This is what the psalmist was referring to: when we keep the commandments of God, we as a nation come under the blessings of [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]]. The ground yielding its increase is exactly that promise.

    Hearken diligently to His commandments and the Most High sets thee on high above all nations; blessed in the city and field, and the fruit of thy body and thy ground and thy cattle.

# Psalm 68: Let God Arise

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 68]]

[[Psalms 68|Psalm 68]] opens with the cry for the Most High to arise and scatter His enemies, and the teaching keeps tying that scattering back to the blessing of [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]] and Leviticus 26: our enemies fleeing before us is part of what comes with being a blessed nation. The chapter moves through the Most High going before His people in the wilderness, the Lord giving the word and the great company that published it, His chariots of twenty thousand, and the tribes marching with singers and damsels playing instruments. The Most High is a man of war, and to Him belong the issues of death.

**[[Psalms 68#^v1|Psalms 68:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 68#^v1]]
![[Psalms 68#^v2]]

- The cry is for the Most High to arise so His enemies are scattered and those that hate Him flee before Him.
- As smoke is driven away, so drive them away; as wax melts before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.
- This is going into the blessing again. Today people say they are blessed as an individual, but we understood that the blessings of God are in [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]], and they refer to our condition as a nation of people. Our enemies fleeing from us is part of that blessing.

    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v1]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v2]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v7]]
    Hearken diligently and the blessings overtake thee as a nation; the Most High causes thine enemies to be smitten before thy face, coming out one way and fleeing seven ways.
  - **[[Leviticus 26#^v7|Leviticus 26:7-8]]**
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v7]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v8]]

- He is a father of the fatherless and a judge of the widows; He sets the solitary in families and brings out those bound with chains, but the rebellious dwell in a dry land.

- The scriptures talk about how when the Most High comes, the hailstorms come, the mountains tremble, and thunder and lightning appear. We serve the only true God, the God of Israel, the God of everybody.

# Psalm 69: Save Me, O God

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 69]]

[[Psalms 69|Psalm 69]] is the cry of a man come into deep waters, hated without a cause, bearing reproach for the Most High's sake. The teaching frames the reproach and shame as the very marks of being on the right track, since the world hates what we teach because it loves its evil deeds, and ties the man being a stranger to his brethren to the curses of [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]]. The chapter turns into imprecation against the persecutors, being blotted out of the book of the living, and closes on the promise that God will save Zion and build the cities of Judah for the seed that loves His name.

**[[Psalms 69#^v1|Psalms 69:1-4]]**

![[Psalms 69#^v1]]
![[Psalms 69#^v2]]
![[Psalms 69#^v3]]
![[Psalms 69#^v4]]

- Save me, O God, for the waters are come in unto my soul; I sink in deep mire where there is no standing, come into deep waters where the floods overflow me.
- I am weary of my crying, my throat is dry, my eyes fail while I wait for my God. They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head.

**[[Psalms 69#^v5|Psalms 69:5]]**

![[Psalms 69#^v5]]

- O God, Thou knowest my foolishness, and my sins are not hid from Thee.
- Nothing is hid from God. Whether it be good or evil, He brings every secret thing into judgment.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 12#^v13|Ecclesiastes 12:13-14]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 12#^v13]]
    ![[Ecclesiastes 12#^v14]]
    Fear God and keep His commandments, for this is the whole duty of man; for God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether good or evil.

**[[Psalms 69#^v6|Psalms 69:6-9]]**

![[Psalms 69#^v6]]
![[Psalms 69#^v7]]
![[Psalms 69#^v8]]
![[Psalms 69#^v9]]

- Let not them that wait on Thee be ashamed for my sake, nor those that seek Thee be confounded, O God of Israel.
- For Thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face. I am become a stranger unto my brethren and an alien unto my mother's children, for the zeal of Thine house hath eaten me up.
- The scripture says blessed are you when they hate you, mistreat you, and speak evil of you. We should look at going through these conditions and know that according to the scriptures we are blessed. It may not look like it, but this is one of the ways you know you are on the right track, because the world hates what we teach because they love their evil deeds.

**[[Psalms 69#^v10|Psalms 69:10-12]]**

![[Psalms 69#^v10]]
![[Psalms 69#^v11]]
![[Psalms 69#^v12]]

- When I wept and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach; I made sackcloth my garment and became a proverb to them.
- Just like it says in [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]], this is how it feels to be under the curses, to be a proverb and an astonishment and a byword. David was like, Lord, I am a king, this is not supposed to be happening.
- They that sit in the gate speak against me, and I was the song of the drunkards.

**[[Psalms 69#^v13|Psalms 69:13-15]]**

![[Psalms 69#^v13]]

- But my prayer is unto Thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time; in the multitude of Thy mercy hear me, in the truth of Thy salvation.
- We may think the Lord does not hear us. That is why in the fullness of time God sent His son. In the Christian church they say He may not come when you want Him, but He is always on time; the Lord comes in an acceptable time.
- Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink; let me be delivered from them that hate me and out of the deep waters. Deliver me from the haters, and let not the deep swallow me up.

**[[Psalms 69#^v16|Psalms 69:16-18]]**

![[Psalms 69#^v16]]

- Hear me, O Lord, for Thy lovingkindness is good; turn unto me according to the multitude of Thy tender mercies, and hide not Thy face from Thy servant, for I am in trouble.
- When we call on God we want Him to hear us. It is not pleasant to call on God and have Him tell you, as in [[Hosea 5#^v15|Hosea 5:15]], that He will return to His place till you acknowledge your offense.
- Hear me speedily; draw nigh unto my soul and redeem it; deliver me because of mine enemies.

- Thou hast known my reproach, my shame, and my dishonour; mine adversaries are all before Thee. Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am full of heaviness.
- Do not give yourself to heaviness, because heaviness and sorrow have killed many people. Through faith and comfort of the scriptures you will have hope, because in this walk you look to this person and that person for comfort and find none. We have to deliver our own soul by our own righteousness.
- I looked for some to take pity, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none. They gave me gall for my meat, and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. This goes into Christ.

You think you are a Christian and good with God going to church every Sunday, but it does not work like that.

- This is why blindness happened to the southern Kingdom leadership that forsook Christ; their eyes were darkened and their table became a snare. You read about all this in [[Romans 11|Romans 11]].
- Add iniquity unto their iniquity, and let them not come into Thy righteousness; let them be blotted out of the book of the living and not be written with the righteous. That is a heavy statement.

# Psalm 70: Make Haste to Deliver Me

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 70]]

[[Psalms 70|Psalm 70]] is the short, urgent cry for the Most High to make haste to deliver, that the enemies who say aha be turned back in shame while those who love His salvation rejoice. The teaching closes the four chapters on patience: the Lord is long suffering, a day is like a thousand years to Him, and in the last days people will ask where is the sign of His coming, but we are to trust that He will deliver the marked remnant in His acceptable time.

**[[Psalms 70#^v1|Psalms 70:1-3]]**

![[Psalms 70#^v1]]
![[Psalms 70#^v2]]
![[Psalms 70#^v3]]

- Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord.
- Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul; let them be turned backward and put to confusion that desire my hurt; let them be turned back that say aha, aha.

**[[Psalms 70#^v4|Psalms 70:4-5]]**

![[Psalms 70#^v4]]
![[Psalms 70#^v5]]

- Let all those that seek Thee rejoice and be glad in Thee, and let such as love Thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.
- But I am poor and needy; make haste unto me, O God; Thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.
- The scripture says a day is like a thousand years to the Lord, and in the last days people will say where is the sign of His coming, but the Lord is long suffering toward us, not willing that any perish but that all come to repentance, meaning all marked to be saved, the 144,000 and the one third. Be patient and trust that the Lord is going to deliver us.
