# Psalm 107: O Give Thanks unto the Lord

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 107]]

This session covers [[Psalms 107|Psalms 107]] through 110, four chapters a day for the nation. [[Psalms 107|Psalm 107]] opens with thanks to the Most High because His mercy endures forever, and the teaching shows that His mercy is to those that keep His commandments. The chapter runs the whole cycle of Israel: the redeemed gathered out of the four corners of the earth, the affliction that came because we rebelled against the words of God, and the deliverance that comes when we cry unto the Lord and acknowledge our offenses.

**[[Psalms 107#^v1|Psalms 107:1]]**

![[Psalms 107#^v1]]

- Give thanks unto the Lord for He is good, for His mercy endureth forever. Throughout Psalms and throughout the Bible it says His mercy endures forever, and the teaching stops to show exactly what that mercy is.
- The mercy is to those that keep His commandments, so His commandments endure forever and they are not going away. This is the same as the grace spoken of in [[Ephesians 2#^v8|Ephesians 2:8]].
  - **[[Exodus 20#^v6|Exodus 20:6]]**
    ![[Exodus 20#^v6]]
    The mercy is shown unto thousands of them that love the Most High and keep His commandments.
  - **[[Ephesians 2#^v8|Ephesians 2:8]]**
    ![[Ephesians 2#^v8]]
    This grace, this mercy, is the same grace through faith spoken of here.

**[[Psalms 107#^v2|Psalms 107:2]]**

![[Psalms 107#^v2]]

- Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom He has redeemed from the hand of the enemy. The redeemed are the Israelites, Zion, and they are the ones that need to say these things.
- Zion shall be redeemed with judgment, and to be converted you have to be keeping God's laws, His righteousness. When you forsake the Lord's laws you are consumed together with the sinners.
  - **[[Isaiah 1#^v27|Isaiah 1:27-28]]**
    ![[Isaiah 1#^v27]]
    ![[Isaiah 1#^v28]]
    Zion, the Israelites, shall be redeemed with judgment and her converts with righteousness, but the transgressors and sinners that forsake the Lord shall be consumed together.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 6#^v25|Deuteronomy 6:25]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 6#^v25]]
    To be converted you must be keeping God's righteousness, His commandments.
  - **[[1 Kings 8#^v1|1 Kings 8:1]]**
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v1]]
    Zion is Israel.
  - **[[Isaiah 59#^v20|Isaiah 59:20]]**
    ![[Isaiah 59#^v20]]
    The Redeemer, referring to Christ, shall come to Zion and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob.

- He led them forth by the right way that they might go to a city of habitation. This is the pattern: crying to the Most High in trouble brings deliverance.

# Psalm 108: My Heart Is Fixed

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 108]]

[[Psalms 108|Psalm 108]] is a psalm of singing praise to the Most High among the nations, letting your light shine. It declares that Israel, the beloved, may be delivered and saved with the Lord's right hand, and it names the tribes and the enemies, showing that the Israelites will be saved by keeping God's commandments.

**[[Psalms 108#^v1|Psalms 108:1-5]]**

![[Psalms 108#^v1]]
![[Psalms 108#^v2]]
![[Psalms 108#^v3]]
![[Psalms 108#^v4]]
![[Psalms 108#^v5]]

- O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise even with my glory. Awake, psaltery and harp; I myself will awake early. We are to be singing praise to the Lord all the time amongst the nations, letting your light shine.
- I will praise thee among the people and sing praises unto thee among the nations, for thy mercy is great above the heavens and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds. Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory above all the earth.

**[[Psalms 108#^v6|Psalms 108:6-9]]**

![[Psalms 108#^v6]]
![[Psalms 108#^v7]]
![[Psalms 108#^v8]]
![[Psalms 108#^v9]]

- That thy beloved may be delivered, save with thy right hand and answer me. The beloved is Israel, and the right hand is full of righteousness, so the Israelites will be saved by keeping God's commandments.
- God has spoken in His holiness: Gilead is His, Manasseh is His, Ephraim is the strength of His head, and Judah is His lawgiver. Judah being the lawgiver is a prophecy of Christ, from whom the sceptre and rulership shall not depart.
- Moab is His washpot, the servant, and over Edom He casts out His shoe, treating Edom as a slave to clean His shoes.
  - **[[Baruch 3#^v35|Baruch 3:35-36]]**
    ![[Baruch 3#^v35]]
    ![[Baruch 3#^v36]]
    Israel is the beloved of the Bible, and this is our God, the God of our forefathers.
  - **[[Psalms 48#^v10|Psalms 48:10]]**
    ![[Psalms 48#^v10]]
    According to His name, so is His praise unto the ends of the earth; His right hand is full of righteousness.
  - **[[Genesis 49#^v10|Genesis 49:10]]**
    ![[Genesis 49#^v10]]
    The sceptre shall not depart from Judah nor a lawgiver from between his feet until Shiloh come, the prophecy of Christ, unto whom the gathering of the people shall be.

**[[Psalms 108#^v10|Psalms 108:10-13]]**

![[Psalms 108#^v10]]
![[Psalms 108#^v11]]
![[Psalms 108#^v12]]
![[Psalms 108#^v13]]

- Who will bring me into the strong city, who will lead me into Edom? Wilt not thou, O God, who hadst cast us off, and wilt not thou go forth with our hosts? We must call upon the name of the Lord to give us help from trouble, and the trouble is our enemies.
- Give us help from trouble, for vain is the help of man. Through God we shall do valiantly, for it is He that shall tread down our enemies.

David gives himself unto prayer, letting God fight his battles against the nations that reward evil for good, and asks that the reward of the adversaries come from the Lord.

# Psalm 110: Sit Thou at My Right Hand

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 110]]

[[Psalms 110|Psalm 110]] is the prophecy of Christ seated at the right hand of the Father until His enemies are made His footstool. The Most High sends the rod of His strength out of Zion, and Christ, a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek, rules in the midst of His enemies and shall judge among the heathen in the day of His wrath, waiting on Israel to keep God's laws so He can activate.

**[[Psalms 110#^v1|Psalms 110:1]]**

![[Psalms 110#^v1]]

- The Lord said unto my Lord, sit thou at my right hand until I make thine enemies thy footstool. The one sitting at the Father's right hand is Christ.
  - **[[Acts 2#^v34|Acts 2:34-36]]**
    ![[Acts 2#^v34]]
    ![[Acts 2#^v35]]
    ![[Acts 2#^v36]]
    David is not ascended into the heavens, but he himself says the Lord said unto my Lord, sit thou on my right hand; so let all the house of Israel know that God has made that same Jesus, whom they crucified, both Lord and Christ.

**[[Psalms 110#^v2|Psalms 110:2-3]]**

![[Psalms 110#^v2]]
![[Psalms 110#^v3]]

- The Lord shall send the rod of thy strength out of Zion; rule thou in the midst of thine enemies. The rod is Christ ruling in the midst of the enemies.
- Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning; thou hast the dew of thy youth. Israel is patiently waiting to be saved.

    ![[Daniel 7#^v13]]
    ![[Daniel 7#^v14]]
