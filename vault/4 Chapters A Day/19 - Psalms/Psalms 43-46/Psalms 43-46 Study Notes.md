# Psalm 43: Send Out Thy Light and Thy Truth

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 43]]

This session moves through [[Psalms 43|Psalms 43]] through 46, read for the nation four chapters a day. [[Psalms 43|Psalm 43]] is the plea of a man cast down, asking the Most High to judge his cause against an ungodly nation and to send out His light and truth to lead him back to the holy hill. The light is defined by the scriptures themselves: the commandment is a lamp and the law is light. The teaching threads through the countenance of the soul, the strength that comes from keeping the commandments, and the health that a merry heart gives even in oppression.

**[[Psalms 43#^v1|Psalms 43:1-2]]**

![[Psalms 43#^v1]]
![[Psalms 43#^v2]]

- David asks the Most High to judge him and plead his cause against an ungodly nation, and to deliver him from the deceitful and unjust man.
- He calls the Most High the God of his strength, then asks why he is cast off and why he goes mourning because of the oppression of the enemy.

**[[Psalms 43#^v3|Psalms 43:3]]**

![[Psalms 43#^v3]]

- The plea is for the Most High to send out His light and His truth so they can lead him to the holy hill and the tabernacles.
- The light is not a mystery: it is the commandment and the law. That is what leads a man to the altar of the Most High.
  - **[[Proverbs 6#^v23|Proverbs 6:23]]**
    ![[Proverbs 6#^v23]]
    The commandment is a lamp and the law is light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life.

**[[Psalms 43#^v4|Psalms 43:4-5]]**

![[Psalms 43#^v4]]
![[Psalms 43#^v5]]

- The altar of the Most High is called his exceeding joy, and he purposes to praise Him upon the harp.
- He questions his own soul, asking why it is cast down and disquieted, and answers himself: hope in God, for He is the health of my countenance.
- The health of the countenance ties to a merry heart. You can always tell when someone carries something heavy; a broken spirit shows on the face. If you truly pay attention to somebody, you can tell when things are not going well with them.
  - **[[Psalms 4#^v5|Psalms 4:5-8]]**
    ![[Psalms 4#^v5]]
    ![[Psalms 4#^v6]]
    ![[Psalms 4#^v7]]
    ![[Psalms 4#^v8]]
    Offer the sacrifices of righteousness and trust in the Most High; the light of His countenance puts gladness in the heart more than corn and wine increase.
  - **[[Proverbs 15#^v13|Proverbs 15:13]]**
    ![[Proverbs 15#^v13]]
    A merry heart makes a cheerful countenance, but by sorrow of the heart the spirit is broken.

# Psalm 44: We Got Not the Land by Our Own Sword

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 44]]

[[Psalms 44|Psalm 44]] recounts how the fathers took the land, not by their own strength but by the right hand of the Most High, and then turns to lament the present captivity. The teaching drives home that the scattering, the shame, the byword status, and the spoiling all trace back to breaking the commandments. This is the difference between coming into the truth and reading and studying, versus staying in Christianity not knowing who your enemy really is.

**[[Psalms 44#^v1|Psalms 44:1-3]]**

![[Psalms 44#^v1]]
![[Psalms 44#^v2]]
![[Psalms 44#^v3]]

- The fathers told what work the Most High did in the times of old, driving out the heathen and planting Israel in the land.
- Israel did not get the land by their own sword or their own arm, but by the right hand and the light of the countenance of the Most High, because He had favor toward them.
- When brothers ask "were you there?" or "how you know what color he was?" the answer is that the fathers heard and witnessed the Red Sea and the wars, and they told the following generations. We are supposed to search the scriptures.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 32#^v7|Deuteronomy 32:7]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v7]]
    Remember the days of old; ask thy father and he will show thee, thy elders and they will tell thee.
  - **[[Malachi 4#^v4|Malachi 4:4-5]]**
    ![[Malachi 4#^v4]]
    ![[Malachi 4#^v5]]
    Remember the law of Moses for all Israel; Elijah is sent to turn the heart of the fathers to the children before the great and dreadful day.
  - **[[Romans 15#^v4|Romans 15:4]]**
    ![[Romans 15#^v4]]
    Things written before were written for our learning, sending us back to find the righteous path.

**[[Psalms 44#^v4|Psalms 44:4-5]]**

![[Psalms 44#^v4]]
![[Psalms 44#^v5]]

- The Most High is called the king who commands deliverance for Jacob, and through Him the enemies are pushed down.
- We cannot go it alone. Those who take up arms on their own without the Most High have examples of failure; men who went to make a name for themselves fell.
- Treading down the enemy through His name is not screaming a name you believe is the name of the Lord. The name means keeping the commandments.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v1|Deuteronomy 28:1,7]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v1]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v7]]
    Hearken diligently to His commandments and He sets thee on high and causes thine enemies to be smitten before thy face, fleeing seven ways.

![[Psalms 44#^v6]]
![[Psalms 44#^v7]]
![[Psalms 44#^v8]]

- David will not trust in his bow, nor will his sword save him; the Most High saved them from their enemies and put to shame those that hate them.
- In God they boast all the day long and praise His name forever. We are looking for that same salvation, which will be the destruction of our enemies.
  - **[[Luke 1#^v70|Luke 1:70-71]]**
    ![[Luke 1#^v70]]
    ![[Luke 1#^v71]]
    Salvation is that we should be saved from our enemies and from the hand of all that hate us.

![[Psalms 44#^v10]]
![[Psalms 44#^v11]]
![[Psalms 44#^v12]]

- Now the Most High has cast off and put them to shame, and goes not out with their armies; He makes them turn back and those that hate them spoil for themselves.
- They are given like sheep appointed for meat and scattered among the heathen. The enemy gained the power to spoil us because the Most High gave us over for our sins.
- He sold His people for nothing and did not increase His wealth by their price.
  - **[[Isaiah 42#^v24|Isaiah 42:24]]**
    ![[Isaiah 42#^v24]]

  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v15|Deuteronomy 28:15,25]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v15]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v25]]

![[Psalms 44#^v13]]
![[Psalms 44#^v14]]

- Israel became a reproach, a scorn, a derision, a byword, and a shaking of the head among the heathen.
- The shaking of the head is because we are an astonishment to our enemies. We went into slavery, our names were changed, our culture and lands were taken, and another nation took our name.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v37|Deuteronomy 28:37]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v37]]
    Thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword among all nations whither the Most High leads thee.

    ![[Lamentations 2#^v15]]

![[Psalms 44#^v15]]
![[Psalms 44#^v16]]

- When the enemy looks at us now, they no longer see a perfection of beauty. They see men sagging their pants, women dressed immodestly, whoredom in the songs, and they shake their heads.
- They will call us any name we want except Esau, as long as we keep celebrating Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Year's, the 4th of July, and birthdays. If they keep us in sin, the Most High will not fight our battles.

- Yet He has sore broken them in the place of dragons and covered them with the shadow of death. The broken part is that He brought down their heart with labor and there was none to help.
- This place is spiritual Egypt. The nations dealt wisely with us and put us to hard labor and bondage, and we are the essential workers on the front line.

# Psalm 45: Thy Throne, O God, Is Forever

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 45]]

[[Psalms 45|Psalm 45]] is a king's psalm, fairer than the children of men, girding his sword and riding prosperously because of truth and meekness and righteousness. The teaching applies the throne to Christ, the everlasting scepter of righteousness held by the ruler from Judah, and the daughter who must forget her own people and father's house to please her lord.

**[[Psalms 45#^v1|Psalms 45:1-5]]**

![[Psalms 45#^v1]]
![[Psalms 45#^v2]]
![[Psalms 45#^v3]]
![[Psalms 45#^v4]]
![[Psalms 45#^v5]]

- The heart indites a good matter concerning the king, who is fairer than the children of men with grace poured into his lips.
- He girds his sword upon his thigh and rides prosperously because of truth, meekness, and righteousness, and his arrows are sharp in the heart of the king's enemies.

**[[Psalms 45#^v6|Psalms 45:6]]**

![[Psalms 45#^v6]]

- The throne of the Most High is forever and ever, and the scepter of the kingdom is a right scepter.
- That rulership belongs to Christ from the tribe of Judah, and if we keep the commandments we will be heirs with Him in the kingdom.

    ![[Genesis 49#^v8]]
    ![[Genesis 49#^v9]]
    ![[Genesis 49#^v10]]

- The scepter of righteousness means there will be peace and righteousness in the kingdom, no more of the wickedness and Jacob's trouble we suffer now.

# Psalm 46: God Is Our Refuge and Maketh Wars to Cease

Read the chapter → [[Psalms 46]]

[[Psalms 46|Psalm 46]] declares the Most High a refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble, unshaken though the earth be removed. The teaching centers on the promise that He makes wars to cease to the ends of the earth, tying it to Christ's prophecy of wars and rumors of wars as the beginning of sorrows, and to the last-days beating of swords into plowshares.

**[[Psalms 46#^v1|Psalms 46:1-3]]**

![[Psalms 46#^v1]]
![[Psalms 46#^v2]]
![[Psalms 46#^v3]]

- The Most High is a refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble, so we will not fear though the earth be removed and the mountains carried into the sea.
- Though the waters roar and be troubled and the mountains shake, the confidence stands.

**[[Psalms 46#^v4|Psalms 46:4-6]]**

![[Psalms 46#^v4]]
![[Psalms 46#^v5]]
![[Psalms 46#^v6]]

- There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the tabernacles of the Most High, and God is in the midst of her; He helps her right early.
- The heathen raged and the kingdoms were moved; He uttered His voice and the earth melted.

**[[Psalms 46#^v7|Psalms 46:7-9]]**

![[Psalms 46#^v7]]
![[Psalms 46#^v8]]
![[Psalms 46#^v9]]

- The Lord of hosts is with us and the God of Jacob is our refuge; behold the desolations He has made in the earth.
- He makes wars to cease unto the end of the earth, breaks the bow, cuts the spear, and burns the chariot in the fire.
- Right now the Most High has not stopped the wars, so this is a prophecy not yet come. We are in the midst of wars and rumors of wars, the beginning of sorrows.
