# Jeremiah 1: The Prophet Ordained unto the Nations

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 1]]

This session, taught in Memphis, covers Jeremiah chapters 1 through 3. Jeremiah is called and set over the nations to root out and to plant; the Most High then lays out His charge against Israel, who has forsaken Him, the fountain of living waters, and gone after other gods. The same word spoken to Jeremiah is set here against the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans today: the men and women the Most High knew from the womb are being raised up to prophesy against the nations and to call a backsliding people to return.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v1|Jeremiah 1:1-3]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v3]]

- The words of Jeremiah, son of Hilkiah of the priests in Anathoth, came in the days of Josiah, through Jehoiakim, and to the end of Zedekiah, up to the carrying away of Jerusalem captive.
- The setting is fixed at the very edge of the captivity, so the prophet is speaking as judgment is already closing in on Judah.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v4|Jeremiah 1:4-5]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v5]]

- Before He formed Jeremiah in the belly the Most High knew him, sanctified him, and ordained him a prophet unto the nations.
- The same thing said to Jeremiah is what is happening today: the brothers and sisters waking up were formed in the belly and known from the beginning, raised up to prophesy against the nations.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v6|Jeremiah 1:6-8]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v6]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v8]]

- Jeremiah answers that he cannot speak, for he is a child, but the Most High tells him not to say he is a child, for he must go and speak whatever he is commanded.
- Be not afraid of their faces, for the Most High is with him to deliver him.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v9|Jeremiah 1:9-10]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v10]]

- The Most High puts forth His hand, touches Jeremiah's mouth, and says He has put His words in his mouth; this is why we stay within these scriptures and never go wrong.
- He is set over the nations and the kingdoms to root out, pull down, destroy, throw down, build, and plant, which is our job today and is spiritual warfare.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v11|Jeremiah 1:11-12]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v12]]

- Jeremiah is shown a rod of an almond tree, and the Most High says He will hasten His word to perform it.
- The vision confirms that the word spoken will be carried out and not delayed.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v13|Jeremiah 1:13-15]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v13]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v15]]

- The second vision is a seething pot with its face toward the north, and out of the north the evil shall break forth upon all the inhabitants of the land.
- The Most High calls all the families of the kingdoms of the north, who set their thrones at the entering of the gates of Jerusalem and against the cities of Judah; the judgment comes through the other nations because of our sins.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v16|Jeremiah 1:16]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v16]]

- The Most High utters His judgments against them for their wickedness in forsaking Him, burning incense to other gods, and worshipping the works of their own hands.
- The charge is idolatry, the very thing the rest of the reading opens up in detail.

**[[Jeremiah 1#^v17|Jeremiah 1:17-19]]**

![[Jeremiah 1#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 1#^v19]]

- Gird up thy loins, arise, and speak all that is commanded; be not dismayed at their faces, lest the Most High confound Jeremiah before them. We cannot go out with a spirit of fear on us.
- He is made a defenced city, an iron pillar, and brasen walls against the whole land; they shall fight but not prevail, for none can prevail against the word of the Most High going out through the prophets.

# Jeremiah 2: Israel's Two Evils and the Forsaken Fountain

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 2]]

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v1|Jeremiah 2:1-3]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v3]]

- The word comes to cry aloud in the ears of Jerusalem: the Most High remembers the kindness of Israel's youth and her love in the wilderness.
- Israel was holiness unto the Most High and the firstfruits of His increase, and all that devour him shall offend; the nations have devoured us because of our sins.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v4|Jeremiah 2:4-6]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v6]]

- This is the question the Most High is asking the so-called blacks and Hispanics today: what iniquity have your fathers found in Me, that they are gone far from Me, walked after vanity, and become vain.
- They did not ask where is the Most High that brought them up out of Egypt and led them through the wilderness; our people keep doing these same things.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v7|Jeremiah 2:7]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v7]]

- He brought them into a plentiful country to eat its fruit and goodness, but when they entered they defiled the land and made His heritage an abomination.
- That is Israel: we had it good, and we messed it up.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v8|Jeremiah 2:8]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v8]]

- The priests did not ask where is the Most High, they that handle the law knew Him not, and the pastors transgressed against Him.
- The prophets prophesied by Baal and walked after things that do not profit; that is the Christian pastor today, breaking the laws and worshipping Satan.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v9|Jeremiah 2:9-11]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v11]]

- The Most High will yet plead with Israel and with their children's children; pass over to the isles of Chittim and see whether a nation has changed its gods, which are yet no gods.
- But My people have changed their glory for that which does not profit; the glory is the kingdom rulership, traded away for things that do not profit.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v12|Jeremiah 2:12-13]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v13]]

- The heavens are called to be astonished, for My people have committed two evils.
- They have forsaken the Most High, the fountain of living waters, and hewed themselves broken cisterns that can hold no water, meaning they hold no truth.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v14|Jeremiah 2:14-16]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v16]]

- Is Israel a servant, is he a homeborn slave, why is he spoiled? That is the question for the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans today.
- The young lions roared upon him and made his land waste, and the children of Noph and Tahapanes, the Egyptians, have broken the crown of his head.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v17|Jeremiah 2:17-19]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v19]]

- Israel procured this unto himself by forsaking the Most High; what has he to do in the way of Egypt to drink the waters of Sihor, or in the way of Assyria to drink the waters of the river.
- Drinking the waters means learning the philosophies and ways of Egypt; thine own wickedness shall correct thee, and thy backslidings shall reprove thee.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v20|Jeremiah 2:20-21]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v20]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v21]]

- Of old the Most High broke Israel's yoke and burst his bands, yet upon every high hill and under every green tree he wandered, playing the harlot in spiritual idolatry.
- He was planted a noble vine, a right seed, but turned into the degenerate plant of a strange vine: worshipping the white man Jesus and the cobblestone.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v22|Jeremiah 2:22-25]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v22]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v23]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v24]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v25]]

- Though washed with nitre and much soap, the iniquity is marked before the Most High; how can Israel say I am not polluted, being a swift dromedary and a wild ass used to the wilderness that snuffs up the wind in her time.
- Israel said there is no hope, for I have loved strangers, and after them will I go; the strangers and the white man, mainly Esau, cannot save us.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v26|Jeremiah 2:26-28]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v26]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v27]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v28]]

- As a thief is ashamed when found, so is the house of Israel ashamed, their kings, princes, priests, and prophets, saying to a stock thou art my father and to a stone thou hast brought me forth: the wooden cross and the cobblestone.
- They turned their backs and not their faces, but in the time of trouble they say arise and save us; where are the gods they have made, let them arise if they can save.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v29|Jeremiah 2:29-32]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v29]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v30]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v31]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v32]]

- The Most High has smitten their children, but they received no correction, and their own sword devoured their prophets like a destroying lion.
- He asks whether He has been a wilderness to Israel; a maid does not forget her ornaments nor a bride her attire, yet My people have forgotten Me days without number.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v33|Jeremiah 2:33-35]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v33]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v34]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v35]]

- Why do you trim your way to seek love from the nations that hate us, and thereby teach even the wicked ones your ways?
- In thy skirts is found the blood of the souls of the poor innocents, yet thou sayest I am innocent; the Most High will plead against that claim.

**[[Jeremiah 2#^v36|Jeremiah 2:36-37]]**

![[Jeremiah 2#^v36]]
![[Jeremiah 2#^v37]]

- Israel gads about so much to change his way, leaning on one nation after another; he shall be ashamed of Egypt as he was ashamed of Assyria.
- Egypt today is spiritual, the white man Esau, and Israel will be ashamed of leaning on the nations, for the Most High has rejected his confidences and he shall not prosper in them.

# Jeremiah 3: Backsliding Israel Called to Return

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 3]]

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v1|Jeremiah 3:1]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v1]]

- If a man puts away his wife and she becomes another man's, he cannot return to her, for the land would be greatly polluted.
- Israel has played the harlot with many lovers, yet the Most High still says return again to Me.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v2|Jeremiah 3:2-3]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v3]]

- Lift up your eyes to the high places and see where Israel has not been lien with; sitting for lovers like the Arabian in the wilderness, she polluted the land with her whoredoms and wickedness.
- This is spiritual fornication and idolatry, learning about Allah from the Arabians and celebrating Islam; therefore the showers and the latter rain were withheld, and she had a whore's forehead and refused to be ashamed.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v4|Jeremiah 3:4-5]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v5]]

- The Most High asks whether Israel will now cry unto Him, My father, thou art the guide of my youth.
- Will He reserve His anger forever? Yet Israel has spoken and done evil things as much as he could.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v6|Jeremiah 3:6-8]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v6]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v8]]

- In the days of Josiah the Most High saw that backsliding Israel had gone up upon every high mountain and under every green tree and played the harlot, and her treacherous sister Judah saw it.
- For all the causes of her adultery He put her away and gave her a bill of divorce, no longer dealing with the northern kingdom, yet Judah feared not and played the harlot also.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v9|Jeremiah 3:9-10]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v10]]

- Through the lightness of her whoredom Israel defiled the land and committed adultery with stones and with stocks: the stones of Islam and the stock, the wood, of Christianity.
- Yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah did not turn to Him with her whole heart, but only feignedly.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v11|Jeremiah 3:11-13]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v13]]

- Backsliding Israel has justified herself more than treacherous Judah; the Most High sends the word toward the north, saying return, backsliding Israel, for I am merciful and will not keep anger forever.
- We have a merciful God whose mercy endures forever; only acknowledge thine iniquity, that thou hast transgressed against the Most High and scattered thy ways to the strangers under every green tree.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v14|Jeremiah 3:14-15]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v15]]

- Turn, O backsliding children, for the Most High is married unto us; He will take one of a city and two of a family and bring them to Zion.
- He will give pastors according to His heart to feed us with knowledge and understanding; a pastor is supposed to feed us with the laws of God, so if your pastor is not feeding you God's laws, you need to run.
  - **[[Malachi 2#^v7|Malachi 2:7]]**
    ![[Malachi 2#^v7]]
    The priest's lips should keep knowledge, for knowledge is God's laws, which the pastor is supposed to feed the people.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v16|Jeremiah 3:16-17]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v16]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v17]]

- When Israel is multiplied and increased in the land, they shall no more speak of the ark of the covenant nor remember it.
- At that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of the Most High, the capital city on the planet, and all the nations shall be gathered unto it, no more walking after their evil hearts.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v18|Jeremiah 3:18]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v18]]

- In those days the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel, coming together out of the land of the north.
- The land of the north is North America; that is the prophecy, that we are coming out to the land given for an inheritance to our fathers.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v19|Jeremiah 3:19-20]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v20]]

- The Most High asks how He shall put Israel among the children and give him a pleasant land, a goodly heritage; and He desires that Israel call Him My father and not turn away.
- But surely as a wife treacherously departs from her husband, so has the house of Israel dealt treacherously with the Most High.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v21|Jeremiah 3:21-22]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v21]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v22]]

- A voice is heard upon the high places, weeping and supplications of the children of Israel, for they have perverted their way and forgotten the Most High their God.
- Return, ye backsliding children, and He will heal your backslidings; the call is to repent and return from the high places of idolatry.

**[[Jeremiah 3#^v23|Jeremiah 3:23-25]]**

![[Jeremiah 3#^v23]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v24]]
![[Jeremiah 3#^v25]]

- In vain is salvation hoped for from the hills and the multitude of mountains; truly in the Most High our God is the salvation of Israel.
- Shame has devoured the labour of our fathers, and we lie down in our shame while our confusion covers us; in Christianity people are confused, taught that a degree or college will make everything okay while the nation still hates them, because they have not been properly trained in the Bible or fed God's laws, and we and our fathers have sinned and not obeyed the voice of the Most High to this day.
