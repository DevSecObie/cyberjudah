# Jeremiah 12: Why Do the Wicked Prosper

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 12]]

This is the fourth chapter reading, a habit set so brothers and sisters read at least four chapters every day. Four chapters is not a lot, and reading the scriptures front to back gives a broad understanding while the precepts open up more as you learn and grow. The point of the four chapter reading is to keep all of us constantly meditating in the word, as [[Joshua 1#^v8|Joshua 1:8]] commands. This session covers [[Jeremiah 12|Jeremiah 12]] through 15: Jeremiah pleading over the prosperity of the wicked, the marred linen girdle, the drought and the false prophets, and the Most High refusing to be entreated for a people who will not turn.

**[[Jeremiah 12#^v1|Jeremiah 12:1-3]]**
![[Jeremiah 12#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v3]]
- Jeremiah pleads with the Most High and asks why the way of the wicked prospers and why those who deal very treacherously are happy.
- We often look at the rich, at actors and actresses, doing the most wicked things. For many it is just a job, but some are involved in pretty wicked things, and we ask why they are prospering.
- They do not say that anything they have pertains to the Most High, and they do not thank Him for anything; it is just "it is what it is" with them. Jeremiah is expressing that same tension about the prosperity of the wicked.

**[[Jeremiah 12#^v4|Jeremiah 12:4-6]]**
![[Jeremiah 12#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v6]]
- Jeremiah asks how long the land will mourn while the beasts and birds are consumed, because the people say the Most High shall not see their last end.
- Even his own brethren in his father's house dealt treacherously with him and called a multitude after him. The Most High warns him to believe them not, though they speak fair words.

**[[Jeremiah 12#^v7|Jeremiah 12:7-9]]**
![[Jeremiah 12#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v9]]
- The Most High has forsaken His house and given the dearly beloved of His soul into the hand of her enemies, calling His heritage a speckled bird with all the other birds against her.
- There are many different shades to the Israelites. Israelites come in all shapes, sizes and colors, and a color does not equal a nationality. Your skin tone does not make you a nationality; what the Most High is saying is that the skin tones vary among Israel.
  - **[[Hosea 7#^v8|Hosea 7:8]]**
    ![[Hosea 7#^v8]]
    Israel mixed among the people is the same speckled heritage, one people made of many shades.

**[[Jeremiah 12#^v10|Jeremiah 12:10-13]]**
![[Jeremiah 12#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v13]]
- Many pastors have destroyed the vineyard and made the pleasant portion a desolate wilderness, and the whole land is made desolate because no man lays it to heart.
- The sword of the Most High devours from one end of the land to the other, and no flesh shall have peace. They sow wheat but reap thorns.
- Even though many of these people are rich and doing many things in the world, if they are not keeping the commandments none of it will matter. When the Most High gives you something you are to keep His commandments, or it becomes a stumbling block, and you can lose your life over what you think you have.

**[[Jeremiah 12#^v14|Jeremiah 12:14-17]]**
![[Jeremiah 12#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v16]]
![[Jeremiah 12#^v17]]
- Against His evil neighbors who touch the inheritance He gave Israel, the Most High will pluck them out of their land, and pluck Judah out from among them, then return and have compassion and bring every man back to his heritage.
- If those nations will diligently learn the ways of His people and swear by His name, they shall be built in the midst of His people; but if they will not obey He will utterly pluck and destroy that nation.
- When the Israelites are the ruling class on earth, every nation will follow the laws of the Most High. We will force every nation to keep the commandments, and if they will not they have a price to pay. That is the patience and the faith of the saints.

# Jeremiah 13: The Marred Linen Girdle

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 13]]

**[[Jeremiah 13#^v1|Jeremiah 13:1-7]]**
![[Jeremiah 13#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v3]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v6]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v7]]
- The Most High tells Jeremiah to get a linen girdle, put it on his loins, and not put it in water. After wearing it he is sent to hide it by the Euphrates, in the area of Iraq.
- After many days he is told to go dig it up, and behold the girdle was marred and profitable for nothing.

**[[Jeremiah 13#^v8|Jeremiah 13:8-11]]**
![[Jeremiah 13#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v11]]
- After this manner the Most High will mar the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem, an evil people who refuse to hear His words and walk after other gods.
- There was a punishment for us going into idolatry and sin, and for being so prideful that we started to forget the Most High is in control of everything. Just as that girdle was good for nothing, the nation of Israel became the same way.
- As the girdle cleaves to the loins of a man, the Most High caused the whole house of Israel and Judah to cleave to Him for a name and a praise, but they would not hear.

**[[Jeremiah 13#^v12|Jeremiah 13:12-14]]**
![[Jeremiah 13#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v13]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v14]]
- Every bottle shall be filled with wine, and the Most High will fill all the inhabitants of the land, even the kings on David's throne and the priests and prophets, with drunkenness, and dash them one against another.
- He will smash the fathers and the sons together and will not pity nor spare nor have mercy. That is why there is such a big discrepancy in the black community between fathers and sons: sons grow without fathers, become fathers, and grow without their children. It is a repeating cycle.

**[[Jeremiah 13#^v15|Jeremiah 13:15-19]]**
![[Jeremiah 13#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v16]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v19]]
- Be not proud; give glory to the Most High before He causes darkness and your feet stumble on the dark mountains. If you will not hear, His soul weeps in secret places because the flock is carried away captive.
- The king and queen are told to humble themselves and sit down, for their principalities and crown shall come down, and Judah shall be wholly carried away captive.

**[[Jeremiah 13#^v20|Jeremiah 13:20-23]]**
![[Jeremiah 13#^v20]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v21]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v22]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v23]]
- Judah is asked what she will say when punished, for she taught her enemies to be captains over her, and sorrows will take her like a woman in travail.
- Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? Then may you also do good who are accustomed to do evil. The people of Ethiopia are very dark; the land is named the land of burnt faces, and they cannot get lighter. In the same way you cannot hide the evil that you do.

**[[Jeremiah 13#^v24|Jeremiah 13:24-27]]**
![[Jeremiah 13#^v24]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v25]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v26]]
![[Jeremiah 13#^v27]]
- Because she forgot the Most High and trusted in falsehood, He will scatter them as stubble and discover their skirts upon their face so their shame appears.
- He has seen the adulteries, the lewdness of the whoredoms, and the abominations. We see it day to day, and there is going to be a judgment for all of it.

# Jeremiah 14: The Drought and the False Prophets

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 14]]

**[[Jeremiah 14#^v1|Jeremiah 14:1-6]]**
![[Jeremiah 14#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v3]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v6]]
- The word comes concerning the dearth. Judah mourns, the gates languish, and the cry of Jerusalem goes up while the nobles send their little ones for water and find none.
- The ground is chapped because there is no rain, and the plowmen are ashamed and cover their heads. That is how you know these are black people; "chapped" is a word black people use, not just for lips but for the cracked, dry ground.

**[[Jeremiah 14#^v7|Jeremiah 14:7-9]]**
![[Jeremiah 14#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v9]]
- Though our iniquities testify against us, the plea is that the Most High act for His name's sake, for our backslidings are many. He is called the hope of Israel and the Savior in time of trouble.
- The cry is for Him not to be a stranger in the land or a mighty man that cannot save, since He is in the midst of us and we are called by His name.

**[[Jeremiah 14#^v10|Jeremiah 14:10-12]]**
![[Jeremiah 14#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v12]]
- The people loved to wander and did not refrain their feet, so the Most High does not accept them; He will remember their iniquity and visit their sins.
- He tells Jeremiah not to pray for this people; even when they fast He will not hear their cry, and their offerings He will not accept, but will consume them by sword, famine and pestilence.
  - **[[John 9#^v31|John 9:31]]**
    ![[John 9#^v31]]
    The Most High does not hear sinners; He told Jeremiah not to pray for them because their sins were, and are, so many, which is why it behooves all of us to repent.

**[[Jeremiah 14#^v13|Jeremiah 14:13-16]]**
![[Jeremiah 14#^v13]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v16]]
- Jeremiah answers that the prophets tell the people they shall not see sword or famine but shall have assured peace. This is what the Christian pastors do today; the verse above said not to pray for these people, yet the false prophets promise nothing but peace and prosperity.
- The Most High says those prophets prophesy lies in His name; He did not send them. By sword and famine those prophets shall be consumed, and the people they deceived cast out in the streets with none to bury them.
- What happened back then is happening today. This country is going to go down by nuclear destruction, whether our people accept it or not, while the false prophets say nothing but peace, and they will be dealt with like all the other nations.

**[[Jeremiah 14#^v17|Jeremiah 14:17-22]]**
![[Jeremiah 14#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v20]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v21]]
![[Jeremiah 14#^v22]]
- Let the eyes run down with tears night and day, for the virgin daughter of the people is broken with a great breach; the slain fill the field and the sick with famine fill the city, while prophet and priest wander into a land they know not.
- We acknowledge our wickedness and the iniquity of our fathers and ask Him not to abhor us for His name's sake or break His covenant. None of the vanities of the gentiles can cause rain; only the Most High, so we wait upon Him.
- We have to acknowledge our offense and the wickedness we have been doing, even the wickedness we support because it does not affect us directly. Your salvation is contingent on keeping the commandments and abhorring that evil.

# Jeremiah 15: The Prophet's Burden

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 15]]

**[[Jeremiah 15#^v1|Jeremiah 15:1-2]]**
![[Jeremiah 15#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v2]]
- Though Moses and Samuel stood before Him, the mind of the Most High could not be toward this people; He says cast them out of My sight.
- When the people ask where they shall go, the answer is such as are for death to death, for the sword to the sword, for the famine to the famine, and for the captivity to the captivity. That is how we got removed from before the Most High.

**[[Jeremiah 15#^v3|Jeremiah 15:3-9]]**
![[Jeremiah 15#^v3]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v6]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v9]]
- He appoints four kinds over them: the sword to slay, the dogs to tear, and the fowls and beasts to devour, and He will remove them into all kingdoms of the earth because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah did in Jerusalem.
- He is weary with repenting and will bereave them of children because they return not from their ways; the mother of young men gets a spoiler at noonday, and she that bore seven languishes and gives up the ghost.
- That hit home; there was a sister last sabbath who had seven sons and was very much depressed. The scripture speaks to a real grief among the people.

**[[Jeremiah 15#^v10|Jeremiah 15:10-14]]**
![[Jeremiah 15#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v13]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v14]]
- Jeremiah cries woe over being born a man of strife and contention, cursed by everyone though he lent to no one on usury. The Most High answers that it shall be well with his remnant.
- That remnant is those who are keeping the commandments. Their substance and treasures will be given to the spoil for all their sins, and they will pass with their enemies into a land they do not know.

**[[Jeremiah 15#^v15|Jeremiah 15:15-18]]**
![[Jeremiah 15#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v16]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v18]]
- Jeremiah asks the Most High to remember him, visit him, and revenge him of his persecutors, and not to take him away in His longsuffering, since for His sake he has suffered rebuke.
- His words were found and Jeremiah did eat them, and they were the joy and rejoicing of his heart, for he is called by the name of the Most High and sat alone, filled with indignation.
- He asks why his pain is perpetual and his wound refuses to be healed. This is what the prophets go through: we bring healing, order, structure and discipline to our people and get hated and treated badly, and sometimes these are the thoughts we have.

**[[Jeremiah 15#^v19|Jeremiah 15:19-21]]**
![[Jeremiah 15#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v20]]
![[Jeremiah 15#^v21]]
- If Jeremiah returns, the Most High will bring him again to stand before Him; if he takes the precious from the vile he shall be as His mouth. Let them return to him, but he is not to return to them.
- He will make Jeremiah a fenced brazen wall; they shall fight against him but not prevail, for the Most High is with him to save and deliver him.
- That is what we live on. When we go out teaching this bible, and when brothers and sisters apply the commandments to their everyday lives, people will come against us, but the Most High is our defense. As long as we keep the commandments and the faith of Christ, no one can prevail, and if they try they will fail.
