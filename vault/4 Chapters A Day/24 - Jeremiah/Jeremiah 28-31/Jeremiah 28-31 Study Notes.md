# Jeremiah 28: Hananiah the False Prophet

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 28]]

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 28 through 31. It opens with Hananiah, a so-called prophet who prophesied peace and a short captivity against the word the Most High had given Jeremiah, and who dies the same year for teaching rebellion. Jeremiah then writes to the captives in Babylon to build, plant, and seek the peace of the city until the seventy years are accomplished, warning against the false prophets among them. The reading closes with the great promise of restoration: the time of Jacob's trouble out of which Israel is saved, the gathering from the four corners of the earth in a straight way, and the new covenant written in the inward parts.

- In the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah, in the fifth month, Hananiah the son of Azur, a prophet of Gibeon, spake in the house of the Lord before the priests and all the people.
- This so-called prophet spoke the things he claimed the Most High was going to do: that He had broken the yoke of the king of Babylon and within two full years would bring back the vessels of the Lord's house and Jeconiah with all the captives of Judah.

- Jeremiah answered Amen, the Lord do so, the Lord perform thy words to bring again the vessels and the captives; but he told Hananiah not to hold on to that.
- The prophets before them prophesied of war, of evil, and of pestilence, nothing of peace; so when a prophet prophesies peace, only when the word comes to pass is that prophet known to be truly sent of the Lord, and if it does not come to pass, the Lord is not working with him.

- He said thus saith the Lord, even so will I break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all nations within two full years; and Jeremiah went his way.

- The word of the Lord came to Jeremiah: thou hast broken the yokes of wood, but thou shalt make for them yokes of iron.
- The Most High had put a yoke of iron upon the neck of all these nations that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, who was the superpower at this time, and He gave him the beasts of the field also.

- Jeremiah told Hananiah the Lord had not sent him, but he made the people trust in a lie and was leading his people astray.
- Because he taught rebellion against the Lord, teaching the people to go against the Most High instead of accepting that they had to go through captivity, the Lord would cast him from off the face of the earth and he would die that year; Hananiah died the same year in the seventh month.

# Jeremiah 29: The Letter to the Captives

![[Jeremiah 29#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 29#^v2]]

- Just like in this captivity, our people still carried those talents and skills; we had carpenters and we had blacksmiths.

- They are to take wives and beget sons and daughters, take wives for their sons and husbands for their daughters, that they may be increased and not diminished, and seek the peace of the city where He caused them to be carried away, for in the peace thereof they shall have peace.

- After seventy years are accomplished at Babylon the Most High will visit them and perform His good word to return them, for His thoughts toward them are of peace and not of evil, to give them an expected end; the same is happening today, knowledge is being increased, we are finding out who we are and coming back to God's laws to be saved out of this captivity.
- When they call upon Him, seek Him, and search for Him with all their heart, He will be found of them and turn away their captivity, gathering them from all the nations and places whither He drove them; this is why the leadership goes out and teaches in the four corners of the earth, because all our people have been driven to the four corners.

# Jeremiah 30: The Time of Jacob's Trouble

- The word came to Jeremiah to write all the words the Lord had spoken unto him in a book, showing that the Most High was dealing with Jeremiah and giving him his instructions unlike the other prophets.
- The days come that He will bring again the captivity of His people Israel and Judah, the northern and southern kingdom, and cause them to return to the land He gave to their fathers to possess it.

- That day is great, so that none is like it; it is the time of Jacob's trouble, but he shall be saved out of it; this goes to the last days, this is prophecy of the great tribulation out of which Israel is saved.

- Fear thou not O Jacob, neither be dismayed, for He will save thee from afar and thy seed from the land of their captivity, and Jacob shall return and be in rest with none to make him afraid.
- Though He makes a full end of all the nations whither He scattered thee, He will not make a full end of thee, but correct thee in measure and not leave thee altogether unpunished; the punishment is for our good, He will not destroy us.

- He appeared of old saying He loved them with an everlasting love and with lovingkindness drew them; again He will build the virgin of Israel, adorned with her tabrets and going forth in the dances, planting vines on the mountains of Samaria until the watchmen on Mount Ephraim cry, Arise, let us go up to Zion unto the Lord our God.

- They shall come with weeping and supplications, and He will lead them and cause them to walk by the rivers of waters in a straight way wherein they shall not stumble, for He is a father to Israel and Ephraim is His firstborn; the straight way is keeping God's laws.

- Is Ephraim His dear son, a pleasant child? Since He spake against him He earnestly remembers him still, His bowels are troubled for him, and He will surely have mercy; Jeremiah speaks these words of the Lord to His people.

- The days come that the Most High will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of man and the seed of beast, so they shall have plenty.
- As He watched over them to pluck up, break down, and destroy, so will He watch over them to build and to plant.
- In those days they shall say no more that the fathers have eaten a sour grape and the children's teeth are set on edge, but every man shall die for his own iniquity; you die for your own sin, not for the sin of the next man, for that dreaded judgment comes behind iniquity.

**[[Jeremiah 31#^v31|Jeremiah 31:31-34]]**

![[Jeremiah 31#^v31]]
![[Jeremiah 31#^v32]]

- The Most High will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and the house of Judah, the northern and southern kingdom, not other nations, not according to the covenant He made with their fathers when He took them out of Egypt, which they brake although He was a husband unto them.
- Just as a husband and wife have a covenant for their marriage, the Most High uses that analogy for the covenant we have with Him.
- He will put His law in their inward parts and write it in their hearts, and be their God and they His people; they shall teach no more every man his neighbor, for they shall all know Him and keep His laws from the least to the greatest, and He will forgive their iniquity and remember their sin no more.

**[[Jeremiah 31#^v35|Jeremiah 31:35-37]]**

![[Jeremiah 31#^v35]]
![[Jeremiah 31#^v36]]
![[Jeremiah 31#^v37]]

- The Lord gives the sun for a light by day and the ordinances of the moon and the stars for a light by night, and divides the sea when the waves roar; the Lord of hosts is His name.
- For the Most High to cast away His people, those ordinances would have to depart, the sun would have to stop shining, the moon and stars stop giving light; that is not going to happen, so He is not casting away the seed of Israel.
- Only a small percentage of the earth has been discovered and the heavens above cannot be measured, so He will not cast off all the seed of Israel for all that they have done; you can even see where the seas are divided from the coast, the Pacific and the Atlantic, one seed of water divided from another.

- The whole valley of the dead bodies and of the ashes, and all the fields unto the brook of Kidron, unto the corner of the horse gate toward the east, shall be holy unto the Lord and shall not be plucked up nor thrown down anymore forever; this goes into the Kingdom.
