# Jeremiah 8: No Peace, No Salvation for a Backsliding People

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 8]]

This session covers [[Jeremiah 8|Jeremiah 8]] through 11, moving through the reading in verse order. The aim here is to promote the daily reading of the four chapters, making a point where a point needs to be made. The thread running through these chapters is a people under judgment who refuse to return: their bones scattered, their false peace exposed, their idols shown to be nothing, and their covenant broken. Passover is approaching, so the call is to examine yourself and purge the leaven out of your own spirit, not just out of your house.

**[[Jeremiah 8#^v1|Jeremiah 8:1-3]]**
![[Jeremiah 8#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v3]]
- This is a good present to use against the comedic brothers, the biblical doubters and heretics who mock the Bible asking where the bones of David, of Jeremiah, of Moses are. The scripture answers it: the bones of our forefathers were scattered and not buried, spread all over the earth. That is why you will not find them.

**[[Jeremiah 8#^v4|Jeremiah 8:4-6]]**
![[Jeremiah 8#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v6]]
- We do not understand how much mercy the Most High has shown the nation of Israel. Read from the beginning up until today and you see much mercy; we deserve all the persecution we get because we have a perpetual backsliding and refuse to return.
- Our people do not want to acknowledge their sins. No man repents saying "what have I done"; everyone turns to his own course like a horse rushing into battle.

**[[Jeremiah 8#^v7|Jeremiah 8:7-10]]**
![[Jeremiah 8#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v10]]
- Even the stork, the turtle, the crane and the swallow know their appointed times, but the Most High's people know not His judgment. The people claim to be wise and to have the law, but they have rejected the word of the Lord, so what wisdom is in them.

**[[Jeremiah 8#^v11|Jeremiah 8:11-13]]**
![[Jeremiah 8#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v13]]
- The prophets and priests healed the hurt of the daughter of the people only slightly, saying peace, peace when there is no peace.
- They committed abomination yet were not ashamed and could not even blush; therefore they shall fall in the time of their visitation.

**[[Jeremiah 8#^v14|Jeremiah 8:14-20]]**
![[Jeremiah 8#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v16]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v20]]
- Look at all the desolation and trouble we are going through today in America in this captivity. Just as Jeremiah's people were under the Babylonians, today we are under the mystery Babylonians.
- The harvest is passed, the summer is ended, and we are not saved. Our people think they are already redeemed and saved in the blood of Jesus, but no. We are still being murdered in the streets, locked up, persecuted, last hired and first fired, oppressed systematically and hated. We still have no land, no country, no standard, no god of our own, and the majority of Israelites do not even know they are Israelites and worship a white Jesus. We are not saved.

**[[Jeremiah 8#^v21|Jeremiah 8:21-22]]**
![[Jeremiah 8#^v21]]
![[Jeremiah 8#^v22]]
- For the hurt of the daughter of the people the prophet is hurt and black astonishment takes hold of him. Is there no balm in Gilead, is there no physician there, so why is the health of the people not recovered.

# Jeremiah 9: What to Glory In

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 9]]

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v1|Jeremiah 9:1-3]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v3]]
- The prophet wishes his head were waters and his eyes a fountain of tears to weep for the slain of his people. He wants a lodging place in the wilderness to leave them, because they are all adulterers and treacherous men who bend their tongues like a bow for lies and proceed from evil to evil.

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v4|Jeremiah 9:4-6]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v6]]
- Trust not in any brother, for every brother will supplant and every neighbor walks with slanders. We have seen this in IUIC and in many of the other camps.
- They have taught their tongue to speak lies and weary themselves to commit iniquity; through deceit they refuse to know the Most High.
  - **[[John 8#^v19|John 8:19]]**
    ![[John 8#^v19]]
    To refuse to know Him through deceit is to have no relationship with Him.

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v7|Jeremiah 9:7-9]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v9]]
- The Most High will melt and try them, because their tongue is an arrow shooting deceit. A man speaks peaceably to his neighbor with his mouth but in his heart lies in wait, thinking of a plot or scheme to destroy, steal from, or overcome him. That is how wicked our people are.

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v10|Jeremiah 9:10-16]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v13]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v16]]
- Because they forsook His law, did not obey His voice, and walked after the imagination of their own heart and after Baal, He will feed them with wormwood, give them water of gall, scatter them among the heathen, and send a sword after them until they are consumed.

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v17|Jeremiah 9:17-21]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v20]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v21]]
- Call for the mourning women to take up a wailing, for death has come up into the windows and into the palaces to cut off the children from the streets and the young men from the squares. This sounds like America, like the south side of Chicago, north Dallas, and southwest Houston.

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v22|Jeremiah 9:22-24]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v22]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v23]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v24]]
- Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, the mighty in his might, or the rich in his riches. Carnal things like looks, strength, and money all fade.
- Glory instead that you understand and know the Most High: that you have been awakened in these last days, shown the truth, the scriptures, the commandments, and what God requires to be saved from the destruction prophesied to come.
- You can know you are an Israelite, know the twelve feathers, the seven heads and ten horns, the mysteries of Genesis, and every precept, but if you have hatred for your brother or sister, if you are a slanderer, gossiper, liar, or fornicator, you will not see the kingdom, only fire. There is a difference between a prophet and a parakeet; anyone can mimic scriptures for a show while being unprofitable to himself.

**[[Jeremiah 9#^v25|Jeremiah 9:25-26]]**
![[Jeremiah 9#^v25]]
![[Jeremiah 9#^v26]]
- The days come when He will punish the circumcised along with the uncircumcised, because all the house of Israel is uncircumcised in the heart. It does not matter if you are circumcised after the manner of Abraham; if you fornicate or hold hatred, your circumcision is made uncircumcision through your sin. Circumcision only means something when you are obedient to the words and statutes of the Lord.

# Jeremiah 10: The Idols Are Nothing, the Lord Is the True God

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 10]]

**[[Jeremiah 10#^v1|Jeremiah 10:1-2]]**
![[Jeremiah 10#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v2]]
- Learn not the way of the heathen and be not dismayed at the signs of heaven. Astrology, numerology, horoscopes, and all this mysticism about the stars, moon, and their alignments cannot be proven. The Most High created those things for a purpose, not for you to worship or fear. People pay more attention to their horoscopes than to "thou shalt not kill," "thou shalt not steal," and "thou shalt not commit adultery."

**[[Jeremiah 10#^v3|Jeremiah 10:3-5]]**
![[Jeremiah 10#^v3]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v5]]
- The custom of cutting a tree out of the forest, decking it with silver and gold, and fastening it with nails and hammers so it does not move is the Christmas tree, the custom of modern Christmas.
- These idols are upright as a palm tree but cannot speak, must be carried because they cannot walk, and can do neither evil nor good; do not be afraid of them.

**[[Jeremiah 10#^v6|Jeremiah 10:6-10]]**
![[Jeremiah 10#^v6]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v8]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v10]]
- There is none like the Most High; He is great and His name is great in might, King of nations. The other nations and their wise men are all together brutish and foolish, their idols a doctrine of vanities. But the Lord is the true God, the living God and everlasting King, at whose wrath the earth trembles.

**[[Jeremiah 10#^v11|Jeremiah 10:11-16]]**
![[Jeremiah 10#^v11]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v13]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v14]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v16]]
- The gods that did not make the heaven and earth shall perish. He made the earth by His power, established the world by His wisdom, and stretched out the heavens; every man is brutish in his knowledge before Him.
- The portion of Jacob is not like the idols, for He is the former of all things and Israel is the rod of His inheritance. Anything good the nations do they got from the children of Israel; when you go into the history, they learned it from us.

**[[Jeremiah 10#^v17|Jeremiah 10:17-21]]**
![[Jeremiah 10#^v17]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v20]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v21]]
- He will sling out the inhabitants of the land at once and distress them. The tabernacle is spoiled, the cords broken, the children gone, because the pastors have become brutish and have not sought the Lord; therefore their flock is scattered.

**[[Jeremiah 10#^v22|Jeremiah 10:22-25]]**
![[Jeremiah 10#^v22]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v23]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v24]]
![[Jeremiah 10#^v25]]
- A great commotion comes out of the north country to make the cities of Judah desolate. The prophet knows the way of man is not in himself, so he asks to be corrected with judgment, not in anger, and prays that fury be poured out on the heathen that know not the Most High and have devoured Jacob.

# Jeremiah 11: The Broken Covenant

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 11]]

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v1|Jeremiah 11:1-3]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v1]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v2]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v3]]
- Hear the words of this covenant: cursed be the man that obeys not its words.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 27#^v26|Deuteronomy 27:26]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 27#^v26]]
    Cursed is everyone that confirms not all the words of this law to do them.
  - **[[Galatians 3#^v10|Galatians 3:10]]**
    ![[Galatians 3#^v10]]
    As many as are of the works of the law are under the curse without continuing in all things written.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 28#^v15|Deuteronomy 28:15]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 28#^v15]]
    All these curses come on those who will not obey the voice of the Lord.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v4|Jeremiah 11:4-6]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v4]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v5]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v6]]
- He commanded the fathers from the iron furnace of Egypt to obey His voice, so they would be His people and He their God, to perform the oath to give a land flowing with milk and honey.
- Just as you see the brothers in purple and gold out on the corners teaching every day in IUIC, the prophets did the exact same thing, proclaiming the words of the covenant in the streets. The thing that has been is that which shall be.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v7|Jeremiah 11:7-8]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v7]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v8]]
- He earnestly protested to the fathers from the day He brought them out of Egypt, rising early and saying obey My voice, yet they obeyed not but walked in the imagination of their evil heart; therefore He brings on them all the words of this covenant.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v9|Jeremiah 11:9-11]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v9]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v10]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v11]]
- A conspiracy is found among the men of Judah; they turned back to the iniquities of their fathers and went after other gods. Therefore He will bring evil they cannot escape, and though they cry to Him He will not hearken. This is the people crying and praying only after Little Ray Ray gets shot, when Ray Ray was a drug dealer, a fornicator, and a murderer, and the no-snitching mentality shielded a killer. Now they cry and the Lord says He will not hear them.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v12|Jeremiah 11:12-13]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v12]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v13]]
- The cities of Judah cry to the gods to whom they burn incense, but those gods cannot save them in the time of trouble. When all hell breaks loose on our people, they go pray to white-man Jesus. According to the number of their cities were their gods.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v14|Jeremiah 11:14]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v14]]
- Therefore pray not for this people, neither lift up a cry or prayer for them, for He will not hear them in the time they cry to Him for their trouble.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v15|Jeremiah 11:15-17]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v15]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v16]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v17]]
- What has His beloved to do in His house, having wrought lewdness with many. He had called her name a green olive tree fair and of goodly fruit, but with a great tumult He kindles fire on it and breaks its branches, for the evil of the house of Israel and the house of Judah, the northern and southern kingdoms, done to provoke Him.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v18|Jeremiah 11:18-20]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v18]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v19]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v20]]
- The Lord gave the prophet knowledge of their doings, but he had been like a lamb or ox brought to the slaughter, not knowing they had devised devices against him to destroy the tree with its fruit and cut him off from the land of the living. He commits his cause to the Lord who judges righteously and asks to see His vengeance on them.

**[[Jeremiah 11#^v21|Jeremiah 11:21-23]]**
![[Jeremiah 11#^v21]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v22]]
![[Jeremiah 11#^v23]]
- Against the men of Anathoth who sought his life and told him not to prophesy in the name of the Lord, He will punish them: the young men die by the sword, their sons and daughters by famine, and no remnant will be left in the year of their visitation.
