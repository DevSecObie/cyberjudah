# Jeremiah 48: The Prophecy Against Moab

Read the chapter → [[Jeremiah 48]]

This session covers [[Jeremiah 48|Jeremiah 48]] through 51, four chapters that move straight into the salvation of Israel by way of the prophesied destruction of our enemies. Jeremiah lived during the time of Babylon, and here he prophesies against Moab (the so-called Chinese), Ammon (the so-called Japanese), Edom (the so-called white man), Damascus, Kedar and Hazor, Elam (the ancient and modern Persians, the East Indians), and finally Babylon the Chaldeans (modern-day America). This is our redemption. Scripture says a Christian is to accept both the salvation of the righteous and the destruction of the enemies, so this reading should be music to your ears.
