# Job 1: The Trial of an Upright Man

Read the chapter → [[Job 1]]

The book of Job is a very deep book, a book that will not be fully explained here because some of it is above any man's pay grade. No one claims to know everything about Job. If you have questions regarding the book of Job, inbox them to IUIC classrooms and they will be answered in order received. This session covers [[Job 1|Job 1]] through 4: the introduction of Job, the conversation between Satan and the Most High, the loss of everything Job had, and the beginning of the talk between Job and his three friends.

**[[Job 1#^v1|Job 1:1]]**
![[Job 1#^v1]]
- This first verse tells you who Job is, what land he lived in, and his character. Just because he lived in the land of Uz does not mean he was of that nation, because he was a son of God.
- It is the same thing today: we live in America but we are not Caucasians. There are brothers that live in Europe and they are not Caucasians either, but they are the sons of God.

**[[Job 1#^v2|Job 1:2-5]]**
![[Job 1#^v2]]
![[Job 1#^v3]]
![[Job 1#^v4]]
![[Job 1#^v5]]
- Job had seven sons and three daughters, and his substance was seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen and five hundred she asses, so that this man was the greatest of all the men of the east.
- His sons went and feasted in their houses, every one his day. When it says "every man his day," they were talking about their birthdays. Whenever somebody's birthday came around they would say, "this is my day, this is my day."
- Now people have gotten to the point where it is, "this is my month, my birthday month, so I am celebrating the whole month." Same thing with this.
- Job sanctified them and rose up early to offer burnt offerings according to the number of them all, for Job said it may be that his sons had sinned and cursed God in their hearts. Thus did Job continually.
  - **[[Job 8#^v4|Job 8:4]]**
    ![[Job 8#^v4]]
    Bildad said if your children sinned against God, He is going to take them away; their sin was celebrating their birthdays, which is not a custom of Israel.

**[[Job 1#^v6|Job 1:6]]**
![[Job 1#^v6]]
- When it says "the sons of God," this is talking about the line, the lineage of Adam. Before we were called Israelites we were called the sons of God, and even still today we are called the sons of the living God.
- So this is talking about the same thing; it is not just talking about angels alone.
  - **[[Exodus 23#^v17|Exodus 23:17]]**
    ![[Exodus 23#^v17]]
    Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord, the same thing being said in Job.

**[[Job 1#^v7|Job 1:7-8]]**
![[Job 1#^v7]]
![[Job 1#^v8]]
- This is Satan talking to the Lord. The Lord says, have you considered My servant Job, have you ever tried Job, you walking up and down the earth, have you tried My servant, because he is an upright man.
- It is the same thing with us today. Every time we do more good works, the harder Satan comes at you. When you fast, the trials get worse, because you are showing God that you are upright through your fasting, striving to be that righteous vessel He wants you to be.
- So in order to see how strong you are, Satan is going to try you, and he is always going to go to the Lord for permission.

**[[Job 1#^v9|Job 1:9-12]]**
![[Job 1#^v9]]
![[Job 1#^v10]]
![[Job 1#^v11]]
![[Job 1#^v12]]
- Satan said, does Job fear God for nothing? Have you not made a hedge about him and blessed the work of his hands? Satan is telling the Most High that Job is only worshiping Him because He gives him a lot of stuff.
- That is the same thing with us today. When everything is going good you do not hear nothing from us; as soon as things go bad, that is when we start complaining and moaning and groaning.
- Satan said put forth thine hand and touch all that he hath, and he will curse thee to thy face. The Lord said, behold, all that he hath is in thy power, only upon himself put not forth thine hand.
- In Christianity they teach that Satan and God had a fight and Satan rebelled and took a third of the angels. That does not sound like what is right here. Job was the subject of discussion between Satan and the Lord, and Satan needed permission from the Lord to mess with him.
- A lot of times in Christianity people think everything bad comes from the devil and everything good comes from God. Wrong. Both good and evil come from the Lord. Satan is not a free spirit out here doing his own thing; he is subject to God, much like us.
  - **[[James 2#^v19|James 2:19]]**
    ![[James 2#^v19]]
    The devils believe there is one God and tremble; the difference is the devils tremble at the fear of God, and in this flesh our people have a tendency not to fear Him.

**[[Job 1#^v13|Job 1:13-19]]**
![[Job 1#^v13]]
![[Job 1#^v14]]
![[Job 1#^v15]]
![[Job 1#^v16]]
![[Job 1#^v17]]
![[Job 1#^v18]]
![[Job 1#^v19]]
- Job lost his oxen: the Sabeans fell upon them, took them away, and slew the servants with the edge of the sword. That is problem number one.
- While the first messenger was yet speaking, another came: the fire of God fell from heaven and burned up the sheep and the servants. That is problem number two.
- While he was yet speaking, another came: the Chaldeans made out three bands, fell upon the camels, carried them away, and slew the servants. That is problem number three.
- While he was yet speaking, another came: a great wind from the wilderness smote the four corners of the house and it fell upon the young men and they are dead. Job just lost his cattle, his servants, and his children. Job just lost everything.
- Why? Because the devil, with the permission of the Lord, was able to put his hand on Job to try him. That is what we need as Israelites; when we do certain things the Lord gives us certain things, and He has to try them to see if the heart is still going to be the same way.
  - **[[Proverbs 30#^v8|Proverbs 30:8-9]]**
    ![[Proverbs 30#^v8]]
    ![[Proverbs 30#^v9]]
    Give me neither poverty nor riches, lest I be full and deny thee, or lest I be poor and steal and take the name of my God in vain.

**[[Job 1#^v20|Job 1:20-22]]**
![[Job 1#^v20]]
![[Job 1#^v21]]
![[Job 1#^v22]]
- Job rent his mantle, shaved his head, fell down and worshiped, and said naked came I out of my mother's womb and naked shall I return; the Lord gave and the Lord hath taken away, blessed be the name of the Lord. In all this Job sinned not nor charged God foolishly.
- Job understood it: everything that we have comes from the Lord, everything that we get comes from the Lord.
- What good is it for us to blame God for stuff that we lose, when He can give it right back to you? There is nothing in this world that you can get yourself without the help of the Lord, unless you serve Satan and he gives it to you, but do you really want to pay that price?
- Even then it was the Lord that gave it to you, because the Lord handed you over to Satan; He put that stumbling block before you and you fell over it.
  - **[[Isaiah 45#^v7|Isaiah 45:7]]**
    ![[Isaiah 45#^v7]]
    I form the light and create darkness, I make peace and create evil, I the Lord do all these things.

# Job 2: Skin for Skin

Read the chapter → [[Job 2]]

**[[Job 2#^v1|Job 2:1-3]]**
![[Job 2#^v1]]
![[Job 2#^v2]]
![[Job 2#^v3]]
- Again there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan came also. The Lord said, hast thou considered My servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, and still he holdeth fast his integrity, although thou movedst Me against him to destroy him without cause.
- The Lord is bragging, He is boasting: even though you made Me go after Job for no reason at all, he is still standing strong, he is still handling his business.
- We have to allow God to brag on us. It is the same thing with all our enemies watching online; you have got to give the Lord a chance to brag.

**[[Job 2#^v4|Job 2:4-6]]**
![[Job 2#^v4]]
![[Job 2#^v5]]
![[Job 2#^v6]]
- Satan said, skin for skin, all that a man hath will he give for his life, but put forth thine hand now and touch his bone and his flesh, and he will curse thee to thy face.
- Possessions are possessions; a brother could lose a car, a job, family members, whatever. But Satan is saying if his health is afflicted, I bet you he will curse you to your face, because that is another thing that gets us.
- A lot of us, material things are not your stronghold. Even if you lost it today it would not do you nothing, but your health, that is your trial. As soon as you get sick you think it is the end of the world, or you think you did something.
- Maybe the Lord is doing this to you so you can be a shining light to somebody going through the same thing. A lot of our trials are not just for us. We read the book of Job because Job went through the things that we go through ourselves.
- The Lord said, behold, he is in thine hand, but save his life. The Lord said touch him but do not kill him. That does not sound like somebody rebellious against God; Satan took orders, just like we take orders.

**[[Job 2#^v7|Job 2:7-8]]**
![[Job 2#^v7]]
![[Job 2#^v8]]
- Satan went forth and smote Job with sore boils from the sole of his foot unto his crown, and Job took a potsherd to scrape himself withal, and he sat down among the ashes.

**[[Job 2#^v9|Job 2:9-10]]**
![[Job 2#^v9]]
![[Job 2#^v10]]
- Then his wife said, dost thou still retain thine integrity? Curse God, and die. This is the other test that brothers get.
- Satan is going to come through your woman. If he cannot get to you, he is going to come through her. Look at what he did with Adam: Satan was not going to get past Adam, so he backdoored and went to Eve. Same thing here; Satan was in his woman too.
- A lot of men, we get emotional behind our woman, and whatever she wants to say we go follow it instead of checking her.
- Job said, thou speakest as one of the foolish women speaketh; what, shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil? In all this did not Job sin with his lips.
- So he told her, woman, you sound foolish. Are we only going to get good from God and not evil? That is what we tell people all the time. We just read in [[Isaiah 45#^v7|Isaiah 45:7]] that God creates evil and creates peace. God is a man of war; He creates life and death. Nothing happens without the Lord; everything asks for permission.

**[[Job 2#^v11|Job 2:11-13]]**
![[Job 2#^v11]]
![[Job 2#^v12]]
![[Job 2#^v13]]
- Job's three friends heard of all this evil that came upon him: Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. They made an appointment together to come to mourn with him and to comfort him.
- When they lifted up their eyes afar off and knew him not, they lifted up their voice and wept, rent every man his mantle, and sprinkled dust upon their heads toward heaven. They sat down with him upon the ground seven days and seven nights, and none spake a word, for they saw that his grief was very great.
- Much like us, our friends hear of our affliction and come to lend their support, to give correction, to give harsh reality and hard truth. We are not talking about people that butter you up knowing you are in the wrong; we are talking about people that give you straight facts about what is going on with you.

# Job 3: Job Curses His Day

Read the chapter → [[Job 3]]

**[[Job 3#^v1|Job 3:1-4]]**
![[Job 3#^v1]]
![[Job 3#^v2]]
![[Job 3#^v3]]
![[Job 3#^v4]]
- After this Job opened his mouth and cursed his day. Why did he curse his day? Because his children were celebrating their day, and Job cursed the day he was born.
- Job said, let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night in which it was said, there is a man child conceived; let that day be darkness, let not God regard it from above, neither let the light shine upon it.
- Job is going in on his birthday. That is why we say to hell with our birthday, our birthday means nothing. The scriptures say better is the day of one's death than the day of one's birth, because when you are born there are no works yet; when you die that is when people remember your works and what kind of person you were.

**[[Job 3#^v5|Job 3:5-10]]**
![[Job 3#^v5]]
![[Job 3#^v6]]
![[Job 3#^v7]]
![[Job 3#^v8]]
![[Job 3#^v9]]
![[Job 3#^v10]]
- Let darkness and the shadow of death stain it, let a cloud dwell upon it, let the blackness of the day terrify it; as for that night, let darkness seize upon it, let it not be joined unto the days of the year.
- Let that night be solitary, let no joyful voice come therein, let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to raise up their mourning. Let the stars of the twilight thereof be dark; let it look for light, but have none.
- Because it shut not up the doors of my mother's womb, nor hid sorrow from mine eyes. Job is not cursing anything else but his birth right here; all the stuff he is going through, he is saying, man, I just should have never been born.

**[[Job 3#^v11|Job 3:11-19]]**
![[Job 3#^v11]]
![[Job 3#^v12]]
![[Job 3#^v13]]
![[Job 3#^v14]]
![[Job 3#^v15]]
![[Job 3#^v16]]
![[Job 3#^v17]]
![[Job 3#^v18]]
![[Job 3#^v19]]
- Why died I not from the womb? Why did I not give up the ghost when I came out of the belly? Job said I should have died at my birth, I should have died stillborn, I should have died in the womb.
- For now I should have lain still and been quiet, I should have slept, then had I been at rest, with kings and counsellors of the earth. Or as a hidden untimely birth I had not been, as infants which never saw light. He is talking about miscarriages: why wasn't I miscarried, why did I have to come into this world?
- There the wicked cease from troubling. When wicked people die, that is it, they can do no more wrong. When Esau is dead the earth is not going to be troubled anymore. There the prisoners rest together, they hear not the voice of the oppressor.
- This is why in New Orleans there is a celebration when people die; they call it a second line, and it goes all the way back to slave times. They are celebrating the fact that the prisoners rest together and hear not the voice of the oppressor; they are celebrating that they are not in slavery anymore.
- The small and great are there, and the servant is free from his master. Wherefore is light given to him that is in misery, and life unto the bitter in soul?

**[[Job 3#^v20|Job 3:20-26]]**
![[Job 3#^v20]]
![[Job 3#^v21]]
![[Job 3#^v22]]
![[Job 3#^v23]]
![[Job 3#^v24]]
![[Job 3#^v25]]
![[Job 3#^v26]]
- Which long for death, but it cometh not, and dig for it more than for hid treasures. It has got to be some serious trouble and anguish that you are in for you to only think about dying. Some of us have gone through situations where we would rather just be dead, but sometimes we go through things like that in order to be stronger.
- Which rejoice exceedingly, and are glad when they can find the grave. If you have had a grandmother or aunt or uncle that was very old, going to dialysis, getting blood transfusions and chemo, after a while they are ready to leave, ready to go. The scriptures say death is better than continual sickness.
- Why is light given to a man whose way is hid, and whom God hath hedged in? For my sighing cometh before I eat, and my roarings are poured out like the waters. For the thing which I greatly feared is come upon me, and that which I was afraid of is come unto me.
- I was not in safety, neither had I rest, neither was I quiet; yet trouble came. It was not like everything was going hunky dory for Job; he was already going through some stuff, and trouble came anyway. He is saying, man, what am I doing wrong?

# Job 4: Eliphaz Answers

Read the chapter → [[Job 4]]

**[[Job 4#^v1|Job 4:1-6]]**
![[Job 4#^v1]]
![[Job 4#^v2]]
![[Job 4#^v3]]
![[Job 4#^v4]]
![[Job 4#^v5]]
![[Job 4#^v6]]
- A lot of us think, well, I did not do nothing wrong, so why is this happening to me, why me? That is a common fleshly response to a lot of things we go through, but we have to take a step back and see who is in control of everything and for what purpose.
- Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: if we assay to commune with thee, wilt thou be grieved? But who can withhold himself from speaking? Eliphaz is like, you go to visit somebody that is sick and you do not want to say the wrong thing, but we just cannot sit here and say nothing.
- Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands; thy words have upholden him that was falling, and thou hast strengthened the feeble knees. But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.
- That right there cuts deep. A lot of times we can give other people the scriptures, First Corinthians 10:13, Ecclesiasticus chapter 2, [[Deuteronomy 28|Deuteronomy 28]], and be the strongest person out there. But then as soon as a trial comes to us, that is when we fall apart. That is what Eliphaz was asking: now trial is coming to you, Job, and now you are falling apart.
- Is not this thy fear, thy confidence, thy hope, and the uprightness of thy ways? Eliphaz is saying, you used to uplift people, and now trouble is coming upon you and you are about to lose your mind. Maybe you did something. Sometimes it is something we did, but a lot of times it is not; it is for us to go through something because we can handle it, and be a shining example to the next person.

**[[Job 4#^v7|Job 4:7-11]]**
![[Job 4#^v7]]
![[Job 4#^v8]]

![[Job 4#^v11]]
- Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? Or where were the righteous cut off? Even as I have seen, they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same.
- By the blast of God they perish, and by the breath of his nostrils are they consumed. The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken. The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the stout lion's whelps are scattered abroad.

![[Job 4#^v12]]
![[Job 4#^v13]]
![[Job 4#^v14]]
![[Job 4#^v15]]
![[Job 4#^v16]]
![[Job 4#^v17]]
![[Job 4#^v18]]
![[Job 4#^v19]]
![[Job 4#^v20]]
![[Job 4#^v21]]
- Now a thing was secretly brought to me, and mine ear received a little thereof. In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men. Now it is talking about the dreams that we have.
- Fear came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones to shake. Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up. It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof; an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice.
- Saying, shall mortal man be more just than God? Shall a man be more pure than his maker? Behold, he put no trust in his servants, and his angels he charged with folly.
- How much less in them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, which are crushed before the moth? They are destroyed from morning to evening; they perish for ever without any regarding it. Doth not their excellency which is in them go away? They die, even without wisdom.
- Eliphaz is basically telling Job, maybe it is something that you did, something you got going on, something you need to examine yourself with. And that is where it ends.
