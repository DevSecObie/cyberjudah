# 2 Esdras 12: The Interpretation of the Eagle

Read the chapter → [[2 Esdras 12]]

## Introduction

This session reads 2 Esdras 12 through 15. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The eagle of the last vision is interpreted as the kingdom Daniel saw, the man rising from the sea is interpreted as the son kept by the Most High, the ten tribes are named as the peaceable multitude gathered to him, and the reading closes with Esdras drinking the cup like fire, dictating the books, and the burden spoken against Babylon and Asia.
