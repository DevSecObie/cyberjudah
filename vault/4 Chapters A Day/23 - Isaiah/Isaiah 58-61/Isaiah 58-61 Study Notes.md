# Isaiah 58: The Fast That the Most High Has Chosen

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 58]]

This session reads the four chapters [[Isaiah 58|Isaiah 58]] through 61. The teacher opens by saying he is not here to teach this time, only to read the scriptures straight through, because these are fairly short chapters. The reading moves from the true fast the Most High has chosen in chapter 58, to the iniquities that separate Israel from their God in chapter 59, to the call to arise and shine in the coming kingdom in chapter 60, and closes with the anointing to preach good tidings in chapter 61. The little exposition given is kept here under the verses it belongs to.

**[[Isaiah 58#^v1|Isaiah 58:1-3]]**

![[Isaiah 58#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v3]]

- The Most High tells the prophet to cry aloud and spare not, to lift up his voice like a trumpet and show the people their transgression and the house of Jacob their sins.
- The people seek him daily and delight to know his ways as a nation that did righteousness, and they ask why they have fasted and afflicted their soul and he has taken no knowledge of it.

**[[Isaiah 58#^v4|Isaiah 58:4-9]]**

![[Isaiah 58#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v9]]

- Their fasting is for strife and debate and to smite with the fist of wickedness, and such a fast will not make their voice to be heard on high. The Most High is not looking for a man to bow his head like a bulrush and spread sackcloth and ashes.
- The fast he has chosen is to loose the bands of wickedness, undo the heavy burdens, let the oppressed go free, break every yoke, deal thy bread to the hungry, bring the poor that are cast out to thy house, and cover the naked.
- The reward is that thy light breaks forth as the morning, thy health springs forth speedily, thy righteousness goes before thee, and the glory of the Lord is thy reward; then thou shalt call and the Lord shall answer.
- "The putting forth of the finger" is when you blame other people. Somebody comes and says a brother did this or that, and instead of taking it, the brother says, "no, but what about her, look what she did." You have to take accountability for your own actions; this is about taking accountability.

**[[Isaiah 58#^v10|Isaiah 58:10-12]]**

![[Isaiah 58#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v12]]

- If thou draw out thy soul to the hungry and satisfy the afflicted soul, thy light shall rise in obscurity and thy darkness be as the noonday, and the Lord shall guide thee continually and make fat thy bones.
- Thou shalt be like a watered garden and a spring of water whose waters fail not, and they that come of thee shall build the old waste places and be called the repairer of the breach, the restorer of paths to dwell in.

**[[Isaiah 58#^v13|Isaiah 58:13-14]]**

![[Isaiah 58#^v13]]
![[Isaiah 58#^v14]]

- If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy own pleasure on his holy day, and call the sabbath a delight and honor him, then thou shalt delight thyself in the Lord.
- He will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father, for the mouth of the Lord has spoken it.

# Isaiah 59: Your Iniquities Have Separated You

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 59]]

**[[Isaiah 59#^v1|Isaiah 59:1-8]]**

![[Isaiah 59#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v8]]

- The Lord's hand is not shortened that it cannot save, neither his ear heavy that it cannot hear, but their iniquities have separated between them and their God, and their sins have hid his face from them.
- Their hands are defiled with blood, their lips speak lies, their tongue mutters perverseness, and none calls for justice nor pleads for truth; they trust in vanity and speak lies.
- They hatch cockatrice eggs and weave the spider's web, their feet run to evil and make haste to shed innocent blood, and the way of peace they know not.

**[[Isaiah 59#^v9|Isaiah 59:9-15]]**

![[Isaiah 59#^v9]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v12]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v13]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v14]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v15]]

- Because of this, judgment is far from them and justice does not overtake them; they wait for light but behold obscurity, and grope for the wall like the blind.
- Their transgressions are multiplied before the Most High and their sins testify against them, for in transgressing and lying against the Lord and departing away from their God, judgment is turned away backward and justice stands afar off.
- Truth fails, and he that departs from evil makes himself a prey; the Lord saw it and it displeased him that there was no judgment.

**[[Isaiah 59#^v16|Isaiah 59:16-21]]**

![[Isaiah 59#^v16]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v17]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v18]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v19]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v20]]
![[Isaiah 59#^v21]]

- The Most High wondered that there was no intercessor, so his own arm brought salvation, and he put on righteousness as a breastplate and a helmet of salvation, and the garment of vengeance for clothing.
- According to their deeds he will repay fury to his adversaries; so shall they fear the name of the Lord from the west, and when the enemy comes in like a flood the Spirit of the Lord shall lift up a standard against him.
- The Redeemer shall come to Zion, and to them that turn from transgression in Jacob; his covenant is that his Spirit and his words shall not depart out of their mouth, nor the mouth of their seed, from henceforth and forever.

# Isaiah 60: Arise and Shine

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 60]]

**[[Isaiah 60#^v1|Isaiah 60:1-5]]**

![[Isaiah 60#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v5]]

- Arise and shine, for thy light is come and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee. Though darkness shall cover the earth and gross darkness the people, the Lord shall arise upon thee and his glory be seen upon thee.
- The Gentiles shall come to the light and kings to the brightness of thy rising; thy sons shall come from far and thy daughters be nursed at thy side, and thy heart shall fear and be enlarged.

**[[Isaiah 60#^v6|Isaiah 60:6-9]]**

![[Isaiah 60#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v9]]

- The multitude of camels shall cover thee, and they from Sheba shall come bringing gold and incense and showing forth the praises of the Lord; the flocks of Kedar and the rams of Nebaioth shall minister and come up with acceptance on the altar.
- The isles shall wait for the Most High, and the ships of Tarshish shall bring thy sons from far, their silver and gold with them, unto the name of the Lord thy God and to the Holy One of Israel.

**[[Isaiah 60#^v10|Isaiah 60:10-14]]**

![[Isaiah 60#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v12]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v13]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v14]]

- The sons of strangers shall build up thy walls and their kings shall minister unto thee; thy gates shall be open continually to bring in the forces of the Gentiles, for the nation and kingdom that will not serve thee shall perish.
- The glory of Lebanon shall come to beautify the place of his sanctuary, and the sons of them that afflicted thee shall come bending, and they that despised thee shall bow at the soles of thy feet and call thee the city of the Lord, the Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**[[Isaiah 60#^v15|Isaiah 60:15-22]]**

![[Isaiah 60#^v15]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v16]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v17]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v18]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v19]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v20]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v21]]
![[Isaiah 60#^v22]]

- For brass he will bring gold, for iron silver, for wood brass and for stones iron; violence shall no more be heard in thy land, and thy walls shall be called Salvation and thy gates Praise.
- The sun and moon shall be no more thy light, for the Lord shall be thy everlasting light and thy God thy glory, and the days of thy mourning shall be ended.
- Thy people shall be all righteous and inherit the land forever; a little one shall become a thousand and a small one a strong nation, and the Lord will hasten it in his time.
- This chapter goes into future prophecy dealing with when we get the kingdom, how the Most High is going to bless us with all of these beautiful things. He is going to give us the heathen to build up our kingdom.

# Isaiah 61: The Spirit of the Lord God Is Upon Me

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 61]]

**[[Isaiah 61#^v1|Isaiah 61:1-3]]**

![[Isaiah 61#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v3]]

- The Spirit of the Lord God is upon the anointed one, sent to preach good tidings unto the meek, to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives and the opening of the prison to them that are bound.
- He is sent to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord and the day of vengeance of our God, to comfort all that mourn, and to give them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, and the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness, that they might be called trees of righteousness.
  - **[[Luke 4#^v18|Luke 4:18]]**
    ![[Luke 4#^v18]]
    Christ quoted this passage when he read in the synagogue, applying the anointing to preach the gospel to himself.

**[[Isaiah 61#^v4|Isaiah 61:4-7]]**

![[Isaiah 61#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v7]]

- They shall build the old wastes and raise up the former desolations, repairing the waste cities the desolations of many generations, and strangers shall stand and feed their flocks and be their plowmen and vinedressers.
- They shall be named the priests of the Lord and called the ministers of God, and they shall eat the riches of the Gentiles and boast in their glory; for their shame they shall have double, and everlasting joy shall be unto them.

**[[Isaiah 61#^v8|Isaiah 61:8-11]]**

![[Isaiah 61#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v9]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 61#^v11]]

- For the Lord loves judgment and hates robbery for burnt offerings; he will direct their work in truth and make an everlasting covenant with them, and their seed shall be known among the Gentiles as the seed which the Lord hath blessed.
- The prophet greatly rejoices in the Lord, for he has clothed him with the garments of salvation and covered him with the robe of righteousness; as the earth brings forth her bud, so the Lord will cause righteousness and praise to spring forth before all nations.
