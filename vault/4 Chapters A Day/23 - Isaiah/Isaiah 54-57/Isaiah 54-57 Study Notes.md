# Isaiah 54: The Restoration of Zion

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 54]]

This session is a four-chapters-a-day reading, picking up at [[Isaiah 54|Isaiah 54]] and running straight through [[Isaiah 57|Isaiah 57]]. The four chapters carry one thread: the Most High comforting and restoring the wife He had forsaken for a moment, calling His people back, and setting a plain line between the righteous and the wicked. This is a reading session, so the teaching is light; the class is encouraged to reread these chapters carefully, write down questions, and take them to the camp leader, because chapters like these confuse Christians and anyone not yet learned, but for Israel it is understood that all of it is the Most High speaking to Israel.

**[[Isaiah 54#^v1|Isaiah 54:1-5]]**
![[Isaiah 54#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v5]]
- The barren woman who did not bear is told to break forth into singing, for the children of the desolate are more than the children of the married wife. This is the promise of increase to a people who had been left desolate.
- She is told to enlarge the place of her tent, stretch forth the curtains, and strengthen her stakes, because she will break forth on the right hand and on the left, and her seed will inherit the Gentiles and make the desolate cities inhabited.
- She is told to fear not and not be ashamed, for she will forget the shame of her youth and the reproach of her widowhood.
- The names given here are titles of the Most High: the Lord of hosts, thy redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, the God of the whole earth. When the Scripture reads a different name it is still the same God under a different title.

**[[Isaiah 54#^v6|Isaiah 54:6-10]]**
![[Isaiah 54#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v9]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v10]]
- The Most High called her as a woman forsaken and grieved in spirit, a wife of youth who was refused. For a small moment He forsook her, but with great mercies He will gather her.
- In a little wrath He hid His face for a moment, but with everlasting kindness He will have mercy on her. He compares this to the waters of Noah: as He swore the waters would no more cover the earth, so He has sworn He would not be wroth with her.
- The mountains may depart and the hills be removed, but His kindness will not depart, and the covenant of His peace will not be removed.

**[[Isaiah 54#^v11|Isaiah 54:11-14]]**
![[Isaiah 54#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v12]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v13]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v14]]
- To the afflicted one, tossed with tempest and not comforted, He promises to lay her stones with fair colours and her foundations with sapphires, her windows of agates, her gates of carbuncles, and all her borders of pleasant stones.
- All her children will be taught of the Most High, and great will be the peace of her children.
- In righteousness she will be established; she will be far from oppression and will not fear, and terror will not come near her.

**[[Isaiah 54#^v15|Isaiah 54:15-17]]**
![[Isaiah 54#^v15]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v16]]
![[Isaiah 54#^v17]]
- Any who gather together against her do not do so by Him, and whoever gathers against her will fall for her sake.
- He created the smith that blows the coals and brings forth an instrument for his work, and He created the waster to destroy. Both maker and destroyer are His.
- No weapon formed against her will prosper, and every tongue that rises against her in judgment she will condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the Most High, and their righteousness is of Him.

# Isaiah 55: A Free Invitation

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 55]]

**[[Isaiah 55#^v1|Isaiah 55:1-5]]**
![[Isaiah 55#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v5]]
- Everyone that thirsts is called to the waters, and he that has no money is told to come, buy, and eat wine and milk without money and without price. The invitation is free.
- He asks why they spend money for that which is not bread and their labour for that which does not satisfy. They are told to hearken and eat that which is good, and let the soul delight itself in fatness.
- If they incline their ear and come, their soul will live, and He will make an everlasting covenant with them, even the sure mercies of David.
- David is given for a witness to the people, a leader and a commander. A nation not known will be called, and nations that did not know her will run to her because of the Most High, who has glorified her.

**[[Isaiah 55#^v6|Isaiah 55:6-9]]**
![[Isaiah 55#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v9]]
- Seek the Most High while He may be found, call upon Him while He is near. The wicked is to forsake his way and the unrighteous man his thoughts, and return to the Most High, who will have mercy and abundantly pardon.
- His thoughts are not our thoughts, and His ways are not our ways. As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are His ways and His thoughts higher than ours.

**[[Isaiah 55#^v10|Isaiah 55:10-13]]**
![[Isaiah 55#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v12]]
![[Isaiah 55#^v13]]
- As the rain and snow come down from heaven and do not return without watering the earth, causing it to bring forth seed and bread, so His word does not return to Him void but accomplishes what He pleases.
- His people will go out with joy and be led forth with peace; the mountains and hills will break forth into singing and the trees of the field will clap their hands.
- Instead of the thorn will come up the fir tree, and instead of the brier the myrtle tree, and this will be to the Most High for a name and an everlasting sign that will not be cut off.

# Isaiah 56: A House of Prayer for All Israel

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 56]]

**[[Isaiah 56#^v1|Isaiah 56:1-2]]**
![[Isaiah 56#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v2]]
- Keep judgment and do justice, for His salvation is near to come and His righteousness to be revealed.
- Blessed is the man that does this and lays hold on it, that keeps the Sabbath from polluting it and keeps his hand from doing any evil.

**[[Isaiah 56#^v3|Isaiah 56:3-8]]**
![[Isaiah 56#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v4]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v8]]
- The son of the stranger that has joined himself to the Most High is not to say the Lord has utterly separated him from His people, and the eunuch is not to say he is a dry tree. These strangers spoken of are Israel; the whole passage is the Most High speaking to Israel.
- To the eunuchs that keep His Sabbaths and take hold of His covenant, He gives a place and a name in His house better than of sons and daughters, an everlasting name that will not be cut off.
- The sons of the stranger that join themselves to Him, keep the Sabbath, and take hold of His covenant, He will bring to His holy mountain and make joyful in His house of prayer; their offerings will be accepted, for His house will be called a house of prayer for all people.
- The Lord God who gathers the outcasts of Israel says He will yet gather others to Him besides those already gathered.

**[[Isaiah 56#^v9|Isaiah 56:9-12]]**
![[Isaiah 56#^v9]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 56#^v12]]
- All the beasts of the field and forest are called to devour. Judgment is turned on the failed leadership.
- The watchmen are blind and ignorant, dumb dogs that cannot bark, sleeping and loving to slumber. They are greedy dogs that never have enough, shepherds that cannot understand.
- They all look to their own way, every one for his gain, saying they will fetch wine and fill themselves with strong drink, and tomorrow will be as this day and much more abundant.

# Isaiah 57: No Peace to the Wicked

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 57]]

**[[Isaiah 57#^v1|Isaiah 57:1-4]]**
![[Isaiah 57#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v4]]
- The righteous perishes and no man lays it to heart; merciful men are taken away, none considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come. He enters into peace and rests in his bed, walking in his uprightness.
- The sons of the sorceress, the seed of the adulterer, are called to draw near. They sport themselves and draw out the tongue.
- They are named as children of transgression, a seed of falsehood.

**[[Isaiah 57#^v5|Isaiah 57:5-10]]**
![[Isaiah 57#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v9]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v10]]
- They inflame themselves with idols under every green tree, slaying the children in the valleys under the clefts of the rocks. Among the smooth stones of the stream is their portion, and to them they poured a drink offering and offered a meat offering.
- Upon a lofty and high mountain they set their bed and went up to offer sacrifice; behind the doors and the posts they set up their remembrance, having discovered themselves to another than the Most High.
- They enlarged their bed and made a covenant with the idols, loving their bed. They went to the king with ointment and increased their perfumes, and sent their messengers far off, debasing themselves even unto hell.
- They wearied themselves in the greatness of their way, yet did not say there is no hope; they found the life of their hand, therefore they were not grieved.

**[[Isaiah 57#^v11|Isaiah 57:11-14]]**
![[Isaiah 57#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v12]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v13]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v14]]
- He asks of whom they were afraid, that they lied and did not remember Him nor lay it to their heart. He held His peace of old, and so they did not fear Him.
- He will declare their righteousness and their works, which will not profit them; when they cry, their companies will not deliver them, but the wind will carry them away.
- But he that puts his trust in Him will possess the land and inherit His holy mountain. The way is to be prepared and the stumbling block taken out of the way of His people.

**[[Isaiah 57#^v15|Isaiah 57:15-21]]**
![[Isaiah 57#^v15]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v16]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v17]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v18]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v19]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v20]]
![[Isaiah 57#^v21]]
- The high and lofty One that inhabits eternity, whose name is Holy, dwells in the high and holy place, and also with him that is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble and the heart of the contrite ones.
- He will not contend forever nor be always wroth, for the spirit would fail before Him. For the iniquity of covetousness He was wroth and smote him, and hid Himself, and the man went on frowardly in the way of his heart.
- He has seen his ways and will heal him, leading him and restoring comforts to him and his mourners, creating peace for him that is far off and him that is near.
- But the wicked are like the troubled sea that cannot rest, whose waters cast up mire and dirt. There is no peace to the wicked, says the Most High.
- This is a good four chapters and a good read; the class is encouraged to read them again carefully, take notes, and bring questions to the camp leader, because the whole of it is the Most High speaking to Israel.
