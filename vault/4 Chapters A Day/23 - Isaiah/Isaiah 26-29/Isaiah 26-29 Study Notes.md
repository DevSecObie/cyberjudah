# Isaiah 26: A Song of Salvation and the Straight Gate

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 26]]

This session moves through [[Isaiah 26|Isaiah 26]] to 29, opening with the class disclaimer that Israel United in Christ is a non-violent, Bible-based movement that condemns violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender, citing [[Leviticus 5#^v1|Leviticus 5:1]] on the duty to report any plot of harm to the proper authorities. Chapter 26 is a future song of salvation sung in Judah, turning on the straight gate through which only the righteous nation that keeps the truth may enter, and on the judgments the Most High will bring down upon the lofty nations. Chapter 27 punishes Leviathan and gathers the scattered children of Israel. Chapter 28 pronounces woe on the proud drunkards of Ephraim who are drunk with philosophies rather than the law, and lays out the method of study: precept upon precept, line upon line. Chapter 29 brings judgment on Ariel and exposes the spiritual blindness that keeps Israel asleep until the deaf hear the words of the book.

- This is a future prophecy: in that day this song shall be sung in the land of Judah, a joyous song of salvation, with a strong city whose walls and bulwarks are salvation appointed by the Most High.
- The gates are opened that the righteous nation which keepeth the truth may enter in, and that nation is the remnant that keeps the commandments. Whose mind is stayed on the Most High is kept in perfect peace because he trusteth in Him.

# Isaiah 28: Woe to the Drunkards of Ephraim and Precept Upon Precept

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 28]]

**[[Isaiah 28#^v1|Isaiah 28:1-4]]**

![[Isaiah 28#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 28#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 28#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 28#^v4]]

- Woe to the crown of pride, the drunkards of Ephraim, whose glorious beauty is a fading flower; pride sits on them like a crown.
- The Most High has a mighty and strong one, like a tempest and a flood of mighty waters, that shall cast down to the earth with the hand; this is the northern kingdom being brought down in its rulership because of pride, refusing the things of the Bible. The crown of pride is trodden under feet.

- In that day the Lord of hosts shall be a crown of glory and a diadem of beauty to the residue of His people, the remnant that escapes the judgment, and a spirit of judgment to those who sit in judgment.
- The priests and prophets err through wine and strong drink and are out of the way; they are not drunk on the law of God but on the philosophies of men, and today this is the pastors and the politicians who err with lies and philosophy and love it. Their tables are full of vomit, so all the religions today are full of vomit and there is no place clean.

    ![[Micah 2#^v11]]

This Bible is the rest and the refreshing, yet they would not hear, so the word came precept upon precept that they might fall backward and be broken and snared.

# Isaiah 29: Woe to Ariel and the Sealed Book

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 29]]

**[[Isaiah 29#^v1|Isaiah 29:1-4]]**

![[Isaiah 29#^v1]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v4]]

- Woe to Ariel, the city where David dwelt; Ariel means lion of God, and this is spoken to Israel, to Jerusalem. Year to year they add and kill sacrifices, yet He will distress Ariel with heaviness and sorrow.
- He will encamp against her round about and lay siege, bringing the nations around because of Israel's sins, and she shall be brought down to speak low out of the ground, her voice like one that hath a familiar spirit whispering out of the dust.

**[[Isaiah 29#^v5|Isaiah 29:5-8]]**

![[Isaiah 29#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v6]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v8]]

- The multitude of her strangers shall be like small dust and the terrible ones as chaff that passes away suddenly; she shall be visited of the Lord of hosts with thunder, earthquake, great noise, storm, tempest, and devouring fire, for Israel is contending against the Most High as well as the nations He brings.
- The multitude of nations that fight against Ariel shall be as a dream of a night vision: like a hungry man who dreams he eats but awakes empty, or a thirsty man who dreams he drinks but awakes faint. The nations dream of destroying Israel, but it shall not come to pass, though it will still be like a nightmare to His people.
  - **[[Psalms 126#^v1|Psalms 126:1]]**
    ![[Psalms 126#^v1]]
    When the Most High turns again the captivity of Zion, they were like them that dream; the nations' desire to destroy Israel will not be fulfilled.

**[[Isaiah 29#^v9|Isaiah 29:9-12]]**

![[Isaiah 29#^v9]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v10]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v11]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v12]]

- They are drunken but not with wine; their spiritual eyes are closed in a deep sleep of blindness, unable to see the things of the Bible. Before coming to the truth many thought Esau was actually white, until the scriptures showed by his description that he is red, showing how deep the spiritual sleep runs.
- The vision is become as the words of a book that is sealed: delivered to the learned who says he cannot read it because it is sealed, and to the unlearned who says he is not learned. Today's pastors say Revelation is still sealed, but it is sealed only to them, not to the righteous; many learn only how to mimic and perform from the television.

**[[Isaiah 29#^v13|Isaiah 29:13-16]]**

![[Isaiah 29#^v13]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v14]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v15]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v16]]

- This people draw near with their mouth and honour Him with their lips but have removed their heart far from Him; they praise God but when [[Deuteronomy 22#^v5|Deuteronomy 22:5]] comes out they will not do it, for their fear toward Him is taught by the precepts of men, by the oppressors.
- Therefore He will do a marvellous work, and the wisdom of their wise men, the oppressors and mainly Esau, shall perish. Woe to them that seek deep to hide their counsel from the Most High and work in the dark saying, Who seeth us? Their turning of things upside down shall be esteemed as the potter's clay, for the Most High is setting His people back in order, turning right side up what Esau turned upside down.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 22#^v5|Deuteronomy 22:5]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 22#^v5]]
    The command against a man or woman wearing what pertains to the other, which the people refuse to keep while still praising God.

**[[Isaiah 29#^v17|Isaiah 29:17-19]]**

![[Isaiah 29#^v17]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v18]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v19]]

- Yet a little while and Lebanon shall be turned into a fruitful field; in that day the deaf shall hear the words of the book, and the deaf are Israel, in a spiritual deep sleep now beginning to wake and hear the word of God rightly.
- The eyes of the blind shall see out of their obscurity, and the blind are Israel, like the obscurity of thinking Esau is white when he is red; the meek shall increase their joy in the Most High.
  - **[[Psalms 149#^v2|Psalms 149:2,4]]**
    ![[Psalms 149#^v2]]
    ![[Psalms 149#^v4]]
    Let Israel rejoice in Him that made him, for the Most High will beautify the meek with salvation; the meek is Israel.

**[[Isaiah 29#^v20|Isaiah 29:20-24]]**

![[Isaiah 29#^v20]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v21]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v22]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v23]]
![[Isaiah 29#^v24]]

- The poor among men shall rejoice in the Holy One of Israel, the poor in spirit, which is us; the terrible one and the scorner, Esau, is brought to naught and consumed, and all that watch for iniquity are cut off, including Israelites who love the oppressor with all their heart.
- Concerning the house of Jacob, the Most High who redeemed Abraham says Jacob shall not be ashamed nor his face wax pale; when he sees his children they shall sanctify His name and fear the God of Israel, and they that erred in spirit shall come to understanding and they that murmured shall learn doctrine.
