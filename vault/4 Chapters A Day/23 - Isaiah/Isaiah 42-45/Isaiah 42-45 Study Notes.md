# Isaiah 42: My Servant and the Light of the Gentiles

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 42]]

This session reads the four chapters [[Isaiah 42|Isaiah 42]] through 45. The thread that ties them together is the servant: the chapter opens on the Most High's elect servant in whom his soul delights, moving into Christ, and then declares plainly that the servant, the witness, and the chosen is Israel. Along the way the teaching drives home who the "gentiles" are, that Israel was robbed and spoiled and hidden in prison houses, that the idols of the nations are nothing but wood and stone, that our name was changed in slavery as prophesied, and that the Most High alone forms light and creates darkness, makes peace and creates evil.

- A bruised reed he shall not break and the smoking flax he shall not quench; he shall bring forth judgment unto truth. He shall not fail nor be discouraged till he has set judgment in the earth, and the isles shall wait for his law.

Fear not, for I will pour water upon him that is thirsty and floods upon the dry ground, and pour my spirit upon thy seed and my blessing upon thine offspring.
- They shall spring up as among the grass, as willows by the watercourses. One shall say I am the Lord's, another shall call himself by the name of Jacob, and another shall subscribe with his hand unto the Lord and surname himself by the name of Israel.

# Isaiah 45: I Form the Light and Create Darkness

- Thus saith the Lord to his anointed, to Cyrus, whose right hand I have holden, to subdue nations before him and loose the loins of kings, to open the two-leaved gates that shall not be shut.
- I will go before thee and make the crooked places straight, break in pieces the gates of brass, and cut asunder the bars of iron. I will give thee the treasures of darkness and the hidden riches of secret places, that thou mayest know that I the Lord which called thee by thy name am the God of Israel.

- I form the light and create darkness, I make peace and create evil; I the Lord do all these things. The Most High creates good and evil, makes alive and kills; this is one of the basic precepts to hold onto.

- We cannot kick against the pricks or grieve the holy spirit; let the Lord do what he needs to do while using us, so we can go home and get out of here.

This is prophecy that the Africans, the men of stature, come over in chains, because they sold us into slavery to the so-called white man, the Grecians.

- The word "world" means a group of people with common goals or interests, like the sports world or basketball world, so "world without end" and God so loved the world are not speaking of everybody but of Israel.
