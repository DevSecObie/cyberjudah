# Isaiah 1: A Sinful Nation, Yet Zion Redeemed With Judgment

Read the chapter → [[Isaiah 1]]

This session turns to [[Isaiah 1|Isaiah 1]]. Isaiah prophesied in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and served as the Most High's prophet during Hezekiah's reign. His book foretells the Assyrian captivity, one of the cruelest captivities Israel ever suffered, along with Babylon, America, the destruction of Edom, and the coming of Christ. Chapter one lays the charge against Israel: a sinful nation that does not know its owner, afflicted again and again yet still rebelling, and yet promised redemption if it will return to the laws of the Most High.

**[[Isaiah 1#^v1|Isaiah 1:1]]**

![[Isaiah 1#^v1]]

- The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz concerns Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah. These are the days in which Isaiah prophesied.
- Isaiah was a servant and prophet during Hezekiah's reign, before the Assyrian captivity destroyed the nation. The split between the northern and southern kingdoms had already come, going back to Solomon's idolatry and the friction between Rehoboam and Jeroboam.
- Isaiah is a heavy, prophetic book; even the Ethiopian eunuch was reading Isaiah when the spirit sent Philip to help him understand it.

**[[Isaiah 1#^v2|Isaiah 1:2-4]]**

![[Isaiah 1#^v2]]
![[Isaiah 1#^v3]]
![[Isaiah 1#^v4]]

- "Hear, O heavens, and give ear, O earth: for the LORD hath spoken." The Most High nourished and brought up children, and they rebelled against Him; this is the nation of Israel.
- The ox knows its owner and the ass its master's crib, but Israel does not know and does not consider. These dull animals still know where they live and who owns them, so that Israel does not is a hard but constructive rebuke.
- This verse is used at camp against a people who will not acknowledge that they are the Israelites. It is not a religion; it is who they were born, and denying it is denying the gift, the spiritual lottery ticket that only needs the laws kept to cash it in.
- Our people would rather take pride in the cursed names given them, so-called African-American, Haitian, Jamaican, West Indian, black, brown, Mexican, Hispanic, Latino, and Native American, than in their own God-given nationality.
- "Ah sinful nation, a people laden with iniquity, a seed of evildoers, children that are corrupters": the nation is covered in sin because it loves what America and the traditions of men have taught, such as keeping Valentine's Day, which traces back to the pagan Lupercalia. They have forsaken the law, the good doctrine of [[Proverbs 4#^v2|Proverbs 4:2]], and provoked the Holy One of Israel to anger, gone away backward.

**[[Isaiah 1#^v5|Isaiah 1:5-6]]**

![[Isaiah 1#^v5]]
![[Isaiah 1#^v6]]

- The Most High asks, "Why should ye be stricken any more? ye will revolt more and more." Like bad children who only get worse the more they are punished, Israel keeps rebelling under affliction, whether earthquakes in Haiti, hurricanes in Puerto Rico and Florida, crime, or prisons filled with His chosen people, and revolts further into unnatural states.
- The whole head is sick and the whole heart faint. The head being sick is the leadership; thanks are given that the southern kingdom's bishops, elders, deacons, and captains are waking up, or the nation would be sicker still.
- "From the sole of the foot even unto the head there is no soundness in it, but wounds, and bruises, and putrefying sores." From the least tribe to Judah and the southern kingdom the nation is wounded, and the sores are not closed, bound up, nor mollified with ointment. The ointment that heals, whether physical or spiritual, is the word of the Most High.
  - **[[Luke 16#^v20|Luke 16:20-21]]**
    ![[Luke 16#^v20]]
    ![[Luke 16#^v21]]
    The sores that go unbound recall the beggar Lazarus, full of sores, in the account of Lazarus and the rich man.

**[[Isaiah 1#^v7|Isaiah 1:7-9]]**

![[Isaiah 1#^v7]]
![[Isaiah 1#^v8]]
![[Isaiah 1#^v9]]

- "Your country is desolate, your cities are burned with fire: your land, strangers devour it in your presence." No number of malls or stores changes that America is destined for destruction. This happened in 1492, when the so-called Mexicans, the Aztecs, had their cities burned and were robbed, raped, and murdered by the caucasian Spaniards, who then taught them Spanish.
- The so-called Native Americans and the Aztecs were once one people who dwelt in harmony, because they are the twelve tribes of Israel; now strangers devour their own land and reservations in their presence, leaving it desolate and barely inhabitable, the worst land to raise children on.
- The same happened at Black Wall Street in Tulsa, Oklahoma, and in Philadelphia, where thriving businesses of the so-called African-Americans and Jamaicans were destroyed and bombed. The neighborhoods are now overthrown by strangers: Arabs owning the gas stations, the Chinese the beauty supplies and restaurants, the East Indians the hotels, and the caucasians the banks and the big stores, while Israel owns nothing but the drug trade, which is against the law.
- "And the daughter of Zion is left as a cottage in a vineyard, as a lodge in a garden of cucumbers, as a besieged city." The nations only use and abuse Israel.
- Except the Most High of hosts had left a very small remnant, Israel should have been as Sodom and like unto Gomorrah. That small remnant is the prophets, Isaiah, Jeremiah, and Moses, and the bible itself, the lifeline; without it delivering the message the nation would have been destroyed.

- "Hear the word of the LORD, ye rulers of Sodom; give ear unto the law of our God, ye people of Gomorrah." The Most High asks what purpose the multitude of their sacrifices serve, for He is full of burnt offerings and delights not in the blood of bullocks, lambs, or he goats.
- Back then rams, goats, beasts, and bullocks were wealth and the means of trade; today the christian church substitutes paper money and tithing. The Most High says the same to them: stop bringing money to the church and keep the commandments and obey His voice, rather than sinning all week and going to church on Sunday, the wrong day, thinking to buy into the kingdom.
- Christians call the bible "basic instructions before leaving earth," yet cannot rightly break down [[John 3#^v16|John 3:16]], which is not speaking of the whole world; this shows they do not understand the book.
- The new moons, sabbaths, and appointed feasts kept in iniquity His soul hates; when they spread forth their hands He hides His eyes, and when they make many prayers He will not hear, for their hands are full of blood. He calls them to wash and make themselves clean and put away the evil of their doings.

- "Cease to do evil; learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow." Washing and making clean is not a shower or a dip in water; it is keeping the commandments, which is how a man ceases to do evil, since evil is sin.
- "Come now, and let us reason together, saith the LORD": though their sins be as scarlet they shall be as white as snow. This shows the Most High's mercy and love, for He has not destroyed them though He could, and still calls them, though in truth it is keep the laws or perish.
- "If ye be willing and obedient, ye shall eat the good of the land." Eating the good of the land is not a fine restaurant; it is rulership, owning the land and the acreage free of taxes, while the other nations are put in subjection to Israel.
- "But if ye refuse and rebel, ye shall be devoured with the sword." That is Israel's condition today, devoured with the sword. The sword is Esau, the wicked, whom the Most High uses to devour His people.

Jakes and Creflo Dollar, contrasted with Nat Turner, who came to understand the bible even if he went about it the wrong way by taking up arms.

The so-called black, Hispanic, and Native American man joins hands with the oppressor or with gang activity; everyone loves gifts and follows after rewards, chasing degrees and approval instead of judging the fatherless or pleading for the widow.

He will fix what is broken; some already keep the laws in the fear of the Most High rather than waiting for affliction to seek Him.
