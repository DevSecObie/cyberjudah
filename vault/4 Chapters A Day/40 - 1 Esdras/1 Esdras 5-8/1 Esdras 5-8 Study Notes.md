# 1 Esdras 5: The Return From the Captivity and the Foundation Laid

Read the chapter → [[1 Esdras 5]]

## Introduction

This session reads 1 Esdras 5 through 8. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The reading covers the register of those who came up out of the captivity with Zorobabel, the altar set up and the foundation of the house laid, the hindering of the work and the letter to Darius that answered it, the finishing and dedication of the house, and then Esdras coming up from Babylon with the commission of Artaxerxes and his prayer over the strange marriages.

- They came with Zorobabel, and Jesus, Nehemias, and Zacharias, and Reesaias, Enenius, Mardocheus, and the rest of their guides; and the number of them of the nation and their governors is set down family by family.
- Of Israel, from them of twelve years old and upward, they were all in number forty thousand, beside menservants and womenservants two thousand three hundred and sixty.
- Certain of the chief of their families, when they came to the temple of God that is in Jerusalem, vowed to set up the house again in his own place according to their ability, and to give into the holy treasure of the works a thousand pounds of gold, five thousand of silver, and an hundred priestly vestments.
- Some who claimed the priesthood and could not be found written in the register were removed from executing the office, until there arose up an high priest clothed with doctrine and truth.

- They held the feast of tabernacles, as it is commanded in the law, and offered the continual oblations, and the sacrifice of the sabbaths, and of the new moons, and of all the holy feasts, although the temple of the Lord was not yet built.

- Then stood up Zorobabel the son of Salathiel, and Jesus the son of Josedec, and began to build the house of the Lord at Jerusalem, the prophets of the Lord being with them, and helping them.

- The letter to Darius reported that the ancients of the Jews were building an house unto the Lord, great and new, of hewn and costly stones, and the timber already laid upon the walls, and that the work went on prosperously in their hands.
- The elders answered, We are the servants of the Lord that made heaven and earth; and as for this house, it was builded many years ago by a king of Israel great and strong, and was finished.
- When our fathers provoked God unto wrath, and sinned against the Lord of Israel which is in heaven, he gave them over into the power of Nabuchodonosor king of Babylon, who pulled down the house, and burned it, and carried away the people captives unto Babylon.
- In the first year that Cyrus reigned over the country of Babylon, king Cyrus wrote to build up this house, and delivered the holy vessels again, with commandment that they should be set in the temple at Jerusalem.
- Now therefore, if it seem good unto the king, let search be made among the records of king Cyrus.

- Darius commanded that Sisinnes and his companions should not meddle with the place, but suffer Zorobabel the servant of the Lord, and governor of Judea, and the elders of the Jews, to build the house of the Lord in that place.
- Out of the tribute of Celosyria and Phenice a portion was to be carefully given for the sacrifices of the Lord continually every year, that offerings may be made unto the most high God for the king and for his children.
- The Lord, whose name is there called upon, utterly destroy every king and nation that stretcheth out his hand to hinder or endamage that house of the Lord in Jerusalem.
