This session is part of the Man Up Mondays study of the Acts of the Apostles, moving through [[Acts 6|Acts 6]] verse by verse. The book of Acts does not close with an amen, because the acts of the apostles continue in our day; what the fathers did on their journey pushing the gospel is the same work being done now in the diaspora. Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, so the history in this chapter teaches the present and the future. From there the chapter sets the order for choosing leaders and shows the false witnesses raised up against Stephen.
