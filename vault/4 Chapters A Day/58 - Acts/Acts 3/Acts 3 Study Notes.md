**[[Acts 3#^v1|Acts 3:1]]**
![[Acts 3#^v1]]
- Peter and John go up to the temple at the hour of prayer, being the ninth hour. Counting the way the scripture counts, the day starts at six in the morning: six is the start, so the third hour is nine in the morning, the sixth hour is noon, and the ninth hour is three in the afternoon. Write that down, because knowing how the hours are counted keeps you from being confused later.
