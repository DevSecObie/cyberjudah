# Acts 4: The Stone the Builders Rejected

Read the chapter → [[Acts 4]]

This session continues the Man Up Mondays series through the Acts of the Apostles, teaching Acts chapter 4 verse by verse. We stress that you cannot dive into the letters of Paul without first understanding the whole war and history of the Acts of the Apostles that led up to those letters, because most Christians run to Paul outside of Christ and the other disciples for a sense of a license to sin. Chapter 4 opens right after the healing at the temple: the priests, the captain of the temple, and the Sadducees seize Peter and John, and the same spirit that was on the religious rulers back then is being activated on the Christian pastors today.

The Sadducees, Pharisees, elders, and chief priests were the religious leaders of that day, and they are the same religious leaders today: your Creflo Dollars, your TD Jakes, your Joel Osteens, your Gino Jennings, speaking the same things they spoke back then.
- The Sadducees were the upper class, the wealthy priestly and merchant families, moved by political and economic position. They came under the influence of Hellenism the same way today's pastors come under the influence of Americanism, preaching a prosperity and a political gospel and adopting their own man-made traditions.
- They were grieved because the teaching pulled the people, the money, and the control away from them. It is jealousy, envy, and the inability to guide the people, the same reason these pastors avoid a real sit-down with someone who actually knows the scriptures.
- The Sadducees did not even believe in the resurrection, yet they used scriptures they did not believe to try to confound Christ, the same way Christians and five-percenters take our scriptures they reject and use them to try to confound us.
