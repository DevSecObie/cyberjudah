# Matthew 10: The Twelve Sent to the Lost Sheep

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 10]]

## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 10 through 13. The twelve are named and sent out, and they are sent to one people only. Christ then answers John the Baptist out of prison, contends with the Pharisees over the sabbath and over the source of his power, and teaches the multitude in parables, explaining to the disciples what the parables mean. The thread is who the message belongs to and who is able to receive it.

**[[Matthew 10#^v1|Matthew 10:1-4]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v1]]
![[Matthew 10#^v2]]
![[Matthew 10#^v3]]
![[Matthew 10#^v4]]

- Christ calls the twelve and gives them power against unclean spirits and to heal all manner of sickness and disease, the same works he was doing himself.
- The twelve are named here, ending with Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.

**[[Matthew 10#^v5|Matthew 10:5-6]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v5]]
![[Matthew 10#^v6]]

- Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not, but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
- The twelve go to the southern kingdom first, before the gospel goes to the northern kingdom.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Matthew 4#^v15|Matthew 4:15-16]]**
    ![[Matthew 4#^v15]]
    ![[Matthew 4#^v16]]
    Zebulun and Nephthalim are called Galilee of the Gentiles, showing that tribes of Israel in a gentile state are spoken of that way.

**[[Matthew 10#^v7|Matthew 10:7-8]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v7]]
![[Matthew 10#^v8]]

- Preach that the kingdom of heaven is at hand, heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils. Freely ye have received, freely give.

**[[Matthew 10#^v9|Matthew 10:9-10]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v9]]
![[Matthew 10#^v10]]

- They are to carry no gold, silver or brass, no scrip, no second coat, no shoes, no staves, for the workman is worthy of his meat.

**[[Matthew 10#^v11|Matthew 10:11-13]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v11]]
![[Matthew 10#^v12]]
![[Matthew 10#^v13]]

- In every city they are to enquire who in it is worthy, and to let their peace rest on a worthy house and return to them from one that is not.

**[[Matthew 10#^v14|Matthew 10:14-15]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v14]]
![[Matthew 10#^v15]]

- Where they are not received they shake off the dust of their feet, and it shall be more tolerable for Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of judgment than for that city.

**[[Matthew 10#^v16|Matthew 10:16]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v16]]

- Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves: be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless as doves.
- This is a word for a people living in captivity. There is only so much that can be said and done on another man's ground, and wisdom is required alongside harmlessness.

**[[Matthew 10#^v17|Matthew 10:17-18]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v17]]
![[Matthew 10#^v18]]

- They will be delivered up to the councils and scourged in the synagogues, which is what the Pharisees had the power to do.
- They are brought before governors and kings for a testimony against them and the Gentiles: against their own people who turned on Christ, and against the rulers of that time.

**[[Matthew 10#^v19|Matthew 10:19-20]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v19]]
![[Matthew 10#^v20]]

- Take no thought how or what ye shall speak, for it shall be given you in that same hour. It is not you that speak, but the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Luke 21#^v15|Luke 21:15]]**
    ![[Luke 21#^v15]]
    I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to gainsay nor resist.

**[[Matthew 10#^v21|Matthew 10:21-22]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v21]]
![[Matthew 10#^v22]]

- The brother shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the child. Serving the Most High brings division, and it comes first inside your own house.
- Ye shall be hated of all men for my name's sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.

**[[Matthew 10#^v23|Matthew 10:23]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v23]]

- When they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another.

**[[Matthew 10#^v24|Matthew 10:24-25]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v24]]
![[Matthew 10#^v25]]

- The disciple is not above his master. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, they will call them of his household worse.

**[[Matthew 10#^v26|Matthew 10:26-27]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v26]]
![[Matthew 10#^v27]]

- There is nothing covered that shall not be revealed. What is told in darkness is to be spoken in light, and what is heard in the ear preached upon the housetops.

**[[Matthew 10#^v28|Matthew 10:28]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v28]]

- Fear not them which kill the body but are not able to kill the soul, but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

**[[Matthew 10#^v29|Matthew 10:29-31]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v29]]
![[Matthew 10#^v30]]
![[Matthew 10#^v31]]

- Not one sparrow falls to the ground without your Father, and the very hairs of your head are all numbered. That is not written for decoration; it is how closely he holds his own.

**[[Matthew 10#^v32|Matthew 10:32-33]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v32]]
![[Matthew 10#^v33]]

- Whosoever shall confess me before men, him will I confess also before my Father; and whosoever shall deny me before men, him will I also deny.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 94#^v16|Psalms 94:16]]**
    ![[Psalms 94#^v16]]
    Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? That standing up is what confessing him before men looks like.

**[[Matthew 10#^v34|Matthew 10:34-36]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v34]]
![[Matthew 10#^v35]]
![[Matthew 10#^v36]]

- I came not to send peace, but a sword. A man's foes shall be they of his own household.

**[[Matthew 10#^v37|Matthew 10:37]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v37]]

- He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me. This is not forbidding natural love for your family; it is forbidding the trimming of your ways to keep peace with them.
- That is how the pagan holidays come back in. A brother in the truth goes to see family he has not seen in a long while, and he sits down in the feast to keep them comfortable.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Jeremiah 2#^v33|Jeremiah 2:33]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 2#^v33]]
    Why trimmest thou thy way to seek love? therefore hast thou also taught the wicked ones thy ways.

**[[Matthew 10#^v38|Matthew 10:38-39]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v38]]
![[Matthew 10#^v39]]

- He that taketh not his cross and followeth after me is not worthy of me. He that findeth his life shall lose it, and he that loseth his life for my sake shall find it.
- What is given up is the sin that was being lived in. That old man is put to death, and the life that is found is coming out of the congregation of the dead.

**[[Matthew 10#^v40|Matthew 10:40-42]]**

![[Matthew 10#^v40]]
![[Matthew 10#^v41]]
![[Matthew 10#^v42]]

- He that receiveth you receiveth me, and he that receiveth me receiveth him that sent me. Even a cup of cold water given in the name of a disciple does not lose its reward, and the reward in view is the kingdom.

# Matthew 11: Art Thou He That Should Come

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 11]]

**[[Matthew 11#^v1|Matthew 11:1-3]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v1]]
![[Matthew 11#^v2]]
![[Matthew 11#^v3]]

- John hears in the prison the works of Christ and sends two of his disciples to ask, Art thou he that should come, or do we look for another?
- The messiah was spoken of from Genesis forward. They knew he was coming and they knew the description given of him.

**[[Matthew 11#^v4|Matthew 11:4-5]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v4]]
![[Matthew 11#^v5]]

- Christ answers by pointing to the works: the blind receive their sight, the lame walk, the lepers are cleansed, the deaf hear, the dead are raised, and the poor have the gospel preached to them. He is quoting Isaiah back to them.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 35#^v4|Isaiah 35:4-6]]**
    ![[Isaiah 35#^v4]]
    ![[Isaiah 35#^v5]]
    ![[Isaiah 35#^v6]]
    Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped, and the tongue of the dumb sing.

**[[Matthew 11#^v6|Matthew 11:6]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v6]]

- Blessed is he, whosoever shall not be offended in me.

**[[Matthew 11#^v7|Matthew 11:7-10]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v7]]
![[Matthew 11#^v8]]
![[Matthew 11#^v9]]
![[Matthew 11#^v10]]

- What went ye out into the wilderness to see? Not a reed shaken with the wind, nor a man in soft clothing, but a prophet, and more than a prophet.
- John is the messenger written of, sent before the face of the Lord to prepare the way.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Malachi 3#^v1|Malachi 3:1-3]]**
    ![[Malachi 3#^v1]]
    ![[Malachi 3#^v2]]
    ![[Malachi 3#^v3]]
    Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me; and he is like a refiner's fire.

**[[Matthew 11#^v11|Matthew 11:11]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v11]]

- Among them that are born of women there hath not risen a greater than John the Baptist, notwithstanding he that is least in the kingdom of heaven is greater than he.

**[[Matthew 11#^v12|Matthew 11:12]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v12]]

- From the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.
- The kingdom in view is Israel. The violence is what was done to them, and it is seen plainly in what the Romans did to Jerusalem, taking the kingdom by bloodshed.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Chronicles 28#^v5|1 Chronicles 28:5]]**
    ![[1 Chronicles 28#^v5]]
    Solomon sat upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel, which is what the kingdom is.

**[[Matthew 11#^v13|Matthew 11:13-14]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v13]]
![[Matthew 11#^v14]]

- All the prophets and the law prophesied until John, and if ye will receive it, this is Elias which was for to come.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Malachi 4#^v5|Malachi 4:5-6]]**
    ![[Malachi 4#^v5]]
    ![[Malachi 4#^v6]]
    I will send you Elijah the prophet, and he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children.

**[[Matthew 11#^v15|Matthew 11:15-19]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v15]]
![[Matthew 11#^v16]]
![[Matthew 11#^v17]]
![[Matthew 11#^v18]]
![[Matthew 11#^v19]]

- That generation is likened to children in the markets who piped and were not danced to. Christ did not move to the beat of their drum.
- John came neither eating nor drinking and they said he hath a devil; the Son of man came eating and drinking and they called him a gluttonous man. Nothing would have satisfied them.

**[[Matthew 11#^v20|Matthew 11:20-24]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v20]]
![[Matthew 11#^v21]]
![[Matthew 11#^v22]]
![[Matthew 11#^v23]]
![[Matthew 11#^v24]]

- Woe unto Chorazin, Bethsaida and Capernaum, because the mighty works were done among them and they repented not.
- Tyre, Sidon and Sodom would have repented at what these cities saw. Men watched works no ordinary man could do and still would not turn.

**[[Matthew 11#^v25|Matthew 11:25-27]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v25]]
![[Matthew 11#^v26]]
![[Matthew 11#^v27]]

- These things are hid from the wise and prudent and revealed unto babes. No man knoweth the Father save the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son will reveal him.

**[[Matthew 11#^v28|Matthew 11:28-30]]**

![[Matthew 11#^v28]]
![[Matthew 11#^v29]]
![[Matthew 11#^v30]]

- Come unto me, all ye that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. My yoke is easy, and my burden is light.

# Matthew 12: Lord of the Sabbath and the Blasphemy Against the Holy Ghost

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 12]]

**[[Matthew 12#^v1|Matthew 12:1-2]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v1]]
![[Matthew 12#^v2]]

- The disciples pluck the ears of corn on the sabbath being an hungred, and the Pharisees accuse them of doing that which is not lawful.

**[[Matthew 12#^v3|Matthew 12:3-4]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v3]]
![[Matthew 12#^v4]]

- Christ answers with what David did: he entered the house of God and ate the shewbread, which was lawful only for the priests, because he was not of Levi.

**[[Matthew 12#^v5|Matthew 12:5-8]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v5]]
![[Matthew 12#^v6]]
![[Matthew 12#^v7]]
![[Matthew 12#^v8]]

- The priests in the temple profane the sabbath and are blameless, because the service itself is commanded.
- If ye had known what this meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice, ye would not have condemned the guiltless. The Son of man is Lord even of the sabbath day.

**[[Matthew 12#^v9|Matthew 12:9-12]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v9]]
![[Matthew 12#^v10]]
![[Matthew 12#^v11]]
![[Matthew 12#^v12]]

- A man with a withered hand is set before him to see whether he would heal on the sabbath, that they might accuse him.
- A man will lift his own sheep out of a pit on the sabbath day, and a man is better than a sheep. Wherefore it is lawful to do well on the sabbath days.
- The distinction is between what falls out and what is planned. A sheep does not fall into a pit by appointment, and spilt milk is cleaned up when it happens. Wilful sin is the thing thought out beforehand.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Hebrews 10#^v26|Hebrews 10:26]]**
    ![[Hebrews 10#^v26]]
    If we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins.

**[[Matthew 12#^v13|Matthew 12:13]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v13]]

- The hand is restored whole, like as the other.

**[[Matthew 12#^v14|Matthew 12:14-21]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v14]]
![[Matthew 12#^v15]]
![[Matthew 12#^v16]]
![[Matthew 12#^v17]]
![[Matthew 12#^v18]]
![[Matthew 12#^v19]]
![[Matthew 12#^v20]]
![[Matthew 12#^v21]]

- The Pharisees hold a council how they might destroy him, and he withdraws himself and heals the multitudes, charging them not to make him known.
- This too was written beforehand: behold my servant, whom I have chosen, and he shall shew judgment to the Gentiles.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 42#^v1|Isaiah 42:1-3]]**
    ![[Isaiah 42#^v1]]
    ![[Isaiah 42#^v2]]
    ![[Isaiah 42#^v3]]
    The prophecy quoted: mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth; a bruised reed shall he not break.

**[[Matthew 12#^v22|Matthew 12:22-24]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v22]]
![[Matthew 12#^v23]]
![[Matthew 12#^v24]]

- One possessed with a devil, blind and dumb, is healed, and the people ask, Is not this the son of David?
- The Pharisees answer that he casts out devils by Beelzebub, saying his power came from the devil.

**[[Matthew 12#^v25|Matthew 12:25-29]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v25]]
![[Matthew 12#^v26]]
![[Matthew 12#^v27]]
![[Matthew 12#^v28]]
![[Matthew 12#^v29]]

- Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation. If Satan cast out Satan, his kingdom cannot stand.
- By whom do your children cast them out? Therefore they shall be your judges.
- No man enters a strong man's house and spoils his goods except he first bind the strong man. The binding is the taking away of his understanding; then his house can be spoiled.

**[[Matthew 12#^v30|Matthew 12:30]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v30]]

- He that is not with me is against me, and he that gathereth not with me scattereth abroad.

**[[Matthew 12#^v31|Matthew 12:31-32]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v31]]
![[Matthew 12#^v32]]

- All manner of sin and blasphemy shall be forgiven unto men, but the blasphemy against the Holy Ghost shall not be forgiven.
- The Holy Ghost is bound to the law, so speaking against it is not only with the mouth. Going back into the world and back into the ways that were left is a man's actions speaking for him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Hebrews 6#^v4|Hebrews 6:4-6]]**
    ![[Hebrews 6#^v4]]
    ![[Hebrews 6#^v5]]
    ![[Hebrews 6#^v6]]
    Those once enlightened, who have tasted the heavenly gift and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost, if they shall fall away, it is impossible to renew them again unto repentance.

**[[Matthew 12#^v33|Matthew 12:33-37]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v33]]
![[Matthew 12#^v34]]
![[Matthew 12#^v35]]
![[Matthew 12#^v36]]
![[Matthew 12#^v37]]

- The tree is known by his fruit, and a man is known by what he brings forth and by those he teaches.
- Out of the abundance of the heart the mouth speaketh, and every idle word that men shall speak they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment.

**[[Matthew 12#^v38|Matthew 12:38-42]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v38]]
![[Matthew 12#^v39]]
![[Matthew 12#^v40]]
![[Matthew 12#^v41]]
![[Matthew 12#^v42]]

- The scribes and Pharisees ask for a sign, and no sign is given but the sign of the prophet Jonas: three days and three nights in the heart of the earth.
- The men of Nineveh repented at the preaching of Jonas, and the queen of the south came from the uttermost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon. These men had a greater than both standing in front of them and would not hear.

**[[Matthew 12#^v43|Matthew 12:43-45]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v43]]
![[Matthew 12#^v44]]
![[Matthew 12#^v45]]

- The unclean spirit returns to a house swept and garnished and brings seven others more wicked than himself, and the last state of that man is worse than the first. That is the end of turning back after knowing.

**[[Matthew 12#^v46|Matthew 12:46-50]]**

![[Matthew 12#^v46]]
![[Matthew 12#^v47]]
![[Matthew 12#^v48]]
![[Matthew 12#^v49]]
![[Matthew 12#^v50]]

- Who is my mother, and who are my brethren? Whosoever shall do the will of my Father which is in heaven, the same is my brother, and sister, and mother.
- This does not unmake a man's natural family. It names a second family, made of those keeping the laws of the Most High.

# Matthew 13: The Parables of the Kingdom

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 13]]

**[[Matthew 13#^v1|Matthew 13:1-9]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v1]]
![[Matthew 13#^v2]]
![[Matthew 13#^v3]]
![[Matthew 13#^v4]]
![[Matthew 13#^v5]]
![[Matthew 13#^v6]]
![[Matthew 13#^v7]]
![[Matthew 13#^v8]]
![[Matthew 13#^v9]]

- A sower goes forth to sow. Seed falls by the way side, on stony places, among thorns, and into good ground, and only the good ground brings forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.

**[[Matthew 13#^v10|Matthew 13:10-13]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v10]]
![[Matthew 13#^v11]]
![[Matthew 13#^v12]]
![[Matthew 13#^v13]]

- It is given unto the disciples to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but not unto them.
- Whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance; but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.

  Precepts:
  - **[[John 3#^v8|John 3:8]]**
    ![[John 3#^v8]]
    The wind bloweth where it listeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit. The word rests on those it is given to.

**[[Matthew 13#^v14|Matthew 13:14-15]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v14]]
![[Matthew 13#^v15]]

- In them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias: by hearing ye shall hear and shall not understand, and seeing ye shall see and shall not perceive.
- They saw the works and did not perceive. The heart was made fat and the ears heavy so that they would not be converted, because judgment had to come.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 6#^v9|Isaiah 6:9-10]]**
    ![[Isaiah 6#^v9]]
    ![[Isaiah 6#^v10]]
    Hear ye indeed, but understand not; make the heart of this people fat, lest they convert and be healed.

**[[Matthew 13#^v16|Matthew 13:16-17]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v16]]
![[Matthew 13#^v17]]

- Blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear. Many prophets and righteous men desired to see these things and did not see them.

**[[Matthew 13#^v18|Matthew 13:18-23]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v18]]
![[Matthew 13#^v19]]
![[Matthew 13#^v20]]
![[Matthew 13#^v21]]
![[Matthew 13#^v22]]
![[Matthew 13#^v23]]

- The seed by the way side is the one who hears and understands not, and the wicked one catches away what was sown in his heart.
- The stony ground is the one who receives it with joy and has no root, and is offended when tribulation or persecution arises. That can come through family or through a job.
- The thorns are the care of this world and the deceitfulness of riches, which choke the word.
- The good ground hears and understands, and brings others in behind him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Mark 4#^v15|Mark 4:15]]**
    ![[Mark 4#^v15]]
    Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.

**[[Matthew 13#^v24|Matthew 13:24-30]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v24]]
![[Matthew 13#^v25]]
![[Matthew 13#^v26]]
![[Matthew 13#^v27]]
![[Matthew 13#^v28]]
![[Matthew 13#^v29]]
![[Matthew 13#^v30]]

- An enemy sows tares among the wheat while men slept, and the householder will not have them gathered up before the harvest lest the wheat be rooted up with them.
- The separating is not ours to do. Both grow together until the harvest.

**[[Matthew 13#^v31|Matthew 13:31-32]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v31]]
![[Matthew 13#^v32]]

- The kingdom of heaven is like a grain of mustard seed, the least of all seeds, which becomes the greatest among herbs. This work starts small and ends covering the earth.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 32#^v2|Deuteronomy 32:2]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v2]]
    My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew: the doctrine goes out over the whole earth, because Israel is scattered over the whole earth.

**[[Matthew 13#^v33|Matthew 13:33-35]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v33]]
![[Matthew 13#^v34]]
![[Matthew 13#^v35]]

- The leaven hid in three measures of meal leavens the whole, and all these things were spoken in parables, that it might be fulfilled, I will utter things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world.

**[[Matthew 13#^v36|Matthew 13:36-43]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v36]]
![[Matthew 13#^v37]]
![[Matthew 13#^v38]]
![[Matthew 13#^v39]]
![[Matthew 13#^v40]]
![[Matthew 13#^v41]]
![[Matthew 13#^v42]]
![[Matthew 13#^v43]]

- Christ gives the meaning himself: the sower is the Son of man, the field is the world, the good seed are the children of the kingdom, the tares are the children of the wicked one, the enemy is the devil, the harvest is the end of the world, and the reapers are the angels.
- The tares are gathered and burned, and the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father.

**[[Matthew 13#^v44|Matthew 13:44]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v44]]

- The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hid in a field, which a man finds and for joy thereof sells all that he hath and buys that field. Finding out who you are is that joy.

**[[Matthew 13#^v45|Matthew 13:45-46]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v45]]
![[Matthew 13#^v46]]

- The merchant man, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had and bought it.

**[[Matthew 13#^v47|Matthew 13:47-50]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v47]]
![[Matthew 13#^v48]]
![[Matthew 13#^v49]]
![[Matthew 13#^v50]]

- The net gathers of every kind, and the good are put into vessels and the bad cast away. The angels sever the wicked from among the just.
- This is not only about the other nations. Israel must keep these laws, and there will be those in these last days who are not keeping them.

**[[Matthew 13#^v51|Matthew 13:51-52]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v51]]
![[Matthew 13#^v52]]

- Every scribe instructed unto the kingdom of heaven is like a householder which bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old.

**[[Matthew 13#^v53|Matthew 13:53-58]]**

![[Matthew 13#^v53]]
![[Matthew 13#^v54]]
![[Matthew 13#^v55]]
![[Matthew 13#^v56]]
![[Matthew 13#^v57]]
![[Matthew 13#^v58]]

- In his own country they are astonished and ask, Is not this the carpenter's son? They name his mother, his brethren James, Joses, Simon and Judas, and his sisters, all of them living among them.
- A prophet is not without honour, save in his own country and in his own house, and he did not many mighty works there because of their unbelief.
