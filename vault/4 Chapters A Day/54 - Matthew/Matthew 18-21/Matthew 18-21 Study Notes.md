# Matthew 18: Offences, the Rule for a Trespassing Brother, and Forgiveness

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 18]]

## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 18 through 21. It is a reading session rather than a full breakdown, so the parables are read through and commented on where they touch the life of the congregation: how to carry an offence, how far forgiveness goes, marriage and divorce, what a rich man cannot buy, the labourers who all receive the same wage, and the husbandmen who killed the son. The session closes with the kingdom taken from those who taught the law and did not do it.

**Matthew 18:1-5**

![[Matthew 18#^v1]]
![[Matthew 18#^v2]]
![[Matthew 18#^v3]]
![[Matthew 18#^v4]]
![[Matthew 18#^v5]]

- Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven. Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, the same is the greatest.
- Coming into this truth means learning again from the beginning, humbling yourself to the men set before you and to the women who have been in it longer than you.

**Matthew 18:6-9**

![[Matthew 18#^v6]]
![[Matthew 18#^v7]]
![[Matthew 18#^v8]]
![[Matthew 18#^v9]]

- Woe unto the world because of offences, for it must needs be that offences come, but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh.
- Offences will come in the congregation because everyone comes out of a different life and is still learning the others. Much of what offends is small and passes once brethren know each other.
- What is in a man that will not let him go on has to be cut off. Better to enter into life maimed than to keep the thing and be cast into the fire.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Corinthians 11#^v19|1 Corinthians 11:19]]**
    ![[1 Corinthians 11#^v19]]
    There must be also heresies among you, that they which are approved may be made manifest among you.

**Matthew 18:10-14**

![[Matthew 18#^v10]]
![[Matthew 18#^v11]]
![[Matthew 18#^v12]]
![[Matthew 18#^v13]]
![[Matthew 18#^v14]]

- Take heed that ye despise not one of these little ones, for in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father.
- The shepherd leaves the ninety and nine to go after the one that is gone astray, and rejoices more over that one when he finds it.
- It is not the will of your Father which is in heaven that one of these little ones should perish. That men still fall short is a matter of their own will, not his.

**Matthew 18:15**

![[Matthew 18#^v15]]

- The first step is between the two of you alone. Go to that brother or sister yourself, and not to the rest of the congregation about what they did to you.
- If he hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother. Very often he did not know the thing was an offence at all.

**Matthew 18:16**

![[Matthew 18#^v16]]

- If he will not hear, take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established. The witnesses are there so that what is said is settled and not left to one man's account.

**Matthew 18:17-18**

![[Matthew 18#^v17]]
![[Matthew 18#^v18]]

- If he neglect to hear them, tell it unto the church. If he neglect to hear the church, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.
- This is the order. Matthew 18 is not to be pulled out over every small thing that has not first been carried to the brother himself.

**Matthew 18:19-20**

![[Matthew 18#^v19]]
![[Matthew 18#^v20]]

- If two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them, for where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.

**Matthew 18:21-22**

![[Matthew 18#^v21]]
![[Matthew 18#^v22]]

- Peter asks how often he must forgive, till seven times, and is answered, until seventy times seven.
- A brother striving for righteousness does not keep committing the same offence once he understands it. A man who does it over and over is showing what he is.

**Matthew 18:23-35**

![[Matthew 18#^v23]]
![[Matthew 18#^v24]]
![[Matthew 18#^v25]]
![[Matthew 18#^v26]]
![[Matthew 18#^v27]]
![[Matthew 18#^v28]]
![[Matthew 18#^v29]]
![[Matthew 18#^v30]]
![[Matthew 18#^v31]]
![[Matthew 18#^v32]]
![[Matthew 18#^v33]]
![[Matthew 18#^v34]]
![[Matthew 18#^v35]]

- The servant owing ten thousand talents is forgiven the whole debt, and then takes his fellowservant by the throat over an hundred pence.
- His lord delivers him to the tormentors, and so shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.
- Holding a grudge against a brother while expecting mercy from the Most High is the very thing this parable forbids.

# Matthew 19: Marriage, the Young Man's Possessions, and the Twelve Thrones

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 19]]

**Matthew 19:1-2**

![[Matthew 19#^v1]]
![[Matthew 19#^v2]]

- He departs from Galilee into the coasts of Judaea beyond Jordan, and great multitudes follow him and are healed.

**Matthew 19:3-6**

![[Matthew 19#^v3]]
![[Matthew 19#^v4]]
![[Matthew 19#^v5]]
![[Matthew 19#^v6]]

- The Pharisees ask, tempting him, whether it is lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause.
- He answers from the beginning: he which made them at the beginning made them male and female, and they twain shall be one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.

**Matthew 19:7-9**

![[Matthew 19#^v7]]
![[Matthew 19#^v8]]
![[Matthew 19#^v9]]

- Moses suffered them to put away their wives because of the hardness of their hearts, but from the beginning it was not so.
- The trouble in that day was men finding any cause at all to put a woman away. Whosoever putteth away his wife, except it be for fornication, and marrieth another, committeth adultery, and puts her into sin as well.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 24#^v1|Deuteronomy 24:1]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 24#^v1]]
    The bill of divorcement Moses allowed, given into her hand when he sent her out of his house.

**Matthew 19:10-12**

![[Matthew 19#^v10]]
![[Matthew 19#^v11]]
![[Matthew 19#^v12]]

- Some are born eunuchs from their mother's womb, some are made eunuchs of men, and some have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven's sake. He that is able to receive it, let him receive it.

**Matthew 19:13-15**

![[Matthew 19#^v13]]
![[Matthew 19#^v14]]
![[Matthew 19#^v15]]

- Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.

**Matthew 19:16-22**

![[Matthew 19#^v16]]
![[Matthew 19#^v17]]
![[Matthew 19#^v18]]
![[Matthew 19#^v19]]
![[Matthew 19#^v20]]
![[Matthew 19#^v21]]
![[Matthew 19#^v22]]

- Asked what good thing a man must do to have eternal life, Christ answers, If thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments, and then names them.
- Told to sell what he had and give to the poor, the young man went away sorrowful, for he had great possessions.

**Matthew 19:23-26**

![[Matthew 19#^v23]]
![[Matthew 19#^v24]]
![[Matthew 19#^v25]]
![[Matthew 19#^v26]]

- It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of heaven.
- Money buys nothing here. What is paid week by week to a preacher washes away no sin. The way in is the keeping of the commandments, as verse 17 already said.
- With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.

**Matthew 19:27-30**

![[Matthew 19#^v27]]
![[Matthew 19#^v28]]
![[Matthew 19#^v29]]
![[Matthew 19#^v30]]

- Ye which have followed me shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
- Every one that hath forsaken houses, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my name's sake, shall receive an hundredfold, and shall inherit everlasting life.
- What is lost coming into this truth is repaid in brethren and sisters and fathers and mothers, and the last part of the verse is the greater part: everlasting life.

- Labourers hired at the third, sixth, ninth and eleventh hours all receive a penny, and those hired first murmur at it.
- The householder answers that he does them no wrong; they agreed with him for a penny. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own?
- The wage here is the kingdom. A man labouring thirty years and a man who came in yesterday are working for the same thing, and each was called at the hour appointed for him.

- That cup is drunk in this day as well. The people are reproached and reviled for this word, and it will grow sharper, not lighter.

- John came in the way of righteousness and they believed him not, and when they had seen it they repented not afterward that they might believe him.

- Therefore the kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof. The chief priests and Pharisees perceived that he spake of them.
