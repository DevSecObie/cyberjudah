# Matthew 2: The Wise Men, Herod's Fear, and the Flight into Egypt

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 2]]

## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 2 through 5. The class was taken on short notice and moves quickly, but the history in these chapters is heavy: the birth of Christ under an Edomite king, the murder of the children in Bethlehem, the ministry of John the Baptist, the temptation in the wilderness, and the opening of the sermon on the mount where Christ magnifies the law rather than destroying it. The thread running through all four chapters is fulfilment. Everything that happens to Christ here was written of him beforehand in the law, the prophets, and the psalms.

**Matthew 2:1-2**

![[Matthew 2#^v1]]
![[Matthew 2#^v2]]

- Wise men come from the east to Jerusalem to worship him. This is prophetic; it was written that kings from far off would come and bow before him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 72#^v10|Psalms 72:10-11]]**
    ![[Psalms 72#^v10]]
    ![[Psalms 72#^v11]]
    The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents, and all kings shall fall down before him.

**Matthew 2:3**

![[Matthew 2#^v3]]

- Herod was set up as king over Judea by Rome, and he was an Edomite, not of Israel. When the messiah is born he is troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.
- If the messiah were coming to save all nations, there would be nothing in his birth to trouble a ruler of the nations. Herod is troubled because the coming of the messiah to Israel means judgment upon those who hold Israel down.

**Matthew 2:4-6**

![[Matthew 2#^v4]]
![[Matthew 2#^v5]]
![[Matthew 2#^v6]]

- The chief priests and the scribes answer at once: Bethlehem of Judea, for so it is written by the prophet. They knew the scriptures concerning the messiah.
- The same men who could quote where he would be born were blinded when he walked among them. Knowing the book is not the same as seeing the one the book speaks of.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Micah 5#^v2|Micah 5:2]]**
    ![[Micah 5#^v2]]
    The prophecy the priests were quoting: out of Bethlehem Ephratah shall he come forth that is to be ruler in Israel.
  - **[[Romans 11#^v7|Romans 11:7-10]]**
    ![[Romans 11#^v7]]
    ![[Romans 11#^v8]]
    ![[Romans 11#^v9]]
    ![[Romans 11#^v10]]
    Israel obtained not that which he seeketh for, and the rest were blinded, and their table was made a snare and a trap unto them.
  - **[[Isaiah 30#^v8|Isaiah 30:8]]**
    ![[Isaiah 30#^v8]]
    The table David spoke of is the writing itself, noted in a book for the time to come.

**Matthew 2:7-8**

![[Matthew 2#^v7]]
![[Matthew 2#^v8]]

- Herod calls the wise men privily and tells them to bring him word that he may come and worship also. He had no worship in mind; the next verses show what he intended.

**Matthew 2:9-11**

![[Matthew 2#^v9]]
![[Matthew 2#^v10]]
![[Matthew 2#^v11]]

- The star goes before them and stands over the place where the young child was, and they fall down and worship him and open their treasures.
- Gold, frankincense and myrrh are the presents and gifts David prophesied of in the psalm. What was written was fulfilled exactly.

**Matthew 2:12**

![[Matthew 2#^v12]]

- Warned of the Most High in a dream, they go home another way and do not report back to Herod.

**Matthew 2:13**

![[Matthew 2#^v13]]

- The angel tells Joseph to take the young child and his mother and flee into Egypt, because Herod will seek the young child to destroy him.
- They hid in Egypt among the Egyptians. A family that did not look like the people of that land would have been no more hidden there than anywhere else.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Revelation 12#^v2|Revelation 12:2-4]]**
    ![[Revelation 12#^v2]]
    ![[Revelation 12#^v3]]
    ![[Revelation 12#^v4]]
    The great red dragon stood before the woman to devour her child as soon as it was born, which is what Herod set out to do.
  - **[[Revelation 12#^v9|Revelation 12:9]]**
    ![[Revelation 12#^v9]]
    That same dragon is called that old serpent, the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world.
  - **[[Genesis 25#^v25|Genesis 25:25]]**
    ![[Genesis 25#^v25]]
    Esau came out red, which is the colour the dragon is given in the vision.
  - **[[Job 30#^v29|Job 30:29]]**
    ![[Job 30#^v29]]
    Job calls himself a brother to dragons, showing that dragons in prophecy stand for men.

**Matthew 2:14-15**

![[Matthew 2#^v14]]
![[Matthew 2#^v15]]

- They remain in Egypt until the death of Herod, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, Out of Egypt have I called my son.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Hosea 11#^v1|Hosea 11:1]]**
    ![[Hosea 11#^v1]]
    The prophecy quoted: when Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.

**Matthew 2:16**

![[Matthew 2#^v16]]

- Herod slays all the children in Bethlehem from two years old and under. This is the same pattern as Egypt, where the ruler killed the young children to stop a deliverer from rising.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Exodus 1#^v22|Exodus 1:22]]**
    ![[Exodus 1#^v22]]
    Pharaoh charged all his people to cast every son into the river, the same attempt to cut off a deliverer before he could grow.

**Matthew 2:17-18**

![[Matthew 2#^v17]]
![[Matthew 2#^v18]]

- The mourning in Ramah was written of beforehand by Jeremiah.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Jeremiah 31#^v15|Jeremiah 31:15]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 31#^v15]]
    Rachel weeping for her children, and would not be comforted, because they were not.

**Matthew 2:19-23**

![[Matthew 2#^v19]]
![[Matthew 2#^v20]]
![[Matthew 2#^v21]]
![[Matthew 2#^v22]]
![[Matthew 2#^v23]]

- After Herod dies the family returns, but Archelaus reigns in Judea, so being warned of the Most High Joseph turns aside into Galilee and dwells in Nazareth.
- Nazareth had no reputation. That the messiah was raised there was itself a fulfilment of what the prophets spoke.

  Precepts:
  - **[[John 1#^v45|John 1:45-46]]**
    ![[John 1#^v45]]
    ![[John 1#^v46]]
    Nathanael asks, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth, which shows how the place was regarded.

# Matthew 3: John the Baptist and the Baptism of the Holy Ghost and Fire

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 3]]

**Matthew 3:1-2**

![[Matthew 3#^v1]]
![[Matthew 3#^v2]]

- John the Baptist preaches in the wilderness of Judea, saying repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand. He is the messenger the prophets said would go before the Lord, coming in the spirit of Elijah.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Malachi 3#^v1|Malachi 3:1]]**
    ![[Malachi 3#^v1]]
    Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me.
  - **[[Malachi 4#^v5|Malachi 4:5-6]]**
    ![[Malachi 4#^v5]]
    ![[Malachi 4#^v6]]
    I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.

**Matthew 3:3**

![[Matthew 3#^v3]]

- The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, is Isaiah's word spoken of John.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 40#^v3|Isaiah 40:3-5]]**
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v3]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v4]]
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v5]]
    The prophecy John fulfilled, word for word.

**Matthew 3:4**

![[Matthew 3#^v4]]

- His raiment of camel's hair and the leathern girdle about his loins are not incidental. That was Elijah's manner, and it marks John as coming in the same spirit.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Kings 1#^v8|2 Kings 1:8]]**
    ![[2 Kings 1#^v8]]
    He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins; and he said, It is Elijah the Tishbite.

**Matthew 3:5-6**

![[Matthew 3#^v5]]
![[Matthew 3#^v6]]

- They were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins. Confession of sin is part of what is happening here, not a bare dipping in water.

**Matthew 3:7-8**

![[Matthew 3#^v7]]
![[Matthew 3#^v8]]

- To the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism John says, O generation of vipers, bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance.
- The fruits meet for repentance are works, not offerings brought to cover sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Acts 26#^v20|Acts 26:20]]**
    ![[Acts 26#^v20]]
    That they should repent and turn to God, and do works meet for repentance.

**Matthew 3:9**

![[Matthew 3#^v9]]

- Do not say within yourselves, We have Abraham to our father. The Most High is able of stones to raise up children unto Abraham.
- Descent from Abraham without the works of Abraham counts for nothing. The works of Abraham are believing the Most High and keeping his commandments.

  Precepts:
  - **[[John 8#^v37|John 8:37]]**
    ![[John 8#^v37]]
    I know that ye are Abraham's seed; but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you.
  - **[[John 8#^v39|John 8:39]]**
    ![[John 8#^v39]]
    If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham.
  - **[[Genesis 26#^v5|Genesis 26:5]]**
    ![[Genesis 26#^v5]]
    Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws; that is the work of Abraham.

**Matthew 3:10**

![[Matthew 3#^v10]]

- Every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down and cast into the fire. Christ says the same thing again later in this gospel.

**Matthew 3:11**

![[Matthew 3#^v11]]

- John says plainly that his water baptism is not the baptism of the one coming after him. That one shall baptize with the Holy Ghost and with fire.
- The Holy Ghost is bound to the law. To resist the Holy Ghost is to resist the commandments, and the Comforter brings the words of Christ to remembrance.

  Precepts:
  - **[[John 4#^v1|John 4:1-2]]**
    ![[John 4#^v1]]
    ![[John 4#^v2]]
    Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples.
  - **[[John 1#^v40|John 1:40]]**
    ![[John 1#^v40]]
    Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, was first a disciple of John, which is how water baptism carried on among some of the disciples.
  - **[[Acts 7#^v51|Acts 7:51]]**
    ![[Acts 7#^v51]]
    Ye do always resist the Holy Ghost: as your fathers did, so do ye.
  - **[[Acts 7#^v53|Acts 7:53]]**
    ![[Acts 7#^v53]]
    What the fathers resisted was the law, received by the disposition of angels and not kept.
  - **[[John 14#^v15|John 14:15]]**
    ![[John 14#^v15]]
    If ye love me, keep my commandments, and the Comforter follows.
  - **[[John 14#^v26|John 14:26]]**
    ![[John 14#^v26]]
    The Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, shall teach you all things and bring all things to your remembrance.
  - **[[John 6#^v63|John 6:63]]**
    ![[John 6#^v63]]
    The words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

**Matthew 3:12**

![[Matthew 3#^v12]]

- He will gather his wheat into the garner and burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire. There are two baptisms in view, and everyone receives one of them.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 1#^v4|Psalms 1:4]]**
    ![[Psalms 1#^v4]]
    The ungodly are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

**Matthew 3:13-15**

![[Matthew 3#^v13]]
![[Matthew 3#^v14]]
![[Matthew 3#^v15]]

- Christ comes to John to be baptized, and when John forbids him he answers, Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness.
- This had to be done before the ministry begins, because the messenger preparing the way was written in the prophets and had to be fulfilled.

**Matthew 3:16-17**

![[Matthew 3#^v16]]
![[Matthew 3#^v17]]

- Christ comes up out of the water, the Spirit of the Most High descends like a dove, and a voice out of the heavens says, This is my beloved son, in whom I am well pleased.
- The one in the water and the voice out of heaven are not the same. There is the Father, and there is his son.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Thessalonians 2#^v11|2 Thessalonians 2:11-12]]**
    ![[2 Thessalonians 2#^v11]]
    ![[2 Thessalonians 2#^v12]]
    The Most High shall send them strong delusion, that they should believe a lie.

# Matthew 4: The Temptation in the Wilderness and the Calling of the Fishers

Read the chapter → [[Matthew 4]]

**Matthew 4:1-3**

![[Matthew 4#^v1]]
![[Matthew 4#^v2]]
![[Matthew 4#^v3]]

- Christ is led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted, and after forty days and forty nights of fasting he is an hungred.
- The tempter comes at the weakest point and goes at the flesh first: command that these stones be made bread.
- This is the same set of temptations the man and the woman met in the garden, and they run through everything the enemy uses.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 John 2#^v15|1 John 2:15-16]]**
    ![[1 John 2#^v15]]
    ![[1 John 2#^v16]]
    The lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

**Matthew 4:4**

![[Matthew 4#^v4]]

- Christ answers with what is written: man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.
- The formula is given here. The flesh is weak and profits nothing; the spirit is strengthened by the word, and that is how the temptation is endured.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 8#^v3|Deuteronomy 8:3]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 8#^v3]]
    The law Christ quoted, written to Israel in the wilderness.
  - **[[John 6#^v63|John 6:63]]**
    ![[John 6#^v63]]
    It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing.
  - **[[Matthew 26#^v41|Matthew 26:41]]**
    ![[Matthew 26#^v41]]
    The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

**Matthew 4:5-6**

![[Matthew 4#^v5]]
![[Matthew 4#^v6]]

- The devil sets him on a pinnacle of the temple and quotes scripture at him. Satan knows the scriptures and will use them.
- This is why brothers and sisters must be able to try what is brought to them. Men can come with fringes and strong classes and still be workers of deceit.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 91#^v11|Psalms 91:11-12]]**
    ![[Psalms 91#^v11]]
    ![[Psalms 91#^v12]]
    The passage the devil quoted: he shall give his angels charge over thee.
  - **[[2 Corinthians 11#^v13|2 Corinthians 11:13-15]]**
    ![[2 Corinthians 11#^v13]]
    ![[2 Corinthians 11#^v14]]
    ![[2 Corinthians 11#^v15]]
    Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light, and his ministers as the ministers of righteousness.

**Matthew 4:7**

![[Matthew 4#^v7]]

- Christ answers again with the law: Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God. He fights the enemy with the commandments, which is what those who say the law is done away have thrown away.

**Matthew 4:8-10**

![[Matthew 4#^v8]]
![[Matthew 4#^v9]]
![[Matthew 4#^v10]]

- The devil shows him all the kingdoms of the world and offers them for worship. This is the pride of life and the lust of the eyes, offered to a man who at that moment was a carpenter out of Nazareth.
- Christ answers a third time out of the law: Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.

**Matthew 4:11**

![[Matthew 4#^v11]]

- The devil leaves him and angels come and minister unto him. The leaving is not permanent.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Luke 4#^v13|Luke 4:13]]**
    ![[Luke 4#^v13]]
    When the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.

**Matthew 4:12-16**

![[Matthew 4#^v12]]
![[Matthew 4#^v13]]
![[Matthew 4#^v14]]
![[Matthew 4#^v15]]
![[Matthew 4#^v16]]

- John is cast into prison and Christ departs into Galilee, and dwells in Capernaum in the borders of Zebulun and Nephthalim.
- The scripture calls that country Galilee of the Gentiles. Zebulun and Nephthalim are tribes of the northern kingdom, and this is the key to who the gentiles are that the gospel goes to after the southern kingdom rejects it.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 9#^v1|Isaiah 9:1-2]]**
    ![[Isaiah 9#^v1]]
    ![[Isaiah 9#^v2]]
    The prophecy quoted, naming the same land and the same people that saw great light.

**Matthew 4:17**

![[Matthew 4#^v17]]

- From that time Christ begins to preach the same word John preached: Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.

**Matthew 4:18-20**

![[Matthew 4#^v18]]
![[Matthew 4#^v19]]
![[Matthew 4#^v20]]

- Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men. The fishers come first; the hunters come after.
- Peter and Andrew straightway left their nets. That was their living, and they left it without hesitation. Many in this truth have been in it for years and know little, because they were not willing to give up what they were building.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Jeremiah 16#^v16|Jeremiah 16:16]]**
    ![[Jeremiah 16#^v16]]
    I will send for many fishers, and after will I send for many hunters.
  - **[[2 Esdras 13#^v54|2 Esdras 13:54]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 13#^v54]]
    For thou hast forsaken thine own way, and applied thy diligence unto my law, and sought it.

**Matthew 4:21-22**

![[Matthew 4#^v21]]
![[Matthew 4#^v22]]

- James and John immediately left the ship and their father and followed him. That is faith, and the Most High uses men who will make that kind of sacrifice.

**Matthew 4:23-25**

![[Matthew 4#^v23]]
![[Matthew 4#^v24]]
![[Matthew 4#^v25]]

- He goes about all Galilee teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness, and great multitudes follow him.

![[Matthew 5#^v3]]

**Matthew 5:17**

![[Matthew 5#^v17]]

  - **[[Zechariah 9#^v9|Zechariah 9:9]]**
    ![[Zechariah 9#^v9]]

**Matthew 5:18-20**

![[Matthew 5#^v18]]
![[Matthew 5#^v19]]
![[Matthew 5#^v20]]

- Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.
- Whosoever breaks one of these least commandments and teaches men so shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven, and whosoever does and teaches them shall be called great.
- Except your righteousness exceed the righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, ye shall in no case enter into the kingdom of heaven.

**Matthew 5:21-26**

![[Matthew 5#^v21]]
![[Matthew 5#^v22]]
![[Matthew 5#^v23]]
![[Matthew 5#^v24]]
![[Matthew 5#^v25]]
![[Matthew 5#^v26]]

- The commandment said Thou shalt not kill; Christ magnifies it to being angry with a brother without a cause and to what a man calls his brother.
- If thou bring thy gift to the altar and rememberest that thy brother hath ought against thee, leave the gift, be reconciled first, and then come and offer.

**Matthew 5:27-32**

![[Matthew 5#^v27]]
![[Matthew 5#^v28]]
![[Matthew 5#^v29]]
![[Matthew 5#^v30]]
![[Matthew 5#^v31]]
![[Matthew 5#^v32]]

- The commandment said Thou shalt not commit adultery; Christ magnifies it to looking on a woman to lust after her.
- Better that one member perish than that the whole body be cast into hell, and putting away a wife saving for the cause of fornication causes her to commit adultery.

**Matthew 5:33-37**

![[Matthew 5#^v33]]
![[Matthew 5#^v34]]
![[Matthew 5#^v35]]
![[Matthew 5#^v36]]
![[Matthew 5#^v37]]

- Swear not at all, neither by heaven nor by the earth nor by Jerusalem nor by thy head. Let your communication be Yea, yea; Nay, nay.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Proverbs 10#^v19|Proverbs 10:19]]**
    ![[Proverbs 10#^v19]]
    In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin.

**Matthew 5:38-39**

![[Matthew 5#^v38]]
![[Matthew 5#^v39]]

- An eye for an eye is answered with resist not evil. This is not a command to stand and be beaten; it is a command not to pay back evil as though it were owed.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Peter 3#^v9|1 Peter 3:9]]**
    ![[1 Peter 3#^v9]]
    Not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing.

**Matthew 5:40-42**

![[Matthew 5#^v40]]
![[Matthew 5#^v41]]
![[Matthew 5#^v42]]

- Give to him that asketh thee, and from him that would borrow of thee turn not thou away. Christ is magnifying a law already written to Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 15#^v8|Deuteronomy 15:8-10]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 15#^v8]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 15#^v9]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 15#^v10]]
    Thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need.

**Matthew 5:43-44**

![[Matthew 5#^v43]]
![[Matthew 5#^v44]]

- Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you.
- The enemy in view here is an enemy within your own people. Saul and David were both Israelites, and Saul was David's enemy.
- The reason for the command is that the enemy may yet repent, as one who persecuted the disciples afterward became one of them.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Samuel 18#^v29|1 Samuel 18:29]]**
    ![[1 Samuel 18#^v29]]
    Saul was yet the more afraid of David; and Saul became David's enemy continually.
  - **[[1 Samuel 24#^v17|1 Samuel 24:17]]**
    ![[1 Samuel 24#^v17]]
    Thou art more righteous than I: for thou hast rewarded me good, whereas I have rewarded thee evil.

**Matthew 5:45-47**

![[Matthew 5#^v45]]
![[Matthew 5#^v46]]
![[Matthew 5#^v47]]

- He maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust. Loving only those who love you is no more than the publicans do.

**Matthew 5:48**

![[Matthew 5#^v48]]

- Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect. Perfection here is a heart perfect with the Most High, walking in his statutes and keeping his commandments.
- Everything from verse 38 down is charity, and charity is the bond of perfectness.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Kings 8#^v61|1 Kings 8:61]]**
