# Luke 2: The Birth at Bethlehem and the Child in the Temple

Read the chapter → [[Luke 2]]

## Introduction

This session covers Luke 2 through 5. It is a reading session, with precepts brought in where they settle a point rather than a full breakdown. The chapters carry the birth of Christ under the taxing of Caesar, his circumcision and presentation according to the law, the preaching of John, the genealogy back to Adam, the temptation in the wilderness, the reading in the synagogue at Nazareth, and the calling of the first disciples. The reason for reading straight through is given in the epistle: give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.

  - **[[1 Timothy 4#^v13|1 Timothy 4:13]]**
