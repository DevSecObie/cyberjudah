# Luke 10: The Seventy Sent and the Good Samaritan

Read the chapter → [[Luke 10]]

## Introduction

This session covers Luke 10 through 13. The video it comes from is titled for John 6 through 9, but the reading given is Luke, and it is Luke that these notes follow. The seventy are sent out two and two, a lawyer is answered with the parable of the good Samaritan, the disciples are taught how to pray, the woes are pronounced upon the Pharisees and the lawyers, and the last chapter closes over Jerusalem. The thread is repentance and the discerning of the time.

**Luke 10:1-2**

![[Luke 10#^v1]]
![[Luke 10#^v2]]

- The Lord appointed other seventy also, and sent them two and two before his face into every city and place whither he himself would come.
- The harvest truly is great, but the labourers are few: pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he would send forth labourers into his harvest.

**Luke 10:3-4**

![[Luke 10#^v3]]
![[Luke 10#^v4]]

![[Luke 10#^v5]]
![[Luke 10#^v6]]
![[Luke 10#^v7]]

- Into whatsoever house ye enter, first say, Peace be to this house, and if the son of peace be there, your peace shall rest upon it.
- In the same house remain, eating and drinking such things as they give: for the labourer is worthy of his hire. Go not from house to house.

**Luke 10:8-9**

![[Luke 10#^v8]]
![[Luke 10#^v9]]

- Heal the sick that are therein, and say unto them, The kingdom of God is come nigh unto you.
- That word is said to a house that received them. Where there is charity and brotherhood enough to take a labourer in, the kingdom is near to that house.

![[Luke 10#^v13]]
![[Luke 10#^v14]]

- Woe unto thee, Chorazin! woe unto thee, Bethsaida! If the mighty works had been done in Tyre and Sidon which have been done in you, they had a great while ago repented, sitting in sackcloth and ashes.
- Other nations would have turned at what Israel watched and did not turn at. That is what a stiffnecked people looks like.
- He that heareth you heareth me; and he that despiseth you despiseth me; and he that despiseth me despiseth him that sent me.

![[Luke 10#^v18]]

![[Luke 10#^v20]]

- The seventy return with joy that even the devils are subject unto them, and he answers, I beheld Satan as lightning fall from heaven.
- Behold, I give unto you power to tread on serpents and scorpions, and over all the power of the enemy: and nothing shall by any means hurt you.
- Notwithstanding in this rejoice not, that the spirits are subject unto you; but rather rejoice, because your names are written in heaven.

- I thank thee, O Father, that thou hast hid these things from the wise and prudent, and hast revealed them unto babes.
- No man knoweth who the Son is, but the Father; and who the Father is, but the Son, and he to whom the Son will reveal him. A man does not know the Father apart from the son.

![[Luke 10#^v24]]

- Blessed are the eyes which see the things that ye see. Many prophets and kings have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them.
- The prophets wrote of these things and did not see the time of them.

- Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and thy neighbour as thyself. Thou hast answered right: this do, and thou shalt live.

**Luke 10:29-37**

![[Luke 10#^v29]]
![[Luke 10#^v30]]

![[Luke 10#^v34]]

- Go, and do thou likewise. The mercy that was withheld between the two houses is the very thing commanded here.

![[Luke 10#^v40]]

![[Luke 10#^v42]]

- Martha was cumbered about much serving and asked that Mary be told to help her; Mary sat at his feet and heard his word.
- Mary hath chosen that good part, which shall not be taken away from her. A man can be so taken up with the business of the day that the word never gets in him.

# Luke 11: How to Pray, the Strong Man, and the Woes

Read the chapter → [[Luke 11]]

**Luke 11:1-4**

![[Luke 11#^v1]]
![[Luke 11#^v2]]
![[Luke 11#^v3]]
![[Luke 11#^v4]]

- Lord, teach us to pray, as John also taught his disciples. He gives them the words: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.

**Luke 11:5-8**

![[Luke 11#^v5]]
![[Luke 11#^v6]]
![[Luke 11#^v7]]
![[Luke 11#^v8]]

- The friend at midnight will not rise because he is his friend, yet because of his importunity he will rise and give him as many as he needeth.
- Importunity is persistence to the point of annoyance. The man kept knocking, and the man inside gave him enough that he would not come back.

**Luke 11:9-13**

![[Luke 11#^v9]]
![[Luke 11#^v10]]
![[Luke 11#^v11]]
![[Luke 11#^v12]]
![[Luke 11#^v13]]

- Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.
- The parable before it is the point: prayer is not said once and left. There is persistence in it, and there is action behind it, because the man in the parable was standing at the door.
- If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children: how much more shall your heavenly Father give the Holy Spirit to them that ask him?

**Luke 11:14-16**

![[Luke 11#^v14]]
![[Luke 11#^v15]]
![[Luke 11#^v16]]

- He casts out a devil, and it was dumb, and when the devil was gone out the dumb spake, and the people wondered. Others said he cast out devils through Beelzebub.

**Luke 11:17-20**

![[Luke 11#^v17]]
![[Luke 11#^v18]]
![[Luke 11#^v19]]
![[Luke 11#^v20]]

- Every kingdom divided against itself is brought to desolation. If Satan also be divided against himself, how shall his kingdom stand?
- If I by Beelzebub cast out devils, by whom do your sons cast them out? therefore shall they be your judges.
- But if I with the finger of God cast out devils, no doubt the kingdom of God is come upon you.

**Luke 11:21-23**

![[Luke 11#^v21]]
![[Luke 11#^v22]]
![[Luke 11#^v23]]

- When a strong man armed keepeth his palace, his goods are in peace; but when a stronger than he shall come upon him, he taketh from him all his armour and divideth his spoils.
- He that is not with me is against me: and he that gathereth not with me scattereth.

**Luke 11:24-26**

![[Luke 11#^v24]]
![[Luke 11#^v25]]
![[Luke 11#^v26]]

- The unclean spirit returns and finds the house swept and garnished, and takes seven other spirits more wicked than himself.
- The house was clean and empty. There was no stronger one dwelling in it, and that is why what came back could enter, and the last state of that man is worse than the first.

**Luke 11:27-28**

![[Luke 11#^v27]]
![[Luke 11#^v28]]

- A woman blesses the womb that bare him, and he answers, Yea rather, blessed are they that hear the word of God, and keep it. He turned honour away from the mother and onto the keeping of the word.

**Luke 11:29-32**

![[Luke 11#^v29]]
![[Luke 11#^v30]]
![[Luke 11#^v31]]
![[Luke 11#^v32]]

- This is an evil generation: they seek a sign; and there shall no sign be given it, but the sign of Jonas the prophet.
- The queen of the south came from the uttermost parts of the earth to hear the wisdom of Solomon, and a greater than Solomon is here.
- The men of Nineveh repented at the preaching of Jonas, and a greater than Jonas is here. Men came a long way for less than what these would not cross the room for.

**Luke 11:33-36**

![[Luke 11#^v33]]
![[Luke 11#^v34]]
![[Luke 11#^v35]]
![[Luke 11#^v36]]

- The light of the body is the eye: when thine eye is single, thy whole body also is full of light; but when thine eye is evil, thy body also is full of darkness.
- One lamp lights a whole room, and put out, the whole room is dark. An eye fixed on the word fills the man with light; an eye that goes looking in dark places fills the whole man, thoughts and judgments together, with darkness.

**Luke 11:37-44**

![[Luke 11#^v37]]
![[Luke 11#^v38]]
![[Luke 11#^v39]]
![[Luke 11#^v40]]
![[Luke 11#^v41]]
![[Luke 11#^v42]]
![[Luke 11#^v43]]
![[Luke 11#^v44]]

- Ye Pharisees make clean the outside of the cup and the platter; but your inward part is full of ravening and wickedness.
- Ye tithe mint and rue and all manner of herbs, and pass over judgment and the love of God: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.
- Ye love the uppermost seats in the synagogues, and greetings in the markets, and ye are as graves which appear not.

**Luke 11:45-52**

![[Luke 11#^v45]]
![[Luke 11#^v46]]
![[Luke 11#^v47]]
![[Luke 11#^v48]]
![[Luke 11#^v49]]
![[Luke 11#^v50]]
![[Luke 11#^v51]]
![[Luke 11#^v52]]

- One of the lawyers says, Master, thus saying thou reproachest us also, and is answered with a woe of his own.
- Ye lade men with burdens grievous to be borne, and ye yourselves touch not the burdens with one of your fingers.
- Ye build the sepulchres of the prophets, and your fathers killed them, so ye bear witness that ye allow the deeds of your fathers.
- The blood of all the prophets, shed from the foundation of the world, from the blood of Abel unto the blood of Zacharias, shall be required of this generation.
- Woe unto you, lawyers! for ye have taken away the key of knowledge: ye entered not in yourselves, and them that were entering in ye hindered.

**Luke 11:53-54**

![[Luke 11#^v53]]
![[Luke 11#^v54]]

- The scribes and Pharisees began to urge him vehemently, laying wait for him, and seeking to catch something out of his mouth, that they might accuse him. From that point they were done with him.

# Luke 12: Hypocrisy, Covetousness, and the Watching Servant

Read the chapter → [[Luke 12]]

**Luke 12:1-3**

![[Luke 12#^v1]]
![[Luke 12#^v2]]
![[Luke 12#^v3]]

- Beware ye of the leaven of the Pharisees, which is hypocrisy.
- There is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed; neither hid, that shall not be known.

**Luke 12:4-7**

![[Luke 12#^v4]]
![[Luke 12#^v5]]
![[Luke 12#^v6]]
![[Luke 12#^v7]]

- Be not afraid of them that kill the body, and after that have no more that they can do. Fear him which after he hath killed hath power to cast into hell.
- Not one sparrow is forgotten before God, and even the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Things happen to a man that no one else sees; they are not unseen.

**Luke 12:8-12**

![[Luke 12#^v8]]
![[Luke 12#^v9]]
![[Luke 12#^v10]]
![[Luke 12#^v11]]
![[Luke 12#^v12]]

- Whosoever shall confess me before men, him shall the Son of man also confess before the angels of God.
- Unto him that blasphemeth against the Holy Ghost it shall not be forgiven.
- When they bring you unto the synagogues, and unto magistrates, and powers, take ye no thought how or what thing ye shall answer, for the Holy Ghost shall teach you in the same hour what ye ought to say.

**Luke 12:13-21**

![[Luke 12#^v13]]
![[Luke 12#^v14]]
![[Luke 12#^v15]]
![[Luke 12#^v16]]
![[Luke 12#^v17]]
![[Luke 12#^v18]]
![[Luke 12#^v19]]
![[Luke 12#^v20]]
![[Luke 12#^v21]]

- A man asks him to make his brother divide the inheritance, and is answered, Man, who made me a judge or a divider over you?
- Take heed, and beware of covetousness: for a man's life consisteth not in the abundance of the things which he possesseth. He discerned the thoughts of both men in that dispute.
- The rich man pulls down his barns to build greater, and is told, Thou fool, this night thy soul shall be required of thee. So is he that layeth up treasure for himself, and is not rich toward God.

**Luke 12:22-31**

![[Luke 12#^v22]]
![[Luke 12#^v23]]
![[Luke 12#^v24]]
![[Luke 12#^v25]]
![[Luke 12#^v26]]
![[Luke 12#^v27]]
![[Luke 12#^v28]]
![[Luke 12#^v29]]
![[Luke 12#^v30]]
![[Luke 12#^v31]]

- Consider the ravens: they neither sow nor reap, and God feedeth them. Consider the lilies: Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.
- After all these things do the nations of the world seek: and your Father knoweth that ye have need of these things. But rather seek ye the kingdom of God; and all these things shall be added unto you.

**Luke 12:32-34**

![[Luke 12#^v32]]
![[Luke 12#^v33]]
![[Luke 12#^v34]]

- Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom.
- Provide yourselves treasure in the heavens that faileth not, where no thief approacheth, neither moth corrupteth. For where your treasure is, there will your heart be also.

**Luke 12:35-40**

![[Luke 12#^v35]]
![[Luke 12#^v36]]
![[Luke 12#^v37]]
![[Luke 12#^v38]]
![[Luke 12#^v39]]
![[Luke 12#^v40]]

- Let your loins be girded about, and your lights burning, and be like men that wait for their lord.
- Blessed are those servants, whom the lord when he cometh shall find watching. Be ye therefore ready also: for the Son of man cometh at an hour when ye think not.

**Luke 12:41-48**

![[Luke 12#^v41]]
![[Luke 12#^v42]]
![[Luke 12#^v43]]
![[Luke 12#^v44]]
![[Luke 12#^v45]]
![[Luke 12#^v46]]
![[Luke 12#^v47]]
![[Luke 12#^v48]]

- The faithful and wise steward gives the household their portion of meat in due season, and is made ruler over all his lord hath.
- The servant that says in his heart, My lord delayeth his coming, and begins to beat the menservants, has his portion appointed with the unbelievers.
- That servant, which knew his lord's will, and prepared not himself, shall be beaten with many stripes. For unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required.

**Luke 12:49-50**

![[Luke 12#^v49]]
![[Luke 12#^v50]]

- I have a baptism to be baptized with; and how am I straitened till it be accomplished. He was in anguish of mind over it. He knew what he had come to do.

**Luke 12:51-53**

![[Luke 12#^v51]]
![[Luke 12#^v52]]
![[Luke 12#^v53]]

- Suppose ye that I am come to give peace on earth? I tell you, Nay; but rather division.
- The division named here is inside a house: father against son, mother against daughter, mother in law against daughter in law. Coming into this truth is where that starts.

**Luke 12:54-59**

![[Luke 12#^v54]]
![[Luke 12#^v55]]
![[Luke 12#^v56]]
![[Luke 12#^v57]]
![[Luke 12#^v58]]
![[Luke 12#^v59]]

- Ye hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the sky and of the earth; but how is it that ye do not discern this time?
- Men who can read the weather and every other sign will not read the one in front of them.
- Agree with thine adversary in the way, lest he hale thee to the judge, and thou shalt not depart thence, till thou hast paid the very last mite.

# Luke 13: Except Ye Repent, and the Strait Gate

Read the chapter → [[Luke 13]]

**Luke 13:1-5**

![[Luke 13#^v1]]
![[Luke 13#^v2]]
![[Luke 13#^v3]]
![[Luke 13#^v4]]
![[Luke 13#^v5]]

- Of the Galilaeans whose blood Pilate mingled with their sacrifices, and of the eighteen upon whom the tower in Siloam fell, he says they were not sinners above all others.
- I tell you, Nay: but, except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish. It is said twice, in the same words.

**Luke 13:6-9**

![[Luke 13#^v6]]
![[Luke 13#^v7]]
![[Luke 13#^v8]]
![[Luke 13#^v9]]

- Three years he came seeking fruit on the fig tree and found none, and the dresser asks for one more year to dig about it and dung it.
- If it bear fruit, well: and if not, then after that thou shalt cut it down. Three years is the length of his own ministry among the people, and Jerusalem was cut down after it.

**Luke 13:10-17**

![[Luke 13#^v10]]
![[Luke 13#^v11]]
![[Luke 13#^v12]]
![[Luke 13#^v13]]
![[Luke 13#^v14]]
![[Luke 13#^v15]]
![[Luke 13#^v16]]
![[Luke 13#^v17]]

- A woman bowed together eighteen years and unable to lift herself up is loosed from her infirmity, and immediately made straight.
- The ruler of the synagogue answers with indignation that there are six days in which men ought to work.
- Thou hypocrite, doth not each one of you on the sabbath loose his ox or his ass from the stall, and lead him away to watering?
- Ought not this woman, being a daughter of Abraham, be loosed from this bond on the sabbath day? All his adversaries were ashamed, and all the people rejoiced.

**Luke 13:18-21**

![[Luke 13#^v18]]
![[Luke 13#^v19]]
![[Luke 13#^v20]]
![[Luke 13#^v21]]

- The kingdom of God is like a grain of mustard seed which grew and waxed a great tree, and like leaven hid in three measures of meal till the whole was leavened.

**Luke 13:22-30**

![[Luke 13#^v22]]
![[Luke 13#^v23]]
![[Luke 13#^v24]]
![[Luke 13#^v25]]
![[Luke 13#^v26]]
![[Luke 13#^v27]]
![[Luke 13#^v28]]
![[Luke 13#^v29]]
![[Luke 13#^v30]]

- Strive to enter in at the strait gate: for many will seek to enter in, and shall not be able.
- The strait way is a narrow one, walked by discipline: correction, falling, getting back up, and correcting yourself. Many hear this word and turn back to the world rather than walk it.
- We have eaten and drunk in thy presence, and thou hast taught in our streets. And he shall say, I know you not whence ye are; depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity.
- They shall come from the east, and from the west, and from the north, and from the south, and shall sit down in the kingdom of God. They come from the four corners because they were scattered to the four corners.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Esdras 7#^v6|2 Esdras 7:6-8]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 7#^v6]]
    ![[2 Esdras 7#^v7]]
    ![[2 Esdras 7#^v8]]
    The way to the city is narrow, set in a dangerous place, and but one man only can go therein at once.

**Luke 13:31-33**

![[Luke 13#^v31]]
![[Luke 13#^v32]]
![[Luke 13#^v33]]

- The Pharisees tell him to depart, for Herod will kill him, and he answers, Go ye, and tell that fox, Behold, I cast out devils, and I do cures to day and to morrow.
- I must walk to day, and to morrow, and the day following: for it cannot be that a prophet perish out of Jerusalem. He knew his work and would not be moved off it by a threat.

**Luke 13:34-35**

![[Luke 13#^v34]]
![[Luke 13#^v35]]

- O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, which killest the prophets, how often would I have gathered thy children together, as a hen doth gather her brood under her wings, and ye would not!
- Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.
