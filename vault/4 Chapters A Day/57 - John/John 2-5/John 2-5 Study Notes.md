# John 2: The First Miracle and the Cleansing of the Temple

Read the chapter → [[John 2]]

## Introduction

This session covers John 2 through 5. The chapters in John run long, so the precepts are pulled where they settle a point. The reading carries the first miracles, the first clashes with the Pharisees, Nicodemus coming by night, the woman at the well in Samaria, and the healing at the pool on the sabbath. Two threads run through it: who the world is that he was sent to, and the unbelief that kept his own from seeing what was standing in front of them.

# John 3: Nicodemus, the Serpent Lifted Up, and Who the World Is

Read the chapter → [[John 3]]

**John 3:1-3**

![[John 3#^v1]]
![[John 3#^v2]]
![[John 3#^v3]]

- Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a ruler of the Jews, comes by night. Those men were the teachers of the synagogues, and it would have cost him standing to come openly.
- Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.
- This is spoken to a man who believed himself already right. Sinners and publicans received Christ more readily because they knew they needed a saviour; a ruler of the Jews had to start over.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Esdras 14#^v34|2 Esdras 14:34]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 14#^v34]]
    If so be that ye will subdue your own understanding, and reform your hearts, ye shall be kept alive, and after death ye shall obtain mercy.

**John 3:4-5**

![[John 3#^v4]]
![[John 3#^v5]]

- Nicodemus takes it literally and asks whether a man can enter the second time into his mother's womb.
- Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. Both are named plainly elsewhere as the word.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Peter 1#^v23|1 Peter 1:23]]**
    ![[1 Peter 1#^v23]]
    Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.

**John 3:6-8**

![[John 3#^v6]]
![[John 3#^v7]]
![[John 3#^v8]]

- That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.
- A man left in the flesh will do the works of the flesh, and those works are named in the epistles.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Romans 8#^v8|Romans 8:8]]**
    ![[Romans 8#^v8]]
    So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.

**John 3:9-13**

![[John 3#^v9]]
![[John 3#^v10]]
![[John 3#^v11]]
![[John 3#^v12]]
![[John 3#^v13]]

- Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things? He was a chief teacher, and men depended on him for the understanding of the law.
- If I have told you earthly things, and ye believe not, how shall ye believe, if I tell you of heavenly things?

**John 3:14-15**

![[John 3#^v14]]
![[John 3#^v15]]

- As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up.
- The history behind that is plain, and so is who it was for. It was Israel in the wilderness that spake against the Most High and against Moses, and Israel that was bitten, and Israel that looked and lived.
- The serpent of brass was a sign of salvation, and it came with remembrance of the commandment of the law. Looking on it was not the whole of it; there was repentance with it.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Numbers 21#^v5|Numbers 21:5-9]]**
    ![[Numbers 21#^v5]]
    ![[Numbers 21#^v6]]
    ![[Numbers 21#^v7]]
    ![[Numbers 21#^v8]]
    ![[Numbers 21#^v9]]
    Much people of Israel died, and every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live.
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 16#^v5|Wisdom of Solomon 16:5-6]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 16#^v5]]
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 16#^v6]]
    They were troubled for a small season, that they might be admonished, having a sign of salvation, to put them in remembrance of the commandment of thy law.

**John 3:16-18**

![[John 3#^v16]]
![[John 3#^v17]]
![[John 3#^v18]]

- For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son. This follows straight on from the serpent, and it is the same people in view.
- John's own gospel names who the world is: the one he was made manifest to, the one the Pharisees said was gone after him, and the one he taught openly in the synagogue and the temple.

  Precepts:
  - **[[John 1#^v29|John 1:29-31]]**
    ![[John 1#^v29]]
    ![[John 1#^v30]]
    ![[John 1#^v31]]
    Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world; that he should be made manifest to Israel.
  - **[[John 12#^v12|John 12:12-13]]**
    ![[John 12#^v12]]
    ![[John 12#^v13]]
    Blessed is the King of Israel that cometh in the name of the Lord.
  - **[[John 12#^v19|John 12:19]]**
    ![[John 12#^v19]]
    The Pharisees said, Behold, the world is gone after him, of that same multitude.
  - **[[John 18#^v20|John 18:20]]**
    ![[John 18#^v20]]
    I spake openly to the world; I ever taught in the synagogue, and in the temple, whither the Jews always resort.

**John 3:19-21**

![[John 3#^v19]]
![[John 3#^v20]]
![[John 3#^v21]]

- This is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil.
- A man can be comfortable and even do well in the darkness he was raised in. Coming into the light requires change, and refusing that change is what condemns him.
- The Pharisees and the rulers were not condemned by Christ; they condemned themselves by not humbling down to repent.
- But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be made manifest, that they are wrought in God.

**John 3:22-30**

![[John 3#^v22]]
![[John 3#^v23]]
![[John 3#^v24]]
![[John 3#^v25]]
![[John 3#^v26]]
![[John 3#^v27]]
![[John 3#^v28]]
![[John 3#^v29]]
![[John 3#^v30]]

- John's disciples notice that all men are coming to Jesus, and John answers, A man can receive nothing, except it be given him from heaven.
- I am not the Christ, but that I am sent before him. He must increase, but I must decrease.

**John 3:31-36**

![[John 3#^v31]]
![[John 3#^v32]]
![[John 3#^v33]]
![[John 3#^v34]]
![[John 3#^v35]]
![[John 3#^v36]]

- He that hath received his testimony hath set to his seal that God is true. Not to receive it is to call the record God gave of his son a lie.
- He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 John 5#^v10|1 John 5:10]]**
    ![[1 John 5#^v10]]
    He that believeth not God hath made him a liar; because he believeth not the record that God gave of his Son.

# John 4: The Woman at the Well and the Nobleman's Son

Read the chapter → [[John 4]]

**John 4:1-6**

![[John 4#^v1]]
![[John 4#^v2]]
![[John 4#^v3]]
![[John 4#^v4]]
![[John 4#^v5]]
![[John 4#^v6]]

- Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples, many of whom had begun with John.
- He must needs go through Samaria, and comes to Sychar, near to the parcel of ground that Jacob gave to his son Joseph. Joseph was the father of Ephraim and Manasseh, so this is northern kingdom ground.

**John 4:7-9**

![[John 4#^v7]]
![[John 4#^v8]]
![[John 4#^v9]]

- How is it that thou, being a Jew, askest drink of me, which am a woman of Samaria? for the Jews have no dealings with the Samaritans.
- The Jews in that day were Judah, Benjamin and Levi, not all twelve tribes. The Samaritans were Ephraim, and the reason for the separation is written in the prophets.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ezra 1#^v5|Ezra 1:5]]**
    ![[Ezra 1#^v5]]
    The chief of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests, and the Levites, went up to build the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem.
  - **[[Isaiah 7#^v8|Isaiah 7:8-9]]**
    ![[Isaiah 7#^v8]]
    ![[Isaiah 7#^v9]]
    Within threescore and five years shall Ephraim be broken, that it be not a people; and the head of Ephraim is Samaria.
  - **[[Hosea 4#^v17|Hosea 4:17]]**
    ![[Hosea 4#^v17]]
    Ephraim is joined to idols: let him alone.

**John 4:10-15**

![[John 4#^v10]]
![[John 4#^v11]]
![[John 4#^v12]]
![[John 4#^v13]]
![[John 4#^v14]]
![[John 4#^v15]]

- If thou knewest the gift of God, and who it is that saith to thee, Give me to drink, thou wouldest have asked of him, and he would have given thee living water. She answers about the depth of the well.
- Art thou greater than our father Jacob, which gave us the well? She names Jacob as her father. Jacob is the progenitor of one nation, and this is the ground he gave to Joseph.
- Whosoever drinketh of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst; it shall be in him a well of water springing up into everlasting life.

**John 4:16-19**

![[John 4#^v16]]
![[John 4#^v17]]
![[John 4#^v18]]
![[John 4#^v19]]

- Go, call thy husband. She answers that she has none, and he tells her she has had five, and the one she has now is not her husband.
- Sir, I perceive that thou art a prophet. Being told her own life by a man who had never met her is what turned her.

**John 4:20-24**

![[John 4#^v20]]
![[John 4#^v21]]
![[John 4#^v22]]
![[John 4#^v23]]
![[John 4#^v24]]

- The hour cometh, when ye shall neither in this mountain, nor yet at Jerusalem, worship the Father. Jerusalem itself was to be laid level with the ground.
- Salvation is of the Jews, because Christ sprang out of Judah.
- The true worshippers shall worship the Father in spirit and in truth. Without temple, sacrifice or priesthood, a scattered people worship in sincerity and in truth, which is what Joshua charged Israel with.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Luke 19#^v41|Luke 19:41-44]]**
    ![[Luke 19#^v41]]
    ![[Luke 19#^v42]]
    ![[Luke 19#^v43]]
    ![[Luke 19#^v44]]
    Thine enemies shall cast a trench about thee, and shall not leave in thee one stone upon another.
  - **[[Joshua 24#^v14|Joshua 24:14]]**
    ![[Joshua 24#^v14]]
    Fear the Lord, and serve him in sincerity and in truth.

**John 4:25-26**

![[John 4#^v25]]
![[John 4#^v26]]

- I know that Messias cometh, which is called Christ. Jesus saith unto her, I that speak unto thee am he.

**John 4:27-30**

![[John 4#^v27]]
![[John 4#^v28]]
![[John 4#^v29]]
![[John 4#^v30]]

- The disciples marvelled that he talked with the woman, because of the separation that had been commanded, yet no man said, Why talkest thou with her?
- She left her waterpot and told the men of the city, Come, see a man, which told me all things that ever I did: is not this the Christ?

**John 4:31-38**

![[John 4#^v31]]
![[John 4#^v32]]
![[John 4#^v33]]
![[John 4#^v34]]
![[John 4#^v35]]
![[John 4#^v36]]
![[John 4#^v37]]
![[John 4#^v38]]

- My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work. The disciples took it for literal food.
- Lift up your eyes, and look on the fields; for they are white already to harvest. One soweth, and another reapeth.

**John 4:39-42**

![[John 4#^v39]]
![[John 4#^v40]]
![[John 4#^v41]]
![[John 4#^v42]]

- Many of the Samaritans believed on him for the saying of the woman, and many more because of his own word, and said, We know that this is indeed the Christ, the Saviour of the world.

**John 4:43-54**

![[John 4#^v43]]
![[John 4#^v44]]
![[John 4#^v45]]
![[John 4#^v46]]
![[John 4#^v47]]
![[John 4#^v48]]
![[John 4#^v49]]
![[John 4#^v50]]
![[John 4#^v51]]
![[John 4#^v52]]
![[John 4#^v53]]
![[John 4#^v54]]

- Except ye see signs and wonders, ye will not believe. The nobleman wants him to come down before his child dies.
- Go thy way; thy son liveth. And the man believed the word that Jesus had spoken unto him, and he went his way. He did not need to see it first.
- The servants meet him with the news, and the hour matches. The believing is what stands out here; unbelief was always the trouble with the people, and this man believed a word without a sign.

# John 5: The Pool of Bethesda and the Witness to the Son

Read the chapter → [[John 5]]

**John 5:1-9**

![[John 5#^v1]]
![[John 5#^v2]]
![[John 5#^v3]]
![[John 5#^v4]]
![[John 5#^v5]]
![[John 5#^v6]]
![[John 5#^v7]]
![[John 5#^v8]]
![[John 5#^v9]]

- At the pool called Bethesda an angel troubled the water at a certain season, and whosoever first stepped in was made whole.
- A man had been there thirty and eight years and had no man to put him in. Wilt thou be made whole?
- Rise, take up thy bed, and walk. He did not have to wait for the water at all, and immediately he was made whole. And on the same day was the sabbath.

**John 5:10-13**

![[John 5#^v10]]
![[John 5#^v11]]
![[John 5#^v12]]
![[John 5#^v13]]

- The first thing said to a man healed after thirty-eight years is that it is not lawful for him to carry his bed. Not one word about the healing.

**John 5:14**

![[John 5#^v14]]

- Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee. The healing came with a charge, and that charge is the keeping of the commandments.

**John 5:15-16**

![[John 5#^v15]]
![[John 5#^v16]]

- The Jews persecuted him, and sought to slay him, because he had done these things on the sabbath day.
- He had broken no sabbath. He answers them elsewhere out of their own practice: they circumcise on the sabbath so that the law of Moses be not broken, and a man made every whit whole is greater than that.

  Precepts:
  - **[[John 7#^v21|John 7:21-24]]**
    ![[John 7#^v21]]
    ![[John 7#^v22]]
    ![[John 7#^v23]]
    ![[John 7#^v24]]
    Are ye angry at me, because I have made a man every whit whole on the sabbath day? Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.

**John 5:17-18**

![[John 5#^v17]]
![[John 5#^v18]]

- My Father worketh hitherto, and I work. They sought the more to kill him, because he said also that God was his Father, making himself equal with God.
- That attitude toward a just man was written down long before.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 2#^v16|Wisdom of Solomon 2:16]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 2#^v16]]
    We are esteemed of him as counterfeits: he abstaineth from our ways as from filthiness, and maketh his boast that God is his father.

**John 5:19-21**

![[John 5#^v19]]
![[John 5#^v20]]
![[John 5#^v21]]

- The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do.
- As the Father raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them; even so the Son quickeneth whom he will. To quicken is to make alive, and it covers both the raising of the dead and the making alive of men dead in their sins.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Peter 3#^v18|1 Peter 3:18]]**
    ![[1 Peter 3#^v18]]
    Being put to death in the flesh, but quickened by the Spirit.

**John 5:22-24**

![[John 5#^v22]]
![[John 5#^v23]]
![[John 5#^v24]]

- The Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgment unto the Son, which is why men stand before the judgment seat of Christ.
- He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Corinthians 5#^v10|2 Corinthians 5:10]]**
    ![[2 Corinthians 5#^v10]]
    We must all appear before the judgment seat of Christ.
  - **[[Romans 8#^v1|Romans 8:1]]**
    ![[Romans 8#^v1]]
    There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.

**John 5:25-29**

![[John 5#^v25]]
![[John 5#^v26]]
![[John 5#^v27]]
![[John 5#^v28]]
![[John 5#^v29]]

- The hour is coming, and now is, when the dead shall hear the voice of the Son of God: and they that hear shall live. That happened before their eyes with Lazarus, and it reaches forward to the last judgment.
- All that are in the graves shall hear his voice, and shall come forth; they that have done good, unto the resurrection of life; and they that have done evil, unto the resurrection of damnation.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Daniel 12#^v2|Daniel 12:2]]**
    ![[Daniel 12#^v2]]
    Many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.

**John 5:30-38**

![[John 5#^v30]]
![[John 5#^v31]]
![[John 5#^v32]]
![[John 5#^v33]]
![[John 5#^v34]]
![[John 5#^v35]]
![[John 5#^v36]]
![[John 5#^v37]]
![[John 5#^v38]]

- I seek not mine own will, but the will of the Father which hath sent me.
- Ye sent unto John, and he bare witness unto the truth. He was a burning and a shining light, and ye were willing for a season to rejoice in his light.
- But I have greater witness than that of John: the works which the Father hath given me to finish bear witness of me.

**John 5:39-40**

![[John 5#^v39]]
![[John 5#^v40]]

- Search the scriptures; for in them ye think ye have eternal life: and they are they which testify of me.
- They could not match the prophecies to the life being lived in front of them. He had to open the scriptures himself, from Moses through all the prophets, to shew what they said of him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Luke 24#^v27|Luke 24:27]]**
    ![[Luke 24#^v27]]
    Beginning at Moses and all the prophets, he expounded unto them in all the scriptures the things concerning himself.

**John 5:41-47**

![[John 5#^v41]]
![[John 5#^v42]]
![[John 5#^v43]]
![[John 5#^v44]]
![[John 5#^v45]]
![[John 5#^v46]]
![[John 5#^v47]]

- I am come in my Father's name, and ye receive me not: if another shall come in his own name, him ye will receive.
- Do not think that I will accuse you to the Father: there is one that accuseth you, even Moses, in whom ye trust. For had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me.
- If ye believe not his writings, how shall ye believe my words? A man who will not take what Moses wrote will not take Christ either.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Acts 3#^v22|Acts 3:22-23]]**
    ![[Acts 3#^v22]]
    ![[Acts 3#^v23]]
    A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear. Every soul which will not hear that prophet shall be destroyed from among the people.
  - **[[Luke 16#^v31|Luke 16:31]]**
    ![[Luke 16#^v31]]
    If they hear not Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded, though one rose from the dead.
