# Genesis 17: The Covenant Sealed in the Flesh

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 17]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Genesis 17|Genesis 17]] through 20. Abraham is a righteous example all across the board: faith, obedience, hospitality, leadership, and immediate action on the commandments of the Most High.

**[[Genesis 17#^v1|Genesis 17:1]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v1]]

- At ninety and nine years old Abram was commanded to walk before the Most High and be perfect. There is no age excuse; Christ said nothing new in [[Matthew 5#^v48|Matthew 5:48]], for the command to be perfect was given thousands of years before.
- Perfect according to the scriptures means to walk in His statutes and keep His commandments; that is how Israel perfects itself.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Kings 8#^v61|1 Kings 8:61]]**
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v61]]
    A perfect heart is one that walks in His statutes and keeps His commandments; that is the meaning of be thou perfect.

**[[Genesis 17#^v4|Genesis 17:4-6]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v4]]
![[Genesis 17#^v5]]
![[Genesis 17#^v6]]

- When the Most High began dealing with Abram on a covenant level, his name was changed: Abram became Abraham, a father of many nations, and kings would come out of him.
- Father of many nations does not mean everybody is spiritually a child of Abraham. The same promise was passed down the seed line: Jacob's name was changed to Israel and told a nation and a company of nations and kings would come out of his loins.
- The blessing of all men and the covenant were made to rest upon the head of Jacob and divided among the twelve tribes. Kings come out of that seed, including Christ, the King of kings.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 35#^v10|Genesis 35:10-11]]**
    ![[Genesis 35#^v10]]
    ![[Genesis 35#^v11]]
    The same covenant words passed to Jacob, named Israel: nations and kings out of his loins.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v19|Ecclesiasticus 44:19-23]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v19]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v20]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v21]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v22]]
    The blessing of all men and the covenant rested on the head of Jacob and was divided among the twelve tribes.

**[[Genesis 17#^v7|Genesis 17:7]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v7]]

- The covenant is with Abraham's seed after him, and seed is literal offspring, not a spiritual metaphor. The Most High promised to be a God to Abraham and to his seed after him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 41#^v8|Isaiah 41:8]]**
    ![[Isaiah 41#^v8]]
    Israel is the servant, Jacob the chosen, the seed of Abraham; Israel is the nation that carries the seed of Abraham.

**[[Genesis 17#^v10|Genesis 17:10-14]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v10]]
![[Genesis 17#^v11]]
![[Genesis 17#^v12]]
![[Genesis 17#^v13]]
![[Genesis 17#^v14]]

- Circumcision is the token of the covenant: every man child circumcised at eight days, and the uncircumcised soul cut off from his people.
- Abraham received circumcision as a seal of the righteousness of the faith he had yet being uncircumcised. He believed and obeyed first, and circumcision sealed it; likewise a brother can come into the truth, get built up in the faith, and then go through with the process.
- It still has to be done. A covenant is a contract with terms; the covenant was confirmed to Jacob for a law, and circumcision is one of the laws within the covenant.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Romans 4#^v11|Romans 4:11]]**
    ![[Romans 4#^v11]]
    Circumcision was a seal of the righteousness of the faith Abraham had yet being uncircumcised.
  - **[[Genesis 15#^v6|Genesis 15:6]]**
    ![[Genesis 15#^v6]]
    Abraham was counted righteous before circumcision was implemented; faith and obedience came first.
  - **[[1 Chronicles 16#^v15|1 Chronicles 16:15-17]]**
    ![[1 Chronicles 16#^v15]]
    ![[1 Chronicles 16#^v16]]
    ![[1 Chronicles 16#^v17]]
    Be mindful always of His covenant, confirmed to Jacob for a law; the covenant carries terms.

**[[Genesis 17#^v15|Genesis 17:15-19]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v15]]
![[Genesis 17#^v16]]
![[Genesis 17#^v17]]
![[Genesis 17#^v18]]
![[Genesis 17#^v19]]

- Sarai's name was changed to Sarah, and she shall be a mother of nations; those are the same nations of the covenant, and she and Abraham had one child together, Isaac.
- Abraham fell on his face and laughed in his heart: shall a child be born to a hundred year old man, and Sarah bear at ninety? The Most High read his thought and named the child from it: Isaac means he will laugh.

**[[Genesis 17#^v20|Genesis 17:20-21]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v20]]
![[Genesis 17#^v21]]

- Ishmael received a blessing: fruitful, multiplied exceedingly, twelve princes, a great nation. But the word to Abraham was kings shall come out of thee, and the covenant passes to Isaac: my covenant will I establish with Isaac.
- Not all who are of Abraham's seed are the children; in Isaac shall thy seed be called. Ishmael was born of the flesh, of Sarai's plan, with no word from God ordaining it; the children of the promise are counted for the seed.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Romans 9#^v7|Romans 9:7-9]]**
    ![[Romans 9#^v7]]
    ![[Romans 9#^v8]]
    ![[Romans 9#^v9]]
    In Isaac shall thy seed be called; the children of the flesh are not the children of God, but the children of the promise are counted for the seed.

**[[Genesis 17#^v23|Genesis 17:23-27]]**

![[Genesis 17#^v23]]
![[Genesis 17#^v24]]
![[Genesis 17#^v25]]
![[Genesis 17#^v26]]
![[Genesis 17#^v27]]

- The selfsame day Abraham circumcised every male of his house, himself at ninety and nine and Ishmael at thirteen. That is the righteous example: he received the commandment and performed it the same day.

# Genesis 18: The Promise of Isaac and the Cry of Sodom

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 18]]

**[[Genesis 18#^v1|Genesis 18:1-8]]**

![[Genesis 18#^v1]]
![[Genesis 18#^v2]]
![[Genesis 18#^v3]]
![[Genesis 18#^v4]]
![[Genesis 18#^v5]]
![[Genesis 18#^v6]]
![[Genesis 18#^v7]]
![[Genesis 18#^v8]]

- The three men who stood by Abraham were angels of the Lord, and they had bodies: their feet were washed with water, they rested under the tree, and they did eat. Be careful how you deal with people, for some have entertained angels unawares.
- Abraham hastened to serve them. He commanded his household; he told Sarah, Make ready quickly. That is the level of his hospitality and his leadership.

**[[Genesis 18#^v9|Genesis 18:9-15]]**

![[Genesis 18#^v9]]
![[Genesis 18#^v10]]
![[Genesis 18#^v11]]
![[Genesis 18#^v12]]
![[Genesis 18#^v13]]
![[Genesis 18#^v14]]
![[Genesis 18#^v15]]

- Sarah was barren from the beginning and it had ceased to be with her after the manner of women; she laughed within herself at the promise of a son.
- The Lord answered the thought she never spoke: Wherefore did Sarah laugh? She denied it out of fear, and He said, Nay, but thou didst laugh. The Most High sees what is within the mind.
- Is any thing too hard for the Lord? The barren woman is made a joyful mother of children. Through faith Sarah received strength to conceive when she was past age, and Abraham, as good as dead at a hundred, staggered not at the promise but was strong in faith, fully persuaded that what He had promised He was able to perform.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 11#^v30|Genesis 11:30]]**
    ![[Genesis 11#^v30]]
    Sarai was barren from the beginning; she had no child.
  - **[[Psalms 113#^v9|Psalms 113:9]]**
    ![[Psalms 113#^v9]]
    The Most High makes the barren woman a joyful mother of children.
  - **[[Hebrews 11#^v11|Hebrews 11:11-12]]**
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v11]]
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v12]]
    Through faith Sara received strength to conceive past age, because she judged Him faithful who had promised; of one as good as dead sprang as many as the stars.
  - **[[Romans 4#^v19|Romans 4:19-21]]**
    ![[Romans 4#^v19]]
    ![[Romans 4#^v20]]
    ![[Romans 4#^v21]]
    Abraham considered not his own body now dead nor the deadness of Sarah's womb; he staggered not at the promise through unbelief.

**[[Genesis 18#^v17|Genesis 18:17-19]]**

![[Genesis 18#^v17]]
![[Genesis 18#^v18]]
![[Genesis 18#^v19]]

- The Lord said, I know Abraham, that he will command his children and his household after him. The man is the leader who makes the rules and keeps his house in the way of the Lord.
- What makes a great husband and father is commanding the household in the way of the Lord, to do justice and judgment. Where men walk away from justice and judgment, they cannot properly command their household.

**[[Genesis 18#^v20|Genesis 18:20-21]]**

![[Genesis 18#^v20]]
![[Genesis 18#^v21]]

- The cry of Sodom and Gomorrah was great and their sin very grievous. The iniquity of Sodom was pride, fulness of bread, and abundance of idleness; she strengthened not the hand of the poor and needy, was haughty, and committed abomination.
- The same iniquities fill the land that is spiritually called Sodom today: pride, wealth, idleness from morning to night, and abomination made law.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ezekiel 16#^v49|Ezekiel 16:49-50]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 16#^v49]]
    ![[Ezekiel 16#^v50]]
    The iniquity of Sodom: pride, fulness of bread, abundance of idleness, and abomination committed before the Most High.

**[[Genesis 18#^v23|Genesis 18:23-33]]**

![[Genesis 18#^v23]]
![[Genesis 18#^v24]]
![[Genesis 18#^v25]]
![[Genesis 18#^v26]]
![[Genesis 18#^v27]]
![[Genesis 18#^v28]]
![[Genesis 18#^v29]]
![[Genesis 18#^v30]]
![[Genesis 18#^v31]]
![[Genesis 18#^v32]]
![[Genesis 18#^v33]]

- Abraham drew near and negotiated with the Most High because his nephew Lot dwelt in Sodom; that is why he pleaded for the city.
- Shall not the Judge of all the earth do right? Abraham reasoned from fifty righteous down to forty and five, forty, thirty, twenty, and ten, and the Lord said, I will not destroy it for ten's sake. Below ten there was no more negotiating, and the Lord went His way.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 13#^v12|Genesis 13:12]]**
    ![[Genesis 13#^v12]]
    Lot dwelt in the cities of the plain and pitched toward Sodom; his dwelling there is why Abraham interceded.

# Genesis 19: The Destruction of Sodom

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 19]]

**[[Genesis 19#^v1|Genesis 19:1-3]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v1]]
![[Genesis 19#^v2]]
![[Genesis 19#^v3]]

- Lot sat in the gate and pressed the two angels into his house, because he knew the men of Sodom. He made them a feast, and they did eat; angels have bodies and functions like men.

**[[Genesis 19#^v4|Genesis 19:4-5]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v4]]
![[Genesis 19#^v5]]

- The men of Sodom, old and young, all the people from every quarter, compassed the house and demanded the men, that we may know them. To know in scripture is to lie with: Rebekah was a virgin, neither had any man known her.
- The whole city, both small and great, was given to this wickedness.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 24#^v16|Genesis 24:16]]**
    ![[Genesis 24#^v16]]
    Neither had any man known her proves that to know means intercourse.

**[[Genesis 19#^v6|Genesis 19:6-11]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v6]]
![[Genesis 19#^v7]]
![[Genesis 19#^v8]]
![[Genesis 19#^v9]]
![[Genesis 19#^v10]]
![[Genesis 19#^v11]]

- Lot went out and said, I pray you, brethren, do not so wickedly. This is Genesis, way before [[Leviticus 18|Leviticus 18]]; sodomy was already wickedness and already unlawful before the law was written at Sinai.
- They pressed on Lot to break the door, and the angels smote them with blindness, small and great, so that they wearied themselves to find the door.

**[[Genesis 19#^v12|Genesis 19:12-17]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v12]]
![[Genesis 19#^v13]]
![[Genesis 19#^v14]]
![[Genesis 19#^v15]]
![[Genesis 19#^v16]]
![[Genesis 19#^v17]]

- Lot's sons in law took him as one that mocked, and they perished. When the warning comes, treating it as a joke is death.
- While Lot lingered, the angels laid hold on his hand, the Lord being merciful to him. Then the instruction: Escape for thy life; look not behind thee; escape to the mountain.

**[[Genesis 19#^v18|Genesis 19:18-25]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v18]]
![[Genesis 19#^v19]]
![[Genesis 19#^v20]]
![[Genesis 19#^v21]]
![[Genesis 19#^v22]]
![[Genesis 19#^v23]]
![[Genesis 19#^v24]]
![[Genesis 19#^v25]]

- The angel told Lot, Haste thee, for I cannot do any thing till thou be come thither. The destruction waited on the righteous being brought out, the same way the angels in [[Revelation 7|Revelation 7]] hold the four winds until the servants of God are sealed.
- When Lot entered Zoar, the Lord rained brimstone and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah. Those cities are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire; what was done there on a small scale is done in spiritual Sodom on a greater scale, and the example stands.
- Just Lot was delivered, a righteous man vexed from day to day with the filthy conversation and unlawful deeds of the wicked. A righteous man cannot be at home with that conduct.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Jude 1#^v7|Jude 1:7]]**
    ![[Jude 1#^v7]]
    Sodom and Gomorrah, giving themselves to fornication and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.
  - **[[2 Peter 2#^v6|2 Peter 2:6-8]]**
    ![[2 Peter 2#^v6]]
    ![[2 Peter 2#^v7]]
    ![[2 Peter 2#^v8]]
    The cities were made an example unto those that after should live ungodly, and just Lot was delivered.

**[[Genesis 19#^v26|Genesis 19:26]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v26]]

- Lot's wife looked back, against the instruction, and became a pillar of salt: a standing monument of an unbelieving soul.
- Remember Lot's wife: three words among the weightiest in the scriptures. As it was in the days of Lot, they ate, drank, bought, sold, planted, and builded until the day the fire fell; so shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed. If you find yourself reminiscing after the old life, remember Lot's wife.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Wisdom of Solomon 10#^v6|Wisdom of Solomon 10:6-7]]**
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 10#^v6]]
    ![[Wisdom of Solomon 10#^v7]]
    The standing pillar of salt is a monument of an unbelieving soul; the wasteland that smoketh is a testimony to this day.
  - **[[Luke 17#^v28|Luke 17:28-32]]**
    ![[Luke 17#^v28]]
    ![[Luke 17#^v29]]
    ![[Luke 17#^v30]]
    ![[Luke 17#^v31]]
    ![[Luke 17#^v32]]
    As it was in the days of Lot, so shall it be in the day the Son of man is revealed; remember Lot's wife.

**[[Genesis 19#^v30|Genesis 19:30-38]]**

![[Genesis 19#^v30]]
![[Genesis 19#^v31]]
![[Genesis 19#^v32]]
![[Genesis 19#^v33]]
![[Genesis 19#^v34]]
![[Genesis 19#^v35]]
![[Genesis 19#^v36]]
![[Genesis 19#^v37]]
![[Genesis 19#^v38]]

- Lot's daughters thought Sodom had been the whole world and no man was left on the earth to give them seed, so they made their father drink wine and lay with him; he perceived not when they lay down nor when they arose.
- Do not take any wayward idea from this: uncovering the nakedness of thy father is against the laws of the Most High. It was unlawful then and it is unlawful now.
- The firstborn bare Moab, father of the Moabites, and the younger bare Benammi, father of the Ammonites, unto this day. In this teaching those nations are identified with the peoples known today as the Chinese and the Japanese.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Leviticus 18#^v7|Leviticus 18:7]]**
    ![[Leviticus 18#^v7]]
    The nakedness of thy father and mother thou shalt not uncover; incest is against the law of the Most High.

# Genesis 20: Sarah and Abimelech

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 20]]

**[[Genesis 20#^v1|Genesis 20:1-2]]**

![[Genesis 20#^v1]]
![[Genesis 20#^v2]]

- Abraham said again, She is my sister, as he had done entering Egypt, for fear the men of the place would kill him for his wife's sake; Sarah was still that fair to look upon even at ninety.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 12#^v11|Genesis 12:11-13]]**
    ![[Genesis 12#^v11]]
    ![[Genesis 12#^v12]]
    ![[Genesis 12#^v13]]
    The saying began at Egypt: they will kill me, but save thee alive; say thou art my sister.

**[[Genesis 20#^v3|Genesis 20:3-7]]**

![[Genesis 20#^v3]]
![[Genesis 20#^v4]]
![[Genesis 20#^v5]]
![[Genesis 20#^v6]]
![[Genesis 20#^v7]]

- God came to Abimelech in a dream: Behold, thou art but a dead man, for the woman is a man's wife. Adultery was sin in Genesis, way before [[Exodus 20#^v14|Exodus 20:14]]; the Most High said, I withheld thee from sinning against me.
- Abimelech had acted in the integrity of his heart, and the Most High withheld him from touching her. Restore the man his wife, for he is a prophet, and he shall pray for thee.

**[[Genesis 20#^v9|Genesis 20:9-13]]**

![[Genesis 20#^v9]]
![[Genesis 20#^v10]]
![[Genesis 20#^v11]]
![[Genesis 20#^v12]]
![[Genesis 20#^v13]]

- Abimelech called it what it is: a great sin. Adultery is one of the weightier matters; there is no animal sacrifice that atones for it.
- Abraham thought, Surely the fear of God is not in this place. And technically she was his sister: the daughter of his father but not of his mother, who became his wife; the sister saying was their agreement in every strange land.

**[[Genesis 20#^v17|Genesis 20:17-18]]**

![[Genesis 20#^v17]]
![[Genesis 20#^v18]]

- Behind the scenes the Lord had fast closed up all the wombs of the house of Abimelech because of Sarah; when Abraham prayed, God healed them.
- The same happened with Pharaoh before. The Most High plagues a household over the prophet's wife; He does not play about that.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 12#^v17|Genesis 12:17]]**
    ![[Genesis 12#^v17]]
    The Lord plagued Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai; it happened both times she was taken.
