# Genesis 32: Wrestling Until the Blessing

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 32]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Genesis 32|Genesis 32]] through 35: Jacob's return, the wrestling at Peniel, the meeting with Esau, the matter of Dinah, and the renewing of the name Israel at Bethel.

**[[Genesis 32#^v1|Genesis 32:1-2]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v1]]
![[Genesis 32#^v2]]

- The angels of God met Jacob as he departed from Laban, and he said, This is God's host, and called the place Mahanaim, which means two hosts or camps.
- When the scriptures speak of the Lord of hosts, the hosts are the armies of angels that stand before Him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Esdras 8#^v21|2 Esdras 8:21]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 8#^v21]]
    Before the throne of the Most High the hosts of angels stand with trembling; the host Jacob saw was the angels.

**[[Genesis 32#^v3|Genesis 32:3-7]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v3]]
![[Genesis 32#^v4]]
![[Genesis 32#^v5]]
![[Genesis 32#^v6]]
![[Genesis 32#^v7]]

- Jacob sent messengers to Esau, and they returned saying Esau was coming with four hundred men, and Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed.
- His fear was rightful: the last word he had of Esau was that Esau purposed in his heart to slay him once their father's mourning came. The hatred began at the blessing, and the scriptures show in Ezekiel that the hatred never ended.
- The violent man imagines mischiefs in his heart and is continually gathered together for war; that is what Jacob understood was coming toward him.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 27#^v41|Genesis 27:41-42]]**
    ![[Genesis 27#^v41]]
    ![[Genesis 27#^v42]]
    Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and said in his heart, I will slay my brother Jacob; that was the last report Jacob had.
  - **[[Psalms 140#^v1|Psalms 140:1-2]]**
    ![[Psalms 140#^v1]]
    ![[Psalms 140#^v2]]
    Deliver me from the evil man, preserve me from the violent man, which imagine mischiefs in their heart; Jacob's prayer fit the psalm.

**[[Genesis 32#^v9|Genesis 32:9-12]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v9]]
![[Genesis 32#^v10]]
![[Genesis 32#^v11]]
![[Genesis 32#^v12]]

- Jacob prayed humbly: I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies; deliver me from the hand of my brother. He was not too proud to confess his fear to the Most High.
- He pleaded the covenant: thou saidst, I will make thy seed as the sand of the sea. That is the pattern for our prayers; plead the promise made to the fathers and ask the Most High to remember His covenant.

**[[Genesis 32#^v13|Genesis 32:13-20]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v13]]
![[Genesis 32#^v14]]
![[Genesis 32#^v15]]
![[Genesis 32#^v16]]
![[Genesis 32#^v17]]
![[Genesis 32#^v18]]
![[Genesis 32#^v19]]
![[Genesis 32#^v20]]

- Jacob sent droves of livestock ahead as a present, spaced apart, each servant instructed what to say. He said, I will appease him with the present that goeth before me. He moved strategically to soften Esau before they met face to face.
- What he did was scriptural: a man's gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Proverbs 18#^v16|Proverbs 18:16]]**
    ![[Proverbs 18#^v16]]
    A man's gift maketh room for him; the present went before Jacob to make room.

**[[Genesis 32#^v24|Genesis 32:24-26]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v24]]
![[Genesis 32#^v25]]
![[Genesis 32#^v26]]

- Jacob was left alone, and there wrestled a man with him until the breaking of the day; the man had to touch the hollow of Jacob's thigh and put it out of joint, because Jacob would not be beaten.
- With his thigh out of joint Jacob still held on: I will not let thee go, except thou bless me. That is faith with works. Cleave unto the Most High and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.
- That is the spirit to carry through hardship: fight the good fight of faith and lay hold on eternal life the way Jacob laid hold and would not let go.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 2#^v3|Ecclesiasticus 2:3]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 2#^v3]]
    Cleave unto Him and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end; hold on until the blessing.
  - **[[1 Timothy 6#^v12|1 Timothy 6:12]]**
    ![[1 Timothy 6#^v12]]
    Fight the good fight of faith; lay hold on eternal life as Jacob laid hold until he was blessed.

**[[Genesis 32#^v27|Genesis 32:27-28]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v27]]
![[Genesis 32#^v28]]

- Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with God and with men, and hast prevailed. Israel means a prince that has power with God.
- The God of Israel is He that giveth strength and power unto His people; He is the power source. When Israel went astray and served other gods, the name and the power were taken, and the nations called the people by every other name. Returning to the Most High is how the power returns, not carnal weapons of warfare.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 68#^v35|Psalms 68:35]]**
    ![[Psalms 68#^v35]]
    The God of Israel is He that giveth strength and power unto His people; the power of the name is from Him.

**[[Genesis 32#^v29|Genesis 32:29-30]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v29]]
![[Genesis 32#^v30]]

- The man would not give his name, but he blessed Jacob there, and Jacob called the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved.
- The one Jacob wrestled was an angel: by his strength he had power with God, yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed. The word God, Elohim, is plural, which clears up the passages where men are said to have seen God and yet no man hath seen the Father.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Hosea 12#^v3|Hosea 12:3-4]]**
    ![[Hosea 12#^v3]]
    ![[Hosea 12#^v4]]
    Jacob had power with God; he had power over the angel, and prevailed; the wrestler was an angel.

**[[Genesis 32#^v31|Genesis 32:31-32]]**

![[Genesis 32#^v31]]
![[Genesis 32#^v32]]

- The sun rose upon him and he halted upon his thigh; therefore the children of Israel eat not of the sinew which shrank, which is upon the hollow of the thigh, unto this day.

# Genesis 33: The Meeting With Esau

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 33]]

**[[Genesis 33#^v1|Genesis 33:1-4]]**

![[Genesis 33#^v1]]
![[Genesis 33#^v2]]
![[Genesis 33#^v3]]
![[Genesis 33#^v4]]

- Jacob bowed himself to the ground seven times as he came near, and Esau ran, embraced him, fell on his neck, and kissed him, and they wept.
- That embrace is not proof that the hatred was gone. Jacob had prayed for deliverance, and when a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; the Most High put that peace on Esau in that moment.
- The same prayer belongs to us: pray for all that are in authority, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness, keeping the commandments until Christ returns.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Proverbs 16#^v7|Proverbs 16:7]]**
    ![[Proverbs 16#^v7]]
    When a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; that is what happened with Esau.
  - **[[1 Timothy 2#^v1|1 Timothy 2:1-2]]**
    ![[1 Timothy 2#^v1]]
    ![[1 Timothy 2#^v2]]
    Pray for all in authority, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

**[[Genesis 33#^v5|Genesis 33:5-11]]**

![[Genesis 33#^v5]]
![[Genesis 33#^v6]]
![[Genesis 33#^v7]]
![[Genesis 33#^v8]]
![[Genesis 33#^v9]]
![[Genesis 33#^v10]]
![[Genesis 33#^v11]]

- Jacob presented the children which God graciously gave thy servant, and urged the present on Esau until he took it. Receiving the gift sealed the peace; Jacob wanted the assurance.

**[[Genesis 33#^v12|Genesis 33:12-16]]**

![[Genesis 33#^v12]]
![[Genesis 33#^v13]]
![[Genesis 33#^v14]]
![[Genesis 33#^v15]]

- Esau offered to travel together and to leave some of his four hundred men, and Jacob declined both, pleading the tender children and the young flocks: I will lead on softly. He stayed wise and kept his distance, and Esau returned that day to Seir.

**[[Genesis 33#^v17|Genesis 33:17-20]]**

![[Genesis 33#^v17]]
![[Genesis 33#^v18]]
![[Genesis 33#^v19]]
![[Genesis 33#^v20]]

- Jacob journeyed to Succoth, which means booths, built him an house, and made booths for his cattle; the forefather was industrious.
- At Shalem he bought a parcel of a field for an hundred pieces of money and erected an altar, and called it Elelohe-Israel: God, the God of Israel.

# Genesis 34: Dinah and the Zeal of Simeon and Levi

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 34]]

**[[Genesis 34#^v1|Genesis 34:1-2]]**

![[Genesis 34#^v1]]
![[Genesis 34#^v2]]

- Dinah, Jacob's only daughter, went out to see the daughters of the land without supervision, and Shechem the son of Hamor took her, and lay with her, and defiled her. This was stranger rape.
- The lesson stands for every generation: keep watch over the daughters. Have a care of their body; do not give overmuch liberty; the father wakes for the daughter and the care for her takes away sleep. Everything the children do should have supervision, especially in this age.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 7#^v24|Ecclesiasticus 7:24]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 7#^v24]]
    Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 26#^v10|Ecclesiasticus 26:10]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 26#^v10]]
    If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 42#^v9|Ecclesiasticus 42:9-11]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 42#^v9]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 42#^v10]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 42#^v11]]
    The father waketh for the daughter when no man knoweth; keep a sure watch, lest she be defiled and made a reproach.

**[[Genesis 34#^v3|Genesis 34:3-7]]**

![[Genesis 34#^v3]]
![[Genesis 34#^v4]]
![[Genesis 34#^v5]]
![[Genesis 34#^v6]]
![[Genesis 34#^v7]]

- Shechem's soul clave to Dinah after he defiled her; he did it backwards, taking first and loving after. That is the world's pattern, not righteousness.
- When Jacob's sons heard, the men were grieved and very wroth, because he had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob's daughter, which thing ought not to be done. The laws were in place and kept long before they were written in stone at Sinai.

**[[Genesis 34#^v8|Genesis 34:8-10]]**

![[Genesis 34#^v8]]
![[Genesis 34#^v9]]
![[Genesis 34#^v10]]

- Hamor proposed the exact opposite of the law: make ye marriages with us, give your daughters unto us, and take our daughters unto you, and dwell with us, and become one people. Assimilation and intermarriage is the offer the nations always make, and it is the offer the law forbids.

**[[Genesis 34#^v13|Genesis 34:13-24]]**

![[Genesis 34#^v13]]
![[Genesis 34#^v14]]
![[Genesis 34#^v15]]
![[Genesis 34#^v16]]
![[Genesis 34#^v17]]
![[Genesis 34#^v18]]
![[Genesis 34#^v19]]
![[Genesis 34#^v20]]
![[Genesis 34#^v21]]
![[Genesis 34#^v22]]
![[Genesis 34#^v23]]
![[Genesis 34#^v24]]

- The sons of Jacob answered deceitfully: every male of the city must be circumcised before they would consent. Hamor and Shechem persuaded the whole city, reasoning that the wealth of Jacob's house would become theirs; every male was circumcised.

**[[Genesis 34#^v25|Genesis 34:25-29]]**

![[Genesis 34#^v25]]
![[Genesis 34#^v26]]
![[Genesis 34#^v27]]
![[Genesis 34#^v28]]
![[Genesis 34#^v29]]

- On the third day, when they were sore, two of the sons, Simeon and Levi, took each man his sword, came upon the city boldly, and slew all the males; they took Dinah out, spoiled the city, and carried away the substance and the households.
- Two men do not take a city alone. The sword of Simeon was given of God to take vengeance of the strangers that defiled the maid; they were moved with the zeal of the Most High, and He was behind the judgment.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Judith 9#^v2|Judith 9:2-4]]**
    ![[Judith 9#^v2]]
    ![[Judith 9#^v3]]
    ![[Judith 9#^v4]]
    O Lord God of my father Simeon, to whom thou gavest a sword to take vengeance of the strangers who defiled the maid; the vengeance was ordained of God, and they were moved with His zeal.

**[[Genesis 34#^v30|Genesis 34:30-31]]**

![[Genesis 34#^v30]]
![[Genesis 34#^v31]]

- Jacob was troubled: ye have made me to stink among the inhabitants of the land, and I being few in number. He feared the nations round about gathering against his house.
- The brothers answered, Should he deal with our sister as with an harlot? There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel; the daughters of Israel are not to be handled so, and the sons of Israel did not go for it.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 23#^v17|Deuteronomy 23:17]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 23#^v17]]
    There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel; the answer of Simeon and Levi stood on that law.

# Genesis 35: Bethel and the Twelve Sons of Israel

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 35]]

**[[Genesis 35#^v1|Genesis 35:1-4]]**

![[Genesis 35#^v1]]
![[Genesis 35#^v2]]
![[Genesis 35#^v3]]
![[Genesis 35#^v4]]

- Before going up to Bethel, Jacob told his household, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean. The strange gods were there because Rachel had stolen her father's images and hidden them, sitting on them when Laban searched.
- Idolatry hidden in the household must be put away before you can stand at Bethel. Rachel finally gave up the images, and Jacob hid them under the oak by Shechem.
- From Eve to Rachel the scriptures warn how sin enters through the household; of the woman came the beginning of sin. Sisters, stay in the spirit and guard against the serpent's beguiling as the mothers were beguiled.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 31#^v19|Genesis 31:19]]**
    ![[Genesis 31#^v19]]
    Rachel had stolen the images that were her father's; that is where the strange gods in the household came from.
  - **[[Genesis 31#^v34|Genesis 31:34-35]]**
    ![[Genesis 31#^v34]]
    ![[Genesis 31#^v35]]
    Rachel hid the images in the camel's furniture and sat upon them, and Laban found them not.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 25#^v24|Ecclesiasticus 25:24]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 25#^v24]]
    Of the woman came the beginning of sin; the warning runs from Eve through the mothers.

**[[Genesis 35#^v5|Genesis 35:5]]**

![[Genesis 35#^v5]]

- The terror of God was upon the cities round about, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob. What Jacob feared after Shechem never came, because the angel of the Lord encamps round about them that fear Him and delivers them.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 34#^v7|Psalms 34:7]]**
    ![[Psalms 34#^v7]]
    The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear Him, and delivereth them.

**[[Genesis 35#^v9|Genesis 35:9-11]]**

![[Genesis 35#^v9]]
![[Genesis 35#^v10]]
![[Genesis 35#^v11]]

- God appeared to Jacob again and confirmed it: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name. Be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins.
- The nation and company of nations of Abraham's covenant is Israel, plain and clear, and kings come out of Israel's loins. Christ washed us from our sins and made us kings and priests unto God; the men of Israel are kings in the sight of the Most High.
- Israel once had mighty kings who ruled over countries beyond the river, and tribute and custom were paid unto them. The kings failed when they forsook the law, and their power was given to a strange nation; that is why the kings of the scriptures are not known as black kings today. When the King of kings returns He restores the crowns, and He sets crowns on the heads of the great multitude on mount Sion.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Revelation 1#^v5|Revelation 1:5-6]]**
    ![[Revelation 1#^v5]]
    ![[Revelation 1#^v6]]
    Christ washed us from our sins and hath made us kings and priests unto God.
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 49#^v4|Ecclesiasticus 49:4-5]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 49#^v4]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 49#^v5]]
    All except David, Ezekias, and Josias were defective and forsook the law; therefore their power was given unto others, and their glory to a strange nation.
  - **[[Ezra 4#^v20|Ezra 4:20]]**
    ![[Ezra 4#^v20]]
    There have been mighty kings over Jerusalem which ruled over all countries beyond the river, and tribute and custom was paid unto them.
  - **[[2 Esdras 2#^v42|2 Esdras 2:42-43]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 2#^v42]]
    ![[2 Esdras 2#^v43]]
    A great people whom none could number stood on mount Sion, and one taller than all the rest set crowns upon every one of their heads.

**[[Genesis 35#^v12|Genesis 35:12]]**

![[Genesis 35#^v12]]

- The land given to Abraham and Isaac was promised to Jacob and his seed: the land of Canaan, which is northeast Africa, the land the Most High named when He brought Israel out of Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Leviticus 25#^v38|Leviticus 25:38]]**
    ![[Leviticus 25#^v38]]
    I am the Lord which brought you out of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan; that is the land of the promise.

![[Genesis 35#^v18]]
![[Genesis 35#^v19]]

- Rachel travailed in hard labour and died bearing her second son; she called him Benoni, but his father called him Benjamin, the son of the right hand. He was the twelfth son, and Jacob set a pillar upon Rachel's grave in the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem.

**[[Genesis 35#^v22|Genesis 35:22]]**

![[Genesis 35#^v22]]

- Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine, and Israel heard it. That is fornication such as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father's wife.
- The judgment followed him: forasmuch as he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph, and the genealogy is not reckoned after the birthright. The firstborn lost the birthright over it.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Corinthians 5#^v1|1 Corinthians 5:1]]**

    ![[1 Chronicles 5#^v1]]
    Reuben was the firstborn, but because he defiled his father's bed his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph.
