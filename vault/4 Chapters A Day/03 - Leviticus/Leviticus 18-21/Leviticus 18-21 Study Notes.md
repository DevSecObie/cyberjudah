# Leviticus 18: After the Doings of Egypt and Canaan Shall Ye Not Do

Read the chapter → [[Leviticus 18]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Leviticus 18|Leviticus 18]] through 21: the unlawful beds forbidden after the doings of Egypt and Canaan, the statutes of holiness for everyday life, the judgments appointed for the abominations, and the holiness required of the priests, the sons of Aaron. All four chapters were read and taught.

**[[Leviticus 18#^v1|Leviticus 18:1-3]]**

![[Leviticus 18#^v1]]
![[Leviticus 18#^v2]]
![[Leviticus 18#^v3]]

- In Egypt the children of Israel served the gods of Egypt, dressed like Egyptians, and were fully engulfed in the Egyptian way of life; Moses himself was raised as an Egyptian in the house of Pharaoh. The Most High told them to leave all of that back in Egypt.
- The same word stands for us: when the Most High redeems us out of this captivity, many of the customs we learned here will be cut off, because they are not coming into the kingdom.
- The land of Canaan was full of idols. If Israel obeyed His voice the Most High would be an enemy to their enemies, bless their bread and water, and take sickness away; the charge in Canaan was not to bow to their gods nor do after their works, but to utterly overthrow them and break down their images.
- Coming out of Egypt required a transformed mind: not conformed to the way of life of Egypt or Canaan, but renewed to the commandments of the Most High.

  - **[[Romans 12#^v2|Romans 12:2]]**

# Leviticus 19: Ye Shall Be Holy, for I the Lord Your God Am Holy

![[Leviticus 19#^v1]]
![[Leviticus 19#^v2]]
![[Leviticus 19#^v3]]

- Ye shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy; this word went to all the congregation of the children of Israel.
- Fear every man his mother and his father goes back to the commandment, honour thy father and thy mother.
- Honour can be kept even at a distance: show respect, and do nothing that would bring shame on your mother. But there is a balance; if she asks for what is against the commandments of the Most High, the commandments come first.
- Keep my sabbaths: the feast days are Sabbaths also. The Passover, the first and last days of the feast of Tabernacles, the day of atonement, and the memorial of blowing of trumpets are all Sabbaths; no servile work, no buying and selling, though cooking is permitted on the feast days.

- The corners of the field, the gleanings of the harvest, and the leavings of the vineyard belonged to the poor and the stranger. The Most High is teaching, do not be selfish; love thy neighbour as thyself and look out for the poor of your people.
- He that provides not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, has denied the faith. Those of his own house are his wife and children; his own is his nation. Care for your people.
- Charge them that are rich that they be not highminded, but ready to distribute, willing to communicate. Most of us today have no fields or vineyards, but some have good jobs and blessed incomes; you cannot take it with you, so give of your substance. The scriptures also say no good can come to him that gives no alms.

![[Leviticus 19#^v11]]

- Be honest. Have an honest job so you do not steal; run an honest business so you do not cheat people with foul practices, selling goods you know will fail or charging a customer for repairs he does not need. That honesty is owed even to the other nations.

The only reason a man swears is that he has lied before and no one believes him; in your repentance take swearing out of your speech, and do not put oaths on your own head or your family either.

- If the people of the land hid their eyes from the man and killed him not, the Most High set His face against that man and against his family. When you see wickedness you must utter it and expose it; a witness that holds his peace bears iniquity.
- Sin brings judgment on a whole house. Achan coveted the goodly Babylonish garment, the silver, and the wedge of gold, and he and his sons and his daughters and all that he had were stoned and burned in the valley of Achor. What you do is not just about you; your sin can bring judgment on your family.

# Leviticus 21: The Holiness of the Priests, the Sons of Aaron

Read the chapter → [[Leviticus 21]]

**[[Leviticus 21#^v1|Leviticus 21:1-4]]**

![[Leviticus 21#^v1]]
![[Leviticus 21#^v2]]
![[Leviticus 21#^v3]]
![[Leviticus 21#^v4]]

- The priests, the sons of Aaron, were not to be defiled for the dead among their people, except for near kin: mother, father, son, daughter, brother, and a virgin sister that had no husband.
- The priests had to remain clean to administer the priesthood, for anyone unclean that approached the sanctuary was put to death; a chief man among his people was not to profane himself.

**[[Leviticus 21#^v5|Leviticus 21:5-6]]**

![[Leviticus 21#^v5]]
![[Leviticus 21#^v6]]

- The priests were not to make baldness upon their head, nor shave off the corner of their beard, nor make any cuttings in their flesh; they were to be holy unto their God, for they offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire.
- Christians will read verse 1 and say this chapter speaks only to the priests, so shaving the head and beard has nothing to do with them. But go back to chapter 19: the Lord spake there to all the children of Israel and said the exact same thing, round not the corners of your head, neither mar the corners of thy beard. The law given to the priests here was already the law of the whole nation; do not isolate the priests to escape the commandment.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Leviticus 19#^v1|Leviticus 19:1-2]]**
    ![[Leviticus 19#^v1]]
    ![[Leviticus 19#^v2]]
    The words of chapter 19 went to all the congregation of the children of Israel, not to the priests only.
  - **[[Leviticus 19#^v27|Leviticus 19:27]]**
    ![[Leviticus 19#^v27]]
    Round not the corners of your head, neither mar the corners of thy beard; the same law binds every man of Israel.

**[[Leviticus 21#^v7|Leviticus 21:7-9]]**

![[Leviticus 21#^v9]]

- The priests could not take a wife that was a whore, or profane, or put away from her husband; they were held to an even higher standard, for the priest is holy unto his God.
- The daughter of any priest that profaned herself by playing the whore profaned her father and was burnt with fire. Under the law it was straight death, and we are all guilty of these things; this is why Christ had to come. Thank the Most High for the blood of Christ, that we can repent, correct ourselves, and keep moving, for a just man falleth and riseth up again.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Proverbs 24#^v16|Proverbs 24:16]]**
    ![[Proverbs 24#^v16]]
    A just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again; under Christ there is room to repent and stand back up.

- The high priest, upon whose head the anointing oil was poured, was not to uncover his head nor rend his clothes, nor go in to any dead body, nor go out of the sanctuary; the crown of the anointing oil of his God was upon him. Under the old covenant his head was covered with the mitre.
- When Christ died the veil of the temple was rent and the priesthood was changed; the high priest now is Christ, for it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda. The sons of Levi no longer carry that office; Judah carries this work to the kingdom.

- A blemish is a disability or birth defect: blind, lame in his walk, a flat nose, any thing superfluous, brokenfooted, brokenhanded, crookbackt, a dwarf, a blemish in the eye, scurvy, scabbed, or stones broken. No man of the seed of Aaron with a blemish could come nigh to offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire.
- Yet the blemished priest still ate the bread of his God, both of the most holy and of the holy; he was provided for, only he went not in unto the vail nor came nigh unto the altar, that the sanctuaries be not profaned.
