# Ezekiel 47: The Waters Issuing from the House

Read the chapter → [[Ezekiel 47]]

This session covers [[Ezekiel 47|Ezekiel 47]] and 48. Ezekiel opens with a similitude of waters issuing from the house and rising higher and higher, a picture of the word of the Most High going out to the four corners of the earth; it closes with the land divided among the tribes and the gates of the city named after the children of Israel. The prophet speaks by similitude, proverbs, and dark sayings, so what is written here is spiritual, not a literal structure.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v1|Ezekiel 47:1-2]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v1]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v2]]
- Ezekiel speaks by similitude and dark sayings, so the house here is not a literal building. This is the spiritual house, which is the house of Israel.
- The waters that issue out from under the threshold are the word of the Most High. Just as a tub fills when the water is left running, the word is now being issued out to the four corners of the earth.
  - **[[2 Timothy 2#^v20|2 Timothy 2:20]]**
    ![[2 Timothy 2#^v20]]
    In a great house there are vessels of gold, silver, wood, and earth; that house is the house of Israel.
  - **[[1 Peter 2#^v5|1 Peter 2:5]]**
    ![[1 Peter 2#^v5]]
    We as lively stones are built up a spiritual house; this spiritual house represents Israel.
  - **[[Ephesians 5#^v26|Ephesians 5:26]]**
    ![[Ephesians 5#^v26]]
    He sanctifies and cleanses it with the washing of water by the word; the water here represents the word.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 32#^v1|Deuteronomy 32:1-3]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v1]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v2]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v3]]
    My doctrine shall drop as the rain; the doctrine of the Most High is the water now distilling to every corner like dew.
  - **[[Proverbs 4#^v2|Proverbs 4:2]]**
    ![[Proverbs 4#^v2]]
    For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law; the doctrines are the laws.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v3|Ezekiel 47:3-5]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v3]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v4]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v5]]
- The man with the measuring line brings the prophet through the waters, and they rise from the ankles, to the knees, to the loins, until it is a river that cannot be passed over, waters to swim in.
- The rising water is the word being preached and the understanding increasing as it goes to the four corners of the earth: people coming to understand that Christ is a black man, that we are the Israelites, and that we must keep the commandments.
- The understanding rises in stages, just as understanding came out over time in the classes, the head covering out of First Corinthians 11, the sabbath, and the new moon being the full moon around 2006 to 2007. As long as we stay in the spirit more revelation will keep coming.
- The measuring line measures us up to the stature of Christ, the understanding of the laws and the doctrines.
  - **[[Ephesians 4#^v11|Ephesians 4:11-13]]**
    ![[Ephesians 4#^v11]]
    ![[Ephesians 4#^v12]]
    ![[Ephesians 4#^v13]]
    He gave apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers, till we all come unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v6|Ezekiel 47:6-7]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v6]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v7]]
- The Most High asks, son of man, hast thou seen this, and shows the prophet very many trees on both banks of the river.
- Because we now understand who we are and can identify our forefathers in this Bible, it means Elijah has already come and gone.
  - **[[Malachi 4#^v5|Malachi 4:5-6]]**
    ![[Malachi 4#^v5]]
    ![[Malachi 4#^v6]]
    I will send you Elijah the prophet, and he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children before the great and dreadful day.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v8|Ezekiel 47:8-9]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v8]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v9]]
- These waters go toward the east country, down into the desert, and into the sea, and the waters are healed so that every thing liveth whithersoever the river cometh.
- We receive life through the commandments.
  - **[[Proverbs 7#^v2|Proverbs 7:2]]**
    ![[Proverbs 7#^v2]]
    Keep my commandments, and live; life comes through keeping the law.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v10|Ezekiel 47:10]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v10]]
- The fishers stand upon it from Engedi to Eneglaim, and the fish are according to their kinds, exceeding many. This is Israel in the last days coming alive again and growing into an exceeding great army.
  - **[[Matthew 4#^v19|Matthew 4:19]]**
    ![[Matthew 4#^v19]]
    Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men; the fishers point to the same last-day gathering of Israel.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v11|Ezekiel 47:11]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v11]]
- The miry and marshy places are not healed; they are given over to salt.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v12|Ezekiel 47:12]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v12]]
- By the river grow trees for meat whose leaf shall not fade, bringing forth new fruit according to their months, because their waters issued out of the sanctuary.
- The tree represents Israel, and once the word spreads we know how to bring forth fruit.
  - **[[Matthew 3#^v8|Matthew 3:8]]**
    ![[Matthew 3#^v8]]
    Bring forth fruits meet for repentance, for the axe is laid to the root of every tree that does not.

- The land falls to Israel for an inheritance, each one receiving his portion as well as another.

**[[Ezekiel 47#^v21|Ezekiel 47:21-23]]**
![[Ezekiel 47#^v21]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v22]]
![[Ezekiel 47#^v23]]
- The land is divided by lot for an inheritance, and the strangers that sojourn among you and beget children are counted as born in the country and given inheritance in the tribe where they sojourn.
- The stranger here is not another nation. A stranger is an Israelite living out of his own tribe's land; if you are of Judah and you sojourn in Benjamin, you are counted a stranger. Christianity uses this verse to claim the other nations dwell among Israel and receive the land, but that is not what is being said.
  - **[[Obadiah 1#^v12|Obadiah 1:12]]**
    ![[Obadiah 1#^v12]]

    ![[1 Esdras 5#^v7]]
    Israel dwelt as strangers away from their own land, showing what a stranger is.

# Ezekiel 48: The Land Divided and the Gates of the Tribes

Read the chapter → [[Ezekiel 48]]

**[[Ezekiel 48#^v1|Ezekiel 48:1]]**
![[Ezekiel 48#^v1]]
- From the north end the tribes are named, and the first portion by its border is for Dan; each tribe receives a bordered portion of land.
  - **[[Deuteronomy 19#^v14|Deuteronomy 19:14]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 19#^v14]]
    Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour's landmark; this is why each tribe's inheritance is marked off by a border.

![[Ezekiel 48#^v2]]

![[Ezekiel 48#^v4]]

![[Ezekiel 48#^v7]]
- From the east side to the west side the portions are given in order: Asher, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, Reuben, and Judah, each by the border of the one before.

- A holy oblation is set apart, with the sanctuary in the midst of it, for the priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge and went not astray when the children of Israel and the Levites went astray.
- This portion is holy unto the Most High; it is not to be sold, exchanged, nor alienated, being the firstfruits of the land.

- Those who serve the city are drawn out of all the tribes of Israel, and the increase of the land serves as food for them.

- This is the land which is divided by lot unto the tribes of Israel for an inheritance.

- The goings out of the city are measured, and the gates of the city are named after the tribes of Israel, three gates on each side, round about eighteen thousand measures.
- This ties directly into prophecy: the gates into the inheritance carry the names of the twelve tribes only. There is no gate for the Edomite, no gate for the East Indian, because this land was given to our fathers and belongs to us. The name of the city from that day is, The Most High is there.
