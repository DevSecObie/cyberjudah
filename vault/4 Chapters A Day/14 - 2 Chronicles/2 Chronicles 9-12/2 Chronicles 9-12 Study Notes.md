# 2 Chronicles 9: The Queen of Sheba and the Glory of Solomon

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 9]]

This session moves through [[2 Chronicles 9|2 Chronicles 9]] to 12, a compressed history that gathers together events told at length in 1 and 2 Samuel and 1 and 2 Kings. It covers the visit of the Queen of Sheba to Solomon, the wealth, fame, and reign of Solomon, and then the death of Solomon and the reign of his son Rehoboam. Under Rehoboam the nation splits, Judah and the northern tribes divide, and the Most High brings correction on those who forsake his law. Read the parallel chapters in 1 and 2 Kings to see the full order of events.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v1|2 Chronicles 9:1-2]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v1]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v2]]
- The Queen of Sheba hears of the fame of Solomon in her own land and travels to Jerusalem to prove him, meaning to test him, with hard questions from what she has heard.
- She comes with a great company, camels bearing spices, gold in abundance, and precious stones, and she opens all that is in her heart to him.
- Solomon answers every question she brings; nothing is hidden from him that he cannot explain to her.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v3|2 Chronicles 9:3-4]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v3]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v4]]
- When she sees the wisdom of Solomon, the house he built, the food on his table, the order of his servants and their apparel, and his ascent into the house of the Lord, there is no more spirit left in her.
- She is overcome by the beauty and order of the kingdom that Solomon has established in Jerusalem.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v5|2 Chronicles 9:5-6]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v5]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v6]]
- She confesses that the report she heard in her own land of his acts and wisdom was true.
- She admits she did not believe the words until she came and saw it with her own eyes, and now she declares that not even half the greatness of his wisdom was told to her; he exceeds the fame that reached her.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v7|2 Chronicles 9:7-8]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v7]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v8]]
- She calls his men and servants happy because they stand continually before him and hear his wisdom.
- She blesses the Lord who delighted in Solomon and set him on the throne, and she states plainly that it is because the Most High loved Israel and would establish them forever that he made Solomon king to do judgment and justice.
- The wisdom and glory of the king trace back to the God of Israel, not to Solomon alone.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v9|2 Chronicles 9:9-12]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v9]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v10]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v11]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v12]]
- The queen gives Solomon 120 talents of gold, spices in great abundance, and precious stones; there was never such spice as she brought.
- The servants of Huram and Solomon bring gold from Ophir along with algum trees and precious stones, and the king makes terraces for the house of the Lord and the palace, and harps and psalteries for singers.
- Solomon gives the queen all she desires beyond what she brought, and she returns to her own land.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v13|2 Chronicles 9:13-16]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v13]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v14]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v15]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v16]]
- The weight of gold coming to Solomon in one year is six hundred sixty and six talents, beyond what merchants and the kings of Arabia and the governors bring.
- Solomon makes two hundred targets and three hundred shields of beaten gold and puts them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.
  - **[[1 Kings 10#^v14|1 Kings 10:14]]**
    ![[1 Kings 10#^v14]]
    The same yearly weight of gold coming to Solomon is recorded, showing this is the compressed retelling of the fuller history.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v17|2 Chronicles 9:17-21]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v17]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v18]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v19]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v20]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v21]]
- The king makes a great throne of ivory overlaid with pure gold, with six steps, a footstool of gold, stays on each side, and lions standing by the stays and on the steps; there was none like it in any kingdom.
- All his drinking vessels and the vessels of the house of the forest of Lebanon are pure gold, none of silver, because silver was not counted for anything in his days.
- His ships go to Tarshish with the servants of Huram and return every three years bringing gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks; consider the amount gathered by a crew laboring three years for one voyage.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v22|2 Chronicles 9:22-24]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v22]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v23]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v24]]
- Solomon surpasses all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom, and all of them seek his presence to hear the wisdom the Most High put in his heart.
- This is a testimony of what the Most High does for his people when they walk according to his law, statutes, and commandments.
- Every man brings his present year by year, vessels of silver and gold, garments, armor, spices, horses, and mules.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v25|2 Chronicles 9:25-28]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v25]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v26]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v27]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v28]]
- Solomon has four thousand stalls for horses and chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, stationed in the chariot cities and at Jerusalem.
- He reigns over all the kings from the river to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt, and he makes silver as plentiful as stones in Jerusalem.
- Horses are brought to him out of Egypt and out of all lands.

**[[2 Chronicles 9#^v29|2 Chronicles 9:29-31]]**
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v29]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v30]]
![[2 Chronicles 9#^v31]]
- The rest of the acts of Solomon are written in the book of Nathan the prophet, the prophecy of Ahijah the Shilonite, and the visions of Iddo the seer against Jeroboam the son of Nebat.
- Solomon reigns in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years, then sleeps with his fathers and is buried in the city of David, and his son Rehoboam reigns in his place.
- After all these great works Solomon dies, and the account turns to his son who is raised up next.

# 2 Chronicles 10: Rehoboam Rejects the Elders and the Kingdom Divides

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 10]]

**[[2 Chronicles 10#^v1|2 Chronicles 10:1-4]]**
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v1]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v2]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v3]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v4]]
- Rehoboam goes to Shechem where all Israel has gathered to make him king, and Jeroboam the son of Nebat returns out of Egypt where he had fled from Solomon.
- The people, led by Jeroboam, ask Rehoboam to ease the grievous yoke and heavy service his father laid on them, promising then to serve him.
- Rehoboam tells them to come again after three days.

**[[2 Chronicles 10#^v5|2 Chronicles 10:5-7]]**
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v5]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v6]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v7]]
- Rehoboam first takes counsel with the old men who stood before Solomon while his father lived, asking what answer to return.
- They advise him that if he is kind to the people, pleases them, and speaks good words, they will be his servants forever.
- This is the wise counsel he should have followed, coming from the elders who had experience in place before him.

**[[2 Chronicles 10#^v8|2 Chronicles 10:8-11]]**
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v8]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v9]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v10]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v11]]
- Rehoboam forsakes the counsel of the old men and turns to the young men brought up with him, including them as if they were rulers with him over the nation.
- This is the same error seen constantly among the people: one comes to the truth and seeks counsel from a new brother or sister rather than from the elder men and women already established in the congregation.
- The young men advise him to answer roughly, saying his little finger will be thicker than his father's loins, that he will add to their yoke, and chastise them with scorpions instead of whips.
  - **[[Proverbs 11#^v14|Proverbs 11:14]]**
    ![[Proverbs 11#^v14]]
    Where no counsel is, the people fall, but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety; Rehoboam rejects the safe counsel of the elders.

**[[2 Chronicles 10#^v12|2 Chronicles 10:12-15]]**
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v12]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v13]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v14]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v15]]
- Jeroboam and the people return on the third day, and the king answers them roughly, forsaking the counsel of the old men and following the young men.
- He tells them he will add to their heavy yoke and chastise them with scorpions.
- The king does not listen to the people, for the cause was of the Most High, that the Lord might fulfill the word spoken by Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam.
  - **[[1 Kings 11#^v9|1 Kings 11:9-11]]**
    ![[1 Kings 11#^v9]]
    ![[1 Kings 11#^v10]]
    ![[1 Kings 11#^v11]]
    The Lord, angry that Solomon's heart turned to other gods, declares he will rend the kingdom from him and give it to his servant; this is the prophecy now being fulfilled.

**[[2 Chronicles 10#^v16|2 Chronicles 10:16-19]]**
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v16]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v17]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v18]]
![[2 Chronicles 10#^v19]]
- When all Israel sees the king will not listen, they answer that they have no portion in David nor inheritance in the son of Jesse, and every man departs to his tent.
- Rehoboam still reigns over the children of Israel who dwell in the cities of Judah, but when he sends Hadoram over the tribute, Israel stones him to death.
- Rehoboam flees by chariot to Jerusalem, and Israel rebels against the house of David to this day; the same division between the kingdoms persists.

# 2 Chronicles 11: Rehoboam Strengthens Judah and the Levites Return

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 11]]

**[[2 Chronicles 11#^v1|2 Chronicles 11:1-4]]**
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v1]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v2]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v3]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v4]]
- When Rehoboam comes to Jerusalem he gathers a hundred and eighty thousand chosen warriors of Judah and Benjamin to fight against Israel and bring the kingdom back to himself.
- The word of the Lord comes to Shemaiah the man of God, telling Rehoboam and all Judah and Benjamin not to go up or fight against their brethren, for this thing is done of the Most High.
- They obey the word of the Lord and return from going against Jeroboam, so the war between north and south does not happen.

**[[2 Chronicles 11#^v5|2 Chronicles 11:5-12]]**
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v5]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v6]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v7]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v8]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v9]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v10]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v11]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v12]]
- Rehoboam dwells in Jerusalem and builds cities for defense in Judah, including Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa, Bethzur, Hebron, and others.
- He fortifies the strongholds, sets captains in them, and stores food, oil, and wine behind the walls he has built.
- He puts shields and spears in every city and makes them exceeding strong, having Judah and Benjamin on his side.

**[[2 Chronicles 11#^v13|2 Chronicles 11:13-17]]**
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v13]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v14]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v15]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v16]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v17]]
- The priests and Levites out of all Israel resort to Rehoboam, leaving their suburbs and possessions to come to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam cast them off from the priest's office.
- Jeroboam had ordained his own priests for the high places, the devils, and the calves he made, so Levi had to return where they could serve.
- Those out of all the tribes who set their hearts to seek the Lord God of Israel come to Jerusalem to sacrifice, and for three years they strengthen the kingdom of Judah and walk in the way of David and Solomon.
  - **[[1 Kings 12#^v28|1 Kings 12:28-30]]**
    ![[1 Kings 12#^v28]]
    ![[1 Kings 12#^v29]]
    ![[1 Kings 12#^v30]]
    Jeroboam makes two calves of gold and tells Israel these are their gods, driving out true worship and forcing the faithful to seek the Lord in Jerusalem.

**[[2 Chronicles 11#^v18|2 Chronicles 11:18-23]]**
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v18]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v19]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v20]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v21]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v22]]
![[2 Chronicles 11#^v23]]
- Rehoboam takes Mahalath and Abihail as wives who bear him children, then takes Maachah the daughter of Absalom, whom he loves above all his wives and concubines.
- He takes eighteen wives and threescore concubines and begets twenty and eight sons and threescore daughters.
- He makes Abijah the son of Maachah chief and ruler among his brethren, intending to make him king, and he deals wisely by dispersing his children through the countries of Judah and Benjamin with abundant provision.

# 2 Chronicles 12: Rehoboam Forsakes the Law and Is Humbled

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 12]]

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v1|2 Chronicles 12:1]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v1]]
- When Rehoboam establishes the kingdom and strengthens himself, he forsakes the law of the Lord, and all Israel with him.
- One who leads goes astray and carries many others with him; this is why the people must be examples to one another in righteousness, of one mind and one spirit in the rebuilding of the kingdom.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v2|2 Chronicles 12:2-4]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v2]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v3]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v4]]
- In the fifth year of Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt comes up against Jerusalem because Rehoboam transgressed against the Lord.
- Shishak comes with twelve hundred chariots, threescore thousand horsemen, and people without number out of Egypt, the Lubims, the Sukkiims, and the Ethiopians.
- He takes the fenced cities of Judah and comes to Jerusalem; the invasion is the direct result of forsaking the law.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v5|2 Chronicles 12:5-6]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v5]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v6]]
- Shemaiah the prophet comes to Rehoboam and the princes of Judah gathered at Jerusalem and declares the word of the Lord: because they have forsaken him, he has left them in the hand of Shishak.
- The princes and the king humble themselves and say the Lord is righteous.
- When correction comes, the right response is to confess that the Most High is righteous in what he does, acknowledging the punishment as just.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v7|2 Chronicles 12:7-8]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v7]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v8]]
- When the Lord sees that they humble themselves, his word comes to Shemaiah saying he will not destroy them but grant them some deliverance, and his wrath will not be poured on Jerusalem by the hand of Shishak.
- Nevertheless they will be Shishak's servants, that they may know the difference between serving the Most High and serving the kingdoms of the countries.
- The people today must likewise humble themselves, relearn to obey the commandments, and accept their condition as righteous judgment from the hand of the Most High.
  - **[[Leviticus 26#^v40|Leviticus 26:40-42]]**
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v40]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v41]]
    ![[Leviticus 26#^v42]]
    If they confess their iniquity and their uncircumcised hearts are humbled and they accept the punishment, then the Most High remembers his covenant; humbling brings deliverance.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v9|2 Chronicles 12:9-11]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v9]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v10]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v11]]
- Shishak takes away the treasures of the house of the Lord and of the king's house, carrying off all the shields of gold Solomon had made.
- Rehoboam makes shields of brass in their place and commits them to the guard that keeps the entrance of the king's house.
- Whenever the king enters the house of the Lord the guard fetches the shields and returns them to the guard chamber.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v12|2 Chronicles 12:12]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v12]]
- Because Rehoboam humbles himself, the wrath of the Lord turns from him so that he is not utterly destroyed, and in Judah things go well.
- Deliverance and the destruction of enemies come to the people when they humble themselves before the Most High.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v13|2 Chronicles 12:13-14]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v13]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v14]]
- Rehoboam strengthens himself in Jerusalem and reigns; he is forty and one years old when he begins to reign and reigns seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the Lord chose to put his name there.
- His mother's name is Naamah an Ammonitess.
- He does evil because he prepares not his heart to seek the Lord; the failure is a heart never set on the Most High.

**[[2 Chronicles 12#^v15|2 Chronicles 12:15-16]]**
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v15]]
![[2 Chronicles 12#^v16]]
- The acts of Rehoboam are written in the book of Shemaiah the prophet and of Iddo the seer concerning genealogies, and there were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.
- Rehoboam sleeps with his fathers, is buried in the city of David, and his son Abijah reigns in his place.
- The account then turns to Abijah's rule over Judah in the next chapter, continuing this compressed history that is told more fully in 1 and 2 Kings.
