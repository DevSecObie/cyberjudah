# 2 Chronicles 17: Jehoshaphat Sends Teachers Through the Cities

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 17]]

This session opens the reign of Jehoshaphat, who strengthened himself, walked in the first ways of his father David, and sent Levites and princes out through the cities of Judah with the book of the Law to teach the people. The pattern is the same one we keep today: wisdom cries out in the streets, the fear of the Most High falls on the nations round about, and wisdom proves better than weapons of war. The teaching moves into chapter 18, where Jehoshaphat joins with Ahab and calls for a true prophet of the Most High rather than the four hundred who only tell the king what he wants to hear.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v1|2 Chronicles 17:1]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v1]]

- Jehoshaphat reigned in the place of his father Asa, taking the throne after him.
- He strengthened himself against Israel, setting himself apart from the northern kingdom's ways.
  - **[[1 Kings 15#^v24|1 Kings 15:24]]**
    ![[1 Kings 15#^v24]]
    Asa slept with his fathers and Jehoshaphat his son reigned in his stead.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v2|2 Chronicles 17:2]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v2]]

- He placed forces and garrisons in the fenced cities of Judah and in the cities of Ephraim that Asa had taken.
- A diligent king secures the land and prepares before trouble comes.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v3|2 Chronicles 17:3]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v3]]

- The Most High was with Jehoshaphat because he walked in the first ways of his father David and sought not unto Baal.
- David was a man after the Most High's own heart because he kept the commandments and charged Solomon to keep them.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v4|2 Chronicles 17:4]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v4]]

- Jehoshaphat sought the Lord God of his fathers and walked in his commandments, not after the doings of Israel.
- The way we must walk is defined by the ordinances and laws we are taught.
  - **[[Exodus 18#^v20|Exodus 18:20]]**
    ![[Exodus 18#^v20]]
    Teach them the ordinances and laws and show them the way wherein they must walk.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v5|2 Chronicles 17:5]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v5]]

- Because he obeyed, the Most High established the kingdom in his hand.
- All Judah brought presents, and he had riches, honor, and abundance.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v6|2 Chronicles 17:6]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v6]]

- His heart was lifted up in the ways of the Lord, not in pride but in devotion.
- He removed the high places and the groves out of Judah, tearing down false worship.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v7|2 Chronicles 17:7-9]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v7]]
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v8]]
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v9]]

- In the third year of his reign he sent princes and Levites to teach in the cities of Judah, and they carried the book of the Law of the Lord with them.
- They went throughout all the cities and taught the people, the same pattern we follow, going out into the streets to teach.
  - **[[Luke 14#^v23|Luke 14:23]]**
    ![[Luke 14#^v23]]
    Go out into the highways and hedges and compel them to come in.
  - **[[Proverbs 8#^v1|Proverbs 8:1]]**
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v1]]
    Doth not wisdom cry and understanding put forth her voice.
  - **[[Proverbs 8#^v2|Proverbs 8:2-3]]**
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v2]]
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v3]]
    She standeth in the high places, at the gates, at the entry of the city.
  - **[[Proverbs 8#^v4|Proverbs 8:4]]**
    ![[Proverbs 8#^v4]]
    Wisdom's call is unto men and her voice to the sons of men, a rally cry for men.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v10|2 Chronicles 17:10]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v10]]

- The fear of the Lord fell upon all the kingdoms round about Judah so that they made no war against Jehoshaphat.
- Teaching the Law had a real effect on the nations; wisdom disarmed his enemies.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 7#^v19|Ecclesiastes 7:19]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 7#^v19]]
    Wisdom strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty men in the city.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 9#^v18|Ecclesiastes 9:18]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 9#^v18]]
    Wisdom is better than weapons of war, but one sinner destroyeth much good.
  - **[[Ecclesiastes 9#^v14|Ecclesiastes 9:14-15]]**
    ![[Ecclesiastes 9#^v14]]
    ![[Ecclesiastes 9#^v15]]
    A poor wise man by his wisdom delivered a little city besieged by a great king.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v11|2 Chronicles 17:11]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v11]]

- Some of the Philistines brought presents and tribute silver, and the Arabians brought him flocks of rams and he goats.
- His obedience brought increase even from surrounding peoples.

**[[2 Chronicles 17#^v12|2 Chronicles 17:12-13]]**
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v12]]
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v13]]

- Jehoshaphat waxed great exceedingly and built castles and cities of store in Judah.
- Cities of store are cities of provision, like Egypt's storehouses under Joseph in the years of plenty; in captivity our people had neither building nor closet, but the diligent tend to plenty.

![[2 Chronicles 17#^v14]]
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v15]]
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v16]]

![[2 Chronicles 17#^v18]]
![[2 Chronicles 17#^v19]]

- He had much business in the cities of Judah, and mighty men of valor were mustered in Jerusalem by the house of their fathers.
- The captains numbered hundreds of thousands of ready men, prepared for war, waiting on the king beside those set in the fenced cities.

# 2 Chronicles 18: Jehoshaphat, Ahab, and the True Prophet

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 18]]

**[[2 Chronicles 18#^v1|2 Chronicles 18:1-3]]**
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v1]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v2]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v3]]

- Jehoshaphat had riches and honor and abundance, and joined affinity with Ahab through marriage.
- Ahab feasted him and persuaded him to go up to Ramoth Gilead, and Jehoshaphat answered, I am as thou art, my people as thy people, we will be with thee in the war.
- His bond with Ahab was a joining together, like the spirit that witnesses with the spirit: what is mine is yours.

**[[2 Chronicles 18#^v4|2 Chronicles 18:4-5]]**
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v4]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v5]]

- Jehoshaphat asked to inquire at the word of the Lord before going to battle.
- The king of Israel gathered four hundred prophets who all said go up and prosper, telling the king only what he wanted to hear.

**[[2 Chronicles 18#^v6|2 Chronicles 18:6-7]]**
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v6]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v7]]

- Jehoshaphat asked if there was not still a prophet of the Lord to inquire of.
- Ahab said there was one man, Micaiah, but he hated him because he never prophesied good, only evil, meaning he told the truth.
  - **[[Isaiah 30#^v9|Isaiah 30:9-11]]**
    ![[Isaiah 30#^v9]]
    ![[Isaiah 30#^v10]]
    ![[Isaiah 30#^v11]]
    A rebellious people who say to the seers, prophesy smooth things, cause the Holy One to cease.
  - **[[2 Timothy 4#^v2|2 Timothy 4:2-4]]**
    ![[2 Timothy 4#^v2]]
    ![[2 Timothy 4#^v3]]
    ![[2 Timothy 4#^v4]]
    Preach the word in season and out; the time comes when men will not endure sound doctrine.

**[[2 Chronicles 18#^v8|2 Chronicles 18:8-11]]**
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v8]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v9]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v10]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v11]]

- The king called for Micaiah while the two kings sat in their robes at the gate of Samaria.
- Zedekiah made horns of iron and all the prophets prophesied victory, making a tangible sign to push their false word.

**[[2 Chronicles 18#^v12|2 Chronicles 18:12-13]]**
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v12]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v13]]

- The messenger urged Micaiah to speak good like the other prophets, with one assent.
- Micaiah answered, as the Lord liveth, even what my God saith, that will I speak.
  - **[[Isaiah 8#^v20|Isaiah 8:20]]**
    ![[Isaiah 8#^v20]]
    If they speak not according to this word it is because there is no light in them.

**[[2 Chronicles 18#^v14|2 Chronicles 18:14-16]]**
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v14]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v15]]
![[2 Chronicles 18#^v16]]

- Micaiah first answered sarcastically, go up and prosper, until the king adjured him to speak nothing but the truth in the name of the Lord.
- Then he told the real word: he saw all Israel scattered upon the mountains as sheep with no shepherd, and the Lord said these have no master, let them return to their houses in peace.

# 2 Chronicles 19: Jehoshaphat Sets Judges in the Land

Read the chapter → [[2 Chronicles 19]]

The remainder of this session (2 Chronicles 19-20) was not reached in the "part 1" recording. The full King James text is included here so the chapters are complete for reading and study.

**[[2 Chronicles 19|2 Chronicles 19]]**

*1 And Jehoshaphat the king of Judah returned to his house in peace to Jerusalem. 2 And Jehu the son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him, and said to king Jehoshaphat, Shouldest thou help the ungodly, and love them that hate the LORD? therefore is wrath upon thee from before the LORD. 3 Nevertheless there are good things found in thee, in that thou hast taken away the groves out of the land, and hast prepared thine heart to seek God. 4 And Jehoshaphat dwelt at Jerusalem: and he went out again through the people from Beer-sheba to mount Ephraim, and brought them back unto the LORD God of their fathers. 5 And he set judges in the land throughout all the fenced cities of Judah, city by city, 6 And said to the judges, Take heed what ye do: for ye judge not for man, but for the LORD, who is with you in the judgment. 7 Wherefore now let the fear of the LORD be upon you; take heed and do it: for there is no iniquity with the LORD our God, nor respect of persons, nor taking of gifts. 8 Moreover in Jerusalem did Jehoshaphat set of the Levites, and of the priests, and of the chief of the fathers of Israel, for the judgment of the LORD, and for controversies, when they returned to Jerusalem. 9 And he charged them, saying, Thus shall ye do in the fear of the LORD, faithfully, and with a perfect heart. 10 And what cause soever shall come to you of your brethren that dwell in their cities, between blood and blood, between law and commandment, statutes and judgments, ye shall even warn them that they trespass not against the LORD, and so wrath come upon you, and upon your brethren: this do, and ye shall not trespass. 11 And, behold, Amariah the chief priest is over you in all matters of the LORD; and Zebadiah the son of Ishmael, the ruler of the house of Judah, for all the king’s matters: also the Levites shall be officers before you. Deal courageously, and the LORD shall be with the good.*
