# Daniel 11: The Kings of the North and the South

Read the chapter → [[Daniel 11]]

This session covers [[Daniel 11|Daniel 11]] and 12. [[Daniel 11|Daniel 11]] lays out the prophecy given after the fall of Persia and the rise of Greece, following the long war between the king of the south and the king of the north, the Ptolemies and the Seleucids, down to Antiochus who came up against the holy covenant. [[Daniel 12|Daniel 12]] closes the book with Michael standing up in the time of trouble, the resurrection, and the sealed words that are now being opened in the last days. The books of the Maccabees fill in the history of this same period and are read alongside the prophecy.

**[[Daniel 11#^v1|Daniel 11:1]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v1]]
- In the first year of Darius the Mede, the messenger stood to confirm and to strengthen him.

**[[Daniel 11#^v2|Daniel 11:2]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v2]]
- Three more kings would stand up in Persia: Cyrus, Cambyses, and Darius.
- The fourth would be far richer than they all. This is Xerxes, who profited from all that the kingdom had already gained, and by his strength through his riches he stirred up all against the realm of Grecia.
- This is where they gathered all the heathen together and formed the Greeks. They conquered the original Greeks and named themselves the Greeks, and all the nations, even some of my own tribes, came together and were called the Greeks.

**[[Daniel 11#^v3|Daniel 11:3-4]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v3]]
![[Daniel 11#^v4]]
- The mighty king that stands up and rules with great dominion, doing according to his will, is Alexander the Greek. When a nation is a superpower it does what it will and nobody can stop it, the same as America does today.
- When he stood up his kingdom was broken, because he eventually died and left no children, so it was not passed to his posterity.
- His kingdom was divided toward the four winds of heaven, to his four generals, Cassander, Lysimachus, Seleucus, and Ptolemy, not according to his own dominion.

**[[Daniel 11#^v5|Daniel 11:5]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v5]]
- The king of the south that grows strong is Ptolemy, who was over Egypt.
- One of his princes grows strong above him with a great dominion. Out of Ptolemy came Antiochus.

**[[Daniel 11#^v6|Daniel 11:6]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v6]]
- In the end of years they join themselves together, and the king's daughter of the south comes to the king of the north to make an agreement.
- She does not retain the power of the arm, neither does he stand nor his arm. The power of the arm is Antiochus' strength.

**[[Daniel 11#^v7|Daniel 11:7-9]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v7]]
![[Daniel 11#^v8]]
![[Daniel 11#^v9]]
- Out of a branch of her roots one stands up in her estate, comes with an army, enters the fortress of the king of the north, deals against them and prevails. It was actual war over the kingdoms.
- He carries captives into Egypt, their gods with their princes and their precious vessels of silver and gold, for Ptolemy was over Egypt, and he continues more years than the king of the north.

**[[Daniel 11#^v10|Daniel 11:10-12]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v10]]
![[Daniel 11#^v11]]
![[Daniel 11#^v12]]
- His sons are stirred up, assemble a multitude of great forces, and one comes and overflows and passes through, even to his fortress.
- The king of the south is moved with anger, comes forth, and fights the king of the north, who sets forth a great multitude, but the multitude is given into the hand of the south.
- When he has taken away the multitude his heart is lifted up and he becomes prideful, casting down many ten thousands, but he is not strengthened by it.

**[[Daniel 11#^v13|Daniel 11:13]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v13]]
- The king of the north returns and sets forth a multitude greater than the former, coming after certain years with a great army and much riches.

**[[Daniel 11#^v14|Daniel 11:14]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v14]]
- In those times many stand up against the king of the south, meaning Ptolemy.
- The robbers of thy people, the heathen, the Greeks who robbed Israel, exalt themselves to establish the vision, but they shall fall.

**[[Daniel 11#^v15|Daniel 11:15-16]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v15]]
![[Daniel 11#^v16]]
- The king of the north comes up, casts up a mount, and takes the most fenced cities, and the arms of the south cannot withstand, neither his chosen people, meaning Israel, nor any strength to withstand.
- He that comes against him does according to his own will, and none stands before him, because he is the power at this time, and he stands in the glorious land which is consumed by his hand.

**[[Daniel 11#^v17|Daniel 11:17-19]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v17]]
![[Daniel 11#^v18]]
![[Daniel 11#^v19]]
- He sets his face to enter with the strength of his whole kingdom, and gives him the daughter of women, corrupting her, but she does not stand on his side.
- He turns his face unto the isles of the coastline and takes many, but a prince causes the reproach he offered to cease and turns it back upon him. Someone causes him to stop conquering land, so he is conquered and falls, and this goes to Ptolemy.
- Then he turns his face toward the fort of his own land, but he stumbles and falls and is not found.

**[[Daniel 11#^v20|Daniel 11:20]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v20]]
- In his estate stands up a raiser of taxes in the glory of the kingdom, a ruler taxing and ruling the people, but within few days he is destroyed, neither in anger nor in battle.

**[[Daniel 11#^v21|Daniel 11:21]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v21]]
- In his estate stands up a vile person to whom they do not give the honor of the kingdom, but he comes in peaceably and obtains the kingdom by flatteries.

**[[Daniel 11#^v22|Daniel 11:22]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v22]]
- With the arms of a flood they are overflown and broken from before him, yea also the prince of the covenant, and after the league made with him he works deceitfully.
  - **[[2 Maccabees 4#^v7|2 Maccabees 4:7]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 4#^v7]]
    Jason bought the high priesthood by intercession and set up a gymnasium to train the youth in the fashions of the heathen, Israel getting in league with the heathen.

**[[Daniel 11#^v23|Daniel 11:23-24]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v23]]
![[Daniel 11#^v24]]
- After the league is made he works deceitfully, this being Jason working deceitfully against his own people. He comes up and becomes strong with a small people.
- He enters peaceably even upon the fattest places of the province, the best places of the land, and does what his fathers and his fathers' fathers had not done, scattering among them the prey and spoil and riches, going against his own people for riches.

**[[Daniel 11#^v25|Daniel 11:25]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v25]]
- He stirs up his power and his courage against the king of the south with a great army, and the king of the south is stirred up to battle with a very great and mighty army.
  - **[[2 Maccabees 5#^v1|2 Maccabees 5:1]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 5#^v1]]
    About the time of Antiochus' second voyage into Egypt, horsemen were seen running in the air, and on a false rumor of his death Jason assaulted the city and slew his own citizens without mercy.

**[[Daniel 11#^v26|Daniel 11:26-27]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v26]]
![[Daniel 11#^v27]]
- They that feed of the portion of his meat destroy him, his army overflows, and many fall down slain.
- Both these kings' hearts are set to do mischief, and they speak lies at one table, but it does not prosper, for the end is yet at the time appointed.

**[[Daniel 11#^v28|Daniel 11:28]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v28]]
- Then he returns into his land with great riches, and his heart is against the holy covenant.
  - **[[2 Maccabees 5#^v21|2 Maccabees 5:21]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 5#^v21]]
    Antiochus stripped a thousand and eight hundred talents out of the temple and left governors to vex the nation.
  - **[[2 Maccabees 5#^v25|2 Maccabees 5:25]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 5#^v25]]
    Pretending peace, they waited for the sabbath and slew great multitudes, but Judas Maccabeus withdrew into the wilderness and fed on herbs, lest they be partakers of the pollution.

**[[Daniel 11#^v29|Daniel 11:29-30]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v29]]
![[Daniel 11#^v30]]
- At the time appointed he returns toward the south, but it is not as the former, for the ships of Chittim come against him, therefore he is grieved and has indignation against the holy covenant.
- He returns and has intelligence with them that forsake the holy covenant, because our people were forsaking the laws at this time.
  - **[[Numbers 24#^v24|Numbers 24:24]]**
    ![[Numbers 24#^v24]]
    Ships shall come from the coast of Chittim and afflict Asshur and Eber, tying together with the ships of Chittim that come against him.
  - **[[Genesis 10#^v4|Genesis 10:4]]**
    ![[Genesis 10#^v4]]
    The sons of Javan include Chittim, the land of Greece, so the ships come from off the coast of that European area.

![[Daniel 11#^v31]]

- They that understand among the people instruct many, yet they fall by the sword, by flame, by captivity, and by spoil many days, and some of understanding fall to try them, to purge, and to make them white, even to the time of the end.
  - **[[2 Maccabees 6#^v1|2 Maccabees 6:1-2]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 6#^v1]]
    ![[2 Maccabees 6#^v2]]
    The temple was filled with fornication and profane things, and whoso would not conform to the manners of the Gentiles was put to death.

**[[Daniel 11#^v36|Daniel 11:36-39]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v36]]
![[Daniel 11#^v37]]
![[Daniel 11#^v38]]
![[Daniel 11#^v39]]
- The king does according to his will, exalts and magnifies himself above every god, and speaks marvelous things against the God of gods, and shall prosper till the indignation is accomplished.
- He does not regard the God of his fathers, nor the desire of women, nor any god, for he magnifies himself above all.
- In his estate he honors the god of forces, a god whom his fathers knew not, with gold and silver and precious stones, and he causes them to rule over many and divides the land for gain.

**[[Daniel 11#^v40|Daniel 11:40-45]]**
![[Daniel 11#^v40]]
![[Daniel 11#^v41]]
![[Daniel 11#^v42]]

- He enters the glorious land, and many countries are overthrown, but Edom and Moab and the chief of the children of Ammon escape out of his hand.
- He stretches his hand upon the countries, and Egypt does not escape, and he has power over the treasures of gold and silver, with the Libyans and the Ethiopians at his steps.
- Tidings out of the east and north trouble him, so he goes forth with great fury to destroy, and plants the tabernacles of his palace between the seas in the glorious holy mountain, yet he comes to his end and none shall help him.

# Daniel 12: The Time of the End

Read the chapter → [[Daniel 12]]

**[[Daniel 12#^v1|Daniel 12:1]]**
![[Daniel 12#^v1]]
- At that time Michael stands up, the great prince which stands for the children of thy people, and there is a time of trouble such as never was since there was a nation.
- At that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that is found written in the book. This goes into the prophecy of the last days.

**[[Daniel 12#^v2|Daniel 12:2-3]]**
![[Daniel 12#^v2]]
![[Daniel 12#^v3]]
- Many that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life and some to shame and everlasting contempt.
- They that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars forever and ever.

**[[Daniel 12#^v4|Daniel 12:4]]**
![[Daniel 12#^v4]]
- Daniel is told to shut up the words and seal the book even to the time of the end, and many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased.
- This is how we know we are in the last days: the book was sealed until the time of the end, and now understanding is coming out and it is opened.

**[[Daniel 12#^v5|Daniel 12:5-7]]**
![[Daniel 12#^v5]]
![[Daniel 12#^v6]]
![[Daniel 12#^v7]]
- Two others stand on the two banks of the river, and one asks the man clothed in linen how long it shall be to the end of these wonders.
- The man clothed in linen holds up both hands to heaven and swears by him that liveth forever that it shall be for a time, times, and a half, and when he has accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people, all these things shall be finished.

**[[Daniel 12#^v8|Daniel 12:8-10]]**
![[Daniel 12#^v8]]
![[Daniel 12#^v9]]
![[Daniel 12#^v10]]
- Daniel hears but understands not, and asks what shall be the end of these things, and he is told to go his way, for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end, which is now being opened.
- Many shall be purified and made white and tried, but the wicked shall do wickedly. Some people are going to repent, but the wicked will continue in their wickedness.
- None of the wicked shall understand, but the wise shall understand, and they shall know that it is mandatory to repent.

**[[Daniel 12#^v11|Daniel 12:11-13]]**
![[Daniel 12#^v11]]
![[Daniel 12#^v12]]
![[Daniel 12#^v13]]
- From the time that the daily sacrifice is taken away and the abomination that maketh desolate is set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days.
- Blessed is he that waiteth and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.
- Daniel is told to go his way till the end, for he shall rest, meaning he shall die, and stand in his lot at the end of the days. This is the Most High sending these leaders back to us.
