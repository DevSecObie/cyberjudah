# Daniel 3: The Fiery Furnace and the Servants of the Most High

Read the chapter → [[Daniel 3]]

This session covers [[Daniel 3|Daniel 3]] through 6, read four chapters at a time by the Orlando camp. It moves through the three Hebrews in the burning fiery furnace, Nebuchadnezzar's dream of the great tree and his abasement until he knew the Most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, the handwriting on the wall at Belshazzar's feast, and Daniel delivered out of the den of lions. Throughout, the heathen kings are forced to acknowledge that Israel serves the one true and living God.

**[[Daniel 3#^v1|Daniel 3:1-3]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v1]]
![[Daniel 3#^v2]]
![[Daniel 3#^v3]]
- Nebuchadnezzar made an image of gold, threescore cubits high and six cubits broad, and set it up in the plain of Dura, then gathered the princes, governors, captains, judges, treasurers, counsellors, sheriffs, and rulers to its dedication.
- We have seen things like this before. When they build a monument or a new federal building they hold a dedication and a ribbon-cutting ceremony. This is very similar.

**[[Daniel 3#^v4|Daniel 3:4-5]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v4]]
![[Daniel 3#^v5]]
- A herald cried that when the people heard the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, dulcimer, and all kinds of music they were to fall down and worship the golden image.
- We see the same thing today when they play the national anthem at ball games and ceremonies. You are supposed to take your hat off and put your hand over your heart. That is a form of worshipping the image of the eagle today.

**[[Daniel 3#^v6|Daniel 3:6-7]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v6]]
![[Daniel 3#^v7]]
- Whoever did not fall down and worship would the same hour be cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace, so when the music sounded all the people, nations, and languages fell down and worshipped the golden image.

**[[Daniel 3#^v8|Daniel 3:8-12]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v8]]
![[Daniel 3#^v9]]
![[Daniel 3#^v10]]
![[Daniel 3#^v11]]
![[Daniel 3#^v12]]
- Certain Chaldeans came near and accused the Jews, telling the king that Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, whom he had set over the affairs of the province, did not regard him, serve his gods, nor worship the golden image.
- The reason we did not bow was according to the law.
  - **[[Exodus 20#^v3|Exodus 20:3-5]]**
    ![[Exodus 20#^v3]]
    ![[Exodus 20#^v4]]
    ![[Exodus 20#^v5]]
    Thou shalt have no other gods and make no graven image, nor bow down to them, for the Most High is a jealous God; we are not to bow and serve gods made by hands.

**[[Daniel 3#^v13|Daniel 3:13-15]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v13]]
![[Daniel 3#^v14]]
![[Daniel 3#^v15]]
- In his rage Nebuchadnezzar brought the three men and demanded whether they would fall down and worship, threatening the furnace and asking, who is that God that shall deliver you out of my hands.
- He is talking tough, talking big, challenging our God when he asks who is going to deliver you out of his hands.

**[[Daniel 3#^v16|Daniel 3:16-18]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v16]]
![[Daniel 3#^v17]]
![[Daniel 3#^v18]]
- The three answered that they were not careful to answer him in this matter: their God whom they serve is able to deliver them from the burning fiery furnace and out of the king's hand.
- But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that they would not serve his gods nor worship the golden image. This is exactly what was read in [[Exodus 20|Exodus 20]].

**[[Daniel 3#^v19|Daniel 3:19-22]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v19]]
![[Daniel 3#^v20]]
![[Daniel 3#^v21]]
![[Daniel 3#^v22]]
- Full of fury, Nebuchadnezzar commanded the furnace heated seven times more than usual and had his mighty men bind the three in their coats and garments and cast them in.
- The furnace was so hot that the flame slew the men who carried Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego up to it.
- For more of this history the Song of the Three Holy Children can be read; for the sake of time it was only referenced here.

**[[Daniel 3#^v23|Daniel 3:23-25]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v23]]
![[Daniel 3#^v24]]
![[Daniel 3#^v25]]
- The three men fell down bound into the furnace, but Nebuchadnezzar rose up astonished and said he saw four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, unhurt, and the form of the fourth was like the Son of God.

**[[Daniel 3#^v26|Daniel 3:26-27]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v26]]
![[Daniel 3#^v27]]
- Nebuchadnezzar called them servants of the Most High God to come forth, and the fire had no power on their bodies: not a hair singed, their coats unchanged, nor the smell of fire on them.
- It was as if they had never fallen into the fire; not even the smell of smoke was on these brothers.

**[[Daniel 3#^v28|Daniel 3:28-30]]**
![[Daniel 3#^v28]]
![[Daniel 3#^v29]]
![[Daniel 3#^v30]]
- Nebuchadnezzar blessed the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who sent his angel to deliver the servants that trusted in him and would not serve nor worship any god except their own God.
- He understood at that time that we had our own God. Why do people not understand that today.
- He decreed that any people, nation, or language that spake amiss against their God would be cut in pieces, because there is no other God that can deliver after this sort, and he promoted the three in the province of Babylon.
- When the nations get jacked up they come to know who has the real, true, living God; other heathen rulers came to the same acknowledgement, such as Antiochus and Naaman the Syrian.
  - **[[Joel 2#^v27|Joel 2:27]]**
    ![[Joel 2#^v27]]
    The Most High is in the midst of Israel and none else, and his people shall never be ashamed.

# Daniel 4: Nebuchadnezzar Abased Until He Knew the Most High Rules

Read the chapter → [[Daniel 4]]

**[[Daniel 4#^v1|Daniel 4:1-6]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v1]]
![[Daniel 4#^v2]]
![[Daniel 4#^v3]]
![[Daniel 4#^v4]]
![[Daniel 4#^v5]]
![[Daniel 4#^v6]]
- Nebuchadnezzar proclaimed to all people, nations, and languages the signs and wonders the high God worked toward him, whose kingdom is everlasting, then told how a dream made him afraid and he decreed to bring in all the wise men of Babylon to interpret it.
- Remember from the day before that he had already brought in all his wise men once and they could not recall or interpret his dream; when the captain went out to slay the wise men he came upon Daniel, who was granted favour, consulted the Most High, and was given the vision.

**[[Daniel 4#^v7|Daniel 4:7-9]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v7]]
![[Daniel 4#^v8]]
![[Daniel 4#^v9]]
- The magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers could not make known the interpretation, but at last Daniel came in, named Belteshazzar after the king's god, in whom is the spirit of the holy gods.
- Daniel was named after one of the king's gods. It is the same thing as Hadassah, whose name was changed to Esther; this is not new for us.

**[[Daniel 4#^v10|Daniel 4:10-17]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v10]]
![[Daniel 4#^v11]]
![[Daniel 4#^v12]]
![[Daniel 4#^v13]]
![[Daniel 4#^v14]]
![[Daniel 4#^v15]]
![[Daniel 4#^v16]]
![[Daniel 4#^v17]]
- Nebuchadnezzar saw a great tree reaching to heaven, feeding and sheltering all flesh, then a watcher and a holy one came down and commanded it hewn down, its stump left bound with iron and brass, its heart changed to a beast's, till seven times pass over him.
- The matter is by the decree of the watchers, to the intent that the living may know that the Most High ruleth in the kingdom of men and giveth it to whomsoever he will and setteth over it the basest of men. That is what we see today, the basest of men ruling the earth.
  - **[[Romans 8#^v19|Romans 8:19]]**
    ![[Romans 8#^v19]]
    The whole creation waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God; the nations are waiting for us to wake up, shake off our sins, and take our place as the kings and priests of the earth.

**[[Daniel 4#^v18|Daniel 4:18-27]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v18]]
![[Daniel 4#^v19]]
![[Daniel 4#^v20]]
![[Daniel 4#^v21]]
![[Daniel 4#^v22]]
![[Daniel 4#^v23]]
![[Daniel 4#^v24]]
![[Daniel 4#^v25]]
![[Daniel 4#^v26]]
![[Daniel 4#^v27]]
- Daniel, astonished for an hour, told the king the dream was for his enemies: the tree is the king himself, grown strong to the ends of the earth.
- The decree of the Most High is that he shall be driven from men to dwell with the beasts and eat grass as oxen, wet with the dew of heaven, till seven times pass and he knows the Most High ruleth; the stump left means his kingdom is kept for him once he knows the heavens do rule.
- Daniel counselled him to break off his sins by righteousness and his iniquities by shewing mercy to the poor, that his tranquillity might be lengthened. You need to walk in righteousness and show mercy to the poor.

**[[Daniel 4#^v28|Daniel 4:28-33]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v28]]
![[Daniel 4#^v29]]
![[Daniel 4#^v30]]
![[Daniel 4#^v31]]
![[Daniel 4#^v32]]
![[Daniel 4#^v33]]
- Twelve months later, walking in his palace, the king boasted, is not this great Babylon that I have built by my might for the honour of my majesty. He was very proud and boastful.
- While the word was in his mouth a voice from heaven pronounced the kingdom departed, and the same hour he was driven from men, ate grass as oxen, his hairs grown like eagles' feathers and his nails like birds' claws.

**[[Daniel 4#^v34|Daniel 4:34-35]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v34]]
![[Daniel 4#^v35]]
- At the end of the days Nebuchadnezzar lifted his eyes to heaven, his understanding returned, and he blessed and honoured the Most High, whose dominion is everlasting and before whom all the inhabitants of the earth are reputed as nothing.
- None can stay his hand or say unto him, what doest thou. When the Most High declares a thing shall come to pass, it comes to pass.
  - **[[Isaiah 40#^v17|Isaiah 40:17]]**
    ![[Isaiah 40#^v17]]
    All nations before the Most High are as nothing, counted less than nothing and vanity; Nebuchadnezzar came to the same understanding.

**[[Daniel 4#^v36|Daniel 4:36-37]]**
![[Daniel 4#^v36]]
![[Daniel 4#^v37]]
- His reason and the glory of his kingdom returned, and he praised and extolled the King of heaven, whose works are truth and ways judgment, and who is able to abase those that walk in pride.
- We already understood that the Most High can bring one up and set another down, but the heathen had to learn it by being jacked up, literally turned to crawl like a bird with the heart of a beast; as soon as his understanding returned he praised the one true living God.
  - **[[1 Samuel 2#^v7|1 Samuel 2:7]]**
    ![[1 Samuel 2#^v7]]
    The Most High maketh poor and maketh rich, he bringeth low and lifteth up.

# Daniel 5: The Handwriting on the Wall

Read the chapter → [[Daniel 5]]

**[[Daniel 5#^v1|Daniel 5:1-4]]**
![[Daniel 5#^v1]]
![[Daniel 5#^v2]]
![[Daniel 5#^v3]]
![[Daniel 5#^v4]]
- Belshazzar the king made a great feast to a thousand of his lords and, while tasting the wine, commanded the golden and silver vessels his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple in Jerusalem be brought so he, his princes, wives, and concubines might drink in them.
- This is Belshazzar the king, not Belteshazzar, who is Daniel. They drank and praised the gods of gold, silver, brass, iron, wood, and stone.
  - **[[Baruch 6#^v3|Baruch 6:3-6]]**
    ![[Baruch 6#^v3]]
    ![[Baruch 6#^v4]]
    ![[Baruch 6#^v5]]

![[Daniel 5#^v9]]
- In the same hour fingers of a man's hand wrote on the plaster of the wall, and the king's countenance changed, his knees smote together, so terrified that the joints of his loins were loosed.
- Picture him sitting back drinking with his lords, wives, and concubines, having a good time, and then a hand starts writing on the wall; he was terrified out of everything.
- He cried aloud for the astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers, offering scarlet, a gold chain, and to be third ruler in the kingdom to whoever could read it, but none of his wise men could read the writing or interpret it, and he was greatly troubled.

- Daniel was brought in, and the king offered him scarlet, a gold chain, and the third rulership if he could read the writing and make known the interpretation.

- Belshazzar had not humbled his heart though he knew all this, but lifted himself against the Lord of heaven, drank from the temple vessels, and praised gods of silver, gold, brass, iron, wood, and stone that see not nor hear, while the God in whose hand his breath is he did not glorify.

- Immediately after the interpretation the kingdom was translated from Belshazzar to the Medes and Persians.

# Daniel 6: Daniel in the Den of Lions

Read the chapter → [[Daniel 6]]

**[[Daniel 6#^v1|Daniel 6:1-3]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v1]]
![[Daniel 6#^v2]]
![[Daniel 6#^v3]]
- It pleased Darius to set a hundred and twenty princes over the kingdom, with three presidents over them, of whom Daniel was first, so the king should have no damage.
- Daniel was preferred above the presidents and princes because an excellent spirit was in him, and the king thought to set him over the whole realm. He was set in a very high position in government.

**[[Daniel 6#^v4|Daniel 6:4-5]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v4]]
![[Daniel 6#^v5]]
- The presidents and princes sought occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom but could find none, because he was faithful and no error or fault was found in him. He was blameless.
- This is how our forefathers rolled, and the same kind of spirit we should be rolling in. They concluded they could only find something against him concerning the law of his God.
  - **[[Acts 24#^v16|Acts 24:16]]**
    ![[Acts 24#^v16]]
    Paul exercised himself to always have a conscience void of offence toward God and toward men, just as Daniel did.

**[[Daniel 6#^v6|Daniel 6:6-9]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v6]]
![[Daniel 6#^v7]]
![[Daniel 6#^v8]]
![[Daniel 6#^v9]]
- The presidents and princes assembled and persuaded Darius to sign a firm decree that whoever asked a petition of any god or man for thirty days, save the king, would be cast into the den of lions, by the unchanging law of the Medes and Persians.

**[[Daniel 6#^v10|Daniel 6:10-13]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v10]]
![[Daniel 6#^v11]]
![[Daniel 6#^v12]]
![[Daniel 6#^v13]]
- When Daniel knew the writing was signed he went home, and with his windows open toward Jerusalem kneeled and prayed three times a day, giving thanks as he did before.
- The men assembled, found him praying, and reported to the king that Daniel, of the children of the captivity of Judah, regarded neither the king nor the decree but made his petition three times a day. They really hated Daniel and knew the only way to find fault was concerning the law of his God.

**[[Daniel 6#^v14|Daniel 6:14-17]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v14]]
![[Daniel 6#^v15]]
![[Daniel 6#^v16]]
![[Daniel 6#^v17]]
- The king was displeased with himself and laboured till sundown to deliver Daniel, but the men pressed that the law could not be changed, so he commanded Daniel cast into the den, saying, thy God whom thou servest continually, he will deliver thee.
- The king already understood why he was under the realm of Babylon and had seen what was going on. A stone was laid on the den and sealed with the king's signet.

**[[Daniel 6#^v18|Daniel 6:18-20]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v18]]
![[Daniel 6#^v19]]
![[Daniel 6#^v20]]
- The king went to his palace and passed the night fasting, with no instruments of music brought before him and his sleep gone from him. Why would the king be fasting.
- He rose very early and went in haste to the den, crying with a lamentable voice, O Daniel, servant of the living God, is thy God able to deliver thee from the lions.

**[[Daniel 6#^v21|Daniel 6:21-24]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v21]]
![[Daniel 6#^v22]]
![[Daniel 6#^v23]]
![[Daniel 6#^v24]]
- Daniel answered that his God had sent his angel to shut the lions' mouths because innocency was found in him and he had done no hurt.
- The king was glad and commanded Daniel taken up, and no manner of hurt was found on him because he believed in his God; then the accusers, their children, and wives were cast in and the lions brake their bones before they reached the bottom.
  - **[[Proverbs 16#^v7|Proverbs 16:7]]**
    ![[Proverbs 16#^v7]]
    When a man's ways please the Most High, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; Daniel was operating in a righteous spirit.
  - **Bel and the Dragon 33-39**  *33 Now there was in Jewry a prophet, called Habbacuc, who had made pottage, and had broken bread in a bowl, and was going into the field, for to bring it to the reapers. 34 But the angel of the Lord said unto Habbacuc, Go, carry the dinner that thou hast into Babylon unto Daniel, who is in the lions' den. 35 And Habbacuc said, Lord, I never saw Babylon; neither do I know where the den is. 36 Then the angel of the Lord took him by the crown, and bare him by the hair of his head, and through the vehemency of his spirit set him in Babylon over the den. 37 And Habbacuc cried, saying, O Daniel, Daniel, take the dinner which God hath sent thee. 38 And Daniel said, Thou hast remembered me, O God: neither hast thou forsaken them that seek thee and love thee. 39 So Daniel arose, and did eat: and the angel of the Lord set Habbacuc in his own place again immediately.*  As Daniel sat in the den the Most High sent the prophet Habakkuk by an angel to carry him food, for he does not forsake them that seek and love him.

**[[Daniel 6#^v25|Daniel 6:25-28]]**
![[Daniel 6#^v25]]
![[Daniel 6#^v26]]
![[Daniel 6#^v27]]
![[Daniel 6#^v28]]
- Darius wrote to all people, nations, and languages that in every dominion men tremble and fear before the God of Daniel, for he is the living God, stedfast for ever, whose kingdom shall not be destroyed and who delivereth and rescueth and worketh signs and wonders.
- So Daniel prospered in the reign of Darius and of Cyrus the Persian. He had a wise and humble spirit, was beloved by the king, could answer visions and interpret dreams, and was of the king's seed as shown in chapter one.
