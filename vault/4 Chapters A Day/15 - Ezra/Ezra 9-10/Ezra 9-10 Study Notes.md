# Ezra 9: Ezra Mourns the Strange Wives

Read the chapter → [[Ezra 9]]

## Introduction

This session covers [[Ezra 9|Ezra 9]] and 10, then moves into [[Nehemiah 1|Nehemiah 1]] and 2. In Ezra the people are already back in Jerusalem after Ezra himself has come up, and the trouble that surfaces is intermarriage with the nations of the lands. Ezra mourns, confesses the sin as the same trespass that carried the fathers into captivity, and the congregation makes a covenant to put away the strange wives and the children born of them. Read it as a picture of a people shown grace out of bondage who then forsake the commandment, and of a leader who gives all the glory to the Most High and calls the nation back to separation. The same account is written in 1 Esdras chapters 8 and 9, which the teaching draws on to fill out the record.

**[[Ezra 9#^v1|Ezra 9:1-2]]**

![[Ezra 9#^v1]]
![[Ezra 9#^v2]]

- The princes come to Ezra reporting that the people of Israel, the priests, and the Levites have not separated themselves from the people of the lands, doing after their abominations, the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites.
- The sin is interracial marriage with the nations, taking their daughters for themselves and their sons, so that the holy seed mingled itself with the people of those lands. It was not only the common people but the Levites, the leaders, that were chief in this trespass.

  - **[[Deuteronomy 7#^v3|Deuteronomy 7:3-4]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 7#^v3]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 7#^v4]]
    The commandment was to make no marriages with the nations, not giving daughters to their sons nor taking their daughters, because they would turn the children away from following the Most High.

**[[Ezra 9#^v3|Ezra 9:3-4]]**

![[Ezra 9#^v3]]
![[Ezra 9#^v4]]

- When Ezra hears this thing he rends his garment and his mantle, plucks off the hair of his head and beard, and sits down astonished, in distress that the people did this after the Most High showed mercy to bring them out of Babylon.
- Then everyone that trembled at the words of the God of Israel assembles to him because of the transgression of those that had been carried away, and he sits astonished until the evening sacrifice.

**[[Ezra 9#^v5|Ezra 9:5-7]]**

![[Ezra 9#^v5]]
![[Ezra 9#^v6]]
![[Ezra 9#^v7]]

- At the evening sacrifice Ezra rises from his heaviness, and having rent his garment and mantle, falls upon his knees and spreads out his hands unto the Lord his God.
- He says he is ashamed and blushes to lift up his face, for their iniquities are increased over their head and their trespass is grown up unto the heavens. Since the days of the fathers they have been in a great trespass unto this day.
- For their iniquities they, their kings, and their priests have been delivered into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, and to spoil. The nation is privy to all the captivities it came from, Egypt, Assyria, and Babylon, and knows why it went, yet does the same sins.

**[[Ezra 9#^v8|Ezra 9:8-9]]**

![[Ezra 9#^v8]]
![[Ezra 9#^v9]]

- Now for a little space grace has been showed from the Lord to leave a remnant to escape and to give them a nail in his holy place, that the Most High may lighten their eyes and give a little reviving in their bondage.
- Though they were bondmen the Most High has not forsaken them but extended mercy in the sight of the kings of Persia, to give a reviving to set up the house of God and give them a wall in Jerusalem. This is the very leeway that let them come back to the land.

**[[Ezra 9#^v10|Ezra 9:10-12]]**

![[Ezra 9#^v10]]
![[Ezra 9#^v11]]
![[Ezra 9#^v12]]

- Ezra asks what they shall say after this, for they have forsaken the commandments. It is like a parent giving you something good and you turning and doing something to make him feel wrong for giving it.
- The commandment given by the prophets said the land they go to possess is an unclean land, filled from one end to another with the filthiness and abominations of the people of the lands.
- Therefore they were not to give their sons or take their daughters, nor seek their peace or wealth, that they might be strong, eat the good of the land, and leave it for an inheritance to their children forever. This command is reiterated all the way back to the time of Moses.

**[[Ezra 9#^v13|Ezra 9:13-15]]**

![[Ezra 9#^v13]]
![[Ezra 9#^v14]]
![[Ezra 9#^v15]]

- After all that has come upon them for their evil deeds, and seeing the Most High has punished their iniquities less than they deserve and given such deliverance, Ezra asks should they again break the commandments and join in affinity with the people of these abominations.
- Would not the Most High be angry until he had consumed them so there should be no remnant left. Oh Lord God of Israel, thou art righteous, for they remain yet escaped as it is this day.
- They stand before the Most High in their trespass, for they cannot stand before him because of it. Even though the Levites and Israel committed this sin, the Most High still showed them mercy and let them stay in the land.

# Ezra 10: The Covenant to Put Away Strange Wives

Read the chapter → [[Ezra 10]]

**[[Ezra 10#^v1|Ezra 10:1-4]]**

![[Ezra 10#^v1]]
![[Ezra 10#^v2]]
![[Ezra 10#^v3]]
![[Ezra 10#^v4]]

- When Ezra had prayed and confessed, weeping and casting himself down before the house of God, a very great congregation of men, women, and children assembles to him, for the people wept very sore.
- Shechaniah the son of Jehiel answers and says they have trespassed against their God in taking strange wives of the people of the land, yet there is hope in Israel concerning this thing.
- He counsels to make a covenant to put away all the wives and the children born of them, according to the counsel of those that tremble at the commandment, and to do it according to the law. This time meant putting away those particular wives and children; it does not mean an Israelite today with a child by another nation must put away the child.

**[[Ezra 10#^v5|Ezra 10:5-6]]**

![[Ezra 10#^v5]]
![[Ezra 10#^v6]]

- Then Ezra rises and makes the chief priests, the Levites, and all Israel swear to do according to this word, and they swear, the same way an oath was made to keep the commandments.
- Ezra rises from before the house of God and goes into the chamber of Johanan, and eats no bread nor drinks water, for he mourns because of the transgression of them that had been carried away.

**[[Ezra 10#^v7|Ezra 10:7-8]]**

![[Ezra 10#^v7]]
![[Ezra 10#^v8]]

- Proclamation is made throughout Judah and Jerusalem to all the children of the captivity that they should gather themselves to Jerusalem.
- Whosoever would not come within three days, according to the counsel of the princes and elders, would have all his substance forfeited and himself separated from the congregation of those that had been carried away.

**[[Ezra 10#^v9|Ezra 10:9-11]]**

![[Ezra 10#^v9]]
![[Ezra 10#^v10]]
![[Ezra 10#^v11]]

- All the men of Judah and Benjamin gather to Jerusalem within three days, in the ninth month on the twentieth day, and sit in the street of the house of God trembling because of this matter and the great rain.
- Ezra the priest stands and says they have transgressed and taken strange wives to increase the trespass of Israel.
- Now they are to make confession unto the Lord God of their fathers, do his pleasure, and separate themselves from the people of the land and from the strange wives. Doing his pleasure today is the same, separating from unclean people.

**[[Ezra 10#^v12|Ezra 10:12-15]]**

![[Ezra 10#^v12]]
![[Ezra 10#^v13]]
![[Ezra 10#^v14]]
![[Ezra 10#^v15]]

- The congregation answers with a loud voice, As thou hast said, so must we do, but the people are many and it is a time of much rain, and they are not able to stand outside, neither is the work of one or two days.
- They ask that the rulers stand for the congregation, and that all who have taken strange wives come at appointed times with the elders and judges of every city, until the fierce wrath of God is turned from them. The fierce wrath is the judgment, and the reference to rain is why they came in portions.
- Only Jonathan and Jahaziah were employed about this matter, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them, so the sin was dealt with in orderly parts.

**[[Ezra 10#^v16|Ezra 10:16-17]]**

![[Ezra 10#^v16]]
![[Ezra 10#^v17]]

- The children of the captivity do so; Ezra and certain chief of the fathers, separated by name, sit down on the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.
- The leaders and those who did righteously and had not taken other nations sit and judge the matter, for one who is himself in the sin cannot judge it. They make an end with all the men that had taken strange wives by the first day of the first month.

- All these had taken strange wives, and some of them had wives by whom they had children, so that some put away only the wives and some the wives and children born of them.
