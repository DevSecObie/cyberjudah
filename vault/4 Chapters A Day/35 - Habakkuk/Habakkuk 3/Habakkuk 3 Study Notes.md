# Habakkuk 3: A Prayer of Habakkuk the Prophet

Read the chapter → [[Habakkuk 3]]

## Introduction

This session reads Habakkuk 3 through Zephaniah 3; these notes cover Habakkuk 3, which opens the reading. It is a reading session, so the chapter is read straight through and the notes follow the text. The prayer asks for the work to be revived and for mercy to be remembered in wrath, recounts how the Most High went forth for the salvation of his people, and ends with the prophet resolving to rejoice even when nothing in the field yields.

- O Lord, I have heard thy speech, and was afraid: O Lord, revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; in wrath remember mercy.
- God came from Teman, and the Holy One from mount Paran. His glory covered the heavens, and the earth was full of his praise.
- He stood, and measured the earth: he beheld, and drove asunder the nations; and the everlasting mountains were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: his ways are everlasting.

- When I heard, my belly trembled; my lips quivered at the voice: rottenness entered into my bones, and I trembled in myself, that I might rest in the day of trouble.
- Although the fig tree shall not blossom, neither shall fruit be in the vines; the labour of the olive shall fail, and the fields shall yield no meat; the flock shall be cut off from the fold, and there shall be no herd in the stalls.
- Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, I will joy in the God of my salvation.
- The Lord God is my strength, and he will make my feet like hinds' feet, and he will make me to walk upon mine high places.
