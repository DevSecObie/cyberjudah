# Zechariah 1: Turn Ye Unto Me, and I Will Turn Unto You

Read the chapter → [[Zechariah 1]]

## Introduction

This session reads Haggai 2 through Zechariah 3; these notes cover Zechariah 1 through 3, which close the reading. Zechariah prophesies in the same period as Haggai, in the second year of Darius, to the remnant come back from Babylon. The three chapters run from the call to turn back, through the man among the myrtle trees and the measuring line over Jerusalem, to Joshua the high priest standing before the angel with his filthy garments taken away.

**[[Zechariah 1#^v1|Zechariah 1:1-4]]**

![[Zechariah 1#^v1]]
![[Zechariah 1#^v2]]
![[Zechariah 1#^v3]]
![[Zechariah 1#^v4]]

- In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the Lord unto Zechariah, the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet.
- The Lord hath been sore displeased with your fathers. Turn ye unto me, saith the Lord of hosts, and I will turn unto you.
- Be ye not as your fathers, unto whom the former prophets have cried, Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings: but they did not hear, nor hearken unto me.
- This is repetitive through the whole history. Any time the Most High sends a prophet to warn his people about destruction, that is compassion, because he did not have to do it.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Chronicles 36#^v15|2 Chronicles 36:15-16]]**
    ![[2 Chronicles 36#^v15]]
    ![[2 Chronicles 36#^v16]]
    He sent to them by his messengers because he had compassion on his people, but they mocked the messengers and misused his prophets, until there was no remedy.

**[[Zechariah 1#^v5|Zechariah 1:5-6]]**

![[Zechariah 1#^v5]]
![[Zechariah 1#^v6]]

- Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, do they live for ever? But my words and my statutes took hold of your fathers.
- They returned and said, Like as the Lord of hosts thought to do unto us, according to our ways, and according to our doings, so hath he dealt with us. You get judged by the Most High based on your actions.
- Talk is not what is weighed. Israel is judged by our doings.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Samuel 2#^v3|1 Samuel 2:3]]**
    ![[1 Samuel 2#^v3]]
    Talk no more so exceeding proudly, for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

- Upon the four and twentieth day of the eleventh month, which is the month Sebat, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the Lord unto Zechariah.
- I saw by night, and behold a man riding upon a red horse, and he stood among the myrtle trees that were in the bottom; and behind him were there red horses, speckled, and white.
- These are they whom the Lord hath sent to walk to and fro through the earth. They report back that all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest.

- O Lord of hosts, how long wilt thou not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which thou hast had indignation these threescore and ten years?
- I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great jealousy. He is jealous over Israel because we are espoused to him; serving other gods breaks the covenant our forefathers made.
- I am very sore displeased with the heathen that are at ease: for I was but a little displeased, and they helped forward the affliction. The nations were used to afflict Israel and went far past what was commanded.
- I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies: my house shall be built in it, and a line shall be stretched forth upon Jerusalem. My cities through prosperity shall yet be spread abroad, and the Lord shall yet comfort Zion, and shall yet choose Jerusalem.

- I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and behold four horns. These are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.
- The Lord shewed me four carpenters, come to fray them, to cast out the horns of the Gentiles, which lifted up their horn over the land of Judah to scatter it.
- That is another scattering precept: the scattering is named plainly, and so is who did it.

# Zechariah 2: The Measuring Line and the Apple of His Eye

Read the chapter → [[Zechariah 2]]

**[[Zechariah 2#^v1|Zechariah 2:1-5]]**

![[Zechariah 2#^v1]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v2]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v3]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v4]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v5]]

- Behold a man with a measuring line in his hand, gone to measure Jerusalem, to see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof.
- Jerusalem shall be inhabited as towns without walls, for the multitude of men and cattle therein.
- I will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her.

**[[Zechariah 2#^v6|Zechariah 2:6-7]]**

![[Zechariah 2#^v6]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v7]]

- Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north, saith the Lord: for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven.
- Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon. This is a future prophecy as well as a past one; the scriptures are twofold.
- The deliverance comes by knowing who you are. There is a doctrine to it: acknowledge the fault, repent, and make supplication, and do it in the land of the enemy where you are.

  Precepts:
  - **[[1 Kings 8#^v46|1 Kings 8:46-48]]**
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v46]]
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v47]]
    ![[1 Kings 8#^v48]]
    If they bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and say, We have sinned, and return unto thee with all their heart in the land of their enemies, and pray toward their land.

**[[Zechariah 2#^v8|Zechariah 2:8-9]]**

![[Zechariah 2#^v8]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v9]]

- He that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye. The nations that dealt with Israel harshly and put us in slavery, he will deal with them himself.
- Behold, I will shake mine hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to their servants: and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me. Israel does not have to lift a finger.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 14#^v2|Isaiah 14:2]]**
    ![[Isaiah 14#^v2]]
    They shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

**[[Zechariah 2#^v10|Zechariah 2:10-11]]**

![[Zechariah 2#^v10]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v11]]

- Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion: for lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.
- Many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and shall be my people. Do not read that with a Christian mind, as though it means the other nations of the earth are being brought in.
- Israel was scattered among the nations, so Israel is in the nations. The one the Lord shakes his hand at is the heathen, so the heathen are not who is being joined to him here.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 4#^v27|Deuteronomy 4:27]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 4#^v27]]
    The Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.
  - **[[James 1#^v1|James 1:1]]**
    ![[James 1#^v1]]
    James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad. The letter names who it is written to.

**[[Zechariah 2#^v12|Zechariah 2:12-13]]**

![[Zechariah 2#^v12]]
![[Zechariah 2#^v13]]

- The Lord shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again. That Jerusalem is chosen is said over and over in the scriptures.
- Be silent, O all flesh, before the Lord: for he is raised up out of his holy habitation.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Chronicles 6#^v6|2 Chronicles 6:6]]**
    ![[2 Chronicles 6#^v6]]
    I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there; and have chosen David to be over my people Israel.

# Zechariah 3: The Filthy Garments Taken Away

Read the chapter → [[Zechariah 3]]

**[[Zechariah 3#^v1|Zechariah 3:1-2]]**

![[Zechariah 3#^v1]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v2]]

- He shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.
- The Lord rebuke thee, O Satan; even the Lord that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?

**[[Zechariah 3#^v3|Zechariah 3:3-5]]**

![[Zechariah 3#^v3]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v4]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v5]]

- Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and stood before the angel. Take away the filthy garments from him.
- Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with change of raiment. And they set a fair mitre upon his head.
- The garment is twofold. The change of raiment is Israel becoming righteous: keeping the commandments, putting the Most High first, and dealing rightly with him and with our own people.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Psalms 132#^v9|Psalms 132:9]]**
    ![[Psalms 132#^v9]]
    Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

**[[Zechariah 3#^v6|Zechariah 3:6-10]]**

![[Zechariah 3#^v6]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v7]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v8]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v9]]
![[Zechariah 3#^v10]]

- If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.
- Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, thou, and thy fellows that sit before thee: for they are men wondered at; for behold, I will bring forth my servant the BRANCH. That is the remnant of Israel, Judah and Benjamin and Levi.
- Behold the stone that I have laid before Joshua; upon one stone shall be seven eyes: and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day.
- In that day shall ye call every man his neighbour under the vine and under the fig tree.
