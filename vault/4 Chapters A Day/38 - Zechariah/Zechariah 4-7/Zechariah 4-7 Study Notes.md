# Zechariah 4: The Candlestick, the Two Olive Trees, and the Word to Zerubbabel

Read the chapter → [[Zechariah 4]]

## Introduction

This is a reading session, so the teaching is brief and the four chapters are largely read straight through. The visions run one after another: the golden candlestick with the two olive trees, the flying roll and the ephah with the woman in it, the four chariots and the crowning of Joshua, and then the question about fasting in the fifth month answered in Zechariah 7. The officer's counsel on these chapters is to write your questions down as you read and bring them to your local leadership, because the visions are hard to understand.
