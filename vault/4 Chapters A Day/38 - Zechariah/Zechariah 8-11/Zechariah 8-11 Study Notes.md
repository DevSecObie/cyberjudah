# Zechariah 8: I Will Return Unto Zion and Dwell in the Midst of Jerusalem

Read the chapter → [[Zechariah 8]]

## Introduction

This session covers Zechariah 8 through 11. The teaching runs on one line the whole way: the promises here are made to Israel and to no other nation, and the bible defines its own terms, so Zion, my people, and the remnant are each proved out of the scriptures themselves. From there it goes to the judgment on the nations that had a hand in the captivity, the King coming lowly and riding upon an ass, and the shepherds who were supposed to feed the flock and did not.

![[Zechariah 8#^v1]]

- I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury.
- I am returned unto Zion, and will dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and Jerusalem shall be called a city of truth; and the mountain of the Lord of hosts the holy mountain.
- The bible has its own definition for the words written in it. Zion is named plainly, so there is no need to guess who he is jealous over and who he returns to.

  - **[[Zephaniah 3#^v14|Zephaniah 3:14]]**

- If it be marvellous in the eyes of the remnant of this people in these days, should it also be marvellous in mine eyes? saith the Lord of hosts.

- They shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness. He is called the God of Israel because he is the God of that people and none else.
- My people is not a loose term in the scriptures. It is named.

- Before these days there was no hire for man, nor any hire for beast; neither was there any peace to him that went out or came in because of the affliction.
- But now I will not be unto the residue of this people as in the former days, saith the Lord of hosts. The seed shall be prosperous; the vine shall give her fruit, and the ground shall give her increase, and the heavens shall give their dew; and I will cause the remnant of this people to possess all these things.
- The remnant that possesses these things is named in the prophets, and it is the children of Israel. This is why the bible must be read precept upon precept, because people hear my people and think it means all nations.

**[[Zechariah 8#^v13|Zechariah 8:13]]**

![[Zechariah 8#^v13]]

- As ye were a curse among the heathen, O house of Judah, and house of Israel; so will I save you, and ye shall be a blessing: fear not, but let your hands be strong.
- Both houses are named, the southern kingdom and the northern kingdom.
- The curse among the heathen is the curse written in the law, and the blessing is the same blessing promised there for keeping the commandments.
- There is nowhere in the scriptures where the Most High says he will save another nation to give them the kingdom. Israel is the one who needs a saviour, and the condition we are in is why.

Read the chapter → [[Zechariah 10]]

**[[Zechariah 10#^v1|Zechariah 10:1-2]]**

![[Zechariah 10#^v1]]
![[Zechariah 10#^v2]]

- Ask ye of the Lord rain in the time of the latter rain; so the Lord shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field.
- The idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd.
- That is the condition of Israel as a nation right now. There is no shepherd, because the men our people look to for leadership are not shepherds according to the word.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 56#^v10|Isaiah 56:10]]**
    ![[Isaiah 56#^v10]]
    His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark. A watchman is set to keep you out of harm's way, and one that cannot bark warns no one.
  - **[[Isaiah 56#^v11|Isaiah 56:11]]**
    ![[Isaiah 56#^v11]]
    They are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain. They teach that the law no longer has to be kept and still ask for the tithe.

- They shall be as mighty men, which tread down their enemies in the mire of the streets in the battle: and they shall fight, because the Lord is with them. That is the benefit of keeping the commandments; he fights the battle, for vengeance is his.

- Open thy doors, O Lebanon, that the fire may devour thy cedars. Howl, fir tree; for the cedar is fallen; because the mighty are spoiled.
- There is a voice of the howling of the shepherds; for their glory is spoiled: a voice of the roaring of young lions; for the pride of Jordan is spoiled.

**[[Zechariah 11#^v4|Zechariah 11:5]]**

![[Zechariah 11#^v4]]
![[Zechariah 11#^v5]]

- Feed the flock of the slaughter, whose possessors slay them, and hold themselves not guilty; and they that sell them say, Blessed be the Lord; for I am rich: and their own shepherds pity them not.
- The verse names a people who were possessed, sold, and slain by those who held themselves not guilty, and whose own shepherds do not pity them. That is Israel, the so-called Blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Lamentations 4#^v17|Lamentations 4:17]]**
    ![[Lamentations 4#^v17]]
    As for us, our eyes as yet failed for our vain help: in our watching we have watched for a nation that could not save us. This is why we do not put our hope in marching, voting, or getting into office; we wait on the Most High to save us.
  - **[[Lamentations 4#^v18|Lamentations 4:18]]**
    ![[Lamentations 4#^v18]]
    They hunt our steps, that we cannot go in our streets: our end is near, our days are fulfilled; for our end is come.

- I will no more pity the inhabitants of the land, saith the Lord: but lo, I will deliver the men every one into his neighbour's hand, and into the hand of his king: and they shall smite the land, and out of their hand I will not deliver them.
- I will feed the flock of slaughter, even you, O poor of the flock. And I took unto me two staves; the one I called Beauty, and the other I called Bands; and I fed the flock.
- Three shepherds also I cut off in one month; and my soul lothed them, and their soul also abhorred me.
- I will not feed you: that that dieth, let it die; and that that is to be cut off, let it be cut off; and let the rest eat every one the flesh of another.
- The scripture says plainly what the end of this land is, and it is why keeping the law matters now.
