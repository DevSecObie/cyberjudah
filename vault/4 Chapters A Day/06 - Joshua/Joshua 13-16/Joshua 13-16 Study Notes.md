# Joshua 13: Much Land Yet to Be Possessed

Read the chapter → [[Joshua 13]]

## Introduction

This session covers [[Joshua 13|Joshua 13]] through 16, where the dividing of the land begins. Joshua is now old, and the Most High names the land that still remains to be possessed, confirms the inheritance Moses gave the two tribes and a half on the other side Jordan, and commands the rest to be divided by lot. Caleb receives Hebron for wholly following the Most High, then the lot of the tribe of Judah and the lot of the children of Joseph are drawn out. These chapters carry a whole lot of names of cities and borders, so as you read and listen back through them, it does not hurt to pull up a map or look through your Bible atlas and find where these places were, to get a geographical sense of the land the children of Israel were moving through and conquering.

![[Joshua 13#^v1]]

- The nations were also left among Israel to prove them, whether they would keep the commandments of the Most High, and so that the generations which had not known the wars of Canaan might be taught war. We all know what ended up happening with that proving, and it comes later on as we read.

- The countries in the land of Canaan were distributed by Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes, by lot, as the Most High commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes and the half tribe; Moses had already given the two tribes and a half their inheritance on the other side Jordan.
- The children of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, therefore no part was given unto the Levites in the land, save cities to dwell in with their suburbs for their cattle and for their substance.
- The Levites were living in the suburbs, which is the towns, right among the people. They did not have a land designated to themselves; they were spread throughout all the many tribes.

- His brethren that went up with him made the heart of the people melt, but he wholly followed the Lord his God. The men that went with him brought up an evil report, made the people afraid, and made them doubt.
- Caleb was not playing any games; he was about that business, ready to go and take the land, because he understood the Most High had already promised us the land, so he had no fear. That is the spirit that says, let us go up at once and possess it.
- So Moses sware on that day that the land whereon Caleb's feet had trodden would be his inheritance and his children's for ever, because he wholly followed the Lord.

**[[Joshua 14#^v10|Joshua 14:10-11]]**

![[Joshua 14#^v10]]

- The Most High has kept Caleb alive these forty and five years, and now at fourscore and five years old he is as strong this day as he was in the day Moses sent him; as his strength was then, even so is his strength now, for war, both to go out and to come in. This man is eighty-five years old and in perfect health.
- Moses was the same way: a hundred and twenty years old with the strength and the vision of a young man. A lot of us are not living to a hundred and twenty, but at the same time we are making it to sixty and seventy, and at fifty we are hooked up on machines. We are all going to die at some point, but that does not mean you have to be miserable, sitting there hooked up to all kind of machines while you are here.
- Leadership has been pushing that we all need to take care of our bodies and our health. A lot of us deal with gluttony even though it is not a topic we talk about all the time; cut down a little what you eat and eat better food.
- The same way we make haste to stop the different sins, be quick to change the diet. If we make haste to change those things, the Most High will also keep away that continual sickness, the diabetes and everything causing our limbs to be cut off just to live.

- This location is the same place where our forefather Jacob was, where he had the vision of the ladder that reached up into heaven. Jacob rose up early, took the stone he had put for his pillows, set it up for a pillar, and called the name of that place Bethel, but the name of that city was called Luz at the first.
- We are reading about it in Joshua where our forefather had already been to that location, and we are just going back to take it back. That is what we are doing.
