## Introduction

Satan's kiss to your demise. When you see that title, you got to pay attention, because the kiss can come as a gift; it can come as opportunity. That is the whole point of this class: Satan uses these devices to appear good, to appear like something righteous, but it ends up ultimately being something evil. And within our lust and the things that we deal with, we will pursue those things, because sin feels good. Remember the clip out of Vampire in Brooklyn: evil is good.

The objective of this class is to understand how sin rarely presents itself as destruction. Instead, it presents itself as opportunity, pleasure, love, comfort, success, escape, and validation. All of those things sound great, but you have to be in the spirit to discern if it is in righteousness or if it is Satan's kiss to you. And you have to be in the scriptures to understand how to discern those things.
