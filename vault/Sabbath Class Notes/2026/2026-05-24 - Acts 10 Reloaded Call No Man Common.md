## Introduction

A feast of Pentecost class from Austin, Texas. The opening prayer thanked the Most High for the feast of weeks, and the whole nation gathered to keep the feast day together: teaching first, then the breaking of bread, then a night of fellowship and performances.

Bishop set up the lesson: I was going through Acts 10 recently and there is some information I would love to share. The key that unlocks the whole class: everything that the prophets or our Lord and Savior Christ spoke in the New Testament was reiterating what we call today the Old Testament, so that it could substantiate the points they were making. When they said something, they had to be able to use the text to justify their saying. That is what leads us into Acts 10, and into Peter's vision: call no man common.

- Take thee one stick, and write upon it, For Judah; then take another stick, and write upon it, For Joseph, the stick of Ephraim. This is what Peter would have had to read to them, to convince them that the cleansing of the northern kingdom was going to happen and they would be brought back in.

- But when Peter was come to Antioch, I withstood him to the face, because he was to be blamed. Before certain came from James, he did eat with the Gentiles: but when they were come, he withdrew and separated himself, fearing them which were of the circumcision. Paul said: you already learned this, Peter; God showed you that you cannot call any man common or unclean. You sat with them; you convinced many of the Jews that these men were all right. But now you are wavering.
- And the other Jews dissembled likewise with him; insomuch that Barnabas also was carried away with their dissimulation. The class looked up dissimulation: deception, hypocrisy, pretense, deceit; in other words, being fake.

He was corrected in front of them all, and he would have been embarrassed and ashamed. But he repented; and as a teacher, what do you teach after that? What you repented from: do not be fake.

## In Closing

The whole class was one demonstration: Peter's vision in Acts 10 was never about food. The vessel knit at the four corners was Israel; the beasts, creeping things, and fowls were our people living as beasts among the nations; and the command to rise, kill, and eat was the commission: it is time to gather the twelve tribes, kill the old man, devour wisdom, and go out and prophesy. What God hath cleansed, call not thou common. And the same gathering is happening now, from South America to Africa to the States. Stay godly, stay fervent, and do the work.

Closing reading at the breaking of bread: 1 Corinthians 11:23-30, the bread and the cup, in remembrance of the Lord's death till he come; let a man examine himself.
