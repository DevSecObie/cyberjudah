# The penitent thief
**Robbery, under sentence of crucifixion** · Put to death · Gospels · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Two malefactors were crucified with Christ. One railed on him; the other rebuked his fellow: we indeed justly, for we receive the due reward of our deeds, but this man hath done nothing amiss. Then: Lord, remember me when thou comest into thy kingdom.

## Offense
Matthew calls them thieves, Luke malefactors; the man's own confession is that the sentence was just. He owned the law's verdict on himself in the hour of its execution.

## Judgment
The sentence of the state was carried out; he died on the cross that day. The sentence of heaven was reversed in the same hour: verily I say unto thee, to day shalt thou be with me in paradise. Isaiah had said the servant would be numbered with the transgressors.
