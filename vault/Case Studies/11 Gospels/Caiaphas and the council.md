# Caiaphas and the council
**Seeking false witness against Christ; condemning him by night; delivering him to the Gentiles; forbidding the apostles; stoning Stephen** · Temporal judgment · Gospels · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Caiaphas had already ruled that it was expedient one man die for the people. The council sought false witness to put Jesus to death and found none that agreed; then the high priest adjured him and condemned him for his answer. They delivered him to Pilate, chose Barabbas, and afterward beat and forbade his apostles, and gnashed on Stephen and stoned him.

## Offense
Every rule of the court broken in one night: witnesses suborned, testimony not agreeing, a capital sentence at night by the accusers themselves, and the prisoner condemned by his own compelled confession. Stephen's charge to them: ye stiffnecked, which of the prophets have not your fathers persecuted? and ye have been now the betrayers and murderers of the Just One.

## Judgment
Christ told them the kingdom would be taken from them and their house left desolate; Paul says the wrath is come upon them to the uttermost. Peter to their faces: the stone which was set at nought of you builders. Within the generation the temple in which they sat was rubble.

## Statutes
- [[4E Witnesses and Testimony#4E.1|4E.1]] You shall not bear false witness against another nor raise a false report.
- [[4E Witnesses and Testimony#4E.4|4E.4]] Do not join your hand with a wicked man to be an unrighteous witness.
- [[4E Witnesses and Testimony#4E.9|4E.9]] You shall not testify in a dispute for the sake of following the majority and thus pervert justice.
- [[4E Witnesses and Testimony#4E.10|4E.10]] At the testimony of two or three witnesses shall the fact of the matter be established.
- [[4F Justice In Law and Judgment#4F.13|4F.13]] Doing injustice by justifying the wicked and condemning the righteous is an abomination and condemned by God.
- [[4F Justice In Law and Judgment#4F.6|4F.6]] You shall keep far from a false matter.
- [[8C Judges & Magistrates#8C.9|8C.9]] The judge that justifies the wicked and condemns the just is an abomination to God.
- [[10A Murder#10A.2|10A.2]] Shedding of innocent blood is an abomination to God.
- [[17K Hypocrisy#17K.5|17K.5]] We are not to have hypocritical leaders because they ensnare the people.
