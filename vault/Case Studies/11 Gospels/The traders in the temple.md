# The traders in the temple
**Making the house of prayer a den of thieves** · Temporal judgment · Gospels · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Twice Christ found the temple court full of those that sold oxen, sheep, and doves, and the changers of money sitting. He made a scourge of small cords, drove them out, poured out the changers' money, overthrew the tables, and would not suffer any man to carry a vessel through the temple.

## Offense
Isaiah had called it a house of prayer for all people; Jeremiah had asked, is this house become a den of robbers in your eyes? The trade occupied the court of the Gentiles, the only place the nations could pray, and did it, on the gospel's account, for profit under the priests' patronage.

## Judgment
Driven out with a whip. The chief priests sought how they might destroy him for it. Zechariah's last verse hangs over the scene: in that day there shall be no more the Canaanite, the trafficker, in the house of the Lord of hosts.
