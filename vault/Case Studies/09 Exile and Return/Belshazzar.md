# Belshazzar
**Drinking from the temple vessels and praising the gods of gold and silver** · Put to death · Exile and Return · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
At a feast for a thousand lords, Belshazzar called for the gold and silver vessels Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the house of God in Jerusalem, drank wine from them with his wives and concubines, and praised the gods of gold, silver, brass, iron, wood, and stone. Fingers of a man's hand wrote on the plaster.

## Offense
Daniel's indictment: thou hast lifted up thyself against the Lord of heaven, and the God in whose hand thy breath is, and whose are all thy ways, hast thou not glorified. He knew what had happened to his father and humbled not his heart.

## Judgment
Mene, mene, tekel, upharsin: numbered, weighed and found wanting, divided. In that night was Belshazzar the king of the Chaldeans slain, and Darius the Median took the kingdom.
