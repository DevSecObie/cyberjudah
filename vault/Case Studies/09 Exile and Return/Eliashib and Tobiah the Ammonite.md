# Eliashib and Tobiah the Ammonite
**Lodging an Ammonite enemy in a chamber of the house of God; a priest's house allied to Sanballat** · Exile · Exile and Return · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
While Nehemiah was away, Eliashib the priest, allied to Tobiah by marriage, cleared out a storechamber of the temple that had held the tithe and the vessels and gave it to the Ammonite who had mocked and opposed the wall. Nehemiah returned and threw Tobiah's household stuff out.

## Offense
Deuteronomy had barred the Ammonite from the congregation of the Lord for ever, and this one was an active enemy. The law also required the priests to keep the charge of the sanctuary and the Levites to be supplied from it; instead the tithe was displaced to house him. The high priest's grandson had married Sanballat's daughter.

## Judgment
It grieved me sore: therefore I cast forth all the household stuff of Tobiah out of the chamber, and commanded the chambers cleansed and the vessels and the tithe brought back. The son-in-law of Sanballat he chased from him. Remember them, O my God, because they have defiled the priesthood.
