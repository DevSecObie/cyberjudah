# Hymenaeus, Alexander, and Philetus
**Making shipwreck of the faith; blasphemy; teaching that the resurrection is past already** · Exile · Apostolic · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Paul names Hymenaeus and Alexander as men who put away faith and a good conscience and made shipwreck, whom I have delivered unto Satan, that they may learn not to blaspheme. Hymenaeus and Philetus erred concerning the truth, saying the resurrection is past already, and overthrew the faith of some. Alexander the coppersmith did him much evil.

## Offense
Heresy that eats as doth a canker, spread inside the church by teachers. Titus gives the procedure: a man that is an heretick after the first and second admonition reject, knowing that he is subverted and sinneth, being condemned of himself.

## Judgment
Delivered unto Satan, the apostolic form of putting out, with the stated aim of correction. Of Alexander Paul adds only: the Lord reward him according to his works.
