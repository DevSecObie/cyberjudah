# The Corinthians at the Lord's supper
**Eating and drinking unworthily; shaming them that have not** · Plague · Apostolic · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
When the Corinthians came together it was not to eat the Lord's supper: every one took his own supper before another, one was hungry and another drunken, and the poor were shamed in the assembly of the church. Paul: what, have ye not houses to eat and to drink in?

## Offense
Contempt for the assembly and for the brethren who had nothing, in the very meal that proclaimed the Lord's death. Whosoever shall eat this bread and drink this cup unworthily shall be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord.

## Judgment
For this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep. Paul frames it as chastening: when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord, that we should not be condemned with the world. The remedy: let a man examine himself, and tarry one for another.
