# Israel in the days of the Judges
**Serving Baalim and Ashtaroth; intermarrying with the Canaanites; every man doing right in his own eyes** · Captivity · Judges · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
After Joshua's generation died a generation arose that knew not the Lord. They forsook him for the gods of the peoples round about, took their daughters as wives, and were sold into the hands of Mesopotamia, Moab, Canaan, Midian, Ammon, and the Philistines in turn.

## Offense
The cycle the book itself describes: the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, served Baalim, forsook the God of their fathers. Judges 3 records the intermarriage that drove it; Psalm 106 says they were mingled among the heathen and learned their works.

## Judgment
The anger of the Lord was hot against Israel, and he delivered them into the hands of spoilers. Each time they cried out he raised a judge; each time the judge died they returned and corrupted themselves more than their fathers. The book ends with no king and every man right in his own eyes.
