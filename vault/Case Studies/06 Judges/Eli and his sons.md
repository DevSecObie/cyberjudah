# Eli and his sons
**Corrupt priesthood; a father who would not restrain it** · Put to death · Judges · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Eli's sons Hophni and Phinehas treated the Lord's offering with contempt and lay with the women at the tabernacle door. Eli rebuked them weakly but did not remove them. The house fell together.

## Offense
The sons seized the sacrifices by force and defiled worship; the father honored his sons above God by tolerating it. A failure of office compounded by a failure of discipline.

## Judgment
Both sons died on the same day in battle; the ark was captured; Eli fell and broke his neck at the news. The priesthood was stripped from his line as foretold.
