# Abimelech son of Gideon
**Murder of seventy brothers on one stone; usurping a crown** · Put to death · Judges · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Gideon's son by a Shechemite concubine hired vain and light persons with silver from the house of Baal-berith, killed his seventy brothers on one stone, and had himself made king at Shechem. Only Jotham escaped, and his parable from Mount Gerizim pronounced the sentence.

## Offense
Fratricide financed by an idol's treasury, in pursuit of a kingship God had not given and Gideon had refused. Jotham: if ye have dealt truly, rejoice in Abimelech; if not, let fire come out from Abimelech and devour the men of Shechem, and fire from Shechem devour Abimelech.

## Judgment
God sent an evil spirit between Abimelech and Shechem; he destroyed the city and sowed it with salt, and at Thebez a woman dropped a millstone on his head. He had his armor-bearer finish him so it would not be said a woman slew him. Thus God rendered the wickedness of Abimelech.

## Statutes
- [[10A Murder#10A.5|10A.5]] If a man acts with premeditation against his neighbor, to kill him with guile, he shall die.
- [[10A Murder#10A.7|10A.7]] Killing for hire is condemned (the assassin and the one who hires him are both guilty of murder).
- [[8A Requirements of Civil Leaders#8A.14|8A.14]] Government officers that engage in conspiracy, treachery and murder are to be put to death.
- [[8B Frame of Government#8B.6|8B.6]] Warnings against an all-powerful leader, king or central government.
- [[2P Judgment and Punishment#2P.2|2P.2]] Punishment is the fruit and reward for iniquity and wicked ways (you shall reap that which you sow).
