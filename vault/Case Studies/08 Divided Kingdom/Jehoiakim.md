# Jehoiakim
**Building with unrighteousness; shedding innocent blood; burning the scroll of the Lord's words** · Cursed · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Necho's puppet king built a great house with unpaid labor, killed the prophet Urijah for prophesying like Jeremiah, and when Jeremiah's scroll was read to him, cut it with a penknife leaf by leaf and burned it in the hearth. Neither he nor his servants were afraid.

## Offense
Jeremiah's indictment: thine eyes and thine heart are not but for thy covetousness, and for to shed innocent blood, and for oppression, and for violence. Withholding wages, murder of a prophet, and the deliberate destruction of the written word.

## Judgment
He shall be buried with the burial of an ass, drawn and cast forth beyond the gates of Jerusalem. He shall have none to sit upon the throne of David, and his dead body shall be cast out in the day to the heat and in the night to the frost. His son reigned three months and went to Babylon; his brother was the last king.
