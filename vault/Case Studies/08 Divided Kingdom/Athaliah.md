# Athaliah
**Destroying the seed royal of Judah; usurping the throne** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Ahab's daughter, married into Judah's line, saw her son Ahaziah dead at Jehu's hand and murdered all the seed royal to reign herself. One infant, Joash, was hidden in the house of the Lord for six years. In the seventh, Jehoiada the priest brought him out.

## Offense
Mass murder of her own grandchildren for a crown, and six years of Baal worship in Jerusalem: her sons had broken up the house of God and given its dedicated things to Baalim.

## Judgment
She heard the trumpets, rent her clothes, and cried, treason. Jehoiada would not have her killed in the house of the Lord; they took her out by the horse gate and slew her there. Then the people tore down Baal's house and killed Mattan his priest.

## Statutes
- [[10A Murder#10A.2|10A.2]] Shedding of innocent blood is an abomination to God.
- [[8A Requirements of Civil Leaders#8A.12|8A.12]] Civil leaders and authorities are to be men not women.
- [[8A Requirements of Civil Leaders#8A.14|8A.14]] Government officers that engage in conspiracy, treachery and murder are to be put to death.
- [[10G Treason and Rebellion#10G.3|10G.3]] Rebellion and insurrection against the government is punishable by death.
- [[2D Idols and Idolatry#2D.3|2D.3]] You shall not engage in idolatry and worship or serve the gods, idols or graven images of other nations.
