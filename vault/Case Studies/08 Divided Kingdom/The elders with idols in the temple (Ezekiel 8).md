# The elders with idols in the temple (Ezekiel 8)
**The image of jealousy; creeping things on the walls; women weeping for Tammuz; twenty-five men worshipping the sun with their backs to the temple** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Carried in vision to Jerusalem, Ezekiel was shown four scenes of greater and greater abomination inside the house of the Lord: an idol at the gate, seventy elders with censers in a chamber of painted beasts, women mourning a pagan god, and men between the porch and the altar bowing east to the sun. The elders said, the Lord seeth us not.

## Offense
Idolatry practiced by the leadership in the sanctuary itself, in secret, on the assumption that God had forsaken the earth. The land was also full of blood and the city of perverseness. Ezekiel 14 adds: these men have set up their idols in their heart.

## Judgment
The glory departed by stages. Six men with slaughter weapons were told to begin at the sanctuary and slay utterly old and young, sparing only those marked who sighed and cried for the abominations. Mine eye shall not spare, neither will I have pity.
