# The captivity of Judah
**Polluting the house of the Lord; mocking the messengers of God; refusing the Sabbath and the land's sabbaths** · Captivity · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
All the chief of the priests and the people transgressed very much after all the abominations of the heathen and polluted the house of the Lord. The Lord sent messengers because he had compassion; they mocked them, despised his words, and misused his prophets until there was no remedy.

## Offense
Ezekiel's catalogue of Jerusalem: princes shedding blood, fathers and mothers set light by, strangers oppressed, sabbaths profaned, incest, bribes for blood, usury, priests who put no difference between holy and profane, prophets daubing with untempered mortar. Jeremiah adds the Sabbath gates: bear no burden on the sabbath day, and they would not hear.

## Judgment
Nebuchadnezzar slew their young men in the sanctuary, burned the house of God, and carried the remnant to Babylon, to fulfil threescore and ten years until the land had enjoyed her sabbaths. Daniel and Baruch both confess it in the same words: the curse is poured upon us, as it is written in the law of Moses.

## Statutes
- [[9D Land Sabbath#9D.2|9D.2]] Every seventh year to be a Sabbath of rest for the land, you shall neither sow your field nor prune your vineyard.
- [[9C Labor Sabbath#9C.8|9C.8]] No burdens to be carried on during the Sabbath.
- [[2H Obedience and Submission#2H.3|2H.3]] Disobedience to God causes national captivity by heathen or enemy nations.
- [[20F Warnings Regarding Priests and Prophets#20F.4|20F.4]] Lying pastors and those that withhold the truth of God's word are cursed for causing national apostasy.
- [[20A Office and Duty of Ministers#20A.7|20A.7]] A priest is to teach the people the difference between holy and profane and to discern between unclean and clean.
- [[4F Justice In Law and Judgment#4F.11|4F.11]] You shall not pervert the judgment of the stranger, the widow, the oppressed nor of the fatherless.
- [[16C Usury#16C.1|16C.1]] If you lend to any of your brethren in need, you are not to take or lay usury upon them.
- [[2P Judgment and Punishment#2P.9|2P.9]] Cursed be he that does not heed to all the words of the law.
