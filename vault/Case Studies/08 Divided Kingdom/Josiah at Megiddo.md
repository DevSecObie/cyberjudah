# Josiah at Megiddo
**Going to battle against Necho without inquiring of the Lord** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The reformer king, promised he would be gathered to his grave in peace, went out against Pharaoh Necho, who was marching to Carchemish and had no quarrel with Judah. Necho sent word that God had commanded him to make haste and that Josiah should not meddle with God, who was with him. Josiah disguised himself and fought anyway.

## Offense
Chronicles is unusually direct: he hearkened not unto the words of Necho from the mouth of God. A war without cause, without inquiry, against a warning the narrator credits as divine. The best king since David died of a battle that was not his.

## Judgment
Archers hit him in the valley of Megiddo; his servants carried him to Jerusalem and he died. Jeremiah lamented for him, and the lament became an ordinance in Israel. The promise of a peaceful grave was kept in the sense that he did not see the evil that followed.
