# Aman, who ensnared Achiacharus
**Treachery against the man who brought him up** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Tobit's dying counsel to his son holds up one case: remember how Aman handled Achiacharus that brought him up, how out of light he brought him into darkness, and how he rewarded him again. Achiacharus, the king's cupbearer and Tobit's kinsman, had once interceded for Tobit himself.

## Offense
Ingratitude become betrayal, against a benefactor who stood as a father. The story assumed by the text is the tale of Ahikar: the adopted nephew who forged evidence to have his uncle executed.

## Judgment
Achiacharus was saved, but the other had his reward: for he went down into darkness. Aman fell into the snare and perished. Tobit's moral: consider what alms doeth, and how righteousness doth deliver.
