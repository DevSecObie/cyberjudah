# Jason the high priest
**Buying the priesthood; setting up a Greek gymnasium; drawing his countrymen to heathen customs** · Exile · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Onias' brother Jason obtained the high priesthood from Antiochus for three hundred and sixty talents and a promise of more, then built a place of exercise under the tower itself, brought the young men under the Greek hat, and had the priests neglect the sacrifices to hasten to the games. He sent silver for the sacrifice of Hercules.

## Offense
The narrator's summary: such was the height of Greek fashions, and increase of heathenish manners, through the exceeding profaneness of Jason, that ungodly wretch, and no high priest. A bought office, a foreign religion imported into the sanctuary's shadow, and the law despised for the sake of the games. Then Menelaus outbid him.

## Judgment
Driven out, he attacked Jerusalem with a thousand men, slew his own citizens without mercy, and was driven out again. He fled from city to city, pursued of all men, hated as a forsaker of the laws; he that had cast out many unburied had none to mourn for him, and perished in a strange land.
