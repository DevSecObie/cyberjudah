# The Hellenizers who forsook the holy covenant
**Covenanting with the heathen; removing circumcision; profaning the Sabbath; sacrificing to idols** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
In the days of Antiochus, wicked men went out of Israel persuading many to make a covenant with the heathen round about. They built a place of exercise at Jerusalem, made themselves uncircumcised, forsook the holy covenant, and were sold to do mischief. When the king's decree came, many Israelites consented to his religion.

## Offense
First Maccabees calls them sinners and wicked men; Second Maccabees traces the profaneness to Jason. Every mark of the covenant was surrendered by choice: circumcision, Sabbath, sacrifice, food, and the company of the nations.

## Judgment
Mattathias and his friends smote sinful men in their anger and wicked men in their wrath; the rest fled to the heathen for succour. Judas burnt up those that vexed his people, and the wicked shrunk for fear of him. After Antiochus, the ungodly out of Israel who sought Alcimus' priesthood found Judas had taken vengeance on their sort.
