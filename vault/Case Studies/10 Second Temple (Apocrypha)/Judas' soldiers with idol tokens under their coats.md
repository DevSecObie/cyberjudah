# Judas' soldiers with idol tokens under their coats
**Carrying things consecrated to idols into battle** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
After the battle near Adullam, Judas and his company came to bury their dead and found under the coat of every slain man things consecrated to the idols of the Jamnites, which is forbidden the Jews by the law. Then every man saw that this was the cause wherefore they were slain.

## Offense
Deuteronomy: thou shalt not bring an abomination into thine house, lest thou be a cursed thing like it. Joshua's men had died at Ai for Achan's hidden spoil; these had died in a battle they otherwise won.

## Judgment
The survivors praised the Lord, the righteous Judge, who had opened the things that were hid, and prayed that the sin might be wholly put out of remembrance. Judas took a collection and sent it to Jerusalem for a sin offering for the dead.
