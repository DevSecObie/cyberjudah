# Menelaus
**Buying the priesthood over Jason; stealing the temple vessels; procuring the murder of Onias; bribing his way past the king's judgment** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Sent by Jason to carry the king's money, Menelaus outbid him by three hundred talents and came back high priest, though he was not of Aaron's line by the account. He stole gold vessels from the temple to pay his debt, had the righteous Onias murdered for exposing him, and bought off the king when the Jews accused him.

## Offense
Second Maccabees lists him as the cause of all the mischief: a great traitor to the laws and to his own country, bringing nothing worthy the high priesthood, but having the fury of a cruel tyrant and the rage of a savage beast. The bribe that acquitted him sent his three accusers to death instead.

## Judgment
At last the King of kings moved Antiochus Eupator against him. In Beroea he was thrust into a tower of ashes fifty cubits high, the death for sacrilege, not having so much as burial in the earth: for inasmuch as he had committed many sins about the altar, whose fire and ashes were holy, he received his death in ashes.
