# David and Bathsheba
**Adultery and the arranged death of Uriah** · Temporal judgment · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The king took another man's wife, and when she conceived, engineered her husband's death to cover it. The prophet Nathan exposed it with a parable, and David confessed, but the consequences ran through his house for years.

## Offense
David lay with Bathsheba, wife of Uriah the Hittite, then recalled Uriah to mask the pregnancy; when that failed, he had him set in the front of battle to die. Adultery, deceit, and murder by another's hand.

## Judgment
The child died. Nathan declared that the sword would never depart from David's house, and it did not, his own sons rose against him. David was not executed; he repented, and the throne stood, but under a lasting wound.
