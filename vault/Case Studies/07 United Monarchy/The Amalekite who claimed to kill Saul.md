# The Amalekite who claimed to kill Saul
**Claiming to have slain the Lord's anointed** · Put to death · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
A young Amalekite came to David at Ziklag with Saul's crown and bracelet and said he had killed the wounded king at his own request. The earlier chapter says Saul fell on his own sword. Either way, his mouth condemned him.

## Offense
He expected reward for the death of Saul. David judged him on his own testimony: how wast thou not afraid to stretch forth thine hand to destroy the Lord's anointed? Whether he lied for gain or did the deed, the charge stood.

## Judgment
Thy blood be upon thy head; for thy mouth hath testified against thee. David had him executed on the spot.
