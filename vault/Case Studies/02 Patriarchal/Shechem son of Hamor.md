# Shechem son of Hamor
**Defiling Dinah, daughter of Jacob** · Put to death · Patriarchal · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Shechem, prince of the country, saw Dinah, took her, and lay with her and defiled her. He then asked for her in marriage and agreed to circumcision to get her. Her brothers answered with the sword.

## Offense
He had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob's daughter, which thing ought not to be done. The law would later require the seducer of an unbetrothed virgin to endow her and the rapist to die.

## Judgment
On the third day, while the men of the city were sore, Simeon and Levi killed every male, Hamor and Shechem among them, and took Dinah out of his house. Jacob condemned the manner of it; the brothers answered, should he deal with our sister as with an harlot?
