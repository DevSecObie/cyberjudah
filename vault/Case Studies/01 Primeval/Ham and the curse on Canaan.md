# Ham and the curse on Canaan
**Uncovering and publishing his father's nakedness** · Cursed · Primeval · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Noah drank of his vineyard and lay uncovered in his tent. Ham saw his father's nakedness and told his two brothers outside, who walked in backward with a garment and covered him without looking.

## Offense
Dishonor of a father, in the very thing the law later forbids: the nakedness of thy father thou shalt not uncover. Shem and Japheth showed what honor looks like; Ham showed the opposite and made it public.

## Judgment
When Noah awoke and knew what his younger son had done to him, he cursed Canaan, Ham's son: a servant of servants shall he be unto his brethren. Shem and Japheth were blessed in the same breath.
