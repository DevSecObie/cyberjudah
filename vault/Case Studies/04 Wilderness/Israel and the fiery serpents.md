# Israel and the fiery serpents
**Speaking against God and against Moses; loathing the bread from heaven** · Plague · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Discouraged on the way around Edom, the people spoke against God and Moses: there is no bread, no water, and our soul loatheth this light bread. The Lord sent fiery serpents, and much people of Israel died.

## Offense
Contempt for the provision and the deliverer in a single complaint. Paul cites it: neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them also tempted, and were destroyed of serpents.

## Judgment
The serpents bit and many died. When the people confessed, Moses made a serpent of brass on a pole; whoever looked, lived. The Wisdom of Solomon says they were troubled for a small season to be admonished, saved not by the thing they saw but by God. Christ took it as the figure of his own lifting up.
