# The generation that died in the wilderness
**Ten provocations; unbelief at Kadesh-barnea** · Put to death · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Those numbered from twenty years old who had seen the signs in Egypt and at the sea, and had tempted the Lord ten times, were sentenced at Kadesh to wander until every one of them had fallen in the wilderness. Only Caleb and Joshua of that generation entered the land.

## Offense
Not one act but a pattern: at the sea, at Marah, over the manna, at Rephidim, at the calf, at Taberah, at Kibroth-hattaavah, and finally refusing the land God had sworn to give. Hebrews names the root: they could not enter in because of unbelief. Then, told the sentence, they presumed to go up anyway and were beaten back.

## Judgment
Forty years, a year for each day of the search. Your carcases shall fall in this wilderness. Sirach counts them: the six hundred thousand footmen gathered together in the hardness of their hearts, whom he pitied not.
