# Korah, Dathan, and Abiram
**Rebellion against God-appointed authority** · Put to death · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Korah led 250 leaders in a challenge to Moses and Aaron, claiming the whole congregation was holy and needed no mediator. The earth answered the claim.

## Offense
They gathered against Moses, charging him with lifting himself above the assembly. It was not merely a personnel dispute but a rejection of the order God had set, framed in the language of equality.

## Judgment
The ground opened and swallowed Korah's company with their households, and fire consumed the 250 who offered incense. When the people murmured the next day, a plague killed 14,700 more until Aaron's incense stayed it.
