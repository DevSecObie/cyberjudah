# Aaron and Miriam
**Speaking against Moses; challenging his authority** · Temporal judgment · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Miriam and Aaron spoke against Moses, using his marriage as a pretext to ask whether the Lord had not also spoken through them. God summoned all three and answered plainly.

## Offense
Envy of Moses' unique standing, aired as a grievance. God declared that with Moses he spoke mouth to mouth, not in visions, and asked why they were not afraid to speak against him.

## Judgment
Miriam became leprous, white as snow; struck, it seems, as the instigator. Moses interceded, and after seven days shut out of the camp she was healed. A measured discipline, not death.
