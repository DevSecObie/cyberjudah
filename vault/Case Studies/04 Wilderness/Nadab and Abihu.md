# Nadab and Abihu
**Offering strange fire not commanded** · Put to death · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Two priests, sons of Aaron, offered fire before the Lord that he had not commanded. Fire came out from the Lord and consumed them on the spot.

## Offense
They took their censers, put fire and incense in them, and offered 'strange fire before the Lord, which he commanded them not.' Presumption in the very act of worship, from those who of all people knew the ordinances.

## Judgment
Fire from before the Lord devoured them, and they died. Aaron was forbidden even to mourn. A warning that nearness to holy things raises the standard rather than lowering it.
