# Lot's wife
**Looking back toward Sodom against the angel's command** · Put to death · Patriarchal · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The angels told Lot's household to escape for their lives and not look behind them. His wife looked back from behind him and became a pillar of salt. Christ made her a proverb in three words.

## Offense
A plain instruction, given for her own life, disregarded. The look back is treated in scripture as a turning of the heart toward what God was destroying.

## Judgment
She became a pillar of salt on the plain, and the Wisdom of Solomon says the standing pillar remained as a monument of an unbelieving soul. Remember Lot's wife.
