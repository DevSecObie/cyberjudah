# Simeon and Levi at Shechem
**Deceitful covenant; slaughter of a city for one man's crime** · Cursed · Patriarchal · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
To avenge Dinah, the brothers proposed circumcision to the men of Shechem as a condition of peace, then killed the whole city while it was disabled and plundered it. Jacob never forgot it.

## Offense
They answered Shechem and Hamor deceitfully. A covenant made in bad faith, used as a weapon; collective slaughter for an individual's offense; and spoil taken from the dead. Vengeance that belonged to God taken into their own hands.

## Judgment
On his deathbed Jacob passed sentence: instruments of cruelty are in their habitations; cursed be their anger, for it was fierce. I will divide them in Jacob and scatter them in Israel. Simeon was absorbed into Judah; Levi had no tribal land.
