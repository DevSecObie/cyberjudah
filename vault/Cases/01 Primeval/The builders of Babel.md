# The builders of Babel
**Building a tower to make themselves a name and resist being scattered** · Exile · Primeval · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
With one language and one purpose, the whole earth settled in Shinar and built a city and a tower whose top would reach to heaven, to make themselves a name and not be scattered abroad. The Lord came down to see it.

## Offense
Direct defiance of the command to replenish the earth, in the form of a monument to human self-sufficiency. The stated aim was a name for themselves, not for God.

## Judgment
The Lord confounded their language so they could not understand one another and scattered them over the face of all the earth. The building stopped, and the place was named Babel, confusion.
