# Adam and Eve
**Eating from the tree God forbade** · Cursed · Primeval · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Given one prohibition in the garden, they ate from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil after the serpent's persuasion. The first transgression, and the pattern for every one after it: a plain command, a rationalization, a broken boundary.

## Offense
The Lord permitted every tree but one. The woman, deceived, took the fruit and gave it to the man, who ate knowingly. Both then hid, and both shifted blame, to the serpent, to each other, to God for the gift of the other.

## Judgment
No death that day, but a sentence on all that followed: pain in childbearing, toil against a cursed ground, and expulsion from the garden lest they eat of the tree of life. Death entered as the long consequence.
