# The sons of God and the giants
**Leaving their proper estate; taking wives of the daughters of men** · Captivity · Primeval · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Before the Flood the sons of God took wives of whomever they chose among the daughters of men, and giants were in the earth in those days. The New Testament and the Apocrypha both treat this as a transgression that was judged.

## Offense
Jude names it: angels who kept not their first estate but left their own habitation. Second Peter calls them the angels that sinned. Sirach calls the giants themselves the old giants who fell away in the strength of their foolishness, and the Wisdom of Solomon says the proud giants perished.

## Judgment
The angels are reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day. The giants and the world they filled with violence went under the Flood; Noah alone was carried through.
