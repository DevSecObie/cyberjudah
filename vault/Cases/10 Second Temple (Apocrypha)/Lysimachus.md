# Lysimachus
**Sacrilege: robbing the temple with Menelaus' consent** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Menelaus' brother committed many sacrileges in the city by his brother's consent and carried away much gold. The multitude gathered against him; he armed three thousand men under a captain of years and madness and attacked the crowd.

## Offense
Plunder of the sanctuary treated as family business, then armed force against the people who objected. The narrator calls him the church-robber.

## Judgment
The people took up stones and clubs and ashes, wounded many, killed some, put the rest to flight, and slew Lysimachus himself beside the treasury. Judgment executed by the congregation at the scene of the crime.
