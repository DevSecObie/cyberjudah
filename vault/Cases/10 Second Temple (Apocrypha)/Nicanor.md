# Nicanor
**Blasphemy against the temple; breaking covenant with Judas; resolving to attack on the Sabbath** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Demetrius' general, having sworn friendship to Judas and broken it at Alcimus' instigation, stretched out his hand toward the temple and swore that if Judas were not delivered to him he would burn the house and build a temple to Bacchus. He resolved to attack on the Sabbath; asked if there were a Mighty one in heaven who commanded the day, he answered that he was mighty on earth.

## Offense
Contempt for the sanctuary, for the Sabbath, and for the God of both, spoken aloud as policy. The priests prayed at the altar for vengeance on the blasphemer; Judas' army prayed the prayer of Hezekiah against Sennacherib.

## Judgment
Nicanor fell first in the battle at Adasa, and his army fled. They cut off his head and the right hand which he stretched out so proudly, cut out the tongue of the blasphemer, hung the head from the tower over against the temple, and ordained the day a yearly feast, the day before Mordecai's day.
