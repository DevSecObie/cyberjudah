# Holofernes
**Making Nebuchadnezzar a god; blasphemy against the God of Israel; lust and drunkenness** · Put to death · Second Temple (Apocrypha) · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Nebuchadnezzar's captain, sent to make all the earth worship the king alone as god, cut down the groves and gods of every nation and besieged Bethulia. Who is God but Nebuchadnezzar, he asked, and told Achior that the God of Israel would not save his people. Judith came to his camp.

## Offense
Idolatry enforced by the sword, and contempt for the Lord spoken to his face through Achior. On the fourth night his heart was ravished with Judith and he was filled with wine, more than he had drunk at any time in one day since he was born.

## Judgment
Judith took his own fauchion, smote twice upon his neck with all her might, and took away his head; his army, finding him headless in his tent, fled and was cut down. Judith's song: the Lord Almighty hath disappointed them by the hand of a woman.
