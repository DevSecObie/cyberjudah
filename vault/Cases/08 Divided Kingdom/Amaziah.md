# Amaziah
**Bringing home the gods of Seir and bowing to them** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Having beaten Edom with the Lord's help, Amaziah brought back the gods of the children of Seir, set them up as his gods, and burned incense to them. A prophet asked why he sought gods that could not deliver their own people; the king told him to stop before he was smitten.

## Offense
Worship of the defeated enemy's idols, which the law says are to be burned, not carried home. Then, lifted up in pride, he provoked a war with Israel he was warned against and lost.

## Judgment
The prophet's parting word: I know that God hath determined to destroy thee, because thou hast done this. Israel broke down Jerusalem's wall and plundered the temple; after that Amaziah's own people conspired against him at Lachish and killed him there.
