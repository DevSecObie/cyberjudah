# The prince of Tyre
**Saying, I am a God, I sit in the seat of God** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Ezekiel's word to the ruler of the merchant city: because thine heart is lifted up, and thou hast said, I am a God, in the midst of the seas; yet thou art a man, and not God. Wiser than Daniel, richer by his traffick, and undone by both.

## Offense
Self-deification on the strength of trade. By the multitude of thy merchandise they have filled the midst of thee with violence, and thou hast sinned. Tyre had also rejoiced at Jerusalem's fall: aha, she is broken, I shall be replenished.

## Judgment
They shall bring thee down to the pit, and thou shalt die the deaths of them that are slain in the midst of the seas. Wilt thou yet say before him that slayeth thee, I am God? Nebuchadnezzar besieged the mainland city; Alexander finished the island.
