# The shepherds of Israel
**Feeding themselves and not the flock; scattering the sheep** · Cursed · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Ezekiel 34 arraigns the rulers and pastors of Israel as shepherds who ate the fat and clothed themselves with the wool, but did not strengthen the diseased, heal the sick, bind up the broken, bring back the driven away, or seek the lost. The flock was scattered on every hill and became meat to the beasts.

## Offense
With force and with cruelty have ye ruled them. Jeremiah brings the same charge: ye have scattered my flock and driven them away and not visited them. Zechariah's idol shepherd leaves the flock and is cursed in his arm and his eye.

## Judgment
Behold, I am against the shepherds; I will require my flock at their hand and cause them to cease from feeding the flock. Then the Lord promises to search his sheep himself and set up one shepherd, his servant David. Christ claimed the office: I am the good shepherd.
