# Jonah
**Fleeing from the presence of the Lord to avoid his commission; anger at God's mercy** · Reprieve · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Told to go east to Nineveh, Jonah bought passage west to Tarshish. A storm, a lot, and a fish brought him back. He preached, the city repented, and he was angry enough to die because God spared it. A gourd, a worm, and a wind finished the lesson.

## Offense
Refusal of a prophet's commission, with the reason given afterward in his own words: I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful. He would rather drown than see Nineveh forgiven. Then anger without cause, twice, at the mercy he himself had received.

## Judgment
Three days and nights in the belly of the fish, out of which he prayed and was vomited on dry land, and the word of the Lord came the second time. The book ends on God's question, unanswered: should not I spare Nineveh? Christ made his three days the sign of the resurrection.
