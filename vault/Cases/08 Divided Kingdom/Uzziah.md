# Uzziah
**Entering the temple to burn incense, which belonged to the priests** · Temporal judgment · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
A strong and prosperous king who sought God in the days of Zechariah. When he was strong his heart was lifted up, and he went into the temple with a censer. Eighty priests withstood him; while he raged at them, leprosy rose in his forehead.

## Offense
Usurping an office God had assigned to the sons of Aaron. The stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death was the statute; Korah had died for the same claim. Azariah said it plainly: it appertaineth not unto thee, Uzziah.

## Judgment
A leper to the day of his death, cut off from the house of the Lord, dwelling in a several house while his son judged the people. He was buried in a field near the kings' sepulchres, not among them, for they said, he is a leper.
