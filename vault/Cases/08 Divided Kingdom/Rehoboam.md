# Rehoboam
**Forsaking the counsel of the old men; forsaking the law of the Lord** · Temporal judgment · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Solomon's son rejected the elders' advice to serve the people and took his young companions' line: my little finger shall be thicker than my father's loins. Ten tribes left him that day. Later, when the kingdom was established, he forsook the law of the Lord and all Israel with him.

## Offense
Pride in the first instance, apostasy in the second. Chronicles: he did evil, because he prepared not his heart to seek the Lord. Sirach calls him the foolishness of the people, one that had no understanding.

## Judgment
Shishak of Egypt came up with twelve hundred chariots and took the treasures of the house of the Lord and the shields of gold. When the princes humbled themselves the Lord granted some deliverance, that they might know the difference between serving him and serving the kingdoms of the countries.
