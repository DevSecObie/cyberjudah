# Ahaz
**Burning his children in the fire; copying the altar of Damascus; sacrificing to the gods that smote him** · Temporal judgment · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Ahaz walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, made molten images for Baalim, burnt his children in the fire after the abominations of the heathen, and hired Assyria against Syria with the temple's silver. Defeated, he sacrificed to the gods of Damascus because they had helped his enemies.

## Offense
Child sacrifice, the sin the Canaanites were destroyed for; a pagan altar pattern installed in the Lord's house; shutting up the temple doors; and the logic of a lost man, worship whoever is winning. Wisdom describes the Canaanites he imitated as merciless murderers of children.

## Judgment
The Lord delivered him into the hand of Syria and of Israel, who slew a hundred and twenty thousand in one day, and Assyria distressed him instead of strengthening him. In the time of his distress did he trespass yet more against the Lord: this is that king Ahaz. He was buried outside the kings' sepulchres.

## Statutes
- [[10H Child Sacrifice#10H.3|10H.3]] You shall not let any of your offspring pass through the fire of Molech.
- [[10H Child Sacrifice#10H.1|10H.1]] Any that offer their children to pagan gods shall be put to death.
- [[2D Idols and Idolatry#2D.3|2D.3]] You shall not engage in idolatry and worship or serve the gods, idols or graven images of other nations.
- [[2G Heathen Practices#2G.1|2G.1]] We are not to learn or follow the ways, customs or abominations of the heathen or of any ungodly nation.
- [[11B Aid and Help#11B.3|11B.3]] It is vain to rely on other nations for help while ignoring the aid of God.
- [[2J Serving and Worshiping God#2J.8|2J.8]] You shall not worship the LORD your God in the manner that the heathen nations serve their gods.
