# Ahab
**Marrying Jezebel; serving Baal; sparing Ben-hadad whom the Lord appointed to destruction** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Ahab did more to provoke the Lord than all the kings before him: he married a Zidonian, built Baal a house in Samaria, and made a grove. He spared the king of Syria for a trade deal after the Lord gave him the victory, and sold himself to work wickedness in Naboth's vineyard.

## Offense
Idolatry, intermarriage, and the release of an enemy God had devoted, on top of the judicial murder his wife arranged for him. Elijah's word: thou hast sold thyself to work evil in the sight of the Lord.

## Judgment
Because he humbled himself at Elijah's sentence, the evil on his house was deferred to his son's days. His own end was not: an arrow at a venture found the joints of his harness at Ramoth-gilead, and dogs licked his blood at the pool of Samaria as the Lord had said.
