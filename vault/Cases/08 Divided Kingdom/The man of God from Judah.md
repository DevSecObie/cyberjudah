# The man of God from Judah
**Turning back to eat and drink at Bethel against the word of the Lord** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Sent to cry against Jeroboam's altar, the prophet was charged to eat no bread, drink no water, and not return the way he came. An old prophet of Bethel lied to him, claiming an angel had said otherwise; he went back and ate. A lion met him on the road.

## Offense
He had refused the king's table and reward; he accepted a colleague's word over God's own. Test the spirits: another prophet's claim did not cancel a command he had received himself. The old prophet's lie is recorded without excuse.

## Judgment
The word of the Lord came through the very man who had deceived him: thy carcase shall not come unto the sepulchre of thy fathers. The lion killed him and stood by the body without eating it or the ass; the old prophet buried him in his own grave.
