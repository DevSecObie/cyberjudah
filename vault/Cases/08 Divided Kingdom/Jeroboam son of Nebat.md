# Jeroboam son of Nebat
**Two golden calves; priests of the lowest of the people; a feast devised of his own heart** · Cursed · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Given ten tribes by the Lord, Jeroboam feared the people would return to Jerusalem to worship, so he set calves in Bethel and Dan, saying, behold thy gods, O Israel, made priests of any who were not Levites, and moved the feast to a month of his own choosing. Every king of Israel after him is measured by his sin.

## Offense
State idolatry engineered for political security, borrowing the words of Aaron's calf. He was warned at the altar itself by a man of God from Judah, saw his hand wither, and after all this returned not from his evil way.

## Judgment
The Lord promised to cut off from Jeroboam every male, and to take away the remnant of his house as a man takes away dung. Baasha killed his son Nadab and all his house. Israel's captivity two centuries later is charged to the sins of Jeroboam, which he made Israel to sin.
