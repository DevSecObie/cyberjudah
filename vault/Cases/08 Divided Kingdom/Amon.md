# Amon
**Serving his father's idols and refusing his father's repentance** · Put to death · Divided Kingdom · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Manasseh's son reigned two years, sacrificed to all the carved images his father had made, and humbled not himself before the Lord as Manasseh had humbled himself, but trespassed more and more.

## Offense
He had both examples in one man, the apostasy and the repentance, and chose the first. He forsook the Lord God of his fathers and walked not in the way of the Lord.

## Judgment
His own servants conspired against him and slew him in his house. The people of the land killed the conspirators and made Josiah, eight years old, king in his stead.
