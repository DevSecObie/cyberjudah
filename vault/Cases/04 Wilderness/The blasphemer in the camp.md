# The blasphemer in the camp
**Blaspheming the Name in a quarrel** · Put to death · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
In a fight within the camp, a man blasphemed the Name and cursed. He was held until the Lord's mind was made known, and his case set the statute for all Israel.

## Offense
The son of an Israelite woman and an Egyptian man blasphemed the Name in the course of a brawl. The offense was against God directly, spoken and heard by witnesses.

## Judgment
The Lord commanded that the whole congregation stone him outside the camp, the witnesses laying their hands on his head. From his case came the law: whoever blasphemes the Name shall be put to death.
