# The ten spies
**Bringing an evil report; making the congregation to murmur** · Plague · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Twelve men searched Canaan forty days. Ten came back and told the people the land eats its inhabitants and the giants made them as grasshoppers. Caleb and Joshua said the Lord would bring them in. The people believed the ten.

## Offense
They did not deny the land was good; they denied that God could give it. Their report turned a nation from faith to fear in one night and had the people ready to choose a captain back to Egypt and stone the two who believed.

## Judgment
The men that brought up the evil report died by the plague before the Lord. Caleb and Joshua lived, and forty-five years later Caleb took his mountain.
