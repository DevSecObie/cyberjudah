# Israel at Baal-peor
**Fornication with the daughters of Moab; sacrificing to their gods** · Plague · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
In Shittim the people began to commit whoredom with the daughters of Moab, who called them to the sacrifices of their gods. Israel ate, bowed down, and joined himself unto Baal-peor. The plague took twenty-four thousand.

## Offense
Balaam's counsel executed: sexual sin as the door to idol worship. Hosea says they went to Baal-peor and separated themselves unto that shame, and their abominations were according as they loved. Psalm 106 adds that they ate the sacrifices of the dead.

## Judgment
The Lord commanded the heads of the offenders hanged up before the sun, and the judges to slay every man his men that were joined to Baal-peor. A plague ran through the camp until Phinehas stayed it. Paul: fell in one day three and twenty thousand.
