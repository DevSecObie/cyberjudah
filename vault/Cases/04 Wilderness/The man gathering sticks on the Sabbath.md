# The man gathering sticks on the Sabbath
**Working on the Sabbath** · Put to death · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
A man was found gathering sticks on the Sabbath day. Because the penalty had not yet been specified, he was held while the Lord's judgment was sought.

## Offense
The command to keep the Sabbath was already given; his act of gathering fuel was plain work on the day of rest, done openly and witnessed.

## Judgment
The Lord commanded that he be put to death; the congregation stoned him outside the camp. A hard case, preserved precisely because the sentence was not obvious and had to be asked.
