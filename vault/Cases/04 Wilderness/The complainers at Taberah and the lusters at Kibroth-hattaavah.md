# The complainers at Taberah and the lusters at Kibroth-hattaavah
**Murmuring against the Lord; lusting after flesh and despising the manna** · Plague · Wilderness · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The people complained and the fire of the Lord burned in the outskirts of the camp. Days later the mixed multitude fell to lusting and Israel wept for the meat of Egypt. Quail came, and with the quail a plague.

## Offense
Contempt for God's provision. They said the manna had dried up their souls and named the fish and cucumbers of Egypt as what they missed, forgetting the brick-kilns. Psalm 78 calls it tempting God in their heart and speaking against him.

## Judgment
Fire consumed the complainers at Taberah until Moses prayed. At Kibroth-hattaavah, while the flesh was yet between their teeth, the Lord smote the people with a very great plague; the place is named for the graves of the lusters. Paul: these things were our examples.
