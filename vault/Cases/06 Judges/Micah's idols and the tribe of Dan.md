# Micah's idols and the tribe of Dan
**A house of gods, a hired Levite, and a tribe that stole both** · Captivity · Judges · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Micah of Ephraim stole silver from his mother, returned it, and she had it made into a graven and molten image. He set up a house of gods, consecrated his own son, then hired a wandering Levite as priest. The Danites, migrating north, took the priest and the idols for themselves.

## Offense
Every layer breaks a law: theft from a parent, images made from the stolen silver, a shrine that was not the chosen place, a priest who was not of Aaron, and then armed robbery of the whole apparatus by a tribe that founded its northern city on it. The narrator's refrain: every man did that which was right in his own eyes.

## Judgment
The Danites set up the graven image and Jonathan son of Gershom and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land. Dan's idolatry ran unbroken from this to Jeroboam's calf at the same city.
