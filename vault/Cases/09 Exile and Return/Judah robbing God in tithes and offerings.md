# Judah robbing God in tithes and offerings
**Withholding the tithe from the storehouse** · Cursed · Exile and Return · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Will a man rob God? Yet ye have robbed me. But ye say, wherein? In tithes and offerings. The returned community had left the Levites unsupported so that they fled to their fields, and the house of God was forsaken while every man ran to his own house.

## Offense
The tenth of the land's increase belongs to the Lord; withholding it is theft from him. Haggai had already named the consequence: ye have sown much and bring in little, because of mine house that is waste. Nehemiah found the same neglect on his return and contended with the rulers over it.

## Judgment
Ye are cursed with a curse: for ye have robbed me, even this whole nation. The remedy offered in the same breath: bring ye all the tithes into the storehouse, and prove me now herewith, if I will not open you the windows of heaven.

## Statutes
- [[20E Tithes and Offerings#20E.4|20E.4]] Withholding tithes and offerings to God is considered as robbing God.
- [[20E Tithes and Offerings#20E.1|20E.1]] A tenth part of all increase (not all wealth owned) is to be given as a tithe.
- [[20E Tithes and Offerings#20E.3|20E.3]] We are to honor God by bringing the choice first fruits of our land into the house of God, so He may bring prosperity ...
- [[20E Tithes and Offerings#20E.13|20E.13]] Tithes are to be given to the priests and ministerial aides so that they may devote themselves to the law of God.
- [[20E Tithes and Offerings#20E.6|20E.6]] A tenth of all the produce of the land is to be tithed to God.
