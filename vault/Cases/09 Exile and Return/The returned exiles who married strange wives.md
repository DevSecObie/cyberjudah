# The returned exiles who married strange wives
**Mingling the holy seed with the peoples of the lands** · Restitution · Exile and Return · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Ezra was told that the people, priests, and Levites had taken wives of the Canaanites, Hittites, Ammonites, Moabites, and Egyptians, and that the princes and rulers had been chief in the trespass. He rent his garment and sat astonished until the evening sacrifice. A generation later Nehemiah found children who could not speak the Jews' language.

## Offense
Deuteronomy 7 was the statute: neither shalt thou make marriages with them, for they will turn away thy son from following me. Ezra's prayer cites it and names the reason it cut deeper after the exile: after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, should we again break thy commandments? Nehemiah pointed at Solomon.

## Judgment
The people confessed, and by covenant separated themselves from the strange wives and from such as were born of them, case by case before the elders and judges of every city, over three months. First Esdras preserves the same proceedings. Nehemiah contended with the later offenders, smote certain of them, and chased away the priest who had married Sanballat's daughter.
