# Nebuchadnezzar
**Pride: is not this great Babylon, that I have built?** · Temporal judgment · Exile and Return · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Warned by Daniel's interpretation of the tree dream to break off his sins by righteousness and his iniquities by showing mercy to the poor, the king walked on his palace roof twelve months later and took the credit for the empire. While the word was in his mouth a voice fell from heaven.

## Offense
The beginning of pride is when one departeth from God, says Sirach; Nebuchadnezzar's was the claim of the builder over the Builder. He had already burned three men for refusing his image and been told whose hand had given him the kingdom.

## Judgment
Driven from men, he ate grass as oxen, his body wet with dew, his hair grown like eagles' feathers and his nails like birds' claws, until seven times passed and he lifted up his eyes to heaven. His reason returned; his kingdom was restored; and he wrote the confession himself: those that walk in pride he is able to abase.
