# Joab
**Murder of Abner and Amasa in time of peace** · Put to death · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
David's general killed Abner in the gate of Hebron under cover of a private word, avenging Asahel's death in battle, and years later killed Amasa with a kiss and a hidden sword. David laid both on Solomon's charge.

## Offense
Two captains of the host, both under the king's protection, both killed by treachery outside any battle. David's sentence in life: the Lord shall reward the doer of evil according to his wickedness. His charge at death: he shed the blood of war in peace.

## Judgment
Joab fled to the horns of the altar. Solomon cited the statute that the altar is no refuge for a murderer and sent Benaiah to kill him there, that the innocent blood be taken away from David's house. He was buried in his own house in the wilderness.
