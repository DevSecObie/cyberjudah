# King Solomon
**Multiplying foreign wives; building high places to their gods** · Temporal judgment · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The wisest king loved many strange women, seven hundred wives and three hundred concubines, from the very nations the Lord had said, ye shall not go in to them. When he was old they turned his heart, and he built high places for Chemosh and Molech in the hill before Jerusalem.

## Offense
Deuteronomy 17 forbade the king to multiply wives, horses, or silver, and named the reason: that his heart turn not away. Solomon did all three. Nehemiah cites him as the proof that even the beloved of God was made to sin by outlandish women. Sirach: thou didst stain thine honour and pollute thy seed.

## Judgment
The Lord was angry, and told him the kingdom would be rent from his son and given to his servant, leaving one tribe for David's sake. Adversaries were raised up in his own lifetime; Jeroboam received the ten tribes at his death. So the kingdom was divided.
