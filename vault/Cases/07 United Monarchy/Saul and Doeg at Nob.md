# Saul and Doeg at Nob
**Slaughter of the priests of the Lord on an informer's report** · Cursed · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Doeg the Edomite, Saul's chief herdsman, told the king that Ahimelech had given David bread and Goliath's sword. Saul summoned the priests, condemned them unheard, and when his own guard refused to strike them, Doeg killed eighty-five men in linen ephods and then the whole city of Nob.

## Offense
For Saul, judicial murder of the priesthood on a suspicion; Ahimelech's defense was true and ignored. For Doeg, a report shaped to kill and a hand willing to do what Israelites would not. Psalm 52 is David's indictment of him: thy tongue deviseth mischiefs, like a sharp razor.

## Judgment
Psalm 52 pronounces it: God shall likewise destroy thee for ever, he shall take thee away, and pluck thee out of thy dwelling place. Saul's own end came at Gilboa. Abiathar alone escaped Nob and carried the ephod to David.
