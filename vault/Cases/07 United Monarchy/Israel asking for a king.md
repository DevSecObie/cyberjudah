# Israel asking for a king
**Rejecting the Lord's rule to be like the nations** · Reprieve · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The elders came to Samuel and demanded a king to judge them like all the nations. The Lord told Samuel: they have not rejected thee, but they have rejected me. Warned what a king would take from them, they said, nay, but we will have a king over us.

## Offense
Deuteronomy allowed a king on God's terms; Israel wanted one on the nations' terms, to go out before them and fight their battles, as if the Lord had not. Samuel later named it: your wickedness is great, which ye have done in the sight of the Lord, in asking you a king. Hosea: I gave thee a king in mine anger.

## Judgment
The Lord granted the request and sent thunder and rain in wheat harvest as a sign of his displeasure. The people feared and confessed, and Samuel answered, fear not; ye have done all this wickedness, yet turn not aside from following the Lord. They got Saul.
