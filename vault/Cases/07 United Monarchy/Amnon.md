# Amnon
**Rape of his sister Tamar** · Put to death · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
David's firstborn feigned sickness to get his half-sister alone, and when she pleaded that such a thing should not be done in Israel, forced her, then hated her more than he had loved her and had her thrown out. David was very wroth and did nothing.

## Offense
Incest and rape together, both capital under the law he was heir to. Tamar had named the law and offered him the lawful way of asking the king; he would not hearken to her voice. Then he added public shame to the crime.

## Judgment
Two years later Absalom had him killed at the sheep-shearing feast when his heart was merry with wine. Nathan's sentence on David's house, the sword shall never depart, began its work here.
