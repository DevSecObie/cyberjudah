# Absalom
**Murder of Amnon; conspiracy and rebellion against the king his father; lying with his father's concubines** · Put to death · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
David's son killed his brother, fled, was restored, then stole the hearts of Israel at the gate with flattery, raised a rebellion from Hebron, drove his father from Jerusalem, and lay with David's concubines on the roof before all Israel by Ahithophel's counsel.

## Offense
The stubborn and rebellious son of Deuteronomy 21 on a national scale: murder, treason, and the uncovering of his father's bed as a political act. The rebellion was fueled by a daily lie at the gate: see, thy matters are good, but there is no man deputed of the king to hear thee.

## Judgment
Caught by his hair in an oak as his mule went from under him, he was run through by Joab and ten armor-bearers, cast into a pit, and covered with a great heap of stones. He had raised a pillar for his own name; the stones were his monument instead.
