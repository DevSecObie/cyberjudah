# Shimei son of Gera
**Cursing the king and casting stones at him** · Put to death · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
As David fled Jerusalem, Shimei of Saul's house came out cursing and throwing stones and dust: come out, thou bloody man, thou man of Belial. David forbade Abishai to kill him and later swore he would not put him to death. Solomon inherited the case.

## Offense
Thou shalt not revile the gods, nor curse the ruler of thy people. He did it in the king's face, in his hour of weakness, with the king's own guard at hand. His later apology at the Jordan was accepted for David's lifetime only.

## Judgment
Solomon confined him to Jerusalem on pain of death. Three years later Shimei went to Gath after runaway servants, and Solomon had Benaiah kill him: the Lord shall return thy wickedness upon thine own head.
