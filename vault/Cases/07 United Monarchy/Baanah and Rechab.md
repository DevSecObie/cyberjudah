# Baanah and Rechab
**Murder of Ish-bosheth in his bed** · Put to death · United Monarchy · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Two captains of Saul's son entered his house at noon under pretense of fetching wheat, killed him as he lay on his bed, cut off his head, and carried it all night to David at Hebron expecting a reward.

## Offense
Murder of a righteous person in his own house upon his bed, as David put it, done with guile for gain. They dressed it as service to the Lord's cause and David's.

## Judgment
David commanded his young men to slay them; their hands and feet were cut off and hung over the pool in Hebron. Ish-bosheth's head was buried in Abner's grave.
