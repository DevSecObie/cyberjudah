# Saul of Tarsus
**Persecuting the church; consenting to Stephen's death; compelling the saints to blaspheme** · Spared · Apostolic · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The young man at whose feet Stephen's killers laid their clothes made havock of the church, entering every house and haling men and women to prison, and set out for Damascus with letters to bind any he found of that way. A light from heaven and a voice met him on the road: why persecutest thou me?

## Offense
By his own later account: I persecuted this way unto the death; I punished them oft in every synagogue and compelled them to blaspheme; being exceedingly mad against them, I persecuted them even unto strange cities. He was a blasphemer, a persecutor, and injurious.

## Judgment
Three days blind and fasting in Damascus, then sight, baptism, and a commission. I obtained mercy, because I did it ignorantly in unbelief. He calls himself the chief of sinners and the pattern of the longsuffering shown to them which should hereafter believe. Ananias was told he would be shown how great things he must suffer.
