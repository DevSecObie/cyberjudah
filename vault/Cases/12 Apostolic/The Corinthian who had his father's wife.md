# The Corinthian who had his father's wife
**Fornication not so much as named among the Gentiles: a man with his father's wife** · Exile · Apostolic · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Paul heard that a member of the Corinthian church was living with his father's wife and that the church, instead of mourning, was puffed up. He judged the case in absence: deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.

## Offense
Leviticus 18 and 20 and Deuteronomy 27 all name it, the last with a curse and the middle with death. Paul's rule follows the statute's shape: put away from among yourselves that wicked person. The church's tolerance was a second offense: a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.

## Judgment
Excommunicated by the assembly. The second letter shows the outcome: the punishment inflicted of many was sufficient, the man was overwhelmed with sorrow, and Paul urged them to forgive and comfort him and confirm their love toward him, lest Satan get an advantage. Banishment, then restoration on repentance.
