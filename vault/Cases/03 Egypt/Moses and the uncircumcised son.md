# Moses and the uncircumcised son
**Neglecting the sign of the covenant in his own house** · Reprieve · Egypt · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
On the road to Egypt to deliver Israel, the Lord met Moses at the inn and sought to kill him. Zipporah took a sharp stone, circumcised her son, and cast the foreskin at his feet. So he let him go.

## Offense
The covenant with Abraham required every male circumcised on the eighth day; the uncircumcised man-child was to be cut off from his people. The deliverer of Israel had not kept it in his own household, and it had to be set right before he could speak for God to Pharaoh.

## Judgment
The death sentence was stayed the moment the neglect was remedied. The narrative gives no further comment; the priority is the comment.
