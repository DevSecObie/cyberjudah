# Adoni-bezek
**Mutilating seventy kings** · Temporal judgment · Conquest · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Judah defeated the Canaanite king Adoni-bezek and cut off his thumbs and great toes. He said it himself: seventy kings with thumbs and toes cut off had gathered their meat under my table; as I have done, so God hath requited me.

## Offense
Systematic cruelty to defeated enemies, which he counted as his glory. The law of Israel limits punishment to measure, eye for eye and no more; he had exceeded all measure on seventy men.

## Judgment
The same mutilation, once. He was brought to Jerusalem and died there. A pagan king pronouncing the lex talionis on himself is the point of the record.
