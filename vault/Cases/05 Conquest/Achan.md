# Achan
**Taking devoted spoil from Jericho** · Put to death · Conquest · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
Jericho's spoil was placed under the ban, devoted wholly to God. Achan took a garment, silver, and a wedge of gold and hid them in his tent. Israel's next battle failed because of it.

## Offense
He 'saw, coveted, and took,' then buried the goods under his tent. One man's hidden theft brought defeat at Ai and the death of thirty-six soldiers before the cause was found by lot.

## Judgment
Achan and his household, with the stolen goods, were stoned and burned in the valley of Achor. The accursed thing, once purged, restored Israel's standing.
