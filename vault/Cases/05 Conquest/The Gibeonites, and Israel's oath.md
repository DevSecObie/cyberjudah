# The Gibeonites, and Israel's oath
**Obtaining a covenant by fraud; Israel making it without asking counsel of the Lord** · Cursed · Conquest · [[Cases index]]

## Summary
The Gibeonites came to Joshua in old clothes with moldy bread claiming to be from a far country, and Israel made peace with them. Three days later the fraud was found out. The oath stood anyway.

## Offense
On the Gibeonite side, a treaty by deception. On Israel's side, the text is explicit: the men took of their victuals, and asked not counsel at the mouth of the Lord. The covenant forbidden with the inhabitants of the land was made through negligence.

## Judgment
Because the princes had sworn by the Lord, the Gibeonites could not be killed, but were made hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation and the altar. Centuries later, when Saul broke that oath, God sent famine on Israel for it.
