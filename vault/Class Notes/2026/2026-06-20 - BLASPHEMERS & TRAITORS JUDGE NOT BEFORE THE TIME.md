## Introduction

Shalom, everybody, and happy Sabbath. Today's topic is entitled Blasphemers and Traitors: Judge Not Before the Time. This class is going to offend a lot of people, in the congregation now, those who have left, and those outside of our congregation; if the shoe fits, you wear it.

Definition read in class: blasphemy is the act of expressing contempt, scorn, or profound disrespect toward a deity, sacred objects, or religious beliefs. It encompasses insulting speech, writing, or actions directed at the divine. And blasphemy has levels; the word in its context has many different levels to it, and that is what we go through today.
