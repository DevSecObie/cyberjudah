## Introduction

Early morning Sabbath class from Captain Abiel, live from San Diego at 9:00 a.m. East Coast time, during the season of Passover while the worldwide blitz covers the four corners of the earth. The class opens with a mini movie built on Obadiah: I have made thee small among the nations, thou art greatly despised; though thou exalt thyself as the eagle, thence will I bring thee down; as thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee; but upon Mount Zion shall be deliverance, and the kingdom shall be the Lord's.

The question that drives the whole class: who is Esau, how did he become the greatly despised, and why? The class walks from the birth of Jacob and Esau through the birthright and the blessing, then through the receipts of history, decade by decade, asking one question over and over: why is Esau greatly despised?

This class is the companion to Captain Zephaniah's class Puerto Rico: The Flame of Resistance, which follows it the same morning.
