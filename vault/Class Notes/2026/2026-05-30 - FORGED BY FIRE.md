## Introduction

Class opened in Rochester, New York, with the reading of Psalm 116 at the trumpets: I love the Lord, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications; I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people.

The thumbnail showed gold being forged and poured, because we are not just in the fire; we are being turned into something. Something is being burned out of us to turn us into the next element. A soldier from the congregation set the frame for the class: forged by fire is the trials we go through that purify us, the tribulations that clean out the wickedness we have inside of us.

- For gold is tried in the fire. It is not just something burning; it is something being purged, being tried, being cleaned. Fire is a purifier, one of the best purifiers, and the Most High uses fire all through the Bible to purify, because it burns out the impurities, the bad elements, the things that will corrupt you. If he did not use the fire, you would not get cleaned. The metalworkers do the same thing: they heat up the elements to burn out the impurities and get the best element. That is us. To get the best you, the impurities have to be burned out.
- My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation: that is the qualifier. When you sign up to do the Lord's work, now you are in the furnace; now the fire turns on.
- Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure: because the fire never turns off. The impurities never stop; you are constantly being bombarded, constantly pressured to do the wrong thing. Look at social media; look at how the world is being turned upside down. Everything that is wrong in the earth right now is because the people over the earth are wicked. So if you find yourself at rest, that means you are not being purged; you are off the stove, you are cold; matter of fact, you are in the refrigerator.
- Make not haste in time of trouble: the fire has to stay on you to burn the impurities out. If you make haste and leave, you cool down and go back to the state you were in. And once you turn your back to him, who is going to turn the fire back on? Satan makes it easy for you; Satan gives you your heart's desire, everything you want. You want your coworker, you can have him; you like watching porn, here is your whole algorithm. The restraint is the fire: putting the phone down, putting blocks on the phone, throwing the phone in the trash if it got too bad for you. The fire is the restraint, turning away from evil.
- Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end. A brother answered what the increase at the last end is: getting the kingdom. That is why we go through this; the fire has a reason, the restraint has a reason. You have to go through this to get to the next level. There is no neutral gear; either you are moving forward or you are moving backwards, either you are hot and fervent or you are ice cold. Christ said pick one; if you are in the middle, you are going to die.
- Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully: whatever trial comes, take it cheerfully, because the trials have to come to purge it out of you. You go through trials and it feels like your family is against you; do you turn back, go eat the ham sandwich and open the Christmas gifts? Do you give up ten or fifteen years in this truth because you lost your job, so your family can welcome you back with open arms? When you give up on the brothers and sisters that labor in this truth with you, where are you going back to, and what is waiting for you when you get there? Absolutely nothing. Putting the ball down is easy; walking on and laboring with your brothers is the hard part, because we labor together.
- Unity is knowing my brother is going through it the same way I am going through it, and being able to lean on each other: hey, I know you lost your job; you good, your family good? If you need something, let me know. Imagine going through this together for ten years and I act like I do not know you when you are going through something. And if you walk away from the battlefield and leave your men on the field, in the military they declare you a deserter and put you to death, because leaving your people on the field is saying, I hope you die too.

## In Closing

Endure the chastening, stay in the body, keep the commandments, and come out the other side proven like fine gold, worthy of the kingdom.
