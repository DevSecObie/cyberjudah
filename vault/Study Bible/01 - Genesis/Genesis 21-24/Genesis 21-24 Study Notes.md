# Genesis 21: Isaac the Child of the Promise

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 21]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Genesis 21|Genesis 21]] through 24: the birth of Isaac, the offering on Moriah, the death of Sarah, and the taking of a wife for Isaac from Abraham's own kindred.

**[[Genesis 21#^v1|Genesis 21:1-6]]**

![[Genesis 21#^v1]]
![[Genesis 21#^v2]]
![[Genesis 21#^v3]]
![[Genesis 21#^v4]]
![[Genesis 21#^v5]]
![[Genesis 21#^v6]]

- The Lord visited Sarah as He had said, and she bare Isaac at the set time. Abraham was a hundred years old, and he circumcised Isaac at eight days as commanded.
- God hath made me to laugh: Isaac's name means he will laugh, for the story of a hundred year old father and a ninety year old mother is a joyful and comical thing.

**[[Genesis 21#^v8|Genesis 21:8]]**

![[Genesis 21#^v8]]

- The great feast the day Isaac was weaned was not a birthday celebration. Weaned means finished being breastfed, and the mothers gave their children suck about three years; Abraham was celebrating Isaac coming off his mother's breast at around three years old.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Maccabees 7#^v27|2 Maccabees 7:27]]**
    ![[2 Maccabees 7#^v27]]
    The mothers gave their sons suck three years; being weaned was a milestone at about that age, not a birthday.

**[[Genesis 21#^v9|Genesis 21:9-13]]**

![[Genesis 21#^v9]]
![[Genesis 21#^v10]]
![[Genesis 21#^v11]]
![[Genesis 21#^v12]]
![[Genesis 21#^v13]]

- Sarah saw the son of Hagar mocking and said, Cast out this bondwoman and her son; the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with Isaac.
- It was grievous to Abraham because he loved his firstborn, but God said, Hearken unto her voice, for in Isaac shall thy seed be called. Sarah was on point; Isaac is the child of the promise.
- Ishmael would still be made a nation, because he was Abraham's seed.

**[[Genesis 21#^v14|Genesis 21:14-19]]**

![[Genesis 21#^v14]]
![[Genesis 21#^v15]]
![[Genesis 21#^v16]]
![[Genesis 21#^v17]]
![[Genesis 21#^v18]]
![[Genesis 21#^v19]]

- When the water was spent, Hagar cast the child under a shrub and wept, thinking he would die of thirst. God heard the voice of the lad and opened her eyes to a well of water.
- The lad could not die there; the prophecy of chapter 16 stood over him, that he would become a great nation.

**[[Genesis 21#^v20|Genesis 21:20-21]]**

![[Genesis 21#^v20]]
![[Genesis 21#^v21]]

- Ishmael dwelt in the wilderness and became an archer; he learned the skills of combat and war, according to his nature.
- The word over his lineage stands: a wild people, his hand against every man and every man's hand against him, dwelling in the presence of all his brethren in the Middle East. His mother took him a wife out of Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 16#^v11|Genesis 16:11-12]]**
    ![[Genesis 16#^v11]]
    ![[Genesis 16#^v12]]
    The prophecy over Ishmael's posterity: a wild man, his hand against every man, dwelling in the presence of all his brethren.

![[Genesis 21#^v23]]

- Abraham reproved Abimelech over a well his servants had taken violently, and they made a covenant; the seven ewe lambs stood as a witness that Abraham digged the well.
- The place was called Beersheba, which means the well of the oath, because there they sware both of them.

# Genesis 22: The Offering of Isaac

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 22]]

**[[Genesis 22#^v1|Genesis 22:1-3]]**

![[Genesis 22#^v1]]
![[Genesis 22#^v2]]
![[Genesis 22#^v3]]

- God did tempt Abraham: Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac. Ishmael was alive, but the Most High counted Isaac the only son, because Ishmael was born of the flesh, of a plan the Lord never spoke, while Isaac was the child of the promise.
- Abraham rose up early in the morning and went. No hesitation, no debating with the word; that is obedience.

**[[Genesis 22#^v7|Genesis 22:7-8]]**

![[Genesis 22#^v7]]
![[Genesis 22#^v8]]

- Isaac asked, Where is the lamb for a burnt offering? Abraham answered, My son, God will provide himself a lamb. He trusted the Most High with what was missing.

**[[Genesis 22#^v9|Genesis 22:9-12]]**

![[Genesis 22#^v9]]
![[Genesis 22#^v10]]
![[Genesis 22#^v11]]
![[Genesis 22#^v12]]

- Abraham bound Isaac, laid him on the altar, and stretched forth his hand with the knife; the angel called out of heaven in the midst of it: Lay not thine hand upon the lad.
- Now I know that thou fearest God. The fear of God is proved in obedience; they that fear the Lord will not disobey His word.
- Abraham's inner faith carried him: he accounted that God was able to raise Isaac up, even from the dead, since the promise stood in Isaac who had no children yet. Faith and the commandments go together; you cannot have one without the other.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 2#^v15|Ecclesiasticus 2:15]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 2#^v15]]
    They that fear the Lord will not disobey His word; Abraham's fear was proved by his obedience.
  - **[[Hebrews 11#^v17|Hebrews 11:17-19]]**
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v17]]
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v18]]
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v19]]
    By faith Abraham offered up Isaac, accounting that God was able to raise him up even from the dead.

**[[Genesis 22#^v13|Genesis 22:13-14]]**

![[Genesis 22#^v13]]
![[Genesis 22#^v14]]

- Once the obedience was fulfilled, God provided the sacrifice: a ram caught in a thicket by his horns, offered in the stead of his son, just as Abraham had told Isaac. Abraham called the place Jehovahjireh: in the mount of the Lord it shall be seen.

**[[Genesis 22#^v15|Genesis 22:15-18]]**

![[Genesis 22#^v15]]
![[Genesis 22#^v16]]
![[Genesis 22#^v17]]
![[Genesis 22#^v18]]

- By myself have I sworn, saith the Lord: in multiplying I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heaven and as the sand upon the sea shore, and thy seed shall possess the gate of his enemies.
- The seed possessing the gate of his enemies is part of the covenant: the seed of Jacob shall possess the remnant of Edom and of all the heathen, and Edom shall be a possession. That is why all nations cannot be inside this covenant; the covenant has Israel possessing their enemies.
- In thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed: those nations are the nations of Israel, the twelve tribes.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Amos 9#^v11|Amos 9:11-12]]**
    ![[Amos 9#^v11]]
    ![[Amos 9#^v12]]
    The tabernacle of David is raised up, that they may possess the remnant of Edom and of all the heathen.
  - **[[Numbers 24#^v17|Numbers 24:17-18]]**
    ![[Numbers 24#^v17]]
    ![[Numbers 24#^v18]]
    A Star out of Jacob and a Sceptre out of Israel; Edom shall be a possession, and Israel shall do valiantly.

# Genesis 24: A Wife for Isaac

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 24]]

**[[Genesis 24#^v1|Genesis 24:1-4]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v1]]
![[Genesis 24#^v2]]
![[Genesis 24#^v3]]
![[Genesis 24#^v4]]

- Abraham made his eldest servant swear that he would not take a wife for Isaac of the daughters of the Canaanites, but go to Abraham's country and kindred. The Most High never condoned marrying into the other nations; the custom was kept from the beginning, way before Moses and [[Deuteronomy 7#^v3|Deuteronomy 7:3]].

  Precepts:
  - **[[Tobit 4#^v12|Tobit 4:12]]**
    ![[Tobit 4#^v12]]
    Take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, as Noah, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob did from the beginning, and their seed shall inherit the land.

**[[Genesis 24#^v7|Genesis 24:7]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v7]]

- Abraham told the servant, The Lord God of heaven shall send His angel before thee. He trusted the oath would be fulfilled because the Most High goes before the errand.

**[[Genesis 24#^v12|Genesis 24:12-16]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v12]]
![[Genesis 24#^v13]]
![[Genesis 24#^v14]]
![[Genesis 24#^v15]]
![[Genesis 24#^v16]]

- The servant prayed for a sign at the well: the damsel who offers to draw for the camels also is the one appointed. Before he had done speaking, Rebekah came out.
- Rebekah was very fair to look upon, a virgin, neither had any man known her: undefiled and beautiful, the kind of wife the forefathers had from the beginning.

**[[Genesis 24#^v26|Genesis 24:26-27]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v26]]
![[Genesis 24#^v27]]

- When the sign came to pass exactly, the servant bowed his head and worshipped the Lord: I being in the way, the Lord led me to the house of my master's brethren.

**[[Genesis 24#^v58|Genesis 24:58-60]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v58]]
![[Genesis 24#^v59]]
![[Genesis 24#^v60]]

- Rebekah said, I will go. Her family blessed her: Be thou the mother of thousands of millions, and let thy seed possess the gate of those which hate them.
- The covenant narrows again here: it runs through Rebekah's children, and of her two sons it is Jacob who carries it, for Esau sold his birthright. The covenants in Genesis cannot be broken.

**[[Genesis 24#^v63|Genesis 24:63-65]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v63]]
![[Genesis 24#^v64]]
![[Genesis 24#^v65]]

- Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide; that was a custom of the forefathers, showing the Lord was dealing with them long before the law was written at Sinai.
- When Rebekah saw Isaac she lighted off the camel, and she took a vail and covered herself. The four mothers covered their heads with actual material as a sign of submission; that is the custom [[1 Corinthians 11|1 Corinthians 11]] goes into.

**[[Genesis 24#^v66|Genesis 24:66-67]]**

![[Genesis 24#^v66]]
![[Genesis 24#^v67]]

- Isaac brought Rebekah into his mother's tent, and she became his wife, and he loved her; and Isaac was comforted after his mother's death, for he received a righteous wife like unto his mother.
