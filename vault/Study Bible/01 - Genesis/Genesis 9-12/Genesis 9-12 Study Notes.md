# Genesis 9: The Laws Before Moses and the Covenant of the Bow

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 9]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Genesis 9|Genesis 9]] through 12, read with precepts as the chapters go. The laws you meet in these chapters stand from the beginning, long before Moses.

**[[Genesis 9#^v1|Genesis 9:1]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v1]]

- Noah and his sons were told to replenish the earth after the flood, and the earth is replenished with righteous children; a multitude of ungodly children profits nothing.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 16#^v1|Ecclesiasticus 16:1-3]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 16#^v1]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 16#^v2]]

**[[Genesis 9#^v2|Genesis 9:2]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v2]]

- The fear and dread of man upon the beasts is not how it was in the beginning, and not how it will remain; the original relationship between man and the animals will be restored.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Isaiah 11#^v6|Isaiah 11:6-9]]**

**[[Genesis 9#^v3|Genesis 9:3]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v3]]

- This is the introduction of man eating meat. The Most High did not give man a new stomach; the body was already made for it and it lay dormant until after the flood.
- Having the liberty to eat meat does not mean eating without balance; be not too greedy upon meats, for excess of meats brings sickness.
- The law of what may be eaten was already understood; you could not eat everything. The doctrine that says you can pray over anything and eat it is a seducing spirit and a doctrine of devils.
- Fish is meat; all flesh is not the same flesh, but the flesh of beasts, of fishes, and of birds are all flesh. Every creature of God is good for what it was made for, and it is sanctified by the word of God, and the word that sanctifies what may be eaten is the dietary law.

**[[Genesis 9#^v5|Genesis 9:5]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v5]]

- The Most High requiring blood at the hand of every beast is animal sacrifice, still in place with Noah, passed down generation to generation.
- The judgment for sin has always been death. When Eve sinned and caused Adam to sin, either they had to die, or something had to stand in their place; the blood of the animal stood in for them.
- That is the scapegoat: it takes the wrongdoings, mistakes, and faults of others and places them on the animal. That is why Cain and Abel were bringing offerings, because they knew of the sacrifice from [[Genesis 3|Genesis 3]].

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 3#^v21|Genesis 3:21]]**
    ![[Genesis 3#^v21]]

    ![[Genesis 4#^v4]]

**[[Genesis 9#^v6|Genesis 9:6]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v6]]

- Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed: life for life. This law is way before Moses, so it cannot be dismissed as the law of Moses.
- The laws were never done away with; Christ came not to destroy the law but to fulfil, and not one jot or tittle passes from the law.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Matthew 5#^v17|Matthew 5:17-18]]**
    ![[Matthew 5#^v17]]
    ![[Matthew 5#^v18]]
    Christ came not to destroy the law or the prophets; not one jot or one tittle shall pass from the law.

**[[Genesis 9#^v9|Genesis 9:9-11]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v9]]
![[Genesis 9#^v10]]
![[Genesis 9#^v11]]

- The covenant established here is with Noah, his seed, and every living creature: all flesh shall never again be cut off by the waters of a flood.
- This is a general covenant with all flesh. The covenant with Israel is found in [[Jeremiah 31|Jeremiah 31]].

![[Genesis 9#^v13]]

- The world has taken that same bow, the token of the covenant, and turned it into the symbol of sodomy. The spiritual eyes will see what that means: the great city is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 43#^v11|Ecclesiasticus 43:11-12]]**

    ![[Revelation 11#^v8]]

**[[Genesis 9#^v14|Genesis 9:14-15]]**

![[Genesis 9#^v14]]
![[Genesis 9#^v15]]

- The waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh. The next time the destruction is not water but fire, when the day of the Lord comes and the elements melt with fervent heat.

![[Genesis 9#^v18]]
![[Genesis 9#^v19]]

- Shem, Ham, and Japheth went forth out of the ark and the whole earth was overspread from them. Ham dwelt in what is called Africa; Shem in the land mass toward Arabia; Japheth went into Europe.
- Ham is the father of Canaan, and Canaan dwelt in the land later called the land of Israel. That is how you know the land of Israel is a part of Africa; the maps separate Israel from Africa so that the connection is not made.

- Understand Noah's heart: he preached repentance for around a hundred years, and everyone he knew outside his house drowned. Strong drink is for him that is ready to perish and wine for those of heavy hearts; Noah drank too much, but he remained righteous in the eyes of the Most High.
- Ham saw the nakedness of his father and did not cover him; Shem and Japheth went backward and covered their father without looking. The commandments concerning nakedness were being applied; [[Leviticus 18|Leviticus 18]] opens those laws.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Peter 2#^v5|2 Peter 2:5]]**

    Noah was found perfect and righteous and was left as a remnant unto the earth.
  - **[[Proverbs 24#^v16|Proverbs 24:16]]**

    A just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again.
  - **[[Proverbs 31#^v6|Proverbs 31:6-7]]**

    ![[Proverbs 31#^v7]]

- Blessed be the Lord God of Shem: it was revealed that the Messiah and the righteous seed would come through Shem.

# Genesis 10: Shem, Ham, Japheth, and Nimrod

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 10]]

**[[Genesis 10#^v1|Genesis 10:1-5]]**

![[Genesis 10#^v1]]
![[Genesis 10#^v2]]
![[Genesis 10#^v3]]
![[Genesis 10#^v4]]
![[Genesis 10#^v5]]

- The sons of Japheth settled what became Europe. Ashkenaz is a Hebrew name for Germany; Tarshish is Spain; Kittim is Rome; Javan is Greece; you can see Meshech, Tubal, and Magog on the old maps.
- These were the isles of the Gentiles, divided in their lands, every one after his tongue.

**[[Genesis 10#^v6|Genesis 10:6-7]]**

![[Genesis 10#^v6]]
![[Genesis 10#^v7]]

- The sons of Ham: Cush is Ethiopia; Mizraim is Egypt; Phut is Libya, to the left of Egypt; and Canaan is the land where Jerusalem sits.
- Ham inhabited what is known as Africa, and Canaan's land is the land of Israel; so the land of Israel is part of Africa.
- The Israelites are of Shem, not of Ham; the children of Israel are Shemites scattered among the children of Ham in Africa.

**[[Genesis 10#^v8|Genesis 10:8-11]]**

![[Genesis 10#^v8]]
![[Genesis 10#^v9]]
![[Genesis 10#^v10]]
![[Genesis 10#^v11]]

- Cush begat Nimrod, the mighty hunter, and the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh in the land of Shinar; Shinar is what is known as Iraq. Out of that land Asshur, the Assyrians, built Nineveh.
- History records that Nimrod was the first king of Nineveh; the name Nineveh means the habitation of Ninus, which was Nimrod. He was the first mortal deified at his death, the father of the gods, and that pagan practice of deifying kings spread from Mesopotamia to Egypt and beyond.
- Nimrod, his queen Semiramis, and her child Tammuz became the first pagan trinity, and their worship spread under different names through every nation: Baal, Bel, Zeus, Jupiter, Ra, Thor; Ishtar, Ashtaroth, Isis, Diana.
- Sunday sacredness, Christmas, the Christmas tree, Easter, the Easter egg, and Good Friday have their origin in that Babylonian sun worship, not in the Old and New Testaments. This is why the scriptures must be studied precept upon precept, so ancient paganism mixed in is not mistaken for the truth.
- The origins of these places, Babel, Erech, Accad, Shinar, still sit on the eastern side of the world, which is why the history reads like a fairy tale to a people carried to the western side with none of it to see.

**[[Genesis 10#^v15|Genesis 10:15-19]]**

![[Genesis 10#^v15]]
![[Genesis 10#^v16]]
![[Genesis 10#^v17]]
![[Genesis 10#^v18]]
![[Genesis 10#^v19]]

- The border of the Canaanites reached unto Sodom and Gomorrah, and Admah and Zeboim; when Sodom and Gomorrah were destroyed, those cities went into destruction with them. Deuteronomy names the cities together.

**[[Genesis 10#^v21|Genesis 10:21]]**

![[Genesis 10#^v21]]

- Shem is the father of all the children of Eber, and Eber is where the name Hebrew comes from.

# Genesis 11: Babel and the Separation of the Nations

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 11]]

**[[Genesis 11#^v1|Genesis 11:1-4]]**

![[Genesis 11#^v1]]
![[Genesis 11#^v2]]
![[Genesis 11#^v3]]
![[Genesis 11#^v4]]

- The whole earth was of one language, and they journeyed from the east to the plain of Shinar. Coming from Jerusalem eastward lands you in Shinar, in Babylon.
- They built the tower so they would not be scattered abroad; the fear of the flood was still on them, and they wanted to make themselves a name.

**[[Genesis 11#^v5|Genesis 11:5-9]]**

![[Genesis 11#^v5]]
![[Genesis 11#^v6]]
![[Genesis 11#^v7]]
![[Genesis 11#^v8]]
![[Genesis 11#^v9]]

- The people being one was not going to produce righteousness; nothing would be restrained from what they imagined to do, and it would be evil, so the Most High confounded their language and scattered them.
- There has never been a doctrine of all nations holding hands as one in the scriptures. The Most High divided the nations their inheritance and separated the sons of Adam, setting the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel.
- The man of sin opposes this: he exalts himself above all that is called God, gathers all nations to himself as one, and deifies himself, making God and the Messiah after his own image. That is the same thing Nimrod did in the land of Shinar.
- After Babel the Most High sent every family back to where it was supposed to be: Ham to his place, Shem to his, Japheth to his.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 32#^v7|Deuteronomy 32:7-9]]**

    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v8]]
    ![[Deuteronomy 32#^v9]]
    The Most High divided the nations and separated the sons of Adam by the number of the children of Israel; Jacob is His portion.
  - **[[2 Thessalonians 2#^v3|2 Thessalonians 2:3-4]]**
    ![[2 Thessalonians 2#^v3]]
    ![[2 Thessalonians 2#^v4]]
    The man of sin exalts himself above all that is called God, sitting as God; the spirit of Nimrod all over again.

- Each of these generations begat sons and daughters for hundreds of years; the earth is being populated again all through this chapter.

# Genesis 12: The Calling of Abram

**[[Genesis 12#^v1|Genesis 12:1]]**

![[Genesis 12#^v1]]

- The Most High called Abram to get out of his country, from his kindred, and from his father's house. He called Abraham alone, and blessed him, and increased him.
- Some hold back from repenting because of what family or friends may say. Do not let your kindred hold you back from being blessed and increased; when the Most High calls you out, you have to go.
- Confessing Christ before men means something. When Christ asked, Whom say ye that I am, Peter confessed, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God; whoever confesses Him before men, He confesses before His Father, and He came to set a man at variance against his own household for the truth's sake.
