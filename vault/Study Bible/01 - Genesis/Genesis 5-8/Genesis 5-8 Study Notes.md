# Genesis 5: The Book of the Generations of Adam

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 5]]

## Introduction

This reading covers [[Genesis 5|Genesis 5]] through 8, read straight through, with the key points opened after the reading. The 365 reading is to give faith through hearing the word of the Most High.

**[[Romans 10#^v17|Romans 10:17]]**

![[Romans 10#^v17]]

- The daily reading of the scriptures gives faith through hearing the word of the Most High.

**[[Genesis 5#^v1|Genesis 5:1-2]]**

![[Genesis 5#^v1]]
![[Genesis 5#^v2]]

- The words made he him speak of the first Adam. This Adam was made in the likeness of God, a direct descendant of the Most High.
- Male and female created he them, and called their name Adam: they were not that Adam; these were the other people on the earth. When the Most High creates a fish He does not create only a salmon; there is mackerel and tuna and all types of fish. In the same way, He created Adam and He created the other people.
- Chapter 5 is the direct lineage of Adam. That lineage is the sons of God you read of in chapter 6, and the later chapters of Genesis tie the Israelites directly to the lineage of Adam through Shem.
- When Christ said, Ye are gods, He was saying we come from that line of Adam.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 1#^v26|Genesis 1:26-27]]**
    ![[Genesis 1#^v26]]
    ![[Genesis 1#^v27]]
    The man made in our image, created he him, is the first Adam; male and female created he them is the other people.
  - **[[Luke 3#^v36|Luke 3:36-38]]**
    ![[Luke 3#^v36]]
    ![[Luke 3#^v37]]
    ![[Luke 3#^v38]]
    Christ's lineage on his mother's side runs back through Seth to Adam, the son of God.

**[[Genesis 5#^v24|Genesis 5:24]]**

![[Genesis 5#^v24]]

- Enoch walked with God and was not: the Most High took him up, and Enoch did not taste death, the same way Elijah did not taste death.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Kings 2#^v9|2 Kings 2:9-11]]**
    ![[2 Kings 2#^v9]]
    ![[2 Kings 2#^v10]]
    ![[2 Kings 2#^v11]]
    Elijah was taken up by a whirlwind into heaven; that is what it means that God took Enoch.
  - **[[Acts 1#^v9|Acts 1:9]]**
    ![[Acts 1#^v9]]
    Christ was taken up and received in a cloud; taken up means received by the Most High.

**[[Genesis 5#^v32|Genesis 5:32]]**

![[Genesis 5#^v32]]

- Noah was five hundred years old here, and he was six hundred when the flood came ([[Genesis 7#^v6|Genesis 7:6]]), so Noah preached for around a hundred years, give or take.

# Genesis 6: The Sons of God and the Preparing of the Ark

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 6]]

**[[Genesis 6#^v1|Genesis 6:1-2]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v1]]
![[Genesis 6#^v2]]

- The men who began to multiply on the face of the earth were of the other nations; they were not the direct descendants of Adam. The sons of God are the lineage of Adam laid out in chapter 5; that is the distinction between the sons of God and the daughters of men.
- The law against marrying into the other nations began here in Genesis. It was already known, and Moses had to reiterate that same law to the children of Israel when they came over Jordan.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Deuteronomy 7#^v3|Deuteronomy 7:3]]**
    ![[Deuteronomy 7#^v3]]
    Moses reiterated to the sons of God the law against taking wives of the other nations.

**[[Genesis 6#^v3|Genesis 6:3]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v3]]

- Here the Most High shortened the lifespan of man to a hundred and twenty years. In chapter 5 the forefathers lived seven, eight, and nine hundred years.

**[[Genesis 6#^v4|Genesis 6:4]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v4]]

- The giants in the earth came from the sons of God intermingling with the other nations, and the children born to them became mighty men of renown.
- The angels which kept not their first estate are these same men of [[Genesis 6#^v4|Genesis 6:4]]; they came from the lineage of Adam, and their first estate was sons of God. Another word for angel is messenger.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Jude 1#^v6|Jude 1:6]]**
    ![[Jude 1#^v6]]
    The angels left their first estate as sons of God, and the Most High reserved them unto judgment.

**[[Genesis 6#^v8|Genesis 6:8-9]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v8]]
![[Genesis 6#^v9]]

- Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord; he was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.
- Without Noah none of us would be here; Shem, Ham, and Japheth repopulated the earth after the flood.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v17|Ecclesiasticus 44:17-18]]**
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v17]]
    ![[Ecclesiasticus 44#^v18]]
    Noah was found perfect and righteous and was left as a remnant unto the earth when the flood came.

**[[Genesis 6#^v14|Genesis 6:14]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v14]]

- The ark was a literal boat, made of gopher wood.

**[[Genesis 6#^v21|Genesis 6:21]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v21]]

- At this time the animals were vegetarian; that is why the Most High explained to Noah to gather food for himself, for his family, and for the animals, and why the animals did not eat one another on the ark.

**[[Genesis 6#^v22|Genesis 6:22]]**

![[Genesis 6#^v22]]

- Noah did all that the Most High commanded him by faith. Up to that time rain had never been seen; it takes faith to build an ark for a thing that has never happened.
- While the ark was preparing, Noah was a preacher of righteousness, telling his people to repent, and the longsuffering of the Most High waited for them to repent.
- Eight souls were saved by water; the ones that were actually in the water died, so this is not talking about getting baptized.
- As Noah prepared the ark to the saving of his house, we must prepare our households for when Christ comes back; that takes the same faith. In the days of Noah they were eating, drinking, and marrying until Noah entered the ark and there was no more chance to repent; so shall the coming of the Son of man be. We have to put on the spirit of Noah.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Hebrews 11#^v1|Hebrews 11:1]]**
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v1]]
    Faith is the evidence of things not seen; with Noah the thing not seen was rain.
  - **[[Hebrews 11#^v6|Hebrews 11:6-7]]**
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v6]]
    ![[Hebrews 11#^v7]]
    By faith Noah, warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear and prepared an ark to the saving of his house.
  - **[[1 Peter 3#^v20|1 Peter 3:20]]**
    ![[1 Peter 3#^v20]]
    The longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah while the ark was preparing, and eight souls were saved by water.
  - **[[2 Peter 2#^v5|2 Peter 2:5]]**
    ![[2 Peter 2#^v5]]
    Noah was the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness.
  - **[[Matthew 24#^v37|Matthew 24:37-39]]**
    ![[Matthew 24#^v37]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v38]]
    ![[Matthew 24#^v39]]
    As the days of Noah were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.

# Genesis 7: The First Rain and the Flood

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 7]]

**[[Genesis 7#^v2|Genesis 7:2-3]]**

![[Genesis 7#^v2]]
![[Genesis 7#^v3]]

- The dietary law was not yet instituted, but the Most High had already defined what was a clean beast and what was an unclean beast, all the way back in Genesis; Moses reiterated that law to the children of Israel.
- Today this gets confused with the doctrine that says I can eat all things through Christ who strengthens me, and that is not true; the laws were instituted for us to discern between the unclean and the clean.
- The beasts went in male and female to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth, so that they could repopulate once the earth dried up; nature itself tells you what we should and should not be doing.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Leviticus 11#^v45|Leviticus 11:45-47]]**
    ![[Leviticus 11#^v45]]
    ![[Leviticus 11#^v46]]
    ![[Leviticus 11#^v47]]
    The law of the beasts makes a difference between the unclean and the clean, the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.
  - **[[Ezekiel 44#^v23|Ezekiel 44:23]]**
    ![[Ezekiel 44#^v23]]
    The people are taught to discern between the unclean and the clean; that is what the laws were instituted for.

**[[Genesis 7#^v4|Genesis 7:4]]**

![[Genesis 7#^v4]]

- Up until this point it had never rained on the earth; a mist went up from the earth and watered the ground. Understand the faith Noah had to have, being told to build an ark because it was going to rain, a thing that had never happened.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Genesis 2#^v5|Genesis 2:5-6]]**
    ![[Genesis 2#^v5]]
    ![[Genesis 2#^v6]]
    The Most High had not caused it to rain upon the earth; a mist watered the whole face of the ground.

**[[Genesis 7#^v11|Genesis 7:11]]**

![[Genesis 7#^v11]]

- The great deep is the seas, the rivers, and the oceans. The windows of heaven are the sky, the firmament, and their opening is the rain.
- This is the first time it ever rained on the earth, and when the rain came it came with destruction.

**[[Genesis 7#^v21|Genesis 7:21-23]]**

![[Genesis 7#^v21]]
![[Genesis 7#^v22]]
![[Genesis 7#^v23]]

- This was the first time the Most High destroyed the earth for the wickedness that was being done. The flood came because they despised the commandments; destruction came because of sin.
- Of them that dwelt in the world only Noah and his household were left, of whom came all righteous men: through Shem's seed to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and through their line the Israelites.
- The breath of life is twofold. One is the commandments, which were given to Adam; the other is the breathing life, the oxygen. Here everything on the dry land that breathed died; the fish were not touched.

  Precepts:
  - **[[2 Esdras 3#^v8|2 Esdras 3:8-11]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 3#^v8]]
    ![[2 Esdras 3#^v9]]
    ![[2 Esdras 3#^v10]]
    ![[2 Esdras 3#^v11]]
    The flood came upon them that despised the commandments, and Noah alone was left with his household, of whom came all righteous men.
  - **[[2 Esdras 3#^v5|2 Esdras 3:5]]**
    ![[2 Esdras 3#^v5]]
    The Most High breathed into Adam the breath of life; that breath is the commandments.
  - **[[Proverbs 7#^v2|Proverbs 7:2]]**
    ![[Proverbs 7#^v2]]
    Keep my commandments, and live; the commandments are the breath of life.

# Genesis 8: The Earth Dried and the Covenant

Read the chapter → [[Genesis 8]]

**[[Genesis 8#^v2|Genesis 8:2]]**

![[Genesis 8#^v2]]

- The windows of heaven being stopped and the rain from heaven being restrained shows you that the windows of heaven refer to the rain.

**[[Genesis 8#^v20|Genesis 8:20-22]]**

![[Genesis 8#^v20]]
![[Genesis 8#^v21]]
![[Genesis 8#^v22]]

- Noah built an altar and offered burnt offerings of every clean beast and every clean fowl, and the Most High smelled a sweet savour and said He would not again curse the ground any more for man's sake.
- An everlasting covenant was made with Noah that all flesh should perish no more by a flood. Before it was water; the next time it is going to be fire, so let us get our minds right.
