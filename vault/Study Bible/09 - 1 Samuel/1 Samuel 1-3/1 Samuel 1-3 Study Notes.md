# 1 Samuel 1: Hannah's Vow and the Birth of Samuel

Read the chapter → [[1 Samuel 1]]

## Introduction

This session covers [[Ruth 4|Ruth 4]] together with 1 Samuel 1-3; these notes cover [[1 Samuel 1|1 Samuel 1]] through 3. With first Samuel the class gets into the history of Israel, our people, as we move through the Bible. The main threads of the teaching: Hannah barren and provoked by her adversary, the vow of the Nazarite she vowed for a man child, the petition granted according to the will of the Most High, the vow paid when Samuel is lent to the Lord all the days of his life, Hannah's prophecy that they that stumbled are girded with strength, the sons of Eli who were sons of Belial and knew not the Lord, the judgment on the house of Eli for honouring his sons above the Most High, and the Lord coming and standing to reveal himself to Samuel, the same way he stands at the door of the spirit of all Israel and knocks.

- Hannah answered, No, my lord, I am a woman of a sorrowful spirit: I have drunk neither wine nor strong drink, but have poured out my soul before the Lord; count not thine handmaid for a daughter of Belial. Then Eli answered, Go in peace: and the God of Israel grant thee thy petition that thou hast asked of him.
- So the woman went her way, and did eat, and her countenance was no more sad. What did she do? She believed when she heard the word of Eli.
- Why did the Lord grant her petition? It was the will of the Most High for Samuel to come through Hannah; it was just in his time. And she was granted it because of what she did before she even asked: she kept his commandments and did those things that are pleasing in his sight.

**[[1 Samuel 2#^v3|1 Samuel 2:3]]**

![[1 Samuel 2#^v3]]

- Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed. What the Lord weighs is actions: if it came out of your mouth, are you going to do it?
- All of us in this walk, keeping the commandments and the faith in Christ, have taken covenant and proclaimed out of our mouths that we are going to endure to the end; we broke the bread and drank the wine and took the covenant with the Lord. Now the Lord is judging you by your actions: are you going to endure to the end, whether to Christ's return or until you leave this earth, if you truly love him?

  Precepts:
  - **[[Luke 6#^v46|Luke 6:46]]**
    ![[Luke 6#^v46]]
    Why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say? You cannot just say you love the Lord, that you believe in the God of Israel, and do nothing of what he says: not loving your neighbour, holding grudges and envy, respecting leadership above your own husband who is Christ in the house.
  - **[[Hebrews 10#^v7|Hebrews 10:7]]**
    ![[Hebrews 10#^v7]]
    Lo, I come, in the volume of the book it is written of me, to do thy will, O God. The letters of Paul, the book of Samuel, the book of Genesis, the laws of Moses: everything we read ultimately leads up to the life of Christ and his return, and all of it is the spirit of Christ telling you to do those things.

    ![[Matthew 12#^v36]]
    ![[Matthew 12#^v37]]

Make sure what comes out of your mouth is calculated; that whole "we were arguing and it just slipped" will not fly on judgment day. Repent, and make a door and a bar for thy mouth, as the scriptures say.

- "I have a personal relationship with God; God knows my heart; God loves me no matter what": that is arrogancy coming out of the mouth. The scripture says he loves you if you keep the commandments.

- They were Levites, priests in the house of the Lord doing the work of the Lord, and yet it says they knew not the Lord. Why? Because they did not keep the commandments true in their spirit; they kept it on the surface where you can see it. They looked the part: they had on the fringes, they had the beard, they showed up on the sabbath day; but their spirit was not right with the Lord, and they were really the sons of the devil. Do not let that be you, Israel.

They would dip it in, and what came up was the priest's portion of meat for that day; all the Levites did that, based on the sacrifices Israel brought in.
- This is how the Lord took care of Levi. He told Levi in the law that Levi would have no inheritance of land among the tribes; the Lord provided for them through the sacrifices that Israel brought, so they had need of nothing.

That is talking about Levi, going into Moses. Did I choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be my priest, to offer upon mine altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before me? and did I give unto the house of thy father all the offerings made by fire of the children of Israel? You are disrespecting me, is what he is saying: I am supposed to be honoured in everything before the children of Israel, and you let your sons disrespect my offerings.

Can they do no wrong, even when they are doing real wrong against the Lord, keeping no commandments, never showing up on the sabbath day, and still living under your roof grown? If they are of age, they know right from wrong, and they are under your roof while you keep the commandments, then they had better be keeping the commandments: showing up to every feast day, the blowing of trumpets, the day of atonement, the passover, the same as you. When they are of age they can go their own way; but they should not be living under your roof and breaking the commandments of God. You honour your children above God when you allow that.

# 1 Samuel 3: The Lord Reveals Himself to Samuel

![[1 Samuel 3#^v1]]

- The child Samuel ministered unto the Lord before Eli. And the word of the Lord was precious in those days; there was no open vision. Precious means the Lord was not dealing with everybody: he was not dealing with the priests, and there were no prophets around saying, thus saith the Lord. The word was about to go only to Samuel.

**[[1 Samuel 3#^v2|1 Samuel 3:2-3]]**

- Eli was laid down in his place, his eyes beginning to wax dim, and ere the lamp of God went out in the temple of the Lord, where the ark of God was, Samuel was laid down to sleep. Ere means before: before the lamp of God went out. That light in the temple by the ark was never to go out, though at a point in time it did go out.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Exodus 27#^v20|Exodus 27:20-21]]**
    ![[Exodus 27#^v20]]
    ![[Exodus 27#^v21]]
    The children of Israel brought pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamp to burn always; Aaron and his sons ordered it from evening to morning before the Lord, a statute for ever.

- Christ spoke about that fire in the temple: on the sabbath days the priests in the temple profane the sabbath, and are blameless. Israel was not to kindle a fire on the sabbath when it comes to food, but the priests had to keep that fire burning, and they sacrificed on the sabbath days, a lamb in the morning and a lamb in the evening; they broke the sabbath yet are blameless, because they were doing thus saith the Lord.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Matthew 12#^v5|Matthew 12:5-7]]**
    ![[Matthew 12#^v5]]
    ![[Matthew 12#^v6]]
    ![[Matthew 12#^v7]]
    Have ye not read in the law, how that on the sabbath days the priests in the temple profane the sabbath, and are blameless? In this place is one greater than the temple; and if ye had known what this meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice, ye would not have condemned the guiltless.
  - **[[Numbers 28#^v9|Numbers 28:9-10]]**
    ![[Numbers 28#^v9]]
    ![[Numbers 28#^v10]]
    The burnt offering of every sabbath: the priests sacrificed a lamb in the morning and in the evening on the sabbath because the Lord commanded it.

- Sacrifice was never the most important thing to the Lord; not the blood of bulls and of goats, but mercy upon one another and the keeping of the commandments. The sacrifices were added because of transgression, the animal's blood taken in your place so the Most High would not have to kill you; you read of it from [[Genesis 3|Genesis 3]], where the Lord took the skins after Adam sinned. Yet there were some things you could not sacrifice for, abominations for which a man's own blood was required.

    I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings. This is what Christ was quoting, for the New Testament was not yet written; the knowledge of God is the keeping of the commandments.

- The Lord called Samuel, and he answered, Here am I, and ran unto Eli; and Eli said, I called not; lie down again. The Lord called yet again, and again Samuel went to Eli. Now Samuel did not yet know the Lord, neither was the word of the Lord yet revealed unto him.
- Look at that. Samuel had been in the house of the Lord since he was weaned, three or four years old, and he still did not know the Lord. This goes in on those playing church with their children: just because your children come and sit and listen to the teaching does not mean they know the Lord; they are there because you brought them there. They can hear all the words they want, but until the Lord reveals himself to them, they do not know him. What makes your kid so special, greater than Samuel?
- Do not have your children going into a covenant with Christ that he will judge them for if they break it; the scripture says, for this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep. You do not know if your kids are going to serve the Lord when they go out on their own, or what kind of lusts are in them that will draw them away into the world when they come of age. It is your job as a parent to train them up and pray they stay in it: kids learn by watching, so let them see you take that covenant over and over again until they are ready, and make them rehearse the righteous acts. When the child comes to the age of reasoning and knows the Lord for himself, and the Lord reveals himself to him, you will know it; do not rush it.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Proverbs 22#^v6|Proverbs 22:6]]**
    ![[Proverbs 22#^v6]]
    Train up a child in the way he should go: your job is the training; the revealing belongs to the Most High.
  - **[[1 Corinthians 11#^v29|1 Corinthians 11:29-30]]**
    ![[1 Corinthians 11#^v29]]
    ![[1 Corinthians 11#^v30]]
    He that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh damnation to himself; for this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep. That covenant is not to be forced on children who do not yet know the Lord.

- No one knows the Father until Christ reveals him, because Christ is the mediator. That is why our brothers and sisters caught up in Christianity and Islam do not know the Most High: he has not been revealed to them, and they have not been shown who they are. The word comes down in order, from the Most High to Christ, to the angels, and into our spirit when it is time to wake up; the Most High does not deal with us directly right now, because we are too full of sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[[Matthew 11#^v27|Matthew 11:27]]**
    ![[Matthew 11#^v27]]
    No man knoweth the Son, but the Father; neither knoweth any man the Father, save the Son, and he to whomsoever the Son will reveal him.
  - **[[John 14#^v26|John 14:26]]**
    ![[John 14#^v26]]
    The Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance.
  - **[[Revelation 1#^v1|Revelation 1:1]]**
    ![[Revelation 1#^v1]]
    The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, and he sent and signified it by his angel unto his servant John: that is the order, from the Most High to Christ to the angels to the servants; that is how the word comes down and how Israel wakes up.

- The Lord called Samuel again the third time, and Eli perceived that the Lord had called the child; therefore Eli said unto Samuel, Go, lie down: and it shall be, if he call thee, that thou shalt say, Speak, Lord; for thy servant heareth.
- The Lord is merciful with us even in these days, in this wickedness: he calls out to us numerous times until we wake up. We do not know who is chosen, so we never know when we are going to wake up; it might be the first time, the second time, or the third time you hear the voice of the Lord calling. Some heard the call the first time because the Most High ordained them to be built up as leaders, to get the spirits of the world off of them so they could be a righteous example and a fit guide for you; you came in at the third call, after they were prepared. Your job is not to try to supersede those that came in before you, but to follow them; God set it up that way for a reason.

**[[1 Samuel 3#^v10|1 Samuel 3:10]]**

- And the Lord came and stood, and called as at other times, Samuel, Samuel. The Lord did not just call from the heavens: the Lord came down and stood by Samuel and called out to him. That is cold-blooded. Samuel could not see him, but he heard him; that is what happens in the spirit to all of us.
- Notice in the scriptures that the Most High calls the name two times: Moses, Moses; Samuel, Samuel. That may be a question to ask Christ, Lord's will, for him that endures to the end.
- What was happening spiritually when the Most High was revealing himself to Samuel and about to pour his word into him? The same thing that happens to all Israel: he stands at the door and knocks. That door is your spirit. The knocking is the Lord calling out through the videos, the flyers, the four chapters a day, the classes; that is the Lord standing and knocking at the spirit of all Israel before he cuts you off.

Opening the door is repentance: put away the drugs, the whoredom, and the ways of the world, apply thus saith the Lord, and you will be full of the Lord.

That is exactly what was read of Samuel: the word of the Lord was not yet revealed to him, and when the Lord called and he turned, the word was made known to him. But because I have called, and ye refused, I also will laugh at your calamity.

We do not know when he will cut a man off and leave him to his own devices to destroy himself; and when he cries, the Lord will not hear.

- The Lord said to Samuel, Behold, I will do a thing in Israel, at which both the ears of every one that heareth it shall tingle. In that day I will perform against Eli all things which I have spoken concerning his house: when I begin, I will also make an end. For I have told him that I will judge his house for ever for the iniquity which he knoweth; because his sons made themselves vile, and he restrained them not. Eli let his children break the commandments of the Lord and lead the people astray, and he restrained them not.
- Therefore I have sworn unto the house of Eli, that the iniquity of Eli's house shall not be purged with sacrifice nor offering for ever. When the Lord is mad, the Lord is mad: thus we are in the lands of our captivity 400 years and counting, and you have heard the history of the Spanish Inquisition, how we were burned for keeping the commandments; his anger burns unto the lowest hell.
- Do not let that be you. Do not let the Lord pass a judgment on your house where Christ prays for you and the Most High says, No, I will bring a judgment on his house for ever. Do not sin a sin so great that it cannot be prayed for, so that nothing is left but the fiery day of judgment.

- Samuel lay until the morning, and opened the doors of the house of the Lord; and Samuel feared to shew Eli the vision. But Eli called him and said, What is the thing that the Lord hath said unto thee? I pray thee hide it not from me: God do so to thee, and more also, if thou hide any thing from me of all the things that he said unto thee.
- Samuel was going to be a judge of Israel, and here is his first test as a judge: do not sugarcoat what the Lord has said, and do not trim your words to seek love. And Samuel told him every whit, and hid nothing from him.
- Eli said, It is the Lord: let him do what seemeth him good. Eli had no control of his kids, but he accepted the judgment of the Lord: thy will be done. Maybe the Lord had mercy on Eli; that is of the Lord, and we will not know.

- Samuel grew, and the Lord was with him, and did let none of his words fall to the ground. What does that mean? What Samuel said, it came to pass.
- All Israel from Dan even to Beersheba knew that Samuel was established to be a prophet of the Lord. And the Lord appeared again in Shiloh: for the Lord revealed himself to Samuel in Shiloh by the word of the Lord.
- That is good history we read today about Samuel; take heed to what you have heard, and do not let it slip. Some of you may be in these same situations: putting other people before God, honouring your children above God and not restraining them, or hearing the Lord call once, twice, three times without answering the call. This history is here for us to learn from; use it, lest the judgments of God be passed upon us when he is angry, for whatever the Most High says, Christ follows, because that is the order: God, Christ, man, woman, children.
