## Scriptures Opened

**[Ecclesiastes 10:6](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v6)**  *[[7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNrmPZFxjXA&t=420s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v6)</sup> Folly is set in great dignity, and the rich sit in low place.

- Social media influencer is probably the fastest growing occupation ever over the past five years.

- King Solomon is seeing into the future: the descendants of his servants, the …, set in great dignity, the ruling power of the last kingdom of the world.

- In the last days you'll see silly, meaningless, vain, pointless stuff set in great dignity: lots of followers, views and attention, monetized. Folly will reach celebrity status in the earth.

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/15#v21)</sup> Folly is joy to him that is destitute of wisdom: but a man of understanding walketh uprightly.

- Only a person destitute of wisdom takes joy in folly to the point of setting it in great dignity, and the state of this world is destitute of wisdom.

- So it's no shock that social media influencer is the new career at career day at the elementary school, and grown people in their 20s and 30s aspire to it.


**[Exodus 23:2](/bible/exodus/23#v2)**  *[[12:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNrmPZFxjXA&t=753s)]*

**[Sirach 26:5](/bible/sirach/26#v5)**  *[[13:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNrmPZFxjXA&t=811s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/26#v5)</sup> There be three things that mine heart feareth; and for the fourth I was sore afraid: the slander of a city, the gathering together of an unruly multitude, and a false accusation: all these are worse than death.

- The gathering together of an unruly multitude is worse than death, and that's what you saw in New York in the name of Kai Cenat. What caused it?


**[Titus 1:10](/bible/titus/1#v10)**  *[[14:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tNrmPZFxjXA&t=867s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/titus/1#v10)</sup> For there are many unruly and vain talkers and deceivers, specially they of the circumcision:

- Unruly and vain talkers is mainly the Christian church, but a lot of what fits that category in these last days is social media influencers: they aren't talking about anything, and they're unruly.

- The life they portray tells you that you don't have to work hard or have discipline; you can just have fun and indulge and make a living. It's an unruly influence that gathers an unruly multitude.

> <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v12)</sup> For the bewitching of naughtiness doth obscure things that are honest; and the wandering of concupiscence doth undermine the simple mind.

- The more time on social media, the more the mind is weakened, compromised, damaged, crippled. And it isn't just the children.
