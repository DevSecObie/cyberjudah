## Introduction

*[[0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=5s)]* Escaping the Plantation, IUIC Raleigh, on the modern black woman meeting Fannie Lou Hamer. The other America, token integration and affirmative action give the illusion that everything is all right. Fannie Lou Hamer said in 1968 that the flag is drenched with our blood, the same thing Revelation 17:6 prophesied. Nothing has changed: Rasheem Carter's death in Mississippi, the projects built like a rat experiment, and the men of IUIC marching in Taylorsville. We are living in hell, and our justice comes from heaven.

## Scriptures Opened

*[[0:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=5s)]* **In The News: Fannie Lou Hamer in 1968: Mississippi is America's problem; 40 to 50 churches bombed and burned; the flag is drenched with our blood; give the young people a chance to be respected as men** — There are two types of Christians: the white Christian with a hood who bombed the churches, and your people praying to a white Jesus while they bust you on the head. Our sisters knew who the enemy was back then, not through feminism.

**[Revelation 17:6](/bible/revelation/17#v6)**  *[[4:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=271s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/17#v6)</sup> And I saw the woman drunken with the blood of the saints, and with the blood of the martyrs of Jesus: and when I saw her, I wondered with great admiration.

- The woman drunken is America, with the blood of the saints. Fannie Lou Hamer said the American flag is drenched with our blood: that's prophecy, and she said the same exact thing before we even knew the Bible. Ain't no way that was rigged thousands of years before it happened.

**[Psalms 50:5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)**  *[[5:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=322s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

- To make it plain and simple for a Christian: the saints are those that made a covenant with God by sacrifice. Only the Israelites made a covenant with God by sacrifice. The blood of the saints is the blood of the Israelites.


*[[6:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=419s)]* **In The News: The Fannie Lou Hamer Park in Ruleville, Mississippi, run down, and a brother who came from the East Coast to see it** — The white man gives us a museum, a statue or a holiday. Fannie Lou Hamer fought for black people to vote, went toe to toe with the white man, was beaten, and was sterilized without her knowledge. She didn't know the scriptures but put her life on the line, and they don't even teach about her. She wore her natural hair.

*[[9:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=583s)]* **In The News: News: the death of Rasheem Carter of Fayette, Mississippi, called a modern-day lynching; his remains were found a month after he went missing, and the Department of Justice is looking into it** — Nothing has changed since the 60s. We still go to the white man to right our wrongs, in a court system built on the bones and blood of the Native American Indians, who are our people. Our justice comes from heaven.

*[[11:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=714s)]* **In The News: IUIC in Taylorsville, Mississippi: thousands of men marching for Rasheem Carter, to show his mother, Tiffany Carter, that we care** — One of the greatest experiences in the truth: brothers came from all over the country, fell into line, and marched on despite death threats. You men, don't sit back; be a part of this.

**[Psalms 44:14](/bible/psalms/44#v14)**  *[[14:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=845s)]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/44#v14)</sup> Thou makest us a byword among the heathen, a shaking of the head among the people.

- Black men rising up for their people. Thou makest us a byword among the heathen: they call us … and fools, they shoot us down in the streets, and they shake their head when they see us. My confusion is continually before me: we're in these streets confused. That's why we march: to show our people unity is possible, black men, Hispanic men, Native American men on one accord in the spirit of God.

**[Psalms 44:22](/bible/psalms/44#v22)**  *[[15:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=907s)]*

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/44#v22)</sup> Yea, for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.

- That's what we rise up for: every time a brother or sister gets shot down unjustly, whether by white folk or by their own people. We went to the old block in Chicago, to St. Louis, to Kansas City, even when it was black on black. We're killed all the day long because we're God's chosen people and the nations hate us; they kill us with no remorse.


**[Isaiah 5:8](/bible/isaiah/5#v8)**  *[[17:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=1026s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/5#v8)</sup> Woe unto them that join house to house, that lay field to field, till there be no place, that they may be placed alone in the midst of the earth!

- We are indeed in living hell. Woe to them that join house to house: the projects, set up for us. At the old block in Chicago you have roughly 2,000 people registered on the lease in about 694 units, packed on top of each other, and that breeds contempt and brother killing brother. Ten blocks away they live in houses worth half a million or 700 thousand dollars. An Edomite did an experiment with lab rats in close proximity: the longer they were together the more violent they became, and the male rats started to sleep with each other. They test on animals and then test it on us.


**[Micah 2:1](/bible/micah/2#v1)**  *[[19:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nZTbI949ok&t=1171s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/micah/2#v1)</sup> Woe to them that devise iniquity, and work evil upon their beds! when the morning is light, they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand.

- They devise iniquity upon their beds and practise it in the morning: start a clinic, Plan B, the school-to-prison pipeline, the projects. Low-income housing with no grocery stores, food deserts, no businesses for jobs, and cocaine, guns and rap music put in the community to destroy our kids. Meek Mill said he can't rap about more because they'd take resources from him: he loves his money more than his people. They covet fields and oppress a man, even a man and his heritage: this Bible is our heritage. They took it from us and gave us their understanding, and now our own brothers, the urban apologetics, come against us with what they learned from their slave master.
