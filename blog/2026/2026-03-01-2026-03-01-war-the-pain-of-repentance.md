## Class Questions

- **Who are the kings of the Medes the Lord raised up against Babylon?** Iran, Jeremiah 51:11. The Lord's device is against Babylon to destroy it as the vengeance of his temple.
- **What are the caterpillars in Jeremiah 51:14?** The men that fill Babylon and lift up a shout against her: terrorists and sleeper cells who eat the tree from within, the same as those who rise up suddenly and bite in Habakkuk 2:7.
- **What does the wine Edom transgresses by represent?** The spirit of falsehood, Micah 2:11: his Christianity and his democracy, which are the same thing. All nations drank that wine, Revelation 18:3.
- **How does the class prove Edom is America and not the Arabs or the American Indian?** Obadiah 1:4: exalted as the eagle, the animal on the dollar, with its nest among the stars, satellites and space travel. The Arabs do not do that, and the Indians would have destroyed the white man if they had power.
- **Why is the prince of Tyrus called a man and not God?** Ezekiel 28:2 and 9 say yet thou art a man; Isaiah 14:16 asks is this the man; Revelation 13:18 says the number of the beast is the number of a man. The same man set himself up as God in the midst of the seas.
- **Who holds the balances on the black horse?** The white man over the dark nations, Revelation 6:5-6. A measure of wheat for a penny is the World Bank and IMF dictating the world's prices and declaring other nations' money worthless.
- **What does a covetous man do according to Sirach 10:9?** He setteth his own soul to sale and casteth away his bowels. That is the price of a piece of the American pie, Revelation 18:13, the souls of men.
- **What is the domino effect of covetousness?** It is the tenth commandment and starts in the mind; if you covet you will steal, if you steal you will lie, and then you will murder. The love of money is the root of all evil, 1 Timothy 6:10.
- **When was our calling given, according to Paul?** According to his own purpose and grace, given us in Christ Jesus before the world began, 2 Timothy 1:9; chosen in him before the foundation of the world, Ephesians 1:4.
- **What is the falling away that must come before the day of Christ?** All twelve tribes falling into captivity, Luke 21:24, led away captive into all nations until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled, 2 Thessalonians 2:3.
- **Who left Paul, and why does that matter?** All in Asia, including Phygellus and Hermogenes, 2 Timothy 1:15; Demas for love of this present world, Crescens, Titus, 2 Timothy 4:10; Alexander the coppersmith withstood him. Many will be offended and betray one another, Matthew 24:10, and it happened here in 2018.
- **Who enters the gates of the strong city in Isaiah 26:2?** The righteous nation which keepeth the truth, God's law, Psalms 119:142. The gates are named for the twelve tribes, Revelation 21:12, and only those who do his commandments enter, Revelation 22:14.
- **How many syllables in the name of the Lord?** Three, as Jehovah shows in Isaiah 26:4, with no J in Hebrew: Yah-ha-wah, not Yahweh.
- **Who is the I AM of Exodus 3:14?** Christ, John 8:58, before Abraham was, I am. He is the everlasting Father of Isaiah 9:6 by whom all things were made, John 1:3, though he is not the Father himself.
- **How often should the poor of the flock pray?** Evening, and morning, and at noon, Psalms 55:17; with my soul have I desired thee in the night, and I will seek thee early, Isaiah 26:9.
- **Who are the wicked who will not learn righteousness in Isaiah 26:10?** Edom, the border of wickedness, against whom the Lord hath indignation for ever, Malachi 1:4. They deal unjustly in the land of uprightness, in Gaza and against our children's schools.
- **What cities does Zephaniah 2:4 name, and when?** Gaza forsaken, Ashkelon a desolation, Ashdod driven out at the noon day, Ekron rooted up. The same modern names on the map.
- **How does Israel return to the land?** In increments. The Lord sends those that escape, the 144,000, to the nations that have not heard his fame, and they bring all their brethren to his holy mountain Jerusalem, Isaiah 66:19-21.
- **Who are the other lords that had dominion over us?** Esau and Ishmael, Isaiah 26:13: the trans-Atlantic trade and Leopold's Congo, and the slave markets of Mecca, Zanzibar, Tunisia, and Libya.
- **What does the woman in travail mean in Isaiah 26:17?** The plagues on the earth come like the pains within two or three hours of birth that slack not a moment, 2 Esdras 16:38-39. We have been with child, Christ, and in pain, captivity, but brought forth wind, disobedience.
- **Who judges at the great white throne, and who is in the book of life?** Christ, John 5:22 and 2 Corinthians 5:10, judging every man according to his works. Israel is written in the book, Daniel 12:1; the nations were born in vain, 2 Esdras 9:22, and whoever is not found written is cast into the lake of fire, Revelation 20:15.
