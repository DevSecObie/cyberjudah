## Introduction

*[[6:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tmB8z0qSYo4&t=372s)]* The class is about traitors. Every leader America overthrew fell through someone close to them: Malcolm X, Lumumba, Sankara and now Maduro, betrayed by his own bodyguards. The same spirit rose against Moses in Korah's rebellion, against David in the friend who ate his bread, and against Christ in Judas's kiss. The same will happen inside Israel, where brethren who covet position and money will be paid to set up their leaders. The class then shows the power behind these betrayals, America's coups, embargoes and weapons, and warns the brethren in Venezuela and everywhere not to take up arms. The war is spiritual, and the Lord is a man of war who will bring down the daughter of Babylon.

## Class Questions

- **How did America overthrow leaders like Lumumba, Sankara and Maduro?** Through someone close to them, a friend, officer or bodyguard paid or promised power. Psalm 41:9 describes the familiar friend who lifts up his heel.
- **What was Korah's sin?** He and 250 princes coveted the priesthood and rose against Moses and Aaron, despising the place God had already given them. It was rebellion against God himself (Numbers 16).
- **Why does David say the betrayal of a friend is worse?** An enemy he could have hidden from, but it was a man his equal, with whom he took sweet counsel (Psalm 55:12–14).
- **Should Israel take up arms against America?** No. The weapons of Israel's warfare are not carnal (2 Corinthians 10:4). The Lord is the man of war who will bring Babylon down.
- **How does America prepare a regime change?** Embargoes and sanctions to break the economy, agents to stir the people, and insiders to open the door, as the class showed in Venezuela, Iran and elsewhere.

## In Closing

*[[2:21:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tmB8z0qSYo4&t=8485s)]* The weapons of our warfare are not carnal but mighty through God. Get your mind right, build yourself up, and trust the Lord, the man of war, to bring down the daughter of Babylon.
