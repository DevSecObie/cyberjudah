## Introduction

*[[6:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXeqc75txGk&t=394s)]* Captain Zephaniah asks why so many so-called Latinos voted for Donald Trump, then watched ICE come for their own families. He answers from the scriptures. Esau is a nation of fierce countenance that spares no one who looks like Israel. The Latinos are Israelites, the Ladinos or Latinized Negroes of Portuguese history. He is the man of sin now being revealed, and he will be broken without hand when Christ returns.

## Class Questions

- **Why can't Latinos who support Trump escape deportation?** Deuteronomy 28:50 calls the oppressor a nation of fierce countenance that shows no favour, and Jeremiah 50:33 says Israel and Judah are oppressed together. Colour, language and loyalty do not change that.
- **Who are the aliens in scripture?** Lamentations 5:2 says our houses were turned to aliens. The aliens are the strangers who took Israel's inheritance, not Israel.
- **Which tribe does the class connect to South America?** Asher, from Deuteronomy 33:24–25: blessed with children, dipping his foot in oil, with shoes of iron.
- **Why must Israel not set a stranger as king?** Deuteronomy 17:15 forbids it, and Daniel 4:17 shows God sets the basest of men over those who want a king from another nation.
- **How will the son of perdition be destroyed?** Christ will consume him with the spirit of his mouth and the brightness of his coming (2 Thessalonians 2:8). He shall be broken without hand (Daniel 8:25).
