## Introduction

Shalom, Israel. Happy Sabbath, happy new moon. Last week we finished celebrating Passover. You know how we do Passover: Messiah God sent us all over the world to keep Passover with our people, our brothers and sisters. Captains went to Tanzania and Zanzibar, to Kenya; I was in Liberia.

This Passover we got a lot of new officers: rank ups, soldiers made officers of 10, officers of 10 up to 20 and 50, newly captains. What I want you to understand is that those positions come with very heavy consequences and responsibilities. How does the scripture go? To whom much is given, much is required. When you got this position, do not take it lightly. Some of you do not understand what you stepping into. And keep in mind, old age doesn't mean you got wisdom; it just means you got old age. We got young men in the midst who got more wisdom than the old age. The Most High is not looking at your old age.

When you become an officer, you are a judge. You go to the courthouse, you go in front of a judge: nobody has more power in that courthouse than the judge. Whatever you say goes; based on what you say, you can destroy or you can build. That's what you were called to be. You were not called to just sit here; you were called to be a judge. However, being a judge, you are also commanded to be just. That's why I call it Called to Judge, Commanded to Be Just. You cannot be a wicked judge. Some of you accept the position to be a judge, but you do not accept the command to be just.
