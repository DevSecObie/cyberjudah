>

>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v9)</sup> And he gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the strangers with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon: for they fell to him out of Israel in abundance, when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.

- Now for a long season Israel hath been without the true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law: It's the exact same thing Ephesians 2 says.

- But when they in their trouble did turn unto the Lord God of Israel, and sought him, he was found of them: God heard their prayers.

- And the strangers with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon: The strangers out of Ephraim, Manasseh and Simeon. The northern kingdom was separated from God. That's who the son of the stranger is.

- For they fell to him out of Israel in abundance: Out of Israel.

- And they entered into a covenant: That's Isaiah 56, taking hold of the covenant. Now you know who the strangers are in Isaiah 56.

**[Isaiah 56:6-8](/bible/isaiah/56#v6)**  *[[1:52:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P4cR6i32U80&t=6760s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/56#v6)</sup> Also the sons of the stranger, that join themselves to the Lord, to serve him, and to love the name of the Lord, to be his servants, every one that keepeth the sabbath from polluting it, and taketh hold of my covenant;

- Their burnt offerings and their sacrifices shall be accepted upon mine altar; for mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people: Remember, Solomon said that when Israel is established, the nations will come and bring their offerings. That's true, but it starts with Israel first and foremost. Then the other nations, under us, bring their forces. Darius brought sacrifices, Cyrus offered sacrifices. It's no different. They are going to get the kingdom, just not the way they want it.

- The Lord God which gathereth the outcasts of Israel saith, Yet will I gather others to him, beside those that are gathered unto him: Those already gathered unto him were Judah. Close on John 11.

## Class Questions

- **During Exodus 12, were we in slavery or were we free?** Free. And when the Lord told us we could take bondmen and bondwomen, we were free, not in bondage. The law of the stranger applies when you are not removed out of your land and not enslaved.
- **If you are a stranger yourself in a foreign land as a slave, does the law of the stranger apply?** No, it does not.
- **In Solomon's time, were we in slavery or ruling? Do we have a great name now?** We were ruling. We do not have that great name now; we are not in that condition in any way, shape or form.
- **How will their kings be brought, on an Amazon truck, a school bus, a yacht?** In chains, and their nobles in fetters of iron (Psalms 149:8).
- **Can a nation receive salvation?** Yes. Saved to be enslaved.
