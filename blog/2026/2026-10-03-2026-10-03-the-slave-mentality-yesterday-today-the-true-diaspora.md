**[Hosea 4:6](/bible/hosea/4#v6)**  *[[2:13:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8034s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/4#v6)</sup> My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.

- We are destroyed for lack of knowledge, and what he blames is not ignorance but rejection: because thou hast rejected knowledge.

- Because we rejected it he rejected us, and thou shalt be no priest to me.

- Seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God. We forgot the laws of God and became degenerate plants, and unto strangers we went.


**[Proverbs 13:12](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)**  *[[2:14:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8077s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)</sup> Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life.

- Deferred means it is put on pause, it is stopped, it is ended.

- Earlier we read that we said there is no hope. Hope deferred makes the mind sick, and the sickness is the madness, the blindness and the astonishment: unable to see the truth, unable to see reality.

- But when the desire cometh it is a tree of life, when the desire to keep God's commandments comes.


**[Esther (Greek) 14:19](/bible/esther-greek/14#v19)**  *[[2:15:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8123s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/esther-greek/14#v19)</sup> O thou mighty God above all, hear the voice of the forlorn and deliver us out of the hands of the mischievous, and deliver me out of my fear.

- Hear the voice of the forlorn. That is not a common word, so understand it: lonely, unhappy and miserable, from abandonment or lack of care.

- Empty or neglected, describing a place that is run down. That is the ghettos and the slums.

- Hopeless, unlikely to succeed or achieve a positive outcome. That is the blindness.

- The word comes from the Middle English meaning lost completely, from the Old English verb to lose, abandon or destroy. Forlorn means the lost, and Christ said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel, not the lost sheep of everybody.


**[Judith 9:11](/bible/judith/9#v11)**  *[[2:17:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8266s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/judith/9#v11)</sup> For thy power standeth not in multitude nor thy might in strong men: for thou art a God of the afflicted, an helper of the oppressed, an upholder of the weak, a protector of the forlorn, a saviour of them that are without hope.

- Thy power standeth not in multitude, nor thy might in strong men.

- Thou art a God of the afflicted, an helper of the oppressed, an upholder of the weak. Are we an oppressed people here in America and everywhere else? Then he is a God of us.

- A protector of the forlorn and a saviour of them that are without hope. He is the protector of the lost, the hopeless, the abandoned, the outcast. That is us.


**[Isaiah 2:6](/bible/isaiah/2#v6)**  *[[2:18:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8306s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/2#v6)</sup> Therefore thou hast forsaken thy people the house of Jacob, because they be replenished from the east, and are soothsayers like the Philistines, and they please themselves in the children of strangers.

- Thou hast forsaken thy people, the house of Jacob, because they be replenished from the east and are soothsayers like the Philistines.

- They please themselves in the children of strangers. We follow other nations' ideologies, doctrines and philosophies.

- You will argue that Christ is three persons in one, but you will not argue against the origins of Halloween or Christmas or New Year's. That is out for discussion. I will be in idolatry, but while I am in idolatry I know Christ is three in one. We are completely lost and destroyed.


**[Jeremiah 17:4](/bible/jeremiah/17#v4)**  *[[2:20:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8412s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/17#v4)</sup> And thou, even thyself, shalt discontinue from thine heritage that I gave thee; and I will cause thee to serve thine enemies in the land which thou knowest not: for ye have kindled a fire in mine anger, which shall burn for ever.

- Thou shalt discontinue from thine heritage that I gave thee, and I will cause thee to serve thine enemies in the land which thou knowest not.

- Heritage is the history, traditions, beliefs and objects passed down: the tangible ones are buildings, monuments, art and landscapes, and the intangible ones are language, oral stories, music, social customs and recipes.

- Heritage is one word, and that word is identity. Discontinue from your language, your oral stories and your social customs and you lose your identity. He said you will discontinue, which means you are going to lose it, you will forget, you will be lost and forlorn.


**[Sirach 17:11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)**  *[[2:20:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8457s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)</sup> Beside this he gave them knowledge, and the law of life for an heritage.

- He gave us the laws of God for an inheritance, the law of life.

- Once our laws were removed from us we were indoctrinated with Christianity. When you strip us of our laws you strip us of our identity.

- Our laws are part of our race. Our oral stories go back to the law, our customs go back to the law, our monuments go back to the law. No law means no identity, no culture, no heritage.


**[Psalms 119:111](/bible/psalms/119#v111)**  *[[2:21:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8515s)]*

> <sup>[111](/bible/psalms/119#v111)</sup> Thy testimonies have I taken as an heritage for ever: for they are the rejoicing of my heart.

- Even David said the testimonies of God, the laws, are a heritage, and he took them as a heritage for ever.

- If we are stripped of that we lose everything. Christianity is literally an identity stripper, because it is a doctrine of lawlessness.

- Without our laws we are not the people. What makes us a peculiar people is the peculiar laws he gave us in the first place, and they go hand in hand with our race.


**[2 Maccabees 6:6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)**  *[[2:22:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8573s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)</sup> Neither was it lawful for a man to keep sabbath days or ancient fasts, or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.

- The Greeks made it unlawful to keep the sabbath days, the ancient feasts, or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.

- We were forbidden to keep our laws, and over time we became integrated and assimilated into Greek society and were lost in Greco culture.

- Hellenised Jews who spoke Greek and grew up in Greek, that is all they knew, but they were still our people and they are who Paul was writing to.

- Assyria did the same thing: houses, land, agricultural starter kits and lessons in the imperial language, conquered peoples legally declared Assyrian subjects with equal duties, and intermarriage actively encouraged. Erasure of identity, and that is where America got it from.


**[Hosea 1:8-9](/bible/hosea/1#v8)**  *[[2:26:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8764s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/1#v8)</sup> Now when she had weaned Lo–ruhamah, she conceived, and bare a son.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/1#v9)</sup> Then said God, Call his name Lo–ammi: for ye are not my people, and I will not be your God.

- Call his name Loammi, for ye are not my people and I will not be your God. That is said regarding Ephraim.

- Loammi, not a people, describes a state of spiritual alienation, a broken covenant and separation from God, where a group does not belong to God's chosen community or share in his protective mercy.

- Spiritual alienation directly causes a loss of identity, because in the Bible your identity is not something you invent yourself, it is entirely defined by who you belong to.

- They have moved me to jealousy with that which is not God, and provoked me to anger with their vanities.

**[1 Peter 1:1](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)**  *[[2:27:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PkFnZPllE5w&t=8868s)]*
