## Introduction

*[[7:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V9oc-I5QZjQ&t=420s)]* Taught on the eve of a winter storm, this class reads the news through prophecy. America is Babylon, the hammer of the whole earth and the golden cup in the Lord's hand. It will make the nations drink oppression until Europe and the nations hate it and burn it. The teacher also turns to the immigration raids: Aquila and Priscilla were deported from Rome and still became Paul's helpers, so Israel must not fear. Isaiah 19 foretells Egyptian against Egyptian, and the rest of the class traces how America's founders wrote the Second Amendment for a militia against tyranny. Secession talk, a cruel lord and rumors of war in the land all point to a civil war before Babylon's fall. The son of perdition, half of the title, was set aside for lack of time.

## Class Questions

- **Why does the class call America the oppressor?** Isaiah 14:4 names the king of Babylon the oppressor and the golden city, and Jeremiah 50:23 calls Babylon the hammer of the whole earth. America is using that power on every nation, not only on Israel.
- **How should an Israelite face deportation?** Without fear. Aquila and Priscilla were expelled from Rome and became Paul's helpers, the hairs of your head are numbered, and all things work together for good to them that love God.
- **What does Isaiah 30 say the affliction is for?** Through the bread of adversity and the water of affliction, the people will see their teachers, hear this is the way, and cast away their idols.
- **Which scriptures foretell a civil war in Babylon?** Isaiah 19:2 sets Egyptian against Egyptian, Isaiah 14:20 says Babylon slew its own people, Jeremiah 51:46 speaks of rumors and violence in the land, and Revelation 16:19 divides the great city into three parts.
- **What was the Second Amendment written for?** The founders wrote it so white citizens could form militias against a tyrannical government, as they did against Britain. It prepared the way for civil war.
