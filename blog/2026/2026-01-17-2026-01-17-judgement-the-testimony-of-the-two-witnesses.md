## Introduction

*[[4:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xVwQNhmyxFw&t=281s)]* This class explains why judgment starts at the house of God, then shows that God never judges without two or three witnesses. The ICE raids and the brutality in Minneapolis are the bread of adversity sent to wake an Israel grown comfortable in America, and the teachers must be there to tell the people why. The law of two witnesses forbids slander and false witness, and the class warns those who spread lies about the ministry. Christ sent the seventy out two by two to bind and loose. The two witnesses of Revelation 11, the prophets and all Israel, now testify against Edom and the synagogue of Satan until the seven last plagues are poured out.

## Class Questions

- **Why does judgment begin at the house of God?** To make Israel seek God early (Hosea 5:15) and to prove them. God opens their ears in oppression (Job 36:15).
- **What is the law of witnesses?** At the mouth of two or three witnesses a matter is established, and a false witness receives the punishment he intended for his brother (Deuteronomy 19:15–19).
- **Why did Christ send the seventy out two by two?** Judgment requires two witnesses. They bound and loosed on earth, marking cities that rejected them for judgment.
- **Who are the two witnesses of Revelation 11?** God always sends two prophets before judgment, and Israel as a whole are his witnesses (Isaiah 43:10), testifying the law and the prophets.
- **How should Israel respond to brethren who slander and fight them?** Love your enemies within the nation (Luke 6:27), greet them and go teach elsewhere, and follow Matthew 18:15 with witnesses when a brother trespasses.
