## Introduction

A Sabbath class out of the Jersey school, taught by a deacon who had spent the previous two weeks in Connecticut. It opens with 1 John 4 read over the intro, and the whole class is built on that passage: try the spirits, because every spirit that confesseth not that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is not of God, and this is that spirit of antichrist.

Before the topic there is a wisdom for the week, and then a run of earthquake footage set against Matthew 24. Then the subject proper, which is two questions the class is asked to answer precisely rather than vaguely: who is Lucifer, and who or what is the antichrist.

The teaching runs against the standard church answer on both. Lucifer is not a fallen angel who was cast out of the sky in the past, because the fall in Isaiah 14 has not happened yet. The antichrist is not one man arriving in the future with a mark on his head, because John said in his own time there were already many antichrists. The class then traces the word through John, Paul, Daniel and Revelation, and lands on a system rather than a person: Rome, and its continuation in Europe and America, the fourth beast.

## In Closing

Two corrections, made from the text rather than from tradition.

Lucifer is not a fallen angel. Isaiah 14 names him four verses earlier as the king of Babylon, calls him the oppressor and the golden city, and then asks over him, is this the man. He carries the title morning star for the same reason Paul gives in 2 Corinthians 11: false apostles transform themselves into the apostles of Christ, exactly as Satan transformed himself into an angel of light. A nation that colonised and enslaved and then rebranded itself as a God-fearing Christian society has done precisely that, and the King James translators reached for a Latin word to keep it distinct from the true morning star in Revelation 22.

And the antichrist is not one man arriving later. John said many, in his own lifetime. Christ said many shall come in my name. Paul called the same thing the man of sin, the son of perdition and the mystery of iniquity. Daniel called it the fourth beast, and Revelation called it the beast whose deadly wound was healed. It began with Rome, it killed our king and the apostles, it came back in the 1400s conquering on a white horse in Christ's name, and it is finishing now in Europe and America.

The fall in Isaiah 14 is therefore still ahead. Hell from beneath is moved to meet him, the kings of the nations rise from their thrones, Michael stands up, and the war in heaven of Revelation 12 is the sword of Isaiah 34 coming down on Idumea. Read Revelation 19 at home, because it is the same war.
