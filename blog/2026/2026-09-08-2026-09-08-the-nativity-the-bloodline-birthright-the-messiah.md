## Introduction

*[[13:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=819s)]* A Labor Day class in Philadelphia in two parts. Deacon Malachi opens on Carnival: the West Indian parade in Brooklyn and Notting Hill is not our culture but a Roman Catholic pre-Lent festival of Bacchus that the slaves learned from their colonizers. Then the main teaching, the nativity: the bloodline, the birthright and the Messiah, walked from Judah and Tamar through Ruth, Tobit and the kings of Judah to show that the land and the throne pass by the father's line, that Luke 3 is Mary's father's line and Matthew 1 is Joseph's, and that Christ was born through Joseph and Mary as every king before him was born.

## In The News

- *[[19:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=1188s)]* **Street footage from the Notting Hill carnival in London and the Labor Day carnival on Eastern Parkway, with white commentators watching it** — The nations look at our people humping each other half naked in the street and call it a culture; that is the astonishment, proverb and byword of Deuteronomy 28.
- *[[31:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=1889s)]* **A short film on the origins of Mardi Gras in New Orleans** — It began with French settlers in 1699 as the celebration before Lent; the masquerade balls and parades came straight from France, and the mask is where playing mas gets its name.
- *[[51:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=3075s)]* **Articles on Jesse Waddell and Claudia Jones, the Trinidadians who started the indoor costume balls in Harlem in the 1920s and the carnival that became Notting Hill** — Carnival came to New York and Britain through Trinidad, and Trinidad kept it the way the Europeans kept it, in February before Lent.
- *[[55:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=3352s)]* **A history of Trinidad carnival: French settlers brought their masquerade balls in the late 1700s and the slaves made their own version** — The slaves envied what they were not allowed to join and copied it; steel pan and calypso were added to a European festival, they did not replace it.
- *[[1:09:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=4156s)]* **A film on the Venice carnival and its masks** — Italy still keeps carnival in the weeks before Lent to this day, masks and revelry, so the feathers and costumes on Eastern Parkway came from Rome.
- *[[1:14:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=4444s)]* **The etymology of carnival and of Lent** — Carnival is literally to raise the flesh, which the church later folk-etymologized into farewell to meat; Lent means springtime and runs forty days to Easter, so the days of indulgence run up to Ash Wednesday and the penance after.
- *[[1:42:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=6151s)]* **Trinidad soca songs about bacchanal** — Our own songs say carnival is bacchanal, and bacchanal is the Roman Bacchanalia, the feast of Bacchus; the word was learned in slavery and kept in everyday Trinidadian speech.
- *[[3:18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=11884s)]* **A short on what it takes to keep a Y chromosome alive** — A man with only daughters ends his line on paper, yet the daughters are still on the tree; the class used it to show why the record follows the father, with the caveat that a brother's line can carry it on.
- *[[6:19:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=22783s)]* **A search result on why Joseph travelled to Bethlehem for the census** — Roman censuses registered people where they lived or where they held taxable property, which is why Joseph had to go to Bethlehem: he had land there as well as in Nazareth.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Sirach 17:11-12](/bible/sirach/17#v11)**  *[[17:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=1041s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)</sup> Beside this he gave them knowledge, and the law of life for an heritage.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/17#v12)</sup> He made an everlasting covenant with them, and shewed them his judgments.

- The law of life is our heritage, and our heritage is our culture: the new moons, the Sabbaths, the feast of tabernacles and the commandments are what God gave the children of Israel.

- What our people celebrate in the Caribbean islands and on Eastern Parkway is not our culture; it was made up in slavery or learned from the white man.

- A slave has no culture of his own to point to: you were destroyed, and what you follow you either invented in bondage or copied from your oppressor.


**[Deuteronomy 28:37](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v37)**  *[[40:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=2434s)]*

> <sup>[37](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v37)</sup> And thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword, among all nations whither the Lord shall lead thee.

- God showed Moses the future; Moses saw our people on the streets of Babylon acting as they act at carnival, the same way he saw them rise up to play at the foot of the mountain.

- An astonishment means people are shocked: look at these black people humping each other in the street and calling it a culture.

- A proverb and a byword means they call us out of our name among all the nations where the Lord leads us.

- Psalms 44 says the same: we are a reproach to our neighbors and a shaking of the head among the people, the white man in his window shaking his head at the parade; and Jeremiah 2:21 says God planted us a noble vine and we turned into the degenerate plant of a strange vine.


**[Psalms 106:35-36](/bible/psalms/106#v35)**  *[[47:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=2839s)]*

> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/106#v35)</sup> But were mingled among the heathen, and learned their works.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/106#v36)</sup> And they served their idols: which were a snare unto them.

- We mingled among the heathen and learned their works; nowhere in Africa do you see people do what the West Indians do on Labor Day.

- We learned carnival from the European colonizer, and their idols became a snare to us, which is why we are still out there acting the same way.

- So when Europeans scorn us for it, understand it is their own culture we are practicing in the open.


**[Proverbs 3:31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)**  *[[57:32](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=3452s)]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

- The slaves saw the colonizers' masquerade balls, were not allowed to take part, and envied them; then they chose their ways.

- Carnival is not even an African culture; it is a European celebration of Bacchus folded into Lent by the Roman Catholic Church, and we learned it in slavery.

> <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v27)</sup> For I know thy rebellion, and thy stiff neck: behold, while I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against the Lord; and how much more after my death?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v28)</sup> Gather unto me all the elders of your tribes, and your officers, that I may speak these words in their ears, and call heaven and earth to record against them.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v29)</sup> For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves, and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you; and evil will befall you in the latter days; because ye will do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands.

- Moses told them: you are rebellious now, and after my death you will be worse; he had seen what we would do in the streets of New Orleans, Brazil, the Caribbean and North America.

- We have utterly corrupted ourselves in these festivals and turned aside from our own heritage to make our oppressor's heritage our own.

- The evil that befalls us in the latter days, slavery included, is the consequence of that turning aside.


**[1 Corinthians 3:3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)**  *[[1:23:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=4985s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)</sup> For ye are yet carnal: for whereas there is among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not carnal, and walk as men?

- Carnal, carnival and carnivore all go back to the same root, the flesh; when Paul says ye are yet carnal he means sinful, fleshly.

- Carnival means raising the flesh: two days of indulging every sexual and fleshly desire, and then penance from the priest on Ash Wednesday.

- The church changed the meaning to removing meat, and a folk etymology is a fake change; the word means raising the flesh, not farewell to it.


**[2 Maccabees 6:6-7](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)**  *[[1:34:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q4vL3X9huPQ&t=5698s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)</sup> Neither was it lawful for a man to keep sabbath days or ancient fasts, or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v7)</sup> And in the day of the king’s birth every month they were brought by bitter constraint to eat of the sacrifices; and when the fast of Bacchus was kept, the Jews were compelled to go in procession to Bacchus, carrying ivy.

- When the feast of Bacchus was kept, the Jews were compelled to go in procession carrying ivy; the wreaths of ivy and flowers on the head were the Greek dress for it, and the same wreath hangs on the Christmas door today.

- Carnival is the celebration of Bacchus: orgies, wine and drunkenness in his honor, and the word bacchanal that every Trinidadian uses for scandal and revelry is his name.

- Those on Eastern Parkway today are worshiping Bacchus without knowing it; that is European culture, Christian culture and Roman culture, not yours.

- Easter, too, was a Roman goddess of fertility and spring in the same season as the days of unleavened bread, as Acts 12 shows Herod waiting until after Easter; Lent means springtime and runs forty days to it.

## Class Questions

- **What does carnival mean?** To raise the flesh; the church later changed it to farewell to meat, but the folk etymology is a fake change, and the celebration is the feast of Bacchus before Lent.
- **Where was Israel when Sheshan gave his daughter to his Egyptian servant?** In Egypt, with no land of inheritance; that is why the line is recorded, so that when the land was divided that line, going back to an Egyptian, received none.
- **What does Zerubbabel mean?** Born in Babylon; it was a common name for the children born in that captivity, which is why Salathiel's son and Pedaiah's son both bore it.
- **Can a man come from both Nathan and Solomon at the same time?** No; a man cannot have two great-grandfathers on his father's side, so Luke 3 is Mary's father's line and Joseph is written in it as Heli's son by inheritance, as Tobias inherited Raguel.
- **Was the sign of Isaiah 7:14 the virgin or the child?** The child; Isaiah says I and the children are for signs, Ezekiel was a sign, Jonas was a sign, and the babe in swaddling clothes was the sign to the shepherds; there is no scripture where a woman by herself is a sign.
- **What does the days were accomplished mean?** Nine months, a full term; she was great with child and delivered when the days were fulfilled.
- **Why would Joseph hide Mary privily?** Because the child was his: a pregnant girl sent home to her father would be made a public example and killed, and a just man would neither hide a woman he had not impregnated nor cover an adulteress.
