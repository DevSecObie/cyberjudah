- And if thou doest not well, sin lieth at the door, and unto thee shall be his desire, and thou shalt rule over him. Ask yourself: what was Satan doing there? Moses is seeing what took place, and the Lord is showing him Satan present. When Cain got angry and his countenance fell, that is when Satan attacked him. Satan was there messing with Cain, and the Lord saw it. The Lord told Cain: fight Satan and do what is right, otherwise he will rule you and control you. He became the servant of Satan, for whosoever committeth sin is the servant of sin.
- Satan was whispering all kinds of things in Cain's head concerning his brother, and what Cain ended up doing was rising up and killing his brother. Satan was behind all of that. Satan will play with your mind.

**[Matthew 16:21-23](/bible/matthew/16#v21)**  *[[1:39:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Qdksp7DVZI&t=5976s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/16#v21)</sup> From that time forth began Jesus to shew unto his disciples, how that he must go unto Jerusalem, and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/16#v22)</sup> Then Peter took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Lord: this shall not be unto thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/16#v23)</sup> But he turned, and said unto Peter, Get thee behind me, Satan: thou art an offence unto me: for thou savourest not the things that be of God, but those that be of men.

- Christ began to shew unto his disciples how that he must go unto Jerusalem, and suffer many things of the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised again the third day. Was that a good thing or a bad thing? It was a good thing: he had to die to redeem the nation of Israel. It is hard to understand, like when we tell you some of us are going to be killed for teaching this truth; but Revelation 6 says the fellowservants must be killed as they were, and that has to be fulfilled.
- Then Peter took him, and began to rebuke him, saying, Be it far from thee, Lord: this shall not be unto thee. Peter looked at Christ's death as a bad thing.
- But he turned, and said unto Peter, Get thee behind me, Satan. What happened there? Satan literally jumped on Peter. That was not Peter; that was Satan talking to Christ through Peter, and Christ was literally talking to Satan. That is why in another place Christ told Peter, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat: but I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not.
- Satan comes for all of us. He comes different ways to defile us and destroy us: for some it is money, for some it is sex, for some it is hatred. Satan only comes at you at your weakness. So examine yourself: what is my weakness? That is what I need to strengthen, because that is what Satan is going to try to destroy me with. The worst brothers and sisters are the ones that say they have no weakness, that they are perfect; those are the ones dealing with the heaviest demons and just hiding it. Everybody has weakness. The Lord told Paul, my strength is made perfect in weakness; Paul asked three times for his weakness to be removed, and the Lord said no, fight it and become strong.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 4:13](/bible/luke/4#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/luke/4#v13)</sup> And when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.

    When the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season. You think Satan stopped tempting Christ? He never stopped; he just left for a season and came back in different ways. When Christ was about to be crucified, Satan came back, and he came back by jumping on Peter.

**[Acts 5:3](/bible/acts/5#v3)**  *[[1:51:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Qdksp7DVZI&t=6661s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/5#v3)</sup> But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost, and to keep back part of the price of the land?

- But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost? That is what happened with Cain, and that is what Satan does to us: he fills the heart.
- Ananias and Sapphira sold their possession, and Satan came to them and said, act like you are giving them all the money, but keep back part for yourself, and do not let them know anything. If they wanted to keep back part for themselves, they could have just told Peter openly: we kept back a little part, and this part we are giving to the church. But Satan told them to hide it. The reason that thought came to their mind was Satan.
- The same way on your job: a thought comes into your mind out of nowhere and you wonder, why did that thought come to me? Satan will play with those things. The war we are fighting you cannot see; it is in your mind. Satan will play with your emotions, play with people you care about, and put thoughts in your mind that were not there, to get you to go against God. That is how the root of bitterness destroys brothers. You have to learn to control your thoughts.

**[James 1:13-15](/bible/james/1#v13)**  *[[1:56:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Qdksp7DVZI&t=6988s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/james/1#v13)</sup> Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God: for God cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/james/1#v14)</sup> But every man is tempted, when he is drawn away of his own lust, and enticed.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/james/1#v15)</sup> Then when lust hath conceived, it bringeth forth sin: and sin, when it is finished, bringeth forth death.

- Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God: for God cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man. When you get wicked thoughts in your mind, that is not God; that is Satan.
- But every man is tempted, when he is drawn away of his own lust, and enticed. What does Satan tempt you with? Your own lust, the things that you like. Your lusts and enticements are your weaknesses; that is what he is going to try to get you with. That is why you got to put on the whole armour and examine yourself to know your weakness. Do not ask anybody else what your weakness is; you know it. Figure out what you need to strengthen so Satan does not destroy you.
- Then when lust hath conceived, it bringeth forth sin. Where is lust conceived? In your mind. The teacher put a picture of an immodestly dressed woman on the screen and asked the brothers one by one what they saw, to teach this: we all see beautiful women, but you have to train yourself to hate evil. It does not come naturally; you cannot come to the truth and all of a sudden hate what you see out there. You train your mind, when you see it, to reject it and turn away. The older brothers with experience have matured past it; the young brothers dealing with youthful lusts have to train. Sisters too: before you came to this truth a lot of you dressed the same way, and now you have changed how you dress. When Christ returns there will be a modesty law set up.
- If the thought comes and you shake it off and rebuke it, lust is not conceived. But if you sit down and entertain it, it brings forth sin. That is why Christ said adultery does not start when you lie down with the woman; adultery starts in the mind.
- And sin, when it is finished, bringeth forth death. Some of you say, I fornicated and I did not die. That death is talking about damnation, your soul being destroyed. God will judge you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:28](/bible/matthew/5#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/5#v28)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.

    Whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart. That is exactly how lust is conceived: it starts with the look, and then the thought, in your heart, before anything physical ever happens.

**[Mark 7:21-23](/bible/mark/7#v21)**  *[[2:14:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Qdksp7DVZI&t=8097s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/mark/7#v21)</sup> For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/mark/7#v22)</sup> Thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/mark/7#v23)</sup> All these evil things come from within, and defile the man.

- For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders, thefts, covetousness, wickedness, deceit, lasciviousness, an evil eye, blasphemy, pride, foolishness. An evil eye is jealousy.
- All these evil things come from within, and defile the man. The thoughts you be having, the things you be hearing in your mind, a lot of times those things are Satan putting things there that are not there, that are not real, because Satan wants to defile you and put the root of bitterness inside of you. It comes from within, it forms a root, and it defiles you.

**[Proverbs 15:10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)**  *[[2:17:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Qdksp7DVZI&t=8224s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)</sup> Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die.

- Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way. One of the key points in the root of bitterness is correction: people become bitter a lot of times when they are corrected. Brothers get corrected, and in their mind Satan enters and says, they hate you, they never liked you, they are not right. And you are listening to all these thoughts, which is Satan. Like Wormtongue whispering in the king's ear: it is either Satan in your mind, or the person next to you speaking evil in your ears and you listening to it.
- Before bitterness becomes a root, it must be a seed first. Something gets whispered in your mind, and once that seed starts growing into roots inside of you, you change, and you change for the worse. You might think nobody sees your change; you come to class and try to play it off, but everybody sees it, because you cannot hide bitterness.
- Did Cain forsake the way? Was Cain corrected? Yes. The Lord corrected him and said, if you do well I will accept you, and Cain refused the correction; it was grievous to him, he became bitter against his brother, and he went and killed him.
- He that hateth reproof shall die. If you hate when you are corrected, and you do not want anybody to tell you anything, you are going to die. Some of you might get corrected harshly, and you get offended and say, I am not coming to the school anymore. Come on, man: what shall separate you from the love of God? You are going to let a brother speaking to you in a way you did not like separate you from Christ?
- A lot of sisters, after they have been corrected by their husband, become bitter against their husband. I have seen sisters become bitter and leave the truth because their husband corrected them. Sisters do not want to take accountability; if they do something wrong, they do not want to say I am sorry, and everything has to be a fight. That is Satan doing a job on our sisters. Correction from your husband is part of him ruling over you, and to rule does not mean slavery or abuse: it means to provide for her, to guide her, to teach her, to correct her, all the things a man is supposed to do for a woman he loves. Adam's flaw was that he did not correct his wife; Eve was deceived, and Adam went along with it willingly.

**[Genesis 30:1-2](/bible/genesis/30#v1)**  *[[2:28:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Qdksp7DVZI&t=8901s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/30#v1)</sup> And when Rachel saw that she bare Jacob no children, Rachel envied her sister; and said unto Jacob, Give me children, or else I die.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/30#v2)</sup> And Jacob’s anger was kindled against Rachel: and he said, Am I in God’s stead, who hath withheld from thee the fruit of the womb?

- When Rachel saw that she bare Jacob no children, Rachel envied her sister. Envy: that is Satan. And she said unto Jacob, Give me children, or else I die: give me a baby or I am going to kill myself. A very beautiful woman can be very manipulative and use her beauty to control you; Eve did that to Adam, and here the same thing is being done to Jacob. When a woman knows you love her more than God, she will try to use certain things against you. Brothers, never put a woman above your God: God comes first.
- And Jacob's anger was kindled against Rachel: and he said, Am I in God's stead, who hath withheld from thee the fruit of the womb? This is an argument Moses saw between Jacob and his wife, and he wrote it down in a kosher way for us. Jacob was angry, and when a man is angry he lifts his voice. That is not abuse. Sisters, when your husband gets angry and yells and corrects you, that is not abuse; take the correction, see what he is saying, and fix it, instead of trying to justify yourself. Because when a woman tries to justify herself, Satan comes in her mind saying, he was wrong for what he said to you, that is abuse right there; and if you listen to Satan, you end up divorced. A sister called the teacher this same week saying her husband was abusive; when she explained, it was arguments, and some things the brother should not have said, but it was still not abuse. Abuse is abuse, and correction is not it.
