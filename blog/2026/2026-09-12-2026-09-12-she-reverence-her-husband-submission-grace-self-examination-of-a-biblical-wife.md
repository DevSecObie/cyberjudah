When you are feeling that way, when those thoughts come out, when you are rebellious to the submission, you must use the word of God — and not the Instagram sister that is miserable, living a fantasy online, who is going to try to guide your relationship so that you can classify your man and put him in a box.

- Remember, we started all the way in the beginning: examine yourself whether you be in the faith. It says Christ is in there, unless you are reprobate, meaning void of God and understanding. You talk about living by the word of God, but you do not.

**[Ephesians 6:18](/bible/ephesians/6#v18)**  *[[2:33:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BcG8X9aUt8s&t=9217s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/6#v18)</sup> Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints;

- Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit. Being mindful of all this, we must always pray. Pray for unity. Pray to be able to see the things that are wrong in you. It is like in that psalm with David when he says, show me any wicked way in me.

- And watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints. For all of us. We need to look for that unity and that bond. The family should not be fighting each other while the enemy is shooting at us.

## In Closing

The family should not be fighting each other while the enemy is shooting at all of us. Submission, grace, self-examination, marriage, unity, the armour of God — all of it comes together. The wife must examine whether she is governed by the word when pressure enters into the marriage. The husband must examine whether he is loving and leading like Christ. And the entire household has to remember that you are all called to stand together under Christ against a common enemy, and we are not it.

That is why that unity and those things are only going to be present with the right order and structure of how we have things set up. That is why we do things the way we do, very much in particular a certain way. It carries from marriage to parenting to the congregation, and then how we even deal with each other. And if you are against that, then you are against what God is willing for us to do together, to protect us from all that evil that is out there. I pray you will get some understanding, that you can step it up and elevate and apply these things.
