## Introduction

*[[0:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=35s)]* Our forefathers built this; we as sons must preserve this. A local class on why everything in the congregation is done by design, from security to serve-safe protocol, and above all the courtship, betrothal, Ladies in Waiting, and Men of Valor programs. A film on black marriages in the 1940s shows announcements read from the pulpit, elders counselling the groom, older women teaching the bride, and courting on the front porch under everyone's eyes: our people kept scripture by instinct and morality without knowing it, and IUIC is bringing it back. Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain; prove a friend first; commend not a man for his beauty; bring not every man into thine house. Leadership has a proven track record of hundreds of couples, and the men giving the counsel made the mistakes themselves.

## In The News

- *[[2:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=154s)]* **A film on black marriages in the 1940s: unions that were fortresses, and 25 secrets people who lived through that era never forgot.** — Pay attention now or in six months we will be dealing with the situations. A fortress defends and protects the marriage from whatever outside would corrupt it; that is what the counsel is for, not to hinder anyone from marrying. A family builds a nation.
- *[[3:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=199s)]* **Marriage announcements read from the pulpit for weeks before the wedding, so the community could offer blessings and advice.** — That is a betrothal. When a brother and sister have been courting and the family or leadership gives the blessing, it is announced so the congregation knows. They did that in the forties without knowing they were Israelites.
- *[[4:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=290s)]* **Elders pulling the groom aside after service to share wisdom about providing, and older women visiting the bride to teach recipes and household skills.** — The married brothers and sisters here got counsel all the way to the wedding day and still get it. The men help a brother structure his resume, budget, and pick a trade during the year or two of proving; the older women are Titus 2. Community is common unity, everybody thinking the same.
- *[[14:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=840s)]* **The couple wrapped in a network of support that followed them for decades.** — When you come through the doors we say welcome home. Marry in the body and keep the commandments and you have a network for life; leadership has helped families through unemployment and transitions because they see the diligence.
- *[[16:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=963s)]* **Courting on the front porch in plain view of parents and neighbours, intentions clear, no sneaking around, no secret meetings, nothing physical, with word travelling fast if a young man acted out of line.** — Be here at six, sit on the porch, the window is up, and cousin Ray across the street is watching. No cell phones, no Instagram, no way to reach my daughter except through me, and never show up uninvited. That is how fathers proved a young man's intentions, and that is what IUIC is bringing back, not controlling your life but protecting you.
- *[[37:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=2223s)]* **The definition of spinster and spinsterhood: an unmarried woman past the usual marrying age, carrying social pressure and stigma, though today also framed as choice and independence.** — White women marry in their early twenties; when a black woman hits thirty-two or thirty-five the family asks when, and if no kids, what is wrong with her body. The clock is ticking, and that anxiety, as the single sisters admitted, makes a woman take any abuse or fornication to get a husband, or join the group booking the trip to Jamaica. Then the negro creeps in on that anxiety and you get a backdoor.
- *[[49:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=2958s)]* **The closing IUIC montage: from city streets to distant lands the message is the same, marriages celebrated, the dead honoured, Sabbaths gathered, tried from the stones in Virginia to the hatred in Montana, building communities and raising children of royalty.** — This is Israel United in Christ, a nation.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Psalms 127:1-2](/bible/psalms/127#v1)**  *[[10:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=610s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/127#v1)</sup> Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the Lord keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/127#v2)</sup> It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.

- The Lord set up IUIC under Bishop Nathanyel, Bishop Yahoshua, and Bishop Kohath, and the spirit of Christ moved these men to build it; we are all parts of the body of Christ helping with the building. Out of their experience they developed programs: security, food service with protocols so nobody gets sick and blames Israel United in Christ, and the rest. Everything is done by design; your clever idea has already been looked at, and wisdom comes from the top down.

- Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it. The house is the marriage, and the Lord has to be in it; try to build it yourself and it does not work. It is vain to rise up early and sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: waking in the night in regret over who you married because God did not build the house, and losing sleep to stress. All of us did that before the truth, and me and my wife would not be together without it; we were corrupted by worldly family, friends, ambitions, and lust and on our way to divorce.

- We know what men and women want; none of these men up here has never wanted sex. The Bible says surely he shall endow her to be his wife, but do that before the sex, so you do not make our mistakes. I was a father at nineteen, running up and down the basketball court, asking my mother for diaper money, no business having a baby. So when we tell a young man to slow down and prove a friend and he says he is grown, he learns the hard way.

- Imagine being out of your daughter's life from two until seventeen. Some of these women had no father their whole life, and when they came into the truth and got spiritual fathers they rebelled, because in the world they never had to listen to a man.


**[Sirach 6:7-10](/bible/sirach/6#v7)**  *[[18:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=1100s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/6#v7)</sup> If thou wouldest get a friend, prove him first and be not hasty to credit him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/6#v8)</sup> For some man is a friend for his own occasion, and will not abide in the day of thy trouble.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/6#v9)</sup> And there is a friend, who being turned to enmity, and strife will discover thy reproach.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/6#v10)</sup> Again, some friend is a companion at the table, and will not continue in the day of thy affliction.

- If thou wouldest get a friend, prove him first, and be not hasty to credit him. They understood in the forties that it takes time to know someone's true intentions. Most of you come in grown; the Bible says lord not over the flock, so the captains, officers, and bishop cannot make you do anything, only give fatherly advice, the word of God, and what we have seen.

- For some man is a friend for his own occasion, and will not abide in the day of thy trouble: some just want sex, and we have them among us, men and women. You are not looking across the room wondering whether she can help with your resume; you are thinking about the curves, the lips, and the perfume. The fear of the Lord teaches it is more than how she looks, and the same for the sisters.

- There is a friend who being turned to enmity and strife will discover thy reproach, and some friend is a companion at the table and will not continue in the day of thy affliction. Sirach 11:29, bring not every man into thine house, for the deceitful man hath many trains, sits with these; Sirach 6 through 15 connect, from proving a friend to not letting just anyone stroll into your house, with chapter 12 adding trust not thine enemy, for as iron rusteth so is his wickedness. You cannot know someone in three months or even a year: a person of twenty-seven has twenty-six years you know nothing of, family history, mental illness, diabetes, disease.

  Precepts:
  - **[Sirach 11:1-2](/bible/sirach/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/11#v1)</sup> Wisdom lifteth up the head of him that is of low degree, and maketh him to sit among great men.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/11#v2)</sup> Commend not a man for his beauty; neither abhor a man for his outward appearance.

    Wisdom lifteth up the head of him that is of low degree, and maketh him to sit among great men; a brother starting from the bottom at a job does not matter. Commend not a man for his beauty, neither abhor a man for his outward appearance. How many sisters were deceived by a good-looking man before the truth? Every woman wants a man, and a woman who says she does not need one goes against nature: her hormones release when she is under her husband, which is why she falls asleep ten minutes into the movie she asked to watch. She is bone of his bone, made in his image, which is why little girls look like their daddy; the body is the portal but the seed comes from the man. Sisters, release the feminism.


**[Sirach 11:29](/bible/sirach/11#v29)**  *[[25:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r1rk8TpIX_A&t=1523s)]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/11#v29)</sup> Bring not every man into thine house: for the deceitful man hath many trains.

- A sister in Columbia was shown a good brother, own business booked through March, speaks multiple languages, in the green, good-looking, and said she could not see herself with him. Did you see yourself forty-something and not married? Women stuck in Disney and Love and Hip Hop waiting for Prince Charming miss good husbands, and a father's job is guiding the daughters' minds toward what a good man is.

- Before a brother even asks about a sister, we have grilled him: pull up the Chime app, let me see the Wells Fargo and the Chase account. Three hundred dollars and a light bill of three-fifty, and you think I would give my baby to a man who cannot buy her shoes? Fathers do the math on the car note and the bills; that is why the Bible says commend not a man for his beauty, because every brother is not Boris Kodjoe or Idris Elba.

- My grandmother, married forty-seven years, would say that's Shemar Moore in front of my grandfather as he left for work with his back bad. That is not a small thing; it is disrespect, and it is why women fall into lust and adultery or leave a husband who just does not do it for her anymore.

- A quiet light-skinned sister came into the body with a big buff brother who never asked questions; she would not be with him until he came in and married her, and his weak nature bugged out. Years later a diligent officer asked about her, and six months into proving she called my wife crying that he was not her type physically. Did the type you were attracted to not have sex with you and leave? They married, and at the men's conference in 2024 she confessed adultery with her boss, who looked exactly like the first brother. She never got over the lust from the world, the Lord blocked the children, and the brother divorced her; Jeremiah 3, she had to go. Commend a man for his beauty and you end up with an ungodly man, or take anyone because you are in your thirties and think you should be married by now.

Then we can give a good report, exchange information, prove, and two or three years later have a wedding feast, then a baby, then five more, big at every Tabernacles. Trouble in the flesh still comes, but God is with you because you did it the right way.

Leadership gives you instructions from the Bible with a proven track record of hundreds of couples; you do your own thing, it fails, and then we are the devil? You would take tools from a coach with a winning record. Satan enters when you are not studying.

## Class Questions

- **Why is everything in the congregation done by design?** Leadership built the programs out of years of experience, from security to food protocols to courtship. Wisdom comes from the top down, and the clever idea you have has already been considered.
- **What did the pulpit announcements of the 1940s correspond to in scripture?** Betrothal. The congregation and the world are told who is marrying whom before it happens, and the community counsels the couple.
- **What is the front porch courtship, and what does IUIC call it?** Courting in plain view of the father and neighbours, no secret meetings and nothing physical. That is proving under the eyes of the fathers, for protection rather than control.
- **What happens when a house is built without the Lord?** They labour in vain and eat the bread of sorrows, Psalms 127:1-2: waking in regret over who you married and losing sleep to stress.
- **Why prove a friend first?** Because some man is a friend for his own occasion and will not abide in the day of trouble, Sirach 6:7-10. Some just want sex, and you cannot know a grown person's twenty-six years of history in three months.
- **Why commend not a man for his beauty, Sirach 11:2?** Wisdom lifts up the man of low degree, and looks deceive; the sister who married an officer not her type committed adultery with a boss who looked like the man she lusted after in the world.
- **What do the fathers check before a brother asks about a sister?** His accounts, his bills, and his ability to provide, because a father would not give his daughter to a man who cannot pay the light bill. Then his name and work in the body.
- **What is a spinster, and why does it matter?** An unmarried woman past the usual marrying age. The stigma and the ticking clock produce anxiety that makes sisters accept anything for a husband, which is where the backdoor creeps in.
- **What is the keyword in Ladies in Waiting?** Wait. The Lord knows what you are not ready for; graduate with a good name while the brothers go through Men of Valor, then prove, then the wedding feast.
- **How does Sirach 11:29 connect to Sirach 6?** Bring not every man into thine house, for the deceitful man hath many trains, is the same counsel as proving a friend; Sirach 6 through 15 are connected on proving people.
