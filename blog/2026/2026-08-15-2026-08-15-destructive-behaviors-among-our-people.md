## Introduction

The class opens with prayer for the sick, for the brothers and sisters suffering in the hand of the enemy, for the spirit to overcome, and for the men in the forefront who wake the people.

Then a warning about what the title is not. The picture on the thumbnail makes people assume this is about our people in the world. It is not. This is about destructive behavior among the Israelites, inside the truth, and it is the subject everybody wants swept under the rug. Coming back from the main conference the same things are still there: brothers and sisters who have sat here five, six, seven years still carrying what they walked in with. Bishop set this title for the deacons and captains to cover and it went uncovered, so it gets covered here.

The rule for listening to it is given up front. Take what is said and examine yourself against it. Be honest with yourself and with the Most High, not with the man teaching.

## In The News

Before the class proper, a word on the prophecy moving right now, for anyone who still treats this book as a fairy tale:

- The Mecca Agreement, signed this week by Pakistan, Turkey and Saudi Arabia, and almost entirely ignored by the American media. A trilateral security pact on the NATO principle: attack one and you have attacked all. Turkey holds one of the largest militaries in the world and Pakistan holds nuclear weapons, so this is not a symbolic signing.
- Why it happened. Saudi Arabia had the largest oil arrangement with America, cheap oil in exchange for protection. Iran struck American bases across the region and demonstrated that the protection does not hold. Every country on that list saw it. So they have begun to protect themselves, and they are uniting to do it.
- And when the Houthis struck Saudi Arabia days later, the pact did not turn on them. They are Arab too. The alliance is not being built to fight other Arabs.
- Ten countries meeting in Amman with Turkey, Pakistan, Indonesia and Malaysia, building a mechanism to track Israel's every move in East Jerusalem. Three have signed so far. The rest will follow.

## In Closing

Past hurt is what gets used to justify present anger. That is the whole mechanism, and it is why a man can sit here five and seven years and still be fighting the spirit he walked in with. He never admitted it was broken, so it was never fixed.

The class closed by setting each destructive thing beside what replaces it. Pride against humility. Anger against self-control. Bitterness against forgiveness. Jealousy against contentment. Rebellion against obedience. Selfishness against service. Murmuring against gratitude. Hatred against love. Impatience against patience. Harshness against gentleness. Division against unity. Arrogance against a teachable spirit.

The white man is the devil, and not one of them can keep a single person in this room out of the kingdom. Only you can take your own name out of the book of life. So examine yourself, admit what is broken, repent quickly, renew the mind, govern the tongue, and forgive the person sitting next to you before you ask to be forgiven yourself.
