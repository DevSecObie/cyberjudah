## Introduction

Integrity is a decision that has to be made every time. If you are not reading your four chapters a day, you are not studying, you don't give the Most High nothing to work with; how can you apply when it is time to apply if you are not reading and studying the scriptures? This is a fix your spirit class. We all got to fix our spirit; Satan is coming, and we got to tighten up our spirit, because people are going to get put in compromising positions where you have to use that integrity and have good character to get out of it.

## Class Questions

- Leadership gives instruction you don't personally agree with, but it's not against the commandments; what shows integrity? Answer: follow respectfully and maintain order. It is not sin what this person is asking you. Complain quietly afterwards means you did it with malice and no effort. Ignore the instruction means I don't agree with it, I ain't doing it. Pretend outwardly while causing division privately means now you're sowing discord and bringing somebody else into your lack of judgment and character.
- A lot of times that complaining spirit, that doubtful spirit, is that you don't have faith. You don't believe that the bishops, the deacons, the men over you are put in place by God. You're not denying them, you're denying God, and that evil spirit of unbelief is hindering your understanding.
- A person acts righteous around leadership, but differently everywhere else; what does that reveal? Answer: fear of men more than fear of God. That is image management, not integrity; you're trying to manage a false image of yourself. Integrity means being consistent everywhere, in any moment, when things are hard and inconvenient for you. You still uphold your vows, your values, your morals, and your ethics.
- What's harder for most people? Answer: actually changing privately. Many people know truth but struggle with transformation; it's easier to look righteous publicly than to change privately. Until you apply what the Bible says, it's just words on the page. You got to believe what you're reading to be able to put it into action; you ain't reading it just to remember it, you're reading it so you can apply it.
- A brother or sister apologizes sincerely after wrongdoing; what shows good character? Answer: forgive wisely and allow room for repentance and growth. You really see the sincerity over time; your actions have to show that you sincerely apologize for the wrong you did. And let the bitterness go: a brother held on to anger years after the other person apologized, when the brother thought it was over with. Let it go.
- Which matters more to the Most High? Answer: true integrity from the heart. The Most High always gave us the answer; he said, I set before you death and life, choose life. He repeated himself over and over, and you still going to choose the wrong answer.
- Integrity also means you're not going to get everything right. When you mess up: I got to say something. That is integrity too.
- If you struggling with porn, a lot of brothers struggling with that; tell someone. Get someone who has integrity, go to that person and let them know, look, I'm struggling with this. That hatred, that envy will come out and show itself, I don't care if it's five years later.
- Murmuring is a lack of integrity and character, and we have a lot of it; murmuring is big in the body. We got to shut that down.

## In Closing

Wisdom is not proven by how deep you sound, how many scriptures you know, how spiritual you appear. Wisdom is revealed through consistency, discipline, humility, honesty, and integrity.
