- Then said Abishai the son of Zeruiah unto the king, Why should this dead dog curse my lord the king? let me go over, I pray thee, and take off his head.

- And the king said, What have I to do with you, ye sons of Zeruiah? so let him curse, because the Lord hath said unto him, Curse David. Who shall then say, Wherefore hast thou done so? So King David said, you know what, let this man curse me out. Now David is a king. His mighty men are ready to put this dude to death. But he says, let him be. He said it had to be the Lord that spoke unto him and told him to come and curse me.

- And David said to Abishai, and to all his servants, Behold, my son, which came forth of my bowels, seeketh my life: how much more now may this Benjamite do it? let him alone, and let him curse; for the Lord hath bidden him. Now King David is in his repentant state. He said, let him curse, because the Lord sent him to curse me.

- It may be that the Lord will look on mine affliction, and that the Lord will requite me good for his cursing this day. And my hope is that in this man cursing me for the evil I did, I pray he will have mercy. This is why David is a man after the Lord's heart, because he was able to acknowledge his errors. He is able to acknowledge his faults, his shortcomings, his sins, and always go to the Lord asking for mercy.

- And as David and his men went by the way, Shimei went along on the hill's side over against him, and cursed as he went, and threw stones at him, and cast dust. So as they are walking, this man went up on the hillside above them, following them, throwing stones at them.

- So David saw this as an opportunity to turn back to the Lord, repenting for what he did to Uriah. But what I want you to see is, whether it be lust and covetousness, then came about the hatred to the point where you wanted to put that brother to death, and did. The same exact way envy, jealousy, rebellion against God caused King Saul to do it to King David. So everyone has their path, but that icing on the cake is bitterness for everyone.

**[Sirach 37:19-20](/bible/sirach/37#v19)**  *[[2:20:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9CXnvRB7es&t=8420s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/37#v20)</sup> There is one that sheweth wisdom in words, and is hated: he shall be destitute of all food.

Your neighbour sees you driving out of your driveway or going to your car in your purple and gold. Oh, you are with those guys? I saw them here, I saw them there. You are with those guys?

Due to your lack of application, or examining yourself, you are unprofitable to yourself. You were beneficial to everybody else. Everyone that came your way, you were able to answer questions. - So my point is, Satan is right at your door. Because if those other building blocks, that may seem like nothing to you, are already in place, all he needs is the bitterness to be full-fledged and overflowing. So as you get a message or an order or a directive and you see your phone, you are like, here we go. When that happens, Satan is right there like, I bet he did not ask anybody else to do it. **[1 Corinthians 9:27](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v27)**  *[[2:24:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9CXnvRB7es&t=8691s)]*

**[Proverbs 16:2-3](/bible/proverbs/16#v2)**  *[[2:26:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d9CXnvRB7es&t=8803s)]*

- But the Lord weigheth the spirits. But the Lord knew what was going on within King Saul. The Lord knew what was going on within King David. The Lord knows what is going on within each of us.

- Commit thy works unto the Lord, and thy thoughts shall be established. So as your thoughts or your intentions are discerned by the word of God, God says I will establish your thought process, I will establish your intentions according to the word of God. If you are not living a double life, but you are committing your works unto the Lord, the Bible says he will establish your thoughts. That bitterness that is brewing within us, that bitterness that is stirring up, that hatred, the Lord will establish all of that if you commit yourself to the works.

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)</sup> But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

- Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone. So when King Solomon wrote it in Proverbs, meaning the writings of wisdom, Christ is writing the same wisdom in Matthew chapter 18. The Lord wrote it in Leviticus chapter 19. What do you think the Lord is telling you about not bearing a grudge, but rebuking thy neighbour and not suffering sin upon him? Does that mean you are going to tell everybody? No, you go to that person and let them know what the offence is. So the Lord said it in the law. King Solomon said it in the writings of wisdom. And Christ himself is saying the same exact thing. These are all the ways, both in the law and the prophets, to show how to alleviate your soul being destroyed due to bitterness.

- If he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother. Because what is the objective of going to that person? To resolve it and to gain that brother, to gain that sister, to rekindle and gain that love in the marriage. Because we went over husbands not being bitter against your wives. The hope is to regain it. That is the hope. Regain that brotherly love that the Lord commanded us to endeavour to keep.

- But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

If you do not want to hear your brother out to gain your brother, you might need to be put away from the church. Why? Because you do not love your neighbour as yourself.
