## In Closing

We have been talking and going over the ills in the black and brown community, the raising up of children, and the trauma that many of us suffer from. But there is hope at the end of the rainbow.

We love prophecy. We love walking with the giants. We love those deep, dark mysteries. But unless we get God's laws right in our lives, we are not making the kingdom. You can fool yourself if you want — you are not making the kingdom on earth. I know people and brothers like the mysteries and the parables, but if we cannot get this together, this basic law of honouring your father and mother, we are messed up. We are not getting any kingdom.

The Most High loves judgment, and judgment is therapy. It is therapy for all of the abuse, all of the hardship, all of the pain. Look at that Life magazine cover — Medgar Evers' wife sitting at the funeral beside her son, trying to console the boy whose father was murdered in the driveway of their own home. He had to teach his family how to run into the bathtub to shield from bullets, because the bathtub was cast iron. They lived through that. You can feel that pain. That is the kind of thing our people went through, and then not even to know why it is happening. We have the answers here.

That is why we need that good therapy. Somebody has got to pay for this. I do not understand that garbage Christianity that wants you to bypass all this and leave our people in trauma forever with no therapy. We need that therapy, and the Lord is going to give it to us when we do right by each other. When we do right by each other — children, parents, brothers and sisters — the Lord is going to honour us with that. But we have got to do better. We have got to get ourselves together so that we can get that therapy, because we need it.
