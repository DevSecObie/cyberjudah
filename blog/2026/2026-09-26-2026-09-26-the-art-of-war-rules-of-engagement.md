**[James 4:1](/bible/james/4#v1)**  *[[2:04:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=7485s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/james/4#v1)</sup> From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?

- Wars and fightings come from the lusts in your members: Satan knows your needs and your battles and manipulates a situation to produce the outcome he wants.

- Nobody is innocent in an argument; it takes an aggressor and a receiver, and how you reply to the aggression dictates how the war goes.

- Know your own triggers and document them: the day, the hour and the angle a fight always comes from, so a plan is in place when it comes.


**[Colossians 3:5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)**  *[[2:06:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=7571s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

- Mortify your members on the earth: fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, covetousness.

- Whatever member is in you, sexual or anger, is the cause of the contention in the marriage; identify what in you keeps the marriage stagnant.


**[Ephesians 4:3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)**  *[[2:07:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=7659s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)</sup> Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

- An argument is a choice: someone decided to act on a thought, or the other decided to take the enticement; God called us to peace, not contention.

- Endeavour means fight, to keep the unity of the spirit in the bond of peace; know what was whispered into your thoughts that you agreed to bring to your spouse.


**[Ephesians 5:23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)**  *[[2:10:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=7840s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.

- The husband is the head and the final authority, so when a wife approaches him it is a tactical engagement, never just a thought or an idea; ask whether the concern is legitimate or a thought planted to start an argument, and whether he came home from a hard day.

- What to expect when you approach a head: defensiveness, a desire for preeminence, disagreement taken as a challenge to his authority, advice rejected, withdrawal from the conversation, questioning your approach, pride and stubbornness, a desire to control the outcome, delayed decisions, selective listening.

- Sisters, your approach and your timing are everything: get to the point in a sentence, not a book; act on your own outside his guidance and that is the next argument.


**[1 Peter 3:7](/bible/1-peter/3#v7)**  *[[2:16:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=8180s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/3#v7)</sup> Likewise, ye husbands, dwell with them according to knowledge, giving honour unto the wife, as unto the weaker vessel, and as being heirs together of the grace of life; that your prayers be not hindered.

- Dwell with your wife according to knowledge, the scriptures, not your emotions; there is an evil way and a right way to deal with her, and saying a thing out of spite because you know her triggers is the evil way.

- Give honour to the wife: a man who looks at every flaw cannot be happy; understand how she was created and order, structure and teach her within that creation.

- What to expect from the weaker vessel: emotion, many words, a conversation that goes in every direction, being easily offended, correction taken personally, the past brought up, unrelated issues, withdrawn affection, and the expectation that you read her mind.


**[Proverbs 13:10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)**  *[[2:21:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=8506s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)</sup> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.

- The first cause of fights in a marriage is pride and the need to be right: one party will not receive the correction or hear the other, and by pride comes contention.

- The well-advised has wisdom: I may be right, but to keep the peace I will suffer wrongfully and let the Lord intervene, as he did for Sarah with Abraham.


**[Ephesians 5:33](/bible/ephesians/5#v33)**  *[[2:23:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=8590s)]*

> <sup>[33](/bible/ephesians/5#v33)</sup> Nevertheless let every one of you in particular so love his wife even as himself; and the wife see that she reverence her husband.

- The second cause is feeling disrespected and unappreciated: the husband wants his providing acknowledged, the lights, the heat, the roof; the wife wants the children, the cooking and the house acknowledged.

- Love your wife as yourself and let the wife reverence her husband: appreciate each other's role, because everyone needs a compliment to keep going.

- Brothers show appreciation with affection, a kiss, a word that she looks nice; a woman was made to please her husband, and being told she does gives her purpose and joy.


**[James 1:19-20](/bible/james/1#v19)**  *[[2:26:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=8767s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/james/1#v19)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/james/1#v20)</sup> For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.

- The third cause is poor communication and failure to listen: be swift to hear and slow to speak, because a response ready before you have heard the concern may be a thought placed in you to ignite the argument.

- Meditate on the response: is it profitable, is it beneficial; the wrath of man works not the righteousness of God.


**[Proverbs 21:5](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)**  *[[2:27:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=8872s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)</sup> The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.

- The fourth cause is money: the diligent head tends to plenteousness, keeping a reserve for a rainy day, while the hasty tend only to want.

- Husband and wife think about money differently, so it has to be communicated: saving on one side, her needs on the other; one brother's long trouble ended when he gave his wife money to get her nails done.


**[Proverbs 25:28](/bible/proverbs/25#v28)**  *[[2:30:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=9006s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/25#v28)</sup> He that hath no rule over his own spirit is like a city that is broken down, and without walls.

- The fifth cause is a lack of self-control and anger: a man with no rule over his own spirit is a city without walls.

- Both know exactly what to say to break the other; rule your mouth.


**[Ephesians 4:31-32](/bible/ephesians/4#v31)**  *[[2:30:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=9054s)]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/ephesians/4#v31)</sup> Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32)</sup> And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.

- The sixth cause is unresolved resentment and bitterness: put away the grudge from five conversations or ten moons ago.

- Bringing up a past situation in an argument shows you never grew from it and were waiting to use it; that is why the love and affection never came; be kind one to another.


**[Philippians 2:4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)**  *[[2:32:35](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=9155s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)</sup> Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.

- The seventh cause is looking only on your own things: the husband feels the financial weight, the wife feels the children and the house all day, sunup to sundown, which will drive anyone to the edge.

- Give her the space and rest to stabilise her mind and be her best for you; and sisters, think on the husband's burdens too, the work and the bills men do not talk about.


**[1 Corinthians 7:3](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v3)**  *[[2:35:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=9300s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v3)</sup> Let the husband render unto the wife due benevolence: and likewise also the wife unto the husband.

- The eighth cause is a lack of affection and emotional connection: the husband renders due benevolence to the wife and the wife to the husband, because both need it whatever they say.

- Be always ravished with her love; a lack of it in the marriage breeds anger on both sides.


## In Closing

*[[2:36:40](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eNMvid6j-qk&t=9400s)]* There are rules of engagement in every aspect of life, every direction, every thought that filters through your mind: what somebody said, why they said it, what response it was trying to get from me, how Satan is moving and what he is planning for the future. Write it down, study it, acknowledge it, and fight.
