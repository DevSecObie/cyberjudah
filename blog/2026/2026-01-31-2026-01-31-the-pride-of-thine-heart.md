## Introduction

*[[3:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_zOB4Ip0MNk&t=183s)]* Captain Zakar teaches from IUIC Oklahoma City, with Captain AZ, Captain Noah, Officer Ari and later Captain Paloo beside him. After opening with the history of how Constantine and the Council of Laodicea turned the Sabbath into Sunday, and how late Christianity reached Ethiopia, the class defines pride and shows it as the root of every fall: pride is departing from your maker, it is first on God's list of hates, and it destroyed the northern kingdom, Judah, Assyria, Nebuchadnezzar, Antiochus and Herod. The last and proudest is Edom, and the class closes with the eagle of 2 Esdras 11 whose pride has come up unto the Mighty.

## Class Questions

- **Where did Sunday worship come from?** From Rome. Constantine, still a sun worshipper, declared the day of the sun the day of rest in 321, and the Council of Laodicea forbade resting on the Sabbath as 'Judaizing,' ordered work on Saturday, and cursed those who kept it. The Catholic Church itself says no scripture requires Sunday.
- **What is the difference between being proud and being prideful?** Being proud of who God made you is fine. Being prideful is an inflated sense of self-importance that looks down on others, cannot admit fault or ask for help, and rejoices when others fail. Sirach 10:12 says it begins when a man departs from God, and it can be quiet, like refusing help or holding a grudge.
- **Why is pride so serious to God?** It is first on his list of seven abominations in Proverbs 6:16-17 and is hateful before God and man in Sirach 10:7. Our whole life is under two hours in God's time and we die like beasts, so a creature that did not make itself has no ground for pride. Satan hunts the proud, 1 Peter 5:8.
- **Which kingdoms did pride destroy?** Ephraim and Judah, Assyria under Sennacherib, Babylon under Nebuchadnezzar, Greece through Antiochus, and Rome through Herod. Obadiah and Jeremiah 49 say the proudest of all is Edom, who set his nest among the stars and says who shall bring me down, and God says I will bring thee down from thence.
