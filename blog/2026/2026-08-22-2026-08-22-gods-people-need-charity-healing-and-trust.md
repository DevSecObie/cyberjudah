## Introduction

Today's class is going to be based loosely, well, it is for the whole nation, I should say, not just for Sack, but some of the things should strike home with you all concerning things that have happened in the past here.

Today's class is entitled God's People Need Charity, Healing and Trust. So, a brief statement. The body of Christ cannot grow in unity while hatred, unresolved wounds, suspicion and selfishness remain amongst God's people. We come out of the world, we come to this truth, and we all come in here sick. We all have issues that we have to overcome. But if we want to grow a nation, it begins on the individual level, as a man or a woman, and then we come collectively as a body. Some of us in our families come collectively as a family to help build. But as long as we have things that are unresolved, that we do not change, you are going to hit a ceiling. The congregation will hit a ceiling.

I want you all to take notes, and this is for the whole nation online. People watch online. Most High Christ bless you all. Stay faithful. Stay godly. Happy Sabbath.

I spoke to the men out here. They went to camp today. They said Sack has tremendous potential. This whole area between Sack, San Francisco and Oakland, they said it is tremendous potential, and we want to see God's word flourish here.

The trip: Deacon Abio spoke and said he was coming out here, and I told him I am going to make the trip, Bishop. So we made the trip and it turned into something else. But it is because we do care. Even though you do not hear from us, do not think our eyes and ears are not paying attention. It is just on the back end things have to go through a process, but know for certain we do not move like that.
