## Introduction

*[[6:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zdkkMC_4xfg&t=415s)]* This class sets out to settle who Cornelius the centurion was. It traces the split between Judah and Ephraim from Solomon's strange wives to Jeroboam's golden calves, and shows how God said through Hosea that the northern kingdom would be not a people. Judah then treated the northern tribes as Gentiles, strangers and unclean, while the prophets promised the two houses would be joined again. Hezekiah's Passover, where unclean men of Ephraim and Manasseh were pardoned and healed, is the key to Peter's vision. Cornelius came from Caesarea, on the land of West Manasseh, so the man Peter was told not to call common or unclean was a northern Israelite, not a heathen.

## Class Questions

- **Was Cornelius an Italian Gentile?** No. The Italian band was a Roman army unit. He lived at Caesarea Maritima, below Dor, in the land Joshua 17 gives to Manasseh, and he gave alms and prayed always like the captive Israelite Tobit.
- **Why did Judah call the northern tribes Gentiles and unclean?** God named Hosea's son Lo-ammi, ye are not my people, and Isaiah said Ephraim would be not a people. Judah held to that and ignored Hosea's promise that they would again be called the sons of the living God.
- **What does Peter's vision of the sheet mean?** It is about men, not food. The sheet knit at the four corners is scattered Israel, and what God hath cleansed matches Hezekiah's Passover, where God pardoned and healed unclean men of Ephraim and Manasseh.
- **Who are the Jews and Gentiles in Romans 9?** Paul explains them by Hosea 1. The Jews are Judah and the Gentiles are the northern kingdom that was once not God's people.
- **Who was the woman at the well?** An Israelite of Samaria. She called Jacob our father and said our fathers worshipped in this mountain.
