## Introduction

*[[1:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IprnAchscMs&t=60s)]* On the street in Raleigh, the brothers open the Bible with a young man who stops to argue. He wants to know why the judgment is always laid on the so-called white man, says Esau was a black man and Jacob's twin who sold his own people out, and brings the book of Jasher to say that Esau killed Nimrod.
