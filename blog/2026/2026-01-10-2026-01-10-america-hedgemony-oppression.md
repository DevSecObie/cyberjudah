## Introduction

*[[7:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=udNwS8tEwrg&t=465s)]* The class opens with a call to repent in earnest. The gifts and calling of God are without repentance, but those who return to fornication and other sins after being called will not inherit the kingdom, so every brother and sister must examine themselves. It then turns to America's hegemony. Revelation 17 shows the ten horns, Europe, giving their power to the beast after 1945 through NATO. They will one day hate the whore and burn her. The pale horse rules the fourth part of the earth, and the Monroe Doctrine, now the 'Donroe Doctrine,' is the throne in the sides of the north. God set this oppressor up as his sword, and the nations drinking from America's cup will turn against it.

## Class Questions

- **Does God's calling excuse returning to sin?** No. The calling is without repentance, but those who go back to fornication and have not repented shall not inherit the kingdom (2 Corinthians 12:21; 1 Corinthians 6:9).
- **Who are the ten horns that gave their power to the beast?** The European nations, which after World War II placed their strength under America through NATO. Revelation 17:16 says they will turn and hate the whore.
- **What does 'the fourth part of the earth' refer to?** The Americas, where the pale horse was given power to kill with sword, hunger, death and the beasts of the earth (Revelation 6:8).
- **Why is there no forgiveness for a traitor?** Betraying your people breaks the command to love your neighbour, meaning your own nation. Judas sold Christ and was not forgiven.
- **What does 'never trust thine enemy' mean?** Sirach 12:10 says his wickedness returns like rust on iron. The kinder face America showed after the civil rights era was never a change of nature.
