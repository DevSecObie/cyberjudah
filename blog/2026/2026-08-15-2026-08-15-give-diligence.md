## Introduction

The class opens with the Lord's prayer and the Sabbath greeting. Captain Zakar teaches, with Captain Azariah and Officer Yan alongside him.

It runs in two halves that meet at the end. The first is history: how Esau changed the commandments and the Sabbath day on his own authority, and where the power to do that came from. The second is the answer to it: the Most High has sent saviors into every captivity, that is what the prophets were, and any man who understands this word has been sent for the same purpose. The line the whole class turns on was written during the night while the class was being put together. A prophet's purpose is always greater than his rank.

## In The News

The documents opened before the scriptures:

- The Convert's Catechism of Catholic Doctrine, pages 49 and 50. It lists the second commandment as taking the name in vain and the third as keeping the Sabbath, which does not match the text and is not supposed to. The commandment against graven images has been removed outright, and the tenth has been split in two to keep the count at ten.
- Cardinal Gibbons, The Faith of Our Fathers, page 111: read the Bible from Genesis to Revelation and you will not find a line authorising the sanctification of Sunday. The scriptures enforce the observance of Saturday, a day which we never sanctify.
- The same catechism answering why. Sunday is observed instead of Saturday because the Catholic Church, at the Council of Laodicea, transferred the solemnity. They give the council and they give the date.
- Canon 29 of that council itself: Christians must not Judaize by resting on the Saturday Sabbath, but should work that day and honour Sunday instead, and anyone caught keeping the Sabbath could be declared anathema, meaning cut off. That threat still works. In the remote villages in India where the church holds the resources, our people said plainly they knew the idols were idols and would not remove them, for fear of being cut off.
- The list of atrocities against black Americans put on the screen by century, from the transatlantic trade through the terror after emancipation, the massacres of the 1900s, Tuskegee, the church bombing in Birmingham, the murdered civil rights leaders, and police violence into the present. The challenge with it: name a century in which our people have had pure peace under this man. And remember that list is only what was reported.

- If thou givest him not warning, the same wicked man shall die in his iniquity, but his blood will I require at thine hand. Every man dies for his own sin, so why is a watchman's soul on the line for somebody else? Because you were sent here to save them and you agreed to it before you came.

- Yet if thou warn the wicked, and he turn not from his wickedness, he shall die in his iniquity, but thou hast delivered thy soul. Warning them is what clears you. Their answer to it is theirs.

**[Galatians 1:15-16](/bible/galatians/1#v15)**  *[1:22:53]*

- Read the order. Separated, then called, then the Son revealed, then sent. When it pleased God means at the appointed time in whatever captivity it is, and it is how the agreement made in the heavens plays out on the earth for every one of us who comes to understand this word.

## In Closing

The first half of the class establishes what was done and by whose power. The commandment against images was struck out of the catechism, the Sabbath was moved by a council that recorded its own date, and a man who could do that had to be getting his authority from somewhere. Genesis 27 gave Esau the fatness of the earth by the sword, and Matthew 4 shows where the sword came from.

The second half is what we do about it. In every captivity the Most High has sent saviors. Moses in Egypt, Joshua after him, Samuel, Nathan, David, Elijah, the twelve prophets, and Obadiah says plainly that saviors shall come up on mount Zion to judge the mount of Esau, which is this captivity and this generation. Every one of them was known before he was formed and every one of them said send me.

So the watchman's soul is on the line for people who will each die for their own sin, because the warning is the thing he agreed to carry. And the whole class comes down to one line: a prophet's purpose is always greater than his rank. Not the numbers, not the position, not how it feels. Do not merely remember that you are a soldier. Remember what you were sent to do.
