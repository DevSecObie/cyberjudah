## Introduction

*[[7:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sgeJEYu1ZEg&t=448s)]* This class reads Donald Trump as the sword of God: a king whose heart the Lord turns wherever he wills to judge the nations. The truth that makes Israel free says a day is coming that burns as an oven, and it will not come until the man of sin, America, is revealed. America threatens Iran, seizes Venezuela, eyes Greenland and bombs nations across the earth, and the class matches this with Psalm 10, Isaiah 14 and Isaiah 47. The Lord's long-suffering is the only reason Israel has not been swallowed by the nations, and he calls every Israelite to repent and gather. The sword then turns on Amalek, the Philistines, Moab, Ammon and finally Babylon, and the apostles and prophets rejoice when God avenges them.

## Class Questions

- **Why call Trump the sword of God?** The king's heart is in the hand of the Lord (Proverbs 21:1), and God sets up the basest of men to judge the nations (Daniel 4:17). Jeremiah 47:6 describes the sword that cannot be quiet while the Lord gives it a charge.
- **Why hasn't Israel been destroyed by the nations?** If it had not been the Lord on our side, they had swallowed us up quick (Psalm 124). It is of the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed (Lamentations 3:22).
- **What must happen before the day of Christ?** A falling away, Israel's captivity, and the revealing of the man of sin, the son of perdition (2 Thessalonians 2:3).
- **Why does the day of the Lord seem slow?** The Lord is longsuffering, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance (2 Peter 3:9).
- **What will the prophets do when Babylon falls?** Rejoice, for God hath avenged them on her (Revelation 18:20).

## In Closing

*[[2:08:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sgeJEYu1ZEg&t=7685s)]* The sword of the Lord will not rest until every nation is judged and Babylon is burned. Repent, gather together, keep charity toward one another, and rejoice when God avenges his apostles and prophets.
