## Class Questions

- **What is prophecy supposed to do for us?** Give us courage to put away our idols and secret sins, as Asa did when he heard Oded, 2 Chronicles 15:7-8. It is doctrine poured out as prophecy, Sirach 24:33, not entertainment.
- **Who made you a watchman?** The Lord, Ezekiel 3:17, not the Bishop, deacons, or captains. You were called to warn Israel to repent.
- **Where in the vision is the current war?** 2 Esdras 15:20, the Lord calling the kings of the earth against one another; Joel 3:2, the nations gathered to the valley of Jehoshaphat; Isaiah 13:17, the Medes stirred up against Babylon.
- **Why does the captain say Trump is doing this for the future?** America knows the prophecy that the Medes will be stirred up against them and will not regard silver or gold. Killing Soleimani and the Ayatollah only united the Muslim world and made martyrs.
- **What is the Fattah 2 in the class?** A hypersonic glide missile that manoeuvres mid-flight and defeats the Iron Dome. The Lord put the spirit on the scientists; it is the technology the prophets saw in vision, the dragon tails over Israel.
- **Is 2 Esdras 15:28-30 fulfilled?** Not yet. The dragons of Arabia and the Carmanians come with great power together, and the Arab world has not united and is against Iran. This is a birthing pain.
- **Why are the nations mad in Jeremiah 51:7?** They drank Babylon's wine, her lies and philosophies, which Daniel 8:25 calls his policy, and Habakkuk 2:8 says the spoiled remnant will spoil her back.
- **What is our power in this war?** The foolishness of preaching, 1 Corinthians 1:21. Not missiles or guns; foretelling from the Bible, which the Lord uses to bring America down.
- **Whom is the law made for?** The lawless, 1 Timothy 1:9-10: murderers, whoremongers, homosexuals, liars. There is repentance for all of them, and you sitting in the building is the proof they can be won.
- **How is even a prince persuaded?** By long forbearing and a soft tongue, Proverbs 25:15. Israel means prince of the power, so be patient and diplomatic with our people.
- **What do the five seeds of Isaiah 28 teach?** Different people need different instruments: fitches are delicate, rye is hard, barley is appointed in season. His God instructs the plowman to discretion, knowing how and when to speak.
- **Why did the brother not come in after you taught him?** Ecclesiastes 3:1, to everything there is a season. You may have spoken out of season; wait, like the brother who waited two years for Leroy to call.
- **What spirit did Christ rebuke in James and John?** Calling down fire on the Samaritans, Luke 9:55. Do not run to the death scriptures; know what manner of spirit you are of, brothers and sisters alike.
- **What does the Lord promise the prisoners of hope?** Zechariah 9:12, turn to the stronghold and he will render double, bend Judah as his bow, fill it with Ephraim, and raise up Zion's sons against Greece.
