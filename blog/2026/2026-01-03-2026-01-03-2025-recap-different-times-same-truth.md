## Introduction

*[[7:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l-1Rp_fFl7U&t=476s)]* A recap of 2025 through one lens: the times change, but the truth does not. The class argues that the Bible can only be understood from the right perspective, which means knowing who you are. It uses the Matrix, the Lion King and the billion-dollar genetics industry to show that identity matters, answers a pastor who says race is not biblical, and proves from Deuteronomy 28 that the curses identify Israel. It then turns to Trump's first acts in office, the rollback of DEI, and Black leaders calling him 'daddy.' The class went on to show how the TikTok sale to Oracle's pro-Israel owners and the retraining of its algorithm are used to hide Israel's teaching, as the chief priests once conspired to stop the apostles' word spreading.

## Class Questions

- **Why does identity matter for reading the Bible?** The Bible is a history of a people. Read without knowing who they are, it will be misunderstood, as Job 8:8 tells Israel to search out its fathers.
- **Is race a biblical idea?** Yes. Genesis 10 is a table of nations, and Sirach 26:20 tells a man to keep his seed that his race may be magnified.
- **How do the curses identify Israel?** Deuteronomy 28 foretells a people brought into Egypt again with ships, sold as slaves, serving other gods and wearing a yoke of iron. Only one people fits.
- **What knowledge is Israel destroyed for lacking?** The law (Hosea 4:6; Malachi 2:7): the Sabbath, the dietary laws and the other commandments.
- **Why try to control social media?** As in John 11:48 and Acts 4:17, rulers fear that if the word spreads, all will believe. Hiding it only proves the teaching true.
