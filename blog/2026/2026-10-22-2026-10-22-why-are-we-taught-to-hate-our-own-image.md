## Introduction

*[1:11]* Shalom. We are Israel United in Christ, here to teach the so-called blacks, Hispanics and Native Americans that you are indeed the Israelites of the Bible. On the street in Raleigh with a young sister, a brother and a seventeen-year-old named Elijah, the brother teaching shows why we are not to envy the oppressor or choose any of his ways: the dyed blonde hair our people put on is the yellow thin hair of the plague in Leviticus 13, and Christ himself had white woolly hair. From there, how a daughter of Israel is to dress, and the Sabbath: six days to labour and do your own pleasure, and the seventh to come back to who you are.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 3:31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)**  *[1:47]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

- Envy thou not the oppressor: Who is oppressing us today? Is there a race of people that keeps us on the bottom? The so-called white man. They are red and hairy.

- Don't envy them. Don't look at how they look and want to be like them in any way.

- And choose none of his ways: This is one of the ways our people, mostly our sisters, and brothers do it too, envy the people who keep us on the bottom and do all those atrocities to us. And it is real easy to get out of it. It is real easy to change.


**[Leviticus 13:29-30](/bible/leviticus/13#v29)**  *[2:38]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/13#v29)</sup> If a man or woman have a plague upon the head or the beard;
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/13#v30)</sup> Then the priest shall see the plague: and, behold, if it be in sight deeper than the skin; and there be in it a yellow thin hair; then the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is a dry scall, even a leprosy upon the head or beard.

- If a man or woman: We have men that do it, but women do it mostly.

- Have a plague upon the head or the beard: What is a plague? Is that something good? That is something bad.

- There be in it a yellow thin hair: A blonde hair. It can be red too; some of the sisters and brothers do red, blue, green, orange. But a blonde hair, a yellow thin hair.

- Then the priest shall pronounce him unclean: The priest pronounces him unclean.

- It is a dry scall, even a leprosy upon the head or beard: It is a leprosy upon the head or beard. We should not choose that; we should not wear it. Keep your natural hair. Rock your natural hair.

- When the sister says her natural hair gets aggravated: you have to realize you were created in the image that Christ was.


**[Revelation 1:14](/bible/revelation/1#v14)**  *[4:20]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/1#v14)</sup> His head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire;

- His head and his hairs were white like wool: White in color and woolly in texture. This is Jesus the Christ. She knew he was brown-skinned; it says he had white woolly hair. Did he dye his hair white? No. It naturally became white.

- As white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire: He had red and white in his eyes. Why? What was Christ's first miracle? He turned water into wine at the wedding feast, and he drank the wine. That is the prophecy in the beginning of the book.

- This is how you read the Bible to get understanding. This is how you get wisdom: precept upon precept. When you make up your own thing and go about it, that is why our people are lost.


**[Genesis 49:12](/bible/genesis/49#v12)**  *[6:13]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/49#v12)</sup> His eyes shall be red with wine, and his teeth white with milk.

- His eyes shall be red with wine: That is Christ, the eyes as a flame of fire.

- And his teeth white with milk: So now we have the white woolly hair. You have woolly hair. You should not get tired of it. It is a law. It stands up in the heat and in the winter. It is protection.


**[Revelation 1:14-15](/bible/revelation/1#v14)**  *[6:45]*

> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/1#v14)</sup> His head and his hairs were white like wool, as white as snow; and his eyes were as a flame of fire;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/1#v15)</sup> And his feet like unto fine brass, as if they burned in a furnace; and his voice as the sound of many waters.

- His feet like unto fine brass: White and woolly hair, eyes as a flame of fire, feet like unto fine brass. You see that? It is a law.

- What you need to do is get around righteous sisters who have the same hair, who are doing the same thing, who realize who they are. Come back to who you are, and when you feel like you are going off, the sisters are going to read you the scriptures.


**[1 Timothy 2:9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)**  *[8:02]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)</sup> In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety; not with broided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly array;

- This is the King James Version Bible, King James 1611.

- That women adorn themselves in modest apparel: There is a dress code in how we have to dress. The dress has to be modest.

- Modest apparel means no man should see your body. We should not see the shape of it out here. That is for your husband at home.


**[Exodus 20:8-10](/bible/exodus/20#v8)**  *[9:35]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/20#v8)</sup> Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/20#v9)</sup> Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/20#v10)</sup> But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:

- Remember the sabbath day: It says remember, because you forgot. We are bringing it back to your memory so you can keep it. You say you know the commandments; this is one of them.

- To keep it holy: Holy means separate, separate from all the other days of the week.

- Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: You have six days to do everything you want to do, your labour, your work, the gym. Elijah was on his way to the gym and then to work, on the Sabbath. Now you know.

- Ask your boss: I need to keep the Sabbath, I want Saturday off. You can work Saturday night or Sunday morning, however the business runs, all the way through Friday before it gets dark. Once it gets dark on Friday it is the Sabbath, from Friday night to Saturday night. It is easy to do. Just ask.

- But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: That is Saturday.

- In it thou shalt not do any work: You should not be doing any work, and you should not be going to the gym either. That is doing your own pleasure.

- At seventeen you should be training up with the rest of the men. They are out teaching the youth today, how to come back to who they are and stop the crime and the fighting, so we can get out of the condition we are in. By twenty you will be out here teaching everybody, just like we are showing you.


**[Isaiah 58:13-14](/bible/isaiah/58#v13)**  *[12:30]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/58#v13)</sup> If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy pleasure on my holy day; and call the sabbath a delight, the holy of the Lord, honourable; and shalt honour him, not doing thine own ways, nor finding thine own pleasure, nor speaking thine own words:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/58#v14)</sup> Then shalt thou delight thyself in the Lord; and I will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth, and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.

- If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy pleasure on my holy day: Going to the gym is your pleasure. God said, come back.

- And call the sabbath a delight: A delight is something you like, something you can't wait for. I can't wait until the Sabbath gets here.

- Not doing thine own ways: Not going to the gym, not the football game, the basketball game, skating in the park. None of that.

- Nor finding thine own pleasure: You are going to find more pleasure here, coming back to who you are, being around your family, keeping your own culture. Every other race of people out here has their culture. The Chinese keep to themselves and keep their own way, and they have their own food. That is what we are doing: coming back to ourselves. God said make a delight your culture, but you have to find out what it is first, and the only way is to be amongst the men who are studying and the family that is coming back to its culture.

- If you want, you can be racist like the Most High. Racist just means you love your own people, you love what you have. You don't have to be involved with them. You can have your own food.

- Then shalt thou delight thyself in the Lord; and I will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth: Do you think we are on high right now above all the other races? We are on low. But if we were on high, would it matter if somebody said they were racist against me? No. I am going to eat my food in my restaurant and wear our clothes.

- And feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: Get your heritage back. He is going to establish our ways.


## In Closing

*[16:16]* Get your heritage back. When we keep the Sabbath and come back to who we are, the Lord will cause us to ride upon the high places of the earth and feed us with the heritage of Jacob our father, and he will establish our ways.
