## Introduction

Bishop's first class back after the Passover blitz. Thanks to the booster club for helping financially and with prayers: the footprint is everywhere, and to all the men that traveled and laid their lives on the line, salute; but there is a lot of work in front of us.

Many people ask leadership, how do you do it? Thirty years in this truth, what is the key? It is very simple: listen, learn, and live. Be willing to listen to understand and apply. Ask the Lord, and the Lord will send people into your life to help you along your journey in his truth, and you have to be willing to take counsel. People that are easily offended when scriptures come out: your days are numbered in this. We all come to this truth with problems, all trying to get right, and we have to be willing to listen and take accountability to get better.

Chart shown in class comparing the elements in soil and in the human body: oxygen, iron, calcium, sodium, potassium, magnesium and the rest, in different percentages. When God made us from the dust of the ground, he manipulated those elements, what we call the periodic table today, to form man, land, and sea. Look outside at a tree taking in carbon dioxide and giving back oxygen: that is the wisdom of the Most High operating in everything seen and unseen.
- For she is the breath of the power of God, and a pure influence flowing from the glory of the Almighty. That is the breath that was breathed into us in Genesis 2. If those are the attributes of wisdom, you want to be tethered to wisdom, so you can discern, so you understand why people do the things they do, so you know the intents of a person's heart when they speak.

**[Luke 6:46-48](/bible/luke/6#v46)**  *[[2:25:45](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wTuOGE-zMSs&t=8745s)]*

> <sup>[46](/bible/luke/6#v46)</sup> And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/luke/6#v47)</sup> Whosoever cometh to me, and heareth my sayings, and doeth them, I will shew you to whom he is like:
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/luke/6#v48)</sup> He is like a man which built an house, and digged deep, and laid the foundation on a rock: and when the flood arose, the stream beat vehemently upon that house, and could not shake it: for it was founded upon a rock.

- And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say? You call on him but refuse to do the things he says; what is the purpose?
- Whosoever cometh to me, and heareth my sayings, and doeth them, is like a man which built an house, and digged deep, and laid the foundation on a rock: and when the flood arose, the stream beat vehemently upon that house, and could not shake it. He was warned that a flood was coming, and he prepared himself. That is us: prepare before the decree brings forth, be rooted in Christ, not just a hearer but a doer. If you only heard, the house stays as it was and the flood takes it. If you heard, there has to be some type of change.

**[Psalms 50:16-23](/bible/psalms/50#v16)**  *[[2:27:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wTuOGE-zMSs&t=8879s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/50#v16)</sup> But unto the wicked God saith, What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/50#v17)</sup> Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/50#v18)</sup> When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/50#v19)</sup> Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/50#v20)</sup> Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother’s son.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/50#v21)</sup> These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/50#v22)</sup> Now consider this, ye that forget God, lest I tear you in pieces, and there be none to deliver.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/50#v23)</sup> Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God.

- But unto the wicked God saith, What hast thou to do to declare my statutes? Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee. This is calling Lord, Lord, but hating to be instructed; hardened now.
- When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers. You are supposed to resist the devil, not entertain the devil. Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit: walking dishonestly, handling the word of God dishonestly.
- Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother's son. These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes. Just because he did not act right away does not mean you got away with it.
- Now consider this, ye that forget God: this is talking about Israel, because the other nations never knew God to forget him. Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God; and the salvation is to Israel.

    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/19#v14)</sup> Admonish thy friend, it may be he hath not said it: and if he have, that he speak it not again.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/19#v15)</sup> Admonish a friend: for many times it is a slander, and believe not every tale.

Admonish a friend: for many times it is a slander, and believe not every tale. Sometimes a brother admonishes you and now you are angry behind it and speaking against him. Why is it bothering you?
  - **[Proverbs 18:24](/bible/proverbs/18#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/18#v24)</sup> A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: and there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother.

    A man that hath friends must shew himself friendly: and there is a friend that sticketh closer than a brother. Friend and brother are synonymous in this teaching; the slander of Psalms 50:20 lands on both.

> <sup>[28](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v28)</sup> And God hath set some in the church, first apostles, secondarily prophets, thirdly teachers, after that miracles, then gifts of healings, helps, governments, diversities of tongues.

>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v31)</sup> But covet earnestly the best gifts: and yet shew I unto you a more excellent way.

These men are supposed to guide you and show you a more excellent way: how to serve God. That is the purpose for which they were sent.

That is the charge: teach the people the good and the right way to walk, and the people have to be willing to listen. But if ye shall still do wickedly, ye shall be consumed. The teacher's hands are clean when he has done his part; what you do with it is on you.
