## Introduction

*[1:02]* And we talked to the brothers about repentance and actionable items that they could actually do to begin to change their life while they were in prison. And then we were followed up by a Christian pastor, and his message to the prisoners was hope, hope. That ain't going to work. And as I'm sitting there listening, it was so agonizing, because when you're in a situation like that there is no rebuttal, no interruptions. So I was just squirming, squirming in my linen breeches, because I'm listening to him talk to them about hope. And granted, I understand there is a need for hope. You can't go through a situation like that and not have something to look forward to. But then I ultimately started thinking about our captivity: man, we are in that same position.

But the hope that he was telling them about was not the same hope that we have. Their hope is that by some miraculous act they will be released from prison. Our hope is that by some miraculous act we know we are going to get released from prison. Our hope is in our saviour Jesus the Christ. We know through these scriptures that we have something to look forward to. We know that there's an outcome for our obedience. Whereas the men in the situation I was talking about have no outcome from their disobedience, yet no one wants to tell them that they have to be obedient to the Lord: that in the time they are doing, they could begin to change some of those things while they are there, to move into a more positive position with the Lord, so that he can maintain their cause. Let's get started.

- The definition of hope: Hope, noun: a feeling of expectation and desire for a certain thing to happen; a feeling of trust. Synonyms: aspiration, desire, wish, expectation, ambition, aim, plan. - That at that time ye were without Christ: When we were away, when we were separated because of our iniquity, when we had departed from the Lord, at that time we were without Christ.

- Being aliens from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers from the covenants of promise: When we had departed from Christ we were strangers to salvation, strangers to repentance. We were friends with the judgment and the rod of wrath. That was our partner. The wrath of God was our homeboy; we were inseparable.

- Having no hope, and without God in the world: You had no hope of salvation. You had nothing to look forward to. The only thing the Israelites have to look forward to without Christ is wrath, destruction and death. That's all that's waiting for us when we turn away from God.

- A reason of the hope that is in you: So when somebody asks for the hope that lies in you: the hope within me is that I am an Israelite and I am not a stranger. I am a brother of the covenant, a son of the covenant. I am with Christ, with all hope in the world. If you go to 1 Peter 3 for me, that's what I'm going to tell you: I'm an Israelite, and I know the promises of the covenant are for me. A hundred percent. The salvation is for me, and the destruction of the wicked is for you. That's what I've got hope in.

**[Deuteronomy 18:15-16](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v15)**  *[49:30]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v15)</sup> The Lord thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me; unto him ye shall hearken;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v16)</sup> According to all that thou desiredst of the Lord thy God in Horeb in the day of the assembly, saying, Let me not hear again the voice of the Lord my God, neither let me see this great fire any more, that I die not.

- Christ as a genie: Now we have Christ. Everybody's saying Jesus, Jesus, Jesus. But I'm talking about people in the world who try to use Christ as some kind of genie for their evil. How are you going to tell somebody to have hope when they're consumed in evil? Are they going to have hope that they can do more evil? I just couldn't get it.

- The Lord thy God will raise up unto thee a Prophet from the midst of thee, of thy brethren, like unto me: This is Moses speaking to the children of Israel: somebody is coming, a prophet like me.

- According to all that thou desiredst of the Lord thy God in Horeb: Everything we hoped for of the Lord while we were at Mount Horeb. The one that's coming is going to be like unto me, and this is where your hope is going to be. We've got Christ now. That's why we just read in Ephesians that before Christ we had no hope. We were a done deal.

- Unto him ye shall hearken: Moses told our people that way back then, and some of our people held on to that hope, knowing one was coming, one like unto Moses. As an Israelite you had to continue to have hope to ride with that. Can you imagine how many years went by, how many captivities went by? First we had to look forward to him coming. That's what we had to have faith in: he's coming to get us and take away all this stuff we've got going on.

- For he shall save his people from their sins: What else are you going to hope for in an evil world like this? This is why we have hope in Christ the saviour. We know he's going to save us from our sins. We know this is the only chance we've got of getting the kingdom. There's no other way.

What are you doing? How are you going to make it? You've got some goats and some Levites somewhere that we don't know anything about? Where is your hope? What are you looking forward to? What are you aiming for? Where are your aspirations? You think you're going to beat this red man by yourself? Stop.

- His people: It was written that he's going to save his people. That's something to have hope in. Our forefathers, when they heard it, had to have hope in it.

**[Acts 1:6-7](/bible/acts/1#v6)**  *[53:39]*

- It is not for you to know the times or the seasons, which the Father hath put in his own power: So you've got a little more to hope on. In our position, we read it and say, it's not up to us to know when it's going to happen; it's not up to us to try to clock it. But he did tell us to watch and pray, to know the signs of the times. All of this brings us hope that we're on the right path, that we're lining up with what the Lord says he wants for us.

**[Isaiah 14:2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)**  *[55:44]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)</sup> And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

Y'all can say, oh, y'all racist. If it's in the book, the book says I'm supposed to have comfort in the scriptures and have some hope. I hope I get to rule over my oppressors.

- Aspire, don't daydream: I'm going to aspire and I'm going to aim. I'm not going to daydream about it or wish for it. I'm going to work hard to make sure it happens. That's what hope is: working hard for something to happen, not just standing by. That's why Christ said the kingdom doesn't come with observation. You can have hope that it's there; you've just got to work hard to get to it.

- A little progress, a little hope: That builds your hope. It's like us burning the fat right now. Some of us have been trying to burn fat for years. The spirit of Christ put it on the leadership to put certain things in place, and we've seen some stuff shrink that we hadn't seen shrink before. It gave you a little more: I don't even need both of them cookies. I'm going to eat one, but I ain't going to eat both. Why? Because I'm aspiring to something else. You get a little hope when you see a little progress. When we see Trump putting stuff in place, we get a little hope. When we see a little persecution on the prophets as they teach this glorious gospel, we get a little more hope.

## Class Questions

- **What is the opposite of hope?** The screen gives skepticism, caution, despair, concern, apprehension. It doesn't have unbelief, but the opposite of hope is unbelief: if you don't believe the Lord, you can't do anything he asks of you, not joyfully, not willingly, not to the best of your ability (Hebrews 3:12).
- **What lies in you, if someone asks the reason of the hope that is in you?** That I am an Israelite and not a stranger, a son of the covenant, with Christ; the salvation is for me and the destruction of the wicked is for you (1 Peter 3:15, Ephesians 2:12).
