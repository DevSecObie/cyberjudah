## Introduction

The class opens with Psalms 47 read before the congregation and prayer for the leadership, for the sick, and for the scattered of the diaspora in Africa, the West Indies and across Europe.

The subject is the FBA movement, foundational black American, and the delineation it preaches: black Americans on one side of a drawn line, Caribbean and continental Africans on the other. The class renames it foundational Babylonian advocates, because a movement whose whole pride is being here from the beginning has made a foundation out of Babylon. The title carries the second half of the problem with it: division among the dispersion. We are the dispersed. The word diaspora comes out of the scriptures concerning us, and a movement that hardens the lines between scattered Israelites works against the only thing that gathers them.

## In The News

The material shown and answered in class:

- The FBA position as its pioneer presents it: a lineage rather than a movement, ancestry that traces to the American south, ethnogenesis meaning your racial beginning is here, and a date of 1526 attached to it. No one was calling themselves foundational black Americans in 1526. That year is a year of ignorance, and the people using it cannot say what was happening in 1526.
- The complaint underneath it is real. Caribbean and continental blacks do speak ill of black Americans, and they do it with the colonizer's brain, having been colonized by the same man on the other side of the water. But the anger is aimed the wrong way. Where are the sundown towns of the Jamaicans? Where are the Africans hanging black Americans in Mississippi? The animosity runs toward people who look like you and were conquered like you, and the man who did it to both of you walks past untouched. That is displaced anger.
- The denial of the slave ships was answered plainly. The Europeans took silver, gold, diamonds and rubber out of Africa. They did not walk it out and they did not fly it out, because the aeroplane was not built until 1903. It went by ship. The ships were there before we were put in the bottom of them, and when slavery ended the ships were not thrown away, they went back to what they carried before.
- Malcolm X was played at length: the Bandung conference as a model of unity, catching hell for being a black man rather than a Baptist or a Democrat, America meaning prison, and revolution being founded on land. His Bible sat open at Psalms 83 in the display case at the museum in Harlem. He knew who he was. What he lacked was the name the scriptures give us, and what he saw of unity, he saw globally, never domestically.

## In Closing

FBA is the newest byword in a long line of them, and it is built on the belief that America lasts for ever. It does not. This is the land of exile, the land of darkness and the shadow of death, the prison house the prophets named, and the Most High has already said he will bring his people out of it and redeem them himself.

The complaint that started the movement is real: our brothers from the islands and the continent do speak against us, and we have spoken against them the same way for as long. But the answer Christ gave is to go to your brother alone and be reconciled before you bring the gift, and the answer the prophets gave is to gather before the fierce anger of the Lord comes, as a nation and not as a neighbourhood.

The bones in the valley did not come together because they found a better name for themselves. They came together when the word of the Lord was prophesied over them, and they only stood up as an army after the breath went in. Delineation cuts the army in pieces before it stands. Our job is the shepherd's job that Israel's shepherds refused: to search and seek the flock scattered upon all the face of the earth, and to bring them into one fold under one shepherd, twelve tribes, wherever they are found.
