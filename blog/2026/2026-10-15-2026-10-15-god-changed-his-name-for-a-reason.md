## Introduction

*[0:45]* Teaching in the street at a Caribbean festival in Raleigh, North Carolina, with a young brother who calls himself a Christian. Isn't the church supposed to be teaching the Bible? The Bible is only for one people on this side, and the man shouting at the brothers is mad because God is not for him. The same people who did this to our people gave us their Jesus. God is for the chosen people of God, the Israelites. Asked what the word gospel means, the young brother could not say. You claim a religion that is supposed to teach you the gospel, and you can't define the word.

## Scriptures Opened


**[Psalms 147:19-20](/bible/psalms/147#v19)**  *[0:52]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/147#v19)</sup> He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/147#v20)</sup> He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the Lord.

- He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel: God says the Bible is for these people on this side, the chosen people of God, the Israelites.

- He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them: Nobody has known God's judgments but us. That is why we are out here celebrating festivals like this: because we are destroyed and have lost our culture. Now we claim Jamaican, now we claim Caribbean, but God calls you Israel. That is not our culture.

**[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**  *[2:35]*

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

- The word gospel means good news. What people on the earth need good news? The people going through bad situations and bad times. Everybody doesn't need the gospel like we do. We are the oppressed, suffering curses to this day, still treated like slaves, still hanging from trees on college campuses.

- But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God: This is Moses talking to the Israelites. If we did not keep God's commandments, all these curses would come upon us and overtake us. We live under curses for disobeying his commandments. What you just saw is a product of our disobedience to our God; it is judgment for not keeping his commandments.

**[Deuteronomy 28:28](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v28)**  *[3:31]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v28)</sup> The Lord shall smite thee with madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart:

- The Lord shall smite thee with madness, and blindness, and astonishment of heart: Black people walk around in broad daylight lost, like we're blind, with no common sense, because we disobeyed God by following our enemies. God said he would make us crazy and delusional. Go around Raleigh: who is gathered at the bus stations, homeless, out of their mind, strung out on drugs? That's our people, because we disobeyed God.

**[Deuteronomy 28:16](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v16)**  *[4:00]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v16)</sup> Cursed shalt thou be in the city, and cursed shalt thou be in the field.

- Cursed shalt thou be in the city: Where would that madness reside? In the city. Name a city in North Carolina, Raleigh or wherever, and you will see our people smitten with madness. Look at my brother: he is a son of God, chosen to be above all nations, and he has no sense of self, because we broke God's commandments. Now we walk the street like the walking dead. That show is about our people; they are mocking us.

- And cursed shalt thou be in the field: In the cotton fields in the early 1800s. That was our foreparents. You don't learn this in school. It is your history in the Bible, and they won't teach it to you because they know you're a son of God.

*[5:14]* The young brother has a cross tattooed on his shoulder and says it means truly humble under the Lord. What happened to Jesus on that cross? He died. Then why walk around with a symbol of death? He was not the only one on a cross: two brothers hung beside him, and before and after, many of our people were hanged on crosses. That's how they killed us. They don't hang us on the wood now; they hang us from the wood. We are still being hung on trees.


**[Acts 5:29-31](/bible/acts/5#v29)**  *[6:38]*

> <sup>[29](/bible/acts/5#v29)</sup> Then Peter and the other apostles answered and said, We ought to obey God rather than men.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/acts/5#v30)</sup> The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom ye slew and hanged on a tree.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/acts/5#v31)</sup> Him hath God exalted with his right hand to be a Prince and a Saviour, for to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness of sins.

- We ought to obey God rather than men: Our people don't obey God because we don't even know how. The instruction he gave us is to spread the gospel, to get others to spread it and to gather our people, the Israelites. How can you spread the gospel if you don't know who you are, or what the gospel is?

- The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom ye slew and hanged on a tree: Our ancestors knew Christ died on the cross for us.

- For to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness of sins: Jesus Christ died for these people on this side. That is what we spread when we push the gospel: repentance and forgiveness of sins through Jesus Christ, the black Messiah. He didn't die on that cross so you could walk around with a tattoo.

**[Leviticus 19:28](/bible/leviticus/19#v28)**  *[8:37]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/19#v28)</sup> Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: I am the Lord.

- We are under curses because we broke God's commandments. Your body is a temple, and we were never supposed to do this to our skin. We learned it from the Africans and Egyptians, who are not our people; they had us in slavery before the white man, and they taught us to decorate our bodies like ornaments.

- Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: That is a commandment of God for the sons of God. God chose a people to be special unto himself, and it's hard to see we are special because we go by these names on this side. You say you're black; God never called you that. He calls you Judah, of the twelve tribes of Israel. You are a real Jew.

**[Colossians 2:8](/bible/colossians/2#v8)**  *[9:50]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/2#v8)</sup> Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.

- Beware: Beware means be cautious, be aware: there is danger before you. At this festival God is telling you to be cautious.

- Lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit: Beware of being tricked by the ways of man, their philosophies and vain deceits. Black people love festivals with culture, food and drinks, but none of this has anything to do with your culture. The signs here say King of Kings, which is Selassie; he even said himself that he is not the savior. The Lion of Judah sign is our true history. Through philosophy and vain deceit we follow the ways of man, thinking Jamaican or Caribbean is our culture. All of that was put on us by our enemies.

- After the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ: The traditions of men include the holidays. We learned many customs and cultures from our enemies. Did we just take them on willingly, or did something happen to us? Asked how our people got to this side of the earth, a young brother named Deon answered the slave trade and Christopher Columbus. What you weren't taught in school is that Columbus was a slave master.

**[Deuteronomy 28:68](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v68)**  *[12:34]*

> <sup>[68](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v68)</sup> And the Lord shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships, by the way whereof I spake unto thee, Thou shalt see it no more again: and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you.

- This is the same book our people have had for centuries in every church in the community, and still we haven't learned that our history is in it.

- And the Lord shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships: Moses is talking to a people who would go into Egypt, and Egypt is in Africa. So he was talking to people who had to look something like us, a melanated people. How would they go back into bondage? Again, with ships. What people in history went into slavery on ships? That's our people. Moses is talking to us. The Bible is your book.

- Thou shalt see it no more again: That is proof we are the Israelites. We say we are from Africa, from Jamaica, from the Caribbean islands, but God said we would not see our homeland again. Our people are in Jamaica and the islands, so we are not from those places. They scattered us there in slavery on ships.

- *[17:09]* And there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen: The ships brought us from our homeland to this side of the earth, and here we were sold as slave men and slave women in America. Columbus did not discover America; the Native Americans were already here. It is the same land we were brought to as slaves. A sign at the festival shows twelve hundred dollars, what they were selling us for, as products and merchandise.

- And no man shall buy you: We were bought as slaves, but buy here means to redeem, to bring back. Nobody has brought us back from slavery. That is why you are still standing in Raleigh, North Carolina: we have yet to be redeemed from slavery.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 20:2](/bible/exodus/20#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/20#v2)</sup> I am the Lord thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.

    God brought a people out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage. That is what Israel knew Egypt to be. The twelve tribes of Israel built the pyramids, enslaved to the Egyptians. Many of our people claim to be Egyptians, not knowing they were our slave masters before the white man.

**[Galatians 4:26](/bible/galatians/4#v26)**  *[15:20]*

> <sup>[26](/bible/galatians/4#v26)</sup> But Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all.

- The transatlantic slave routes brought us to North and South America, and they told us the ships came from the west coast of Africa. Before those ships docked there, captives were carried over to those places. Where were we coming from?

- But Jerusalem: Where is Jerusalem? In Israel. Where is Israel? A young brother said the Middle East. See how confusing that is. There is no such thing as the Middle East; where is the Middle West or the Middle South? Our enemies tricked us. They separated the landmass of Arabia from Africa with a canal, and now they claim it is separate. Before that canal, Israel was attached to Africa.

- Which is the mother of us all: That's the motherland: the land of Israel. That is our homeland, where we are from. When the Bible talks about Israel being taken on slave ships, that's y'all, our ancestors.

## In Closing

*[16:47]* What you are learning today you will never learn in school, in college or in history class, because they want the sons of God to stay at the bottom of society. We got next. We have rulership over this place. That is our promise: to have immortality and to rule everything once again.
