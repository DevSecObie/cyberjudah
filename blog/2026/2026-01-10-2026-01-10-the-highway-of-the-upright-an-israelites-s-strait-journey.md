## Introduction

*[[9:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vV5sPkqhVi4&t=554s)]* Captain Shem teaches that the highway of the upright is the strait path of keeping the commandments. It is difficult by design and full of obstacles, and at its centre is the family God ordered: Christ the head of the man, the man the head of the woman. He shows how Black and Latino marriage held strong through slavery and Jim Crow, then collapsed after 1965. Welfare rules favoured single mothers, and the result is fatherless children, weak men, obesity and poor finances. The class calls Israel to restore the father as the law of the house, gather together, and build group economics, trades, credit and health, until the ransomed return to Zion.

## Class Questions

- **Why is the path to the kingdom called strait?** Strait means narrow and difficult. The broad way offers liberty to do as you please, but the strait way requires keeping the commandments through every obstacle (Matthew 7:13–14).
- **What is God's order for the house?** Christ is the head of the man, the man the head of the woman, and God the head of Christ (1 Corinthians 11:3).
- **What changed in Black family life after 1965?** Programs designed after the Watts riot supported single mothers, making fathers unnecessary. Two-parent homes fell from about 80 percent to about 20 percent.
- **What is the role of a father?** He is the law and breadwinner of the house and shows his children the way they must walk and the work they must do (Exodus 18:20).
- **How does the class say Israel should rebuild?** Gather together (Zephaniah 2:1), bear one another's burdens, restore marriages, get healthy, and build group economics through trades, savings and good credit.
