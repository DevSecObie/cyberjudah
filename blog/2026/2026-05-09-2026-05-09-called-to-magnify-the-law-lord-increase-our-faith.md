## Introduction

The topic of today's class is going into faith. But not just faith: why we are called. Who called us, and what is our purpose? When we say called, that is a light way of saying we are requested of God to magnify his law. And in that process we must continue to build and increase our faith, and there is only one way to do that: with action and serving the Lord. Remember the scripture speaks of the great fiery trial of our faith: once we begin to keep God's commandments in the faith of Christ, the Lord starts testing us to show us that the faith he created us with is in you; you just have to bring it out. The meaning of this class is for us to increase our faith in the most high God in the spirit of Christ, to remember who we are, to bethink ourselves, to encourage endurance in righteousness, and to teach us how to stand against sin, deception, and spiritual wickedness in high places. Still coming off the Passover trips overseas, the flame has not gone out; oppression tried to lower it a little, but we have to keep it going.
