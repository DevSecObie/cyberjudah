## Class Questions

- **How does the spirit of Cain pass to Esau?** Cain murdered his brother by deceit, his line made weapons, and Lamech boasted of killing. Esau lives by the sword and comforts himself with plans to kill Jacob (Genesis 27:41).
- **Why was Cain's judgment not carried out at once?** God set a mark on him and deferred his destruction to a set time, as Luke 21:24 speaks of the times of the Gentiles being fulfilled.
- **What does Isaiah 54:16 call the weapon God created?** The waster. Zechariah 14:12 and 2 Peter 3:12 describe flesh consumed on its feet and elements melting with fervent heat.
- **What does 'framing mischief by a law' look like?** Psalms 94:20. Kidnapping is called law enforcement, and migrants with no other crime are called criminals.
- **Why does Ephraim in Latin America look so mixed?** Hosea 7:8 says Ephraim mixed himself among the people, and 1 Esdras 8:69 says the holy seed was mixed. The whitening policy was the enemy sowing tares, and God will sort his people at the harvest.
