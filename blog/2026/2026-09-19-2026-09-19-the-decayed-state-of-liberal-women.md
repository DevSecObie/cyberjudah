## Introduction

The class begins with endurance and humility, then presents the teacher's critique of modern social ideas, marriage, and parenting. Later sections discuss childbirth, support for mothers, and marital covenant. The title is retained from the recording for identification.

These notes paraphrase the lesson and distinguish the teacher's interpretations from the words of scripture. The broadcast includes broad negative claims about women, racial groups, and sexual minorities; those claims are not adopted as factual descriptions of those groups. News clips and third-party commentary are summarized rather than reproduced.

**Health context:** The recording dismisses postpartum depression and makes claims about prenatal vitamins and virginity. Those claims should not guide medical decisions. Postpartum depression is a treatable medical condition, not a moral failing; postpartum psychosis is an emergency requiring immediate care. Persistent symptoms deserve assessment by a health professional. [NIMH: Perinatal Depression](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/publications/perinatal-depression). Additional source notes appear beside the relevant sections below.

The opening prayer asks for forgiveness, healing, protection, and strength for those teaching. The teacher greets visiting captains before beginning a short lesson on adversity.
