## Class Questions

- **What does the title mean?** The love shown to our people by the Christian white man for four hundred years, from the papal bulls that authorized conquest in Africa to the pastors sent with his doctrine, produced the slave ship, the loss of our name and law, and the self-hatred in the clips: sold souls, poisoned babies, women calling white boys master. That is the hate hidden in Christian love.
- **How was Israel's unity broken?** Psalm 2 records the heathen saying, let us break their bands asunder and cast away their cords, and Psalm 83 records their crafty counsel to cut Israel off from being a nation. They split the kingdoms, renamed us by landmass, handed us rival religions, and now FBA turns the twelve tribes against each other instead of against Esau.
- **Why do Christians sound wrong when they explain the Bible?** Psalm 137:6 says if we forget Jerusalem our tongue cleaves to the roof of our mouth, and Ezekiel 3:26 says God makes the tongue cleave so the people are dumb and cannot reprove. Losing our identity meant losing the fiery law from God's right hand, so Christians make up doctrines and sound fine only to other Christians.
- **Why does the class end on judgment for the nations and mercy for Israel?** Jeremiah 25 says God begins the evil at the city called by his name, so the nations that hurt Israel certainly drink the cup and are given to the sword; Job 18 describes the wicked's light put out and his memory cut off. But Job 5 says he saves the poor from the sword, delivers them in six troubles and touches them with no evil in the seventh, if they acknowledge their iniquity and return.
