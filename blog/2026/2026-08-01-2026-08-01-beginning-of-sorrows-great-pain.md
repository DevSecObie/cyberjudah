- Behold, the day of the Lord cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate. The land is America, Mystery Babylon, going to be destroyed. The stars of heaven shall not give their light; the sun shall be darkened: there is going to be so much war that the sun and the moon are blacked out.
- I will punish the world for their evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; I will make a man more precious than fine gold: that is going to be us.
- Therefore I will shake the heavens: the heavens are the nations; he is going to shake them and cause them to fight against each other. They shall every man turn to his own people, and flee every one into his own land. All that democracy talk about being one people is done in that day: everybody is going to turn to his own people and his own land.
- Every one that is found shall be thrust through: this is talking about the destruction of America, and also the destruction of those over there calling themselves Jews. Their children also shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses shall be spoiled: that is what is going to happen over there in that land.
- Behold, I will stir up the Medes against them. God says he is going to stir up the Medes and the Persians: that is Iran. Stir them up against whom? Against America and Israel. That is what you see taking place right now. It is so ironic: ancient Babylon was destroyed by the Persians and the Medes, and today the Persians and the Medes are going to play a role in destroying Babylon again.
- Which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it. The Iranians do not care about your money. You cannot pay them off like you pay off other nations. You killed a man's father, his child, his wife; now you want to bargain with gold? He wants vengeance. And God is behind all of that.
- Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces. Their bows is talking about missiles. News clip read: Iran's missiles are hitting with growing precision, and US officials are questioning how; reports say the increasing accuracy has sparked concerns that hostile actors could be helping Tehran locate and target US military personnel and CIA facilities.
- And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees' excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah. How was Sodom and Gomorrah destroyed? With brimstone and fire. Was ancient Babylon destroyed with fire? No, it fell from within. So which Babylon is this talking about? America. And what is it telling you about Iran? Eventually they are going to develop nuclear weapons. The same way God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah with brimstone and fire, it is going to be the same thing with Israel and America.

## In Closing

Brothers and sisters, the time we are living in is the beginning of sorrows and great pain. The Lord has begun to visit the earth that he made, and everything you see taking place is him making preparation to deliver his people. The end is not yet, but the pains are going to increase like a woman in travail. Do not sit down and think everything is going to stay good like it is right now. Watch the signs of the time; get your mind together; stop worrying about the wrong things; keep the commandments, have faith and works so you are preserved from the perils that are coming; make you ready to the battle, and prepare your mind, your spirit, and your house for what is coming. With that, Shalom, Most High and Christ bless; Bishop is coming up next.
