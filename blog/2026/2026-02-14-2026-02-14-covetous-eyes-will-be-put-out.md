## Introduction

*[[6:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwW7PKHkqjs&t=416s)]* A captain at IUIC Boston takes on covetousness among the brothers: bad business dealings, money taken for jobs never done, and the reputation it costs the whole camp. Starting from 'walk in the spirit,' the class ties the blessing of reading the Bible to keeping it, shows how one covetous heart breaks every commandment, and closes with Gehazi, whose lie for Naaman's silver put the leprosy on him. The Lord exposes hidden sin as a mercy, and a covetous man who will not change is working toward having his lights put out.

## In The News

- *[[36:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwW7PKHkqjs&t=2184s)]* **A retired athlete asked whether any sum could bring him back to the ring says no, because that would be selling his soul. His podcast co-host answers that for a hundred million dollars, you can have his soul.** — Now we know his character. A covetous man will sell his soul, and some do it for a five-dollar rock or a cheeseburger. Character makes you predictable and dependable, and once people know yours they know whether to do business with you.
- *[[1:22:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwW7PKHkqjs&t=4956s)]* **A cartoon scene of a debt collector beating a man who asked for another week to pay.** — That is what happens to men who owe and do not pay. After the beating you still owe the money, so deal righteously and never put yourself there.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Galatians 5:16-17](/bible/galatians/5#v16)**  *[[8:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwW7PKHkqjs&t=526s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/5#v16)</sup> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)</sup> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.

- Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. The Spirit is the law, which is spiritual; the words of Christ are spirit and life. Walking in the ordinances, statutes and commandments is how you never do amiss.

- The flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh. There is a war inside you, and the old man keeps showing up suggesting the old hustles. You only win it by reading the Bible and applying what you read.

- If you do not read, pray or fast, miss every new moon and feast day, and show up on the Sabbath for appearance, you will not be able to fight the lust in your flesh. The Bible has to sit in your forehead like a receptionist, turning away every temptation before it gets in.

- The camp is not small any more. A brother making a delivery in another state got recognized by someone outside the truth. If he had been doing wickedness, that is how easily it gets found out.

Everyone wants blessings, and this is the formula: read four chapters a day, hear the word, and apply it. Read 'thou shalt not steal,' keep it, and nobody will ever call you a thief.

**[Sirach 7:36](/bible/sirach/7#v36)**  *[[24:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwW7PKHkqjs&t=1497s)]*

> <sup>[36](/bible/sirach/7#v36)</sup> Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss.

- Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss. Think before you act, because the flesh wants evil in every situation. Remember the ultimate end, which is fire.

- Even in the street there are principles; men get shot over a dollar on principle. How can you be in the truth, with fringes on, and have none? Take inventory of yourself and recognize the demons that plague you. They are not gone; they wait outside the door for you to leave the building.

- In business, do not short-change people. Think of the worst a man might do, and if that does not scare you, think of what the Most High will do to you for defrauding your brothers.

- Acknowledge that you are a sinner; that is the only way to become righteous, because once you know the sin is there you work on it. When people keep bringing your name up without being asked, the Lord is exposing you so that you fix yourself.

> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/20#v15)</sup> Thou shalt not steal.

A covetous man breaks every commandment: he puts money before God, works the Sabbath for the extra hours, lies to cover the job he never did, kills the witness or the man who fights back, and covets his brother's wife into adultery.

- When you are covetous you are your own god. You only see you, and you never seek counsel.

- All your sins will be exposed here because darkness and light cannot dwell together. See it as help, medicine for the soul, and thank the Lord for revealing you to yourself and to the brothers who can help. Covet good gifts: learn under the brother who has a skill instead of hating him for his accolades.

- Unless a man hold himself diligently in the fear of the Lord, his house shall soon be overthrown. Diligent means steady, earnest, painstaking effort. Idleness teaches much evil, so stay busy and stay in the Bible, where the stories teach you the do's and don'ts, like Joseph who ran.

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/5#v19)</sup> And he said unto him, Go in peace. So he departed from him a little way.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/5#v20)</sup> But Gehazi, the servant of Elisha the man of God, said, Behold, my master hath spared Naaman this Syrian, in not receiving at his hands that which he brought: but, as the Lord liveth, I will run after him, and take somewhat of him.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-kings/5#v21)</sup> So Gehazi followed after Naaman. And when Naaman saw him running after him, he lighted down from the chariot to meet him, and said, Is all well?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-kings/5#v22)</sup> And he said, All is well. My master hath sent me, saying, Behold, even now there be come to me from mount Ephraim two young men of the sons of the prophets: give them, I pray thee, a talent of silver, and two changes of garments.

> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/5#v23)</sup> And Naaman said, Be content, take two talents. And he urged him, and bound two talents of silver in two bags, with two changes of garments, and laid them upon two of his servants; and they bare them before him.

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-kings/5#v24)</sup> And when he came to the tower, he took them from their hand, and bestowed them in the house: and he let the men go, and they departed.

> <sup>[25](/bible/2-kings/5#v25)</sup> But he went in, and stood before his master. And Elisha said unto him, Whence comest thou, Gehazi? And he said, Thy servant went no whither.

> <sup>[26](/bible/2-kings/5#v26)</sup> And he said unto him, Went not mine heart with thee, when the man turned again from his chariot to meet thee? Is it a time to receive money, and to receive garments, and oliveyards, and vineyards, and sheep, and oxen, and menservants, and maidservants?
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-kings/5#v27)</sup> The leprosy therefore of Naaman shall cleave unto thee, and unto thy seed for ever. And he went out from his presence a leper as white as snow.

- Naaman, captain of the host of Syria, was a great man but a leper. A captive Israelite maid pointed him to the prophet in Samaria, and he came with silver, gold and raiment, because a man will give everything for his health.

- Elisha sent word to wash seven times in Jordan. Naaman was wroth because he expected the prophet to come out and wave his hand; he had better rivers at home. That pride almost cost him his healing. When the Lord says do, do; stop wanting God to do it your way.

- His servants talked sense into him, he dipped seven times, and his flesh came again like a little child's. He offered a blessing and Elisha refused it, as the Lord liveth, and sent him away in peace with his treasure.

- Gehazi, Elisha's servant, saw all that glitter and ran after Naaman with a lie: my master sent me, two sons of the prophets have come, give them a talent of silver and two garments. Covetousness made him lie, and when Elisha asked where he had been, he lied again. Sin was added to sin because he never controlled the covetousness.

- 'Went not mine heart with thee?' The spirit of the Lord reveals; Bishop once asked a brother twice whether he was dealing drugs, and the next class played the raid on his grow house. When the prophets ask about business you told nobody, take heed.

- The leprosy of Naaman shall cleave unto thee and thy seed for ever, and Gehazi went out a leper white as snow. God does not like covetous people; Gehazi was judged immediately. Many of us are not judged right away and think we are good, but the money we love will destroy us.

## Class Questions

- **Why is covetousness called idolatry?** Colossians 3:5 says so directly. A covetous man is his own god: whatever he wants is what happens, he listens only to himself and seeks no counsel. From there he breaks every commandment, working the Sabbath for money, lying to cover undone jobs, hating and even killing over what he coveted.
- **What should a brother do when his bad dealings keep getting exposed?** Recognize that the Lord is exposing him so he can change before it costs his life. After the first person speaks, go make things right with everyone else, apologize, make amends, and walk in the spirit. Be glad the sin was found, because your own sins will be your accusers on the day of judgment otherwise.
- **What does Gehazi teach about covetousness?** Elisha refused Naaman's treasure, but Gehazi ran after it with a lie, then lied again to Elisha, adding sin to sin because he never mortified the covetousness. The leprosy that left Naaman cleaved to him and his seed for ever. God judged him immediately; most of us are not judged that fast and mistake the delay for safety.
- **How does reading the Bible stop a man from doing amiss?** Revelation 1:3 blesses him that reads, hears, and keeps what is written. Four chapters a day puts the word in your forehead like a receptionist that turns temptation away before it enters, so when a stack of money or a beautiful woman is in front of you, 'thou shalt not steal' and Job's covenant with his eyes are already there.

## In Closing

*[[2:04:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wwW7PKHkqjs&t=7465s)]* Seek the Lord while he may be found, forsake your own way and your own thoughts, and return to him, because he will abundantly pardon. His ways are higher than ours, so choose his. A covetous man who keeps stealing, lying, and tilting the scale is working toward an early grave, but the man who reads, keeps what he reads, and deals justly with his brothers will have his days lengthened in the land.
