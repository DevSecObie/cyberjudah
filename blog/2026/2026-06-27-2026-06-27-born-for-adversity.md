> <sup>[35](/bible/romans/8#v35)</sup> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/romans/8#v36)</sup> As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter.

- Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword? As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long. Back then we were killed all the day long for Christ's sake, and it is going to happen again in the future.
- The point is that you should not allow anything to separate you from the love of Christ: not tribulation, not offenses, nothing from nobody. Nothing should separate you from keeping the commandments in Christ and building with the men the Lord sent you to build with.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:21-22](/bible/sirach/4#v21)**  *[[2:19:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmH1fhfwlzM&t=8384s)]*

> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/4#v21)</sup> For there is a shame that bringeth sin; and there is a shame which is glory and grace.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/4#v22)</sup> Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.

- For there is a shame that bringeth sin; and there is a shame which is glory and grace. Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.
- You cannot allow the reverence of anybody to cause you to fall. You have reverence for a person, and that person bugs out and says, I am out; he went through his trials and he failed. So because he failed when he was tested by the Lord, now you leave too, because you like him? You are supposed to be in this truth because you love the Lord.

**[1 Corinthians 3:12-13](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v12)**  *[[2:21:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmH1fhfwlzM&t=8481s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v12)</sup> Now if any man build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v13)</sup> Every man’s work shall be made manifest: for the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire; and the fire shall try every man’s work of what sort it is.

- If any man build upon this foundation gold, silver, precious stones, wood, hay, stubble; every man's work shall be made manifest. The gold, the silver, the precious stones are the brothers that go through the fire, that go through trials, and were not destroyed. The hay and the stubble are those who, when affliction, persecution, or even offenses come, cannot endure the fire: they give up.
- For the day shall declare it, because it shall be revealed by fire. That day is tribulation; when the fire comes, it tells what kind of work you are, whether you are that gold or that hay. Fire brings the impurities out of you. All of us have to go through that fire. The hay gets burnt up and leaves; the gold goes through the pain, comes out on top, and becomes stronger and better.
- You prayed, Lord, help me to love my neighbour as myself. Then God allowed your neighbour to offend you, and you are mad and do not want to forgive him. The Lord says, I am trying to answer your prayer: apply it. That is how the Most High works.

**[Isaiah 48:10](/bible/isaiah/48#v10)**  *[[2:24:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmH1fhfwlzM&t=8659s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/48#v10)</sup> Behold, I have refined thee, but not with silver; I have chosen thee in the furnace of affliction.

- Behold, I have refined thee, but not with silver; I have chosen thee in the furnace of affliction. In order for God to choose you, you have to go through the furnace of affliction. God is not choosing you if you have not gone through anything.
- We have to prove to him that we are worthy, and in order to prove that, he puts us through things that hurt, even among each other in how we deal with each other. You pray for strength: he makes bad things happen to make you strong. You pray for faith: he makes you go through things to build your faith. You pray to learn to love your neighbour: he allows your neighbour to offend you, to see if you will apply that law. This is how the Lord proves who is real and who is not.

**[Ecclesiasticus 2:1-7](/bible/sirach/2#v1)**  *[[2:26:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmH1fhfwlzM&t=8768s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/2#v2)</sup> Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/2#v3)</sup> Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/2#v4)</sup> Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully, and be patient when thou art changed to a low estate.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/2#v5)</sup> For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/2#v6)</sup> Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/2#v7)</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and go not aside, lest ye fall.

- For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity. To be acceptable means to be chosen. The same way gold is tried in fire, if you are acceptable, God puts you through adversity: tribulation, persecution, sickness. The way Job went through it. Every son that he chooses, he chastens; and the reason he puts us through these things is to make us perfect.
- My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation. Everybody in here came to serve the Lord, right? Then bad things are going to happen in your life. Why? Because he that departeth from evil maketh himself a prey: your adversary the devil is coming for you, and he is coming hard.
- Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble. When Satan comes and bad things happen, keep enduring. Do not make haste: some of you sisters go through problems in your marriage and want to up and leave. Trouble and affliction come through your spouse and your marriage too; do not make haste. Unless there is real danger or abuse and you have to separate for a time, when things get bad you should not give up or walk out. Things get serious and some of you say, I am out; that is fake and phony, and the Lord is showing who is fake and phony, who loves him and who does not.
- Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end. Cleave unto the Lord when you are going through bad things: when you are sick, when your child is sick, when you lost your house, when you are in prison. When you are by yourself, it is only you and the Lord; learn to cleave to him and talk to him. In the end of everything, you are going to increase. Brothers went through the fire and lost everything, and the Most High blessed them; they increased in the end.
- Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully, and be patient when thou art changed to a low estate. Sometimes we are going to be brought very low, but we must be patient.
- Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him. Did it say trust in Malachiah? Trust in God. Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and go not aside, lest ye fall.

**[1 Peter 4:12-14](/bible/1-peter/4#v12)**  *[[2:33:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LmH1fhfwlzM&t=9232s)]*

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-peter/4#v12)</sup> Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened unto you:

- Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened unto you. The fiery trials are the things we go through. Do not think it is strange, asking why is this happening in my life; it is to try you. When bad things happen in your life, you are being tried.
- But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ's sufferings; that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.
- If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye. They say we are a hate group because we teach that Jesus Christ is black and we are the Israelites. If we are hated and persecuted for that, happy are ye; for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you.

- Knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience. When you go through bad things, it is to try your faith, whether you believe or not. That is why that sister offended you, that is why that brother offended you: how are you going to deal with it? Do you believe, or are you going to leave?
- But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/5#v3)</sup> And not only so, but we glory in tribulations also: knowing that tribulation worketh patience;

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/5#v4)</sup> And patience, experience; and experience, hope:

- And not only so, but we glory in tribulations also: knowing that tribulation worketh patience. When you go through bad things, it teaches you to wait on the Lord.
- And patience, experience. The only way you become strong and learn experience in this truth is through tribulations. If you have not been through anything, you lack experience. You have to go through things.
- And experience, hope: and hope maketh not ashamed.

- Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might. As we go through things, we have to be strong in the Lord.
- Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil. Satan is coming to destroy you; to stand against him you must put on the full armour.
- For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.
- Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand. Did it say you are not going to fall? It did not say that. When you are fighting as a warrior, you will fall; but you must get back up. Sometimes you do everything and you still fall; you still have to stand.
- Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, the laws; and having on the breastplate of righteousness; and your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace.
- Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked. Your faith is your belief; your number one weapon. Remember Christ told Peter, I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: because even if you fall, you can get back up, because you believe.
- And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God: praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints.

## In Closing

We were born for adversity. Do not ask God for strength and not understand that you are going to go through things. The Lord is going to put you through some bad things; when they come, endure, cleave unto him, and keep standing. With that, the Lord, Yahawashi, and Christ bless.
