## Introduction

The class opens with prayer for the twelve tribes scattered through the four corners of the earth, for restoration, for the sick and the low in spirit, and for the wombs of the sisters to bring forth prophets in these last days.

Almost no videos in this one. It is a class of straight Bible, and it runs one argument from end to end: there is a spirit set on you, it was set before you were formed, and everything from that point is a question of which spirit you are walking in. Staying in the spirit does not always mean staying quiet, either. Sometimes it requires you to speak, and the spirit is what gives you the discernment to know which the moment calls for. It makes no sense to hold a book of instruction this size and apply so little of it.

- I was cast upon thee from the womb, thou art my God from my mother's belly. David understood that the Most High had been his God since before he was brought forth, and was already at work before his mother and father ever came together. None of it is happenstance and none of it is osmosis.

**[Galatians 5:7-9](/bible/galatians/5#v7)**  *[2:23:12]*

**[Galatians 5:16](/bible/galatians/5#v16)**  *[2:36:50]*

- This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. That is the whole class in one line. Not manage the flesh, not negotiate with it. Walk in the spirit and the other thing does not get fulfilled.

## In Closing

The spirit was placed before the womb and the election was made before the foundation of the world. Paul said it in Ephesians, Peter said it in his epistle, Esdras said it as a prophet, and Jeremiah was told it directly. Nobody in the room arrived by accident, and the teachers were never going to stay in a corner.

Which means the only live question is which spirit a man is walking in, and the scripture makes that easy to check. Obedience is one spirit and disobedience is the other. Caleb had another spirit on him and it showed when he would not go along with ten men, and it cost him: the congregation reached for stones. Speaking up is part of staying in the spirit, not the opposite of it.

The adversary works the weak moments, the way he waited out forty days in the wilderness before he opened his mouth. He wants the thing kept quiet, because confession is what the Most High wants. He plays long, joining a grievance from last year to one from this week. And he uses men who went out from us and were never of us, who come back teaching, because that is how the seduction is actually delivered.

So walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.
