## Introduction

The class covers the rise of Haiti after ruin, exploring the history and biblical connections of the island. The teacher discusses the tribes of Israel, the practice of witchcraft and voodoo, and the importance of unity among the 12 tribes. The class also touches on the Haitian Revolution, the exploitation of Haiti by other nations, and the need for self-sufficiency. The teacher emphasizes the importance of understanding the dual meaning of scriptures and following God's instructions. The class moves through various biblical passages, including Psalms, Deuteronomy, Job, and Genesis, to support the teacher's interpretations. The teacher's goal is to help the congregation understand the truth and prepare for the feast of tabernacles. The class is part of a larger study course, and the teacher encourages the congregation to read four chapters a day and participate in upcoming events. The teacher also announces the release of new music albums and videos, and introduces a meal plan program to help members achieve their fitness and health goals. The class ends with a call to action, encouraging the congregation to prepare for the Day of Atonement and the feast of tabernacles, and to support the establishment of a school in Haiti. The teacher also reminds the congregation of the importance of marriage and the proper way to get married according to the Bible. The class concludes with a sense of community and a call to unity among the 12 tribes of Israel.
