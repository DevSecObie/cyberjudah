## Introduction

The class opens with Psalms 46 read before the congregation and prayer for the leadership, for the sick, for the weak in faith, and for the downfall of this kingdom.

The subject is the character of Esau, taken in four parts: thief, hater, liar and killer. The moving thumbnail sets it out. Before nations divided, hatred entered the heart of one brother against another. Cain hated the righteous and murdered his own brother, and that spirit did not disappear. Esau pursued his brother with the sword and cast off all pity, and his hatred became perpetual. The centuries changed, the kingdoms changed and the weapons changed, but the hatred remained, and behind the kingdom stood the dragon giving them power and a throne and authority. The hatred became theft, the theft was rewritten as history, and the thief called himself righteous. What the thief overlooked is that the prophecy was already written.

## In Closing

Thief, hater, liar and killer is not four accusations, it is one character described from four sides. He hated his brother over a blessing before there was a nation to hate. He killed the righteous brother rather than take the correction. He stole a people and a land and built his house by unrighteousness. He swore falsely by the name and called himself the Jew and the Christian while running the inquisition, the auction block and the hood.

The flying roll in Zechariah names both of them, the one that stealeth and the one that sweareth, and it does not stop at the door. It enters into the house and remains in the midst of it. The law of Exodus already fixed the sentence for a man-stealer with the stolen man still found in his hand, and that is precisely where the chariots will find us.

So the work now is not to reform him, because his malice was bred in him and his cogitation will never be changed, and there is no pardon written for that seed. The work is to build faith before the pressure comes, to come out of the churches that were built to hold us, and to reveal the man of sin so that the people stop mistaking him for God.
