## Class Questions

- **Why does a New Testament verse tell us to flee idolatry if the law is done away?** Because it is not done away. 1 Corinthians 10:14 comes straight from Exodus 20:3-5, and Galatians 5 lists idolatry among the works that keep you out of the kingdom. Valentine's Day descends from Lupercalia and the god Februus, so celebrating it puts another god before the Lord.
- **How do we know Babylon the Great is America and not some other nation?** Revelation 17 describes a power sitting on many waters, over peoples, nations and tongues, and Habakkuk 2:5 describes a proud man who never keeps at home. Only one nation has hundreds of bases in every hemisphere and hundreds of foreign interventions since 1798, and its own strategy paper declares the aim of staying the strongest and richest power on earth.
- **Is the captain telling the congregation to go and fight?** No. The camp is nonviolent and only wants to serve the Lord. The charge is to prepare mentally, spiritually and physically, and to be able to protect your family and household. Following groups that talk about taking up arms against the state is a losing fight against cyber and technological warfare.
- **What does preparing spiritually look like?** Watch and pray: that you do not drift or get isolated, for protection and the exposure of the enemy's plots, for prophecy to be fulfilled and to be counted worthy to escape, for your household, and with thanksgiving. Stay sober and vigilant, and hold on to the sound mind God gave you instead of fear.
