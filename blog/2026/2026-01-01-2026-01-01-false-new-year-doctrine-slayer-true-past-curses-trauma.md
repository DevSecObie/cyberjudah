## Introduction

*[[0:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWI2pcKI1hA&t=7s)]* A New Year's Day marathon held against Esau's new year, tracing the true past of Israel's curses and the trauma they left. The teachers show from paintings, histories and old maps that Black Israelites were given as gifts and servants across Europe and Asia. Portugal's Jews had their children taken and shipped to São Tomé, and the first Africans in America arrived with Spanish names from Iberia. Joseph, Moses and Paul all passed for Egyptians, and Lamentations says Zion's visage became blacker than a coal. Old records call Whydah 'Judah' and the Loango coast 'the Jews' Bay,' and Arab, Hamite and European traders sold the tribes through the Middle Passage. The day moves through lynching, the brick quotas of Egypt and the madness of Deuteronomy 28, then closes on 'seasoning,' the mind broken in slavery, the healing Christ proclaims in Isaiah 61, and the Maccabees, who slew the traitors who sold their people.

## Class Questions

- **How do Joseph, Moses and Paul show the colour of the Hebrews?** All three were taken for Egyptians (Genesis 42; Exodus 2:19; Acts 21:38). Moses' Ethiopian wife and Pharaoh's daughter needed circumcision, not colour, to tell a Hebrew.
- **What does 'blacker than a coal' in Lamentations 4:8 mean?** Famine made Zion's dark skin darker. Lamentations 5:10 says 'our skin was black like an oven because of the terrible famine.'
- **Who sold Israel into the Atlantic trade?** Joel 3 names Tyre, Sidon and Palestine, and history records Arab and Hamite raids on Negro communities, with Portuguese, Spanish and other Europeans buying and shipping them.
- **What does Deuteronomy 28:66 describe?** A life hanging in doubt, fearing day and night. The class shows it fulfilled in lynching.
- **Is anger at oppression righteous?** Ecclesiastes 7:7 says oppression maketh a wise man mad, and Deuteronomy 28:34 foretold that Israel would be mad at the sight of its eyes.

## In Closing

*[[6:22:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UWI2pcKI1hA&t=22956s)]* Oppression maketh a wise man mad. Know the history the enemy wants erased, repent, keep the law, and come together, for Christ was sent to bind up the brokenhearted and proclaim liberty to the captives.
