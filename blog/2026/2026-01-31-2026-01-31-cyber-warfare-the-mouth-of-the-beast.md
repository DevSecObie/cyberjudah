## Class Questions

- **What is the mouth of the beast?** The little horn of Daniel 7:8, America, has eyes like a man and a mouth speaking great things. The mouth is its power to speak to the whole world, grown from telegraph and radio into television, the internet and social media, and Revelation 13:15 shows it giving the image of the beast life and speech on screen.
- **Why does the deacon say social media was set up to monitor us?** Ezekiel 28:3 says no secret can be hidden from the prince of Tyrus, and Psalm 10:8 says his eyes are privily set against the poor. The clips show real-time phone interception, Palantir and Flock cameras, spyware like Pegasus and Graphite, the ADL watching around the clock, and Netanyahu calling TikTok and X weapons. Post only what serves the truth.
- **How should believers carry themselves under this surveillance?** As men and women being watched, because Psalm 37:32 says the wicked watcheth the righteous and Luke 20:20 says spies feign themselves just men. Stop posting personal business, keep your speech scriptural, stop watching what you should not, and expect the tribulation of Acts 14:22 to intensify.
- **What does God say he will do about their devices?** Job 5:12 says he disappoints the devices of the crafty so their hands cannot perform their enterprise, and saves the poor from their mouth. Psalm 64:8 says their own tongue shall fall upon themselves, and Job 20 ends with the heaven revealing his iniquity and the earth rising up against him.
