## Introduction

*[[6:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GEZnJtTw5Bs&t=370s)]* Why do fools fall in love: a class on proving a spouse, taught because of what is happening in the body. A fool is one who despises wisdom and instruction, and the class walks the eight stages people mistake for love, from infatuation and lust to trauma bonding, then the scriptures on lust, fornication and haste, the red flags in a man and in a woman, the counsel God has put in place, and the house built on sand. Prove all things, take counsel before every action, and do not let love blind wisdom.

## Class Questions

- **What is your purpose?** To fear God and keep his commandments, for this is the whole duty of man.
- **How does James 1:14 play into proving?** You give in to the red flags and false perceptions you created because you want to fulfil a lust, even the lust to have a wife and be on the cabin trip.
- **How would you know you have a contentious woman?** She gainsays you, adds her two cents to instructions that did not need it, and every conversation turns into a fight.
- **What does the husband being the head as Christ is the head of the church show?** That the husband has the same authority over the wife that Christ has over the church, and a woman who understands structure respects that order.
