## Introduction

The class develops a practical question: how does a student move from knowing basic commandments to exercising mature judgment? Its answer connects regular study, obedience, prayer, experience, and counsel. Knowing a reference is only the beginning; applying it faithfully in relationships and difficult decisions develops discernment.

These are paraphrased study notes in teaching order, with scripture quotations checked against the site's KJV and Apocrypha. The interpretations and examples are those presented in the recording. Timestamp links open near the beginning of each discussion; repeated readings are consolidated.

**Recording coverage:** The available captions cover the main teaching through its closing at approximately 2:45, followed by announcements and music. The export ends at 3:18:20 of the 3:29:34 recording. The final eleven minutes are not represented here.

The opening prayer asks for protection, support for the sick and afflicted, and strength for those teaching and traveling. Before the main lesson, the speakers return to the earlier class's discussion of support after childbirth.
