## Introduction

*[[7:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=446s)]* Alcohol, and the comfort we go to it for. He had been watching the camp's classes on alcoholism and saw that they get the fewest views of anything in the library, because our people do not watch what holds them accountable -- so he taught it again as a self-evaluation: where the comfort is supposed to come from, what the scriptures call drunkenness, the four stages of alcoholism and what moderation actually means. The cup becomes a chain when it is the thing you reach for to keep from feeling what you have been through.

## In The News

- *[[29:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=1788s)]* **The class thumbnail: "I can't keep doing this. I got to stop. But I just can't."** — He played it after asking why we use alcohol for comfort. He wanted the why, not the habit.
- *[[32:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=1929s)]* **Stage one of an alcoholism stages chart, the pre-alcoholic stage: drinking socially but beginning to use alcohol for relief of stress, sleep and anxiety; tolerance gradually increasing; reaching for alcohol after a stressful day; drinking more than peers but still appearing normal; thinking about alcohol often and using it as a coping mechanism** — Many of us fall into one of these stages. You may not be stage four, but you may be at the beginning of stage one. I had a stressful day, I deserve this bottle, I deserve getting a buzz -- that is the behaviour.
- *[[34:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=2047s)]* **Stage two, the early prodromal stage: blackouts begin; loss of control over how much once drinking starts; drinking alone or secretly; hiding bottles or lying about intake; drinking before events to pre-game; guilt or shame after drinking** — Now you go through instances where you do not remember what you did. And in order to be yourself you have to be intoxicated -- it is not even yourself, it is what you want to be. That liquid courage has you talking to everybody in the club: you know you are a two, but the alcohol has you feeling like a ten.
- *[[36:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=2199s)]* **Stage three, the middle crucial stage: dependence becomes physical and psychological; withdrawal without alcohol; drinking early in the day; missing work; making excuses to drink; failed attempts to quit; drinking becomes a priority over responsibilities** — Your responsibilities are not just the house, they are spiritual. Reading your Bible is a responsibility, fasting and praying is a responsibility, and now everything runs through whether you can drink: if I fast I cannot drink, so there is no point sitting down with the Bible -- the Bible is a buzz kill. That is the mind of a drunk.
- *[[41:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=2480s)]* **Stage four, the late chronic stage: serious physical and mental damage; drinking to function, not to get drunk; drinking in the morning to stop the shaking; neglecting hygiene, nutrition and responsibilities; loss of interest in everything except alcohol** — Now you are a functioning alcoholic, up at six and in bed at eleven and drinking the whole time, a pint or a fifth a day. You are fasting while you sleep, so your body goes through withdrawal and you wake up with the shakes. And nothing interests you: not the Bible, not the school, not the camp, not the blitzes, not your wife, not your husband. People who stop coming to the school -- do not put it past them.
- *[[49:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=2990s)]* **The dictionary entry for alcoholism: a chronic, progressive, potentially fatal disorder marked by excessive and usually compulsive drinking, leading to psychological and physical dependence or addiction, typically characterised by the inability to control drinking** — Potentially fatal: cirrhosis of the liver, DUIs. You think you are a functioning alcoholic who can drive. It does not matter how well you walk and how well you talk -- you blow over the limit and you are going to jail.
- *[[53:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=3239s)]* **The dictionary entry for a chain: a series of links used for support, restraint or the transmission of power; something that confines, restrains or secures** — That is the class. When the cup becomes a chain you cannot break from it. That is what a chain does: it confines and it restrains.
- *[[1:04:44](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=3884s)]* **An article on the ten most common causes of alcoholism: family history, drinking from an early age, mental health disorders, a stressful environment, taking alcohol with medication, peer pressure, frequent consumption over time, trauma including childhood, domestic and sexual abuse, and a lack of family supervision** — These are the issues our people are dealing with, and they come into the truth carrying them. A lot of us did not drink out in the world, we smoked -- so we came in, had to stop smoking, saw everybody drinking, and replaced the weed with liquor. We replaced the pills, the crack, the fentanyl and even the medication with liquor.
- *[[1:11:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=4275s)]* **The same article on preventing alcoholism: observe how people feel before they drink, because people try to drink away their problems and negative feelings, and it will only temporarily ease their pain** — What is causing you to drink? How do you feel before you drink? That is what has to be cured, and only temporarily easing the pain is not curing it.
- *[[1:12:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=4325s)]* **A news segment on White Clay, Nebraska -- a town of twenty-two people with four liquor stores, a few yards from the dry Pine Ridge Indian Reservation, where alcoholism is put as high as 80 per cent and almost 99 per cent of some thirteen thousand crimes were connected to alcohol** — The nations are making billions off us not being able to find solutions to our problems. We have been to that reservation and the people say there is nothing to do but drink. Our land was stolen, drink. Our chiefs were murdered, drink. Our women were stolen and raped, drink. And when I drink I become the white man -- the robber, the murderer -- and that brings me comfort.
- *[[1:22:56](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=4976s)]* **A clip of a man who had one drink too many stumbling down the street, swaying like a leaf in a hurricane** — That is the man lying on the top of a mast, like you are on a ship in the ocean. And some of you are sitting there right now saying you are good, you do not get drunk.
- *[[1:27:54](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5274s)]* **The dictionary entry for moderate: avoiding extremes of behaviour or expression, observing reasonable limits, calm and temperate** — That first one is the one. We put a feeling on moderation instead of a pattern: I am drinking in moderation because I do not get drunk. Moderation involves a pattern of behaviour.
- *[[1:29:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5398s)]* **An article on alcohol dependence and mental health: anxiety, depression and suicidal feelings develop because heavy drinking interferes with the chemicals the brain needs; it affects your partner, your family and friends, your work and your finances, makes you more aggressive, and using alcohol to improve your mood starts a vicious cycle** — You always see this in the people who are really drunk: anxiety, depression, suicidal thoughts. Now you cannot go to work, or you are at work getting drunk -- you need a shot to get up and go.
- *[[1:47:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6441s)]* **The entry for an alcogenic environment, or drinking culture: a social setting where drinking alcohol is normalized, encouraged and heavily promoted, with accessibility, affordability, marketing and social pressure** — This is America. You do not even have to go to a bar any more: you can buy a machine, put your tequila, your vodka and your scotch in it, tell it what you want, and it makes your drink.

**[Sirach 31:27](/bible/sirach/31#v27)**  *[[1:26:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5218s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/31#v27)</sup> Wine is as good as life to a man, if it be drunk moderately: what life is then to a man that is without wine? for it was made to make men glad.

- Wine is as good as life to a man, if it be drunk moderately, for it was made to make men glad. Drinking is not a sin, and we should not need a disclaimer to say it.

- But watch that word moderately. We put a feeling on moderation instead of a pattern: I am moderate because I do not get drunk. A pattern is part of being moderate.

- If there is a pattern behind it, evaluate yourself -- it is drunkenness. You can become an alcohol dependent without ever blacking out. I had to evaluate my own self watching those classes.


**[Sirach 30:23](/bible/sirach/30#v23)**  *[[1:31:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5484s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/30#v23)</sup> Love thine own soul, and comfort thy heart, remove sorrow far from thee: for sorrow hath killed many, and there is no profit therein.

- Love thine own soul and comfort thy heart, remove sorrow far from thee. Love your own soul -- do not use alcohol to love yourself.

- For sorrow hath killed many, and there is no profit therein. The depression and the anxiety we just read about are the sorrow, and we turn to the bottle over it and become a slave to liquor, a captive to liquor.

- To get the sorrow off you, you turn to alcoholism, to adultery, to fornication, to drugs, even to doubting God and moving away from the truth.

- One sister said she did not want to bring her issues to the body. That is like saying I know I have cancer but I do not want to plague the doctor with my problems. That is what leadership is there for: to help you, to counsel you, to take you through the scriptures.


**[Sirach 38:18](/bible/sirach/38#v18)**  *[[1:32:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5532s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/38#v18)</sup> For of heaviness cometh death, and the heaviness of the heart breaketh strength.

- For of heaviness cometh death, and the heaviness of the heart breaketh strength. Heaviness can kill you, and carrying it takes your strength away.


**[Sirach 31:28-30](/bible/sirach/31#v28)**  *[[1:33:55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5635s)]*

> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/31#v28)</sup> Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/31#v29)</sup> But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind, with brawling and quarrelling.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/31#v30)</sup> Drunkenness increaseth the rage of a fool till he offend: it diminisheth strength, and maketh wounds.

- Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart and cheerfulness of the mind. In season -- drinking every day is not in season.

- But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind. They call it spirits for a reason: one drink brings back the old fling, another brings back the military days, another brings out the trauma. It plagues the mind.

- With brawling and quarrelling, and drunkenness increaseth the rage of a fool till he offend. You say things drunk you would never say sober, and you offend somebody or you get disrespectful. It brings out the truth in you too: I always hated your ass, nigga -- and that was the truth.

- It diminisheth strength and maketh wounds. You wake up with a tooth knocked out and no idea how it happened.

- And the first person who sees the pattern is your wife. You cannot hide the true you from your wife; she finds the bottles in the car, and deep down you know she knows.


**[1 Peter 5:8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)**  *[[1:38:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=5921s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)</sup> Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:

- Be sober, be vigilant, because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about seeking whom he may devour. We understand it is talking about Satan -- and the white man is waiting on you to get drunk.

- People break into houses all the time. If they run up in your spot in these last days, are you going to be able to defend your house, or are you going to be caught slipping -- laid out, blacked out, tipsy, not in your top shape?

- Vigilance comes with being sober. You cannot be drunk and vigilant at the same time. It is like the man who gets drunk and drives: you cross that line once and you stick out like a sore thumb.


**[Titus 2:1-2](/bible/titus/2#v1)**  *[[1:41:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6091s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/titus/2#v1)</sup> But speak thou the things which become sound doctrine:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/2#v2)</sup> That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience.

- Speak thou the things which become sound doctrine, that the aged men be sober. You have to be sober, brothers.

- Stop turning to liquor to dissolve your issues. Face your problems like a man; sisters, face them like a woman. Until you face them they only get worse, and they will cost you your spiritual growth.

- Grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience -- you cannot be any of that if you are not sober. Your reason why is hindering you from reaching your true potential.

- I am not saying drinking is a sin. Over-consumption and getting drunk is, and so is using alcohol as a crutch to blanket the trauma and the issues you do not want to deal with. Get below the surface, heal that, and it will take the alcoholism with it.


**[Psalms 34:4](/bible/psalms/34#v4)**  *[[1:43:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6208s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/34#v4)</sup> I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and delivered me from all my fears.

- I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and he delivered me from all my fears. He delivers you from the very fear that is the reason why you drink excessively, why you get drunk, why you cannot stop.

- And if you cannot understand that alcoholism is an issue in our community, you are contributing to the problem. You are in denial -- you are at stage three.


**[Psalms 42:11](/bible/psalms/42#v11)**  *[[1:45:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6334s)]*

- Why art thou cast down, O my soul, and why art thou disquieted within me? That cast down and disquieted is where the drinking starts.

- Hope thou in God, for I shall yet praise him. That is the answer the psalm gives, and we shall yet praise the Lord -- he is the health of my countenance and my God.


**[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**  *[[1:46:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6396s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

- Be not conformed to this world. What world is this? The white man's world -- and the drinking culture is his: normalized, encouraged, heavily promoted, accessible, affordable, marketed, with the social pressure on top of it.

- But be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind. We have to be transformed, become a new man and a new woman, and that happens in the mind.


**[Luke 21:34](/bible/luke/21#v34)**  *[[1:49:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6544s)]*

- Take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting and drunkenness and cares of this life. Take heed to yourselves -- this is a self-evaluation.

- And so that day come upon you unawares. That day is destruction, and it comes on you unawares because drunkenness takes away your perception of time and your judgment.


**[Psalms 34:17](/bible/psalms/34#v17)**  *[[1:49:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6593s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/34#v17)</sup> The righteous cry, and the Lord heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles.

- The righteous cry, and the Lord heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles. You have woes, you have pain, you have trauma -- cry out to the Lord about it.

- Repent, turn away from your sins, cast away the affairs of this world, remove the sorrow from you. Do not call out for liquor; do not go to the liquor store, go to the Lord.

- Fast, pray, study, apply, accountability. Take a self-evaluation: am I being overly consumed with this thing? Am I hiding it? If the answer is yes, say I have a problem and deal with it instead of arranging things so nobody sees it. That is what men and women of the Lord do.

## Class Questions

- **We use alcohol for comfort. Why?** The why is your fear -- whatever you cannot face sober -- and you are stronger than you think.
- **Go through the stages. What stage are you?** Be honest with yourself. You may not be stage four, but you may be at the beginning of stage one.
- **Are we trying to drink away our problems and our negative feelings?** That only temporarily eases the pain. What has to be cured is what you feel before you drink.
- **Can you take a month, two months, three months off, or keep it to a feast day?** If you can do that, do it. If you cannot do it, that is the problem we have to fix.

## In Closing

*[[1:51:38](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gF8ALt0DzR4&t=6698s)]* We have to overcome these things to be ready for what is coming down the pipeline. Esau is gearing up, the nations are gearing up, and they know their time is short, so we have to prepare our minds and let go of the sins that so easily beset us. If you know you are dealing with alcoholism, or you do not get drunk but you drink every day, take some time off -- a month, two months, three months. Keep it to a feast day. Say, I am only drinking on new moons and Sabbaths, or only with my wife. Put something in season to it, so it is not a pattern and not a dependency. If you can do that, do it; if you cannot, that is a problem we have to fix, and we can fix it together. See the problem, address the problem, be held accountable -- and for those who want to join us, feel free. Keep fighting, and get those chains off your mind. All praise to the Most High. Shalom.
