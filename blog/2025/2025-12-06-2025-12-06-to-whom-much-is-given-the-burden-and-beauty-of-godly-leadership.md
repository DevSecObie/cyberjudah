## Introduction

*[[13:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=789s)]* What the crown of leadership actually weighs — a condensed follow-on to the "heavy is the head" class he gave on the Phoenix page a couple of new moons back. He chose it for the feast of dedication because the history of our forefathers under the Maccabees shows every layer of leadership at once: bold and courageous men, the burdens they carried, and the traitors and hypocrites among our own. The first half lays out five burdens every leader bears; the second turns to the home, because the house a man rules is the first congregation he will ever lead.

## Scriptures Opened

**[James 3:1](/bible/james/3#v1)**  *[[18:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=1099s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/james/3#v1)</sup> My brethren, be not many masters, knowing that we shall receive the greater condemnation.

- Masters are the teachers, the ones they called rabbi. We hear the word and think owners and authority; it is aimed at the men who stand up and expound.

- Leadership is a select few. The more a man professes to know, or to have taken responsibility for, the more he is held to.

- The greater condemnation falls on the men out front, not on the congregation.


**[Luke 12:48](/bible/luke/12#v48)**  *[[20:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=1204s)]*

> <sup>[48](/bible/luke/12#v48)</sup> But he that knew not, and did commit things worthy of stripes, shall be beaten with few stripes. For unto whomsoever much is given, of him shall be much required: and to whom men have committed much, of him they will ask the more.

- Every one of us carries a weight to bear for our own sins and for the sins of our forefathers.

- More is shown to us than men understand, and that is the much given. What is required back is how we move as a body and a nation in this wilderness.

- Authority demands righteous discernment and it is not optional. Not only knowing which precept says what — the sign left unchanged out front for two months while people think it is a different church, and knowing the SOPs for every office you hold.


**[James 3:3](/bible/james/3#v3)**  *[[21:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=1289s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/3#v3)</sup> Behold, we put bits in the horses’ mouths, that they may obey us; and we turn about their whole body.

- The bits are the scriptures, because that is how you lead a horse. Leaders put them in the people's mouths so the whole body turns from the old man.

- That power can be wielded responsibly or irresponsibly. The Pharisees wielded it, and so did the men in the Maccabees' day who would have loved to make amity with the Greeks.

- The wide gate is the status quo and it is easy. Standing stiffly for Christ against whatever the world is saying is the strait one.


**[Galatians 6:1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)**  *[[28:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=1707s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)</sup> Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

- The burden of empathy, not sympathy. Empaths feel what others feel, and it is the spiritual who are charged to restore a man overtaken in a fault, in meekness.

- "Considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted" is why part of the questioning in an inquisition is what drove him to it — is he just an evil-doer, or is it something we all deal with?

- Nothing comes on us but such as is common to man, so the man judging has to see himself in the man judged.


**[Revelation 2:9](/bible/revelation/2#v9)**  *[[31:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=1910s)]*

> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/2#v9)</sup> I know thy works, and tribulation, and poverty, (but thou art rich) and I know the blasphemy of them which say they are Jews, and are not, but are the synagogue of Satan.

- We read "I know thy works" as the all-powerful God speaking down at us, and forget that it behooved him to be made like us in all things.

- He knows the tribulation and the poverty because he lived it the thirty-odd years he was here. He walked many miles in our shoes.

- Every forefather was refined and you can watch the maturation — Moses, David, Paul, Samuel from a child, Joshua. Saul had the same refinement in front of him and it went the other way.


**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:1-4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v1)**  *[[33:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=2037s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v1)</sup> Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v2)</sup> Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v3)</sup> For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v4)</sup> Because, being ministers of his kingdom, ye have not judged aright, nor kept the law, nor walked after the counsel of God;

- The kings and judges of the ends of the earth are Israelites put in authority, and the power they hold is given them of the Lord.

- Your works will be tried and your counsels searched out. The moment you step into leadership, God is measuring.

- Measure your words. Wrong counsel can condemn the man who took it; he answers, but you answer more. Say "let me get back to you" and go up your chain — knowledge puffs up, and a title does not entitle you to answer everything.

- To be a soldier, officer, captain or deacon is to be a minister of the kingdom of God. The charge against those ministers is verse 4: they judged not aright, kept not the law themselves, walked not after the counsel of God.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 6:2-3](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v2)</sup> Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world? and if the world shall be judged by you, are ye unworthy to judge the smallest matters?
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v3)</sup> Know ye not that we shall judge angels? how much more things that pertain to this life?

    The ends of the earth is the whole wide world and the angels that rule it, not the world of Israel only. If that judgment is coming, we have to measure judgment now among our own people.
  - **[Hebrews 5:12-14](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)</sup> For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/5#v13)</sup> For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)</sup> But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.

    Go back to the first principles of the oracles of God before you counsel anybody. The ones who qualify have their senses exercised by use — study, application, experience.
  - **[Job 32:7-8](/bible/job/32#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/job/32#v7)</sup> I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/job/32#v8)</sup> But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.

    Let days speak: let God speak. There is a spirit in man, which is why God tries it, and why a leader stops to ask whether this is godly counsel or just him talking.


**[Ecclesiastes 1:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/1#v18)**  *[[43:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=2609s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/1#v18)</sup> For in much wisdom is much grief: and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth sorrow.

- The burden of awareness. The more you perceive the more you carry, and what the Most High opens up on the other side of leadership you never unsee. Ignorance is comforting; insight is how discernment grows.

- Falling inside the structure of leadership does not mean you matured through any of it. Take moral inventory instead of nodding along with your chin in the air — you are accountable to us too, and we review you.

- Some of you hold a willful ignorance so you never face what is in front of you. That is Cipher asking to be plugged back in, and leaders do not have that luxury.


**[Sirach 10:1-3](/bible/sirach/10#v1)**  *[[48:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=2900s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/10#v1)</sup> A wise judge will instruct his people; and the government of a prudent man is well ordered.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/10#v2)</sup> As the judge of the people is himself, so are his officers; and what manner of man the ruler of the city is, such are all they that dwell therein.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/10#v3)</sup> An unwise king destroyeth his people; but through the prudence of them which are in authority the city shall be inhabited.

- It is government being set up, and we are called to put government together. The smaller cumulative things that make up the whole are critical.

- Many of you are one-sided leaders: a mighty teacher in the streets while the roof of the congregation is falling, literally and spiritually.

- Be careful blaming others. "It is the men that I got" is the same spirit as "it is the woman you gave me, Lord." What leader cannot find diamonds in the rough?

- A congregation that is not growing is not a camp-spot problem — ask whether that school is run prudently. When leadership is prudent, cities grow: in understanding, in operations, in the kind of problems that get put behind you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Titus 1:5](/bible/titus/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/titus/1#v5)</sup> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

    Elders were ordained in every city to set in order the things that are wanting. The structure, the rank and the processes behind them are what make a government well ordered.


**[Numbers 11:13-17](/bible/numbers/11#v13)**  *[[57:50](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=3470s)]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/11#v13)</sup> Whence should I have flesh to give unto all this people? for they weep unto me, saying, Give us flesh, that we may eat.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/11#v14)</sup> I am not able to bear all this people alone, because it is too heavy for me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/11#v15)</sup> And if thou deal thus with me, kill me, I pray thee, out of hand, if I have found favour in thy sight; and let me not see my wretchedness.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/11#v16)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Gather unto me seventy men of the elders of Israel, whom thou knowest to be the elders of the people, and officers over them; and bring them unto the tabernacle of the congregation, that they may stand there with thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/11#v17)</sup> And I will come down and talk with thee there: and I will take of the spirit which is upon thee, and will put it upon them; and they shall bear the burden of the people with thee, that thou bear it not thyself alone.

- They petitioned him for flesh, and that is leadership: people look to you. He told the Lord plainly he was not able to bear them alone, and asked to be killed out of hand rather than be left alone with them.

- He did not go off to his tent over how nobody likes him. He prayed, and the Lord had mercy — but not the way he expected.

- Seventy elders and officers were brought to the tabernacle, and the Lord took of the spirit upon Moses and put it on them. One spirit on seventy men is one accord: we teach differently, but one body, one Lord, one doctrine.

- Even Moses reached his limit, and God's answer was delegation. This great work cannot be done by one man.


**[Acts 6:2-4,7](/bible/acts/6#v2)**  *[[1:04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=3867s)]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/acts/6#v2)</sup> Then the twelve called the multitude of the disciples unto them, and said, It is not reason that we should leave the word of God, and serve tables.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/6#v3)</sup> Wherefore, brethren, look ye out among you seven men of honest report, full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/acts/6#v4)</sup> But we will give ourselves continually to prayer, and to the ministry of the word.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/acts/6#v7)</sup> And the word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly; and a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.

- "Not reason that we should leave the word of God and serve tables" is not a man saying the work is beneath him. It is a man who knows where he fits in the government.

- It is knowing there are able-bodied people who can bear those things, and knowing which to pass off and which not to.

- Seven men of honest report were set over that business so the twelve could give themselves to prayer and the ministry of the word. With Moses it was seventy; here, seven.

- Then the word of God increased and the disciples multiplied greatly. That is the fruit of not bearing it alone.


**[1 Kings 3:7-12](/bible/1-kings/3#v7)**  *[[1:08:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=4088s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/3#v7)</sup> And now, O Lord my God, thou hast made thy servant king instead of David my father: and I am but a little child: I know not how to go out or come in.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-kings/3#v8)</sup> And thy servant is in the midst of thy people which thou hast chosen, a great people, that cannot be numbered nor counted for multitude.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/3#v9)</sup> Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/3#v10)</sup> And the speech pleased the Lord, that Solomon had asked this thing.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/3#v11)</sup> And God said unto him, Because thou hast asked this thing, and hast not asked for thyself long life; neither hast asked riches for thyself, nor hast asked the life of thine enemies; but hast asked for thyself understanding to discern judgment;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-kings/3#v12)</sup> Behold, I have done according to thy words: lo, I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart; so that there was none like thee before thee, neither after thee shall any arise like unto thee.

- A king in title and lands who says he is but a little child. Some of you have not learned the basics of your own house and want to lead the church.

- The awareness of a people that cannot be numbered is compassion, not fear of the weight — the same awareness Christ had when he saw the multitudes lacked a shepherd.

- He asked for an understanding heart to discern between good and bad, not long life, not riches, not the life of his enemies, and the speech pleased the Lord.

- Wisdom came as a gift and became a trial: the same man wrote that increasing knowledge increases sorrow. It can crush you or make you rise.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 38:4](/bible/psalms/38#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/38#v4)</sup> For mine iniquities are gone over mine head: as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

    David felt his own iniquities as a burden too heavy and leaned on the Lord — show me where I am wrong. A lot of you take correction and then pray the Lord judge the man who corrected you.


**[Joshua 1:5-9](/bible/joshua/1#v5)**  *[[1:13:16](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=4396s)]*

> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/1#v5)</sup> There shall not any man be able to stand before thee all the days of thy life: as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee: I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/1#v6)</sup> Be strong and of a good courage: for unto this people shalt thou divide for an inheritance the land, which I sware unto their fathers to give them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/1#v7)</sup> Only be thou strong and very courageous, that thou mayest observe to do according to all the law, which Moses my servant commanded thee: turn not from it to the right hand or to the left, that thou mayest prosper whithersoever thou goest.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/1#v9)</sup> Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the Lord thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

- He was about to take Moses' place and divide the land God swore to their fathers.

- Nowhere does Joshua say he was afraid, but the Lord tries the reins and knows the heart. He told him be not dismayed because he was — not a cowering fear, but an honest internal dialogue God interceded in.

- The book of the law was not to depart out of his mouth, day and night. Don't bear it yourself; seek the counsel in the book of the law.

- "The Lord thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest" was enough for him. Read the history after that and he was rocking and rolling.


**[Ezekiel 9:4-5](/bible/ezekiel/9#v4)**  *[[1:16:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=4580s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/9#v4)</sup> And the Lord said unto him, Go through the midst of the city, through the midst of Jerusalem, and set a mark upon the foreheads of the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations that be done in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/9#v5)</sup> And to the others he said in mine hearing, Go ye after him through the city, and smite: let not your eye spare, neither have ye pity:

- The mark goes on the men that sigh and that cry for all the abominations done in the midst of the city.

- Leadership is marked by the men who sighed and cried for the people. That is empathy.

- We read it for the anger in it, and the anger is there. But it is a man looking at the state of his people and hurting over them.


**[Matthew 9:36-38](/bible/matthew/9#v36)**  *[[1:18:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=4684s)]*

> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/9#v36)</sup> But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/9#v37)</sup> Then saith he unto his disciples, The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/9#v38)</sup> Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.

- He was moved with compassion because they fainted and were scattered abroad as sheep having no shepherd — no guidance, no leadership.

- Moses was given help, Joshua was given help, Solomon was given help, in Acts they were given help, and Christ prayed for the same. More labourers is God's wisdom — but leaders according to his heart.

- In every one of those examples the motive is not image and not status but a brokenhearted, contrite compassion for the people. Want leadership for anything else and you will fail.


**[2 Corinthians 11:27-28](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v27)**  *[[1:21:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=4881s)]*

> <sup>[27](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v27)</sup> In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v28)</sup> Beside those things that are without, that which cometh upon me daily, the care of all the churches.

- Weariness, painfulness, watchings, hunger, cold and nakedness — and that is only the summation of what Paul bore with his face out front.

- Besides all that comes on him from without, the care of all the churches is on him daily. That is the invisible pressure the rest of the body never sees.

- Bishop Nathanyel will send a message out of nowhere asking how this person is, what is going on with that, always about how the church is moving. Look at the hour he sends it.

- To lead is to carry other people's struggles in silence and anticipate a need before it is spoken: food pantries because famine is on the brink, a prison ministry, real support for the sisters on what never gets addressed.


**[John 19:1-3](/bible/john/19#v1)**  *[[1:25:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=5113s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/john/19#v1)</sup> Then Pilate therefore took Jesus, and scourged him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/john/19#v2)</sup> And the soldiers platted a crown of thorns, and put it on his head, and they put on him a purple robe,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/john/19#v3)</sup> And said, Hail, King of the Jews! and they smote him with their hands.

- They platted a crown of thorns, put a purple robe on him and hailed him King of the Jews to mock him.

- Take it as the visual for what leadership is. Real crowns bleed. You bleed with the people and move with the people, and that is the lifeblood of what we do.

- His crown was not gold with rubies and studs. It was thorns. Leadership is sacrifice, not status.

- Leadership is not a ladder, not applause, not visibility. The hierarchy is reversed: he who will be great will be a servant.


**[Psalms 75:6-7](/bible/psalms/75#v6)**  *[[1:29:00](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=5340s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/75#v6)</sup> For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/75#v7)</sup> But God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up another.

- Promotion comes neither from the east nor the west nor the south. God putteth down one and setteth up another.

- If God has not promoted you, your works are not complete, your spirit is not ready, or it is not your time.

- "I have been here longer, look what I have done, why were men raised up before me" is corporate ambition, and it reinforces why you were not called.

- Do the work expecting nothing in return. Let your works and the men around you speak for you. If you were where you needed to be, don't you believe God would have put you there?


**[Luke 7:31-32](/bible/luke/7#v31)**  *[[1:31:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=5494s)]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/luke/7#v31)</sup> And the Lord said, Whereunto then shall I liken the men of this generation? and to what are they like?
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/luke/7#v32)</sup> They are like unto children sitting in the marketplace, and calling one to another, and saying, We have piped unto you, and ye have not danced; we have mourned to you, and ye have not wept.

- The men of this generation are children sitting in the marketplace piping to one another. That is what Israel is like.

- We are grown men and we do not know what to do with the authority God gave us.

- Broken home or complete home, it was not a biblically led home, so we were taught that authority equals dominance. Authority is stewardship and sacrifice.

- That is why brothers yell and curse their wives and children into subjection instead of being refined into leadership.


**[1 Timothy 3:4-5](/bible/1-timothy/3#v4)**  *[[1:32:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=5543s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/3#v4)</sup> One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/3#v5)</sup> (For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?)

- You are harping on why you were not promoted while your house is not run the way it should be. Awareness, judgment, empathy, accountability and refinement have to be in the house too.

- That is the first test of leadership, and it is a daily one. You need not be married; then the first touch of leadership is ruling your own spirit. It is not teaching and not speaking.

- Paul did not say a man leaves his house with a heavy hand. He said rules well — stability, financial and emotional and doctrinal, structure, discipline, emotional safety, wise decisions, consistency.

- Ruling well is character, not force. Screaming and cursing is the lowest form of what you think authority is, and a tyrant at home is a spirit breaker in the congregation.

  Precepts:
  - **[Titus 1:6-9](/bible/titus/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/titus/1#v6)</sup> If any be blameless, the husband of one wife, having faithful children not accused of riot or unruly.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/titus/1#v7)</sup> For a bishop must be blameless, as the steward of God; not selfwilled, not soon angry, not given to wine, no striker, not given to filthy lucre;
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/titus/1#v8)</sup> But a lover of hospitality, a lover of good men, sober, just, holy, temperate;
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/titus/1#v9)</sup> Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

    Faithful children, not accused of riot or unruly, and a man not self-willed and not soon angry. A child we see running around unruly tells us what kind of leader you are at home, and covering it is not addressing it.
  - **[1 Corinthians 11:3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)</sup> But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.

    The order is God, Christ, man, woman. There is a leadership structure and a rank in the home before there is ever one in the church.


**[Ephesians 5:23-24,27-28,32](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)**  *[[1:43:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=6231s)]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ephesians/5#v24)</sup> Therefore as the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ephesians/5#v27)</sup> That he might present it to himself a glorious church, not having spot, or wrinkle, or any such thing; but that it should be holy and without blemish.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ephesians/5#v28)</sup> So ought men to love their wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his wife loveth himself.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/5#v32)</sup> This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church.

- The husband is head of the wife even as Christ is head of the church, and he is the saviour of the body — your wife's life and your children's lives are on the line too.

- The first real trial of your crown is marriage and children, and it carries over because the wife represents the church, and so do the children.

- Then a man reads verse 24 and decides it means he gets to scream and shout instead of being the wise ruler. Everybody was into the first half of this class; meld it with this half or it will do nothing for you.

- How your home is built is how you will build the church. A man always late, sloppy and messy, children disheveled, would run a school the same way. The wedding starts off all order and structure, and then the household is a mess.

- The man who thinks authority is dominance is in his feelings when we are firm with him, and gives his own house no mercy over trivial things. That is a hypocrite and it is two-faced.


**[Deuteronomy 6:6-9](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v6)**  *[[1:49:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=6549s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v6)</sup> And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v7)</sup> And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v8)</sup> And thou shalt bind them for a sign upon thine hand, and they shall be as frontlets between thine eyes.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v9)</sup> And thou shalt write them upon the posts of thy house, and on thy gates.

- The words are in your heart first, then taught diligently when you sit, walk, lie down and rise up. It is not a sit-down family class; the law is taught in your behaviour.

- The frontlets are the laws kept in the forefront of your mind, with your hand and your actions showing it.

- Your children get Bible study, then they watch how you act at home. They need no grasp of hypocrisy to know something is off, and now you have soured your own children.

- A child counting the days until he can move out is a sentiment none of us should have in this truth, and the man responsible is the head of that house.

  Precepts:
  - **[Joshua 24:15](/bible/joshua/24#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/24#v15)</sup> And if it seem evil unto you to serve the Lord, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.

    Everybody says they will serve the Lord — can you really examine that? You either rule the house the way Ephesians and Timothy lay it out, or the way the world taught you authority was.
  - **[Proverbs 24:3-4](/bible/proverbs/24#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/24#v3)</sup> Through wisdom is an house builded; and by understanding it is established:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/24#v4)</sup> And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches.

    A house is built through wisdom and established by understanding. Wisdom and understanding fill a house; force does not.


**[Psalms 127:1-5](/bible/psalms/127#v1)**  *[[1:53:48](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=6828s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/127#v1)</sup> Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the Lord keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/127#v2)</sup> It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/127#v3)</sup> Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/127#v4)</sup> As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/127#v5)</sup> Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them: they shall not be ashamed, but they shall speak with the enemies in the gate.

- Your home and the church both. Unless it is built on the scriptures and God's counsel, you labour in vain — up early, up late, eating the bread of sorrows over all of it.

- The solution brothers reach for is a date or a cruise after she cursed him out. The majority of your marriage is the everyday, and a date is cost prohibitive and time prohibitive both.

- Children are an heritage of the Lord. Arrows are for war and for attack, and that is the heritage you leave behind.

- They shall speak with the enemies in the gates because you raised them in the way they should go. Or they cannot wait to get out, because they saw how you moved with their mother.


**[2 Esdras 14:10-13](/bible/2-esdras/14#v10)**  *[[1:59:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=7191s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-esdras/14#v10)</sup> For the world hath lost his youth, and the times begin to wax old.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/14#v11)</sup> For the world is divided into twelve parts, and the ten parts of it are gone already, and half of a tenth part:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-esdras/14#v12)</sup> And there remaineth that which is after the half of the tenth part.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-esdras/14#v13)</sup> Now therefore set thine house in order, and reprove thy people, comfort such of them as be in trouble, and now renounce corruption,

- Of the twelve parts, ten and half of a tenth are gone already. In Esdras' time it was half past ten on the clock; we are in the eleventh hour.

- Knowing that, the first charge is set thine house in order — and only then reprove thy people and comfort those in trouble.

- The mission is the principal thing, but the house has to be in order too, or how can you take care of the church of God?

- Your people start in the house. Leadership is stewardship under God, and the home is your first congregation.


**[Proverbs 16:32](/bible/proverbs/16#v32)**  *[[2:05:57](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=7557s)]*

> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/16#v32)</sup> He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.

- Authority exposes your character the way money does. Most men never had authority before marriage — never led a home, a woman, a child or a family budget.

- An insecure man becomes controlling down to the colour of the grout. An angry man becomes harsh, an immature man inconsistent, a lazy man negligent, a prideful man a tyrant. A wise man becomes a foundation; a man who fears God becomes a shepherd to his household.

- Authority exposes the man, not the wife. That is why you cannot say it is the woman you were given.

- In the red rage you are blinded. Everybody knows where your buttons are; a man disciplined in the spirit controls that. Rule your spirit and you will conquer cities.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 25:28](/bible/proverbs/25#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/25#v28)</sup> He that hath no rule over his own spirit is like a city that is broken down, and without walls.

    A man with no rule over his own spirit is a city broken down and without walls — every provocation walks straight in.
  - **[James 1:19-20](/bible/james/1#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/james/1#v19)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/james/1#v20)</sup> For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.

    That one verse explains it all: when you are angry nothing godlike is coming out of you. This is the carnal wrath of a man, not the righteous indignation we rebuke with in the streets.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 7:9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v9)</sup> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.

    Anger resting in the bosom of fools means it is always ready to pop out. You wear it on your chest and that is your default, and it is a childish reaction.


**[Sirach 32:7-9](/bible/sirach/32#v7)**  *[[2:10:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=7852s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/32#v7)</sup> Speak, young man, if there be need of thee: and yet scarcely when thou art twice asked.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/32#v8)</sup> Let thy speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one that knoweth and yet holdeth his tongue.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/32#v9)</sup> If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.

- Speak if there be need of thee, and scarcely then. All of us would do better to listen more.

- "Be as one that knoweth and yet holdeth his tongue" answers the brothers who finish your sentences to make themselves equal to you.

- When you are not understood, the burden is the speaker's — consider your audience and make it plain, the way Paul was weak to the weak.

- Two ears and one mouth: listen twice as much as you speak and you will get a lot more done.


**[Sirach 4:30](/bible/sirach/4#v30)**  *[[2:15:06](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=8106s)]*

> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/4#v30)</sup> Be not as a lion in thy house, nor frantick among thy servants.

- A direct commandment against a tyrant-like spirit in the house.

- A man who roars at home destroys his wife's spirit and provokes his children to wrath, because they watch how he treats their mother.

- It creates fear instead of order and rules from intimidation instead of inspiration. You do have to command your house, and sometimes that means raising your voice — but if your default is to cuss and throw a tantrum, how effective is that?

- She goes quiet because she does not want to deal with it, and you call that effective while you are breaking her spirit. Leaders build; they do not only break.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 1:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/1#v9)</sup> Then the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth. And the Lord said unto me, Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v10)</sup> See, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down, to build, and to plant.

    Set over nations to root out, pull down and throw down — and to build and to plant. Breaking down the spirit a man carries is part of it, but the building has to come behind it, and that authority must be wielded justly.


**[Colossians 3:19-21](/bible/colossians/3#v19)**  *[[2:18:26](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=8306s)]*

> <sup>[19](/bible/colossians/3#v19)</sup> Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/colossians/3#v20)</sup> Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/colossians/3#v21)</sup> Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged.

- Husbands are commanded not to be bitter against their wives. Ask why that had to be said at all.

- Some of you carry "I wish I would have never married you." One brother sent up a prayer in earshot of his wife asking the Lord to kill her and give him another one.

- The Lord knows why it had to be said, because we are the woman to him. We do the same thing to Christ and still want mercy.

- Fathers provoke not your children to anger. They see the instability, then watch you teach boldly in the streets and ask why you are not like that at home.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 6:4](/bible/ephesians/6#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ephesians/6#v4)</sup> And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

    Notice the admonition; nothing in it says soft or not firm. Provoking them to wrath is what sends them into the world, so build the foundation while they are young.


**[1 Peter 3:7](/bible/1-peter/3#v7)**  *[[2:23:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=8617s)]*

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/3#v7)</sup> Likewise, ye husbands, dwell with them according to knowledge, giving honour unto the wife, as unto the weaker vessel, and as being heirs together of the grace of life; that your prayers be not hindered.

- Dwelling with her according to knowledge is knowing her emotional makeup, her fears, her triggers, her strengths, her spiritual needs and what your impact on her is. That is what the proving is for.

- It is not positional power; it is shepherding the home. The home is the first church and the husband the first pastor, and a man who cannot shepherd his own household cannot pastor in the church.

- Husband comes from husbandman — a farmer, a gardener, a cultivator. Husbandry is the planting, the watering, sometimes the pruning, the protecting, overseeing the growth of something fragile.

- Most men understand authority and very few understand cultivation, but God's model is cultivation: planting is instruction, watering is time, pruning is discipline, sheltering is protection.


**[Proverbs 22:6](/bible/proverbs/22#v6)**  *[[2:27:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=8830s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/22#v6)</sup> Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

- Train him in the way he should go and he will not depart from it when he is old. We do the same in the body.

- Daughters marry what their fathers were, and sons become the kind of father and husband you are. If you were a tyrant or a drunkard, that is the quality she will look for and how he will move.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 29:15,17-18](/bible/proverbs/29#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/29#v15)</sup> The rod and reproof give wisdom: but a child left to himself bringeth his mother to shame.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/29#v17)</sup> Correct thy son, and he shall give thee rest; yea, he shall give delight unto thy soul.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/29#v18)</sup> Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.

    The rod and reproof give wisdom, so there is a structure to discipline in the home. And "where there is no vision the people perish" is not only the body — as the man of the house you dictate the vision, and some of you have to ask whether you ever set one.


**[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**  *[[2:29:36](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=8976s)]*

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

- Most of us read provision as income only. Provision is emotional too, and your kids need to see it; a man's love shows differently, but that does not mean you hold it back.

- Thinking the raiment and the money is provision is the same error as a woman who thinks cooking and cleaning makes her a good wife — a maid can do that and you can pay for it.

- Stability and security are provision. Vision, instruction, direction, conflict management and patience come from the man, and above all the godly order in the house.

- The title of husband no more came with the understanding of it than the title of soldier, officer, captain or deacon did. You have to go through the paces.

  Precepts:
  - **[Sirach 25:22](/bible/sirach/25#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/25#v22)</sup> A woman, if she maintain her husband, is full of anger, impudence, and much reproach.

    We used to bring this out only for the dead beat who would not work. But a man who leaves everything else unfilled makes her maintain that role too, and she was never constructed for it.


**[1 Timothy 3:1-6](/bible/1-timothy/3#v1)**  *[[2:36:11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=9371s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/3#v1)</sup> This is a true saying, If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/3#v2)</sup> A bishop then must be blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/3#v3)</sup> Not given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre; but patient, not a brawler, not covetous;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/3#v4)</sup> One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/3#v5)</sup> (For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?)
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-timothy/3#v6)</sup> Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he fall into the condemnation of the devil.

- In the kingdom, work qualifies the man. Ambition does not.

- The desire is not for the title. Paul goes straight into who you have to be, then what you do, and the rest comes after it. The real desire is what the office requires — the five burdens, and being refined by them.

- Filthy lucre is not only money. Take it as any kind of gain, and the gain here is the status and the title. Brothers will say they are not about money while they want the recognition.

- "Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he fall into the condemnation of the devil." If it has not manifested yet, something is off between your house, yourself and what you do in the body. Realign your desires, then be useful or be cast to the side.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 37:4-5](/bible/psalms/37#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/37#v4)</sup> Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/37#v5)</sup> Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

    He gives the desires of your heart, but they have to be aligned with what he wants — not a Corvette or fifteen pounds of muscle. Commit thy way unto the Lord and he shall bring it to pass.


**[Genesis 4:6-7](/bible/genesis/4#v6)**  *[[2:42:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=9728s)]*

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/4#v6)</sup> And the Lord said unto Cain, Why art thou wroth? and why is thy countenance fallen?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/4#v7)</sup> If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted? and if thou doest not well, sin lieth at the door. And unto thee shall be his desire, and thou shalt rule over him.

- It did not matter that Cain desired the attention Abel was getting, or felt it was something he should be given.

- "If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted?" You are qualified by the work you do.

- If thou doest not well, sin lieth at the door, because pride enters in instead of examining what has to happen for the Lord to call you up.

- Now Satan has his target and his eyes set on you, and he will be there at every turn once you move in that spirit.

  Precepts:
  - **[James 3:16](/bible/james/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/james/3#v16)</sup> For where envying and strife is, there is confusion and every evil work.

    Envying and strife mean Satan is present, and it works from the church to the household to you as an individual.


## Class Questions

- **"Be not many masters" — who knows what that means?** The teachers were the ones called masters, and the more a man professes to know, the more he is held accountable to it.
- **What does he mean, judges of the ends of the earth?** Not the world of Israel only — the whole wide world and everything in it once the promises are fulfilled, and the angels that rule the earth.
- **Nowhere does Joshua say he was afraid, so why would the Lord have to tell him to be strong and of a good courage?** Because the Lord tries the reins and knows the heart. Joshua was intimidated by the role, and God met the internal dialogue he was at least honest about.
- **What are the arrows for?** For war, for attack. That is the heritage you leave behind.
- **When are you supposed to be ready for the Sabbath?** Before the Sabbath comes — that is what the laws on preparation say.
- **When you are not understood, whose burden is that — the listener's or the one speaking?** The one speaking. Consider your audience and make it plain to the people.

## In Closing

*[[2:43:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xIzsjL7PUx8&t=9826s)]* That is what it really means to be in leadership. Heavy is that head that has to be in that role, and all the knowledge and the awareness that come with it cannot be put back in the bottle. Meditate on the burdens you have to go through and the labours that are there, and on how beautiful that is in the end, and how glorious an honour it is to be called into that work. I pray you get some understanding, and more importantly application, to move with urgency on these things.
