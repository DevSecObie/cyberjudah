## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IDPM2zzSSvU&t=14s)]* The Revolutionaries of Gad, from IUIC Raleigh, on the Navajo Code Talkers: in World War II Native American Indians from the Choctaw, the Comanche and the Navajo transmitted secret messages in their own languages in codes the enemy could never break, saving countless lives, and came home to a country that still treated them as less than. God gave the knowledge to Israel; never trust thine enemy.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Baruch 3:36](/bible/baruch/3#v36)**  *[[1:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IDPM2zzSSvU&t=87s)]*

> <sup>[36](/bible/baruch/3#v36)</sup> He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.

- They created codes so complex the enemy forces could never break them, and they stayed classified by the US government and never received their just due. He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant: the Lord gave the tribe of Gad the intelligence to create secret messages with their native languages. Only the Israelites can do something like that.

- Codes like Morse code kept being broken, and they were ambushed and losing battles; when all else failed, they came to the Navajo Code Talkers, and the enemy's most skilled cryptographers couldn't break it.


**[Sirach 12:10](/bible/sirach/12#v10)**  *[[3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IDPM2zzSSvU&t=195s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/12#v10)</sup> Never trust thine enemy: for like as iron rusteth, so is his wickedness.

- Never trust thine enemy: our so-called Native American Indian brothers trusted the US military and the so-called white men that if they helped win the war they would be treated as equals, and returned to a country that treated them like three fifths of a man. They will always treat you as less than. The Code Talkers stand as a reminder of the courage, intelligence and resilience of the tribe of Gad.
