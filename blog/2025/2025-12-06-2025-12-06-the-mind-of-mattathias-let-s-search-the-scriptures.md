## Introduction

*[[14:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCE0PnbRYB8&t=868s)]* The feast of dedication is a feast of overcoming our oppressors through war, and the men of Israel have to meet it with the mind of a warrior, in battle mode. The class walks the history of war in the scriptures — the armies numbered inside every tribe, Gideon's three hundred, Ehud, Shamgar, Judith — and lands on Mattathias, the Levite father of Judas Maccabeus, whose mind set the revolt in motion because he knew his forefathers' history and used it to hold his five sons and his people together. The charge running through all of it is against the victim mind and against men who are not fit for the battle.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**  *[[18:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCE0PnbRYB8&t=1095s)]*

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

- Paul wrote his letters while Rome was in power and could not be as open as he wanted; the Romans checked the paperwork of what the Israelites were writing.

- That is the mind of Mattathias: a mind to remember what was written before, because it was written for our learning.


**[2 Timothy 2:3](/bible/2-timothy/2#v3)**  *[[19:04](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCE0PnbRYB8&t=1144s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-timothy/2#v3)</sup> Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.

- Hardness goes with soldier. The way we think as soldiers in Christ has to be hardcore — how do we increase this troop, how do we put our brothers in position to go do the work.

- Twelve tribes worldwide. We are not going on vacation. Go over the scriptures with the men until two and three in the morning, hit the streets with them, show them how to war in the spirit.

- Enduring hardness means the three trials: the personal trial on your job and in your day to day, the marital trial of holding on until the Lord sends a wife or a husband, and the congregational trial in the body.


**[Exodus 15:3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)**  *[[21:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCE0PnbRYB8&t=1297s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)</sup> The Lord is a man of war: the Lord is his name.

- If the Lord is a man of war, why did he let us go into captivity? Because when we did what he commanded, we destroyed nations and overcame our enemies — and when we fell off, he used our enemies to war against us.

- Chattel slavery, then 1865 and the emancipation proclamation, then reconstruction, black codes, Jim Crow and on through civil rights — and we are still going through it, because we left God's commandments.

- So the way we are going to be hardcore as soldiers in this feast of dedication is to keep the commandments, push this truth, overcome our own faults and rise above.

- That is why black men in Detroit, in Cincinnati, in New York, in Compton do not feel that Christian white nationalist church — they know there is no power in it, and they know in their spirit that they are powerful and strong.

- We are the gods of the earth and we are over all these nations. Last hired and first fired, and we still knew deep down we were something special. We are going to be gods upon this earth, but right now we deal in the vessel of men.

## Class Questions

- **The first day of the second month — what would that be?** A new moon.
- **What did it mean for the other nine thousand seven hundred men?** They did not have their minds on the big picture; they had their minds on themselves and lapped like dogs. The three hundred put their hand to their mouth so they could keep their eyes up and survey everything around them.
- **Was Eglon a regular fat man?** A very fat man — which is why the dagger had to be a cubit long and had to be thrust in.
- **"And he before them" — what does that mean?** He was a leader. He went out in front of them, and we have to lead by example in this truth.
- **Shamgar slew six hundred men with what?** With an ox goad — a spiked stick used for driving cattle.
- **How was Abraham tempted?** He was given an order by God that most men would not carry out, and he had to overcome his own mind to obey it.
