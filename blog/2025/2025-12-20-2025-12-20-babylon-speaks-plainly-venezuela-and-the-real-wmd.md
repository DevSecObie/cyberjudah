## Introduction

*[[9:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ExR7Cd5rqyQ&t=593s)]* Deacon Yashua reads the pressure on Venezuela through Habakkuk 2, Micah 2 and Revelation 18. America is modern Babylon, the proud man who gathers all nations and is never satisfied. The class looks at the strikes on boats in the Caribbean, the order classing fentanyl as a weapon of mass destruction, and the claim that Venezuela 'stole' American oil. It sets them beside Iraq, the scriptures on crafty counsel, and those who covet fields and take them by violence. It then explains how Bretton Woods and the dollar made the United States the world's banker, why Israel in the Americas and Africa lives under economic violence, and why only the Redeemer can restore what was robbed and spoiled.

## Class Questions

- **How does Habakkuk 2 describe the proud man?** He enlarges his desire as hell, cannot be satisfied, and gathers all nations unto him.
- **What does Micah 2:1–2 say the wicked do?** They devise iniquity, covet fields, and take them by violence because it is in the power of their hand.
- **What makes a man 'a man of blood' in Sirach 34:22?** Defrauding the labourer of his hire and taking away the bread of the needy.
- **How did Bretton Woods make America Babylon's banker?** It tied world trade to the dollar and set up the IMF and World Bank, so the dollar became a gatekeeper and a weapon.
- **Who will restore what was robbed and spoiled?** Israel's Redeemer, who is strong and will plead their cause (Jeremiah 50:34).

## In Closing

*[[2:51:34](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ExR7Cd5rqyQ&t=10294s)]* Babylon cannot fall until God brings her down, and he will reward her double. Keep the statutes, deal righteously with one another now, and wait on the Redeemer who will restore what was robbed and spoiled.
