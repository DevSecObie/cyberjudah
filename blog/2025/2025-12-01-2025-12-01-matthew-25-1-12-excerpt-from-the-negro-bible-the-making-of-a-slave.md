## Introduction

    Through a glass darkly now, face to face then; we know in part until we are taught as we were known.

**[Revelation 3:18](/bible/revelation/3#v18)**  *[[19:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8F_OivZ9pYg&t=1183s)]*

> <sup>[18](/bible/revelation/3#v18)</sup> I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire, that thou mayest be rich; and white raiment, that thou mayest be clothed, and that the shame of thy nakedness do not appear; and anoint thine eyes with eyesalve, that thou mayest see.

- Go to them that sell and buy — here is the precept for it. Christ says, I counsel thee to buy of me gold tried in the fire, so you have to go to the Lord.

- That thou mayest be clothed in white raiment and the shame of thy nakedness do not appear: the shame of your sin, which you want forgiven.

- Anoint thine eyes with eyesalve, that thou mayest see — so that your spiritual eyes can see.


**[Acts 26:17-18](/bible/acts/26#v17)**  *[[20:41](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8F_OivZ9pYg&t=1241s)]*

> <sup>[17](/bible/acts/26#v17)</sup> Delivering thee from the people, and from the Gentiles, unto whom now I send thee,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/acts/26#v18)</sup> To open their eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins, and inheritance among them which are sanctified by faith that is in me.

- Delivering thee from the people, the people of Israel, and from the Gentiles, those scattered Israelites, unto whom now I send you.

- To open their eyes, their spiritual eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light and from the power of Satan unto God.

- That they may receive forgiveness of sins and inheritance among them which are sanctified by faith: that goes for us as well.
