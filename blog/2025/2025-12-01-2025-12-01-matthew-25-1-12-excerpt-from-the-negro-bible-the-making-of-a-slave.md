## Introduction

*[[0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8F_OivZ9pYg&t=46s)]* The parable of the ten virgins, broken down verse by verse: the virgins are the hundred and forty-four thousand of the twelve tribes, the lamps are the commandments, the oil is the understanding, and the five that are shut out are the ones who prophesied in the name without keeping the law. The lesson closes in Allen H. Godbey's The Lost Tribes a Myth, on the Judaism found among West Africans and among the slaves brought to America.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Matthew 25:1](/bible/matthew/25#v1)**  *[[0:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8F_OivZ9pYg&t=46s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/25#v1)</sup> Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.

- The ten virgins are the elect of God, and Paul gives the precept that identifies them: he espoused us to one husband, to present us as a chaste virgin to Christ.

- They went forth to meet the bridegroom carrying lamps, and the lamp is the commandment: the commandment is a lamp and the law is light.

- So what these ten virgins took with them is the commandments and their understanding.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 11:2](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v2)</sup> For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.

    Paul is presenting us as a chaste virgin to Christ, so the virgins are us.
  - **[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

    The commandment is a lamp and the law is light, so the lamps the virgins took are the commandments.
