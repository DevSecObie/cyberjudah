## Introduction

*[[4:58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugTJ25CoQns&t=298s)]* A class on the war inside every Israelite. The title does not mean carnal weapons: either you kill the old man that still reigns in your members, or you will be killed serving him. The teacher starts from John 8:32 and 2 Corinthians 13:5. Know yourself and be honest about your hang-ups, because the enemy within is more deadly than any nation. The class works through the fear of the Lord that hates pride and the froward mouth, lust and covetousness, and Ezra's warning about the mixed holy seed. James 4 on wars that come from lusts leads to the renewing of the mind in Ephesians 4, and the class closes on dying daily and taking up the cross.

## Class Questions

- **What does 'kill or be killed' mean in this class?** Either you put to death the old man that reigns in your members, or you will die serving him.
- **Where do wars and fightings come from, according to James 4:1?** From the lusts that war in your members.
- **What does the fear of the Lord hate?** Evil, pride, arrogancy, the evil way and the froward mouth (Proverbs 8:13).
- **How is a man cleansed?** With the washing of water by the word, keeping the commandments (Ephesians 5:26).
- **What did Paul say about the old man?** I die daily (1 Corinthians 15:31).

## In Closing

*[[2:03:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugTJ25CoQns&t=7400s)]* Die daily. Deny yourself, take up your cross and follow Christ, putting off the old man and putting on the new, for the war begins within.
