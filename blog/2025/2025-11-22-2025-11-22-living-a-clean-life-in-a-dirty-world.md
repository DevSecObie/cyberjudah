> <sup>[9](/bible/james/4#v9)</sup> Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep: let your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to heaviness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/james/4#v10)</sup> Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up.

- Submit yourselves therefore to God: to God, to Messiah — not to a bishop and not to a deacon.

- Resist the devil, and he will flee from you. Resist sin and sin flees; but some of you love working for sin and believe you can work for sin and work for Messiah at the same time. Lukewarm gets spewed out, and if you are in the midst of it, today is your last warning to repent before the judgment comes.

- Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you — and you draw nigh with this book in your hand.

- Purify your hearts, ye double minded: your body is here on the Sabbath and your mind is somewhere else.

- Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep, and humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord and he shall lift you up. To be afflicted for this truth is a blessing. All of us are in mourning right now; there is no joy in this captivity, where a man is afraid of being fired for keeping the Sabbath and the New Moon. Our joy is the kingdom, so we have nothing to lose — keep the law and the kingdom is ours.


**[Galatians 5:16-25](/bible/galatians/5#v16)**  *[[1:38:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9D1WJlaxrsY&t=5898s)]*

> <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/5#v16)</sup> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)</sup> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/galatians/5#v18)</sup> But if ye be led of the Spirit, ye are not under the law.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/galatians/5#v20)</sup> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/5#v21)</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/galatians/5#v22)</sup> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/galatians/5#v23)</sup> Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/galatians/5#v24)</sup> And they that are Christ’s have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/galatians/5#v25)</sup> If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.

- Walk in the spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. Every one of us in here has a lust: money, a woman, another man, another woman, your children, your job.

- The flesh lusteth against the spirit, and the spirit against the flesh, and these are contrary the one to the other. This is a spiritual war, we carry both sides, and the only question is which one wins — and it does not stop until Christ comes back.

- Satan did not fire you, you fired him, so when you do the Most High's work expect to be attacked through your wife, your children and your past. Do not let him use your past against you: the past is what made you the man you are today, the Most High put it there for a reason, and Paul said look ahead — there is nothing you can do about what is behind you.

- The works of the flesh are named out loud: adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness, idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies, envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings. They which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God, and every one of us in this room is guilty of one of them.

- The fruit of the spirit is the flip side: love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, meekness — which is humble — and temperance, which is self-control. A brother who puts his hands on his wife has no self-control, and neither does a sister who puts hers on her husband.

- They that are Christ's have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts. If we live in the spirit, stay in the spirit and walk in it as well, because you have to live what you teach.


**[Romans 8:1-14](/bible/romans/8#v1)**  *[[1:49:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9D1WJlaxrsY&t=6550s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/8#v1)</sup> There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/8#v2)</sup> For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/8#v3)</sup> For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/8#v4)</sup> That the righteousness of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/romans/8#v5)</sup> For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/8#v6)</sup> For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/8#v7)</sup> Because the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/8#v8)</sup> So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/8#v9)</sup> But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/8#v10)</sup> And if Christ be in you, the body is dead because of sin; but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/romans/8#v11)</sup> But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/8#v12)</sup> Therefore, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live after the flesh.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/8#v13)</sup> For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/8#v14)</sup> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.

- There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh but after the spirit.

- What the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh: under the law adultery was death and homosexuality was death, nothing covered them. Under Messiah a man can repent, and the righteousness of the law is fulfilled in us who walk after the spirit.

- To be carnally minded is death, but to be spiritually minded is life and peace. The carnal mind is enmity against God and is not subject to his law, so they that are in the flesh cannot please God — which is why Christianity cannot please him, whether you sit in that church from seven in the morning to eleven at night.

- If any man have not the spirit of Christ, he is none of his. The goal is to become like Christ, and if he did it in the flesh we can do it: it was not a puff of smoke that came down here.

- If ye through the spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live. As many as are led by the spirit of God, they are the sons of God.


## Class Questions

- **What does the word holy mean?** Separate.
- **What does the word sanctify mean?** Set apart, or declared holy — the same thing as separate.
- **What is your cross?** Whatever you are willing to sacrifice for this truth: your son, your wife, your husband, your job.
- **What does the word mortify mean?** Kill.
- **What does the word concupiscence mean?** Deep sexual desire.
- **What does the word wrath mean?** Anger.
- **What does the word malice mean?** Hatred.
- **What is righteousness?** Observing to do all these commandments, as Deuteronomy 6:25 says it.
- **What does the word meekness mean?** Humble.
- **What does the word temperance mean?** Self-control.
