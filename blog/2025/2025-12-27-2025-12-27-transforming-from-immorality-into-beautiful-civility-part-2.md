## Introduction

*[[4:23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6rbgmjYODZE&t=263s)]* Part two of the series on turning from immorality back to civility, centred on marriage as the crown of a civilised nation. Bishop Yawasap opens on the idols of the nations and the tree of Jeremiah 10, then shows how Satan went to Eve first and still uses the woman to keep the man subdued. Slavery stripped fathers from the home and broke the family on purpose. The class turns to the fear of God as the whole duty of man, the beauty that deceives in Judith and Proverbs, the narrow path of 2 Esdras, and the man who rules his own house. It closes on the meek and quiet spirit of 1 Peter 3, the kind tongue of Sirach, and the need to prove a spouse by the Bible before marriage.

## Class Questions

- **Why did Satan go to Eve rather than Adam?** She was the weaker vessel. He showed her power over her husband, and she brought the sin to Adam (Genesis 3).
- **What is the whole duty of man?** To fear God and keep his commandments, for God brings every secret thing into judgment (Ecclesiastes 12:13–14).
- **What does Proverbs 31:30 teach a man to look for in a wife?** Not favour or beauty, which deceive and fade, but a woman who fears the Lord.
- **What qualifies a man to lead?** Ruling his own house well, with his children in subjection with all gravity (1 Timothy 3:4).
- **What adorning does 1 Peter 3 value most?** A meek and quiet spirit, which is of great price in the sight of God.

## In Closing

*[[2:49:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6rbgmjYODZE&t=10154s)]* Prove each other by the Bible before marriage. Men must rule their houses in righteousness and hedge their wives, and women must keep a meek spirit, so marriage becomes paradise rather than prison.
