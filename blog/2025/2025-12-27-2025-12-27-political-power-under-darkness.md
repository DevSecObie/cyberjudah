## Introduction

*[[5:43](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuHEXERuOKU&t=343s)]* A class on political power in a world given into the hand of the wicked. The war is against spiritual wickedness in high places, and the system of America was never built for the children of slaves. God rules in the kingdom of men and sets up the basest of men, and Israel was told to set a king only from among its brethren. The class looks at Black public figures who praise the administration, a famous athlete marrying outside the nation, and the Christmas Day strikes on Nigeria sold as the defence of Christians. It reads Micah, Job and Isaiah on the oppressor who covets fields and takes houses by violence, then turns to Daniel's stone kingdom and the power over the nations promised to the saints who overcome.

## Class Questions

- **Who sets up the rulers of this world?** The Most High, who rules in the kingdom of men and sets up over it the basest of men (Daniel 4:17).
- **What did God command about choosing a king?** Set one from among thy brethren, never a stranger (Deuteronomy 17:15).
- **What does Micah 2 say the oppressor does?** He covets fields and takes them by violence, because it is in the power of his hand.
- **What kingdom breaks the image in Daniel 2?** The stone cut without hands, God's kingdom, which shall never be destroyed.
- **Who receives power over the nations?** He that overcomes and keeps Christ's works to the end (Revelation 2:26).

## In Closing

*[[2:05:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BuHEXERuOKU&t=7520s)]* God is restoring Israel's judges little by little. Stay in the scriptures, overcome, and wait for the power he will give, for the kingdom of the saints shall never be destroyed.
