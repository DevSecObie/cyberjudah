## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uztvw3SRufQ&t=14s)]* The Revolutionaries of Gad, from IUIC Raleigh, on Black Elk: an Oglala Lakota warrior, holy man, healer and prophet, who at nine years old was shown by the star people, the thunder beings, whom God calls the angels, the suffering his people would go through at the hands of the so-called white man. Everything they told him came to pass, as Deuteronomy 28 says.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Amos 2:11](/bible/amos/2#v11)**  *[[1:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uztvw3SRufQ&t=78s)]*

> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/2#v11)</sup> And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazarites. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the Lord.

- I raised up of your sons for prophets: God's prophets are the children of Israel, which is exactly what Black Elk was. That's how we know who the Israelites are.


**[Deuteronomy 28:49](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)**  *[[2:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uztvw3SRufQ&t=138s)]*

> <sup>[49](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)</sup> The Lord shall bring a nation against thee from far, from the end of the earth, as swift as the eagle flieth; a nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand;

- The star people foretold the loss of their land and traditions at the arrival of the so-called white men, so-called because they're really red. The Lord shall bring a nation against thee from far: the Europeans, the conquistadors. A nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand: they spoke Spanish; we spoke Hebrew.


**[Job 38:31](/bible/job/38#v31)**  *[[4:19](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uztvw3SRufQ&t=259s)]*

> <sup>[31](/bible/job/38#v31)</sup> Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion?

- Black Elk said the star people, the ancestors of the people, came from Sirius and the Pleiades. Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades: God already had it recorded in the Bible.


<div class="reading">

Reading from Black Elk's account of his vision  *[[5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uztvw3SRufQ&t=330s)]*

> When we had camped again I was lying in our teepee and my mother and father was sitting beside me. I could see out through the opening, and there two men were coming from the clouds, head first.
>
> And I knew they were the same that I had seen before. Each now carried a long spear, and from the points of these a jagged lightning flashed. They came clear down to the ground this time, and stood a little way off and looked at me and said, hurry, come, your grandfathers are calling you.

> When I got up to follow, my legs did not hurt me anymore, and I was very light. I went outside the teepee, and yonder where the men with flaming spears were going, a little cloud was coming very fast. It came and stooped and took me and turned back to where it came from, flying fast. And when I looked down I could see my mother and my father yonder, and I felt sorry to be leaving.

- The cloud stooped over him and lifted him off the ground so high he had to look down to see his father and mother: he was in a cloud with these two thunder beings.

</div>


**[Psalms 104:3](/bible/psalms/104#v3)**  *[[7:33](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uztvw3SRufQ&t=453s)]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/104#v3)</sup> Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind:

- Who maketh the clouds his chariot: what y'all like to call UFOs, God calls chariots. Black Elk was taken up in a chariot of God. The curses of Deuteronomy 28:15-68 happened to the children of Israel and lined up with what happened to the so-called Native American Indians, so everything the star people told him came to pass: Black Elk was a prophet of the Most High, and his visions remain influential among the tribe of Gad.
