## Introduction

*[[19:13](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YRKZ18wNt8M&t=1153s)]* A class on Revelation 13 read against the history of the Moors of Spain and the Christian slave trade that followed their fall. The beast from the sea is a race of men, its heads and horns the European nations, and Rome's deadly wound was an African emperor on its throne. Dictionaries define the Moor as a negro and a blackamoor, and paintings show Moors in royal dress before 711 turned to captivity. Portugal shipped Jewish children to São Tomé, Europe gave Black servants as gifts, and Sinterklaas still parades a blackened helper. The class then turns to the true calendar, the moon as the sign of the world, the Gentiles who will serve in the kingdom, the law against strange marriages, and the saints who will judge the earth.

- In the kingdom the sun and moon will no longer be Israel's light, for the Lord is the everlasting light.

- The nations that are saved walk in the light of the city, but saved means spared from destruction, not salvation.

- The Gentiles come to Israel's light by going up yearly to keep the feast of tabernacles.

- Only those written in the Lamb's book of life enter, the twelve tribes of Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 21:23](/bible/revelation/21#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/revelation/21#v23)</sup> And the city had no need of the sun, neither of the moon, to shine in it: for the glory of God did lighten it, and the Lamb is the light thereof.

    The city needs no sun or moon, for the Lamb is the light thereof.

    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/60#v3)</sup> And the Gentiles shall come to thy light, and kings to the brightness of thy rising.

  - **[Isaiah 66:23](/bible/isaiah/66#v23)**

## Class Questions

- **What is the deadly wound in Revelation 13:3?** An African emperor, Septimius Severus, on the throne of Rome. Europe healed it by taking the throne back.
- **Who is the second beast of Revelation 13:11?** America. Its two horns are its two parties, and it exercises all the power of Rome.
- **When does the biblical year begin?** In Abib, the spring month of Passover (Exodus 12:1; Deuteronomy 16:1), not in January. Daniel 7:25 says the beast would change times and laws.
- **What does Sirach 43:6 say the moon is for?** A declaration of times and a sign of the world. Its phases trace Israel from the kingdom through captivity and back to full light.
- **What does 'saved' mean in Revelation 21:24?** The nations left from destruction, who go up yearly to worship the King and keep the feast of tabernacles (Zechariah 14:16).
- **Why were Israelites forbidden to marry the nations?** They turn the sons away from God and become snares and traps (Deuteronomy 7:3; Joshua 23:12).
