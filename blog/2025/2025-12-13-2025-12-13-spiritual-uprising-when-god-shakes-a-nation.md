## Introduction

*[[7:08](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEwdiVcsTg&t=428s)]* The class breaks down a spiritual uprising: Israel fell through sin, idolatry, and corrupt leadership, and can only rise again through repentance, unity, and obedience to God’s commandments. The charge is to reject carnal revolt, rebuild the nation through the scriptures, and stay focused on the kingdom and coming judgment.

## In The News

- *[[36:31](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEwdiVcsTg&t=2191s)]* **A clip listing facts about American slavery and its aftermath** — It was used to show that stolen labor built institutions and that oppression only changed form after emancipation.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Proverbs 14:34](/bible/proverbs/14#v34)**  *[[11:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EBEwdiVcsTg&t=697s)]*

> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/14#v34)</sup> Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.

- Righteousness raises a nation, and sin brings national shame and collapse.

- Israel did not fall for being righteous; we were brought down for disobedience.

- No people can survive in sin and still expect strength, power, and safety.

## Class Questions

- **Why did we fall as a nation?** We fell for sin, idolatry, and disobedience, not because we were righteous.
- **Does change come through weapons or money?** No; the uprising starts with the mind changing through God’s word.
- **How do we know who Christ’s disciples are?** By love among brethren, unity, and obedience to the commandments.
