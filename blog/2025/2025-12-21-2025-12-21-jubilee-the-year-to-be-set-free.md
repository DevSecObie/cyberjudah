## Introduction

*[[5:37](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-QJZ04vGiMw&t=337s)]* A class on the seventh-year release and the year of Jubilee, the law that kept any Israelite from staying poor or enslaved forever. The Sabbath of the land rested the fields every seventh year, cancelled debts between brethren, and freed Hebrew servants. Every fiftieth year, from the Day of Atonement, land returned to the family and tribe. The class sets that servitude against the chattel slavery of the nations, answers the verses used to call the Bible a slave book, and follows Israel's broken covenants in Jeremiah 34 and Nehemiah 5. It ends on Isaiah 61, where Christ proclaims liberty to the captives, and the acceptable year of the Lord is Israel's Jubilee.

## Class Questions

- **What happened in the seventh year?** The land rested, debts between brethren were released, and Hebrew servants went free (Exodus 23:10; Deuteronomy 15:1).
- **When was the Jubilee trumpet sounded?** On the Day of Atonement in the fiftieth year, proclaiming liberty and returning every man to his possession (Leviticus 25:9–10).
- **How was a poor brother to be treated?** As a hired servant until the Jubilee, never ruled over with rigour (Leviticus 25:39–43).
- **Why did God send Judah to the sword in Jeremiah 34?** They freed their Hebrew servants by covenant and then took them back into subjection.
- **What is Israel's Jubilee in prophecy?** The acceptable year of the Lord, when Christ proclaims liberty to the captives (Isaiah 61:1–2; Luke 4:18).
