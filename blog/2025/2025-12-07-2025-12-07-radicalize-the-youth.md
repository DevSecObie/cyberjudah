- **What does it mean that there was no more sea?** The other nations, as Isaiah 17:12 has them. A sixteen-year-old gave it.
- **Out of the fearful, the unbelieving, the sorcerers and all liars, which word am I looking for?** Sorcerers, because the seducing is the sorcery.
- **Who is the lender?** Our enemies: the stranger who gets up above us very high lends to us.
- **If the fathers teach the children and the children teach the generation to come, what is that called?** Radicalization.
