## Introduction

*[[0:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LLXXoFRS7Aw&t=14s)]* The Revolutionaries of Gad, from IUIC Raleigh, on Chief Red Cloud: the Oglala Lakota leader of Red Cloud's War, 1866 to 1868, against the US government's Bozeman Trail through the Powder River Country, and the Fort Laramie Treaty the government broke once it found gold. The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart.

## Scriptures Opened

**[Deuteronomy 33:20](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v20)**  *[[1:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LLXXoFRS7Aw&t=82s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v20)</sup> And of Gad he said, Blessed be he that enlargeth Gad: he dwelleth as a lion, and teareth the arm with the crown of the head.

- Blessed be he that enlargeth Gad: the Native American Indians had a large mass of land here in North America. He dwelleth as a lion: they wore war paint on their faces. And teareth the arm: the blood pacts with their brothers; with the crown of the head: the headdress the chiefs wore.

- He provided the first part for himself, because there, in a portion of the lawgiver, was he seated: the tribe of Gad had a part to himself and executed the judgments in Israel while in North America.

**[Psalms 55:20](/bible/psalms/55#v20)**  *[[3:15](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LLXXoFRS7Aw&t=195s)]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/55#v20)</sup> He hath put forth his hands against such as be at peace with him: he hath broken his covenant.

- The US government wanted the Bozeman Trail, connecting the Oregon Trail travelers to the gold fields of Montana, straight through the Powder River Country, the vital hunting ground of all the tribes, in present-day Wyoming and Montana. Red Cloud assembled warriors from all the tribes and with guerrilla warfare ambushed and killed 81 soldiers under Captain William Fetterman.

- He hath put forth his hands against such as be at peace with him: he hath broken his covenant: the government made the Fort Laramie Treaty, promising to remove its troops, stay off the sacred hunting grounds, break down its trails and continue education and aid, and broke it when it found gold.

- The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: smooth words for Red Cloud and for Sitting Bull, but war the whole time. Red Cloud will be remembered as a fierce protector and a staunch defender of the Native Americans, who are the tribe of Gad.
