- After the tradition of men is what Christianity taught them: that it is all right to celebrate Thanksgiving, eat unclean food and break the Lord's Sabbath day.

- After the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ. Christ never told you to do that.

- We teach the law, and that means all of it: marriage, the dietary law, the civil law and the moral law according to God, according to the Bible. That is what is missing in the black community.


**[Sirach 2:1-2](/bible/sirach/2#v1)**  *[[2:08:52](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r2TgBDuOkyY&t=7732s)]*

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/2#v2)</sup> Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.

- If you have come into this truth and you are serving the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation — and that temptation is going to come from the people who so-called love you, the people you grew up with, the people who raised you.

- Set thy heart aright: get your mind right every year, especially if you have only been here six months or a year, so your family knows not to text you happy Thanksgiving or merry Christmas.

- You can do it respectfully. You do not have to disrespect anybody; just let your light shine and do not answer back. They know you did not answer and they know you did not show up.

- Constantly endure: do not let them pull you and make you feel bad for not coming.


**[1 Corinthians 15:58](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v58)**  *[[2:10:12](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r2TgBDuOkyY&t=7812s)]*

> <sup>[58](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v58)</sup> Therefore, my beloved brethren, be ye stedfast, unmoveable, always abounding in the work of the Lord, forasmuch as ye know that your labour is not in vain in the Lord.

- Be ye stedfast and unmoveable: you were in Christianity twenty, thirty, forty, fifty, sixty, seventy years, and your family is only now hearing the truth. Do not let anybody take you back.

- Always abounding in the work of the Lord is keeping his Sabbath day and his laws, always doing what the Lord says do.

- Your labour is not in vain. If you missed Thanksgiving, know that you are here on the Sabbath day, watching class and taking notes, getting ready for the Sabbath — do not let that detour you back into the delusion of Christianity, because that is Satan's job.


**[Sirach 5:10](/bible/sirach/5#v10)**  *[[2:11:51](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r2TgBDuOkyY&t=7911s)]*

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/5#v10)</sup> Be stedfast in thy understanding; and let thy word be the same.

- Be stedfast in thine understanding: do not let your family sway you back into Christianity, and know that the Lord woke you up for a reason.

- Let thy word be the same — do not be a hypocrite. You cannot spend January to November trying to get your people to repent and then in November say you will just take one bowl of chitlins home, or accept just one Christmas gift. They laugh at you for it.


## Class Questions

- **What makes you a good parent?** Not what you can buy them. Teaching your children the law, statutes and commandments of God, keeping them yourself, learning your identity in the Bible and teaching that to your children. A lot of men and women go broke buying their kids Jordans and a PlayStation for Christmas, and that does not make you a good parent.
- **What is the love they received not?** 1 John 5:3 — this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments.
- **What is the truth they received not?** Psalms 119:142 — thy law is the truth.
- **What would be worth debating?** Who the real Jews are over there in Israel, and how they left the land black and returned white. They do not want to debate that; they want to debate stupid stuff, like whether God is one or three.
