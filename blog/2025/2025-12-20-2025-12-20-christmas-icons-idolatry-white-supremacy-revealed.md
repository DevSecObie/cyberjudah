## Introduction

*[[3:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jUTjrFS8YGQ&t=190s)]* A hidden-history class on how the Renaissance, the revival of Greco-Roman culture, whitewashed the image of Christ and the prophets. The teacher reads from Wikipedia on the Renaissance, humanism and 'Western culture,' then shows centuries of Byzantine, Coptic and Russian icons of a Black Christ, Black apostles, Black Job and David, and a Black St. Nicholas. It shows how later painters went over those images. Revelation 13 is read as the European beast and America its image, and Jeremiah 10 as the Christmas tree itself, a sacred tree from Assyria to Germanic paganism. The class closes on Black Pete, Krampus and the gifts that blind the eyes of the wise.

## Class Questions

- **What was the Renaissance a revival of?** Classical Greek and Roman culture, which repainted the Bible in a European image.
- **How do the old icons contradict the white Christ?** Byzantine, Coptic and Russian icons show Christ, the apostles, Job, David and St. Nicholas as Black, and some were later painted over.
- **What does Jeremiah 10:3–4 describe?** A tree cut from the forest, decked with silver and gold and fastened with nails so it will not move.
- **What does Sirach 20:29 say about gifts?** Presents and gifts blind the eyes of the wise.
- **What did the apostles tell the church to hold?** The traditions they taught by word or epistle, not the customs of men (2 Thessalonians 2:15; Colossians 2:8).

## In Closing

*[[2:16:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jUTjrFS8YGQ&t=8202s)]* Instruct those who oppose themselves in meekness, that God may give them repentance and they may escape the snare of the devil. Put away the tree, the gifts and the images, and hold the traditions of the apostles.
