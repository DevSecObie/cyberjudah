## Class Questions

- **What was the Renaissance a revival of?** Classical Greek and Roman culture, which repainted the Bible in a European image.
- **How do the old icons contradict the white Christ?** Byzantine, Coptic and Russian icons show Christ, the apostles, Job, David and St. Nicholas as Black, and some were later painted over.
- **What does Jeremiah 10:3–4 describe?** A tree cut from the forest, decked with silver and gold and fastened with nails so it will not move.
- **What does Sirach 20:29 say about gifts?** Presents and gifts blind the eyes of the wise.
- **What did the apostles tell the church to hold?** The traditions they taught by word or epistle, not the customs of men (2 Thessalonians 2:15; Colossians 2:8).
