## Class Questions

- **What is our job?** Just follow orders. Follow the men set before us, and the Lord will bless us.
- **How many fell in one day in the wilderness?** Three and twenty thousand.
- **Why was the anger of the Lord kindled against Uzza for putting his hand to the ark?** Only the priests, Aaron's sons, could deal with the ark. He had good intentions, but poor judgment.

## In Closing

*[[2:02:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bx1Ub_OMoZ4&t=7330s)]* Ninety per cent of the time them good intentions end up being bad results, bad decisions, bad judgment. Understand that good intentions are a cover-up for doing evil work a lot of times, so be mindful when a brother talks about he had good intentions: a lot of times that is a cover-up for him not completing his task, for him not doing what he needs to do, or an escape route and an excuse ready for when he fails.
