## Introduction

*[[3:53](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aRTWuD2pOek&t=233s)]* A class aired on The Power Hour, taught outdoors after an officer's questions kept the deacon two hours after class: why did the people in John 7:31 ask whether Christ, when he comes, would do more miracles than this man? Because Christ never openly declared himself the Christ to the masses: he charged the disciples, the healed and even the unclean spirits to tell no man, that the scriptures might be fulfilled, and declared himself only by his works, the scriptures and his "I am" sayings. Flesh and blood can't reveal Christ to you; the Father must. So examine yourselves whether ye be in the faith, pray morning, noon and evening, and fast with a willing heart. The class was titled at the end.
