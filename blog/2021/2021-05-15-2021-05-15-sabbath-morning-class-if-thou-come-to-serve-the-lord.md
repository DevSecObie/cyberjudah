## Introduction

Shalom Israel, most high in Christ bless. You tuned in to IUIC in the ClassRoom, the morning class. Today we are just going to go over a little bit of basic stuff, because this class is normally for people that are kind of new, and we do not go over the things you should expect when you come: having patience, enduring in this truth. Because things are going to come your way when you come.

The reason I am going over this is because when I first came into the truth I had that burning fire that I learned when I came in, and everybody got that. Facebook — if you did not understand what I knew, you dumb, you stupid, and you talking about two hundred comments under a post going back and forth all day. That was my life for the first six, seven months, probably the first year. And with that I probably burned some bridges, I probably rubbed people the wrong way, and when I used to go back and teach my family they did not want to hear it. That kind of messed me up, because I am a family man. I would think that me being around my family, me teaching them these new things, me changing my life, would be a great representation to bring my family into the truth. But I was sadly mistaken.

So everybody comes in at their own time, but when we come in we have got to make sure that we get ourselves together first. Be an example; do not try to force the example. Some of you might be new last week, last month, maybe even yesterday, and you were doing some wicked stuff with your family members, and now all of a sudden today you just super high holy and you know the whole Bible. You have got to be able to understand where you were when you came in, and give that same respect to your family members, your friends, and the people that you love and are around. Coming in this truth is not easy. Learning is what we need to do — but when you have got to apply it and then deal with your family that is not in the truth, that is kind of hard. But we are going to get through it.

- And beside this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge; and to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness. So all this works hand in hand. As you gain wisdom and understanding you start to deal with things differently. You start to not be so frantic, you start to not feel so down when you are going through things — you call on the Most High, and you know how things roll, so you know how to deal with things now, because you have got the experience and you have the patience for it.

- As you grow in this truth — the truth is not easy, it is not for the swift at all. You have got to endure. That is why I said constantly endure. If you endure, if you stick with keeping the commandments and you endure, the kingdom is yours. Straight up, the kingdom is yours.

- But before all these, they shall lay their hands on you, and persecute you. This is about us going out to camp and teaching, putting our life on the line. Sisters, if you stand next to your husband and he is out teaching, he is putting his brick in, and he is keeping the commandments, and you still look at your husband with disgust — something is wrong with you. You really do not believe that he is a prophet on this earth. A brother gets more respect when he goes out of town than he does in his own household. Brothers and sisters represent him more than his own wife. That is a problem. You should be his biggest fan, his biggest supporter. Seeing your husband change, you should feel great about it, that he is conducting his life in a righteous manner.

- Delivering you up to the synagogues, and into prisons, being brought before kings and rulers for my name's sake. And guess what, your own people will do that to you. Your own people, the other nations — everybody is against us. Everybody is against a righteous black man, Hispanic and Native American man, a brother trying to change his life and keep the commandments. They are going to persecute us, they are going to try to put their hands on us, they are going to try to put us in prison, they are going to bring us up before governments. They are trying to do that already, they put it in place. This stuff is written here for a reason, because it is going to happen.

- And it shall turn to you for a testimony.

- Settle it therefore in your hearts, not to meditate before what ye shall answer: for I will give you a mouth and wisdom. Because the Most High God is going to give you the words to speak to the people, regardless. The Most High is a mastermind, he is going to take care of you. That is why you have got to be fearless, that is why you have got to take camp one-on-ones and all of this seriously. The Most High is going to take care of you brothers for going out and teaching the word of God. You just have got to make sure you do your part. You speak his words, and everything else is going to be fine.

- Which all your adversaries shall not be able to gainsay nor resist. Because people come up to the camp trying to debunk the word of God, and when somebody comes to IUIC they get bodied by the scriptures, because we live by what we are talking about. We are serious about this, and the Most High is going to destroy them every time when we use his words — not talking out of our own mouth and going to all these different books. No, we use the word of God, and the Most High is going to confound anybody that comes to us. From the soldier to the bishop, you are going to get destroyed. No pastor, no apologist, no Muslim can come up and try to talk crazy — you are going to get destroyed, period.

- And ye shall be betrayed both by parents, and brethren, and kinsfolks, and friends. You are going to be betrayed by your parents. Your brethren are going to hate you. They want to put you to death because, oh, I cannot stand him, he thinks he is better than me. He is doing better, he just bought himself a house and everything is good and he changed his life — and he is mad at you because you are getting all of that and he is struggling. Well, you need to repent. Your cousins, your kinfolk are going to come against you. That is what I say: be ready. The Most High sent Christ down to put a variance in the household, to split it up, because they are going to hate each other because you are speaking God's words. Be happy that God chose you. And your friends — your best friends became your worst enemies, because you teach the word of God.

- And some of you shall they cause to be put to death. Some of them — your parents, your friends, your brothers, your kinfolk — are going to get you put to death for teaching the laws and commandments. Is that not what the Bible just said? So do not be soaking in your feelings because your family does not listen. Be that example, because somebody hates you, somebody wishes they were you right now. They wish they could walk around with their chest poked out and happy, because you are serving the one true God in all sincerity and in truth, keeping the commandments and faith in Christ. You have got your chest poked out with no worries in the world, and they are going to hate you for that.

- And ye shall be hated of all men for my name's sake. We are going to be hated by everybody. Look at what these pastors are doing — they are doing a little Christian summit to stop these Israelites from teaching. Why? Because they are losing members left and right. They are losing pastors, deacons, they are losing the praise and worship people. Everybody is repenting.

- But there shall not an hair of your head perish. You are not going to die. Your spirit is not going to die. It is going to go on, you are going to go to the kingdom, because you are teaching God's word. That fleshly body is nothing, but your spirit is not going anywhere.

- In your patience possess ye your souls. In your patience you possess your soul.

## In Closing

Shalom Israel, most high in Christ bless. I enjoy doing this class. Listen: keep enduring, keep enduring. You are coming in, you are new to this truth — endure to the end. If you need help, get help; that is what the body is there for. Let go of everything you went through, let it go, no matter what it was. Move forward in self-development. Develop your life in righteousness. Shalom Israel, most high in Christ bless.
