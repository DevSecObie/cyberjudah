Today's class is entitled Betrayers and Secret Admirers. The class spawned off a class I was watching that Bishop Nathanyel did some time ago concerning people with the spirit of envy. A lot of times you have people that are envious because they are not capable of doing what you're doing. Loosely, I'm saying what he said, but that's the point.
