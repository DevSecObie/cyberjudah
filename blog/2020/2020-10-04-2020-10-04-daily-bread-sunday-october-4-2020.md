## Introduction

*[[12:21](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=elg6Vk2VV3M&t=741s)]* Sunday morning Daily Bread from the Houston camp: Officer Michael, with Officer Abraham and another officer, teaches "Israel's Everlasting Covenant": "forever and ever and ever. He shows that the covenant, the laws, statutes and commandments and the sabbath, was given to Israel only and for ever, how our forefathers broke it and we are living through the curses, and how to come back to it.
