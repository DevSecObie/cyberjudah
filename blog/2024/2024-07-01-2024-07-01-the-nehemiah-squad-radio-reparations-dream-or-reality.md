## Introduction

*[[5:39](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DO6KijJECVI&t=339s)]* The third episode of The Nehemiah Squad, with Captain Zakai, Officer Shim and Officer Judah, on reparations: "what's happening with the reparations movement and what the Bible says about the reparations that's going to come." Their guest is Chris of the Coalition for a Just and Equitable California, who works on reparations in California. ## Class Questions

- **How did you get into the movement for reparations?** Chris: I started in reparations work in California around 2016 or 2017 and got serious in 2018 and 2019. I was born in Manhattan and raised in Manhattan and Brooklyn, Bed-Stuy and Brownsville.
- **Why is California ground zero?** Chris: about a hundred cities, counties and states are attempting something called reparations. California has the first mover advantage: people here started first, and others saw an opportunity.
- **Why state by state, when our work built the whole country?** Chris: the federal government is 400-plus years late; a national reparations committee bill has been introduced since 1988 and gone nowhere. So we do what we can locally and statewide to motivate the federal government. The states, counties, cities, schools, religious organizations and businesses all had a hand in it, and everybody who owes has got to pay.
- **What's the number you're looking for? They cannot pay us for the work that we did.** Chris: AB 3121, by then Assembly Member Dr. Shirley Weber, created a nine-member state reparations task force that worked from June 2021 to mid-2023. Its economists found close to $800 billion taken from us across a subgroup of areas, at minimum close to a trillion dollars statewide, with checks depending on how long you've lived in California; nationally at least $7 trillion, or 10 to 12 trillion figuring the 40 acres and a mule with interest. Last week the state budget had its first line item for reparations, about $12 million to get ready for payouts, and SB 1403 would create the California American Freedmen Affairs Agency with an office of genealogy.
- **What happened with repealing the involuntary servitude exception?** Chris: California and 16 other states have the 13th Amendment's exception, except as punishment for a crime, in their constitutions, and the prisons use it to force our brothers and sisters into paid and unpaid labor. We lost in 2021 because they said it would cost too much; last week ACA 8 passed, and the voters will decide in November.
- **When they pay the so-called blacks and then the Native Americans, where is the white man going to be?** Chris: I can't speak for them. We plan and win political battles every day; it's a war, and in war you win battles and you lose battles but you don't give up.
- **What's to stop white people from saying they have a black ancestor and want the money?** Chris: eligibility is not a racial classification; it is whether you descend from people enslaved and emancipated in this country. We stayed away from the words black and African American, which we did not create for ourselves. SB 189 section 14 (2022) lets people identify as descendants of those whose ancestors built this country.
- **Chris asked: if you got reparations tomorrow, what would you do with it?** Gather our people together (Zephaniah 2:1), build houses and plant gardens (Jeremiah 29:5), buy land and build schools, and put it back into our community.
