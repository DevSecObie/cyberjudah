Class notes: clips sit where the class played them, before an article read on screen

`class_order.py` timed an on-screen reading (`<div class="reading">`) by the passage before it,
because the reading's own moment is on its `Reading from` line, not its first line. A clip played
before the reading was therefore placed after it. The reading is now timed by its own moment, and
the 47 notes it affected are re-ordered. Lines only move; no wording changes.
