- Precepts in the concordance carry their own KJV words (`kjv`, for a precept of four verses or fewer), so the Bishops' commentary can show the scripture under each precept without leaving the page.

## Scripture he quotes in gold, Christ's words in red (2026-11-02)
- `data/red-letter.json`: the words of Christ in each KJV verse, marked from the public-domain KJV (eBible.org OSIS, words-of-Jesus marks), lined up with our own text; narration the source marked by mistake (Matthew 24:1-2, Mark 8:27, 8:34, Luke 7) taken out by hand.
- Chapter API verses carry `red` (his words in that verse); precepts carry `red` for their KJV words.
- Commentary rows and precepts carry `scripture`: what the teacher quotes inside his own words (in quote marks, or a whole sentence of five words or more) that is the KJV word for word; `red` when the words are found only among Christ's words.
