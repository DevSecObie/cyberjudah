# claude/project-thread-mimbcg

Giving The Sense From A to Z gets its own section on the site, the way Our Hidden History has one.

## What
- New page `/giving-the-sense`: the series' notes (the captains' notes filed under `collection: "Giving The Sense From A to Z"`), with the same browser as the other sections and the count of transcribed recordings.
- The Teaching menu, the footer and the command menu link to it, next to Our Hidden History.
- `/shows/giving-the-sense-from-a-to-z` redirects to it, and the Shows & Series list and the sitemap point to it. `data/shows.json` is unchanged, so the series keeps its transcribed-recordings count.
