# Verse readings from every feed, study-note sources, caption corrections

- `scripts/precepts/readings.py` reads all three transcript feeds (Sabbath classes, 15 Min w/ Captains, Our Hidden History), not only the Sabbath classes. A reading is followed for as long as each verse is heard within two minutes of the one before it, so a teacher who stops to teach after every verse is followed through the chapter. A bare "verse 16" (or "chapter 23 verse 4") resolves against the chapter last opened, up to 15 minutes back, and counts only when the verse is then read. Each row now says how it was found (`how`), and `data/precepts/readings/calls/<book>.json` keeps the caption words where each reference was said.
- `scripts/notes/prep.py` reads spelled-out numbers to ninety-nine ("chapter twenty three"), digit ordinals ("2 Esdras"), and the captions' "second edges" (2 Esdras), "villa cos" (Leviticus) and "sirac" (Sirach). Recall on the 315-note corpus rises from 76.6% to 77.2% with fewer stray finds.
- The concordance's `read` rows carry `how`, `said` and `feed`. Recordings from all three feeds now show on the verses they read.
- `scripts/study/sources.py` looks for each 4 Chapters a Day bullet in the precept passes and in the transcripts after a reading of the same verses, and records a source only on a plain wording match (`data/study/sources.json`); the rest are marked unsourced. The concordance's study-note commentary rows carry `sources`, in step with `points`.
- `scripts/transcripts/corrections.py` writes clear caption errors for book and feast words to `data/transcripts/corrections.json`, with the caption's own words beside each correction. The transcripts are not changed.
- `scripts/notes/coverage.py` reports notes coverage per feed and the recordings without notes that read the most verses.
- Dates: "15 Minutes with the Captains | Gods Holidays" 2020-05-25 (YouTube premiere date); "Passover Class: Exodus From America Modern Day Egypt" 2026-04-17 (the recording's publish date, which its transcript and precept pass already use; the note's estimated date was 2026-04-16). Both through `data/sources/class-teachers.tsv`, so no URL changes.
