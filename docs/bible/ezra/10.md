<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now when Ezra had prayed, and when he had confessed, weeping and casting himself down before the house of God, there assembled unto him out of Israel a very great congregation of men and women and children: for the people wept very sore.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And Shechaniah the son of Jehiel, one of the sons of Elam, answered and said unto Ezra, We have trespassed against our God, and have taken strange wives of the people of the land: yet now there is hope in Israel concerning this thing.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Now therefore let us make a covenant with our God to put away all the wives, and such as are born of them, according to the counsel of my lord, and of those that tremble at the commandment of our God; and let it be done according to the law.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Arise; for this matter belongeth unto thee: we also will be with thee: be of good courage, and do it.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Then arose Ezra, and made the chief priests, the Levites, and all Israel, to swear that they should do according to this word. And they sware.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then Ezra rose up from before the house of God, and went into the chamber of Johanan the son of Eliashib: and when he came thither, he did eat no bread, nor drink water: for he mourned because of the transgression of them that had been carried away.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And they made proclamation throughout Judah and Jerusalem unto all the children of the captivity, that they should gather themselves together unto Jerusalem;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And that whosoever would not come within three days, according to the counsel of the princes and the elders, all his substance should be forfeited, and himself separated from the congregation of those that had been carried away.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then all the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered themselves together unto Jerusalem within three days. It was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month; and all the people sat in the street of the house of God, trembling because of this matter, and for the great rain.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Ezra the priest stood up, and said unto them, Ye have transgressed, and have taken strange wives, to increase the trespass of Israel.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Now therefore make confession unto the Lord God of your fathers, and do his pleasure: and separate yourselves from the people of the land, and from the strange wives.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Then all the congregation answered and said with a loud voice, As thou hast said, so must we do.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But the people are many, and it is a time of much rain, and we are not able to stand without, neither is this a work of one day or two: for we are many that have transgressed in this thing.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Let now our rulers of all the congregation stand, and let all them which have taken strange wives in our cities come at appointed times, and with them the elders of every city, and the judges thereof, until the fierce wrath of our God for this matter be turned from us.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Only Jonathan the son of Asahel and Jahaziah the son of Tikvah were employed about this matter: and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the children of the captivity did so. And Ezra the priest, with certain chief of the fathers, after the house of their fathers, and all of them by their names, were separated, and sat down in the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And they made an end with all the men that had taken strange wives by the first day of the first month.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And among the sons of the priests there were found that had taken strange wives: namely, of the sons of Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and his brethren; Maaseiah, and Eliezer, and Jarib, and Gedaliah.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And they gave their hands that they would put away their wives; and being guilty, they offered a ram of the flock for their trespass.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And of the sons of Immer; Hanani, and Zebadiah.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And of the sons of Harim; Maaseiah, and Elijah, and Shemaiah, and Jehiel, and Uzziah.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And of the sons of Pashur; Elioenai, Maaseiah, Ishmael, Nethaneel, Jozabad, and Elasah.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Also of the Levites; Jozabad, and Shimei, and Kelaiah, (the same is Kelita,) Pethahiah, Judah, and Eliezer.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Of the singers also; Eliashib: and of the porters; Shallum, and Telem, and Uri.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Moreover of Israel: of the sons of Parosh; Ramiah, and Jeziah, and Malchiah, and Miamin, and Eleazar, and Malchijah, and Benaiah.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And of the sons of Elam; Mattaniah, Zechariah, and Jehiel, and Abdi, and Jeremoth, and Eliah.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And of the sons of Zattu; Elioenai, Eliashib, Mattaniah, and Jeremoth, and Zabad, and Aziza.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Of the sons also of Bebai; Jehohanan, Hananiah, Zabbai, and Athlai.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And of the sons of Bani; Meshullam, Malluch, and Adaiah, Jashub, and Sheal, and Ramoth.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And of the sons of Pahath–moab; Adna, and Chelal, Benaiah, Maaseiah, Mattaniah, Bezaleel, and Binnui, and Manasseh.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And of the sons of Harim; Eliezer, Ishijah, Malchiah, Shemaiah, Shimeon,

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Benjamin, Malluch, and Shemariah.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Of the sons of Hashum; Mattenai, Mattathah, Zabad, Eliphelet, Jeremai, Manasseh, and Shimei.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Of the sons of Bani; Maadai, Amram, and Uel,

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Benaiah, Bedeiah, Chelluh,

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Vaniah, Meremoth, Eliashib,

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Mattaniah, Mattenai, and Jaasau,

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And Bani, and Binnui, Shimei,

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And Shelemiah, and Nathan, and Adaiah,

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Machnadebai, Shashai, Sharai,

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Azareel, and Shelemiah, Shemariah,

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> Shallum, Amariah, and Joseph.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Of the sons of Nebo; Jeiel, Mattithiah, Zabad, Zebina, Jadau, and Joel, Benaiah.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> All these had taken strange wives: and some of them had wives by whom they had children.
