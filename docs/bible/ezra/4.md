<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now when the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin heard that the children of the captivity builded the temple unto the Lord God of Israel;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then they came to Zerubbabel, and to the chief of the fathers, and said unto them, Let us build with you: for we seek your God, as ye do; and we do sacrifice unto him since the days of Esar–haddon king of Assur, which brought us up hither.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But Zerubbabel, and Jeshua, and the rest of the chief of the fathers of Israel, said unto them, Ye have nothing to do with us to build an house unto our God; but we ourselves together will build unto the Lord God of Israel, as king Cyrus the king of Persia hath commanded us.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Then the people of the land weakened the hands of the people of Judah, and troubled them in building,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And hired counsellors against them, to frustrate their purpose, all the days of Cyrus king of Persia, even until the reign of Darius king of Persia.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And in the reign of Ahasuerus, in the beginning of his reign, wrote they unto him an accusation against the inhabitants of Judah and Jerusalem.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And in the days of Artaxerxes wrote Bishlam, Mithredath, Tabeel, and the rest of their companions, unto Artaxerxes king of Persia; and the writing of the letter was written in the Syrian tongue, and interpreted in the Syrian tongue.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Rehum the chancellor and Shimshai the scribe wrote a letter against Jerusalem to Artaxerxes the king in this sort:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then wrote Rehum the chancellor, and Shimshai the scribe, and the rest of their companions; the Dinaites, the Apharsathchites, the Tarpelites, the Apharsites, the Archevites, the Babylonians, the Susanchites, the Dehavites, and the Elamites,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the rest of the nations whom the great and noble Asnappar brought over, and set in the cities of Samaria, and the rest that are on this side the river, and at such a time.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> This is the copy of the letter that they sent unto him, even unto Artaxerxes the king; Thy servants the men on this side the river, and at such a time.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Be it known unto the king, that the Jews which came up from thee to us are come unto Jerusalem, building the rebellious and the bad city, and have set up the walls thereof, and joined the foundations.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Be it known now unto the king, that, if this city be builded, and the walls set up again, then will they not pay toll, tribute, and custom, and so thou shalt endamage the revenue of the kings.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Now because we have maintenance from the king’s palace, and it was not meet for us to see the king’s dishonour, therefore have we sent and certified the king;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> That search may be made in the book of the records of thy fathers: so shalt thou find in the book of the records, and know that this city is a rebellious city, and hurtful unto kings and provinces, and that they have moved sedition within the same of old time: for which cause was this city destroyed.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> We certify the king that, if this city be builded again, and the walls thereof set up, by this means thou shalt have no portion on this side the river.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Then sent the king an answer unto Rehum the chancellor, and to Shimshai the scribe, and to the rest of their companions that dwell in Samaria, and unto the rest beyond the river, Peace, and at such a time.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The letter which ye sent unto us hath been plainly read before me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And I commanded, and search hath been made, and it is found that this city of old time hath made insurrection against kings, and that rebellion and sedition have been made therein.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> There have been mighty kings also over Jerusalem, which have ruled over all countries beyond the river; and toll, tribute, and custom, was paid unto them.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Give ye now commandment to cause these men to cease, and that this city be not builded, until another commandment shall be given from me.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Take heed now that ye fail not to do this: why should damage grow to the hurt of the kings?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Now when the copy of king Artaxerxes’ letter was read before Rehum, and Shimshai the scribe, and their companions, they went up in haste to Jerusalem unto the Jews, and made them to cease by force and power.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Then ceased the work of the house of God which is at Jerusalem. So it ceased unto the second year of the reign of Darius king of Persia.
