*Taught in [Judges 1-4: Judges 1: Judah Goes Up First, but the Inhabitants Are Not Driven Out](/study/judges/1-4)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now these are the nations which the Lord left, to prove Israel by them, even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars of Canaan;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at the least such as before knew nothing thereof;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Namely, five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, and the Sidonians, and the Hivites that dwelt in mount Lebanon, from mount Baal–hermon unto the entering in of Hamath.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And they were to prove Israel by them, to know whether they would hearken unto the commandments of the Lord, which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And they took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and forgat the Lord their God, and served Baalim and the groves.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Therefore the anger of the Lord was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Chushan–rishathaim king of Mesopotamia: and the children of Israel served Chushan–rishathaim eight years.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And when the children of Israel cried unto the Lord, the Lord raised up a deliverer to the children of Israel, who delivered them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war: and the Lord delivered Chushan–rishathaim king of Mesopotamia into his hand; and his hand prevailed against Chushan–rishathaim.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And the land had rest forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Lord: and the Lord strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the Lord.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And he gathered unto him the children of Ammon and Amalek, and went and smote Israel, and possessed the city of palm trees.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> So the children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But when the children of Israel cried unto the Lord, the Lord raised them up a deliverer, Ehud the son of Gera, a Benjamite, a man lefthanded: and by him the children of Israel sent a present unto Eglon the king of Moab.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But Ehud made him a dagger which had two edges, of a cubit length; and he did gird it under his raiment upon his right thigh.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And he brought the present unto Eglon king of Moab: and Eglon was a very fat man.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And when he had made an end to offer the present, he sent away the people that bare the present.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But he himself turned again from the quarries that were by Gilgal, and said, I have a secret errand unto thee, O king: who said, Keep silence. And all that stood by him went out from him.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Ehud came unto him; and he was sitting in a summer parlour, which he had for himself alone. And Ehud said, I have a message from God unto thee. And he arose out of his seat.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Ehud put forth his left hand, and took the dagger from his right thigh, and thrust it into his belly:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And the haft also went in after the blade; and the fat closed upon the blade, so that he could not draw the dagger out of his belly; and the dirt came out.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Then Ehud went forth through the porch, and shut the doors of the parlour upon him, and locked them.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> When he was gone out, his servants came; and when they saw that, behold, the doors of the parlour were locked, they said, Surely he covereth his feet in his summer chamber.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And they tarried till they were ashamed: and, behold, he opened not the doors of the parlour; therefore they took a key, and opened them: and, behold, their lord was fallen down dead on the earth.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And Ehud escaped while they tarried, and passed beyond the quarries, and escaped unto Seirath.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And it came to pass, when he was come, that he blew a trumpet in the mountain of Ephraim, and the children of Israel went down with him from the mount, and he before them.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And he said unto them, Follow after me: for the Lord hath delivered your enemies the Moabites into your hand. And they went down after him, and took the fords of Jordan toward Moab, and suffered not a man to pass over.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And they slew of Moab at that time about ten thousand men, all lusty, and all men of valour; and there escaped not a man.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> So Moab was subdued that day under the hand of Israel. And the land had rest fourscore years.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And after him was Shamgar the son of Anath, which slew of the Philistines six hundred men with an ox goad: and he also delivered Israel.
