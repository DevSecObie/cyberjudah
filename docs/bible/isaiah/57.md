<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The righteous perisheth, and no man layeth it to heart: and merciful men are taken away, none considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He shall enter into peace: they shall rest in their beds, each one walking in his uprightness.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But draw near hither, ye sons of the sorceress, the seed of the adulterer and the whore.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Against whom do ye sport yourselves? against whom make ye a wide mouth, and draw out the tongue? are ye not children of transgression, a seed of falsehood,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Enflaming yourselves with idols under every green tree, slaying the children in the valleys under the clifts of the rocks?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Among the smooth stones of the stream is thy portion; they, they are thy lot: even to them hast thou poured a drink offering, thou hast offered a meat offering. Should I receive comfort in these?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Upon a lofty and high mountain hast thou set thy bed: even thither wentest thou up to offer sacrifice.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Behind the doors also and the posts hast thou set up thy remembrance: for thou hast discovered thyself to another than me, and art gone up; thou hast enlarged thy bed, and made thee a covenant with them; thou lovedst their bed where thou sawest it.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And thou wentest to the king with ointment, and didst increase thy perfumes, and didst send thy messengers far off, and didst debase thyself even unto hell.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thou art wearied in the greatness of thy way; yet saidst thou not, There is no hope: thou hast found the life of thine hand; therefore thou wast not grieved.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And of whom hast thou been afraid or feared, that thou hast lied, and hast not remembered me, nor laid it to thy heart? have not I held my peace even of old, and thou fearest me not?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will declare thy righteousness, and thy works; for they shall not profit thee.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> When thou criest, let thy companies deliver thee; but the wind shall carry them all away; vanity shall take them: but he that putteth his trust in me shall possess the land, and shall inherit my holy mountain;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And shall say, Cast ye up, cast ye up, prepare the way, take up the stumblingblock out of the way of my people.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For thus saith the high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy; I dwell in the high and holy place, with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite ones.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For I will not contend for ever, neither will I be always wroth: for the spirit should fail before me, and the souls which I have made.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For the iniquity of his covetousness was I wroth, and smote him: I hid me, and was wroth, and he went on frowardly in the way of his heart.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I have seen his ways, and will heal him: I will lead him also, and restore comforts unto him and to his mourners.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> I create the fruit of the lips; Peace, peace to him that is far off, and to him that is near, saith the Lord; and I will heal him.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But the wicked are like the troubled sea, when it cannot rest, whose waters cast up mire and dirt.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> There is no peace, saith my God, to the wicked.
