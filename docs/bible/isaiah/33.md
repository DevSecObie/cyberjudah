<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; and when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O Lord, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> At the noise of the tumult the people fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were scattered.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And your spoil shall be gathered like the gathering of the caterpiller: as the running to and fro of locusts shall he run upon them.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The Lord is exalted; for he dwelleth on high: he hath filled Zion with judgment and righteousness.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Behold, their valiant ones shall cry without: the ambassadors of peace shall weep bitterly.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The highways lie waste, the wayfaring man ceaseth: he hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The earth mourneth and languisheth: Lebanon is ashamed and hewn down: Sharon is like a wilderness; and Bashan and Carmel shake off their fruits.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Now will I rise, saith the Lord; now will I be exalted; now will I lift up myself.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Ye shall conceive chaff, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath, as fire, shall devour you.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the people shall be as the burnings of lime: as thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Hear, ye that are far off, what I have done; and, ye that are near, acknowledge my might.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The sinners in Zion are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites. Who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting burnings?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He shall dwell on high: his place of defence shall be the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Thine eyes shall see the king in his beauty: they shall behold the land that is very far off.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Thine heart shall meditate terror. Where is the scribe? where is the receiver? where is he that counted the towers?

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thou shalt not see a fierce people, a people of a deeper speech than thou canst perceive; of a stammering tongue, that thou canst not understand.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tabernacle that shall not be taken down; not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But there the glorious Lord will be unto us a place of broad rivers and streams; wherein shall go no galley with oars, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; he will save us.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Thy tacklings are loosed; they could not well strengthen their mast, they could not spread the sail: then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the inhabitant shall not say, I am sick: the people that dwell therein shall be forgiven their iniquity.
