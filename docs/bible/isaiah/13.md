<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I have commanded my sanctified ones, I have also called my mighty ones for mine anger, even them that rejoice in my highness.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The noise of a multitude in the mountains, like as of a great people; a tumultuous noise of the kingdoms of nations gathered together: the Lord of hosts mustereth the host of the battle.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They come from a far country, from the end of heaven, even the Lord, and the weapons of his indignation, to destroy the whole land.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Howl ye; for the day of the Lord is at hand; it shall come as a destruction from the Almighty.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Therefore shall all hands be faint, and every man’s heart shall melt:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces shall be as flames.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Behold, the day of the Lord cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For the stars of heaven and the constellations thereof shall not give their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not cause her light to shine.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And I will punish the world for their evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth shall remove out of her place, in the wrath of the Lord of hosts, and in the day of his fierce anger.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And it shall be as the chased roe, and as a sheep that no man taketh up: they shall every man turn to his own people, and flee every one into his own land.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Every one that is found shall be thrust through; and every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Their children also shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses shall be spoiled, and their wives ravished.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Behold, I will stir up the Medes against them, which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces; and they shall have no pity on the fruit of the womb; their eye shall not spare children.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees’ excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> It shall never be inhabited, neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation: neither shall the Arabian pitch tent there; neither shall the shepherds make their fold there.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But wild beasts of the desert shall lie there; and their houses shall be full of doleful creatures; and owls shall dwell there, and satyrs shall dance there.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And the wild beasts of the islands shall cry in their desolate houses, and dragons in their pleasant palaces: and her time is near to come, and her days shall not be prolonged.
