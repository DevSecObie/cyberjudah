<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> For Zion’s sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem’s sake I will not rest, until the righteousness thereof go forth as brightness, and the salvation thereof as a lamp that burneth.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And the Gentiles shall see thy righteousness, and all kings thy glory: and thou shalt be called by a new name, which the mouth of the Lord shall name.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou shalt also be a crown of glory in the hand of the Lord, and a royal diadem in the hand of thy God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou shalt no more be termed Forsaken; neither shall thy land any more be termed Desolate: but thou shalt be called Hephzi–bah, and thy land Beulah: for the Lord delighteth in thee, and thy land shall be married.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For as a young man marrieth a virgin, so shall thy sons marry thee: and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the Lord, keep not silence,

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Lord hath sworn by his right hand, and by the arm of his strength, Surely I will no more give thy corn to be meat for thine enemies; and the sons of the stranger shall not drink thy wine, for the which thou hast laboured:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But they that have gathered it shall eat it, and praise the Lord; and they that have brought it together shall drink it in the courts of my holiness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Go through, go through the gates; prepare ye the way of the people; cast up, cast up the highway; gather out the stones; lift up a standard for the people.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Behold, the Lord hath proclaimed unto the end of the world, Say ye to the daughter of Zion, Behold, thy salvation cometh; behold, his reward is with him, and his work before him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And they shall call them, The holy people, The redeemed of the Lord: and thou shalt be called, Sought out, A city not forsaken.
