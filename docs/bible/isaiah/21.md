<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The burden of the desert of the sea. As whirlwinds in the south pass through; so it cometh from the desert, from a terrible land.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> A grievous vision is declared unto me; the treacherous dealer dealeth treacherously, and the spoiler spoileth. Go up, O Elam: besiege, O Media; all the sighing thereof have I made to cease.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Therefore are my loins filled with pain: pangs have taken hold upon me, as the pangs of a woman that travaileth: I was bowed down at the hearing of it; I was dismayed at the seeing of it.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> My heart panted, fearfulness affrighted me: the night of my pleasure hath he turned into fear unto me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Prepare the table, watch in the watchtower, eat, drink: arise, ye princes, and anoint the shield.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For thus hath the Lord said unto me, Go, set a watchman, let him declare what he seeth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And he saw a chariot with a couple of horsemen, a chariot of asses, and a chariot of camels; and he hearkened diligently with much heed:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And he cried, A lion: My lord, I stand continually upon the watchtower in the daytime, and I am set in my ward whole nights:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And, behold, here cometh a chariot of men, with a couple of horsemen. And he answered and said, Babylon is fallen, is fallen; and all the graven images of her gods he hath broken unto the ground.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> O my threshing, and the corn of my floor: that which I have heard of the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, have I declared unto you.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The burden of Dumah. He calleth to me out of Seir, Watchman, what of the night? Watchman, what of the night?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The watchman said, The morning cometh, and also the night: if ye will enquire, enquire ye: return, come.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The burden upon Arabia. In the forest in Arabia shall ye lodge, O ye travelling companies of Dedanim.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The inhabitants of the land of Tema brought water to him that was thirsty, they prevented with their bread him that fled.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For they fled from the swords, from the drawn sword, and from the bent bow, and from the grievousness of war.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For thus hath the Lord said unto me, Within a year, according to the years of an hireling, and all the glory of Kedar shall fail:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And the residue of the number of archers, the mighty men of the children of Kedar, shall be diminished: for the Lord God of Israel hath spoken it.
