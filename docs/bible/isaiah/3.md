<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> For, behold, the Lord, the Lord of hosts, doth take away from Jerusalem and from Judah the stay and the staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The mighty man, and the man of war, the judge, and the prophet, and the prudent, and the ancient,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The captain of fifty, and the honourable man, and the counsellor, and the cunning artificer, and the eloquent orator.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And I will give children to be their princes, and babes shall rule over them.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the people shall be oppressed, every one by another, and every one by his neighbour: the child shall behave himself proudly against the ancient, and the base against the honourable.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> When a man shall take hold of his brother of the house of his father, saying, Thou hast clothing, be thou our ruler, and let this ruin be under thy hand:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In that day shall he swear, saying, I will not be an healer; for in my house is neither bread nor clothing: make me not a ruler of the people.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For Jerusalem is ruined, and Judah is fallen: because their tongue and their doings are against the Lord, to provoke the eyes of his glory.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The shew of their countenance doth witness against them; and they declare their sin as Sodom, they hide it not. Woe unto their soul! for they have rewarded evil unto themselves.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Say ye to the righteous, that it shall be well with him: for they shall eat the fruit of their doings.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Woe unto the wicked! it shall be ill with him: for the reward of his hands shall be given him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> As for my people, children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. O my people, they which lead thee cause thee to err, and destroy the way of thy paths.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The Lord standeth up to plead, and standeth to judge the people.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Lord will enter into judgment with the ancients of his people, and the princes thereof: for ye have eaten up the vineyard; the spoil of the poor is in your houses.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> What mean ye that ye beat my people to pieces, and grind the faces of the poor? saith the Lord God of hosts.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Moreover the Lord saith, Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and walk with stretched forth necks and wanton eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Therefore the Lord will smite with a scab the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and the Lord will discover their secret parts.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> In that day the Lord will take away the bravery of their tinkling ornaments about their feet, and their cauls, and their round tires like the moon,

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The chains, and the bracelets, and the mufflers,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The bonnets, and the ornaments of the legs, and the headbands, and the tablets, and the earrings,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The rings, and nose jewels,

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The changeable suits of apparel, and the mantles, and the wimples, and the crisping pins,

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The glasses, and the fine linen, and the hoods, and the vails.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And it shall come to pass, that instead of sweet smell there shall be stink; and instead of a girdle a rent; and instead of well set hair baldness; and instead of a stomacher a girding of sackcloth; and burning instead of beauty.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Thy men shall fall by the sword, and thy mighty in the war.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And her gates shall lament and mourn; and she being desolate shall sit upon the ground.
