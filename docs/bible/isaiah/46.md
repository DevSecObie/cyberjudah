<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth, their idols were upon the beasts, and upon the cattle: your carriages were heavy loaden; they are a burden to the weary beast.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> They stoop, they bow down together; they could not deliver the burden, but themselves are gone into captivity.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Hearken unto me, O house of Jacob, and all the remnant of the house of Israel, which are borne by me from the belly, which are carried from the womb:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you: I have made, and I will bear; even I will carry, and will deliver you.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> To whom will ye liken me, and make me equal, and compare me, that we may be like?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They lavish gold out of the bag, and weigh silver in the balance, and hire a goldsmith; and he maketh it a god: they fall down, yea, they worship.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They bear him upon the shoulder, they carry him, and set him in his place, and he standeth; from his place shall he not remove: yea, one shall cry unto him, yet can he not answer, nor save him out of his trouble.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Remember this, and shew yourselves men: bring it again to mind, O ye transgressors.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Remember the former things of old: for I am God, and there is none else; I am God, and there is none like me,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Calling a ravenous bird from the east, the man that executeth my counsel from a far country: yea, I have spoken it, I will also bring it to pass; I have purposed it, I will also do it.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Hearken unto me, ye stouthearted, that are far from righteousness:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> I bring near my righteousness; it shall not be far off, and my salvation shall not tarry: and I will place salvation in Zion for Israel my glory.
