<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Woe to her that is filthy and polluted, to the oppressing city!

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> She obeyed not the voice; she received not correction; she trusted not in the Lord; she drew not near to her God.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Her princes within her are roaring lions; her judges are evening wolves; they gnaw not the bones till the morrow.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Her prophets are light and treacherous persons: her priests have polluted the sanctuary, they have done violence to the law.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The just Lord is in the midst thereof; he will not do iniquity: every morning doth he bring his judgment to light, he faileth not; but the unjust knoweth no shame.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I have cut off the nations: their towers are desolate; I made their streets waste, that none passeth by: their cities are destroyed, so that there is no man, that there is none inhabitant.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I said, Surely thou wilt fear me, thou wilt receive instruction; so their dwelling should not be cut off, howsoever I punished them: but they rose early, and corrupted all their doings.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Therefore wait ye upon me, saith the Lord, until the day that I rise up to the prey: for my determination is to gather the nations, that I may assemble the kingdoms, to pour upon them mine indignation, even all my fierce anger: for all the earth shall be devoured with the fire of my jealousy.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For then will I turn to the people a pure language, that they may all call upon the name of the Lord, to serve him with one consent.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> From beyond the rivers of Ethiopia my suppliants, even the daughter of my dispersed, shall bring mine offering.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> In that day shalt thou not be ashamed for all thy doings, wherein thou hast transgressed against me: for then I will take away out of the midst of thee them that rejoice in thy pride, and thou shalt no more be haughty because of my holy mountain.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will also leave in the midst of thee an afflicted and poor people, and they shall trust in the name of the Lord.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The remnant of Israel shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: for they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Sing, O daughter of Zion; shout, O Israel; be glad and rejoice with all the heart, O daughter of Jerusalem.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The Lord hath taken away thy judgments, he hath cast out thine enemy: the king of Israel, even the Lord, is in the midst of thee: thou shalt not see evil any more.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> In that day it shall be said to Jerusalem, Fear thou not: and to Zion, Let not thine hands be slack.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The Lord thy God in the midst of thee is mighty; he will save, he will rejoice over thee with joy; he will rest in his love, he will joy over thee with singing.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I will gather them that are sorrowful for the solemn assembly, who are of thee, to whom the reproach of it was a burden.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Behold, at that time I will undo all that afflict thee: and I will save her that halteth, and gather her that was driven out; and I will get them praise and fame in every land where they have been put to shame.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> At that time will I bring you again, even in the time that I gather you: for I will make you a name and a praise among all people of the earth, when I turn back your captivity before your eyes, saith the Lord.
