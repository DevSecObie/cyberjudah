<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Let not your heart be troubled: ye believe in God, believe also in me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> In my Father’s house are many mansions: if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again, and receive you unto myself; that where I am, there ye may be also.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And whither I go ye know, and the way ye know.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thomas saith unto him, Lord, we know not whither thou goest; and how can we know the way?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also: and from henceforth ye know him, and have seen him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Philip saith unto him, Lord, shew us the Father, and it sufficeth us.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Jesus saith unto him, Have I been so long time with you, and yet hast thou not known me, Philip? he that hath seen me hath seen the Father; and how sayest thou then, Shew us the Father?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Believest thou not that I am in the Father, and the Father in me? the words that I speak unto you I speak not of myself: but the Father that dwelleth in me, he doeth the works.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Believe me that I am in the Father, and the Father in me: or else believe me for the very works’ sake.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me, the works that I do shall he do also; and greater works than these shall he do; because I go unto my Father.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And whatsoever ye shall ask in my name, that will I do, that the Father may be glorified in the Son.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> If ye shall ask any thing in my name, I will do it.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Yet a little while, and the world seeth me no more; but ye see me: because I live, ye shall live also.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> At that day ye shall know that I am in my Father, and ye in me, and I in you.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Judas saith unto him, not Iscariot, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself unto us, and not unto the world?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Jesus answered and said unto him, If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He that loveth me not keepeth not my sayings: and the word which ye hear is not mine, but the Father’s which sent me.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> These things have I spoken unto you, being yet present with you.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Ye have heard how I said unto you, I go away, and come again unto you. If ye loved me, ye would rejoice, because I said, I go unto the Father: for my Father is greater than I.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And now I have told you before it come to pass, that, when it is come to pass, ye might believe.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Hereafter I will not talk much with you: for the prince of this world cometh, and hath nothing in me.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> But that the world may know that I love the Father; and as the Father gave me commandment, even so I do. Arise, let us go hence.
