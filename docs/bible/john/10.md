<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that entereth not by the door into the sheepfold, but climbeth up some other way, the same is a thief and a robber.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> But he that entereth in by the door is the shepherd of the sheep.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> To him the porter openeth; and the sheep hear his voice: and he calleth his own sheep by name, and leadeth them out.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And when he putteth forth his own sheep, he goeth before them, and the sheep follow him: for they know his voice.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And a stranger will they not follow, but will flee from him: for they know not the voice of strangers.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> This parable spake Jesus unto them: but they understood not what things they were which he spake unto them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Then said Jesus unto them again, Verily, verily, I say unto you, I am the door of the sheep.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> All that ever came before me are thieves and robbers: but the sheep did not hear them.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But he that is an hireling, and not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, seeth the wolf coming, and leaveth the sheep, and fleeth: and the wolf catcheth them, and scattereth the sheep.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The hireling fleeth, because he is an hireling, and careth not for the sheep.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I am the good shepherd, and know my sheep, and am known of mine.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> As the Father knoweth me, even so know I the Father: and I lay down my life for the sheep.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Therefore doth my Father love me, because I lay down my life, that I might take it again.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> No man taketh it from me, but I lay it down of myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again. This commandment have I received of my Father.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> There was a division therefore again among the Jews for these sayings.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And many of them said, He hath a devil, and is mad; why hear ye him?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Others said, These are not the words of him that hath a devil. Can a devil open the eyes of the blind?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And it was at Jerusalem the feast of the dedication, and it was winter.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Jesus walked in the temple in Solomon’s porch.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Then came the Jews round about him, and said unto him, How long dost thou make us to doubt? If thou be the Christ, tell us plainly.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Jesus answered them, I told you, and ye believed not: the works that I do in my Father’s name, they bear witness of me.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But ye believe not, because ye are not of my sheep, as I said unto you.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And I give unto them eternal life; and they shall never perish, neither shall any man pluck them out of my hand.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> My Father, which gave them me, is greater than all; and no man is able to pluck them out of my Father’s hand.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> I and my Father are one.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Then the Jews took up stones again to stone him.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Jesus answered them, Many good works have I shewed you from my Father; for which of those works do ye stone me?

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> The Jews answered him, saying, For a good work we stone thee not; but for blasphemy; and because that thou, being a man, makest thyself God.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> If he called them gods, unto whom the word of God came, and the scripture cannot be broken;

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Say ye of him, whom the Father hath sanctified, and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest; because I said, I am the Son of God?

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> If I do not the works of my Father, believe me not.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> But if I do, though ye believe not me, believe the works: that ye may know, and believe, that the Father is in me, and I in him.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Therefore they sought again to take him: but he escaped out of their hand,

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And went away again beyond Jordan into the place where John at first baptized; and there he abode.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And many resorted unto him, and said, John did no miracle: but all things that John spake of this man were true.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> And many believed on him there.
