<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> After these things Jesus walked in Galilee: for he would not walk in Jewry, because the Jews sought to kill him.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Now the Jews’ feast of tabernacles was at hand.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> His brethren therefore said unto him, Depart hence, and go into Judea, that thy disciples also may see the works that thou doest.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For there is no man that doeth any thing in secret, and he himself seeketh to be known openly. If thou do these things, shew thyself to the world.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For neither did his brethren believe in him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then Jesus said unto them, My time is not yet come: but your time is alway ready.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The world cannot hate you; but me it hateth, because I testify of it, that the works thereof are evil.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Go ye up unto this feast: I go not up yet unto this feast; for my time is not yet full come.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> When he had said these words unto them, he abode still in Galilee.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But when his brethren were gone up, then went he also up unto the feast, not openly, but as it were in secret.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Then the Jews sought him at the feast, and said, Where is he?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And there was much murmuring among the people concerning him: for some said, He is a good man: others said, Nay; but he deceiveth the people.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Howbeit no man spake openly of him for fear of the Jews.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Now about the midst of the feast Jesus went up into the temple, and taught.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And the Jews marvelled, saying, How knoweth this man letters, having never learned?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Jesus answered them, and said, My doctrine is not mine, but his that sent me.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> If any man will do his will, he shall know of the doctrine, whether it be of God, or whether I speak of myself.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He that speaketh of himself seeketh his own glory: but he that seeketh his glory that sent him, the same is true, and no unrighteousness is in him.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Did not Moses give you the law, and yet none of you keepeth the law? Why go ye about to kill me?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The people answered and said, Thou hast a devil: who goeth about to kill thee?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Jesus answered and said unto them, I have done one work, and ye all marvel.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Moses therefore gave unto you circumcision; (not because it is of Moses, but of the fathers;) and ye on the sabbath day circumcise a man.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> If a man on the sabbath day receive circumcision, that the law of Moses should not be broken; are ye angry at me, because I have made a man every whit whole on the sabbath day?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Judge not according to the appearance, but judge righteous judgment.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Then said some of them of Jerusalem, Is not this he, whom they seek to kill?

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But, lo, he speaketh boldly, and they say nothing unto him. Do the rulers know indeed that this is the very Christ?

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Howbeit we know this man whence he is: but when Christ cometh, no man knoweth whence he is.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Then cried Jesus in the temple as he taught, saying, Ye both know me, and ye know whence I am: and I am not come of myself, but he that sent me is true, whom ye know not.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But I know him: for I am from him, and he hath sent me.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Then they sought to take him: but no man laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And many of the people believed on him, and said, When Christ cometh, will he do more miracles than these which this man hath done?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> The Pharisees heard that the people murmured such things concerning him; and the Pharisees and the chief priests sent officers to take him.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Then said Jesus unto them, Yet a little while am I with you, and then I go unto him that sent me.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Ye shall seek me, and shall not find me: and where I am, thither ye cannot come.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Then said the Jews among themselves, Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> What manner of saying is this that he said, Ye shall seek me, and shall not find me: and where I am, thither ye cannot come?

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> In the last day, that great day of the feast, Jesus stood and cried, saying, If any man thirst, let him come unto me, and drink.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> (But this spake he of the Spirit, which they that believe on him should receive: for the Holy Ghost was not yet given; because that Jesus was not yet glorified.)

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Many of the people therefore, when they heard this saying, said, Of a truth this is the Prophet.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Others said, This is the Christ. But some said, Shall Christ come out of Galilee?

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> Hath not the scripture said, That Christ cometh of the seed of David, and out of the town of Bethlehem, where David was?

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> So there was a division among the people because of him.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And some of them would have taken him; but no man laid hands on him.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> Then came the officers to the chief priests and Pharisees; and they said unto them, Why have ye not brought him?

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> The officers answered, Never man spake like this man.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> Then answered them the Pharisees, Are ye also deceived?

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> Have any of the rulers or of the Pharisees believed on him?

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> But this people who knoweth not the law are cursed.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> Nicodemus saith unto them, (he that came to Jesus by night, being one of them,)

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> Doth our law judge any man, before it hear him, and know what he doeth?

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> They answered and said unto him, Art thou also of Galilee? Search, and look: for out of Galilee ariseth no prophet.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> And every man went unto his own house.
