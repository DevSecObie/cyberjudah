<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Every branch in me that beareth not fruit he taketh away: and every branch that beareth fruit, he purgeth it, that it may bring forth more fruit.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Abide in me, and I in you. As the branch cannot bear fruit of itself, except it abide in the vine; no more can ye, except ye abide in me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> If a man abide not in me, he is cast forth as a branch, and is withered; and men gather them, and cast them into the fire, and they are burned.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If ye abide in me, and my words abide in you, ye shall ask what ye will, and it shall be done unto you.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Herein is my Father glorified, that ye bear much fruit; so shall ye be my disciples.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> As the Father hath loved me, so have I loved you: continue ye in my love.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> If ye keep my commandments, ye shall abide in my love; even as I have kept my Father’s commandments, and abide in his love.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> These things have I spoken unto you, that my joy might remain in you, and that your joy might be full.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> This is my commandment, That ye love one another, as I have loved you.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Greater love hath no man than this, that a man lay down his life for his friends.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Ye are my friends, if ye do whatsoever I command you.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Henceforth I call you not servants; for the servant knoweth not what his lord doeth: but I have called you friends; for all things that I have heard of my Father I have made known unto you.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Ye have not chosen me, but I have chosen you, and ordained you, that ye should go and bring forth fruit, and that your fruit should remain: that whatsoever ye shall ask of the Father in my name, he may give it you.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> These things I command you, that ye love one another.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> If the world hate you, ye know that it hated me before it hated you.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> If ye were of the world, the world would love his own: but because ye are not of the world, but I have chosen you out of the world, therefore the world hateth you.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Remember the word that I said unto you, The servant is not greater than his lord. If they have persecuted me, they will also persecute you; if they have kept my saying, they will keep yours also.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But all these things will they do unto you for my name’s sake, because they know not him that sent me.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> If I had not come and spoken unto them, they had not had sin: but now they have no cloke for their sin.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> He that hateth me hateth my Father also.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> If I had not done among them the works which none other man did, they had not had sin: but now have they both seen and hated both me and my Father.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> But this cometh to pass, that the word might be fulfilled that is written in their law, They hated me without a cause.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But when the Comforter is come, whom I will send unto you from the Father, even the Spirit of truth, which proceedeth from the Father, he shall testify of me:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And ye also shall bear witness, because ye have been with me from the beginning.
