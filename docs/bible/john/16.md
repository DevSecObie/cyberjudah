<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> These things have I spoken unto you, that ye should not be offended.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> They shall put you out of the synagogues: yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And these things will they do unto you, because they have not known the Father, nor me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But these things have I told you, that when the time shall come, ye may remember that I told you of them. And these things I said not unto you at the beginning, because I was with you.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But now I go my way to him that sent me; and none of you asketh me, Whither goest thou?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But because I have said these things unto you, sorrow hath filled your heart.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Nevertheless I tell you the truth; It is expedient for you that I go away: for if I go not away, the Comforter will not come unto you; but if I depart, I will send him unto you.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And when he is come, he will reprove the world of sin, and of righteousness, and of judgment:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Of sin, because they believe not on me;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Of righteousness, because I go to my Father, and ye see me no more;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Of judgment, because the prince of this world is judged.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I have yet many things to say unto you, but ye cannot bear them now.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Howbeit when he, the Spirit of truth, is come, he will guide you into all truth: for he shall not speak of himself; but whatsoever he shall hear, that shall he speak: and he will shew you things to come.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He shall glorify me: for he shall receive of mine, and shall shew it unto you.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> All things that the Father hath are mine: therefore said I, that he shall take of mine, and shall shew it unto you.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again, a little while, and ye shall see me, because I go to the Father.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Then said some of his disciples among themselves, What is this that he saith unto us, A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again, a little while, and ye shall see me: and, Because I go to the Father?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> They said therefore, What is this that he saith, A little while? we cannot tell what he saith.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Now Jesus knew that they were desirous to ask him, and said unto them, Do ye enquire among yourselves of that I said, A little while, and ye shall not see me: and again, a little while, and ye shall see me?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, That ye shall weep and lament, but the world shall rejoice: and ye shall be sorrowful, but your sorrow shall be turned into joy.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> A woman when she is in travail hath sorrow, because her hour is come: but as soon as she is delivered of the child, she remembereth no more the anguish, for joy that a man is born into the world.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And ye now therefore have sorrow: but I will see you again, and your heart shall rejoice, and your joy no man taketh from you.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And in that day ye shall ask me nothing. Verily, verily, I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall ask the Father in my name, he will give it you.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Hitherto have ye asked nothing in my name: ask, and ye shall receive, that your joy may be full.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> These things have I spoken unto you in proverbs: but the time cometh, when I shall no more speak unto you in proverbs, but I shall shew you plainly of the Father.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> At that day ye shall ask in my name: and I say not unto you, that I will pray the Father for you:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For the Father himself loveth you, because ye have loved me, and have believed that I came out from God.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> I came forth from the Father, and am come into the world: again, I leave the world, and go to the Father.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> His disciples said unto him, Lo, now speakest thou plainly, and speakest no proverb.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Now are we sure that thou knowest all things, and needest not that any man should ask thee: by this we believe that thou camest forth from God.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Jesus answered them, Do ye now believe?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Behold, the hour cometh, yea, is now come, that ye shall be scattered, every man to his own, and shall leave me alone: and yet I am not alone, because the Father is with me.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.
