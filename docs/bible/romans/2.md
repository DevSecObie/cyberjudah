<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Therefore thou art inexcusable, O man, whosoever thou art that judgest: for wherein thou judgest another, thou condemnest thyself; for thou that judgest doest the same things.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> But we are sure that the judgment of God is according to truth against them which commit such things.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And thinkest thou this, O man, that judgest them which do such things, and doest the same, that thou shalt escape the judgment of God?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Or despisest thou the riches of his goodness and forbearance and longsuffering; not knowing that the goodness of God leadeth thee to repentance?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But after thy hardness and impenitent heart treasurest up unto thyself wrath against the day of wrath and revelation of the righteous judgment of God;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Who will render to every man according to his deeds:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> To them who by patient continuance in well doing seek for glory and honour and immortality, eternal life:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But unto them that are contentious, and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, indignation and wrath,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that doeth evil, of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But glory, honour, and peace, to every man that worketh good, to the Jew first, and also to the Gentile:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For there is no respect of persons with God.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> (For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For when the Gentiles, which have not the law, do by nature the things contained in the law, these, having not the law, are a law unto themselves:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Which shew the work of the law written in their hearts, their conscience also bearing witness, and their thoughts the mean while accusing or else excusing one another;)

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> In the day when God shall judge the secrets of men by Jesus Christ according to my gospel.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Behold, thou art called a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast of God,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And knowest his will, and approvest the things that are more excellent, being instructed out of the law;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And art confident that thou thyself art a guide of the blind, a light of them which are in darkness,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, which hast the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law: but if thou be a breaker of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Therefore if the uncircumcision keep the righteousness of the law, shall not his uncircumcision be counted for circumcision?

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And shall not uncircumcision which is by nature, if it fulfil the law, judge thee, who by the letter and circumcision dost transgress the law?

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For he is not a Jew, which is one outwardly; neither is that circumcision, which is outward in the flesh:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But he is a Jew, which is one inwardly; and circumcision is that of the heart, in the spirit, and not in the letter; whose praise is not of men, but of God.
