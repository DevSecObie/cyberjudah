<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For what if some did not believe? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But if our unrighteousness commend the righteousness of God, what shall we say? Is God unrighteous who taketh vengeance? (I speak as a man)

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> God forbid: for then how shall God judge the world?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For if the truth of God hath more abounded through my lie unto his glory; why yet am I also judged as a sinner?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And not rather, (as we be slanderously reported, and as some affirm that we say,) Let us do evil, that good may come? whose damnation is just.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> What then? are we better than they? No, in no wise: for we have before proved both Jews and Gentiles, that they are all under sin;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As it is written, There is none righteous, no, not one:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Whose mouth is full of cursing and bitterness:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Their feet are swift to shed blood:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Destruction and misery are in their ways:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And the way of peace have they not known:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> There is no fear of God before their eyes.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Now we know that what things soever the law saith, it saith to them who are under the law: that every mouth may be stopped, and all the world may become guilty before God.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Therefore by the deeds of the law there shall no flesh be justified in his sight: for by the law is the knowledge of sin.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But now the righteousness of God without the law is manifested, being witnessed by the law and the prophets;

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Even the righteousness of God which is by faith of Jesus Christ unto all and upon all them that believe: for there is no difference:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God;

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Whom God hath set forth to be a propitiation through faith in his blood, to declare his righteousness for the remission of sins that are past, through the forbearance of God;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> To declare, I say, at this time his righteousness: that he might be just, and the justifier of him which believeth in Jesus.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Where is boasting then? It is excluded. By what law? of works? Nay: but by the law of faith.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Therefore we conclude that a man is justified by faith without the deeds of the law.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Is he the God of the Jews only? is he not also of the Gentiles? Yes, of the Gentiles also:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Seeing it is one God, which shall justify the circumcision by faith, and uncircumcision through faith.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Do we then make void the law through faith? God forbid: yea, we establish the law.
