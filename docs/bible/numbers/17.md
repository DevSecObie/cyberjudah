*Taught in [Numbers 15-18: Numbers 15: Offerings, Presumptuous Sin, and the Fringes](/study/numbers/15-18)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and take of every one of them a rod according to the house of their fathers, of all their princes according to the house of their fathers twelve rods: write thou every man’s name upon his rod.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And thou shalt write Aaron’s name upon the rod of Levi: for one rod shall be for the head of the house of their fathers.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And thou shalt lay them up in the tabernacle of the congregation before the testimony, where I will meet with you.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And it shall come to pass, that the man’s rod, whom I shall choose, shall blossom: and I will make to cease from me the murmurings of the children of Israel, whereby they murmur against you.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And Moses spake unto the children of Israel, and every one of their princes gave him a rod apiece, for each prince one, according to their fathers’ houses, even twelve rods: and the rod of Aaron was among their rods.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And Moses laid up the rods before the Lord in the tabernacle of witness.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And it came to pass, that on the morrow Moses went into the tabernacle of witness; and, behold, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi was budded, and brought forth buds, and bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And Moses brought out all the rods from before the Lord unto all the children of Israel: and they looked, and took every man his rod.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Bring Aaron’s rod again before the testimony, to be kept for a token against the rebels; and thou shalt quite take away their murmurings from me, that they die not.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And Moses did so: as the Lord commanded him, so did he.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the children of Israel spake unto Moses, saying, Behold, we die, we perish, we all perish.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Whosoever cometh any thing near unto the tabernacle of the Lord shall die: shall we be consumed with dying?
