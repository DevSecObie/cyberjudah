*Taught in [Numbers 27-30: Numbers 27: The Daughters of Zelophehad and the Ordination of Joshua](/study/numbers/27-30)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Command the children of Israel, and say unto them, My offering, and my bread for my sacrifices made by fire, for a sweet savour unto me, shall ye observe to offer unto me in their due season.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And thou shalt say unto them, This is the offering made by fire which ye shall offer unto the Lord; two lambs of the first year without spot day by day, for a continual burnt offering.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The one lamb shalt thou offer in the morning, and the other lamb shalt thou offer at even;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And a tenth part of an ephah of flour for a meat offering, mingled with the fourth part of an hin of beaten oil.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> It is a continual burnt offering, which was ordained in mount Sinai for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the drink offering thereof shall be the fourth part of an hin for the one lamb: in the holy place shalt thou cause the strong wine to be poured unto the Lord for a drink offering.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And the other lamb shalt thou offer at even: as the meat offering of the morning, and as the drink offering thereof, thou shalt offer it, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And on the sabbath day two lambs of the first year without spot, and two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, and the drink offering thereof:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> This is the burnt offering of every sabbath, beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And in the beginnings of your months ye shall offer a burnt offering unto the Lord; two young bullocks, and one ram, seven lambs of the first year without spot;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And three tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, for one bullock; and two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, for one ram;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And a several tenth deal of flour mingled with oil for a meat offering unto one lamb; for a burnt offering of a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And their drink offerings shall be half an hin of wine unto a bullock, and the third part of an hin unto a ram, and a fourth part of an hin unto a lamb: this is the burnt offering of every month throughout the months of the year.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And one kid of the goats for a sin offering unto the Lord shall be offered, beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And in the fourteenth day of the first month is the passover of the Lord.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And in the fifteenth day of this month is the feast: seven days shall unleavened bread be eaten.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> In the first day shall be an holy convocation; ye shall do no manner of servile work therein:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But ye shall offer a sacrifice made by fire for a burnt offering unto the Lord; two young bullocks, and one ram, and seven lambs of the first year: they shall be unto you without blemish:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil: three tenth deals shall ye offer for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram;

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> A several tenth deal shalt thou offer for every lamb, throughout the seven lambs:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And one goat for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Ye shall offer these beside the burnt offering in the morning, which is for a continual burnt offering.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> After this manner ye shall offer daily, throughout the seven days, the meat of the sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord: it shall be offered beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And on the seventh day ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Also in the day of the firstfruits, when ye bring a new meat offering unto the Lord, after your weeks be out, ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> But ye shall offer the burnt offering for a sweet savour unto the Lord; two young bullocks, one ram, seven lambs of the first year;

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And their meat offering of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals unto one bullock, two tenth deals unto one ram,

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> A several tenth deal unto one lamb, throughout the seven lambs;

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And one kid of the goats, to make an atonement for you.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Ye shall offer them beside the continual burnt offering, and his meat offering, (they shall be unto you without blemish) and their drink offerings.
