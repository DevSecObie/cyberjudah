<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Forasmuch as many have taken in hand to set forth in order a declaration of those things which are most surely believed among us,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Even as they delivered them unto us, which from the beginning were eyewitnesses, and ministers of the word;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> It seemed good to me also, having had perfect understanding of all things from the very first, to write unto thee in order, most excellent Theophilus,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> That thou mightest know the certainty of those things, wherein thou hast been instructed.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> There was in the days of Herod, the king of Judea, a certain priest named Zacharias, of the course of Abia: and his wife was of the daughters of Aaron, and her name was Elisabeth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And they were both righteous before God, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And they had no child, because that Elisabeth was barren, and they both were now well stricken in years.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And it came to pass, that while he executed the priest’s office before God in the order of his course,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> According to the custom of the priest’s office, his lot was to burn incense when he went into the temple of the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the whole multitude of the people were praying without at the time of incense.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And there appeared unto him an angel of the Lord standing on the right side of the altar of incense.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And when Zacharias saw him, he was troubled, and fear fell upon him.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But the angel said unto him, Fear not, Zacharias: for thy prayer is heard; and thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And thou shalt have joy and gladness; and many shall rejoice at his birth.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother’s womb.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And many of the children of Israel shall he turn to the Lord their God.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And he shall go before him in the spirit and power of Elias, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Zacharias said unto the angel, Whereby shall I know this? for I am an old man, and my wife well stricken in years.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And the angel answering said unto him, I am Gabriel, that stand in the presence of God; and am sent to speak unto thee, and to shew thee these glad tidings.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And, behold, thou shalt be dumb, and not able to speak, until the day that these things shall be performed, because thou believest not my words, which shall be fulfilled in their season.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And the people waited for Zacharias, and marvelled that he tarried so long in the temple.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And when he came out, he could not speak unto them: and they perceived that he had seen a vision in the temple: for he beckoned unto them, and remained speechless.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And it came to pass, that, as soon as the days of his ministration were accomplished, he departed to his own house.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And after those days his wife Elisabeth conceived, and hid herself five months, saying,

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Thus hath the Lord dealt with me in the days wherein he looked on me, to take away my reproach among men.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And in the sixth month the angel Gabriel was sent from God unto a city of Galilee, named Nazareth,

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> To a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin’s name was Mary.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And the angel came in unto her, and said, Hail, thou that art highly favoured, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And when she saw him, she was troubled at his saying, and cast in her mind what manner of salutation this should be.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And the angel said unto her, Fear not, Mary: for thou hast found favour with God.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And, behold, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call his name JESUS.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David:

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and of his kingdom there shall be no end.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Then said Mary unto the angel, How shall this be, seeing I know not a man?

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And the angel answered and said unto her, The Holy Ghost shall come upon thee, and the power of the Highest shall overshadow thee: therefore also that holy thing which shall be born of thee shall be called the Son of God.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And, behold, thy cousin Elisabeth, she hath also conceived a son in her old age: and this is the sixth month with her, who was called barren.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> For with God nothing shall be impossible.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And Mary said, Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it unto me according to thy word. And the angel departed from her.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And Mary arose in those days, and went into the hill country with haste, into a city of Juda;

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And entered into the house of Zacharias, and saluted Elisabeth.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And it came to pass, that, when Elisabeth heard the salutation of Mary, the babe leaped in her womb; and Elisabeth was filled with the Holy Ghost:

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> And she spake out with a loud voice, and said, Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And whence is this to me, that the mother of my Lord should come to me?

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> For, lo, as soon as the voice of thy salutation sounded in mine ears, the babe leaped in my womb for joy.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And blessed is she that believed: for there shall be a performance of those things which were told her from the Lord.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And Mary said, My soul doth magnify the Lord,

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> For he hath regarded the low estate of his handmaiden: for, behold, from henceforth all generations shall call me blessed.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> For he that is mighty hath done to me great things; and holy is his name.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> And his mercy is on them that fear him from generation to generation.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> He hath shewed strength with his arm; he hath scattered the proud in the imagination of their hearts.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> He hath put down the mighty from their seats, and exalted them of low degree.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> He hath filled the hungry with good things; and the rich he hath sent empty away.

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> He hath holpen his servant Israel, in remembrance of his mercy;

<a id="v55"></a><sup class="vn">55</sup> As he spake to our fathers, to Abraham, and to his seed for ever.

<a id="v56"></a><sup class="vn">56</sup> And Mary abode with her about three months, and returned to her own house.

<a id="v57"></a><sup class="vn">57</sup> Now Elisabeth’s full time came that she should be delivered; and she brought forth a son.

<a id="v58"></a><sup class="vn">58</sup> And her neighbours and her cousins heard how the Lord had shewed great mercy upon her; and they rejoiced with her.

<a id="v59"></a><sup class="vn">59</sup> And it came to pass, that on the eighth day they came to circumcise the child; and they called him Zacharias, after the name of his father.

<a id="v60"></a><sup class="vn">60</sup> And his mother answered and said, Not so; but he shall be called John.

<a id="v61"></a><sup class="vn">61</sup> And they said unto her, There is none of thy kindred that is called by this name.

<a id="v62"></a><sup class="vn">62</sup> And they made signs to his father, how he would have him called.

<a id="v63"></a><sup class="vn">63</sup> And he asked for a writing table, and wrote, saying, His name is John. And they marvelled all.

<a id="v64"></a><sup class="vn">64</sup> And his mouth was opened immediately, and his tongue loosed, and he spake, and praised God.

<a id="v65"></a><sup class="vn">65</sup> And fear came on all that dwelt round about them: and all these sayings were noised abroad throughout all the hill country of Judea.

<a id="v66"></a><sup class="vn">66</sup> And all they that heard them laid them up in their hearts, saying, What manner of child shall this be! And the hand of the Lord was with him.

<a id="v67"></a><sup class="vn">67</sup> And his father Zacharias was filled with the Holy Ghost, and prophesied, saying,

<a id="v68"></a><sup class="vn">68</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people,

<a id="v69"></a><sup class="vn">69</sup> And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David;

<a id="v70"></a><sup class="vn">70</sup> As he spake by the mouth of his holy prophets, which have been since the world began:

<a id="v71"></a><sup class="vn">71</sup> That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us;

<a id="v72"></a><sup class="vn">72</sup> To perform the mercy promised to our fathers, and to remember his holy covenant;

<a id="v73"></a><sup class="vn">73</sup> The oath which he sware to our father Abraham,

<a id="v74"></a><sup class="vn">74</sup> That he would grant unto us, that we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies might serve him without fear,

<a id="v75"></a><sup class="vn">75</sup> In holiness and righteousness before him, all the days of our life.

<a id="v76"></a><sup class="vn">76</sup> And thou, child, shalt be called the prophet of the Highest: for thou shalt go before the face of the Lord to prepare his ways;

<a id="v77"></a><sup class="vn">77</sup> To give knowledge of salvation unto his people by the remission of their sins,

<a id="v78"></a><sup class="vn">78</sup> Through the tender mercy of our God; whereby the dayspring from on high hath visited us,

<a id="v79"></a><sup class="vn">79</sup> To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.

<a id="v80"></a><sup class="vn">80</sup> And the child grew, and waxed strong in spirit, and was in the deserts till the day of his shewing unto Israel.
