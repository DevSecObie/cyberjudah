<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now when Darius reigned, he made a great feast unto all his subjects, and unto all his household, and unto all the princes of Media and Persia,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And to all the governors and captains and lieutenants that were under him, from India unto Ethiopia, of an hundred twenty and seven provinces.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And when they had eaten and drunken, and being satisfied were gone home, then Darius the king went into his bedchamber, and slept, and soon after awaked.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Then three young men, that were of the guard that kept the king’s body, spake one to another;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let every one of us speak a sentence: he that shall overcome, and whose sentence shall seem wiser than the others, unto him shall the king Darius give great gifts, and great things in token of victory:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> As, to be clothed in purple, to drink in gold, and to sleep upon gold, and a chariot with bridles of gold, and an headtire of fine linen, and a chain about his neck:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And he shall sit next to Darius because of his wisdom, and shall be called Darius his cousin.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And then every one wrote his sentence, sealed it, and laid it under king Darius his pillow;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And said that, when the king is risen, some will give him the writings; and of whose side the king and the three princes of Persia shall judge that his sentence is the wisest, to him shall the victory be given, as was appointed.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The first wrote, Wine is the strongest.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The second wrote, The king is strongest.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The third wrote, Women are strongest: but above all things Truth beareth away the victory.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Now when the king was risen up, they took their writings, and delivered them unto him, and so he read them:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And sending forth he called all the princes of Persia and Media, and the governors, and the captains, and the lieutenants, and the chief officers;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And sat him down in the royal seat of judgment; and the writings were read before them.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And he said, Call the young men, and they shall declare their own sentences. So they were called, and came in.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And he said unto them, Declare unto us your mind concerning the writings. Then began the first, who had spoken of the strength of wine;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And he said thus, O ye men, how exceeding strong is wine! it causeth all men to err that drink it:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> It maketh the mind of the king and of the fatherless child to be all one; of the bondman and of the freeman, of the poor man and of the rich:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> It turneth also every thought into jollity and mirth, so that a man remembereth neither sorrow nor debt:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And it maketh every heart rich, so that a man remembereth neither king nor governor; and it maketh to speak all things by talents:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And when they are in their cups, they forget their love both to friends and brethren, and a little after draw out swords:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> But when they are from the wine, they remember not what they have done.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> O ye men, is not wine the strongest, that enforceth to do thus? And when he had so spoken, he held his peace.
