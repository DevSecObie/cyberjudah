<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And ye know that he was manifested to take away our sins; and in him is no sin.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Whosoever abideth in him sinneth not: whosoever sinneth hath not seen him, neither known him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Little children, let no man deceive you: he that doeth righteousness is righteous, even as he is righteous.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin; for his seed remaineth in him: and he cannot sin, because he is born of God.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil: whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, neither he that loveth not his brother.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For this is the message that ye heard from the beginning, that we should love one another.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one, and slew his brother. And wherefore slew he him? Because his own works were evil, and his brother’s righteous.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Marvel not, my brethren, if the world hate you.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> We know that we have passed from death unto life, because we love the brethren. He that loveth not his brother abideth in death.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Hereby perceive we the love of God, because he laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> But whoso hath this world’s good, and seeth his brother have need, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> My little children, let us not love in word, neither in tongue; but in deed and in truth.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And hereby we know that we are of the truth, and shall assure our hearts before him.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For if our heart condemn us, God is greater than our heart, and knoweth all things.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Beloved, if our heart condemn us not, then have we confidence toward God.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And whatsoever we ask, we receive of him, because we keep his commandments, and do those things that are pleasing in his sight.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And this is his commandment, That we should believe on the name of his Son Jesus Christ, and love one another, as he gave us commandment.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And he that keepeth his commandments dwelleth in him, and he in him. And hereby we know that he abideth in us, by the Spirit which he hath given us.
