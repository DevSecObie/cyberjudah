<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now Benjamin begat Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, and Aharah the third,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And the sons of Bela were, Addar, and Gera, and Abihud,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And Abishua, and Naaman, and Ahoah,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And these are the sons of Ehud: these are the heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Geba, and they removed them to Manahath:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And Naaman, and Ahiah, and Gera, he removed them, and begat Uzza, and Ahihud.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Shaharaim begat children in the country of Moab, after he had sent them away; Hushim and Baara were his wives.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And he begat of Hodesh his wife, Jobab, and Zibia, and Mesha, and Malcham,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Jeuz, and Shachia, and Mirma. These were his sons, heads of the fathers.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And of Hushim he begat Abitub, and Elpaal.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The sons of Elpaal; Eber, and Misham, and Shamed, who built Ono, and Lod, with the towns thereof:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Beriah also, and Shema, who were heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who drove away the inhabitants of Gath:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Ahio, Shashak, and Jeremoth,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And Zebadiah, and Arad, and Ader,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And Michael, and Ispah, and Joha, the sons of Beriah;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Zebadiah, and Meshullam, and Hezeki, and Heber,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Ishmerai also, and Jezliah, and Jobab, the sons of Elpaal;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And Jakim, and Zichri, and Zabdi,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Elienai, and Zilthai, and Eliel,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Adaiah, and Beraiah, and Shimrath, the sons of Shimhi;

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And Ishpan, and Heber, and Eliel,

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Abdon, and Zichri, and Hanan,

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And Hananiah, and Elam, and Antothijah,

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And Iphedeiah, and Penuel, the sons of Shashak;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And Shamsherai, and Shehariah, and Athaliah,

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And Jaresiah, and Eliah, and Zichri, the sons of Jeroham.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> These were heads of the fathers, by their generations, chief men. These dwelt in Jerusalem.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon; whose wife’s name was Maachah:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And his firstborn son Abdon, and Zur, and Kish, and Baal, and Nadab,

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And Mikloth begat Shimeah. And these also dwelt with their brethren in Jerusalem, over against them.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And Ner begat Kish, and Kish begat Saul, and Saul begat Jonathan, and Malchi–shua, and Abinadab, and Esh–baal.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And the son of Jonathan was Merib–baal; and Merib–baal begat Micah.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And the sons of Micah were, Pithon, and Melech, and Tarea, and Ahaz.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And Ahaz begat Jehoadah; and Jehoadah begat Alemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri begat Moza,

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> And Moza begat Binea: Rapha was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son:

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And Azel had six sons, whose names are these, Azrikam, Bocheru, and Ishmael, and Sheariah, and Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And the sons of Eshek his brother were, Ulam his firstborn, Jehush the second, and Eliphelet the third.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And the sons of Ulam were mighty men of valour, archers, and had many sons, and sons’ sons, an hundred and fifty. All these are of the sons of Benjamin.
