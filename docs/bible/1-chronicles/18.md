<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now after this it came to pass, that David smote the Philistines, and subdued them, and took Gath and her towns out of the hand of the Philistines.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And he smote Moab; and the Moabites became David’s servants, and brought gifts.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And David smote Hadarezer king of Zobah unto Hamath, as he went to stablish his dominion by the river Euphrates.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And David took from him a thousand chariots, and seven thousand horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen: David also houghed all the chariot horses, but reserved of them an hundred chariots.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And when the Syrians of Damascus came to help Hadarezer king of Zobah, David slew of the Syrians two and twenty thousand men.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then David put garrisons in Syria–damascus; and the Syrians became David’s servants, and brought gifts. Thus the Lord preserved David whithersoever he went.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadarezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Likewise from Tibhath, and from Chun, cities of Hadarezer, brought David very much brass, wherewith Solomon made the brasen sea, and the pillars, and the vessels of brass.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Now when Tou king of Hamath heard how David had smitten all the host of Hadarezer king of Zobah;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He sent Hadoram his son to king David, to enquire of his welfare, and to congratulate him, because he had fought against Hadarezer, and smitten him; (for Hadarezer had war with Tou;) and with him all manner of vessels of gold and silver and brass.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Them also king David dedicated unto the Lord, with the silver and the gold that he brought from all these nations; from Edom, and from Moab, and from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines, and from Amalek.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Moreover Abishai the son of Zeruiah slew of the Edomites in the valley of salt eighteen thousand.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And he put garrisons in Edom; and all the Edomites became David’s servants. Thus the Lord preserved David whithersoever he went.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> So David reigned over all Israel, and executed judgment and justice among all his people.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the host; and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, recorder.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And Zadok the son of Ahitub, and Abimelech the son of Abiathar, were the priests; and Shavsha was scribe;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and the sons of David were chief about the king.
