<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> These are the sons of Israel; Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Dan, Joseph, and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The sons of Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah: which three were born unto him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the sight of the Lord; and he slew him.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And Tamar his daughter in law bare him Pharez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The sons of Pharez; Hezron, and Hamul.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And the sons of Zerah; Zimri, and Ethan, and Heman, and Calcol, and Dara: five of them in all.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the sons of Carmi; Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the thing accursed.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And the sons of Ethan; Azariah.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The sons also of Hezron, that were born unto him; Jerahmeel, and Ram, and Chelubai.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Ram begat Amminadab; and Amminadab begat Nahshon, prince of the children of Judah;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And Nahshon begat Salma, and Salma begat Boaz,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Boaz begat Obed, and Obed begat Jesse,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Jesse begat his firstborn Eliab, and Abinadab the second, and Shimma the third,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Nethaneel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Ozem the sixth, David the seventh:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Whose sisters were Zeruiah, and Abigail. And the sons of Zeruiah; Abishai, and Joab, and Asahel, three.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Abigail bare Amasa: and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmeelite.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Caleb the son of Hezron begat children of Azubah his wife, and of Jerioth: her sons are these; Jesher, and Shobab, and Ardon.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And when Azubah was dead, Caleb took unto him Ephrath, which bare him Hur.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Hur begat Uri, and Uri begat Bezaleel.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And afterward Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead, whom he married when he was threescore years old; and she bare him Segub.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And Segub begat Jair, who had three and twenty cities in the land of Gilead.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And he took Geshur, and Aram, with the towns of Jair, from them, with Kenath, and the towns thereof, even threescore cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir the father of Gilead.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And after that Hezron was dead in Caleb–ephratah, then Abiah Hezron’s wife bare him Ashur the father of Tekoa.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And the sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron were, Ram the firstborn, and Bunah, and Oren, and Ozem, and Ahijah.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Jerahmeel had also another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And the sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerahmeel were, Maaz, and Jamin, and Eker.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And the sons of Onam were, Shammai, and Jada. And the sons of Shammai; Nadab, and Abishur.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And the name of the wife of Abishur was Abihail, and she bare him Ahban, and Molid.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And the sons of Nadab; Seled, and Appaim: but Seled died without children.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And the sons of Appaim; Ishi. And the sons of Ishi; Sheshan. And the children of Sheshan; Ahlai.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And the sons of Jada the brother of Shammai; Jether, and Jonathan: and Jether died without children.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And the sons of Jonathan; Peleth, and Zaza. These were the sons of Jerahmeel.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Now Sheshan had no sons, but daughters. And Sheshan had a servant, an Egyptian, whose name was Jarha.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And Sheshan gave his daughter to Jarha his servant to wife; and she bare him Attai.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And Attai begat Nathan, and Nathan begat Zabad,

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> And Zabad begat Ephlal, and Ephlal begat Obed,

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And Obed begat Jehu, and Jehu begat Azariah,

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And Azariah begat Helez, and Helez begat Eleasah,

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And Eleasah begat Sisamai, and Sisamai begat Shallum,

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And Shallum begat Jekamiah, and Jekamiah begat Elishama.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> Now the sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel were, Mesha his firstborn, which was the father of Ziph; and the sons of Mareshah the father of Hebron.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And the sons of Hebron; Korah, and Tappuah, and Rekem, and Shema.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And Shema begat Raham, the father of Jorkoam: and Rekem begat Shammai.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And the son of Shammai was Maon: and Maon was the father of Beth–zur.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And Ephah, Caleb’s concubine, bare Haran, and Moza, and Gazez: and Haran begat Gazez.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And the sons of Jahdai; Regem, and Jotham, and Geshan, and Pelet, and Ephah, and Shaaph.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> Maachah, Caleb’s concubine, bare Sheber, and Tirhanah.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> She bare also Shaaph the father of Madmannah, Sheva the father of Machbenah, and the father of Gibea: and the daughter of Caleb was Achsah.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> These were the sons of Caleb the son of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah; Shobal the father of Kirjath–jearim,

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> Salma the father of Beth–lehem, Hareph the father of Beth–gader.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> And Shobal the father of Kirjath–jearim had sons; Haroeh, and half of the Manahethites.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> And the families of Kirjath–jearim; the Ithrites, and the Puhites, and the Shumathites, and the Mishraites; of them came the Zareathites, and the Eshtaulites.

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> The sons of Salma; Beth–lehem, and the Netophathites, Ataroth, the house of Joab, and half of the Manahethites, the Zorites.

<a id="v55"></a><sup class="vn">55</sup> And the families of the scribes which dwelt at Jabez; the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and Suchathites. These are the Kenites that came of Hemath, the father of the house of Rechab.
