<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now it came to pass, as David sat in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, Lo, I dwell in an house of cedars, but the ark of the covenant of the Lord remaineth under curtains.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then Nathan said unto David, Do all that is in thine heart; for God is with thee.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And it came to pass the same night, that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Go and tell David my servant, Thus saith the Lord, Thou shalt not build me an house to dwell in:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For I have not dwelt in an house since the day that I brought up Israel unto this day; but have gone from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Wheresoever I have walked with all Israel, spake I a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why have ye not built me an house of cedars?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, even from following the sheep, that thou shouldest be ruler over my people Israel:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And I have been with thee whithersoever thou hast walked, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee, and have made thee a name like the name of the great men that are in the earth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Also I will ordain a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell in their place, and shall be moved no more; neither shall the children of wickedness waste them any more, as at the beginning,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. Moreover I will subdue all thine enemies. Furthermore I tell thee that the Lord will build thee an house.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And it shall come to pass, when thy days be expired that thou must go to be with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed after thee, which shall be of thy sons; and I will establish his kingdom.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He shall build me an house, and I will stablish his throne for ever.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> I will be his father, and he shall be my son: and I will not take my mercy away from him, as I took it from him that was before thee:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But I will settle him in mine house and in my kingdom for ever: and his throne shall be established for evermore.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And David the king came and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord God, and what is mine house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And yet this was a small thing in thine eyes, O God; for thou hast also spoken of thy servant’s house for a great while to come, and hast regarded me according to the estate of a man of high degree, O Lord God.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> What can David speak more to thee for the honour of thy servant? for thou knowest thy servant.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> O Lord, for thy servant’s sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all this greatness, in making known all these great things.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> O Lord, there is none like thee, neither is there any God beside thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And what one nation in the earth is like thy people Israel, whom God went to redeem to be his own people, to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness, by driving out nations from before thy people, whom thou hast redeemed out of Egypt?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For thy people Israel didst thou make thine own people for ever; and thou, Lord, becamest their God.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Therefore now, Lord, let the thing that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant and concerning his house be established for ever, and do as thou hast said.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Let it even be established, that thy name may be magnified for ever, saying, The Lord of hosts is the God of Israel, even a God to Israel: and let the house of David thy servant be established before thee.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For thou, O my God, hast told thy servant that thou wilt build him an house: therefore thy servant hath found in his heart to pray before thee.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And now, Lord, thou art God, and hast promised this goodness unto thy servant:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Now therefore let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may be before thee for ever: for thou blessest, O Lord, and it shall be blessed for ever.
