<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now these were the sons of David, which were born unto him in Hebron; the firstborn Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; the second Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The third, Absalom the son of Maachah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur: the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The fifth, Shephatiah of Abital: the sixth, Ithream by Eglah his wife.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> These six were born unto him in Hebron; and there he reigned seven years and six months: and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty and three years.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And these were born unto him in Jerusalem; Shimea, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon, four, of Bath–shua the daughter of Ammiel:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Ibhar also, and Elishama, and Eliphelet,

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> These were all the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines, and Tamar their sister.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Solomon’s son was Rehoboam, Abia his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Amon his son, Josiah his son.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And the sons of Josiah were, the firstborn Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And the sons of Jeconiah; Assir, Salathiel his son,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Malchiram also, and Pedaiah, and Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And the sons of Pedaiah were, Zerubbabel, and Shimei: and the sons of Zerubbabel; Meshullam, and Hananiah, and Shelomith their sister:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Hashubah, and Ohel, and Berechiah, and Hasadiah, Jushab–hesed, five.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And the sons of Hananiah; Pelatiah, and Jesaiah: the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shechaniah.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And the sons of Shechaniah; Shemaiah: and the sons of Shemaiah; Hattush, and Igeal, and Bariah, and Neariah, and Shaphat, six.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the sons of Neariah; Elioenai, and Hezekiah, and Azrikam, three.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the sons of Elioenai were, Hodaiah, and Eliashib, and Pelaiah, and Akkub, and Johanan, and Dalaiah, and Anani, seven.
