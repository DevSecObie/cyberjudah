<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Adam, Sheth, Enosh,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jered,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Henoch, Methuselah, Lamech,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And the sons of Gomer; Ashchenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The sons of Ham; Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raamah, and Sabtecha. And the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be mighty upon the earth.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And Mizraim begat Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Pathrusim, and Casluhim, (of whom came the Philistines,) and Caphthorim.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Canaan begat Zidon his firstborn, and Heth,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Jebusite also, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The sons of Shem; Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Arphaxad begat Shelah, and Shelah begat Eber.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg; because in his days the earth was divided: and his brother’s name was Joktan.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Hadoram also, and Uzal, and Diklah,

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Eber, Peleg, Reu,

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Serug, Nahor, Terah,

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Abram; the same is Abraham.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The sons of Abraham; Isaac, and Ishmael.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> These are their generations: The firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema,

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Now the sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan; Sheba, and Dedan.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And the sons of Midian; Ephah, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaah. All these are the sons of Keturah.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And Abraham begat Isaac. The sons of Isaac; Esau and Israel.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> The sons of Esau; Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> The sons of Eliphaz; Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> The sons of Reuel; Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And the sons of Seir; Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And the sons of Lotan; Hori, and Homam: and Timna was Lotan’s sister.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> The sons of Shobal; Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. And the sons of Zibeon; Aiah, and Anah.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> The sons of Anah; Dishon. And the sons of Dishon; Amram, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> The sons of Ezer; Bilhan, and Zavan, and Jakan. The sons of Dishan; Uz, and Aran.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Now these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the children of Israel; Bela the son of Beor: and the name of his city was Dinhabah.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And when Bela was dead, Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And when Jobab was dead, Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his stead.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And when Husham was dead, Hadad the son of Bedad, which smote Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Avith.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And when Hadad was dead, Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> And when Samlah was dead, Shaul of Rehoboth by the river reigned in his stead.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> And when Shaul was dead, Baal–hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> And when Baal–hanan was dead, Hadad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Pai; and his wife’s name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> Hadad died also. And the dukes of Edom were; duke Timnah, duke Aliah, duke Jetheth,

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> Duke Aholibamah, duke Elah, duke Pinon,

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> Duke Kenaz, duke Teman, duke Mibzar,

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> Duke Magdiel, duke Iram. These are the dukes of Edom.
