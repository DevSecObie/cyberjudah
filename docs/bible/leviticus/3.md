*Taught in [Leviticus 2-5: Leviticus 2: The Meat Offering of Fine Flour](/study/leviticus/2-5)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And if his oblation be a sacrifice of peace offering, if he offer it of the herd; whether it be a male or female, he shall offer it without blemish before the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his offering, and kill it at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation: and Aaron’s sons the priests shall sprinkle the blood upon the altar round about.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And he shall offer of the sacrifice of the peace offering an offering made by fire unto the Lord; the fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And the two kidneys, and the fat that is on them, which is by the flanks, and the caul above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Aaron’s sons shall burn it on the altar upon the burnt sacrifice, which is upon the wood that is on the fire: it is an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And if his offering for a sacrifice of peace offering unto the Lord be of the flock; male or female, he shall offer it without blemish.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If he offer a lamb for his offering, then shall he offer it before the Lord.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of his offering, and kill it before the tabernacle of the congregation: and Aaron’s sons shall sprinkle the blood thereof round about upon the altar.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And he shall offer of the sacrifice of the peace offering an offering made by fire unto the Lord; the fat thereof, and the whole rump, it shall he take off hard by the backbone; and the fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, which is by the flanks, and the caul above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And the priest shall burn it upon the altar: it is the food of the offering made by fire unto the Lord.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And if his offering be a goat, then he shall offer it before the Lord.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And he shall lay his hand upon the head of it, and kill it before the tabernacle of the congregation: and the sons of Aaron shall sprinkle the blood thereof upon the altar round about.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And he shall offer thereof his offering, even an offering made by fire unto the Lord; the fat that covereth the inwards, and all the fat that is upon the inwards,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, which is by the flanks, and the caul above the liver, with the kidneys, it shall he take away.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the priest shall burn them upon the altar: it is the food of the offering made by fire for a sweet savour: all the fat is the Lord’s.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> It shall be a perpetual statute for your generations throughout all your dwellings, that ye eat neither fat nor blood.
