*Taught in [Leviticus 18-21: Leviticus 18: After the Doings of Egypt and Canaan Shall Ye Not Do](/study/leviticus/18-21)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy: for I the Lord your God am holy.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the Lord your God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Turn ye not unto idols, nor make to yourselves molten gods: I am the Lord your God.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And if ye offer a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the Lord, ye shall offer it at your own will.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if ought remain until the third day, it shall be burnt in the fire.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is abominable; it shall not be accepted.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Therefore every one that eateth it shall bear his iniquity, because he hath profaned the hallowed thing of the Lord: and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the Lord your God.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I am the Lord.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the Lord, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, even a ram for a trespass offering.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering before the Lord for his sin which he hath done: and the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised: three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you: it shall not be eaten of.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy to praise the Lord withal.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am the Lord your God.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Ye shall not eat any thing with the blood: neither shall ye use enchantment, nor observe times.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: I am the Lord.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Do not prostitute thy daughter, to cause her to be a whore; lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the Lord.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Regard not them that have familiar spirits, neither seek after wizards, to be defiled by them: I am the Lord your God.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the Lord.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Therefore shall ye observe all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: I am the Lord.
