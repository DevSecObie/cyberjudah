*Taught in [Exodus 6-9: Exodus 6: The Promise Renewed and the Generations of Moses and Aaron](/study/exodus/6-9)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then the Lord said unto Moses, Now shalt thou see what I will do to Pharaoh: for with a strong hand shall he let them go, and with a strong hand shall he drive them out of his land.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And God spake unto Moses, and said unto him, I am the Lord:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, by the name of God Almighty, but by my name Jehovah was I not known to them.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And I have also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their pilgrimage, wherein they were strangers.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And I have also heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage; and I have remembered my covenant.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Wherefore say unto the children of Israel, I am the Lord, and I will bring you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians, and I will rid you out of their bondage, and I will redeem you with a stretched out arm, and with great judgments:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God: and ye shall know that I am the Lord your God, which bringeth you out from under the burdens of the Egyptians.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And I will bring you in unto the land, concerning the which I did swear to give it to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob; and I will give it you for an heritage: I am the Lord.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And Moses spake so unto the children of Israel: but they hearkened not unto Moses for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Go in, speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Moses spake before the Lord, saying, Behold, the children of Israel have not hearkened unto me; how then shall Pharaoh hear me, who am of uncircumcised lips?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, and gave them a charge unto the children of Israel, and unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> These be the heads of their fathers’ houses: The sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel; Hanoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi: these be the families of Reuben.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And the sons of Simeon; Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanitish woman: these are the families of Simeon.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations; Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari: and the years of the life of Levi were an hundred thirty and seven years.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The sons of Gershon; Libni, and Shimi, according to their families.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And the sons of Kohath; Amram, and Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel: and the years of the life of Kohath were an hundred thirty and three years.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And the sons of Merari; Mahali and Mushi: these are the families of Levi according to their generations.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Amram took him Jochebed his father’s sister to wife; and she bare him Aaron and Moses: and the years of the life of Amram were an hundred and thirty and seven years.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And the sons of Izhar; Korah, and Nepheg, and Zichri.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And the sons of Uzziel; Mishael, and Elzaphan, and Zithri.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Aaron took him Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab, sister of Naashon, to wife; and she bare him Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the sons of Korah; Assir, and Elkanah, and Abiasaph: these are the families of the Korhites.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And Eleazar Aaron’s son took him one of the daughters of Putiel to wife; and she bare him Phinehas: these are the heads of the fathers of the Levites according to their families.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> These are that Aaron and Moses, to whom the Lord said, Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their armies.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> These are they which spake to Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring out the children of Israel from Egypt: these are that Moses and Aaron.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And it came to pass on the day when the Lord spake unto Moses in the land of Egypt,

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> That the Lord spake unto Moses, saying, I am the Lord: speak thou unto Pharaoh king of Egypt all that I say unto thee.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Moses said before the Lord, Behold, I am of uncircumcised lips, and how shall Pharaoh hearken unto me?
