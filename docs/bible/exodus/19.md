*Taught in [Exodus 18-21: Exodus 18: Jethro's Counsel and Righteous Leadership](/study/exodus/18-21)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In the third month, when the children of Israel were gone forth out of the land of Egypt, the same day came they into the wilderness of Sinai.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For they were departed from Rephidim, and were come to the desert of Sinai, and had pitched in the wilderness; and there Israel camped before the mount.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And Moses went up unto God, and the Lord called unto him out of the mountain, saying, Thus shalt thou say to the house of Jacob, and tell the children of Israel;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Ye have seen what I did unto the Egyptians, and how I bare you on eagles’ wings, and brought you unto myself.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Now therefore, if ye will obey my voice indeed, and keep my covenant, then ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people: for all the earth is mine:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation. These are the words which thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And Moses came and called for the elders of the people, and laid before their faces all these words which the Lord commanded him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And all the people answered together, and said, All that the Lord hath spoken we will do. And Moses returned the words of the people unto the Lord.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Lo, I come unto thee in a thick cloud, that the people may hear when I speak with thee, and believe thee for ever. And Moses told the words of the people unto the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Go unto the people, and sanctify them to day and to morrow, and let them wash their clothes,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And be ready against the third day: for the third day the Lord will come down in the sight of all the people upon mount Sinai.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And thou shalt set bounds unto the people round about, saying, Take heed to yourselves, that ye go not up into the mount, or touch the border of it: whosoever toucheth the mount shall be surely put to death:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> There shall not an hand touch it, but he shall surely be stoned, or shot through; whether it be beast or man, it shall not live: when the trumpet soundeth long, they shall come up to the mount.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Moses went down from the mount unto the people, and sanctified the people; and they washed their clothes.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And he said unto the people, Be ready against the third day: come not at your wives.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And it came to pass on the third day in the morning, that there were thunders and lightnings, and a thick cloud upon the mount, and the voice of the trumpet exceeding loud; so that all the people that was in the camp trembled.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Moses brought forth the people out of the camp to meet with God; and they stood at the nether part of the mount.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And mount Sinai was altogether on a smoke, because the Lord descended upon it in fire: and the smoke thereof ascended as the smoke of a furnace, and the whole mount quaked greatly.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And when the voice of the trumpet sounded long, and waxed louder and louder, Moses spake, and God answered him by a voice.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And the Lord came down upon mount Sinai, on the top of the mount: and the Lord called Moses up to the top of the mount; and Moses went up.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Go down, charge the people, lest they break through unto the Lord to gaze, and many of them perish.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And let the priests also, which come near to the Lord, sanctify themselves, lest the Lord break forth upon them.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Moses said unto the Lord, The people cannot come up to mount Sinai: for thou chargedst us, saying, Set bounds about the mount, and sanctify it.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the Lord said unto him, Away, get thee down, and thou shalt come up, thou, and Aaron with thee: but let not the priests and the people break through to come up unto the Lord, lest he break forth upon them.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> So Moses went down unto the people, and spake unto them.
