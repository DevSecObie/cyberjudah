*Taught in [Exodus 22-25: Exodus 22: The Civil Laws of Restitution](/study/exodus/22-25)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Thou shalt not raise a false report: put not thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause to decline after many to wrest judgment:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Neither shalt thou countenance a poor man in his cause.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> If thou meet thine enemy’s ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under his burden, and wouldest forbear to help him, thou shalt surely help with him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thou shalt not wrest the judgment of thy poor in his cause.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Keep thee far from a false matter; and the innocent and righteous slay thou not: for I will not justify the wicked.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And thou shalt take no gift: for the gift blindeth the wise, and perverteth the words of the righteous.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Also thou shalt not oppress a stranger: for ye know the heart of a stranger, seeing ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And six years thou shalt sow thy land, and shalt gather in the fruits thereof:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But the seventh year thou shalt let it rest and lie still; that the poor of thy people may eat: and what they leave the beasts of the field shall eat. In like manner thou shalt deal with thy vineyard, and with thy oliveyard.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Six days thou shalt do thy work, and on the seventh day thou shalt rest: that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid, and the stranger, may be refreshed.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And in all things that I have said unto you be circumspect: and make no mention of the name of other gods, neither let it be heard out of thy mouth.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Three times thou shalt keep a feast unto me in the year.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Thou shalt keep the feast of unleavened bread: (thou shalt eat unleavened bread seven days, as I commanded thee, in the time appointed of the month Abib; for in it thou camest out from Egypt: and none shall appear before me empty:)

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the feast of harvest, the firstfruits of thy labours, which thou hast sown in the field: and the feast of ingathering, which is in the end of the year, when thou hast gathered in thy labours out of the field.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord God.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leavened bread; neither shall the fat of my sacrifice remain until the morning.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The first of the firstfruits of thy land thou shalt bring into the house of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Behold, I send an Angel before thee, to keep thee in the way, and to bring thee into the place which I have prepared.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not; for he will not pardon your transgressions: for my name is in him.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But if thou shalt indeed obey his voice, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For mine Angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Amorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites: and I will cut them off.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Thou shalt not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor do after their works: but thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and quite break down their images.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And ye shall serve the Lord your God, and he shall bless thy bread, and thy water; and I will take sickness away from the midst of thee.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> There shall nothing cast their young, nor be barren, in thy land: the number of thy days I will fulfil.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> I will send my fear before thee, and will destroy all the people to whom thou shalt come, and I will make all thine enemies turn their backs unto thee.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And I will send hornets before thee, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> I will not drive them out from before thee in one year; lest the land become desolate, and the beast of the field multiply against thee.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased, and inherit the land.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And I will set thy bounds from the Red sea even unto the sea of the Philistines, and from the desert unto the river: for I will deliver the inhabitants of the land into your hand; and thou shalt drive them out before thee.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor with their gods.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they make thee sin against me: for if thou serve their gods, it will surely be a snare unto thee.
