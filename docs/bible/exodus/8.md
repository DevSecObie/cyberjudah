*Taught in [Exodus 6-9: Exodus 6: The Promise Renewed and the Generations of Moses and Aaron](/study/exodus/6-9)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, Go unto Pharaoh, and say unto him, Thus saith the Lord, Let my people go, that they may serve me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And if thou refuse to let them go, behold, I will smite all thy borders with frogs:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And the river shall bring forth frogs abundantly, which shall go up and come into thine house, and into thy bedchamber, and upon thy bed, and into the house of thy servants, and upon thy people, and into thine ovens, and into thy kneadingtroughs:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And the frogs shall come up both on thee, and upon thy people, and upon all thy servants.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch forth thine hand with thy rod over the streams, over the rivers, and over the ponds, and cause frogs to come up upon the land of Egypt.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt; and the frogs came up, and covered the land of Egypt.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the magicians did so with their enchantments, and brought up frogs upon the land of Egypt.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Then Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron, and said, Intreat the Lord, that he may take away the frogs from me, and from my people; and I will let the people go, that they may do sacrifice unto the Lord.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And Moses said unto Pharaoh, Glory over me: when shall I intreat for thee, and for thy servants, and for thy people, to destroy the frogs from thee and thy houses, that they may remain in the river only?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And he said, To morrow. And he said, Be it according to thy word: that thou mayest know that there is none like unto the Lord our God.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And the frogs shall depart from thee, and from thy houses, and from thy servants, and from thy people; they shall remain in the river only.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh: and Moses cried unto the Lord because of the frogs which he had brought against Pharaoh.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And the Lord did according to the word of Moses; and the frogs died out of the houses, out of the villages, and out of the fields.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And they gathered them together upon heaps: and the land stank.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But when Pharaoh saw that there was respite, he hardened his heart, and hearkened not unto them; as the Lord had said.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch out thy rod, and smite the dust of the land, that it may become lice throughout all the land of Egypt.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And they did so; for Aaron stretched out his hand with his rod, and smote the dust of the earth, and it became lice in man, and in beast; all the dust of the land became lice throughout all the land of Egypt.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And the magicians did so with their enchantments to bring forth lice, but they could not: so there were lice upon man, and upon beast.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Then the magicians said unto Pharaoh, This is the finger of God: and Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he hearkened not unto them; as the Lord had said.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh; lo, he cometh forth to the water; and say unto him, Thus saith the Lord, Let my people go, that they may serve me.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Else, if thou wilt not let my people go, behold, I will send swarms of flies upon thee, and upon thy servants, and upon thy people, and into thy houses: and the houses of the Egyptians shall be full of swarms of flies, and also the ground whereon they are.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And I will sever in that day the land of Goshen, in which my people dwell, that no swarms of flies shall be there; to the end thou mayest know that I am the Lord in the midst of the earth.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And I will put a division between my people and thy people: to morrow shall this sign be.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the Lord did so; and there came a grievous swarm of flies into the house of Pharaoh, and into his servants’ houses, and into all the land of Egypt: the land was corrupted by reason of the swarm of flies.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And Pharaoh called for Moses and for Aaron, and said, Go ye, sacrifice to your God in the land.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And Moses said, It is not meet so to do; for we shall sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians to the Lord our God: lo, shall we sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians before their eyes, and will they not stone us?

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> We will go three days’ journey into the wilderness, and sacrifice to the Lord our God, as he shall command us.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And Pharaoh said, I will let you go, that ye may sacrifice to the Lord your God in the wilderness; only ye shall not go very far away: intreat for me.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And Moses said, Behold, I go out from thee, and I will intreat the Lord that the swarms of flies may depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people, to morrow: but let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more in not letting the people go to sacrifice to the Lord.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Moses went out from Pharaoh, and intreated the Lord.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And the Lord did according to the word of Moses; and he removed the swarms of flies from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; there remained not one.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And Pharaoh hardened his heart at this time also, neither would he let the people go.
