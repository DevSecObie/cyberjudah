*Taught in [Exodus 26-29: Exodus 26: The Pattern Shown in the Mount](/study/exodus/26-29)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And thou shalt make an altar of shittim wood, five cubits long, and five cubits broad; the altar shall be foursquare: and the height thereof shall be three cubits.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And thou shalt make the horns of it upon the four corners thereof: his horns shall be of the same: and thou shalt overlay it with brass.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And thou shalt make his pans to receive his ashes, and his shovels, and his basons, and his fleshhooks, and his firepans: all the vessels thereof thou shalt make of brass.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And thou shalt make for it a grate of network of brass; and upon the net shalt thou make four brasen rings in the four corners thereof.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And thou shalt put it under the compass of the altar beneath, that the net may be even to the midst of the altar.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And thou shalt make staves for the altar, staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with brass.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the staves shall be put into the rings, and the staves shall be upon the two sides of the altar, to bear it.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Hollow with boards shalt thou make it: as it was shewed thee in the mount, so shall they make it.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And thou shalt make the court of the tabernacle: for the south side southward there shall be hangings for the court of fine twined linen of an hundred cubits long for one side:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the twenty pillars thereof and their twenty sockets shall be of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets shall be of silver.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And likewise for the north side in length there shall be hangings of an hundred cubits long, and his twenty pillars and their twenty sockets of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets of silver.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And for the breadth of the court on the west side shall be hangings of fifty cubits: their pillars ten, and their sockets ten.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And the breadth of the court on the east side eastward shall be fifty cubits.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The hangings of one side of the gate shall be fifteen cubits: their pillars three, and their sockets three.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And on the other side shall be hangings fifteen cubits: their pillars three, and their sockets three.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And for the gate of the court shall be an hanging of twenty cubits, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, wrought with needlework: and their pillars shall be four, and their sockets four.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> All the pillars round about the court shall be filleted with silver; their hooks shall be of silver, and their sockets of brass.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The length of the court shall be an hundred cubits, and the breadth fifty every where, and the height five cubits of fine twined linen, and their sockets of brass.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> All the vessels of the tabernacle in all the service thereof, and all the pins thereof, and all the pins of the court, shall be of brass.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And thou shalt command the children of Israel, that they bring thee pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamp to burn always.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> In the tabernacle of the congregation without the vail, which is before the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall order it from evening to morning before the Lord: it shall be a statute for ever unto their generations on the behalf of the children of Israel.
