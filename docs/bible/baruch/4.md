<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endureth for ever: all they that keep it shall come to life; but such as leave it shall die.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Turn thee, O Jacob, and take hold of it: walk in the presence of the light thereof, that thou mayest be illuminated.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Give not thine honour to another, nor the things that are profitable unto thee to a strange nation.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> O Israel, happy are we: for things that are pleasing to God are made known unto us.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Be of good cheer, my people, the memorial of Israel.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Ye were sold to the nations, not for your destruction: but because ye moved God to wrath, ye were delivered unto the enemies.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For ye provoked him that made you by sacrificing unto devils, and not to God.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Ye have forgotten the everlasting God, that brought you up; and ye have grieved Jerusalem, that nursed you.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For when she saw the wrath of God coming upon you, she said, Hearken, O ye that dwell about Sion: God hath brought upon me great mourning;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For I saw the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting brought upon them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> With joy did I nourish them; but sent them away with weeping and mourning.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Let no man rejoice over me, a widow, and forsaken of many, who for the sins of my children am left desolate; because they departed from the law of God.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> They knew not his statutes, nor walked in the ways of his commandments, nor trod in the paths of discipline in his righteousness.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Let them that dwell about Sion come, and remember ye the captivity of my sons and daughters, which the Everlasting hath brought upon them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For he hath brought a nation upon them from far, a shameless nation, and of a strange language, who neither reverenced old man, nor pitied child.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> These have carried away the dear beloved children of the widow, and left her that was alone desolate without daughters.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> But what can I help you?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For he that brought these plagues upon you will deliver you from the hands of your enemies.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Go your way, O my children, go your way: for I am left desolate.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> I have put off the clothing of peace, and put upon me the sackcloth of my prayer: I will cry unto the Everlasting in my days.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Be of good cheer, O my children, cry unto the Lord, and he will deliver you from the power and hand of the enemies.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For my hope is in the Everlasting, that he will save you; and joy is come unto me from the Holy One, because of the mercy which shall soon come unto you from the Everlasting our Saviour.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For I sent you out with mourning and weeping: but God will give you to me again with joy and gladness for ever.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Like as now the neighbours of Sion have seen your captivity: so shall they see shortly your salvation from our God which shall come upon you with great glory, and brightness of the Everlasting.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> My children, suffer patiently the wrath that is come upon you from God: for thine enemy hath persecuted thee; but shortly thou shalt see his destruction, and shalt tread upon his neck.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> My delicate ones have gone rough ways, and were taken away as a flock caught of the enemies.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Be of good comfort, O my children, and cry unto God: for ye shall be remembered of him that brought these things upon you.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For as it was your mind to go astray from God: so, being returned, seek him ten times more.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For he that hath brought these plagues upon you shall bring you everlasting joy with your salvation.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Take a good heart, O Jerusalem: for he that gave thee that name will comfort thee.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Miserable are they that afflicted thee, and rejoiced at thy fall.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Miserable are the cities which thy children served: miserable is she that received thy sons.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> For as she rejoiced at thy ruin, and was glad of thy fall: so shall she be grieved for her own desolation.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> For I will take away the rejoicing of her great multitude, and her pride shall be turned into mourning.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> For fire shall come upon her from the Everlasting, long to endure; and she shall be inhabited of devils for a great time.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> O Jerusalem, look about thee toward the east, and behold the joy that cometh unto thee from God.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Lo, thy sons come, whom thou sentest away, they come gathered together from the east to the west by the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the glory of God.
