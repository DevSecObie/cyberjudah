<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O Lord Almighty, God of Israel, the soul in anguish the troubled spirit, crieth unto thee.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hear, O Lord, and have mercy; art thou art merciful: and have pity upon us, because we have sinned before thee.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For thou endurest for ever, and we perish utterly.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> O Lord Almighty, thou God of Israel, hear now the prayers of the dead Israelites, and of their children, which have sinned before thee, and not hearkened unto the voice of thee their God: for the which cause these plagues cleave unto us.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Remember not the iniquities of our forefathers: but think upon thy power and thy name now at this time.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For thou art the Lord our God, and thee, O Lord, will we praise.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And for this cause thou hast put thy fear in our hearts, to the intent that we should call upon thy name, and praise thee in our captivity: for we have called to mind all the iniquity of our forefathers, that sinned before thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity, where thou hast scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to payments, according to all the iniquities of our fathers, which departed from the Lord our God.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Hear, Israel, the commandments of life: give ear to understand wisdom.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> How happeneth it Israel, that thou art in thine enemies’ land, that thou art waxen old in a strange country, that thou art defiled with the dead,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> That thou art counted with them that go down into the grave?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Thou hast forsaken the fountain of wisdom.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For if thou hadst walked in the way of God, thou shouldest have dwelled in peace for ever.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Learn where is wisdom, where is strength, where is understanding; that thou mayest know also where is length of days, and life, where is the light of the eyes, and peace.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Who hath found out her place? or who hath come into her treasures ?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Where are the princes of the heathen become, and such as ruled the beasts upon the earth;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> They that had their pastime with the fowls of the air, and they that hoarded up silver and gold, wherein men trust, and made no end of their getting?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For they that wrought in silver, and were so careful, and whose works are unsearchable,

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> They are vanished and gone down to the grave, and others are come up in their steads.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Young men have seen light, and dwelt upon the earth: but the way of knowledge have they not known,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Nor understood the paths thereof, nor laid hold of it: their children were far off from that way.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> It hath not been heard of in Chanaan, neither hath it been seen in Theman.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The Agarenes that seek wisdom upon earth, the merchants of Meran and of Theman, the authors of fables, and searchers out of understanding; none of these have known the way of wisdom, or remember her paths.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> O Israel, how great is the house of God! and how large is the place of his possession!

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Great, and hath none end; high, and unmeasurable.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> There were the giants famous from the beginning, that were of so great stature, and so expert in war.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Those did not the Lord choose, neither gave he the way of knowledge unto them:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> But they were destroyed, because they had no wisdom, and perished through their own foolishness.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Who hath gone up into heaven, and taken her, and brought her down from the clouds?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Who hath gone over the sea, and found her, and will bring her for pure gold?

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> No man knoweth her way, nor thinketh of her path.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> But he that knoweth all things knoweth her, and hath found her out with his understanding: he that prepared the earth for evermore hath filled it with fourfooted beasts:

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> He that sendeth forth light, and it goeth, calleth it again, and it obeyeth him with fear.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> The stars shined in their watches, and rejoiced: when he calleth them, they say, Here we be; and so with cheerfulness they shewed light unto him that made them.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Afterward did he shew himself upon earth, and conversed with men.
