*Taught in [Genesis 44-47: Genesis 44: The Cup in Benjamin's Sack and Judah's Surety](/study/genesis/44-47)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And Israel took his journey with all that he had, and came to Beer–sheba, and offered sacrifices unto the God of his father Isaac.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And God spake unto Israel in the visions of the night, and said, Jacob, Jacob. And he said, Here am I.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And he said, I am God, the God of thy father: fear not to go down into Egypt; for I will there make of thee a great nation:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I will go down with thee into Egypt; and I will also surely bring thee up again: and Joseph shall put his hand upon thine eyes.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Jacob rose up from Beer–sheba: and the sons of Israel carried Jacob their father, and their little ones, and their wives, in the wagons which Pharaoh had sent to carry him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And they took their cattle, and their goods, which they had gotten in the land of Canaan, and came into Egypt, Jacob, and all his seed with him:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> His sons, and his sons’ sons with him, his daughters, and his sons’ daughters, and all his seed brought he with him into Egypt.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And these are the names of the children of Israel, which came into Egypt, Jacob and his sons: Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And the sons of Reuben; Hanoch, and Phallu, and Hezron, and Carmi.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the sons of Simeon; Jemuel, and Jamin, and Ohad, and Jachin, and Zohar, and Shaul the son of a Canaanitish woman.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And the sons of Levi; Gershon, Kohath, and Merari.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the sons of Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah, and Pharez, and Zerah: but Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan. And the sons of Pharez were Hezron and Hamul.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And the sons of Issachar; Tola, and Phuvah, and Job, and Shimron.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And the sons of Zebulun; Sered, and Elon, and Jahleel.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> These be the sons of Leah, which she bare unto Jacob in Padan–aram, with his daughter Dinah: all the souls of his sons and his daughters were thirty and three.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the sons of Gad; Ziphion, and Haggi, Shuni, and Ezbon, Eri, and Arodi, and Areli.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And the sons of Asher; Jimnah, and Ishuah, and Isui, and Beriah, and Serah their sister: and the sons of Beriah; Heber, and Malchiel.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> These are the sons of Zilpah, whom Laban gave to Leah his daughter, and these she bare unto Jacob, even sixteen souls.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The sons of Rachel Jacob’s wife; Joseph, and Benjamin.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And unto Joseph in the land of Egypt were born Manasseh and Ephraim, which Asenath the daughter of Poti–pherah priest of On bare unto him.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And the sons of Benjamin were Belah, and Becher, and Ashbel, Gera, and Naaman, Ehi, and Rosh, Muppim, and Huppim, and Ard.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> These are the sons of Rachel, which were born to Jacob: all the souls were fourteen.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the sons of Dan; Hushim.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the sons of Naphtali; Jahzeel, and Guni, and Jezer, and Shillem.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> These are the sons of Bilhah, which Laban gave unto Rachel his daughter, and she bare these unto Jacob: all the souls were seven.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> All the souls that came with Jacob into Egypt, which came out of his loins, besides Jacob’s sons’ wives, all the souls were threescore and six;

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And the sons of Joseph, which were born him in Egypt, were two souls: all the souls of the house of Jacob, which came into Egypt, were threescore and ten.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And he sent Judah before him unto Joseph, to direct his face unto Goshen; and they came into the land of Goshen.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And Joseph made ready his chariot, and went up to meet Israel his father, to Goshen, and presented himself unto him; and he fell on his neck, and wept on his neck a good while.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Israel said unto Joseph, Now let me die, since I have seen thy face, because thou art yet alive.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And Joseph said unto his brethren, and unto his father’s house, I will go up, and shew Pharaoh, and say unto him, My brethren, and my father’s house, which were in the land of Canaan, are come unto me;

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And the men are shepherds, for their trade hath been to feed cattle; and they have brought their flocks, and their herds, and all that they have.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And it shall come to pass, when Pharaoh shall call you, and shall say, What is your occupation?

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> That ye shall say, Thy servants’ trade hath been about cattle from our youth even until now, both we, and also our fathers: that ye may dwell in the land of Goshen; for every shepherd is an abomination unto the Egyptians.
