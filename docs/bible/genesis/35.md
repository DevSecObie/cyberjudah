*Taught in [Genesis 32-35: Genesis 32: Wrestling Until the Blessing](/study/genesis/32-35)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And God said unto Jacob, Arise, go up to Beth–el, and dwell there: and make there an altar unto God, that appeared unto thee when thou fleddest from the face of Esau thy brother.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then Jacob said unto his household, and to all that were with him, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean, and change your garments:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And let us arise, and go up to Beth–el; and I will make there an altar unto God, who answered me in the day of my distress, and was with me in the way which I went.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And they gave unto Jacob all the strange gods which were in their hand, and all their earrings which were in their ears; and Jacob hid them under the oak which was by Shechem.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And they journeyed: and the terror of God was upon the cities that were round about them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> So Jacob came to Luz, which is in the land of Canaan, that is, Beth–el, he and all the people that were with him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And he built there an altar, and called the place El–beth–el: because there God appeared unto him, when he fled from the face of his brother.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But Deborah Rebekah’s nurse died, and she was buried beneath Beth–el under an oak: and the name of it was called Allon–bachuth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And God appeared unto Jacob again, when he came out of Padan–aram, and blessed him.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And God said unto him, Thy name is Jacob: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name: and he called his name Israel.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And God said unto him, I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the land which I gave Abraham and Isaac, to thee I will give it, and to thy seed after thee will I give the land.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And God went up from him in the place where he talked with him.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Jacob set up a pillar in the place where he talked with him, even a pillar of stone: and he poured a drink offering thereon, and he poured oil thereon.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And Jacob called the name of the place where God spake with him, Beth–el.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And they journeyed from Beth–el; and there was but a little way to come to Ephrath: and Rachel travailed, and she had hard labour.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And it came to pass, when she was in hard labour, that the midwife said unto her, Fear not; thou shalt have this son also.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she died) that she called his name Ben–oni: but his father called him Benjamin.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And Rachel died, and was buried in the way to Ephrath, which is Beth–lehem.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Jacob set a pillar upon her grave: that is the pillar of Rachel’s grave unto this day.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Israel journeyed, and spread his tent beyond the tower of Edar.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And it came to pass, when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father’s concubine: and Israel heard it. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The sons of Leah; Reuben, Jacob’s firstborn, and Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Zebulun:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The sons of Rachel; Joseph, and Benjamin:

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And the sons of Bilhah, Rachel’s handmaid; Dan, and Naphtali:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And the sons of Zilpah, Leah’s handmaid; Gad, and Asher: these are the sons of Jacob, which were born to him in Padan–aram.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And Jacob came unto Isaac his father unto Mamre, unto the city of Arbah, which is Hebron, where Abraham and Isaac sojourned.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And the days of Isaac were an hundred and fourscore years.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And Isaac gave up the ghost, and died, and was gathered unto his people, being old and full of days: and his sons Esau and Jacob buried him.
