*Taught in [Genesis 48-50: Genesis 48: Ephraim and Manasseh Blessed as Sons of Israel](/study/genesis/48-50)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And Joseph fell upon his father’s face, and wept upon him, and kissed him.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And Joseph commanded his servants the physicians to embalm his father: and the physicians embalmed Israel.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And forty days were fulfilled for him; for so are fulfilled the days of those which are embalmed: and the Egyptians mourned for him threescore and ten days.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And when the days of his mourning were past, Joseph spake unto the house of Pharaoh, saying, If now I have found grace in your eyes, speak, I pray you, in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> My father made me swear, saying, Lo, I die: in my grave which I have digged for me in the land of Canaan, there shalt thou bury me. Now therefore let me go up, I pray thee, and bury my father, and I will come again.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And Pharaoh said, Go up, and bury thy father, according as he made thee swear.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And Joseph went up to bury his father: and with him went up all the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And all the house of Joseph, and his brethren, and his father’s house: only their little ones, and their flocks, and their herds, they left in the land of Goshen.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And there went up with him both chariots and horsemen: and it was a very great company.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And they came to the threshingfloor of Atad, which is beyond Jordan, and there they mourned with a great and very sore lamentation: and he made a mourning for his father seven days.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And when the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, saw the mourning in the floor of Atad, they said, This is a grievous mourning to the Egyptians: wherefore the name of it was called Abel–mizraim, which is beyond Jordan.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And his sons did unto him according as he commanded them:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For his sons carried him into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah, which Abraham bought with the field for a possession of a buryingplace of Ephron the Hittite, before Mamre.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Joseph returned into Egypt, he, and his brethren, and all that went up with him to bury his father, after he had buried his father.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And when Joseph’s brethren saw that their father was dead, they said, Joseph will peradventure hate us, and will certainly requite us all the evil which we did unto him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And they sent a messenger unto Joseph, saying, Thy father did command before he died, saying,

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> So shall ye say unto Joseph, Forgive, I pray thee now, the trespass of thy brethren, and their sin; for they did unto thee evil: and now, we pray thee, forgive the trespass of the servants of the God of thy father. And Joseph wept when they spake unto him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And his brethren also went and fell down before his face; and they said, Behold, we be thy servants.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And Joseph said unto them, Fear not: for am I in the place of God?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Now therefore fear ye not: I will nourish you, and your little ones. And he comforted them, and spake kindly unto them.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And Joseph dwelt in Egypt, he, and his father’s house: and Joseph lived an hundred and ten years.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Joseph saw Ephraim’s children of the third generation: the children also of Machir the son of Manasseh were brought up upon Joseph’s knees.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And Joseph said unto his brethren, I die: and God will surely visit you, and bring you out of this land unto the land which he sware to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And Joseph took an oath of the children of Israel, saying, God will surely visit you, and ye shall carry up my bones from hence.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> So Joseph died, being an hundred and ten years old: and they embalmed him, and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.
