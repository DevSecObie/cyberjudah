*Taught in [Genesis 48-50: Genesis 48: Ephraim and Manasseh Blessed as Sons of Israel](/study/genesis/48-50)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And it came to pass after these things, that one told Joseph, Behold, thy father is sick: and he took with him his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And one told Jacob, and said, Behold, thy son Joseph cometh unto thee: and Israel strengthened himself, and sat upon the bed.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And Jacob said unto Joseph, God Almighty appeared unto me at Luz in the land of Canaan, and blessed me,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And said unto me, Behold, I will make thee fruitful, and multiply thee, and I will make of thee a multitude of people; and will give this land to thy seed after thee for an everlasting possession.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And now thy two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, which were born unto thee in the land of Egypt before I came unto thee into Egypt, are mine; as Reuben and Simeon, they shall be mine.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And thy issue, which thou begettest after them, shall be thine, and shall be called after the name of their brethren in their inheritance.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And as for me, when I came from Padan, Rachel died by me in the land of Canaan in the way, when yet there was but a little way to come unto Ephrath: and I buried her there in the way of Ephrath; the same is Beth–lehem.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Israel beheld Joseph’s sons, and said, Who are these?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And Joseph said unto his father, They are my sons, whom God hath given me in this place. And he said, Bring them, I pray thee, unto me, and I will bless them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Now the eyes of Israel were dim for age, so that he could not see. And he brought them near unto him; and he kissed them, and embraced them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And Israel said unto Joseph, I had not thought to see thy face: and, lo, God hath shewed me also thy seed.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Joseph brought them out from between his knees, and he bowed himself with his face to the earth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Joseph took them both, Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel’s left hand, and Manasseh in his left hand toward Israel’s right hand, and brought them near unto him.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Israel stretched out his right hand, and laid it upon Ephraim’s head, who was the younger, and his left hand upon Manasseh’s head, guiding his hands wittingly; for Manasseh was the firstborn.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And he blessed Joseph, and said, God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac did walk, the God which fed me all my life long unto this day,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The Angel which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be named on them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and let them grow into a multitude in the midst of the earth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And when Joseph saw that his father laid his right hand upon the head of Ephraim, it displeased him: and he held up his father’s hand, to remove it from Ephraim’s head unto Manasseh’s head.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Joseph said unto his father, Not so, my father: for this is the firstborn; put thy right hand upon his head.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And his father refused, and said, I know it, my son, I know it: he also shall become a people, and he also shall be great: but truly his younger brother shall be greater than he, and his seed shall become a multitude of nations.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And he blessed them that day, saying, In thee shall Israel bless, saying, God make thee as Ephraim and as Manasseh: and he set Ephraim before Manasseh.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Israel said unto Joseph, Behold, I die: but God shall be with you, and bring you again unto the land of your fathers.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Moreover I have given to thee one portion above thy brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorite with my sword and with my bow.
