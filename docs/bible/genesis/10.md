*Taught in [Genesis 9-12: Genesis 9: The Laws Before Moses and the Covenant of the Bow](/study/genesis/9-12)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now these are the generations of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth: and unto them were sons born after the flood.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And the sons of Gomer; Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> By these were the isles of the Gentiles divided in their lands; every one after his tongue, after their families, in their nations.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And the sons of Ham; Cush, and Mizraim, and Phut, and Canaan.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabtah, and Raamah, and Sabtecha: and the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be a mighty one in the earth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He was a mighty hunter before the Lord: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Out of that land went forth Asshur, and builded Nineveh, and the city Rehoboth, and Calah,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Resen between Nineveh and Calah: the same is a great city.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Mizraim begat Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Pathrusim, and Casluhim, (out of whom came Philistim,) and Caphtorim.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And Canaan begat Sidon his firstborn, and Heth,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgasite,

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite: and afterward were the families of the Canaanites spread abroad.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And the border of the Canaanites was from Sidon, as thou comest to Gerar, unto Gaza; as thou goest, unto Sodom, and Gomorrah, and Admah, and Zeboim, even unto Lasha.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> These are the sons of Ham, after their families, after their tongues, in their countries, and in their nations.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Unto Shem also, the father of all the children of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder, even to him were children born.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The children of Shem; Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the children of Aram; Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Mash.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And Arphaxad begat Salah; and Salah begat Eber.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of one was Peleg; for in his days was the earth divided; and his brother’s name was Joktan.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And Hadoram, and Uzal, and Diklah,

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And Obal, and Abimael, and Sheba,

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab: all these were the sons of Joktan.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And their dwelling was from Mesha, as thou goest unto Sephar a mount of the east.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> These are the sons of Shem, after their families, after their tongues, in their lands, after their nations.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> These are the families of the sons of Noah, after their generations, in their nations: and by these were the nations divided in the earth after the flood.
