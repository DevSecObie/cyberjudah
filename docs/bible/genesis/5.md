*Taught in [Genesis 5-8: Genesis 5: The Book of the Generations of Adam](/study/genesis/5-8)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And Adam lived an hundred and thirty years, and begat a son in his own likeness, after his image; and called his name Seth:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And the days of Adam after he had begotten Seth were eight hundred years: and he begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And all the days that Adam lived were nine hundred and thirty years: and he died.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And Seth lived an hundred and five years, and begat Enos:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And Seth lived after he begat Enos eight hundred and seven years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And all the days of Seth were nine hundred and twelve years: and he died.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And Enos lived ninety years, and begat Cainan:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Enos lived after he begat Cainan eight hundred and fifteen years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And all the days of Enos were nine hundred and five years: and he died.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Cainan lived seventy years, and begat Mahalaleel:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Cainan lived after he begat Mahalaleel eight hundred and forty years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And all the days of Cainan were nine hundred and ten years: and he died.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And Mahalaleel lived sixty and five years, and begat Jared:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And Mahalaleel lived after he begat Jared eight hundred and thirty years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And all the days of Mahalaleel were eight hundred ninety and five years: and he died.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Jared lived an hundred sixty and two years, and he begat Enoch:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And Jared lived after he begat Enoch eight hundred years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And all the days of Jared were nine hundred sixty and two years: and he died.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Enoch lived sixty and five years, and begat Methuselah:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And Enoch walked with God after he begat Methuselah three hundred years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And all the days of Enoch were three hundred sixty and five years:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And Methuselah lived an hundred eighty and seven years, and begat Lamech:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And Methuselah lived after he begat Lamech seven hundred eighty and two years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And all the days of Methuselah were nine hundred sixty and nine years: and he died.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And Lamech lived an hundred eighty and two years, and begat a son:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And he called his name Noah, saying, This same shall comfort us concerning our work and toil of our hands, because of the ground which the Lord hath cursed.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Lamech lived after he begat Noah five hundred ninety and five years, and begat sons and daughters:

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And all the days of Lamech were seven hundred seventy and seven years: and he died.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And Noah was five hundred years old: and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
