*Taught in [Genesis 1-4: Genesis 1: The Creation of Light and Mankind](/study/genesis/1-4)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived, and bare Cain, and said, I have gotten a man from the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And she again bare his brother Abel. And Abel was a keeper of sheep, but Cain was a tiller of the ground.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And in process of time it came to pass, that Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the Lord.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And Abel, he also brought of the firstlings of his flock and of the fat thereof. And the Lord had respect unto Abel and to his offering:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect. And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And the Lord said unto Cain, Why art thou wroth? and why is thy countenance fallen?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted? and if thou doest not well, sin lieth at the door. And unto thee shall be his desire, and thou shalt rule over him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Cain talked with Abel his brother: and it came to pass, when they were in the field, that Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and slew him.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And the Lord said unto Cain, Where is Abel thy brother? And he said, I know not: Am I my brother’s keeper?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And he said, What hast thou done? the voice of thy brother’s blood crieth unto me from the ground.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And now art thou cursed from the earth, which hath opened her mouth to receive thy brother’s blood from thy hand;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> When thou tillest the ground, it shall not henceforth yield unto thee her strength; a fugitive and a vagabond shalt thou be in the earth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Cain said unto the Lord, My punishment is greater than I can bear.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Behold, thou hast driven me out this day from the face of the earth; and from thy face shall I be hid; and I shall be a fugitive and a vagabond in the earth; and it shall come to pass, that every one that findeth me shall slay me.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And the Lord said unto him, Therefore whosoever slayeth Cain, vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold. And the Lord set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And Cain went out from the presence of the Lord, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived, and bare Enoch: and he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And unto Enoch was born Irad: and Irad begat Mehujael: and Mehujael begat Methusael: and Methusael begat Lamech.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Adah bare Jabal: he was the father of such as dwell in tents, and of such as have cattle.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And his brother’s name was Jubal: he was the father of all such as handle the harp and organ.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And Zillah, she also bare Tubal–cain, an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron: and the sister of Tubal–cain was Naamah.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamech, hearken unto my speech: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And Adam knew his wife again; and she bare a son, and called his name Seth: For God, said she, hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos: then began men to call upon the name of the Lord.
