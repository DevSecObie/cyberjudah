*Taught in [Genesis 24-27: Genesis 24: A Wife From the Kindred](/study/genesis/24-27)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then again Abraham took a wife, and her name was Keturah.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And she bare him Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And Jokshan begat Sheba, and Dedan. And the sons of Dedan were Asshurim, and Letushim, and Leummim.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And the sons of Midian; Ephah, and Epher, and Hanoch, and Abida, and Eldaah. All these were the children of Keturah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Abraham gave all that he had unto Isaac.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But unto the sons of the concubines, which Abraham had, Abraham gave gifts, and sent them away from Isaac his son, while he yet lived, eastward, unto the east country.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And these are the days of the years of Abraham’s life which he lived, an hundred threescore and fifteen years.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Then Abraham gave up the ghost, and died in a good old age, an old man, and full of years; and was gathered to his people.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And his sons Isaac and Ishmael buried him in the cave of Machpelah, in the field of Ephron the son of Zohar the Hittite, which is before Mamre;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The field which Abraham purchased of the sons of Heth: there was Abraham buried, and Sarah his wife.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And it came to pass after the death of Abraham, that God blessed his son Isaac; and Isaac dwelt by the well Lahai–roi.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Now these are the generations of Ishmael, Abraham’s son, whom Hagar the Egyptian, Sarah’s handmaid, bare unto Abraham:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And these are the names of the sons of Ishmael, by their names, according to their generations: the firstborn of Ishmael, Nebajoth; and Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Mishma, and Dumah, and Massa,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Hadar, and Tema, Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> These are the sons of Ishmael, and these are their names, by their towns, and by their castles; twelve princes according to their nations.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And these are the years of the life of Ishmael, an hundred and thirty and seven years: and he gave up the ghost and died; and was gathered unto his people.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And they dwelt from Havilah unto Shur, that is before Egypt, as thou goest toward Assyria: and he died in the presence of all his brethren.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And these are the generations of Isaac, Abraham’s son: Abraham begat Isaac:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Isaac was forty years old when he took Rebekah to wife, the daughter of Bethuel the Syrian of Padan–aram, the sister to Laban the Syrian.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Isaac intreated the Lord for his wife, because she was barren: and the Lord was intreated of him, and Rebekah his wife conceived.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And the children struggled together within her; and she said, If it be so, why am I thus? And she went to enquire of the Lord.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the Lord said unto her, Two nations are in thy womb, and two manner of people shall be separated from thy bowels; and the one people shall be stronger than the other people; and the elder shall serve the younger.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And when her days to be delivered were fulfilled, behold, there were twins in her womb.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And the first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name Esau.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And after that came his brother out, and his hand took hold on Esau’s heel; and his name was called Jacob: and Isaac was threescore years old when she bare them.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And the boys grew: and Esau was a cunning hunter, a man of the field; and Jacob was a plain man, dwelling in tents.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And Isaac loved Esau, because he did eat of his venison: but Rebekah loved Jacob.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And Jacob sod pottage: and Esau came from the field, and he was faint:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Esau said to Jacob, Feed me, I pray thee, with that same red pottage; for I am faint: therefore was his name called Edom.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And Jacob said, Sell me this day thy birthright.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And Esau said, Behold, I am at the point to die: and what profit shall this birthright do to me?

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And Jacob said, Swear to me this day; and he sware unto him: and he sold his birthright unto Jacob.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Then Jacob gave Esau bread and pottage of lentiles; and he did eat and drink, and rose up, and went his way: thus Esau despised his birthright.
