*Taught in [Genesis 48-50: Genesis 48: Ephraim and Manasseh Blessed as Sons of Israel](/study/genesis/48-50)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And Jacob called unto his sons, and said, Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which shall befall you in the last days.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Gather yourselves together, and hear, ye sons of Jacob; and hearken unto Israel your father.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Reuben, thou art my firstborn, my might, and the beginning of my strength, the excellency of dignity, and the excellency of power:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Unstable as water, thou shalt not excel; because thou wentest up to thy father’s bed; then defiledst thou it: he went up to my couch.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Simeon and Levi are brethren; instruments of cruelty are in their habitations.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> O my soul, come not thou into their secret; unto their assembly, mine honour, be not thou united: for in their anger they slew a man, and in their selfwill they digged down a wall.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Cursed be their anger, for it was fierce; and their wrath, for it was cruel: I will divide them in Jacob, and scatter them in Israel.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Judah, thou art he whom thy brethren shall praise: thy hand shall be in the neck of thine enemies; thy father’s children shall bow down before thee.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Judah is a lion’s whelp: from the prey, my son, thou art gone up: he stooped down, he couched as a lion, and as an old lion; who shall rouse him up?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Binding his foal unto the vine, and his ass’s colt unto the choice vine; he washed his garments in wine, and his clothes in the blood of grapes:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> His eyes shall be red with wine, and his teeth white with milk.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea; and he shall be for an haven of ships; and his border shall be unto Zidon.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Issachar is a strong ass couching down between two burdens:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And he saw that rest was good, and the land that it was pleasant; and bowed his shoulder to bear, and became a servant unto tribute.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Dan shall judge his people, as one of the tribes of Israel.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder in the path, that biteth the horse heels, so that his rider shall fall backward.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I have waited for thy salvation, O Lord.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Gad, a troop shall overcome him: but he shall overcome at the last.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Out of Asher his bread shall be fat, and he shall yield royal dainties.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Naphtali is a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Joseph is a fruitful bough, even a fruitful bough by a well; whose branches run over the wall:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The archers have sorely grieved him, and shot at him, and hated him:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But his bow abode in strength, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob; (from thence is the shepherd, the stone of Israel:)

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Even by the God of thy father, who shall help thee; and by the Almighty, who shall bless thee with blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lieth under, blessings of the breasts, and of the womb:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The blessings of thy father have prevailed above the blessings of my progenitors unto the utmost bound of the everlasting hills: they shall be on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of him that was separate from his brethren.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Benjamin shall ravin as a wolf: in the morning he shall devour the prey, and at night he shall divide the spoil.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> All these are the twelve tribes of Israel: and this is it that their father spake unto them, and blessed them; every one according to his blessing he blessed them.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And he charged them, and said unto them, I am to be gathered unto my people: bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> In the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, which is before Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field of Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a buryingplace.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> The purchase of the field and of the cave that is therein was from the children of Heth.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And when Jacob had made an end of commanding his sons, he gathered up his feet into the bed, and yielded up the ghost, and was gathered unto his people.
