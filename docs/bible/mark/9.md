<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And he said unto them, Verily I say unto you, That there be some of them that stand here, which shall not taste of death, till they have seen the kingdom of God come with power.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And after six days Jesus taketh with him Peter, and James, and John, and leadeth them up into an high mountain apart by themselves: and he was transfigured before them.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And his raiment became shining, exceeding white as snow; so as no fuller on earth can white them.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And there appeared unto them Elias with Moses: and they were talking with Jesus.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Peter answered and said to Jesus, Master, it is good for us to be here: and let us make three tabernacles; one for thee, and one for Moses, and one for Elias.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For he wist not what to say; for they were sore afraid.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And there was a cloud that overshadowed them: and a voice came out of the cloud, saying, This is my beloved Son: hear him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And suddenly, when they had looked round about, they saw no man any more, save Jesus only with themselves.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And as they came down from the mountain, he charged them that they should tell no man what things they had seen, till the Son of man were risen from the dead.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And they kept that saying with themselves, questioning one with another what the rising from the dead should mean.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And they asked him, saying, Why say the scribes that Elias must first come?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And he answered and told them, Elias verily cometh first, and restoreth all things; and how it is written of the Son of man, that he must suffer many things, and be set at nought.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But I say unto you, That Elias is indeed come, and they have done unto him whatsoever they listed, as it is written of him.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And when he came to his disciples, he saw a great multitude about them, and the scribes questioning with them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And straightway all the people, when they beheld him, were greatly amazed, and running to him saluted him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And he asked the scribes, What question ye with them?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And one of the multitude answered and said, Master, I have brought unto thee my son, which hath a dumb spirit;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And wheresoever he taketh him, he teareth him: and he foameth, and gnasheth with his teeth, and pineth away: and I spake to thy disciples that they should cast him out; and they could not.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He answereth him, and saith, O faithless generation, how long shall I be with you? how long shall I suffer you? bring him unto me.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And they brought him unto him: and when he saw him, straightway the spirit tare him; and he fell on the ground, and wallowed foaming.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And he asked his father, How long is it ago since this came unto him? And he said, Of a child.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And ofttimes it hath cast him into the fire, and into the waters, to destroy him: but if thou canst do any thing, have compassion on us, and help us.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Jesus said unto him, If thou canst believe, all things are possible to him that believeth.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And straightway the father of the child cried out, and said with tears, Lord, I believe; help thou mine unbelief.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> When Jesus saw that the people came running together, he rebuked the foul spirit, saying unto him, Thou dumb and deaf spirit, I charge thee, come out of him, and enter no more into him.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And the spirit cried, and rent him sore, and came out of him: and he was as one dead; insomuch that many said, He is dead.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> But Jesus took him by the hand, and lifted him up; and he arose.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And when he was come into the house, his disciples asked him privately, Why could not we cast him out?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And he said unto them, This kind can come forth by nothing, but by prayer and fasting.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And they departed thence, and passed through Galilee; and he would not that any man should know it.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> For he taught his disciples, and said unto them, The Son of man is delivered into the hands of men, and they shall kill him; and after that he is killed, he shall rise the third day.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> But they understood not that saying, and were afraid to ask him.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And he came to Capernaum: and being in the house he asked them, What was it that ye disputed among yourselves by the way?

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> But they held their peace: for by the way they had disputed among themselves, who should be the greatest.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And he sat down, and called the twelve, and saith unto them, If any man desire to be first, the same shall be last of all, and servant of all.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And he took a child, and set him in the midst of them: and when he had taken him in his arms, he said unto them,

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Whosoever shall receive one of such children in my name, receiveth me: and whosoever shall receive me, receiveth not me, but him that sent me.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And John answered him, saying, Master, we saw one casting out devils in thy name, and he followeth not us: and we forbad him, because he followeth not us.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> But Jesus said, Forbid him not: for there is no man which shall do a miracle in my name, that can lightly speak evil of me.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> For he that is not against us is on our part.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> For whosoever shall give you a cup of water to drink in my name, because ye belong to Christ, verily I say unto you, he shall not lose his reward.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> And whosoever shall offend one of these little ones that believe in me, it is better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and he were cast into the sea.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And if thy hand offend thee, cut it off: it is better for thee to enter into life maimed, than having two hands to go into hell, into the fire that never shall be quenched:

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> Where their worm dieth not, and the fire is not quenched.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And if thy foot offend thee, cut it off: it is better for thee to enter halt into life, than having two feet to be cast into hell, into the fire that never shall be quenched:

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> Where their worm dieth not, and the fire is not quenched.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And if thine eye offend thee, pluck it out: it is better for thee to enter into the kingdom of God with one eye, than having two eyes to be cast into hell fire:

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> Where their worm dieth not, and the fire is not quenched.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> For every one shall be salted with fire, and every sacrifice shall be salted with salt.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> Salt is good: but if the salt have lost his saltness, wherewith will ye season it? Have salt in yourselves, and have peace one with another.
