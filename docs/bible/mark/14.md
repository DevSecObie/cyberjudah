<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> After two days was the feast of the passover, and of unleavened bread: and the chief priests and the scribes sought how they might take him by craft, and put him to death.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> But they said, Not on the feast day, lest there be an uproar of the people.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And being in Bethany in the house of Simon the leper, as he sat at meat, there came a woman having an alabaster box of ointment of spikenard very precious; and she brake the box, and poured it on his head.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And there were some that had indignation within themselves, and said, Why was this waste of the ointment made?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For it might have been sold for more than three hundred pence, and have been given to the poor. And they murmured against her.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And Jesus said, Let her alone; why trouble ye her? she hath wrought a good work on me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For ye have the poor with you always, and whensoever ye will ye may do them good: but me ye have not always.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> She hath done what she could: she is come aforehand to anoint my body to the burying.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Verily I say unto you, Wheresoever this gospel shall be preached throughout the whole world, this also that she hath done shall be spoken of for a memorial of her.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And Judas Iscariot, one of the twelve, went unto the chief priests, to betray him unto them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And when they heard it, they were glad, and promised to give him money. And he sought how he might conveniently betray him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the first day of unleavened bread, when they killed the passover, his disciples said unto him, Where wilt thou that we go and prepare that thou mayest eat the passover?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And he sendeth forth two of his disciples, and saith unto them, Go ye into the city, and there shall meet you a man bearing a pitcher of water: follow him.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And wheresoever he shall go in, say ye to the goodman of the house, The Master saith, Where is the guestchamber, where I shall eat the passover with my disciples?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And he will shew you a large upper room furnished and prepared: there make ready for us.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And his disciples went forth, and came into the city, and found as he had said unto them: and they made ready the passover.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And in the evening he cometh with the twelve.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And as they sat and did eat, Jesus said, Verily I say unto you, One of you which eateth with me shall betray me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And they began to be sorrowful, and to say unto him one by one, Is it I? and another said, Is it I?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And he answered and said unto them, It is one of the twelve, that dippeth with me in the dish.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The Son of man indeed goeth, as it is written of him: but woe to that man by whom the Son of man is betrayed! good were it for that man if he had never been born.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And as they did eat, Jesus took bread, and blessed, and brake it, and gave to them, and said, Take, eat: this is my body.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And he took the cup, and when he had given thanks, he gave it to them: and they all drank of it.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And he said unto them, This is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Verily I say unto you, I will drink no more of the fruit of the vine, until that day that I drink it new in the kingdom of God.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And when they had sung an hymn, they went out into the mount of Olives.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And Jesus saith unto them, All ye shall be offended because of me this night: for it is written, I will smite the shepherd, and the sheep shall be scattered.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> But after that I am risen, I will go before you into Galilee.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But Peter said unto him, Although all shall be offended, yet will not I.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Jesus saith unto him, Verily I say unto thee, That this day, even in this night, before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> But he spake the more vehemently, If I should die with thee, I will not deny thee in any wise. Likewise also said they all.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And they came to a place which was named Gethsemane: and he saith to his disciples, Sit ye here, while I shall pray.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And he taketh with him Peter and James and John, and began to be sore amazed, and to be very heavy;

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And saith unto them, My soul is exceeding sorrowful unto death: tarry ye here, and watch.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And he went forward a little, and fell on the ground, and prayed that, if it were possible, the hour might pass from him.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And he said, Abba, Father, all things are possible unto thee; take away this cup from me: nevertheless not what I will, but what thou wilt.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> And he cometh, and findeth them sleeping, and saith unto Peter, Simon, sleepest thou? couldest not thou watch one hour?

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Watch ye and pray, lest ye enter into temptation. The spirit truly is ready, but the flesh is weak.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And again he went away, and prayed, and spake the same words.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And when he returned, he found them asleep again, (for their eyes were heavy,) neither wist they what to answer him.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And he cometh the third time, and saith unto them, Sleep on now, and take your rest: it is enough, the hour is come; behold, the Son of man is betrayed into the hands of sinners.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> Rise up, let us go; lo, he that betrayeth me is at hand.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And immediately, while he yet spake, cometh Judas, one of the twelve, and with him a great multitude with swords and staves, from the chief priests and the scribes and the elders.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And he that betrayed him had given them a token, saying, Whomsoever I shall kiss, that same is he; take him, and lead him away safely.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And as soon as he was come, he goeth straightway to him, and saith, Master, master; and kissed him.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And they laid their hands on him, and took him.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And one of them that stood by drew a sword, and smote a servant of the high priest, and cut off his ear.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Are ye come out, as against a thief, with swords and with staves to take me?

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> I was daily with you in the temple teaching, and ye took me not: but the scriptures must be fulfilled.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> And they all forsook him, and fled.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> And there followed him a certain young man, having a linen cloth cast about his naked body; and the young men laid hold on him:

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> And he left the linen cloth, and fled from them naked.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> And they led Jesus away to the high priest: and with him were assembled all the chief priests and the elders and the scribes.

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> And Peter followed him afar off, even into the palace of the high priest: and he sat with the servants, and warmed himself at the fire.

<a id="v55"></a><sup class="vn">55</sup> And the chief priests and all the council sought for witness against Jesus to put him to death; and found none.

<a id="v56"></a><sup class="vn">56</sup> For many bare false witness against him, but their witness agreed not together.

<a id="v57"></a><sup class="vn">57</sup> And there arose certain, and bare false witness against him, saying,

<a id="v58"></a><sup class="vn">58</sup> We heard him say, I will destroy this temple that is made with hands, and within three days I will build another made without hands.

<a id="v59"></a><sup class="vn">59</sup> But neither so did their witness agree together.

<a id="v60"></a><sup class="vn">60</sup> And the high priest stood up in the midst, and asked Jesus, saying, Answerest thou nothing? what is it which these witness against thee?

<a id="v61"></a><sup class="vn">61</sup> But he held his peace, and answered nothing. Again the high priest asked him, and said unto him, Art thou the Christ, the Son of the Blessed?

<a id="v62"></a><sup class="vn">62</sup> And Jesus said, I am: and ye shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven.

<a id="v63"></a><sup class="vn">63</sup> Then the high priest rent his clothes, and saith, What need we any further witnesses?

<a id="v64"></a><sup class="vn">64</sup> Ye have heard the blasphemy: what think ye? And they all condemned him to be guilty of death.

<a id="v65"></a><sup class="vn">65</sup> And some began to spit on him, and to cover his face, and to buffet him, and to say unto him, Prophesy: and the servants did strike him with the palms of their hands.

<a id="v66"></a><sup class="vn">66</sup> And as Peter was beneath in the palace, there cometh one of the maids of the high priest:

<a id="v67"></a><sup class="vn">67</sup> And when she saw Peter warming himself, she looked upon him, and said, And thou also wast with Jesus of Nazareth.

<a id="v68"></a><sup class="vn">68</sup> But he denied, saying, I know not, neither understand I what thou sayest. And he went out into the porch; and the cock crew.

<a id="v69"></a><sup class="vn">69</sup> And a maid saw him again, and began to say to them that stood by, This is one of them.

<a id="v70"></a><sup class="vn">70</sup> And he denied it again. And a little after, they that stood by said again to Peter, Surely thou art one of them: for thou art a Galilean, and thy speech agreeth thereto.

<a id="v71"></a><sup class="vn">71</sup> But he began to curse and to swear, saying, I know not this man of whom ye speak.

<a id="v72"></a><sup class="vn">72</sup> And the second time the cock crew. And Peter called to mind the word that Jesus said unto him, Before the cock crow twice, thou shalt deny me thrice. And when he thought thereon, he wept.
