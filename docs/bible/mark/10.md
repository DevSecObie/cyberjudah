<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And he arose from thence, and cometh into the coasts of Judea by the farther side of Jordan: and the people resort unto him again; and, as he was wont, he taught them again.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And the Pharisees came to him, and asked him, Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife? tempting him.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And he answered and said unto them, What did Moses command you?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And they said, Moses suffered to write a bill of divorcement, and to put her away.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, For the hardness of your heart he wrote you this precept.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But from the beginning of the creation God made them male and female.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and cleave to his wife;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And they twain shall be one flesh: so then they are no more twain, but one flesh.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And in the house his disciples asked him again of the same matter.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And he saith unto them, Whosoever shall put away his wife, and marry another, committeth adultery against her.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And if a woman shall put away her husband, and be married to another, she committeth adultery.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And they brought young children to him, that he should touch them: and his disciples rebuked those that brought them.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But when Jesus saw it, he was much displeased, and said unto them, Suffer the little children to come unto me, and forbid them not: for of such is the kingdom of God.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Verily I say unto you, Whosoever shall not receive the kingdom of God as a little child, he shall not enter therein.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And he took them up in his arms, put his hands upon them, and blessed them.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And when he was gone forth into the way, there came one running, and kneeled to him, and asked him, Good Master, what shall I do that I may inherit eternal life?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Jesus said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thou knowest the commandments, Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Defraud not, Honour thy father and mother.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And he answered and said unto him, Master, all these have I observed from my youth.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Then Jesus beholding him loved him, and said unto him, One thing thou lackest: go thy way, sell whatsoever thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven: and come, take up the cross, and follow me.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And he was sad at that saying, and went away grieved: for he had great possessions.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And Jesus looked round about, and saith unto his disciples, How hardly shall they that have riches enter into the kingdom of God!

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the disciples were astonished at his words. But Jesus answereth again, and saith unto them, Children, how hard is it for them that trust in riches to enter into the kingdom of God!

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And they were astonished out of measure, saying among themselves, Who then can be saved?

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And Jesus looking upon them saith, With men it is impossible, but not with God: for with God all things are possible.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Then Peter began to say unto him, Lo, we have left all, and have followed thee.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And Jesus answered and said, Verily I say unto you, There is no man that hath left house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my sake, and the gospel’s,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> But he shall receive an hundredfold now in this time, houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and lands, with persecutions; and in the world to come eternal life.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> But many that are first shall be last; and the last first.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And they were in the way going up to Jerusalem; and Jesus went before them: and they were amazed; and as they followed, they were afraid. And he took again the twelve, and began to tell them what things should happen unto him,

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Saying, Behold, we go up to Jerusalem; and the Son of man shall be delivered unto the chief priests, and unto the scribes; and they shall condemn him to death, and shall deliver him to the Gentiles:

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And they shall mock him, and shall scourge him, and shall spit upon him, and shall kill him: and the third day he shall rise again.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And James and John, the sons of Zebedee, come unto him, saying, Master, we would that thou shouldest do for us whatsoever we shall desire.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And he said unto them, What would ye that I should do for you?

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> They said unto him, Grant unto us that we may sit, one on thy right hand, and the other on thy left hand, in thy glory.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> But Jesus said unto them, Ye know not what ye ask: can ye drink of the cup that I drink of? and be baptized with the baptism that I am baptized with?

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And they said unto him, We can. And Jesus said unto them, Ye shall indeed drink of the cup that I drink of; and with the baptism that I am baptized withal shall ye be baptized:

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> But to sit on my right hand and on my left hand is not mine to give; but it shall be given to them for whom it is prepared.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And when the ten heard it, they began to be much displeased with James and John.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> But Jesus called them to him, and saith unto them, Ye know that they which are accounted to rule over the Gentiles exercise lordship over them; and their great ones exercise authority upon them.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> But so shall it not be among you: but whosoever will be great among you, shall be your minister:

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And whosoever of you will be the chiefest, shall be servant of all.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And they came to Jericho: and as he went out of Jericho with his disciples and a great number of people, blind Bartimeus, the son of Timeus, sat by the highway side begging.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And when he heard that it was Jesus of Nazareth, he began to cry out, and say, Jesus, thou Son of David, have mercy on me.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> And many charged him that he should hold his peace: but he cried the more a great deal, Thou Son of David, have mercy on me.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> And Jesus stood still, and commanded him to be called. And they call the blind man, saying unto him, Be of good comfort, rise; he calleth thee.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> And he, casting away his garment, rose, and came to Jesus.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto him, What wilt thou that I should do unto thee? The blind man said unto him, Lord, that I might receive my sight.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> And Jesus said unto him, Go thy way; thy faith hath made thee whole. And immediately he received his sight, and followed Jesus in the way.
