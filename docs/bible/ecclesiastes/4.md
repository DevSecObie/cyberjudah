<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> So I returned, and considered all the oppressions that are done under the sun: and behold the tears of such as were oppressed, and they had no comforter; and on the side of their oppressors there was power; but they had no comforter.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Wherefore I praised the dead which are already dead more than the living which are yet alive.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Yea, better is he than both they, which hath not yet been, who hath not seen the evil work that is done under the sun.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Again, I considered all travail, and every right work, that for this a man is envied of his neighbour. This is also vanity and vexation of spirit.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The fool foldeth his hands together, and eateth his own flesh.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Better is an handful with quietness, than both the hands full with travail and vexation of spirit.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Then I returned, and I saw vanity under the sun.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> There is one alone, and there is not a second; yea, he hath neither child nor brother: yet is there no end of all his labour; neither is his eye satisfied with riches; neither saith he, For whom do I labour, and bereave my soul of good? This is also vanity, yea, it is a sore travail.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Again, if two lie together, then they have heat: but how can one be warm alone?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And if one prevail against him, two shall withstand him; and a threefold cord is not quickly broken.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Better is a poor and a wise child than an old and foolish king, who will no more be admonished.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For out of prison he cometh to reign; whereas also he that is born in his kingdom becometh poor.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I considered all the living which walk under the sun, with the second child that shall stand up in his stead.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> There is no end of all the people, even of all that have been before them: they also that come after shall not rejoice in him. Surely this also is vanity and vexation of spirit.
