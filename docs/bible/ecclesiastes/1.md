<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The words of the Preacher, the son of David, king in Jerusalem.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Vanity of vanities, saith the Preacher, vanity of vanities; all is vanity.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> What profit hath a man of all his labour which he taketh under the sun?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> One generation passeth away, and another generation cometh: but the earth abideth for ever.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The sun also ariseth, and the sun goeth down, and hasteth to his place where he arose.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The wind goeth toward the south, and turneth about unto the north; it whirleth about continually, and the wind returneth again according to his circuits.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> All the rivers run into the sea; yet the sea is not full; unto the place from whence the rivers come, thither they return again.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> All things are full of labour; man cannot utter it: the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The thing that hath been, it is that which shall be; and that which is done is that which shall be done: and there is no new thing under the sun.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Is there any thing whereof it may be said, See, this is new? it hath been already of old time, which was before us.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> There is no remembrance of former things; neither shall there be any remembrance of things that are to come with those that shall come after.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I the Preacher was king over Israel in Jerusalem.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And I gave my heart to seek and search out by wisdom concerning all things that are done under heaven: this sore travail hath God given to the sons of man to be exercised therewith.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I have seen all the works that are done under the sun; and, behold, all is vanity and vexation of spirit.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> That which is crooked cannot be made straight: and that which is wanting cannot be numbered.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I communed with mine own heart, saying, Lo, I am come to great estate, and have gotten more wisdom than all they that have been before me in Jerusalem: yea, my heart had great experience of wisdom and knowledge.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And I gave my heart to know wisdom, and to know madness and folly: I perceived that this also is vexation of spirit.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For in much wisdom is much grief: and he that increaseth knowledge increaseth sorrow.
