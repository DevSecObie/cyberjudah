<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> To every thing there is a season, and a time to every purpose under the heaven:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> A time to be born, and a time to die; a time to plant, and a time to pluck up that which is planted;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> A time to kill, and a time to heal; a time to break down, and a time to build up;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> A time to weep, and a time to laugh; a time to mourn, and a time to dance;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A time to cast away stones, and a time to gather stones together; a time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> A time to get, and a time to lose; a time to keep, and a time to cast away;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> A time to rend, and a time to sew; a time to keep silence, and a time to speak;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> A time to love, and a time to hate; a time of war, and a time of peace.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> What profit hath he that worketh in that wherein he laboureth?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I have seen the travail, which God hath given to the sons of men to be exercised in it.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He hath made every thing beautiful in his time: also he hath set the world in their heart, so that no man can find out the work that God maketh from the beginning to the end.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I know that there is no good in them, but for a man to rejoice, and to do good in his life.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And also that every man should eat and drink, and enjoy the good of all his labour, it is the gift of God.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I know that, whatsoever God doeth, it shall be for ever: nothing can be put to it, nor any thing taken from it: and God doeth it, that men should fear before him.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> That which hath been is now; and that which is to be hath already been; and God requireth that which is past.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And moreover I saw under the sun the place of judgment, that wickedness was there; and the place of righteousness, that iniquity was there.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I said in mine heart, God shall judge the righteous and the wicked: for there is a time there for every purpose and for every work.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I said in mine heart concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other; yea, they have all one breath; so that a man hath no preeminence above a beast: for all is vanity.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> All go unto one place; all are of the dust, and all turn to dust again.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Who knoweth the spirit of man that goeth upward, and the spirit of the beast that goeth downward to the earth?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Wherefore I perceive that there is nothing better, than that a man should rejoice in his own works; for that is his portion: for who shall bring him to see what shall be after him?
