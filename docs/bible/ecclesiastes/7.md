<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> A good name is better than precious ointment; and the day of death than the day of one’s birth.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> It is better to go to the house of mourning, than to go to the house of feasting: for that is the end of all men; and the living will lay it to his heart.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Sorrow is better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise, than for a man to hear the song of fools.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For as the crackling of thorns under a pot, so is the laughter of the fool: this also is vanity.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Surely oppression maketh a wise man mad; and a gift destroyeth the heart.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Better is the end of a thing than the beginning thereof: and the patient in spirit is better than the proud in spirit.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Say not thou, What is the cause that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not enquire wisely concerning this.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wisdom is good with an inheritance: and by it there is profit to them that see the sun.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For wisdom is a defence, and money is a defence: but the excellency of knowledge is, that wisdom giveth life to them that have it.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Consider the work of God: for who can make that straight, which he hath made crooked?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> In the day of prosperity be joyful, but in the day of adversity consider: God also hath set the one over against the other, to the end that man should find nothing after him.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> All things have I seen in the days of my vanity: there is a just man that perisheth in his righteousness, and there is a wicked man that prolongeth his life in his wickedness.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Be not righteous over much; neither make thyself over wise: why shouldest thou destroy thyself?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Be not over much wicked, neither be thou foolish: why shouldest thou die before thy time?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> It is good that thou shouldest take hold of this; yea, also from this withdraw not thine hand: for he that feareth God shall come forth of them all.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Wisdom strengtheneth the wise more than ten mighty men which are in the city.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For there is not a just man upon earth, that doeth good, and sinneth not.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Also take no heed unto all words that are spoken; lest thou hear thy servant curse thee:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For oftentimes also thine own heart knoweth that thou thyself likewise hast cursed others.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> All this have I proved by wisdom: I said, I will be wise; but it was far from me.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> That which is far off, and exceeding deep, who can find it out?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> I applied mine heart to know, and to search, and to seek out wisdom, and the reason of things, and to know the wickedness of folly, even of foolishness and madness:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And I find more bitter than death the woman, whose heart is snares and nets, and her hands as bands: whoso pleaseth God shall escape from her; but the sinner shall be taken by her.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Behold, this have I found, saith the preacher, counting one by one, to find out the account:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Which yet my soul seeketh, but I find not: one man among a thousand have I found; but a woman among all those have I not found.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Lo, this only have I found, that God hath made man upright; but they have sought out many inventions.
