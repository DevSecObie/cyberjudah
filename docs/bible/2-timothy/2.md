<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Thou therefore, my son, be strong in the grace that is in Christ Jesus.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And the things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life; that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And if a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, except he strive lawfully.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The husbandman that laboureth must be first partaker of the fruits.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Consider what I say; and the Lord give thee understanding in all things.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Remember that Jesus Christ of the seed of David was raised from the dead according to my gospel:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Wherein I suffer trouble, as an evil doer, even unto bonds; but the word of God is not bound.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Therefore I endure all things for the elect’s sakes, that they may also obtain the salvation which is in Christ Jesus with eternal glory.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> It is a faithful saying: For if we be dead with him, we shall also live with him:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If we suffer, we shall also reign with him: if we deny him, he also will deny us:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> If we believe not, yet he abideth faithful: he cannot deny himself.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Of these things put them in remembrance, charging them before the Lord that they strive not about words to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But shun profane and vain babblings: for they will increase unto more ungodliness.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And their word will eat as doth a canker: of whom is Hymeneus and Philetus;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Who concerning the truth have erred, saying that the resurrection is past already; and overthrow the faith of some.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Nevertheless the foundation of God standeth sure, having this seal, The Lord knoweth them that are his. And, Let every one that nameth the name of Christ depart from iniquity.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some to honour, and some to dishonour.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> If a man therefore purge himself from these, he shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified, and meet for the master’s use, and prepared unto every good work.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Flee also youthful lusts: but follow righteousness, faith, charity, peace, with them that call on the Lord out of a pure heart.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> But foolish and unlearned questions avoid, knowing that they do gender strifes.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And the servant of the Lord must not strive; but be gentle unto all men, apt to teach, patient,

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> In meekness instructing those that oppose themselves; if God peradventure will give them repentance to the acknowledging of the truth;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And that they may recover themselves out of the snare of the devil, who are taken captive by him at his will.
