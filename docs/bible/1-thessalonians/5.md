<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But of the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that I write unto you.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Ye are all the children of light, and the children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Therefore let us not sleep, as do others; but let us watch and be sober.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For they that sleep sleep in the night; and they that be drunken are drunken in the night.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But let us, who are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For God hath not appointed us to wrath, but to obtain salvation by our Lord Jesus Christ,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who died for us, that, whether we wake or sleep, we should live together with him.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And we beseech you, brethren, to know them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And to esteem them very highly in love for their work’s sake. And be at peace among yourselves.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Now we exhort you, brethren, warn them that are unruly, comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> See that none render evil for evil unto any man; but ever follow that which is good, both among yourselves, and to all men.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Rejoice evermore.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Pray without ceasing.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Quench not the Spirit.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Despise not prophesyings.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Abstain from all appearance of evil.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Faithful is he that calleth you, who also will do it.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Brethren, pray for us.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Greet all the brethren with an holy kiss.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> I charge you by the Lord that this epistle be read unto all the holy brethren.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen. The first epistle unto the Thessalonians was written from Athens.
