<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And they walked in the midst of the fire, praising God, and blessing the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then Azarias stood up, and prayed on this manner; and opening his mouth in the midst of the fire said,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: thy name is worthy to be praised and glorified for evermore:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done to us: yea, true are all thy works, thy ways are right, and all thy judgments truth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> In all the things that thou hast brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, even Jerusalem, thou hast executed true judgment: for according to truth and judgment didst thou bring all these things upon us because of our sins.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For we have sinned and committed iniquity, departing from thee.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In all things have we trespassed, and not obeyed thy commandments, nor kept them, neither done as thou hast commanded us, that it might go well with us.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Wherefore all that thou hast brought upon us, and every thing that thou hast done to us, thou hast done in true judgment.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And thou didst deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies, most hateful forsakers of God, and to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And now we cannot open our mouths, we are become a shame and reproach to thy servants; and to them that worship thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Yet deliver us not up wholly, for thy name’s sake, neither disannul thou thy covenant:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And cause not thy mercy to depart from us, for thy beloved Abraham’s sake, for thy servant Issac’s sake, and for thy holy Israel’s sake;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> To whom thou hast spoken and promised, that thou wouldest multiply their seed as the stars of heaven, and as the sand that lieth upon the seashore.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For we, O Lord, are become less than any nation, and be kept under this day in all the world because of our sins.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Neither is there at this time prince, or prophet, or leader, or burnt offering, or sacrifice, or oblation, or incense, or place to sacrifice before thee, and to find mercy.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Nevertheless in a contrite heart and an humble spirit let us be accepted.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Like as in the burnt offerings of rams and bullocks, and like as in ten thousands of fat lambs: so let our sacrifice be in thy sight this day, and grant that we may wholly go after thee: for they shall not be confounded that put their trust in thee.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And now we follow thee with all our heart, we fear thee, and seek thy face.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Put us not to shame: but deal with us after thy lovingkindness, and according to the multitude of thy mercies.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Deliver us also according to thy marvellous works, and give glory to thy name, O Lord: and let all them that do thy servants hurt be ashamed;

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And let them be confounded in all their power and might, and let their strength be broken;

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And let them know that thou art God, the only God, and glorious over the whole world.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the king’s servants, that put them in, ceased not to make the oven hot with rosin, pitch, tow, and small wood;

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> So that the flame streamed forth above the furnace forty and nine cubits.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And it passed through, and burned those Chaldeans it found about the furnace.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But the angel of the Lord came down into the oven together with Azarias and his fellows, and smote the flame of the fire out of the oven;

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And made the midst of the furnace as it had been a moist whistling wind, so that the fire touched them not at all, neither hurt nor troubled them.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Then the three, as out of one mouth, praised, glorified, and blessed, God in the furnace, saying,

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And blessed is thy glorious and holy name: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Blessed art thou in the temple of thine holy glory: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Blessed art thou that beholdest the depths, and sittest upon the cherubims: and to be praised and exalted above all for ever.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Blessed art thou on the glorious throne of thy kingdom: and to be praised and glorified above all for ever.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Blessed art thou in the firmament of heaven: and above all to be praised and glorified for ever.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> O all ye works of the Lord, bless ye the Lord : praise and exalt him above all for ever,

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> O ye heavens, bless ye the Lord : praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> O ye angels of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> O all ye waters that be above the heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> O all ye powers of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> O ye sun and moon, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> O ye stars of heaven, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> O every shower and dew, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> O all ye winds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever,

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> O ye fire and heat, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> O ye winter and summer, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> O ye dews and storms of snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> O ye nights and days, bless ye the Lord: bless and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> O ye light and darkness, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> O ye ice and cold, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> O ye frost and snow, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> O ye lightnings and clouds, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> O let the earth bless the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> O ye mountains and little hills, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> O all ye things that grow in the earth, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v55"></a><sup class="vn">55</sup> O ye mountains, bless ye the Lord: Praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v56"></a><sup class="vn">56</sup> O ye seas and rivers, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v57"></a><sup class="vn">57</sup> O ye whales, and all that move in the waters, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v58"></a><sup class="vn">58</sup> O all ye fowls of the air, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v59"></a><sup class="vn">59</sup> O all ye beasts and cattle, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v60"></a><sup class="vn">60</sup> O ye children of men, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v61"></a><sup class="vn">61</sup> O Israel, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v62"></a><sup class="vn">62</sup> O ye priests of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v63"></a><sup class="vn">63</sup> O ye servants of the Lord, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v64"></a><sup class="vn">64</sup> O ye spirits and souls of the righteous, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v65"></a><sup class="vn">65</sup> O ye holy and humble men of heart, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever.

<a id="v66"></a><sup class="vn">66</sup> O Ananias, Azarias, and Misael, bless ye the Lord: praise and exalt him above all for ever: far he hath delivered us from hell, and saved us from the hand of death, and delivered us out of the midst of the furnace and burning flame: even out of the midst of the fire hath he delivered us.

<a id="v67"></a><sup class="vn">67</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord, because he is gracious: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v68"></a><sup class="vn">68</sup> O all ye that worship the Lord, bless the God of gods, praise him, and give him thanks: for his mercy endureth for ever.
