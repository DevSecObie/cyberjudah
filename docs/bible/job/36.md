<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Elihu also proceeded, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Suffer me a little, and I will shew thee that I have yet to speak on God’s behalf.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For truly my words shall not be false: he that is perfect in knowledge is with thee.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Behold, God is mighty, and despiseth not any: he is mighty in strength and wisdom.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He preserveth not the life of the wicked: but giveth right to the poor.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous: but with kings are they on the throne; yea, he doth establish them for ever, and they are exalted.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And if they be bound in fetters, and be holden in cords of affliction;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then he sheweth them their work, and their transgressions that they have exceeded.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He openeth also their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> If they obey and serve him, they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But if they obey not, they shall perish by the sword, and they shall die without knowledge.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But the hypocrites in heart heap up wrath: they cry not when he bindeth them.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> They die in youth, and their life is among the unclean.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He delivereth the poor in his affliction, and openeth their ears in oppression.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Even so would he have removed thee out of the strait into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and that which should be set on thy table should be full of fatness.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> But thou hast fulfilled the judgment of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold on thee.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Because there is wrath, beware lest he take thee away with his stroke: then a great ransom cannot deliver thee.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Will he esteem thy riches? no, not gold, nor all the forces of strength.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Desire not the night, when people are cut off in their place.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Take heed, regard not iniquity: for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Behold, God exalteth by his power: who teacheth like him?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Who hath enjoined him his way? or who can say, Thou hast wrought iniquity?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Remember that thou magnify his work, which men behold.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Every man may see it; man may behold it afar off.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Behold, God is great, and we know him not, neither can the number of his years be searched out.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For he maketh small the drops of water: they pour down rain according to the vapour thereof:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Which the clouds do drop and distil upon man abundantly.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Also can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, or the noise of his tabernacle?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Behold, he spreadeth his light upon it, and covereth the bottom of the sea.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> For by them judgeth he the people; he giveth meat in abundance.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> With clouds he covereth the light; and commandeth it not to shine by the cloud that cometh betwixt.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> The noise thereof sheweth concerning it, the cattle also concerning the vapour.
