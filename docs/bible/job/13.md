<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lo, mine eye hath seen all this, mine ear hath heard and understood it.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> What ye know, the same do I know also: I am not inferior unto you.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Surely I would speak to the Almighty, and I desire to reason with God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But ye are forgers of lies, ye are all physicians of no value.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> O that ye would altogether hold your peace! and it should be your wisdom.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Hear now my reasoning, and hearken to the pleadings of my lips.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Will ye speak wickedly for God? and talk deceitfully for him?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Will ye accept his person? will ye contend for God?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Is it good that he should search you out? or as one man mocketh another, do ye so mock him?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He will surely reprove you, if ye do secretly accept persons.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Shall not his excellency make you afraid? and his dread fall upon you?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Your remembrances are like unto ashes, your bodies to bodies of clay.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Hold your peace, let me alone, that I may speak, and let come on me what will.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Wherefore do I take my flesh in my teeth, and put my life in mine hand?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Though he slay me, yet will I trust in him: but I will maintain mine own ways before him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He also shall be my salvation: for an hypocrite shall not come before him.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Hear diligently my speech, and my declaration with your ears.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Behold now, I have ordered my cause; I know that I shall be justified.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Who is he that will plead with me? for now, if I hold my tongue, I shall give up the ghost.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Only do not two things unto me: then will I not hide myself from thee.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Withdraw thine hand far from me: and let not thy dread make me afraid.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Then call thou, and I will answer: or let me speak, and answer thou me.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> How many are mine iniquities and sins? make me to know my transgression and my sin.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Wherefore hidest thou thy face, and holdest me for thine enemy?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Wilt thou break a leaf driven to and fro? and wilt thou pursue the dry stubble?

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For thou writest bitter things against me, and makest me to possess the iniquities of my youth.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Thou puttest my feet also in the stocks, and lookest narrowly unto all my paths; thou settest a print upon the heels of my feet.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And he, as a rotten thing, consumeth, as a garment that is moth eaten.
