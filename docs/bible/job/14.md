<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Man that is born of a woman is of few days, and full of trouble.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He cometh forth like a flower, and is cut down: he fleeth also as a shadow, and continueth not.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And dost thou open thine eyes upon such an one, and bringest me into judgment with thee?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Who can bring a clean thing out of an unclean? not one.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Seeing his days are determined, the number of his months are with thee, thou hast appointed his bounds that he cannot pass;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Turn from him, that he may rest, till he shall accomplish, as an hireling, his day.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For there is hope of a tree, if it be cut down, that it will sprout again, and that the tender branch thereof will not cease.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Though the root thereof wax old in the earth, and the stock thereof die in the ground;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Yet through the scent of water it will bud, and bring forth boughs like a plant.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But man dieth, and wasteth away: yea, man giveth up the ghost, and where is he?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> As the waters fail from the sea, and the flood decayeth and drieth up:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So man lieth down, and riseth not: till the heavens be no more, they shall not awake, nor be raised out of their sleep.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> O that thou wouldest hide me in the grave, that thou wouldest keep me secret, until thy wrath be past, that thou wouldest appoint me a set time, and remember me!

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> If a man die, shall he live again? all the days of my appointed time will I wait, till my change come.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Thou shalt call, and I will answer thee: thou wilt have a desire to the work of thine hands.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For now thou numberest my steps: dost thou not watch over my sin?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> My transgression is sealed up in a bag, and thou sewest up mine iniquity.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And surely the mountain falling cometh to nought, and the rock is removed out of his place.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The waters wear the stones: thou washest away the things which grow out of the dust of the earth; and thou destroyest the hope of man.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Thou prevailest for ever against him, and he passeth: thou changest his countenance, and sendest him away.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> His sons come to honour, and he knoweth it not; and they are brought low, but he perceiveth it not of them.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But his flesh upon him shall have pain, and his soul within him shall mourn.
