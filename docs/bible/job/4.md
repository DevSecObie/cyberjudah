<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> If we assay to commune with thee, wilt thou be grieved? but who can withhold himself from speaking?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thy words have upholden him that was falling, and thou hast strengthened the feeble knees.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Is not this thy fear, thy confidence, thy hope, and the uprightness of thy ways?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? or where were the righteous cut off?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Even as I have seen, they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> By the blast of God they perish, and by the breath of his nostrils are they consumed.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the stout lion’s whelps are scattered abroad.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Now a thing was secretly brought to me, and mine ear received a little thereof.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Fear came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones to shake.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof: an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice, saying,

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Shall mortal man be more just than God? shall a man be more pure than his maker?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Behold, he put no trust in his servants; and his angels he charged with folly:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> How much less in them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, which are crushed before the moth?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> They are destroyed from morning to evening: they perish for ever without any regarding it.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Doth not their excellency which is in them go away? they die, even without wisdom.
