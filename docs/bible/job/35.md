<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Elihu spake moreover, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thinkest thou this to be right, that thou saidst, My righteousness is more than God’s?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For thou saidst, What advantage will it be unto thee? and, What profit shall I have, if I be cleansed from my sin?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I will answer thee, and thy companions with thee.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Look unto the heavens, and see; and behold the clouds which are higher than thou.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> If thou sinnest, what doest thou against him? or if thy transgressions be multiplied, what doest thou unto him?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If thou be righteous, what givest thou him? or what receiveth he of thine hand?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thy wickedness may hurt a man as thou art; and thy righteousness may profit the son of man.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> By reason of the multitude of oppressions they make the oppressed to cry: they cry out by reason of the arm of the mighty.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But none saith, Where is God my maker, who giveth songs in the night;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Who teacheth us more than the beasts of the earth, and maketh us wiser than the fowls of heaven?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> There they cry, but none giveth answer, because of the pride of evil men.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Surely God will not hear vanity, neither will the Almighty regard it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Although thou sayest thou shalt not see him, yet judgment is before him; therefore trust thou in him.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But now, because it is not so, he hath visited in his anger; yet he knoweth it not in great extremity:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Therefore doth Job open his mouth in vain; he multiplieth words without knowledge.
