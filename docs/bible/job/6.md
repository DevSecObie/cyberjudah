<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Oh that my grief were throughly weighed, and my calamity laid in the balances together!

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For now it would be heavier than the sand of the sea: therefore my words are swallowed up.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For the arrows of the Almighty are within me, the poison whereof drinketh up my spirit: the terrors of God do set themselves in array against me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Doth the wild ass bray when he hath grass? or loweth the ox over his fodder?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Can that which is unsavoury be eaten without salt? or is there any taste in the white of an egg?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The things that my soul refused to touch are as my sorrowful meat.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Oh that I might have my request; and that God would grant me the thing that I long for!

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Even that it would please God to destroy me; that he would let loose his hand, and cut me off!

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Then should I yet have comfort; yea, I would harden myself in sorrow: let him not spare; for I have not concealed the words of the Holy One.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> What is my strength, that I should hope? and what is mine end, that I should prolong my life?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Is my strength the strength of stones? or is my flesh of brass?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Is not my help in me? and is wisdom driven quite from me?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> To him that is afflicted pity should be shewed from his friend; but he forsaketh the fear of the Almighty.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> My brethren have dealt deceitfully as a brook, and as the stream of brooks they pass away;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Which are blackish by reason of the ice, and wherein the snow is hid:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> What time they wax warm, they vanish: when it is hot, they are consumed out of their place.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The paths of their way are turned aside; they go to nothing, and perish.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The troops of Tema looked, the companies of Sheba waited for them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> They were confounded because they had hoped; they came thither, and were ashamed.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For now ye are nothing; ye see my casting down, and are afraid.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Did I say, Bring unto me? or, Give a reward for me of your substance?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Or, Deliver me from the enemy’s hand? or, Redeem me from the hand of the mighty?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Teach me, and I will hold my tongue: and cause me to understand wherein I have erred.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> How forcible are right words! but what doth your arguing reprove?

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Do ye imagine to reprove words, and the speeches of one that is desperate, which are as wind?

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Yea, ye overwhelm the fatherless, and ye dig a pit for your friend.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Now therefore be content, look upon me; for it is evident unto you if I lie.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Return, I pray you, let it not be iniquity; yea, return again, my righteousness is in it.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Is there iniquity in my tongue? cannot my taste discern perverse things?
