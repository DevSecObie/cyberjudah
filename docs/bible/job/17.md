<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My breath is corrupt, my days are extinct, the graves are ready for me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Are there not mockers with me? and doth not mine eye continue in their provocation?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Lay down now, put me in a surety with thee; who is he that will strike hands with me?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thou hast hid their heart from understanding: therefore shalt thou not exalt them.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He that speaketh flattery to his friends, even the eyes of his children shall fail.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He hath made me also a byword of the people; and aforetime I was as a tabret.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Mine eye also is dim by reason of sorrow, and all my members are as a shadow.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Upright men shall be astonied at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The righteous also shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But as for you all, do ye return, and come now: for I cannot find one wise man among you.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> My days are past, my purposes are broken off, even the thoughts of my heart.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They change the night into day: the light is short because of darkness.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> If I wait, the grave is mine house: I have made my bed in the darkness.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I have said to corruption, Thou art my father: to the worm, Thou art my mother, and my sister.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And where is now my hope? as for my hope, who shall see it?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> They shall go down to the bars of the pit, when our rest together is in the dust.
