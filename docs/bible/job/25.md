<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Dominion and fear are with him, he maketh peace in his high places.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Is there any number of his armies? and upon whom doth not his light arise?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> How then can man be justified with God? or how can he be clean that is born of a woman?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Behold even to the moon, and it shineth not; yea, the stars are not pure in his sight.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> How much less man, that is a worm? and the son of man, which is a worm?
