<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram: against Job was his wrath kindled, because he justified himself rather than God.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Also against his three friends was his wrath kindled, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Now Elihu had waited till Job had spoken, because they were elder than he.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, then his wrath was kindled.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young, and ye are very old; wherefore I was afraid, and durst not shew you mine opinion.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Great men are not always wise: neither do the aged understand judgment.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Therefore I said, Hearken to me; I also will shew mine opinion.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Behold, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasons, whilst ye searched out what to say.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Yea, I attended unto you, and, behold, there was none of you that convinced Job, or that answered his words:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Lest ye should say, We have found out wisdom: God thrusteth him down, not man.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Now he hath not directed his words against me: neither will I answer him with your speeches.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> They were amazed, they answered no more: they left off speaking.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> When I had waited, (for they spake not, but stood still, and answered no more;)

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I said, I will answer also my part, I also will shew mine opinion.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For I am full of matter, the spirit within me constraineth me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; it is ready to burst like new bottles.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> I will speak, that I may be refreshed: I will open my lips and answer.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Let me not, I pray you, accept any man’s person, neither let me give flattering titles unto man.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For I know not to give flattering titles; in so doing my maker would soon take me away.
