<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Canst thou draw out leviathan with an hook? or his tongue with a cord which thou lettest down?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Canst thou put an hook into his nose? or bore his jaw through with a thorn?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Will he make many supplications unto thee? will he speak soft words unto thee?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Will he make a covenant with thee? wilt thou take him for a servant for ever?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Wilt thou play with him as with a bird? or wilt thou bind him for thy maidens?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Shall the companions make a banquet of him? shall they part him among the merchants?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Canst thou fill his skin with barbed irons? or his head with fish spears?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Lay thine hand upon him, remember the battle, do no more.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Behold, the hope of him is in vain: shall not one be cast down even at the sight of him?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> None is so fierce that dare stir him up: who then is able to stand before me?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Who hath prevented me, that I should repay him? whatsoever is under the whole heaven is mine.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will not conceal his parts, nor his power, nor his comely proportion.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Who can discover the face of his garment? or who can come to him with his double bridle?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Who can open the doors of his face? his teeth are terrible round about.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> His scales are his pride, shut up together as with a close seal.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> One is so near to another, that no air can come between them.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> They are joined one to another, they stick together, that they cannot be sundered.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> By his neesings a light doth shine, and his eyes are like the eyelids of the morning.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Out of his mouth go burning lamps, and sparks of fire leap out.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Out of his nostrils goeth smoke, as out of a seething pot or caldron.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> His breath kindleth coals, and a flame goeth out of his mouth.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> In his neck remaineth strength, and sorrow is turned into joy before him.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The flakes of his flesh are joined together: they are firm in themselves; they cannot be moved.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> His heart is as firm as a stone; yea, as hard as a piece of the nether millstone.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> When he raiseth up himself, the mighty are afraid: by reason of breakings they purify themselves.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The sword of him that layeth at him cannot hold: the spear, the dart, nor the habergeon.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He esteemeth iron as straw, and brass as rotten wood.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The arrow cannot make him flee: slingstones are turned with him into stubble.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Darts are counted as stubble: he laugheth at the shaking of a spear.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Sharp stones are under him: he spreadeth sharp pointed things upon the mire.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> He maketh the deep to boil like a pot: he maketh the sea like a pot of ointment.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> He maketh a path to shine after him; one would think the deep to be hoary.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Upon earth there is not his like, who is made without fear.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> He beholdeth all high things: he is a king over all the children of pride.
