<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How hast thou helped him that is without power? how savest thou the arm that hath no strength?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> How hast thou counselled him that hath no wisdom? and how hast thou plentifully declared the thing as it is?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose spirit came from thee?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Dead things are formed from under the waters, and the inhabitants thereof.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Hell is naked before him, and destruction hath no covering.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He stretcheth out the north over the empty place, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He bindeth up the waters in his thick clouds; and the cloud is not rent under them.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He holdeth back the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud upon it.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He hath compassed the waters with bounds, until the day and night come to an end.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The pillars of heaven tremble and are astonished at his reproof.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He divideth the sea with his power, and by his understanding he smiteth through the proud.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> By his spirit he hath garnished the heavens; his hand hath formed the crooked serpent.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Lo, these are parts of his ways: but how little a portion is heard of him? but the thunder of his power who can understand?
