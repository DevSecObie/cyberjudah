<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Is there not an appointed time to man upon earth? are not his days also like the days of an hireling?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> As a servant earnestly desireth the shadow, and as an hireling looketh for the reward of his work:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> So am I made to possess months of vanity, and wearisome nights are appointed to me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> When I lie down, I say, When shall I arise, and the night be gone? and I am full of tossings to and fro unto the dawning of the day.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> My flesh is clothed with worms and clods of dust; my skin is broken, and become loathsome.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> My days are swifter than a weaver’s shuttle, and are spent without hope.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> O remember that my life is wind: mine eye shall no more see good.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The eye of him that hath seen me shall see me no more: thine eyes are upon me, and I am not.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> As the cloud is consumed and vanisheth away: so he that goeth down to the grave shall come up no more.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He shall return no more to his house, neither shall his place know him any more.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Therefore I will not refrain my mouth; I will speak in the anguish of my spirit; I will complain in the bitterness of my soul.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Am I a sea, or a whale, that thou settest a watch over me?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> When I say, My bed shall comfort me, my couch shall ease my complaint;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Then thou scarest me with dreams, and terrifiest me through visions:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> So that my soul chooseth strangling, and death rather than my life.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I loathe it; I would not live alway: let me alone; for my days are vanity.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> What is man, that thou shouldest magnify him? and that thou shouldest set thine heart upon him?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And that thou shouldest visit him every morning, and try him every moment?

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> How long wilt thou not depart from me, nor let me alone till I swallow down my spittle?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> I have sinned; what shall I do unto thee, O thou preserver of men? why hast thou set me as a mark against thee, so that I am a burden to myself?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And why dost thou not pardon my transgression, and take away mine iniquity? for now shall I sleep in the dust; and thou shalt seek me in the morning, but I shall not be.
