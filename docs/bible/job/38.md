<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then the Lord answered Job out of the whirlwind, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Who is this that darkeneth counsel by words without knowledge?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Gird up now thy loins like a man; for I will demand of thee, and answer thou me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Where wast thou when I laid the foundations of the earth? declare, if thou hast understanding.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who hath laid the measures thereof, if thou knowest? or who hath stretched the line upon it?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Whereupon are the foundations thereof fastened? or who laid the corner stone thereof;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> When the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Or who shut up the sea with doors, when it brake forth, as if it had issued out of the womb?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> When I made the cloud the garment thereof, and thick darkness a swaddlingband for it,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And brake up for it my decreed place, and set bars and doors,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And said, Hitherto shalt thou come, but no further: and here shall thy proud waves be stayed?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Hast thou commanded the morning since thy days; and caused the dayspring to know his place;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> That it might take hold of the ends of the earth, that the wicked might be shaken out of it?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> It is turned as clay to the seal; and they stand as a garment.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And from the wicked their light is withholden, and the high arm shall be broken.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Hast thou entered into the springs of the sea? or hast thou walked in the search of the depth?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Have the gates of death been opened unto thee? or hast thou seen the doors of the shadow of death?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Hast thou perceived the breadth of the earth? declare if thou knowest it all.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Where is the way where light dwelleth? and as for darkness, where is the place thereof,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> That thou shouldest take it to the bound thereof, and that thou shouldest know the paths to the house thereof?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Knowest thou it, because thou wast then born? or because the number of thy days is great?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Hast thou entered into the treasures of the snow? or hast thou seen the treasures of the hail,

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Which I have reserved against the time of trouble, against the day of battle and war?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> By what way is the light parted, which scattereth the east wind upon the earth?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Who hath divided a watercourse for the overflowing of waters, or a way for the lightning of thunder;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> To cause it to rain on the earth, where no man is; on the wilderness, wherein there is no man;

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> To satisfy the desolate and waste ground; and to cause the bud of the tender herb to spring forth?

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Hath the rain a father? or who hath begotten the drops of dew?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Out of whose womb came the ice? and the hoary frost of heaven, who hath gendered it?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> The waters are hid as with a stone, and the face of the deep is frozen.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Canst thou bind the sweet influences of Pleiades, or loose the bands of Orion?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Canst thou bring forth Mazzaroth in his season? or canst thou guide Arcturus with his sons?

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Knowest thou the ordinances of heaven? canst thou set the dominion thereof in the earth?

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Canst thou lift up thy voice to the clouds, that abundance of waters may cover thee?

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Canst thou send lightnings, that they may go, and say unto thee, Here we are?

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Who hath put wisdom in the inward parts? or who hath given understanding to the heart?

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Who can number the clouds in wisdom? or who can stay the bottles of heaven,

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> When the dust groweth into hardness, and the clods cleave fast together?

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Wilt thou hunt the prey for the lion? or fill the appetite of the young lions,

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> When they couch in their dens, and abide in the covert to lie in wait?

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Who provideth for the raven his food? when his young ones cry unto God, they wander for lack of meat.
