<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Therefore do my thoughts cause me to answer, and for this I make haste.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I have heard the check of my reproach, and the spirit of my understanding causeth me to answer.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Knowest thou not this of old, since man was placed upon earth,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> That the triumphing of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Though his excellency mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung: they which have seen him shall say, Where is he?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He shall fly away as a dream, and shall not be found: yea, he shall be chased away as a vision of the night.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The eye also which saw him shall see him no more; neither shall his place any more behold him.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> His children shall seek to please the poor, and his hands shall restore their goods.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> His bones are full of the sin of his youth, which shall lie down with him in the dust.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Though wickedness be sweet in his mouth, though he hide it under his tongue;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Though he spare it, and forsake it not; but keep it still within his mouth:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Yet his meat in his bowels is turned, it is the gall of asps within him.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He hath swallowed down riches, and he shall vomit them up again: God shall cast them out of his belly.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He shall suck the poison of asps: the viper’s tongue shall slay him.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He shall not see the rivers, the floods, the brooks of honey and butter.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> That which he laboured for shall he restore, and shall not swallow it down: according to his substance shall the restitution be, and he shall not rejoice therein.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Because he hath oppressed and hath forsaken the poor; because he hath violently taken away an house which he builded not;

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Surely he shall not feel quietness in his belly, he shall not save of that which he desired.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> There shall none of his meat be left; therefore shall no man look for his goods.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> In the fulness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits: every hand of the wicked shall come upon him.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> When he is about to fill his belly, God shall cast the fury of his wrath upon him, and shall rain it upon him while he is eating.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He shall flee from the iron weapon, and the bow of steel shall strike him through.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> It is drawn, and cometh out of the body; yea, the glittering sword cometh out of his gall: terrors are upon him.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> All darkness shall be hid in his secret places: a fire not blown shall consume him; it shall go ill with him that is left in his tabernacle.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> The heaven shall reveal his iniquity; and the earth shall rise up against him.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The increase of his house shall depart, and his goods shall flow away in the day of his wrath.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed unto him by God.
