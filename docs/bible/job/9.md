<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I know it is so of a truth: but how should man be just with God?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> If he will contend with him, he cannot answer him one of a thousand.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He is wise in heart, and mighty in strength: who hath hardened himself against him, and hath prospered?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Which removeth the mountains, and they know not: which overturneth them in his anger.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Which shaketh the earth out of her place, and the pillars thereof tremble.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Which commandeth the sun, and it riseth not; and sealeth up the stars.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Which alone spreadeth out the heavens, and treadeth upon the waves of the sea.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Which maketh Arcturus, Orion, and Pleiades, and the chambers of the south.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Which doeth great things past finding out; yea, and wonders without number.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Lo, he goeth by me, and I see him not: he passeth on also, but I perceive him not.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Behold, he taketh away, who can hinder him? who will say unto him, What doest thou?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> If God will not withdraw his anger, the proud helpers do stoop under him.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> How much less shall I answer him, and choose out my words to reason with him?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Whom, though I were righteous, yet would I not answer, but I would make supplication to my judge.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> If I had called, and he had answered me; yet would I not believe that he had hearkened unto my voice.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For he breaketh me with a tempest, and multiplieth my wounds without cause.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He will not suffer me to take my breath, but filleth me with bitterness.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> If I speak of strength, lo, he is strong: and if of judgment, who shall set me a time to plead?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> If I justify myself, mine own mouth shall condemn me: if I say, I am perfect, it shall also prove me perverse.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Though I were perfect, yet would I not know my soul: I would despise my life.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> This is one thing, therefore I said it, He destroyeth the perfect and the wicked.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> If the scourge slay suddenly, he will laugh at the trial of the innocent.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The earth is given into the hand of the wicked: he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, and who is he?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Now my days are swifter than a post: they flee away, they see no good.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> They are passed away as the swift ships: as the eagle that hasteth to the prey.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> If I say, I will forget my complaint, I will leave off my heaviness, and comfort myself:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> I am afraid of all my sorrows, I know that thou wilt not hold me innocent.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> If I be wicked, why then labour I in vain?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> If I wash myself with snow water, and make my hands never so clean;

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Yet shalt thou plunge me in the ditch, and mine own clothes shall abhor me.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> For he is not a man, as I am, that I should answer him, and we should come together in judgment.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Neither is there any daysman betwixt us, that might lay his hand upon us both.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Let him take his rod away from me, and let not his fear terrify me:

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Then would I speak, and not fear him; but it is not so with me.
