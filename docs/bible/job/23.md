<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Even to day is my complaint bitter: my stroke is heavier than my groaning.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Oh that I knew where I might find him! that I might come even to his seat!

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I would order my cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I would know the words which he would answer me, and understand what he would say unto me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Will he plead against me with his great power? No; but he would put strength in me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> There the righteous might dispute with him; so should I be delivered for ever from my judge.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Behold, I go forward, but he is not there; and backward, but I cannot perceive him:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> On the left hand, where he doth work, but I cannot behold him: he hideth himself on the right hand, that I cannot see him:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But he knoweth the way that I take: when he hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> My foot hath held his steps, his way have I kept, and not declined.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Neither have I gone back from the commandment of his lips; I have esteemed the words of his mouth more than my necessary food.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But he is in one mind, and who can turn him? and what his soul desireth, even that he doeth.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For he performeth the thing that is appointed for me: and many such things are with him.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Therefore am I troubled at his presence: when I consider, I am afraid of him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For God maketh my heart soft, and the Almighty troubleth me:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Because I was not cut off before the darkness, neither hath he covered the darkness from my face.
