<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Moreover Job continued his parable, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> As God liveth, who hath taken away my judgment; and the Almighty, who hath vexed my soul;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> All the while my breath is in me, and the spirit of God is in my nostrils;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> My lips shall not speak wickedness, nor my tongue utter deceit.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> God forbid that I should justify you: till I die I will not remove mine integrity from me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> My righteousness I hold fast, and will not let it go: my heart shall not reproach me so long as I live.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Let mine enemy be as the wicked, and he that riseth up against me as the unrighteous.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For what is the hope of the hypocrite, though he hath gained, when God taketh away his soul?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Will God hear his cry when trouble cometh upon him?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Will he delight himself in the Almighty? will he always call upon God?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I will teach you by the hand of God: that which is with the Almighty will I not conceal.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Behold, all ye yourselves have seen it; why then are ye thus altogether vain?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> This is the portion of a wicked man with God, and the heritage of oppressors, which they shall receive of the Almighty.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> If his children be multiplied, it is for the sword: and his offspring shall not be satisfied with bread.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Those that remain of him shall be buried in death: and his widows shall not weep.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Though he heap up silver as the dust, and prepare raiment as the clay;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He may prepare it, but the just shall put it on, and the innocent shall divide the silver.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He buildeth his house as a moth, and as a booth that the keeper maketh.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The rich man shall lie down, but he shall not be gathered: he openeth his eyes, and he is not.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Terrors take hold on him as waters, a tempest stealeth him away in the night.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The east wind carrieth him away, and he departeth: and as a storm hurleth him out of his place.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For God shall cast upon him, and not spare: he would fain flee out of his hand.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Men shall clap their hands at him, and shall hiss him out of his place.
