<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Why, seeing times are not hidden from the Almighty, do they that know him not see his days?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Some remove the landmarks; they violently take away flocks, and feed thereof.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take the widow’s ox for a pledge.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They turn the needy out of the way: the poor of the earth hide themselves together.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Behold, as wild asses in the desert, go they forth to their work; rising betimes for a prey: the wilderness yieldeth food for them and for their children.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They reap every one his corn in the field: and they gather the vintage of the wicked.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They cause the naked to lodge without clothing, that they have no covering in the cold.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They are wet with the showers of the mountains, and embrace the rock for want of a shelter.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> They pluck the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge of the poor.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and they take away the sheaf from the hungry;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Which make oil within their walls, and tread their winepresses, and suffer thirst.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Men groan from out of the city, and the soul of the wounded crieth out: yet God layeth not folly to them.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> They are of those that rebel against the light; they know not the ways thereof, nor abide in the paths thereof.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The murderer rising with the light killeth the poor and needy, and in the night is as a thief.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The eye also of the adulterer waiteth for the twilight, saying, No eye shall see me: and disguiseth his face.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> In the dark they dig through houses, which they had marked for themselves in the daytime: they know not the light.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For the morning is to them even as the shadow of death: if one know them, they are in the terrors of the shadow of death.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He is swift as the waters; their portion is cursed in the earth: he beholdeth not the way of the vineyards.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Drought and heat consume the snow waters: so doth the grave those which have sinned.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The womb shall forget him; the worm shall feed sweetly on him; he shall be no more remembered; and wickedness shall be broken as a tree.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He evil entreateth the barren that beareth not: and doeth not good to the widow.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He draweth also the mighty with his power: he riseth up, and no man is sure of life.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Though it be given him to be in safety, whereon he resteth; yet his eyes are upon their ways.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> They are exalted for a little while, but are gone and brought low; they are taken out of the way as all other, and cut off as the tops of the ears of corn.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And if it be not so now, who will make me a liar, and make my speech nothing worth?
