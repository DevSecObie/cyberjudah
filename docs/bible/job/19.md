<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How long will ye vex my soul, and break me in pieces with words?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> These ten times have ye reproached me: ye are not ashamed that ye make yourselves strange to me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And be it indeed that I have erred, mine error remaineth with myself.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If indeed ye will magnify yourselves against me, and plead against me my reproach:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Know now that God hath overthrown me, and hath compassed me with his net.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Behold, I cry out of wrong, but I am not heard: I cry aloud, but there is no judgment.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He hath fenced up my way that I cannot pass, and he hath set darkness in my paths.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He hath stripped me of my glory, and taken the crown from my head.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He hath destroyed me on every side, and I am gone: and mine hope hath he removed like a tree.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He hath also kindled his wrath against me, and he counteth me unto him as one of his enemies.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> His troops come together, and raise up their way against me, and encamp round about my tabernacle.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He hath put my brethren far from me, and mine acquaintance are verily estranged from me.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> My kinsfolk have failed, and my familiar friends have forgotten me.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> They that dwell in mine house, and my maids, count me for a stranger: I am an alien in their sight.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I called my servant, and he gave me no answer; I intreated him with my mouth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> My breath is strange to my wife, though I intreated for the children’s sake of mine own body.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Yea, young children despised me; I arose, and they spake against me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> All my inward friends abhorred me: and they whom I loved are turned against me.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My bone cleaveth to my skin and to my flesh, and I am escaped with the skin of my teeth.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Have pity upon me, have pity upon me, O ye my friends; for the hand of God hath touched me.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Why do ye persecute me as God, and are not satisfied with my flesh?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Oh that my words were now written! oh that they were printed in a book!

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> That they were graven with an iron pen and lead in the rock for ever!

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And though after my skin worms destroy this body, yet in my flesh shall I see God:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Whom I shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another; though my reins be consumed within me.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> But ye should say, Why persecute we him, seeing the root of the matter is found in me?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Be ye afraid of the sword: for wrath bringeth the punishments of the sword, that ye may know there is a judgment.
