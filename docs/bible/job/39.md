<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Knowest thou the time when the wild goats of the rock bring forth? or canst thou mark when the hinds do calve?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Canst thou number the months that they fulfil? or knowest thou the time when they bring forth?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They bow themselves, they bring forth their young ones, they cast out their sorrows.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Their young ones are in good liking, they grow up with corn; they go forth, and return not unto them.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who hath sent out the wild ass free? or who hath loosed the bands of the wild ass?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Whose house I have made the wilderness, and the barren land his dwellings.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He scorneth the multitude of the city, neither regardeth he the crying of the driver.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The range of the mountains is his pasture, and he searcheth after every green thing.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Will the unicorn be willing to serve thee, or abide by thy crib?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Canst thou bind the unicorn with his band in the furrow? or will he harrow the valleys after thee?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wilt thou trust him, because his strength is great? or wilt thou leave thy labour to him?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Wilt thou believe him, that he will bring home thy seed, and gather it into thy barn?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Gavest thou the goodly wings unto the peacocks? or wings and feathers unto the ostrich?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Which leaveth her eggs in the earth, and warmeth them in dust,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And forgetteth that the foot may crush them, or that the wild beast may break them.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> She is hardened against her young ones, as though they were not hers: her labour is in vain without fear;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Because God hath deprived her of wisdom, neither hath he imparted to her understanding.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> What time she lifteth up herself on high, she scorneth the horse and his rider.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Hast thou given the horse strength? hast thou clothed his neck with thunder?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Canst thou make him afraid as a grasshopper? the glory of his nostrils is terrible.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He paweth in the valley, and rejoiceth in his strength: he goeth on to meet the armed men.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He mocketh at fear, and is not affrighted; neither turneth he back from the sword.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The quiver rattleth against him, the glittering spear and the shield.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He swalloweth the ground with fierceness and rage: neither believeth he that it is the sound of the trumpet.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> He saith among the trumpets, Ha, ha; and he smelleth the battle afar off, the thunder of the captains, and the shouting.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Doth the hawk fly by thy wisdom, and stretch her wings toward the south?

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Doth the eagle mount up at thy command, and make her nest on high?

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> She dwelleth and abideth on the rock, upon the crag of the rock, and the strong place.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> From thence she seeketh the prey, and her eyes behold afar off.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Her young ones also suck up blood: and where the slain are, there is she.
