<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Call now, if there be any that will answer thee; and to which of the saints wilt thou turn?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For wrath killeth the foolish man, and envy slayeth the silly one.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I have seen the foolish taking root: but suddenly I cursed his habitation.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> His children are far from safety, and they are crushed in the gate, neither is there any to deliver them.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Whose harvest the hungry eateth up, and taketh it even out of the thorns, and the robber swalloweth up their substance.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Although affliction cometh not forth of the dust, neither doth trouble spring out of the ground;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Yet man is born unto trouble, as the sparks fly upward.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I would seek unto God, and unto God would I commit my cause:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Which doeth great things and unsearchable; marvellous things without number:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who giveth rain upon the earth, and sendeth waters upon the fields:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> To set up on high those that be low; that those which mourn may be exalted to safety.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He disappointeth the devices of the crafty, so that their hands cannot perform their enterprise.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He taketh the wise in their own craftiness: and the counsel of the froward is carried headlong.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> They meet with darkness in the daytime, and grope in the noonday as in the night.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But he saveth the poor from the sword, from their mouth, and from the hand of the mighty.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> So the poor hath hope, and iniquity stoppeth her mouth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Behold, happy is the man whom God correcteth: therefore despise not thou the chastening of the Almighty:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For he maketh sore, and bindeth up: he woundeth, and his hands make whole.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He shall deliver thee in six troubles: yea, in seven there shall no evil touch thee.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> In famine he shall redeem thee from death: and in war from the power of the sword.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Thou shalt be hid from the scourge of the tongue: neither shalt thou be afraid of destruction when it cometh.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> At destruction and famine thou shalt laugh: neither shalt thou be afraid of the beasts of the earth.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For thou shalt be in league with the stones of the field: and the beasts of the field shall be at peace with thee.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And thou shalt know that thy tabernacle shall be in peace; and thou shalt visit thy habitation, and shalt not sin.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Thou shalt know also that thy seed shall be great, and thine offspring as the grass of the earth.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Thou shalt come to thy grave in a full age, like as a shock of corn cometh in in his season.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Lo this, we have searched it, so it is; hear it, and know thou it for thy good.
