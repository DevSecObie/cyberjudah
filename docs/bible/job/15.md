<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then answered Eliphaz the Temanite, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Should a wise man utter vain knowledge, and fill his belly with the east wind?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Should he reason with unprofitable talk? or with speeches wherewith he can do no good?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Yea, thou castest off fear, and restrainest prayer before God.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For thy mouth uttereth thine iniquity, and thou choosest the tongue of the crafty.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thine own mouth condemneth thee, and not I: yea, thine own lips testify against thee.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Art thou the first man that was born? or wast thou made before the hills?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Hast thou heard the secret of God? and dost thou restrain wisdom to thyself?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> What knowest thou, that we know not? what understandest thou, which is not in us?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> With us are both the grayheaded and very aged men, much elder than thy father.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Are the consolations of God small with thee? is there any secret thing with thee?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Why doth thine heart carry thee away? and what do thy eyes wink at,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> That thou turnest thy spirit against God, and lettest such words go out of thy mouth?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> What is man, that he should be clean? and he which is born of a woman, that he should be righteous?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Behold, he putteth no trust in his saints; yea, the heavens are not clean in his sight.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> How much more abominable and filthy is man, which drinketh iniquity like water?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I will shew thee, hear me; and that which I have seen I will declare;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Which wise men have told from their fathers, and have not hid it:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Unto whom alone the earth was given, and no stranger passed among them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The wicked man travaileth with pain all his days, and the number of years is hidden to the oppressor.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> A dreadful sound is in his ears: in prosperity the destroyer shall come upon him.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He believeth not that he shall return out of darkness, and he is waited for of the sword.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> He wandereth abroad for bread, saying, Where is it? he knoweth that the day of darkness is ready at his hand.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Trouble and anguish shall make him afraid; they shall prevail against him, as a king ready to the battle.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For he stretcheth out his hand against God, and strengtheneth himself against the Almighty.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He runneth upon him, even on his neck, upon the thick bosses of his bucklers:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Because he covereth his face with his fatness, and maketh collops of fat on his flanks.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And he dwelleth in desolate cities, and in houses which no man inhabiteth, which are ready to become heaps.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> He shall not be rich, neither shall his substance continue, neither shall he prolong the perfection thereof upon the earth.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> He shall not depart out of darkness; the flame shall dry up his branches, and by the breath of his mouth shall he go away.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Let not him that is deceived trust in vanity: for vanity shall be his recompence.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> It shall be accomplished before his time, and his branch shall not be green.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> He shall shake off his unripe grape as the vine, and shall cast off his flower as the olive.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> For the congregation of hypocrites shall be desolate, and fire shall consume the tabernacles of bribery.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> They conceive mischief, and bring forth vanity, and their belly prepareth deceit.
