<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I have heard many such things: miserable comforters are ye all.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Shall vain words have an end? or what emboldeneth thee that thou answerest?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I also could speak as ye do: if your soul were in my soul’s stead, I could heap up words against you, and shake mine head at you.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But I would strengthen you with my mouth, and the moving of my lips should asswage your grief.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Though I speak, my grief is not asswaged: and though I forbear, what am I eased?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But now he hath made me weary: thou hast made desolate all my company.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And thou hast filled me with wrinkles, which is a witness against me: and my leanness rising up in me beareth witness to my face.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He teareth me in his wrath, who hateth me: he gnasheth upon me with his teeth; mine enemy sharpeneth his eyes upon me.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> They have gaped upon me with their mouth; they have smitten me upon the cheek reproachfully; they have gathered themselves together against me.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> God hath delivered me to the ungodly, and turned me over into the hands of the wicked.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I was at ease, but he hath broken me asunder: he hath also taken me by my neck, and shaken me to pieces, and set me up for his mark.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> His archers compass me round about, he cleaveth my reins asunder, and doth not spare; he poureth out my gall upon the ground.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He breaketh me with breach upon breach, he runneth upon me like a giant.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I have sewed sackcloth upon my skin, and defiled my horn in the dust.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> My face is foul with weeping, and on my eyelids is the shadow of death;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Not for any injustice in mine hands: also my prayer is pure.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> O earth, cover not thou my blood, and let my cry have no place.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Also now, behold, my witness is in heaven, and my record is on high.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My friends scorn me: but mine eye poureth out tears unto God.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> O that one might plead for a man with God, as a man pleadeth for his neighbour!

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> When a few years are come, then I shall go the way whence I shall not return.
