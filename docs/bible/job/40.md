<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Moreover the Lord answered Job, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Shall he that contendeth with the Almighty instruct him? he that reproveth God, let him answer it.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Then Job answered the Lord, and said,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Behold, I am vile; what shall I answer thee? I will lay mine hand upon my mouth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Once have I spoken; but I will not answer: yea, twice; but I will proceed no further.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then answered the Lord unto Job out of the whirlwind, and said,

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Gird up thy loins now like a man: I will demand of thee, and declare thou unto me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Wilt thou also disannul my judgment? wilt thou condemn me, that thou mayest be righteous?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Hast thou an arm like God? or canst thou thunder with a voice like him?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Deck thyself now with majesty and excellency; and array thyself with glory and beauty.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Cast abroad the rage of thy wrath: and behold every one that is proud, and abase him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Look on every one that is proud, and bring him low; and tread down the wicked in their place.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Hide them in the dust together; and bind their faces in secret.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Then will I also confess unto thee that thine own right hand can save thee.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Behold now behemoth, which I made with thee; he eateth grass as an ox.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Lo now, his strength is in his loins, and his force is in the navel of his belly.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He moveth his tail like a cedar: the sinews of his stones are wrapped together.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> His bones are as strong pieces of brass; his bones are like bars of iron.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He is the chief of the ways of God: he that made him can make his sword to approach unto him.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Surely the mountains bring him forth food, where all the beasts of the field play.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He lieth under the shady trees, in the covert of the reed, and fens.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The shady trees cover him with their shadow; the willows of the brook compass him about.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Behold, he drinketh up a river, and hasteth not: he trusteth that he can draw up Jordan into his mouth.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He taketh it with his eyes: his nose pierceth through snares.
