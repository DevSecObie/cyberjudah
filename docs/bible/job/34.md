<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Furthermore Elihu answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hear my words, O ye wise men; and give ear unto me, ye that have knowledge.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For the ear trieth words, as the mouth tasteth meat.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Let us choose to us judgment: let us know among ourselves what is good.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For Job hath said, I am righteous: and God hath taken away my judgment.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Should I lie against my right? my wound is incurable without transgression.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> What man is like Job, who drinketh up scorning like water?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Which goeth in company with the workers of iniquity, and walketh with wicked men.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For he hath said, It profiteth a man nothing that he should delight himself with God.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Therefore hearken unto me, ye men of understanding: far be it from God, that he should do wickedness; and from the Almighty, that he should commit iniquity.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the work of a man shall he render unto him, and cause every man to find according to his ways.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Yea, surely God will not do wickedly, neither will the Almighty pervert judgment.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Who hath given him a charge over the earth? or who hath disposed the whole world?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> If he set his heart upon man, if he gather unto himself his spirit and his breath;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> All flesh shall perish together, and man shall turn again unto dust.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> If now thou hast understanding, hear this: hearken to the voice of my words.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Shall even he that hateth right govern? and wilt thou condemn him that is most just?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Is it fit to say to a king, Thou art wicked? and to princes, Ye are ungodly?

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> How much less to him that accepteth not the persons of princes, nor regardeth the rich more than the poor? for they all are the work of his hands.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> In a moment shall they die, and the people shall be troubled at midnight, and pass away: and the mighty shall be taken away without hand.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For his eyes are upon the ways of man, and he seeth all his goings.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For he will not lay upon man more than right; that he should enter into judgment with God.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He shall break in pieces mighty men without number, and set others in their stead.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Therefore he knoweth their works, and he overturneth them in the night, so that they are destroyed.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He striketh them as wicked men in the open sight of others;

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Because they turned back from him, and would not consider any of his ways:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> So that they cause the cry of the poor to come unto him, and he heareth the cry of the afflicted.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> When he giveth quietness, who then can make trouble? and when he hideth his face, who then can behold him? whether it be done against a nation, or against a man only:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> That the hypocrite reign not, lest the people be ensnared.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Surely it is meet to be said unto God, I have borne chastisement, I will not offend any more:

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> That which I see not teach thou me: if I have done iniquity, I will do no more.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Should it be according to thy mind? he will recompense it, whether thou refuse, or whether thou choose; and not I: therefore speak what thou knowest.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Let men of understanding tell me, and let a wise man hearken unto me.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Job hath spoken without knowledge, and his words were without wisdom.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> My desire is that Job may be tried unto the end because of his answers for wicked men.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> For he addeth rebellion unto his sin, he clappeth his hands among us, and multiplieth his words against God.
