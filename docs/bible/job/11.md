<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Should not the multitude of words be answered? and should a man full of talk be justified?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Should thy lies make men hold their peace? and when thou mockest, shall no man make thee ashamed?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thou hast said, My doctrine is pure, and I am clean in thine eyes.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Canst thou by searching find out God? canst thou find out the Almighty unto perfection?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> It is as high as heaven; what canst thou do? deeper than hell; what canst thou know?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The measure thereof is longer than the earth, and broader than the sea.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> If he cut off, and shut up, or gather together, then who can hinder him?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For he knoweth vain men: he seeth wickedness also; will he not then consider it?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For vain man would be wise, though man be born like a wild ass’s colt.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> If thou prepare thine heart, and stretch out thine hands toward him;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> If iniquity be in thine hand, put it far away, and let not wickedness dwell in thy tabernacles.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For then shalt thou lift up thy face without spot; yea, thou shalt be stedfast, and shalt not fear:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Because thou shalt forget thy misery, and remember it as waters that pass away:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And thine age shall be clearer than the noonday; thou shalt shine forth, thou shalt be as the morning.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And thou shalt be secure, because there is hope; yea, thou shalt dig about thee, and thou shalt take thy rest in safety.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Also thou shalt lie down, and none shall make thee afraid; yea, many shall make suit unto thee.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But the eyes of the wicked shall fail, and they shall not escape, and their hope shall be as the giving up of the ghost.
