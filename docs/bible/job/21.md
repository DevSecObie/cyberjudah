<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But Job answered and said,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hear diligently my speech, and let this be your consolations.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Suffer me that I may speak; and after that I have spoken, mock on.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> As for me, is my complaint to man? and if it were so, why should not my spirit be troubled?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Mark me, and be astonished, and lay your hand upon your mouth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Even when I remember I am afraid, and trembling taketh hold on my flesh.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Wherefore do the wicked live, become old, yea, are mighty in power?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Their seed is established in their sight with them, and their offspring before their eyes.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Their houses are safe from fear, neither is the rod of God upon them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Their bull gendereth, and faileth not; their cow calveth, and casteth not her calf.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> They send forth their little ones like a flock, and their children dance.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They take the timbrel and harp, and rejoice at the sound of the organ.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> They spend their days in wealth, and in a moment go down to the grave.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Therefore they say unto God, Depart from us; for we desire not the knowledge of thy ways.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> What is the Almighty, that we should serve him? and what profit should we have, if we pray unto him?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Lo, their good is not in their hand: the counsel of the wicked is far from me.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> How oft is the candle of the wicked put out! and how oft cometh their destruction upon them! God distributeth sorrows in his anger.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> They are as stubble before the wind, and as chaff that the storm carrieth away.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> God layeth up his iniquity for his children: he rewardeth him, and he shall know it.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> His eyes shall see his destruction, and he shall drink of the wrath of the Almighty.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For what pleasure hath he in his house after him, when the number of his months is cut off in the midst?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Shall any teach God knowledge? seeing he judgeth those that are high.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> One dieth in his full strength, being wholly at ease and quiet.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> His breasts are full of milk, and his bones are moistened with marrow.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And another dieth in the bitterness of his soul, and never eateth with pleasure.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> They shall lie down alike in the dust, and the worms shall cover them.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Behold, I know your thoughts, and the devices which ye wrongfully imagine against me.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For ye say, Where is the house of the prince? and where are the dwelling places of the wicked?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Have ye not asked them that go by the way? and do ye not know their tokens,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> That the wicked is reserved to the day of destruction? they shall be brought forth to the day of wrath.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Who shall declare his way to his face? and who shall repay him what he hath done?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Yet shall he be brought to the grave, and shall remain in the tomb.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> The clods of the valley shall be sweet unto him, and every man shall draw after him, as there are innumerable before him.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> How then comfort ye me in vain, seeing in your answers there remaineth falsehood?
