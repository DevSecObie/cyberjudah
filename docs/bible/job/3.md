<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> After this opened Job his mouth, and cursed his day.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And Job spake, and said,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night in which it was said, There is a man child conceived.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Let that day be darkness; let not God regard it from above, neither let the light shine upon it.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let darkness and the shadow of death stain it; let a cloud dwell upon it; let the blackness of the day terrify it.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> As for that night, let darkness seize upon it; let it not be joined unto the days of the year, let it not come into the number of the months.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Lo, let that night be solitary, let no joyful voice come therein.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to raise up their mourning.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Let the stars of the twilight thereof be dark; let it look for light, but have none; neither let it see the dawning of the day:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Because it shut not up the doors of my mother’s womb, nor hid sorrow from mine eyes.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Why died I not from the womb? why did I not give up the ghost when I came out of the belly?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Why did the knees prevent me? or why the breasts that I should suck?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For now should I have lain still and been quiet, I should have slept: then had I been at rest,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> With kings and counsellors of the earth, which built desolate places for themselves;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Or with princes that had gold, who filled their houses with silver:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Or as an hidden untimely birth I had not been; as infants which never saw light.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> There the wicked cease from troubling; and there the weary be at rest.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> There the prisoners rest together; they hear not the voice of the oppressor.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The small and great are there; and the servant is free from his master.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Wherefore is light given to him that is in misery, and life unto the bitter in soul;

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Which long for death, but it cometh not; and dig for it more than for hid treasures;

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Which rejoice exceedingly, and are glad, when they can find the grave?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Why is light given to a man whose way is hid, and whom God hath hedged in?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For my sighing cometh before I eat, and my roarings are poured out like the waters.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For the thing which I greatly feared is come upon me, and that which I was afraid of is come unto me.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> I was not in safety, neither had I rest, neither was I quiet; yet trouble came.
