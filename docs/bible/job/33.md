<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Wherefore, Job, I pray thee, hear my speeches, and hearken to all my words.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Behold, now I have opened my mouth, my tongue hath spoken in my mouth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> My words shall be of the uprightness of my heart: and my lips shall utter knowledge clearly.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If thou canst answer me, set thy words in order before me, stand up.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Behold, I am according to thy wish in God’s stead: I also am formed out of the clay.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Behold, my terror shall not make thee afraid, neither shall my hand be heavy upon thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Surely thou hast spoken in mine hearing, and I have heard the voice of thy words, saying,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I am clean without transgression, I am innocent; neither is there iniquity in me.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Behold, he findeth occasions against me, he counteth me for his enemy,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He putteth my feet in the stocks, he marketh all my paths.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Behold, in this thou art not just: I will answer thee, that God is greater than man.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Why dost thou strive against him? for he giveth not account of any of his matters.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For God speaketh once, yea twice, yet man perceiveth it not.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, in slumberings upon the bed;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Then he openeth the ears of men, and sealeth their instruction,

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> That he may withdraw man from his purpose, and hide pride from man.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He keepeth back his soul from the pit, and his life from perishing by the sword.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, and the multitude of his bones with strong pain:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> So that his life abhorreth bread, and his soul dainty meat.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen; and his bones that were not seen stick out.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Yea, his soul draweth near unto the grave, and his life to the destroyers.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> If there be a messenger with him, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to shew unto man his uprightness:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Then he is gracious unto him, and saith, Deliver him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> His flesh shall be fresher than a child’s: he shall return to the days of his youth:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He shall pray unto God, and he will be favourable unto him: and he shall see his face with joy: for he will render unto man his righteousness.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He looketh upon men, and if any say, I have sinned, and perverted that which was right, and it profited me not;

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> He will deliver his soul from going into the pit, and his life shall see the light.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Lo, all these things worketh God oftentimes with man,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> To bring back his soul from the pit, to be enlightened with the light of the living.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Mark well, O Job, hearken unto me: hold thy peace, and I will speak.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> If thou hast any thing to say, answer me: speak, for I desire to justify thee.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> If not, hearken unto me: hold thy peace, and I shall teach thee wisdom.
