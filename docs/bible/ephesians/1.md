<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Wherein he hath abounded toward us in all wisdom and prudence;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> That we should be to the praise of his glory, who first trusted in Christ.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> In whom ye also trusted, after that ye heard the word of truth, the gospel of your salvation: in whom also after that ye believed, ye were sealed with that holy Spirit of promise,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Which is the earnest of our inheritance until the redemption of the purchased possession, unto the praise of his glory.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Wherefore I also, after I heard of your faith in the Lord Jesus, and love unto all the saints,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Cease not to give thanks for you, making mention of you in my prayers;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Which he wrought in Christ, when he raised him from the dead, and set him at his own right hand in the heavenly places,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Far above all principality, and power, and might, and dominion, and every name that is named, not only in this world, but also in that which is to come:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And hath put all things under his feet, and gave him to be the head over all things to the church,

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Which is his body, the fulness of him that filleth all in all.
