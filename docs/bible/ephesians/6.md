<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Children, obey your parents in the Lord: for this is right.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Honour thy father and mother; (which is the first commandment with promise;)

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> That it may be well with thee, and thou mayest live long on the earth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And, ye fathers, provoke not your children to wrath: but bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Servants, be obedient to them that are your masters according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as unto Christ;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Not with eyeservice, as menpleasers; but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> With good will doing service, as to the Lord, and not to men:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Knowing that whatsoever good thing any man doeth, the same shall he receive of the Lord, whether he be bond or free.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And, ye masters, do the same things unto them, forbearing threatening: knowing that your Master also is in heaven; neither is there respect of persons with him.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Finally, my brethren, be strong in the Lord, and in the power of his might.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Wherefore take unto you the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to withstand in the evil day, and having done all, to stand.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Stand therefore, having your loins girt about with truth, and having on the breastplate of righteousness;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And your feet shod with the preparation of the gospel of peace;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And take the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Praying always with all prayer and supplication in the Spirit, and watching thereunto with all perseverance and supplication for all saints;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And for me, that utterance may be given unto me, that I may open my mouth boldly, to make known the mystery of the gospel,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For which I am an ambassador in bonds: that therein I may speak boldly, as I ought to speak.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But that ye also may know my affairs, and how I do, Tychicus, a beloved brother and faithful minister in the Lord, shall make known to you all things:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Whom I have sent unto you for the same purpose, that ye might know our affairs, and that he might comfort your hearts.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Peace be to the brethren, and love with faith, from God the Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Grace be with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity. Amen. Written from Rome unto the Ephesians by Tychicus.
