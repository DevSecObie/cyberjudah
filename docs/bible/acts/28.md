<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And when they were escaped, then they knew that the island was called Melita.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And the barbarous people shewed us no little kindness: for they kindled a fire, and received us every one, because of the present rain, and because of the cold.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And when Paul had gathered a bundle of sticks, and laid them on the fire, there came a viper out of the heat, and fastened on his hand.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And when the barbarians saw the venomous beast hang on his hand, they said among themselves, No doubt this man is a murderer, whom, though he hath escaped the sea, yet vengeance suffereth not to live.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And he shook off the beast into the fire, and felt no harm.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Howbeit they looked when he should have swollen, or fallen down dead suddenly: but after they had looked a great while, and saw no harm come to him, they changed their minds, and said that he was a god.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In the same quarters were possessions of the chief man of the island, whose name was Publius; who received us, and lodged us three days courteously.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And it came to pass, that the father of Publius lay sick of a fever and of a bloody flux: to whom Paul entered in, and prayed, and laid his hands on him, and healed him.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> So when this was done, others also, which had diseases in the island, came, and were healed:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who also honoured us with many honours; and when we departed, they laded us with such things as were necessary.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And after three months we departed in a ship of Alexandria, which had wintered in the isle, whose sign was Castor and Pollux.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And landing at Syracuse, we tarried there three days.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And from thence we fetched a compass, and came to Rhegium: and after one day the south wind blew, and we came the next day to Puteoli:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Where we found brethren, and were desired to tarry with them seven days: and so we went toward Rome.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And from thence, when the brethren heard of us, they came to meet us as far as Appii forum, and The three taverns: whom when Paul saw, he thanked God, and took courage.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And when we came to Rome, the centurion delivered the prisoners to the captain of the guard: but Paul was suffered to dwell by himself with a soldier that kept him.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And it came to pass, that after three days Paul called the chief of the Jews together: and when they were come together, he said unto them, Men and brethren, though I have committed nothing against the people, or customs of our fathers, yet was I delivered prisoner from Jerusalem into the hands of the Romans.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Who, when they had examined me, would have let me go, because there was no cause of death in me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But when the Jews spake against it, I was constrained to appeal unto Cesar; not that I had ought to accuse my nation of.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For this cause therefore have I called for you, to see you, and to speak with you: because that for the hope of Israel I am bound with this chain.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And they said unto him, We neither received letters out of Judea concerning thee, neither any of the brethren that came shewed or spake any harm of thee.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But we desire to hear of thee what thou thinkest: for as concerning this sect, we know that every where it is spoken against.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And when they had appointed him a day, there came many to him into his lodging; to whom he expounded and testified the kingdom of God, persuading them concerning Jesus, both out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets, from morning till evening.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And some believed the things which were spoken, and some believed not.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And when they agreed not among themselves, they departed, after that Paul had spoken one word, Well spake the Holy Ghost by Esaias the prophet unto our fathers,

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Saying, Go unto this people, and say, Hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and not perceive:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For the heart of this people is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes have they closed; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Be it known therefore unto you, that the salvation of God is sent unto the Gentiles, and that they will hear it.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And when he had said these words, the Jews departed, and had great reasoning among themselves.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And Paul dwelt two whole years in his own hired house, and received all that came in unto him,

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Preaching the kingdom of God, and teaching those things which concern the Lord Jesus Christ, with all confidence, no man forbidding him.
