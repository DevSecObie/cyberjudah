<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And in those days, when the number of the disciples was multiplied, there arose a murmuring of the Grecians against the Hebrews, because their widows were neglected in the daily ministration.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then the twelve called the multitude of the disciples unto them, and said, It is not reason that we should leave the word of God, and serve tables.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Wherefore, brethren, look ye out among you seven men of honest report, full of the Holy Ghost and wisdom, whom we may appoint over this business.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But we will give ourselves continually to prayer, and to the ministry of the word.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the saying pleased the whole multitude: and they chose Stephen, a man full of faith and of the Holy Ghost, and Philip, and Prochorus, and Nicanor, and Timon, and Parmenas, and Nicolas a proselyte of Antioch:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Whom they set before the apostles: and when they had prayed, they laid their hands on them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the word of God increased; and the number of the disciples multiplied in Jerusalem greatly; and a great company of the priests were obedient to the faith.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Stephen, full of faith and power, did great wonders and miracles among the people.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then there arose certain of the synagogue, which is called the synagogue of the Libertines, and Cyrenians, and Alexandrians, and of them of Cilicia and of Asia, disputing with Stephen.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And they were not able to resist the wisdom and the spirit by which he spake.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Then they suborned men, which said, We have heard him speak blasphemous words against Moses, and against God.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And they stirred up the people, and the elders, and the scribes, and came upon him, and caught him, and brought him to the council,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And set up false witnesses, which said, This man ceaseth not to speak blasphemous words against this holy place, and the law:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For we have heard him say, that this Jesus of Nazareth shall destroy this place, and shall change the customs which Moses delivered us.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And all that sat in the council, looking stedfastly on him, saw his face as it had been the face of an angel.
