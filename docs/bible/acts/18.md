<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> After these things Paul departed from Athens, and came to Corinth;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And found a certain Jew named Aquila, born in Pontus, lately come from Italy, with his wife Priscilla; (because that Claudius had commanded all Jews to depart from Rome:) and came unto them.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And because he was of the same craft, he abode with them, and wrought: for by their occupation they were tentmakers.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And he reasoned in the synagogue every sabbath, and persuaded the Jews and the Greeks.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And when Silas and Timotheus were come from Macedonia, Paul was pressed in the spirit, and testified to the Jews that Jesus was Christ.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And when they opposed themselves, and blasphemed, he shook his raiment, and said unto them, Your blood be upon your own heads; I am clean: from henceforth I will go unto the Gentiles.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And he departed thence, and entered into a certain man’s house, named Justus, one that worshipped God, whose house joined hard to the synagogue.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Crispus, the chief ruler of the synagogue, believed on the Lord with all his house; and many of the Corinthians hearing believed, and were baptized.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then spake the Lord to Paul in the night by a vision, Be not afraid, but speak, and hold not thy peace:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For I am with thee, and no man shall set on thee to hurt thee: for I have much people in this city.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And he continued there a year and six months, teaching the word of God among them.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And when Gallio was the deputy of Achaia, the Jews made insurrection with one accord against Paul, and brought him to the judgment seat,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Saying, This fellow persuadeth men to worship God contrary to the law.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And when Paul was now about to open his mouth, Gallio said unto the Jews, If it were a matter of wrong or wicked lewdness, O ye Jews, reason would that I should bear with you:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But if it be a question of words and names, and of your law, look ye to it; for I will be no judge of such matters.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And he drave them from the judgment seat.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Then all the Greeks took Sosthenes, the chief ruler of the synagogue, and beat him before the judgment seat. And Gallio cared for none of those things.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Paul after this tarried there yet a good while, and then took his leave of the brethren, and sailed thence into Syria, and with him Priscilla and Aquila; having shorn his head in Cenchrea: for he had a vow.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And he came to Ephesus, and left them there: but he himself entered into the synagogue, and reasoned with the Jews.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> When they desired him to tarry longer time with them, he consented not;

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But bade them farewell, saying, I must by all means keep this feast that cometh in Jerusalem: but I will return again unto you, if God will. And he sailed from Ephesus.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And when he had landed at Cesarea, and gone up, and saluted the church, he went down to Antioch.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And after he had spent some time there, he departed, and went over all the country of Galatia and Phrygia in order, strengthening all the disciples.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And a certain Jew named Apollos, born at Alexandria, an eloquent man, and mighty in the scriptures, came to Ephesus.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> This man was instructed in the way of the Lord; and being fervent in the spirit, he spake and taught diligently the things of the Lord, knowing only the baptism of John.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And he began to speak boldly in the synagogue: whom when Aquila and Priscilla had heard, they took him unto them, and expounded unto him the way of God more perfectly.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And when he was disposed to pass into Achaia, the brethren wrote, exhorting the disciples to receive him: who, when he was come, helped them much which had believed through grace:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For he mightily convinced the Jews, and that publickly, shewing by the scriptures that Jesus was Christ.
