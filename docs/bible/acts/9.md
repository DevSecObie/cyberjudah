<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And Saul, yet breathing out threatenings and slaughter against the disciples of the Lord, went unto the high priest,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And desired of him letters to Damascus to the synagogues, that if he found any of this way, whether they were men or women, he might bring them bound unto Jerusalem.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And as he journeyed, he came near Damascus: and suddenly there shined round about him a light from heaven:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And he fell to the earth, and heard a voice saying unto him, Saul, Saul, why persecutest thou me?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And he said, Who art thou, Lord? And the Lord said, I am Jesus whom thou persecutest: it is hard for thee to kick against the pricks.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And he trembling and astonished said, Lord, what wilt thou have me to do? And the Lord said unto him, Arise, and go into the city, and it shall be told thee what thou must do.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the men which journeyed with him stood speechless, hearing a voice, but seeing no man.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Saul arose from the earth; and when his eyes were opened, he saw no man: but they led him by the hand, and brought him into Damascus.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And he was three days without sight, and neither did eat nor drink.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And there was a certain disciple at Damascus, named Ananias; and to him said the Lord in a vision, Ananias. And he said, Behold, I am here, Lord.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And the Lord said unto him, Arise, and go into the street which is called Straight, and enquire in the house of Judas for one called Saul, of Tarsus: for, behold, he prayeth,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And hath seen in a vision a man named Ananias coming in, and putting his hand on him, that he might receive his sight.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Then Ananias answered, Lord, I have heard by many of this man, how much evil he hath done to thy saints at Jerusalem:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And here he hath authority from the chief priests to bind all that call on thy name.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But the Lord said unto him, Go thy way: for he is a chosen vessel unto me, to bear my name before the Gentiles, and kings, and the children of Israel:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For I will shew him how great things he must suffer for my name’s sake.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Ananias went his way, and entered into the house; and putting his hands on him said, Brother Saul, the Lord, even Jesus, that appeared unto thee in the way as thou camest, hath sent me, that thou mightest receive thy sight, and be filled with the Holy Ghost.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And immediately there fell from his eyes as it had been scales: and he received sight forthwith, and arose, and was baptized.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And when he had received meat, he was strengthened. Then was Saul certain days with the disciples which were at Damascus.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And straightway he preached Christ in the synagogues, that he is the Son of God.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But all that heard him were amazed, and said; Is not this he that destroyed them which called on this name in Jerusalem, and came hither for that intent, that he might bring them bound unto the chief priests?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But Saul increased the more in strength, and confounded the Jews which dwelt at Damascus, proving that this is very Christ.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And after that many days were fulfilled, the Jews took counsel to kill him:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But their laying await was known of Saul. And they watched the gates day and night to kill him.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Then the disciples took him by night, and let him down by the wall in a basket.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And when Saul was come to Jerusalem, he assayed to join himself to the disciples: but they were all afraid of him, and believed not that he was a disciple.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> But Barnabas took him, and brought him to the apostles, and declared unto them how he had seen the Lord in the way, and that he had spoken to him, and how he had preached boldly at Damascus in the name of Jesus.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And he was with them coming in and going out at Jerusalem.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And he spake boldly in the name of the Lord Jesus, and disputed against the Grecians: but they went about to slay him.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Which when the brethren knew, they brought him down to Cesarea, and sent him forth to Tarsus.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Then had the churches rest throughout all Judea and Galilee and Samaria, and were edified; and walking in the fear of the Lord, and in the comfort of the Holy Ghost, were multiplied.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And it came to pass, as Peter passed throughout all quarters, he came down also to the saints which dwelt at Lydda.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And there he found a certain man named Eneas, which had kept his bed eight years, and was sick of the palsy.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And Peter said unto him, Eneas, Jesus Christ maketh thee whole: arise, and make thy bed. And he arose immediately.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And all that dwelt at Lydda and Saron saw him, and turned to the Lord.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Now there was at Joppa a certain disciple named Tabitha, which by interpretation is called Dorcas: this woman was full of good works and almsdeeds which she did.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> And it came to pass in those days, that she was sick, and died: whom when they had washed, they laid her in an upper chamber.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And forasmuch as Lydda was nigh to Joppa, and the disciples had heard that Peter was there, they sent unto him two men, desiring him that he would not delay to come to them.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Then Peter arose and went with them. When he was come, they brought him into the upper chamber: and all the widows stood by him weeping, and shewing the coats and garments which Dorcas made, while she was with them.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> But Peter put them all forth, and kneeled down, and prayed; and turning him to the body said, Tabitha, arise. And she opened her eyes: and when she saw Peter, she sat up.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And he gave her his hand, and lifted her up, and when he had called the saints and widows, presented her alive.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> And it was known throughout all Joppa; and many believed in the Lord.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And it came to pass, that he tarried many days in Joppa with one Simon a tanner.
