<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Masters, give unto your servants that which is just and equal; knowing that ye also have a Master in heaven.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Continue in prayer, and watch in the same with thanksgiving;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Withal praying also for us, that God would open unto us a door of utterance, to speak the mystery of Christ, for which I am also in bonds:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> That I may make it manifest, as I ought to speak.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Let your speech be alway with grace, seasoned with salt, that ye may know how ye ought to answer every man.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> All my state shall Tychicus declare unto you, who is a beloved brother, and a faithful minister and fellowservant in the Lord:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Whom I have sent unto you for the same purpose, that he might know your estate, and comfort your hearts;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> With Onesimus, a faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They shall make known unto you all things which are done here.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Aristarchus my fellowprisoner saluteth you, and Marcus, sister’s son to Barnabas, (touching whom ye received commandments: if he come unto you, receive him;)

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And Jesus, which is called Justus, who are of the circumcision. These only are my fellowworkers unto the kingdom of God, which have been a comfort unto me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ, saluteth you, always labouring fervently for you in prayers, that ye may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For I bear him record, that he hath a great zeal for you, and them that are in Laodicea, and them in Hierapolis.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Luke, the beloved physician, and Demas, greet you.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Salute the brethren which are in Laodicea, and Nymphas, and the church which is in his house.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And when this epistle is read among you, cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans; and that ye likewise read the epistle from Laodicea.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And say to Archippus, Take heed to the ministry which thou hast received in the Lord, that thou fulfil it.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The salutation by the hand of me Paul. Remember my bonds. Grace be with you. Amen. Written from Rome to the Colossians by Tychicus and Onesimus.
