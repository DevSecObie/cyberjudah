<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> If ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ sitteth on the right hand of God.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Set your affection on things above, not on things on the earth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For ye are dead, and your life is hid with Christ in God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> When Christ, who is our life, shall appear, then shall ye also appear with him in glory.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For which things’ sake the wrath of God cometh on the children of disobedience:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In the which ye also walked some time, when ye lived in them.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And have put on the new man, which is renewed in knowledge after the image of him that created him:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Where there is neither Greek nor Jew, circumcision nor uncircumcision, Barbarian, Scythian, bond nor free: but Christ is all, and in all.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Put on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom; teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And whatsoever ye do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving thanks to God and the Father by him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as it is fit in the Lord.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Children, obey your parents in all things: for this is well pleasing unto the Lord.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Fathers, provoke not your children to anger, lest they be discouraged.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Servants, obey in all things your masters according to the flesh; not with eyeservice, as menpleasers; but in singleness of heart, fearing God:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men;

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Knowing that of the Lord ye shall receive the reward of the inheritance: for ye serve the Lord Christ.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> But he that doeth wrong shall receive for the wrong which he hath done: and there is no respect of persons.
