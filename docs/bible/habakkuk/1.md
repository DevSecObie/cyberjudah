<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The burden which Habakkuk the prophet did see.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O Lord, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! even cry out unto thee of violence, and thou wilt not save!

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Why dost thou shew me iniquity, and cause me to behold grievance? for spoiling and violence are before me: and there are that raise up strife and contention.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Therefore the law is slacked, and judgment doth never go forth: for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore wrong judgment proceedeth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Behold ye among the heathen, and regard, and wonder marvellously: for I will work a work in your days, which ye will not believe, though it be told you.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For, lo, I raise up the Chaldeans, that bitter and hasty nation, which shall march through the breadth of the land, to possess the dwellingplaces that are not theirs.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They are terrible and dreadful: their judgment and their dignity shall proceed of themselves.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Their horses also are swifter than the leopards, and are more fierce than the evening wolves: and their horsemen shall spread themselves, and their horsemen shall come from far; they shall fly as the eagle that hasteth to eat.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> They shall come all for violence: their faces shall sup up as the east wind, and they shall gather the captivity as the sand.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And they shall scoff at the kings, and the princes shall be a scorn unto them: they shall deride every strong hold; for they shall heap dust, and take it.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Then shall his mind change, and he shall pass over, and offend, imputing this his power unto his god.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Art thou not from everlasting, O Lord my God, mine Holy One? we shall not die. O Lord, thou hast ordained them for judgment; and, O mighty God, thou hast established them for correction.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou art of purer eyes than to behold evil, and canst not look on iniquity: wherefore lookest thou upon them that deal treacherously, and holdest thy tongue when the wicked devoureth the man that is more righteous than he?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And makest men as the fishes of the sea, as the creeping things, that have no ruler over them?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> They take up all of them with the angle, they catch them in their net, and gather them in their drag: therefore they rejoice and are glad.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Therefore they sacrifice unto their net, and burn incense unto their drag; because by them their portion is fat, and their meat plenteous.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Shall they therefore empty their net, and not spare continually to slay the nations?
