<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Finally, my brethren, rejoice in the Lord. To write the same things to you, to me indeed is not grievous, but for you it is safe.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Beware of dogs, beware of evil workers, beware of the concision.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For we are the circumcision, which worship God in the spirit, and rejoice in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Though I might also have confidence in the flesh. If any other man thinketh that he hath whereof he might trust in the flesh, I more:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Circumcised the eighth day, of the stock of Israel, of the tribe of Benjamin, an Hebrew of the Hebrews; as touching the law, a Pharisee;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Concerning zeal, persecuting the church; touching the righteousness which is in the law, blameless.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But what things were gain to me, those I counted loss for Christ.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Yea doubtless, and I count all things but loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and do count them but dung, that I may win Christ,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And be found in him, not having mine own righteousness, which is of the law, but that which is through the faith of Christ, the righteousness which is of God by faith:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect: but I follow after, if that I may apprehend that for which also I am apprehended of Christ Jesus.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Let us therefore, as many as be perfect, be thus minded: and if in any thing ye be otherwise minded, God shall reveal even this unto you.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained, let us walk by the same rule, let us mind the same thing.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Brethren, be followers together of me, and mark them which walk so as ye have us for an ensample.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> (For many walk, of whom I have told you often, and now tell you even weeping, that they are the enemies of the cross of Christ:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Whose end is destruction, whose God is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things.)

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For our conversation is in heaven; from whence also we look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Who shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body, according to the working whereby he is able even to subdue all things unto himself.
