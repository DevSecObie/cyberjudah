<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> If there be therefore any consolation in Christ, if any comfort of love, if any fellowship of the Spirit, if any bowels and mercies,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Fulfil ye my joy, that ye be likeminded, having the same love, being of one accord, of one mind.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Do all things without murmurings and disputings:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> That ye may be blameless and harmless, the sons of God, without rebuke, in the midst of a crooked and perverse nation, among whom ye shine as lights in the world;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Holding forth the word of life; that I may rejoice in the day of Christ, that I have not run in vain, neither laboured in vain.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Yea, and if I be offered upon the sacrifice and service of your faith, I joy, and rejoice with you all.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For the same cause also do ye joy, and rejoice with me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But I trust in the Lord Jesus to send Timotheus shortly unto you, that I also may be of good comfort, when I know your state.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For I have no man likeminded, who will naturally care for your state.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For all seek their own, not the things which are Jesus Christ’s.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But ye know the proof of him, that, as a son with the father, he hath served with me in the gospel.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Him therefore I hope to send presently, so soon as I shall see how it will go with me.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But I trust in the Lord that I also myself shall come shortly.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Yet I supposed it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother, and companion in labour, and fellowsoldier, but your messenger, and he that ministered to my wants.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For he longed after you all, and was full of heaviness, because that ye had heard that he had been sick.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him; and not on him only, but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> I sent him therefore the more carefully, that, when ye see him again, ye may rejoice, and that I may be the less sorrowful.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness; and hold such in reputation:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death, not regarding his life, to supply your lack of service toward me.
