<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Paul and Timotheus, the servants of Jesus Christ, to all the saints in Christ Jesus which are at Philippi, with the bishops and deacons:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I thank my God upon every remembrance of you,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Always in every prayer of mine for you all making request with joy,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For your fellowship in the gospel from the first day until now;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Being confident of this very thing, that he which hath begun a good work in you will perform it until the day of Jesus Christ:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Even as it is meet for me to think this of you all, because I have you in my heart; inasmuch as both in my bonds, and in the defence and confirmation of the gospel, ye all are partakers of my grace.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For God is my record, how greatly I long after you all in the bowels of Jesus Christ.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And this I pray, that your love may abound yet more and more in knowledge and in all judgment;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> That ye may approve things that are excellent; that ye may be sincere and without offence till the day of Christ;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Being filled with the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ, unto the glory and praise of God.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But I would ye should understand, brethren, that the things which happened unto me have fallen out rather unto the furtherance of the gospel;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> So that my bonds in Christ are manifest in all the palace, and in all other places;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And many of the brethren in the Lord, waxing confident by my bonds, are much more bold to speak the word without fear.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Some indeed preach Christ even of envy and strife; and some also of good will:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The one preach Christ of contention, not sincerely, supposing to add affliction to my bonds:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> But the other of love, knowing that I am set for the defence of the gospel.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> What then? notwithstanding, every way, whether in pretence, or in truth, Christ is preached; and I therein do rejoice, yea, and will rejoice.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For I know that this shall turn to my salvation through your prayer, and the supply of the Spirit of Jesus Christ,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> According to my earnest expectation and my hope, that in nothing I shall be ashamed, but that with all boldness, as always, so now also Christ shall be magnified in my body, whether it be by life, or by death.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For to me to live is Christ, and to die is gain.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But if I live in the flesh, this is the fruit of my labour: yet what I shall choose I wot not.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For I am in a strait betwixt two, having a desire to depart, and to be with Christ; which is far better:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Nevertheless to abide in the flesh is more needful for you.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And having this confidence, I know that I shall abide and continue with you all for your furtherance and joy of faith;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> That your rejoicing may be more abundant in Jesus Christ for me by my coming to you again.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Only let your conversation be as it becometh the gospel of Christ: that whether I come and see you, or else be absent, I may hear of your affairs, that ye stand fast in one spirit, with one mind striving together for the faith of the gospel;

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And in nothing terrified by your adversaries: which is to them an evident token of perdition, but to you of salvation, and that of God.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For unto you it is given in the behalf of Christ, not only to believe on him, but also to suffer for his sake;

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Having the same conflict which ye saw in me, and now hear to be in me.
