<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Come, and let us return unto the Lord: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the Lord: his going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of my mouth: and thy judgments are as the light that goeth forth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But they like men have transgressed the covenant: there have they dealt treacherously against me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Gilead is a city of them that work iniquity, and is polluted with blood.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And as troops of robbers wait for a man, so the company of priests murder in the way by consent: for they commit lewdness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I have seen an horrible thing in the house of Israel: there is the whoredom of Ephraim, Israel is defiled.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Also, O Judah, he hath set an harvest for thee, when I returned the captivity of my people.
