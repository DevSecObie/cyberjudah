<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind: he daily increaseth lies and desolation; and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his doings will he recompense him.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed: he wept, and made supplication unto him: he found him in Beth–el, and there he spake with us;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Even the Lord God of hosts; the Lord is his memorial.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and wait on thy God continually.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He is a merchant, the balances of deceit are in his hand: he loveth to oppress.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Ephraim said, Yet I am become rich, I have found me out substance: in all my labours they shall find none iniquity in me that were sin.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And I that am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt will yet make thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the solemn feast.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Is there iniquity in Gilead? surely they are vanity: they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal; yea, their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the fields.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And by a prophet the Lord brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Ephraim provoked him to anger most bitterly: therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him.
