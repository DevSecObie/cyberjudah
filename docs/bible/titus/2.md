<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But speak thou the things which become sound doctrine:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The aged women likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> To be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Young men likewise exhort to be sober minded.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In all things shewing thyself a pattern of good works: in doctrine shewing uncorruptness, gravity, sincerity,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Sound speech, that cannot be condemned; that he that is of the contrary part may be ashamed, having no evil thing to say of you.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Exhort servants to be obedient unto their own masters, and to please them well in all things; not answering again;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Not purloining, but shewing all good fidelity; that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Who gave himself for us, that he might redeem us from all iniquity, and purify unto himself a peculiar people, zealous of good works.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> These things speak, and exhort, and rebuke with all authority. Let no man despise thee.
