<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For this is good and acceptable in the sight of God our Saviour;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Who will have all men to be saved, and to come unto the knowledge of the truth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Who gave himself a ransom for all, to be testified in due time.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Whereunto I am ordained a preacher, and an apostle, (I speak the truth in Christ, and lie not;) a teacher of the Gentiles in faith and verity.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I will therefore that men pray every where, lifting up holy hands, without wrath and doubting.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety; not with broided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly array;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But (which becometh women professing godliness) with good works.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man, but to be in silence.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For Adam was first formed, then Eve.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived was in the transgression.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Notwithstanding she shall be saved in childbearing, if they continue in faith and charity and holiness with sobriety.
