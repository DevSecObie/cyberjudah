<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Forbidding to marry, and commanding to abstain from meats, which God hath created to be received with thanksgiving of them which believe and know the truth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> If thou put the brethren in remembrance of these things, thou shalt be a good minister of Jesus Christ, nourished up in the words of faith and of good doctrine, whereunto thou hast attained.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But refuse profane and old wives’ fables, and exercise thyself rather unto godliness.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For bodily exercise profiteth little: but godliness is profitable unto all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> This is a faithful saying and worthy of all acceptation.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For therefore we both labour and suffer reproach, because we trust in the living God, who is the Saviour of all men, specially of those that believe.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> These things command and teach.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Let no man despise thy youth; but be thou an example of the believers, in word, in conversation, in charity, in spirit, in faith, in purity.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Till I come, give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Neglect not the gift that is in thee, which was given thee by prophecy, with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them; that thy profiting may appear to all.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Take heed unto thyself, and unto the doctrine; continue in them: for in doing this thou shalt both save thyself, and them that hear thee.
