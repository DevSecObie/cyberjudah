<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The fear of a king is as the roaring of a lion: whoso provoketh him to anger sinneth against his own soul.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Counsel in the heart of man is like deep water; but a man of understanding will draw it out.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Most men will proclaim every one his own goodness: but a faithful man who can find?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The just man walketh in his integrity: his children are blessed after him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> A king that sitteth in the throne of judgment scattereth away all evil with his eyes.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Who can say, I have made my heart clean, I am pure from my sin?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Divers weights, and divers measures, both of them are alike abomination to the Lord.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Even a child is known by his doings, whether his work be pure, and whether it be right.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The hearing ear, and the seeing eye, the Lord hath made even both of them.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, and thou shalt be satisfied with bread.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> It is naught, it is naught, saith the buyer: but when he is gone his way, then he boasteth.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> There is gold, and a multitude of rubies: but the lips of knowledge are a precious jewel.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Take his garment that is surety for a stranger: and take a pledge of him for a strange woman.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Bread of deceit is sweet to a man; but afterwards his mouth shall be filled with gravel.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Every purpose is established by counsel: and with good advice make war.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Whoso curseth his father or his mother, his lamp shall be put out in obscure darkness.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> An inheritance may be gotten hastily at the beginning; but the end thereof shall not be blessed.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Say not thou, I will recompense evil; but wait on the Lord, and he shall save thee.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Divers weights are an abomination unto the Lord; and a false balance is not good.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Man’s goings are of the Lord; how can a man then understand his own way?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> It is a snare to the man who devoureth that which is holy, and after vows to make enquiry.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> A wise king scattereth the wicked, and bringeth the wheel over them.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> The spirit of man is the candle of the Lord, searching all the inward parts of the belly.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Mercy and truth preserve the king: and his throne is upholden by mercy.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> The glory of young men is their strength: and the beauty of old men is the gray head.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> The blueness of a wound cleanseth away evil: so do stripes the inward parts of the belly.
