<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> A false balance is abomination to the Lord: but a just weight is his delight.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The integrity of the upright shall guide them: but the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The righteousness of the perfect shall direct his way: but the wicked shall fall by his own wickedness.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The righteousness of the upright shall deliver them: but transgressors shall be taken in their own naughtiness.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> When a wicked man dieth, his expectation shall perish: and the hope of unjust men perisheth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked cometh in his stead.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> An hypocrite with his mouth destroyeth his neighbour: but through knowledge shall the just be delivered.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> When it goeth well with the righteous, the city rejoiceth: and when the wicked perish, there is shouting.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted: but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding holdeth his peace.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He that is surety for a stranger shall smart for it: and he that hateth suretiship is sure.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> A gracious woman retaineth honour: and strong men retain riches.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The merciful man doeth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel troubleth his own flesh.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The wicked worketh a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness shall be a sure reward.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> As righteousness tendeth to life: so he that pursueth evil pursueth it to his own death.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> They that are of a froward heart are abomination to the Lord: but such as are upright in their way are his delight.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Though hand join in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished: but the seed of the righteous shall be delivered.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> As a jewel of gold in a swine’s snout, so is a fair woman which is without discretion.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The desire of the righteous is only good: but the expectation of the wicked is wrath.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The liberal soul shall be made fat: and he that watereth shall be watered also himself.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him: but blessing shall be upon the head of him that selleth it.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He that diligently seeketh good procureth favour: but he that seeketh mischief, it shall come unto him.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the righteous shall flourish as a branch.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> He that troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: and the fool shall be servant to the wise of heart.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls is wise.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Behold, the righteous shall be recompensed in the earth: much more the wicked and the sinner.
