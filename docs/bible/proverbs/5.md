<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> That thou mayest regard discretion, and that thy lips may keep knowledge.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For the lips of a strange woman drop as an honeycomb, and her mouth is smoother than oil:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a twoedged sword.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on hell.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, that thou canst not know them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Hear me now therefore, O ye children, and depart not from the words of my mouth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Lest thou give thine honour unto others, and thy years unto the cruel:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth; and thy labours be in the house of a stranger;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And thou mourn at the last, when thy flesh and thy body are consumed,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me!

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Drink waters out of thine own cistern, and running waters out of thine own well.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad, and rivers of waters in the streets.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Let them be only thine own, and not strangers’ with thee.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe; let her breasts satisfy thee at all times; and be thou ravished always with her love.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For the ways of man are before the eyes of the Lord, and he pondereth all his goings.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> His own iniquities shall take the wicked himself, and he shall be holden with the cords of his sins.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> He shall die without instruction; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.
