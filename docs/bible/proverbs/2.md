<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and hide my commandments with thee;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom, and apply thine heart to understanding;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Yea, if thou criest after knowledge, and liftest up thy voice for understanding;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> If thou seekest her as silver, and searchest for her as for hid treasures;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and find the knowledge of God.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For the Lord giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous: he is a buckler to them that walk uprightly.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He keepeth the paths of judgment, and preserveth the way of his saints.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then shalt thou understand righteousness, and judgment, and equity; yea, every good path.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> When wisdom entereth into thine heart, and knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Discretion shall preserve thee, understanding shall keep thee:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> To deliver thee from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh froward things;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Who rejoice to do evil, and delight in the frowardness of the wicked;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Whose ways are crooked, and they froward in their paths:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> To deliver thee from the strange woman, even from the stranger which flattereth with her words;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Which forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth the covenant of her God.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For her house inclineth unto death, and her paths unto the dead.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> None that go unto her return again, neither take they hold of the paths of life.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> That thou mayest walk in the way of good men, and keep the paths of the righteous.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For the upright shall dwell in the land, and the perfect shall remain in it.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth, and the transgressors shall be rooted out of it.
