<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Every wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish plucketh it down with her hands.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He that walketh in his uprightness feareth the Lord: but he that is perverse in his ways despiseth him.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> In the mouth of the foolish is a rod of pride: but the lips of the wise shall preserve them.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Where no oxen are, the crib is clean: but much increase is by the strength of the ox.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A faithful witness will not lie: but a false witness will utter lies.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not: but knowledge is easy unto him that understandeth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Go from the presence of a foolish man, when thou perceivest not in him the lips of knowledge.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The wisdom of the prudent is to understand his way: but the folly of fools is deceit.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Fools make a mock at sin: but among the righteous there is favour.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The heart knoweth his own bitterness; and a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The house of the wicked shall be overthrown: but the tabernacle of the upright shall flourish.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the end of that mirth is heaviness.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The backslider in heart shall be filled with his own ways: and a good man shall be satisfied from himself.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The simple believeth every word: but the prudent man looketh well to his going.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is confident.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly: and a man of wicked devices is hated.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The simple inherit folly: but the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The evil bow before the good; and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The poor is hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich hath many friends.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Do they not err that devise evil? but mercy and truth shall be to them that devise good.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The crown of the wise is their riches: but the foolishness of fools is folly.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> A true witness delivereth souls: but a deceitful witness speaketh lies.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> In the fear of the Lord is strong confidence: and his children shall have a place of refuge.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> The fear of the Lord is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> In the multitude of people is the king’s honour: but in the want of people is the destruction of the prince.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> The wicked is driven away in his wickedness: but the righteous hath hope in his death.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Wisdom resteth in the heart of him that hath understanding: but that which is in the midst of fools is made known.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> The king’s favour is toward a wise servant: but his wrath is against him that causeth shame.
