<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> When the righteous are in authority, the people rejoice: but when the wicked beareth rule, the people mourn.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Whoso loveth wisdom rejoiceth his father: but he that keepeth company with harlots spendeth his substance.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The king by judgment establisheth the land: but he that receiveth gifts overthroweth it.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A man that flattereth his neighbour spreadeth a net for his feet.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> In the transgression of an evil man there is a snare: but the righteous doth sing and rejoice.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The righteous considereth the cause of the poor: but the wicked regardeth not to know it.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Scornful men bring a city into a snare: but wise men turn away wrath.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> If a wise man contendeth with a foolish man, whether he rage or laugh, there is no rest.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The bloodthirsty hate the upright: but the just seek his soul.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> A fool uttereth all his mind: but a wise man keepeth it in till afterwards.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If a ruler hearken to lies, all his servants are wicked.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The poor and the deceitful man meet together: the Lord lighteneth both their eyes.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The king that faithfully judgeth the poor, his throne shall be established for ever.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The rod and reproof give wisdom: but a child left to himself bringeth his mother to shame.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> When the wicked are multiplied, transgression increaseth: but the righteous shall see their fall.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Correct thy son, and he shall give thee rest; yea, he shall give delight unto thy soul.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Where there is no vision, the people perish: but he that keepeth the law, happy is he.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> A servant will not be corrected by words: for though he understand he will not answer.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Seest thou a man that is hasty in his words? there is more hope of a fool than of him.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He that delicately bringeth up his servant from a child shall have him become his son at the length.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> An angry man stirreth up strife, and a furious man aboundeth in transgression.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> A man’s pride shall bring him low: but honour shall uphold the humble in spirit.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Whoso is partner with a thief hateth his own soul: he heareth cursing, and bewrayeth it not.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The fear of man bringeth a snare: but whoso putteth his trust in the Lord shall be safe.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Many seek the ruler’s favour; but every man’s judgment cometh from the Lord.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> An unjust man is an abomination to the just: and he that is upright in the way is abomination to the wicked.
