<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> To know wisdom and instruction; to perceive the words of understanding;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> To receive the instruction of wisdom, justice, and judgment, and equity;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> To give subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> To understand a proverb, and the interpretation; the words of the wise, and their dark sayings.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For they shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> My son, if sinners entice thee, consent thou not.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Let us swallow them up alive as the grave; and whole, as those that go down into the pit:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoil:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Cast in thy lot among us; let us all have one purse:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> My son, walk not thou in the way with them; refrain thy foot from their path:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Surely in vain the net is spread in the sight of any bird.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And they lay wait for their own blood; they lurk privily for their own lives.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> So are the ways of every one that is greedy of gain; which taketh away the life of the owners thereof.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Wisdom crieth without; she uttereth her voice in the streets:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> She crieth in the chief place of concourse, in the openings of the gates: in the city she uttereth her words, saying,

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity? and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you, I will make known my words unto you.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded;

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your fear cometh;

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> When your fear cometh as desolation, and your destruction cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish cometh upon you.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Then shall they call upon me, but I will not answer; they shall seek me early, but they shall not find me:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For that they hated knowledge, and did not choose the fear of the Lord:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> They would none of my counsel: they despised all my reproof.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely, and shall be quiet from fear of evil.
