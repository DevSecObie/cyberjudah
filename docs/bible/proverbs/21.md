<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The king’s heart is in the hand of the Lord, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the Lord pondereth the hearts.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the Lord than sacrifice.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The way of man is froward and strange: but as for the pure, his work is right.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the Lord.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the Lord.
