<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The words of king Lemuel, the prophecy that his mother taught him.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> What, my son? and what, the son of my womb? and what, the son of my vows?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Give not thy strength unto women, nor thy ways to that which destroyeth kings.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> It is not for kings, O Lemuel, it is not for kings to drink wine; nor for princes strong drink:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Lest they drink, and forget the law, and pervert the judgment of any of the afflicted.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Give strong drink unto him that is ready to perish, and wine unto those that be of heavy hearts.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Let him drink, and forget his poverty, and remember his misery no more.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Open thy mouth for the dumb in the cause of all such as are appointed to destruction.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Open thy mouth, judge righteously, and plead the cause of the poor and needy.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The heart of her husband doth safely trust in her, so that he shall have no need of spoil.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> She will do him good and not evil all the days of her life.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> She seeketh wool, and flax, and worketh willingly with her hands.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> She is like the merchants’ ships; she bringeth her food from afar.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> She riseth also while it is yet night, and giveth meat to her household, and a portion to her maidens.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> She considereth a field, and buyeth it: with the fruit of her hands she planteth a vineyard.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> She girdeth her loins with strength, and strengtheneth her arms.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> She perceiveth that her merchandise is good: her candle goeth not out by night.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> She layeth her hands to the spindle, and her hands hold the distaff.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> She stretcheth out her hand to the poor; yea, she reacheth forth her hands to the needy.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> She is not afraid of the snow for her household: for all her household are clothed with scarlet.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> She maketh herself coverings of tapestry; her clothing is silk and purple.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Her husband is known in the gates, when he sitteth among the elders of the land.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> She maketh fine linen, and selleth it; and delivereth girdles unto the merchant.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Strength and honour are her clothing; and she shall rejoice in time to come.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> She openeth her mouth with wisdom; and in her tongue is the law of kindness.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> She looketh well to the ways of her household, and eateth not the bread of idleness.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Her children arise up, and call her blessed; her husband also, and he praiseth her.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Many daughters have done virtuously, but thou excellest them all.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Favour is deceitful, and beauty is vain: but a woman that feareth the Lord, she shall be praised.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Give her of the fruit of her hands; and let her own works praise her in the gates.
