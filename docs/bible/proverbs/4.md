<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For I was my father’s son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Get wisdom, get understanding: forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Forsake her not, and she shall preserve thee: love her, and she shall keep thee.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace: a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go: keep her; for she is thy life.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For they sleep not, except they have done mischief; and their sleep is taken away, unless they cause some to fall.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For they eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thine heart.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.
