<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his lips, and is a fool.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Also, that the soul be without knowledge, it is not good; and he that hasteth with his feet sinneth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The foolishness of man perverteth his way: and his heart fretteth against the Lord.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Wealth maketh many friends; but the poor is separated from his neighbour.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall not escape.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Many will intreat the favour of the prince: and every man is a friend to him that giveth gifts.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> All the brethren of the poor do hate him: how much more do his friends go far from him? he pursueth them with words, yet they are wanting to him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He that getteth wisdom loveth his own soul: he that keepeth understanding shall find good.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall perish.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Delight is not seemly for a fool; much less for a servant to have rule over princes.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The king’s wrath is as the roaring of a lion; but his favour is as dew upon the grass.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> A foolish son is the calamity of his father: and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> House and riches are the inheritance of fathers: and a prudent wife is from the Lord.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the Lord; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Chasten thy son while there is hope, and let not thy soul spare for his crying.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment: for if thou deliver him, yet thou must do it again.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> There are many devices in a man’s heart; nevertheless the counsel of the Lord, that shall stand.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The desire of a man is his kindness: and a poor man is better than a liar.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The fear of the Lord tendeth to life: and he that hath it shall abide satisfied; he shall not be visited with evil.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> A slothful man hideth his hand in his bosom, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth again.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding, and he will understand knowledge.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He that wasteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is a son that causeth shame, and bringeth reproach.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Cease, my son, to hear the instruction that causeth to err from the words of knowledge.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> An ungodly witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Judgments are prepared for scorners, and stripes for the back of fools.
