<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The tongue of the wise useth knowledge aright: but the mouth of fools poureth out foolishness.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The eyes of the Lord are in every place, beholding the evil and the good.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> A wholesome tongue is a tree of life: but perverseness therein is a breach in the spirit.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A fool despiseth his father’s instruction: but he that regardeth reproof is prudent.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> In the house of the righteous is much treasure: but in the revenues of the wicked is trouble.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The lips of the wise disperse knowledge: but the heart of the foolish doeth not so.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: but the prayer of the upright is his delight.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The way of the wicked is an abomination unto the Lord: but he loveth him that followeth after righteousness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more then the hearts of the children of men?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> A scorner loveth not one that reproveth him: neither will he go unto the wise.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> A merry heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by sorrow of the heart the spirit is broken.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The heart of him that hath understanding seeketh knowledge: but the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> All the days of the afflicted are evil: but he that is of a merry heart hath a continual feast.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Better is little with the fear of the Lord than great treasure and trouble therewith.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Better is a dinner of herbs where love is, than a stalled ox and hatred therewith.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The way of the slothful man is as an hedge of thorns: but the way of the righteous is made plain.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish man despiseth his mother.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Folly is joy to him that is destitute of wisdom: but a man of understanding walketh uprightly.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth: and a word spoken in due season, how good is it!

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The way of life is above to the wise, that he may depart from hell beneath.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The Lord will destroy the house of the proud: but he will establish the border of the widow.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The thoughts of the wicked are an abomination to the Lord: but the words of the pure are pleasant words.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; but he that hateth gifts shall live.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The heart of the righteous studieth to answer: but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> The Lord is far from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart: and a good report maketh the bones fat.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The ear that heareth the reproof of life abideth among the wise.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul: but he that heareth reproof getteth understanding.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> The fear of the Lord is the instruction of wisdom; and before honour is humility.
