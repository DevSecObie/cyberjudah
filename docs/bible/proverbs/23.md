<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before thee:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Be not desirous of his dainties: for they are deceitful meat.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Labour not to be rich: cease from thine own wisdom.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Wilt thou set thine eyes upon that which is not? for riches certainly make themselves wings; they fly away as an eagle toward heaven.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The morsel which thou hast eaten shalt thou vomit up, and lose thy sweet words.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Speak not in the ears of a fool: for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Remove not the old landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For their redeemer is mighty; he shall plead their cause with thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Apply thine heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Withhold not correction from the child: for if thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from hell.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> My son, if thine heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Yea, my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips speak right things.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Let not thine heart envy sinners: but be thou in the fear of the Lord all the day long.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For surely there is an end; and thine expectation shall not be cut off.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Hear thou, my son, and be wise, and guide thine heart in the way.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Buy the truth, and sell it not; also wisdom, and instruction, and understanding.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The father of the righteous shall greatly rejoice: and he that begetteth a wise child shall have joy of him.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Thy father and thy mother shall be glad, and she that bare thee shall rejoice.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For a whore is a deep ditch; and a strange woman is a narrow pit.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> She also lieth in wait as for a prey, and increaseth the transgressors among men.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, when it giveth his colour in the cup, when it moveth itself aright.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> At the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thine heart shall utter perverse things.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Yea, thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, or as he that lieth upon the top of a mast.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> They have stricken me, shalt thou say, and I was not sick; they have beaten me, and I felt it not: when shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.
