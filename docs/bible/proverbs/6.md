<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Deliver thyself as a roe from the hand of the hunter, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> A naughty person, a wicked man, walketh with a froward mouth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Frowardness is in his heart, he deviseth mischief continually; he soweth discord.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly; suddenly shall he be broken without remedy.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> An heart that deviseth wicked imaginations, feet that be swift in running to mischief,

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My son, keep thy father’s commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Bind them continually upon thine heart, and tie them about thy neck.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> When thou goest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and when thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> To keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Lust not after her beauty in thine heart; neither let her take thee with her eyelids.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For by means of a whorish woman a man is brought to a piece of bread: and the adulteress will hunt for the precious life.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his clothes not be burned?

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be burned?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> So he that goeth in to his neighbour’s wife; whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Men do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry;

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> But if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the substance of his house.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> But whoso committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> A wound and dishonour shall he get; and his reproach shall not be wiped away.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> For jealousy is the rage of a man: therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> He will not regard any ransom; neither will he rest content, though thou givest many gifts.
