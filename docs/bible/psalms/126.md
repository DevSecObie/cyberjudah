<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> When the Lord turned again the captivity of Zion, we were like them that dream.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then was our mouth filled with laughter, and our tongue with singing: then said they among the heathen, The Lord hath done great things for them.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The Lord hath done great things for us; whereof we are glad.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Turn again our captivity, O Lord, as the streams in the south.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They that sow in tears shall reap in joy.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He that goeth forth and weepeth, bearing precious seed, shall doubtless come again with rejoicing, bringing his sheaves with him.
