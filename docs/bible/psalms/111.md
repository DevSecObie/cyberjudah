<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. I will praise the Lord with my whole heart, in the assembly of the upright, and in the congregation.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The works of the Lord are great, sought out of all them that have pleasure therein.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> His work is honourable and glorious: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He hath made his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is gracious and full of compassion.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He hath given meat unto them that fear him: he will ever be mindful of his covenant.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He hath shewed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They stand fast for ever and ever, and are done in truth and uprightness.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He sent redemption unto his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever: holy and reverend is his name.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.
