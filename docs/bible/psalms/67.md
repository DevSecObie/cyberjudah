<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> God be merciful unto us, and bless us; and cause his face to shine upon us; Selah.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> That thy way may be known upon earth, thy saving health among all nations.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> O let the nations be glad and sing for joy: for thou shalt judge the people righteously, and govern the nations upon earth. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> God shall bless us; and all the ends of the earth shall fear him.
