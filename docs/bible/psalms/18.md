<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will love thee, O Lord, my strength.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I will call upon the Lord, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The sorrows of death compassed me, and the floods of ungodly men made me afraid.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The sorrows of hell compassed me about: the snares of death prevented me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried unto my God: he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were shaken, because he was wroth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He bowed the heavens also, and came down: and darkness was under his feet.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly: yea, he did fly upon the wings of the wind.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He made darkness his secret place; his pavilion round about him were dark waters and thick clouds of the skies.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> At the brightness that was before him his thick clouds passed, hail stones and coals of fire.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The Lord also thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice; hail stones and coals of fire.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Yea, he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Then the channels of waters were seen, and the foundations of the world were discovered at thy rebuke, O Lord, at the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of many waters.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them which hated me: for they were too strong for me.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the Lord was my stay.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He brought me forth also into a large place; he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For all his judgments were before me, and I did not put away his statutes from me.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> I was also upright before him, and I kept myself from mine iniquity.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Therefore hath the Lord recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his eyesight.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful; with an upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> With the pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt shew thyself froward.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For thou wilt save the afflicted people; but wilt bring down high looks.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For thou wilt light my candle: the Lord my God will enlighten my darkness.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the Lord is tried: he is a buckler to all those that trust in him.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> For who is God save the Lord? or who is a rock save our God?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> He maketh my feet like hinds’ feet, and setteth me upon my high places.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> He teacheth my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath holden me up, and thy gentleness hath made me great.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> I have pursued mine enemies, and overtaken them: neither did I turn again till they were consumed.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> I have wounded them that they were not able to rise: they are fallen under my feet.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> For thou hast girded me with strength unto the battle: thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies; that I might destroy them that hate me.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> They cried, but there was none to save them: even unto the Lord, but he answered them not.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> Then did I beat them small as the dust before the wind: I did cast them out as the dirt in the streets.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; and thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve me.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me: the strangers shall submit themselves unto me.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> The strangers shall fade away, and be afraid out of their close places.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> The Lord liveth; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> It is God that avengeth me, and subdueth the people under me.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> He delivereth me from mine enemies: yea, thou liftest me up above those that rise up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> Therefore will I give thanks unto thee, O Lord, among the heathen, and sing praises unto thy name.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> Great deliverance giveth he to his king; and sheweth mercy to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for evermore.
