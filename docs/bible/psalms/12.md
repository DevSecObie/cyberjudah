<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Help, Lord; for the godly man ceaseth; for the faithful fail from among the children of men.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> They speak vanity every one with his neighbour: with flattering lips and with a double heart do they speak.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The Lord shall cut off all flattering lips, and the tongue that speaketh proud things:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Who have said, With our tongue will we prevail; our lips are our own: who is lord over us?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For the oppression of the poor, for the sighing of the needy, now will I arise, saith the Lord; I will set him in safety from him that puffeth at him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The words of the Lord are pure words: as silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou shalt keep them, O Lord, thou shalt preserve them from this generation for ever.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The wicked walk on every side, when the vilest men are exalted.
