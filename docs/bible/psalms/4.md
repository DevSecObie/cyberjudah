<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness: thou hast enlarged me when I was in distress; have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O ye sons of men, how long will ye turn my glory into shame? how long will ye love vanity, and seek after leasing? Selah.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But know that the Lord hath set apart him that is godly for himself: the Lord will hear when I call unto him.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Offer the sacrifices of righteousness, and put your trust in the Lord.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> There be many that say, Who will shew us any good? Lord, lift thou up the light of thy countenance upon us.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time that their corn and their wine increased.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety.
