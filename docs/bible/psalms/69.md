<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Save me, O God; for the waters are come in unto my soul.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing: I am come into deep waters, where the floods overflow me.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I am weary of my crying: my throat is dried: mine eyes fail while I wait for my God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of mine head: they that would destroy me, being mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Let not them that wait on thee, O Lord God of hosts, be ashamed for my sake: let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake, O God of Israel.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother’s children.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For the zeal of thine house hath eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee are fallen upon me.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> When I wept, and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb to them.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They that sit in the gate speak against me; and I was the song of the drunkards.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But as for me, my prayer is unto thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Let not the waterflood overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Hear me, O Lord; for thy lovingkindness is good: turn unto me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And hide not thy face from thy servant; for I am in trouble: hear me speedily.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Draw nigh unto my soul, and redeem it: deliver me because of mine enemies.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thou hast known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour: mine adversaries are all before thee.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Reproach hath broken my heart; and I am full of heaviness: and I looked for some to take pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> They gave me also gall for my meat; and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to shake.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Pour out thine indignation upon them, and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Let their habitation be desolate; and let none dwell in their tents.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For they persecute him whom thou hast smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Add iniquity unto their iniquity: and let them not come into thy righteousness.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But I am poor and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O God, set me up on high.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> I will praise the name of God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> This also shall please the Lord better than an ox or bullock that hath horns and hoofs.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> The humble shall see this, and be glad: and your heart shall live that seek God.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> For the Lord heareth the poor, and despiseth not his prisoners.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Let the heaven and earth praise him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may dwell there, and have it in possession.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> The seed also of his servants shall inherit it: and they that love his name shall dwell therein.
