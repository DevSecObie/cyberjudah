<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Deliver me, O Lord, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They have sharpened their tongues like a serpent; adders’ poison is under their lips. Selah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Keep me, O Lord, from the hands of the wicked; preserve me from the violent man; who have purposed to overthrow my goings.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The proud have hid a snare for me, and cords; they have spread a net by the wayside; they have set gins for me. Selah.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I said unto the Lord, Thou art my God: hear the voice of my supplications, O Lord.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> O God the Lord, the strength of my salvation, thou hast covered my head in the day of battle.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Grant not, O Lord, the desires of the wicked: further not his wicked device; lest they exalt themselves. Selah.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> As for the head of those that compass me about, let the mischief of their own lips cover them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Let burning coals fall upon them: let them be cast into the fire; into deep pits, that they rise not up again.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Let not an evil speaker be established in the earth: evil shall hunt the violent man to overthrow him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I know that the Lord will maintain the cause of the afflicted, and the right of the poor.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Surely the righteous shall give thanks unto thy name: the upright shall dwell in thy presence.
