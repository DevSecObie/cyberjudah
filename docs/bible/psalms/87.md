<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> His foundation is in the holy mountains.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia; this man was born there.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And of Zion it shall be said, This and that man was born in her: and the highest himself shall establish her.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The Lord shall count, when he writeth up the people, that this man was born there. Selah.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> As well the singers as the players on instruments shall be there: all my springs are in thee.
