<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The Lord reigneth; let the people tremble: he sitteth between the cherubims; let the earth be moved.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord is great in Zion; and he is high above all the people.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let them praise thy great and terrible name; for it is holy.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The king’s strength also loveth judgment; thou dost establish equity, thou executest judgment and righteousness in Jacob.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship at his footstool; for he is holy.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among them that call upon his name; they called upon the Lord, and he answered them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He spake unto them in the cloudy pillar: they kept his testimonies, and the ordinance that he gave them.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thou answeredst them, O Lord our God: thou wast a God that forgavest them, though thou tookest vengeance of their inventions.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Exalt the Lord our God, and worship at his holy hill; for the Lord our God is holy.
