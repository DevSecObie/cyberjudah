<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed is every one that feareth the Lord; that walketh in his ways.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine by the sides of thine house: thy children like olive plants round about thy table.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The Lord shall bless thee out of Zion: and thou shalt see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Yea, thou shalt see thy children’s children, and peace upon Israel.
