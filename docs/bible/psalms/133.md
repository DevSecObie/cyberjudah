<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron’s beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> As the dew of Hermon, and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Zion: for there the Lord commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.
