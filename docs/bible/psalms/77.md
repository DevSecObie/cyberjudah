<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I cried unto God with my voice, even unto God with my voice; and he gave ear unto me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord: my sore ran in the night, and ceased not: my soul refused to be comforted.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I remembered God, and was troubled: I complained, and my spirit was overwhelmed. Selah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou holdest mine eyes waking: I am so troubled that I cannot speak.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I have considered the days of old, the years of ancient times.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I call to remembrance my song in the night: I commune with mine own heart: and my spirit made diligent search.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will he be favourable no more?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Is his mercy clean gone for ever? doth his promise fail for evermore?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Hath God forgotten to be gracious? hath he in anger shut up his tender mercies? Selah.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And I said, This is my infirmity: but I will remember the years of the right hand of the most High.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I will remember the works of the Lord: surely I will remember thy wonders of old.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will meditate also of all thy work, and talk of thy doings.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thy way, O God, is in the sanctuary: who is so great a God as our God?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou art the God that doest wonders: thou hast declared thy strength among the people.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Thou hast with thine arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee; they were afraid: the depths also were troubled.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The clouds poured out water: the skies sent out a sound: thine arrows also went abroad.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The voice of thy thunder was in the heaven: the lightnings lightened the world: the earth trembled and shook.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thy way is in the sea, and thy path in the great waters, and thy footsteps are not known.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Thou leddest thy people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.
