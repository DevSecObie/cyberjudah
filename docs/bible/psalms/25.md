<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O my God, I trust in thee: let me not be ashamed, let not mine enemies triumph over me.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Yea, let none that wait on thee be ashamed: let them be ashamed which transgress without cause.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Shew me thy ways, O Lord; teach me thy paths.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Lead me in thy truth, and teach me: for thou art the God of my salvation; on thee do I wait all the day.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Remember, O Lord, thy tender mercies and thy lovingkindnesses; for they have been ever of old.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Remember not the sins of my youth, nor my transgressions: according to thy mercy remember thou me for thy goodness’ sake, O Lord.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Good and upright is the Lord: therefore will he teach sinners in the way.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The meek will he guide in judgment: and the meek will he teach his way.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> All the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth unto such as keep his covenant and his testimonies.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For thy name’s sake, O Lord, pardon mine iniquity; for it is great.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> What man is he that feareth the Lord? him shall he teach in the way that he shall choose.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> His soul shall dwell at ease; and his seed shall inherit the earth.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The secret of the Lord is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his covenant.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Mine eyes are ever toward the Lord; for he shall pluck my feet out of the net.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Turn thee unto me, and have mercy upon me; for I am desolate and afflicted.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The troubles of my heart are enlarged: O bring thou me out of my distresses.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Look upon mine affliction and my pain; and forgive all my sins.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Consider mine enemies; for they are many; and they hate me with cruel hatred.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> O keep my soul, and deliver me: let me not be ashamed; for I put my trust in thee.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Let integrity and uprightness preserve me; for I wait on thee.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Redeem Israel, O God, out of all his troubles.
