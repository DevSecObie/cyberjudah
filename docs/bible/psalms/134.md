<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Behold, bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord, which by night stand in the house of the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the Lord.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The Lord that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.
