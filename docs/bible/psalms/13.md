<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> How long wilt thou forget me, O Lord? for ever? how long wilt thou hide thy face from me?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How long shall I take counsel in my soul, having sorrow in my heart daily? how long shall mine enemy be exalted over me?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Consider and hear me, O Lord my God: lighten mine eyes, lest I sleep the sleep of death;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Lest mine enemy say, I have prevailed against him; and those that trouble me rejoice when I am moved.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But I have trusted in thy mercy; my heart shall rejoice in thy salvation.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I will sing unto the Lord, because he hath dealt bountifully with me.
