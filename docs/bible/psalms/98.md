<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O sing unto the Lord a new song; for he hath done marvellous things: his right hand, and his holy arm, hath gotten him the victory.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord hath made known his salvation: his righteousness hath he openly shewed in the sight of the heathen.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He hath remembered his mercy and his truth toward the house of Israel: all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all the earth: make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Sing unto the Lord with the harp; with the harp, and the voice of a psalm.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> With trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the Lord, the King.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let the floods clap their hands: let the hills be joyful together

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Before the Lord; for he cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall he judge the world, and the people with equity.
