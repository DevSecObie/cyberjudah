<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And he led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Because they rebelled against the words of God, and contemned the counsel of the most High:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Therefore he brought down their heart with labour; they fell down, and there was none to help.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he saved them out of their distresses.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and brake their bands in sunder.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For he hath broken the gates of brass, and cut the bars of iron in sunder.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Their soul abhorreth all manner of meat; and they draw near unto the gates of death.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble, and he saveth them out of their distresses.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And let them sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works with rejoicing.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters;

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> These see the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up the waves thereof.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> They mount up to the heaven, they go down again to the depths: their soul is melted because of trouble.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and are at their wits’ end.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresses.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> He maketh the storm a calm, so that the waves thereof are still.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Then are they glad because they be quiet; so he bringeth them unto their desired haven.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Let them exalt him also in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the elders.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> He turneth rivers into a wilderness, and the watersprings into dry ground;

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> He turneth the wilderness into a standing water, and dry ground into watersprings.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, that they may prepare a city for habitation;

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> And sow the fields, and plant vineyards, which may yield fruits of increase.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied greatly; and suffereth not their cattle to decrease.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Again, they are minished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> He poureth contempt upon princes, and causeth them to wander in the wilderness, where there is no way.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Yet setteth he the poor on high from affliction, and maketh him families like a flock.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> The righteous shall see it, and rejoice: and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Whoso is wise, and will observe these things, even they shall understand the lovingkindness of the Lord.
