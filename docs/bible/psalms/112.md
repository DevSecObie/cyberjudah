<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Blessed is the man that feareth the Lord, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Wealth and riches shall be in his house: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness: he is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A good man sheweth favour, and lendeth: he will guide his affairs with discretion.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Surely he shall not be moved for ever: the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: his heart is fixed, trusting in the Lord.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> His heart is established, he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He hath dispersed, he hath given to the poor; his righteousness endureth for ever; his horn shall be exalted with honour.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The wicked shall see it, and be grieved; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away: the desire of the wicked shall perish.
