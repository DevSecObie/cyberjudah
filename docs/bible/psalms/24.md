<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The earth is the Lord’s, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For he hath founded it upon the seas, and established it upon the floods.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Who shall ascend into the hill of the Lord? or who shall stand in his holy place?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He shall receive the blessing from the Lord, and righteousness from the God of his salvation.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> This is the generation of them that seek him, that seek thy face, O Jacob. Selah.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Lift up your heads, O ye gates; and be ye lift up, ye everlasting doors; and the King of glory shall come in.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Who is this King of glory? The Lord strong and mighty, the Lord mighty in battle.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Lift up your heads, O ye gates; even lift them up, ye everlasting doors; and the King of glory shall come in.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who is this King of glory? The Lord of hosts, he is the King of glory. Selah.
