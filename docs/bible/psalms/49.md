<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear this, all ye people; give ear, all ye inhabitants of the world:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Both low and high, rich and poor, together.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> My mouth shall speak of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I will incline mine ear to a parable: I will open my dark saying upon the harp.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil, when the iniquity of my heels shall compass me about?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They that trust in their wealth, and boast themselves in the multitude of their riches;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> None of them can by any means redeem his brother, nor give to God a ransom for him:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> (For the redemption of their soul is precious, and it ceaseth for ever:)

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> That he should still live for ever, and not see corruption.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For he seeth that wise men die, likewise the fool and the brutish person perish, and leave their wealth to others.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Their inward thought is, that their houses shall continue for ever, and their dwelling places to all generations; they call their lands after their own names.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Nevertheless man being in honour abideth not: he is like the beasts that perish.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> This their way is their folly: yet their posterity approve their sayings. Selah.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Like sheep they are laid in the grave; death shall feed on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning; and their beauty shall consume in the grave from their dwelling.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But God will redeem my soul from the power of the grave: for he shall receive me. Selah.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Be not thou afraid when one is made rich, when the glory of his house is increased;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For when he dieth he shall carry nothing away: his glory shall not descend after him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Though while he lived he blessed his soul: and men will praise thee, when thou doest well to thyself.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He shall go to the generation of his fathers; they shall never see light.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Man that is in honour, and understandeth not, is like the beasts that perish.
