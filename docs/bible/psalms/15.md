<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He that backbiteth not with his tongue, nor doeth evil to his neighbour, nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbour.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> In whose eyes a vile person is contemned; but he honoureth them that fear the Lord. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.
