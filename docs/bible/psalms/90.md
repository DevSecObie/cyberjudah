<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, thou hast been our dwelling place in all generations.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou turnest man to destruction; and sayest, Return, ye children of men.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For a thousand years in thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep: in the morning they are like grass which groweth up.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> In the morning it flourisheth, and groweth up; in the evening it is cut down, and withereth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For we are consumed by thine anger, and by thy wrath are we troubled.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For all our days are passed away in thy wrath: we spend our years as a tale that is told.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The days of our years are threescore years and ten; and if by reason of strength they be fourscore years, yet is their strength labour and sorrow; for it is soon cut off, and we fly away.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Who knoweth the power of thine anger? even according to thy fear, so is thy wrath.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Return, O Lord, how long? and let it repent thee concerning thy servants.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> O satisfy us early with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Make us glad according to the days wherein thou hast afflicted us, and the years wherein we have seen evil.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Let thy work appear unto thy servants, and thy glory unto their children.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And let the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.
