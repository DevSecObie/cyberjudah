<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For thine arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand presseth me sore.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine anger; neither is there any rest in my bones because of my sin.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For mine iniquities are gone over mine head: as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> My wounds stink and are corrupt because of my foolishness.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For my loins are filled with a loathsome disease: and there is no soundness in my flesh.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I am feeble and sore broken: I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hid from thee.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They also that seek after my life lay snares for me: and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For in thee, O Lord, do I hope: thou wilt hear, O Lord my God.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For I said, Hear me, lest otherwise they should rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth, they magnify themselves against me.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For I am ready to halt, and my sorrow is continually before me.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For I will declare mine iniquity; I will be sorry for my sin.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But mine enemies are lively, and they are strong: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> They also that render evil for good are mine adversaries; because I follow the thing that good is.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Forsake me not, O Lord: O my God, be not far from me.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Make haste to help me, O Lord my salvation.
