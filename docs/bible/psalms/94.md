<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth; O God, to whom vengeance belongeth, shew thyself.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Lift up thyself, thou judge of the earth: render a reward to the proud.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Lord, how long shall the wicked, how long shall the wicked triumph?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> How long shall they utter and speak hard things? and all the workers of iniquity boast themselves?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They break in pieces thy people, O Lord, and afflict thine heritage.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They slay the widow and the stranger, and murder the fatherless.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Yet they say, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Understand, ye brutish among the people: and ye fools, when will ye be wise?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? he that formed the eye, shall he not see?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He that chastiseth the heathen, shall not he correct? he that teacheth man knowledge, shall not he know?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The Lord knoweth the thoughts of man, that they are vanity.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Lord, and teachest him out of thy law;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> That thou mayest give him rest from the days of adversity, until the pit be digged for the wicked.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For the Lord will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But judgment shall return unto righteousness: and all the upright in heart shall follow it.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Unless the Lord had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> When I said, My foot slippeth; thy mercy, O Lord, held me up.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by a law?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> They gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But the Lord is my defence; and my God is the rock of my refuge.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And he shall bring upon them their own iniquity, and shall cut them off in their own wickedness; yea, the Lord our God shall cut them off.
