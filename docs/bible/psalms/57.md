<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Be merciful unto me, O God, be merciful unto me: for my soul trusteth in thee: yea, in the shadow of thy wings will I make my refuge, until these calamities be overpast.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I will cry unto God most high; unto God that performeth all things for me.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He shall send from heaven, and save me from the reproach of him that would swallow me up. Selah. God shall send forth his mercy and his truth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> My soul is among lions: and I lie even among them that are set on fire, even the sons of men, whose teeth are spears and arrows, and their tongue a sharp sword.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens; let thy glory be above all the earth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They have prepared a net for my steps; my soul is bowed down: they have digged a pit before me, into the midst whereof they are fallen themselves. Selah.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> My heart is fixed, O God, my heart is fixed: I will sing and give praise.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Awake up, my glory; awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord, among the people: I will sing unto thee among the nations.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For thy mercy is great unto the heavens, and thy truth unto the clouds.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens: let thy glory be above all the earth.
