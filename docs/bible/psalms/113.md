<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise, O ye servants of the Lord, praise the name of the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and for evermore.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same the Lord’s name is to be praised.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The Lord is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who is like unto the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high,

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Who humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven, and in the earth!

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise ye the Lord.
