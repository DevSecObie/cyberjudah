<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Unto thee will I cry, O Lord my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hear the voice of my supplications, when I cry unto thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy oracle.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Draw me not away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, which speak peace to their neighbours, but mischief is in their hearts.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Give them according to their deeds, and according to the wickedness of their endeavours: give them after the work of their hands; render to them their desert.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Because they regard not the works of the Lord, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them, and not build them up.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Blessed be the Lord, because he hath heard the voice of my supplications.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Lord is their strength, and he is the saving strength of his anointed.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: feed them also, and lift them up for ever.
