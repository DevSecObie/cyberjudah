<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Why do the heathen rage, and the people imagine a vain thing?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The kings of the earth set themselves, and the rulers take counsel together, against the Lord, and against his anointed, saying,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let us break their bands asunder, and cast away their cords from us.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He that sitteth in the heavens shall laugh: the Lord shall have them in derision.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Then shall he speak unto them in his wrath, and vex them in his sore displeasure.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Yet have I set my king upon my holy hill of Zion.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I will declare the decree: the Lord hath said unto me, Thou art my Son; this day have I begotten thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Ask of me, and I shall give thee the heathen for thine inheritance, and the uttermost parts of the earth for thy possession.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Thou shalt break them with a rod of iron; thou shalt dash them in pieces like a potter’s vessel.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Be wise now therefore, O ye kings: be instructed, ye judges of the earth.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Serve the Lord with fear, and rejoice with trembling.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Kiss the Son, lest he be angry, and ye perish from the way, when his wrath is kindled but a little. Blessed are all they that put their trust in him.
