<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Behold, as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress; so our eyes wait upon the Lord our God, until that he have mercy upon us.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud.
