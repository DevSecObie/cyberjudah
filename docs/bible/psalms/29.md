<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Give unto the Lord, O ye mighty, give unto the Lord glory and strength.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name; worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The voice of the Lord is upon the waters: the God of glory thundereth: the Lord is upon many waters.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The voice of the Lord is powerful; the voice of the Lord is full of majesty.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The voice of the Lord breaketh the cedars; yea, the Lord breaketh the cedars of Lebanon.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He maketh them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young unicorn.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The voice of the Lord divideth the flames of fire.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The voice of the Lord shaketh the wilderness; the Lord shaketh the wilderness of Kadesh.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The voice of the Lord maketh the hinds to calve, and discovereth the forests: and in his temple doth every one speak of his glory.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The Lord sitteth upon the flood; yea, the Lord sitteth King for ever.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The Lord will give strength unto his people; the Lord will bless his people with peace.
