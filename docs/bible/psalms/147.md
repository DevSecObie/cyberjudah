<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord: for it is good to sing praises unto our God; for it is pleasant; and praise is comely.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord doth build up Jerusalem: he gathereth together the outcasts of Israel.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He telleth the number of the stars; he calleth them all by their names.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Great is our Lord, and of great power: his understanding is infinite.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The Lord lifteth up the meek: he casteth the wicked down to the ground.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Sing unto the Lord with thanksgiving; sing praise upon the harp unto our God:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Who covereth the heaven with clouds, who prepareth rain for the earth, who maketh grass to grow upon the mountains.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He giveth to the beast his food, and to the young ravens which cry.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He delighteth not in the strength of the horse: he taketh not pleasure in the legs of a man.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The Lord taketh pleasure in them that fear him, in those that hope in his mercy.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Praise the Lord, O Jerusalem; praise thy God, O Zion.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For he hath strengthened the bars of thy gates; he hath blessed thy children within thee.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He maketh peace in thy borders, and filleth thee with the finest of the wheat.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He sendeth forth his commandment upon earth: his word runneth very swiftly.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He giveth snow like wool: he scattereth the hoarfrost like ashes.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He casteth forth his ice like morsels: who can stand before his cold?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He sendeth out his word, and melteth them: he causeth his wind to blow, and the waters flow.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the Lord.
