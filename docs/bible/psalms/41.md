<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed is he that considereth the poor: the Lord will deliver him in time of trouble.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord will preserve him, and keep him alive; and he shall be blessed upon the earth: and thou wilt not deliver him unto the will of his enemies.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The Lord will strengthen him upon the bed of languishing: thou wilt make all his bed in his sickness.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I said, Lord, be merciful unto me: heal my soul; for I have sinned against thee.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Mine enemies speak evil of me, When shall he die, and his name perish?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And if he come to see me, he speaketh vanity: his heart gathereth iniquity to itself; when he goeth abroad, he telleth it.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> All that hate me whisper together against me: against me do they devise my hurt.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> An evil disease, say they, cleaveth fast unto him: and now that he lieth he shall rise up no more.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Yea, mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted, which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But thou, O Lord, be merciful unto me, and raise me up, that I may requite them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> By this I know that thou favourest me, because mine enemy doth not triumph over me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And as for me, thou upholdest me in mine integrity, and settest me before thy face for ever.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting, and to everlasting. Amen, and Amen.
