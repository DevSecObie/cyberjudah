<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I cried unto the Lord with my voice; with my voice unto the Lord did I make my supplication.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I poured out my complaint before him; I shewed before him my trouble.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> When my spirit was overwhelmed within me, then thou knewest my path. In the way wherein I walked have they privily laid a snare for me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I looked on my right hand, and beheld, but there was no man that would know me: refuge failed me; no man cared for my soul.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I cried unto thee, O Lord: I said, Thou art my refuge and my portion in the land of the living.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Attend unto my cry; for I am brought very low: deliver me from my persecutors; for they are stronger than I.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Bring my soul out of prison, that I may praise thy name: the righteous shall compass me about; for thou shalt deal bountifully with me.
