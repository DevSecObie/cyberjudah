<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Lord, hear my voice: let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> If thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I wait for the Lord, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.
