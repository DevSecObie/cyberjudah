<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Day unto day uttereth speech, and night unto night sheweth knowledge.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Which is as a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, and rejoiceth as a strong man to run a race.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and his circuit unto the ends of it: and there is nothing hid from the heat thereof.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The statutes of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the Lord is pure, enlightening the eyes.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The fear of the Lord is clean, enduring for ever: the judgments of the Lord are true and righteous altogether.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> More to be desired are they than gold, yea, than much fine gold: sweeter also than honey and the honeycomb.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Moreover by them is thy servant warned: and in keeping of them there is great reward.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from secret faults.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be upright, and I shall be innocent from the great transgression.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Let the words of my mouth, and the meditation of my heart, be acceptable in thy sight, O Lord, my strength, and my redeemer.
