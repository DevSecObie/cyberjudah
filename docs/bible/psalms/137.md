<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> By the rivers of Babylon, there we sat down, yea, we wept, when we remembered Zion.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> We hanged our harps upon the willows in the midst thereof.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For there they that carried us away captive required of us a song; and they that wasted us required of us mirth, saying, Sing us one of the songs of Zion.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> How shall we sing the Lord’s song in a strange land?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If I forget thee, O Jerusalem, let my right hand forget her cunning.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> If I do not remember thee, let my tongue cleave to the roof of my mouth; if I prefer not Jerusalem above my chief joy.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Remember, O Lord, the children of Edom in the day of Jerusalem; who said, Rase it, rase it, even to the foundation thereof.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> O daughter of Babylon, who art to be destroyed; happy shall he be, that rewardeth thee as thou hast served us.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Happy shall he be, that taketh and dasheth thy little ones against the stones.
