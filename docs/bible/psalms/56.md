<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Be merciful unto me, O God: for man would swallow me up; he fighting daily oppresseth me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Mine enemies would daily swallow me up: for they be many that fight against me, O thou most High.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> In God I will praise his word, in God I have put my trust; I will not fear what flesh can do unto me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Every day they wrest my words: all their thoughts are against me for evil.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They gather themselves together, they hide themselves, they mark my steps, when they wait for my soul.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Shall they escape by iniquity? in thine anger cast down the people, O God.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> When I cry unto thee, then shall mine enemies turn back: this I know; for God is for me.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> In God will I praise his word: in the Lord will I praise his word.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> In God have I put my trust: I will not be afraid what man can do unto me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Thy vows are upon me, O God: I will render praises unto thee.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For thou hast delivered my soul from death: wilt not thou deliver my feet from falling, that I may walk before God in the light of the living?
