<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will lift up mine eyes unto the hills, from whence cometh my help.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> My help cometh from the Lord, which made heaven and earth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He will not suffer thy foot to be moved: he that keepeth thee will not slumber.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Behold, he that keepeth Israel shall neither slumber nor sleep.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The Lord is thy keeper: the Lord is thy shade upon thy right hand.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The sun shall not smite thee by day, nor the moon by night.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Lord shall preserve thy going out and thy coming in from this time forth, and even for evermore.
