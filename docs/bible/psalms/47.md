<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O clap your hands, all ye people; shout unto God with the voice of triumph.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For the Lord most high is terrible; he is a great King over all the earth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He shall subdue the people under us, and the nations under our feet.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He shall choose our inheritance for us, the excellency of Jacob whom he loved. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> God is gone up with a shout, the Lord with the sound of a trumpet.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Sing praises to God, sing praises: sing praises unto our King, sing praises.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For God is the King of all the earth: sing ye praises with understanding.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> God reigneth over the heathen: God sitteth upon the throne of his holiness.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The princes of the people are gathered together, even the people of the God of Abraham: for the shields of the earth belong unto God: he is greatly exalted.
