<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, and to sing praises unto thy name, O most High:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> To shew forth thy lovingkindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Upon an instrument of ten strings, and upon the psaltery; upon the harp with a solemn sound.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thou, Lord, hast made me glad through thy work: I will triumph in the works of thy hands.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> O Lord, how great are thy works! and thy thoughts are very deep.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> A brutish man knoweth not; neither doth a fool understand this.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> When the wicked spring as the grass, and when all the workers of iniquity do flourish; it is that they shall be destroyed for ever:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But thou, Lord, art most high for evermore.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For, lo, thine enemies, O Lord, for, lo, thine enemies shall perish; all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But my horn shalt thou exalt like the horn of an unicorn: I shall be anointed with fresh oil.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Mine eye also shall see my desire on mine enemies, and mine ears shall hear my desire of the wicked that rise up against me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree: he shall grow like a cedar in Lebanon.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Those that be planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of our God.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> They shall still bring forth fruit in old age; they shall be fat and flourishing;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> To shew that the Lord is upright: he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him.
