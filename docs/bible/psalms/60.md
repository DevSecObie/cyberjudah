<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O God, thou hast cast us off, thou hast scattered us, thou hast been displeased; O turn thyself to us again.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou hast made the earth to tremble; thou hast broken it: heal the breaches thereof; for it shaketh.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou hast shewed thy people hard things: thou hast made us to drink the wine of astonishment.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou hast given a banner to them that fear thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> That thy beloved may be delivered; save with thy right hand, and hear me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Gilead is mine, and Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe: Philistia, triumph thou because of me.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Wilt not thou, O God, which hadst cast us off? and thou, O God, which didst not go out with our armies?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies.
