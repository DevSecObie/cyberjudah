<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Truly God is good to Israel, even to such as are of a clean heart.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> But as for me, my feet were almost gone; my steps had well nigh slipped.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For I was envious at the foolish, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For there are no bands in their death: but their strength is firm.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They are not in trouble as other men; neither are they plagued like other men.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Therefore pride compasseth them about as a chain; violence covereth them as a garment.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Their eyes stand out with fatness: they have more than heart could wish.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They are corrupt, and speak wickedly concerning oppression: they speak loftily.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> They set their mouth against the heavens, and their tongue walketh through the earth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Therefore his people return hither: and waters of a full cup are wrung out to them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And they say, How doth God know? and is there knowledge in the most High?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Behold, these are the ungodly, who prosper in the world; they increase in riches.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Verily I have cleansed my heart in vain, and washed my hands in innocency.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For all the day long have I been plagued, and chastened every morning.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> If I say, I will speak thus; behold, I should offend against the generation of thy children.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> When I thought to know this, it was too painful for me;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Until I went into the sanctuary of God; then understood I their end.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Surely thou didst set them in slippery places: thou castedst them down into destruction.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> How are they brought into desolation, as in a moment! they are utterly consumed with terrors.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> As a dream when one awaketh; so, O Lord, when thou awakest, thou shalt despise their image.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Thus my heart was grieved, and I was pricked in my reins.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> So foolish was I, and ignorant: I was as a beast before thee.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast holden me by my right hand.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Thou shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me to glory.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Whom have I in heaven but thee? and there is none upon earth that I desire beside thee.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For, lo, they that are far from thee shall perish: thou hast destroyed all them that go a whoring from thee.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> But it is good for me to draw near to God: I have put my trust in the Lord God, that I may declare all thy works.
