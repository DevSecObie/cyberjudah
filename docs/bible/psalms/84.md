<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> How amiable are thy tabernacles, O Lord of hosts!

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> My soul longeth, yea, even fainteth for the courts of the Lord: my heart and my flesh crieth out for the living God.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Yea, the sparrow hath found an house, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, even thine altars, O Lord of hosts, my King, and my God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: they will be still praising thee. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; in whose heart are the ways of them.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Who passing through the valley of Baca make it a well; the rain also filleth the pools.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They go from strength to strength, every one of them in Zion appeareth before God.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> O Lord God of hosts, hear my prayer: give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Behold, O God our shield, and look upon the face of thine anointed.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For a day in thy courts is better than a thousand. I had rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the Lord God is a sun and shield: the Lord will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> O Lord of hosts, blessed is the man that trusteth in thee.
