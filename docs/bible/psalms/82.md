<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> God standeth in the congregation of the mighty; he judgeth among the gods.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How long will ye judge unjustly, and accept the persons of the wicked? Selah.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Defend the poor and fatherless: do justice to the afflicted and needy.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Deliver the poor and needy: rid them out of the hand of the wicked.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They know not, neither will they understand; they walk on in darkness: all the foundations of the earth are out of course.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I have said, Ye are gods; and all of you are children of the most High.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But ye shall die like men, and fall like one of the princes.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Arise, O God, judge the earth: for thou shalt inherit all nations.
