<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise ye the Lord from the heavens: praise him in the heights.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Praise ye him, all his angels: praise ye him, all his hosts.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Praise ye him, sun and moon: praise him, all ye stars of light.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Praise him, ye heavens of heavens, and ye waters that be above the heavens.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let them praise the name of the Lord: for he commanded, and they were created.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He hath also stablished them for ever and ever: he hath made a decree which shall not pass.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Praise the Lord from the earth, ye dragons, and all deeps:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Fire, and hail; snow, and vapour; stormy wind fulfilling his word:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Mountains, and all hills; fruitful trees, and all cedars:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Beasts, and all cattle; creeping things, and flying fowl:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Kings of the earth, and all people; princes, and all judges of the earth:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Both young men, and maidens; old men, and children:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Let them praise the name of the Lord: for his name alone is excellent; his glory is above the earth and heaven.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.
