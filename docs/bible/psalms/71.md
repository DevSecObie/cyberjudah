<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In thee, O Lord, do I put my trust: let me never be put to confusion.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Deliver me in thy righteousness, and cause me to escape: incline thine ear unto me, and save me.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Be thou my strong habitation, whereunto I may continually resort: thou hast given commandment to save me; for thou art my rock and my fortress.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the wicked, out of the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For thou art my hope, O Lord God: thou art my trust from my youth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> By thee have I been holden up from the womb: thou art he that took me out of my mother’s bowels: my praise shall be continually of thee.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I am as a wonder unto many; but thou art my strong refuge.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let my mouth be filled with thy praise and with thy honour all the day.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Cast me not off in the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength faileth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For mine enemies speak against me; and they that lay wait for my soul take counsel together,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Saying, God hath forsaken him: persecute and take him; for there is none to deliver him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> O God, be not far from me: O my God, make haste for my help.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Let them be confounded and consumed that are adversaries to my soul; let them be covered with reproach and dishonour that seek my hurt.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But I will hope continually, and will yet praise thee more and more.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> My mouth shall shew forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I will go in the strength of the Lord God: I will make mention of thy righteousness, even of thine only.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> O God, thou hast taught me from my youth: and hitherto have I declared thy wondrous works.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Now also when I am old and grayheaded, O God, forsake me not; until I have shewed thy strength unto this generation, and thy power to every one that is to come.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thy righteousness also, O God, is very high, who hast done great things: O God, who is like unto thee!

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Thou, which hast shewed me great and sore troubles, shalt quicken me again, and shalt bring me up again from the depths of the earth.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Thou shalt increase my greatness, and comfort me on every side.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> I will also praise thee with the psaltery, even thy truth, O my God: unto thee will I sing with the harp, O thou Holy One of Israel.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> My lips shall greatly rejoice when I sing unto thee; and my soul, which thou hast redeemed.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> My tongue also shall talk of thy righteousness all the day long: for they are confounded, for they are brought unto shame, that seek my hurt.
