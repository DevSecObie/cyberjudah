<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> We have heard with our ears, O God, our fathers have told us, what work thou didst in their days, in the times of old.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How thou didst drive out the heathen with thy hand, and plantedst them; how thou didst afflict the people, and cast them out.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For they got not the land in possession by their own sword, neither did their own arm save them: but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou hadst a favour unto them.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou art my King, O God: command deliverances for Jacob.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Through thee will we push down our enemies: through thy name will we tread them under that rise up against us.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For I will not trust in my bow, neither shall my sword save me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But thou hast saved us from our enemies, and hast put them to shame that hated us.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> In God we boast all the day long, and praise thy name for ever. Selah.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But thou hast cast off, and put us to shame; and goest not forth with our armies.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thou makest us to turn back from the enemy: and they which hate us spoil for themselves.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thou hast given us like sheep appointed for meat; and hast scattered us among the heathen.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Thou sellest thy people for nought, and dost not increase thy wealth by their price.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and a derision to them that are round about us.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou makest us a byword among the heathen, a shaking of the head among the people.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> My confusion is continually before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For the voice of him that reproacheth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and avenger.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> All this is come upon us; yet have we not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt falsely in thy covenant.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Our heart is not turned back, neither have our steps declined from thy way;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Though thou hast sore broken us in the place of dragons, and covered us with the shadow of death.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> If we have forgotten the name of our God, or stretched out our hands to a strange god;

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Shall not God search this out? for he knoweth the secrets of the heart.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Yea, for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Awake, why sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, cast us not off for ever.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Wherefore hidest thou thy face, and forgettest our affliction and our oppression?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For our soul is bowed down to the dust: our belly cleaveth unto the earth.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Arise for our help, and redeem us for thy mercies’ sake.
