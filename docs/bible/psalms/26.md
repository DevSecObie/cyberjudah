<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Judge me, O Lord; for I have walked in mine integrity: I have trusted also in the Lord; therefore I shall not slide.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Examine me, O Lord, and prove me; try my reins and my heart.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For thy lovingkindness is before mine eyes: and I have walked in thy truth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I have not sat with vain persons, neither will I go in with dissemblers.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I have hated the congregation of evil doers; and will not sit with the wicked.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I will wash mine hands in innocency: so will I compass thine altar, O Lord:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> That I may publish with the voice of thanksgiving, and tell of all thy wondrous works.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Lord, I have loved the habitation of thy house, and the place where thine honour dwelleth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Gather not my soul with sinners, nor my life with bloody men:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> In whose hands is mischief, and their right hand is full of bribes.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But as for me, I will walk in mine integrity: redeem me, and be merciful unto me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> My foot standeth in an even place: in the congregations will I bless the Lord.
