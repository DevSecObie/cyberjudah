<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O Lord our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth! who hast set thy glory above the heavens.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honour.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> All sheep and oxen, yea, and the beasts of the field;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The fowl of the air, and the fish of the sea, and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> O Lord our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth!
