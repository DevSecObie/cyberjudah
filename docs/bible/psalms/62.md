<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Truly my soul waiteth upon God: from him cometh my salvation.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He only is my rock and my salvation; he is my defence; I shall not be greatly moved.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> How long will ye imagine mischief against a man? ye shall be slain all of you: as a bowing wall shall ye be, and as a tottering fence.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They only consult to cast him down from his excellency: they delight in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation is from him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He only is my rock and my salvation: he is my defence; I shall not be moved.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In God is my salvation and my glory: the rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Trust in him at all times; ye people, pour out your heart before him: God is a refuge for us. Selah.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Surely men of low degree are vanity, and men of high degree are a lie: to be laid in the balance, they are altogether lighter than vanity.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, set not your heart upon them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Also unto thee, O Lord, belongeth mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work.
