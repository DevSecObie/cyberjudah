<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will extol thee, O Lord; for thou hast lifted me up, and hast not made my foes to rejoice over me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O Lord my God, I cried unto thee, and thou hast healed me.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> O Lord, thou hast brought up my soul from the grave: thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Sing unto the Lord, O ye saints of his, and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For his anger endureth but a moment; in his favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And in my prosperity I said, I shall never be moved.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Lord, by thy favour thou hast made my mountain to stand strong: thou didst hide thy face, and I was troubled.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I cried to thee, O Lord; and unto the Lord I made supplication.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to the pit? Shall the dust praise thee? shall it declare thy truth?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Hear, O Lord, and have mercy upon me: Lord, be thou my helper.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thou hast turned for me my mourning into dancing: thou hast put off my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> To the end that my glory may sing praise to thee, and not be silent. O Lord my God, I will give thanks unto thee for ever.
