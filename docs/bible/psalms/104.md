<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honour and majesty.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys unto the place which thou hast founded for them.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man’s heart.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The trees of the Lord are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Where the birds make their nests: as for the stork, the fir trees are her house.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Thou makest darkness, and it is night: wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their meat from God.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The sun ariseth, they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> O Lord, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> So is this great and wide sea, wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> There go the ships: there is that leviathan, whom thou hast made to play therein.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> These wait all upon thee; that thou mayest give them their meat in due season.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> That thou givest them they gather: thou openest thine hand, they are filled with good.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled: thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created: and thou renewest the face of the earth.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The glory of the Lord shall endure for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth: he toucheth the hills, and they smoke.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> I will sing unto the Lord as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the Lord.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the Lord, O my soul. Praise ye the Lord.
