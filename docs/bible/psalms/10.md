<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For the wicked boasteth of his heart’s desire, and blesseth the covetous, whom the Lord abhorreth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> His ways are always grievous; thy judgments are far above out of his sight: as for all his enemies, he puffeth at them.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He hath said in his heart, I shall not be moved: for I shall never be in adversity.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and vanity.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He sitteth in the lurking places of the villages: in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes are privily set against the poor.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He lieth in wait secretly as a lion in his den: he lieth in wait to catch the poor: he doth catch the poor, when he draweth him into his net.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He croucheth, and humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten: he hideth his face; he will never see it.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Arise, O Lord; O God, lift up thine hand: forget not the humble.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? he hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou hast seen it; for thou beholdest mischief and spite, to requite it with thy hand: the poor committeth himself unto thee; thou art the helper of the fatherless.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Break thou the arm of the wicked and the evil man: seek out his wickedness till thou find none.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The Lord is King for ever and ever: the heathen are perished out of his land.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Lord, thou hast heard the desire of the humble: thou wilt prepare their heart, thou wilt cause thine ear to hear:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> To judge the fatherless and the oppressed, that the man of the earth may no more oppress.
