<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord shall send the rod of thy strength out of Zion: rule thou in the midst of thine enemies.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The Lord hath sworn, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The Lord at thy right hand shall strike through kings in the day of his wrath.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He shall judge among the heathen, he shall fill the places with the dead bodies; he shall wound the heads over many countries.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He shall drink of the brook in the way: therefore shall he lift up the head.
