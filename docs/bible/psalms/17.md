<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear the right, O Lord, attend unto my cry, give ear unto my prayer, that goeth not out of feigned lips.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let my sentence come forth from thy presence; let thine eyes behold the things that are equal.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou hast proved mine heart; thou hast visited me in the night; thou hast tried me, and shalt find nothing; I am purposed that my mouth shall not transgress.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Concerning the works of men, by the word of thy lips I have kept me from the paths of the destroyer.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Hold up my goings in thy paths, that my footsteps slip not.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I have called upon thee, for thou wilt hear me, O God: incline thine ear unto me, and hear my speech.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Shew thy marvellous lovingkindness, O thou that savest by thy right hand them which put their trust in thee from those that rise up against them.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> From the wicked that oppress me, from my deadly enemies, who compass me about.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> They are inclosed in their own fat: with their mouth they speak proudly.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> They have now compassed us in our steps: they have set their eyes bowing down to the earth;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Like as a lion that is greedy of his prey, and as it were a young lion lurking in secret places.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Arise, O Lord, disappoint him, cast him down: deliver my soul from the wicked, which is thy sword:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> From men which are thy hand, O Lord, from men of the world, which have their portion in this life, and whose belly thou fillest with thy hid treasure: they are full of children, and leave the rest of their substance to their babes.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> As for me, I will behold thy face in righteousness: I shall be satisfied, when I awake, with thy likeness.
