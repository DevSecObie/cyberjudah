<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I love the Lord, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, therefore will I call upon him as long as I live.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The sorrows of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Then called I upon the name of the Lord; O Lord, I beseech thee, deliver my soul.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The Lord preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he helped me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord hath dealt bountifully with thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I will walk before the Lord in the land of the living.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I believed, therefore have I spoken: I was greatly afflicted:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I said in my haste, All men are liars.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> What shall I render unto the Lord for all his benefits toward me?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> O Lord, truly I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid: thou hast loosed my bonds.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call upon the name of the Lord.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people,

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> In the courts of the Lord’s house, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord.
