<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; call upon his name: make known his deeds among the people.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him: talk ye of all his wondrous works.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Seek the Lord, and his strength: seek his face evermore.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Remember his marvellous works that he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> O ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye children of Jacob his chosen.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He hath remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Which covenant he made with Abraham, and his oath unto Isaac;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> When they were but a few men in number; yea, very few, and strangers in it.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> When they went from one nation to another, from one kingdom to another people;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He suffered no man to do them wrong: yea, he reproved kings for their sakes;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Saying, Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Moreover he called for a famine upon the land: he brake the whole staff of bread.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He sent a man before them, even Joseph, who was sold for a servant:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Whose feet they hurt with fetters: he was laid in iron:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Until the time that his word came: the word of the Lord tried him.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The king sent and loosed him; even the ruler of the people, and let him go free.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He made him lord of his house, and ruler of all his substance:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> To bind his princes at his pleasure; and teach his senators wisdom.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Israel also came into Egypt; and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And he increased his people greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal subtilly with his servants.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He sent Moses his servant; and Aaron whom he had chosen.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> They shewed his signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> He sent darkness, and made it dark; and they rebelled not against his word.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Their land brought forth frogs in abundance, in the chambers of their kings.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> He spake, and there came divers sorts of flies, and lice in all their coasts.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> He gave them hail for rain, and flaming fire in their land.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> He smote their vines also and their fig trees; and brake the trees of their coasts.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> He spake, and the locusts came, and caterpillers, and that without number,

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And did eat up all the herbs in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> He smote also all the firstborn in their land, the chief of all their strength.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> He brought them forth also with silver and gold: and there was not one feeble person among their tribes.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them fell upon them.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> He spread a cloud for a covering; and fire to give light in the night.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> The people asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of heaven.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> He opened the rock, and the waters gushed out; they ran in the dry places like a river.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> For he remembered his holy promise, and Abraham his servant.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness:

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And gave them the lands of the heathen: and they inherited the labour of the people;

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> That they might observe his statutes, and keep his laws. Praise ye the Lord.
