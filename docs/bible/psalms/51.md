<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy lovingkindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Behold, I was shapen in iniquity; and in sin did my mother conceive me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Behold, thou desirest truth in the inward parts: and in the hidden part thou shalt make me to know wisdom.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Make me to hear joy and gladness; that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Hide thy face from my sins, and blot out all mine iniquities.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy holy spirit from me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Restore unto me the joy of thy salvation; and uphold me with thy free spirit.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Then will I teach transgressors thy ways; and sinners shall be converted unto thee.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Deliver me from bloodguiltiness, O God, thou God of my salvation: and my tongue shall sing aloud of thy righteousness.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> O Lord, open thou my lips; and my mouth shall shew forth thy praise.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For thou desirest not sacrifice; else would I give it: thou delightest not in burnt offering.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The sacrifices of God are a broken spirit: a broken and a contrite heart, O God, thou wilt not despise.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Do good in thy good pleasure unto Zion: build thou the walls of Jerusalem.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Then shalt thou be pleased with the sacrifices of righteousness, with burnt offering and whole burnt offering: then shall they offer bullocks upon thine altar.
