<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord, with my whole heart; I will shew forth all thy marvellous works.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I will be glad and rejoice in thee: I will sing praise to thy name, O thou most High.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> When mine enemies are turned back, they shall fall and perish at thy presence.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thou hast maintained my right and my cause; thou satest in the throne judging right.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thou hast rebuked the heathen, thou hast destroyed the wicked, thou hast put out their name for ever and ever.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> O thou enemy, destructions are come to a perpetual end: and thou hast destroyed cities; their memorial is perished with them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But the Lord shall endure for ever: he hath prepared his throne for judgment.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And he shall judge the world in righteousness, he shall minister judgment to the people in uprightness.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The Lord also will be a refuge for the oppressed, a refuge in times of trouble.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And they that know thy name will put their trust in thee: for thou, Lord, hast not forsaken them that seek thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Sing praises to the Lord, which dwelleth in Zion: declare among the people his doings.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> When he maketh inquisition for blood, he remembereth them: he forgetteth not the cry of the humble.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Have mercy upon me, O Lord; consider my trouble which I suffer of them that hate me, thou that liftest me up from the gates of death:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> That I may shew forth all thy praise in the gates of the daughter of Zion: I will rejoice in thy salvation.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The heathen are sunk down in the pit that they made: in the net which they hid is their own foot taken.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The Lord is known by the judgment which he executeth: the wicked is snared in the work of his own hands. Higgaion. Selah.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The wicked shall be turned into hell, and all the nations that forget God.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For the needy shall not alway be forgotten: the expectation of the poor shall not perish for ever.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Arise, O Lord; let not man prevail: let the heathen be judged in thy sight.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Put them in fear, O Lord: that the nations may know themselves to be but men. Selah.
