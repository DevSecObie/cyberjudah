<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The Lord reigneth; let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of isles be glad thereof.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Clouds and darkness are round about him: righteousness and judgment are the habitation of his throne.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> A fire goeth before him, and burneth up his enemies round about.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> His lightnings enlightened the world: the earth saw, and trembled.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The hills melted like wax at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The heavens declare his righteousness, and all the people see his glory.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Confounded be all they that serve graven images, that boast themselves of idols: worship him, all ye gods.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Zion heard, and was glad; and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of thy judgments, O Lord.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For thou, Lord, art high above all the earth: thou art exalted far above all gods.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Ye that love the Lord, hate evil: he preserveth the souls of his saints; he delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Light is sown for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Rejoice in the Lord, ye righteous; and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness.
