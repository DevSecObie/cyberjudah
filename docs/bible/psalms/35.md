<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Plead my cause, O Lord, with them that strive with me: fight against them that fight against me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Take hold of shield and buckler, and stand up for mine help.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Draw out also the spear, and stop the way against them that persecute me: say unto my soul, I am thy salvation.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Let them be confounded and put to shame that seek after my soul: let them be turned back and brought to confusion that devise my hurt.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let them be as chaff before the wind: and let the angel of the Lord chase them.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Let their way be dark and slippery: and let the angel of the Lord persecute them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For without cause have they hid for me their net in a pit, which without cause they have digged for my soul.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let destruction come upon him at unawares; and let his net that he hath hid catch himself: into that very destruction let him fall.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And my soul shall be joyful in the Lord: it shall rejoice in his salvation.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> All my bones shall say, Lord, who is like unto thee, which deliverest the poor from him that is too strong for him, yea, the poor and the needy from him that spoileth him?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> False witnesses did rise up; they laid to my charge things that I knew not.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They rewarded me evil for good to the spoiling of my soul.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth: I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer returned into mine own bosom.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I behaved myself as though he had been my friend or brother: I bowed down heavily, as one that mourneth for his mother.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But in mine adversity they rejoiced, and gathered themselves together: yea, the abjects gathered themselves together against me, and I knew it not; they did tear me, and ceased not:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> With hypocritical mockers in feasts, they gnashed upon me with their teeth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Lord, how long wilt thou look on? rescue my soul from their destructions, my darling from the lions.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I will give thee thanks in the great congregation: I will praise thee among much people.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Let not them that are mine enemies wrongfully rejoice over me: neither let them wink with the eye that hate me without a cause.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For they speak not peace: but they devise deceitful matters against them that are quiet in the land.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Yea, they opened their mouth wide against me, and said, Aha, aha, our eye hath seen it.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> This thou hast seen, O Lord: keep not silence: O Lord, be not far from me.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Stir up thyself, and awake to my judgment, even unto my cause, my God and my Lord.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Judge me, O Lord my God, according to thy righteousness; and let them not rejoice over me.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Let them not say in their hearts, Ah, so would we have it: let them not say, We have swallowed him up.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Let them be ashamed and brought to confusion together that rejoice at mine hurt: let them be clothed with shame and dishonour that magnify themselves against me.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Let them shout for joy, and be glad, that favour my righteous cause: yea, let them say continually, Let the Lord be magnified, which hath pleasure in the prosperity of his servant.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And my tongue shall speak of thy righteousness and of thy praise all the day long.
