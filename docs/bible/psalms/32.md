<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Blessed is the man unto whom the Lord imputeth not iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no guile.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> When I kept silence, my bones waxed old through my roaring all the day long.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: my moisture is turned into the drought of summer. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I acknowledged my sin unto thee, and mine iniquity have I not hid. I said, I will confess my transgressions unto the Lord; and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. Selah.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For this shall every one that is godly pray unto thee in a time when thou mayest be found: surely in the floods of great waters they shall not come nigh unto him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou art my hiding place; thou shalt preserve me from trouble; thou shalt compass me about with songs of deliverance. Selah.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Be ye not as the horse, or as the mule, which have no understanding: whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle, lest they come near unto thee.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Many sorrows shall be to the wicked: but he that trusteth in the Lord, mercy shall compass him about.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Be glad in the Lord, and rejoice, ye righteous: and shout for joy, all ye that are upright in heart.
