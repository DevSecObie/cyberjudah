<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Preserve me, O God: for in thee do I put my trust.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O my soul, thou hast said unto the Lord, Thou art my Lord: my goodness extendeth not to thee;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But to the saints that are in the earth, and to the excellent, in whom is all my delight.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god: their drink offerings of blood will I not offer, nor take up their names into my lips.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The Lord is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I will bless the Lord, who hath given me counsel: my reins also instruct me in the night seasons.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I have set the Lord always before me: because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Therefore my heart is glad, and my glory rejoiceth: my flesh also shall rest in hope.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore.
