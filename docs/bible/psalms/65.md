<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise waiteth for thee, O God, in Sion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Iniquities prevail against me: as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> By terrible things in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation; who art the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are afar off upon the sea:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Which by his strength setteth fast the mountains; being girded with power:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Which stilleth the noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid at thy tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou greatly enrichest it with the river of God, which is full of water: thou preparest them corn, when thou hast so provided for it.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thou waterest the ridges thereof abundantly: thou settlest the furrows thereof: thou makest it soft with showers: thou blessest the springing thereof.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They drop upon the pastures of the wilderness: and the little hills rejoice on every side.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing.
