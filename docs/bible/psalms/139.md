<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O Lord, thou hast searched me, and known me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou knowest my downsitting and mine uprising, thou understandest my thought afar off.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou compassest my path and my lying down, and art acquainted with all my ways.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For there is not a word in my tongue, but, lo, O Lord, thou knowest it altogether.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thou hast beset me behind and before, and laid thine hand upon me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Such knowledge is too wonderful for me; it is high, I cannot attain unto it.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Whither shall I go from thy spirit? or whither shall I flee from thy presence?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> If I ascend up into heaven, thou art there: if I make my bed in hell, behold, thou art there.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> If I take the wings of the morning, and dwell in the uttermost parts of the sea;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Even there shall thy hand lead me, and thy right hand shall hold me.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> If I say, Surely the darkness shall cover me; even the night shall be light about me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Yea, the darkness hideth not from thee; but the night shineth as the day: the darkness and the light are both alike to thee.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For thou hast possessed my reins: thou hast covered me in my mother’s womb.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and wonderfully made: marvellous are thy works; and that my soul knoweth right well.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> My substance was not hid from thee, when I was made in secret, and curiously wrought in the lowest parts of the earth.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Thine eyes did see my substance, yet being unperfect; and in thy book all my members were written, which in continuance were fashioned, when as yet there was none of them.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> How precious also are thy thoughts unto me, O God! how great is the sum of them!

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> If I should count them, they are more in number than the sand: when I awake, I am still with thee.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Surely thou wilt slay the wicked, O God: depart from me therefore, ye bloody men.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For they speak against thee wickedly, and thine enemies take thy name in vain.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Do not I hate them, O Lord, that hate thee? and am not I grieved with those that rise up against thee?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> I hate them with perfect hatred: I count them mine enemies.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.
