<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O Lord.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They run and prepare themselves without my fault: awake to help me, and behold.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thou therefore, O Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, awake to visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any wicked transgressors. Selah.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they, doth hear?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But thou, O Lord, shalt laugh at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defence.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The God of my mercy shall prevent me: God shall let me see my desire upon mine enemies.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Slay them not, lest my people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord our shield.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride: and for cursing and lying which they speak.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Consume them in wrath, consume them, that they may not be: and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. Selah.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And at evening let them return; and let them make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Let them wander up and down for meat, and grudge if they be not satisfied.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing: for God is my defence, and the God of my mercy.
