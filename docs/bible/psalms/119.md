<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Blessed are they that keep his testimonies, and that seek him with the whole heart.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They also do no iniquity: they walk in his ways.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou hast commanded us to keep thy precepts diligently.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> O that my ways were directed to keep thy statutes!

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then shall I not be ashamed, when I have respect unto all thy commandments.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I will praise thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned thy righteous judgments.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I will keep thy statutes: O forsake me not utterly.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> With my whole heart have I sought thee: O let me not wander from thy commandments.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thy word have I hid in mine heart, that I might not sin against thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Blessed art thou, O Lord: teach me thy statutes.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> With my lips have I declared all the judgments of thy mouth.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I have rejoiced in the way of thy testimonies, as much as in all riches.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I will meditate in thy precepts, and have respect unto thy ways.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I will delight myself in thy statutes: I will not forget thy word.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Deal bountifully with thy servant, that I may live, and keep thy word.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> I am a stranger in the earth: hide not thy commandments from me.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My soul breaketh for the longing that it hath unto thy judgments at all times.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Thou hast rebuked the proud that are cursed, which do err from thy commandments.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Remove from me reproach and contempt; for I have kept thy testimonies.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Princes also did sit and speak against me: but thy servant did meditate in thy statutes.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Thy testimonies also are my delight and my counsellors.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> My soul cleaveth unto the dust: quicken thou me according to thy word.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> I have declared my ways, and thou heardest me: teach me thy statutes.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Make me to understand the way of thy precepts: so shall I talk of thy wondrous works.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> My soul melteth for heaviness: strengthen thou me according unto thy word.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Remove from me the way of lying: and grant me thy law graciously.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> I have chosen the way of truth: thy judgments have I laid before me.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> I have stuck unto thy testimonies: O Lord, put me not to shame.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> I will run the way of thy commandments, when thou shalt enlarge my heart.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Teach me, O Lord, the way of thy statutes; and I shall keep it unto the end.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Give me understanding, and I shall keep thy law; yea, I shall observe it with my whole heart.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Make me to go in the path of thy commandments; for therein do I delight.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Incline my heart unto thy testimonies, and not to covetousness.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Turn away mine eyes from beholding vanity; and quicken thou me in thy way.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Stablish thy word unto thy servant, who is devoted to thy fear.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Turn away my reproach which I fear: for thy judgments are good.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Behold, I have longed after thy precepts: quicken me in thy righteousness.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Let thy mercies come also unto me, O Lord, even thy salvation, according to thy word.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> So shall I have wherewith to answer him that reproacheth me: for I trust in thy word.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth; for I have hoped in thy judgments.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> So shall I keep thy law continually for ever and ever.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And I will walk at liberty: for I seek thy precepts.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> I will speak of thy testimonies also before kings, and will not be ashamed.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> And I will delight myself in thy commandments, which I have loved.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> My hands also will I lift up unto thy commandments, which I have loved; and I will meditate in thy statutes.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> Remember the word unto thy servant, upon which thou hast caused me to hope.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> This is my comfort in my affliction: for thy word hath quickened me.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> The proud have had me greatly in derision: yet have I not declined from thy law.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> I remembered thy judgments of old, O Lord; and have comforted myself.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> Horror hath taken hold upon me because of the wicked that forsake thy law.

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> Thy statutes have been my songs in the house of my pilgrimage.

<a id="v55"></a><sup class="vn">55</sup> I have remembered thy name, O Lord, in the night, and have kept thy law.

<a id="v56"></a><sup class="vn">56</sup> This I had, because I kept thy precepts.

<a id="v57"></a><sup class="vn">57</sup> Thou art my portion, O Lord: I have said that I would keep thy words.

<a id="v58"></a><sup class="vn">58</sup> I intreated thy favour with my whole heart: be merciful unto me according to thy word.

<a id="v59"></a><sup class="vn">59</sup> I thought on my ways, and turned my feet unto thy testimonies.

<a id="v60"></a><sup class="vn">60</sup> I made haste, and delayed not to keep thy commandments.

<a id="v61"></a><sup class="vn">61</sup> The bands of the wicked have robbed me: but I have not forgotten thy law.

<a id="v62"></a><sup class="vn">62</sup> At midnight I will rise to give thanks unto thee because of thy righteous judgments.

<a id="v63"></a><sup class="vn">63</sup> I am a companion of all them that fear thee, and of them that keep thy precepts.

<a id="v64"></a><sup class="vn">64</sup> The earth, O Lord, is full of thy mercy: teach me thy statutes.

<a id="v65"></a><sup class="vn">65</sup> Thou hast dealt well with thy servant, O Lord, according unto thy word.

<a id="v66"></a><sup class="vn">66</sup> Teach me good judgment and knowledge: for I have believed thy commandments.

<a id="v67"></a><sup class="vn">67</sup> Before I was afflicted I went astray: but now have I kept thy word.

<a id="v68"></a><sup class="vn">68</sup> Thou art good, and doest good; teach me thy statutes.

<a id="v69"></a><sup class="vn">69</sup> The proud have forged a lie against me: but I will keep thy precepts with my whole heart.

<a id="v70"></a><sup class="vn">70</sup> Their heart is as fat as grease; but I delight in thy law.

<a id="v71"></a><sup class="vn">71</sup> It is good for me that I have been afflicted; that I might learn thy statutes.

<a id="v72"></a><sup class="vn">72</sup> The law of thy mouth is better unto me than thousands of gold and silver.

<a id="v73"></a><sup class="vn">73</sup> Thy hands have made me and fashioned me: give me understanding, that I may learn thy commandments.

<a id="v74"></a><sup class="vn">74</sup> They that fear thee will be glad when they see me; because I have hoped in thy word.

<a id="v75"></a><sup class="vn">75</sup> I know, O Lord, that thy judgments are right, and that thou in faithfulness hast afflicted me.

<a id="v76"></a><sup class="vn">76</sup> Let, I pray thee, thy merciful kindness be for my comfort, according to thy word unto thy servant.

<a id="v77"></a><sup class="vn">77</sup> Let thy tender mercies come unto me, that I may live: for thy law is my delight.

<a id="v78"></a><sup class="vn">78</sup> Let the proud be ashamed; for they dealt perversely with me without a cause: but I will meditate in thy precepts.

<a id="v79"></a><sup class="vn">79</sup> Let those that fear thee turn unto me, and those that have known thy testimonies.

<a id="v80"></a><sup class="vn">80</sup> Let my heart be sound in thy statutes; that I be not ashamed.

<a id="v81"></a><sup class="vn">81</sup> My soul fainteth for thy salvation: but I hope in thy word.

<a id="v82"></a><sup class="vn">82</sup> Mine eyes fail for thy word, saying, When wilt thou comfort me?

<a id="v83"></a><sup class="vn">83</sup> For I am become like a bottle in the smoke; yet do I not forget thy statutes.

<a id="v84"></a><sup class="vn">84</sup> How many are the days of thy servant? when wilt thou execute judgment on them that persecute me?

<a id="v85"></a><sup class="vn">85</sup> The proud have digged pits for me, which are not after thy law.

<a id="v86"></a><sup class="vn">86</sup> All thy commandments are faithful: they persecute me wrongfully; help thou me.

<a id="v87"></a><sup class="vn">87</sup> They had almost consumed me upon earth; but I forsook not thy precepts.

<a id="v88"></a><sup class="vn">88</sup> Quicken me after thy lovingkindness; so shall I keep the testimony of thy mouth.

<a id="v89"></a><sup class="vn">89</sup> For ever, O Lord, thy word is settled in heaven.

<a id="v90"></a><sup class="vn">90</sup> Thy faithfulness is unto all generations: thou hast established the earth, and it abideth.

<a id="v91"></a><sup class="vn">91</sup> They continue this day according to thine ordinances: for all are thy servants.

<a id="v92"></a><sup class="vn">92</sup> Unless thy law had been my delights, I should then have perished in mine affliction.

<a id="v93"></a><sup class="vn">93</sup> I will never forget thy precepts: for with them thou hast quickened me.

<a id="v94"></a><sup class="vn">94</sup> I am thine, save me; for I have sought thy precepts.

<a id="v95"></a><sup class="vn">95</sup> The wicked have waited for me to destroy me: but I will consider thy testimonies.

<a id="v96"></a><sup class="vn">96</sup> I have seen an end of all perfection: but thy commandment is exceeding broad.

<a id="v97"></a><sup class="vn">97</sup> O how love I thy law! it is my meditation all the day.

<a id="v98"></a><sup class="vn">98</sup> Thou through thy commandments hast made me wiser than mine enemies: for they are ever with me.

<a id="v99"></a><sup class="vn">99</sup> I have more understanding than all my teachers: for thy testimonies are my meditation.

<a id="v100"></a><sup class="vn">100</sup> I understand more than the ancients, because I keep thy precepts.

<a id="v101"></a><sup class="vn">101</sup> I have refrained my feet from every evil way, that I might keep thy word.

<a id="v102"></a><sup class="vn">102</sup> I have not departed from thy judgments: for thou hast taught me.

<a id="v103"></a><sup class="vn">103</sup> How sweet are thy words unto my taste! yea, sweeter than honey to my mouth!

<a id="v104"></a><sup class="vn">104</sup> Through thy precepts I get understanding: therefore I hate every false way.

<a id="v105"></a><sup class="vn">105</sup> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

<a id="v106"></a><sup class="vn">106</sup> I have sworn, and I will perform it, that I will keep thy righteous judgments.

<a id="v107"></a><sup class="vn">107</sup> I am afflicted very much: quicken me, O Lord, according unto thy word.

<a id="v108"></a><sup class="vn">108</sup> Accept, I beseech thee, the freewill offerings of my mouth, O Lord, and teach me thy judgments.

<a id="v109"></a><sup class="vn">109</sup> My soul is continually in my hand: yet do I not forget thy law.

<a id="v110"></a><sup class="vn">110</sup> The wicked have laid a snare for me: yet I erred not from thy precepts.

<a id="v111"></a><sup class="vn">111</sup> Thy testimonies have I taken as an heritage for ever: for they are the rejoicing of my heart.

<a id="v112"></a><sup class="vn">112</sup> I have inclined mine heart to perform thy statutes alway, even unto the end.

<a id="v113"></a><sup class="vn">113</sup> I hate vain thoughts: but thy law do I love.

<a id="v114"></a><sup class="vn">114</sup> Thou art my hiding place and my shield: I hope in thy word.

<a id="v115"></a><sup class="vn">115</sup> Depart from me, ye evildoers: for I will keep the commandments of my God.

<a id="v116"></a><sup class="vn">116</sup> Uphold me according unto thy word, that I may live: and let me not be ashamed of my hope.

<a id="v117"></a><sup class="vn">117</sup> Hold thou me up, and I shall be safe: and I will have respect unto thy statutes continually.

<a id="v118"></a><sup class="vn">118</sup> Thou hast trodden down all them that err from thy statutes: for their deceit is falsehood.

<a id="v119"></a><sup class="vn">119</sup> Thou puttest away all the wicked of the earth like dross: therefore I love thy testimonies.

<a id="v120"></a><sup class="vn">120</sup> My flesh trembleth for fear of thee; and I am afraid of thy judgments.

<a id="v121"></a><sup class="vn">121</sup> I have done judgment and justice: leave me not to mine oppressors.

<a id="v122"></a><sup class="vn">122</sup> Be surety for thy servant for good: let not the proud oppress me.

<a id="v123"></a><sup class="vn">123</sup> Mine eyes fail for thy salvation, and for the word of thy righteousness.

<a id="v124"></a><sup class="vn">124</sup> Deal with thy servant according unto thy mercy, and teach me thy statutes.

<a id="v125"></a><sup class="vn">125</sup> I am thy servant; give me understanding, that I may know thy testimonies.

<a id="v126"></a><sup class="vn">126</sup> It is time for thee, Lord, to work: for they have made void thy law.

<a id="v127"></a><sup class="vn">127</sup> Therefore I love thy commandments above gold; yea, above fine gold.

<a id="v128"></a><sup class="vn">128</sup> Therefore I esteem all thy precepts concerning all things to be right; and I hate every false way.

<a id="v129"></a><sup class="vn">129</sup> Thy testimonies are wonderful: therefore doth my soul keep them.

<a id="v130"></a><sup class="vn">130</sup> The entrance of thy words giveth light; it giveth understanding unto the simple.

<a id="v131"></a><sup class="vn">131</sup> I opened my mouth, and panted: for I longed for thy commandments.

<a id="v132"></a><sup class="vn">132</sup> Look thou upon me, and be merciful unto me, as thou usest to do unto those that love thy name.

<a id="v133"></a><sup class="vn">133</sup> Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.

<a id="v134"></a><sup class="vn">134</sup> Deliver me from the oppression of man: so will I keep thy precepts.

<a id="v135"></a><sup class="vn">135</sup> Make thy face to shine upon thy servant; and teach me thy statutes.

<a id="v136"></a><sup class="vn">136</sup> Rivers of waters run down mine eyes, because they keep not thy law.

<a id="v137"></a><sup class="vn">137</sup> Righteous art thou, O Lord, and upright are thy judgments.

<a id="v138"></a><sup class="vn">138</sup> Thy testimonies that thou hast commanded are righteous and very faithful.

<a id="v139"></a><sup class="vn">139</sup> My zeal hath consumed me, because mine enemies have forgotten thy words.

<a id="v140"></a><sup class="vn">140</sup> Thy word is very pure: therefore thy servant loveth it.

<a id="v141"></a><sup class="vn">141</sup> I am small and despised: yet do not I forget thy precepts.

<a id="v142"></a><sup class="vn">142</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

<a id="v143"></a><sup class="vn">143</sup> Trouble and anguish have taken hold on me: yet thy commandments are my delights.

<a id="v144"></a><sup class="vn">144</sup> The righteousness of thy testimonies is everlasting: give me understanding, and I shall live.

<a id="v145"></a><sup class="vn">145</sup> I cried with my whole heart; hear me, O Lord: I will keep thy statutes.

<a id="v146"></a><sup class="vn">146</sup> I cried unto thee; save me, and I shall keep thy testimonies.

<a id="v147"></a><sup class="vn">147</sup> I prevented the dawning of the morning, and cried: I hoped in thy word.

<a id="v148"></a><sup class="vn">148</sup> Mine eyes prevent the night watches, that I might meditate in thy word.

<a id="v149"></a><sup class="vn">149</sup> Hear my voice according unto thy lovingkindness: O Lord, quicken me according to thy judgment.

<a id="v150"></a><sup class="vn">150</sup> They draw nigh that follow after mischief: they are far from thy law.

<a id="v151"></a><sup class="vn">151</sup> Thou art near, O Lord; and all thy commandments are truth.

<a id="v152"></a><sup class="vn">152</sup> Concerning thy testimonies, I have known of old that thou hast founded them for ever.

<a id="v153"></a><sup class="vn">153</sup> Consider mine affliction, and deliver me: for I do not forget thy law.

<a id="v154"></a><sup class="vn">154</sup> Plead my cause, and deliver me: quicken me according to thy word.

<a id="v155"></a><sup class="vn">155</sup> Salvation is far from the wicked: for they seek not thy statutes.

<a id="v156"></a><sup class="vn">156</sup> Great are thy tender mercies, O Lord: quicken me according to thy judgments.

<a id="v157"></a><sup class="vn">157</sup> Many are my persecutors and mine enemies; yet do I not decline from thy testimonies.

<a id="v158"></a><sup class="vn">158</sup> I beheld the transgressors, and was grieved; because they kept not thy word.

<a id="v159"></a><sup class="vn">159</sup> Consider how I love thy precepts: quicken me, O Lord, according to thy lovingkindness.

<a id="v160"></a><sup class="vn">160</sup> Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever.

<a id="v161"></a><sup class="vn">161</sup> Princes have persecuted me without a cause: but my heart standeth in awe of thy word.

<a id="v162"></a><sup class="vn">162</sup> I rejoice at thy word, as one that findeth great spoil.

<a id="v163"></a><sup class="vn">163</sup> I hate and abhor lying: but thy law do I love.

<a id="v164"></a><sup class="vn">164</sup> Seven times a day do I praise thee because of thy righteous judgments.

<a id="v165"></a><sup class="vn">165</sup> Great peace have they which love thy law: and nothing shall offend them.

<a id="v166"></a><sup class="vn">166</sup> Lord, I have hoped for thy salvation, and done thy commandments.

<a id="v167"></a><sup class="vn">167</sup> My soul hath kept thy testimonies; and I love them exceedingly.

<a id="v168"></a><sup class="vn">168</sup> I have kept thy precepts and thy testimonies: for all my ways are before thee.

<a id="v169"></a><sup class="vn">169</sup> Let my cry come near before thee, O Lord: give me understanding according to thy word.

<a id="v170"></a><sup class="vn">170</sup> Let my supplication come before thee: deliver me according to thy word.

<a id="v171"></a><sup class="vn">171</sup> My lips shall utter praise, when thou hast taught me thy statutes.

<a id="v172"></a><sup class="vn">172</sup> My tongue shall speak of thy word: for all thy commandments are righteousness.

<a id="v173"></a><sup class="vn">173</sup> Let thine hand help me; for I have chosen thy precepts.

<a id="v174"></a><sup class="vn">174</sup> I have longed for thy salvation, O Lord; and thy law is my delight.

<a id="v175"></a><sup class="vn">175</sup> Let my soul live, and it shall praise thee; and let thy judgments help me.

<a id="v176"></a><sup class="vn">176</sup> I have gone astray like a lost sheep; seek thy servant; for I do not forget thy commandments.
