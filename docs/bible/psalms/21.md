<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The king shall joy in thy strength, O Lord; and in thy salvation how greatly shall he rejoice!

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou hast given him his heart’s desire, and hast not withholden the request of his lips. Selah.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For thou preventest him with the blessings of goodness: thou settest a crown of pure gold on his head.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He asked life of thee, and thou gavest it him, even length of days for ever and ever.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> His glory is great in thy salvation: honour and majesty hast thou laid upon him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For thou hast made him most blessed for ever: thou hast made him exceeding glad with thy countenance.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For the king trusteth in the Lord, and through the mercy of the most High he shall not be moved.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thine hand shall find out all thine enemies: thy right hand shall find out those that hate thee.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven in the time of thine anger: the Lord shall swallow them up in his wrath, and the fire shall devour them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For they intended evil against thee: they imagined a mischievous device, which they are not able to perform.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Therefore shalt thou make them turn their back, when thou shalt make ready thine arrows upon thy strings against the face of them.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Be thou exalted, Lord, in thine own strength: so will we sing and praise thy power.
