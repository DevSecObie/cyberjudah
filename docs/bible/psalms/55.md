<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not thyself from my supplication.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Attend unto me, and hear me: I mourn in my complaint, and make a noise;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked: for they cast iniquity upon me, and in wrath they hate me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> My heart is sore pained within me: and the terrors of death are fallen upon me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Fearfulness and trembling are come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And I said, Oh that I had wings like a dove! for then would I fly away, and be at rest.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Lo, then would I wander far off, and remain in the wilderness. Selah.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Destroy, O Lord, and divide their tongues: for I have seen violence and strife in the city.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Day and night they go about it upon the walls thereof: mischief also and sorrow are in the midst of it.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wickedness is in the midst thereof: deceit and guile depart not from her streets.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For it was not an enemy that reproached me; then I could have borne it: neither was it he that hated me that did magnify himself against me; then I would have hid myself from him:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But it was thou, a man mine equal, my guide, and mine acquaintance.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> We took sweet counsel together, and walked unto the house of God in company.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Let death seize upon them, and let them go down quick into hell: for wickedness is in their dwellings, and among them.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> As for me, I will call upon God; and the Lord shall save me.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He hath delivered my soul in peace from the battle that was against me: for there were many with me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> God shall hear, and afflict them, even he that abideth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, therefore they fear not God.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> He hath put forth his hands against such as be at peace with him: he hath broken his covenant.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Cast thy burden upon the Lord, and he shall sustain thee: he shall never suffer the righteous to be moved.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> But thou, O God, shalt bring them down into the pit of destruction: bloody and deceitful men shall not live out half their days; but I will trust in thee.
