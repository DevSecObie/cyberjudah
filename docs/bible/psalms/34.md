<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will bless the Lord at all times: his praise shall continually be in my mouth.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> My soul shall make her boast in the Lord: the humble shall hear thereof, and be glad.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> O magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt his name together.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and delivered me from all my fears.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They looked unto him, and were lightened: and their faces were not ashamed.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> O taste and see that the Lord is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> O fear the Lord, ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger: but they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Come, ye children, hearken unto me: I will teach you the fear of the Lord.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> What man is he that desireth life, and loveth many days, that he may see good?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking guile.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and his ears are open unto their cry.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The face of the Lord is against them that do evil, to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The righteous cry, and the Lord heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Many are the afflictions of the righteous: but the Lord delivereth him out of them all.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> He keepeth all his bones: not one of them is broken.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Evil shall slay the wicked: and they that hate the righteous shall be desolate.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The Lord redeemeth the soul of his servants: and none of them that trust in him shall be desolate.
