<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O God, why hast thou cast us off for ever? why doth thine anger smoke against the sheep of thy pasture?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Remember thy congregation, which thou hast purchased of old; the rod of thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed; this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Lift up thy feet unto the perpetual desolations; even all that the enemy hath done wickedly in the sanctuary.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thine enemies roar in the midst of thy congregations; they set up their ensigns for signs.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A man was famous according as he had lifted up axes upon the thick trees.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But now they break down the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They have cast fire into thy sanctuary, they have defiled by casting down the dwelling place of thy name to the ground.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They said in their hearts, Let us destroy them together: they have burned up all the synagogues of God in the land.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> We see not our signs: there is no more any prophet: neither is there among us any that knoweth how long.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> O God, how long shall the adversary reproach? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Why withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? pluck it out of thy bosom.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For God is my King of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Thou didst cleave the fountain and the flood: thou driedst up mighty rivers.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The day is thine, the night also is thine: thou hast prepared the light and the sun.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Thou hast set all the borders of the earth: thou hast made summer and winter.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Remember this, that the enemy hath reproached, O Lord, and that the foolish people have blasphemed thy name.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> O deliver not the soul of thy turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked: forget not the congregation of thy poor for ever.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Have respect unto the covenant: for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> O let not the oppressed return ashamed: let the poor and needy praise thy name.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Arise, O God, plead thine own cause: remember how the foolish man reproacheth thee daily.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Forget not the voice of thine enemies: the tumult of those that rise up against thee increaseth continually.
