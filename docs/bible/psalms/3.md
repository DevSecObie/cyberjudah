<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, how are they increased that trouble me! many are they that rise up against me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Many there be which say of my soul, There is no help for him in God. Selah.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But thou, O Lord, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I cried unto the Lord with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord sustained me.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have set themselves against me round about.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Arise, O Lord; save me, O my God: for thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Salvation belongeth unto the Lord: thy blessing is upon thy people. Selah.
