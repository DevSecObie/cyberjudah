<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Keep not thou silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For, lo, thine enemies make a tumult: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy hidden ones.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For they have consulted together with one consent: they are confederate against thee:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The tabernacles of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Assur also is joined with them: they have holpen the children of Lot. Selah.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Do unto them as unto the Midianites; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook of Kison:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Which perished at En–dor: they became as dung for the earth.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Make their nobles like Oreb, and like Zeeb: yea, all their princes as Zebah, and as Zalmunna:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Who said, Let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> O my God, make them like a wheel; as the stubble before the wind.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> As the fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek thy name, O Lord.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Let them be confounded and troubled for ever; yea, let them be put to shame, and perish:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> That men may know that thou, whose name alone is Jehovah, art the most high over all the earth.
