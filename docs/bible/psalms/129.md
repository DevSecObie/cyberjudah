<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, may Israel now say:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth: yet they have not prevailed against me.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The plowers plowed upon my back: they made long their furrows.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The Lord is righteous: he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Zion.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Let them be as the grass upon the housetops, which withereth afore it groweth up:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand; nor he that bindeth sheaves his bosom.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Neither do they which go by say, The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we bless you in the name of the Lord.
