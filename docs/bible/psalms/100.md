<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all ye lands.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Serve the Lord with gladness: come before his presence with singing.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Know ye that the Lord he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For the Lord is good; his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations.
