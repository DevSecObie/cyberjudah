<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> O give thanks unto the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> O give thanks to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> To him who alone doeth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> To him that by wisdom made the heavens: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> To him that stretched out the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> To him that made great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The sun to rule by day: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The moon and stars to rule by night: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> To him that smote Egypt in their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> With a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> To him which divided the Red sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And made Israel to pass through the midst of it: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red sea: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> To him which led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> To him which smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And slew famous kings: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Sihon king of the Amorites: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Og the king of Bashan: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And gave their land for an heritage: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Even an heritage unto Israel his servant: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Who remembered us in our low estate: for his mercy endureth for ever:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And hath redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> O give thanks unto the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever.
