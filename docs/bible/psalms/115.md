<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us, but unto thy name give glory, for thy mercy, and for thy truth’s sake.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Wherefore should the heathen say, Where is now their God?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But our God is in the heavens: he hath done whatsoever he hath pleased.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> O Israel, trust thou in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> O house of Aaron, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The Lord hath been mindful of us: he will bless us; he will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He will bless them that fear the Lord, both small and great.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Lord shall increase you more and more, you and your children.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Ye are blessed of the Lord which made heaven and earth.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The heaven, even the heavens, are the Lord’s: but the earth hath he given to the children of men.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The dead praise not the Lord, neither any that go down into silence.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> But we will bless the Lord from this time forth and for evermore. Praise the Lord.
