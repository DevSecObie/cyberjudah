<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let Israel hope in the Lord from henceforth and for ever.
