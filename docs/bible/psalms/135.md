<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise ye the name of the Lord; praise him, O ye servants of the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Ye that stand in the house of the Lord, in the courts of the house of our God,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Praise the Lord; for the Lord is good: sing praises unto his name; for it is pleasant.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For the Lord hath chosen Jacob unto himself, and Israel for his peculiar treasure.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For I know that the Lord is great, and that our Lord is above all gods.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Whatsoever the Lord pleased, that did he in heaven, and in earth, in the seas, and all deep places.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth; he maketh lightnings for the rain; he bringeth the wind out of his treasuries.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Who smote the firstborn of Egypt, both of man and beast.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Who sent tokens and wonders into the midst of thee, O Egypt, upon Pharaoh, and upon all his servants.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who smote great nations, and slew mighty kings;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Sihon king of the Amorites, and Og king of Bashan, and all the kingdoms of Canaan:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And gave their land for an heritage, an heritage unto Israel his people.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thy name, O Lord, endureth for ever; and thy memorial, O Lord, throughout all generations.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For the Lord will judge his people, and he will repent himself concerning his servants.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The idols of the heathen are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> They have mouths, but they speak not; eyes have they, but they see not;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> They have ears, but they hear not; neither is there any breath in their mouths.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> They that make them are like unto them: so is every one that trusteth in them.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Bless the Lord, O house of Israel: bless the Lord, O house of Aaron:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Bless the Lord, O house of Levi: ye that fear the Lord, bless the Lord.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Blessed be the Lord out of Zion, which dwelleth at Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord.
