<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I waited patiently for the Lord; and he inclined unto me, and heard my cry.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He brought me up also out of an horrible pit, out of the miry clay, and set my feet upon a rock, and established my goings.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And he hath put a new song in my mouth, even praise unto our God: many shall see it, and fear, and shall trust in the Lord.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Blessed is that man that maketh the Lord his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Many, O Lord my God, are thy wonderful works which thou hast done, and thy thoughts which are to us-ward: they cannot be reckoned up in order unto thee: if I would declare and speak of them, they are more than can be numbered.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Sacrifice and offering thou didst not desire; mine ears hast thou opened: burnt offering and sin offering hast thou not required.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Then said I, Lo, I come: in the volume of the book it is written of me,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I have preached righteousness in the great congregation: lo, I have not refrained my lips, O Lord, thou knowest.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I have not hid thy righteousness within my heart; I have declared thy faithfulness and thy salvation: I have not concealed thy lovingkindness and thy truth from the great congregation.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Withhold not thou thy tender mercies from me, O Lord: let thy lovingkindness and thy truth continually preserve me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For innumerable evils have compassed me about: mine iniquities have taken hold upon me, so that I am not able to look up; they are more than the hairs of mine head: therefore my heart faileth me.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Be pleased, O Lord, to deliver me: O Lord, make haste to help me.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Let them be ashamed and confounded together that seek after my soul to destroy it; let them be driven backward and put to shame that wish me evil.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Let them be desolate for a reward of their shame that say unto me, Aha, aha.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: let such as love thy salvation say continually, The Lord be magnified.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> But I am poor and needy; yet the Lord thinketh upon me: thou art my help and my deliverer; make no tarrying, O my God.
