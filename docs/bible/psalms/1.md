<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Therefore the ungodly shall not stand in the judgment, nor sinners in the congregation of the righteous.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For the Lord knoweth the way of the righteous: but the way of the ungodly shall perish.
