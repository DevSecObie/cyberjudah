<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Selah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> There is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God, the holy place of the tabernacles of the most High.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: God shall help her, and that right early.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved: he uttered his voice, the earth melted.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Come, behold the works of the Lord, what desolations he hath made in the earth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the earth; he breaketh the bow, and cutteth the spear in sunder; he burneth the chariot in the fire.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.
