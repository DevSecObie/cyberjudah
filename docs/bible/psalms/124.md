<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, now may Israel say;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, when men rose up against us:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Then they had swallowed us up quick, when their wrath was kindled against us:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Then the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Then the proud waters had gone over our soul.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Blessed be the Lord, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.
