<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> As the hart panteth after the water brooks, so panteth my soul after thee, O God.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> My soul thirsteth for God, for the living God: when shall I come and appear before God?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> My tears have been my meat day and night, while they continually say unto me, Where is thy God?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> When I remember these things, I pour out my soul in me: for I had gone with the multitude, I went with them to the house of God, with the voice of joy and praise, with a multitude that kept holyday.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted in me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him for the help of his countenance.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> O my God, my soul is cast down within me: therefore will I remember thee from the land of Jordan, and of the Hermonites, from the hill Mizar.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Deep calleth unto deep at the noise of thy waterspouts: all thy waves and thy billows are gone over me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Yet the Lord will command his lovingkindness in the daytime, and in the night his song shall be with me, and my prayer unto the God of my life.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I will say unto God my rock, Why hast thou forgotten me? why go I mourning because of the oppression of the enemy?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As with a sword in my bones, mine enemies reproach me; while they say daily unto me, Where is thy God?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Why art thou cast down, O my soul? and why art thou disquieted within me? hope thou in God: for I shall yet praise him, who is the health of my countenance, and my God.
