<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: because his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let Israel now say, that his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let the house of Aaron now say, that his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Let them now that fear the Lord say, that his mercy endureth for ever.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I called upon the Lord in distress: the Lord answered me, and set me in a large place.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The Lord is on my side; I will not fear: what can man do unto me?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The Lord taketh my part with them that help me: therefore shall I see my desire upon them that hate me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> It is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in man.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> It is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in princes.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> All nations compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord will I destroy them.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> They compassed me about; yea, they compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I will destroy them.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They compassed me about like bees; they are quenched as the fire of thorns: for in the name of the Lord I will destroy them.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou hast thrust sore at me that I might fall: but the Lord helped me.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Lord is my strength and song, and is become my salvation.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The voice of rejoicing and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous: the right hand of the Lord doeth valiantly.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The right hand of the Lord is exalted: the right hand of the Lord doeth valiantly.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I shall not die, but live, and declare the works of the Lord.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The Lord hath chastened me sore: but he hath not given me over unto death.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will go into them, and I will praise the Lord:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> This gate of the Lord, into which the righteous shall enter.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> I will praise thee: for thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> This is the Lord’s doing; it is marvellous in our eyes.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> This is the day which the Lord hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Save now, I beseech thee, O Lord: O Lord, I beseech thee, send now prosperity.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord: we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> God is the Lord, which hath shewed us light: bind the sacrifice with cords, even unto the horns of the altar.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Thou art my God, and I will praise thee: thou art my God, I will exalt thee.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.
