<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> They that trust in the Lord shall be as mount Zion, which cannot be removed, but abideth for ever.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> As the mountains are round about Jerusalem, so the Lord is round about his people from henceforth even for ever.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For the rod of the wicked shall not rest upon the lot of the righteous; lest the righteous put forth their hands unto iniquity.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Do good, O Lord, unto those that be good, and to them that are upright in their hearts.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> As for such as turn aside unto their crooked ways, the Lord shall lead them forth with the workers of iniquity: but peace shall be upon Israel.
