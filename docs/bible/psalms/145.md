<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will extol thee, my God, O king; and I will bless thy name for ever and ever.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Every day will I bless thee; and I will praise thy name for ever and ever.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised; and his greatness is unsearchable.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> One generation shall praise thy works to another, and shall declare thy mighty acts.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I will speak of the glorious honour of thy majesty, and of thy wondrous works.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And men shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and I will declare thy greatness.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They shall abundantly utter the memory of thy great goodness, and shall sing of thy righteousness.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Lord is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The Lord is good to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> All thy works shall praise thee, O Lord; and thy saints shall bless thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom, and talk of thy power;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> To make known to the sons of men his mighty acts, and the glorious majesty of his kingdom.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thy kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Lord upholdeth all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest them their meat in due season.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Thou openest thine hand, and satisfiest the desire of every living thing.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The Lord is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him: he also will hear their cry, and will save them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The Lord preserveth all them that love him: but all the wicked will he destroy.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever.
