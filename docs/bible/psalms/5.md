<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Give ear to my words, O Lord, consider my meditation.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King, and my God: for unto thee will I pray.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O Lord; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness: neither shall evil dwell with thee.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The foolish shall not stand in thy sight: thou hatest all workers of iniquity.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing: the Lord will abhor the bloody and deceitful man.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But as for me, I will come into thy house in the multitude of thy mercy: and in thy fear will I worship toward thy holy temple.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Lead me, O Lord, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For there is no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness; their throat is an open sepulchre; they flatter with their tongue.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Destroy thou them, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions; for they have rebelled against thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice: let them ever shout for joy, because thou defendest them: let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For thou, Lord, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield.
