<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Jerusalem is builded as a city that is compact together:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Whither the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord, unto the testimony of Israel, to give thanks unto the name of the Lord.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For there are set thrones of judgment, the thrones of the house of David.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For my brethren and companions’ sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Because of the house of the Lord our God I will seek thy good.
