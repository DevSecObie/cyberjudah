<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Save me, O God, by thy name, and judge me by thy strength.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hear my prayer, O God; give ear to the words of my mouth.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For strangers are risen up against me, and oppressors seek after my soul: they have not set God before them. Selah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Behold, God is mine helper: the Lord is with them that uphold my soul.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He shall reward evil unto mine enemies: cut them off in thy truth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I will freely sacrifice unto thee: I will praise thy name, O Lord; for it is good.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For he hath delivered me out of all trouble: and mine eye hath seen his desire upon mine enemies.
