<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will sing of mercy and judgment: unto thee, O Lord, will I sing.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I will behave myself wisely in a perfect way. O when wilt thou come unto me? I will walk within my house with a perfect heart.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that turn aside; it shall not cleave to me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> A froward heart shall depart from me: I will not know a wicked person.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walketh in a perfect way, he shall serve me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I will early destroy all the wicked of the land; that I may cut off all wicked doers from the city of the Lord.
