<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, remember David, and all his afflictions:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How he sware unto the Lord, and vowed unto the mighty God of Jacob;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Surely I will not come into the tabernacle of my house, nor go up into my bed;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I will not give sleep to mine eyes, or slumber to mine eyelids,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Until I find out a place for the Lord, an habitation for the mighty God of Jacob.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Lo, we heard of it at Ephratah: we found it in the fields of the wood.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> We will go into his tabernacles: we will worship at his footstool.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Arise, O Lord, into thy rest; thou, and the ark of thy strength.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For thy servant David’s sake turn not away the face of thine anointed.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The Lord hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If thy children will keep my covenant and my testimony that I shall teach them, their children shall also sit upon thy throne for evermore.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For the Lord hath chosen Zion; he hath desired it for his habitation.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell; for I have desired it.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I will abundantly bless her provision: I will satisfy her poor with bread.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I will also clothe her priests with salvation: and her saints shall shout aloud for joy.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> There will I make the horn of David to bud: I have ordained a lamp for mine anointed.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> His enemies will I clothe with shame: but upon himself shall his crown flourish.
