<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise, even with my glory.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord, among the people: and I will sing praises unto thee among the nations.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens: and thy glory above all the earth;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> That thy beloved may be delivered: save with thy right hand, and answer me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; over Philistia will I triumph.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our hosts?

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies.
