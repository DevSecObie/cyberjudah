<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, thou hast been favourable unto thy land: thou hast brought back the captivity of Jacob.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of thy people, thou hast covered all their sin. Selah.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou hast taken away all thy wrath: thou hast turned thyself from the fierceness of thine anger.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Turn us, O God of our salvation, and cause thine anger toward us to cease.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Wilt thou be angry with us for ever? wilt thou draw out thine anger to all generations?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Wilt thou not revive us again: that thy people may rejoice in thee?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Shew us thy mercy, O Lord, and grant us thy salvation.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I will hear what God the Lord will speak: for he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: but let them not turn again to folly.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Surely his salvation is nigh them that fear him; that glory may dwell in our land.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Yea, the Lord shall give that which is good; and our land shall yield her increase.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Righteousness shall go before him; and shall set us in the way of his steps.
