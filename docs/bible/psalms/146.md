<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise the Lord, O my soul.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> While I live will I praise the Lord: I will sing praises unto my God while I have any being.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Happy is he that hath the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the Lord his God:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Which made heaven, and earth, the sea, and all that therein is: which keepeth truth for ever:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Which executeth judgment for the oppressed: which giveth food to the hungry. The Lord looseth the prisoners:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Lord openeth the eyes of the blind: the Lord raiseth them that are bowed down: the Lord loveth the righteous:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The Lord preserveth the strangers; he relieveth the fatherless and widow: but the way of the wicked he turneth upside down.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The Lord shall reign for ever, even thy God, O Zion, unto all generations. Praise ye the Lord.
