<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.
