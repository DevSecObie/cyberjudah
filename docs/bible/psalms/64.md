<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.
