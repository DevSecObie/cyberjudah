<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O sing unto the Lord a new song: sing unto the Lord, all the earth.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Sing unto the Lord, bless his name; shew forth his salvation from day to day.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Declare his glory among the heathen, his wonders among all people.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For all the gods of the nations are idols: but the Lord made the heavens.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Honour and majesty are before him: strength and beauty are in his sanctuary.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Give unto the Lord, O ye kindreds of the people, give unto the Lord glory and strength.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name: bring an offering, and come into his courts.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> O worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness: fear before him, all the earth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Say among the heathen that the Lord reigneth: the world also shall be established that it shall not be moved: he shall judge the people righteously.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Let the field be joyful, and all that is therein: then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Before the Lord: for he cometh, for he cometh to judge the earth: he shall judge the world with righteousness, and the people with his truth.
