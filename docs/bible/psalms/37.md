<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Fret not thyself because of evildoers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For they shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither as the green herb.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Trust in the Lord, and do good; so shalt thou dwell in the land, and verily thou shalt be fed.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Rest in the Lord, and wait patiently for him: fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself in any wise to do evil.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For evildoers shall be cut off: but those that wait upon the Lord, they shall inherit the earth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The wicked plotteth against the just, and gnasheth upon him with his teeth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The Lord shall laugh at him: for he seeth that his day is coming.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, and to slay such as be of upright conversation.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Their sword shall enter into their own heart, and their bows shall be broken.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> A little that a righteous man hath is better than the riches of many wicked.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For the arms of the wicked shall be broken: but the Lord upholdeth the righteous.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The Lord knoweth the days of the upright: and their inheritance shall be for ever.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> They shall not be ashamed in the evil time: and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the Lord shall be as the fat of lambs: they shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For such as be blessed of him shall inherit the earth; and they that be cursed of him shall be cut off.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the Lord upholdeth him with his hand.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He is ever merciful, and lendeth; and his seed is blessed.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; and dwell for evermore.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For the Lord loveth judgment, and forsaketh not his saints; they are preserved for ever: but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> The mouth of the righteous speaketh wisdom, and his tongue talketh of judgment.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The law of his God is in his heart; none of his steps shall slide.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> The wicked watcheth the righteous, and seeketh to slay him.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> The Lord will not leave him in his hand, nor condemn him when he is judged.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Wait on the Lord, and keep his way, and he shall exalt thee to inherit the land: when the wicked are cut off, thou shalt see it.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> I have seen the wicked in great power, and spreading himself like a green bay tree.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Yet he passed away, and, lo, he was not: yea, I sought him, but he could not be found.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Mark the perfect man, and behold the upright: for the end of that man is peace.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> But the transgressors shall be destroyed together: the end of the wicked shall be cut off.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> But the salvation of the righteous is of the Lord: he is their strength in the time of trouble.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And the Lord shall help them, and deliver them: he shall deliver them from the wicked, and save them, because they trust in him.
