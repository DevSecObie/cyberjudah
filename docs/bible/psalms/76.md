<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In Judah is God known: his name is great in Israel.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> In Salem also is his tabernacle, and his dwelling place in Zion.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> There brake he the arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the battle. Selah.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou art more glorious and excellent than the mountains of prey.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep: and none of the men of might have found their hands.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou, even thou, art to be feared: and who may stand in thy sight when once thou art angry?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven; the earth feared, and was still,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> When God arose to judgment, to save all the meek of the earth. Selah.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Surely the wrath of man shall praise thee: the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Vow, and pay unto the Lord your God: let all that be round about him bring presents unto him that ought to be feared.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He shall cut off the spirit of princes: he is terrible to the kings of the earth.
