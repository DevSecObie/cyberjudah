<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Lord, I cry unto thee: make haste unto me; give ear unto my voice, when I cry unto thee.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let my prayer be set forth before thee as incense; and the lifting up of my hands as the evening sacrifice.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Set a watch, O Lord, before my mouth; keep the door of my lips.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Incline not my heart to any evil thing, to practise wicked works with men that work iniquity: and let me not eat of their dainties.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let the righteous smite me; it shall be a kindness: and let him reprove me; it shall be an excellent oil, which shall not break my head: for yet my prayer also shall be in their calamities.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> When their judges are overthrown in stony places, they shall hear my words; for they are sweet.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Our bones are scattered at the grave’s mouth, as when one cutteth and cleaveth wood upon the earth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But mine eyes are unto thee, O God the Lord: in thee is my trust; leave not my soul destitute.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Keep me from the snares which they have laid for me, and the gins of the workers of iniquity.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Let the wicked fall into their own nets, whilst that I withal escape.
