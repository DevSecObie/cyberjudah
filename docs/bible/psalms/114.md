<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Judah was his sanctuary, and Israel his dominion.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The sea saw it, and fled: Jordan was driven back.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The mountains skipped like rams, and the little hills like lambs.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou wast driven back?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams; and ye little hills, like lambs?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Which turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters.
