<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Sing aloud unto God our strength: make a joyful noise unto the God of Jacob.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel, the pleasant harp with the psaltery.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Blow up the trumpet in the new moon, in the time appointed, on our solemn feast day.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For this was a statute for Israel, and a law of the God of Jacob.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> This he ordained in Joseph for a testimony, when he went out through the land of Egypt: where I heard a language that I understood not.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I removed his shoulder from the burden: his hands were delivered from the pots.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou calledst in trouble, and I delivered thee; I answered thee in the secret place of thunder: I proved thee at the waters of Meribah. Selah.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Hear, O my people, and I will testify unto thee: O Israel, if thou wilt hearken unto me;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> There shall no strange god be in thee; neither shalt thou worship any strange god.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I am the Lord thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But my people would not hearken to my voice; and Israel would none of me.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So I gave them up unto their own hearts’ lust: and they walked in their own counsels.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Oh that my people had hearkened unto me, and Israel had walked in my ways!

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I should soon have subdued their enemies, and turned my hand against their adversaries.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The haters of the Lord should have submitted themselves unto him: but their time should have endured for ever.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He should have fed them also with the finest of the wheat: and with honey out of the rock should I have satisfied thee.
