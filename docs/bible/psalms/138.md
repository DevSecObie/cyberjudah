<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will praise thee with my whole heart: before the gods will I sing praise unto thee.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> In the day when I cried thou answeredst me, and strengthenedst me with strength in my soul.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> All the kings of the earth shall praise thee, O Lord, when they hear the words of thy mouth.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Yea, they shall sing in the ways of the Lord: for great is the glory of the Lord.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Though the Lord be high, yet hath he respect unto the lowly: but the proud he knoweth afar off.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Though I walk in the midst of trouble, thou wilt revive me: thou shalt stretch forth thine hand against the wrath of mine enemies, and thy right hand shall save me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Lord will perfect that which concerneth me: thy mercy, O Lord, endureth for ever: forsake not the works of thine own hands.
