<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Bow down thine ear, O Lord, hear me: for I am poor and needy.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Be merciful unto me, O Lord: for I cry unto thee daily.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For thou, Lord, art good, and ready to forgive; and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Give ear, O Lord, unto my prayer; and attend to the voice of my supplications.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In the day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Among the gods there is none like unto thee, O Lord; neither are there any works like unto thy works.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For thou art great, and doest wondrous things: thou art God alone.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Teach me thy way, O Lord; I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with all my heart: and I will glorify thy name for evermore.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For great is thy mercy toward me: and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> O God, the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men have sought after my soul; and have not set thee before them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But thou, O Lord, art a God full of compassion, and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> O turn unto me, and have mercy upon me; give thy strength unto thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Shew me a token for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed: because thou, Lord, hast holpen me, and comforted me.
