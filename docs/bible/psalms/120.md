<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In my distress I cried unto the Lord, and he heard me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Deliver my soul, O Lord, from lying lips, and from a deceitful tongue.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> What shall be given unto thee? or what shall be done unto thee, thou false tongue?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals of juniper.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech, that I dwell in the tents of Kedar!

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I am for peace: but when I speak, they are for war.
