<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise God in his sanctuary: praise him in the firmament of his power.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Praise him for his mighty acts: praise him according to his excellent greatness.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Praise him with the sound of the trumpet: praise him with the psaltery and harp.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Praise him with the timbrel and dance: praise him with stringed instruments and organs.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Praise him upon the loud cymbals: praise him upon the high sounding cymbals.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Let every thing that hath breath praise the Lord. Praise ye the Lord.
