<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O come, let us sing unto the Lord: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For the Lord is a great God, and a great King above all gods.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> In his hand are the deep places of the earth: the strength of the hills is his also.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the Lord our maker.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For he is our God; and we are the people of his pasture, and the sheep of his hand. To day if ye will hear his voice,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Harden not your heart, as in the provocation, and as in the day of temptation in the wilderness:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my work.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Forty years long was I grieved with this generation, and said, It is a people that do err in their heart, and they have not known my ways:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Unto whom I sware in my wrath that they should not enter into my rest.
