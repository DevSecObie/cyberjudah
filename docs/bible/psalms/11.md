<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In the Lord put I my trust: how say ye to my soul, Flee as a bird to your mountain?

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For, lo, the wicked bend their bow, they make ready their arrow upon the string, that they may privily shoot at the upright in heart.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> If the foundations be destroyed, what can the righteous do?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The Lord is in his holy temple, the Lord’s throne is in heaven: his eyes behold, his eyelids try, the children of men.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The Lord trieth the righteous: but the wicked and him that loveth violence his soul hateth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Upon the wicked he shall rain snares, fire and brimstone, and an horrible tempest: this shall be the portion of their cup.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For the righteous Lord loveth righteousness; his countenance doth behold the upright.
