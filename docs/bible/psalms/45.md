<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My heart is inditing a good matter: I speak of the things which I have made touching the king: my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thou art fairer than the children of men: grace is poured into thy lips: therefore God hath blessed thee for ever.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O most mighty, with thy glory and thy majesty.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And in thy majesty ride prosperously because of truth and meekness and righteousness; and thy right hand shall teach thee terrible things.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thine arrows are sharp in the heart of the king’s enemies; whereby the people fall under thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a right sceptre.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> All thy garments smell of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia, out of the ivory palaces, whereby they have made thee glad.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Kings’ daughters were among thy honourable women: upon thy right hand did stand the queen in gold of Ophir.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear; forget also thine own people, and thy father’s house;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty: for he is thy Lord; and worship thou him.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the daughter of Tyre shall be there with a gift; even the rich among the people shall intreat thy favour.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The king’s daughter is all glorious within: her clothing is of wrought gold.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> She shall be brought unto the king in raiment of needlework: the virgins her companions that follow her shall be brought unto thee.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> With gladness and rejoicing shall they be brought: they shall enter into the king’s palace.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children, whom thou mayest make princes in all the earth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I will make thy name to be remembered in all generations: therefore shall the people praise thee for ever and ever.
