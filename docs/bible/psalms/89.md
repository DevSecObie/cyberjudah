<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will sing of the mercies of the Lord for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very heavens.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I have made a covenant with my chosen, I have sworn unto David my servant,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thy seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Selah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O Lord: thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord? who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto the Lord?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> God is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence of all them that are about him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy strong arm.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: as for the world and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> In thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy favour our horn shall be exalted.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For the Lord is our defence; and the Holy One of Israel is our king.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Then thou spakest in vision to thy holy one, and saidst, I have laid help upon one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen out of the people.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> With whom my hand shall be established: mine arm also shall strengthen him.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The enemy shall not exact upon him; nor the son of wickedness afflict him.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And I will beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He shall cry unto me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> His seed also will I make to endure for ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments;

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Nevertheless my lovingkindness will I not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie unto David.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven. Selah.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> But thou hast cast off and abhorred, thou hast been wroth with thine anointed.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his crown by casting it to the ground.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast brought his strong holds to ruin.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> All that pass by the way spoil him: he is a reproach to his neighbours.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> Thou hast set up the right hand of his adversaries; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Thou hast also turned the edge of his sword, and hast not made him to stand in the battle.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> Thou hast made his glory to cease, and cast his throne down to the ground.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> The days of his youth hast thou shortened: thou hast covered him with shame. Selah.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> How long, Lord? wilt thou hide thyself for ever? shall thy wrath burn like fire?

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> Remember how short my time is: wherefore hast thou made all men in vain?

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> What man is he that liveth, and shall not see death? shall he deliver his soul from the hand of the grave? Selah.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> Lord, where are thy former lovingkindnesses, which thou swarest unto David in thy truth?

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> Remember, Lord, the reproach of thy servants; how I do bear in my bosom the reproach of all the mighty people;

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> Wherewith thine enemies have reproached, O Lord; wherewith they have reproached the footsteps of thine anointed.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> Blessed be the Lord for evermore. Amen, and Amen.
