<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then verily the first covenant had also ordinances of divine service, and a worldly sanctuary.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For there was a tabernacle made; the first, wherein was the candlestick, and the table, and the shewbread; which is called the sanctuary.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And after the second veil, the tabernacle which is called the Holiest of all;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Which had the golden censer, and the ark of the covenant overlaid round about with gold, wherein was the golden pot that had manna, and Aaron’s rod that budded, and the tables of the covenant;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And over it the cherubims of glory shadowing the mercyseat; of which we cannot now speak particularly.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Now when these things were thus ordained, the priests went always into the first tabernacle, accomplishing the service of God.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But into the second went the high priest alone once every year, not without blood, which he offered for himself, and for the errors of the people:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The Holy Ghost this signifying, that the way into the holiest of all was not yet made manifest, while as the first tabernacle was yet standing:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Which was a figure for the time then present, in which were offered both gifts and sacrifices, that could not make him that did the service perfect, as pertaining to the conscience;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Which stood only in meats and drinks, and divers washings, and carnal ordinances, imposed on them until the time of reformation.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But Christ being come an high priest of good things to come, by a greater and more perfect tabernacle, not made with hands, that is to say, not of this building;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Neither by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, having obtained eternal redemption for us.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For if the blood of bulls and of goats, and the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> How much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without spot to God, purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For where a testament is, there must also of necessity be the death of the testator.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For a testament is of force after men are dead: otherwise it is of no strength at all while the testator liveth.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Whereupon neither the first testament was dedicated without blood.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For when Moses had spoken every precept to all the people according to the law, he took the blood of calves and of goats, with water, and scarlet wool, and hyssop, and sprinkled both the book, and all the people,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Saying, This is the blood of the testament which God hath enjoined unto you.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Moreover he sprinkled with blood both the tabernacle, and all the vessels of the ministry.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And almost all things are by the law purged with blood; and without shedding of blood is no remission.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> It was therefore necessary that the patterns of things in the heavens should be purified with these; but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For Christ is not entered into the holy places made with hands, which are the figures of the true; but into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God for us:

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Nor yet that he should offer himself often, as the high priest entereth into the holy place every year with blood of others;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For then must he often have suffered since the foundation of the world: but now once in the end of the world hath he appeared to put away sin by the sacrifice of himself.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And as it is appointed unto men once to die, but after this the judgment:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> So Christ was once offered to bear the sins of many; and unto them that look for him shall he appear the second time without sin unto salvation.
