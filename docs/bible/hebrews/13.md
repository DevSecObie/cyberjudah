<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Let brotherly love continue.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Be not carried about with divers and strange doctrines. For it is a good thing that the heart be established with grace; not with meats, which have not profited them that have been occupied therein.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> We have an altar, whereof they have no right to eat which serve the tabernacle.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sin, are burned without the camp.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For here have we no continuing city, but we seek one to come.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> By him therefore let us offer the sacrifice of praise to God continually, that is, the fruit of our lips giving thanks to his name.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But to do good and to communicate forget not: for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account, that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Pray for us: for we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But I beseech you the rather to do this, that I may be restored to you the sooner.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Now the God of peace, that brought again from the dead our Lord Jesus, that great shepherd of the sheep, through the blood of the everlasting covenant,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Make you perfect in every good work to do his will, working in you that which is wellpleasing in his sight, through Jesus Christ; to whom be glory for ever and ever. Amen.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And I beseech you, brethren, suffer the word of exhortation: for I have written a letter unto you in few words.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Know ye that our brother Timothy is set at liberty; with whom, if he come shortly, I will see you.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Salute all them that have the rule over you, and all the saints. They of Italy salute you.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Grace be with you all. Amen. Written to the Hebrews from Italy by Timothy.
