<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now of the things which we have spoken this is the sum: We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> A minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For every high priest is ordained to offer gifts and sacrifices: wherefore it is of necessity that this man have somewhat also to offer.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For if he were on earth, he should not be a priest, seeing that there are priests that offer gifts according to the law:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But now hath he obtained a more excellent ministry, by how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant, which was established upon better promises.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For if that first covenant had been faultless, then should no place have been sought for the second.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt; because they continued not in my covenant, and I regarded them not, saith the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, saith the Lord; I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts: and I will be to them a God, and they shall be to me a people:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And they shall not teach every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for all shall know me, from the least to the greatest.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For I will be merciful to their unrighteousness, and their sins and their iniquities will I remember no more.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old. Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away.
