<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then Tobias called Raphael, and said unto him,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Brother Azarias, take with thee a servant, and two camels, and go to Rages of Media to Gabael, and bring me the money, and bring him to the wedding.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For Raguel hath sworn that I shall not depart.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But my father counteth the days; and if I tarry long, he will be very sorry.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> So Raphael went out, and lodged with Gabael, and gave him the handwriting: who brought forth bags which were sealed up, and gave them to him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And early in the morning they went forth both together, and came to the wedding: and Tobias blessed his wife.
