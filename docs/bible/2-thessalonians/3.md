<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Finally, brethren, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may have free course, and be glorified, even as it is with you:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And that we may be delivered from unreasonable and wicked men: for all men have not faith.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But the Lord is faithful, who shall stablish you, and keep you from evil.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And we have confidence in the Lord touching you, that ye both do and will do the things which we command you.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the Lord direct your hearts into the love of God, and into the patient waiting for Christ.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Now we command you, brethren, in the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye withdraw yourselves from every brother that walketh disorderly, and not after the tradition which he received of us.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For yourselves know how ye ought to follow us: for we behaved not ourselves disorderly among you;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Neither did we eat any man’s bread for nought; but wrought with labour and travail night and day, that we might not be chargeable to any of you:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Not because we have not power, but to make ourselves an ensample unto you to follow us.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if any would not work, neither should he eat.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For we hear that there are some which walk among you disorderly, working not at all, but are busybodies.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Now them that are such we command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ, that with quietness they work, and eat their own bread.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But ye, brethren, be not weary in well doing.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And if any man obey not our word by this epistle, note that man, and have no company with him, that he may be ashamed.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Yet count him not as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Now the Lord of peace himself give you peace always by all means. The Lord be with you all.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The salutation of Paul with mine own hand, which is the token in every epistle: so I write.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you all. Amen. The second epistle to the Thessalonians was written from Athens.
