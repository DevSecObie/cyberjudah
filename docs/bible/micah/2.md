<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Woe to them that devise iniquity, and work evil upon their beds! when the morning is light, they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And they covet fields, and take them by violence; and houses, and take them away: so they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, against this family do I devise an evil, from which ye shall not remove your necks; neither shall ye go haughtily: for this time is evil.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> In that day shall one take up a parable against you, and lament with a doleful lamentation, and say, We be utterly spoiled: he hath changed the portion of my people: how hath he removed it from me! turning away he hath divided our fields.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Therefore thou shalt have none that shall cast a cord by lot in the congregation of the Lord.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Prophesy ye not, say they to them that prophesy: they shall not prophesy to them, that they shall not take shame.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> O thou that art named the house of Jacob, is the spirit of the Lord straitened? are these his doings? do not my words do good to him that walketh uprightly?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Even of late my people is risen up as an enemy: ye pull off the robe with the garment from them that pass by securely as men averse from war.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The women of my people have ye cast out from their pleasant houses; from their children have ye taken away my glory for ever.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your rest: because it is polluted, it shall destroy you, even with a sore destruction.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> If a man walking in the spirit and falsehood do lie, saying, I will prophesy unto thee of wine and of strong drink; he shall even be the prophet of this people.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I will surely assemble, O Jacob, all of thee; I will surely gather the remnant of Israel; I will put them together as the sheep of Bozrah, as the flock in the midst of their fold: they shall make great noise by reason of the multitude of men.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The breaker is come up before them: they have broken up, and have passed through the gate, and are gone out by it: and their king shall pass before them, and the Lord on the head of them.
