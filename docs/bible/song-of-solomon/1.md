<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The song of songs, which is Solomon’s.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Let him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth: for thy love is better than wine.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Because of the savour of thy good ointments thy name is as ointment poured forth, therefore do the virgins love thee.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Draw me, we will run after thee: the king hath brought me into his chambers: we will be glad and rejoice in thee, we will remember thy love more than wine: the upright love thee.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I am black, but comely, O ye daughters of Jerusalem, as the tents of Kedar, as the curtains of Solomon.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Look not upon me, because I am black, because the sun hath looked upon me: my mother’s children were angry with me; they made me the keeper of the vineyards; but mine own vineyard have I not kept.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Tell me, O thou whom my soul loveth, where thou feedest, where thou makest thy flock to rest at noon: for why should I be as one that turneth aside by the flocks of thy companions?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> If thou know not, O thou fairest among women, go thy way forth by the footsteps of the flock, and feed thy kids beside the shepherds’ tents.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I have compared thee, O my love, to a company of horses in Pharaoh’s chariots.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thy cheeks are comely with rows of jewels, thy neck with chains of gold.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> We will make thee borders of gold with studs of silver.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> While the king sitteth at his table, my spikenard sendeth forth the smell thereof.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> A bundle of myrrh is my wellbeloved unto me; he shall lie all night betwixt my breasts.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> My beloved is unto me as a cluster of camphire in the vineyards of En–gedi.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Behold, thou art fair, my love; behold, thou art fair; thou hast doves’ eyes.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Behold, thou art fair, my beloved, yea, pleasant: also our bed is green.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The beams of our house are cedar, and our rafters of fir.
