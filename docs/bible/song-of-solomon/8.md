<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O that thou wert as my brother, that sucked the breasts of my mother! when I should find thee without, I would kiss thee; yea, I should not be despised.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I would lead thee, and bring thee into my mother’s house, who would instruct me: I would cause thee to drink of spiced wine of the juice of my pomegranate.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> His left hand should be under my head, and his right hand should embrace me.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem, that ye stir not up, nor awake my love, until he please.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who is this that cometh up from the wilderness, leaning upon her beloved? I raised thee up under the apple tree: there thy mother brought thee forth: there she brought thee forth that bare thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Set me as a seal upon thine heart, as a seal upon thine arm: for love is strong as death; jealousy is cruel as the grave: the coals thereof are coals of fire, which hath a most vehement flame.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Many waters cannot quench love, neither can the floods drown it: if a man would give all the substance of his house for love, it would utterly be contemned.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> We have a little sister, and she hath no breasts: what shall we do for our sister in the day when she shall be spoken for?

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> If she be a wall, we will build upon her a palace of silver: and if she be a door, we will inclose her with boards of cedar.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I am a wall, and my breasts like towers: then was I in his eyes as one that found favour.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Solomon had a vineyard at Baal–hamon; he let out the vineyard unto keepers; every one for the fruit thereof was to bring a thousand pieces of silver.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> My vineyard, which is mine, is before me: thou, O Solomon, must have a thousand, and those that keep the fruit thereof two hundred.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thou that dwellest in the gardens, the companions hearken to thy voice: cause me to hear it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Make haste, my beloved, and be thou like to a roe or to a young hart upon the mountains of spices.
