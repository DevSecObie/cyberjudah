<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Behold, thou art fair, my love; behold, thou art fair; thou hast doves’ eyes within thy locks: thy hair is as a flock of goats, that appear from mount Gilead.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thy teeth are like a flock of sheep that are even shorn, which came up from the washing; whereof every one bear twins, and none is barren among them.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thy lips are like a thread of scarlet, and thy speech is comely: thy temples are like a piece of a pomegranate within thy locks.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thy neck is like the tower of David builded for an armoury, whereon there hang a thousand bucklers, all shields of mighty men.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Thy two breasts are like two young roes that are twins, which feed among the lilies.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Until the day break, and the shadows flee away, I will get me to the mountain of myrrh, and to the hill of frankincense.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou art all fair, my love; there is no spot in thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Come with me from Lebanon, my spouse, with me from Lebanon: look from the top of Amana, from the top of Shenir and Hermon, from the lions’ dens, from the mountains of the leopards.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Thou hast ravished my heart, my sister, my spouse; thou hast ravished my heart with one of thine eyes, with one chain of thy neck.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> How fair is thy love, my sister, my spouse! how much better is thy love than wine! and the smell of thine ointments than all spices!

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thy lips, O my spouse, drop as the honeycomb: honey and milk are under thy tongue; and the smell of thy garments is like the smell of Lebanon.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> A garden inclosed is my sister, my spouse; a spring shut up, a fountain sealed.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thy plants are an orchard of pomegranates, with pleasant fruits; camphire, with spikenard,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Spikenard and saffron; calamus and cinnamon, with all trees of frankincense; myrrh and aloes, with all the chief spices:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> A fountain of gardens, a well of living waters, and streams from Lebanon.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Awake, O north wind; and come, thou south; blow upon my garden, that the spices thereof may flow out. Let my beloved come into his garden, and eat his pleasant fruits.
