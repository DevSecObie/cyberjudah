<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I am come into my garden, my sister, my spouse: I have gathered my myrrh with my spice; I have eaten my honeycomb with my honey; I have drunk my wine with my milk: eat, O friends; drink, yea, drink abundantly, O beloved.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I sleep, but my heart waketh: it is the voice of my beloved that knocketh, saying, Open to me, my sister, my love, my dove, my undefiled: for my head is filled with dew, and my locks with the drops of the night.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I have put off my coat; how shall I put it on? I have washed my feet; how shall I defile them?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> My beloved put in his hand by the hole of the door, and my bowels were moved for him.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I rose up to open to my beloved; and my hands dropped with myrrh, and my fingers with sweet smelling myrrh, upon the handles of the lock.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> I opened to my beloved; but my beloved had withdrawn himself, and was gone: my soul failed when he spake: I sought him, but I could not find him; I called him, but he gave me no answer.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The watchmen that went about the city found me, they smote me, they wounded me; the keepers of the walls took away my veil from me.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I charge you, O daughters of Jerusalem, if ye find my beloved, that ye tell him, that I am sick of love.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> What is thy beloved more than another beloved, O thou fairest among women? what is thy beloved more than another beloved, that thou dost so charge us?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> My beloved is white and ruddy, the chiefest among ten thousand.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> His head is as the most fine gold, his locks are bushy, and black as a raven.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> His eyes are as the eyes of doves by the rivers of waters, washed with milk, and fitly set.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> His cheeks are as a bed of spices, as sweet flowers: his lips like lilies, dropping sweet smelling myrrh.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> His hands are as gold rings set with the beryl: his belly is as bright ivory overlaid with sapphires.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> His legs are as pillars of marble, set upon sockets of fine gold: his countenance is as Lebanon, excellent as the cedars.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> His mouth is most sweet: yea, he is altogether lovely. This is my beloved, and this is my friend, O daughters of Jerusalem.
