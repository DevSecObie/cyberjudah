<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I am the rose of Sharon, and the lily of the valleys.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> As the lily among thorns, so is my love among the daughters.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> As the apple tree among the trees of the wood, so is my beloved among the sons. I sat down under his shadow with great delight, and his fruit was sweet to my taste.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He brought me to the banqueting house, and his banner over me was love.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Stay me with flagons, comfort me with apples: for I am sick of love.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> His left hand is under my head, and his right hand doth embrace me.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> I charge you, O ye daughters of Jerusalem, by the roes, and by the hinds of the field, that ye stir not up, nor awake my love, till he please.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The voice of my beloved! behold, he cometh leaping upon the mountains, skipping upon the hills.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> My beloved is like a roe or a young hart: behold, he standeth behind our wall, he looketh forth at the windows, shewing himself through the lattice.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> My beloved spake, and said unto me, Rise up, my love, my fair one, and come away.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For, lo, the winter is past, the rain is over and gone;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The flowers appear on the earth; the time of the singing of birds is come, and the voice of the turtle is heard in our land;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The fig tree putteth forth her green figs, and the vines with the tender grape give a good smell. Arise, my love, my fair one, and come away.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> O my dove, that art in the clefts of the rock, in the secret places of the stairs, let me see thy countenance, let me hear thy voice; for sweet is thy voice, and thy countenance is comely.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Take us the foxes, the little foxes, that spoil the vines: for our vines have tender grapes.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> My beloved is mine, and I am his: he feedeth among the lilies.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Until the day break, and the shadows flee away, turn, my beloved, and be thou like a roe or a young hart upon the mountains of Bether.
