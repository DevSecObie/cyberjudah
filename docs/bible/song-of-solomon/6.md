<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Whither is thy beloved gone, O thou fairest among women? whither is thy beloved turned aside? that we may seek him with thee.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> My beloved is gone down into his garden, to the beds of spices, to feed in the gardens, and to gather lilies.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I am my beloved’s, and my beloved is mine: he feedeth among the lilies.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou art beautiful, O my love, as Tirzah, comely as Jerusalem, terrible as an army with banners.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Turn away thine eyes from me, for they have overcome me: thy hair is as a flock of goats that appear from Gilead.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thy teeth are as a flock of sheep which go up from the washing, whereof every one beareth twins, and there is not one barren among them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> As a piece of a pomegranate are thy temples within thy locks.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> There are threescore queens, and fourscore concubines, and virgins without number.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> My dove, my undefiled is but one; she is the only one of her mother, she is the choice one of her that bare her. The daughters saw her, and blessed her; yea, the queens and the concubines, and they praised her.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who is she that looketh forth as the morning, fair as the moon, clear as the sun, and terrible as an army with banners?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I went down into the garden of nuts to see the fruits of the valley, and to see whether the vine flourished, and the pomegranates budded.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Or ever I was aware, my soul made me like the chariots of Amminadib.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Return, return, O Shulamite; return, return, that we may look upon thee. What will ye see in the Shulamite? As it were the company of two armies.
