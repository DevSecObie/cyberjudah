<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The elders which are among you I exhort, who am also an elder, and a witness of the sufferings of Christ, and also a partaker of the glory that shall be revealed:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Feed the flock of God which is among you, taking the oversight thereof, not by constraint, but willingly; not for filthy lucre, but of a ready mind;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Neither as being lords over God’s heritage, but being ensamples to the flock.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And when the chief Shepherd shall appear, ye shall receive a crown of glory that fadeth not away.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Likewise, ye younger, submit yourselves unto the elder. Yea, all of you be subject one to another, and be clothed with humility: for God resisteth the proud, and giveth grace to the humble.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Humble yourselves therefore under the mighty hand of God, that he may exalt you in due time:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Whom resist stedfast in the faith, knowing that the same afflictions are accomplished in your brethren that are in the world.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> To him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> By Silvanus, a faithful brother unto you, as I suppose, I have written briefly, exhorting, and testifying that this is the true grace of God wherein ye stand.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The church that is at Babylon, elected together with you, saluteth you; and so doth Marcus my son.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Greet ye one another with a kiss of charity. Peace be with you all that are in Christ Jesus. Amen.
