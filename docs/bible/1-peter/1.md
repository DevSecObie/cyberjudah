<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which according to his abundant mercy hath begotten us again unto a lively hope by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> To an inheritance incorruptible, and undefiled, and that fadeth not away, reserved in heaven for you,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Who are kept by the power of God through faith unto salvation ready to be revealed in the last time.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Wherein ye greatly rejoice, though now for a season, if need be, ye are in heaviness through manifold temptations:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> That the trial of your faith, being much more precious than of gold that perisheth, though it be tried with fire, might be found unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Whom having not seen, ye love; in whom, though now ye see him not, yet believing, ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Receiving the end of your faith, even the salvation of your souls.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Of which salvation the prophets have enquired and searched diligently, who prophesied of the grace that should come unto you:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Searching what, or what manner of time the Spirit of Christ which was in them did signify, when it testified beforehand the sufferings of Christ, and the glory that should follow.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Unto whom it was revealed, that not unto themselves, but unto us they did minister the things, which are now reported unto you by them that have preached the gospel unto you with the Holy Ghost sent down from heaven; which things the angels desire to look into.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Wherefore gird up the loins of your mind, be sober, and hope to the end for the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> As obedient children, not fashioning yourselves according to the former lusts in your ignorance:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And if ye call on the Father, who without respect of persons judgeth according to every man’s work, pass the time of your sojourning here in fear:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Forasmuch as ye know that ye were not redeemed with corruptible things, as silver and gold, from your vain conversation received by tradition from your fathers;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But with the precious blood of Christ, as of a lamb without blemish and without spot:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Who verily was foreordained before the foundation of the world, but was manifest in these last times for you,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Who by him do believe in God, that raised him up from the dead, and gave him glory; that your faith and hope might be in God.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Seeing ye have purified your souls in obeying the truth through the Spirit unto unfeigned love of the brethren, see that ye love one another with a pure heart fervently:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For all flesh is as grass, and all the glory of man as the flower of grass. The grass withereth, and the flower thereof falleth away:

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> But the word of the Lord endureth for ever. And this is the word which by the gospel is preached unto you.
