<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I lifted up mine eyes again, and looked, and behold a man with a measuring line in his hand.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then said I, Whither goest thou? And he said unto me, To measure Jerusalem, to see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And, behold, the angel that talked with me went forth, and another angel went out to meet him,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And said unto him, Run, speak to this young man, saying, Jerusalem shall be inhabited as towns without walls for the multitude of men and cattle therein:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For I, saith the Lord, will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north, saith the Lord: for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven, saith the Lord.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For, behold, I will shake mine hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to their servants: and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion: for, lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and shall be my people: and I will dwell in the midst of thee, and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me unto thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And the Lord shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Be silent, O all flesh, before the Lord: for he is raised up out of his holy habitation.
