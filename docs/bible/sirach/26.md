<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed is the man that hath a virtuous wife, for the number of his days shall be double.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> A virtuous woman rejoiceth her husband, and he shall fulfil the years of his life in peace.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> A good wife is a good portion, which shall be given in the portion of them that fear the Lord.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Whether a man be rich or poor, if he have a good heart toward the Lord, he shall at all times rejoice with a cheerful countenance.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> There be three things that mine heart feareth; and for the fourth I was sore afraid: the slander of a city, the gathering together of an unruly multitude, and a false accusation: all these are worse than death.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But a grief of heart and sorrow is a woman that is jealous over another woman, and a scourge of the tongue which communicateth with all.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> An evil wife is a yoke shaken to and fro: he that hath hold of her is as though he held a scorpion.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> A drunken woman and a gadder abroad causeth great anger, and she will not cover her own shame.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The whoredom of a woman may be known in her haughty looks and eyelids.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Watch over an impudent eye: and marvel not if she trespass against thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> She will open her mouth, as a thirsty traveller when he hath found a fountain, and drink of every water near her: by every hedge will she sit down, and open her quiver against every arrow.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The grace of a wife delighteth her husband, and her discretion will fatten his bones.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> A silent and loving woman is a gift of the Lord; and there is nothing so much worth as a mind well instructed.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> A shamefaced and faithful woman is a double grace, and her continent mind cannot be valued.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> As the sun when it ariseth in the high heaven; so is the beauty of a good wife in the ordering of her house.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> As the clear light is upon the holy candlestick; so is the beauty of the face in ripe age.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> As the golden pillars are upon the sockets of silver; so are the fair feet with a constant heart.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> My son, keep the flower of thine age sound; and give not thy strength to strangers.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> When thou hast gotten a fruitful possession through all the field, sow it with thine own seed, trusting in the goodness of thy stock.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> So thy race which thou leavest shall be magnified, having the confidence of their good descent.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> An harlot shall be accounted as spittle; but a married woman is a tower against death to her husband.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> A wicked woman is given as a portion to a wicked man: but a godly woman is given to him that feareth the Lord.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> A dishonest woman contemneth shame: but an honest woman will reverence her husband.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> A shameless woman shall be counted as a dog; but she that is shamefaced will fear the Lord.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> A woman that honoureth her husband shall be judged wise of all; but she that dishonoureth him in her pride shall be counted ungodly of all.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> A loud crying woman and a scold shall be sought out to drive away the enemies.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> There be two things that grieve my heart; and the third maketh me angry: a man of war that suffereth poverty; and men of understanding that are not set by; and one that returneth from righteousness to sin; the Lord prepareth such an one for the sword.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> A merchant shall hardly keep himself from doing wrong; and an huckster shall not be freed from sin.
