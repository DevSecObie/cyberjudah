<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Blessed is the man that hath not slipped with his mouth, and is not pricked with the multitude of sins.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Blessed is he whose conscience hath not condemned him, and who is not fallen from his hope in the Lord.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Riches are not comely for a niggard: and what should an envious man do with money?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He that gathereth by defrauding his own soul gathereth for others, that shall spend his goods riotously.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He that is evil to himself, to whom will he be good? he shall not take pleasure in his goods.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> There is none worse than he that envieth himself; and this is a recompence of his wickedness.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And if he doeth good, he doeth it unwillingly; and at the last he will declare his wickedness.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The envious man hath a wicked eye; he turneth away his face, and despiseth men.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> A covetous man’s eye is not satisfied with his portion; and the iniquity of the wicked drieth up his soul.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> A wicked eye envieth his bread, and he is a niggard at his table.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> My son, according to thy ability do good to thyself, and give the Lord his due offering.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Remember that death will not be long in coming, and that the covenant of the grave is not shewed unto thee.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Do good unto thy friend before thou die, and according to thy ability stretch out thy hand and give to him.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Defraud not thyself of the good day, and let not the part of a good desire overpass thee.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Shalt thou not leave thy travails unto another? and thy labours to be divided by lot?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Give, and take, and sanctify thy soul; for there is no seeking of dainties in the grave.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> All flesh waxeth old as a garment: for the covenant from the beginning is, Thou shalt die the death.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> As of the green leaves on a thick tree, some fall, and some grow; so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Every work rotteth and consumeth away, and the worker thereof shall go withal.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Blessed is the man that doth meditate good things in wisdom, and that reasoneth of holy things by his understanding.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> He that considereth her ways in his heart shall also have understanding in her secrets.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Go after her as one that traceth, and lie in wait in her ways.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> He that prieth in at her windows shall also hearken at her doors.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He that doth lodge near her house shall also fasten a pin in her walls.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> He shall pitch his tent nigh unto her, and shall lodge in a lodging where good things are.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He shall set his children under her shelter, and shall lodge under her branches.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> By her he shall be covered from heat, and in her glory shall he dwell.
