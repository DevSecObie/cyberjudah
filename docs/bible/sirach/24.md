<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Wisdom shall praise herself, and shall glory in the midst of her people.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> In the congregation of the most High shall she open her mouth, and triumph before his power.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I came out of the mouth of the most High, and covered the earth as a cloud.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I dwelt in high places, and my throne is in a cloudy pillar.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I alone compassed the circuit of heaven, and walked in the bottom of the deep.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> In the waves of the sea and in all the earth, and in every people and nation, I got a possession.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> With all these I sought rest: and in whose inheritance shall I abide?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> So the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and he that made me caused my tabernacle to rest, and said, Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thine inheritance in Israel.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He created me from the beginning before the world, and I shall never fail.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> In the holy tabernacle I served before him; and so was I established in Sion.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Likewise in the beloved city he gave me rest, and in Jerusalem was my power.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And I took root in an honourable people, even in the portion of the Lord’s inheritance.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree upon the mountains of Hermon.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I was exalted like a palm tree in En-gaddi, and as a rose plant in Jericho, as a fair olive tree in a pleasant field, and grew up as a plane tree by the water.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I gave a sweet smell like cinnamon and aspalathus, and I yielded a pleasant odour like the best myrrh, as galbanum, and onyx, and sweet storax, and as the fume of frankincense in the tabernacle.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> As the turpentine tree I stretched out my branches, and my branches are the branches of honour and grace.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> As the vine brought I forth pleasant savour, and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> I am the mother of fair love, and fear, and knowledge, and holy hope: I therefore, being eternal, am given to all my children which are named of him.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Come unto me, all ye that be desirous of me, and fill yourselves with my fruits.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For my memorial is sweeter than honey, and mine inheritance than the honeycomb.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> They that eat me shall yet be hungry, and they that drink me shall yet be thirsty.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He that obeyeth me shall never be confounded, and they that work by me shall not do amiss.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> All these things are the book of the covenant of the most high God, even the law which Moses commanded for an heritage unto the congregations of Jacob.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Faint not to be strong in the Lord; that he may confirm you, cleave unto him: for the Lord Almighty is God alone, and beside him there is no other Saviour.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> He filleth all things with his wisdom, as Phison and as Tigris in the time of the new fruits.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> He maketh the understanding to abound like Euphrates, and as Jordan in the time of the harvest.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He maketh the doctrine of knowledge appear as the light, and as Geon in the time of vintage.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The first man knew her not perfectly: no more shall the last find her out.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For her thoughts are more than the sea, and her counsels profounder than the great deep.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> I also came out as a brook from a river, and as a conduit into a garden.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> I said, I will water my best garden, and will water abundantly my garden bed: and, lo, my brook became a river, and my river became a sea.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> I will yet make doctrine to shine as the morning, and will send forth her light afar off.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and leave it to all ages for ever.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Behold that I have not laboured for myself only, but for all them that seek wisdom.
