<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Every friend saith, I am his friend also: but there is a friend, which is only a friend in name.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Is it not a grief unto death, when a companion and friend is turned to an enemy?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> O wicked imagination, whence camest thou in to cover the earth with deceit?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> There is a companion, which rejoiceth in the prosperity of a friend, but in the time of trouble will be against him.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> There is a companion, which helpeth his friend for the belly, and taketh up the buckler against the enemy.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Forget not thy friend in thy mind, and be not unmindful of him in thy riches.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Every counsellor extolleth counsel; but there is some that counselleth for himself.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Beware of a counsellor, and know before what need he hath; for he will counsel for himself; lest he cast the lot upon thee,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And say unto thee, Thy way is good: and afterward he stand on the other side, to see what shall befall thee.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Consult not with one that suspecteth thee: and hide thy counsel from such as envy thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Neither consult with a woman touching her of whom she is jealous; neither with a coward in matters of war; nor with a merchant concerning exchange; nor with a buyer of selling; nor with an envious man of thankfulness; nor with an unmerciful man touching kindness; nor with the slothful for any work; nor with an hireling for a year of finishing work; nor with an idle servant of much business: hearken not unto these in any matter of counsel.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But be continually with a godly man, whom thou knowest to keep the commandments of the Lord, whose, mind is according to thy mind, and will sorrow with thee, if thou shalt miscarry.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And let the counsel of thine own heart stand: for there is no man more faithful unto thee than it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For a man’s mind is sometime wont to tell him more than seven watchmen, that sit above in an high tower.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And above all this pray to the most High, that he will direct thy way in truth.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Let reason go before every enterprize, and counsel before every action.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The countenance is a sign of changing of the heart.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Four manner of things appear: good and evil, life and death: but the tongue ruleth over them continually.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> There is one that is wise and teacheth many, and yet is unprofitable to himself.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> There is one that sheweth wisdom in words, and is hated: he shall be destitute of all food.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For grace is not given, him from the Lord, because he is deprived of all wisdom.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Another is wise to himself; and the fruits of understanding are commendable in his mouth.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> A wise man instructeth his people; and the fruits of his understanding fail not.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> A wise man shall be filled with blessing; and all they that see him shall count him happy.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The days of the life of man may be numbered: but the days of Israel are innumerable.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> A wise man shall inherit glory among his people, and his name shall be perpetual.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> My son, prove thy soul in thy life, and see what is evil for it, and give not that unto it.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For all things are not profitable for all men, neither hath every soul pleasure in every thing.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Be not unsatiable in any dainty thing, nor too greedy upon meats:

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> For excess of meats bringeth sickness, and surfeiting will turn into choler.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> By surfeiting have many perished; but he that taketh heed prolongeth his life.
