<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Be not jealous over the wife of thy bosom, and teach her not an evil lesson against thyself.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Give not thy soul unto a woman to set her foot upon thy substance.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Meet not with an harlot, lest thou fall into her snares.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Use not much the company of a woman that is a singer, lest thou be taken with her attempts.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Gaze not on a maid, that thou fall not by those things that are precious in her.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Give not thy soul unto harlots, that thou lose not thine inheritance.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Look not round about thee in the streets of the city, neither wander thou in the solitary place thereof.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Turn away thine eye from a beautiful woman, and look not upon another’s beauty; for many have been deceived by the beauty of a woman; for herewith love is kindled as a fire.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Sit not at all with another man’s wife, nor sit down with her in thine arms, and spend not thy money with her at the wine; lest thine heart incline unto her, and so through thy desire thou fall into destruction.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Forsake not an old friend; for the new is not comparable to him: a new friend is as new wine; when it is old, thou shalt drink it with pleasure.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Envy not the glory of a sinner: for thou knowest not what shall be his end.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Delight not in the thing that the ungodly have pleasure in; but remember they shall not go unpunished unto their grave.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Keep thee far from the man that hath power to kill; so shalt thou not doubt the fear of death: and if thou come unto him, make no fault, lest he take away thy life presently: remember that thou goest in the midst of snares, and that thou walkest upon the battlements of the city.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> As near as thou canst, guess at thy neighbour, and consult with the wise.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And let just men eat and drink with thee; and let thy glorying be in the fear of the Lord.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For the hand of the artificer the work shall be commended: and the wise ruler of the people for his speech.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> A man of an ill tongue is dangerous in his city; and he that is rash in his talk shall be hated.
