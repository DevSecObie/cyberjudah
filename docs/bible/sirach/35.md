<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> He that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough: he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He that requiteth a good turn offereth fine flour; and he that giveth alms sacrificeth praise.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> To depart from wickedness is a thing pleasing to the Lord; and to forsake unrighteousness is a propitiation.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou shalt not appear empty before the Lord.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For all these things are to be done because of the commandment.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The offering of the righteous maketh the altar fat, and the sweet savour thereof is before the most High.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> The sacrifice of a just man is acceptable. and the memorial thereof shall never be forgotten.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Give the Lord his honour with a good eye, and diminish not the firstfruits of thine hands.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> In all thy gifts shew a cheerful countenance, and dedicate thy tithes with gladness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Give unto the most High according as he hath enriched thee; and as thou hast gotten, give with a cheerful eye.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the Lord recompenseth, and will give thee seven times as much.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Do not think to corrupt with gifts; for such he will not receive: and trust not to unrighteous sacrifices; for the Lord is judge, and with him is no respect of persons.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He will not accept any person against a poor man, but will hear the prayer of the oppressed.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He will not despise the supplication of the fatherless; nor the widow, when she poureth out her complaint.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Do not the tears run down the widow’s cheeks? and is not her cry against him that causeth them to fall?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He that serveth the Lord shall be accepted with favour, and his prayer shall reach unto the clouds.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The prayer of the humble pierceth the clouds: and till it come nigh, he will not be comforted; and will not depart, till the most High shall behold to judge righteously, and execute judgment.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For the Lord will not be slack, neither will the Mighty be patient toward them, till he have smitten in sunder the loins of the unmerciful, and repayed vengeance to the heathen; till he have taken away the multitude of the proud, and broken the sceptre of the unrighteous;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Till he have rendered to every man according to his deeds, and to the works of men according to their devices; till he have judged the cause of his people, and made them to rejoice in his mercy.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Mercy is seasonable in the time of affliction, as clouds of rain in the time of drought.
