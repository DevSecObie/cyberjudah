<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers that begat us.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The Lord hath wrought great glory by them through his great power from the beginning.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Such as did bear rule in their kingdoms, men renowned for their power, giving counsel by their understanding, and declaring prophecies:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Leaders of the people by their counsels, and by their knowledge of learning meet for the people, wise and eloquent are their instructions:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Such as found out musical tunes, and recited verses in writing:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Rich men furnished with ability, living peaceably in their habitations:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> All these were honoured in their generations, and were the glory of their times.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> There be of them, that have left a name behind them, that their praises might be reported.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And some there be, which have no memorial; who are perished, as though they had never been; and are become as though they had never been born; and their children after them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But these were merciful men, whose righteousness hath not been forgotten.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> With their seed shall continually remain a good inheritance, and their children are within the covenant.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Their seed standeth fast, and their children for their sakes.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Their seed shall remain for ever, and their glory shall not be blotted out.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Their bodies are buried in peace; but their name liveth for evermore.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The people will tell of their wisdom, and the congregation will shew forth their praise.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Enoch pleased the Lord, and was translated, being an example of repentance to all generations.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Noah was found perfect and righteous; in the time of wrath he was taken in exchange for the world; therefore was he left as a remnant unto the earth, when the flood came.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> An everlasting covenant was made with him, that all flesh should perish no more by the flood.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Abraham was a great father of many people: in glory was there none like unto him;

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Who kept the law of the most High, and was in covenant with him: he established the covenant in his flesh; and when he was proved, he was found faithful.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Therefore he assured him by an oath, that he would bless the nations in his seed, and that he would multiply him as the dust of the earth, and exalt his seed as the stars, and cause them to inherit from sea to sea, and from the river unto the utmost part of the land.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> With Isaac did he establish likewise for Abraham his father’s sake the blessing of all men, and the covenant, And made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; among the twelve tribes did he part them.
