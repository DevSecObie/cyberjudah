<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I will thank thee, O Lord and King, and praise thee, O God my Saviour: I do give praise unto thy name:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For thou art my defender and helper, and has preserved my body from destruction, and from the snare of the slanderous tongue, and from the lips that forge lies, and has been mine helper against mine adversaries:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And hast delivered me, according to the multitude of they mercies and greatness of thy name, from the teeth of them that were ready to devour me, and out of the hands of such as sought after my life, and from the manifold afflictions which I had;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> From the choking of fire on every side, and from the midst of the fire which I kindled not;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> From the depth of the belly of hell, from an unclean tongue, and from lying words.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> By an accusation to the king from an unrighteous tongue my soul drew near even unto death, my life was near to the hell beneath.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> They compassed me on every side, and there was no man to help me: I looked for the succour of men, but there was none.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Then thought I upon thy mercy, O Lord, and upon thy acts of old, how thou deliverest such as wait for thee, and savest them out of the hands of the enemies.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then lifted I up my supplications from the earth, and prayed for deliverance from death.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I called upon the Lord, the Father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the days of my trouble, and in the time of the proud, when there was no help.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I will praise thy name continually, and will sing praises with thanksgiving; and so my prayer was heard:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For thou savedst me from destruction, and deliveredst me from the evil time: therefore will I give thanks, and praise thee, and bless they name, O Lord.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> When I was yet young, or ever I went abroad, I desired wisdom openly in my prayer.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I prayed for her before the temple, and will seek her out even to the end.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Even from the flower till the grape was ripe hath my heart delighted in her: my foot went the right way, from my youth up sought I after her.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I bowed down mine ear a little, and received her, and gat much learning.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I profited therein, therefore will I ascribe glory unto him that giveth me wisdom.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For I purposed to do after her, and earnestly I followed that which is good; so shall I not be confounded.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> My soul hath wrestled with her, and in my doings I was exact: I stretched forth my hands to the heaven above, and bewailed my ignorances of her.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> I directed my soul unto her, and I found her in pureness: I have had my heart joined with her from the beginning, therefore shall I not be forsaken.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> My heart was troubled in seeking her: therefore have I gotten a good possession.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The Lord hath given me a tongue for my reward, and I will praise him therewith.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Draw near unto me, ye unlearned, and dwell in the house of learning.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Wherefore are ye slow, and what say ye to these things, seeing your souls are very thirsty?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> I opened my mouth, and said, Buy her for yourselves without money.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Put your neck under the yoke, and let your soul receive instruction: she is hard at hand to find.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Behold with your eyes, how that I have but little labour, and have gotten unto me much rest.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Get learning with a great sum of money, and get much gold by her.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Let your soul rejoice in his mercy, and be not ashamed of his praise.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Work your work betimes, and in his time he will give you your reward.
