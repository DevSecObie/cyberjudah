<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Instead of a friend become not an enemy; for thereby thou shalt inherit an ill name, shame, and reproach: even so shall a sinner that hath a double tongue.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Extol not thyself in the counsel of thine own heart; that thy soul be not torn in pieces as a bull straying alone.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Thou shalt eat up thy leaves, and lose thy fruit, and leave thyself as a dry tree.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> A wicked soul shall destroy him that hath it, and shall make him to be laughed to scorn of his enemies.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Sweet language will multiply friends: and a fairspeaking tongue will increase kind greetings.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Be in peace with many: nevertheless have but one counsellor of a thousand.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If thou wouldest get a friend, prove him first and be not hasty to credit him.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For some man is a friend for his own occasion, and will not abide in the day of thy trouble.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And there is a friend, who being turned to enmity, and strife will discover thy reproach.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Again, some friend is a companion at the table, and will not continue in the day of thy affliction.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But in thy prosperity he will be as thyself, and will be bold over thy servants.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If thou be brought low, he will be against thee, and will hide himself from thy face.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Separate thyself from thine enemies, and take heed of thy friends.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> A faithfull friend is a strong defence: and he that hath found such an one hath found a treasure.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Nothing doth countervail a faithful friend, and his excellency is invaluable.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> A faithful friend is the medicine of life; and they that fear the Lord shall find him.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord shall direct his friendship aright: for as he is, so shall his neighbour be also.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> My son, gather instruction from thy youth up: so shalt thou find wisdom till thine old age.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Come unto her as one that ploweth and soweth, and wait for her good fruits: for thou shalt not toil much in labouring about her, but thou shalt eat of her fruits right soon.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> She is very unpleasant to the unlearned: he that is without understanding will not remain with her.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> She will lie upon him as a mighty stone of trial; and he will cast her from him ere it be long.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For wisdom is according to her name, and she is not manifest unto many.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Give ear, my son, receive my advice, and refuse not my counsel,

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And put thy feet into her fetters, and thy neck into her chain.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Bow down thy shoulder, and bear her, and be not grieved with her bonds.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Come unto her with thy whole heart, and keep her ways with all thy power.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Search, and seek, and she shall be made known unto thee: and when thou hast got hold of her, let her not go.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For at the last thou shalt find her rest, and that shall be turned to thy joy.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Then shall her fetters be a strong defence for thee, and her chains a robe of glory.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> For there is a golden ornament upon her, and her bands are purple lace.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Thou shalt put her on as a robe of honour, and shalt put her about thee as a crown of joy.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> My son, if thou wilt, thou shalt be taught: and if thou wilt apply thy mind, thou shalt be prudent.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> If thou love to hear, thou shalt receive understanding: and if thou bow thine ear, thou shalt be wise,

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Stand in the multitude of the elders; and cleave unto him that is wise.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Be willing to hear every godly discourse; and let not the parables of understanding escape thee.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And if thou seest a man of understanding, get thee betimes unto him, and let thy foot wear the steps of his door.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Let thy mind be upon the ordinances of the Lord and meditate continually in his commandments: he shall establish thine heart, and give thee wisdom at thine owns desire.
