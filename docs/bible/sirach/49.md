<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The remembrance of Josias is like the composition of the perfume that is made by the art of the apothecary: it is sweet as honey in all mouths, and as musick at a banquet of wine.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He behaved himself uprightly in the conversion of the people, and took away the abominations of iniquity.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He directed his heart unto the Lord, and in the time of the ungodly he established the worship of God.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> All, except David and Ezekias and Josias, were defective: for they forsook the law of the most High, even the kings of Juda failed.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Therefore he gave their power unto others, and their glory to a strange nation.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> They burnt the chosen city of the sanctuary, and made the streets desolate, according to the prophecy of Jeremias.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For they entreated him evil, who nevertheless was a prophet, sanctified in his mother’s womb, that he might root out, and afflict, and destroy; and that he might build up also, and plant.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> It was Ezekiel who saw the glorious vision, which was shewed him upon the chariot of the cherubims.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For he made mention of the enemies under the figure of the rain, and directed them that went right.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And of the twelve prophets let the memorial be blessed, and let their bones flourish again out of their place: for they comforted Jacob, and delivered them by assured hope.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> How shall we magnify Zorobabel? even he was as a signet on the right hand:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So was Jesus the son of Josedec: who in their time builded the house, and set up an holy temple to the Lord, which was prepared for everlasting glory.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And among the elect was Neemias, whose renown is great, who raised up for us the walls that were fallen, and set up the gates and the bars, and raised up our ruins again.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But upon the earth was no man created like Enoch; for he was taken from the earth.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Neither was there a young man born like Joseph, a governor of his brethren, a stay of the people, whose bones were regarded of the Lord.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Sem and Seth were in great honour among men, and so was Adam above every living thing in creation.
