<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The hopes of a man void of understanding are vain and false: and dreams lift up fools.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Whoso regardeth dreams is like him that catcheth at a shadow, and followeth after the wind.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, even as the likeness of a face to a face.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Of an unclean thing what can be cleansed? and from that thing which is false what truth can come?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Divinations, and soothsayings, and dreams, are vain: and the heart fancieth, as a woman’s heart in travail.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> If they be not sent from the most High in thy visitation, set not thy heart upon them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For dreams have deceived many, and they have failed that put their trust in them.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> A man that hath travelled knoweth many things; and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He that hath no experience knoweth little: but he that hath travelled is full of prudence.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> When I travelled, I saw many things; and I understand more than I can express.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I was ofttimes in danger of death: yet I was delivered because of these things.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The spirit of those that fear the Lord shall live; for their hope is in him that saveth them.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord shall not fear nor be afraid; for he is his hope.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Blessed is the soul of him that feareth the Lord: to whom doth he look? and who is his strength?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, a preservation from stumbling, and an help from falling.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He raiseth up the soul, and lighteneth the eyes: he giveth health, life, and blessing.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He that sacrificeth of a thing wrongfully gotten, his offering is ridiculous; and the gifts of unjust men are not accepted.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The most High is not pleased with the offerings of the wicked; neither is he pacified for sin by the multitude of sacrifices.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Whoso bringeth an offering of the goods of the poor doeth as one that killeth the son before his father’s eyes.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The bread of the needy is their life: he that defraudeth him thereof is a man of blood.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He that taketh away his neighbour’s living slayeth him; and he that defraudeth the labourer of his hire is a bloodshedder.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> When one buildeth, and another pulleth down, what profit have they then but labour?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> When one prayeth, and another curseth, whose voice will the Lord hear?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> He that washeth himself after the touching of a dead body, if he touch it again, what availeth his washing?

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> So is it with a man that fasteth for his sins, and goeth again, and doeth the same: who will hear his prayer? or what doth his humbling profit him?
