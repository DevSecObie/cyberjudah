<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> He that revengeth shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Forgive thy neighbour the hurt that he hath done unto thee, so shall thy sins also be forgiven when thou prayest.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> One man beareth hatred against another, and doth he seek pardon from the Lord?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He sheweth no mercy to a man, which is like himself: and doth he ask forgiveness of his own sins?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If he that is but flesh nourish hatred, who will intreat for pardon of his sins?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Remember thy end, and let enmity cease; remember corruption and death, and abide in the commandments.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Remember the commandments, and bear no malice to thy neighbour: remember the covenant of the Highest, and wink at ignorance.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Abstain from strife, and thou shalt diminish thy sins: for a furious man will kindle strife,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> A sinful man disquieteth friends, and maketh debate among them that be at peace.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As the matter of the fire is, so it burneth: and as a man’s strength is, so is his wrath; and according to his riches his anger riseth; and the stronger they are which contend, the more they will be inflamed.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> An hasty contention kindleth a fire: and an hasty fighting sheddeth blood.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If thou blow the spark, it shall burn: if thou spit upon it, it shall be quenched: and both these come out of thy mouth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> A backbiting tongue hath disquieted many, and driven them from nation to nation: strong cities hath it pulled down, and overthrown the houses of great men.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> A backbiting tongue hath cast out virtuous women, and deprived them of their labours.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Whoso hearkeneth unto it shall never find rest, and never dwell quietly.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The stroke of the whip maketh marks in the flesh: but the stroke of the tongue breaketh the bones.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Many have fallen by the edge of the sword: but not so many as have fallen by the tongue.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Well is he that is defended through the venom thereof; who hath not drawn the yoke thereof, nor hath been bound in her bands.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For the yoke thereof is a yoke of iron, and the bands thereof are bands of brass.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The death thereof is an evil death, the grave were better than it.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> It shall not have rule over them that fear God, neither shall they be burned with the flame thereof.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Such as forsake the Lord shall fall into it; and it shall burn in them, and not be quenched; it shall be sent upon them as a lion, and devour them as a leopard.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Look that thou hedge thy possession about with thorns, and bind up thy silver and gold,

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And weigh thy words in a balance, and make a door and bar for thy mouth.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Beware thou slide not by it, lest thou fall before him that lieth in wait.
