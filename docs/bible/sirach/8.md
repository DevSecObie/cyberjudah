<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Strive not with a mighty man, lest thou fall into his hands.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Be not at variance with a rich man, lest he overweigh thee: for gold hath destroyed many, and perverted the hearts of kings.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Strive not with a man that is full of tongue, and heap not wood upon his fire.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Jest not with a rude man, lest thy ancestors be disgraced.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Reproach not a man that turneth from sin, but remember that we are all worthy of punishment.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Dishonour not a man in his old age: for even some of us wax old.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Rejoice not over thy greatest enemy being dead, but remember that we die all.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Despise not the discourse of the wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs: for of them thou shalt learn instruction, and how to serve great men with ease.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Miss not the discourse of the elders: for they also learned of their fathers, and of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give answer as need requireth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Kindle not the coals of a sinner, lest thou be burnt with the flame of his fire.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Rise not up in anger at the presence of an injurious person, lest he lie in wait to entrap thee in thy words

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Lend not unto him that is mightier than thyself; for if thou lendest him, count it but lost.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Be not surety above thy power: for if thou be surety, take care to pay it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Go not to law with a judge; for they will judge for him according to his honour.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Travel not by the way with a bold fellow, lest he become grievous unto thee: for he will do according to his own will, and thou shalt perish with him through his folly.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Strive not with an angry man, and go not with him into a solitary place: for blood is as nothing in his sight, and where there is no help, he will overthrow thee.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Consult not with a fool; for he cannot keep counsel.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Do no secret thing before a stranger; for thou knowest not what he will bring forth.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Open not thine heart to every man, lest he requite thee with a shrewd turn.
