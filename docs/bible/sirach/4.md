<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My son, defraud not the poor of his living, and make not the needy eyes to wait long.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Make not an hungry soul sorrowful; neither provoke a man in his distress.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Add not more trouble to an heart that is vexed; and defer not to give to him that is in need.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Reject not the supplication of the afflicted; neither turn away thy face from a poor man.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Turn not away thine eye from the needy, and give him none occasion to curse thee:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For if he curse thee in the bitterness of his soul, his prayer shall be heard of him that made him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Get thyself the love of the congregation, and bow thy head to a great man.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let it not grieve thee to bow down thine ear to the poor, and give him a friendly answer with meekness.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Deliver him that suffereth wrong from the hand of the oppressor; and be not fainthearted when thou sittest in judgment.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Be as a father unto the fatherless, and instead of an husband unto their mother: so shalt thou be as the son of the most High, and he shall love thee more than thy mother doth.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wisdom exalteth her children, and layeth hold of them that seek her.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He that loveth her loveth life; and they that seek to her early shall be filled with joy.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He that holdeth her fast shall inherit glory; and wheresoever she entereth, the Lord will bless.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> They that serve her shall minister to the Holy One: and them that love her the Lord doth love.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Whoso giveth ear unto her shall judge the nations: and he that attendeth unto her shall dwell securely.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> If a man commit himself unto her, he shall inherit her; and his generation shall hold her in possession.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For at the first she will walk with him by crooked ways, and bring fear and dread upon him, and torment him with her discipline, until she may trust his soul, and try him by her laws.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Then will she return the straight way unto him, and comfort him, and shew him her secrets.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> But if he go wrong, she will forsake him, and give him over to his own ruin.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Observe the opportunity, and beware of evil; and be not ashamed when it concerneth thy soul.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For there is a shame that bringeth sin; and there is a shame which is glory and grace.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And refrain not to speak, when there is occasion to do good, and hide not thy wisdom in her beauty.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For by speech wisdom shall be known: and learning by the word of the tongue.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> In no wise speak against the truth; but be abashed of the error of thine ignorance.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Be not ashamed to confess thy sins; and force not the course of the river.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Make not thyself an underling to a foolish man; neither accept the person of the mighty.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Strive for the truth unto death, and the Lord shall fight for thee.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Be not hasty in thy tongue, and in thy deeds slack and remiss.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Be not as a lion in thy house, nor frantick among thy servants.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Let not thine hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldest repay.
