<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully, and be patient when thou art changed to a low estate.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and go not aside, lest ye fall.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, believe him; and your reward shall not fail.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, hope for good, and for everlasting joy and mercy.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the Lord is full of compassion and mercy, longsuffering, and very pitiful, and forgiveth sins, and saveth in time of affliction.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Woe be to fearful hearts, and faint hands, and the sinner that goeth two ways!

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Woe unto him that is fainthearted! for he believeth not; therefore shall he not be defended.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Woe unto you that have lost patience! and what will ye do when the Lord shall visit you?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> They that fear the Lord will not disobey his Word; and they that love him will keep his ways.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> They that fear the Lord will seek that which is well, pleasing unto him; and they that love him shall be filled with the law.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> They that fear the Lord will prepare their hearts, and humble their souls in his sight,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Saying, We will fall into the hands of the Lord, and not into the hands of men: for as his majesty is, so is his mercy.
