<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Watching for riches consumeth the flesh, and the care thereof driveth away sleep.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Watching care will not let a man slumber, as a sore disease breaketh sleep,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The rich hath great labour in gathering riches together; and when he resteth, he is filled with his delicates.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The poor laboureth in his poor estate; and when he leaveth off, he is still needy.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He that loveth gold shall not be justified, and he that followeth corruption shall have enough thereof.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Gold hath been the ruin of many, and their destruction was present.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> It is a stumblingblock unto them that sacrifice unto it, and every fool shall be taken therewith.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Blessed is the rich that is found without blemish, and hath not gone after gold.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Who is he? and we will call him blessed: for wonderful things hath he done among his people.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Who hath been tried thereby, and found perfect? then let him glory. Who might offend, and hath not offended? or done evil, and hath not done it?

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> His goods shall be established, and the congregation shall declare his alms.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If thou sit at a bountiful table, be not greedy upon it, and say not, There is much meat on it.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Remember that a wicked eye is an evil thing: and what is created more wicked than an eye? therefore it weepeth upon every occasion.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Stretch not thine hand whithersoever it looketh, and thrust it not with him into the dish.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Judge not thy neighbour by thyself: and be discreet in every point.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Eat as it becometh a man, those things which are set before thee; and devour note, lest thou be hated.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Leave off first for manners’ sake; and be not unsatiable, lest thou offend.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> When thou sittest among many, reach not thine hand out first of all.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> A very little is sufficient for a man well nurtured, and he fetcheth not his wind short upon his bed.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Sound sleep cometh of moderate eating: he riseth early, and his wits are with him: but the pain of watching, and choler, and pangs of the belly, are with an unsatiable man.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And if thou hast been forced to eat, arise, go forth, vomit, and thou shalt have rest.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> My son, hear me, and despise me not, and at the last thou shalt find as I told thee: in all thy works be quick, so shall there no sickness come unto thee.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Whoso is liberal of his meat, men shall speak well of him; and the report of his good housekeeping will be believed.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But against him that is a niggard of his meat the whole city shall murmur; and the testimonies of his niggardness shall not be doubted of.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Shew not thy valiantness in wine; for wine hath destroyed many.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The furnace proveth the edge by dipping: so doth wine the hearts of the proud by drunkenness.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Wine is as good as life to a man, if it be drunk moderately: what life is then to a man that is without wine? for it was made to make men glad.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind, with brawling and quarrelling.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Drunkenness increaseth the rage of a fool till he offend: it diminisheth strength, and maketh wounds.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Rebuke not thy neighbour at the wine, and despise him not in his mirth: give him no despiteful words, and press not upon him with urging him to drink.
