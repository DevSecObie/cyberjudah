<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear me your father, O children, and do thereafter, that ye may be safe.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For the Lord hath given the father honour over the children, and hath confirmed the authority of the mother over the sons.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Whoso honoureth his father maketh an atonement for his sins:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And he that honoureth his mother is as one that layeth up treasure.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Whoso honoureth his father shall have joy of his own children; and when he maketh his prayer, he shall be heard.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He that honoureth his father shall have a long life; and he that is obedient unto the Lord shall be a comfort to his mother.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He that feareth the Lord will honour his father, and will do service unto his parents, as to his masters.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Honour thy father and mother both in word and deed, that a blessing may come upon thee from them.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For the blessing of the father establisheth the houses of children; but the curse of the mother rooteth out foundations.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Glory not in the dishonour of thy father; for thy father’s dishonour is no glory unto thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father; and a mother in dishonour is a reproach to the children.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> My son, help thy father in his age, and grieve him not as long as he liveth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And if his understanding fail, have patience with him; and despise him not when thou art in thy full strength.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For the relieving of thy father shall not be forgotten: and instead of sins it shall be added to build thee up.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> In the day of thine affliction it shall be remembered; thy sins also shall melt away, as the ice in the fair warm weather.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He that forsaketh his father is as a blasphemer; and he that angereth his mother is cursed: of God.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> My son, go on with thy business in meekness; so shalt thou be beloved of him that is approved.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The greater thou art, the more humble thyself, and thou shalt find favour before the Lord.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Many are in high place, and of renown: but mysteries are revealed unto the meek.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For the power of the Lord is great, and he is honoured of the lowly.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Seek not out things that are too hard for thee, neither search the things that are above thy strength.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But what is commanded thee, think thereupon with reverence, for it is not needful for thee to see with thine eyes the things that are in secret.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Be not curious in unnecessary matters: for more things are shewed unto thee than men understand.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For many are deceived by their own vain opinion; and an evil suspicion hath overthrown their judgment.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Without eyes thou shalt want light: profess not the knowledge therefore that thou hast not.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> A stubborn heart shall fare evil at the last; and he that loveth danger shall perish therein.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> An obstinate heart shall be laden with sorrows; and the wicked man shall heap sin upon sin.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> In the punishment of the proud there is no remedy; for the plant of wickedness hath taken root in him.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> The heart of the prudent will understand a parable; and an attentive ear is the desire of a wise man.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Water will quench a flaming fire; and alms maketh an atonement for sins.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And he that requiteth good turns is mindful of that which may come hereafter; and when he falleth, he shall find a stay.
