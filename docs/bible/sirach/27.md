<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Many have sinned for a small matter; and he that seeketh for abundance will turn his eyes away.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> As a nail sticketh fast between the joinings of the stones; so doth sin stick close between buying and selling.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Unless a man hold himself diligently in the fear of the Lord, his house shall soon be overthrown.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> As when one sifteth with a sieve, the refuse remaineth; so the filth of man in his talk.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The furnace proveth the potter’s vessels; so the trial of man is in his reasoning.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The fruit declareth if the tree have been dressed; so is the utterance of a conceit in the heart of man.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Praise no man before thou hearest him speak; for this is the trial of men.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> If thou followest righteousness, thou shalt obtain her, and put her on, as a glorious long robe.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The birds will resort unto their like; so will truth return unto them that practise in her.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As the lion lieth in wait for the prey; so sin for them that work iniquity.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The discourse of a godly man is always with wisdom; but a fool changeth as the moon.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> If thou be among the indiscreet, observe the time; but be continually among men of understanding.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The discourse of fools is irksome, and their sport is the wantonness of sin.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The talk of him that sweareth much maketh the hair stand upright; and their brawls make one stop his ears.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The strife of the proud is bloodshedding, and their revilings are grievous to the ear.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Whoso discovereth secrets loseth his credit; and shall never find friend to his mind.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Love thy friend, and be faithful unto him: but if thou betrayest his secrets, follow no more after him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For as a man hath destroyed his enemy; so hast thou lost the love of thy neighbor.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> As one that letteth a bird go out of his hand, so hast thou let thy neighbour go, and shalt not get him again

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Follow after him no more, for he is too far off; he is as a roe escaped out of the snare.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> As for a wound, it may be bound up; and after reviling there may be reconcilement: but he that betrayeth secrets is without hope.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He that winketh with the eyes worketh evil: and he that knoweth him will depart from him.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> When thou art present, he will speak sweetly, and will admire thy words: but at the last he will writhe his mouth, and slander thy sayings.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> I have hated many things, but nothing like him; for the Lord will hate him.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Whoso casteth a stone on high casteth it on his own head; and a deceitful stroke shall make wounds.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Whoso diggeth a pit shall fall therein: and he that setteth a trap shall be taken therein.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He that worketh mischief, it shall fall upon him, and he shall not know whence it cometh.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Mockery and reproach are from the proud; but vengeance, as a lion, shall lie in wait for them.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> They that rejoice at the fall of the righteous shall be taken in the snare; and anguish shall consume them before they die.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Malice and wrath, even these are abominations; and the sinful man shall have them both.
