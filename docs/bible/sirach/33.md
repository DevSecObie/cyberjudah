<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> There shall no evil happen unto him that feareth the Lord; but in temptation even again he will deliver him.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> A wise man hateth not the law; but he that is an hypocrite therein is as a ship in a storm.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> A man of understanding trusteth in the law; and the law is faithful unto him, as an oracle.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Prepare what to say, and so thou shalt be heard: and bind up instruction, and then make answer.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The heart of the foolish is like a cartwheel; and his thoughts are like a rolling axletree.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> A stallion horse is as a mocking friend, he neigheth under every one that sitteth upon him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Why doth one day excel another, when as all the light of every day in the year is of the sun?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> By the knowledge of the Lord they were distinguished: and he altered seasons and feasts.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Some of them hath he made high days, and hallowed them, and some of them hath he made ordinary days.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And all men are from the ground, and Adam was created of earth:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> In much knowledge the Lord hath divided them, and made their ways diverse.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Some of them hath he blessed and exalted and some of them he sanctified, and set near himself: but some of them hath he cursed and brought low, and turned out of their places.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> As the clay is in the potter’s hand, to fashion it at his pleasure: so man is in the hand of him that made him, to render to them as liketh him best.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Good is set against evil, and life against death: so is the godly against the sinner, and the sinner against the godly.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> So look upon all the works of the most High; and there are two and two, one against another.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I awaked up last of all, as one that gathereth after the grapegatherers: by the blessing of the Lord I profited, and trod my winepress like a gatherer of grapes.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Hear me, O ye great men of the people, and hearken with your ears, ye rulers of the congregation.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Give not thy son and wife, thy brother and friend, power over thee while thou livest, and give not thy goods to another: lest it repent thee, and thou intreat for the same again.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> As long as thou livest and hast breath in thee, give not thyself over to any.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For better it is that thy children should seek to thee, than that thou shouldest stand to their courtesy.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> In all thy works keep to thyself the preeminence; leave not a stain in thine honour.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> At the time when thou shalt end thy days, and finish thy life, distribute thine inheritance.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Fodder, a wand, and burdens, are for the ass; and bread, correction, and work, for a servant. .

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> If thou set thy servant to labour, thou shalt find rest: but if thou let him go idle, he shall seek liberty.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> A yoke and a collar do bow the neck: so are tortures and torments for an evil servant.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Send him to labour, that he be not idle; for idleness teacheth much evil.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Set him to work, as is fit for him: if he be not obedient, put on more heavy fetters.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But be not excessive toward any; and without discretion do nothing.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> If thou have a servant, let him be unto thee as thyself, because thou hast bought him with a price.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> If thou have a servant, entreat him as a brother: for thou hast need of him, as of thine own soul: if thou entreat him evil, and he run from thee, which way wilt thou go to seek him?
