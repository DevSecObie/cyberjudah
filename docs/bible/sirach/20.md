<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> There is a reproof that is not comely: again, some man holdeth his tongue, and he is wise.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> It is much better to reprove, than to be angry secretly: and he that confesseth his fault shall be preserved from hurt.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> How good is it, when thou art reproved, to shew repentance! for so shalt thou escape wilful sin.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> As is the lust of an eunuch to deflower a virgin; so is he that executeth judgment with violence.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> There is one that keepeth silence, and is found wise: and another by much babbling becometh hateful.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Some man holdeth his tongue, because he hath not to answer: and some keepeth silence, knowing his time.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> A wise man will hold his tongue till he see opportunity: but a babbler and a fool will regard no time.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He that useth many words shall be abhorred; and he that taketh to himself authority therein shall be hated.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> There is a sinner that hath good success in evil things; and there is a gain that turneth to loss.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> There is a gift that shall not profit thee; and there is a gift whose recompence is double.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> There is an abasement because of glory; and there is that lifteth up his head from a low estate.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> There is that buyeth much for a little, and repayeth it sevenfold.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> A wise man by his words maketh him beloved: but the graces of fools shall be poured out.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The gift of a fool shall do thee no good when thou hast it; neither yet of the envious for his necessity: for he looketh to receive many things for one.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He giveth little, and upbraideth much; he openeth his mouth like a crier; to day he lendeth, and to morrow will he ask it again: such an one is to be hated of God and man.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The fool saith, I have no friends, I have no thank for all my good deeds, and they that eat my bread speak evil of me.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> How oft, and of how many shall he be laughed to scorn! for he knoweth not aright what it is to have; and it is all one unto him as if he had it not.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> To slip upon a pavement is better than to slip with the tongue: so the fall of the wicked shall come speedily.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> An unseasonable tale will always be in the mouth of the unwise.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> A wise sentence shall be rejected when it cometh out of a fool’s mouth; for he will not speak it in due season.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> There is that is hindered from sinning through want: and when he taketh rest, he shall not be troubled.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> There is that destroyeth his own soul through bashfulness, and by accepting of persons overthroweth himself.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> There is that for bashfulness promiseth to his friend, and maketh him his enemy for nothing.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> A lie is a foul blot in a man, yet it is continually in the mouth of the untaught.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> A thief is better than a man that is accustomed to lie: but they both shall have destruction to heritage.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The disposition of a liar is dishonourable, and his shame is ever with him.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> A wise man shall promote himself to honour with his words: and he that hath understanding will please great men.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> He that tilleth his land shall increase his heap: and he that pleaseth great men shall get pardon for iniquity.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Presents and gifts blind the eyes of the wise, and stop up his mouth that he cannot reprove.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is hoarded up, what profit is in them both?

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Better is he that hideth his folly than a man that hideth his wisdom.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Necessary patience in seeking the Lord is better than he that leadeth his life without a guide.
