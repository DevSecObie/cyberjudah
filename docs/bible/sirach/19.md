<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> A labouring man that A is given to drunkenness shall not be rich: and he that contemneth small things shall fall by little and little.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Wine and women will make men of understanding to fall away: and he that cleaveth to harlots will become impudent.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Moths and worms shall have him to heritage, and a bold man shall be taken away.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He that is hasty to give credit is lightminded; and he that sinneth shall offend against his own soul.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Whoso taketh pleasure in wickedness shall be condemned: but he that resisteth pleasures crowneth his life.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He that can rule his tongue shall live without strife; and he that hateth babbling shall have less evil.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Rehearse not unto another that which is told unto thee, and thou shalt fare never the worse.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Whether it be to friend or foe, talk not of other men’s lives; and if thou canst without offence, reveal them not.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For he heard and observed thee, and when time cometh he will hate thee.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> If thou hast heard a word, let it die with thee; and be bold, it will not burst thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> A fool travaileth with a word, as a woman in labour of a child.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> As an arrow that sticketh in a man’s thigh, so is a word within a fool’s belly.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Admonish a friend, it may be he hath not done it: and if he have done it, that he do it no more.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Admonish thy friend, it may be he hath not said it: and if he have, that he speak it not again.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Admonish a friend: for many times it is a slander, and believe not every tale.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> There is one that slippeth in his speech, but not from his heart; and who is he that hath not offended with his tongue?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Admonish thy neighbour before thou threaten him; and not being angry, give place to the law of the most High.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The fear of the Lord is the first step to be accepted of him, and wisdom obtaineth his love.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The knowledge of the commandments of the Lord is the doctrine of life: and they that do things that please him shall receive the fruit of the tree of immortality.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> The fear of the Lord is all wisdom; and in all wisdom is the performance of the law, and the knowledge of his omnipotency.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> If a servant say to his master, I will not do as it pleaseth thee; though afterward he do it, he angereth him that nourisheth him.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The knowledge of wickedness is not wisdom, neither at any time the counsel of sinners prudence.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> There is a wickedness, and the same an abomination; and there is a fool wanting in wisdom.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He that hath small understanding, and feareth God, is better than one that hath much wisdom, and transgresseth the law of the most High.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> There is an exquisite subtilty, and the same is unjust; and there is one that turneth aside to make judgment appear; and there is a wise man that justifieth in judgment.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> There is a wicked man that hangeth down his head sadly; but inwardly he is full of deceit,

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Casting down his countenance, and making as if he heard not: where he is not known, he will do thee a mischief before thou be aware.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And if for want of power he be hindered from sinning, yet when he findeth opportunity he will do evil.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> A man may be known by his look, and one that hath understanding by his countenance, when thou meetest him.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> A man’s attire, and excessive laughter, and gait, shew what he is.
