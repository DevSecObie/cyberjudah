<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> When thou wilt do good know to whom thou doest it; so shalt thou be thanked for thy benefits.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Do good to the godly man, and thou shalt find a recompence; and if not from him, yet from the most High.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> There can no good come to him that is always occupied in evil, nor to him that giveth no alms.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Give to the godly man, and help not a sinner.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Do well unto him that is lowly, but give not to the ungodly: hold back thy bread, and give it not unto him, lest he overmaster thee thereby: for else thou shalt receive twice as much evil for all the good thou shalt have done unto him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For the most High hateth sinners, and will repay vengeance unto the ungodly, and keepeth them against the mighty day of their punishment.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Give unto the good, and help not the sinner.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> A friend cannot be known in prosperity: and an enemy cannot be hidden in adversity.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> In the prosperity of a man enemies will be grieved: but in his adversity even a friend will depart.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Never trust thine enemy: for like as iron rusteth, so is his wickedness.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Though he humble himself, and go crouching, yet take good heed and beware of him, and thou shalt be unto him as if thou hadst wiped a lookingglass, and thou shalt know that his rust hath not been altogether wiped away.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Set him not by thee, lest, when he hath overthrown thee, he stand up in thy place; neither let him sit at thy right hand, lest he seek to take thy seat, and thou at the last remember my words, and be pricked therewith.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Who will pity a charmer that is bitten with a serpent, or any such as come nigh wild beasts?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> So one that goeth to a sinner, and is defiled with him in his sins, who will pity?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For a while he will abide with thee, but if thou begin to fall, he will not tarry.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> An enemy speaketh sweetly with his lips, but in his heart he imagineth how to throw thee into a pit: he will weep with his eyes, but if he find opportunity, he will not be satisfied with blood.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> If adversity come upon thee, thou shalt find him there first; and though he pretend to help thee, yet shall he undermine thee.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He will shake his head, and clap his hands, and whisper much, and change his countenance.
