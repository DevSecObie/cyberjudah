<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Have mercy upon us, O Lord God of all, and behold us:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And send thy fear upon all the nations that seek not after thee.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Lift up thy hand against the strange nations, and let them see thy power.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> As thou wast sanctified in us before them: so be thou magnified among them before us.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And let them know thee, as we have known thee, that there is no God but only thou, O God.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Shew new signs, and make other strange wonders: glorify thy hand and thy right arm, that they may set forth thy wondrous works.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath: take away the adversary, and destroy the enemy.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Sake the time short, remember the covenant, and let them declare thy wonderful works.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Let him that escapeth be consumed by the rage of the fire; and let them perish that oppress the people.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Smite in sunder the heads of the rulers of the heathen, that say, There is none other but we.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Gather all the tribes of Jacob together, and inherit thou them, as from the beginning.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> O Lord, have mercy upon the people that is called by thy name, and upon Israel, whom thou hast named thy firstborn.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> O be merciful unto Jerusalem, thy holy city, the place of thy rest.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Fill Sion with thine unspeakable oracles, and thy people with thy glory:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Give testimony unto those that thou hast possessed from the beginning, and raise up prophets that have been in thy name.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Reward them that wait for thee, and let thy prophets be found faithful.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> O Lord, hear the prayer of thy servants, according to the blessing of Aaron over thy people, that all they which dwell upon the earth may know that thou art the Lord, the eternal God.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The belly devoureth all meats, yet is one meat better than another.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> As the palate tasteth divers kinds of venison: so doth an heart of understanding false speeches.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> A froward heart causeth heaviness: but a man of experience will recompense him.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> A woman will receive every man, yet is one daughter better than another.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The beauty of a woman cheereth the countenance, and a man loveth nothing better.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> If there be kindness, meekness, and comfort, in her tongue, then is not her husband like other men.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He that getteth a wife beginneth a possession, a help like unto himself, and a pillar of rest.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Where no hedge is, there the possession is spoiled: and he that hath no wife will wander up and down mourning.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Who will trust a thief well appointed, that skippeth from city to city? so who will believe a man that hath no house, and lodgeth wheresoever the night taketh him?
