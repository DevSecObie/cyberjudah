<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My son, hast thou sinned? do so no more, but ask pardon for thy former sins.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Flee from sin as from the face of a serpent: for if thou comest too near it, it will bite thee: the teeth thereof are as the teeth of a lion, slaying the souls of men.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> All iniquity is as a two edged sword, the wounds whereof cannot be healed.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> To terrify and do wrong will waste riches: thus the house of proud men shall be made desolate.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A prayer out of a poor man’s mouth reacheth to the ears of God, and his judgment cometh speedily.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He that hateth to be reproved is in the way of sinners: but he that feareth the Lord will repent from his heart.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> An eloquent man is known far and near; but a man of understanding knoweth when he slippeth.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He that buildeth his house with other men’s money is like one that gathereth himself stones for the tomb of his burial.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The congregation of the wicked is like tow wrapped together: and the end of them is a flame of fire to destroy them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The way of sinners is made plain with stones, but at the end thereof is the pit of hell.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He that keepeth the law of the Lord getteth the understanding thereof: and the perfection of the fear of the Lord is wisdom.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He that is not wise will not be taught: but there is a wisdom which multiplieth bitterness.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The knowledge of a wise man shall abound like a flood: and his counsel is like a pure fountain of life.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The inner parts of a fool are like a broken vessel, and he will hold no knowledge as long as he liveth.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> If a skilful man hear a wise word, he will commend it, and add unto it: but as soon as one of no understanding heareth it, it displeaseth him, and he casteth it behind his back.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The talking of a fool is like a burden in the way: but grace shall be found in the lips of the wise.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> They enquire at the mouth of the wise man in the congregation, and they shall ponder his words in their heart.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> As is a house that is destroyed, so is wisdom to a fool: and the knowledge of the unwise is as talk without sense.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Doctrine unto fools is as fetters on the feet, and like manacles on the right hand.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> A fool lifteth up his voice with laughter; but a wise man doth scarce smile a little.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Learning is unto a wise man as an ornament of gold, and like a bracelet upon his right arm.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> A foolish man’s foot is soon in his neighbour’s house: but a man of experience is ashamed of him.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> A fool will peep in at the door into the house: but he that is well nurtured will stand without.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> It is the rudeness of a man to hearken at the door: but a wise man will be grieved with the disgrace.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The lips of talkers will be telling such things as pertain not unto them: but the words of such as have understanding are weighed in the balance.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The heart of fools is in their mouth: but the mouth of the wise is in their heart.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> When the ungodly curseth Satan, he curseth his own soul.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> A whisperer defileth his own soul, and is hated wheresoever he dwelleth.
