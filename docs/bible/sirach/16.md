<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For by one that hath understanding shall the city be replenished: but the kindred of the wicked shall speedily become desolate.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Many such things have I seen with mine eyes, and mine ear hath heard greater things than these.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> In the congregation of the ungodly shall a fire be kindled; and in a rebellious nation wrath is set on fire.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He was not pacified toward the old giants, who fell away in the strength of their foolishness.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for their pride.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He pitied not the people of perdition, who were taken away in their sins:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Nor the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And if there be one stiffnecked among the people, it is marvel if he escape unpunished: for mercy and wrath are with him; he is mighty to forgive, and to pour out displeasure.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> As his mercy is great, so is his correction also: he judgeth a man according to his works

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The sinner shall not escape with his spoils: and the patience of the godly shall not be frustrate.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Make way for every work of mercy: for every man shall find according to his works.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The Lord hardened Pharaoh, that he should not know him, that his powerful works might be known to the world.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> His mercy is manifest to every creature; and he hath separated his light from the darkness with an adamant.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Say not thou, I will hide myself from the Lord: shall any remember me from above? I shall not be remembered among so many people: for what is my soul among such an infinite number of creatures?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Behold, the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, and all that therein is, shall be moved when he shall visit.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The mountains also and foundations of the earth be shaken with trembling, when the Lord looketh upon them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> No heart can think upon these things worthily: and who is able to conceive his ways?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> It is a tempest which no man can see: for the most part of his works are hid.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Who can declare the works of his justice? or who can endure them? for his covenant is afar off, and the trial of all things is in the end.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> He that wanteth understanding will think upon vain things: and a foolish man erring imagineth follies.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> by son, hearken unto me, and learn knowledge, and mark my words with thy heart.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> I will shew forth doctrine in weight, and declare his knowledge exactly.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> The works of the Lord are done in judgment from the beginning: and from the time he made them he disposed the parts thereof.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> He garnished his works for ever, and in his hand are the chief of them unto all generations: they neither labour, nor are weary, nor cease from their works.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> None of them hindereth another, and they shall never disobey his word.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> After this the Lord looked upon the earth, and filled it with his blessings.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> With all manner of living things hath he covered the face thereof; and they shall return into it again.
