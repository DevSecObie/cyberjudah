<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> If thou be made the master of a feast, lift not thyself up, but be among them as one of the rest; take diligent care for them, and so sit down.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And when thou hast done all thy office, take thy place, that thou mayest be merry with them, and receive a crown for thy well ordering of the feast.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Speak, thou that art the elder, for it becometh thee, but with sound judgment; and hinder not musick.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Pour not out words where there is a musician, and shew not forth wisdom out of time.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> A concert of musick in a banquet of wine is as a signet of carbuncle set in gold.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> As a signet of an emerald set in a work of gold, so is the melody of musick with pleasant wine.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Speak, young man, if there be need of thee: and yet scarcely when thou art twice asked.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let thy speech be short, comprehending much in few words; be as one that knoweth and yet holdeth his tongue.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Before the thunder goeth lightning; and before a shamefaced man shall go favour.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Rise up betimes, and be not the last; but get thee home without delay.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> There take thy pastime, and do what thou wilt: but sin not by proud speech.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And for these things bless him that made thee, and hath replenished thee with his good things.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favour.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He that seeketh the law shall be filled therewith: but the hypocrite will be offended thereat.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> A man of counsel will be considerate; but a strange and proud man is not daunted with fear, even when of himself he hath done without counsel.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Do nothing without advice; and when thou hast once done, repent not.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Go not in a way wherein thou mayest fall, and stumble not among the stones.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Be not confident in a plain way.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And beware of thine own children.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> In every good work trust thy own soul; for this is the keeping of the commandments.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse.
