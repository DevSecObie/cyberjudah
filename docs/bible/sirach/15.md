<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> He that feareth the Lord will do good, and he that hath the knowledge of the law shall obtain her.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And as a mother shall she meet him, and receive him as a wife married of a virgin.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> With the bread of understanding shall she feed him, and give him the water of wisdom to drink.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> He shall be stayed upon her, and shall not be moved; and shall rely upon her, and shall not be confounded.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> She shall exalt him above his neighbours, and in the midst of the congregation shall she open his mouth.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He shall find joy and a crown of gladness, and she shall cause him to inherit an everlasting name.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But foolish men shall not attain unto her, and sinners shall not see her.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For she is far from pride, and men that are liars cannot remember her.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Praise is not seemly in the mouth of a sinner, for it was not sent him of the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For praise shall be uttered in wisdom, and the Lord will prosper it.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Say not thou, It is through the Lord that I fell away: for thou oughtest not to do the things that he hateth.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Say not thou, He hath caused me to err: for he hath no need of the sinful man.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> The Lord hateth all abomination; and they that fear God love it not.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel;

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> If thou wilt, to keep the commandments, and to perform acceptable faithfulness.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He hath set fire and water before thee: stretch forth thy hand unto whether thou wilt.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Before man is life and death; and whether him liketh shall be given him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For the wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholdeth all things:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And his eyes are upon them that fear him, and he knoweth every work of man.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin.
