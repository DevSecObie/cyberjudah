<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Do no evil, so shall no harm come unto thee.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Depart from the unjust, and iniquity shall turn away from thee.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> My son, sow not upon the furrows of unrighteousness, and thou shalt not reap them sevenfold.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Seek not of the Lord preeminence, neither of the king the seat of honour.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> justify not thyself before the Lord; and boast not of thy wisdom before the king.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Seek not to be judge, being not able to take away iniquity; lest at any time thou fear the person of the mighty, an stumblingblock in the way of thy uprightness.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Offend not against the multitude of a city, and then thou shalt not cast thyself down among the people.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Bind not one sin upon another; for in one thou shalt not be unpunished.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Say not, God will look upon the multitude of my oblations, and when I offer to the most high God, he will accept it.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Be not fainthearted when thou makest thy prayer, and neglect not to give alms.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Laugh no man to scorn in the bitterness of his soul: for there is one which humbleth and exalteth.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Devise not a lie against thy brother; neither do the like to thy friend.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Use not to make any manner of lie: for the custom thereof is not good.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Use not many words in a multitude of elders, and make not much babbling when thou prayest.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Hate not laborious work, neither husbandry, which the most High hath ordained.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Number not thyself among the multitude of sinners, but remember that wrath will not tarry long.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Humble thyself greatly: for the vengeance of the ungodly is fire and worms.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Change not a friend for any good by no means; neither a faithful brother for the gold of Ophir.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Forego not a wise and good woman: for her grace is above gold.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Whereas thy servant worketh truly, entreat him not evil. nor the hireling that bestoweth himself wholly for thee.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Let thy soul love a good servant, and defraud him not of liberty.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Hast thou cattle? have an eye to them: and if they be for thy profit, keep them with thee.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Hast thou children? instruct them, and bow down their neck from their youth.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Marry thy daughter, and so shalt thou have performed a weighty matter: but give her to a man of understanding.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Hast thou a wife after thy mind? forsake her not: but give not thyself over to a light woman.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Honour thy father with thy whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of thy mother.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Remember that thou wast begotten of them; and how canst thou recompense them the things that they have done for thee?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Fear the Lord with all thy soul, and reverence his priests.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Love him that made thee with all thy strength, and forsake not his ministers.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Fear the Lord, and honor the priest; and give him his portion, as it is commanded thee; the firstfruits, and the trespass offering, and the gift of the shoulders, and the sacrifice of sanctification, and the firstfruits of the holy things.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And stretch thine hand unto the poor, that thy blessing may be perfected.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> A gift hath grace in the sight of every man living; and for the dead detain it not.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Fail not to be with them that weep, and mourn with them that mourn.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Be not slow to visit the sick: for that shall make thee to be beloved.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Whatsoever thou takest in hand, remember the end, and thou shalt never do amiss.
