<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The Lord created man of the earth, and turned him into it again.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He gave them few days, and a short time, and power also over the things therein.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> He endued them with strength by themselves, and made them according to his image,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And put the fear of man upon all flesh, and gave him dominion over beasts and fowls.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They received the use of the five operations of the Lord, and in the sixth place he imparted them understanding, and in the seventh speech, an interpreter of the cogitations thereof.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Counsel, and a tongue, and eyes, ears, and a heart, gave he them to understand.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Withal he filled them with the knowledge of understanding, and shewed them good and evil.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> He set his eye upon their hearts, that he might shew them the greatness of his works.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He gave them to glory in his marvellous acts for ever, that they might declare his works with understanding.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And the elect shall praise his holy name.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Beside this he gave them knowledge, and the law of life for an heritage.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He made an everlasting covenant with them, and shewed them his judgments.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Their eyes saw the majesty of his glory, and their ears heard his glorious voice.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And he said unto them, Beware of all unrighteousness; and he gave every man commandment concerning his neighbour.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Their ways are ever before him, and shall not be hid from his eyes.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Every man from his youth is given to evil; neither could they make to themselves fleshy hearts for stony.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For in the division of the nations of the whole earth he set a ruler over every people; but Israel is the Lord’s portion:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Whom, being his firstborn, he nourisheth with discipline, and giving him the light of his love doth not forsake him.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Therefore all their works are as the sun before him, and his eyes are continually upon their ways.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> None of their unrighteous deeds are hid from him, but all their sins are before the Lord

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But the Lord being gracious and knowing his workmanship, neither left nor forsook them, but spared them.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The alms of a man is as a signet with him, and he will keep the good deeds of man as the apple of the eye, and give repentance to his sons and daughters.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Afterwards he will rise up and reward them, and render their recompence upon their heads.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But unto them that repent, he granted them return, and comforted those that failed in patience.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Return unto the Lord, and forsake thy sins, make thy prayer before his face, and offend less.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Turn again to the most High, and turn away from iniquity: for he will lead thee out of darkness into the light of health, and hate thou abomination vehemently.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Who shall praise the most High in the grave, instead of them which live and give thanks?

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Thanksgiving perisheth from the dead, as from one that is not: the living and sound in heart shall praise the Lord.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> How great is the lovingkindness of the Lord our God, and his compassion unto such as turn unto him in holiness!

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> For all things cannot be in men, because the son of man is not immortal.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> What is brighter than the sun? yet the light thereof faileth; and flesh and blood will imagine evil.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> He vieweth the power of the height of heaven; and all men are but earth and ashes.
