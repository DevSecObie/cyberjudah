<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The pride of the height, the clear firmament, the beauty of heaven, with his glorious shew;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The sun when it appeareth, declaring at his rising a marvellous instrument, the work of the most High:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> At noon it parcheth the country, and who can abide the burning heat thereof?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> A man blowing a furnace is in works of heat, but the sun burneth the mountains three times more; breathing out fiery vapours, and sending forth bright beams, it dimmeth the eyes.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Great is the Lord that made it; and at his commandment runneth hastily.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> From the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her changing, being an instrument of the armies above, shining in the firmament of heaven;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> The beauty of heaven, the glory of the stars, an ornament giving light in the highest places of the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> At the commandment of the Holy One they will stand in their order, and never faint in their watches.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Look upon the rainbow, and praise him that made it; very beautiful it is in the brightness thereof.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> It compasseth the heaven about with a glorious circle, and the hands of the most High have bended it.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> By his commandment he maketh the snow to fall aplace, and sendeth swiftly the lightnings of his judgment.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Through this the treasures are opened: and clouds fly forth as fowls.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> By his great power he maketh the clouds firm, and the hailstones are broken small.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> At his sight the mountains are shaken, and at his will the south wind bloweth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The noise of the thunder maketh the earth to tremble: so doth the northern storm and the whirlwind: as birds flying he scattereth the snow, and the falling down thereof is as the lighting of grasshoppers:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> The eye marvelleth at the beauty of the whiteness thereof, and the heart is astonished at the raining of it.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The hoarfrost also as salt he poureth on the earth, and being congealed, it lieth on the top of sharp stakes.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> When the cold north wind bloweth, and the water is congealed into ice, it abideth upon every gathering together of water, and clotheth the water as with a breastplate.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> It devoureth the mountains, and burneth the wilderness, and consumeth the grass as fire.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> A present remedy of all is a mist coming speedily, a dew coming after heat refresheth.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> By his counsel he appeaseth the deep, and planteth islands therein.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> They that sail on the sea tell of the danger thereof; and when we hear it with our ears, we marvel thereat.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For therein be strange and wondrous works, variety of all kinds of beasts and whales created.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> By him the end of them hath prosperous success, and by his word all things consist.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> We may speak much, and yet come short: wherefore in sum, he is all.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> How shall we be able to magnify him? for he is great above all his works.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> The Lord is terrible and very great, and marvellous is his power.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> When ye glorify the Lord, exalt him as much as ye can; for even yet will he far exceed: and when ye exalt him, put forth all your strength, and be not weary; for ye can never go far enough.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Who hath seen him, that he might tell us? and who can magnify him as he is?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> There are yet hid greater things than these be, for we have seen but a few of his works.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> For the Lord hath made all things; and to the godly hath he given wisdom.
