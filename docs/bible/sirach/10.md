<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> A wise judge will instruct his people; and the government of a prudent man is well ordered.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> As the judge of the people is himself, so are his officers; and what manner of man the ruler of the city is, such are all they that dwell therein.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> An unwise king destroyeth his people; but through the prudence of them which are in authority the city shall be inhabited.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> The power of the earth is in the hand of the Lord, and in due time he will set over it one that is profitable.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> In the hand of God is the prosperity of man: and upon the person of the scribe shall he lay his honour.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Bear not hatred to thy neighbour for every wrong; and do nothing at all by injurious practices.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Pride is hateful before God and man: and by both doth one commit iniquity.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Because of unrighteous dealings, injuries, and riches got by deceit, the kingdom is translated from one people to another.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Why is earth and ashes proud? There is not a more wicked thing than a covetous man: for such an one setteth his own soul to sale; because while he liveth he casteth away his bowels.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> The physician cutteth off a long disease; and he that is to day a king to morrow shall die.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For when a man is dead, he shall inherit creeping things, beasts, and worms.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The beginning of pride is when one departeth from God, and his heart is turned away from his Maker.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For pride is the beginning of sin, and he that hath it shall pour out abomination: and therefore the Lord brought upon them strange calamities, and overthrew them utterly.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Lord hath cast down the thrones of proud princes, and set up the meek in their stead.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The Lord hath plucked up the roots of the proud nations, and planted the lowly in their place.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The Lord overthrew countries of the heathen, and destroyed them to the foundations of the earth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He took some of them away, and destroyed them, and hath made their memorial to cease from the earth.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Pride was not made for men, nor furious anger for them that are born of a woman.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> They that fear the Lord are a sure seed, and they that love him an honourable plant: they that regard not the law are a dishonourable seed; they that transgress the commandments are a deceivable seed.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Among brethren he that is chief is honorable; so are they that fear the Lord in his eyes.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The fear of the Lord goeth before the obtaining of authority: but roughness and pride is the losing thereof.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Whether he be rich, noble, or poor, their glory is the fear of the Lord.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> It is not meet to despise the poor man that hath understanding; neither is it convenient to magnify a sinful man.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Great men, and judges, and potentates, shall be honoured; yet is there none of them greater than he that feareth the Lord.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Unto the servant that is wise shall they that are free do service: and he that hath knowledge will not grudge when he is reformed.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Be not overwise in doing thy business; and boast not thyself in the time of thy distress.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Better is he that laboureth, and aboundeth in all things, than he that boasteth himself, and wanteth bread.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> My son, glorify thy soul in meekness, and give it honour according to the dignity thereof.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Who will justify him that sinneth against his own soul? and who will honour him that dishonoureth his own life?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> The poor man is honoured for his skill, and the rich man is honoured for his riches.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> He that is honoured in poverty, how much more in riches? and he that is dishonourable in riches, how much more in poverty?
