<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Wisdom lifteth up the head of him that is of low degree, and maketh him to sit among great men.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Commend not a man for his beauty; neither abhor a man for his outward appearance.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The bee is little among such as fly; but her fruit is the chief of sweet things.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Boast not of thy clothing and raiment, and exalt not thyself in the day of honour: for the works of the Lord are wonderful, and his works among men are hidden.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Many kings have sat down upon the ground; and one that was never thought of hath worn the crown.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Many mighty men have been greatly disgraced; and the honourable delivered into other men’s hands.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Blame not before thou hast examined the truth: understand first, and then rebuke.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Answer not before thou hast heard the cause: neither interrupt men in the midst of their talk.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Strive not in a matter that concerneth thee not; and sit not in judgment with sinners.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> My son, meddle not with many matters: for if thou meddle much, thou shalt not be innocent; and if thou follow after, thou shalt not obtain, neither shalt thou escape by fleeing.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> There is one that laboureth, and taketh pains, and maketh haste, and is so much the more behind.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Again, there is another that is slow, and hath need of help, wanting ability, and full of poverty; yet the eye of the Lord looked upon him for good, and set him up from his low estate,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And lifted up his head from misery; so that many that saw from him is peace over all the

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Prosperity and adversity, life and death, poverty and riches, come of the Lord.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Wisdom, knowledge, and understanding of the law, are of the Lord: love, and the way of good works, are from him.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Error and darkness had their beginning together with sinners: and evil shall wax old with them that glory therein.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> The gift of the Lord remaineth with the ungodly, and his favour bringeth prosperity for ever.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> There is that waxeth rich by his wariness and pinching, and this his the portion of his reward:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Whereas he saith, I have found rest, and now will eat continually of my goods; and yet he knoweth not what time shall come upon him, and that he must leave those things to others, and die.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Be stedfast in thy covenant, and be conversant therein, and wax old in thy work.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Marvel not at the works of sinners; but trust in the Lord, and abide in thy labour: for it is an easy thing in the sight of the Lord on the sudden to make a poor man rich.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The blessing of the Lord is in the reward of the godly, and suddenly he maketh his blessing flourish.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Say not, What profit is there of my service? and what good things shall I have hereafter?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Again, say not, I have enough, and possess many things, and what evil shall I have hereafter?

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> In the day of prosperity there is a forgetfulness of affliction: and in the day of affliction there is no more remembrance of prosperity.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For it is an easy thing unto the Lord in the day of death to reward a man according to his ways.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> The affliction of an hour maketh a man forget pleasure: and in his end his deeds shall be discovered.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Judge none blessed before his death: for a man shall be known in his children.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Bring not every man into thine house: for the deceitful man hath many trains.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Like as a partridge taken and kept in a cage, so is the heart of the proud; and like as a spy, watcheth he for thy fall:

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> For he lieth in wait, and turneth good into evil, and in things worthy praise will lay blame upon thee.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Of a spark of fire a heap of coals is kindled: and a sinful man layeth wait for blood.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Take heed of a mischievous man, for he worketh wickedness; lest he bring upon thee a perpetual blot.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Receive a stranger into thine house, and he will disturb thee, and turn thee out of thine own.
