<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Moreover, brethren, I would not that ye should be ignorant, how that all our fathers were under the cloud, and all passed through the sea;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And were all baptized unto Moses in the cloud and in the sea;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And did all eat the same spiritual meat;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And did all drink the same spiritual drink: for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But with many of them God was not well pleased: for they were overthrown in the wilderness.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Now these things were our examples, to the intent we should not lust after evil things, as they also lusted.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Neither be ye idolaters, as were some of them; as it is written, The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed, and fell in one day three and twenty thousand.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them also tempted, and were destroyed of serpents.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Neither murmur ye, as some of them also murmured, and were destroyed of the destroyer.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Wherefore let him that thinketh he standeth take heed lest he fall.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Wherefore, my dearly beloved, flee from idolatry.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I speak as to wise men; judge ye what I say.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The cup of blessing which we bless, is it not the communion of the blood of Christ? The bread which we break, is it not the communion of the body of Christ?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For we being many are one bread, and one body: for we are all partakers of that one bread.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Behold Israel after the flesh: are not they which eat of the sacrifices partakers of the altar?

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> What say I then? that the idol is any thing, or that which is offered in sacrifice to idols is any thing?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But I say, that the things which the Gentiles sacrifice, they sacrifice to devils, and not to God: and I would not that ye should have fellowship with devils.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Ye cannot drink the cup of the Lord, and the cup of devils: ye cannot be partakers of the Lord’s table, and of the table of devils.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Do we provoke the Lord to jealousy? are we stronger than he?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> All things are lawful for me, but all things are not expedient: all things are lawful for me, but all things edify not.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Let no man seek his own, but every man another’s wealth.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Whatsoever is sold in the shambles, that eat, asking no question for conscience sake:

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For the earth is the Lord’s, and the fulness thereof.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> If any of them that believe not bid you to a feast, and ye be disposed to go; whatsoever is set before you, eat, asking no question for conscience sake.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> But if any man say unto you, This is offered in sacrifice unto idols, eat not for his sake that shewed it, and for conscience sake: for the earth is the Lord’s, and the fulness thereof:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Conscience, I say, not thine own, but of the other: for why is my liberty judged of another man’s conscience?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> For if I by grace be a partaker, why am I evil spoken of for that for which I give thanks?

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Whether therefore ye eat, or drink, or whatsoever ye do, do all to the glory of God.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Give none offence, neither to the Jews, nor to the Gentiles, nor to the church of God:

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Even as I please all men in all things, not seeking mine own profit, but the profit of many, that they may be saved.
