<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For I verily, as absent in body, but present in spirit, have judged already, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done this deed,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> I wrote unto you in an epistle not to company with fornicators:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Yet not altogether with the fornicators of this world, or with the covetous, or extortioners, or with idolaters; for then must ye needs go out of the world.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But now I have written unto you not to keep company, if any man that is called a brother be a fornicator, or covetous, or an idolater, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner; with such an one no not to eat.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For what have I to do to judge them also that are without? do not ye judge them that are within?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But them that are without God judgeth. Therefore put away from among yourselves that wicked person.
