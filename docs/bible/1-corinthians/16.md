<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I have given order to the churches of Galatia, even so do ye.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Upon the first day of the week let every one of you lay by him in store, as God hath prospered him, that there be no gatherings when I come.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And when I come, whomsoever ye shall approve by your letters, them will I send to bring your liberality unto Jerusalem.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And if it be meet that I go also, they shall go with me.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Now I will come unto you, when I shall pass through Macedonia: for I do pass through Macedonia.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And it may be that I will abide, yea, and winter with you, that ye may bring me on my journey whithersoever I go.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For I will not see you now by the way; but I trust to tarry a while with you, if the Lord permit.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But I will tarry at Ephesus until Pentecost.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For a great door and effectual is opened unto me, and there are many adversaries.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Now if Timotheus come, see that he may be with you without fear: for he worketh the work of the Lord, as I also do.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Let no man therefore despise him: but conduct him forth in peace, that he may come unto me: for I look for him with the brethren.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> As touching our brother Apollos, I greatly desired him to come unto you with the brethren: but his will was not at all to come at this time; but he will come when he shall have convenient time.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Let all your things be done with charity.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I beseech you, brethren, (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints,)

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> That ye submit yourselves unto such, and to every one that helpeth with us, and laboureth.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I am glad of the coming of Stephanas and Fortunatus and Achaicus: for that which was lacking on your part they have supplied.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For they have refreshed my spirit and yours: therefore acknowledge ye them that are such.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> The churches of Asia salute you. Aquila and Priscilla salute you much in the Lord, with the church that is in their house.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> All the brethren greet you. Greet ye one another with an holy kiss.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The salutation of me Paul with mine own hand.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> If any man love not the Lord Jesus Christ, let him be Anathema Maran-atha.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> My love be with you all in Christ Jesus. Amen. The first epistle to the Corinthians was written from Philippi by Stephanas, and Fortunatus, and Achaicus, and Timotheus.
