*Taught in [Deuteronomy 23-26: Deuteronomy 23: Who May Enter the Congregation and the Holy Camp](/study/deuteronomy/23-26)*

<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her: then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And when she is departed out of his house, she may go and be another man’s wife.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And if the latter husband hate her, and write her a bill of divorcement, and giveth it in her hand, and sendeth her out of his house; or if the latter husband die, which took her to be his wife;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Her former husband, which sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife, after that she is defiled; for that is abomination before the Lord: and thou shalt not cause the land to sin, which the Lord thy God giveth thee for an inheritance.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> When a man hath taken a new wife, he shall not go out to war, neither shall he be charged with any business: but he shall be free at home one year, and shall cheer up his wife which he hath taken.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> No man shall take the nether or the upper millstone to pledge: for he taketh a man’s life to pledge.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> If a man be found stealing any of his brethren of the children of Israel, and maketh merchandise of him, or selleth him; then that thief shall die; and thou shalt put evil away from among you.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Take heed in the plague of leprosy, that thou observe diligently, and do according to all that the priests the Levites shall teach you: as I commanded them, so ye shall observe to do.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Remember what the Lord thy God did unto Miriam by the way, after that ye were come forth out of Egypt.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> When thou dost lend thy brother any thing, thou shalt not go into his house to fetch his pledge.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thou shalt stand abroad, and the man to whom thou dost lend shall bring out the pledge abroad unto thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And if the man be poor, thou shalt not sleep with his pledge:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> In any case thou shalt deliver him the pledge again when the sun goeth down, that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee: and it shall be righteousness unto thee before the Lord thy God.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Thou shalt not oppress an hired servant that is poor and needy, whether he be of thy brethren, or of thy strangers that are in thy land within thy gates:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> At his day thou shalt give him his hire, neither shall the sun go down upon it; for he is poor, and setteth his heart upon it: lest he cry against thee unto the Lord, and it be sin unto thee.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: every man shall be put to death for his own sin.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Thou shalt not pervert the judgment of the stranger, nor of the fatherless; nor take a widow’s raiment to pledge:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> But thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in Egypt, and the Lord thy God redeemed thee thence: therefore I command thee to do this thing.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> When thou cuttest down thine harvest in thy field, and hast forgot a sheaf in the field, thou shalt not go again to fetch it: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow: that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all the work of thine hands.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> When thou beatest thine olive tree, thou shalt not go over the boughs again: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> When thou gatherest the grapes of thy vineyard, thou shalt not glean it afterward: it shall be for the stranger, for the fatherless, and for the widow.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in the land of Egypt: therefore I command thee to do this thing.
