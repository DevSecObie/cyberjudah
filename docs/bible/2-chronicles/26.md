<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then all the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in the room of his father Amaziah.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He built Eloth, and restored it to Judah, after that the king slept with his fathers.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Sixteen years old was Uzziah when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty and two years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Amaziah did.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And he sought God in the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the visions of God: and as long as he sought the Lord, God made him to prosper.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And he went forth and warred against the Philistines, and brake down the wall of Gath, and the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod, and built cities about Ashdod, and among the Philistines.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And God helped him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians that dwelt in Gur–baal, and the Mehunims.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And the Ammonites gave gifts to Uzziah: and his name spread abroad even to the entering in of Egypt; for he strengthened himself exceedingly.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Moreover Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the corner gate, and at the valley gate, and at the turning of the wall, and fortified them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Also he built towers in the desert, and digged many wells: for he had much cattle, both in the low country, and in the plains: husbandmen also, and vine dressers in the mountains, and in Carmel: for he loved husbandry.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Moreover Uzziah had an host of fighting men, that went out to war by bands, according to the number of their account by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the ruler, under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king’s captains.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The whole number of the chief of the fathers of the mighty men of valour were two thousand and six hundred.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And under their hand was an army, three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred, that made war with mighty power, to help the king against the enemy.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And Uzziah prepared for them throughout all the host shields, and spears, and helmets, and habergeons, and bows, and slings to cast stones.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And he made in Jerusalem engines, invented by cunning men, to be on the towers and upon the bulwarks, to shoot arrows and great stones withal. And his name spread far abroad; for he was marvellously helped, till he was strong.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed against the Lord his God, and went into the temple of the Lord to burn incense upon the altar of incense.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him fourscore priests of the Lord, that were valiant men:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And they withstood Uzziah the king, and said unto him, It appertaineth not unto thee, Uzziah, to burn incense unto the Lord, but to the priests the sons of Aaron, that are consecrated to burn incense: go out of the sanctuary; for thou hast trespassed; neither shall it be for thine honour from the Lord God.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Then Uzziah was wroth, and had a censer in his hand to burn incense: and while he was wroth with the priests, the leprosy even rose up in his forehead before the priests in the house of the Lord, from beside the incense altar.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And Azariah the chief priest, and all the priests, looked upon him, and, behold, he was leprous in his forehead, and they thrust him out from thence; yea, himself hasted also to go out, because the Lord had smitten him.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And Uzziah the king was a leper unto the day of his death, and dwelt in a several house, being a leper; for he was cut off from the house of the Lord: and Jotham his son was over the king’s house, judging the people of the land.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, did Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, write.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> So Uzziah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial which belonged to the kings; for they said, He is a leper: and Jotham his son reigned in his stead.
