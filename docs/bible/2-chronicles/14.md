<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> So Abijah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David: and Asa his son reigned in his stead. In his days the land was quiet ten years.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And Asa did that which was good and right in the eyes of the Lord his God:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For he took away the altars of the strange gods, and the high places, and brake down the images, and cut down the groves:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And commanded Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers, and to do the law and the commandment.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Also he took away out of all the cities of Judah the high places and the images: and the kingdom was quiet before him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And he built fenced cities in Judah: for the land had rest, and he had no war in those years; because the Lord had given him rest.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Therefore he said unto Judah, Let us build these cities, and make about them walls, and towers, gates, and bars, while the land is yet before us; because we have sought the Lord our God, we have sought him, and he hath given us rest on every side. So they built and prospered.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And Asa had an army of men that bare targets and spears, out of Judah three hundred thousand; and out of Benjamin, that bare shields and drew bows, two hundred and fourscore thousand: all these were mighty men of valour.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And there came out against them Zerah the Ethiopian with an host of a thousand thousand, and three hundred chariots; and came unto Mareshah.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Then Asa went out against him, and they set the battle in array in the valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And Asa cried unto the Lord his God, and said, Lord, it is nothing with thee to help, whether with many, or with them that have no power: help us, O Lord our God; for we rest on thee, and in thy name we go against this multitude. O Lord, thou art our God; let not man prevail against thee.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So the Lord smote the Ethiopians before Asa, and before Judah; and the Ethiopians fled.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And Asa and the people that were with him pursued them unto Gerar: and the Ethiopians were overthrown, that they could not recover themselves; for they were destroyed before the Lord, and before his host; and they carried away very much spoil.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And they smote all the cities round about Gerar; for the fear of the Lord came upon them: and they spoiled all the cities; for there was exceeding much spoil in them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> They smote also the tents of cattle, and carried away sheep and camels in abundance, and returned to Jerusalem.
