<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But I determined this with myself, that I would not come again to you in heaviness.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For if I make you sorry, who is he then that maketh me glad, but the same which is made sorry by me?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And I wrote this same unto you, lest, when I came, I should have sorrow from them of whom I ought to rejoice; having confidence in you all, that my joy is the joy of you all.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For out of much affliction and anguish of heart I wrote unto you with many tears; not that ye should be grieved, but that ye might know the love which I have more abundantly unto you.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But if any have caused grief, he hath not grieved me, but in part: that I may not overcharge you all.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Sufficient to such a man is this punishment, which was inflicted of many.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> So that contrariwise ye ought rather to forgive him, and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Wherefore I beseech you that ye would confirm your love toward him.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For to this end also did I write, that I might know the proof of you, whether ye be obedient in all things.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> To whom ye forgive any thing, I forgive also: for if I forgave any thing, to whom I forgave it, for your sakes forgave I it in the person of Christ;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Furthermore, when I came to Troas to preach Christ’s gospel, and a door was opened unto me of the Lord,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> I had no rest in my spirit, because I found not Titus my brother: but taking my leave of them, I went from thence into Macedonia.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Now thanks be unto God, which always causeth us to triumph in Christ, and maketh manifest the savour of his knowledge by us in every place.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> To the one we are the savour of death unto death; and to the other the savour of life unto life. And who is sufficient for these things?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For we are not as many, which corrupt the word of God: but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ.
