<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Moreover, brethren, we do you to wit of the grace of God bestowed on the churches of Macedonia;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> How that in a great trial of affliction the abundance of their joy and their deep poverty abounded unto the riches of their liberality.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For to their power, I bear record, yea, and beyond their power they were willing of themselves;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Praying us with much intreaty that we would receive the gift, and take upon us the fellowship of the ministering to the saints.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And this they did, not as we hoped, but first gave their own selves to the Lord, and unto us by the will of God.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Insomuch that we desired Titus, that as he had begun, so he would also finish in you the same grace also.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Therefore, as ye abound in every thing, in faith, and utterance, and knowledge, and in all diligence, and in your love to us, see that ye abound in this grace also.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I speak not by commandment, but by occasion of the forwardness of others, and to prove the sincerity of your love.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For ye know the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, that, though he was rich, yet for your sakes he became poor, that ye through his poverty might be rich.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And herein I give my advice: for this is expedient for you, who have begun before, not only to do, but also to be forward a year ago.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Now therefore perform the doing of it; that as there was a readiness to will, so there may be a performance also out of that which ye have.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For if there be first a willing mind, it is accepted according to that a man hath, and not according to that he hath not.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For I mean not that other men be eased, and ye burdened:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But by an equality, that now at this time your abundance may be a supply for their want, that their abundance also may be a supply for your want: that there may be equality:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> As it is written, He that had gathered much had nothing over; and he that had gathered little had no lack.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But thanks be to God, which put the same earnest care into the heart of Titus for you.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For indeed he accepted the exhortation; but being more forward, of his own accord he went unto you.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And we have sent with him the brother, whose praise is in the gospel throughout all the churches;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And not that only, but who was also chosen of the churches to travel with us with this grace, which is administered by us to the glory of the same Lord, and declaration of your ready mind:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Avoiding this, that no man should blame us in this abundance which is administered by us:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Providing for honest things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And we have sent with them our brother, whom we have oftentimes proved diligent in many things, but now much more diligent, upon the great confidence which I have in you.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Whether any do enquire of Titus, he is my partner and fellowhelper concerning you: or our brethren be enquired of, they are the messengers of the churches, and the glory of Christ.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Wherefore shew ye to them, and before the churches, the proof of your love, and of our boasting on your behalf.
