<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Would to God ye could bear with me a little in my folly: and indeed bear with me.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But I fear, lest by any means, as the serpent beguiled Eve through his subtilty, so your minds should be corrupted from the simplicity that is in Christ.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For if he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or if ye receive another spirit, which ye have not received, or another gospel, which ye have not accepted, ye might well bear with him.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For I suppose I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But though I be rude in speech, yet not in knowledge; but we have been throughly made manifest among you in all things.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Have I committed an offence in abasing myself that ye might be exalted, because I have preached to you the gospel of God freely?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> I robbed other churches, taking wages of them, to do you service.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And when I was present with you, and wanted, I was chargeable to no man: for that which was lacking to me the brethren which came from Macedonia supplied: and in all things I have kept myself from being burdensome unto you, and so will I keep myself.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As the truth of Christ is in me, no man shall stop me of this boasting in the regions of Achaia.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wherefore? because I love you not? God knoweth.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off occasion from them which desire occasion; that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For such are false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Therefore it is no great thing if his ministers also be transformed as the ministers of righteousness; whose end shall be according to their works.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> I say again, Let no man think me a fool; if otherwise, yet as a fool receive me, that I may boast myself a little.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> That which I speak, I speak it not after the Lord, but as it were foolishly, in this confidence of boasting.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Seeing that many glory after the flesh, I will glory also.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For ye suffer fools gladly, seeing ye yourselves are wise.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For ye suffer, if a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour you, if a man take of you, if a man exalt himself, if a man smite you on the face.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> I speak as concerning reproach, as though we had been weak. Howbeit whereinsoever any is bold, (I speak foolishly,) I am bold also.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Are they Hebrews? so am I. Are they Israelites? so am I. Are they the seed of Abraham? so am I.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Are they ministers of Christ? (I speak as a fool) I am more; in labours more abundant, in stripes above measure, in prisons more frequent, in deaths oft.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Of the Jews five times received I forty stripes save one.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Thrice was I beaten with rods, once was I stoned, thrice I suffered shipwreck, a night and a day I have been in the deep;

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> In journeyings often, in perils of waters, in perils of robbers, in perils by mine own countrymen, in perils by the heathen, in perils in the city, in perils in the wilderness, in perils in the sea, in perils among false brethren;

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> In weariness and painfulness, in watchings often, in hunger and thirst, in fastings often, in cold and nakedness.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Beside those things that are without, that which cometh upon me daily, the care of all the churches.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Who is weak, and I am not weak? who is offended, and I burn not?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> If I must needs glory, I will glory of the things which concern mine infirmities.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, which is blessed for evermore, knoweth that I lie not.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> In Damascus the governor under Aretas the king kept the city of the Damascenes with a garrison, desirous to apprehend me:

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And through a window in a basket was I let down by the wall, and escaped his hands.
