<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> (For he saith, I have heard thee in a time accepted, and in the day of salvation have I succoured thee: behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation.)

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Giving no offence in any thing, that the ministry be not blamed:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But in all things approving ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in afflictions, in necessities, in distresses,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> In stripes, in imprisonments, in tumults, in labours, in watchings, in fastings;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> By pureness, by knowledge, by longsuffering, by kindness, by the Holy Ghost, by love unfeigned,

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> By the word of truth, by the power of God, by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> By honour and dishonour, by evil report and good report: as deceivers, and yet true;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> As unknown, and yet well known; as dying, and, behold, we live; as chastened, and not killed;

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As sorrowful, yet alway rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; as having nothing, and yet possessing all things.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> O ye Corinthians, our mouth is open unto you, our heart is enlarged.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Ye are not straitened in us, but ye are straitened in your own bowels.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Now for a recompence in the same, (I speak as unto my children,) be ye also enlarged.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? and what communion hath light with darkness?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And what concord hath Christ with Belial? or what part hath he that believeth with an infidel?

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? for ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said, I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.
