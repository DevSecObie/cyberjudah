<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> So they sent ambassadors unto him to treat of peace, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Behold, we the servants of Nabuchodonosor the great king lie before thee; use us as shall be good in thy sight.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Behold, our houses, and all our places, and all our fields of wheat, and flocks, and herds, and all the lodges of our tents lie before thy face; use them as it pleaseth thee.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Behold, even our cities and the inhabitants thereof are thy servants; come and deal with them as seemeth good unto thee.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> So the men came to Holofernes, and declared unto him after this manner.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then came he down toward the sea coast, both he and his army, and set garrisons in the high cities, and took out of them chosen men for aid.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> So they and all the country round about received them with garlands, with dances, and with timbrels.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Yet he did cast down their frontiers, and cut down their groves: for he had decreed to destroy all the gods of the land, that all nations should worship Nabuchodonosor only, and that all tongues and tribes should call upon him as god.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Also he came over against Esdraelon near unto Judea, over against the great strait of Judea.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And he pitched between Geba and Scythopolis, and there he tarried a whole month, that he might gather together all the carriages of his army.
