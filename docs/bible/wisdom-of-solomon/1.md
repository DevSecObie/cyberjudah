<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Love righteousness, ye that be judges of the earth: think of the Lord with a good (heart,) and in simplicity of heart seek him.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For he will be found of them that tempt him not; and sheweth himself unto such as do not distrust him.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; nor dwell in the body that is subject unto sin.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For wisdom is a loving spirit; and will not acquit a blasphemer of his words: for God is witness of his reins, and a true beholder of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For the Spirit of the Lord filleth the world: and that which containeth all things hath knowledge of the voice.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Therefore he that speaketh unrighteous things cannot be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punisheth, pass by him.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For inquisition shall be made into the counsels of the ungodly: and the sound of his words shall come unto the Lord for the manifestation of his wicked deeds.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For the ear of jealousy heareth all things: and the noise of murmurings is not hid.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Therefore beware of murmuring, which is unprofitable; and refrain your tongue from backbiting: for there is no word so secret, that shall go for nought: and the mouth that belieth slayeth the soul.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Seek not death in the error of your life: and pull not upon yourselves destruction with the works of your hands.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For God made not death: neither hath he pleasure in the destruction of the living.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For he created all things, that they might have their being: and the generations of the world were healthful; and there is no poison of destruction in them, nor the kingdom of death upon the earth:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> (For righteousness is immortal:)

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> But ungodly men with their works and words called it to them: for when they thought to have it their friend, they consumed to nought, and made a covenant with it, because they are worthy to take part with it.
