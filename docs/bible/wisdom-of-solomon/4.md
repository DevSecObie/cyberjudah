<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Better it is to have no children, and to have virtue: for the memorial thereof is immortal: because it is known with God, and with men.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> When it is present, men take example at it; and when it is gone, they desire it: it weareth a crown, and triumpheth for ever, having gotten the victory, striving for undefiled rewards.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> But the multiplying brood of the ungodly shall not thrive, nor take deep rooting from bastard slips, nor lay any fast foundation.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For though they flourish in branches for a time; yet standing not last, they shall be shaken with the wind, and through the force of winds they shall be rooted out.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> The imperfect branches shall be broken off, their fruit unprofitable, not ripe to eat, yea, meet for nothing.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For children begotten of unlawful beds are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> But though the righteous be prevented with death, yet shall he be in rest.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For honourable age is not that which standeth in length of time, nor that is measured by number of years.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But wisdom is the gray hair unto men, and an unspotted life is old age.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He pleased God, and was beloved of him: so that living among sinners he was translated.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Yea speedily was he taken away, lest that wickedness should alter his understanding, or deceit beguile his soul.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For the bewitching of naughtiness doth obscure things that are honest; and the wandering of concupiscence doth undermine the simple mind.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He, being made perfect in a short time, fulfilled a long time:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For his soul pleased the Lord: therefore hasted he to take him away from among the wicked.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> This the people saw, and understood it not, neither laid they up this in their minds, That his grace and mercy is with his saints, and that he hath respect unto his chosen.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Thus the righteous that is dead shall condemn the ungodly which are living; and youth that is soon perfected the many years and old age of the unrighteous.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For they shall see the end of the wise, and shall not understand what God in his counsel hath decreed of him, and to what end the Lord hath set him in safety.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> They shall see him, and despise him; but God shall laugh them to scorn: and they shall hereafter be a vile carcase, and a reproach among the dead for evermore.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For he shall rend them, and cast them down headlong, that they shall be speechless; and he shall shake them from the foundation; and they shall be utterly laid waste, and be in sorrow; and their memorial shall perish.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And when they cast up the accounts of their sins, they shall come with fear: and their own iniquities shall convince them to their face.
