<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> For the ungodly said, reasoning with themselves, but not aright, Our life is short and tedious, and in the death of a man there is no remedy: neither was there any man known to have returned from the grave.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For we are born at all adventure: and we shall be hereafter as though we had never been: for the breath in our nostrils is as smoke, and a little spark in the moving of our heart:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Which being extinguished, our body shall be turned into ashes, and our spirit shall vanish as the soft air,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And our name shall be forgotten in time, and no man shall have our works in remembrance, and our life shall pass away as the trace of a cloud, and shall be dispersed as a mist, that is driven away with the beams of the sun, and overcome with the heat thereof.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For our time is a very shadow that passeth away; and after our end there is no returning: for it is fast sealed, so that no man cometh again.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Come on therefore, let us enjoy the good things that are present: and let us speedily use the creatures like as in youth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Let us fill ourselves with costly wine and ointments: and let no flower of the spring pass by us:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Let us crown ourselves with rosebuds, before they be withered:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Let none of us go without his part of our voluptuousness: let us leave tokens of our joyfulness in every place: for this is our portion, and our lot is this.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Let us oppress the poor righteous man, let us not spare the widow, nor reverence the ancient gray hairs of the aged.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Let our strength be the law of justice: for that which is feeble is found to be nothing worth.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Therefore let us lie in wait for the righteous; because he is not for our turn, and he is clean contrary to our doings: he upbraideth us with our offending the law, and objecteth to our infamy the transgressings of our education.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He professeth to have the knowledge of God: and he calleth himself the child of the Lord.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> He was made to reprove our thoughts.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He is grievous unto us even to behold: for his life is not like other men’s, his ways are of another fashion.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> We are esteemed of him as counterfeits: he abstaineth from our ways as from filthiness: he pronounceth the end of the just to be blessed, and maketh his boast that God is his father.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Let us see if his words be true: and let us prove what shall happen in the end of him.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For if the just man be the son of God, he will help him, and deliver him from the hand of his enemies.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Let us examine him with despitefulness and torture, that we may know his meekness, and prove his patience.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Let us condemn him with a shameful death: for by his own saying he shall be respected.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Such things they did imagine, and were deceived: for their own wickedness hath blinded them.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not: neither hoped they for the wages of righteousness, nor discerned a reward for blameless souls.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For God created man to be immortal, and made him to be an image of his own eternity.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Nevertheless through envy of the devil came death into the world: and they that do hold of his side do find it.
