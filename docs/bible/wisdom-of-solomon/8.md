<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Wisdom reacheth from one end to another mightily: and sweetly doth she order all things.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> I loved her, and sought her out from my youth, I desired to make her my spouse, and I was a lover of her beauty.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> In that she is conversant with God, she magnifieth her nobility: yea, the Lord of all things himself loved her.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For she is privy to the mysteries of the knowledge of God, and a lover of his works.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If riches be a possession to be desired in this life; what is richer than wisdom, that worketh all things?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And if prudence work; who of all that are is a more cunning workman than she?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And if a man love righteousness her labours are virtues: for she teacheth temperance and prudence, justice and fortitude: which are such things, as men can have nothing more profitable in their life.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> If a man desire much experience, she knoweth things of old, and conjectureth aright what is to come: she knoweth the subtilties of speeches, and can expound dark sentences: she foreseeth signs and wonders, and the events of seasons and times.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Therefore I purposed to take her to me to live with me, knowing that she would be a counsellor of good things, and a comfort in cares and grief.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For her sake I shall have estimation among the multitude, and honour with the elders, though I be young.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgment, and shall be admired in the sight of great men.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> When I hold my tongue, they shall bide my leisure, and when I speak, they shall give good ear unto me: if I talk much, they shall lay their hands upon their mouth.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Moreover by the means of her I shall obtain immortality, and leave behind me an everlasting memorial to them that come after me.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I shall set the people in order, and the nations shall be subject unto me.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Horrible tyrants shall be afraid, when they do but hear of me; I shall be found good among the multitude, and valiant in war.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> After I am come into mine house, I will repose myself with her: for her conversation hath no bitterness; and to live with her hath no sorrow, but mirth and joy.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Now when I considered these things in myself, and pondered them in my heart, how that to be allied unto wisdom is immortality;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And great pleasure it is to have her friendship; and in the works of her hands are infinite riches; and in the exercise of conference with her, prudence; and in talking with her, a good report; I went about seeking how to take her to me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For I was a witty child, and had a good spirit.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Yea rather, being good, I came into a body undefiled.
