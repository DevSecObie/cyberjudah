<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> But the souls of the righteous are in the hand of God, and there shall no torment touch them.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> In the sight of the unwise they seemed to die: and their departure is taken for misery,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And their going from us to be utter destruction: but they are in peace.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For though they be punished in the sight of men, yet is their hope full of immortality.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And having been a little chastised, they shall be greatly rewarded: for God proved them, and found them worthy for himself.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> As gold in the furnace hath he tried them, and received them as a burnt offering.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And in the time of their visitation they shall shine, and run to and fro like sparks among the stubble.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They shall judge the nations, and have dominion over the people, and their Lord shall reign for ever.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> They that put their trust in him shall understand the truth: and such as be faithful in love shall abide with him: for grace and mercy is to his saints, and he hath care for his elect.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But the ungodly shall be punished according to their own imaginations, which have neglected the righteous, and forsaken the Lord.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For whoso despiseth wisdom and nurture, he is miserable, and their hope is vain, their labours unfruitful, and their works unprofitable:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Their wives are foolish, and their children wicked:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Their offspring is cursed. Wherefore blessed is the barren that is undefiled, which hath not known the sinful bed: she shall have fruit in the visitation of souls.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And blessed is the eunuch, which with his hands hath wrought no iniquity, nor imagined wicked things against God: for unto him shall be given the special gift of faith, and an inheritance in the temple of the Lord more acceptable to his mind.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For glorious is the fruit of good labours: and the root of wisdom shall never fall away.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> As for the children of adulterers, they shall not come to their perfection, and the seed of an unrighteous bed shall be rooted out.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For though they live long, yet shall they be nothing regarded: and their last age shall be without honour.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Or, if they die quickly, they have no hope, neither comfort in the day of trial.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For horrible is the end of the unrighteous generation.
