<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> O God of my fathers, and Lord of mercy, who hast made all things with thy word,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And ordained man through thy wisdom, that he should have dominion over the creatures which thou hast made,

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And order the world according to equity and righteousness, and execute judgment with an upright heart:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Give me wisdom, that sitteth by thy throne; and reject me not from among thy children:

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For I thy servant and son of thine handmaid am a feeble person, and of a short time, and too young for the understanding of judgment and laws.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For though a man be never so perfect among the children of men, yet if thy wisdom be not with him, he shall be nothing regarded.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Thou hast chosen me to be a king of thy people, and a judge of thy sons and daughters:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thou hast commanded me to build a temple upon thy holy mount, and an altar in the city wherein thou dwellest, a resemblance of the holy tabernacle, which thou hast prepared from the beginning.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And wisdom was with thee: which knoweth thy works, and was present when thou madest the world, and knew what was acceptable in thy sight, and right in thy commandments.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> O send her out of thy holy heavens, and from the throne of thy glory, that being present she may labour with me, that I may know what is pleasing unto thee.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For she knoweth and understandeth all things, and she shall lead me soberly in my doings, and preserve me in her power.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So shall my works be acceptable, and then shall I judge thy people righteously, and be worthy to sit in my father’s seat.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For what man is he that can know the counsel of God? or who can think what the will of the Lord is?

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For the thoughts of mortal men are miserable, and our devices are but uncertain.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth, and with labour do we find the things that are before us: but the things that are in heaven who hath searched out?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And thy counsel who hath known, except thou give wisdom, and send thy Holy Spirit from above?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed, and men were taught the things that are pleasing unto thee, and were saved through wisdom.
