<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> She prospered their works in the hand of the holy prophet.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> They went through the wilderness that was not inhabited, and pitched tents in places where there lay no way.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They stood against their enemies, and were avenged of their adversaries.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> When they were thirsty, they called upon thee, and water was given them out of the flinty rock, and their thirst was quenched out of the hard stone.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For by what things their enemies were punished, by the same they in their need were benefited.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For instead of a perpetual running river troubled with foul blood,

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For a manifest reproof of that commandment, whereby the infants were slain, thou gavest unto them abundance of water by a means which they hoped not for:

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Declaring by that thirst then how thou hadst punished their adversaries.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For when they were tried albeit but in mercy chastised, they knew how the ungodly were judged in wrath and tormented, thirsting in another manner than the just.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For these thou didst admonish and try, as a father: but the other, as a severe king, thou didst condemn and punish.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Whether they were absent or present, they were vexed alike.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For a double grief came upon them, and a groaning for the remembrance of things past.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For when they heard by their own punishments the other to be benefited, they had some feeling of the Lord.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For whom they respected with scorn, when he was long before thrown out at the casting forth of the infants, him in the end, when they saw what came to pass, they admired.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But for the foolish devices of their wickedness, wherewith being deceived they worshipped serpents void of reason, and vile beasts, thou didst send a multitude of unreasonable beasts upon them for vengeance;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> That they might know, that wherewithal a man sinneth, by the same also shall he be punished.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For thy Almighty hand, that made the world of matter without form, wanted not means to send among them a multitude of bears or fierce lions,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Or unknown wild beasts, full of rage, newly created, breathing out either a fiery vapour, or filthy scents of scattered smoke, or shooting horrible sparkles out of their eyes:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Whereof not only the harm might dispatch them at once, but also the terrible sight utterly destroy them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Yea, and without these might they have fallen down with one blast, being persecuted of vengeance, and scattered abroad through the breath of thy power: but thou hast ordered all things in measure and number and weight.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For thou canst shew thy great strength at all times when thou wilt; and who may withstand the power of thine arm?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For the whole world before thee is as a little grain of the balance, yea, as a drop of the morning dew that falleth down upon the earth.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> But thou hast mercy upon all; for thou canst do all things, and winkest at the sins of men, because they should amend.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For thou lovest all the things that are, and abhorrest nothing which thou hast made: for never wouldest thou have made any thing, if thou hadst hated it.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And how could any thing have endured, if it had not been thy will? or been preserved, if not called by thee?

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But thou sparest all: for they are thine, O Lord, thou lover of souls.
