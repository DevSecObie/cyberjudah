<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Because, being ministers of his kingdom, ye have not judged aright, nor kept the law, nor walked after the counsel of God;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Horribly and speedily shall he come upon you: for a sharp judgment shall be to them that be in high places.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For mercy will soon pardon the meanest: but mighty men shall be mightily tormented.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For he which is Lord over all shall fear no man’s person, neither shall he stand in awe of any man’s greatness: for he hath made the small and great, and careth for all alike.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But a sore trial shall come upon the mighty.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Unto you therefore, O kings, do I speak, that ye may learn wisdom, and not fall away.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For they that keep holiness holily shall be judged holy: and they that have learned such things shall find what to answer.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Wherefore set your affection upon my words; desire them, and ye shall be instructed.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Wisdom is glorious, and never fadeth away: yea, she is easily seen of them that love her, and found of such as seek her.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> She preventeth them that desire her, in making herself first known unto them.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Whoso seeketh her early shall have no great travail: for he shall find her sitting at his doors.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> To think therefore upon her is perfection of wisdom: and whoso watcheth for her shall quickly be without care.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> For she goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her, sheweth herself favourably unto them in the ways, and meeteth them in every thought.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And love is the keeping of her laws; and the giving heed unto her laws is the assurance of incorruption;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And incorruption maketh us near unto God:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Therefore the desire of wisdom bringeth to a kingdom.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> If your delight be then in thrones and sceptres, O ye kings of the people, honour wisdom, that ye may reign for evermore.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> As for wisdom, what she is, and how she came up, I will tell you, and will not hide mysteries from you: but will seek her out from the beginning of her nativity, and bring the knowledge of her into light, and will not pass over the truth.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Neither will I go with consuming envy; for such a man shall have no fellowship with wisdom.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But the multitude of the wise is the welfare of the world: and a wise king is the upholding of the people.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Receive therefore instruction through my words, and it shall do you good.
