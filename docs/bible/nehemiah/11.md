<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And the rulers of the people dwelt at Jerusalem: the rest of the people also cast lots, to bring one of ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts to dwell in other cities.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And the people blessed all the men, that willingly offered themselves to dwell at Jerusalem.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Now these are the chief of the province that dwelt in Jerusalem: but in the cities of Judah dwelt every one in his possession in their cities, to wit, Israel, the priests, and the Levites, and the Nethinims, and the children of Solomon’s servants.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And at Jerusalem dwelt certain of the children of Judah, and of the children of Benjamin. Of the children of Judah; Athaiah the son of Uzziah, the son of Zechariah, the son of Amariah, the son of Shephatiah, the son of Mahalaleel, of the children of Perez;

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And Maaseiah the son of Baruch, the son of Col–hozeh, the son of Hazaiah, the son of Adaiah, the son of Joiarib, the son of Zechariah, the son of Shiloni.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> All the sons of Perez that dwelt at Jerusalem were four hundred threescore and eight valiant men.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And these are the sons of Benjamin; Sallu the son of Meshullam, the son of Joed, the son of Pedaiah, the son of Kolaiah, the son of Maaseiah, the son of Ithiel, the son of Jesaiah.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And after him Gabbai, Sallai, nine hundred twenty and eight.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And Joel the son of Zichri was their overseer: and Judah the son of Senuah was second over the city.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Of the priests: Jedaiah the son of Joiarib, Jachin.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Seraiah the son of Hilkiah, the son of Meshullam, the son of Zadok, the son of Meraioth, the son of Ahitub, was the ruler of the house of God.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And their brethren that did the work of the house were eight hundred twenty and two: and Adaiah the son of Jeroham, the son of Pelaliah, the son of Amzi, the son of Zechariah, the son of Pashur, the son of Malchiah,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And his brethren, chief of the fathers, two hundred forty and two: and Amashai the son of Azareel, the son of Ahasai, the son of Meshillemoth, the son of Immer,

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And their brethren, mighty men of valour, an hundred twenty and eight: and their overseer was Zabdiel, the son of one of the great men.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Also of the Levites: Shemaiah the son of Hashub, the son of Azrikam, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Bunni;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And Shabbethai and Jozabad, of the chief of the Levites, had the oversight of the outward business of the house of God.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Mattaniah the son of Micha, the son of Zabdi, the son of Asaph, was the principal to begin the thanksgiving in prayer: and Bakbukiah the second among his brethren, and Abda the son of Shammua, the son of Galal, the son of Jeduthun.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> All the Levites in the holy city were two hundred fourscore and four.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Moreover the porters, Akkub, Talmon, and their brethren that kept the gates, were an hundred seventy and two.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And the residue of Israel, of the priests, and the Levites, were in all the cities of Judah, every one in his inheritance.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> But the Nethinims dwelt in Ophel: and Ziha and Gispa were over the Nethinims.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> The overseer also of the Levites at Jerusalem was Uzzi the son of Bani, the son of Hashabiah, the son of Mattaniah, the son of Micha. Of the sons of Asaph, the singers were over the business of the house of God.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For it was the king’s commandment concerning them, that a certain portion should be for the singers, due for every day.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And Pethahiah the son of Meshezabeel, of the children of Zerah the son of Judah, was at the king’s hand in all matters concerning the people.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And for the villages, with their fields, some of the children of Judah dwelt at Kirjath–arba, and in the villages thereof, and at Dibon, and in the villages thereof, and at Jekabzeel, and in the villages thereof,

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And at Jeshua, and at Moladah, and at Beth–phelet,

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And at Hazar–shual, and at Beer–sheba, and in the villages thereof,

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And at Ziklag, and at Mekonah, and in the villages thereof,

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And at En–rimmon, and at Zareah, and at Jarmuth,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Zanoah, Adullam, and in their villages, at Lachish, and the fields thereof, at Azekah, and in the villages thereof. And they dwelt from Beer–sheba unto the valley of Hinnom.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The children also of Benjamin from Geba dwelt at Michmash, and Aija, and Beth–el, and in their villages,

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And at Anathoth, Nob, Ananiah,

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Hazor, Ramah, Gittaim,

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Hadid, Zeboim, Neballat,

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Lod, and Ono, the valley of craftsmen.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> And of the Levites were divisions in Judah, and in Benjamin.
