<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Then Mardocheus said, God hath done these things.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For I remember a dream which I saw concerning these matters, and nothing thereof hath failed.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> A little fountain became a river, and there was light, and the sun, and much water: this river is Esther, whom the king married, and made queen:

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And the two dragons are I and Aman.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And the nations were those that were assembled to destroy the name of the Jews:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And my nation is this Israel, which cried to God, and were saved: for the Lord hath saved his people, and the Lord hath delivered us from all those evils, and God hath wrought signs and great wonders, which have not been done among the Gentiles.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Therefore hath he made two lots, one for the people of God, and another for all the Gentiles.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And these two lots came at the hour, and time, and day of judgment, before God among all nations.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So God remembered his people, and justified his inheritance.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Therefore those days shall be unto them in the month Adar, the fourteenth and fifteenth day of the same month, with an assembly, and joy, and with gladness before God, according to the generations for ever among his people.
