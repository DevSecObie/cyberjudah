<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And upon the third day, when she had ended her prayers, she laid away her mourning garments, and put on her glorious apparel.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And being gloriously adorned, after she had called upon God, who is the beholder and saviour of all things, she took two maids with her:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And upon the one she leaned, as carrying herself daintily;

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And the other followed, bearing up her train.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And she was ruddy through the perfection of her beauty, and her countenance was cheerful and very amiable: but her heart was in anguish for fear.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then having passed through all the doors, she stood before the king, who sat upon his royal throne, and was clothed with all his robes of majesty, all glittering with gold and precious stones; and he was very dreadful.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Then lifting up his countenance that shone with majesty, he looked very fiercely upon her: and the queen fell down, and was pale, and fainted, and bowed herself upon the head of the maid that went before her.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Then God changed the spirit of the king into mildness, who in a fear leaped from his throne, and took her in his arms, till she came to herself again, and comforted her with loving words and said unto her,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Esther, what is the matter? I am thy brother, be of good cheer:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thou shalt not die, though our our commandment be general: come near.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And so be held up his golden sceptre, and laid it upon her neck,

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And embraced her, and said, Speak unto me.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Then said she unto him, I saw thee, my lord, as an angel of God, and my heart was troubled for fear of thy majesty.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For wonderful art thou, lord, and thy countenance is full of grace.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And as she was speaking, she fell down for faintness.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Then the king was troubled, and all his servants comforted her.
