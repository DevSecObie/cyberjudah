<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Paul, a prisoner of Jesus Christ, and Timothy our brother, unto Philemon our dearly beloved, and fellowlabourer,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And to our beloved Apphia, and Archippus our fellowsoldier, and to the church in thy house:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Grace to you, and peace, from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I thank my God, making mention of thee always in my prayers,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Hearing of thy love and faith, which thou hast toward the Lord Jesus, and toward all saints;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> That the communication of thy faith may become effectual by the acknowledging of every good thing which is in you in Christ Jesus.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For we have great joy and consolation in thy love, because the bowels of the saints are refreshed by thee, brother.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Wherefore, though I might be much bold in Christ to enjoin thee that which is convenient,

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Yet for love’s sake I rather beseech thee, being such an one as Paul the aged, and now also a prisoner of Jesus Christ.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I beseech thee for my son Onesimus, whom I have begotten in my bonds:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Which in time past was to thee unprofitable, but now profitable to thee and to me:

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Whom I have sent again: thou therefore receive him, that is, mine own bowels:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Whom I would have retained with me, that in thy stead he might have ministered unto me in the bonds of the gospel:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But without thy mind would I do nothing; that thy benefit should not be as it were of necessity, but willingly.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For perhaps he therefore departed for a season, that thou shouldest receive him for ever;

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Not now as a servant, but above a servant, a brother beloved, specially to me, but how much more unto thee, both in the flesh, and in the Lord?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> If thou count me therefore a partner, receive him as myself.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> If he hath wronged thee, or oweth thee ought, put that on mine account;

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> I Paul have written it with mine own hand, I will repay it: albeit I do not say to thee how thou owest unto me even thine own self besides.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Yea, brother, let me have joy of thee in the Lord: refresh my bowels in the Lord.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Having confidence in thy obedience I wrote unto thee, knowing that thou wilt also do more than I say.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But withal prepare me also a lodging: for I trust that through your prayers I shall be given unto you.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> There salute thee Epaphras, my fellowprisoner in Christ Jesus;

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Marcus, Aristarchus, Demas, Lucas, my fellowlabourers.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. Written from Rome to Philemon, by Onesimus a servant.
