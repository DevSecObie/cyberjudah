<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Son of man, What is the vine tree more than any tree, or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest?

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work? or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. Is it meet for any work?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work: how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; As the vine tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And I will set my face against them; they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I set my face against them.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And I will make the land desolate, because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord God.
