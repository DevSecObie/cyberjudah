<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Now, thou son of man, wilt thou judge, wilt thou judge the bloody city? yea, thou shalt shew her all her abominations.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Then say thou, Thus saith the Lord God, The city sheddeth blood in the midst of it, that her time may come, and maketh idols against herself to defile herself.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Thou art become guilty in thy blood that thou hast shed; and hast defiled thyself in thine idols which thou hast made; and thou hast caused thy days to draw near, and art come even unto thy years: therefore have I made thee a reproach unto the heathen, and a mocking to all countries.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Those that be near, and those that be far from thee, shall mock thee, which art infamous and much vexed.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Behold, the princes of Israel, every one were in thee to their power to shed blood.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In thee have they set light by father and mother: in the midst of thee have they dealt by oppression with the stranger: in thee have they vexed the fatherless and the widow.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Thou hast despised mine holy things, and hast profaned my sabbaths.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> In thee are men that carry tales to shed blood: and in thee they eat upon the mountains: in the midst of thee they commit lewdness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> In thee have they discovered their fathers’ nakedness: in thee have they humbled her that was set apart for pollution.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And one hath committed abomination with his neighbour’s wife; and another hath lewdly defiled his daughter in law; and another in thee hath humbled his sister, his father’s daughter.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Behold, therefore I have smitten mine hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Can thine heart endure, or can thine hands be strong, in the days that I shall deal with thee? I the Lord have spoken it, and will do it.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And I will scatter thee among the heathen, and disperse thee in the countries, and will consume thy filthiness out of thee.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And thou shalt take thine inheritance in thyself in the sight of the heathen, and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Son of man, the house of Israel is to me become dross: all they are brass, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of the furnace; they are even the dross of silver.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because ye are all become dross, behold, therefore I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> As they gather silver, and brass, and iron, and lead, and tin, into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, to melt it; so will I gather you in mine anger and in my fury, and I will leave you there, and melt you.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Yea, I will gather you, and blow upon you in the fire of my wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst thereof.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I the Lord have poured out my fury upon you.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Son of man, say unto her, Thou art the land that is not cleansed, nor rained upon in the day of indignation.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> There is a conspiracy of her prophets in the midst thereof, like a roaring lion ravening the prey; they have devoured souls; they have taken the treasure and precious things; they have made her many widows in the midst thereof.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Her priests have violated my law, and have profaned mine holy things: they have put no difference between the holy and profane, neither have they shewed difference between the unclean and the clean, and have hid their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Her princes in the midst thereof are like wolves ravening the prey, to shed blood, and to destroy souls, to get dishonest gain.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And her prophets have daubed them with untempered morter, seeing vanity, and divining lies unto them, saying, Thus saith the Lord God, when the Lord hath not spoken.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> The people of the land have used oppression, and exercised robbery, and have vexed the poor and needy: yea, they have oppressed the stranger wrongfully.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Therefore have I poured out mine indignation upon them; I have consumed them with the fire of my wrath: their own way have I recompensed upon their heads, saith the Lord God.
