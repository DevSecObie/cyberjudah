<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> My brethren, have not the faith of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Lord of glory, with respect of persons.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For if there come unto your assembly a man with a gold ring, in goodly apparel, and there come in also a poor man in vile raiment;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And ye have respect to him that weareth the gay clothing, and say unto him, Sit thou here in a good place; and say to the poor, Stand thou there, or sit here under my footstool:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Are ye not then partial in yourselves, and are become judges of evil thoughts?

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Hearken, my beloved brethren, Hath not God chosen the poor of this world rich in faith, and heirs of the kingdom which he hath promised to them that love him?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But ye have despised the poor. Do not rich men oppress you, and draw you before the judgment seats?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Do not they blaspheme that worthy name by the which ye are called?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> If ye fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, ye do well:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But if ye have respect to persons, ye commit sin, and are convinced of the law as transgressors.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For whosoever shall keep the whole law, and yet offend in one point, he is guilty of all.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For he that said, Do not commit adultery, said also, Do not kill. Now if thou commit no adultery, yet if thou kill, thou art become a transgressor of the law.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> So speak ye, and so do, as they that shall be judged by the law of liberty.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For he shall have judgment without mercy, that hath shewed no mercy; and mercy rejoiceth against judgment.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> What doth it profit, my brethren, though a man say he hath faith, and have not works? can faith save him?

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> If a brother or sister be naked, and destitute of daily food,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And one of you say unto them, Depart in peace, be ye warmed and filled; notwithstanding ye give them not those things which are needful to the body; what doth it profit?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Even so faith, if it hath not works, is dead, being alone.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Yea, a man may say, Thou hast faith, and I have works: shew me thy faith without thy works, and I will shew thee my faith by my works.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thou believest that there is one God; thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Was not Abraham our father justified by works, when he had offered Isaac his son upon the altar?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Seest thou how faith wrought with his works, and by works was faith made perfect?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And the scripture was fulfilled which saith, Abraham believed God, and it was imputed unto him for righteousness: and he was called the Friend of God.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Ye see then how that by works a man is justified, and not by faith only.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Likewise also was not Rahab the harlot justified by works, when she had received the messengers, and had sent them out another way?

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.
