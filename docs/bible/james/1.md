<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> My brethren, count it all joy when ye fall into divers temptations;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Knowing this, that the trying of your faith worketh patience.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But let patience have her perfect work, that ye may be perfect and entire, wanting nothing.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> But let him ask in faith, nothing wavering. For he that wavereth is like a wave of the sea driven with the wind and tossed.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> For let not that man think that he shall receive any thing of the Lord.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> A double minded man is unstable in all his ways.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Let the brother of low degree rejoice in that he is exalted:

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But the rich, in that he is made low: because as the flower of the grass he shall pass away.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the sun is no sooner risen with a burning heat, but it withereth the grass, and the flower thereof falleth, and the grace of the fashion of it perisheth: so also shall the rich man fade away in his ways.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Blessed is the man that endureth temptation: for when he is tried, he shall receive the crown of life, which the Lord hath promised to them that love him.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Let no man say when he is tempted, I am tempted of God: for God cannot be tempted with evil, neither tempteth he any man:

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But every man is tempted, when he is drawn away of his own lust, and enticed.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Then when lust hath conceived, it bringeth forth sin: and sin, when it is finished, bringeth forth death.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Do not err, my beloved brethren.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights, with whom is no variableness, neither shadow of turning.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Of his own will begat he us with the word of truth, that we should be a kind of firstfruits of his creatures.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Wherefore lay apart all filthiness and superfluity of naughtiness, and receive with meekness the engrafted word, which is able to save your souls.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For if any be a hearer of the word, and not a doer, he is like unto a man beholding his natural face in a glass:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For he beholdeth himself, and goeth his way, and straightway forgetteth what manner of man he was.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> But whoso looketh into the perfect law of liberty, and continueth therein, he being not a forgetful hearer, but a doer of the work, this man shall be blessed in his deed.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> If any man among you seem to be religious, and bridleth not his tongue, but deceiveth his own heart, this man’s religion is vain.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Pure religion and undefiled before God and the Father is this, To visit the fatherless and widows in their affliction, and to keep himself unspotted from the world.
