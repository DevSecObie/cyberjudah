<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For every man shall bear his own burden.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Let him that is taught in the word communicate unto him that teacheth in all good things.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For he that soweth to his flesh shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that soweth to the Spirit shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And let us not be weary in well doing: for in due season we shall reap, if we faint not.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> As we have therefore opportunity, let us do good unto all men, especially unto them who are of the household of faith.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Ye see how large a letter I have written unto you with mine own hand.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> As many as desire to make a fair shew in the flesh, they constrain you to be circumcised; only lest they should suffer persecution for the cross of Christ.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For neither they themselves who are circumcised keep the law; but desire to have you circumcised, that they may glory in your flesh.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> But God forbid that I should glory, save in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ, by whom the world is crucified unto me, and I unto the world.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision, but a new creature.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And as many as walk according to this rule, peace be on them, and mercy, and upon the Israel of God.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> From henceforth let no man trouble me: for I bear in my body the marks of the Lord Jesus.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Brethren, the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. Unto the Galatians written from Rome.
