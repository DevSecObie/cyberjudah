<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For we through the Spirit wait for the hope of righteousness by faith.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> For in Jesus Christ neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision; but faith which worketh by love.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Ye did run well; who did hinder you that ye should not obey the truth?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> This persuasion cometh not of him that calleth you.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> A little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> I have confidence in you through the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded: but he that troubleth you shall bear his judgment, whosoever he be.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? then is the offence of the cross ceased.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> I would they were even cut off which trouble you.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> For, brethren, ye have been called unto liberty; only use not liberty for an occasion to the flesh, but by love serve one another.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this; Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But if ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one of another.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> But if ye be led of the Spirit, ye are not under the law.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And they that are Christ’s have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Let us not be desirous of vain glory, provoking one another, envying one another.
