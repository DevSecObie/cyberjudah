<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Even so we, when we were children, were in bondage under the elements of the world:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Howbeit then, when ye knew not God, ye did service unto them which by nature are no gods.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But now, after that ye have known God, or rather are known of God, how turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements, whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> I am afraid of you, lest I have bestowed upon you labour in vain.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Brethren, I beseech you, be as I am; for I am as ye are: ye have not injured me at all.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Ye know how through infirmity of the flesh I preached the gospel unto you at the first.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And my temptation which was in my flesh ye despised not, nor rejected; but received me as an angel of God, even as Christ Jesus.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Where is then the blessedness ye spake of? for I bear you record, that, if it had been possible, ye would have plucked out your own eyes, and have given them to me.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Am I therefore become your enemy, because I tell you the truth?

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> They zealously affect you, but not well; yea, they would exclude you, that ye might affect them.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> But it is good to be zealously affected always in a good thing, and not only when I am present with you.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> My little children, of whom I travail in birth again until Christ be formed in you,

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> I desire to be present with you now, and to change my voice; for I stand in doubt of you.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law?

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> But he who was of the bondwoman was born after the flesh; but he of the freewoman was by promise.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Which things are an allegory: for these are the two covenants; the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For this Agar is mount Sinai in Arabia, and answereth to Jerusalem which now is, and is in bondage with her children.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> But Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For it is written, Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not; break forth and cry, thou that travailest not: for the desolate hath many more children than she which hath an husband.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> But as then he that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit, even so it is now.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Nevertheless what saith the scripture? Cast out the bondwoman and her son: for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the freewoman.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> So then, brethren, we are not children of the bondwoman, but of the free.
