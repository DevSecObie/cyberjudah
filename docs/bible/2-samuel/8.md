<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And after this it came to pass, that David smote the Philistines, and subdued them: and David took Metheg–ammah out of the hand of the Philistines.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And he smote Moab, and measured them with a line, casting them down to the ground; even with two lines measured he to put to death, and with one full line to keep alive. And so the Moabites became David’s servants, and brought gifts.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> David smote also Hadadezer, the son of Rehob, king of Zobah, as he went to recover his border at the river Euphrates.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And David took from him a thousand chariots, and seven hundred horsemen, and twenty thousand footmen: and David houghed all the chariot horses, but reserved of them for an hundred chariots.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And when the Syrians of Damascus came to succour Hadadezer king of Zobah, David slew of the Syrians two and twenty thousand men.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Then David put garrisons in Syria of Damascus: and the Syrians became servants to David, and brought gifts. And the Lord preserved David whithersoever he went.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And David took the shields of gold that were on the servants of Hadadezer, and brought them to Jerusalem.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And from Betah, and from Berothai, cities of Hadadezer, king David took exceeding much brass.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> When Toi king of Hamath heard that David had smitten all the host of Hadadezer,

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Then Toi sent Joram his son unto king David, to salute him, and to bless him, because he had fought against Hadadezer, and smitten him: for Hadadezer had wars with Toi. And Joram brought with him vessels of silver, and vessels of gold, and vessels of brass:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Which also king David did dedicate unto the Lord, with the silver and gold that he had dedicated of all nations which he subdued;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Of Syria, and of Moab, and of the children of Ammon, and of the Philistines, and of Amalek, and of the spoil of Hadadezer, son of Rehob, king of Zobah.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And David gat him a name when he returned from smiting of the Syrians in the valley of salt, being eighteen thousand men.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And he put garrisons in Edom; throughout all Edom put he garrisons, and all they of Edom became David’s servants. And the Lord preserved David whithersoever he went.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And David reigned over all Israel; and David executed judgment and justice unto all his people.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And Joab the son of Zeruiah was over the host; and Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud was recorder;

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And Zadok the son of Ahitub, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, were the priests; and Seraiah was the scribe;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over both the Cherethites and the Pelethites; and David’s sons were chief rulers.
