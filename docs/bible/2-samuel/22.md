<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And David spake unto the Lord the words of this song in the day that the Lord had delivered him out of the hand of all his enemies, and out of the hand of Saul:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And he said, The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer;

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> The God of my rock; in him will I trust: he is my shield, and the horn of my salvation, my high tower, and my refuge, my saviour; thou savest me from violence.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> I will call on the Lord, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> When the waves of death compassed me, the floods of ungodly men made me afraid;

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> The sorrows of hell compassed me about; the snares of death prevented me;

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried to my God: and he did hear my voice out of his temple, and my cry did enter into his ears.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations of heaven moved and shook, because he was wroth.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He bowed the heavens also, and came down; and darkness was under his feet.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly: and he was seen upon the wings of the wind.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And he made darkness pavilions round about him, dark waters, and thick clouds of the skies.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Through the brightness before him were coals of fire kindled.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The Lord thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And the channels of the sea appeared, the foundations of the world were discovered, at the rebuking of the Lord, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> He sent from above, he took me; he drew me out of many waters;

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them that hated me: for they were too strong for me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the Lord was my stay.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> He brought me forth also into a large place: he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness: according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> For all his judgments were before me: and as for his statutes, I did not depart from them.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> I was also upright before him, and have kept myself from mine iniquity.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Therefore the Lord hath recompensed me according to my righteousness; according to my cleanness in his eye sight.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful, and with the upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> With the pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt shew thyself unsavoury.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And the afflicted people thou wilt save: but thine eyes are upon the haughty, that thou mayest bring them down.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For thou art my lamp, O Lord: and the Lord will lighten my darkness.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> For by thee I have run through a troop: by my God have I leaped over a wall.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> As for God, his way is perfect; the word of the Lord is tried: he is a buckler to all them that trust in him.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> For who is God, save the Lord? and who is a rock, save our God?

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> God is my strength and power: and he maketh my way perfect.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> He maketh my feet like hinds’ feet: and setteth me upon my high places.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> He teacheth my hands to war; so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy gentleness hath made me great.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me; so that my feet did not slip.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> I have pursued mine enemies, and destroyed them; and turned not again until I had consumed them.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> And I have consumed them, and wounded them, that they could not arise: yea, they are fallen under my feet.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> For thou hast girded me with strength to battle: them that rose up against me hast thou subdued under me.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies, that I might destroy them that hate me.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> They looked, but there was none to save; even unto the Lord, but he answered them not.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Then did I beat them as small as the dust of the earth, I did stamp them as the mire of the street, and did spread them abroad.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> Thou also hast delivered me from the strivings of my people, thou hast kept me to be head of the heathen: a people which I knew not shall serve me.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> Strangers shall submit themselves unto me: as soon as they hear, they shall be obedient unto me.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> Strangers shall fade away, and they shall be afraid out of their close places.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> The Lord liveth; and blessed be my rock; and exalted be the God of the rock of my salvation.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> It is God that avengeth me, and that bringeth down the people under me,

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> And that bringeth me forth from mine enemies: thou also hast lifted me up on high above them that rose up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> Therefore I will give thanks unto thee, O Lord, among the heathen, and I will sing praises unto thy name.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> He is the tower of salvation for his king: and sheweth mercy to his anointed, unto David, and to his seed for evermore.
