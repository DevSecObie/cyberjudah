<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And it came to pass, when the king sat in his house, and the Lord had given him rest round about from all his enemies;

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> That the king said unto Nathan the prophet, See now, I dwell in an house of cedar, but the ark of God dwelleth within curtains.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And Nathan said to the king, Go, do all that is in thine heart; for the Lord is with thee.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And it came to pass that night, that the word of the Lord came unto Nathan, saying,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Go and tell my servant David, Thus saith the Lord, Shalt thou build me an house for me to dwell in?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Whereas I have not dwelt in any house since the time that I brought up the children of Israel out of Egypt, even to this day, but have walked in a tent and in a tabernacle.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> In all the places wherein I have walked with all the children of Israel spake I a word with any of the tribes of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people Israel, saying, Why build ye not me an house of cedar?

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Now therefore so shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, from following the sheep, to be ruler over my people, over Israel:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And I was with thee whithersoever thou wentest, and have cut off all thine enemies out of thy sight, and have made thee a great name, like unto the name of the great men that are in the earth.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Moreover I will appoint a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, that they may dwell in a place of their own, and move no more; neither shall the children of wickedness afflict them any more, as beforetime,

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And as since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel, and have caused thee to rest from all thine enemies. Also the Lord telleth thee that he will make thee an house.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He shall build an house for my name, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I will be his father, and he shall be my son. If he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> But my mercy shall not depart away from him, as I took it from Saul, whom I put away before thee.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And thine house and thy kingdom shall be established for ever before thee: thy throne shall be established for ever.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Then went king David in, and sat before the Lord, and he said, Who am I, O Lord God? and what is my house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And this was yet a small thing in thy sight, O Lord God; but thou hast spoken also of thy servant’s house for a great while to come. And is this the manner of man, O Lord God?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And what can David say more unto thee? for thou, Lord God, knowest thy servant.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For thy word’s sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all these great things, to make thy servant know them.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Wherefore thou art great, O Lord God: for there is none like thee, neither is there any God beside thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And what one nation in the earth is like thy people, even like Israel, whom God went to redeem for a people to himself, and to make him a name, and to do for you great things and terrible, for thy land, before thy people, which thou redeemedst to thee from Egypt, from the nations and their gods?

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> For thou hast confirmed to thyself thy people Israel to be a people unto thee for ever: and thou, Lord, art become their God.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And now, O Lord God, the word that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant, and concerning his house, establish it for ever, and do as thou hast said.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And let thy name be magnified for ever, saying, The Lord of hosts is the God over Israel: and let the house of thy servant David be established before thee.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For thou, O Lord of hosts, God of Israel, hast revealed to thy servant, saying, I will build thee an house: therefore hath thy servant found in his heart to pray this prayer unto thee.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And now, O Lord God, thou art that God, and thy words be true, and thou hast promised this goodness unto thy servant:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Therefore now let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may continue for ever before thee: for thou, O Lord God, hast spoken it: and with thy blessing let the house of thy servant be blessed for ever.
