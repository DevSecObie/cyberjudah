<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In the thirtieth year after the ruin of the city I was in Babylon, and lay troubled upon my bed, and my thoughts came up over my heart:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> For I saw the desolation of Sion, and the wealth of them that dwelt at Babylon.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And my spirit was sore moved, so that I began to speak words full of fear to the most High, and said,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> O Lord, who bearest rule, thou spakest at the beginning, when thou didst plant the earth, and that thyself alone, and commandedst the people,

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And gavest a body unto Adam without soul, which was the workmanship of thine hands, and didst breathe into him the breath of life, and he was made living before thee.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And thou leadest him into paradise, which thy right hand had planted, before ever the earth came forward.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And unto him thou gavest commandment to love thy way: which he transgressed, and immediately thou appointedst death in him and in his generations, of whom came nations, tribes, people, and kindreds, out of number.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And every people walked after their own will, and did wonderful things before thee, and despised thy commandments.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And again in process of time thou broughtest the flood upon those that dwelt in the world, and destroyedst them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And it came to pass in every of them, that as death was to Adam, so was the flood to these.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Nevertheless one of them thou leftest, namely, Noah with his household, of whom came all righteous men.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And it happened, that when they that dwelt upon the earth began to multiply, and had gotten them many children, and were a great people, they began again to be more ungodly than the first.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Now when they lived so wickedly before thee, thou didst choose thee a man from among them, whose name was Abraham.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Him thou lovedst, and unto him only thou shewedst thy will:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And madest an everlasting covenant with him, promising him that thou wouldest never forsake his seed.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And unto him thou gavest Isaac, and unto Isaac also thou gavest Jacob and Esau. As for Jacob, thou didst choose him to thee, and put by Esau: and so Jacob became a great multitude.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And it came to pass, that when thou leadest his seed out of Egypt, thou broughtest them up to the mount Sinai.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And bowing the heavens, thou didst set fast the earth, movedst the whole world, and madest the depths to tremble, and troubledst the men of that age.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And thy glory went through four gates, of fire, and of earthquake, and of wind, and of cold; that thou mightest give the law unto the seed of Jacob, and diligence unto the generation of Israel.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And yet tookest thou not away from them a wicked heart, that thy law might bring forth fruit in them.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For the first Adam bearing a wicked heart transgressed, and was overcome; and so be all they that are born of him.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Thus infirmity was made permanent; and the law (also) in the heart of the people with the malignity of the root; so that the good departed away, and the evil abode still.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> So the times passed away, and the years were brought to an end: then didst thou raise thee up a servant, called David:

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Whom thou commandedst to build a city unto thy name, and to offer incense and oblations unto thee therein.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> When this was done many years, then they that inhabited the city forsook thee,

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And in all things did even as Adam and all his generations had done: for they also had a wicked heart:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And so thou gavest thy city over into the hands of thine enemies.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Are their deeds then any better that inhabit Babylon, that they should therefore have the dominion over Sion?

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For when I came thither, and had seen impieties without number, then my soul saw many evildoers in this thirtieth year, so that my heart failed me.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> For I have seen how thou sufferest them sinning, and hast spared wicked doers: and hast destroyed thy people, and hast preserved thine enemies, and hast not signified it.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> I do not remember how this way may be left: Are they then of Babylon better than they of Sion?

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Or is there any other people that knoweth thee beside Israel? or what generation hath so believed thy covenants as Jacob?

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And yet their reward appeareth not, and their labour hath no fruit: for I have gone here and there through the heathen, and I see that they flow in wealth, and think not upon thy commandments.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Weigh thou therefore our wickedness now in the balance, and their’s also that dwell the world; and so shall thy name no where be found but in Israel.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Or when was it that they which dwell upon the earth have not sinned in thy sight? or what people have so kept thy commandments?

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Thou shalt find that Israel by name hath kept thy precepts; but not the heathen.
