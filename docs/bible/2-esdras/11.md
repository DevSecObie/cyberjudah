<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then saw I a dream, and, behold, there came up from the sea an eagle, which had twelve feathered wings, and three heads.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And I saw, and, behold, she spread her wings over all the earth, and all the winds of the air blew on her, and were gathered together.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And I beheld, and out of her feathers there grew other contrary feathers; and they became little feathers and small.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But her heads were at rest: the head in the midst was greater than the other, yet rested it with the residue.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Moreover I beheld, and, lo, the eagle flew with her feathers, and reigned upon earth, and over them that dwelt therein.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And I saw that all things under heaven were subject unto her, and no man spake against her, no, not one creature upon earth.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And I beheld, and, lo, the eagle rose upon her talons, and spake to her feathers, saying,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Watch not all at once: sleep every one in his own place, and watch by course:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But let the heads be preserved for the last.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And I beheld, and, lo, the voice went not out of her heads, but from the midst of her body.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And I numbered her contrary feathers, and, behold, there were eight of them.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And I looked, and, behold, on the right side there arose one feather, and reigned over all the earth;

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And so it was, that when it reigned, the end of it came, and the place thereof appeared no more: so the next following stood up. and reigned, and had a great time;

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And it happened, that when it reigned, the end of it came also, like as the first, so that it appeared no more.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Then came there a voice unto it, and said,

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Hear thou that hast borne rule over the earth so long: this I say unto thee, before thou beginnest to appear no more,

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> There shall none after thee attain unto thy time, neither unto the half thereof.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Then arose the third, and reigned as the other before, and appeared no more also.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> So went it with all the residue one after another, as that every one reigned, and then appeared no more.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Then I beheld, and, lo, in process of time the feathers that followed stood up upon the right side, that they might rule also; and some of them ruled, but within a while they appeared no more:

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For some of them were set up, but ruled not.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> After this I looked, and, behold, the twelve feathers appeared no more, nor the two little feathers:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> And there was no more upon the eagle’s body, but three heads that rested, and six little wings.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Then saw I also that two little feathers divided themselves from the six, and remained under the head that was upon the right side: for the four continued in their place.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And I beheld, and, lo, the feathers that were under the wing thought to set up themselves and to have the rule.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And I beheld, and, lo, there was one set up, but shortly it appeared no more.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> And the second was sooner away than the first.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And I beheld, and, lo, the two that remained thought also in themselves to reign:

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And when they so thought, behold, there awaked one of the heads that were at rest, namely, it that was in the midst; for that was greater than the two other heads.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And then I saw that the two other heads were joined with it.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And, behold, the head was turned with them that were with it, and did eat up the two feathers under the wing that would have reigned.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> But this head put the whole earth in fear, and bare rule in it over all those that dwelt upon the earth with much oppression; and it had the governance of the world more than all the wings that had been.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And after this I beheld, and, lo, the head that was in the midst suddenly appeared no more, like as the wings.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> But there remained the two heads, which also in like sort ruled upon the earth, and over those that dwelt therein.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> And I beheld, and, lo, the head upon the right side devoured it that was upon the left side.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Then I head a voice, which said unto me, Look before thee, and consider the thing that thou seest.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> And I beheld, and lo, as it were a roaring lion chased out of the wood: and I saw that he sent out a man’s voice unto the eagle, and said,

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Hear thou, I will talk with thee, and the Highest shall say unto thee,

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Art not thou it that remainest of the four beasts, whom I made to reign in my world, that the end of their times might come through them?

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And the fourth came, and overcame all the beasts that were past, and had power over the world with great fearfulness, and over the whole compass of the earth with much wicked oppression; and so long time dwelt he upon the earth with deceit.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> For the earth hast thou not judged with truth.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> For thou hast afflicted the meek, thou hast hurt the peaceable, thou hast loved liars, and destroyed the dwellings of them that brought forth fruit, and hast cast down the walls of such as did thee no harm.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Therefore is thy wrongful dealing come up unto the Highest, and thy pride unto the Mighty.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> The Highest also hath looked upon the proud times, and, behold, they are ended, and his abominations are fulfilled.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> And therefore appear no more, thou eagle, nor thy horrible wings, nor thy wicked feathers nor thy malicious heads, nor thy hurtful claws, nor all thy vain body:

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> That all the earth may be refreshed, and may return, being delivered from thy violence, and that she may hope for the judgment and mercy of him that made her.
