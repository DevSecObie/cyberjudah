<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Thus saith the Lord, I brought this people out of bondage, and I gave them my commandments by menservants the prophets; whom they would not hear, but despised my counsels.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> The mother that bare them saith unto them, Go your way, ye children; for I am a widow and forsaken.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> I brought you up with gladness; but with sorrow and heaviness have I lost you: for ye have sinned before the Lord your God, and done that thing that is evil before him.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But what shall I now do unto you? I am a widow and forsaken: go your way, O my children, and ask mercy of the Lord.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> As for me, O father, I call upon thee for a witness over the mother of these children, which would not keep my covenant,

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> That thou bring them to confusion, and their mother to a spoil, that there may be no offspring of them.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Let them be scattered abroad among the heathen, let their names be put out of the earth: for they have despised my covenant.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Woe be unto thee, Assur, thou that hidest the unrighteous in thee! O thou wicked people, remember what I did unto Sodom and Gomorrha;

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Whose land lieth in clods of pitch and heaps of ashes: even so also will I do unto them that hear me not, saith the Almighty Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Thus saith the Lord unto Esdras, Tell my people that I will give them the kingdom of Jerusalem, which I would have given unto Israel.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Their glory also will I take unto me, and give these the everlasting tabernacles, which I had prepared for them.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They shall have the tree of life for an ointment of sweet savour; they shall neither labour, nor be weary.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Go, and ye shall receive: pray for few days unto you, that they may be shortened: the kingdom is already prepared for you: watch.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Take heaven and earth to witness; for I have broken the evil in pieces, and created the good: for I live, saith the Lord.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Mother, embrace thy children, and bring them up with gladness, make their feet as fast as a pillar: for I have chosen thee, saith the Lord.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And those that be dead will I raise up again from their places, and bring them out of the graves: for I have known my name in Israel.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Fear not, thou mother of the children: for I have chosen thee, saith the Lord.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For thy help will I send my servants Esau and Jeremy, after whose counsel I have sanctified and prepared for thee twelve trees laden with divers fruits,

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And as many fountains flowing with milk and honey, and seven mighty mountains, whereupon there grow roses and lilies, whereby I will fill thy children with joy.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Do right to the widow, judge for the fatherless, give to the poor, defend the orphan, clothe the naked,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Heal the broken and the weak, laugh not a lame man to scorn, defend the maimed, and let the blind man come into the sight of my clearness.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Keep the old and young within thy walls.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Wheresoever thou findest the dead, take them and bury them, and I will give thee the first place in my resurrection.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Abide still, O my people, and take thy rest, for thy quietness still come.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Nourish thy children, O thou good nurse; stablish their feet.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> As for the servants whom I have given thee, there shall not one of them perish; for I will require them from among thy number.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Be not weary: for when the day of trouble and heaviness cometh, others shall weep and be sorrowful, but thou shalt be merry and have abundance.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> The heathen shall envy thee, but they shall be able to do nothing against thee, saith the Lord.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> My hands shall cover thee, so that thy children shall not see hell.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> Be joyful, O thou mother, with thy children; for I will deliver thee, saith the Lord.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Remember thy children that sleep, for I shall bring them out of the sides of the earth, and shew mercy unto them: for I am merciful, saith the Lord Almighty.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Embrace thy children until I come and shew mercy unto them: for my wells run over, and my grace shall not fail.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> I Esdras received a charge of the Lord upon the mount Oreb, that I should go unto Israel; but when I came unto them, they set me at nought, and despised the commandment of the Lord.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And therefore I say unto you, O ye heathen, that hear and understand, look for your Shepherd, he shall give you everlasting rest; for he is nigh at hand, that shall come in the end of the world.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> Be ready to the reward of the kingdom, for the everlasting light shall shine upon you for evermore.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Flee the shadow of this world, receive the joyfulness of your glory: I testify my Saviour openly.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> O receive the gift that is given you, and be glad, giving thanks unto him that hath led you to the heavenly kingdom.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Arise up and stand, behold the number of those that be sealed in the feast of the Lord;

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Which are departed from the shadow of the world, and have received glorious garments of the Lord.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Take thy number, O Sion, and shut up those of thine that are clothed in white, which have fulfilled the law of the Lord.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> The number of thy children, whom thou longedst for, is fulfilled: beseech the power of the Lord, that thy people, which have been called from the beginning, may be hallowed.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> I Esdras saw upon the mount Sion a great people, whom I could not number, and they all praised the Lord with songs.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> And in the midst of them there was a young man of a high stature, taller than all the rest, and upon every one of their heads he set crowns, and was more exalted; which I marvelled at greatly.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> So I asked the angel, and said, Sir, what are these?

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> He answered and said unto me, These be they that have put off the mortal clothing, and put on the immortal, and have confessed the name of God: now are they crowned, and receive palms.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> Then said I unto the angel, What young person is it that crowneth them, and giveth them palms in their hands?

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> So he answered and said unto me, It is the Son of God, whom they have confessed in the world. Then began I greatly to commend them that stood so stiffly for the name of the Lord.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> Then the angel said unto me, Go thy way, and tell my people what manner of things, and how great wonders of the Lord thy God, thou hast seen.
