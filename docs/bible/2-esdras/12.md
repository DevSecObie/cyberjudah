<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> And it came to pass, whiles the lion spake these words unto the eagle, I saw,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And, behold, the head that remained and the four wings appeared no more, and the two went unto it and set themselves up to reign, and their kingdom was small, and fill of uproar.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And I saw, and, behold, they appeared no more, and the whole body of the eagle was burnt so that the earth was in great fear: then awaked I out of the trouble and trance of my mind, and from great fear, and said unto my spirit,

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Lo, this hast thou done unto me, in that thou searchest out the ways of the Highest.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Lo, yet am I weary in my mind, and very weak in my spirit; and little strength is there in me, for the great fear wherewith I was afflicted this night.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Therefore will I now beseech the Highest, that he will comfort me unto the end.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And I said, Lord that bearest rule, if I have found grace before thy sight, and if I am justified with thee before many others, and if my prayer indeed be come up before thy face;

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Comfort me then, and shew me thy servant the interpretation and plain difference of this fearful vision, that thou mayest perfectly comfort my soul.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> For thou hast judged me worthy to shew me the last times.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And he said unto me, This is the interpretation of the vision:

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The eagle, whom thou sawest come up from the sea, is the kingdom which was seen in the vision of thy brother Daniel.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But it was not expounded unto him, therefore now I declare it unto thee.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Behold, the days will come, that there shall rise up a kingdom upon earth, and it shall be feared above all the kingdoms that were before it.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> In the same shall twelve kings reign, one after another:

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Whereof the second shall begin to reign, and shall have more time than any of the twelve.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> And this do the twelve wings signify, which thou sawest.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> As for the voice which thou heardest speak, and that thou sawest not to go out from the heads but from the midst of the body thereof, this is the interpretation:

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> That after the time of that kingdom there shall arise great strivings, and it shall stand in peril of failing: nevertheless it shall not then fall, but shall be restored again to his beginning.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And whereas thou sawest the eight small under feathers sticking to her wings, this is the interpretation:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> That in him there shall arise eight kings, whose times shall be but small, and their years swift.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And two of them shall perish, the middle time approaching: four shall be kept until their end begin to approach: but two shall be kept unto the end.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And whereas thou sawest three heads resting, this is the interpretation:

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> In his last days shall the most High raise up three kingdoms, and renew many things therein, and they shall have the dominion of the earth,

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> And of those that dwell therein, with much oppression, above all those that were before them: therefore are they called the heads of the eagle.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> For these are they that shall accomplish his wickedness, and that shall finish his last end.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> And whereas thou sawest that the great head appeared no more, it signifieth that one of them shall die upon his bed, and yet with pain.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> For the two that remain shall be slain with the sword.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> For the sword of the one shall devour the other: but at the last shall he fall through the sword himself.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> And whereas thou sawest two feathers under the wings passing over the head that is on the right side;

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> It signifieth that these are they, whom the Highest hath kept unto their end: this is the small kingdom and full of trouble, as thou sawest.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> And the lion, whom thou sawest rising up out of the wood, and roaring, and speaking to the eagle, and rebuking her for her unrighteousness with all the words which thou hast heard;

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> This is the anointed, which the Highest hath kept for them and for their wickedness unto the end: he shall reprove them, and shall upbraid them with their cruelty.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> For he shall set them before him alive in judgment, and shall rebuke them, and correct them.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> For the rest of my people shall he deliver with mercy, those that have been pressed upon my borders, and he shall make them joyful until the coming of the day of judgment, whereof I have spoken unto thee from the the beginning.

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> This is the dream that thou sawest, and these are the interpretations.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Thou only hast been meet to know this secret of the Highest.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Therefore write all these things that thou hast seen in a book, and hide them:

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And teach them to the wise of the people, whose hearts thou knowest may comprehend and keep these secrets.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> But wait thou here thyself yet seven days more, that it may be shewed thee, whatsoever it pleaseth the Highest to declare unto thee. And with that he went his way.

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And it came to pass, when all the people saw that the seven days were past, and I not come again into the city, they gathered them all together, from the least unto the greatest, and came unto me, and said,

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> What have we offended thee? and what evil have we done against thee, that thou forsakest us, and sittest here in this place?

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> For of all the prophets thou only art left us, as a cluster of the vintage, and as a candle in a dark place, and as a haven or ship preserved from the tempest.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Are not the evils which are come to us sufficient?

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> If thou shalt forsake us, how much better had it been for us, if we also had been burned in the midst of Sion?

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> For we are not better than they that died there. And they wept with a loud voice. Then answered I them, and said,

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> Be of good comfort, O Israel; and be not heavy, thou house of Jacob:

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> For the Highest hath you in remembrance, and the Mighty hath not forgotten you in temptation.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> As for me, I have not forsaken you, neither am I departed from you: but am come into this place, to pray for the desolation of Sion, and that I might seek mercy for the low estate of your sanctuary.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> And now go your way home every man, and after these days will I come unto you.

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> So the people went their way into the city, like as I commanded them:
