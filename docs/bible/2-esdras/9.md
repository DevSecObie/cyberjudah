<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> He answered me then, and said, Measure thou the time diligently in itself: and when thou seest part of the signs past, which I have told thee before,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Then shalt thou understand, that it is the very same time, wherein the Highest will begin to visit the world which he made.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Therefore when there shall be seen earthquakes and uproars of the people in the world:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Then shalt thou well understand, that the most High spake of those things from the days that were before thee, even from the beginning.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For like as all that is made in the world hath a beginning and an end, and the end is manifest:

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Even so the times also of the Highest have plain beginnings in wonder and powerful works, and endings in effects and signs.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, and by faith, whereby ye have believed,

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Shall be preserved from the said perils, and shall see my salvation in my land, and within my borders: for I have sanctified them for me from the beginning.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Then shall they be in pitiful case, which now have abused my ways: and they that have cast them away despitefully shall dwell in torments.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> For such as in their life have received benefits, and have not known me;

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> And they that have loathed my law, while they had yet liberty, and, when as yet place of repentance was open unto them, understood not, but despised it;

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> The same must know it after death by pain.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And therefore be thou not curious how the ungodly shall be punished, and when: but enquire how the righteous shall be saved, whose the world is, and for whom the world is created.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Then answered I and said,

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> I have said before, and now do speak, and will speak it also hereafter, that there be many more of them which perish, than of them which shall be saved:

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Like as a wave is greater than a drop.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And he answered me, saying, Like as the field is, so is also the seed; as the flowers be, such are the colours also; such as the workman is, such also is the work; and as the husbandman is himself, so is his husbandry also: for it was the time of the world.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And now when I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live, no man spake against me.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> For then every one obeyed: but now the manners of them which are created in this world that is made are corrupted by a perpetual seed, and by a law which is unsearchable rid themselves.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> So I considered the world, and, behold, there was peril because of the devices that were come into it.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And I saw, and spared it greatly, and have kept me a grape of the cluster, and a plant of a great people.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Let the multitude perish then, which was born in vain; and let my grape be kept, and my plant; for with great labour have I made it perfect.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Nevertheless, if thou wilt cease yet seven days more, (but thou shalt not fast in them,

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> But go into a field of flowers, where no house is builded, and eat only the flowers of the field; taste no flesh, drink no wine, but eat flowers only;)

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And pray unto the Highest continually, then will I come and talk with thee.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> So I went my way into the field which is called Ardath, like as he commanded me; and there I sat among the flowers, and did eat of the herbs of the field, and the meat of the same satisfied me.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> After seven days I sat upon the grass, and my heart was vexed within me, like as before:

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> And I opened my mouth, and began to talk before the most High, and said,

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> O Lord, thou that shewest thyself unto us, thou wast shewed unto our fathers in the wilderness, in a place where no man treadeth, in a barren place, when they came out of Egypt.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And thou spakest saying, Hear me, O Israel; and mark my words, thou seed of Jacob.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> For, behold, I sow my law in you, and it shall bring fruit in you, and ye shall be honoured in it for ever.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> But our fathers, which received the law, kept it not, and observed not thy ordinances: and though the fruit of thy law did not perish, neither could it, for it was thine;

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Yet they that received it perished, because they kept not the thing that was sown in them.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> And, lo, it is a custom, when the ground hath received seed, or the sea a ship, or any vessel meat or drink, that, that being perished wherein it was sown or cast into,

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> That thing also which was sown, or cast therein, or received, doth perish, and remaineth not with us: but with us it hath not happened so.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> For we that have received the law perish by sin, and our heart also which received it

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Notwithstanding the law perisheth not, but remaineth in his force.

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> And when I spake these things in my heart, I looked back with mine eyes, and upon the right side I saw a woman, and, behold, she mourned and wept with a loud voice, and was much grieved in heart, and her clothes were rent, and she had ashes upon her head.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Then let I my thoughts go that I was in, and turned me unto her,

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And said unto her, Wherefore weepest thou? why art thou so grieved in thy mind?

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> And she said unto me, Sir, let me alone, that I may bewail myself, and add unto my sorrow, for I am sore vexed in my mind, and brought very low.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> And I said unto her, What aileth thee? tell me.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> She said unto me, I thy servant have been barren, and had no child, though I had an husband thirty years,

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> And those thirty years I did nothing else day and night, and every hour, but make my, prayer to the Highest.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> After thirty years God heard me thine handmaid, looked upon my misery, considered my trouble, and gave me a son: and I was very glad of him, so was my husband also, and all my neighbours: and we gave great honour unto the Almighty.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And I nourished him with great travail.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> So when he grew up, and came to the time that he should have a wife, I made a feast.
