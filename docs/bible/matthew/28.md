<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> In the end of the sabbath, as it began to dawn toward the first day of the week, came Mary Magdalene and the other Mary to see the sepulchre.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And, behold, there was a great earthquake: for the angel of the Lord descended from heaven, and came and rolled back the stone from the door, and sat upon it.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> His countenance was like lightning, and his raiment white as snow:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And for fear of him the keepers did shake, and became as dead men.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> And the angel answered and said unto the women, Fear not ye: for I know that ye seek Jesus, which was crucified.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He is not here: for he is risen, as he said. Come, see the place where the Lord lay.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And go quickly, and tell his disciples that he is risen from the dead; and, behold, he goeth before you into Galilee; there shall ye see him: lo, I have told you.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And they departed quickly from the sepulchre with fear and great joy; and did run to bring his disciples word.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And as they went to tell his disciples, behold, Jesus met them, saying, All hail. And they came and held him by the feet, and worshipped him.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Then said Jesus unto them, Be not afraid: go tell my brethren that they go into Galilee, and there shall they see me.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Now when they were going, behold, some of the watch came into the city, and shewed unto the chief priests all the things that were done.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And when they were assembled with the elders, and had taken counsel, they gave large money unto the soldiers,

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Saying, Say ye, His disciples came by night, and stole him away while we slept.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And if this come to the governor’s ears, we will persuade him, and secure you.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> So they took the money, and did as they were taught: and this saying is commonly reported among the Jews until this day.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Then the eleven disciples went away into Galilee, into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And when they saw him, they worshipped him: but some doubted.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.
