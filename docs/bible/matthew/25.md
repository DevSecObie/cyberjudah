<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And five of them were wise, and five were foolish.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> They that were foolish took their lamps, and took no oil with them:

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> And the foolish said unto the wise, Give us of your oil; for our lamps are gone out.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But the wise answered, saying, Not so; lest there be not enough for us and you: but go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> And while they went to buy, the bridegroom came; and they that were ready went in with him to the marriage: and the door was shut.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Afterward came also the other virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> But he answered and said, Verily I say unto you, I know you not.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> For the kingdom of heaven is as a man travelling into a far country, who called his own servants, and delivered unto them his goods.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> And unto one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one; to every man according to his several ability; and straightway took his journey.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Then he that had received the five talents went and traded with the same, and made them other five talents.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And likewise he that had received two, he also gained other two.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> But he that had received one went and digged in the earth, and hid his lord’s money.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> After a long time the lord of those servants cometh, and reckoneth with them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And so he that had received five talents came and brought other five talents, saying, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> He also that had received two talents came and said, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me two talents: behold, I have gained two other talents beside them.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> His lord said unto him, Well done, good and faithful servant; thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed:

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth: lo, there thou hast that is thine.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> His lord answered and said unto him, Thou wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I have not strawed:

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming I should have received mine own with usury.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> For unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance: but from him that hath not shall be taken away even that which he hath.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And cast ye the unprofitable servant into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory:

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> And before him shall be gathered all nations: and he shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats:

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Then shall the King say unto them on his right hand, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in:

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink?

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee?

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink:

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous into life eternal.
