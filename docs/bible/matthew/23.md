<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then spake Jesus to the multitude, and to his disciples,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat:

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> But all their works they do for to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> And love the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues,

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your Master, even Christ; and all ye are brethren.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> And call no man your father upon the earth: for one is your Father, which is in heaven.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Neither be ye called masters: for one is your Master, even Christ.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye devour widows’ houses, and for a pretence make long prayer: therefore ye shall receive the greater damnation.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of hell than yourselves.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Woe unto you, ye blind guides, which say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor!

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold?

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever sweareth by the gift that is upon it, he is guilty.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Whoso therefore shall swear by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all things thereon.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And whoso shall swear by the temple, sweareth by it, and by him that dwelleth therein.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And he that shall swear by heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him that sitteth thereon.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Ye blind guides, which strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye make clean the outside of the cup and of the platter, but within they are full of extortion and excess.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> Thou blind Pharisee, cleanse first that which is within the cup and platter, that the outside of them may be clean also.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> Even so ye also outwardly appear righteous unto men, but within ye are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! because ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous,

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> Wherefore ye be witnesses unto yourselves, that ye are the children of them which killed the prophets.

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> Ye serpents, ye generation of vipers, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> Wherefore, behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes: and some of them ye shall kill and crucify; and some of them shall ye scourge in your synagogues, and persecute them from city to city:

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> That upon you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel unto the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar.

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, thou that killest the prophets, and stonest them which are sent unto thee, how often would I have gathered thy children together, even as a hen gathereth her chickens under her wings, and ye would not!

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> For I say unto you, Ye shall not see me henceforth, till ye shall say, Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord.
