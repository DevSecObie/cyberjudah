<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Remember, O Lord, what is come upon us: consider, and behold our reproach.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Our inheritance is turned to strangers, our houses to aliens.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> We are orphans and fatherless, our mothers are as widows.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> We have drunken our water for money; our wood is sold unto us.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> Our necks are under persecution: we labour, and have no rest.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> We have given the hand to the Egyptians, and to the Assyrians, to be satisfied with bread.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Our fathers have sinned, and are not; and we have borne their iniquities.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Servants have ruled over us: there is none that doth deliver us out of their hand.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> We gat our bread with the peril of our lives because of the sword of the wilderness.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Our skin was black like an oven because of the terrible famine.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> They ravished the women in Zion, and the maids in the cities of Judah.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Princes are hanged up by their hand: the faces of elders were not honoured.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> They took the young men to grind, and the children fell under the wood.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> The elders have ceased from the gate, the young men from their musick.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> The joy of our heart is ceased; our dance is turned into mourning.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The crown is fallen from our head: woe unto us, that we have sinned!

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For this our heart is faint; for these things our eyes are dim.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Because of the mountain of Zion, which is desolate, the foxes walk upon it.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Thou, O Lord, remainest for ever; thy throne from generation to generation.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Wherefore dost thou forget us for ever, and forsake us so long time?

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Turn thou us unto thee, O Lord, and we shall be turned; renew our days as of old.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> But thou hast utterly rejected us; thou art very wroth against us.
