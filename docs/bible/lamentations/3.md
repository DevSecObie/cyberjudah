<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the Lord:

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The Lord is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope.

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach.

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> For the Lord will not cast off for ever:

<a id="v32"></a><sup class="vn">32</sup> But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies.

<a id="v33"></a><sup class="vn">33</sup> For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men.

<a id="v34"></a><sup class="vn">34</sup> To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,

<a id="v35"></a><sup class="vn">35</sup> To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High,

<a id="v36"></a><sup class="vn">36</sup> To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not.

<a id="v37"></a><sup class="vn">37</sup> Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not?

<a id="v38"></a><sup class="vn">38</sup> Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good?

<a id="v39"></a><sup class="vn">39</sup> Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?

<a id="v40"></a><sup class="vn">40</sup> Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the Lord.

<a id="v41"></a><sup class="vn">41</sup> Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens.

<a id="v42"></a><sup class="vn">42</sup> We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned.

<a id="v43"></a><sup class="vn">43</sup> Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.

<a id="v44"></a><sup class="vn">44</sup> Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that our prayer should not pass through.

<a id="v45"></a><sup class="vn">45</sup> Thou hast made us as the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people.

<a id="v46"></a><sup class="vn">46</sup> All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.

<a id="v47"></a><sup class="vn">47</sup> Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction.

<a id="v48"></a><sup class="vn">48</sup> Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water for the destruction of the daughter of my people.

<a id="v49"></a><sup class="vn">49</sup> Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,

<a id="v50"></a><sup class="vn">50</sup> Till the Lord look down, and behold from heaven.

<a id="v51"></a><sup class="vn">51</sup> Mine eye affecteth mine heart because of all the daughters of my city.

<a id="v52"></a><sup class="vn">52</sup> Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause.

<a id="v53"></a><sup class="vn">53</sup> They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon me.

<a id="v54"></a><sup class="vn">54</sup> Waters flowed over mine head; then I said, I am cut off.

<a id="v55"></a><sup class="vn">55</sup> I called upon thy name, O Lord, out of the low dungeon.

<a id="v56"></a><sup class="vn">56</sup> Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry.

<a id="v57"></a><sup class="vn">57</sup> Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not.

<a id="v58"></a><sup class="vn">58</sup> O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life.

<a id="v59"></a><sup class="vn">59</sup> O Lord, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause.

<a id="v60"></a><sup class="vn">60</sup> Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me.

<a id="v61"></a><sup class="vn">61</sup> Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me;

<a id="v62"></a><sup class="vn">62</sup> The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day.

<a id="v63"></a><sup class="vn">63</sup> Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick.

<a id="v64"></a><sup class="vn">64</sup> Render unto them a recompence, O Lord, according to the work of their hands.

<a id="v65"></a><sup class="vn">65</sup> Give them sorrow of heart, thy curse unto them.

<a id="v66"></a><sup class="vn">66</sup> Persecute and destroy them in anger from under the heavens of the Lord.
