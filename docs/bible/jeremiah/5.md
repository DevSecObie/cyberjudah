<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Run ye to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be any that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth; and I will pardon it.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And though they say, The Lord liveth; surely they swear falsely.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> O Lord, are not thine eyes upon the truth? thou hast stricken them, but they have not grieved; thou hast consumed them, but they have refused to receive correction: they have made their faces harder than a rock; they have refused to return.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> Therefore I said, Surely these are poor; they are foolish: for they know not the way of the Lord, nor the judgment of their God.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> I will get me unto the great men, and will speak unto them; for they have known the way of the Lord, and the judgment of their God: but these have altogether broken the yoke, and burst the bonds.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Wherefore a lion out of the forest shall slay them, and a wolf of the evenings shall spoil them, a leopard shall watch over their cities: every one that goeth out thence shall be torn in pieces: because their transgressions are many, and their backslidings are increased.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> How shall I pardon thee for this? thy children have forsaken me, and sworn by them that are no gods: when I had fed them to the full, they then committed adultery, and assembled themselves by troops in the harlots’ houses.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> They were as fed horses in the morning: every one neighed after his neighbour’s wife.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Shall I not visit for these things? saith the Lord: and shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Go ye up upon her walls, and destroy; but make not a full end: take away her battlements; for they are not the Lord’s.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have dealt very treacherously against me, saith the Lord.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> They have belied the Lord, and said, It is not he; neither shall evil come upon us; neither shall we see sword nor famine:

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> And the prophets shall become wind, and the word is not in them: thus shall it be done unto them.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God of hosts, Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make my words in thy mouth fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Lo, I will bring a nation upon you from far, O house of Israel, saith the Lord: it is a mighty nation, it is an ancient nation, a nation whose language thou knowest not, neither understandest what they say.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Their quiver is as an open sepulchre, they are all mighty men.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> And they shall eat up thine harvest, and thy bread, which thy sons and thy daughters should eat: they shall eat up thy flocks and thine herds: they shall eat up thy vines and thy fig trees: they shall impoverish thy fenced cities, wherein thou trustedst, with the sword.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Nevertheless in those days, saith the Lord, I will not make a full end with you.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And it shall come to pass, when ye shall say, Wherefore doeth the Lord our God all these things unto us? then shalt thou answer them, Like as ye have forsaken me, and served strange gods in your land, so shall ye serve strangers in a land that is not yours.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Declare this in the house of Jacob, and publish it in Judah, saying,

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> Hear now this, O foolish people, and without understanding; which have eyes, and see not; which have ears, and hear not:

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Fear ye not me? saith the Lord: will ye not tremble at my presence, which have placed the sand for the bound of the sea by a perpetual decree, that it cannot pass it: and though the waves thereof toss themselves, yet can they not prevail; though they roar, yet can they not pass over it?

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> But this people hath a revolting and a rebellious heart; they are revolted and gone.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> Neither say they in their heart, Let us now fear the Lord our God, that giveth rain, both the former and the latter, in his season: he reserveth unto us the appointed weeks of the harvest.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Your iniquities have turned away these things, and your sins have withholden good things from you.

<a id="v26"></a><sup class="vn">26</sup> For among my people are found wicked men: they lay wait, as he that setteth snares; they set a trap, they catch men.

<a id="v27"></a><sup class="vn">27</sup> As a cage is full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit: therefore they are become great, and waxen rich.

<a id="v28"></a><sup class="vn">28</sup> They are waxen fat, they shine: yea, they overpass the deeds of the wicked: they judge not the cause, the cause of the fatherless, yet they prosper; and the right of the needy do they not judge.

<a id="v29"></a><sup class="vn">29</sup> Shall I not visit for these things? saith the Lord: shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?

<a id="v30"></a><sup class="vn">30</sup> A wonderful and horrible thing is committed in the land;

<a id="v31"></a><sup class="vn">31</sup> The prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests bear rule by their means; and my people love to have it so: and what will ye do in the end thereof?
