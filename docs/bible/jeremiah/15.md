<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people: cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth.

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> And it shall come to pass, if they say unto thee, Whither shall we go forth? then thou shalt tell them, Thus saith the Lord; Such as are for death, to death; and such as are for the sword, to the sword; and such as are for the famine, to the famine; and such as are for the captivity, to the captivity.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> And I will appoint over them four kinds, saith the Lord: the sword to slay, and the dogs to tear, and the fowls of the heaven, and the beasts of the earth, to devour and destroy.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And I will cause them to be removed into all kingdoms of the earth, because of Manasseh the son of Hezekiah king of Judah, for that which he did in Jerusalem.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For who shall have pity upon thee, O Jerusalem? or who shall bemoan thee? or who shall go aside to ask how thou doest?

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Thou hast forsaken me, saith the Lord, thou art gone backward: therefore will I stretch out my hand against thee, and destroy thee; I am weary with repenting.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> And I will fan them with a fan in the gates of the land; I will bereave them of children, I will destroy my people, since they return not from their ways.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> Their widows are increased to me above the sand of the seas: I have brought upon them against the mother of the young men a spoiler at noonday: I have caused him to fall upon it suddenly, and terrors upon the city.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> She that hath borne seven languisheth: she hath given up the ghost; her sun is gone down while it was yet day: she hath been ashamed and confounded: and the residue of them will I deliver to the sword before their enemies, saith the Lord.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Woe is me, my mother, that thou hast borne me a man of strife and a man of contention to the whole earth! I have neither lent on usury, nor men have lent to me on usury; yet every one of them doth curse me.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> The Lord said, Verily it shall be well with thy remnant; verily I will cause the enemy to entreat thee well in the time of evil and in the time of affliction.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> Shall iron break the northern iron and the steel?

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> Thy substance and thy treasures will I give to the spoil without price, and that for all thy sins, even in all thy borders.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> And I will make thee to pass with thine enemies into a land which thou knowest not: for a fire is kindled in mine anger, which shall burn upon you.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> O Lord, thou knowest: remember me, and visit me, and revenge me of my persecutors; take me not away in thy longsuffering: know that for thy sake I have suffered rebuke.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart: for I am called by thy name, O Lord God of hosts.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> I sat not in the assembly of the mockers, nor rejoiced; I sat alone because of thy hand: for thou hast filled me with indignation.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Why is my pain perpetual, and my wound incurable, which refuseth to be healed? wilt thou be altogether unto me as a liar, and as waters that fail?

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord, If thou return, then will I bring thee again, and thou shalt stand before me: and if thou take forth the precious from the vile, thou shalt be as my mouth: let them return unto thee; but return not thou unto them.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> And I will make thee unto this people a fenced brasen wall: and they shall fight against thee, but they shall not prevail against thee: for I am with thee to save thee and to deliver thee, saith the Lord.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And I will deliver thee out of the hand of the wicked, and I will redeem thee out of the hand of the terrible.
