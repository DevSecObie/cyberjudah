<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> Hear ye the word which the Lord speaketh unto you, O house of Israel:

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Learn not the way of the heathen, and be not dismayed at the signs of heaven; for the heathen are dismayed at them.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For the customs of the people are vain: for one cutteth a tree out of the forest, the work of the hands of the workman, with the axe.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> They deck it with silver and with gold; they fasten it with nails and with hammers, that it move not.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> They are upright as the palm tree, but speak not: they must needs be borne, because they cannot go. Be not afraid of them; for they cannot do evil, neither also is it in them to do good.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Forasmuch as there is none like unto thee, O Lord; thou art great, and thy name is great in might.

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Who would not fear thee, O King of nations? for to thee doth it appertain: forasmuch as among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, there is none like unto thee.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> But they are altogether brutish and foolish: the stock is a doctrine of vanities.

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> Silver spread into plates is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz, the work of the workman, and of the hands of the founder: blue and purple is their clothing: they are all the work of cunning men.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> But the Lord is the true God, he is the living God, and an everlasting king: at his wrath the earth shall tremble, and the nations shall not be able to abide his indignation.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> Thus shall ye say unto them, The gods that have not made the heavens and the earth, even they shall perish from the earth, and from under these heavens.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> He hath made the earth by his power, he hath established the world by his wisdom, and hath stretched out the heavens by his discretion.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> When he uttereth his voice, there is a multitude of waters in the heavens, and he causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth; he maketh lightnings with rain, and bringeth forth the wind out of his treasures.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> Every man is brutish in his knowledge: every founder is confounded by the graven image: for his molten image is falsehood, and there is no breath in them.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> They are vanity, and the work of errors: in the time of their visitation they shall perish.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> The portion of Jacob is not like them: for he is the former of all things; and Israel is the rod of his inheritance: The Lord of hosts is his name.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> Gather up thy wares out of the land, O inhabitant of the fortress.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> For thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will sling out the inhabitants of the land at this once, and will distress them, that they may find it so.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> Woe is me for my hurt! my wound is grievous: but I said, Truly this is a grief, and I must bear it.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> My tabernacle is spoiled, and all my cords are broken: my children are gone forth of me, and they are not: there is none to stretch forth my tent any more, and to set up my curtains.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> For the pastors are become brutish, and have not sought the Lord: therefore they shall not prosper, and all their flocks shall be scattered.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> Behold, the noise of the bruit is come, and a great commotion out of the north country, to make the cities of Judah desolate, and a den of dragons.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> O Lord, I know that the way of man is not in himself: it is not in man that walketh to direct his steps.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> O Lord, correct me, but with judgment; not in thine anger, lest thou bring me to nothing.

<a id="v25"></a><sup class="vn">25</sup> Pour out thy fury upon the heathen that know thee not, and upon the families that call not on thy name: for they have eaten up Jacob, and devoured him, and consumed him, and have made his habitation desolate.
