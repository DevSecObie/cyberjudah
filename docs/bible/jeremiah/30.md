<a id="v1"></a><sup class="vn">1</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,

<a id="v2"></a><sup class="vn">2</sup> Thus speaketh the Lord God of Israel, saying, Write thee all the words that I have spoken unto thee in a book.

<a id="v3"></a><sup class="vn">3</sup> For, lo, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will bring again the captivity of my people Israel and Judah, saith the Lord: and I will cause them to return to the land that I gave to their fathers, and they shall possess it.

<a id="v4"></a><sup class="vn">4</sup> And these are the words that the Lord spake concerning Israel and concerning Judah.

<a id="v5"></a><sup class="vn">5</sup> For thus saith the Lord; We have heard a voice of trembling, of fear, and not of peace.

<a id="v6"></a><sup class="vn">6</sup> Ask ye now, and see whether a man doth travail with child? wherefore do I see every man with his hands on his loins, as a woman in travail, and all faces are turned into paleness?

<a id="v7"></a><sup class="vn">7</sup> Alas! for that day is great, so that none is like it: it is even the time of Jacob’s trouble; but he shall be saved out of it.

<a id="v8"></a><sup class="vn">8</sup> For it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord of hosts, that I will break his yoke from off thy neck, and will burst thy bonds, and strangers shall no more serve themselves of him:

<a id="v9"></a><sup class="vn">9</sup> But they shall serve the Lord their God, and David their king, whom I will raise up unto them.

<a id="v10"></a><sup class="vn">10</sup> Therefore fear thou not, O my servant Jacob, saith the Lord; neither be dismayed, O Israel: for, lo, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and shall be in rest, and be quiet, and none shall make him afraid.

<a id="v11"></a><sup class="vn">11</sup> For I am with thee, saith the Lord, to save thee: though I make a full end of all nations whither I have scattered thee, yet will I not make a full end of thee: but I will correct thee in measure, and will not leave thee altogether unpunished.

<a id="v12"></a><sup class="vn">12</sup> For thus saith the Lord, Thy bruise is incurable, and thy wound is grievous.

<a id="v13"></a><sup class="vn">13</sup> There is none to plead thy cause, that thou mayest be bound up: thou hast no healing medicines.

<a id="v14"></a><sup class="vn">14</sup> All thy lovers have forgotten thee; they seek thee not; for I have wounded thee with the wound of an enemy, with the chastisement of a cruel one, for the multitude of thine iniquity; because thy sins were increased.

<a id="v15"></a><sup class="vn">15</sup> Why criest thou for thine affliction? thy sorrow is incurable for the multitude of thine iniquity: because thy sins were increased, I have done these things unto thee.

<a id="v16"></a><sup class="vn">16</sup> Therefore all they that devour thee shall be devoured; and all thine adversaries, every one of them, shall go into captivity; and they that spoil thee shall be a spoil, and all that prey upon thee will I give for a prey.

<a id="v17"></a><sup class="vn">17</sup> For I will restore health unto thee, and I will heal thee of thy wounds, saith the Lord; because they called thee an Outcast, saying, This is Zion, whom no man seeketh after.

<a id="v18"></a><sup class="vn">18</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will bring again the captivity of Jacob’s tents, and have mercy on his dwellingplaces; and the city shall be builded upon her own heap, and the palace shall remain after the manner thereof.

<a id="v19"></a><sup class="vn">19</sup> And out of them shall proceed thanksgiving and the voice of them that make merry: and I will multiply them, and they shall not be few; I will also glorify them, and they shall not be small.

<a id="v20"></a><sup class="vn">20</sup> Their children also shall be as aforetime, and their congregation shall be established before me, and I will punish all that oppress them.

<a id="v21"></a><sup class="vn">21</sup> And their nobles shall be of themselves, and their governor shall proceed from the midst of them; and I will cause him to draw near, and he shall approach unto me: for who is this that engaged his heart to approach unto me? saith the Lord.

<a id="v22"></a><sup class="vn">22</sup> And ye shall be my people, and I will be your God.

<a id="v23"></a><sup class="vn">23</sup> Behold, the whirlwind of the Lord goeth forth with fury, a continuing whirlwind: it shall fall with pain upon the head of the wicked.

<a id="v24"></a><sup class="vn">24</sup> The fierce anger of the Lord shall not return, until he have done it, and until he have performed the intents of his heart: in the latter days ye shall consider it.
