## Introduction

This session reads 1 Esdras 5 through 8. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The reading covers the register of those who came up out of the captivity with Zorobabel, the altar set up and the foundation of the house laid, the hindering of the work and the letter to Darius that answered it, the finishing and dedication of the house, and then Esdras coming up from Babylon with the commission of Artaxerxes and his prayer over the strange marriages.
