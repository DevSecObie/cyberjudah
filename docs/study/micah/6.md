**[Micah 6:5](/bible/micah/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/micah/6#v1)</sup> Hear ye now what the Lord saith; Arise, contend thou before the mountains, and let the hills hear thy voice.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/micah/6#v2)</sup> Hear ye, O mountains, the Lord’s controversy, and ye strong foundations of the earth: for the Lord hath a controversy with his people, and he will plead with Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/micah/6#v3)</sup> O my people, what have I done unto thee? and wherein have I wearied thee? testify against me.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/micah/6#v4)</sup> For I brought thee up out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed thee out of the house of servants; and I sent before thee Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/micah/6#v5)</sup> O my people, remember now what Balak king of Moab consulted, and what Balaam the son of Beor answered him from Shittim unto Gilgal; that ye may know the righteousness of the Lord.

**[Micah 6:8](/bible/micah/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/micah/6#v6)</sup> Wherewith shall I come before the Lord, and bow myself before the high God? shall I come before him with burnt offerings, with calves of a year old?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/micah/6#v7)</sup> Will the Lord be pleased with thousands of rams, or with ten thousands of rivers of oil? shall I give my firstborn for my transgression, the fruit of my body for the sin of my soul?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/micah/6#v8)</sup> He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God?

- Wherewith shall I come before the Lord, and bow myself before the high God? shall I come before him with burnt offerings, with calves of a year old?

**[Micah 6:16](/bible/micah/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/micah/6#v9)</sup> The Lord’s voice crieth unto the city, and the man of wisdom shall see thy name: hear ye the rod, and who hath appointed it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/micah/6#v10)</sup> Are there yet the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is abominable?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/micah/6#v11)</sup> Shall I count them pure with the wicked balances, and with the bag of deceitful weights?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/micah/6#v12)</sup> For the rich men thereof are full of violence, and the inhabitants thereof have spoken lies, and their tongue is deceitful in their mouth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/micah/6#v13)</sup> Therefore also will I make thee sick in smiting thee, in making thee desolate because of thy sins.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/micah/6#v14)</sup> Thou shalt eat, but not be satisfied; and thy casting down shall be in the midst of thee; and thou shalt take hold, but shalt not deliver; and that which thou deliverest will I give up to the sword.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/micah/6#v15)</sup> Thou shalt sow, but thou shalt not reap; thou shalt tread the olives, but thou shalt not anoint thee with oil; and sweet wine, but shalt not drink wine.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/micah/6#v16)</sup> For the statutes of Omri are kept, and all the works of the house of Ahab, and ye walk in their counsels; that I should make thee a desolation, and the inhabitants thereof an hissing: therefore ye shall bear the reproach of my people.
