**[Micah 3:4](/bible/micah/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/micah/3#v1)</sup> And I said, Hear, I pray you, O heads of Jacob, and ye princes of the house of Israel; Is it not for you to know judgment?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/micah/3#v2)</sup> Who hate the good, and love the evil; who pluck off their skin from off them, and their flesh from off their bones;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/micah/3#v3)</sup> Who also eat the flesh of my people, and flay their skin from off them; and they break their bones, and chop them in pieces, as for the pot, and as flesh within the caldron.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/micah/3#v4)</sup> Then shall they cry unto the Lord, but he will not hear them: he will even hide his face from them at that time, as they have behaved themselves ill in their doings.

- Hear, I pray you, O heads of Jacob, and ye princes of the house of Israel; Is it not for you to know judgment?
- Who hate the good, and love the evil; who pluck off their skin from off them, and their flesh from off their bones; who also eat the flesh of my people.
- Then shall they cry unto the Lord, but he will not hear them: he will even hide his face from them at that time, as they have behaved themselves ill in their doings.

**[Micah 3:8](/bible/micah/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/micah/3#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that make my people err, that bite with their teeth, and cry, Peace; and he that putteth not into their mouths, they even prepare war against him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/micah/3#v6)</sup> Therefore night shall be unto you, that ye shall not have a vision; and it shall be dark unto you, that ye shall not divine; and the sun shall go down over the prophets, and the day shall be dark over them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/micah/3#v7)</sup> Then shall the seers be ashamed, and the diviners confounded: yea, they shall all cover their lips; for there is no answer of God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/micah/3#v8)</sup> But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the Lord, and of judgment, and of might, to declare unto Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin.

- Thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that make my people err, that bite with their teeth, and cry, Peace; and he that putteth not into their mouths, they even prepare war against him.

- But truly I am full of power by the spirit of the Lord, and of judgment, and of might, to declare unto Jacob his transgression, and to Israel his sin.

**[Micah 3:12](/bible/micah/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/micah/3#v9)</sup> Hear this, I pray you, ye heads of the house of Jacob, and princes of the house of Israel, that abhor judgment, and pervert all equity.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/micah/3#v10)</sup> They build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/micah/3#v11)</sup> The heads thereof judge for reward, and the priests thereof teach for hire, and the prophets thereof divine for money: yet will they lean upon the Lord, and say, Is not the Lord among us? none evil can come upon us.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/micah/3#v12)</sup> Therefore shall Zion for your sake be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of the forest.

- Ye heads of the house of Jacob, and princes of the house of Israel, that abhor judgment, and pervert all equity; they build up Zion with blood, and Jerusalem with iniquity.
- The heads thereof judge for reward, and the priests thereof teach for hire, and the prophets thereof divine for money: yet will they lean upon the Lord, and say, Is not the Lord among us? none evil can come upon us.
- Therefore shall Zion for your sake be plowed as a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of the forest.
