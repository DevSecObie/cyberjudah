## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Thessalonians 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Thessalonians 2:1](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v1)</sup> Now we beseech you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and by our gathering together unto him,

- “By the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and by our gathering together unto him.” Our gathering together unto him is what Genesis 49:10 speaks of: unto Christ shall the gathering of the people be. That is what Paul was talking about.
- “By our gathering together unto him”: that him is Christ. When you hear people on earth say they are the ones Christ is using to gather the 12 tribes, don't listen to that. Christ is the only one that can gather all 12 tribes together.
- “Now we beseech you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ”, the coming of the Messiah, “and by our gathering together unto him”: that is what we are doing right now in repentance. We are gathering unto him.
