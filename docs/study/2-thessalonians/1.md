## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Thessalonians 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-10, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Thessalonians 1:3-4](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v3)</sup> We are bound to thank God always for you, brethren, as it is meet, because that your faith groweth exceedingly, and the charity of every one of you all toward each other aboundeth;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v4)</sup> So that we ourselves glory in you in the churches of God for your patience and faith in all your persecutions and tribulations that ye endure:

- The salutation: “We are bound to thank God always for you, brethren”, glorying in them “for your patience and faith in all your persecutions and tribulations that ye endure”.
- “The charity of every one of you all toward each other aboundeth.” That is what we need in the body: the brothers and the sisters to be charitable, and for that charity amongst each other to abound.
- “So that we ourselves glory in you in the churches of God for your patience and faith in all your persecutions and tribulations that ye endure”. We glory in you for your patience. That is what these classes are about: to endure, because our people need us out there. In your patience you possess your soul.
- We are in a job of enduring. If you find yourself in jail, endure. Afflicted, endure. Put to death, endure. If they raid your house for Christ's sake and take your stuff, endure: you can buy those things again. The key is to endure.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 13:23](/bible/2-esdras/13#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/2-esdras/13#v23)</sup> He that shall endure the peril in that time hath kept himself: they that be fallen into danger are such as have works, and faith toward the Almighty.

    “He that shall endure the peril in that time hath kept himself: they that be fallen into danger are such as have works, and faith toward the Almighty.” The brothers who fall into danger are the brothers who put the work in, who have faith toward God and faith in Christ and keep pushing no matter what.
  - **[2 Esdras 9:7](/bible/2-esdras/9#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-esdras/9#v7)</sup> And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, and by faith, whereby ye have believed,

    “And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, and by faith, whereby ye have believed”. The brothers who put their works in are the ones who will escape in these last days, because they keep it pushing by faith.

**[2 Thessalonians 1:5](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v5)</sup> Which is a manifest token of the righteous judgment of God, that ye may be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which ye also suffer:

- “That ye may be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which ye also suffer”.
- Going through those things and enduring them shows “that ye may be counted worthy of the kingdom of God, for which ye also suffer.” You are going to suffer for the kingdom of God, and in your suffering you still must maintain love for your neighbor. It is not about you or about one man. It is about the body of Christ.

**[2 Thessalonians 1:6](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v6)</sup> Seeing it is a righteous thing with God to recompense tribulation to them that trouble you;

- Still dealing with deliverance from evil. “Seeing it is a righteous thing with God to recompense tribulation to them that trouble you”. Who’s troubling us? The United States government, with Fauci. Not just him: the CDC, the NIH, and Fauci’s filthy wife is over one of those damn things. They’re creating all these: oh, we got a new vaccine; you had one yet? We got two more for you. The hell is this?
- That mother waited three years for justice for her son, shot in the back on video, and got a plea deal. So she said he had better watch his back in jail. That is her frustration talking. When our people feel we can’t get no justice anywhere else, they go underground and say ‘street justice’, take it to the streets. That is what happened in L.A. with the brothers during the Rodney King thing: it was on video, and it was the same foolishness. And the black preacher don’t see nothing wrong with these kinds of situations and never says a word.
- Was that sister justified in being angry? Yes. Her son was murdered, shot, and she was justified in being angry. But if you brothers and sisters want vengeance for all the evil being done to your people, you know what you have to do: keep these commandments. Stop lying, stop stealing, stop killing, stop hating each other. You want vengeance, keep the commandments.
- “Seeing it is a righteous thing with God to recompense tribulation to them that trouble you”. To recompense is to pay back: it is a righteous thing with God to pay back tribulation on them that trouble us, them that oppress us and afflict us. All of these judges, all of these lawyers, all of them: the Lord is going to recompense tribulation on them for all that evil they are doing. The nation that eats us up, the Lord is going to recompense tribulation on them for all the evil they have done to us.

**[2 Thessalonians 1:7-9](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v7)</sup> And to you who are troubled rest with us, when the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven with his mighty angels,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v8)</sup> In flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God, and that obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v9)</sup> Who shall be punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord, and from the glory of his power;

- “When the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven with his mighty angels, In flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God, and that obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ: Who shall be punished with everlasting destruction from the presence of the Lord”.
- “And to you who are troubled rest with us, when the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven with his mighty angels, In flaming fire taking vengeance on them that know not God.” Christ is coming back for vengeance. That is never taught in church, but it has been in the Bible since before any of us were here. This is the New Testament.
- “And that obey not the gospel of our Lord Jesus Christ.” They do not obey the gospel. The majority of our people just finished celebrating New Year’s; a week before that it was Christmas, a month before that Thanksgiving, a month before that Halloween. Every year they keep repeating the same evil. By now they know these things are evil and not of God, and they choose the wickedness.
- “And to you who are troubled rest with us”: come into this truth, learn. “When the Lord Jesus shall be revealed from heaven with his mighty angels”.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 35:4](/bible/isaiah/35#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/35#v4)</sup> Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence; he will come and save you.

    “Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence.” That is the recompense and the vengeance just read in 2 Thessalonians 1: the Old Testament says the same as the New.

**[2 Thessalonians 1:10](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v10)</sup> When he shall come to be glorified in his saints, and to be admired in all them that believe (because our testimony among you was believed) in that day.

- “When he shall come to be glorified in his saints, and to be admired in all them that believe”: we believe, we glorify him and we admire him. That’s why we’re bringing out the Messiah as it is written. Y’all keep going to church and letting them lie to you.
