## Introduction

This session covers [Joshua 13](/bible/joshua/13) through 16, where the dividing of the land begins. Joshua is now old, and the Most High names the land that still remains to be possessed, confirms the inheritance Moses gave the two tribes and a half on the other side Jordan, and commands the rest to be divided by lot. Caleb receives Hebron for wholly following the Most High, then the lot of the tribe of Judah and the lot of the children of Joseph are drawn out. These chapters carry a whole lot of names of cities and borders, so as you read and listen back through them, it does not hurt to pull up a map or look through your Bible atlas and find where these places were, to get a geographical sense of the land the children of Israel were moving through and conquering.

**[Joshua 13:1](/bible/joshua/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/13#v1)</sup> Now Joshua was old and stricken in years; and the Lord said unto him, Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed.

- Joshua is old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed. The Most High had already said he was not going to drive out these nations in one year, but little and little as Israel increased, so much land still remained.
- The nations were also left among Israel to prove them, whether they would keep the commandments of the Most High, and so that the generations which had not known the wars of Canaan might be taught war. We all know what ended up happening with that proving, and it comes later on as we read.
