**[Joshua 6:1-2](/bible/joshua/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/6#v1)</sup> Now Jericho was straitly shut up because of the children of Israel: none went out, and none came in.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/6#v2)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, See, I have given into thine hand Jericho, and the king thereof, and the mighty men of valour.

- Jericho was straitly shut up because of the children of Israel: none went out and none came in. Jericho had a military, and a garrison, a military base, was built there, with the walls shut so nobody could enter or leave.
- The Lord said unto Joshua, See, I have given into thine hand Jericho, and the king thereof, and the mighty men of valour: the battle was already decided.

**[Joshua 6:3-5](/bible/joshua/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/6#v3)</sup> And ye shall compass the city, all ye men of war, and go round about the city once. Thus shalt thou do six days.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/6#v4)</sup> And seven priests shall bear before the ark seven trumpets of rams’ horns: and the seventh day ye shall compass the city seven times, and the priests shall blow with the trumpets.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/6#v5)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that when they make a long blast with the ram’s horn, and when ye hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout with a great shout; and the wall of the city shall fall down flat, and the people shall ascend up every man straight before him.

- The battle plan came from the Lord: compass the city with all the men of war once a day for six days, seven priests bearing seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark; on the seventh day compass the city seven times, and at the long blast all the people shout with a great shout, and the wall shall fall down flat.
- The blowing of the trumpets activates the armies of the Lord, the angels, to help in battle. Remember Elisha's servant: when his eyes were opened he saw the host of angels that were there warring and protecting Israel.
- This is the spirit behind the men of Israel rehearsing the righteous acts with trumpets and shofars today, at the camps and at the new moons; it is spiritual and an act of faith.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 10:9](/bible/numbers/10#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/10#v9)</sup> And if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets; and ye shall be remembered before the Lord your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.

    If ye go to war against the enemy that oppresseth you, blow an alarm with the trumpets, and ye shall be remembered before the Lord and saved from your enemies.

**[Joshua 6:6-11](/bible/joshua/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/6#v6)</sup> And Joshua the son of Nun called the priests, and said unto them, Take up the ark of the covenant, and let seven priests bear seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the ark of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/6#v7)</sup> And he said unto the people, Pass on, and compass the city, and let him that is armed pass on before the ark of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/6#v8)</sup> And it came to pass, when Joshua had spoken unto the people, that the seven priests bearing the seven trumpets of rams’ horns passed on before the Lord, and blew with the trumpets: and the ark of the covenant of the Lord followed them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/6#v9)</sup> And the armed men went before the priests that blew with the trumpets, and the rereward came after the ark, the priests going on, and blowing with the trumpets.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/6#v10)</sup> And Joshua had commanded the people, saying, Ye shall not shout, nor make any noise with your voice, neither shall any word proceed out of your mouth, until the day I bid you shout; then shall ye shout.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/6#v11)</sup> So the ark of the Lord compassed the city, going about it once: and they came into the camp, and lodged in the camp.

- Joshua called the priests and gave the order: seven priests bear seven trumpets before the ark, the armed men pass on before the ark, and the rereward, the guards in the rear, came after the ark.
- Joshua commanded the people, Ye shall not shout, nor make any noise with your voice, neither shall any word proceed out of your mouth, until the day I bid you shout; then shall ye shout.
- The ark of the Lord compassed the city once, and they came into the camp and lodged there.

**[Joshua 6:12-14](/bible/joshua/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/6#v12)</sup> And Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/6#v13)</sup> And seven priests bearing seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the ark of the Lord went on continually, and blew with the trumpets: and the armed men went before them; but the rereward came after the ark of the Lord, the priests going on, and blowing with the trumpets.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/6#v14)</sup> And the second day they compassed the city once, and returned into the camp: so they did six days.

- They repeated the act six days back to back, rising early, the priests bearing the ark and blowing the trumpets, the armed men before and the rereward after, one compass of the city each day.
- Every army in the world stole this from the Israelites: the pass and review of the militaries, with their battalions and units, their guidons with the eagle on the tip of the spear, marching round the field sounding off with cadences and trumpets, is patterned after what Israel did here.

**[Joshua 6:15-16](/bible/joshua/6#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/6#v15)</sup> And it came to pass on the seventh day, that they rose early about the dawning of the day, and compassed the city after the same manner seven times: only on that day they compassed the city seven times.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/joshua/6#v16)</sup> And it came to pass at the seventh time, when the priests blew with the trumpets, Joshua said unto the people, Shout; for the Lord hath given you the city.

- On the seventh day they rose about the dawning of the day and compassed the city seven times. There is something about the number seven that the Lord loves; you read about it in Revelation.
- At the seventh time, when the priests blew with the trumpets, Joshua said unto the people, Shout; for the Lord hath given you the city.
- The men apply [Joshua 6](/bible/joshua/6) to this day: when a scoffer will not hear the word and thinks to outcry the truth, the brothers say the scripture together with one mind, one body, one spirit. That unity is spiritual, a rehearsal of the righteous acts.
- Unity and order are what the enemies fear: not gangs and drug dealing, but the so-called Black man, Hispanic man, and Native American man unified in the order of a military, with structure and organization. That is what puts fear in the nations.

**[Joshua 6:17-19](/bible/joshua/6#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/joshua/6#v17)</sup> And the city shall be accursed, even it, and all that are therein, to the Lord: only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers that we sent.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/joshua/6#v18)</sup> And ye, in any wise keep yourselves from the accursed thing, lest ye make yourselves accursed, when ye take of the accursed thing, and make the camp of Israel a curse, and trouble it.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/6#v19)</sup> But all the silver, and gold, and vessels of brass and iron, are consecrated unto the Lord: they shall come into the treasury of the Lord.

- The city shall be accursed, even it and all that are therein, to the Lord: only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers. She made a covenant with the spies: she would conceal them and assist Israel, knowing the Lord was with Israel, as long as her family was saved when the city fell.
- Rahab was a Canaanite, a heathen, yet the heathen can be blessed when they do it to benefit Israel; blessed is he that blesseth thee.
- Rahab is the scripture's own example that a man is justified by works and not by faith only: faith and works go hand in hand, for your actions prove whether you believe. Was not Rahab the harlot justified by works, when she had received the messengers and sent them out another way? As the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.
- Works by the Bible's own dictionary is keeping his commandments: set your hope in God, forget not his works, but keep his commandments. Words say a lot, but actions always speak louder, and by him actions are weighed.
- Keep yourselves from the accursed thing, lest ye make yourselves accursed and make the camp of Israel a curse, and trouble it: your individual sins affect the whole nation of Israel, your brothers, your sisters, and the children. That is why we must examine ourselves whether we be in the faith.
- All the silver and gold and vessels of brass and iron were consecrated unto the Lord, separated and sanctified for his treasury alone: nobody was to gain personally from this war.

    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/james/2#v26)</sup> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.

    Set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments; works means keeping the commandments.

    The Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

**[Joshua 6:20-21](/bible/joshua/6#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/6#v20)</sup> So the people shouted when the priests blew with the trumpets: and it came to pass, when the people heard the sound of the trumpet, and the people shouted with a great shout, that the wall fell down flat, so that the people went up into the city, every man straight before him, and they took the city.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/joshua/6#v21)</sup> And they utterly destroyed all that was in the city, both man and woman, young and old, and ox, and sheep, and ass, with the edge of the sword.

- The people shouted when the priests blew with the trumpets, and the wall fell down flat. That shout is [Isaiah 58:1](/bible/isaiah/58#v1) in action, cry aloud and spare not: the sound coming out of their mouths with the trumpets broke everything down, like thousands of lions roaring at once.
- The people went up into the city, every man straight before him, and they took the city and utterly destroyed all that was in it, both man and woman, young and old, ox, sheep, and ass, with the edge of the sword.

**[Joshua 6:22-25](/bible/joshua/6#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/joshua/6#v22)</sup> But Joshua had said unto the two men that had spied out the country, Go into the harlot’s house, and bring out thence the woman, and all that she hath, as ye sware unto her.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/joshua/6#v23)</sup> And the young men that were spies went in, and brought out Rahab, and her father, and her mother, and her brethren, and all that she had; and they brought out all her kindred, and left them without the camp of Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/6#v24)</sup> And they burnt the city with fire, and all that was therein: only the silver, and the gold, and the vessels of brass and of iron, they put into the treasury of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/joshua/6#v25)</sup> And Joshua saved Rahab the harlot alive, and her father’s household, and all that she had; and she dwelleth in Israel even unto this day; because she hid the messengers, which Joshua sent to spy out Jericho.

- Joshua sent the two spies into the harlot's house to bring out Rahab and all that she had, as they sware unto her; they brought out her father, mother, brethren, and all her kindred, and left them without the camp of Israel.
- They burnt the city with fire and all that was therein; only the silver, gold, and vessels of brass and iron went into the treasury of the house of the Lord.
- Joshua saved Rahab the harlot alive and her father's household, and she dwelleth in Israel even unto this day, because she hid the messengers. She helped the nation of Israel toward rulership, and that is the only help Israel wants from the other nations: not food stamps, welfare, or reparations, but tell our people who they are, the Israelites the Bible speaks of, the so-called Blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans, and that we must keep the commandments of the Most High. Whoever does that will receive mercy just as Rahab did.
