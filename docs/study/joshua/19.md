**[Joshua 19:1](/bible/joshua/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/19#v1)</sup> And the second lot came forth to Simeon, even for the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families: and their inheritance was within the inheritance of the children of Judah.

- The second lot came forth to Simeon, and their inheritance was within the inheritance of the children of Judah.
- On the map the whole land Judah possessed is marked as one, and within it sits the portion where Simeon resided, down by Beer-sheba. The so-called Dominicans, of Simeon, were living within the land of the so-called African Americans, of Judah; this is our history and where we lived.
- It only sounds crazy because we are not taught anything prior to 1492; we are the real Jews, and these maps show where our forefathers and our foremothers were living.

**[Joshua 19:8-9](/bible/joshua/19#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/19#v8)</sup> And all the villages that were round about these cities to Baalath–beer, Ramath of the south. This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/19#v9)</sup> Out of the portion of the children of Judah was the inheritance of the children of Simeon: for the part of the children of Judah was too much for them: therefore the children of Simeon had their inheritance within the inheritance of them.

- This is the inheritance of the tribe of the children of Simeon according to their families, out of the portion of the children of Judah: for the part of the children of Judah was too much for them.

**[Joshua 19:10](/bible/joshua/19#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/19#v10)</sup> And the third lot came up for the children of Zebulun according to their families: and the border of their inheritance was unto Sarid:

**[Joshua 19:32-35](/bible/joshua/19#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/joshua/19#v32)</sup> The sixth lot came out to the children of Naphtali, even for the children of Naphtali according to their families.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/joshua/19#v33)</sup> And their coast was from Heleph, from Allon to Zaanannim, and Adami, Nekeb, and Jabneel, unto Lakum; and the outgoings thereof were at Jordan:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/joshua/19#v34)</sup> And then the coast turneth westward to Aznoth–tabor, and goeth out from thence to Hukkok, and reacheth to Zebulun on the south side, and reacheth to Asher on the west side, and to Judah upon Jordan toward the sunrising.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/joshua/19#v35)</sup> And the fenced cities are Ziddim, Zer, and Hammath, Rakkath, and Chinnereth,

- The sixth lot came out to the children of Naphtali; their coast reached to Zebulun on the south side, to Asher on the west side, and to Judah upon Jordan toward the sunrising, meaning the east.
- The fenced cities are cities with great walls; they were bordered and protected for military purposes, so that the land and the city would not be infiltrated.

**[Joshua 19:47](/bible/joshua/19#v47)**

> <sup>[47](/bible/joshua/19#v47)</sup> And the coast of the children of Dan went out too little for them: therefore the children of Dan went up to fight against Leshem, and took it, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and possessed it, and dwelt therein, and called Leshem, Dan, after the name of Dan their father.

- The coast of the children of Dan went out too little for them: they lost their portion, they let it slip, and they had to go and conquer different territory.
- Therefore the children of Dan went up to fight against Leshem, took it, smote it with the edge of the sword, possessed it, and dwelt therein, and called Leshem, Dan, after the name of Dan their father.
