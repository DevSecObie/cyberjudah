**[Joshua 8:1-2](/bible/joshua/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/8#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Fear not, neither be thou dismayed: take all the people of war with thee, and arise, go up to Ai: see, I have given into thy hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/8#v2)</sup> And thou shalt do to Ai and her king as thou didst unto Jericho and her king: only the spoil thereof, and the cattle thereof, shall ye take for a prey unto yourselves: lay thee an ambush for the city behind it.

- With the accursed thing purged, the Lord said unto Joshua, Fear not, neither be thou dismayed: take all the people of war with thee and go up to Ai; see, I have given into thy hand the king of Ai, and his people, and his city, and his land.
- This time the spoil thereof and the cattle shall ye take for a prey unto yourselves, unlike Jericho where everything was consecrated to the treasury of the Lord; and the command was to lay an ambush for the city behind it.

**[Joshua 8:3-8](/bible/joshua/8#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/8#v3)</sup> So Joshua arose, and all the people of war, to go up against Ai: and Joshua chose out thirty thousand mighty men of valour, and sent them away by night.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/8#v4)</sup> And he commanded them, saying, Behold, ye shall lie in wait against the city, even behind the city: go not very far from the city, but be ye all ready:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/8#v5)</sup> And I, and all the people that are with me, will approach unto the city: and it shall come to pass, when they come out against us, as at the first, that we will flee before them,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/8#v6)</sup> (For they will come out after us) till we have drawn them from the city; for they will say, They flee before us, as at the first: therefore we will flee before them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/8#v7)</sup> Then ye shall rise up from the ambush, and seize upon the city: for the Lord your God will deliver it into your hand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/8#v8)</sup> And it shall be, when ye have taken the city, that ye shall set the city on fire: according to the commandment of the Lord shall ye do. See, I have commanded you.

- Joshua chose out thirty thousand mighty men of valour and sent them away by night to lie in ambush behind the city, not very far from it, always ready. These were warriors, men of courage, but none of that matters unless the spirit is right with the Lord first.
- The strategy: Joshua and the people with him approach the city, and when the men of Ai come out, Israel flees before them as at the first, drawing them away from the city; then the ambush rises up and seizes the city and sets it on fire, according to the commandment of the Lord.
- Note the wording: for the Lord your God will deliver it into your hand. He did not say ye shall win the battle; the Most High is the one who delivers the enemy into Israel's hand.

**[Joshua 8:9-13](/bible/joshua/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/8#v9)</sup> Joshua therefore sent them forth: and they went to lie in ambush, and abode between Beth–el and Ai, on the west side of Ai: but Joshua lodged that night among the people.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/8#v10)</sup> And Joshua rose up early in the morning, and numbered the people, and went up, he and the elders of Israel, before the people to Ai.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/8#v11)</sup> And all the people, even the people of war that were with him, went up, and drew nigh, and came before the city, and pitched on the north side of Ai: now there was a valley between them and Ai.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/8#v12)</sup> And he took about five thousand men, and set them to lie in ambush between Beth–el and Ai, on the west side of the city.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/8#v13)</sup> And when they had set the people, even all the host that was on the north of the city, and their liers in wait on the west of the city, Joshua went that night into the midst of the valley.

- The ambush went and abode between Bethel and Ai, on the west side of Ai, while Joshua lodged that night among the people; he rose early, numbered the people, and went up with the elders of Israel and pitched on the north side of Ai, with a valley between them and the city.
- He took about five thousand men and set them to lie in ambush between Bethel and Ai on the west side: every position was assigned, everything strategic. Joshua was carrying out the playbook, gifted in war strategy and technique.

**[Joshua 8:14-17](/bible/joshua/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/8#v14)</sup> And it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw it, that they hasted and rose up early, and the men of the city went out against Israel to battle, he and all his people, at a time appointed, before the plain; but he wist not that there were liers in ambush against him behind the city.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/8#v15)</sup> And Joshua and all Israel made as if they were beaten before them, and fled by the way of the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/joshua/8#v16)</sup> And all the people that were in Ai were called together to pursue after them: and they pursued after Joshua, and were drawn away from the city.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/joshua/8#v17)</sup> And there was not a man left in Ai or Beth–el, that went not out after Israel: and they left the city open, and pursued after Israel.

- When the king of Ai saw it, the men of the city hasted and rose up early and went out against Israel to battle at a time appointed, not knowing there were liers in ambush behind the city.
- Joshua and all Israel made as if they were beaten before them, and fled by the way of the wilderness: part of the strategy, feigning defeat so Ai would chase them straight into the ambush.
- All the people in Ai were called together to pursue, and they were drawn away from the city: not a man was left in Ai or Bethel that went not out after Israel, and they left the city open.

**[Joshua 8:18-19](/bible/joshua/8#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/joshua/8#v18)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Stretch out the spear that is in thy hand toward Ai; for I will give it into thine hand. And Joshua stretched out the spear that he had in his hand toward the city.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/8#v19)</sup> And the ambush arose quickly out of their place, and they ran as soon as he had stretched out his hand: and they entered into the city, and took it, and hasted and set the city on fire.

- The Lord said unto Joshua, Stretch out the spear that is in thy hand toward Ai; for I will give it into thine hand.
- As soon as he stretched out his hand, the ambush arose quickly out of their place, ran, entered into the city, took it, and hasted and set the city on fire.

**[Joshua 8:20-23](/bible/joshua/8#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/8#v20)</sup> And when the men of Ai looked behind them, they saw, and, behold, the smoke of the city ascended up to heaven, and they had no power to flee this way or that way: and the people that fled to the wilderness turned back upon the pursuers.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/joshua/8#v21)</sup> And when Joshua and all Israel saw that the ambush had taken the city, and that the smoke of the city ascended, then they turned again, and slew the men of Ai.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/joshua/8#v22)</sup> And the other issued out of the city against them; so they were in the midst of Israel, some on this side, and some on that side: and they smote them, so that they let none of them remain or escape.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/joshua/8#v23)</sup> And the king of Ai they took alive, and brought him to Joshua.

- When the men of Ai looked behind them, the smoke of their city ascended up to heaven, and they had no power to flee this way or that way; the people that fled to the wilderness turned back upon the pursuers.
- The men of Ai were in the midst of Israel, some on this side and some on that side, and Israel smote them until none remained or escaped; they were surrounded, exactly as drawn up. The king of Ai they took alive and brought him to Joshua.

**[Joshua 8:24-26](/bible/joshua/8#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/8#v24)</sup> And it came to pass, when Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness wherein they chased them, and when they were all fallen on the edge of the sword, until they were consumed, that all the Israelites returned unto Ai, and smote it with the edge of the sword.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/joshua/8#v25)</sup> And so it was, that all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/joshua/8#v26)</sup> For Joshua drew not his hand back, wherewith he stretched out the spear, until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai.

- When Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field and in the wilderness, all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai. Women are casualties of war.
- That point stands as a warning for the sisters serving unrepented in the militaries of the nations, standing stiffly for the oppressor: when Christ comes back he is coming to kill, and a uniform and a weapon will not save; men and women will be dying alike. It is best in these last days for a woman to stand by and support her own man and nation, not another nation.
- Joshua drew not his hand back until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai, meaning the children were put to death also. This was not new: at Sihon's cities Israel utterly destroyed the men, the women, and the little ones. The children's song teaching that Christ loves all the children of every nation alike does not stand with these scriptures; the Lord's love is toward the children of Israel.

    How long wilt thou go about, O thou backsliding daughter? the Lord hath created a new thing in the earth, A woman shall compass a man.
  - **[Deuteronomy 2:34](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v34)</sup> And we took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed the men, and the women, and the little ones, of every city, we left none to remain:

    We took all his cities at that time, and utterly destroyed the men, and the women, and the little ones, of every city; we left none to remain.

**[Joshua 8:27-29](/bible/joshua/8#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/joshua/8#v27)</sup> Only the cattle and the spoil of that city Israel took for a prey unto themselves, according unto the word of the Lord which he commanded Joshua.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/joshua/8#v28)</sup> And Joshua burnt Ai, and made it an heap for ever, even a desolation unto this day.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/joshua/8#v29)</sup> And the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until eventide: and as soon as the sun was down, Joshua commanded that they should take his carcase down from the tree, and cast it at the entering of the gate of the city, and raise thereon a great heap of stones, that remaineth unto this day.

- Only the cattle and the spoil of that city Israel took for a prey unto themselves, according unto the word of the Lord which he commanded Joshua: they carried out the order exactly as instructed.
- Joshua burnt Ai and made it an heap for ever, even a desolation unto this day; the king of Ai he hanged on a tree until eventide, and as soon as the sun was down his carcase was taken down, cast at the entering of the gate of the city, and a great heap of stones raised thereon.

**[Joshua 8:30-32](/bible/joshua/8#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/joshua/8#v30)</sup> Then Joshua built an altar unto the Lord God of Israel in mount Ebal,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/joshua/8#v31)</sup> As Moses the servant of the Lord commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, an altar of whole stones, over which no man hath lift up any iron: and they offered thereon burnt offerings unto the Lord, and sacrificed peace offerings.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/joshua/8#v32)</sup> And he wrote there upon the stones a copy of the law of Moses, which he wrote in the presence of the children of Israel.

- Then Joshua built an altar unto the Lord God of Israel in mount Ebal, as Moses commanded and as it is written in the book of the law: an altar of whole stones, over which no man hath lift up any iron; and they offered burnt offerings and sacrificed peace offerings, and he wrote upon the stones a copy of the law of Moses.
- We are still under the law of sacrifice today, but the sacrifice is your body, not the blood of bulls and goats: you sacrifice time, money, pain, and labor to push this truth and do the work, spiritual sacrifices through your works by keeping the laws.
- The peace offering today is keeping the commandments: he that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough, and he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering. Under grace, peace is a dispensation: the more sin you commit, the less peace you will have, so keep the commandments and do not frustrate the grace and peace the Most High has with you.
- The Most High hath a controversy with the inhabitants of the land; if you do not want the Lord to have a controversy with you, keep the laws. And keeping the laws is more than beards, fringes, borders of blue, and not eating pork: it is purging the unfruitful spirits you know dwell inside you, the jealousy, envy, covetousness, malice, hatred, grudging, backbiting, murmuring, and gossip.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 35:1](/bible/sirach/35#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/35#v1)</sup> He that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough: he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering.

    Today's peace offering is keeping the commandments.
  - **[Hosea 4:1](/bible/hosea/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/4#v1)</sup> Hear the word of the Lord, ye children of Israel: for the Lord hath a controversy with the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God in the land.

    The Lord hath a controversy with the inhabitants of the land; keep the laws so the controversy is not with you.

**[Joshua 8:33](/bible/joshua/8#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/joshua/8#v33)</sup> And all Israel, and their elders, and officers, and their judges, stood on this side the ark and on that side before the priests the Levites, which bare the ark of the covenant of the Lord, as well the stranger, as he that was born among them; half of them over against mount Gerizim, and half of them over against mount Ebal; as Moses the servant of the Lord had commanded before, that they should bless the people of Israel.

- All Israel, and their elders, and officers, and their judges stood on this side the ark and on that side, before the priests the Levites, the stranger as well as he that was born among them, half over against mount Gerizim and half over against mount Ebal, as Moses commanded, that they should bless the people of Israel.
- Why does the body set up rank instead of saying I only follow Christ? Because we are commanded to rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord: reading the wars and works of Joshua, Moses, and the officers, judges, and elders, we practice what our forefathers did, in order, as an organized army.
- Rehearse means practice, and practice makes perfect: there are rehearsals every day, classes and meetings for the men, the women, and the children, because this is not our rest.

  Precepts:
  - **[Judges 5:11](/bible/judges/5#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/judges/5#v11)</sup> They that are delivered from the noise of archers in the places of drawing water, there shall they rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, even the righteous acts toward the inhabitants of his villages in Israel: then shall the people of the Lord go down to the gates.

    There shall they rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord; the order of officers and judges is rehearsed in the body today.

**[Joshua 8:34-35](/bible/joshua/8#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/joshua/8#v34)</sup> And afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessings and cursings, according to all that is written in the book of the law.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/joshua/8#v35)</sup> There was not a word of all that Moses commanded, which Joshua read not before all the congregation of Israel, with the women, and the little ones, and the strangers that were conversant among them.

- Afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessings and cursings, according to all that is written in the book of the law. We tell our neighbor God bless you, but by the same book the Most High and Christ will curse you too if you do not keep the laws.
- Heaven and earth are called to record: everything we do is recorded, and life and death, blessing and cursing, are set before us. Life is the keeping of the commandments of the Most High, and death is the wages of sin; it is the one thing left optional, so choose life, for it is not just you that pays but thy seed as well.
- How do you become blessed? Read the last page of the book: blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life. A few verses before your Bible says the end, it says keep the commandments, so no man in his right mind can say the laws are done away with; that is justifying wickedness. Keep the sabbath holy, leave the swine alone, dress in modesty, and keep the commandments if you want the blessings.
- There was not a word of all that Moses commanded which Joshua read not before all the congregation, with the women, and the little ones, and the strangers that were conversant among them: those were the strangers that fell in line with Israel, with the fear of the Lord in them, as Rahab had.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 30:19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)</sup> I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live:

    I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live.
  - **[Proverbs 7:2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

    Keep my commandments, and live; life is keeping the commandments.
  - **[Romans 6:23](/bible/romans/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/6#v23)</sup> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

    The wages of sin is death; sin is what brings the cursing and the death.
  - **[Revelation 22:14](/bible/revelation/22#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/22#v14)</sup> Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city.

    Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life; the blessing at the last page of the book.
