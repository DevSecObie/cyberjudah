## Introduction

This session covers [Joshua 9](/bible/joshua/9) through 12, the heart of the conquest of the land. Chapter 9 is the wile of the Gibeonites, who beguile Israel into a league because the princes asked not counsel at the mouth of the Most High. Chapter 10 is the day the sun stood still: the Most High casts down hailstones from heaven, five kings are drawn out of their cave, and the whole south country is destroyed. Chapter 11 is the northern kings gathered as the sand of the sea and utterly destroyed, and chapter 12 is the roll of the thirty and one kings Joshua smote. What we read in Joshua will play out again when Christ returns, for there is nothing new under the sun. So study, pray, apply, endure to the end, and seek ye out of the book of the Lord and read.

- These are the kings of the country which Joshua and the children of Israel smote on this side Jordan on the west, which Joshua gave unto the tribes of Israel for a possession according to their divisions: the kings of Jericho, Ai, Jerusalem, Hebron, Jarmuth, Lachish, Eglon, Gezer, Debir, Geder, Hormah, Arad, Libnah, Adullam, Makkedah, Bethel, Tappuah, Hepher, Aphek, Lasharon, Madon, Hazor, Shimron-meron, Achshaph, Taanach, Megiddo, Kedesh, Jokneam of Carmel, Dor, the nations of Gilgal, and Tirzah: all the kings thirty and one.
- Practice reading these names aloud; it loosens up the tongue and helps you pronounce the names in the Bible that may run two or three syllables.
- Joshua was a mighty man: thirty and one kings he took down in the power of the Most High. Let us now praise famous men and our fathers that begat us. In Ecclesiasticus he is called Jesus the son of Nave, for Joshua translated into the Greek is Jesus, and Nave is Nun: Joshua the son of Nun, valiant in the wars, the successor of Moses in prophecies, who according to his name was made great for the saving of the elect of God.
- Did not the sun go back by his means, and was not one day as long as two? He said, Sun, be thou still, and there were two days of sunlight on the earth; and with hailstones of mighty power the Most High made the battle to fall violently upon the nations in the descent of Beth-horon.
- His counterpart Caleb was a mighty man too: he and Caleb withstood the congregation and withheld the people from sin, and of six hundred thousand people on foot, they two were preserved to bring them into the heritage. The Most High gave Caleb strength unto his old age, fighting in his eighties like he was in his twenties, and you will read of his strength in the next few chapters.
- Take pride in this: we are the descendants of the most famous men on the face of the earth. Our forefathers are mentioned in every single nation, and everybody wants to lay claim to them and to our book. That is why all nations hate us, and we will be famous again: the Most High will get Israel praise and fame in every land where they have been put to shame, when he turns back our captivity before our eyes. Remember, Jerusalem is a people before it is a place.
