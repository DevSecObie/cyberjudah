## Introduction

This session opens the book of Joshua, a mighty and action packed book of history. Moses delivered the children of Israel out of Egypt and led them through the wilderness; Joshua is the man anointed to rise up in his stead and lead them over Jordan into the promised land. The reading covers [Joshua 1](/bible/joshua/1) through 4: the charge and commission to Joshua, the formula for success in [Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8), Rahab and the spies at Jericho, Israel crossing Jordan on dry ground, and the twelve stone memorial at Gilgal.

**[Joshua 1:1-2](/bible/joshua/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/1#v1)</sup> Now after the death of Moses the servant of the Lord it came to pass, that the Lord spake unto Joshua the son of Nun, Moses’ minister, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/1#v2)</sup> Moses my servant is dead; now therefore arise, go over this Jordan, thou, and all this people, unto the land which I do give to them, even to the children of Israel.

- Joshua is called Moses' minister because when Moses was the leader, Joshua was the one always cleaving to him. He was not close to Moses in age; he was one of the young men that cleaved unto his elder, and if Moses was a good leader, beloved among the children of Israel, who better to rise up in his stead than the man who has his spirit?

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 11:28](/bible/numbers/11#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/11#v28)</sup> And Joshua the son of Nun, the servant of Moses, one of his young men, answered and said, My lord Moses, forbid them.

    In the wilderness Joshua was the servant of Moses, one of his young men, his protege; those were his humble beginnings.
  - **[Exodus 33:10-11](/bible/exodus/33#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/33#v10)</sup> And all the people saw the cloudy pillar stand at the tabernacle door: and all the people rose up and worshipped, every man in his tent door.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/33#v11)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses face to face, as a man speaketh unto his friend. And he turned again into the camp: but his servant Joshua, the son of Nun, a young man, departed not out of the tabernacle.

    All the people worshipped at their tent doors while the Lord spake unto Moses face to face, but his servant Joshua, a young man, departed not out of the tabernacle; he wanted to be right there with his leader.

- That is the spirit the young men coming up in this truth should aspire to: cleave to your leadership. It is not being around the longest that qualifies a man to rise up in another's stead; it is being the diligent servant who learned the ins and outs of how his leader was successful, and even observed his leader's mistakes so he knows what not to do in that position.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 6:34](/bible/sirach/6#v34)**
> <sup>[34](/bible/sirach/6#v34)</sup> Stand in the multitude of the elders; and cleave unto him that is wise.

    Do not spend your hours joking with your peers; cleave unto the wise men that sit over you, as Joshua did with Moses.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 6:36](/bible/sirach/6#v36)**
> <sup>[36](/bible/sirach/6#v36)</sup> And if thou seest a man of understanding, get thee betimes unto him, and let thy foot wear the steps of his door.

    Get to him early, because you never know how long that brother will be around; wherever he is, be there to receive whatever knowledge he has to offer.

- Why was Joshua the one anointed? When the Lord told Moses he would see the land but not enter it, Moses did not argue or justify himself. His humble response was to ask the Lord to set a man over the congregation, that the congregation of the Lord be not as sheep which have no shepherd. Moses brought no candidates; he let the Lord pick, and the Lord picked the young man who had cleaved to him: promotion comes from the Lord, and he selects the leaders over his people.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 27:15-17](/bible/numbers/27#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/27#v15)</sup> And Moses spake unto the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/27#v16)</sup> Let the Lord, the God of the spirits of all flesh, set a man over the congregation,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/27#v17)</sup> Which may go out before them, and which may go in before them, and which may lead them out, and which may bring them in; that the congregation of the Lord be not as sheep which have no shepherd.

    Moses asked the Lord to set a man over the congregation to lead them out and bring them in, that Israel be not as sheep without a shepherd.
  - **[Numbers 27:18-20](/bible/numbers/27#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/27#v18)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and lay thine hand upon him;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/27#v19)</sup> And set him before Eleazar the priest, and before all the congregation; and give him a charge in their sight.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/27#v20)</sup> And thou shalt put some of thine honour upon him, that all the congregation of the children of Israel may be obedient.

    The Lord said, Take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit; Moses laid his hand on him, set him before Eleazar and the congregation, and put some of his honour upon him so the people would give Joshua the same respect and be obedient.

- Moses said set a man over the congregation, not a woman, because women were never ordained to lead the children of Israel; that does not fit the divine order of the Most High. Miriam was a prophetess and one of the chief women, and she led the women; women have a real role in the nation, but leading the body is not it. When Miriam got puffed up and spoke against Moses, she was judged.

**[Joshua 1:3-5](/bible/joshua/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/1#v3)</sup> Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon, that have I given unto you, as I said unto Moses.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/1#v4)</sup> From the wilderness and this Lebanon even unto the great river, the river Euphrates, all the land of the Hittites, and unto the great sea toward the going down of the sun, shall be your coast.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/1#v5)</sup> There shall not any man be able to stand before thee all the days of thy life: as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee: I will not fail thee, nor forsake thee.

- Every place that the sole of your foot shall tread upon, that have I given unto you: the Lord let Joshua know that everywhere his foot stepped on this path would belong to the children of Israel, from the wilderness and Lebanon unto the great river Euphrates and unto the great sea toward the going down of the sun. These are the borders of the journey he would lead them through.
- As I was with Moses, so I will be with thee: not any man would be able to stand before Joshua all the days of his life. When the Lord tells you that, you carry the spirit that backs down from nothing.
- This is the same word the Lord foretold to Moses that Joshua now fulfills. The promised land was meant to be as the days of heaven upon the earth, and the condition was diligently keeping all the commandments, to love the Lord, to walk in all his ways, and to cleave unto him; then the Lord would drive out nations greater and mightier than Israel. Heaven was never about everybody being equal and holding hands; heaven is Israel gaining possession and power over the nations, and that has always been the gospel.

  - **[Deuteronomy 11:21-25](/bible/deuteronomy/11#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/11#v21)</sup> That your days may be multiplied, and the days of your children, in the land which the Lord sware unto your fathers to give them, as the days of heaven upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/11#v22)</sup> For if ye shall diligently keep all these commandments which I command you, to do them, to love the Lord your God, to walk in all his ways, and to cleave unto him;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/11#v23)</sup> Then will the Lord drive out all these nations from before you, and ye shall possess greater nations and mightier than yourselves.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/11#v24)</sup> Every place whereon the soles of your feet shall tread shall be yours: from the wilderness and Lebanon, from the river, the river Euphrates, even unto the uttermost sea shall your coast be.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/11#v25)</sup> There shall no man be able to stand before you: for the Lord your God shall lay the fear of you and the dread of you upon all the land that ye shall tread upon, as he hath said unto you.

    Days as the days of heaven upon the earth; if ye diligently keep the commandments, every place whereon the soles of your feet shall tread shall be yours, no man shall be able to stand before you, and the Lord shall lay the fear and dread of you upon all the land, the very promise repeated to Joshua.
  - **[Hebrews 4:2](/bible/hebrews/4#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/4#v2)</sup> For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it.

    The gospel was preached unto them in the wilderness as it is preached unto us: keep the commandments and inherit the kingdom prepared for Israel; under Christ the difference is an eternal reign, with our spirit changed so we cannot transgress.

- The proof that we have not kept the commandments is that we own nothing today. Even land bought and paid for is not truly yours: fail to pay the real estate tax and it is taken by eminent domain and sold at auction. It was never meant to be that way; Israel was supposed to have ownership of the earth.

**[Joshua 1:6-7](/bible/joshua/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/1#v6)</sup> Be strong and of a good courage: for unto this people shalt thou divide for an inheritance the land, which I sware unto their fathers to give them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/1#v7)</sup> Only be thou strong and very courageous, that thou mayest observe to do according to all the law, which Moses my servant commanded thee: turn not from it to the right hand or to the left, that thou mayest prosper whithersoever thou goest.

- The first thing the Lord commands the new leader is to be strong and of a good courage. This truth is not for the weak, not for people easily discouraged when trials and tribulations occur and people bring burdens of matters unto you; you have to be built for it.
- He has to reiterate it: only be thou strong. You are dealing with the Israelites, a stiffnecked, rebellious, cold hearted people, so there is no room for mild moments or weak moments.
- It takes strength to observe to do according to all the law; that is why the scriptures speak of building up your faith and being exercised in the things you learn. You have to be spiritually fit to survive in this truth, especially as a leader. The word for being strong and very courageous is fortitude, one of the virtues that comes with wisdom.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 8:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v7)</sup> And if a man love righteousness her labours are virtues: for she teacheth temperance and prudence, justice and fortitude: which are such things, as men can have nothing more profitable in their life.

    Wisdom teaches fortitude, the very strength and courage the Lord commanded Joshua to have.
  - **[1 Samuel 15:9](/bible/1-samuel/15#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/15#v9)</sup> But Saul and the people spared Agag, and the best of the sheep, and of the oxen, and of the fatlings, and the lambs, and all that was good, and would not utterly destroy them: but every thing that was vile and refuse, that they destroyed utterly.

    Turn not to the right hand or to the left means no deviating from the word and no hunting loopholes; Saul was told to spare nothing, yet he spared Agag and the best of the animals, and it cost him the kingdom.

**[Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

- This verse is the recipe for success, the formula for success, the true prosperity doctrine, and it has three steps: the law in your mouth, meditation day and night, and observing to do all that is written therein.
- This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth: when you open your mouth, what comes out is supposed to be scriptural; your conversation has to be modeled after what this book says. When people leave this truth the Lord removes his words out of their mouth, and their whole conversation changes: nothing is left but slander, falsehood, strange doctrines, and flattering speech.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 119:43](/bible/psalms/119#v43)**

    David prayed, take not the word of truth utterly out of my mouth; do not let me reach the point where I utter foolishness, hatred, and evil communications.
  - **[Psalms 51:11](/bible/psalms/51#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/51#v11)</sup> Cast me not away from thy presence; and take not thy holy spirit from me.

    It is the same prayer as take not thy holy spirit from me; when the Lord removes that spirit, the words of truth leave the conversation with it.
  - **[Romans 16:18](/bible/romans/16#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/16#v18)</sup> For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple.

    Those who serve their own belly deceive the hearts of the simple by good words and fair speeches; it is no longer the book and the straight commandments, just flattery and shade on the next man.
  - **[Psalms 71:24](/bible/psalms/71#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/71#v24)</sup> My tongue also shall talk of thy righteousness all the day long: for they are confounded, for they are brought unto shame, that seek my hurt.

    My tongue shall talk of thy righteousness all the day long: solutions, building one another, correction and exhortation, not backbiting, gossip, and murmuring.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 9:15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)</sup> Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.

    You cannot keep the law in your mouth while conversing with people who know nothing of it; talk with those who love the Lord and have wisdom.

- Thou shalt meditate therein day and night: to meditate is to think deeply and carefully, to contemplate, consider, ponder, and muse. Many read their four chapters a day, listen to videos, and quote their precept lists, but never meditate on what they read. You cannot just slap the butter on the biscuit; you have to let it sink into every layer.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 22:17](/bible/sirach/22#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/22#v17)</sup> A heart settled upon a thought of understanding is as a fair plaistering on the wall of a gallery.

    Meditation is like a portrait in an art gallery: the one who loves it stands and examines a thousand details the surface look never sees; be settled on the scripture, the counsel, and the class the same way.
  - **[Psalms 143:5](/bible/psalms/143#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/143#v5)</sup> I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the work of thy hands.

    I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the work of thy hands: to muse is to be absorbed in thought on what the Most High has done.

- Solomon shows what a companion meditation makes of wisdom: when he came into his house he reposed himself with her, for her conversation has no bitterness and living with her has no sorrow, but mirth and joy. Get in the habit of chilling with wisdom when you get home. When you ponder the whole benefit package that comes with wisdom, you understand that to be allied unto wisdom is immortality; the wiser you get, the more spiritually invincible you become.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 8:1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v1)</sup> Wisdom reacheth from one end to another mightily: and sweetly doth she order all things.

    The chapter speaks of wisdom as a she who orders all things sweetly and mightily.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 8:16-17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v16)</sup> After I am come into mine house, I will repose myself with her: for her conversation hath no bitterness; and to live with her hath no sorrow, but mirth and joy.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v17)</sup> Now when I considered these things in myself, and pondered them in my heart, how that to be allied unto wisdom is immortality;

    Solomon pondered these things in his heart; that pondering is the meditation the Lord commanded Joshua.

- Make meditation a daily custom, a holy habit, as our forefathers did: somewhere alone and peaceful where the mind can be clear, because you cannot meditate in the middle of activity and noise. When you meditate continually on the commandments, the Lord establishes your heart, and the evil thoughts you battle begin to flush out of your mind.

  - **[Genesis 24:63](/bible/genesis/24#v63)**
> <sup>[63](/bible/genesis/24#v63)</sup> And Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide: and he lifted up his eyes, and saw, and, behold, the camels were coming.

    Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide; he had an appointed place and time of day, alone at the going down of the sun, to reflect on the works of the Most High.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 6:37](/bible/sirach/6#v37)**

  - **[Isaiah 26:3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)**

- That thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: the end goal of talking about the commandments and meditating on the commandments is to do the commandments. Attending every class, hearing every breakdown, and keeping every precept list is all in vain if you never do thus saith the Lord; not doing the word of God is why we went into slavery and sit at the bottom of society.

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v19)</sup> And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life: that he may learn to fear the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them:

    Every king of Israel had to write himself a copy of the law and read therein all the days of his life, not just on a sabbath, that he may learn to fear the Lord and keep the words of this law to do them; you are what you eat, and what you put within comes out in your conversation and your actions.

    Be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves; it is applying what you heard that saves your soul, not attendance.

- For then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success: only after the mouth, the meditation, and the doing does prosperity follow. Thus saith God, the second you transgress the commandments of the Lord you cannot prosper, so any pastor teaching prosperity while saying the law is done away with is teaching a lie. Real prosperity never came from sowing a seed into a basket; it comes from obeying the laws of the Most High, and that applies to us as a whole nation of people.

  - **[1 Chronicles 22:13](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v13)**

    Then shalt thou prosper, if thou takest heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments which the Lord charged Moses with concerning Israel; the same charge, be strong and of good courage.
  - **[Psalms 1:1-3](/bible/psalms/1#v1)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/1#v9)</sup> Have not I commanded thee? Be strong and of a good courage; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the Lord thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.

- Have not I commanded thee: not being afraid is a commandment. Fear does not come from the Most High; the only fear he deals with is fearing him and the judgments you suffer for disobeying him. Being scared of what man can do to you is a spirit from the devil, not from God.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Timothy 1:7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)</sup> For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

- Look at the end result of a fearful spirit that is never shaken: the fearful are the first named of those whose part is in the lake which burneth with fire and brimstone. The Lord gave us the spirit of power; as long as you serve and obey him, there is nothing to be scared of.

  Precepts:

    Be strong in the Lord and in the power of his might; that is the spirit given in place of fear.

> <sup>[18](/bible/joshua/4#v18)</sup> And it came to pass, when the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the Lord were come up out of the midst of Jordan, and the soles of the priests’ feet were lifted up unto the dry land, that the waters of Jordan returned unto their place, and flowed over all his banks, as they did before.

- The priests stayed firm at their post in the riverbed even after all the people had passed over, until the Lord spake to Joshua and Joshua gave the order: come ye up out of Jordan. That is discipline, order, fortitude, and faith; they held their place in the midst of a stopped river without wavering.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 14:29-31](/bible/matthew/14#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/14#v29)</sup> And he said, Come. And when Peter was come down out of the ship, he walked on the water, to go to Jesus.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/14#v30)</sup> But when he saw the wind boisterous, he was afraid; and beginning to sink, he cried, saying, Lord, save me.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/14#v31)</sup> And immediately Jesus stretched forth his hand, and caught him, and said unto him, O thou of little faith, wherefore didst thou doubt?

    Peter walked on the water, but as soon as doubt came he began to sink; had the priests doubted and thought the water was about to fall, they could not have stood, but their feet stood firm.

- As soon as the soles of the priests' feet were lifted up unto the dry land, the waters of Jordan returned unto their place and flowed over all his banks as they did before. Everything held its place exactly as long as Israel needed it to.

**[Joshua 4:19-24](/bible/joshua/4#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/4#v19)</sup> And the people came up out of Jordan on the tenth day of the first month, and encamped in Gilgal, in the east border of Jericho.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/4#v20)</sup> And those twelve stones, which they took out of Jordan, did Joshua pitch in Gilgal.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/joshua/4#v21)</sup> And he spake unto the children of Israel, saying, When your children shall ask their fathers in time to come, saying, What mean these stones?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/joshua/4#v22)</sup> Then ye shall let your children know, saying, Israel came over this Jordan on dry land.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/joshua/4#v23)</sup> For the Lord your God dried up the waters of Jordan from before you, until ye were passed over, as the Lord your God did to the Red sea, which he dried up from before us, until we were gone over:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/4#v24)</sup> That all the people of the earth might know the hand of the Lord, that it is mighty: that ye might fear the Lord your God for ever.

- The people came up out of Jordan on the tenth day of the first month and encamped in Gilgal, in the east border of Jericho, and there Joshua pitched the twelve stones they took out of Jordan.
- The fathers were given the answer for their children: Israel came over this Jordan on dry land, for the Lord your God dried up the waters of Jordan from before you until ye were passed over, as the Lord your God did to the Red Sea. The later generation witnessed the same kind of miracle the previous generation saw, because Joshua carried the same spirit Moses had, and the Lord rocked with him the same way.
- The purpose is written: that all the people of the earth might know the hand of the Lord, that it is mighty, that ye might fear the Lord your God for ever. The world was made for Israel's sake; the waters and everything else in the earth answer to what the Most High commands for his people, but when the Lord is not dwelling among Israel, that power is not there. Seeing what he does in the twinkling of an eye is faith building, and it teaches the fear of the Lord.
