**[Joshua 23:1-5](/bible/joshua/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/23#v1)</sup> And it came to pass a long time after that the Lord had given rest unto Israel from all their enemies round about, that Joshua waxed old and stricken in age.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/23#v2)</sup> And Joshua called for all Israel, and for their elders, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers, and said unto them, I am old and stricken in age:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/23#v3)</sup> And ye have seen all that the Lord your God hath done unto all these nations because of you; for the Lord your God is he that hath fought for you.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/23#v4)</sup> Behold, I have divided unto you by lot these nations that remain, to be an inheritance for your tribes, from Jordan, with all the nations that I have cut off, even unto the great sea westward.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/23#v5)</sup> And the Lord your God, he shall expel them from before you, and drive them from out of your sight; and ye shall possess their land, as the Lord your God hath promised unto you.

- A long time after the Most High had given rest unto Israel from all their enemies round about, Joshua waxed old and stricken in age, and he called for all Israel, their elders, heads, judges, and officers to give his last charge.
- Ye have seen all that the LORD your God hath done unto all these nations because of you, for the LORD your God is he that hath fought for you.
- The nations that remained were divided by lot unto the tribes for an inheritance, from Jordan unto the great sea westward, which is the Mediterranean Sea. Those remaining nations belonged to Israel, as in the earlier chapters where they were made tributaries and served Israel.
- The promise still ran ahead of them: the LORD your God, he shall expel them from before you and drive them from out of your sight, and ye shall possess their land, as the LORD your God hath promised unto you.

**[Joshua 23:6-8](/bible/joshua/23#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/23#v6)</sup> Be ye therefore very courageous to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, that ye turn not aside therefrom to the right hand or to the left;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/23#v7)</sup> That ye come not among these nations, these that remain among you; neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow yourselves unto them:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/23#v8)</sup> But cleave unto the Lord your God, as ye have done unto this day.

- Be ye therefore very courageous to keep and to do all that is written in the book of the law of Moses, turning not aside therefrom to the right hand or to the left.
- Come not among these nations that remain among you; neither make mention of the name of their gods, nor cause to swear by them, neither serve them, nor bow yourselves unto them. Israel was not to go off into idolatry and worship the gods of the nations round about; that is what those nations do, not what Israel does.
- But cleave unto the LORD your God, as ye have done unto this day.

**[Joshua 23:9-10](/bible/joshua/23#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/23#v9)</sup> For the Lord hath driven out from before you great nations and strong: but as for you, no man hath been able to stand before you unto this day.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/23#v10)</sup> One man of you shall chase a thousand: for the Lord your God, he it is that fighteth for you, as he hath promised you.

- The Most High drove out great and strong nations from before Israel; no man had been able to stand before them unto that day.
- One man of you shall chase a thousand: for the LORD your God, he it is that fighteth for you, as he hath promised you. Picture one man beating up a thousand; you cannot do that unless God is with you, and that is what it looked like.

**[Joshua 23:11-13](/bible/joshua/23#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/23#v11)</sup> Take good heed therefore unto yourselves, that ye love the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/23#v12)</sup> Else if ye do in any wise go back, and cleave unto the remnant of these nations, even these that remain among you, and shall make marriages with them, and go in unto them, and they to you:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/23#v13)</sup> Know for a certainty that the Lord your God will no more drive out any of these nations from before you; but they shall be snares and traps unto you, and scourges in your sides, and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off this good land which the Lord your God hath given you.

- Take good heed therefore unto yourselves, that ye love the LORD your God.
- The warning is plain: if ye go back and cleave unto the remnant of these nations and make marriages with them, know for a certainty that the Most High will no more drive out any of these nations from before you; they shall be snares and traps unto you, scourges in your sides and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off this good land.
- This is the same thing that happened at Baalpeor, when Israel went and mingled with the women of Moab and then worshipped their gods. The warning here is do not do that thing.

**[Joshua 23:14-16](/bible/joshua/23#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/23#v14)</sup> And, behold, this day I am going the way of all the earth: and ye know in all your hearts and in all your souls, that not one thing hath failed of all the good things which the Lord your God spake concerning you; all are come to pass unto you, and not one thing hath failed thereof.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/23#v15)</sup> Therefore it shall come to pass, that as all good things are come upon you, which the Lord your God promised you; so shall the Lord bring upon you all evil things, until he have destroyed you from off this good land which the Lord your God hath given you.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/joshua/23#v16)</sup> When ye have transgressed the covenant of the Lord your God, which he commanded you, and have gone and served other gods, and bowed yourselves to them; then shall the anger of the Lord be kindled against you, and ye shall perish quickly from off the good land which he hath given unto you.

- Joshua, going the way of all the earth, made Israel acknowledge in all their hearts and souls that not one thing had failed of all the good things which the Lord spake concerning them; all came to pass.
- The other side of that is a heavy scripture: therefore as all good things are come upon you, so shall the LORD bring upon you all evil things, until he have destroyed you from off this good land. Many come out of Christianity being told God is good all the time, yet the scripture says plainly that the Most High will destroy you when you go off, because that also is what he promised.
- When ye have transgressed the covenant and served other gods, ye shall perish quickly from off the good land. That is exactly why Israel is not at home right now; we did exactly that. And that is what the waking up is for today: the classes going out all day, the teaching in the streets, trying to bring our people back to the laws of God.

    As the Lord rejoiced over you to do you good, so the Lord will rejoice over you to destroy you, and ye shall be plucked from off the land whither thou goest to possess it.
