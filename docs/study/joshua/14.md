**[Joshua 14:1-5](/bible/joshua/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/14#v1)</sup> And these are the countries which the children of Israel inherited in the land of Canaan, which Eleazar the priest, and Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel, distributed for inheritance to them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/14#v2)</sup> By lot was their inheritance, as the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes, and for the half tribe.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/14#v3)</sup> For Moses had given the inheritance of two tribes and an half tribe on the other side Jordan: but unto the Levites he gave none inheritance among them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/14#v4)</sup> For the children of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim: therefore they gave no part unto the Levites in the land, save cities to dwell in, with their suburbs for their cattle and for their substance.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/14#v5)</sup> As the Lord commanded Moses, so the children of Israel did, and they divided the land.

- The countries in the land of Canaan were distributed by Eleazar the priest, Joshua the son of Nun, and the heads of the fathers of the tribes, by lot, as the Most High commanded by the hand of Moses, for the nine tribes and the half tribe; Moses had already given the two tribes and a half their inheritance on the other side Jordan.
- The children of Joseph were two tribes, Manasseh and Ephraim, therefore no part was given unto the Levites in the land, save cities to dwell in with their suburbs for their cattle and for their substance.
- The Levites were living in the suburbs, which is the towns, right among the people. They did not have a land designated to themselves; they were spread throughout all the many tribes.

**[Joshua 14:6-9](/bible/joshua/14#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/14#v6)</sup> Then the children of Judah came unto Joshua in Gilgal: and Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenezite said unto him, Thou knowest the thing that the Lord said unto Moses the man of God concerning me and thee in Kadesh–barnea.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/14#v7)</sup> Forty years old was I when Moses the servant of the Lord sent me from Kadesh–barnea to espy out the land; and I brought him word again as it was in mine heart.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/14#v8)</sup> Nevertheless my brethren that went up with me made the heart of the people melt: but I wholly followed the Lord my God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/14#v9)</sup> And Moses sware on that day, saying, Surely the land whereon thy feet have trodden shall be thine inheritance, and thy children’s for ever, because thou hast wholly followed the Lord my God.

- Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenezite comes to Joshua in Gilgal and reminds him of the thing the Most High said unto Moses concerning them both in Kadeshbarnea: Caleb was forty years old when Moses sent him from Kadeshbarnea to spy out the land, and he brought word again as it was in his heart.
- His brethren that went up with him made the heart of the people melt, but he wholly followed the Lord his God. The men that went with him brought up an evil report, made the people afraid, and made them doubt.
- Caleb was not playing any games; he was about that business, ready to go and take the land, because he understood the Most High had already promised us the land, so he had no fear. That is the spirit that says, let us go up at once and possess it.
- So Moses sware on that day that the land whereon Caleb's feet had trodden would be his inheritance and his children's for ever, because he wholly followed the Lord.

**[Joshua 14:10-11](/bible/joshua/14#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/14#v10)</sup> And now, behold, the Lord hath kept me alive, as he said, these forty and five years, even since the Lord spake this word unto Moses, while the children of Israel wandered in the wilderness: and now, lo, I am this day fourscore and five years old.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/14#v11)</sup> As yet I am as strong this day as I was in the day that Moses sent me: as my strength was then, even so is my strength now, for war, both to go out, and to come in.

- The Most High has kept Caleb alive these forty and five years, and now at fourscore and five years old he is as strong this day as he was in the day Moses sent him; as his strength was then, even so is his strength now, for war, both to go out and to come in. This man is eighty-five years old and in perfect health.
- Moses was the same way: a hundred and twenty years old with the strength and the vision of a young man. A lot of us are not living to a hundred and twenty, but at the same time we are making it to sixty and seventy, and at fifty we are hooked up on machines. We are all going to die at some point, but that does not mean you have to be miserable, sitting there hooked up to all kind of machines while you are here.
- Leadership has been pushing that we all need to take care of our bodies and our health. A lot of us deal with gluttony even though it is not a topic we talk about all the time; cut down a little what you eat and eat better food.
- The same way we make haste to stop the different sins, be quick to change the diet. If we make haste to change those things, the Most High will also keep away that continual sickness, the diabetes and everything causing our limbs to be cut off just to live.
