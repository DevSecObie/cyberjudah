**[Joshua 24:1-3](/bible/joshua/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/24#v1)</sup> And Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, and called for the elders of Israel, and for their heads, and for their judges, and for their officers; and they presented themselves before God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/24#v2)</sup> And Joshua said unto all the people, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Your fathers dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time, even Terah, the father of Abraham, and the father of Nachor: and they served other gods.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/24#v3)</sup> And I took your father Abraham from the other side of the flood, and led him throughout all the land of Canaan, and multiplied his seed, and gave him Isaac.

- Joshua gathered all the tribes of Israel to Shechem, with the elders, heads, judges, and officers, and they presented themselves before God for the covenant.
- The history begins on the other side of the flood: your fathers dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time, even Terah the father of Abraham and the father of Nachor, and they served other gods.
- Abraham was chosen out of his family: the Most High took him from the other side of the flood, led him throughout all the land of Canaan, multiplied his seed, and gave him Isaac. Abraham was told to get up and leave his family and follow God, and he did just that.

**[Joshua 24:4-7](/bible/joshua/24#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/24#v4)</sup> And I gave unto Isaac Jacob and Esau: and I gave unto Esau mount Seir, to possess it; but Jacob and his children went down into Egypt.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/24#v5)</sup> I sent Moses also and Aaron, and I plagued Egypt, according to that which I did among them: and afterward I brought you out.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/24#v6)</sup> And I brought your fathers out of Egypt: and ye came unto the sea; and the Egyptians pursued after your fathers with chariots and horsemen unto the Red sea.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/24#v7)</sup> And when they cried unto the Lord, he put darkness between you and the Egyptians, and brought the sea upon them, and covered them; and your eyes have seen what I have done in Egypt: and ye dwelt in the wilderness a long season.

- Unto Isaac were given Jacob and Esau; Esau received mount Seir to possess it, but Jacob and his children went down into Egypt. A whole lot of time passes in just those few verses.
- The Most High sent Moses and Aaron, plagued Egypt, and brought Israel out; and when the Egyptians pursued with chariots and horsemen unto the Red sea, he put darkness between Israel and the Egyptians and brought the sea upon them and covered them.
- Your eyes have seen what I have done in Egypt: the ones under twenty years old who went in with Joshua and Caleb saw these things as children, and now they were grown with children of their own, being reminded of what they saw with their own eyes.
- Ye dwelt in the wilderness a long season: that long season was the forty years.

**[Joshua 24:8-10](/bible/joshua/24#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/24#v8)</sup> And I brought you into the land of the Amorites, which dwelt on the other side Jordan; and they fought with you: and I gave them into your hand, that ye might possess their land; and I destroyed them from before you.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/24#v9)</sup> Then Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and warred against Israel, and sent and called Balaam the son of Beor to curse you:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/24#v10)</sup> But I would not hearken unto Balaam; therefore he blessed you still: so I delivered you out of his hand.

- The Most High brought Israel into the land of the Amorites on the other side Jordan, and they fought with Israel, and he gave them into Israel's hand and destroyed them from before them; that history is in [Numbers 21](/bible/numbers/21).
- Then Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, arose and warred against Israel, and sent and called Balaam the son of Beor to curse Israel; that is [Numbers 22](/bible/numbers/22), read only days before this class.
- But I would not hearken unto Balaam; therefore he blessed you still: so I delivered you out of his hand.

**[Joshua 24:11-13](/bible/joshua/24#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/24#v11)</sup> And ye went over Jordan, and came unto Jericho: and the men of Jericho fought against you, the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Girgashites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; and I delivered them into your hand.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/24#v12)</sup> And I sent the hornet before you, which drave them out from before you, even the two kings of the Amorites; but not with thy sword, nor with thy bow.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/24#v13)</sup> And I have given you a land for which ye did not labour, and cities which ye built not, and ye dwell in them; of the vineyards and oliveyards which ye planted not do ye eat.

- Israel went over Jordan unto Jericho, and the men of Jericho and the seven nations fought against them, and the Most High delivered them into Israel's hand.
- He sent the hornet before them, which drave out the two kings of the Amorites from before them, but not with thy sword, nor with thy bow. God was doing the fighting; great nations bigger and more than Israel fell because the Most High was with them.
- I have given you a land for which ye did not labour, and cities which ye built not, and ye dwell in them; of the vineyards and oliveyards which ye planted not do ye eat. Israel took over everything the nations had in those lands, the ships, the vineyards, the merchandise, and became the merchants of the land; it was theirs, because it was promised.

    > <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/23#v28)</sup> And I will send hornets before thee, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee.

    I will send hornets before thee, which shall drive out the Hivite, the Canaanite, and the Hittite, from before thee.

**[Joshua 24:14-15](/bible/joshua/24#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/24#v14)</sup> Now therefore fear the Lord, and serve him in sincerity and in truth: and put away the gods which your fathers served on the other side of the flood, and in Egypt; and serve ye the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/24#v15)</sup> And if it seem evil unto you to serve the Lord, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.

- Now therefore fear the LORD, and serve him in sincerity and in truth, and put away the gods which your fathers served on the other side of the flood, and in Egypt.
- Choose you this day whom ye will serve; but as for me and my house, we will serve the LORD. A lot of people hang that quote up in the house and do not keep one law; everybody reads this verse and reads nothing before it about being put to death for going off. Lord willing they have space and time to repent.
- Moses said the same thing: he set life and death, blessing and cursing before Israel and said choose life. [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) laid it out, blessings in verses 1 through 14 if Israel obeyed, to be above all people and rule; curses from 15 down to 68 if not. Israel chose the cursing and the death, and that is what we are in now, trying to come back and get out of it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 30:19-20](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)</sup> I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v20)</sup> That thou mayest love the Lord thy God, and that thou mayest obey his voice, and that thou mayest cleave unto him: for he is thy life, and the length of thy days: that thou mayest dwell in the land which the Lord sware unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob, to give them.

    I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing; therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live, that thou mayest love the Lord thy God, obey his voice, and cleave unto him.

**[Joshua 24:16-18](/bible/joshua/24#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/joshua/24#v16)</sup> And the people answered and said, God forbid that we should forsake the Lord, to serve other gods;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/joshua/24#v17)</sup> For the Lord our God, he it is that brought us up and our fathers out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage, and which did those great signs in our sight, and preserved us in all the way wherein we went, and among all the people through whom we passed:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/joshua/24#v18)</sup> And the Lord drave out from before us all the people, even the Amorites which dwelt in the land: therefore will we also serve the Lord; for he is our God.

- The people answered, God forbid that we should forsake the LORD, to serve other gods.
- For the LORD our God, he it is that brought us up and our fathers out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage: Egypt is the house of bondage, and he did those great signs in Israel's sight and preserved them in all the way they went.
- Therefore will we also serve the LORD; for he is our God.

**[Joshua 24:19-20](/bible/joshua/24#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/24#v19)</sup> And Joshua said unto the people, Ye cannot serve the Lord: for he is an holy God; he is a jealous God; he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/24#v20)</sup> If ye forsake the Lord, and serve strange gods, then he will turn and do you hurt, and consume you, after that he hath done you good.

- Joshua said unto the people, Ye cannot serve the LORD: for he is an holy God; he is a jealous God; he will not forgive your transgressions nor your sins.
- If ye forsake the LORD, and serve strange gods, then he will turn and do you hurt, and consume you, after that he hath done you good. Everything he promised for good had come to pass and Israel was on top in the land; all they had to do was keep the commandments. Going off meant being taken out of that land into captivity, and because that is what Israel decided to do, that is the predicament we are in today.

**[Joshua 24:21-25](/bible/joshua/24#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/joshua/24#v21)</sup> And the people said unto Joshua, Nay; but we will serve the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/joshua/24#v22)</sup> And Joshua said unto the people, Ye are witnesses against yourselves that ye have chosen you the Lord, to serve him. And they said, We are witnesses.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/joshua/24#v23)</sup> Now therefore put away, said he, the strange gods which are among you, and incline your heart unto the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/24#v24)</sup> And the people said unto Joshua, The Lord our God will we serve, and his voice will we obey.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/joshua/24#v25)</sup> So Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and set them a statute and an ordinance in Shechem.

- The people said unto Joshua, Nay; but we will serve the LORD. It is the same thing Israel told Moses back then; we came into that covenant again, this time without the blood.
- Joshua said, Ye are witnesses against yourselves that ye have chosen you the LORD, to serve him; and they said, We are witnesses. Everybody was in full agreement.
- Put away the strange gods which are among you, and incline your heart unto the LORD God of Israel; and the people said, The LORD our God will we serve, and his voice will we obey.
- So Joshua made a covenant with the people that day, and set them a statute and an ordinance in Shechem, the same thing Moses his predecessor did at the mountain; Joshua was his successor and did the same, though without the blood.
