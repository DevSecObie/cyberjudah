## Introduction

This session covered [Joshua 5](/bible/joshua/5) through 8: the circumcision of the wilderness generation at Gilgal and what the uncircumcised heart means, the Captain of the host of the Lord, the fall of Jericho and the faith of Rahab, the accursed thing and the sin of Achan, the taking of Ai, and the blessings and cursings read at mount Ebal. By way of recap of chapters 1 through 4: after the death of Moses, Joshua was set over the host of Israel, the Lord dried up the river Jordan so the people crossed on dry ground, and the spies Joshua sent had linked up with Rahab the harlot in Jericho. Rahab worked as counterintelligence for Israel; she dwelt on the side of the wall in the poor quarter of the city, and such people know the secret goings-on of a city, so she gave the spies information to carry back to Joshua.

**[Joshua 5:1](/bible/joshua/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/5#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when all the kings of the Amorites, which were on the side of Jordan westward, and all the kings of the Canaanites, which were by the sea, heard that the Lord had dried up the waters of Jordan from before the children of Israel, until we were passed over, that their heart melted, neither was there spirit in them any more, because of the children of Israel.

- The kings of the Amorites and the Canaanites heard that the Lord had dried up the waters of Jordan from before the children of Israel, and their heart melted: they were paralyzed with fear.
- They had heard how the Most High split the Red sea in Exodus, and now he had done it again at Jordan, so neither was there spirit in them any more because of the children of Israel.

**[Joshua 5:2-3](/bible/joshua/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/5#v2)</sup> At that time the Lord said unto Joshua, Make thee sharp knives, and circumcise again the children of Israel the second time.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/5#v3)</sup> And Joshua made him sharp knives, and circumcised the children of Israel at the hill of the foreskins.

- Circumcise again the children of Israel the second time: the children of Israel had not been keeping the commandments, being stiffnecked and hardheaded, and the Lord told Joshua to fix them.
- Circumcision is the covenant the Most High instituted with our forefather Abraham: every man child circumcised at eight days old, and the servant born in the house or bought with money circumcised likewise and keeping the laws as well.
- When Abraham received the commandment he performed it, circumcising every male of his house the selfsame day; he showed the Lord by his actions that he believed. So the men in Joshua who had not been circumcised showed a form of repentance by applying the law of circumcision.

- The eighth day is the wisdom of the Most High for the body: before then a baby's blood has not begun to coagulate as it needs to, and the child could bleed out; by the eighth day he is strong enough to withstand the process and heal.
- There are physical benefits to the law: when the foreskin remains, moisture gets trapped and creates an environment for bacteria, raising the risk of infection and urinary tract infections, and being circumcised even reduces a wife's risk of cervical cancer. This is the Most High as a Father giving his children counsel on how to live and stay clear of disease.
- The physical circumcision is symbolic of spiritual circumcision. The heart in scripture is the mind, for out of the heart of men proceed evil thoughts; sin is what makes a man dirty, nasty, and filthy, so the uncircumcised heart is the mind that will not keep the commandments.
- No surgery can circumcise the brain: circumcision of the heart is repentance. You wash you and make you clean by ceasing to do evil, and a young man cleanses his way by taking heed thereto according to the word of the Most High.

**[Joshua 5:4-6](/bible/joshua/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/5#v4)</sup> And this is the cause why Joshua did circumcise: All the people that came out of Egypt, that were males, even all the men of war, died in the wilderness by the way, after they came out of Egypt.

- This is the cause of the circumcision: all the men of war that came out of Egypt died in the wilderness because they obeyed not the voice of the Lord, and the generation born by the way had not been circumcised.
- That they had not circumcised themselves means they did not obey the voice of the Lord: they should have been circumcised physically and also mentally and spiritually.
- The land that floweth with milk and honey is understood by the Bible's own dictionary, precept upon precept: the rebels in Numbers called Egypt the land flowing with milk and honey, and Israel was brought out of the land of Egypt, so the phrase describes a land abundant in food. The milk goes into your cattle and your meats, and the honey goes into your vegetation.
- The mixed multitude and the children of Israel wept for the fish, cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic they ate in Egypt: they were comfortable in their oppression and did not understand they were not free, the same mindset among our people in captivity today who say they are free and living their best life.
- The honey teaching carries a dietary lesson: hast thou found honey, eat so much as is sufficient for thee. Do not overeat; balance your salts, sugars, meats, and dainties moderately.

- The man answered, Nay; but as captain of the host of the Lord am I now come. Host means army: he is the captain, the leader, of the army of the Lord.
- When Joshua realized who stood before him, the fear came: he fell on his face to the earth and did worship, and the leader of Israel's army called himself a servant, saying, What saith my lord unto his servant? Joshua had cleaved to Moses and understood who the captain of the host is.
- The captain of the host of the Lord is Christ, the captain of our salvation. This is why Joshua was afraid: the return of Christ is not the soft picture Christianity paints, for he is not coming back made like unto his brethren as at the first, but with the power of the living God, to take vengeance and destroy. No missiles or fighter jets will do anything against him.

# Joshua 6: The Fall of Jericho and the Faith of Rahab

Read the chapter → [Joshua 6](/bible/joshua/6)

**[Joshua 6:1-2](/bible/joshua/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/6#v1)</sup> Now Jericho was straitly shut up because of the children of Israel: none went out, and none came in.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/6#v2)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, See, I have given into thine hand Jericho, and the king thereof, and the mighty men of valour.

- Jericho was straitly shut up because of the children of Israel: none went out and none came in. Jericho had a military, and a garrison, a military base, was built there, with the walls shut so nobody could enter or leave.
- The Lord said unto Joshua, See, I have given into thine hand Jericho, and the king thereof, and the mighty men of valour: the battle was already decided.

**[Joshua 6:3-5](/bible/joshua/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/6#v3)</sup> And ye shall compass the city, all ye men of war, and go round about the city once. Thus shalt thou do six days.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/6#v4)</sup> And seven priests shall bear before the ark seven trumpets of rams’ horns: and the seventh day ye shall compass the city seven times, and the priests shall blow with the trumpets.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/6#v5)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that when they make a long blast with the ram’s horn, and when ye hear the sound of the trumpet, all the people shall shout with a great shout; and the wall of the city shall fall down flat, and the people shall ascend up every man straight before him.

- The battle plan came from the Lord: compass the city with all the men of war once a day for six days, seven priests bearing seven trumpets of rams' horns before the ark; on the seventh day compass the city seven times, and at the long blast all the people shout with a great shout, and the wall shall fall down flat.
- The blowing of the trumpets activates the armies of the Lord, the angels, to help in battle. Remember Elisha's servant: when his eyes were opened he saw the host of angels that were there warring and protecting Israel.
- This is the spirit behind the men of Israel rehearsing the righteous acts with trumpets and shofars today, at the camps and at the new moons; it is spiritual and an act of faith.

**[Joshua 6:12-14](/bible/joshua/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/6#v12)</sup> And Joshua rose early in the morning, and the priests took up the ark of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/6#v13)</sup> And seven priests bearing seven trumpets of rams’ horns before the ark of the Lord went on continually, and blew with the trumpets: and the armed men went before them; but the rereward came after the ark of the Lord, the priests going on, and blowing with the trumpets.
>

- They repeated the act six days back to back, rising early, the priests bearing the ark and blowing the trumpets, the armed men before and the rereward after, one compass of the city each day.
- Every army in the world stole this from the Israelites: the pass and review of the militaries, with their battalions and units, their guidons with the eagle on the tip of the spear, marching round the field sounding off with cadences and trumpets, is patterned after what Israel did here.

- On the seventh day they rose about the dawning of the day and compassed the city seven times. There is something about the number seven that the Lord loves; you read about it in Revelation.
- At the seventh time, when the priests blew with the trumpets, Joshua said unto the people, Shout; for the Lord hath given you the city.
- The men apply [Joshua 6](/bible/joshua/6) to this day: when a scoffer will not hear the word and thinks to outcry the truth, the brothers say the scripture together with one mind, one body, one spirit. That unity is spiritual, a rehearsal of the righteous acts.
- Unity and order are what the enemies fear: not gangs and drug dealing, but the so-called Black man, Hispanic man, and Native American man unified in the order of a military, with structure and organization. That is what puts fear in the nations.

**[Joshua 6:17-19](/bible/joshua/6#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/joshua/6#v17)</sup> And the city shall be accursed, even it, and all that are therein, to the Lord: only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers that we sent.

- The city shall be accursed, even it and all that are therein, to the Lord: only Rahab the harlot shall live, she and all that are with her in the house, because she hid the messengers. She made a covenant with the spies: she would conceal them and assist Israel, knowing the Lord was with Israel, as long as her family was saved when the city fell.
- Rahab was a Canaanite, a heathen, yet the heathen can be blessed when they do it to benefit Israel; blessed is he that blesseth thee.
- Rahab is the scripture's own example that a man is justified by works and not by faith only: faith and works go hand in hand, for your actions prove whether you believe. Was not Rahab the harlot justified by works, when she had received the messengers and sent them out another way? As the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.
- Works by the Bible's own dictionary is keeping his commandments: set your hope in God, forget not his works, but keep his commandments. Words say a lot, but actions always speak louder, and by him actions are weighed.
- Keep yourselves from the accursed thing, lest ye make yourselves accursed and make the camp of Israel a curse, and trouble it: your individual sins affect the whole nation of Israel, your brothers, your sisters, and the children. That is why we must examine ourselves whether we be in the faith.
- All the silver and gold and vessels of brass and iron were consecrated unto the Lord, separated and sanctified for his treasury alone: nobody was to gain personally from this war.

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/8#v14)</sup> And it came to pass, when the king of Ai saw it, that they hasted and rose up early, and the men of the city went out against Israel to battle, he and all his people, at a time appointed, before the plain; but he wist not that there were liers in ambush against him behind the city.

- All the people in Ai were called together to pursue, and they were drawn away from the city: not a man was left in Ai or Bethel that went not out after Israel, and they left the city open.

- The men of Ai were in the midst of Israel, some on this side and some on that side, and Israel smote them until none remained or escaped; they were surrounded, exactly as drawn up. The king of Ai they took alive and brought him to Joshua.

**[Joshua 8:24-26](/bible/joshua/8#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/8#v24)</sup> And it came to pass, when Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field, in the wilderness wherein they chased them, and when they were all fallen on the edge of the sword, until they were consumed, that all the Israelites returned unto Ai, and smote it with the edge of the sword.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/joshua/8#v25)</sup> And so it was, that all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai.

> <sup>[26](/bible/joshua/8#v26)</sup> For Joshua drew not his hand back, wherewith he stretched out the spear, until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai.

- When Israel had made an end of slaying all the inhabitants of Ai in the field and in the wilderness, all that fell that day, both of men and women, were twelve thousand, even all the men of Ai. Women are casualties of war.
- That point stands as a warning for the sisters serving unrepented in the militaries of the nations, standing stiffly for the oppressor: when Christ comes back he is coming to kill, and a uniform and a weapon will not save; men and women will be dying alike. It is best in these last days for a woman to stand by and support her own man and nation, not another nation.
- Joshua drew not his hand back until he had utterly destroyed all the inhabitants of Ai, meaning the children were put to death also. This was not new: at Sihon's cities Israel utterly destroyed the men, the women, and the little ones. The children's song teaching that Christ loves all the children of every nation alike does not stand with these scriptures; the Lord's love is toward the children of Israel.

**[Joshua 8:30-32](/bible/joshua/8#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/joshua/8#v30)</sup> Then Joshua built an altar unto the Lord God of Israel in mount Ebal,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/joshua/8#v31)</sup> As Moses the servant of the Lord commanded the children of Israel, as it is written in the book of the law of Moses, an altar of whole stones, over which no man hath lift up any iron: and they offered thereon burnt offerings unto the Lord, and sacrificed peace offerings.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/joshua/8#v32)</sup> And he wrote there upon the stones a copy of the law of Moses, which he wrote in the presence of the children of Israel.

- Then Joshua built an altar unto the Lord God of Israel in mount Ebal, as Moses commanded and as it is written in the book of the law: an altar of whole stones, over which no man hath lift up any iron; and they offered burnt offerings and sacrificed peace offerings, and he wrote upon the stones a copy of the law of Moses.
- We are still under the law of sacrifice today, but the sacrifice is your body, not the blood of bulls and goats: you sacrifice time, money, pain, and labor to push this truth and do the work, spiritual sacrifices through your works by keeping the laws.
- The peace offering today is keeping the commandments: he that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough, and he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering. Under grace, peace is a dispensation: the more sin you commit, the less peace you will have, so keep the commandments and do not frustrate the grace and peace the Most High has with you.
- The Most High hath a controversy with the inhabitants of the land; if you do not want the Lord to have a controversy with you, keep the laws. And keeping the laws is more than beards, fringes, borders of blue, and not eating pork: it is purging the unfruitful spirits you know dwell inside you, the jealousy, envy, covetousness, malice, hatred, grudging, backbiting, murmuring, and gossip.

- Rehearse means practice, and practice makes perfect: there are rehearsals every day, classes and meetings for the men, the women, and the children, because this is not our rest.

>
> <sup>[35](/bible/joshua/8#v35)</sup> There was not a word of all that Moses commanded, which Joshua read not before all the congregation of Israel, with the women, and the little ones, and the strangers that were conversant among them.

- Afterward he read all the words of the law, the blessings and cursings, according to all that is written in the book of the law. We tell our neighbor God bless you, but by the same book the Most High and Christ will curse you too if you do not keep the laws.
- Heaven and earth are called to record: everything we do is recorded, and life and death, blessing and cursing, are set before us. Life is the keeping of the commandments of the Most High, and death is the wages of sin; it is the one thing left optional, so choose life, for it is not just you that pays but thy seed as well.
- How do you become blessed? Read the last page of the book: blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life. A few verses before your Bible says the end, it says keep the commandments, so no man in his right mind can say the laws are done away with; that is justifying wickedness. Keep the sabbath holy, leave the swine alone, dress in modesty, and keep the commandments if you want the blessings.
