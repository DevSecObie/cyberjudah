## Introduction

This session covers [Joshua 17](/bible/joshua/17) through 20 verse by verse: the lot of the tribe of Manasseh, the seven tribes still waiting for their inheritance and the lots cast at Shiloh, the portions of Benjamin, Simeon, Zebulun, Issachar, Asher, Naphtali, and Dan, and the cities of refuge with the laws of the slayer. Bible maps of the conquest of the land of Canaan are used throughout to bring the scriptures to life, so we can see where our forefathers and foremothers, the real twelve tribes of Israel, lived in the land.

**[Joshua 17:1](/bible/joshua/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/17#v1)</sup> There was also a lot for the tribe of Manasseh; for he was the firstborn of Joseph; to wit, for Machir the firstborn of Manasseh, the father of Gilead: because he was a man of war, therefore he had Gilead and Bashan.

- A lot goes into an allotment, a portion; there was a portion for the tribe of Manasseh, for he was the firstborn of Joseph. Machir, the firstborn of Manasseh and the father of Gilead, was a man of war, therefore he had Gilead and Bashan.
- Bashan was the kingdom of Og, one of the giants that Israel conquered under Joshua. Before the conquest all this territory was called the land of Canaan, with the Canaanites, the Perizzites, and the Jebusites and their families dwelling in it.
- Prior to this chapter Judah received his inheritance in chapter 15 and the inheritance of Joseph, Ephraim, came in chapter 16; after the victories the Most High gave through Christ, the land was divided by lot so that every tribe had its portion.
- The Bible maps that even the enemies of Israel put together according to the scriptures show this history: Manasseh held Bashan and a portion by Gilead on the other side of Jordan. The so-called Cubans come from this tribe; this is their history that their enemies do not want them to know.

**[Joshua 17:2](/bible/joshua/17#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/17#v2)</sup> There was also a lot for the rest of the children of Manasseh by their families; for the children of Abiezer, and for the children of Helek, and for the children of Asriel, and for the children of Shechem, and for the children of Hepher, and for the children of Shemida: these were the male children of Manasseh the son of Joseph by their families.

- There was also a lot for the rest of the children of Manasseh by their families, Abiezer, Helek, Asriel, Shechem, Hepher, and Shemida: these were the male children of Manasseh the son of Joseph.
- Remember that Manasseh was the son of Joseph; Joseph had two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh.

- They came near before Eleazar the priest, before Joshua the son of Nun, and before the princes, saying the Lord commanded Moses to give them an inheritance among their brethren; and according to the commandment of the Lord he gave them an inheritance among the brethren of their father.

**[Joshua 17:5-6](/bible/joshua/17#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/17#v5)</sup> And there fell ten portions to Manasseh, beside the land of Gilead and Bashan, which were on the other side Jordan;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/17#v6)</sup> Because the daughters of Manasseh had an inheritance among his sons: and the rest of Manasseh’s sons had the land of Gilead.

- Ten portions fell to Manasseh, beside the land of Gilead and Bashan which were on the other side of Jordan. On the map the Jordan river flows down through the land, and across the river sat Gilead and Bashan, the lands Manasseh already held.
- Down south the Jordan empties into the Dead Sea, and Sodom and Gomorrah sat south of the Dead Sea, by where Edom was residing; the maps give a good feel for what the Bible is talking about.
- The daughters of Manasseh had an inheritance among his sons, just as the Lord commanded for the daughters of Zelophehad, and the rest of Manasseh's sons had the land of Gilead.

**[Joshua 17:7-8](/bible/joshua/17#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/17#v7)</sup> And the coast of Manasseh was from Asher to Michmethah, that lieth before Shechem; and the border went along on the right hand unto the inhabitants of En–tappuah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/17#v8)</sup> Now Manasseh had the land of Tappuah: but Tappuah on the border of Manasseh belonged to the children of Ephraim;

- The coast of Manasseh ran from Asher to Michmethah; Manasseh had the land of Tappuah, but Tappuah itself on the border of Manasseh belonged to the children of Ephraim.
- We have to know where our forefathers came from and what the Bible is speaking about; that is why we search out these borders on the maps, where Tappuah sat on the southern border by Shechem.

- The coast descended to the river Kanah: southward of the river the cities were Ephraim's, and northward it was Manasseh's, the sea was his border, and they met together in Asher on the north and in Issachar on the east.
- This land of Canaan is also known as Israel, and this is where our foremothers and forefathers lived: the so-called Mexicans, Guatemalans, and Hondurans lived here, and the so-called Brazilians, Colombians, and Venezuelans up in these coasts. A lot of times we read that Asher lived here and forget it is talking about us; our people need the visual.
- Manasseh also had within Issachar and Asher the cities of Beth-shean, Ibleam, Dor, En-dor, Taanach, and Megiddo with their towns, even three countries.

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/17#v14)</sup> And the children of Joseph spake unto Joshua, saying, Why hast thou given me but one lot and one portion to inherit, seeing I am a great people, forasmuch as the Lord hath blessed me hitherto?

- The children of Joseph, led by Ephraim, asked Joshua why he gave them but one lot to inherit, seeing they are a great people; great here means numerous. Ephraim and Manasseh were among the most populated tribes, and Ephraim's own portion had already been given back in chapter 16.
- Their fruitfulness was prophesied when Israel blessed Joseph; the very name Ephraim goes into being fruitful.

- The children of Joseph answered that the hill is not enough for them, and the Canaanites that dwell in the land of the valley have chariots of iron; those nations had military power too.
- On the map those Canaanites sat right north of Manasseh, in Beth-shean and her towns and in the valley of Jezreel, dwelling in the midst of our brothers with an army; they were a problem, and Manasseh was already having a hard time with them.

# Joshua 18: The Lots Cast at Shiloh

Read the chapter → [Joshua 18](/bible/joshua/18)

**[Joshua 18:1-2](/bible/joshua/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/18#v1)</sup> And the whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at Shiloh, and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there. And the land was subdued before them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/18#v2)</sup> And there remained among the children of Israel seven tribes, which had not yet received their inheritance.

- The whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at Shiloh and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there, and the land was subdued before them: they won the war, and the land was now in their control.
- There remained among the children of Israel seven tribes which had not yet received their inheritance.

**[Joshua 18:3](/bible/joshua/18#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/18#v3)</sup> And Joshua said unto the children of Israel, How long are ye slack to go to possess the land, which the Lord God of your fathers hath given you?

- How long are ye slack to go possess the land which the Lord God of your fathers hath given you? The land was already given; they were waiting instead of going in to possess it. Slack means lazy, slothful, full of excuses.
- It is the same spirit a lot of our brothers and sisters have today: you have gifts you bring to the table, but you exercise them for your boss at work and are slack when it comes to the necessities of your nation. This shows we are the children of Israel, because that is the spirit we have, and we have to come out of that thing.
- We cannot sit back knowing Christ is coming and not do the work; the time is short, and this is not our rest. We have to bring our brothers and sisters to repentance.

# Joshua 20: The Cities of Refuge

Read the chapter → [Joshua 20](/bible/joshua/20)

**[Joshua 20:1-3](/bible/joshua/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/20#v1)</sup> The Lord also spake unto Joshua, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/20#v2)</sup> Speak to the children of Israel, saying, Appoint out for you cities of refuge, whereof I spake unto you by the hand of Moses:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/20#v3)</sup> That the slayer that killeth any person unawares and unwittingly may flee thither: and they shall be your refuge from the avenger of blood.

- Now that every tribe had its inheritance, cities of refuge had to be instituted, as the Lord spake by the hand of Moses: that the slayer that killeth any person unawares and unwittingly may flee thither, and they shall be your refuge from the avenger of blood.
- Unwittingly means unknowingly; remember the word wittingly in [Genesis 48](/bible/genesis/48), where Israel guided his hands knowing what he was doing. This is accidental death, not intentional murder, like leaving a co-worker shut in the freezer by mistake because you were focused on going home.
- Notice the scripture says slayer, not murderer: a slayer may be innocent, it may have been a mistake or self-defense, so there are stipulations and laws for his case.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:1-3](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v1)</sup> When the Lord thy God hath cut off the nations, whose land the Lord thy God giveth thee, and thou succeedest them, and dwellest in their cities, and in their houses;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v2)</sup> Thou shalt separate three cities for thee in the midst of thy land, which the Lord thy God giveth thee to possess it.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v3)</sup> Thou shalt prepare thee a way, and divide the coasts of thy land, which the Lord thy God giveth thee to inherit, into three parts, that every slayer may flee thither.

    Moses gave the instruction to separate cities of refuge in the midst of the land; in Joshua 20 Joshua is applying the law.
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:4-6](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v4)</sup> And this is the case of the slayer, which shall flee thither, that he may live: Whoso killeth his neighbour ignorantly, whom he hated not in time past;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v5)</sup> As when a man goeth into the wood with his neighbour to hew wood, and his hand fetcheth a stroke with the axe to cut down the tree, and the head slippeth from the helve, and lighteth upon his neighbour, that he die; he shall flee unto one of those cities, and live:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v6)</sup> Lest the avenger of the blood pursue the slayer, while his heart is hot, and overtake him, because the way is long, and slay him; whereas he was not worthy of death, inasmuch as he hated him not in time past.

    The case of the slayer: two neighbours go into the wood to hew wood, the axe head slips from the helve and lights upon his neighbour that he die; he hated him not in time past, so he was not worthy of death.
  - **[Numbers 35:11-12](/bible/numbers/35#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/35#v11)</sup> Then ye shall appoint you cities to be cities of refuge for you; that the slayer may flee thither, which killeth any person at unawares.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/35#v12)</sup> And they shall be unto you cities for refuge from the avenger; that the manslayer die not, until he stand before the congregation in judgment.

    The cities of refuge were appointed that the manslayer die not, until he stand before the congregation in judgment.
  - **[Exodus 21:12-13](/bible/exodus/21#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/21#v12)</sup> He that smiteth a man, so that he die, shall be surely put to death.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/21#v13)</sup> And if a man lie not in wait, but God deliver him into his hand; then I will appoint thee a place whither he shall flee.

    The law goes back to Exodus: he that smiteth a man that he die shall surely be put to death, but if a man lie not in wait and God deliver him into his hand, a place is appointed whither he shall flee.

- The avenger of blood is a family member of the one killed, and while his heart is hot he is not thinking logically; he is guided by his emotions, and that is how you end up rendering evil for evil. Heart is hot is the same way we talk today, I was hot, I am heated: our forefathers and foremothers spoke the same way.

  Precepts:
  - **[Nehemiah 13:21](/bible/nehemiah/13#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/nehemiah/13#v21)</sup> Then I testified against them, and said unto them, Why lodge ye about the wall? if ye do so again, I will lay hands on you. From that time forth came they no more on the sabbath.

    Nehemiah said, if ye do so again, I will lay hands on you; the same spirit and speech is in our people to this day.
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:11-13](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v11)</sup> But if any man hate his neighbour, and lie in wait for him, and rise up against him, and smite him mortally that he die, and fleeth into one of these cities:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v12)</sup> Then the elders of his city shall send and fetch him thence, and deliver him into the hand of the avenger of blood, that he may die.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v13)</sup> Thine eye shall not pity him, but thou shalt put away the guilt of innocent blood from Israel, that it may go well with thee.

    But if any man hate his neighbour and lie in wait and smite him mortally, the elders of his city shall fetch him from the refuge and deliver him to the avenger of blood; thine eye shall not pity him.
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:21](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v21)</sup> And thine eye shall not pity; but life shall go for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth, hand for hand, foot for foot.

    Life shall go for life, eye for eye, tooth for tooth: that was the judgment instituted in that time; today we are under different laws of the land, and you forgive your brother seventy times seven.
  - **[Numbers 35:22-25](/bible/numbers/35#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/35#v22)</sup> But if he thrust him suddenly without enmity, or have cast upon him any thing without laying of wait,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/35#v23)</sup> Or with any stone, wherewith a man may die, seeing him not, and cast it upon him, that he die, and was not his enemy, neither sought his harm:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/35#v24)</sup> Then the congregation shall judge between the slayer and the revenger of blood according to these judgments:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/35#v25)</sup> And the congregation shall deliver the slayer out of the hand of the revenger of blood, and the congregation shall restore him to the city of his refuge, whither he was fled: and he shall abide in it unto the death of the high priest, which was anointed with the holy oil.

    If he thrust him suddenly without enmity, or cast a stone seeing him not, the congregation shall judge between the slayer and the revenger of blood and deliver the innocent slayer back to his city of refuge.

- The murderer cannot run to a city of refuge to lay low; those cities were not established for him. Even today a killer can cross the border into another jurisdiction and go free, and that is evil; but when Israel controlled the land you could not cross a border to get away with murder, for the elders would deliver you right back to the city you came from. Your sin will find you out.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 19:13-17](/bible/sirach/19#v13)**
