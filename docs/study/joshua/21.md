## Introduction

This reading closes out the book of Joshua, covering chapters 21 through 24: the cities and suburbs given to the Levites out of every tribe, the altar of witness built by the two and a half tribes on the other side Jordan, Joshua's farewell charge to cleave unto the Most High, and the covenant made at Shechem where all Israel chose to serve the Lord. The book of Joshua, like the books before it, is the history of the tribes; it goes further into how Israel got the land, and precept upon precept some things are more detailed in Numbers while Joshua carries the same history further. The Q&A after the reading is folded into the verses it belongs to.

**[Joshua 21:1-3](/bible/joshua/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/21#v1)</sup> Then came near the heads of the fathers of the Levites unto Eleazar the priest, and unto Joshua the son of Nun, and unto the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/21#v2)</sup> And they spake unto them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, The Lord commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to dwell in, with the suburbs thereof for our cattle.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/21#v3)</sup> And the children of Israel gave unto the Levites out of their inheritance, at the commandment of the Lord, these cities and their suburbs.

- The heads of the fathers of the Levites came near to Eleazar the priest, to Joshua, and to the heads of the tribes at Shiloh, because the tribe of Levi had been appointed cities to dwell in with suburbs for their cattle, and the time had come to receive them.
- This was not a new request; it was commanded of the Most High by the hand of Moses that all twelve tribes should give of their inheritance to the Levites.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 35:2-3](/bible/numbers/35#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/35#v2)</sup> Command the children of Israel, that they give unto the Levites of the inheritance of their possession cities to dwell in; and ye shall give also unto the Levites suburbs for the cities round about them.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/35#v3)</sup> And the cities shall they have to dwell in; and the suburbs of them shall be for their cattle, and for their goods, and for all their beasts.

    The commandment by the hand of Moses: give unto the Levites cities to dwell in and suburbs round about them for their cattle, their goods, and all their beasts.

**[Joshua 21:9-19](/bible/joshua/21#v9)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/joshua/21#v18)</sup> Anathoth with her suburbs, and Almon with her suburbs; four cities.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/21#v19)</sup> All the cities of the children of Aaron, the priests, were thirteen cities with their suburbs.

- Out of Judah and Simeon the children of Aaron received nine cities by name, and out of Benjamin four more, thirteen cities in all for the priests.
- They received Hebron in the hill country of Judah, the city of Arba the father of Anak, to be a city of refuge for the slayer; but the fields and villages of the city belonged to Caleb the son of Jephunneh for his possession.
- From the Q&A: not all the cities given to the Levites were cities of refuge. Only certain cities were designated for that, six in all, for the man that killed his brother accidentally.
- The city of refuge worked like protective custody: the slayer that killed a person unawares fled there from the avenger of blood and stayed there until he stood before the congregation and his case was judged.

**[Joshua 21:43](/bible/joshua/21#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/joshua/21#v43)</sup> And the Lord gave unto Israel all the land which he sware to give unto their fathers; and they possessed it, and dwelt therein.

- All the different lands and places just named, everything the tribes inherited at that time, had been promised all the way back to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob. Israel was always promised to have that land, and now they possessed it and dwelt therein.

>

- The Most High gave Israel rest round about according to all that he sware unto their fathers; not a man of all their enemies stood before them, for the Lord delivered all their enemies into their hand.
- There failed not ought of any good thing which the Lord had spoken unto the house of Israel; all came to pass. Everything the Most High said he would do and every good thing he promised, he did it, because God does not lie.
