**[Joshua 3:1-4](/bible/joshua/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/3#v1)</sup> And Joshua rose early in the morning; and they removed from Shittim, and came to Jordan, he and all the children of Israel, and lodged there before they passed over.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/3#v2)</sup> And it came to pass after three days, that the officers went through the host;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/3#v3)</sup> And they commanded the people, saying, When ye see the ark of the covenant of the Lord your God, and the priests the Levites bearing it, then ye shall remove from your place, and go after it.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/3#v4)</sup> Yet there shall be a space between you and it, about two thousand cubits by measure: come not near unto it, that ye may know the way by which ye must go: for ye have not passed this way heretofore.

- Joshua rose early in the morning, and Israel removed from Shittim and came to Jordan and lodged there before passing over. After three days the officers went through the host to spread the orders and instructions the people had to abide by.
- The instruction: when ye see the ark of the covenant and the priests the Levites bearing it, remove from your place and go after it, keeping a space of about two thousand cubits between you and it, for ye have not passed this way heretofore.
- Officers passing through the host with orders is the same order we rehearse today, with officers and captains coming through the lines at the marches and the men's conferences. We are not reading these stories just to marvel at them; we observe them to do them, learning how to set the nation in order with ranks and delegation. We are rehearsing the righteous acts of our fathers and living the words of this Bible.

**[Joshua 3:5-8](/bible/joshua/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/3#v5)</sup> And Joshua said unto the people, Sanctify yourselves: for to morrow the Lord will do wonders among you.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/3#v6)</sup> And Joshua spake unto the priests, saying, Take up the ark of the covenant, and pass over before the people. And they took up the ark of the covenant, and went before the people.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/3#v7)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, This day will I begin to magnify thee in the sight of all Israel, that they may know that, as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/3#v8)</sup> And thou shalt command the priests that bear the ark of the covenant, saying, When ye are come to the brink of the water of Jordan, ye shall stand still in Jordan.

- Sanctify yourselves: for tomorrow the Lord will do wonders among you. Israel had to prepare themselves for miracles; the Lord announced ahead what he was about to do.
- The Lord told Joshua, this day will I begin to magnify thee in the sight of all Israel, that they may know that as I was with Moses, so I will be with thee. The people were about to see the Lord rocking with Joshua the same way he rocked with Moses.
- Joshua was to command the priests bearing the ark of the covenant: when ye are come to the brink of the water of Jordan, ye shall stand still in Jordan.

**[Joshua 3:9-13](/bible/joshua/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/3#v9)</sup> And Joshua said unto the children of Israel, Come hither, and hear the words of the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/3#v10)</sup> And Joshua said, Hereby ye shall know that the living God is among you, and that he will without fail drive out from before you the Canaanites, and the Hittites, and the Hivites, and the Perizzites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Jebusites.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/3#v11)</sup> Behold, the ark of the covenant of the Lord of all the earth passeth over before you into Jordan.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/3#v12)</sup> Now therefore take you twelve men out of the tribes of Israel, out of every tribe a man.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/3#v13)</sup> And it shall come to pass, as soon as the soles of the feet of the priests that bear the ark of the Lord, the Lord of all the earth, shall rest in the waters of Jordan, that the waters of Jordan shall be cut off from the waters that come down from above; and they shall stand upon an heap.

- Joshua called Israel to hear the words of the Lord: hereby ye shall know that the living God is among you, and that he will without fail drive out from before you the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Hivites, the Perizzites, the Girgashites, the Amorites, and the Jebusites.
- Twelve men were to be taken out of the tribes of Israel, one man out of every tribe, prepared ahead for what would follow the crossing.
- The sign given in advance: as soon as the soles of the feet of the priests that bear the ark of the Lord rest in the waters of Jordan, the waters coming down from above shall be cut off and stand upon an heap, and a riverbed becomes dry land for a whole nation to walk through. Hear a word like that today and men would call you crazy, but that is how the Lord dealt with Israel; they had to be ready for miracles.

**[Joshua 3:14-17](/bible/joshua/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/3#v14)</sup> And it came to pass, when the people removed from their tents, to pass over Jordan, and the priests bearing the ark of the covenant before the people;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/3#v15)</sup> And as they that bare the ark were come unto Jordan, and the feet of the priests that bare the ark were dipped in the brim of the water, (for Jordan overfloweth all his banks all the time of harvest,)
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/joshua/3#v16)</sup> That the waters which came down from above stood and rose up upon an heap very far from the city Adam, that is beside Zaretan: and those that came down toward the sea of the plain, even the salt sea, failed, and were cut off: and the people passed over right against Jericho.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/joshua/3#v17)</sup> And the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan, and all the Israelites passed over on dry ground, until all the people were passed clean over Jordan.

- Jordan overflows all his banks all the time of harvest, yet the moment the feet of the priests bearing the ark were dipped in the brim of the water, the waters which came down from above rose up upon an heap very far from the city Adam beside Zaretan, and the waters coming down toward the salt sea failed and were cut off.
- No man can stand in a river, but the priests that bare the ark of the covenant of the Lord stood firm on dry ground in the midst of Jordan, and all Israel passed over on dry ground right against Jericho, until all the people were passed clean over.
- When the Lord is dwelling within Israel, the creation itself moves for them; only for the Israelites will a river fall back. You do not know the manner of love the Most High bestowed upon this people to call them the children of God.
