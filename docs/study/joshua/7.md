**[Joshua 7:1](/bible/joshua/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/7#v1)</sup> But the children of Israel committed a trespass in the accursed thing: for Achan, the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took of the accursed thing: and the anger of the Lord was kindled against the children of Israel.

- Who committed the trespass? Many will answer Achan, but read the first sentence: the children of Israel committed a trespass in the accursed thing. Achan the son of Carmi, the son of Zabdi, the son of Zerah, of the tribe of Judah, took of the accursed thing, and the anger of the Lord was kindled against all the children of Israel.
- Your individual sins can hurt the rest of the nation. You can be the hindrance to why your camp is not growing, why your family, your marriage, or your children have issues, when you have a spirit on you that you know about and keep secret, not praying, not fasting, not seeking counsel. That is the spirit of Achan, and the children of Israel pay for it; we are supposed to be one mind, one body, one spirit.

**[Joshua 7:2-5](/bible/joshua/7#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/7#v2)</sup> And Joshua sent men from Jericho to Ai, which is beside Beth–aven, on the east side of Beth–el, and spake unto them, saying, Go up and view the country. And the men went up and viewed Ai.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/7#v3)</sup> And they returned to Joshua, and said unto him, Let not all the people go up; but let about two or three thousand men go up and smite Ai; and make not all the people to labour thither; for they are but few.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/7#v4)</sup> So there went up thither of the people about three thousand men: and they fled before the men of Ai.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/7#v5)</sup> And the men of Ai smote of them about thirty and six men: for they chased them from before the gate even unto Shebarim, and smote them in the going down: wherefore the hearts of the people melted, and became as water.

- The men who viewed Ai came back confident: let not all the people go up, but about two or three thousand men, for they are but few. The way Israel had been winning, they thought they could not lose; but what happened in verse 1 was about to play its part.
- About three thousand went up, and they fled before the men of Ai, who smote about thirty and six men and chased them from before the gate; the hearts of the people melted and became as water.
- The Lord is the one who wins the battles and gets the victory, not us. Pick out the weakest nation on earth: no matter how strong Israel is physically, if we are not spiritually right with the Lord, he will make us lose, for this fight is not carnal.
- Hearts melting as water is the curse for disobedience: the Most High sends a faintness into the heart, so that the mighty flee as fleeing from a sword and fall when none pursueth, like the athlete in the scary movie suddenly stumbling from sheer fear.

**[Joshua 7:6-9](/bible/joshua/7#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/7#v6)</sup> And Joshua rent his clothes, and fell to the earth upon his face before the ark of the Lord until the eventide, he and the elders of Israel, and put dust upon their heads.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/7#v7)</sup> And Joshua said, Alas, O Lord God, wherefore hast thou at all brought this people over Jordan, to deliver us into the hand of the Amorites, to destroy us? would to God we had been content, and dwelt on the other side Jordan!
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/7#v8)</sup> O Lord, what shall I say, when Israel turneth their backs before their enemies!
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/7#v9)</sup> For the Canaanites and all the inhabitants of the land shall hear of it, and shall environ us round, and cut off our name from the earth: and what wilt thou do unto thy great name?

- Joshua rent his clothes and fell to the earth upon his face before the ark until the eventide, he and the elders of Israel, and put dust upon their heads, grieving over the war just lost and the men that died.
- Israel turning their backs means they were fleeing, retreating, defeated. Joshua understood what the nations do when they see Israel take a loss: the Canaanites would hear of it, environ us round, surround us, capitalize while we are down, and cut off our name from the earth.
- And what wilt thou do unto thy great name? If Israel is cut off, who will be there to praise the Most High?

**[Joshua 7:10-12](/bible/joshua/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/7#v10)</sup> And the Lord said unto Joshua, Get thee up; wherefore liest thou thus upon thy face?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/7#v11)</sup> Israel hath sinned, and they have also transgressed my covenant which I commanded them: for they have even taken of the accursed thing, and have also stolen, and dissembled also, and they have put it even among their own stuff.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/7#v12)</sup> Therefore the children of Israel could not stand before their enemies, but turned their backs before their enemies, because they were accursed: neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the accursed from among you.

- The Lord said, Get thee up; wherefore liest thou upon thy face? Israel hath sinned. It does not say Achan sinned; it says Israel. This walk is not selfish or individual: when you are in sin you hurt your wife, your husband, your children, the congregation, and the leadership, for we are all our brother's keeper and responsible for one another.
- They have taken of the accursed thing, and stolen, and dissembled, and put it among their own stuff: the Lord says they. As a father who finds mischief chastens all his children together when none confesses, so the Most High deals with his children as one house; we pay for one another's sins.
- Therefore the children of Israel could not stand before their enemies: the Lord was not going to let them win, and he put a mightier warring spirit on the men of Ai. However well you train with your sword and shield, the Most High controls the outcome.
- His power cannot be comprehended: great things doeth he, which we cannot comprehend; touching the Almighty, we cannot find him out. Stop trying to figure the Creator out and questioning why he created what he created; you are the creation, so keep the commandments and play your role.
- Neither will I be with you any more, except ye destroy the accursed from among you: what is accursed must be destroyed; that is the only way to remove it.

**[Joshua 7:13-15](/bible/joshua/7#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/7#v13)</sup> Up, sanctify the people, and say, Sanctify yourselves against to morrow: for thus saith the Lord God of Israel, There is an accursed thing in the midst of thee, O Israel: thou canst not stand before thine enemies, until ye take away the accursed thing from among you.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/7#v14)</sup> In the morning therefore ye shall be brought according to your tribes: and it shall be, that the tribe which the Lord taketh shall come according to the families thereof; and the family which the Lord shall take shall come by households; and the household which the Lord shall take shall come man by man.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/7#v15)</sup> And it shall be, that he that is taken with the accursed thing shall be burnt with fire, he and all that he hath: because he hath transgressed the covenant of the Lord, and because he hath wrought folly in Israel.

- Up, sanctify the people against tomorrow: there is an accursed thing in the midst of thee, O Israel; thou canst not stand before thine enemies until ye take away the accursed thing from among you.
- The search would narrow by the Lord's own taking: tribe, then family, then household, then man by man; and he that is taken with the accursed thing shall be burnt with fire, he and all that he hath.
- The folly was wrought against a plain commandment: Joshua had already charged them in [Joshua 6:18-19](/bible/joshua/6#v18) to keep from the accursed thing lest they trouble the camp, and adjured them in [Joshua 6:26](/bible/joshua/6#v26); the transgressor troubled the camp, the army and congregation of Israel.

**[Joshua 7:19](/bible/joshua/7#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/7#v19)</sup> And Joshua said unto Achan, My son, give, I pray thee, glory to the Lord God of Israel, and make confession unto him; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide it not from me.

- My son, give, I pray thee, glory to the Lord God of Israel, and make confession unto him; and tell me now what thou hast done; hide it not from me. Joshua said hide it not from me because it was already not hidden from One mightier than he: we cannot hide what we do from the Lord.
- The eyes of the Lord are in every place, beholding the evil and the good; he sees everything we do. If you are in this walk as a manpleaser, the Lord sees what you do away from the brotherhood and the sisterhood.
- The Lord gives a dispensation of time and grace to get the mind right, to repent, pray, and fast over what we struggle with; the just man falleth seven times and riseth up again. When leadership corrects you, do not think evil of them: the Lord did not have to send leadership, he could have sent evil your way instead; correction is the warning and the mercy.

**[Joshua 7:20-21](/bible/joshua/7#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/7#v20)</sup> And Achan answered Joshua, and said, Indeed I have sinned against the Lord God of Israel, and thus and thus have I done:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/joshua/7#v21)</sup> When I saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, and two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold of fifty shekels weight, then I coveted them, and took them; and, behold, they are hid in the earth in the midst of my tent, and the silver under it.

- Achan confessed: indeed I have sinned against the Lord God of Israel. He saw among the spoils a goodly Babylonish garment, two hundred shekels of silver, and a wedge of gold of fifty shekels weight; he coveted them, took them, and hid them in the earth in the midst of his tent.
- Joshua had said all the silver and gold and vessels were consecrated unto the Lord for his treasury, so Achan was not stealing from men; he was literally stealing from the Most High.
- I coveted them: covetousness is the last of the ten commandments, and as a father saves the weightiest warning for last but not least, the Lord ends the ten with thou shalt not covet.
- To covet is to carry the spirit of jealousy and envy, and there is not a more wicked thing than a covetous man: he sets his own soul to sale, selling out his dignity and self-respect to fulfil his desires no matter what it takes.

**[Joshua 7:22-26](/bible/joshua/7#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/joshua/7#v22)</sup> So Joshua sent messengers, and they ran unto the tent; and, behold, it was hid in his tent, and the silver under it.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/joshua/7#v23)</sup> And they took them out of the midst of the tent, and brought them unto Joshua, and unto all the children of Israel, and laid them out before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/joshua/7#v24)</sup> And Joshua, and all Israel with him, took Achan the son of Zerah, and the silver, and the garment, and the wedge of gold, and his sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, and his asses, and his sheep, and his tent, and all that he had: and they brought them unto the valley of Achor.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/joshua/7#v25)</sup> And Joshua said, Why hast thou troubled us? the Lord shall trouble thee this day. And all Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire, after they had stoned them with stones.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/joshua/7#v26)</sup> And they raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day. So the Lord turned from the fierceness of his anger. Wherefore the name of that place was called, The valley of Achor, unto this day.

- Joshua sent messengers, and they ran unto the tent, and behold, it was hid in his tent, and the silver under it; they laid it all out before the Lord with the whole congregation present. The Lord revealed it, and the scripture cannot be broken: as Jericho was made a curse, so the one who took of it.
- Joshua and all Israel took Achan, the silver, the garment, the wedge of gold, and his sons, and his daughters, and his oxen, asses, sheep, tent, and all that he had, unto the valley of Achor. His sons and daughters did not steal it, yet because he lacked knowledge and understanding they paid for the covetous spirit he carried.
- Why hast thou troubled us? the Lord shall trouble thee this day. Achan was the reason men died in that battle and the nation was nearly overtaken; his selfish, individual spirit never weighed what his family would pay.
- All Israel stoned him with stones, and burned them with fire, after they had stoned them with stones: them, because his household went with him. My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: reject knowledge and the Lord will reject thee, and the end of [Hosea 4:6](/bible/hosea/4#v6) that we seldom quote says, I will also forget thy children. The Lord hits where it hurts, and your sin reaches your children, your wife, your husband, your brothers and sisters.
- It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God, and that is where Achan fell, and unfortunately his sons and daughters and all he had with him. Some of our people in the islands and elsewhere still ignorantly carry out such public executions to this day; we do not condone it, for we are not under the law of sacrifice now but under grace, and the judgment is of the Lord.
- Consider the contrast that closes these chapters: Achan, an Israelite of Judah, lacked faith and was put to death, while Rahab the Canaanite harlot was saved alive for her faith. When Israel will not obey his voice, the Lord likens the house of Judah unto all the heathen; so we must have the fear of the Lord in our hearts.
- They raised over him a great heap of stones unto this day, and the Lord turned from the fierceness of his anger; wherefore the name of that place was called the valley of Achor unto this day.
