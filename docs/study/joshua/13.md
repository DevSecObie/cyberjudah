## Introduction

This session covers [Joshua 13](/bible/joshua/13) through 16, where the dividing of the land begins. Joshua is now old, and the Most High names the land that still remains to be possessed, confirms the inheritance Moses gave the two tribes and a half on the other side Jordan, and commands the rest to be divided by lot. Caleb receives Hebron for wholly following the Most High, then the lot of the tribe of Judah and the lot of the children of Joseph are drawn out. These chapters carry a whole lot of names of cities and borders, so as you read and listen back through them, it does not hurt to pull up a map or look through your Bible atlas and find where these places were, to get a geographical sense of the land the children of Israel were moving through and conquering.

**[Joshua 13:1](/bible/joshua/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/13#v1)</sup> Now Joshua was old and stricken in years; and the Lord said unto him, Thou art old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed.

- Joshua is old and stricken in years, and there remaineth yet very much land to be possessed. The Most High had already said he was not going to drive out these nations in one year, but little and little as Israel increased, so much land still remained.
- The nations were also left among Israel to prove them, whether they would keep the commandments of the Most High, and so that the generations which had not known the wars of Canaan might be taught war. We all know what ended up happening with that proving, and it comes later on as we read.

    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/23#v30)</sup> By little and little I will drive them out from before thee, until thou be increased, and inherit the land.

    I will not drive them out before thee in one year, but by little and little, until thou be increased and inherit the land.
  - **[Judges 3:1-2](/bible/judges/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/judges/3#v1)</sup> Now these are the nations which the Lord left, to prove Israel by them, even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars of Canaan;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/judges/3#v2)</sup> Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at the least such as before knew nothing thereof;

    These are the nations the Most High left to prove Israel, that the generations might know, to teach them war.

- These are the different peoples that still had to be driven out of these lands.

**[Joshua 13:6-7](/bible/joshua/13#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/13#v6)</sup> All the inhabitants of the hill country from Lebanon unto Misrephoth–maim, and all the Sidonians, them will I drive out from before the children of Israel: only divide thou it by lot unto the Israelites for an inheritance, as I have commanded thee.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/13#v7)</sup> Now therefore divide this land for an inheritance unto the nine tribes, and the half tribe of Manasseh,

- The Most High himself will drive out the inhabitants of the hill country of Lebanon and all the Sidonians from before the children of Israel; Joshua's part is only to divide the land by lot unto the Israelites for an inheritance, as he was commanded.
- The land is to be divided only unto the Israelites: unto the nine tribes and the half tribe of Manasseh, for the other two and a half tribes had already received theirs. It had already been told that these lands must be divided among our people.

    Ye shall divide the land by lot for an inheritance among your families; the command was given long before.

- Nevertheless the children of Israel expelled not the Geshurites nor the Maachathites, but they dwelt among the Israelites until this day.
- Only unto the tribe of Levi he gave none inheritance; the sacrifices of the Lord God of Israel made by fire are their inheritance. The Most High had already said the same thing way back to Aaron, that Levi would have no inheritance in the land but would live among the people, and what was stated back then is being carried out in what we are reading now.
