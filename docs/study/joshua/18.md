**[Joshua 18:1-2](/bible/joshua/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/18#v1)</sup> And the whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at Shiloh, and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there. And the land was subdued before them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/18#v2)</sup> And there remained among the children of Israel seven tribes, which had not yet received their inheritance.

- The whole congregation of the children of Israel assembled together at Shiloh and set up the tabernacle of the congregation there, and the land was subdued before them: they won the war, and the land was now in their control.
- There remained among the children of Israel seven tribes which had not yet received their inheritance.

**[Joshua 18:3](/bible/joshua/18#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/18#v3)</sup> And Joshua said unto the children of Israel, How long are ye slack to go to possess the land, which the Lord God of your fathers hath given you?

- How long are ye slack to go possess the land which the Lord God of your fathers hath given you? The land was already given; they were waiting instead of going in to possess it. Slack means lazy, slothful, full of excuses.
- It is the same spirit a lot of our brothers and sisters have today: you have gifts you bring to the table, but you exercise them for your boss at work and are slack when it comes to the necessities of your nation. This shows we are the children of Israel, because that is the spirit we have, and we have to come out of that thing.
- We cannot sit back knowing Christ is coming and not do the work; the time is short, and this is not our rest. We have to bring our brothers and sisters to repentance.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 12:24](/bible/proverbs/12#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/12#v24)</sup> The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute.

    The hand of the diligent shall bear rule, but the slothful shall be under tribute; the other tribes were diligent and went and conquered their land.
  - **[Micah 4:10](/bible/micah/4#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/micah/4#v10)</sup> Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail: for now shalt thou go forth out of the city, and thou shalt dwell in the field, and thou shalt go even to Babylon; there shalt thou be delivered; there the Lord shall redeem thee from the hand of thine enemies.

    Be in pain and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion; the daughter of Zion is the children of Israel.
  - **[Luke 17:21](/bible/luke/17#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/luke/17#v21)</sup> Neither shall they say, Lo here! or, lo there! for, behold, the kingdom of God is within you.

    The kingdom of God is within you; it is up to us to labour and bring forth the kingdom.

**[Joshua 18:4-5](/bible/joshua/18#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/18#v4)</sup> Give out from among you three men for each tribe: and I will send them, and they shall rise, and go through the land, and describe it according to the inheritance of them; and they shall come again to me.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/18#v5)</sup> And they shall divide it into seven parts: Judah shall abide in their coast on the south, and the house of Joseph shall abide in their coasts on the north.

- Joshua asked for three men from each tribe, that he may send them to rise and go through the land and describe it according to the inheritance of them, dividing it into seven parts; he wanted a good description of the land.
- Judah shall abide in their coast on the south, and the house of Joseph in their coasts on the north; when you look at the map you see Judah sitting south of Ephraim, exactly as it is written.

**[Joshua 18:6-7](/bible/joshua/18#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/18#v6)</sup> Ye shall therefore describe the land into seven parts, and bring the description hither to me, that I may cast lots for you here before the Lord our God.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/18#v7)</sup> But the Levites have no part among you; for the priesthood of the Lord is their inheritance: and Gad, and Reuben, and half the tribe of Manasseh, have received their inheritance beyond Jordan on the east, which Moses the servant of the Lord gave them.

- The description of the seven parts was to be brought to Joshua, that he may cast lots for them before the Lord. Casting lots is like throwing dice or drawing straws: you may be the one throwing, but the Lord decides what comes up.

  - **[Proverbs 16:33](/bible/proverbs/16#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/16#v33)</sup> The lot is cast into the lap; but the whole disposing thereof is of the Lord.

    The lot is cast into the lap, but the whole disposing thereof is of the Lord; the Lord decided the outcome of the inheritance of these lands.

- Jonah is also the picture of the slack spirit: he wanted nothing to do with prophesying to wake his people up, and he lay fast asleep down in the sides of the ship while the tempest was ready to break it. America is that spiritual ship that is going to sink, and a lot of our people are asleep in the middle of the chaos, comfortable while a pestilence from the Lord is on the earth.
- The Levites have no part among you, for the priesthood of the Lord is their inheritance. The Levites were well taken care of; they dwelt in the suburbs of the cities and had the best of everything.

>

- To get actual depictions of what is being described, look up Bible maps from the time of Joshua's conquest of the land of Canaan; the Lord told Joshua to destroy those nations so that all Israel, his children, had an inheritance.
