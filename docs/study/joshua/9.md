## Introduction

This session covers [Joshua 9](/bible/joshua/9) through 12, the heart of the conquest of the land. Chapter 9 is the wile of the Gibeonites, who beguile Israel into a league because the princes asked not counsel at the mouth of the Most High. Chapter 10 is the day the sun stood still: the Most High casts down hailstones from heaven, five kings are drawn out of their cave, and the whole south country is destroyed. Chapter 11 is the northern kings gathered as the sand of the sea and utterly destroyed, and chapter 12 is the roll of the thirty and one kings Joshua smote. What we read in Joshua will play out again when Christ returns, for there is nothing new under the sun. So study, pray, apply, endure to the end, and seek ye out of the book of the Lord and read.

**[Joshua 9:1-2](/bible/joshua/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/9#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when all the kings which were on this side Jordan, in the hills, and in the valleys, and in all the coasts of the great sea over against Lebanon, the Hittite, and the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite, heard thereof;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/9#v2)</sup> That they gathered themselves together, to fight with Joshua and with Israel, with one accord.

- The kings on this side Jordan, in the hills, in the valleys, and in all the coasts of the great sea over against Lebanon: the Hittite, the Amorite, the Canaanite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite. These are Hamites, better known as Nilotic Africans.
- When they heard what had been done, they gathered themselves together to fight with Joshua and with Israel with one accord.

**[Joshua 9:3-6](/bible/joshua/9#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/9#v3)</sup> And when the inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done unto Jericho and to Ai,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/joshua/9#v4)</sup> They did work wilily, and went and made as if they had been ambassadors, and took old sacks upon their asses, and wine bottles, old, and rent, and bound up;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joshua/9#v5)</sup> And old shoes and clouted upon their feet, and old garments upon them; and all the bread of their provision was dry and mouldy.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joshua/9#v6)</sup> And they went to Joshua unto the camp at Gilgal, and said unto him, and to the men of Israel, We be come from a far country: now therefore make ye a league with us.

- The inhabitants of Gibeon heard what Joshua had done to Jericho and to Ai, and they did work wilily: they made as if they had been ambassadors, with old sacks upon their asses, wine bottles old and rent and bound up, old shoes and old garments, and bread dry and mouldy.
- They came to Joshua at the camp at Gilgal saying, We be come from a far country: now therefore make ye a league with us. Israel was commanded to destroy all the inhabitants of the land, the very nations listed in verse 1, so the Gibeonites' only hope was to pretend they were not of the land.

**[Joshua 9:7-13](/bible/joshua/9#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/joshua/9#v7)</sup> And the men of Israel said unto the Hivites, Peradventure ye dwell among us; and how shall we make a league with you?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/9#v8)</sup> And they said unto Joshua, We are thy servants. And Joshua said unto them, Who are ye? and from whence come ye?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/joshua/9#v9)</sup> And they said unto him, From a very far country thy servants are come because of the name of the Lord thy God: for we have heard the fame of him, and all that he did in Egypt,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/joshua/9#v10)</sup> And all that he did to the two kings of the Amorites, that were beyond Jordan, to Sihon king of Heshbon, and to Og king of Bashan, which was at Ashtaroth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/joshua/9#v11)</sup> Wherefore our elders and all the inhabitants of our country spake to us, saying, Take victuals with you for the journey, and go to meet them, and say unto them, We are your servants: therefore now make ye a league with us.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/9#v12)</sup> This our bread we took hot for our provision out of our houses on the day we came forth to go unto you; but now, behold, it is dry, and it is mouldy:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/9#v13)</sup> And these bottles of wine, which we filled, were new; and, behold, they be rent: and these our garments and our shoes are become old by reason of the very long journey.

- The men of Israel were suspicious at first: peradventure ye dwell among us, and how shall we make a league with you? Joshua pressed them, Who are ye, and from whence come ye?
- The Gibeonites leaned on the fame of the Most High: we have heard all that he did in Egypt, and to Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan, the two kings of the Amorites beyond Jordan.
- Their props sold the story. In ancient times wine did not come in bottles like ours; wine came in bags, sometimes made of leather, and they would rend through wear and tear over time.

**[Joshua 9:14-15](/bible/joshua/9#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/joshua/9#v14)</sup> And the men took of their victuals, and asked not counsel at the mouth of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/9#v15)</sup> And Joshua made peace with them, and made a league with them, to let them live: and the princes of the congregation sware unto them.

- The men took of their victuals, and asked not counsel at the mouth of the LORD. That is the first mistake, and not asking counsel of the Most High at this moment ended up being a snare and a trap to us as a people, as the rest of the scriptures show.
- Let reason think on whether it is the right move, and even before you reason within yourself, the Most High says counsel, counsel, counsel before every action.
- This type of ordeal will play out again in the times when Christ returns: the sons of strangers shall come to build up Israel's walls, their kings shall minister unto Israel, and the nation and kingdom that will not serve thee shall perish. There is nothing new under the sun.

**[Joshua 9:16-19](/bible/joshua/9#v16)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/joshua/9#v18)</sup> And the children of Israel smote them not, because the princes of the congregation had sworn unto them by the Lord God of Israel. And all the congregation murmured against the princes.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/joshua/9#v19)</sup> But all the princes said unto all the congregation, We have sworn unto them by the Lord God of Israel: now therefore we may not touch them.

- At the end of three days Israel heard that they were their neighbours, dwelling among them. The children of Israel smote them not, because the princes of the congregation had sworn unto them by the LORD God of Israel.
- All the congregation murmured against the princes, but the princes held the line: we have sworn unto them by the LORD God of Israel, now therefore we may not touch them. Our forefathers were wrong in one situation, but they were upright men in holding up to their word.

    When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; what comes out of your mouth, be sure to do it.

**[Joshua 9:20-21](/bible/joshua/9#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/joshua/9#v20)</sup> This we will do to them; we will even let them live, lest wrath be upon us, because of the oath which we sware unto them.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/joshua/9#v21)</sup> And the princes said unto them, Let them live; but let them be hewers of wood and drawers of water unto all the congregation; as the princes had promised them.

- We will even let them live, lest wrath be upon us, because of the oath which we sware unto them. A vow made before the Most High stands, even one gotten by guile.
- Let them be hewers of wood and drawers of water unto all the congregation. Hewers of wood and drawers of water means slaves.
- This is according to the law of the Most High pertaining to other nations. If you just read the Old Testament, there was no equality among the nations, and there is still no equality among nations: they were made for a certain purpose. The reason they are not in that purpose now is because we have sinned and failed, and one day Israel will rise again to put them back in their rightful places.

    Thy bondmen and bondmaids shall be of the heathen round about you; the other nations were made to be Israel's servants, but over your brethren ye shall not rule with rigour.

- Now therefore ye are cursed, and there shall none of you be freed from being bondmen, and hewers of wood and drawers of water for the house of my God. They were servants all the way up and through.

>
> <sup>[25](/bible/joshua/9#v25)</sup> And now, behold, we are in thine hand: as it seemeth good and right unto thee to do unto us, do.

> <sup>[26](/bible/joshua/9#v26)</sup> And so did he unto them, and delivered them out of the hand of the children of Israel, that they slew them not.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/joshua/9#v27)</sup> And Joshua made them that day hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation, and for the altar of the Lord, even unto this day, in the place which he should choose.

- The Gibeonites answered honestly: it was certainly told thy servants how the LORD thy God commanded his servant Moses to give you all the land and to destroy all the inhabitants, therefore we were sore afraid of our lives because of you. Behold, we are in thine hand.
- So did he unto them, and delivered them out of the hand of the children of Israel, that they slew them not; and Joshua made them that day hewers of wood and drawers of water for the congregation and for the altar of the LORD, even unto this day.
