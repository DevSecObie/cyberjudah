**[Judith 10:3](/bible/judith/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/judith/10#v3)</sup> And pulled off the sackcloth which she had on, and put off the garments of her widowhood, and washed her body all over with water, and anointed herself with precious ointment, and braided the hair of her head, and put on a tire upon it, and put on her garments of gladness, wherewith she was clad during the life of Manasses her husband.

- The next sign is a woman's desire being unto her husband. A woman's desire unto her husband is really going into a woman's job to serve her husband and to please her husband. That's what it mean, her desire is unto you. You all know when sisters cook and they stand up over you to see, waiting for you to taste the food to see how it tastes, just watching you while you eating, just for you to say, damn, this is good -- that's a sign of a woman's desire being unto you. A woman that want to have sex with you, that's another desire. If your woman don't want to have sex with you, something wrong; her desire is not unto you. She must be craving you. When you're not at home, she can't sleep.

When her husband was alive it had a certain way she dressed. She washed herself and anointed herself with precious ointment and braided her hair and put a tire on it; she put herself together, and everybody went crazy over her. So the way a woman dress also tells if her desire is unto her husband.
