## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Judith 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 5-8, 12-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Judith 5:1](/bible/judith/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judith/5#v1)</sup> Then was it declared to Holofernes, the chief captain of the army of Assur, that the children of Israel had prepared for war, and had shut up the passages of the hill country, and had fortified all the tops of the high hills, and had laid impediments in the champaign countries:

- Israel had prepared for war by securing the hill-country passages, fortifying the high places and laying impediments. The high ground gave a defensive advantage, and the impediments obstructed the approaches. The enemy later used a different method, cutting the water supply and wearing the people down through attrition rather than relying only on an uphill assault.

**[Judith 5:2-3](/bible/judith/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/judith/5#v2)</sup> Wherewith he was very angry, and called all the princes of Moab, and the captains of Ammon, and all the governors of the sea coast,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judith/5#v3)</sup> And he said unto them, Tell me now, ye sons of Chanaan, who this people is, that dwelleth in the hill country, and what are the cities that they inhabit, and what is the multitude of their army, and wherein is their power and strength, and what king is set over them, or captain of their army;

- Holofernes called the princes of Moab, captains of Ammon and governors of the sea coast to consider Israel's power, army and king. Even the heathen understood the need for counsel and strategy. Their discussion recognized Israel as a people with a history and a power that could not safely be ignored.

**[Judith 5:5](/bible/judith/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/judith/5#v5)</sup> Then said Achior, the captain of all the sons of Ammon, Let my lord now hear a word from the mouth of thy servant, and I will declare unto thee the truth concerning this people, which dwelleth near thee, and inhabiteth the hill countries: and there shall no lie come out of the mouth of thy servant.

- Let’s see if the heathens outside of Israel knew Israel was a race. Maybe they didn’t; maybe they were confused. This is the time of the general of Nebuchadnezzar, when he was seeking knowledge of the nations he was trying to conquer, seeking information regarding our nation, and this heathen gave him intel regarding our people. He was “the captain of all the sons of Ammon”: he was an Ammonite. “I will declare unto thee the truth concerning this people”.

**[Judith 5:6](/bible/judith/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/judith/5#v6)</sup> This people are descended of the Chaldeans:

- “This people are descended of the Chaldeans”. What is he talking about? We are not Chaldeans. He means they descended from a man who came out of there: he is talking about Abraham, who resided in Ur of the Chaldees, which is modern day Iraq.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 11:31](/bible/genesis/11#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/11#v31)</sup> And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran his son’s son, and Sarai his daughter in law, his son Abram’s wife; and they went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go into the land of Canaan; and they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.

    “They went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldees”: that’s where Abraham and them came from, “to go into the land of Canaan”. The Lord promised Abraham land, and Abraham believed and he left: he left with his father, his wife and his nephew, and Lot’s family as well. “And they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.” That is Mesopotamia as well. So the people descended of the Chaldeans means descended of Abraham, who came out of there.

**[Judith 5:7](/bible/judith/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judith/5#v7)</sup> And they sojourned heretofore in Mesopotamia, because they would not follow the gods of their fathers, which were in the land of Chaldea.

- “They would not follow the gods of their fathers, which were in the land of Chaldea.” He refused the gods of his fathers: that is Abraham, because his family members were wicked as hell, including his father.

  Precepts:
  - **[Joshua 24:2-3](/bible/joshua/24#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/24#v2)</sup> And Joshua said unto all the people, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Your fathers dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time, even Terah, the father of Abraham, and the father of Nachor: and they served other gods.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/24#v3)</sup> And I took your father Abraham from the other side of the flood, and led him throughout all the land of Canaan, and multiplied his seed, and gave him Isaac.

    “Your fathers dwelt on the other side of the flood in old time”, even the father of Abraham, “and they served other gods.” They served what? Other gods. It goes back to what was said in Judith: they would not follow the gods of their fathers.

**[Judith 5:8](/bible/judith/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/judith/5#v8)</sup> For they left the way of their ancestors, and worshipped the God of heaven, the God whom they knew: so they cast them out from the face of their gods, and they fled into Mesopotamia, and sojourned there many days.

- “For they left the way of their ancestors, and worshipped the God of heaven, the God whom they knew”. That is about Abraham. “And they fled into Mesopotamia, and sojourned there many days”: that is Haran.
- So even the heathen knew this people by their descent and their ancestors. Reading about our forefathers all throughout the Bible, yet somehow they’re not a race.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 3:12-16](/bible/2-esdras/3#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-esdras/3#v12)</sup> And it happened, that when they that dwelt upon the earth began to multiply, and had gotten them many children, and were a great people, they began again to be more ungodly than the first.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-esdras/3#v13)</sup> Now when they lived so wickedly before thee, thou didst choose thee a man from among them, whose name was Abraham.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-esdras/3#v14)</sup> Him thou lovedst, and unto him only thou shewedst thy will:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-esdras/3#v15)</sup> And madest an everlasting covenant with him, promising him that thou wouldest never forsake his seed.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/2-esdras/3#v16)</sup> And unto him thou gavest Isaac, and unto Isaac also thou gavest Jacob and Esau. As for Jacob, thou didst choose him to thee, and put by Esau: and so Jacob became a great multitude.

    This gives more detail. “And it happened, that when they that dwelt upon the earth began to multiply, and had gotten them many children”: this is when the earth was replenished after the flood. Generations. They “were a great people, they began again to be more ungodly than the first”: worse than those who were killed by the flood.

**[Judith 5:12-16](/bible/judith/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/judith/5#v12)</sup> Then they cried unto their God, and he smote all the land of Egypt with incurable plagues: so the Egyptians cast them out of their sight.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judith/5#v13)</sup> And God dried the Red sea before them,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judith/5#v14)</sup> And brought them to mount Sina, and Cades-Barne, and cast forth all that dwelt in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/judith/5#v15)</sup> So they dwelt in the land of the Amorites, and they destroyed by their strength all them of Esebon, and passing over Jordan they possessed all the hill country.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judith/5#v16)</sup> And they cast forth before them the Chanaanite, the Pherezite, the Jebusite, and the Sychemite, and all the Gergesites, and they dwelt in that country many days.

- The account before the enemy recalled Israel crying to God, the incurable plagues in Egypt, the drying of the Red Sea and the journey through Sinai and Cades-Barne. It continued through the defeat of the people of Heshbon, the crossing of Jordan and the removal of the Canaanites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Sychemites and Gergesites. The surrounding nations knew how God had made room for Israel and given the people victory.

**[Judith 5:17-19](/bible/judith/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/judith/5#v17)</sup> And whilst they sinned not before their God, they prospered, because the God that hateth iniquity was with them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judith/5#v18)</sup> But when they departed from the way which he appointed them, they were destroyed in many battles very sore, and were led captives into a land that was not theirs, and the temple of their God was cast to the ground, and their cities were taken by the enemies.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/judith/5#v19)</sup> But now are they returned to their God, and are come up from the places where they were scattered, and have possessed Jerusalem, where their sanctuary is, and are seated in the hill country; for it was desolate.

- “And whilst they sinned not before their God, they prospered, because the God that hateth iniquity was with them.” The Israelites prospered because they were not in sin. That is what we need: a congregation of righteousness. When the bishops travel to the different schools, the congregations bring gifts, and they are appreciated, but the greatest gift the leadership is looking for is a sinless congregation. Given the choice between the two, give us a sinless congregation.
- God “hateth iniquity.” If we know that God hates sin, why in the hell would we allow it among us while we are looking for blessings? That makes no sense. We want the Lord to prosper us, and we will prosper if there is no iniquity among us.
- This is Leviticus 5:1, quoted on every page and every video we put out: it is time to execute it. You see some evil, you hear some evil, you report that damn thing, the hell with their feelings. Evil left sitting in the body will erode the spirits of everyone in it. It is not only in one school: the reports of fornication, adultery and lasciviousness are coming in from all the congregations, and at some point we have to put a stop to it.
- This is what the nations know: a biblical example of what the history shows. Achior is explaining to the chief captain of the army, who is asking about Israel and how to take them down. He runs down our whole history and lets him know the kind of waspness he is about to mess with if it is connected with God: let me tell you about these people.

  Precepts:
  - **[Judith 5:3](/bible/judith/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/judith/5#v3)</sup> And he said unto them, Tell me now, ye sons of Chanaan, who this people is, that dwelleth in the hill country, and what are the cities that they inhabit, and what is the multitude of their army, and wherein is their power and strength, and what king is set over them, or captain of their army;

    Before verse 17 comes the question he asks about us: “Tell me now, ye sons of Chanaan, who this people is, that dwelleth in the hill country, and what are the cities that they inhabit”. He says he doesn’t know who we are; he sees us coming out of the Persian captivity and asks why we are not coming to honour him. He needs to know where our strength is, where our power is: “and what is the multitude of their army, and wherein is their power and strength, and what king is set over them, or captain of their army”.

**[Judith 5:20](/bible/judith/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/judith/5#v20)</sup> Now therefore, my lord and governor, if there be any error in this people, and they sin against their God, let us consider that this shall be their ruin, and let us go up, and we shall overcome them.

- “Now therefore, my lord and governor, if there be any error...” That is the key. If there is any error, any sin, in these sanctuaries and schools, and we watch it and let the sinners beguile us into thinking it is righteous, that is crazy, and it is not going to happen.
- “And they sin against their God, let us consider that this shall be their ruin.” This is what the nations are saying, the same nations in the cities around us, with the slave figurines in their yards. We do not want our ruin to be imminent; we want blessings. So any iota of evil, sin and wickedness, your job is to expose it, tell it, bring it forward. The men are to be the vanguards of righteousness, so that any spirit that comes in wanting to be evil sees the eyes on it and says those people are too strict. That is a compliment. We are looking for a few good men and a few good women.
- The gospel is here to fix us in our sickness: all of us who come in, the bishop included, came in sick, and this sanctuary is the hospital, a safe haven to heal spiritual wounds. But whoever is not willing to be fixed, and wants to leave an egg of deception to hatch in the congregation and destroy what God is building, has to go.
- “And let us go up, and we shall overcome them.” If we are in sin, the nations say they can overthrow us, because the God that hateth iniquity is not with us. Be at peace with all men if it be possible, but watch your surroundings, and the best protection we have is being in line with the Lord. That is the posture we must take as men, fathers, elders, sisters, mothers and wives: the vanguard of righteousness. We cannot entertain filth. This is grown folks business.

**[Judith 5:21](/bible/judith/5#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/judith/5#v21)</sup> But if there be no iniquity in their nation, let my lord now pass by, lest their Lord defend them, and their God be for them, and we become a reproach before all the world.

- “But if there be no iniquity in their nation, let my lord now pass by, lest their Lord defend them, and their God be for them, and we become a reproach before all the world.” They don’t want that to happen. The same knowledge persists today: if they can keep us in sin, they can keep exploiting, dominating, robbing and pillaging us, and leave us in a wilderness of foolishness.
- So underneath all the fireworks and foolishness of this weekend, how many more Mike Browns, how many more Rasheem Carters and Kendrick Johnsons? How many more abortions, where our women extinguish our children because they feel they have no hope? These things come because we simply don’t keep the commandments of God. We have work to do.
- “But if there be no iniquity in their nation, let my lord now pass by”: if there is no sin in our nation, with us checking each other and making sure we are all good, they said leave them the hell alone. That is where our power is. “lest their Lord defend them, and their God be for them, and we become a reproach before all the world”: we are the apple of his eye, and whoever puts their filthy fat fingers on God’s people while they keep his commandments becomes a reproach, a fool in history, like Pharaoh, and as he will do Esau.
- That is why they want us subdued and dysfunctional: so we cannot organize the greatness the Lord put in us by nature. They didn’t grab people who knew nothing to build this country; it is what it is because we are here. Black men came from a country where they were warriors and family heads, and in the Ghanaian states were kings, princes, governors, generals, judges, architects, engineers, doctors, mathematicians, goldsmiths, blacksmiths, an industrious and skillful people. So they built a slave system designed to destroy the manhood of the black male, killing the sons in the mind first. We are not niggas, not coons, not Ham; that lie was given to us to make us think we are a different image than what God made. Even cut off from everything, with no money, no 40 acres, no mule, we built Black Wall Street in Tulsa, in Durham, in Rosewood, with our own hospitals, hotels and theaters, and they got mad and bombed it. They know how great a resource we are.
