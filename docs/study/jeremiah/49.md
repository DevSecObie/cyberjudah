**[Jeremiah 49:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/49#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/49#v1)</sup> Concerning the Ammonites, thus saith the Lord; Hath Israel no sons? hath he no heir? why then doth their king inherit Gad, and his people dwell in his cities?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/49#v2)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will cause an alarm of war to be heard in Rabbah of the Ammonites; and it shall be a desolate heap, and her daughters shall be burned with fire: then shall Israel be heir unto them that were his heirs, saith the Lord.

- This is the word against the Ammonites, the so-called Japanese. The Most High asks, hath Israel no sons? Why then doth their king inherit Gad and his people dwell in his cities?
- That shows Gad was intermingling with the Ammonites. This is why parts of the Northern Kingdom sometimes carry an Asian look, because we were once in captivity to Ammon and mixed among them; not everyone in Israel looks the same.
- The days come when an alarm of war is heard in Rabbah, it becomes a desolate heap, and then shall Israel be heir unto them that were his heirs.

**[Jeremiah 49:3-5](/bible/jeremiah/49#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/49#v3)</sup> Howl, O Heshbon, for Ai is spoiled: cry, ye daughters of Rabbah, gird you with sackcloth; lament, and run to and fro by the hedges; for their king shall go into captivity, and his priests and his princes together.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/49#v4)</sup> Wherefore gloriest thou in the valleys, thy flowing valley, O backsliding daughter? that trusted in her treasures, saying, Who shall come unto me?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/49#v5)</sup> Behold, I will bring a fear upon thee, saith the Lord God of hosts, from all those that be about thee; and ye shall be driven out every man right forth; and none shall gather up him that wandereth.

- Howl, O Heshbon; cry, ye daughters of Rabbah; gird with sackcloth and lament. Their king shall go into captivity with his priests and his princes together.
- The backsliding daughter trusted in her treasures, saying, who shall come unto me? The Most High brings fear upon her from all sides, and none shall gather up him that wandereth.

**[Jeremiah 49:6](/bible/jeremiah/49#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/49#v6)</sup> And afterward I will bring again the captivity of the children of Ammon, saith the Lord.

- Afterward the Most High will bring again the captivity of the children of Ammon. Both Moab and Ammon go into captivity, and both are promised a return, but the reckoning comes first.
- This is our salvation and redemption. Every nation has held Israel captive at one time, not just the friendly neighborhood white man: the Assyrians, Babylon, Persia and the Medes, Greece and Rome, the many captivities in the book of Judges, the Arabs and Ishmael in the trans-Saharan slave trade, and America. Whosoever leads into captivity shall go into captivity.

    He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity; he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword.

**[Jeremiah 49:17-18](/bible/jeremiah/49#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/49#v17)</sup> Also Edom shall be a desolation: every one that goeth by it shall be astonished, and shall hiss at all the plagues thereof.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/49#v18)</sup> As in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah and the neighbour cities thereof, saith the Lord, no man shall abide there, neither shall a son of man dwell in it.

- Now the word turns to Edom, the so-called white man. Bible dictionaries and encyclopedias confirm Edom points to the Romans, the Greeks, and the Americans, so this is not merely an Israelite interpretation.
- Edom shall be a desolation; everyone that passes by shall be astonished and hiss at all its plagues, as in the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah. No man shall abide there.
  - **[Genesis 25:25](/bible/genesis/25#v25)**

    Esau came out red and hairy; he is the one nation without the pigmentation the rest of the world carries.

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/49#v20)</sup> Therefore hear the counsel of the Lord, that he hath taken against Edom; and his purposes, that he hath purposed against the inhabitants of Teman: Surely the least of the flock shall draw them out: surely he shall make their habitations desolate with them.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/49#v21)</sup> The earth is moved at the noise of their fall, at the cry the noise thereof was heard in the Red sea.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/49#v22)</sup> Behold, he shall come up and fly as the eagle, and spread his wings over Bozrah: and at that day shall the heart of the mighty men of Edom be as the heart of a woman in her pangs.

- Hear the counsel the Lord has taken against Edom: surely the least of the flock shall draw them out, and their habitations shall be made desolate. The earth is moved at the noise of their fall.
- This is a very great nation whose fall astonishes the world, a people who almost ruled the earth with army bases and embassies everywhere. Babylon the Great, which is America, will fall; it is inevitable.
- He shall fly as an eagle and spread his wings over Bozrah, and the heart of the mighty men of Edom that day shall be as the heart of a woman in her pains. This is our salvation, which comes only with the destruction of our enemies.

>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/49#v25)</sup> How is the city of praise not left, the city of my joy!
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/49#v26)</sup> Therefore her young men shall fall in her streets, and all the men of war shall be cut off in that day, saith the Lord of hosts.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/49#v27)</sup> And I will kindle a fire in the wall of Damascus, and it shall consume the palaces of Ben–hadad.

**[Jeremiah 49:28-33](/bible/jeremiah/49#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/49#v28)</sup> Concerning Kedar, and concerning the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon shall smite, thus saith the Lord; Arise ye, go up to Kedar, and spoil the men of the east.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/49#v29)</sup> Their tents and their flocks shall they take away: they shall take to themselves their curtains, and all their vessels, and their camels; and they shall cry unto them, Fear is on every side.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/49#v30)</sup> Flee, get you far off, dwell deep, O ye inhabitants of Hazor, saith the Lord; for Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon hath taken counsel against you, and hath conceived a purpose against you.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/49#v31)</sup> Arise, get you up unto the wealthy nation, that dwelleth without care, saith the Lord, which have neither gates nor bars, which dwell alone.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/49#v32)</sup> And their camels shall be a booty, and the multitude of their cattle a spoil: and I will scatter into all winds them that are in the utmost corners; and I will bring their calamity from all sides thereof, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/49#v33)</sup> And Hazor shall be a dwelling for dragons, and a desolation for ever: there shall no man abide there, nor any son of man dwell in it.

- Concerning Kedar and the kingdoms of Hazor, which Nebuchadnezzar shall smite: arise and spoil the men of the east; their tents, flocks, and camels are taken, and fear is on every side.
- Nebuchadnezzar has conceived a purpose against the wealthy nation that dwells without care, without gates or bars. Hazor becomes a dwelling for dragons and a desolation forever.

**[Jeremiah 49:34-39](/bible/jeremiah/49#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/49#v34)</sup> The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah the prophet against Elam in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, saying,
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/49#v35)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Behold, I will break the bow of Elam, the chief of their might.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/49#v36)</sup> And upon Elam will I bring the four winds from the four quarters of heaven, and will scatter them toward all those winds; and there shall be no nation whither the outcasts of Elam shall not come.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/jeremiah/49#v37)</sup> For I will cause Elam to be dismayed before their enemies, and before them that seek their life: and I will bring evil upon them, even my fierce anger, saith the Lord; and I will send the sword after them, till I have consumed them:
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/jeremiah/49#v38)</sup> And I will set my throne in Elam, and will destroy from thence the king and the princes, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/jeremiah/49#v39)</sup> But it shall come to pass in the latter days, that I will bring again the captivity of Elam, saith the Lord.

- This word came against Elam in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah, king of Judah. Elam is the ancient and modern Persians, the so-called East Indians found in India, Malaysia, Bangladesh, Afghanistan, and Iran.
- The Most High will break the bow of Elam, bring four winds from the four quarters of heaven, and scatter them among all nations. He will set his throne in Elam and destroy their king and princes.
- Elam fell once already in the days of Alexander the Great around 333 BC, but the Most High says he will bring again the captivity of Elam in the latter days. When Christ returns, every nation will be subject to one government, the government of Israel given by the Most High in Christ.
