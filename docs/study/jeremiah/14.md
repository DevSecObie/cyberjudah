**[Jeremiah 14:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/14#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/14#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah concerning the dearth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/14#v2)</sup> Judah mourneth, and the gates thereof languish; they are black unto the ground; and the cry of Jerusalem is gone up.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/14#v3)</sup> And their nobles have sent their little ones to the waters: they came to the pits, and found no water; they returned with their vessels empty; they were ashamed and confounded, and covered their heads.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/14#v4)</sup> Because the ground is chapt, for there was no rain in the earth, the plowmen were ashamed, they covered their heads.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/14#v5)</sup> Yea, the hind also calved in the field, and forsook it, because there was no grass.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/14#v6)</sup> And the wild asses did stand in the high places, they snuffed up the wind like dragons; their eyes did fail, because there was no grass.

- The word comes concerning the dearth. Judah mourns, the gates languish, and the cry of Jerusalem goes up while the nobles send their little ones for water and find none.
- The ground is chapped because there is no rain, and the plowmen are ashamed and cover their heads. That is how you know these are black people; "chapped" is a word black people use, not just for lips but for the cracked, dry ground.

**[Jeremiah 14:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/14#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/14#v7)</sup> O Lord, though our iniquities testify against us, do thou it for thy name’s sake: for our backslidings are many; we have sinned against thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/14#v8)</sup> O the hope of Israel, the saviour thereof in time of trouble, why shouldest thou be as a stranger in the land, and as a wayfaring man that turneth aside to tarry for a night?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/14#v9)</sup> Why shouldest thou be as a man astonied, as a mighty man that cannot save? yet thou, O Lord, art in the midst of us, and we are called by thy name; leave us not.

- Though our iniquities testify against us, the plea is that the Most High act for His name's sake, for our backslidings are many. He is called the hope of Israel and the Savior in time of trouble.
- The cry is for Him not to be a stranger in the land or a mighty man that cannot save, since He is in the midst of us and we are called by His name.

**[Jeremiah 14:10-12](/bible/jeremiah/14#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/14#v10)</sup> Thus saith the Lord unto this people, Thus have they loved to wander, they have not refrained their feet, therefore the Lord doth not accept them; he will now remember their iniquity, and visit their sins.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/14#v11)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Pray not for this people for their good.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/14#v12)</sup> When they fast, I will not hear their cry; and when they offer burnt offering and an oblation, I will not accept them: but I will consume them by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence.

- The people loved to wander and did not refrain their feet, so the Most High does not accept them; He will remember their iniquity and visit their sins.
- He tells Jeremiah not to pray for this people; even when they fast He will not hear their cry, and their offerings He will not accept, but will consume them by sword, famine and pestilence.
  - **[John 9:31](/bible/john/9#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/john/9#v31)</sup> Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.

    The Most High does not hear sinners; He told Jeremiah not to pray for them because their sins were, and are, so many, which is why it behooves all of us to repent.

**[Jeremiah 14:13-16](/bible/jeremiah/14#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/14#v13)</sup> Then said I, Ah, Lord God! behold, the prophets say unto them, Ye shall not see the sword, neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/14#v14)</sup> Then the Lord said unto me, The prophets prophesy lies in my name: I sent them not, neither have I commanded them, neither spake unto them: they prophesy unto you a false vision and divination, and a thing of nought, and the deceit of their heart.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/14#v15)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that prophesy in my name, and I sent them not, yet they say, Sword and famine shall not be in this land; By sword and famine shall those prophets be consumed.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/14#v16)</sup> And the people to whom they prophesy shall be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem because of the famine and the sword; and they shall have none to bury them, them, their wives, nor their sons, nor their daughters: for I will pour their wickedness upon them.

- Jeremiah answers that the prophets tell the people they shall not see sword or famine but shall have assured peace. This is what the Christian pastors do today; the verse above said not to pray for these people, yet the false prophets promise nothing but peace and prosperity.
- The Most High says those prophets prophesy lies in His name; He did not send them. By sword and famine those prophets shall be consumed, and the people they deceived cast out in the streets with none to bury them.
- What happened back then is happening today. This country is going to go down by nuclear destruction, whether our people accept it or not, while the false prophets say nothing but peace, and they will be dealt with like all the other nations.

**[Jeremiah 14:17-22](/bible/jeremiah/14#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/14#v17)</sup> Therefore thou shalt say this word unto them; Let mine eyes run down with tears night and day, and let them not cease: for the virgin daughter of my people is broken with a great breach, with a very grievous blow.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/14#v18)</sup> If I go forth into the field, then behold the slain with the sword! and if I enter into the city, then behold them that are sick with famine! yea, both the prophet and the priest go about into a land that they know not.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/14#v19)</sup> Hast thou utterly rejected Judah? hath thy soul lothed Zion? why hast thou smitten us, and there is no healing for us? we looked for peace, and there is no good; and for the time of healing, and behold trouble!
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/14#v20)</sup> We acknowledge, O Lord, our wickedness, and the iniquity of our fathers: for we have sinned against thee.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/14#v21)</sup> Do not abhor us, for thy name’s sake, do not disgrace the throne of thy glory: remember, break not thy covenant with us.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/14#v22)</sup> Are there any among the vanities of the Gentiles that can cause rain? or can the heavens give showers? art not thou he, O Lord our God? therefore we will wait upon thee: for thou hast made all these things.

- Let the eyes run down with tears night and day, for the virgin daughter of the people is broken with a great breach; the slain fill the field and the sick with famine fill the city, while prophet and priest wander into a land they know not.
- We acknowledge our wickedness and the iniquity of our fathers and ask Him not to abhor us for His name's sake or break His covenant. None of the vanities of the gentiles can cause rain; only the Most High, so we wait upon Him.
- We have to acknowledge our offense and the wickedness we have been doing, even the wickedness we support because it does not affect us directly. Your salvation is contingent on keeping the commandments and abhorring that evil.
