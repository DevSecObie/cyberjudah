Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 4](/bible/jeremiah/4)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 4 through 7. The Most High calls a backsliding Israel to return, to put away their abominations, and to circumcise the foreskin of the heart; if they will not, He will bring an ancient nation from the north as His sword to lay the land waste. The same charge stands against the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans today, who are scattered and serving strangers in a land that is not ours because we went against the laws. The opening framing reaches back to the husband-and-wife analogy of chapter 3 and to the beginning of spiritual fornication named in the Wisdom of Solomon.

**[Jeremiah 4:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/4#v1)</sup> If thou wilt return, O Israel, saith the Lord, return unto me: and if thou wilt put away thine abominations out of my sight, then shalt thou not remove.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/4#v2)</sup> And thou shalt swear, The Lord liveth, in truth, in judgment, and in righteousness; and the nations shall bless themselves in him, and in him shall they glory.

- If Israel will return, saith the Most High, and put away their abominations out of His sight, then they shall not be removed out of their land.
- When they swear that the Lord liveth in truth, in judgment, and in righteousness, the nations shall bless themselves in Him; as long as we follow Him, we do not have to worry about the nations coming against us.
  - **[Jeremiah 3:1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)</sup> They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted? but thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me, saith the Lord.

    The Most High uses the analogy of a man who puts away his wife and she becomes another man's, showing Israel played the harlot with many nations yet is still called to return.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 14:12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/14#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/14#v12)</sup> For the devising of idols was the beginning of spiritual fornication, and the invention of them the corruption of life.

    Idolatry is spiritual fornication, which is why the Most High uses the marriage analogy for Israel.

**[Jeremiah 4:3-4](/bible/jeremiah/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/4#v3)</sup> For thus saith the Lord to the men of Judah and Jerusalem, Break up your fallow ground, and sow not among thorns.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/4#v4)</sup> Circumcise yourselves to the Lord, and take away the foreskins of your heart, ye men of Judah and inhabitants of Jerusalem: lest my fury come forth like fire, and burn that none can quench it, because of the evil of your doings.

- The Most High tells the men of Judah and Jerusalem to break up their fallow ground and not to sow among thorns, using the analogy of a seed: break up the hard ground and do not scatter seed where the land is not fertile.
- Circumcise yourselves to the Most High and take away the foreskins of your heart, lest His fury come forth like fire and burn where none can quench it, because of the evil of their doings.

**[Jeremiah 4:5-6](/bible/jeremiah/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/4#v5)</sup> Declare ye in Judah, and publish in Jerusalem; and say, Blow ye the trumpet in the land: cry, gather together, and say, Assemble yourselves, and let us go into the defenced cities.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/4#v6)</sup> Set up the standard toward Zion: retire, stay not: for I will bring evil from the north, and a great destruction.

- Declare in Judah and publish in Jerusalem, blow the trumpet, and gather into the fenced cities, for destruction is about to come because they went against the Most High.
- Set up the standard toward Zion, for He will bring evil from the north and a great destruction by the other nations.
  - **[Isaiah 13:2](/bible/isaiah/13#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/13#v2)</sup> Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles.

    Lift up a banner upon the high mountain and shake the hand, meaning establish these laws and this book toward Zion.

**[Jeremiah 4:7-8](/bible/jeremiah/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/4#v7)</sup> The lion is come up from his thicket, and the destroyer of the Gentiles is on his way; he is gone forth from his place to make thy land desolate; and thy cities shall be laid waste, without an inhabitant.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/4#v8)</sup> For this gird you with sackcloth, lament and howl: for the fierce anger of the Lord is not turned back from us.

- The lion is come up from his thicket and the destroyer of the gentiles is on his way to make the land desolate and lay the cities waste without an inhabitant, which is why we are not in our own land today.
- Gird with sackcloth, lament, and howl, for the fierce anger of the Most High is not turned back; He established it and it is going to happen.
  - **[Psalm 17:13](/bible/psalms/17#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/17#v13)</sup> Arise, O Lord, disappoint him, cast him down: deliver my soul from the wicked, which is thy sword:

    The wicked is called the Most High's sword, showing the other nations are the weapon He uses to judge His people.

**[Jeremiah 4:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/4#v9)</sup> And it shall come to pass at that day, saith the Lord, that the heart of the king shall perish, and the heart of the princes; and the priests shall be astonished, and the prophets shall wonder.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/4#v10)</sup> Then said I, Ah, Lord God! surely thou hast greatly deceived this people and Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall have peace; whereas the sword reacheth unto the soul.

- In that day the heart of the king shall perish, the princes and priests shall be astonished, and the prophets shall wonder, for the whole nation was going to receive this judgment.
- The false peace preached to Jerusalem is exposed, for the sword reacheth unto the soul.

**[Jeremiah 4:11-12](/bible/jeremiah/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/4#v11)</sup> At that time shall it be said to this people and to Jerusalem, A dry wind of the high places in the wilderness toward the daughter of my people, not to fan, nor to cleanse,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/4#v12)</sup> Even a full wind from those places shall come unto me: now also will I give sentence against them.

- A dry wind of the high places shall come toward the daughter of His people, not to fan nor to cleanse but a full wind of judgment and destruction.
- Now He gives sentence against them; this is twofold, the destruction that happened in ancient time and the destruction that is coming today.

**[Jeremiah 4:13-14](/bible/jeremiah/4#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/4#v13)</sup> Behold, he shall come up as clouds, and his chariots shall be as a whirlwind: his horses are swifter than eagles. Woe unto us! for we are spoiled.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/4#v14)</sup> O Jerusalem, wash thine heart from wickedness, that thou mayest be saved. How long shall thy vain thoughts lodge within thee?

- He shall come up as clouds, His chariots as a whirlwind, His horses swifter than eagles; woe unto us, for we are spoiled.
- Wash the heart from wickedness that thou mayest be saved; the heart here is the mind, for thoughts come from the mind, not from the organ that pumps the blood.

**[Jeremiah 4:15-18](/bible/jeremiah/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/4#v15)</sup> For a voice declareth from Dan, and publisheth affliction from mount Ephraim.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/4#v16)</sup> Make ye mention to the nations; behold, publish against Jerusalem, that watchers come from a far country, and give out their voice against the cities of Judah.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/4#v17)</sup> As keepers of a field, are they against her round about; because she hath been rebellious against me, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/4#v18)</sup> Thy way and thy doings have procured these things unto thee; this is thy wickedness, because it is bitter, because it reacheth unto thine heart.

- A voice declareth affliction from Dan and Mount Ephraim, and watchers come from a far country against the cities of Judah, meaning the nations come against the whole of Israel.
- All this is the fruit of our own way and doings; our wickedness has procured these things, and it reacheth unto the heart because we have been rebellious against the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 4:19-22](/bible/jeremiah/4#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/4#v19)</sup> My bowels, my bowels! I am pained at my very heart; my heart maketh a noise in me; I cannot hold my peace, because thou hast heard, O my soul, the sound of the trumpet, the alarm of war.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/4#v20)</sup> Destruction upon destruction is cried; for the whole land is spoiled: suddenly are my tents spoiled, and my curtains in a moment.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/4#v21)</sup> How long shall I see the standard, and hear the sound of the trumpet?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/4#v22)</sup> For my people is foolish, they have not known me; they are sottish children, and they have none understanding: they are wise to do evil, but to do good they have no knowledge.

- The prophet is pained at his very heart at the sound of the trumpet and the alarm of war, for destruction upon destruction is cried and the whole land is spoiled suddenly.
- His people are foolish and have not known Him; they are wise to do evil but to do good they have no knowledge, finding every way to keep the pagan feast days while refusing to learn to serve the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 4:23-26](/bible/jeremiah/4#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/4#v23)</sup> I beheld the earth, and, lo, it was without form, and void; and the heavens, and they had no light.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/4#v24)</sup> I beheld the mountains, and, lo, they trembled, and all the hills moved lightly.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/4#v25)</sup> I beheld, and, lo, there was no man, and all the birds of the heavens were fled.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/4#v26)</sup> I beheld, and, lo, the fruitful place was a wilderness, and all the cities thereof were broken down at the presence of the Lord, and by his fierce anger.

- Jeremiah beheld the earth without form and void and the heavens with no light, and the mountains trembled and the hills moved lightly at the presence of the Most High.
- He beheld no man, the birds of heaven fled, and the fruitful place became a wilderness with its cities broken down before His fierce anger.

**[Jeremiah 4:27-28](/bible/jeremiah/4#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/4#v27)</sup> For thus hath the Lord said, The whole land shall be desolate; yet will I not make a full end.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/4#v28)</sup> For this shall the earth mourn, and the heavens above be black: because I have spoken it, I have purposed it, and will not repent, neither will I turn back from it.

- The whole land shall be desolate, yet He will not make a full end, for He does not destroy everything.
- The earth shall mourn and the heavens above be black, because He has spoken it and purposed it and will not repent nor turn back from it.

**[Jeremiah 4:29-31](/bible/jeremiah/4#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/4#v29)</sup> The whole city shall flee for the noise of the horsemen and bowmen; they shall go into thickets, and climb up upon the rocks: every city shall be forsaken, and not a man dwell therein.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/4#v30)</sup> And when thou art spoiled, what wilt thou do? Though thou clothest thyself with crimson, though thou deckest thee with ornaments of gold, though thou rentest thy face with painting, in vain shalt thou make thyself fair; thy lovers will despise thee, they will seek thy life.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/4#v31)</sup> For I have heard a voice as of a woman in travail, and the anguish as of her that bringeth forth her first child, the voice of the daughter of Zion, that bewaileth herself, that spreadeth her hands, saying, Woe is me now! for my soul is wearied because of murderers.

- The whole city shall flee at the noise of the horsemen and bowmen, and every city shall be forsaken with no man dwelling therein.
- Though Israel clothes herself with crimson and gold and paints her face, in vain does she make herself fair, for her lovers, the nations she followed, will despise her and seek her life; the voice of the daughter of Zion is heard mourning.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 8:11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)</sup> Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

    Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, we get comfortable sinning and set our hearts fully to do evil once we get away with it.

# Jeremiah 5: None That Executeth Judgment

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 5](/bible/jeremiah/5)

**[Jeremiah 5:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/5#v1)</sup> Run ye to and fro through the streets of Jerusalem, and see now, and know, and seek in the broad places thereof, if ye can find a man, if there be any that executeth judgment, that seeketh the truth; and I will pardon it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/5#v2)</sup> And though they say, The Lord liveth; surely they swear falsely.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/5#v3)</sup> O Lord, are not thine eyes upon the truth? thou hast stricken them, but they have not grieved; thou hast consumed them, but they have refused to receive correction: they have made their faces harder than a rock; they have refused to return.

- Run through the streets of Jerusalem and seek in the broad places for a man that executeth judgment and seeketh the truth, and if one righteous man be found the Most High will pardon it.
- Though they say the Lord liveth, they swear falsely; He has stricken and consumed them, but they refused correction and made their faces harder than a rock.

**[Jeremiah 5:4-5](/bible/jeremiah/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/5#v4)</sup> Therefore I said, Surely these are poor; they are foolish: for they know not the way of the Lord, nor the judgment of their God.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/5#v5)</sup> I will get me unto the great men, and will speak unto them; for they have known the way of the Lord, and the judgment of their God: but these have altogether broken the yoke, and burst the bonds.

- The poor are foolish and know not the way of the Most High nor the judgment of their God, for when you sin you should know a judgment comes behind it.
- Even the great men who should have known the way have altogether broken the yoke and burst the bonds.
  - **[Matthew 7:26](/bible/matthew/7#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/7#v26)</sup> And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand:

    Everyone that heareth His sayings and doeth them not is likened to a foolish man who built his house upon the sand, and great was the fall of it.

**[Jeremiah 5:6](/bible/jeremiah/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/5#v6)</sup> Wherefore a lion out of the forest shall slay them, and a wolf of the evenings shall spoil them, a leopard shall watch over their cities: every one that goeth out thence shall be torn in pieces: because their transgressions are many, and their backslidings are increased.

- A lion out of the forest, a wolf of the evenings, and a leopard shall watch over their cities and tear in pieces everyone that goes out, because their transgressions are many.
- This judgment comes through the Most High using the other nations against His people.

**[Jeremiah 5:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/5#v7)</sup> How shall I pardon thee for this? thy children have forsaken me, and sworn by them that are no gods: when I had fed them to the full, they then committed adultery, and assembled themselves by troops in the harlots’ houses.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/5#v8)</sup> They were as fed horses in the morning: every one neighed after his neighbour’s wife.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/5#v9)</sup> Shall I not visit for these things? saith the Lord: and shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?

- How shall He pardon them, when their children have forsaken Him and sworn by them that are no gods, bowing down and swearing to idols.
- When He had fed them to the full they committed adultery and assembled in the harlot's house, every one neighing after his neighbor's wife; shall He not visit for these things and be avenged on such a nation.

**[Jeremiah 5:10-11](/bible/jeremiah/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/5#v10)</sup> Go ye up upon her walls, and destroy; but make not a full end: take away her battlements; for they are not the Lord’s.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/5#v11)</sup> For the house of Israel and the house of Judah have dealt very treacherously against me, saith the Lord.

- Go up upon her walls and destroy, but make not a full end, and take away her battlements, for they are not the Most High's at this time.
- The house of Israel and the house of Judah have dealt very treacherously against Him, as our people still do today.

**[Jeremiah 5:12-13](/bible/jeremiah/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/5#v12)</sup> They have belied the Lord, and said, It is not he; neither shall evil come upon us; neither shall we see sword nor famine:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/5#v13)</sup> And the prophets shall become wind, and the word is not in them: thus shall it be done unto them.

- They belied the Most High and said it is not He, neither shall evil, sword, nor famine come upon them, denying that judgment would fall.
- The prophets shall become wind and the word is not in them, for there was no teaching going on.

**[Jeremiah 5:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/5#v14)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God of hosts, Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make my words in thy mouth fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/5#v15)</sup> Lo, I will bring a nation upon you from far, O house of Israel, saith the Lord: it is a mighty nation, it is an ancient nation, a nation whose language thou knowest not, neither understandest what they say.

- Because Jeremiah speaks this word, the Most High will make His words in his mouth fire to devour the people, and everything Jeremiah said came to pass.
- He will bring a mighty and ancient nation from far, whose language they know not, upon the house of Israel.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:49-50](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)**
    > <sup>[49](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)</sup> The Lord shall bring a nation against thee from far, from the end of the earth, as swift as the eagle flieth; a nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand;
    >
    > <sup>[50](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v50)</sup> A nation of fierce countenance, which shall not regard the person of the old, nor shew favour to the young:

    The Most High foretold He would bring a nation from the end of the earth, swift as an eagle, of fierce countenance and strange tongue, that regards neither old nor young.

**[Jeremiah 5:16-17](/bible/jeremiah/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/5#v16)</sup> Their quiver is as an open sepulchre, they are all mighty men.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/5#v17)</sup> And they shall eat up thine harvest, and thy bread, which thy sons and thy daughters should eat: they shall eat up thy flocks and thine herds: they shall eat up thy vines and thy fig trees: they shall impoverish thy fenced cities, wherein thou trustedst, with the sword.

- Their quiver is as an open sepulchre and they are all mighty men, the best of the nation for war against us.
- They shall eat up the harvest, the bread, the flocks, the herds, the vines, and the figs, and impoverish the fenced cities that Israel trusted in with the sword.

**[Jeremiah 5:18-19](/bible/jeremiah/5#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/5#v18)</sup> Nevertheless in those days, saith the Lord, I will not make a full end with you.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/5#v19)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when ye shall say, Wherefore doeth the Lord our God all these things unto us? then shalt thou answer them, Like as ye have forsaken me, and served strange gods in your land, so shall ye serve strangers in a land that is not yours.

- Even in those days He will not make a full end, but will leave a remnant when He brings the judgment.
- Because they forsook Him and served strange gods in their land, they shall serve strangers in a land that is not theirs, which is exactly our condition today, working and giving it right back to our oppressors.

**[Jeremiah 5:20-22](/bible/jeremiah/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/5#v20)</sup> Declare this in the house of Jacob, and publish it in Judah, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/5#v21)</sup> Hear now this, O foolish people, and without understanding; which have eyes, and see not; which have ears, and hear not:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/5#v22)</sup> Fear ye not me? saith the Lord: will ye not tremble at my presence, which have placed the sand for the bound of the sea by a perpetual decree, that it cannot pass it: and though the waves thereof toss themselves, yet can they not prevail; though they roar, yet can they not pass over it?

- Declare to the foolish people without understanding, who have eyes and see not and ears and hear not, asking whether they will not fear Him.
- He placed the sand for the bound of the sea by a perpetual decree, a law the waters cannot pass though they toss and roar, yet the people still do not fear that power.

**[Jeremiah 5:23-25](/bible/jeremiah/5#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/5#v23)</sup> But this people hath a revolting and a rebellious heart; they are revolted and gone.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/5#v24)</sup> Neither say they in their heart, Let us now fear the Lord our God, that giveth rain, both the former and the latter, in his season: he reserveth unto us the appointed weeks of the harvest.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/5#v25)</sup> Your iniquities have turned away these things, and your sins have withholden good things from you.

- These people have a revolting and rebellious heart and will not say in their heart, let us fear the Most High who gives the former and latter rain in his season.
- Their iniquities have turned away these things, for the Most High wants to bless us, but we cannot be blessed while in the midst of sin.

**[Jeremiah 5:26-28](/bible/jeremiah/5#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/5#v26)</sup> For among my people are found wicked men: they lay wait, as he that setteth snares; they set a trap, they catch men.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/5#v27)</sup> As a cage is full of birds, so are their houses full of deceit: therefore they are become great, and waxen rich.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/5#v28)</sup> They are waxen fat, they shine: yea, they overpass the deeds of the wicked: they judge not the cause, the cause of the fatherless, yet they prosper; and the right of the needy do they not judge.

- Among His people are found wicked men who lay wait as one that sets snares, catching men as a cage full of birds, which is happening today as we rob and kill one another.
- Their houses are full of deceit; they are waxen fat and shining and overpass the deeds of the wicked, yet they judge not the cause of the fatherless nor the right of the needy.

**[Jeremiah 5:29-31](/bible/jeremiah/5#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/5#v29)</sup> Shall I not visit for these things? saith the Lord: shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/5#v30)</sup> A wonderful and horrible thing is committed in the land;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/5#v31)</sup> The prophets prophesy falsely, and the priests bear rule by their means; and my people love to have it so: and what will ye do in the end thereof?

- Shall He not visit for these things and be avenged on such a wicked nation.
- A wonderful and horrible thing is committed in the land: the prophets prophesy falsely, the priests bear rule by their means, and the people love to have it so; what will they do in the end when it is time to be judged.

# Jeremiah 6: The Ancient Nation from the North

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 6](/bible/jeremiah/6)

**[Jeremiah 6:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/6#v1)</sup> O ye children of Benjamin, gather yourselves to flee out of the midst of Jerusalem, and blow the trumpet in Tekoa, and set up a sign of fire in Beth–haccerem: for evil appeareth out of the north, and great destruction.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)</sup> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.

- The children of Benjamin are told to flee out of Jerusalem, blow the trumpet in Tekoa, and set up a sign of fire, for evil and great destruction appear out of the north.
- The Most High has likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman, for He often speaks of the nation of Israel as a woman or a daughter.

**[Jeremiah 6:3-5](/bible/jeremiah/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/6#v3)</sup> The shepherds with their flocks shall come unto her; they shall pitch their tents against her round about; they shall feed every one in his place.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/6#v4)</sup> Prepare ye war against her; arise, and let us go up at noon. Woe unto us! for the day goeth away, for the shadows of the evening are stretched out.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/6#v5)</sup> Arise, and let us go by night, and let us destroy her palaces.

- The shepherds with their flocks come against her and pitch their tents round about, preparing war and going up against her at noon and by night.
- They set out to destroy her palaces, showing the nations the Most High raises up to come against Israel.

**[Jeremiah 6:6-8](/bible/jeremiah/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/6#v6)</sup> For thus hath the Lord of hosts said, Hew ye down trees, and cast a mount against Jerusalem: this is the city to be visited; she is wholly oppression in the midst of her.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/6#v7)</sup> As a fountain casteth out her waters, so she casteth out her wickedness: violence and spoil is heard in her; before me continually is grief and wounds.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/6#v8)</sup> Be thou instructed, O Jerusalem, lest my soul depart from thee; lest I make thee desolate, a land not inhabited.

- Hew down trees and cast a mount against Jerusalem, the city to be visited, wholly oppression in the midst of her, casting out her wickedness as a fountain casts out her waters.
- Be instructed, O Jerusalem, lest His soul depart and He make the land desolate, for at this time we were violent even among ourselves, killing, robbing, and committing adultery against each other.

**[Jeremiah 6:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/6#v9)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall throughly glean the remnant of Israel as a vine: turn back thine hand as a grapegatherer into the baskets.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/6#v10)</sup> To whom shall I speak, and give warning, that they may hear? behold, their ear is uncircumcised, and they cannot hearken: behold, the word of the Lord is unto them a reproach; they have no delight in it.

- They shall thoroughly glean the remnant of Israel as a grape gatherer turns his hand into the baskets.
- Their ear is uncircumcised and they cannot hearken; the word of the Most High is a reproach to them and they have no delight in it, a prime example of our people today.

**[Jeremiah 6:11-12](/bible/jeremiah/6#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/6#v11)</sup> Therefore I am full of the fury of the Lord; I am weary with holding in: I will pour it out upon the children abroad, and upon the assembly of young men together: for even the husband with the wife shall be taken, the aged with him that is full of days.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/6#v12)</sup> And their houses shall be turned unto others, with their fields and wives together: for I will stretch out my hand upon the inhabitants of the land, saith the Lord.

- The prophet is full of the fury of the Most High and weary with holding it in, and He will pour it out upon the children, the young men, the husband with the wife, and the aged alike.
- Their houses, fields, and wives shall be turned unto others, for He will stretch out His hand upon the inhabitants of the land.

**[Jeremiah 6:13](/bible/jeremiah/6#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/6#v13)</sup> For from the least of them even unto the greatest of them every one is given to covetousness; and from the prophet even unto the priest every one dealeth falsely.

- From the least to the greatest every one is given to covetousness, and from the prophet to the priest every one deals falsely, for the priests were only about gain at this time.
  - **[Isaiah 56:11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11)</sup> Yea, they are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain, from his quarter.

    They are greedy dogs that can never have enough and shepherds that cannot understand, every one looking to his own way and his own gain.

**[Jeremiah 6:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/6#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/6#v14)</sup> They have healed also the hurt of the daughter of my people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when there is no peace.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/6#v15)</sup> Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were not at all ashamed, neither could they blush: therefore they shall fall among them that fall: at the time that I visit them they shall be cast down, saith the Lord.

- They healed the hurt of the Most High's people slightly, saying peace, peace, when there is no peace.
- They were not ashamed when they committed abomination and could not blush; therefore they shall fall and be cast down in the time He visits them.

**[Jeremiah 6:16-17](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/6#v17)</sup> Also I set watchmen over you, saying, Hearken to the sound of the trumpet. But they said, We will not hearken.

- Stand in the ways and ask for the old paths, the good way we kept from the beginning, and walk in the laws of the Most High to find rest for your souls, but they said they would not walk therein.
- He set watchmen to say hearken to the sound of the trumpet, but they said they would not hearken and refused to listen to the prophets.

**[Jeremiah 6:18-20](/bible/jeremiah/6#v18)**

>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/6#v19)</sup> Hear, O earth: behold, I will bring evil upon this people, even the fruit of their thoughts, because they have not hearkened unto my words, nor to my law, but rejected it.

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/6#v20)</sup> To what purpose cometh there to me incense from Sheba, and the sweet cane from a far country? your burnt offerings are not acceptable, nor your sacrifices sweet unto me.

- Incense from Sheba and sweet cane from a far country are of no purpose; their burnt offerings and sacrifices are not acceptable to Him.
  - **[Isaiah 1:11](/bible/isaiah/1#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/1#v11)</sup> To what purpose is the multitude of your sacrifices unto me? saith the Lord: I am full of the burnt offerings of rams, and the fat of fed beasts; and I delight not in the blood of bullocks, or of lambs, or of he goats.

    The Most High stopped accepting the multitude of sacrifices, having no delight in the blood of bullocks, lambs, and goats from a sinful people that kept on sinning.

**[Jeremiah 6:21-23](/bible/jeremiah/6#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/6#v21)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will lay stumblingblocks before this people, and the fathers and the sons together shall fall upon them; the neighbour and his friend shall perish.

- He will lay stumbling blocks before this people, and fathers and sons, neighbor and friend, shall perish together.
- A cruel people with no mercy comes from the north country, laying hold on bow and spear, their voice roaring like the sea, riding upon horses set in array for war against the daughter of Zion.

- Gird with sackcloth, wallow in ashes, and make mourning as for an only son, for the spoiler shall suddenly come upon them.

# Jeremiah 7: Amend Your Ways, The Temple of the Lord

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 7](/bible/jeremiah/7)

**[Jeremiah 7:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/7#v1)</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/7#v2)</sup> Stand in the gate of the Lord’s house, and proclaim there this word, and say, Hear the word of the Lord, all ye of Judah, that enter in at these gates to worship the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/7#v3)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, Amend your ways and your doings, and I will cause you to dwell in this place.

- The word came to Jeremiah to stand in the gate of the Lord's house and proclaim to all Judah that enter in to worship.
- Amend your ways and your doings and He will cause you to dwell in this place, which ties back to how our iniquities turn away the good things, for as long as we keep the law He will bless us.

**[Jeremiah 7:4-7](/bible/jeremiah/7#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/7#v4)</sup> Trust ye not in lying words, saying, The temple of the Lord, The temple of the Lord, The temple of the Lord, are these.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/7#v5)</sup> For if ye throughly amend your ways and your doings; if ye throughly execute judgment between a man and his neighbour;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/7#v6)</sup> If ye oppress not the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, and shed not innocent blood in this place, neither walk after other gods to your hurt:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/7#v7)</sup> Then will I cause you to dwell in this place, in the land that I gave to your fathers, for ever and ever.

- Trust not in lying words, saying, The temple of the Lord, the temple of the Lord, as though the building itself would save them.
- If they truly amend their ways, execute judgment between a man and his neighbor, oppress not the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow, shed not innocent blood, and walk not after other gods, then He will let them dwell in the land He gave their fathers.

**[Jeremiah 7:8-11](/bible/jeremiah/7#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/7#v8)</sup> Behold, ye trust in lying words, that cannot profit.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/7#v9)</sup> Will ye steal, murder, and commit adultery, and swear falsely, and burn incense unto Baal, and walk after other gods whom ye know not;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/7#v10)</sup> And come and stand before me in this house, which is called by my name, and say, We are delivered to do all these abominations?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/7#v11)</sup> Is this house, which is called by my name, become a den of robbers in your eyes? Behold, even I have seen it, saith the Lord.

- They trust in lying words that cannot profit while they steal, murder, commit adultery, swear falsely, and burn incense unto Baal.
- They walk after other gods and then come and stand before Him in the house called by His name, saying they are delivered to do all these abominations; has this house become a den of robbers in their eyes.

**[Jeremiah 7:12-13](/bible/jeremiah/7#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/7#v12)</sup> But go ye now unto my place which was in Shiloh, where I set my name at the first, and see what I did to it for the wickedness of my people Israel.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/7#v13)</sup> And now, because ye have done all these works, saith the Lord, and I spake unto you, rising up early and speaking, but ye heard not; and I called you, but ye answered not;

- Go now to Shiloh where He first set His name and see what He did to it for the wickedness of His people Israel; we cannot always blame the other nations, for we did these things while still in our own land.
- Because they did all these works and He spoke to them rising up early, but they heard not, and He called but they answered not.

**[Jeremiah 7:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/7#v14)</sup> Therefore will I do unto this house, which is called by my name, wherein ye trust, and unto the place which I gave to you and to your fathers, as I have done to Shiloh.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/7#v15)</sup> And I will cast you out of my sight, as I have cast out all your brethren, even the whole seed of Ephraim.

- Therefore He will do to this house, called by His name and trusted in, as He did to Shiloh.
- He will cast them out of His sight as He cast out their brethren, the whole seed of Ephraim, the northern kingdom.

**[Jeremiah 7:16-18](/bible/jeremiah/7#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/7#v16)</sup> Therefore pray not thou for this people, neither lift up cry nor prayer for them, neither make intercession to me: for I will not hear thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/7#v17)</sup> Seest thou not what they do in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/7#v18)</sup> The children gather wood, and the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead their dough, to make cakes to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto other gods, that they may provoke me to anger.

- Pray not for this people, neither lift up cry nor prayer nor make intercession, for He will not hear.
- The children gather wood, the fathers kindle the fire, and the women knead their dough to make cakes to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings to other gods to provoke Him to anger.

**[Jeremiah 7:19-20](/bible/jeremiah/7#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/7#v19)</sup> Do they provoke me to anger? saith the Lord: do they not provoke themselves to the confusion of their own faces?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/7#v20)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, mine anger and my fury shall be poured out upon this place, upon man, and upon beast, and upon the trees of the field, and upon the fruit of the ground; and it shall burn, and shall not be quenched.

- Do they provoke Him to anger, or do they not rather provoke themselves to the confusion of their own faces.
- His anger and fury shall be poured out upon this place, upon man and beast, the trees and the fruit, and it shall burn and not be quenched.

**[Jeremiah 7:21-23](/bible/jeremiah/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/7#v21)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Put your burnt offerings unto your sacrifices, and eat flesh.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/7#v22)</sup> For I spake not unto your fathers, nor commanded them in the day that I brought them out of the land of Egypt, concerning burnt offerings or sacrifices:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/7#v23)</sup> But this thing commanded I them, saying, Obey my voice, and I will be your God, and ye shall be my people: and walk ye in all the ways that I have commanded you, that it may be well unto you.

- Put your burnt offerings unto your sacrifices and eat flesh, for He spoke not to their fathers concerning burnt offerings when He brought them out of Egypt.
- This one thing He commanded them: obey My voice, and I will be your God and ye shall be My people, and walk in all the ways He commanded, that it may be well with them.

**[Jeremiah 7:24-26](/bible/jeremiah/7#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/7#v24)</sup> But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear, but walked in the counsels and in the imagination of their evil heart, and went backward, and not forward.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/7#v25)</sup> Since the day that your fathers came forth out of the land of Egypt unto this day I have even sent unto you all my servants the prophets, daily rising up early and sending them:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/7#v26)</sup> Yet they hearkened not unto me, nor inclined their ear, but hardened their neck: they did worse than their fathers.

- They hearkened not nor inclined their ear but walked in the counsels and imagination of their evil heart and went backward, not forward.
- He sent all His servants the prophets daily, rising up early, yet they hearkened not, hardened their neck, and did worse than their fathers.

**[Jeremiah 7:27-28](/bible/jeremiah/7#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/7#v27)</sup> Therefore thou shalt speak all these words unto them; but they will not hearken to thee: thou shalt also call unto them; but they will not answer thee.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/7#v28)</sup> But thou shalt say unto them, This is a nation that obeyeth not the voice of the Lord their God, nor receiveth correction: truth is perished, and is cut off from their mouth.

- The Most High tells Jeremiah he shall speak all these words to them, but they will not hearken, and call to them, but they will not answer.
- This is a nation that obeyeth not His voice nor receiveth correction; truth is perished and cut off from their mouth.

**[Jeremiah 7:29-31](/bible/jeremiah/7#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/7#v29)</sup> Cut off thine hair, O Jerusalem, and cast it away, and take up a lamentation on high places; for the Lord hath rejected and forsaken the generation of his wrath.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/7#v30)</sup> For the children of Judah have done evil in my sight, saith the Lord: they have set their abominations in the house which is called by my name, to pollute it.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/7#v31)</sup> And they have built the high places of Tophet, which is in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire; which I commanded them not, neither came it into my heart.

- Cut off thine hair, O Jerusalem, and take up a lamentation, for the Most High has rejected and forsaken the generation of His wrath.
- The children of Judah set their abominations in the house called by His name and built the high places of Tophet to burn their sons and daughters in the fire, which points to the abortions of today, a thing He never commanded.

**[Jeremiah 7:32-34](/bible/jeremiah/7#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/7#v32)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that it shall no more be called Tophet, nor the valley of the son of Hinnom, but the valley of slaughter: for they shall bury in Tophet, till there be no place.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/7#v33)</sup> And the carcases of this people shall be meat for the fowls of the heaven, and for the beasts of the earth; and none shall fray them away.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/7#v34)</sup> Then will I cause to cease from the cities of Judah, and from the streets of Jerusalem, the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride: for the land shall be desolate.

- The days come when it shall be called the valley of slaughter, for they shall bury in Tophet till there is no more room.
- The carcasses shall be meat for the fowls and beasts with none to fray them away, and He will cause to cease the voice of mirth, gladness, the bridegroom, and the bride, for the land shall be desolate.
