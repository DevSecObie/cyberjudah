**[Jeremiah 3:1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)</sup> They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted? but thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me, saith the Lord.

- If a man puts away his wife and she becomes another man's, he cannot return to her, for the land would be greatly polluted.
- Israel has played the harlot with many lovers, yet the Most High still says return again to Me.

**[Jeremiah 3:2-3](/bible/jeremiah/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/3#v2)</sup> Lift up thine eyes unto the high places, and see where thou hast not been lien with. In the ways hast thou sat for them, as the Arabian in the wilderness; and thou hast polluted the land with thy whoredoms and with thy wickedness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/3#v3)</sup> Therefore the showers have been withholden, and there hath been no latter rain; and thou hadst a whore’s forehead, thou refusedst to be ashamed.

- Lift up your eyes to the high places and see where Israel has not been lien with; sitting for lovers like the Arabian in the wilderness, she polluted the land with her whoredoms and wickedness.
- This is spiritual fornication and idolatry, learning about Allah from the Arabians and celebrating Islam; therefore the showers and the latter rain were withheld, and she had a whore's forehead and refused to be ashamed.

**[Jeremiah 3:4-5](/bible/jeremiah/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/3#v4)</sup> Wilt thou not from this time cry unto me, My father, thou art the guide of my youth?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/3#v5)</sup> Will he reserve his anger for ever? will he keep it to the end? Behold, thou hast spoken and done evil things as thou couldest.

- The Most High asks whether Israel will now cry unto Him, My father, thou art the guide of my youth.
- Will He reserve His anger forever? Yet Israel has spoken and done evil things as much as he could.

**[Jeremiah 3:6-8](/bible/jeremiah/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/3#v6)</sup> The Lord said also unto me in the days of Josiah the king, Hast thou seen that which backsliding Israel hath done? she is gone up upon every high mountain and under every green tree, and there hath played the harlot.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/3#v7)</sup> And I said after she had done all these things, Turn thou unto me. But she returned not. And her treacherous sister Judah saw it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/3#v8)</sup> And I saw, when for all the causes whereby backsliding Israel committed adultery I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce; yet her treacherous sister Judah feared not, but went and played the harlot also.

- In the days of Josiah the Most High saw that backsliding Israel had gone up upon every high mountain and under every green tree and played the harlot, and her treacherous sister Judah saw it.
- For all the causes of her adultery He put her away and gave her a bill of divorce, no longer dealing with the northern kingdom, yet Judah feared not and played the harlot also.

**[Jeremiah 3:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/3#v9)</sup> And it came to pass through the lightness of her whoredom, that she defiled the land, and committed adultery with stones and with stocks.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/3#v10)</sup> And yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah hath not turned unto me with her whole heart, but feignedly, saith the Lord.

- Through the lightness of her whoredom Israel defiled the land and committed adultery with stones and with stocks: the stones of Islam and the stock, the wood, of Christianity.
- Yet for all this her treacherous sister Judah did not turn to Him with her whole heart, but only feignedly.

**[Jeremiah 3:11-13](/bible/jeremiah/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/3#v11)</sup> And the Lord said unto me, The backsliding Israel hath justified herself more than treacherous Judah.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/3#v12)</sup> Go and proclaim these words toward the north, and say, Return, thou backsliding Israel, saith the Lord; and I will not cause mine anger to fall upon you: for I am merciful, saith the Lord, and I will not keep anger for ever.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/3#v13)</sup> Only acknowledge thine iniquity, that thou hast transgressed against the Lord thy God, and hast scattered thy ways to the strangers under every green tree, and ye have not obeyed my voice, saith the Lord.

- Backsliding Israel has justified herself more than treacherous Judah; the Most High sends the word toward the north, saying return, backsliding Israel, for I am merciful and will not keep anger forever.
- We have a merciful God whose mercy endures forever; only acknowledge thine iniquity, that thou hast transgressed against the Most High and scattered thy ways to the strangers under every green tree.

**[Jeremiah 3:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/3#v14)</sup> Turn, O backsliding children, saith the Lord; for I am married unto you: and I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Zion:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/3#v15)</sup> And I will give you pastors according to mine heart, which shall feed you with knowledge and understanding.

- Turn, O backsliding children, for the Most High is married unto us; He will take one of a city and two of a family and bring them to Zion.
- He will give pastors according to His heart to feed us with knowledge and understanding; a pastor is supposed to feed us with the laws of God, so if your pastor is not feeding you God's laws, you need to run.
  - **[Malachi 2:7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)</sup> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.

    The priest's lips should keep knowledge, for knowledge is God's laws, which the pastor is supposed to feed the people.

**[Jeremiah 3:16-17](/bible/jeremiah/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/3#v16)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when ye be multiplied and increased in the land, in those days, saith the Lord, they shall say no more, The ark of the covenant of the Lord: neither shall it come to mind: neither shall they remember it; neither shall they visit it; neither shall that be done any more.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/3#v17)</sup> At that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of the Lord; and all the nations shall be gathered unto it, to the name of the Lord, to Jerusalem: neither shall they walk any more after the imagination of their evil heart.

- When Israel is multiplied and increased in the land, they shall no more speak of the ark of the covenant nor remember it.
- At that time they shall call Jerusalem the throne of the Most High, the capital city on the planet, and all the nations shall be gathered unto it, no more walking after their evil hearts.

**[Jeremiah 3:18](/bible/jeremiah/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/3#v18)</sup> In those days the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel, and they shall come together out of the land of the north to the land that I have given for an inheritance unto your fathers.

- In those days the house of Judah shall walk with the house of Israel, coming together out of the land of the north.
- The land of the north is North America; that is the prophecy, that we are coming out to the land given for an inheritance to our fathers.

**[Jeremiah 3:19-20](/bible/jeremiah/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/3#v19)</sup> But I said, How shall I put thee among the children, and give thee a pleasant land, a goodly heritage of the hosts of nations? and I said, Thou shalt call me, My father; and shalt not turn away from me.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/3#v20)</sup> Surely as a wife treacherously departeth from her husband, so have ye dealt treacherously with me, O house of Israel, saith the Lord.

- The Most High asks how He shall put Israel among the children and give him a pleasant land, a goodly heritage; and He desires that Israel call Him My father and not turn away.
- But surely as a wife treacherously departs from her husband, so has the house of Israel dealt treacherously with the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 3:21-22](/bible/jeremiah/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/3#v21)</sup> A voice was heard upon the high places, weeping and supplications of the children of Israel: for they have perverted their way, and they have forgotten the Lord their God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/3#v22)</sup> Return, ye backsliding children, and I will heal your backslidings. Behold, we come unto thee; for thou art the Lord our God.

- A voice is heard upon the high places, weeping and supplications of the children of Israel, for they have perverted their way and forgotten the Most High their God.
- Return, ye backsliding children, and He will heal your backslidings; the call is to repent and return from the high places of idolatry.

**[Jeremiah 3:23-25](/bible/jeremiah/3#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/3#v23)</sup> Truly in vain is salvation hoped for from the hills, and from the multitude of mountains: truly in the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/3#v24)</sup> For shame hath devoured the labour of our fathers from our youth; their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/3#v25)</sup> We lie down in our shame, and our confusion covereth us: for we have sinned against the Lord our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even unto this day, and have not obeyed the voice of the Lord our God.

- In vain is salvation hoped for from the hills and the multitude of mountains; truly in the Most High our God is the salvation of Israel.
- Shame has devoured the labour of our fathers, and we lie down in our shame while our confusion covers us; in Christianity people are confused, taught that a degree or college will make everything okay while the nation still hates them, because they have not been properly trained in the Bible or fed God's laws, and we and our fathers have sinned and not obeyed the voice of the Most High to this day.
