Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 28](/bible/jeremiah/28)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 28 through 31. It opens with Hananiah, a so-called prophet who prophesied peace and a short captivity against the word the Most High had given Jeremiah, and who dies the same year for teaching rebellion. Jeremiah then writes to the captives in Babylon to build, plant, and seek the peace of the city until the seventy years are accomplished, warning against the false prophets among them. The reading closes with the great promise of restoration: the time of Jacob's trouble out of which Israel is saved, the gathering from the four corners of the earth in a straight way, and the new covenant written in the inward parts.

**[Jeremiah 28:1-4](/bible/jeremiah/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/28#v1)</sup> And it came to pass the same year, in the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the fourth year, and in the fifth month, that Hananiah the son of Azur the prophet, which was of Gibeon, spake unto me in the house of the Lord, in the presence of the priests and of all the people, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/28#v2)</sup> Thus speaketh the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, saying, I have broken the yoke of the king of Babylon.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/28#v3)</sup> Within two full years will I bring again into this place all the vessels of the Lord’s house, that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took away from this place, and carried them to Babylon:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/28#v4)</sup> And I will bring again to this place Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, with all the captives of Judah, that went into Babylon, saith the Lord: for I will break the yoke of the king of Babylon.

- In the beginning of the reign of Zedekiah, in the fifth month, Hananiah the son of Azur, a prophet of Gibeon, spake in the house of the Lord before the priests and all the people.
- This so-called prophet spoke the things he claimed the Most High was going to do: that He had broken the yoke of the king of Babylon and within two full years would bring back the vessels of the Lord's house and Jeconiah with all the captives of Judah.

**[Jeremiah 28:5-9](/bible/jeremiah/28#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/28#v5)</sup> Then the prophet Jeremiah said unto the prophet Hananiah in the presence of the priests, and in the presence of all the people that stood in the house of the Lord,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/28#v6)</sup> Even the prophet Jeremiah said, Amen: the Lord do so: the Lord perform thy words which thou hast prophesied, to bring again the vessels of the Lord’s house, and all that is carried away captive, from Babylon into this place.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/28#v7)</sup> Nevertheless hear thou now this word that I speak in thine ears, and in the ears of all the people;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/28#v8)</sup> The prophets that have been before me and before thee of old prophesied both against many countries, and against great kingdoms, of war, and of evil, and of pestilence.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/28#v9)</sup> The prophet which prophesieth of peace, when the word of the prophet shall come to pass, then shall the prophet be known, that the Lord hath truly sent him.

- Jeremiah answered Amen, the Lord do so, the Lord perform thy words to bring again the vessels and the captives; but he told Hananiah not to hold on to that.
- The prophets before them prophesied of war, of evil, and of pestilence, nothing of peace; so when a prophet prophesies peace, only when the word comes to pass is that prophet known to be truly sent of the Lord, and if it does not come to pass, the Lord is not working with him.

**[Jeremiah 28:10-11](/bible/jeremiah/28#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/28#v10)</sup> Then Hananiah the prophet took the yoke from off the prophet Jeremiah’s neck, and brake it.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/28#v11)</sup> And Hananiah spake in the presence of all the people, saying, Thus saith the Lord; Even so will I break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all nations within the space of two full years. And the prophet Jeremiah went his way.

- Hananiah took the yoke from off Jeremiah's neck, the yoke he wore in his captivity, and broke it before all the people.
- He said thus saith the Lord, even so will I break the yoke of Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon from the neck of all nations within two full years; and Jeremiah went his way.

**[Jeremiah 28:12-14](/bible/jeremiah/28#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/28#v12)</sup> Then the word of the Lord came unto Jeremiah the prophet, after that Hananiah the prophet had broken the yoke from off the neck of the prophet Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/28#v13)</sup> Go and tell Hananiah, saying, Thus saith the Lord; Thou hast broken the yokes of wood; but thou shalt make for them yokes of iron.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/28#v14)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; I have put a yoke of iron upon the neck of all these nations, that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon; and they shall serve him: and I have given him the beasts of the field also.

- The word of the Lord came to Jeremiah: thou hast broken the yokes of wood, but thou shalt make for them yokes of iron.
- The Most High had put a yoke of iron upon the neck of all these nations that they may serve Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, who was the superpower at this time, and He gave him the beasts of the field also.

**[Jeremiah 28:15-17](/bible/jeremiah/28#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/28#v15)</sup> Then said the prophet Jeremiah unto Hananiah the prophet, Hear now, Hananiah; The Lord hath not sent thee; but thou makest this people to trust in a lie.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/28#v16)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will cast thee from off the face of the earth: this year thou shalt die, because thou hast taught rebellion against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/28#v17)</sup> So Hananiah the prophet died the same year in the seventh month.

- Jeremiah told Hananiah the Lord had not sent him, but he made the people trust in a lie and was leading his people astray.
- Because he taught rebellion against the Lord, teaching the people to go against the Most High instead of accepting that they had to go through captivity, the Lord would cast him from off the face of the earth and he would die that year; Hananiah died the same year in the seventh month.

- The Most High will bring again the captivity of Jacob's tents; the city shall be built upon her own heap, the palace remain, and out of them shall proceed thanksgiving and the voice of them that make merry, and He will multiply them and they shall not be few.
- He will glorify them and their children shall be as aforetime, their congregation established before Him with the Lord in the midst; nobles shall be of themselves and their governor from the midst of them, so no other nation rules over us, we rule ourselves under Christ who sets the order of who rules.
- The whirlwind of the Lord goes forth with fury upon the head of the wicked, and His fierce anger shall not return until He has performed the intent of His heart; in the latter days ye shall consider it, for He comes against the wicked of the other nations and those of our own people who will not submit to the laws of God.

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/31#v8)</sup> Behold, I will bring them from the north country, and gather them from the coasts of the earth, and with them the blind and the lame, the woman with child and her that travaileth with child together: a great company shall return thither.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/31#v9)</sup> They shall come with weeping, and with supplications will I lead them: I will cause them to walk by the rivers of waters in a straight way, wherein they shall not stumble: for I am a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my firstborn.

- Sing with gladness for Jacob and shout among the chief of the nations, saying, O Lord, save thy people the remnant of Israel; He will bring them from the north country and gather them from the coasts of the earth, the blind and the lame, the woman with child, a great company returning.
- They shall come with weeping and supplications, and He will lead them and cause them to walk by the rivers of waters in a straight way wherein they shall not stumble, for He is a father to Israel and Ephraim is His firstborn; the straight way is keeping God's laws.
- Ephraim being the firstborn means Israel is the firstborn of the Most High, set higher than the kings of the earth, who are the other nations, so all of Israel shall be over all the nations; the reason Ephraim was singled out is the tribes were not the first tribe that came, but the promise is to all Israel.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)**

    Stand in the ways and ask for the old paths, the good way, and walk therein to find rest for your souls, though they said we will not; the old paths are keeping God's laws.

- Just as a husband and wife have a covenant for their marriage, the Most High uses that analogy for the covenant we have with Him.
- He will put His law in their inward parts and write it in their hearts, and be their God and they His people; they shall teach no more every man his neighbor, for they shall all know Him and keep His laws from the least to the greatest, and He will forgive their iniquity and remember their sin no more.

- For the Most High to cast away His people, those ordinances would have to depart, the sun would have to stop shining, the moon and stars stop giving light; that is not going to happen, so He is not casting away the seed of Israel.
- Only a small percentage of the earth has been discovered and the heavens above cannot be measured, so He will not cast off all the seed of Israel for all that they have done; you can even see where the seas are divided from the coast, the Pacific and the Atlantic, one seed of water divided from another.
