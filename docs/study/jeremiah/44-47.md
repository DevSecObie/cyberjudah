Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 44](/bible/jeremiah/44)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 44 through 47, coming to the end of the book of Jeremiah in the Babylonian captivity. The Most High tells the remnant that fled to Egypt that the very curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) will pursue and overtake them there, because they went right back to idolatry, burning incense to the queen of heaven. The women worshipped without their men and the men had no rule over their houses, the same disorder we see in Babylon the great today. Then come the words of comfort to Baruch the scribe, whose writings are preserved in the Apocrypha, and the prophecies against the gentile nations, Egypt and the Philistines, that had a hand in spoiling Israel. This is the book we are the people of, and the Most High raised up His prophets, the Israelites, rising early to warn us not to do the abominable things He hates.

**[Jeremiah 44:1-4](/bible/jeremiah/44#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/44#v1)</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews which dwell in the land of Egypt, which dwell at Migdol, and at Tahpanhes, and at Noph, and in the country of Pathros, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/44#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Ye have seen all the evil that I have brought upon Jerusalem, and upon all the cities of Judah; and, behold, this day they are a desolation, and no man dwelleth therein,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/44#v3)</sup> Because of their wickedness which they have committed to provoke me to anger, in that they went to burn incense, and to serve other gods, whom they knew not, neither they, ye, nor your fathers.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/44#v4)</sup> Howbeit I sent unto you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending them, saying, Oh, do not this abominable thing that I hate.

- The word came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews dwelling in the land of Egypt and in the country of Pathros; the Most High reminded them of all the evil He had brought on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, now a desolation where no man dwelt, because of the wickedness they committed provoking Him to anger.
- They went to burn incense and serve other gods that neither they nor their fathers knew, yet the Most High sent all His servants the prophets, rising early and sending them, pleading with them not to do this abominable thing that He hates.
- The same work goes on today; we fill up the street corners all over the earth bringing forth the glorious gospel of the one true living God and Christ, the prophets He raised up to give warning.
  - **[Amos 2:11](/bible/amos/2#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/amos/2#v11)</sup> And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazarites. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the Lord.

    The prophets come out of the nation of Israel and no other, because the Most High is in the midst of Israel and none else and the Bible is given to the Israelites and none else.

**[Jeremiah 44:5-8](/bible/jeremiah/44#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/44#v5)</sup> But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness, to burn no incense unto other gods.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/44#v6)</sup> Wherefore my fury and mine anger was poured forth, and was kindled in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem; and they are wasted and desolate, as at this day.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/44#v7)</sup> Therefore now thus saith the Lord, the God of hosts, the God of Israel; Wherefore commit ye this great evil against your souls, to cut off from you man and woman, child and suckling, out of Judah, to leave you none to remain;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/44#v8)</sup> In that ye provoke me unto wrath with the works of your hands, burning incense unto other gods in the land of Egypt, whither ye be gone to dwell, that ye might cut yourselves off, and that ye might be a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

- They hearkened not nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness and burn no incense to other gods, so the Most High's fury and anger was poured forth and kindled in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem until they were wasted and desolate.
- He asks why they commit this great evil against their own souls, cutting off man and woman, child and suckling out of Judah, leaving none to remain.
- They provoke Him to wrath with the works of their hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt where they had gone to dwell, cutting themselves off to become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth.
  - **[Proverbs 8:36](/bible/proverbs/8#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/proverbs/8#v36)</sup> But he that sinneth against me wrongeth his own soul: all they that hate me love death.

    He that sins against the Most High wrongs his own soul, for the wages of sin is death, and they that hate Him love death.

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/44#v9)</sup> Have ye forgotten the wickedness of your fathers, and the wickedness of the kings of Judah, and the wickedness of their wives, and your own wickedness, and the wickedness of your wives, which they have committed in the land of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/44#v10)</sup> They are not humbled even unto this day, neither have they feared, nor walked in my law, nor in my statutes, that I set before you and before your fathers.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/44#v11)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will set my face against you for evil, and to cut off all Judah.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/44#v12)</sup> And I will take the remnant of Judah, that have set their faces to go into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, and they shall all be consumed, and fall in the land of Egypt; they shall even be consumed by the sword and by the famine: they shall die, from the least even unto the greatest, by the sword and by the famine: and they shall be an execration, and an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/44#v13)</sup> For I will punish them that dwell in the land of Egypt, as I have punished Jerusalem, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/44#v14)</sup> So that none of the remnant of Judah, which are gone into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, shall escape or remain, that they should return into the land of Judah, to the which they have a desire to return to dwell there: for none shall return but such as shall escape.

- The Most High asks whether they have forgotten the wickedness of their fathers, the kings of Judah, their wives, and their own wickedness committed in the land of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem; they were not humbled even to that day, nor did they fear or walk in His law and statutes.
- Therefore He will set His face against them for evil, to cut off all Judah, and take the remnant that set their faces to go into Egypt to sojourn there; they shall all be consumed and fall by the sword and the famine, from the least to the greatest.
- They shall become an execration, an astonishment, a curse, and a reproach; only a small number escaping the sword shall return, for none shall return to Judah though they have a desire to dwell there.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:45](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v45)</sup> Moreover all these curses shall come upon thee, and shall pursue thee, and overtake thee, till thou be destroyed; because thou hearkenedst not unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to keep his commandments and his statutes which he commanded thee:

    All these curses of the law shall come upon them, pursue them, and overtake them until they are destroyed, even those who sought refuge in Egypt, because they hearkened not to the voice of the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 44:15-19](/bible/jeremiah/44#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/44#v15)</sup> Then all the men which knew that their wives had burned incense unto other gods, and all the women that stood by, a great multitude, even all the people that dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/44#v16)</sup> As for the word that thou hast spoken unto us in the name of the Lord, we will not hearken unto thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/44#v17)</sup> But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn incense unto the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, as we have done, we, and our fathers, our kings, and our princes, in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem: for then had we plenty of victuals, and were well, and saw no evil.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/44#v18)</sup> But since we left off to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, we have wanted all things, and have been consumed by the sword and by the famine.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/44#v19)</sup> And when we burned incense to the queen of heaven, and poured out drink offerings unto her, did we make her cakes to worship her, and pour out drink offerings unto her, without our men?

- All the men who knew their wives had burned incense to other gods, and all the women, a great multitude in Egypt and Pathros, answered Jeremiah defiantly.
- These men did not rule their houses well and had no authority over their households; their wives were in the midst of idolatry, the same disorder we see in this place spiritually called Egypt, in Babylon the great today.
- They said they would not hearken to the word spoken in the name of the Most High but would perform their vows to burn incense to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings, claiming they had plenty of victuals and saw no evil until they left off, and then wanted all things and were consumed by the sword and famine.
- The women declared they made their cakes to worship her and poured out drink offerings without their men; the times women gather together without their men, such as baby showers with cakes and drinks and partying, are burning incense to other gods to keep in mind.

**[Jeremiah 44:20-23](/bible/jeremiah/44#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/44#v20)</sup> Then Jeremiah said unto all the people, to the men, and to the women, and to all the people which had given him that answer, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/44#v21)</sup> The incense that ye burned in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, ye, and your fathers, your kings, and your princes, and the people of the land, did not the Lord remember them, and came it not into his mind?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/44#v22)</sup> So that the Lord could no longer bear, because of the evil of your doings, and because of the abominations which ye have committed; therefore is your land a desolation, and an astonishment, and a curse, without an inhabitant, as at this day.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/44#v23)</sup> Because ye have burned incense, and because ye have sinned against the Lord, and have not obeyed the voice of the Lord, nor walked in his law, nor in his statutes, nor in his testimonies; therefore this evil is happened unto you, as at this day.

- Jeremiah answered all the people, men and women, that the incense they burned in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, they and their fathers, kings, and princes, the Most High remembered and it came into His mind.
- The Most High could no longer bear the evil of their doings and the abominations they committed, therefore their land became a desolation, an astonishment, and a curse without an inhabitant as at that day.
- This evil happened to them because they burned incense and sinned against the Most High and did not obey His voice nor walk in His law, statutes, and testimonies.

**[Jeremiah 44:24-28](/bible/jeremiah/44#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/44#v24)</sup> Moreover Jeremiah said unto all the people, and to all the women, Hear the word of the Lord, all Judah that are in the land of Egypt:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/44#v25)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, saying; Ye and your wives have both spoken with your mouths, and fulfilled with your hand, saying, We will surely perform our vows that we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her: ye will surely accomplish your vows, and surely perform your vows.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/44#v26)</sup> Therefore hear ye the word of the Lord, all Judah that dwell in the land of Egypt; Behold, I have sworn by my great name, saith the Lord, that my name shall no more be named in the mouth of any man of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, The Lord God liveth.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/44#v27)</sup> Behold, I will watch over them for evil, and not for good: and all the men of Judah that are in the land of Egypt shall be consumed by the sword and by the famine, until there be an end of them.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/44#v28)</sup> Yet a small number that escape the sword shall return out of the land of Egypt into the land of Judah, and all the remnant of Judah, that are gone into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, shall know whose words shall stand, mine, or theirs.

- Jeremiah told all the people and all the women that they and their wives had both spoken with their mouths and fulfilled with their hands their vows to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and would surely accomplish them.
- Therefore the Most High swore by His great name that His name shall no more be named in the mouth of any man of Judah in all Egypt, saying "The Lord GOD liveth"; He will watch over them for evil and not for good until they are consumed by the sword and famine.
- Only a small number escaping the sword shall return out of Egypt into Judah, and all the remnant that went to Egypt shall know whose words shall stand, His or theirs.

**[Jeremiah 44:29-30](/bible/jeremiah/44#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/44#v29)</sup> And this shall be a sign unto you, saith the Lord, that I will punish you in this place, that ye may know that my words shall surely stand against you for evil:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/44#v30)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will give Pharaoh–hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies, and into the hand of them that seek his life; as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, his enemy, and that sought his life.

- The Most High gives a sign that He will punish them in that place so they may know His words shall surely stand against them for evil; He will give Pharaoh-hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies as He gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.
- Evil does not come merely by chance; the Most High brings that evil upon us when we turn from Him and follow our own mind, sinning against the one true living God in Christ.

# Jeremiah 45: The Word of Comfort to Baruch the Scribe

**[Jeremiah 45:1-5](/bible/jeremiah/45#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/45#v1)</sup> The word that Jeremiah the prophet spake unto Baruch the son of Neriah, when he had written these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/45#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, unto thee, O Baruch;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/45#v3)</sup> Thou didst say, Woe is me now! for the Lord hath added grief to my sorrow; I fainted in my sighing, and I find no rest.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/45#v4)</sup> Thus shalt thou say unto him, The Lord saith thus; Behold, that which I have built will I break down, and that which I have planted I will pluck up, even this whole land.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/45#v5)</sup> And seekest thou great things for thyself? seek them not: for, behold, I will bring evil upon all flesh, saith the Lord: but thy life will I give unto thee for a prey in all places whither thou goest.

- This is the word Jeremiah spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah when he had written these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah.
- The Most High addresses him directly, "unto thee O Baruch," and the words of the scribe Baruch are preserved in the Apocrypha, so get yourself a 1611 King James Version Bible with the Apocrypha.
- Baruch had said woe is me, for the Most High added grief to his sorrow and he fainted in his sighing and found no rest; the Most High answers that what He built He will break down and what He planted He will pluck up, even this whole land.
- Baruch is told not to seek great things for himself, for the Most High will bring evil upon all flesh, but his life He will give him for a prey in all places wherever he goes.
  - **[Baruch 2:29-33](/bible/baruch/2#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/baruch/2#v29)</sup> If ye will not hear my voice, surely this very great multitude shall be turned into a small number among the nations, where I will scatter them.
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/baruch/2#v30)</sup> For I knew that they would not hear me, because it is a stiffnecked people: but in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/baruch/2#v31)</sup> And shall know that I am the Lord their God: for I will give them an heart, and ears to hear:
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/baruch/2#v32)</sup> And they shall praise me in the land of their captivity, and think upon my name,
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/baruch/2#v33)</sup> And return from their stiff neck, and from their wicked deeds: for they shall remember the way of their fathers, which sinned before the Lord.

    This is the understanding the christian church lacks; our forefathers learned through the prophets that in the land of our captivity we would remember ourselves, and one way we think upon His name is rehearsing His righteous acts at the Passover.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    In our affliction we will seek Him early, so in the land of our captivity He gives us a heart and ears to hear.

# Jeremiah 46: The Prophecy Against Egypt

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 46](/bible/jeremiah/46)

**[Jeremiah 46:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/46#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/46#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord which came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Gentiles;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/46#v2)</sup> Against Egypt, against the army of Pharaoh–necho king of Egypt, which was by the river Euphrates in Carchemish, which Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon smote in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/46#v3)</sup> Order ye the buckler and shield, and draw near to battle.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/46#v4)</sup> Harness the horses; and get up, ye horsemen, and stand forth with your helmets; furbish the spears, and put on the brigandines.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/46#v5)</sup> Wherefore have I seen them dismayed and turned away back? and their mighty ones are beaten down, and are fled apace, and look not back: for fear was round about, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/46#v6)</sup> Let not the swift flee away, nor the mighty man escape; they shall stumble, and fall toward the north by the river Euphrates.

- The word of the Most High came to Jeremiah against the gentiles, against Egypt and the army of Pharaoh-necho king of Egypt by the river Euphrates at Carchemish, which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon smote in the fourth year of Jehoiakim.
- The Egyptians are gentiles and heathens; they are called to order the buckler and shield, draw near to battle, harness the horses, furnish the spears, and put on the brigandines.
- Yet the Most High has seen them dismayed and turned back, their mighty ones beaten down and fleeing without looking back, for fear was round about; the swift shall not flee away nor the mighty man escape, stumbling and falling toward the north by the river Euphrates.

**[Jeremiah 46:7-12](/bible/jeremiah/46#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/46#v7)</sup> Who is this that cometh up as a flood, whose waters are moved as the rivers?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/46#v8)</sup> Egypt riseth up like a flood, and his waters are moved like the rivers; and he saith, I will go up, and will cover the earth; I will destroy the city and the inhabitants thereof.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/46#v9)</sup> Come up, ye horses; and rage, ye chariots; and let the mighty men come forth; the Ethiopians and the Libyans, that handle the shield; and the Lydians, that handle and bend the bow.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/46#v10)</sup> For this is the day of the Lord God of hosts, a day of vengeance, that he may avenge him of his adversaries: and the sword shall devour, and it shall be satiate and made drunk with their blood: for the Lord God of hosts hath a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/46#v11)</sup> Go up into Gilead, and take balm, O virgin, the daughter of Egypt: in vain shalt thou use many medicines; for thou shalt not be cured.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/46#v12)</sup> The nations have heard of thy shame, and thy cry hath filled the land: for the mighty man hath stumbled against the mighty, and they are fallen both together.

- Egypt rises up like a flood whose waters are moved like the rivers, boasting he will go up and cover the earth and destroy the city and its inhabitants, calling forth his horses, chariots, and mighty men, the Ethiopians, Libyans, and Lydians that handle the shield and bend the bow.
- But this is the day of the Lord GOD of hosts, a day of vengeance to avenge Him of His adversaries; the sword shall devour and be made drunk with their blood, for the Most High has a sacrifice in the north country by the river Euphrates.
- The virgin daughter of Egypt is told to go up into Gilead and take balm, but in vain shall she use many medicines, for she shall not be cured; the nations have heard of her shame and her cry filled the land as her mighty men stumbled and fell together.

**[Jeremiah 46:13-19](/bible/jeremiah/46#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/46#v13)</sup> The word that the Lord spake to Jeremiah the prophet, how Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon should come and smite the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/46#v14)</sup> Declare ye in Egypt, and publish in Migdol, and publish in Noph and in Tahpanhes: say ye, Stand fast, and prepare thee; for the sword shall devour round about thee.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/46#v15)</sup> Why are thy valiant men swept away? they stood not, because the Lord did drive them.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/46#v16)</sup> He made many to fall, yea, one fell upon another: and they said, Arise, and let us go again to our own people, and to the land of our nativity, from the oppressing sword.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/46#v17)</sup> They did cry there, Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise; he hath passed the time appointed.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/46#v18)</sup> As I live, saith the King, whose name is the Lord of hosts, Surely as Tabor is among the mountains, and as Carmel by the sea, so shall he come.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/46#v19)</sup> O thou daughter dwelling in Egypt, furnish thyself to go into captivity: for Noph shall be waste and desolate without an inhabitant.

- The Most High spoke to Jeremiah how Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon should come and smite the land of Egypt; the word is to be declared in Migdol, Noph, and Tahpanhes, calling them to stand fast and prepare, for the sword shall devour round about them.
- Their valiant men are swept away and stand not because the Most High drove them; they fall one upon another and say let us go again to our own people and the land of our nativity from the oppressing sword.
- Pharaoh king of Egypt is but a noise who has passed the time appointed; as surely as Tabor is among the mountains and Carmel by the sea, so shall the conqueror come, and the daughter dwelling in Egypt must furnish herself to go into captivity, for Noph shall be waste and desolate without inhabitant.

**[Jeremiah 46:20-26](/bible/jeremiah/46#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/46#v20)</sup> Egypt is like a very fair heifer, but destruction cometh; it cometh out of the north.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/46#v21)</sup> Also her hired men are in the midst of her like fatted bullocks; for they also are turned back, and are fled away together: they did not stand, because the day of their calamity was come upon them, and the time of their visitation.
>

>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/46#v23)</sup> They shall cut down her forest, saith the Lord, though it cannot be searched; because they are more than the grasshoppers, and are innumerable.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/46#v24)</sup> The daughter of Egypt shall be confounded; she shall be delivered into the hand of the people of the north.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/46#v25)</sup> The Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, saith; Behold, I will punish the multitude of No, and Pharaoh, and Egypt, with their gods, and their kings; even Pharaoh, and all them that trust in him:
>

- Egypt is like a very fair heifer, but destruction comes out of the north; even her hired men in the midst of her are like fatted bullocks, turned back and fled away together, not standing because the day of their calamity and the time of their visitation was upon them.
- The voice shall go like a serpent as they march with an army and come against her with axes like hewers of wood to cut down her forest, though it cannot be searched, because they are more than the grasshoppers and are innumerable.
- The Most High will punish the multitude of No, Pharaoh, and Egypt with their gods and their kings, delivering them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar and his servants; afterward it shall be inhabited as in the days of old.

**[Jeremiah 46:27-28](/bible/jeremiah/46#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/46#v27)</sup> But fear not thou, O my servant Jacob, and be not dismayed, O Israel: for, behold, I will save thee from afar off, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and be in rest and at ease, and none shall make him afraid.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/46#v28)</sup> Fear thou not, O Jacob my servant, saith the Lord: for I am with thee; for I will make a full end of all the nations whither I have driven thee: but I will not make a full end of thee, but correct thee in measure; yet will I not leave thee wholly unpunished.

- The Most High turns to comfort His own, telling Jacob His servant not to fear nor be dismayed, for He will save him from afar off and his seed from the land of their captivity, and Jacob shall return and be in rest and at ease with none to make him afraid.
- He will make a full end of all the nations where He has driven us, but He will not make a full end of us; He will correct us in measure and will not leave us wholly unpunished, so there is redemption for us but still a reminder of punishment because of our sins.

# Jeremiah 47: The Prophecy Against the Philistines

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 47](/bible/jeremiah/47)

**[Jeremiah 47:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/47#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/47#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah the prophet against the Philistines, before that Pharaoh smote Gaza.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/47#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Behold, waters rise up out of the north, and shall be an overflowing flood, and shall overflow the land, and all that is therein; the city, and them that dwell therein: then the men shall cry, and all the inhabitants of the land shall howl.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/47#v3)</sup> At the noise of the stamping of the hoofs of his strong horses, at the rushing of his chariots, and at the rumbling of his wheels, the fathers shall not look back to their children for feebleness of hands;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/47#v4)</sup> Because of the day that cometh to spoil all the Philistines, and to cut off from Tyrus and Zidon every helper that remaineth: for the Lord will spoil the Philistines, the remnant of the country of Caphtor.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/47#v5)</sup> Baldness is come upon Gaza; Ashkelon is cut off with the remnant of their valley: how long wilt thou cut thyself?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/47#v6)</sup> O thou sword of the Lord, how long will it be ere thou be quiet? put up thyself into thy scabbard, rest, and be still.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/47#v7)</sup> How can it be quiet, seeing the Lord hath given it a charge against Ashkelon, and against the sea shore? there hath he appointed it.

- The word of the Most High came to Jeremiah against the Philistines before Pharaoh smote Gaza; waters rise up out of the north and become an overflowing flood that shall overflow the land, the city, and all that dwell therein.
- Men shall cry and all the inhabitants howl at the noise of the stamping hooves of the strong horses, the rushing of the chariots, and the rumbling of the wheels; the fathers shall not look back toward their children for feebleness of hands, because of the day that comes to spoil all the Philistines and cut off from Tyrus and Zidon every helper.
- The Most High will spoil the Philistines, the remnant of the country of Caphtor; baldness has come upon Gaza and Ashkelon is cut off with the remnant of their valley.
- He cries out how long the sword of the Most High will not be quiet, then tells it to put itself into its scabbard and rest, yet how can it be still when the Most High has given it a charge against Ashkelon and the seashore, where He has appointed it.
- As we go into chapters 48 and 49 we read more prophecies of the destruction of the other nations, for there has not been any nation that did not have a hand in spoiling us.

    > <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/2#v8)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.

    He that touches us, Jacob the children of Israel, touches the apple of the Most High's eye.
