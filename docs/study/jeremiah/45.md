**[Jeremiah 45:1-5](/bible/jeremiah/45#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/45#v1)</sup> The word that Jeremiah the prophet spake unto Baruch the son of Neriah, when he had written these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/45#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, unto thee, O Baruch;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/45#v3)</sup> Thou didst say, Woe is me now! for the Lord hath added grief to my sorrow; I fainted in my sighing, and I find no rest.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/45#v4)</sup> Thus shalt thou say unto him, The Lord saith thus; Behold, that which I have built will I break down, and that which I have planted I will pluck up, even this whole land.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/45#v5)</sup> And seekest thou great things for thyself? seek them not: for, behold, I will bring evil upon all flesh, saith the Lord: but thy life will I give unto thee for a prey in all places whither thou goest.

- This is the word Jeremiah spoke to Baruch the son of Neriah when he had written these words in a book at the mouth of Jeremiah, in the fourth year of Jehoiakim son of Josiah king of Judah.
- The Most High addresses him directly, "unto thee O Baruch," and the words of the scribe Baruch are preserved in the Apocrypha, so get yourself a 1611 King James Version Bible with the Apocrypha.
- Baruch had said woe is me, for the Most High added grief to his sorrow and he fainted in his sighing and found no rest; the Most High answers that what He built He will break down and what He planted He will pluck up, even this whole land.
- Baruch is told not to seek great things for himself, for the Most High will bring evil upon all flesh, but his life He will give him for a prey in all places wherever he goes.
  - **[Baruch 2:29-33](/bible/baruch/2#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/baruch/2#v29)</sup> If ye will not hear my voice, surely this very great multitude shall be turned into a small number among the nations, where I will scatter them.
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/baruch/2#v30)</sup> For I knew that they would not hear me, because it is a stiffnecked people: but in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/baruch/2#v31)</sup> And shall know that I am the Lord their God: for I will give them an heart, and ears to hear:
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/baruch/2#v32)</sup> And they shall praise me in the land of their captivity, and think upon my name,
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/baruch/2#v33)</sup> And return from their stiff neck, and from their wicked deeds: for they shall remember the way of their fathers, which sinned before the Lord.

    This is the understanding the christian church lacks; our forefathers learned through the prophets that in the land of our captivity we would remember ourselves, and one way we think upon His name is rehearsing His righteous acts at the Passover.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    In our affliction we will seek Him early, so in the land of our captivity He gives us a heart and ears to hear.
