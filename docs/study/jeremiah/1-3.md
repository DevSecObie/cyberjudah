Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 1](/bible/jeremiah/1)

This session, taught in Memphis, covers Jeremiah chapters 1 through 3. Jeremiah is called and set over the nations to root out and to plant; the Most High then lays out His charge against Israel, who has forsaken Him, the fountain of living waters, and gone after other gods. The same word spoken to Jeremiah is set here against the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans today: the men and women the Most High knew from the womb are being raised up to prophesy against the nations and to call a backsliding people to return.

**[Jeremiah 1:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/1#v1)</sup> The words of Jeremiah the son of Hilkiah, of the priests that were in Anathoth in the land of Benjamin:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/1#v2)</sup> To whom the word of the Lord came in the days of Josiah the son of Amon king of Judah, in the thirteenth year of his reign.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/1#v3)</sup> It came also in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, unto the end of the eleventh year of Zedekiah the son of Josiah king of Judah, unto the carrying away of Jerusalem captive in the fifth month.

- The words of Jeremiah, son of Hilkiah of the priests in Anathoth, came in the days of Josiah, through Jehoiakim, and to the end of Zedekiah, up to the carrying away of Jerusalem captive.
- The setting is fixed at the very edge of the captivity, so the prophet is speaking as judgment is already closing in on Judah.

**[Jeremiah 1:4-5](/bible/jeremiah/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/1#v4)</sup> Then the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/1#v5)</sup> Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations.

- Before He formed Jeremiah in the belly the Most High knew him, sanctified him, and ordained him a prophet unto the nations.
- The same thing said to Jeremiah is what is happening today: the brothers and sisters waking up were formed in the belly and known from the beginning, raised up to prophesy against the nations.

**[Jeremiah 1:6-8](/bible/jeremiah/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/1#v6)</sup> Then said I, Ah, Lord God! behold, I cannot speak: for I am a child.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/1#v7)</sup> But the Lord said unto me, Say not, I am a child: for thou shalt go to all that I shall send thee, and whatsoever I command thee thou shalt speak.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/1#v8)</sup> Be not afraid of their faces: for I am with thee to deliver thee, saith the Lord.

- Jeremiah answers that he cannot speak, for he is a child, but the Most High tells him not to say he is a child, for he must go and speak whatever he is commanded.
- Be not afraid of their faces, for the Most High is with him to deliver him.

**[Jeremiah 1:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/1#v9)</sup> Then the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth. And the Lord said unto me, Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v10)</sup> See, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down, to build, and to plant.

- The Most High puts forth His hand, touches Jeremiah's mouth, and says He has put His words in his mouth; this is why we stay within these scriptures and never go wrong.
- He is set over the nations and the kingdoms to root out, pull down, destroy, throw down, build, and plant, which is our job today and is spiritual warfare.

**[Jeremiah 1:11-12](/bible/jeremiah/1#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/1#v11)</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying, Jeremiah, what seest thou? And I said, I see a rod of an almond tree.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/1#v12)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Thou hast well seen: for I will hasten my word to perform it.

- Jeremiah is shown a rod of an almond tree, and the Most High says He will hasten His word to perform it.
- The vision confirms that the word spoken will be carried out and not delayed.

**[Jeremiah 1:13-15](/bible/jeremiah/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/1#v13)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me the second time, saying, What seest thou? And I said, I see a seething pot; and the face thereof is toward the north.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/1#v14)</sup> Then the Lord said unto me, Out of the north an evil shall break forth upon all the inhabitants of the land.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/1#v15)</sup> For, lo, I will call all the families of the kingdoms of the north, saith the Lord; and they shall come, and they shall set every one his throne at the entering of the gates of Jerusalem, and against all the walls thereof round about, and against all the cities of Judah.

- The second vision is a seething pot with its face toward the north, and out of the north the evil shall break forth upon all the inhabitants of the land.
- The Most High calls all the families of the kingdoms of the north, who set their thrones at the entering of the gates of Jerusalem and against the cities of Judah; the judgment comes through the other nations because of our sins.

**[Jeremiah 1:16](/bible/jeremiah/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/1#v16)</sup> And I will utter my judgments against them touching all their wickedness, who have forsaken me, and have burned incense unto other gods, and worshipped the works of their own hands.

- The Most High utters His judgments against them for their wickedness in forsaking Him, burning incense to other gods, and worshipping the works of their own hands.
- The charge is idolatry, the very thing the rest of the reading opens up in detail.

**[Jeremiah 1:17-19](/bible/jeremiah/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/1#v17)</sup> Thou therefore gird up thy loins, and arise, and speak unto them all that I command thee: be not dismayed at their faces, lest I confound thee before them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/1#v18)</sup> For, behold, I have made thee this day a defenced city, and an iron pillar, and brasen walls against the whole land, against the kings of Judah, against the princes thereof, against the priests thereof, and against the people of the land.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/1#v19)</sup> And they shall fight against thee; but they shall not prevail against thee; for I am with thee, saith the Lord, to deliver thee.

- Gird up thy loins, arise, and speak all that is commanded; be not dismayed at their faces, lest the Most High confound Jeremiah before them. We cannot go out with a spirit of fear on us.
- He is made a defenced city, an iron pillar, and brasen walls against the whole land; they shall fight but not prevail, for none can prevail against the word of the Most High going out through the prophets.

# Jeremiah 2: Israel's Two Evils and the Forsaken Fountain

**[Jeremiah 2:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/2#v1)**

>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/2#v3)</sup> Israel was holiness unto the Lord, and the firstfruits of his increase: all that devour him shall offend; evil shall come upon them, saith the Lord.

- Israel was holiness unto the Most High and the firstfruits of His increase, and all that devour him shall offend; the nations have devoured us because of our sins.

**[Jeremiah 2:4-6](/bible/jeremiah/2#v4)**

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/2#v6)</sup> Neither said they, Where is the Lord that brought us up out of the land of Egypt, that led us through the wilderness, through a land of deserts and of pits, through a land of drought, and of the shadow of death, through a land that no man passed through, and where no man dwelt?

- This is the question the Most High is asking the so-called blacks and Hispanics today: what iniquity have your fathers found in Me, that they are gone far from Me, walked after vanity, and become vain.

**[Jeremiah 2:7](/bible/jeremiah/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/2#v7)</sup> And I brought you into a plentiful country, to eat the fruit thereof and the goodness thereof; but when ye entered, ye defiled my land, and made mine heritage an abomination.

- He brought them into a plentiful country to eat its fruit and goodness, but when they entered they defiled the land and made His heritage an abomination.
- That is Israel: we had it good, and we messed it up.

**[Jeremiah 2:8](/bible/jeremiah/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/2#v8)</sup> The priests said not, Where is the Lord? and they that handle the law knew me not: the pastors also transgressed against me, and the prophets prophesied by Baal, and walked after things that do not profit.

- The priests did not ask where is the Most High, they that handle the law knew Him not, and the pastors transgressed against Him.
- The prophets prophesied by Baal and walked after things that do not profit; that is the Christian pastor today, breaking the laws and worshipping Satan.

**[Jeremiah 2:9-11](/bible/jeremiah/2#v9)**

- But My people have changed their glory for that which does not profit; the glory is the kingdom rulership, traded away for things that do not profit.

**[Jeremiah 2:12-13](/bible/jeremiah/2#v12)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/2#v16)</sup> Also the children of Noph and Tahapanes have broken the crown of thy head.

**[Jeremiah 2:17-19](/bible/jeremiah/2#v17)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/2#v18)</sup> And now what hast thou to do in the way of Egypt, to drink the waters of Sihor? or what hast thou to do in the way of Assyria, to drink the waters of the river?

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/2#v19)</sup> Thine own wickedness shall correct thee, and thy backslidings shall reprove thee: know therefore and see that it is an evil thing and bitter, that thou hast forsaken the Lord thy God, and that my fear is not in thee, saith the Lord God of hosts.

**[Jeremiah 2:20-21](/bible/jeremiah/2#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/2#v20)</sup> For of old time I have broken thy yoke, and burst thy bands; and thou saidst, I will not transgress; when upon every high hill and under every green tree thou wanderest, playing the harlot.

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)</sup> They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted? but thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me, saith the Lord.

**[Jeremiah 3:2-3](/bible/jeremiah/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/3#v2)</sup> Lift up thine eyes unto the high places, and see where thou hast not been lien with. In the ways hast thou sat for them, as the Arabian in the wilderness; and thou hast polluted the land with thy whoredoms and with thy wickedness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/3#v3)</sup> Therefore the showers have been withholden, and there hath been no latter rain; and thou hadst a whore’s forehead, thou refusedst to be ashamed.

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/3#v19)</sup> But I said, How shall I put thee among the children, and give thee a pleasant land, a goodly heritage of the hosts of nations? and I said, Thou shalt call me, My father; and shalt not turn away from me.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/3#v20)</sup> Surely as a wife treacherously departeth from her husband, so have ye dealt treacherously with me, O house of Israel, saith the Lord.

- The Most High asks how He shall put Israel among the children and give him a pleasant land, a goodly heritage; and He desires that Israel call Him My father and not turn away.
- But surely as a wife treacherously departs from her husband, so has the house of Israel dealt treacherously with the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 3:21-22](/bible/jeremiah/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/3#v21)</sup> A voice was heard upon the high places, weeping and supplications of the children of Israel: for they have perverted their way, and they have forgotten the Lord their God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/3#v22)</sup> Return, ye backsliding children, and I will heal your backslidings. Behold, we come unto thee; for thou art the Lord our God.

- A voice is heard upon the high places, weeping and supplications of the children of Israel, for they have perverted their way and forgotten the Most High their God.
- Return, ye backsliding children, and He will heal your backslidings; the call is to repent and return from the high places of idolatry.

**[Jeremiah 3:23-25](/bible/jeremiah/3#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/3#v23)</sup> Truly in vain is salvation hoped for from the hills, and from the multitude of mountains: truly in the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/3#v24)</sup> For shame hath devoured the labour of our fathers from our youth; their flocks and their herds, their sons and their daughters.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/3#v25)</sup> We lie down in our shame, and our confusion covereth us: for we have sinned against the Lord our God, we and our fathers, from our youth even unto this day, and have not obeyed the voice of the Lord our God.

- In vain is salvation hoped for from the hills and the multitude of mountains; truly in the Most High our God is the salvation of Israel.
- Shame has devoured the labour of our fathers, and we lie down in our shame while our confusion covers us; in Christianity people are confused, taught that a degree or college will make everything okay while the nation still hates them, because they have not been properly trained in the Bible or fed God's laws, and we and our fathers have sinned and not obeyed the voice of the Most High to this day.
