**[Jeremiah 51:1-4](/bible/jeremiah/51#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/51#v1)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will raise up against Babylon, and against them that dwell in the midst of them that rise up against me, a destroying wind;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/51#v2)</sup> And will send unto Babylon fanners, that shall fan her, and shall empty her land: for in the day of trouble they shall be against her round about.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/51#v3)</sup> Against him that bendeth let the archer bend his bow, and against him that lifteth himself up in his brigandine: and spare ye not her young men; destroy ye utterly all her host.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/51#v4)</sup> Thus the slain shall fall in the land of the Chaldeans, and they that are thrust through in her streets.

- The Most High raises against Babylon a destroying wind and sends fanners to fan her and empty her land. Spare not her young men; destroy utterly all her host.
- The slain fall in the land of the Chaldeans, thrust through in her streets, for Israel has not been forsaken, nor Judah of his God, though their land was filled with sin against the Holy One of Israel.

**[Jeremiah 51:5-6](/bible/jeremiah/51#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/51#v5)</sup> For Israel hath not been forsaken, nor Judah of his God, of the Lord of hosts; though their land was filled with sin against the Holy One of Israel.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/51#v6)</sup> Flee out of the midst of Babylon, and deliver every man his soul: be not cut off in her iniquity; for this is the time of the Lord’s vengeance; he will render unto her a recompence.

- Israel and Judah have not been forsaken of the Most High. Flee out of the midst of Babylon and deliver every man his soul; do not be cut off in her iniquity, for this is the time of the Lord's vengeance.

    Come out of her, my people, that ye be not partakers of her sins and receive not of her plagues.

**[Jeremiah 51:7-8](/bible/jeremiah/51#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/51#v7)</sup> Babylon hath been a golden cup in the Lord’s hand, that made all the earth drunken: the nations have drunken of her wine; therefore the nations are mad.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/51#v8)</sup> Babylon is suddenly fallen and destroyed: howl for her; take balm for her pain, if so be she may be healed.

- Babylon has been a golden cup in the Lord's hand that made all the earth drunken; the nations drank of her wine and are mad. All the nations partook, and even the children of Israel were made to drink of the cup of our own destruction.
- Babylon is suddenly fallen and destroyed. We would have healed Babylon, but she is not healed; forsake her, and let everyone go into his own country.

**[Jeremiah 51:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/51#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/51#v9)</sup> We would have healed Babylon, but she is not healed: forsake her, and let us go every one into his own country: for her judgment reacheth unto heaven, and is lifted up even to the skies.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/51#v10)</sup> The Lord hath brought forth our righteousness: come, and let us declare in Zion the work of the Lord our God.

- Her judgment reaches unto heaven and is lifted up even to the skies. The Most High has brought forth our righteousness; come, and let us declare in Zion the work of the Lord our God.

**[Jeremiah 51:11-12](/bible/jeremiah/51#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/51#v11)</sup> Make bright the arrows; gather the shields: the Lord hath raised up the spirit of the kings of the Medes: for his device is against Babylon, to destroy it; because it is the vengeance of the Lord, the vengeance of his temple.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/51#v12)</sup> Set up the standard upon the walls of Babylon, make the watch strong, set up the watchmen, prepare the ambushes: for the Lord hath both devised and done that which he spake against the inhabitants of Babylon.

- Make bright the arrows and gather the shields; the Lord has raised up the spirit of the kings of the Medes, for his device against Babylon is to destroy it, the vengeance of his temple.
- Set up the standard upon the walls of Babylon, make the watch strong, set the watchmen, and prepare the ambushes. The standard is the Bible, and the men are made ready, for the Most High has both devised and done what he spoke.

**[Jeremiah 51:13-14](/bible/jeremiah/51#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/51#v13)</sup> O thou that dwellest upon many waters, abundant in treasures, thine end is come, and the measure of thy covetousness.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/51#v14)</sup> The Lord of hosts hath sworn by himself, saying, Surely I will fill thee with men, as with caterpillers; and they shall lift up a shout against thee.

- Babylon dwells upon many waters, abundant in treasures, but thine end is come. She dwells upon many waters because her embassies reach into every land, from South Africa to Europe to the Philippines; her treasure was built on the backs of the slaves and the stolen Western Hemisphere.
- The Lord of hosts has sworn to fill her with men as with caterpillars who lift up a shout against her. She is destroyed from within, because being a melting pot, the other nations infiltrate and destroy America from the inside out, the way a caterpillar becomes an infestation that devours a whole land.

>

> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/51#v33)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; The daughter of Babylon is like a threshingfloor, it is time to thresh her: yet a little while, and the time of her harvest shall come.

**[Jeremiah 51:34-37](/bible/jeremiah/51#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/51#v34)</sup> Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon hath devoured me, he hath crushed me, he hath made me an empty vessel, he hath swallowed me up like a dragon, he hath filled his belly with my delicates, he hath cast me out.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/51#v35)</sup> The violence done to me and to my flesh be upon Babylon, shall the inhabitant of Zion say; and my blood upon the inhabitants of Chaldea, shall Jerusalem say.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/51#v36)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will plead thy cause, and take vengeance for thee; and I will dry up her sea, and make her springs dry.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/jeremiah/51#v37)</sup> And Babylon shall become heaps, a dwellingplace for dragons, an astonishment, and an hissing, without an inhabitant.

- Nebuchadnezzar has devoured Israel, crushed her, made her an empty vessel, and swallowed her like a dragon. Let the violence done to Zion be upon Babylon, and her blood upon the inhabitants of Chaldea.
- The Most High will plead Zion's cause, take vengeance for her, and dry up Babylon's sea and springs. Babylon shall become heaps, a dwelling place for dragons, an astonishment and a hissing without inhabitant.

**[Jeremiah 51:59-64](/bible/jeremiah/51#v59)**

> <sup>[59](/bible/jeremiah/51#v59)</sup> The word which Jeremiah the prophet commanded Seraiah the son of Neriah, the son of Maaseiah, when he went with Zedekiah the king of Judah into Babylon in the fourth year of his reign. And this Seraiah was a quiet prince.
>
> <sup>[60](/bible/jeremiah/51#v60)</sup> So Jeremiah wrote in a book all the evil that should come upon Babylon, even all these words that are written against Babylon.
>
> <sup>[61](/bible/jeremiah/51#v61)</sup> And Jeremiah said to Seraiah, When thou comest to Babylon, and shalt see, and shalt read all these words;
>
> <sup>[62](/bible/jeremiah/51#v62)</sup> Then shalt thou say, O Lord, thou hast spoken against this place, to cut it off, that none shall remain in it, neither man nor beast, but that it shall be desolate for ever.
>
> <sup>[63](/bible/jeremiah/51#v63)</sup> And it shall be, when thou hast made an end of reading this book, that thou shalt bind a stone to it, and cast it into the midst of Euphrates:
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/jeremiah/51#v64)</sup> And thou shalt say, Thus shall Babylon sink, and shall not rise from the evil that I will bring upon her: and they shall be weary. Thus far are the words of Jeremiah.

- Jeremiah commanded Seraiah, when he went with Zedekiah to Babylon, to read all the evil written against Babylon and then declare that the Most High has spoken to cut off this place forever.
- After reading the book, Seraiah was to bind a stone to it and cast it into the midst of the Euphrates, saying, thus shall Babylon sink and not rise from the evil the Most High brings upon her. Thus far are the words of Jeremiah. Rejoice in the destruction of our enemies, which is the salvation of the righteous.
