This session covers [Jeremiah 48](/bible/jeremiah/48) through 51, four chapters that move straight into the salvation of Israel by way of the prophesied destruction of our enemies. Jeremiah lived during the time of Babylon, and here he prophesies against Moab (the so-called Chinese), Ammon (the so-called Japanese), Edom (the so-called white man), Damascus, Kedar and Hazor, Elam (the ancient and modern Persians, the East Indians), and finally Babylon the Chaldeans (modern-day America). This is our redemption. Scripture says a Christian is to accept both the salvation of the righteous and the destruction of the enemies, so this reading should be music to your ears.

**[Jeremiah 48:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/48#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/48#v1)</sup> Against Moab thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Woe unto Nebo! for it is spoiled: Kiriathaim is confounded and taken: Misgab is confounded and dismayed.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/48#v2)</sup> There shall be no more praise of Moab: in Heshbon they have devised evil against it; come, and let us cut it off from being a nation. Also thou shalt be cut down, O Madmen; the sword shall pursue thee.

- This is the word of the Most High against Moab. Woe unto Nebo, for it is spoiled; Kiriathaim is confounded and taken. There shall be no more praise of Moab.
- In Heshbon they have devised evil against it: come, let us cut it off from being a nation. These are the Moabites, and God calls the so-called Chinese by that name.

>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/48#v5)</sup> For in the going up of Luhith continual weeping shall go up; for in the going down of Horonaim the enemies have heard a cry of destruction.

- A voice of crying goes up from Horonaim, spoiling and great destruction. Moab is destroyed, and her little ones cause a cry to be heard.
- Flee, save your lives, and be like the heath in the wilderness. There is no standing against the judgment that is coming.

# Jeremiah 49: Judgment on Ammon, Edom, Damascus, Kedar, and Elam

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 49](/bible/jeremiah/49)

**[Jeremiah 49:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/49#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/49#v1)</sup> Concerning the Ammonites, thus saith the Lord; Hath Israel no sons? hath he no heir? why then doth their king inherit Gad, and his people dwell in his cities?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/49#v2)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will cause an alarm of war to be heard in Rabbah of the Ammonites; and it shall be a desolate heap, and her daughters shall be burned with fire: then shall Israel be heir unto them that were his heirs, saith the Lord.

- This is the word against the Ammonites, the so-called Japanese. The Most High asks, hath Israel no sons? Why then doth their king inherit Gad and his people dwell in his cities?
- That shows Gad was intermingling with the Ammonites. This is why parts of the Northern Kingdom sometimes carry an Asian look, because we were once in captivity to Ammon and mixed among them; not everyone in Israel looks the same.
- The days come when an alarm of war is heard in Rabbah, it becomes a desolate heap, and then shall Israel be heir unto them that were his heirs.
