**[Jeremiah 33:1-5](/bible/jeremiah/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/33#v1)</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came unto Jeremiah the second time, while he was yet shut up in the court of the prison, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/33#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord the maker thereof, the Lord that formed it, to establish it; the Lord is his name;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/33#v3)</sup> Call unto me, and I will answer thee, and shew thee great and mighty things, which thou knowest not.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/33#v4)</sup> For thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, concerning the houses of this city, and concerning the houses of the kings of Judah, which are thrown down by the mounts, and by the sword;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/33#v5)</sup> They come to fight with the Chaldeans, but it is to fill them with the dead bodies of men, whom I have slain in mine anger and in my fury, and for all whose wickedness I have hid my face from this city.

- The word came to Jeremiah a second time while still shut up in the prison: the Most High who made and formed the earth to establish it invites him to call, promising to answer and show him great and mighty things he knows not.
- The houses thrown down by the mount and the sword would be filled with the dead bodies of men He slew in His anger, for He hid His face from the city for all their wickedness.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    He returns to His place until they acknowledge their offense and seek His face, and in their affliction they will seek Him early.
  - **[1 Kings 8:47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;

    We would have to acknowledge our offenses before the Most High, and in the affliction of the other nations we return to Him.

**[Jeremiah 33:6-9](/bible/jeremiah/33#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/33#v6)</sup> Behold, I will bring it health and cure, and I will cure them, and will reveal unto them the abundance of peace and truth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/33#v7)</sup> And I will cause the captivity of Judah and the captivity of Israel to return, and will build them, as at the first.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/33#v8)</sup> And I will cleanse them from all their iniquity, whereby they have sinned against me; and I will pardon all their iniquities, whereby they have sinned, and whereby they have transgressed against me.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/33#v9)</sup> And it shall be to me a name of joy, a praise and an honour before all the nations of the earth, which shall hear all the good that I do unto them: and they shall fear and tremble for all the goodness and for all the prosperity that I procure unto it.

- The Most High will bring health and cure and reveal the abundance of peace and truth, which has not yet been fulfilled because there is still much violence in Israel and against us.
- He will cause the captivity of Judah and Israel to return and build them as at the first, cleansing them from all iniquity and pardoning all their transgressions against Him.
- It shall be to Him a name of joy, a praise, and an honor before all nations, who will fear and tremble at the goodness He procures, for the nations know this is true and are struck when they see us carry ourselves as sons and daughters of the covenant.
  - **[Amos 9:11](/bible/amos/9#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/amos/9#v11)</sup> In that day will I raise up the tabernacle of David that is fallen, and close up the breaches thereof; and I will raise up his ruins, and I will build it as in the days of old:

    In that day He will raise up the fallen tabernacle of David, close up the breaches, and build it as in the days of old.
  - **[Acts 15:15-16](/bible/acts/15#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/acts/15#v15)</sup> And to this agree the words of the prophets; as it is written,
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/acts/15#v16)</sup> After this I will return, and will build again the tabernacle of David, which is fallen down; and I will build again the ruins thereof, and I will set it up:

    Even the New Testament points to future prophecy that He will return and build again the ruins of the tabernacle of David.

**[Jeremiah 33:10-14](/bible/jeremiah/33#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/33#v10)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Again there shall be heard in this place, which ye say shall be desolate without man and without beast, even in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, that are desolate, without man, and without inhabitant, and without beast,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/33#v11)</sup> The voice of joy, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride, the voice of them that shall say, Praise the Lord of hosts: for the Lord is good; for his mercy endureth for ever: and of them that shall bring the sacrifice of praise into the house of the Lord. For I will cause to return the captivity of the land, as at the first, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/33#v12)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Again in this place, which is desolate without man and without beast, and in all the cities thereof, shall be an habitation of shepherds causing their flocks to lie down.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/33#v13)</sup> In the cities of the mountains, in the cities of the vale, and in the cities of the south, and in the land of Benjamin, and in the places about Jerusalem, and in the cities of Judah, shall the flocks pass again under the hands of him that telleth them, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/33#v14)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will perform that good thing which I have promised unto the house of Israel and to the house of Judah.

- In the place they say is desolate without man or beast, there shall again be heard the voice of joy and gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the bride, and the voice of them that praise the Most High, for His mercy endureth for ever.
- In the cities of the mountains, valley, south, Benjamin, and about Jerusalem, the flocks shall pass again under the hands of him that telleth them.
- Behold, the days come that He will perform the good thing He has promised to the house of Israel and the house of Judah.
  - **[Romans 9:4](/bible/romans/9#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/9#v4)</sup> Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises;

    To the Israelites pertain the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the law, the service of God, and the promises, which is why we read the prophecy everywhere in the Old Testament.

**[Jeremiah 33:15-18](/bible/jeremiah/33#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/33#v15)</sup> In those days, and at that time, will I cause the Branch of righteousness to grow up unto David; and he shall execute judgment and righteousness in the land.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/33#v16)</sup> In those days shall Judah be saved, and Jerusalem shall dwell safely: and this is the name wherewith she shall be called, The Lord our righteousness.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/33#v17)</sup> For thus saith the Lord; David shall never want a man to sit upon the throne of the house of Israel;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/33#v18)</sup> Neither shall the priests the Levites want a man before me to offer burnt offerings, and to kindle meat offerings, and to do sacrifice continually.

- In those days He will cause the branch of righteousness to grow up unto David, who shall execute judgment and righteousness in the land; in those days Judah shall be saved and Jerusalem dwell safely.
- She shall be called The Most High our righteousness, and David shall never want a man to sit upon the throne of the house of Israel.
- Neither shall the priests the Levites want a man to offer burnt offerings, kindle meat offerings, and do sacrifice continually.

**[Jeremiah 33:19-26](/bible/jeremiah/33#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/33#v19)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/33#v20)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; If ye can break my covenant of the day, and my covenant of the night, and that there should not be day and night in their season;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/33#v21)</sup> Then may also my covenant be broken with David my servant, that he should not have a son to reign upon his throne; and with the Levites the priests, my ministers.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/33#v22)</sup> As the host of heaven cannot be numbered, neither the sand of the sea measured: so will I multiply the seed of David my servant, and the Levites that minister unto me.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/33#v23)</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/33#v24)</sup> Considerest thou not what this people have spoken, saying, The two families which the Lord hath chosen, he hath even cast them off? thus they have despised my people, that they should be no more a nation before them.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/33#v25)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; If my covenant be not with day and night, and if I have not appointed the ordinances of heaven and earth;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/33#v26)</sup> Then will I cast away the seed of Jacob, and David my servant, so that I will not take any of his seed to be rulers over the seed of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: for I will cause their captivity to return, and have mercy on them.

- As surely as His covenant of the day and the night cannot be broken, so His covenant with David cannot be broken, that he shall not want a son to reign upon his throne.
- As the host of heaven cannot be numbered nor the sand of the sea measured, so He will multiply the seed of David His servant and the Levites that minister unto Him.
- He tells Jeremiah not to regard the people who say the two families the Most High chose He has now cast off and despised so they should be no more a nation; if His covenant with day and night stands, He will not cast away the seed of Jacob and David.
  - **[Romans 11:1-2](/bible/romans/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/11#v1)</sup> I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/11#v2)</sup> God hath not cast away his people which he foreknew. Wot ye not what the scripture saith of Elias? how he maketh intercession to God against Israel, saying,

    God has not cast away His people whom He foreknew, and we are the living proof, our hearts turned back to keeping the commandments and the faith of Christ without bodily eyes having seen Him.
