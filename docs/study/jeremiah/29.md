**[Jeremiah 29:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/29#v1)</sup> Now these are the words of the letter that Jeremiah the prophet sent from Jerusalem unto the residue of the elders which were carried away captives, and to the priests, and to the prophets, and to all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried away captive from Jerusalem to Babylon;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/29#v2)</sup> (After that Jeconiah the king, and the queen, and the eunuchs, the princes of Judah and Jerusalem, and the carpenters, and the smiths, were departed from Jerusalem;)
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/29#v3)</sup> By the hand of Elasah the son of Shaphan, and Gemariah the son of Hilkiah, (whom Zedekiah king of Judah sent unto Babylon to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon) saying,

- Jeremiah sent a letter from Jerusalem to the residue of the elders, the priests, the prophets, and all the people whom Nebuchadnezzar had carried captive to Babylon, after Jeconiah, the queen, the eunuchs, the princes, the carpenters, and the smiths had departed.
- Just like in this captivity, our people still carried those talents and skills; we had carpenters and we had blacksmiths.

**[Jeremiah 29:4-7](/bible/jeremiah/29#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/29#v4)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, unto all that are carried away captives, whom I have caused to be carried away from Jerusalem unto Babylon;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/29#v5)</sup> Build ye houses, and dwell in them; and plant gardens, and eat the fruit of them;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/29#v6)</sup> Take ye wives, and beget sons and daughters; and take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, that they may bear sons and daughters; that ye may be increased there, and not diminished.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/29#v7)</sup> And seek the peace of the city whither I have caused you to be carried away captives, and pray unto the Lord for it: for in the peace thereof shall ye have peace.

- The Most High tells the captives that even in their captivity they should build houses and dwell in them, buy homes, plant gardens and eat the fruit of them.
- They are to take wives and beget sons and daughters, take wives for their sons and husbands for their daughters, that they may be increased and not diminished, and seek the peace of the city where He caused them to be carried away, for in the peace thereof they shall have peace.

**[Jeremiah 29:8-14](/bible/jeremiah/29#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/29#v8)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Let not your prophets and your diviners, that be in the midst of you, deceive you, neither hearken to your dreams which ye cause to be dreamed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/29#v9)</sup> For they prophesy falsely unto you in my name: I have not sent them, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/29#v10)</sup> For thus saith the Lord, That after seventy years be accomplished at Babylon I will visit you, and perform my good word toward you, in causing you to return to this place.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/29#v11)</sup> For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, saith the Lord, thoughts of peace, and not of evil, to give you an expected end.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/29#v12)</sup> Then shall ye call upon me, and ye shall go and pray unto me, and I will hearken unto you.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/29#v13)</sup> And ye shall seek me, and find me, when ye shall search for me with all your heart.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/29#v14)</sup> And I will be found of you, saith the Lord: and I will turn away your captivity, and I will gather you from all the nations, and from all the places whither I have driven you, saith the Lord; and I will bring you again into the place whence I caused you to be carried away captive.

- Let not the false prophets and diviners in the midst of the people deceive them, nor their dreams; today Christianity has so-called prophets and Islam has so-called prophecy, but they deceive people because that is not the right way to serve the right God.
- After seventy years are accomplished at Babylon the Most High will visit them and perform His good word to return them, for His thoughts toward them are of peace and not of evil, to give them an expected end; the same is happening today, knowledge is being increased, we are finding out who we are and coming back to God's laws to be saved out of this captivity.
- When they call upon Him, seek Him, and search for Him with all their heart, He will be found of them and turn away their captivity, gathering them from all the nations and places whither He drove them; this is why the leadership goes out and teaches in the four corners of the earth, because all our people have been driven to the four corners.

**[Jeremiah 29:15-19](/bible/jeremiah/29#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/29#v15)</sup> Because ye have said, The Lord hath raised us up prophets in Babylon;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/29#v16)</sup> Know that thus saith the Lord of the king that sitteth upon the throne of David, and of all the people that dwelleth in this city, and of your brethren that are not gone forth with you into captivity;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/29#v17)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Behold, I will send upon them the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, and will make them like vile figs, that cannot be eaten, they are so evil.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/29#v18)</sup> And I will persecute them with the sword, with the famine, and with the pestilence, and will deliver them to be removed to all the kingdoms of the earth, to be a curse, and an astonishment, and an hissing, and a reproach, among all the nations whither I have driven them:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/29#v19)</sup> Because they have not hearkened to my words, saith the Lord, which I sent unto them by my servants the prophets, rising up early and sending them; but ye would not hear, saith the Lord.

- Because they said the Lord had raised them up prophets in Babylon, the Most High declares He will send the sword, the famine, and the pestilence upon them and make them like vile figs that cannot be eaten, and deliver them to be a curse, an astonishment, a hissing, and a reproach among all the nations.
- This is because they had not hearkened to His words, which He sent by His servants the prophets, rising up early; so it behooves us to follow God's laws, for the prophets today are the ones going out teaching on the corners every Sabbath and seven days a week.
- The Most High never comes down here Himself to speak to the people; He speaks through His servants the prophets.

**[Jeremiah 29:20-23](/bible/jeremiah/29#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/29#v20)</sup> Hear ye therefore the word of the Lord, all ye of the captivity, whom I have sent from Jerusalem to Babylon:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/29#v21)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, of Ahab the son of Kolaiah, and of Zedekiah the son of Maaseiah, which prophesy a lie unto you in my name; Behold, I will deliver them into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon; and he shall slay them before your eyes;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/29#v22)</sup> And of them shall be taken up a curse by all the captivity of Judah which are in Babylon, saying, The Lord make thee like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/29#v23)</sup> Because they have committed villany in Israel, and have committed adultery with their neighbours’ wives, and have spoken lying words in my name, which I have not commanded them; even I know, and am a witness, saith the Lord.

- Concerning Ahab the son of Kolaiah and Zedekiah the son of Maaseiah, who prophesied a lie in His name, the Most High would deliver them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar to be slain before the captives' eyes.
- They would become a curse, the Lord make thee like Zedekiah and like Ahab whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire, because they committed villainy in Israel, committed adultery with their brothers' wives, and spoke lying words in His name.
  - **[Jeremiah 23:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)**

**[Jeremiah 29:24-32](/bible/jeremiah/29#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/29#v24)</sup> Thus shalt thou also speak to Shemaiah the Nehelamite, saying,
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/29#v25)</sup> Thus speaketh the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, saying, Because thou hast sent letters in thy name unto all the people that are at Jerusalem, and to Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest, and to all the priests, saying,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/29#v26)</sup> The Lord hath made thee priest in the stead of Jehoiada the priest, that ye should be officers in the house of the Lord, for every man that is mad, and maketh himself a prophet, that thou shouldest put him in prison, and in the stocks.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/29#v27)</sup> Now therefore why hast thou not reproved Jeremiah of Anathoth, which maketh himself a prophet to you?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/29#v28)</sup> For therefore he sent unto us in Babylon, saying, This captivity is long: build ye houses, and dwell in them; and plant gardens, and eat the fruit of them.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/29#v29)</sup> And Zephaniah the priest read this letter in the ears of Jeremiah the prophet.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/29#v30)</sup> Then came the word of the Lord unto Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/29#v31)</sup> Send to all them of the captivity, saying, Thus saith the Lord concerning Shemaiah the Nehelamite; Because that Shemaiah hath prophesied unto you, and I sent him not, and he caused you to trust in a lie:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/29#v32)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will punish Shemaiah the Nehelamite, and his seed: he shall not have a man to dwell among this people; neither shall he behold the good that I will do for my people, saith the Lord; because he hath taught rebellion against the Lord.

- To Shemaiah the Nehelamite: because he sent letters in his own name to all the people at Jerusalem and to Zephaniah the priest, claiming the Lord had made Zephaniah priest in the stead of Jehoiada to put every man that makes himself a prophet in prison and the stocks, and asking why he had not reproved Jeremiah for telling the captives their captivity was long and to build houses.
- Zephaniah read the letter in the ears of Jeremiah, and the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, for the Lord was dealing with Jeremiah, that was the difference.
- Because Shemaiah prophesied when the Lord sent him not and caused the people to trust in a lie, the Most High would punish him and his seed; he shall not have a man to dwell among the people nor behold the good the Lord will do, because he taught rebellion against the Lord.
