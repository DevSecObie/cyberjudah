Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 24](/bible/jeremiah/24)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 24 through 27. The Most High shows Jeremiah two baskets of figs, good and evil, that set the pattern for the whole reading: good Israelites went into captivity alongside the wicked, some for their good and some for straight punishment, and the same nations that carried Israel down would themselves drink the cup of the Most High's fury. The prophet is threatened for speaking these words, and the Most High commands the nations to put their necks under the yoke of Babylon, His servant, and live.

**[Jeremiah 24:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/24#v1)</sup> The Lord shewed me, and, behold, two baskets of figs were set before the temple of the Lord, after that Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon had carried away captive Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah, and the princes of Judah, with the carpenters and smiths, from Jerusalem, and had brought them to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/24#v2)</sup> One basket had very good figs, even like the figs that are first ripe: and the other basket had very naughty figs, which could not be eaten, they were so bad.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/24#v3)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, What seest thou, Jeremiah? And I said, Figs; the good figs, very good; and the evil, very evil, that cannot be eaten, they are so evil.

- The Most High shows Jeremiah two baskets of figs set before the temple after Nebuchadnezzar had carried Jeconiah, the princes of Judah, the carpenters, and the smiths away captive to Babylon.
- One basket held very good figs like the first ripe, and the other held very naughty figs so bad they could not be eaten; the baskets represent the house of Israel.

**[Jeremiah 24:4-7](/bible/jeremiah/24#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/24#v4)</sup> Again the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/24#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; Like these good figs, so will I acknowledge them that are carried away captive of Judah, whom I have sent out of this place into the land of the Chaldeans for their good.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/24#v6)</sup> For I will set mine eyes upon them for good, and I will bring them again to this land: and I will build them, and not pull them down; and I will plant them, and not pluck them up.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/24#v7)</sup> And I will give them an heart to know me, that I am the Lord: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God: for they shall return unto me with their whole heart.

- Like the good figs, the Most High will acknowledge them that are carried away captive into the land of the Chaldeans for their good, to set His eyes upon them, bring them again to this land, build them, and plant them.
- There were good Israelites among us who had to suffer with the bad; some of our people, like Daniel and the three Hebrew boys called Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego by their Babylonian names, were kept in captivity for their good.
- He will give them a heart to know Him, and they shall be His people and return unto Him with their whole heart.

**[Jeremiah 24:8-10](/bible/jeremiah/24#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/24#v8)</sup> And as the evil figs, which cannot be eaten, they are so evil; surely thus saith the Lord, So will I give Zedekiah the king of Judah, and his princes, and the residue of Jerusalem, that remain in this land, and them that dwell in the land of Egypt:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/24#v9)</sup> And I will deliver them to be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth for their hurt, to be a reproach and a proverb, a taunt and a curse, in all places whither I shall drive them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/24#v10)</sup> And I will send the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, among them, till they be consumed from off the land that I gave unto them and to their fathers.

- Like the evil figs that cannot be eaten, so the Most High will give up Zedekiah, his princes, and the residue of Jerusalem, including them that dwell in Egypt, to be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth for their hurt.
- They become a reproach, a proverb, a taunt, and a curse in every place, and He sends the sword, the famine, and the pestilence among them until they are consumed; sin took over everybody and the whole nation went into captivity, some there for their good and others for raw servitude and punishment.

# Jeremiah 25: The Seventy Years Captivity and the Cup of Fury

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 25](/bible/jeremiah/25)

**[Jeremiah 25:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/25#v1)</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the people of Judah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, that was the first year of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/25#v2)</sup> The which Jeremiah the prophet spake unto all the people of Judah, and to all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/25#v3)</sup> From the thirteenth year of Josiah the son of Amon king of Judah, even unto this day, that is the three and twentieth year, the word of the Lord hath come unto me, and I have spoken unto you, rising early and speaking; but ye have not hearkened.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/25#v4)</sup> And the Lord hath sent unto you all his servants the prophets, rising early and sending them; but ye have not hearkened, nor inclined your ear to hear.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/25#v5)</sup> They said, Turn ye again now every one from his evil way, and from the evil of your doings, and dwell in the land that the Lord hath given unto you and to your fathers for ever and ever:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/25#v6)</sup> And go not after other gods to serve them, and to worship them, and provoke me not to anger with the works of your hands; and I will do you no hurt.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/25#v7)</sup> Yet ye have not hearkened unto me, saith the Lord; that ye might provoke me to anger with the works of your hands to your own hurt.

- The word came to Jeremiah in the fourth year of Jehoiakim, the first year of Nebuchadnezzar, recounting that for twenty-three years, from the thirteenth year of Josiah, the Most High had risen early and spoken but the people would not hearken.
- He sent all His servants the prophets to call every man to turn from his evil way and not go after other gods, promising He would do them no hurt, yet they provoked Him to anger with the works of their own hands.

**[Jeremiah 25:8-11](/bible/jeremiah/25#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/25#v8)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts; Because ye have not heard my words,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/25#v9)</sup> Behold, I will send and take all the families of the north, saith the Lord, and Nebuchadrezzar the king of Babylon, my servant, and will bring them against this land, and against the inhabitants thereof, and against all these nations round about, and will utterly destroy them, and make them an astonishment, and an hissing, and perpetual desolations.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/25#v10)</sup> Moreover I will take from them the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride, the sound of the millstones, and the light of the candle.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/25#v11)</sup> And this whole land shall be a desolation, and an astonishment; and these nations shall serve the king of Babylon seventy years.

- Because they would not hear, the Most High will send and take all the families of the north and Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, His servant, against this land to utterly destroy it and make it a perpetual desolation, an astonishment, and a hissing.
- He takes from them the voice of mirth and gladness, the voice of the bridegroom and the bride, the sound of the millstones, and the light of the candle; this is the prophecy that these nations shall serve the king of Babylon seventy years.

**[Jeremiah 25:12-14](/bible/jeremiah/25#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/25#v12)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when seventy years are accomplished, that I will punish the king of Babylon, and that nation, saith the Lord, for their iniquity, and the land of the Chaldeans, and will make it perpetual desolations.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/25#v13)</sup> And I will bring upon that land all my words which I have pronounced against it, even all that is written in this book, which Jeremiah hath prophesied against all the nations.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/25#v14)</sup> For many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of them also: and I will recompense them according to their deeds, and according to the works of their own hands.

- When the seventy years are accomplished, the Most High will punish the king of Babylon and that nation for their iniquity and make the land of the Chaldeans a perpetual desolation.
- Even though He may take us down with one kingdom, He then takes that kingdom and punishes it with a new kingdom; the Most High uses people, He used Nebuchadnezzar, He used Cyrus, some of it for our good and some for our punishment.
- Many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of them, and He will recompense them according to their deeds and the works of their own hands.

**[Jeremiah 25:15-29](/bible/jeremiah/25#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/25#v15)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God of Israel unto me; Take the wine cup of this fury at my hand, and cause all the nations, to whom I send thee, to drink it.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/25#v16)</sup> And they shall drink, and be moved, and be mad, because of the sword that I will send among them.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/25#v17)</sup> Then took I the cup at the Lord’s hand, and made all the nations to drink, unto whom the Lord had sent me:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/25#v18)</sup> To wit, Jerusalem, and the cities of Judah, and the kings thereof, and the princes thereof, to make them a desolation, an astonishment, an hissing, and a curse; as it is this day;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/25#v19)</sup> Pharaoh king of Egypt, and his servants, and his princes, and all his people;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/25#v20)</sup> And all the mingled people, and all the kings of the land of Uz, and all the kings of the land of the Philistines, and Ashkelon, and Azzah, and Ekron, and the remnant of Ashdod,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/25#v21)</sup> Edom, and Moab, and the children of Ammon,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/25#v22)</sup> And all the kings of Tyrus, and all the kings of Zidon, and the kings of the isles which are beyond the sea,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/25#v23)</sup> Dedan, and Tema, and Buz, and all that are in the utmost corners,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/25#v24)</sup> And all the kings of Arabia, and all the kings of the mingled people that dwell in the desert,
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/25#v25)</sup> And all the kings of Zimri, and all the kings of Elam, and all the kings of the Medes,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/25#v26)</sup> And all the kings of the north, far and near, one with another, and all the kingdoms of the world, which are upon the face of the earth: and the king of Sheshach shall drink after them.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/25#v27)</sup> Therefore thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Drink ye, and be drunken, and spue, and fall, and rise no more, because of the sword which I will send among you.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/25#v28)</sup> And it shall be, if they refuse to take the cup at thine hand to drink, then shalt thou say unto them, Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Ye shall certainly drink.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/25#v29)</sup> For, lo, I begin to bring evil on the city which is called by my name, and should ye be utterly unpunished? Ye shall not be unpunished: for I will call for a sword upon all the inhabitants of the earth, saith the Lord of hosts.

- The Most High gives Jeremiah the wine cup of His fury to make all the nations drink, beginning with Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, so they become a desolation, an astonishment, a hissing, and a curse.
- The cup passes to Egypt, the mingled people, Uz, the Philistines, Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyrus, Zidon, Dedan, Tema, Buz, Arabia, Zimri, Elam, the Medes, all the kingdoms of the world, and last the king of Sheshach.
- They shall drink, be drunken, spew, fall, and rise no more; the Most High is bringing evil first on the city called by His name, and none of the inhabitants of the earth shall go unpunished.
- Just as we went into captivity, were placed under the neck of our enemies, and had all manner of wickedness done to us, they will not escape; we will repent and be back on top, and everything we went through, they will go through.

**[Jeremiah 25:30-38](/bible/jeremiah/25#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/25#v30)</sup> Therefore prophesy thou against them all these words, and say unto them, The Lord shall roar from on high, and utter his voice from his holy habitation; he shall mightily roar upon his habitation; he shall give a shout, as they that tread the grapes, against all the inhabitants of the earth.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/25#v31)</sup> A noise shall come even to the ends of the earth; for the Lord hath a controversy with the nations, he will plead with all flesh; he will give them that are wicked to the sword, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/25#v32)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, Behold, evil shall go forth from nation to nation, and a great whirlwind shall be raised up from the coasts of the earth.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/25#v33)</sup> And the slain of the Lord shall be at that day from one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth: they shall not be lamented, neither gathered, nor buried; they shall be dung upon the ground.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/25#v34)</sup> Howl, ye shepherds, and cry; and wallow yourselves in the ashes, ye principal of the flock: for the days of your slaughter and of your dispersions are accomplished; and ye shall fall like a pleasant vessel.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/25#v35)</sup> And the shepherds shall have no way to flee, nor the principal of the flock to escape.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/25#v36)</sup> A voice of the cry of the shepherds, and an howling of the principal of the flock, shall be heard: for the Lord hath spoiled their pasture.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/jeremiah/25#v37)</sup> And the peaceable habitations are cut down because of the fierce anger of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/jeremiah/25#v38)</sup> He hath forsaken his covert, as the lion: for their land is desolate because of the fierceness of the oppressor, and because of his fierce anger.

- The Most High will roar from on high against all the inhabitants of the earth, for He has a controversy with the nations and will plead with all flesh and give the wicked to the sword.
- Evil shall go forth from nation to nation, a great whirlwind is raised up, and the slain of the Most High shall lie from one end of the earth to the other, unlamented and unburied.
- The shepherds and the principal of the flock are told to howl and wallow in ashes, for the days of their slaughter are accomplished; all the wickedness the nations did to us comes back on them, and they drink of the Most High's fury exactly as we did.

# Jeremiah 26: Jeremiah Threatened for Prophesying

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 26](/bible/jeremiah/26)

**[Jeremiah 26:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/26#v1)</sup> In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah came this word from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/26#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Stand in the court of the Lord’s house, and speak unto all the cities of Judah, which come to worship in the Lord’s house, all the words that I command thee to speak unto them; diminish not a word:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/26#v3)</sup> If so be they will hearken, and turn every man from his evil way, that I may repent me of the evil, which I purpose to do unto them because of the evil of their doings.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/26#v4)</sup> And thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord; If ye will not hearken to me, to walk in my law, which I have set before you,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/26#v5)</sup> To hearken to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I sent unto you, both rising up early, and sending them, but ye have not hearkened;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/26#v6)</sup> Then will I make this house like Shiloh, and will make this city a curse to all the nations of the earth.

- In the beginning of Jehoiakim's reign the Most High commands Jeremiah to stand in the court of the Lord's house and speak all His words to the cities of Judah, diminishing not a word, that they might hearken and turn from their evil way.
- If they will not hearken and walk in His law, He will make this house like Shiloh and this city a curse to all the nations of the earth.

**[Jeremiah 26:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/26#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/26#v7)</sup> So the priests and the prophets and all the people heard Jeremiah speaking these words in the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/26#v8)</sup> Now it came to pass, when Jeremiah had made an end of speaking all that the Lord had commanded him to speak unto all the people, that the priests and the prophets and all the people took him, saying, Thou shalt surely die.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/26#v9)</sup> Why hast thou prophesied in the name of the Lord, saying, This house shall be like Shiloh, and this city shall be desolate without an inhabitant? And all the people were gathered against Jeremiah in the house of the Lord.

- When Jeremiah finished speaking all that the Most High commanded, the priests, the prophets, and all the people took him and said, Thou shalt surely die.
- They were angry that he prophesied the house would be like Shiloh and the city desolate; this is the same thing we go through when we read the Bible to people and tell them thus saith the Lord, and they get mad and want to harm us.

**[Jeremiah 26:10-11](/bible/jeremiah/26#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/26#v10)</sup> When the princes of Judah heard these things, then they came up from the king’s house unto the house of the Lord, and sat down in the entry of the new gate of the Lord’s house.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/26#v11)</sup> Then spake the priests and the prophets unto the princes and to all the people, saying, This man is worthy to die; for he hath prophesied against this city, as ye have heard with your ears.

- When the princes of Judah heard, they came up from the king's house and sat in the entry of the new gate, and the priests and prophets said this man is worthy to die for prophesying against this city.
- It was not about Jeremiah speaking the word of the Most High but about their feelings getting hurt; he prophesied only because the Most High told him what to say, the same way people today say you cannot speak against this country when we are only saying what the Bible says.

**[Jeremiah 26:12-15](/bible/jeremiah/26#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/26#v12)</sup> Then spake Jeremiah unto all the princes and to all the people, saying, The Lord sent me to prophesy against this house and against this city all the words that ye have heard.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/26#v13)</sup> Therefore now amend your ways and your doings, and obey the voice of the Lord your God; and the Lord will repent him of the evil that he hath pronounced against you.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/26#v14)</sup> As for me, behold, I am in your hand: do with me as seemeth good and meet unto you.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/26#v15)</sup> But know ye for certain, that if ye put me to death, ye shall surely bring innocent blood upon yourselves, and upon this city, and upon the inhabitants thereof: for of a truth the Lord hath sent me unto you to speak all these words in your ears.

- Jeremiah answers the princes and all the people that the Most High sent him to prophesy against this house and city, and tells them to amend their ways and obey, that the Most High might repent of the evil pronounced against them.
- He puts himself in their hand to do as they see good, but warns that if they put him to death they bring innocent blood upon themselves and upon the city, for of a truth the Most High sent him to speak these words.

**[Jeremiah 26:16-19](/bible/jeremiah/26#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/26#v16)</sup> Then said the princes and all the people unto the priests and to the prophets; This man is not worthy to die: for he hath spoken to us in the name of the Lord our God.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/26#v17)</sup> Then rose up certain of the elders of the land, and spake to all the assembly of the people, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/26#v18)</sup> Micah the Morasthite prophesied in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and spake to all the people of Judah, saying, Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Zion shall be plowed like a field, and Jerusalem shall become heaps, and the mountain of the house as the high places of a forest.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/26#v19)</sup> Did Hezekiah king of Judah and all Judah put him at all to death? did he not fear the Lord, and besought the Lord, and the Lord repented him of the evil which he had pronounced against them? Thus might we procure great evil against our souls.

- The princes and all the people then say to the priests and prophets that this man is not worthy to die, for he has spoken in the name of the Most High; our people were rebellious but they were not that dumb.
- The elders rise and recall that Micah the Morasthite prophesied in the days of Hezekiah that Zion would be plowed like a field, yet Hezekiah did not put him to death but feared the Most High, who then repented of the evil.

**[Jeremiah 26:20-24](/bible/jeremiah/26#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/26#v20)</sup> And there was also a man that prophesied in the name of the Lord, Urijah the son of Shemaiah of Kirjath–jearim, who prophesied against this city and against this land according to all the words of Jeremiah:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/26#v21)</sup> And when Jehoiakim the king, with all his mighty men, and all the princes, heard his words, the king sought to put him to death: but when Urijah heard it, he was afraid, and fled, and went into Egypt;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/26#v22)</sup> And Jehoiakim the king sent men into Egypt, namely, Elnathan the son of Achbor, and certain men with him into Egypt.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/26#v23)</sup> And they fetched forth Urijah out of Egypt, and brought him unto Jehoiakim the king; who slew him with the sword, and cast his dead body into the graves of the common people.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/26#v24)</sup> Nevertheless the hand of Ahikam the son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah, that they should not give him into the hand of the people to put him to death.

- The elders also recall Urijah the son of Shemaiah, who prophesied against the city like Jeremiah; when Jehoiakim sought to kill him he fled to Egypt, but the king sent men who fetched him back and slew him with the sword.
- One king knew the word came from the Most High and spared the prophet, while another king put his prophet to death; nevertheless the hand of Ahikam the son of Shaphan was with Jeremiah so he was not given to the people to be killed.

# Jeremiah 27: The Yoke of Babylon

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 27](/bible/jeremiah/27)

**[Jeremiah 27:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/27#v1)</sup> In the beginning of the reign of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah came this word unto Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/27#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord to me; Make thee bonds and yokes, and put them upon thy neck,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/27#v3)</sup> And send them to the king of Edom, and to the king of Moab, and to the king of the Ammonites, and to the king of Tyrus, and to the king of Zidon, by the hand of the messengers which come to Jerusalem unto Zedekiah king of Judah;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/27#v4)</sup> And command them to say unto their masters, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Thus shall ye say unto your masters;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/27#v5)</sup> I have made the earth, the man and the beast that are upon the ground, by my great power and by my outstretched arm, and have given it unto whom it seemed meet unto me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/27#v6)</sup> And now have I given all these lands into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, my servant; and the beasts of the field have I given him also to serve him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/27#v7)</sup> And all nations shall serve him, and his son, and his son’s son, until the very time of his land come: and then many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of him.

- The Most High tells Jeremiah to make bonds and yokes, put them on his neck, and send them to the kings of Edom, Moab, Ammon, Tyrus, and Zidon by their messengers to Zedekiah.
- The Most High made the earth, man, and beast by His power and outstretched arm and gives it to whom it seems meet to Him; He has now given all these lands and the beasts of the field into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar His servant.
- All the nations shall serve him, his son, and his son's son until the time of his land comes, when many nations and great kings shall serve themselves of him; the Most High uses people, He used Nebuchadnezzar, Cyrus, and Pharaoh alike.

**[Jeremiah 27:8-11](/bible/jeremiah/27#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/27#v8)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that the nation and kingdom which will not serve the same Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, and that will not put their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, that nation will I punish, saith the Lord, with the sword, and with the famine, and with the pestilence, until I have consumed them by his hand.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/27#v9)</sup> Therefore hearken not ye to your prophets, nor to your diviners, nor to your dreamers, nor to your enchanters, nor to your sorcerers, which speak unto you, saying, Ye shall not serve the king of Babylon:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/27#v10)</sup> For they prophesy a lie unto you, to remove you far from your land; and that I should drive you out, and ye should perish.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/27#v11)</sup> But the nations that bring their neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him, those will I let remain still in their own land, saith the Lord; and they shall till it, and dwell therein.

- The nation and kingdom that will not serve Nebuchadnezzar and will not put its neck under the yoke of Babylon the Most High will punish with the sword, the famine, and the pestilence until they are consumed.
- Therefore they must not hearken to their prophets, diviners, dreamers, enchanters, and sorcerers who say they shall not serve the king of Babylon, for they prophesy a lie.
- The nations that bring their neck under the yoke and serve him He will let remain in their own land to till it and dwell there; this is the Most High saying that whatever we are commanded to do, even the other nations are told to serve Babylon or answer to Him.

**[Jeremiah 27:12-15](/bible/jeremiah/27#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/27#v12)</sup> I spake also to Zedekiah king of Judah according to all these words, saying, Bring your necks under the yoke of the king of Babylon, and serve him and his people, and live.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/27#v13)</sup> Why will ye die, thou and thy people, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence, as the Lord hath spoken against the nation that will not serve the king of Babylon?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/27#v14)</sup> Therefore hearken not unto the words of the prophets that speak unto you, saying, Ye shall not serve the king of Babylon: for they prophesy a lie unto you.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/27#v15)</sup> For I have not sent them, saith the Lord, yet they prophesy a lie in my name; that I might drive you out, and that ye might perish, ye, and the prophets that prophesy unto you.

- Jeremiah speaks the same words to Zedekiah, telling him to bring his neck under the yoke of the king of Babylon and serve him and live, rather than die by the sword, the famine, and the pestilence.
- He is not to hearken to the prophets who say he shall not serve Babylon, for they prophesy a lie in the Most High's name that will drive the people out to perish along with the prophets themselves.

**[Jeremiah 27:16-22](/bible/jeremiah/27#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/27#v16)</sup> Also I spake to the priests and to all this people, saying, Thus saith the Lord; Hearken not to the words of your prophets that prophesy unto you, saying, Behold, the vessels of the Lord’s house shall now shortly be brought again from Babylon: for they prophesy a lie unto you.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/27#v17)</sup> Hearken not unto them; serve the king of Babylon, and live: wherefore should this city be laid waste?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/27#v18)</sup> But if they be prophets, and if the word of the Lord be with them, let them now make intercession to the Lord of hosts, that the vessels which are left in the house of the Lord, and in the house of the king of Judah, and at Jerusalem, go not to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/27#v19)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts concerning the pillars, and concerning the sea, and concerning the bases, and concerning the residue of the vessels that remain in this city,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/27#v20)</sup> Which Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon took not, when he carried away captive Jeconiah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah from Jerusalem to Babylon, and all the nobles of Judah and Jerusalem;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/27#v21)</sup> Yea, thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, concerning the vessels that remain in the house of the Lord, and in the house of the king of Judah and of Jerusalem;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/27#v22)</sup> They shall be carried to Babylon, and there shall they be until the day that I visit them, saith the Lord; then will I bring them up, and restore them to this place.

- Jeremiah warns the priests and all the people not to listen to the prophets who claim the vessels of the Lord's house will shortly be brought back from Babylon, for they prophesy a lie; they should serve the king of Babylon and live.
- If they were true prophets they would make intercession that the remaining vessels not go to Babylon, but instead the pillars, the sea, the bases, and the residue of the vessels shall be carried to Babylon and remain there until the day the Most High visits them and restores them to this place.
- The whole warning is that Babylon is one the Most High will use to take over everything, and the people had better listen and serve him, because all these prophecies were meant to happen and they did happen.
