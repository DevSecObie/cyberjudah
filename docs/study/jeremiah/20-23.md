Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 20](/bible/jeremiah/20)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 20 through 23. Jeremiah is persecuted for bringing forth the prophecies the Most High gave him concerning the sins of Israel and the punishment coming from Babylon. He grows so weary of the mocking and the stocks that he resolves to stop speaking, but the word burns in him like a fire he cannot hold. From there the reading moves through the way of life and the way of death set before Zedekiah, the woe pronounced on the unrighteous kings of Judah down to the curse on Coniah, and finally the promise of the Righteous Branch set against the lying prophets who speak visions out of their own hearts.

**[Jeremiah 20:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/20#v1)</sup> Now Pashur the son of Immer the priest, who was also chief governor in the house of the Lord, heard that Jeremiah prophesied these things.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/20#v2)</sup> Then Pashur smote Jeremiah the prophet, and put him in the stocks that were in the high gate of Benjamin, which was by the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/20#v3)</sup> And it came to pass on the morrow, that Pashur brought forth Jeremiah out of the stocks. Then said Jeremiah unto him, The Lord hath not called thy name Pashur, but Magor–missabib.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/20#v4)</sup> For thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will make thee a terror to thyself, and to all thy friends: and they shall fall by the sword of their enemies, and thine eyes shall behold it: and I will give all Judah into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall carry them captive into Babylon, and shall slay them with the sword.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/20#v5)</sup> Moreover I will deliver all the strength of this city, and all the labours thereof, and all the precious things thereof, and all the treasures of the kings of Judah will I give into the hand of their enemies, which shall spoil them, and take them, and carry them to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/20#v6)</sup> And thou, Pashur, and all that dwell in thine house shall go into captivity: and thou shalt come to Babylon, and there thou shalt die, and shalt be buried there, thou, and all thy friends, to whom thou hast prophesied lies.

- Pashur the priest, chief governor in the house of the Most High, hears Jeremiah prophesy, smites him, and puts him in the stocks in the high gate of Benjamin.
- When he is let out the next day, Jeremiah tells him the Most High has renamed him Magor-missabib, a terror to himself and all his friends, for they shall fall by the sword of the king of Babylon.
- All Judah, all the strength and treasures of the city, and Pashur's whole household will be carried captive to Babylon, and Pashur will die there and be buried there with all the friends to whom he prophesied lies.

**[Jeremiah 20:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/20#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/20#v7)</sup> O Lord, thou hast deceived me, and I was deceived: thou art stronger than I, and hast prevailed: I am in derision daily, every one mocketh me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/20#v8)</sup> For since I spake, I cried out, I cried violence and spoil; because the word of the Lord was made a reproach unto me, and a derision, daily.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/20#v9)</sup> Then I said, I will not make mention of him, nor speak any more in his name. But his word was in mine heart as a burning fire shut up in my bones, and I was weary with forbearing, and I could not stay.

- Jeremiah is going through it so bad, mocked and reproached daily, that he says he will not make mention of the Most High nor speak any more in His name.
- Just like us when we go out to camp, if somebody does not speak evil of us every time we do something big, there is a hater or somebody trying to demonize us.
- But the word was in his heart like a burning fire shut up in his bones; he was weary with holding it in and could not stay, he had to blast them, he could not hold it.

**[Jeremiah 20:10-13](/bible/jeremiah/20#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/20#v10)</sup> For I heard the defaming of many, fear on every side. Report, say they, and we will report it. All my familiars watched for my halting, saying, Peradventure he will be enticed, and we shall prevail against him, and we shall take our revenge on him.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/20#v11)</sup> But the Lord is with me as a mighty terrible one: therefore my persecutors shall stumble, and they shall not prevail: they shall be greatly ashamed; for they shall not prosper: their everlasting confusion shall never be forgotten.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/20#v12)</sup> But, O Lord of hosts, that triest the righteous, and seest the reins and the heart, let me see thy vengeance on them: for unto thee have I opened my cause.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/20#v13)</sup> Sing unto the Lord, praise ye the Lord: for he hath delivered the soul of the poor from the hand of evildoers.

- Jeremiah hears the defaming of many and his familiar friends watching for his stumbling, hoping to be enticed against him and take their revenge.
- The Most High is with him as a mighty terrible one, so his persecutors will stumble, not prevail, and be greatly ashamed in an everlasting confusion that will never be forgotten.
- He commits his cause to the Most High who tries the righteous and sees the reins and the heart, and sings praise because He delivers the soul of the poor from the hand of evildoers.

**[Jeremiah 20:14-18](/bible/jeremiah/20#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/20#v14)</sup> Cursed be the day wherein I was born: let not the day wherein my mother bare me be blessed.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/20#v15)</sup> Cursed be the man who brought tidings to my father, saying, A man child is born unto thee; making him very glad.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/20#v16)</sup> And let that man be as the cities which the Lord overthrew, and repented not: and let him hear the cry in the morning, and the shouting at noontide;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/20#v17)</sup> Because he slew me not from the womb; or that my mother might have been my grave, and her womb to be always great with me.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/20#v18)</sup> Wherefore came I forth out of the womb to see labour and sorrow, that my days should be consumed with shame?

- Crushed by the persecution, Jeremiah curses the day he was born and the man who brought his father the news of a man child, wishing that man to be like the cities the Most High overthrew.
- He asks why he came forth out of the womb only to see labor and sorrow and to have his days consumed with shame.

# Jeremiah 21: The Way of Life and the Way of Death

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 21](/bible/jeremiah/21)

**[Jeremiah 21:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/21#v1)</sup> The word which came unto Jeremiah from the Lord, when king Zedekiah sent unto him Pashur the son of Melchiah, and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/21#v2)</sup> Enquire, I pray thee, of the Lord for us; for Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon maketh war against us; if so be that the Lord will deal with us according to all his wondrous works, that he may go up from us.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/21#v3)</sup> Then said Jeremiah unto them, Thus shall ye say to Zedekiah:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/21#v4)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God of Israel; Behold, I will turn back the weapons of war that are in your hands, wherewith ye fight against the king of Babylon, and against the Chaldeans, which besiege you without the walls, and I will assemble them into the midst of this city.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/21#v5)</sup> And I myself will fight against you with an outstretched hand and with a strong arm, even in anger, and in fury, and in great wrath.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/21#v6)</sup> And I will smite the inhabitants of this city, both man and beast: they shall die of a great pestilence.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/21#v7)</sup> And afterward, saith the Lord, I will deliver Zedekiah king of Judah, and his servants, and the people, and such as are left in this city from the pestilence, from the sword, and from the famine, into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of those that seek their life: and he shall smite them with the edge of the sword; he shall not spare them, neither have pity, nor have mercy.

- King Zedekiah sends Pashur and Zephaniah the priest to ask Jeremiah to inquire of the Most High, hoping He will deal with them according to His wondrous works while Nebuchadnezzar makes war on them.
- The answer is that the Most High will turn back their own weapons and Himself fight against them with an outstretched hand, in anger, fury, and great wrath, smiting man and beast with a great pestilence.
- Afterward He will deliver Zedekiah, his servants, and the people into Nebuchadnezzar's hand, to be smitten with the sword without pity or mercy.

**[Jeremiah 21:8-10](/bible/jeremiah/21#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/21#v8)</sup> And unto this people thou shalt say, Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I set before you the way of life, and the way of death.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/21#v9)</sup> He that abideth in this city shall die by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence: but he that goeth out, and falleth to the Chaldeans that besiege you, he shall live, and his life shall be unto him for a prey.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/21#v10)</sup> For I have set my face against this city for evil, and not for good, saith the Lord: it shall be given into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire.

- The Most High sets before the people the way of life and the way of death: he that stays in the city dies by the sword, famine, and pestilence, but he that goes out and falls to the Chaldeans shall live.
- His face is set against this city for evil and not for good, and it shall be given into the king of Babylon's hand to be burned with fire.

**[Jeremiah 21:11-14](/bible/jeremiah/21#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/21#v11)</sup> And touching the house of the king of Judah, say, Hear ye the word of the Lord;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/21#v12)</sup> O house of David, thus saith the Lord; Execute judgment in the morning, and deliver him that is spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor, lest my fury go out like fire, and burn that none can quench it, because of the evil of your doings.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/21#v13)</sup> Behold, I am against thee, O inhabitant of the valley, and rock of the plain, saith the Lord; which say, Who shall come down against us? or who shall enter into our habitations?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/21#v14)</sup> But I will punish you according to the fruit of your doings, saith the Lord: and I will kindle a fire in the forest thereof, and it shall devour all things round about it.

- To the house of David the Most High commands they execute judgment in the morning and deliver the spoiled out of the oppressor's hand, lest His fury go out like an unquenchable fire.
- He stands against the inhabitant of the valley and rock of the plain who think none can come down against them, and will punish them according to the fruit of their doings.

# Jeremiah 22: Woe to the Unrighteous Kings of Judah

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 22](/bible/jeremiah/22)

**[Jeremiah 22:1-9](/bible/jeremiah/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/22#v1)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Go down to the house of the king of Judah, and speak there this word,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/22#v2)</sup> And say, Hear the word of the Lord, O king of Judah, that sittest upon the throne of David, thou, and thy servants, and thy people that enter in by these gates:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/22#v3)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Execute ye judgment and righteousness, and deliver the spoiled out of the hand of the oppressor: and do no wrong, do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, nor the widow, neither shed innocent blood in this place.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/22#v4)</sup> For if ye do this thing indeed, then shall there enter in by the gates of this house kings sitting upon the throne of David, riding in chariots and on horses, he, and his servants, and his people.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/22#v5)</sup> But if ye will not hear these words, I swear by myself, saith the Lord, that this house shall become a desolation.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/22#v6)</sup> For thus saith the Lord unto the king’s house of Judah; Thou art Gilead unto me, and the head of Lebanon: yet surely I will make thee a wilderness, and cities which are not inhabited.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/22#v7)</sup> And I will prepare destroyers against thee, every one with his weapons: and they shall cut down thy choice cedars, and cast them into the fire.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/22#v8)</sup> And many nations shall pass by this city, and they shall say every man to his neighbour, Wherefore hath the Lord done thus unto this great city?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/22#v9)</sup> Then they shall answer, Because they have forsaken the covenant of the Lord their God, and worshipped other gods, and served them.

- Jeremiah is sent down to the king of Judah's house to command the king to execute judgment and righteousness, deliver the oppressed, and do no violence to the stranger, the fatherless, or the widow.
- If they obey, kings will keep entering the gates riding in chariots and on horses, but if they will not hear, the Most High swears by Himself that this house shall become a desolation.
- Many nations will pass by and ask why He did this to so great a city, and the answer is that they forsook His covenant and worshipped other gods.

**[Jeremiah 22:10-12](/bible/jeremiah/22#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/22#v10)</sup> Weep ye not for the dead, neither bemoan him: but weep sore for him that goeth away: for he shall return no more, nor see his native country.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/22#v11)</sup> For thus saith the Lord touching Shallum the son of Josiah king of Judah, which reigned instead of Josiah his father, which went forth out of this place; He shall not return thither any more:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/22#v12)</sup> But he shall die in the place whither they have led him captive, and shall see this land no more.

- The people are told not to weep for the dead but to weep sore for him that goes away, Shallum the son of Josiah who reigned in his father's place.
- Shallum went forth out of his place and shall not return, but shall die in the land where they led him captive and see his native country no more.

**[Jeremiah 22:13-19](/bible/jeremiah/22#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/22#v13)</sup> Woe unto him that buildeth his house by unrighteousness, and his chambers by wrong; that useth his neighbour’s service without wages, and giveth him not for his work;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/22#v14)</sup> That saith, I will build me a wide house and large chambers, and cutteth him out windows; and it is cieled with cedar, and painted with vermilion.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/22#v15)</sup> Shalt thou reign, because thou closest thyself in cedar? did not thy father eat and drink, and do judgment and justice, and then it was well with him?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/22#v16)</sup> He judged the cause of the poor and needy; then it was well with him: was not this to know me? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/22#v17)</sup> But thine eyes and thine heart are not but for thy covetousness, and for to shed innocent blood, and for oppression, and for violence, to do it.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/22#v18)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah; They shall not lament for him, saying, Ah my brother! or, Ah sister! they shall not lament for him, saying, Ah lord! or, Ah his glory!
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/22#v19)</sup> He shall be buried with the burial of an ass, drawn and cast forth beyond the gates of Jerusalem.

- Woe is pronounced on Jehoiakim, who builds his house by unrighteousness and his chambers by wrong, using his neighbor's service without wages and painting his wide house with vermilion.
- His father ate and drank yet did judgment and justice for the poor and needy, and it was well with him, but Jehoiakim's eyes and heart are set only on covetousness, innocent blood, oppression, and violence.
- Therefore no one will lament him; he shall be buried with the burial of an ass, drawn and cast forth beyond the gates of Jerusalem.

**[Jeremiah 22:20-30](/bible/jeremiah/22#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/22#v20)</sup> Go up to Lebanon, and cry; and lift up thy voice in Bashan, and cry from the passages: for all thy lovers are destroyed.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/22#v21)</sup> I spake unto thee in thy prosperity; but thou saidst, I will not hear. This hath been thy manner from thy youth, that thou obeyedst not my voice.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/22#v22)</sup> The wind shall eat up all thy pastors, and thy lovers shall go into captivity: surely then shalt thou be ashamed and confounded for all thy wickedness.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/22#v23)</sup> O inhabitant of Lebanon, that makest thy nest in the cedars, how gracious shalt thou be when pangs come upon thee, the pain as of a woman in travail!
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/22#v24)</sup> As I live, saith the Lord, though Coniah the son of Jehoiakim king of Judah were the signet upon my right hand, yet would I pluck thee thence;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/22#v25)</sup> And I will give thee into the hand of them that seek thy life, and into the hand of them whose face thou fearest, even into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, and into the hand of the Chaldeans.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/22#v26)</sup> And I will cast thee out, and thy mother that bare thee, into another country, where ye were not born; and there shall ye die.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/22#v27)</sup> But to the land whereunto they desire to return, thither shall they not return.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/22#v28)</sup> Is this man Coniah a despised broken idol? is he a vessel wherein is no pleasure? wherefore are they cast out, he and his seed, and are cast into a land which they know not?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/22#v29)</sup> O earth, earth, earth, hear the word of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/22#v30)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Write ye this man childless, a man that shall not prosper in his days: for no man of his seed shall prosper, sitting upon the throne of David, and ruling any more in Judah.

- Jerusalem is told to cry from Lebanon and Bashan because all her lovers are destroyed; she would not hear in her prosperity, which has been her manner from her youth.
- Coniah the son of Jehoiakim, even were he the signet on the Most High's right hand, will be plucked off and given into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar and the Chaldeans whose face he fears, cast out with his mother into a country where they were not born to die.
- The Most High writes this man childless, for no man of his seed shall prosper sitting upon the throne of David or ruling in Judah anymore; Deacon Marathon and the History of a King class break down this curse of Coniah in fuller detail.

# Jeremiah 23: The Righteous Branch and the Lying Prophets

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 23](/bible/jeremiah/23)

**[Jeremiah 23:1-4](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)</sup> Woe be unto the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture! saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/23#v2)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God of Israel against the pastors that feed my people; Ye have scattered my flock, and driven them away, and have not visited them: behold, I will visit upon you the evil of your doings, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/23#v3)</sup> And I will gather the remnant of my flock out of all countries whither I have driven them, and will bring them again to their folds; and they shall be fruitful and increase.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/23#v4)</sup> And I will set up shepherds over them which shall feed them: and they shall fear no more, nor be dismayed, neither shall they be lacking, saith the Lord.

- Woe is pronounced on the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of the Most High's pasture and have not visited them, so He will visit upon them the evil of their doings.
- He will gather the remnant of His flock out of the countries where He drove them, bring them again to their folds, and set up shepherds who will feed them so they fear no more and none are lacking.

**[Jeremiah 23:5-8](/bible/jeremiah/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/23#v5)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/23#v6)</sup> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/23#v7)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that they shall no more say, The Lord liveth, which brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/23#v8)</sup> But, The Lord liveth, which brought up and which led the seed of the house of Israel out of the north country, and from all countries whither I had driven them; and they shall dwell in their own land.

- The Most High promises to raise unto David a righteous Branch, a King who shall reign, prosper, and execute judgment and justice, in whose days Judah shall be saved and Israel dwell safely.
- His name is called THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS; this is plainly referring to Christ.
- The day comes when they will no longer swear by the Most High who brought Israel out of Egypt, but by Him who led the seed of the house of Israel out of the north country and all the countries where He had driven them, back to their own land.

**[Jeremiah 23:9-15](/bible/jeremiah/23#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/23#v9)</sup> Mine heart within me is broken because of the prophets; all my bones shake; I am like a drunken man, and like a man whom wine hath overcome, because of the Lord, and because of the words of his holiness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/23#v10)</sup> For the land is full of adulterers; for because of swearing the land mourneth; the pleasant places of the wilderness are dried up, and their course is evil, and their force is not right.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/23#v11)</sup> For both prophet and priest are profane; yea, in my house have I found their wickedness, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/23#v12)</sup> Wherefore their way shall be unto them as slippery ways in the darkness: they shall be driven on, and fall therein: for I will bring evil upon them, even the year of their visitation, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/23#v13)</sup> And I have seen folly in the prophets of Samaria; they prophesied in Baal, and caused my people Israel to err.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/23#v14)</sup> I have seen also in the prophets of Jerusalem an horrible thing: they commit adultery, and walk in lies: they strengthen also the hands of evildoers, that none doth return from his wickedness: they are all of them unto me as Sodom, and the inhabitants thereof as Gomorrah.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/23#v15)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts concerning the prophets; Behold, I will feed them with wormwood, and make them drink the water of gall: for from the prophets of Jerusalem is profaneness gone forth into all the land.

- Jeremiah's heart is broken and his bones shake like a drunken man because of the prophets and the words of the Most High's holiness, for the land is full of adulterers and mourns under swearing.
- Both prophet and priest are profane, and the Most High has found their wickedness even in His house, so their way shall be as slippery ways in the darkness where they are driven and fall.
- The prophets of Samaria prophesied in Baal and caused Israel to err, and the prophets of Jerusalem commit adultery, walk in lies, and strengthen the hands of evildoers so that none return from wickedness, becoming as Sodom and Gomorrah before Him.

**[Jeremiah 23:16-22](/bible/jeremiah/23#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/23#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, Hearken not unto the words of the prophets that prophesy unto you: they make you vain: they speak a vision of their own heart, and not out of the mouth of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/23#v17)</sup> They say still unto them that despise me, The Lord hath said, Ye shall have peace; and they say unto every one that walketh after the imagination of his own heart, No evil shall come upon you.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/23#v18)</sup> For who hath stood in the counsel of the Lord, and hath perceived and heard his word? who hath marked his word, and heard it?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/23#v19)</sup> Behold, a whirlwind of the Lord is gone forth in fury, even a grievous whirlwind: it shall fall grievously upon the head of the wicked.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/23#v20)</sup> The anger of the Lord shall not return, until he have executed, and till he have performed the thoughts of his heart: in the latter days ye shall consider it perfectly.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/23#v21)</sup> I have not sent these prophets, yet they ran: I have not spoken to them, yet they prophesied.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/23#v22)</sup> But if they had stood in my counsel, and had caused my people to hear my words, then they should have turned them from their evil way, and from the evil of their doings.

- The people are told not to hearken to the prophets who make them vain and speak a vision of their own heart, not out of the mouth of the Most High, telling those who despise Him that they shall have peace and no evil shall come.
- This is like the pastor who says, let me tell you what God told me to tell you, when it is a lie.
- None of these prophets has stood in the counsel of the Most High; had they stood there and caused the people to hear His words, they would have turned them from their evil way, but instead the whirlwind of His anger will fall grievously upon the head of the wicked in the latter days.

**[Jeremiah 23:23-32](/bible/jeremiah/23#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/23#v23)</sup> Am I a God at hand, saith the Lord, and not a God afar off?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/23#v24)</sup> Can any hide himself in secret places that I shall not see him? saith the Lord. Do not I fill heaven and earth? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/23#v25)</sup> I have heard what the prophets said, that prophesy lies in my name, saying, I have dreamed, I have dreamed.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/23#v26)</sup> How long shall this be in the heart of the prophets that prophesy lies? yea, they are prophets of the deceit of their own heart;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/23#v27)</sup> Which think to cause my people to forget my name by their dreams which they tell every man to his neighbour, as their fathers have forgotten my name for Baal.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/23#v28)</sup> The prophet that hath a dream, let him tell a dream; and he that hath my word, let him speak my word faithfully. What is the chaff to the wheat? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/23#v29)</sup> Is not my word like as a fire? saith the Lord; and like a hammer that breaketh the rock in pieces?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/23#v30)</sup> Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets, saith the Lord, that steal my words every one from his neighbour.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/23#v31)</sup> Behold, I am against the prophets, saith the Lord, that use their tongues, and say, He saith.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/23#v32)</sup> Behold, I am against them that prophesy false dreams, saith the Lord, and do tell them, and cause my people to err by their lies, and by their lightness; yet I sent them not, nor commanded them: therefore they shall not profit this people at all, saith the Lord.

- The Most High asks whether He is a God only near and not far off, when He fills heaven and earth and no one can hide in secret places where He cannot see him.
- He has heard the prophets who prophesy lies in His name saying, I have dreamed, thinking to cause His people to forget His name by their dreams as their fathers forgot it for Baal.
- Let the prophet with a dream tell a dream, but let him that has His word speak it faithfully, for His word is like a fire and like a hammer that breaks the rock in pieces; He is against those who steal His words and use their own tongues to lie on the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 23:33-40](/bible/jeremiah/23#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/23#v33)</sup> And when this people, or the prophet, or a priest, shall ask thee, saying, What is the burden of the Lord? thou shalt then say unto them, What burden? I will even forsake you, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/23#v34)</sup> And as for the prophet, and the priest, and the people, that shall say, The burden of the Lord, I will even punish that man and his house.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/23#v35)</sup> Thus shall ye say every one to his neighbour, and every one to his brother, What hath the Lord answered? and, What hath the Lord spoken?
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/23#v36)</sup> And the burden of the Lord shall ye mention no more: for every man’s word shall be his burden; for ye have perverted the words of the living God, of the Lord of hosts our God.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/jeremiah/23#v37)</sup> Thus shalt thou say to the prophet, What hath the Lord answered thee? and, What hath the Lord spoken?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/jeremiah/23#v38)</sup> But since ye say, The burden of the Lord; therefore thus saith the Lord; Because ye say this word, The burden of the Lord, and I have sent unto you, saying, Ye shall not say, The burden of the Lord;
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/jeremiah/23#v39)</sup> Therefore, behold, I, even I, will utterly forget you, and I will forsake you, and the city that I gave you and your fathers, and cast you out of my presence:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/jeremiah/23#v40)</sup> And I will bring an everlasting reproach upon you, and a perpetual shame, which shall not be forgotten.

- When the people, prophet, or priest ask what is the burden of the Most High, Jeremiah is to answer, What burden? for He will forsake them, and every man who claims the burden of the Most High He will punish in his house.
- Instead of saying the burden of the Most High, they are to ask one another what the Most High has answered and spoken, for they have perverted the words of the living God.
- Because they keep saying the burden of the Most High after being told not to, He will utterly forget them, forsake them, and cast them out of His presence with an everlasting reproach and perpetual shame that shall not be forgotten.
