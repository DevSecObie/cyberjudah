**[Jeremiah 34:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/34#v1)</sup> The word which came unto Jeremiah from the Lord, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth of his dominion, and all the people, fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities thereof, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/34#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and tell him, Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/34#v3)</sup> And thou shalt not escape out of his hand, but shalt surely be taken, and delivered into his hand; and thine eyes shall behold the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he shall speak with thee mouth to mouth, and thou shalt go to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/34#v4)</sup> Yet hear the word of the Lord, O Zedekiah king of Judah; Thus saith the Lord of thee, Thou shalt not die by the sword:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/34#v5)</sup> But thou shalt die in peace: and with the burnings of thy fathers, the former kings which were before thee, so shall they burn odours for thee; and they will lament thee, saying, Ah lord! for I have pronounced the word, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/34#v6)</sup> Then Jeremiah the prophet spake all these words unto Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/34#v7)</sup> When the king of Babylon’s army fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish, and against Azekah: for these defenced cities remained of the cities of Judah.

- The word came when Nebuchadnezzar and all his army fought against Jerusalem: Jeremiah is to tell Zedekiah that the Most High will give the city into Babylon's hand to be burned with fire, and Zedekiah shall not escape.
- His eyes shall behold the king of Babylon's eyes and he shall speak with him mouth to mouth and go to Babylon, yet he shall not die by the sword but die in peace, with the burnings and lamentations of the former kings.
- Jeremiah spoke these words while Babylon's army fought against Jerusalem, Lachish, and Azekah, which alone remained of the defensed cities of Judah.

**[Jeremiah 34:8-11](/bible/jeremiah/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/34#v8)</sup> This is the word that came unto Jeremiah from the Lord, after that the king Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people which were at Jerusalem, to proclaim liberty unto them;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/34#v9)</sup> That every man should let his manservant, and every man his maidservant, being an Hebrew or an Hebrewess, go free; that none should serve himself of them, to wit, of a Jew his brother.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/34#v10)</sup> Now when all the princes, and all the people, which had entered into the covenant, heard that every one should let his manservant, and every one his maidservant, go free, that none should serve themselves of them any more, then they obeyed, and let them go.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/34#v11)</sup> But afterward they turned, and caused the servants and the handmaids, whom they had let go free, to return, and brought them into subjection for servants and for handmaids.

- Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people at Jerusalem to proclaim liberty, that every man let his Hebrew manservant and maidservant go free and none serve himself of his Jewish brother.
- The princes and people obeyed and let them go, but afterward they turned and brought the servants and handmaids back into subjection.
  - **[Leviticus 25:9-10](/bible/leviticus/25#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/25#v9)</sup> Then shalt thou cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the day of atonement shall ye make the trumpet sound throughout all your land.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)</sup> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.

    The trumpet of the jubilee sounds on the day of atonement to hallow the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty, returning every man to his possession and to his family.

**[Jeremiah 34:12-16](/bible/jeremiah/34#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/34#v12)</sup> Therefore the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/34#v13)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; I made a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondmen, saying,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/34#v14)</sup> At the end of seven years let ye go every man his brother an Hebrew, which hath been sold unto thee; and when he hath served thee six years, thou shalt let him go free from thee: but your fathers hearkened not unto me, neither inclined their ear.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/34#v15)</sup> And ye were now turned, and had done right in my sight, in proclaiming liberty every man to his neighbour; and ye had made a covenant before me in the house which is called by my name:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/34#v16)</sup> But ye turned and polluted my name, and caused every man his servant, and every man his handmaid, whom ye had set at liberty at their pleasure, to return, and brought them into subjection, to be unto you for servants and for handmaids.

- The Most High reminds them He made a covenant with their fathers when He brought them out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, that at the end of seven years every man should let his Hebrew brother go free.
- The fathers hearkened not, and though this generation had turned and done right in proclaiming liberty, making a covenant before Him in the house called by His name, they turned and polluted His name.
- They caused every man to bring his servant and handmaid back into subjection to be servants and handmaids again.
  - **[Exodus 21:2](/bible/exodus/21#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/21#v2)</sup> If thou buy an Hebrew servant, six years he shall serve: and in the seventh he shall go out free for nothing.

    The commandment was that a Hebrew servant serve six years and in the seventh go out free.
  - **[Psalms 138:2](/bible/psalms/138#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/138#v2)</sup> I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name.

    Polluting His name was not mispronouncing a name but breaking His commandments, for He magnified His word above His name.

**[Jeremiah 34:17-18](/bible/jeremiah/34#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/34#v17)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Ye have not hearkened unto me, in proclaiming liberty, every one to his brother, and every man to his neighbour: behold, I proclaim a liberty for you, saith the Lord, to the sword, to the pestilence, and to the famine; and I will make you to be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/34#v18)</sup> And I will give the men that have transgressed my covenant, which have not performed the words of the covenant which they had made before me, when they cut the calf in twain, and passed between the parts thereof,

- Because they would not proclaim liberty to their brothers, the Most High proclaims a liberty to the sword, the pestilence, and the famine, and will make them to be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth, meaning we would be scattered.
- He will give the men who transgressed His covenant, who cut the calf in twain and passed between the parts thereof, into the hand of their enemies.
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.

    He warned we would be scattered among the nations, left few in number.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    He would scatter us among all people from one end of the earth to the other.

**[Jeremiah 34:19-22](/bible/jeremiah/34#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/34#v19)</sup> The princes of Judah, and the princes of Jerusalem, the eunuchs, and the priests, and all the people of the land, which passed between the parts of the calf;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/34#v20)</sup> I will even give them into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life: and their dead bodies shall be for meat unto the fowls of the heaven, and to the beasts of the earth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/34#v21)</sup> And Zedekiah king of Judah and his princes will I give into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life, and into the hand of the king of Babylon’s army, which are gone up from you.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/34#v22)</sup> Behold, I will command, saith the Lord, and cause them to return to this city; and they shall fight against it, and take it, and burn it with fire: and I will make the cities of Judah a desolation without an inhabitant.

- The princes, eunuchs, priests, and all the people who passed between the parts of the calf He will give into the hand of their enemies, and their dead bodies shall be meat for the fowls of heaven and the beasts of the earth.
- Zedekiah and his princes He will give into the hand of Babylon's army, which He will cause to return, take, and burn the city, and make the cities of Judah a desolation without inhabitant.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:26](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v26)</sup> And thy carcase shall be meat unto all fowls of the air, and unto the beasts of the earth, and no man shall fray them away.

    Our carcasses would be meat unto the fowls of the air and the beasts of the earth.
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:26](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v26)</sup> I said, I would scatter them into corners, I would make the remembrance of them to cease from among men:

    Being scattered ties into loving your neighbor as yourself; going into the Passover season we must purge out the old leaven of malice and hatred and get our spirits right.
