**[Jeremiah 15:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/15#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/15#v1)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people: cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/15#v2)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if they say unto thee, Whither shall we go forth? then thou shalt tell them, Thus saith the Lord; Such as are for death, to death; and such as are for the sword, to the sword; and such as are for the famine, to the famine; and such as are for the captivity, to the captivity.

- Though Moses and Samuel stood before Him, the mind of the Most High could not be toward this people; He says cast them out of My sight.
- When the people ask where they shall go, the answer is such as are for death to death, for the sword to the sword, for the famine to the famine, and for the captivity to the captivity. That is how we got removed from before the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 15:3-9](/bible/jeremiah/15#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/15#v3)</sup> And I will appoint over them four kinds, saith the Lord: the sword to slay, and the dogs to tear, and the fowls of the heaven, and the beasts of the earth, to devour and destroy.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/15#v4)</sup> And I will cause them to be removed into all kingdoms of the earth, because of Manasseh the son of Hezekiah king of Judah, for that which he did in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/15#v5)</sup> For who shall have pity upon thee, O Jerusalem? or who shall bemoan thee? or who shall go aside to ask how thou doest?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/15#v6)</sup> Thou hast forsaken me, saith the Lord, thou art gone backward: therefore will I stretch out my hand against thee, and destroy thee; I am weary with repenting.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/15#v7)</sup> And I will fan them with a fan in the gates of the land; I will bereave them of children, I will destroy my people, since they return not from their ways.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/15#v8)</sup> Their widows are increased to me above the sand of the seas: I have brought upon them against the mother of the young men a spoiler at noonday: I have caused him to fall upon it suddenly, and terrors upon the city.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/15#v9)</sup> She that hath borne seven languisheth: she hath given up the ghost; her sun is gone down while it was yet day: she hath been ashamed and confounded: and the residue of them will I deliver to the sword before their enemies, saith the Lord.

- He appoints four kinds over them: the sword to slay, the dogs to tear, and the fowls and beasts to devour, and He will remove them into all kingdoms of the earth because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah did in Jerusalem.
- He is weary with repenting and will bereave them of children because they return not from their ways; the mother of young men gets a spoiler at noonday, and she that bore seven languishes and gives up the ghost.
- That hit home; there was a sister last sabbath who had seven sons and was very much depressed. The scripture speaks to a real grief among the people.

**[Jeremiah 15:10-14](/bible/jeremiah/15#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/15#v10)</sup> Woe is me, my mother, that thou hast borne me a man of strife and a man of contention to the whole earth! I have neither lent on usury, nor men have lent to me on usury; yet every one of them doth curse me.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/15#v11)</sup> The Lord said, Verily it shall be well with thy remnant; verily I will cause the enemy to entreat thee well in the time of evil and in the time of affliction.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/15#v12)</sup> Shall iron break the northern iron and the steel?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/15#v13)</sup> Thy substance and thy treasures will I give to the spoil without price, and that for all thy sins, even in all thy borders.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/15#v14)</sup> And I will make thee to pass with thine enemies into a land which thou knowest not: for a fire is kindled in mine anger, which shall burn upon you.

- Jeremiah cries woe over being born a man of strife and contention, cursed by everyone though he lent to no one on usury. The Most High answers that it shall be well with his remnant.
- That remnant is those who are keeping the commandments. Their substance and treasures will be given to the spoil for all their sins, and they will pass with their enemies into a land they do not know.

**[Jeremiah 15:15-18](/bible/jeremiah/15#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/15#v15)</sup> O Lord, thou knowest: remember me, and visit me, and revenge me of my persecutors; take me not away in thy longsuffering: know that for thy sake I have suffered rebuke.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/15#v16)</sup> Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart: for I am called by thy name, O Lord God of hosts.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/15#v17)</sup> I sat not in the assembly of the mockers, nor rejoiced; I sat alone because of thy hand: for thou hast filled me with indignation.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/15#v18)</sup> Why is my pain perpetual, and my wound incurable, which refuseth to be healed? wilt thou be altogether unto me as a liar, and as waters that fail?

- Jeremiah asks the Most High to remember him, visit him, and revenge him of his persecutors, and not to take him away in His longsuffering, since for His sake he has suffered rebuke.
- His words were found and Jeremiah did eat them, and they were the joy and rejoicing of his heart, for he is called by the name of the Most High and sat alone, filled with indignation.
- He asks why his pain is perpetual and his wound refuses to be healed. This is what the prophets go through: we bring healing, order, structure and discipline to our people and get hated and treated badly, and sometimes these are the thoughts we have.

**[Jeremiah 15:19-21](/bible/jeremiah/15#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/15#v19)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord, If thou return, then will I bring thee again, and thou shalt stand before me: and if thou take forth the precious from the vile, thou shalt be as my mouth: let them return unto thee; but return not thou unto them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/15#v20)</sup> And I will make thee unto this people a fenced brasen wall: and they shall fight against thee, but they shall not prevail against thee: for I am with thee to save thee and to deliver thee, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/15#v21)</sup> And I will deliver thee out of the hand of the wicked, and I will redeem thee out of the hand of the terrible.

- If Jeremiah returns, the Most High will bring him again to stand before Him; if he takes the precious from the vile he shall be as His mouth. Let them return to him, but he is not to return to them.
- He will make Jeremiah a fenced brazen wall; they shall fight against him but not prevail, for the Most High is with him to save and deliver him.
- That is what we live on. When we go out teaching this bible, and when brothers and sisters apply the commandments to their everyday lives, people will come against us, but the Most High is our defense. As long as we keep the commandments and the faith of Christ, no one can prevail, and if they try they will fail.
