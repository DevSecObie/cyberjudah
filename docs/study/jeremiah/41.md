**[Jeremiah 41:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/41#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/41#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass in the seventh month, that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah the son of Elishama, of the seed royal, and the princes of the king, even ten men with him, came unto Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to Mizpah; and there they did eat bread together in Mizpah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/41#v2)</sup> Then arose Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and the ten men that were with him, and smote Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan with the sword, and slew him, whom the king of Babylon had made governor over the land.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/41#v3)</sup> Ishmael also slew all the Jews that were with him, even with Gedaliah, at Mizpah, and the Chaldeans that were found there, and the men of war.

- In the seventh month Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, of the royal seed and a prince of the king, came with ten men to Gedaliah at Mizpah, and they ate bread together at a banquet held in Ishmael's honor.
- Ishmael and his ten men rose up and smote Gedaliah the son of Ahikam with the sword and slew him, the governor the king of Babylon had made over the land.
- Ishmael also slew all the Jews with Gedaliah at Mizpah and the Chaldeans and men of war that were found there.

**[Jeremiah 41:4-9](/bible/jeremiah/41#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/41#v4)</sup> And it came to pass the second day after he had slain Gedaliah, and no man knew it,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/41#v5)</sup> That there came certain from Shechem, from Shiloh, and from Samaria, even fourscore men, having their beards shaven, and their clothes rent, and having cut themselves, with offerings and incense in their hand, to bring them to the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/41#v6)</sup> And Ishmael the son of Nethaniah went forth from Mizpah to meet them, weeping all along as he went: and it came to pass, as he met them, he said unto them, Come to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/41#v7)</sup> And it was so, when they came into the midst of the city, that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah slew them, and cast them into the midst of the pit, he, and the men that were with him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/41#v8)</sup> But ten men were found among them that said unto Ishmael, Slay us not: for we have treasures in the field, of wheat, and of barley, and of oil, and of honey. So he forbare, and slew them not among their brethren.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/41#v9)</sup> Now the pit wherein Ishmael had cast all the dead bodies of the men, whom he had slain because of Gedaliah, was it which Asa the king had made for fear of Baasha king of Israel: and Ishmael the son of Nethaniah filled it with them that were slain.

- The second day after the murder, before anyone knew of it, eighty men came from Shechem, Shiloh, and Samaria with their beards shaven, their clothes rent, and their bodies cut, bringing offerings and incense to the house of the Most High.
- Ishmael went out from Mizpah weeping as he met them, playing a part, and invited them in to Gedaliah; when they came into the midst of the city he and his men slew them and cast them into a pit.
- Ten of the eighty were spared when they told Ishmael they had hidden treasures of wheat, barley, oil, and honey in the field.
- The pit Ishmael filled with the slain was the one Asa the king had made for fear of Baasha king of Israel during the war between them.

    > <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/15#v22)</sup> Then king Asa made a proclamation throughout all Judah; none was exempted: and they took away the stones of Ramah, and the timber thereof, wherewith Baasha had builded; and king Asa built with them Geba of Benjamin, and Mizpah.

    Asa fortified Mizpah in his war with Baasha king of Israel, and that pit became the grave Ishmael used.

**[Jeremiah 41:10-15](/bible/jeremiah/41#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/41#v10)</sup> Then Ishmael carried away captive all the residue of the people that were in Mizpah, even the king’s daughters, and all the people that remained in Mizpah, whom Nebuzar–adan the captain of the guard had committed to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam: and Ishmael the son of Nethaniah carried them away captive, and departed to go over to the Ammonites.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/41#v11)</sup> But when Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces that were with him, heard of all the evil that Ishmael the son of Nethaniah had done,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/41#v12)</sup> Then they took all the men, and went to fight with Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, and found him by the great waters that are in Gibeon.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/41#v13)</sup> Now it came to pass, that when all the people which were with Ishmael saw Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces that were with him, then they were glad.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/41#v14)</sup> So all the people that Ishmael had carried away captive from Mizpah cast about and returned, and went unto Johanan the son of Kareah.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/41#v15)</sup> But Ishmael the son of Nethaniah escaped from Johanan with eight men, and went to the Ammonites.

- Ishmael carried away captive all the residue of the people at Mizpah, including the king's daughters, whom Nebuzaradan had committed to Gedaliah, and departed toward the Ammonites.
- When Johanan and the captains heard of all the evil Ishmael had done, not only killing Gedaliah but at least eighty other men, they took their men and went to fight him and found him by the great waters in Gibeon.
- All the people Ishmael had taken captive were glad to see Johanan, turned about, and returned to him, but Ishmael escaped from Johanan with eight men and went to the Ammonites.

**[Jeremiah 41:16-18](/bible/jeremiah/41#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/41#v16)</sup> Then took Johanan the son of Kareah, and all the captains of the forces that were with him, all the remnant of the people whom he had recovered from Ishmael the son of Nethaniah, from Mizpah, after that he had slain Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, even mighty men of war, and the women, and the children, and the eunuchs, whom he had brought again from Gibeon:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/41#v17)</sup> And they departed, and dwelt in the habitation of Chimham, which is by Beth–lehem, to go to enter into Egypt,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/41#v18)</sup> Because of the Chaldeans: for they were afraid of them, because Ishmael the son of Nethaniah had slain Gedaliah the son of Ahikam, whom the king of Babylon made governor in the land.

- Johanan took the whole remnant he had recovered from Ishmael, the mighty men of war, the women, the children, and the eunuchs, and departed to dwell near Bethlehem on the way toward Egypt.
- They set out to seek refuge in Egypt because they were afraid of the Chaldeans, since Ishmael had slain Gedaliah whom the king of Babylon had made governor; with Gedaliah dead they felt they had no reason to stay.
