Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 44](/bible/jeremiah/44)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 44 through 47, coming to the end of the book of Jeremiah in the Babylonian captivity. The Most High tells the remnant that fled to Egypt that the very curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) will pursue and overtake them there, because they went right back to idolatry, burning incense to the queen of heaven. The women worshipped without their men and the men had no rule over their houses, the same disorder we see in Babylon the great today. Then come the words of comfort to Baruch the scribe, whose writings are preserved in the Apocrypha, and the prophecies against the gentile nations, Egypt and the Philistines, that had a hand in spoiling Israel. This is the book we are the people of, and the Most High raised up His prophets, the Israelites, rising early to warn us not to do the abominable things He hates.

**[Jeremiah 44:1-4](/bible/jeremiah/44#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/44#v1)</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews which dwell in the land of Egypt, which dwell at Migdol, and at Tahpanhes, and at Noph, and in the country of Pathros, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/44#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Ye have seen all the evil that I have brought upon Jerusalem, and upon all the cities of Judah; and, behold, this day they are a desolation, and no man dwelleth therein,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/44#v3)</sup> Because of their wickedness which they have committed to provoke me to anger, in that they went to burn incense, and to serve other gods, whom they knew not, neither they, ye, nor your fathers.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/44#v4)</sup> Howbeit I sent unto you all my servants the prophets, rising early and sending them, saying, Oh, do not this abominable thing that I hate.

- The word came to Jeremiah concerning all the Jews dwelling in the land of Egypt and in the country of Pathros; the Most High reminded them of all the evil He had brought on Jerusalem and the cities of Judah, now a desolation where no man dwelt, because of the wickedness they committed provoking Him to anger.
- They went to burn incense and serve other gods that neither they nor their fathers knew, yet the Most High sent all His servants the prophets, rising early and sending them, pleading with them not to do this abominable thing that He hates.
- The same work goes on today; we fill up the street corners all over the earth bringing forth the glorious gospel of the one true living God and Christ, the prophets He raised up to give warning.
  - **[Amos 2:11](/bible/amos/2#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/amos/2#v11)</sup> And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazarites. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the Lord.

    The prophets come out of the nation of Israel and no other, because the Most High is in the midst of Israel and none else and the Bible is given to the Israelites and none else.

**[Jeremiah 44:5-8](/bible/jeremiah/44#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/44#v5)</sup> But they hearkened not, nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness, to burn no incense unto other gods.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/44#v6)</sup> Wherefore my fury and mine anger was poured forth, and was kindled in the cities of Judah and in the streets of Jerusalem; and they are wasted and desolate, as at this day.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/44#v7)</sup> Therefore now thus saith the Lord, the God of hosts, the God of Israel; Wherefore commit ye this great evil against your souls, to cut off from you man and woman, child and suckling, out of Judah, to leave you none to remain;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/44#v8)</sup> In that ye provoke me unto wrath with the works of your hands, burning incense unto other gods in the land of Egypt, whither ye be gone to dwell, that ye might cut yourselves off, and that ye might be a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth?

- They hearkened not nor inclined their ear to turn from their wickedness and burn no incense to other gods, so the Most High's fury and anger was poured forth and kindled in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem until they were wasted and desolate.
- He asks why they commit this great evil against their own souls, cutting off man and woman, child and suckling out of Judah, leaving none to remain.
- They provoke Him to wrath with the works of their hands, burning incense to other gods in the land of Egypt where they had gone to dwell, cutting themselves off to become a curse and a reproach among all the nations of the earth.
  - **[Proverbs 8:36](/bible/proverbs/8#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/proverbs/8#v36)</sup> But he that sinneth against me wrongeth his own soul: all they that hate me love death.

    He that sins against the Most High wrongs his own soul, for the wages of sin is death, and they that hate Him love death.

**[Jeremiah 44:9-14](/bible/jeremiah/44#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/44#v9)</sup> Have ye forgotten the wickedness of your fathers, and the wickedness of the kings of Judah, and the wickedness of their wives, and your own wickedness, and the wickedness of your wives, which they have committed in the land of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/44#v10)</sup> They are not humbled even unto this day, neither have they feared, nor walked in my law, nor in my statutes, that I set before you and before your fathers.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/44#v11)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will set my face against you for evil, and to cut off all Judah.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/44#v12)</sup> And I will take the remnant of Judah, that have set their faces to go into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, and they shall all be consumed, and fall in the land of Egypt; they shall even be consumed by the sword and by the famine: they shall die, from the least even unto the greatest, by the sword and by the famine: and they shall be an execration, and an astonishment, and a curse, and a reproach.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/44#v13)</sup> For I will punish them that dwell in the land of Egypt, as I have punished Jerusalem, by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/44#v14)</sup> So that none of the remnant of Judah, which are gone into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, shall escape or remain, that they should return into the land of Judah, to the which they have a desire to return to dwell there: for none shall return but such as shall escape.

- The Most High asks whether they have forgotten the wickedness of their fathers, the kings of Judah, their wives, and their own wickedness committed in the land of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem; they were not humbled even to that day, nor did they fear or walk in His law and statutes.
- Therefore He will set His face against them for evil, to cut off all Judah, and take the remnant that set their faces to go into Egypt to sojourn there; they shall all be consumed and fall by the sword and the famine, from the least to the greatest.
- They shall become an execration, an astonishment, a curse, and a reproach; only a small number escaping the sword shall return, for none shall return to Judah though they have a desire to dwell there.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:45](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v45)</sup> Moreover all these curses shall come upon thee, and shall pursue thee, and overtake thee, till thou be destroyed; because thou hearkenedst not unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to keep his commandments and his statutes which he commanded thee:

    All these curses of the law shall come upon them, pursue them, and overtake them until they are destroyed, even those who sought refuge in Egypt, because they hearkened not to the voice of the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 44:15-19](/bible/jeremiah/44#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/44#v15)</sup> Then all the men which knew that their wives had burned incense unto other gods, and all the women that stood by, a great multitude, even all the people that dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/44#v16)</sup> As for the word that thou hast spoken unto us in the name of the Lord, we will not hearken unto thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/44#v17)</sup> But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn incense unto the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, as we have done, we, and our fathers, our kings, and our princes, in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem: for then had we plenty of victuals, and were well, and saw no evil.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/44#v18)</sup> But since we left off to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, we have wanted all things, and have been consumed by the sword and by the famine.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/44#v19)</sup> And when we burned incense to the queen of heaven, and poured out drink offerings unto her, did we make her cakes to worship her, and pour out drink offerings unto her, without our men?

- All the men who knew their wives had burned incense to other gods, and all the women, a great multitude in Egypt and Pathros, answered Jeremiah defiantly.
- These men did not rule their houses well and had no authority over their households; their wives were in the midst of idolatry, the same disorder we see in this place spiritually called Egypt, in Babylon the great today.
- They said they would not hearken to the word spoken in the name of the Most High but would perform their vows to burn incense to the queen of heaven and pour out drink offerings, claiming they had plenty of victuals and saw no evil until they left off, and then wanted all things and were consumed by the sword and famine.
- The women declared they made their cakes to worship her and poured out drink offerings without their men; the times women gather together without their men, such as baby showers with cakes and drinks and partying, are burning incense to other gods to keep in mind.

**[Jeremiah 44:20-23](/bible/jeremiah/44#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/44#v20)</sup> Then Jeremiah said unto all the people, to the men, and to the women, and to all the people which had given him that answer, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/44#v21)</sup> The incense that ye burned in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, ye, and your fathers, your kings, and your princes, and the people of the land, did not the Lord remember them, and came it not into his mind?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/44#v22)</sup> So that the Lord could no longer bear, because of the evil of your doings, and because of the abominations which ye have committed; therefore is your land a desolation, and an astonishment, and a curse, without an inhabitant, as at this day.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/44#v23)</sup> Because ye have burned incense, and because ye have sinned against the Lord, and have not obeyed the voice of the Lord, nor walked in his law, nor in his statutes, nor in his testimonies; therefore this evil is happened unto you, as at this day.

- Jeremiah answered all the people, men and women, that the incense they burned in the cities of Judah and the streets of Jerusalem, they and their fathers, kings, and princes, the Most High remembered and it came into His mind.
- The Most High could no longer bear the evil of their doings and the abominations they committed, therefore their land became a desolation, an astonishment, and a curse without an inhabitant as at that day.
- This evil happened to them because they burned incense and sinned against the Most High and did not obey His voice nor walk in His law, statutes, and testimonies.

**[Jeremiah 44:24-28](/bible/jeremiah/44#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/44#v24)</sup> Moreover Jeremiah said unto all the people, and to all the women, Hear the word of the Lord, all Judah that are in the land of Egypt:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/44#v25)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel, saying; Ye and your wives have both spoken with your mouths, and fulfilled with your hand, saying, We will surely perform our vows that we have vowed, to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her: ye will surely accomplish your vows, and surely perform your vows.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/44#v26)</sup> Therefore hear ye the word of the Lord, all Judah that dwell in the land of Egypt; Behold, I have sworn by my great name, saith the Lord, that my name shall no more be named in the mouth of any man of Judah in all the land of Egypt, saying, The Lord God liveth.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/44#v27)</sup> Behold, I will watch over them for evil, and not for good: and all the men of Judah that are in the land of Egypt shall be consumed by the sword and by the famine, until there be an end of them.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/44#v28)</sup> Yet a small number that escape the sword shall return out of the land of Egypt into the land of Judah, and all the remnant of Judah, that are gone into the land of Egypt to sojourn there, shall know whose words shall stand, mine, or theirs.

- Jeremiah told all the people and all the women that they and their wives had both spoken with their mouths and fulfilled with their hands their vows to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and would surely accomplish them.
- Therefore the Most High swore by His great name that His name shall no more be named in the mouth of any man of Judah in all Egypt, saying "The Lord GOD liveth"; He will watch over them for evil and not for good until they are consumed by the sword and famine.
- Only a small number escaping the sword shall return out of Egypt into Judah, and all the remnant that went to Egypt shall know whose words shall stand, His or theirs.

**[Jeremiah 44:29-30](/bible/jeremiah/44#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/44#v29)</sup> And this shall be a sign unto you, saith the Lord, that I will punish you in this place, that ye may know that my words shall surely stand against you for evil:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/44#v30)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will give Pharaoh–hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies, and into the hand of them that seek his life; as I gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, his enemy, and that sought his life.

- The Most High gives a sign that He will punish them in that place so they may know His words shall surely stand against them for evil; He will give Pharaoh-hophra king of Egypt into the hand of his enemies as He gave Zedekiah king of Judah into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar.
- Evil does not come merely by chance; the Most High brings that evil upon us when we turn from Him and follow our own mind, sinning against the one true living God in Christ.
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:39](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v39)**
    > <sup>[39](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v39)</sup> See now that I, even I, am he, and there is no god with me: I kill, and I make alive; I wound, and I heal: neither is there any that can deliver out of my hand.

    The Most High declares there is no god with Him; He kills and makes alive, wounds and heals, and none can deliver out of His hand, so when He swears a counsel by His own name it stands.
  - **[Amos 3:6](/bible/amos/3#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/amos/3#v6)</sup> Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the Lord hath not done it?

    If a trumpet is blown in the city the people are afraid, and there is no evil in the city that the Most High has not done, so we cannot attribute everything bad to the devil.
