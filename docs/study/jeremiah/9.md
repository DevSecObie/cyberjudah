**[Jeremiah 9:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/9#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/9#v1)</sup> Oh that my head were waters, and mine eyes a fountain of tears, that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/9#v2)</sup> Oh that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfaring men; that I might leave my people, and go from them! for they be all adulterers, an assembly of treacherous men.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/9#v3)</sup> And they bend their tongues like their bow for lies: but they are not valiant for the truth upon the earth; for they proceed from evil to evil, and they know not me, saith the Lord.

- The prophet wishes his head were waters and his eyes a fountain of tears to weep for the slain of his people. He wants a lodging place in the wilderness to leave them, because they are all adulterers and treacherous men who bend their tongues like a bow for lies and proceed from evil to evil.

**[Jeremiah 9:4-6](/bible/jeremiah/9#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/9#v4)</sup> Take ye heed every one of his neighbour, and trust ye not in any brother: for every brother will utterly supplant, and every neighbour will walk with slanders.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/9#v5)</sup> And they will deceive every one his neighbour, and will not speak the truth: they have taught their tongue to speak lies, and weary themselves to commit iniquity.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/9#v6)</sup> Thine habitation is in the midst of deceit; through deceit they refuse to know me, saith the Lord.

- Trust not in any brother, for every brother will supplant and every neighbor walks with slanders. We have seen this in IUIC and in many of the other camps.
- They have taught their tongue to speak lies and weary themselves to commit iniquity; through deceit they refuse to know the Most High.
  - **[John 8:19](/bible/john/8#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/john/8#v19)</sup> Then said they unto him, Where is thy Father? Jesus answered, Ye neither know me, nor my Father: if ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also.

    To refuse to know Him through deceit is to have no relationship with Him.

**[Jeremiah 9:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/9#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/9#v7)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, Behold, I will melt them, and try them; for how shall I do for the daughter of my people?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/9#v8)</sup> Their tongue is as an arrow shot out; it speaketh deceit: one speaketh peaceably to his neighbour with his mouth, but in heart he layeth his wait.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/9#v9)</sup> Shall I not visit them for these things? saith the Lord: shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?

- The Most High will melt and try them, because their tongue is an arrow shooting deceit. A man speaks peaceably to his neighbor with his mouth but in his heart lies in wait, thinking of a plot or scheme to destroy, steal from, or overcome him. That is how wicked our people are.

**[Jeremiah 9:10-16](/bible/jeremiah/9#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/9#v10)</sup> For the mountains will I take up a weeping and wailing, and for the habitations of the wilderness a lamentation, because they are burned up, so that none can pass through them; neither can men hear the voice of the cattle; both the fowl of the heavens and the beast are fled; they are gone.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/9#v11)</sup> And I will make Jerusalem heaps, and a den of dragons; and I will make the cities of Judah desolate, without an inhabitant.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/9#v12)</sup> Who is the wise man, that may understand this? and who is he to whom the mouth of the Lord hath spoken, that he may declare it, for what the land perisheth and is burned up like a wilderness, that none passeth through?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/9#v13)</sup> And the Lord saith, Because they have forsaken my law which I set before them, and have not obeyed my voice, neither walked therein;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/9#v14)</sup> But have walked after the imagination of their own heart, and after Baalim, which their fathers taught them:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/9#v15)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will feed them, even this people, with wormwood, and give them water of gall to drink.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/9#v16)</sup> I will scatter them also among the heathen, whom neither they nor their fathers have known: and I will send a sword after them, till I have consumed them.

- Because they forsook His law, did not obey His voice, and walked after the imagination of their own heart and after Baal, He will feed them with wormwood, give them water of gall, scatter them among the heathen, and send a sword after them until they are consumed.

**[Jeremiah 9:17-21](/bible/jeremiah/9#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/9#v17)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, Consider ye, and call for the mourning women, that they may come; and send for cunning women, that they may come:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/9#v18)</sup> And let them make haste, and take up a wailing for us, that our eyes may run down with tears, and our eyelids gush out with waters.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/9#v19)</sup> For a voice of wailing is heard out of Zion, How are we spoiled! we are greatly confounded, because we have forsaken the land, because our dwellings have cast us out.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/9#v20)</sup> Yet hear the word of the Lord, O ye women, and let your ear receive the word of his mouth, and teach your daughters wailing, and every one her neighbour lamentation.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/9#v21)</sup> For death is come up into our windows, and is entered into our palaces, to cut off the children from without, and the young men from the streets.

- Call for the mourning women to take up a wailing, for death has come up into the windows and into the palaces to cut off the children from the streets and the young men from the squares. This sounds like America, like the south side of Chicago, north Dallas, and southwest Houston.

**[Jeremiah 9:22-24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/9#v22)</sup> Speak, Thus saith the Lord, Even the carcases of men shall fall as dung upon the open field, and as the handful after the harvestman, and none shall gather them.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/9#v23)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, neither let the mighty man glory in his might, let not the rich man glory in his riches:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v24)</sup> But let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me, that I am the Lord which exercise lovingkindness, judgment, and righteousness, in the earth: for in these things I delight, saith the Lord.

- Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, the mighty in his might, or the rich in his riches. Carnal things like looks, strength, and money all fade.
- Glory instead that you understand and know the Most High: that you have been awakened in these last days, shown the truth, the scriptures, the commandments, and what God requires to be saved from the destruction prophesied to come.
- You can know you are an Israelite, know the twelve feathers, the seven heads and ten horns, the mysteries of Genesis, and every precept, but if you have hatred for your brother or sister, if you are a slanderer, gossiper, liar, or fornicator, you will not see the kingdom, only fire. There is a difference between a prophet and a parakeet; anyone can mimic scriptures for a show while being unprofitable to himself.

**[Jeremiah 9:25-26](/bible/jeremiah/9#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/9#v25)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will punish all them which are circumcised with the uncircumcised;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/9#v26)</sup> Egypt, and Judah, and Edom, and the children of Ammon, and Moab, and all that are in the utmost corners, that dwell in the wilderness: for all these nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel are uncircumcised in the heart.

- The days come when He will punish the circumcised along with the uncircumcised, because all the house of Israel is uncircumcised in the heart. It does not matter if you are circumcised after the manner of Abraham; if you fornicate or hold hatred, your circumcision is made uncircumcision through your sin. Circumcision only means something when you are obedient to the words and statutes of the Lord.
