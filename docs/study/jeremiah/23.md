**[Jeremiah 23:1-4](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)</sup> Woe be unto the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture! saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/23#v2)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God of Israel against the pastors that feed my people; Ye have scattered my flock, and driven them away, and have not visited them: behold, I will visit upon you the evil of your doings, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/23#v3)</sup> And I will gather the remnant of my flock out of all countries whither I have driven them, and will bring them again to their folds; and they shall be fruitful and increase.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/23#v4)</sup> And I will set up shepherds over them which shall feed them: and they shall fear no more, nor be dismayed, neither shall they be lacking, saith the Lord.

- Woe is pronounced on the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of the Most High's pasture and have not visited them, so He will visit upon them the evil of their doings.
- He will gather the remnant of His flock out of the countries where He drove them, bring them again to their folds, and set up shepherds who will feed them so they fear no more and none are lacking.

**[Jeremiah 23:5-8](/bible/jeremiah/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/23#v5)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will raise unto David a righteous Branch, and a King shall reign and prosper, and shall execute judgment and justice in the earth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/23#v6)</sup> In his days Judah shall be saved, and Israel shall dwell safely: and this is his name whereby he shall be called, THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/23#v7)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that they shall no more say, The Lord liveth, which brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/23#v8)</sup> But, The Lord liveth, which brought up and which led the seed of the house of Israel out of the north country, and from all countries whither I had driven them; and they shall dwell in their own land.

- The Most High promises to raise unto David a righteous Branch, a King who shall reign, prosper, and execute judgment and justice, in whose days Judah shall be saved and Israel dwell safely.
- His name is called THE LORD OUR RIGHTEOUSNESS; this is plainly referring to Christ.
- The day comes when they will no longer swear by the Most High who brought Israel out of Egypt, but by Him who led the seed of the house of Israel out of the north country and all the countries where He had driven them, back to their own land.

**[Jeremiah 23:9-15](/bible/jeremiah/23#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/23#v9)</sup> Mine heart within me is broken because of the prophets; all my bones shake; I am like a drunken man, and like a man whom wine hath overcome, because of the Lord, and because of the words of his holiness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/23#v10)</sup> For the land is full of adulterers; for because of swearing the land mourneth; the pleasant places of the wilderness are dried up, and their course is evil, and their force is not right.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/23#v11)</sup> For both prophet and priest are profane; yea, in my house have I found their wickedness, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/23#v12)</sup> Wherefore their way shall be unto them as slippery ways in the darkness: they shall be driven on, and fall therein: for I will bring evil upon them, even the year of their visitation, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/23#v13)</sup> And I have seen folly in the prophets of Samaria; they prophesied in Baal, and caused my people Israel to err.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/23#v14)</sup> I have seen also in the prophets of Jerusalem an horrible thing: they commit adultery, and walk in lies: they strengthen also the hands of evildoers, that none doth return from his wickedness: they are all of them unto me as Sodom, and the inhabitants thereof as Gomorrah.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/23#v15)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts concerning the prophets; Behold, I will feed them with wormwood, and make them drink the water of gall: for from the prophets of Jerusalem is profaneness gone forth into all the land.

- Jeremiah's heart is broken and his bones shake like a drunken man because of the prophets and the words of the Most High's holiness, for the land is full of adulterers and mourns under swearing.
- Both prophet and priest are profane, and the Most High has found their wickedness even in His house, so their way shall be as slippery ways in the darkness where they are driven and fall.
- The prophets of Samaria prophesied in Baal and caused Israel to err, and the prophets of Jerusalem commit adultery, walk in lies, and strengthen the hands of evildoers so that none return from wickedness, becoming as Sodom and Gomorrah before Him.

**[Jeremiah 23:16-22](/bible/jeremiah/23#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/23#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, Hearken not unto the words of the prophets that prophesy unto you: they make you vain: they speak a vision of their own heart, and not out of the mouth of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/23#v17)</sup> They say still unto them that despise me, The Lord hath said, Ye shall have peace; and they say unto every one that walketh after the imagination of his own heart, No evil shall come upon you.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/23#v18)</sup> For who hath stood in the counsel of the Lord, and hath perceived and heard his word? who hath marked his word, and heard it?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/23#v19)</sup> Behold, a whirlwind of the Lord is gone forth in fury, even a grievous whirlwind: it shall fall grievously upon the head of the wicked.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/23#v20)</sup> The anger of the Lord shall not return, until he have executed, and till he have performed the thoughts of his heart: in the latter days ye shall consider it perfectly.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/23#v21)</sup> I have not sent these prophets, yet they ran: I have not spoken to them, yet they prophesied.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/23#v22)</sup> But if they had stood in my counsel, and had caused my people to hear my words, then they should have turned them from their evil way, and from the evil of their doings.

- The people are told not to hearken to the prophets who make them vain and speak a vision of their own heart, not out of the mouth of the Most High, telling those who despise Him that they shall have peace and no evil shall come.
- This is like the pastor who says, let me tell you what God told me to tell you, when it is a lie.
- None of these prophets has stood in the counsel of the Most High; had they stood there and caused the people to hear His words, they would have turned them from their evil way, but instead the whirlwind of His anger will fall grievously upon the head of the wicked in the latter days.

**[Jeremiah 23:23-32](/bible/jeremiah/23#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/23#v23)</sup> Am I a God at hand, saith the Lord, and not a God afar off?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/23#v24)</sup> Can any hide himself in secret places that I shall not see him? saith the Lord. Do not I fill heaven and earth? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/23#v25)</sup> I have heard what the prophets said, that prophesy lies in my name, saying, I have dreamed, I have dreamed.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/23#v26)</sup> How long shall this be in the heart of the prophets that prophesy lies? yea, they are prophets of the deceit of their own heart;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/23#v27)</sup> Which think to cause my people to forget my name by their dreams which they tell every man to his neighbour, as their fathers have forgotten my name for Baal.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/23#v28)</sup> The prophet that hath a dream, let him tell a dream; and he that hath my word, let him speak my word faithfully. What is the chaff to the wheat? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/23#v29)</sup> Is not my word like as a fire? saith the Lord; and like a hammer that breaketh the rock in pieces?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/23#v30)</sup> Therefore, behold, I am against the prophets, saith the Lord, that steal my words every one from his neighbour.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/23#v31)</sup> Behold, I am against the prophets, saith the Lord, that use their tongues, and say, He saith.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/23#v32)</sup> Behold, I am against them that prophesy false dreams, saith the Lord, and do tell them, and cause my people to err by their lies, and by their lightness; yet I sent them not, nor commanded them: therefore they shall not profit this people at all, saith the Lord.

- The Most High asks whether He is a God only near and not far off, when He fills heaven and earth and no one can hide in secret places where He cannot see him.
- He has heard the prophets who prophesy lies in His name saying, I have dreamed, thinking to cause His people to forget His name by their dreams as their fathers forgot it for Baal.
- Let the prophet with a dream tell a dream, but let him that has His word speak it faithfully, for His word is like a fire and like a hammer that breaks the rock in pieces; He is against those who steal His words and use their own tongues to lie on the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 23:33-40](/bible/jeremiah/23#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/23#v33)</sup> And when this people, or the prophet, or a priest, shall ask thee, saying, What is the burden of the Lord? thou shalt then say unto them, What burden? I will even forsake you, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/23#v34)</sup> And as for the prophet, and the priest, and the people, that shall say, The burden of the Lord, I will even punish that man and his house.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/23#v35)</sup> Thus shall ye say every one to his neighbour, and every one to his brother, What hath the Lord answered? and, What hath the Lord spoken?
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/23#v36)</sup> And the burden of the Lord shall ye mention no more: for every man’s word shall be his burden; for ye have perverted the words of the living God, of the Lord of hosts our God.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/jeremiah/23#v37)</sup> Thus shalt thou say to the prophet, What hath the Lord answered thee? and, What hath the Lord spoken?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/jeremiah/23#v38)</sup> But since ye say, The burden of the Lord; therefore thus saith the Lord; Because ye say this word, The burden of the Lord, and I have sent unto you, saying, Ye shall not say, The burden of the Lord;
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/jeremiah/23#v39)</sup> Therefore, behold, I, even I, will utterly forget you, and I will forsake you, and the city that I gave you and your fathers, and cast you out of my presence:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/jeremiah/23#v40)</sup> And I will bring an everlasting reproach upon you, and a perpetual shame, which shall not be forgotten.

- When the people, prophet, or priest ask what is the burden of the Most High, Jeremiah is to answer, What burden? for He will forsake them, and every man who claims the burden of the Most High He will punish in his house.
- Instead of saying the burden of the Most High, they are to ask one another what the Most High has answered and spoken, for they have perverted the words of the living God.
- Because they keep saying the burden of the Most High after being told not to, He will utterly forget them, forsake them, and cast them out of His presence with an everlasting reproach and perpetual shame that shall not be forgotten.
