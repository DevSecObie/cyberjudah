**[Jeremiah 31:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/31#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/31#v1)</sup> At the same time, saith the Lord, will I be the God of all the families of Israel, and they shall be my people.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/31#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, The people which were left of the sword found grace in the wilderness; even Israel, when I went to cause him to rest.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/31#v3)</sup> The Lord hath appeared of old unto me, saying, Yea, I have loved thee with an everlasting love: therefore with lovingkindness have I drawn thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/31#v4)</sup> Again I will build thee, and thou shalt be built, O virgin of Israel: thou shalt again be adorned with thy tabrets, and shalt go forth in the dances of them that make merry.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/31#v5)</sup> Thou shalt yet plant vines upon the mountains of Samaria: the planters shall plant, and shall eat them as common things.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/31#v6)</sup> For there shall be a day, that the watchmen upon the mount Ephraim shall cry, Arise ye, and let us go up to Zion unto the Lord our God.

- At the same time the Most High will be the God of all the families of Israel and they shall be His people; the people left of the sword found grace in the wilderness when He went to give Israel rest.
- He appeared of old saying He loved them with an everlasting love and with lovingkindness drew them; again He will build the virgin of Israel, adorned with her tabrets and going forth in the dances, planting vines on the mountains of Samaria until the watchmen on Mount Ephraim cry, Arise, let us go up to Zion unto the Lord our God.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)</sup> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.

    The Most High likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman, and that comely delicate woman is Israel.

**[Jeremiah 31:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/31#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/31#v7)</sup> For thus saith the Lord; Sing with gladness for Jacob, and shout among the chief of the nations: publish ye, praise ye, and say, O Lord, save thy people, the remnant of Israel.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/31#v8)</sup> Behold, I will bring them from the north country, and gather them from the coasts of the earth, and with them the blind and the lame, the woman with child and her that travaileth with child together: a great company shall return thither.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/31#v9)</sup> They shall come with weeping, and with supplications will I lead them: I will cause them to walk by the rivers of waters in a straight way, wherein they shall not stumble: for I am a father to Israel, and Ephraim is my firstborn.

- Sing with gladness for Jacob and shout among the chief of the nations, saying, O Lord, save thy people the remnant of Israel; He will bring them from the north country and gather them from the coasts of the earth, the blind and the lame, the woman with child, a great company returning.
- They shall come with weeping and supplications, and He will lead them and cause them to walk by the rivers of waters in a straight way wherein they shall not stumble, for He is a father to Israel and Ephraim is His firstborn; the straight way is keeping God's laws.
- Ephraim being the firstborn means Israel is the firstborn of the Most High, set higher than the kings of the earth, who are the other nations, so all of Israel shall be over all the nations; the reason Ephraim was singled out is the tribes were not the first tribe that came, but the promise is to all Israel.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.

    Stand in the ways and ask for the old paths, the good way, and walk therein to find rest for your souls, though they said we will not; the old paths are keeping God's laws.
  - **[2 Esdras 7:7-8](/bible/2-esdras/7#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-esdras/7#v7)</sup> The entrance thereof is narrow, and is set in a dangerous place to fall, like as if there were a fire on the right hand, and on the left a deep water:
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-esdras/7#v8)</sup> And one only path between them both, even between the fire and the water, so small that there could but one man go there at once.

    The entrance is narrow, set in a dangerous place with fire on the right hand and deep water on the left, one only path between them for one man to walk.
  - **[Matthew 7:13-14](/bible/matthew/7#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/7#v13)</sup> Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat:
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/7#v14)</sup> Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

    Wide is the gate and broad the way that leadeth to destruction which many find, but strait is the gate and narrow the way which leadeth unto life, and few find it.
  - **[Psalms 89:27](/bible/psalms/89#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/89#v27)</sup> Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.

    Also He will make him His firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth, that is, higher than the other nations.
  - **[Isaiah 1:3](/bible/isaiah/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/1#v3)</sup> The ox knoweth his owner, and the ass his master’s crib: but Israel doth not know, my people doth not consider.

    The ox knoweth his owner and the ass his master's crib, but Israel does not know; most of our people do not consider that they are Israel.

**[Jeremiah 31:10-14](/bible/jeremiah/31#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/31#v10)</sup> Hear the word of the Lord, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off, and say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/31#v11)</sup> For the Lord hath redeemed Jacob, and ransomed him from the hand of him that was stronger than he.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/31#v12)</sup> Therefore they shall come and sing in the height of Zion, and shall flow together to the goodness of the Lord, for wheat, and for wine, and for oil, and for the young of the flock and of the herd: and their soul shall be as a watered garden; and they shall not sorrow any more at all.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/31#v13)</sup> Then shall the virgin rejoice in the dance, both young men and old together: for I will turn their mourning into joy, and will comfort them, and make them rejoice from their sorrow.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/31#v14)</sup> And I will satiate the soul of the priests with fatness, and my people shall be satisfied with my goodness, saith the Lord.

- Hear the word of the Lord, O ye nations, and declare it in the isles afar off: He that scattered Israel will gather him and keep him as a shepherd doth his flock, for the Lord has redeemed Jacob and ransomed him from the hand of him that was stronger than he.
- They shall come and sing in the height of Zion and flow together to the goodness of the Lord, for the wheat, the wine, the oil, and the young of the flock and herd, and their soul shall be as a watered garden and sorrow no more.
- Both young men and old shall rejoice in the dance, for He will turn their mourning into joy, comfort them, and satiate the soul of the priest with fatness, and His people shall be satisfied with His goodness.
  - **[Isaiah 43:1](/bible/isaiah/43#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/43#v1)</sup> But now thus saith the Lord that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel, Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.

    Thus saith the Lord that created thee O Jacob and formed thee O Israel, fear not, for I have redeemed thee and called thee by thy name, thou art mine.

**[Jeremiah 31:15-20](/bible/jeremiah/31#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/31#v15)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping; Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/31#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Refrain thy voice from weeping, and thine eyes from tears: for thy work shall be rewarded, saith the Lord; and they shall come again from the land of the enemy.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/31#v17)</sup> And there is hope in thine end, saith the Lord, that thy children shall come again to their own border.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/31#v18)</sup> I have surely heard Ephraim bemoaning himself thus; Thou hast chastised me, and I was chastised, as a bullock unaccustomed to the yoke: turn thou me, and I shall be turned; for thou art the Lord my God.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/31#v19)</sup> Surely after that I was turned, I repented; and after that I was instructed, I smote upon my thigh: I was ashamed, yea, even confounded, because I did bear the reproach of my youth.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/31#v20)</sup> Is Ephraim my dear son? is he a pleasant child? for since I spake against him, I do earnestly remember him still: therefore my bowels are troubled for him; I will surely have mercy upon him, saith the Lord.

- A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation and bitter weeping, Rachel weeping for her children and refusing to be comforted because they were not; but the Most High tells her to refrain her voice from weeping, for her work shall be rewarded and her children shall come again from the land of the enemy to their own border.
- He heard Ephraim bemoaning himself, saying thou hast chastised me and I was chastised as a bullock unaccustomed to the yoke; turn thou me and I shall be turned, for after he was turned he repented and was ashamed, bearing the reproach of his youth.
- Is Ephraim His dear son, a pleasant child? Since He spake against him He earnestly remembers him still, His bowels are troubled for him, and He will surely have mercy; Jeremiah speaks these words of the Lord to His people.

**[Jeremiah 31:21-26](/bible/jeremiah/31#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/31#v21)</sup> Set thee up waymarks, make thee high heaps: set thine heart toward the highway, even the way which thou wentest: turn again, O virgin of Israel, turn again to these thy cities.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/31#v22)</sup> How long wilt thou go about, O thou backsliding daughter? for the Lord hath created a new thing in the earth, A woman shall compass a man.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/31#v23)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; As yet they shall use this speech in the land of Judah and in the cities thereof, when I shall bring again their captivity; The Lord bless thee, O habitation of justice, and mountain of holiness.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/31#v24)</sup> And there shall dwell in Judah itself, and in all the cities thereof together, husbandmen, and they that go forth with flocks.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/31#v25)</sup> For I have satiated the weary soul, and I have replenished every sorrowful soul.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/31#v26)</sup> Upon this I awaked, and beheld; and my sleep was sweet unto me.

- Set up waymarks and high heaps, set thine heart toward the highway, and turn again O virgin of Israel to these thy cities; how long wilt thou go about, O backsliding daughter?
- The Lord has created a new thing in the earth, a woman shall compass a man; it is a new thing that women would be out front of the man and the man pretty much serving the woman, and we can see the fruition of that today.
- When He brings again the captivity, the Lord bless thee O habitation of justice and mountain of holiness; there shall dwell together in Judah the husbandmen, who are the prophets bringing the word of the Lord, and they that go forth with flocks, for He has satiated the weary soul and replenished the sorrowful soul.

**[Jeremiah 31:27-30](/bible/jeremiah/31#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/31#v27)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of man, and with the seed of beast.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/31#v28)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that like as I have watched over them, to pluck up, and to break down, and to throw down, and to destroy, and to afflict; so will I watch over them, to build, and to plant, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/31#v29)</sup> In those days they shall say no more, The fathers have eaten a sour grape, and the children’s teeth are set on edge.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/31#v30)</sup> But every one shall die for his own iniquity: every man that eateth the sour grape, his teeth shall be set on edge.

- The days come that the Most High will sow the house of Israel and the house of Judah with the seed of man and the seed of beast, so they shall have plenty.
- As He watched over them to pluck up, break down, and destroy, so will He watch over them to build and to plant.
- In those days they shall say no more that the fathers have eaten a sour grape and the children's teeth are set on edge, but every man shall die for his own iniquity; you die for your own sin, not for the sin of the next man, for that dreaded judgment comes behind iniquity.

**[Jeremiah 31:31-34](/bible/jeremiah/31#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/31#v31)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel, and with the house of Judah:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/31#v32)</sup> Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day that I took them by the hand to bring them out of the land of Egypt; which my covenant they brake, although I was an husband unto them, saith the Lord:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/31#v33)</sup> But this shall be the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel; After those days, saith the Lord, I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/31#v34)</sup> And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for they shall all know me, from the least of them unto the greatest of them, saith the Lord: for I will forgive their iniquity, and I will remember their sin no more.

- The Most High will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and the house of Judah, the northern and southern kingdom, not other nations, not according to the covenant He made with their fathers when He took them out of Egypt, which they brake although He was a husband unto them.
- Just as a husband and wife have a covenant for their marriage, the Most High uses that analogy for the covenant we have with Him.
- He will put His law in their inward parts and write it in their hearts, and be their God and they His people; they shall teach no more every man his neighbor, for they shall all know Him and keep His laws from the least to the greatest, and He will forgive their iniquity and remember their sin no more.

**[Jeremiah 31:35-37](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The Lord of hosts is his name:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v36)</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the Lord, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/jeremiah/31#v37)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; If heaven above can be measured, and the foundations of the earth searched out beneath, I will also cast off all the seed of Israel for all that they have done, saith the Lord.

- The Lord gives the sun for a light by day and the ordinances of the moon and the stars for a light by night, and divides the sea when the waves roar; the Lord of hosts is His name.
- For the Most High to cast away His people, those ordinances would have to depart, the sun would have to stop shining, the moon and stars stop giving light; that is not going to happen, so He is not casting away the seed of Israel.
- Only a small percentage of the earth has been discovered and the heavens above cannot be measured, so He will not cast off all the seed of Israel for all that they have done; you can even see where the seas are divided from the coast, the Pacific and the Atlantic, one seed of water divided from another.

> <sup>[38](/bible/jeremiah/31#v38)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that the city shall be built to the Lord from the tower of Hananeel unto the gate of the corner.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/jeremiah/31#v39)</sup> And the measuring line shall yet go forth over against it upon the hill Gareb, and shall compass about to Goath.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/jeremiah/31#v40)</sup> And the whole valley of the dead bodies, and of the ashes, and all the fields unto the brook of Kidron, unto the corner of the horse gate toward the east, shall be holy unto the Lord; it shall not be plucked up, nor thrown down any more for ever.

- The days come that the city shall be built to the Lord from the tower of Hananeel unto the gate of the corner, and the measuring line shall go forth over the hill Gareb and compass about to Goath.
- The whole valley of the dead bodies and of the ashes, and all the fields unto the brook of Kidron, unto the corner of the horse gate toward the east, shall be holy unto the Lord and shall not be plucked up nor thrown down anymore forever; this goes into the Kingdom.
