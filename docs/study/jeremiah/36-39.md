Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 36](/bible/jeremiah/36)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 36 through 39. The Most High commands Jeremiah to write all His words in a roll of a book; Baruch reads it in the temple, the princes hear it, and King Jehoiakim cuts it with a penknife and burns it leaf by leaf, so the Most High commands it rewritten with a curse added against the king. The reading then follows Zedekiah's reign, Jeremiah's arrest and imprisonment, his sinking in the miry dungeon and rescue by Abedmelech the Ethiopian, and finally the fall of Jerusalem, the blinding of Zedekiah, and the Most High's promise to spare Abedmelech. These four chapters read like a moving narrative, and the teacher pressed the class to envision the whole scene as it unfolds.

**[Jeremiah 36:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/36#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in the fourth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, that this word came unto Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/36#v2)</sup> Take thee a roll of a book, and write therein all the words that I have spoken unto thee against Israel, and against Judah, and against all the nations, from the day I spake unto thee, from the days of Josiah, even unto this day.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/36#v3)</sup> It may be that the house of Judah will hear all the evil which I purpose to do unto them; that they may return every man from his evil way; that I may forgive their iniquity and their sin.

- In the fourth year of Jehoiakim, the Most High tells Jeremiah to take a roll of a book and write in it all the words He has spoken against Israel, against Judah, and against all the nations, from the days of Josiah until that day.
- The purpose is that the house of Judah, hearing all the evil purposed against them, might return every man from his evil way so that He would forgive their iniquity and their sin.

**[Jeremiah 36:4-8](/bible/jeremiah/36#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/36#v4)</sup> Then Jeremiah called Baruch the son of Neriah: and Baruch wrote from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the Lord, which he had spoken unto him, upon a roll of a book.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/36#v5)</sup> And Jeremiah commanded Baruch, saying, I am shut up; I cannot go into the house of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/36#v6)</sup> Therefore go thou, and read in the roll, which thou hast written from my mouth, the words of the Lord in the ears of the people in the Lord’s house upon the fasting day: and also thou shalt read them in the ears of all Judah that come out of their cities.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/36#v7)</sup> It may be they will present their supplication before the Lord, and will return every one from his evil way: for great is the anger and the fury that the Lord hath pronounced against this people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/36#v8)</sup> And Baruch the son of Neriah did according to all that Jeremiah the prophet commanded him, reading in the book the words of the Lord in the Lord’s house.

- Jeremiah calls Baruch the son of Neriah, who writes from Jeremiah's mouth all the words of the Most High upon the roll.
- Because Jeremiah is shut up and cannot go into the house of the Most High, he sends Baruch to read the roll in the ears of the people on the fasting day, that they might present their supplication and turn from their evil way, for great is the anger and fury pronounced against this people.
- Baruch does all that the prophet commanded, reading the book of the words of the Most High in the Most High's house.

**[Jeremiah 36:9-13](/bible/jeremiah/36#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/36#v9)</sup> And it came to pass in the fifth year of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, in the ninth month, that they proclaimed a fast before the Lord to all the people in Jerusalem, and to all the people that came from the cities of Judah unto Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/36#v10)</sup> Then read Baruch in the book the words of Jeremiah in the house of the Lord, in the chamber of Gemariah the son of Shaphan the scribe, in the higher court, at the entry of the new gate of the Lord’s house, in the ears of all the people.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/36#v11)</sup> When Michaiah the son of Gemariah, the son of Shaphan, had heard out of the book all the words of the Lord,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/36#v12)</sup> Then he went down into the king’s house, into the scribe’s chamber: and, lo, all the princes sat there, even Elishama the scribe, and Delaiah the son of Shemaiah, and Elnathan the son of Achbor, and Gemariah the son of Shaphan, and Zedekiah the son of Hananiah, and all the princes.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/36#v13)</sup> Then Michaiah declared unto them all the words that he had heard, when Baruch read the book in the ears of the people.

- In the fifth year of Jehoiakim, in the ninth month, a fast is proclaimed before the Most High to all the people in Jerusalem and those who came from the cities of Judah.
- Baruch reads the words of Jeremiah in the temple in the chamber of Gemariah, in the ears of all the people.
- Michaiah the son of Gemariah, having heard the words, goes down into the king's house to the scribe's chamber where all the princes are sitting, and declares to them all he had heard when Baruch read.

**[Jeremiah 36:14-19](/bible/jeremiah/36#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/36#v14)</sup> Therefore all the princes sent Jehudi the son of Nethaniah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Cushi, unto Baruch, saying, Take in thine hand the roll wherein thou hast read in the ears of the people, and come. So Baruch the son of Neriah took the roll in his hand, and came unto them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/36#v15)</sup> And they said unto him, Sit down now, and read it in our ears. So Baruch read it in their ears.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/36#v16)</sup> Now it came to pass, when they had heard all the words, they were afraid both one and other, and said unto Baruch, We will surely tell the king of all these words.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/36#v17)</sup> And they asked Baruch, saying, Tell us now, How didst thou write all these words at his mouth?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/36#v18)</sup> Then Baruch answered them, He pronounced all these words unto me with his mouth, and I wrote them with ink in the book.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/36#v19)</sup> Then said the princes unto Baruch, Go, hide thee, thou and Jeremiah; and let no man know where ye be.

- The princes send Jehudi to bring Baruch with the roll, and they ask him to sit and read it in their ears.
- When they hear all the words they are afraid, both one and another, and say they will surely tell the king; they ask Baruch how he wrote all these words, and he answers that Jeremiah pronounced them with his mouth while Baruch wrote them with ink in the book.
- The princes tell Baruch to go hide himself and Jeremiah, and to let no man know where they are.

**[Jeremiah 36:20-26](/bible/jeremiah/36#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/36#v20)</sup> And they went in to the king into the court, but they laid up the roll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe, and told all the words in the ears of the king.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/36#v21)</sup> So the king sent Jehudi to fetch the roll: and he took it out of Elishama the scribe’s chamber. And Jehudi read it in the ears of the king, and in the ears of all the princes which stood beside the king.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/36#v22)</sup> Now the king sat in the winterhouse in the ninth month: and there was a fire on the hearth burning before him.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/36#v23)</sup> And it came to pass, that when Jehudi had read three or four leaves, he cut it with the penknife, and cast it into the fire that was on the hearth, until all the roll was consumed in the fire that was on the hearth.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/36#v24)</sup> Yet they were not afraid, nor rent their garments, neither the king, nor any of his servants that heard all these words.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/36#v25)</sup> Nevertheless Elnathan and Delaiah and Gemariah had made intercession to the king that he would not burn the roll: but he would not hear them.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/36#v26)</sup> But the king commanded Jerahmeel the son of Hammelech, and Seraiah the son of Azriel, and Shelemiah the son of Abdeel, to take Baruch the scribe and Jeremiah the prophet: but the Lord hid them.

- The princes lay up the roll in the chamber of Elishama the scribe and tell all the words in the ears of the king, who sends Jehudi to fetch it and read it before him and the princes.
- The king sits in the winter house in the ninth month with a fire burning on the hearth; as Jehudi reads three or four leaves, the king cuts them with a penknife and casts them into the fire until the whole roll is consumed.
- Neither the king nor his servants are afraid nor rend their garments; though Elnathan, Delaiah, and Gemariah make intercession that he not burn the roll, he will not hear, and he commands that Baruch and Jeremiah be seized, but the Most High hides them.

**[Jeremiah 36:27-32](/bible/jeremiah/36#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/36#v27)</sup> Then the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah, after that the king had burned the roll, and the words which Baruch wrote at the mouth of Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/36#v28)</sup> Take thee again another roll, and write in it all the former words that were in the first roll, which Jehoiakim the king of Judah hath burned.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/jeremiah/36#v29)</sup> And thou shalt say to Jehoiakim king of Judah, Thus saith the Lord; Thou hast burned this roll, saying, Why hast thou written therein, saying, The king of Babylon shall certainly come and destroy this land, and shall cause to cease from thence man and beast?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/36#v30)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of Jehoiakim king of Judah; He shall have none to sit upon the throne of David: and his dead body shall be cast out in the day to the heat, and in the night to the frost.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/36#v31)</sup> And I will punish him and his seed and his servants for their iniquity; and I will bring upon them, and upon the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and upon the men of Judah, all the evil that I have pronounced against them; but they hearkened not.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/jeremiah/36#v32)</sup> Then took Jeremiah another roll, and gave it to Baruch the scribe, the son of Neriah; who wrote therein from the mouth of Jeremiah all the words of the book which Jehoiakim king of Judah had burned in the fire: and there were added besides unto them many like words.

- After the king burns the roll, the word of the Most High comes to Jeremiah to take another roll and write all the former words that Jehoiakim had burned.
- Against Jehoiakim He pronounces that he shall have none to sit upon the throne of David, his dead body shall be cast out to the heat by day and the frost by night, and He will punish him, his seed, and his servants for their iniquity.
- Jeremiah gives a second roll to Baruch, who writes from Jeremiah's mouth all the words of the burned book, and many like words are added besides.

# Jeremiah 37: The Prophet Arrested and Imprisoned

**[Jeremiah 37:1-5](/bible/jeremiah/37#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/37#v1)</sup> And king Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigned instead of Coniah the son of Jehoiakim, whom Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon made king in the land of Judah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/37#v2)</sup> But neither he, nor his servants, nor the people of the land, did hearken unto the words of the Lord, which he spake by the prophet Jeremiah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/37#v3)</sup> And Zedekiah the king sent Jehucal the son of Shelemiah and Zephaniah the son of Maaseiah the priest to the prophet Jeremiah, saying, Pray now unto the Lord our God for us.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/37#v4)</sup> Now Jeremiah came in and went out among the people: for they had not put him into prison.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/37#v5)</sup> Then Pharaoh’s army was come forth out of Egypt: and when the Chaldeans that besieged Jerusalem heard tidings of them, they departed from Jerusalem.

- King Zedekiah the son of Josiah reigns in place of Coniah, whom Nebuchadnezzar made king in the land of Judah, but neither he nor his servants nor the people hearken to the words of the Most High spoken by Jeremiah.
- Zedekiah sends men to ask Jeremiah to pray to the Most High for them, while Jeremiah is still going in and out among the people, not yet put into prison.
- Pharaoh's army comes out of Egypt, and when the Chaldeans besieging Jerusalem hear of it, they depart from the city.

**[Jeremiah 37:6-10](/bible/jeremiah/37#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/37#v6)</sup> Then came the word of the Lord unto the prophet Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/37#v7)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; Thus shall ye say to the king of Judah, that sent you unto me to enquire of me; Behold, Pharaoh’s army, which is come forth to help you, shall return to Egypt into their own land.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/37#v8)</sup> And the Chaldeans shall come again, and fight against this city, and take it, and burn it with fire.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/37#v9)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Deceive not yourselves, saying, The Chaldeans shall surely depart from us: for they shall not depart.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/37#v10)</sup> For though ye had smitten the whole army of the Chaldeans that fight against you, and there remained but wounded men among them, yet should they rise up every man in his tent, and burn this city with fire.

- The word of the Most High comes to Jeremiah for the king of Judah: Pharaoh's army that came to help shall return to Egypt into their own land.
- The Chaldeans shall come again, fight against this city, take it, and burn it with fire; the people are not to deceive themselves that the Chaldeans have surely departed.
- Even if they smote the whole Chaldean army so that only wounded men remained, those men would rise up in their tents and burn the city with fire.

**[Jeremiah 37:11-15](/bible/jeremiah/37#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/37#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, that when the army of the Chaldeans was broken up from Jerusalem for fear of Pharaoh’s army,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/37#v12)</sup> Then Jeremiah went forth out of Jerusalem to go into the land of Benjamin, to separate himself thence in the midst of the people.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/37#v13)</sup> And when he was in the gate of Benjamin, a captain of the ward was there, whose name was Irijah, the son of Shelemiah, the son of Hananiah; and he took Jeremiah the prophet, saying, Thou fallest away to the Chaldeans.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/37#v14)</sup> Then said Jeremiah, It is false; I fall not away to the Chaldeans. But he hearkened not to him: so Irijah took Jeremiah, and brought him to the princes.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/37#v15)</sup> Wherefore the princes were wroth with Jeremiah, and smote him, and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the scribe: for they had made that the prison.

- When the Chaldean army breaks up from Jerusalem for fear of Pharaoh's army, Jeremiah goes forth toward the land of Benjamin to separate himself among the people.
- At the gate of Benjamin a captain of the ward named Irijah seizes him, charging that he is falling away to the Chaldeans; Jeremiah answers that it is false, but Irijah will not hear him.
- Irijah brings Jeremiah to the princes, who are wroth with him, smite him, and put him in prison in the house of Jonathan the scribe.

**[Jeremiah 37:16-21](/bible/jeremiah/37#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/37#v16)</sup> When Jeremiah was entered into the dungeon, and into the cabins, and Jeremiah had remained there many days;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/37#v17)</sup> Then Zedekiah the king sent, and took him out: and the king asked him secretly in his house, and said, Is there any word from the Lord? And Jeremiah said, There is: for, said he, thou shalt be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/37#v18)</sup> Moreover Jeremiah said unto king Zedekiah, What have I offended against thee, or against thy servants, or against this people, that ye have put me in prison?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/37#v19)</sup> Where are now your prophets which prophesied unto you, saying, The king of Babylon shall not come against you, nor against this land?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/37#v20)</sup> Therefore hear now, I pray thee, O my lord the king: let my supplication, I pray thee, be accepted before thee; that thou cause me not to return to the house of Jonathan the scribe, lest I die there.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/37#v21)</sup> Then Zedekiah the king commanded that they should commit Jeremiah into the court of the prison, and that they should give him daily a piece of bread out of the bakers’ street, until all the bread in the city were spent. Thus Jeremiah remained in the court of the prison.

- After Jeremiah has remained many days in the dungeon and its cells, Zedekiah secretly sends for him and asks if there is any word from the Most High; Jeremiah answers there is, that the king shall be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon.
- Jeremiah asks what he has offended against the king, his servants, or the people that they have put him in prison, and where now are the prophets who prophesied that the king of Babylon would not come.
- He pleads not to be sent back to the house of Jonathan the scribe lest he die there; Zedekiah commands he be kept in the court of the prison and given a daily piece of bread out of the bakers' street until all the bread in the city is spent.

# Jeremiah 38: The Miry Dungeon and Abedmelech's Rescue

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 38](/bible/jeremiah/38)

**[Jeremiah 38:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/38#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/38#v1)</sup> Then Shephatiah the son of Mattan, and Gedaliah the son of Pashur, and Jucal the son of Shelemiah, and Pashur the son of Malchiah, heard the words that Jeremiah had spoken unto all the people, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/38#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, He that remaineth in this city shall die by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence: but he that goeth forth to the Chaldeans shall live; for he shall have his life for a prey, and shall live.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/38#v3)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, This city shall surely be given into the hand of the king of Babylon’s army, which shall take it.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/38#v4)</sup> Therefore the princes said unto the king, We beseech thee, let this man be put to death: for thus he weakeneth the hands of the men of war that remain in this city, and the hands of all the people, in speaking such words unto them: for this man seeketh not the welfare of this people, but the hurt.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/38#v5)</sup> Then Zedekiah the king said, Behold, he is in your hand: for the king is not he that can do any thing against you.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/38#v6)</sup> Then took they Jeremiah, and cast him into the dungeon of Malchiah the son of Hammelech, that was in the court of the prison: and they let down Jeremiah with cords. And in the dungeon there was no water, but mire: so Jeremiah sunk in the mire.

- Shephatiah, Gedaliah, Jucal, and Pashur hear Jeremiah telling the people that whoever remains in the city shall die by sword, famine, and pestilence, but whoever goes forth to the Chaldeans shall live and have his life for a prey.
- The princes tell the king that Jeremiah must be put to death because he weakens the hands of the men of war and seeks not the welfare of the people but their hurt; the king answers that he is in their hand and the king can do nothing against them.
- They cast Jeremiah into the dungeon of Malchiah in the court of the prison, letting him down with cords into a place with no water but mire, and Jeremiah sinks in the mire.

**[Jeremiah 38:7-13](/bible/jeremiah/38#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/38#v7)</sup> Now when Ebed–melech the Ethiopian, one of the eunuchs which was in the king’s house, heard that they had put Jeremiah in the dungeon; the king then sitting in the gate of Benjamin;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/38#v8)</sup> Ebed–melech went forth out of the king’s house, and spake to the king, saying,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/38#v9)</sup> My lord the king, these men have done evil in all that they have done to Jeremiah the prophet, whom they have cast into the dungeon; and he is like to die for hunger in the place where he is: for there is no more bread in the city.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/38#v10)</sup> Then the king commanded Ebed–melech the Ethiopian, saying, Take from hence thirty men with thee, and take up Jeremiah the prophet out of the dungeon, before he die.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/38#v11)</sup> So Ebed–melech took the men with him, and went into the house of the king under the treasury, and took thence old cast clouts and old rotten rags, and let them down by cords into the dungeon to Jeremiah.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/38#v12)</sup> And Ebed–melech the Ethiopian said unto Jeremiah, Put now these old cast clouts and rotten rags under thine armholes under the cords. And Jeremiah did so.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/38#v13)</sup> So they drew up Jeremiah with cords, and took him up out of the dungeon: and Jeremiah remained in the court of the prison.

- Abedmelech the Ethiopian, one of the eunuchs in the king's house, hears that Jeremiah has been put in the dungeon while the king sits in the gate of Benjamin.
- Abedmelech goes to the king and says these men have done evil in all they have done to Jeremiah, who is likely to die of hunger where he is, for there is no more bread in the city; the king commands him to take thirty men and lift the prophet out before he dies.
- Abedmelech takes old cast clouts and rotten rags, lets them down by cords, and tells Jeremiah to put them under his armholes under the cords; they draw him up out of the dungeon, and Jeremiah remains in the court of the prison.

**[Jeremiah 38:14-20](/bible/jeremiah/38#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/38#v14)</sup> Then Zedekiah the king sent, and took Jeremiah the prophet unto him into the third entry that is in the house of the Lord: and the king said unto Jeremiah, I will ask thee a thing; hide nothing from me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/38#v15)</sup> Then Jeremiah said unto Zedekiah, If I declare it unto thee, wilt thou not surely put me to death? and if I give thee counsel, wilt thou not hearken unto me?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/38#v16)</sup> So Zedekiah the king sware secretly unto Jeremiah, saying, As the Lord liveth, that made us this soul, I will not put thee to death, neither will I give thee into the hand of these men that seek thy life.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/38#v17)</sup> Then said Jeremiah unto Zedekiah, Thus saith the Lord, the God of hosts, the God of Israel; If thou wilt assuredly go forth unto the king of Babylon’s princes, then thy soul shall live, and this city shall not be burned with fire; and thou shalt live, and thine house:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/38#v18)</sup> But if thou wilt not go forth to the king of Babylon’s princes, then shall this city be given into the hand of the Chaldeans, and they shall burn it with fire, and thou shalt not escape out of their hand.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/38#v19)</sup> And Zedekiah the king said unto Jeremiah, I am afraid of the Jews that are fallen to the Chaldeans, lest they deliver me into their hand, and they mock me.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/38#v20)</sup> But Jeremiah said, They shall not deliver thee. Obey, I beseech thee, the voice of the Lord, which I speak unto thee: so it shall be well unto thee, and thy soul shall live.

- Zedekiah sends for Jeremiah into the third entry of the house of the Most High and asks him to hide nothing; Jeremiah answers that if he declares it the king will surely put him to death, and if he counsels him the king will not hearken.
- The king swears secretly that as the Most High liveth he will not put him to death nor give him into the hand of those who seek his life.
- Jeremiah tells him that if he goes forth to the king of Babylon's princes his soul shall live and the city shall not be burned, but if he will not go forth the city shall be given to the Chaldeans and burned; the king says he is afraid of the Jews already fallen to the Chaldeans, and Jeremiah urges him to obey the voice of the Most High that it may be well with him.

**[Jeremiah 38:21-28](/bible/jeremiah/38#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/38#v21)</sup> But if thou refuse to go forth, this is the word that the Lord hath shewed me:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/38#v22)</sup> And, behold, all the women that are left in the king of Judah’s house shall be brought forth to the king of Babylon’s princes, and those women shall say, Thy friends have set thee on, and have prevailed against thee: thy feet are sunk in the mire, and they are turned away back.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/38#v23)</sup> So they shall bring out all thy wives and thy children to the Chaldeans: and thou shalt not escape out of their hand, but shalt be taken by the hand of the king of Babylon: and thou shalt cause this city to be burned with fire.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/38#v24)</sup> Then said Zedekiah unto Jeremiah, Let no man know of these words, and thou shalt not die.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/38#v25)</sup> But if the princes hear that I have talked with thee, and they come unto thee, and say unto thee, Declare unto us now what thou hast said unto the king, hide it not from us, and we will not put thee to death; also what the king said unto thee:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/38#v26)</sup> Then thou shalt say unto them, I presented my supplication before the king, that he would not cause me to return to Jonathan’s house, to die there.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/38#v27)</sup> Then came all the princes unto Jeremiah, and asked him: and he told them according to all these words that the king had commanded. So they left off speaking with him; for the matter was not perceived.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/jeremiah/38#v28)</sup> So Jeremiah abode in the court of the prison until the day that Jerusalem was taken: and he was there when Jerusalem was taken.

- If the king refuses to go forth, Jeremiah shows him that all the women left in the king's house shall be brought out to the king of Babylon's princes, saying his friends have prevailed against him and his feet are sunk in the mire and turned away back.
- All his wives and children shall be brought to the Chaldeans, and he shall not escape but be taken by the hand of the king of Babylon and cause the city to be burned with fire.
- Zedekiah charges Jeremiah to let no man know their words, and to tell the princes only that he presented his supplication not to be returned to Jonathan's house; the princes come and ask, Jeremiah answers as the king commanded, and he abides in the court of the prison until the day Jerusalem is taken.

# Jeremiah 39: The Fall of Jerusalem

**[Jeremiah 39:1-8](/bible/jeremiah/39#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/39#v1)</sup> In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, came Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon and all his army against Jerusalem, and they besieged it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/39#v2)</sup> And in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, the ninth day of the month, the city was broken up.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/39#v3)</sup> And all the princes of the king of Babylon came in, and sat in the middle gate, even Nergal–sharezer, Samgar–nebo, Sarsechim, Rab–saris, Nergal–sharezer, Rab–mag, with all the residue of the princes of the king of Babylon.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/39#v4)</sup> And it came to pass, that when Zedekiah the king of Judah saw them, and all the men of war, then they fled, and went forth out of the city by night, by the way of the king’s garden, by the gate betwixt the two walls: and he went out the way of the plain.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/39#v5)</sup> But the Chaldeans’ army pursued after them, and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho: and when they had taken him, they brought him up to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he gave judgment upon him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/39#v6)</sup> Then the king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah in Riblah before his eyes: also the king of Babylon slew all the nobles of Judah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/39#v7)</sup> Moreover he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, and bound him with chains, to carry him to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/39#v8)</sup> And the Chaldeans burned the king’s house, and the houses of the people, with fire, and brake down the walls of Jerusalem.

- In the ninth year of Zedekiah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar and all his army come against Jerusalem and besiege it, and in the eleventh year, the fourth month, the ninth day, the city is broken up.
- The princes of the king of Babylon come in and sit in the middle gate; when Zedekiah and the men of war see them, they flee by night by the way of the king's garden through the gate between the two walls toward the plain.
- The Chaldean army pursues and overtakes Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho, and they bring him up to Nebuchadnezzar at Riblah, where the king of Babylon slays Zedekiah's sons and all the nobles of Judah before his eyes, then puts out Zedekiah's eyes, binds him with chains for Babylon, and burns the king's house and the houses of the people, breaking down the walls of Jerusalem.
