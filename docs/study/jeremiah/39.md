**[Jeremiah 39:1-8](/bible/jeremiah/39#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/39#v1)</sup> In the ninth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, in the tenth month, came Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon and all his army against Jerusalem, and they besieged it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/39#v2)</sup> And in the eleventh year of Zedekiah, in the fourth month, the ninth day of the month, the city was broken up.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/39#v3)</sup> And all the princes of the king of Babylon came in, and sat in the middle gate, even Nergal–sharezer, Samgar–nebo, Sarsechim, Rab–saris, Nergal–sharezer, Rab–mag, with all the residue of the princes of the king of Babylon.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/39#v4)</sup> And it came to pass, that when Zedekiah the king of Judah saw them, and all the men of war, then they fled, and went forth out of the city by night, by the way of the king’s garden, by the gate betwixt the two walls: and he went out the way of the plain.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/39#v5)</sup> But the Chaldeans’ army pursued after them, and overtook Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho: and when they had taken him, they brought him up to Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon to Riblah in the land of Hamath, where he gave judgment upon him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/39#v6)</sup> Then the king of Babylon slew the sons of Zedekiah in Riblah before his eyes: also the king of Babylon slew all the nobles of Judah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/39#v7)</sup> Moreover he put out Zedekiah’s eyes, and bound him with chains, to carry him to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/39#v8)</sup> And the Chaldeans burned the king’s house, and the houses of the people, with fire, and brake down the walls of Jerusalem.

- In the ninth year of Zedekiah, in the tenth month, Nebuchadnezzar and all his army come against Jerusalem and besiege it, and in the eleventh year, the fourth month, the ninth day, the city is broken up.
- The princes of the king of Babylon come in and sit in the middle gate; when Zedekiah and the men of war see them, they flee by night by the way of the king's garden through the gate between the two walls toward the plain.
- The Chaldean army pursues and overtakes Zedekiah in the plains of Jericho, and they bring him up to Nebuchadnezzar at Riblah, where the king of Babylon slays Zedekiah's sons and all the nobles of Judah before his eyes, then puts out Zedekiah's eyes, binds him with chains for Babylon, and burns the king's house and the houses of the people, breaking down the walls of Jerusalem.

**[Jeremiah 39:9-14](/bible/jeremiah/39#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/39#v9)</sup> Then Nebuzar–adan the captain of the guard carried away captive into Babylon the remnant of the people that remained in the city, and those that fell away, that fell to him, with the rest of the people that remained.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/39#v10)</sup> But Nebuzar–adan the captain of the guard left of the poor of the people, which had nothing, in the land of Judah, and gave them vineyards and fields at the same time.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/39#v11)</sup> Now Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon gave charge concerning Jeremiah to Nebuzar–adan the captain of the guard, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/39#v12)</sup> Take him, and look well to him, and do him no harm; but do unto him even as he shall say unto thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/39#v13)</sup> So Nebuzar–adan the captain of the guard sent, and Nebushasban, Rab–saris, and Nergal–sharezer, Rab–mag, and all the king of Babylon’s princes;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/39#v14)</sup> Even they sent, and took Jeremiah out of the court of the prison, and committed him unto Gedaliah the son of Ahikam the son of Shaphan, that he should carry him home: so he dwelt among the people.

- Nebuzaradan the captain of the guard carries away captive to Babylon the remnant of the people that remained in the city, those who fell away to him, and the rest, but leaves the poor of the people who had nothing in the land of Judah and gives them vineyards and fields.
- Nebuchadnezzar gives charge concerning Jeremiah that he be taken, looked to well, and done no harm, but treated as he himself shall say.
- The princes of Babylon send and take Jeremiah out of the court of the prison and commit him to Gedaliah the son of Ahikam to carry him home, so he dwells among the people.

**[Jeremiah 39:15-18](/bible/jeremiah/39#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/39#v15)</sup> Now the word of the Lord came unto Jeremiah, while he was shut up in the court of the prison, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/39#v16)</sup> Go and speak to Ebed–melech the Ethiopian, saying, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will bring my words upon this city for evil, and not for good; and they shall be accomplished in that day before thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/39#v17)</sup> But I will deliver thee in that day, saith the Lord: and thou shalt not be given into the hand of the men of whom thou art afraid.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/39#v18)</sup> For I will surely deliver thee, and thou shalt not fall by the sword, but thy life shall be for a prey unto thee: because thou hast put thy trust in me, saith the Lord.

- While Jeremiah is still shut up in the court of the prison, the word of the Most High comes to him for Abedmelech the Ethiopian, declaring that He will bring His words upon this city for evil and not for good, and they shall be accomplished in that day.
- But in that day He will deliver Abedmelech, who shall not be given into the hand of the men he is afraid of, and shall not fall by the sword, because he has put his trust in the Most High; the Most High deals with the Ethiopian because he was used to help Jeremiah, sent to take the prophet up out of the dungeon before he died.
  - **[Jeremiah 38:10](/bible/jeremiah/38#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/38#v10)</sup> Then the king commanded Ebed–melech the Ethiopian, saying, Take from hence thirty men with thee, and take up Jeremiah the prophet out of the dungeon, before he die.

    Abedmelech's deliverance is tied back to the moment the king commanded him to lift Jeremiah out of the miry dungeon before he perished.
