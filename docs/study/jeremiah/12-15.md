Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 12](/bible/jeremiah/12)

This is the fourth chapter reading, a habit set so brothers and sisters read at least four chapters every day. Four chapters is not a lot, and reading the scriptures front to back gives a broad understanding while the precepts open up more as you learn and grow. The point of the four chapter reading is to keep all of us constantly meditating in the word, as [Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8) commands. This session covers [Jeremiah 12](/bible/jeremiah/12) through 15: Jeremiah pleading over the prosperity of the wicked, the marred linen girdle, the drought and the false prophets, and the Most High refusing to be entreated for a people who will not turn.

**[Jeremiah 12:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/12#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/12#v1)</sup> Righteous art thou, O Lord, when I plead with thee: yet let me talk with thee of thy judgments: Wherefore doth the way of the wicked prosper? wherefore are all they happy that deal very treacherously?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/12#v2)</sup> Thou hast planted them, yea, they have taken root: they grow, yea, they bring forth fruit: thou art near in their mouth, and far from their reins.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/12#v3)</sup> But thou, O Lord, knowest me: thou hast seen me, and tried mine heart toward thee: pull them out like sheep for the slaughter, and prepare them for the day of slaughter.

- Jeremiah pleads with the Most High and asks why the way of the wicked prospers and why those who deal very treacherously are happy.
- We often look at the rich, at actors and actresses, doing the most wicked things. For many it is just a job, but some are involved in pretty wicked things, and we ask why they are prospering.
- They do not say that anything they have pertains to the Most High, and they do not thank Him for anything; it is just "it is what it is" with them. Jeremiah is expressing that same tension about the prosperity of the wicked.

**[Jeremiah 12:4-6](/bible/jeremiah/12#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/12#v4)</sup> How long shall the land mourn, and the herbs of every field wither, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein? the beasts are consumed, and the birds; because they said, He shall not see our last end.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/12#v5)</sup> If thou hast run with the footmen, and they have wearied thee, then how canst thou contend with horses? and if in the land of peace, wherein thou trustedst, they wearied thee, then how wilt thou do in the swelling of Jordan?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/12#v6)</sup> For even thy brethren, and the house of thy father, even they have dealt treacherously with thee; yea, they have called a multitude after thee: believe them not, though they speak fair words unto thee.

- Jeremiah asks how long the land will mourn while the beasts and birds are consumed, because the people say the Most High shall not see their last end.
- Even his own brethren in his father's house dealt treacherously with him and called a multitude after him. The Most High warns him to believe them not, though they speak fair words.

**[Jeremiah 12:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/12#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/12#v7)</sup> I have forsaken mine house, I have left mine heritage; I have given the dearly beloved of my soul into the hand of her enemies.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/12#v8)</sup> Mine heritage is unto me as a lion in the forest; it crieth out against me: therefore have I hated it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/12#v9)</sup> Mine heritage is unto me as a speckled bird, the birds round about are against her; come ye, assemble all the beasts of the field, come to devour.

- The Most High has forsaken His house and given the dearly beloved of His soul into the hand of her enemies, calling His heritage a speckled bird with all the other birds against her.
- There are many different shades to the Israelites. Israelites come in all shapes, sizes and colors, and a color does not equal a nationality. Your skin tone does not make you a nationality; what the Most High is saying is that the skin tones vary among Israel.
  - **[Hosea 7:8](/bible/hosea/7#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/7#v8)</sup> Ephraim, he hath mixed himself among the people; Ephraim is a cake not turned.

    Israel mixed among the people is the same speckled heritage, one people made of many shades.

**[Jeremiah 12:10-13](/bible/jeremiah/12#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/12#v10)</sup> Many pastors have destroyed my vineyard, they have trodden my portion under foot, they have made my pleasant portion a desolate wilderness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/12#v11)</sup> They have made it desolate, and being desolate it mourneth unto me; the whole land is made desolate, because no man layeth it to heart.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/12#v12)</sup> The spoilers are come upon all high places through the wilderness: for the sword of the Lord shall devour from the one end of the land even to the other end of the land: no flesh shall have peace.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/12#v13)</sup> They have sown wheat, but shall reap thorns: they have put themselves to pain, but shall not profit: and they shall be ashamed of your revenues because of the fierce anger of the Lord.

- Many pastors have destroyed the vineyard and made the pleasant portion a desolate wilderness, and the whole land is made desolate because no man lays it to heart.
- The sword of the Most High devours from one end of the land to the other, and no flesh shall have peace. They sow wheat but reap thorns.
- Even though many of these people are rich and doing many things in the world, if they are not keeping the commandments none of it will matter. When the Most High gives you something you are to keep His commandments, or it becomes a stumbling block, and you can lose your life over what you think you have.

**[Jeremiah 12:14-17](/bible/jeremiah/12#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/12#v14)</sup> Thus saith the Lord against all mine evil neighbours, that touch the inheritance which I have caused my people Israel to inherit; Behold, I will pluck them out of their land, and pluck out the house of Judah from among them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/12#v15)</sup> And it shall come to pass, after that I have plucked them out I will return, and have compassion on them, and will bring them again, every man to his heritage, and every man to his land.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/12#v16)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if they will diligently learn the ways of my people, to swear by my name, The Lord liveth; as they taught my people to swear by Baal; then shall they be built in the midst of my people.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/12#v17)</sup> But if they will not obey, I will utterly pluck up and destroy that nation, saith the Lord.

- Against His evil neighbors who touch the inheritance He gave Israel, the Most High will pluck them out of their land, and pluck Judah out from among them, then return and have compassion and bring every man back to his heritage.
- If those nations will diligently learn the ways of His people and swear by His name, they shall be built in the midst of His people; but if they will not obey He will utterly pluck and destroy that nation.
- When the Israelites are the ruling class on earth, every nation will follow the laws of the Most High. We will force every nation to keep the commandments, and if they will not they have a price to pay. That is the patience and the faith of the saints.

# Jeremiah 13: The Marred Linen Girdle

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 13](/bible/jeremiah/13)

**[Jeremiah 13:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/13#v1)</sup> Thus saith the Lord unto me, Go and get thee a linen girdle, and put it upon thy loins, and put it not in water.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/13#v2)</sup> So I got a girdle according to the word of the Lord, and put it on my loins.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/13#v3)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me the second time, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/13#v4)</sup> Take the girdle that thou hast got, which is upon thy loins, and arise, go to Euphrates, and hide it there in a hole of the rock.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/13#v5)</sup> So I went, and hid it by Euphrates, as the Lord commanded me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/13#v6)</sup> And it came to pass after many days, that the Lord said unto me, Arise, go to Euphrates, and take the girdle from thence, which I commanded thee to hide there.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/13#v7)</sup> Then I went to Euphrates, and digged, and took the girdle from the place where I had hid it: and, behold, the girdle was marred, it was profitable for nothing.

- The Most High tells Jeremiah to get a linen girdle, put it on his loins, and not put it in water. After wearing it he is sent to hide it by the Euphrates, in the area of Iraq.
- After many days he is told to go dig it up, and behold the girdle was marred and profitable for nothing.

**[Jeremiah 13:8-11](/bible/jeremiah/13#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/13#v8)</sup> Then the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/13#v9)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, After this manner will I mar the pride of Judah, and the great pride of Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/13#v10)</sup> This evil people, which refuse to hear my words, which walk in the imagination of their heart, and walk after other gods, to serve them, and to worship them, shall even be as this girdle, which is good for nothing.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/13#v11)</sup> For as the girdle cleaveth to the loins of a man, so have I caused to cleave unto me the whole house of Israel and the whole house of Judah, saith the Lord; that they might be unto me for a people, and for a name, and for a praise, and for a glory: but they would not hear.

- After this manner the Most High will mar the pride of Judah and the great pride of Jerusalem, an evil people who refuse to hear His words and walk after other gods.
- There was a punishment for us going into idolatry and sin, and for being so prideful that we started to forget the Most High is in control of everything. Just as that girdle was good for nothing, the nation of Israel became the same way.
- As the girdle cleaves to the loins of a man, the Most High caused the whole house of Israel and Judah to cleave to Him for a name and a praise, but they would not hear.

**[Jeremiah 13:12-14](/bible/jeremiah/13#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/13#v12)</sup> Therefore thou shalt speak unto them this word; Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Every bottle shall be filled with wine: and they shall say unto thee, Do we not certainly know that every bottle shall be filled with wine?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/13#v13)</sup> Then shalt thou say unto them, Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will fill all the inhabitants of this land, even the kings that sit upon David’s throne, and the priests, and the prophets, and all the inhabitants of Jerusalem, with drunkenness.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/13#v14)</sup> And I will dash them one against another, even the fathers and the sons together, saith the Lord: I will not pity, nor spare, nor have mercy, but destroy them.

- Every bottle shall be filled with wine, and the Most High will fill all the inhabitants of the land, even the kings on David's throne and the priests and prophets, with drunkenness, and dash them one against another.
- He will smash the fathers and the sons together and will not pity nor spare nor have mercy. That is why there is such a big discrepancy in the black community between fathers and sons: sons grow without fathers, become fathers, and grow without their children. It is a repeating cycle.

**[Jeremiah 13:15-19](/bible/jeremiah/13#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/13#v15)</sup> Hear ye, and give ear; be not proud: for the Lord hath spoken.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/13#v16)</sup> Give glory to the Lord your God, before he cause darkness, and before your feet stumble upon the dark mountains, and, while ye look for light, he turn it into the shadow of death, and make it gross darkness.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/13#v17)</sup> But if ye will not hear it, my soul shall weep in secret places for your pride; and mine eye shall weep sore, and run down with tears, because the Lord’s flock is carried away captive.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/13#v18)</sup> Say unto the king and to the queen, Humble yourselves, sit down: for your principalities shall come down, even the crown of your glory.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/13#v19)</sup> The cities of the south shall be shut up, and none shall open them: Judah shall be carried away captive all of it, it shall be wholly carried away captive.

- Be not proud; give glory to the Most High before He causes darkness and your feet stumble on the dark mountains. If you will not hear, His soul weeps in secret places because the flock is carried away captive.
- The king and queen are told to humble themselves and sit down, for their principalities and crown shall come down, and Judah shall be wholly carried away captive.

**[Jeremiah 13:20-23](/bible/jeremiah/13#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/13#v20)</sup> Lift up your eyes, and behold them that come from the north: where is the flock that was given thee, thy beautiful flock?
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/13#v21)</sup> What wilt thou say when he shall punish thee? for thou hast taught them to be captains, and as chief over thee: shall not sorrows take thee, as a woman in travail?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/13#v22)</sup> And if thou say in thine heart, Wherefore come these things upon me? For the greatness of thine iniquity are thy skirts discovered, and thy heels made bare.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/13#v23)</sup> Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil.

- Judah is asked what she will say when punished, for she taught her enemies to be captains over her, and sorrows will take her like a woman in travail.
- Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? Then may you also do good who are accustomed to do evil. The people of Ethiopia are very dark; the land is named the land of burnt faces, and they cannot get lighter. In the same way you cannot hide the evil that you do.

**[Jeremiah 13:24-27](/bible/jeremiah/13#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/13#v24)</sup> Therefore will I scatter them as the stubble that passeth away by the wind of the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/13#v25)</sup> This is thy lot, the portion of thy measures from me, saith the Lord; because thou hast forgotten me, and trusted in falsehood.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/13#v26)</sup> Therefore will I discover thy skirts upon thy face, that thy shame may appear.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/jeremiah/13#v27)</sup> I have seen thine adulteries, and thy neighings, the lewdness of thy whoredom, and thine abominations on the hills in the fields. Woe unto thee, O Jerusalem! wilt thou not be made clean? when shall it once be?

- Because she forgot the Most High and trusted in falsehood, He will scatter them as stubble and discover their skirts upon their face so their shame appears.
- He has seen the adulteries, the lewdness of the whoredoms, and the abominations. We see it day to day, and there is going to be a judgment for all of it.

# Jeremiah 14: The Drought and the False Prophets

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 14](/bible/jeremiah/14)

**[Jeremiah 14:1-6](/bible/jeremiah/14#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/14#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord that came to Jeremiah concerning the dearth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/14#v2)</sup> Judah mourneth, and the gates thereof languish; they are black unto the ground; and the cry of Jerusalem is gone up.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/14#v3)</sup> And their nobles have sent their little ones to the waters: they came to the pits, and found no water; they returned with their vessels empty; they were ashamed and confounded, and covered their heads.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/14#v4)</sup> Because the ground is chapt, for there was no rain in the earth, the plowmen were ashamed, they covered their heads.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/14#v5)</sup> Yea, the hind also calved in the field, and forsook it, because there was no grass.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/14#v6)</sup> And the wild asses did stand in the high places, they snuffed up the wind like dragons; their eyes did fail, because there was no grass.

- The word comes concerning the dearth. Judah mourns, the gates languish, and the cry of Jerusalem goes up while the nobles send their little ones for water and find none.
- The ground is chapped because there is no rain, and the plowmen are ashamed and cover their heads. That is how you know these are black people; "chapped" is a word black people use, not just for lips but for the cracked, dry ground.

**[Jeremiah 14:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/14#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/14#v7)</sup> O Lord, though our iniquities testify against us, do thou it for thy name’s sake: for our backslidings are many; we have sinned against thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/14#v8)</sup> O the hope of Israel, the saviour thereof in time of trouble, why shouldest thou be as a stranger in the land, and as a wayfaring man that turneth aside to tarry for a night?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/14#v9)</sup> Why shouldest thou be as a man astonied, as a mighty man that cannot save? yet thou, O Lord, art in the midst of us, and we are called by thy name; leave us not.

- Though our iniquities testify against us, the plea is that the Most High act for His name's sake, for our backslidings are many. He is called the hope of Israel and the Savior in time of trouble.
- The cry is for Him not to be a stranger in the land or a mighty man that cannot save, since He is in the midst of us and we are called by His name.

**[Jeremiah 14:10-12](/bible/jeremiah/14#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/14#v10)</sup> Thus saith the Lord unto this people, Thus have they loved to wander, they have not refrained their feet, therefore the Lord doth not accept them; he will now remember their iniquity, and visit their sins.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/14#v11)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Pray not for this people for their good.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/14#v12)</sup> When they fast, I will not hear their cry; and when they offer burnt offering and an oblation, I will not accept them: but I will consume them by the sword, and by the famine, and by the pestilence.

- The people loved to wander and did not refrain their feet, so the Most High does not accept them; He will remember their iniquity and visit their sins.
- He tells Jeremiah not to pray for this people; even when they fast He will not hear their cry, and their offerings He will not accept, but will consume them by sword, famine and pestilence.
  - **[John 9:31](/bible/john/9#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/john/9#v31)</sup> Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.

    The Most High does not hear sinners; He told Jeremiah not to pray for them because their sins were, and are, so many, which is why it behooves all of us to repent.

**[Jeremiah 14:13-16](/bible/jeremiah/14#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/14#v13)</sup> Then said I, Ah, Lord God! behold, the prophets say unto them, Ye shall not see the sword, neither shall ye have famine; but I will give you assured peace in this place.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/14#v14)</sup> Then the Lord said unto me, The prophets prophesy lies in my name: I sent them not, neither have I commanded them, neither spake unto them: they prophesy unto you a false vision and divination, and a thing of nought, and the deceit of their heart.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/14#v15)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord concerning the prophets that prophesy in my name, and I sent them not, yet they say, Sword and famine shall not be in this land; By sword and famine shall those prophets be consumed.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/14#v16)</sup> And the people to whom they prophesy shall be cast out in the streets of Jerusalem because of the famine and the sword; and they shall have none to bury them, them, their wives, nor their sons, nor their daughters: for I will pour their wickedness upon them.

- Jeremiah answers that the prophets tell the people they shall not see sword or famine but shall have assured peace. This is what the Christian pastors do today; the verse above said not to pray for these people, yet the false prophets promise nothing but peace and prosperity.
- The Most High says those prophets prophesy lies in His name; He did not send them. By sword and famine those prophets shall be consumed, and the people they deceived cast out in the streets with none to bury them.
- What happened back then is happening today. This country is going to go down by nuclear destruction, whether our people accept it or not, while the false prophets say nothing but peace, and they will be dealt with like all the other nations.

**[Jeremiah 14:17-22](/bible/jeremiah/14#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/14#v17)</sup> Therefore thou shalt say this word unto them; Let mine eyes run down with tears night and day, and let them not cease: for the virgin daughter of my people is broken with a great breach, with a very grievous blow.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/14#v18)</sup> If I go forth into the field, then behold the slain with the sword! and if I enter into the city, then behold them that are sick with famine! yea, both the prophet and the priest go about into a land that they know not.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/14#v19)</sup> Hast thou utterly rejected Judah? hath thy soul lothed Zion? why hast thou smitten us, and there is no healing for us? we looked for peace, and there is no good; and for the time of healing, and behold trouble!
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/14#v20)</sup> We acknowledge, O Lord, our wickedness, and the iniquity of our fathers: for we have sinned against thee.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/14#v21)</sup> Do not abhor us, for thy name’s sake, do not disgrace the throne of thy glory: remember, break not thy covenant with us.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/14#v22)</sup> Are there any among the vanities of the Gentiles that can cause rain? or can the heavens give showers? art not thou he, O Lord our God? therefore we will wait upon thee: for thou hast made all these things.

- Let the eyes run down with tears night and day, for the virgin daughter of the people is broken with a great breach; the slain fill the field and the sick with famine fill the city, while prophet and priest wander into a land they know not.
- We acknowledge our wickedness and the iniquity of our fathers and ask Him not to abhor us for His name's sake or break His covenant. None of the vanities of the gentiles can cause rain; only the Most High, so we wait upon Him.
- We have to acknowledge our offense and the wickedness we have been doing, even the wickedness we support because it does not affect us directly. Your salvation is contingent on keeping the commandments and abhorring that evil.

# Jeremiah 15: The Prophet's Burden

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 15](/bible/jeremiah/15)

**[Jeremiah 15:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/15#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/15#v1)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Though Moses and Samuel stood before me, yet my mind could not be toward this people: cast them out of my sight, and let them go forth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/15#v2)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if they say unto thee, Whither shall we go forth? then thou shalt tell them, Thus saith the Lord; Such as are for death, to death; and such as are for the sword, to the sword; and such as are for the famine, to the famine; and such as are for the captivity, to the captivity.

- Though Moses and Samuel stood before Him, the mind of the Most High could not be toward this people; He says cast them out of My sight.
- When the people ask where they shall go, the answer is such as are for death to death, for the sword to the sword, for the famine to the famine, and for the captivity to the captivity. That is how we got removed from before the Most High.

**[Jeremiah 15:3-9](/bible/jeremiah/15#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/15#v3)</sup> And I will appoint over them four kinds, saith the Lord: the sword to slay, and the dogs to tear, and the fowls of the heaven, and the beasts of the earth, to devour and destroy.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/15#v4)</sup> And I will cause them to be removed into all kingdoms of the earth, because of Manasseh the son of Hezekiah king of Judah, for that which he did in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/15#v5)</sup> For who shall have pity upon thee, O Jerusalem? or who shall bemoan thee? or who shall go aside to ask how thou doest?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/15#v6)</sup> Thou hast forsaken me, saith the Lord, thou art gone backward: therefore will I stretch out my hand against thee, and destroy thee; I am weary with repenting.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/15#v7)</sup> And I will fan them with a fan in the gates of the land; I will bereave them of children, I will destroy my people, since they return not from their ways.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/15#v8)</sup> Their widows are increased to me above the sand of the seas: I have brought upon them against the mother of the young men a spoiler at noonday: I have caused him to fall upon it suddenly, and terrors upon the city.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/15#v9)</sup> She that hath borne seven languisheth: she hath given up the ghost; her sun is gone down while it was yet day: she hath been ashamed and confounded: and the residue of them will I deliver to the sword before their enemies, saith the Lord.

- He appoints four kinds over them: the sword to slay, the dogs to tear, and the fowls and beasts to devour, and He will remove them into all kingdoms of the earth because of what Manasseh son of Hezekiah did in Jerusalem.
- He is weary with repenting and will bereave them of children because they return not from their ways; the mother of young men gets a spoiler at noonday, and she that bore seven languishes and gives up the ghost.
- That hit home; there was a sister last sabbath who had seven sons and was very much depressed. The scripture speaks to a real grief among the people.

**[Jeremiah 15:10-14](/bible/jeremiah/15#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/15#v10)</sup> Woe is me, my mother, that thou hast borne me a man of strife and a man of contention to the whole earth! I have neither lent on usury, nor men have lent to me on usury; yet every one of them doth curse me.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/15#v11)</sup> The Lord said, Verily it shall be well with thy remnant; verily I will cause the enemy to entreat thee well in the time of evil and in the time of affliction.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/15#v12)</sup> Shall iron break the northern iron and the steel?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/15#v13)</sup> Thy substance and thy treasures will I give to the spoil without price, and that for all thy sins, even in all thy borders.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/15#v14)</sup> And I will make thee to pass with thine enemies into a land which thou knowest not: for a fire is kindled in mine anger, which shall burn upon you.

- Jeremiah cries woe over being born a man of strife and contention, cursed by everyone though he lent to no one on usury. The Most High answers that it shall be well with his remnant.
- That remnant is those who are keeping the commandments. Their substance and treasures will be given to the spoil for all their sins, and they will pass with their enemies into a land they do not know.

**[Jeremiah 15:15-18](/bible/jeremiah/15#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/15#v15)</sup> O Lord, thou knowest: remember me, and visit me, and revenge me of my persecutors; take me not away in thy longsuffering: know that for thy sake I have suffered rebuke.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/15#v16)</sup> Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart: for I am called by thy name, O Lord God of hosts.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/15#v17)</sup> I sat not in the assembly of the mockers, nor rejoiced; I sat alone because of thy hand: for thou hast filled me with indignation.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/15#v18)</sup> Why is my pain perpetual, and my wound incurable, which refuseth to be healed? wilt thou be altogether unto me as a liar, and as waters that fail?

- Jeremiah asks the Most High to remember him, visit him, and revenge him of his persecutors, and not to take him away in His longsuffering, since for His sake he has suffered rebuke.
- His words were found and Jeremiah did eat them, and they were the joy and rejoicing of his heart, for he is called by the name of the Most High and sat alone, filled with indignation.
- He asks why his pain is perpetual and his wound refuses to be healed. This is what the prophets go through: we bring healing, order, structure and discipline to our people and get hated and treated badly, and sometimes these are the thoughts we have.

**[Jeremiah 15:19-21](/bible/jeremiah/15#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/15#v19)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord, If thou return, then will I bring thee again, and thou shalt stand before me: and if thou take forth the precious from the vile, thou shalt be as my mouth: let them return unto thee; but return not thou unto them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/15#v20)</sup> And I will make thee unto this people a fenced brasen wall: and they shall fight against thee, but they shall not prevail against thee: for I am with thee to save thee and to deliver thee, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/15#v21)</sup> And I will deliver thee out of the hand of the wicked, and I will redeem thee out of the hand of the terrible.

- If Jeremiah returns, the Most High will bring him again to stand before Him; if he takes the precious from the vile he shall be as His mouth. Let them return to him, but he is not to return to them.
- He will make Jeremiah a fenced brazen wall; they shall fight against him but not prevail, for the Most High is with him to save and deliver him.
- That is what we live on. When we go out teaching this bible, and when brothers and sisters apply the commandments to their everyday lives, people will come against us, but the Most High is our defense. As long as we keep the commandments and the faith of Christ, no one can prevail, and if they try they will fail.
