Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 32](/bible/jeremiah/32)

This session covers Jeremiah chapters 32 through 35 during the Babylonian captivity of the Israelites, a history also found in 2 Kings, 2 Chronicles, and the prophets Daniel and Ezekiel. While Jerusalem is besieged and Jeremiah is shut up in prison, the Most High commands him to buy a field as a sign that houses, fields, and vineyards shall be possessed again in the land after the seventy years. The reading moves through the prophecy of full restoration, the everlasting covenant and the branch of righteousness raised up to David, the broken covenant over the liberty of the servants, and the obedience of the Rechabites held up as a rebuke to Judah.

**[Jeremiah 32:1-5](/bible/jeremiah/32#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/32#v1)</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, which was the eighteenth year of Nebuchadrezzar.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/32#v2)</sup> For then the king of Babylon’s army besieged Jerusalem: and Jeremiah the prophet was shut up in the court of the prison, which was in the king of Judah’s house.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/32#v3)</sup> For Zedekiah king of Judah had shut him up, saying, Wherefore dost thou prophesy, and say, Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall take it;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/32#v4)</sup> And Zedekiah king of Judah shall not escape out of the hand of the Chaldeans, but shall surely be delivered into the hand of the king of Babylon, and shall speak with him mouth to mouth, and his eyes shall behold his eyes;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/32#v5)</sup> And he shall lead Zedekiah to Babylon, and there shall he be until I visit him, saith the Lord: though ye fight with the Chaldeans, ye shall not prosper.

- The word came to Jeremiah in the tenth year of Zedekiah king of Judah, the eighteenth year of Nebuchadnezzar, when the king of Babylon's army besieged Jerusalem and Jeremiah was shut up in the court of the prison.
- Zedekiah had imprisoned him for prophesying that the Most High would give the city into the hand of the king of Babylon; Zedekiah himself would not escape but would be led to Babylon, speak with him mouth to mouth, and behold his eyes.
- This was the word of the Most High that came through Jeremiah to the king, that though they fight against the Chaldeans they should not prosper.

> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/32#v7)</sup> Behold, Hanameel the son of Shallum thine uncle shall come unto thee, saying, Buy thee my field that is in Anathoth: for the right of redemption is thine to buy it.

Then I knew that this was the word of the Lord.

> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/32#v10)</sup> And I subscribed the evidence, and sealed it, and took witnesses, and weighed him the money in the balances.

- Hanameel his uncle's son came to Jeremiah in the court of the prison to sell him the field at Anathoth, for the right of redemption and inheritance was Jeremiah's; then he knew it was the word of the Most High.
- He bought the field, weighed him seventeen shekels of silver, subscribed the evidence, sealed it, and took witnesses, taking both the sealed evidence and the open according to the law and custom, and delivered it to Baruch the son of Neriah.
- Anathoth was in the country of Benjamin because the priests were given cities and suburbs to dwell in.

- He tells Jeremiah not to regard the people who say the two families the Most High chose He has now cast off and despised so they should be no more a nation; if His covenant with day and night stands, He will not cast away the seed of Jacob and David.
  - **[Romans 11:1-2](/bible/romans/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/11#v1)</sup> I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/11#v2)</sup> God hath not cast away his people which he foreknew. Wot ye not what the scripture saith of Elias? how he maketh intercession to God against Israel, saying,

    God has not cast away His people whom He foreknew, and we are the living proof, our hearts turned back to keeping the commandments and the faith of Christ without bodily eyes having seen Him.

# Jeremiah 34: Liberty Proclaimed and Then Revoked

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 34](/bible/jeremiah/34)

**[Jeremiah 34:1-7](/bible/jeremiah/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/34#v1)</sup> The word which came unto Jeremiah from the Lord, when Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and all his army, and all the kingdoms of the earth of his dominion, and all the people, fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities thereof, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/34#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; Go and speak to Zedekiah king of Judah, and tell him, Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I will give this city into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall burn it with fire:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/34#v3)</sup> And thou shalt not escape out of his hand, but shalt surely be taken, and delivered into his hand; and thine eyes shall behold the eyes of the king of Babylon, and he shall speak with thee mouth to mouth, and thou shalt go to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/34#v4)</sup> Yet hear the word of the Lord, O Zedekiah king of Judah; Thus saith the Lord of thee, Thou shalt not die by the sword:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/34#v5)</sup> But thou shalt die in peace: and with the burnings of thy fathers, the former kings which were before thee, so shall they burn odours for thee; and they will lament thee, saying, Ah lord! for I have pronounced the word, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/34#v6)</sup> Then Jeremiah the prophet spake all these words unto Zedekiah king of Judah in Jerusalem,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/34#v7)</sup> When the king of Babylon’s army fought against Jerusalem, and against all the cities of Judah that were left, against Lachish, and against Azekah: for these defenced cities remained of the cities of Judah.

- The word came when Nebuchadnezzar and all his army fought against Jerusalem: Jeremiah is to tell Zedekiah that the Most High will give the city into Babylon's hand to be burned with fire, and Zedekiah shall not escape.
- His eyes shall behold the king of Babylon's eyes and he shall speak with him mouth to mouth and go to Babylon, yet he shall not die by the sword but die in peace, with the burnings and lamentations of the former kings.
- Jeremiah spoke these words while Babylon's army fought against Jerusalem, Lachish, and Azekah, which alone remained of the defensed cities of Judah.

**[Jeremiah 34:8-11](/bible/jeremiah/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/34#v8)</sup> This is the word that came unto Jeremiah from the Lord, after that the king Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people which were at Jerusalem, to proclaim liberty unto them;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/34#v9)</sup> That every man should let his manservant, and every man his maidservant, being an Hebrew or an Hebrewess, go free; that none should serve himself of them, to wit, of a Jew his brother.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/34#v10)</sup> Now when all the princes, and all the people, which had entered into the covenant, heard that every one should let his manservant, and every one his maidservant, go free, that none should serve themselves of them any more, then they obeyed, and let them go.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/34#v11)</sup> But afterward they turned, and caused the servants and the handmaids, whom they had let go free, to return, and brought them into subjection for servants and for handmaids.

- Zedekiah had made a covenant with all the people at Jerusalem to proclaim liberty, that every man let his Hebrew manservant and maidservant go free and none serve himself of his Jewish brother.
- The princes and people obeyed and let them go, but afterward they turned and brought the servants and handmaids back into subjection.
  - **[Leviticus 25:9-10](/bible/leviticus/25#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/25#v9)</sup> Then shalt thou cause the trumpet of the jubile to sound on the tenth day of the seventh month, in the day of atonement shall ye make the trumpet sound throughout all your land.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/25#v10)</sup> And ye shall hallow the fiftieth year, and proclaim liberty throughout all the land unto all the inhabitants thereof: it shall be a jubile unto you; and ye shall return every man unto his possession, and ye shall return every man unto his family.

    The trumpet of the jubilee sounds on the day of atonement to hallow the fiftieth year and proclaim liberty, returning every man to his possession and to his family.

**[Jeremiah 34:12-16](/bible/jeremiah/34#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/34#v12)</sup> Therefore the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/34#v13)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel; I made a covenant with your fathers in the day that I brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondmen, saying,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/34#v14)</sup> At the end of seven years let ye go every man his brother an Hebrew, which hath been sold unto thee; and when he hath served thee six years, thou shalt let him go free from thee: but your fathers hearkened not unto me, neither inclined their ear.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/34#v15)</sup> And ye were now turned, and had done right in my sight, in proclaiming liberty every man to his neighbour; and ye had made a covenant before me in the house which is called by my name:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/34#v16)</sup> But ye turned and polluted my name, and caused every man his servant, and every man his handmaid, whom ye had set at liberty at their pleasure, to return, and brought them into subjection, to be unto you for servants and for handmaids.

- The Most High reminds them He made a covenant with their fathers when He brought them out of Egypt, out of the house of bondage, that at the end of seven years every man should let his Hebrew brother go free.
- The fathers hearkened not, and though this generation had turned and done right in proclaiming liberty, making a covenant before Him in the house called by His name, they turned and polluted His name.
- They caused every man to bring his servant and handmaid back into subjection to be servants and handmaids again.

    The commandment was that a Hebrew servant serve six years and in the seventh go out free.
  - **[Psalms 138:2](/bible/psalms/138#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/138#v2)</sup> I will worship toward thy holy temple, and praise thy name for thy lovingkindness and for thy truth: for thou hast magnified thy word above all thy name.

    Polluting His name was not mispronouncing a name but breaking His commandments, for He magnified His word above His name.

**[Jeremiah 34:17-18](/bible/jeremiah/34#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/34#v17)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Ye have not hearkened unto me, in proclaiming liberty, every one to his brother, and every man to his neighbour: behold, I proclaim a liberty for you, saith the Lord, to the sword, to the pestilence, and to the famine; and I will make you to be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/34#v18)</sup> And I will give the men that have transgressed my covenant, which have not performed the words of the covenant which they had made before me, when they cut the calf in twain, and passed between the parts thereof,

- He will give the men who transgressed His covenant, who cut the calf in twain and passed between the parts thereof, into the hand of their enemies.
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.

    He would scatter us among all people from one end of the earth to the other.

**[Jeremiah 34:19-22](/bible/jeremiah/34#v19)**

>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/34#v20)</sup> I will even give them into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life: and their dead bodies shall be for meat unto the fowls of the heaven, and to the beasts of the earth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/34#v21)</sup> And Zedekiah king of Judah and his princes will I give into the hand of their enemies, and into the hand of them that seek their life, and into the hand of the king of Babylon’s army, which are gone up from you.

# Jeremiah 35: The Obedience of the Rechabites

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 35](/bible/jeremiah/35)

**[Jeremiah 35:1-11](/bible/jeremiah/35#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/35#v1)</sup> The word which came unto Jeremiah from the Lord in the days of Jehoiakim the son of Josiah king of Judah, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/35#v2)</sup> Go unto the house of the Rechabites, and speak unto them, and bring them into the house of the Lord, into one of the chambers, and give them wine to drink.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/35#v3)</sup> Then I took Jaazaniah the son of Jeremiah, the son of Habaziniah, and his brethren, and all his sons, and the whole house of the Rechabites;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/35#v4)</sup> And I brought them into the house of the Lord, into the chamber of the sons of Hanan, the son of Igdaliah, a man of God, which was by the chamber of the princes, which was above the chamber of Maaseiah the son of Shallum, the keeper of the door:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/35#v5)</sup> And I set before the sons of the house of the Rechabites pots full of wine, and cups, and I said unto them, Drink ye wine.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/35#v6)</sup> But they said, We will drink no wine: for Jonadab the son of Rechab our father commanded us, saying, Ye shall drink no wine, neither ye, nor your sons for ever:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/35#v7)</sup> Neither shall ye build house, nor sow seed, nor plant vineyard, nor have any: but all your days ye shall dwell in tents; that ye may live many days in the land where ye be strangers.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/35#v8)</sup> Thus have we obeyed the voice of Jonadab the son of Rechab our father in all that he hath charged us, to drink no wine all our days, we, our wives, our sons, nor our daughters;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/35#v9)</sup> Nor to build houses for us to dwell in: neither have we vineyard, nor field, nor seed:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/35#v10)</sup> But we have dwelt in tents, and have obeyed, and done according to all that Jonadab our father commanded us.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/35#v11)</sup> But it came to pass, when Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon came up into the land, that we said, Come, and let us go to Jerusalem for fear of the army of the Chaldeans, and for fear of the army of the Syrians: so we dwell at Jerusalem.

- In the days of Jehoiakim the Most High sent Jeremiah to the house of the Rechabites to bring them into the temple chambers and set wine before them to drink.
- Jaazaniah and the whole house of the Rechabites refused, saying Jonadab the son of Rechab their father commanded them to drink no wine, build no house, sow no seed, and plant no vineyard, but dwell in tents all their days that they might live long in the land.
- They had obeyed the voice of Jonadab in all things, dwelling in tents, and only came to Jerusalem for fear of the army of the Chaldeans and the Syrians.

**[Jeremiah 35:12-19](/bible/jeremiah/35#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/35#v12)</sup> Then came the word of the Lord unto Jeremiah, saying,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/35#v13)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Go and tell the men of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, Will ye not receive instruction to hearken to my words? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/35#v14)</sup> The words of Jonadab the son of Rechab, that he commanded his sons not to drink wine, are performed; for unto this day they drink none, but obey their father’s commandment: notwithstanding I have spoken unto you, rising early and speaking; but ye hearkened not unto me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/35#v15)</sup> I have sent also unto you all my servants the prophets, rising up early and sending them, saying, Return ye now every man from his evil way, and amend your doings, and go not after other gods to serve them, and ye shall dwell in the land which I have given to you and to your fathers: but ye have not inclined your ear, nor hearkened unto me.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/35#v16)</sup> Because the sons of Jonadab the son of Rechab have performed the commandment of their father, which he commanded them; but this people hath not hearkened unto me:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/35#v17)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will bring upon Judah and upon all the inhabitants of Jerusalem all the evil that I have pronounced against them: because I have spoken unto them, but they have not heard; and I have called unto them, but they have not answered.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/35#v18)</sup> And Jeremiah said unto the house of the Rechabites, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Because ye have obeyed the commandment of Jonadab your father, and kept all his precepts, and done according unto all that he hath commanded you:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/35#v19)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Jonadab the son of Rechab shall not want a man to stand before me for ever.

- The Most High tells Jeremiah to point Judah to the Rechabites: the sons of Jonadab kept their father's command to drink no wine to this day, but this people would not hearken to Him though He rose early and spoke.
- He sent all His servants the prophets calling every man to return from his evil way, amend his doings, and go not after other gods, promising they would dwell in the land, yet they would not incline their ear.
- Because the Rechabites obeyed the commandment of their father, Jonadab shall not want a man to stand before Him for ever; but because Judah would not hear or answer, He will bring upon them all the evil He pronounced.
