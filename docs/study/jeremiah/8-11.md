Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 8](/bible/jeremiah/8)

This session covers [Jeremiah 8](/bible/jeremiah/8) through 11, moving through the reading in verse order. The aim here is to promote the daily reading of the four chapters, making a point where a point needs to be made. The thread running through these chapters is a people under judgment who refuse to return: their bones scattered, their false peace exposed, their idols shown to be nothing, and their covenant broken. Passover is approaching, so the call is to examine yourself and purge the leaven out of your own spirit, not just out of your house.

**[Jeremiah 8:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/8#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/8#v1)</sup> At that time, saith the Lord, they shall bring out the bones of the kings of Judah, and the bones of his princes, and the bones of the priests, and the bones of the prophets, and the bones of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, out of their graves:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/8#v2)</sup> And they shall spread them before the sun, and the moon, and all the host of heaven, whom they have loved, and whom they have served, and after whom they have walked, and whom they have sought, and whom they have worshipped: they shall not be gathered, nor be buried; they shall be for dung upon the face of the earth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/8#v3)</sup> And death shall be chosen rather than life by all the residue of them that remain of this evil family, which remain in all the places whither I have driven them, saith the Lord of hosts.

- This is a good present to use against the comedic brothers, the biblical doubters and heretics who mock the Bible asking where the bones of David, of Jeremiah, of Moses are. The scripture answers it: the bones of our forefathers were scattered and not buried, spread all over the earth. That is why you will not find them.

**[Jeremiah 8:4-6](/bible/jeremiah/8#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/8#v4)</sup> Moreover thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord; Shall they fall, and not arise? shall he turn away, and not return?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/8#v5)</sup> Why then is this people of Jerusalem slidden back by a perpetual backsliding? they hold fast deceit, they refuse to return.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/8#v6)</sup> I hearkened and heard, but they spake not aright: no man repented him of his wickedness, saying, What have I done? every one turned to his course, as the horse rusheth into the battle.

- We do not understand how much mercy the Most High has shown the nation of Israel. Read from the beginning up until today and you see much mercy; we deserve all the persecution we get because we have a perpetual backsliding and refuse to return.
- Our people do not want to acknowledge their sins. No man repents saying "what have I done"; everyone turns to his own course like a horse rushing into battle.

**[Jeremiah 8:7-10](/bible/jeremiah/8#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/8#v7)</sup> Yea, the stork in the heaven knoweth her appointed times; and the turtle and the crane and the swallow observe the time of their coming; but my people know not the judgment of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/8#v8)</sup> How do ye say, We are wise, and the law of the Lord is with us? Lo, certainly in vain made he it; the pen of the scribes is in vain.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/8#v9)</sup> The wise men are ashamed, they are dismayed and taken: lo, they have rejected the word of the Lord; and what wisdom is in them?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/8#v10)</sup> Therefore will I give their wives unto others, and their fields to them that shall inherit them: for every one from the least even unto the greatest is given to covetousness, from the prophet even unto the priest every one dealeth falsely.

- Even the stork, the turtle, the crane and the swallow know their appointed times, but the Most High's people know not His judgment. The people claim to be wise and to have the law, but they have rejected the word of the Lord, so what wisdom is in them.

**[Jeremiah 8:11-13](/bible/jeremiah/8#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/8#v11)</sup> For they have healed the hurt of the daughter of my people slightly, saying, Peace, peace; when there is no peace.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/8#v12)</sup> Were they ashamed when they had committed abomination? nay, they were not at all ashamed, neither could they blush: therefore shall they fall among them that fall: in the time of their visitation they shall be cast down, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/8#v13)</sup> I will surely consume them, saith the Lord: there shall be no grapes on the vine, nor figs on the fig tree, and the leaf shall fade; and the things that I have given them shall pass away from them.

- The prophets and priests healed the hurt of the daughter of the people only slightly, saying peace, peace when there is no peace.
- They committed abomination yet were not ashamed and could not even blush; therefore they shall fall in the time of their visitation.

**[Jeremiah 8:14-20](/bible/jeremiah/8#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/8#v14)</sup> Why do we sit still? assemble yourselves, and let us enter into the defenced cities, and let us be silent there: for the Lord our God hath put us to silence, and given us water of gall to drink, because we have sinned against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/8#v15)</sup> We looked for peace, but no good came; and for a time of health, and behold trouble!
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/8#v16)</sup> The snorting of his horses was heard from Dan: the whole land trembled at the sound of the neighing of his strong ones; for they are come, and have devoured the land, and all that is in it; the city, and those that dwell therein.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/8#v17)</sup> For, behold, I will send serpents, cockatrices, among you, which will not be charmed, and they shall bite you, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/8#v18)</sup> When I would comfort myself against sorrow, my heart is faint in me.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/8#v19)</sup> Behold the voice of the cry of the daughter of my people because of them that dwell in a far country: Is not the Lord in Zion? is not her king in her? Why have they provoked me to anger with their graven images, and with strange vanities?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/8#v20)</sup> The harvest is past, the summer is ended, and we are not saved.

- Look at all the desolation and trouble we are going through today in America in this captivity. Just as Jeremiah's people were under the Babylonians, today we are under the mystery Babylonians.
- The harvest is passed, the summer is ended, and we are not saved. Our people think they are already redeemed and saved in the blood of Jesus, but no. We are still being murdered in the streets, locked up, persecuted, last hired and first fired, oppressed systematically and hated. We still have no land, no country, no standard, no god of our own, and the majority of Israelites do not even know they are Israelites and worship a white Jesus. We are not saved.

**[Jeremiah 8:21-22](/bible/jeremiah/8#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/8#v21)</sup> For the hurt of the daughter of my people am I hurt; I am black; astonishment hath taken hold on me.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/8#v22)</sup> Is there no balm in Gilead; is there no physician there? why then is not the health of the daughter of my people recovered?

- For the hurt of the daughter of the people the prophet is hurt and black astonishment takes hold of him. Is there no balm in Gilead, is there no physician there, so why is the health of the people not recovered.

# Jeremiah 9: What to Glory In

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 9](/bible/jeremiah/9)

**[Jeremiah 9:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/9#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/9#v1)</sup> Oh that my head were waters, and mine eyes a fountain of tears, that I might weep day and night for the slain of the daughter of my people!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/9#v2)</sup> Oh that I had in the wilderness a lodging place of wayfaring men; that I might leave my people, and go from them! for they be all adulterers, an assembly of treacherous men.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/9#v3)</sup> And they bend their tongues like their bow for lies: but they are not valiant for the truth upon the earth; for they proceed from evil to evil, and they know not me, saith the Lord.

- The prophet wishes his head were waters and his eyes a fountain of tears to weep for the slain of his people. He wants a lodging place in the wilderness to leave them, because they are all adulterers and treacherous men who bend their tongues like a bow for lies and proceed from evil to evil.

**[Jeremiah 9:4-6](/bible/jeremiah/9#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/9#v4)</sup> Take ye heed every one of his neighbour, and trust ye not in any brother: for every brother will utterly supplant, and every neighbour will walk with slanders.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/9#v5)</sup> And they will deceive every one his neighbour, and will not speak the truth: they have taught their tongue to speak lies, and weary themselves to commit iniquity.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/9#v6)</sup> Thine habitation is in the midst of deceit; through deceit they refuse to know me, saith the Lord.

- Trust not in any brother, for every brother will supplant and every neighbor walks with slanders. We have seen this in IUIC and in many of the other camps.
- They have taught their tongue to speak lies and weary themselves to commit iniquity; through deceit they refuse to know the Most High.
  - **[John 8:19](/bible/john/8#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/john/8#v19)</sup> Then said they unto him, Where is thy Father? Jesus answered, Ye neither know me, nor my Father: if ye had known me, ye should have known my Father also.

    To refuse to know Him through deceit is to have no relationship with Him.

**[Jeremiah 9:7-9](/bible/jeremiah/9#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/9#v7)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, Behold, I will melt them, and try them; for how shall I do for the daughter of my people?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/9#v8)</sup> Their tongue is as an arrow shot out; it speaketh deceit: one speaketh peaceably to his neighbour with his mouth, but in heart he layeth his wait.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/9#v9)</sup> Shall I not visit them for these things? saith the Lord: shall not my soul be avenged on such a nation as this?

- The Most High will melt and try them, because their tongue is an arrow shooting deceit. A man speaks peaceably to his neighbor with his mouth but in his heart lies in wait, thinking of a plot or scheme to destroy, steal from, or overcome him. That is how wicked our people are.

**[Jeremiah 9:10-16](/bible/jeremiah/9#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/9#v10)</sup> For the mountains will I take up a weeping and wailing, and for the habitations of the wilderness a lamentation, because they are burned up, so that none can pass through them; neither can men hear the voice of the cattle; both the fowl of the heavens and the beast are fled; they are gone.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/9#v11)</sup> And I will make Jerusalem heaps, and a den of dragons; and I will make the cities of Judah desolate, without an inhabitant.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/9#v12)</sup> Who is the wise man, that may understand this? and who is he to whom the mouth of the Lord hath spoken, that he may declare it, for what the land perisheth and is burned up like a wilderness, that none passeth through?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/9#v13)</sup> And the Lord saith, Because they have forsaken my law which I set before them, and have not obeyed my voice, neither walked therein;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/9#v14)</sup> But have walked after the imagination of their own heart, and after Baalim, which their fathers taught them:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/9#v15)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will feed them, even this people, with wormwood, and give them water of gall to drink.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/9#v16)</sup> I will scatter them also among the heathen, whom neither they nor their fathers have known: and I will send a sword after them, till I have consumed them.

- Because they forsook His law, did not obey His voice, and walked after the imagination of their own heart and after Baal, He will feed them with wormwood, give them water of gall, scatter them among the heathen, and send a sword after them until they are consumed.

**[Jeremiah 9:17-21](/bible/jeremiah/9#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/9#v17)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts, Consider ye, and call for the mourning women, that they may come; and send for cunning women, that they may come:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/9#v18)</sup> And let them make haste, and take up a wailing for us, that our eyes may run down with tears, and our eyelids gush out with waters.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/9#v19)</sup> For a voice of wailing is heard out of Zion, How are we spoiled! we are greatly confounded, because we have forsaken the land, because our dwellings have cast us out.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/9#v20)</sup> Yet hear the word of the Lord, O ye women, and let your ear receive the word of his mouth, and teach your daughters wailing, and every one her neighbour lamentation.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/9#v21)</sup> For death is come up into our windows, and is entered into our palaces, to cut off the children from without, and the young men from the streets.

- Call for the mourning women to take up a wailing, for death has come up into the windows and into the palaces to cut off the children from the streets and the young men from the squares. This sounds like America, like the south side of Chicago, north Dallas, and southwest Houston.

**[Jeremiah 9:22-24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/9#v22)</sup> Speak, Thus saith the Lord, Even the carcases of men shall fall as dung upon the open field, and as the handful after the harvestman, and none shall gather them.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/9#v23)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, neither let the mighty man glory in his might, let not the rich man glory in his riches:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/9#v24)</sup> But let him that glorieth glory in this, that he understandeth and knoweth me, that I am the Lord which exercise lovingkindness, judgment, and righteousness, in the earth: for in these things I delight, saith the Lord.

- Let not the wise man glory in his wisdom, the mighty in his might, or the rich in his riches. Carnal things like looks, strength, and money all fade.
- Glory instead that you understand and know the Most High: that you have been awakened in these last days, shown the truth, the scriptures, the commandments, and what God requires to be saved from the destruction prophesied to come.
- You can know you are an Israelite, know the twelve feathers, the seven heads and ten horns, the mysteries of Genesis, and every precept, but if you have hatred for your brother or sister, if you are a slanderer, gossiper, liar, or fornicator, you will not see the kingdom, only fire. There is a difference between a prophet and a parakeet; anyone can mimic scriptures for a show while being unprofitable to himself.

**[Jeremiah 9:25-26](/bible/jeremiah/9#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/9#v25)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that I will punish all them which are circumcised with the uncircumcised;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/jeremiah/9#v26)</sup> Egypt, and Judah, and Edom, and the children of Ammon, and Moab, and all that are in the utmost corners, that dwell in the wilderness: for all these nations are uncircumcised, and all the house of Israel are uncircumcised in the heart.

- The days come when He will punish the circumcised along with the uncircumcised, because all the house of Israel is uncircumcised in the heart. It does not matter if you are circumcised after the manner of Abraham; if you fornicate or hold hatred, your circumcision is made uncircumcision through your sin. Circumcision only means something when you are obedient to the words and statutes of the Lord.

# Jeremiah 10: The Idols Are Nothing, the Lord Is the True God

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 10](/bible/jeremiah/10)

**[Jeremiah 10:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/10#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/10#v1)</sup> Hear ye the word which the Lord speaketh unto you, O house of Israel:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/10#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Learn not the way of the heathen, and be not dismayed at the signs of heaven; for the heathen are dismayed at them.

- Learn not the way of the heathen and be not dismayed at the signs of heaven. Astrology, numerology, horoscopes, and all this mysticism about the stars, moon, and their alignments cannot be proven. The Most High created those things for a purpose, not for you to worship or fear. People pay more attention to their horoscopes than to "thou shalt not kill," "thou shalt not steal," and "thou shalt not commit adultery."

**[Jeremiah 10:3-5](/bible/jeremiah/10#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/10#v3)</sup> For the customs of the people are vain: for one cutteth a tree out of the forest, the work of the hands of the workman, with the axe.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/10#v4)</sup> They deck it with silver and with gold; they fasten it with nails and with hammers, that it move not.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/10#v5)</sup> They are upright as the palm tree, but speak not: they must needs be borne, because they cannot go. Be not afraid of them; for they cannot do evil, neither also is it in them to do good.

- The custom of cutting a tree out of the forest, decking it with silver and gold, and fastening it with nails and hammers so it does not move is the Christmas tree, the custom of modern Christmas.
- These idols are upright as a palm tree but cannot speak, must be carried because they cannot walk, and can do neither evil nor good; do not be afraid of them.

**[Jeremiah 10:6-10](/bible/jeremiah/10#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/10#v6)</sup> Forasmuch as there is none like unto thee, O Lord; thou art great, and thy name is great in might.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/10#v7)</sup> Who would not fear thee, O King of nations? for to thee doth it appertain: forasmuch as among all the wise men of the nations, and in all their kingdoms, there is none like unto thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/10#v8)</sup> But they are altogether brutish and foolish: the stock is a doctrine of vanities.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/10#v9)</sup> Silver spread into plates is brought from Tarshish, and gold from Uphaz, the work of the workman, and of the hands of the founder: blue and purple is their clothing: they are all the work of cunning men.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/10#v10)</sup> But the Lord is the true God, he is the living God, and an everlasting king: at his wrath the earth shall tremble, and the nations shall not be able to abide his indignation.

- There is none like the Most High; He is great and His name is great in might, King of nations. The other nations and their wise men are all together brutish and foolish, their idols a doctrine of vanities. But the Lord is the true God, the living God and everlasting King, at whose wrath the earth trembles.

**[Jeremiah 10:11-16](/bible/jeremiah/10#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/10#v11)</sup> Thus shall ye say unto them, The gods that have not made the heavens and the earth, even they shall perish from the earth, and from under these heavens.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/10#v12)</sup> He hath made the earth by his power, he hath established the world by his wisdom, and hath stretched out the heavens by his discretion.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/10#v13)</sup> When he uttereth his voice, there is a multitude of waters in the heavens, and he causeth the vapours to ascend from the ends of the earth; he maketh lightnings with rain, and bringeth forth the wind out of his treasures.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/10#v14)</sup> Every man is brutish in his knowledge: every founder is confounded by the graven image: for his molten image is falsehood, and there is no breath in them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/10#v15)</sup> They are vanity, and the work of errors: in the time of their visitation they shall perish.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/10#v16)</sup> The portion of Jacob is not like them: for he is the former of all things; and Israel is the rod of his inheritance: The Lord of hosts is his name.

- The gods that did not make the heaven and earth shall perish. He made the earth by His power, established the world by His wisdom, and stretched out the heavens; every man is brutish in his knowledge before Him.
- The portion of Jacob is not like the idols, for He is the former of all things and Israel is the rod of His inheritance. Anything good the nations do they got from the children of Israel; when you go into the history, they learned it from us.

**[Jeremiah 10:17-21](/bible/jeremiah/10#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/10#v17)</sup> Gather up thy wares out of the land, O inhabitant of the fortress.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/10#v18)</sup> For thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will sling out the inhabitants of the land at this once, and will distress them, that they may find it so.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/10#v19)</sup> Woe is me for my hurt! my wound is grievous: but I said, Truly this is a grief, and I must bear it.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/10#v20)</sup> My tabernacle is spoiled, and all my cords are broken: my children are gone forth of me, and they are not: there is none to stretch forth my tent any more, and to set up my curtains.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/10#v21)</sup> For the pastors are become brutish, and have not sought the Lord: therefore they shall not prosper, and all their flocks shall be scattered.

- He will sling out the inhabitants of the land at once and distress them. The tabernacle is spoiled, the cords broken, the children gone, because the pastors have become brutish and have not sought the Lord; therefore their flock is scattered.

**[Jeremiah 10:22-25](/bible/jeremiah/10#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/10#v22)</sup> Behold, the noise of the bruit is come, and a great commotion out of the north country, to make the cities of Judah desolate, and a den of dragons.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/10#v23)</sup> O Lord, I know that the way of man is not in himself: it is not in man that walketh to direct his steps.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/jeremiah/10#v24)</sup> O Lord, correct me, but with judgment; not in thine anger, lest thou bring me to nothing.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/jeremiah/10#v25)</sup> Pour out thy fury upon the heathen that know thee not, and upon the families that call not on thy name: for they have eaten up Jacob, and devoured him, and consumed him, and have made his habitation desolate.

- A great commotion comes out of the north country to make the cities of Judah desolate. The prophet knows the way of man is not in himself, so he asks to be corrected with judgment, not in anger, and prays that fury be poured out on the heathen that know not the Most High and have devoured Jacob.

# Jeremiah 11: The Broken Covenant

Read the chapter → [Jeremiah 11](/bible/jeremiah/11)

**[Jeremiah 11:1-3](/bible/jeremiah/11#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/11#v1)</sup> The word that came to Jeremiah from the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/11#v2)</sup> Hear ye the words of this covenant, and speak unto the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/11#v3)</sup> And say thou unto them, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel; Cursed be the man that obeyeth not the words of this covenant,

- Hear the words of this covenant: cursed be the man that obeys not its words.
  - **[Deuteronomy 27:26](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v26)</sup> Cursed be he that confirmeth not all the words of this law to do them. And all the people shall say, Amen.

    Cursed is everyone that confirms not all the words of this law to do them.
  - **[Galatians 3:10](/bible/galatians/3#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/galatians/3#v10)</sup> For as many as are of the works of the law are under the curse: for it is written, Cursed is every one that continueth not in all things which are written in the book of the law to do them.

    As many as are of the works of the law are under the curse without continuing in all things written.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

    All these curses come on those who will not obey the voice of the Lord.

**[Jeremiah 11:4-6](/bible/jeremiah/11#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/11#v4)</sup> Which I commanded your fathers in the day that I brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, from the iron furnace, saying, Obey my voice, and do them, according to all which I command you: so shall ye be my people, and I will be your God:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/11#v5)</sup> That I may perform the oath which I have sworn unto your fathers, to give them a land flowing with milk and honey, as it is this day. Then answered I, and said, So be it, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/11#v6)</sup> Then the Lord said unto me, Proclaim all these words in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem, saying, Hear ye the words of this covenant, and do them.

- He commanded the fathers from the iron furnace of Egypt to obey His voice, so they would be His people and He their God, to perform the oath to give a land flowing with milk and honey.
- Just as you see the brothers in purple and gold out on the corners teaching every day in IUIC, the prophets did the exact same thing, proclaiming the words of the covenant in the streets. The thing that has been is that which shall be.

**[Jeremiah 11:7-8](/bible/jeremiah/11#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jeremiah/11#v7)</sup> For I earnestly protested unto your fathers in the day that I brought them up out of the land of Egypt, even unto this day, rising early and protesting, saying, Obey my voice.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/11#v8)</sup> Yet they obeyed not, nor inclined their ear, but walked every one in the imagination of their evil heart: therefore I will bring upon them all the words of this covenant, which I commanded them to do; but they did them not.

- He earnestly protested to the fathers from the day He brought them out of Egypt, rising early and saying obey My voice, yet they obeyed not but walked in the imagination of their evil heart; therefore He brings on them all the words of this covenant.

**[Jeremiah 11:9-11](/bible/jeremiah/11#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/11#v9)</sup> And the Lord said unto me, A conspiracy is found among the men of Judah, and among the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/11#v10)</sup> They are turned back to the iniquities of their forefathers, which refused to hear my words; and they went after other gods to serve them: the house of Israel and the house of Judah have broken my covenant which I made with their fathers.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/11#v11)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will bring evil upon them, which they shall not be able to escape; and though they shall cry unto me, I will not hearken unto them.

- A conspiracy is found among the men of Judah; they turned back to the iniquities of their fathers and went after other gods. Therefore He will bring evil they cannot escape, and though they cry to Him He will not hearken. This is the people crying and praying only after Little Ray Ray gets shot, when Ray Ray was a drug dealer, a fornicator, and a murderer, and the no-snitching mentality shielded a killer. Now they cry and the Lord says He will not hear them.

> <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/11#v13)</sup> For according to the number of thy cities were thy gods, O Judah; and according to the number of the streets of Jerusalem have ye set up altars to that shameful thing, even altars to burn incense unto Baal.

> <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/11#v16)</sup> The Lord called thy name, A green olive tree, fair, and of goodly fruit: with the noise of a great tumult he hath kindled fire upon it, and the branches of it are broken.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/11#v17)</sup> For the Lord of hosts, that planted thee, hath pronounced evil against thee, for the evil of the house of Israel and of the house of Judah, which they have done against themselves to provoke me to anger in offering incense unto Baal.
