**[Jeremiah 50:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/50#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/50#v1)</sup> The word that the Lord spake against Babylon and against the land of the Chaldeans by Jeremiah the prophet.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/50#v2)</sup> Declare ye among the nations, and publish, and set up a standard; publish, and conceal not: say, Babylon is taken, Bel is confounded, Merodach is broken in pieces; her idols are confounded, her images are broken in pieces.

- This is the word the Lord spoke against Babylon and the land of the Chaldeans. Declare among the nations, publish and set up a standard: Babylon is taken, Bel is confounded, and her idols are broken in pieces.
- Set up a standard means lift up the greatest standard ever placed on earth, the Bible. The laws show us where we are evil and where we can do better; the Bible is our law and our banner. The scriptures are twofold, so this speaks against ancient Babylon and against modern-day Babylon, which is America.
  - **[Isaiah 13:2](/bible/isaiah/13#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/13#v2)</sup> Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles.

    Lift up a banner upon the high mountain and exalt the voice; the banner is the Bible, and we cry aloud for the people to raise their standard by keeping the laws.
  - **[Job 11:6](/bible/job/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/job/11#v6)</sup> And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.

    The secrets of wisdom are double, so the prophecy applies both to ancient Babylon and to America.

- Out of the north comes a nation against Babylon to make her land desolate. None shall dwell therein; both man and beast remove and depart.

**[Jeremiah 50:4-5](/bible/jeremiah/50#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/50#v4)</sup> In those days, and in that time, saith the Lord, the children of Israel shall come, they and the children of Judah together, going and weeping: they shall go, and seek the Lord their God.

- They ask the way to Zion with their faces that direction, saying, come and let us join ourselves to the Lord in a perpetual covenant. There will come a day when we stop seeking the white man through protesting, marching, and voting, and are gathered together only by the word of God.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**

    In their affliction they will seek the Most High early; trouble is what turns Israel back to him.
