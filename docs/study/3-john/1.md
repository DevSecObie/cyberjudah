## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 3 John 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 9-11, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[3 John 1:9-10](/bible/3-john/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/3-john/1#v9)</sup> I wrote unto the church: but Diotrephes, who loveth to have the preeminence among them, receiveth us not.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/3-john/1#v10)</sup> Wherefore, if I come, I will remember his deeds which he doeth, prating against us with malicious words: and not content therewith, neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and forbiddeth them that would, and casteth them out of the church.

- “but Diotrephes, who loveth to have the preeminence among them, receiveth us not.” “prating against us with malicious words”. He's talking against the leaders, the elders, Peter and them, the men who helped set him up. Now he's highheaded, highminded, sitting on his high horse. He's the HNC, talking crap to the men who set him up as an elder of the school. I don't care if they're the damn disciples of Christ; I don't care if they walked with Christ; you're in my church. That's how he's talking.
- “and not content therewith”. He's not content being the leader over there. “neither doth he himself receive the brethren, and forbiddeth them that would, and casteth them out of the church.” He doesn't want the apostles coming into the church, and he doesn't want the brothers receiving them either. If you go out there to see them because they walked with Christ, don't come back in my building. These same demons are back on this earth, prideful and arrogant.
- “Wherefore, if I come, I will remember his deeds which he doeth”. Those malicious words against the leaders, refusing the brethren and throwing out the ones who would receive them—that's what he'll remember.
- Here is another type of hireling: Diotrephes. “I wrote unto the church: but Diotrephes, who loveth to have the preeminence among them, receiveth us not.” The apostles set him up, and he made it appear he was in the truth.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 16:18](/bible/proverbs/16#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/16#v18)</sup> Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.

    “Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.” That's what happened with that brother. He got prideful and destroyed his own soul. The Lord is going to destroy spirits like that. He acted like he had good intentions, but when the apostles came he wouldn't let them in. Why? He's scared the congregation will follow them, scared he'll lose his following. It's about him, not building up the people's minds and spirits. The people in there are worshipping him. Right behind all that pride, your fall is coming.
  - **[Colossians 1:18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)</sup> And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.

    There is only one preeminent, and that is Christ. He is the King, not you, not me. “And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.”

**[3 John 1:11](/bible/3-john/1#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/3-john/1#v11)</sup> Beloved, follow not that which is evil, but that which is good. He that doeth good is of God: but he that doeth evil hath not seen God.

- “Beloved, follow not that which is evil, but that which is good.” Don't follow that evil. Turn around and do the right thing; follow what's good. “He that doeth good is of God: but he that doeth evil hath not seen God.” Even with fringes and a border of blue, supposed to be repentant, he hasn't seen God. He should welcome them, be happy they came: this is my time to get built up and talk to the men who walked with Christ. Instead he won't let them in and tells the brothers not to come back if they go out to them. That's how evil and prideful these demons are. They use good intentions to cover their evil.
