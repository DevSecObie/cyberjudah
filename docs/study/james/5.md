## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on James 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7-16, 19-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[James 5:7](/bible/james/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/james/5#v7)</sup> Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord. Behold, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth, and hath long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain.

- “Be patient therefore, brethren, unto the coming of the Lord”: all of us, men and women, let us all be patient for the coming of the Lord God.
- “Behold, the husbandman waiteth for the precious fruit of the earth”: the husbandman is the man that plants crops. He plants seed today, and he’s got to wait till that particular season comes for that seed to sprout and grow. So he “hath long patience for it, until he receive the early and latter rain”: the early crops at the beginning of the season and the crops at the end of the season.

**[James 5:8](/bible/james/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/james/5#v8)</sup> Be ye also patient; stablish your hearts: for the coming of the Lord draweth nigh.

- “Be ye also patient; stablish your hearts”: James is telling us we got to be patient like that husbandman, just like that. “For the coming of the Lord draweth nigh”: it comes near. Understand that thing.

**[James 5:9](/bible/james/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/james/5#v9)</sup> Grudge not one against another, brethren, lest ye be condemned: behold, the judge standeth before the door.

- “Grudge not one against another, brethren, lest ye be condemned.” The root of a grudge is hatred. You got many Israelite camps who have a grudge against Israel United in Christ, not that we’ve done anything to them, but because God is showing us favor and not them: ‘y’all are getting the audience, y’all are getting the people… let’s set up camp right next to them, let’s do something evil against them.’ These camps that have grudges against us for no reason will be condemned by God.
- So brothers, we can’t roll like that. We are the sons and daughters of God; we can’t have grudges. Those are our brothers, those are our sisters.
- It goes for husband and wife too. When you’re single and in a congregation holding grudges, you will be condemned. When you get married and have a grudge against your spouse, you will be condemned. Israelite camp, you have a grudge against another camp, you will be condemned.
- “Behold, the judge standeth before the door”: the Lord is the judge, and he stands before the door.

**[James 5:10](/bible/james/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/james/5#v10)</sup> Take, my brethren, the prophets, who have spoken in the name of the Lord, for an example of suffering affliction, and of patience.

- “Take, my brethren, the prophets, who have spoken in the name of the Lord, for an example of suffering affliction, and of patience”: consider the prophets who have spoken and prophesied in the name of the Lord, as an example of suffering affliction and of patience. Like who? Like the forefather Job.

**[James 5:11](/bible/james/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/james/5#v11)</sup> Behold, we count them happy which endure. Ye have heard of the patience of Job, and have seen the end of the Lord; that the Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.

- “Behold, we count them happy which endure. Ye have heard of the patience of Job”: he gives you an example in case you couldn’t think of one.
- “And have seen the end of the Lord”: you saw what the Lord did at the end, and we’re going to touch on that. “That the Lord is very pitiful, and of tender mercy.” So we got to remember Job in this truth, brothers and sisters: how the Lord was very merciful and pitiful upon Job.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 5:3-5](/bible/romans/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/romans/5#v3)</sup> And not only so, but we glory in tribulations also: knowing that tribulation worketh patience;
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/5#v4)</sup> And patience, experience; and experience, hope:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/5#v5)</sup> And hope maketh not ashamed; because the love of God is shed abroad in our hearts by the Holy Ghost which is given unto us.

    Here’s more examples. “And not only so, but we glory in tribulations also.” Why would Christ say that through the prophet Paul? “Knowing that tribulation worketh patience”: that’s why. Tribulation teaches us patience.
  - **[Acts 14:22](/bible/acts/14#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/acts/14#v22)</sup> Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

    “Confirming the souls of the disciples”: our job is to confirm your souls as well. “And exhorting them to continue in the faith”: our job is to exhort every man and every woman in this truth to continue in this faith. Do not leave this truth. Don’t let it be another 2018, and all them people now are trying to come back. You don’t want to be in that number.

**[James 5:12](/bible/james/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/james/5#v12)</sup> But above all things, my brethren, swear not, neither by heaven, neither by the earth, neither by any other oath: but let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation.

- “But above all things, my brethren, swear not, neither by heaven, neither by the earth, neither by any other oath: but let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation.” When you say yes, I'm going to do it, do it. When you say no, you can't, then don't. But never say yes, get paid to do it, and then not do it.
- Condemnation: the state of being condemned; to condemn is to declare to be reprehensible, wrong, or evil, usually after weighing evidence and without reservation. The Lord is going to judge you. He will review your case like a docket in court: how do you plead? Not guilty. Bring the witness. You didn't know they had witnesses, but the Lord knows everything. The eyes of the Lord are in every place, beholding the evil and the good, and a little bird carries the news.
- You do evil in a different state with someone we never met, and another brother goes to that state for a job and meets that same person. What are the odds? When people are just minding their business and your evil comes up without anybody asking one question, that should make you reflect: the Most High is doing it that way because he wants you to get right. He could have let you do it to the wrong person, and we would find out when you were six feet under, shot in the back over a hundred grand. You can sit at home and hate me, or see that the Lord wants to help you. Recognize your mistakes, apologize, make amends, and walk in the spirit so that blessing may come your way.
- “Let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)</sup> But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

    Matthew says it in a way you might get it, showing why you fall into condemnation: “But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.”
  - **[Colossians 3:9](/bible/colossians/3#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/colossians/3#v9)</sup> Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds;

    “Lie not one to another”. It is forbidden for us to lie one to another. We must be men of character, men of honor, because the world must look at us and say surely this is a wise and understanding people.

**[James 5:13](/bible/james/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/james/5#v13)</sup> Is any among you afflicted? let him pray. Is any merry? let him sing psalms.

- “Is any among you afflicted? let him pray”: pray to the Lord.

**[James 5:14-15](/bible/james/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/james/5#v14)</sup> Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/james/5#v15)</sup> And the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up; and if he have committed sins, they shall be forgiven him.

- “Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord: And the prayer of faith shall save the sick, and the Lord shall raise him up; and if he have committed sins, they shall be forgiven him.” The prayer of what? The prayer of faith. There is faith again: it is the prayer of faith that saves the sick.

**[James 5:16](/bible/james/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/james/5#v16)</sup> Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

- No one taught us how to process emotion. Men are frowned upon for expressing how they feel; we are told to suppress, to be strong, not to break down. So sadness gets buried, fear gets converted into anger, and the mask is so convincing we forget what is underneath. We bottle it up, and we don’t take it out on our boss or the congregation or the brothers and sisters finding out who they are. We take it out on our spouse.
- “Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.” The Bible tells us to confess our faults, and men don’t like doing that. Brothers will name you as their counsellor of a thousand when they haven’t talked to you in a year, because they don’t want to say they have no counsel. They’ll ask you to be their counsellor and never call, or call only to talk about what happened at camp, never about what they are dealing with.
- To be relieved of that inner pain we must process it and channel it through God’s word. The problem is we don’t counsel about our issues, or we counsel with the wrong person.
- “Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed.” We ought to confess our faults one to another, and pray one for another. “The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 9:1](/bible/sirach/9#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/9#v1)</sup> Be not jealous over the wife of thy bosom, and teach her not an evil lesson against thyself.

    The wrong person is your wife. You go into all your emotional issues with her: when I was young this happened to me, this hurt me. You have to counsel about these issues, but there is somebody you shouldn’t be counselling with about them.
  - **[Micah 7:5](/bible/micah/7#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/micah/7#v5)</sup> Trust ye not in a friend, put ye not confidence in a guide: keep the doors of thy mouth from her that lieth in thy bosom.

    “Trust ye not in a friend, put ye not confidence in a guide: keep the doors of thy mouth from her that lieth in thy bosom.” Don’t be like Samson with Delilah, telling her all his secrets. Don’t counsel with your wife about stuff like that.
  - **[Proverbs 27:17](/bible/proverbs/27#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/27#v17)</sup> Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.

    Who you need to counsel with is the brothers. Men need correction from other men. “Iron sharpeneth iron; so a man sharpeneth the countenance of his friend.” As men we sharpen one another. Something going on, something from your past you are dealing with: go to a righteous counsellor, go to your brother, bring it to your brother.

**[James 5:19](/bible/james/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/james/5#v19)</sup> Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth, and one convert him;

- This is about conversion: the word that is able to save your souls, and David saying he would teach transgressors so sinners would be converted. “Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth, and one convert him”: if you make a mistake like David did, you fall, but you rise again and you rise better. And one corrects him or her.
- “Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth”: if you commit a fault, if you fall, if you find yourself committing a sin. “And one convert him”: convert means change. One helps him get better, helps him to change.

While that is going on we are marching and teaching our people, and they can't say a word. It is the perfect season; the Most High God moved our spirit to do it. To hide a multitude of sins means they get mercy for their sins and repent from them.
