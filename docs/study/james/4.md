## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on James 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-10, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[James 4:1](/bible/james/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/james/4#v1)</sup> From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?

- You are in a relationship and you keep arguing all the time; you ever wonder, why do we keep bumping heads? “From whence come wars and fightings among you?” Why do we keep going back and forth with each other?
- “Come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?” Something inside of you is making this conflict come up all the time. We just went over this last week, we just went over this yesterday.
- Examine yourself: do I have that lustful spirit? Do I desire more than I need? “From whence come wars and fightings among you? come they not hence, even of your lusts that war in your members?” The wars and fightings among you manifest from your lusts, a constant battle between the spiritual man and the carnal man.
- Many brothers deal heavily with porn, desensitizing themselves until it will be curtains for them even when they marry. How can you waste royal seed that is here to bring forth and build back a nation, sitting there aroused watching another man with a sister, tapes of things actors do, then shooting off in a napkin or the drain and crying, oh gosh, I feel so convicted, Deacons and Captains, I was playing with my tally whacker. Our brothers have got to come out of that. You have to kill that old man.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Esdras 8:69-70](/bible/1-esdras/8#v69)**
    > <sup>[69](/bible/1-esdras/8#v69)</sup> The nation of Israel, the princes, the priests and Levites, have not put away from them the strange people of the land, nor the pollutions of the Gentiles, to wit, of the Canaanites, Hittites, Pheresites, Jebusites, and the Moabites, Egyptians, and Edomites.
    >
    > <sup>[70](/bible/1-esdras/8#v70)</sup> For both they and their sons have married with their daughters, and the holy seed is mixed with the strange people of the land; and from the beginning of this matter the rulers and the great men have been partakers of this iniquity.

    For all you chicken chokers out there: kill that old man. “The nation of Israel, the princes, the priests and Levites, have not put away from them the strange people of the land, nor the pollutions of the Gentiles”: the Israelites had not removed themselves from the heathen. It is like that today; the next pollution of the Gentiles is evil ass Christmas. The Canaanites, Hittites, Pheresites, Jebusites, Moabites and Egyptians are the nations of today: the Africans, the Chinese, the Hamites and the white men.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 8:11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)</sup> Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

    “Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily”: the judgment of the Lord for the ill-doings brothers and sisters do in the dark, outside this congregation, does not fall immediately. “Therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil”: the heart is the mind, and the mind is set to do it over and over again.
  - **[Colossians 3:5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

    “Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth”: kill your members upon the earth. “fornication”: that's the lust within you. You might argue because you're not getting enough, not engaging sexually with your spouse; so sometimes you fornicate, sometimes you watch porn. Something in you is causing contention in the marriage. “uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence”: evil sexual desires. “and covetousness, which is idolatry”.

**[James 4:2](/bible/james/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/james/4#v2)</sup> Ye lust, and have not: ye kill, and desire to have, and cannot obtain: ye fight and war, yet ye have not, because ye ask not.

- “Ye lust, and have not: ye kill, and desire to have, and cannot obtain”. No matter how much you fight, you have nothing; you are ready to kill and you still have nothing. That is how Donald Trump is: he has everything, and it is not enough for him.
- “Ye fight and war, yet ye have not, because ye ask not.” You are not asking the right way, according to what God says.
- “Ye lust, and have not”: you lust after things but don’t obtain them. “Ye kill, and desire to have, and cannot obtain”: you slander a brother or sister who has what you want, give them the evil eye, speak nasty about them, say they must be doing some dirt. That is the mentality of brothers and sisters, the spirit inside thyself. “Ye fight and war, yet ye have not, because ye ask not.”

**[James 4:3](/bible/james/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/4#v3)</sup> Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts.

- “Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss”. There is a way to ask for things, and it is according to the Lord. You want peace? The Lord has to get on board for peace. Some people you are not going to have peace with. In the congregation, in relationships, in our houses, you know who the troublemaker is; you have to be honest with yourself. America has to be honest about who the troublemaker is: why gas prices are so high, why food is so high, why we are searched at the borders and airports, why there are no jobs, why life is so difficult. From the government to the congregation to your house and your marriage, be honest about who the starter is, and point them out so everybody knows. That is why we do counsel: we hear both sides and say, it is him, it is her; we let the child speak and say, you are the problem, you are the reason there is no peace.
- “That ye may consume it upon your lusts.” Whatever you get is just to fulfill your lusts. You don’t care about the God we should trust, and people like that wind up in the dust. There is no future for them.
- “Ye ask, and receive not”: a home, a car, even a wife. God is not against marriage; marriage is honorable. Yet some brothers slander another brother because the sister they wanted to prove is not interested, when they show they don’t have their stuff together. “Because ye ask amiss”: for the wrong reasons, to stunt in front of brothers and sisters who don’t have, to have a trophy wife with not a hill of beans of understanding in her brain. “That ye may consume it upon your lusts.” Of course the Lord knows you need a house to cover shame, a wife if you burn, transportation. But you asked to be the HNIC, to be seen of men, not in sincerity that his will be done and he be magnified and glorified through it.

**[James 4:4](/bible/james/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/james/4#v4)</sup> Ye adulterers and adulteresses, know ye not that the friendship of the world is enmity with God? whosoever therefore will be a friend of the world is the enemy of God.

- “Ye adulterers and adulteresses”: when you go contrary to the laws of God and these other things become a god to you, you have committed adultery on the heavenly Father. Kill that adulterous spirit.
- “Know ye not that the friendship of the world is enmity with God?” Loving this society and this captivity is hatred against the heavenly Father and his Son Jesus the Christ. Brothers go above and beyond to keep this captivity going instead of helping bring it down through the word of God and waking up the 12 tribes. You love the place, the oppression of your brothers and sisters, the census killings, the ICE raids, as long as you can pull up to camp in your new Range Rover. God doesn’t give a damn about that Range. He is going to blow your ass; when the kingdom comes and he drops that nuclear bomb on that ass, it is going to be ranging then.
- “Whosoever therefore will be a friend of the world is the enemy of God.” You have deemed yourself the enemy of God. Some of our mindsets are worse than the other nations: by any means you will take your brother out for what he has, by any means be part of this wicked society. Some sneak off to big mama’s house on Christmas and leave with fifteen presents in the trunk, saying they won’t open them till the 27th. Come the 25th, New Year’s Eve, Thanksgiving and Halloween, there will be empty seats, because they cannot shake this world and are not ready to kill the old mentality. If they keep playing, the Lord will take them out. Either you kill or you will be killed.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 2:15](/bible/1-john/2#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/2#v15)</sup> Love not the world, neither the things that are in the world. If any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him.

    If you love the world you hate Christ. “Love not the world, neither the things that are in the world”: love not this captivity, its precious things and riches, the mansion on the hill, not even your job. Many love their jobs more than this truth. These things are hooks inside you that will pull you out of this truth faster than you know. They are devices of Satan, the tangible trinkets he gives our brothers and sisters to lust after as status symbols, when the kingdom of heaven is at hand.
  - **[Matthew 6:24](/bible/matthew/6#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/6#v24)</sup> No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other; or else he will hold to the one, and despise the other. Ye cannot serve God and mammon.

    Many do not hate the world; they hate having to change, repent, get their minds right and come back to the heavenly Father. “No man can serve two masters: for either he will hate the one, and love the other”: you will have enmity against God and love money, your career, your bed, the Christmas stocking full of abominations. Many snuck off to celebrate the slaughter of their brothers, the tribe of Gad, with a turkey drumstick and the football game, then came back saying shalom.

**[James 4:5](/bible/james/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/james/4#v5)</sup> Do ye think that the scripture saith in vain, The spirit that dwelleth in us lusteth to envy?

- “The spirit that dwelleth in us lusteth to envy.” Do you think the scripture says that in vain? It is not there by accident. There is something in us, and it is there for a reason.
- “The spirit that dwelleth in us lusteth to envy.” Many still have an affinity for the world. Everything in this society is built on lust; everything as far as the eye can see appeals to the flesh, I got to have, I got to have. Those who have travelled know you don’t see that in other countries, but in Babylon everything is at your fingertips, and it plays with you.

**[James 4:6](/bible/james/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/james/4#v6)</sup> But he giveth more grace. Wherefore he saith, God resisteth the proud, but giveth grace unto the humble.

- “But he giveth more grace.” He gives us grace to get over that spirit of envy and hatred; he gives us space to fix it. This class is for somebody battling that right now, and it is for all of us.
- “God resisteth the proud”: the Lord hates the spirit of a proud brother or a proud sister, as Proverbs 8:13 says. “But giveth grace unto the humble”: he gives time to the humble, those who fear him and keep his commandments faithfully. You don’t have to ask them to be here or to volunteer; they despise the place where they get their pecuniary compensation, and there is no better comfort to them than being among believing brothers and sisters.
- The Lord gives them a grace period to get themselves right and be chosen for the kingdom. They are sharpening their swords to subdue the evil spirit that plagued them for years before this glorious gospel. This is a hospital; don’t run from it. All of us are working on something, all of us sharpening the sword to kill the old man or woman, and all we have is each other. You can’t get that from a white man’s couch or from cashing a check. Pay all the bills and you will still feel empty without the Lord.

**[James 4:7](/bible/james/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/james/4#v7)</sup> Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

- “Submit yourselves therefore to God”: submit yourself to Messiah. “Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.” If you resist sin, sin is going to flee from you. But some love working for sin, and believe they can work for sin and work for Messiah. It does not work like that.
- Remember the scripture in Revelation about the lukewarm whom Christ is going to spew out: spew out means he is going to kill you. You are on both sides: here on the Sabbath, but also in the club, in the bar drinking, smoking weed, sleeping around. It is a matter of time before Messiah has to put you to death. If you are in the midst of it, repent. Today is your last warning; repent before judgment comes.
- We have to cleanse ourselves from all uncleanness, and we have to abstain so we can overcome. Now’s the time to do it; now’s the time to tap into that full potential.
- “Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.” But you can’t be like the fearful of our people when the army was coming against us, doubting the Lord. You can’t do that. You have to put your full assurance and your trust in the Lord in order to resist.

**[James 4:8](/bible/james/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/james/4#v8)</sup> Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you. Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts, ye double minded.

- “Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you.” Call on God and he will give you a way to overcome the spirit of emulation, envy and jealousy, and take away the spirit of being a betrayer.
- “Purify your hearts, ye double minded.” The double-minded man sits in here bouncing back and forth in his mind. They put on the garment and come every Sabbath, and secretly they have hatred. They want to be here, but they murmur and talk about you; they watch class and take notes, and they murmur. That is madness.
- “Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you.” How do you draw nigh to Messiah? With the scriptures. “Cleanse your hands, ye sinners; and purify your hearts”: purify your mind, “ye double minded.” Some are double minded: your body is here, but your mind is not here.
- “Draw nigh to God”: be here, be in the circles, give your time. “And he will draw nigh to you”: he will reveal himself and open his understanding to you, the dark mysteries and the parables, so you can guide your household and meet every temptation with thus saith the Lord, and reach out to the officers over you when you can’t. Those who won’t come up to the table, or sit way in the back when leadership teaches, are not drawing near to the Lord either. They are waiting for the wine table at the new moon. It is a commandment to blow the shofar at the new moon, yet brothers come in with three-hundred-dollar bottles and no shofar. Examine yourself: you might be an alcoholic. Do you believe this, or are you here for the bottle and the mirth? That is another spirit to kill.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 3:3](/bible/1-john/3#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/3#v3)</sup> And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure.

    We just read it in First John: “every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure.” Drawing nigh to God is purifying yourself.
  - **[Ephesians 5:26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)</sup> That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word,

    How do we purify our minds? “That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word.” Christianity thinks dipping in water makes you a whole new person, but the true baptism is washing yourself with the word, as we are doing right now in this class.
  - **[Psalms 19:7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

    “The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.” The law of the Lord is what cleanses you and makes you perfect. Keeping the commandments is the cleansing that purifies the heart of the double minded.

**[James 4:9-10](/bible/james/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/james/4#v9)</sup> Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep: let your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to heaviness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/james/4#v10)</sup> Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up.

- “Be afflicted, and mourn, and weep: let your laughter be turned to mourning, and your joy to heaviness. Humble yourselves in the sight of the Lord, and he shall lift you up.” Humble ourselves in the sight of Messiah and he is going to lift us up.
- To be afflicted for this truth is a blessing. Right now all of us are in mourning. Our joy is going to be the kingdom. There is no joy here in this captivity. We live a fearful life every day: some cannot even keep the Sabbath because they are afraid of being fired, and some fear for their job when the new moon comes and have to tell some type of nonsense just to keep the Most High God’s high holidays. Black people can make something out of nothing, but there is no life here.
- So we have nothing to lose. All we have to do is keep the law, and the kingdom is ours. No matter what happens, you should never find yourself leaving this truth saying, I used to be an Israelite.
