**[Lamentations 3:1-9](/bible/lamentations/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/lamentations/3#v1)</sup> I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/lamentations/3#v2)</sup> He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/lamentations/3#v3)</sup> Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/lamentations/3#v4)</sup> My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/lamentations/3#v5)</sup> He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/lamentations/3#v6)</sup> He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/lamentations/3#v7)</sup> He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/lamentations/3#v8)</sup> Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/lamentations/3#v9)</sup> He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked.

- He is the man that has seen affliction by the rod of the Most High's wrath, led into darkness and not into light, his flesh and skin made old and his bones broken.
- The Most High has hedged him about with gall and travail, set him in dark places like the dead of old, made his chain heavy, and shut out his prayer.
- He has enclosed his ways with hewn stone and made his paths crooked.

**[Lamentations 3:10-18](/bible/lamentations/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/lamentations/3#v10)</sup> He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/lamentations/3#v11)</sup> He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/lamentations/3#v12)</sup> He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/lamentations/3#v13)</sup> He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/lamentations/3#v14)</sup> I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/lamentations/3#v15)</sup> He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/lamentations/3#v16)</sup> He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/lamentations/3#v17)</sup> And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/lamentations/3#v18)</sup> And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the Lord:

- The Most High was to him as a bear lying in wait and a lion in secret places, turning aside his ways, pulling him in pieces, and making him desolate.
- He bent His bow and set him as a mark for the arrow, filled him with bitterness, made him drunken with wormwood, and broke his teeth with gravel stones.
- He covered him with ashes and removed his soul far from peace, so that he forgot prosperity and said his strength and hope had perished from the Most High.

**[Lamentations 3:19-24](/bible/lamentations/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/lamentations/3#v19)</sup> Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/lamentations/3#v20)</sup> My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/lamentations/3#v21)</sup> This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)</sup> It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/lamentations/3#v23)</sup> They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/lamentations/3#v24)</sup> The Lord is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

- Remembering his affliction and misery, the wormwood and the gall, his soul is humbled in him.
- This he recalls to mind, therefore he has hope: it is of the Most High's mercies that we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not and are new every morning; great is His faithfulness.
- It takes a spiritual mind, in the midst of great distress and servitude, to acknowledge that this is really mercy and compassion from the Most High even in our low state.
- The Most High is his portion, saith his soul, therefore he will hope in Him.

**[Lamentations 3:25-30](/bible/lamentations/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/lamentations/3#v25)</sup> The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/lamentations/3#v26)</sup> It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/lamentations/3#v27)</sup> It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/lamentations/3#v28)</sup> He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/lamentations/3#v29)</sup> He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/lamentations/3#v30)</sup> He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach.

- The Most High is good to them that wait for Him and to the soul that seeks Him, and it is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Most High.
- It is good for a man to bear the yoke in his youth, to sit alone and keep silence, and to put his mouth in the dust if so be there may be hope.
- He gives his cheek to him that smites him and is filled full with reproach; this points to Christ, who gave His cheek to the smiters, as recorded in the book of Matthew.

**[Lamentations 3:31-36](/bible/lamentations/3#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/lamentations/3#v31)</sup> For the Lord will not cast off for ever:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/lamentations/3#v32)</sup> But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/lamentations/3#v33)</sup> For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/lamentations/3#v34)</sup> To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/lamentations/3#v35)</sup> To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High,
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/lamentations/3#v36)</sup> To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not.

- The Most High will not cast off forever; though He causes grief, yet will He have compassion according to the multitude of His mercies, for He does not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men.
- To crush underfoot the prisoners of the earth, to turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High, and to subvert a man in his cause, the Most High approves not.

**[Lamentations 3:37-42](/bible/lamentations/3#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/lamentations/3#v37)</sup> Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/lamentations/3#v38)</sup> Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/lamentations/3#v39)</sup> Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/lamentations/3#v40)</sup> Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the Lord.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/lamentations/3#v41)</sup> Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/lamentations/3#v42)</sup> We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned.

- Who is he that says and it comes to pass when the Most High commands it not? No prophet can say a thing to come to pass unless the Most High puts His spirit on him; that is how powerful He is, and it is why Israel were the greatest nation on earth, for out of His mouth proceeds both evil and good.
- Why does a living man complain for the punishment of his sins? Let us search and try our ways and turn again to the Most High, instead of only complaining about our conditions while we still dwell in sin.
- We have transgressed and rebelled, and He has not pardoned.

**[Lamentations 3:43-54](/bible/lamentations/3#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/lamentations/3#v43)</sup> Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/lamentations/3#v44)</sup> Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that our prayer should not pass through.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/lamentations/3#v45)</sup> Thou hast made us as the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/lamentations/3#v46)</sup> All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/lamentations/3#v47)</sup> Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/lamentations/3#v48)</sup> Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water for the destruction of the daughter of my people.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/lamentations/3#v49)</sup> Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/lamentations/3#v50)</sup> Till the Lord look down, and behold from heaven.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/lamentations/3#v51)</sup> Mine eye affecteth mine heart because of all the daughters of my city.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/lamentations/3#v52)</sup> Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause.
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/lamentations/3#v53)</sup> They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon me.
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/lamentations/3#v54)</sup> Waters flowed over mine head; then I said, I am cut off.

- The Most High covered Himself with anger and persecuted them, covered Himself with a cloud that their prayer should not pass through, and made them as the offscouring in the midst of the people.
- All their enemies opened their mouths against them, and fear and a snare, desolation and destruction came upon them, so that his eye runs down with rivers of water for the destruction of his people.
- The enemies chased him like a bird, cut off his life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon him, and the waters flowed over his head until he said he was cut off.

**[Lamentations 3:55-66](/bible/lamentations/3#v55)**

> <sup>[55](/bible/lamentations/3#v55)</sup> I called upon thy name, O Lord, out of the low dungeon.
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/lamentations/3#v56)</sup> Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry.
>
> <sup>[57](/bible/lamentations/3#v57)</sup> Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not.
>
> <sup>[58](/bible/lamentations/3#v58)</sup> O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life.
>
> <sup>[59](/bible/lamentations/3#v59)</sup> O Lord, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause.
>
> <sup>[60](/bible/lamentations/3#v60)</sup> Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me.
>
> <sup>[61](/bible/lamentations/3#v61)</sup> Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me;
>
> <sup>[62](/bible/lamentations/3#v62)</sup> The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day.
>
> <sup>[63](/bible/lamentations/3#v63)</sup> Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/lamentations/3#v64)</sup> Render unto them a recompence, O Lord, according to the work of their hands.
>
> <sup>[65](/bible/lamentations/3#v65)</sup> Give them sorrow of heart, thy curse unto them.
>
> <sup>[66](/bible/lamentations/3#v66)</sup> Persecute and destroy them in anger from under the heavens of the Lord.

- He called on the Most High's name out of the low dungeon, and He heard his voice and drew near in the day he called, saying, Fear not; the Most High pleaded the causes of his soul and redeemed his life.
- He asks the Most High to judge his cause, having seen all the enemies' vengeance and imaginations against him, and to render them a recompense according to the work of their hands, giving them sorrow of heart and His curse.
- Jeremiah prayed for the Most High's curse on the enemy because he knew the heathen would have to go through the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) when it is all said and done; we are the ones redeemed from the curse, and we will see our enemies go through it for eternity while we reign forever.
  - **[Deuteronomy 30:7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)</sup> And the Lord thy God will put all these curses upon thine enemies, and on them that hate thee, which persecuted thee.

    The Most High will put all these curses upon our enemies and on them that hate and persecute us.
  - **[Galatians 3:13](/bible/galatians/3#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/galatians/3#v13)</sup> Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:

    Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law, so Israel alone is delivered from the curses of Deuteronomy while the other nations must go through them.
