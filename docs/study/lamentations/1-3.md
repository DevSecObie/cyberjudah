Read the chapter → [Lamentations 1](/bible/lamentations/1)

This session covers Lamentations chapters 1 through 3. The word lamentations means to mourn and to grieve, and that is the whole context of the book: it is the aftermath of being taken captive into Babylon, the temple destroyed, the mighty men slain, and the famine that came with the siege. It is a very sad book, and the whole of it precepts with [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) and [Leviticus 26](/bible/leviticus/26); rather than play ping-pong back and forth we keep the key precepts. Through it all our forefathers confess that the Most High is righteous in His judgment, that we deserve what came upon us for our sins, and yet they pray for the destruction of the enemy and hope for the salvation of the Most High.

**[Lamentations 1:1-2](/bible/lamentations/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/lamentations/1#v1)</sup> How doth the city sit solitary, that was full of people! how is she become as a widow! she that was great among the nations, and princess among the provinces, how is she become tributary!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/lamentations/1#v2)</sup> She weepeth sore in the night, and her tears are on her cheeks: among all her lovers she hath none to comfort her: all her friends have dealt treacherously with her, they are become her enemies.

- The city that was full of people now sits solitary as a widow; she that was great among the nations and a princess among the provinces has become tributary. Jerusalem that used to be jumping, the nation of the chosen people, is now a desolate, empty land.
- She weeps sore in the night with tears on her cheeks, and among all her lovers she has none to comfort her, for all her friends dealt treacherously and became her enemies.
- Israel's lovers and friends were other nations serving other gods, and when it got real they turned their backs, just as the Most High warned.
  - **[Jeremiah 30:14](/bible/jeremiah/30#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/30#v14)</sup> All thy lovers have forgotten thee; they seek thee not; for I have wounded thee with the wound of an enemy, with the chastisement of a cruel one, for the multitude of thine iniquity; because thy sins were increased.

    All thy lovers have forgotten thee and seek thee not, for the Most High wounded thee with the wound of an enemy for the multitude of thine iniquity.

**[Lamentations 1:3-6](/bible/lamentations/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/lamentations/1#v3)</sup> Judah is gone into captivity because of affliction, and because of great servitude: she dwelleth among the heathen, she findeth no rest: all her persecutors overtook her between the straits.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/lamentations/1#v4)</sup> The ways of Zion do mourn, because none come to the solemn feasts: all her gates are desolate: her priests sigh, her virgins are afflicted, and she is in bitterness.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/lamentations/1#v5)</sup> Her adversaries are the chief, her enemies prosper; for the Lord hath afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions: her children are gone into captivity before the enemy.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/lamentations/1#v6)</sup> And from the daughter of Zion all her beauty is departed: her princes are become like harts that find no pasture, and they are gone without strength before the pursuer.

- Judah has gone into captivity because of affliction and great servitude; she dwells among the heathen and finds no rest, and all her persecutors overtook her between the straits.
- No servitude in history compares to ours; the scriptures say they had dominion over our bodies, which is beyond the Irish indentured servants and beyond the holocaust of the fake jews with the Germans.
- The ways of Zion mourn because none come to the solemn feasts, her adversaries are the chief and her enemies prosper, for the Most High afflicted her for the multitude of her transgressions, and her children are gone into captivity before the enemy.
- We went from a nation others looked up to and envied, called the perfection of beauty, to a people whose enemies are now the head and prosper, exactly as the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) and [Leviticus 26](/bible/leviticus/26) foretold.

**[Lamentations 1:7-11](/bible/lamentations/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/lamentations/1#v7)</sup> Jerusalem remembered in the days of her affliction and of her miseries all her pleasant things that she had in the days of old, when her people fell into the hand of the enemy, and none did help her: the adversaries saw her, and did mock at her sabbaths.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/lamentations/1#v8)</sup> Jerusalem hath grievously sinned; therefore she is removed: all that honoured her despise her, because they have seen her nakedness: yea, she sigheth, and turneth backward.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/lamentations/1#v9)</sup> Her filthiness is in her skirts; she remembereth not her last end; therefore she came down wonderfully: she had no comforter. O Lord, behold my affliction: for the enemy hath magnified himself.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/lamentations/1#v10)</sup> The adversary hath spread out his hand upon all her pleasant things: for she hath seen that the heathen entered into her sanctuary, whom thou didst command that they should not enter into thy congregation.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/lamentations/1#v11)</sup> All her people sigh, they seek bread; they have given their pleasant things for meat to relieve the soul: see, O Lord, and consider; for I am become vile.

- Jerusalem remembered in the days of her affliction all the pleasant things she had of old, when her people fell into the enemy's hand and none did help her, and the adversaries mocked at her sabbaths.
- Jerusalem grievously sinned, so she is removed; her filthiness is in her skirts, she remembered not her last end, and she has no comforter, for the heathen entered her sanctuary, whom the Most High commanded should not enter His congregation.
- All her people sigh and seek bread, and they gave their pleasant things for meat to relieve the soul; when things get that serious, people give up whatever they once thought they could not live without.
  - **[Psalm 79:1](/bible/psalms/79#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/79#v1)</sup> O God, the heathen are come into thine inheritance; thy holy temple have they defiled; they have laid Jerusalem on heaps.

    The heathen came into the Most High's inheritance, defiled His holy temple, and laid Jerusalem on heaps.

**[Lamentations 1:12-16](/bible/lamentations/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/lamentations/1#v12)</sup> Is it nothing to you, all ye that pass by? behold, and see if there be any sorrow like unto my sorrow, which is done unto me, wherewith the Lord hath afflicted me in the day of his fierce anger.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/lamentations/1#v13)</sup> From above hath he sent fire into my bones, and it prevaileth against them: he hath spread a net for my feet, he hath turned me back: he hath made me desolate and faint all the day.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/lamentations/1#v14)</sup> The yoke of my transgressions is bound by his hand: they are wreathed, and come up upon my neck: he hath made my strength to fall, the Lord hath delivered me into their hands, from whom I am not able to rise up.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/lamentations/1#v15)</sup> The Lord hath trodden under foot all my mighty men in the midst of me: he hath called an assembly against me to crush my young men: the Lord hath trodden the virgin, the daughter of Judah, as in a winepress.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/lamentations/1#v16)</sup> For these things I weep; mine eye, mine eye runneth down with water, because the comforter that should relieve my soul is far from me: my children are desolate, because the enemy prevailed.

- She calls to all who pass by to see if there is any sorrow like her sorrow, which the Most High afflicted in the day of His fierce anger, sending fire into her bones and spreading a net for her feet.
- The yoke of her transgressions is bound by His hand upon her neck, and the Most High trod underfoot all her mighty men in the midst of her and called an assembly to crush her young men, the leaders taken out first.
- She weeps because the comforter that should relieve her soul is far from her; there was oppression but no comforter to relieve them.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 4:1](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v1)</sup> So I returned, and considered all the oppressions that are done under the sun: and behold the tears of such as were oppressed, and they had no comforter; and on the side of their oppressors there was power; but they had no comforter.

    On the side of the oppressor there was power, but the oppressed had no comforter, which came to pass in the Babylonian captivity.

**[Lamentations 1:17-19](/bible/lamentations/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/lamentations/1#v17)</sup> Zion spreadeth forth her hands, and there is none to comfort her: the Lord hath commanded concerning Jacob, that his adversaries should be round about him: Jerusalem is as a menstruous woman among them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/lamentations/1#v18)</sup> The Lord is righteous; for I have rebelled against his commandment: hear, I pray you, all people, and behold my sorrow: my virgins and my young men are gone into captivity.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/lamentations/1#v19)</sup> I called for my lovers, but they deceived me: my priests and mine elders gave up the ghost in the city, while they sought their meat to relieve their souls.

- Zion spreads forth her hands and there is none to comfort her; the Most High commanded that Jacob's adversaries should be round about him, and Jerusalem is as a menstruous woman among them.
- The Most High is righteous, for Jerusalem rebelled against His commandment; it takes a spiritual mind to be in great distress and still confess that the Most High is right in His judgment, because we have committed fornication against Him.
- She called for her lovers but they deceived her, and her priests and elders gave up the ghost in the city while they sought meat to relieve their souls.
  - **The Three Holy Children 3-5**  *3 Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: thy name is worthy to be praised and glorified for evermore: 4 For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done to us: yea, true are all thy works, thy ways are right, and all thy judgments truth. 5 In all the things that thou hast brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, even Jerusalem, thou hast executed true judgment, for according to truth and judgment didst thou bring all these things upon us because of our sins.*  The three holy children confessed the Most High was righteous in all He brought upon Jerusalem because of their sins.

**[Lamentations 1:20-22](/bible/lamentations/1#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/lamentations/1#v20)</sup> Behold, O Lord; for I am in distress: my bowels are troubled; mine heart is turned within me; for I have grievously rebelled: abroad the sword bereaveth, at home there is as death.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/lamentations/1#v21)</sup> They have heard that I sigh: there is none to comfort me: all mine enemies have heard of my trouble; they are glad that thou hast done it: thou wilt bring the day that thou hast called, and they shall be like unto me.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/1#v22)</sup> Let all their wickedness come before thee; and do unto them, as thou hast done unto me for all my transgressions: for my sighs are many, and my heart is faint.

- She cries that she is in distress, her bowels troubled and her heart turned within her, for she grievously rebelled; abroad the sword bereaves and at home there is death.
- Her enemies heard of her trouble and are glad that the Most High has done it, and she prays that He would bring the day He has called, that they would be like her.
- Even in judgment our forefathers still prayed for the destruction of the enemy, asking the Most High to render to them all their wickedness and do to them as He had done to Jerusalem.
  - **[Baruch 4:31-35](/bible/baruch/4#v31)**

    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/baruch/4#v32)</sup> Miserable are the cities which thy children served: miserable is she that received thy sons.
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/baruch/4#v33)</sup> For as she rejoiced at thy ruin, and was glad of thy fall: so shall she be grieved for her own desolation.
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/baruch/4#v34)</sup> For I will take away the rejoicing of her great multitude, and her pride shall be turned into mourning.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/baruch/4#v35)</sup> For fire shall come upon her from the Everlasting, long to endure; and she shall be inhabited of devils for a great time.

    Those who afflicted and rejoiced over Israel will be grieved for their own desolation, and fire shall come upon Babylon the great.

# Lamentations 2: The Most High Turned Enemy Against Zion

Read the chapter → [Lamentations 2](/bible/lamentations/2)

**[Lamentations 2:1-5](/bible/lamentations/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/lamentations/2#v1)</sup> How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger, and cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel, and remembered not his footstool in the day of his anger!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/lamentations/2#v2)</sup> The Lord hath swallowed up all the habitations of Jacob, and hath not pitied: he hath thrown down in his wrath the strong holds of the daughter of Judah; he hath brought them down to the ground: he hath polluted the kingdom and the princes thereof.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/lamentations/2#v3)</sup> He hath cut off in his fierce anger all the horn of Israel: he hath drawn back his right hand from before the enemy, and he burned against Jacob like a flaming fire, which devoureth round about.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/lamentations/2#v4)</sup> He hath bent his bow like an enemy: he stood with his right hand as an adversary, and slew all that were pleasant to the eye in the tabernacle of the daughter of Zion: he poured out his fury like fire.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/lamentations/2#v5)</sup> The Lord was as an enemy: he hath swallowed up Israel, he hath swallowed up all her palaces: he hath destroyed his strong holds, and hath increased in the daughter of Judah mourning and lamentation.

- The Most High covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in His anger and cast down the beauty of Israel from heaven to the earth; this heaven means rulership, not that Jacob and his tribes took a plane to the sky and were kicked back down.
- We used to bear rule over nations and were the most beautiful nation on earth, with nations paying tribute to us, but the Most High cast us down from that rulership.
- He swallowed up the habitations of Jacob without pity, threw down the strongholds of Judah, cut off the horn of Israel, and became like an enemy who slew all that was pleasant to the eye in the tabernacle of Zion.

**[Lamentations 2:6-9](/bible/lamentations/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/lamentations/2#v6)</sup> And he hath violently taken away his tabernacle, as if it were of a garden: he hath destroyed his places of the assembly: the Lord hath caused the solemn feasts and sabbaths to be forgotten in Zion, and hath despised in the indignation of his anger the king and the priest.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/lamentations/2#v7)</sup> The Lord hath cast off his altar, he hath abhorred his sanctuary, he hath given up into the hand of the enemy the walls of her palaces; they have made a noise in the house of the Lord, as in the day of a solemn feast.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/lamentations/2#v8)</sup> The Lord hath purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion: he hath stretched out a line, he hath not withdrawn his hand from destroying: therefore he made the rampart and the wall to lament; they languished together.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/lamentations/2#v9)</sup> Her gates are sunk into the ground; he hath destroyed and broken her bars: her king and her princes are among the Gentiles: the law is no more; her prophets also find no vision from the Lord.

- The Most High violently took away His tabernacle, destroyed His places of assembly, caused the solemn feasts and sabbaths to be forgotten, and abhorred His sanctuary, giving the walls of her palaces into the enemy's hand.
- He purposed to destroy the wall of the daughter of Zion; her gates are sunk into the ground, her king and princes are among the Gentiles, the law is no more, and her prophets find no vision from the Most High.
- When the enemy entered the sanctuary and burned everything, they burned our torahs, records, and scrolls, which is why the law is said to be no more.
  - **[2 Esdras 14:21](/bible/2-esdras/14#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/2-esdras/14#v21)</sup> For thy law is burnt, therefore no man knoweth the things that are done of thee, or the work that shall begin.

    Ezra testified the law was burnt, so the Most High put His spirit on Ezra and other men to rewrite the torah.

**[Lamentations 2:10-13](/bible/lamentations/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/lamentations/2#v10)</sup> The elders of the daughter of Zion sit upon the ground, and keep silence: they have cast up dust upon their heads; they have girded themselves with sackcloth: the virgins of Jerusalem hang down their heads to the ground.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/lamentations/2#v11)</sup> Mine eyes do fail with tears, my bowels are troubled, my liver is poured upon the earth, for the destruction of the daughter of my people; because the children and the sucklings swoon in the streets of the city.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/lamentations/2#v12)</sup> They say to their mothers, Where is corn and wine? when they swooned as the wounded in the streets of the city, when their soul was poured out into their mothers’ bosom.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/lamentations/2#v13)</sup> What thing shall I take to witness for thee? what thing shall I liken to thee, O daughter of Jerusalem? what shall I equal to thee, that I may comfort thee, O virgin daughter of Zion? for thy breach is great like the sea: who can heal thee?

- The elders of Zion sit upon the ground and keep silence with dust on their heads and sackcloth, and the virgins of Jerusalem hang their heads to the ground.
- Her eyes fail with tears for the destruction of her people, because the children and the sucklings swoon, meaning they faint from hunger, in the streets of the city.
- The children cry to their mothers for corn and wine as they swoon like the wounded and pour out their souls into their mothers' bosom, for there was nothing left to eat at all.

**[Lamentations 2:14-17](/bible/lamentations/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/lamentations/2#v14)</sup> Thy prophets have seen vain and foolish things for thee: and they have not discovered thine iniquity, to turn away thy captivity; but have seen for thee false burdens and causes of banishment.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/lamentations/2#v15)</sup> All that pass by clap their hands at thee; they hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, Is this the city that men call The perfection of beauty, The joy of the whole earth?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/lamentations/2#v16)</sup> All thine enemies have opened their mouth against thee: they hiss and gnash the teeth: they say, We have swallowed her up: certainly this is the day that we looked for; we have found, we have seen it.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/lamentations/2#v17)</sup> The Lord hath done that which he had devised; he hath fulfilled his word that he had commanded in the days of old: he hath thrown down, and hath not pitied: and he hath caused thine enemy to rejoice over thee, he hath set up the horn of thine adversaries.

- Her prophets have seen vain and foolish things for her and have not discovered her iniquity to turn away her captivity, but seen for her false burdens and causes of banishment.
- This is fulfilled by the pastors who teach vain and foolish things like a bunny laying eggs, which has nothing to do with Christ but everything to do with the goddess Ishtar and idolatry; they read the bible in and out yet never address the sins that keep us in captivity.
- All that pass by clap their hands, hiss, and wag their heads at the city that men called the perfection of beauty, the joy of the whole earth, and the enemies open their mouths, saying this is the day they looked for and have seen.

**[Lamentations 2:18-22](/bible/lamentations/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/lamentations/2#v18)</sup> Their heart cried unto the Lord, O wall of the daughter of Zion, let tears run down like a river day and night: give thyself no rest; let not the apple of thine eye cease.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/lamentations/2#v19)</sup> Arise, cry out in the night: in the beginning of the watches pour out thine heart like water before the face of the Lord: lift up thy hands toward him for the life of thy young children, that faint for hunger in the top of every street.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/lamentations/2#v20)</sup> Behold, O Lord, and consider to whom thou hast done this. Shall the women eat their fruit, and children of a span long? shall the priest and the prophet be slain in the sanctuary of the Lord?
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/lamentations/2#v21)</sup> The young and the old lie on the ground in the streets: my virgins and my young men are fallen by the sword; thou hast slain them in the day of thine anger; thou hast killed, and not pitied.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/2#v22)</sup> Thou hast called as in a solemn day my terrors round about, so that in the day of the Lord’s anger none escaped nor remained: those that I have swaddled and brought up hath mine enemy consumed.

- Their heart cried to the Most High; let tears run down like a river day and night for the young children that faint for hunger at the top of every street.
- She asks whether the women should eat their fruit, children of a span long, and whether the priest and prophet should be slain in the sanctuary, while the young and old lie on the ground in the streets, fallen by the sword.
- The Most High slew them in the day of His anger and did not pity, and the enemy consumed those she had swaddled and brought up.

# Lamentations 3: Affliction, and Hope in the Most High's Mercies

Read the chapter → [Lamentations 3](/bible/lamentations/3)

**[Lamentations 3:1-9](/bible/lamentations/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/lamentations/3#v1)</sup> I am the man that hath seen affliction by the rod of his wrath.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/lamentations/3#v2)</sup> He hath led me, and brought me into darkness, but not into light.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/lamentations/3#v3)</sup> Surely against me is he turned; he turneth his hand against me all the day.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/lamentations/3#v4)</sup> My flesh and my skin hath he made old; he hath broken my bones.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/lamentations/3#v5)</sup> He hath builded against me, and compassed me with gall and travail.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/lamentations/3#v6)</sup> He hath set me in dark places, as they that be dead of old.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/lamentations/3#v7)</sup> He hath hedged me about, that I cannot get out: he hath made my chain heavy.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/lamentations/3#v8)</sup> Also when I cry and shout, he shutteth out my prayer.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/lamentations/3#v9)</sup> He hath inclosed my ways with hewn stone, he hath made my paths crooked.

- He is the man that has seen affliction by the rod of the Most High's wrath, led into darkness and not into light, his flesh and skin made old and his bones broken.
- The Most High has hedged him about with gall and travail, set him in dark places like the dead of old, made his chain heavy, and shut out his prayer.
- He has enclosed his ways with hewn stone and made his paths crooked.

**[Lamentations 3:10-18](/bible/lamentations/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/lamentations/3#v10)</sup> He was unto me as a bear lying in wait, and as a lion in secret places.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/lamentations/3#v11)</sup> He hath turned aside my ways, and pulled me in pieces: he hath made me desolate.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/lamentations/3#v12)</sup> He hath bent his bow, and set me as a mark for the arrow.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/lamentations/3#v13)</sup> He hath caused the arrows of his quiver to enter into my reins.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/lamentations/3#v14)</sup> I was a derision to all my people; and their song all the day.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/lamentations/3#v15)</sup> He hath filled me with bitterness, he hath made me drunken with wormwood.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/lamentations/3#v16)</sup> He hath also broken my teeth with gravel stones, he hath covered me with ashes.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/lamentations/3#v17)</sup> And thou hast removed my soul far off from peace: I forgat prosperity.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/lamentations/3#v18)</sup> And I said, My strength and my hope is perished from the Lord:

- The Most High was to him as a bear lying in wait and a lion in secret places, turning aside his ways, pulling him in pieces, and making him desolate.
- He bent His bow and set him as a mark for the arrow, filled him with bitterness, made him drunken with wormwood, and broke his teeth with gravel stones.
- He covered him with ashes and removed his soul far from peace, so that he forgot prosperity and said his strength and hope had perished from the Most High.

**[Lamentations 3:19-24](/bible/lamentations/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/lamentations/3#v19)</sup> Remembering mine affliction and my misery, the wormwood and the gall.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/lamentations/3#v20)</sup> My soul hath them still in remembrance, and is humbled in me.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/lamentations/3#v21)</sup> This I recall to my mind, therefore have I hope.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)</sup> It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/lamentations/3#v23)</sup> They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/lamentations/3#v24)</sup> The Lord is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him.

- Remembering his affliction and misery, the wormwood and the gall, his soul is humbled in him.
- This he recalls to mind, therefore he has hope: it is of the Most High's mercies that we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not and are new every morning; great is His faithfulness.
- It takes a spiritual mind, in the midst of great distress and servitude, to acknowledge that this is really mercy and compassion from the Most High even in our low state.
- The Most High is his portion, saith his soul, therefore he will hope in Him.

**[Lamentations 3:25-30](/bible/lamentations/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/lamentations/3#v25)</sup> The Lord is good unto them that wait for him, to the soul that seeketh him.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/lamentations/3#v26)</sup> It is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/lamentations/3#v27)</sup> It is good for a man that he bear the yoke in his youth.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/lamentations/3#v28)</sup> He sitteth alone and keepeth silence, because he hath borne it upon him.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/lamentations/3#v29)</sup> He putteth his mouth in the dust; if so be there may be hope.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/lamentations/3#v30)</sup> He giveth his cheek to him that smiteth him: he is filled full with reproach.

- The Most High is good to them that wait for Him and to the soul that seeks Him, and it is good that a man should both hope and quietly wait for the salvation of the Most High.
- It is good for a man to bear the yoke in his youth, to sit alone and keep silence, and to put his mouth in the dust if so be there may be hope.
- He gives his cheek to him that smites him and is filled full with reproach; this points to Christ, who gave His cheek to the smiters, as recorded in the book of Matthew.

**[Lamentations 3:31-36](/bible/lamentations/3#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/lamentations/3#v31)</sup> For the Lord will not cast off for ever:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/lamentations/3#v32)</sup> But though he cause grief, yet will he have compassion according to the multitude of his mercies.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/lamentations/3#v33)</sup> For he doth not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/lamentations/3#v34)</sup> To crush under his feet all the prisoners of the earth,
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/lamentations/3#v35)</sup> To turn aside the right of a man before the face of the most High,
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/lamentations/3#v36)</sup> To subvert a man in his cause, the Lord approveth not.

- The Most High will not cast off forever; though He causes grief, yet will He have compassion according to the multitude of His mercies, for He does not afflict willingly nor grieve the children of men.
- To crush underfoot the prisoners of the earth, to turn aside the right of a man before the face of the Most High, and to subvert a man in his cause, the Most High approves not.

**[Lamentations 3:37-42](/bible/lamentations/3#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/lamentations/3#v37)</sup> Who is he that saith, and it cometh to pass, when the Lord commandeth it not?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/lamentations/3#v38)</sup> Out of the mouth of the most High proceedeth not evil and good?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/lamentations/3#v39)</sup> Wherefore doth a living man complain, a man for the punishment of his sins?
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/lamentations/3#v40)</sup> Let us search and try our ways, and turn again to the Lord.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/lamentations/3#v41)</sup> Let us lift up our heart with our hands unto God in the heavens.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/lamentations/3#v42)</sup> We have transgressed and have rebelled: thou hast not pardoned.

- Who is he that says and it comes to pass when the Most High commands it not? No prophet can say a thing to come to pass unless the Most High puts His spirit on him; that is how powerful He is, and it is why Israel were the greatest nation on earth, for out of His mouth proceeds both evil and good.
- Why does a living man complain for the punishment of his sins? Let us search and try our ways and turn again to the Most High, instead of only complaining about our conditions while we still dwell in sin.
- We have transgressed and rebelled, and He has not pardoned.

**[Lamentations 3:43-54](/bible/lamentations/3#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/lamentations/3#v43)</sup> Thou hast covered with anger, and persecuted us: thou hast slain, thou hast not pitied.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/lamentations/3#v44)</sup> Thou hast covered thyself with a cloud, that our prayer should not pass through.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/lamentations/3#v45)</sup> Thou hast made us as the offscouring and refuse in the midst of the people.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/lamentations/3#v46)</sup> All our enemies have opened their mouths against us.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/lamentations/3#v47)</sup> Fear and a snare is come upon us, desolation and destruction.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/lamentations/3#v48)</sup> Mine eye runneth down with rivers of water for the destruction of the daughter of my people.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/lamentations/3#v49)</sup> Mine eye trickleth down, and ceaseth not, without any intermission,
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/lamentations/3#v50)</sup> Till the Lord look down, and behold from heaven.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/lamentations/3#v51)</sup> Mine eye affecteth mine heart because of all the daughters of my city.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/lamentations/3#v52)</sup> Mine enemies chased me sore, like a bird, without cause.
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/lamentations/3#v53)</sup> They have cut off my life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon me.
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/lamentations/3#v54)</sup> Waters flowed over mine head; then I said, I am cut off.

- The Most High covered Himself with anger and persecuted them, covered Himself with a cloud that their prayer should not pass through, and made them as the offscouring in the midst of the people.
- All their enemies opened their mouths against them, and fear and a snare, desolation and destruction came upon them, so that his eye runs down with rivers of water for the destruction of his people.
- The enemies chased him like a bird, cut off his life in the dungeon, and cast a stone upon him, and the waters flowed over his head until he said he was cut off.

**[Lamentations 3:55-66](/bible/lamentations/3#v55)**

> <sup>[55](/bible/lamentations/3#v55)</sup> I called upon thy name, O Lord, out of the low dungeon.
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/lamentations/3#v56)</sup> Thou hast heard my voice: hide not thine ear at my breathing, at my cry.
>
> <sup>[57](/bible/lamentations/3#v57)</sup> Thou drewest near in the day that I called upon thee: thou saidst, Fear not.
>
> <sup>[58](/bible/lamentations/3#v58)</sup> O Lord, thou hast pleaded the causes of my soul; thou hast redeemed my life.
>
> <sup>[59](/bible/lamentations/3#v59)</sup> O Lord, thou hast seen my wrong: judge thou my cause.
>
> <sup>[60](/bible/lamentations/3#v60)</sup> Thou hast seen all their vengeance and all their imaginations against me.
>
> <sup>[61](/bible/lamentations/3#v61)</sup> Thou hast heard their reproach, O Lord, and all their imaginations against me;
>
> <sup>[62](/bible/lamentations/3#v62)</sup> The lips of those that rose up against me, and their device against me all the day.
>
> <sup>[63](/bible/lamentations/3#v63)</sup> Behold their sitting down, and their rising up; I am their musick.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/lamentations/3#v64)</sup> Render unto them a recompence, O Lord, according to the work of their hands.
>
> <sup>[65](/bible/lamentations/3#v65)</sup> Give them sorrow of heart, thy curse unto them.
>
> <sup>[66](/bible/lamentations/3#v66)</sup> Persecute and destroy them in anger from under the heavens of the Lord.

- He called on the Most High's name out of the low dungeon, and He heard his voice and drew near in the day he called, saying, Fear not; the Most High pleaded the causes of his soul and redeemed his life.
- He asks the Most High to judge his cause, having seen all the enemies' vengeance and imaginations against him, and to render them a recompense according to the work of their hands, giving them sorrow of heart and His curse.
- Jeremiah prayed for the Most High's curse on the enemy because he knew the heathen would have to go through the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) when it is all said and done; we are the ones redeemed from the curse, and we will see our enemies go through it for eternity while we reign forever.
  - **[Deuteronomy 30:7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)</sup> And the Lord thy God will put all these curses upon thine enemies, and on them that hate thee, which persecuted thee.

    The Most High will put all these curses upon our enemies and on them that hate and persecute us.
  - **[Galatians 3:13](/bible/galatians/3#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/galatians/3#v13)</sup> Christ hath redeemed us from the curse of the law, being made a curse for us: for it is written, Cursed is every one that hangeth on a tree:

    Christ redeemed us from the curse of the law, so Israel alone is delivered from the curses of Deuteronomy while the other nations must go through them.
