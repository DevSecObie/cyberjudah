> <sup>[36](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v36)</sup> And when the king was come again from the places about Cilicia, the Jews that were in the city, and certain of the Greeks that abhorred the fact also, complained because Onias was slain without cause.

- “Not only the Jews, but many also of other nations, took great indignation”, because other nations respected Onias III. They complained “because Onias was slain without cause.” They knew he was killed for no reason.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 3:7](/bible/1-timothy/3#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-timothy/3#v7)</sup> Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without; lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.

    Onias had a good report among the Jews and among the other nations. He was not trying to join with the other nations; he said, I’m not joining with you, yet he still had a good report among them. “Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without; lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.”
  - **[1 Thessalonians 4:12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)</sup> That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without, and that ye may have lack of nothing.

    Somebody might say that is only for a man who desires the office of a bishop. No: “That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without, and that ye may have lack of nothing.” You have to walk honestly toward them that are without the truth. You can’t be outside the school robbing, stealing, lying, using drugs, whatever little secret things you have going on.
  - **[Colossians 4:5](/bible/colossians/4#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/4#v5)</sup> Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time.

    “Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time.” Walk in wisdom toward them that don’t know the truth. Redeeming the time: be aware of the time you are in.

**[2 Maccabees 4:37](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/2-maccabees/4#v37)</sup> Therefore Antiochus was heartily sorry, and moved to pity, and wept, because of the sober and modest behaviour of him that was dead.

- “Antiochus was heartily sorry, and moved to pity, and wept, because of the sober and modest behaviour of him that was dead.” Even the white man was crying for Onias.
