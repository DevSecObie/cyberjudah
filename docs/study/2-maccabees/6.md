## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 6-9, 18, 21-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 6:1-2](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v1)</sup> Not long after this the king sent an old man of Athens to compel the Jews to depart from the laws of their fathers, and not to live after the laws of God:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v2)</sup> And to pollute also the temple in Jerusalem, and to call it the temple of Jupiter Olympius; and that in Garizim, of Jupiter the Defender of strangers, as they did desire that dwelt in the place.

- Assyria, Babylon, Persia, and now the Greeks. The king is Antiochus Epiphanes, who sent “an old man of Athens to compel the Jews to depart from the laws of their fathers”. This is an attack on Judah, and his goal was “to pollute also the temple in Jerusalem, and to call it the temple of Jupiter Olympius”.
- “And that in Garizim, of Jupiter the Defender of strangers, as they did desire that dwelt in the place.” Write this down: Garizim, Shechem, is the northern kingdom’s capital. In this time the northern kingdom were prone to idolatry. Judah didn’t want it; Ephraim did.
- This goes to those that sit upon the mountain of Samaria. “Not long after this the king sent an old man of Athens to compel the Jews to depart from the laws of their fathers, and not to live after the laws of God”. The king here is Antiochus Epiphanes, and he sent his right-hand man.
- “And to pollute also the temple in Jerusalem, and to call it the temple of Jupiter Olympius”: he defiled the temple in Jerusalem in Judah and called it the temple of Jupiter Olympius, or Zeus Olympius.

**[2 Maccabees 6:6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v6)</sup> Neither was it lawful for a man to keep sabbath days or ancient feasts, or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.

- Carnival is not a Dominican, Haitian, Brazilian or Israelite custom. Its roots go back to the pagan festivals of the Romans, the Saturnalia and Lupercalia, and the Greek Dionysian festivals of revelry and feasting; the word comes from carne levare, farewell to meat, before the Catholic season of Lent. It is masked balls, street parades, elaborate costumes, devil masks and loud music, and indulgence, meaning fornication and adultery, gotten out before they quote unquote repent for forty days of Lent and walk around with ashes on their foreheads thinking they are righteous. A dude in a fishnet shirt, belly out, licking a lollipop: that is some gay ass stuff. It is a temporary reversal of social rules: marriage, honouring and loving one another turned upside down. That day you can be a homo, like that fat homo with a lollipop in his mouth, and the women are butt ass naked shaking their ass. It is all demonic, idolatry and fornication, and for without are dogs: those practising it are not going to get the kingdom. The Romans and the Greeks gave it to us.
- “Neither was it lawful for a man to keep sabbath days”: an Israelite man was not allowed to keep the day the Most High made holy, nor the ancient feasts of Israel in captivity, “or to profess himself at all to be a Jew.” That is why we call ourselves Dominican, Haitian, Latino and black: it was not lawful to call yourself a Jew.

**[2 Maccabees 6:7](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v7)</sup> And in the day of the king’s birth every month they were brought by bitter constraint to eat of the sacrifices; and when the feast of Bacchus was kept, the Jews were compelled to go in procession to Bacchus, carrying ivy.

- When “the feast of Bacchus was kept, the Jews were compelled to go in procession to Bacchus, carrying ivy.” Bacchus is the god of wine, and his feast is the bacchanal. The Greeks and Romans decorated themselves in ivy and flowers, crowns of leaves on their heads, and held orgies in his honor. That is what carnival is: the celebration of Bacchus. The same wreath is on the Christmas door.
- In Trinidad we use the word bacchanal every day for drama, scandal and wild celebration, and we never knew why. A slave people learned that old Roman word from the Europeans celebrating carnival, because it was Bacchus they were celebrating. The carnival songs say it plainly: bring the bacchanal army, carnival is bacchanal. Carnival and bacchanal are the same thing.
- So to all the carnival brothers and sisters on Labor Day saying it is part of your culture: it is European culture, Christian culture, Roman culture. They still celebrate it in Italy to this day.
- “They were brought by bitter constraint to eat of the sacrifices”: bitter constraint means forced, forced to eat swine’s flesh. “When the feast of Bacchus was kept”: Bacchanal and carnival are the same thing, feasting, revelry and excess. Bacchus was an idol, a false god of the Greeks and the Romans. “The Jews were compelled to go in procession to Bacchus”, to march and parade, “carrying ivy.” That is why our people are dressed up carrying ivy and flowers; we saw it in Guatemala and Chile, carrying flowers for the Virgin Mary in costumes like the carnival ones.

**[2 Maccabees 6:8](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v8)</sup> Moreover there went out a decree to the neighbour cities of the heathen, by the suggestion of Ptolemee, against the Jews, that they should observe the same fashions, and be partakers of their sacrifices:

- “Moreover there went out a decree to the neighbour cities of the heathen, by the suggestion of Ptolemee”, the Greek ruler who ruled Egypt, “against the Jews, that they should observe the same fashions.” We were not keeping it, so he made a law that we must. The same thing will happen in these last days: they will make laws that we must keep the Catholic religion, or whatever religion they come up with, and they will imprison and kill many of us. Right now our people follow these customs willingly, because we do not know our history and think we came up with them.
- “And be partakers of their sacrifices.” They were sacrificing children, and it still happens. What comes out of carnival is carnival babies, conceived in those wicked parades, and because the child is not the husband’s, there is an abortion: children sacrificed unto these devils.

**[2 Maccabees 6:9](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v9)</sup> And whoso would not conform themselves to the manners of the Gentiles should be put to death. Then might a man have seen the present misery.

- “And whoso would not conform themselves to the manners of the Gentiles should be put to death.” We were put to death. It is easy to say I would rather die, but a time is coming when you will have to choose God’s side or give up just to live a little longer in this wicked captivity. Better to die keeping God’s laws, as our forefathers did, because after this life we live on if we keep God’s commandments.
- “Then might a man have seen the present misery.” That is the present misery of our people. They are not happy doing these things; it is slave trauma. The so-called Dominicans at carnival oil themselves with black, wear crosses, the religion of Caesar Borgia, and lick fire, the same as Trinidad and Brazil. Carnival was first practised in the Americas in La Vega and Santo Domingo in the early 1500s, right after Columbus, brought by the Spanish conquistadors through the Roman Catholic authorities, who put crosses on it to give it a semblance of righteousness. It began with the people dressing themselves as Moors and Christians: they were mocking the Israelites, the Moors, the morenos, who were expelled from Spain and Portugal in the 1400s.
