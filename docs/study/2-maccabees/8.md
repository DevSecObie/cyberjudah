## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 8-9, 12-14, 18, 36, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 8:1-2](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v1)</sup> Then Judas Maccabeus, and they that were with him, went privily into the towns, and called their kinsfolks together, and took unto them all such as continued in the Jews’ religion, and assembled about six thousand men.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v2)</sup> And they called upon the Lord, that he would look upon the people that was trodden down of all; and also pity the temple profaned of ungodly men;

- Judas Maccabeus “and they that were with him, went privily into the towns”, gathered all “such as continued in the Jews’ religion, and assembled about six thousand men.” “And they called upon the Lord, that he would look upon the people that was trodden down of all; and also pity the temple profaned of ungodly men.” They called upon the Lord.
- Judas Maccabeus went privily into the towns, called his kinsfolks together, “and took unto them all such as continued in the Jews’ religion.” The Jews’ religion: keeping the commandments of God.
- “And also pity the temple profaned of ungodly men.” Those other nations are ungodly men.

**[2 Maccabees 8:8](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v8)</sup> So when Philip saw that this man increased by little and little, and that things prospered with him still more and more, he wrote unto Ptolemeus, the governor of Celosyria and Phenice, to yield more aid to the king’s affairs.

- “So when Philip saw that this man increased by little and little”: this man is Judas Maccabeus. “And that things prospered with him still more and more”, so Philip wrote to the governor “to yield more aid to the king’s affairs.”

**[2 Maccabees 8:9](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v9)</sup> Then forthwith choosing Nicanor the son of Patroclus, one of his special friends, he sent him with no fewer than twenty thousand of all nations under him, to root out the whole generation of the Jews; and with him he joined also Gorgias a captain, who in matters of war had great experience.

- He chose Nicanor “the son of Patroclus, one of his special friends”, and “sent him with no fewer than twenty thousand of all nations under him.” They called on Nicanor to come against our forefather Judas Maccabeus, because he was doing much work and putting many to death. He was a problem they couldn’t deal with.

**[2 Maccabees 8:12-13](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v12)</sup> Now when word was brought unto Judas of Nicanor’s coming, and he had imparted unto those that were with him that the army was at hand,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v13)</sup> They that were fearful, and distrusted the justice of God, fled, and conveyed themselves away.

- “Now when word was brought unto Judas of Nicanor’s coming”, “they that were fearful, and distrusted the justice of God, fled, and conveyed themselves away.” Those who are fearful and run do not believe in God at all.
- What is the justice of God? Recompensing vengeance to our enemies, taking us out of slavery, putting us in rulership and them in captivity. That is God’s justice. When you flee from that, you do not believe in God.
- They are still dealing with fear here. “When word was brought unto Judas of Nicanor’s coming, and he had imparted unto those that were with him that the army was at hand”: he told his men, Nicanor is coming against us; the army is at hand.
- “They that were fearful, and distrusted the justice of God, fled, and conveyed themselves away.” They distrusted the justice of God. When the trouble comes on this side, brothers and sisters, those who are fearful are going to do the exact same thing. And that is going to be your name, something you can’t undo. Build yourselves up. Tap into your full potential so this doesn’t end up being you.

**[2 Maccabees 8:14](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v14)</sup> Others sold all that they had left, and withal besought the Lord to deliver them, being sold by the wicked Nicanor before they met together:

- “Others sold all that they had left, and withal besought the Lord to deliver them.” The others had full assurance and full confidence; they trusted that the Lord would deliver them, though they were “sold by the wicked Nicanor before they met together.” So you have a choice: flee with the fearful, or trust the Lord to deliver you.

**[2 Maccabees 8:18](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v18)</sup> For they, said he, trust in their weapons and boldness; but our confidence is in the Almighty God, who at a beck can cast down both them that come against us, and also all the world.

- “For they, said he, trust in their weapons and boldness; but our confidence is in the Almighty God, who at a beck can cast down both them that come against us, and also all the world.” The nations trust in their weapons and boldness; our confidence is in the Almighty. If God wanted to, he could cast down the whole world for us.

**[2 Maccabees 8:36](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/2-maccabees/8#v36)</sup> Thus he, that took upon him to make good to the Romans their tribute by means of the captives in Jerusalem, told abroad, that the Jews had God to fight for them, and therefore they could not be hurt, because they followed the laws that he gave them.

- Nicanor, “that took upon him to make good to the Romans their tribute by means of the captives in Jerusalem, told abroad, that the Jews had God to fight for them, and therefore they could not be hurt, because they followed the laws that he gave them.” Our God fights for us when we follow his laws.
