## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 16-22, 33-34, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 10:16](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v16)</sup> Then they that were with Maccabeus made supplication, and besought God that he would be their helper; and so they ran with violence upon the strong holds of the Idumeans,

- “Then they that were with Maccabeus made supplication, and besought God that he would be their helper; and so they ran with violence upon the strong holds of the Idumeans”: fighting the Europeans.

**[2 Maccabees 10:17](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v17)</sup> And assaulting them strongly, they won the holds, and kept off all that fought upon the wall, and slew all that fell into their hands, and killed no fewer than twenty thousand.

- “And assaulting them strongly, they won the holds”, and “killed no fewer than twenty thousand.” We won.

**[2 Maccabees 10:18](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v18)</sup> And because certain, who were no less than nine thousand, were fled together into two very strong castles, having all manner of things convenient to sustain the siege,

- “Nine thousand, were fled together into two very strong castles, having all manner of things convenient to sustain the siege”: the nine thousand who got away fortified themselves so they could survive.

**[2 Maccabees 10:19](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v19)</sup> Maccabeus left Simon and Joseph, and Zaccheus also, and them that were with him, who were enough to besiege them, and departed himself unto those places which more needed his help.

- “Maccabeus left Simon and Joseph, and Zaccheus also, and them that were with him, who were enough to besiege them”: you take over and kill these, we'll be back.
- They were at war. Maccabeus had to leave some of his men and go somewhere else where there was a hot fight.

**[2 Maccabees 10:20](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v20)</sup> Now they that were with Simon, being led with covetousness, were persuaded for money, through certain of those that were in the castle, and took seventy thousand drachms, and let some of them escape.

- “Now they that were with Simon, being led with covetousness, were persuaded for money, through certain of those that were in the castle, and took seventy thousand drachms, and let some of them escape.” The ones who fled into the castles bribed them: let us escape and we'll pay you.
- “Now they that were with Simon, being led with covetousness, were persuaded for money”. A lot of you are going to leave for money. They “took seventy thousand drachms, and let some of them escape”: they let the enemies go, through money.

**[2 Maccabees 10:21-22](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v21)</sup> But when it was told Maccabeus what was done, he called the governors of the people together, and accused those men, that they had sold their brethren for money, and set their enemies free to fight against them.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v22)</sup> So he slew those that were found traitors, and immediately took the two castles.

- Maccabeus “accused those men, that they had sold their brethren for money, and set their enemies free to fight against them.” They sold out their brothers for money.
- “So he slew those that were found traitors”. That is what they did to traitors back then: killed them. Traitors get death.
- He accused those men “that they had sold their brethren for money, and set their enemies free to fight against them.” Many of you will sell out your brothers for money. The things written aforetime were written for our learning. Setting the enemy free is letting the white man have free reign in your mind, and it has already happened: a lot of brothers left and went back to the church.
- “So he slew those that were found traitors”. They were found traitors; they became treacherous.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 13:21](/bible/2-maccabees/13#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/2-maccabees/13#v21)</sup> But Rhodocus, who was in the Jews’ host, disclosed the secrets to the enemies; therefore he was sought out, and when they had gotten him, they put him in prison.

    Here comes another one: Simon, Jason, Menelaus, and now Rhodocus. “But Rhodocus, who was in the Jews’ host, disclosed the secrets to the enemies”. He was among us, an informant, like some sitting in here telling the heathen what is going on with us.

**[2 Maccabees 10:33-34](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v33)</sup> But they that were with Maccabeus laid siege against the fortress courageously four days.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/2-maccabees/10#v34)</sup> And they that were within, trusting to the strength of the place, blasphemed exceedingly, and uttered wicked words.

- They that were with Maccabeus laid siege to a fortress for four days, and the heathen inside, “trusting to the strength of the place”, were arrogant: you can’t get in here, your God ain’t going to do nothing. They “blasphemed exceedingly, and uttered wicked words.”
- So blaspheming is uttering wicked words against the Lord. Cursing, reproaching and blaspheming are synonymous.
