## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 1:1](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v1)</sup> The brethren, the Jews that be at Jerusalem and in the land of Judea, wish unto the brethren, the Jews that are throughout Egypt, health and peace:

- Some exhortation. The brethren at Jerusalem “wish unto the brethren, the Jews that are throughout Egypt, health and peace”. That’s what we got to do: wish and pray for our brothers and sisters in our various captivities, scattered abroad. We got to pray for health and peace for one another.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 91:6](/bible/psalms/91#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/91#v6)</sup> Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.

    Good health, because of “the pestilence that walketh in darkness”. You got these different diseases, and now something new with these toxic chemical outbreaks. You can’t see all of that. It already burned; it’s already in the air. You’re going to work every day, or school, or the grocery store, and that pestilence is moving around in darkness, something you cannot see. That’s why we got to pray for one another, and pray for health for our brothers and sisters.
  - **[Isaiah 26:3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)</sup> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

    Because it says peace. “Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee”. God will keep him in perfect peace if his mind is stayed on him. So throughout the turmoil, throughout the chaos, we got to keep our minds focused on doing the work of the Lord: studying, exhorting one another.

**[2 Maccabees 1:2](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v2)</sup> God be gracious unto you, and remember his covenant that he made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, his faithful servants;

- “God be gracious unto you, and remember his covenant that he made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, his faithful servants”. That’s the key. Verse 1 says health and peace, and verse 2 shows the only way we’re going to get that health and peace: if we remember his covenant.
- “God be gracious unto you, and remember his covenant that he made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, his faithful servants”. That is why we are not consumed: he remembers the agreement he made with us way back then.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 33:6](/bible/isaiah/33#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/33#v6)</sup> And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.

    Verse 1 says health and peace, and verse 2 says the only way we get it is if we remember his covenant. “And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.”

**[2 Maccabees 1:3](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v3)</sup> And give you all an heart to serve him, and to do his will, with a good courage and a willing mind;

- “And give you all an heart to serve him, and to do his will, with a good courage”. Courage is the opposite of fear. When you read Wisdom of Solomon 8, it describes the attributes that come along with wisdom, and fortitude is one of them: the ability to see what’s going on, the perils, and still keep your composure and deal with it as it comes. “With a good courage and a willing mind”.
- “And give you all an heart to serve him, and to do his will”. If you want to return and repent, he will give you a heart to serve him and to do his will, to keep his law, statutes and commandments.
- “With a good courage and a willing mind”. We have to have a willing mind to serve the Lord, come with a sincere heart, be loyal in serving him, and come out of all this wickedness we learned here in America.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 9:16-17](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v16)</sup> For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of: for necessity is laid upon me; yea, woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v17)</sup> For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward: but if against my will, a dispensation of the gospel is committed unto me.

    “For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of”: we have nothing to glory of, because all the glory goes back to the Father. That is what Paul was saying.

**[2 Maccabees 1:4-5](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v4)</sup> And open your hearts in his law and commandments, and send you peace,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v5)</sup> And hear your prayers, and be at one with you, and never forsake you in time of trouble.

- “And open your hearts in his law and commandments, and send you peace, And hear your prayers, and be at one with you, and never forsake you in time of trouble.” Our fortitude and our courage come from our trust in God. That’s it. So let’s focus on that and build on it: build on our faith, and on our focus and belief that God will deliver us from these perils.
- “And open your hearts in his law and commandments, and send you peace”. We have to open our minds back to his laws and commandments; that is why we have to return, wake up, and keep them. You wonder why you have no peace in your life, why you are all over the place? Because you are not serving him, not keeping his law and commandments in sincerity.
- “And hear your prayers, and be at one with you, and never forsake you in time of trouble.” He will hear your prayers, and you will be at one with him. He will never forsake you in the time of trouble if you are sincere and honest with him, walking right in his sight, waking up and returning to him in sincerity. He will hear your cries and prayers, be peace to you, and help you through your hard times.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 1:11](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v11)</sup> Insomuch as God hath delivered us from great perils, we thank him highly, as having been in battle against a king.

    “Insomuch as God hath delivered us from great perils, we thank him highly, as having been in battle against a king.” We’re reading what happened for our forefathers aforetime. If the Most High didn’t forsake our forefathers then, he’s not going to forsake us now. And we got to believe that.
  - **[Isaiah 59:1-2](/bible/isaiah/59#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/59#v1)</sup> Behold, the Lord’s hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/59#v2)</sup> But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.

    This is how you would not be at one with God. “Behold, the Lord's hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear”. His hand is not too short to reach down and help you when you are afflicted or in trouble, and his ear is not too heavy to hear your prayers and your cries.

**[2 Maccabees 1:6](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/1#v6)</sup> And now we be here praying for you.

- “And now we be here praying for you.” Repent, wake up, come out of this hypnosis we have been in in America, and start serving the Lord in righteousness.
