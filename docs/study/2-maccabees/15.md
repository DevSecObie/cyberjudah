## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 15 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 11-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 15:11](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v11)</sup> Thus he armed every one of them, not so much with defence of shields and spears, as with comfortable and good words: and beside that, he told them a dream worthy to be believed, as if it had been so indeed, which did not a little rejoice them.

- Judas Maccabeus armed every one of his men “not so much with defence of shields and spears, as with comfortable and good words”, and told them “a dream worthy to be believed”. That dream inspired them to keep fighting.

**[2 Maccabees 15:12](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v12)</sup> And this was his vision: That Onias, who had been high priest, a virtuous and a good man, reverend in conversation, gentle in condition, well spoken also, and exercised from a child in all points of virtue, holding up his hands prayed for the whole body of the Jews.

- This shows the character of Onias, a brother and a priest: Onias III, the high priest in the days of the Maccabees. He was “a virtuous and a good man, reverend in conversation, gentle in condition, well spoken also, and exercised from a child in all points of virtue”, and he “prayed for the whole body of the Jews.” This is the good brother the whole history turns on, the man who was betrayed.

**[2 Maccabees 15:13-16](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v13)</sup> This done, in like manner there appeared a man with gray hairs, and exceeding glorious, who was of a wonderful and excellent majesty.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v14)</sup> Then Onias answered, saying, This is a lover of the brethren, who prayeth much for the people, and for the holy city, to wit, Jeremias the prophet of God.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v15)</sup> Whereupon Jeremias holding forth his right hand gave to Judas a sword of gold, and in giving it spake thus,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v16)</sup> Take this holy sword, a gift from God, with the which thou shalt wound the adversaries.

- The dream had both Onias and Jeremiah in it. Onias said of Jeremiah, “This is a lover of the brethren, who prayeth much for the people, and for the holy city”, and Jeremiah gave Judas “a sword of gold”, saying, “Take this holy sword, a gift from God, with the which thou shalt wound the adversaries.”
- We must step into our vocation and not be afraid of being great. The Lord compares us to fine gold, and gold is not easy to get; you have to mine for it. A leader is needed to bring comfort to a people in decay, to those who have been robbed, raped and murdered, and no nation on this planet needs great leaders more than ours.
- “Take this holy sword, a gift from God, with the which thou shalt wound the adversaries.” This is Judas Maccabeus preparing the men for war. We are at war, brothers, against sin and against our physical enemies, the children of Satan, Edom, Esau.

**[2 Maccabees 15:17-18](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v17)</sup> Thus being well comforted by the words of Judas, which were very good, and able to stir them up to valour, and to encourage the hearts of the young men, they determined not to pitch camp, but courageously to set upon them, and manfully to try the matter by conflict, because the city and the sanctuary and the temple were in danger.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v18)</sup> For the care that they took for their wives, and their children, their brethren, and kinsfolks, was in least account with them: but the greatest and principal fear was for the holy temple.

- Those words stirred the young men “to valour”, and they set courageously upon the enemy “because the city and the sanctuary and the temple were in danger.”
- “The care that they took for their wives, and their children, their brethren, and kinsfolks, was in least account with them: but the greatest and principal fear was for the holy temple.” It is not that they did not care for their wives, their children and their brethren. Their love for the temple was greater than the love of brother and sister, man and wife.
- “Thus being well comforted by the words of Judas”: not Judas's own mindset, the words of God. The words of God comfort people who have a spirit of fear and stir them up to be courageous in the time of fear. That is why we have leaders, and that is why the nations destroy the leaders first: in some cases the leaders are the lifeline of the people.
- His words were able “to stir them up to valour, and to encourage the hearts of the young men, they determined not to pitch camp, but courageously to set upon them, and manfully to try the matter by conflict.” That is the result of a great leader: through comfort he stirred the people up to try the matter in battle, and gave them the wisdom they needed to fight against evil.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 15:12](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v12)</sup> And this was his vision: That Onias, who had been high priest, a virtuous and a good man, reverend in conversation, gentle in condition, well spoken also, and exercised from a child in all points of virtue, holding up his hands prayed for the whole body of the Jews.

    How do you become a man known for giving comfort to the people like Judas? “And this was his vision”: leaders are visionaries, and where there is no vision the people perish.
  - **[2 Maccabees 15:9-10](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v9)</sup> And so comforting them out of the law and the prophets, and withal putting them in mind of the battles that they won afore, he made them more cheerful.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v10)</sup> And when he had stirred up their minds, he gave them their charge, shewing them therewithal the falsehood of the heathen, and the breach of oaths.

    To be a leader you have to know the condition of the battle. If the first time you are hearing that there is a war going on is now, almost a month in, you are asleep.
