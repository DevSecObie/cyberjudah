## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3, 5-7, 37-38, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 14:3](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v3)</sup> Now one Alcimus, who had been high priest, and had defiled himself wilfully in the times of their mingling with the Gentiles, seeing that by no means he could save himself, nor have any more access to the holy altar,

- Of old time there was a house negro who wanted position and authority, and he decided to slander his brothers in order to gain that authority. You have them on YouTube too, and you used to have one up in here who is now gone.
- This man “had been high priest, and had defiled himself wilfully in the times of their mingling with the Gentiles”. He defiled himself willingly; he gave in. He wasn’t forced. He wanted to fall behind white folks: he loved white folks, he loved the Greeks. “Nor have any more access to the holy altar”, because he was of the priestly line.

**[2 Maccabees 14:5-6](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v5)</sup> Howbeit, having gotten opportunity to further his foolish enterprize, and being called into counsel by Demetrius, and asked how the Jews stood affected, and what they intended, he answered thereunto:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v6)</sup> Those of the Jews that be called Assideans, whose captain is Judas Maccabeus, nourish war, and are seditious, and will not let the realm be in peace.

- “Howbeit, having gotten opportunity to further his foolish enterprize”: to gain position as high priest. Called into counsel and asked how the Jews stood affected and what they intended, he answered that those “whose captain is Judas Maccabeus, nourish war, and are seditious”. So he is slandering them.

**[2 Maccabees 14:7](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v7)</sup> Therefore I, being deprived of mine ancestors’ honour, I mean the high priesthood, am now come hither:

- “Therefore I, being deprived of mine ancestors’ honour, I mean the high priesthood, am now come hither”. Being deprived of what? His ancestors’ honour, which was the Levitical priesthood. He is referring to his priesthood regarding his ancestry, the Levitical line. But the key word here is ancestors. Even our wicked forefathers used the term. Ancestors is in the Bible.

**[2 Maccabees 14:37](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v37)</sup> Now was there accused unto Nicanor one Razis, one of the elders of Jerusalem, a lover of his countrymen, and a man of very good report, who for his kindness was called a father of the Jews.

- Razis, one of the elders of Jerusalem, was “a lover of his countrymen.” That is nationalism. That is loving your race. He was “a man of very good report, who for his kindness was called a father of the Jews.”

**[2 Maccabees 14:38](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v38)</sup> For in the former times, when they mingled not themselves with the Gentiles, he had been accused of Judaism, and did boldly jeopard his body and life with all vehemency for the religion of the Jews.

- “For in the former times, when they mingled not themselves with the Gentiles”: that is religious exclusivism. “He had been accused of Judaism, and did boldly jeopard his body and life with all vehemency for the religion of the Jews.”
