## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Maccabees 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 29, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Maccabees 2:29](/bible/2-maccabees/2#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/2-maccabees/2#v29)</sup> For as the master builder of a new house must care for the whole building; but he that undertaketh to set it out, and paint it, must seek out fit things for the adorning thereof: even so I think it is with us.

- “For as the master builder of a new house must care for the whole building; but he that undertaketh to set it out, and paint it, must seek out fit things for the adorning thereof: even so I think it is with us.” The writer is saying that we are that master builder. They were building a temple and putting things in it, the same way you do when you get a new house. That’s how it is in God’s truth: we build one another, we got to bring things in.
