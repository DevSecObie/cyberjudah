## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Song of the Three Holy Children 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-9, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Song of the Three Holy Children 1:3](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v3)</sup> Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers: thy name is worthy to be praised and glorified for evermore:

- What the three Hebrews said about Babylon was true back then and is truer for us today. “Blessed art thou, O Lord God of our fathers”: they are in the fire, going through tribulation. It is what Spain called the auto de fe, the act of faith: during the Spanish Inquisition they burned the Black Moors and the black Jews alive if they did not become Christian. They got that from Babylon: if you did not worship Nebuchadnezzar’s idol, he threw you in the fire.

**[Song of the Three Holy Children 1:4](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v4)</sup> For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done to us: yea, true are all thy works, thy ways are right, and all thy judgments truth.

- “For thou art righteous in all the things that thou hast done to us.” Imagine it: they are in the fire, and they say you are righteous for all you have done to us. This is what black and Latino Christians need to understand: everything that has happened to us in this country and wherever we are scattered, God is just. Slavery, tribulation, persecution, mutilation: we have deserved it and more. “True are all thy works, thy ways are right, and all thy judgments truth.”

**[Song of the Three Holy Children 1:5](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v5)</sup> In all the things that thou hast brought upon us, and upon the holy city of our fathers, even Jerusalem, thou hast executed true judgment: for according to truth and judgment didst thou bring all these things upon us because of our sins.

- “Thou hast executed true judgment.” It was prophesied that if we broke the laws this would happen; our people did not believe it, and we suffered the consequences. “For according to truth and judgment didst thou bring all these things upon us because of our sins.”

**[Song of the Three Holy Children 1:6](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v6)</sup> For we have sinned and committed iniquity, departing from thee.

- “For we have sinned and committed iniquity, departing from thee.” This is what many of our people will not confess. Christians will not take any accountability for what our forefathers did: we did not do nothing.

**[Song of the Three Holy Children 1:7](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v7)</sup> In all things have we trespassed, and not obeyed thy commandments, nor kept them, neither done as thou hast commanded us, that it might go well with us.

- “In all things have we trespassed”: we broke all the commandments. “That it might go well with us”: that we might stay in the promised land.

**[Song of the Three Holy Children 1:8-9](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v8)</sup> Wherefore all that thou hast brought upon us, and every thing that thou hast done to us, thou hast done in true judgment.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/song-of-the-three-children/1#v9)</sup> And thou didst deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies, most hateful forsakers of God, and to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world.

- “Thou hast done in true judgment. And thou didst deliver us into the hands of lawless enemies.” If you thought ancient Babylon was lawless, this place is way worse: this is Babylon the great. At least back then we knew our identity, our own language and customs; this place destroyed everything from us.
- “Most hateful forsakers of God”: what is worse, they have the Bible but will not obey it. “And to an unjust king, and the most wicked in all the world”: this place, the most wicked in all the world.
