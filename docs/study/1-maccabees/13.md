## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 13 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-4, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 13:2](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v2)</sup> And saw that the people was in great trembling and fear, he went up to Jerusalem, and gathered the people together,

- Simon Maccabeus looked at his people and “saw that the people was in great trembling and fear”. This is the time of the Maccabees, when Greece was ruling.
- He “went up to Jerusalem, and gathered the people together”. Simon took the initiative: when he saw the problems of his people, he gathered them together.

**[1 Maccabees 13:3](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v3)</sup> And gave them exhortation, saying, Ye yourselves know what great things I, and my brethren, and my father’s house, have done for the laws and the sanctuary, the battles also and troubles which we have seen,

- He “gave them exhortation”. That is what our people need: exhortation. Simon reminded them “what great things I, and my brethren, and my father’s house, have done for the laws and the sanctuary, the battles also and troubles which we have seen.”

**[1 Maccabees 13:4](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v4)</sup> By reason whereof all my brethren are slain for Israel’s sake, and I am left alone.

- “By reason whereof all my brethren are slain for Israel’s sake.” These few scriptures deal with fixing our people: a leader sees the problem in the nation, gathers the people, and exhorts them.
