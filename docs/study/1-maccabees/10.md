## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 33, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 10:33](/bible/1-maccabees/10#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-maccabees/10#v33)</sup> Moreover I freely set at liberty every one of the Jews, that were carried captives out of the land of Judea into any part of my kingdom, and I will that all my officers remit the tributes even of their cattle.

- “Moreover I freely set at liberty every one of the Jews, that were carried captives out of the land of Judea into any part of my kingdom”. Part of his kingdom was Asia Minor. Our people were scattered throughout the area of the Greek Empire; diaspora means scattered.
- In the history of the northern diaspora, the Seleucids took Judea and Antiochus III moved several thousand Jewish soldiers and their families from Babylonia to Asia Minor. When you read about the seven churches of Asia Minor, these are Jews. These are not other races; these are our people that were scattered throughout Asia Minor, put there by King Antiochus.
- This is why Esau, cleverly, craftily, said remove the book of Maccabees, the books of the Apocrypha: so that when they read the New Testament they are all confused, and say it’s anybody that believes in Jesus. Nope, it’s not anybody. These are Jews.
- And they always throw the suffix ish on: Jewish. That is to make you think it’s talking about white folks. Ish is a suffix that means somewhat like, not the original; meet me around five-ish is a little before five or a little after, but not five o’clock. When they say they are Jewish, they are telling you in that one word that they are converts, not the originals.
