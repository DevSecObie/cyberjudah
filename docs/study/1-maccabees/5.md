## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 55-63, 67, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 5:55-57](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v55)**

> <sup>[55](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v55)</sup> Now what time as Judas and Jonathan were in the land of Galaad, and Simon his brother in Galilee before Ptolemais,
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v56)</sup> Joseph the son of Zacharias, and Azarias, captains of the garrisons, heard of the valiant acts and warlike deeds which they had done.
>
> <sup>[57](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v57)</sup> Wherefore they said, Let us also get us a name, and go fight against the heathen that are round about us.

- “Now what time as Judas and Jonathan were in the land of Galaad, and Simon his brother in Galilee before Ptolemais, Joseph the son of Zacharias, and Azarias, captains of the garrisons, heard of the valiant acts and warlike deeds which they had done. Wherefore they said, Let us also get us a name, and go fight against the heathen that are round about us.” These men wanted to make a name for themselves like Judas and his brethren.
- “heard of the valiant acts and warlike deeds which they had done.” Joseph and Azarias heard what Judas and Jonathan were doing. “Let us also get us a name”. Let us be like them and get a name. “and go fight against the heathen that are round about us.” We're going to fight the heathen and do like Judas was doing.

**[1 Maccabees 5:58-61](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v58)**

> <sup>[58](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v58)</sup> So when they had given charge unto the garrison that was with them, they went toward Jamnia.
>
> <sup>[59](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v59)</sup> Then came Gorgias and his men out of the city to fight against them.
>
> <sup>[60](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v60)</sup> And so it was, that Joseph and Azarias were put to flight, and pursued unto the borders of Judea: and there were slain that day of the people of Israel about two thousand men.
>
> <sup>[61](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v61)</sup> Thus was there a great overthrow among the children of Israel, because they were not obedient unto Judas and his brethren, but thought to do some valiant act.

- They went toward Jamnia, Gorgias came out against them, Joseph and Azaras were put to flight, “and there were slain that day of the people of Israel about two thousand men. Thus was there a great overthrow among the children of Israel, because they were not obedient unto Judas and his brethren, but thought to do some valiant act.” The ones left in charge to watch over the people tried to be brave and make a name for themselves, and got people killed in the process: pretenders, not real leaders.
- “So when they had given charge unto the garrison that was with them”. The garrison was a group of men; they had thousands of men following their foolishness.
- “Joseph and Azarias were put to flight”. Nobody told them to go out and fight. The Lord put the spirit on Judas, Jonathan, and the brothers, not on them to go out there. They wanted to make a name for themselves under the guise of good intentions, just going out to destroy the people. “there were slain that day of the people of Israel about two thousand men.” Two thousand men got slain because they tried to do a mighty deed under the guise of good intentions.
- “because they were not obedient unto Judas and his brethren”. They were self-willed. They didn't want to follow the men before them. The same thing happens in this truth: men want to recreate the wheel and make their own blueprint when the blueprint is laid out already. The Lord put the spirit on the men who came before them. Our job is to follow orders, follow the men set before us, and the Lord will bless us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 16:25](/bible/proverbs/16#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/16#v25)</sup> There is a way that seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.

    “There is a way that seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.” It seems right to go do it, but it ends in death. We cannot play with God or this Bible. We can't use good intents to do evil works or cover foolishness. We have to learn to follow orders in this truth and follow what the Most High set before us.

**[1 Maccabees 5:62](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v62)**

> <sup>[62](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v62)</sup> Moreover these men came not of the seed of those, by whose hand deliverance was given unto Israel.

- “Moreover these men came not of the seed of those, by whose hand deliverance was given unto Israel.” There is a certain seed the Lord raises up to bring forth deliverance to the people, and everybody is not that seed. Some are meant to watch the doors. There are many Israelites now who are Israel, but not the particular group the Lord is using to bring understanding to the people.
- There is a special seed set up in each captivity: in Babylon’s time Daniel, Ezekiel, Jeremiah and Isaiah; in Persia Zerubbabel, Mordecai, Esther and Joshua; in Greece the Maccabees; in Christ’s time the disciples; and even now.

**[1 Maccabees 5:63](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v63)**

> <sup>[63](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v63)</sup> Howbeit the man Judas and his brethren were greatly renowned in the sight of all Israel, and of all the heathen, wheresoever their name was heard of;

- “Howbeit the man Judas and his brethren were greatly renowned in the sight of all Israel, and of all the heathen, wheresoever their name was heard of”: their names were known, even among the heathen.

**[1 Maccabees 5:67](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v67)**

> <sup>[67](/bible/1-maccabees/5#v67)</sup> At that time certain priests, desirous to shew their valour, were slain in battle, for that they went out to fight unadvisedly.

- “At that time certain priests, desirous to shew their valour, were slain in battle, for that they went out to fight unadvisedly.” You have to follow counsel and do as you are told, or you get yourself hurt. You are not cut out for that battle.
