## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 18, 21, 25, 41, 43, 48, 59, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 3:1](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v1)</sup> Then his son Judas, called Maccabeus, rose up in his stead.

- “Then his son Judas, called Maccabeus, rose up in his stead.”

**[1 Maccabees 3:2](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v2)</sup> And all his brethren helped him, and so did all they that held with his father, and they fought with cheerfulness the battle of Israel.

- “All his brethren helped him”. His brothers helped him, “and so did all they that held with his father, and they fought with cheerfulness the battle of Israel.”

**[1 Maccabees 3:3](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v3)</sup> So he gat his people great honour, and put on a breastplate as a giant, and girt his warlike harness about him, and he made battles, protecting the host with his sword.

- “He gat his people great honour, and put on a breastplate as a giant”, “protecting the host with his sword.”

**[1 Maccabees 3:18](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v18)</sup> Unto whom Judas answered, It is no hard matter for many to be shut up in the hands of a few; and with the God of heaven it is all one, to deliver with a great multitude, or a small company:

- “Unto whom Judas answered, It is no hard matter for many to be shut up in the hands of a few; and with the God of heaven it is all one, to deliver with a great multitude, or a small company:” A few can overcome many when the God of heaven gives deliverance. The strength of the work is not a head count. Israel can face the world's opposition because God supplies the power.
- The questions about how so many men can gather, march in step, know the scriptures and maintain order have an answer: the commandments of God. That discipline is not difficult for God to produce among a people who obey him. Those rejecting the commandments cannot explain that spirit while claiming the same connection to Christ.
- Hostile comments, restrictions on visibility online and efforts to stop the message are reasons to keep moving, not proof that the people have no power. Others would not fight the work so hard if nothing were happening. Continue reaching the streets and standing with the nation even when the opposition has resources beyond those of the brothers.

**[1 Maccabees 3:21](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v21)</sup> But we fight for our lives and our laws.

- She said it is our duty to fight for our freedom. The Maccabees said, “But we fight for our lives and our laws.” Remember, she didn’t know the Bible; she was not into the scriptures at all. In that time period they wasn’t dealing with the word of God, but they knew about revolution to a degree, something that has been lost by generations today: ‘huh, I can’t spell revolution.’

**[1 Maccabees 3:25](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v25)</sup> Then began the fear of Judas and his brethren, and an exceeding great dread, to fall upon the nations round about them:

- “Then began the fear of Judas and his brethren, and an exceeding great dread, to fall upon the nations round about them:” Judas and his brethren brought fear upon the nations around them. The present unity and public work of Israel are connected to that same fear: the nations see men moving together in the spirit of God and keeping the commandments. Their efforts to fight back do not mean that Israel should stop.
- The victory comes from God. Prayer acknowledges that the people are not winning by their own power. The work of reaching the earth with the scriptures continues because the Most High supplies the strength, even when opposition comes from many directions.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 11:11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)</sup> And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.

    “And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.” The three days and a half are understood as three hundred fifty years. The spirit of life entering the people is the commandments of God coming into Israel, bringing the people to their feet. Their renewed obedience and visible work produce fear in those who see them.

**[1 Maccabees 3:41](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v41)</sup> And the merchants of the country, hearing the fame of them, took silver and gold very much, with servants, and came into the camp to buy the children of Israel for slaves: a power also of Syria and of the land of the Philistines joined themselves unto them.

- After the Persian captivity came the Greek captivity. What did the Greeks do? “The merchants of the country, hearing the fame of them, took silver and gold very much, with servants, and came into the camp to buy the children of Israel for slaves.” This slavery is different from what we read in Leviticus 25. We look at the slave auctions and think this is the first time it ever happened; no, the Greeks did this too. “A power also of Syria and of the land of the Philistines joined themselves unto them”: the other nations were helping the Greeks.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:41](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v41)</sup> Thou shalt beget sons and daughters, but thou shalt not enjoy them; for they shall go into captivity.

    What kind of slavery is 1 Maccabees 3:41? This kind: “Thou shalt beget sons and daughters, but thou shalt not enjoy them; for they shall go into captivity.” That is slavery, the curse.

**[1 Maccabees 3:43](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v43)</sup> They said one to another, Let us restore the decayed estate of our people, and let us fight for our people and the sanctuary.

- “They said one to another, Let us restore the decayed estate of our people, and let us fight for our people and the sanctuary.” From Babylon to Persia to Greece to Rome, there was always a brother, an Israelite, who would rise up for the welfare of his people. Here it is Mattathias and his sons, Judas Maccabeus, Eleazar, Joannan, Jonathan and Simon: these men sought the welfare and care of their people.
- When we do that, we're racist, a black radical hate group. When white folks do that, they're white nationalists, and that's okay. Why? They don't want to see us unite.
- Judas and his brethren, the Maccabees, a group of Levites, “said one to another, Let us restore the decayed” estate of our people. Let us fix the problem we see with our nation.
- That is our condition today. Blacks and Hispanics are a decayed people. We are low, and the reason is that we don’t keep God’s commandments. All of us see the condition of our people every day on the way to work: Brooklyn is jacked up, and the Bronx above all.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Maccabees 3:59](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v59)**
    > <sup>[59](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v59)</sup> For it is better for us to die in battle, than to behold the calamities of our people and our sanctuary.

    “For it is better for us to die in battle, than to behold the calamities of our people and our sanctuary.” Same thing: the Maccabees would rather die fighting for their people than watch their calamities. That is seeking the welfare of your own.
  - **[Jeremiah 2:21](/bible/jeremiah/2#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/jeremiah/2#v21)</sup> Yet I had planted thee a noble vine, wholly a right seed: how then art thou turned into the degenerate plant of a strange vine unto me?

    “Yet I had planted thee a noble vine, wholly a right seed”. The Lord planted us a noble vine: he gave us the commandments and the scriptures and made us the elite, the top people on the planet, holy and right. “How then art thou turned into the degenerate plant of a strange vine unto me?” The Lord looked at us and said, how did you become degenerate?
  - **[Jeremiah 2:19](/bible/jeremiah/2#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/2#v19)</sup> Thine own wickedness shall correct thee, and thy backslidings shall reprove thee: know therefore and see that it is an evil thing and bitter, that thou hast forsaken the Lord thy God, and that my fear is not in thee, saith the Lord God of hosts.

    How did we become that degenerate plant? Verse 19 tells it: “Thine own wickedness shall correct thee, and thy backslidings shall reprove thee”. That is how we became degenerate: our own wickedness. “Know therefore and see that it is an evil thing and bitter, that thou hast forsaken the Lord thy God, and that my fear is not in thee, saith the Lord God of hosts.” Look at our conditions. We are feeling it now.

**[1 Maccabees 3:48](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v48)**

> <sup>[48](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v48)</sup> And laid open the book of the law, wherein the heathen had sought to paint the likeness of their images.

- “And laid open the book of the law, wherein the heathen had sought to paint the likeness of their images.” The Greeks tried to put the likeness of their images into the Bible, and God created man in his image. What race were the Greeks? White people. So white people tried to put their likeness and their image in the Bible when they came against us in the time of Judas Maccabeus. If the Jews were already white, there would have been no reason for them to put their likeness and image in it. That is how important likeness and images are.
- Why do we need a man like Judas? Because the white man destroyed our artwork. They “laid open the book of the law, wherein the heathen had sought to paint the likeness of their images.” The Maccabees saw that the white man painted over the artwork we had of our forefathers.
- That is iconoclasm, the destruction of art. Teach it on the subway. When the brothers presented at a college, the class asked what iconoclasm is; our people don’t know these things.
- The heathen didn’t do that in Kings, in Esther, or in Joshua. There other nations followed our laws. But when the white man took our records, he painted over our forefathers.

  Precepts:
  - **[Habakkuk 2:4](/bible/habakkuk/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/habakkuk/2#v4)</sup> Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith.

    This is why the white man did that: “his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him”.

**[1 Maccabees 3:59](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v59)**

> <sup>[59](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v59)</sup> For it is better for us to die in battle, than to behold the calamities of our people and our sanctuary.

- “For it is better for us to die in battle, than to behold the calamities of our people and our sanctuary.” It is better to die fighting than to stand by while the nation and sanctuary suffer calamity. The feast of dedication honours that resolve. Living in shame while the people are destroyed is not the standard for men of God.
- The challenge is brought home to men who want the position of leaders without discipline, responsibility or self-control. A woman's criticism of men who demand submission while lacking order in their own lives introduces the need to examine that weakness. The warrior's commitment has to shape conduct, not remain a slogan.
