## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 26-38, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 7:26](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v26)</sup> Then the king sent Nicanor, one of his honourable princes, a man that bare deadly hate unto Israel, with commandment to destroy the people.

- The king sent Nicanor, “a man that bare deadly hate unto Israel”, who hated Israel with a passion, “with commandment to destroy the people.”

**[1 Maccabees 7:27-30](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v27)</sup> So Nicanor came to Jerusalem with a great force; and sent unto Judas and his brethren deceitfully with friendly words, saying,
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v28)</sup> Let there be no battle between me and you; I will come with a few men, that I may see you in peace.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v29)</sup> He came therefore to Judas, and they saluted one another peaceably. Howbeit the enemies were prepared to take away Judas by violence.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v30)</sup> Which thing after it was known to Judas, to wit, that he came unto him with deceit, he was sore afraid of him, and would see his face no more.

- He sent to Judas “deceitfully with friendly words”: “Let there be no battle between me and you.” I just want to talk, no beef, I’ll bring a few men. That is Esau. But “the enemies were prepared to take away Judas by violence”: he was plotting on him. When Judas knew it, he would see his face no more.

**[1 Maccabees 7:31-32](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v31)</sup> Nicanor also, when he saw that his counsel was discovered, went out to fight against Judas beside Capharsalama:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v32)</sup> Where there were slain of Nicanor’s side about five thousand men, and the rest fled into the city of David.

- Nicanor’s counsel was discovered, he got exposed, and he went out to fight Judas, and about five thousand of his men were slain. Judas was a fighter, a warrior.

**[1 Maccabees 7:33-34](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v33)</sup> After this went Nicanor up to mount Sion, and there came out of the sanctuary certain of the priests and certain of the elders of the people, to salute him peaceably, and to shew him the burnt sacrifice that was offered for the king.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v34)</sup> But he mocked them, and laughed at them, and abused them shamefully, and spake proudly,

- Priests and elders came out of the sanctuary to salute him peaceably; the news of the beef between him and Judas had not reached them yet. “But he mocked them, and laughed at them, and abused them shamefully, and spake proudly.”

**[1 Maccabees 7:35](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v35)</sup> And sware in his wrath, saying, Unless Judas and his host be now delivered into my hands, if ever I come again in safety, I will burn up this house: and with that he went out in a great rage.

- He swore that unless Judas were delivered into his hands, “I will burn up this house”: bring me Judas or I’ll burn this temple to the ground.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 14:31-36](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v31)</sup> But the other, knowing that he was notably prevented by Judas’ policy, came into the great and holy temple, and commanded the priests, that were offering their usual sacrifices, to deliver him the man.
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v32)</sup> And when they sware that they could not tell where the man was whom he sought,
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v33)</sup> He stretched out his right hand toward the temple, and made an oath in this manner: If ye will not deliver me Judas as a prisoner, I will lay this temple of God even with the ground, and I will break down the altar, and erect a notable temple unto Bacchus.
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v34)</sup> After these words he departed. Then the priests lifted up their hands toward heaven, and besought him that was ever a defender of their nation, saying in this manner;
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v35)</sup> Thou, O Lord of all things, who hast need of nothing, wast pleased that the temple of thine habitation should be among us:
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/2-maccabees/14#v36)</sup> Therefore now, O holy Lord of all holiness, keep this house ever undefiled, which lately was cleansed, and stop every unrighteous mouth.

    The same account told in more detail. Nicanor came into the temple and commanded the priests to deliver him Judas, and when they swore they could not tell where he was, “He stretched out his right hand toward the temple, and made an oath”: “I will lay this temple of God even with the ground, and I will break down the altar, and erect a notable temple unto Bacchus.” He would tear down the temple and set up another god in it.

**[1 Maccabees 7:36-38](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v36)</sup> Then the priests entered in, and stood before the altar and the temple, weeping, and saying,
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v37)</sup> Thou, O Lord, didst choose this house to be called by thy name, and to be a house of prayer and petition for thy people:
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-maccabees/7#v38)</sup> Be avenged of this man and his host, and let them fall by the sword: remember their blasphemies, and suffer them not to continue any longer.

- The priests stood before the altar weeping: “Be avenged of this man and his host, and let them fall by the sword: remember their blasphemies, and suffer them not to continue any longer.” We prayed for his death. We prayed, Lord, please kill this man for threatening to destroy your temple. We did not say, Lord, forgive him. That is white Jesus talk, Cesare Borgia talk.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 15:30-34](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v30)</sup> And Judas, who was ever the chief defender of the citizens both in body and mind, and who continued his love toward his countrymen all his life, commanded to strike off Nicanor’s head, and his hand with his shoulder, and bring them to Jerusalem.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v31)</sup> So when he was there, and called them of his nation together, and set the priests before the altar, he sent for them that were of the tower,
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v32)</sup> And shewed them vile Nicanor’s head, and the hand of that blasphemer, which with proud brags he had stretched out against the holy temple of the Almighty.
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v33)</sup> And when he had cut out the tongue of that ungodly Nicanor, he commanded that they should give it by pieces unto the fowls, and hang up the reward of his madness before the temple.
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/2-maccabees/15#v34)</sup> So every man praised toward the heaven the glorious Lord, saying, Blessed be he that hath kept his own place undefiled.

    The prayer was answered. Judas “commanded to strike off Nicanor’s head, and his hand with his shoulder”: his whole right arm, the one he stretched out against the temple. He brought them to Jerusalem “And shewed them vile Nicanor’s head, and the hand of that blasphemer, which with proud brags he had stretched out against the holy temple of the Almighty.”
