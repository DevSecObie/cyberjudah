## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 41-53, 56, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 4:41-42](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v41)</sup> Then Judas appointed certain men to fight against those that were in the fortress, until he had cleansed the sanctuary.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v42)</sup> So he chose priests of blameless conversation, such as had pleasure in the law:

- Chapter 4 is the cleansing of the temple and the rededicating of the altar. “He chose priests of blameless conversation, such as had pleasure in the law”. That is who your circle has to be. If you want things to get better, change your circle. Fringes and a border of blue don’t make everybody right. Coming in, a man can keep getting in trouble because his circle is idiots: fights on 42nd Street with some clowns, jumping the turnstile and getting arrested. Look around and see it: these are a bunch of idiots, and I am an idiot for being with these clowns. Change your circle, and go be around the elders.

**[1 Maccabees 4:43-46](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v43)</sup> Who cleansed the sanctuary, and bare out the defiled stones into an unclean place.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v44)</sup> And when as they consulted what to do with the altar of burnt offerings, which was profaned;
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v45)</sup> They thought it best to pull it down, lest it should be a reproach to them, because the heathen had defiled it: wherefore they pulled it down,
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v46)</sup> And laid up the stones in the mountain of the temple in a convenient place, until there should come a prophet to shew what should be done with them.

- They cleansed the sanctuary and bare out the defiled stones. The altar of burnt offerings the heathen had defiled they pulled down, and laid up the stones “until there should come a prophet to shew what should be done with them.” They said, we must wait for a prophet to show us what to do.

**[1 Maccabees 4:47-51](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v47)**

> <sup>[47](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v47)</sup> Then they took whole stones according to the law, and built a new altar according to the former;
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v48)</sup> And made up the sanctuary, and the things that were within the temple, and hallowed the courts.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v49)</sup> They made also new holy vessels, and into the temple they brought the candlestick, and the altar of burnt offerings, and of incense, and the table.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v50)</sup> And upon the altar they burned incense, and the lamps that were upon the candlestick they lighted, that they might give light in the temple.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v51)</sup> Furthermore they set the loaves upon the table, and spread out the veils, and finished all the works which they had begun to make.

- They built a new altar of whole stones according to the law, made new holy vessels, and “into the temple they brought the candlestick”: that is the menorah. They lighted the lamps “that they might give light in the temple”, set the loaves on the table and spread out the veils.

**[1 Maccabees 4:52-53](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v52)**

> <sup>[52](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v52)</sup> Now on the five and twentieth day of the ninth month, which is called the month Casleu, in the hundred forty and eighth year, they rose up betimes in the morning,
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v53)</sup> And offered sacrifice according to the law upon the new altar of burnt offerings, which they had made.

- “On the five and twentieth day of the ninth month, which is called the month Casleu”: the ninth month is November. They offered sacrifice according to the law on the new altar. That is the feast of dedication.

**[1 Maccabees 4:56](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v56)**

> <sup>[56](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v56)</sup> And so they kept the dedication of the altar eight days, and offered burnt offerings with gladness, and sacrificed the sacrifice of deliverance and praise.

- “They kept the dedication of the altar eight days, and offered burnt offerings with gladness, and sacrificed the sacrifice of deliverance and praise.”

  Precepts:
  - **[John 10:22-23](/bible/john/10#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/john/10#v22)</sup> And it was at Jerusalem the feast of the dedication, and it was winter.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/john/10#v23)</sup> And Jesus walked in the temple in Solomon’s porch.

    “It was at Jerusalem the feast of the dedication, and it was winter. And Jesus walked in the temple in Solomon’s porch.” In November it gets cold. Christ kept the feast of dedication in the winter, and walked in the temple because it was a holy convocation.
