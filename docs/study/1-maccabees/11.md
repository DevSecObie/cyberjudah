## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 11 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 21, 34, 57, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 11:21](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v21)</sup> Then certain ungodly persons, who hated their own people, went unto the king, and told him that Jonathan besieged the tower.

- “Then certain ungodly persons, who hated their own people”: you have pastors that literally hate their congregation. Old women, old men and kids coming in there trying to get themselves right, and men who hate their own people are teaching them a doctrine that is going to get them killed. How do you know? If they hear what we are bringing out, why don’t they go and teach it to their congregation? They know we ain’t lying, and they can’t ignore what the Bible says.
- “Went unto the king, and told him that Jonathan besieged the tower”: quote-unquote snitched. That is what happened in the time of Nat Turner, Denmark Vesey and Gabriel Prosser, when we were trying to get our way out of slavery. Some went and snitched and got the brothers jacked up, because they loved massa so much they could not see themselves without massa. That is called trauma bonding, and it runs deep.
- The angry Chinese woman made a point of saying that seven black jurors found Mr. Chow not guilty. I wonder why. And the boy did not steal any water: he left his bag at the front, went to the water, put it back, and was walking out when they told him to check his pockets, and he had nothing in them.
- There were “ungodly persons, who hated their own people,” who went unto the king and told on Jonathan. We always had a certain remnant of our people who love other races better than their own. You have negroes out there, Christians, who hate their own people. What was true back then in the time of the Maccabees is true today.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 9:6](/bible/romans/9#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/romans/9#v6)</sup> Not as though the word of God hath taken none effect. For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel:

    Not everybody who looks like you is of us. “Not as though the word of God hath taken none effect”: the word of God has not failed and is not going to fail. “For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel.” Just because someone looks like you, speaks the same language as you and eats the same food as you does not mean he is of your people. We have to be aware of this.
  - **[Matthew 24:12](/bible/matthew/24#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/24#v12)</sup> And because iniquity shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold.

    “Because iniquity shall abound”: because sin shall abound, “the love of many shall wax cold.” Emotionless, no empathy, no feeling for your people or even your parents. That is what we are seeing.

**[1 Maccabees 11:34](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v34)</sup> Wherefore we have ratified unto them the borders of Judea, with the three governments of Apherema and Lydda and Ramathem, that are added unto Judea from the country of Samaria, and all things appertaining unto them, for all such as do sacrifice in Jerusalem, instead of the payments which the king received of them yearly aforetime out of the fruits of the earth and of trees.

- Now the Greeks. “Wherefore we have ratified unto them the borders of Judea, with the three governments of Apherema and Lydda and Ramathem, that are added unto Judea from the country of Samaria”. The Greeks had all of Israel to themselves, and as a kind of alliance they rewarded the southern kingdom, the Hasmonean family of Levi, with portions of the land of Samaria, the northern kingdom. The lands, not the people.

**[1 Maccabees 11:57](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v57)**

> <sup>[57](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v57)</sup> At that time young Antiochus wrote unto Jonathan, saying, I confirm thee in the high priesthood, and appoint thee ruler over the four governments, and to be one of the king’s friends.

- When they fought against the Greeks, we gained some power and authority. “At that time young Antiochus wrote unto Jonathan, saying, I confirm thee in the high priesthood, and appoint thee ruler over the four governments”. What are the four governments? Judea is one portion of them.
- “At that time young Antiochus wrote unto Jonathan, saying, I confirm thee in the high priesthood, and appoint thee ruler over the four governments”. Young Antiochus was a later Greek king of the line of the Antiochus read before. The Hasmonean family, Levites, gained power in the time of the Greeks: we fought against the Greeks and gained some independence and sovereignty, and the Greeks gave Jonathan rule over these four governments. Keep this in mind.
- “I confirm thee in the high priesthood, and appoint thee ruler over the four governments”. Portions of the northern kingdom were given back, and that gave the remnants of the northern kingdom who had been exiled the opportunity to return to their allotments during the time of the Greeks. Judah moved into those lands too: Christ was in Galilee, the northern kingdom's land.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Maccabees 11:34](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v34)</sup> Wherefore we have ratified unto them the borders of Judea, with the three governments of Apherema and Lydda and Ramathem, that are added unto Judea from the country of Samaria, and all things appertaining unto them, for all such as do sacrifice in Jerusalem, instead of the payments which the king received of them yearly aforetime out of the fruits of the earth and of trees.

    “Wherefore we have ratified unto them the borders of Judea, with the three governments of Apherema and Lydda and Ramathem, that are added unto Judea”: that’s four, Judea, Apherema, Lydda and Ramathem. “From the country of Samaria”: these are portions of Samaria, “and all things appertaining unto them”, all the lands pertaining to Samaria, “for all such as do sacrifice in Jerusalem, instead of the payments which the king received”.
  - **[1 Maccabees 10:38](/bible/1-maccabees/10#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/1-maccabees/10#v38)</sup> And concerning the three governments that are added to Judea from the country of Samaria, let them be joined with Judea, that they may be reckoned to be under one, nor bound to obey other authority than the high priest’s.

    “And concerning the three governments that are added to Judea from the country of Samaria, let them be joined with Judea, that they may be reckoned to be under one, nor bound to obey other authority than the high priest’s.” Three of the governments were out of the country of Samaria, the land of the northern kingdom, joined with Judea under the high priest.
