## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 21-22, 41-44, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 14:21](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v21)</sup> The ambassadors that were sent unto our people certified us of your glory and honour: wherefore we were glad of their coming,

- This shows where we came from. “The ambassadors that were sent unto our people certified us of your glory and honour: wherefore we were glad of their coming”. Other nations sent ambassadors to us and honoured us.

**[1 Maccabees 14:22](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v22)</sup> And did register the things that they spake in the council of the people in this manner; Numenius son of Antiochus, and Antipater son of Jason, the Jews’ ambassadors, came unto us to renew the friendship they had with us.

- Our forefathers “did register the things that they spake in the council of the people”. We had a registry. We had a system, we had administration. We were independent at one time and governed ourselves, though at this time the Greeks were oppressing us. The Jews’ ambassadors, “Numenius son of Antiochus, and Antipater son of Jason”, came “to renew the friendship they had with us.”
- So even though we are broken as a people, we had records, we had a system, we had order. The average black man doesn’t know this, because he views the Bible as a white man’s book.

**[1 Maccabees 14:41](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v41)</sup> Also that the Jews and priests were well pleased that Simon should be their governor and high priest for ever, until there should arise a faithful prophet;

- “The Jews and priests were well pleased that Simon should be their governor and high priest for ever, until there should arise a faithful prophet”. Simon was a leader: he was the governor and the high priest.
- These men were not prideful enough to make themselves prophets. They understood they were not prophets, and there was no prophet in that time through whom the Most High was communicating to let the people know what to do. Read Kings and Chronicles: there was a king and there was a prophet, and the king consulted the prophet, as King Hezekiah consulted Isaiah. David was both a king and a prophet, and that is what makes David so unique and special, on another level.
- The sisters raise their hands when asked who has read Kings and Chronicles, and the brothers who are supposed to be in the streets are looking down. The brothers have to do some reading.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 17:18-20](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v18)</sup> And it shall be, when he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom, that he shall write him a copy of this law in a book out of that which is before the priests the Levites:
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v19)</sup> And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life: that he may learn to fear the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v20)</sup> That his heart be not lifted up above his brethren, and that he turn not aside from the commandment, to the right hand, or to the left: to the end that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he, and his children, in the midst of Israel.

    This is the exhortation, like Simon’s. The sons of God are the leaders of the earth, and restoring the decayed estate of our nation begins with us. How are we going to remember when we were kings if we don’t go to it?

**[1 Maccabees 14:42](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v42)**

> <sup>[42](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v42)</sup> Moreover that he should be their captain, and should take charge of the sanctuary, to set them over their works, and over the country, and over the armour, and over the fortresses, that, I say, he should take charge of the sanctuary;

- He was made “their captain”, to “take charge of the sanctuary, to set them over their works, and over the country, and over the armour, and over the fortresses”.

**[1 Maccabees 14:43](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v43)</sup> Beside this, that he should be obeyed of every man, and that all the writings in the country should be made in his name, and that he should be clothed in purple, and wear gold:

- “He should be obeyed of every man, and that all the writings in the country should be made in his name”. We were governing ourselves, though the Greeks were oppressing us. A lot of our brothers don’t notice this, and when they hear it they ask what Bible we are reading, because these scriptures are not read.
- He was to “be clothed in purple, and wear gold”. Purple and gold are royal colors, and they are the colors of IUIC.

**[1 Maccabees 14:44](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/1-maccabees/14#v44)</sup> Also that it should be lawful for none of the people or priests to break any of these things, or to gainsay his words, or to gather an assembly in the country without him, or to be clothed in purple, or wear a buckle of gold;

- “It should be lawful for none of the people or priests to break any of these things, or to gainsay his words, or to gather an assembly in the country without him, or to be clothed in purple, or wear a buckle of gold”.
- This shows that we had order, a government, a registry, administration and records. We literally had a kingdom. Our brothers and sisters don’t know it, and that is why it is important to study. The sisters asking what they are supposed to do: don’t be a dumb woman, be reading the Bible, because the sisters in the streets need to learn.
