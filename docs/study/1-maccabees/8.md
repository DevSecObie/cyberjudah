## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 4, 11-16, 24-30, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 8:1](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v1)</sup> Now Judas had heard of the fame of the Romans, that they were mighty and valiant men, and such as would lovingly accept all that joined themselves unto them, and make a league of amity with all that came unto them;

- Write this down: the Roman Republic was Japheth. They helped us against the Greeks in 1 Maccabees chapter 8. The Roman Republic describes the period in which the city state of Rome existed as a republican government, up to 27 BCE, one of the earliest examples of representative democracy in the world. The time of the Maccabees was approximately 166, so this was during the time of the Roman Republic. Judas had heard of the Romans, “that they were mighty and valiant men”: that was the seed of Japheth.
- So the question is, when did Esau take over Rome? Write this down: during this time of the Roman Republic the white man was called the Latins. The Latins belong to a group of Indo-European speaking tribes, conventionally known as the Italic tribes, that populated central and southern Italy during the Italian Iron Age, which began around 900 BC. This is where Esau was. They were called the Latins, and many of them had joined the Roman army of the Roman Republic.
- Then the white man comes in. Julius Caesar was the white man. He began his rise to power in 60 BCE by forging an alliance with another general, another Edomite, Pompey, and a wealthy patrician, and together these three men assumed control of the Roman Republic. Julius Caesar took over.
- Judas Maccabeus had heard of the Romans, “that they were mighty and valiant men, and such as would lovingly accept all that joined themselves unto them, and make a league of amity with all that came unto them”. The Maccabees were around 167 or 168 BC, and the Roman Republic ran from 509 BC, when the last Etruscan king was deposed, to 27 BC. So 1 Maccabees 8 is the Roman Republic: this is Japheth, the seat of Japheth at that time.

**[1 Maccabees 8:4](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v4)</sup> And that by their policy and patience they had conquered all the place, though it were very far from them; and the kings also that came against them from the uttermost part of the earth, till they had discomfited them, and given them a great overthrow, so that the rest did give them tribute every year:

- “And that by their policy and patience they had conquered all the place, though it were very far from them”. Their policy and their patience: they put things in place and move a certain way, and one modern form of it is the School of the Americas. They gave the kings that came against them “a great overthrow, so that the rest did give them tribute every year”. When they can back it up with military power, everybody else falls in line.

**[1 Maccabees 8:11-12](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v11)</sup> It was told him besides, how they destroyed and brought under their dominion all other kingdoms and isles that at any time resisted them;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v12)</sup> But with their friends and such as relied upon them they kept amity: and that they had conquered kingdoms both far and nigh, insomuch as all that heard of their name were afraid of them:

- “They destroyed and brought under their dominion all other kingdoms and isles that at any time resisted them”: resist and you will be crushed. “But with their friends and such as relied upon them they kept amity”, those relying on them for military strength. “All that heard of their name were afraid of them”: they no longer even have to send the military. They send an emissary with their conditions and the threat, and nations far away say, we’ll roll with you. Rome was that powerful, and so is the US: it is commerce now, enforced by the threat of military power, as everyone saw in Venezuela. Nobody throws the first punch.

**[1 Maccabees 8:14-16](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v14)</sup> Yet for all this none of them wore a crown, or was clothed in purple, to be magnified thereby:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-maccabees/8#v15)</sup> Moreover how they had made for themselves a senate house, wherein three hundred and twenty men sat in council daily, consulting alway for the people, to the end they might be well ordered:
>

- These are the articles of war; write it down. This is where America gets it from. “If there come first any war upon the Romans or any of their confederates throughout all their dominion, The people of the Jews shall help them”: if Rome gets hit with something, the Jews must bring their military. “Neither shall they give any thing unto them that make war upon them, or aid them with victuals, weapons, money, or ships”: Israel could give no help, food, weapons, money or ships, to Rome's enemies. “but they shall keep their covenants”.
- “In the same manner also, if war come first upon the nation of the Jews, the Romans shall help them with all their heart”. That is where the NATO articles come from: if you hit one NATO nation, all the other NATO nations must help. “According to these articles did the Romans make a covenant with the people of the Jews.”
- “Howbeit if hereafter the one party or the other shall think meet to add or diminish any thing, they may do it at their pleasures”: see the trick bag? Today Trump bombs Iran, then tells NATO, y'all are supposed to come and help me. They say no: the articles say if any of us is attacked we help each other, and Iran did not attack America; America attacked Iran. So Spain said no, Germany said no, France said no, Britain said no, because you're wrong.
- This is an article of their policy, how they support the ones they put in. Judas Maccabeus was catching so much hell from the Greeks that he went to Rome for help, and this is what Rome told him: “If there come first any war upon the Romans or any of their confederates throughout all their dominion, The people of the Jews shall help them.” What Rome said here is what America does today.
