## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Maccabees 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 23-24, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Maccabees 16:1](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v1)</sup> Then came up John from Gazara, and told Simon his father what Cendebeus had done.

- This is the family tree of our ancestors, the Maccabees. The Hasmonean dynasty refers to Simon: Hasmonean is Simeon, so the Hasmonean dynasty is the descendants of Simon. Simon Maccabeus had a son named John Hyrcanus.
- The history in Josephus shows what John Hyrcanus did. He conquered the whole of Edom and forced its people to convert to Judaism. He took Dora and Marissa, cities of Idumea, and Idumea is Edom. He let the Idumeans stay in the country if they would circumcise themselves and make use of the laws of the Jews: Genesis, Exodus, Leviticus, Numbers, Deuteronomy and the other books. They so desired to live in that country that they submitted to circumcision and the Jewish ways of living, and from then on they were no other than Jews. These were white people. Now you have white people wearing fringes, circumcising themselves and keeping the sabbath day, because they were under our government and forced to live under God’s laws.
- “Then came up John from Gazara”. This John is John Hyrcanus.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Maccabees 13:53](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v53)**
    > <sup>[53](/bible/1-maccabees/13#v53)</sup> And when Simon saw that John his son was a valiant man, he made him captain of all the hosts; and he dwelt in Gazara.

    “When Simon saw that John his son was a valiant man, he made him captain of all the hosts; and he dwelt in Gazara.” That is the same John who comes up from Gazera in 16:1: John Hyrcanus, Simon’s son, the captain of all the hosts.

**[1 Maccabees 16:2](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v2)</sup> Wherefore Simon called his two eldest sons, Judas and John, and said unto them, I, and my brethren, and my father’s house, have ever from our youth unto this day fought against the enemies of Israel; and things have prospered so well in our hands, that we have delivered Israel oftentimes.

- “Simon called his two eldest sons, Judas and John”. It says eldest, not oldest. This is Simon speaking to his son: “I, and my brethren, and my father’s house, have ever from our youth unto this day fought against the enemies of Israel; and things have prospered so well in our hands, that we have delivered Israel oftentimes.”

**[1 Maccabees 16:23](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v23)</sup> As concerning the rest of the acts of John, and his wars, and worthy deeds which he did, and the building of the walls which he made, and his doings,

- “As concerning the rest of the acts of John”: this John is Simon’s son; write it down, this John became John Hyrcanus, the son of Simon, Judas Maccabeus’s brother. “And his wars”: he took over land. He conquered Idumea, he conquered the Arabians, and he forced Idumea to convert to Judaism when he took their land over. That’s how Herod’s family came into power, into existence. “And worthy deeds which he did, and the building of the walls which he made, and his doings”.
- “As concerning the rest of the acts of John, and his wars, and worthy deeds which he did”. This is still John Hyrcanus. Part of those wars were the wars he had against Edom. Our forefathers knew in the records what John did.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 6:2](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v2)</sup> And to pollute also the temple in Jerusalem, and to call it the temple of Jupiter Olympius; and that in Garizim, of Jupiter the Defender of strangers, as they did desire that dwelt in the place.

    When Antiochus defiled the temple and named it Jupiter Olympius, Judas Maccabeus and them came along, conquered the Greeks, cleansed the temple and rededicated it, and they had Hanukkah. The temple was no longer called that. But he had also set up a temple called Jupiter the Defender of strangers in Garizim, as verse two says. John Hyrcanus destroyed it: no more, you’re going to offer sacrifices in Jerusalem. That’s historical. Why? Because he gained authority over that land.

**[1 Maccabees 16:24](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-maccabees/16#v24)</sup> Behold, these are written in the chronicles of his priesthood, from the time he was made high priest after his father.

- “Behold, these are written in the chronicles of his priesthood, from the time he was made high priest after his father.”
- “These are written in the chronicles of his priesthood, from the time he was made high priest after his father.” We don’t have that book, so we rely on Josephus and other sources. But Esau knows this. We forced the Edomites to keep the laws of God.
