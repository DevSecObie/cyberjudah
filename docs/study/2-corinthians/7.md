## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 9-10, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 7:1](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v1)</sup> Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.

- “Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh and spirit, perfecting holiness in the fear of God.” The promises are that he will be our Father and we his sons and daughters. When you cleanse yourself from all this filthiness, you are new.
- We do this in the fear of God, the fear of his judgment. That is why we perfect ourselves.
- “Having therefore these promises, dearly beloved, let us cleanse ourselves from all filthiness of the flesh.” Remember what we read earlier in Ecclesiasticus 17: the Most High watches all of our works, good and bad. So we have to make sure we cleanse ourselves, so we can tap into our full potential.
- We have to cleanse ourselves and abstain from that. You have to abstain.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 6:17-18](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v17)</sup> Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you,
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v18)</sup> And will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.

    What are “these promises”? “And will be a Father unto you, and ye shall be my sons and daughters, saith the Lord Almighty.” But to be his sons and daughters you have to do this: “Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you”. Then you are not giving offense to the ministry, and he can receive you.
  - **[2 Corinthians 5:17](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v17)</sup> Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

    “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature”, “behold, all things are become new.” Cleansed from all filthiness, all things are new. Then I will be your Father and you will be my sons and daughters. But you have to come out of this world; you can’t live in hypocrisy.
  - **[2 Corinthians 5:11-15](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v11)</sup> Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v12)</sup> For we commend not ourselves again unto you, but give you occasion to glory on our behalf, that ye may have somewhat to answer them which glory in appearance, and not in heart.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v13)</sup> For whether we be beside ourselves, it is to God: or whether we be sober, it is for your cause.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v14)</sup> For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v15)</sup> And that he died for all, that they which live should not henceforth live unto themselves, but unto him which died for them, and rose again.

    “Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men”. We know the terror and the fear of God, so we persuade all people to come out of that, because you are going to die in it. Meanwhile you are walking as a hypocrite? Our job is to persuade men because we understand the fear of God, and that is why we cleanse ourselves, “perfecting holiness in the fear of God.”

**[2 Corinthians 7:2](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v2)</sup> Receive us; we have wronged no man, we have corrupted no man, we have defrauded no man.

- “Receive us; we have wronged no man, we have corrupted no man, we have defrauded no man.” We are not moving in deceit; we have not defrauded or wronged anybody.

**[2 Corinthians 7:9](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v9)</sup> Now I rejoice, not that ye were made sorry, but that ye sorrowed to repentance: for ye were made sorry after a godly manner, that ye might receive damage by us in nothing.

- We have seen how wicked Israel is, how rebellious we are and how rebellious the forefathers were. Now it is time to see how the Most High has mercy on us, how he will get us out of this condition and save us through his mercies. That is the only way we will make it to the kingdom of heaven: through his mercy, not because we are some great people, not because we have fringes and borders of blue on.
- “Now I rejoice, not that ye were made sorry, but that ye sorrowed to repentance”: that is what we try to bring you to. You know what you do behind closed doors. Each and every one of us has an angel next to us who knows what we did; the Heavenly Father knows, Christ knows. “For ye were made sorry after a godly manner, that ye might receive damage by us in nothing”: we don’t want to destroy you or quench your spirit. We are trying to fix you, not break you or keep you broken; to put you back together through correction, through rebuke, through the upliftment of the scriptures.

**[2 Corinthians 7:10](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v10)</sup> For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of: but the sorrow of the world worketh death.

- “For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation”: if you have a conscience in you, godly sorrow works repentance in you, so you can get the kingdom of heaven. You take the correction, fix yourself and build off it, and become better. Sometimes you brothers and sisters get mad when you get corrected, but we are trying to get you to the kingdom of heaven, and you can’t get there without being corrected.
- “Not to be repented of”: not so you go back and do it again and have to get blasted again. “But the sorrow of the world worketh death”: if you keep doing it, the Most High is going to cut you off in your wickedness.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:20](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v20)</sup> The Lord shall send upon thee cursing, vexation, and rebuke, in all that thou settest thine hand unto for to do, until thou be destroyed, and until thou perish quickly; because of the wickedness of thy doings, whereby thou hast forsaken me.

    Correction comes with the curses. “The Lord shall send upon thee cursing, vexation, and rebuke”: vexation is being irritated and aggravated; rebuke is correction. Correction is part of the curses, because we forsook our God. So you men who don’t like to be corrected don’t want the kingdom of heaven, and you sisters who don’t like to be corrected don’t want the kingdom of heaven.
