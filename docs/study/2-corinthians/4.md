## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 16-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 4:1-2](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v1)</sup> Therefore seeing we have this ministry, as we have received mercy, we faint not;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v2)</sup> But have renounced the hidden things of dishonesty, not walking in craftiness, nor handling the word of God deceitfully; but by manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every man’s conscience in the sight of God.

- “Therefore seeing we have this ministry, as we have received mercy, we faint not”: we do not faint in this truth.
- “Therefore seeing we have this ministry, as we have received mercy, we faint not”. Now that we have this ministry, we don't faint.
- “But have renounced the hidden things of dishonesty, not walking in craftiness, nor handling the word of God deceitfully”. Not walking in craftiness means our speech is plain speech. We are talking to the average brother out there on the corner. Black men don’t talk about exegesis and eisegesis and Hebrew Israelism and legalism. That is old church foolishness to make you sound deep. The average brother in the street is asking, why are we getting beat over our head? Why are we going through this? Because we sinned against God. The wisdom of the world is foolishness with him. That does not work on us.
- “But by manifestation of the truth commending ourselves to every man’s conscience in the sight of God.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 48:10](/bible/jeremiah/48#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/48#v10)</sup> Cursed be he that doeth the work of the Lord deceitfully, and cursed be he that keepeth back his sword from blood.

    Handling the word of God deceitfully: “Cursed be he that doeth the work of the Lord deceitfully, and cursed be he that keepeth back his sword from blood.” Talk plain to black people; we are a plain people and we need plain words.
  - **[John 7:38](/bible/john/7#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/john/7#v38)</sup> He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said, out of his belly shall flow rivers of living water.

    This is Christ speaking: “He that believeth on me, as the scripture hath said”. Believe on him as it is written. Don’t give exegesis, eisegesis, Hebrewism or hermeneutics. Just believe what is written, in plain speech.
  - **[Nehemiah 8:8](/bible/nehemiah/8#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/nehemiah/8#v8)</sup> So they read in the book in the law of God distinctly, and gave the sense, and caused them to understand the reading.

    “So they read in the book in the law of God distinctly, and gave the sense, and caused them to understand the reading.” They gave the sense of what was being said. Don’t try to be deep with all the semantics. They caused the people to understand, and to understand you have to keep the commandments.

**[2 Corinthians 4:3](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v3)</sup> But if our gospel be hid, it is hid to them that are lost:

- “But if our gospel be hid, it is hid to them that are lost”. So if your mothers can’t understand that the Israelites and Christ are our people, they’re lost. Your mothers, your fathers, sisters, brothers, some of your wives, some of your kids can’t see it: they’re lost.
- “But if our gospel be hid, it is hid to them that are lost”. If the gospel is not hid to you, you understand, and if you understand, you are keeping the commandments. If the gospel is hid to you, you don’t understand, and that is because you are lost in sin.
- “But if our gospel be hid, it is hid to them that are lost”. The gospel we teach is to be beheld with an open face, renouncing sin. If you don't understand that you have to renounce sin, you're lost.
- That's the Christian church: we're no longer under the laws of God, we're under grace, you can't call me a sinner, I've been saved. You have not renounced the hidden things of dishonesty. You're an undercover homo, still fornicating, still smoking weed, effeminate, popping gum with your sister's lip gloss on, singing soprano. You have not renounced it. You're lost.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 2:3](/bible/2-esdras/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-esdras/2#v3)</sup> I brought you up with gladness; but with sorrow and heaviness have I lost you: for ye have sinned before the Lord your God, and done that thing that is evil before him.

    Is there a scripture that says the gospel is hid to the lost because of sin? “I brought you up with gladness; but with sorrow and heaviness have I lost you: for ye have sinned before the Lord your God, and done that thing that is evil before him.” The gospel is hid to them that have sinned. That is why they don’t understand it: they are lost.

**[2 Corinthians 4:4](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v4)</sup> In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.

- “In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not”. That goes with what was read in Revelation 12:9, how Satan deceived the whole world, and with Isaiah 29: who seeth us, whose works are in the dark. That’s Esau, that’s the so-called white man. He is the god of this world, “lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.”
- “In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not.” Pay attention: the god of this world. Remember the original sin was idolatry. Where is he fighting them? In their minds. This is where everything takes place. If your mind is weak, meaning your spirit is weak, you will not be able to fight. That is why the Bishop has told us for years: study, pray and apply. Without application you are just reading a book; you might as well pick up Harry Potter or Lord of the Rings. If you don't see that these things were written aforetime for your learning, what are we doing?
- “Lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.” That is the whole intent of Satan: to blind the mind so they cannot see the truth. How many of us fought the truth when we first heard it? Pulling out John 3:16 with a New King James with no dust on it, thinking we knew something, and getting our whole face fixed. That is what Satan wants: for you to think you know something, to think you are loved by the Lord because you were washed in the blood and got baptized. So you're saved, you're good? No, you're not.
- “In whom the god of this world”: who is the god of this world? The white man, the one that sees all things. Some people say it is the spiritual demon, Satan, but you always have to start with his physical counterpart, the so-called white man. He is the one creating all these technologies; he is the one who deceived the world physically.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 2:2](/bible/ephesians/2#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/2#v2)</sup> Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience:

    Notice it said the god of this world. Has anybody seen Satan? There is a realm where all this fighting takes place, and they have literal access to you in this realm. You can't see it with your eye, but you know it is there.
  - **[John 14:30](/bible/john/14#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/john/14#v30)</sup> Hereafter I will not talk much with you: for the prince of this world cometh, and hath nothing in me.

    “Hereafter I will not talk much with you: for the prince of this world cometh.” Christ is warning the apostles about his departure, and he says when he leaves Satan is coming. You don't see him, but Christ said he is coming, and that lets you know his presence is really here among us, just as the spirit is really among you if you believe and keep the commandments and faith. That should scare the mess out of you. Some people are used to it and say it's not a big deal. The hell it isn't.
  - **[1 John 5:18-19](/bible/1-john/5#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/1-john/5#v18)</sup> We know that whosoever is born of God sinneth not; but he that is begotten of God keepeth himself, and that wicked one toucheth him not.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/1-john/5#v19)</sup> And we know that we are of God, and the whole world lieth in wickedness.

    “We know that whosoever is born of God sinneth not; but he that is begotten of God keepeth himself.” To be born of God means you believe, you walk after the commandments, and you believe on his son. “And that wicked one toucheth him not.” The way you keep yourself is by keeping the commandments; when you do that you are protected, because the wicked one can't touch you. You have on the full armor of God and he has no access.

**[2 Corinthians 4:16](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v16)</sup> For which cause we faint not; but though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.

- “For which cause we faint not”: it gets exhausting and tiring, yet we faint not. “But though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.” Day by day. As with the rewiring of the mind day by day, there is a physical change happening in you as you feed your spirit. The outward man dies; the inward man must be fed. Daily bread for a dying man.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 3:16-17](/bible/ephesians/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/ephesians/3#v16)</sup> That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man;
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/ephesians/3#v17)</sup> That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love,

    “That he would grant you, according to the riches of his glory, to be strengthened with might by his Spirit in the inner man.” It is all about strengthening the inner man.
  - **[Colossians 3:16](/bible/colossians/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/colossians/3#v16)</sup> Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom; teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord.

    “Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly in all wisdom”: let it dwell in you, strengthening the inner man, “teaching and admonishing one another in psalms and hymns and spiritual songs, singing with grace in your hearts to the Lord.”

**[2 Corinthians 4:17](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v17)</sup> For our light affliction, which is but for a moment, worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory;

- It is not to scare anybody, but it has to be gone over: see what the Most High calls it. “For our light affliction”. He calls it light, because the kingdom is so great that we cannot even imagine what is coming. All the tribulation, persecution, famine and sword is “but for a moment”, only for a moment.
- That light affliction “worketh for us a far more exceeding and eternal weight of glory.” If we want that glorified state, that glorification, we have to go through this light affliction. People say, man, how are you calling that light? It is the Messiah, and we are not going to understand his ways. He is telling us: you think that is bad, but that is how great it is going to be in the kingdom.

**[2 Corinthians 4:18](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v18)</sup> While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal.

- “While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal; but the things which are not seen are eternal.”
- We have to have foresight and understand that the things we don’t see in front of us are the prophecies that are going to come to pass: the understanding of what will happen at the end, which the Lord is revealing to us.
- “While we look not at the things which are seen, but at the things which are not seen: for the things which are seen are temporal”. This ain’t real. Outside the matrix, anything that don’t last forever ain’t real. The monetary system ain’t real. Your job ain’t real, because they can fire you tomorrow, and if you get sick and die, won’t they replace you the next day?
- The only thing that is real and has substance is something that can last forever: “the things which are not seen are eternal.” That is what we have to strive for. We have to change the way that we think.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 11:1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)</sup> Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

    “Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.” The evidence of things not seen: things buried in the scriptures that God reveals to his servants the prophets. He makes clear to us the things necessary for salvation and reveals spiritual things that the world will never be able to see.
  - **[2 Timothy 2:4](/bible/2-timothy/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-timothy/2#v4)</sup> No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life; that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.

    “No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life”: anybody who's in this war against evil is not going to entangle himself with the affairs of this life, “that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.”
  - **[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

    It's a spiritual battle, a spiritual war. “And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind”. We are new creatures in Christ, so the mindset has to change: the things that used to trip us up so often and so easily in the world can't any more.
