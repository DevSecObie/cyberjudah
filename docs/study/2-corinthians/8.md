## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 19-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 8:19](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v19)</sup> And not that only, but who was also chosen of the churches to travel with us with this grace, which is administered by us to the glory of the same Lord, and declaration of your ready mind:

- “Who was also chosen of the churches to travel with us with this grace, which is administered by us”. Whenever the offerings were brought to Jerusalem, Paul took with him somebody of good report in the body. He knew that man had no bad intent, but he knew how other people can be when it comes to money.

**[2 Corinthians 8:20](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v20)</sup> Avoiding this, that no man should blame us in this abundance which is administered by us:

- “Avoiding this, that no man should blame us in this abundance which is administered by us”. The abundance is the help given by the congregation. When the booster club funds are given to go out and preach the gospel, that is an abundance the people minister to the prophets, and the leadership has control of it. If the leadership dealt falsely with the money, buying Bentleys like the churches, the people would not trust them with it.
- So among us the fail-safe is that the leaders don't touch the money. There are men over that, two people and backups upon backups, so that too much trust is never put in one single person and nobody can say it's on the leaders. There is a process the people can be confident in, so they know their funds are being stewarded the right way; not just anybody can touch those matters. When there is privacy around those things, it isn't to deceive anybody; everybody has different spirits. You avoid people even remotely thinking you are dealing unjustly, because we understand how people think.

**[2 Corinthians 8:21](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v21)</sup> Providing for honest things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.

- “Providing for honest things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.” The Lord knows all things, but men have to be able to see it too. A leader should be in a place where no man can blame him. Otherwise it is a blight: people come in and say, this is like the church; that brother doesn't hold a job, so how is he paying his bills, with a new pair and a new phone? And the ministry gets blamed.
- Trust between leadership and the congregation is built through righteous character, transparency, fairness, compassion, accountability and consistency. Across the line every school is supposed to follow the protocol and do things the same way: wash, rinse, repeat, cookie cutter. The systems are in place for a reason, and if you follow them you avoid this. The policies exist, but you have to seek them out; don't be so confident in something you haven't confirmed that you don't check with your leads.
