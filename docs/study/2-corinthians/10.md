## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-6, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 10:3-4](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v3)</sup> For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v4)</sup> (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;)

- “For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal”: it has nothing to do with guns, sticks or knives; that is carnal. They are “mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds”. As we go as ambassadors for Christ, as servants, we are pulling down strongholds. Christianity is a stronghold. Islam is a stronghold. Atheists is a stronghold on some of our people’s minds.
- “For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal”: we put more emphasis on the physical war overseas and don’t think about the spiritual war we need to fight within ourselves. The Lord isn’t asking you to do anything out there. He is asking you to get your mind right and change your behavior. You are at war right now, brothers and sisters, in your spirit, with all the evil temptations pushed out on all fronts: family, school, work, everywhere you go, even the evil thoughts flowing through your mind as you sit here. That is the war you are fighting.
- This is not about picking up guns. It is about getting the weapons in your mind to fight evil temptation. That said, if anyone comes at you with a rope or a knife and you have the means to protect yourself, make sure they don’t get the chance to use it on anybody else; no one should allow themselves to be lynched or murdered. But we do not advocate any of us going out and initiating aggression toward anybody. Some take the words of the Most High, go off and do something crazy, then blame us. We are not advocating that at all. Get your mind right. A lot of these brothers can’t even discipline their food: horrible eating habits, no exercise, can’t run ten steps. Burn the fat.
- The weapons are the commandments of God. You are supposed to answer your impulses, your sinful temptations, with the commandments of God; that is what keeps you from going through that door of temptation. “But mighty through God”: these commandments are mighty. “To the pulling down of strong holds”: mighty to pull down lies. We open the Bible and swallow up the lies and get rid of those impulsive sinful thoughts. If you understood there was death around the corner you would listen to these laws, but because judgment is not immediate, the wicked man’s mind is emboldened to commit evil and leave the commandments to the side.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 6:12](/bible/ephesians/6#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/6#v12)</sup> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

    “For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers.” In Colossians 1 we just read who created the principalities and the powers: Christ. Paul is encouraging the church of Ephesus that there is a fight that has to happen, a war going on, and it is not a physical war between flesh and blood. It is a spiritual war.

**[2 Corinthians 10:5](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v5)</sup> Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God, and bringing into captivity every thought to the obedience of Christ;

- America says freedom of religion; that is a lie. They say you are free to serve whatever: you can be a Muslim, a Christian, a Catholic, an Adventist, serving garbage. The Muslim says our God's name is Allah, you have Krishna, you have Buddha; in reality everybody is serving the same God. The proof: it doesn't matter what religion you are, when Christmas comes everybody is saying Merry Christmas. I thought you were a Muslim? Or if they try to get smart, happy holidays: same thing. They are all worshipping the same thing; they are worshipping the beast, like in Babylon when the king said everybody has to bow down.
- “Casting down imaginations, and every high thing that exalteth itself against the knowledge of God,” This is read so you brothers can see what we are facing. This is not going to be a walk in the park; nobody goes to war thinking it is a walk in the park; there are going to be casualties, and this is spiritual warfare, not knives and guns. You are at war to wake up your people, to change the mind of the black man in America and all over the world, to save the soul of our people. Esau, the enemy, is not going to take it lying down after spending billions of dollars over 400 years to keep you stupid; now you are waking up, they are coming after you, making up laws and making up things so they can come after you. That is the type of war you are in.
- Casting down imaginations: you might imagine Jesus Christ is white; as leaders we have to cast that down. You might imagine the Sabbath is in the morning; we have to cast that garbage down. You might imagine I'm going to eat fish for Passover, buy a box of Popeyes chicken; we have to cast that down. As leaders that is your job, and it is not over yet: there is going to be a lot more doctrine come, as Paul said those things must come to pass, and it is going to be brothers and sisters sitting right next to you. Some of you think you are in la-la land and everything is going to be fine; your trial and tribulation is coming. Remember us in Egypt: we became so low we started to serve an animal as a god, an animal you can kill. It is the same thing in America: God says lamb, the brother says chicken; God says goat, the brother says chicken. That dude said you don't have to do the Day of Atonement; the Day of Atonement, is that good or bad? It's good for you, so why tell me not to do it? They don't want you to see the light, and you follow because you move by emotion and feeling, not by laws: he's a good teacher, I love the brother. The brother is a damn ashy black devil; that is what he is going to die like. When you are caught up in your feelings and emotion, what happens next? Death. The holy days mean days separated from other days, each with its own spirit; a foolish man tells you to change what you do on the holy days and you don't see the devil coming for you. Don't come to the feast days, the Passover, the new moon with a sad face; get up and do a little dance for the Lord. Sister, you are going to die with depression.
- This right here is your job description; this is why we have been sent here. Sisters too: you have to cast these imaginations down in here as well. A lot of times you see the brother or sister next to you starting to malfunction and you say nothing, because you are non-confrontational, you don't want to ruffle feathers, you want someone else to do it. No: that is a symptom, he's tripping, and you could have stopped it right there: hey bro, that ain't what the scripture is talking about. It takes believing brothers and sisters to stop the nonsense in its tracks. There was a flat earth situation: people who were in the Navy, who travelled around the world and didn't fall off the side, now think the earth is flat; they say we teleported. Because you don't want to be that person, that nonsense grows and festers, and now two or three people in here think the earth might be flat. The point is not the earth being flat; it is the demonic spirit that comes with that doctrine. Froward thoughts separate from God (Wisdom of Solomon 1:3): when you hear things separating from the Bible, unbiblical, stomp it right in its tracks; be that backbone. If you don't check it you will see the spirit leaving that person slowly, and next thing they are eating pancakes and bacon and eggs for the Passover. You are watchmen; your job description is right there in 2 Corinthians 10:5: cast down any nonsense that comes out, throw it to the ground violently, not literally. Don't play with it; not for an hour; we are not going to sit and argue with you. When you catch cancer early you have a better chance, but you let these brothers get to stage four. Be responsible. Walk outside, look up at the moon and ask him about that curvature; he'll say I'll get back to you and go Google some stuff. A good understanding have all they that do his commandments. When we witness evil in our presence and don't immediately stomp it out, it sends a message that the gospel we hold is not worth fighting for; that small piece of leaven, that small seed of evil, will destroy everything we are building. Let us take heed to the gospel we have been taught, lest at any time we let it slip (Hebrews 2:1). Like on your job: the minute you do anything against the company's policies you are dealt with immediately, because they see that small infraction as a detriment to the whole company. That is the seriousness in making sure things stay on course.

**[2 Corinthians 10:6](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v6)</sup> And having in a readiness to revenge all disobedience, when your obedience is fulfilled.

- “And having in a readiness to revenge all disobedience, when your obedience is fulfilled.” That means we got to start with us first, the individual, the man in the mirror. Start with him and her first, then you can do the rest.
- “And having in a readiness to revenge all disobedience.” Some of our people can’t wait: they want to be in the war on the streets, casting out demons, getting their name on the Saturday night specials, getting the good camera angle so their video goes up. But here is the stipulation: “when your obedience is fulfilled.” That means you have no impediments in your spirit to commit sin; you let the commandments make you walk straight in your spirit, in your household, with your children, all around you, so that you are blameless. Then you can go out and correct your people.
- We want to play warrior, but not war for the obedience to Christ; we want to look good while we have demons all up in us. Look how bad it is: a ranking brother in the body re-enlisted after war was announced over there, as if he didn’t know prophecy. There is a time and a season for all things, and if you know the time you are supposed to operate by the knowledge of the times. When Christ comes back to fight against our enemies, you would be in the armies told to go and fight against Christ. Meanwhile a young woman in the world, who joined because she couldn’t find a job, took the signing bonus and now regrets it, is trying to get out. They keep our people poor and put recruiting stations in all the black cities, like Planned Parenthood, so we put our bodies on the field to be all we could never be in America, fighting for their cause to keep oppressing the rest of our people. It is a trick bag.
- And a woman in the world had enough sense not to get entangled when a BHI brother tried to post up on her; she saw he broke character, Bible in hand. Meanwhile sisters in the body get played by brothers like that. People in the world have better sense than some of us in this truth. Our spirits aren’t right.
