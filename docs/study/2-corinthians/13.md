## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 13 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-5, 8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 13:1](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v1)</sup> This is the third time I am coming to you. In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established.

- “In the mouth of two or three witnesses shall every word be established”: that is the law. Being called means we are requested of God to magnify his law, and in that process we must continue to build and increase our faith. There is only one way to do that: with action and serving the Lord.
- Once we begin to keep God’s commandments in the faith of Christ, the Lord starts testing us, the great fiery trial of our faith, to show us that the faith he created us with is in us. We just have to bring it out.

**[2 Corinthians 13:2](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v2)</sup> I told you before, and foretell you, as if I were present, the second time; and being absent now I write to them which heretofore have sinned, and to all other, that, if I come again, I will not spare:

- Read again before verse 5, as it was read earlier: Paul writes “to them which heretofore have sinned, and to all other, that, if I come again, I will not spare”. He has dealt with their sins before, and if he must come about the same things again, he will not spare them.

**[2 Corinthians 13:3](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v3)</sup> Since ye seek a proof of Christ speaking in me, which to you-ward is not weak, but is mighty in you.

- “Since ye seek a proof of Christ speaking in me”: Paul was in the spirit of Christ, and he was explaining that all the things he spoke of, he was strong in Christ, strong in the power of the Lord. Christ is his source. Christ is the power.

**[2 Corinthians 13:4](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v4)</sup> For though he was crucified through weakness, yet he liveth by the power of God. For we also are weak in him, but we shall live with him by the power of God toward you.

- “For we also are weak in him”: the mortal body. We all have down times, and that is why we need to increase our faith. We are not immortal yet, so Christ is here to maintain and build our faith. When we are at our weakest, that is when Christ is strong within us.
- That is the key thing to remember as times get hard: we do have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous. Paul understood that.
- “For though he was crucified through weakness, yet he liveth by the power of God.” As they were learning the gospel of Christ, Paul affirms the power of God among them: how God raised up Jesus through his power, the same way he is going to do with us; “we shall live with him by the power of God toward you.”

**[2 Corinthians 13:5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)</sup> Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?

- “Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?” Today’s class is about self-examination, for all of us.
- One brother just left because, he said, Captain Abiel was putting too much pressure on him: he was forcing him to go out and teach, to do God’s work, to be accountable. Spirits like that, just leave. Nobody is going to cry over spilled milk.
- This is one of my favorites; I always meditate on it. “Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves.” Prove yourself and know yourself, but know yourself in what? “How that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates”. He dwells in us except we be reprobates, void of judgment. If we’re not applying the things he’s saying in order to keep him in us, then eventually he’ll leave, like with Solomon. It seems obvious, but it’s the ‘in you’ part that y’all are not really hitting home.
- “Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves.” This takes actually doing it, all of us: taking a step back and looking at ourselves. When somebody passes away, when the Day of Atonement comes, it reminds us to look at ourselves and see where we are slacking, because we want to make our calling and election sure. We are supposed to do it daily. If you are one of them, you have to be moving like one of them; you don't turn it off. You are who you are supposed to be at all times, and that takes constant self-examination, because the adversary the devil is lurking. You could have the devil on you, or be living with the devil right now, and think you are right.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 18:20](/bible/sirach/18#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/18#v20)</sup> Before judgment examine thyself, and in the day of visitation thou shalt find mercy.

    “Before judgment examine thyself, and in the day of visitation thou shalt find mercy.” Examine yourselves: it goes for all of us.
  - **[2 Corinthians 6:3](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v3)</sup> Giving no offence in any thing, that the ministry be not blamed:

    “Giving no offence in any thing.” Satan makes us believe it is just a little thing, that this little thing will not lead you to shame the ministry. But the scripture describes Satan as a roaring lion, ready to devour souls.
  - **[Galatians 6:4](/bible/galatians/6#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/6#v4)</sup> But let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.

    “But let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.” The scripture commands us to prove our own work.

**[2 Corinthians 13:8](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v8)</sup> For we can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.

- The Southern Poverty Law Center put Israel United in Christ at number three on its list of America’s largest hate groups, and the Ku Klux Klan at number twenty. Yet the woman who put us there, a high official of that same center, is accused of sending $1.2 million in donor money to an informant inside a white supremacist group, who was also her lover. The center paid Klan informants, one of them an imperial wizard who never showed a sign of reform and died a free man in 2023, from the same Klan that bombed the Birmingham church and killed four children. The Black Panthers, formed to protect our people from the Klan, were dismantled and some of their members are still in jail, while the Klan still exists. Have we ever lynched anyone or terrorized anybody? No. It is the scripture that is terrorizing them: to us it is a savour of life, and to them a savour of death.
- But “we can do nothing against the truth, but for the truth.” There is nothing they can do against this truth but for the truth. No matter what light they paint us in, it is going to be for the growth of this truth. By putting us on that list they drew interest: people ask how a group can be number three and they never heard of it, and naturally they look into it. Some do not take anyone’s word at face value; they research and watch the videos to see what makes us a hate group. There is no such thing as bad publicity. They opened the door for a lot of people who never even heard of us to know that we exist.
- And those who are meant to be in this truth will come, whether they heard of it in a positive light or a negative light. They watch the videos and say, they are actually speaking the truth, I need to join. The true people of the Lord are like Manchurian candidates: the moment we hear it, we know that is the way.
