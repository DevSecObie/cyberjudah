## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 9 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-5, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 9:1](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v1)</sup> For as touching the ministering to the saints, it is superfluous for me to write to you:

- “For as touching the ministering to the saints”: that is what you see us doing, developing, growing, instructing how we minister to the saints, and the saints of the Most High are you, the nation of Israel. “It is superfluous for me to write to you”: superfluous meaning extra.

**[2 Corinthians 9:2-4](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v2)</sup> For I know the forwardness of your mind, for which I boast of you to them of Macedonia, that Achaia was ready a year ago; and your zeal hath provoked very many.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v3)</sup> Yet have I sent the brethren, lest our boasting of you should be in vain in this behalf; that, as I said, ye may be ready:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v4)</sup> Lest haply if they of Macedonia come with me, and find you unprepared, we (that we say not, ye) should be ashamed in this same confident boasting.

- “For I know the forwardness of your mind, for which I boast of you to them of Macedonia, that Achaia was ready a year ago; and your zeal hath provoked very many.” Don’t put us in the category of the Christian church and the other religious organizations: our mission is to minister to the saints. Paul was boasting about where they were going, and telling this church to step its game up, lest they of Macedonia come and find them unprepared.

**[2 Corinthians 9:5](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v5)</sup> Therefore I thought it necessary to exhort the brethren, that they would go before unto you, and make up beforehand your bounty, whereof ye had notice before, that the same might be ready, as a matter of bounty, and not as of covetousness.

- “Therefore I thought it necessary to exhort the brethren, that they would go before unto you, and make up beforehand your bounty”: the bounty is the money, so we can minister to the saints. It should not come out of our own pockets. “That the same might be ready, as a matter of bounty, and not as of covetousness.” When we tell you to donate, it is so we can go and minister to the saints, not for covetousness like the Christian church, where you give your money and watch them get multimillion-dollar homes, cars and jets.

    > <sup>[25](/bible/acts/11#v25)</sup> Then departed Barnabas to Tarsus, for to seek Saul:
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/acts/11#v26)</sup> And when he had found him, he brought him unto Antioch. And it came to pass, that a whole year they assembled themselves with the church, and taught much people. And the disciples were called Christians first in Antioch.

    “Then departed Barnabas to Tarsus, for to seek Saul: And when he had found him, he brought him unto Antioch. And it came to pass, that a whole year they assembled themselves with the church, and taught much people.” That is what they were doing when they ministered to the saints and taught salvation in the Messiah.
