## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-10, 14-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 6:1](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v1)</sup> We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.

- “We then, as workers together with him, beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain.” We are workers together with him, and we beg that you don’t receive this word in vain: that it becomes profitable to you, that you become prophets of God, that the women bear our prophets back on the earth and our daughters are righteous.
- Paul gets deep on us here; write this down. Watch how Paul explains how we’re viewed by the world. If you think you repented as Israel and you’re going to receive love, you’ve been mistaken. You’d better get ready for the hate, get ready for your family to hate you, get ready for trials and tribulations. Your mother’s going to leave you, your father’s going to leave you; a lot of our mothers don’t believe. So you’ve got to separate. Get your mind right.
- “We then, as workers together with him”: do you know that we’re working with Christ? We’re doing the work. We are damn near a thousand deep in Atlanta, probably past a thousand, to teach our people this is the way.
- “That ye receive not the grace of God in vain.” You at home watching, don’t receive the grace of God in vain. What are you still so confused about? Repent, join us, keep the commandments. Don’t just sit there: ‘should I join, what do I do, I don’t know, I need a few more classes.’ You’re taking too long. You need to make up your mind.

**[2 Corinthians 6:3](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v3)</sup> Giving no offence in any thing, that the ministry be not blamed:

- “Giving no offence in any thing, that the ministry be not blamed”. Don’t be the setback to the ministry: one of us out there selling drugs, one a murderer, one a thief. Being called as ambassadors, don’t be that brother.
- “Giving no offence in any thing”. Right now it’s the end days. America is about to be destroyed; we don’t know when, but it’s soon to come. So the Bible says now is the accepted time, and right now we can’t give offense in anything. Why? “That the ministry be not blamed”. If we give offense in something, whether adultery or fornication, they’re going to blame the whole movement. Sometimes we get weak; that’s why we’ve got to build each other up, call each other every day, strengthen each other. We get weak, but don’t give in. Hold on.
- “Giving no offence in any thing”: meaning we have to keep the commandments. If you’re not offending, that means you’re keeping the commandments; so you can’t abuse the grace, you can’t receive it in vain, as verse one was saying. “That the ministry be not blamed”: that the understanding of the liberty of Christ be not tainted in what it really means.
- Just like the Captain said, when we continue to do these things we crucify Christ afresh, killing him all over again. Sin will take you farther than you want to go and keep you longer than you want to stay, and if you get caught out there and don’t get back up under that grace of repentance, you’re in danger of the second death; you’re going to be destroyed. It’s no time to be playing around: the Most High could judge you instantly, driving down the road, walking down the street, walking into a store, falling over dead, a heart attack. You never know when the Most High says, that’s it, I’m tired, cut it off; I’ll see him in the second death. It may be your last chance. This thing is serious, and if you find yourself falling short, fix it as quick as you can: Lord, please forgive me, have mercy on me.
