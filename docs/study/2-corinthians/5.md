## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 7-9, 11, 17-18, 20-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 5:1](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v1)</sup> For we know that if our earthly house of this tabernacle were dissolved, we have a building of God, an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.

- “For we know that if our earthly house of this tabernacle were dissolved”: the flesh is a veil, and here it is also an earthly house called a tabernacle. A tabernacle is a place where you dwell. This body is only a place for the soul to reside in this realm, this present world. “We have a building of God, an house not made with hands, eternal in the heavens.”
- “For we know that if our earthly house of this tabernacle were dissolved”: the earthly house of this tabernacle is our bodies, our temple, and it’s going to be changed. This body holds us. “Were dissolved”: you die and turn into dust.
- “We have a building of God, an house not made with hands”: a body that was not made with hands. In Genesis the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground; this other body was not made of this earth, of flesh. It is the body God created for us.
- “Eternal in the heavens”: that is not the kingdom. We have a house, a body, that is eternal in the heavens. A lot of times we ask ourselves what the Bible is talking about when it says we’re going to be changed in the twinkling of an eye. It means the Lord is going to give us that body he has prepared for us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 9:28-31](/bible/luke/9#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/luke/9#v28)</sup> And it came to pass about an eight days after these sayings, he took Peter and John and James, and went up into a mountain to pray.
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/luke/9#v29)</sup> And as he prayed, the fashion of his countenance was altered, and his raiment was white and glistering.
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/luke/9#v30)</sup> And, behold, there talked with him two men, which were Moses and Elias:
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/luke/9#v31)</sup> Who appeared in glory, and spake of his decease which he should accomplish at Jerusalem.

    The transfiguration, where Luke goes into it a little more. Christ took Peter and John and James up into a mountain to pray, “and as he prayed, the fashion of his countenance was altered, and his raiment was white and glistering.”
  - **[Isaiah 54:17](/bible/isaiah/54#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/54#v17)</sup> No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord, and their righteousness is of me, saith the Lord.

    “No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper”: once we put on that spiritual body, no weapon formed against us shall prosper. When we are changed in the twinkling of an eye, the Lord is going to give us our house that was prepared for us in heaven, our spiritual body, the same body that Elijah and Moses had when they saw Christ.

**[2 Corinthians 5:2](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v2)</sup> For in this we groan, earnestly desiring to be clothed upon with our house which is from heaven:

- “For in this we groan, earnestly desiring to be clothed upon with our house which is from heaven.” In this body we groan. We have a real house, a real tabernacle for our spirit, where we don't have to fight: not full of gas, no incontinence, the back doesn't hurt, the feet don't stink. Everything that comes with this body draws us and is always fighting our spirit. As your spirit grows in understanding, it is like living with a spouse you hate and who hates you; before you understood, you were quite comfortable in that relationship with your body. That is why it says to mortify your members: you have to cut it off.
- “For in this we groan”: we groan in this body we are living in because this body is susceptible to sin. You keep messing up; you’re trying to do right, but you keep messing up, so you’ve got to keep going off of forgiveness and pray he don’t kill you.
- “Earnestly desiring to be clothed upon with our house which is from heaven”: we want to be clothed with our spiritual body. Pay attention to the word he used: “our house”. Our house means we had it before, and now our desire is to have it back. Our house is in heaven.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 8:23](/bible/romans/8#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/8#v23)</sup> And not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.

    To sum up what Paul said: “even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.” We are spiritual souls groaning within our carnal selves. That adoption is the redemption of our real body. No body we have had here is your real body; that is not how you look, and it is not the perfection waiting for us after Christ returns.

**[2 Corinthians 5:3](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v3)</sup> If so be that being clothed we shall not be found naked.

- “If so be that being clothed we shall not be found naked.” Right now we are without defense, which is why we are told to put on the whole armor of God.

**[2 Corinthians 5:4](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v4)</sup> For we that are in this tabernacle do groan, being burdened: not for that we would be unclothed, but clothed upon, that mortality might be swallowed up of life.

- “For we that are in this tabernacle do groan, being burdened.” This body is a pain; it presses us down, as in Wisdom of Solomon 9. “Not for that we would be unclothed”: not that we want to be without a tabernacle; it is just this one we don't want. “But clothed upon”, with the one that is waiting for us, fit for us, “that mortality might be swallowed up of life”, with eternal life.

**[2 Corinthians 5:7](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v7)</sup> (For we walk by faith, not by sight:)

- “For we walk by faith, not by sight.” Faith without works is dead. You cannot just say you have faith and never put in works; faith and works go hand in hand, and by your works we see your faith. Some come every week, listen, and bounce after class without a meaningful conversation with a brother or sister, yet have meaningful conversations with the wicked at the job, at the bar, or on the phone with wicked family. You think you have faith, but you cannot fool us, and you cannot fool the Most High.

**[2 Corinthians 5:8](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v8)</sup> We are confident, I say, and willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.

- “Willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.” Paul understood something about death that many do not.

**[2 Corinthians 5:9](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v9)</sup> Wherefore we labour, that, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.

- “Wherefore we labour”: we put in works, “that, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.” The work is not only on the Sabbath. Some soldiers make every Camp 101 and every weeknight class, while some leaders and some with no job never come. Examine yourselves: do you really believe this, and do you really want the kingdom? Some cannot see themselves without America and the white man, spoiled by internet, television, cell phones, lights and food twenty-four seven.

**[2 Corinthians 5:11](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v11)</sup> Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men; but we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.

- “Knowing therefore the terror of the Lord, we persuade men.” What must we do? Persuade men, because we know what the Lord is going to do. We are the watchmen the Lord set up, and since we know the terror of the Lord, we must persuade men. “But we are made manifest unto God; and I trust also are made manifest in your consciences.”
- Who are we trying to persuade? Our people, in the midst of all kinds of sins. Brothers, we must be studying, so that when we come across our people we are able to help them. The whole head is sick; we are sick individuals. But we must learn to discipline ourselves, and we must learn how to persuade our people to change their life.

**[2 Corinthians 5:17](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v17)</sup> Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

- In politics they dig up a man’s past to unseat him; why does that not work in this truth? “Therefore if any man be in Christ”: to be in Christ means you have repented and you walk by the guidelines Christ gave, walking and talking and living the way Christ did on this earth. “He is a new creature: old things are passed away”: so you can’t dig up a man’s old things. “Behold, all things are become new.”
- If that were not so, Paul could not have written all these letters to the churches. He was the most wicked man you read about in the New Testament, but he took his lumps and fought hard to show the people he had changed, and they followed him. Who will dig up a man’s old things in this truth? A person who is not in Christ: a devil, a traitor, trying to move the people the same way they do in politics, to draw away disciples after them. If they were in Christ and had a gripe, they would have followed the steps in Matthew 18: go to the brother first, then with witnesses, then before the church, not on Facebook.
- If I were the devil, I would not follow billions of people to catch them in sin. You are here because of your faith, so I would break your faith, by attacking the people you look at as faithful.
- Putting away the old man. “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away”: the old sin. “Behold, all things are become new.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.

    Your faith is going to be tested. “Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm.” A lot of you are here because you trust in man and not in what the Bible says, and men will pull you the way they want you to go. The arm that guides us is the spiritual arm of God, not the fleshly arm of man. When the man you look up to falls, or looks bad, you fall; Satan knows that, so he attacks specific individuals. He gets most of the souls by breaking your faith, because you did not trust in what the Bible says.
  - **[Ephesians 4:21-24](/bible/ephesians/4#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/ephesians/4#v21)</sup> If so be that ye have heard him, and have been taught by him, as the truth is in Jesus:
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/4#v22)</sup> That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man, which is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts;
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/4#v23)</sup> And be renewed in the spirit of your mind;
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/ephesians/4#v24)</sup> And that ye put on the new man, which after God is created in righteousness and true holiness.

    This is how the old things pass away. “That ye put off concerning the former conversation the old man”: put off that old talk, that wicked, evil, worldly talk, and put on his Bible. The old man “is corrupt according to the deceitful lusts”; the old man is filthy, so your mind has to be changed.

**[2 Corinthians 5:18](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v18)</sup> And all things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ, and hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation;

- “And all things are of God, who hath reconciled us to himself by Jesus Christ”: he brought us back together. “And hath given to us the ministry of reconciliation.” That is why we have to bring our people back to the Heavenly Father in the faith of Christ: go out and be the saviors to our people and wake them up.

**[2 Corinthians 5:20](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v20)</sup> Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to God.

- Remember what the Lord is setting up: a government. “Now then we are ambassadors for Christ.” An ambassador is an accredited diplomat sent by a country as its official representative to a foreign country. Brothers, you are an official representative of the kingdom of heaven, Jerusalem. The country is Jerusalem, the real Jerusalem, the heavenly Jerusalem that is coming on earth, and you are its official representative to a foreign country.
- “As though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to God.” Be reconciled to God means repent, return to God.
- We have to start speaking like ambassadors and conducting ourselves like ambassadors. Our pants should not be below our butt. We should not look unkempt, meaning homeless; in fact there should be nobody in here homeless. We should all become well studied.
- “Now then we are ambassadors for Christ, as though God did beseech you by us: we pray you in Christ’s stead, be ye reconciled to God.” We are ambassadors.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 1:10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v10)</sup> See, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down, to build, and to plant.

    That’s why the Lord said: “See, I have this day set thee over the nations”. He told Jeremiah what he said to all the prophets, all of us: I have set you over the nations. What does that mean? You are an ambassador.
  - **[Hebrews 11:14-15](/bible/hebrews/11#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/11#v14)</sup> For they that say such things declare plainly that they seek a country.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hebrews/11#v15)</sup> And truly, if they had been mindful of that country from whence they came out, they might have had opportunity to have returned.

    We are ambassadors for Christ; well, if you are an ambassador, where is your country? “For they that say such things declare plainly that they seek a country.” As ambassadors, brothers, we must declare plainly that we seek a country. “And truly, if they had been mindful of that country from whence they came out, they might have had opportunity to have returned.”

**[2 Corinthians 5:21](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v21)</sup> For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.

- “For he hath made him to be sin for us, who knew no sin; that we might be made the righteousness of God in him.” The him he made to be sin is Christ. Christ knew no sin.
