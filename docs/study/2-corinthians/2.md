## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Corinthians 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 11, 14-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Corinthians 2:11](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v11)</sup> Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.

- “Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.” This is where you have to examine yourself. Are you ignorant of Satan’s devices? Has he been using some form of pride to hold you back and keep you down? It might be subtle: not asking for help, thinking too highly of yourself. Satan has many devices; he is able to transform himself into an angel of light, and he knows how to get to you to hold you back. Is Satan’s device getting you?
- Even the fat brothers: too prideful to change their eating habits.
- Every year the Passover comes around and we see the same thing happen: people start bugging out, and people who've been with us see it, yet they still fall for it. We have to fight, and not play into Satan's devices. He wants you right where he's got you. Some are on their way out because they're not paying attention to class, not studying, not praying, not getting their four chapters.
- “Lest Satan should get an advantage of us: for we are not ignorant of his devices.” We should not be ignorant of his devices, but some remain ignorant, because they despise wisdom and don't want to be taught.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 21:12](/bible/sirach/21#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/21#v12)</sup> He that is not wise will not be taught: but there is a wisdom which multiplieth bitterness.

    “He that is not wise will not be taught”. If you're a fool, it doesn't matter what somebody does for you: you're not going to be taught, you're not going to retain it.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 3:26](/bible/sirach/3#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/3#v26)</sup> A stubborn heart shall fare evil at the last; and he that loveth danger shall perish therein.

    “A stubborn heart shall fare evil at the last”: that's the bitterness. Because you're stubborn, because you don't want to learn, because when wise words come out you're in the back, or because you've got something against somebody and don't want to hear anything from them.

**[2 Corinthians 2:14](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v14)</sup> Now thanks be unto God, which always causeth us to triumph in Christ, and maketh manifest the savour of his knowledge by us in every place.

- “Now thanks be unto God, which always causeth us to triumph in Christ.” We are going to triumph in Christ. “And maketh manifest the savour of his knowledge”: we are going to make it plain and bring it out. “By us in every place”: by you brothers who used to live evil, crooked lives and are waking up now and coming back to this truth to serve God in righteousness. You are going to be an example to your community that they can change: a mentor, a big brother, a father to the fatherless, once you get yourself right in this truth.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 14:23](/bible/luke/14#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/luke/14#v23)</sup> And the lord said unto the servant, Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.

    This is the work of making his knowledge manifest in every place. “And the lord said unto the servant, Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.” We have to go out to the highways and hedges, the corners and the blocks, so his house can be filled.

**[2 Corinthians 2:15](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v15)</sup> For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish:

- “For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish.” We go and teach this word so they can be saved in Christ.
- Everything that is good for us is bad for the rest of the world, and that is why we are the world’s most hated. “For we are unto God a sweet savour of Christ, in them that are saved, and in them that perish”.

**[2 Corinthians 2:16](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v16)</sup> To the one we are the savour of death unto death; and to the other the savour of life unto life. And who is sufficient for these things?

- “To the one we are the savour of death unto death.” When some people hear the rebuke coming out, we are like death to them. We are marking them, so when Christ comes they have no cloak and no excuse. “And to the other the savour of life unto life”: we breathe the scriptures into them and bring them back to life, like Ezekiel 37, out of the valley of dry bones and standing on their feet. “And who is sufficient for these things?”
- “To the one we are the savour of death unto death”. When the nations hear the message and the prophecies, Psalms 149, Psalms 2, taking the heathen for our inheritance, Revelation 2:26, Revelation 13:10, we are the smell of death to them.
- “And to the other the savour of life unto life”: to our people we are life. They say, that’s talking about me. There is redemption for our forefathers and foremothers, for our people that had crack cocaine brought into their neighborhoods, our brothers and sisters that were shot up with syphilis, that had their communities literally bombed, and flooded, with lakes built on top of them, that were given AIDS, whose music industry was hijacked.
- That is how this truth is viewed by the world. When Captain Osee was fired, the letter denied it was about his private religious activities or freedom of speech; it said it was based solely on his teaching of a false faith, in direct conflict with their established doctrine and mission. Everything he taught was read out of the Bible, so that is what the world thinks of the Bible. These people do not believe the Bible, and the same organization has a big ass Christmas tree up right now and nobody has a problem with it.

**[2 Corinthians 2:17](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v17)</sup> For we are not as many, which corrupt the word of God: but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ.

- Brothers, I need y’all to understand the scriptures, because the nations, particularly this white man, have corrupted the truth. “For we are not as many”: meaning the Israelites that are rising up in the faith of Christ, keeping the commandments, are not like the many “which corrupt the word of God.” So many have corrupted the word of God: that’s your church system.
- “But as of sincerity”: we teach the Bible in sincerity, brothers and sisters. “But as of God”: we teach as God called us. “In the sight of God”: we teach in the sight of God. “Speak we in Christ”: we speak in the name of Christ.
- “For we are not as many, which corrupt the word of God: but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ.” We are going to speak the words of Christ and the truth, and be those saviors who shake this world up, coming out of a low state.

  - **[Galatians 1:6-7](/bible/galatians/1#v6)**

You go back to being a Negro: I used to be an Israelite, now I am a born again Christian again, now I am a Catholic again, now I am a Protestant.
