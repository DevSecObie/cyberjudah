These chapters have no recorded class session in the "4 Chapters a Day" series. The full King James text is included here so the book of Hosea is complete for reading and study.

**[Hosea 3:1-5](/bible/hosea/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/3#v1)</sup> Then said the Lord unto me, Go yet, love a woman beloved of her friend, yet an adulteress, according to the love of the Lord toward the children of Israel, who look to other gods, and love flagons of wine.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/3#v2)</sup> So I bought her to me for fifteen pieces of silver, and for an homer of barley, and an half homer of barley:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/3#v3)</sup> And I said unto her, Thou shalt abide for me many days; thou shalt not play the harlot, and thou shalt not be for another man: so will I also be for thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/3#v4)</sup> For the children of Israel shall abide many days without a king, and without a prince, and without a sacrifice, and without an image, and without an ephod, and without teraphim:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/3#v5)</sup> Afterward shall the children of Israel return, and seek the Lord their God, and David their king; and shall fear the Lord and his goodness in the latter days.

# Hosea 4: The Lord's Controversy With the Land

Read the chapter → [Hosea 4](/bible/hosea/4)

**[Hosea 4:1-10](/bible/hosea/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/4#v1)</sup> Hear the word of the Lord, ye children of Israel: for the Lord hath a controversy with the inhabitants of the land, because there is no truth, nor mercy, nor knowledge of God in the land.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/4#v2)</sup> By swearing, and lying, and killing, and stealing, and committing adultery, they break out, and blood toucheth blood.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/4#v3)</sup> Therefore shall the land mourn, and every one that dwelleth therein shall languish, with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven; yea, the fishes of the sea also shall be taken away.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/4#v4)</sup> Yet let no man strive, nor reprove another: for thy people are as they that strive with the priest.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/4#v5)</sup> Therefore shalt thou fall in the day, and the prophet also shall fall with thee in the night, and I will destroy thy mother.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/4#v6)</sup> My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/4#v7)</sup> As they were increased, so they sinned against me: therefore will I change their glory into shame.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/4#v8)</sup> They eat up the sin of my people, and they set their heart on their iniquity.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/4#v9)</sup> And there shall be, like people, like priest: and I will punish them for their ways, and reward them their doings.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/4#v10)</sup> For they shall eat, and not have enough: they shall commit whoredom, and shall not increase: because they have left off to take heed to the Lord.

**[Hosea 4:11-19](/bible/hosea/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/4#v11)</sup> Whoredom and wine and new wine take away the heart.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/4#v12)</sup> My people ask counsel at their stocks, and their staff declareth unto them: for the spirit of whoredoms hath caused them to err, and they have gone a whoring from under their God.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/4#v13)</sup> They sacrifice upon the tops of the mountains, and burn incense upon the hills, under oaks and poplars and elms, because the shadow thereof is good: therefore your daughters shall commit whoredom, and your spouses shall commit adultery.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/4#v14)</sup> I will not punish your daughters when they commit whoredom, nor your spouses when they commit adultery: for themselves are separated with whores, and they sacrifice with harlots: therefore the people that doth not understand shall fall.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/4#v15)</sup> Though thou, Israel, play the harlot, yet let not Judah offend; and come not ye unto Gilgal, neither go ye up to Beth–aven, nor swear, The Lord liveth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/hosea/4#v16)</sup> For Israel slideth back as a backsliding heifer: now the Lord will feed them as a lamb in a large place.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/hosea/4#v17)</sup> Ephraim is joined to idols: let him alone.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/hosea/4#v18)</sup> Their drink is sour: they have committed whoredom continually: her rulers with shame do love, Give ye.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/hosea/4#v19)</sup> The wind hath bound her up in her wings, and they shall be ashamed because of their sacrifices.

# Hosea 5: The Pride of Israel Testifieth to His Face

Read the chapter → [Hosea 5](/bible/hosea/5)

**[Hosea 5:1-7](/bible/hosea/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/5#v1)</sup> Hear ye this, O priests; and hearken, ye house of Israel; and give ye ear, O house of the king; for judgment is toward you, because ye have been a snare on Mizpah, and a net spread upon Tabor.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/5#v2)</sup> And the revolters are profound to make slaughter, though I have been a rebuker of them all.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/5#v3)</sup> I know Ephraim, and Israel is not hid from me: for now, O Ephraim, thou committest whoredom, and Israel is defiled.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/5#v4)</sup> They will not frame their doings to turn unto their God: for the spirit of whoredoms is in the midst of them, and they have not known the Lord.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/5#v5)</sup> And the pride of Israel doth testify to his face: therefore shall Israel and Ephraim fall in their iniquity; Judah also shall fall with them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/5#v6)</sup> They shall go with their flocks and with their herds to seek the Lord; but they shall not find him; he hath withdrawn himself from them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/5#v7)</sup> They have dealt treacherously against the Lord: for they have begotten strange children: now shall a month devour them with their portions.

**[Hosea 5:8-15](/bible/hosea/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/5#v8)</sup> Blow ye the cornet in Gibeah, and the trumpet in Ramah: cry aloud at Beth–aven, after thee, O Benjamin.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/5#v9)</sup> Ephraim shall be desolate in the day of rebuke: among the tribes of Israel have I made known that which shall surely be.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/5#v10)</sup> The princes of Judah were like them that remove the bound: therefore I will pour out my wrath upon them like water.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/5#v11)</sup> Ephraim is oppressed and broken in judgment, because he willingly walked after the commandment.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/5#v12)</sup> Therefore will I be unto Ephraim as a moth, and to the house of Judah as rottenness.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/5#v13)</sup> When Ephraim saw his sickness, and Judah saw his wound, then went Ephraim to the Assyrian, and sent to king Jareb: yet could he not heal you, nor cure you of your wound.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/5#v14)</sup> For I will be unto Ephraim as a lion, and as a young lion to the house of Judah: I, even I, will tear and go away; I will take away, and none shall rescue him.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

# Hosea 6: In the Morning Will I Seek Him

Read the chapter → [Hosea 6](/bible/hosea/6)

**[Hosea 6:1-11](/bible/hosea/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/6#v1)</sup> Come, and let us return unto the Lord: for he hath torn, and he will heal us; he hath smitten, and he will bind us up.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/6#v2)</sup> After two days will he revive us: in the third day he will raise us up, and we shall live in his sight.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/6#v3)</sup> Then shall we know, if we follow on to know the Lord: his going forth is prepared as the morning; and he shall come unto us as the rain, as the latter and former rain unto the earth.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/6#v4)</sup> O Ephraim, what shall I do unto thee? O Judah, what shall I do unto thee? for your goodness is as a morning cloud, and as the early dew it goeth away.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/6#v5)</sup> Therefore have I hewed them by the prophets; I have slain them by the words of my mouth: and thy judgments are as the light that goeth forth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/6#v6)</sup> For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/6#v7)</sup> But they like men have transgressed the covenant: there have they dealt treacherously against me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/6#v8)</sup> Gilead is a city of them that work iniquity, and is polluted with blood.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/6#v9)</sup> And as troops of robbers wait for a man, so the company of priests murder in the way by consent: for they commit lewdness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/6#v10)</sup> I have seen an horrible thing in the house of Israel: there is the whoredom of Ephraim, Israel is defiled.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/6#v11)</sup> Also, O Judah, he hath set an harvest for thee, when I returned the captivity of my people.

# Hosea 7: Ephraim Is a Cake Not Turned

Read the chapter → [Hosea 7](/bible/hosea/7)

**[Hosea 7:1-8](/bible/hosea/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/7#v1)</sup> When I would have healed Israel, then the iniquity of Ephraim was discovered, and the wickedness of Samaria: for they commit falsehood; and the thief cometh in, and the troop of robbers spoileth without.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/7#v2)</sup> And they consider not in their hearts that I remember all their wickedness: now their own doings have beset them about; they are before my face.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/7#v3)</sup> They make the king glad with their wickedness, and the princes with their lies.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/7#v4)</sup> They are all adulterers, as an oven heated by the baker, who ceaseth from raising after he hath kneaded the dough, until it be leavened.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/7#v5)</sup> In the day of our king the princes have made him sick with bottles of wine; he stretched out his hand with scorners.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/7#v6)</sup> For they have made ready their heart like an oven, whiles they lie in wait: their baker sleepeth all the night; in the morning it burneth as a flaming fire.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/7#v7)</sup> They are all hot as an oven, and have devoured their judges; all their kings are fallen: there is none among them that calleth unto me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/7#v8)</sup> Ephraim, he hath mixed himself among the people; Ephraim is a cake not turned.

**[Hosea 7:9-16](/bible/hosea/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/7#v9)</sup> Strangers have devoured his strength, and he knoweth it not: yea, gray hairs are here and there upon him, yet he knoweth not.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/7#v10)</sup> And the pride of Israel testifieth to his face: and they do not return to the Lord their God, nor seek him for all this.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/7#v11)</sup> Ephraim also is like a silly dove without heart: they call to Egypt, they go to Assyria.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/7#v12)</sup> When they shall go, I will spread my net upon them; I will bring them down as the fowls of the heaven; I will chastise them, as their congregation hath heard.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/7#v13)</sup> Woe unto them! for they have fled from me: destruction unto them! because they have transgressed against me: though I have redeemed them, yet they have spoken lies against me.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/7#v14)</sup> And they have not cried unto me with their heart, when they howled upon their beds: they assemble themselves for corn and wine, and they rebel against me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/7#v15)</sup> Though I have bound and strengthened their arms, yet do they imagine mischief against me.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/hosea/7#v16)</sup> They return, but not to the most High: they are like a deceitful bow: their princes shall fall by the sword for the rage of their tongue: this shall be their derision in the land of Egypt.

# Hosea 8: They Have Sown the Wind

Read the chapter → [Hosea 8](/bible/hosea/8)

**[Hosea 8:1-7](/bible/hosea/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/8#v1)</sup> Set the trumpet to thy mouth. He shall come as an eagle against the house of the Lord, because they have transgressed my covenant, and trespassed against my law.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/8#v2)</sup> Israel shall cry unto me, My God, we know thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/8#v3)</sup> Israel hath cast off the thing that is good: the enemy shall pursue him.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/8#v4)</sup> They have set up kings, but not by me: they have made princes, and I knew it not: of their silver and their gold have they made them idols, that they may be cut off.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/8#v5)</sup> Thy calf, O Samaria, hath cast thee off; mine anger is kindled against them: how long will it be ere they attain to innocency?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/8#v6)</sup> For from Israel was it also: the workman made it; therefore it is not God: but the calf of Samaria shall be broken in pieces.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/8#v7)</sup> For they have sown the wind, and they shall reap the whirlwind: it hath no stalk: the bud shall yield no meal: if so be it yield, the strangers shall swallow it up.

**[Hosea 8:8-14](/bible/hosea/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/8#v8)</sup> Israel is swallowed up: now shall they be among the Gentiles as a vessel wherein is no pleasure.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/8#v9)</sup> For they are gone up to Assyria, a wild ass alone by himself: Ephraim hath hired lovers.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/8#v10)</sup> Yea, though they have hired among the nations, now will I gather them, and they shall sorrow a little for the burden of the king of princes.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/8#v11)</sup> Because Ephraim hath made many altars to sin, altars shall be unto him to sin.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/8#v12)</sup> I have written to him the great things of my law, but they were counted as a strange thing.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/8#v13)</sup> They sacrifice flesh for the sacrifices of mine offerings, and eat it; but the Lord accepteth them not; now will he remember their iniquity, and visit their sins: they shall return to Egypt.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/8#v14)</sup> For Israel hath forgotten his Maker, and buildeth temples; and Judah hath multiplied fenced cities: but I will send a fire upon his cities, and it shall devour the palaces thereof.

# Hosea 9: The Days of Visitation Are Come

Read the chapter → [Hosea 9](/bible/hosea/9)

**[Hosea 9:1-9](/bible/hosea/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/9#v1)</sup> Rejoice not, O Israel, for joy, as other people: for thou hast gone a whoring from thy God, thou hast loved a reward upon every cornfloor.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/9#v2)</sup> The floor and the winepress shall not feed them, and the new wine shall fail in her.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/9#v3)</sup> They shall not dwell in the Lord’s land; but Ephraim shall return to Egypt, and they shall eat unclean things in Assyria.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/9#v4)</sup> They shall not offer wine offerings to the Lord, neither shall they be pleasing unto him: their sacrifices shall be unto them as the bread of mourners; all that eat thereof shall be polluted: for their bread for their soul shall not come into the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/9#v5)</sup> What will ye do in the solemn day, and in the day of the feast of the Lord?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/9#v6)</sup> For, lo, they are gone because of destruction: Egypt shall gather them up, Memphis shall bury them: the pleasant places for their silver, nettles shall possess them: thorns shall be in their tabernacles.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/9#v7)</sup> The days of visitation are come, the days of recompence are come; Israel shall know it: the prophet is a fool, the spiritual man is mad, for the multitude of thine iniquity, and the great hatred.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/9#v8)</sup> The watchman of Ephraim was with my God: but the prophet is a snare of a fowler in all his ways, and hatred in the house of his God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/9#v9)</sup> They have deeply corrupted themselves, as in the days of Gibeah: therefore he will remember their iniquity, he will visit their sins.

**[Hosea 9:10-17](/bible/hosea/9#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/9#v10)</sup> I found Israel like grapes in the wilderness; I saw your fathers as the firstripe in the fig tree at her first time: but they went to Baal–peor, and separated themselves unto that shame; and their abominations were according as they loved.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/9#v11)</sup> As for Ephraim, their glory shall fly away like a bird, from the birth, and from the womb, and from the conception.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/9#v12)</sup> Though they bring up their children, yet will I bereave them, that there shall not be a man left: yea, woe also to them when I depart from them!
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/9#v13)</sup> Ephraim, as I saw Tyrus, is planted in a pleasant place: but Ephraim shall bring forth his children to the murderer.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/9#v14)</sup> Give them, O Lord: what wilt thou give? give them a miscarrying womb and dry breasts.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/9#v15)</sup> All their wickedness is in Gilgal: for there I hated them: for the wickedness of their doings I will drive them out of mine house, I will love them no more: all their princes are revolters.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/hosea/9#v16)</sup> Ephraim is smitten, their root is dried up, they shall bear no fruit: yea, though they bring forth, yet will I slay even the beloved fruit of their womb.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/hosea/9#v17)</sup> My God will cast them away, because they did not hearken unto him: and they shall be wanderers among the nations.

# Hosea 10: Israel Is an Empty Vine

Read the chapter → [Hosea 10](/bible/hosea/10)

**[Hosea 10:1-8](/bible/hosea/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/10#v1)</sup> Israel is an empty vine, he bringeth forth fruit unto himself: according to the multitude of his fruit he hath increased the altars; according to the goodness of his land they have made goodly images.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/10#v2)</sup> Their heart is divided; now shall they be found faulty: he shall break down their altars, he shall spoil their images.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/10#v3)</sup> For now they shall say, We have no king, because we feared not the Lord; what then should a king do to us?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/10#v4)</sup> They have spoken words, swearing falsely in making a covenant: thus judgment springeth up as hemlock in the furrows of the field.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/10#v5)</sup> The inhabitants of Samaria shall fear because of the calves of Beth–aven: for the people thereof shall mourn over it, and the priests thereof that rejoiced on it, for the glory thereof, because it is departed from it.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/10#v6)</sup> It shall be also carried unto Assyria for a present to king Jareb: Ephraim shall receive shame, and Israel shall be ashamed of his own counsel.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/10#v7)</sup> As for Samaria, her king is cut off as the foam upon the water.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/10#v8)</sup> The high places also of Aven, the sin of Israel, shall be destroyed: the thorn and the thistle shall come up on their altars; and they shall say to the mountains, Cover us; and to the hills, Fall on us.

**[Hosea 10:9-15](/bible/hosea/10#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/10#v9)</sup> O Israel, thou hast sinned from the days of Gibeah: there they stood: the battle in Gibeah against the children of iniquity did not overtake them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/10#v10)</sup> It is in my desire that I should chastise them; and the people shall be gathered against them, when they shall bind themselves in their two furrows.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/10#v11)</sup> And Ephraim is as an heifer that is taught, and loveth to tread out the corn; but I passed over upon her fair neck: I will make Ephraim to ride; Judah shall plow, and Jacob shall break his clods.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/10#v12)</sup> Sow to yourselves in righteousness, reap in mercy; break up your fallow ground: for it is time to seek the Lord, till he come and rain righteousness upon you.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/10#v13)</sup> Ye have plowed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity; ye have eaten the fruit of lies: because thou didst trust in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/10#v14)</sup> Therefore shall a tumult arise among thy people, and all thy fortresses shall be spoiled, as Shalman spoiled Beth–arbel in the day of battle: the mother was dashed in pieces upon her children.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/10#v15)</sup> So shall Beth–el do unto you because of your great wickedness: in a morning shall the king of Israel utterly be cut off.
