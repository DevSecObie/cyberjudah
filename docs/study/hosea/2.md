**[Hosea 2:1-2](/bible/hosea/2#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/2#v1)</sup> Say ye unto your brethren, Ammi; and to your sisters, Ru–hamah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/2#v2)</sup> Plead with your mother, plead: for she is not my wife, neither am I her husband: let her therefore put away her whoredoms out of her sight, and her adulteries from between her breasts;

- Say unto your brethren Ammi, my people, and to your sisters Ruhamah, that have obtained mercy.
- Plead with your mother, plead, for she is not my wife, neither am I her husband; let her put away her whoredoms and her adulteries from between her breasts.

**[Hosea 2:3-5](/bible/hosea/2#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/2#v3)</sup> Lest I strip her naked, and set her as in the day that she was born, and make her as a wilderness, and set her like a dry land, and slay her with thirst.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/2#v4)</sup> And I will not have mercy upon her children; for they be the children of whoredoms.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/2#v5)</sup> For their mother hath played the harlot: she that conceived them hath done shamefully: for she said, I will go after my lovers, that give me my bread and my water, my wool and my flax, mine oil and my drink.

- Lest he strip her naked and set her as in the day she was born, make her a wilderness, set her like a dry land, and slay her with thirst.
- He will not have mercy upon her children, for they be children of whoredoms, and their mother has played the harlot, saying she will go after her lovers that give her bread, water, wool, flax, oil, and drink. This is Israel following the other nations that ruled over them.
  - **[Jeremiah 3:1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)</sup> They say, If a man put away his wife, and she go from him, and become another man’s, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted? but thou hast played the harlot with many lovers; yet return again to me, saith the Lord.

    Israel played the harlot with many lovers, yet the Most High still calls her to return again to him.
  - **[Jeremiah 3:8](/bible/jeremiah/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/jeremiah/3#v8)</sup> And I saw, when for all the causes whereby backsliding Israel committed adultery I had put her away, and given her a bill of divorce; yet her treacherous sister Judah feared not, but went and played the harlot also.

    For all the causes whereby backsliding Israel committed adultery, he put her away and gave her a bill of divorcement, and her treacherous sister Judah followed and played the harlot also.

**[Hosea 2:6-8](/bible/hosea/2#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/2#v6)</sup> Therefore, behold, I will hedge up thy way with thorns, and make a wall, that she shall not find her paths.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/2#v7)</sup> And she shall follow after her lovers, but she shall not overtake them; and she shall seek them, but shall not find them: then shall she say, I will go and return to my first husband; for then was it better with me than now.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/2#v8)</sup> For she did not know that I gave her corn, and wine, and oil, and multiplied her silver and gold, which they prepared for Baal.

- Therefore he will hedge up her way with thorns and make a wall, that she shall not find her paths; she shall follow her lovers but not overtake them, and seek them but not find them.
- Then she says she will go and return to her first husband, for then it was better with her than now, for she did not know that he gave her the corn, the wine, and the oil, and multiplied her silver and gold, which they prepared for Baal while she went to idolatry.

**[Hosea 2:9-10](/bible/hosea/2#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/2#v9)</sup> Therefore will I return, and take away my corn in the time thereof, and my wine in the season thereof, and will recover my wool and my flax given to cover her nakedness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/2#v10)</sup> And now will I discover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and none shall deliver her out of mine hand.

- Therefore he will return and take away his corn and his wine in their season, and recover his wool and his flax given to cover her nakedness. Even though it came through the nations, he allowed them to give it to her and made sure they nurtured her, so she was not left out in the cold even while oppressed.
- Now he will discover her lewdness in the sight of her lovers, and none shall deliver her out of his hand; when he gets ready to chastise, nobody is going to stop it.

**[Hosea 2:11-13](/bible/hosea/2#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/2#v11)</sup> I will also cause all her mirth to cease, her feast days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/2#v12)</sup> And I will destroy her vines and her fig trees, whereof she hath said, These are my rewards that my lovers have given me: and I will make them a forest, and the beasts of the field shall eat them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/2#v13)</sup> And I will visit upon her the days of Baalim, wherein she burned incense to them, and she decked herself with her earrings and her jewels, and she went after her lovers, and forgat me, saith the Lord.

- He will cause all her mirth to cease, her feast days, her new moons, her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts, stripping the high holy days from her.
- He will destroy her vines and her fig trees, whereof she said these are my rewards that my lovers have given me, and make them a forest for the beasts to eat. Like today, all people think America is blessing them, but it is the Most High giving them whatever little they have.
- He will visit upon her the days of Baalim, wherein she burned incense, decked herself with earrings and jewels, went after her lovers, and forgot him.

**[Hosea 2:14-15](/bible/hosea/2#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/2#v14)</sup> Therefore, behold, I will allure her, and bring her into the wilderness, and speak comfortably unto her.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/2#v15)</sup> And I will give her her vineyards from thence, and the valley of Achor for a door of hope: and she shall sing there, as in the days of her youth, and as in the day when she came up out of the land of Egypt.

- Therefore he will allure her, bring her into the wilderness, and speak comfortably unto her.
- He will give her her vineyards from thence, and the valley of Achor for a door of hope, and she shall sing there as in the days of her youth, as in the day she came up out of the land of Egypt.

**[Hosea 2:16-17](/bible/hosea/2#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/hosea/2#v16)</sup> And it shall be at that day, saith the Lord, that thou shalt call me Ishi; and shalt call me no more Baali.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/hosea/2#v17)</sup> For I will take away the names of Baalim out of her mouth, and they shall no more be remembered by their name.

- At that day she shall call him Ishi, my husband, and no more Baali, my lord.
- He will take away the names of Baalim out of her mouth, and they shall no more be remembered by their name.

**[Hosea 2:18-20](/bible/hosea/2#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/hosea/2#v18)</sup> And in that day will I make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, and with the fowls of heaven, and with the creeping things of the ground: and I will break the bow and the sword and the battle out of the earth, and will make them to lie down safely.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/hosea/2#v19)</sup> And I will betroth thee unto me for ever; yea, I will betroth thee unto me in righteousness, and in judgment, and in lovingkindness, and in mercies.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/hosea/2#v20)</sup> I will even betroth thee unto me in faithfulness: and thou shalt know the Lord.

- In that day he will make a covenant for them with the beasts of the field, the fowls of heaven, and the creeping things of the ground, and break the bow and the sword and the battle out of the earth, and make them to lie down safely.
- He will betroth her unto himself forever, in righteousness, in judgment, in lovingkindness, and in mercies, and in faithfulness, and she shall know the Lord. This is where the Most High accepts us again, serving one God and being faithful to that one God.
- The spirit of the Lord is on earth right now because he is cleaning his people up so he can accept them again.

**[Hosea 2:21-23](/bible/hosea/2#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/hosea/2#v21)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, I will hear, saith the Lord, I will hear the heavens, and they shall hear the earth;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/hosea/2#v22)</sup> And the earth shall hear the corn, and the wine, and the oil; and they shall hear Jezreel.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/hosea/2#v23)</sup> And I will sow her unto me in the earth; and I will have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy; and I will say to them which were not my people, Thou art my people; and they shall say, Thou art my God.

- In that day he will hear the heavens, and they shall hear the earth, and the earth shall hear the corn and the wine and the oil, and they shall hear Jezreel.
- He will sow her unto himself in the earth, have mercy upon her that had not obtained mercy, and say to them which were not his people, Thou art my people, and they shall say, Thou art my God. This is what is going on now as all people are repenting.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)</sup> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.

    The Most High keeps referring to Israel as her because he has likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman, a bride.
