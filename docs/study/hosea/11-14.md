Read the chapter → [Hosea 11](/bible/hosea/11)

This session covers [Hosea 11](/bible/hosea/11) through 14, the closing chapters of the book, which move into the Assyrian captivity of the northern kingdom. When you read the prophets like Hosea you can go to [Hosea 1:1](/bible/hosea/1#v1) and see the time frame in which he prophesied, in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah, and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash, king of Israel. That Jeroboam is Jeroboam the second, not the Jeroboam of the split kingdom. Uzziah is called Uzziah in Second Chronicles 26 and Azariah in the book of Kings. You can go to [Isaiah 1:1](/bible/isaiah/1#v1), Amos, and Micah and see that all of them prophesied around the same time; this is how you line up the different time frames of the prophets.

**[Hosea 11:1](/bible/hosea/11#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/11#v1)</sup> When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.

- When Israel was a child, then the Most High loved him, and called his son out of Egypt.
  - **[Exodus 4:22](/bible/exodus/4#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/4#v22)</sup> And thou shalt say unto Pharaoh, Thus saith the Lord, Israel is my son, even my firstborn:

    Israel is called the Most High's son, even his firstborn.

**[Hosea 11:2](/bible/hosea/11#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/11#v2)</sup> As they called them, so they went from them: they sacrificed unto Baalim, and burned incense to graven images.

- As they called them, so they went from them; they sacrificed unto Baalim and burned incense to graven images. At that time the northern kingdom was in the midst of idolatry.

**[Hosea 11:3-4](/bible/hosea/11#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/11#v3)</sup> I taught Ephraim also to go, taking them by their arms; but they knew not that I healed them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/11#v4)</sup> I drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love: and I was to them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws, and I laid meat unto them.

- The Most High taught Ephraim also to go, taking them by their arms, but they knew not that he healed them.
- He drew them with cords of a man, with bands of love, and was to them as they that take off the yoke on their jaws, and he laid meat unto them.

**[Hosea 11:5](/bible/hosea/11#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/11#v5)</sup> He shall not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be his king, because they refused to return.

- He shall not return into the land of Egypt, but the Assyrian shall be his king, because they refused to return. That is the prophecy that Assyria was going to come and take the northern kingdom out of the land, because they refused to return, which means to repent.

**[Hosea 11:6](/bible/hosea/11#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/11#v6)</sup> And the sword shall abide on his cities, and shall consume his branches, and devour them, because of their own counsels.

- The sword shall abide on his cities and consume his branches and devour them, because of their own counsels.

**[Hosea 11:7](/bible/hosea/11#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/11#v7)</sup> And my people are bent to backsliding from me: though they called them to the most High, none at all would exalt him.

- His people are bent to backsliding from him; though they called them to the Most High, none at all would exalt him. You cannot serve two masters; you cannot exalt the Lord and exalt your idol gods at the same time.

**[Hosea 11:8-9](/bible/hosea/11#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/11#v8)</sup> How shall I give thee up, Ephraim? how shall I deliver thee, Israel? how shall I make thee as Admah? how shall I set thee as Zeboim? mine heart is turned within me, my repentings are kindled together.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/11#v9)</sup> I will not execute the fierceness of mine anger, I will not return to destroy Ephraim: for I am God, and not man; the Holy One in the midst of thee: and I will not enter into the city.

- How shall the Most High give up Ephraim, how shall he deliver Israel? His heart is turned within him, his repentings are kindled together.
- He will not execute the fierceness of his anger, for he is God and not man, the Holy One in the midst of thee.
  - **[Joel 2:27](/bible/joel/2#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/joel/2#v27)</sup> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.

    The Lord is in the midst of Israel and there is none else.

**[Hosea 11:12](/bible/hosea/11#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/11#v12)</sup> Ephraim compasseth me about with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit: but Judah yet ruleth with God, and is faithful with the saints.

- Ephraim compasseth him about with lies, and the house of Israel with deceit, but Judah yet ruleth with God and is faithful with the saints. Notice that it is Judah that stays faithful.

# Hosea 12: Ephraim Feedeth on the Wind

Read the chapter → [Hosea 12](/bible/hosea/12)

**[Hosea 12:1](/bible/hosea/12#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/12#v1)</sup> Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind: he daily increaseth lies and desolation; and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.

- Ephraim feedeth on wind and followeth after the east wind; he daily increaseth lies and desolation, and they make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.

**[Hosea 12:2](/bible/hosea/12#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/12#v2)</sup> The Lord hath also a controversy with Judah, and will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his doings will he recompense him.

- The Lord has a controversy with Judah and will punish Jacob according to his ways; according to his doings will he recompense him.

**[Hosea 12:3-4](/bible/hosea/12#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/12#v3)</sup> He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/12#v4)</sup> Yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed: he wept, and made supplication unto him: he found him in Beth–el, and there he spake with us;

- He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God. This refers to when they came out of the womb, and also when Jacob wrestled with the angel.
- He had power over the angel and prevailed; he wept and made supplication unto him; he found him in Bethel, and there he spake with us.

**[Hosea 12:5-6](/bible/hosea/12#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/12#v5)</sup> Even the Lord God of hosts; the Lord is his memorial.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/12#v6)</sup> Therefore turn thou to thy God: keep mercy and judgment, and wait on thy God continually.

- Even the Lord God of hosts; the Lord is his memorial. Therefore turn thou to thy God, keep mercy and judgment, and wait on thy God continually.

**[Hosea 12:7-8](/bible/hosea/12#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/12#v7)</sup> He is a merchant, the balances of deceit are in his hand: he loveth to oppress.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/12#v8)</sup> And Ephraim said, Yet I am become rich, I have found me out substance: in all my labours they shall find none iniquity in me that were sin.

- He is a merchant, the balances of deceit are in his hand, he loveth to oppress.
- Ephraim said, Yet I am become rich, I have found me out substance in all my labours; they shall find none iniquity in me that were sin.

**[Hosea 12:9](/bible/hosea/12#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/12#v9)</sup> And I that am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt will yet make thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the solemn feast.

- The Most High that is the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt will yet make thee to dwell in tabernacles, as in the days of the solemn feast.

**[Hosea 12:10](/bible/hosea/12#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/12#v10)</sup> I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.

- The Most High has spoken by the prophets, multiplied visions, and used similitudes by the ministry of the prophets. This is the scripture the bishops pull out concerning parables, because similitudes are parables.

**[Hosea 12:11](/bible/hosea/12#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/12#v11)</sup> Is there iniquity in Gilead? surely they are vanity: they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal; yea, their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the fields.

- Is there iniquity in Gilead? Surely they are vanity; they sacrifice bullocks in Gilgal, and their altars are as heaps in the furrows of the fields.

**[Hosea 12:12](/bible/hosea/12#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/12#v12)</sup> And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.

- Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep. That goes back to when Jacob served Laban for his wife.
  - **[Genesis 29:20](/bible/genesis/29#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/29#v20)</sup> And Jacob served seven years for Rachel; and they seemed unto him but a few days, for the love he had to her.

    Jacob served seven years for Rachel, and they seemed to him but a few days for the love he had for her.

**[Hosea 12:13](/bible/hosea/12#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/12#v13)</sup> And by a prophet the Lord brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved.

- By a prophet the Lord brought Israel out of Egypt, and by a prophet was he preserved. That is speaking about Moses; he led them out of Egypt.

**[Hosea 12:14](/bible/hosea/12#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/12#v14)</sup> Ephraim provoked him to anger most bitterly: therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him.

- Ephraim provoked him to anger most bitterly; therefore shall he leave his blood upon him, and his reproach shall his Lord return unto him.

# Hosea 13: In Me Is Thine Help

Read the chapter → [Hosea 13](/bible/hosea/13)

**[Hosea 13:1](/bible/hosea/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/13#v1)</sup> When Ephraim spake trembling, he exalted himself in Israel; but when he offended in Baal, he died.

- When Ephraim spake trembling, he exalted himself in Israel, but when he offended in Baal, he died.

**[Hosea 13:2](/bible/hosea/13#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/13#v2)</sup> And now they sin more and more, and have made them molten images of their silver, and idols according to their own understanding, all of it the work of the craftsmen: they say of them, Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves.

- Now they sin more and more and have made them molten images of their silver, idols according to their own understanding, all of it the work of the craftsmen; they say of them, Let the men that sacrifice kiss the calves. Remember the time frame and the other prophets that were prophesying during that time; the craftsman was making idols in the images of men.
  - **[Isaiah 44:10-17](/bible/isaiah/44#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/44#v10)</sup> Who hath formed a god, or molten a graven image that is profitable for nothing?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/44#v11)</sup> Behold, all his fellows shall be ashamed: and the workmen, they are of men: let them all be gathered together, let them stand up; yet they shall fear, and they shall be ashamed together.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/44#v12)</sup> The smith with the tongs both worketh in the coals, and fashioneth it with hammers, and worketh it with the strength of his arms: yea, he is hungry, and his strength faileth: he drinketh no water, and is faint.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/44#v13)</sup> The carpenter stretcheth out his rule; he marketh it out with a line; he fitteth it with planes, and he marketh it out with the compass, and maketh it after the figure of a man, according to the beauty of a man; that it may remain in the house.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/44#v14)</sup> He heweth him down cedars, and taketh the cypress and the oak, which he strengtheneth for himself among the trees of the forest: he planteth an ash, and the rain doth nourish it.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/44#v15)</sup> Then shall it be for a man to burn: for he will take thereof, and warm himself; yea, he kindleth it, and baketh bread; yea, he maketh a god, and worshippeth it; he maketh it a graven image, and falleth down thereto.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/44#v16)</sup> He burneth part thereof in the fire; with part thereof he eateth flesh; he roasteth roast, and is satisfied: yea, he warmeth himself, and saith, Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire:
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/44#v17)</sup> And the residue thereof he maketh a god, even his graven image: he falleth down unto it, and worshippeth it, and prayeth unto it, and saith, Deliver me; for thou art my god.

    The workman forms a graven image profitable for nothing, fashions it after the figure of a man, then burns part of the same wood for fire and falls down to worship the rest as a god.

**[Hosea 13:3](/bible/hosea/13#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/13#v3)</sup> Therefore they shall be as the morning cloud, and as the early dew that passeth away, as the chaff that is driven with the whirlwind out of the floor, and as the smoke out of the chimney.

- Therefore they shall be as the morning cloud, as the early dew that passeth away, as the chaff driven with the whirlwind out of the floor, and as the smoke out of the chimney.

**[Hosea 13:4](/bible/hosea/13#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/13#v4)</sup> Yet I am the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt, and thou shalt know no god but me: for there is no saviour beside me.

- Yet the Most High is the Lord thy God from the land of Egypt, and thou shalt know no god but him, for there is no saviour beside him.

**[Hosea 13:5-6](/bible/hosea/13#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/13#v5)</sup> I did know thee in the wilderness, in the land of great drought.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/13#v6)</sup> According to their pasture, so were they filled; they were filled, and their heart was exalted; therefore have they forgotten me.

- He knew them in the wilderness, in the land of great drought. According to their pasture they were filled, and their heart was exalted; therefore they forgot him. That is what happens to us; when we get all the things we need, our food, water, and clothing, and a little excess, we forget the Lord.

**[Hosea 13:7-8](/bible/hosea/13#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/13#v7)</sup> Therefore I will be unto them as a lion: as a leopard by the way will I observe them:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/13#v8)</sup> I will meet them as a bear that is bereaved of her whelps, and will rend the caul of their heart, and there will I devour them like a lion: the wild beast shall tear them.

- Therefore he will be unto them as a lion, as a leopard by the way will he observe them; he will meet them as a bear bereaved of her whelps, rend the caul of their heart, and there devour them like a lion; the wild beast shall tear them.

**[Hosea 13:9](/bible/hosea/13#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/13#v9)</sup> O Israel, thou hast destroyed thyself; but in me is thine help.

- O Israel, thou hast destroyed thyself, but in the Most High is thine help.

**[Hosea 13:10](/bible/hosea/13#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/13#v10)</sup> I will be thy king: where is any other that may save thee in all thy cities? and thy judges of whom thou saidst, Give me a king and princes?

- He will be thy king; where is any other that may save thee in all thy cities, and thy judges of whom thou saidst, Give me a king and princes? The Lord is telling us there is none that saveth but him.
  - **[Daniel 2:47](/bible/daniel/2#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/daniel/2#v47)</sup> The king answered unto Daniel, and said, Of a truth it is, that your God is a God of gods, and a Lord of kings, and a revealer of secrets, seeing thou couldest reveal this secret.

    Even Nebuchadnezzar acknowledged that the God of Israel is the only true living God, a God of gods and a Lord of kings.

**[Hosea 13:11](/bible/hosea/13#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/13#v11)</sup> I gave thee a king in mine anger, and took him away in my wrath.

- He gave thee a king in his anger and took him away in his wrath.

**[Hosea 13:12-13](/bible/hosea/13#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/13#v12)</sup> The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up; his sin is hid.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/13#v13)</sup> The sorrows of a travailing woman shall come upon him: he is an unwise son; for he should not stay long in the place of the breaking forth of children.

- The iniquity of Ephraim is bound up, his sin is hid. The sorrows of a travailing woman shall come upon him; he is an unwise son, for he should not stay long in the place of the breaking forth of children.

**[Hosea 13:14](/bible/hosea/13#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/hosea/13#v14)</sup> I will ransom them from the power of the grave; I will redeem them from death: O death, I will be thy plagues; O grave, I will be thy destruction: repentance shall be hid from mine eyes.

- The Most High will ransom them from the power of the grave and redeem them from death; O death, he will be thy plagues; O grave, he will be thy destruction; repentance shall be hid from his eyes.

**[Hosea 13:15](/bible/hosea/13#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/13#v15)</sup> Though he be fruitful among his brethren, an east wind shall come, the wind of the Lord shall come up from the wilderness, and his spring shall become dry, and his fountain shall be dried up: he shall spoil the treasure of all pleasant vessels.

- Though he be fruitful among his brethren, an east wind shall come, the wind of the Lord coming up from the wilderness, and his spring shall become dry and his fountain dried up; he shall spoil the treasure of all pleasant vessels.

**[Hosea 13:16](/bible/hosea/13#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/hosea/13#v16)</sup> Samaria shall become desolate; for she hath rebelled against her God: they shall fall by the sword: their infants shall be dashed in pieces, and their women with child shall be ripped up.

- Samaria shall become desolate, for she hath rebelled against her God; they shall fall by the sword, their infants dashed in pieces, and their women with child ripped up. This was a future prophecy at the time Hosea prophesied, about Assyria coming into the land.
  - **[2 Kings 17:18](/bible/2-kings/17#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/2-kings/17#v18)</sup> Therefore the Lord was very angry with Israel, and removed them out of his sight: there was none left but the tribe of Judah only.

    The Lord was very angry with Israel and removed them out of his sight, and there was none left but the tribe of Judah only, when the northern kingdom was carried out and heathen were placed in the land.

# Hosea 14: O Israel, Return Unto the Lord

Read the chapter → [Hosea 14](/bible/hosea/14)

**[Hosea 14:1](/bible/hosea/14#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/14#v1)</sup> O Israel, return unto the Lord thy God; for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity.

- O Israel, return unto the Lord thy God, for thou hast fallen by thine iniquity.

**[Hosea 14:2](/bible/hosea/14#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/hosea/14#v2)</sup> Take with you words, and turn to the Lord: say unto him, Take away all iniquity, and receive us graciously: so will we render the calves of our lips.

- Take with you words and turn to the Lord; say unto him, Take away all iniquity and receive us graciously, so will we render the calves of our lips.

**[Hosea 14:3](/bible/hosea/14#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/14#v3)</sup> Asshur shall not save us; we will not ride upon horses: neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, Ye are our gods: for in thee the fatherless findeth mercy.

- Asshur shall not save us, we will not ride upon horses, neither will we say any more to the work of our hands, Ye are our gods, for in thee the fatherless findeth mercy. This is going into the northern kingdom repenting from idolatry.

**[Hosea 14:4](/bible/hosea/14#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/14#v4)</sup> I will heal their backsliding, I will love them freely: for mine anger is turned away from him.

- He will heal their backsliding and love them freely, for his anger is turned away from him.

**[Hosea 14:5-6](/bible/hosea/14#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/hosea/14#v5)</sup> I will be as the dew unto Israel: he shall grow as the lily, and cast forth his roots as Lebanon.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/14#v6)</sup> His branches shall spread, and his beauty shall be as the olive tree, and his smell as Lebanon.

- He will be as the dew unto Israel; he shall grow as the lily and cast forth his roots as Lebanon. His branches shall spread, his beauty shall be as the olive tree, and his smell as Lebanon.

**[Hosea 14:7](/bible/hosea/14#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/hosea/14#v7)</sup> They that dwell under his shadow shall return; they shall revive as the corn, and grow as the vine: the scent thereof shall be as the wine of Lebanon.

- They that dwell under his shadow shall return; they shall revive as the corn and grow as the vine, and the scent thereof shall be as the wine of Lebanon.

**[Hosea 14:8](/bible/hosea/14#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/hosea/14#v8)</sup> Ephraim shall say, What have I to do any more with idols? I have heard him, and observed him: I am like a green fir tree. From me is thy fruit found.

- Ephraim shall say, What have I to do any more with idols? The Most High has heard him and observed him; he is like a green fir tree, and from him is thy fruit found. There it is, a future prophecy of when Ephraim is freed of his idolatry; this is the ten tribes in the last hour coming to the fold, and we are getting close.

**[Hosea 14:9](/bible/hosea/14#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/hosea/14#v9)</sup> Who is wise, and he shall understand these things? prudent, and he shall know them? for the ways of the Lord are right, and the just shall walk in them: but the transgressors shall fall therein.

- Who is wise, and he shall understand these things? Prudent, and he shall know them? For the ways of the Lord are right, and the just shall walk in them, but the transgressors shall fall therein.
  - **[Luke 13:3](/bible/luke/13#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/luke/13#v3)</sup> I tell you, Nay: but, except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish.

    Except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish; the same warning that the transgressors shall fall.
  - **[Psalm 19:12-13](/bible/psalms/19#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/19#v12)</sup> Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from secret faults.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/19#v13)</sup> Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be upright, and I shall be innocent from the great transgression.

    A prayer for the last days, that the Lord cleanse us from secret faults and keep us back from presumptuous sins, that they not have dominion over us, so we be upright and innocent from the great transgression.
