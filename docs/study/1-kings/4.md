**[1 Kings 4:1-7](/bible/1-kings/4#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/4#v1)</sup> So king Solomon was king over all Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/4#v2)</sup> And these were the princes which he had; Azariah the son of Zadok the priest,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/4#v3)</sup> Elihoreph and Ahiah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/4#v4)</sup> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the host: and Zadok and Abiathar were the priests:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/4#v5)</sup> And Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers: and Zabud the son of Nathan was principal officer, and the king’s friend:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/4#v6)</sup> And Ahishar was over the household: and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tribute.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/4#v7)</sup> And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, which provided victuals for the king and his household: each man his month in a year made provision.

- Solomon is king over all Israel, and these are the princes he set: priests, scribes, a recorder, one over the host, one over the officers, one over the household, and one over the tribute. Twelve officers provide victuals for the king and his household, each man his month in the year.
- This is the establishment of government, showing order. There is the king, then ranking men, each with a position of order, the same pattern of captains and officers set up in the days of Moses.

**[1 Kings 4:20-25](/bible/1-kings/4#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/4#v20)</sup> Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking, and making merry.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-kings/4#v21)</sup> And Solomon reigned over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt: they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/4#v22)</sup> And Solomon’s provision for one day was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/4#v23)</sup> Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and an hundred sheep, beside harts, and roebucks, and fallowdeer, and fatted fowl.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/4#v24)</sup> For he had dominion over all the region on this side the river, from Tiphsah even to Azzah, over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/4#v25)</sup> And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beer–sheba, all the days of Solomon.

- Judah and Israel are as many as the sand by the sea, eating, drinking, and making merry, and Solomon reigns over all the kingdoms from the river to the border of Egypt, who bring presents and serve him. Every man dwells safely under his vine and fig tree from Dan to Beersheba.
- Israel dwells safely because of the wars of King David, who put down all the surrounding nations and put them to tribute. That is why Solomon is able to rule in a time of peace.

**[1 Kings 4:29-34](/bible/1-kings/4#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/4#v29)</sup> And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/4#v30)</sup> And Solomon’s wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east country, and all the wisdom of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/4#v31)</sup> For he was wiser than all men; than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/4#v32)</sup> And he spake three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/4#v33)</sup> And he spake of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-kings/4#v34)</sup> And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom.

- The Most High gives Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, largeness of heart as the sand on the seashore, so that his wisdom excels all the children of the east country and all the wisdom of Egypt. He is wiser than all men, speaks three thousand proverbs, and his songs are a thousand and five, which is why we have Proverbs and the Song of Songs.
- He speaks of trees, beasts, fowl, creeping things, and fishes, and people of all nations come to hear his wisdom. Since his wisdom already excelled Egypt's, we have no need to dabble in Egyptology or any doctrine outside the scriptures given to us.
