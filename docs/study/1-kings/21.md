## Introduction

This session moves through the account of Naboth's vineyard. King Ahab covets a vineyard he cannot lawfully take, his wife Jezebel plots murder to seize it, and the Most High sends Elijah to pronounce judgment on the house of Ahab. The chapter shows covetousness turning into extortion and false witness, and it shows that even a righteous judgment can be delayed when a wicked man humbles himself.

**[1 Kings 21:1-3](/bible/1-kings/21#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/21#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after these things, that Naboth the Jezreelite had a vineyard, which was in Jezreel, hard by the palace of Ahab king of Samaria.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/21#v2)</sup> And Ahab spake unto Naboth, saying, Give me thy vineyard, that I may have it for a garden of herbs, because it is near unto my house: and I will give thee for it a better vineyard than it; or, if it seem good to thee, I will give thee the worth of it in money.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/21#v3)</sup> And Naboth said to Ahab, The Lord forbid it me, that I should give the inheritance of my fathers unto thee.

- Naboth had a vineyard next to Ahab's palace in Jezreel, and Ahab asked to trade or buy it, offering a better vineyard or its worth in money.
- Naboth refused because the land was the inheritance of his fathers. He could not simply hand off an inheritance that was given to him.

  - **[Leviticus 25:23](/bible/leviticus/25#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/25#v23)</sup> The land shall not be sold for ever: for the land is mine; for ye are strangers and sojourners with me.

    The land was not to be sold forever, for it belonged to the Most High and was held as a family inheritance.

**[1 Kings 21:4](/bible/1-kings/21#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/21#v4)</sup> And Ahab came into his house heavy and displeased because of the word which Naboth the Jezreelite had spoken to him: for he had said, I will not give thee the inheritance of my fathers. And he laid him down upon his bed, and turned away his face, and would eat no bread.

- Ahab went home heavy and displeased, laid down on his bed, turned away his face, and would eat no bread.
- He threw a fit and sulked because he could not get his way, angry that a subject would not surrender his inheritance.

**[1 Kings 21:5-7](/bible/1-kings/21#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/21#v5)</sup> But Jezebel his wife came to him, and said unto him, Why is thy spirit so sad, that thou eatest no bread?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/21#v6)</sup> And he said unto her, Because I spake unto Naboth the Jezreelite, and said unto him, Give me thy vineyard for money; or else, if it please thee, I will give thee another vineyard for it: and he answered, I will not give thee my vineyard.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/21#v7)</sup> And Jezebel his wife said unto him, Dost thou now govern the kingdom of Israel? arise, and eat bread, and let thine heart be merry: I will give thee the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite.

- Jezebel came to Ahab and asked why his spirit was so sad that he would not eat, and he told her Naboth's refusal.
- Jezebel mocked his weakness, telling him he was king and should let his heart be merry, and she promised to get the vineyard of Naboth for him.

**[1 Kings 21:8-10](/bible/1-kings/21#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-kings/21#v8)</sup> So she wrote letters in Ahab’s name, and sealed them with his seal, and sent the letters unto the elders and to the nobles that were in his city, dwelling with Naboth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/21#v9)</sup> And she wrote in the letters, saying, Proclaim a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/21#v10)</sup> And set two men, sons of Belial, before him, to bear witness against him, saying, Thou didst blaspheme God and the king. And then carry him out, and stone him, that he may die.

- Jezebel wrote letters in Ahab's name and sealed them with his seal, sending them to the elders and nobles of Naboth's city.
- The seal was a signet, an official mark of the king's authority, so the letters carried royal weight as if Ahab himself had written them.
- She commanded them to proclaim a fast, set Naboth on high, and set two sons of Belial before him to bear false witness that he blasphemed God and the king, then stone him. This was evil counsel and a murderous plot.

  - **[Daniel 6:17](/bible/daniel/6#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/6#v17)</sup> And a stone was brought, and laid upon the mouth of the den; and the king sealed it with his own signet, and with the signet of his lords; that the purpose might not be changed concerning Daniel.

    A king sealed the den with his own signet so the decree could not be changed, showing the binding authority of a royal seal.
  - **[Exodus 20:16](/bible/exodus/20#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/20#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not bear false witness against thy neighbour.

    The commandment forbids bearing false witness, which is exactly what the sons of Belial were hired to do.

**[1 Kings 21:11-14](/bible/1-kings/21#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/21#v11)</sup> And the men of his city, even the elders and the nobles who were the inhabitants in his city, did as Jezebel had sent unto them, and as it was written in the letters which she had sent unto them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-kings/21#v12)</sup> They proclaimed a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-kings/21#v13)</sup> And there came in two men, children of Belial, and sat before him: and the men of Belial witnessed against him, even against Naboth, in the presence of the people, saying, Naboth did blaspheme God and the king. Then they carried him forth out of the city, and stoned him with stones, that he died.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-kings/21#v14)</sup> Then they sent to Jezebel, saying, Naboth is stoned, and is dead.

- The elders and nobles obeyed Jezebel's letters, proclaimed a fast, and set Naboth on high among the people.
- Two children of Belial, children of the devil, sat before Naboth and falsely witnessed that he had blasphemed God and the king.
- They carried Naboth out and stoned him to death, then sent word to Jezebel that he was dead.

**[1 Kings 21:15-16](/bible/1-kings/21#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-kings/21#v15)</sup> And it came to pass, when Jezebel heard that Naboth was stoned, and was dead, that Jezebel said to Ahab, Arise, take possession of the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, which he refused to give thee for money: for Naboth is not alive, but dead.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-kings/21#v16)</sup> And it came to pass, when Ahab heard that Naboth was dead, that Ahab rose up to go down to the vineyard of Naboth the Jezreelite, to take possession of it.

- When Jezebel heard Naboth was dead, she told Ahab to arise and take possession of the vineyard he had been refused.
- Ahab rose up and went down to take the vineyard. This is extortion, seizing what another has by violence and force.
- Ahab shares the guilt. He sat by and let Jezebel commit the crime because he coveted the field, then turned his face from the evil and went to claim the reward.

  - **[Ezekiel 22:12-13](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)</sup> In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/22#v13)</sup> Behold, therefore I have smitten mine hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee.

    The Most High condemns those who greedily gain of their neighbors by extortion and shed blood, smiting his hand against dishonest gain.

**[1 Kings 21:17-19](/bible/1-kings/21#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-kings/21#v17)</sup> And the word of the Lord came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-kings/21#v18)</sup> Arise, go down to meet Ahab king of Israel, which is in Samaria: behold, he is in the vineyard of Naboth, whither he is gone down to possess it.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-kings/21#v19)</sup> And thou shalt speak unto him, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Hast thou killed, and also taken possession? And thou shalt speak unto him, saying, Thus saith the Lord, In the place where dogs licked the blood of Naboth shall dogs lick thy blood, even thine.

- The word of the Lord came to Elijah the Tishbite to go down and meet Ahab in the vineyard of Naboth.
- Elijah was to declare, Thus saith the Lord, hast thou killed and also taken possession, and in the place where dogs licked the blood of Naboth, dogs would lick Ahab's own blood.

**[1 Kings 21:20-22](/bible/1-kings/21#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/21#v20)</sup> And Ahab said to Elijah, Hast thou found me, O mine enemy? And he answered, I have found thee: because thou hast sold thyself to work evil in the sight of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-kings/21#v21)</sup> Behold, I will bring evil upon thee, and will take away thy posterity, and will cut off from Ahab him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left in Israel,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/21#v22)</sup> And will make thine house like the house of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, and like the house of Baasha the son of Ahijah, for the provocation wherewith thou hast provoked me to anger, and made Israel to sin.

- Ahab called Elijah his enemy, and Elijah answered that he had found him because Ahab had sold himself to work evil in the sight of the Lord.
- The Most High would bring evil on Ahab, cut off every male of his house, and make his house like the house of Jeroboam and the house of Baasha because of the provocation by which he made Israel to sin.
- The phrase him that pisseth against the wall refers to the males, whom the Most High said he would cut off entirely.

  - **[1 Kings 14:10](/bible/1-kings/14#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/14#v10)</sup> Therefore, behold, I will bring evil upon the house of Jeroboam, and will cut off from Jeroboam him that pisseth against the wall, and him that is shut up and left in Israel, and will take away the remnant of the house of Jeroboam, as a man taketh away dung, till it be all gone.

    The same judgment was spoken over Jeroboam, cutting off every male and taking away the remnant of his house like dung.
  - **[1 Kings 16:11](/bible/1-kings/16#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/16#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, when he began to reign, as soon as he sat on his throne, that he slew all the house of Baasha: he left him not one that pisseth against a wall, neither of his kinsfolks, nor of his friends.

    When Zimri reigned he slew all the house of Baasha, leaving not one male among his kinsfolk or friends.

**[1 Kings 21:23-24](/bible/1-kings/21#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/21#v23)</sup> And of Jezebel also spake the Lord, saying, The dogs shall eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/21#v24)</sup> Him that dieth of Ahab in the city the dogs shall eat; and him that dieth in the field shall the fowls of the air eat.

- The Most High declared that the dogs would eat Jezebel by the wall of Jezreel.
- Any of Ahab's house dying in the city the dogs would eat, and any dying in the field the fowls of the air would eat, matching the curses spoken in the law.

  - **[Deuteronomy 28:26](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v26)</sup> And thy carcase shall be meat unto all fowls of the air, and unto the beasts of the earth, and no man shall fray them away.

    A curse of disobedience is that the carcass becomes meat for the fowls of the air and the beasts of the earth, with none to fray them away.

**[1 Kings 21:25-26](/bible/1-kings/21#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/21#v25)</sup> But there was none like unto Ahab, which did sell himself to work wickedness in the sight of the Lord, whom Jezebel his wife stirred up.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-kings/21#v26)</sup> And he did very abominably in following idols, according to all things as did the Amorites, whom the Lord cast out before the children of Israel.

- There was none like Ahab, who sold himself to work wickedness in the sight of the Lord, stirred up by Jezebel his wife.
- He did very abominably in following idols, as did the Amorites whom the Most High cast out before the children of Israel.

**[1 Kings 21:27-29](/bible/1-kings/21#v27)**
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-kings/21#v27)</sup> And it came to pass, when Ahab heard those words, that he rent his clothes, and put sackcloth upon his flesh, and fasted, and lay in sackcloth, and went softly.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-kings/21#v28)</sup> And the word of the Lord came to Elijah the Tishbite, saying,
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/21#v29)</sup> Seest thou how Ahab humbleth himself before me? because he humbleth himself before me, I will not bring the evil in his days: but in his son’s days will I bring the evil upon his house.

- When Ahab heard these words he rent his clothes, put on sackcloth, fasted, and went softly, humbling himself before the Most High.
- Because Ahab humbled himself, the Most High told Elijah he would not bring the evil in Ahab's own days but would bring it upon his house in his son's days.
- The judgment was not cancelled but delayed and transferred to the next generation, showing that genuine humbling before the Most High moves his hand.
