Read the chapter → [1 Kings 13](/bible/1-kings/13)

This session picks up from the reading of Solomon's decline. Against the counsel of his father David, of Moses, and of the prophets, Solomon entangled himself with foreign nations and their women and turned his heart to serve their gods. Because of this the kingdom was rent: ten tribes followed Jeroboam, while Judah and Benjamin stayed with Rehoboam. Chapters 13 through 16 trace the pattern of kings who inherit their fathers' sins, the judgments the Most High pronounces through His prophets, and the slow but certain fulfillment of His word. The chapters open at Bethel, where Jeroboam still burns incense on the altar he devised in his own heart.

**[1 Kings 13:1-5](/bible/1-kings/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/13#v1)</sup> And, behold, there came a man of God out of Judah by the word of the Lord unto Beth–el: and Jeroboam stood by the altar to burn incense.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/13#v2)</sup> And he cried against the altar in the word of the Lord, and said, O altar, altar, thus saith the Lord; Behold, a child shall be born unto the house of David, Josiah by name; and upon thee shall he offer the priests of the high places that burn incense upon thee, and men’s bones shall be burnt upon thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/13#v3)</sup> And he gave a sign the same day, saying, This is the sign which the Lord hath spoken; Behold, the altar shall be rent, and the ashes that are upon it shall be poured out.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/13#v4)</sup> And it came to pass, when king Jeroboam heard the saying of the man of God, which had cried against the altar in Beth–el, that he put forth his hand from the altar, saying, Lay hold on him. And his hand, which he put forth against him, dried up, so that he could not pull it in again to him.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/13#v5)</sup> The altar also was rent, and the ashes poured out from the altar, according to the sign which the man of God had given by the word of the Lord.

- A man of God comes out of Judah by the word of the Most High and cries against Jeroboam's altar in Bethel while the king stands by it burning incense.
- He prophesies that a child named Josiah, of the house of David, will one day burn the false priests of the high places on that very altar and burn men's bones upon it.
- He gives a sign: the altar will be rent and its ashes poured out. When Jeroboam stretches out his hand to have him seized, that hand dries up and cannot be pulled back, and the altar is rent as the sign declared.

    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v21)</sup> And if thou say in thine heart, How shall we know the word which the Lord hath not spoken?
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v22)</sup> When a prophet speaketh in the name of the Lord, if the thing follow not, nor come to pass, that is the thing which the Lord hath not spoken, but the prophet hath spoken it presumptuously: thou shalt not be afraid of him.

    The test of a true prophet is that the thing he speaks in the name of the Most High comes to pass.

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/13#v6)</sup> And the king answered and said unto the man of God, Intreat now the face of the Lord thy God, and pray for me, that my hand may be restored me again. And the man of God besought the Lord, and the king’s hand was restored him again, and became as it was before.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/13#v7)</sup> And the king said unto the man of God, Come home with me, and refresh thyself, and I will give thee a reward.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-kings/13#v8)</sup> And the man of God said unto the king, If thou wilt give me half thine house, I will not go in with thee, neither will I eat bread nor drink water in this place:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/13#v9)</sup> For so was it charged me by the word of the Lord, saying, Eat no bread, nor drink water, nor turn again by the same way that thou camest.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/13#v10)</sup> So he went another way, and returned not by the way that he came to Beth–el.

- Jeroboam begs the man of God to entreat the Most High so his hand can be restored, and it is healed and becomes as before.
- The king offers reward and a meal, but the man of God refuses even half the house, because the Most High charged him to eat no bread, drink no water, and return by another way.
- He obeys and goes home by a different route than he came, honoring the exact terms of his commission out of Judah.

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-kings/13#v18)</sup> He said unto him, I am a prophet also as thou art; and an angel spake unto me by the word of the Lord, saying, Bring him back with thee into thine house, that he may eat bread and drink water. But he lied unto him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-kings/13#v19)</sup> So he went back with him, and did eat bread in his house, and drank water.

- An old prophet in Bethel hears from his sons all that the man of God did, and rides after him, finding him resting under an oak.
- The old prophet invites him home to eat, and the man of God repeats the charge he received, that he may not eat, drink, or return the same way.
- The old prophet claims an angel spoke to him by the word of the Most High telling him to bring the man back, but the scripture plainly states he lied. The man of God, giving reverence to the older prophet, goes back and eats.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 4:22](/bible/sirach/4#v22)**  Accept no person against thy soul, and let not the reverence of any man cause thee to fall.

**[1 Kings 13:20-25](/bible/1-kings/13#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/13#v20)</sup> And it came to pass, as they sat at the table, that the word of the Lord came unto the prophet that brought him back:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-kings/13#v21)</sup> And he cried unto the man of God that came from Judah, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Forasmuch as thou hast disobeyed the mouth of the Lord, and hast not kept the commandment which the Lord thy God commanded thee,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/13#v22)</sup> But camest back, and hast eaten bread and drunk water in the place, of the which the Lord did say to thee, Eat no bread, and drink no water; thy carcase shall not come unto the sepulchre of thy fathers.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/13#v23)</sup> And it came to pass, after he had eaten bread, and after he had drunk, that he saddled for him the ass, to wit, for the prophet whom he had brought back.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/13#v24)</sup> And when he was gone, a lion met him by the way, and slew him: and his carcase was cast in the way, and the ass stood by it, the lion also stood by the carcase.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/13#v25)</sup> And, behold, men passed by, and saw the carcase cast in the way, and the lion standing by the carcase: and they came and told it in the city where the old prophet dwelt.

- As they sit at the table, the word of the Most High comes through the old prophet who lied, declaring that because the man of God disobeyed the mouth of the Most High, his carcass will not come to the sepulchre of his fathers.
- After he departs, a lion meets him on the way and slays him, and his carcass is cast in the road with the ass and the lion standing by it, neither eaten nor torn.
- The Most High used the deceiver to prove whether the man would keep the charge, and disobedience under that dispensation carried the penalty of death.
  - **[Job 12:16](/bible/job/12#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/job/12#v16)</sup> With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his.

    With the Most High is strength and wisdom; the deceived and the deceiver are His.

>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-kings/13#v27)</sup> And he spake to his sons, saying, Saddle me the ass. And they saddled him.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-kings/13#v28)</sup> And he went and found his carcase cast in the way, and the ass and the lion standing by the carcase: the lion had not eaten the carcase, nor torn the ass.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/13#v29)</sup> And the prophet took up the carcase of the man of God, and laid it upon the ass, and brought it back: and the old prophet came to the city, to mourn and to bury him.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/13#v30)</sup> And he laid his carcase in his own grave; and they mourned over him, saying, Alas, my brother!

> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/13#v31)</sup> And it came to pass, after he had buried him, that he spake to his sons, saying, When I am dead, then bury me in the sepulchre wherein the man of God is buried; lay my bones beside his bones:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/13#v32)</sup> For the saying which he cried by the word of the Lord against the altar in Beth–el, and against all the houses of the high places which are in the cities of Samaria, shall surely come to pass.

- The old prophet hears of it, recognizes the man of God who disobeyed, and goes to recover the body, laying it in his own grave and mourning, "Alas, my brother."
- He charges his sons to bury him beside the man of God's bones, confessing that the word cried against the altar of Bethel and the high places of Samaria will surely come to pass.

**[1 Kings 13:33-34](/bible/1-kings/13#v33)**
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/13#v33)</sup> After this thing Jeroboam returned not from his evil way, but made again of the lowest of the people priests of the high places: whosoever would, he consecrated him, and he became one of the priests of the high places.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-kings/13#v34)</sup> And this thing became sin unto the house of Jeroboam, even to cut it off, and to destroy it from off the face of the earth.

- After all these signs, Jeroboam does not turn from his evil way but again makes priests of the high places from the lowest of the people.
- This thing becomes sin to the house of Jeroboam, enough to cut it off and destroy it from the face of the earth.
