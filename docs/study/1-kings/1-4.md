Read the chapter → [1 Kings 1](/bible/1-kings/1)

This session moves through the first four chapters of 1 Kings, the transition of the kingdom from David to Solomon. Chapter one sets the aged David against Adonijah, who exalts himself to be king, and shows how Nathan and Bathsheba act to secure the throne for Solomon by the oath the Most High put in David's mouth. The teaching stays close to the reading, drawing supporting scriptures to explain why two lie together for warmth, how the throne was promised to Solomon, and why order and righteous judgment matter for a people.

**[1 Kings 1:1-4](/bible/1-kings/1#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/1#v1)</sup> Now king David was old and stricken in years; and they covered him with clothes, but he gat no heat.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/1#v2)</sup> Wherefore his servants said unto him, Let there be sought for my lord the king a young virgin: and let her stand before the king, and let her cherish him, and let her lie in thy bosom, that my lord the king may get heat.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/1#v3)</sup> So they sought for a fair damsel throughout all the coasts of Israel, and found Abishag a Shunammite, and brought her to the king.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/1#v4)</sup> And the damsel was very fair, and cherished the king, and ministered to him: but the king knew her not.

- David is old and cannot stay warm, so his servants seek a fair young woman to lie in his bosom and give him heat. This is one of the things that happens with age, when the body no longer produces heat as it did in youth and strength.
- Abishag the Shunammite is found and ministers to the king, but he does not know her. That means he did not have relations with her; she was there only to keep him warm.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 4:9-11](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v9)</sup> Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v10)</sup> For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v11)</sup> Again, if two lie together, then they have heat: but how can one be warm alone?

    Two are better than one, and if two lie together they have heat, which is exactly what David's servants sought for him.

**[1 Kings 1:5](/bible/1-kings/1#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/1#v5)</sup> Then Adonijah the son of Haggith exalted himself, saying, I will be king: and he prepared him chariots and horsemen, and fifty men to run before him.

- Adonijah exalts himself, saying he will be king, and prepares chariots and horsemen to promote himself. He takes the throne to himself rather than receiving it from the Most High.

**[1 Kings 1:30](/bible/1-kings/1#v30)**
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/1#v30)</sup> Even as I sware unto thee by the Lord God of Israel, saying, Assuredly Solomon thy son shall reign after me, and he shall sit upon my throne in my stead; even so will I certainly do this day.

- David repeats the oath he swore by the Lord God of Israel, that Solomon his son shall reign after him and sit upon his throne in his stead. The throne belongs to Solomon by that sworn word.
  - **[1 Chronicles 28:4-5](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v4)</sup> Howbeit the Lord God of Israel chose me before all the house of my father to be king over Israel for ever: for he hath chosen Judah to be the ruler; and of the house of Judah, the house of my father; and among the sons of my father he liked me to make me king over all Israel:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v5)</sup> And of all my sons, (for the Lord hath given me many sons,) he hath chosen Solomon my son to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.

    The Most High chose Judah, then David's house, then Solomon among David's sons to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.

**[1 Kings 1:32-40](/bible/1-kings/1#v32)**
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/1#v32)</sup> And king David said, Call me Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada. And they came before the king.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/1#v33)</sup> The king also said unto them, Take with you the servants of your lord, and cause Solomon my son to ride upon mine own mule, and bring him down to Gihon:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-kings/1#v34)</sup> And let Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him there king over Israel: and blow ye with the trumpet, and say, God save king Solomon.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-kings/1#v35)</sup> Then ye shall come up after him, that he may come and sit upon my throne; for he shall be king in my stead: and I have appointed him to be ruler over Israel and over Judah.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-kings/1#v36)</sup> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada answered the king, and said, Amen: the Lord God of my lord the king say so too.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-kings/1#v37)</sup> As the Lord hath been with my lord the king, even so be he with Solomon, and make his throne greater than the throne of my lord king David.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-kings/1#v38)</sup> So Zadok the priest, and Nathan the prophet, and Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and the Cherethites, and the Pelethites, went down, and caused Solomon to ride upon king David’s mule, and brought him to Gihon.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-kings/1#v39)</sup> And Zadok the priest took an horn of oil out of the tabernacle, and anointed Solomon. And they blew the trumpet; and all the people said, God save king Solomon.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-kings/1#v40)</sup> And all the people came up after him, and the people piped with pipes, and rejoiced with great joy, so that the earth rent with the sound of them.

- David commands that Solomon be set on the king's own mule and brought to Gihon, where Zadok the priest and Nathan the prophet anoint him king over Israel. The people come up rejoicing so that the earth rings with the sound.

# 1 Kings 2: The Charge to Solomon and the Kingdom Established

Read the chapter → [1 Kings 2](/bible/1-kings/2)

**[1 Kings 2:1-4](/bible/1-kings/2#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/2#v1)</sup> Now the days of David drew nigh that he should die; and he charged Solomon his son, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/2#v2)</sup> I go the way of all the earth: be thou strong therefore, and shew thyself a man;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/2#v3)</sup> And keep the charge of the Lord thy God, to walk in his ways, to keep his statutes, and his commandments, and his judgments, and his testimonies, as it is written in the law of Moses, that thou mayest prosper in all that thou doest, and whithersoever thou turnest thyself:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/2#v4)</sup> That the Lord may continue his word which he spake concerning me, saying, If thy children take heed to their way, to walk before me in truth with all their heart and with all their soul, there shall not fail thee (said he) a man on the throne of Israel.

- The days of David draw near that he should die, and he charges Solomon to be strong, to keep the charge of the Lord his God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his statutes, commandments, and judgments as written in the law of Moses, that he may prosper.
- There was a stipulation for the throne to continue: if his children take heed to their way and walk before the Most High in truth with all their heart, the throne stands. Reading through the kings, we see many who broke the commandments, and among their children some were righteous and some unrighteous.
  - **[Psalm 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    The righteousness of the Most High is everlasting and his law is the truth, the truth in which David's seed was to walk.

**[1 Kings 2:27](/bible/1-kings/2#v27)**
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-kings/2#v27)</sup> So Solomon thrust out Abiathar from being priest unto the Lord; that he might fulfil the word of the Lord, which he spake concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh.

- Solomon thrusts out Abiathar from being priest unto the Lord, fulfilling the word the Most High spoke concerning the house of Eli in Shiloh. The old judgment on Eli's line is carried out.

**[1 Kings 2:28-34](/bible/1-kings/2#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-kings/2#v28)</sup> Then tidings came to Joab: for Joab had turned after Adonijah, though he turned not after Absalom. And Joab fled unto the tabernacle of the Lord, and caught hold on the horns of the altar.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/2#v29)</sup> And it was told king Solomon that Joab was fled unto the tabernacle of the Lord; and, behold, he is by the altar. Then Solomon sent Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, saying, Go, fall upon him.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/2#v30)</sup> And Benaiah came to the tabernacle of the Lord, and said unto him, Thus saith the king, Come forth. And he said, Nay; but I will die here. And Benaiah brought the king word again, saying, Thus said Joab, and thus he answered me.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/2#v31)</sup> And the king said unto him, Do as he hath said, and fall upon him, and bury him; that thou mayest take away the innocent blood, which Joab shed, from me, and from the house of my father.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/2#v32)</sup> And the Lord shall return his blood upon his own head, who fell upon two men more righteous and better than he, and slew them with the sword, my father David not knowing thereof, to wit, Abner the son of Ner, captain of the host of Israel, and Amasa the son of Jether, captain of the host of Judah.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/2#v33)</sup> Their blood shall therefore return upon the head of Joab, and upon the head of his seed for ever: but upon David, and upon his seed, and upon his house, and upon his throne, shall there be peace for ever from the Lord.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-kings/2#v34)</sup> So Benaiah the son of Jehoiada went up, and fell upon him, and slew him: and he was buried in his own house in the wilderness.

- Joab, who had turned after Adonijah, flees to the tabernacle of the Lord and catches hold of the horns of the altar, but Solomon sends Benaiah to fall upon him. Joab dies for the innocent blood he shed when he slew Abner and Amasa, two men more righteous and better than he, without David's knowledge.
- The blood returns upon Joab's own head and upon his seed, while upon David and his seed and throne there shall be peace forever from the Lord. Benaiah is set over the host in his place.

**[1 Kings 2:36-46](/bible/1-kings/2#v36)**
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-kings/2#v36)</sup> And the king sent and called for Shimei, and said unto him, Build thee an house in Jerusalem, and dwell there, and go not forth thence any whither.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-kings/2#v37)</sup> For it shall be, that on the day thou goest out, and passest over the brook Kidron, thou shalt know for certain that thou shalt surely die: thy blood shall be upon thine own head.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-kings/2#v38)</sup> And Shimei said unto the king, The saying is good: as my lord the king hath said, so will thy servant do. And Shimei dwelt in Jerusalem many days.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-kings/2#v39)</sup> And it came to pass at the end of three years, that two of the servants of Shimei ran away unto Achish son of Maachah king of Gath. And they told Shimei, saying, Behold, thy servants be in Gath.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-kings/2#v40)</sup> And Shimei arose, and saddled his ass, and went to Gath to Achish to seek his servants: and Shimei went, and brought his servants from Gath.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/1-kings/2#v41)</sup> And it was told Solomon that Shimei had gone from Jerusalem to Gath, and was come again.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-kings/2#v42)</sup> And the king sent and called for Shimei, and said unto him, Did I not make thee to swear by the Lord, and protested unto thee, saying, Know for a certain, on the day thou goest out, and walkest abroad any whither, that thou shalt surely die? and thou saidst unto me, The word that I have heard is good.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/1-kings/2#v43)</sup> Why then hast thou not kept the oath of the Lord, and the commandment that I have charged thee with?
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/1-kings/2#v44)</sup> The king said moreover to Shimei, Thou knowest all the wickedness which thine heart is privy to, that thou didst to David my father: therefore the Lord shall return thy wickedness upon thine own head;
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/1-kings/2#v45)</sup> And king Solomon shall be blessed, and the throne of David shall be established before the Lord for ever.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/1-kings/2#v46)</sup> So the king commanded Benaiah the son of Jehoiada; which went out, and fell upon him, that he died. And the kingdom was established in the hand of Solomon.

- Shimei is commanded to build a house in Jerusalem and dwell there, never crossing the brook Kidron on pain of death, and he agrees. After three years he goes to Gath after his runaway servants and breaks the oath, so Solomon has Benaiah put him to death, and the kingdom is established in Solomon's hand.

# 1 Kings 3: Solomon Asks for Wisdom to Judge

Read the chapter → [1 Kings 3](/bible/1-kings/3)

**[1 Kings 3:1-4](/bible/1-kings/3#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/3#v1)</sup> And Solomon made affinity with Pharaoh king of Egypt, and took Pharaoh’s daughter, and brought her into the city of David, until he had made an end of building his own house, and the house of the Lord, and the wall of Jerusalem round about.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/3#v2)</sup> Only the people sacrificed in high places, because there was no house built unto the name of the Lord, until those days.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/3#v3)</sup> And Solomon loved the Lord, walking in the statutes of David his father: only he sacrificed and burnt incense in high places.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/3#v4)</sup> And the king went to Gibeon to sacrifice there; for that was the great high place: a thousand burnt offerings did Solomon offer upon that altar.

- Solomon makes affinity with Pharaoh king of Egypt and takes his daughter, while building his own house, the house of the Lord, and the wall of Jerusalem. He loves the Lord and walks in the statutes of David his father, though the people still sacrifice in high places because no house was yet built to the name of the Lord.

**[1 Kings 3:5-9](/bible/1-kings/3#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/3#v5)</sup> In Gibeon the Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream by night: and God said, Ask what I shall give thee.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/3#v6)</sup> And Solomon said, Thou hast shewed unto thy servant David my father great mercy, according as he walked before thee in truth, and in righteousness, and in uprightness of heart with thee; and thou hast kept for him this great kindness, that thou hast given him a son to sit on his throne, as it is this day.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/3#v7)</sup> And now, O Lord my God, thou hast made thy servant king instead of David my father: and I am but a little child: I know not how to go out or come in.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-kings/3#v8)</sup> And thy servant is in the midst of thy people which thou hast chosen, a great people, that cannot be numbered nor counted for multitude.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/3#v9)</sup> Give therefore thy servant an understanding heart to judge thy people, that I may discern between good and bad: for who is able to judge this thy so great a people?

- At Gibeon the Most High appears to Solomon in a dream and says, ask what I shall give thee. Solomon, calling himself a little child who knows not how to go out or come in, asks for an understanding heart to judge the great people, to discern between good and bad.
- This is what we still need to pray for. We should pray that the Most High endows our leadership with wisdom, and that we ourselves learn wisdom in these scriptures and how to judge matters among one another.
  - **[1 Corinthians 6:1-3](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v1)</sup> Dare any of you, having a matter against another, go to law before the unjust, and not before the saints?
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v2)</sup> Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world? and if the world shall be judged by you, are ye unworthy to judge the smallest matters?
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v3)</sup> Know ye not that we shall judge angels? how much more things that pertain to this life?

    The saints shall judge the world and even angels, so those in the church should be able to judge the smallest matters of this life.
  - **[Psalm 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    The Most High exalts the horn of his people, the children of Israel, a people near unto him, who are the saints.

**[1 Kings 3:11-14](/bible/1-kings/3#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/3#v11)</sup> And God said unto him, Because thou hast asked this thing, and hast not asked for thyself long life; neither hast asked riches for thyself, nor hast asked the life of thine enemies; but hast asked for thyself understanding to discern judgment;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-kings/3#v12)</sup> Behold, I have done according to thy words: lo, I have given thee a wise and an understanding heart; so that there was none like thee before thee, neither after thee shall any arise like unto thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-kings/3#v13)</sup> And I have also given thee that which thou hast not asked, both riches, and honour: so that there shall not be any among the kings like unto thee all thy days.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-kings/3#v14)</sup> And if thou wilt walk in my ways, to keep my statutes and my commandments, as thy father David did walk, then I will lengthen thy days.

- Because Solomon asked for understanding to discern judgment and not for long life, riches, or the life of his enemies, the Most High gives him a wise and understanding heart like no other before or after. He also gives what Solomon did not ask, riches and honor, and promises length of days if he walks in his ways.

>

>

- Two harlots come before the king, each claiming the living child, after one woman's child died in the night and she exchanged it. Solomon calls for a sword and commands the living child divided in two, and gives half to each.
- The true mother pleads that the child be given alive to the other rather than slain, and by this the king discerns her, for her bowels yearned upon her son. All Israel hears the judgment and fears the king, for they see the wisdom of God is in him to do judgment.

# 1 Kings 4: Government, Peace, and the Wisdom That Excelled All

Read the chapter → [1 Kings 4](/bible/1-kings/4)

**[1 Kings 4:1-7](/bible/1-kings/4#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/4#v1)</sup> So king Solomon was king over all Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/4#v2)</sup> And these were the princes which he had; Azariah the son of Zadok the priest,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/4#v3)</sup> Elihoreph and Ahiah, the sons of Shisha, scribes; Jehoshaphat the son of Ahilud, the recorder.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/4#v4)</sup> And Benaiah the son of Jehoiada was over the host: and Zadok and Abiathar were the priests:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/4#v5)</sup> And Azariah the son of Nathan was over the officers: and Zabud the son of Nathan was principal officer, and the king’s friend:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/4#v6)</sup> And Ahishar was over the household: and Adoniram the son of Abda was over the tribute.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/4#v7)</sup> And Solomon had twelve officers over all Israel, which provided victuals for the king and his household: each man his month in a year made provision.

- Solomon is king over all Israel, and these are the princes he set: priests, scribes, a recorder, one over the host, one over the officers, one over the household, and one over the tribute. Twelve officers provide victuals for the king and his household, each man his month in the year.
- This is the establishment of government, showing order. There is the king, then ranking men, each with a position of order, the same pattern of captains and officers set up in the days of Moses.

**[1 Kings 4:20-25](/bible/1-kings/4#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/4#v20)</sup> Judah and Israel were many, as the sand which is by the sea in multitude, eating and drinking, and making merry.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-kings/4#v21)</sup> And Solomon reigned over all kingdoms from the river unto the land of the Philistines, and unto the border of Egypt: they brought presents, and served Solomon all the days of his life.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/4#v22)</sup> And Solomon’s provision for one day was thirty measures of fine flour, and threescore measures of meal,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/4#v23)</sup> Ten fat oxen, and twenty oxen out of the pastures, and an hundred sheep, beside harts, and roebucks, and fallowdeer, and fatted fowl.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/4#v24)</sup> For he had dominion over all the region on this side the river, from Tiphsah even to Azzah, over all the kings on this side the river: and he had peace on all sides round about him.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/4#v25)</sup> And Judah and Israel dwelt safely, every man under his vine and under his fig tree, from Dan even to Beer–sheba, all the days of Solomon.

- Judah and Israel are as many as the sand by the sea, eating, drinking, and making merry, and Solomon reigns over all the kingdoms from the river to the border of Egypt, who bring presents and serve him. Every man dwells safely under his vine and fig tree from Dan to Beersheba.
- Israel dwells safely because of the wars of King David, who put down all the surrounding nations and put them to tribute. That is why Solomon is able to rule in a time of peace.

**[1 Kings 4:29-34](/bible/1-kings/4#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/4#v29)</sup> And God gave Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, and largeness of heart, even as the sand that is on the sea shore.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/4#v30)</sup> And Solomon’s wisdom excelled the wisdom of all the children of the east country, and all the wisdom of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/4#v31)</sup> For he was wiser than all men; than Ethan the Ezrahite, and Heman, and Chalcol, and Darda, the sons of Mahol: and his fame was in all nations round about.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/4#v32)</sup> And he spake three thousand proverbs: and his songs were a thousand and five.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/4#v33)</sup> And he spake of trees, from the cedar tree that is in Lebanon even unto the hyssop that springeth out of the wall: he spake also of beasts, and of fowl, and of creeping things, and of fishes.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-kings/4#v34)</sup> And there came of all people to hear the wisdom of Solomon, from all kings of the earth, which had heard of his wisdom.

- The Most High gives Solomon wisdom and understanding exceeding much, largeness of heart as the sand on the seashore, so that his wisdom excels all the children of the east country and all the wisdom of Egypt. He is wiser than all men, speaks three thousand proverbs, and his songs are a thousand and five, which is why we have Proverbs and the Song of Songs.
- He speaks of trees, beasts, fowl, creeping things, and fishes, and people of all nations come to hear his wisdom. Since his wisdom already excelled Egypt's, we have no need to dabble in Egyptology or any doctrine outside the scriptures given to us.
