**[1 Kings 18:1-6](/bible/1-kings/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/18#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after many days, that the word of the Lord came to Elijah in the third year, saying, Go, shew thyself unto Ahab; and I will send rain upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/18#v2)</sup> And Elijah went to shew himself unto Ahab. And there was a sore famine in Samaria.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/18#v3)</sup> And Ahab called Obadiah, which was the governor of his house. (Now Obadiah feared the Lord greatly:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/18#v4)</sup> For it was so, when Jezebel cut off the prophets of the Lord, that Obadiah took an hundred prophets, and hid them by fifty in a cave, and fed them with bread and water.)
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/18#v5)</sup> And Ahab said unto Obadiah, Go into the land, unto all fountains of water, and unto all brooks: peradventure we may find grass to save the horses and mules alive, that we lose not all the beasts.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/18#v6)</sup> So they divided the land between them to pass throughout it: Ahab went one way by himself, and Obadiah went another way by himself.

- After many days the word of the Lord came to Elijah in the third year: Go show thyself unto Ahab, and I will send rain upon the earth. There was a sore famine in Samaria.
- Ahab called Obadiah, governor of his house, who feared the Lord greatly; when Jezebel cut off the prophets of the Lord, Obadiah took an hundred prophets and hid them by fifty in a cave and fed them with bread and water, putting his own life in danger to save them.
- The famine was so sore it was hitting the animals, so Ahab and Obadiah divided the land between them, each going his own way to seek fountains and brooks to find grass to save the horses and mules alive. This was the fruit of the drought Elijah had pronounced.

**[1 Kings 18:7-16](/bible/1-kings/18#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/18#v7)</sup> And as Obadiah was in the way, behold, Elijah met him: and he knew him, and fell on his face, and said, Art thou that my lord Elijah?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-kings/18#v8)</sup> And he answered him, I am: go, tell thy lord, Behold, Elijah is here.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/18#v9)</sup> And he said, What have I sinned, that thou wouldest deliver thy servant into the hand of Ahab, to slay me?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/18#v10)</sup> As the Lord thy God liveth, there is no nation or kingdom, whither my lord hath not sent to seek thee: and when they said, He is not there; he took an oath of the kingdom and nation, that they found thee not.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/18#v11)</sup> And now thou sayest, Go, tell thy lord, Behold, Elijah is here.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-kings/18#v12)</sup> And it shall come to pass, as soon as I am gone from thee, that the Spirit of the Lord shall carry thee whither I know not; and so when I come and tell Ahab, and he cannot find thee, he shall slay me: but I thy servant fear the Lord from my youth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-kings/18#v13)</sup> Was it not told my lord what I did when Jezebel slew the prophets of the Lord, how I hid an hundred men of the Lord’s prophets by fifty in a cave, and fed them with bread and water?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-kings/18#v14)</sup> And now thou sayest, Go, tell thy lord, Behold, Elijah is here: and he shall slay me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-kings/18#v15)</sup> And Elijah said, As the Lord of hosts liveth, before whom I stand, I will surely shew myself unto him to day.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-kings/18#v16)</sup> So Obadiah went to meet Ahab, and told him: and Ahab went to meet Elijah.

- As Obadiah went his way, Elijah met him; Obadiah fell on his face and said, Art thou my lord Elijah? Elijah told him to go tell Ahab that Elijah is here.
- Obadiah was afraid, for Ahab had searched every nation and kingdom to find and slay Elijah; that is how badly Jezebel wanted him dead, so she got Ahab to do her bidding. This is why Elijah had been on the run, first by Jordan, then years with the widow.
- Obadiah feared that the spirit of the Lord would carry Elijah away and Ahab would slay him for a false report, so he pleaded his own record: he feared the Lord from his youth and had hidden a hundred of the Lord's prophets by fifty in a cave. Elijah swore by the Lord of hosts that he would surely show himself to Ahab that day, and so Obadiah went to Ahab, and Ahab came to meet Elijah.

**[1 Kings 18:17-19](/bible/1-kings/18#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-kings/18#v17)</sup> And it came to pass, when Ahab saw Elijah, that Ahab said unto him, Art thou he that troubleth Israel?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-kings/18#v18)</sup> And he answered, I have not troubled Israel; but thou, and thy father’s house, in that ye have forsaken the commandments of the Lord, and thou hast followed Baalim.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-kings/18#v19)</sup> Now therefore send, and gather to me all Israel unto mount Carmel, and the prophets of Baal four hundred and fifty, and the prophets of the groves four hundred, which eat at Jezebel’s table.

- When Ahab saw Elijah he said, Art thou he that troubleth Israel? That is the mind of a wicked person: he blamed the trouble on the ordained prophet of God rather than on his own wickedness. When the spirit is gone, wisdom cannot enter a malicious soul, so Ahab had thoughts without understanding.
- Elijah answered, I have not troubled Israel, but thou and thy father's house, in that ye have forsaken the commandments of the Lord and thou hast followed Baalim. It was Ahab following the sin of Jeroboam the son of Nebat, worshipping the golden calves, that brought the trouble.
- Elijah commanded that all Israel be gathered to Mount Carmel with the four hundred and fifty prophets of Baal and the four hundred prophets of the groves that eat at Jezebel's table. Jezebel was the one feeding these eight hundred and fifty false prophets and encouraging them, and Ahab never checked her.

    > <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v3)</sup> For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v4)</sup> For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; nor dwell in the body that is subject unto sin.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)</sup> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

    Into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; sin comes in and wisdom goes out. Ahab was full of sin, so his wisdom was hindered and he had thoughts without understanding, blaming the righteous man for his own doings.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 4:11](/bible/sirach/4#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/4#v11)</sup> Wisdom exalteth her children, and layeth hold of them that seek her.

    Wisdom exalteth her children and layeth hold of them that seek her; especially with the new, the Most High deals with each differently and gives time to get it right.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 4:17-19](/bible/sirach/4#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/4#v17)</sup> For at the first she will walk with him by crooked ways, and bring fear and dread upon him, and torment him with her discipline, until she may trust his soul, and try him by her laws.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/4#v18)</sup> Then will she return the straight way unto him, and comfort him, and shew him her secrets.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/4#v19)</sup> But if he go wrong, she will forsake him, and give him over to his own ruin.

    Wisdom walks with a man by crooked ways at the first, tormenting him in his mind until he rids his body of sin; if he goes wrong she forsakes him and gives him over to his own ruin, which is what happened to Ahab.

**[1 Kings 18:20-24](/bible/1-kings/18#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/18#v20)</sup> So Ahab sent unto all the children of Israel, and gathered the prophets together unto mount Carmel.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-kings/18#v21)</sup> And Elijah came unto all the people, and said, How long halt ye between two opinions? if the Lord be God, follow him: but if Baal, then follow him. And the people answered him not a word.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/18#v22)</sup> Then said Elijah unto the people, I, even I only, remain a prophet of the Lord; but Baal’s prophets are four hundred and fifty men.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/18#v23)</sup> Let them therefore give us two bullocks; and let them choose one bullock for themselves, and cut it in pieces, and lay it on wood, and put no fire under: and I will dress the other bullock, and lay it on wood, and put no fire under:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/18#v24)</sup> And call ye on the name of your gods, and I will call on the name of the Lord: and the God that answereth by fire, let him be God. And all the people answered and said, It is well spoken.

- Ahab gathered the prophets and all Israel to Mount Carmel, and Elijah said unto the people, How long halt ye between two opinions? If the Lord be God, follow him, but if Baal, then follow him. And the people answered him not a word.
- That is a call for all of us to examine ourselves: cast away every burden and sin and follow the Most High and his son fully, keeping the commandments, and do not keep one foot in and one foot out. Being lukewarm, playing the middle ground, ends with Christ spewing a man out of his mouth, and the next step after that is death.
- Elijah proposed the test: let each side dress a bullock, lay it on wood, and put no fire under, and the God that answereth by fire, let him be God. All the people said, It is well spoken.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 3:15-16](/bible/revelation/3#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/3#v15)</sup> I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot: I would thou wert cold or hot.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/revelation/3#v16)</sup> So then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spue thee out of my mouth.

    Christ said, I know thy works, that thou art neither cold nor hot; I would thou wert cold or hot; so then because thou art lukewarm, and neither cold nor hot, I will spew thee out of my mouth. The lukewarm who know the truth but keep no commandments will be cast away.

**[1 Kings 18:25-29](/bible/1-kings/18#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/18#v25)</sup> And Elijah said unto the prophets of Baal, Choose you one bullock for yourselves, and dress it first; for ye are many; and call on the name of your gods, but put no fire under.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-kings/18#v26)</sup> And they took the bullock which was given them, and they dressed it, and called on the name of Baal from morning even until noon, saying, O Baal, hear us. But there was no voice, nor any that answered. And they leaped upon the altar which was made.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-kings/18#v27)</sup> And it came to pass at noon, that Elijah mocked them, and said, Cry aloud: for he is a god; either he is talking, or he is pursuing, or he is in a journey, or peradventure he sleepeth, and must be awaked.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-kings/18#v28)</sup> And they cried aloud, and cut themselves after their manner with knives and lancets, till the blood gushed out upon them.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/18#v29)</sup> And it came to pass, when midday was past, and they prophesied until the time of the offering of the evening sacrifice, that there was neither voice, nor any to answer, nor any that regarded.

- The prophets of Baal dressed their bullock first and called on the name of Baal from morning until noon, saying, O Baal, hear us, but there was no voice nor any that answered, and they leaped upon the altar. Picture the scene: eight hundred and fifty men doing this, running wild, while Elijah sat back watching the madness.
- At noon Elijah mocked them, saying, Cry aloud, for he is a god; either he is talking, or pursuing, or on a journey, or peradventure he sleepeth and must be awaked. They cried aloud and cut themselves with knives and lancets till the blood gushed out, for cutting themselves was part of their false worship whenever they called on Baal.
- From midday past the time of the evening sacrifice there was neither voice nor any to answer nor any that regarded. No one answered because the Most High will not give his glory to another, and the gods of the nations are idols that are not real; they are made up by people who have no God, so when they call on them the idols cannot answer.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 42:8](/bible/isaiah/42#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/42#v8)</sup> I am the Lord: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images.

    I am the Lord, that is my name, and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images. That is why no fire came for Baal.
  - **[Psalms 96:4-5](/bible/psalms/96#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/96#v4)</sup> For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/96#v5)</sup> For all the gods of the nations are idols: but the Lord made the heavens.

    The Lord is great and greatly to be praised, he is to be feared above all gods, for all the gods of the nations are idols, but the Lord made the heavens. The idols of the nations are not real; only the Most High made the heavens.

**[1 Kings 18:30-40](/bible/1-kings/18#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/18#v30)</sup> And Elijah said unto all the people, Come near unto me. And all the people came near unto him. And he repaired the altar of the Lord that was broken down.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/18#v31)</sup> And Elijah took twelve stones, according to the number of the tribes of the sons of Jacob, unto whom the word of the Lord came, saying, Israel shall be thy name:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/18#v32)</sup> And with the stones he built an altar in the name of the Lord: and he made a trench about the altar, as great as would contain two measures of seed.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/18#v33)</sup> And he put the wood in order, and cut the bullock in pieces, and laid him on the wood, and said, Fill four barrels with water, and pour it on the burnt sacrifice, and on the wood.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-kings/18#v34)</sup> And he said, Do it the second time. And they did it the second time. And he said, Do it the third time. And they did it the third time.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-kings/18#v35)</sup> And the water ran round about the altar; and he filled the trench also with water.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-kings/18#v36)</sup> And it came to pass at the time of the offering of the evening sacrifice, that Elijah the prophet came near, and said, Lord God of Abraham, Isaac, and of Israel, let it be known this day that thou art God in Israel, and that I am thy servant, and that I have done all these things at thy word.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-kings/18#v37)</sup> Hear me, O Lord, hear me, that this people may know that thou art the Lord God, and that thou hast turned their heart back again.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-kings/18#v38)</sup> Then the fire of the Lord fell, and consumed the burnt sacrifice, and the wood, and the stones, and the dust, and licked up the water that was in the trench.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-kings/18#v39)</sup> And when all the people saw it, they fell on their faces: and they said, The Lord, he is the God; the Lord, he is the God.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-kings/18#v40)</sup> And Elijah said unto them, Take the prophets of Baal; let not one of them escape. And they took them: and Elijah brought them down to the brook Kishon, and slew them there.

- Elijah repaired the altar of the Lord with twelve stones according to the twelve tribes of the sons of Jacob, made a trench about it, laid the wood and the bullock in order, and had them drench the sacrifice with four barrels of water three times until the water filled the trench.
- At the time of the evening sacrifice Elijah prayed, Lord God of Abraham, Isaac, and of Israel, let it be known this day that thou art God in Israel and that I am thy servant. As the law appointed a sacrifice in the morning and in the evening, this was the evening sacrifice as the sun went down.
- Elijah prayed, Hear me, O Lord, that this people may know that thou art the Lord God and that thou hast turned their heart back again. Then the fire of the Lord fell and consumed the burnt sacrifice, the wood, the stones, and the dust, and licked up the water in the trench.
- When all the people saw it they fell on their faces and cried, The Lord, he is the God; the Lord, he is the God. Elijah commanded, Take the prophets of Baal, let not one of them escape, and they were brought down to the brook Kishon and slain there. In the last days it will be the same: when the Most High rains down fire again by the hands of Christ, no false prophet of Baal will escape, for they that feed the sheep of God lies will not be able to flee.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 23:1-2](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/jeremiah/23#v1)</sup> Woe be unto the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture! saith the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/23#v2)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God of Israel against the pastors that feed my people; Ye have scattered my flock, and driven them away, and have not visited them: behold, I will visit upon you the evil of your doings, saith the Lord.

    Woe be unto the pastors that destroy and scatter the sheep of my pasture, saith the Lord; the Lord will visit upon them the evil of their doings for feeding his people lies. The same judgment on the prophets of Baal will fall on the false shepherds today.

**[1 Kings 18:41-46](/bible/1-kings/18#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/1-kings/18#v41)</sup> And Elijah said unto Ahab, Get thee up, eat and drink; for there is a sound of abundance of rain.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-kings/18#v42)</sup> So Ahab went up to eat and to drink. And Elijah went up to the top of Carmel; and he cast himself down upon the earth, and put his face between his knees,
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/1-kings/18#v43)</sup> And said to his servant, Go up now, look toward the sea. And he went up, and looked, and said, There is nothing. And he said, Go again seven times.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/1-kings/18#v44)</sup> And it came to pass at the seventh time, that he said, Behold, there ariseth a little cloud out of the sea, like a man’s hand. And he said, Go up, say unto Ahab, Prepare thy chariot, and get thee down, that the rain stop thee not.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/1-kings/18#v45)</sup> And it came to pass in the mean while, that the heaven was black with clouds and wind, and there was a great rain. And Ahab rode, and went to Jezreel.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/1-kings/18#v46)</sup> And the hand of the Lord was on Elijah; and he girded up his loins, and ran before Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.

- Elijah told Ahab to get up and eat and drink, for there was a sound of abundance of rain. Ahab went up to eat while Elijah went to the top of Carmel, cast himself down upon the earth, and put his face between his knees to pray.
- Elijah sent his servant seven times to look toward the sea; at the seventh time he reported a little cloud out of the sea like a man's hand. Elijah sent word to Ahab to prepare his chariot and get down before the rain stopped him, for the hand of the Lord was coming.
- The heaven grew black with clouds and wind and there was a great rain; Ahab rode to Jezreel, a city in the north in Samaria, and the hand of the Lord was on Elijah so that he girded up his loins and ran before Ahab to the entrance of Jezreel.
