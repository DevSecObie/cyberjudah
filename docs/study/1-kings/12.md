**[1 Kings 12:1-5](/bible/1-kings/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/12#v1)</sup> And Rehoboam went to Shechem: for all Israel were come to Shechem to make him king.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/12#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, when Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who was yet in Egypt, heard of it, (for he was fled from the presence of king Solomon, and Jeroboam dwelt in Egypt;)
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/12#v3)</sup> That they sent and called him. And Jeroboam and all the congregation of Israel came, and spake unto Rehoboam, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/12#v4)</sup> Thy father made our yoke grievous: now therefore make thou the grievous service of thy father, and his heavy yoke which he put upon us, lighter, and we will serve thee.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-kings/12#v5)</sup> And he said unto them, Depart yet for three days, then come again to me. And the people departed.

- Rehoboam went to Shechem, where all Israel came to make him king. Jeroboam, still in Egypt, heard of it and returned, and the congregation of Israel came to Rehoboam asking him to lighten the grievous yoke and heavy service his father had put on them.
- The heavy yoke was the labor of the building; Solomon did not enslave his own people as he did the other nations, but they did have to put in that work. Rehoboam told them to depart three days and return, which was wisdom, for it is right to take time and get counsel before giving an answer.

**[1 Kings 12:6-11](/bible/1-kings/12#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-kings/12#v6)</sup> And king Rehoboam consulted with the old men, that stood before Solomon his father while he yet lived, and said, How do ye advise that I may answer this people?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/12#v7)</sup> And they spake unto him, saying, If thou wilt be a servant unto this people this day, and wilt serve them, and answer them, and speak good words to them, then they will be thy servants for ever.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-kings/12#v8)</sup> But he forsook the counsel of the old men, which they had given him, and consulted with the young men that were grown up with him, and which stood before him:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/12#v9)</sup> And he said unto them, What counsel give ye that we may answer this people, who have spoken to me, saying, Make the yoke which thy father did put upon us lighter?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/12#v10)</sup> And the young men that were grown up with him spake unto him, saying, Thus shalt thou speak unto this people that spake unto thee, saying, Thy father made our yoke heavy, but make thou it lighter unto us; thus shalt thou say unto them, My little finger shall be thicker than my father’s loins.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/12#v11)</sup> And now whereas my father did lade you with a heavy yoke, I will add to your yoke: my father hath chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.

- Rehoboam consulted the old men that stood before Solomon his father, who advised that if he would be a servant to the people and speak good words, they would be his servants forever, but he forsook their counsel and consulted the young men he grew up with.
- Solomon was the wisest man on earth and still had counselors, yet Rehoboam forsook the counselors of the wisest man for his peers. The problem is getting counsel from those on your own level rather than from elders.
- The young men told him to say his little finger would be thicker than his father's loins, and that where his father chastised them with whips he would chastise them with scorpions, which was disrespectful.

**[1 Kings 12:12-15](/bible/1-kings/12#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-kings/12#v12)</sup> So Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam the third day, as the king had appointed, saying, Come to me again the third day.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-kings/12#v13)</sup> And the king answered the people roughly, and forsook the old men’s counsel that they gave him;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-kings/12#v14)</sup> And spake to them after the counsel of the young men, saying, My father made your yoke heavy, and I will add to your yoke: my father also chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-kings/12#v15)</sup> Wherefore the king hearkened not unto the people; for the cause was from the Lord, that he might perform his saying, which the Lord spake by Ahijah the Shilonite unto Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

- When Jeroboam and the people came the third day, Rehoboam answered roughly, forsaking the old men's counsel, and told them he would add to their yoke and chastise them with scorpions.
- The king hearkened not to the people, for the cause was from the Most High, that he might perform the saying he spoke by Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam; the prophecy had to come to pass.

**[1 Kings 12:16-20](/bible/1-kings/12#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-kings/12#v16)</sup> So when all Israel saw that the king hearkened not unto them, the people answered the king, saying, What portion have we in David? neither have we inheritance in the son of Jesse: to your tents, O Israel: now see to thine own house, David. So Israel departed unto their tents.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-kings/12#v17)</sup> But as for the children of Israel which dwelt in the cities of Judah, Rehoboam reigned over them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-kings/12#v18)</sup> Then king Rehoboam sent Adoram, who was over the tribute; and all Israel stoned him with stones, that he died. Therefore king Rehoboam made speed to get him up to his chariot, to flee to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-kings/12#v19)</sup> So Israel rebelled against the house of David unto this day.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/12#v20)</sup> And it came to pass, when all Israel heard that Jeroboam was come again, that they sent and called him unto the congregation, and made him king over all Israel: there was none that followed the house of David, but the tribe of Judah only.

- When all Israel saw the king would not hearken, they said, What portion have we in David? To your tents, O Israel. So Israel departed and rebelled against the house of David to this day, and only over those in the cities of Judah did Rehoboam reign.
- Rehoboam sent Adoram who was over the tribute, and all Israel stoned him with stones, so the king fled in his chariot to Jerusalem. This is the split, the northern tribes rebelling against the house of David until this day.
- When Israel heard Jeroboam had returned, they called him to the congregation and made him king over Israel; none followed the house of David but the tribe of Judah only. That is how Jeroboam got the ten tribes.

**[1 Kings 12:21-24](/bible/1-kings/12#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-kings/12#v21)</sup> And when Rehoboam was come to Jerusalem, he assembled all the house of Judah, with the tribe of Benjamin, an hundred and fourscore thousand chosen men, which were warriors, to fight against the house of Israel, to bring the kingdom again to Rehoboam the son of Solomon.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-kings/12#v22)</sup> But the word of God came unto Shemaiah the man of God, saying,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/12#v23)</sup> Speak unto Rehoboam, the son of Solomon, king of Judah, and unto all the house of Judah and Benjamin, and to the remnant of the people, saying,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/12#v24)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Ye shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren the children of Israel: return every man to his house; for this thing is from me. They hearkened therefore to the word of the Lord, and returned to depart, according to the word of the Lord.

- Rehoboam assembled the house of Judah with Benjamin, a hundred and eighty thousand chosen warriors, to fight against the house of Israel and bring the kingdom back to himself.
- But the word of the Most High came to Shemaiah the man of God, telling Rehoboam and the house of Judah and Benjamin not to fight against their brethren the children of Israel, for this thing was from the Most High; and they hearkened and returned home.

**[1 Kings 12:25-33](/bible/1-kings/12#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/12#v25)</sup> Then Jeroboam built Shechem in mount Ephraim, and dwelt therein; and went out from thence, and built Penuel.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-kings/12#v26)</sup> And Jeroboam said in his heart, Now shall the kingdom return to the house of David:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-kings/12#v27)</sup> If this people go up to do sacrifice in the house of the Lord at Jerusalem, then shall the heart of this people turn again unto their lord, even unto Rehoboam king of Judah, and they shall kill me, and go again to Rehoboam king of Judah.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-kings/12#v28)</sup> Whereupon the king took counsel, and made two calves of gold, and said unto them, It is too much for you to go up to Jerusalem: behold thy gods, O Israel, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/12#v29)</sup> And he set the one in Beth–el, and the other put he in Dan.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/12#v30)</sup> And this thing became a sin: for the people went to worship before the one, even unto Dan.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/12#v31)</sup> And he made an house of high places, and made priests of the lowest of the people, which were not of the sons of Levi.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-kings/12#v32)</sup> And Jeroboam ordained a feast in the eighth month, on the fifteenth day of the month, like unto the feast that is in Judah, and he offered upon the altar. So did he in Beth–el, sacrificing unto the calves that he had made: and he placed in Beth–el the priests of the high places which he had made.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/12#v33)</sup> So he offered upon the altar which he had made in Beth–el the fifteenth day of the eighth month, even in the month which he had devised of his own heart; and ordained a feast unto the children of Israel: and he offered upon the altar, and burnt incense.

- Jeroboam built Shechem in mount Ephraim and Penuel, and feared in his heart that if the people went up to sacrifice at Jerusalem their hearts would turn again to Rehoboam king of Judah and they would kill him.
- Jeroboam took counsel and made two calves of gold, telling Israel it was too much to go up to Jerusalem, and setting one calf in Bethel and the other in Dan, and this thing became a sin. He made a house of high places and priests of the lowest of the people, not of the sons of Levi.
- Jeroboam ordained a feast in the eighth month on the fifteenth day, a feast he devised of his own heart, and offered on the altar at Bethel sacrificing to the calves he made. Devising your own holy days and throwing off the days of the Most High is the same error men make today, and after forty years of peace under Solomon the nation of Israel began to decline even more.
