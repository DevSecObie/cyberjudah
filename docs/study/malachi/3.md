## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Malachi 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, 16, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Malachi 3:1](/bible/malachi/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/3#v1)</sup> Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me: and the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple, even the messenger of the covenant, whom ye delight in: behold, he shall come, saith the Lord of hosts.

- “Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me”. You may think at first it is talking about John the Baptist. That is a good thought, but it is actually talking about John the Baptist in the last days.

  Precepts:
  - **[Malachi 4:4-6](/bible/malachi/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/4#v4)</sup> Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/4#v5)</sup> Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/4#v6)</sup> And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.

    “Remember ye the law of Moses my servant”. “Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord”: that is still John the Baptist, but in the last days he would be Elijah, the messenger of Malachi 3:1.

**[Malachi 3:2](/bible/malachi/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/malachi/3#v2)</sup> But who may abide the day of his coming? and who shall stand when he appeareth? for he is like a refiner’s fire, and like fullers’ soap:

- “But who may abide the day of his coming? and who shall stand when he appeareth?” That is how you know it is the last days. “For he is like a refiner’s fire, and like fullers’ soap”: Christ is like a refiner’s fire.

  Precepts:
  - **[Zechariah 13:8-9](/bible/zechariah/13#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/13#v8)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that in all the land, saith the Lord, two parts therein shall be cut off and die; but the third shall be left therein.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/13#v9)</sup> And I will bring the third part through the fire, and will refine them as silver is refined, and will try them as gold is tried: they shall call on my name, and I will hear them: I will say, It is my people: and they shall say, The Lord is my God.

    This is what it means, the refiner’s fire and the fullers’ soap. “Two parts therein shall be cut off and die”: two out of every three of our people shall be cut off and die. Why? Because we are no damn good. “But the third shall be left therein”: one third is going to be saved from this land, America.

**[Malachi 3:3-4](/bible/malachi/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/malachi/3#v3)</sup> And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver: and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/3#v4)</sup> Then shall the offering of Judah and Jerusalem be pleasant unto the Lord, as in the days of old, and as in former years.

- “He shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness. Then shall the offering of Judah and Jerusalem be pleasant unto the Lord, as in the days of old, and as in former years.”
- “And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver: and he shall purify the sons of Levi”. God said he is going to purify the sons of Levi, “and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Malachi 1:11](/bible/malachi/1#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/malachi/1#v11)</sup> For from the rising of the sun even unto the going down of the same my name shall be great among the Gentiles; and in every place incense shall be offered unto my name, and a pure offering: for my name shall be great among the heathen, saith the Lord of hosts.

    What is the offering of Judah and Jerusalem? “In every place incense shall be offered unto my name, and a pure offering: for my name shall be great among the heathen, saith the Lord of hosts.”

**[Malachi 3:5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/3#v5)</sup> And I will come near to you to judgment; and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers, and against the adulterers, and against false swearers, and against those that oppress the hireling in his wages, the widow, and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger from his right, and fear not me, saith the Lord of hosts.

- “And I will come near to you to judgment; and I will be a swift witness against the sorcerers”. Growing numbers of black women are leaving the church for witchcraft, worshipping spirits and deities, Oshun, the deity of beauty and sex, and they call it connecting to their ancestors. Sisters come in with crystals: that is witchcraft. Oh no, it’s just spirituality. Brothers, don’t fall for it, or you will have demons all up in your house. God is against sorcery.
- “And against the adulterers”: swingers, the lifestyle they advertise to people of color, is adultery. There was a couple among us who swapped partners with another couple for months, and it only came out because he got mad when his wife left him for the other husband. We had to put him out, and now he is on YouTube and TikTok like an all-knowing teacher, and he will not reveal that he was that swinger brother.
- “And against false swearers”: that goes into perjury and false statements. “And against those that oppress the hireling in his wages”. You might say this does not apply to us, but it took place right here: brothers started a trucking business together, and the one with his own authority gave the brothers he liked the good, high-paying jobs and the others little work; some were paid late and not paid the same. Everything was unjust. “The widow, and the fatherless, and that turn aside the stranger from his right, and fear not me, saith the Lord of hosts.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 22:18](/bible/exodus/22#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/22#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not suffer a witch to live.

    “Thou shalt not suffer a witch to live.” That is why God will be a swift witness against the sorcerers. Black women leaving the church for witchcraft, sisters bringing in crystals and calling it spirituality: it is witchcraft.
  - **[Deuteronomy 22:22](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v22)</sup> If a man be found lying with a woman married to an husband, then they shall both of them die, both the man that lay with the woman, and the woman: so shalt thou put away evil from Israel.

    What is adultery? In the sit-down, the Christian could not explain it. “If a man be found lying with a woman married to an husband, then they shall both of them die, both the man that lay with the woman, and the woman: so shalt thou put away evil from Israel.” The swingers’ lifestyle is adultery.
  - **[Leviticus 19:12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)</sup> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

    What is a false swearer? “And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.” That goes into perjury and false statements.

**[Malachi 3:6](/bible/malachi/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/3#v6)</sup> For I am the Lord, I change not; therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed.

- “For I am the Lord, I change not; therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed.” That is why we are still in existence today: twelve tribes.
- We are talking about the covenant for eternal life or eternal death, which is damnation. Don’t y’all want to last and exist forever in life? Eternal is without end or beginning: it don’t end, and you can’t even number the beginning of it. That’s how God is. God is eternal; there’s no beginning or ending of him. And there is eternal damnation; it’s not only eternal life. Eternal is essentially unchanging: it’s not going to change. Sometimes we don’t look up these words to get the true clarification; we just say the word and scrape across the surface of it. And God is universal, omnipresent, everywhere. The eyes of the Lord are everywhere beholding the good and the evil. You can’t hide from God.
- “For I am the Lord, I change not.” Christianity knows this. “Therefore ye sons of Jacob are not consumed”: because God made a covenant with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, that’s why we’re not consumed or destroyed. That’s the reason Jacob ain’t destroyed: God made a covenant with the twelve tribes of the children of Israel. God could have killed us and destroyed us and started over; he was going to start over with Moses.

**[Malachi 3:16](/bible/malachi/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/malachi/3#v16)</sup> Then they that feared the Lord spake often one to another: and the Lord hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the Lord, and that thought upon his name.

- What anchors hold you down when you’re going through a trial? “Then they that feared the Lord spake often one to another: and the Lord hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the Lord, and that thought upon his name.” You got to learn to communicate. Going through a trial, you talk to people. Delegation, communication, conversation: all those are pivotal in this truth, communicating with your brothers. If you stay doing your own thing in your own head, you fall.
- Even when you are getting tried, there is another element the Lord is showing you. A lot of brothers and sisters like to suffer in silence, so the Lord is not only trying you but showing you that your communication is weak and you need to grow in that area. The Lord is giving us lifelines, and we have to use them.
- “Then they that feared the Lord spake often one to another”. When you are going through things you lean on the brothers: pick up the phone, Captain, Officer, I'm struggling in this area. But because of pride we suffer in silence, and then you start to be offended with everybody around you, they don't really like me, where's the charity, and you find yourself out the door. That's how brothers and sisters get down. Spake often: not only on the Sabbath. And when a brother asks how you doing and you say, I'm enduring, that was an opportunity right there.
- And it is not really silence, because the spirit is seen on you. Are you okay, sister? I'm okay, while you look absolutely bugged out. There's nothing worse than seeing a brother pout. We all go through trials and tribulations, but we are here for each other. This is about unity.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 5:1-2](/bible/sirach/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/5#v1)</sup> Set not thy heart upon thy goods; and say not, I have enough for my life.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/5#v2)</sup> Follow not thine own mind and thy strength, to walk in the ways of thy heart:

    “Say not, I have enough for my life.” Then: “Follow not thine own mind and thy strength, to walk in the ways of thy heart”. That’s what you don’t do when you go through a trial. People going through a trial end up in their head, always in their own mind. Over time you overthink things, or you don’t think at all and you’re just emotional, leaning one way or the other, because you’re not talking to nobody.
