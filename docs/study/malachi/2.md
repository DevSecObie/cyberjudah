## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Malachi 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-9, 14-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Malachi 2:1-2](/bible/malachi/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/2#v1)</sup> And now, O ye priests, this commandment is for you.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/malachi/2#v2)</sup> If ye will not hear, and if ye will not lay it to heart, to give glory unto my name, saith the Lord of hosts, I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have cursed them already, because ye do not lay it to heart.

- “And now, O ye priests, this commandment is for you.” The priests were the Levites, so here the prophet Malachi is speaking directly to the tribe of Levi.
- “I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings: yea, I have cursed them already”. When you read chapter 1, Levi had started to sacrifice blind and broken sheep and goats with three legs; everything was jacked up. They were sacrificing things to the Lord the Lord did not want.
- “And now, O ye priests, this commandment is for you.” “I will even send a curse upon you, and I will curse your blessings.” God sent a curse upon Levi, the so-called Haitian.

**[Malachi 2:3](/bible/malachi/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/malachi/2#v3)</sup> Behold, I will corrupt your seed, and spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your solemn feasts; and one shall take you away with it.

- “Behold, I will corrupt your seed”: I will corrupt your children. “And spread dung upon your faces, even the dung of your solemn feasts”. People read that and say it's metaphoric. No, it is literal as well. Mainstream Haitian voodoo says feces are not a feature of standard practice, but they are used in three places: magical defenses and contagious magic, the black magic of secret societies like the Bizango, and around the bayakou, the laborers who empty the pit latrines by hand at night. Hair, fingernails, clothing and bodily waste are believed to carry a person's essence, so a sorcerer who gets them can anchor a curse; in aggressive spells a target's feces is mixed with hot peppers or graveyard dirt to rot them from the inside out. Talismanic bundles, charms and ritual tokens are found hidden in the latrines. Didn't we read in Deuteronomy not to deal with that stuff? It's all there.
- “And one shall take you away with it”: one shall deceive you and convince you to go that way.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 23:23](/bible/numbers/23#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/23#v23)</sup> Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel: according to this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath God wrought!

    For anyone getting nervous now, saying the stuff might work on me, my neighbor put a voodoo curse on me, she took my drawers and buried them in the yard: “Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel”. Brothers and sisters, if you are in this truth, witchcraft don't work on you. No divination can work against Israel. Balaam the witch was telling Balak, witchcraft don't work on these people.

**[Malachi 2:4-6](/bible/malachi/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/2#v4)</sup> And ye shall know that I have sent this commandment unto you, that my covenant might be with Levi, saith the Lord of hosts.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/2#v5)</sup> My covenant was with him of life and peace; and I gave them to him for the fear wherewith he feared me, and was afraid before my name.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/2#v6)</sup> The law of truth was in his mouth, and iniquity was not found in his lips: he walked with me in peace and equity, and did turn many away from iniquity.

- “That my covenant might be with Levi”. “My covenant was with him of life and peace”: the Lord's covenant was with the Levites. “For the fear wherewith he feared me, and was afraid before my name”: Levi at one time respected the one true God, the God of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob. “The law of truth was in his mouth”: Levi respected the law at one time. “And iniquity was not found in his lips”: they told no lies. “He walked with me in peace and equity, and did turn many away from iniquity”. That starts with Moses.
- “That my covenant might be with Levi.” God had a covenant with Levi, and Levi broke it. Instead of helping the people, Levi brought the people to sin, so God broke the covenant with Levi.
- “My covenant was with him of life and peace; and I gave them to him for the fear wherewith he feared me”: there was a time Levi feared the Most High.
- “The law of truth was in his mouth”: there was a time Levi was righteous, a time the so-called Haitian was righteous. Not today; there is a lot of evil going on in the midst of them. “And did turn many away from iniquity”: Levi was the one who turned the other tribes from sinning, and now Levi does the opposite and causes the others to sin.

**[Malachi 2:7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)</sup> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.

- “For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth”. Brothers who call themselves priests, your lips should keep knowledge, and the people should seek the law at the priest's mouth, “for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.”
- “For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge”: the knowledge is the law. “And they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.” There was a time Levi was the messenger.
- “For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge”. The priest should teach the people how to get themselves together. “and they should seek the law at his mouth”. We should go to the priest or pastor to learn God's laws: stop eating pork, stop celebrating fake holidays, get your house in order, do the righteous things God said, and keep the high holy days. “for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.” He's supposed to teach you to wear fringes and keep the Sabbath holy—not Sunday, the Sabbath day. That's what they should teach in these buildings.

**[Malachi 2:8](/bible/malachi/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/malachi/2#v8)</sup> But ye are departed out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith the Lord of hosts.

- “But ye are departed out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble at the law”. Today, you Haitians have caused many to stumble at God's law. “Ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi”.
- “But ye are departed out of the way”: Levi departed from the law, from the message, from what the Most High wanted him to do. “Ye have caused many to stumble at the law”: Levi caused the other brothers and sisters, the other tribes, to sin. “Ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi”: Levi has been corrupted.

**[Malachi 2:9](/bible/malachi/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/malachi/2#v9)</sup> Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people, according as ye have not kept my ways, but have been partial in the law.

- “Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people”: hated. That is why Judah used to chase the Haitians down the street and whip them for their clothes, high waters and polka-dot socks; then they would go home and try to put a voodoo spell on us, and that don't work. Among Dominicans it is a big insult to be called Haitian. This ain't man saying this; it is what the Bible said, prophecy, what God would do to the tribe of Levi, “according as ye have not kept my ways, but have been partial in the law.”
- “Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people.” Of the 12 tribes, Levi is the base: Haiti is the poorest country in the hemisphere. Why? Sin.
- The only way this changes is to repent and come back to the law of God. You want change in Haiti? Repent; that is the solution. And it is not only for Levi: Judah, Benjamin, the whole 12 tribes, if you want change, repent.

  Precepts:
  - **[Malachi 3:3](/bible/malachi/3#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/malachi/3#v3)</sup> And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver: and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness.

    This is what the Most High has for Levi: “he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness.” That is talking about repentance. Levi might be wicked as hell, but if they repent they are still going to get the kingdom: God is going to bring Levi back.

**[Malachi 2:14](/bible/malachi/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/malachi/2#v14)</sup> Yet ye say, Wherefore? Because the Lord hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom thou hast dealt treacherously: yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant.

- Why do our women have that in their wombs? “Because the Lord hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth”: our women, of our own kind, “against whom thou hast dealt treacherously”, by laying with other nations. “Yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant”: she belongs to you, and you were supposed to marry her.
- Why did God give a woman a hymen? “The Lord hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth”: the woman you married when you were both young. “Against whom thou hast dealt treacherously”: she got old, the titties sagging, her body changed, she is not the same, and now you are watching the young one and dealing treacherously with her. “Yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant.” Brothers who marry a woman, she falls under your covenant.
- So you had the paperwork, and you also had the sheets: the token of her virginity, the blood by which she entered into that covenant with you. She should belong to you for the rest of your life.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 9:18](/bible/hebrews/9#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/9#v18)</sup> Whereupon neither the first testament was dedicated without blood.

    “Whereupon neither the first testament was dedicated without blood.” A covenant is a promise or agreement, and the first covenant was established with blood: Moses killed the lambs and sprinkled the blood.
  - **[Matthew 26:28](/bible/matthew/26#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/26#v28)</sup> For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins.

    “For this is my blood of the new testament”: the new testament was established with Christ’s blood.

**[Malachi 2:15](/bible/malachi/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/malachi/2#v15)</sup> And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit. And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth.

- “And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit.” He made Adam and gave him Eve. “And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed.” The woman's womb is the housing for gods; that is why that is in our women, the womb of the gods. “Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth.” Do not put your seed in a womb that does not belong to God; put your seed in the womb of your own women. We were giving our seed to Moab and Ammon and Edom, and he said don't do that. That soil belongs to you. It is like putting a triangle peg in a square hole: it doesn't fit, and the child grows up confused, not knowing what he is, shunned by both sides.
- “And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit. And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth”: the woman you married when you were young.

  Precepts:

    “My son, keep the flower of thine age sound; and give not thy strength to strangers.” Your strength is your seed, your legacy. “When thou hast gotten a fruitful possession through all the field”: the fruitful possession is a woman, and through all the field means our people found all throughout the world. “Sow it with thine own seed, trusting in the goodness of thy stock”: your woman is your stock, your kind.

**[Malachi 2:16](/bible/malachi/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/malachi/2#v16)</sup> For the Lord, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away: for one covereth violence with his garment, saith the Lord of hosts: therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye deal not treacherously.

- This is the verse that ends the madness of people doing evil behind everybody’s back and then, when caught, saying they were keeping the commandments. “For the Lord, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away.” The subject was the priests putting away their wives, wickedness among the priests that the prophet had to check.
- “For one covereth violence with his garment.” Violence is equal to violation, as brought out with Exodus 22. The garment has the borders and the fringes, and the fringes represent the laws. So he did evil, and then covers it with a failsafe law, giving the sister a so-called law from the Bible to say they are correct. You step past the fact that you deceived to get her: you covered violence and said you were in the scriptures, and sisters get caught like that.
- “Therefore take heed to your spirit, that ye deal not treacherously.” The violence is dealing treacherously with the wives while saying we are in the scriptures. That is what covering it up means: bringing these negro laws to say we are all right.
- “For the Lord, the God of Israel, saith that he hateth putting away.” God hates putting away. You are not supposed to put away the woman you marry, because you and that woman are under a blood covenant. She is part of you and you are part of her. In the same way the Lord cannot just get rid of Israel, because of the oath he made with our fathers; because of that promise he is going to have mercy on us.

    >

    What does the garment mean? “The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat: All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.” These are the same men who tempted Christ in the 19th chapter with a failsafe law, when Christ took them all the way back to the beginning: there would be no need for a failsafe law if you were keeping the commandments of God.

    “Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery: and whoso marrieth her which is put away doth commit adultery.”

**[Malachi 2:17](/bible/malachi/2#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/malachi/2#v17)</sup> Ye have wearied the Lord with your words. Yet ye say, Wherein have we wearied him? When ye say, Every one that doeth evil is good in the sight of the Lord, and he delighteth in them; or, Where is the God of judgment?

- “Ye have wearied the Lord with your words.” The Lord says, I see your BS; you have wearied Me with your lies, even though you used a scripture. Many people are not catching this. “Yet ye say, Wherein have we wearied him?” They answer back, where have we wearied the Lord? We have Exodus 22:16; that is in the Bible. Wherein have we wearied the Lord, when we are keeping the commandments?
- “When ye say, Every one that doeth evil is good in the sight of the Lord.” We weary the Lord when we call evil good. The evil was enticing the sister, and covering it up by saying you are using the law, using the words of the Bible against righteousness to take the daughter from the father. That is wicked as hell. If a man violated a father on that level, that father would never want to see him again.
- This is a warning: do not get caught in any more foolishness. The leadership has approved instituting it across the groups: no more backdoor marriages. Courting is to be done the old way, in plain view, as it once was on the front porches under the watchful eyes of the parents and the neighbours, where there was no sneaking, a young man earned the family’s approval before the woman’s hand, and the community knew what was being dealt with. There is no enticement in that. You covered the violence of violating the fathers, enticing the daughter away from her protection to lay with her, and then used a negro law to say you were in the Bible: that is violence covered with the garment, the long fringes the Pharisees wore. They were hypocritical as hell, just like the men who deal with you women this way, and the women who deal with the men the same way. Both of you watch out.

  Precepts:

    When they say, wherein have we wearied the Lord, this is what they are talking about. “When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes”: he does not desire her any more. “Because he hath found some uncleanness in her”: some uncleanness could be anything. Men were finding reasons to get rid of their wives for BS reasons, and that is what Christ was dealing with in Matthew 19.
