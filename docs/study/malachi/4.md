## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Malachi 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Malachi 4:1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)</sup> For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.

- There is a sign to let us know that it is near. We do not know the day or the hour, but we know we are in the season, and knowing these things are coming, what manner of persons ought we to be? We need to get our minds and spirits right.
- “For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble.” If your mind and spirit are not right when that day comes, you are going to die. Woe unto them that desire the day of the Lord: what good is it to you when you are on the wrong side of the Lord? You will be howling, for the day of the Lord is at hand, and the destruction will come on those of us who are in evil and wickedness.
- “The day that cometh shall burn them up”: the wicked, Israelites and all the nations. That is the same day spoken of in Revelation 7, when the nuclear fire comes from the missiles and the angels come with fire to burn everything up and purify the earth of its wickedness, so that a righteous new seed can be born: the nation of Israel in its repentance, ready to run the earth in righteousness. “It shall leave them neither root nor branch”: those are the trees of Revelation 7. It is the same exact incident.
- “For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven”: when Christ returns you are gonna feel like you are inside of an oven, in the midst of an oven. That’s how that day is coming. “And all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble”: you are gonna be burnt up. “And the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 7:2-3](/bible/revelation/7#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/7#v2)</sup> And I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God: and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels, to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea,
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/7#v3)</sup> Saying, Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads.

    Malachi says the day “shall leave them neither root nor branch”, and the trees of Revelation 7 are those same trees. It is still talking about the same exact incident.
  - **[2 Esdras 13:10-11](/bible/2-esdras/13#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-esdras/13#v10)</sup> But only I saw that he sent out of his mouth as it had been a blast of fire, and out of his lips a flaming breath, and out of his tongue he cast out sparks and tempests.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/13#v11)</sup> And they were all mixed together; the blast of fire, the flaming breath, and the great tempest; and fell with violence upon the multitude which was prepared to fight, and burned them up every one, so that upon a sudden of an innumerable multitude nothing was to be perceived, but only dust and smell of smoke: when I saw this I was afraid.

    This is Christ about to destroy the armies of the earth: “I saw that he sent out of his mouth as it had been a blast of fire, and out of his lips a flaming breath, and out of his tongue he cast out sparks and tempests. And they were all mixed together; the blast of fire, the flaming breath, and the great tempest; and fell with violence upon the multitude which was prepared to fight”. The multitude is all the armies, the armies from Europe and all the nations that are going to come to fight against Christ.
  - **[Isaiah 9:5](/bible/isaiah/9#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/9#v5)</sup> For every battle of the warrior is with confused noise, and garments rolled in blood; but this shall be with burning and fuel of fire.

    “For every battle of the warrior is with confused noise, and garments rolled in blood”: every battle of the warriors is like that, everybody getting bloody. “But this shall be with burning and fuel of fire.” That is Christ destroying all the armies of the earth. He gonna burn everybody up with burning and fuel of fire.

**[Malachi 4:2-3](/bible/malachi/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/malachi/4#v2)</sup> But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings; and ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/malachi/4#v3)</sup> And ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this, saith the Lord of hosts.

- “But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings”, and “ye shall tread down the wicked; for they shall be ashes under the soles of your feet in the day that I shall do this.” That is what is coming. All this destruction is on its way.
- “But unto you that fear my name”: those of us that fear God’s name and keep his commandments, “shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings”. That is us being protected. When that day come that gonna burn as an oven, the Lord is going to protect us. “And ye shall go forth, and grow up as calves of the stall.”
- “But unto you that fear my name shall the Sun of righteousness arise with healing in his wings.” Spell it: S-U-N, the Sun of righteousness. What does the sun do? It arises, with healing in his wings.
- Once he arises, “ye shall go forth”, and grow up: we will be in the kingdom. So the sunrise represents us being delivered, getting out of here. The sun and the moon utter knowledge: when we wake up and the sun is up in the air, that is a sign that we are going to get out of here.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 34:16](/bible/sirach/34#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/34#v16)</sup> For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, a preservation from stumbling, and an help from falling.

    “For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat.” That is Malachi 4: he shall come with healing in his wings, and when the day burns as hot as an oven, he will be our defense from the heat, just as he was in the furnace, when four were seen in there and one was a Son of God.

**[Malachi 4:4](/bible/malachi/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/4#v4)</sup> Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments.

- “Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments.” We know about what is going on in the Middle East, but we forget what is said right here. The statutes and judgments are the laws and commandments that keep us out of trouble, the same thing brought out in Judith: keep them, and they will keep the enemies at bay.
- This is not speaking now of the law of sacrifice, but of the other commandments: thou shalt not steal, thou shalt not commit adultery, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s ox or his ass. Laws like that had to be given because of the negro mentality among our people, a mentality that is pervasive and that we need to get rid of, because you were not made that. Go to the root of the word negro: they will say it means black, but it also means dead, as in necro, necropolis, necrosis.
- “Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments.” For who? For all Israel.
- “Remember ye the law of Moses my servant, which I commanded unto him in Horeb for all Israel, with the statutes and judgments.” He is telling us again: remember, remember, remember. We were going to forget his statutes and judgments. We have to come back and remember who we are in these last days.

**[Malachi 4:5-6](/bible/malachi/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/4#v5)</sup> Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/4#v6)</sup> And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.

- “Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.” This is the sign that we are near the end: the prophet brought this truth back, and that is how we know that we are the Israelites. This is the valley of the dry bones of Ezekiel 37: we are being rebuilt and brought back. Revelation says the same: their dead bodies lay in the street of the great city, spiritually called Sodom and Egypt, where they lied to us about Christ, three days and a half, and then the spirit of life from God entered into them, they stood up, and great fear fell on the enemies that saw them.
- That is what is happening in these racist towns around us. We have committed no violence on anybody. We deal with everybody civilly, hold the doors for them, call the authorities to make sure they are all right. But our enemies want us to keep a wicked mentality, so they can accuse us to the Father and say, look at what they are doing.
- “Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.” The great and dreadful day of the Lord is the day of verse 1, the day that shall burn as an oven.
- “And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children.” The heart of the fathers is the Bible, the book of our fathers, and the spirit of the Lord coming through the prophets turns the mind of the fathers, this Bible, to the children: to us, the repentant ones coming into these sanctuaries, turning our minds back to our Bible. “And the heart of the children to their fathers”: our hearts and minds turned back to the Bible.

  Precepts:
  - **[Malachi 4:1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)</sup> For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.

    “For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts.” That is the great and dreadful day of the Lord that Elijah comes before.
  - **[Matthew 24:32-33](/bible/matthew/24#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/24#v32)</sup> Now learn a parable of the fig tree; When his branch is yet tender, and putteth forth leaves, ye know that summer is nigh:
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/24#v33)</sup> So likewise ye, when ye shall see all these things, know that it is near, even at the doors.

    Malachi warns, lest I “smite the earth with a curse”, and the curse is the destruction now sitting in the silos. It is at the doors, as Matthew 24 says. We are looking for a sign, and you will not get a bigger sign than that.
  - **[Matthew 17:11](/bible/matthew/17#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/17#v11)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Elias truly shall first come, and restore all things.

    “And Jesus answered and said unto them, Elias truly shall first come, and restore all things.” Elias is the Greek way of saying Elijah. Christ is making reference to Malachi chapter 4: Elijah shall truly come, and restore all things. Turning the hearts of the fathers and the children is that restoring.
