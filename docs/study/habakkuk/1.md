## Introduction

This session reads Nahum 2 through Habakkuk 2; these notes cover Habakkuk 1 and 2, which close the reading. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. Habakkuk asks how long the violence goes unanswered, is told the Chaldeans are being raised up, and then asks how a nation more wicked can be used against one more righteous. The answer comes back as a vision written plain upon tables, followed by five woes on the man who builds by blood and covetousness.

**[Habakkuk 1:1-4](/bible/habakkuk/1#v1)**

>
> <sup>[2](/bible/habakkuk/1#v2)</sup> O Lord, how long shall I cry, and thou wilt not hear! even cry out unto thee of violence, and thou wilt not save!

>
> <sup>[4](/bible/habakkuk/1#v4)</sup> Therefore the law is slacked, and judgment doth never go forth: for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore wrong judgment proceedeth.
