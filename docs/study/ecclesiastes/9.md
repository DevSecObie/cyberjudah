Read the chapter → [Ecclesiastes 9](/bible/ecclesiastes/9)

Chapter 9 declares that the righteous and the wise and their works are in the hand of the Most High, and that one event, death, comes alike to all. Solomon counsels the living to enjoy their labor and to stay in righteousness, and he closes on the theme that wisdom is better than strength, though the poor man's wisdom is despised.

**[Ecclesiastes 9:1-3](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v1)</sup> For all this I considered in my heart even to declare all this, that the righteous, and the wise, and their works, are in the hand of God: no man knoweth either love or hatred by all that is before them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v2)</sup> All things come alike to all: there is one event to the righteous, and to the wicked; to the good and to the clean, and to the unclean; to him that sacrificeth, and to him that sacrificeth not: as is the good, so is the sinner; and he that sweareth, as he that feareth an oath.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v3)</sup> This is an evil among all things that are done under the sun, that there is one event unto all: yea, also the heart of the sons of men is full of evil, and madness is in their heart while they live, and after that they go to the dead.

- The righteous and the wise and their works are in the hand of the Most High, and no man knows love or hatred by all that is before him.
- All things come alike to all, one event to the righteous and the wicked, the clean and the unclean; this is an evil, that one event comes to all, and the heart of the sons of men is full of evil while they live.

**[Ecclesiastes 9:4-6](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v4)</sup> For to him that is joined to all the living there is hope: for a living dog is better than a dead lion.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v5)</sup> For the living know that they shall die: but the dead know not any thing, neither have they any more a reward; for the memory of them is forgotten.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v6)</sup> Also their love, and their hatred, and their envy, is now perished; neither have they any more a portion for ever in any thing that is done under the sun.

- To him that is joined to the living there is hope, for a living dog is better than a dead lion.
- The living know that they shall die, but the dead know not anything, and their love, hatred, and envy have perished, and they have no more portion in anything done under the sun.

**[Ecclesiastes 9:7-10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v7)</sup> Go thy way, eat thy bread with joy, and drink thy wine with a merry heart; for God now accepteth thy works.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v8)</sup> Let thy garments be always white; and let thy head lack no ointment.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v9)</sup> Live joyfully with the wife whom thou lovest all the days of the life of thy vanity, which he hath given thee under the sun, all the days of thy vanity: for that is thy portion in this life, and in thy labour which thou takest under the sun.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)</sup> Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.

- Eat thy bread with joy and drink thy wine with a merry heart, for the Most High now accepts thy works.
- Let thy garments be always white, which means stay in righteousness, and let thy head lack no ointment, the ointment being the understanding.
- Live joyfully with the wife thou lovest all the days of thy vain life, and whatsoever thy hand finds to do, do it with thy might, for there is no work nor device nor knowledge nor wisdom in the grave.

**[Ecclesiastes 9:11-12](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v11)</sup> I returned, and saw under the sun, that the race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, neither yet bread to the wise, nor yet riches to men of understanding, nor yet favour to men of skill; but time and chance happeneth to them all.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v12)</sup> For man also knoweth not his time: as the fishes that are taken in an evil net, and as the birds that are caught in the snare; so are the sons of men snared in an evil time, when it falleth suddenly upon them.

- The race is not to the swift, nor the battle to the strong, nor bread to the wise, nor riches to men of understanding, but time and chance happen to them all.
- Time and chance here means death; man knows not his time, and like fishes taken in a net and birds caught in a snare, the sons of men are snared in an evil time when it falls suddenly upon them.

**[Ecclesiastes 9:13-16](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v13)</sup> This wisdom have I seen also under the sun, and it seemed great unto me:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v14)</sup> There was a little city, and few men within it; and there came a great king against it, and besieged it, and built great bulwarks against it:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v15)</sup> Now there was found in it a poor wise man, and he by his wisdom delivered the city; yet no man remembered that same poor man.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v16)</sup> Then said I, Wisdom is better than strength: nevertheless the poor man’s wisdom is despised, and his words are not heard.

- Solomon saw a wisdom that seemed great to him: a little city with few men, against which a great king came and built great bulwarks, a bulwark being a defensive wall or fortification.
- A poor wise man in the city delivered it by his wisdom, yet no man remembered that same poor man; so wisdom is better than strength, even though the poor man's wisdom is despised and his words are not heard.

**[Ecclesiastes 9:17-18](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v17)</sup> The words of wise men are heard in quiet more than the cry of him that ruleth among fools.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v18)</sup> Wisdom is better than weapons of war: but one sinner destroyeth much good.

- The words of wise men are heard in quiet more than the cry of him that rules among fools.
- Wisdom is better than weapons of war, but one sinner destroys much good, closing the reading on the worth of wisdom and the harm a single sinner can do.
