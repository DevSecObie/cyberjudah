**[Judges 7:1-3](/bible/judges/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/7#v1)</sup> Then Jerubbaal, who is Gideon, and all the people that were with him, rose up early, and pitched beside the well of Harod: so that the host of the Midianites were on the north side of them, by the hill of Moreh, in the valley.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/7#v2)</sup> And the Lord said unto Gideon, The people that are with thee are too many for me to give the Midianites into their hands, lest Israel vaunt themselves against me, saying, Mine own hand hath saved me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/7#v3)</sup> Now therefore go to, proclaim in the ears of the people, saying, Whosoever is fearful and afraid, let him return and depart early from mount Gilead. And there returned of the people twenty and two thousand; and there remained ten thousand.

- The Lord tells Gideon the people with him are too many for him to give Midian into their hands, lest Israel vaunt themselves against him, saying, mine own hand hath saved me. That is Israel: look at what I did. The Lord knows how we think.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 8:11](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v11)</sup> Beware that thou forget not the Lord thy God, in not keeping his commandments, and his judgments, and his statutes, which I command thee this day:

    Beware that thou forget not the Lord thy God in not keeping his commandments; the warning was given before they ever entered the land.
  - **[Deuteronomy 8:14-18](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v14)</sup> Then thine heart be lifted up, and thou forget the Lord thy God, which brought thee forth out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage;
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v15)</sup> Who led thee through that great and terrible wilderness, wherein were fiery serpents, and scorpions, and drought, where there was no water; who brought thee forth water out of the rock of flint;
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v16)</sup> Who fed thee in the wilderness with manna, which thy fathers knew not, that he might humble thee, and that he might prove thee, to do thee good at thy latter end;
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v17)</sup> And thou say in thine heart, My power and the might of mine hand hath gotten me this wealth.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v18)</sup> But thou shalt remember the Lord thy God: for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth, that he may establish his covenant which he sware unto thy fathers, as it is this day.

    Lest thine heart be lifted up and thou say in thine heart, my power and the might of mine hand hath gotten me this wealth; thou shalt remember the Lord thy God, for it is he that giveth thee power to get wealth.

- Whosoever is fearful and afraid, let him return: twenty and two thousand bounced back to Gilead, and ten thousand remained. That is why God always lowered the numbers going into battle, so Israel would know it was by the Lord's strength that we overcame our enemies; and it is going to be the same thing again, few numbers going into battle against the nations when that time comes.

**[Judges 7:4-6](/bible/judges/7#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/7#v4)</sup> And the Lord said unto Gideon, The people are yet too many; bring them down unto the water, and I will try them for thee there: and it shall be, that of whom I say unto thee, This shall go with thee, the same shall go with thee; and of whomsoever I say unto thee, This shall not go with thee, the same shall not go.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/7#v5)</sup> So he brought down the people unto the water: and the Lord said unto Gideon, Every one that lappeth of the water with his tongue, as a dog lappeth, him shalt thou set by himself; likewise every one that boweth down upon his knees to drink.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/7#v6)</sup> And the number of them that lapped, putting their hand to their mouth, were three hundred men: but all the rest of the people bowed down upon their knees to drink water.

- The people are yet too many; the Lord tries them at the water. Some lapped the water with their hand to their mouth as a dog lappeth, and all the rest bowed down upon their knees to drink; the lappers were three hundred men.
- What can we learn from this? You have to be willing to let go of some people. The Lord said, of whom I say, this shall not go with thee, the same shall not go: some people are not going to be beneficial to the war ahead of you, and the Lord will move them out of the way, but you have to be ready to let them go.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 12:1](/bible/genesis/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/12#v1)</sup> Now the Lord had said unto Abram, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father’s house, unto a land that I will shew thee:

    The same word came to Abraham: get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred; there were things the Lord had to do for him that required letting go.
  - **[Matthew 10:34-36](/bible/matthew/10#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/10#v34)</sup> Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/10#v35)</sup> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/10#v36)</sup> And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household.

    Christ came to divide and separate, father, mother, husband, wife, family; let that ring in your mind, you have to be willing to let go of some people for the battle ahead.

**[Judges 7:7-8](/bible/judges/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/7#v7)</sup> And the Lord said unto Gideon, By the three hundred men that lapped will I save you, and deliver the Midianites into thine hand: and let all the other people go every man unto his place.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/7#v8)</sup> So the people took victuals in their hand, and their trumpets: and he sent all the rest of Israel every man unto his tent, and retained those three hundred men: and the host of Midian was beneath him in the valley.

- By the three hundred men that lapped will I save you, and deliver the Midianites into thine hand. The Lord sent the rest of Israel every man unto his tent and retained the three hundred, with victuals and trumpets in their hands.
- Now you know where the famous story of the three hundred came from: they got it from our ancestors, from Gideon. The nations take our history and make it entertainment, and even the artwork drew those three hundred with locks and braids and gave the other host the same, because the artist knew whose history he was drawing; those three hundred were Israelites.

>
> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/7#v10)</sup> But if thou fear to go down, go thou with Phurah thy servant down to the host:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/7#v11)</sup> And thou shalt hear what they say; and afterward shall thine hands be strengthened to go down unto the host. Then went he down with Phurah his servant unto the outside of the armed men that were in the host.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/7#v12)</sup> And the Midianites and the Amalekites and all the children of the east lay along in the valley like grasshoppers for multitude; and their camels were without number, as the sand by the sea side for multitude.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/7#v13)</sup> And when Gideon was come, behold, there was a man that told a dream unto his fellow, and said, Behold, I dreamed a dream, and, lo, a cake of barley bread tumbled into the host of Midian, and came unto a tent, and smote it that it fell, and overturned it, that the tent lay along.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/7#v14)</sup> And his fellow answered and said, This is nothing else save the sword of Gideon the son of Joash, a man of Israel: for into his hand hath God delivered Midian, and all the host.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/7#v15)</sup> And it was so, when Gideon heard the telling of the dream, and the interpretation thereof, that he worshipped, and returned into the host of Israel, and said, Arise; for the Lord hath delivered into your hand the host of Midian.

- The same night the Lord sends Gideon down to the outside of the enemy host with Phurah his servant: thou shalt hear what they say, and afterward shall thine hands be strengthened. There he hears a man telling his fellow a dream of a cake of barley bread tumbling into the host and overturning a tent, and his fellow interprets it: this is nothing else save the sword of Gideon, for into his hand hath God delivered Midian.
- The Lord put that dream into the mind of the enemy and sent Gideon down to hear it, so he could go into the battle valiant and strong, without any doubt. When Gideon heard the dream and the interpretation, he worshipped, and returned to say, arise; for the Lord hath delivered into your hand the host of Midian.

**[Judges 7:16-18](/bible/judges/7#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/7#v16)</sup> And he divided the three hundred men into three companies, and he put a trumpet in every man’s hand, with empty pitchers, and lamps within the pitchers.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/7#v17)</sup> And he said unto them, Look on me, and do likewise: and, behold, when I come to the outside of the camp, it shall be that, as I do, so shall ye do.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/7#v18)</sup> When I blow with a trumpet, I and all that are with me, then blow ye the trumpets also on every side of all the camp, and say, The sword of the Lord, and of Gideon.

- Gideon divides the three hundred into three companies, a hundred to the left flank, a hundred down the middle, a hundred to the right, and puts a trumpet in every man's hand, with empty pitchers and lamps within the pitchers. Look on me, and do likewise: pay attention, and when I blow the trumpet, blow ye also on every side of the camp, and say, the sword of the Lord, and of Gideon.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 10:9](/bible/numbers/10#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/10#v9)</sup> And if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets; and ye shall be remembered before the Lord your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.

    If ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets, and ye shall be remembered before the Lord your God, and saved from your enemies. This is the history behind the shofars we blow; those trumpets are not just for looks and thumbnails, there is a deep meaning behind them going into the memorial of blowing of trumpets.

**[Judges 7:19-22](/bible/judges/7#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/7#v19)</sup> So Gideon, and the hundred men that were with him, came unto the outside of the camp in the beginning of the middle watch; and they had but newly set the watch: and they blew the trumpets, and brake the pitchers that were in their hands.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/7#v20)</sup> And the three companies blew the trumpets, and brake the pitchers, and held the lamps in their left hands, and the trumpets in their right hands to blow withal: and they cried, The sword of the Lord, and of Gideon.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/judges/7#v21)</sup> And they stood every man in his place round about the camp: and all the host ran, and cried, and fled.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/judges/7#v22)</sup> And the three hundred blew the trumpets, and the Lord set every man’s sword against his fellow, even throughout all the host: and the host fled to Beth–shittah in Zererath, and to the border of Abel–meholah, unto Tabbath.

- Gideon and his hundred come to the outside of the camp in the beginning of the middle watch, when they had but newly set the watch; in war you always have watchmen taking first, second, and third watch, and Gideon struck right in between the changing of the watchmen.
- The three companies blew the trumpets, brake the pitchers, held the lamps in their left hands and the trumpets in their right, and cried, the sword of the Lord, and of Gideon; and every man stood in his place round about the camp. Three hundred against thousands, you have to do something to mess with their mind: those horns blowing in unison put fear in the nations, and all the host ran, and cried, and fled.
- And the Lord set every man's sword against his fellow, even throughout all the host. The enemy destroyed itself; that is the Lord fighting the battle.

- The men of Naphtali, Asher, and all Manasseh gathered themselves and pursued after the Midianites, and Gideon sent messengers throughout all mount Ephraim to come down and take the waters before them; and Ephraim took two princes of Midian, slaying Oreb upon the rock Oreb and Zeeb at the winepress of Zeeb.
- Ephraim, stand up; get this spirit back and come join your brothers in the war, because Judah is calling you right now. For those that may not know, that is you so-called Hispanics, you Puerto Ricans: you are Ephraim.
