**[Judges 2:1-5](/bible/judges/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/2#v1)</sup> And an angel of the Lord came up from Gilgal to Bochim, and said, I made you to go up out of Egypt, and have brought you unto the land which I sware unto your fathers; and I said, I will never break my covenant with you.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/2#v2)</sup> And ye shall make no league with the inhabitants of this land; ye shall throw down their altars: but ye have not obeyed my voice: why have ye done this?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/2#v3)</sup> Wherefore I also said, I will not drive them out from before you; but they shall be as thorns in your sides, and their gods shall be a snare unto you.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/2#v4)</sup> And it came to pass, when the angel of the Lord spake these words unto all the children of Israel, that the people lifted up their voice, and wept.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/2#v5)</sup> And they called the name of that place Bochim: and they sacrificed there unto the Lord.

- An angel of the Most High comes up from Gilgal to Bochim and rehearses the covenant: I made you to go up out of Egypt, I brought you unto the land I sware unto your fathers, and I said I will never break my covenant with you. The command was plain: ye shall make no league with the inhabitants of this land, ye shall throw down their altars. But ye have not obeyed my voice. Why have ye done this?
- The same is true today in the places where we are scattered: we have made leagues with the inhabitants of the land. We find ourselves in their sports leagues and bowling leagues, at their dinner parties, when the scripture says envy not the oppressor and choose none of his ways.
- The Most High warned against letting them dwell in the land because they would make us sin against him. Dwelling among them breeds curiosity after their gods: how did these nations serve their gods, what is that thing they are doing with that tree, what are they hanging on the tree, why are they giving each other gifts? That curiosity is the snare, and we are not to add to his commandments or take away from them.
- Throwing down their altars meant burning their graven images with fire, not even desiring the silver or gold on them, lest it become a snare. The Most High keeps reiterating that we will be snared and trapped by serving what these nations serve.
- Because Israel disobeyed, the sentence falls: I will not drive them out from before you, but they shall be as thorns in your sides, and their gods shall be a snare unto you. The people lifted up their voice and wept, called the place Bochim, and sacrificed there unto the Most High.

>

>

- That whole generation is gathered unto their fathers, and there arises another generation which knew not the Most High, nor the works he had done for Israel, and the children of Israel do evil and serve Baalim and Ashtaroth.
- Here a pattern starts to develop: when there is a judge in Israel and he dies, the people just go off. It shows we are a people that need leadership; that is why we are told to trust in the Most High and lean not to our own understanding, and he shall direct our paths.
- We see the same today. Brothers and sisters come with theories they think are their own thoughts, but those thoughts were given to them, mostly by the television. You are either going to follow the one true living God of Israel or the prince of the power of the air, Satan the devil.

**[Judges 2:14-15](/bible/judges/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/2#v14)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was hot against Israel, and he delivered them into the hands of spoilers that spoiled them, and he sold them into the hands of their enemies round about, so that they could not any longer stand before their enemies.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/2#v15)</sup> Whithersoever they went out, the hand of the Lord was against them for evil, as the Lord had said, and as the Lord had sworn unto them: and they were greatly distressed.

- Because Israel forsook the Most High, his anger was hot against them; he delivered them into the hands of spoilers and sold them into the hands of their enemies, so that they could not any longer stand before their enemies. Look at us today: Black Lives Matter, hands up don't shoot. We cannot stand before our enemies, so now we beg our enemies to see us as people, to see us as human.
- Our offence is leaving off the commandments to serve other gods, and the Most High has gone and returned to his place until we acknowledge that offence; in our affliction we will seek him early. Judges is the record of that affliction beginning to come upon our people for turning our backs on the one true living God.
- Whithersoever they went, the hand of the Most High was against them for evil, and they were greatly distressed. Is that not true today? We wonder why we cannot get anywhere as a people; we have individual millionaires, yet no infrastructure, no farms enough to set up our own stores and feed ourselves, no distribution, no hospitals, no textiles, none of the things a nation needs to sustain a nation.
- The curse was to be cursed coming in and cursed going out, coming in from war and going out to war, and the same is happening to this very day. Our people do not think we are at war, but when the enemy gives you a false image, a false religion and a false education, slums and ghettos to live in, and bad foods, he is at war with you.

- We are the apple of his eye; he that toucheth Israel toucheth the apple of his eye. The Most High does not want to see us on the bottom, but we trespassed against his commandments, so we have to be punished for it.
- When the judge was dead, they returned and corrupted themselves more than their fathers, ceasing not from their own doings nor from their stubborn way. As soon as we got out of the condition we were in, we went right back to doing the same thing again, because this is a rebellious and stubborn people, even to this very day.

**[Judges 2:20-23](/bible/judges/2#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/2#v20)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was hot against Israel; and he said, Because that this people hath transgressed my covenant which I commanded their fathers, and have not hearkened unto my voice;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/judges/2#v21)</sup> I also will not henceforth drive out any from before them of the nations which Joshua left when he died:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/judges/2#v22)</sup> That through them I may prove Israel, whether they will keep the way of the Lord to walk therein, as their fathers did keep it, or not.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/judges/2#v23)</sup> Therefore the Lord left those nations, without driving them out hastily; neither delivered he them into the hand of Joshua.

- Because this people transgressed the covenant and did not hearken, the Most High declares he will not henceforth drive out any of the nations which Joshua left when he died. This is exactly what Joshua warned before he died: if ye cleave unto the remnant of these nations and make marriages with them, know for a certainty the Most High will no more drive them out, but they shall be snares and traps, scourges in your sides and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off the good land.
- In the course of making leagues with the inhabitants, what else did we do? We made marriages with them, the very thing the warning named.
- The nations were left without being driven out hastily, that through them the Most High might prove Israel, whether they would keep his way to walk therein as their fathers did, or not.
