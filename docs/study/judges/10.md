**[Judges 10:1-2](/bible/judges/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/10#v1)</sup> And after Abimelech there arose to defend Israel Tola the son of Puah, the son of Dodo, a man of Issachar; and he dwelt in Shamir in mount Ephraim.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/10#v2)</sup> And he judged Israel twenty and three years, and died, and was buried in Shamir.

- After Abimelech there arose to defend Israel Tola the son of Puah, a man of Issachar; he judged Israel twenty and three years, and died, and was buried in Shamir.
- Notice it says to defend Israel. Israel was in the land, but do not forget that the Lord had left nations among us, so the judges had to defend the people.

  Precepts:
  - **[Judges 3:1-4](/bible/judges/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/judges/3#v1)</sup> Now these are the nations which the Lord left, to prove Israel by them, even as many of Israel as had not known all the wars of Canaan;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/judges/3#v2)</sup> Only that the generations of the children of Israel might know, to teach them war, at the least such as before knew nothing thereof;
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/judges/3#v3)</sup> Namely, five lords of the Philistines, and all the Canaanites, and the Sidonians, and the Hivites that dwelt in mount Lebanon, from mount Baal–hermon unto the entering in of Hamath.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/judges/3#v4)</sup> And they were to prove Israel by them, to know whether they would hearken unto the commandments of the Lord, which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

    The Lord left nations to prove Israel, that the generations which had not known all the wars of Canaan might be taught war, and to know whether Israel would hearken unto the commandments which he commanded their fathers by the hand of Moses.

**[Judges 10:3-5](/bible/judges/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/10#v3)</sup> And after him arose Jair, a Gileadite, and judged Israel twenty and two years.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/10#v4)</sup> And he had thirty sons that rode on thirty ass colts, and they had thirty cities, which are called Havoth–jair unto this day, which are in the land of Gilead.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/10#v5)</sup> And Jair died, and was buried in Camon.

- After him arose Jair, a Gileadite, and judged Israel twenty and two years. He had thirty sons that rode on thirty ass colts, and they had thirty cities in the land of Gilead, which are called Havoth-jair unto this day; and Jair died and was buried in Camon.

**[Judges 10:6](/bible/judges/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/10#v6)</sup> And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Lord, and served Baalim, and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, and the gods of Zidon, and the gods of Moab, and the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines, and forsook the Lord, and served not him.

- The children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the LORD. That is the cycle of this whole book: after a judge died and Israel had peace and safety for a while, the people went right back into their sin, just like always.
- Israel served Baalim and Ashtaroth, and the gods of Syria, the gods of Zidon, the gods of Moab, the gods of the children of Ammon, and the gods of the Philistines, and forsook the LORD, and served not him. Ashtaroth is Ishtar or Astarte, the so called queen of heaven; the heathen carried the same goddess under different names, the same one the Greeks called Aphrodite.

**[Judges 10:7-9](/bible/judges/10#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/10#v7)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hands of the Philistines, and into the hands of the children of Ammon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/10#v8)</sup> And that year they vexed and oppressed the children of Israel: eighteen years, all the children of Israel that were on the other side Jordan in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/10#v9)</sup> Moreover the children of Ammon passed over Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim; so that Israel was sore distressed.

- The anger of the LORD was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hands of the Philistines and into the hands of the children of Ammon. Israel was serving their gods, so the Lord sold Israel to them.
- Eighteen years they vexed and oppressed all the children of Israel that were on the other side Jordan, in the land of the Amorites, which is in Gilead: that was Reuben, Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh in their allotments across the river.
- Moreover the children of Ammon passed over Jordan to fight also against Judah, and against Benjamin, and against the house of Ephraim, which shows those tribes dwelling in their own allotments on the other side; so that Israel was sore distressed.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:32](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v32)</sup> Thy sons and thy daughters shall be given unto another people, and thine eyes shall look, and fail with longing for them all the day long: and there shall be no might in thine hand.

    One of the curses for breaking the laws: thy sons and thy daughters shall be given unto another people, and there shall be no might in thine hand.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:41](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v41)</sup> Thou shalt beget sons and daughters, but thou shalt not enjoy them; for they shall go into captivity.

    Thou shalt beget sons and daughters, but thou shalt not enjoy them, for they shall go into captivity; the curse of the law was already working on Israel in the land.

**[Judges 10:10-14](/bible/judges/10#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/10#v10)</sup> And the children of Israel cried unto the Lord, saying, We have sinned against thee, both because we have forsaken our God, and also served Baalim.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/10#v11)</sup> And the Lord said unto the children of Israel, Did not I deliver you from the Egyptians, and from the Amorites, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/10#v12)</sup> The Zidonians also, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites, did oppress you; and ye cried to me, and I delivered you out of their hand.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/10#v13)</sup> Yet ye have forsaken me, and served other gods: wherefore I will deliver you no more.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/10#v14)</sup> Go and cry unto the gods which ye have chosen; let them deliver you in the time of your tribulation.

- The children of Israel cried unto the LORD, saying, We have sinned against thee, both because we have forsaken our God, and also served Baalim. The enemy had destroyed on one side of Jordan and was crossing over against Judah, Benjamin, and Ephraim, so now Israel wanted to confess.
- The Lord answered by rehearsing the deliverances: did not I deliver you from the Egyptians, and from the Amorites, from the children of Ammon, and from the Philistines? the Zidonians also, and the Amalekites, and the Maonites did oppress you, and ye cried to me, and I delivered you out of their hand. All through the early chapters of this book he kept delivering us over and over.
- Yet ye have forsaken me, and served other gods: wherefore I will deliver you no more. Go and cry unto the gods which ye have chosen; let them deliver you in the time of your tribulation. The Lord is not unrighteous; he gave a fair covenant: do right and he is with you above all nations, do wrong and you go into captivity. He will not break his word, and everybody's grace is not the same.

  Precepts:
  - **[Nehemiah 9:26-28](/bible/nehemiah/9#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/nehemiah/9#v26)</sup> Nevertheless they were disobedient, and rebelled against thee, and cast thy law behind their backs, and slew thy prophets which testified against them to turn them to thee, and they wrought great provocations.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/nehemiah/9#v27)</sup> Therefore thou deliveredst them into the hand of their enemies, who vexed them: and in the time of their trouble, when they cried unto thee, thou heardest them from heaven; and according to thy manifold mercies thou gavest them saviours, who saved them out of the hand of their enemies.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/nehemiah/9#v28)</sup> But after they had rest, they did evil again before thee: therefore leftest thou them in the hand of their enemies, so that they had the dominion over them: yet when they returned, and cried unto thee, thou heardest them from heaven; and many times didst thou deliver them according to thy mercies;

    The whole cycle of the book of Judges in one passage: Israel was disobedient, cast thy law behind their backs, and slew the prophets; the Lord delivered them into the hand of their enemies; in the time of their trouble they cried, and he heard from heaven and gave them saviours who saved them; and after they had rest, they did evil again before thee.

**[Judges 10:15-16](/bible/judges/10#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/10#v15)</sup> And the children of Israel said unto the Lord, We have sinned: do thou unto us whatsoever seemeth good unto thee; deliver us only, we pray thee, this day.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/10#v16)</sup> And they put away the strange gods from among them, and served the Lord: and his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel.

- The children of Israel said unto the LORD, We have sinned: do thou unto us whatsoever seemeth good unto thee; deliver us only, we pray thee, this day. And they put away the strange gods from among them and served the LORD. This time the repentance came with action.
- And his soul was grieved for the misery of Israel. The Most High loves Israel; it grieved him to have his own children in captivity, but he cannot break his word, so the repentance had to come first. That is also why Christ was sent to die for our sins, so we could have a chance to return to him in the last days.

**[Judges 10:17-18](/bible/judges/10#v17)**

>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/10#v18)</sup> And the people and princes of Gilead said one to another, What man is he that will begin to fight against the children of Ammon? he shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead.

- The children of Ammon were gathered together and encamped in Gilead, and the children of Israel assembled themselves together and encamped in Mizpeh.
- The people and princes of Gilead said one to another, What man is he that will begin to fight against the children of Ammon? he shall be head over all the inhabitants of Gilead. That question is answered in chapter 11.
