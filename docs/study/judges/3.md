**[Judges 3:5-7](/bible/judges/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/3#v5)</sup> And the children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, and Amorites, and Perizzites, and Hivites, and Jebusites:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/3#v6)</sup> And they took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/3#v7)</sup> And the children of Israel did evil in the sight of the Lord, and forgat the Lord their God, and served Baalim and the groves.

- The children of Israel dwelt among the Canaanites, Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites. Stop: are those not the same people the Most High said to utterly destroy? But we are dwelling among them.
- They took their daughters to be their wives, and gave their daughters to their sons, and served their gods. The law had said it plainly: thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them, neither shalt thou make marriages with them, for they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve other gods.
- The law of war made a different stipulation for these people. For far off cities Israel was to proclaim peace first, and if they answered peace they became tributaries, just as we read the tribes putting the Canaanites to tribute. But of the cities of these people, the Hittites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, thou shalt save alive nothing that breatheth, total destruction, that they teach you not to do after all their abominations which they have done unto their gods.
- Going out to battle against horses and chariots and people more than thou, Israel was told, be not afraid of them, for the Most High is with thee. He constantly repeats these things because he knew we were going to be a stubborn and rebellious people, and he wants us to acknowledge him and only him. Instead Israel did evil, forgat the Most High, and served Baalim and the groves.

> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/3#v8)</sup> Therefore the anger of the Lord was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Chushan–rishathaim king of Mesopotamia: and the children of Israel served Chushan–rishathaim eight years.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/3#v9)</sup> And when the children of Israel cried unto the Lord, the Lord raised up a deliverer to the children of Israel, who delivered them, even Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb’s younger brother.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/3#v10)</sup> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he judged Israel, and went out to war: and the Lord delivered Chushan–rishathaim king of Mesopotamia into his hand; and his hand prevailed against Chushan–rishathaim.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/3#v11)</sup> And the land had rest forty years. And Othniel the son of Kenaz died.

- The anger of the Most High was hot against Israel, and he sold them into the hand of Chushan-rishathaim king of Mesopotamia, and Israel served him eight years. When they cried unto the Most High, he raised up a deliverer, Othniel the son of Kenaz, Caleb's younger brother.
- The spirit of the Most High came upon Othniel, and he judged Israel. But today our people say, you can't judge me.
- He judged Israel and went out to war, because the Lord is a man of war. That answers those who ask, when we are out on the streets teaching, where are your women. They are not at war; the Lord is a man of war.
- And for those who do not think we are at war now: though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh. It is a spiritual war, but we are still nonetheless at war, and the weapon of our warfare today is this Bible, mighty through God to the pulling down of strongholds, exposing all the lies on the earth.
- The Most High delivered Chushan-rishathaim into Othniel's hand, and the land had rest forty years, until Othniel the son of Kenaz died.

**[Judges 3:12-14](/bible/judges/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/3#v12)</sup> And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Lord: and the Lord strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel, because they had done evil in the sight of the Lord.
>

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/3#v14)</sup> So the children of Israel served Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.

- The children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Most High, and he strengthened Eglon the king of Moab against Israel because they had done evil.
- Wait a minute: it says the Most High strengthened him. In Joshua, the hearts of the peoples melted because they heard of the things the Most High did for us; now he is strengthening them against us for breaking his commandments.
- Eglon gathers the children of Ammon and Amalek, smites Israel, and possesses the city of palm trees, and the children of Israel serve Eglon the king of Moab eighteen years.
