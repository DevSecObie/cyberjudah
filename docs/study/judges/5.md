## Introduction

This session covers [Judges 5](/bible/judges/5) through 8: the song of Deborah and Barak after the victory over Sisera, the call of Gideon while Israel is wasted under Midian, the three hundred men by whom the Lord saves Israel, and Israel's return to Baal worship as soon as Gideon is dead. The class opens under the order of [1 Corinthians 14:34-35](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34), that the women keep silence in the assembly, for it is not permitted unto them to speak, but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law. The running thread is that everything Israel does for the Most High must come from a willing heart, and that the victory of battle stands not in the multitude of an host, but strength comes from heaven.

**[Judges 5:1-2](/bible/judges/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/5#v1)</sup> Then sang Deborah and Barak the son of Abinoam on that day, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/5#v2)</sup> Praise ye the Lord for the avenging of Israel, when the people willingly offered themselves.

- Deborah and Barak sing praise to the Lord for the avenging of Israel, when the people willingly offered themselves. That is what the Most High wants from us: a willing heart in all that we do, in the keeping of the commandments, in the keeping of the feast days, and in the praise of him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 35:22](/bible/exodus/35#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/35#v22)</sup> And they came, both men and women, as many as were willing hearted, and brought bracelets, and earrings, and rings, and tablets, all jewels of gold: and every man that offered offered an offering of gold unto the Lord.

    At the building of the tabernacle in the wilderness, both men and women as many as were willing hearted brought their gold unto the Lord; we have to have that same willing heart today.

- How do we willingly offer ourselves to the Lord in the twenty-first century? By giving up whatever presents itself as an obstacle between us and the Lord. It may be father, mother, husband, wife, or children; it may be drugs, money, or working on the sabbath. If you willingly offer yourself, you will not let any stumbling block keep you from the Lord.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 10:34-39](/bible/matthew/10#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/10#v34)</sup> Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/10#v35)</sup> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/10#v36)</sup> And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household.
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)</sup> He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me: and he that loveth son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/10#v38)</sup> And he that taketh not his cross, and followeth after me, is not worthy of me.
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/10#v39)</sup> He that findeth his life shall lose it: and he that loseth his life for my sake shall find it.

    Christ came not to send peace but a sword, to set a man at variance against his own household; he that loveth father or mother more than him is not worthy of him, and he that loseth his life for his sake shall find it.
  - **[Galatians 6:5](/bible/galatians/6#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/galatians/6#v5)</sup> For every man shall bear his own burden.

    Taking up your cross does not mean buying a cross from the pawn shop to wear around your neck; every man shall bear his own burden, enduring his own tests in this truth with prayer and fasting.

**[Judges 5:3-5](/bible/judges/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/5#v3)</sup> Hear, O ye kings; give ear, O ye princes; I, even I, will sing unto the Lord; I will sing praise to the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/5#v4)</sup> Lord, when thou wentest out of Seir, when thou marchedst out of the field of Edom, the earth trembled, and the heavens dropped, the clouds also dropped water.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/5#v5)</sup> The mountains melted from before the Lord, even that Sinai from before the Lord God of Israel.

- The Lord marching out of the field of Edom, the earth trembling, the heavens dropping, and the mountains melting from before the Lord God of Israel is future prophecy; this has not happened yet.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 63:1-4](/bible/isaiah/63#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/63#v1)</sup> Who is this that cometh from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah? this that is glorious in his apparel, travelling in the greatness of his strength? I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save.

    The one who cometh from Edom with dyed garments, treading the winepress alone in the day of vengeance, is the same picture the song is singing of.

**[Judges 5:6-9](/bible/judges/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/5#v6)</sup> In the days of Shamgar the son of Anath, in the days of Jael, the highways were unoccupied, and the travellers walked through byways.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/5#v7)</sup> The inhabitants of the villages ceased, they ceased in Israel, until that I Deborah arose, that I arose a mother in Israel.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/5#v8)</sup> They chose new gods; then was war in the gates: was there a shield or spear seen among forty thousand in Israel?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/5#v9)</sup> My heart is toward the governors of Israel, that offered themselves willingly among the people. Bless ye the Lord.

- This passage is for all the closet feminists who think Deborah justifies women leading men. She did not lead men into any kind of war; she did not put on body armor and a breastplate, rise up on a horse, and lead the charge. She was the motivating factor to men that were shaky and fearful during that time, a mother in Israel, a guiding light.
- Verse 9 proves it: my heart is toward the governors of Israel, the leaders who offered themselves willingly among the people. Her heart was with the men who led; she was not ruling over them.

  Precepts:

    > <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/27#v16)</sup> Let the Lord, the God of the spirits of all flesh, set a man over the congregation,

    > <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/27#v17)</sup> Which may go out before them, and which may go in before them, and which may lead them out, and which may bring them in; that the congregation of the Lord be not as sheep which have no shepherd.

    Moses asked the Lord to set a man over the congregation, a man to go out before them and bring them in, that the congregation of the Lord be not as sheep which have no shepherd.
  - **[Numbers 27:18](/bible/numbers/27#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/27#v18)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and lay thine hand upon him;

    The Lord answered in total agreement with what Moses spoke: take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit; the Lord set a man over the congregation.
  - **[1 Timothy 2:11-12](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)</sup> Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/2#v12)</sup> But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man, but to be in silence.

    The order did not change in the New Testament: the woman is to learn in silence with all subjection, not to teach nor to usurp authority over the man.

**[Judges 5:10-11](/bible/judges/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/5#v10)</sup> Speak, ye that ride on white asses, ye that sit in judgment, and walk by the way.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/5#v11)</sup> They that are delivered from the noise of archers in the places of drawing water, there shall they rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, even the righteous acts toward the inhabitants of his villages in Israel: then shall the people of the Lord go down to the gates.

- They that are delivered from the noise of archers in the places of drawing water shall rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord. That is where we are right now: in the places of drawing water, rehearsing the righteous acts of the Most High while the arrows are still being shot.

  Precepts:
  - **[Baruch 3:8](/bible/baruch/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/baruch/3#v8)</sup> Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity, where thou hast scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to payments, according to all the iniquities of our fathers, which departed from the Lord our God.

    We are yet this day in our captivity, subject unto payments for the iniquities of our fathers; we have to obey the laws of the land under the hand of our oppressor, endure, and exercise faith in the keeping of the commandments until Christ redeems us.

**[Judges 5:12-18](/bible/judges/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/5#v12)</sup> Awake, awake, Deborah: awake, awake, utter a song: arise, Barak, and lead thy captivity captive, thou son of Abinoam.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/5#v13)</sup> Then he made him that remaineth have dominion over the nobles among the people: the Lord made me have dominion over the mighty.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/5#v14)</sup> Out of Ephraim was there a root of them against Amalek; after thee, Benjamin, among thy people; out of Machir came down governors, and out of Zebulun they that handle the pen of the writer.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/5#v15)</sup> And the princes of Issachar were with Deborah; even Issachar, and also Barak: he was sent on foot into the valley. For the divisions of Reuben there were great thoughts of heart.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/5#v16)</sup> Why abodest thou among the sheepfolds, to hear the bleatings of the flocks? For the divisions of Reuben there were great searchings of heart.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/5#v17)</sup> Gilead abode beyond Jordan: and why did Dan remain in ships? Asher continued on the sea shore, and abode in his breaches.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/5#v18)</sup> Zebulun and Naphtali were a people that jeoparded their lives unto the death in the high places of the field.

- The song rehearses which tribes came out. Out of Ephraim, Benjamin, Machir, and Zebulun came governors and they that handle the pen of the writer, and the princes of Issachar were with Deborah and Barak in the valley.
- For the divisions of Reuben there were great thoughts and great searchings of heart: they were second-guessing themselves. Gilead abode beyond Jordan, Dan remained in ships, and Asher continued on the seashore; the song is getting on the tribes that stayed home.
- Zebulun and Naphtali were a people that jeoparded their lives unto the death in the high places of the field; those tribes are warriors.

**[Judges 5:19-23](/bible/judges/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/5#v19)</sup> The kings came and fought, then fought the kings of Canaan in Taanach by the waters of Megiddo; they took no gain of money.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/5#v20)</sup> They fought from heaven; the stars in their courses fought against Sisera.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/judges/5#v21)</sup> The river of Kishon swept them away, that ancient river, the river Kishon. O my soul, thou hast trodden down strength.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/judges/5#v22)</sup> Then were the horsehoofs broken by the means of the pransings, the pransings of their mighty ones.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/judges/5#v23)</sup> Curse ye Meroz, said the angel of the Lord, curse ye bitterly the inhabitants thereof; because they came not to the help of the Lord, to the help of the Lord against the mighty.

- The kings of Canaan fought by the waters of Megiddo and took no gain of money; they fought from heaven, and the stars in their courses fought against Sisera. Sisera is the general of the Canaanite host, not Syria; the river Kishon swept them away.
- The angel of the Lord said curse ye Meroz, curse ye bitterly the inhabitants thereof, because they came not to the help of the Lord against the mighty. Sitting out the battle of the Lord brings a curse.

> <sup>[25](/bible/judges/5#v25)</sup> He asked water, and she gave him milk; she brought forth butter in a lordly dish.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/judges/5#v26)</sup> She put her hand to the nail, and her right hand to the workmen’s hammer; and with the hammer she smote Sisera, she smote off his head, when she had pierced and stricken through his temples.

    The account itself: Jael covered him with a mantle, gave him milk when he asked water, and when he was fast asleep and weary she smote the nail into his temples and fastened it into the ground; when Barak came, Sisera lay dead with the nail in his temples.

**[Judges 5:28-31](/bible/judges/5#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/judges/5#v28)</sup> The mother of Sisera looked out at a window, and cried through the lattice, Why is his chariot so long in coming? why tarry the wheels of his chariots?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/judges/5#v29)</sup> Her wise ladies answered her, yea, she returned answer to herself,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/judges/5#v30)</sup> Have they not sped? have they not divided the prey; to every man a damsel or two; to Sisera a prey of divers colours, a prey of divers colours of needlework, of divers colours of needlework on both sides, meet for the necks of them that take the spoil?
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/judges/5#v31)</sup> So let all thine enemies perish, O Lord: but let them that love him be as the sun when he goeth forth in his might. And the land had rest forty years.

- The mother of Sisera looked out at a window and cried, why is his chariot so long in coming? Her wise ladies answer her with imaginations of victory, that they are dividing the prey and the needlework spoil. She is looking for a son who is never coming home; he lay dead in Jael's tent.
- So let all thine enemies perish, O Lord: but let them that love him be as the sun when he goeth forth in his might. And the land had rest forty years.
