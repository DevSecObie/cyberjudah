**[Judges 15:1-3](/bible/judges/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/15#v1)</sup> But it came to pass within a while after, in the time of wheat harvest, that Samson visited his wife with a kid; and he said, I will go in to my wife into the chamber. But her father would not suffer him to go in.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/15#v2)</sup> And her father said, I verily thought that thou hadst utterly hated her; therefore I gave her to thy companion: is not her younger sister fairer than she? take her, I pray thee, instead of her.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/15#v3)</sup> And Samson said concerning them, Now shall I be more blameless than the Philistines, though I do them a displeasure.

- Within a while, in the time of wheat harvest, Samson visited his wife with a kid and said, I will go in to my wife into the chamber. He knew she had betrayed him and still went back to her; a weakness for a woman really does beat on Israelite men.
- Her father would not suffer him to go in, saying, I verily thought that thou hadst utterly hated her, therefore I gave her to thy companion; is not her younger sister fairer than she? take her, I pray thee, instead of her.
- Samson said concerning them, now shall I be more blameless than the Philistines, though I do them a displeasure; he was set to avenge himself.

**[Judges 15:4-5](/bible/judges/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/15#v4)</sup> And Samson went and caught three hundred foxes, and took firebrands, and turned tail to tail, and put a firebrand in the midst between two tails.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/15#v5)</sup> And when he had set the brands on fire, he let them go into the standing corn of the Philistines, and burnt up both the shocks, and also the standing corn, with the vineyards and olives.

- Samson went and caught three hundred foxes by himself, took firebrands, turned the foxes tail to tail, and put a firebrand in the midst between two tails.
- When he had set the brands on fire, he let them go into the standing corn of the Philistines and burnt up both the shocks and the standing corn, with the vineyards and olives; their food source and their wine source, all of it, because they crossed him.

**[Judges 15:6](/bible/judges/15#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/15#v6)</sup> Then the Philistines said, Who hath done this? And they answered, Samson, the son in law of the Timnite, because he had taken his wife, and given her to his companion. And the Philistines came up, and burnt her and her father with fire.

- The Philistines asked who had done this, and were told, Samson, the son in law of the Timnite, because he had taken his wife and given her to his companion.
- The Philistines came up and burnt her and her father with fire; the very thing she betrayed her husband to escape came upon her from her own people.

**[Judges 15:7-8](/bible/judges/15#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/15#v7)</sup> And Samson said unto them, Though ye have done this, yet will I be avenged of you, and after that I will cease.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/15#v8)</sup> And he smote them hip and thigh with a great slaughter: and he went down and dwelt in the top of the rock Etam.

- Samson said unto them, though ye have done this, yet will I be avenged of you, and after that I will cease; he would get the last word.
- He smote them hip and thigh with a great slaughter, dropping them like flies, and went down and dwelt in the top of the rock Etam.

**[Judges 15:9-13](/bible/judges/15#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/15#v9)</sup> Then the Philistines went up, and pitched in Judah, and spread themselves in Lehi.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/15#v10)</sup> And the men of Judah said, Why are ye come up against us? And they answered, To bind Samson are we come up, to do to him as he hath done to us.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/15#v11)</sup> Then three thousand men of Judah went to the top of the rock Etam, and said to Samson, Knowest thou not that the Philistines are rulers over us? what is this that thou hast done unto us? And he said unto them, As they did unto me, so have I done unto them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/15#v12)</sup> And they said unto him, We are come down to bind thee, that we may deliver thee into the hand of the Philistines. And Samson said unto them, Swear unto me, that ye will not fall upon me yourselves.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/15#v13)</sup> And they spake unto him, saying, No; but we will bind thee fast, and deliver thee into their hand: but surely we will not kill thee. And they bound him with two new cords, and brought him up from the rock.

- The Philistines went up and pitched in Judah and spread themselves in Lehi, and the men of Judah asked, why are ye come up against us? We are already under tribute; what is the problem? They answered, to bind Samson are we come up, to do to him as he hath done to us. Samson was not even of Judah; he was a Danite.
- Three thousand men of Judah went to the top of the rock Etam and said to Samson, knowest thou not that the Philistines are rulers over us? what is this that thou hast done unto us? Instead of standing with their brother, the whole tribe came to hand him over to the oppressor.
- He answered, as they did unto me, so have I done unto them. He let them bind him, only making them swear that they would not fall upon him themselves, and they bound him with two new cords and brought him up from the rock.

**[Judges 15:14-15](/bible/judges/15#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/15#v14)</sup> And when he came unto Lehi, the Philistines shouted against him: and the Spirit of the Lord came mightily upon him, and the cords that were upon his arms became as flax that was burnt with fire, and his bands loosed from off his hands.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/15#v15)</sup> And he found a new jawbone of an ass, and put forth his hand, and took it, and slew a thousand men therewith.

- When he came unto Lehi, the Philistines shouted against him, and the spirit of the Lord came mightily upon him: the cords that were upon his arms became as flax that was burnt with fire, and his bands loosed from off his hands.
- He found a new jawbone of an ass, put forth his hand and took it, and slew a thousand men therewith. This is not normal; you have not seen this in any comic book. One man against a thousand, and all he had was the jawbone of a donkey, and he laid all of them down.
- This is the superpower class of Israel's history, like walking with the giants; Israel really was nothing to play with when the Most High was dealing with us, and the nations knew it.

**[Judges 15:16-17](/bible/judges/15#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/15#v16)</sup> And Samson said, With the jawbone of an ass, heaps upon heaps, with the jaw of an ass have I slain a thousand men.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/15#v17)</sup> And it came to pass, when he had made an end of speaking, that he cast away the jawbone out of his hand, and called that place Ramath–lehi.

- Samson said, with the jawbone of an ass, heaps upon heaps, with the jaw of an ass have I slain a thousand men.
- When he had made an end of speaking, he cast away the jawbone out of his hand and called that place Ramath-lehi.

**[Judges 15:18-19](/bible/judges/15#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/15#v18)</sup> And he was sore athirst, and called on the Lord, and said, Thou hast given this great deliverance into the hand of thy servant: and now shall I die for thirst, and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/15#v19)</sup> But God clave an hollow place that was in the jaw, and there came water thereout; and when he had drunk, his spirit came again, and he revived: wherefore he called the name thereof En–hakkore, which is in Lehi unto this day.

- He was sore athirst and called on the Lord: thou hast given this great deliverance into the hand of thy servant, and now shall I die for thirst, and fall into the hand of the uncircumcised? You kill a thousand men with a jawbone, you are going to be parched.
- God clave an hollow place that was in the jaw, and there came water thereout; and when he had drunk, his spirit came again, and he revived. It is the same as after a fast, when the first food and drink make you feel like a whole new person.
- He called the name of that place En-hakkore, which is in Lehi unto this day.

**[Judges 15:20](/bible/judges/15#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/15#v20)</sup> And he judged Israel in the days of the Philistines twenty years.

- Samson judged Israel in the days of the Philistines twenty years; he ended up a leadership figure, a judge over Israel.
