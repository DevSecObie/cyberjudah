## Introduction

This session covers the whole history of Samson, one of the most famous forefathers and one of the most illustrated stories in the Bible: his birth announced by the angel of the Lord in chapter 13, his marriage among the Philistines and the riddle in chapter 14, the jawbone of an ass in chapter 15, and Delilah and his death in chapter 16. In the book of Judges, after Joshua led Israel into the promised land and died, Israel immediately started going off; before the major captivities of Assyria and Babylon came about, Israel was oppressed by the Moabites, the Philistines, and other nations round about. Here Israel is under the hand of the Philistines, and the Most High raises up a deliverer from the tribe of Dan.

**[Judges 13:1](/bible/judges/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/13#v1)</sup> And the children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Lord; and the Lord delivered them into the hand of the Philistines forty years.

- The children of Israel did evil again in the sight of the Lord, and the Lord delivered them into the hand of the Philistines forty years. This is the pattern of the book of Judges: after Joshua's death Israel went off, and nation after nation was given power over them before the great captivities ever came.

**[Judges 13:2](/bible/judges/13#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/13#v2)</sup> And there was a certain man of Zorah, of the family of the Danites, whose name was Manoah; and his wife was barren, and bare not.

- There was a certain man of Zorah, of the family of the Danites, whose name was Manoah. He was married, but his wife was barren; she was not able to have any children.

**[Judges 13:3](/bible/judges/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/13#v3)</sup> And the angel of the Lord appeared unto the woman, and said unto her, Behold now, thou art barren, and bearest not: but thou shalt conceive, and bear a son.

- The angel of the Lord appeared unto the woman and told her plainly: you are barren now, but you shall conceive and bear a son, a man child.
- This is one of many examples in the Bible of our foremothers going through barrenness, and when the Most High was ready he enabled them to conceive. Sisters who have tried for years without children should in patience possess their souls, take comfort of the scriptures, meditate on the foremothers, and believe what the Bible says.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 113:9](/bible/psalms/113#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/113#v9)</sup> He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise ye the Lord.

    The Most High maketh the barren woman to keep house and to be a joyful mother of children.

**[Judges 13:4-5](/bible/judges/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/13#v4)</sup> Now therefore beware, I pray thee, and drink not wine nor strong drink, and eat not any unclean thing:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/13#v5)</sup> For, lo, thou shalt conceive, and bear a son; and no razor shall come on his head: for the child shall be a Nazarite unto God from the womb: and he shall begin to deliver Israel out of the hand of the Philistines.

- The angel commanded the mother herself to drink no wine nor strong drink and to eat no unclean thing, because the child would be a Nazarite unto God from the womb; even in the womb he was to be kept pure.
- No razor was to come on his head, for the child would be a Nazarite unto God from the womb, and he would begin to deliver Israel out of the hand of the Philistines. This is one of the first examples in scripture of somebody actually living the vow that was laid out in [Numbers 6](/bible/numbers/6).
- Understand the difference between a Nazarite and a Nazarene. Christ was a Nazarene because he was from the city of Nazareth, not a Nazarite; he drank wine, and Nazarites could not drink the blood of the grape at all.
- The locks of the Nazarite also witness to who Israel is; the hair of Israel naturally grows into locks.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 6:1-4](/bible/numbers/6#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/6#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/6#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When either man or woman shall separate themselves to vow a vow of a Nazarite, to separate themselves unto the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/6#v3)</sup> He shall separate himself from wine and strong drink, and shall drink no vinegar of wine, or vinegar of strong drink, neither shall he drink any liquor of grapes, nor eat moist grapes, or dried.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/6#v4)</sup> All the days of his separation shall he eat nothing that is made of the vine tree, from the kernels even to the husk.

    When a man or woman separates themselves to vow a vow of a Nazarite, they are on another level of dedication to the Lord: no wine, no strong drink, no vinegar, no liquor of grapes, nothing made of the vine tree from the kernels even to the husk.
  - **[Numbers 6:5](/bible/numbers/6#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/6#v5)</sup> All the days of the vow of his separation there shall no razor come upon his head: until the days be fulfilled, in the which he separateth himself unto the Lord, he shall be holy, and shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow.

    All the days of the vow no razor shall come upon his head; he shall be holy and shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow, which is why the angel gave Manoah's wife these instructions.

**[Judges 13:6-7](/bible/judges/13#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/13#v6)</sup> Then the woman came and told her husband, saying, A man of God came unto me, and his countenance was like the countenance of an angel of God, very terrible: but I asked him not whence he was, neither told he me his name:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/13#v7)</sup> But he said unto me, Behold, thou shalt conceive, and bear a son; and now drink no wine nor strong drink, neither eat any unclean thing: for the child shall be a Nazarite to God from the womb to the day of his death.

- The woman came and told her husband that a man of God came unto her whose countenance was like the countenance of an angel of God, very terrible. In verse 3 the scripture says it was the angel of the Lord, but he did not tell her, so she was not sure; she only knew his face was terrible like an angel's.
- The popular pictures of angels as cute babies with smooth cheeks and little white wings do not match the biblical descriptions at all. To see an angel of the Most High was a terrifying sight, not something to jump for joy about.
- She repeated the message: the child shall be a Nazarite to God from the womb to the day of his death, and she was to drink no wine nor strong drink nor eat any unclean thing.

> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/13#v8)</sup> Then Manoah intreated the Lord, and said, O my Lord, let the man of God which thou didst send come again unto us, and teach us what we shall do unto the child that shall be born.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/13#v9)</sup> And God hearkened to the voice of Manoah; and the angel of God came again unto the woman as she sat in the field: but Manoah her husband was not with her.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/13#v10)</sup> And the woman made haste, and ran, and shewed her husband, and said unto him, Behold, the man hath appeared unto me, that came unto me the other day.

- The Most High hearkened to the voice of Manoah, and the angel of God came again unto the woman as she sat in the field; she made haste and ran and showed her husband that the man was back.

**[Judges 13:11-14](/bible/judges/13#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/13#v11)</sup> And Manoah arose, and went after his wife, and came to the man, and said unto him, Art thou the man that spakest unto the woman? And he said, I am.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/13#v12)</sup> And Manoah said, Now let thy words come to pass. How shall we order the child, and how shall we do unto him?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/13#v13)</sup> And the angel of the Lord said unto Manoah, Of all that I said unto the woman let her beware.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/13#v14)</sup> She may not eat of any thing that cometh of the vine, neither let her drink wine or strong drink, nor eat any unclean thing: all that I commanded her let her observe.

- Manoah arose and went after his wife, came to the man, and asked, art thou the man that spakest unto the woman? He said, I am.
- Manoah asked how they should order the child and what they should do unto him, and the angel reiterated the instructions he had already given: of all that I said unto the woman let her beware, and all that I commanded her let her observe while she carries this son.

**[Judges 13:15-16](/bible/judges/13#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/13#v15)</sup> And Manoah said unto the angel of the Lord, I pray thee, let us detain thee, until we shall have made ready a kid for thee.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/13#v16)</sup> And the angel of the Lord said unto Manoah, Though thou detain me, I will not eat of thy bread: and if thou wilt offer a burnt offering, thou must offer it unto the Lord. For Manoah knew not that he was an angel of the Lord.

- Manoah tried to detain the angel and make ready a kid for him, but the angel refused: though thou detain me, I will not eat of thy bread, and if thou wilt offer a burnt offering, thou must offer it unto the Lord.
- The angel knew Manoah might try to offer it up to him, since he appeared like a god, so he directed the offering to the Lord. Israel is quick to worship anything that looks like it could be God because we cannot see the Most High, which is why the scriptures warn against the worshipping of angels.
- Manoah still did not know that he was an angel of the Lord.

**[Judges 13:17-18](/bible/judges/13#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/13#v17)</sup> And Manoah said unto the angel of the Lord, What is thy name, that when thy sayings come to pass we may do thee honour?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/13#v18)</sup> And the angel of the Lord said unto him, Why askest thou thus after my name, seeing it is secret?

- Manoah asked the angel his name, so that when his sayings came to pass they might do him honour.
- The angel answered, why askest thou thus after my name, seeing it is secret? His name was too wonderful to be uttered to him.

**[Judges 13:19-21](/bible/judges/13#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/13#v19)</sup> So Manoah took a kid with a meat offering, and offered it upon a rock unto the Lord: and the angel did wondrously; and Manoah and his wife looked on.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/13#v20)</sup> For it came to pass, when the flame went up toward heaven from off the altar, that the angel of the Lord ascended in the flame of the altar. And Manoah and his wife looked on it, and fell on their faces to the ground.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/judges/13#v21)</sup> But the angel of the Lord did no more appear to Manoah and to his wife. Then Manoah knew that he was an angel of the Lord.

- Manoah took a kid with a meat offering and offered it upon a rock unto the Lord, and the angel did wondrously: when the flame went up toward heaven from off the altar, the angel of the Lord ascended in the flame and went out of their sight.
- Manoah and his wife fell on their faces to the ground at the sight. This is the kind of thing Israel was able to see and witness with their own eyes.
- The angel appeared no more, and then Manoah knew that he was an angel of the Lord.

**[Judges 13:22-23](/bible/judges/13#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/judges/13#v22)</sup> And Manoah said unto his wife, We shall surely die, because we have seen God.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/judges/13#v23)</sup> But his wife said unto him, If the Lord were pleased to kill us, he would not have received a burnt offering and a meat offering at our hands, neither would he have shewed us all these things, nor would as at this time have told us such things as these.

- Manoah said to his wife, we shall surely die, because we have seen God. This is a passage the contradictors point to, since the Bible says no man hath seen God; but God here is Elohim, which is plural and speaks of heavenly beings. They saw an angel, not the Most High God.
- No flesh can look upon the Most High God; he is far too fierce and terrible. The furthest anybody got was Moses seeing his back parts.
- His wife reasoned rightly: if the Lord were pleased to kill us, he would not have received a burnt offering and a meat offering at our hands, neither would he have shewed us all these things.
