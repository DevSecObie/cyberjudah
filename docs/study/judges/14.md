**[Judges 14:1-3](/bible/judges/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/14#v1)</sup> And Samson went down to Timnath, and saw a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/14#v2)</sup> And he came up, and told his father and his mother, and said, I have seen a woman in Timnath of the daughters of the Philistines: now therefore get her for me to wife.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/14#v3)</sup> Then his father and his mother said unto him, Is there never a woman among the daughters of thy brethren, or among all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines? And Samson said unto his father, Get her for me; for she pleaseth me well.

- Samson went down to Timnath and saw a woman of the daughters of the Philistines, and told his father and mother, get her for me to wife. As he grew up this brother had a lust issue with heathen women; that is the infamy the story of Samson is known for.
- His parents answered him rightly: is there never a woman among the daughters of thy brethren, or among all my people, that thou goest to take a wife of the uncircumcised Philistines? There were twelve tribes; there was no shortage of women in all the coasts of Israel.
- Samson insisted, get her for me, for she pleaseth me well. The forefathers always charged their sons not to do this.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 28:1](/bible/genesis/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/28#v1)</sup> And Isaac called Jacob, and blessed him, and charged him, and said unto him, Thou shalt not take a wife of the daughters of Canaan.

    Isaac called Jacob, blessed him, and charged him: thou shalt not take a wife of the daughters of Canaan; the fathers commanded their sons to choose from their own people.

**[Judges 14:4](/bible/judges/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/14#v4)</sup> But his father and his mother knew not that it was of the Lord, that he sought an occasion against the Philistines: for at that time the Philistines had dominion over Israel.

- His father and his mother knew not that it was of the Lord, that he sought an occasion against the Philistines, for at that time the Philistines had dominion over Israel. The Lord was moving Samson's spirit, and the parents did not understand, just as we often question the Most High when we have no idea the depths of his wisdom and what he is really doing.
- The Most High was using Samson's flaw, his lust after Philistine women, as an occasion against the Philistines, to destroy them for oppressing his people. That is the basis of the whole story: the Lord was behind all of it, pulling the strings.
- The Most High is in control of everything, good and evil alike. Even affliction that comes on his people is his tactic to make them seek him and repent; nothing anybody does can alter the outcome he has already ordained, for he declared the end from the beginning.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 55:8-9](/bible/isaiah/55#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/55#v8)</sup> For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/55#v9)</sup> For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts.

    His thoughts are not our thoughts, neither are our ways his ways; his thoughts are higher than our thoughts.
  - **[Job 12:16](/bible/job/12#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/job/12#v16)</sup> With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his.

    With him is strength and wisdom; the deceived and the deceiver are his. The Most High used these heathen women, and later Delilah, to get Samson caught up with the Philistines so that he would end up destroying them.
  - **[Proverbs 21:30](/bible/proverbs/21#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/21#v30)</sup> There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the Lord.

    There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the Lord; everything ultimately works within his plan.
  - **[Amos 3:6](/bible/amos/3#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/amos/3#v6)</sup> Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the Lord hath not done it?

    Shall there be evil in a city, and the Lord hath not done it? The evil is wrong, but the Lord is behind it, working his purpose.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    In their affliction they will seek me early; affliction is how the Most High wakes his people up to repent.

**[Judges 14:5-6](/bible/judges/14#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/14#v5)</sup> Then went Samson down, and his father and his mother, to Timnath, and came to the vineyards of Timnath: and, behold, a young lion roared against him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/14#v6)</sup> And the Spirit of the Lord came mightily upon him, and he rent him as he would have rent a kid, and he had nothing in his hand: but he told not his father or his mother what he had done.

- As Samson went down to the vineyards of Timnath, a young lion roared against him, challenging him. Here you begin to see the divine power on Samson.
- The spirit of the Lord came mightily upon him, and he rent the lion as he would have rent a kid, and he had nothing in his hand: no weapon, no sword, nothing. He tore up a young lion, a prince of the jungle, like it was a little goat. This is one of the forms of the spirit of the Lord, spiritual power.
- He told not his father or his mother what he had done; he did not boast about it.

**[Judges 14:7-9](/bible/judges/14#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/14#v7)</sup> And he went down, and talked with the woman; and she pleased Samson well.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/14#v8)</sup> And after a time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcase of the lion: and, behold, there was a swarm of bees and honey in the carcase of the lion.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/14#v9)</sup> And he took thereof in his hands, and went on eating, and came to his father and mother, and he gave them, and they did eat: but he told not them that he had taken the honey out of the carcase of the lion.

- He went down and talked with the woman, and she pleased Samson well.
- After a time he returned to take her, and he turned aside to see the carcase of the lion he had slain, and behold, there was a swarm of bees and honey in the carcase.
- He took of the honey in his hands and went on eating, and gave to his father and mother and they did eat, but he told them not that he had taken the honey out of the carcase of the lion.

**[Judges 14:10-14](/bible/judges/14#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/14#v10)</sup> So his father went down unto the woman: and Samson made there a feast; for so used the young men to do.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/14#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, when they saw him, that they brought thirty companions to be with him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/14#v12)</sup> And Samson said unto them, I will now put forth a riddle unto you: if ye can certainly declare it me within the seven days of the feast, and find it out, then I will give you thirty sheets and thirty change of garments:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/14#v13)</sup> But if ye cannot declare it me, then shall ye give me thirty sheets and thirty change of garments. And they said unto him, Put forth thy riddle, that we may hear it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/14#v14)</sup> And he said unto them, Out of the eater came forth meat, and out of the strong came forth sweetness. And they could not in three days expound the riddle.

- His father went down unto the woman, and Samson made there a feast, as the young men used to do, and when the Philistines saw him they brought thirty companions to be with him.
- Samson put forth a riddle: if they could declare it within the seven days of the feast, he would give them thirty sheets and thirty change of garments, and if they could not, they would give him the same.
- The riddle: out of the eater came forth meat, and out of the strong came forth sweetness. In three days they could not expound it.

**[Judges 14:15-17](/bible/judges/14#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/14#v15)</sup> And it came to pass on the seventh day, that they said unto Samson’s wife, Entice thy husband, that he may declare unto us the riddle, lest we burn thee and thy father’s house with fire: have ye called us to take that we have? is it not so?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/14#v16)</sup> And Samson’s wife wept before him, and said, Thou dost but hate me, and lovest me not: thou hast put forth a riddle unto the children of my people, and hast not told it me. And he said unto her, Behold, I have not told it my father nor my mother, and shall I tell it thee?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/14#v17)</sup> And she wept before him the seven days, while their feast lasted: and it came to pass on the seventh day, that he told her, because she lay sore upon him: and she told the riddle to the children of her people.

- On the seventh day they said unto Samson's wife, entice thy husband that he may declare unto us the riddle, lest we burn thee and thy father's house with fire. They threatened to burn their own countrywoman in her father's house over thirty outfits.
- She turned to manipulation and wept before him: thou dost but hate me, and lovest me not; thou hast put forth a riddle unto the children of my people and hast not told it me. He answered, I have not told it my father nor my mother, and shall I tell it thee?
- She wept before him the seven days while their feast lasted, and on the seventh day he told her, because she lay sore upon him. This big strong mighty brother caved in because of the tears of a woman.

**[Judges 14:18](/bible/judges/14#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/14#v18)</sup> And the men of the city said unto him on the seventh day before the sun went down, What is sweeter than honey? and what is stronger than a lion? And he said unto them, If ye had not plowed with my heifer, ye had not found out my riddle.

- She told the riddle to the children of her people, and the men of the city answered him before the sun went down: what is sweeter than honey, and what is stronger than a lion?
- Samson knew exactly what happened: if ye had not plowed with my heifer, ye had not found out my riddle.
- Never trust your enemy. That was his wife, and her loyalty belonged to him, but when she got the riddle out of him she went and told it to her heathen people, his enemy. That is why it makes no sense to marry the enemy.

**[Judges 14:19-20](/bible/judges/14#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/14#v19)</sup> And the Spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon, and slew thirty men of them, and took their spoil, and gave change of garments unto them which expounded the riddle. And his anger was kindled, and he went up to his father’s house.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/14#v20)</sup> But Samson’s wife was given to his companion, whom he had used as his friend.

- The spirit of the Lord came upon him, and he went down to Ashkelon and slew thirty men of the Philistines, took their spoil, and gave the change of garments unto them which expounded the riddle; the spirit of the Lord came upon him even for this, against the nation that oppressed Israel.
- His anger was kindled, and he went up to his father's house, and Samson's wife was given to his companion, whom he had used as his friend; she was his wife to be, for they had not yet consummated the marriage.
