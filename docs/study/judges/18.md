**[Judges 18:1-2](/bible/judges/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/18#v1)</sup> In those days there was no king in Israel: and in those days the tribe of the Danites sought them an inheritance to dwell in; for unto that day all their inheritance had not fallen unto them among the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/18#v2)</sup> And the children of Dan sent of their family five men from their coasts, men of valour, from Zorah, and from Eshtaol, to spy out the land, and to search it; and they said unto them, Go, search the land: who when they came to mount Ephraim, to the house of Micah, they lodged there.

- Again the scripture reiterates there was no king in Israel. In those days the tribe of the Danites was still seeking an inheritance to dwell in, because unto that day all their inheritance had not fallen unto them among the tribes; they had conquered in Joshua's day but never attained all their land.
- The children of Dan sent five men of valour from Zorah and Eshtaol to spy out land to conquer, and on their way they came to mount Ephraim and lodged at the house of Micah.

  Precepts:
  - **[Joshua 19:47](/bible/joshua/19#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/joshua/19#v47)</sup> And the coast of the children of Dan went out too little for them: therefore the children of Dan went up to fight against Leshem, and took it, and smote it with the edge of the sword, and possessed it, and dwelt therein, and called Leshem, Dan, after the name of Dan their father.

    The coast of the children of Dan went out too little for them, so Dan went up to fight against Leshem and took it; this campaign against Laish is that history.

**[Judges 18:3-6](/bible/judges/18#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/18#v3)</sup> When they were by the house of Micah, they knew the voice of the young man the Levite: and they turned in thither, and said unto him, Who brought thee hither? and what makest thou in this place? and what hast thou here?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/18#v4)</sup> And he said unto them, Thus and thus dealeth Micah with me, and hath hired me, and I am his priest.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/18#v5)</sup> And they said unto him, Ask counsel, we pray thee, of God, that we may know whether our way which we go shall be prosperous.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/18#v6)</sup> And the priest said unto them, Go in peace: before the Lord is your way wherein ye go.

- By the house of Micah they knew the voice of the young man the Levite, and they turned in and questioned him: who brought thee hither? what makest thou in this place? A Levite serving in a house of idols raised questions.
- He told them plainly, Micah hired me and I am his priest. They asked him to ask counsel of God whether their way would be prosperous, and the priest answered, go in peace: before the LORD is your way wherein ye go.

**[Judges 18:7-10](/bible/judges/18#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/18#v7)</sup> Then the five men departed, and came to Laish, and saw the people that were therein, how they dwelt careless, after the manner of the Zidonians, quiet and secure; and there was no magistrate in the land, that might put them to shame in any thing; and they were far from the Zidonians, and had no business with any man.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/18#v8)</sup> And they came unto their brethren to Zorah and Eshtaol: and their brethren said unto them, What say ye?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/18#v9)</sup> And they said, Arise, that we may go up against them: for we have seen the land, and, behold, it is very good: and are ye still? be not slothful to go, and to enter to possess the land.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/18#v10)</sup> When ye go, ye shall come unto a people secure, and to a large land: for God hath given it into your hands; a place where there is no want of any thing that is in the earth.

- The five men came to Laish and saw the people that were therein, how they dwelt careless, quiet and secure, after the manner of the Zidonians, the nearby nation whose lifestyle they copied. They were a chill people with no worries, not even watching for anyone to come in and conquer them.
- A magistrate is a judge: there was no judicial system in that land to put a man to shame or convict him for a crime. They were also far from the Zidonians, too far for Zidon to interfere, and had no business with any man, no allies. The spies peeped all of it.
- Their report back at Zorah and Eshtaol: arise, that we may go up against them, for we have seen the land, and behold, it is very good; a place where there is no want of any thing that is in the earth, and God hath given it into your hands. Do not sit still; let us move now.

**[Judges 18:11-14](/bible/judges/18#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/18#v11)</sup> And there went from thence of the family of the Danites, out of Zorah and out of Eshtaol, six hundred men appointed with weapons of war.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/18#v12)</sup> And they went up, and pitched in Kirjath–jearim, in Judah: wherefore they called that place Mahaneh–dan unto this day: behold, it is behind Kirjath–jearim.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/18#v13)</sup> And they passed thence unto mount Ephraim, and came unto the house of Micah.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/18#v14)</sup> Then answered the five men that went to spy out the country of Laish, and said unto their brethren, Do ye know that there is in these houses an ephod, and teraphim, and a graven image, and a molten image? now therefore consider what ye have to do.

- Six hundred men of the family of the Danites appointed with weapons of war went up from Zorah and Eshtaol, camped at Mahanehdan behind Kirjathjearim, and passed again unto mount Ephraim to the house of Micah.
- The first time Micah let the five spies lodge in his house, they saw his expensive looking gods and got covetous. Now passing back through they tell their brethren, do ye know that there is in these houses an ephod, and teraphim, and a graven image, and a molten image? now therefore consider what ye have to do. On the way to the lick they spotted another lick.
- The Bible addresses everything: do not bring just anybody into your house, even brethren, because the deceitful man has many tricks and approaches to finesse you out of the goods in your house.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 11:29](/bible/sirach/11#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/11#v29)</sup> Bring not every man into thine house: for the deceitful man hath many trains.

    Micah brought every man into his house and lost everything in it; the deceitful man has many trains, many tricks and approaches.

**[Judges 18:15-20](/bible/judges/18#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/18#v15)</sup> And they turned thitherward, and came to the house of the young man the Levite, even unto the house of Micah, and saluted him.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/18#v16)</sup> And the six hundred men appointed with their weapons of war, which were of the children of Dan, stood by the entering of the gate.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/18#v17)</sup> And the five men that went to spy out the land went up, and came in thither, and took the graven image, and the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image: and the priest stood in the entering of the gate with the six hundred men that were appointed with weapons of war.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/18#v18)</sup> And these went into Micah’s house, and fetched the carved image, the ephod, and the teraphim, and the molten image. Then said the priest unto them, What do ye?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/18#v19)</sup> And they said unto him, Hold thy peace, lay thine hand upon thy mouth, and go with us, and be to us a father and a priest: is it better for thee to be a priest unto the house of one man, or that thou be a priest unto a tribe and a family in Israel?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/18#v20)</sup> And the priest’s heart was glad, and he took the ephod, and the teraphim, and the graven image, and went in the midst of the people.

- They turned in to the house and saluted the Levite, a friendly greeting at the door; that is one of the deceitful man's many trains. While the six hundred armed men stood by the entering of the gate keeping the priest occupied, the five spies went up inside and took the graven image, the ephod, the teraphim, and the molten image.
- When the priest saw them coming out with the gods and asked, what do ye? they told him hold thy peace, lay thine hand upon thy mouth: is it better for thee to be a priest unto the house of one man, or unto a tribe and a family in Israel? Do not snitch; roll with us and be the man over a whole tribe.
- The priest's heart was glad, and he took the ephod, the teraphim, and the graven image and went in the midst of the people. His covetous spirit was plain: already sold out to serve idols for Micah's silver, he dropped the man who treated him as a son the moment a bigger position was offered. Disloyal through and through.

**[Judges 18:21-26](/bible/judges/18#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/judges/18#v21)</sup> So they turned and departed, and put the little ones and the cattle and the carriage before them.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/judges/18#v22)</sup> And when they were a good way from the house of Micah, the men that were in the houses near to Micah’s house were gathered together, and overtook the children of Dan.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/judges/18#v23)</sup> And they cried unto the children of Dan. And they turned their faces, and said unto Micah, What aileth thee, that thou comest with such a company?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/judges/18#v24)</sup> And he said, Ye have taken away my gods which I made, and the priest, and ye are gone away: and what have I more? and what is this that ye say unto me, What aileth thee?
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/judges/18#v25)</sup> And the children of Dan said unto him, Let not thy voice be heard among us, lest angry fellows run upon thee, and thou lose thy life, with the lives of thy household.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/judges/18#v26)</sup> And the children of Dan went their way: and when Micah saw that they were too strong for him, he turned and went back unto his house.

- When Micah and the men near his house gathered and overtook the children of Dan, Dan asked, what aileth thee, that thou comest with such a company? He answered, ye have taken away my gods which I made, and the priest, and ye are gone away: and what have I more?
- Examine how foolish that sounds: my gods which I made. How can a man make his own god? That is how simple we had become without leadership.
- Dan's answer: let not thy voice be heard among us, lest angry fellows run upon thee, and thou lose thy life, with the lives of thy household. In plain terms, hush, because you are going to anger some of these brothers, they will run up on you, and you and your folks will not make it home. Micah saw they were too strong for him and turned back to his house. Dan was a problem.

**[Judges 18:27-29](/bible/judges/18#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/judges/18#v27)</sup> And they took the things which Micah had made, and the priest which he had, and came unto Laish, unto a people that were at quiet and secure: and they smote them with the edge of the sword, and burnt the city with fire.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/judges/18#v28)</sup> And there was no deliverer, because it was far from Zidon, and they had no business with any man; and it was in the valley that lieth by Beth–rehob. And they built a city, and dwelt therein.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/judges/18#v29)</sup> And they called the name of the city Dan, after the name of Dan their father, who was born unto Israel: howbeit the name of the city was Laish at the first.

- The Danites took the things which Micah had made and the priest which he had, came unto Laish, unto a people quiet and secure, smote them with the edge of the sword, and burnt the city with fire.
- There was no deliverer, because Laish was far from Zidon and had no business with any man, exactly what the spies had scoped out; no neighbor and no ally came to save them.
- They built the city again, dwelt therein, and called the name of it Dan, after Dan their father which was born unto Israel; howbeit the name of the city was Laish at the first.

**[Judges 18:30-31](/bible/judges/18#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/judges/18#v30)</sup> And the children of Dan set up the graven image: and Jonathan, the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/judges/18#v31)</sup> And they set them up Micah’s graven image, which he made, all the time that the house of God was in Shiloh.

- The first thing the children of Dan did in the land the Most High allowed them to take was set up the graven image, a god made by the hands of a man they had just robbed. Jonathan the son of Gershom, the son of Manasseh, he and his sons were priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land, and they kept Micah's graven image all the time the house of God was in Shiloh.
- Dan stayed heavy in idolatry all the way through history and never changed. After the kingdom split, Jeroboam feared that if the people kept going up to sacrifice at the house of the LORD in Jerusalem, their heart would turn again unto Rehoboam king of Judah and they would kill him; so he took counsel and made two calves of gold with his own hands, doubling the iniquity of the wilderness, and said, behold thy gods, O Israel, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.

- Hear the word against Dan by the prophet: they that swear by the sin of Samaria and say, thy god, O Dan, liveth, shall fall and never rise up again. Mind you, it is an idol made with hands, yet they called it living. That is why Dan has no gate among the sealed tribes in [Revelation 7](/bible/revelation/7); Dan was discontinued as a tribe of itself and intertwined with Benjamin for the most part.
