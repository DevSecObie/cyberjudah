## Introduction

This session covers [Judges 21](/bible/judges/21) together with Ruth chapters 1 through 3; these notes cover [Judges 21](/bible/judges/21), the closing chapter of the book. After the war against Benjamin in the previous chapters, Israel weeps that a whole tribe is nearly cut off, but the men of Israel had sworn at Mizpeh not to give their daughters to Benjamin to wife, and an oath sworn to the Most High had to be performed. The chapter shows how Israel provided wives for the remnant of Benjamin, first from Jabeshgilead and then from the daughters of Shiloh, and it closes the book on the key of the whole era: in those days there was no king in Israel, and every man did that which was right in his own eyes.

**[Judges 21:1-6](/bible/judges/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/judges/21#v1)</sup> Now the men of Israel had sworn in Mizpeh, saying, There shall not any of us give his daughter unto Benjamin to wife.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/21#v2)</sup> And the people came to the house of God, and abode there till even before God, and lifted up their voices, and wept sore;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/21#v3)</sup> And said, O Lord God of Israel, why is this come to pass in Israel, that there should be to day one tribe lacking in Israel?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/21#v4)</sup> And it came to pass on the morrow, that the people rose early, and built there an altar, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/judges/21#v5)</sup> And the children of Israel said, Who is there among all the tribes of Israel that came not up with the congregation unto the Lord? For they had made a great oath concerning him that came not up to the Lord to Mizpeh, saying, He shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/judges/21#v6)</sup> And the children of Israel repented them for Benjamin their brother, and said, There is one tribe cut off from Israel this day.

- The men of Israel had sworn at Mizpeh that not one of them would give his daughter unto Benjamin to wife. This follows the previous chapter, where the tribes went to war against Benjamin.
- The people came to the house of God and sat there till evening before God, lifted up their voices and wept sore, asking why it had come to pass that there should be one tribe lacking in Israel. The next day they rose early, built an altar, and offered burnt offerings and peace offerings.
- The children of Israel repented them for Benjamin their brother, for there was one tribe cut off from Israel that day. They had also made a great oath that whoever came not up to the Lord at Mizpeh would surely be put to death.

**[Judges 21:7](/bible/judges/21#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/judges/21#v7)</sup> How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing we have sworn by the Lord that we will not give them of our daughters to wives?

- The question of the chapter: how shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing we have sworn by the Lord not to give them our daughters? They made a vow, they swore to the Lord, and the law says a sworn oath must be performed; that is why they could not simply turn around and give their daughters.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 30:1-2](/bible/numbers/30#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/30#v1)</sup> And Moses spake unto the heads of the tribes concerning the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which the Lord hath commanded.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/30#v2)</sup> If a man vow a vow unto the Lord, or swear an oath to bind his soul with a bond; he shall not break his word, he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth.

    If a man vow a vow unto the Lord or swear an oath, he shall not break his word; he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth. This is the law Israel was bound by when they swore concerning their daughters.

**[Judges 21:8-12](/bible/judges/21#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/judges/21#v8)</sup> And they said, What one is there of the tribes of Israel that came not up to Mizpeh to the Lord? And, behold, there came none to the camp from Jabesh–gilead to the assembly.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/judges/21#v9)</sup> For the people were numbered, and, behold, there were none of the inhabitants of Jabesh–gilead there.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/judges/21#v10)</sup> And the congregation sent thither twelve thousand men of the valiantest, and commanded them, saying, Go and smite the inhabitants of Jabesh–gilead with the edge of the sword, with the women and the children.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/judges/21#v11)</sup> And this is the thing that ye shall do, Ye shall utterly destroy every male, and every woman that hath lain by man.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/judges/21#v12)</sup> And they found among the inhabitants of Jabesh–gilead four hundred young virgins, that had known no man by lying with any male: and they brought them unto the camp to Shiloh, which is in the land of Canaan.

- They searched which tribe came not up to Mizpeh, and behold, none had come from Jabeshgilead to the assembly. So under the great oath the congregation sent twelve thousand of the valiantest men to smite Jabeshgilead with the edge of the sword, destroying every male and every woman that had lain by man.
- Among the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead they found four hundred young virgins that had not known man by lying with any male, and brought them to the camp at Shiloh in the land of Canaan.
- Verse 12 is the precept for what it means in scripture to know somebody: to lie with them, meaning to have sex with them.

**[Judges 21:13-18](/bible/judges/21#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/judges/21#v13)</sup> And the whole congregation sent some to speak to the children of Benjamin that were in the rock Rimmon, and to call peaceably unto them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/judges/21#v14)</sup> And Benjamin came again at that time; and they gave them wives which they had saved alive of the women of Jabesh–gilead: and yet so they sufficed them not.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/judges/21#v15)</sup> And the people repented them for Benjamin, because that the Lord had made a breach in the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/judges/21#v16)</sup> Then the elders of the congregation said, How shall we do for wives for them that remain, seeing the women are destroyed out of Benjamin?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/judges/21#v17)</sup> And they said, There must be an inheritance for them that be escaped of Benjamin, that a tribe be not destroyed out of Israel.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/judges/21#v18)</sup> Howbeit we may not give them wives of our daughters: for the children of Israel have sworn, saying, Cursed be he that giveth a wife to Benjamin.

- The whole congregation sent to speak peaceably to the children of Benjamin at the rock Rimmon, and Benjamin came again. They gave them the wives saved alive of the women of Jabeshgilead, but they sufficed not; there were not enough for all the men that remained.
- The people repented them for Benjamin because the Lord had made a breach in the tribes of Israel, and the elders said there must be an inheritance for them that be escaped of Benjamin, that a tribe be not destroyed out of Israel.
- Verse 18 restates why they could not just give their own daughters: the children of Israel had sworn, saying, Cursed be he that giveth a wife to Benjamin. That is the same oath read in verse 7.

**[Judges 21:19-23](/bible/judges/21#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/judges/21#v19)</sup> Then they said, Behold, there is a feast of the Lord in Shiloh yearly in a place which is on the north side of Beth–el, on the east side of the highway that goeth up from Beth–el to Shechem, and on the south of Lebonah.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/judges/21#v20)</sup> Therefore they commanded the children of Benjamin, saying, Go and lie in wait in the vineyards;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/judges/21#v21)</sup> And see, and, behold, if the daughters of Shiloh come out to dance in dances, then come ye out of the vineyards, and catch you every man his wife of the daughters of Shiloh, and go to the land of Benjamin.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/judges/21#v22)</sup> And it shall be, when their fathers or their brethren come unto us to complain, that we will say unto them, Be favourable unto them for our sakes: because we reserved not to each man his wife in the war: for ye did not give unto them at this time, that ye should be guilty.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/judges/21#v23)</sup> And the children of Benjamin did so, and took them wives, according to their number, of them that danced, whom they caught: and they went and returned unto their inheritance, and repaired the cities, and dwelt in them.

- The solution: there was a yearly feast of the Lord in Shiloh, so they commanded the children of Benjamin to lie in wait in the vineyards, and when the daughters of Shiloh came out to dance in the dances, every man was to catch him a wife of the daughters of Shiloh and go to the land of Benjamin.
- When the fathers or brethren came to complain, the elders would answer, Be favourable unto them for our sakes; because the fathers did not give their daughters, they would not be guilty of breaking the oath.
- The children of Benjamin did so and took wives according to their number of them that danced, then returned to their inheritance, repaired the cities, and dwelt in them.

**[Judges 21:24-25](/bible/judges/21#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/judges/21#v24)</sup> And the children of Israel departed thence at that time, every man to his tribe and to his family, and they went out from thence every man to his inheritance.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/judges/21#v25)</sup> In those days there was no king in Israel: every man did that which was right in his own eyes.

- The children of Israel departed thence at that time, every man to his tribe, to his family, and to his inheritance.
- In those days there was no king in Israel; every man did that which was right in his own eyes. We see that same thing today: most of our people are still in that state, doing what is right in their own eyes. There is nothing new under the sun.
