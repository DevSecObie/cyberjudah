- They turned their backs before the men of Israel unto the way of the wilderness, toward the rock of Rimmon, but the battle overtook them: eighteen thousand men of valour fell, five thousand were gleaned in the highways, and two thousand slain at Gidom, twenty and five thousand in all, including all those mighty lefthanded men of valour.
