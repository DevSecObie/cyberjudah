## Introduction

This reading covers [Leviticus 26](/bible/leviticus/26) through Numbers 2: the blessings and the curses upon the children of Israel, the law of vows and estimations, the numbering of the armies of Israel, and the order of the camps about the tabernacle. [Leviticus 26](/bible/leviticus/26) goes in parallel with Deuteronomy the 28th chapter: the blessings for walking in the commandments of the Most High and the curses for breaking the covenant.

**[Leviticus 26:1](/bible/leviticus/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/26#v1)</sup> Ye shall make you no idols nor graven image, neither rear you up a standing image, neither shall ye set up any image of stone in your land, to bow down unto it: for I am the Lord your God.

- The children of Israel are to make no idols nor graven images, rear up no standing image, and set up no image of stone in the land to bow down to it.
- That covers the images bowed to today: the cobblestone of Islam, the cross, and the relics and Egyptian heads that egyptologists worship. The Israelites are not to do that.

**[Leviticus 26:2](/bible/leviticus/26#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/26#v2)</sup> Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the Lord.

- Keep the sabbaths of the Most High and reverence His sanctuary. When you came into the sanctuary you had better come with respect, because the Most High was putting men to death over it.
- Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, offered strange fire before the Lord which He commanded them not, and fire went out from the Lord and devoured them; that is what reverencing the sanctuary means.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 10:1-3](/bible/leviticus/10#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/10#v1)</sup> And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, took either of them his censer, and put fire therein, and put incense thereon, and offered strange fire before the Lord, which he commanded them not.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/10#v2)</sup> And there went out fire from the Lord, and devoured them, and they died before the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/10#v3)</sup> Then Moses said unto Aaron, This is it that the Lord spake, saying, I will be sanctified in them that come nigh me, and before all the people I will be glorified. And Aaron held his peace.

    Nadab and Abihu offered strange fire which the Lord commanded them not, and fire from the Lord devoured them; I will be sanctified in them that come nigh me.

**[Leviticus 26:3-4](/bible/leviticus/26#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/26#v3)</sup> If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/26#v4)</sup> Then I will give you rain in due season, and the land shall yield her increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.

- These are the blessings for walking in the statutes and keeping the commandments, the same blessings and curses read in Deuteronomy the 28th chapter.
- If Israel kept the commandments there would be no famine: rain in due season, the crops continually growing, the harvest coming in on time, and rain in the land even in a drought.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:1-2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v2)</sup> And all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God.

    If thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord, all these blessings shall come on thee and overtake thee; the same covenant of blessing and curse.

**[Leviticus 26:5](/bible/leviticus/26#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/26#v5)</sup> And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing time: and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land safely.

- The threshing is the corn and the vintage is the grapes for the wine. The threshing reaching to the vintage and the vintage to the sowing time means one season's plenty would carry into the next.
- Israel would never be without wine, without crops, without food; each harvest would reach to the next season, and the bread would be eaten to the full in safety.

**[Leviticus 26:6](/bible/leviticus/26#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/26#v6)</sup> And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will rid evil beasts out of the land, neither shall the sword go through your land.

- The Most High would give peace in the land: no enemies able to come against Israel, no war in the land, no one making the people afraid. That was fulfilled in the days of king Solomon, when there was rest on every side.
- That is also how you know the people in the land of Israel today are not the real Jews: this prophecy of rest is not fulfilled on them. When the Most High saves Israel from captivity it will be the same again, and none shall make him afraid.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 30:10](/bible/jeremiah/30#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/30#v10)</sup> Therefore fear thou not, O my servant Jacob, saith the Lord; neither be dismayed, O Israel: for, lo, I will save thee from afar, and thy seed from the land of their captivity; and Jacob shall return, and shall be in rest, and be quiet, and none shall make him afraid.

    Jacob shall return, and shall be in rest, and be quiet, and none shall make him afraid; the future deliverance carries the same promise as this blessing.

**[Leviticus 26:7-9](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)</sup> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/26#v8)</sup> And five of you shall chase an hundred, and an hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight: and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/26#v9)</sup> For I will have respect unto you, and make you fruitful, and multiply you, and establish my covenant with you.

- Israel would chase their enemies, and the enemies would fall before them by the sword; the nations feared Israel when the nation kept this covenant, as the book of Joshua shows.
- Five would chase an hundred, and an hundred put ten thousand to flight: that is how much power the Most High was going to bestow on His people, having respect unto them, making them fruitful, and establishing His covenant with them and their children.

**[Leviticus 26:10-13](/bible/leviticus/26#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/26#v10)</sup> And ye shall eat old store, and bring forth the old because of the new.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/26#v11)</sup> And I will set my tabernacle among you: and my soul shall not abhor you.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/26#v12)</sup> And I will walk among you, and will be your God, and ye shall be my people.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/26#v13)</sup> I am the Lord your God, which brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, that ye should not be their bondmen; and I have broken the bands of your yoke, and made you go upright.

- Eating the old store and bringing forth the old because of the new means the storehouses would be so full that the old crop would have to be carried out to make room for the new; that is how plenteous the land would be.
- The Most High would set His tabernacle among the people, walk among them, and be their God; He brought them out of Egypt so they should not be bondmen, and broke the bands of their yoke.

**[Leviticus 26:14-16](/bible/leviticus/26#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/26#v14)</sup> But if ye will not hearken unto me, and will not do all these commandments;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/26#v15)</sup> And if ye shall despise my statutes, or if your soul abhor my judgments, so that ye will not do all my commandments, but that ye break my covenant:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/26#v16)</sup> I also will do this unto you; I will even appoint over you terror, consumption, and the burning ague, that shall consume the eyes, and cause sorrow of heart: and ye shall sow your seed in vain, for your enemies shall eat it.

- This is the flip side. If Israel would not hearken but despised the statutes and broke the covenant, the Most High would walk contrary to the people, standing in exact opposition so that nothing would prosper.
- Terror is the terrorism lived under every single day: terrorized by wicked corrupt officers, by our own brothers and sisters, by the nations. Consumption is being consumed in the land, with diseases, oppression, financial mishaps, and the prison system; the burning ague goes into disease, harm to the eyes, and sorrow of heart in the mind.
- Sowing seed in vain: vineyards planted that the enemies eat, and children sown in vain, born only to be killed, incarcerated, or raised up under the curse.

**[Leviticus 26:17](/bible/leviticus/26#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/26#v17)</sup> And I will set my face against you, and ye shall be slain before your enemies: they that hate you shall reign over you; and ye shall flee when none pursueth you.

- The Most High set His face against Israel, and the people are slain before their enemies; you see that every single day.
- They that hate you shall reign over you: that is the clear explanation of why no laws will be passed to deliver this people and why there will be no reparations. No political party, Democratic or Republican, will be beneficial, because the curse stands and they that hate us now reign over us; and we flee when none pursueth.

**[Leviticus 26:18-20](/bible/leviticus/26#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/26#v18)</sup> And if ye will not yet for all this hearken unto me, then I will punish you seven times more for your sins.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/26#v19)</sup> And I will break the pride of your power; and I will make your heaven as iron, and your earth as brass:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/26#v20)</sup> And your strength shall be spent in vain: for your land shall not yield her increase, neither shall the trees of the land yield their fruits.

- Seven times more punishment for the sins, and the pride of Israel's power broken: the very power promised in the blessings, when enemies would flee, was taken away.
- That is the explanation of how the slave masters could bring Israel into slavery: they are not bigger or stronger nations, but it was divine intervention; the Most High broke our power and took our strength so the enemies could prevail. We outnumbered them many times in the cotton fields, yet could do nothing.
- The heaven as iron and the earth as brass also goes into the shackles on the neck and on the feet; and the strength is spent in vain, for the land yields no increase.

**[Leviticus 26:21-22](/bible/leviticus/26#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/26#v21)</sup> And if ye walk contrary unto me, and will not hearken unto me; I will bring seven times more plagues upon you according to your sins.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/26#v22)</sup> I will also send wild beasts among you, which shall rob you of your children, and destroy your cattle, and make you few in number; and your high ways shall be desolate.

- Walking contrary still brings seven times more plagues according to the sins.
- Wild beasts sent among the people to rob them of their children, destroy their cattle, and make them few in number: wolves, coyotes, bears, whatever the Most High would allow to come upon the people for their wickedness. That is real evil brought for real rebellion.
- It also goes into the pestilences and diseases loosed among the people in these last days, as [Revelation 6](/bible/revelation/6) bears out with death by sword, hunger, and the beasts of the earth.

  - **[Revelation 6:8](/bible/revelation/6#v8)**

    Power was given them to kill with sword, with hunger, with death, and with the beasts of the earth; the same instruments of judgment.

**[Leviticus 26:23-26](/bible/leviticus/26#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/26#v23)</sup> And if ye will not be reformed by me by these things, but will walk contrary unto me;

> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/26#v24)</sup> Then will I also walk contrary unto you, and will punish you yet seven times for your sins.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/26#v25)</sup> And I will bring a sword upon you, that shall avenge the quarrel of my covenant: and when ye are gathered together within your cities, I will send the pestilence among you; and ye shall be delivered into the hand of the enemy.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/26#v26)</sup> And when I have broken the staff of your bread, ten women shall bake your bread in one oven, and they shall deliver you your bread again by weight: and ye shall eat, and not be satisfied.

- If the people would not be reformed by these things, the Most High would punish yet seven times more and bring a sword to avenge the quarrel of His covenant; gathered into their cities, the pestilence would be sent among them and they would be delivered into the hand of the enemy.
- Breaking the staff of bread, with ten women baking bread in one oven and delivering bread again by weight, is straight poverty: a lack of resources and food. Many of us grew up literally knocking on the neighbor's door to borrow sugar, borrow the iron, borrow bread, use the grill at such and such house because our appliances did not work; that is this verse, the same thing to this day, eating and not being satisfied.

**[Leviticus 26:27-29](/bible/leviticus/26#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/26#v27)</sup> And if ye will not for all this hearken unto me, but walk contrary unto me;

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/26#v29)</sup> And ye shall eat the flesh of your sons, and the flesh of your daughters shall ye eat.

- Eating the flesh of sons and daughters goes into 70 AD, and it also happened in the days of the kings when the city was besieged and there was such famine that a woman's son was boiled and eaten.

**[Leviticus 26:30](/bible/leviticus/26#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/26#v30)</sup> And I will destroy your high places, and cut down your images, and cast your carcases upon the carcases of your idols, and my soul shall abhor you.

- The high places destroyed, the images cut down, and the carcasses of the people cast upon the carcasses of their idols: our people dying in the christian church with a cross around the neck, our people dying up against the cobblestone, still worshipping while they die.
- Even the high places themselves fall in judgment, as when a storm brought a church down on the congregation in Africa during a so-called easter program; that is what this verse reads about.

**[Leviticus 26:31-33](/bible/leviticus/26#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/26#v31)</sup> And I will make your cities waste, and bring your sanctuaries unto desolation, and I will not smell the savour of your sweet odours.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/26#v32)</sup> And I will bring the land into desolation: and your enemies which dwell therein shall be astonished at it.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/leviticus/26#v33)</sup> And I will scatter you among the heathen, and will draw out a sword after you: and your land shall be desolate, and your cities waste.

- The Most High made the cities waste and brought the sanctuaries to desolation, and would not smell the savour of the sweet odours: He was not accepting the sacrifices, and He used the enemy to destroy the sanctuary. The Babylonians destroyed the temple, then the Greeks, then the Romans.
- When Babylon came against Jerusalem, Esau stood on the side cheering it on to the foundation, as [Psalms 137](/bible/psalms/137) records.
- Scattered among the heathen with a sword drawn out after them: not only the scattering, but death and anguish sent among the people in the lands of the captivity.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 137:7](/bible/psalms/137#v7)**

    Remember the children of Edom in the day of Jerusalem, who said, Rase it, rase it, even to the foundation thereof; Esau cheered the destruction on.

**[Leviticus 26:34-35](/bible/leviticus/26#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/26#v34)</sup> Then shall the land enjoy her sabbaths, as long as it lieth desolate, and ye be in your enemies’ land; even then shall the land rest, and enjoy her sabbaths.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/26#v35)</sup> As long as it lieth desolate it shall rest; because it did not rest in your sabbaths, when ye dwelt upon it.

- Then shall the land enjoy her sabbaths as long as it lieth desolate. The land was to rest every seventh year, and the sabbaths were not kept: no cooking or tilling on the sabbath, the land resting every seven years, none of it.
- The Most High said the land could not rest while the people dwelt on it doing evil, so He rid the people out of the land so that the land could enjoy her sabbaths while it lay desolate without them.

**[Leviticus 26:36-38](/bible/leviticus/26#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/26#v36)</sup> And upon them that are left alive of you I will send a faintness into their hearts in the lands of their enemies; and the sound of a shaken leaf shall chase them; and they shall flee, as fleeing from a sword; and they shall fall when none pursueth.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/leviticus/26#v37)</sup> And they shall fall one upon another, as it were before a sword, when none pursueth: and ye shall have no power to stand before your enemies.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/leviticus/26#v38)</sup> And ye shall perish among the heathen, and the land of your enemies shall eat you up.

- Upon them that are left alive a faintness is sent into their hearts in the lands of their enemies, so that the sound of a shaken leaf chases them; the same trembling heart, failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind that [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) describes for the land of the enemies.
- Falling one upon another as it were before a sword when none pursueth: even at these protests, when the rubber bullets, the tear gas, the water hoses, and the dogs come out, the people stumble and fall over one another trying to get away. Still to this day the same thing.
- And ye shall perish among the heathen: many of us die in this land and in all the lands where we are scattered, and the land of the enemies eats the people up in oppression.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:65-67](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)**
    > <sup>[65](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)</sup> And among these nations shalt thou find no ease, neither shall the sole of thy foot have rest: but the Lord shall give thee there a trembling heart, and failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind:

    Among these nations shalt thou find no ease, but a trembling heart, and failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind, fearing day and night with no assurance of life.

**[Leviticus 26:39](/bible/leviticus/26#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/leviticus/26#v39)</sup> And they that are left of you shall pine away in their iniquity in your enemies’ lands; and also in the iniquities of their fathers shall they pine away with them.

- They that are left pine away in their iniquity in the enemies' land, and in the iniquities of their fathers. You see people just decay in their sickness: the Most High may not judge a man right away but gives him space to repent, yet he keeps eating the defiled food, smoking his weed, doping, in and out of jail, and slowly pines away.
- It is a miserable life to live; we have to get right with the Most High.

**[Leviticus 26:40-41](/bible/leviticus/26#v40)**

> <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/26#v40)</sup> If they shall confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers, with their trespass which they trespassed against me, and that also they have walked contrary unto me;
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/leviticus/26#v41)</sup> And that I also have walked contrary unto them, and have brought them into the land of their enemies; if then their uncircumcised hearts be humbled, and they then accept of the punishment of their iniquity:

- Confessing the iniquity of the fathers and their own trespass, with the uncircumcised heart humbled, is exactly what is being done right now: repenting, confessing the sins, remembering that the sins of the forefathers are ours, and turning the uncircumcised mind back to the Father.
- Accepting the punishment of the iniquity means going through the trials without fighting the sentence: not marching for reparations or so-called equality, but accepting the punishment for the iniquities committed against the Most High.

**[Leviticus 26:42-43](/bible/leviticus/26#v42)**

> <sup>[42](/bible/leviticus/26#v42)</sup> Then will I remember my covenant with Jacob, and also my covenant with Isaac, and also my covenant with Abraham will I remember; and I will remember the land.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/leviticus/26#v43)</sup> The land also shall be left of them, and shall enjoy her sabbaths, while she lieth desolate without them: and they shall accept of the punishment of their iniquity: because, even because they despised my judgments, and because their soul abhorred my statutes.

- When the people repent and turn back, the Most High remembers His covenant with Jacob, with Isaac, and with Abraham, the covenant passed down to their children, the children of Israel; and He remembers the land.
- The land enjoys her sabbaths while she lieth desolate without them, and the people must accept that they were evil and wicked against the Most High, because they despised His judgments and their soul abhorred His statutes.

    If they bethink themselves in the land of their captivity, and repent, and return unto thee with all their heart, the Most High hears; repentance in captivity brings the covenant back to remembrance.

**[Leviticus 26:44-45](/bible/leviticus/26#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/26#v44)</sup> And yet for all that, when they be in the land of their enemies, I will not cast them away, neither will I abhor them, to destroy them utterly, and to break my covenant with them: for I am the Lord their God.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/26#v45)</sup> But I will for their sakes remember the covenant of their ancestors, whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt in the sight of the heathen, that I might be their God: I am the Lord.

- Yet for all that, in the land of their enemies the Most High will not cast the people away, neither abhor them to destroy them utterly and break His covenant. He sent Israel into captivity not for all to die, but to be afflicted until they finally turn back to the Lord God of Israel.
- So when you hear people say the Israelites are done away, that is a lie: the Most High promised He would not destroy the seed of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, but would give them a chance to repent and would remember the covenant of their ancestors whom He brought out of Egypt in the sight of the heathen. It also carries into the future tense.

**[Leviticus 26:46](/bible/leviticus/26#v46)**

> <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/26#v46)</sup> These are the statutes and judgments and laws, which the Lord made between him and the children of Israel in mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.

- These are the statutes and judgments and laws which the Most High made between Him and the children of Israel in mount Sinai by the hand of Moses.
- The quick recap of the chapter: these are the blessings and the curses that come upon the children of Israel for obedience or disobedience, and the chapter runs parallel with Deuteronomy the 28th chapter.

# Leviticus 27: Vows and Estimations

Read the chapter → [Leviticus 27](/bible/leviticus/27)

**[Leviticus 27:1-2](/bible/leviticus/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/27#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/27#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When a man shall make a singular vow, the persons shall be for the Lord by thy estimation.

- A singular vow is a man binding himself to the Most High: promising that in exchange for a blessing he will give himself, or what is his, to the service of the Lord. The persons vowed were valued by the estimation of the priest.
- Hannah is the example: in bitterness of soul she vowed that if the Lord gave her a man child she would give him unto the Lord all the days of his life, and that is how the story of Samuel began.
- Christ taught on this because a vow is dangerous: you must pay your vow. Better not to swear at all, but let your communication be yea, yea; that is why we just say Lord's will, Lord's will I get this done.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 1:10-11](/bible/1-samuel/1#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-samuel/1#v10)</sup> And she was in bitterness of soul, and prayed unto the Lord, and wept sore.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-samuel/1#v11)</sup> And she vowed a vow, and said, O Lord of hosts, if thou wilt indeed look on the affliction of thine handmaid, and remember me, and not forget thine handmaid, but wilt give unto thine handmaid a man child, then I will give him unto the Lord all the days of his life, and there shall no razor come upon his head.

    Hannah vowed a vow: give unto thine handmaid a man child, and I will give him unto the Lord all the days of his life; the pattern of a singular vow.
  - **[Matthew 5:33-37](/bible/matthew/5#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/5#v33)</sup> Again, ye have heard that it hath been said by them of old time, Thou shalt not forswear thyself, but shalt perform unto the Lord thine oaths:
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/5#v34)</sup> But I say unto you, Swear not at all; neither by heaven; for it is God’s throne:
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/5#v35)</sup> Nor by the earth; for it is his footstool: neither by Jerusalem; for it is the city of the great King.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/5#v36)</sup> Neither shalt thou swear by thy head, because thou canst not make one hair white or black.
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)</sup> But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

    Perform unto the Lord thine oaths; but swear not at all, and let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay.

**[Leviticus 27:3-8](/bible/leviticus/27#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/27#v3)</sup> And thy estimation shall be of the male from twenty years old even unto sixty years old, even thy estimation shall be fifty shekels of silver, after the shekel of the sanctuary.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/27#v4)</sup> And if it be a female, then thy estimation shall be thirty shekels.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/27#v5)</sup> And if it be from five years old even unto twenty years old, then thy estimation shall be of the male twenty shekels, and for the female ten shekels.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/27#v6)</sup> And if it be from a month old even unto five years old, then thy estimation shall be of the male five shekels of silver, and for the female thy estimation shall be three shekels of silver.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/27#v7)</sup> And if it be from sixty years old and above; if it be a male, then thy estimation shall be fifteen shekels, and for the female ten shekels.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/27#v8)</sup> But if he be poorer than thy estimation, then he shall present himself before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to his ability that vowed shall the priest value him.

- The estimation is the value set for redeeming a person out of the vow. A male from twenty to sixty years old, the prime of life, was fifty shekels of silver after the shekel of the sanctuary, and a female thirty shekels.
- From five years to twenty years old the male was twenty shekels and the female ten; from a month old to five years the male was five shekels and the female three; and from sixty years old and above the male was fifteen shekels and the female ten.
- If the man was poorer than the estimation and could not pay it, he presented himself before the priest, and the priest valued him according to the ability of him that vowed, his worth and what he was able to bring forth.

**[Leviticus 27:9-13](/bible/leviticus/27#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/27#v9)</sup> And if it be a beast, whereof men bring an offering unto the Lord, all that any man giveth of such unto the Lord shall be holy.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/27#v10)</sup> He shall not alter it, nor change it, a good for a bad, or a bad for a good: and if he shall at all change beast for beast, then it and the exchange thereof shall be holy.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/27#v11)</sup> And if it be any unclean beast, of which they do not offer a sacrifice unto the Lord, then he shall present the beast before the priest:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/27#v12)</sup> And the priest shall value it, whether it be good or bad: as thou valuest it, who art the priest, so shall it be.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/27#v13)</sup> But if he will at all redeem it, then he shall add a fifth part thereof unto thy estimation.

- If a beast was brought as an offering to the Most High it had to be holy, not blemished; it could not be altered or changed, a good for a bad or a bad for a good, and if it was changed at all, both it and the exchange became holy.
- An unclean beast, one with a blemish that could not be sacrificed, was presented before the priest and the priest valued it, good or bad; and to redeem it, a fifth part had to be added to the estimation to get that beast back.

**[Leviticus 27:14-15](/bible/leviticus/27#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/27#v14)</sup> And when a man shall sanctify his house to be holy unto the Lord, then the priest shall estimate it, whether it be good or bad: as the priest shall estimate it, so shall it stand.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/27#v15)</sup> And if he that sanctified it will redeem his house, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of thy estimation unto it, and it shall be his.

- A man could sanctify his house to be holy unto the Most High, and the priest estimated it, good or bad, and as the priest estimated it, so it stood.
- To redeem the house he added the fifth part of the money of the estimation: if the house was worth a hundred thousand, he paid that hundred thousand plus the extra twenty thousand, and it was his again.

**[Leviticus 27:16-25](/bible/leviticus/27#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/27#v16)</sup> And if a man shall sanctify unto the Lord some part of a field of his possession, then thy estimation shall be according to the seed thereof: an homer of barley seed shall be valued at fifty shekels of silver.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/27#v17)</sup> If he sanctify his field from the year of jubile, according to thy estimation it shall stand.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/27#v18)</sup> But if he sanctify his field after the jubile, then the priest shall reckon unto him the money according to the years that remain, even unto the year of the jubile, and it shall be abated from thy estimation.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/27#v19)</sup> And if he that sanctified the field will in any wise redeem it, then he shall add the fifth part of the money of thy estimation unto it, and it shall be assured to him.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/27#v20)</sup> And if he will not redeem the field, or if he have sold the field to another man, it shall not be redeemed any more.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/27#v21)</sup> But the field, when it goeth out in the jubile, shall be holy unto the Lord, as a field devoted; the possession thereof shall be the priest’s.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/27#v22)</sup> And if a man sanctify unto the Lord a field which he hath bought, which is not of the fields of his possession;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/27#v23)</sup> Then the priest shall reckon unto him the worth of thy estimation, even unto the year of the jubile: and he shall give thine estimation in that day, as a holy thing unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/27#v24)</sup> In the year of the jubile the field shall return unto him of whom it was bought, even to him to whom the possession of the land did belong.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/27#v25)</sup> And all thy estimations shall be according to the shekel of the sanctuary: twenty gerahs shall be the shekel.

- A field sanctified to the Most High was estimated according to the seed thereof, an homer of barley seed at fifty shekels of silver; from the year of jubile the estimation stood full, but after the jubile the priest reckoned the money according to the years that remained, and it was abated from the estimation.
- The jubile was the year of release, when the land had to rest, so the value was reckoned by how close the jubile was: the fewer years left to reap the field, the lower the estimation. It is like trading in a car; a two year old car with twenty thousand miles brings more value than an old car with a hundred and fifty thousand miles on it.
- To redeem a sanctified field the fifth part was added to the estimation. A field not redeemed, or sold to another man, could not be redeemed any more, but went out in the jubile holy unto the Lord, a devoted field, the possession of the priest; and in the year of jubile a bought field returned to him of whom it was bought.
- All the estimations were according to the shekel of the sanctuary, twenty gerahs to the shekel; that was the standard, the set price.

**[Leviticus 27:26-29](/bible/leviticus/27#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/27#v26)</sup> Only the firstling of the beasts, which should be the Lord’s firstling, no man shall sanctify it; whether it be ox, or sheep: it is the Lord’s.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/27#v27)</sup> And if it be of an unclean beast, then he shall redeem it according to thine estimation, and shall add a fifth part of it thereto: or if it be not redeemed, then it shall be sold according to thy estimation.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/27#v28)</sup> Notwithstanding no devoted thing, that a man shall devote unto the Lord of all that he hath, both of man and beast, and of the field of his possession, shall be sold or redeemed: every devoted thing is most holy unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/27#v29)</sup> None devoted, which shall be devoted of men, shall be redeemed; but shall surely be put to death.

- The firstling of the beasts could not be sanctified by any man, because as a firstling it was the Lord's already, whether ox or sheep; an unclean firstling was redeemed by the estimation with the fifth part added, or sold according to the estimation.
- No devoted thing that a man devoted unto the Most High, of man, beast, or field of his possession, could be sold or redeemed: every devoted thing is most holy unto the Lord, and a devoted man was surely put to death.

**[Leviticus 27:30-34](/bible/leviticus/27#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/27#v30)</sup> And all the tithe of the land, whether of the seed of the land, or of the fruit of the tree, is the Lord’s: it is holy unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/27#v31)</sup> And if a man will at all redeem ought of his tithes, he shall add thereto the fifth part thereof.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/27#v32)</sup> And concerning the tithe of the herd, or of the flock, even of whatsoever passeth under the rod, the tenth shall be holy unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/leviticus/27#v33)</sup> He shall not search whether it be good or bad, neither shall he change it: and if he change it at all, then both it and the change thereof shall be holy; it shall not be redeemed.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/27#v34)</sup> These are the commandments, which the Lord commanded Moses for the children of Israel in mount Sinai.

- All the tithe of the land, of the seed of the land or the fruit of the tree, is the Lord's; it is holy unto the Lord. To redeem out of the tithes a man added the fifth part thereto.
- The tithe of the herd and the flock, the tenth of whatsoever passeth under the rod, was holy unto the Lord; he was not to search whether it was good or bad or change it, and if he changed it at all, both it and the change were holy and could not be redeemed.
- The quick recap of the chapter: when a brother or sister vowed themselves to the Most High, the redemption from that vow went according to the estimation, by age and by sex, and likewise a house or a field; all these things go into how the priest calculated the estimation of these particular things to release you from the vow. The chapter can be tricky, so read it over and over.
