**[Leviticus 11:1-3](/bible/leviticus/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/11#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and to Aaron, saying unto them,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/11#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, These are the beasts which ye shall eat among all the beasts that are on the earth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/11#v3)</sup> Whatsoever parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, and cheweth the cud, among the beasts, that shall ye eat.

- [Leviticus 11](/bible/leviticus/11) tells you what you can and cannot eat. Among all the beasts of the earth, ye shall eat whatsoever parteth the hoof, and is clovenfooted, and cheweth the cud.
- A cloven hoof is a hoof split into two toes; mammals with this hoof include cattle, deer, antelopes, gazelles, goats, and sheep.
- Chewing the cud means the animal has more than one stomach: it chews its food, brings it back up into its mouth to be chewed again, and then digests it through another stomach.

**[Leviticus 11:4-8](/bible/leviticus/11#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/11#v4)</sup> Nevertheless these shall ye not eat of them that chew the cud, or of them that divide the hoof: as the camel, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/11#v5)</sup> And the coney, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/11#v6)</sup> And the hare, because he cheweth the cud, but divideth not the hoof; he is unclean unto you.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/11#v7)</sup> And the swine, though he divide the hoof, and be clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean to you.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/11#v8)</sup> Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch; they are unclean to you.

- Both marks must be present. There are animals that chew the cud and animals that divide the hoof but do not have both; those are unclean. The camel cheweth the cud but divideth not the hoof: you cannot eat camel.
- The coney is the pika, a small mountain dwelling mammal that resembles its close relative the rabbit, found at heights among the highest of any mammal. The scriptures already had it right: the rocks are a refuge for the conies, and the conies, a feeble folk, make their houses in the rocks. You cannot eat coney, and the hare likewise is unclean.
- The swine has only the one mark: he divideth the hoof and is clovenfooted, yet he cheweth not the cud; he is unclean unto you. You cannot eat pork; leave it alone.
- Their carcase shall ye not touch sits inside the dietary law: it goes into sacrificing the unclean thing and eating it, touching it with the mouth. It does not mean a dead animal in your path cannot be moved out of the way with the hands; the tabernacle itself had to be built by hand with badgers' skins.

**[Leviticus 11:9-12](/bible/leviticus/11#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/11#v9)</sup> These shall ye eat of all that are in the waters: whatsoever hath fins and scales in the waters, in the seas, and in the rivers, them shall ye eat.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/11#v10)</sup> And all that have not fins and scales in the seas, and in the rivers, of all that move in the waters, and of any living thing which is in the waters, they shall be an abomination unto you:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/11#v11)</sup> They shall be even an abomination unto you; ye shall not eat of their flesh, but ye shall have their carcases in abomination.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/11#v12)</sup> Whatsoever hath no fins nor scales in the waters, that shall be an abomination unto you.

- Of all that are in the waters, ye shall eat whatsoever hath fins and scales; whatever has not fins and scales is an abomination. No shrimp, no crab, no lobster, no bottom dwellers.
- The Most High gave an excellent dietary law: high blood pressure and the like come from eating shellfish. He made these animals and knows what they do; if you eat what he allowed you will be fine. It is simple: if it does not have both fins and scales, leave it alone.
- A fish must have both scales and fins. Tilapia has scales and fins, so it may be eaten if you want it; sardines do not show both, so it looks like sardines are a no.

**[Leviticus 11:13-19](/bible/leviticus/11#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/11#v13)</sup> And these are they which ye shall have in abomination among the fowls; they shall not be eaten, they are an abomination: the eagle, and the ossifrage, and the ospray,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/11#v14)</sup> And the vulture, and the kite after his kind;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/11#v15)</sup> Every raven after his kind;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/11#v16)</sup> And the owl, and the night hawk, and the cuckow, and the hawk after his kind,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/11#v17)</sup> And the little owl, and the cormorant, and the great owl,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/11#v18)</sup> And the swan, and the pelican, and the gier eagle,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/11#v19)</sup> And the stork, the heron after her kind, and the lapwing, and the bat.

- These are the fowls in abomination that shall not be eaten: the eagle, the ossifrage, the osprey, the vulture and the kite after his kind, every raven after his kind, the owl, the nighthawk, the cuckow, the hawk, the little owl, the cormorant, the great owl, the swan, the pelican, the gier eagle, the stork, the heron after her kind, the lapwing, and the bat.
- Look these up. The ossifrage, the bone breaker, is an older name for the bearded vulture, a scavenger famous for dropping bones on rocks to crack them open. The osprey is the seahawk or fishhawk, a fish eating bird of prey. The kite is a bird of prey in the family of the eagle.
- What is interesting is that all of these are scavengers and hunters; even in the waters the Most High did not want you to have the scavengers, and no shark, a hunter. He gave you this diet because he made these animals and knows what they do.
- After his kind: when the Most High made one, he made many of them, so the rule covers the whole family.
- On duck: the swan's closest relatives include the geese and ducks. The swan is unclean, and duck falls in the family of the swan, so you cannot eat duck; they are related, and it is the same thing.

**[Leviticus 11:20-23](/bible/leviticus/11#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/11#v20)</sup> All fowls that creep, going upon all four, shall be an abomination unto you.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/11#v21)</sup> Yet these may ye eat of every flying creeping thing that goeth upon all four, which have legs above their feet, to leap withal upon the earth;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/11#v22)</sup> Even these of them ye may eat; the locust after his kind, and the bald locust after his kind, and the beetle after his kind, and the grasshopper after his kind.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/11#v23)</sup> But all other flying creeping things, which have four feet, shall be an abomination unto you.

- All fowls that creep, going upon all four, are an abomination, yet those which have legs above their feet to leap withal upon the earth may be eaten: the locust, the bald locust, the beetle, and the grasshopper after his kind. You can eat locusts; John the Baptist ate locusts.

**[Leviticus 11:24-28](/bible/leviticus/11#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/11#v24)</sup> And for these ye shall be unclean: whosoever toucheth the carcase of them shall be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/11#v25)</sup> And whosoever beareth ought of the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/11#v26)</sup> The carcases of every beast which divideth the hoof, and is not clovenfooted, nor cheweth the cud, are unclean unto you: every one that toucheth them shall be unclean.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/11#v27)</sup> And whatsoever goeth upon his paws, among all manner of beasts that go on all four, those are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/11#v28)</sup> And he that beareth the carcase of them shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: they are unclean unto you.

- The carcases of every beast which divideth the hoof and is not clovenfooted nor cheweth the cud are unclean; whoever touches them is unclean until the even, and whoever bears the carcase washes his clothes.
- Whatsoever goeth upon his paws, among all manner of beasts that go on all four, is unclean: you cannot eat mountain lion, tiger, cougar, gorilla, dog, or cat; they have paws and walk on all fours.

**[Leviticus 11:29-38](/bible/leviticus/11#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/11#v29)</sup> These also shall be unclean unto you among the creeping things that creep upon the earth; the weasel, and the mouse, and the tortoise after his kind,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/11#v30)</sup> And the ferret, and the chameleon, and the lizard, and the snail, and the mole.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/11#v31)</sup> These are unclean to you among all that creep: whosoever doth touch them, when they be dead, shall be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/11#v32)</sup> And upon whatsoever any of them, when they are dead, doth fall, it shall be unclean; whether it be any vessel of wood, or raiment, or skin, or sack, whatsoever vessel it be, wherein any work is done, it must be put into water, and it shall be unclean until the even; so it shall be cleansed.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/leviticus/11#v33)</sup> And every earthen vessel, whereinto any of them falleth, whatsoever is in it shall be unclean; and ye shall break it.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/11#v34)</sup> Of all meat which may be eaten, that on which such water cometh shall be unclean: and all drink that may be drunk in every such vessel shall be unclean.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/11#v35)</sup> And every thing whereupon any part of their carcase falleth shall be unclean; whether it be oven, or ranges for pots, they shall be broken down: for they are unclean, and shall be unclean unto you.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/11#v36)</sup> Nevertheless a fountain or pit, wherein there is plenty of water, shall be clean: but that which toucheth their carcase shall be unclean.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/leviticus/11#v37)</sup> And if any part of their carcase fall upon any sowing seed which is to be sown, it shall be clean.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/leviticus/11#v38)</sup> But if any water be put upon the seed, and any part of their carcase fall thereon, it shall be unclean unto you.

- These creeping things are unclean: the weasel, the mouse, the tortoise after his kind, the ferret, the chameleon, the lizard, the snail, and the mole. You cannot eat tortoise.
- If you find a dead mouse in your house, do not touch it: sweep it into a bag and throw it out.
- Whatever vessel one of them falls into when dead is unclean until the even and must be put into water; if it falls into one of your pots, clean that pot out and cook nothing in it until the even. An earthen vessel it falls into is broken and thrown out, and ovens and ranges for pots are broken down.
- If any part of their carcase falls on sowing seed which is to be sown, the seed is clean; but if water be put upon the seed and the carcase fall thereon, it is unclean and no good.

**[Leviticus 11:39-43](/bible/leviticus/11#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/leviticus/11#v39)</sup> And if any beast, of which ye may eat, die; he that toucheth the carcase thereof shall be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/11#v40)</sup> And he that eateth of the carcase of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: he also that beareth the carcase of it shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/leviticus/11#v41)</sup> And every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth shall be an abomination; it shall not be eaten.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/leviticus/11#v42)</sup> Whatsoever goeth upon the belly, and whatsoever goeth upon all four, or whatsoever hath more feet among all creeping things that creep upon the earth, them ye shall not eat; for they are an abomination.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/leviticus/11#v43)</sup> Ye shall not make yourselves abominable with any creeping thing that creepeth, neither shall ye make yourselves unclean with them, that ye should be defiled thereby.

- Even of a beast which ye may eat, if it die of itself, he that toucheth, eateth of, or beareth the carcase is unclean until the even and washes his clothes.
- Every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth is an abomination: whatsoever goeth upon the belly, whatsoever goeth upon all four, and whatsoever hath more feet. You cannot eat centipedes and millipedes.

**[Leviticus 11:44-47](/bible/leviticus/11#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/11#v44)</sup> For I am the Lord your God: ye shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/11#v45)</sup> For I am the Lord that bringeth you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/11#v46)</sup> This is the law of the beasts, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth:
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/11#v47)</sup> To make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.

- Sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy, for I am holy: for I am the Lord that bringeth you up out of the land of Egypt to be your God. This is the law of the beast and of the fowl and of every living creature that moveth in the waters and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth; the Most High explained everything and then summed it all up, to make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.
- Do not confuse yourself with [1 Timothy 4:4](/bible/1-timothy/4#v4), that you can eat whatever you want. Every creature is good and nothing to be refused if it be received with thanksgiving, for it is sanctified by the word of God; so find what the word of God sanctified, and that is the law of the beast in [Leviticus 11](/bible/leviticus/11). What is sanctified is what he already made clean.
- The law perisheth not, but remaineth in his force; that law did not change at all. Do not let anybody confuse you, especially if you are new in the truth: eat what the Most High instructed you to eat.
