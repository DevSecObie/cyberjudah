**[Leviticus 13:1-8](/bible/leviticus/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/13#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/13#v2)</sup> When a man shall have in the skin of his flesh a rising, a scab, or bright spot, and it be in the skin of his flesh like the plague of leprosy; then he shall be brought unto Aaron the priest, or unto one of his sons the priests:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/13#v3)</sup> And the priest shall look on the plague in the skin of the flesh: and when the hair in the plague is turned white, and the plague in sight be deeper than the skin of his flesh, it is a plague of leprosy: and the priest shall look on him, and pronounce him unclean.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/13#v4)</sup> If the bright spot be white in the skin of his flesh, and in sight be not deeper than the skin, and the hair thereof be not turned white; then the priest shall shut up him that hath the plague seven days:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/13#v5)</sup> And the priest shall look on him the seventh day: and, behold, if the plague in his sight be at a stay, and the plague spread not in the skin; then the priest shall shut him up seven days more:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/13#v6)</sup> And the priest shall look on him again the seventh day: and, behold, if the plague be somewhat dark, and the plague spread not in the skin, the priest shall pronounce him clean: it is but a scab: and he shall wash his clothes, and be clean.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/13#v7)</sup> But if the scab spread much abroad in the skin, after that he hath been seen of the priest for his cleansing, he shall be seen of the priest again:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/13#v8)</sup> And if the priest see that, behold, the scab spreadeth in the skin, then the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is a leprosy.

- The plague of leprosy is discerned by the priest. When the hair in the plague is turned white and the plague in sight is deeper than the skin of the flesh, it is a plague of leprosy and the priest pronounces him unclean; if not, the priest shuts him up seven days, and seven days more if the plague is at a stay, and if the plague is somewhat dark and spread not, it is but a scab and he is pronounced clean; but a scab that spreads much abroad after his cleansing is a leprosy.

**[Leviticus 13:12-13](/bible/leviticus/13#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/13#v12)</sup> And if a leprosy break out abroad in the skin, and the leprosy cover all the skin of him that hath the plague from his head even to his foot, wheresoever the priest looketh;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/13#v13)</sup> Then the priest shall consider: and, behold, if the leprosy have covered all his flesh, he shall pronounce him clean that hath the plague: it is all turned white: he is clean.

- If the leprosy break out abroad and cover all the flesh from head to foot, and it be all turned white, the priest shall pronounce him clean.
- That is the albino: if the leprosy covers all the skin, as in an albino, he is still clean. A man can be albino and still be Israel; if his father is Judah, he is clean. It is not about your skin, it is about your nationality.

**[Leviticus 13:29-30](/bible/leviticus/13#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/13#v29)</sup> If a man or woman have a plague upon the head or the beard;
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/13#v30)</sup> Then the priest shall see the plague: and, behold, if it be in sight deeper than the skin; and there be in it a yellow thin hair; then the priest shall pronounce him unclean: it is a dry scall, even a leprosy upon the head or beard.

- For a plague upon the head or the beard, if it be in sight deeper than the skin, and there be in it a yellow thin hair, it is a dry scall, even a leprosy upon the head or beard, and the priest pronounces him unclean.
- Yellow thin hair is called a plague. Our hair does not come like that; the nations that have blonde hair are naturally that way. When Israel, blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans dye their hair blonde, they are taking after the customs of Esau, emulating who they want to be like, and the word calls that yellow thin hair a plague on our people.
- There is no law against color as such: the scripture names yellow thin hair; but go with what the word says.

**[Leviticus 13:40-41](/bible/leviticus/13#v40)**

> <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/13#v40)</sup> And the man whose hair is fallen off his head, he is bald; yet is he clean.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/leviticus/13#v41)</sup> And he that hath his hair fallen off from the part of his head toward his face, he is forehead bald: yet is he clean.

- The man whose hair is fallen off his head is bald, yet is he clean; and he whose hair is fallen off toward his face is forehead bald, yet is he clean.
- So do not assume against a brother: if he goes bald naturally, the word says the man is clean, and a brother may be forehead bald with hair further back you cannot see.

**[Leviticus 13:45-46](/bible/leviticus/13#v45)**

> <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/13#v45)</sup> And the leper in whom the plague is, his clothes shall be rent, and his head bare, and he shall put a covering upon his upper lip, and shall cry, Unclean, unclean.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/13#v46)</sup> All the days wherein the plague shall be in him he shall be defiled; he is unclean: he shall dwell alone; without the camp shall his habitation be.

- The leper in whom the plague is, his clothes shall be rent, his head bare, a covering upon his upper lip, and he shall cry, Unclean, unclean; all the days the plague is in him he is defiled, and he shall dwell alone, without the camp.
- Miriam is the example. She and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married, Miriam trying to usurp authority over the man, and the Lord heard it; the cloud departed, and behold, Miriam became leprous, white as snow.
- The Lord said, if her father had but spit in her face, should she not be ashamed seven days? Let her be shut out from the camp seven days, and after that let her be received in again. That is the same law working here: the leper is put without the camp.

  Precepts:

    > <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/12#v1)</sup> And Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married: for he had married an Ethiopian woman.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/12#v2)</sup> And they said, Hath the Lord indeed spoken only by Moses? hath he not spoken also by us? And the Lord heard it.

    Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman, and the Lord heard it.
  - **[Numbers 12:10](/bible/numbers/12#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/12#v10)</sup> And the cloud departed from off the tabernacle; and, behold, Miriam became leprous, white as snow: and Aaron looked upon Miriam, and, behold, she was leprous.

    The cloud departed from off the tabernacle, and Miriam became leprous, white as snow.
  - **[Numbers 12:14-15](/bible/numbers/12#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/12#v14)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, If her father had but spit in her face, should she not be ashamed seven days? let her be shut out from the camp seven days, and after that let her be received in again.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/12#v15)</sup> And Miriam was shut out from the camp seven days: and the people journeyed not till Miriam was brought in again.

    Let her be shut out from the camp seven days, and after that received in again; the leper dwells without the camp.

**[Leviticus 13:47-52](/bible/leviticus/13#v47)**

> <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/13#v47)</sup> The garment also that the plague of leprosy is in, whether it be a woollen garment, or a linen garment;
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/leviticus/13#v48)</sup> Whether it be in the warp, or woof; of linen, or of woollen; whether in a skin, or in any thing made of skin;
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/leviticus/13#v49)</sup> And if the plague be greenish or reddish in the garment, or in the skin, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin; it is a plague of leprosy, and shall be shewed unto the priest:
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/leviticus/13#v50)</sup> And the priest shall look upon the plague, and shut up it that hath the plague seven days:
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/leviticus/13#v51)</sup> And he shall look on the plague on the seventh day: if the plague be spread in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in a skin, or in any work that is made of skin; the plague is a fretting leprosy; it is unclean.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/leviticus/13#v52)</sup> He shall therefore burn that garment, whether warp or woof, in woollen or in linen, or any thing of skin, wherein the plague is: for it is a fretting leprosy; it shall be burnt in the fire.

- The plague of leprosy can be in a garment, woollen or linen, in the warp or woof or in any thing made of skin, greenish or reddish; it is shewed unto the priest and shut up seven days, and if the plague be spread in the garment it is a fretting leprosy: the garment is burnt in the fire. As with Miriam, the clothing wherein the plague is had to be dealt with.

**[Leviticus 13:53-59](/bible/leviticus/13#v53)**

> <sup>[53](/bible/leviticus/13#v53)</sup> And if the priest shall look, and, behold, the plague be not spread in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin;
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/leviticus/13#v54)</sup> Then the priest shall command that they wash the thing wherein the plague is, and he shall shut it up seven days more:
>
> <sup>[55](/bible/leviticus/13#v55)</sup> And the priest shall look on the plague, after that it is washed: and, behold, if the plague have not changed his colour, and the plague be not spread; it is unclean; thou shalt burn it in the fire; it is fret inward, whether it be bare within or without.
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/leviticus/13#v56)</sup> And if the priest look, and, behold, the plague be somewhat dark after the washing of it; then he shall rend it out of the garment, or out of the skin, or out of the warp, or out of the woof:
>
> <sup>[57](/bible/leviticus/13#v57)</sup> And if it appear still in the garment, either in the warp, or in the woof, or in any thing of skin; it is a spreading plague: thou shalt burn that wherein the plague is with fire.
>
> <sup>[58](/bible/leviticus/13#v58)</sup> And the garment, either warp, or woof, or whatsoever thing of skin it be, which thou shalt wash, if the plague be departed from them, then it shall be washed the second time, and shall be clean.
>
> <sup>[59](/bible/leviticus/13#v59)</sup> This is the law of the plague of leprosy in a garment of woollen or linen, either in the warp, or woof, or any thing of skins, to pronounce it clean, or to pronounce it unclean.

- If the plague be not spread, the thing is washed and shut up seven days more; if the plague have not changed his colour it is burnt, and if it be somewhat dark after washing, the piece is rent out; if it appear still, it is a spreading plague and burnt with fire; and the garment that the plague departs from is washed the second time and shall be clean. This is the law of the plague of leprosy in a garment, to pronounce it clean or to pronounce it unclean.
