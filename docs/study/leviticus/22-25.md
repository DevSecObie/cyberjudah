## Introduction

This reading covers [Leviticus 22](/bible/leviticus/22) through 25: the holy things of the priests and the law of offerings without blemish, the feasts of the Lord, the pure oil and the shewbread and the judgment of the blasphemer, and the sabbath of the land with the year of jubile. All four chapters were read, with precepts brought in along the way and questions answered after the reading.

**[Leviticus 22:1-3](/bible/leviticus/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/22#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/22#v2)</sup> Speak unto Aaron and to his sons, that they separate themselves from the holy things of the children of Israel, and that they profane not my holy name in those things which they hallow unto me: I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/22#v3)</sup> Say unto them, Whosoever he be of all your seed among your generations, that goeth unto the holy things, which the children of Israel hallow unto the Lord, having his uncleanness upon him, that soul shall be cut off from my presence: I am the Lord.

- The Levitical priesthood was established in Aaron and his sons. Certain things were consecrated that could not be touched by the rest of Israel, and a priest with his uncleanness upon him could not go unto the holy things, lest that soul be cut off from the presence of the Most High.
- Aaron was consecrated and anointed to minister in the priest's office, and his sons were clothed and anointed as their father, an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 40:13-15](/bible/exodus/40#v13)**

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/40#v14)</sup> And thou shalt bring his sons, and clothe them with coats:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/40#v15)</sup> And thou shalt anoint them, as thou didst anoint their father, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office: for their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.

    Aaron and his sons were anointed and sanctified to minister in the priest's office, an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/22#v4)</sup> What man soever of the seed of Aaron is a leper, or hath a running issue; he shall not eat of the holy things, until he be clean. And whoso toucheth any thing that is unclean by the dead, or a man whose seed goeth from him;
>

>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/22#v7)</sup> And when the sun is down, he shall be clean, and shall afterward eat of the holy things; because it is his food.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/22#v8)</sup> That which dieth of itself, or is torn with beasts, he shall not eat to defile himself therewith: I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/22#v9)</sup> They shall therefore keep mine ordinance, lest they bear sin for it, and die therefore, if they profane it: I the Lord do sanctify them.

- A man of the seed of Aaron that was a leper, had a running issue, or touched any unclean thing could not eat of the holy things until he was clean: he washed his flesh with water, and when the sun was down he was clean and could afterward eat.
- That which dieth of itself, or is torn with beasts, he shall not eat to defile himself therewith. That same law elsewhere says to give such a thing unto the stranger or sell it to an alien, for thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God: these laws were given to Israel alone, and the other nations may eat those things because they are not the holy people. There is no equality in that.

Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

    The thing that dieth of itself may be given to the stranger or sold to an alien, for thou art an holy people; the law marks the difference between Israel and the nations.

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/22#v10)</sup> There shall no stranger eat of the holy thing: a sojourner of the priest, or an hired servant, shall not eat of the holy thing.

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/22#v14)</sup> And if a man eat of the holy thing unwittingly, then he shall put the fifth part thereof unto it, and shall give it unto the priest with the holy thing.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/22#v15)</sup> And they shall not profane the holy things of the children of Israel, which they offer unto the Lord;
>

- No stranger may eat of the holy thing, neither a sojourner of the priest nor an hired servant. But the soul the priest buys with his money, and he that is born in his house, they eat of his meat, because they are joined to the priest's household.
- The priest's daughter married unto a stranger may not eat of the offering of the holy things; but if she is a widow or divorced and has no child, and is returned unto her father's house as in her youth, she eats of her father's meat, because she returns to her father's covering.
- A woman was commonly under her father's covering until she came under a husband's. The independent woman was not birthed from the Bible; she was birthed from Babylon.
- He that eats of the holy thing unwittingly adds the fifth part unto it and gives it to the priest with the holy thing; the priests must not suffer the children of Israel to bear the iniquity of trespass when they eat their holy things.

- Neither from a stranger's hand shall ye offer the bread of your God of any of these, because their corruption is in them and blemishes be in them; they shall not be accepted for you.

# Leviticus 23: The Feasts of the Lord

Read the chapter → [Leviticus 23](/bible/leviticus/23)

**[Leviticus 23:1-3](/bible/leviticus/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/23#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/23#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, Concerning the feasts of the Lord, which ye shall proclaim to be holy convocations, even these are my feasts.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/23#v3)</sup> Six days shall work be done: but the seventh day is the sabbath of rest, an holy convocation; ye shall do no work therein: it is the sabbath of the Lord in all your dwellings.

- These are the feasts of the Lord, holy convocations which He Himself proclaimed. The Most High gave us holy days; a holiday not listed in this Bible and not ordained of the Most High has nothing holy about it. By the knowledge of the Lord the days were distinguished: some He made high days, as the Passover, Tabernacles, the new moon, and the Sabbath, and the days He did not name are ordinary days.
- Six days shall work be done, but the seventh day is the sabbath of rest, an holy convocation. No matter where your dwelling is, Israel, North America, London, or Australia, the Sabbath is still the Sabbath: Friday sundown to Saturday sundown in whatever time zone you are in.
- Keeping the Sabbath holy is more than no working. Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day, so there is no cooking on the Sabbath; and if the people of the land bring ware or victuals on the sabbath day to sell, we would not buy it of them, so there is no buying and no selling.
- It is a holy convocation, which means a gathering. Keeping the Sabbath sitting at home with your gerbil or your hamster is not keeping the Sabbath; you have to come out of your comfort zone and congregate.

**[Leviticus 23:4-8](/bible/leviticus/23#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/23#v4)</sup> These are the feasts of the Lord, even holy convocations, which ye shall proclaim in their seasons.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/23#v5)</sup> In the fourteenth day of the first month at even is the Lord’s passover.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/23#v6)</sup> And on the fifteenth day of the same month is the feast of unleavened bread unto the Lord: seven days ye must eat unleavened bread.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/23#v7)</sup> In the first day ye shall have an holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work therein.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/23#v8)</sup> But ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the Lord seven days: in the seventh day is an holy convocation: ye shall do no servile work therein.

- In the fourteenth day of the first month at even is the Lord's passover. The fourteenth day at even means the fifteenth day, so the verse about the fifteenth day is saying the same thing: the first day of the Passover is also the first day of the feast of unleavened bread.
- On the first day and on the seventh day is an holy convocation: no buying, no selling, no servile work. But you can cook on the Passover, because there is no Passover without the Passover meal, the lamb, unleavened bread, and bitter herbs.
- On the last day of unleavened bread you may also cook, unless it falls on the seventh day Sabbath. The only time you can cook on the seventh day Sabbath is when the Passover falls on that day, because you cannot have the Passover without the lamb roast with fire; no other high holy day gives you a set meal, and no other feast overrides the Sabbath cooking prohibition.

**[Leviticus 23:15-21](/bible/leviticus/23#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/23#v15)</sup> And ye shall count unto you from the morrow after the sabbath, from the day that ye brought the sheaf of the wave offering; seven sabbaths shall be complete:

>

>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/23#v19)</sup> Then ye shall sacrifice one kid of the goats for a sin offering, and two lambs of the first year for a sacrifice of peace offerings.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/23#v20)</sup> And the priest shall wave them with the bread of the firstfruits for a wave offering before the Lord, with the two lambs: they shall be holy to the Lord for the priest.

- From the morrow after the sabbath, from the day the sheaf of the wave offering was brought, count seven sabbaths complete, seven times seven which is forty and nine, and the morrow after the seventh sabbath is the fiftieth day. That is why the feast is called Pentecost, from fifty, as a pentagon has five sides; it is also called the feast of weeks, because of the seven weeks that are counted.
- With the two wave loaves came burnt offerings, a sin offering, and peace offerings, meat offerings and drink offerings. That is what Colossians is talking about when it says let no man judge you in meat or in drink or in respect of an holyday: those who did not believe in Christ condemned the followers of Christ as wicked for not sacrificing on the holy days. We still keep the holy days, but we do not offer the offerings, because the body of Christ has been given.
- The meat offerings and drink offerings and carnal ordinances were a figure for the time then present, imposed until the time of reformation; they could not make him that did the service perfect as pertaining to the conscience. But Christ being come, an high priest of good things to come, Christ is now in place of all those oblations and sacrifices.

**[Leviticus 23:23-25](/bible/leviticus/23#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/23#v23)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/23#v24)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, In the seventh month, in the first day of the month, shall ye have a sabbath, a memorial of blowing of trumpets, an holy convocation.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/23#v25)</sup> Ye shall do no servile work therein: but ye shall offer an offering made by fire unto the Lord.

- In the seventh month, in the first day of the month, is a sabbath, a memorial of blowing of trumpets. The new moon starts the new month, so the memorial of blowing of trumpets is a new moon; the seventh new moon is the special one, and that one is called the memorial of blowing of trumpets.
- The Most High made the moon to serve in her season for a declaration of times: from the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreaseth in her perfection. The moon is needed to calculate the feasts; the month is called after her name, because the new moon starts the new month, increasing wonderfully in her changing through the phases.
- But the moon has nothing to do with the seventh day Sabbath. The day starts at evening, for the evening and the morning were the first day; darkness was here first and has seniority. The moon was not even created on the first day of the week but on the fourth day, and the heavens and the earth were finished and the Most High still rested on the seventh day. The seven day rotation has run since the first day and nothing changes it; the Sabbath will always be Friday sundown to Saturday sundown.
- If the new moon falls on a seventh day Sabbath there is absolutely no cooking; the only day that permits cooking on the Sabbath is the Passover.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 43:6-8](/bible/sirach/43#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/43#v6)</sup> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.

> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/25#v34)</sup> But the field of the suburbs of their cities may not be sold; for it is their perpetual possession.

- If a man sell a dwelling house in a walled city, he may redeem it within a whole year after it is sold; if it be not redeemed within the space of a full year, the house in the walled city is established for ever to him that bought it. But the houses of the villages which have no wall are counted as the fields of the country: they may be redeemed, and they go out in the jubile.
- Notwithstanding, the cities of the Levites, the houses of the cities of their possession, may the Levites redeem at any time, for the houses of the cities of the Levites are their possession among the children of Israel. Levi was not given any land; his portion was the offerings of the Lord, and the fields of the suburbs of their cities are their perpetual possession and may not be sold.

**[Leviticus 25:35-38](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)</sup> And if thy brother be waxen poor, and fallen in decay with thee; then thou shalt relieve him: yea, though he be a stranger, or a sojourner; that he may live with thee.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/25#v36)</sup> Take thou no usury of him, or increase: but fear thy God; that thy brother may live with thee.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/leviticus/25#v37)</sup> Thou shalt not give him thy money upon usury, nor lend him thy victuals for increase.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/leviticus/25#v38)</sup> I am the Lord your God, which brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan, and to be your God.

- If thy brother be waxen poor and fallen in decay with thee, then thou shalt relieve him, yea, though he be a stranger or a sojourner, that he may live with thee. The word stranger does not always mean a heathen; sometimes it is your own brother sojourning away from his land, and the context of the verse tells you which.
- Thy brother is thy neighbour and the children of thy people: thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. Brother, neighbour, and children of thy people all mean the same thing.
- Take thou no usury of him, or increase, but fear thy God, that thy brother may live with thee. Usury is interest, a fee accumulating over time; thou shalt not give him thy money upon usury, nor lend him thy victuals for increase. Lending for the opportunity to make a profit off your own people is evil.
- I am the Lord your God, which brought you forth out of the land of Egypt, to give you the land of Canaan. The Most High is not the God of everybody: to bring Israel out He judged Egypt, and to give Israel Canaan He cast out the seven nations, greater and mightier than Israel, delivered them before Israel to be smitten, with no covenant and no mercy shewed, during that time period.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 19:17-18](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

    Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself; brother, neighbour, and children of thy people are the same.
  - **[Isaiah 43:3-4](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)</sup> For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour: I gave Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/43#v4)</sup> Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life.

    I gave Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee; since thou wast precious in my sight, I have loved thee, therefore will I give men for thee. A ransom secures the safe exit of the victim; the Most High gave Egypt and Ethiopia their own lives to bring Israel out.
  - **[Deuteronomy 7:1-2](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v1)</sup> When the Lord thy God shall bring thee into the land whither thou goest to possess it, and hath cast out many nations before thee, the Hittites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, seven nations greater and mightier than thou;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v2)</sup> And when the Lord thy God shall deliver them before thee; thou shalt smite them, and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them:

    Seven nations greater and mightier than Israel were cast out and delivered before Israel to be utterly destroyed, with no covenant made and no mercy shewed; that is how the Most High gave the land of Canaan.

**[Leviticus 25:39-43](/bible/leviticus/25#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/leviticus/25#v39)</sup> And if thy brother that dwelleth by thee be waxen poor, and be sold unto thee; thou shalt not compel him to serve as a bondservant:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/25#v40)</sup> But as an hired servant, and as a sojourner, he shall be with thee, and shall serve thee unto the year of jubile:
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/leviticus/25#v41)</sup> And then shall he depart from thee, both he and his children with him, and shall return unto his own family, and unto the possession of his fathers shall he return.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/leviticus/25#v42)</sup> For they are my servants, which I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: they shall not be sold as bondmen.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/leviticus/25#v43)</sup> Thou shalt not rule over him with rigour; but shalt fear thy God.

- If thy brother that dwelleth by thee be waxen poor and be sold unto thee, thou shalt not compel him to serve as a bondservant, but as an hired servant and as a sojourner he shall be with thee, and shall serve thee unto the year of jubile; then shall he depart from thee, both he and his children with him, and shall return unto his own family and unto the possession of his fathers.
- The Hebrew servant serves six years, and in the seventh he goes out free for nothing; if he came in by himself he goes out by himself, and if he were married his wife goes out with him. That seventh year of release is that servant's jubile: a servant taken in 2012 goes out free in 2019, and one taken in 2019 goes out free in 2026.
- For they are my servants, which I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: they shall not be sold as bondmen. Thou shalt not rule over him with rigour, but shalt fear thy God; deal with him properly, for the Most High judges both the poor man and the one he serves.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 21:2-3](/bible/exodus/21#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/21#v2)</sup> If thou buy an Hebrew servant, six years he shall serve: and in the seventh he shall go out free for nothing.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/21#v3)</sup> If he came in by himself, he shall go out by himself: if he were married, then his wife shall go out with him.

    If thou buy an Hebrew servant, six years he shall serve, and in the seventh he shall go out free for nothing; the brother sold to thee goes out at the release.

**[Leviticus 25:44-46](/bible/leviticus/25#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/25#v44)</sup> Both thy bondmen, and thy bondmaids, which thou shalt have, shall be of the heathen that are round about you; of them shall ye buy bondmen and bondmaids.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/25#v45)</sup> Moreover of the children of the strangers that do sojourn among you, of them shall ye buy, and of their families that are with you, which they begat in your land: and they shall be your possession.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/25#v46)</sup> And ye shall take them as an inheritance for your children after you, to inherit them for a possession; they shall be your bondmen for ever: but over your brethren the children of Israel, ye shall not rule one over another with rigour.

- Both thy bondmen and thy bondmaids, which thou shalt have, shall be of the heathen that are round about you; of them shall ye buy bondmen and bondmaids, and of the children of the strangers that do sojourn among you, and they shall be your possession.
- Ye shall take them as an inheritance for your children after you, to inherit them for a possession; they shall be your bondmen for ever. But over your brethren the children of Israel, ye shall not rule one over another with rigour.

**[Leviticus 25:47-55](/bible/leviticus/25#v47)**

> <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/25#v47)</sup> And if a sojourner or stranger wax rich by thee, and thy brother that dwelleth by him wax poor, and sell himself unto the stranger or sojourner by thee, or to the stock of the stranger’s family:
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/leviticus/25#v48)</sup> After that he is sold he may be redeemed again; one of his brethren may redeem him:
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/leviticus/25#v49)</sup> Either his uncle, or his uncle’s son, may redeem him, or any that is nigh of kin unto him of his family may redeem him; or if he be able, he may redeem himself.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/leviticus/25#v50)</sup> And he shall reckon with him that bought him from the year that he was sold to him unto the year of jubile: and the price of his sale shall be according unto the number of years, according to the time of an hired servant shall it be with him.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/leviticus/25#v51)</sup> If there be yet many years behind, according unto them he shall give again the price of his redemption out of the money that he was bought for.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/leviticus/25#v52)</sup> And if there remain but few years unto the year of jubile, then he shall count with him, and according unto his years shall he give him again the price of his redemption.
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/leviticus/25#v53)</sup> And as a yearly hired servant shall he be with him: and the other shall not rule with rigour over him in thy sight.
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/leviticus/25#v54)</sup> And if he be not redeemed in these years, then he shall go out in the year of jubile, both he, and his children with him.
>
> <sup>[55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)</sup> For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

- If a sojourner or stranger wax rich by thee, and thy brother that dwelleth by him wax poor and sell himself unto the stranger, after that he is sold he may be redeemed again: one of his brethren may redeem him, either his uncle, or his uncle's son, or any that is nigh of kin unto him of his family; or if he be able, he may redeem himself, working as an hired servant and earning the price of his own freedom.
- Here again redeem means buy, as in buying a man out of slavery back to the position of freedom; and the price of his redemption was reckoned with him that bought him, according to the number of years from the year he was sold unto the year of jubile, as the time of an hired servant. If he be not redeemed in these years, then he shall go out in the year of jubile, both he and his children with him.
- For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God. Everybody is not the servant of the Most High; the children of Israel are His servants, and this teaching identifies them today as the so-called Blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans.
