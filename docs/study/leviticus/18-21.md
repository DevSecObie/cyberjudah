## Introduction

This reading covers [Leviticus 18](/bible/leviticus/18) through 21: the unlawful beds forbidden after the doings of Egypt and Canaan, the statutes of holiness for everyday life, the judgments appointed for the abominations, and the holiness required of the priests, the sons of Aaron. All four chapters were read and taught.

**[Leviticus 18:1-3](/bible/leviticus/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/18#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/18#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3)</sup> After the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do: and after the doings of the land of Canaan, whither I bring you, shall ye not do: neither shall ye walk in their ordinances.

- In Egypt the children of Israel served the gods of Egypt, dressed like Egyptians, and were fully engulfed in the Egyptian way of life; Moses himself was raised as an Egyptian in the house of Pharaoh. The Most High told them to leave all of that back in Egypt.
- The same word stands for us: when the Most High redeems us out of this captivity, many of the customs we learned here will be cut off, because they are not coming into the kingdom.
- The land of Canaan was full of idols. If Israel obeyed His voice the Most High would be an enemy to their enemies, bless their bread and water, and take sickness away; the charge in Canaan was not to bow to their gods nor do after their works, but to utterly overthrow them and break down their images.
- Coming out of Egypt required a transformed mind: not conformed to the way of life of Egypt or Canaan, but renewed to the commandments of the Most High.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 23:22-25](/bible/exodus/23#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/23#v22)</sup> But if thou shalt indeed obey his voice, and do all that I speak; then I will be an enemy unto thine enemies, and an adversary unto thine adversaries.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/23#v23)</sup> For mine Angel shall go before thee, and bring thee in unto the Amorites, and the Hittites, and the Perizzites, and the Canaanites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites: and I will cut them off.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/23#v24)</sup> Thou shalt not bow down to their gods, nor serve them, nor do after their works: but thou shalt utterly overthrow them, and quite break down their images.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/23#v25)</sup> And ye shall serve the Lord your God, and he shall bless thy bread, and thy water; and I will take sickness away from the midst of thee.

    Obey His voice and He is an enemy to thine enemies; the nations of Canaan and their gods were to be overthrown, not learned from.
  - **[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

    Be not conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind.

**[Leviticus 18:4-6](/bible/leviticus/18#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/18#v4)</sup> Ye shall do my judgments, and keep mine ordinances, to walk therein: I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/18#v5)</sup> Ye shall therefore keep my statutes, and my judgments: which if a man do, he shall live in them: I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/18#v6)</sup> None of you shall approach to any that is near of kin to him, to uncover their nakedness: I am the Lord.

- Keep the statutes and judgments of the Most High, which if a man do, he shall live in them.
- The Ten Commandments are not the whole of the law but a summary. Under the Sabbath commandment sit sub-statutes such as no buying, no selling, no working; likewise under the law of adultery sit all the laws of this chapter, the ordinances of marriage and the forbidden beds.
- None of you shall approach to any that is near of kin to uncover their nakedness; these laws name who a man may not lay down with.

**[Leviticus 18:7-8](/bible/leviticus/18#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/18#v7)</sup> The nakedness of thy father, or the nakedness of thy mother, shalt thou not uncover: she is thy mother; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/18#v8)</sup> The nakedness of thy father’s wife shalt thou not uncover: it is thy father’s nakedness.

- The nakedness of thy father's wife is thy father's nakedness; that is the stepmother. The wicked mind reasons that she is not the biological mother, so it is fine to lust after her; the scripture says no, that is wicked.
- This very sin was reported among the Corinthians, a fornication not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father's wife. The law had already forbidden it, and it was being done in Egypt and Canaan; that is why the Most High opened the chapter the way He did.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 5:1](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v1)</sup> It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife.

    Fornication that one should have his father's wife; the law of Leviticus 18:8 was being broken in the days of the apostles.

**[Leviticus 18:9-11](/bible/leviticus/18#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/18#v9)</sup> The nakedness of thy sister, the daughter of thy father, or daughter of thy mother, whether she be born at home, or born abroad, even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/18#v10)</sup> The nakedness of thy son’s daughter, or of thy daughter’s daughter, even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover: for theirs is thine own nakedness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/18#v11)</sup> The nakedness of thy father’s wife’s daughter, begotten of thy father, she is thy sister, thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.

- The sister, whether born at home or born abroad, and the son's daughter and the daughter's daughter are all forbidden; the grandchildren's nakedness is thine own nakedness.
- There are sick spirits out there, and some of them are grandfathers, uncles, and fathers. Be careful and mindful who you leave your children around; do not hand your children to just anybody for the sake of your own pleasure.

**[Leviticus 18:12-14](/bible/leviticus/18#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/18#v12)</sup> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s sister: she is thy father’s near kinswoman.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/18#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy mother’s sister: for she is thy mother’s near kinswoman.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/18#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy father’s brother, thou shalt not approach to his wife: she is thine aunt.

- The father's sister, the mother's sister, and the wife of the father's brother, thine aunt, are all near kinswomen and off limits.
- Incest is still practiced among the nations of this land, and children born of incestuous unions often carry defects and disabilities; the law guards the seed.

**[Leviticus 18:15-18](/bible/leviticus/18#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/18#v15)</sup> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy daughter in law: she is thy son’s wife; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/18#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of thy brother’s wife: it is thy brother’s nakedness.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/18#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not uncover the nakedness of a woman and her daughter, neither shalt thou take her son’s daughter, or her daughter’s daughter, to uncover her nakedness; for they are her near kinswomen: it is wickedness.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/18#v18)</sup> Neither shalt thou take a wife to her sister, to vex her, to uncover her nakedness, beside the other in her life time.

- The daughter in law, the brother's wife, and a woman with her daughter or her granddaughter are all forbidden; it is wickedness.
- Neither take a wife to her sister to vex her, to uncover her nakedness beside the other in her lifetime; that also shuts down what the world today calls threesomes.

**[Leviticus 18:19](/bible/leviticus/18#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/18#v19)</sup> Also thou shalt not approach unto a woman to uncover her nakedness, as long as she is put apart for her uncleanness.

- A woman put apart for her uncleanness is a woman in her menstrual cycle. A man must wait the appointed seven days, and if the issue runs beyond, wait again; [Leviticus 15](/bible/leviticus/15) details these laws of uncleanness, taught in the previous class.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 15:19](/bible/leviticus/15#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/15#v19)</sup> And if a woman have an issue, and her issue in her flesh be blood, she shall be put apart seven days: and whosoever toucheth her shall be unclean until the even.

    The law of her separation: she shall be put apart seven days.

**[Leviticus 18:20](/bible/leviticus/18#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/18#v20)</sup> Moreover thou shalt not lie carnally with thy neighbour’s wife, to defile thyself with her.

- To lie carnally with thy neighbour's wife is adultery. This world even sings songs celebrating adultery; the law forbids defiling thyself with her.

**[Leviticus 18:21](/bible/leviticus/18#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/18#v21)</sup> And thou shalt not let any of thy seed pass through the fire to Molech, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

- Passing seed through the fire to Molech was child sacrifice: the nations slew their newborn children and offered them to that god, and Israel learned it from them, as Ezekiel testifies of Jerusalem slaying the children of the Most High.
- The abortions of today are no different. The Most High is bringing spirits into the earth, and the child is slain so the parents can go on living as they please; that is the same sacrifice unto Molech, and it is wickedness.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 16:20-21](/bible/ezekiel/16#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/16#v20)</sup> Moreover thou hast taken thy sons and thy daughters, whom thou hast borne unto me, and these hast thou sacrificed unto them to be devoured. Is this of thy whoredoms a small matter,
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/16#v21)</sup> That thou hast slain my children, and delivered them to cause them to pass through the fire for them?

    Thou hast slain my children and delivered them to cause them to pass through the fire; that is what giving seed to Molech means.

**[Leviticus 18:22](/bible/leviticus/18#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/18#v22)</sup> Thou shalt not lie with mankind, as with womankind: it is abomination.

- Abomination means a thing that causes disgust or hatred. When a man lies with mankind as with womankind, the Most High calls it disgusting and hateful.
- What a man hates and finds disgusting he removes far from himself; so we must teach our people to repent and come out of the things the Most High hates.

**[Leviticus 18:23](/bible/leviticus/18#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/18#v23)</sup> Neither shalt thou lie with any beast to defile thyself therewith: neither shall any woman stand before a beast to lie down thereto: it is confusion.

- Lying with any beast is confusion, for man and for woman alike. The nations have set up places where these strange lusts are fulfilled, and the sin is found among every people; it is all wickedness.

**[Leviticus 18:24-30](/bible/leviticus/18#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/18#v24)</sup> Defile not ye yourselves in any of these things: for in all these the nations are defiled which I cast out before you:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/18#v25)</sup> And the land is defiled: therefore I do visit the iniquity thereof upon it, and the land itself vomiteth out her inhabitants.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/18#v26)</sup> Ye shall therefore keep my statutes and my judgments, and shall not commit any of these abominations; neither any of your own nation, nor any stranger that sojourneth among you:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/18#v27)</sup> (For all these abominations have the men of the land done, which were before you, and the land is defiled;)
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/18#v28)</sup> That the land spue not you out also, when ye defile it, as it spued out the nations that were before you.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/18#v29)</sup> For whosoever shall commit any of these abominations, even the souls that commit them shall be cut off from among their people.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/18#v30)</sup> Therefore shall ye keep mine ordinance, that ye commit not any one of these abominable customs, which were committed before you, and that ye defile not yourselves therein: I am the Lord your God.

- In all these things the nations were defiled which the Most High cast out; the Egyptians and the Canaanites were doing these very abominations, and the land itself was defiled by them.
- The land spews out the defiled. That is why Israel was put out of Jerusalem: we could not abide in the land with our sins, sacrificing to idol gods and working all manner of evil, so the Most High raised up an enemy to carry us into bondage and remove us out of the land.
- Whosoever commits any of these abominations, the souls that commit them shall be cut off from among their people, meaning put to death.

# Leviticus 19: Ye Shall Be Holy, for I the Lord Your God Am Holy

Read the chapter → [Leviticus 19](/bible/leviticus/19)

**[Leviticus 19:1-3](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/19#v2)</sup> Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy: for I the Lord your God am holy.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/19#v3)</sup> Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the Lord your God.

- Ye shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy; this word went to all the congregation of the children of Israel.
- Fear every man his mother and his father goes back to the commandment, honour thy father and thy mother.
- Honour can be kept even at a distance: show respect, and do nothing that would bring shame on your mother. But there is a balance; if she asks for what is against the commandments of the Most High, the commandments come first.
- Keep my sabbaths: the feast days are Sabbaths also. The Passover, the first and last days of the feast of Tabernacles, the day of atonement, and the memorial of blowing of trumpets are all Sabbaths; no servile work, no buying and selling, though cooking is permitted on the feast days.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 20:12](/bible/exodus/20#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/20#v12)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

    Honour thy father and thy mother; fearing them in Leviticus 19 is the same commandment.

**[Leviticus 19:4](/bible/leviticus/19#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/19#v4)</sup> Turn ye not unto idols, nor make to yourselves molten gods: I am the Lord your God.

- Turn not unto idols, nor make molten gods; this is the commandment against making any graven image.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 20:4](/bible/exodus/20#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/20#v4)</sup> Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:

    Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image.

**[Leviticus 19:5-8](/bible/leviticus/19#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/19#v5)</sup> And if ye offer a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the Lord, ye shall offer it at your own will.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/19#v6)</sup> It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if ought remain until the third day, it shall be burnt in the fire.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/19#v7)</sup> And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is abominable; it shall not be accepted.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/19#v8)</sup> Therefore every one that eateth it shall bear his iniquity, because he hath profaned the hallowed thing of the Lord: and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

- The peace offering was eaten the same day it was offered or on the morrow; whatever remained to the third day was burnt. Eaten on the third day it is abominable, and the eater bears his iniquity, for he has profaned the hallowed thing of the Lord.

**[Leviticus 19:9-10](/bible/leviticus/19#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/19#v9)</sup> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/19#v10)</sup> And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the Lord your God.

- The corners of the field, the gleanings of the harvest, and the leavings of the vineyard belonged to the poor and the stranger. The Most High is teaching, do not be selfish; love thy neighbour as thyself and look out for the poor of your people.
- He that provides not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, has denied the faith. Those of his own house are his wife and children; his own is his nation. Care for your people.
- Charge them that are rich that they be not highminded, but ready to distribute, willing to communicate. Most of us today have no fields or vineyards, but some have good jobs and blessed incomes; you cannot take it with you, so give of your substance. The scriptures also say no good can come to him that gives no alms.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

    Provide for thine own and specially for those of thine own house; the own is the nation, the house is the family.
  - **[1 Timothy 6:17-18](/bible/1-timothy/6#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/1-timothy/6#v17)</sup> Charge them that are rich in this world, that they be not highminded, nor trust in uncertain riches, but in the living God, who giveth us richly all things to enjoy;
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/1-timothy/6#v18)</sup> That they do good, that they be rich in good works, ready to distribute, willing to communicate;

    The rich are charged to be ready to distribute, willing to communicate; the law of the gleanings teaches the same giving.

**[Leviticus 19:11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

- Dealing falsely is any dishonesty: skewing the truth to get your way, or cheating.
- Be honest. Have an honest job so you do not steal; run an honest business so you do not cheat people with foul practices, selling goods you know will fail or charging a customer for repairs he does not need. That honesty is owed even to the other nations.

**[Leviticus 19:12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)</sup> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

- Do not swear by His name falsely. Saying I swear to God over a lie profanes the name of the Most High.
- Christ said swear not at all, neither by heaven, nor by the earth, nor by thy head; let your communication be yea, yea and nay, nay. The only reason a man swears is that he has lied before and no one believes him; in your repentance take swearing out of your speech, and do not put oaths on your own head or your family either.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:33-37](/bible/matthew/5#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/5#v33)</sup> Again, ye have heard that it hath been said by them of old time, Thou shalt not forswear thyself, but shalt perform unto the Lord thine oaths:
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/5#v34)</sup> But I say unto you, Swear not at all; neither by heaven; for it is God’s throne:
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/5#v35)</sup> Nor by the earth; for it is his footstool: neither by Jerusalem; for it is the city of the great King.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/5#v36)</sup> Neither shalt thou swear by thy head, because thou canst not make one hair white or black.
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)</sup> But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

    Swear not at all; let your communication be yea, yea and nay, nay, for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

**[Leviticus 19:13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

- The wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning: when a man works for you, pay him that same day. Holding back the wage is defrauding thy neighbour, so businesses in Israel should pay their people the way the scriptures say.
- If both parties agree beforehand to payment the next day, that agreement is between you, the Lord, and that man; the scripture states the standard.

**[Leviticus 19:14-15](/bible/leviticus/19#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/19#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

- Curse not the deaf, put no stumblingblock before the blind, but fear thy God.
- Do no unrighteousness in judgment; respect not the person of the poor nor honour the person of the mighty. The courts of this land do the opposite: our people receive far harsher sentences than others for the same crime with the same priors, sometimes before the very same judge.

**[Leviticus 19:16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

- A talebearer revealeth secrets, but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter. What a brother or sister tells you in confidence is not yours to spread; carrying it to the next person is talebearing, and it happens even in this truth.
- Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: much strife and contention comes from messy people. Repent of that messy spirit; it has no place in Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 11:13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)</sup> A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.

    A talebearer revealeth secrets; the faithful spirit conceals the matter.
  - **[Proverbs 26:20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)</sup> Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth.

    Where no talebearer is, the strife ceaseth; messy people feed the fire.

**[Leviticus 19:17-18](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

- Hate not thy brother in thine heart; rebuke thy neighbour and suffer not sin upon him. If you love your brother or sister you correct them when they are going off into sin; that is real love.
- Love thy neighbour as thyself: thy neighbour is the children of thy people. Get that right, against the teaching that makes the neighbour everybody.

**[Leviticus 19:19](/bible/leviticus/19#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/19#v19)</sup> Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee.

- Keep the statutes: no gendering cattle with a diverse kind, no sowing the field with mingled seed, and no garment mingled of linen and woollen; that is the law against mixed fabrics.

**[Leviticus 19:20-22](/bible/leviticus/19#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/19#v20)</sup> And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/19#v21)</sup> And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the Lord, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, even a ram for a trespass offering.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/19#v22)</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for him with the ram of the trespass offering before the Lord for his sin which he hath done: and the sin which he hath done shall be forgiven him.

- The bondmaid betrothed to an husband and not redeemed was scourged, not put to death, because she was not free; the man brought a ram for a trespass offering, and the priest made atonement, and the sin was forgiven him.

**[Leviticus 19:23-25](/bible/leviticus/19#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/19#v23)</sup> And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised: three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you: it shall not be eaten of.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/19#v24)</sup> But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy to praise the Lord withal.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/19#v25)</sup> And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am the Lord your God.

- The priests could not take a wife that was a whore, or profane, or put away from her husband; they were held to an even higher standard, for the priest is holy unto his God.
- The daughter of any priest that profaned herself by playing the whore profaned her father and was burnt with fire. Under the law it was straight death, and we are all guilty of these things; this is why Christ had to come. Thank the Most High for the blood of Christ, that we can repent, correct ourselves, and keep moving, for a just man falleth and riseth up again.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 24:16](/bible/proverbs/24#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/24#v16)</sup> For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.

    A just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again; under Christ there is room to repent and stand back up.

**[Leviticus 21:10-12](/bible/leviticus/21#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/21#v10)</sup> And he that is the high priest among his brethren, upon whose head the anointing oil was poured, and that is consecrated to put on the garments, shall not uncover his head, nor rend his clothes;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/21#v11)</sup> Neither shall he go in to any dead body, nor defile himself for his father, or for his mother;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/21#v12)</sup> Neither shall he go out of the sanctuary, nor profane the sanctuary of his God; for the crown of the anointing oil of his God is upon him: I am the Lord.

- The high priest, upon whose head the anointing oil was poured, was not to uncover his head nor rend his clothes, nor go in to any dead body, nor go out of the sanctuary; the crown of the anointing oil of his God was upon him. Under the old covenant his head was covered with the mitre.
- When Christ died the veil of the temple was rent and the priesthood was changed; the high priest now is Christ, for it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda. The sons of Levi no longer carry that office; Judah carries this work to the kingdom.

    The veil of the temple was rent in twain when Christ died.

**[Leviticus 21:13-15](/bible/leviticus/21#v13)**

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/21#v14)</sup> A widow, or a divorced woman, or profane, or an harlot, these shall he not take: but he shall take a virgin of his own people to wife.
>

- The high priest took a wife in her virginity: not a widow, nor a divorced woman, nor profane, nor an harlot, but a virgin of his own people, that he profane not his seed among his people.

**[Leviticus 21:16-24](/bible/leviticus/21#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/21#v16)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/21#v17)</sup> Speak unto Aaron, saying, Whosoever he be of thy seed in their generations that hath any blemish, let him not approach to offer the bread of his God.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/21#v18)</sup> For whatsoever man he be that hath a blemish, he shall not approach: a blind man, or a lame, or he that hath a flat nose, or any thing superfluous,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/21#v19)</sup> Or a man that is brokenfooted, or brokenhanded,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/21#v20)</sup> Or crookbackt, or a dwarf, or that hath a blemish in his eye, or be scurvy, or scabbed, or hath his stones broken;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/21#v21)</sup> No man that hath a blemish of the seed of Aaron the priest shall come nigh to offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire: he hath a blemish; he shall not come nigh to offer the bread of his God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/21#v22)</sup> He shall eat the bread of his God, both of the most holy, and of the holy.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/21#v23)</sup> Only he shall not go in unto the vail, nor come nigh unto the altar, because he hath a blemish; that he profane not my sanctuaries: for I the Lord do sanctify them.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/21#v24)</sup> And Moses told it unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel.

- A blemish is a disability or birth defect: blind, lame in his walk, a flat nose, any thing superfluous, brokenfooted, brokenhanded, crookbackt, a dwarf, a blemish in the eye, scurvy, scabbed, or stones broken. No man of the seed of Aaron with a blemish could come nigh to offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire.
- Yet the blemished priest still ate the bread of his God, both of the most holy and of the holy; he was provided for, only he went not in unto the vail nor came nigh unto the altar, that the sanctuaries be not profaned.
- Moses told it unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel.
