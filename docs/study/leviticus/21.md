**[Leviticus 21:1-4](/bible/leviticus/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/21#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Speak unto the priests the sons of Aaron, and say unto them, There shall none be defiled for the dead among his people:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/21#v2)</sup> But for his kin, that is near unto him, that is, for his mother, and for his father, and for his son, and for his daughter, and for his brother,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/21#v3)</sup> And for his sister a virgin, that is nigh unto him, which hath had no husband; for her may he be defiled.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/21#v4)</sup> But he shall not defile himself, being a chief man among his people, to profane himself.

- The priests, the sons of Aaron, were not to be defiled for the dead among their people, except for near kin: mother, father, son, daughter, brother, and a virgin sister that had no husband.
- The priests had to remain clean to administer the priesthood, for anyone unclean that approached the sanctuary was put to death; a chief man among his people was not to profane himself.

**[Leviticus 21:5-6](/bible/leviticus/21#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/21#v5)</sup> They shall not make baldness upon their head, neither shall they shave off the corner of their beard, nor make any cuttings in their flesh.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/21#v6)</sup> They shall be holy unto their God, and not profane the name of their God: for the offerings of the Lord made by fire, and the bread of their God, they do offer: therefore they shall be holy.

- The priests were not to make baldness upon their head, nor shave off the corner of their beard, nor make any cuttings in their flesh; they were to be holy unto their God, for they offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire.
- Christians will read verse 1 and say this chapter speaks only to the priests, so shaving the head and beard has nothing to do with them. But go back to chapter 19: the Lord spake there to all the children of Israel and said the exact same thing, round not the corners of your head, neither mar the corners of thy beard. The law given to the priests here was already the law of the whole nation; do not isolate the priests to escape the commandment.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 19:1-2](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/19#v2)</sup> Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy: for I the Lord your God am holy.

    The words of chapter 19 went to all the congregation of the children of Israel, not to the priests only.
  - **[Leviticus 19:27](/bible/leviticus/19#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/19#v27)</sup> Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard.

    Round not the corners of your head, neither mar the corners of thy beard; the same law binds every man of Israel.

**[Leviticus 21:7-9](/bible/leviticus/21#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/21#v7)</sup> They shall not take a wife that is a whore, or profane; neither shall they take a woman put away from her husband: for he is holy unto his God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/21#v8)</sup> Thou shalt sanctify him therefore; for he offereth the bread of thy God: he shall be holy unto thee: for I the Lord, which sanctify you, am holy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/21#v9)</sup> And the daughter of any priest, if she profane herself by playing the whore, she profaneth her father: she shall be burnt with fire.

- The priests could not take a wife that was a whore, or profane, or put away from her husband; they were held to an even higher standard, for the priest is holy unto his God.
- The daughter of any priest that profaned herself by playing the whore profaned her father and was burnt with fire. Under the law it was straight death, and we are all guilty of these things; this is why Christ had to come. Thank the Most High for the blood of Christ, that we can repent, correct ourselves, and keep moving, for a just man falleth and riseth up again.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 24:16](/bible/proverbs/24#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/24#v16)</sup> For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.

    A just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again; under Christ there is room to repent and stand back up.

**[Leviticus 21:10-12](/bible/leviticus/21#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/21#v10)</sup> And he that is the high priest among his brethren, upon whose head the anointing oil was poured, and that is consecrated to put on the garments, shall not uncover his head, nor rend his clothes;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/21#v11)</sup> Neither shall he go in to any dead body, nor defile himself for his father, or for his mother;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/21#v12)</sup> Neither shall he go out of the sanctuary, nor profane the sanctuary of his God; for the crown of the anointing oil of his God is upon him: I am the Lord.

- The high priest, upon whose head the anointing oil was poured, was not to uncover his head nor rend his clothes, nor go in to any dead body, nor go out of the sanctuary; the crown of the anointing oil of his God was upon him. Under the old covenant his head was covered with the mitre.
- When Christ died the veil of the temple was rent and the priesthood was changed; the high priest now is Christ, for it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda. The sons of Levi no longer carry that office; Judah carries this work to the kingdom.

    The veil of the temple was rent in twain when Christ died.
  - **[Hebrews 7:12-14](/bible/hebrews/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/7#v12)</sup> For the priesthood being changed, there is made of necessity a change also of the law.
    >

    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/7#v14)</sup> For it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood.

    The priesthood being changed, there is a change also of the law; it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda.

**[Leviticus 21:13-15](/bible/leviticus/21#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/21#v13)</sup> And he shall take a wife in her virginity.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/21#v14)</sup> A widow, or a divorced woman, or profane, or an harlot, these shall he not take: but he shall take a virgin of his own people to wife.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/21#v15)</sup> Neither shall he profane his seed among his people: for I the Lord do sanctify him.

- The high priest took a wife in her virginity: not a widow, nor a divorced woman, nor profane, nor an harlot, but a virgin of his own people, that he profane not his seed among his people.

**[Leviticus 21:16-24](/bible/leviticus/21#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/21#v16)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/21#v17)</sup> Speak unto Aaron, saying, Whosoever he be of thy seed in their generations that hath any blemish, let him not approach to offer the bread of his God.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/21#v18)</sup> For whatsoever man he be that hath a blemish, he shall not approach: a blind man, or a lame, or he that hath a flat nose, or any thing superfluous,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/21#v19)</sup> Or a man that is brokenfooted, or brokenhanded,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/21#v20)</sup> Or crookbackt, or a dwarf, or that hath a blemish in his eye, or be scurvy, or scabbed, or hath his stones broken;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/21#v21)</sup> No man that hath a blemish of the seed of Aaron the priest shall come nigh to offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire: he hath a blemish; he shall not come nigh to offer the bread of his God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/21#v22)</sup> He shall eat the bread of his God, both of the most holy, and of the holy.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/21#v23)</sup> Only he shall not go in unto the vail, nor come nigh unto the altar, because he hath a blemish; that he profane not my sanctuaries: for I the Lord do sanctify them.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/21#v24)</sup> And Moses told it unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel.

- A blemish is a disability or birth defect: blind, lame in his walk, a flat nose, any thing superfluous, brokenfooted, brokenhanded, crookbackt, a dwarf, a blemish in the eye, scurvy, scabbed, or stones broken. No man of the seed of Aaron with a blemish could come nigh to offer the offerings of the Lord made by fire.
- Yet the blemished priest still ate the bread of his God, both of the most holy and of the holy; he was provided for, only he went not in unto the vail nor came nigh unto the altar, that the sanctuaries be not profaned.
- Moses told it unto Aaron, and to his sons, and unto all the children of Israel.
