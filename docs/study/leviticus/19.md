**[Leviticus 19:1-3](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/19#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/19#v2)</sup> Speak unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, and say unto them, Ye shall be holy: for I the Lord your God am holy.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/19#v3)</sup> Ye shall fear every man his mother, and his father, and keep my sabbaths: I am the Lord your God.

- Ye shall be holy, for I the Lord your God am holy; this word went to all the congregation of the children of Israel.
- Fear every man his mother and his father goes back to the commandment, honour thy father and thy mother.
- Honour can be kept even at a distance: show respect, and do nothing that would bring shame on your mother. But there is a balance; if she asks for what is against the commandments of the Most High, the commandments come first.
- Keep my sabbaths: the feast days are Sabbaths also. The Passover, the first and last days of the feast of Tabernacles, the day of atonement, and the memorial of blowing of trumpets are all Sabbaths; no servile work, no buying and selling, though cooking is permitted on the feast days.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 20:12](/bible/exodus/20#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/20#v12)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

    Honour thy father and thy mother; fearing them in Leviticus 19 is the same commandment.

**[Leviticus 19:4](/bible/leviticus/19#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/19#v4)</sup> Turn ye not unto idols, nor make to yourselves molten gods: I am the Lord your God.

- Turn not unto idols, nor make molten gods; this is the commandment against making any graven image.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 20:4](/bible/exodus/20#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/20#v4)</sup> Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:

    Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image.

**[Leviticus 19:5-8](/bible/leviticus/19#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/19#v5)</sup> And if ye offer a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the Lord, ye shall offer it at your own will.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/19#v6)</sup> It shall be eaten the same day ye offer it, and on the morrow: and if ought remain until the third day, it shall be burnt in the fire.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/19#v7)</sup> And if it be eaten at all on the third day, it is abominable; it shall not be accepted.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/19#v8)</sup> Therefore every one that eateth it shall bear his iniquity, because he hath profaned the hallowed thing of the Lord: and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.

- The peace offering was eaten the same day it was offered or on the morrow; whatever remained to the third day was burnt. Eaten on the third day it is abominable, and the eater bears his iniquity, for he has profaned the hallowed thing of the Lord.

**[Leviticus 19:9-10](/bible/leviticus/19#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/19#v9)</sup> And when ye reap the harvest of your land, thou shalt not wholly reap the corners of thy field, neither shalt thou gather the gleanings of thy harvest.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/19#v10)</sup> And thou shalt not glean thy vineyard, neither shalt thou gather every grape of thy vineyard; thou shalt leave them for the poor and stranger: I am the Lord your God.

- The corners of the field, the gleanings of the harvest, and the leavings of the vineyard belonged to the poor and the stranger. The Most High is teaching, do not be selfish; love thy neighbour as thyself and look out for the poor of your people.
- He that provides not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, has denied the faith. Those of his own house are his wife and children; his own is his nation. Care for your people.
- Charge them that are rich that they be not highminded, but ready to distribute, willing to communicate. Most of us today have no fields or vineyards, but some have good jobs and blessed incomes; you cannot take it with you, so give of your substance. The scriptures also say no good can come to him that gives no alms.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

    Provide for thine own and specially for those of thine own house; the own is the nation, the house is the family.
  - **[1 Timothy 6:17-18](/bible/1-timothy/6#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/1-timothy/6#v17)</sup> Charge them that are rich in this world, that they be not highminded, nor trust in uncertain riches, but in the living God, who giveth us richly all things to enjoy;
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/1-timothy/6#v18)</sup> That they do good, that they be rich in good works, ready to distribute, willing to communicate;

    The rich are charged to be ready to distribute, willing to communicate; the law of the gleanings teaches the same giving.

**[Leviticus 19:11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

- Dealing falsely is any dishonesty: skewing the truth to get your way, or cheating.
- Be honest. Have an honest job so you do not steal; run an honest business so you do not cheat people with foul practices, selling goods you know will fail or charging a customer for repairs he does not need. That honesty is owed even to the other nations.

**[Leviticus 19:12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/19#v12)</sup> And ye shall not swear by my name falsely, neither shalt thou profane the name of thy God: I am the Lord.

- Do not swear by His name falsely. Saying I swear to God over a lie profanes the name of the Most High.
- Christ said swear not at all, neither by heaven, nor by the earth, nor by thy head; let your communication be yea, yea and nay, nay. The only reason a man swears is that he has lied before and no one believes him; in your repentance take swearing out of your speech, and do not put oaths on your own head or your family either.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:33-37](/bible/matthew/5#v33)**

    Swear not at all; let your communication be yea, yea and nay, nay, for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

**[Leviticus 19:13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

- The wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning: when a man works for you, pay him that same day. Holding back the wage is defrauding thy neighbour, so businesses in Israel should pay their people the way the scriptures say.
- If both parties agree beforehand to payment the next day, that agreement is between you, the Lord, and that man; the scripture states the standard.

**[Leviticus 19:14-15](/bible/leviticus/19#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/19#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not curse the deaf, nor put a stumblingblock before the blind, but shalt fear thy God: I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/19#v15)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment: thou shalt not respect the person of the poor, nor honour the person of the mighty: but in righteousness shalt thou judge thy neighbour.

- Curse not the deaf, put no stumblingblock before the blind, but fear thy God.
- Do no unrighteousness in judgment; respect not the person of the poor nor honour the person of the mighty. The courts of this land do the opposite: our people receive far harsher sentences than others for the same crime with the same priors, sometimes before the very same judge.

**[Leviticus 19:16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

- A talebearer revealeth secrets, but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter. What a brother or sister tells you in confidence is not yours to spread; carrying it to the next person is talebearing, and it happens even in this truth.
- Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: much strife and contention comes from messy people. Repent of that messy spirit; it has no place in Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 11:13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)</sup> A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.

    A talebearer revealeth secrets; the faithful spirit conceals the matter.
  - **[Proverbs 26:20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)</sup> Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth.

    Where no talebearer is, the strife ceaseth; messy people feed the fire.

**[Leviticus 19:17-18](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

- Hate not thy brother in thine heart; rebuke thy neighbour and suffer not sin upon him. If you love your brother or sister you correct them when they are going off into sin; that is real love.
- Love thy neighbour as thyself: thy neighbour is the children of thy people. Get that right, against the teaching that makes the neighbour everybody.

**[Leviticus 19:19](/bible/leviticus/19#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/19#v19)</sup> Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee.

- Keep the statutes: no gendering cattle with a diverse kind, no sowing the field with mingled seed, and no garment mingled of linen and woollen; that is the law against mixed fabrics.

> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/19#v20)</sup> And whosoever lieth carnally with a woman, that is a bondmaid, betrothed to an husband, and not at all redeemed, nor freedom given her; she shall be scourged; they shall not be put to death, because she was not free.

> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/19#v21)</sup> And he shall bring his trespass offering unto the Lord, unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, even a ram for a trespass offering.

- The bondmaid betrothed to an husband and not redeemed was scourged, not put to death, because she was not free; the man brought a ram for a trespass offering, and the priest made atonement, and the sin was forgiven him.

**[Leviticus 19:23-25](/bible/leviticus/19#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/19#v23)</sup> And when ye shall come into the land, and shall have planted all manner of trees for food, then ye shall count the fruit thereof as uncircumcised: three years shall it be as uncircumcised unto you: it shall not be eaten of.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/19#v24)</sup> But in the fourth year all the fruit thereof shall be holy to praise the Lord withal.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/19#v25)</sup> And in the fifth year shall ye eat of the fruit thereof, that it may yield unto you the increase thereof: I am the Lord your God.

- Fruit of a newly planted tree was counted as uncircumcised for three years and not eaten; in the fourth year all the fruit was holy to praise the Lord withal; only in the fifth year could the fruit be eaten, that it might yield its increase.

**[Leviticus 19:26](/bible/leviticus/19#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/19#v26)</sup> Ye shall not eat any thing with the blood: neither shall ye use enchantment, nor observe times.

- Eat nothing with the blood: cook the blood out of the meat. No rare or bloody meat, none of the raw-meat customs of the other nations.
- Use no enchantments nor observe times; that goes into the horoscopes, astrology, and the rest of that nonsense. Leave it alone.

**[Leviticus 19:27](/bible/leviticus/19#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/19#v27)</sup> Ye shall not round the corners of your heads, neither shalt thou mar the corners of thy beard.

- Round not the corners of your head, meaning do not bald your head; mar not the corners of thy beard, meaning do not shave off the beard. This is written to all the children of Israel, not to the priests only.

**[Leviticus 19:28](/bible/leviticus/19#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/19#v28)</sup> Ye shall not make any cuttings in your flesh for the dead, nor print any marks upon you: I am the Lord.

- No cuttings in the flesh for the dead, nor any marks printed upon you: that is the tattoo. The RIP tattoo for a dead family member is both a cutting in the flesh for the dead and a mark printed upon you.
- If you came into this truth already carrying tattoos, repent, ask the Most High for forgiveness, and get no more.
- Ear and nose piercings are not forbidden by this verse; the cutting forbidden here is the cutting in the flesh for the dead.

**[Leviticus 19:29](/bible/leviticus/19#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/19#v29)</sup> Do not prostitute thy daughter, to cause her to be a whore; lest the land fall to whoredom, and the land become full of wickedness.

- Prostituting your daughter to cause her to be a whore takes several forms: letting her dress immodestly, letting a little girl wear makeup and grown attire, and letting her have a boyfriend. There are no boyfriends and girlfriends in the scriptures.
- The father waketh for the daughter: his charge is to watch over her, know her clothes, her phone, her friends and their parents, and keep her under supervision, lest she pass away the flower of her age, which is her virginity being taken. Keeping a sure watch over her is not being too hard on her; it is the commandment, lest she make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies.
- Many daughters go the way of the mother, as the proverb says, as is the mother, so is her daughter; but Israel is teaching the sisters self-respect again, with the scriptures on modest apparel and on not wearing that which pertaineth to a man.
- When daughters are left without watch, the land falls into whoredom and fills with wickedness; the state of the community today shows the fruit of neglecting this law.

**[Leviticus 19:33-34](/bible/leviticus/19#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/leviticus/19#v33)</sup> And if a stranger sojourn with thee in your land, ye shall not vex him.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/19#v34)</sup> But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

- Vex not the stranger that sojourns with thee in the land; he shall be as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself, for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.
- The stranger of this law is an Israelite of another tribe sojourning outside the allotment of his own tribe: a man of Judah dwelling in the land of Benjamin is a stranger to that land, because that was not the allotment the Most High gave him. When your brother the stranger falls into decay, relieve him and let him sojourn with you; give him work to deliver him out of his low state until he can stand again.
- The bondmen laws show the contrast in how the word stranger is used. The bondmen and bondmaids were to be of the heathen round about, and in that context the children of the strangers sojourning among you are the heathen, a possession and an inheritance; but over your brethren the children of Israel ye shall not rule with rigour. Then the sojourner or stranger that waxeth rich by thee returns to the Israelite stranger, for the bondservant of the heathen did not wax rich.

    Thy brother waxen poor is called a stranger or a sojourner, so the stranger of this law is your own brother outside his allotment.

**[Leviticus 19:35-37](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/19#v36)</sup> Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/leviticus/19#v37)</sup> Therefore shall ye observe all my statutes, and all my judgments, and do them: I am the Lord.

- Just balances, just weights, a just ephah and a just hin. Falsifying the balance is rigging the scale so that one pound weighs out as two and the buyer pays double; our people were doing that wickedness, and the Most High requires honest measure.
- Therefore observe all my statutes and all my judgments and do them: the Lord which brought Israel out of the land of Egypt requires it.
