**[Leviticus 12:1-2](/bible/leviticus/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/12#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/12#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a woman have conceived seed, and born a man child: then she shall be unclean seven days; according to the days of the separation for her infirmity shall she be unclean.

- If a woman have conceived seed and born a man child, she shall be unclean seven days, according to the days of the separation for her infirmity. Conceived seed means the sperm has met the egg: at intercourse about 300 million sperm enter, most die on the journey, and fertilization is one sperm uniting with the egg to form new life. All of that has to happen; that is conceiving seed.
- At the moment of fertilization a unique genetic code arises, instantly determining gender: an X with an X chromosome is a female, and an X with a Y chromosome is a male. Gender is determined at conception; you do not get to choose it, and there is no changing it afterward.
- Even inside the woman it is a male and a female pronucleus that come together to form the baby: male and female, not two males.
- The seven days match the law of the woman's issue: when her issue is blood, she is put apart seven days; those are the days of her separation spoken of here.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 15:19](/bible/leviticus/15#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/15#v19)</sup> And if a woman have an issue, and her issue in her flesh be blood, she shall be put apart seven days: and whosoever toucheth her shall be unclean until the even.

    If a woman have an issue of blood she shall be put apart seven days; those are the days of the separation.

**[Leviticus 12:3](/bible/leviticus/12#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/12#v3)</sup> And in the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.

- In the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised. The law is Genesis 17: every man child among you shall be circumcised at eight days old, a token of the covenant, and my covenant shall be in your flesh for an everlasting covenant; the uncircumcised man child whose flesh is not circumcised, that soul shall be cut off from his people. Brothers coming into the truth must be circumcised.
- That did not change. On the eighth day they came to circumcise John the Baptist, and when eight days were accomplished for the circumcising of the child, his name was called Jesus, which was so named of the angel before he was conceived in the womb.

**[Leviticus 12:4](/bible/leviticus/12#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/12#v4)</sup> And she shall then continue in the blood of her purifying three and thirty days; she shall touch no hallowed thing, nor come into the sanctuary, until the days of her purifying be fulfilled.

- She shall then continue in the blood of her purifying three and thirty days: seven days of separation plus thirty three days is forty days. If she has a male child she is unclean forty days, touching no hallowed thing and coming not into the sanctuary until the days of her purifying be fulfilled.
- Mary is the fulfilment of that: when the days of her purification according to the law of Moses were accomplished, they brought him to Jerusalem to present him to the Lord; Mary had to do this law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 2:22](/bible/luke/2#v22)**

    When the days of her purification according to the law of Moses were accomplished; Mary kept the law of the forty days.

**[Leviticus 12:5](/bible/leviticus/12#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/12#v5)</sup> But if she bear a maid child, then she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her separation: and she shall continue in the blood of her purifying threescore and six days.

- If she bear a maid child, she shall be unclean two weeks, as in her separation, and continue in the blood of her purifying threescore and six days: fourteen days plus sixty six days is eighty days for a daughter.
- Solomon describes the birth every man has: he was a mortal man, fashioned to be flesh in his mother's womb in the time of ten months, compacted in blood of the seed of man. A full term pregnancy is forty weeks, which is ten months; they say nine months but forget the month of conception.
- There is no king that had any other beginning of birth, and all men have one entrance into life. Christ was no exception: he was made of the seed of David according to the flesh, so it took seed to come through David's line.
