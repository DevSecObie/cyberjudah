**[Leviticus 8:1-5](/bible/leviticus/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/8#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/8#v2)</sup> Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and a bullock for the sin offering, and two rams, and a basket of unleavened bread;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/8#v3)</sup> And gather thou all the congregation together unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/8#v4)</sup> And Moses did as the Lord commanded him; and the assembly was gathered together unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/8#v5)</sup> And Moses said unto the congregation, This is the thing which the Lord commanded to be done.

- Take Aaron and his sons with him, and the garments, and the anointing oil, and a bullock for the sin offering, and two rams, and a basket of unleavened bread; and all the congregation was gathered unto the door of the tabernacle.
- Mark the list: if you want to live under the Old Covenant, you had better have a priest, the garments, the anointing oil, a bullock for the sin offering, two rams, a basket of unleavened bread, and a temple to gather at. The covenant of sacrifice cannot be kept today.

**[Leviticus 8:6-9](/bible/leviticus/8#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/8#v6)</sup> And Moses brought Aaron and his sons, and washed them with water.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/8#v7)</sup> And he put upon him the coat, and girded him with the girdle, and clothed him with the robe, and put the ephod upon him, and he girded him with the curious girdle of the ephod, and bound it unto him therewith.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/8#v8)</sup> And he put the breastplate upon him: also he put in the breastplate the Urim and the Thummim.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/8#v9)</sup> And he put the mitre upon his head; also upon the mitre, even upon his forefront, did he put the golden plate, the holy crown; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Moses washed Aaron and his sons with water, and put upon him the coat, the girdle, the robe, and the ephod, and he put the breastplate upon him, and in the breastplate the Urim and the Thummim.
- He put the mitre upon his head, and upon the mitre, upon his forefront, the golden plate, the holy crown, as the LORD commanded Moses.

**[Leviticus 8:10-13](/bible/leviticus/8#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/8#v10)</sup> And Moses took the anointing oil, and anointed the tabernacle and all that was therein, and sanctified them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/8#v11)</sup> And he sprinkled thereof upon the altar seven times, and anointed the altar and all his vessels, both the laver and his foot, to sanctify them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/8#v12)</sup> And he poured of the anointing oil upon Aaron’s head, and anointed him, to sanctify him.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/8#v13)</sup> And Moses brought Aaron’s sons, and put coats upon them, and girded them with girdles, and put bonnets upon them; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Moses anointed the tabernacle and all that was therein, sprinkled the altar seven times, and poured of the anointing oil upon Aaron's head, and anointed him, to sanctify him, to set him apart.
- This is what David is going back to in the psalm: behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity; it is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron's beard, down to the skirts of his garments. That oil could be poured on no one but the high priest; that is how precious unity among the people is to be.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 133:1-3](/bible/psalms/133#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/133#v1)</sup> Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/133#v2)</sup> It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron’s beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments;
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/133#v3)</sup> As the dew of Hermon, and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Zion: for there the Lord commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.

    Unity among brethren is like the precious ointment upon Aaron's head that ran down his beard to the skirts of his garments; for there the LORD commanded the blessing.

**[Leviticus 8:14-17](/bible/leviticus/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/8#v14)</sup> And he brought the bullock for the sin offering: and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the bullock for the sin offering.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/8#v15)</sup> And he slew it; and Moses took the blood, and put it upon the horns of the altar round about with his finger, and purified the altar, and poured the blood at the bottom of the altar, and sanctified it, to make reconciliation upon it.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/8#v16)</sup> And he took all the fat that was upon the inwards, and the caul above the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat, and Moses burned it upon the altar.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/8#v17)</sup> But the bullock, and his hide, his flesh, and his dung, he burnt with fire without the camp; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the bullock for the sin offering, and Moses put the blood upon the horns of the altar with his finger, purified the altar, and sanctified it, to make reconciliation upon it; reconciliation goes back to making reconciliation for the house of Israel.
- The fat, the caul, and the kidneys, the guts, were burnt upon the altar, but the bullock with his hide and flesh was burnt with fire without the camp. Christ's crucifixion lines up with the law of sacrifice: he was taken outside Jerusalem to the place called Golgotha and crucified there.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 45:17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)</sup> And it shall be the prince’s part to give burnt offerings, and meat offerings, and drink offerings, in the feasts, and in the new moons, and in the sabbaths, in all solemnities of the house of Israel: he shall prepare the sin offering, and the meat offering, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make reconciliation for the house of Israel.

    The sin offering and the burnt offering were prepared to make reconciliation for the house of Israel; the purified altar carried that reconciliation.

**[Leviticus 8:18-21](/bible/leviticus/8#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/8#v18)</sup> And he brought the ram for the burnt offering: and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the ram.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/8#v19)</sup> And he killed it; and Moses sprinkled the blood upon the altar round about.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/leviticus/8#v20)</sup> And he cut the ram into pieces; and Moses burnt the head, and the pieces, and the fat.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/8#v21)</sup> And he washed the inwards and the legs in water; and Moses burnt the whole ram upon the altar: it was a burnt sacrifice for a sweet savour, and an offering made by fire unto the Lord; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The ram for the burnt offering was cut into pieces, the inwards and legs washed in water, and Moses burnt the whole ram upon the altar: a burnt sacrifice for a sweet savour, an offering made by fire unto the LORD.

**[Leviticus 8:22-24](/bible/leviticus/8#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/8#v22)</sup> And he brought the other ram, the ram of consecration: and Aaron and his sons laid their hands upon the head of the ram.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/8#v23)</sup> And he slew it; and Moses took of the blood of it, and put it upon the tip of Aaron’s right ear, and upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/leviticus/8#v24)</sup> And he brought Aaron’s sons, and Moses put of the blood upon the tip of their right ear, and upon the thumbs of their right hands, and upon the great toes of their right feet: and Moses sprinkled the blood upon the altar round about.

- Moses slew the other ram, the ram of consecration, and put the blood upon the tip of Aaron's right ear, upon the thumb of his right hand, and upon the great toe of his right foot, and the same for his sons.
- The blood went on the right side. The right side is the favored side with the Most High, and the volume of the book bears the pattern: when Christ rose he went to his Father and sat on the right hand of God.

**[Leviticus 8:25-29](/bible/leviticus/8#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/leviticus/8#v25)</sup> And he took the fat, and the rump, and all the fat that was upon the inwards, and the caul above the liver, and the two kidneys, and their fat, and the right shoulder:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/8#v26)</sup> And out of the basket of unleavened bread, that was before the Lord, he took one unleavened cake, and a cake of oiled bread, and one wafer, and put them on the fat, and upon the right shoulder:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/8#v27)</sup> And he put all upon Aaron’s hands, and upon his sons’ hands, and waved them for a wave offering before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/leviticus/8#v28)</sup> And Moses took them from off their hands, and burnt them on the altar upon the burnt offering: they were consecrations for a sweet savour: it is an offering made by fire unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/leviticus/8#v29)</sup> And Moses took the breast, and waved it for a wave offering before the Lord: for of the ram of consecration it was Moses’ part; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The fat, the right shoulder, and out of the basket one unleavened cake, a cake of oiled bread, and one wafer were put upon the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved for a wave offering before the LORD, then burnt on the altar for a sweet savour.
- Moses took the breast and waved it for a wave offering before the LORD, lifting it up and waving it as in [Exodus 29](/bible/exodus/29); it was Moses' part of the ram of consecration.

**[Leviticus 8:30-36](/bible/leviticus/8#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/leviticus/8#v30)</sup> And Moses took of the anointing oil, and of the blood which was upon the altar, and sprinkled it upon Aaron, and upon his garments, and upon his sons, and upon his sons’ garments with him; and sanctified Aaron, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons’ garments with him.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/8#v31)</sup> And Moses said unto Aaron and to his sons, Boil the flesh at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation: and there eat it with the bread that is in the basket of consecrations, as I commanded, saying, Aaron and his sons shall eat it.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/8#v32)</sup> And that which remaineth of the flesh and of the bread shall ye burn with fire.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/leviticus/8#v33)</sup> And ye shall not go out of the door of the tabernacle of the congregation in seven days, until the days of your consecration be at an end: for seven days shall he consecrate you.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/8#v34)</sup> As he hath done this day, so the Lord hath commanded to do, to make an atonement for you.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/8#v35)</sup> Therefore shall ye abide at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation day and night seven days, and keep the charge of the Lord, that ye die not: for so I am commanded.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/8#v36)</sup> So Aaron and his sons did all things which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses.

- Moses sprinkled the anointing oil and the blood upon Aaron and his garments and upon his sons and their garments, and sanctified them. They boiled the flesh at the door of the tabernacle and ate it there with the bread of consecration, and abode at the door of the tabernacle day and night seven days, until the days of their consecration were at an end.
- The blood, sprinkled again and again about the altar, is spiritual: they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony, which is the Bible, and they loved not their lives unto the death. That is how Satan and all the nations are overcome; the blood of the sacrifices went all the way back to Christ.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 12:11](/bible/revelation/12#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/12#v11)</sup> And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; and they loved not their lives unto the death.

    They overcame him by the blood of the Lamb, and by the word of their testimony; the sprinkled blood of the law is the shadow of that blood.
