**[Ezra 3:1-2](/bible/ezra/3#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/3#v1)</sup> And when the seventh month was come, and the children of Israel were in the cities, the people gathered themselves together as one man to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/3#v2)</sup> Then stood up Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and his brethren the priests, and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and his brethren, and builded the altar of the God of Israel, to offer burnt offerings thereon, as it is written in the law of Moses the man of God.

- In the seventh month the children of Israel gather as one man to Jerusalem. The seventh month marks the Feast of Tabernacles and the day of atonement, so knowing the month reveals what they were doing.
- Jeshua and Zerubbabel build the altar of the God of Israel to offer burnt offerings as written in the law of Moses.

**[Ezra 3:3-6](/bible/ezra/3#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/3#v3)</sup> And they set the altar upon his bases; for fear was upon them because of the people of those countries: and they offered burnt offerings thereon unto the Lord, even burnt offerings morning and evening.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezra/3#v4)</sup> They kept also the feast of tabernacles, as it is written, and offered the daily burnt offerings by number, according to the custom, as the duty of every day required;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/3#v5)</sup> And afterward offered the continual burnt offering, both of the new moons, and of all the set feasts of the Lord that were consecrated, and of every one that willingly offered a freewill offering unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezra/3#v6)</sup> From the first day of the seventh month began they to offer burnt offerings unto the Lord. But the foundation of the temple of the Lord was not yet laid.

- They set the altar on its bases despite fear of the surrounding peoples and offer burnt offerings morning and evening.
- They keep the Feast of Tabernacles and the daily offerings, but the foundation of the temple was not yet laid.

**[Ezra 3:7](/bible/ezra/3#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezra/3#v7)</sup> They gave money also unto the masons, and to the carpenters; and meat, and drink, and oil, unto them of Zidon, and to them of Tyre, to bring cedar trees from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa, according to the grant that they had of Cyrus king of Persia.

- They give money to the masons and carpenters, and meat, drink, and oil to the men of Zidon and Tyre to bring cedars from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa, according to the grant of Cyrus.

**[Ezra 3:8-9](/bible/ezra/3#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezra/3#v8)</sup> Now in the second year of their coming unto the house of God at Jerusalem, in the second month, began Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and the remnant of their brethren the priests and the Levites, and all they that were come out of the captivity unto Jerusalem; and appointed the Levites, from twenty years old and upward, to set forward the work of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezra/3#v9)</sup> Then stood Jeshua with his sons and his brethren, Kadmiel and his sons, the sons of Judah, together, to set forward the workmen in the house of God: the sons of Henadad, with their sons and their brethren the Levites.

- In the second year of their coming, Zerubbabel and Jeshua and the remnant of their brethren the priests and Levites appoint the Levites from twenty years old and upward to set forward the work.
- The remnant here shows Judah, Benjamin, and Levi with those who had remained. Jeshua and his sons and brethren stand together to oversee the workmen.

**[Ezra 3:10-11](/bible/ezra/3#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezra/3#v10)</sup> And when the builders laid the foundation of the temple of the Lord, they set the priests in their apparel with trumpets, and the Levites the sons of Asaph with cymbals, to praise the Lord, after the ordinance of David king of Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezra/3#v11)</sup> And they sang together by course in praising and giving thanks unto the Lord; because he is good, for his mercy endureth for ever toward Israel. And all the people shouted with a great shout, when they praised the Lord, because the foundation of the house of the Lord was laid.

- When the builders lay the foundation, the priests stand in their apparel with trumpets and the Levites with cymbals to praise the Lord after the ordinance of David.
- They sing together, praising and giving thanks, because the Most High is good and his mercy endures forever toward Israel, and all the people shout with a great shout.

**[Ezra 3:12-13](/bible/ezra/3#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezra/3#v12)</sup> But many of the priests and Levites and chief of the fathers, who were ancient men, that had seen the first house, when the foundation of this house was laid before their eyes, wept with a loud voice; and many shouted aloud for joy:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/3#v13)</sup> So that the people could not discern the noise of the shout of joy from the noise of the weeping of the people: for the people shouted with a loud shout, and the noise was heard afar off.

- Many of the ancient priests and Levites who had seen the first house weep with a loud voice, while many shout aloud for joy.
- The people had endured Babylon tearing down Jerusalem, and now seeing the foundation raised again the noise of joy and weeping could not be discerned apart and was heard far off.
