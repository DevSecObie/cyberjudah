## Introduction

This session covers [Ezra 9](/bible/ezra/9) and 10, then moves into [Nehemiah 1](/bible/nehemiah/1) and 2. In Ezra the people are already back in Jerusalem after Ezra himself has come up, and the trouble that surfaces is intermarriage with the nations of the lands. Ezra mourns, confesses the sin as the same trespass that carried the fathers into captivity, and the congregation makes a covenant to put away the strange wives and the children born of them. Read it as a picture of a people shown grace out of bondage who then forsake the commandment, and of a leader who gives all the glory to the Most High and calls the nation back to separation. The same account is written in 1 Esdras chapters 8 and 9, which the teaching draws on to fill out the record.

**[Ezra 9:1-2](/bible/ezra/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/9#v1)</sup> Now when these things were done, the princes came to me, saying, The people of Israel, and the priests, and the Levites, have not separated themselves from the people of the lands, doing according to their abominations, even of the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Jebusites, the Ammonites, the Moabites, the Egyptians, and the Amorites.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/9#v2)</sup> For they have taken of their daughters for themselves, and for their sons: so that the holy seed have mingled themselves with the people of those lands: yea, the hand of the princes and rulers hath been chief in this trespass.

- The princes come to Ezra reporting that the people of Israel, the priests, and the Levites have not separated themselves from the people of the lands, doing after their abominations, the Canaanites, Hittites, Perizzites, Jebusites, Ammonites, Moabites, Egyptians, and Amorites.
- The sin is interracial marriage with the nations, taking their daughters for themselves and their sons, so that the holy seed mingled itself with the people of those lands. It was not only the common people but the Levites, the leaders, that were chief in this trespass.

  - **[Deuteronomy 7:3-4](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them; thy daughter thou shalt not give unto his son, nor his daughter shalt thou take unto thy son.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v4)</sup> For they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve other gods: so will the anger of the Lord be kindled against you, and destroy thee suddenly.

    The commandment was to make no marriages with the nations, not giving daughters to their sons nor taking their daughters, because they would turn the children away from following the Most High.

**[Ezra 9:3-4](/bible/ezra/9#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/9#v3)</sup> And when I heard this thing, I rent my garment and my mantle, and plucked off the hair of my head and of my beard, and sat down astonied.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezra/9#v4)</sup> Then were assembled unto me every one that trembled at the words of the God of Israel, because of the transgression of those that had been carried away; and I sat astonied until the evening sacrifice.

- When Ezra hears this thing he rends his garment and his mantle, plucks off the hair of his head and beard, and sits down astonished, in distress that the people did this after the Most High showed mercy to bring them out of Babylon.
- Then everyone that trembled at the words of the God of Israel assembles to him because of the transgression of those that had been carried away, and he sits astonished until the evening sacrifice.

**[Ezra 9:5-7](/bible/ezra/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/9#v5)</sup> And at the evening sacrifice I arose up from my heaviness; and having rent my garment and my mantle, I fell upon my knees, and spread out my hands unto the Lord my God,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezra/9#v6)</sup> And said, O my God, I am ashamed and blush to lift up my face to thee, my God: for our iniquities are increased over our head, and our trespass is grown up unto the heavens.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezra/9#v7)</sup> Since the days of our fathers have we been in a great trespass unto this day; and for our iniquities have we, our kings, and our priests, been delivered into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, and to a spoil, and to confusion of face, as it is this day.

- At the evening sacrifice Ezra rises from his heaviness, and having rent his garment and mantle, falls upon his knees and spreads out his hands unto the Lord his God.
- He says he is ashamed and blushes to lift up his face, for their iniquities are increased over their head and their trespass is grown up unto the heavens. Since the days of the fathers they have been in a great trespass unto this day.
- For their iniquities they, their kings, and their priests have been delivered into the hand of the kings of the lands, to the sword, to captivity, and to spoil. The nation is privy to all the captivities it came from, Egypt, Assyria, and Babylon, and knows why it went, yet does the same sins.

**[Ezra 9:8-9](/bible/ezra/9#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezra/9#v8)</sup> And now for a little space grace hath been shewed from the Lord our God, to leave us a remnant to escape, and to give us a nail in his holy place, that our God may lighten our eyes, and give us a little reviving in our bondage.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezra/9#v9)</sup> For we were bondmen; yet our God hath not forsaken us in our bondage, but hath extended mercy unto us in the sight of the kings of Persia, to give us a reviving, to set up the house of our God, and to repair the desolations thereof, and to give us a wall in Judah and in Jerusalem.

- Now for a little space grace has been showed from the Lord to leave a remnant to escape and to give them a nail in his holy place, that the Most High may lighten their eyes and give a little reviving in their bondage.
- Though they were bondmen the Most High has not forsaken them but extended mercy in the sight of the kings of Persia, to give a reviving to set up the house of God and give them a wall in Jerusalem. This is the very leeway that let them come back to the land.

**[Ezra 9:10-12](/bible/ezra/9#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/ezra/9#v10)</sup> And now, O our God, what shall we say after this? for we have forsaken thy commandments,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezra/9#v11)</sup> Which thou hast commanded by thy servants the prophets, saying, The land, unto which ye go to possess it, is an unclean land with the filthiness of the people of the lands, with their abominations, which have filled it from one end to another with their uncleanness.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezra/9#v12)</sup> Now therefore give not your daughters unto their sons, neither take their daughters unto your sons, nor seek their peace or their wealth for ever: that ye may be strong, and eat the good of the land, and leave it for an inheritance to your children for ever.

- Ezra asks what they shall say after this, for they have forsaken the commandments. It is like a parent giving you something good and you turning and doing something to make him feel wrong for giving it.
- The commandment given by the prophets said the land they go to possess is an unclean land, filled from one end to another with the filthiness and abominations of the people of the lands.
- Therefore they were not to give their sons or take their daughters, nor seek their peace or wealth, that they might be strong, eat the good of the land, and leave it for an inheritance to their children forever. This command is reiterated all the way back to the time of Moses.

**[Ezra 9:13-15](/bible/ezra/9#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)</sup> And after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for our great trespass, seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezra/9#v14)</sup> Should we again break thy commandments, and join in affinity with the people of these abominations? wouldest not thou be angry with us till thou hadst consumed us, so that there should be no remnant nor escaping?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezra/9#v15)</sup> O Lord God of Israel, thou art righteous: for we remain yet escaped, as it is this day: behold, we are before thee in our trespasses: for we cannot stand before thee because of this.

- After all that has come upon them for their evil deeds, and seeing the Most High has punished their iniquities less than they deserve and given such deliverance, Ezra asks should they again break the commandments and join in affinity with the people of these abominations.
- Would not the Most High be angry until he had consumed them so there should be no remnant left. Oh Lord God of Israel, thou art righteous, for they remain yet escaped as it is this day.
- They stand before the Most High in their trespass, for they cannot stand before him because of it. Even though the Levites and Israel committed this sin, the Most High still showed them mercy and let them stay in the land.

# Ezra 10: The Covenant to Put Away Strange Wives

Read the chapter → [Ezra 10](/bible/ezra/10)

**[Ezra 10:1-4](/bible/ezra/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/10#v1)</sup> Now when Ezra had prayed, and when he had confessed, weeping and casting himself down before the house of God, there assembled unto him out of Israel a very great congregation of men and women and children: for the people wept very sore.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/10#v2)</sup> And Shechaniah the son of Jehiel, one of the sons of Elam, answered and said unto Ezra, We have trespassed against our God, and have taken strange wives of the people of the land: yet now there is hope in Israel concerning this thing.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/10#v3)</sup> Now therefore let us make a covenant with our God to put away all the wives, and such as are born of them, according to the counsel of my lord, and of those that tremble at the commandment of our God; and let it be done according to the law.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezra/10#v4)</sup> Arise; for this matter belongeth unto thee: we also will be with thee: be of good courage, and do it.

- When Ezra had prayed and confessed, weeping and casting himself down before the house of God, a very great congregation of men, women, and children assembles to him, for the people wept very sore.
- Shechaniah the son of Jehiel answers and says they have trespassed against their God in taking strange wives of the people of the land, yet there is hope in Israel concerning this thing.
- He counsels to make a covenant to put away all the wives and the children born of them, according to the counsel of those that tremble at the commandment, and to do it according to the law. This time meant putting away those particular wives and children; it does not mean an Israelite today with a child by another nation must put away the child.

**[Ezra 10:5-6](/bible/ezra/10#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/10#v5)</sup> Then arose Ezra, and made the chief priests, the Levites, and all Israel, to swear that they should do according to this word. And they sware.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezra/10#v6)</sup> Then Ezra rose up from before the house of God, and went into the chamber of Johanan the son of Eliashib: and when he came thither, he did eat no bread, nor drink water: for he mourned because of the transgression of them that had been carried away.

- Then Ezra rises and makes the chief priests, the Levites, and all Israel swear to do according to this word, and they swear, the same way an oath was made to keep the commandments.
- Ezra rises from before the house of God and goes into the chamber of Johanan, and eats no bread nor drinks water, for he mourns because of the transgression of them that had been carried away.

**[Ezra 10:7-8](/bible/ezra/10#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/ezra/10#v7)</sup> And they made proclamation throughout Judah and Jerusalem unto all the children of the captivity, that they should gather themselves together unto Jerusalem;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezra/10#v8)</sup> And that whosoever would not come within three days, according to the counsel of the princes and the elders, all his substance should be forfeited, and himself separated from the congregation of those that had been carried away.

- Proclamation is made throughout Judah and Jerusalem to all the children of the captivity that they should gather themselves to Jerusalem.
- Whosoever would not come within three days, according to the counsel of the princes and elders, would have all his substance forfeited and himself separated from the congregation of those that had been carried away.

**[Ezra 10:9-11](/bible/ezra/10#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezra/10#v9)</sup> Then all the men of Judah and Benjamin gathered themselves together unto Jerusalem within three days. It was the ninth month, on the twentieth day of the month; and all the people sat in the street of the house of God, trembling because of this matter, and for the great rain.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezra/10#v10)</sup> And Ezra the priest stood up, and said unto them, Ye have transgressed, and have taken strange wives, to increase the trespass of Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezra/10#v11)</sup> Now therefore make confession unto the Lord God of your fathers, and do his pleasure: and separate yourselves from the people of the land, and from the strange wives.

- All the men of Judah and Benjamin gather to Jerusalem within three days, in the ninth month on the twentieth day, and sit in the street of the house of God trembling because of this matter and the great rain.
- Ezra the priest stands and says they have transgressed and taken strange wives to increase the trespass of Israel.
- Now they are to make confession unto the Lord God of their fathers, do his pleasure, and separate themselves from the people of the land and from the strange wives. Doing his pleasure today is the same, separating from unclean people.

**[Ezra 10:12-15](/bible/ezra/10#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezra/10#v12)</sup> Then all the congregation answered and said with a loud voice, As thou hast said, so must we do.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/10#v13)</sup> But the people are many, and it is a time of much rain, and we are not able to stand without, neither is this a work of one day or two: for we are many that have transgressed in this thing.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezra/10#v14)</sup> Let now our rulers of all the congregation stand, and let all them which have taken strange wives in our cities come at appointed times, and with them the elders of every city, and the judges thereof, until the fierce wrath of our God for this matter be turned from us.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezra/10#v15)</sup> Only Jonathan the son of Asahel and Jahaziah the son of Tikvah were employed about this matter: and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them.

- The congregation answers with a loud voice, As thou hast said, so must we do, but the people are many and it is a time of much rain, and they are not able to stand outside, neither is the work of one or two days.
- They ask that the rulers stand for the congregation, and that all who have taken strange wives come at appointed times with the elders and judges of every city, until the fierce wrath of God is turned from them. The fierce wrath is the judgment, and the reference to rain is why they came in portions.
- Only Jonathan and Jahaziah were employed about this matter, and Meshullam and Shabbethai the Levite helped them, so the sin was dealt with in orderly parts.

**[Ezra 10:16-17](/bible/ezra/10#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/ezra/10#v16)</sup> And the children of the captivity did so. And Ezra the priest, with certain chief of the fathers, after the house of their fathers, and all of them by their names, were separated, and sat down in the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezra/10#v17)</sup> And they made an end with all the men that had taken strange wives by the first day of the first month.

- The children of the captivity do so; Ezra and certain chief of the fathers, separated by name, sit down on the first day of the tenth month to examine the matter.
- The leaders and those who did righteously and had not taken other nations sit and judge the matter, for one who is himself in the sin cannot judge it. They make an end with all the men that had taken strange wives by the first day of the first month.

- Among the sons of the priests found to have taken strange wives were sons of Jeshua, and they gave their hands to put away their wives, offering a ram of the flock for their trespass, being guilty.
- The chapter names the Levites, singers, porters, and the men of Israel by their families who had taken strange wives, a long roll of those who had transgressed in this thing.
- All these had taken strange wives, and some of them had wives by whom they had children, so that some put away only the wives and some the wives and children born of them.

  - **[1 Esdras 9:36](/bible/1-esdras/9#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/1-esdras/9#v36)</sup> All these had taken strange wives, and they put them away with their children.

    The parallel account states plainly that they put away the strange wives together with the children born of them.
  - **[1 Esdras 9:37-40](/bible/1-esdras/9#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/1-esdras/9#v37)</sup> And the priests and Levites, and they that were of Israel, dwelt in Jerusalem, and in the country, in the first day of the seventh month: so the children of Israel were in their habitations.
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/1-esdras/9#v38)</sup> And the whole multitude came together with one accord into the broad place of the holy porch toward the east:
    >

    >

    After the sin was dealt with, the multitude gathered and asked Ezra the reader to bring the law of Moses, showing Ezra came also to teach the people the law.
  - **[1 Esdras 9:41-42](/bible/1-esdras/9#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/1-esdras/9#v41)</sup> And he read in the broad court before the holy porch from morning unto midday, before both men and women; and the multitude gave heed unto the law.
    >
    > <sup>[42](/bible/1-esdras/9#v42)</sup> And Esdras the priest and reader of the law stood up upon a pulpit of wood, which was made for that purpose.

    Ezra read the law from morning until midday, and stood upon a pulpit of wood made for that purpose, the same order followed at camp with a reader and a pulpit.
  - **[1 Esdras 9:46-47](/bible/1-esdras/9#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/1-esdras/9#v46)</sup> And when he opened the law, they stood all straight up. So Esdras blessed the Lord God most High, the God of hosts, Almighty.
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-esdras/9#v47)</sup> And all the people answered, Amen; and lifting up their hands they fell to the ground, and worshipped the Lord.

    When Ezra opened the law the people stood straight up, he blessed the Most High, and all the people answered Amen, lifted their hands, and fell to the earth to worship, the same order kept in the classes today.
