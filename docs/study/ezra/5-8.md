## Introduction

This session moves through Ezra chapters five through eight. It opens with the work on the house of the Most High being hindered by adversaries, then follows the decrees of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes that funded and protected the rebuilding, the dedication of the house and the keeping of the passover in Jerusalem, and finally Ezra the ready scribe preparing his heart to seek the law and leading a company back from Babylon. As a why-we-study preface, the word and the judgments of the Most High are the heritage of Israel and no other nation.

  - **[Psalms 147:19-20](/bible/psalms/147#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/147#v19)</sup> He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/147#v20)</sup> He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the Lord.

    The word, statutes, and judgments are given to Jacob alone and belong to Israel as their heritage.

**[Ezra 5:1-2](/bible/ezra/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/5#v1)</sup> Then the prophets, Haggai the prophet, and Zechariah the son of Iddo, prophesied unto the Jews that were in Judah and Jerusalem in the name of the God of Israel, even unto them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/5#v2)</sup> Then rose up Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel, and Jeshua the son of Jozadak, and began to build the house of God which is at Jerusalem: and with them were the prophets of God helping them.

- After the work had been hindered for roughly two years, the prophets Haggai and Zechariah stirred up the leaders, and Zerubbabel and Jeshua rose again to build the house of God.
- The prophets came together to accomplish a common mission. The work of the house does not get done unless the prophets and the people gather and labor together.

    The nation is called to gather itself together and seek the Most High before the day of his anger.

    Jacob calls his sons to gather together so they may hear what shall befall them in the last days.

> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/5#v3)</sup> At the same time came to them Tatnai, governor on this side the river, and Shethar–boznai, and their companions, and said thus unto them, Who hath commanded you to build this house, and to make up this wall?

- Tatnai the governor and his companions challenged the elders, demanding who commanded them to build the house and the wall.
- The eye of their God was upon the elders of the Jews, so the adversaries could not force the work to stop while a letter went to Darius.

**[Ezra 5:11-13](/bible/ezra/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ezra/5#v11)</sup> And thus they returned us answer, saying, We are the servants of the God of heaven and earth, and build the house that was builded these many years ago, which a great king of Israel builded and set up.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezra/5#v12)</sup> But after that our fathers had provoked the God of heaven unto wrath, he gave them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon, the Chaldean, who destroyed this house, and carried the people away into Babylon.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/5#v13)</sup> But in the first year of Cyrus the king of Babylon the same king Cyrus made a decree to build this house of God.

- The elders answered plainly that they are servants of the God of heaven and earth rebuilding a house a great king of Israel had built long before.
- They confessed that the fathers provoked the God of heaven, who delivered them into the hand of Nebuchadnezzar and carried them to Babylon. This captivity is read in Second Kings, where Assyria took Israel and later Babylon took Judah.

    >

    Assyria carried Israel away captive because they had sinned against the Most High their God.

>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezra/5#v15)</sup> And said unto him, Take these vessels, go, carry them into the temple that is in Jerusalem, and let the house of God be builded in his place.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezra/5#v16)</sup> Then came the same Sheshbazzar, and laid the foundation of the house of God which is in Jerusalem: and since that time even until now hath it been in building, and yet it is not finished.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezra/5#v17)</sup> Now therefore, if it seem good to the king, let there be search made in the king’s treasure house, which is there at Babylon, whether it be so, that a decree was made of Cyrus the king to build this house of God at Jerusalem, and let the king send his pleasure to us concerning this matter.

- Cyrus is called king of Babylon because he conquered Babylon and took possession of all it held, though he reigned out of Persia. He restored the gold and silver vessels Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple.
- The letter asks Darius to search the king's treasure house to confirm that Cyrus made a decree to build the house of God at Jerusalem. The heathen always keep records on the children of Israel, so the matter had to be proven from the rolls.

# Ezra 6: The Decree Confirmed And The House Dedicated

Read the chapter → [Ezra 6](/bible/ezra/6)

**[Ezra 6:1-5](/bible/ezra/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/6#v1)</sup> Then Darius the king made a decree, and search was made in the house of the rolls, where the treasures were laid up in Babylon.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/6#v2)</sup> And there was found at Achmetha, in the palace that is in the province of the Medes, a roll, and therein was a record thus written:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/6#v3)</sup> In the first year of Cyrus the king the same Cyrus the king made a decree concerning the house of God at Jerusalem, Let the house be builded, the place where they offered sacrifices, and let the foundations thereof be strongly laid; the height thereof threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof threescore cubits;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezra/6#v4)</sup> With three rows of great stones, and a row of new timber: and let the expenses be given out of the king’s house:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/6#v5)</sup> And also let the golden and silver vessels of the house of God, which Nebuchadnezzar took forth out of the temple which is at Jerusalem, and brought unto Babylon, be restored, and brought again unto the temple which is at Jerusalem, every one to his place, and place them in the house of God.

- Darius made search, and in the palace at Achmetha in the province of the Medes the roll was found recording the decree of Cyrus.
- The decree ordered the house rebuilt with strong foundations and set dimensions, the expenses given out of the king's house, and the gold and silver vessels restored to their place. Cyrus helped fund the rebuilding of the house of Israel.

**[Ezra 6:6-8](/bible/ezra/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezra/6#v6)</sup> Now therefore, Tatnai, governor beyond the river, Shethar–boznai, and your companions the Apharsachites, which are beyond the river, be ye far from thence:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezra/6#v7)</sup> Let the work of this house of God alone; let the governor of the Jews and the elders of the Jews build this house of God in his place.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezra/6#v8)</sup> Moreover I make a decree what ye shall do to the elders of these Jews for the building of this house of God: that of the king’s goods, even of the tribute beyond the river, forthwith expenses be given unto these men, that they be not hindered.

- Darius commanded Tatnai and his companions to leave the work alone and let the elders of the Jews build.
- He decreed that the cost be paid from the king's tribute beyond the river so the builders would not be hindered. Once a Persian decree was made it was upheld rather than vetoed. Even in Esther a decree against the Jews was not revoked but answered by a stronger decree in their favor.

  - **[Esther 8:8](/bible/esther/8#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/esther/8#v8)</sup> Write ye also for the Jews, as it liketh you, in the king’s name, and seal it with the king’s ring: for the writing which is written in the king’s name, and sealed with the king’s ring, may no man reverse.

    A writing sealed with the king's ring may not be reversed, so a second decree is made to deliver the Jews.

>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezra/6#v10)</sup> That they may offer sacrifices of sweet savours unto the God of heaven, and pray for the life of the king, and of his sons.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezra/6#v11)</sup> Also I have made a decree, that whosoever shall alter this word, let timber be pulled down from his house, and being set up, let him be hanged thereon; and let his house be made a dunghill for this.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezra/6#v12)</sup> And the God that hath caused his name to dwell there destroy all kings and people, that shall put to their hand to alter and to destroy this house of God which is at Jerusalem. I Darius have made a decree; let it be done with speed.

- The king ordered daily provision of bullocks, rams, lambs, wheat, salt, wine, and oil so the priests could offer sacrifices without fail, and that they might pray for the life of the king and his sons.
- Whoever altered the decree was to be hanged on timber pulled from his own house, and his house made a dunghill. The God who caused his name to dwell there was called on to destroy any king or people who tried to destroy this house.

- The house was finished, and the children of the captivity kept the dedication with joy, offering a hundred bullocks, two hundred rams, four hundred lambs, and twelve he goats for a sin offering according to the number of the tribes of Israel. This was a great praise, and the Levites' hands were full doing the service.
- The priests were set in their divisions and the Levites in their courses as it is written in the book of Moses, every man having his ordered task.

# Ezra 7: Ezra The Ready Scribe Prepares His Heart

Read the chapter → [Ezra 7](/bible/ezra/7)

**[Ezra 7:1-6](/bible/ezra/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/7#v1)</sup> Now after these things, in the reign of Artaxerxes king of Persia, Ezra the son of Seraiah, the son of Azariah, the son of Hilkiah,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/7#v2)</sup> The son of Shallum, the son of Zadok, the son of Ahitub,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/7#v3)</sup> The son of Amariah, the son of Azariah, the son of Meraioth,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezra/7#v4)</sup> The son of Zerahiah, the son of Uzzi, the son of Bukki,

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/7#v5)</sup> The son of Abishua, the son of Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the chief priest:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezra/7#v6)</sup> This Ezra went up from Babylon; and he was a ready scribe in the law of Moses, which the Lord God of Israel had given: and the king granted him all his request, according to the hand of the Lord his God upon him.

- Ezra's genealogy is traced back through Zadok and Phinehas to Eleazar the son of Aaron the chief priest, marking him a priest of the true line.
- Ezra went up from Babylon a ready scribe in the law of Moses, and the king granted all his requests because the hand of the Most High was upon him.

**[Ezra 7:7-10](/bible/ezra/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/ezra/7#v7)</sup> And there went up some of the children of Israel, and of the priests, and the Levites, and the singers, and the porters, and the Nethinims, unto Jerusalem, in the seventh year of Artaxerxes the king.

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezra/7#v8)</sup> And he came to Jerusalem in the fifth month, which was in the seventh year of the king.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezra/7#v9)</sup> For upon the first day of the first month began he to go up from Babylon, and on the first day of the fifth month came he to Jerusalem, according to the good hand of his God upon him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezra/7#v10)</sup> For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the Lord, and to do it, and to teach in Israel statutes and judgments.

- Some of the children of Israel, priests, Levites, singers, porters, and Nethinims went up to Jerusalem in the seventh year of Artaxerxes, and Ezra came in the fifth month by the good hand of his God.
- Ezra prepared his heart to seek the law of the Most High, to do it, and to teach statutes and judgments in Israel. Preparing the heart means setting the mind and spirit in order, examining oneself, and putting away the weak nature of sin before teaching others.

  - **[2 Esdras 14:13-14](/bible/2-esdras/14#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-esdras/14#v13)</sup> Now therefore set thine house in order, and reprove thy people, comfort such of them as be in trouble, and now renounce corruption,
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-esdras/14#v14)</sup> Let go from thee mortal thoughts, cast away the burdens of man, put off now the weak nature,

    Preparing the heart is setting the house in order and casting off mortal thoughts and the weak nature.
  - **[1 Esdras 8:7](/bible/1-esdras/8#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-esdras/8#v7)</sup> For Esdras had very great skill, so that he omitted nothing of the law and commandments of the Lord, but taught all Israel the ordinances and judgments.

    Ezra's great skill in the word came because he prayed and fasted to understand, and the Most High did great works through him.
  - **[James 1:5](/bible/james/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/james/1#v5)</sup> If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

    Whoever lacks wisdom may ask of God, who gives to all men liberally, and it shall be given.

>

- The king and his counselors freely offered silver and gold for the house of God, for sacrifices, and for whatever else was needful, drawn even from the king's treasure house. This was favor, letting the captivity return with the treasures of Babylon, as the nations will one day bring back all Israel's substance.

    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/60#v5)</sup> Then thou shalt see, and flow together, and thine heart shall fear, and be enlarged; because the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee.
    >

**[Ezra 7:21-24](/bible/ezra/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezra/7#v21)</sup> And I, even I Artaxerxes the king, do make a decree to all the treasurers which are beyond the river, that whatsoever Ezra the priest, the scribe of the law of the God of heaven, shall require of you, it be done speedily,

- Artaxerxes decreed to the treasurers beyond the river to supply Ezra speedily with silver, wheat, wine, oil, and salt, so that no wrath would come against the king and his sons.
- The priests, Levites, singers, porters, and Nethinims were freed from toll, tribute, and custom. This is using the favor of those set over you without abusing it, being granted the freedom the world around still had to pay.

**[Ezra 7:25-28](/bible/ezra/7#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/ezra/7#v25)</sup> And thou, Ezra, after the wisdom of thy God, that is in thine hand, set magistrates and judges, which may judge all the people that are beyond the river, all such as know the laws of thy God; and teach ye them that know them not.
>

- Ezra was told, after the wisdom of his God in his hand, to set magistrates and judges to judge the people and to teach those who did not know the laws of the Most High.
- The king decreed judgment on any who would not do the law of God and the law of the king. Ezra then blessed the Most High who put it in the king's heart to beautify the house, and was strengthened as the hand of his God was upon him. A heathen king supported the work of the prophets even though he was not an Israelite.

# Ezra 8: The Company Returns And Fasts At Ahava

Read the chapter → [Ezra 8](/bible/ezra/8)

**[Ezra 8:1-14](/bible/ezra/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezra/8#v1)</sup> These are now the chief of their fathers, and this is the genealogy of them that went up with me from Babylon, in the reign of Artaxerxes the king.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezra/8#v2)</sup> Of the sons of Phinehas; Gershom: of the sons of Ithamar; Daniel: of the sons of David; Hattush.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezra/8#v3)</sup> Of the sons of Shechaniah, of the sons of Pharosh; Zechariah: and with him were reckoned by genealogy of the males an hundred and fifty.
>

- The chief of the fathers and their genealogy are recorded, listing the heads of families who went up with Ezra from Babylon and the number of males with each.
- Every name is set down deliberately. These were actual people who put their work into the building of the house, and names mean something, which is why Israel desires their names written in the book.

**[Ezra 8:15-20](/bible/ezra/8#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/ezra/8#v15)</sup> And I gathered them together to the river that runneth to Ahava; and there abode we in tents three days: and I viewed the people, and the priests, and found there none of the sons of Levi.

>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezra/8#v20)</sup> Also of the Nethinims, whom David and the princes had appointed for the service of the Levites, two hundred and twenty Nethinims: all of them were expressed by name.

- Ezra gathered the company at the river Ahava, abode three days, and viewing the people found none of the sons of Levi, so he sent men of understanding to Iddo at Casiphia to bring ministers for the house.
- By the good hand of the Most High they brought back men of understanding of the sons of Levi and a large number of Nethinims appointed for the service, all expressed by name. Ezra knew which men among him had understanding and called on them.

**[Ezra 8:21-22](/bible/ezra/8#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezra/8#v21)</sup> Then I proclaimed a fast there, at the river of Ahava, that we might afflict ourselves before our God, to seek of him a right way for us, and for our little ones, and for all our substance.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezra/8#v22)</sup> For I was ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help us against the enemy in the way: because we had spoken unto the king, saying, The hand of our God is upon all them for good that seek him; but his power and his wrath is against all them that forsake him.

- Ezra proclaimed a fast at the river Ahava that they might afflict their souls before the Most High and seek of him a right way for themselves, their little ones, and their substance. To afflict the soul is to fast, taking neither food nor water, and one may fast and pray for wisdom and a right path.
- Ezra was ashamed to require of the king a band of soldiers and horsemen to help against the enemy in the way, having testified that the hand of God is upon all who seek him for good.

  - **[Isaiah 58:5](/bible/isaiah/58#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/58#v5)</sup> Is it such a fast that I have chosen? a day for a man to afflict his soul? is it to bow down his head as a bulrush, and to spread sackcloth and ashes under him? wilt thou call this a fast, and an acceptable day to the Lord?

    The fast the Most High chooses is a day to afflict the soul before him.
  - **[James 1:5](/bible/james/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/james/1#v5)</sup> If any of you lack wisdom, let him ask of God, that giveth to all men liberally, and upbraideth not; and it shall be given him.

    Lacking wisdom, one may ask of God, who gives liberally, and it shall be given.

The recording ended at [Ezra 8:22](/bible/ezra/8#v22). The remainder of the chapter is included here in full King James text so the chapter is complete for reading and study.
