## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Peter 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-8, 10-15, 17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Peter 2:1](/bible/2-peter/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-peter/2#v1)</sup> But there were false prophets also among the people, even as there shall be false teachers among you, who privily shall bring in damnable heresies, even denying the Lord that bought them, and bring upon themselves swift destruction.

- “But there were false prophets also among the people, even as there shall be false teachers among you”: as Christ said, false prophets will arise in the time of tribulation, when iniquity abounds. In chapter 1 Peter speaks of his death; these arise after he is gone. As the truth spreads and persecution takes place, many of the prominent Israelite leaders, primarily of this camp, will be killed, locked up or exiled, and then the wicked Israelites, Christian and individualite alike, will come in to spread confusion.
- That is what happened when Peter, Paul and John were dead. The church fathers came along, Justin Martyr, Tertullian, Augustine, Irenaeus, Ignatius, and pushed ideologies with the Bible. Most of them were into Greek philosophy and used the Greek way of thinking to interpret the Bible, and came out with the Trinity and the Logos, Greek metaphysics. Once John and Polycarp were dead and gone, madness arose. Justin Martyr taught the Trinity and that Christians could be good Romans and all be saved. He was a sellout, and he got martyred anyway. Many Israelites are doing the exact same thing: teach that everyone can be saved, avoid persecution, and live out captivity peacefully. That’s not how that works.
- “Who privily shall bring in damnable heresies, even denying the Lord that bought them”: saying Christ had no color, that he was translucent and you couldn’t see him. “And bring upon themselves swift destruction.”
- “There were false prophets also among the people, even as there shall be false teachers among you”: like these lying churches, Methodist, Baptist, Jehovah’s Witness, 7th Day Adventist, apologists. They don’t know the Bible, they don’t talk prophecy, and they can’t see that the God of this Bible is angry and is going to destroy people. They argue about whether the Gentiles will be saved, when you don’t know if you’re going to be saved yourself. You’re worried about them, and your ass is going to get burned up when God turns it up.
