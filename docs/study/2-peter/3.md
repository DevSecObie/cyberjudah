**[2 Peter 3:17](/bible/2-peter/3#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-peter/3#v17)</sup> Ye therefore, beloved, seeing ye know these things before, beware lest ye also, being led away with the error of the wicked, fall from your own stedfastness.

- “Ye therefore, beloved, seeing ye know these things before”: you know beforehand that the unlearned and unstable twist Paul’s letters. “beware lest ye also, being led away with the error of the wicked, fall from your own stedfastness.”
- The error of the wicked comes from those that wrest the other scriptures and Paul’s letters. When you start teaching that your oppressor can be granted the same benefits as you, that the nations can receive some twisted salvation of some kind, you are in the error of the wicked. It is a whole different gospel.
- “Beware lest ye also, being led away with the error of the wicked, fall from your own stedfastness.” Don't fall from your steadfast belief in the validity of Paul's writings because of the error of the wicked. The error of the wicked takes Paul's letters and says all nations can be saved; that Paul grafted the other nations in; that we can commit adultery; that the Sabbath day is changed; that we don't have to keep the laws anymore. All of that is the error of the wicked.
