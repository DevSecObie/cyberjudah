## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Peter 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4-11, 13-15, 19-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Peter 1:4](/bible/2-peter/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-peter/1#v4)</sup> Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.

- “Whereby are given unto us exceeding great and precious promises: that by these ye might be partakers of the divine nature, having escaped the corruption that is in the world through lust.” The promises belong to Israel. The divine nature is not the world's nature; the commandments show the difference. Escape from corruption requires leaving the lust that drives the surrounding society.
- Corruption is applied to sexual disorder, including men lying with men, women lying with women, paedophilia and abortion, and to a man so controlled by lust that he will pursue sex without restraint. A man cannot excuse the inability to govern his own body while expecting a woman to recognize his leadership. God's promises call the people out of those patterns.
- The same separation includes honouring God's feast days rather than choosing the world's holidays. The feast of dedication is about the recovery and cleansing of the temple, not continuing the habits of the people who profaned it.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 1:1](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)</sup> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

    “Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,” Peter writes to the scattered strangers in Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia and Bithynia. These are Israelites scattered among those places. The audience must be identified before the promises are applied. Galatia is also the region addressed in Paul's letter to the Galatians; a foreign place name does not by itself make the people another nation.
  - **[1 Peter 2:9](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)</sup> But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:

    “But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:” The chosen generation, royal priesthood, holy nation and peculiar people are Israel. This identifies the people meant by the promises and the call out of darkness. The national description is not discarded because the letter is in the New Testament or because the people live scattered among other nations.
  - **[Romans 9:4](/bible/romans/9#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/9#v4)</sup> Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises;

    “Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises;” Adoption, glory, covenants, the giving of the law, service and promises belong to the Israelites. The glory is connected to the kingdom. The list establishes who received the promises Peter describes and why the scattered people are being called back to God's nature.

**[2 Peter 1:5-7](/bible/2-peter/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-peter/1#v5)</sup> And beside this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-peter/1#v6)</sup> And to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-peter/1#v7)</sup> And to godliness brotherly kindness; and to brotherly kindness charity.

- Here are the fruits of the spirit also. “And beside this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge.” You must have virtue. You must have knowledge: learning the scriptures, learning how to break them down. We are not going to be on social media all the time, so you have to learn the knowledge of God. Brothers among us who are not coming around the deacons, the bishops and the officers to learn the knowledge of God are not going to last in this truth.
- “And to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness”: you must learn patience and learn to be godly. “And to godliness brotherly kindness”: we must be brotherly and kind to each other. “And to brotherly kindness charity”: love God with all your heart and love your brother as yourself. All of these are the fruits your tree must be bringing forth.
- “And beside this, giving all diligence, add to your faith virtue; and to virtue knowledge;” Faith must be developed with diligence, virtue and knowledge. Virtue concerns the strength and character needed to govern oneself. The work of becoming a man of God includes learning and applying the scriptures, not merely professing belief.
- “And to knowledge temperance; and to temperance patience; and to patience godliness;” Temperance is self-control. A man needs command of his appetites, patience and godliness rather than continually giving in to sexual desire or other impulses. These qualities are built together; the discipline expected of a warrior is also required in his private conduct.

**[2 Peter 1:8](/bible/2-peter/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-peter/1#v8)</sup> For if these things be in you, and abound, they make you that ye shall neither be barren nor unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.

- “For if these things be in you, and abound.” To abound means they stay in you. These things can be in you and then leave you: you can be bearing fruits and stop. Some are among us six or seven years, growing and developing fruits, leaves green, and then somebody offends them and they stop bringing forth fruits; all these things leave them.
- “They make you that ye shall neither be barren nor unfruitful in the knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ.” For you not to be barren and unfruitful, these things have to be in you and stay in you. We do not change up.

**[2 Peter 1:9](/bible/2-peter/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-peter/1#v9)</sup> But he that lacketh these things is blind, and cannot see afar off, and hath forgotten that he was purged from his old sins.

- “But he that lacketh these things is blind, and cannot see afar off, and hath forgotten that he was purged from his old sins.” If you lack these things you are blind, and you have forgotten that you were purged from your old sins. You have to have these things inside of you for your tree to bring forth the fruits of the spirit.

**[2 Peter 1:10](/bible/2-peter/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-peter/1#v10)</sup> Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence to make your calling and election sure: for if ye do these things, ye shall never fall:

- “Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence”: write this down, diligence means persistent work. Give diligence “to make your calling and election sure: for if ye do these things, ye shall never fall”.
- You know why some of y’all get caught up in sins? Because you have idle time, nothing to do. And brothers and sisters, there is always something to do. This truth is too big. There are too many Israelites scattered worldwide; the Bible says we number as the sands of the sea, and we have not reached most of them, and you have idle time. That’s why you are in the midst of adultery. Look at all this work we got to do.
- A lot of times they think Esau’s work is what they are supposed to be occupied in, but it is the Lord’s work you are supposed to be occupied in. Don’t think you are going to show diligence in Esau’s kingdom; you are supposed to show diligence in the Lord’s business.
- “Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence”. Brothers, you feel that battle fatigue? The Bible says give diligence. How many of you ever been in a fist fight? You get tired, but you know if you give up you done. That’s how this truth is. You hope somebody break it up, but if nobody does and you give up, you getting stomped out, your teeth on the curb. Damn, nobody want to go through that. I don’t care how much battle fatigue: give diligence.

**[2 Peter 1:11](/bible/2-peter/1#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-peter/1#v11)</sup> For so an entrance shall be ministered unto you abundantly into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

- And what is waiting on you? “For so an entrance shall be ministered unto you abundantly into the everlasting kingdom of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.”
- When your mission is done you will hear the words, Well done, thou good and faithful servant, enter into the paradise prepared for you, because you as a brother, a soldier, a prophet of God, gave diligence to the mission that was at hand while you were upon the earth.

**[2 Peter 1:13](/bible/2-peter/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-peter/1#v13)</sup> Yea, I think it meet, as long as I am in this tabernacle, to stir you up by putting you in remembrance;

- Regarding the tabernacle: I dwell in them. Peter says “as long as I am in this tabernacle.” What tabernacle? The earthy tabernacle, his body of flesh. While he was still in it, he would “stir you up by putting you in remembrance.”

**[2 Peter 1:14](/bible/2-peter/1#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-peter/1#v14)</sup> Knowing that shortly I must put off this my tabernacle, even as our Lord Jesus Christ hath shewed me.

- “Knowing that shortly I must put off this my tabernacle.” Peter’s tabernacle is his body. When you read about tabernacles, it is not just talking about the booths we dwell in; it also means this body, this flesh we have. “Even as our Lord Jesus Christ hath shewed me”: Christ told him he was going to die.
- “Knowing that shortly I must put off this my tabernacle”: I must put this tabernacle off, meaning I am going to die. “Even as our Lord Jesus Christ hath shewed me”, because Christ told Peter he would be put to death as an old man.
- The point drawn from it is the putting off of this earthly tabernacle. John speaks of the tabernacle, and Peter speaks of it.

**[2 Peter 1:15](/bible/2-peter/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-peter/1#v15)</sup> Moreover I will endeavour that ye may be able after my decease to have these things always in remembrance.

- “That ye may be able after my decease to have these things always in remembrance”: after I die and put off this tabernacle. That is how you put off this tabernacle: this flesh has to die. Scripture calls it transfiguration.

**[2 Peter 1:19](/bible/2-peter/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-peter/1#v19)</sup> We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts:

- Some fool says, we are not the prophets. You’re crazy if you think we’re not the prophets. “We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place.” We are that light that shineth in a dark place. America is the dark place; the whole world is a dark place, brothers, and the Israelite is that light, “until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts.”
- “We have also a more sure word of prophecy”: Peter is speaking, the apostles. Everybody doesn’t have it. The apostles understood the prophecies and what took place in the past. “A light that shineth in a dark place”: that is us.

**[2 Peter 1:20](/bible/2-peter/1#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-peter/1#v20)</sup> Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation.

- “Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation.” There is no private interpretation. Whatever we say the scripture says, that’s what it says. That is the authority we have over this Bible, because we are the prophets. You were looking for the prophets? Here we are. We outside.
- “Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation.” Know this before you start reading the Bible. There is no different interpretation of the Bible: it says the same thing from the beginning to the end. There is a right way to interpret it and a wrong way to break it down. No: well, I think it means this.
- There are key principles. The foundation of being an Israelite is that you went into slavery for breaking God’s laws; Jesus Christ is a black man, and he is coming back to redeem you, and only you, from your sins and to save you from your enemies. You have to keep God’s laws: stop committing adultery, stop lying and stealing, stop being evil, stop following Babylon and Babylon’s lies, and come out from among her.
- As time goes by the Most High is going to increase all of us with more understanding of the scriptures. Our understanding grows as we grow spiritually, so we understand the hope and why we are doing what we are doing. And those the Lord God has set up to teach, the apostles on the earth, are going to teach it the right way.

    “And though I have the gift of prophecy, and understand all mysteries, and all knowledge”. Prophecy is a gift. Paul had the gift of prophecy and understood all mysteries.
  - **[Matthew 13:11](/bible/matthew/13#v11)**

God reveals it to them, they reveal it to the people, and the people understand it. It is not given to everyone.

**[2 Peter 1:21](/bible/2-peter/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-peter/1#v21)</sup> For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.

- “For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.” You’re looking at the holy men of God: Bishop Nathanyel, the bishops, the deacons, the captains, the officers. We are the prophets. You did not know what the Bible says until the prophet told you, and we are telling you what is about to happen.
- At the Million Man March an old fool cut Deacon Asaph off after five minutes so he could watch young boys dance, when everyone else finished their speech. The men around Bishop know the Bible; Bishop is the only one breaking down Revelation from chapter 1 to the last chapter and backward. And even if you don’t believe, Christ said believe for the works’ sake. Nobody is doing the work IUIC is doing. We go by air, by sea, by land, on a donkey, on a horse, in a boat with no engine and a hole in it. Go do better then. You can’t.
- “For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man”: man didn’t sit down and make stuff up. “But holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.” The Most High spoke through the prophets, our forefathers.
- All the prophets say the same thing, but in different ways, some with more detail than others. Anytime you think you see something in the scriptures and the other prophets do not say the same thing, you have your own interpretation.
