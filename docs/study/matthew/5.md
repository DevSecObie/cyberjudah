**[Matthew 5:1-12](/bible/matthew/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/5#v1)</sup> And seeing the multitudes, he went up into a mountain: and when he was set, his disciples came unto him:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/5#v2)</sup> And he opened his mouth, and taught them, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/5#v3)</sup> Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/5#v4)</sup> Blessed are they that mourn: for they shall be comforted.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/5#v5)</sup> Blessed are the meek: for they shall inherit the earth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/5#v6)</sup> Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness: for they shall be filled.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/5#v7)</sup> Blessed are the merciful: for they shall obtain mercy.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/5#v8)</sup> Blessed are the pure in heart: for they shall see God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/5#v9)</sup> Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/5#v10)</sup> Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness’ sake: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/5#v11)</sup> Blessed are ye, when men shall revile you, and persecute you, and shall say all manner of evil against you falsely, for my sake.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/5#v12)</sup> Rejoice, and be exceeding glad: for great is your reward in heaven: for so persecuted they the prophets which were before you.

- Seeing the multitudes he goes up into a mountain and opens the blessings: the poor in spirit, they that mourn, the meek, they which hunger and thirst after righteousness, the merciful, the pure in heart, the peacemakers, and they which are persecuted for righteousness sake.
- The last of them is spoken directly to those hearing him: rejoice when men revile and persecute you falsely, for so persecuted they the prophets which were before you.

**[Matthew 5:13-16](/bible/matthew/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/5#v13)</sup> Ye are the salt of the earth: but if the salt have lost his savour, wherewith shall it be salted? it is thenceforth good for nothing, but to be cast out, and to be trodden under foot of men.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/5#v14)</sup> Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/5#v15)</sup> Neither do men light a candle, and put it under a bushel, but on a candlestick; and it giveth light unto all that are in the house.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/5#v16)</sup> Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works, and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

- Ye are the salt of the earth and the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hid, and a candle is not lit to be put under a bushel.
- Let your light so shine before men that they may see your good works and glorify your Father which is in heaven.

**[Matthew 5:17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)</sup> Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.

- Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.
- What he came to fulfil is the prophecies written concerning him, not the commandments given to Israel to keep. Everything in chapter 2 of this same gospel is an example of that fulfilling.

**[Matthew 5:18-20](/bible/matthew/5#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/5#v18)</sup> For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/5#v19)</sup> Whosoever therefore shall break one of these least commandments, and shall teach men so, he shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven: but whosoever shall do and teach them, the same shall be called great in the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/5#v20)</sup> For I say unto you, That except your righteousness shall exceed the righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, ye shall in no case enter into the kingdom of heaven.

- Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.
- Whosoever breaks one of these least commandments and teaches men so shall be called the least in the kingdom of heaven, and whosoever does and teaches them shall be called great.
- Except your righteousness exceed the righteousness of the scribes and Pharisees, ye shall in no case enter into the kingdom of heaven.

**[Matthew 5:21-26](/bible/matthew/5#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/5#v21)</sup> Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not kill; and whosoever shall kill shall be in danger of the judgment:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause shall be in danger of the judgment: and whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council: but whosoever shall say, Thou fool, shall be in danger of hell fire.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/5#v23)</sup> Therefore if thou bring thy gift to the altar, and there rememberest that thy brother hath ought against thee;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/5#v24)</sup> Leave there thy gift before the altar, and go thy way; first be reconciled to thy brother, and then come and offer thy gift.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/5#v25)</sup> Agree with thine adversary quickly, whiles thou art in the way with him; lest at any time the adversary deliver thee to the judge, and the judge deliver thee to the officer, and thou be cast into prison.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/5#v26)</sup> Verily I say unto thee, Thou shalt by no means come out thence, till thou hast paid the uttermost farthing.

- The commandment said Thou shalt not kill; Christ magnifies it to being angry with a brother without a cause and to what a man calls his brother.
- If thou bring thy gift to the altar and rememberest that thy brother hath ought against thee, leave the gift, be reconciled first, and then come and offer.

**[Matthew 5:27-32](/bible/matthew/5#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/5#v27)</sup> Ye have heard that it was said by them of old time, Thou shalt not commit adultery:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/5#v28)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever looketh on a woman to lust after her hath committed adultery with her already in his heart.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/5#v29)</sup> And if thy right eye offend thee, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: for it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not that thy whole body should be cast into hell.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/5#v30)</sup> And if thy right hand offend thee, cut it off, and cast it from thee: for it is profitable for thee that one of thy members should perish, and not that thy whole body should be cast into hell.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/5#v31)</sup> It hath been said, Whosoever shall put away his wife, let him give her a writing of divorcement:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/5#v32)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever shall put away his wife, saving for the cause of fornication, causeth her to commit adultery: and whosoever shall marry her that is divorced committeth adultery.

- The commandment said Thou shalt not commit adultery; Christ magnifies it to looking on a woman to lust after her.
- Better that one member perish than that the whole body be cast into hell, and putting away a wife saving for the cause of fornication causes her to commit adultery.

**[Matthew 5:33-37](/bible/matthew/5#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/5#v33)</sup> Again, ye have heard that it hath been said by them of old time, Thou shalt not forswear thyself, but shalt perform unto the Lord thine oaths:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/5#v34)</sup> But I say unto you, Swear not at all; neither by heaven; for it is God’s throne:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/5#v35)</sup> Nor by the earth; for it is his footstool: neither by Jerusalem; for it is the city of the great King.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/5#v36)</sup> Neither shalt thou swear by thy head, because thou canst not make one hair white or black.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/5#v37)</sup> But let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.

- Swear not at all, neither by heaven nor by the earth nor by Jerusalem nor by thy head. Let your communication be Yea, yea; Nay, nay.

    In the multitude of words there wanteth not sin.

**[Matthew 5:38-39](/bible/matthew/5#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/5#v38)</sup> Ye have heard that it hath been said, An eye for an eye, and a tooth for a tooth:
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/5#v39)</sup> But I say unto you, That ye resist not evil: but whosoever shall smite thee on thy right cheek, turn to him the other also.

- An eye for an eye is answered with resist not evil. This is not a command to stand and be beaten; it is a command not to pay back evil as though it were owed.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 3:9](/bible/1-peter/3#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/3#v9)</sup> Not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing; knowing that ye are thereunto called, that ye should inherit a blessing.

    Not rendering evil for evil, or railing for railing: but contrariwise blessing.

> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/5#v41)</sup> And whosoever shall compel thee to go a mile, go with him twain.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/5#v42)</sup> Give to him that asketh thee, and from him that would borrow of thee turn not thou away.

- Give to him that asketh thee, and from him that would borrow of thee turn not thou away. Christ is magnifying a law already written to Israel.

    Thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need.

**[Matthew 5:43-44](/bible/matthew/5#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/5#v43)</sup> Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/5#v44)</sup> But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you;

- Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you.
- The enemy in view here is an enemy within your own people. Saul and David were both Israelites, and Saul was David's enemy.
- The reason for the command is that the enemy may yet repent, as one who persecuted the disciples afterward became one of them.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 18:29](/bible/1-samuel/18#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/1-samuel/18#v29)</sup> And Saul was yet the more afraid of David; and Saul became David’s enemy continually.

    Saul was yet the more afraid of David; and Saul became David's enemy continually.

    > <sup>[17](/bible/1-samuel/24#v17)</sup> And he said to David, Thou art more righteous than I: for thou hast rewarded me good, whereas I have rewarded thee evil.

    Thou art more righteous than I: for thou hast rewarded me good, whereas I have rewarded thee evil.

**[Matthew 5:45-47](/bible/matthew/5#v45)**

> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/5#v45)</sup> That ye may be the children of your Father which is in heaven: for he maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/5#v46)</sup> For if ye love them which love you, what reward have ye? do not even the publicans the same?
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/matthew/5#v47)</sup> And if ye salute your brethren only, what do ye more than others? do not even the publicans so?

- He maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust. Loving only those who love you is no more than the publicans do.

**[Matthew 5:48](/bible/matthew/5#v48)**

> <sup>[48](/bible/matthew/5#v48)</sup> Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect.

- Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect. Perfection here is a heart perfect with the Most High, walking in his statutes and keeping his commandments.
- Everything from verse 38 down is charity, and charity is the bond of perfectness.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Kings 8:61](/bible/1-kings/8#v61)**
    > <sup>[61](/bible/1-kings/8#v61)</sup> Let your heart therefore be perfect with the Lord our God, to walk in his statutes, and to keep his commandments, as at this day.

    Let your heart therefore be perfect with the Lord our God, to walk in his statutes, and to keep his commandments.
  - **[Colossians 3:14](/bible/colossians/3#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/colossians/3#v14)</sup> And above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness.

    And above all these things put on charity, which is the bond of perfectness.
