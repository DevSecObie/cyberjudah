**[Matthew 25:1-13](/bible/matthew/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/25#v1)</sup> Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/25#v2)</sup> And five of them were wise, and five were foolish.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/25#v3)</sup> They that were foolish took their lamps, and took no oil with them:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/25#v4)</sup> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/25#v5)</sup> While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/25#v6)</sup> And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/25#v7)</sup> Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/25#v8)</sup> And the foolish said unto the wise, Give us of your oil; for our lamps are gone out.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/25#v9)</sup> But the wise answered, saying, Not so; lest there be not enough for us and you: but go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/25#v10)</sup> And while they went to buy, the bridegroom came; and they that were ready went in with him to the marriage: and the door was shut.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/25#v11)</sup> Afterward came also the other virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/25#v12)</sup> But he answered and said, Verily I say unto you, I know you not.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/25#v13)</sup> Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh.

- Five were wise and five were foolish. The foolish took their lamps and took no oil with them; the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps. The oil is the wisdom of the scriptures.
- The foolish come afterward saying, Lord, Lord, open to us, and are answered, Verily I say unto you, I know you not.
- Calling on his name while breaking the commandments is exactly this. Men bless what the law calls an abomination and still say Lord, Lord.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 7:21](/bible/matthew/7#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/7#v21)</sup> Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.

    Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father.
  - **[Psalms 40:8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)</sup> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.

    I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart. That is the will of the Father, and the foolish did not have it.

**[Matthew 25:14-30](/bible/matthew/25#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/25#v14)</sup> For the kingdom of heaven is as a man travelling into a far country, who called his own servants, and delivered unto them his goods.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/25#v15)</sup> And unto one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one; to every man according to his several ability; and straightway took his journey.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/25#v16)</sup> Then he that had received the five talents went and traded with the same, and made them other five talents.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/25#v17)</sup> And likewise he that had received two, he also gained other two.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/25#v18)</sup> But he that had received one went and digged in the earth, and hid his lord’s money.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/25#v19)</sup> After a long time the lord of those servants cometh, and reckoneth with them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/25#v20)</sup> And so he that had received five talents came and brought other five talents, saying, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/25#v21)</sup> His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/25#v22)</sup> He also that had received two talents came and said, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me two talents: behold, I have gained two other talents beside them.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/25#v23)</sup> His lord said unto him, Well done, good and faithful servant; thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/25#v24)</sup> Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/25#v25)</sup> And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth: lo, there thou hast that is thine.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/25#v26)</sup> His lord answered and said unto him, Thou wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I have not strawed:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/25#v27)</sup> Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming I should have received mine own with usury.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/25#v28)</sup> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/25#v29)</sup> For unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance: but from him that hath not shall be taken away even that which he hath.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/25#v30)</sup> And cast ye the unprofitable servant into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

- Every man received according to his several ability, and the two who traded were told, Well done, thou good and faithful servant.
- The one who hid his talent blamed his lord and called him a hard man, and was answered, Thou wicked and slothful servant. That fearful spirit is not of the Most High, and a man should examine himself for it.
- Unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance; but from him that hath not shall be taken away even that which he hath. Knowledge that is not used is knowledge that is lost.

**[Matthew 25:31-46](/bible/matthew/25#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/25#v31)</sup> When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/25#v32)</sup> And before him shall be gathered all nations: and he shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/25#v33)</sup> And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/25#v34)</sup> Then shall the King say unto them on his right hand, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/25#v35)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/25#v36)</sup> Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/25#v37)</sup> Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/25#v38)</sup> When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/25#v39)</sup> Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/25#v40)</sup> And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/25#v41)</sup> Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/25#v42)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink:
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/25#v43)</sup> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/25#v44)</sup> Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/25#v45)</sup> Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/25#v46)</sup> And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous into life eternal.

- He separates them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats, and says to those on the right hand, Inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.
- Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me. Meeting the need of a brother or sister in the body is counted as done to Christ himself.
- Those on the left hand are answered with the same measure: inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.
