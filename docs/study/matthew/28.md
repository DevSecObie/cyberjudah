## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 28 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 16, 18-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 28:16](/bible/matthew/28#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/28#v16)</sup> Then the eleven disciples went away into Galilee, into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them.

- Remember what Christ said: he’s going to visit Teman. I’m going to visit you. “Then the eleven disciples went away into Galilee, into a mountain where Jesus had appointed them”: keep in mind he told the disciples to meet him at a mountain where he appointed them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 16:14-16](/bible/mark/16#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/mark/16#v14)</sup> Afterward he appeared unto the eleven as they sat at meat, and upbraided them with their unbelief and hardness of heart, because they believed not them which had seen him after he was risen.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/mark/16#v15)</sup> And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/mark/16#v16)</sup> He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned.

    “Afterward he appeared unto the eleven as they sat at meat, and upbraided them with their unbelief and hardness of heart”: he cursed them out for not believing, “because they believed not them which had seen him after he was risen.” “And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature”: where Israel is found. “He that believeth and is baptized shall be saved; but he that believeth not shall be damned.”
  - **[Mark 16:19-20](/bible/mark/16#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/mark/16#v19)</sup> So then after the Lord had spoken unto them, he was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/mark/16#v20)</sup> And they went forth, and preached every where, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word with signs following. Amen.

    “So then after the Lord had spoken unto them, he was received up into heaven, and sat on the right hand of God. And they went forth, and preached every where, the Lord working with them, and confirming the word with signs following.” Mark 16 is pretty much the introduction to Acts chapter 1; it is a precept to Acts 1.
  - **[Luke 24:50-51](/bible/luke/24#v50)**
    > <sup>[50](/bible/luke/24#v50)</sup> And he led them out as far as to Bethany, and he lifted up his hands, and blessed them.
    >
    > <sup>[51](/bible/luke/24#v51)</sup> And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them, and carried up into heaven.

    He said meet me at this mountain, and from that same mountain he flew up from them. “And he led them out as far as to Bethany”: that same mountain. “And he lifted up his hands, and blessed them. And it came to pass, while he blessed them, he was parted from them, and carried up into heaven.”

**[Matthew 28:18](/bible/matthew/28#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/28#v18)</sup> And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.

- “And Jesus came and spake unto them, saying, All power is given unto me in heaven and in earth.” When Christ died, the Lord said good job; now when the time comes you will go and take what’s yours. That is all power.

**[Matthew 28:19-20](/bible/matthew/28#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/28#v19)</sup> Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/28#v20)</sup> Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world. Amen.

- “Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost: teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, even unto the end of the world.” We were commanded to go to all nations. Why? Because Israel is scattered in all nations.
- This is the gospel being preached, the first link in the chain of events that leads to our deliverance: the gospel preached to Israel, then the economic reset, then tribulation, then war, Armageddon, then deliverance.
- Christ said “Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost”. That’s the verse Christians use to say the gospel is for everybody. But Christ said teach all nations because the Israelites would be scattered in all nations. Going back to it after the precepts: now we understand what he means, because the Israelites would be scattered in all nations.
- “Go ye therefore, and teach all nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Ghost.” Many times Christians use that to say the gospel is for everybody. But that is not the reason he said teach all nations. He said it because the twelve tribes of Israel were scattered into all nations, and the teaching is what raises them up.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.

    “And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations”: the Israelites would be scattered. That’s the prophecy, and this is why Christ said go and teach all nations, because the Israelites would be scattered in all nations. “And ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.”
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    “And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.” Wood and stone: Islam and Christianity, the two major religions of our people.
  - **[Acts 2:5](/bible/acts/2#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/acts/2#v5)</sup> And there were dwelling at Jerusalem Jews, devout men, out of every nation under heaven.

    “And there were dwelling at Jerusalem Jews, devout men, out of every nation under heaven”: out of every nation, because we were scattered in all nations.
