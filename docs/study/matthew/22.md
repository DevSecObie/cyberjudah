## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 22 through 25. It is a long reading with precepts brought in along the way. Christ answers the Pharisees, the Herodians and the Sadducees in turn and silences them, then turns and pronounces the woes upon the scribes and Pharisees. Chapter 24 gives the signs of the end and chapter 25 closes with three parables of judgment: the ten virgins, the talents, and the sheep divided from the goats.

**[Matthew 22:1-6](/bible/matthew/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/22#v1)</sup> And Jesus answered and spake unto them again by parables, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/22#v2)</sup> The kingdom of heaven is like unto a certain king, which made a marriage for his son,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/22#v3)</sup> And sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden to the wedding: and they would not come.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/22#v4)</sup> Again, he sent forth other servants, saying, Tell them which are bidden, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come unto the marriage.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/22#v5)</sup> But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his farm, another to his merchandise:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/22#v6)</sup> And the remnant took his servants, and entreated them spitefully, and slew them.

- The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a king which made a marriage for his son and sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden, and they would not come.
- The servants in this parable are not any man who says he loves God. The scripture names who the servants are.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 25:55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)**
    > <sup>[55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)</sup> For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

    For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt.

**[Matthew 22:7-10](/bible/matthew/22#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/22#v7)</sup> But when the king heard thereof, he was wroth: and he sent forth his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned up their city.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/22#v8)</sup> Then saith he to his servants, The wedding is ready, but they which were bidden were not worthy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/22#v9)</sup> Go ye therefore into the highways, and as many as ye shall find, bid to the marriage.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/22#v10)</sup> So those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good: and the wedding was furnished with guests.

- The king sends his armies and destroys those murderers and burns up their city, then sends into the highways, and the wedding is furnished with guests, both bad and good.

**[Matthew 22:11-14](/bible/matthew/22#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/22#v11)</sup> And when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man which had not on a wedding garment:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/22#v12)</sup> And he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding garment? And he was speechless.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/22#v13)</sup> Then said the king to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/22#v14)</sup> For many are called, but few are chosen.

- The man without a wedding garment is speechless, and is bound hand and foot and cast into outer darkness. Many are called, but few are chosen.
- The garment is not cloth. It is righteousness, which is the keeping of the laws of the Most High.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 132:9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)</sup> Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

    Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

**[Matthew 22:15-22](/bible/matthew/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/22#v15)</sup> Then went the Pharisees, and took counsel how they might entangle him in his talk.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/22#v16)</sup> And they sent out unto him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Master, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God in truth, neither carest thou for any man: for thou regardest not the person of men.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/22#v17)</sup> Tell us therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Cesar, or not?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/22#v18)</sup> But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, Why tempt ye me, ye hypocrites?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/22#v19)</sup> Shew me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a penny.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/22#v20)</sup> And he saith unto them, Whose is this image and superscription?
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/22#v21)</sup> They say unto him, Cesar’s. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Cesar the things which are Cesar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/22#v22)</sup> When they had heard these words, they marvelled, and left him, and went their way.

- The Pharisees take counsel how to entangle him in his talk and send their disciples with the Herodians to ask about tribute to Caesar.
- He perceives their wickedness, calls them hypocrites, and answers by the image on the coin: Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's.

**[Matthew 22:23-30](/bible/matthew/22#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/22#v23)</sup> The same day came to him the Sadducees, which say that there is no resurrection, and asked him,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/22#v24)</sup> Saying, Master, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/22#v25)</sup> Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first, when he had married a wife, deceased, and, having no issue, left his wife unto his brother:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/22#v26)</sup> Likewise the second also, and the third, unto the seventh.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/22#v27)</sup> And last of all the woman died also.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/22#v28)</sup> Therefore in the resurrection whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/22#v29)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/22#v30)</sup> For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.

- The Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, bring the case of seven brethren and one wife to make the resurrection look absurd.
- Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God. In the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.
- This is not saying there is no marriage in the kingdom. It answers the law they built the question on, which will not need to be kept when there is no more dying.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 25:5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)</sup> If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no child, the wife of the dead shall not marry without unto a stranger: her husband’s brother shall go in unto her, and take her to him to wife, and perform the duty of an husband’s brother unto her.

    The law they were standing on: her husband's brother shall go in unto her, and perform the duty of an husband's brother unto her.

**[Matthew 22:31-33](/bible/matthew/22#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/22#v31)</sup> But as touching the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by God, saying,
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/22#v32)</sup> I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/22#v33)</sup> And when the multitude heard this, they were astonished at his doctrine.

- I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob. God is not the God of the dead, but of the living. The multitude was astonished at his doctrine.

**[Matthew 22:34-40](/bible/matthew/22#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/22#v34)</sup> But when the Pharisees had heard that he had put the Sadducees to silence, they were gathered together.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/22#v35)</sup> Then one of them, which was a lawyer, asked him a question, tempting him, and saying,
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/22#v36)</sup> Master, which is the great commandment in the law?
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/22#v37)</sup> Jesus said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/22#v38)</sup> This is the first and great commandment.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)</sup> And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/22#v40)</sup> On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets.

- Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart is the first and great commandment, and the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
- On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets. That is not a reduction of the law to two rules; these two are the ones the rest hang upon.

**[Matthew 22:41-46](/bible/matthew/22#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/22#v41)</sup> While the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them,
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/22#v42)</sup> Saying, What think ye of Christ? whose son is he? They say unto him, The Son of David.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/22#v43)</sup> He saith unto them, How then doth David in spirit call him Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/22#v44)</sup> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool?
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/22#v45)</sup> If David then call him Lord, how is he his son?
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/22#v46)</sup> And no man was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.

- He asks them whose son Christ is, and they answer, the son of David. Then how does David in spirit call him Lord?
- No man was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.
- He perceived what they came for and answered what they needed rather than what they asked. That is how to deal with men who come only to scoff.
