**[Matthew 27:18](/bible/matthew/27#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/27#v18)</sup> For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.

- “For he knew that for envy they had delivered him.” Envy and hatred are real, brothers. It is a strong demon. It was written all over their faces and their spirit; they could not hide it. Even a heathen could see it.

Heathens make videos sitting down with other heathens: this camp believes this, that camp believes that; this camp is worldwide, an organized movement, global, not domestic; and this camp with the studs on, looking like the Legion of Doom from the WWF, hates these men in purple. Even the heathen know it. That is a damn shame.

    “For he knew that the chief priests had delivered him for envy.” Pilate, a heathen, could see it: it was for envy.

**[Matthew 27:29-30](/bible/matthew/27#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/27#v29)</sup> And when they had platted a crown of thorns, they put it upon his head, and a reed in his right hand: and they bowed the knee before him, and mocked him, saying, Hail, King of the Jews!

And they spit upon him, and took the reed, and smote him on the head.” They mocked Christ, spit on him and hit him on top of the head.
