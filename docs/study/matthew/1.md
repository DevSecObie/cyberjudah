## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 3-23, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 1:17](/bible/matthew/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/1#v17)</sup> So all the generations from Abraham to David are fourteen generations; and from David until the carrying away into Babylon are fourteen generations; and from the carrying away into Babylon unto Christ are fourteen generations.

- “From David until the carrying away into Babylon are fourteen generations”. Write this down, this is what happened within those fourteen generations. The nation of Israel was split into two kingdoms, the Southern Kingdom and the Northern Kingdom: the Southern Kingdom led by Judah, the Northern Kingdom led by Ephraim. After that came the Assyrian captivity: they conquered the Northern Kingdom and took them into another land. After that came Babylon. Babylon not only took over Assyria and the Northern Kingdom, they conquered Judah, the Southern Kingdom, as well and made us slaves. Babylon were ancient Kush, Ethiopia. Write that down.
- “And from the carrying away into Babylon unto Christ are fourteen generations.” From Babylon the Persian Mede captivity came about, then the Greek captivity, and then Christ was born during the Roman captivity. In case you don’t understand: Christ was born when Rome was ruling.
- “From David until the carrying away into Babylon are fourteen generations”: notice he mentions Babylon. “And from the carrying away into Babylon unto Christ are fourteen generations.”
- What are the next empires that rose from Babylon to Christ? After Babylon you have Persia-Media, Greece and Rome. When Christ was born it was during the Roman Empire. So Matthew here is talking about the same history that Christians like to ignore.

- “And she shall bring forth a son”: the angel Gabriel is telling Joseph about the child his wife is going to bring forth. Did Gabriel come with his own thoughts? He saves his people from their sins because the Israelites were in sin. That is why he told the woman caught in adultery, Go and sin no more. He is not talking about the other nations; they didn’t have the covenants or the laws, and the Lord did not deal with them.

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/1#v23)</sup> Behold, a virgin shall be with child, and shall bring forth a son, and they shall call his name Emmanuel, which being interpreted is, God with us.

- “That it might be fulfilled which was spoken of the Lord by the prophet”: Matthew is pointing back to Isaiah 7, a child born as a sign that God was with them.
