## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 22 through 25. It is a long reading with precepts brought in along the way. Christ answers the Pharisees, the Herodians and the Sadducees in turn and silences them, then turns and pronounces the woes upon the scribes and Pharisees. Chapter 24 gives the signs of the end and chapter 25 closes with three parables of judgment: the ten virgins, the talents, and the sheep divided from the goats.

**[Matthew 22:1-6](/bible/matthew/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/22#v1)</sup> And Jesus answered and spake unto them again by parables, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/22#v2)</sup> The kingdom of heaven is like unto a certain king, which made a marriage for his son,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/22#v3)</sup> And sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden to the wedding: and they would not come.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/22#v4)</sup> Again, he sent forth other servants, saying, Tell them which are bidden, Behold, I have prepared my dinner: my oxen and my fatlings are killed, and all things are ready: come unto the marriage.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/22#v5)</sup> But they made light of it, and went their ways, one to his farm, another to his merchandise:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/22#v6)</sup> And the remnant took his servants, and entreated them spitefully, and slew them.

- The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a king which made a marriage for his son and sent forth his servants to call them that were bidden, and they would not come.
- The servants in this parable are not any man who says he loves God. The scripture names who the servants are.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 25:55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)**
    > <sup>[55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)</sup> For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

    For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt.

**[Matthew 22:7-10](/bible/matthew/22#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/22#v7)</sup> But when the king heard thereof, he was wroth: and he sent forth his armies, and destroyed those murderers, and burned up their city.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/22#v8)</sup> Then saith he to his servants, The wedding is ready, but they which were bidden were not worthy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/22#v9)</sup> Go ye therefore into the highways, and as many as ye shall find, bid to the marriage.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/22#v10)</sup> So those servants went out into the highways, and gathered together all as many as they found, both bad and good: and the wedding was furnished with guests.

- The king sends his armies and destroys those murderers and burns up their city, then sends into the highways, and the wedding is furnished with guests, both bad and good.

**[Matthew 22:11-14](/bible/matthew/22#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/22#v11)</sup> And when the king came in to see the guests, he saw there a man which had not on a wedding garment:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/22#v12)</sup> And he saith unto him, Friend, how camest thou in hither not having a wedding garment? And he was speechless.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/22#v13)</sup> Then said the king to the servants, Bind him hand and foot, and take him away, and cast him into outer darkness; there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/22#v14)</sup> For many are called, but few are chosen.

- The man without a wedding garment is speechless, and is bound hand and foot and cast into outer darkness. Many are called, but few are chosen.
- The garment is not cloth. It is righteousness, which is the keeping of the laws of the Most High.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 132:9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)</sup> Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

    Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

**[Matthew 22:15-22](/bible/matthew/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/22#v15)</sup> Then went the Pharisees, and took counsel how they might entangle him in his talk.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/22#v16)</sup> And they sent out unto him their disciples with the Herodians, saying, Master, we know that thou art true, and teachest the way of God in truth, neither carest thou for any man: for thou regardest not the person of men.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/22#v17)</sup> Tell us therefore, What thinkest thou? Is it lawful to give tribute unto Cesar, or not?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/22#v18)</sup> But Jesus perceived their wickedness, and said, Why tempt ye me, ye hypocrites?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/22#v19)</sup> Shew me the tribute money. And they brought unto him a penny.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/22#v20)</sup> And he saith unto them, Whose is this image and superscription?
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/22#v21)</sup> They say unto him, Cesar’s. Then saith he unto them, Render therefore unto Cesar the things which are Cesar’s; and unto God the things that are God’s.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/22#v22)</sup> When they had heard these words, they marvelled, and left him, and went their way.

- The Pharisees take counsel how to entangle him in his talk and send their disciples with the Herodians to ask about tribute to Caesar.
- He perceives their wickedness, calls them hypocrites, and answers by the image on the coin: Render therefore unto Caesar the things which are Caesar's; and unto God the things that are God's.

**[Matthew 22:23-30](/bible/matthew/22#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/22#v23)</sup> The same day came to him the Sadducees, which say that there is no resurrection, and asked him,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/22#v24)</sup> Saying, Master, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/22#v25)</sup> Now there were with us seven brethren: and the first, when he had married a wife, deceased, and, having no issue, left his wife unto his brother:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/22#v26)</sup> Likewise the second also, and the third, unto the seventh.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/22#v27)</sup> And last of all the woman died also.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/22#v28)</sup> Therefore in the resurrection whose wife shall she be of the seven? for they all had her.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/22#v29)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto them, Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/22#v30)</sup> For in the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.

- The Sadducees, who say there is no resurrection, bring the case of seven brethren and one wife to make the resurrection look absurd.
- Ye do err, not knowing the scriptures, nor the power of God. In the resurrection they neither marry, nor are given in marriage, but are as the angels of God in heaven.
- This is not saying there is no marriage in the kingdom. It answers the law they built the question on, which will not need to be kept when there is no more dying.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 25:5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)</sup> If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no child, the wife of the dead shall not marry without unto a stranger: her husband’s brother shall go in unto her, and take her to him to wife, and perform the duty of an husband’s brother unto her.

    The law they were standing on: her husband's brother shall go in unto her, and perform the duty of an husband's brother unto her.

**[Matthew 22:31-33](/bible/matthew/22#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/22#v31)</sup> But as touching the resurrection of the dead, have ye not read that which was spoken unto you by God, saying,
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/22#v32)</sup> I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob? God is not the God of the dead, but of the living.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/22#v33)</sup> And when the multitude heard this, they were astonished at his doctrine.

- I am the God of Abraham, and the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob. God is not the God of the dead, but of the living. The multitude was astonished at his doctrine.

**[Matthew 22:34-40](/bible/matthew/22#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/22#v34)</sup> But when the Pharisees had heard that he had put the Sadducees to silence, they were gathered together.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/22#v35)</sup> Then one of them, which was a lawyer, asked him a question, tempting him, and saying,
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/22#v36)</sup> Master, which is the great commandment in the law?
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/22#v37)</sup> Jesus said unto him, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy mind.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/22#v38)</sup> This is the first and great commandment.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)</sup> And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/22#v40)</sup> On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets.

- Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart is the first and great commandment, and the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
- On these two commandments hang all the law and the prophets. That is not a reduction of the law to two rules; these two are the ones the rest hang upon.

**[Matthew 22:41-46](/bible/matthew/22#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/22#v41)</sup> While the Pharisees were gathered together, Jesus asked them,
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/22#v42)</sup> Saying, What think ye of Christ? whose son is he? They say unto him, The Son of David.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/22#v43)</sup> He saith unto them, How then doth David in spirit call him Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/22#v44)</sup> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand, till I make thine enemies thy footstool?
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/22#v45)</sup> If David then call him Lord, how is he his son?
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/22#v46)</sup> And no man was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.

- He asks them whose son Christ is, and they answer, the son of David. Then how does David in spirit call him Lord?
- No man was able to answer him a word, neither durst any man from that day forth ask him any more questions.
- He perceived what they came for and answered what they needed rather than what they asked. That is how to deal with men who come only to scoff.

# Matthew 23: The Woes upon the Scribes and Pharisees

Read the chapter → [Matthew 23](/bible/matthew/23)

**[Matthew 23:1-4](/bible/matthew/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/23#v1)</sup> Then spake Jesus to the multitude, and to his disciples,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/23#v2)</sup> Saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/23#v3)</sup> All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/23#v4)</sup> For they bind heavy burdens and grievous to be borne, and lay them on men’s shoulders; but they themselves will not move them with one of their fingers.

- The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses' seat. Whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works, for they say, and do not.
- They bind heavy burdens and lay them on men's shoulders, but will not move them with one of their fingers.

**[Matthew 23:5-12](/bible/matthew/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/23#v5)</sup> But all their works they do for to be seen of men: they make broad their phylacteries, and enlarge the borders of their garments,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/23#v6)</sup> And love the uppermost rooms at feasts, and the chief seats in the synagogues,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/23#v7)</sup> And greetings in the markets, and to be called of men, Rabbi, Rabbi.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/23#v8)</sup> But be not ye called Rabbi: for one is your Master, even Christ; and all ye are brethren.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/23#v9)</sup> And call no man your father upon the earth: for one is your Father, which is in heaven.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/23#v10)</sup> Neither be ye called masters: for one is your Master, even Christ.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/23#v11)</sup> But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/23#v12)</sup> And whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased; and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.

- All their works they do for to be seen of men. They love the uppermost rooms at feasts and the chief seats in the synagogues and the greetings in the markets.
- He that is greatest among you shall be your servant. Whosoever shall exalt himself shall be abased, and he that shall humble himself shall be exalted.

**[Matthew 23:13-14](/bible/matthew/23#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/23#v13)</sup> But woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men: for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/23#v14)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye devour widows’ houses, and for a pretence make long prayer: therefore ye shall receive the greater damnation.

- Woe unto you: ye shut up the kingdom of heaven against men, for ye neither go in yourselves, neither suffer ye them that are entering to go in.
- They devour widows' houses, and for a pretence make long prayer, and shall receive the greater damnation.
- There is a lesson in what they would not do. Where leadership provokes a man to works and he finds himself falling behind, he should take it to heart, because telling people who they are without putting in the labour yields no fruit.

**[Matthew 23:15-22](/bible/matthew/23#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/23#v15)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and when he is made, ye make him twofold more the child of hell than yourselves.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/23#v16)</sup> Woe unto you, ye blind guides, which say, Whosoever shall swear by the temple, it is nothing; but whosoever shall swear by the gold of the temple, he is a debtor!
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/23#v17)</sup> Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gold, or the temple that sanctifieth the gold?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/23#v18)</sup> And, Whosoever shall swear by the altar, it is nothing; but whosoever sweareth by the gift that is upon it, he is guilty.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/23#v19)</sup> Ye fools and blind: for whether is greater, the gift, or the altar that sanctifieth the gift?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/23#v20)</sup> Whoso therefore shall swear by the altar, sweareth by it, and by all things thereon.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/23#v21)</sup> And whoso shall swear by the temple, sweareth by it, and by him that dwelleth therein.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/23#v22)</sup> And he that shall swear by heaven, sweareth by the throne of God, and by him that sitteth thereon.

- They compass sea and land to make one proselyte, and make him twofold more the child of hell than themselves.
- Their rules about swearing by the gold rather than the temple, and by the gift rather than the altar, are called out as blindness: the temple sanctifies the gold and the altar sanctifies the gift.

**[Matthew 23:23-24](/bible/matthew/23#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/23#v23)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye pay tithe of mint and anise and cummin, and have omitted the weightier matters of the law, judgment, mercy, and faith: these ought ye to have done, and not to leave the other undone.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/23#v24)</sup> Ye blind guides, which strain at a gnat, and swallow a camel.

>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/23#v27)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! for ye are like unto whited sepulchres, which indeed appear beautiful outward, but are within full of dead men’s bones, and of all uncleanness.

- They are likened unto whited sepulchres, beautiful outward but within full of dead men's bones. Outwardly they appear righteous unto men, but within are full of hypocrisy and iniquity.

**[Matthew 23:29-36](/bible/matthew/23#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/23#v29)</sup> Woe unto you, scribes and Pharisees, hypocrites! because ye build the tombs of the prophets, and garnish the sepulchres of the righteous,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/23#v30)</sup> And say, If we had been in the days of our fathers, we would not have been partakers with them in the blood of the prophets.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/23#v31)</sup> Wherefore ye be witnesses unto yourselves, that ye are the children of them which killed the prophets.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/23#v32)</sup> Fill ye up then the measure of your fathers.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/23#v33)</sup> Ye serpents, ye generation of vipers, how can ye escape the damnation of hell?

> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/23#v34)</sup> Wherefore, behold, I send unto you prophets, and wise men, and scribes: and some of them ye shall kill and crucify; and some of them shall ye scourge in your synagogues, and persecute them from city to city:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/23#v35)</sup> That upon you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel unto the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/23#v36)</sup> Verily I say unto you, All these things shall come upon this generation.

- They build the tombs of the prophets and say they would not have shed their blood, and by saying it they witness against themselves that they are the children of them which killed the prophets.
- Upon them comes all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel unto the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias.
- This is the plainest speaking in the gospel. Christ was no soft man with these teachers of the law who did not believe it.

- Behold, your house is left unto you desolate.

# Matthew 24: The Signs of the End and the Watchful Servant

Read the chapter → [Matthew 24](/bible/matthew/24)

**[Matthew 24:1-3](/bible/matthew/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/24#v1)</sup> And Jesus went out, and departed from the temple: and his disciples came to him for to shew him the buildings of the temple.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/24#v2)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, See ye not all these things? verily I say unto you, There shall not be left here one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/24#v3)</sup> And as he sat upon the mount of Olives, the disciples came unto him privately, saying, Tell us, when shall these things be? and what shall be the sign of thy coming, and of the end of the world?

- Of the buildings of the temple he says there shall not be left here one stone upon another that shall not be thrown down, and on the mount of Olives the disciples ask when, and what shall be the sign.

**[Matthew 24:4-8](/bible/matthew/24#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/24#v4)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Take heed that no man deceive you.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/24#v5)</sup> For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/24#v6)</sup> And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/24#v7)</sup> For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/24#v8)</sup> All these are the beginning of sorrows.

- Take heed that no man deceive you. Many shall come in my name saying I am Christ, and shall deceive many.
- Wars and rumours of wars, nation against nation, famines, pestilences and earthquakes are all the beginning of sorrows, not the end.

**[Matthew 24:9-13](/bible/matthew/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/24#v9)</sup> Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted, and shall kill you: and ye shall be hated of all nations for my name’s sake.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)</sup> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/24#v11)</sup> And many false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/24#v12)</sup> And because iniquity shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/24#v13)</sup> But he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved.

- Ye shall be hated of all nations for my name's sake, many shall be offended and betray one another, and because iniquity shall abound the love of many shall wax cold.
- He that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved. That verse alone answers any man who says he is already saved and has nothing left to endure.

**[Matthew 24:14](/bible/matthew/24#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/24#v14)</sup> And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come.

- This gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations, and then shall the end come.

**[Matthew 24:15](/bible/matthew/24#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/24#v15)</sup> When ye therefore shall see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, stand in the holy place, (whoso readeth, let him understand:)

- The abomination of desolation spoken of by Daniel the prophet standing in the holy place. That has a fulfilment already in history, when swine's flesh was offered in the temple.

**[Matthew 24:16-22](/bible/matthew/24#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/24#v16)</sup> Then let them which be in Judea flee into the mountains:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/24#v17)</sup> Let him which is on the housetop not come down to take any thing out of his house:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/24#v18)</sup> Neither let him which is in the field return back to take his clothes.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/24#v19)</sup> And woe unto them that are with child, and to them that give suck in those days!
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/24#v20)</sup> But pray ye that your flight be not in the winter, neither on the sabbath day:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/24#v21)</sup> For then shall be great tribulation, such as was not since the beginning of the world to this time, no, nor ever shall be.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/24#v22)</sup> And except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved: but for the elect’s sake those days shall be shortened.

- Then let them which be in Judaea flee into the mountains, and pray that the flight be not in the winter, neither on the sabbath day.
- Then shall be great tribulation such as was not since the beginning of the world, and except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved; but for the elect's sake those days shall be shortened.

# Matthew 25: The Ten Virgins, the Talents, and the Sheep and the Goats

Read the chapter → [Matthew 25](/bible/matthew/25)

**[Matthew 25:1-13](/bible/matthew/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/25#v1)</sup> Then shall the kingdom of heaven be likened unto ten virgins, which took their lamps, and went forth to meet the bridegroom.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/25#v2)</sup> And five of them were wise, and five were foolish.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/25#v3)</sup> They that were foolish took their lamps, and took no oil with them:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/25#v4)</sup> But the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/25#v5)</sup> While the bridegroom tarried, they all slumbered and slept.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/25#v6)</sup> And at midnight there was a cry made, Behold, the bridegroom cometh; go ye out to meet him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/25#v7)</sup> Then all those virgins arose, and trimmed their lamps.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/25#v8)</sup> And the foolish said unto the wise, Give us of your oil; for our lamps are gone out.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/25#v9)</sup> But the wise answered, saying, Not so; lest there be not enough for us and you: but go ye rather to them that sell, and buy for yourselves.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/25#v10)</sup> And while they went to buy, the bridegroom came; and they that were ready went in with him to the marriage: and the door was shut.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/25#v11)</sup> Afterward came also the other virgins, saying, Lord, Lord, open to us.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/25#v12)</sup> But he answered and said, Verily I say unto you, I know you not.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/25#v13)</sup> Watch therefore, for ye know neither the day nor the hour wherein the Son of man cometh.

- Five were wise and five were foolish. The foolish took their lamps and took no oil with them; the wise took oil in their vessels with their lamps. The oil is the wisdom of the scriptures.
- The foolish come afterward saying, Lord, Lord, open to us, and are answered, Verily I say unto you, I know you not.
- Calling on his name while breaking the commandments is exactly this. Men bless what the law calls an abomination and still say Lord, Lord.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 7:21](/bible/matthew/7#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/7#v21)</sup> Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.

    Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father.
  - **[Psalms 40:8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)</sup> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.

    I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart. That is the will of the Father, and the foolish did not have it.

**[Matthew 25:14-30](/bible/matthew/25#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/25#v14)</sup> For the kingdom of heaven is as a man travelling into a far country, who called his own servants, and delivered unto them his goods.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/25#v15)</sup> And unto one he gave five talents, to another two, and to another one; to every man according to his several ability; and straightway took his journey.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/25#v16)</sup> Then he that had received the five talents went and traded with the same, and made them other five talents.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/25#v17)</sup> And likewise he that had received two, he also gained other two.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/25#v18)</sup> But he that had received one went and digged in the earth, and hid his lord’s money.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/25#v19)</sup> After a long time the lord of those servants cometh, and reckoneth with them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/25#v20)</sup> And so he that had received five talents came and brought other five talents, saying, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/25#v21)</sup> His lord said unto him, Well done, thou good and faithful servant: thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/25#v22)</sup> He also that had received two talents came and said, Lord, thou deliveredst unto me two talents: behold, I have gained two other talents beside them.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/25#v23)</sup> His lord said unto him, Well done, good and faithful servant; thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy lord.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/25#v24)</sup> Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/25#v25)</sup> And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth: lo, there thou hast that is thine.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/25#v26)</sup> His lord answered and said unto him, Thou wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I have not strawed:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/25#v27)</sup> Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming I should have received mine own with usury.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/25#v28)</sup> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/25#v29)</sup> For unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance: but from him that hath not shall be taken away even that which he hath.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/25#v30)</sup> And cast ye the unprofitable servant into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

- Every man received according to his several ability, and the two who traded were told, Well done, thou good and faithful servant.
- The one who hid his talent blamed his lord and called him a hard man, and was answered, Thou wicked and slothful servant. That fearful spirit is not of the Most High, and a man should examine himself for it.
- Unto every one that hath shall be given, and he shall have abundance; but from him that hath not shall be taken away even that which he hath. Knowledge that is not used is knowledge that is lost.

**[Matthew 25:31-46](/bible/matthew/25#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/25#v31)</sup> When the Son of man shall come in his glory, and all the holy angels with him, then shall he sit upon the throne of his glory:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/25#v32)</sup> And before him shall be gathered all nations: and he shall separate them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/25#v33)</sup> And he shall set the sheep on his right hand, but the goats on the left.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/25#v34)</sup> Then shall the King say unto them on his right hand, Come, ye blessed of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/25#v35)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/25#v36)</sup> Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/25#v37)</sup> Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/25#v38)</sup> When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/25#v39)</sup> Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/25#v40)</sup> And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/25#v41)</sup> Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/25#v42)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink:
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/25#v43)</sup> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/25#v44)</sup> Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/25#v45)</sup> Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/25#v46)</sup> And these shall go away into everlasting punishment: but the righteous into life eternal.

- He separates them one from another, as a shepherd divideth his sheep from the goats, and says to those on the right hand, Inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.
- Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me. Meeting the need of a brother or sister in the body is counted as done to Christ himself.
- Those on the left hand are answered with the same measure: inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.
