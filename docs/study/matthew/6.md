Saying no is easy while you sit here; when the temptation comes, that is the real test. Those who say God will understand, I am in captivity, were tested and failed. Daniel in captivity asked for the Sabbath off and would not eat unclean food, and our forefathers in the time of the Maccabees would rather die than break the Sabbath. The Sabbath is not only resting your body: it represents righteousness, paradise, where we were in the beginning, the kingdom of God when we are at rest from these nations ruling over us. Don’t ask the leaders if it is okay; the answer is no. And the one who says he will take the Sabbath job and give half to the truth ends up working all the damn time and nobody sees him anymore. That is how Satan sets you up.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 41:1](/bible/sirach/41#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/41#v1)</sup> O death, how bitter is the remembrance of thee to a man that liveth at rest in his possessions, unto the man that hath nothing to vex him, and that hath prosperity in all things: yea, unto him that is yet able to receive meat!

    “O death, how bitter is the remembrance of thee to a man that liveth at rest in his possessions”. That man is living so good that the moment he thinks of death he says, I have to leave all this behind?

**[Matthew 6:25](/bible/matthew/6#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/6#v25)</sup> Therefore I say unto you, Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on. Is not the life more than meat, and the body than raiment?

- “Take no thought for your life”: this is the state of mind the followers of Christ must have. Many think they can direct their life: by this age I will accomplish this, have my house, have five kids. Stop taking thought for your life, what you will do tomorrow.
- “Is not the life more than meat, and the body than raiment?” People died last night, people are sick and dying of diseases, and you are here worrying about a damn light bill, a car note, a mortgage, the rent, what you will eat next week. Often only when something bad happens, when sickness comes, do you realize your health and your body are worth more than all of that.
- Stop stressing over Esau’s jobs. Esau doesn’t give a damn about any of you. Some of you stress and get depressed over a company that does not belong to you, and it is going to bring death on you. You are just a number; the minute you drop dead you are replaced before you are in the grave. Go to work, do the best you can, and go home, because when you drop dead the only ones who lose are your wife and your children.
- “Take no thought for your life, what ye shall eat, or what ye shall drink; nor yet for your body, what ye shall put on.” Christ says don’t worry about what you are going to eat, what you are going to drink, or what you are going to wear.

**[Matthew 6:26](/bible/matthew/6#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/6#v26)</sup> Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?

- “Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?” The Lord knows what you need and he will help you: he will allow opportunities to come along. But you have to believe that.
- “Behold the fowls of the air: for they sow not, neither do they reap, nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feedeth them. Are ye not much better than they?” When was the last time you saw a bird plant anything? The bird doesn’t plant and doesn’t work, yet God finds a way to feed it every day. You are more important to God than the bird. The bird doesn’t worry about how it is going to eat tomorrow; why do you?

**[Matthew 6:27](/bible/matthew/6#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/6#v27)</sup> Which of you by taking thought can add one cubit unto his stature?

- “Which of you by taking thought can add one cubit unto his stature?” By stressing and worrying, can you change anything? Can you change tomorrow? Then why are you worried about tomorrow?

**[Matthew 6:28-30](/bible/matthew/6#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/6#v28)</sup> And why take ye thought for raiment? Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they toil not, neither do they spin:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/6#v29)</sup> And yet I say unto you, That even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/6#v30)</sup> Wherefore, if God so clothe the grass of the field, which to day is, and to morrow is cast into the oven, shall he not much more clothe you, O ye of little faith?

- “And why take ye thought for raiment?” To take thought is to worry. Why worry about clothes? The lilies of the field toil not, and “even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these.” If God clothes the grass of the field, “shall he not much more clothe you, O ye of little faith?”
- Little faith is the key. The one who keeps worrying about bills, the sister stressing her husband out, worries because of little faith. You serve the creator of the heaven and the earth and you worry about minor things. Have faith that the Lord has you: he blesses in different ways, through the people around you, through jobs he sends, even a job from a white man.
- “Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow.” The lily is a flower; it doesn’t go to work, yet it lives. “Shall he not much more clothe you, O ye of little faith?”
- You are more important to God than the grass outside.

**[Matthew 6:31-32](/bible/matthew/6#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/6#v31)</sup> Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/6#v32)</sup> (For after all these things do the Gentiles seek:) for your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things.

- “Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat? or, What shall we drink? or, Wherewithal shall we be clothed?” “For after all these things do the Gentiles seek”. White people worry about these things, and because we have been enslaved among them they taught us to worry about them: go to college, finish this, by forty start having children. The Gentiles, the other nations, care about these things; we shouldn’t. “Your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things.”
- “For after all these things do the Gentiles seek.” The other nations have to worry about these things, not you. Some of us are stressed because we have very small faith, or no faith at all. Build your faith up; ask God to increase your faith. “For your heavenly Father knoweth that ye have need of all these things.”
- “Therefore take no thought, saying, What shall we eat?” Don’t take thought about the physical food of the earth; you have other food you need to be eating and digesting and getting in your belly right now.

  - **[1 John 2:15-16](/bible/1-john/2#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/2#v15)</sup> Love not the world, neither the things that are in the world. If any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/1-john/2#v16)</sup> For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

    The Gentiles worry about these things, but we are not to. “Love not the world, neither the things that are in the world.” The things of this world must not be put above God. “If any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him”: if you love this society, this kingdom, God’s spirit is not in you.

**[Matthew 6:33](/bible/matthew/6#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/6#v33)</sup> But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.

- “But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness”, and his righteousness is keeping the commandments. Many end up in bad situations because they do not seek God first: four, five, six jobs, no prayer, not coming around to learn. Some are going through hard times right now because they don’t put God first, and the Lord makes it hard until they learn to seek him first.
- You must seek first the kingdom of God, to hell with your damn job. But that does not mean quit your job on Monday and then ask the deacon to pay your rent. It is a balanced state. Put God first, and “all these things shall be added unto you”: food, clothing, shelter, what you need to take care of yourself and your family in this wicked world. These things are not bad; the Lord gives them when you seek him first.
- “But seek ye first the kingdom of God.” Some are so stressed they can’t even open the Bible to read, so stressed they can’t fall asleep. Seek first the kingdom of God “and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.” If you do what the Bible says, you should not worry about anything. As long as you stay in the Bible and keep the laws, don’t worry about everything else.
- “But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.” With all the temptations and distractions of this world, Christ told us the main thing to focus on to survive and make it through, so they don’t draw us out to the wide road that leads to destruction.

    Here is more proof of it. “But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone.” Look at what Christ is telling you about that physical bread that we as a people lust after so. How many people have you met who say ‘oh, I love food, I love to eat out’? Some in the truth take it that way too.

**[Matthew 6:34](/bible/matthew/6#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/6#v34)</sup> Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.

- “Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself.” Stop worrying about tomorrow, the bill that will be disconnected. When something is out of your hands and there is nothing you can do, stop worrying and ask the Lord for help.
- “Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.” Every day something bad happens; that is life. The evil of each day is enough for itself.
- “Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself.” Let tomorrow worry about itself. The problem is that some of us are never satisfied and never have enough. How many here have no food at home, no water, no clothes on their back, no house to cover them? None.
