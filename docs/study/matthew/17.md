## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 17 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 10-13, 15, 17, 20-21, 24-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 17:10](/bible/matthew/17#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/17#v10)</sup> And his disciples asked him, saying, Why then say the scribes that Elias must first come?

- This shows how the spirit of life from God entered into us. “Why then say the scribes that Elias must first come?”

**[Matthew 17:11](/bible/matthew/17#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/17#v11)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Elias truly shall first come, and restore all things.

- “Elias truly shall first come, and restore all things.” Restore all things goes back to the remembrance of us ceasing from among men: we would forget who we are, scattered throughout the four corners of the earth. But the Bible says Elijah is going to come and restore all things, meaning Elijah is going to come and teach us who we are.
- Read it again: Elias truly “shall” first come. Shall means in the future. This is talking about the last days, the 60s. That goes back to there must be a falling away first: Israel had to be scattered, Israel had to forget their nationality, the power of the holy people had to be taken. Then Elijah comes and teaches us who we are.
- Bishop taught us this years ago, and we’re not 100% but it’s up there: when the Bible talks about Elijah shall come and restore things, it might be, because Christ didn’t tell us, but the prophecy fits this man: it might have been Abba Bivens. And even though it was him, he’s back again on the earth. You think he just came and restored and that’s it? No, he’s going to keep coming; he ain’t going to stop coming until Christ returns.
- Abba Bivens was the leader who started the school in 1969, and under him were seven elders. These are the elders Bishop Nathanyel learned from. Out of the seven elders, a lot of them went back into the world, but we don’t speak evil of these men, because back then they did a lot for the truth. Abba was the first street teacher, and he was killed by a bunch of Muslims while teaching in the street: they jumped him and beat him bad, and he died from his injuries. Elijah came through him, or one that came through his teachings. The spirit of life entering into us came through that line, and the Bishop came from those men and is continuing the mission he was in since his 20s, and has passed what he learned down to us.

**[Matthew 17:12](/bible/matthew/17#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/17#v12)</sup> But I say unto you, That Elias is come already, and they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed. Likewise shall also the Son of man suffer of them.

- “But I say unto you, That Elias is come already”: he came already, as John the Baptist at that time, and he prepared the way for Christ. “And they knew him not, but have done unto him whatsoever they listed”: they did whatever they wanted to do to him. They killed him; they cut his head off. “Likewise shall also the Son of man suffer of them.”

**[Matthew 17:13](/bible/matthew/17#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/17#v13)</sup> Then the disciples understood that he spake unto them of John the Baptist.

- “Then the disciples understood that he spake unto them of John the Baptist.” So John the Baptist was Elijah, but Christ also said Elias truly shall first come: in the future.

**[Matthew 17:15](/bible/matthew/17#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/17#v15)</sup> Lord, have mercy on my son: for he is lunatick, and sore vexed: for ofttimes he falleth into the fire, and oft into the water.

- Some brothers say, I cut off the phone and the laptop, I got married, and I’m still burning; I’m reading Ezekiel and an evil thought pops up out of nowhere. That happens to many of us. “For he is lunatick”: some of us have become chronic pornographic lunatics, lunatics over adultery and fornication, driving 300 miles for foot fetishes. We have run out of our wits; we are sick people, and the only thing that’s going to heal us is this Bible. “For ofttimes he falleth into the fire, and oft into the water”: we take this verbatim, a child with schizophrenia dropping into fire and water, but look at the allegory. Sin has made us lunatics; that water and fire is going into trials and sin, and he kept on falling right back into it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Job 11:5-6](/bible/job/11#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/job/11#v5)</sup> But oh that God would speak, and open his lips against thee;
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/job/11#v6)</sup> And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.

    The scripture says it has double meaning. “And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is!” The fire and the water in Matthew 17 are also going into sin.

**[Matthew 17:17](/bible/matthew/17#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/17#v17)</sup> Then Jesus answered and said, O faithless and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? how long shall I suffer you? bring him hither to me.

- “O faithless and perverse generation”: that’s us, that’s the Negroes. Look up the definition of Negroes and you’ll see faithless and perverse. We’ve been polluted with the pollutions of Babylon, filthy communication, unfruitful works of darkness.

**[Matthew 17:20](/bible/matthew/17#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/17#v20)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you.

- We say, I can’t get over this adultery, this fornication, this porn; we get those messages all the time. “If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed, ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove”: if you had enough faith you could move a damn mountain, and the same with this sin. But faith without action is dead faith. Putting those blocks on your phone and computer and having a brother over you to hold you accountable is action that shows you believe.
- How do we get out of this bashful spirit, where we can’t unite, tiptoe around things, have no sense of urgency, unsure and unconfident? “Because of your unbelief: for verily I say unto you, If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed.” If you have faith, brothers, you will also have confidence. You will be able to speak strong; you won’t be bashful if you’re confident. “Ye shall say unto this mountain, Remove hence to yonder place; and it shall remove; and nothing shall be impossible unto you.” That is the level we all must get to: nothing is impossible for us.

**[Matthew 17:21](/bible/matthew/17#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/17#v21)</sup> Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.

- “Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.” We pray three times a day, but how many times are you fasting? Are you doing your four chapters every day? When you’re fasting it’s easy not to think of those demons, but the minute you come off your fast the devil’s right back at your front door. It’s an everlasting race; study, pray, fast and apply until the race is over, and it don’t stop until you die. When the devil departed from Christ he left for a season; it’s going to come again.
- “Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.” If you have the bashful spirit, lacking confidence and self-esteem, pray and fast about it. And if you see your leader lacking in confidence and bashful, pray and fast for him. Pray for your leadership wherever they are lacking, because we are all lacking in something and all trying to get it right. The leadership didn’t grow up in the truth either; we were all dealing with some of the same things. Whatever you want to improve, you have to be willing to be uncomfortable to attain it, and fight that spirit to be men of God.
- Repentance, meditation in God’s word, faith in God’s word, and fasting, cleaning your body up. “Howbeit this kind goeth not out but by prayer and fasting.” That is the only way to get rid of certain demons. You have to believe his word, meditate in it, change your mind according to it, and then fast.

  Precepts:
  - **[Tobit 12:8](/bible/tobit/12#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/tobit/12#v8)</sup> Prayer is good with fasting and alms and righteousness. A little with righteousness is better than much with unrighteousness. It is better to give alms than to lay up gold:

    “Prayer is good with fasting and alms and righteousness.” Notice it says righteousness: that’s the action behind the prayer and fasting. Just because you pray once or fast once the temptation is not going to jump off you; it has to be a continuation, multiple actions of belief that show you’re applying what the word says. “A little with righteousness is better than much with unrighteousness.” You brothers and sisters battling that spirit, do not sleep on it; it’s a heavy spirit. The Bible gives you ways to overcome, but you got to believe and you have to apply.

**[Matthew 17:24](/bible/matthew/17#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/17#v24)</sup> And when they were come to Capernaum, they that received tribute money came to Peter, and said, Doth not your master pay tribute?

- Watch what Christ said about that. “They that received tribute money”: tribute money is taxes. “Doth not your master pay tribute?” Does your master not pay taxes?

**[Matthew 17:25](/bible/matthew/17#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/17#v25)</sup> He saith, Yes. And when he was come into the house, Jesus prevented him, saying, What thinkest thou, Simon? of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers?

- “Of whom do the kings of the earth take custom or tribute? of their own children, or of strangers?” Who does Rome collect taxes from: of their own people, or of strange nations, meaning other nations?

**[Matthew 17:26](/bible/matthew/17#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/17#v26)</sup> Peter saith unto him, Of strangers. Jesus saith unto him, Then are the children free.

- “Of strangers”: other nations. Rome was collecting taxes from other nations. “Then are the children free.” When you are collecting taxes, that’s when you’re free. As long as you’re paying another nation taxes you’re not free; you’re subject to their rules, their guidelines, their policies.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 60:14-17](/bible/isaiah/60#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/60#v14)</sup> The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee; and all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet; and they shall call thee, The city of the Lord, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/60#v15)</sup> Whereas thou hast been forsaken and hated, so that no man went through thee, I will make thee an eternal excellency, a joy of many generations.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/60#v16)</sup> Thou shalt also suck the milk of the Gentiles, and shalt suck the breast of kings: and thou shalt know that I the Lord am thy Saviour and thy Redeemer, the mighty One of Jacob.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/60#v17)</sup> For brass I will bring gold, and for iron I will bring silver, and for wood brass, and for stones iron: I will also make thy officers peace, and thine exactors righteousness.

    This is what “Then are the children free” means. “The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee; and all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet”: everybody that hates us is going to bow down at the soles of our feet. “And they shall call thee, The city of the Lord, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel”: that’s what we’re going to be called.
