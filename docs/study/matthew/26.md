## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 26 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2, 14-21, 26-28, 41, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 26:2](/bible/matthew/26#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/26#v2)</sup> Ye know that after two days is the feast of the passover, and the Son of man is betrayed to be crucified.

- “Ye know that after two days is the feast of the passover, and the Son of man is betrayed to be crucified.” Christ lets it be known that the Passover is coming up, but he also lets you know something else: Christ died on the high holy day of the Passover.

**[Matthew 26:16](/bible/matthew/26#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/26#v16)</sup> And from that time he sought opportunity to betray him.

- “And from that time he sought opportunity to betray him.” Judas betrayed the Lord Jesus Christ. He chased money and destroyed himself; money was his demise.

**[Matthew 26:17](/bible/matthew/26#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/26#v17)</sup> Now the first day of the feast of unleavened bread the disciples came to Jesus, saying unto him, Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eat the passover?

- Some will ask why we eat lamb and keep the Passover today. “The first day of the feast of unleavened bread the disciples came to Jesus, saying unto him, Where wilt thou that we prepare for thee to eat the passover?” The disciples came to Christ to prepare the Passover, meaning they ate lamb.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 1:29](/bible/john/1#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/john/1#v29)</sup> The next day John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.

    John sees Jesus coming and says, “Behold the Lamb of God.” Christ is called the Lamb of God. That male lamb of the first year points to him: he is that sacrifice.

**[Matthew 26:18-19](/bible/matthew/26#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/26#v18)</sup> And he said, Go into the city to such a man, and say unto him, The Master saith, My time is at hand; I will keep the passover at thy house with my disciples.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/26#v19)</sup> And the disciples did as Jesus had appointed them; and they made ready the passover.

- “I will keep the passover at thy house with my disciples.” Christ kept the Passover. “And they made ready the passover”: the lamb and the unleavened bread.

**[Matthew 26:20-21](/bible/matthew/26#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/26#v20)</sup> Now when the even was come, he sat down with the twelve.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/26#v21)</sup> And as they did eat, he said, Verily I say unto you, that one of you shall betray me.

- “Now when the even was come, he sat down with the twelve.” They made ready on the fourteenth; when the evening came, that is the fifteenth. “And as they did eat”, they ate the Passover dinner.

**[Matthew 26:26](/bible/matthew/26#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/26#v26)</sup> And as they were eating, Jesus took bread, and blessed it, and brake it, and gave it to the disciples, and said, Take, eat; this is my body.

- “Jesus took bread, and blessed it, and brake it, and gave it to the disciples, and said, Take, eat; this is my body.” The bread represents Christ's body.

**[Matthew 26:27-28](/bible/matthew/26#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/26#v27)</sup> And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and gave it to them, saying, Drink ye all of it;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/26#v28)</sup> For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins.

- “For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins.” The blood that we took and put on top of the doorpost in ancient Egypt represents Christ's blood: Christ coming and dying for our sins, and the new covenant.
- So don't take blood and put it on the doorpost. Christ dying and shedding his blood for us is what that blood represented.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 5:7](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v7)**
