**[Matthew 13:1-9](/bible/matthew/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/13#v1)</sup> The same day went Jesus out of the house, and sat by the sea side.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/13#v2)</sup> And great multitudes were gathered together unto him, so that he went into a ship, and sat; and the whole multitude stood on the shore.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/13#v3)</sup> And he spake many things unto them in parables, saying, Behold, a sower went forth to sow;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/13#v4)</sup> And when he sowed, some seeds fell by the way side, and the fowls came and devoured them up:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/13#v5)</sup> Some fell upon stony places, where they had not much earth: and forthwith they sprung up, because they had no deepness of earth:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/13#v6)</sup> And when the sun was up, they were scorched; and because they had no root, they withered away.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/13#v7)</sup> And some fell among thorns; and the thorns sprung up, and choked them:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/13#v8)</sup> But other fell into good ground, and brought forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/13#v9)</sup> Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.

- A sower goes forth to sow. Seed falls by the way side, on stony places, among thorns, and into good ground, and only the good ground brings forth fruit, some an hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.

**[Matthew 13:10-13](/bible/matthew/13#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/13#v10)</sup> And the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/13#v11)</sup> He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/13#v12)</sup> For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/13#v13)</sup> Therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.

- It is given unto the disciples to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but not unto them.
- Whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance; but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 3:8](/bible/john/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/john/3#v8)</sup> The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.

    The wind bloweth where it listeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit. The word rests on those it is given to.

**[Matthew 13:14-15](/bible/matthew/13#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/13#v14)</sup> And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias, which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/13#v15)</sup> For this people’s heart is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes they have closed; lest at any time they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and should understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.

- In them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias: by hearing ye shall hear and shall not understand, and seeing ye shall see and shall not perceive.
- They saw the works and did not perceive. The heart was made fat and the ears heavy so that they would not be converted, because judgment had to come.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 6:9-10](/bible/isaiah/6#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/6#v9)</sup> And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/6#v10)</sup> Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes; lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed.

    Hear ye indeed, but understand not; make the heart of this people fat, lest they convert and be healed.

**[Matthew 13:16-17](/bible/matthew/13#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/13#v16)</sup> But blessed are your eyes, for they see: and your ears, for they hear.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/13#v17)</sup> For verily I say unto you, That many prophets and righteous men have desired to see those things which ye see, and have not seen them; and to hear those things which ye hear, and have not heard them.

- Blessed are your eyes, for they see, and your ears, for they hear. Many prophets and righteous men desired to see these things and did not see them.

**[Matthew 13:18-23](/bible/matthew/13#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/13#v18)</sup> Hear ye therefore the parable of the sower.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/13#v19)</sup> When any one heareth the word of the kingdom, and understandeth it not, then cometh the wicked one, and catcheth away that which was sown in his heart. This is he which received seed by the way side.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/13#v20)</sup> But he that received the seed into stony places, the same is he that heareth the word, and anon with joy receiveth it;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/13#v21)</sup> Yet hath he not root in himself, but dureth for a while: for when tribulation or persecution ariseth because of the word, by and by he is offended.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/13#v22)</sup> He also that received seed among the thorns is he that heareth the word; and the care of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, choke the word, and he becometh unfruitful.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/13#v23)</sup> But he that received seed into the good ground is he that heareth the word, and understandeth it; which also beareth fruit, and bringeth forth, some an hundredfold, some sixty, some thirty.

- The seed by the way side is the one who hears and understands not, and the wicked one catches away what was sown in his heart.
- The stony ground is the one who receives it with joy and has no root, and is offended when tribulation or persecution arises. That can come through family or through a job.
- The thorns are the care of this world and the deceitfulness of riches, which choke the word.
- The good ground hears and understands, and brings others in behind him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 4:15](/bible/mark/4#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/mark/4#v15)</sup> And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown; but when they have heard, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.

    Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.

**[Matthew 13:24-30](/bible/matthew/13#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/13#v24)</sup> Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is likened unto a man which sowed good seed in his field:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/13#v25)</sup> But while men slept, his enemy came and sowed tares among the wheat, and went his way.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/13#v26)</sup> But when the blade was sprung up, and brought forth fruit, then appeared the tares also.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/13#v27)</sup> So the servants of the householder came and said unto him, Sir, didst not thou sow good seed in thy field? from whence then hath it tares?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/13#v28)</sup> He said unto them, An enemy hath done this. The servants said unto him, Wilt thou then that we go and gather them up?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/13#v29)</sup> But he said, Nay; lest while ye gather up the tares, ye root up also the wheat with them.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/13#v30)</sup> Let both grow together until the harvest: and in the time of harvest I will say to the reapers, Gather ye together first the tares, and bind them in bundles to burn them: but gather the wheat into my barn.

- An enemy sows tares among the wheat while men slept, and the householder will not have them gathered up before the harvest lest the wheat be rooted up with them.
- The separating is not ours to do. Both grow together until the harvest.

**[Matthew 13:31-32](/bible/matthew/13#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/13#v31)</sup> Another parable put he forth unto them, saying, The kingdom of heaven is like to a grain of mustard seed, which a man took, and sowed in his field:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/13#v32)</sup> Which indeed is the least of all seeds: but when it is grown, it is the greatest among herbs, and becometh a tree, so that the birds of the air come and lodge in the branches thereof.

- The kingdom of heaven is like a grain of mustard seed, the least of all seeds, which becomes the greatest among herbs. This work starts small and ends covering the earth.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:2](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v2)</sup> My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew, as the small rain upon the tender herb, and as the showers upon the grass:

    My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew: the doctrine goes out over the whole earth, because Israel is scattered over the whole earth.

**[Matthew 13:33-35](/bible/matthew/13#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/13#v33)</sup> Another parable spake he unto them; The kingdom of heaven is like unto leaven, which a woman took, and hid in three measures of meal, till the whole was leavened.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/13#v34)</sup> All these things spake Jesus unto the multitude in parables; and without a parable spake he not unto them:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/13#v35)</sup> That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying, I will open my mouth in parables; I will utter things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world.

- The leaven hid in three measures of meal leavens the whole, and all these things were spoken in parables, that it might be fulfilled, I will utter things which have been kept secret from the foundation of the world.

**[Matthew 13:36-43](/bible/matthew/13#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/13#v36)</sup> Then Jesus sent the multitude away, and went into the house: and his disciples came unto him, saying, Declare unto us the parable of the tares of the field.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/13#v37)</sup> He answered and said unto them, He that soweth the good seed is the Son of man;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/13#v38)</sup> The field is the world; the good seed are the children of the kingdom; but the tares are the children of the wicked one;
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/13#v39)</sup> The enemy that sowed them is the devil; the harvest is the end of the world; and the reapers are the angels.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/13#v40)</sup> As therefore the tares are gathered and burned in the fire; so shall it be in the end of this world.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/13#v41)</sup> The Son of man shall send forth his angels, and they shall gather out of his kingdom all things that offend, and them which do iniquity;
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/13#v42)</sup> And shall cast them into a furnace of fire: there shall be wailing and gnashing of teeth.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/13#v43)</sup> Then shall the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father. Who hath ears to hear, let him hear.

- Christ gives the meaning himself: the sower is the Son of man, the field is the world, the good seed are the children of the kingdom, the tares are the children of the wicked one, the enemy is the devil, the harvest is the end of the world, and the reapers are the angels.
- The tares are gathered and burned, and the righteous shine forth as the sun in the kingdom of their Father.

**[Matthew 13:44](/bible/matthew/13#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/13#v44)</sup> Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto treasure hid in a field; the which when a man hath found, he hideth, and for joy thereof goeth and selleth all that he hath, and buyeth that field.

- The kingdom of heaven is like treasure hid in a field, which a man finds and for joy thereof sells all that he hath and buys that field. Finding out who you are is that joy.

**[Matthew 13:45-46](/bible/matthew/13#v45)**

> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/13#v45)</sup> Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a merchant man, seeking goodly pearls:
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/13#v46)</sup> Who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it.

- The merchant man, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had and bought it.

**[Matthew 13:47-50](/bible/matthew/13#v47)**

> <sup>[47](/bible/matthew/13#v47)</sup> Again, the kingdom of heaven is like unto a net, that was cast into the sea, and gathered of every kind:
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/matthew/13#v48)</sup> Which, when it was full, they drew to shore, and sat down, and gathered the good into vessels, but cast the bad away.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/matthew/13#v49)</sup> So shall it be at the end of the world: the angels shall come forth, and sever the wicked from among the just,
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/matthew/13#v50)</sup> And shall cast them into the furnace of fire: there shall be wailing and gnashing of teeth.

- The net gathers of every kind, and the good are put into vessels and the bad cast away. The angels sever the wicked from among the just.
- This is not only about the other nations. Israel must keep these laws, and there will be those in these last days who are not keeping them.

**[Matthew 13:51-52](/bible/matthew/13#v51)**

> <sup>[51](/bible/matthew/13#v51)</sup> Jesus saith unto them, Have ye understood all these things? They say unto him, Yea, Lord.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/matthew/13#v52)</sup> Then said he unto them, Therefore every scribe which is instructed unto the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old.

- Every scribe instructed unto the kingdom of heaven is like a householder which bringeth forth out of his treasure things new and old.

**[Matthew 13:53-58](/bible/matthew/13#v53)**

> <sup>[53](/bible/matthew/13#v53)</sup> And it came to pass, that when Jesus had finished these parables, he departed thence.
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/matthew/13#v54)</sup> And when he was come into his own country, he taught them in their synagogue, insomuch that they were astonished, and said, Whence hath this man this wisdom, and these mighty works?
>
> <sup>[55](/bible/matthew/13#v55)</sup> Is not this the carpenter’s son? is not his mother called Mary? and his brethren, James, and Joses, and Simon, and Judas?
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/matthew/13#v56)</sup> And his sisters, are they not all with us? Whence then hath this man all these things?
>
> <sup>[57](/bible/matthew/13#v57)</sup> And they were offended in him. But Jesus said unto them, A prophet is not without honour, save in his own country, and in his own house.
>
> <sup>[58](/bible/matthew/13#v58)</sup> And he did not many mighty works there because of their unbelief.

- In his own country they are astonished and ask, Is not this the carpenter's son? They name his mother, his brethren James, Joses, Simon and Judas, and his sisters, all of them living among them.
- A prophet is not without honour, save in his own country and in his own house, and he did not many mighty works there because of their unbelief.
