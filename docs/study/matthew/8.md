## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 11-12, 28-29, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 8:11](/bible/matthew/8#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/8#v11)</sup> And I say unto you, That many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, in the kingdom of heaven.

- A new thing Christians are pushing: that other nations will come into the kingdom through the same gate Abraham came through, because Abraham was not an Israelite. Abraham was not one of the 12, but he was the father of the 12.
- So read what Christ said about Abraham: “many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, in the kingdom of heaven.” Find out who is coming from the east and the west. Zechariah answers it: God's people, Zion, the Israelites. That cuts his argument about Abraham. And the very fact that he has to address it shows the teaching is impacting the church.
- “And I say unto you, That many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, in the kingdom of heaven.” Stop right there: from the east and west.
- “Many shall come from the east and west, and shall sit down with Abraham, and Isaac, and Jacob, in the kingdom of heaven.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Zechariah 8:7-8](/bible/zechariah/8#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/8#v7)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country;
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/8#v8)</sup> And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.

    Who is coming from the east and west? “Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Behold, I will save my people from the east country, and from the west country; And I will bring them, and they shall dwell in the midst of Jerusalem: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God, in truth and in righteousness.”
  - **[Zechariah 8:1-2](/bible/zechariah/8#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/8#v1)</sup> Again the word of the Lord of hosts came to me, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/8#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; I was jealous for Zion with great jealousy, and I was jealous for her with great fury.

    Read verse 1 with it: “Thus saith the Lord of hosts; I was jealous for Zion.” For who? For Zion. Zion is not all nations on the planet. The ones coming from the east country and the west country to sit down with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob are Zion.
  - **[Romans 9:7](/bible/romans/9#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/romans/9#v7)</sup> Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children: but, In Isaac shall thy seed be called.

    They always go to Abraham as though that brings in the other nations, and Paul had to clean up that confusion back then: “Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children: but, In Isaac shall thy seed be called.”

**[Matthew 8:12](/bible/matthew/8#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/8#v12)</sup> But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

- “But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness”. The children of the kingdom are the builders, the wicked elders. Outer darkness: closed eyes, the sun going down upon them, as written in Psalms and Isaiah. “There shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth”: they are going to die terrible deaths, and they are back again today.
- “But the children of the kingdom shall be cast out into outer darkness: there shall be weeping and gnashing of teeth.” Abraham, Isaac and Jacob will be back too, but the wicked elders are not going to make it. The children of the kingdom here are the Pharisees, and you pastors, you elders, you Christian cult member leaders: that’s you, you’re the evil elders of the kingdom, you’re going to die here.
- You are the Pharisees and Sadducees, and worse than them. The Pharisees at least taught the law and didn’t keep it; you teach there’s no law at all. Calling you a Pharisee is a compliment, because Christ said of the Pharisees, observe and do what they say, and we can’t even tell anybody to do what you say, because you don’t do nothing.

**[Matthew 8:28](/bible/matthew/8#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/8#v28)</sup> And when he was come to the other side into the country of the Gergesenes, there met him two possessed with devils, coming out of the tombs, exceeding fierce, so that no man might pass by that way.

- “There met him two possessed with devils”. It says devils, plural. This is not the devil; this is devils.

**[Matthew 8:29](/bible/matthew/8#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/8#v29)</sup> And, behold, they cried out, saying, What have we to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of God? art thou come hither to torment us before the time?

- “What have we to do with thee, Jesus, thou Son of God? art thou come hither to torment us before the time?” There’s a time sanctioned when these devils, plural, that are inside of people will be bound.
