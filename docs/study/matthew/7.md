## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-23, 26-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 7:1](/bible/matthew/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/7#v1)</sup> Judge not, that ye be not judged.

- “Judge not, that ye be not judged.” A lot of our people do not understand that. That is why we constantly have to pray for forgiveness: a lot of us want to judge brothers and sisters while we ourselves are not right.

**[Matthew 7:2](/bible/matthew/7#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/7#v2)</sup> For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.

- “For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.” Be very mindful when you judge. If you judge with mercy, the same mercy will reach out to you; if you judge with cruelty, when your judgment comes, there will be cruelty too.

**[Matthew 7:3](/bible/matthew/7#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/7#v3)</sup> And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?

- “And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye”: some brothers and sisters can see someone else's sin clearly. “But considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?” We have to consider the beam in our own eye, the sin we do not acknowledge, which keeps us from judging right. That is why we have to judge with mercy.
- A brother condemns a brother for watching porn, and the night before he was watching a movie with naked things in it, and told himself it was just the movie. The thought goes by in a second and you deceive your mind. How can you judge the brother that watches porn while you love movies that are half porn? Consider yourself.

**[Matthew 7:4](/bible/matthew/7#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/7#v4)</sup> Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye; and, behold, a beam is in thine own eye?

- “Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye; and, behold, a beam is in thine own eye?” In the Maccabees we learned that sport is not for our people, but some brothers watch every season of baseball, football and basketball and know every star, and say, my faith is strong. That is not strong faith. A sister will go to a Chris Brown concert wearing fringes. Imagine a shootout at that concert, and a deacon, a captain, an officer and sisters are among the dead: would that look good for us? The same idol you worshipped in the world, the same music, you are still serving in Christ. Don't let Satan deceive you. Know your beam; know your sin.
- Bishop told of a brother who had just condemned another brother for watching porn, then went to show the Bishop something on his phone, and porn popped up. You can be just in the eyes of men, but you want to be just in the eyes of God, because the flesh easily deceives us into believing we are in this truth.

**[Matthew 7:5](/bible/matthew/7#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/7#v5)</sup> Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother’s eye.

- “Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother’s eye.” Paul said, examine yourself, to see if you are in the faith. One year, two years, nine, ten, eleven years in the truth and not growing: examine yourself. Go inside your mind, like the man in the film Incarnate who went inside his clients' minds to find when the demon came in. Find when your weakness came in: the demon of fatigue against God's law and God's work. Every man knows his weakness. Meditation is going inside the mind, five or ten minutes, and removing whatever stops you from growing. If you lack zeal for study, pray: Lord, give me the first zeal I had when I first knew I was Israel.

**[Matthew 7:6](/bible/matthew/7#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/7#v6)</sup> Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine, lest they trample them under their feet, and turn again and rend you.

- “Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine”. Don’t waste your time talking to dumbass people. “Lest they trample them under their feet, and turn again and rend you”: they will turn against you and fight you. There is no magic scripture. You cannot save everyone; some are predestined to be stupid.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 9:5](/bible/luke/9#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/luke/9#v5)</sup> And whosoever will not receive you, when ye go out of that city, shake off the very dust from your feet for a testimony against them.

    “And whosoever will not receive you, when ye go out of that city, shake off the very dust from your feet for a testimony against them.” Wash your hands of them and leave them alone. Explain to them that the heavenly Father is the Most High God, that he has a son, and that his son has sons, the children of Israel, and they can’t understand it: no, they’re all one. Leave that negro alone. You’re wasting your time; he is on medications, or should be on medications.
  - **[Proverbs 29:9](/bible/proverbs/29#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/29#v9)</sup> If a wise man contendeth with a foolish man, whether he rage or laugh, there is no rest.

    “If a wise man contendeth with a foolish man, whether he rage or laugh, there is no rest.” If a wise man debates with a stupid man or woman, whether that fool rages or laughs, there is no conclusion. It is a circular argument: you argue the same thing over and over and it goes nowhere.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:5](/bible/sirach/33#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/33#v5)</sup> The heart of the foolish is like a cartwheel; and his thoughts are like a rolling axletree.

    “The heart of the foolish is like a cartwheel; and his thoughts are like a rolling axletree.” It just goes in circles, nonstop: three is one, one is three, they’re all the same, the Father and the Son homoousian. You can’t win. Their mind is like a circle, dead set in it, seared as with a hot iron. That is an unreasonable person, and you have to let them go. Doing the same thing over and over and expecting different results is insanity.

**[Matthew 7:7-8](/bible/matthew/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/7#v7)</sup> Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/7#v8)</sup> For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.

- Now the pursuit of wisdom and understanding, the part of Proverbs 4 that says get wisdom, get understanding, and of Isaiah 34 that says seek. “Ask, and it shall be given you; seek, and ye shall find; knock, and it shall be opened unto you.” As the horse is prepared for battle but safety is of the Lord, we put forth the effort and then wisdom and understanding are given. “For every one that asketh receiveth; and he that seeketh findeth.”
- “For every one that asketh receiveth”. If you ask for wisdom you will receive it, but wisdom is not a spirit that just jumps on you. God puts you in the environment of wisdom: being around like-minded brothers who have the wisdom, so that it spreads to you. A brother who will not come to the table for wisdom thinks he already has his own, and then he is confused by a simple question, like the difference between Christ and an animal sacrifice.
- “And he that seeketh findeth; and to him that knocketh it shall be opened.” When we first came in, we never had a day off. Brothers took a three-hour train to class, stayed till four or five in the morning with work the next day; brothers met on Friday nights and on weekday classes. We were knocking because we saw these men as men of wisdom. When you get knocked down, get up again and keep knocking. The Bishop made a book, a fifty-page timeline of Syria, Babylon, Persia, Greece and Rome, for the real disciples, and brothers given it have not read past page 12. We will not be around each other much longer; while we have the Bishop and deacons, learn as much as possible, because millions of our people need to be saved.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 3:20](/bible/revelation/3#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/revelation/3#v20)</sup> Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.

    Christ talks about knocking and seeking, and here he says he is the one knocking and waiting. “Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door.” To open the door means saying, I will do what you say. Notice the action, like the pizza and the car: not calling on the name of the Lord and nothing else. If you never open the door, you cannot receive what is at the door.
  - **[2 Timothy 2:15](/bible/2-timothy/2#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-timothy/2#v15)</sup> Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

    “Study to shew thyself approved unto God”. We are not knocking you for no reason: the scripture tells you to study to show yourself approved. “A workman that needeth not to be ashamed”: if you are a workman, you have to study not to be ashamed. If you are not growing in the spirit, examine it: when you teach in the street and your voice is soft, you have to come with more power and make your voice heard, the way singers train their voice. You are a mighty man in this earth; act like it. Sitting alone in front of a computer, not out teaching anybody, is a weak spirit.

**[Matthew 7:9-11](/bible/matthew/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/7#v9)</sup> Or what man is there of you, whom if his son ask bread, will he give him a stone?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/7#v10)</sup> Or if he ask a fish, will he give him a serpent?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/7#v11)</sup> If ye then, being evil, know how to give good gifts unto your children, how much more shall your Father which is in heaven give good things to them that ask him?

- “If his son ask bread, will he give him a stone?” This is the spirit the Father and Christ move in: he will not give you something contrary to what is needful. You want to be like Jeremiah, the disciples, Paul, Christ? He will give it to you, but you have to show him you are ready to give yourself wholly. If it has not been given, it is because you are still double minded and unstable. “How much more shall your Father which is in heaven give good things to them that ask him?”

**[Matthew 7:12](/bible/matthew/7#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/7#v12)</sup> Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.

- “All things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.” Christianity takes this as do unto others. We apply the royal law, but this really goes into 1 Timothy 4:16: by giving yourself wholly you save yourself and others. We build ourselves so we can build others, with that recruitment mentality, so others come behind us as living epistles, keeping the new covenant with fleshy hearts.
- Taking a counsel to a captain once, not knowing how to counsel it, the answer came back: Cap, you don’t have a daughter, so you are counselling from this side; you don’t want your daughter going through stuff like this. Those of us with daughters deal with it differently.
- “Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.” So men, ask yourself: is the way you treat your wife the way you want your daughter’s husband to treat her? If the answer is no, you have to fix it. Fix yourself: deal with your past, drop the pride, don’t deal in misplaced anger, and don’t dominate with control.

**[Matthew 7:13](/bible/matthew/7#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/7#v13)</sup> Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat:

- “For wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction.” That is all the things out in the world; it is broad, and many things can destroy you in this world. “And many there be which go in thereat”: many people get caught up in a lot of evil.
- It is going to be a fight. “Enter ye in at the strait gate”: that is the narrow way just spoken of, with water on one side and fire on the other. Your lust, your flesh, is fighting and contrary to your spirit; they are always at war with one another.
- “For wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction”: that is why you have to beware. It is easy to fall into sin and easy to do those things, but it is hard to keep the commandments and follow that straight path. We have to keep fighting. “And many there be which go in thereat.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 5:17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)</sup> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.

    It is always going to be a fight, and that is why Hebrews 10:25 matters: it is always to your benefit to be around like-minded people. When you start going off, you can talk to a brother or a sister who will help you in the scriptures, someone to confide in who will build you up. When you forsake those things, you are leaning on your own understanding, with only yourself to talk things over with, and yourself is going to lead you to sin: nah, you all right brother, don’t worry about it, just keep on doing what you doing. That is destruction.

**[Matthew 7:14](/bible/matthew/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/7#v14)</sup> Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it.

- “Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life.” That narrow way is the commandments of God. If you want life, the kingdom, you have to walk that narrow path by keeping the commandments.
- “And few there be that find it.” That goes back to many are called, few are chosen, and to not everyone in whom the seed is sown being saved.
- “Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life.” Stay on that straight path no matter who or what is trying to pull you into that wide gate of destruction. It is going to be a hard walk, but the Most High ain’t tempt us with nothing that we couldn’t do. Keeping the laws is that straight path; the laws keep you going straight. And brothers and sisters are going to help you when you congregate; they will correct you.
- “And few there be that find it.” Not everybody is going to find that thing. It is going to be few.

**[Matthew 7:15-16](/bible/matthew/7#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/7#v15)</sup> Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/7#v16)</sup> Ye shall know them by their fruits. Do men gather grapes of thorns, or figs of thistles?

- “Beware of false prophets, which come to you in sheep’s clothing, but inwardly they are ravening wolves. Ye shall know them by their fruits.” The fruits they bring forth from their spirit. If they don’t have charity, brotherly kindness, love, keeping the commandments, joy, they don’t have the fruit of the spirit. If they have envy, emulation, hatred, adultery and fornication, those are the fruits of the flesh. A false prophet is going to be doing evil things; that is how we will know if somebody is not right.

**[Matthew 7:17-18](/bible/matthew/7#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/7#v17)</sup> Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/7#v18)</sup> A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit.

- “Even so every good tree bringeth forth good fruit; but a corrupt tree bringeth forth evil fruit.” Brothers and sisters, you want to bring forth good fruits. When you see brothers and sisters keep doing evil and not repent of it, that tree is corrupt. “A good tree cannot bring forth evil fruit, neither can a corrupt tree bring forth good fruit.”

**[Matthew 7:19-20](/bible/matthew/7#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/7#v19)</sup> Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/7#v20)</sup> Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.

- “Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit”, the commandments in Christ, “is hewn down, and cast into the fire.” What kind of fire is that? That is the unquenchable fire.
- “Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.”
- “Every tree that bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.” If you are not bringing forth the fruits of the spirit, the Lord is going to cut you down and cast you into the fire, and that is damnation. If you are among us, examine yourself: am I bringing forth good fruits or evil fruits?
- “Wherefore by their fruits ye shall know them.” That is why some are not to be dealt with: they are bringing forth evil fruits and not repenting, so be very mindful.

**[Matthew 7:21](/bible/matthew/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/7#v21)</sup> Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.

- “Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven; but he that doeth the will of my Father which is in heaven.”
- “Not every one that saith unto me, Lord, Lord, shall enter into the kingdom of heaven.” There will be a lot of brothers and sisters who learn this gospel, but not because you say Lord, Lord are you going to enter the kingdom of heaven.

**[Matthew 7:22-23](/bible/matthew/7#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/7#v22)</sup> Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/7#v23)</sup> And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.

- “Many will say to me in that day, Lord, Lord, have we not prophesied in thy name? and in thy name have cast out devils? and in thy name done many wonderful works? And then will I profess unto them, I never knew you: depart from me, ye that work iniquity.” They call out Yahawah bahasham Yahawashi, but the Lord ain’t hearing that, because they have done much evil on this earth.

**[Matthew 7:26](/bible/matthew/7#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/7#v26)</sup> And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand:

- “And every one that heareth these sayings of mine, and doeth them not, shall be likened unto a foolish man, which built his house upon the sand.” Make sure the foundation is not sand. The way you begin your marriage and your relationship affects the rest of it: the probability of it being successful and being in Christ is affected by how you started.
- That does not mean those who did a back-door marriage are doomed forever. You have to make the best of your situation, and it can get better. The point is to spare people the hardship that comes from doing things unadvisedly, without counsel, because they were hard-headed. Just think what life would be like if you didn't have to go through it.

**[Matthew 7:27](/bible/matthew/7#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/7#v27)</sup> And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it.

- “And the rain descended, and the floods came, and the winds blew, and beat upon that house; and it fell: and great was the fall of it.” The rain and floods are the problems; the winds are the tribulations. Anything we do that is not rooted and founded on Christ is going to fall. Be mindful, take the counsel, and do not ignore the red flags. Love should not blind wisdom, and if something feels wrong now it won't magically fix itself after marriage.
