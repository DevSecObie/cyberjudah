**[Matthew 4:1-3](/bible/matthew/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/4#v1)</sup> Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/4#v2)</sup> And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/4#v3)</sup> And when the tempter came to him, he said, If thou be the Son of God, command that these stones be made bread.

- Christ is led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted, and after forty days and forty nights of fasting he is an hungred.
- The tempter comes at the weakest point and goes at the flesh first: command that these stones be made bread.
- This is the same set of temptations the man and the woman met in the garden, and they run through everything the enemy uses.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 2:15-16](/bible/1-john/2#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/2#v15)</sup> Love not the world, neither the things that are in the world. If any man love the world, the love of the Father is not in him.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/1-john/2#v16)</sup> For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

    The lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world.

**[Matthew 4:4](/bible/matthew/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/4#v4)</sup> But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.

- Christ answers with what is written: man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.
- The formula is given here. The flesh is weak and profits nothing; the spirit is strengthened by the word, and that is how the temptation is endured.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 8:3](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v3)</sup> And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the Lord doth man live.

    The law Christ quoted, written to Israel in the wilderness.
  - **[John 6:63](/bible/john/6#v63)**
    > <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

    It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing.
  - **[Matthew 26:41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)</sup> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

    The spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

**[Matthew 4:5-6](/bible/matthew/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/4#v5)</sup> Then the devil taketh him up into the holy city, and setteth him on a pinnacle of the temple,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/4#v6)</sup> And saith unto him, If thou be the Son of God, cast thyself down: for it is written, He shall give his angels charge concerning thee: and in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.

- The devil sets him on a pinnacle of the temple and quotes scripture at him. Satan knows the scriptures and will use them.
- This is why brothers and sisters must be able to try what is brought to them. Men can come with fringes and strong classes and still be workers of deceit.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 91:11-12](/bible/psalms/91#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/91#v11)</sup> For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/91#v12)</sup> They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.

    The passage the devil quoted: he shall give his angels charge over thee.
  - **[2 Corinthians 11:13-15](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v13)</sup> For such are false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v14)</sup> And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v15)</sup> Therefore it is no great thing if his ministers also be transformed as the ministers of righteousness; whose end shall be according to their works.

    Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light, and his ministers as the ministers of righteousness.

**[Matthew 4:7](/bible/matthew/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/4#v7)</sup> Jesus said unto him, It is written again, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.

- Christ answers again with the law: Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God. He fights the enemy with the commandments, which is what those who say the law is done away have thrown away.

**[Matthew 4:8-10](/bible/matthew/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/4#v8)</sup> Again, the devil taketh him up into an exceeding high mountain, and sheweth him all the kingdoms of the world, and the glory of them;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/4#v9)</sup> And saith unto him, All these things will I give thee, if thou wilt fall down and worship me.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/4#v10)</sup> Then saith Jesus unto him, Get thee hence, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.

- The devil shows him all the kingdoms of the world and offers them for worship. This is the pride of life and the lust of the eyes, offered to a man who at that moment was a carpenter out of Nazareth.
- Christ answers a third time out of the law: Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.

**[Matthew 4:11](/bible/matthew/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/4#v11)</sup> Then the devil leaveth him, and, behold, angels came and ministered unto him.

- The devil leaves him and angels come and minister unto him. The leaving is not permanent.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 4:13](/bible/luke/4#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/luke/4#v13)</sup> And when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.

    When the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.

**[Matthew 4:12-16](/bible/matthew/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/4#v12)</sup> Now when Jesus had heard that John was cast into prison, he departed into Galilee;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/4#v13)</sup> And leaving Nazareth, he came and dwelt in Capernaum, which is upon the sea coast, in the borders of Zabulon and Nephthalim:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/4#v14)</sup> That it might be fulfilled which was spoken by Esaias the prophet, saying,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/4#v15)</sup> The land of Zabulon, and the land of Nephthalim, by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/4#v16)</sup> The people which sat in darkness saw great light; and to them which sat in the region and shadow of death light is sprung up.

- John is cast into prison and Christ departs into Galilee, and dwells in Capernaum in the borders of Zebulun and Nephthalim.
- The scripture calls that country Galilee of the Gentiles. Zebulun and Nephthalim are tribes of the northern kingdom, and this is the key to who the gentiles are that the gospel goes to after the southern kingdom rejects it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 9:1-2](/bible/isaiah/9#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/9#v1)</sup> Nevertheless the dimness shall not be such as was in her vexation, when at the first he lightly afflicted the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, and afterward did more grievously afflict her by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, in Galilee of the nations.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/9#v2)</sup> The people that walked in darkness have seen a great light: they that dwell in the land of the shadow of death, upon them hath the light shined.

    The prophecy quoted, naming the same land and the same people that saw great light.

**[Matthew 4:17](/bible/matthew/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/4#v17)</sup> From that time Jesus began to preach, and to say, Repent: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.

- From that time Christ begins to preach the same word John preached: Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.

**[Matthew 4:18-20](/bible/matthew/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/4#v18)</sup> And Jesus, walking by the sea of Galilee, saw two brethren, Simon called Peter, and Andrew his brother, casting a net into the sea: for they were fishers.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/4#v19)</sup> And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/4#v20)</sup> And they straightway left their nets, and followed him.

- Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men. The fishers come first; the hunters come after.
- Peter and Andrew straightway left their nets. That was their living, and they left it without hesitation. Many in this truth have been in it for years and know little, because they were not willing to give up what they were building.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 16:16](/bible/jeremiah/16#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/16#v16)</sup> Behold, I will send for many fishers, saith the Lord, and they shall fish them; and after will I send for many hunters, and they shall hunt them from every mountain, and from every hill, and out of the holes of the rocks.

    I will send for many fishers, and after will I send for many hunters.
  - **[2 Esdras 13:54](/bible/2-esdras/13#v54)**
    > <sup>[54](/bible/2-esdras/13#v54)</sup> For thou hast forsaken thine own way, and applied thy diligence unto my law, and sought it.

    For thou hast forsaken thine own way, and applied thy diligence unto my law, and sought it.

**[Matthew 4:21-22](/bible/matthew/4#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/4#v21)</sup> And going on from thence, he saw other two brethren, James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, in a ship with Zebedee their father, mending their nets; and he called them.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/4#v22)</sup> And they immediately left the ship and their father, and followed him.

- James and John immediately left the ship and their father and followed him. That is faith, and the Most High uses men who will make that kind of sacrifice.

**[Matthew 4:23-25](/bible/matthew/4#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/4#v23)</sup> And Jesus went about all Galilee, teaching in their synagogues, and preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness and all manner of disease among the people.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/4#v24)</sup> And his fame went throughout all Syria: and they brought unto him all sick people that were taken with divers diseases and torments, and those which were possessed with devils, and those which were lunatick, and those that had the palsy; and he healed them.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/4#v25)</sup> And there followed him great multitudes of people from Galilee, and from Decapolis, and from Jerusalem, and from Judea, and from beyond Jordan.

- He goes about all Galilee teaching in their synagogues, preaching the gospel of the kingdom, and healing all manner of sickness, and great multitudes follow him.
