## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 10 through 13. The twelve are named and sent out, and they are sent to one people only. Christ then answers John the Baptist out of prison, contends with the Pharisees over the sabbath and over the source of his power, and teaches the multitude in parables, explaining to the disciples what the parables mean. The thread is who the message belongs to and who is able to receive it.

**[Matthew 10:1-4](/bible/matthew/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/10#v1)</sup> And when he had called unto him his twelve disciples, he gave them power against unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal all manner of sickness and all manner of disease.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/10#v2)</sup> Now the names of the twelve apostles are these; The first, Simon, who is called Peter, and Andrew his brother; James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/10#v3)</sup> Philip, and Bartholomew; Thomas, and Matthew the publican; James the son of Alpheus, and Lebbeus, whose surname was Thaddeus;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/10#v4)</sup> Simon the Canaanite, and Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.

- Christ calls the twelve and gives them power against unclean spirits and to heal all manner of sickness and disease, the same works he was doing himself.
- The twelve are named here, ending with Judas Iscariot, who also betrayed him.

**[Matthew 10:5-6](/bible/matthew/10#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/10#v5)</sup> These twelve Jesus sent forth, and commanded them, saying, Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/10#v6)</sup> But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

- Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not, but go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
- The twelve go to the southern kingdom first, before the gospel goes to the northern kingdom.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 4:15-16](/bible/matthew/4#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/4#v15)</sup> The land of Zabulon, and the land of Nephthalim, by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles;
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/4#v16)</sup> The people which sat in darkness saw great light; and to them which sat in the region and shadow of death light is sprung up.

    Zebulun and Nephthalim are called Galilee of the Gentiles, showing that tribes of Israel in a gentile state are spoken of that way.

**[Matthew 10:7-8](/bible/matthew/10#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/10#v7)</sup> And as ye go, preach, saying, The kingdom of heaven is at hand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/10#v8)</sup> Heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils: freely ye have received, freely give.

- Preach that the kingdom of heaven is at hand, heal the sick, cleanse the lepers, raise the dead, cast out devils. Freely ye have received, freely give.

**[Matthew 10:9-10](/bible/matthew/10#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/10#v9)</sup> Provide neither gold, nor silver, nor brass in your purses,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/10#v10)</sup> Nor scrip for your journey, neither two coats, neither shoes, nor yet staves: for the workman is worthy of his meat.

- They are to carry no gold, silver or brass, no scrip, no second coat, no shoes, no staves, for the workman is worthy of his meat.

**[Matthew 10:11-13](/bible/matthew/10#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/10#v11)</sup> And into whatsoever city or town ye shall enter, enquire who in it is worthy; and there abide till ye go thence.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/10#v12)</sup> And when ye come into an house, salute it.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/10#v13)</sup> And if the house be worthy, let your peace come upon it: but if it be not worthy, let your peace return to you.

- In every city they are to enquire who in it is worthy, and to let their peace rest on a worthy house and return to them from one that is not.

**[Matthew 10:14-15](/bible/matthew/10#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/10#v14)</sup> And whosoever shall not receive you, nor hear your words, when ye depart out of that house or city, shake off the dust of your feet.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/10#v15)</sup> Verily I say unto you, It shall be more tolerable for the land of Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of judgment, than for that city.

- Where they are not received they shake off the dust of their feet, and it shall be more tolerable for Sodom and Gomorrha in the day of judgment than for that city.

**[Matthew 10:16](/bible/matthew/10#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/10#v16)</sup> Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves: be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless as doves.

- Behold, I send you forth as sheep in the midst of wolves: be ye therefore wise as serpents, and harmless as doves.
- This is a word for a people living in captivity. There is only so much that can be said and done on another man's ground, and wisdom is required alongside harmlessness.

**[Matthew 10:17-18](/bible/matthew/10#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/10#v17)</sup> But beware of men: for they will deliver you up to the councils, and they will scourge you in their synagogues;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/10#v18)</sup> And ye shall be brought before governors and kings for my sake, for a testimony against them and the Gentiles.

- They will be delivered up to the councils and scourged in the synagogues, which is what the Pharisees had the power to do.
- They are brought before governors and kings for a testimony against them and the Gentiles: against their own people who turned on Christ, and against the rulers of that time.

**[Matthew 10:19-20](/bible/matthew/10#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/10#v19)</sup> But when they deliver you up, take no thought how or what ye shall speak: for it shall be given you in that same hour what ye shall speak.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/10#v20)</sup> For it is not ye that speak, but the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you.

- Take no thought how or what ye shall speak, for it shall be given you in that same hour. It is not you that speak, but the Spirit of your Father which speaketh in you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 21:15](/bible/luke/21#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/luke/21#v15)</sup> For I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to gainsay nor resist.

    I will give you a mouth and wisdom, which all your adversaries shall not be able to gainsay nor resist.

**[Matthew 10:21-22](/bible/matthew/10#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/10#v21)</sup> And the brother shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the child: and the children shall rise up against their parents, and cause them to be put to death.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/10#v22)</sup> And ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.

- The brother shall deliver up the brother to death, and the father the child. Serving the Most High brings division, and it comes first inside your own house.
- Ye shall be hated of all men for my name's sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.

**[Matthew 10:23](/bible/matthew/10#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/10#v23)</sup> But when they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another: for verily I say unto you, Ye shall not have gone over the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be come.

- When they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another.

**[Matthew 10:24-25](/bible/matthew/10#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/10#v24)</sup> The disciple is not above his master, nor the servant above his lord.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/10#v25)</sup> It is enough for the disciple that he be as his master, and the servant as his lord. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, how much more shall they call them of his household?

- The disciple is not above his master. If they have called the master of the house Beelzebub, they will call them of his household worse.

**[Matthew 10:26-27](/bible/matthew/10#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/10#v26)</sup> Fear them not therefore: for there is nothing covered, that shall not be revealed; and hid, that shall not be known.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/10#v27)</sup> What I tell you in darkness, that speak ye in light: and what ye hear in the ear, that preach ye upon the housetops.

- There is nothing covered that shall not be revealed. What is told in darkness is to be spoken in light, and what is heard in the ear preached upon the housetops.

**[Matthew 10:28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)</sup> And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

- Fear not them which kill the body but are not able to kill the soul, but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

**[Matthew 10:29-31](/bible/matthew/10#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/10#v29)</sup> Are not two sparrows sold for a farthing? and one of them shall not fall on the ground without your Father.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/10#v30)</sup> But the very hairs of your head are all numbered.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/10#v31)</sup> Fear ye not therefore, ye are of more value than many sparrows.

- Not one sparrow falls to the ground without your Father, and the very hairs of your head are all numbered. That is not written for decoration; it is how closely he holds his own.

**[Matthew 10:32-33](/bible/matthew/10#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/10#v32)</sup> Whosoever therefore shall confess me before men, him will I confess also before my Father which is in heaven.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/10#v33)</sup> But whosoever shall deny me before men, him will I also deny before my Father which is in heaven.

- Whosoever shall confess me before men, him will I confess also before my Father; and whosoever shall deny me before men, him will I also deny.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 94:16](/bible/psalms/94#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/94#v16)</sup> Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

    Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? That standing up is what confessing him before men looks like.

**[Matthew 10:34-36](/bible/matthew/10#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/10#v34)</sup> Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/10#v35)</sup> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/10#v36)</sup> And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household.

- I came not to send peace, but a sword. A man's foes shall be they of his own household.

**[Matthew 10:37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)</sup> He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me: and he that loveth son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.

- He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me. This is not forbidding natural love for your family; it is forbidding the trimming of your ways to keep peace with them.
- That is how the pagan holidays come back in. A brother in the truth goes to see family he has not seen in a long while, and he sits down in the feast to keep them comfortable.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 2:33](/bible/jeremiah/2#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/2#v33)</sup> Why trimmest thou thy way to seek love? therefore hast thou also taught the wicked ones thy ways.

    Why trimmest thou thy way to seek love? therefore hast thou also taught the wicked ones thy ways.

**[Matthew 10:38-39](/bible/matthew/10#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/10#v38)</sup> And he that taketh not his cross, and followeth after me, is not worthy of me.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/10#v39)</sup> He that findeth his life shall lose it: and he that loseth his life for my sake shall find it.

- He that taketh not his cross and followeth after me is not worthy of me. He that findeth his life shall lose it, and he that loseth his life for my sake shall find it.
- What is given up is the sin that was being lived in. That old man is put to death, and the life that is found is coming out of the congregation of the dead.

**[Matthew 10:40-42](/bible/matthew/10#v40)**

> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/10#v40)</sup> He that receiveth you receiveth me, and he that receiveth me receiveth him that sent me.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/10#v41)</sup> He that receiveth a prophet in the name of a prophet shall receive a prophet’s reward; and he that receiveth a righteous man in the name of a righteous man shall receive a righteous man’s reward.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/10#v42)</sup> And whosoever shall give to drink unto one of these little ones a cup of cold water only in the name of a disciple, verily I say unto you, he shall in no wise lose his reward.

- He that receiveth you receiveth me, and he that receiveth me receiveth him that sent me. Even a cup of cold water given in the name of a disciple does not lose its reward, and the reward in view is the kingdom.
