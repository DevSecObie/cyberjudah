## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 15 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-9, 13-14, 16-18, 22-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 15:1-3](/bible/matthew/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/15#v1)</sup> Then came to Jesus scribes and Pharisees, which were of Jerusalem, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/15#v2)</sup> Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/15#v3)</sup> But he answered and said unto them, Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God by your tradition?

- “Then came to Jesus scribes and Pharisees, which were of Jerusalem”. Christ was travelling, and they came from Jerusalem, following him around from place to place. They follow us from state to state, and soon it will be country to country. Do not be shocked.
- This is where the title comes from. The scribes and Pharisees ask, “Why do thy disciples transgress the tradition of the elders? for they wash not their hands when they eat bread.” Christ answers, “Why do ye also transgress the commandment of God by your tradition?”
- That is what we ask Christians all the time. Why are you not keeping a Sabbath day? Why aren’t the women dressing modestly? Why do you eat unclean foods? And these are just some of the basic laws.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 3:22](/bible/mark/3#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/mark/3#v22)</sup> And the scribes which came down from Jerusalem said, He hath Beelzebub, and by the prince of the devils casteth he out devils.

    “And the scribes which came down from Jerusalem said, He hath Beelzebub”. The scribes came down from Jerusalem: they had started to follow Christ from region to region.
  - **[Mark 7:1](/bible/mark/7#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/mark/7#v1)</sup> Then came together unto him the Pharisees, and certain of the scribes, which came from Jerusalem.

    “Then came together unto him the Pharisees, and certain of the scribes, which came from Jerusalem.” They came from Jerusalem, following him. The same thing done yesterday is being done this very day.

**[Matthew 15:4](/bible/matthew/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/15#v4)</sup> For God commanded, saying, Honour thy father and mother: and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death.

- The honour of leadership and parents has to be at the forefront of the young men’s minds; without it they end up like the young men in the courtroom. Honour is moral integrity, honesty and a strong code of conduct, and the high respect a person earns through their actions, or a special privilege.
- “For God commanded, saying”: this is not a commandment that man stated; it is what God commanded. “Honour thy father and mother”: have moral integrity, honesty and a strong code of conduct toward your father and your mother, and give them that high respect.
- “and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death.” That is the judgment for not honouring your father and mother.
- “Honour thy father and mother: and, He that curseth father or mother, let him die the death.” So you children at home putting your hands on your mama or your daddy: Christ said, let him die the death.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 7:27-28](/bible/sirach/7#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/7#v27)</sup> Honour thy father with thy whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of thy mother.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/7#v28)</sup> Remember that thou wast begotten of them; and how canst thou recompense them the things that they have done for thee?

    “Honour thy father with thy whole heart.” The root of integrity is integer, meaning whole: honour them with the whole heart. “and forget not the sorrows of thy mother”: the troubles of raising you, that she had to carry you for nine months and give birth to you.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 3:10-13](/bible/sirach/3#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/3#v10)</sup> Glory not in the dishonour of thy father; for thy father’s dishonour is no glory unto thee.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/3#v11)</sup> For the glory of a man is from the honour of his father; and a mother in dishonour is a reproach to the children.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/3#v12)</sup> My son, help thy father in his age, and grieve him not as long as he liveth.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/3#v13)</sup> And if his understanding fail, have patience with him; and despise him not when thou art in thy full strength.

    “Glory not in the dishonour of thy father; for thy father’s dishonour is no glory unto thee.” This is for the older ones too, who may have parents that are not in the truth. You might think that because your parent is not in the truth you don’t have to give them the respect of a parent. That is not what the Lord said. It is nothing to glory in that the Lord called you and your father or mother is not in this truth.

**[Matthew 15:5-6](/bible/matthew/15#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/15#v5)</sup> But ye say, Whosoever shall say to his father or his mother, It is a gift, by whatsoever thou mightest be profited by me;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/15#v6)</sup> And honour not his father or his mother, he shall be free. Thus have ye made the commandment of God of none effect by your tradition.

That is modern day Christianity: by their traditions they make the commandments of God of none effect. Like the Sabbath day: by their traditions they do not honour the Sabbath day.

But the doctrine is right where our people are falling off the horse, and Christianity is all about the praise and worship team. They took their European music and building styles into other parts of the world, and built churches, sang songs and prayed in a local ethnic style to appeal to the ones they were trying to win over. That is why they are always in our neighborhoods.

**[Matthew 15:16](/bible/matthew/15#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/15#v16)</sup> And Jesus said, Are ye also yet without understanding?

- The good news is that you can reprogram your mind. The brain is like a computer: it takes in inputs, runs them through thought patterns, and puts out feelings and actions, and faulty patterns can be edited like code. Esau, wiser than Daniel, built the computer after the brain, its neurons and its memory storage, and social media is built to work with the brain. That is why we cannot stop scrolling: they made it an extension of you. The input devices of the brain are the senses: seeing, hearing, touching and tasting.
- The computer was fashioned after the brain, to mimic the operating system the Most High gave us: the programmer inputs information to get the computer to do and think what he wants, and out comes an output. The Pharisees are dumb. They were arguing about food, about eating with unwashed hands, and Christ said, are you stupid? “Are ye also yet without understanding?”

**[Matthew 15:18](/bible/matthew/15#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/15#v18)</sup> But those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart; and they defile the man.

- “But those things which proceed out of the mouth come forth from the heart”: what comes out of your mouth is the output, and it comes from your mind, from whatever was put into you. “And they defile the man.”
- What you put in is stored, short term and long term, like RAM and a hard drive; that is why a song or a thought from years ago suddenly comes back out of you. Whoever is putting the input into your processor controls the output, and often we do not even realize we are being controlled. Those are not your words: you were made in a factory called America. The elites who control the system manipulate the education, the politics and the TV to program you to function the way they want. The only thing that breaks that code is to reprogram and recode it. Christ is the one who hacked the system and broke the code Satan encoded into the whole world, and he wiped out that virus called sin within us.

**[Matthew 15:22-24](/bible/matthew/15#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/15#v22)</sup> And, behold, a woman of Canaan came out of the same coasts, and cried unto him, saying, Have mercy on me, O Lord, thou Son of David; my daughter is grievously vexed with a devil.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/15#v23)</sup> But he answered her not a word. And his disciples came and besought him, saying, Send her away; for she crieth after us.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/15#v24)</sup> But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel.

- “Have mercy on me, O Lord, thou Son of David”. Did she curse at him? Did she rebuke him, the way they were doing us? No. Those are some kind words. She was intreating him kindly. “My daughter is grievously vexed with a devil.” Certainly, she thought, he could help with that.
- Why deal with this at all? Because the Christianity preachers are trying to make you think Christ is for everybody. When black folks subscribe to that and advocate for it, what we are really saying is that we think so low of ourselves we have to be for everybody, while everybody hates us and we are okay with that.
- “But he answered her not a word.” She didn’t curse at him to make him ignore her. What kind of Jesus is this, that they say sent his disciples out to all nations? Christ didn’t even dignify her with a response. And then they say we are twisting scripture.
- “And his disciples came and besought him, saying, Send her away; for she crieth after us.” The disciples begged Jesus to tell this woman to get the hell out of there. Why? Because Christ had already instructed them five chapters earlier not to go into the way of the Gentiles. The disciples were in order. They understood the order, and we have to learn that.

**[Matthew 15:26](/bible/matthew/15#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/15#v26)</sup> But he answered and said, It is not meet to take the children’s bread, and to cast it to dogs.

- “But he answered and said, It is not meet to take the children’s bread, and to cast it to dogs.” This tells you Christ got mad: I already told you what I am here for. It is not right for me to disregard what my Father told me to do, and take the healing power and the blessings meant for the children of Israel and cast them to dogs. The children’s bread is the gifts, and they were for Israel.
- “But he answered and said, It is not meet to take the children’s bread, and to cast it to dogs.” The children and the dogs are distinguished. The children's bread belongs to Israel. Christ's words are used to establish the place of God's sons and daughters rather than the denial and insults directed at them by those claiming a share in their kingdom.

    Prove that the children’s bread was the gifts: “These twelve Jesus sent forth, and commanded them, saying, Go not into the way of the Gentiles, and into any city of the Samaritans enter ye not: But go rather to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.” Don’t go to the other nations, and when it said the other nations it was only talking about the Israelites; don’t even deal with the northern kingdom yet.

**[Matthew 15:27](/bible/matthew/15#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/15#v27)</sup> And she said, Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.

- “And she said, Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.” The woman acknowledged the master and accepted the crumbs. Her humility is contrasted with those who refuse to acknowledge Israel as God's children or accept service in the kingdom. She was prepared to take the portion available to her instead of claiming the children's bread.
- The crumbs are connected to the gleanings left after the harvest. The harvest and bread belong to Israel, while provision is left for the stranger. The distinction gives the woman help without making her the owner of what belongs to the children. The nations' participation is understood as cleaving to Israel in service, not taking Israel's place.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 14:2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)</sup> And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

    “And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids:” The house of Israel possesses the nations as servants and handmaids in the Lord's land. This is joined to the woman's acceptance of the crumbs and of the master's place. The kingdom, like the harvest, belongs to Israel; the strangers receive provision while serving.
