## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 6, 13, 15-18, 24-25, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 16:1-2](/bible/matthew/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/16#v1)</sup> The Pharisees also with the Sadducees came, and tempting desired him that he would shew them a sign from heaven.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/16#v2)</sup> He answered and said unto them, When it is evening, ye say, It will be fair weather: for the sky is red.

- When did the beginning of sorrows start? To answer it we first have to learn how to read the times. The Pharisees and the Sadducees came tempting Christ, wanting him to “shew them a sign from heaven.”
- “When it is evening, ye say, It will be fair weather: for the sky is red.” In the evening, when the sun is about to go down, you can look up at the sky, see it red, and tell it is going to be fair weather.
- The Pharisees and Sadducees came tempting Christ for a sign from heaven, and he answered, “When it is evening, ye say, It will be fair weather: for the sky is red.” Jake always thinks he is a meteorologist; we just stick a hand out the window and say it is going to be nice today.

**[Matthew 16:3](/bible/matthew/16#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/16#v3)</sup> And in the morning, It will be foul weather to day: for the sky is red and lowring. O ye hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the sky; but can ye not discern the signs of the times?

- “And in the morning, It will be foul weather to day: for the sky is red and lowring.” In the morning you go outside, see the sky full of clouds, and you can tell it is going to rain today. “Ye can discern the face of the sky.” We are able to do that.
- “But can ye not discern the signs of the times?” That is what we have to learn: to discern the signs of the times. The Most High gave us signs to look for, so that we know what time we are living in. In Matthew 24 the apostles asked Christ what signs to look for before he returned, and he went over them.
- “O ye hypocrites, ye can discern the face of the sky; but can ye not discern the signs of the times?” You can predict the weather, but you cannot line up what is going on: you do not see what time we are living in, that we are in the latter days, that prophecy is being fulfilled. All of us should be able to discern the signs of the times.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 26:41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)</sup> Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.

    “Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation.” We should not be living under a rock or be clueless. We should know what is going on around us and be up to speed with current events, not only in Babylon but all around the world, because our people are scattered to the four corners of the earth. Our forefathers kept up with what was going on, and they were able to line those things up with the scriptures.
  - **[Daniel 9:2-3](/bible/daniel/9#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/9#v2)</sup> In the first year of his reign I Daniel understood by books the number of the years, whereof the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/9#v3)</sup> And I set my face unto the Lord God, to seek by prayer and supplications, with fasting, and sackcloth, and ashes:

    “In the first year of his reign I Daniel understood by books the number of the years, whereof the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.” The books are the scriptures, the prophecies. Daniel matched the current events of his time with what was written, and saw that he was living in the time Jeremiah spoke of in Jeremiah 29.

**[Matthew 16:6](/bible/matthew/16#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/16#v6)</sup> Then Jesus said unto them, Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.

- “Take heed and beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and of the Sadducees.” Christ warned us of their leaven. What was their leaven? They were hypocrites, and they were following after the manner of the wicked. Leaven is an influence that spreads; bitterness is on the inside and it corrupts the spirit.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 23:1-3](/bible/matthew/23#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/23#v1)</sup> Then spake Jesus to the multitude, and to his disciples,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/23#v2)</sup> Saying, The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/23#v3)</sup> All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.

    “The scribes and the Pharisees sit in Moses’ seat: All therefore whatsoever they bid you observe, that observe and do; but do not ye after their works: for they say, and do not.” What the Pharisees had going was hypocrisy. They say and do not. Whatever they tell you to do from Moses’ seat, do it, but do not do after their works, because of whom their works were after.
  - **[Mark 8:15](/bible/mark/8#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/mark/8#v15)</sup> And he charged them, saying, Take heed, beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, and of the leaven of Herod.

    “Take heed, beware of the leaven of the Pharisees, and of the leaven of Herod.” The leaven of Herod lets you know they were following the white man, Esau. They were not just hypocrites; they were following after the manner of the wicked. That is why Christ said beware.
  - **[John 11:45-48](/bible/john/11#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/john/11#v45)</sup> Then many of the Jews which came to Mary, and had seen the things which Jesus did, believed on him.
    >
    > <sup>[46](/bible/john/11#v46)</sup> But some of them went their ways to the Pharisees, and told them what things Jesus had done.
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/john/11#v47)</sup> Then gathered the chief priests and the Pharisees a council, and said, What do we? for this man doeth many miracles.
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/john/11#v48)</sup> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation.

    Many of the Jews who saw what Jesus did “believed on him. But some of them went their ways to the Pharisees, and told them what things Jesus had done.” Wherever there is good, evil has to be there: there were the believers, and then there were the coons who went and told.

**[Matthew 16:13](/bible/matthew/16#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/16#v13)</sup> When Jesus came into the coasts of Cesarea Philippi, he asked his disciples, saying, Whom do men say that I the Son of man am?

- “Whom do men say that I the Son of man am?” Son of man, not son of woman, not son of the Holy Spirit. Son of man.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 2:1-2](/bible/ezekiel/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/2#v1)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, stand upon thy feet, and I will speak unto thee.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/2#v2)</sup> And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me, and set me upon my feet, that I heard him that spake unto me.

    “And he said unto me, Son of man, stand upon thy feet.” Ezekiel is called son of man.
  - **[Daniel 8:17](/bible/daniel/8#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/8#v17)</sup> So he came near where I stood: and when he came, I was afraid, and fell upon my face: but he said unto me, Understand, O son of man: for at the time of the end shall be the vision.

    “Understand, O son of man.” Daniel is called son of man. Ezekiel and Daniel both had parents, and Christ is called son of man the same way. There is nothing to be confused about.

**[Matthew 16:15-17](/bible/matthew/16#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/16#v15)</sup> He saith unto them, But whom say ye that I am?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/16#v16)</sup> And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/16#v17)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Simon Bar–jona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.

- Christ asks, “But whom say ye that I am?” and Simon Peter answers, “Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God.” Flesh and blood did not reveal it to him, but the Father which is in heaven.

**[Matthew 16:18](/bible/matthew/16#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/16#v18)</sup> And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

- Eighteen is the key. “Upon this rock”: Christ is talking about himself, not Peter. “I will build my church”: that church is the government we just read about in Isaiah 9, the same church that was in the wilderness in Acts 7:38.
- “And the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.” That means there ain’t no stopping us. You cannot stop this truth from continuing. Once you get the proper understanding of the word of God, you can’t stop it. It is going to flourish and it is going to grow.
- The gates of hell will attempt to stop it, but they won’t prevail. They attempted to stop it when they murdered Nat Turner, when they murdered Denmark Vesey, when they murdered Gabriel Prosser. Those men knew that they were Israelites, and they said, kill them, kill them. It didn’t stop. Through whispers and hushes, this word kept going.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 6:12](/bible/ephesians/6#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/6#v12)</sup> For we wrestle not against flesh and blood, but against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.

    What are the gates of hell that are going to try to stop this? “For we wrestle not against flesh and blood”. Sometimes we get in our carnal mind and say, if we organize, we can overthrow the system. No, you can’t. This ain’t a flesh and blood fight. It is “against principalities, against powers, against the rulers of the darkness of this world, against spiritual wickedness in high places.”
  - **[Daniel 4:17](/bible/daniel/4#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/4#v17)</sup> This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones: to the intent that the living may know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will, and setteth up over it the basest of men.

    Daniel proves it: the Lord sets these wicked men up. “This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones: to the intent that the living may know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will, and setteth up over it the basest of men.”
  - **[1 Corinthians 1:26-29](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v26)</sup> For ye see your calling, brethren, how that not many wise men after the flesh, not many mighty, not many noble, are called:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v27)</sup> But God hath chosen the foolish things of the world to confound the wise; and God hath chosen the weak things of the world to confound the things which are mighty;
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v28)</sup> And base things of the world, and things which are despised, hath God chosen, yea, and things which are not, to bring to nought things that are:
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v29)</sup> That no flesh should glory in his presence.

    We are at the bottom of that pyramid, and they say we are nothing, we’re sheep. So how do we get out of this system? First: “Because the foolishness of God is wiser than men; and the weakness of God is stronger than men.”

**[Matthew 16:24](/bible/matthew/16#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/16#v24)</sup> Then said Jesus unto his disciples, If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.

- “If any man will come after me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross, and follow me.” In this walk there is going to be sacrifice. You cannot get the kingdom if you are not ready to sacrifice; you are going to have to sacrifice something.

**[Matthew 16:25](/bible/matthew/16#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/16#v25)</sup> For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: and whosoever will lose his life for my sake shall find it.

- “For whosoever will save his life shall lose it: and whosoever will lose his life for my sake shall find it.” Find it where? He is talking about the kingdom. You have to be ready to sacrifice everything; that is the only way to get the kingdom.
- Traveling all over the world to teach and wake up our people is a big sacrifice: time away from your family, time away from your job. Those are sacrifices, but they are very little to pay to wake up your people, and you have to be able to make them.
