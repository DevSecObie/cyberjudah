## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 18 through 21. It is a reading session rather than a full breakdown, so the parables are read through and commented on where they touch the life of the congregation: how to carry an offence, how far forgiveness goes, marriage and divorce, what a rich man cannot buy, the labourers who all receive the same wage, and the husbandmen who killed the son. The session closes with the kingdom taken from those who taught the law and did not do it.

**[Matthew 18:1-5](/bible/matthew/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/18#v1)</sup> At the same time came the disciples unto Jesus, saying, Who is the greatest in the kingdom of heaven?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/18#v2)</sup> And Jesus called a little child unto him, and set him in the midst of them,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)</sup> And said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/18#v4)</sup> Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/18#v5)</sup> And whoso shall receive one such little child in my name receiveth me.

- Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven. Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, the same is the greatest.
- Coming into this truth means learning again from the beginning, humbling yourself to the men set before you and to the women who have been in it longer than you.

**[Matthew 18:6-9](/bible/matthew/18#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/18#v6)</sup> But whoso shall offend one of these little ones which believe in me, it were better for him that a millstone were hanged about his neck, and that he were drowned in the depth of the sea.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/18#v7)</sup> Woe unto the world because of offences! for it must needs be that offences come; but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh!
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/18#v8)</sup> Wherefore if thy hand or thy foot offend thee, cut them off, and cast them from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life halt or maimed, rather than having two hands or two feet to be cast into everlasting fire.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/18#v9)</sup> And if thine eye offend thee, pluck it out, and cast it from thee: it is better for thee to enter into life with one eye, rather than having two eyes to be cast into hell fire.

- Woe unto the world because of offences, for it must needs be that offences come, but woe to that man by whom the offence cometh.
- Offences will come in the congregation because everyone comes out of a different life and is still learning the others. Much of what offends is small and passes once brethren know each other.
- What is in a man that will not let him go on has to be cut off. Better to enter into life maimed than to keep the thing and be cast into the fire.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 11:19](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v19)</sup> For there must be also heresies among you, that they which are approved may be made manifest among you.

    There must be also heresies among you, that they which are approved may be made manifest among you.

**[Matthew 18:10-14](/bible/matthew/18#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/18#v10)</sup> Take heed that ye despise not one of these little ones; for I say unto you, That in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father which is in heaven.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/18#v11)</sup> For the Son of man is come to save that which was lost.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/18#v12)</sup> How think ye? if a man have an hundred sheep, and one of them be gone astray, doth he not leave the ninety and nine, and goeth into the mountains, and seeketh that which is gone astray?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/18#v13)</sup> And if so be that he find it, verily I say unto you, he rejoiceth more of that sheep, than of the ninety and nine which went not astray.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/18#v14)</sup> Even so it is not the will of your Father which is in heaven, that one of these little ones should perish.

- Take heed that ye despise not one of these little ones, for in heaven their angels do always behold the face of my Father.
- The shepherd leaves the ninety and nine to go after the one that is gone astray, and rejoices more over that one when he finds it.
- It is not the will of your Father which is in heaven that one of these little ones should perish. That men still fall short is a matter of their own will, not his.

**[Matthew 18:15](/bible/matthew/18#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/18#v15)</sup> Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother.

- The first step is between the two of you alone. Go to that brother or sister yourself, and not to the rest of the congregation about what they did to you.
- If he hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother. Very often he did not know the thing was an offence at all.

**[Matthew 18:16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)</sup> But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.

- If he will not hear, take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established. The witnesses are there so that what is said is settled and not left to one man's account.

**[Matthew 18:17-18](/bible/matthew/18#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/18#v17)</sup> And if he shall neglect to hear them, tell it unto the church: but if he neglect to hear the church, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/18#v18)</sup> Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.

- If he neglect to hear them, tell it unto the church. If he neglect to hear the church, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.
- This is the order. Matthew 18 is not to be pulled out over every small thing that has not first been carried to the brother himself.

**[Matthew 18:19-20](/bible/matthew/18#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/18#v19)</sup> Again I say unto you, That if two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father which is in heaven.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/18#v20)</sup> For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.

- If two of you shall agree on earth as touching any thing that they shall ask, it shall be done for them, for where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.

**[Matthew 18:21-22](/bible/matthew/18#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/18#v21)</sup> Then came Peter to him, and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? till seven times?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/18#v22)</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven.

- Peter asks how often he must forgive, till seven times, and is answered, until seventy times seven.
- A brother striving for righteousness does not keep committing the same offence once he understands it. A man who does it over and over is showing what he is.

**[Matthew 18:23-35](/bible/matthew/18#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/18#v23)</sup> Therefore is the kingdom of heaven likened unto a certain king, which would take account of his servants.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/18#v24)</sup> And when he had begun to reckon, one was brought unto him, which owed him ten thousand talents.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/18#v25)</sup> But forasmuch as he had not to pay, his lord commanded him to be sold, and his wife, and children, and all that he had, and payment to be made.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/18#v26)</sup> The servant therefore fell down, and worshipped him, saying, Lord, have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/18#v27)</sup> Then the lord of that servant was moved with compassion, and loosed him, and forgave him the debt.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/18#v28)</sup> But the same servant went out, and found one of his fellowservants, which owed him an hundred pence: and he laid hands on him, and took him by the throat, saying, Pay me that thou owest.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/18#v29)</sup> And his fellowservant fell down at his feet, and besought him, saying, Have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/18#v30)</sup> And he would not: but went and cast him into prison, till he should pay the debt.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/18#v31)</sup> So when his fellowservants saw what was done, they were very sorry, and came and told unto their lord all that was done.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/18#v32)</sup> Then his lord, after that he had called him, said unto him, O thou wicked servant, I forgave thee all that debt, because thou desiredst me:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/18#v33)</sup> Shouldest not thou also have had compassion on thy fellowservant, even as I had pity on thee?
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/18#v34)</sup> And his lord was wroth, and delivered him to the tormentors, till he should pay all that was due unto him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/18#v35)</sup> So likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.

- The servant owing ten thousand talents is forgiven the whole debt, and then takes his fellowservant by the throat over an hundred pence.
- His lord delivers him to the tormentors, and so shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.
- Holding a grudge against a brother while expecting mercy from the Most High is the very thing this parable forbids.

# Matthew 19: Marriage, the Young Man's Possessions, and the Twelve Thrones

Read the chapter → [Matthew 19](/bible/matthew/19)

**[Matthew 19:1-2](/bible/matthew/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/19#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, that when Jesus had finished these sayings, he departed from Galilee, and came into the coasts of Judea beyond Jordan;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/19#v2)</sup> And great multitudes followed him; and he healed them there.

- He departs from Galilee into the coasts of Judaea beyond Jordan, and great multitudes follow him and are healed.

**[Matthew 19:3-6](/bible/matthew/19#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/19#v3)</sup> The Pharisees also came unto him, tempting him, and saying unto him, Is it lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/19#v4)</sup> And he answered and said unto them, Have ye not read, that he which made them at the beginning made them male and female,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/19#v5)</sup> And said, For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife: and they twain shall be one flesh?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/19#v6)</sup> Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.

- The Pharisees ask, tempting him, whether it is lawful for a man to put away his wife for every cause.
- He answers from the beginning: he which made them at the beginning made them male and female, and they twain shall be one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.

**[Matthew 19:7-9](/bible/matthew/19#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/19#v7)</sup> They say unto him, Why did Moses then command to give a writing of divorcement, and to put her away?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/19#v8)</sup> He saith unto them, Moses because of the hardness of your hearts suffered you to put away your wives: but from the beginning it was not so.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/19#v9)</sup> And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery: and whoso marrieth her which is put away doth commit adultery.

- Moses suffered them to put away their wives because of the hardness of their hearts, but from the beginning it was not so.
- The trouble in that day was men finding any cause at all to put a woman away. Whosoever putteth away his wife, except it be for fornication, and marrieth another, committeth adultery, and puts her into sin as well.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 24:1](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v1)</sup> When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her: then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.

    The bill of divorcement Moses allowed, given into her hand when he sent her out of his house.

**[Matthew 19:10-12](/bible/matthew/19#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/19#v10)</sup> His disciples say unto him, If the case of the man be so with his wife, it is not good to marry.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/19#v11)</sup> But he said unto them, All men cannot receive this saying, save they to whom it is given.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/19#v12)</sup> For there are some eunuchs, which were so born from their mother’s womb: and there are some eunuchs, which were made eunuchs of men: and there be eunuchs, which have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven’s sake. He that is able to receive it, let him receive it.

- Some are born eunuchs from their mother's womb, some are made eunuchs of men, and some have made themselves eunuchs for the kingdom of heaven's sake. He that is able to receive it, let him receive it.

**[Matthew 19:13-15](/bible/matthew/19#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/19#v13)</sup> Then were there brought unto him little children, that he should put his hands on them, and pray: and the disciples rebuked them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/19#v14)</sup> But Jesus said, Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/19#v15)</sup> And he laid his hands on them, and departed thence.

- Suffer little children, and forbid them not, to come unto me: for of such is the kingdom of heaven.

**[Matthew 19:16-22](/bible/matthew/19#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/19#v16)</sup> And, behold, one came and said unto him, Good Master, what good thing shall I do, that I may have eternal life?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/19#v17)</sup> And he said unto him, Why callest thou me good? there is none good but one, that is, God: but if thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/19#v18)</sup> He saith unto him, Which? Jesus said, Thou shalt do no murder, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/19#v19)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother: and, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/19#v20)</sup> The young man saith unto him, All these things have I kept from my youth up: what lack I yet?
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/19#v21)</sup> Jesus said unto him, If thou wilt be perfect, go and sell that thou hast, and give to the poor, and thou shalt have treasure in heaven: and come and follow me.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/19#v22)</sup> But when the young man heard that saying, he went away sorrowful: for he had great possessions.

- Asked what good thing a man must do to have eternal life, Christ answers, If thou wilt enter into life, keep the commandments, and then names them.
- Told to sell what he had and give to the poor, the young man went away sorrowful, for he had great possessions.

**[Matthew 19:23-26](/bible/matthew/19#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/19#v23)</sup> Then said Jesus unto his disciples, Verily I say unto you, That a rich man shall hardly enter into the kingdom of heaven.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/19#v24)</sup> And again I say unto you, It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle, than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of God.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/19#v25)</sup> When his disciples heard it, they were exceedingly amazed, saying, Who then can be saved?
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/19#v26)</sup> But Jesus beheld them, and said unto them, With men this is impossible; but with God all things are possible.

- It is easier for a camel to go through the eye of a needle than for a rich man to enter into the kingdom of heaven.
- Money buys nothing here. What is paid week by week to a preacher washes away no sin. The way in is the keeping of the commandments, as verse 17 already said.
- With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible.

**[Matthew 19:27-30](/bible/matthew/19#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/19#v27)</sup> Then answered Peter and said unto him, Behold, we have forsaken all, and followed thee; what shall we have therefore?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/19#v28)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, Verily I say unto you, That ye which have followed me, in the regeneration when the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his glory, ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/19#v29)</sup> And every one that hath forsaken houses, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my name’s sake, shall receive an hundredfold, and shall inherit everlasting life.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/19#v30)</sup> But many that are first shall be last; and the last shall be first.

- Ye which have followed me shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.
- Every one that hath forsaken houses, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my name's sake, shall receive an hundredfold, and shall inherit everlasting life.
- What is lost coming into this truth is repaid in brethren and sisters and fathers and mothers, and the last part of the verse is the greater part: everlasting life.

# Matthew 20: The Labourers in the Vineyard and the Cup He Drank

Read the chapter → [Matthew 20](/bible/matthew/20)

**[Matthew 20:1-16](/bible/matthew/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/20#v1)</sup> For the kingdom of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/20#v2)</sup> And when he had agreed with the labourers for a penny a day, he sent them into his vineyard.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/20#v3)</sup> And he went out about the third hour, and saw others standing idle in the marketplace,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/20#v4)</sup> And said unto them; Go ye also into the vineyard, and whatsoever is right I will give you. And they went their way.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/20#v5)</sup> Again he went out about the sixth and ninth hour, and did likewise.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/20#v6)</sup> And about the eleventh hour he went out, and found others standing idle, and saith unto them, Why stand ye here all the day idle?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/20#v7)</sup> They say unto him, Because no man hath hired us. He saith unto them, Go ye also into the vineyard; and whatsoever is right, that shall ye receive.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/20#v8)</sup> So when even was come, the lord of the vineyard saith unto his steward, Call the labourers, and give them their hire, beginning from the last unto the first.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/20#v9)</sup> And when they came that were hired about the eleventh hour, they received every man a penny.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/20#v10)</sup> But when the first came, they supposed that they should have received more; and they likewise received every man a penny.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/20#v11)</sup> And when they had received it, they murmured against the goodman of the house,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/20#v12)</sup> Saying, These last have wrought but one hour, and thou hast made them equal unto us, which have borne the burden and heat of the day.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/20#v13)</sup> But he answered one of them, and said, Friend, I do thee no wrong: didst not thou agree with me for a penny?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/20#v14)</sup> Take that thine is, and go thy way: I will give unto this last, even as unto thee.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/20#v15)</sup> Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own? Is thine eye evil, because I am good?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/20#v16)</sup> So the last shall be first, and the first last: for many be called, but few chosen.

- Labourers hired at the third, sixth, ninth and eleventh hours all receive a penny, and those hired first murmur at it.
- The householder answers that he does them no wrong; they agreed with him for a penny. Is it not lawful for me to do what I will with mine own?
- The wage here is the kingdom. A man labouring thirty years and a man who came in yesterday are working for the same thing, and each was called at the hour appointed for him.

**[Matthew 20:17-19](/bible/matthew/20#v17)**

- Ye know not what ye ask. They shall drink of his cup and be baptized with his baptism, but the seats are not his to give; they are for them for whom it is prepared of his Father.

- The Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many. Men set over the congregation are servants to it, not lords over it.

# Matthew 21: The King Cometh Meek, and the Kingdom Taken Away

Read the chapter → [Matthew 21](/bible/matthew/21)

**[Matthew 21:1-11](/bible/matthew/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/21#v1)</sup> And when they drew nigh unto Jerusalem, and were come to Bethphage, unto the mount of Olives, then sent Jesus two disciples,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/21#v2)</sup> Saying unto them, Go into the village over against you, and straightway ye shall find an ass tied, and a colt with her: loose them, and bring them unto me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/21#v3)</sup> And if any man say ought unto you, ye shall say, The Lord hath need of them; and straightway he will send them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/21#v4)</sup> All this was done, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, saying,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/21#v5)</sup> Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass, and a colt the foal of an ass.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/21#v6)</sup> And the disciples went, and did as Jesus commanded them,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/21#v7)</sup> And brought the ass, and the colt, and put on them their clothes, and they set him thereon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/21#v8)</sup> And a very great multitude spread their garments in the way; others cut down branches from the trees, and strawed them in the way.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/21#v9)</sup> And the multitudes that went before, and that followed, cried, saying, Hosanna to the Son of David: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord; Hosanna in the highest.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/21#v10)</sup> And when he was come into Jerusalem, all the city was moved, saying, Who is this?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/21#v11)</sup> And the multitude said, This is Jesus the prophet of Nazareth of Galilee.

- He rides into Jerusalem on an ass and a colt, and the multitudes spread their garments and branches in the way, crying Hosanna to the son of David.
- All this was done that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet.

  Precepts:
  - **[Zechariah 9:9](/bible/zechariah/9#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/9#v9)</sup> Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.

    Tell ye the daughter of Sion, Behold, thy King cometh unto thee, meek, and sitting upon an ass.

**[Matthew 21:12-14](/bible/matthew/21#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/21#v12)</sup> And Jesus went into the temple of God, and cast out all them that sold and bought in the temple, and overthrew the tables of the moneychangers, and the seats of them that sold doves,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/21#v13)</sup> And said unto them, It is written, My house shall be called the house of prayer; but ye have made it a den of thieves.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/21#v14)</sup> And the blind and the lame came to him in the temple; and he healed them.

- He casts out them that sold and bought in the temple and overthrows the tables of the moneychangers, saying, It is written, My house shall be called the house of prayer; but ye have made it a den of thieves.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 56:7](/bible/isaiah/56#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/56#v7)</sup> Even them will I bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer: their burnt offerings and their sacrifices shall be accepted upon mine altar; for mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people.

    Mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people.

**[Matthew 21:15-16](/bible/matthew/21#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/21#v15)</sup> And when the chief priests and scribes saw the wonderful things that he did, and the children crying in the temple, and saying, Hosanna to the Son of David; they were sore displeased,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/21#v16)</sup> And said unto him, Hearest thou what these say? And Jesus saith unto them, Yea; have ye never read, Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings thou hast perfected praise?

- The chief priests and scribes saw the wonderful things that he did and were sore displeased. The works themselves angered them.

**[Matthew 21:17-22](/bible/matthew/21#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/21#v17)</sup> And he left them, and went out of the city into Bethany; and he lodged there.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/21#v18)</sup> Now in the morning as he returned into the city, he hungered.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/21#v19)</sup> And when he saw a fig tree in the way, he came to it, and found nothing thereon, but leaves only, and said unto it, Let no fruit grow on thee henceforward for ever. And presently the fig tree withered away.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/21#v20)</sup> And when the disciples saw it, they marvelled, saying, How soon is the fig tree withered away!
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/21#v21)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto them, Verily I say unto you, If ye have faith, and doubt not, ye shall not only do this which is done to the fig tree, but also if ye shall say unto this mountain, Be thou removed, and be thou cast into the sea; it shall be done.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/21#v22)</sup> And all things, whatsoever ye shall ask in prayer, believing, ye shall receive.

- The fig tree with leaves and no fruit withers away at his word, and the disciples marvel at how soon it withered.
- If ye have faith, and doubt not, ye shall not only do this which is done to the fig tree, but also shall say unto this mountain, Be thou removed, and it shall be done.

**[Matthew 21:23-27](/bible/matthew/21#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/21#v23)</sup> And when he was come into the temple, the chief priests and the elders of the people came unto him as he was teaching, and said, By what authority doest thou these things? and who gave thee this authority?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/21#v24)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, I also will ask you one thing, which if ye tell me, I in like wise will tell you by what authority I do these things.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/21#v25)</sup> The baptism of John, whence was it? from heaven, or of men? And they reasoned with themselves, saying, If we shall say, From heaven; he will say unto us, Why did ye not then believe him?
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/21#v26)</sup> But if we shall say, Of men; we fear the people; for all hold John as a prophet.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/21#v27)</sup> And they answered Jesus, and said, We cannot tell. And he said unto them, Neither tell I you by what authority I do these things.

- Asked by what authority he does these things, he asks them first whether the baptism of John was from heaven or of men.
- They reason among themselves and will not answer, because either answer condemns them, so he does not answer them either.

**[Matthew 21:28-32](/bible/matthew/21#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/21#v28)</sup> But what think ye? A certain man had two sons; and he came to the first, and said, Son, go work to day in my vineyard.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/21#v29)</sup> He answered and said, I will not: but afterward he repented, and went.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/21#v30)</sup> And he came to the second, and said likewise. And he answered and said, I go, sir: and went not.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/21#v31)</sup> Whether of them twain did the will of his father? They say unto him, The first. Jesus saith unto them, Verily I say unto you, That the publicans and the harlots go into the kingdom of God before you.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/21#v32)</sup> For John came unto you in the way of righteousness, and ye believed him not: but the publicans and the harlots believed him: and ye, when ye had seen it, repented not afterward, that ye might believe him.

- Of the two sons, the one who said I will not and afterward repented and went is the one that did the will of his father.
- The publicans and the harlots go into the kingdom of God before you. These were teachers of the law who were not doers of it.
- John came in the way of righteousness and they believed him not, and when they had seen it they repented not afterward that they might believe him.

**[Matthew 21:33-46](/bible/matthew/21#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/21#v33)</sup> Hear another parable: There was a certain householder, which planted a vineyard, and hedged it round about, and digged a winepress in it, and built a tower, and let it out to husbandmen, and went into a far country:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/21#v34)</sup> And when the time of the fruit drew near, he sent his servants to the husbandmen, that they might receive the fruits of it.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/21#v35)</sup> And the husbandmen took his servants, and beat one, and killed another, and stoned another.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/21#v36)</sup> Again, he sent other servants more than the first: and they did unto them likewise.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/21#v37)</sup> But last of all he sent unto them his son, saying, They will reverence my son.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/21#v38)</sup> But when the husbandmen saw the son, they said among themselves, This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and let us seize on his inheritance.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/21#v39)</sup> And they caught him, and cast him out of the vineyard, and slew him.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/21#v40)</sup> When the lord therefore of the vineyard cometh, what will he do unto those husbandmen?
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/21#v41)</sup> They say unto him, He will miserably destroy those wicked men, and will let out his vineyard unto other husbandmen, which shall render him the fruits in their seasons.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/21#v42)</sup> Jesus saith unto them, Did ye never read in the scriptures, The stone which the builders rejected, the same is become the head of the corner: this is the Lord’s doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes?
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/21#v43)</sup> Therefore say I unto you, The kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/21#v44)</sup> And whosoever shall fall on this stone shall be broken: but on whomsoever it shall fall, it will grind him to powder.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/21#v45)</sup> And when the chief priests and Pharisees had heard his parables, they perceived that he spake of them.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/21#v46)</sup> But when they sought to lay hands on him, they feared the multitude, because they took him for a prophet.

- The householder sends servants for the fruits of his vineyard and they are beaten, killed and stoned; last of all he sends his son, and the husbandmen kill him to seize the inheritance.
- The stone which the builders rejected is become the head of the corner. This is the Lord's doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes.
- Therefore the kingdom of God shall be taken from you, and given to a nation bringing forth the fruits thereof. The chief priests and Pharisees perceived that he spake of them.
