## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Matthew 9 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 10-13, 36-38, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Matthew 9:10-11](/bible/matthew/9#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/9#v10)</sup> And it came to pass, as Jesus sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/9#v11)</sup> And when the Pharisees saw it, they said unto his disciples, Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?

- “And it came to pass, as Jesus sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him and his disciples.” The Pharisees asked, “Why eateth your Master with publicans and sinners?” Why is Christ sitting with ungodly people? He sat and communed with them to bring them to repentance. That was the whole purpose.
- That is why he said he did not come for the righteous but for the sinners. So if you think you are right, he is not here for you. The first step of changing is to acknowledge that we have issues.
- Someone said, sounds like you’re a product of your environment, you need to get the hell out of your environment. Let’s see if Christ agrees with that statement. “And it came to pass, as Jesus sat at meat in the house, behold, many publicans and sinners came and sat down with him”. What type of people was Christ around? Publicans and sinners. So yes, I am a product of my environment: wherever the sinners are and the publicans that were hated, that is my environment, and Christ was not trying to get away from that.
- Many Christian pastors remove themselves from that environment. Men of God, soldiers, warriors that lay down their life for their people as Christ did, surround themselves with people that are hated and sinners.

**[Matthew 9:12](/bible/matthew/9#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/9#v12)</sup> But when Jesus heard that, he said unto them, They that be whole need not a physician, but they that are sick.

- “But when Jesus heard”: Jesus heard them talking behind his back. He “said unto them”, they that be whole “need not a physician”. That’s why T.D. Jakes and Creflo Dollar surround themselves with rich people with nice white picket fences and six-figure jobs; they’re not letting those sinners come around them. “But they that are sick”: the people that are sick is where you’re going to find me. I’m not looking for a place of comfort.

**[Matthew 9:13](/bible/matthew/9#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/9#v13)</sup> But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.

- All of us come into this truth sick, with sins. Don't come to this truth with rose colored glasses on thinking everything is going to be perfect. We are going to see the same issues and the same situations in here that we saw in the world. The only difference is that now we have to apply the scriptures to remedy these things. The scriptures can cure us. This book has zero power until you apply what it says: until then it is just a page with ink on it. The word comes alive when you manifest it in your life and allow it to guide you and move you, and then you see the power of God. It is somewhat of a mental disorder to come into this truth and never evolve, never grow, never measure up.
- There is a foul spirit of fornication in the congregation. Fornication will always be around till Christ returns, but that does not negate that we have to change, men and women both: there are hoes out there too, and whoremongers, and it has to change. If you don't want the kingdom, take off the purple and the fringe and go back to the club and twerk. At the end you are not going to get it anyway.
- “I will have mercy, and not sacrifice.” What we used to do was always give up sacrifices to absolve us of the sins we committed. That's not how it works. Sacrifice was given to us because we sinned against God, but that is not how it was supposed to be from the beginning. We kept on choosing sin. He is saying, I want to see you change rather than continue sacrificing.
- “For I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.” Are there any that are righteous? No. So the ones he did not come to call are those who believe they are righteous, who think they are holy. Many people are right in their own eyes. We don't want to see ourselves in a negative light; we want to see the best side of us, and act like the parts of us that are not right are going to be all right with God. If we continue in that mindset of not changing, not becoming better, not becoming more disciplined, we will fall short of the kingdom.

  Precepts:
  - **[James 5:16](/bible/james/5#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/james/5#v16)</sup> Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

    “Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed.” You have to confess your faults so that we can pray, and you can be healed. There is no healing without confession.
  - **[Psalms 107:20](/bible/psalms/107#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/107#v20)</sup> He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.

    So what do we use to heal ourselves? The word of God. “He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.” Write that down: he sent his word down to heal them.
  - **[2 Esdras 7:69-70](/bible/2-esdras/7#v69)**
    > <sup>[69](/bible/2-esdras/7#v69)</sup> And being judge, if he should not forgive them that are cured with his word, and put out the multitude of contentions,
    >
    > <sup>[70](/bible/2-esdras/7#v70)</sup> There should be very few left peradventure in an innumerable multitude.

    The word of God can cure. Ezra speaks of “them that are cured with his word”, and if the Most High did not forgive, “There should be very few left peradventure in an innumerable multitude.”

**[Matthew 9:36-38](/bible/matthew/9#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/9#v36)</sup> But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/9#v37)</sup> Then saith he unto his disciples, The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/9#v38)</sup> Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.

- “But when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them, because they fainted, and were scattered abroad, as sheep having no shepherd.” “The harvest truly is plenteous, but the labourers are few; Pray ye therefore the Lord of the harvest, that he will send forth labourers into his harvest.” What kind of laborers is he looking to send into his harvest? Laborers moved with compassion, as he was: laborers who care about their people and want to fill that breach, because our people out there are sick.
- The people are coming in, and the leaders must be the example to them. Love your people. Do right by them. Be that proper example, or else you will hit a ceiling, and Sacramento won't grow, this nation won't grow. If you want to be those men, you have to walk the walk and live it. Show what the power of God can do.
- We have to have the mind and spirit of Christ, and here is what moved him: “when he saw the multitudes, he was moved with compassion on them.” Verse 35 says he was teaching in their synagogues, so these are Israelites. It is not compassion on the whole world, but on his people, because he will save his people from their sins, which is us.
- “Because they fainted.” Think about how they fainted: alcoholism, drugs, whoremongers, fornicators, men not taking care of their families, lost in Christianity, lost in politics. When you are not living your true heritage you have fainted, because the real you is not alive, not conscious. Satan is in the driver’s seat.

  Precepts:
  - **[Zephaniah 2:1-2](/bible/zephaniah/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/zephaniah/2#v1)</sup> Gather yourselves together, yea, gather together, O nation not desired;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/zephaniah/2#v2)</sup> Before the decree bring forth, before the day pass as the chaff, before the fierce anger of the Lord come upon you, before the day of the Lord’s anger come upon you.

    Harvest literally means to gather the crops. “Gather yourselves together, yea, gather together, O nation not desired.” The nation not desired goes into Christ’s compassion on those that fainted and had no shepherd, stricken with misfortune.
