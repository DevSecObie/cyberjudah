## Introduction

This session covers Matthew 2 through 5. The class was taken on short notice and moves quickly, but the history in these chapters is heavy: the birth of Christ under an Edomite king, the murder of the children in Bethlehem, the ministry of John the Baptist, the temptation in the wilderness, and the opening of the sermon on the mount where Christ magnifies the law rather than destroying it. The thread running through all four chapters is fulfilment. Everything that happens to Christ here was written of him beforehand in the law, the prophets, and the psalms.

**[Matthew 2:1-2](/bible/matthew/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/2#v1)</sup> Now when Jesus was born in Bethlehem of Judea in the days of Herod the king, behold, there came wise men from the east to Jerusalem,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/2#v2)</sup> Saying, Where is he that is born King of the Jews? for we have seen his star in the east, and are come to worship him.

- Wise men come from the east to Jerusalem to worship him. This is prophetic; it was written that kings from far off would come and bow before him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 72:10-11](/bible/psalms/72#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/72#v10)</sup> The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents: the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/72#v11)</sup> Yea, all kings shall fall down before him: all nations shall serve him.

    The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents, and all kings shall fall down before him.

**[Matthew 2:3](/bible/matthew/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/2#v3)</sup> When Herod the king had heard these things, he was troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.

- Herod was set up as king over Judea by Rome, and he was an Edomite, not of Israel. When the messiah is born he is troubled, and all Jerusalem with him.
- If the messiah were coming to save all nations, there would be nothing in his birth to trouble a ruler of the nations. Herod is troubled because the coming of the messiah to Israel means judgment upon those who hold Israel down.

**[Matthew 2:4-6](/bible/matthew/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/2#v4)</sup> And when he had gathered all the chief priests and scribes of the people together, he demanded of them where Christ should be born.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/2#v5)</sup> And they said unto him, In Bethlehem of Judea: for thus it is written by the prophet,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/2#v6)</sup> And thou Bethlehem, in the land of Juda, art not the least among the princes of Juda: for out of thee shall come a Governor, that shall rule my people Israel.

- The chief priests and the scribes answer at once: Bethlehem of Judea, for so it is written by the prophet. They knew the scriptures concerning the messiah.
- The same men who could quote where he would be born were blinded when he walked among them. Knowing the book is not the same as seeing the one the book speaks of.

  Precepts:
  - **[Micah 5:2](/bible/micah/5#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/micah/5#v2)</sup> But thou, Beth–lehem Ephratah, though thou be little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of thee shall he come forth unto me that is to be ruler in Israel; whose goings forth have been from of old, from everlasting.

    The prophecy the priests were quoting: out of Bethlehem Ephratah shall he come forth that is to be ruler in Israel.
  - **[Romans 11:7-10](/bible/romans/11#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/romans/11#v7)</sup> What then? Israel hath not obtained that which he seeketh for; but the election hath obtained it, and the rest were blinded
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/romans/11#v8)</sup> (According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/romans/11#v9)</sup> And David saith, Let their table be made a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/romans/11#v10)</sup> Let their eyes be darkened, that they may not see, and bow down their back alway.

    Israel obtained not that which he seeketh for, and the rest were blinded, and their table was made a snare and a trap unto them.
  - **[Isaiah 30:8](/bible/isaiah/30#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/30#v8)</sup> Now go, write it before them in a table, and note it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever:

    The table David spoke of is the writing itself, noted in a book for the time to come.

**[Matthew 2:7-8](/bible/matthew/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/2#v7)</sup> Then Herod, when he had privily called the wise men, enquired of them diligently what time the star appeared.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/2#v8)</sup> And he sent them to Bethlehem, and said, Go and search diligently for the young child; and when ye have found him, bring me word again, that I may come and worship him also.

- Herod calls the wise men privily and tells them to bring him word that he may come and worship also. He had no worship in mind; the next verses show what he intended.

**[Matthew 2:9-11](/bible/matthew/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/2#v9)</sup> When they had heard the king, they departed; and, lo, the star, which they saw in the east, went before them, till it came and stood over where the young child was.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/2#v10)</sup> When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceeding great joy.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/2#v11)</sup> And when they were come into the house, they saw the young child with Mary his mother, and fell down, and worshipped him: and when they had opened their treasures, they presented unto him gifts; gold, and frankincense, and myrrh.

- The star goes before them and stands over the place where the young child was, and they fall down and worship him and open their treasures.
- Gold, frankincense and myrrh are the presents and gifts David prophesied of in the psalm. What was written was fulfilled exactly.

**[Matthew 2:12](/bible/matthew/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/2#v12)</sup> And being warned of God in a dream that they should not return to Herod, they departed into their own country another way.

- Warned of the Most High in a dream, they go home another way and do not report back to Herod.

**[Matthew 2:13](/bible/matthew/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/2#v13)</sup> And when they were departed, behold, the angel of the Lord appeareth to Joseph in a dream, saying, Arise, and take the young child and his mother, and flee into Egypt, and be thou there until I bring thee word: for Herod will seek the young child to destroy him.

- The angel tells Joseph to take the young child and his mother and flee into Egypt, because Herod will seek the young child to destroy him.
- They hid in Egypt among the Egyptians. A family that did not look like the people of that land would have been no more hidden there than anywhere else.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 12:2-4](/bible/revelation/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/12#v2)</sup> And she being with child cried, travailing in birth, and pained to be delivered.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/12#v3)</sup> And there appeared another wonder in heaven; and behold a great red dragon, having seven heads and ten horns, and seven crowns upon his heads.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/12#v4)</sup> And his tail drew the third part of the stars of heaven, and did cast them to the earth: and the dragon stood before the woman which was ready to be delivered, for to devour her child as soon as it was born.

    The great red dragon stood before the woman to devour her child as soon as it was born, which is what Herod set out to do.
  - **[Revelation 12:9](/bible/revelation/12#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/12#v9)</sup> And the great dragon was cast out, that old serpent, called the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world: he was cast out into the earth, and his angels were cast out with him.

    That same dragon is called that old serpent, the Devil, and Satan, which deceiveth the whole world.
  - **[Genesis 25:25](/bible/genesis/25#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/25#v25)</sup> And the first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name Esau.

    Esau came out red, which is the colour the dragon is given in the vision.
  - **[Job 30:29](/bible/job/30#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/job/30#v29)</sup> I am a brother to dragons, and a companion to owls.

    Job calls himself a brother to dragons, showing that dragons in prophecy stand for men.

**[Matthew 2:14-15](/bible/matthew/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/2#v14)</sup> When he arose, he took the young child and his mother by night, and departed into Egypt:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/2#v15)</sup> And was there until the death of Herod: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken of the Lord by the prophet, saying, Out of Egypt have I called my son.

- They remain in Egypt until the death of Herod, that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophet, Out of Egypt have I called my son.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 11:1](/bible/hosea/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/11#v1)</sup> When Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.

    The prophecy quoted: when Israel was a child, then I loved him, and called my son out of Egypt.

**[Matthew 2:16](/bible/matthew/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/2#v16)</sup> Then Herod, when he saw that he was mocked of the wise men, was exceeding wroth, and sent forth, and slew all the children that were in Bethlehem, and in all the coasts thereof, from two years old and under, according to the time which he had diligently enquired of the wise men.

- Herod slays all the children in Bethlehem from two years old and under. This is the same pattern as Egypt, where the ruler killed the young children to stop a deliverer from rising.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 1:22](/bible/exodus/1#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/1#v22)</sup> And Pharaoh charged all his people, saying, Every son that is born ye shall cast into the river, and every daughter ye shall save alive.

    Pharaoh charged all his people to cast every son into the river, the same attempt to cut off a deliverer before he could grow.

**[Matthew 2:17-18](/bible/matthew/2#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/2#v17)</sup> Then was fulfilled that which was spoken by Jeremy the prophet, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/2#v18)</sup> In Rama was there a voice heard, lamentation, and weeping, and great mourning, Rachel weeping for her children, and would not be comforted, because they are not.

- The mourning in Ramah was written of beforehand by Jeremiah.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 31:15](/bible/jeremiah/31#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/31#v15)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; A voice was heard in Ramah, lamentation, and bitter weeping; Rahel weeping for her children refused to be comforted for her children, because they were not.

    Rachel weeping for her children, and would not be comforted, because they were not.

**[Matthew 2:19-23](/bible/matthew/2#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/2#v19)</sup> But when Herod was dead, behold, an angel of the Lord appeareth in a dream to Joseph in Egypt,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/2#v20)</sup> Saying, Arise, and take the young child and his mother, and go into the land of Israel: for they are dead which sought the young child’s life.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/2#v21)</sup> And he arose, and took the young child and his mother, and came into the land of Israel.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/2#v22)</sup> But when he heard that Archelaus did reign in Judea in the room of his father Herod, he was afraid to go thither: notwithstanding, being warned of God in a dream, he turned aside into the parts of Galilee:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/2#v23)</sup> And he came and dwelt in a city called Nazareth: that it might be fulfilled which was spoken by the prophets, He shall be called a Nazarene.

- After Herod dies the family returns, but Archelaus reigns in Judea, so being warned of the Most High Joseph turns aside into Galilee and dwells in Nazareth.
- Nazareth had no reputation. That the messiah was raised there was itself a fulfilment of what the prophets spoke.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 1:45-46](/bible/john/1#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/john/1#v45)</sup> Philip findeth Nathanael, and saith unto him, We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.
    >
    > <sup>[46](/bible/john/1#v46)</sup> And Nathanael said unto him, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth? Philip saith unto him, Come and see.

    Nathanael asks, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth, which shows how the place was regarded.

# Matthew 3: John the Baptist and the Baptism of the Holy Ghost and Fire

Read the chapter → [Matthew 3](/bible/matthew/3)

**[Matthew 3:1-2](/bible/matthew/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/3#v1)</sup> In those days came John the Baptist, preaching in the wilderness of Judea,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/3#v2)</sup> And saying, Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.

- John the Baptist preaches in the wilderness of Judea, saying repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand. He is the messenger the prophets said would go before the Lord, coming in the spirit of Elijah.

  Precepts:
  - **[Malachi 3:1](/bible/malachi/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/3#v1)</sup> Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me: and the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple, even the messenger of the covenant, whom ye delight in: behold, he shall come, saith the Lord of hosts.

    Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me.
  - **[Malachi 4:5-6](/bible/malachi/4#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/4#v5)</sup> Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/4#v6)</sup> And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.

    I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord.

**[Matthew 3:3](/bible/matthew/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/3#v3)</sup> For this is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.

- The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, is Isaiah's word spoken of John.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 40:3-5](/bible/isaiah/40#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/40#v3)</sup> The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight in the desert a highway for our God.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/40#v4)</sup> Every valley shall be exalted, and every mountain and hill shall be made low: and the crooked shall be made straight, and the rough places plain:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/40#v5)</sup> And the glory of the Lord shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see it together: for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.

    The prophecy John fulfilled, word for word.

**[Matthew 3:4](/bible/matthew/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/3#v4)</sup> And the same John had his raiment of camel’s hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey.

- His raiment of camel's hair and the leathern girdle about his loins are not incidental. That was Elijah's manner, and it marks John as coming in the same spirit.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Kings 1:8](/bible/2-kings/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/1#v8)</sup> And they answered him, He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins. And he said, It is Elijah the Tishbite.

    He was an hairy man, and girt with a girdle of leather about his loins; and he said, It is Elijah the Tishbite.

**[Matthew 3:5-6](/bible/matthew/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/3#v5)</sup> Then went out to him Jerusalem, and all Judea, and all the region round about Jordan,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/3#v6)</sup> And were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins.

- They were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins. Confession of sin is part of what is happening here, not a bare dipping in water.

**[Matthew 3:7-8](/bible/matthew/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/3#v7)</sup> But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism, he said unto them, O generation of vipers, who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/3#v8)</sup> Bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance:

- To the Pharisees and Sadducees coming to his baptism John says, O generation of vipers, bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance.
- The fruits meet for repentance are works, not offerings brought to cover sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 26:20](/bible/acts/26#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/acts/26#v20)</sup> But shewed first unto them of Damascus, and at Jerusalem, and throughout all the coasts of Judea, and then to the Gentiles, that they should repent and turn to God, and do works meet for repentance.

    That they should repent and turn to God, and do works meet for repentance.

**[Matthew 3:9](/bible/matthew/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/3#v9)</sup> And think not to say within yourselves, We have Abraham to our father: for I say unto you, that God is able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham.

- Do not say within yourselves, We have Abraham to our father. The Most High is able of stones to raise up children unto Abraham.
- Descent from Abraham without the works of Abraham counts for nothing. The works of Abraham are believing the Most High and keeping his commandments.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 8:37](/bible/john/8#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/john/8#v37)</sup> I know that ye are Abraham’s seed; but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you.

    I know that ye are Abraham's seed; but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you.
  - **[John 8:39](/bible/john/8#v39)**
    > <sup>[39](/bible/john/8#v39)</sup> They answered and said unto him, Abraham is our father. Jesus saith unto them, If ye were Abraham’s children, ye would do the works of Abraham.

    If ye were Abraham's children, ye would do the works of Abraham.
  - **[Genesis 26:5](/bible/genesis/26#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/26#v5)</sup> Because that Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.

    Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws; that is the work of Abraham.

**[Matthew 3:10](/bible/matthew/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/3#v10)</sup> And now also the axe is laid unto the root of the trees: therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.

- Every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down and cast into the fire. Christ says the same thing again later in this gospel.

**[Matthew 3:11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)</sup> I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:

- John says plainly that his water baptism is not the baptism of the one coming after him. That one shall baptize with the Holy Ghost and with fire.
- The Holy Ghost is bound to the law. To resist the Holy Ghost is to resist the commandments, and the Comforter brings the words of Christ to remembrance.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 4:1-2](/bible/john/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/john/4#v1)</sup> When therefore the Lord knew how the Pharisees had heard that Jesus made and baptized more disciples than John,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/john/4#v2)</sup> (Though Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples,)

    Jesus himself baptized not, but his disciples.
  - **[John 1:40](/bible/john/1#v40)**
    > <sup>[40](/bible/john/1#v40)</sup> One of the two which heard John speak, and followed him, was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.

    Andrew, Simon Peter's brother, was first a disciple of John, which is how water baptism carried on among some of the disciples.
  - **[Acts 7:51](/bible/acts/7#v51)**
    > <sup>[51](/bible/acts/7#v51)</sup> Ye stiffnecked and uncircumcised in heart and ears, ye do always resist the Holy Ghost: as your fathers did, so do ye.

    Ye do always resist the Holy Ghost: as your fathers did, so do ye.
  - **[Acts 7:53](/bible/acts/7#v53)**
    > <sup>[53](/bible/acts/7#v53)</sup> Who have received the law by the disposition of angels, and have not kept it.

    What the fathers resisted was the law, received by the disposition of angels and not kept.
  - **[John 14:15](/bible/john/14#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.

    If ye love me, keep my commandments, and the Comforter follows.
  - **[John 14:26](/bible/john/14#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/john/14#v26)</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

    The Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, shall teach you all things and bring all things to your remembrance.
  - **[John 6:63](/bible/john/6#v63)**
    > <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

    The words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

**[Matthew 3:12](/bible/matthew/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/3#v12)</sup> Whose fan is in his hand, and he will throughly purge his floor, and gather his wheat into the garner; but he will burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire.

- He will gather his wheat into the garner and burn up the chaff with unquenchable fire. There are two baptisms in view, and everyone receives one of them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 1:4](/bible/psalms/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/1#v4)</sup> The ungodly are not so: but are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

    The ungodly are like the chaff which the wind driveth away.

**[Matthew 3:13-15](/bible/matthew/3#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/3#v13)</sup> Then cometh Jesus from Galilee to Jordan unto John, to be baptized of him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/3#v14)</sup> But John forbad him, saying, I have need to be baptized of thee, and comest thou to me?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/3#v15)</sup> And Jesus answering said unto him, Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness. Then he suffered him.

- Christ comes to John to be baptized, and when John forbids him he answers, Suffer it to be so now: for thus it becometh us to fulfil all righteousness.
- This had to be done before the ministry begins, because the messenger preparing the way was written in the prophets and had to be fulfilled.

**[Matthew 3:16-17](/bible/matthew/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/3#v16)</sup> And Jesus, when he was baptized, went up straightway out of the water: and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/3#v17)</sup> And lo a voice from heaven, saying, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.

- Christ comes up out of the water, the Spirit of the Most High descends like a dove, and a voice out of the heavens says, This is my beloved son, in whom I am well pleased.
- The one in the water and the voice out of heaven are not the same. There is the Father, and there is his son.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Thessalonians 2:11-12](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v11)</sup> And for this cause God shall send them strong delusion, that they should believe a lie:
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v12)</sup> That they all might be damned who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.

    The Most High shall send them strong delusion, that they should believe a lie.

# Matthew 4: The Temptation in the Wilderness and the Calling of the Fishers

Read the chapter → [Matthew 4](/bible/matthew/4)

**[Matthew 4:1-3](/bible/matthew/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/4#v1)</sup> Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/4#v2)</sup> And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/4#v3)</sup> And when the tempter came to him, he said, If thou be the Son of God, command that these stones be made bread.

- Christ is led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted, and after forty days and forty nights of fasting he is an hungred.
- The tempter comes at the weakest point and goes at the flesh first: command that these stones be made bread.
- This is the same set of temptations the man and the woman met in the garden, and they run through everything the enemy uses.

  Precepts:

**[Matthew 5:43-44](/bible/matthew/5#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/5#v43)</sup> Ye have heard that it hath been said, Thou shalt love thy neighbour, and hate thine enemy.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/5#v44)</sup> But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you;

- Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you.
- The enemy in view here is an enemy within your own people. Saul and David were both Israelites, and Saul was David's enemy.
- The reason for the command is that the enemy may yet repent, as one who persecuted the disciples afterward became one of them.

**[Matthew 5:45-47](/bible/matthew/5#v45)**

> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/5#v45)</sup> That ye may be the children of your Father which is in heaven: for he maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust.

- He maketh his sun to rise on the evil and on the good, and sendeth rain on the just and on the unjust. Loving only those who love you is no more than the publicans do.

**[Matthew 5:48](/bible/matthew/5#v48)**

> <sup>[48](/bible/matthew/5#v48)</sup> Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect.

- Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect. Perfection here is a heart perfect with the Most High, walking in his statutes and keeping his commandments.
- Everything from verse 38 down is charity, and charity is the bond of perfectness.
