**[Zechariah 10:1-2](/bible/zechariah/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/10#v1)</sup> Ask ye of the Lord rain in the time of the latter rain; so the Lord shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/10#v2)</sup> For the idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd.

- Ask ye of the Lord rain in the time of the latter rain; so the Lord shall make bright clouds, and give them showers of rain, to every one grass in the field.
- The idols have spoken vanity, and the diviners have seen a lie, and have told false dreams; they comfort in vain: therefore they went their way as a flock, they were troubled, because there was no shepherd.
- That is the condition of Israel as a nation right now. There is no shepherd, because the men our people look to for leadership are not shepherds according to the word.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 56:10](/bible/isaiah/56#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/56#v10)</sup> His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark; sleeping, lying down, loving to slumber.

    His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark. A watchman is set to keep you out of harm's way, and one that cannot bark warns no one.
  - **[Isaiah 56:11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11)</sup> Yea, they are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain, from his quarter.

    They are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain. They teach that the law no longer has to be kept and still ask for the tithe.

**[Zechariah 10:5](/bible/zechariah/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/10#v3)</sup> Mine anger was kindled against the shepherds, and I punished the goats: for the Lord of hosts hath visited his flock the house of Judah, and hath made them as his goodly horse in the battle.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/10#v4)</sup> Out of him came forth the corner, out of him the nail, out of him the battle bow, out of him every oppressor together.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/10#v5)</sup> And they shall be as mighty men, which tread down their enemies in the mire of the streets in the battle: and they shall fight, because the Lord is with them, and the riders on horses shall be confounded.

- Mine anger was kindled against the shepherds, and I punished the goats: for the Lord of hosts hath visited his flock the house of Judah, and hath made them as his goodly horse in the battle.
- Out of him came forth the corner, out of him the nail, out of him the battle bow, out of him every oppressor together.
- They shall be as mighty men, which tread down their enemies in the mire of the streets in the battle: and they shall fight, because the Lord is with them. That is the benefit of keeping the commandments; he fights the battle, for vengeance is his.

**[Zechariah 10:12](/bible/zechariah/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/10#v6)</sup> And I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will bring them again to place them; for I have mercy upon them: and they shall be as though I had not cast them off: for I am the Lord their God, and will hear them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/10#v7)</sup> And they of Ephraim shall be like a mighty man, and their heart shall rejoice as through wine: yea, their children shall see it, and be glad; their heart shall rejoice in the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/10#v8)</sup> I will hiss for them, and gather them; for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/10#v9)</sup> And I will sow them among the people: and they shall remember me in far countries; and they shall live with their children, and turn again.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/10#v10)</sup> I will bring them again also out of the land of Egypt, and gather them out of Assyria; and I will bring them into the land of Gilead and Lebanon; and place shall not be found for them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/zechariah/10#v11)</sup> And he shall pass through the sea with affliction, and shall smite the waves in the sea, and all the deeps of the river shall dry up: and the pride of Assyria shall be brought down, and the sceptre of Egypt shall depart away.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/10#v12)</sup> And I will strengthen them in the Lord; and they shall walk up and down in his name, saith the Lord.

- I will strengthen the house of Judah, and I will save the house of Joseph, and I will bring them again to place them; for I have mercy upon them: and they shall be as though I had not cast them off.
- I will hiss for them, and gather them; for I have redeemed them: and they shall increase as they have increased.
- I will sow them among the people: and they shall remember me in far countries; and they shall live with their children, and turn again. Our people are scattered all over the earth, and he says he will bring them again out of Egypt and gather them out of Assyria.
- I will strengthen them in the Lord; and they shall walk up and down in his name, saith the Lord. That is what we endure to the end for.
