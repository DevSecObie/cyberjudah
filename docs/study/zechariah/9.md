**[Zechariah 9:1-7](/bible/zechariah/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/9#v1)</sup> The burden of the word of the Lord in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus shall be the rest thereof: when the eyes of man, as of all the tribes of Israel, shall be toward the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/9#v2)</sup> And Hamath also shall border thereby; Tyrus, and Zidon, though it be very wise.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/9#v3)</sup> And Tyrus did build herself a strong hold, and heaped up silver as the dust, and fine gold as the mire of the streets.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/9#v4)</sup> Behold, the Lord will cast her out, and he will smite her power in the sea; and she shall be devoured with fire.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/9#v5)</sup> Ashkelon shall see it, and fear; Gaza also shall see it, and be very sorrowful, and Ekron; for her expectation shall be ashamed; and the king shall perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall not be inhabited.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/9#v6)</sup> And a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/9#v7)</sup> And I will take away his blood out of his mouth, and his abominations from between his teeth: but he that remaineth, even he, shall be for our God, and he shall be as a governor in Judah, and Ekron as a Jebusite.

- The burden of the word of the Lord in the land of Hadrach, and Damascus shall be the rest thereof: when the eyes of man, as of all the tribes of Israel, shall be toward the Lord.
- Tyrus did build herself a strong hold, and heaped up silver as the dust, and fine gold as the mire of the streets. Behold, the Lord will cast her out, and he will smite her power in the sea; and she shall be devoured with fire.
- Ashkelon shall see it, and fear; Gaza also shall see it, and be very sorrowful, and Ekron, for her expectation shall be ashamed; and the king shall perish from Gaza, and Ashkelon shall not be inhabited.
- These nations are judged for the part they had in the captivity, and they had a part.
- He that remaineth, even he, shall be for our God, and he shall be as a governor in Judah, and Ekron as a Jebusite. Those that remain are the ones who will reverence Israel and serve.

    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-maccabees/2#v11)</sup> All her ornaments are taken away; of a free woman she is become a bondslave.

    What nation hath not had a part in her kingdom, and gotten of her spoils? All her ornaments are taken away; of a free woman she is become a bondslave.
  - **[Acts 1:6](/bible/acts/1#v6)**

    Lord, wilt thou at this time restore again the kingdom to Israel? The kingdom was Israel's and was lost for disobedience.

**[Zechariah 9:10](/bible/zechariah/9#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/9#v9)</sup> Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/9#v10)</sup> And I will cut off the chariot from Ephraim, and the horse from Jerusalem, and the battle bow shall be cut off: and he shall speak peace unto the heathen: and his dominion shall be from sea even to sea, and from the river even to the ends of the earth.

- Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.
- That is how you know the verse is speaking of Christ, and salvation is what Israel is waiting on him to bring.
- He shall speak peace unto the heathen: and his dominion shall be from sea even to sea, and from the river even to the ends of the earth.
