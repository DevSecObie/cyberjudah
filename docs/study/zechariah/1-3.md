## Introduction

This session reads Haggai 2 through Zechariah 3; these notes cover Zechariah 1 through 3, which close the reading. Zechariah prophesies in the same period as Haggai, in the second year of Darius, to the remnant come back from Babylon. The three chapters run from the call to turn back, through the man among the myrtle trees and the measuring line over Jerusalem, to Joshua the high priest standing before the angel with his filthy garments taken away.

**[Zechariah 1:1-4](/bible/zechariah/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/1#v1)</sup> In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the Lord unto Zechariah, the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/1#v2)</sup> The Lord hath been sore displeased with your fathers.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/1#v3)</sup> Therefore say thou unto them, Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Turn ye unto me, saith the Lord of hosts, and I will turn unto you, saith the Lord of hosts.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/1#v4)</sup> Be ye not as your fathers, unto whom the former prophets have cried, saying, Thus saith the Lord of hosts; Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings: but they did not hear, nor hearken unto me, saith the Lord.

- In the eighth month, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the Lord unto Zechariah, the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet.
- The Lord hath been sore displeased with your fathers. Turn ye unto me, saith the Lord of hosts, and I will turn unto you.
- Be ye not as your fathers, unto whom the former prophets have cried, Turn ye now from your evil ways, and from your evil doings: but they did not hear, nor hearken unto me.
- This is repetitive through the whole history. Any time the Most High sends a prophet to warn his people about destruction, that is compassion, because he did not have to do it.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Chronicles 36:15-16](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v15)</sup> And the Lord God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising up betimes, and sending; because he had compassion on his people, and on his dwelling place:
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v16)</sup> But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets, until the wrath of the Lord arose against his people, till there was no remedy.

    He sent to them by his messengers because he had compassion on his people, but they mocked the messengers and misused his prophets, until there was no remedy.

**[Zechariah 1:5-6](/bible/zechariah/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/1#v5)</sup> Your fathers, where are they? and the prophets, do they live for ever?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/1#v6)</sup> But my words and my statutes, which I commanded my servants the prophets, did they not take hold of your fathers? and they returned and said, Like as the Lord of hosts thought to do unto us, according to our ways, and according to our doings, so hath he dealt with us.

- Your fathers, where are they? And the prophets, do they live for ever? But my words and my statutes took hold of your fathers.
- They returned and said, Like as the Lord of hosts thought to do unto us, according to our ways, and according to our doings, so hath he dealt with us. You get judged by the Most High based on your actions.
- Talk is not what is weighed. Israel is judged by our doings.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

    Talk no more so exceeding proudly, for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

**[Zechariah 1:7-11](/bible/zechariah/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/1#v7)</sup> Upon the four and twentieth day of the eleventh month, which is the month Sebat, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the Lord unto Zechariah, the son of Berechiah, the son of Iddo the prophet, saying,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/1#v8)</sup> I saw by night, and behold a man riding upon a red horse, and he stood among the myrtle trees that were in the bottom; and behind him were there red horses, speckled, and white.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/1#v9)</sup> Then said I, O my lord, what are these? And the angel that talked with me said unto me, I will shew thee what these be.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/1#v10)</sup> And the man that stood among the myrtle trees answered and said, These are they whom the Lord hath sent to walk to and fro through the earth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/zechariah/1#v11)</sup> And they answered the angel of the Lord that stood among the myrtle trees, and said, We have walked to and fro through the earth, and, behold, all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest.

- Upon the four and twentieth day of the eleventh month, which is the month Sebat, in the second year of Darius, came the word of the Lord unto Zechariah.
- I saw by night, and behold a man riding upon a red horse, and he stood among the myrtle trees that were in the bottom; and behind him were there red horses, speckled, and white.
- These are they whom the Lord hath sent to walk to and fro through the earth. They report back that all the earth sitteth still, and is at rest.

**[Zechariah 1:12-17](/bible/zechariah/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/1#v12)</sup> Then the angel of the Lord answered and said, O Lord of hosts, how long wilt thou not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which thou hast had indignation these threescore and ten years?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/zechariah/1#v13)</sup> And the Lord answered the angel that talked with me with good words and comfortable words.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/zechariah/1#v14)</sup> So the angel that communed with me said unto me, Cry thou, saying, Thus saith the Lord of hosts; I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great jealousy.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/zechariah/1#v15)</sup> And I am very sore displeased with the heathen that are at ease: for I was but a little displeased, and they helped forward the affliction.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/zechariah/1#v16)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies: my house shall be built in it, saith the Lord of hosts, and a line shall be stretched forth upon Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/zechariah/1#v17)</sup> Cry yet, saying, Thus saith the Lord of hosts; My cities through prosperity shall yet be spread abroad; and the Lord shall yet comfort Zion, and shall yet choose Jerusalem.

- O Lord of hosts, how long wilt thou not have mercy on Jerusalem and on the cities of Judah, against which thou hast had indignation these threescore and ten years?
- I am jealous for Jerusalem and for Zion with a great jealousy. He is jealous over Israel because we are espoused to him; serving other gods breaks the covenant our forefathers made.
- I am very sore displeased with the heathen that are at ease: for I was but a little displeased, and they helped forward the affliction. The nations were used to afflict Israel and went far past what was commanded.
- I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies: my house shall be built in it, and a line shall be stretched forth upon Jerusalem. My cities through prosperity shall yet be spread abroad, and the Lord shall yet comfort Zion, and shall yet choose Jerusalem.

**[Zechariah 1:18-21](/bible/zechariah/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/zechariah/1#v18)</sup> Then lifted I up mine eyes, and saw, and behold four horns.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/zechariah/1#v19)</sup> And I said unto the angel that talked with me, What be these? And he answered me, These are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/zechariah/1#v20)</sup> And the Lord shewed me four carpenters.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/zechariah/1#v21)</sup> Then said I, What come these to do? And he spake, saying, These are the horns which have scattered Judah, so that no man did lift up his head: but these are come to fray them, to cast out the horns of the Gentiles, which lifted up their horn over the land of Judah to scatter it.

- I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and behold four horns. These are the horns which have scattered Judah, Israel, and Jerusalem.
- The Lord shewed me four carpenters, come to fray them, to cast out the horns of the Gentiles, which lifted up their horn over the land of Judah to scatter it.
- That is another scattering precept: the scattering is named plainly, and so is who did it.

# Zechariah 2: The Measuring Line and the Apple of His Eye

**[Zechariah 2:1-5](/bible/zechariah/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/2#v1)</sup> I lifted up mine eyes again, and looked, and behold a man with a measuring line in his hand.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/2#v2)</sup> Then said I, Whither goest thou? And he said unto me, To measure Jerusalem, to see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/2#v3)</sup> And, behold, the angel that talked with me went forth, and another angel went out to meet him,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/2#v4)</sup> And said unto him, Run, speak to this young man, saying, Jerusalem shall be inhabited as towns without walls for the multitude of men and cattle therein:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/2#v5)</sup> For I, saith the Lord, will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her.

- Behold a man with a measuring line in his hand, gone to measure Jerusalem, to see what is the breadth thereof, and what is the length thereof.
- Jerusalem shall be inhabited as towns without walls, for the multitude of men and cattle therein.
- I will be unto her a wall of fire round about, and will be the glory in the midst of her.

**[Zechariah 2:6-7](/bible/zechariah/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/2#v6)</sup> Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north, saith the Lord: for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/2#v7)</sup> Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon.

- Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north, saith the Lord: for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven.
- Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon. This is a future prophecy as well as a past one; the scriptures are twofold.
- The deliverance comes by knowing who you are. There is a doctrine to it: acknowledge the fault, repent, and make supplication, and do it in the land of the enemy where you are.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Kings 8:46-48](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)</sup> If they sin against thee, (for there is no man that sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captives unto the land of the enemy, far or near;
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)</sup> And so return unto thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their enemies, which led them away captive, and pray unto thee toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, the city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name:

    If they bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and say, We have sinned, and return unto thee with all their heart in the land of their enemies, and pray toward their land.

**[Zechariah 2:8-9](/bible/zechariah/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/2#v8)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts; After the glory hath he sent me unto the nations which spoiled you: for he that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/2#v9)</sup> For, behold, I will shake mine hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to their servants: and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me.

- He that toucheth you toucheth the apple of his eye. The nations that dealt with Israel harshly and put us in slavery, he will deal with them himself.
- Behold, I will shake mine hand upon them, and they shall be a spoil to their servants: and ye shall know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me. Israel does not have to lift a finger.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 14:2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)</sup> And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

    They shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.

**[Zechariah 2:10-11](/bible/zechariah/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/2#v10)</sup> Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion: for, lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/zechariah/2#v11)</sup> And many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and shall be my people: and I will dwell in the midst of thee, and thou shalt know that the Lord of hosts hath sent me unto thee.

- Sing and rejoice, O daughter of Zion: for lo, I come, and I will dwell in the midst of thee, saith the Lord.
- Many nations shall be joined to the Lord in that day, and shall be my people. Do not read that with a Christian mind, as though it means the other nations of the earth are being brought in.
- Israel was scattered among the nations, so Israel is in the nations. The one the Lord shakes his hand at is the heathen, so the heathen are not who is being joined to him here.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.

    The Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.
  - **[James 1:1](/bible/james/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/james/1#v1)</sup> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.

    James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad. The letter names who it is written to.

**[Zechariah 2:12-13](/bible/zechariah/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/2#v12)</sup> And the Lord shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/zechariah/2#v13)</sup> Be silent, O all flesh, before the Lord: for he is raised up out of his holy habitation.

- The Lord shall inherit Judah his portion in the holy land, and shall choose Jerusalem again. That Jerusalem is chosen is said over and over in the scriptures.
- Be silent, O all flesh, before the Lord: for he is raised up out of his holy habitation.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Chronicles 6:6](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v6)</sup> But I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there; and have chosen David to be over my people Israel.

    I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there; and have chosen David to be over my people Israel.

# Zechariah 3: The Filthy Garments Taken Away

Read the chapter → [Zechariah 3](/bible/zechariah/3)

**[Zechariah 3:1-2](/bible/zechariah/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/3#v1)</sup> And he shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/3#v2)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, The Lord rebuke thee, O Satan; even the Lord that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?

- He shewed me Joshua the high priest standing before the angel of the Lord, and Satan standing at his right hand to resist him.
- The Lord rebuke thee, O Satan; even the Lord that hath chosen Jerusalem rebuke thee: is not this a brand plucked out of the fire?

**[Zechariah 3:3-5](/bible/zechariah/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/3#v3)</sup> Now Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and stood before the angel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/3#v4)</sup> And he answered and spake unto those that stood before him, saying, Take away the filthy garments from him. And unto him he said, Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with change of raiment.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/3#v5)</sup> And I said, Let them set a fair mitre upon his head. So they set a fair mitre upon his head, and clothed him with garments. And the angel of the Lord stood by.

- Joshua was clothed with filthy garments, and stood before the angel. Take away the filthy garments from him.
- Behold, I have caused thine iniquity to pass from thee, and I will clothe thee with change of raiment. And they set a fair mitre upon his head.
- The garment is twofold. The change of raiment is Israel becoming righteous: keeping the commandments, putting the Most High first, and dealing rightly with him and with our own people.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 132:9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)</sup> Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

    Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.

**[Zechariah 3:6-10](/bible/zechariah/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/3#v6)</sup> And the angel of the Lord protested unto Joshua, saying,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/3#v7)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/zechariah/3#v8)</sup> Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, thou, and thy fellows that sit before thee: for they are men wondered at: for, behold, I will bring forth my servant the BRANCH.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/3#v9)</sup> For behold the stone that I have laid before Joshua; upon one stone shall be seven eyes: behold, I will engrave the graving thereof, saith the Lord of hosts, and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/zechariah/3#v10)</sup> In that day, saith the Lord of hosts, shall ye call every man his neighbour under the vine and under the fig tree.

- If thou wilt walk in my ways, and if thou wilt keep my charge, then thou shalt also judge my house, and shalt also keep my courts, and I will give thee places to walk among these that stand by.
- Hear now, O Joshua the high priest, thou, and thy fellows that sit before thee: for they are men wondered at; for behold, I will bring forth my servant the BRANCH. That is the remnant of Israel, Judah and Benjamin and Levi.
- Behold the stone that I have laid before Joshua; upon one stone shall be seven eyes: and I will remove the iniquity of that land in one day.
- In that day shall ye call every man his neighbour under the vine and under the fig tree.
