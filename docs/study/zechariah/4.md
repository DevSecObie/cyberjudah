## Introduction

This is a reading session, so the teaching is brief and the four chapters are largely read straight through. The visions run one after another: the golden candlestick with the two olive trees, the flying roll and the ephah with the woman in it, the four chariots and the crowning of Joshua, and then the question about fasting in the fifth month answered in Zechariah 7. The officer's counsel on these chapters is to write your questions down as you read and bring them to your local leadership, because the visions are hard to understand.

**[Zechariah 4:1-5](/bible/zechariah/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/4#v1)</sup> And the angel that talked with me came again, and waked me, as a man that is wakened out of his sleep,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/4#v2)</sup> And said unto me, What seest thou? And I said, I have looked, and behold a candlestick all of gold, with a bowl upon the top of it, and his seven lamps thereon, and seven pipes to the seven lamps, which are upon the top thereof:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/zechariah/4#v3)</sup> And two olive trees by it, one upon the right side of the bowl, and the other upon the left side thereof.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/4#v4)</sup> So I answered and spake to the angel that talked with me, saying, What are these, my lord?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/4#v5)</sup> Then the angel that talked with me answered and said unto me, Knowest thou not what these be? And I said, No, my lord.

- The angel came again and waked me, as a man that is wakened out of his sleep, and asked what I saw.
- A candlestick all of gold, with a bowl upon the top of it, and his seven lamps thereon, and seven pipes to the seven lamps.
- Two olive trees by it, one upon the right side of the bowl, and the other upon the left side thereof.
