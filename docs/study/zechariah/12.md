## Introduction

This session reads Zechariah 11 through 14; these notes cover Zechariah 12 through 14, the chapters that close the book. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The burden of the word of the Lord for Israel runs from Jerusalem made a cup of trembling and a burdensome stone, through the fountain opened for sin and uncleanness and the shepherd smitten with the sheep scattered, to the day the Lord goes forth to fight, his feet standing on the mount of Olives, and every pot in Jerusalem holiness unto the Lord of hosts.

**[Zechariah 12:1-6](/bible/zechariah/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/zechariah/12#v1)</sup> The burden of the word of the Lord for Israel, saith the Lord, which stretcheth forth the heavens, and layeth the foundation of the earth, and formeth the spirit of man within him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/zechariah/12#v2)</sup> Behold, I will make Jerusalem a cup of trembling unto all the people round about, when they shall be in the siege both against Judah and against Jerusalem.

>
> <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/12#v4)</sup> In that day, saith the Lord, I will smite every horse with astonishment, and his rider with madness: and I will open mine eyes upon the house of Judah, and will smite every horse of the people with blindness.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/12#v5)</sup> And the governors of Judah shall say in their heart, The inhabitants of Jerusalem shall be my strength in the Lord of hosts their God.
