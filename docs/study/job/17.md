Read the chapter → [Job 17](/bible/job/17)

This session of "4 Chapters a Day" moves through [Job 17](/bible/job/17) to 20, and it was read straight through with only a few points of exposition, because the class fell on the closing of the feast and the new moon and many were already traveling to the school feast. The spirit was put on the bishop and the leaders to have the congregation read four chapters a day and read the Bible in a year, and we started in Genesis and are now up to Job. In these chapters Job continues to answer his friends: his breath is corrupt and his days are extinct, Bildad the Shuhite paints the ruin of the wicked, Job cries that God has overthrown him and yet declares that his Redeemer liveth, and Zophar the Naamathite says the triumphing of the wicked is short. The teacher held these chapters lightly, told the class he was not going through precepts today and to post any questions to the captains, and pointed to the places where the reading is going into things that are happening right now.

**[Job 17:1-2](/bible/job/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/17#v1)</sup> My breath is corrupt, my days are extinct, the graves are ready for me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/17#v2)</sup> Are there not mockers with me? and doth not mine eye continue in their provocation?

- Job says his breath is corrupt, his days are extinct, and the graves are ready for him.
- He is surrounded by mockers, and his eye continues in their provocation, so his complaint is that even now the men around him do not comfort him but scorn him.

**[Job 17:6-9](/bible/job/17#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/job/17#v6)</sup> He hath made me also a byword of the people; and aforetime I was as a tabret.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/17#v7)</sup> Mine eye also is dim by reason of sorrow, and all my members are as a shadow.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/17#v8)</sup> Upright men shall be astonied at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/17#v9)</sup> The righteous also shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger.

- Job has been made a byword of the people, a man in whose face others spit, and by reason of sorrow all his members are as a shadow.
- Even so, upright men shall be astonished at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.
- The righteous shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger, so the trial does not turn the righteous back but presses him forward.

**[Job 17:11-16](/bible/job/17#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/job/17#v11)</sup> My days are past, my purposes are broken off, even the thoughts of my heart.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/17#v12)</sup> They change the night into day: the light is short because of darkness.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/17#v13)</sup> If I wait, the grave is mine house: I have made my bed in the darkness.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/17#v14)</sup> I have said to corruption, Thou art my father: to the worm, Thou art my mother, and my sister.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/17#v15)</sup> And where is now my hope? as for my hope, who shall see it?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/17#v16)</sup> They shall go down to the bars of the pit, when our rest together is in the dust.

- Job's purposes are broken off and the thoughts of his heart are undone, so that he speaks as a man with no future left in this life.
- He has made his bed in the darkness, called corruption his father and the worm his mother and sister, and asks where his hope now is.
- His hope goes down with him to the bars of the pit, when their rest together is in the dust, which is Job speaking as one who looks only to the grave.
