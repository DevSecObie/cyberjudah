Read the chapter → [Job 29](/bible/job/29)

This session continues the journey through Job, reading chapters 29 to 32. Job is in a conversation with his friends. Much of what Job speaks is in parable form, and a lot of it is above our pay grade. For clarity on what is happening in the book of Job, the class recommends Captain Shamaya's "I Am Job" video. In these chapters Job remembers his former glory, describes how the base people of Esau now have him in derision, begins to justify himself, and then Elihu the young man rises up in his wrath against Job because Job justified himself rather than the Most High.

**[Job 29:1-6](/bible/job/29#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/29#v1)</sup> Moreover Job continued his parable, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/29#v2)</sup> Oh that I were as in months past, as in the days when God preserved me;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/29#v3)</sup> When his candle shined upon my head, and when by his light I walked through darkness;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/29#v4)</sup> As I was in the days of my youth, when the secret of God was upon my tabernacle;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/29#v5)</sup> When the Almighty was yet with me, when my children were about me;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/29#v6)</sup> When I washed my steps with butter, and the rock poured me out rivers of oil;

- Job continues his parable and remembers the months past when the Most High preserved him, when His candle shined upon his head and by His light Job walked through darkness.
- These were the days of his youth, when the secret of God was upon his tabernacle, when the Almighty was yet with him and his children were about him, when he washed his steps with butter and the rock poured him out rivers of oil.
- Remember verse 1 says Job continued his parable. A lot of what Job is talking about, not the entire book but a lot of it, is in parable form, and most of it is above the pay grade.

**[Job 29:7-11](/bible/job/29#v7)**

>

>

>

>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/29#v11)</sup> When the ear heard me, then it blessed me; and when the eye saw me, it gave witness to me:

- When Job went out to the gate of the city and prepared his seat in the street, the young men saw him and hid themselves, and the aged arose and stood up.
- The princes refrained talking and laid their hand on their mouth. The nobles held their peace and their tongue cleaved to the roof of their mouth. When the ear heard him it blessed him, and when the eye saw him it gave witness to him.

**[Job 29:12-17](/bible/job/29#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/29#v12)</sup> Because I delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, and him that had none to help him.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/29#v13)</sup> The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon me: and I caused the widow’s heart to sing for joy.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/29#v14)</sup> I put on righteousness, and it clothed me: my judgment was as a robe and a diadem.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/29#v15)</sup> I was eyes to the blind, and feet was I to the lame.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/29#v16)</sup> I was a father to the poor: and the cause which I knew not I searched out.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/29#v17)</sup> And I brake the jaws of the wicked, and plucked the spoil out of his teeth.

- Job delivered the poor that cried, and the fatherless, and him that had none to help him. The blessing of him that was ready to perish came upon him, and he caused the widow's heart to sing for joy.
- He put on righteousness and it clothed him, his judgment was as a robe and a diadem. He was eyes to the blind and feet to the lame, a father to the poor, and he searched out the cause he knew not. He broke the jaws of the wicked and plucked the spoil out of his teeth.

**[Job 29:21-25](/bible/job/29#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/29#v21)</sup> Unto me men gave ear, and waited, and kept silence at my counsel.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/29#v22)</sup> After my words they spake not again; and my speech dropped upon them.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/29#v23)</sup> And they waited for me as for the rain; and they opened their mouth wide as for the latter rain.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/29#v24)</sup> If I laughed on them, they believed it not; and the light of my countenance they cast not down.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/29#v25)</sup> I chose out their way, and sat chief, and dwelt as a king in the army, as one that comforteth the mourners.

- Men gave ear to Job and waited and kept silence at his counsel. After his words they spake not again, and his speech dropped upon them. They waited for him as for the rain and opened their mouth wide as for the latter rain.
- If he laughed on them they believed it not, and the light of his countenance they cast not down. He chose out their way and sat chief, dwelling as a king in the army, as one that comforteth the mourners. This is a deep parable by Job.
