**[Job 2:1-3](/bible/job/2#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/2#v1)</sup> Again there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan came also among them to present himself before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/2#v2)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, From whence comest thou? And Satan answered the Lord, and said, From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/2#v3)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth God, and escheweth evil? and still he holdeth fast his integrity, although thou movedst me against him, to destroy him without cause.

- Again there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan came also. The Lord said, hast thou considered My servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, and still he holdeth fast his integrity, although thou movedst Me against him to destroy him without cause.
- The Lord is bragging, He is boasting: even though you made Me go after Job for no reason at all, he is still standing strong, he is still handling his business.
- We have to allow God to brag on us. It is the same thing with all our enemies watching online; you have got to give the Lord a chance to brag.

**[Job 2:4-6](/bible/job/2#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/2#v4)</sup> And Satan answered the Lord, and said, Skin for skin, yea, all that a man hath will he give for his life.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/2#v5)</sup> But put forth thine hand now, and touch his bone and his flesh, and he will curse thee to thy face.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/2#v6)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, Behold, he is in thine hand; but save his life.

- Satan said, skin for skin, all that a man hath will he give for his life, but put forth thine hand now and touch his bone and his flesh, and he will curse thee to thy face.
- Possessions are possessions; a brother could lose a car, a job, family members, whatever. But Satan is saying if his health is afflicted, I bet you he will curse you to your face, because that is another thing that gets us.
- A lot of us, material things are not your stronghold. Even if you lost it today it would not do you nothing, but your health, that is your trial. As soon as you get sick you think it is the end of the world, or you think you did something.
- Maybe the Lord is doing this to you so you can be a shining light to somebody going through the same thing. A lot of our trials are not just for us. We read the book of Job because Job went through the things that we go through ourselves.
- The Lord said, behold, he is in thine hand, but save his life. The Lord said touch him but do not kill him. That does not sound like somebody rebellious against God; Satan took orders, just like we take orders.

**[Job 2:7-8](/bible/job/2#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/2#v7)</sup> So went Satan forth from the presence of the Lord, and smote Job with sore boils from the sole of his foot unto his crown.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/2#v8)</sup> And he took him a potsherd to scrape himself withal; and he sat down among the ashes.

- Satan went forth and smote Job with sore boils from the sole of his foot unto his crown, and Job took a potsherd to scrape himself withal, and he sat down among the ashes.

**[Job 2:9-10](/bible/job/2#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/2#v9)</sup> Then said his wife unto him, Dost thou still retain thine integrity? curse God, and die.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/2#v10)</sup> But he said unto her, Thou speakest as one of the foolish women speaketh. What? shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil? In all this did not Job sin with his lips.

- Then his wife said, dost thou still retain thine integrity? Curse God, and die. This is the other test that brothers get.
- Satan is going to come through your woman. If he cannot get to you, he is going to come through her. Look at what he did with Adam: Satan was not going to get past Adam, so he backdoored and went to Eve. Same thing here; Satan was in his woman too.
- A lot of men, we get emotional behind our woman, and whatever she wants to say we go follow it instead of checking her.
- Job said, thou speakest as one of the foolish women speaketh; what, shall we receive good at the hand of God, and shall we not receive evil? In all this did not Job sin with his lips.
- So he told her, woman, you sound foolish. Are we only going to get good from God and not evil? That is what we tell people all the time. We just read in [Isaiah 45:7](/bible/isaiah/45#v7) that God creates evil and creates peace. God is a man of war; He creates life and death. Nothing happens without the Lord; everything asks for permission.

**[Job 2:11-13](/bible/job/2#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/2#v11)</sup> Now when Job’s three friends heard of all this evil that was come upon him, they came every one from his own place; Eliphaz the Temanite, and Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite: for they had made an appointment together to come to mourn with him and to comfort him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/2#v12)</sup> And when they lifted up their eyes afar off, and knew him not, they lifted up their voice, and wept; and they rent every one his mantle, and sprinkled dust upon their heads toward heaven.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/2#v13)</sup> So they sat down with him upon the ground seven days and seven nights, and none spake a word unto him: for they saw that his grief was very great.

- Job's three friends heard of all this evil that came upon him: Eliphaz the Temanite, Bildad the Shuhite, and Zophar the Naamathite. They made an appointment together to come to mourn with him and to comfort him.
- When they lifted up their eyes afar off and knew him not, they lifted up their voice and wept, rent every man his mantle, and sprinkled dust upon their heads toward heaven. They sat down with him upon the ground seven days and seven nights, and none spake a word, for they saw that his grief was very great.
- Much like us, our friends hear of our affliction and come to lend their support, to give correction, to give harsh reality and hard truth. We are not talking about people that butter you up knowing you are in the wrong; we are talking about people that give you straight facts about what is going on with you.
