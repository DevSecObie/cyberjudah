**[Job 4:1-6](/bible/job/4#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/4#v1)</sup> Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/4#v2)</sup> If we assay to commune with thee, wilt thou be grieved? but who can withhold himself from speaking?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/4#v3)</sup> Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/4#v4)</sup> Thy words have upholden him that was falling, and thou hast strengthened the feeble knees.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/4#v5)</sup> But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/4#v6)</sup> Is not this thy fear, thy confidence, thy hope, and the uprightness of thy ways?

- A lot of us think, well, I did not do nothing wrong, so why is this happening to me, why me? That is a common fleshly response to a lot of things we go through, but we have to take a step back and see who is in control of everything and for what purpose.
- Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered: if we assay to commune with thee, wilt thou be grieved? But who can withhold himself from speaking? Eliphaz is like, you go to visit somebody that is sick and you do not want to say the wrong thing, but we just cannot sit here and say nothing.
- Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands; thy words have upholden him that was falling, and thou hast strengthened the feeble knees. But now it is come upon thee, and thou faintest; it toucheth thee, and thou art troubled.
- That right there cuts deep. A lot of times we can give other people the scriptures, First Corinthians 10:13, Ecclesiasticus chapter 2, [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), and be the strongest person out there. But then as soon as a trial comes to us, that is when we fall apart. That is what Eliphaz was asking: now trial is coming to you, Job, and now you are falling apart.
- Is not this thy fear, thy confidence, thy hope, and the uprightness of thy ways? Eliphaz is saying, you used to uplift people, and now trouble is coming upon you and you are about to lose your mind. Maybe you did something. Sometimes it is something we did, but a lot of times it is not; it is for us to go through something because we can handle it, and be a shining example to the next person.

**[Job 4:7-11](/bible/job/4#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/4#v7)</sup> Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? or where were the righteous cut off?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/4#v8)</sup> Even as I have seen, they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/4#v9)</sup> By the blast of God they perish, and by the breath of his nostrils are they consumed.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/4#v10)</sup> The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/4#v11)</sup> The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the stout lion’s whelps are scattered abroad.

- Remember, I pray thee, who ever perished, being innocent? Or where were the righteous cut off? Even as I have seen, they that plow iniquity, and sow wickedness, reap the same.
- By the blast of God they perish, and by the breath of his nostrils are they consumed. The roaring of the lion, and the voice of the fierce lion, and the teeth of the young lions, are broken. The old lion perisheth for lack of prey, and the stout lion's whelps are scattered abroad.

**[Job 4:12-21](/bible/job/4#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/4#v12)</sup> Now a thing was secretly brought to me, and mine ear received a little thereof.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/4#v13)</sup> In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/4#v14)</sup> Fear came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones to shake.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/4#v15)</sup> Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/4#v16)</sup> It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof: an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice, saying,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/4#v17)</sup> Shall mortal man be more just than God? shall a man be more pure than his maker?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/4#v18)</sup> Behold, he put no trust in his servants; and his angels he charged with folly:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/4#v19)</sup> How much less in them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, which are crushed before the moth?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/4#v20)</sup> They are destroyed from morning to evening: they perish for ever without any regarding it.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/4#v21)</sup> Doth not their excellency which is in them go away? they die, even without wisdom.

- Now a thing was secretly brought to me, and mine ear received a little thereof. In thoughts from the visions of the night, when deep sleep falleth on men. Now it is talking about the dreams that we have.
- Fear came upon me, and trembling, which made all my bones to shake. Then a spirit passed before my face; the hair of my flesh stood up. It stood still, but I could not discern the form thereof; an image was before mine eyes, there was silence, and I heard a voice.
- Saying, shall mortal man be more just than God? Shall a man be more pure than his maker? Behold, he put no trust in his servants, and his angels he charged with folly.
- How much less in them that dwell in houses of clay, whose foundation is in the dust, which are crushed before the moth? They are destroyed from morning to evening; they perish for ever without any regarding it. Doth not their excellency which is in them go away? They die, even without wisdom.
- Eliphaz is basically telling Job, maybe it is something that you did, something you got going on, something you need to examine yourself with. And that is where it ends.
