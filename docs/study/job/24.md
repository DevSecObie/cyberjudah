**[Job 24:1-4](/bible/job/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/24#v1)</sup> Why, seeing times are not hidden from the Almighty, do they that know him not see his days?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/24#v2)</sup> Some remove the landmarks; they violently take away flocks, and feed thereof.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/24#v3)</sup> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take the widow’s ox for a pledge.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/24#v4)</sup> They turn the needy out of the way: the poor of the earth hide themselves together.

- Why, seeing times are not hidden from the Almighty, do they that know him not see his days? Some remove the landmarks, they violently take away flocks and feed thereof. This is the wicked moving into your land and naming it after themselves.
- They drive away the ass of the fatherless and take the widow's ox for a pledge. They turn the needy out of the way, and the poor of the earth hide themselves together.
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:14](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour’s landmark, which they of old time have set in thine inheritance, which thou shalt inherit in the land that the Lord thy God giveth thee to possess it.

    Thou shalt not remove thy neighbor's landmark which they of old time have set in thine inheritance.
  - **[Habakkuk 2:4-5](/bible/habakkuk/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/habakkuk/2#v4)</sup> Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/habakkuk/2#v5)</sup> Yea also, because he transgresseth by wine, he is a proud man, neither keepeth at home, who enlargeth his desire as hell, and is as death, and cannot be satisfied, but gathereth unto him all nations, and heapeth unto him all people:

    His soul lifted up is not upright; he transgresseth by wine, enlargeth his desire as hell, and gathereth unto him all nations and all people; that is America.
  - **[Habakkuk 2:9](/bible/habakkuk/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/habakkuk/2#v9)</sup> Woe to him that coveteth an evil covetousness to his house, that he may set his nest on high, that he may be delivered from the power of evil!

    Woe to him that coveteth an evil covetousness to his house.
  - **[Micah 2:2](/bible/micah/2#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/micah/2#v2)</sup> And they covet fields, and take them by violence; and houses, and take them away: so they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.

    They covet fields and take them by violence, and houses and take them away; they oppress a man, his house, his heritage, changing your name.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:49-51](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)**
    > <sup>[49](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)</sup> The Lord shall bring a nation against thee from far, from the end of the earth, as swift as the eagle flieth; a nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand;
    >
    > <sup>[50](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v50)</sup> A nation of fierce countenance, which shall not regard the person of the old, nor shew favour to the young:
    >
    > <sup>[51](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v51)</sup> And he shall eat the fruit of thy cattle, and the fruit of thy land, until thou be destroyed: which also shall not leave thee either corn, wine, or oil, or the increase of thy kine, or flocks of thy sheep, until he have destroyed thee.

    A nation swift as the eagle, of fierce countenance, whose symbol is the eagle, shall eat the fruit of your cattle and land until you are destroyed.

**[Job 24:5-8](/bible/job/24#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/job/24#v5)</sup> Behold, as wild asses in the desert, go they forth to their work; rising betimes for a prey: the wilderness yieldeth food for them and for their children.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/24#v6)</sup> They reap every one his corn in the field: and they gather the vintage of the wicked.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/24#v7)</sup> They cause the naked to lodge without clothing, that they have no covering in the cold.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/24#v8)</sup> They are wet with the showers of the mountains, and embrace the rock for want of a shelter.

- Behold, as wild asses in the desert go they forth to their work, rising betimes for a prey; the wilderness yieldeth food for them and their children. They reap every one his corn in the field and gather the vintage of the wicked.
- They cause the naked to lodge without clothing, that they have no covering in the cold. They are wet with the showers of the mountains and embrace the rock for want of a shelter.

**[Job 24:9-11](/bible/job/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/job/24#v9)</sup> They pluck the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge of the poor.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/24#v10)</sup> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and they take away the sheaf from the hungry;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/24#v11)</sup> Which make oil within their walls, and tread their winepresses, and suffer thirst.

- They pluck the fatherless from the breast and take a pledge of the poor. They cause him to go naked without clothing and take away the sheep from the hungry.
- They which make oil within their walls and tread their winepresses still suffer thirst. This is the curse where you plant vineyards and do not eat from them, left neither corn nor wine.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:32](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v32)</sup> Thy sons and thy daughters shall be given unto another people, and thine eyes shall look, and fail with longing for them all the day long: and there shall be no might in thine hand.

    Thy sons and daughters given to another people, and thine eyes shall look and fail with longing for them all day long.
  - **[Lamentations 1:16](/bible/lamentations/1#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/lamentations/1#v16)</sup> For these things I weep; mine eye, mine eye runneth down with water, because the comforter that should relieve my soul is far from me: my children are desolate, because the enemy prevailed.

    Mine eyes run down with water, because the comforter that should relieve my soul is far from me.

**[Job 24:12-14](/bible/job/24#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/job/24#v12)</sup> Men groan from out of the city, and the soul of the wounded crieth out: yet God layeth not folly to them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/24#v13)</sup> They are of those that rebel against the light; they know not the ways thereof, nor abide in the paths thereof.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/24#v14)</sup> The murderer rising with the light killeth the poor and needy, and in the night is as a thief.

- Men groan from out of the city and the soul of the wounded crieth out, yet God layeth not folly to them. The comforter has left, as read in Lamentations.
- They are of those that rebel against the light; they know not the ways thereof nor abide in the paths thereof. The murderer rising with the light killeth the poor and needy, and in the night is as a thief. He will kill you in broad daylight.
  - **[Zechariah 11:5](/bible/zechariah/11#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/zechariah/11#v5)</sup> Whose possessors slay them, and hold themselves not guilty: and they that sell them say, Blessed be the Lord; for I am rich: and their own shepherds pity them not.

    Whose possessors slay them and hold themselves not guilty; your last name shows who owns you, that is the real name brand.
  - **[Zechariah 11:4](/bible/zechariah/11#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/zechariah/11#v4)</sup> Thus saith the Lord my God; Feed the flock of the slaughter;

    Thus saith the Lord my God, feed the flock of the slaughter; we are the ones being killed with no assurance of life.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:67](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v67)**
    > <sup>[67](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v67)</sup> In the morning thou shalt say, Would God it were even! and at even thou shalt say, Would God it were morning! for the fear of thine heart wherewith thou shalt fear, and for the sight of thine eyes which thou shalt see.

    In the morning you shall say, would God it were even, and at evening, would God it were morning; you cannot tell if day or night is worse.

**[Job 24:15-17](/bible/job/24#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/job/24#v15)</sup> The eye also of the adulterer waiteth for the twilight, saying, No eye shall see me: and disguiseth his face.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/24#v16)</sup> In the dark they dig through houses, which they had marked for themselves in the daytime: they know not the light.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/24#v17)</sup> For the morning is to them even as the shadow of death: if one know them, they are in the terrors of the shadow of death.

- The eye of the adulterer waited for the twilight, saying no eye shall see me, and disguiseth his face. In the dark they dig through houses which they marked for themselves in the daytime; they know not the light.
- They scope you out in the daytime, marking your house, then come back at nighttime to hit you. For the morning is to them as the shadow of death; if one knows them, they are in the terrors of the shadow of death.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 23:17-19](/bible/sirach/23#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/23#v17)</sup> All bread is sweet to a whoremonger, he will not leave off till he die.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/23#v18)</sup> A man that breaketh wedlock, saying thus in his heart, Who seeth me? I am compassed about with darkness, the walls cover me, and no body seeth me; what need I to fear? the most High will not remember my sins:
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/23#v19)</sup> Such a man only feareth the eyes of men, and knoweth not that the eyes of the Lord are ten thousand times brighter than the sun, beholding all the ways of men, and considering the most secret parts.

**[Job 24:18-21](/bible/job/24#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/job/24#v18)</sup> He is swift as the waters; their portion is cursed in the earth: he beholdeth not the way of the vineyards.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/24#v19)</sup> Drought and heat consume the snow waters: so doth the grave those which have sinned.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/24#v20)</sup> The womb shall forget him; the worm shall feed sweetly on him; he shall be no more remembered; and wickedness shall be broken as a tree.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/24#v21)</sup> He evil entreateth the barren that beareth not: and doeth not good to the widow.

- He is swift as the waters; their portion is cursed in the earth; he beholdeth not the way of the vineyards. Drought and heat consume the snow waters; so doth the grave those which have sinned, just as heat consumes the snow.
- The womb shall forget him, the worm shall feed sweetly on him; he shall be no more remembered, and wickedness shall be broken as a tree. He evil entreateth the barren that beareth not and doeth not good to the widow.
  - **[Isaiah 66:24](/bible/isaiah/66#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/66#v24)</sup> And they shall go forth, and look upon the carcases of the men that have transgressed against me: for their worm shall not die, neither shall their fire be quenched; and they shall be an abhorring unto all flesh.

    They shall look upon the carcasses of the men that transgressed; their worm shall not die, neither shall their fire be quenched.

**[Job 24:22-25](/bible/job/24#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/job/24#v22)</sup> He draweth also the mighty with his power: he riseth up, and no man is sure of life.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/24#v23)</sup> Though it be given him to be in safety, whereon he resteth; yet his eyes are upon their ways.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/24#v24)</sup> They are exalted for a little while, but are gone and brought low; they are taken out of the way as all other, and cut off as the tops of the ears of corn.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/24#v25)</sup> And if it be not so now, who will make me a liar, and make my speech nothing worth?

- He draweth also the mighty with his power; he riseth up and no man is sure of life. This is the curse of no assurance of life, failing eyes, and sorrow of mind.
- Though it be given him to be in safety, his eyes are upon their ways. They are exalted for a little while, but are gone and brought low, taken out of the way and cut off as the tops of the ears of corn.
- And if it be not so now, who will make Job a liar and make his speech nothing worth? The prophecy will come true because Job is speaking as the mouthpiece of God.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:66](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v66)**
    > <sup>[66](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v66)</sup> And thy life shall hang in doubt before thee; and thou shalt fear day and night, and shalt have none assurance of thy life:

    Thy life shall hang in doubt before thee, and thou shalt fear day and night, and have none assurance of thy life.
  - **[Romans 3:3-4](/bible/romans/3#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/romans/3#v3)</sup> For what if some did not believe? shall their unbelief make the faith of God without effect?
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/3#v4)</sup> God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.

    Shall the unbelief of some make the faith of God without effect? God forbid; let God be true, but every man a liar, that Job be justified in his sayings.
