**[Job 27:1-4](/bible/job/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/27#v1)</sup> Moreover Job continued his parable, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/27#v2)</sup> As God liveth, who hath taken away my judgment; and the Almighty, who hath vexed my soul;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/27#v3)</sup> All the while my breath is in me, and the spirit of God is in my nostrils;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/27#v4)</sup> My lips shall not speak wickedness, nor my tongue utter deceit.

- Job continues his parable, saying as God liveth, who hath taken away my judgment, and the Almighty, who hath vexed my soul, all the while my breath is in me and the spirit of God is in my nostrils.
- This ties back to Genesis where the Most High breathed into man and he became a living soul; that breath is the spirit of God going into man.
- Job says his lips shall not speak wickedness nor his tongue utter deceit; like the valley of dry bones where the Most High breathed into them, he breathes in his law, statutes, and commandments so that our lips no longer speak wickedness and our tongue no longer utters deceit.
- In these last days when we wake up, we are to speak truth one to our neighbor, apply [Leviticus 5:1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1), think ourselves men, and rehearse the righteous acts.

**[Job 27:5-6](/bible/job/27#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/job/27#v5)</sup> God forbid that I should justify you: till I die I will not remove mine integrity from me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/27#v6)</sup> My righteousness I hold fast, and will not let it go: my heart shall not reproach me so long as I live.

- Job says God forbid that he should justify his friends, and that till he dies he will not remove his integrity from him; this is him justifying that he has had integrity with the Most High.
- Integrity is the quality of being honest and having strong moral principles, moral uprightness, honesty, good character, righteousness, and doing what is right even when no one is looking.
- Job keeps the commandments to the best of his ability because he loves the Lord and fears the Lord; he does what he is supposed to do when nobody is watching, not just getting in order when the righteous come around or when he fears correction from men.
- There is the ring of Gyges in Plato's Republic, a ring that turns you invisible; the question of ethics is whether you still do the right thing and uphold your integrity, or go against everything you learned in uprightness. You do good whether you are invisible or not, being faithful to the Most High and keeping his laws at all times.
- Job says his righteousness he holds fast and will not let it go, and his heart shall not reproach him so long as he lives, saying he is not a hypocrite; what he says, he does.

**[Job 27:7-12](/bible/job/27#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/job/27#v7)</sup> Let mine enemy be as the wicked, and he that riseth up against me as the unrighteous.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/27#v8)</sup> For what is the hope of the hypocrite, though he hath gained, when God taketh away his soul?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/27#v9)</sup> Will God hear his cry when trouble cometh upon him?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/27#v10)</sup> Will he delight himself in the Almighty? will he always call upon God?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/27#v11)</sup> I will teach you by the hand of God: that which is with the Almighty will I not conceal.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/27#v12)</sup> Behold, all ye yourselves have seen it; why then are ye thus altogether vain?

- Job says let his enemy be as the wicked and he that riseth up against him as the unrighteous, then asks what is the hope of the hypocrite when God taketh away his soul.
- This ties to what Christ said, what does it profit to gain the world and lose your soul; the hypocrite says one thing and does another.
- He asks whether God will hear the hypocrite's cry when trouble comes, whether he will delight himself in the Almighty and always call upon God, and says he will teach by the hand of God and not conceal that which is with the Almighty.
- Job says all of them have seen it themselves, so why then are they altogether vain; this rebukes hypocrites like America that say God has blessed them with riches, and the hip-hop artists and fake pastors who claim to love the people but do nothing for them and are not real doers of the word.
