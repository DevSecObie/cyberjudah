Read the chapter → [Job 33](/bible/job/33)

This session covers Job chapters 33 through 36, where Elihu continues his address to Job. Job is still going through his trials, and Elihu gives him words of encouragement and correction, laying out how the Most High deals with men: how our day-to-day lives are shaped by what he seals in our minds, how he chastens men to turn them from pride, and how he delivers the soul that repents. The teaching moves through the deep parables of these chapters, showing that nothing on earth is done apart from God, and that no man can bring a new revelation or correct what God has put upon him.

**[Job 33:1-4](/bible/job/33#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/33#v1)</sup> Wherefore, Job, I pray thee, hear my speeches, and hearken to all my words.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/33#v2)</sup> Behold, now I have opened my mouth, my tongue hath spoken in my mouth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/33#v3)</sup> My words shall be of the uprightness of my heart: and my lips shall utter knowledge clearly.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/33#v4)</sup> The Spirit of God hath made me, and the breath of the Almighty hath given me life.

- This is Elihu speaking to Job. Job is still going through his trials, and Elihu opens his mouth to speak words of uprightness from his heart.
- Elihu declares his lips will utter knowledge clearly, that the spirit of God made him and the breath of the Almighty gave him life.

**[Job 33:5-7](/bible/job/33#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/33#v5)</sup> If thou canst answer me, set thy words in order before me, stand up.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/33#v6)</sup> Behold, I am according to thy wish in God’s stead: I also am formed out of the clay.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/33#v7)</sup> Behold, my terror shall not make thee afraid, neither shall my hand be heavy upon thee.

- Elihu tells Job that if he can answer, set his words in order and stand up.
- Elihu stands in God's stead, also formed out of the clay, so his terror will not make Job afraid nor his hand be heavy upon him.

**[Job 33:8-11](/bible/job/33#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/33#v8)</sup> Surely thou hast spoken in mine hearing, and I have heard the voice of thy words, saying,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/33#v9)</sup> I am clean without transgression, I am innocent; neither is there iniquity in me.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/33#v10)</sup> Behold, he findeth occasions against me, he counteth me for his enemy,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/33#v11)</sup> He putteth my feet in the stocks, he marketh all my paths.

- Elihu repeats back what he heard Job say in his ears: that Job claimed to be clean, without transgression, innocent, and that there is no iniquity in him.
- Job had said God finds occasions against him, counts him for an enemy, puts his feet in the stocks, and marks all his paths.

**[Job 33:12-14](/bible/job/33#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/33#v12)</sup> Behold, in this thou art not just: I will answer thee, that God is greater than man.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/33#v13)</sup> Why dost thou strive against him? for he giveth not account of any of his matters.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/33#v14)</sup> For God speaketh once, yea twice, yet man perceiveth it not.

- Elihu answers that in this Job is not just, for God is greater than man, and asks why Job strives against him.
- God gives no account of any of his matters, and speaks once, yea twice, yet man does not perceive it.

**[Job 33:15-18](/bible/job/33#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/33#v15)</sup> In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, in slumberings upon the bed;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/33#v16)</sup> Then he openeth the ears of men, and sealeth their instruction,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/33#v17)</sup> That he may withdraw man from his purpose, and hide pride from man.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/33#v18)</sup> He keepeth back his soul from the pit, and his life from perishing by the sword.

- Here Elihu speaks on where the ways of man come from and how our day-to-day lives are affected by what the Most High seals within our minds.
- In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falls upon men upon their beds, then he opens the ears of men and seals their instruction.
- He does this to withdraw man from his purpose and hide pride from man, keeping back his soul from the pit and his life from perishing by the sword.
- Everything we go through day in and day out, we may think we are doing what we want to do, but it is sealed in our minds what the Most High wants to happen in our lives.
- Many times we set out to do one thing and end up doing another, and later say the Lord was with me on this one, because he saved us from something terrible.
  - **[Proverbs 20:24](/bible/proverbs/20#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/20#v24)</sup> Man’s goings are of the Lord; how can a man then understand his own way?

    Man's goings are of the Lord, so how can a man understand his own way; we must be mindful as we move through our day.

**[Job 33:19-22](/bible/job/33#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/33#v19)</sup> He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, and the multitude of his bones with strong pain:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/33#v20)</sup> So that his life abhorreth bread, and his soul dainty meat.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/33#v21)</sup> His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen; and his bones that were not seen stick out.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/33#v22)</sup> Yea, his soul draweth near unto the grave, and his life to the destroyers.

- God also chastens man with pain upon his bed and the multitude of his bones with strong pain, so that his life abhors bread and his soul dainty meat.
- His flesh is consumed away till it cannot be seen, his bones stick out, his soul draws near the grave and his life to the destroyers.

**[Job 33:23-26](/bible/job/33#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/33#v23)</sup> If there be a messenger with him, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to shew unto man his uprightness:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/33#v24)</sup> Then he is gracious unto him, and saith, Deliver him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/33#v25)</sup> His flesh shall be fresher than a child’s: he shall return to the days of his youth:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/job/33#v26)</sup> He shall pray unto God, and he will be favourable unto him: and he shall see his face with joy: for he will render unto man his righteousness.

- If there be a messenger, an interpreter, one among a thousand to show man his uprightness, then God is gracious and says deliver him from going down to the pit, for he has found a ransom.
- His flesh shall be fresher than a child's, he shall return to the days of his youth, pray unto God, be favorable to him, and see his face with joy.
- Each man, woman, and child upon earth is rendered to according to our righteousness.

**[Job 33:27-28](/bible/job/33#v27)**
> <sup>[27](/bible/job/33#v27)</sup> He looketh upon men, and if any say, I have sinned, and perverted that which was right, and it profited me not;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/job/33#v28)</sup> He will deliver his soul from going into the pit, and his life shall see the light.

- If any man says I have sinned and perverted that which was right and it profited me not, God will deliver his soul from going to the pit and his life shall see light.
- When we recognize we are in our transgressions and iniquity and we repent, then the Lord hears us.
  - **[1 Kings 8:46-49](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)</sup> If they sin against thee, (for there is no man that sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captives unto the land of the enemy, far or near;
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)</sup> And so return unto thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their enemies, which led them away captive, and pray unto thee toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, the city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name:
    >
    > <sup>[49](/bible/1-kings/8#v49)</sup> Then hear thou their prayer and their supplication in heaven thy dwelling place, and maintain their cause,

    There is no man that sinneth not; when the captives in the land of their enemy repent, confess they have sinned and done perversely, and turn again with all their heart, God hears their prayer.
  - **[2 Peter 2:9](/bible/2-peter/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-peter/2#v9)</sup> The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished:

    The Lord knows how to deliver the godly out of temptation and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment; when we walk godly and according to the laws, the Lord has a way out for us.

**[Job 33:29-30](/bible/job/33#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/job/33#v29)</sup> Lo, all these things worketh God oftentimes with man,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/job/33#v30)</sup> To bring back his soul from the pit, to be enlightened with the light of the living.

- God does this thing often with men, righteous men and women who repent, to bring back their soul from the pit and enlighten them with the light of the living.

**[Job 33:31-33](/bible/job/33#v31)**
> <sup>[31](/bible/job/33#v31)</sup> Mark well, O Job, hearken unto me: hold thy peace, and I will speak.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/job/33#v32)</sup> If thou hast any thing to say, answer me: speak, for I desire to justify thee.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/job/33#v33)</sup> If not, hearken unto me: hold thy peace, and I shall teach thee wisdom.

- Elihu tells Job to mark well, hearken, and hold his peace while Elihu speaks, and if Job has anything to say, to answer, for Elihu desires to justify him.
- If not, Job is to hold his peace and Elihu will teach him wisdom.

# Job 34: God Renders to Every Man According to His Ways

Read the chapter → [Job 34](/bible/job/34)

**[Job 34:1-4](/bible/job/34#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/34#v1)</sup> Furthermore Elihu answered and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/34#v2)</sup> Hear my words, O ye wise men; and give ear unto me, ye that have knowledge.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/34#v3)</sup> For the ear trieth words, as the mouth tasteth meat.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/34#v4)</sup> Let us choose to us judgment: let us know among ourselves what is good.

- Elihu answers again, calling the wise men and men of knowledge to hear him, for the ear tries words as the mouth tastes meat.
- He calls them to choose judgment and to know among themselves what is good.

**[Job 34:5-9](/bible/job/34#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/34#v5)</sup> For Job hath said, I am righteous: and God hath taken away my judgment.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/34#v6)</sup> Should I lie against my right? my wound is incurable without transgression.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/34#v7)</sup> What man is like Job, who drinketh up scorning like water?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/34#v8)</sup> Which goeth in company with the workers of iniquity, and walketh with wicked men.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/34#v9)</sup> For he hath said, It profiteth a man nothing that he should delight himself with God.

- Elihu recounts that Job said he is righteous and that God has taken away his judgment, that his wound is incurable without transgression.
- He asks what man is like Job, who drinks up scorning like water, goes in company with the workers of iniquity, and says it profits a man nothing to delight himself with God.

**[Job 34:10-12](/bible/job/34#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/34#v10)</sup> Therefore hearken unto me, ye men of understanding: far be it from God, that he should do wickedness; and from the Almighty, that he should commit iniquity.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/34#v11)</sup> For the work of a man shall he render unto him, and cause every man to find according to his ways.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/34#v12)</sup> Yea, surely God will not do wickedly, neither will the Almighty pervert judgment.

- Elihu calls men of understanding to hear: far be it from God to do wickedness and from the Almighty to commit iniquity.
- For the work of a man God will render unto him and cause every man to find according to his ways; surely God will not do wickedly nor pervert judgment.

**[Job 34:13-15](/bible/job/34#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/34#v13)</sup> Who hath given him a charge over the earth? or who hath disposed the whole world?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/34#v14)</sup> If he set his heart upon man, if he gather unto himself his spirit and his breath;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/34#v15)</sup> All flesh shall perish together, and man shall turn again unto dust.

- Who has given God a charge over the earth, or who has disposed the whole world.
- If God set his heart upon man and gather his spirit and breath, all flesh shall perish together and man shall turn again unto dust.

**[Job 34:16-20](/bible/job/34#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/34#v16)</sup> If now thou hast understanding, hear this: hearken to the voice of my words.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/34#v17)</sup> Shall even he that hateth right govern? and wilt thou condemn him that is most just?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/34#v18)</sup> Is it fit to say to a king, Thou art wicked? and to princes, Ye are ungodly?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/34#v19)</sup> How much less to him that accepteth not the persons of princes, nor regardeth the rich more than the poor? for they all are the work of his hands.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/34#v20)</sup> In a moment shall they die, and the people shall be troubled at midnight, and pass away: and the mighty shall be taken away without hand.

- Elihu asks if one who hates right should govern, and whether it is fit to say to a king, thou art wicked, or to princes, ye are ungodly.
- God accepts not the persons of princes nor regards the rich more than the poor, for they are all the work of his hands; in a moment they die and the mighty are taken away without hand.

**[Job 34:21-23](/bible/job/34#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/34#v21)</sup> For his eyes are upon the ways of man, and he seeth all his goings.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/34#v22)</sup> There is no darkness, nor shadow of death, where the workers of iniquity may hide themselves.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/34#v23)</sup> For he will not lay upon man more than right; that he should enter into judgment with God.

- God's eyes are upon the ways of men and he sees all their goings; there is no darkness nor shadow of death where the workers of iniquity may hide.
- We all come from the world and from sin, and are subject to fall back into those traps if we do not keep our eyes stayed in these commandments.
- The eyes of the Lord are ten times brighter than the sun; once we come into this truth we have an angel that reports on our behalf, so we are not getting away with anything.
- When the books are opened and our lives are laid before the Most High at the throne, the deeds we try to hide will be revealed.
- God will not lay upon man more than right, that he should enter into judgment with God, because we are judged according to our own ways and actions.
- We are held accountable for our own actions; nobody else around us will answer for our actions.

**[Job 34:24-28](/bible/job/34#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/34#v24)</sup> He shall break in pieces mighty men without number, and set others in their stead.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/34#v25)</sup> Therefore he knoweth their works, and he overturneth them in the night, so that they are destroyed.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/job/34#v26)</sup> He striketh them as wicked men in the open sight of others;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/job/34#v27)</sup> Because they turned back from him, and would not consider any of his ways:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/job/34#v28)</sup> So that they cause the cry of the poor to come unto him, and he heareth the cry of the afflicted.

- God breaks in pieces mighty men without number and sets others in their stead, knowing their works and overturning them in the night so they are destroyed.
- He strikes them as wicked men in the open sight of others because they turned back from him and would not consider his ways, so that the cry of the poor comes up to him and he hears the cry of the afflicted.
- That is us now; we are afflicted, and the Most High has heard the prayers of the Israelites here in Babylon the great because the spirit of the Lord is going out and Israel is waking up and returning.
- We have to go out and do the work in the vineyard, helping our people understand who they are and our purpose in Babylon the great, so that our affliction is heard and we can be delivered from this place.

**[Job 34:29-30](/bible/job/34#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/job/34#v29)</sup> When he giveth quietness, who then can make trouble? and when he hideth his face, who then can behold him? whether it be done against a nation, or against a man only:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/job/34#v30)</sup> That the hypocrite reign not, lest the people be ensnared.

- When God gives quietness, who then can make trouble; if the Lord shuts this kingdom down, nobody can stop him.
- When he hides his face, who can behold him, whether it be done against a nation or against a man.
- The Most High hid his face and turned his back on us until we acknowledge our offense and seek his face; we could not make him turn around, but in our affliction we seek him early.
- This was done not just against Adam and Eve but against all their descendants, because we broke the commandments and turned our back on the Most High first, so our punishment was that God turned his back on us.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    The Most High says he will go and return to his place till we acknowledge our offense and seek his face; in our affliction we will seek him early.

**[Job 34:31-33](/bible/job/34#v31)**
> <sup>[31](/bible/job/34#v31)</sup> Surely it is meet to be said unto God, I have borne chastisement, I will not offend any more:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/job/34#v32)</sup> That which I see not teach thou me: if I have done iniquity, I will do no more.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/job/34#v33)</sup> Should it be according to thy mind? he will recompense it, whether thou refuse, or whether thou choose; and not I: therefore speak what thou knowest.

- Surely it is right to say unto God, I have borne chastisement, I will not offend anymore; teach me what I see not, and if I have done iniquity I will do no more.
- Should it be according to Job's mind that God recompenses, whether Job refuses or chooses; Job is told to speak what he knows.

**[Job 34:34-37](/bible/job/34#v34)**
> <sup>[34](/bible/job/34#v34)</sup> Let men of understanding tell me, and let a wise man hearken unto me.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/job/34#v35)</sup> Job hath spoken without knowledge, and his words were without wisdom.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/job/34#v36)</sup> My desire is that Job may be tried unto the end because of his answers for wicked men.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/job/34#v37)</sup> For he addeth rebellion unto his sin, he clappeth his hands among us, and multiplieth his words against God.

- Men of understanding tell Elihu that Job has spoken without knowledge and his words were without wisdom.
- Elihu's desire is that Job may be tried unto the end because of his answers for wicked men, for he added rebellion unto his sin, clapped his hands among them, and multiplied his words against God.
- Study the deep parables Elihu is speaking with your local leadership, your officers and soldiers, because there is much detail in these scriptures that cannot be covered in the four-chapters-a-day reading.

- He teaches us more than the beasts of the earth and makes us wiser than the fowls of heaven; they cry but none gives answer because of the pride of evil men, and God will not hear vanity.

**[Job 35:14-16](/bible/job/35#v14)**

- Because God has visited in his anger yet Job knows it not in great extremity, Job opens his mouth in vain and multiplies words without knowledge.

# Job 36: God Is Mighty and Despises Not Any

Read the chapter → [Job 36](/bible/job/36)

**[Job 36:1-4](/bible/job/36#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/36#v1)</sup> Elihu also proceeded, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/36#v2)</sup> Suffer me a little, and I will shew thee that I have yet to speak on God’s behalf.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/36#v3)</sup> I will fetch my knowledge from afar, and will ascribe righteousness to my Maker.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/36#v4)</sup> For truly my words shall not be false: he that is perfect in knowledge is with thee.

- Elihu proceeds, asking Job to suffer him a little, for he has yet to speak on God's behalf.
- He will fetch his knowledge from afar and ascribe righteousness to his Maker, for his words shall not be false, and he that is perfect in knowledge is with Job.

**[Job 36:5-7](/bible/job/36#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/36#v5)</sup> Behold, God is mighty, and despiseth not any: he is mighty in strength and wisdom.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/36#v6)</sup> He preserveth not the life of the wicked: but giveth right to the poor.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/36#v7)</sup> He withdraweth not his eyes from the righteous: but with kings are they on the throne; yea, he doth establish them for ever, and they are exalted.

- God is mighty and despises not any; he is mighty in strength and wisdom.
- He preserves not the life of the wicked but gives right to the poor; he withdraws not his eyes from the righteous, but with kings on the throne he establishes them forever and they are exalted.

**[Job 36:8-12](/bible/job/36#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/36#v8)</sup> And if they be bound in fetters, and be holden in cords of affliction;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/36#v9)</sup> Then he sheweth them their work, and their transgressions that they have exceeded.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/36#v10)</sup> He openeth also their ear to discipline, and commandeth that they return from iniquity.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/36#v11)</sup> If they obey and serve him, they shall spend their days in prosperity, and their years in pleasures.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/36#v12)</sup> But if they obey not, they shall perish by the sword, and they shall die without knowledge.

- If they be bound in fetters and held in cords of affliction, then he shows them their work and their transgressions that they have exceeded.
- He opens their ear to discipline and commands them to return from iniquity; if they obey and serve him, they spend their days in prosperity and years in pleasure, but if not they perish by the sword and die without knowledge.

**[Job 36:13-16](/bible/job/36#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/36#v13)</sup> But the hypocrites in heart heap up wrath: they cry not when he bindeth them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/36#v14)</sup> They die in youth, and their life is among the unclean.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/36#v15)</sup> He delivereth the poor in his affliction, and openeth their ears in oppression.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/36#v16)</sup> Even so would he have removed thee out of the strait into a broad place, where there is no straitness; and that which should be set on thy table should be full of fatness.

- The hypocrites in heart heap up wrath; they cry not when he binds them, they die in youth and their life is among the unclean.
- He delivers the poor in his affliction and opens their ears in oppression, and even so would have removed Job out of the strait into a broad place with no straitness, his table full of fatness.

**[Job 36:17-21](/bible/job/36#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/36#v17)</sup> But thou hast fulfilled the judgment of the wicked: judgment and justice take hold on thee.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/36#v18)</sup> Because there is wrath, beware lest he take thee away with his stroke: then a great ransom cannot deliver thee.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/36#v19)</sup> Will he esteem thy riches? no, not gold, nor all the forces of strength.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/36#v20)</sup> Desire not the night, when people are cut off in their place.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/36#v21)</sup> Take heed, regard not iniquity: for this hast thou chosen rather than affliction.

- But Job has fulfilled the judgment of the wicked, so judgment and justice take hold on him; because there is wrath, he is warned lest God take him away with his stroke, for then a great ransom cannot deliver him.
- God will not esteem his riches, not gold nor all the forces of strength; Job is told not to desire the night when people are cut off, and to take heed and regard not iniquity, which he has chosen rather than affliction.

**[Job 36:22-25](/bible/job/36#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/36#v22)</sup> Behold, God exalteth by his power: who teacheth like him?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/36#v23)</sup> Who hath enjoined him his way? or who can say, Thou hast wrought iniquity?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/36#v24)</sup> Remember that thou magnify his work, which men behold.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/36#v25)</sup> Every man may see it; man may behold it afar off.

- God exalts by his power; who teaches like him, who has enjoined him his way, or who can say thou hast wrought iniquity.
- Remember to magnify his work which men behold and may see afar off.

**[Job 36:26-29](/bible/job/36#v26)**
> <sup>[26](/bible/job/36#v26)</sup> Behold, God is great, and we know him not, neither can the number of his years be searched out.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/job/36#v27)</sup> For he maketh small the drops of water: they pour down rain according to the vapour thereof:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/job/36#v28)</sup> Which the clouds do drop and distil upon man abundantly.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/job/36#v29)</sup> Also can any understand the spreadings of the clouds, or the noise of his tabernacle?

- God is great and we know him not, neither can the number of his years be searched out.
- He makes small the drops of water; they pour down rain according to the vapor thereof, which the clouds drop and distill upon man abundantly; who can understand the spreadings of the clouds or the noise of his tabernacle.

**[Job 36:30-33](/bible/job/36#v30)**
> <sup>[30](/bible/job/36#v30)</sup> Behold, he spreadeth his light upon it, and covereth the bottom of the sea.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/job/36#v31)</sup> For by them judgeth he the people; he giveth meat in abundance.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/job/36#v32)</sup> With clouds he covereth the light; and commandeth it not to shine by the cloud that cometh betwixt.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/job/36#v33)</sup> The noise thereof sheweth concerning it, the cattle also concerning the vapour.

- He spreads his light upon it and covers the bottom of the sea; by them he judges the people and gives meat in abundance.
- With clouds he covers the light and commands it not to shine; the noise thereof shows concerning it, and the cattle also concerning the vapor.
- Elihu continues to give Job a desolation of the works and doings of the Most High in our afflictions and trials, that nothing on earth is done apart from God, and no man can bring a new revelation or correct what God has put upon him.
