Read the chapter → [Job 1](/bible/job/1)

The book of Job is a very deep book, a book that will not be fully explained here because some of it is above any man's pay grade. No one claims to know everything about Job. If you have questions regarding the book of Job, inbox them to IUIC classrooms and they will be answered in order received. This session covers [Job 1](/bible/job/1) through 4: the introduction of Job, the conversation between Satan and the Most High, the loss of everything Job had, and the beginning of the talk between Job and his three friends.

**[Job 1:1](/bible/job/1#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/1#v1)</sup> There was a man in the land of Uz, whose name was Job; and that man was perfect and upright, and one that feared God, and eschewed evil.

- This first verse tells you who Job is, what land he lived in, and his character. Just because he lived in the land of Uz does not mean he was of that nation, because he was a son of God.
- It is the same thing today: we live in America but we are not Caucasians. There are brothers that live in Europe and they are not Caucasians either, but they are the sons of God.

**[Job 1:2-5](/bible/job/1#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/1#v2)</sup> And there were born unto him seven sons and three daughters.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/1#v3)</sup> His substance also was seven thousand sheep, and three thousand camels, and five hundred yoke of oxen, and five hundred she asses, and a very great household; so that this man was the greatest of all the men of the east.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/1#v4)</sup> And his sons went and feasted in their houses, every one his day; and sent and called for their three sisters to eat and to drink with them.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/1#v5)</sup> And it was so, when the days of their feasting were gone about, that Job sent and sanctified them, and rose up early in the morning, and offered burnt offerings according to the number of them all: for Job said, It may be that my sons have sinned, and cursed God in their hearts. Thus did Job continually.

- Job had seven sons and three daughters, and his substance was seven thousand sheep, three thousand camels, five hundred yoke of oxen and five hundred she asses, so that this man was the greatest of all the men of the east.
- His sons went and feasted in their houses, every one his day. When it says "every man his day," they were talking about their birthdays. Whenever somebody's birthday came around they would say, "this is my day, this is my day."
- Now people have gotten to the point where it is, "this is my month, my birthday month, so I am celebrating the whole month." Same thing with this.
- Job sanctified them and rose up early to offer burnt offerings according to the number of them all, for Job said it may be that his sons had sinned and cursed God in their hearts. Thus did Job continually.
  - **[Job 8:4](/bible/job/8#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/job/8#v4)</sup> If thy children have sinned against him, and he have cast them away for their transgression;

    Bildad said if your children sinned against God, He is going to take them away; their sin was celebrating their birthdays, which is not a custom of Israel.

**[Job 1:6](/bible/job/1#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/1#v6)</sup> Now there was a day when the sons of God came to present themselves before the Lord, and Satan came also among them.

- When it says "the sons of God," this is talking about the line, the lineage of Adam. Before we were called Israelites we were called the sons of God, and even still today we are called the sons of the living God.
- So this is talking about the same thing; it is not just talking about angels alone.
  - **[Exodus 23:17](/bible/exodus/23#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/23#v17)</sup> Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord God.

    Three times in the year all thy males shall appear before the Lord, the same thing being said in Job.

**[Job 1:7-8](/bible/job/1#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/1#v7)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, Whence comest thou? Then Satan answered the Lord, and said, From going to and fro in the earth, and from walking up and down in it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/1#v8)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, Hast thou considered my servant Job, that there is none like him in the earth, a perfect and an upright man, one that feareth God, and escheweth evil?

- This is Satan talking to the Lord. The Lord says, have you considered My servant Job, have you ever tried Job, you walking up and down the earth, have you tried My servant, because he is an upright man.
- It is the same thing with us today. Every time we do more good works, the harder Satan comes at you. When you fast, the trials get worse, because you are showing God that you are upright through your fasting, striving to be that righteous vessel He wants you to be.
- So in order to see how strong you are, Satan is going to try you, and he is always going to go to the Lord for permission.

**[Job 1:9-12](/bible/job/1#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/1#v9)</sup> Then Satan answered the Lord, and said, Doth Job fear God for nought?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/1#v10)</sup> Hast not thou made an hedge about him, and about his house, and about all that he hath on every side? thou hast blessed the work of his hands, and his substance is increased in the land.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/1#v11)</sup> But put forth thine hand now, and touch all that he hath, and he will curse thee to thy face.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/1#v12)</sup> And the Lord said unto Satan, Behold, all that he hath is in thy power; only upon himself put not forth thine hand. So Satan went forth from the presence of the Lord.

- Satan said, does Job fear God for nothing? Have you not made a hedge about him and blessed the work of his hands? Satan is telling the Most High that Job is only worshiping Him because He gives him a lot of stuff.
- That is the same thing with us today. When everything is going good you do not hear nothing from us; as soon as things go bad, that is when we start complaining and moaning and groaning.
- Satan said put forth thine hand and touch all that he hath, and he will curse thee to thy face. The Lord said, behold, all that he hath is in thy power, only upon himself put not forth thine hand.
- In Christianity they teach that Satan and God had a fight and Satan rebelled and took a third of the angels. That does not sound like what is right here. Job was the subject of discussion between Satan and the Lord, and Satan needed permission from the Lord to mess with him.
- A lot of times in Christianity people think everything bad comes from the devil and everything good comes from God. Wrong. Both good and evil come from the Lord. Satan is not a free spirit out here doing his own thing; he is subject to God, much like us.
  - **[James 2:19](/bible/james/2#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/james/2#v19)</sup> Thou believest that there is one God; thou doest well: the devils also believe, and tremble.

    The devils believe there is one God and tremble; the difference is the devils tremble at the fear of God, and in this flesh our people have a tendency not to fear Him.

**[Job 1:13-19](/bible/job/1#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/1#v13)</sup> And there was a day when his sons and his daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/1#v14)</sup> And there came a messenger unto Job, and said, The oxen were plowing, and the asses feeding beside them:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/1#v15)</sup> And the Sabeans fell upon them, and took them away; yea, they have slain the servants with the edge of the sword; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/1#v16)</sup> While he was yet speaking, there came also another, and said, The fire of God is fallen from heaven, and hath burned up the sheep, and the servants, and consumed them; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/1#v17)</sup> While he was yet speaking, there came also another, and said, The Chaldeans made out three bands, and fell upon the camels, and have carried them away, yea, and slain the servants with the edge of the sword; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/1#v18)</sup> While he was yet speaking, there came also another, and said, Thy sons and thy daughters were eating and drinking wine in their eldest brother’s house:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/1#v19)</sup> And, behold, there came a great wind from the wilderness, and smote the four corners of the house, and it fell upon the young men, and they are dead; and I only am escaped alone to tell thee.

- Job lost his oxen: the Sabeans fell upon them, took them away, and slew the servants with the edge of the sword. That is problem number one.
- While the first messenger was yet speaking, another came: the fire of God fell from heaven and burned up the sheep and the servants. That is problem number two.
- While he was yet speaking, another came: the Chaldeans made out three bands, fell upon the camels, carried them away, and slew the servants. That is problem number three.
- While he was yet speaking, another came: a great wind from the wilderness smote the four corners of the house and it fell upon the young men and they are dead. Job just lost his cattle, his servants, and his children. Job just lost everything.
- Why? Because the devil, with the permission of the Lord, was able to put his hand on Job to try him. That is what we need as Israelites; when we do certain things the Lord gives us certain things, and He has to try them to see if the heart is still going to be the same way.
  - **[Proverbs 30:8-9](/bible/proverbs/30#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/30#v8)</sup> Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me:
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/30#v9)</sup> Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the Lord? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain.

    Give me neither poverty nor riches, lest I be full and deny thee, or lest I be poor and steal and take the name of my God in vain.

**[Job 1:20-22](/bible/job/1#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/1#v20)</sup> Then Job arose, and rent his mantle, and shaved his head, and fell down upon the ground, and worshipped,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/1#v21)</sup> And said, Naked came I out of my mother’s womb, and naked shall I return thither: the Lord gave, and the Lord hath taken away; blessed be the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/1#v22)</sup> In all this Job sinned not, nor charged God foolishly.

- Job rent his mantle, shaved his head, fell down and worshiped, and said naked came I out of my mother's womb and naked shall I return; the Lord gave and the Lord hath taken away, blessed be the name of the Lord. In all this Job sinned not nor charged God foolishly.
- Job understood it: everything that we have comes from the Lord, everything that we get comes from the Lord.
- What good is it for us to blame God for stuff that we lose, when He can give it right back to you? There is nothing in this world that you can get yourself without the help of the Lord, unless you serve Satan and he gives it to you, but do you really want to pay that price?
- Even then it was the Lord that gave it to you, because the Lord handed you over to Satan; He put that stumbling block before you and you fell over it.
  - **[Isaiah 45:7](/bible/isaiah/45#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/45#v7)</sup> I form the light, and create darkness: I make peace, and create evil: I the Lord do all these things.

    I form the light and create darkness, I make peace and create evil, I the Lord do all these things.
