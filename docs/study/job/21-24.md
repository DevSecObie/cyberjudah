Read the chapter → [Job 21](/bible/job/21)

This session covers [Job 21](/bible/job/21) through 24 on the feast of dedication. Job answers his friends by pressing the hard question they keep dodging: if calamity always falls on the wicked, then why do the wicked live, grow old, and grow mighty in power? Job walks through the visible prosperity of the wicked, the twofold reckoning of the wicked of Israel and the wicked of Esau, and the certainty that the wicked are reserved to the day of destruction. Eliphaz answers back in [Job 22](/bible/job/22) with accusations and a call to acquaint yourself with the Most High, Job replies in [Job 23](/bible/job/23) that he would come even to the seat of God and come forth as gold, and [Job 24](/bible/job/24) lays out the crimes of the oppressor who removes the landmarks and gathers all nations to himself.

- Mark me and be astonished, and lay your hand upon your mouth. When Job remembers, he is afraid, and trembling takes hold on his flesh.

# Job 23: I Shall Come Forth As Gold

Read the chapter → [Job 23](/bible/job/23)

**[Job 23:1-4](/bible/job/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/23#v1)</sup> Then Job answered and said,
>

> <sup>[3](/bible/job/23#v3)</sup> Oh that I knew where I might find him! that I might come even to his seat!
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/23#v4)</sup> I would order my cause before him, and fill my mouth with arguments.

- Then Job answers: even today is my complaint bitter, my stroke is heavier than my groaning. He does have a complaint, and the stroke on him is heavier than the sound of his groaning.
- Oh that I knew where I might find him, that I might come even to his seat. I would order my cause before him and fill my mouth with arguments. Job wants to bring his case straight to God's seat.
  - **[Hebrews 4:16](/bible/hebrews/4#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/hebrews/4#v16)</sup> Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.

    Let us come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy and find grace to help in time of need; we go straight to Christ our high priest.

**[Job 23:5-9](/bible/job/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/job/23#v5)</sup> I would know the words which he would answer me, and understand what he would say unto me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/23#v6)</sup> Will he plead against me with his great power? No; but he would put strength in me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/23#v7)</sup> There the righteous might dispute with him; so should I be delivered for ever from my judge.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/23#v8)</sup> Behold, I go forward, but he is not there; and backward, but I cannot perceive him:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/23#v9)</sup> On the left hand, where he doth work, but I cannot behold him: he hideth himself on the right hand, that I cannot see him:

- I would know the words he would answer me and understand what he would say. Will he plead against me with his great power? No, but he would put strength in me. Job comes to the Lord pointing to the very promises in the word and asking God to fulfill them in him.
- The Most High has many names for different reasons, called on when you build up your prayer life: Jehovah Nissi when he won against Amalek, Jehovah Tsidkenu when you need him to be your righteousness.
- There the righteous might dispute with him, and Job would be delivered forever from his judge. Job goes forward but God is not there, backward but cannot perceive him, on the left and right he cannot behold him. Like Jacob wrestling with God, we war against principalities to come out victorious.

**[Job 23:10-12](/bible/job/23#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/job/23#v10)</sup> But he knoweth the way that I take: when he hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/23#v11)</sup> My foot hath held his steps, his way have I kept, and not declined.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/23#v12)</sup> Neither have I gone back from the commandment of his lips; I have esteemed the words of his mouth more than my necessary food.

- But he knoweth the way that I take; when he hath tried me, I shall come forth as gold. Job's foot has held his steps, he has kept God's way and not declined. Regardless of what he is going through, Job keeps the commandments.
- Neither has Job gone back from the commandment of his lips; he has esteemed the words of his mouth more than his necessary food. Let us see if Job was moving in the spirit of Christ when he said that.
  - **[John 4:32-34](/bible/john/4#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/john/4#v32)</sup> But he said unto them, I have meat to eat that ye know not of.
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/john/4#v33)</sup> Therefore said the disciples one to another, Hath any man brought him ought to eat?
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/john/4#v34)</sup> Jesus saith unto them, My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work.

    Christ said, I have meat to eat that you know not of; my meat is to do the will of him that sent me and to finish his work.

**[Job 23:13-17](/bible/job/23#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/job/23#v13)</sup> But he is in one mind, and who can turn him? and what his soul desireth, even that he doeth.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/23#v14)</sup> For he performeth the thing that is appointed for me: and many such things are with him.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/23#v15)</sup> Therefore am I troubled at his presence: when I consider, I am afraid of him.

>

- But he is in one mind, and who can turn him? What his soul desireth, even that he doeth. In another scripture he says, who can prevent me? No one can stop God from doing what he wants.
- He performeth the thing that is appointed for me, and many such things are with him. Therefore Job is troubled at his presence; when he considers it, he is afraid of him.
- God maketh his heart soft and the Almighty troubleth him, because he was not cut off before the darkness, neither has he covered the darkness from Job's face.
