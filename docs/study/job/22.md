**[Job 22:1-5](/bible/job/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/22#v1)</sup> Then Eliphaz the Temanite answered and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/22#v2)</sup> Can a man be profitable unto God, as he that is wise may be profitable unto himself?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/22#v3)</sup> Is it any pleasure to the Almighty, that thou art righteous? or is it gain to him, that thou makest thy ways perfect?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/22#v4)</sup> Will he reprove thee for fear of thee? will he enter with thee into judgment?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/22#v5)</sup> Is not thy wickedness great? and thine iniquities infinite?

- Then Eliphaz the Temanite answers: can a man be profitable unto God, as he that is wise is profitable to himself? Is it any pleasure to the Almighty that you are righteous, or gain that you make your ways perfect?
- Will he reprove you for fear of you? Will he enter with you into judgment? Is not your wickedness great and your iniquity infinite? Eliphaz throws Job's righteousness back as filthy rags, as if keeping the commandments profits nothing.
- But Christ told us if we keep the commandments and follow him, we will rule on thrones with him, so there is falsehood in Eliphaz's answer.

**[Job 22:6-11](/bible/job/22#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/job/22#v6)</sup> For thou hast taken a pledge from thy brother for nought, and stripped the naked of their clothing.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/22#v7)</sup> Thou hast not given water to the weary to drink, and thou hast withholden bread from the hungry.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/22#v8)</sup> But as for the mighty man, he had the earth; and the honourable man dwelt in it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/22#v9)</sup> Thou hast sent widows away empty, and the arms of the fatherless have been broken.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/22#v10)</sup> Therefore snares are round about thee, and sudden fear troubleth thee;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/22#v11)</sup> Or darkness, that thou canst not see; and abundance of waters cover thee.

- Eliphaz accuses Job: you have taken a pledge from your brother for naught and stripped the naked of their clothing, withheld water from the weary and bread from the hungry, sent widows away empty, and broken the arms of the fatherless.
- Therefore snares are round about you, and sudden fear troubleth you. Eliphaz claims Job's suffering proves Job was sinning, but the Lord was only trying him.
- The truth is the opposite: when Job had his riches he dealt righteously, helped people, taught people, and was looked up to like an elder in the city.

    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/jonah/3#v8)</sup> But let man and beast be covered with sackcloth, and cry mightily unto God: yea, let them turn every one from his evil way, and from the violence that is in their hands.

**[Job 22:15-20](/bible/job/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/job/22#v15)</sup> Hast thou marked the old way which wicked men have trodden?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/22#v16)</sup> Which were cut down out of time, whose foundation was overflown with a flood:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/22#v17)</sup> Which said unto God, Depart from us: and what can the Almighty do for them?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/22#v18)</sup> Yet he filled their houses with good things: but the counsel of the wicked is far from me.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/22#v19)</sup> The righteous see it, and are glad: and the innocent laugh them to scorn.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/22#v20)</sup> Whereas our substance is not cut down, but the remnant of them the fire consumeth.

- Have you marked the old way which wicked men have trodden, which were cut down out of time, whose foundation was overthrown with a flood? This points to the men destroyed in the flood.
- They said unto God, depart from us, and what can the Almighty do for them, yet he filled their houses with good things. But the counsel of the wicked is far from Job.
- The righteous see it and are glad, and the innocent laugh them to scorn, while their substance is not cut down but the remnant of them the fire consumes.

**[Job 22:21-25](/bible/job/22#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/job/22#v21)</sup> Acquaint now thyself with him, and be at peace: thereby good shall come unto thee.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/22#v22)</sup> Receive, I pray thee, the law from his mouth, and lay up his words in thine heart.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/22#v23)</sup> If thou return to the Almighty, thou shalt be built up, thou shalt put away iniquity far from thy tabernacles.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/22#v24)</sup> Then shalt thou lay up gold as dust, and the gold of Ophir as the stones of the brooks.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/22#v25)</sup> Yea, the Almighty shall be thy defence, and thou shalt have plenty of silver.

- Acquaint now thyself with him and be at peace; thereby good shall come unto thee. You do not acquaint yourself with God by whispering that you love him; you hear from God through his prophets and his people to whom he gave his word.
- Receive the law from his mouth and lay up his words in your heart. If you return to the Almighty you shall be built up and put away iniquity far from your tabernacle. This was our job, to build up our people.

**[Job 22:26-30](/bible/job/22#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/job/22#v26)</sup> For then shalt thou have thy delight in the Almighty, and shalt lift up thy face unto God.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/job/22#v27)</sup> Thou shalt make thy prayer unto him, and he shall hear thee, and thou shalt pay thy vows.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/job/22#v28)</sup> Thou shalt also decree a thing, and it shall be established unto thee: and the light shall shine upon thy ways.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/job/22#v29)</sup> When men are cast down, then thou shalt say, There is lifting up; and he shall save the humble person.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/job/22#v30)</sup> He shall deliver the island of the innocent: and it is delivered by the pureness of thine hands.

- Then you shall have your delight in the Almighty and lift up your face unto God. You shall make your prayer unto him and he shall hear you, for he hears you when you keep the commandments.
- You shall pay your vow, the original agreement you made in the covenant to keep his commandments. You shall decree a thing and it shall be established, and the light shall shine upon your ways.
- When men are cast down you shall say there is lifting up, and he shall save the humble person. He shall deliver the island of the innocent, delivered by the pureness of your hands.
