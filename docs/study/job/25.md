Read the chapter → [Job 25](/bible/job/25)

This session covers [Job 25](/bible/job/25) through 28, a short reading held to be full of parables, similitudes, and double meanings, so it is a hard book to take straight off the top. Bildad the Shuhite speaks his final short answer, and then Job answers him. Job flexes his own knowledge to show his so-called friend that his wisdom is not superior, and moves deeper into his integrity, his righteousness, and his acts and thoughts. The thread running through these chapters is integrity, the fear of the Lord, and the wisdom that is above rubies, given only to the remnant of Israel and not to the heathen. The teaching runs the wicked man and the rich nations up against the coming judgment, the nukes that fall as the east wind, and the promise that the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.

**[Job 25:1-6](/bible/job/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/25#v1)</sup> Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/25#v2)</sup> Dominion and fear are with him, he maketh peace in his high places.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/25#v3)</sup> Is there any number of his armies? and upon whom doth not his light arise?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/25#v4)</sup> How then can man be justified with God? or how can he be clean that is born of a woman?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/25#v5)</sup> Behold even to the moon, and it shineth not; yea, the stars are not pure in his sight.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/25#v6)</sup> How much less man, that is a worm? and the son of man, which is a worm?

- Bildad the Shuhite gives his last short answer in this conversation with Job, saying dominion and fear are with the Most High and he maketh peace in his high places.
- He asks whether there is any number of his armies and upon whom his light does not arise, then how can man be justified with God, or how can he be clean that is born of a woman.
- Bildad says that even the moon does not shine and the stars are not pure in his sight, so how much less is man, who is a worm, and the son of man, which is a worm.
