Read the chapter → [Job 12](/bible/job/12)

Job answers again, no longer inferior to his friends, declaring that he too has understanding and already knows the things they tell him. He observes that the tabernacles of robbers prosper and that those who provoke God are secure, and he sees this both in his own day and prophetically in the wicked nation to come. He then magnifies the Most High as the one with whom is strength and wisdom, who owns both the deceived and the deceiver, who leads counselors away spoiled, pours contempt upon princes, increases and destroys nations, and takes away the heart of the chief of the people, all of which he applies to the state of Israel.

**[Job 12:1-3](/bible/job/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/12#v1)</sup> And Job answered and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/12#v2)</sup> No doubt but ye are the people, and wisdom shall die with you.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/12#v3)</sup> But I have understanding as well as you; I am not inferior to you: yea, who knoweth not such things as these?

- Job answers, no doubt but ye are the people and wisdom shall die with you, but Job has understanding as well as they and is not inferior to them.
- Everything they tell him he already knows; he studies, prays, and applies, and he knows that if he sins the Most High will come after him, and if he does not repent and lets wickedness dwell in his tabernacle the Most High will destroy him.
- This is like counsel that does not help; when you lose your phone and a friend asks where the last place you had it was, you would already have checked the last place, so that does not help.

**[Job 12:4-6](/bible/job/12#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/job/12#v4)</sup> I am as one mocked of his neighbour, who calleth upon God, and he answereth him: the just upright man is laughed to scorn.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/12#v5)</sup> He that is ready to slip with his feet is as a lamp despised in the thought of him that is at ease.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/12#v6)</sup> The tabernacles of robbers prosper, and they that provoke God are secure; into whose hand God bringeth abundantly.

- Job is as one mocked of his neighbor, who calleth upon God and he answereth him; the just upright man is laughed to scorn.
- He that is ready to slip with his feet is as a lamp despised in the thought of him that is at ease.
- The tabernacles of robbers prosper and they that provoke God are secure, into whose hand God bringeth abundantly.
- Even looking at America, the tabernacles of robbers prosper; America has raped, robbed, and pillaged basically every nation on the face of the earth and is prospering right now.
- The Most High gave Job the sight to see that in the future the wicked just continue to prosper, into whose hand God brings abundant countries and abundant wealth, which is how America became the number one superpower on the whole earth.

**[Job 12:12-16](/bible/job/12#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/job/12#v12)</sup> With the ancient is wisdom; and in length of days understanding.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/12#v13)</sup> With him is wisdom and strength, he hath counsel and understanding.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/12#v14)</sup> Behold, he breaketh down, and it cannot be built again: he shutteth up a man, and there can be no opening.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/12#v15)</sup> Behold, he withholdeth the waters, and they dry up: also he sendeth them out, and they overturn the earth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/12#v16)</sup> With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his.

- With the ancient is wisdom, and in length of days understanding.
- The Most High breaketh down and it cannot be built up again; he shutteth up a man and there can be no opening.
- He withholdeth the waters and they dry up; he sendeth them out and they overturn the earth.
- With him is strength and wisdom; the deceived and the deceiver are his. This is a heavy precept, because the Most High uses anybody he wants and the deceived and the deceiver are ordained by him.
- A lot of people think the Most High is all hugs and kisses, roses and daisies, but he is loving and he will also lay it to you; the devil, Satan, works for the Most High, and nothing can be done without him allowing it, for they are his.

    If the prophet be deceived when he hath spoken a thing, the Most High hath deceived that prophet and will stretch out his hand and destroy him from the midst of his people Israel.

    The Most High on his throne asks who shall entice Ahab, and a spirit comes forth and says he will go be a lying spirit in the mouth of all Ahab's prophets, and the Most High sends him to prevail.

**[Job 12:21-25](/bible/job/12#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/job/12#v21)</sup> He poureth contempt upon princes, and weakeneth the strength of the mighty.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/12#v22)</sup> He discovereth deep things out of darkness, and bringeth out to light the shadow of death.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/12#v23)</sup> He increaseth the nations, and destroyeth them: he enlargeth the nations, and straiteneth them again.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/12#v24)</sup> He taketh away the heart of the chief of the people of the earth, and causeth them to wander in a wilderness where there is no way.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/12#v25)</sup> They grope in the dark without light, and he maketh them to stagger like a drunken man.

- He poureth contempt upon princes and weakeneth the strength of the mighty.
- He discovereth deep things out of darkness and bringeth out to light the shadow of death.
- He increaseth the nations and destroyeth them; he enlargeth the nations and straiteneth them again. This is us, Israel, for the curses destroyed us to the point where we do not realize we are the Israelites, God's chosen people, and then he heals us back up again.
- He taketh away the heart of the chief of the people of the earth and causeth them to wander in a wilderness where there is no way.
- They grope in the dark without light and he maketh them to stagger like a drunken man; this is [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), and you see the aged of the people lose their wisdom and the strong lose their might, like Muhammad Ali and others we thought were noblemen for the nation of Israel.
- Look at what they did to the northern kingdom, taking away the heart of the chief, the mighty warriors, infiltrating them with alcohol and diseases, then sending them on their way with smallpox blankets so they stagger like drunken men; many of our northern kingdom brothers, especially Gad, deal with drunkenness, opium, and the war on drugs, but we keep the commandments and are coming back to be straightened out.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:29](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v29)</sup> And thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways: and thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save thee.

    Thou shalt grope at noonday as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways, describing how our people stagger though the light is brightest.
