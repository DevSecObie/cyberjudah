**[Job 3:1-4](/bible/job/3#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/3#v1)</sup> After this opened Job his mouth, and cursed his day.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/3#v2)</sup> And Job spake, and said,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/3#v3)</sup> Let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night in which it was said, There is a man child conceived.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/3#v4)</sup> Let that day be darkness; let not God regard it from above, neither let the light shine upon it.

- After this Job opened his mouth and cursed his day. Why did he curse his day? Because his children were celebrating their day, and Job cursed the day he was born.
- Job said, let the day perish wherein I was born, and the night in which it was said, there is a man child conceived; let that day be darkness, let not God regard it from above, neither let the light shine upon it.
- Job is going in on his birthday. That is why we say to hell with our birthday, our birthday means nothing. The scriptures say better is the day of one's death than the day of one's birth, because when you are born there are no works yet; when you die that is when people remember your works and what kind of person you were.

**[Job 3:5-10](/bible/job/3#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/3#v5)</sup> Let darkness and the shadow of death stain it; let a cloud dwell upon it; let the blackness of the day terrify it.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/3#v6)</sup> As for that night, let darkness seize upon it; let it not be joined unto the days of the year, let it not come into the number of the months.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/3#v7)</sup> Lo, let that night be solitary, let no joyful voice come therein.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/3#v8)</sup> Let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to raise up their mourning.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/3#v9)</sup> Let the stars of the twilight thereof be dark; let it look for light, but have none; neither let it see the dawning of the day:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/3#v10)</sup> Because it shut not up the doors of my mother’s womb, nor hid sorrow from mine eyes.

- Let darkness and the shadow of death stain it, let a cloud dwell upon it, let the blackness of the day terrify it; as for that night, let darkness seize upon it, let it not be joined unto the days of the year.
- Let that night be solitary, let no joyful voice come therein, let them curse it that curse the day, who are ready to raise up their mourning. Let the stars of the twilight thereof be dark; let it look for light, but have none.
- Because it shut not up the doors of my mother's womb, nor hid sorrow from mine eyes. Job is not cursing anything else but his birth right here; all the stuff he is going through, he is saying, man, I just should have never been born.

**[Job 3:11-19](/bible/job/3#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/3#v11)</sup> Why died I not from the womb? why did I not give up the ghost when I came out of the belly?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/3#v12)</sup> Why did the knees prevent me? or why the breasts that I should suck?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/3#v13)</sup> For now should I have lain still and been quiet, I should have slept: then had I been at rest,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/3#v14)</sup> With kings and counsellors of the earth, which built desolate places for themselves;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/3#v15)</sup> Or with princes that had gold, who filled their houses with silver:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/3#v16)</sup> Or as an hidden untimely birth I had not been; as infants which never saw light.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/3#v17)</sup> There the wicked cease from troubling; and there the weary be at rest.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/3#v18)</sup> There the prisoners rest together; they hear not the voice of the oppressor.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/3#v19)</sup> The small and great are there; and the servant is free from his master.

- Why died I not from the womb? Why did I not give up the ghost when I came out of the belly? Job said I should have died at my birth, I should have died stillborn, I should have died in the womb.
- For now I should have lain still and been quiet, I should have slept, then had I been at rest, with kings and counsellors of the earth. Or as a hidden untimely birth I had not been, as infants which never saw light. He is talking about miscarriages: why wasn't I miscarried, why did I have to come into this world?
- There the wicked cease from troubling. When wicked people die, that is it, they can do no more wrong. When Esau is dead the earth is not going to be troubled anymore. There the prisoners rest together, they hear not the voice of the oppressor.
- This is why in New Orleans there is a celebration when people die; they call it a second line, and it goes all the way back to slave times. They are celebrating the fact that the prisoners rest together and hear not the voice of the oppressor; they are celebrating that they are not in slavery anymore.
- The small and great are there, and the servant is free from his master. Wherefore is light given to him that is in misery, and life unto the bitter in soul?

**[Job 3:20-26](/bible/job/3#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/3#v20)</sup> Wherefore is light given to him that is in misery, and life unto the bitter in soul;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/3#v21)</sup> Which long for death, but it cometh not; and dig for it more than for hid treasures;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/3#v22)</sup> Which rejoice exceedingly, and are glad, when they can find the grave?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/3#v23)</sup> Why is light given to a man whose way is hid, and whom God hath hedged in?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/3#v24)</sup> For my sighing cometh before I eat, and my roarings are poured out like the waters.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/3#v25)</sup> For the thing which I greatly feared is come upon me, and that which I was afraid of is come unto me.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/job/3#v26)</sup> I was not in safety, neither had I rest, neither was I quiet; yet trouble came.

- Which long for death, but it cometh not, and dig for it more than for hid treasures. It has got to be some serious trouble and anguish that you are in for you to only think about dying. Some of us have gone through situations where we would rather just be dead, but sometimes we go through things like that in order to be stronger.
- Which rejoice exceedingly, and are glad when they can find the grave. If you have had a grandmother or aunt or uncle that was very old, going to dialysis, getting blood transfusions and chemo, after a while they are ready to leave, ready to go. The scriptures say death is better than continual sickness.
- Why is light given to a man whose way is hid, and whom God hath hedged in? For my sighing cometh before I eat, and my roarings are poured out like the waters. For the thing which I greatly feared is come upon me, and that which I was afraid of is come unto me.
- I was not in safety, neither had I rest, neither was I quiet; yet trouble came. It was not like everything was going hunky dory for Job; he was already going through some stuff, and trouble came anyway. He is saying, man, what am I doing wrong?
