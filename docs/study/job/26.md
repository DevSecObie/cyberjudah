**[Job 26:1-7](/bible/job/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/26#v1)</sup> But Job answered and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/26#v2)</sup> How hast thou helped him that is without power? how savest thou the arm that hath no strength?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/26#v3)</sup> How hast thou counselled him that hath no wisdom? and how hast thou plentifully declared the thing as it is?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/26#v4)</sup> To whom hast thou uttered words? and whose spirit came from thee?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/26#v5)</sup> Dead things are formed from under the waters, and the inhabitants thereof.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/26#v6)</sup> Hell is naked before him, and destruction hath no covering.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/26#v7)</sup> He stretcheth out the north over the empty place, and hangeth the earth upon nothing.

- Job answers Bildad, asking how he has helped him that is without power, how he saves the arm that has no strength, how he has counseled him that has no wisdom, and how he has plentifully declared the thing.
- Job drops that wisdom, saying dead things are formed from under the waters and the inhabitations thereof; going back to Genesis, the fish, the birds of the air, and the things the Most High created were formed from the waters by his word and his works alone.
- He says hell is naked before him and destruction has no covering, and that he stretcheth out the north over the empty place and hangeth the earth upon nothing.
- On the flat earth idea, scripture says he hangeth the earth upon nothing; the Bible does not substantiate Greek mythology where Atlas or a man under the earth holds it up on his back.
- The earth is a circle; everything else in the heavens is a full circle, the sun which is a star, the moon, the planets, so it makes no sense to say everything is circular except the earth. This is something Esau made up and distributed throughout the minds of Israelite brothers and sisters worldwide, and it has to be cut.
  - **[Isaiah 40:22](/bible/isaiah/40#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/40#v22)</sup> It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers; that stretcheth out the heavens as a curtain, and spreadeth them out as a tent to dwell in:

    He that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, a full circle and not a plate or a flat surface, stretcheth out the heavens as a curtain and a tent to dwell in.

**[Job 26:8-14](/bible/job/26#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/job/26#v8)</sup> He bindeth up the waters in his thick clouds; and the cloud is not rent under them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/26#v9)</sup> He holdeth back the face of his throne, and spreadeth his cloud upon it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/26#v10)</sup> He hath compassed the waters with bounds, until the day and night come to an end.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/26#v11)</sup> The pillars of heaven tremble and are astonished at his reproof.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/26#v12)</sup> He divideth the sea with his power, and by his understanding he smiteth through the proud.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/26#v13)</sup> By his spirit he hath garnished the heavens; his hand hath formed the crooked serpent.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/26#v14)</sup> Lo, these are parts of his ways: but how little a portion is heard of him? but the thunder of his power who can understand?

- Job says he bindeth up the waters in his thick clouds and the cloud is not rent under them, he holdeth back the face of his throne and spreadeth his cloud upon it.
- He hath compassed the waters with bounds until the day and night come to an end, the pillars of heaven tremble and are astonished at his reproof, and he divideth the sea with his power and by his understanding he smiteth through the proud.
- By his spirit he hath garnished the heavens and his hand hath formed the crooked serpent; these are parts of his ways, but how little a portion is heard of him, and the thunder of his power who can understand.
- This is Job showing his understanding, his integrity, and his righteousness; he had to flex his knowledge in the previous chapters to let Bildad know that his so-called wisdom is not superior and that Job is not inferior to him.
