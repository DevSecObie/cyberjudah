Read the chapter → [Job 17](/bible/job/17)

This session of "4 Chapters a Day" moves through [Job 17](/bible/job/17) to 20, and it was read straight through with only a few points of exposition, because the class fell on the closing of the feast and the new moon and many were already traveling to the school feast. The spirit was put on the bishop and the leaders to have the congregation read four chapters a day and read the Bible in a year, and we started in Genesis and are now up to Job. In these chapters Job continues to answer his friends: his breath is corrupt and his days are extinct, Bildad the Shuhite paints the ruin of the wicked, Job cries that God has overthrown him and yet declares that his Redeemer liveth, and Zophar the Naamathite says the triumphing of the wicked is short. The teacher held these chapters lightly, told the class he was not going through precepts today and to post any questions to the captains, and pointed to the places where the reading is going into things that are happening right now.

**[Job 17:1-2](/bible/job/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/17#v1)</sup> My breath is corrupt, my days are extinct, the graves are ready for me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/17#v2)</sup> Are there not mockers with me? and doth not mine eye continue in their provocation?

- Job says his breath is corrupt, his days are extinct, and the graves are ready for him.
- He is surrounded by mockers, and his eye continues in their provocation, so his complaint is that even now the men around him do not comfort him but scorn him.

**[Job 17:6-9](/bible/job/17#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/job/17#v6)</sup> He hath made me also a byword of the people; and aforetime I was as a tabret.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/17#v7)</sup> Mine eye also is dim by reason of sorrow, and all my members are as a shadow.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/17#v8)</sup> Upright men shall be astonied at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/17#v9)</sup> The righteous also shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger.

- Job has been made a byword of the people, a man in whose face others spit, and by reason of sorrow all his members are as a shadow.
- Even so, upright men shall be astonished at this, and the innocent shall stir up himself against the hypocrite.
- The righteous shall hold on his way, and he that hath clean hands shall be stronger and stronger, so the trial does not turn the righteous back but presses him forward.

**[Job 17:11-16](/bible/job/17#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/job/17#v11)</sup> My days are past, my purposes are broken off, even the thoughts of my heart.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/17#v12)</sup> They change the night into day: the light is short because of darkness.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/17#v13)</sup> If I wait, the grave is mine house: I have made my bed in the darkness.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/17#v14)</sup> I have said to corruption, Thou art my father: to the worm, Thou art my mother, and my sister.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/17#v15)</sup> And where is now my hope? as for my hope, who shall see it?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/17#v16)</sup> They shall go down to the bars of the pit, when our rest together is in the dust.

- Job's purposes are broken off and the thoughts of his heart are undone, so that he speaks as a man with no future left in this life.
- He has made his bed in the darkness, called corruption his father and the worm his mother and sister, and asks where his hope now is.
- His hope goes down with him to the bars of the pit, when their rest together is in the dust, which is Job speaking as one who looks only to the grave.

# Job 18: The Light of the Wicked Shall Be Put Out

Read the chapter → [Job 18](/bible/job/18)

**[Job 18:1-4](/bible/job/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/18#v1)</sup> Then answered Bildad the Shuhite, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/18#v2)</sup> How long will it be ere ye make an end of words? mark, and afterwards we will speak.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/18#v3)</sup> Wherefore are we counted as beasts, and reputed vile in your sight?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/18#v4)</sup> He teareth himself in his anger: shall the earth be forsaken for thee? and shall the rock be removed out of his place?

- Bildad the Shuhite answers and asks Job how long it will be before he makes an end of words, telling him to mark, and afterward they will speak.
- He is offended that Job counts them as beasts and reputes them vile, and says Job tears himself in his anger, as if the earth should be forsaken for his sake.

**[Job 18:5-11](/bible/job/18#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/job/18#v5)</sup> Yea, the light of the wicked shall be put out, and the spark of his fire shall not shine.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/18#v6)</sup> The light shall be dark in his tabernacle, and his candle shall be put out with him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/18#v7)</sup> The steps of his strength shall be straitened, and his own counsel shall cast him down.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/18#v8)</sup> For he is cast into a net by his own feet, and he walketh upon a snare.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/18#v9)</sup> The gin shall take him by the heel, and the robber shall prevail against him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/18#v10)</sup> The snare is laid for him in the ground, and a trap for him in the way.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/18#v11)</sup> Terrors shall make him afraid on every side, and shall drive him to his feet.

- Bildad lays out the doctrine that the light of the wicked shall be put out and the spark of his fire shall not shine.
- The wicked man is cast into a net by his own feet, the gin takes him by the heel, and the robber prevails against him, so his own steps bring him down.
- Terrors make him afraid on every side and drive him to his feet, which is the picture of a man overtaken by the ruin he set for himself.

**[Job 18:12-21](/bible/job/18#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/job/18#v12)</sup> His strength shall be hungerbitten, and destruction shall be ready at his side.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/18#v13)</sup> It shall devour the strength of his skin: even the firstborn of death shall devour his strength.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/18#v14)</sup> His confidence shall be rooted out of his tabernacle, and it shall bring him to the king of terrors.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/18#v15)</sup> It shall dwell in his tabernacle, because it is none of his: brimstone shall be scattered upon his habitation.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/18#v16)</sup> His roots shall be dried up beneath, and above shall his branch be cut off.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/18#v17)</sup> His remembrance shall perish from the earth, and he shall have no name in the street.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/18#v18)</sup> He shall be driven from light into darkness, and chased out of the world.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/18#v19)</sup> He shall neither have son nor nephew among his people, nor any remaining in his dwellings.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/18#v20)</sup> They that come after him shall be astonied at his day, as they that went before were affrighted.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/18#v21)</sup> Surely such are the dwellings of the wicked, and this is the place of him that knoweth not God.

- His strength is hunger-bitten, destruction is ready at his side, and the firstborn of death devours his strength.
- His confidence is rooted out of his tabernacle and he is brought to the king of terrors, his remembrance perishes from the earth, and he has no name in the street.
- He is driven from light into darkness and chased out of the world, with neither son nor nephew nor any remaining in his dwellings, and this is the place of him that knoweth not God.

# Job 19: I Know That My Redeemer Liveth

Read the chapter → [Job 19](/bible/job/19)

**[Job 19:1-6](/bible/job/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/19#v1)</sup> Then Job answered and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/19#v2)</sup> How long will ye vex my soul, and break me in pieces with words?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/19#v3)</sup> These ten times have ye reproached me: ye are not ashamed that ye make yourselves strange to me.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/19#v4)</sup> And be it indeed that I have erred, mine error remaineth with myself.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/19#v5)</sup> If indeed ye will magnify yourselves against me, and plead against me my reproach:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/19#v6)</sup> Know now that God hath overthrown me, and hath compassed me with his net.

- Job answers and asks how long they will vex his soul and break him in pieces with words, saying they have reproached him ten times and are not ashamed.
- He grants that if indeed he has erred, his error remains with himself, but tells them that God has overthrown him and compassed him with his net.

**[Job 19:7-12](/bible/job/19#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/job/19#v7)</sup> Behold, I cry out of wrong, but I am not heard: I cry aloud, but there is no judgment.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/19#v8)</sup> He hath fenced up my way that I cannot pass, and he hath set darkness in my paths.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/19#v9)</sup> He hath stripped me of my glory, and taken the crown from my head.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/19#v10)</sup> He hath destroyed me on every side, and I am gone: and mine hope hath he removed like a tree.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/19#v11)</sup> He hath also kindled his wrath against me, and he counteth me unto him as one of his enemies.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/19#v12)</sup> His troops come together, and raise up their way against me, and encamp round about my tabernacle.

- Job cries out of wrong and is not heard, and there is no judgment, for God has fenced up his way and set darkness in his paths.
- God has stripped him of his glory, taken the crown from his head, destroyed him on every side, and removed his hope like a tree that is pulled up.
- God's troops come together and raise up their way against him and camp round about his tabernacle, so Job feels himself besieged by the hand of the Most High.

**[Job 19:13-22](/bible/job/19#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/job/19#v13)</sup> He hath put my brethren far from me, and mine acquaintance are verily estranged from me.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/19#v14)</sup> My kinsfolk have failed, and my familiar friends have forgotten me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/19#v15)</sup> They that dwell in mine house, and my maids, count me for a stranger: I am an alien in their sight.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/19#v16)</sup> I called my servant, and he gave me no answer; I intreated him with my mouth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/19#v17)</sup> My breath is strange to my wife, though I intreated for the children’s sake of mine own body.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/19#v18)</sup> Yea, young children despised me; I arose, and they spake against me.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/19#v19)</sup> All my inward friends abhorred me: and they whom I loved are turned against me.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/19#v20)</sup> My bone cleaveth to my skin and to my flesh, and I am escaped with the skin of my teeth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/19#v21)</sup> Have pity upon me, have pity upon me, O ye my friends; for the hand of God hath touched me.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/19#v22)</sup> Why do ye persecute me as God, and are not satisfied with my flesh?

- Job's brethren are put far from him, his acquaintance are estranged, his kinsfolk have failed, and his familiar friends have forgotten him.
- He is an alien in the sight of his own household, his servant gives him no answer, and his breath is strange to his wife.
- Even young children despise him and those he loved are turned against him, so he pleads, Have pity upon me, O ye my friends, for the hand of God hath touched me.

**[Job 19:23-27](/bible/job/19#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/job/19#v23)</sup> Oh that my words were now written! oh that they were printed in a book!
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/19#v24)</sup> That they were graven with an iron pen and lead in the rock for ever!
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/19#v25)</sup> For I know that my redeemer liveth, and that he shall stand at the latter day upon the earth:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/job/19#v26)</sup> And though after my skin worms destroy this body, yet in my flesh shall I see God:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/job/19#v27)</sup> Whom I shall see for myself, and mine eyes shall behold, and not another; though my reins be consumed within me.

- Job wishes his words were written and printed in a book, graven with an iron pen and lead in the rock forever, so that this testimony would stand.
- He declares that he knows his Redeemer liveth and shall stand at the latter day upon the earth, and that though worms destroy his body, yet in his flesh shall he see God.
- Verses 25 through 27 are going into the regeneration; when you go back and read them, this is Job looking past the grave to seeing God for himself in a resurrected body.
  - **[Matthew 19:28](/bible/matthew/19#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/19#v28)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, Verily I say unto you, That ye which have followed me, in the regeneration when the Son of man shall sit in the throne of his glory, ye also shall sit upon twelve thrones, judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

    In the regeneration the Son of man sits in the throne of his glory and the servants judge the tribes of Israel, which is the day Job looks to.

**[Job 19:28-29](/bible/job/19#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/job/19#v28)</sup> But ye should say, Why persecute we him, seeing the root of the matter is found in me?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/job/19#v29)</sup> Be ye afraid of the sword: for wrath bringeth the punishments of the sword, that ye may know there is a judgment.

- Job warns his friends that if they say the root of the matter is found in him and keep persecuting him, they should be afraid of the sword themselves.
- The wrath brings the punishments of the sword, that they may know there is a judgment, so Job turns their own accusation back on them.

# Job 20: The Triumphing of the Wicked Is Short

Read the chapter → [Job 20](/bible/job/20)

The teacher said he was not going through precepts on this chapter, and that if there were questions the class should tune in or post them to the captains. He still marked the places in the chapter where the reading touches what is happening right now.

**[Job 20:1-5](/bible/job/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/job/20#v1)</sup> Then answered Zophar the Naamathite, and said,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/20#v2)</sup> Therefore do my thoughts cause me to answer, and for this I make haste.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/20#v3)</sup> I have heard the check of my reproach, and the spirit of my understanding causeth me to answer.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/20#v4)</sup> Knowest thou not this of old, since man was placed upon earth,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/20#v5)</sup> That the triumphing of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment?

- Zophar the Naamathite answers, saying his thoughts cause him to answer and for this he makes haste, having heard the check of his reproach.
- He asks whether Job does not know that since man was placed upon earth the triumphing of the wicked is short and the joy of the hypocrite is but for a moment.

**[Job 20:6-11](/bible/job/20#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/job/20#v6)</sup> Though his excellency mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/20#v7)</sup> Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung: they which have seen him shall say, Where is he?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/20#v8)</sup> He shall fly away as a dream, and shall not be found: yea, he shall be chased away as a vision of the night.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/20#v9)</sup> The eye also which saw him shall see him no more; neither shall his place any more behold him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/20#v10)</sup> His children shall seek to please the poor, and his hands shall restore their goods.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/20#v11)</sup> His bones are full of the sin of his youth, which shall lie down with him in the dust.

- Though the wicked man's excellency mounts up to the heavens and his head reaches to the clouds, yet he shall perish forever like his own dung, and they that saw him shall say, Where is he.
- He flies away as a dream and is chased away as a vision of the night, and the eye that saw him shall see him no more.
- His bones are full of the sin of his youth, which shall lie down with him in the dust.
- Verses 5 and 11 are going on right now: the truth is springing out of the earth and this Bible is revealing him, so the short triumph of the wicked and the buried sin of his youth are being uncovered in this age.
  - **[Psalm 85:11](/bible/psalms/85#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/85#v11)</sup> Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven.

    Truth shall spring out of the earth, and righteousness shall look down from heaven, which is the uncovering happening now.

**[Job 20:12-19](/bible/job/20#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/job/20#v12)</sup> Though wickedness be sweet in his mouth, though he hide it under his tongue;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/20#v13)</sup> Though he spare it, and forsake it not; but keep it still within his mouth:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/20#v14)</sup> Yet his meat in his bowels is turned, it is the gall of asps within him.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/20#v15)</sup> He hath swallowed down riches, and he shall vomit them up again: God shall cast them out of his belly.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/20#v16)</sup> He shall suck the poison of asps: the viper’s tongue shall slay him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/20#v17)</sup> He shall not see the rivers, the floods, the brooks of honey and butter.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/20#v18)</sup> That which he laboured for shall he restore, and shall not swallow it down: according to his substance shall the restitution be, and he shall not rejoice therein.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/20#v19)</sup> Because he hath oppressed and hath forsaken the poor; because he hath violently taken away an house which he builded not;

- Though wickedness is sweet in his mouth and he hides it under his tongue, yet his meat in his bowels is turned, it is the gall of asps within him.
- He has swallowed down riches, but God shall cast them out of his belly and he shall vomit them up again.
- He shall suck the poison of asps and the viper's tongue shall slay him, and he shall not see the rivers, the floods, the brooks of honey and butter.
- Because he has oppressed and forsaken the poor and violently taken away a house which he built not, that which he labored for he shall restore and shall not rejoice therein.

**[Job 20:20-29](/bible/job/20#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/job/20#v20)</sup> Surely he shall not feel quietness in his belly, he shall not save of that which he desired.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/20#v21)</sup> There shall none of his meat be left; therefore shall no man look for his goods.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/20#v22)</sup> In the fulness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits: every hand of the wicked shall come upon him.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/job/20#v23)</sup> When he is about to fill his belly, God shall cast the fury of his wrath upon him, and shall rain it upon him while he is eating.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/job/20#v24)</sup> He shall flee from the iron weapon, and the bow of steel shall strike him through.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/job/20#v25)</sup> It is drawn, and cometh out of the body; yea, the glittering sword cometh out of his gall: terrors are upon him.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/job/20#v26)</sup> All darkness shall be hid in his secret places: a fire not blown shall consume him; it shall go ill with him that is left in his tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/job/20#v27)</sup> The heaven shall reveal his iniquity; and the earth shall rise up against him.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/job/20#v28)</sup> The increase of his house shall depart, and his goods shall flow away in the day of his wrath.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/job/20#v29)</sup> This is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed unto him by God.

- The wicked man shall not feel quietness in his belly and shall not save of that which he desired, for there shall none of his meat be left.
- In the fullness of his sufficiency he shall be in straits, every hand of the wicked shall come upon him, and God shall cast the fury of his wrath upon him while he is eating.
- He flees from the iron weapon, but the bow of steel strikes him through, and the glittering sword comes out of his gall, with terrors upon him.
- The heaven reveals his iniquity and the earth rises up against him; this is the portion of a wicked man from God, and the heritage appointed unto him by God.
