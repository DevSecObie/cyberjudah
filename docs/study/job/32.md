**[Job 32:1-5](/bible/job/32#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/job/32#v1)</sup> So these three men ceased to answer Job, because he was righteous in his own eyes.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/job/32#v2)</sup> Then was kindled the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram: against Job was his wrath kindled, because he justified himself rather than God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/job/32#v3)</sup> Also against his three friends was his wrath kindled, because they had found no answer, and yet had condemned Job.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/job/32#v4)</sup> Now Elihu had waited till Job had spoken, because they were elder than he.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/job/32#v5)</sup> When Elihu saw that there was no answer in the mouth of these three men, then his wrath was kindled.

- These three men ceased to answer Job because he was righteous in his own eyes. Then the wrath of Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite, of the kindred of Ram, was kindled against Job because he justified himself rather than God.
- Elihu's wrath was also kindled against the three friends because they found no answer and yet condemned Job. Elihu had waited until Job had spoken because they were elder than he. This is a young man, and in this chapter alone you learn a lot about how young men should be with men that are aged, especially wise men in this truth.
- Elihu recognized who his elders were. He did not talk out of turn or try to make himself equal to them. He sat back like a young man should, let the older men discuss what they needed to discuss, and came afterward once they were finished.

**[Job 32:6-10](/bible/job/32#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/job/32#v6)</sup> And Elihu the son of Barachel the Buzite answered and said, I am young, and ye are very old; wherefore I was afraid, and durst not shew you mine opinion.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/job/32#v7)</sup> I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/job/32#v8)</sup> But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/32#v9)</sup> Great men are not always wise: neither do the aged understand judgment.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/32#v10)</sup> Therefore I said, Hearken to me; I also will shew mine opinion.

- Elihu answered and said he is young and they are very old, so he was afraid and durst not show his opinion. He said days should speak and multitude of years should teach wisdom.
- But there is a spirit in man, and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding. Great men are not always wise, neither do the aged always understand judgment; therefore he says hearken unto me, I also will show mine opinion.

**[Job 32:11-14](/bible/job/32#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/job/32#v11)</sup> Behold, I waited for your words; I gave ear to your reasons, whilst ye searched out what to say.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/job/32#v12)</sup> Yea, I attended unto you, and, behold, there was none of you that convinced Job, or that answered his words:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/job/32#v13)</sup> Lest ye should say, We have found out wisdom: God thrusteth him down, not man.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/job/32#v14)</sup> Now he hath not directed his words against me: neither will I answer him with your speeches.

- Elihu waited for their words and gave ear to their reasons while they searched out what to say. He attended unto them and behold there was none that convinced Job or answered his words.
- Lest they should say they have found out wisdom, he warns that God thrusteth Job down, not man. Now Job has not directed his words against Elihu, neither will Elihu answer him with their speeches. Once Elihu saw that Job justified himself and the other men had nothing else to say, he had to say something.

**[Job 32:15-22](/bible/job/32#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/job/32#v15)</sup> They were amazed, they answered no more: they left off speaking.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/job/32#v16)</sup> When I had waited, (for they spake not, but stood still, and answered no more;)
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/job/32#v17)</sup> I said, I will answer also my part, I also will shew mine opinion.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/job/32#v18)</sup> For I am full of matter, the spirit within me constraineth me.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/job/32#v19)</sup> Behold, my belly is as wine which hath no vent; it is ready to burst like new bottles.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/job/32#v20)</sup> I will speak, that I may be refreshed: I will open my lips and answer.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/job/32#v21)</sup> Let me not, I pray you, accept any man’s person, neither let me give flattering titles unto man.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/32#v22)</sup> For I know not to give flattering titles; in so doing my maker would soon take me away.

- They were amazed and answered no more; they left off speaking. When Elihu had waited and they stood still and answered no more, he said he too will show his opinion, for he is full of matter and the spirit within him constraineth him.
- His belly is as wine which hath no vent, ready to burst like new bottles. He will speak that he may be refreshed. He will not accept any man's person nor give flattering titles, for he knows not to give flattering titles, and in so doing his Maker would soon take him away. This is the four chapters in Job for today.
