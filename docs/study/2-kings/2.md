**[2 Kings 2:1-11](/bible/2-kings/2#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/2#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when the Lord would take up Elijah into heaven by a whirlwind, that Elijah went with Elisha from Gilgal.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/2#v2)</sup> And Elijah said unto Elisha, Tarry here, I pray thee; for the Lord hath sent me to Beth–el. And Elisha said unto him, As the Lord liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee. So they went down to Beth–el.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/2#v3)</sup> And the sons of the prophets that were at Beth–el came forth to Elisha, and said unto him, Knowest thou that the Lord will take away thy master from thy head to day? And he said, Yea, I know it; hold ye your peace.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/2#v4)</sup> And Elijah said unto him, Elisha, tarry here, I pray thee; for the Lord hath sent me to Jericho. And he said, As the Lord liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee. So they came to Jericho.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/2#v5)</sup> And the sons of the prophets that were at Jericho came to Elisha, and said unto him, Knowest thou that the Lord will take away thy master from thy head to day? And he answered, Yea, I know it; hold ye your peace.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/2#v6)</sup> And Elijah said unto him, Tarry, I pray thee, here; for the Lord hath sent me to Jordan. And he said, As the Lord liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee. And they two went on.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/2#v7)</sup> And fifty men of the sons of the prophets went, and stood to view afar off: and they two stood by Jordan.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/2#v8)</sup> And Elijah took his mantle, and wrapped it together, and smote the waters, and they were divided hither and thither, so that they two went over on dry ground.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/2#v9)</sup> And it came to pass, when they were gone over, that Elijah said unto Elisha, Ask what I shall do for thee, before I be taken away from thee. And Elisha said, I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/2#v10)</sup> And he said, Thou hast asked a hard thing: nevertheless, if thou see me when I am taken from thee, it shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/2#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, as they still went on, and talked, that, behold, there appeared a chariot of fire, and horses of fire, and parted them both asunder; and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven.

- When the Most High is about to take up Elijah into heaven by a whirlwind, Elijah goes with Elisha from Gilgal and tells him to tarry, but Elisha refuses to leave him. At Bethel and at Jericho the sons of the prophets tell Elisha that his master will be taken from him today, and each time he answers that he knows it and bids them hold their peace.
- The sons of the prophets are a prophetic order or company, not the literal blood sons of the prophets. Fifty of them stand afar off to view as Elijah and Elisha come to the Jordan.
- Elijah takes his mantle, wraps it together, and smites the waters, which divide so the two go over on dry ground. Elijah asks what he shall do for Elisha before he is taken, and Elisha asks for a double portion of his spirit; Elijah says it is a hard thing, granted only if Elisha sees him taken. A chariot of fire and horses of fire part them, and Elijah goes up by a whirlwind into heaven.

**[2 Kings 2:12-15](/bible/2-kings/2#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/2#v12)</sup> And Elisha saw it, and he cried, My father, my father, the chariot of Israel, and the horsemen thereof. And he saw him no more: and he took hold of his own clothes, and rent them in two pieces.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/2#v13)</sup> He took up also the mantle of Elijah that fell from him, and went back, and stood by the bank of Jordan;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/2#v14)</sup> And he took the mantle of Elijah that fell from him, and smote the waters, and said, Where is the Lord God of Elijah? and when he also had smitten the waters, they parted hither and thither: and Elisha went over.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/2#v15)</sup> And when the sons of the prophets which were to view at Jericho saw him, they said, The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha. And they came to meet him, and bowed themselves to the ground before him.

- Elisha sees it and cries, my father, my father, the chariot of Israel and the horsemen thereof, and sees him no more. He takes hold of his own clothes and rends them in two pieces and takes up the mantle of Elijah that fell from him.
- Elisha returns and smites the waters of Jordan with the mantle, asking, where is the Lord God of Elijah, and the waters part so he goes over. The sons of the prophets at Jericho see it and say the spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha, and they bow themselves to the ground before him.

**[2 Kings 2:16-18](/bible/2-kings/2#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/2#v16)</sup> And they said unto him, Behold now, there be with thy servants fifty strong men; let them go, we pray thee, and seek thy master: lest peradventure the Spirit of the Lord hath taken him up, and cast him upon some mountain, or into some valley. And he said, Ye shall not send.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/2#v17)</sup> And when they urged him till he was ashamed, he said, Send. They sent therefore fifty men; and they sought three days, but found him not.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-kings/2#v18)</sup> And when they came again to him, (for he tarried at Jericho,) he said unto them, Did I not say unto you, Go not?

- The sons of the prophets press Elisha to let fifty strong men go and seek his master, in case the spirit of the Lord has cast him upon some mountain or valley. Elisha tells them not to send.
- They urge him until he is ashamed, and he lets them go, but after three days they do not find Elijah. When they return, he reminds them that he told them not to go.

**[2 Kings 2:19-22](/bible/2-kings/2#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/2#v19)</sup> And the men of the city said unto Elisha, Behold, I pray thee, the situation of this city is pleasant, as my lord seeth: but the water is naught, and the ground barren.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/2#v20)</sup> And he said, Bring me a new cruse, and put salt therein. And they brought it to him.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-kings/2#v21)</sup> And he went forth unto the spring of the waters, and cast the salt in there, and said, Thus saith the Lord, I have healed these waters; there shall not be from thence any more death or barren land.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-kings/2#v22)</sup> So the waters were healed unto this day, according to the saying of Elisha which he spake.

- The men of Jericho tell Elisha that the city is pleasant but the water is bad and the ground barren. Elisha calls for a new cruse with salt in it, casts the salt into the spring, and says, thus saith the Lord, I have healed these waters; there shall not be from thence any more death or barren land.
- The waters are healed to this day according to the saying of Elisha. The sign shows that the spirit that was on Elijah now works through Elisha.

**[2 Kings 2:23-25](/bible/2-kings/2#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/2#v23)</sup> And he went up from thence unto Beth–el: and as he was going up by the way, there came forth little children out of the city, and mocked him, and said unto him, Go up, thou bald head; go up, thou bald head.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-kings/2#v24)</sup> And he turned back, and looked on them, and cursed them in the name of the Lord. And there came forth two she bears out of the wood, and tare forty and two children of them.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-kings/2#v25)</sup> And he went from thence to mount Carmel, and from thence he returned to Samaria.

- As Elisha goes up to Bethel, little children come out of the city and mock him, saying, go up, thou bald head. He turns back, looks on them, and curses them in the name of the Lord.
- Two she bears come out of the wood and tear forty and two of the children. Elisha goes on from there to Carmel and returns to Samaria.
