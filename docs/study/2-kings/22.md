**[2 Kings 22:1-7](/bible/2-kings/22#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/22#v1)</sup> Josiah was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned thirty and one years in Jerusalem. And his mother’s name was Jedidah, the daughter of Adaiah of Boscath.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/22#v2)</sup> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, and walked in all the way of David his father, and turned not aside to the right hand or to the left.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/22#v3)</sup> And it came to pass in the eighteenth year of king Josiah, that the king sent Shaphan the son of Azaliah, the son of Meshullam, the scribe, to the house of the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/22#v4)</sup> Go up to Hilkiah the high priest, that he may sum the silver which is brought into the house of the Lord, which the keepers of the door have gathered of the people:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/22#v5)</sup> And let them deliver it into the hand of the doers of the work, that have the oversight of the house of the Lord: and let them give it to the doers of the work which is in the house of the Lord, to repair the breaches of the house,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/22#v6)</sup> Unto carpenters, and builders, and masons, and to buy timber and hewn stone to repair the house.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/22#v7)</sup> Howbeit there was no reckoning made with them of the money that was delivered into their hand, because they dealt faithfully.

- Josiah begins to reign at eight years old and reigns thirty-one years, doing that which is right in the sight of the Most High and walking in all the way of David, turning neither to the right nor the left.
- In his eighteenth year he sends Shaphan the scribe to Hilkiah the priest to gather the silver brought to the house of the Most High and deliver it to the workmen for repairs.
- The money is given to carpenters, builders, and masons and for timber and hewn stone, and no reckoning is made with them because they dealt faithfully.
- Josiah rebuilds and reestablishes what Manasseh and Amon destroyed, and he makes sure the workmen are paid, for by the law the workman is worthy of his hire.

**[2 Kings 22:8-13](/bible/2-kings/22#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/22#v8)</sup> And Hilkiah the high priest said unto Shaphan the scribe, I have found the book of the law in the house of the Lord. And Hilkiah gave the book to Shaphan, and he read it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/22#v9)</sup> And Shaphan the scribe came to the king, and brought the king word again, and said, Thy servants have gathered the money that was found in the house, and have delivered it into the hand of them that do the work, that have the oversight of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/22#v10)</sup> And Shaphan the scribe shewed the king, saying, Hilkiah the priest hath delivered me a book. And Shaphan read it before the king.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/22#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, when the king had heard the words of the book of the law, that he rent his clothes.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/22#v12)</sup> And the king commanded Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam the son of Shaphan, and Achbor the son of Michaiah, and Shaphan the scribe, and Asahiah a servant of the king’s, saying,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/22#v13)</sup> Go ye, enquire of the Lord for me, and for the people, and for all Judah, concerning the words of this book that is found: for great is the wrath of the Lord that is kindled against us, because our fathers have not hearkened unto the words of this book, to do according unto all that which is written concerning us.

- Hilkiah the high priest finds the book of the law in the house of the Most High and gives it to Shaphan, who reads it. Manasseh and Amon were so wicked the very book of the law had been lost.
- Shaphan reports to the king about the money and the workmen, then tells him Hilkiah delivered him a book, and reads it before the king.
- When the king hears the words of the book of the law he rends his clothes, for the king was meant to read the law before all the people, which the wicked kings had not done.
- Josiah commands Hilkiah and his servants to inquire of the Most High for him and the people, for great is the wrath kindled against them because their fathers did not hearken to this book.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

    If they will not hearken to do all the commandments, all these curses shall come upon them, the wrath Josiah heard when the book was read.

**[2 Kings 22:14-20](/bible/2-kings/22#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/22#v14)</sup> So Hilkiah the priest, and Ahikam, and Achbor, and Shaphan, and Asahiah, went unto Huldah the prophetess, the wife of Shallum the son of Tikvah, the son of Harhas, keeper of the wardrobe; (now she dwelt in Jerusalem in the college;) and they communed with her.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/22#v15)</sup> And she said unto them, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Tell the man that sent you to me,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/22#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will bring evil upon this place, and upon the inhabitants thereof, even all the words of the book which the king of Judah hath read:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/22#v17)</sup> Because they have forsaken me, and have burned incense unto other gods, that they might provoke me to anger with all the works of their hands; therefore my wrath shall be kindled against this place, and shall not be quenched.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-kings/22#v18)</sup> But to the king of Judah which sent you to enquire of the Lord, thus shall ye say to him, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, As touching the words which thou hast heard;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/22#v19)</sup> Because thine heart was tender, and thou hast humbled thyself before the Lord, when thou heardest what I spake against this place, and against the inhabitants thereof, that they should become a desolation and a curse, and hast rent thy clothes, and wept before me; I also have heard thee, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/22#v20)</sup> Behold therefore, I will gather thee unto thy fathers, and thou shalt be gathered into thy grave in peace; and thine eyes shall not see all the evil which I will bring upon this place. And they brought the king word again.

- Hilkiah and the king's men go to Huldah the prophetess, who dwells in Jerusalem in the college; there were women among the people who were prophetesses, known by their works.
- Huldah returns the word of the Most High: He will bring evil upon this place and its inhabitants, all the words of the book, because they forsook Him and burned incense to other gods, and His wrath shall not be quenched.
- But to Josiah, because his heart was tender and he humbled himself and wept before the Most High, the word is that he shall be gathered to his fathers and to his grave in peace, and his eyes shall not see the evil to come.
- Because Josiah was righteous, repaired the temple, found the book, and began to keep the commandments again, he found mercy; yet the word spoken against Israel for its sin would still come to pass.
