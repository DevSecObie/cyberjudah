**[2 Kings 16:1-4](/bible/2-kings/16#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/16#v1)</sup> In the seventeenth year of Pekah the son of Remaliah Ahaz the son of Jotham king of Judah began to reign.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/16#v2)</sup> Twenty years old was Ahaz when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem, and did not that which was right in the sight of the Lord his God, like David his father.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/16#v3)</sup> But he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, yea, and made his son to pass through the fire, according to the abominations of the heathen, whom the Lord cast out from before the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/16#v4)</sup> And he sacrificed and burnt incense in the high places, and on the hills, and under every green tree.

- Ahaz son of Jotham begins to reign over Judah and does not that which is right, but walks in the way of the kings of Israel.
- He makes his son to pass through the fire after the abominations of the heathen whom the Most High cast out before Israel.
- He sacrifices and burns incense in the high places, on the hills, and under every green tree, the very idolatry the law forbade.
  - **[Deuteronomy 12:2-4](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v2)</sup> Ye shall utterly destroy all the places, wherein the nations which ye shall possess served their gods, upon the high mountains, and upon the hills, and under every green tree:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v3)</sup> And ye shall overthrow their altars, and break their pillars, and burn their groves with fire; and ye shall hew down the graven images of their gods, and destroy the names of them out of that place.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v4)</sup> Ye shall not do so unto the Lord your God.

    The nations' places of worship on the mountains, hills, and under every green tree were to be utterly destroyed, not imitated.

**[2 Kings 16:5-9](/bible/2-kings/16#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/16#v5)</sup> Then Rezin king of Syria and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel came up to Jerusalem to war: and they besieged Ahaz, but could not overcome him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/16#v6)</sup> At that time Rezin king of Syria recovered Elath to Syria, and drave the Jews from Elath: and the Syrians came to Elath, and dwelt there unto this day.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/16#v7)</sup> So Ahaz sent messengers to Tiglath–pileser king of Assyria, saying, I am thy servant and thy son: come up, and save me out of the hand of the king of Syria, and out of the hand of the king of Israel, which rise up against me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/16#v8)</sup> And Ahaz took the silver and gold that was found in the house of the Lord, and in the treasures of the king’s house, and sent it for a present to the king of Assyria.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/16#v9)</sup> And the king of Assyria hearkened unto him: for the king of Assyria went up against Damascus, and took it, and carried the people of it captive to Kir, and slew Rezin.

- Rezin king of Syria and Pekah king of Israel come up to war against Jerusalem and besiege Ahaz but cannot overcome him.
- Syria recovers Elath and drives the Jews out, and Ahaz is left weakened.
- Rather than trust the Most High, Ahaz sends to Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria saying, I am thy servant and thy son, come up and save me.
- This is much like the nation today looking to Esau as though he were a savior instead of turning to the Most High.
- Ahaz strips the silver and gold from the house of the Most High and the king's treasures and sends it as a present to Assyria, who then takes Damascus and slays Rezin.

**[2 Kings 16:10-16](/bible/2-kings/16#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/16#v10)</sup> And king Ahaz went to Damascus to meet Tiglath–pileser king of Assyria, and saw an altar that was at Damascus: and king Ahaz sent to Urijah the priest the fashion of the altar, and the pattern of it, according to all the workmanship thereof.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/16#v11)</sup> And Urijah the priest built an altar according to all that king Ahaz had sent from Damascus: so Urijah the priest made it against king Ahaz came from Damascus.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/16#v12)</sup> And when the king was come from Damascus, the king saw the altar: and the king approached to the altar, and offered thereon.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/16#v13)</sup> And he burnt his burnt offering and his meat offering, and poured his drink offering, and sprinkled the blood of his peace offerings, upon the altar.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/16#v14)</sup> And he brought also the brasen altar, which was before the Lord, from the forefront of the house, from between the altar and the house of the Lord, and put it on the north side of the altar.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/16#v15)</sup> And king Ahaz commanded Urijah the priest, saying, Upon the great altar burn the morning burnt offering, and the evening meat offering, and the king’s burnt sacrifice, and his meat offering, with the burnt offering of all the people of the land, and their meat offering, and their drink offerings; and sprinkle upon it all the blood of the burnt offering, and all the blood of the sacrifice: and the brasen altar shall be for me to enquire by.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/16#v16)</sup> Thus did Urijah the priest, according to all that king Ahaz commanded.

- Ahaz goes to Damascus to meet the king of Assyria and sees an altar there, then sends its pattern and workmanship to Urijah the priest.
- Urijah builds the altar according to the pattern before Ahaz returns, and the king offers his burnt offering, meat offering, drink offering, and peace offering upon it.
- The true altar was made after the pattern of heavenly things given through Moses, but Ahaz copies a heathen altar he admired in Damascus.
- Ahaz moves the brazen altar and commands Urijah to make the new altar the place of all the offerings, keeping the brazen altar for himself to inquire by.
  - **[Hebrews 8:5](/bible/hebrews/8#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/8#v5)</sup> Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.

    Moses was warned to make all things according to the pattern shown in the mount, not after the fashion of the nations.

**[2 Kings 16:17-20](/bible/2-kings/16#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/16#v17)</sup> And king Ahaz cut off the borders of the bases, and removed the laver from off them; and took down the sea from off the brasen oxen that were under it, and put it upon a pavement of stones.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-kings/16#v18)</sup> And the covert for the sabbath that they had built in the house, and the king’s entry without, turned he from the house of the Lord for the king of Assyria.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/16#v19)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Ahaz which he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/16#v20)</sup> And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David: and Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead.

- Ahaz cuts off the borders of the bases, removes the laver, and takes down the brazen sea from off the oxen, dismantling the vessels of the temple.
- He turns the covert for the sabbath and the king's entry away from the house of the Most High for the sake of the king of Assyria.
- Ahaz sleeps with his fathers and Hezekiah his son reigns in his stead.
