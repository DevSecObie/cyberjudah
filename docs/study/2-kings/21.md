**[2 Kings 21:1-9](/bible/2-kings/21#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/21#v1)</sup> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and reigned fifty and five years in Jerusalem. And his mother’s name was Hephzi–bah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/21#v2)</sup> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, after the abominations of the heathen, whom the Lord cast out before the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/21#v3)</sup> For he built up again the high places which Hezekiah his father had destroyed; and he reared up altars for Baal, and made a grove, as did Ahab king of Israel; and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/21#v4)</sup> And he built altars in the house of the Lord, of which the Lord said, In Jerusalem will I put my name.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/21#v5)</sup> And he built altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/21#v6)</sup> And he made his son pass through the fire, and observed times, and used enchantments, and dealt with familiar spirits and wizards: he wrought much wickedness in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/21#v7)</sup> And he set a graven image of the grove that he had made in the house, of which the Lord said to David, and to Solomon his son, In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen out of all tribes of Israel, will I put my name for ever:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/21#v8)</sup> Neither will I make the feet of Israel move any more out of the land which I gave their fathers; only if they will observe to do according to all that I have commanded them, and according to all the law that my servant Moses commanded them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/21#v9)</sup> But they hearkened not: and Manasseh seduced them to do more evil than did the nations whom the Lord destroyed before the children of Israel.

- Manasseh begins to reign at twelve years old and reigns fifty-five years, doing evil after the abominations of the heathen the Most High cast out before Israel.
- He rebuilds the high places Hezekiah destroyed, rears altars for Baal, makes a grove as Ahab did, and worships the host of heaven, even building altars in the house of the Most High where He set His name; this is a wickedness with zero fear of the Most High.
- The people did not serve idols for nothing but for the self-gratification in it, the same as men today keep pagan feasts for the gifts, the food, and the reveling, not for what the day claims to be.
- He makes his son pass through the fire, observes times, uses enchantments, and deals with familiar spirits and wizards, provoking the Most High to anger, and seduces Judah to do more evil than the nations the Most High destroyed.
- Repentance is always possible; our people are repenting, not repented, so no man should judge before the time who is or is not of the two-thirds. Continue in the work of the Most High and let Christ judge in the end.

**[2 Kings 21:10-15](/bible/2-kings/21#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/21#v10)</sup> And the Lord spake by his servants the prophets, saying,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/21#v11)</sup> Because Manasseh king of Judah hath done these abominations, and hath done wickedly above all that the Amorites did, which were before him, and hath made Judah also to sin with his idols:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/21#v12)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Behold, I am bringing such evil upon Jerusalem and Judah, that whosoever heareth of it, both his ears shall tingle.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/21#v13)</sup> And I will stretch over Jerusalem the line of Samaria, and the plummet of the house of Ahab: and I will wipe Jerusalem as a man wipeth a dish, wiping it, and turning it upside down.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/21#v14)</sup> And I will forsake the remnant of mine inheritance, and deliver them into the hand of their enemies; and they shall become a prey and a spoil to all their enemies;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/21#v15)</sup> Because they have done that which was evil in my sight, and have provoked me to anger, since the day their fathers came forth out of Egypt, even unto this day.

- The Most High speaks by His servants the prophets that because Manasseh did wickedly above all the Amorites and made Judah to sin, He is bringing such evil upon Jerusalem that both ears of whoever hears it shall tingle.
- He will stretch over Jerusalem the line of Samaria and the plummet of the house of Ahab, and wipe Jerusalem as a man wipes a dish and turns it upside down; the same desolation done to Samaria will reach Judah.
- He will forsake the remnant of His inheritance and deliver them into the hand of their enemies to be a prey and a spoil, because they have provoked Him to anger since the day their fathers came out of Egypt. The Most High has borne with this wickedness for hundreds of years.
  - **[Isaiah 5:5-6](/bible/isaiah/5#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/5#v5)</sup> And now go to; I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard: I will take away the hedge thereof, and it shall be eaten up; and break down the wall thereof, and it shall be trodden down:
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/5#v6)</sup> And I will lay it waste: it shall not be pruned, nor digged; but there shall come up briers and thorns: I will also command the clouds that they rain no rain upon it.

    The Most High will take away the hedge of His vineyard, lay it waste, and command the clouds to rain no rain, the same desolation promised over Jerusalem.
  - **[Hosea 4:6](/bible/hosea/4#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/4#v6)</sup> My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge: because thou hast rejected knowledge, I will also reject thee, that thou shalt be no priest to me: seeing thou hast forgotten the law of thy God, I will also forget thy children.

    The people are destroyed for lack of knowledge, and because they rejected the knowledge of the law, the Most High rejects them.
  - **[Isaiah 5:13-14](/bible/isaiah/5#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/5#v13)</sup> Therefore my people are gone into captivity, because they have no knowledge: and their honourable men are famished, and their multitude dried up with thirst.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/5#v14)</sup> Therefore hell hath enlarged herself, and opened her mouth without measure: and their glory, and their multitude, and their pomp, and he that rejoiceth, shall descend into it.

    The people are gone into captivity for having no knowledge, and hell, meaning captivity, has enlarged herself and opened her mouth without measure.

**[2 Kings 21:16-18](/bible/2-kings/21#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/21#v16)</sup> Moreover Manasseh shed innocent blood very much, till he had filled Jerusalem from one end to another; beside his sin wherewith he made Judah to sin, in doing that which was evil in the sight of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/21#v17)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and all that he did, and his sin that he sinned, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-kings/21#v18)</sup> And Manasseh slept with his fathers, and was buried in the garden of his own house, in the garden of Uzza: and Amon his son reigned in his stead.

- Manasseh sheds innocent blood very much, filling Jerusalem from one end to the other, beside the sin with which he made Judah to sin.
- His remaining acts are written in the chronicles of the kings of Judah, and the prayer of Manasseh is recorded in the Apocrypha, prayed while he was held captive in Babylon.
- Chronicles shows the captains of the king of Assyria took Manasseh among the thorns and carried him with fetters to Babylon, then a province under Assyria; there he repented and prayed.
- The history shows a power struggle beginning, where Assyrian kings ruled over Babylon, and later Babylonian kings ruled over Assyria; Manasseh sleeps with his fathers and Amon his son reigns.
  - **[2 Chronicles 33:11](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v11)</sup> Wherefore the Lord brought upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria, which took Manasseh among the thorns, and bound him with fetters, and carried him to Babylon.

    The captains of the king of Assyria take Manasseh among the thorns and carry him bound to Babylon, then held by Assyria.
  - **[Judith 1:1](/bible/judith/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/judith/1#v1)</sup> In the twelfth year of the reign of Nabuchodonosor, who reigned in Nineve, the great city; in the days of Arphaxad, which reigned over the Medes in Ecbatane,

    Nebuchadnezzar, a Babylonian, reigns over the Assyrians in Nineveh, showing Babylon rising to take all that Assyria held.

**[2 Kings 21:19-26](/bible/2-kings/21#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/21#v19)</sup> Amon was twenty and two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned two years in Jerusalem. And his mother’s name was Meshullemeth, the daughter of Haruz of Jotbah.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/21#v20)</sup> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as his father Manasseh did.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-kings/21#v21)</sup> And he walked in all the way that his father walked in, and served the idols that his father served, and worshipped them:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-kings/21#v22)</sup> And he forsook the Lord God of his fathers, and walked not in the way of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/21#v23)</sup> And the servants of Amon conspired against him, and slew the king in his own house.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-kings/21#v24)</sup> And the people of the land slew all them that had conspired against king Amon; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his stead.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-kings/21#v25)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Amon which he did, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/2-kings/21#v26)</sup> And he was buried in his sepulchre in the garden of Uzza: and Josiah his son reigned in his stead.

- Amon begins to reign at twenty-two years old and reigns two years, doing evil as his father Manasseh did, walking in all his ways and serving the idols his father served.
- He forsakes the Most High God of his fathers and walks not in His way.
- His own servants conspire against him and slay him in his house; the people of the land slay the conspirators and make Josiah his son king in his stead.
