**[2 Kings 4:1-7](/bible/2-kings/4#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/4#v1)</sup> Now there cried a certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets unto Elisha, saying, Thy servant my husband is dead; and thou knowest that thy servant did fear the Lord: and the creditor is come to take unto him my two sons to be bondmen.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/4#v2)</sup> And Elisha said unto her, What shall I do for thee? tell me, what hast thou in the house? And she said, Thine handmaid hath not any thing in the house, save a pot of oil.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/4#v3)</sup> Then he said, Go, borrow thee vessels abroad of all thy neighbours, even empty vessels; borrow not a few.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/4#v4)</sup> And when thou art come in, thou shalt shut the door upon thee and upon thy sons, and shalt pour out into all those vessels, and thou shalt set aside that which is full.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/4#v5)</sup> So she went from him, and shut the door upon her and upon her sons, who brought the vessels to her; and she poured out.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/4#v6)</sup> And it came to pass, when the vessels were full, that she said unto her son, Bring me yet a vessel. And he said unto her, There is not a vessel more. And the oil stayed.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/4#v7)</sup> Then she came and told the man of God. And he said, Go, sell the oil, and pay thy debt, and live thou and thy children of the rest.

- A certain woman of the wives of the sons of the prophets cries to Elisha that her husband is dead, that her husband feared the Lord, and that the creditor has come to take her two sons to be bondmen. Because the husband died in debt, the creditor comes to take the sons to work off what is owed.
- Elisha asks what she has in the house, and she says nothing but a pot of oil. He tells her to borrow empty vessels from all her neighbors, not a few, then shut the door upon herself and her sons and pour oil into all the vessels.
- She pours out, and the oil continues until every vessel is full, and only then does the oil stay. Elisha tells her to sell the oil, pay her debt, and live with her children on the rest. The empty vessels she borrowed from her people, the children of Israel, were filled through the spirit of the Lord.

**[2 Kings 4:8-17](/bible/2-kings/4#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/4#v8)</sup> And it fell on a day, that Elisha passed to Shunem, where was a great woman; and she constrained him to eat bread. And so it was, that as oft as he passed by, he turned in thither to eat bread.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/4#v9)</sup> And she said unto her husband, Behold now, I perceive that this is an holy man of God, which passeth by us continually.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/4#v10)</sup> Let us make a little chamber, I pray thee, on the wall; and let us set for him there a bed, and a table, and a stool, and a candlestick: and it shall be, when he cometh to us, that he shall turn in thither.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/4#v11)</sup> And it fell on a day, that he came thither, and he turned into the chamber, and lay there.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/4#v12)</sup> And he said to Gehazi his servant, Call this Shunammite. And when he had called her, she stood before him.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/4#v13)</sup> And he said unto him, Say now unto her, Behold, thou hast been careful for us with all this care; what is to be done for thee? wouldest thou be spoken for to the king, or to the captain of the host? And she answered, I dwell among mine own people.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/4#v14)</sup> And he said, What then is to be done for her? And Gehazi answered, Verily she hath no child, and her husband is old.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/4#v15)</sup> And he said, Call her. And when he had called her, she stood in the door.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/4#v16)</sup> And he said, About this season, according to the time of life, thou shalt embrace a son. And she said, Nay, my lord, thou man of God, do not lie unto thine handmaid.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/4#v17)</sup> And the woman conceived, and bare a son at that season that Elisha had said unto her, according to the time of life.

- Elisha passes through Shunem, where a great woman constrains him to eat bread, and as often as he passes by he turns in there to eat. She perceives that he is a holy man of God and tells her husband to make a little chamber on the wall with a bed, a table, a stool, and a candlestick, so he may turn in whenever he comes.
- On a day when he rests in the chamber, Elisha wants to bless her and sends Gehazi to ask what may be done for her, whether she would be spoken for to the king or the captain of the host. She answers that she dwells among her own people.
- Gehazi tells Elisha the woman has no child and her husband is old. Elisha tells her that about this season, according to the time of life, she shall embrace a son. Like our mother Sarah, she does not believe it and tells the man of God not to lie to his handmaid, yet she conceives and bears a son at that season.
  - **[Genesis 18:11-14](/bible/genesis/18#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/18#v11)</sup> Now Abraham and Sarah were old and well stricken in age; and it ceased to be with Sarah after the manner of women.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/18#v12)</sup> Therefore Sarah laughed within herself, saying, After I am waxed old shall I have pleasure, my lord being old also?
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/18#v13)</sup> And the Lord said unto Abraham, Wherefore did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I of a surety bear a child, which am old?
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/18#v14)</sup> Is any thing too hard for the Lord? At the time appointed I will return unto thee, according to the time of life, and Sarah shall have a son.

    Sarah laughs within herself at the promise of a son because she and Abraham are old, and the Lord asks whether anything is too hard for him.

>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/4#v19)</sup> And he said unto his father, My head, my head. And he said to a lad, Carry him to his mother.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/4#v20)</sup> And when he had taken him, and brought him to his mother, he sat on her knees till noon, and then died.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-kings/4#v21)</sup> And she went up, and laid him on the bed of the man of God, and shut the door upon him, and went out.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-kings/4#v22)</sup> And she called unto her husband, and said, Send me, I pray thee, one of the young men, and one of the asses, that I may run to the man of God, and come again.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/4#v23)</sup> And he said, Wherefore wilt thou go to him to day? it is neither new moon, nor sabbath. And she said, It shall be well.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-kings/4#v24)</sup> Then she saddled an ass, and said to her servant, Drive, and go forward; slack not thy riding for me, except I bid thee.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-kings/4#v25)</sup> So she went and came unto the man of God to mount Carmel. And it came to pass, when the man of God saw her afar off, that he said to Gehazi his servant, Behold, yonder is that Shunammite:

- When the child is grown, he goes out to his father to the reapers and cries, my head, my head. His father has a lad carry him to his mother, and he sits on her knees till noon and then dies, from what seemed only a headache.
- She carries the child up and lays him on the bed of the man of God, the bed in the chamber she had made for Elisha, shuts the door, and goes out. She calls to her husband for a young man and an ass so she may run to the man of God, and though he asks why, since it is neither new moon nor sabbath, she answers, it shall be well.
- She rides to Elisha at Carmel, and when the man of God sees her afar off, he sends Gehazi to ask whether it is well with her, her husband, and the child, and she answers, it is well.

>

>
> <sup>[28](/bible/2-kings/4#v28)</sup> Then she said, Did I desire a son of my lord? did I not say, Do not deceive me?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/2-kings/4#v29)</sup> Then he said to Gehazi, Gird up thy loins, and take my staff in thine hand, and go thy way: if thou meet any man, salute him not; and if any salute thee, answer him not again: and lay my staff upon the face of the child.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/2-kings/4#v30)</sup> And the mother of the child said, As the Lord liveth, and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee. And he arose, and followed her.
>

- When she comes to Elisha at the hill, she catches him by the feet, and Gehazi comes near to thrust her away, but the man of God says let her alone, for her soul is vexed within her and the Lord has hid it from him. She says, did I desire a son of my lord, did I not say do not deceive me.
- Elisha tells Gehazi to gird up his loins, take his staff, greet no man on the way, and lay the staff on the face of the child. The mother says, as the Lord liveth and as thy soul liveth, I will not leave thee, so Elisha rises and follows her.
- Gehazi goes on before them and lays the staff on the face of the child, but there is neither voice nor hearing. He returns to tell Elisha the child is not awake.

>
> <sup>[33](/bible/2-kings/4#v33)</sup> He went in therefore, and shut the door upon them twain, and prayed unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/2-kings/4#v34)</sup> And he went up, and lay upon the child, and put his mouth upon his mouth, and his eyes upon his eyes, and his hands upon his hands: and he stretched himself upon the child; and the flesh of the child waxed warm.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/2-kings/4#v35)</sup> Then he returned, and walked in the house to and fro; and went up, and stretched himself upon him: and the child sneezed seven times, and the child opened his eyes.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/2-kings/4#v36)</sup> And he called Gehazi, and said, Call this Shunammite. So he called her. And when she was come in unto him, he said, Take up thy son.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/2-kings/4#v37)</sup> Then she went in, and fell at his feet, and bowed herself to the ground, and took up her son, and went out.

- When Elisha comes to the house, the child is dead, laid upon his bed. He goes in, shuts the door upon them both, and prays to the Lord.
- He goes up and lies upon the child, putting his mouth on his mouth, his eyes on his eyes, and his hands on his hands, and stretches himself upon the child, and the flesh of the child grows warm. He walks in the house to and fro, goes up and stretches himself upon him again, and the child sneezes seven times and opens his eyes.
- Elisha calls Gehazi to call the Shunammite, and when she comes he tells her to take up her son. She falls at his feet, bows to the ground, takes up her son, and goes out. This is another act of the Most High through Elisha, restoring the child to life.

**[2 Kings 4:38-41](/bible/2-kings/4#v38)**
> <sup>[38](/bible/2-kings/4#v38)</sup> And Elisha came again to Gilgal: and there was a dearth in the land; and the sons of the prophets were sitting before him: and he said unto his servant, Set on the great pot, and seethe pottage for the sons of the prophets.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/2-kings/4#v39)</sup> And one went out into the field to gather herbs, and found a wild vine, and gathered thereof wild gourds his lap full, and came and shred them into the pot of pottage: for they knew them not.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/2-kings/4#v40)</sup> So they poured out for the men to eat. And it came to pass, as they were eating of the pottage, that they cried out, and said, O thou man of God, there is death in the pot. And they could not eat thereof.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/2-kings/4#v41)</sup> But he said, Then bring meal. And he cast it into the pot; and he said, Pour out for the people, that they may eat. And there was no harm in the pot.

- Elisha comes again to Gilgal, where there is a dearth in the land, and the sons of the prophets sit before him. He tells his servant to set on the great pot and seethe pottage for them.
- One goes out into the field to gather herbs, finds a wild vine, and gathers his lap full of wild gourds, shredding them into the pot, for they knew them not. As the men eat, they cry out, O thou man of God, there is death in the pot, and they cannot eat.
- Elisha tells them to bring meal, casts it into the pot, and says pour out for the people that they may eat, and there is no harm in the pot. He cures the poisoned food so the men may eat safely.

> <sup>[43](/bible/2-kings/4#v43)</sup> And his servitor said, What, should I set this before an hundred men? He said again, Give the people, that they may eat: for thus saith the Lord, They shall eat, and shall leave thereof.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/2-kings/4#v44)</sup> So he set it before them, and they did eat, and left thereof, according to the word of the Lord.

- A man comes from Baal-shalisha and brings the man of God bread of the firstfruits, twenty loaves of barley and full ears of corn in the husk. Elisha says, give to the people that they may eat.
- His servant asks how he should set this before a hundred men, but Elisha says again, give to the people, for thus saith the Lord, they shall eat and shall leave thereof. He sets it before them, and they eat and leave some, according to the word of the Lord.
