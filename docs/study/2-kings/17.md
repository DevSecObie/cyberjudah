**[2 Kings 17:1-6](/bible/2-kings/17#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/17#v1)</sup> In the twelfth year of Ahaz king of Judah began Hoshea the son of Elah to reign in Samaria over Israel nine years.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/17#v2)</sup> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, but not as the kings of Israel that were before him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/17#v3)</sup> Against him came up Shalmaneser king of Assyria; and Hoshea became his servant, and gave him presents.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/17#v4)</sup> And the king of Assyria found conspiracy in Hoshea: for he had sent messengers to So king of Egypt, and brought no present to the king of Assyria, as he had done year by year: therefore the king of Assyria shut him up, and bound him in prison.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/17#v5)</sup> Then the king of Assyria came up throughout all the land, and went up to Samaria, and besieged it three years.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/17#v6)</sup> In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria, and carried Israel away into Assyria, and placed them in Halah and in Habor by the river of Gozan, and in the cities of the Medes.

- Hoshea son of Elah reigns nine years over Israel in Samaria and does evil, though not as the kings before him.
- Shalmaneser king of Assyria comes up, and Hoshea becomes his servant and pays tribute.
- The king of Assyria finds conspiracy in Hoshea because he sent messengers to So king of Egypt and stopped his yearly tribute, so he shuts him up in prison.
- Assyria besieges Samaria three years, then in the ninth year of Hoshea takes it and carries Israel away to Assyria, placing them in Halah, Habor, and the cities of the Medes.

**[2 Kings 17:7-12](/bible/2-kings/17#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/17#v7)</sup> For so it was, that the children of Israel had sinned against the Lord their God, which had brought them up out of the land of Egypt, from under the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and had feared other gods,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/17#v8)</sup> And walked in the statutes of the heathen, whom the Lord cast out from before the children of Israel, and of the kings of Israel, which they had made.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/17#v9)</sup> And the children of Israel did secretly those things that were not right against the Lord their God, and they built them high places in all their cities, from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/17#v10)</sup> And they set them up images and groves in every high hill, and under every green tree:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/17#v11)</sup> And there they burnt incense in all the high places, as did the heathen whom the Lord carried away before them; and wrought wicked things to provoke the Lord to anger:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/17#v12)</sup> For they served idols, whereof the Lord had said unto them, Ye shall not do this thing.

- This captivity comes because the children of Israel sinned against the Most High who brought them out of Egypt and feared other gods.
- They walked in the statutes of the heathen, built high places in all their cities, and set up images and groves on every high hill and under every green tree.
- They wrought wicked things to provoke the Most High and served idols, though He had said plainly, ye shall not do this thing.
  - **[Deuteronomy 12:2-3](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v2)</sup> Ye shall utterly destroy all the places, wherein the nations which ye shall possess served their gods, upon the high mountains, and upon the hills, and under every green tree:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v3)</sup> And ye shall overthrow their altars, and break their pillars, and burn their groves with fire; and ye shall hew down the graven images of their gods, and destroy the names of them out of that place.

    The law had already commanded Israel to destroy such high places, not build them.

**[2 Kings 17:13-17](/bible/2-kings/17#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/17#v13)</sup> Yet the Lord testified against Israel, and against Judah, by all the prophets, and by all the seers, saying, Turn ye from your evil ways, and keep my commandments and my statutes, according to all the law which I commanded your fathers, and which I sent to you by my servants the prophets.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/17#v14)</sup> Notwithstanding they would not hear, but hardened their necks, like to the neck of their fathers, that did not believe in the Lord their God.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/17#v15)</sup> And they rejected his statutes, and his covenant that he made with their fathers, and his testimonies which he testified against them; and they followed vanity, and became vain, and went after the heathen that were round about them, concerning whom the Lord had charged them, that they should not do like them.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-kings/17#v16)</sup> And they left all the commandments of the Lord their God, and made them molten images, even two calves, and made a grove, and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served Baal.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-kings/17#v17)</sup> And they caused their sons and their daughters to pass through the fire, and used divination and enchantments, and sold themselves to do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger.

- The Most High testified against Israel and Judah by all the prophets and seers, calling them to turn from evil and keep His commandments.
- They would not hear but hardened their necks like their fathers who did not believe in the Most High.
- To believe in the Most High is to take heed to the commandments, so those who claim belief while breaking the commandments do not truly believe.
- They rejected His statutes and covenant, followed vanity, made molten images and two calves, worshipped the host of heaven, and served Baal.
- They caused their sons and daughters to pass through the fire, used divinations and enchantments, and sold themselves to do evil.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 32:24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)</sup> He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse.

    He that believeth in the Most High taketh heed to the commandments, tying true belief to obedience.
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:16-19](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v16)</sup> Lest ye corrupt yourselves, and make you a graven image, the similitude of any figure, the likeness of male or female,
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v17)</sup> The likeness of any beast that is on the earth, the likeness of any winged fowl that flieth in the air,
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v18)</sup> The likeness of any thing that creepeth on the ground, the likeness of any fish that is in the waters beneath the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v19)</sup> And lest thou lift up thine eyes unto heaven, and when thou seest the sun, and the moon, and the stars, even all the host of heaven, shouldest be driven to worship them, and serve them, which the Lord thy God hath divided unto all nations under the whole heaven.

    Israel was warned not to corrupt themselves by worshipping the host of heaven.

**[2 Kings 17:18-23](/bible/2-kings/17#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-kings/17#v18)</sup> Therefore the Lord was very angry with Israel, and removed them out of his sight: there was none left but the tribe of Judah only.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-kings/17#v19)</sup> Also Judah kept not the commandments of the Lord their God, but walked in the statutes of Israel which they made.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/17#v20)</sup> And the Lord rejected all the seed of Israel, and afflicted them, and delivered them into the hand of spoilers, until he had cast them out of his sight.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-kings/17#v21)</sup> For he rent Israel from the house of David; and they made Jeroboam the son of Nebat king: and Jeroboam drave Israel from following the Lord, and made them sin a great sin.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-kings/17#v22)</sup> For the children of Israel walked in all the sins of Jeroboam which he did; they departed not from them;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/17#v23)</sup> Until the Lord removed Israel out of his sight, as he had said by all his servants the prophets. So was Israel carried away out of their own land to Assyria unto this day.

- The Most High is very angry and removes Israel out of His sight, leaving only the tribe of Judah.
- Judah also fails to keep the commandments and walks in the statutes that Israel made.
- The Most High rejects all the seed of Israel, afflicts them, and delivers them into the hand of spoilers until He casts them out of His sight.
- This rejection is why the seed of Israel are later called heathens and Gentiles in the New Testament, which resolves much of the struggle over Jews and Gentiles there.
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:21](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v21)</sup> They have moved me to jealousy with that which is not God; they have provoked me to anger with their vanities: and I will move them to jealousy with those which are not a people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation.

    The Most High moved Israel to jealousy and anger, foretelling their rejection among the nations.

**[2 Kings 17:24-28](/bible/2-kings/17#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-kings/17#v24)</sup> And the king of Assyria brought men from Babylon, and from Cuthah, and from Ava, and from Hamath, and from Sepharvaim, and placed them in the cities of Samaria instead of the children of Israel: and they possessed Samaria, and dwelt in the cities thereof.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-kings/17#v25)</sup> And so it was at the beginning of their dwelling there, that they feared not the Lord: therefore the Lord sent lions among them, which slew some of them.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/2-kings/17#v26)</sup> Wherefore they spake to the king of Assyria, saying, The nations which thou hast removed, and placed in the cities of Samaria, know not the manner of the God of the land: therefore he hath sent lions among them, and, behold, they slay them, because they know not the manner of the God of the land.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-kings/17#v27)</sup> Then the king of Assyria commanded, saying, Carry thither one of the priests whom ye brought from thence; and let them go and dwell there, and let him teach them the manner of the God of the land.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/2-kings/17#v28)</sup> Then one of the priests whom they had carried away from Samaria came and dwelt in Beth–el, and taught them how they should fear the Lord.

- The king of Assyria brings men from Babylon, Cuthah, Ava, Hamath, and Sepharvaim to dwell in the cities of Samaria in Israel's place.
- Because they do not fear the Most High, He sends lions among them that slay some of them.
- One of the carried-away priests is brought back to Bethel to teach the settlers how they should fear the Most High.

**[2 Kings 17:29-34](/bible/2-kings/17#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/2-kings/17#v29)</sup> Howbeit every nation made gods of their own, and put them in the houses of the high places which the Samaritans had made, every nation in their cities wherein they dwelt.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/2-kings/17#v30)</sup> And the men of Babylon made Succoth–benoth, and the men of Cuth made Nergal, and the men of Hamath made Ashima,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/2-kings/17#v31)</sup> And the Avites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burnt their children in fire to Adrammelech and Anammelech, the gods of Sepharvaim.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/2-kings/17#v32)</sup> So they feared the Lord, and made unto themselves of the lowest of them priests of the high places, which sacrificed for them in the houses of the high places.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/2-kings/17#v33)</sup> They feared the Lord, and served their own gods, after the manner of the nations whom they carried away from thence.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/2-kings/17#v34)</sup> Unto this day they do after the former manners: they fear not the Lord, neither do they after their statutes, or after their ordinances, or after the law and commandment which the Lord commanded the children of Jacob, whom he named Israel;

- Every nation makes gods of its own and sets them in the houses of the high places the Samaritans had made.
- The men of Babylon made Succoth-benoth, Cuth made Nergal, Hamath made Ashima, the Avites made Nibhaz and Tartak, and the Sepharvites burnt their children to Adrammelech and Anammelech.
- They feared the Most High and also served their own gods after the manner of the nations they came from.
- The Most High made His covenant and charged the children of Jacob whom He named Israel, showing the covenant belongs to Israel and not to all nations.

**[2 Kings 17:35-41](/bible/2-kings/17#v35)**
> <sup>[35](/bible/2-kings/17#v35)</sup> With whom the Lord had made a covenant, and charged them, saying, Ye shall not fear other gods, nor bow yourselves to them, nor serve them, nor sacrifice to them:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/2-kings/17#v36)</sup> But the Lord, who brought you up out of the land of Egypt with great power and a stretched out arm, him shall ye fear, and him shall ye worship, and to him shall ye do sacrifice.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/2-kings/17#v37)</sup> And the statutes, and the ordinances, and the law, and the commandment, which he wrote for you, ye shall observe to do for evermore; and ye shall not fear other gods.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/2-kings/17#v38)</sup> And the covenant that I have made with you ye shall not forget; neither shall ye fear other gods.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/2-kings/17#v39)</sup> But the Lord your God ye shall fear; and he shall deliver you out of the hand of all your enemies.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/2-kings/17#v40)</sup> Howbeit they did not hearken, but they did after their former manner.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/2-kings/17#v41)</sup> So these nations feared the Lord, and served their graven images, both their children, and their children’s children: as did their fathers, so do they unto this day.

- The Most High commanded Israel not to fear, bow to, serve, or sacrifice to other gods, but to fear and worship Him alone who brought them out of Egypt with a stretched out arm.
- They were to observe His statutes, ordinances, and commandments forevermore, and He would deliver them out of the hand of all their enemies.
- No nation and no treaty can redeem Israel from their condition, only turning to the Most High can.
- These settler nations feared the Most High yet served their graven images to this day, and as a nation Israel does the same, serving the gods of the nations.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

    No man can buy or redeem the nation out of its condition, for the curses come from forsaking the Most High.
