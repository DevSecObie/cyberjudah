Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 47](/bible/ezekiel/47)

This session covers [Ezekiel 47](/bible/ezekiel/47) and 48. Ezekiel opens with a similitude of waters issuing from the house and rising higher and higher, a picture of the word of the Most High going out to the four corners of the earth; it closes with the land divided among the tribes and the gates of the city named after the children of Israel. The prophet speaks by similitude, proverbs, and dark sayings, so what is written here is spiritual, not a literal structure.

**[Ezekiel 47:1-2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/47#v1)</sup> Afterward he brought me again unto the door of the house; and, behold, waters issued out from under the threshold of the house eastward: for the forefront of the house stood toward the east, and the waters came down from under from the right side of the house, at the south side of the altar.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v2)</sup> Then brought he me out of the way of the gate northward, and led me about the way without unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward; and, behold, there ran out waters on the right side.

- Ezekiel speaks by similitude and dark sayings, so the house here is not a literal building. This is the spiritual house, which is the house of Israel.
- The waters that issue out from under the threshold are the word of the Most High. Just as a tub fills when the water is left running, the word is now being issued out to the four corners of the earth.

**[Ezekiel 47:3-5](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)</sup> And when the man that had the line in his hand went forth eastward, he measured a thousand cubits, and he brought me through the waters; the waters were to the ankles.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/47#v4)</sup> Again he measured a thousand, and brought me through the waters; the waters were to the knees. Again he measured a thousand, and brought me through; the waters were to the loins.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/47#v5)</sup> Afterward he measured a thousand; and it was a river that I could not pass over: for the waters were risen, waters to swim in, a river that could not be passed over.

- The man with the measuring line brings the prophet through the waters, and they rise from the ankles, to the knees, to the loins, until it is a river that cannot be passed over, waters to swim in.
- The rising water is the word being preached and the understanding increasing as it goes to the four corners of the earth: people coming to understand that Christ is a black man, that we are the Israelites, and that we must keep the commandments.
- The understanding rises in stages, just as understanding came out over time in the classes, the head covering out of First Corinthians 11, the sabbath, and the new moon being the full moon around 2006 to 2007. As long as we stay in the spirit more revelation will keep coming.
- The measuring line measures us up to the stature of Christ, the understanding of the laws and the doctrines.

**[Ezekiel 47:21-23](/bible/ezekiel/47#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/47#v21)</sup> So shall ye divide this land unto you according to the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/47#v22)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that ye shall divide it by lot for an inheritance unto you, and to the strangers that sojourn among you, which shall beget children among you: and they shall be unto you as born in the country among the children of Israel; they shall have inheritance with you among the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/47#v23)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that in what tribe the stranger sojourneth, there shall ye give him his inheritance, saith the Lord God.

- The land is divided by lot for an inheritance, and the strangers that sojourn among you and beget children are counted as born in the country and given inheritance in the tribe where they sojourn.
- The stranger here is not another nation. A stranger is an Israelite living out of his own tribe's land; if you are of Judah and you sojourn in Benjamin, you are counted a stranger. Christianity uses this verse to claim the other nations dwell among Israel and receive the land, but that is not what is being said.
