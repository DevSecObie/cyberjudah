Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 23](/bible/ezekiel/23)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 23 through 26. The first two chapters show how the Most High judged us for our spiritual whoredom and idolatry: chapter 23 is the allegory of two sisters, Aholah the northern kingdom and Aholibah the southern kingdom, who went whoring after the idols of the nations, and chapter 24 is the boiling pot and the death of Ezekiel's wife as a sign of the siege. The last two chapters turn on the heathen: chapters 25 and 26 declare how the Most High will judge the surrounding nations for mocking us and rejoicing when we were carried into captivity. It is a heavy book, twofold, dealing both with what happened then in history and what is still to come upon these nations.

**[Ezekiel 23:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/23#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord came again unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/23#v2)</sup> Son of man, there were two women, the daughters of one mother:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/23#v3)</sup> And they committed whoredoms in Egypt; they committed whoredoms in their youth: there were their breasts pressed, and there they bruised the teats of their virginity.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/23#v4)</sup> And the names of them were Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister: and they were mine, and they bare sons and daughters. Thus were their names; Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah.

- The word of the Most High comes to Ezekiel about two women, the daughters of one mother, an allegory to depict the whoredoms we committed as a nation. They committed whoredoms in Egypt in their youth, where their breasts were pressed and the teats of their virginity bruised, meaning Israel was already living loose.
- The elder is Aholah and her sister is Aholibah; they were the Most High's and bare sons and daughters. Aholah is Samaria, the northern kingdom, and Aholibah is Jerusalem, the southern kingdom.
  - **[Isaiah 7:9](/bible/isaiah/7#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/7#v9)</sup> And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah’s son. If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established.

    The capital of Ephraim is Samaria, proving Aholah/Samaria is the northern kingdom of Ephraim.
  - **[Ezra 1:5](/bible/ezra/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/1#v5)</sup> Then rose up the chief of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests, and the Levites, with all them whose spirit God had raised, to go up to build the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem.

    Judah, Benjamin, and Levi were the ones the Most High raised up to return and build the house in Jerusalem, marking the southern kingdom as Aholibah/Jerusalem.

**[Ezekiel 23:5-8](/bible/ezekiel/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/23#v5)</sup> And Aholah played the harlot when she was mine; and she doted on her lovers, on the Assyrians her neighbours,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/23#v6)</sup> Which were clothed with blue, captains and rulers, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding upon horses.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/23#v7)</sup> Thus she committed her whoredoms with them, with all them that were the chosen men of Assyria, and with all on whom she doted: with all their idols she defiled herself.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/23#v8)</sup> Neither left she her whoredoms brought from Egypt: for in her youth they lay with her, and they bruised the breasts of her virginity, and poured their whoredom upon her.

- Aholah played the harlot while she was still the Most High's and doted on her lovers, the Assyrians her neighbours. To dote is to love to excess or extravagance, so the northern kingdom was overdoing it, excessively in love upon the Assyrians who were clothed in blue, captains and rulers, desirable young men riding horses.
- She committed her whoredoms with the chosen men of Assyria and defiled herself with all their idols, and she never left the whoredoms she brought from Egypt, for in her youth they lay with her and bruised her breasts. The whoredom is idolatry taking place in the spirit realm.
  - **[Hosea 12:1](/bible/hosea/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/12#v1)</sup> Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind: he daily increaseth lies and desolation; and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.

    Ephraim made a covenant with the Assyrians while oil was carried into Egypt, which is how we thought to trust upon them.

**[Ezekiel 23:9-10](/bible/ezekiel/23#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/23#v9)</sup> Wherefore I have delivered her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the Assyrians, upon whom she doted.
>

    In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria and carried Israel away captive into Assyria, when the judgment came to pass.

**[Ezekiel 23:11-13](/bible/ezekiel/23#v11)**

- She doted upon the Assyrians too, their captains and rulers clothed most gorgeously, desirable young men riding horses. Both sisters took one way, and the Most High saw she was defiled.

- As soon as she saw them with her eyes she doted upon them and sent messengers to them into Chaldea, going after them immediately.

**[Ezekiel 23:17-21](/bible/ezekiel/23#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/23#v17)</sup> And the Babylonians came to her into the bed of love, and they defiled her with their whoredom, and she was polluted with them, and her mind was alienated from them.
>

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/23#v19)</sup> Yet she multiplied her whoredoms, in calling to remembrance the days of her youth, wherein she had played the harlot in the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/23#v20)</sup> For she doted upon their paramours, whose flesh is as the flesh of asses, and whose issue is like the issue of horses.

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/23#v21)</sup> Thus thou calledst to remembrance the lewdness of thy youth, in bruising thy teats by the Egyptians for the paps of thy youth.

- The Babylonians came to her into the bed of love and defiled her with their whoredom, and then her mind was alienated from them, just as a woman who lies with a man she is not married to becomes alienated and is left out there. So the Most High alienated His mind from us as He did from her sister.
- Yet she multiplied her whoredoms, calling to remembrance the days of her youth in Egypt, for she doted upon paramours whose flesh is as the flesh of asses and whose issue is like that of horses. Like a harlot who keeps seeking bigger because she has stopped feeling, we constantly sought the nations with the biggest and baddest idols.

**[Ezekiel 23:22-27](/bible/ezekiel/23#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/23#v22)</sup> Therefore, O Aholibah, thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will raise up thy lovers against thee, from whom thy mind is alienated, and I will bring them against thee on every side;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/23#v23)</sup> The Babylonians, and all the Chaldeans, Pekod, and Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians with them: all of them desirable young men, captains and rulers, great lords and renowned, all of them riding upon horses.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/23#v24)</sup> And they shall come against thee with chariots, wagons, and wheels, and with an assembly of people, which shall set against thee buckler and shield and helmet round about: and I will set judgment before them, and they shall judge thee according to their judgments.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/23#v25)</sup> And I will set my jealousy against thee, and they shall deal furiously with thee: they shall take away thy nose and thine ears; and thy remnant shall fall by the sword: they shall take thy sons and thy daughters; and thy residue shall be devoured by the fire.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/23#v26)</sup> They shall also strip thee out of thy clothes, and take away thy fair jewels.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/23#v27)</sup> Thus will I make thy lewdness to cease from thee, and thy whoredom brought from the land of Egypt: so that thou shalt not lift up thine eyes unto them, nor remember Egypt any more.

- Because her mind is alienated from them, the Most High will raise up her former lovers against her on every side: the Babylonians, all the Chaldeans, Pekod, Shoa, and Koa, and all the Assyrians, with chariots, wagons, and an assembly of people set against her.
- They shall judge her according to their judgments, and He will set His jealousy against her so they deal furiously and take away her nose and her ears. It was all love and doting before, but now it becomes rape, one of the atrocities we have suffered throughout our captivities.
- Her remnant shall fall by the sword, her sons and daughters be taken, the residue devoured by fire; they strip her clothes and take her jewels, making her lewdness and her Egyptian whoredoms to cease so she will not lift her eyes to the nations anymore.

>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ezekiel/23#v29)</sup> And they shall deal with thee hatefully, and shall take away all thy labour, and shall leave thee naked and bare: and the nakedness of thy whoredoms shall be discovered, both thy lewdness and thy whoredoms.

>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/23#v31)</sup> Thou hast walked in the way of thy sister; therefore will I give her cup into thine hand.

- The Most High will deliver her into the hand of them she hated, from whom her mind is alienated, and they shall deal with her hatefully, take away all her labour, and leave her naked and bare, the way men turn on a harlot down the line.
- The nakedness of her whoredom shall be discovered because she went whoring after the heathen and polluted herself with their idols. Because she walked in the way of her sister, He gives her sister's cup into her hand.

**[Ezekiel 23:32-34](/bible/ezekiel/23#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/ezekiel/23#v32)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; Thou shalt drink of thy sister’s cup deep and large: thou shalt be laughed to scorn and had in derision; it containeth much.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/ezekiel/23#v33)</sup> Thou shalt be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, with the cup of astonishment and desolation, with the cup of thy sister Samaria.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/ezekiel/23#v34)</sup> Thou shalt even drink it and suck it out, and thou shalt break the sherds thereof, and pluck off thine own breasts: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God.

- She shall drink of her sister Samaria's cup, deep and large, a cup of astonishment and desolation, and be laughed to scorn and had in derision. The Most High is explicit with His judgment because we explicitly violated His laws.
- She shall be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, drink the cup and suck it out, break the sherds thereof, and pluck off her own breasts, because being a harlot has a terrible end.

**[Ezekiel 23:35](/bible/ezekiel/23#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/ezekiel/23#v35)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because thou hast forgotten me, and cast me behind thy back, therefore bear thou also thy lewdness and thy whoredoms.

- Because she forgot the Most High and cast Him behind her back, she must bear her lewdness and her whoredoms.
- Like a wife who leaves a husband who provided and protected her for other men who only take advantage, we turned our back and traded the life of a wife for the life of a victim run through.

- They defiled His sanctuary the same day and profaned His sabbaths, for when they had slain their children to their idols they came the very same day into His sanctuary to profane it, one foot in and one foot out, exactly as we still do when we sin all night and sit in church the next morning.

Like an adulteress fixing herself up in her husband's house with his own things to lie with another man, we prepared the bed with His incense for the nations.
- A voice of a multitude at ease was with her, and Sabeans and drunkards were brought from the wilderness who put bracelets on her hands and crowns on her head; being old in adulteries, the nations went in unto her as men go in unto a harlot.

**[Ezekiel 23:45-49](/bible/ezekiel/23#v45)**

> <sup>[45](/bible/ezekiel/23#v45)</sup> And the righteous men, they shall judge them after the manner of adulteresses, and after the manner of women that shed blood; because they are adulteresses, and blood is in their hands.

>
> <sup>[47](/bible/ezekiel/23#v47)</sup> And the company shall stone them with stones, and dispatch them with their swords; they shall slay their sons and their daughters, and burn up their houses with fire.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/ezekiel/23#v48)</sup> Thus will I cause lewdness to cease out of the land, that all women may be taught not to do after your lewdness.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/ezekiel/23#v49)</sup> And they shall recompense your lewdness upon you, and ye shall bear the sins of your idols: and ye shall know that I am the Lord God.

- The righteous men shall judge them after the manner of adulteresses and women that shed blood, for they are adulterous and blood is in their hands. The righteous call out the whoredom and warn of the judgment, unlike the unrighteous man who tells a woman to just do her and offers no correction.
- The Most High will bring up a company against them to remove and spoil them, stone them with stones, dispatch them with swords, slay their sons and daughters, and burn their houses. This causes lewdness to cease out of the land so that all women may be taught not to do after their lewdness, and they shall bear the sins of their idols and know that He is the Lord God.
