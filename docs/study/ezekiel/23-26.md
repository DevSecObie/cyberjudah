Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 23](/bible/ezekiel/23)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 23 through 26. The first two chapters show how the Most High judged us for our spiritual whoredom and idolatry: chapter 23 is the allegory of two sisters, Aholah the northern kingdom and Aholibah the southern kingdom, who went whoring after the idols of the nations, and chapter 24 is the boiling pot and the death of Ezekiel's wife as a sign of the siege. The last two chapters turn on the heathen: chapters 25 and 26 declare how the Most High will judge the surrounding nations for mocking us and rejoicing when we were carried into captivity. It is a heavy book, twofold, dealing both with what happened then in history and what is still to come upon these nations.

**[Ezekiel 23:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/23#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord came again unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/23#v2)</sup> Son of man, there were two women, the daughters of one mother:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/23#v3)</sup> And they committed whoredoms in Egypt; they committed whoredoms in their youth: there were their breasts pressed, and there they bruised the teats of their virginity.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/23#v4)</sup> And the names of them were Aholah the elder, and Aholibah her sister: and they were mine, and they bare sons and daughters. Thus were their names; Samaria is Aholah, and Jerusalem Aholibah.

- The word of the Most High comes to Ezekiel about two women, the daughters of one mother, an allegory to depict the whoredoms we committed as a nation. They committed whoredoms in Egypt in their youth, where their breasts were pressed and the teats of their virginity bruised, meaning Israel was already living loose.
- The elder is Aholah and her sister is Aholibah; they were the Most High's and bare sons and daughters. Aholah is Samaria, the northern kingdom, and Aholibah is Jerusalem, the southern kingdom.
  - **[Isaiah 7:9](/bible/isaiah/7#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/7#v9)</sup> And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah’s son. If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established.

    The capital of Ephraim is Samaria, proving Aholah/Samaria is the northern kingdom of Ephraim.
  - **[Ezra 1:5](/bible/ezra/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezra/1#v5)</sup> Then rose up the chief of the fathers of Judah and Benjamin, and the priests, and the Levites, with all them whose spirit God had raised, to go up to build the house of the Lord which is in Jerusalem.

    Judah, Benjamin, and Levi were the ones the Most High raised up to return and build the house in Jerusalem, marking the southern kingdom as Aholibah/Jerusalem.

**[Ezekiel 23:5-8](/bible/ezekiel/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/23#v5)</sup> And Aholah played the harlot when she was mine; and she doted on her lovers, on the Assyrians her neighbours,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/23#v6)</sup> Which were clothed with blue, captains and rulers, all of them desirable young men, horsemen riding upon horses.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/23#v7)</sup> Thus she committed her whoredoms with them, with all them that were the chosen men of Assyria, and with all on whom she doted: with all their idols she defiled herself.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/23#v8)</sup> Neither left she her whoredoms brought from Egypt: for in her youth they lay with her, and they bruised the breasts of her virginity, and poured their whoredom upon her.

- Aholah played the harlot while she was still the Most High's and doted on her lovers, the Assyrians her neighbours. To dote is to love to excess or extravagance, so the northern kingdom was overdoing it, excessively in love upon the Assyrians who were clothed in blue, captains and rulers, desirable young men riding horses.
- She committed her whoredoms with the chosen men of Assyria and defiled herself with all their idols, and she never left the whoredoms she brought from Egypt, for in her youth they lay with her and bruised her breasts. The whoredom is idolatry taking place in the spirit realm.

    > <sup>[1](/bible/hosea/12#v1)</sup> Ephraim feedeth on wind, and followeth after the east wind: he daily increaseth lies and desolation; and they do make a covenant with the Assyrians, and oil is carried into Egypt.

    Ephraim made a covenant with the Assyrians while oil was carried into Egypt, which is how we thought to trust upon them.

**[Ezekiel 23:9-10](/bible/ezekiel/23#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/23#v9)</sup> Wherefore I have delivered her into the hand of her lovers, into the hand of the Assyrians, upon whom she doted.

    In the ninth year of Hoshea the king of Assyria took Samaria and carried Israel away captive into Assyria, when the judgment came to pass.

So the Most High alienated His mind from us as He did from her sister.
- Yet she multiplied her whoredoms, calling to remembrance the days of her youth in Egypt, for she doted upon paramours whose flesh is as the flesh of asses and whose issue is like that of horses. Like a harlot who keeps seeking bigger because she has stopped feeling, we constantly sought the nations with the biggest and baddest idols.

- They shall judge her according to their judgments, and He will set His jealousy against her so they deal furiously and take away her nose and her ears. It was all love and doting before, but now it becomes rape, one of the atrocities we have suffered throughout our captivities.
- Her remnant shall fall by the sword, her sons and daughters be taken, the residue devoured by fire; they strip her clothes and take her jewels, making her lewdness and her Egyptian whoredoms to cease so she will not lift her eyes to the nations anymore.

- The nakedness of her whoredom shall be discovered because she went whoring after the heathen and polluted herself with their idols. Because she walked in the way of her sister, He gives her sister's cup into her hand.

- She shall drink of her sister Samaria's cup, deep and large, a cup of astonishment and desolation, and be laughed to scorn and had in derision. The Most High is explicit with His judgment because we explicitly violated His laws.
- She shall be filled with drunkenness and sorrow, drink the cup and suck it out, break the sherds thereof, and pluck off her own breasts, because being a harlot has a terrible end.

**[Ezekiel 23:35](/bible/ezekiel/23#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/ezekiel/23#v35)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because thou hast forgotten me, and cast me behind thy back, therefore bear thou also thy lewdness and thy whoredoms.

- Like a wife who leaves a husband who provided and protected her for other men who only take advantage, we turned our back and traded the life of a wife for the life of a victim run through.

# Ezekiel 24: The Boiling Pot and the Sign of the Prophet's Wife

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 24](/bible/ezekiel/24)

**[Ezekiel 24:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/24#v1)</sup> Again in the ninth year, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/24#v2)</sup> Son of man, write thee the name of the day, even of this same day: the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem this same day.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/24#v3)</sup> And utter a parable unto the rebellious house, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; Set on a pot, set it on, and also pour water into it:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/24#v4)</sup> Gather the pieces thereof into it, even every good piece, the thigh, and the shoulder; fill it with the choice bones.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/24#v5)</sup> Take the choice of the flock, and burn also the bones under it, and make it boil well, and let them seethe the bones of it therein.

- In the ninth year, tenth month, and tenth day, the word of the Most High comes on the very day the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem, and Ezekiel is told to write the name of the day and utter a parable to the rebellious house.
- He is told to set on a pot, pour in water, and gather the pieces, the thigh and the shoulder and the choice bones, taking the choice of the flock and burning the bones under it to make it boil well. This pictures Babylon taking out the best of our mighty men and leaders first.

**[Ezekiel 24:6-8](/bible/ezekiel/24#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/24#v6)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God; Woe to the bloody city, to the pot whose scum is therein, and whose scum is not gone out of it! bring it out piece by piece; let no lot fall upon it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/24#v7)</sup> For her blood is in the midst of her; she set it upon the top of a rock; she poured it not upon the ground, to cover it with dust;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/24#v8)</sup> That it might cause fury to come up to take vengeance; I have set her blood upon the top of a rock, that it should not be covered.

- Woe to the bloody city, to the pot whose scum is therein; He calls the choice pieces, the best of the Israelites we looked up to, scum, because of the laws we broke. The Most High will call you something you do not want to be called.
- Her blood is in the midst of her, set upon the top of a rock and not poured on the ground to be covered, so that it might cause fury to come up and take vengeance.

**[Ezekiel 24:9-13](/bible/ezekiel/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/24#v9)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Woe to the bloody city! I will even make the pile for fire great.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/24#v10)</sup> Heap on wood, kindle the fire, consume the flesh, and spice it well, and let the bones be burned.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/24#v11)</sup> Then set it empty upon the coals thereof, that the brass of it may be hot, and may burn, and that the filthiness of it may be molten in it, that the scum of it may be consumed.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/24#v12)</sup> She hath wearied herself with lies, and her great scum went not forth out of her: her scum shall be in the fire.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/24#v13)</sup> In thy filthiness is lewdness: because I have purged thee, and thou wast not purged, thou shalt not be purged from thy filthiness any more, till I have caused my fury to rest upon thee.

- Woe to the bloody city: the Most High will make the pile for fire great, heap on wood, kindle the fire, consume the flesh, spice it well, and let the bones be burnt, then set the empty pot on the coals till the brass is hot and the scum consumed. He does not merely cook it and let the flame die; He stacks the wood and keeps it lit so we cook long and well.
- Because her filthiness is lewdness and He purged her yet she was not purged, she shall not be purged from her filthiness anymore until He has caused His fury to rest upon her. He will not go back, nor spare, nor repent; there is no more sackcloth or fasting to turn Him back at this point.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:49-52](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)**
    > <sup>[49](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v49)</sup> The Lord shall bring a nation against thee from far, from the end of the earth, as swift as the eagle flieth; a nation whose tongue thou shalt not understand;
    >

    >

    The Most High warned that a nation from far, whose tongue we understand not, would besiege us in all our gates, the curse that had to be inflicted once we went too far.

**[Ezekiel 24:15-18](/bible/ezekiel/24#v15)**

>

>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/24#v17)</sup> Forbear to cry, make no mourning for the dead, bind the tire of thine head upon thee, and put on thy shoes upon thy feet, and cover not thy lips, and eat not the bread of men.
>

- Ezekiel spoke to the people in the morning, and in the evening his wife died, and he did in the morning as he was commanded. This shows the life of a prophet, whose purpose is above family, love, and children, so when a wife or a distraction interferes with the work the Most High assigned, the purpose comes first.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 19:2](/bible/sirach/19#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/19#v2)</sup> Wine and women will make men of understanding to fall away: and he that cleaveth to harlots will become impudent.

  - **[1 Corinthians 7:35](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v35)</sup> And this I speak for your own profit; not that I may cast a snare upon you, but for that which is comely, and that ye may attend upon the Lord without distraction.

    Paul urged the single life so that a man may attend upon the Most High without distraction, which is exactly what the Most High secured for Ezekiel.

**[Ezekiel 24:19-24](/bible/ezekiel/24#v19)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/24#v23)</sup> And your tires shall be upon your heads, and your shoes upon your feet: ye shall not mourn nor weep; but ye shall pine away for your iniquities, and mourn one toward another.

Just as his wife was the desire of his eyes, the beautiful temple was the desire of the nation's eyes.

    The prophet and the children the Most High gave him are set for signs and wonders in Israel, as He used prophets, their wives, and their children to picture what would come upon the nation.

**[Ezekiel 24:25-27](/bible/ezekiel/24#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/24#v25)</sup> Also, thou son of man, shall it not be in the day when I take from them their strength, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and that whereupon they set their minds, their sons and their daughters,

- In that day Ezekiel's mouth shall be opened to the one who escaped, and he shall be no more dumb but a sign unto them, that they may know He is the Lord. When it comes to pass they will come spooked and shocked, and he will tell them the Most High had told them.

# Ezekiel 25: Judgment on Ammon, Moab, Edom, and the Philistines

**[Ezekiel 25:1-7](/bible/ezekiel/25#v1)**

>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/25#v2)</sup> Son of man, set thy face against the Ammonites, and prophesy against them;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/25#v3)</sup> And say unto the Ammonites, Hear the word of the Lord God; Thus saith the Lord God; Because thou saidst, Aha, against my sanctuary, when it was profaned; and against the land of Israel, when it was desolate; and against the house of Judah, when they went into captivity;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/25#v4)</sup> Behold, therefore I will deliver thee to the men of the east for a possession, and they shall set their palaces in thee, and make their dwellings in thee: they shall eat thy fruit, and they shall drink thy milk.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/25#v5)</sup> And I will make Rabbah a stable for camels, and the Ammonites a couchingplace for flocks: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/25#v6)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God; Because thou hast clapped thine hands, and stamped with the feet, and rejoiced in heart with all thy despite against the land of Israel;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/25#v7)</sup> Behold, therefore I will stretch out mine hand upon thee, and will deliver thee for a spoil to the heathen; and I will cut thee off from the people, and I will cause thee to perish out of the countries: I will destroy thee; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.

- The word of the Most High comes against the Ammonites, the so-called Japanese, because they said Aha against His sanctuary when it was profaned, against the land of Israel when it was desolate, and against the house of Judah when they went into captivity.
- Because they clapped their hands, stamped their feet, and rejoiced in heart with all their despite against Israel, cheering our destruction, He will deliver them to the men of the east for a possession, cut them off from the people, and cause them to perish, that they may know He is the Lord.

**[Ezekiel 25:8-11](/bible/ezekiel/25#v8)**

>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/25#v9)</sup> Therefore, behold, I will open the side of Moab from the cities, from his cities which are on his frontiers, the glory of the country, Beth–jeshimoth, Baal–meon, and Kiriathaim,

> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/25#v10)</sup> Unto the men of the east with the Ammonites, and will give them in possession, that the Ammonites may not be remembered among the nations.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/25#v11)</sup> And I will execute judgments upon Moab; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- Because Moab, the so-called Chinese, and Seir say that the house of Judah is like unto all the heathen, claiming Judah is no longer the Most High's chosen people, judgment is pronounced against them.
- The Most High will open the side of Moab from his frontier cities, the glory of the country, and deliver them to the men of the east along with the Ammonites so that the Ammonites be not remembered among the nations, and He will execute judgments upon Moab.

**[Ezekiel 25:12-14](/bible/ezekiel/25#v12)**

>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/25#v13)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; I will also stretch out mine hand upon Edom, and will cut off man and beast from it; and I will make it desolate from Teman; and they of Dedan shall fall by the sword.

- Because Edom, the so-called white man, dealt against the house of Judah by taking vengeance and greatly offended, the Most High will stretch out His hand upon Edom, cut off man and beast, and make it desolate from Teman, and they of Dedan shall fall by the sword.
- He will lay His vengeance upon Edom by the hand of His people Israel, and they shall do in Edom according to His anger and His fury. This is future prophecy that has not yet come to pass, for the Most High must first change our minds to act according to His anger to perform righteous recompense with our own hands.

  - **[Obadiah 1:21](/bible/obadiah/1#v21)**

# Ezekiel 26: The Fall of Tyrus

**[Ezekiel 26:1-6](/bible/ezekiel/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/26#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/26#v2)</sup> Son of man, because that Tyrus hath said against Jerusalem, Aha, she is broken that was the gates of the people: she is turned unto me: I shall be replenished, now she is laid waste:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/26#v3)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I am against thee, O Tyrus, and will cause many nations to come up against thee, as the sea causeth his waves to come up.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/26#v4)</sup> And they shall destroy the walls of Tyrus, and break down her towers: I will also scrape her dust from her, and make her like the top of a rock.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/26#v5)</sup> It shall be a place for the spreading of nets in the midst of the sea: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God: and it shall become a spoil to the nations.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/26#v6)</sup> And her daughters which are in the field shall be slain by the sword; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- In the eleventh year, on the first day of the month, the word comes because Tyrus said against Jerusalem, Aha, she that was the gate of the peoples is broken and turned to me, so I shall be replenished now she is laid waste. Tyrus saw our loss of Jerusalem as their come-up and profit.
- Therefore the Most High is against Tyrus and will cause many nations to come up against her like the sea casting up its waves; they shall destroy her walls, break her towers, scrape her dust, and make her like the top of a rock, a place for spreading nets, and her daughters in the field shall be slain.

**[Ezekiel 26:7-14](/bible/ezekiel/26#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/26#v7)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will bring upon Tyrus Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, a king of kings, from the north, with horses, and with chariots, and with horsemen, and companies, and much people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/26#v8)</sup> He shall slay with the sword thy daughters in the field: and he shall make a fort against thee, and cast a mount against thee, and lift up the buckler against thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/26#v9)</sup> And he shall set engines of war against thy walls, and with his axes he shall break down thy towers.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/26#v10)</sup> By reason of the abundance of his horses their dust shall cover thee: thy walls shall shake at the noise of the horsemen, and of the wheels, and of the chariots, when he shall enter into thy gates, as men enter into a city wherein is made a breach.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/26#v11)</sup> With the hoofs of his horses shall he tread down all thy streets: he shall slay thy people by the sword, and thy strong garrisons shall go down to the ground.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/26#v12)</sup> And they shall make a spoil of thy riches, and make a prey of thy merchandise: and they shall break down thy walls, and destroy thy pleasant houses: and they shall lay thy stones and thy timber and thy dust in the midst of the water.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/26#v13)</sup> And I will cause the noise of thy songs to cease; and the sound of thy harps shall be no more heard.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/26#v14)</sup> And I will make thee like the top of a rock: thou shalt be a place to spread nets upon; thou shalt be built no more: for I the Lord have spoken it, saith the Lord God.

- The Most High will bring Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon, a king of kings from the north, with horses, chariots, and horsemen, to slay her daughters, build a fort, cast a mount, and set engines of war against her walls and towers. Babylon serves as His sword against the nations that thought our downfall was their gain.
- His horses shall cover her with dust and tread down her streets; he shall slay her people, spoil her riches, prey on her merchandise, break her walls, destroy her pleasant houses, and lay her stones, timber, and dust in the midst of the water. He will cause the noise of her songs to cease and make her like the top of a rock, a place to spread nets, built no more.

    The Most High sets His prophet over the nations to root out, pull down, and destroy, which is why Ezekiel had to prophesy Tyrus's total ruin to her face.
