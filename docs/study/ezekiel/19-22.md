Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 19](/bible/ezekiel/19)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 19 through 22. It opens with a lamentation over the princes of Israel, picturing the mother as a lioness whose whelps became young lions that devoured men and were dragged off in chains, and as a vine plucked up in fury and cast down from rulership. From there the reading moves to the Most High rehearsing Israel's long rebellion and the reason we are scattered across the four corners of the globe, to the sword drawn against Jerusalem, and finally to the bloody city whose corrupt leaders, priests, and prophets bring the fire of His wrath.

**[Ezekiel 19:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/19#v1)</sup> Moreover take thou up a lamentation for the princes of Israel,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/19#v2)</sup> And say, What is thy mother? A lioness: she lay down among lions, she nourished her whelps among young lions.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/19#v3)</sup> And she brought up one of her whelps: it became a young lion, and it learned to catch the prey; it devoured men.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/19#v4)</sup> The nations also heard of him; he was taken in their pit, and they brought him with chains unto the land of Egypt.

- The Most High tells Ezekiel to take up a lamentation for the princes of Israel, asking what is thy mother, a lioness that lay down among lions and nourished her whelps among young lions.
- One of her whelps became a young lion that learned to catch the prey and devoured men, picturing how a particular leader devoured his own people.
- The nations heard of him, took him in their pit, and brought him with chains unto the land of Egypt, showing that when the leader is judged he is dragged off into captivity.

**[Ezekiel 19:5-9](/bible/ezekiel/19#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/19#v5)</sup> Now when she saw that she had waited, and her hope was lost, then she took another of her whelps, and made him a young lion.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/19#v6)</sup> And he went up and down among the lions, he became a young lion, and learned to catch the prey, and devoured men.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/19#v7)</sup> And he knew their desolate palaces, and he laid waste their cities; and the land was desolate, and the fulness thereof, by the noise of his roaring.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/19#v8)</sup> Then the nations set against him on every side from the provinces, and spread their net over him: he was taken in their pit.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/19#v9)</sup> And they put him in ward in chains, and brought him to the king of Babylon: they brought him into holds, that his voice should no more be heard upon the mountains of Israel.

- When the mother saw her hope was lost she took another of her whelps and made him a young lion, and he too learned to catch the prey and devoured men.
- He knew their desolate places, laid waste their cities, and left the land desolate by the noise of his roaring, so the nations set against him on every side and spread their net over him.
- The judgment is that the Most High always sends the sword, and the sword is the nations; they came up and set a net and a trap all around Jerusalem so there was no escape.
- They put him in a ward and chains and brought him to the king of Babylon, so he is no longer roaring like a lion but sits chained in Babylon.

**[Ezekiel 19:10-14](/bible/ezekiel/19#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/19#v10)</sup> Thy mother is like a vine in thy blood, planted by the waters: she was fruitful and full of branches by reason of many waters.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/19#v11)</sup> And she had strong rods for the sceptres of them that bare rule, and her stature was exalted among the thick branches, and she appeared in her height with the multitude of her branches.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/19#v12)</sup> But she was plucked up in fury, she was cast down to the ground, and the east wind dried up her fruit: her strong rods were broken and withered; the fire consumed them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/19#v13)</sup> And now she is planted in the wilderness, in a dry and thirsty ground.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/19#v14)</sup> And fire is gone out of a rod of her branches, which hath devoured her fruit, so that she hath no strong rod to be a sceptre to rule. This is a lamentation, and shall be for a lamentation.

- The mother is likened to a vine planted by the waters, fruitful and full of branches, with strong rods for the scepters of them that bear rule, exalted among the thick branches.
- But she was plucked up in fury and cast down to the ground, meaning cast down from rulership, and the east wind, which is judgment, dried up her fruit.
- Her strong rods were broken and withered and the fire consumed them, and now she is planted in the wilderness in a dry and thirsty ground with no strong rod left to be a scepter to rule; this is the lamentation.

# Ezekiel 20: I Will Not Be Inquired Of

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 20](/bible/ezekiel/20)

**[Ezekiel 20:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/20#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in the seventh year, in the fifth month, the tenth day of the month, that certain of the elders of Israel came to enquire of the Lord, and sat before me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/20#v2)</sup> Then came the word of the Lord unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/20#v3)</sup> Son of man, speak unto the elders of Israel, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; Are ye come to enquire of me? As I live, saith the Lord God, I will not be enquired of by you.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/20#v4)</sup> Wilt thou judge them, son of man, wilt thou judge them? cause them to know the abominations of their fathers:

- In the seventh year, the fifth month, the tenth day, certain of the elders of Israel came to inquire of the Most High and sat before Ezekiel.
- The Most High refuses to be inquired of by them and instead tells Ezekiel to judge them and cause them to know the abominations of their fathers.
- Just as the Most High through Ezekiel had to remind the elders what He did for their forefathers, today through the prophets we have to remind our people where we came from and what happened to us.

**[Ezekiel 20:5-9](/bible/ezekiel/20#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/20#v5)</sup> And say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; In the day when I chose Israel, and lifted up mine hand unto the seed of the house of Jacob, and made myself known unto them in the land of Egypt, when I lifted up mine hand unto them, saying, I am the Lord your God;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/20#v6)</sup> In the day that I lifted up mine hand unto them, to bring them forth of the land of Egypt into a land that I had espied for them, flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/20#v7)</sup> Then said I unto them, Cast ye away every man the abominations of his eyes, and defile not yourselves with the idols of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/20#v8)</sup> But they rebelled against me, and would not hearken unto me: they did not every man cast away the abominations of their eyes, neither did they forsake the idols of Egypt: then I said, I will pour out my fury upon them, to accomplish my anger against them in the midst of the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/20#v9)</sup> But I wrought for my name’s sake, that it should not be polluted before the heathen, among whom they were, in whose sight I made myself known unto them, in bringing them forth out of the land of Egypt.

- The Most High rehearses how in the day He chose Israel He lifted up His hand unto the seed of the house of Jacob and made Himself known unto them in the land of Egypt, saying I am the Lord your God.
- He swore to bring them out of Egypt into a land flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands, and commanded every man to cast away the abominations of his eyes and not defile himself with the idols of Egypt.
- The idols and doings of Egypt are the immoral laws laid out in [Leviticus 18](/bible/leviticus/18), and even the doings of spiritual Egypt today still apply and are not to be done.
- They rebelled and would not cast away the abominations of their eyes nor forsake the idols of Egypt, so He said He would pour out His fury, but He wrought for His name's sake that it should not be polluted before the heathen.
  - **[Leviticus 18:3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3)</sup> After the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do: and after the doings of the land of Canaan, whither I bring you, shall ye not do: neither shall ye walk in their ordinances.

    You shall not do after the doings of the land of Egypt or of Canaan, which is why the idols of Egypt point to the immoral laws that follow in that chapter.

**[Ezekiel 20:10-17](/bible/ezekiel/20#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/20#v10)</sup> Wherefore I caused them to go forth out of the land of Egypt, and brought them into the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/20#v11)</sup> And I gave them my statutes, and shewed them my judgments, which if a man do, he shall even live in them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/20#v12)</sup> Moreover also I gave them my sabbaths, to be a sign between me and them, that they might know that I am the Lord that sanctify them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/20#v13)</sup> But the house of Israel rebelled against me in the wilderness: they walked not in my statutes, and they despised my judgments, which if a man do, he shall even live in them; and my sabbaths they greatly polluted: then I said, I would pour out my fury upon them in the wilderness, to consume them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/20#v14)</sup> But I wrought for my name’s sake, that it should not be polluted before the heathen, in whose sight I brought them out.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/20#v15)</sup> Yet also I lifted up my hand unto them in the wilderness, that I would not bring them into the land which I had given them, flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/20#v16)</sup> Because they despised my judgments, and walked not in my statutes, but polluted my sabbaths: for their heart went after their idols.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/20#v17)</sup> Nevertheless mine eye spared them from destroying them, neither did I make an end of them in the wilderness.

- He caused them to go forth out of Egypt, brought them into the wilderness, and gave them His statutes and showed them His judgments, which if a man do he shall even live in them.
- The Most High gave us the laws and the judgments behind the laws, and Israel is the only one nation of people that He chose to receive them.
- He also gave them His sabbaths to be a sign between Him and them, a sign given to His chosen people Israel, who are the so-called black, Hispanic, and Native American people today.
- But the house of Israel rebelled in the wilderness, walked not in His statutes, despised His judgments, and greatly polluted His sabbaths, because they hated His laws and did not want to do what the Bible says.
- Nevertheless He spared them and did not make an end of them in the wilderness, which is the mercy that endures forever; He chastises His children but did not destroy us utterly.

**[Ezekiel 20:18-26](/bible/ezekiel/20#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/20#v18)</sup> But I said unto their children in the wilderness, Walk ye not in the statutes of your fathers, neither observe their judgments, nor defile yourselves with their idols:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/20#v19)</sup> I am the Lord your God; walk in my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/20#v20)</sup> And hallow my sabbaths; and they shall be a sign between me and you, that ye may know that I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/20#v21)</sup> Notwithstanding the children rebelled against me: they walked not in my statutes, neither kept my judgments to do them, which if a man do, he shall even live in them; they polluted my sabbaths: then I said, I would pour out my fury upon them, to accomplish my anger against them in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/20#v22)</sup> Nevertheless I withdrew mine hand, and wrought for my name’s sake, that it should not be polluted in the sight of the heathen, in whose sight I brought them forth.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/20#v23)</sup> I lifted up mine hand unto them also in the wilderness, that I would scatter them among the heathen, and disperse them through the countries;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/20#v24)</sup> Because they had not executed my judgments, but had despised my statutes, and had polluted my sabbaths, and their eyes were after their fathers’ idols.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/20#v25)</sup> Wherefore I gave them also statutes that were not good, and judgments whereby they should not live;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/20#v26)</sup> And I polluted them in their own gifts, in that they caused to pass through the fire all that openeth the womb, that I might make them desolate, to the end that they might know that I am the Lord.

- He told the children in the wilderness not to walk in the statutes of their fathers but to walk in His statutes, keep His judgments, and hallow His sabbaths as a sign between Him and them.
- Notwithstanding, the children also rebelled, walked not in His statutes, and polluted His sabbaths, so He lifted up His hand to scatter them among the heathen and disperse them through the countries.
- This is the reason we are scattered across the four corners of the globe today: because they had not executed His judgments, despised His statutes, polluted His sabbaths, and their eyes were after their fathers' idols; it is the same thing today.
- Because they refused His law He gave them also statutes that were not good and judgments whereby they should not live, giving His own people over to their own ways and to passing their children through the fire.
  - **[Romans 1:23-25](/bible/romans/1#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/1#v23)</sup> And changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/romans/1#v24)</sup> Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves:
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/romans/1#v25)</sup> Who changed the truth of God into a lie, and worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.

    Because they changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into images, He gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, which is what being given statutes that are not good goes into.

> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/20#v27)</sup> Therefore, son of man, speak unto the house of Israel, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; Yet in this your fathers have blasphemed me, in that they have committed a trespass against me.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ezekiel/20#v28)</sup> For when I had brought them into the land, for the which I lifted up mine hand to give it to them, then they saw every high hill, and all the thick trees, and they offered there their sacrifices, and there they presented the provocation of their offering: there also they made their sweet savour, and poured out there their drink offerings.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ezekiel/20#v29)</sup> Then I said unto them, What is the high place whereunto ye go? And the name thereof is called Bamah unto this day.

- The fathers blasphemed the Most High and committed a trespass, for when He brought them into the land they saw every high hill and thick tree and offered their sacrifices and drink offerings there.
- These were the high places where Israel worshiped idols, and the name of the high place was called Bamah unto this day.

**[Ezekiel 20:30-32](/bible/ezekiel/20#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/20#v30)</sup> Wherefore say unto the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord God; Are ye polluted after the manner of your fathers? and commit ye whoredom after their abominations?
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/20#v31)</sup> For when ye offer your gifts, when ye make your sons to pass through the fire, ye pollute yourselves with all your idols, even unto this day: and shall I be enquired of by you, O house of Israel? As I live, saith the Lord God, I will not be enquired of by you.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ezekiel/20#v32)</sup> And that which cometh into your mind shall not be at all, that ye say, We will be as the heathen, as the families of the countries, to serve wood and stone.

- He asks whether they are polluted after the manner of their fathers, making their sons pass through the fire and polluting themselves with idols even unto this day.
- He will not be inquired of by a people whose mind says we will be as the heathen to serve wood and stone; though we have the one true power, we want to be like everybody else.

**[Ezekiel 20:33-38](/bible/ezekiel/20#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/ezekiel/20#v33)</sup> As I live, saith the Lord God, surely with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out, will I rule over you:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/ezekiel/20#v34)</sup> And I will bring you out from the people, and will gather you out of the countries wherein ye are scattered, with a mighty hand, and with a stretched out arm, and with fury poured out.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/ezekiel/20#v35)</sup> And I will bring you into the wilderness of the people, and there will I plead with you face to face.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/ezekiel/20#v36)</sup> Like as I pleaded with your fathers in the wilderness of the land of Egypt, so will I plead with you, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/ezekiel/20#v37)</sup> And I will cause you to pass under the rod, and I will bring you into the bond of the covenant:
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/ezekiel/20#v38)</sup> And I will purge out from among you the rebels, and them that transgress against me: I will bring them forth out of the country where they sojourn, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

- With a mighty hand and a stretched out arm and fury poured out He will rule over them, which means two-thirds of us must go for the Most High to rule, and that is prophesied.
- He will bring them out from the people, gather them out of the countries, separate them from the heathen they wanted to be like, and bring them into the wilderness of the people to plead with them face to face and teach them everything they do not yet know of this Bible.
- The word pleaded means He put our fathers to death in the wilderness of Egypt, and it will be the same when we go back to the wilderness.
- He will cause them to pass under the rod, bring them into the bond of the covenant, and purge out the rebels who transgress, and they shall not enter into the land of Israel.
- The way you rehearse and practice now is how you will play in the wilderness, and Christ can read minds, so those who are fronting, faking, and holding secret hate may be purged out and called rebels.
  - **[Judges 5:11](/bible/judges/5#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/judges/5#v11)</sup> They that are delivered from the noise of archers in the places of drawing water, there shall they rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, even the righteous acts toward the inhabitants of his villages in Israel: then shall the people of the Lord go down to the gates.

    Those delivered from the noise of the archers shall rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, meaning we practice now how we will play when it is game time in the wilderness.

- When He brings them into the land of Israel they shall remember their ways and loathe themselves in their own sight, feeling disgusted and hating what they have done, and shall know that He is the Lord when He wrought with them for His name's sake and not according to their wicked ways.

# Ezekiel 21: The Sword of the Lord Is Drawn

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 21](/bible/ezekiel/21)

**[Ezekiel 21:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/21#v1)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/21#v2)</sup> Son of man, set thy face toward Jerusalem, and drop thy word toward the holy places, and prophesy against the land of Israel,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/21#v3)</sup> And say to the land of Israel, Thus saith the Lord; Behold, I am against thee, and will draw forth my sword out of his sheath, and will cut off from thee the righteous and the wicked.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/21#v4)</sup> Seeing then that I will cut off from thee the righteous and the wicked, therefore shall my sword go forth out of his sheath against all flesh from the south to the north:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/21#v5)</sup> That all flesh may know that I the Lord have drawn forth my sword out of his sheath: it shall not return any more.

- The word of the Most High comes to Ezekiel to set his face toward Jerusalem, drop his word toward the holy places, and prophesy against the land of Israel.
- The Most High is against her and will draw forth His sword out of his sheath to cut off the righteous and the wicked, the sword being the nations He sends.
- He judges us as a nation, so the righteous and the wicked all get judged together; the sword goes forth against all flesh from the south to the north and shall not return.

**[Ezekiel 21:6-7](/bible/ezekiel/21#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/21#v6)</sup> Sigh therefore, thou son of man, with the breaking of thy loins; and with bitterness sigh before their eyes.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/21#v7)</sup> And it shall be, when they say unto thee, Wherefore sighest thou? that thou shalt answer, For the tidings; because it cometh: and every heart shall melt, and all hands shall be feeble, and every spirit shall faint, and all knees shall be weak as water: behold, it cometh, and shall be brought to pass, saith the Lord God.

- Ezekiel is told to sigh with the breaking of his loins and with bitterness before their eyes.
- When they ask why he sighs he is to answer that it is for the tidings that come, because every heart shall melt, all hands be feeble, every spirit faint, and all knees be weak as water.

**[Ezekiel 21:8-13](/bible/ezekiel/21#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/21#v8)</sup> Again the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/21#v9)</sup> Son of man, prophesy, and say, Thus saith the Lord; Say, A sword, a sword is sharpened, and also furbished:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/21#v10)</sup> It is sharpened to make a sore slaughter; it is furbished that it may glitter: should we then make mirth? it contemneth the rod of my son, as every tree.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/21#v11)</sup> And he hath given it to be furbished, that it may be handled: this sword is sharpened, and it is furbished, to give it into the hand of the slayer.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/21#v12)</sup> Cry and howl, son of man: for it shall be upon my people, it shall be upon all the princes of Israel: terrors by reason of the sword shall be upon my people: smite therefore upon thy thigh.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/21#v13)</sup> Because it is a trial, and what if the sword contemn even the rod? it shall be no more, saith the Lord God.

- A sword is sharpened and furbished, meaning polished, sharpened to make a sore slaughter and furbished that it may glitter.
- It contemneth the rod of His son, and Judah is that rod; the sword is given to be furbished into the hand of the slayer.
- Ezekiel is to cry and howl, for the sword shall be upon His people and upon all the princes of Israel; it is a trial, a sword that has already been tested by other nations that the Lord uses.

**[Ezekiel 21:14-17](/bible/ezekiel/21#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/21#v14)</sup> Thou therefore, son of man, prophesy, and smite thine hands together, and let the sword be doubled the third time, the sword of the slain: it is the sword of the great men that are slain, which entereth into their privy chambers.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/21#v15)</sup> I have set the point of the sword against all their gates, that their heart may faint, and their ruins be multiplied: ah! it is made bright, it is wrapped up for the slaughter.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/21#v16)</sup> Go thee one way or other, either on the right hand, or on the left, whithersoever thy face is set.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/21#v17)</sup> I will also smite mine hands together, and I will cause my fury to rest: I the Lord have said it.

- Ezekiel is to smite his hands together and let the sword be doubled the third time, the sword of the slain that enters even into their private chambers so there is no escape.
- The point of the sword is set against all their gates, and that point is Babylon pointing at all the gates of Jerusalem.
- The sword goes one way or the other, to the right hand or the left, whichever way it turns to seek judgment and destruction upon Jerusalem, and the Most High's fury shall rest there.

**[Ezekiel 21:18-23](/bible/ezekiel/21#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/21#v18)</sup> The word of the Lord came unto me again, saying,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/21#v19)</sup> Also, thou son of man, appoint thee two ways, that the sword of the king of Babylon may come: both twain shall come forth out of one land: and choose thou a place, choose it at the head of the way to the city.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/21#v20)</sup> Appoint a way, that the sword may come to Rabbath of the Ammonites, and to Judah in Jerusalem the defenced.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/21#v21)</sup> For the king of Babylon stood at the parting of the way, at the head of the two ways, to use divination: he made his arrows bright, he consulted with images, he looked in the liver.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/21#v22)</sup> At his right hand was the divination for Jerusalem, to appoint captains, to open the mouth in the slaughter, to lift up the voice with shouting, to appoint battering rams against the gates, to cast a mount, and to build a fort.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/21#v23)</sup> And it shall be unto them as a false divination in their sight, to them that have sworn oaths: but he will call to remembrance the iniquity, that they may be taken.

- The word of the Most High comes again to appoint two ways for the sword of the king of Babylon to come, both out of one land.
- One way points to Rabbath of the Ammonites and the other to Judah in fenced Jerusalem, and the king of Babylon stands at the parting of the ways to use divination, which is witchcraft.
- He makes his arrows bright, consults with images, and looks in the liver, and at his right hand the divination points for Jerusalem to appoint captains and set battering rams against the gates.

**[Ezekiel 21:24-27](/bible/ezekiel/21#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/21#v24)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because ye have made your iniquity to be remembered, in that your transgressions are discovered, so that in all your doings your sins do appear; because, I say, that ye are come to remembrance, ye shall be taken with the hand.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/21#v25)</sup> And thou, profane wicked prince of Israel, whose day is come, when iniquity shall have an end,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/21#v26)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; Remove the diadem, and take off the crown: this shall not be the same: exalt him that is low, and abase him that is high.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/21#v27)</sup> I will overturn, overturn, overturn, it: and it shall be no more, until he come whose right it is; and I will give it him.

- Because their iniquity is remembered and their transgressions discovered so that their sins appear, they shall be taken with the hand.
- To the profane wicked prince of Israel whose day is come, He says remove the diadem and take off the crown, exalt him that is low and abase him that is high.
- He will overturn, overturn, overturn it, and it shall be no more until he come whose right it is, and He will give it to him.

**[Ezekiel 21:28-32](/bible/ezekiel/21#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/ezekiel/21#v28)</sup> And thou, son of man, prophesy and say, Thus saith the Lord God concerning the Ammonites, and concerning their reproach; even say thou, The sword, the sword is drawn: for the slaughter it is furbished, to consume because of the glittering:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ezekiel/21#v29)</sup> Whiles they see vanity unto thee, whiles they divine a lie unto thee, to bring thee upon the necks of them that are slain, of the wicked, whose day is come, when their iniquity shall have an end.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/21#v30)</sup> Shall I cause it to return into his sheath? I will judge thee in the place where thou wast created, in the land of thy nativity.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/21#v31)</sup> And I will pour out mine indignation upon thee, I will blow against thee in the fire of my wrath, and deliver thee into the hand of brutish men, and skilful to destroy.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ezekiel/21#v32)</sup> Thou shalt be for fuel to the fire; thy blood shall be in the midst of the land; thou shalt be no more remembered: for I the Lord have spoken it.

- Concerning the Ammonites and their reproach, the sword is drawn and furbished to consume because of the glittering.
- While the diviners see vanity and divine a lie, the sword will bring them upon the necks of the slain whose day is come.
- He will judge them in the place where they were created, in the land of their nativity, pour out His indignation, blow against them in the fire of His wrath, and deliver them into the hand of brutish men skilful to destroy; they shall be for fuel to the fire and be no more remembered.

# Ezekiel 22: The Bloody City

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 22](/bible/ezekiel/22)

**[Ezekiel 22:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/22#v1)</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/22#v2)</sup> Now, thou son of man, wilt thou judge, wilt thou judge the bloody city? yea, thou shalt shew her all her abominations.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/22#v3)</sup> Then say thou, Thus saith the Lord God, The city sheddeth blood in the midst of it, that her time may come, and maketh idols against herself to defile herself.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/22#v4)</sup> Thou art become guilty in thy blood that thou hast shed; and hast defiled thyself in thine idols which thou hast made; and thou hast caused thy days to draw near, and art come even unto thy years: therefore have I made thee a reproach unto the heathen, and a mocking to all countries.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/22#v5)</sup> Those that be near, and those that be far from thee, shall mock thee, which art infamous and much vexed.

- The word of the Most High comes to Ezekiel to judge the bloody city, Jerusalem, and show her all her abominations.
- She has shed blood in her midst and made idols against herself to defile herself, causing her days to draw near.
- Because of her idolatry and wickedness, once judgment comes He makes her a reproach unto the heathen and a mocking to all countries, both near and far.

**[Ezekiel 22:6-12](/bible/ezekiel/22#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/22#v6)</sup> Behold, the princes of Israel, every one were in thee to their power to shed blood.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/22#v7)</sup> In thee have they set light by father and mother: in the midst of thee have they dealt by oppression with the stranger: in thee have they vexed the fatherless and the widow.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/22#v8)</sup> Thou hast despised mine holy things, and hast profaned my sabbaths.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/22#v9)</sup> In thee are men that carry tales to shed blood: and in thee they eat upon the mountains: in the midst of thee they commit lewdness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/22#v10)</sup> In thee have they discovered their fathers’ nakedness: in thee have they humbled her that was set apart for pollution.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/22#v11)</sup> And one hath committed abomination with his neighbour’s wife; and another hath lewdly defiled his daughter in law; and another in thee hath humbled his sister, his father’s daughter.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)</sup> In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.

- The princes of Israel used their power to shed blood, set light by father and mother, dealt by oppression with the stranger, and vexed the fatherless and the widow.
- They despised His holy things, profaned His sabbaths, carried tales to shed blood, ate upon the mountains, and committed lewdness in her midst.
- They broke all manner of the immoral laws of [Leviticus 18](/bible/leviticus/18), committing abomination with a neighbor's wife, defiling a daughter-in-law, and humbling a sister, doing the doings of the other nations.
- They took gifts to shed blood, being put on the take and paid off to shed a man's blood, took usury and increase, and greedily gained by extortion, and have forgotten Him; these are wicked leaders.

**[Ezekiel 22:13-16](/bible/ezekiel/22#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/22#v13)</sup> Behold, therefore I have smitten mine hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/22#v14)</sup> Can thine heart endure, or can thine hands be strong, in the days that I shall deal with thee? I the Lord have spoken it, and will do it.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/22#v15)</sup> And I will scatter thee among the heathen, and disperse thee in the countries, and will consume thy filthiness out of thee.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/22#v16)</sup> And thou shalt take thine inheritance in thyself in the sight of the heathen, and thou shalt know that I am the Lord.

- The Most High smites His hand at their dishonest gain and at the blood shed in their midst, asking whether their heart can endure or their hands be strong in the day He deals with them.
- He will scatter them among the heathen, disperse them in the countries, and consume their filthiness out of them, and they shall know that He is the Lord when they take their inheritance in the sight of the heathen.

**[Ezekiel 22:17-22](/bible/ezekiel/22#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/22#v17)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/22#v18)</sup> Son of man, the house of Israel is to me become dross: all they are brass, and tin, and iron, and lead, in the midst of the furnace; they are even the dross of silver.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/22#v19)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because ye are all become dross, behold, therefore I will gather you into the midst of Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/22#v20)</sup> As they gather silver, and brass, and iron, and lead, and tin, into the midst of the furnace, to blow the fire upon it, to melt it; so will I gather you in mine anger and in my fury, and I will leave you there, and melt you.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/22#v21)</sup> Yea, I will gather you, and blow upon you in the fire of my wrath, and ye shall be melted in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/22#v22)</sup> As silver is melted in the midst of the furnace, so shall ye be melted in the midst thereof; and ye shall know that I the Lord have poured out my fury upon you.

- The house of Israel is become dross to Him, all brass, tin, iron, and lead, the dross of silver in the midst of the furnace.
- He will gather them into the midst of Jerusalem as men gather metal into the furnace, and blow the fire of His wrath upon them.
- They shall be melted in the midst of the furnace as silver is melted, and shall know that He is the Lord who poured out His fury upon them.

**[Ezekiel 22:23-31](/bible/ezekiel/22#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/22#v23)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/22#v24)</sup> Son of man, say unto her, Thou art the land that is not cleansed, nor rained upon in the day of indignation.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/22#v25)</sup> There is a conspiracy of her prophets in the midst thereof, like a roaring lion ravening the prey; they have devoured souls; they have taken the treasure and precious things; they have made her many widows in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/22#v26)</sup> Her priests have violated my law, and have profaned mine holy things: they have put no difference between the holy and profane, neither have they shewed difference between the unclean and the clean, and have hid their eyes from my sabbaths, and I am profaned among them.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/22#v27)</sup> Her princes in the midst thereof are like wolves ravening the prey, to shed blood, and to destroy souls, to get dishonest gain.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ezekiel/22#v28)</sup> And her prophets have daubed them with untempered morter, seeing vanity, and divining lies unto them, saying, Thus saith the Lord God, when the Lord hath not spoken.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ezekiel/22#v29)</sup> The people of the land have used oppression, and exercised robbery, and have vexed the poor and needy: yea, they have oppressed the stranger wrongfully.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/22#v30)</sup> And I sought for a man among them, that should make up the hedge, and stand in the gap before me for the land, that I should not destroy it: but I found none.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/22#v31)</sup> Therefore have I poured out mine indignation upon them; I have consumed them with the fire of my wrath: their own way have I recompensed upon their heads, saith the Lord God.

- The land is not cleansed nor rained upon, and there is a conspiracy of her prophets in her midst, like a roaring lion ravening the prey, devouring souls and making many widows; the leaders of Israel are very corrupt.
- Her priests violated His law and profaned His holy things, putting no difference between the holy and the profane or the clean and the unclean and hiding their eyes from His sabbaths, just like today where our people put no difference between anything, and it is called Christianity.
- Her princes are like wolves ravening the prey to shed blood and destroy souls for dishonest gain, and her prophets daub them with untempered mortar, seeing vanity and divining lies, saying thus saith the Lord God when He has not spoken.
- The people used oppression, exercised robbery, and vexed the poor and needy and the stranger wrongfully, showing the same oppression, robbery, and extortion by the leadership.
- He sought for a man to make up the hedge and stand in the gap before Him so He would not destroy the land, but He found none, so He poured out His indignation and consumed them with the fire of His wrath.

    > <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/94#v16)</sup> Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

    Who will rise up against the evildoers and stand up against the workers of iniquity, which is the hedge and the gap of one who would turn the Lord's wrath from the people.
