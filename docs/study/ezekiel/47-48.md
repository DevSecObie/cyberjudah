Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 47](/bible/ezekiel/47)

This session covers [Ezekiel 47](/bible/ezekiel/47) and 48. Ezekiel opens with a similitude of waters issuing from the house and rising higher and higher, a picture of the word of the Most High going out to the four corners of the earth; it closes with the land divided among the tribes and the gates of the city named after the children of Israel. The prophet speaks by similitude, proverbs, and dark sayings, so what is written here is spiritual, not a literal structure.

**[Ezekiel 47:1-2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/47#v1)</sup> Afterward he brought me again unto the door of the house; and, behold, waters issued out from under the threshold of the house eastward: for the forefront of the house stood toward the east, and the waters came down from under from the right side of the house, at the south side of the altar.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v2)</sup> Then brought he me out of the way of the gate northward, and led me about the way without unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward; and, behold, there ran out waters on the right side.

- Ezekiel speaks by similitude and dark sayings, so the house here is not a literal building. This is the spiritual house, which is the house of Israel.
- The waters that issue out from under the threshold are the word of the Most High. Just as a tub fills when the water is left running, the word is now being issued out to the four corners of the earth.
  - **[2 Timothy 2:20](/bible/2-timothy/2#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/2-timothy/2#v20)</sup> But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some to honour, and some to dishonour.

    In a great house there are vessels of gold, silver, wood, and earth; that house is the house of Israel.
  - **[1 Peter 2:5](/bible/1-peter/2#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/1-peter/2#v5)</sup> Ye also, as lively stones, are built up a spiritual house, an holy priesthood, to offer up spiritual sacrifices, acceptable to God by Jesus Christ.

    We as lively stones are built up a spiritual house; this spiritual house represents Israel.
  - **[Ephesians 5:26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)</sup> That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word,

    He sanctifies and cleanses it with the washing of water by the word; the water here represents the word.
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:1-3](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v1)</sup> Give ear, O ye heavens, and I will speak; and hear, O earth, the words of my mouth.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v2)</sup> My doctrine shall drop as the rain, my speech shall distil as the dew, as the small rain upon the tender herb, and as the showers upon the grass:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v3)</sup> Because I will publish the name of the Lord: ascribe ye greatness unto our God.

    My doctrine shall drop as the rain; the doctrine of the Most High is the water now distilling to every corner like dew.
  - **[Proverbs 4:2](/bible/proverbs/4#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/4#v2)</sup> For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law.

    For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law; the doctrines are the laws.

**[Ezekiel 47:3-5](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)</sup> And when the man that had the line in his hand went forth eastward, he measured a thousand cubits, and he brought me through the waters; the waters were to the ankles.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/47#v4)</sup> Again he measured a thousand, and brought me through the waters; the waters were to the knees. Again he measured a thousand, and brought me through; the waters were to the loins.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/47#v5)</sup> Afterward he measured a thousand; and it was a river that I could not pass over: for the waters were risen, waters to swim in, a river that could not be passed over.

- The man with the measuring line brings the prophet through the waters, and they rise from the ankles, to the knees, to the loins, until it is a river that cannot be passed over, waters to swim in.
- The rising water is the word being preached and the understanding increasing as it goes to the four corners of the earth: people coming to understand that Christ is a black man, that we are the Israelites, and that we must keep the commandments.
- The understanding rises in stages, just as understanding came out over time in the classes, the head covering out of First Corinthians 11, the sabbath, and the new moon being the full moon around 2006 to 2007. As long as we stay in the spirit more revelation will keep coming.
- The measuring line measures us up to the stature of Christ, the understanding of the laws and the doctrines.
  - **[Ephesians 4:11-13](/bible/ephesians/4#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/4#v11)</sup> And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers;
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/4#v12)</sup> For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ:
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ephesians/4#v13)</sup> Till we all come in the unity of the faith, and of the knowledge of the Son of God, unto a perfect man, unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ:

    He gave apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers, till we all come unto the measure of the stature of the fulness of Christ.

**[Ezekiel 47:6-7](/bible/ezekiel/47#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/47#v6)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, hast thou seen this? Then he brought me, and caused me to return to the brink of the river.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/47#v7)</sup> Now when I had returned, behold, at the bank of the river were very many trees on the one side and on the other.

- The Most High asks, son of man, hast thou seen this, and shows the prophet very many trees on both banks of the river.
- Because we now understand who we are and can identify our forefathers in this Bible, it means Elijah has already come and gone.
  - **[Malachi 4:5-6](/bible/malachi/4#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/4#v5)</sup> Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/4#v6)</sup> And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.

    I will send you Elijah the prophet, and he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children before the great and dreadful day.

**[Ezekiel 47:8-9](/bible/ezekiel/47#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/47#v8)</sup> Then said he unto me, These waters issue out toward the east country, and go down into the desert, and go into the sea: which being brought forth into the sea, the waters shall be healed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/47#v9)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that every thing that liveth, which moveth, whithersoever the rivers shall come, shall live: and there shall be a very great multitude of fish, because these waters shall come thither: for they shall be healed; and every thing shall live whither the river cometh.

- These waters go toward the east country, down into the desert, and into the sea, and the waters are healed so that every thing liveth whithersoever the river cometh.
- We receive life through the commandments.
  - **[Proverbs 7:2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

    Keep my commandments, and live; life comes through keeping the law.

**[Ezekiel 47:10](/bible/ezekiel/47#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/47#v10)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that the fishers shall stand upon it from En–gedi even unto En–eglaim; they shall be a place to spread forth nets; their fish shall be according to their kinds, as the fish of the great sea, exceeding many.

- The fishers stand upon it from Engedi to Eneglaim, and the fish are according to their kinds, exceeding many. This is Israel in the last days coming alive again and growing into an exceeding great army.
  - **[Matthew 4:19](/bible/matthew/4#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/4#v19)</sup> And he saith unto them, Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men.

    Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men; the fishers point to the same last-day gathering of Israel.

**[Ezekiel 47:11](/bible/ezekiel/47#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/47#v11)</sup> But the miry places thereof and the marishes thereof shall not be healed; they shall be given to salt.

- The miry and marshy places are not healed; they are given over to salt.

**[Ezekiel 47:12](/bible/ezekiel/47#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/47#v12)</sup> And by the river upon the bank thereof, on this side and on that side, shall grow all trees for meat, whose leaf shall not fade, neither shall the fruit thereof be consumed: it shall bring forth new fruit according to his months, because their waters they issued out of the sanctuary: and the fruit thereof shall be for meat, and the leaf thereof for medicine.

- By the river grow trees for meat whose leaf shall not fade, bringing forth new fruit according to their months, because their waters issued out of the sanctuary.
- The tree represents Israel, and once the word spreads we know how to bring forth fruit.
  - **[Matthew 3:8](/bible/matthew/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/3#v8)</sup> Bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance:

    Bring forth fruits meet for repentance, for the axe is laid to the root of every tree that does not.

**[Ezekiel 47:13-14](/bible/ezekiel/47#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/47#v13)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; This shall be the border, whereby ye shall inherit the land according to the twelve tribes of Israel: Joseph shall have two portions.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/47#v14)</sup> And ye shall inherit it, one as well as another: concerning the which I lifted up mine hand to give it unto your fathers: and this land shall fall unto you for inheritance.

- This is the border by which the land is inherited according to the twelve tribes of Israel, and Joseph shall have two portions.
- The land falls to Israel for an inheritance, each one receiving his portion as well as another.

**[Ezekiel 47:15-20](/bible/ezekiel/47#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/47#v15)</sup> And this shall be the border of the land toward the north side, from the great sea, the way of Hethlon, as men go to Zedad;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/47#v16)</sup> Hamath, Berothah, Sibraim, which is between the border of Damascus and the border of Hamath; Hazar–hatticon, which is by the coast of Hauran.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/47#v17)</sup> And the border from the sea shall be Hazar–enan, the border of Damascus, and the north northward, and the border of Hamath. And this is the north side.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/47#v18)</sup> And the east side ye shall measure from Hauran, and from Damascus, and from Gilead, and from the land of Israel by Jordan, from the border unto the east sea. And this is the east side.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/47#v19)</sup> And the south side southward, from Tamar even to the waters of strife in Kadesh, the river to the great sea. And this is the south side southward.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/47#v20)</sup> The west side also shall be the great sea from the border, till a man come over against Hamath. This is the west side.

- The Most High marks out the borders of the land on the north side, the east side, the south side, and the west side.

**[Ezekiel 47:21-23](/bible/ezekiel/47#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/47#v21)</sup> So shall ye divide this land unto you according to the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/47#v22)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that ye shall divide it by lot for an inheritance unto you, and to the strangers that sojourn among you, which shall beget children among you: and they shall be unto you as born in the country among the children of Israel; they shall have inheritance with you among the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/47#v23)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that in what tribe the stranger sojourneth, there shall ye give him his inheritance, saith the Lord God.

- The land is divided by lot for an inheritance, and the strangers that sojourn among you and beget children are counted as born in the country and given inheritance in the tribe where they sojourn.
- The stranger here is not another nation. A stranger is an Israelite living out of his own tribe's land; if you are of Judah and you sojourn in Benjamin, you are counted a stranger. Christianity uses this verse to claim the other nations dwell among Israel and receive the land, but that is not what is being said.
  - **[Obadiah 1:12](/bible/obadiah/1#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/obadiah/1#v12)</sup> But thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have rejoiced over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly in the day of distress.

    Thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; a stranger is one living out of his own land among his tribesmen.
  - **[1 Esdras 5:7](/bible/1-esdras/5#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-esdras/5#v7)</sup> And these are they of Jewry that came up from the captivity, where they dwelt as strangers, whom Nabuchodonosor the king of Babylon had carried away unto Babylon.

    Israel dwelt as strangers away from their own land, showing what a stranger is.

# Ezekiel 48: The Land Divided and the Gates of the Tribes

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 48](/bible/ezekiel/48)

**[Ezekiel 48:1](/bible/ezekiel/48#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/48#v1)</sup> Now these are the names of the tribes. From the north end to the coast of the way of Hethlon, as one goeth to Hamath, Hazar–enan, the border of Damascus northward, to the coast of Hamath; for these are his sides east and west; a portion for Dan.

- From the north end the tribes are named, and the first portion by its border is for Dan; each tribe receives a bordered portion of land.
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:14](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour’s landmark, which they of old time have set in thine inheritance, which thou shalt inherit in the land that the Lord thy God giveth thee to possess it.

    Thou shalt not remove thy neighbour's landmark; this is why each tribe's inheritance is marked off by a border.

**[Ezekiel 48:2-7](/bible/ezekiel/48#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/48#v2)</sup> And by the border of Dan, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Asher.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/48#v3)</sup> And by the border of Asher, from the east side even unto the west side, a portion for Naphtali.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/48#v4)</sup> And by the border of Naphtali, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Manasseh.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/48#v5)</sup> And by the border of Manasseh, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Ephraim.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/48#v6)</sup> And by the border of Ephraim, from the east side even unto the west side, a portion for Reuben.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/48#v7)</sup> And by the border of Reuben, from the east side unto the west side, a portion for Judah.

- From the east side to the west side the portions are given in order: Asher, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, Reuben, and Judah, each by the border of the one before.

**[Ezekiel 48:8-14](/bible/ezekiel/48#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/48#v8)</sup> And by the border of Judah, from the east side unto the west side, shall be the offering which ye shall offer of five and twenty thousand reeds in breadth, and in length as one of the other parts, from the east side unto the west side: and the sanctuary shall be in the midst of it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/48#v9)</sup> The oblation that ye shall offer unto the Lord shall be of five and twenty thousand in length, and of ten thousand in breadth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/48#v10)</sup> And for them, even for the priests, shall be this holy oblation; toward the north five and twenty thousand in length, and toward the west ten thousand in breadth, and toward the east ten thousand in breadth, and toward the south five and twenty thousand in length: and the sanctuary of the Lord shall be in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/48#v11)</sup> It shall be for the priests that are sanctified of the sons of Zadok; which have kept my charge, which went not astray when the children of Israel went astray, as the Levites went astray.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/48#v12)</sup> And this oblation of the land that is offered shall be unto them a thing most holy by the border of the Levites.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/48#v13)</sup> And over against the border of the priests the Levites shall have five and twenty thousand in length, and ten thousand in breadth: all the length shall be five and twenty thousand, and the breadth ten thousand.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/48#v14)</sup> And they shall not sell of it, neither exchange, nor alienate the firstfruits of the land: for it is holy unto the Lord.

- A holy oblation is set apart, with the sanctuary in the midst of it, for the priests, the sons of Zadok, who kept the charge and went not astray when the children of Israel and the Levites went astray.
- This portion is holy unto the Most High; it is not to be sold, exchanged, nor alienated, being the firstfruits of the land.

**[Ezekiel 48:15-20](/bible/ezekiel/48#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/48#v15)</sup> And the five thousand, that are left in the breadth over against the five and twenty thousand, shall be a profane place for the city, for dwelling, and for suburbs: and the city shall be in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/48#v16)</sup> And these shall be the measures thereof; the north side four thousand and five hundred, and the south side four thousand and five hundred, and on the east side four thousand and five hundred, and the west side four thousand and five hundred.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/48#v17)</sup> And the suburbs of the city shall be toward the north two hundred and fifty, and toward the south two hundred and fifty, and toward the east two hundred and fifty, and toward the west two hundred and fifty.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/48#v18)</sup> And the residue in length over against the oblation of the holy portion shall be ten thousand eastward, and ten thousand westward: and it shall be over against the oblation of the holy portion; and the increase thereof shall be for food unto them that serve the city.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/48#v19)</sup> And they that serve the city shall serve it out of all the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/48#v20)</sup> All the oblation shall be five and twenty thousand by five and twenty thousand: ye shall offer the holy oblation foursquare, with the possession of the city.

- The five thousand that remain are a profane place for the city, for dwelling and for suburbs, with the city in the midst, measured out on every side.
- Those who serve the city are drawn out of all the tribes of Israel, and the increase of the land serves as food for them.

**[Ezekiel 48:21-22](/bible/ezekiel/48#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/48#v21)</sup> And the residue shall be for the prince, on the one side and on the other of the holy oblation, and of the possession of the city, over against the five and twenty thousand of the oblation toward the east border, and westward over against the five and twenty thousand toward the west border, over against the portions for the prince: and it shall be the holy oblation; and the sanctuary of the house shall be in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/48#v22)</sup> Moreover from the possession of the Levites, and from the possession of the city, being in the midst of that which is the prince’s, between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin, shall be for the prince.

- The residue on both sides of the holy oblation belongs to the prince, lying between the border of Judah and the border of Benjamin.

**[Ezekiel 48:23-29](/bible/ezekiel/48#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/48#v23)</sup> As for the rest of the tribes, from the east side unto the west side, Benjamin shall have a portion.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/48#v24)</sup> And by the border of Benjamin, from the east side unto the west side, Simeon shall have a portion.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/48#v25)</sup> And by the border of Simeon, from the east side unto the west side, Issachar a portion.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/48#v26)</sup> And by the border of Issachar, from the east side unto the west side, Zebulun a portion.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/48#v27)</sup> And by the border of Zebulun, from the east side unto the west side, Gad a portion.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ezekiel/48#v28)</sup> And by the border of Gad, at the south side southward, the border shall be even from Tamar unto the waters of strife in Kadesh, and to the river toward the great sea.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ezekiel/48#v29)</sup> This is the land which ye shall divide by lot unto the tribes of Israel for inheritance, and these are their portions, saith the Lord God.

- The rest of the tribes receive their portions from the east side to the west side: Benjamin, Simeon, Issachar, Zebulun, and Gad, with the south border running from Tamar to the waters of strife in Kadesh.
- This is the land which is divided by lot unto the tribes of Israel for an inheritance.

**[Ezekiel 48:30-35](/bible/ezekiel/48#v30)**
> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/48#v30)</sup> And these are the goings out of the city on the north side, four thousand and five hundred measures.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/48#v31)</sup> And the gates of the city shall be after the names of the tribes of Israel: three gates northward; one gate of Reuben, one gate of Judah, one gate of Levi.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ezekiel/48#v32)</sup> And at the east side four thousand and five hundred: and three gates; and one gate of Joseph, one gate of Benjamin, one gate of Dan.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/ezekiel/48#v33)</sup> And at the south side four thousand and five hundred measures: and three gates; one gate of Simeon, one gate of Issachar, one gate of Zebulun.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/ezekiel/48#v34)</sup> At the west side four thousand and five hundred, with their three gates; one gate of Gad, one gate of Asher, one gate of Naphtali.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/ezekiel/48#v35)</sup> It was round about eighteen thousand measures: and the name of the city from that day shall be, The Lord is there.

- The goings out of the city are measured, and the gates of the city are named after the tribes of Israel, three gates on each side, round about eighteen thousand measures.
- This ties directly into prophecy: the gates into the inheritance carry the names of the twelve tribes only. There is no gate for the Edomite, no gate for the East Indian, because this land was given to our fathers and belongs to us. The name of the city from that day is, The Most High is there.
