Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 1](/bible/ezekiel/1)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 1 and 2. Ezekiel is among the captives by the river Chebar when the heavens are opened and he sees the vision of the four living creatures, the wheels, and the glory of the Most High above the firmament. The heavy point of this reading is that the spirit in the chariot is Christ, the same cloud that led our forefathers in the wilderness, teaching us to be on one accord and move only when He moves. Chapter 2 then commissions Ezekiel as a prophet to a rebellious house.

**[Ezekiel 1:1-3](/bible/ezekiel/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/1#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass in the thirtieth year, in the fourth month, in the fifth day of the month, as I was among the captives by the river of Chebar, that the heavens were opened, and I saw visions of God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/1#v2)</sup> In the fifth day of the month, which was the fifth year of king Jehoiachin’s captivity,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/1#v3)</sup> The word of the Lord came expressly unto Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans by the river Chebar; and the hand of the Lord was there upon him.

- In the thirtieth year, in the fourth month and the fifth day, as Ezekiel was among the captives by the river Chebar, the heavens were opened and he saw visions of God.
- This was the fifth year of king Jehoiachin's captivity, when the word of the Most High came expressly unto Ezekiel the priest, the son of Buzi, in the land of the Chaldeans, and the hand of the Most High was upon him.

**[Ezekiel 1:4-6](/bible/ezekiel/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/1#v4)</sup> And I looked, and, behold, a whirlwind came out of the north, a great cloud, and a fire infolding itself, and a brightness was about it, and out of the midst thereof as the colour of amber, out of the midst of the fire.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/1#v5)</sup> Also out of the midst thereof came the likeness of four living creatures. And this was their appearance; they had the likeness of a man.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/1#v6)</sup> And every one had four faces, and every one had four wings.

- Ezekiel looked and beheld a whirlwind coming out of the north, a great cloud and a fire enfolding itself, with a brightness about it and the color of amber out of the midst of the fire.
- Out of the midst came the likeness of four living creatures, and this was their appearance: they had the likeness of a man, and every one had four faces and four wings.

**[Ezekiel 1:7-11](/bible/ezekiel/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/1#v7)</sup> And their feet were straight feet; and the sole of their feet was like the sole of a calf’s foot: and they sparkled like the colour of burnished brass.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/1#v8)</sup> And they had the hands of a man under their wings on their four sides; and they four had their faces and their wings.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/1#v9)</sup> Their wings were joined one to another; they turned not when they went; they went every one straight forward.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/1#v10)</sup> As for the likeness of their faces, they four had the face of a man, and the face of a lion, on the right side: and they four had the face of an ox on the left side; they four also had the face of an eagle.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/1#v11)</sup> Thus were their faces: and their wings were stretched upward; two wings of every one were joined one to another, and two covered their bodies.

- Their feet were straight feet, and the sole of their feet was like the sole of a calf's foot, and they sparkled like the color of burnished brass; they had the hands of a man under their wings on their four sides.
- Their wings were joined one to another and they turned not when they went but went every one straight forward, each with the face of a man, the face of a lion on the right, the face of an ox on the left, and the face of an eagle.

**[Ezekiel 1:12-14](/bible/ezekiel/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/1#v12)</sup> And they went every one straight forward: whither the spirit was to go, they went; and they turned not when they went.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/1#v13)</sup> As for the likeness of the living creatures, their appearance was like burning coals of fire, and like the appearance of lamps: it went up and down among the living creatures; and the fire was bright, and out of the fire went forth lightning.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/1#v14)</sup> And the living creatures ran and returned as the appearance of a flash of lightning.

- They went every one straight forward, whither the spirit was to go they went, and they turned not when they went, moving only as the spirit directed.
- Their appearance was like burning coals of fire and like lamps; the fire went up and down among the creatures, was bright, and out of the fire went forth lightning, and the creatures ran and returned as a flash of lightning.

**[Ezekiel 1:15-21](/bible/ezekiel/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/1#v15)</sup> Now as I beheld the living creatures, behold one wheel upon the earth by the living creatures, with his four faces.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/1#v16)</sup> The appearance of the wheels and their work was like unto the colour of a beryl: and they four had one likeness: and their appearance and their work was as it were a wheel in the middle of a wheel.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/1#v17)</sup> When they went, they went upon their four sides: and they turned not when they went.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/1#v18)</sup> As for their rings, they were so high that they were dreadful; and their rings were full of eyes round about them four.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/1#v19)</sup> And when the living creatures went, the wheels went by them: and when the living creatures were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/1#v20)</sup> Whithersoever the spirit was to go, they went, thither was their spirit to go; and the wheels were lifted up over against them: for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/1#v21)</sup> When those went, these went; and when those stood, these stood; and when those were lifted up from the earth, the wheels were lifted up over against them: for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels.

- Ezekiel beheld one wheel upon the earth by the living creatures with his four faces; their work was like the color of a beryl, and their appearance was as a wheel in the middle of a wheel, and their rings were so high they were dreadful and full of eyes round about.
- When the living creatures went the wheels went by them, and when the creatures were lifted up from the earth the wheels were lifted up, for the spirit of the living creature was in the wheels; when those went these went, when those stood these stood.
- This answers the question of who rides in the chariots: the spirit in the chariot is Christ, and wherever He said go, the chariots went, and when He stood still, they stood still.
- The lesson is that the Most High wants us on one accord, moving when He moves and standing when He stands, just as the powers of heaven do only what He commands.

# Ezekiel 2: Son of Man, I Send Thee

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 2](/bible/ezekiel/2)

**[Ezekiel 2:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/2#v1)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, stand upon thy feet, and I will speak unto thee.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/2#v2)</sup> And the spirit entered into me when he spake unto me, and set me upon my feet, that I heard him that spake unto me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/2#v3)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, I send thee to the children of Israel, to a rebellious nation that hath rebelled against me: they and their fathers have transgressed against me, even unto this very day.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/2#v4)</sup> For they are impudent children and stiffhearted. I do send thee unto them; and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/2#v5)</sup> And they, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear, (for they are a rebellious house,) yet shall know that there hath been a prophet among them.

- The voice calls Ezekiel son of man and tells him to stand upon his feet, and the spirit enters into him and sets him upon his feet so that he can hear the One that speaks to him.
- The Most High sends him to the children of Israel, a rebellious nation that has transgressed against Him even unto this very day, impudent children and stiffhearted, to speak the words of the Lord God whether they hear or forbear, that they may know a prophet has been among them.

**[Ezekiel 2:6-8](/bible/ezekiel/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/2#v6)</sup> And thou, son of man, be not afraid of them, neither be afraid of their words, though briers and thorns be with thee, and thou dost dwell among scorpions: be not afraid of their words, nor be dismayed at their looks, though they be a rebellious house.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/2#v7)</sup> And thou shalt speak my words unto them, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear: for they are most rebellious.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/2#v8)</sup> But thou, son of man, hear what I say unto thee; Be not thou rebellious like that rebellious house: open thy mouth, and eat that I give thee.

- Ezekiel is told not to be afraid of them nor of their words, though briers and thorns be with him and he dwell among scorpions, and not to be dismayed at their looks though they are a rebellious house.
- He is to speak the Most High's words to them whether they hear or forbear, and unlike that rebellious house he is to open his mouth and eat what the Most High gives him.

**[Ezekiel 2:9-10](/bible/ezekiel/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/2#v9)</sup> And when I looked, behold, an hand was sent unto me; and, lo, a roll of a book was therein;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/2#v10)</sup> And he spread it before me; and it was written within and without: and there was written therein lamentations, and mourning, and woe.

- A hand is sent unto Ezekiel with a roll of a book in it, which is spread before him and written within and without.
- What was written in it was lamentations, and mourning, and woe, the message of judgment he was being sent to carry to a rebellious people.
