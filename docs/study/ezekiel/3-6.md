Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 3](/bible/ezekiel/3)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 3 through 6. Ezekiel eats the roll, is set as a watchman over a rebellious house, and is bound and made dumb until the Most High opens his mouth. Chapter 4 lays out the siege of Jerusalem as a sign, with bread baked in defilement. Chapter 5 divides the prophet's hair as the judgment of the city that multiplied more than the nations round about. Chapter 6 prophesies against the mountains of Israel and their idols, closing on the truth that the Most High gets our attention through affliction and judgment.

**[Ezekiel 3:1-3](/bible/ezekiel/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/3#v1)</sup> Moreover he said unto me, Son of man, eat that thou findest; eat this roll, and go speak unto the house of Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/3#v2)</sup> So I opened my mouth, and he caused me to eat that roll.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/3#v3)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, cause thy belly to eat, and fill thy bowels with this roll that I give thee. Then did I eat it; and it was in my mouth as honey for sweetness.

- The Most High tells Ezekiel to eat the roll and go speak unto the house of Israel, so he opens his mouth and is caused to eat it, filling his bowels with the roll that is given him.
- In his mouth the roll was as honey for sweetness, showing that receiving the word first for himself was sweet before he was sent to carry it.

**[Ezekiel 3:4-7](/bible/ezekiel/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/3#v4)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, go, get thee unto the house of Israel, and speak with my words unto them.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/3#v5)</sup> For thou art not sent to a people of a strange speech and of an hard language, but to the house of Israel;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/3#v6)</sup> Not to many people of a strange speech and of an hard language, whose words thou canst not understand. Surely, had I sent thee to them, they would have hearkened unto thee.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/3#v7)</sup> But the house of Israel will not hearken unto thee; for they will not hearken unto me: for all the house of Israel are impudent and hardhearted.

- Ezekiel is sent to the house of Israel, not to a people of strange speech and a hard language whose words he could not understand.
- Had the Most High sent him to such a people they would have hearkened, but the house of Israel will not hearken to Ezekiel because they will not hearken unto the Most High, for all the house of Israel are impudent and hardhearted.

**[Ezekiel 3:8-11](/bible/ezekiel/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/3#v8)</sup> Behold, I have made thy face strong against their faces, and thy forehead strong against their foreheads.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/3#v9)</sup> As an adamant harder than flint have I made thy forehead: fear them not, neither be dismayed at their looks, though they be a rebellious house.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/3#v10)</sup> Moreover he said unto me, Son of man, all my words that I shall speak unto thee receive in thine heart, and hear with thine ears.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/3#v11)</sup> And go, get thee to them of the captivity, unto the children of thy people, and speak unto them, and tell them, Thus saith the Lord God; whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear.

- The Most High makes Ezekiel's face and forehead strong against their faces, as an adamant harder than flint, so he need not fear them nor be dismayed at their looks though they be a rebellious house.
- He is to receive all the words in his heart, hear with his ears, and go to them of the captivity and speak, telling them "Thus saith the Lord GOD," whether they hear or whether they forbear.

**[Ezekiel 3:12-15](/bible/ezekiel/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/3#v12)</sup> Then the spirit took me up, and I heard behind me a voice of a great rushing, saying, Blessed be the glory of the Lord from his place.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/3#v13)</sup> I heard also the noise of the wings of the living creatures that touched one another, and the noise of the wheels over against them, and a noise of a great rushing.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/3#v14)</sup> So the spirit lifted me up, and took me away, and I went in bitterness, in the heat of my spirit; but the hand of the Lord was strong upon me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/3#v15)</sup> Then I came to them of the captivity at Tel–abib, that dwelt by the river of Chebar, and I sat where they sat, and remained there astonished among them seven days.

- The spirit takes Ezekiel up and he hears behind him a great rushing voice, "Blessed be the glory of the LORD from his place," along with the noise of the wings of the creatures and the wheels.
- The spirit lifts him and takes him away, and he goes in bitterness in the heat of his spirit, but the hand of the Most High is strong upon him; he comes to the captivity at Tel-abib by the river Chebar and sits astonished among them seven days.

**[Ezekiel 3:16-21](/bible/ezekiel/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/3#v16)</sup> And it came to pass at the end of seven days, that the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/3#v17)</sup> Son of man, I have made thee a watchman unto the house of Israel: therefore hear the word at my mouth, and give them warning from me.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/3#v18)</sup> When I say unto the wicked, Thou shalt surely die; and thou givest him not warning, nor speakest to warn the wicked from his wicked way, to save his life; the same wicked man shall die in his iniquity; but his blood will I require at thine hand.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/3#v19)</sup> Yet if thou warn the wicked, and he turn not from his wickedness, nor from his wicked way, he shall die in his iniquity; but thou hast delivered thy soul.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/3#v20)</sup> Again, When a righteous man doth turn from his righteousness, and commit iniquity, and I lay a stumblingblock before him, he shall die: because thou hast not given him warning, he shall die in his sin, and his righteousness which he hath done shall not be remembered; but his blood will I require at thine hand.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/3#v21)</sup> Nevertheless if thou warn the righteous man, that the righteous sin not, and he doth not sin, he shall surely live, because he is warned; also thou hast delivered thy soul.

- At the end of seven days the word of the Most High makes Ezekiel a watchman unto the house of Israel, charged to hear the word and give the people warning.
- If Ezekiel fails to warn the wicked, that wicked man dies in his iniquity but his blood is required at the watchman's hand; if he warns and the man turns not, the man dies but Ezekiel has delivered his own soul.
- The same holds for a righteous man who turns to iniquity: if unwarned, he dies in his sin and his blood is required at the watchman's hand, but if warned and he sins not, he shall surely live and the watchman is delivered.

**[Ezekiel 3:22-27](/bible/ezekiel/3#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/3#v22)</sup> And the hand of the Lord was there upon me; and he said unto me, Arise, go forth into the plain, and I will there talk with thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/3#v23)</sup> Then I arose, and went forth into the plain: and, behold, the glory of the Lord stood there, as the glory which I saw by the river of Chebar: and I fell on my face.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/3#v24)</sup> Then the spirit entered into me, and set me upon my feet, and spake with me, and said unto me, Go, shut thyself within thine house.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/3#v25)</sup> But thou, O son of man, behold, they shall put bands upon thee, and shall bind thee with them, and thou shalt not go out among them:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/3#v26)</sup> And I will make thy tongue cleave to the roof of thy mouth, that thou shalt be dumb, and shalt not be to them a reprover: for they are a rebellious house.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/3#v27)</sup> But when I speak with thee, I will open thy mouth, and thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; He that heareth, let him hear; and he that forbeareth, let him forbear: for they are a rebellious house.

- The hand of the Most High is upon Ezekiel and he is sent out into the plain, where the glory of the Most High stands as the glory he saw by the river Chebar, and he falls on his face.
- The spirit sets him on his feet and tells him to shut himself in his house, where they shall bind him with bands; the Most High makes his tongue cleave to the roof of his mouth so he is dumb and cannot be a reprover, until the Most High opens his mouth to say, "Thus saith the Lord GOD," for they are a rebellious house.

# Ezekiel 4: The Siege of Jerusalem and Defiled Bread

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 4](/bible/ezekiel/4)

**[Ezekiel 4:1-3](/bible/ezekiel/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/4#v1)</sup> Thou also, son of man, take thee a tile, and lay it before thee, and pourtray upon it the city, even Jerusalem:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/4#v2)</sup> And lay siege against it, and build a fort against it, and cast a mount against it; set the camp also against it, and set battering rams against it round about.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/4#v3)</sup> Moreover take thou unto thee an iron pan, and set it for a wall of iron between thee and the city: and set thy face against it, and it shall be besieged, and thou shalt lay siege against it. This shall be a sign to the house of Israel.

- Ezekiel is told to take a tile and portray the city of Jerusalem upon it, then lay siege against it, build a fort, cast a mount, set the camp, and set battering rams round about.
- He takes an iron pan and sets it for a wall of iron between himself and the city, setting his face against it; this is a sign to the house of Israel.

**[Ezekiel 4:4-8](/bible/ezekiel/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/4#v4)</sup> Lie thou also upon thy left side, and lay the iniquity of the house of Israel upon it: according to the number of the days that thou shalt lie upon it thou shalt bear their iniquity.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/4#v5)</sup> For I have laid upon thee the years of their iniquity, according to the number of the days, three hundred and ninety days: so shalt thou bear the iniquity of the house of Israel.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/4#v6)</sup> And when thou hast accomplished them, lie again on thy right side, and thou shalt bear the iniquity of the house of Judah forty days: I have appointed thee each day for a year.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/4#v7)</sup> Therefore thou shalt set thy face toward the siege of Jerusalem, and thine arm shall be uncovered, and thou shalt prophesy against it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/4#v8)</sup> And, behold, I will lay bands upon thee, and thou shalt not turn thee from one side to another, till thou hast ended the days of thy siege.

- Ezekiel lies on his left side to bear the iniquity of the house of Israel, 390 days, each day appointed for a year, then on his right side forty days for the house of Judah.
- His face is set toward the siege of Jerusalem, his arm uncovered to prophesy against it, and bands are laid upon him so he cannot turn from one side to another until the days of the siege are ended.

**[Ezekiel 4:9-11](/bible/ezekiel/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/4#v9)</sup> Take thou also unto thee wheat, and barley, and beans, and lentiles, and millet, and fitches, and put them in one vessel, and make thee bread thereof, according to the number of the days that thou shalt lie upon thy side, three hundred and ninety days shalt thou eat thereof.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/4#v10)</sup> And thy meat which thou shalt eat shall be by weight, twenty shekels a day: from time to time shalt thou eat it.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/4#v11)</sup> Thou shalt drink also water by measure, the sixth part of an hin: from time to time shalt thou drink.

- He takes wheat, barley, beans, lentils, millet, and fitches into one vessel to make bread for the 390 days he lies on his side.
- His meat is measured by weight, twenty shekels a day, and his water by measure, the sixth part of a hin, eaten and drunk from time to time.

**[Ezekiel 4:12-15](/bible/ezekiel/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/4#v12)</sup> And thou shalt eat it as barley cakes, and thou shalt bake it with dung that cometh out of man, in their sight.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/4#v13)</sup> And the Lord said, Even thus shall the children of Israel eat their defiled bread among the Gentiles, whither I will drive them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/4#v14)</sup> Then said I, Ah Lord God! behold, my soul hath not been polluted: for from my youth up even till now have I not eaten of that which dieth of itself, or is torn in pieces; neither came there abominable flesh into my mouth.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/4#v15)</sup> Then he said unto me, Lo, I have given thee cow’s dung for man’s dung, and thou shalt prepare thy bread therewith.

- Ezekiel is told to bake his bread with dung that comes from man in their sight, a hard and startling command.
- The Most High declares that even thus shall the children of Israel eat their defiled bread among the Gentiles whither He drives them; this is why our food is so defiled now, because we are eating our bread defiled among the nations.
- When Ezekiel protests that his soul has never been polluted with abominable flesh, the Most High allows him cow's dung instead of man's dung to prepare his bread.

**[Ezekiel 4:16-17](/bible/ezekiel/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/4#v16)</sup> Moreover he said unto me, Son of man, behold, I will break the staff of bread in Jerusalem: and they shall eat bread by weight, and with care; and they shall drink water by measure, and with astonishment:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/4#v17)</sup> That they may want bread and water, and be astonied one with another, and consume away for their iniquity.

- The Most High will break the staff of bread in Jerusalem so they eat bread by weight and with care and drink water by measure and with astonishment.
- They shall want bread and water and be astonished one with another and consume away for their iniquity.

# Ezekiel 5: The Razor and the Divided Hair

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 5](/bible/ezekiel/5)

**[Ezekiel 5:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/5#v1)</sup> And thou, son of man, take thee a sharp knife, take thee a barber’s razor, and cause it to pass upon thine head and upon thy beard: then take thee balances to weigh, and divide the hair.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/5#v2)</sup> Thou shalt burn with fire a third part in the midst of the city, when the days of the siege are fulfilled: and thou shalt take a third part, and smite about it with a knife: and a third part thou shalt scatter in the wind; and I will draw out a sword after them.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/5#v3)</sup> Thou shalt also take thereof a few in number, and bind them in thy skirts.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/5#v4)</sup> Then take of them again, and cast them into the midst of the fire, and burn them in the fire; for thereof shall a fire come forth into all the house of Israel.

- Ezekiel takes a sharp knife as a barber's razor and passes it upon his head and beard, then weighs and divides the hair with balances.
- A third part he burns with fire in the city when the siege is fulfilled, a third he smites with the knife round about, and a third he scatters in the wind while a sword is drawn out after them; a few he binds in his skirts, and some of those are cast into the fire.

**[Ezekiel 5:5-7](/bible/ezekiel/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/5#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; This is Jerusalem: I have set it in the midst of the nations and countries that are round about her.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/5#v6)</sup> And she hath changed my judgments into wickedness more than the nations, and my statutes more than the countries that are round about her: for they have refused my judgments and my statutes, they have not walked in them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/5#v7)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because ye multiplied more than the nations that are round about you, and have not walked in my statutes, neither have kept my judgments, neither have done according to the judgments of the nations that are round about you;

- The Most High declares, "This is Jerusalem," set in the midst of the nations, and she has changed His judgments into wickedness more than the countries round about, refusing His judgments and statutes.
- On the word that Israel multiplied more than the nations round about, the point is that if it were not for all the abortions our people get, we would be vast throughout the four corners, in America alone.
- These children would not stay one generation but would multiply into more children compounding, one plus two plus four plus eight, so the nation would be as the stars for number.
- The other nations know how to deal with that growth: they set up abortion clinics and promote fornication to hinder the growth of the nation, a diabolical scheme straight from Satan, yet the Most High still said the seed of Israel would be as the sand of the sea.

**[Ezekiel 5:8-12](/bible/ezekiel/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/5#v8)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I, even I, am against thee, and will execute judgments in the midst of thee in the sight of the nations.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/5#v9)</sup> And I will do in thee that which I have not done, and whereunto I will not do any more the like, because of all thine abominations.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/5#v10)</sup> Therefore the fathers shall eat the sons in the midst of thee, and the sons shall eat their fathers; and I will execute judgments in thee, and the whole remnant of thee will I scatter into all the winds.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/5#v11)</sup> Wherefore, as I live, saith the Lord God; Surely, because thou hast defiled my sanctuary with all thy detestable things, and with all thine abominations, therefore will I also diminish thee; neither shall mine eye spare, neither will I have any pity.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/5#v12)</sup> A third part of thee shall die with the pestilence, and with famine shall they be consumed in the midst of thee: and a third part shall fall by the sword round about thee; and I will scatter a third part into all the winds, and I will draw out a sword after them.

- The Most High sets Himself against Jerusalem to execute judgments in the sight of the nations, doing in her what He has not done and will not do the like again because of all her abominations.
- Therefore fathers shall eat their sons and sons their fathers in the midst of her, and the whole remnant He will scatter to all the winds.
- Because she defiled His sanctuary with detestable things and abominations, His eye shall not spare nor pity: a third part shall die by pestilence and famine, a third fall by the sword round about, and a third He scatters to the winds with a sword drawn out after them.

**[Ezekiel 5:13-17](/bible/ezekiel/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/5#v13)</sup> Thus shall mine anger be accomplished, and I will cause my fury to rest upon them, and I will be comforted: and they shall know that I the Lord have spoken it in my zeal, when I have accomplished my fury in them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/5#v14)</sup> Moreover I will make thee waste, and a reproach among the nations that are round about thee, in the sight of all that pass by.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/5#v15)</sup> So it shall be a reproach and a taunt, an instruction and an astonishment unto the nations that are round about thee, when I shall execute judgments in thee in anger and in fury and in furious rebukes. I the Lord have spoken it.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/5#v16)</sup> When I shall send upon them the evil arrows of famine, which shall be for their destruction, and which I will send to destroy you: and I will increase the famine upon you, and will break your staff of bread:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/5#v17)</sup> So will I send upon you famine and evil beasts, and they shall bereave thee; and pestilence and blood shall pass through thee; and I will bring the sword upon thee. I the Lord have spoken it.

- The Most High's anger is accomplished and His fury caused to rest upon them, and they shall know that the Lord has spoken it in His zeal when His fury is fulfilled.
- He makes the city a waste and a reproach, a taunt, an instruction, and an astonishment to the nations round about when He executes judgments in anger and furious rebukes.
- He sends the evil arrows of famine for their destruction, increasing the famine and breaking the staff of bread, sending famine, evil beasts, pestilence, and blood, and bringing the sword upon them.

# Ezekiel 6: Against the Mountains of Israel

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 6](/bible/ezekiel/6)

**[Ezekiel 6:1-7](/bible/ezekiel/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/6#v1)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/6#v2)</sup> Son of man, set thy face toward the mountains of Israel, and prophesy against them,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/6#v3)</sup> And say, Ye mountains of Israel, hear the word of the Lord God; Thus saith the Lord God to the mountains, and to the hills, to the rivers, and to the valleys; Behold, I, even I, will bring a sword upon you, and I will destroy your high places.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/6#v4)</sup> And your altars shall be desolate, and your images shall be broken: and I will cast down your slain men before your idols.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/6#v5)</sup> And I will lay the dead carcases of the children of Israel before their idols; and I will scatter your bones round about your altars.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/6#v6)</sup> In all your dwellingplaces the cities shall be laid waste, and the high places shall be desolate; that your altars may be laid waste and made desolate, and your idols may be broken and cease, and your images may be cut down, and your works may be abolished.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/6#v7)</sup> And the slain shall fall in the midst of you, and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

- The word of the Most High sets Ezekiel's face toward the mountains of Israel to prophesy against them and against the hills, rivers, and valleys, bringing a sword to destroy their high places.
- The altars shall be desolate, the images broken, and the slain men cast down before their idols, with the dead carcasses of the children of Israel laid before the idols and their bones scattered round about the altars.
- Their cities shall be laid waste and their high places desolate, their idols broken and works abolished, and the slain shall fall in the midst of them, that they may know He is the Lord.

**[Ezekiel 6:8-10](/bible/ezekiel/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/6#v8)</sup> Yet will I leave a remnant, that ye may have some that shall escape the sword among the nations, when ye shall be scattered through the countries.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/6#v9)</sup> And they that escape of you shall remember me among the nations whither they shall be carried captives, because I am broken with their whorish heart, which hath departed from me, and with their eyes, which go a whoring after their idols: and they shall lothe themselves for the evils which they have committed in all their abominations.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/6#v10)</sup> And they shall know that I am the Lord, and that I have not said in vain that I would do this evil unto them.

- Yet the Most High leaves a remnant, some that escape the sword among the nations when they are scattered through the countries.
- They that escape shall remember Him among the nations whither they are carried captive, because He is broken with their whorish heart and their eyes that go whoring after idols, and they shall loathe themselves for the evils they have committed.

**[Ezekiel 6:11-14](/bible/ezekiel/6#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/6#v11)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; Smite with thine hand, and stamp with thy foot, and say, Alas for all the evil abominations of the house of Israel! for they shall fall by the sword, by the famine, and by the pestilence.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/6#v12)</sup> He that is far off shall die of the pestilence; and he that is near shall fall by the sword; and he that remaineth and is besieged shall die by the famine: thus will I accomplish my fury upon them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/6#v13)</sup> Then shall ye know that I am the Lord, when their slain men shall be among their idols round about their altars, upon every high hill, in all the tops of the mountains, and under every green tree, and under every thick oak, the place where they did offer sweet savour to all their idols.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/6#v14)</sup> So will I stretch out my hand upon them, and make the land desolate, yea, more desolate than the wilderness toward Diblath, in all their habitations: and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- Ezekiel is told to smite with the hand and stamp with the foot and cry "Alas" for all the evil abominations of the house of Israel, for they shall fall by the sword, the famine, and the pestilence, near and far.
- The slain shall lie among their idols round about their altars, upon every high hill and mountaintop, under every green tree and thick oak where they offered sweet savor to their idols, and the land shall be made more desolate than the wilderness.
- Affliction and judgment are how we come to know the Most High; the Christianity spirit of prosperity does not know Him, for the Lord gets our attention through judgment.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    The Most High returns to His place until they acknowledge their offence and seek His face, for in their affliction they will seek Him early, just as this reading shows.
