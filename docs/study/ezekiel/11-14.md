Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 11](/bible/ezekiel/11)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 11 through 14, taught live in IUIC Memphis. The thread running through all four chapters is false security: wicked counselors, false prophets, and lying women told our people that Jerusalem was a safe iron pot and that judgment would never fall, while the Most High declared that the sword, famine, noisome beasts, and pestilence were coming because of our sins. Chapter 11 opens with twenty-five men giving wicked counsel that the city is the cauldron and they are the flesh, and the Most High answers that the city will not be their protection but their judgment, while promising a scattered remnant a little sanctuary and a new heart.

**[Ezekiel 11:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/11#v1)</sup> Moreover the spirit lifted me up, and brought me unto the east gate of the Lord’s house, which looketh eastward: and behold at the door of the gate five and twenty men; among whom I saw Jaazaniah the son of Azur, and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, princes of the people.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/11#v2)</sup> Then said he unto me, Son of man, these are the men that devise mischief, and give wicked counsel in this city:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/11#v3)</sup> Which say, It is not near; let us build houses: this city is the caldron, and we be the flesh.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/11#v4)</sup> Therefore prophesy against them, prophesy, O son of man.

- The spirit lifted Ezekiel up and brought him to the east gate of the Lord's house, where he saw five and twenty men, among whom were Jaazaniah the son of Azur and Pelatiah the son of Benaiah, princes of the people; remember the name Pelatiah.
- These are the men that devise mischief and give wicked counsel in the city, telling the people it is not near, let us build houses, this city is the cauldron and we are the flesh.
- Babylon had already carried away the northern kingdom and some of our people were already in captivity, yet the counsel was that Jerusalem, the iron pot, would protect them, giving the people a false sense of security.
- The Most High tells Ezekiel to prophesy against these twenty-five wicked men.

**[Ezekiel 11:5-8](/bible/ezekiel/11#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/11#v5)</sup> And the Spirit of the Lord fell upon me, and said unto me, Speak; Thus saith the Lord; Thus have ye said, O house of Israel: for I know the things that come into your mind, every one of them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/11#v6)</sup> Ye have multiplied your slain in this city, and ye have filled the streets thereof with the slain.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/11#v7)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Your slain whom ye have laid in the midst of it, they are the flesh, and this city is the caldron: but I will bring you forth out of the midst of it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/11#v8)</sup> Ye have feared the sword; and I will bring a sword upon you, saith the Lord God.

- The spirit of the Most High fell upon Ezekiel and told him to say that He knows the things that come into their minds, every one of them.
- They had multiplied their slain in the city and filled the streets with the dead, for the land was full of blood and violence.
- The dead they laid in the midst of the city are the flesh, and the city is the cauldron, but the Most High will bring them forth out of the midst of it.
- Because they feared the sword, He will bring a sword upon them, meaning He will bring the nations against Jerusalem.

**[Ezekiel 11:9-12](/bible/ezekiel/11#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/11#v9)</sup> And I will bring you out of the midst thereof, and deliver you into the hands of strangers, and will execute judgments among you.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/11#v10)</sup> Ye shall fall by the sword; I will judge you in the border of Israel; and ye shall know that I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/11#v11)</sup> This city shall not be your caldron, neither shall ye be the flesh in the midst thereof; but I will judge you in the border of Israel:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/11#v12)</sup> And ye shall know that I am the Lord: for ye have not walked in my statutes, neither executed my judgments, but have done after the manners of the heathen that are round about you.

- The Most High will deliver them into the hands of strangers, the nations, and execute judgments among them so that they fall by the sword in the border of Israel and know that He is the Lord.
- This city will not be their cauldron, neither will they be the flesh in the midst of it; He will judge them in the border of Israel.
- We get judged because we do not keep the Most High's laws, the same then as today; they had not walked in His statutes but had done after the manners of the heathen around about them.
  - **[Leviticus 18:3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/18#v3)</sup> After the doings of the land of Egypt, wherein ye dwelt, shall ye not do: and after the doings of the land of Canaan, whither I bring you, shall ye not do: neither shall ye walk in their ordinances.

    The manner of the heathen is the immoral laws and spiritual idolatry that we followed back then and still follow today.

**[Ezekiel 11:13](/bible/ezekiel/11#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/11#v13)</sup> And it came to pass, when I prophesied, that Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died. Then fell I down upon my face, and cried with a loud voice, and said, Ah Lord God! wilt thou make a full end of the remnant of Israel?

- While Ezekiel was prophesying, Pelatiah the son of Benaiah died, one of the wicked men that had given wicked counsel, put to death by the Most High.
- Ezekiel fell upon his face and cried with a loud voice, asking if the Most High would make a full end of the remnant of Israel.

**[Ezekiel 11:14-16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/11#v14)</sup> Again the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/11#v15)</sup> Son of man, thy brethren, even thy brethren, the men of thy kindred, and all the house of Israel wholly, are they unto whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem have said, Get you far from the Lord: unto us is this land given in possession.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)</sup> Therefore say, Thus saith the Lord God; Although I have cast them far off among the heathen, and although I have scattered them among the countries, yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they shall come.

- The word of the Lord came again, calling them Ezekiel's brethren, the men of his kindred and the house of Israel, unto whom the inhabitants of Jerusalem had said, get you far from the Lord, this land is given to us in possession.
- Although the Most High has cast them far off among the heathen and scattered them among the countries, He will yet be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they come.
- Everywhere we are scattered the little sanctuaries are a comfort, just like the schools IUIC is setting up in all our scattered lands, where you come and learn the word of God.

**[Ezekiel 11:17-21](/bible/ezekiel/11#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/11#v17)</sup> Therefore say, Thus saith the Lord God; I will even gather you from the people, and assemble you out of the countries where ye have been scattered, and I will give you the land of Israel.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/11#v18)</sup> And they shall come thither, and they shall take away all the detestable things thereof and all the abominations thereof from thence.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/11#v19)</sup> And I will give them one heart, and I will put a new spirit within you; and I will take the stony heart out of their flesh, and will give them an heart of flesh:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/11#v20)</sup> That they may walk in my statutes, and keep mine ordinances, and do them: and they shall be my people, and I will be their God.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/11#v21)</sup> But as for them whose heart walketh after the heart of their detestable things and their abominations, I will recompense their way upon their own heads, saith the Lord God.

- The Most High will gather them from the people and assemble them out of the countries where they were scattered and give them the land of Israel, and they shall take away all its detestable things and abominations.
- He will give them one heart and put a new spirit within them, putting all of us on one accord with the word of God.
- He will take the stony heart, the rebellious mind that rebels against His laws, out of their flesh and give them a heart of flesh, a future prophecy of what will happen, that they may walk in His statutes and be His people.
- But as for those whose heart walks after their detestable things, the wicked Israelites who will not do this, He will recompense their way upon their own heads; there is a big payback when you will not keep His laws.

# Ezekiel 12: Prepare Thy Stuff for Removing

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 12](/bible/ezekiel/12)

**[Ezekiel 12:1-2](/bible/ezekiel/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/12#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord also came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/12#v2)</sup> Son of man, thou dwellest in the midst of a rebellious house, which have eyes to see, and see not; they have ears to hear, and hear not: for they are a rebellious house.

- The word of the Lord came saying that Ezekiel dwells in the midst of a rebellious house, which have eyes to see and see not, and ears to hear and hear not.
- When you do not keep the Most High's laws you lose your understanding and wander in the way of the congregation of the dead, rebellious with no understanding, and that was us back then and today.

**[Ezekiel 12:3-7](/bible/ezekiel/12#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/12#v3)</sup> Therefore, thou son of man, prepare thee stuff for removing, and remove by day in their sight; and thou shalt remove from thy place to another place in their sight: it may be they will consider, though they be a rebellious house.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/12#v4)</sup> Then shalt thou bring forth thy stuff by day in their sight, as stuff for removing: and thou shalt go forth at even in their sight, as they that go forth into captivity.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/12#v5)</sup> Dig thou through the wall in their sight, and carry out thereby.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/12#v6)</sup> In their sight shalt thou bear it upon thy shoulders, and carry it forth in the twilight: thou shalt cover thy face, that thou see not the ground: for I have set thee for a sign unto the house of Israel.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/12#v7)</sup> And I did so as I was commanded: I brought forth my stuff by day, as stuff for captivity, and in the even I digged through the wall with mine hand; I brought it forth in the twilight, and I bare it upon my shoulder in their sight.

- Ezekiel is told to prepare stuff for removing and to remove by day in their sight from one place to another, that it may be they will consider, though they be a rebellious house.
- He is to bring forth his stuff by day as stuff for captivity and go forth in the evening in their sight, as they that go forth into captivity.
- He is to dig down through the wall in their sight while they watch, carry his stuff out through it upon his shoulders in the twilight, and cover his face that he see not the ground, meaning disguise his face, for the Most High set him for a sign unto the house of Israel.
- Ezekiel did as commanded, bringing forth his stuff by day, digging through the wall by hand in the evening, and bearing it upon his shoulder in their sight.

**[Ezekiel 12:8-12](/bible/ezekiel/12#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/12#v8)</sup> And in the morning came the word of the Lord unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/12#v9)</sup> Son of man, hath not the house of Israel, the rebellious house, said unto thee, What doest thou?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/12#v10)</sup> Say thou unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; This burden concerneth the prince in Jerusalem, and all the house of Israel that are among them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/12#v11)</sup> Say, I am your sign: like as I have done, so shall it be done unto them: they shall remove and go into captivity.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/12#v12)</sup> And the prince that is among them shall bear upon his shoulder in the twilight, and shall go forth: they shall dig through the wall to carry out thereby: he shall cover his face, that he see not the ground with his eyes.

- In the morning the word of the Lord asked whether the rebellious house had not asked Ezekiel what he was doing.
- He is to tell them this burden concerns the prince in Jerusalem, King Zedekiah, and all the house of Israel among them; as Ezekiel had done, so shall it be done unto them.
- Just as Ezekiel's example showed, the southern kingdom with King Zedekiah shall remove and go into captivity.
- The prince shall bear his stuff upon his shoulder in the twilight and go forth, digging through the wall to carry out, and cover his face that he see not the ground, for when you read the history he tried to escape and disguise himself.

**[Ezekiel 12:13-16](/bible/ezekiel/12#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/12#v13)</sup> My net also will I spread upon him, and he shall be taken in my snare: and I will bring him to Babylon to the land of the Chaldeans; yet shall he not see it, though he shall die there.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/12#v14)</sup> And I will scatter toward every wind all that are about him to help him, and all his bands; and I will draw out the sword after them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/12#v15)</sup> And they shall know that I am the Lord, when I shall scatter them among the nations, and disperse them in the countries.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/12#v16)</sup> But I will leave a few men of them from the sword, from the famine, and from the pestilence; that they may declare all their abominations among the heathen whither they come; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- The Most High will spread His net upon him and take him in His snare, bringing him to Babylon in the land of the Chaldeans, yet he shall not see it though he shall die there.
- There is no place to run and no place to hide; the net is for all the children going into the Babylonian captivity, but that prince will die in Babylon.
- He will scatter toward every wind all that are about him to help him, and draw out the sword after them, that they may know He is the Lord.
- He will leave a few men from the sword, famine, and pestilence, a remnant alive, that they may declare all their abominations among the heathen where they come.

**[Ezekiel 12:17-20](/bible/ezekiel/12#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/12#v17)</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came to me, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/12#v18)</sup> Son of man, eat thy bread with quaking, and drink thy water with trembling and with carefulness;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/12#v19)</sup> And say unto the people of the land, Thus saith the Lord God of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and of the land of Israel; They shall eat their bread with carefulness, and drink their water with astonishment, that her land may be desolate from all that is therein, because of the violence of all them that dwell therein.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/12#v20)</sup> And the cities that are inhabited shall be laid waste, and the land shall be desolate; and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

- The word of the Lord told Ezekiel to eat his bread with quaking and drink his water with trembling and carefulness.
- He is to say to the people of Jerusalem and the land of Israel that they shall eat their bread with carefulness and drink their water with astonishment, and the land shall be desolate because of the violence of them that dwell therein.
- At one time we did not have to tremble over what we ate and drank, but because the wicked counsel gave the people a false sense of security, the Most High promised our people would tremble and shake over their food and drink, a reality check.

**[Ezekiel 12:21-25](/bible/ezekiel/12#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/12#v21)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/12#v22)</sup> Son of man, what is that proverb that ye have in the land of Israel, saying, The days are prolonged, and every vision faileth?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/12#v23)</sup> Tell them therefore, Thus saith the Lord God; I will make this proverb to cease, and they shall no more use it as a proverb in Israel; but say unto them, The days are at hand, and the effect of every vision.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/12#v24)</sup> For there shall be no more any vain vision nor flattering divination within the house of Israel.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/12#v25)</sup> For I am the Lord: I will speak, and the word that I shall speak shall come to pass; it shall be no more prolonged: for in your days, O rebellious house, will I say the word, and will perform it, saith the Lord God.

- The Lord asked about the proverb in the land of Israel, that the days are prolonged and every vision fails.
- The people heard the prophecy but said it would not come to pass in their time, that it was beyond them and nothing to worry about.
- The Most High will make that proverb cease so they use it no more in Israel, for He will make the prophecies come to pass quickly; the days are at hand and the effect of every vision.
- He will speak, and the word He speaks shall come to pass and be no more prolonged.

>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ezekiel/12#v28)</sup> Therefore say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; There shall none of my words be prolonged any more, but the word which I have spoken shall be done, saith the Lord God.

- The Most High declares that none of His words shall be prolonged any more, but the word which He has spoken shall be done, fulfilled quickly.

# Ezekiel 13: Woe Unto the Foolish Prophets

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 13](/bible/ezekiel/13)

**[Ezekiel 13:1-7](/bible/ezekiel/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/13#v1)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/13#v2)</sup> Son of man, prophesy against the prophets of Israel that prophesy, and say thou unto them that prophesy out of their own hearts, Hear ye the word of the Lord;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/13#v3)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; Woe unto the foolish prophets, that follow their own spirit, and have seen nothing!
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/13#v4)</sup> O Israel, thy prophets are like the foxes in the deserts.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/13#v5)</sup> Ye have not gone up into the gaps, neither made up the hedge for the house of Israel to stand in the battle in the day of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/13#v6)</sup> They have seen vanity and lying divination, saying, The Lord saith: and the Lord hath not sent them: and they have made others to hope that they would confirm the word.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/13#v7)</sup> Have ye not seen a vain vision, and have ye not spoken a lying divination, whereas ye say, The Lord saith it; albeit I have not spoken?

- The word of the Lord came telling Ezekiel to prophesy against the prophets of Israel that prophesy out of their own hearts.
- Woe unto the foolish prophets that follow their own spirit and have seen nothing, just like the pastors today who follow the spirit of the white man, the spirit of Esau, Christmas, and all these things, changing our people and giving them a false sense of security that they will get the kingdom of heaven.
- The prophets are like the foxes in the desert, using craftiness in a ruined and wasted area of Israel to make gain among the people, the same as a well-groomed church in a run-down area of the hood where the pastor makes gain off the people.
- They have not gone up into the gaps nor made up the hedge for the house of Israel to stand in the battle in the day of the Lord, meaning they did not protect Israel from the Lord's wrath the way Moses stood in the gap when we went into idolatry.
- They have seen vanity and lying divination, saying the Lord sent them when He had not, making Israel hope their lies would be confirmed.

**[Ezekiel 13:8-12](/bible/ezekiel/13#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/13#v8)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because ye have spoken vanity, and seen lies, therefore, behold, I am against you, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/13#v9)</sup> And mine hand shall be upon the prophets that see vanity, and that divine lies: they shall not be in the assembly of my people, neither shall they be written in the writing of the house of Israel, neither shall they enter into the land of Israel; and ye shall know that I am the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/13#v10)</sup> Because, even because they have seduced my people, saying, Peace; and there was no peace; and one built up a wall, and, lo, others daubed it with untempered morter:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/13#v11)</sup> Say unto them which daub it with untempered morter, that it shall fall: there shall be an overflowing shower; and ye, O great hailstones, shall fall; and a stormy wind shall rend it.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/13#v12)</sup> Lo, when the wall is fallen, shall it not be said unto you, Where is the daubing wherewith ye have daubed it?

- Because they speak vanity and see lies, the Most High is against them; His hand will be upon the prophets that see vanity and divine lies.
- They shall not be in the assembly of His people, nor written in the writing of the house of Israel, nor enter the land of Israel, meaning they will die in captivity.
- They have seduced His people with another spirit, saying peace when there is no peace, the way pastors today seduce our people with the spirit of white jesus and not the spirit of the Most High of this Bible.
- One built a wall and others daubed it with untempered mortar, patchwork upon the wall of Jerusalem, the prophets saying peace and safety while telling the people to build the walls up.

**[Ezekiel 13:13-16](/bible/ezekiel/13#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/13#v13)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; I will even rend it with a stormy wind in my fury; and there shall be an overflowing shower in mine anger, and great hailstones in my fury to consume it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/13#v14)</sup> So will I break down the wall that ye have daubed with untempered morter, and bring it down to the ground, so that the foundation thereof shall be discovered, and it shall fall, and ye shall be consumed in the midst thereof: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/13#v15)</sup> Thus will I accomplish my wrath upon the wall, and upon them that have daubed it with untempered morter, and will say unto you, The wall is no more, neither they that daubed it;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/13#v16)</sup> To wit, the prophets of Israel which prophesy concerning Jerusalem, and which see visions of peace for her, and there is no peace, saith the Lord God.

- The Most High will rend the wall with a stormy wind in His fury, with an overflowing shower in His anger and great hailstones in His fury to consume it.
- He will break down the wall daubed with untempered mortar and bring it to the ground so its foundation is discovered, and they shall be consumed in the midst of it and know He is the Lord.
- He will accomplish His wrath upon the wall and upon them that daubed it, the prophets of Israel which prophesied concerning Jerusalem and saw visions of peace for her when there was no peace, because of our sins.

**[Ezekiel 13:17-19](/bible/ezekiel/13#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/13#v17)</sup> Likewise, thou son of man, set thy face against the daughters of thy people, which prophesy out of their own heart; and prophesy thou against them,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/13#v18)</sup> And say, Thus saith the Lord God; Woe to the women that sew pillows to all armholes, and make kerchiefs upon the head of every stature to hunt souls! Will ye hunt the souls of my people, and will ye save the souls alive that come unto you?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/13#v19)</sup> And will ye pollute me among my people for handfuls of barley and for pieces of bread, to slay the souls that should not die, and to save the souls alive that should not live, by your lying to my people that hear your lies?

- Ezekiel is to set his face against the daughters of his people which prophesy out of their own heart, just like the female pastors today who want to prophesy and teach our people and teach the men.
- Woe to the women that sew pillows to all armholes and make kerchiefs upon the head of every stature to hunt souls; the pillows are the wide robe-like openings down the arm and the kerchief is the covering placed on the head.
- They hunt the souls of the Most High's people and pollute His word among the people, saying God loves everybody and praise white jesus, doing it back then for handfuls of barley and pieces of bread and today for money.

**[Ezekiel 13:20-21](/bible/ezekiel/13#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/13#v20)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I am against your pillows, wherewith ye there hunt the souls to make them fly, and I will tear them from your arms, and will let the souls go, even the souls that ye hunt to make them fly.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/13#v21)</sup> Your kerchiefs also will I tear, and deliver my people out of your hand, and they shall be no more in your hand to be hunted; and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

- The Most High is against their pillows, wherewith they hunt the souls to make them fly, meaning to fly into sin and wickedness; He will tear them from their arms and let the hunted souls go free.
- He will use men who keep His commandments to go out and teach the people the proper way and tear them from the arms of these wicked women.
- He will tear their kerchiefs and deliver His people out of their hand, so they shall be no more in their hand to be hunted, and they shall know He is the Lord.

**[Ezekiel 13:22-23](/bible/ezekiel/13#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/13#v22)</sup> Because with lies ye have made the heart of the righteous sad, whom I have not made sad; and strengthened the hands of the wicked, that he should not return from his wicked way, by promising him life:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/13#v23)</sup> Therefore ye shall see no more vanity, nor divine divinations: for I will deliver my people out of your hand: and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

- With lies they made the heart of the righteous sad whom the Most High had not made sad, and strengthened the hands of the wicked that he should not return from his wicked way by promising him life.
- That is what happens today: the righteous are made sad by these women carrying on with no Bible in hand, just talking lies, while they strengthen the hands of the wicked and the people that put them in power.
- Therefore they shall see no more vanity nor divine divinations, for the Most High will deliver His people out of their hand, and they shall know He is the Lord.

# Ezekiel 14: Idols in the Heart and the Four Sore Judgments

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 14](/bible/ezekiel/14)

**[Ezekiel 14:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/14#v1)</sup> Then came certain of the elders of Israel unto me, and sat before me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/14#v2)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/14#v3)</sup> Son of man, these men have set up their idols in their heart, and put the stumblingblock of their iniquity before their face: should I be enquired of at all by them?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/14#v4)</sup> Therefore speak unto them, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; Every man of the house of Israel that setteth up his idols in his heart, and putteth the stumblingblock of his iniquity before his face, and cometh to the prophet; I the Lord will answer him that cometh according to the multitude of his idols;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/14#v5)</sup> That I may take the house of Israel in their own heart, because they are all estranged from me through their idols.

- Certain of the elders of Israel came and sat before Ezekiel, and the word of the Lord said these men have set up their idols in their hearts, setting up something above God, and put the stumbling block of their iniquity before their face.
- The Most High asks whether He should be inquired of at all by them.
- Every man of the house of Israel that sets up his idols in his heart and comes to the prophet, the Lord will answer according to the multitude of his idols, that He may take the house of Israel in their own heart, because they are all estranged from Him through their idols and spiritual idolatry.

**[Ezekiel 14:6-8](/bible/ezekiel/14#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/14#v6)</sup> Therefore say unto the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord God; Repent, and turn yourselves from your idols; and turn away your faces from all your abominations.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/14#v7)</sup> For every one of the house of Israel, or of the stranger that sojourneth in Israel, which separateth himself from me, and setteth up his idols in his heart, and putteth the stumblingblock of his iniquity before his face, and cometh to a prophet to enquire of him concerning me; I the Lord will answer him by myself:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/14#v8)</sup> And I will set my face against that man, and will make him a sign and a proverb, and I will cut him off from the midst of my people; and ye shall know that I am the Lord.

- The Most High tells the house of Israel to repent, come back to His laws, and turn themselves from their idols and away from all their abominations.
- Every one of the house of Israel, or the stranger that sojourns in Israel, who separates himself from Him and sets up idols in his heart and comes to a prophet to inquire, the Lord will answer by Himself and set His face against that man.
- He will make him a sign and a proverb, meaning a curse, and cut him off from the midst of His people as an example, that they may know He is the Lord.

**[Ezekiel 14:9-11](/bible/ezekiel/14#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/14#v9)</sup> And if the prophet be deceived when he hath spoken a thing, I the Lord have deceived that prophet, and I will stretch out my hand upon him, and will destroy him from the midst of my people Israel.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/14#v10)</sup> And they shall bear the punishment of their iniquity: the punishment of the prophet shall be even as the punishment of him that seeketh unto him;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/14#v11)</sup> That the house of Israel may go no more astray from me, neither be polluted any more with all their transgressions; but that they may be my people, and I may be their God, saith the Lord God.

- If a prophet is deceived when he has spoken a thing, the Lord Himself has deceived that prophet; do not try to deceive yourself in this truth, and when we get counsel we should take it and keep it moving.
- He will stretch out His hand upon that prophet and destroy him from the midst of His people Israel, and both shall bear the punishment of their iniquity.
- This is so the house of Israel may go no more astray from Him nor be polluted any more with their transgressions, but be His people and He their God.
  - **[Job 12:16](/bible/job/12#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/job/12#v16)</sup> With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his.

    The deceived and the deceiver are the Lord's, so do not try to deceive yourself.

**[Ezekiel 14:12-14](/bible/ezekiel/14#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/14#v12)</sup> The word of the Lord came again to me, saying,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/14#v13)</sup> Son of man, when the land sinneth against me by trespassing grievously, then will I stretch out mine hand upon it, and will break the staff of the bread thereof, and will send famine upon it, and will cut off man and beast from it:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/14#v14)</sup> Though these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, they should deliver but their own souls by their righteousness, saith the Lord God.

- The word of the Lord came saying that when a land sins by trespassing grievously, He will stretch out His hand upon it, break the staff of its bread, and send famine to cut off man and beast.
- Though these three men, Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, they should deliver but their own souls by their righteousness.
- No one can save anyone else; it does not matter who you are or what your status is in Israel, you have to save yourself by your own righteousness.

**[Ezekiel 14:15-16](/bible/ezekiel/14#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/14#v15)</sup> If I cause noisome beasts to pass through the land, and they spoil it, so that it be desolate, that no man may pass through because of the beasts:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/14#v16)</sup> Though these three men were in it, as I live, saith the Lord God, they shall deliver neither sons nor daughters; they only shall be delivered, but the land shall be desolate.

- If He causes noisome beasts to pass through the land and spoil it so that it is desolate and no man may pass through, this is one of His judgments.
- Though these three men were in it, as the Lord lives they shall deliver neither sons nor daughters; they only shall be delivered by their righteousness, but the land shall be desolate.

**[Ezekiel 14:17-18](/bible/ezekiel/14#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/14#v17)</sup> Or if I bring a sword upon that land, and say, Sword, go through the land; so that I cut off man and beast from it:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/14#v18)</sup> Though these three men were in it, as I live, saith the Lord God, they shall deliver neither sons nor daughters, but they only shall be delivered themselves.

- If He brings a sword upon that land to cut off man and beast, though these three men were in it, they shall deliver neither sons nor daughters.
- They only shall deliver themselves by their righteousness.

**[Ezekiel 14:19-20](/bible/ezekiel/14#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/14#v19)</sup> Or if I send a pestilence into that land, and pour out my fury upon it in blood, to cut off from it man and beast:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/14#v20)</sup> Though Noah, Daniel, and Job, were in it, as I live, saith the Lord God, they shall deliver neither son nor daughter; they shall but deliver their own souls by their righteousness.

- If He sends a pestilence into the land and pours out His fury upon it in blood to cut off man and beast, though Noah, Daniel, and Job were in it, they shall deliver neither son nor daughter.
- They shall but deliver their own souls by their righteousness; everyone has to work out their own righteousness in fear and trembling, delivering their own soul by keeping the Most High's commandments.
  - **[Proverbs 19:16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)</sup> He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die.

    He that keeps the commandment keeps his own soul, so you must deliver your own soul by keeping the Most High's commandments.

**[Ezekiel 14:21-23](/bible/ezekiel/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/14#v21)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God; How much more when I send my four sore judgments upon Jerusalem, the sword, and the famine, and the noisome beast, and the pestilence, to cut off from it man and beast?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/14#v22)</sup> Yet, behold, therein shall be left a remnant that shall be brought forth, both sons and daughters: behold, they shall come forth unto you, and ye shall see their way and their doings: and ye shall be comforted concerning the evil that I have brought upon Jerusalem, even concerning all that I have brought upon it.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/14#v23)</sup> And they shall comfort you, when ye see their ways and their doings: and ye shall know that I have not done without cause all that I have done in it, saith the Lord God.

- How much more when the Most High sends His four sore judgments upon Jerusalem, the sword, the famine, the noisome beast, and the pestilence, to cut off man and beast; and these are not just for back then but for today.
- Behold, a remnant shall be left, both sons and daughters, that shall be brought forth unto you, and you shall see their ways and their doings.
- You shall be comforted concerning the evil the Most High brought upon Jerusalem when you see their ways, and you shall know He has not done without cause all that He has done in it.
