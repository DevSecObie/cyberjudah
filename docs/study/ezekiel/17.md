**[Ezekiel 17:1-6](/bible/ezekiel/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/17#v1)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/17#v2)</sup> Son of man, put forth a riddle, and speak a parable unto the house of Israel;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/17#v3)</sup> And say, Thus saith the Lord God; A great eagle with great wings, longwinged, full of feathers, which had divers colours, came unto Lebanon, and took the highest branch of the cedar:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/17#v4)</sup> He cropped off the top of his young twigs, and carried it into a land of traffick; he set it in a city of merchants.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/17#v5)</sup> He took also of the seed of the land, and planted it in a fruitful field; he placed it by great waters, and set it as a willow tree.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/17#v6)</sup> And it grew, and became a spreading vine of low stature, whose branches turned toward him, and the roots thereof were under him: so it became a vine, and brought forth branches, and shot forth sprigs.

- The word of the Most High came to Ezekiel to put forth a riddle and speak a parable unto the house of Israel.
- A great eagle with great wings and divers colors came unto Lebanon, took the highest branch of the cedar, cropped off the top of his young twigs, and carried it into a land of traffic and a city of merchants.
- He took also of the seed of the land, planted it in a fruitful field by great waters, and it grew into a spreading vine of low stature whose branches turned toward him.

**[Ezekiel 17:7-10](/bible/ezekiel/17#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/17#v7)</sup> There was also another great eagle with great wings and many feathers: and, behold, this vine did bend her roots toward him, and shot forth her branches toward him, that he might water it by the furrows of her plantation.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/17#v8)</sup> It was planted in a good soil by great waters, that it might bring forth branches, and that it might bear fruit, that it might be a goodly vine.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/17#v9)</sup> Say thou, Thus saith the Lord God; Shall it prosper? shall he not pull up the roots thereof, and cut off the fruit thereof, that it wither? it shall wither in all the leaves of her spring, even without great power or many people to pluck it up by the roots thereof.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/17#v10)</sup> Yea, behold, being planted, shall it prosper? shall it not utterly wither, when the east wind toucheth it? it shall wither in the furrows where it grew.

- There was another great eagle with great wings and many feathers, and this vine bent her roots and shot forth her branches toward him that he might water it, though it was already planted in good soil by great waters.
- The Most High asks whether it shall prosper; He shall pull up the roots and cut off the fruit that it wither, and when the east wind touches it, it shall utterly wither in the furrows where it grew.

**[Ezekiel 17:11-14](/bible/ezekiel/17#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/17#v11)</sup> Moreover the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/17#v12)</sup> Say now to the rebellious house, Know ye not what these things mean? tell them, Behold, the king of Babylon is come to Jerusalem, and hath taken the king thereof, and the princes thereof, and led them with him to Babylon;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/17#v13)</sup> And hath taken of the king’s seed, and made a covenant with him, and hath taken an oath of him: he hath also taken the mighty of the land:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/17#v14)</sup> That the kingdom might be base, that it might not lift itself up, but that by keeping of his covenant it might stand.

- The Most High tells the rebellious house what these things mean: the king of Babylon came to Jerusalem, took the king and princes to Babylon, took of the king's seed, made a covenant and took an oath of him, and also took the mighty of the land.
- The enemy takes the mighty of the land, the best and brightest, and turns them against their people by giving them the mind of the oppressor, so the kingdom might be base and not lift itself up.
- When our people do well financially, get a good education, and buy into the pick-yourself-up-by-the-bootstraps idea that everyone has the same opportunity, the smart ones are removed from the ghetto so they will not be there to cause the uprising.

**[Ezekiel 17:15-21](/bible/ezekiel/17#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/17#v15)</sup> But he rebelled against him in sending his ambassadors into Egypt, that they might give him horses and much people. Shall he prosper? shall he escape that doeth such things? or shall he break the covenant, and be delivered?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/17#v16)</sup> As I live, saith the Lord God, surely in the place where the king dwelleth that made him king, whose oath he despised, and whose covenant he brake, even with him in the midst of Babylon he shall die.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/17#v17)</sup> Neither shall Pharaoh with his mighty army and great company make for him in the war, by casting up mounts, and building forts, to cut off many persons:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/17#v18)</sup> Seeing he despised the oath by breaking the covenant, when, lo, he had given his hand, and hath done all these things, he shall not escape.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/17#v19)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; As I live, surely mine oath that he hath despised, and my covenant that he hath broken, even it will I recompense upon his own head.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/17#v20)</sup> And I will spread my net upon him, and he shall be taken in my snare, and I will bring him to Babylon, and will plead with him there for his trespass that he hath trespassed against me.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/17#v21)</sup> And all his fugitives with all his bands shall fall by the sword, and they that remain shall be scattered toward all winds: and ye shall know that I the Lord have spoken it.

- The king rebelled against Babylon and sent ambassadors into Egypt for horses and much people; the Most High asks shall he prosper, shall he escape, or shall he break the covenant and be delivered.
- Because he despised the oath and broke the covenant after giving his hand, he shall die in the midst of Babylon, and Pharaoh with his mighty army shall not help him in the war.
- The Most High will spread His net upon him, take him in His snare, bring him to Babylon, and plead with him there; his fugitives shall fall by the sword and the remnant be scattered, and they shall know that He is the Lord.

**[Ezekiel 17:22-24](/bible/ezekiel/17#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/17#v22)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God; I will also take of the highest branch of the high cedar, and will set it; I will crop off from the top of his young twigs a tender one, and will plant it upon an high mountain and eminent:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/17#v23)</sup> In the mountain of the height of Israel will I plant it: and it shall bring forth boughs, and bear fruit, and be a goodly cedar: and under it shall dwell all fowl of every wing; in the shadow of the branches thereof shall they dwell.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/17#v24)</sup> And all the trees of the field shall know that I the Lord have brought down the high tree, have exalted the low tree, have dried up the green tree, and have made the dry tree to flourish: I the Lord have spoken and have done it.

- The Most High will Himself take of the highest branch of the high cedar, crop off a tender twig, and plant it upon the high mountain in the height of Israel.
- It shall bring forth boughs, bear fruit, and be a goodly cedar under which all fowl of every wing shall dwell, and all the trees of the field shall know that He has brought down the high tree and exalted the low tree.
- If the Most High has declared it, it shall come to pass, for the scripture cannot be broken.

    The scripture cannot be broken, so what the Most High has spoken concerning the low tree exalted and the high tree brought down He will surely do.
