Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 29](/bible/ezekiel/29)

As the reading moves through chapters 27, 28, and 29, the pattern is plain: the Most High speaks against the nations, delivering His judgments upon Tyrus, Zidon, and now Egypt.

**[Ezekiel 29:1-7](/bible/ezekiel/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/29#v1)</sup> In the tenth year, in the tenth month, in the twelfth day of the month, the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/29#v2)</sup> Son of man, set thy face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and prophesy against him, and against all Egypt:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/29#v3)</sup> Speak, and say, Thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I am against thee, Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great dragon that lieth in the midst of his rivers, which hath said, My river is mine own, and I have made it for myself.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/29#v4)</sup> But I will put hooks in thy jaws, and I will cause the fish of thy rivers to stick unto thy scales, and I will bring thee up out of the midst of thy rivers, and all the fish of thy rivers shall stick unto thy scales.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/29#v5)</sup> And I will leave thee thrown into the wilderness, thee and all the fish of thy rivers: thou shalt fall upon the open fields; thou shalt not be brought together, nor gathered: I have given thee for meat to the beasts of the field and to the fowls of the heaven.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/29#v6)</sup> And all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am the Lord, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/29#v7)</sup> When they took hold of thee by thy hand, thou didst break, and rend all their shoulder: and when they leaned upon thee, thou brakest, and madest all their loins to be at a stand.

- In the tenth year, tenth month, twelfth day, the word came to set Ezekiel's face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great dragon that lies in the midst of his rivers and says, My river is mine own, and I have made it for myself.
- The Most High will put hooks in his jaws, bring him up out of his rivers, and leave him thrown in the wilderness, because Egypt has been a staff of reed to the house of Israel that broke and tore their shoulder when they leaned upon it.

**[Ezekiel 29:8-16](/bible/ezekiel/29#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/29#v8)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will bring a sword upon thee, and cut off man and beast out of thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/29#v9)</sup> And the land of Egypt shall be desolate and waste; and they shall know that I am the Lord: because he hath said, The river is mine, and I have made it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/29#v10)</sup> Behold, therefore I am against thee, and against thy rivers, and I will make the land of Egypt utterly waste and desolate, from the tower of Syene even unto the border of Ethiopia.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/29#v11)</sup> No foot of man shall pass through it, nor foot of beast shall pass through it, neither shall it be inhabited forty years.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/29#v12)</sup> And I will make the land of Egypt desolate in the midst of the countries that are desolate, and her cities among the cities that are laid waste shall be desolate forty years: and I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/29#v13)</sup> Yet thus saith the Lord God; At the end of forty years will I gather the Egyptians from the people whither they were scattered:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/29#v14)</sup> And I will bring again the captivity of Egypt, and will cause them to return into the land of Pathros, into the land of their habitation; and they shall be there a base kingdom.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/29#v15)</sup> It shall be the basest of the kingdoms; neither shall it exalt itself any more above the nations: for I will diminish them, that they shall no more rule over the nations.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/29#v16)</sup> And it shall be no more the confidence of the house of Israel, which bringeth their iniquity to remembrance, when they shall look after them: but they shall know that I am the Lord God.

- The Most High will bring a sword upon Egypt, cut off man and beast, and make the land utterly waste and desolate from Syene to the border of Ethiopia, uninhabited forty years, scattering the Egyptians among the nations.
- At the end of forty years He will bring the Egyptians again from where they were scattered into the land of Pathros, but they shall be a base kingdom, the basest of kingdoms, no more to rule over the nations or be the confidence of the house of Israel.

**[Ezekiel 29:17-21](/bible/ezekiel/29#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/29#v17)</sup> And it came to pass in the seven and twentieth year, in the first month, in the first day of the month, the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/29#v18)</sup> Son of man, Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon caused his army to serve a great service against Tyrus: every head was made bald, and every shoulder was peeled: yet had he no wages, nor his army, for Tyrus, for the service that he had served against it:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/29#v19)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will give the land of Egypt unto Nebuchadrezzar king of Babylon; and he shall take her multitude, and take her spoil, and take her prey; and it shall be the wages for his army.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/29#v20)</sup> I have given him the land of Egypt for his labour wherewith he served against it, because they wrought for me, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/29#v21)</sup> In that day will I cause the horn of the house of Israel to bud forth, and I will give thee the opening of the mouth in the midst of them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- In the twenty-seventh year the word came that Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon caused his army to serve a great service against Tyrus, yet had no wages for it, every head made bald and every shoulder peeled.
- Therefore the Most High gives the land of Egypt to Nebuchadnezzar as wages for his army, because they wrought for Him; in that day the horn of the house of Israel will bud forth and Ezekiel will be given the opening of the mouth in their midst.
