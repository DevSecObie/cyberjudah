**[Ezekiel 24:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/24#v1)</sup> Again in the ninth year, in the tenth month, in the tenth day of the month, the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/24#v2)</sup> Son of man, write thee the name of the day, even of this same day: the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem this same day.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/24#v3)</sup> And utter a parable unto the rebellious house, and say unto them, Thus saith the Lord God; Set on a pot, set it on, and also pour water into it:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/24#v4)</sup> Gather the pieces thereof into it, even every good piece, the thigh, and the shoulder; fill it with the choice bones.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/24#v5)</sup> Take the choice of the flock, and burn also the bones under it, and make it boil well, and let them seethe the bones of it therein.

- In the ninth year, tenth month, and tenth day, the word of the Most High comes on the very day the king of Babylon set himself against Jerusalem, and Ezekiel is told to write the name of the day and utter a parable to the rebellious house.
- He is told to set on a pot, pour in water, and gather the pieces, the thigh and the shoulder and the choice bones, taking the choice of the flock and burning the bones under it to make it boil well. This pictures Babylon taking out the best of our mighty men and leaders first.

**[Ezekiel 24:6-8](/bible/ezekiel/24#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/24#v6)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God; Woe to the bloody city, to the pot whose scum is therein, and whose scum is not gone out of it! bring it out piece by piece; let no lot fall upon it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/24#v7)</sup> For her blood is in the midst of her; she set it upon the top of a rock; she poured it not upon the ground, to cover it with dust;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/24#v8)</sup> That it might cause fury to come up to take vengeance; I have set her blood upon the top of a rock, that it should not be covered.

- Woe to the bloody city, to the pot whose scum is therein; He calls the choice pieces, the best of the Israelites we looked up to, scum, because of the laws we broke. The Most High will call you something you do not want to be called.
- Her blood is in the midst of her, set upon the top of a rock and not poured on the ground to be covered, so that it might cause fury to come up and take vengeance.

**[Ezekiel 24:9-13](/bible/ezekiel/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/24#v9)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Woe to the bloody city! I will even make the pile for fire great.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/24#v10)</sup> Heap on wood, kindle the fire, consume the flesh, and spice it well, and let the bones be burned.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/24#v11)</sup> Then set it empty upon the coals thereof, that the brass of it may be hot, and may burn, and that the filthiness of it may be molten in it, that the scum of it may be consumed.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/24#v12)</sup> She hath wearied herself with lies, and her great scum went not forth out of her: her scum shall be in the fire.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/24#v13)</sup> In thy filthiness is lewdness: because I have purged thee, and thou wast not purged, thou shalt not be purged from thy filthiness any more, till I have caused my fury to rest upon thee.

- Woe to the bloody city: the Most High will make the pile for fire great, heap on wood, kindle the fire, consume the flesh, spice it well, and let the bones be burnt, then set the empty pot on the coals till the brass is hot and the scum consumed. He does not merely cook it and let the flame die; He stacks the wood and keeps it lit so we cook long and well.
- Because her filthiness is lewdness and He purged her yet she was not purged, she shall not be purged from her filthiness anymore until He has caused His fury to rest upon her. He will not go back, nor spare, nor repent; there is no more sackcloth or fasting to turn Him back at this point.

**[Ezekiel 24:15-18](/bible/ezekiel/24#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/24#v15)</sup> Also the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/24#v16)</sup> Son of man, behold, I take away from thee the desire of thine eyes with a stroke: yet neither shalt thou mourn nor weep, neither shall thy tears run down.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/24#v17)</sup> Forbear to cry, make no mourning for the dead, bind the tire of thine head upon thee, and put on thy shoes upon thy feet, and cover not thy lips, and eat not the bread of men.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/24#v18)</sup> So I spake unto the people in the morning: and at even my wife died; and I did in the morning as I was commanded.

- The Most High tells Ezekiel He will take away the desire of his eyes with a stroke, yet he must not mourn, weep, or make mourning for the dead; he is to bind his tire, put on his shoes, cover not his lips, and eat not the bread of men.
- Ezekiel spoke to the people in the morning, and in the evening his wife died, and he did in the morning as he was commanded. This shows the life of a prophet, whose purpose is above family, love, and children, so when a wife or a distraction interferes with the work the Most High assigned, the purpose comes first.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 19:2](/bible/sirach/19#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/19#v2)</sup> Wine and women will make men of understanding to fall away: and he that cleaveth to harlots will become impudent.

    The Most High took the wife away because such things cause even prophets of understanding to fall away from Him.
  - **[1 Corinthians 7:35](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v35)</sup> And this I speak for your own profit; not that I may cast a snare upon you, but for that which is comely, and that ye may attend upon the Lord without distraction.

    Paul urged the single life so that a man may attend upon the Most High without distraction, which is exactly what the Most High secured for Ezekiel.

**[Ezekiel 24:19-24](/bible/ezekiel/24#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/24#v19)</sup> And the people said unto me, Wilt thou not tell us what these things are to us, that thou doest so?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/24#v20)</sup> Then I answered them, The word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/24#v21)</sup> Speak unto the house of Israel, Thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I will profane my sanctuary, the excellency of your strength, the desire of your eyes, and that which your soul pitieth; and your sons and your daughters whom ye have left shall fall by the sword.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/24#v22)</sup> And ye shall do as I have done: ye shall not cover your lips, nor eat the bread of men.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/24#v23)</sup> And your tires shall be upon your heads, and your shoes upon your feet: ye shall not mourn nor weep; but ye shall pine away for your iniquities, and mourn one toward another.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/24#v24)</sup> Thus Ezekiel is unto you a sign: according to all that he hath done shall ye do: and when this cometh, ye shall know that I am the Lord God.

- When the people ask what these things mean to them, Ezekiel answers that the Most High will profane His sanctuary, the excellency of their strength and the desire of their eyes, and their sons and daughters shall fall by the sword. Just as his wife was the desire of his eyes, the beautiful temple was the desire of the nation's eyes.
- They shall do as Ezekiel did, not covering their lips nor eating the bread of men, but pining away for their iniquities and mourning to one another. Ezekiel is a sign to them, and when it comes they shall know that He is the Lord God.
  - **[Isaiah 8:18](/bible/isaiah/8#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/8#v18)</sup> Behold, I and the children whom the Lord hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the Lord of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion.

    The prophet and the children the Most High gave him are set for signs and wonders in Israel, as He used prophets, their wives, and their children to picture what would come upon the nation.

**[Ezekiel 24:25-27](/bible/ezekiel/24#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/24#v25)</sup> Also, thou son of man, shall it not be in the day when I take from them their strength, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and that whereupon they set their minds, their sons and their daughters,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/24#v26)</sup> That he that escapeth in that day shall come unto thee, to cause thee to hear it with thine ears?
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/24#v27)</sup> In that day shall thy mouth be opened to him which is escaped, and thou shalt speak, and be no more dumb: and thou shalt be a sign unto them; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- In the day the Most High takes from them their strength, the joy of their glory, the desire of their eyes, and their sons and daughters, one that escapes shall come to Ezekiel to bring the report with his own ears.
- In that day Ezekiel's mouth shall be opened to the one who escaped, and he shall be no more dumb but a sign unto them, that they may know He is the Lord. When it comes to pass they will come spooked and shocked, and he will tell them the Most High had told them.
