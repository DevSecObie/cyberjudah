Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 15](/bible/ezekiel/15)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 15 through 18. The Most High gives Ezekiel warning to prophesy of the coming dangers upon Jerusalem. Chapter 15 sets the useless vine tree as a sign of a people fit only for the fire. Chapter 16 lays out how the Most High took us from nothing, built us into a mighty nation, and how we then trusted in our own beauty and played the harlot with the gods and religions of the nations, closing with the everlasting covenant He keeps only with His people. Chapter 17 gives the riddle of the two eagles and how our enemies take the king's seed and the mighty of the land to leave the kingdom base. Chapter 18 teaches individual accountability and repentance: the soul that sinneth, it shall die, and the Most High has no pleasure in the death of the wicked but that he turn and live.

**[Ezekiel 15:1-5](/bible/ezekiel/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/15#v1)</sup> And the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/15#v2)</sup> Son of man, What is the vine tree more than any tree, or than a branch which is among the trees of the forest?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/15#v3)</sup> Shall wood be taken thereof to do any work? or will men take a pin of it to hang any vessel thereon?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/15#v4)</sup> Behold, it is cast into the fire for fuel; the fire devoureth both the ends of it, and the midst of it is burned. Is it meet for any work?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/15#v5)</sup> Behold, when it was whole, it was meet for no work: how much less shall it be meet yet for any work, when the fire hath devoured it, and it is burned?

- The word of the Most High came to Ezekiel asking what the vine tree is more than any other tree, or than a branch among the trees of the forest.
- No wood is taken of it to do any work, nor will men take a pin of it to hang a vessel upon; it is cast into the fire for fuel, and the fire devours both ends and the midst of it.
- If it was fit for no work when it was whole, how much less is it fit for any work when the fire has devoured it and it is burned.

**[Ezekiel 15:6-8](/bible/ezekiel/15#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/15#v6)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; As the vine tree among the trees of the forest, which I have given to the fire for fuel, so will I give the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/15#v7)</sup> And I will set my face against them; they shall go out from one fire, and another fire shall devour them; and ye shall know that I am the Lord, when I set my face against them.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/15#v8)</sup> And I will make the land desolate, because they have committed a trespass, saith the Lord God.

- As the vine tree among the trees of the forest is given to the fire for fuel, so the Most High gives the inhabitants of Jerusalem; they go out from one fire and another fire devours them.
- The Most High sets His face against them and makes the land desolate because they have committed a trespass, and they shall know that He is the Lord.
- This is the Most High giving warning through the prophet Ezekiel of the coming dangers of that time.

# Ezekiel 18: The Soul That Sinneth, It Shall Die

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 18](/bible/ezekiel/18)

**[Ezekiel 18:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/18#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord came unto me again, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/18#v2)</sup> What mean ye, that ye use this proverb concerning the land of Israel, saying, The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children’s teeth are set on edge?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/18#v3)</sup> As I live, saith the Lord God, ye shall not have occasion any more to use this proverb in Israel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/18#v4)</sup> Behold, all souls are mine; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is mine: the soul that sinneth, it shall die.

- The word of the Most High asks what they mean by the proverb, The fathers have eaten sour grapes, and the children's teeth are set on edge.
- As the Most High lives, they shall no more use this proverb in Israel, for all souls are His; as the soul of the father, so also the soul of the son is His.
- The soul that sinneth, it shall die, setting the theme of individual accountability.

**[Ezekiel 18:5-9](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)</sup> But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/18#v6)</sup> And hath not eaten upon the mountains, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, neither hath defiled his neighbour’s wife, neither hath come near to a menstruous woman,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/18#v7)</sup> And hath not oppressed any, but hath restored to the debtor his pledge, hath spoiled none by violence, hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/18#v8)</sup> He that hath not given forth upon usury, neither hath taken any increase, that hath withdrawn his hand from iniquity, hath executed true judgment between man and man,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/18#v9)</sup> Hath walked in my statutes, and hath kept my judgments, to deal truly; he is just, he shall surely live, saith the Lord God.

- If a man be just and do that which is lawful and right, has not eaten upon the mountains nor lifted his eyes to idols, nor defiled his neighbour's wife, nor oppressed any, but restored the debtor his pledge and given his bread to the hungry, he is just and shall surely live.
- He has not given forth upon usury nor taken increase, has withdrawn his hand from iniquity, executed true judgment between man and man, and walked in the Most High's statutes.

**[Ezekiel 18:10-13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/18#v10)</sup> If he beget a son that is a robber, a shedder of blood, and that doeth the like to any one of these things,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/18#v11)</sup> And that doeth not any of those duties, but even hath eaten upon the mountains, and defiled his neighbour’s wife,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/18#v12)</sup> Hath oppressed the poor and needy, hath spoiled by violence, hath not restored the pledge, and hath lifted up his eyes to the idols, hath committed abomination,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v13)</sup> Hath given forth upon usury, and hath taken increase: shall he then live? he shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him.

- If that just man beget a son that is a robber, a shedder of blood, who eats upon the mountains, defiles his neighbour's wife, oppresses the poor and needy, spoils by violence, and lifts his eyes to idols, committing abomination.
- Such a son who gives forth upon usury and takes increase shall not live; he has done all these abominations and shall surely die, and his blood shall be upon him.

**[Ezekiel 18:14-18](/bible/ezekiel/18#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/18#v14)</sup> Now, lo, if he beget a son, that seeth all his father’s sins which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not such like,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/18#v15)</sup> That hath not eaten upon the mountains, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, hath not defiled his neighbour’s wife,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/18#v16)</sup> Neither hath oppressed any, hath not withholden the pledge, neither hath spoiled by violence, but hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/18#v17)</sup> That hath taken off his hand from the poor, that hath not received usury nor increase, hath executed my judgments, hath walked in my statutes; he shall not die for the iniquity of his father, he shall surely live.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/18#v18)</sup> As for his father, because he cruelly oppressed, spoiled his brother by violence, and did that which is not good among his people, lo, even he shall die in his iniquity.

- If that wicked man beget a son that sees all his father's sins, considers, and does not do likewise, but gives his bread to the hungry, covers the naked, takes off his hand from the poor, and keeps the Most High's judgments and statutes.
- That son shall not die for the iniquity of his father; he shall surely live, but the father, because he cruelly oppressed and spoiled his brother, shall die in his own iniquity.

**[Ezekiel 18:19-20](/bible/ezekiel/18#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/18#v19)</sup> Yet say ye, Why? doth not the son bear the iniquity of the father? When the son hath done that which is lawful and right, and hath kept all my statutes, and hath done them, he shall surely live.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/18#v20)</sup> The soul that sinneth, it shall die. The son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son: the righteousness of the righteous shall be upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked shall be upon him.

- They ask why the son does not bear the iniquity of the father, when the son has done that which is lawful and right and kept the statutes; he shall surely live.
- The soul that sinneth, it shall die; the son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, nor the father the iniquity of the son; the righteousness of the righteous is upon him, and the wickedness of the wicked upon him.

**[Ezekiel 18:25-29](/bible/ezekiel/18#v25)**

- When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and dies in his iniquity, it is for the iniquity he has done; when a wicked man turns from his wickedness and does that which is lawful and right, he shall save his soul alive.

**[Ezekiel 18:30-32](/bible/ezekiel/18#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/18#v30)</sup> Therefore I will judge you, O house of Israel, every one according to his ways, saith the Lord God. Repent, and turn yourselves from all your transgressions; so iniquity shall not be your ruin.

> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/18#v31)</sup> Cast away from you all your transgressions, whereby ye have transgressed; and make you a new heart and a new spirit: for why will ye die, O house of Israel?

- Cast away all your transgressions and make you a new heart and a new spirit, the heart being the mind, for the Most High has no pleasure in the death of him that dies; wherefore turn yourselves and live.
- This reading taught repentance, how our enemies take the chief of our young men and women to leave the nation base, and that the Most High has a plan to use the lowly and base to confound the wise, so let your light shine rather than look for any slither of hope to unite with our oppressors.
