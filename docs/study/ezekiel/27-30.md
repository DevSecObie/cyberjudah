Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 27](/bible/ezekiel/27)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 27 through 30. It is largely a straight reading of the four chapters with light exposition. The thread running through chapters 27, 28, and 29 is that the Most High is speaking against different nations, pronouncing His judgments upon Tyrus, Zidon, and Egypt, and chapter 30 continues the burden against Egypt and her allies. Tyrus is lamented as the great merchant city of the sea; her pride and traffic bring her to ruin, and the same word turns to her prince and king, then to Zidon, then to Pharaoh and all Egypt.

**[Ezekiel 27:1-3](/bible/ezekiel/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/27#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord came again unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/27#v2)</sup> Now, thou son of man, take up a lamentation for Tyrus;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/27#v3)</sup> And say unto Tyrus, O thou that art situate at the entry of the sea, which art a merchant of the people for many isles, Thus saith the Lord God; O Tyrus, thou hast said, I am of perfect beauty.

- The word of the Most High came again unto Ezekiel, telling him to take up a lamentation for Tyrus, the city situated at the entry of the sea and a merchant of the people for many isles.
- Tyrus had said, I am of perfect beauty; her borders were in the midst of the seas, and her builders had perfected her beauty.

- The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were her mariners, her wise men were her pilots, and the men of Persia, Lud, and Phut were in her army, hanging their shields and helmets in her to set forth her comeliness.

# Ezekiel 29: The Judgment Against Pharaoh and Egypt

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 29](/bible/ezekiel/29)

As the reading moves through chapters 27, 28, and 29, the pattern is plain: the Most High speaks against the nations, delivering His judgments upon Tyrus, Zidon, and now Egypt.

>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/29#v4)</sup> But I will put hooks in thy jaws, and I will cause the fish of thy rivers to stick unto thy scales, and I will bring thee up out of the midst of thy rivers, and all the fish of thy rivers shall stick unto thy scales.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/29#v5)</sup> And I will leave thee thrown into the wilderness, thee and all the fish of thy rivers: thou shalt fall upon the open fields; thou shalt not be brought together, nor gathered: I have given thee for meat to the beasts of the field and to the fowls of the heaven.

> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/29#v6)</sup> And all the inhabitants of Egypt shall know that I am the Lord, because they have been a staff of reed to the house of Israel.

> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/29#v7)</sup> When they took hold of thee by thy hand, thou didst break, and rend all their shoulder: and when they leaned upon thee, thou brakest, and madest all their loins to be at a stand.

- In the tenth year, tenth month, twelfth day, the word came to set Ezekiel's face against Pharaoh king of Egypt, the great dragon that lies in the midst of his rivers and says, My river is mine own, and I have made it for myself.
- The Most High will put hooks in his jaws, bring him up out of his rivers, and leave him thrown in the wilderness, because Egypt has been a staff of reed to the house of Israel that broke and tore their shoulder when they leaned upon it.

- He will destroy the idols, cause the images to cease out of Noph, set fire in Egypt, pour His fury upon Sin, cut off the multitude of No, and send the cities of Egypt into captivity, so they shall know that He is the Lord.

**[Ezekiel 30:20-26](/bible/ezekiel/30#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/30#v20)</sup> And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the first month, in the seventh day of the month, that the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/30#v21)</sup> Son of man, I have broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt; and, lo, it shall not be bound up to be healed, to put a roller to bind it, to make it strong to hold the sword.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/30#v22)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I am against Pharaoh king of Egypt, and will break his arms, the strong, and that which was broken; and I will cause the sword to fall out of his hand.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/30#v23)</sup> And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and will disperse them through the countries.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/30#v24)</sup> And I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and put my sword in his hand: but I will break Pharaoh’s arms, and he shall groan before him with the groanings of a deadly wounded man.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/30#v25)</sup> But I will strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon, and the arms of Pharaoh shall fall down; and they shall know that I am the Lord, when I shall put my sword into the hand of the king of Babylon, and he shall stretch it out upon the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/30#v26)</sup> And I will scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and disperse them among the countries; and they shall know that I am the Lord.

- In the eleventh year the word came that the Most High has broken the arm of Pharaoh king of Egypt, and it shall not be bound up to be healed or made strong to hold the sword.
- He will break both Pharaoh's arms, strengthen the arms of the king of Babylon and put His sword in his hand, scatter the Egyptians among the nations, and they shall know that He is the Lord.
