Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 39](/bible/ezekiel/39)

This session of "4 Chapters a Day" covers [Ezekiel 39](/bible/ezekiel/39) through 42. [Ezekiel 39](/bible/ezekiel/39) finishes the prophecy against Gog, the chief prince of Meshech, whom the Most High brings against the mountains of Israel only to break his weapons, bury his multitude, and glorify His own name before the heathen. Chapters 40 through 42 turn to the vision of the temple, where a man with a measuring reed shows Ezekiel the courts, gates, chambers, and the most holy place. The teacher sets all of this as future prophecy: it comes to pass after Christ has taken down the nations and Israel dwells again in their own land, when the Most High gathers His people out of their captivity and the temple is established once more.

**[Ezekiel 39:1-3](/bible/ezekiel/39#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/39#v1)</sup> Therefore, thou son of man, prophesy against Gog, and say, Thus saith the Lord God; Behold, I am against thee, O Gog, the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/39#v2)</sup> And I will turn thee back, and leave but the sixth part of thee, and will cause thee to come up from the north parts, and will bring thee upon the mountains of Israel:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/39#v3)</sup> And I will smite thy bow out of thy left hand, and will cause thine arrows to fall out of thy right hand.

- Gog is the chief prince of Meshech and Tubal, and Magog is the land of Russia. Whoever comes out of the land of southern Georgia and Russia we know from the scriptures is Esau. This is all prophecy, going forward to the time after Christ has taken down the other nations, when Israel will be in their land.
- This is the same setting as [Revelation 20](/bible/revelation/20), when Satan is loosed in both the spiritual and the physical form: the spiritual demon Satan and the physical Esau. The Most High smites the bow out of Gog's left hand and causes his arrows to fall out of his right hand, so no weapon formed against Israel will prosper. He smacks their weapons out of their hand and their weapon is for naught.

**[Ezekiel 39:4-7](/bible/ezekiel/39#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/39#v4)</sup> Thou shalt fall upon the mountains of Israel, thou, and all thy bands, and the people that is with thee: I will give thee unto the ravenous birds of every sort, and to the beasts of the field to be devoured.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/39#v5)</sup> Thou shalt fall upon the open field: for I have spoken it, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/39#v6)</sup> And I will send a fire on Magog, and among them that dwell carelessly in the isles: and they shall know that I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/39#v7)</sup> So will I make my holy name known in the midst of my people Israel; and I will not let them pollute my holy name any more: and the heathen shall know that I am the Lord, the Holy One in Israel.

- Gog and all his bands fall upon the mountains of Israel, meaning they will not succeed. They are given to the ravenous birds and the beasts of the field to be devoured. This lets us know it is future prophecy, because we are still in captivity today and this has not happened yet.
- The Most High sends a fire on Magog and among them that dwell carelessly, and He establishes His name. Just as He did in Egypt, Israel will know again that it is the Lord, He will not let them pollute His holy name anymore, and the heathen shall know that He is the Holy One in Israel.

**[Ezekiel 39:8-10](/bible/ezekiel/39#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/39#v8)</sup> Behold, it is come, and it is done, saith the Lord God; this is the day whereof I have spoken.

- The day the Most High has spoken of comes to pass, and those who dwell in the cities of Israel go forth and burn the weapons, the shields, bows, arrows, hand staves, and spears, with fire seven years. Their weapons are taken from them; they will be beaten into submission and forced to work the land instead of making war.
- Israel takes no wood out of the field because they burn the weapons for fuel, and they spoil those that spoiled them and rob those that robbed them. It is a turning: those who took from us, we take from them.

- The Most High gives Gog a place of graves in Israel, the valley of the passengers on the east of the sea, and it is called the valley of Hamongog. They are put to death and buried there.
- For seven months the house of Israel buries them to cleanse the land, and that day is a day of renown when the Most High is glorified. Men of continual employment are set apart to pass through the land, and when they find a man's bones they set up a sign until the buriers bury it, and the city is called Hamonah.

>

- The Most High tells Ezekiel to speak to every feathered fowl and every beast of the field to gather to His sacrifice upon the mountains of Israel, that they may eat flesh and drink blood. There will be a great slaughter in that day.
- They eat the flesh of the mighty and drink the blood of the princes of the earth, of rams, lambs, goats, and bullocks, until they are full and drunken at His table with horses, chariots, and all men of war.

**[Ezekiel 39:21-24](/bible/ezekiel/39#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/39#v21)</sup> And I will set my glory among the heathen, and all the heathen shall see my judgment that I have executed, and my hand that I have laid upon them.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/39#v22)</sup> So the house of Israel shall know that I am the Lord their God from that day and forward.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/39#v23)</sup> And the heathen shall know that the house of Israel went into captivity for their iniquity: because they trespassed against me, therefore hid I my face from them, and gave them into the hand of their enemies: so fell they all by the sword.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/39#v24)</sup> According to their uncleanness and according to their transgressions have I done unto them, and hid my face from them.

- The Most High sets His glory among the heathen, and all the heathen see the judgment He has executed. Right now the Lord is not glorified in this kingdom, but in that day He will be, and He gets His people's attention. Today the world calls the heathen's works great, their space programs and their weapons of mass destruction, but when the Most High shuts all that down we will realize who the real power is.
- From that day the house of Israel knows that He is the Lord their God, and the heathen know that Israel went into captivity for their iniquity, because they trespassed against Him. That is why we are in the position we are in; He hid His face and gave us into the hand of our enemies because we did not obey.

He leaves none of them among the heathen anymore, and He will not hide His face anymore, for He has poured out His spirit upon the house of Israel.

# Ezekiel 40: The Vision of the Temple and the Measuring Reed

Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 40](/bible/ezekiel/40)

**[Ezekiel 40:1-4](/bible/ezekiel/40#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/40#v1)</sup> In the five and twentieth year of our captivity, in the beginning of the year, in the tenth day of the month, in the fourteenth year after that the city was smitten, in the selfsame day the hand of the Lord was upon me, and brought me thither.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/40#v2)</sup> In the visions of God brought he me into the land of Israel, and set me upon a very high mountain, by which was as the frame of a city on the south.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/40#v3)</sup> And he brought me thither, and, behold, there was a man, whose appearance was like the appearance of brass, with a line of flax in his hand, and a measuring reed; and he stood in the gate.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/40#v4)</sup> And the man said unto me, Son of man, behold with thine eyes, and hear with thine ears, and set thine heart upon all that I shall shew thee; for to the intent that I might shew them unto thee art thou brought hither: declare all that thou seest to the house of Israel.

- In the twenty-fifth year of the captivity, the hand of the Lord is upon Ezekiel and brings him in the visions of God into the land of Israel, setting him on a very high mountain where he sees the frame of a city.
- A man whose appearance is like brass stands in the gate with a line of flax and a measuring reed in his hand. This is an angel, and he tells Ezekiel to behold with his eyes, hear with his ears, and set his heart on all that is shown, to declare it to the house of Israel.

**[Ezekiel 40:5](/bible/ezekiel/40#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/40#v5)</sup> And behold a wall on the outside of the house round about, and in the man’s hand a measuring reed of six cubits long by the cubit and an hand breadth: so he measured the breadth of the building, one reed; and the height, one reed.

- There is a wall on the outside of the house round about, and the man holds a measuring reed of six cubits long. He measures the breadth of the building one reed and the height one reed. That comes to about nine feet tall and nine feet thick.

- He measures the porch of the gate, the posts, and the entry, so the gate would be about fifteen feet wide and nineteen and a half feet long. The little chambers are three on each side, all of one measure, with narrow windows, arches, and palm trees on the posts throughout.

The chamber facing south is for the priests who keep the charge of the house, and the chamber facing north is for the priests who keep the charge of the altar, the sons of Zadok among the sons of Levi, who come near the Lord to minister.
- The court is measured a hundred cubits square, with the altar before the house. Ezekiel is brought to the porch of the house, its posts and gate measured, the porch twenty cubits long and eleven broad, with pillars by the posts and steps going up to it.

> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/41#v2)</sup> And the breadth of the door was ten cubits; and the sides of the door were five cubits on the one side, and five cubits on the other side: and he measured the length thereof, forty cubits: and the breadth, twenty cubits.

- The sanctuary and the temple each have two doors with two turning leaves apiece, carved with cherubim and palm trees like those on the walls. There are thick planks upon the face of the porch, and narrow windows and palm trees on the sides.

> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/42#v2)</sup> Before the length of an hundred cubits was the north door, and the breadth was fifty cubits.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/42#v3)</sup> Over against the twenty cubits which were for the inner court, and over against the pavement which was for the utter court, was gallery against gallery in three stories.

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/42#v4)</sup> And before the chambers was a walk of ten cubits breadth inward, a way of one cubit; and their doors toward the north.

- Before the chambers is a walk of ten cubits breadth inward, a way of one cubit, with doors toward the north, about fifteen feet and a foot and a half. The upper chambers are shorter because the galleries take away more from them, and the building has no pillars like the courts. An entry on the east side leads into them from the outer court.

**[Ezekiel 42:10-14](/bible/ezekiel/42#v10)**

- When the priests enter, they shall not go out into the outer court until they lay aside the garments in which they minister, for those garments are holy. They put on other garments before they approach the things that are for the people. These are the laws the Levite priests are to follow.

- When the angel finishes measuring the inner house, he brings Ezekiel out by the east gate and measures the whole compass round about, five hundred reeds on the east side, five hundred on the north, five hundred on the south, and five hundred on the west, about fifty-five hundred feet per side.
- It has a wall round about, five hundred reeds long and five hundred broad, to make a separation between the sanctuary and the profane place. By rehearsing these measurements in such detail, the vision is pointing to the kingdom: the temple will be established again with the same measurements given to King Solomon, done all over again. As for why the Father requires such careful preparation, that is above our pay grade; we are to follow and learn, for His thoughts are not our thoughts.
  - **[Isaiah 55:8](/bible/isaiah/55#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/55#v8)</sup> For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the Lord.

    The Most High's thoughts and ways are higher than ours, so the reason for such exact preparation belongs to Him.
  - **[Isaiah 60:10](/bible/isaiah/60#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/60#v10)</sup> And the sons of strangers shall build up thy walls, and their kings shall minister unto thee: for in my wrath I smote thee, but in my favour have I had mercy on thee.

    The sons of strangers will build up the walls and their kings will minister, so the other nations build the temple while Israel's working days are over.
