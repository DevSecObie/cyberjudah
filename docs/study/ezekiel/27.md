Read the chapter → [Ezekiel 27](/bible/ezekiel/27)

This session covers Ezekiel chapters 27 through 30. It is largely a straight reading of the four chapters with light exposition. The thread running through chapters 27, 28, and 29 is that the Most High is speaking against different nations, pronouncing His judgments upon Tyrus, Zidon, and Egypt, and chapter 30 continues the burden against Egypt and her allies. Tyrus is lamented as the great merchant city of the sea; her pride and traffic bring her to ruin, and the same word turns to her prince and king, then to Zidon, then to Pharaoh and all Egypt.

**[Ezekiel 27:1-3](/bible/ezekiel/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/27#v1)</sup> The word of the Lord came again unto me, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/27#v2)</sup> Now, thou son of man, take up a lamentation for Tyrus;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/27#v3)</sup> And say unto Tyrus, O thou that art situate at the entry of the sea, which art a merchant of the people for many isles, Thus saith the Lord God; O Tyrus, thou hast said, I am of perfect beauty.

- The word of the Most High came again unto Ezekiel, telling him to take up a lamentation for Tyrus, the city situated at the entry of the sea and a merchant of the people for many isles.
- Tyrus had said, I am of perfect beauty; her borders were in the midst of the seas, and her builders had perfected her beauty.

**[Ezekiel 27:4-11](/bible/ezekiel/27#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/27#v4)</sup> Thy borders are in the midst of the seas, thy builders have perfected thy beauty.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/27#v5)</sup> They have made all thy ship boards of fir trees of Senir: they have taken cedars from Lebanon to make masts for thee.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/27#v6)</sup> Of the oaks of Bashan have they made thine oars; the company of the Ashurites have made thy benches of ivory, brought out of the isles of Chittim.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/27#v7)</sup> Fine linen with broidered work from Egypt was that which thou spreadest forth to be thy sail; blue and purple from the isles of Elishah was that which covered thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/27#v8)</sup> The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were thy mariners: thy wise men, O Tyrus, that were in thee, were thy pilots.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/27#v9)</sup> The ancients of Gebal and the wise men thereof were in thee thy calkers: all the ships of the sea with their mariners were in thee to occupy thy merchandise.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/27#v10)</sup> They of Persia and of Lud and of Phut were in thine army, thy men of war: they hanged the shield and helmet in thee; they set forth thy comeliness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/27#v11)</sup> The men of Arvad with thine army were upon thy walls round about, and the Gammadims were in thy towers: they hanged their shields upon thy walls round about; they have made thy beauty perfect.

- Her builders made all her ship boards of fir trees from Senir, took cedars from Lebanon to make masts, and made her oars of the oaks of Bashan, her benches of ivory, and her sails of fine linen from Egypt.
- The inhabitants of Zidon and Arvad were her mariners, her wise men were her pilots, and the men of Persia, Lud, and Phut were in her army, hanging their shields and helmets in her to set forth her comeliness.

**[Ezekiel 27:12-25](/bible/ezekiel/27#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/27#v12)</sup> Tarshish was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of all kind of riches; with silver, iron, tin, and lead, they traded in thy fairs.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/27#v13)</sup> Javan, Tubal, and Meshech, they were thy merchants: they traded the persons of men and vessels of brass in thy market.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/27#v14)</sup> They of the house of Togarmah traded in thy fairs with horses and horsemen and mules.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/27#v15)</sup> The men of Dedan were thy merchants; many isles were the merchandise of thine hand: they brought thee for a present horns of ivory and ebony.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/27#v16)</sup> Syria was thy merchant by reason of the multitude of the wares of thy making: they occupied in thy fairs with emeralds, purple, and broidered work, and fine linen, and coral, and agate.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/27#v17)</sup> Judah, and the land of Israel, they were thy merchants: they traded in thy market wheat of Minnith, and Pannag, and honey, and oil, and balm.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/27#v18)</sup> Damascus was thy merchant in the multitude of the wares of thy making, for the multitude of all riches; in the wine of Helbon, and white wool.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/ezekiel/27#v19)</sup> Dan also and Javan going to and fro occupied in thy fairs: bright iron, cassia, and calamus, were in thy market.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/27#v20)</sup> Dedan was thy merchant in precious clothes for chariots.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ezekiel/27#v21)</sup> Arabia, and all the princes of Kedar, they occupied with thee in lambs, and rams, and goats: in these were they thy merchants.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/27#v22)</sup> The merchants of Sheba and Raamah, they were thy merchants: they occupied in thy fairs with chief of all spices, and with all precious stones, and gold.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/27#v23)</sup> Haran, and Canneh, and Eden, the merchants of Sheba, Asshur, and Chilmad, were thy merchants.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ezekiel/27#v24)</sup> These were thy merchants in all sorts of things, in blue clothes, and broidered work, and in chests of rich apparel, bound with cords, and made of cedar, among thy merchandise.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/ezekiel/27#v25)</sup> The ships of Tarshish did sing of thee in thy market: and thou wast replenished, and made very glorious in the midst of the seas.

- Tarshish, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, Togarmah, Dedan, Syria, Judah and the land of Israel, Damascus, Dan, Arabia, Kedar, Sheba, and many more traded in her fairs with silver, iron, tin, lead, brass, horses, ivory, ebony, emeralds, purple, fine linen, wheat, honey, oil, wine, wool, spices, precious stones, gold, and rich apparel.
- By reason of the multitude of all her riches she was replenished and made very glorious in the midst of the sea, the ships of Tarshish singing of her in her market.

**[Ezekiel 27:26-36](/bible/ezekiel/27#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/ezekiel/27#v26)</sup> Thy rowers have brought thee into great waters: the east wind hath broken thee in the midst of the seas.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/ezekiel/27#v27)</sup> Thy riches, and thy fairs, thy merchandise, thy mariners, and thy pilots, thy calkers, and the occupiers of thy merchandise, and all thy men of war, that are in thee, and in all thy company which is in the midst of thee, shall fall into the midst of the seas in the day of thy ruin.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ezekiel/27#v28)</sup> The suburbs shall shake at the sound of the cry of thy pilots.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ezekiel/27#v29)</sup> And all that handle the oar, the mariners, and all the pilots of the sea, shall come down from their ships, they shall stand upon the land;
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/ezekiel/27#v30)</sup> And shall cause their voice to be heard against thee, and shall cry bitterly, and shall cast up dust upon their heads, they shall wallow themselves in the ashes:
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/27#v31)</sup> And they shall make themselves utterly bald for thee, and gird them with sackcloth, and they shall weep for thee with bitterness of heart and bitter wailing.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ezekiel/27#v32)</sup> And in their wailing they shall take up a lamentation for thee, and lament over thee, saying, What city is like Tyrus, like the destroyed in the midst of the sea?
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/ezekiel/27#v33)</sup> When thy wares went forth out of the seas, thou filledst many people; thou didst enrich the kings of the earth with the multitude of thy riches and of thy merchandise.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/ezekiel/27#v34)</sup> In the time when thou shalt be broken by the seas in the depths of the waters thy merchandise and all thy company in the midst of thee shall fall.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/ezekiel/27#v35)</sup> All the inhabitants of the isles shall be astonished at thee, and their kings shall be sore afraid, they shall be troubled in their countenance.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/ezekiel/27#v36)</sup> The merchants among the people shall hiss at thee; thou shalt be a terror, and never shalt be any more.

- Her rowers brought her into great waters, but the east wind broke her in the midst of the seas; her riches, her merchandise, her mariners, and all her company fell into the midst of the seas in the day of her ruin.
- The pilots and mariners cry bitterly, cast dust on their heads, make themselves bald, and take up a lamentation, asking what city is like Tyrus, destroyed in the midst of the sea; the merchants hiss at her, and she is made a terror and shall never be any more.
