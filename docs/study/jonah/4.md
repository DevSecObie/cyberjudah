**[Jonah 4:1-5](/bible/jonah/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/jonah/4#v1)</sup> But it displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was very angry.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/jonah/4#v2)</sup> And he prayed unto the Lord, and said, I pray thee, O Lord, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/jonah/4#v3)</sup> Therefore now, O Lord, take, I beseech thee, my life from me; for it is better for me to die than to live.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/jonah/4#v4)</sup> Then said the Lord, Doest thou well to be angry?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/jonah/4#v5)</sup> So Jonah went out of the city, and sat on the east side of the city, and there made him a booth, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city.

- It displeased Jonah exceedingly, and he was very angry.
- He said, Was not this my saying when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.
- Take, I beseech thee, my life from me; for it is better for me to die than to live. Then said the Lord, Doest thou well to be angry?
- Jonah went out of the city, and sat on the east side of the city, and made him a booth, and sat under it in the shadow, till he might see what would become of the city.

**[Jonah 4:11](/bible/jonah/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/jonah/4#v6)</sup> And the Lord God prepared a gourd, and made it to come up over Jonah, that it might be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his grief. So Jonah was exceeding glad of the gourd.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/jonah/4#v7)</sup> But God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd that it withered.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/jonah/4#v8)</sup> And it came to pass, when the sun did arise, that God prepared a vehement east wind; and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted, and wished in himself to die, and said, It is better for me to die than to live.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/jonah/4#v9)</sup> And God said to Jonah, Doest thou well to be angry for the gourd? And he said, I do well to be angry, even unto death.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/jonah/4#v10)</sup> Then said the Lord, Thou hast had pity on the gourd, for the which thou hast not laboured, neither madest it grow; which came up in a night, and perished in a night:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/jonah/4#v11)</sup> And should not I spare Nineveh, that great city, wherein are more than sixscore thousand persons that cannot discern between their right hand and their left hand; and also much cattle?

- The Lord God prepared a gourd, and made it to come up over Jonah, to be a shadow over his head, to deliver him from his grief; so Jonah was exceeding glad of the gourd.
- God prepared a worm when the morning rose the next day, and it smote the gourd that it withered; and he prepared a vehement east wind, and the sun beat upon the head of Jonah, that he fainted.
- Thou hast had pity on the gourd, for the which thou hast not laboured, neither madest it grow, which came up in a night, and perished in a night.
- And should not I spare Nineveh, that great city, wherein are more than sixscore thousand persons that cannot discern between their right hand and their left hand; and also much cattle?
