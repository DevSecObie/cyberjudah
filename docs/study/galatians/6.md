## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Galatians 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 7-8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Galatians 6:1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)</sup> Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

- “Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.” Ye which are spiritual: those keeping the commandments.
- Why did Paul say it? Think about Paul's life. He persecuted the church; he was a murderer. Having repented, he could see where the brethren had fallen and restore them out of his own experience, because he knew what it was to be in that situation.
- We all come in sick. Sometimes leaders, brothers and in some cases sisters expect of people what they themselves have not been able to do or have not done; they expect people to be them. That doesn't mean letting people run over you, or letting sin just go. But some brothers in positions are rough with the people and deal with them harshly, and they themselves are not right; in some cases they are stealing, and they show no consideration or mercy toward another brother. The roughness of brothers toward one another, and of some of the senior sisters toward one another: no.
- Never give up, never give in. In the worst difficulty it is not a thought of giving up, because these trials must go on so that we learn from them: learn to be good leaders, good fathers and good brothers.

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 5:22-23](/bible/galatians/5#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/galatians/5#v22)</sup> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/galatians/5#v23)</sup> Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.

    “But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith, Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.” Read before Galatians 6:1 to connect the dot: the brother who fell into sin, you meet him with the fruits of the Spirit, to restore his spirit and build him back.
  - **[2 Esdras 16:76-77](/bible/2-esdras/16#v76)**
    > <sup>[76](/bible/2-esdras/16#v76)</sup> And the guide of them who keep my commandments and precepts, saith the Lord God: let not your sins weigh you down, and let not your iniquities lift up themselves.
    >
    > <sup>[77](/bible/2-esdras/16#v77)</sup> Woe be unto them that are bound with their sins, and covered with their iniquities, like as a field is covered over with bushes, and the path thereof covered with thorns, that no man may travel through!

    This message is for the brothers who want to give up, who sit all the way back in the room, and for the one whose sin is wearing him down. God is “the guide of them who keep my commandments and precepts, saith the Lord God: let not your sins weigh you down.” That is the part every brother and every sister must understand.
  - **[Mark 4:13-17](/bible/mark/4#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/mark/4#v13)</sup> And he said unto them, Know ye not this parable? and how then will ye know all parables?
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/mark/4#v14)</sup> The sower soweth the word.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/mark/4#v15)</sup> And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown; but when they have heard, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/mark/4#v16)</sup> And these are they likewise which are sown on stony ground; who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with gladness;
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/mark/4#v17)</sup> And have no root in themselves, and so endure but for a time: afterward, when affliction or persecution ariseth for the word’s sake, immediately they are offended.

    “Know ye not this parable? and how then will ye know all parables?” “The sower soweth the word.” By the way side, “when they have heard, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.” That is why, when you are in the street, Satan is always alert: he jumps in these people while you are teaching them and gets them away from you. It shows that what you teach your people is valid: law, statutes and commandments, redemption, change.

**[Galatians 6:2-3](/bible/galatians/6#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/galatians/6#v2)</sup> Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/galatians/6#v3)</sup> For if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.

- “Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ. For if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.” Some brothers think themselves to be something because they have a rank. God said you ain't nothing.
- As a leader you have to care for the people. Bear their burdens; be considerate of their plight, of what they are going through. Know how to talk. Some people you have to cut; some brothers are evil, and the scriptures say that some you save with fear and on some have compassion, as we know from Jude. But this is about brothers who are harsh, with roughness in their speech toward another brother. We came in sick. Imagine all the hang-ups we have that nobody knows about; if God decided to deal with us, none of us would make it. Care about the plight of your people. Some brothers sin and are really remorseful about it: restore them in the spirit of meekness.
- “Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.” Why say that? Because you might find yourself doing the same thing that brother did. Be very careful.
- “For if a man think himself to be something, when he is nothing, he deceiveth himself.” Some of us think we are something in this truth. That is the way you have to think of yourself: you are nothing, because you can be taken out at any time. It is by the grace of God that you stand. Always acknowledge that.

**[Galatians 6:4](/bible/galatians/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/6#v4)</sup> But let every man prove his own work, and then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.

- “But let every man prove his own work”: we have to prove our own work so we don't deceive ourselves. “And then shall he have rejoicing in himself alone, and not in another.”
- When you see a brother or sister knocked down in this truth, your job is to fast and pray, not to applaud. A brother applauded when a brother was knocked down, then claimed he was joking. There is no joking in that. Examine your spirit: why would that thought pop up? A thought like that is not the right thought, and now it has manifested in your mouth.
- The Bishop said it: we might be the ones in front of you, teaching you the Bible, uplifting you, there for you, trying to stay in the spirit, but we still need the prayer and the fasting. Praying and fasting for your leadership is the key of love and appreciation, because sometimes we go through things like you go through things. What will not be accepted is applauding when a brother goes down. There are a lot of fake brothers and fake sisters too. Be mindful how you deal with the little ones, so that when it is your turn, it is the same.
- A brother once remembered that when he was down, the Deacon did not hold it against him but reached out to him around the leadership: sit over here, sit over there. Brothers who do that are applauded. Don't kick a brother when he is knocked down. Don't look at the brother as if he is gone. He is still here, fighting the same battle you are fighting. Your sin might not be revealed as quickly as his. Don't be a fool: be a brother's keeper wherever you are.

**[Galatians 6:7](/bible/galatians/6#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/galatians/6#v7)</sup> Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.

- “Be not deceived; God is not mocked: for whatsoever a man soweth, that shall he also reap.” When you put in the sin, the consequences are going to happen, period.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 6:23](/bible/romans/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/6#v23)</sup> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

    “For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.” What you put in, you are going to get. You are going to receive a reward.
  - **[Deuteronomy 30:19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)</sup> I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live:

    “I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live”. Life and death are both set before you; what you sow is your choice.

**[Galatians 6:8](/bible/galatians/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/galatians/6#v8)</sup> For he that soweth to his flesh shall of the flesh reap corruption; but he that soweth to the Spirit shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.

- “For he that soweth to his flesh shall of the flesh reap corruption”: sow that sin to your flesh and you will be judged by it. “but he that soweth to the Spirit shall of the Spirit reap life everlasting.” When you sow yourself in the scriptures, the Lord gives blessings, favor, mercy and abundance.

  Precepts:
  - **[Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

    “This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night”. We should meditate on the scriptures day and night, “that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous”. When we put our mind on the Lord, he makes our way prosperous and gives us the increase we desire, spiritually, emotionally, in all things. That is sowing to the Spirit.
  - **[Joshua 24:15](/bible/joshua/24#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/joshua/24#v15)</sup> And if it seem evil unto you to serve the Lord, choose you this day whom ye will serve; whether the gods which your fathers served that were on the other side of the flood, or the gods of the Amorites, in whose land ye dwell: but as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.

    “And if it seem evil unto you to serve the Lord, choose you this day whom ye will serve”. You cannot serve two masters. You cannot serve the flesh and God at the same time; you have to choose, and there is no in between. That is why he said he would rather you be cold or hot: if you are lukewarm, playing games in the middle ground, he will spew you out of his mouth.
