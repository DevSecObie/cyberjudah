## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Galatians 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 7-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Galatians 5:1](/bible/galatians/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/5#v1)</sup> Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free, and be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.

- “Stand fast therefore in the liberty wherewith Christ hath made us free”: Christ freed us from animal sacrifice. If we had stayed under that we would all be destroyed, because once the temple was destroyed and the priests scattered, how could we atone? You cannot atone for an idolatrous people scattered all over the world. So Christ had to die.
- “And be not entangled again with the yoke of bondage.” The yoke of bondage is sacrifice, imposed on us until the time of reformation.

**[Galatians 5:2](/bible/galatians/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/galatians/5#v2)</sup> Behold, I Paul say unto you, that if ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing.

- “If ye be circumcised, Christ shall profit you nothing”: circumcised after the manner of Moses, with the yoke of bondage again. The Pharisees were in there bewitching them, teaching that you must be circumcised and sacrifice an animal to be saved, the hell with Christ. If they are circumcised and believe in the animals, Christ don’t mean nothing to them.

**[Galatians 5:3](/bible/galatians/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/galatians/5#v3)</sup> For I testify again to every man that is circumcised, that he is a debtor to do the whole law.

- “For I testify again to every man that is circumcised”, after the manner of Moses, “that he is a debtor to do the whole law.” He is indebted to do the whole law. Once that temple was destroyed and the priests scattered, you are done. Go ahead and deal with that if you want to.

**[Galatians 5:4](/bible/galatians/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/5#v4)</sup> Christ is become of no effect unto you, whosoever of you are justified by the law; ye are fallen from grace.

- “Christ is become of no effect unto you”, because Christ’s sacrifice does away with the animal sacrifice. “Whosoever of you are justified by the law”: animal sacrifice, coupled with circumcision after the manner of Moses, which goes into sacrifice. “Ye are fallen from grace”: if you rely on animals to atone for your sins, you have fallen from the grace Christ got you by dying on the cross. You are rejecting Christ’s sacrifice, being bewitched and simple, running right back to the animals, and you will get yourselves condemned. That is what Paul is saying.
- Christians take this verse with Galatians 2:16, 3:2, 3:5 and 3:10-11 and say: see, no commandments, no Sabbath, the hell with that, Christ died for us, we ain’t got to do nothing, just believe. But the law here is sacrifice.

**[Galatians 5:7-8](/bible/galatians/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/galatians/5#v7)</sup> Ye did run well; who did hinder you that ye should not obey the truth?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/galatians/5#v8)</sup> This persuasion cometh not of him that calleth you.

- “Ye did run well; who did hinder you that ye should not obey the truth?” “This persuasion cometh not of him that calleth you.” In First John the seduction came through teaching; here Paul says some will hinder you from following Christ through persuasion. It is all saying the same thing. When someone draws you away from Christ, to another spirit or another gospel, that persuasion does not come from Christ.
- Make sure you are in the spirit, because Satan plays the long game. He plants a seed now and does not revisit it until a year later. You have a small issue with another member of the body; a year later another issue arises, and Satan tells you to put the two together into one claim against that person. If that person apologized a year ago, should it even come back up? Who is bringing it back up? Satan.

Wherefore I beseech you that ye would confirm your love toward him. For to this end also did I write, that I might know the proof of you, whether ye be obedient in all things.” Paul wants to know whether the church of Corinth is obedient in all things.

**[Galatians 5:9](/bible/galatians/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/galatians/5#v9)</sup> A little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.

- “A little leaven leaveneth the whole lump.” A little leaven in the congregation can leaven the whole congregation, fill it with sin.

**[Galatians 5:10](/bible/galatians/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/galatians/5#v10)</sup> I have confidence in you through the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded: but he that troubleth you shall bear his judgment, whosoever he be.

- “I have confidence in you through the Lord, that ye will be none otherwise minded”. Paul’s encouragement: I have no doubt you will remain in the spirit, even though they are trying to persuade you. “but he that troubleth you shall bear his judgment, whosoever he be.” Those troubling them were the scribes, Pharisees, Sadducees and chief priests whom Christ said to beware of, coming in as in Acts 15 and drawing men and women back to animal sacrifice. Going back to animal sacrifice means rejecting Jesus the Christ.

**[Galatians 5:11-12](/bible/galatians/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/galatians/5#v11)</sup> And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? then is the offence of the cross ceased.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/galatians/5#v12)</sup> I would they were even cut off which trouble you.

- “And I, brethren, if I yet preach circumcision, why do I yet suffer persecution? then is the offence of the cross ceased. I would they were even cut off which trouble you.” Paul says nothing different from what Christ said, that it were better they had never been born, and what Ezra said, that they were born in vain. They will have destruction because they are trying to hinder the gospel, trying to hinder the spirit of Christ filling the earth.

**[Galatians 5:13-14](/bible/galatians/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/galatians/5#v13)</sup> For, brethren, ye have been called unto liberty; only use not liberty for an occasion to the flesh, but by love serve one another.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/galatians/5#v14)</sup> For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this; Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

- “For, brethren, ye have been called unto liberty; only use not liberty for an occasion to the flesh, but by love serve one another. For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this; Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.” Paul is quoting Leviticus 19. But loving thy neighbour goes into not holding grudges and into rebuking your neighbour: those two verses are wrapped up in it. This is where Christians omit the totality of what it is talking about. The New Testament is a summarized version of the Old Testament, not expounded as much as some passages in the Old, so for better understanding you have to go to the Old Testament.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 19:17-18](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

    “Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.” Everybody understands that part: don’t hate your brother, don’t hate your neighbour. Yeah, I don’t hate anybody. But keep reading.

**[Galatians 5:15](/bible/galatians/5#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/galatians/5#v15)</sup> But if ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one of another.

- “But if ye bite and devour one another, take heed that ye be not consumed one of another.” Beware of dogs: dogs bite and devour one another. If we follow the same methods as the Pharisees, being underhanded, deceitful, slanderers, murderous, grudgeful, we are no different from those dogs.

**[Galatians 5:16](/bible/galatians/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/5#v16)</sup> This I say then, Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.

- “Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh”: walking in the spirit means walking in God’s laws. That’s what walk in the spirit means.
- Walk in the spirit and stay in the spirit. “Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.” Every one of us has some type of lust. Your lust might be money; the brother next to you, women; another, another man; the sister next to you, another woman; your lust might be children, or your job. If you stay in the spirit, you shall not fulfil these, because all of these are the lust of the flesh.
- “Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh.” Walking in the spirit is not moonwalking or walking on the air. It means to put on Christ, and not to make provision for the sin, not to leave any doors open where you could fall back into it.
- You have to sit down, think about it and talk to yourself: I am dealing with this, so I need to set things up so I do not fall into it. No matter how many scriptures are brought out, some will feel it tonight and say, I have to stop, this is messed up, and still go watch porn and jerk off this week.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 13:14](/bible/romans/13#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/romans/13#v14)</sup> But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.

    “But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.” This explains what it means to walk in the Spirit. Put on the Lord Jesus Christ: that is the commandments and your faith. To make provision for the flesh is to not walk in the spirit.
  - **[Romans 7:14](/bible/romans/7#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/romans/7#v14)</sup> For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.

    “For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.” This is the spirit you must be in so that you do not do amiss: the law is spiritual. We are carnal, sold under sin.
  - **[John 6:63](/bible/john/6#v63)**
    > <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

    “It is the spirit that quickeneth”: only the spirit can change you as a man. “The flesh profiteth nothing.”

**[Galatians 5:17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)</sup> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.

- “For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary”, meaning opposite, “the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.” There is a war, brothers and sisters, within us. Your inner man, your inner woman, the inner Christ within you wants to keep the commandments, but your flesh says no: hold on, wait a minute, get your groove on. Look at sister so-and-so, she got it going on, help her out, do this for her. But she’s married. Back in the world you did it, you can still do it; look at her smiling at you.
- A true story: a brother rode with me to the Men’s Conference, kept checking his phone, hiding it, went to the bathroom. Then another brother at home looks at his wife’s phone, and she has pictures of the hotel room and the bathroom: he was taking pictures of his tallywacker and sending them to the brother’s wife. You can’t make this stuff up. And there’s many other stories. Some brothers come in this truth and they don’t change; they don’t change.
- “For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh.” This is war, spiritual war: one against another, positive against negative, righteous against evil, light against darkness. “These are contrary the one to the other”: they are not the same, and we have both. The question is which one is going to win. This war is not going to stop until Christ comes back.
- Whatever you went through in the past made you the man you are today. Do not try to change the past; the Most High put it there for a reason. The past should inspire you to do greater things and keep you going, because you would not want to go back to it. Paul says look ahead; there are greater things ahead for us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 9:15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)</sup> For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.

    Here’s the precept: “For the corruptible body presseth down the soul”. What’s another word for the corruptible body, brothers? Flesh; the one we read in Corinthians, terrestrial. Your soul is the real you, and this body is made to press your soul down.
  - **[Matthew 26:41](/bible/matthew/26#v41)**

    These are other words for Galatians 5:17: “Watch and pray, that ye enter not into temptation: the spirit indeed is willing, but the flesh is weak.” We must embrace that. You know when you are hungry; some are so used to food in their mouth it is not even hunger. Carry that over to the scriptures, and you will go from level one to level five.
  - **[1 Peter 2:11](/bible/1-peter/2#v11)**

    “Dearly beloved, I beseech you as strangers and pilgrims, abstain from fleshly lusts, which war against the soul.” Satan tells you porn is not that bad: you are not really having sex with someone, not going out and committing adultery, it is just something you are watching. But fleshly lusts war against the soul.

**[Galatians 5:18](/bible/galatians/5#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/galatians/5#v18)</sup> But if ye be led of the Spirit, ye are not under the law.

- “But if ye be led of the Spirit, ye are not under the law”: meaning the law of sacrifice, the old covenant law of sacrifice. Write that down. It’s not talking about the law that says thou shall not steal; you’re still under that.
- “But if ye be led of the Spirit”: led by the spirit and the word of the Most High, “ye are not under the law.” That is speaking of the sacrificial law.

**[Galatians 5:19-21](/bible/galatians/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/galatians/5#v20)</sup> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/5#v21)</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

- “Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery”: that’s the first. The law says thou shalt not commit adultery, and that lets you know we’re still under that law. “Fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness, Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies, Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like”.
- “Of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.” That bottom part proves that we’re still under God’s laws. If you’re doing adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness, idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies, envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and any other thing I forgot to mention, you’re not getting the kingdom, which means the law is in full effect.
- Of all the works of the flesh, one in particular: “emulations”. Many times betrayers betray because they have a spirit of emulation. It is a sad existence to live your life looking at another person’s life and desiring to be them, secretly hating them because you want what they have. It is one thing to covet the best gifts, to be a better teacher. But to secretly hate a sister because she has nice hair or a nice ring: that spirit of emulation has destroyed many people. It is a cunning demon. You are a secret admirer of somebody, and you plot and you scheme.
- “They which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.” That was Eve. Eve wanted to be like Adam; she wanted to be a god, to emulate the man and have dominion. That is not how God set women up to be, and because of that spirit of emulation death was issued into the earth.

**[Galatians 5:22-23](/bible/galatians/5#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/galatians/5#v22)</sup> But the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, longsuffering, gentleness, goodness, faith,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/galatians/5#v23)</sup> Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.

- “But the fruit of the Spirit is love”: love is the first one, and love is keeping God’s laws, his commandments. “Joy, peace, longsuffering”: I was watching Captain Horeb’s class this morning, and he was talking about people that are spiritual vampires. They always depressed, they always complain, they complain, they complain; they’ll suck the life out of you. Stay away from people like that. “Gentleness, goodness, faith, Meekness, temperance: against such there is no law.”
- Meaning there’s no law you can pull on a brother or sister that’s fulfilling the fruit of the spirit. They’re keeping the commandments: that’s the love they got. They got joy, they got peace in their life, they are long-suffering, they are gentle, they have goodness, they have faith in Christ, meekness and temperance. There’s no law you can pull to condemn them or show them that they’re wrong.
- This is the fruit of the branch that bears fruit in John 15:2. “But the fruit of the Spirit is love”: not the way the Christian woman and Christian man say, ‘I just love, love.’ The love the Bible is talking about is keeping the commandments (2 John 1:6), and that is the first fruit of the Spirit. These Christians are not keeping the commandments; they’ll associate with everything else in there, but not keep the first one, love, which is the keeping of God’s commandments.
- “Joy”: some of y’all are keeping the commandments, but you don’t have no joy in this truth. You’re miserable, ‘oh gosh, I gotta keep’ it, because you hate God’s commandments (Amos 8:5).

  Precepts:
  - **[2 John 1:6](/bible/2-john/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-john/1#v6)</sup> And this is love, that we walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.

    “And this is love, that we walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.” We’re not without understanding the biblical context of the word love. The love the Bible is talking about is keeping the commandments, and that is the first fruit of the Spirit. You can’t knock that out.
  - **[Amos 8:5](/bible/amos/8#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/amos/8#v5)</sup> Saying, When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell corn? and the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat, making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit?

    You have no joy, you’re miserable, because you hate God’s commandments: “Saying, When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell corn?” When will the new moon be gone, I can’t stand it; I want to sell, I want to do work, I want to do business. “And the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat”: when will that sabbath be gone, I can’t stand that sabbath day. “Making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit”.
  - **[Revelation 14:4](/bible/revelation/14#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/14#v4)</sup> These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb.

    An example of meekness: “These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth.” They follow Christ wherever he goes. Whatever Christ says do, they do. That is being meek, humble to the word.

**[Galatians 5:24](/bible/galatians/5#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/galatians/5#v24)</sup> And they that are Christ’s have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts.

- “And they that are Christ’s have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts.” If you’re walking in the spirit, keeping the commandments, you have crucified the flesh, because the flesh is filled with adultery, lies, fornication, stealing, uncleanness, witchcraft, hatred.
- “And they that are Christ’s have crucified the flesh with the affections and lusts.”

**[Galatians 5:25](/bible/galatians/5#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/galatians/5#v25)</sup> If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.

- “If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.” If you live in the spirit, also walk in the spirit.
- “If we live in the Spirit, let us also walk in the Spirit.” If you stay in the spirit, as the Bishop always says, you must also walk in it. Stay in the spirit, brothers and sisters. You must live what you teach, and be that example in this dark world, this filthy, nasty world called America.
