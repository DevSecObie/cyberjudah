## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Galatians 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-11, 16, 18, 21-31, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Galatians 4:1-3](/bible/galatians/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/4#v1)</sup> Now I say, That the heir, as long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/galatians/4#v2)</sup> But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/galatians/4#v3)</sup> Even so we, when we were children, were in bondage under the elements of the world:

- “But is under tutors and governors”. “Even so we, when we were children, were in bondage under the elements of the world”: when you thought as a child you were in bondage.
- Remember the context. The schoolmaster is sacrifice, the works of the law found in Galatians 3:2, 3:5, 3:10-11 and 2:16. If your notes are trash, you'll be confused for a very long time.
- “Now I say, That the heir”: the heir here is the same as the heirs according to the promise in Galatians 3:29. “As long as he is a child, differeth nothing from a servant, though he be lord of all”. Christ was an heir and a king, and he was a servant. A king has to serve the people. The prince, as long as he is a child, differs nothing from a servant.
- “But is under tutors and governors until the time appointed of the father.” A child can't reign right away. He has to be under tutors and governors, mentored, with advisors, to groom him for kingship, until he is grown and ready to sit on the throne. The tutors and governors go back to the schoolmaster of chapter 3. Under Moses we were children, not ready to sit on that throne yet, and the sacrificial law was grooming us for kingship, for Christ.

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 2:4](/bible/galatians/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/2#v4)</sup> And that because of false brethren unawares brought in, who came in privily to spy out our liberty which we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into bondage:

    Paul said it earlier: “false brethren unawares brought in, who came in privily to spy out our liberty which we have in Christ Jesus, that they might bring us into bondage”. Unawares brought in: people were coming in to learn but not really to learn. They were infiltrating. They were brought in and no one knew they were not about the truth, so they came in privily.

**[Galatians 4:4-5](/bible/galatians/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/4#v4)</sup> But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/galatians/4#v5)</sup> To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.

- “Made of a woman, made under the law”: that’s Leviticus 12:1, I believe; made under the law meaning sex. “To redeem them that were under the law”: the Israelites, because we were the ones under the law. “That we might receive the adoption of sons.”
- “But when the fulness of the time was come”: back to verse 2, the time appointed of the father. Paul repeats himself. In Paul's letters the answer is usually above the verse, within the verse or under the verse, or it is given more clearly somewhere else, because the same issue was constant throughout the churches, so he addresses it in one church more clearly and in more detail than in another. Those can be precepted together, the letters to the Corinthians with the letters to the Romans, like Romans 14 with 1 Corinthians 8.
- “God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law”.
- “To redeem them that were under the law”. Christ was born of a woman to redeem them that were under the law. “That we might receive the adoption of sons”: the same we of chapter 3. The adoption pertains to the Israelites.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 9:15](/bible/hebrews/9#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hebrews/9#v15)</sup> And for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance.

    This is the nail in the coffin. “And for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament”: Christ is the mediator of the New Testament, not the old. “That by means of death”, by dying for us, hung on the cross, “for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament”. Redemption is the redeeming, the state of redeeming: Christ died for the redeeming of the transgressions that were under the first testament. Who was under the first testament? Israel. So them that were under the law, whom Christ was sent to redeem, are Israel.

**[Galatians 4:6-7](/bible/galatians/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/galatians/4#v6)</sup> And because ye are sons, God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/galatians/4#v7)</sup> Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son; and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.

- “And because ye are sons”, the heirs of the promise, “God hath sent forth the Spirit of his Son into your hearts, crying, Abba, Father. Wherefore thou art no more a servant, but a son”: when Christ died, we became sons of the Father, “and if a son, then an heir of God through Christ.”

**[Galatians 4:8](/bible/galatians/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/galatians/4#v8)</sup> Howbeit then, when ye knew not God, ye did service unto them which by nature are no gods.

- “Ye did service unto them which by nature are no gods”: that’s going into sacrifice.
- “When ye knew not God”, when we sinned against God, “ye did service unto them which by nature are no gods”: we were idolaters.

**[Galatians 4:9](/bible/galatians/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/galatians/4#v9)</sup> But now, after that ye have known God, or rather are known of God, how turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements, whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?

- “How turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements”: you found out about Christ, and now you’re turned again to the weak and beggarly elements. That’s why chapter 3 verse 1 says, O foolish Galatians, who hath bewitched you; that’s the spirit floating around in the Israelite nation, people trying to trick you back into the old covenant. “Whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?”: bondage under the law of sacrifice, which we could not keep perfect at all, nor can we keep it today, because the temple’s not around. You feel like an idiot even saying that, but we’re talking to novices.
- “But now, after that ye have known God”: now we know the true God, not a pagan one, “or rather are known of God”, because Christ died for us. “How turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements”: verse 3 says the elements of the world, and here they are weak and beggarly elements. “Whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?” The same bondage as verse 3, and as chapter 2, where they desired to bring us back into bondage.
- It is said that the works of the law never say the law of sacrifice. But the scripture calls the works of the law bondage, and calls them weak and beggarly elements. The weak and beggarly elements, weak and unprofitable, are the sacrificing of animals.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 15:14](/bible/matthew/15#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/15#v14)</sup> Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch.

    All the schools that come out of that branch out of One West teach the New Covenant under Christ, so where do we get a sect of brothers who say yahawashi but say we’re under the old covenant? “Let them alone: they be blind leaders of the blind. And if the blind lead the blind, both shall fall into the ditch”: maybe their blind leaders led them down that path. So we’re going to reach our hands into the pit and help pull out our blind brothers.
  - **[Isaiah 42:16](/bible/isaiah/42#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/42#v16)</sup> And I will bring the blind by a way that they knew not; I will lead them in paths that they have not known: I will make darkness light before them, and crooked things straight. These things will I do unto them, and not forsake them.

    “And I will bring the blind by a way that they knew not”. “I will make darkness light before them, and crooked things straight”: hopefully that light is made straight in the dark path our brothers are walking down, and Christ is illuminated, and they come to understand Christ and the New Covenant and remove the veil from their face.
  - **[Romans 8:3](/bible/romans/8#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/romans/8#v3)</sup> For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh:

    What is the weak and beggarly element? “For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh”. The law was weak. It was weak through the flesh: through our sin, through us. And what it could not do was make the conscience perfect, as Hebrews 9:9 shows.

**[Galatians 4:10-11](/bible/galatians/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/galatians/4#v10)</sup> Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/galatians/4#v11)</sup> I am afraid of you, lest I have bestowed upon you labour in vain.

- “Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years”: the sacrifices pertaining to days, months, times and years. “I am afraid of you”: you guys are bugged the hell out, going back to the old covenant. “Lest I have bestowed upon you labour in vain.”
- “Ye observe days, and months, and times, and years.” Paul gives an example of how they had turned back: they were back observing the weak and beggarly elements, the sacrifices of the days, the months, the times and the years.

  Precepts:
  - **[Colossians 2:16-17](/bible/colossians/2#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/colossians/2#v16)</sup> Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink, or in respect of an holyday, or of the new moon, or of the sabbath days:
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/colossians/2#v17)</sup> Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ.

    “Let no man therefore judge you in meat, or in drink”: meat offerings and drink offerings, the meats and drinks of Hebrews 9:10. “Or in respect of an holyday”: days. “Or of the new moon”: months. “Or of the sabbath days”: times. “Which are a shadow of things to come; but the body is of Christ.” Christ is the body. He is the sacrifice of all those things, because on each of those days a sacrifice had to be made.
  - **[Ezekiel 45:17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)</sup> And it shall be the prince’s part to give burnt offerings, and meat offerings, and drink offerings, in the feasts, and in the new moons, and in the sabbaths, in all solemnities of the house of Israel: he shall prepare the sin offering, and the meat offering, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make reconciliation for the house of Israel.

    Ezekiel 45:17 answers to Colossians 2:16 and Galatians 4:10. “It shall be the prince’s part to give burnt offerings, and meat offerings, and drink offerings, in the feasts”, times and years, “and in the new moons”, months, “and in the sabbaths”, days, “in all solemnities of the house of Israel”, years: “he shall prepare the sin offering, and the meat offering, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make reconciliation for the house of Israel.”

**[Galatians 4:16](/bible/galatians/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/4#v16)</sup> Am I therefore become your enemy, because I tell you the truth?

- We don’t like looking at ourselves in the mirror. Michael Jackson had a song called Man in the Mirror. “Am I therefore become your enemy, because I tell you the truth?” That’s the question you got to ask yourself.
- We’re on the street telling the revilers the truth and they get so angry, like we just beat their mama up. We’re just reading a book, and that infuriates you so much you want to lay hands on us? And some in here, when your sin is read out, get angry as if we did something personal against you, though we don’t even know what you’re guilty of.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 109:1-4](/bible/psalms/109#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/109#v1)</sup> Hold not thy peace, O God of my praise;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/109#v2)</sup> For the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of the deceitful are opened against me: they have spoken against me with a lying tongue.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/109#v3)</sup> They compassed me about also with words of hatred; and fought against me without a cause.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/109#v4)</sup> For my love they are my adversaries: but I give myself unto prayer.

    “For my love they are my adversaries”: see what the Lord says. From the top: “Hold not thy peace, O God of my praise; For the mouth of the wicked and the mouth of the deceitful are opened against me: they have spoken against me with a lying tongue. They compassed me about also with words of hatred”. This is the black and brown community. “And fought against me without a cause.”
  - **[Proverbs 9:8](/bible/proverbs/9#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/9#v8)</sup> Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.

    “Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee.” A scorner doesn’t want to hear the scriptures. They don’t want to hear the solutions we have according to the Bible to fix their lives. They want to hear they can still live in debauchery, that the black woman is no damn good, that they can sell drugs, beat their kids when they want or not even take care of them. That’s a destroyed mind; our people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.

**[Galatians 4:18](/bible/galatians/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/galatians/4#v18)</sup> But it is good to be zealously affected always in a good thing, and not only when I am present with you.

- Malcolm X believed in the truth in the Bible but not the lies the white man put in it, and he preached that the so-called Negroes are Lazarus, the dry bones in the valley, the prodigal son, the lost sheep. He was quoting the Bible. He knew something, but he could not fully piece it together. The Lord used him; he put his life on the line, and his mission was to make it easier for the Israelites later.
- “But it is good to be zealously affected always in a good thing.” We saw the spirit he had on him: he had zeal. It is always good to be zealous in a good thing.

**[Galatians 4:21](/bible/galatians/4#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/4#v21)</sup> Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law, do ye not hear the law?

- “Tell me, ye that desire to be under the law”: under the law is the sacrifice, the weak and beggarly elements. “Do ye not hear the law?” Do you not hear what the law says?

**[Galatians 4:22-23](/bible/galatians/4#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/galatians/4#v22)</sup> For it is written, that Abraham had two sons, the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/galatians/4#v23)</sup> But he who was of the bondwoman was born after the flesh; but he of the freewoman was by promise.

- “Abraham had two sons, the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman.” The bondmaid is Hagar and the freewoman is Sarah. “He who was of the bondwoman was born after the flesh”: that is Ishmael. “He of the freewoman was by promise”: Sarah was promised to bring forth a child in her old age, the child of promise. And the promises pertain to the Israelites.

**[Galatians 4:24-25](/bible/galatians/4#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/galatians/4#v24)</sup> Which things are an allegory: for these are the two covenants; the one from the mount Sinai, which gendereth to bondage, which is Agar.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/galatians/4#v25)</sup> For this Agar is mount Sinai in Arabia, and answereth to Jerusalem which now is, and is in bondage with her children.

- “These are the two covenants”: the two women stand for two covenants, one for bondage and one for freedom. Hagar is the old covenant; Sarah is the new. “The one from the mount Sinai”, the one that came from Moses, “which gendereth to bondage”: we were bound to offering up animals, and then Christ died, and his death liberated us from that and gave us liberty. Agar is that bondage.
- “And answereth to Jerusalem which now is”: Jerusalem maintained the old covenant law, the testament law, “and is in bondage with her children”, in bondage under the Mosaic law, temple law.

**[Galatians 4:26](/bible/galatians/4#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/galatians/4#v26)</sup> But Jerusalem which is above is free, which is the mother of us all.

- “But Jerusalem which is above is free”, because Christ died and we are free from that, “which is the mother of us all.”

**[Galatians 4:27](/bible/galatians/4#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/galatians/4#v27)</sup> For it is written, Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not; break forth and cry, thou that travailest not: for the desolate hath many more children than she which hath an husband.

- “Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not”: this goes to Sarah.

**[Galatians 4:28](/bible/galatians/4#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/galatians/4#v28)</sup> Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise.

- “Now we, brethren, as Isaac was, are the children of promise.” This makes it clear who the Galatians are. Paul calls them brethren, his brothers, and says we are the children of promise as Isaac was.

**[Galatians 4:29](/bible/galatians/4#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/galatians/4#v29)</sup> But as then he that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit, even so it is now.

- “He that was born after the flesh persecuted him that was born after the Spirit”: Ishmael mocked Isaac our forefather. “Even so it is now”: the Pharisees mocked the followers of Christ, one toward bondage, one toward freedom.

**[Galatians 4:30](/bible/galatians/4#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/galatians/4#v30)</sup> Nevertheless what saith the scripture? Cast out the bondwoman and her son: for the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the freewoman.

- “Nevertheless what saith the scripture?” Despite the persecution: “Cast out the bondwoman and her son”. Get the old covenant out of here, and her son, those who maintain that sacrificial law. “For the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with the son of the freewoman.” We are the sons of the free woman.
- It is twofold. The old covenant is done away with and the new is in. And the son of the bondwoman, the children of the flesh, are not getting the kingdom along with us either. They are not the heir; we are the heirs, the children of promise. No fleshly heathens are getting our inheritance.

**[Galatians 4:31](/bible/galatians/4#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/galatians/4#v31)</sup> So then, brethren, we are not children of the bondwoman, but of the free.

- “So then, brethren, we are not children of the bondwoman, but of the free”: the children of promise.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 1:1-2](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)</sup> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-peter/1#v2)</sup> Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied.

    Who was Paul writing to in Galatians, for the Christians still confused, secretly spying out the liberty we have in Christ? “Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia”. Galatia. The strangers scattered throughout Galatia are the “Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ”: the death of Christ.
  - **[1 Peter 2:9-10](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)</sup> But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-peter/2#v10)</sup> Which in time past were not a people, but are now the people of God: which had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.

    To these same strangers: “But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people”. The strangers of Galatia are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, a holy nation. “Which in time past were not a people, but are now the people of God: which had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.”
