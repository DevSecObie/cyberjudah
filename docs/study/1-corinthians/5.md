## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 5:1](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v1)</sup> It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife.

- “It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you.” Fornication is sexual sins. “That one should have his father’s wife”: this man was banging his father’s wife.
- Under the law of marriage, a woman betrothed is that man’s wife, as Mary was betrothed to Joseph, even if the betrothal lasts five, ten or fifteen years. It may have been a sister betrothed to the brother’s father, and the brother slept with her. A man in his right mind is not going to sleep with his own mother; that was not his mother, but a woman promised to his father. Even so, it was such evil that it was “not so much as named among the Gentiles.”

**[1 Corinthians 5:2](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v2)</sup> And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you.

- “And ye are puffed up, and have not rather mourned, that he that hath done this deed might be taken away from among you.” They were puffed up and did not mourn. They should have put this brother away from among them.

**[1 Corinthians 5:3-5](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v3)</sup> For I verily, as absent in body, but present in spirit, have judged already, as though I were present, concerning him that hath so done this deed,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v4)</sup> In the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, when ye are gathered together, and my spirit, with the power of our Lord Jesus Christ,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v5)</sup> To deliver such an one unto Satan for the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.

- “To deliver such an one unto Satan.” How do you deliver someone to Satan? You kick them out of the school: you can’t be among us anymore. Satan, you can have him; he ain’t right. That is only for those who will not repent and are not trying to change. Paul was pissed when he first heard what the brother did, but when the brother repented, Paul forgave him: if you forgive him, I forgive him too; we can’t be ignorant of Satan’s devices. That is a lesson for us too.
- “For the destruction of the flesh, that the spirit may be saved in the day of the Lord Jesus.” You want to destroy that spirit of adultery. If one brother is allowed to commit adultery with no recompense, everybody starts. Deal with it. Don’t allow fornication to fester.

**[1 Corinthians 5:6](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v6)</sup> Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?

- “Your glorying is not good. Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?” The leaven there was fornication. Allow a little bit of fornication in the schools and it is going to spread. Those in the midst of sin cannot be left among us; you have to change.
- Don’t fear men; fear what God can do to you. There are diseases out there. Some of you brothers, your rod could fall off. Some of these sisters, your vagina could start rotting, smelling like rotten fish. The Deacon’s own little sister died at twenty, HIV positive, and his stepmother died too; he has seen what can happen, and that is why there are things he does not play with. Keep poking here and poking there, and poke the wrong place, and now you are mourning and crying. Some among us right now have the monster. Brothers and sisters, stop playing around, and you young horny sisters too, don’t be stupid. There is a God.
- Paul first wrote that “It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you”: sin was a common thing in Corinth. Then: “Know ye not that a little leaven leaveneth the whole lump?” That leaven is sin. A little bit of sin ruins the entire lump.
- Bitterness is the hidden leaven that must be purged. We are in the midst of Passover, and if it is not purged, bitterness grows into a root that defiles the soul. Many brothers who left the body and came out with crazy videos started with a root of bitterness.

**[1 Corinthians 5:7](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v7)</sup> Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:

- “Purge out therefore the old leaven”: get rid of the sin. Everyone deals with different levels of sin, but it is your job to see what you are dealing with and work on getting rid of it, because after those seven days, if you haven’t gotten rid of it, your soul will be cut off.
- “That ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened.” We are supposed to be unleavened, without sin. Are we without sin? No; whoever says, I’m perfect, is fake and phony as hell. Paul said we die daily: when you make a mistake you go to the Lord, forgive me, have mercy upon me, and you start over, and so you are without sin. Sin is always there in the mind and Satan is always there; it becomes a problem when you act on it.
- “For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us”: he was sacrificed for us, for us to repent of our sins.
- “Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump.” At Passover you have to examine the spirit and purge out the old leaven, the old sin, the old man, so that we may be a new lump, a new creature in Christ, following in his steps.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 2:21](/bible/1-peter/2#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/1-peter/2#v21)</sup> For even hereunto were ye called: because Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that ye should follow his steps:

    “Christ also suffered for us, leaving us an example, that ye should follow his steps.” That goes back to Christ being our Passover, sacrificed for us. He suffered for us and left an example, so as believers in Christ we must follow the steps of Christ.

**[1 Corinthians 5:8](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v8)</sup> Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

- “Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven”: not with the old sins. We are in the feast of unleavened bread and we want to be unleavened. “Neither with the leaven of malice”: malice goes into hatred. “And wickedness.” These things are leaven; we shouldn’t have them in us.
- “But with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.” Ask yourself: am I really sincere in this truth, really trying to do what’s right? That is what the Lord looks for: people that are sincere and really trying to change. When you make a mistake you run to him quick, Lord, forgive me, I’m sorry, I ain’t going to do it again. You are sincere and not fake.
- Some brothers and sisters are not here in sincerity and truth. Learn to pick up on the fake ones: they don’t love their brothers, their sisters or the Lord; they complain, they gossip, they murmur. That is all the people that were killed in the wilderness.
- To be sincere is to believe it: Christ is a black man, and Christ is coming back with vengeance. Don’t lose faith; we are the people of this book. The whole world, the US government and all the governments, is pushing lies, and it is a small remnant pushing the truth. The odds are against us, and that makes it all the sweeter, all the more marvelous, when the Lord does his thing.
