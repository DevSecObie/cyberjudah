## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 13-15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 16:13-14](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v13)</sup> Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v14)</sup> Let all your things be done with charity.

- “Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong. Let all your things be done with charity.” Quit you like men means conduct yourself like a man: keeping the commandments, with maturity, courage, discipline and responsibility. That is how you deal as a man, and that is how you deal with your wife in your household.
- Brothers, we have to fix ourselves. Sisters, if you are a good wife, continue to be a good wife.

**[1 Corinthians 16:15](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v15)</sup> I beseech you, brethren, (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints,)

- Those who leave want to bring division. Why interview somebody who left an organization? To get someone upset and mad to tell their story to everybody. One who left said we study the Bible from sun up to sun down every Sabbath, so it must be a cult. Reading and teaching the Bible is what they call a cult. Nobody put a gun to anybody’s head: you came in the doors, opened your Bible and got the understanding.
- They are addicted to the scriptures: “ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints.” That is what we do here. We are addicted. For those really in the fight it is no grudge to learn the Bible; when you addict yourself to it, you can listen to the word of God all day and night.
- You would not have found out you are an Israelite if it were not for a camp, because the will to put a platform together to teach, educate and nurture our people does not exist with individuals. Everybody that leaves becomes a pastor for two weeks, teaches their own hatred and malice for about two months, and is never heard from again, because the spirit is not there. If what we are doing is evil, leave and build it yourself: in thirteen years nobody has. The spirit is here because we put the commandments and Christ first and foremost.
