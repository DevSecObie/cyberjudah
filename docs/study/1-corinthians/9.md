## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 9 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 14-21, 24-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 9:1](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v1)</sup> Am I not an apostle? am I not free? have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord? are not ye my work in the Lord?

- “Am I not an apostle? am I not free? have I not seen Jesus Christ our Lord?” Paul is an apostle.

**[1 Corinthians 9:14](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v14)</sup> Even so hath the Lord ordained that they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.

- “Even so hath the Lord ordained that they which preach the gospel should live of the gospel.”

**[1 Corinthians 9:15](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v15)</sup> But I have used none of these things: neither have I written these things, that it should be so done unto me: for it were better for me to die, than that any man should make my glorying void.

- “But I have used none of these things: neither have I written these things, that it should be so done unto me: for it were better for me to die, than that any man should make my glorying void.”

**[1 Corinthians 9:16](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v16)</sup> For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of: for necessity is laid upon me; yea, woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!

- “For though I preach the gospel”: all you brothers preach the gospel, and you sisters with children are preaching the gospel to them. “For necessity is laid upon me”: we want to save our lives, we want the coming Jerusalem. “Yea, woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!”
- “For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of: for necessity is laid upon me; yea, woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!”
- “For though I preach the gospel, I have nothing to glory of”: Paul understood he was a servant, sent to serve the people and deliver souls. Paul is saying, I'm a nobody.
- “For necessity is laid upon me; yea, woe is unto me, if I preach not the gospel!” Paul understood his purpose and what he was called for: destruction unto me if I don't teach the gospel, if I don't do what I was sent to do. How many of you can say that? Be honest with yourself. What have you done since you came to IUIC, except show up every Sabbath, open the Bible and sit there? Some of you only pick up the Bible on Saturday, because when we ask you a simple question in the scripture, you cannot answer it.

**[1 Corinthians 9:17](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v17)</sup> For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward: but if against my will, a dispensation of the gospel is committed unto me.

- “For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward”: if you do this willingly, you have a passion for this truth and loyalty to God Almighty, and you have a reward.
- “But if against my will”: somebody always has to yell at me and tell me to do this and that, because I am a hireling. I am not really in this to go out and teach the gospel. You hear how boring I am, the dead spirit on me; I could care less.
- “A dispensation of the gospel is committed unto me”: you get a limited amount of time in this truth, and then God moves you out of the way and brings somebody else. A brother in the class added that it is musical chairs: over the years we have seen whole new congregations come before us: same spirits, different people. Facebook shows you memories from two years ago and you ask, who are these brothers I was standing with? It is all new people, because those dudes fell out.
- “For if I do this thing willingly, I have a reward”. If I teach the gospel willingly, not forced, I have a reward. Nobody forces the brothers to do anything; you have to have the spirit to do this willingly. “But if against my will”, if somebody forced me to do it, “a dispensation of the gospel is committed unto me.” A dispensation is a time period.

**[1 Corinthians 9:18](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v18)</sup> What is my reward then? Verily that, when I preach the gospel, I may make the gospel of Christ without charge, that I abuse not my power in the gospel.

- “What is my reward then? Verily that, when I preach the gospel, I may make the gospel of Christ without charge, that I abuse not my power in the gospel.”
- In the church of Corinth they did not want to help Paul. Paul travelled to many places, by boat, in the sea for weeks at a time, visiting some churches over and over, and somebody had to pay for those trips. The brothers at Corinth would not help him.
- “What is my reward then? Verily that, when I preach the gospel, I may make the gospel of Christ without charge”: Paul was willing to teach the gospel freely. He did not charge.

**[1 Corinthians 9:19](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v19)</sup> For though I be free from all men, yet have I made myself servant unto all, that I might gain the more.

- “For though I be free from all men, yet have I made myself servant unto all, that I might gain the more.” At the end of the day we are servants. Our job is to serve the people.
- “For though I be free from all men, yet have I made myself servant unto all”: Bishop Nathanyel is a servant, the Bishops are servants, and we the deacons are servants. We are Christ's servants, here to serve the people. That is our job and what we were called for: to make sure you are right, to build you up the right way, and to tell you how to be ready for what is coming. “That I might gain the more.”

**[1 Corinthians 9:20-21](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v20)</sup> And unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews; to them that are under the law, as under the law, that I might gain them that are under the law;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v21)</sup> To them that are without law, as without law, (being not without law to God, but under the law to Christ,) that I might gain them that are without law.

- “And unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews; to them that are under the law, as under the law”. Paul made himself weak to win the brothers over. He is not saying he went and committed sin. He is not saying, they invited me to Thanksgiving dinner, so I went to win them over. His job was to gain brothers and sisters over to this gospel, “being not without law to God, but under the law to Christ”.

**[1 Corinthians 9:24](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v24)</sup> Know ye not that they which run in a race run all, but one receiveth the prize? So run, that ye may obtain.

- “Know ye not that they which run in a race run all, but one receiveth the prize?” Again it’s talking about that straight path, the hands that hang down and the feeble knees. You have to realize this is a race; they compare it to a race. The beautiful thing about this walk is that many people can receive a prize: not infinite, one-third. Whatever that final number is, we don’t know, but it’s one-third.
- “So run, that ye may obtain”: run with a purpose. Some of you like to be bystanders, spectators in this, and the scripture teaches us that we got to be involved, that we can’t be idle, that we got to move forward. You don’t move with the urgency like there’s something to obtain in this; we’re cavalier, and this is why we keep hearing these things and this stuff keeps coming up, and it will till Christ returns. But we’re supposed to be rehearsing all those righteous things now. What we do as individuals is multiplied in the body: if a handful of us starts to get it right, then the next handful gets it right, and you’ll start to see the effects of that all over the place. That’s why it’s so critical we stay beholden to what the scriptures say about what goes on in the body, because if not, everybody will be infected, like when the zombie virus is spreading, and then everybody’s caught up in something.

**[1 Corinthians 9:25](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v25)</sup> And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible.

- “And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things”. Temperate is showing moderation or self-restraint. You set the paths, you don’t turn to the right or the left, you keep your mind with all diligence, you move temperately. When you move away from these principles, destruction comes, like the example lately with that fornication spirit. And it’s not just fornication; it’s anything you’re dealing with. You can have all this knowledge, you can even move to the realm of wisdom, and when you let purpose slip, that’s when you fall. Set your focus, keep your mind right, keep your mouth sealed, set your eye and move.
- “Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; but we an incorruptible.” We do it for a crown, and a crown has much significance. The crown is worn where? On your head. There’s a reason he mentions the crown, and it’s not always automatic, oh, the kingdom.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 47:13-21](/bible/sirach/47#v13)**

    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/47#v14)</sup> How wise wast thou in thy youth, and, as a flood, filled with understanding!
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/47#v15)</sup> Thy soul covered the whole earth, and thou filledst it with dark parables.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/47#v16)</sup> Thy name went far unto the islands; and for thy peace thou wast beloved.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/47#v17)</sup> The countries marvelled at thee for thy songs, and proverbs, and parables, and interpretations.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/47#v18)</sup> By the name of the Lord God, which is called the Lord God of Israel, thou didst gather gold as tin, and didst multiply silver as lead.

    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/47#v19)</sup> Thou didst bow thy loins unto women, and by thy body thou wast brought into subjection.

    Here’s the example of letting purpose slip. “Solomon reigned in a peaceable time, and was honoured; for God made all quiet round about him”: the Lord gave him peace during his time so that he could prosper; the scripture says he’ll make even your enemies be at peace with you. Was Solomon not full of wisdom, the wisest man that ever walked the earth? “How wise wast thou in thy youth, and, as a flood, filled with understanding!” “The countries marvelled at thee for thy songs, and proverbs, and parables, and interpretations”: even they went to Solomon for wisdom.

**[1 Corinthians 9:26](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v26)</sup> I therefore so run, not as uncertainly; so fight I, not as one that beateth the air:

- “I therefore so run, not as uncertainly; so fight I, not as one that beateth the air”. In this truth it is like running a race, and your goal is to reach the finish line.
- “I therefore so run, not as uncertainly”: he was not uncertain. He kept his heart, he knew himself, and he knew what he needed to stay focused on. “So fight I, not as one that beateth the air”.
- “So fight I, not as one that beateth the air”: the truth is a fight. You are fighting your own battles, the sins of the world and the sins in yourself.

**[1 Corinthians 9:27](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v27)</sup> But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway.

- “But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection”: mortify your members upon the earth; keep your body under, and bring it into subjection to God's laws. “Lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway.”
- There was a brother named Kenny who cursed out another brother like a dog for watching porn: stop looking at porn, it's the devil. Then he handed the Bishop his phone to show him a Batman trailer, and the last thing he had been looking at popped up: pornography. You don't want to be a hypocrite and be cast away. Get yourself right, and that will help your preaching and your teaching.
- “But I keep under my body”: he fights to keep under his body. That’s why the battle’s here, in the head. He keeps it under because he doesn’t want it to manifest itself in action. He talks about that in detail in Romans 7, and it sounds confusing because he’s going back and forth, but it’s that discussion going on inside, and he makes sure he keeps that thing under control. “And bring it into subjection: lest that by any means, when I have preached to others, I myself should be a castaway.”
- “But I keep under my body, and bring it into subjection”: that is how Paul fights; he has to fight himself all the time. We all have sins in us and a battle within us, so we can’t sit there and say he is condemned while we do the same thing or worse.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 37:19](/bible/sirach/37#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/37#v19)</sup> There is one that is wise and teacheth many, and yet is unprofitable to himself.

    “There is one that is wise and teacheth many, and yet is unprofitable to himself.” Why is he unprofitable to himself? Because he is living his life as a hypocrite. None of us should fall into that. If you find yourself in that situation right now, repent right now and fix it. We have to learn to endure to the end.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 37:19-21](/bible/sirach/37#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/37#v19)</sup> There is one that is wise and teacheth many, and yet is unprofitable to himself.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/37#v20)</sup> There is one that sheweth wisdom in words, and is hated: he shall be destitute of all food.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/37#v21)</sup> For grace is not given him from the Lord; because he is deprived of all wisdom.

    “There is one that is wise and teacheth many, and yet is unprofitable to himself”: that goes with Paul saying, lest when I have preached to others I myself should be a castaway. “There is one that sheweth wisdom in words, and is hated: he shall be destitute of all food. For grace is not given him from the Lord; because he is deprived of all wisdom.” You’re deprived of wisdom because wisdom doesn’t allow you to move into those spaces, to entertain those ways.
