## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-14, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 4:1-2](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v1)</sup> Let a man so account of us, as of the ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v2)</sup> Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.

- “Let a man so account of us, as of the ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God. Moreover it is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.” In a steward, you have to be found faithful.
- “Ministers of Christ, and stewards of the mysteries of God”: that is who we are, declaring the mysteries of God. “It is required in stewards, that a man be found faithful.”

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 5:7](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v7)</sup> Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us:

    “Purge out therefore the old leaven, that ye may be a new lump, as ye are unleavened. For even Christ our passover is sacrificed for us”. If you want to be a minister of Christ and a steward found faithful, you have to purge out the old leaven inside you. You can’t be moving with old leaven in you. Christ died for our sins, so you have to purge it out.

**[1 Corinthians 4:3](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v3)</sup> But with me it is a very small thing that I should be judged of you, or of man’s judgment: yea, I judge not mine own self.

- “With me it is a very small thing that I should be judged of you, or of man’s judgment”. The Corinthians ran their mouths about Paul behind his back, and he said, I don’t care what you think about me. Paul had a past: he killed Christians, hunted us down like a bounty hunter, a madman, but the Lord called him out of that and he became a faithful servant. “Yea, I judge not mine own self”: I can’t say whether I am wicked or righteous.

**[1 Corinthians 4:4](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v4)</sup> For I know nothing by myself; yet am I not hereby justified: but he that judgeth me is the Lord.

- “But he that judgeth me is the Lord.” That is all we say: if you die in that, that is on you.

**[1 Corinthians 4:5](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v5)</sup> Therefore judge nothing before the time, until the Lord come, who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts: and then shall every man have praise of God.

- “Therefore judge nothing before the time”, no one before the time, because what happened to me yesterday will happen to you today, “until the Lord come”. Then we will all know who made it and who didn’t. He “will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts”: in that day it will be known who was doing right, who was doing wrong, and who pretended. “And then shall every man have praise of God”: those who made it will have their praise of God.
- “Who both will bring to light the hidden things of darkness, and will make manifest the counsels of the hearts”: some of you are hiding things, but the Lord will make known the hidden things of the heart.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 7:1-5](/bible/matthew/7#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/7#v1)</sup> Judge not, that ye be not judged.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/7#v2)</sup> For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/7#v3)</sup> And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/7#v4)</sup> Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye; and, behold, a beam is in thine own eye?
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/7#v5)</sup> Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother’s eye.

    “Judge not, that ye be not judged.” Christianity’s favorite scripture. When you say someone blasphemed the Holy Ghost, you can’t make that call; it ain’t your call to make. You are out here blaspheming too, and you are going to the same place you say that person is going: saying he blasphemed the Holy Ghost while you mess with these hoes, endorse gang culture, get drunk at the bars every other week, rob women and rob your congregation to spend their hard-earned money on your clothes and debauchery.
  - **[Luke 6:37](/bible/luke/6#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/luke/6#v37)</sup> Judge not, and ye shall not be judged: condemn not, and ye shall not be condemned: forgive, and ye shall be forgiven:

    Luke explains what judge not means: “Judge not, and ye shall not be judged: condemn not, and ye shall not be condemned”. To judge means to condemn; that is the precept of it. You cannot say someone is two-thirds, or that someone has blasphemed the Holy Ghost. That ain’t your place. Judge not before the time, because you might go to the same place they are going.

**[1 Corinthians 4:6](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v6)</sup> And these things, brethren, I have in a figure transferred to myself and to Apollos for your sakes; that ye might learn in us not to think of men above that which is written, that no one of you be puffed up for one against another.

- “That ye might learn in us not to think of men above that which is written, that no one of you be puffed up for one against another.” Some take these secrets and their understanding and think they are better than each other. It is easy to look at a man who knows the mysteries as if he is so great. We are to be humble with each other; come humble and to learn, and we match your energy, but come with arrogance and you get that work. In this ministry every state has men known for the scriptures, not one man doing every debate. A camp that only lets one man speak is weak, and Paul was against that.

**[1 Corinthians 4:7-8](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v7)</sup> For who maketh thee to differ from another? and what hast thou that thou didst not receive? now if thou didst receive it, why dost thou glory, as if thou hadst not received it?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v8)</sup> Now ye are full, now ye are rich, ye have reigned as kings without us: and I would to God ye did reign, that we also might reign with you.

- “For who maketh thee to differ from another? and what hast thou that thou didst not receive?” “I would to God ye did reign, that we also might reign with you”: when you do good, we do good. These mysteries have been given to all of us, and we are all on the same level. If I don’t know something, I’ll tell you.

**[1 Corinthians 4:9](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v9)</sup> For I think that God hath set forth us the apostles last, as it were appointed to death: for we are made a spectacle unto the world, and to angels, and to men.

- “For I think that God hath set forth us the apostles last, as it were appointed to death: for we are made a spectacle unto the world, and to angels, and to men.” This is the cost: we are a spectacle, appointed unto death. Do you want to die as an apostle with the old leaven still living in you, or be found faithful? Lord, Lord, did I not prophesy in your name? I don’t know you. Why run this whole race and not get it?
- Much is given, much is required. I have given you understanding; where are my benefits back? Are you playing games, treating this Bible as a joke?
- The society we live in shows how destroyed we are as a people: a wife who passed off twins as her husband's for twelve years, a woman with seven children by seven men, a mother who left her young daughters alone for days to fly to Miami. Some of us are stupid, black people are stupid; not all black people, but some of us. And some of you will lose the kingdom for a woman like that. We are destroyed as a people, a broken people; some of us are at the point of no return. And who is going to fix it? The white man and the other nations love stuff like that. T.D. Jakes cannot change it, Creflo Dollar cannot change it. We, the men, are the last hope of our people. But you cannot fix this if your mind is on coochie all the time.
- “For I think that God hath set forth us the apostles last”: that is us, brothers. “As it were appointed to death”: by taking this charge, this job, we are going to die for it, and we have to prepare ourselves to die for it. A real prophet, a real leader, does not choose his destiny; his destiny was picked for him before he was even born. Some of us are destined to die for this, some to be tortured, some to go to jail. This is going to get done with you or without you; you have to choose a side.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 10:33](/bible/hebrews/10#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/hebrews/10#v33)</sup> Partly, whilst ye were made a gazingstock both by reproaches and afflictions; and partly, whilst ye became companions of them that were so used.

    “Partly, whilst ye were made a gazingstock both by reproaches and afflictions”: all of us in this truth are made to be seen, made a gazingstock, the same spectacle unto the world the apostles were. Reproaches are hatred, and afflictions. “And partly, whilst ye became companions of them that were so used.”
  - **[John 10:27](/bible/john/10#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/john/10#v27)</sup> My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me:

    “My sheep hear my voice, and I know them, and they follow me.” We are God’s sheep. Some of us were Muslims and some were Christians, but we heard God’s voice that we are the true Jews, and we follow him.

**[1 Corinthians 4:10-13](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v10)</sup> We are fools for Christ’s sake, but ye are wise in Christ; we are weak, but ye are strong; ye are honourable, but we are despised.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v11)</sup> Even unto this present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no certain dwellingplace;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v12)</sup> And labour, working with our own hands: being reviled, we bless; being persecuted, we suffer it:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v13)</sup> Being defamed, we intreat: we are made as the filth of the world, and are the offscouring of all things unto this day.

- “We are fools for Christ’s sake, but ye are wise in Christ; we are weak, but ye are strong; ye are honourable, but we are despised”: not loved, mocked. “Even unto this present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no certain dwellingplace; and labour, working with our own hands: being reviled”: slandered, lied on, “we bless; being persecuted, we suffer it: being defamed”: spoken ill of. We’re xenophobic, anti-LGBTQ, anti-white, anti-Semitic: we are defamed.
- “We are made as the filth of the world, and are the offscouring of all things unto this day.” Rejected, but the Lord doesn’t reject us. The Lord has our back.
- Have you heard of any Christians being persecuted? Nobody says they can’t celebrate Christmas. If they teach the truth, why is nobody mad about it? “Even unto this present hour we both hunger, and thirst, and are naked, and are buffeted, and have no certain dwellingplace”.
- “Being reviled, we bless; being persecuted, we suffer it: Being defamed, we intreat: we are made as the filth of the world, and are the offscouring of all things unto this day.” To this day we are the offscouring of all things.

**[1 Corinthians 4:14](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/4#v14)</sup> I write not these things to shame you, but as my beloved sons I warn you.

- “I write not these things to shame you, but as my beloved sons I warn you.” Paul said, I’m warning you, and that’s what we’re doing today. Some of you still got one foot in Christianity; you’re about to celebrate Thanksgiving next week and make up a lie while you’re celebrating it. So we are warning you what is coming.
- “I write not these things to shame you, but as my beloved sons I warn you.” Paul warned us of these things that are going to come in these last days. There is no need to worry, no need to cry; in fact you should laugh. Esau says, you’re done. All right, cool, all praises, on to the next one, because we know what’s coming.
