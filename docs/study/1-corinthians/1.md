**[1 Corinthians 1:10-11](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)</sup> Now I beseech you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you; but that ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

The Bishop brings something out, the deacons come after him, and if anything is different it was already discussed: bring that out, don't bring that out yet, we'll go over that later. That is leadership, checks and balances. We don't operate like orphans.

At the blitz the Christian cult members went from camp to camp to find a flaw, and they came to say, you're going to say so and so, because they heard it at the other camp. All praises: we are all saying the exact same thing. They tried to find a kink in the army and couldn't.
