## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 11 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-3, 19, 25, 27-32, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 11:2](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v2)</sup> Now I praise you, brethren, that ye remember me in all things, and keep the ordinances, as I delivered them to you.

- “Keep the ordinances, as I delivered them to you”: keep the order as it was delivered, and then comes the summary.

**[1 Corinthians 11:3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)</sup> But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.

- “But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ”. You got to ask yourself why Paul said this. The women go, Paul must have been a misogynist. No, not at all. The women he was dealing with in Corinth, in Ephesus, were coming from places worshiping the woman goddess, where the woman was on top and the man was on the bottom. Paul was setting things in order.
- “And the head of the woman is the man”. That ain’t what Black Lives Matter told me. The hell with Black Lives Matter: the organization, not the slogan. “And the head of Christ is God.”
- Look at the image on the right: this is today. You got Satan, then white Jesus, then woman, then the man. That is the wrong order, the order of Satan, and this is what society goes by, what Christianity goes by: this is woman, thou art loosed. T.D. Jakes is catching hell for trying to undo that thing. The women in his church are mad as hell; they didn’t waste no time getting on him. They knew what it meant, with Satan ruling them and white Jesus; by him just saying that, they saw the death of their philosophy, so they attacked him.
- The correct order is on the left: the Most High God on top, then Christ, the black Christ by the way, then man, then woman. That is the order of 1 Corinthians 11:3. The one on the right is today’s society. Sisters, when you come in and run your mouth against your husband, you got white Jesus on your brain, who got Satan on his brain. We’re trying to put things in order, and you got to hear such things as, you’re a misogynist, you guys hate women.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 5:23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.

    Paul says it in other places too. “For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church”. Any confusion there, brothers? The husband is the head of the wife, just as Christ is the head of the church.
  - **[Colossians 1:18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)</sup> And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.

    “And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead”. Christ is the head of the church, and the husband is head of the wife even as Christ is head of the church.
  - **[John 5:19-24](/bible/john/5#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/john/5#v19)</sup> Then answered Jesus and said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do: for what things soever he doeth, these also doeth the Son likewise.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/john/5#v20)</sup> For the Father loveth the Son, and sheweth him all things that himself doeth: and he will shew him greater works than these, that ye may marvel.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/john/5#v21)</sup> For as the Father raiseth up the dead, and quickeneth them; even so the Son quickeneth whom he will.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/john/5#v22)</sup> For the Father judgeth no man, but hath committed all judgment unto the Son:
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/john/5#v23)</sup> That all men should honour the Son, even as they honour the Father. He that honoureth not the Son honoureth not the Father which hath sent him.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/john/5#v24)</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that heareth my word, and believeth on him that sent me, hath everlasting life, and shall not come into condemnation; but is passed from death unto life.

    “The Son can do nothing of himself, but what he seeth the Father do: for what things soever he doeth, these also doeth the Son likewise.” If you are still stuck on the Trinity, go sit in the corner somewhere; you just don't get it.

**[1 Corinthians 11:19](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v19)</sup> For there must be also heresies among you, that they which are approved may be made manifest among you.

- “For there must be also heresies among you, that they which are approved may be made manifest among you”: there must be heresies among us. How do you tell who is approved by God? Heresies have to take place.

**[1 Corinthians 11:25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v25)</sup> After the same manner also he took the cup, when he had supped, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.

- “this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.” As often as we drink it, we're supposed to remember Christ.

**[1 Corinthians 11:27](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v27)</sup> Wherefore whosoever shall eat this bread, and drink this cup of the Lord, unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord.

- “Wherefore whosoever shall eat this bread, and drink this cup of the Lord, unworthily, shall be guilty of the body and blood of the Lord.” We go through this every week, and brothers still go out committing adultery, fornication, lying, stealing, dealing falsely and deceitfully. They might be idol worshipping, sneaking and celebrating Thanksgiving or Christmas after leaving the school. We have the getaway on those days; they come in the daytime and then go to their families to celebrate those wicked holidays.
- Some people break this bread unworthily without considering the damage the Lord can do to them for going out and committing evil. These things keep coming up back to back. The righteous fall and get back up, but some people are in the midst of evil for a long time, not taking this truth seriously. They take the law and breaking bread for granted, then go do evil right away with no fear of the Lord. They have good intentions, but the spirit is strong and the flesh is weak.

**[1 Corinthians 11:28](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v28)</sup> But let a man examine himself, and so let him eat of that bread, and drink of that cup.

- “But let a man examine himself”. That's what we're supposed to do in here: examine whether we're walking in the faith and whether we're sincere.

**[1 Corinthians 11:29](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v29)</sup> For he that eateth and drinketh unworthily, eateth and drinketh damnation to himself, not discerning the Lord’s body.

- “eateth and drinketh damnation to himself, not discerning the Lord’s body.” You're sinning against your own soul when you break that bread and go out and do evil, not dealing with Christ.

**[1 Corinthians 11:30](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v30)</sup> For this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep.

- “For this cause many are weak and sickly among you”. That's why a lot among us get weak and sickly. “and many sleep.” Many get put to death.

**[1 Corinthians 11:31](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v31)</sup> For if we would judge ourselves, we should not be judged.

- “For if we would judge ourselves, we should not be judged.” If we judge ourselves, we shouldn't have to be judged. Know what you're supposed to do and walk right. Don't come in here talking about good intentions when you're not doing right. We're supposed to change our minds and spirits, get out of that world, conform to this Bible, and come back to being the righteous people we really are, new children in Christ.
- This isn't time to play. Heavy, hard times are coming in these last days. We have to be sincere around each other, loyal to the Lord, able to trust one another. Remember why you walked through those doors: you wanted change, to learn about the Lord, the kingdom of heaven, immortality, a righteous life. Don't hold on to the old man and say you're trying to do right. Just do it.
- At Passover sisters are good at checking the box, reading the ingredients for the yeast and the bicarbonate. We are good at checking the physical thing, but what about the spirit? We do not see the leaven inside of us: the sin, the hatred, the bitterness, the evil, the envy.
- “For if we would judge ourselves, we should not be judged.” If you judged yourself, you would not have to be told about your bitterness, or the envy, hatred and malice inside of you.

**[1 Corinthians 11:32](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v32)</sup> But when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord, that we should not be condemned with the world.

- “But when we are judged, we are chastened of the Lord.” Now the Lord has to bring that judgment and that chastening, because you refused to get yourself right.
