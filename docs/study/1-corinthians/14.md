## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 27, 29, 33-35, 38, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 14:1](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v1)</sup> Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts, but rather that ye may prophesy.

- “Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts.” All of us should desire certain things in the spirit: when you see a brother's charitable spirit, his spirit in building, in editing, in dealing with the public, in ordering events, you should desire those spiritual gifts.
- “But rather that ye may prophesy.” That is why prophecy has to be dealt with before anything else. The Christian church is not dealing with anything going on in the earth; it is still trying to collect tithes and run your W-2, telling you color doesn't matter. Meanwhile this man is being revealed to the whole world, and every news update can be channeled straight to the scriptures.
- The purpose of prophecy is to build your spirit up and prepare you for temptation and destruction. Prophecy is a warning, thus saith the Lord: when you see these things come to pass, know that Esau, Edom, America, is the enemy the Bible speaks of.

**[1 Corinthians 14:27](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v27)</sup> If any man speak in an unknown tongue, let it be by two, or at the most by three, and that by course; and let one interpret.

- “If any man speak in an unknown tongue, let it be by two, or at the most by three, and that by course; and let one interpret”: if you're speaking around other people, they should not be looking at you wondering what you are saying. That is evil, that's what the Bible says.
- There's a law that you're not supposed to come up here speaking Spanish around other people and they don't know what you're saying. You're not supposed to come here speaking no Spanish, no Haitian Creole, around other people. The scriptures tell you because they have the advantage with another language, just sitting in the congregation having one-on-one conversations.

**[1 Corinthians 14:29](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v29)</sup> Let the prophets speak two or three, and let the other judge.

- “Let the prophets speak two or three, and let the other judge.” Do not talk over each other. Wait your turn, then talk. That is how you judge matters.

> <sup>[33](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v33)</sup> For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace, as in all churches of the saints.

God said if you want understanding, keep my commandments. It is easy to understand. He gave straight commandments.

How can God inhabit that place and move them to understand the Bible while they break the commandments right then? Christ said, if you love me, keep my commandments.
- “For God is not the author of confusion, but of peace”. When a sister goes to another man for understanding instead of her husband, it causes confusion.
