## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 5-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 3:1](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v1)</sup> And I, brethren, could not speak unto you as unto spiritual, but as unto carnal, even as unto babes in Christ.

- “And I, brethren, could not speak unto you as unto spiritual”: to speak to them as spiritual means he wanted to go into the things in the law, the mysteries the laws contain. “But as unto carnal”: he had to deal with them like they were carnal, and carnal deals with sin. “Even as unto babes in Christ.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 7:14](/bible/romans/7#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/romans/7#v14)</sup> For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.

    What it means to be spiritual: “For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.” Carnal references sin, like David said, in sin did my mother conceive me. So when any man or woman says they are spiritual, according to the Bible it means they follow God’s commandments.

**[1 Corinthians 3:2](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v2)</sup> I have fed you with milk, and not with meat: for hitherto ye were not able to bear it, neither yet now are ye able.

- “I have fed you with milk, and not with meat”: I fed you with the basics, and not the meat, the deep mysteries of the Bible. “For hitherto ye were not able to bear it, neither yet now are ye able.”
- Today there is some milk and some meat. Whoever is ready, be ready; if you are not, shame on you, the train keeps moving. The brothers and sisters who have been faithful and unwavering will get the meat. The ones that are carnal won’t get it; they will choke on what we give them.

**[1 Corinthians 3:3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v3)</sup> For ye are yet carnal: for whereas there is among you envying, and strife, and divisions, are ye not carnal, and walk as men?

- Now he explains why: “For ye are yet carnal: for whereas there is among you envying.” The root of envy is hatred; let every man and woman examine themselves. “And strife, and divisions”: that is what we have been going through these past two months, envying and strife and divisions. “Are ye not carnal, and walk as men?”
- “For ye are yet carnal.” Carnal, carnival, carnivore: it all goes back to the same thing, the flesh. When Paul says you are carnal, he means being sinful, indulging the lust of the flesh.
- Carnival comes from the Latin for raising the flesh: giving in to the flesh, overindulging your sexual desires. The Roman Catholic Church changed it to mean removing meat, farewell to the flesh, which is a false folk meaning. Carnival is the time of merrymaking before Lent. Lent begins on Ash Wednesday, forty days before Easter, when they abstain from meat and go to the priest with ashes on their foreheads to do penance for all they did in the days before.
- So the pagans who came into the Church were allowed to keep their paganism: indulge, hold sex orgies, put on masquerade balls, and come on Wednesday for penance. Carnival is a Christian festival. Italy and Venice still keep it in the weeks before Lent, and Trinidad keeps it on the same days the Europeans keep it, which is why it began there. And Paul goes on, “for whereas there is among you envying”: that is the carnal man, the man of the flesh.

**[1 Corinthians 3:5](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v5)</sup> Who then is Paul, and who is Apollos, but ministers by whom ye believed, even as the Lord gave to every man?

- The seed on the rock withered because it had no moisture. Here is how the seed gets its water. “Who then is Paul, and who is Apollos, but ministers by whom ye believed”: they were ministers, planters, farmers.

**[1 Corinthians 3:6](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v6)</sup> I have planted, Apollos watered; but God gave the increase.

- “I have planted, Apollos watered”: Apollos came behind Paul and watered the believers. “But God gave the increase.” God is the only one that can increase you, increase you in knowledge, wisdom and understanding, increase you in terms of brothers and sisters repenting. Only God can do that; only he has the key to unlock the broken soul.
- “I have planted, Apollos watered.” Paul planted the seed: he woke the brothers and sisters up, taught them about Christ and to repent. Apollos came and watered it: he taught them, pouring the water on the seed that was planted. The one pouring the water is the one teaching you, just as the teachers are watering you right now. Apollos could not pour too much water, or the seed would corrupt and not take root; so the teaching draws back from going heavy, though from time to time it has to go heavy.
- There is a skill to it. Paul had a skill of planting and Apollos a skill of watering; if he does not have it, he puts too much water on the plant and messes it up. Not every seed grows the same way. Some seeds can only be planted on certain moons, some plants take a lot of water and some cannot; a farmer understands these things. Wisdom raineth down skill, and Paul called himself a wise master builder, because it is not a simple thing to build men, or even to water.
- “But God gave the increase.” The increase is the fruits coming forth from the tree. The teachers in IUIC water you, go over the law with you, how to fight this and overcome that. It is up to you to take this truth seriously, go home and study, cut out the thorns growing in you and bear root. To bear root is to believe this truth for yourself: not here because of your friend or some brother, but because you believe what the Bible says, trust in the Lord, and study the scriptures at home. Then God gives you the increase. That is the only way you are going to grow as a tree.

**[1 Corinthians 3:7](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v7)</sup> So then neither is he that planteth any thing, neither he that watereth; but God that giveth the increase.

- “So then neither is he that planteth any thing”: Paul said I’m nothing. “Neither he that watereth”: Apollos is nothing, “but God that giveth the increase”, because without God we can do nothing. He’s the one that gives the increase.
- “So then neither is he that planteth any thing, neither he that watereth; but God that giveth the increase.” It is God that does the whole thing, so you cannot be high-minded in this truth, thinking it is you doing it. The brother who taught you that you are Israel did his job, and the one watering you does his, but both are nothing: the Most High could replace either one.

**[1 Corinthians 3:8](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v8)</sup> Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one: and every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.

- “Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one”: we are one in one Spirit. The one that teaches first and foremost, and he that comes behind and waters the flock, are one Spirit. “And every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.” I want you men to understand that.
- “Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one: and every man shall receive his own reward.” Every one of us is going to receive our own reward: the brother on the street planting the seed and the brother watering the seed.

**[1 Corinthians 3:9](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v9)</sup> For we are labourers together with God: ye are God’s husbandry, ye are God’s building.

- “For we are labourers together with God: ye are God’s husbandry”: husbandry means planting; you are God’s planting. “Ye are God’s building.”

**[1 Corinthians 3:10](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v10)</sup> According to the grace of God which is given unto me, as a wise masterbuilder, I have laid the foundation, and another buildeth thereon. But let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon.

- “According to the grace of God which is given unto me”, the apostle Paul, “as a wise masterbuilder”: those were the carpenters we read earlier in Zechariah: Daniel, the three Hebrews, Ezekiel, Jeremiah, Judas Maccabeus and his brothers, Christ, the twelve apostles and Paul, Mordecai, Esther. “I have laid the foundation”: as a wise master builder we lay the foundation in Christ. “And another buildeth thereon”: Apollos came and built thereon, which is the rest of us. “But let every man take heed how he buildeth thereupon.”
- All of us, I want you men to understand that: we must all gear ourselves to becoming wise master builders, in another word carpenters. How to build men, how to build women: we got to learn that according to the scriptures.
- We’re builders, brothers. Sisters, y’all got some kids: you are builders too. You’re supposed to deal with their minds in their first stages of life. You put the wrong thing in children, they grow up all confused, and that’s a negro word, confused.
- “As a wise masterbuilder”: what was the apostle Paul? A wise master builder. What must we become? A wise master builder. We have to be just like that. That’s why when brothers say ‘I reject the apostle Paul’, ignore them; if y’all ain’t like that, you’re going to be confused. A lot of these single sisters online watch all these Israelite camps: ‘we don’t deal with Paul’, ‘neither do we deal with Luke’, ‘we reject 1 John’, ‘we reject 2 Peter’. You keep watching them videos and then you come in, ‘I’m confused’, because you stupid: you reject the scriptures. When you hear people say John the Baptist was fake, he had a fake Israelite camp, reject that. Those are not wise master builders. All we can do is warn y’all, and when you come in here with stupidity, security throws that out.

**[1 Corinthians 3:11](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v11)</sup> For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

- “For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.” So Christ must be the foundation that is laid down. That’s the rock we build upon; he’s the foundation first and foremost, the chief corner stone.
- “For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.” We can never ignore the chief cornerstone. When we see brothers tend to lean to one side and ignore Christ the King, you’re going to have to go. Christ is the King, he’s the Lord of lords, he is the Alpha and Omega.
- “For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.” Remember what got us to where we are. It started with only three families keeping the Passover together, with no garments, three-piece suits, unleavened bread, a lamb, bitter herbs and wine. The bishops would serve all the people at Passover, dishing out the lamb themselves: that is where humility was learned. Things had to change because of expansion and growth, and the changes were not comfortable, but they were necessary to bring us where we are today, one mind, one body, one spirit, understanding the mission.
- “For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.” Our foundation is not of our own works, not of our own opinions, emotions or feelings. It always has to go back to Messiah, our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.
