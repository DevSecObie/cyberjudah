## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 12 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-26, 28-31, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 12:1](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v1)</sup> Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant.

- 1 Corinthians 12 is the embodiment of what a body takes in order to function. We would not be where we are without understanding it, and there is still more brick to put in to go to the next level and do greater things.
- Paul is addressing the body, writing to a congregation about its issues and educating it in the things of the Lord; imagine Paul writing to the Oklahomans. “Now concerning spiritual gifts, brethren, I would not have you ignorant.” Do not get high-minded with the gift you have. To understand the functioning of the body you have to realise what every individual is worth. Nobody is above this. You are special because you are part of the body, but do not think you are more than another part. We are the same people, all working toward the selfsame goal, and we have to do exactly what the Lord told us to do to get where we need to get.
- There are so many offices in this body that it would blow your mind. You came in, got patted down, dropped your kids off and did not think twice about it; you will get a plate of food today with no idea how many hands made it possible. People have gifts to deal with things you never have to think about.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 5:15](/bible/sirach/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/5#v15)</sup> Be not ignorant of any thing in a great matter or a small.

    “Be not ignorant of any thing in a great matter or a small.” Paul tells the Corinthians, I do not want you ignorant of this; you need to learn it and understand it. You have to understand the great matters as well as the small.
  - **[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

    “And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind”. When you come into this truth you are supposed to transform the way you think. The scriptures are supposed to be inside you now, and you have to apply them.
  - **[Psalms 40:8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)</sup> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.

    What is the will of the Lord? “I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.” The will of God is the laws of God. To have them within your heart is to be able to process, to understand, to know and to see clearly.

**[1 Corinthians 12:2](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v2)</sup> Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led.

- “Ye know that ye were Gentiles, carried away unto these dumb idols, even as ye were led.” Paul leads with this before he goes over the gifts and members of the body. Were is past tense: they are not Gentiles any more. Gentile here is the Hellenized Jew. They were not used to the organisation of the Lord, to his laws and his order, because they had been Gentiles. The spirit of niggardom has been around a long time.
- Why does it matter that they were carried away unto dumb idols? Because they were not accustomed to godly order or to sound doctrine, not accustomed to what he is about to go over. They had been following dumb idols, doing what others wanted instead of what the Most High God wanted. To show and teach organisation to a Hellenized Jew is extremely difficult, so Paul reminds them of the mind frame they came from, so they can appreciate where they are and go ten times harder: that ain't us no more; renew your mind; we do not conduct ourselves like that any longer.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Maccabees 1:43-49](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v43)**
    > <sup>[43](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v43)</sup> Yea, many also of the Israelites consented to his religion, and sacrificed unto idols, and profaned the sabbath.
    >
    > <sup>[44](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v44)</sup> For the king had sent letters by messengers unto Jerusalem and the cities of Juda, that they should follow the strange laws of the land,
    >

    >

    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v47)</sup> Set up altars, and groves, and chapels of idols, and sacrifice swine’s flesh, and unclean beasts:
    >

    >
    > <sup>[49](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v49)</sup> To the end they might forget the law, and change all the ordinances.

    This is how Gentiles in the flesh think. “Yea, many also of the Israelites consented to his religion, and sacrificed unto idols, and profaned the sabbath.” In that time people of our own people consented to the religion of the Greeks, which was idolatry. They gave up what God told them to do and did what the Greeks wanted them to do. This is Hellenization.
  - **[Ephesians 2:11](/bible/ephesians/2#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/2#v11)</sup> Wherefore remember, that ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh, who are called Uncircumcision by that which is called the Circumcision in the flesh made by hands;

    Paul deals with the same things in the different congregations, because the same things were happening in each place. “Wherefore remember, that ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh”: remember back in the day, you were Gentiles, Hellenized Jews, Greeks.
  - **[Habakkuk 2:18-19](/bible/habakkuk/2#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/habakkuk/2#v18)</sup> What profiteth the graven image that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, and a teacher of lies, that the maker of his work trusteth therein, to make dumb idols?
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/habakkuk/2#v19)</sup> Woe unto him that saith to the wood, Awake; to the dumb stone, Arise, it shall teach! Behold, it is laid over with gold and silver, and there is no breath at all in the midst of it.

    Think about the mind frame of a person that followed idols. “What profiteth the graven image that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, and a teacher of lies, that the maker of his work trusteth therein, to make dumb idols?” Somebody formed the thing, then turned around and said this is God, this is what brought you out of Egypt. You have to be stupid to follow that.

**[1 Corinthians 12:3](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v3)</sup> Wherefore I give you to understand, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed: and that no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost.

- “Wherefore I give you to understand, that no man speaking by the Spirit of God calleth Jesus accursed”. If anybody is speaking by the spirit of God, would he say Christ is accursed? No. “And that no man can say that Jesus is the Lord, but by the Holy Ghost.” By him actions are weighed. In that day, someone who said Christ was accursed was not of the Lord, and someone who confessed Christ is Lord showed that he believed.

**[1 Corinthians 12:4-6](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v4)</sup> Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v5)</sup> And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v6)</sup> And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all.

- “Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit.” After war, betrayal and family, now the unity of the brotherhood, which is very important now: many gifts and administrations and operations, but the same Spirit, the same Lord, the same God.
- “Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit”: all under one. “And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord”: all under the same thing. “And there are diversities of operations, but it is the same God which worketh all in all.”
- Is that all talking about camp, camp, camp? No. At one time a camp was the brothers that go out in the streets and teach, but we have risen above that; we are more than street teaching now. Youth violence, Saving Sarah’s Daughters, community cleanups, prison ministry, the colleges, music, Original Royalty with its branches and even a modeling section. Back in the day there were two songs in rotation. Look what has been done in the spirit of Christ and his Father. The question is what we are going to do next. The sky is the limit.
- “Now there are diversities of gifts, but the same Spirit. And there are differences of administrations, but the same Lord.” These are not common Hellenized words. An administration is the organisation, management and supervision of the daily operations of a body, coordinating people and resources to reach a goal. There are different administrations, different offices, all working toward the functioning and productivity of one body.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 12:4-5](/bible/romans/12#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/12#v4)</sup> For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/12#v5)</sup> So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.

    “For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office”. One body, but everybody does not do the exact same thing. Your whole function is not just camp, camp, camp. We are evolving, things are changing, and with the exponential growth Bishop spoke of comes responsibility and the organising of these different operations and administrations.
  - **[Ephesians 4:4-6](/bible/ephesians/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ephesians/4#v4)</sup> There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/4#v5)</sup> One Lord, one faith, one baptism,
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ephesians/4#v6)</sup> One God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all.

    “There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling; One Lord, one faith, one baptism, One God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all.” The diversities of gifts, administrations and operations all come back to one: one body, one spirit, one Lord, one God working in all.

**[1 Corinthians 12:7-11](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v7)</sup> But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v8)</sup> For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v9)</sup> To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v10)</sup> To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v11)</sup> But all these worketh that one and the selfsame Spirit, dividing to every man severally as he will.

- “But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.” “For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom; to another the word of knowledge by the same Spirit; To another faith by the same Spirit; to another the gifts of healing by the same Spirit; To another the working of miracles; to another prophecy; to another discerning of spirits; to another divers kinds of tongues; to another the interpretation of tongues.”
- All these different gifts, but they are all for the same purpose: the perfecting of the saints, to profit the ministry, to build up the nation. You weren’t given the gift to hold on to it or keep it to yourself; you were given the gift so it could help somebody else.
- “But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.” Everyone has a gift, going toward the same goal in unison. It is not just for you. However the manifestation comes, different gifts are given so that you can profit with all the nation.
- “For to one is given by the Spirit the word of wisdom”: the wise brothers who can show you something and tell you, do that and this is what will happen; no, do not do that. How many times have we gone against wisdom and found out the hard way? A hard head makes a soft behind. Gifts are given so that people can learn and listen.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 4:10](/bible/1-peter/4#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-peter/4#v10)</sup> As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.

    “As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another”. The gift comes from the Lord, and it is for ministering one to another. You have not been given a gift just for you. If you have a gift and only use it for yourself, it did not come from the Lord. You are supposed to use it to benefit your nation and your people; that is why you were given it.
  - **[Philippians 2:4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)</sup> Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.

    Some people have the gift of gab: brothers who can get a flyer into anybody's hand, even the man who walked off from you telling you to go screw yourself. You may have used a gift like that to hustle and get over on people in the world, but that is not why you were given it. Once your mind is renewed you understand God gave you that gift to get the word out, to relate to people and show them thus saith the Lord. If you are not using it for the Lord, the Lord did not give it to you; Satan did. God gives gifts with purpose.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 20:30-31](/bible/sirach/20#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/20#v30)</sup> Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is hoarded up, what profit is in them both?
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/20#v31)</sup> Better is he that hideth his folly than a man that hideth his wisdom.

    This goes into wisdom, and into the gift, because the wisdom God gives comes through those gifts and the spirit he puts on you. “Wisdom that is hid, and treasure that is hoarded up, what profit is in them both?” There is no profit in hiding the gift, or the administration God has set you in.

**[1 Corinthians 12:12-18](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v12)</sup> For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v13)</sup> For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free; and have been all made to drink into one Spirit.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v14)</sup> For the body is not one member, but many.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v15)</sup> If the foot shall say, Because I am not the hand, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v16)</sup> And if the ear shall say, Because I am not the eye, I am not of the body; is it therefore not of the body?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v17)</sup> If the whole body were an eye, where were the hearing? If the whole were hearing, where were the smelling?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v18)</sup> But now hath God set the members every one of them in the body, as it hath pleased him.

- “For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ. For by one Spirit are we all baptized into one body, whether we be Jews or Gentiles, whether we be bond or free”. “For the body is not one member, but many.” All of us play a part in the body of Christ, and in the end we are all one under Christ. We all unify in the spirit of Christ, the northern kingdom and the southern kingdom alike.
- “For as the body is one, and hath many members, and all the members of that one body, being many, are one body: so also is Christ.” A body is made of different parts that all work together: the heart that gives blood to the body, the brain that helps you think, the arms and legs that help you move. There are many parts in the functioning of a body.
- Israel United in Christ is not many bodies. IUIC Austin, IUIC OKC, Atlanta, Arkansas: many members, but not different bodies. One body, the same, in the same mind and the same spirit. Regardless of where you travel or where you go, it should have the same feel, the same vibes, the same teachings, because we are one.
- Bishop Yawasap has been preparing the body and teaching this for years, and Deacon Asaph taught it as well. Back in 2016 it was shown from this verse: the body is Israel under Christ. Original Royalty, the kitchen, the school with the kids, security, video, music: all of these are components in a society. Different parts, but it all works together for the common good of the whole nation. Ten years ago those offices were what was being set up; now it is old news. That is what vision does for a nation when there is leadership with vision given by God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 4:4-6](/bible/ephesians/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ephesians/4#v4)</sup> There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/4#v5)</sup> One Lord, one faith, one baptism,
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ephesians/4#v6)</sup> One God and Father of all, who is above all, and through all, and in you all.

    “There is one body, and one Spirit, even as ye are called in one hope of your calling.” There are not multiple ways. I don’t care what Oprah said: there are not different ways, not different types, not different forms of God and how to worship God. There is only one way, one truth, and to be in alignment with it you must be doing exactly what the body is supposed to be doing: keeping the commandments and faith in his Son.
  - **[1 Corinthians 1:10](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)</sup> Now I beseech you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you; but that ye be perfectly joined together in the same mind and in the same judgment.

    A precept for the body being one: “Now I beseech you, brethren, by the name of our Lord Jesus Christ, that ye all speak the same thing, and that there be no divisions among you”. The many members speak the same thing, with no divisions, as one body.
  - **[Colossians 2:19](/bible/colossians/2#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/colossians/2#v19)</sup> And not holding the Head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God.

    “And not holding the Head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God.” The whole body is knit together and nourished from the head, and so it grows with the increase of God.

**[1 Corinthians 12:19-21](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v19)</sup> And if they were all one member, where were the body?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v20)</sup> But now are they many members, yet but one body.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v21)</sup> And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you.

- “And if they were all one member, where were the body? But now are they many members, yet but one body. And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee: nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you.” Imagine one member telling another, your job ain't that important. Remove anything and we feel the change. Take the kitchen away and the sabbath feels completely different; let the bathrooms go down with maintenance nowhere to be found and people drive home from the sabbath. We need every portion.
- “But now are they many members, yet but one body.” We have many members, yet we are all one body.
- “And if they were all one member, where were the body? But now are they many members, yet but one body.” One body, one mind, one spirit.
- “And the eye cannot say unto the hand, I have no need of thee”: the Bishops can’t say to the captains, we have no need of you; that would be the stupidest thing ever. “Nor again the head to the feet, I have no need of you”: nor can the head say to the soldiers, we have no need of you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 12:3](/bible/romans/12#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/romans/12#v3)</sup> For I say, through the grace given unto me, to every man that is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.

    If you think of yourself as better than somebody else, you are not in the spirit at all. “For I say, through the grace given unto me, to every man that is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.” To think soberly is to think with a clear mind, understanding that God has dealt severally to the body and that every single person matters.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 4:9-10](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v9)</sup> Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/4#v10)</sup> For if they fall, the one will lift up his fellow: but woe to him that is alone when he falleth; for he hath not another to help him up.

    We have to work together. “Two are better than one; because they have a good reward for their labour.” When we work together there is reward, and the more of us there are, the more we can do. They look at us crazy because we filled a ship with hundreds of people and took the gospel to about eight countries without asking anybody's permission. Collectively, we are strong.
  - **[Philippians 2:3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)</sup> Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.

    “Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.” That is the humility we have to bring ourselves to in dealing with each other: prefer one another, and do nothing in strife or vain glory, for yourself or to glorify yourself. The reward is never of the Lord when you do it like that. From a collective stance it is so much better.

**[1 Corinthians 12:22-23](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v22)</sup> Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v23)</sup> And those members of the body, which we think to be less honourable, upon these we bestow more abundant honour; and our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness.

- “Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary”. The janitorial work seems feeble; the kids corner might seem like whatever, until it stops operating. Some offices never get seen, like whoever is back there hitting the buttons, yet the buttons bring clarity to the class. The brothers of the spirit sounding off when a point is made make a person really consider what was said, and it may stand out when he goes home. Nothing is small in this body; everything has a purpose.
- “And our uncomely parts have more abundant comeliness.” The uncomely parts of the physical body, the pinky toe, even the booty hole: let that close down and you will die within a day or two. The uncomely parts are of the utmost importance, and we cannot look down on them, or they will feel they are not useful. Counsel given in secret, not for vain glory, is like that: it is never told to everybody, but it matters all the time.
- “Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble”: the ones you think are feeble are necessary. Take your pinky toe off and you have a problem walking. Brothers who are older in age or have a handicap, who may be in the stewardship program, are still very necessary. And your wives are very necessary: who is going to make the food and watch your kids running around? You need those members.
- “Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary”. We do not all play the same part; some play a bigger part than others. That does not mean you are not important. Cut off one finger from the hand and you can see the effect it makes.

**[1 Corinthians 12:24-26](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v24)</sup> For our comely parts have no need: but God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v25)</sup> That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v26)</sup> And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it.

- “But God hath tempered the body together, having given more abundant honour to that part which lacked: That there should be no schism in the body”. The Lord tempered the body together. Are schisms going to happen anyway? Oh, yeah; they are promised to happen. Satan attacks unity, and whenever we are unified he will try to jump on somebody.
- These are clear signs that a body is fractured or fracturing, from leadership down: constant murmuring; cliques forming, groups that do not want other people around, brothers who do not want to read the Bible with a certain brother; isolation, people distancing themselves; secret conversations and hidden discussions; prideful individualism, those who will not listen, have their own thoughts on how things are supposed to be and cannot be corrected; emotional decision making instead of doing it scripturally; lying or dealing falsely; and refusal to forgive, holding a grudge. That destroys the body. We have to forgive the way Christ told us to: husbands, wives, brothers, sisters, let it go.
- The signs of a healthy body, fitly joined together: healthy communication; patience, members forbearing one another; forgiveness, not holding a thing over someone's head for the rest of his life; structured leadership; congregational support, showing up for a brother who needs help, as long as he gives us time; humility, which creates unity and builds faith. A humble teacher who does not know the answer says, I don't know, I have to look that up, instead of freestyling a breakdown and sending you home thinking you have understanding. A shared mission: a body that understands the goal is the strongest body you will have. And endurance through pressure, which reveals faith, strengthens unity and develops spiritual maturity. Those who have been through things and are still standing are seen; they are a rock, standing on Christ.
- “That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another.” The members are us. “And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it.” We all wear purple; we are on the same team. You should not come against me, and I should not come against you; that is what the world does. One of us wins, it is a win for us all; one of us is honoured, it is an honour for us all. The black woman should let that sink in.
