    “For I will take you from among the heathen, and gather you out of all countries, and will bring you into your own land. Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you, and ye shall be clean”. The clean water is the commandments of God: he will put his laws in our mind and give us that immortal body. No more sin, no more nervous system to make you feel bad, no more of these somatic sicknesses, mental and physical issues and trauma. The Lord says, I am taking all that and cleaning you up: “from all your filthiness, and from all your idols, will I cleanse you.”

**[1 Corinthians 15:54](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v54)**

> <sup>[54](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v54)</sup> So when this corruptible shall have put on incorruption, and this mortal shall have put on immortality, then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written, Death is swallowed up in victory.

- “So when this corruptible shall have put on incorruption”: when this corruptible man puts on incorruption, he is walking in the laws of God. Then “Death is swallowed up in victory.” It will be swallowed up, but you have to put that on.
- Do not be deceived by being taught, or by thinking, that we can continue the way we are and then when it is time the Lord is going to look at someone and say, don't worry about it. That's not how it works. There is no sin we do in this body that we will not answer for in his time. Everything is going to be brought into judgment. So let's stack up some good fruits towards God to try to abolish what we did wrong. It is a level of insanity to think anything different; something has been communicated to you that is deceiving you. There will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.
- When this mortal has put on immortality, “then shall be brought to pass the saying that is written, Death is swallowed up in victory.”
- No more death, no more misery, no more pain, no more earthly tabernacle. It is finished. It is done. No more vile body.
