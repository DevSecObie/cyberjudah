## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Corinthians 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-16, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Corinthians 2:1-2](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v1)</sup> And I, brethren, when I came to you, came not with excellency of speech or of wisdom, declaring unto you the testimony of God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v2)</sup> For I determined not to know any thing among you, save Jesus Christ, and him crucified.

- “Not with excellency of speech or of wisdom”: not like the Christian church, speaking all proper with big words. “Save Jesus Christ, and him crucified”: that is all Paul taught them. Christ died for your sins; repent and believe on him as your savior.

**[1 Corinthians 2:3-5](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v3)</sup> And I was with you in weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v4)</sup> And my speech and my preaching was not with enticing words of man’s wisdom, but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v5)</sup> That your faith should not stand in the wisdom of men, but in the power of God.

- “In weakness, and in fear, and in much trembling”: they were being persecuted by Rome. “Not with enticing words of man’s wisdom”: not saying things just to make you join, as the church does, “but in demonstration of the Spirit and of power”, the power of teaching about the black Messiah who died to save the 12 tribes of Israel. Christian philosophy, Methodist, Baptist, Catholic, that is the wisdom of men, not God’s plan.
- “That your faith should not stand in the wisdom of men.” Don’t let your faith, brothers and sisters, stand in the wisdom of men: men who speak very well, with six-syllable words, from the poets and philosophers. “But in the power of God”: the power of God is the word of God, because that is the power.

**[1 Corinthians 2:6](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v6)</sup> Howbeit we speak wisdom among them that are perfect: yet not the wisdom of this world, nor of the princes of this world, that come to nought:

- “Yet not the wisdom of this world.” We don’t speak the wisdom of this world. The wisdom of this world says, give us reparations, it will solve all our problems. A lot of black people have hit the lotto and accomplished zero.
- “Nor of the princes of this world”: the princes of this world are the nations, “that come to nought.”
- “Not the wisdom of this world, nor of the princes of this world, that come to nought”: the ideologies they give you to keep you from God, in debt to them and looking up to them. They come to nothing.

**[1 Corinthians 2:7](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v7)</sup> But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom, which God ordained before the world unto our glory:

- “But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom.” It is hidden because the world doesn’t know it. What we say seems so unheard of: it is only for the Israelites? Christ is black and y’all are going to rule the world? It is about gathering the twelve tribes back together? No, it can’t be. It is hidden.
- “Which God ordained before the world unto our glory”: all that understanding was ordained of God beforehand for our glory.
- “But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom”: that is all of us, and that is these scriptures. What is being touched on today is hidden wisdom: the kingdom of Adam. Did Adam have a kingdom? I thought he was running around butt naked. No, there is hidden wisdom.
- “Which God ordained before the world unto our glory”: that hidden wisdom is also going into Christ. Who Christ was and who he is was hid from our people for all ages.

**[1 Corinthians 2:8](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v8)</sup> Which none of the princes of this world knew: for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.

- “Which none of the princes of this world knew: for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.” The princes of this world, the scribes and Pharisees, followed and obeyed Rome. If they had followed God the Father through the Son, they would have been all right. But they said, no, we follow Rome.
- If they had understood the scriptures through the Father and the Son, they would never have crucified Christ.
- “Which none of the princes of this world knew: for had they known it, they would not have crucified the Lord of glory.” If they had known who he was, they would not have killed our king.

    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/john/11#v48)</sup> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation.

    This proves that the princes of this world obeyed Rome. “Then gathered the chief priests and the Pharisees a council, and said, What do we? for this man doeth many miracles. If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation.”
  - **[John 8:44](/bible/john/8#v44)**

He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

**[1 Corinthians 2:9-12](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v9)</sup> But as it is written, Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man, the things which God hath prepared for them that love him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v10)</sup> But God hath revealed them unto us by his Spirit: for the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v11)</sup> For what man knoweth the things of a man, save the spirit of man which is in him? even so the things of God knoweth no man, but the Spirit of God.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v12)</sup> Now we have received, not the spirit of the world, but the spirit which is of God; that we might know the things that are freely given to us of God.

- “The things which God hath prepared for them that love him”: “God hath revealed them unto us by his Spirit.” To understand what God has prepared for you, God has to reveal it to you by his Spirit; he has to put his Holy Spirit in you. Even some of you here believe but don’t fully understand what God has prepared for us: it is on another level. Then you read the scripture and say, this is the holy city; I am going to live forever; if I die right now keeping the commandments, I don’t really die, I am going to be with Christ.
- “For the Spirit searcheth all things, yea, the deep things of God.” When you read, the Spirit is showing you things. There are deep things of God, and some of them are being touched on today.
- That is a heavy verse. There are brothers and sisters in AI who create nice, beautiful things, but what God has prepared, “Eye hath not seen, nor ear heard, neither have entered into the heart of man.” It is unimaginable. We think we can imagine it, but we can’t.
- “But as it is written”: Paul is quoting the prophet. So many of the things Paul says he is getting from the scriptures, the Old Testament. He is not pulling things out of thin air.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 5:40](/bible/2-esdras/5#v40)**
    > <sup>[40](/bible/2-esdras/5#v40)</sup> Then said he unto me, Like as thou canst do none of these things that I have spoken of, even so canst thou not find out my judgment, or in the end the love that I have promised unto my people.

    The Lord is saying, you can’t imagine this. “Then said he unto me, Like as thou canst do none of these things that I have spoken of, even so canst thou not find out my judgment, or in the end the love that I have promised unto my people.”
  - **[Isaiah 64:4](/bible/isaiah/64#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/64#v4)</sup> For since the beginning of the world men have not heard, nor perceived by the ear, neither hath the eye seen, O God, beside thee, what he hath prepared for him that waiteth for him.

    This is where Paul was quoting from: “For since the beginning of the world men have not heard, nor perceived by the ear, neither hath the eye seen, O God, beside thee, what he hath prepared for him that waiteth for him.”
  - **[Ephesians 3:17-21](/bible/ephesians/3#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/ephesians/3#v17)</sup> That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love,
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/3#v18)</sup> May be able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height;
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/ephesians/3#v19)</sup> And to know the love of Christ, which passeth knowledge, that ye might be filled with all the fulness of God.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ephesians/3#v20)</sup> Now unto him that is able to do exceeding abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us,
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/ephesians/3#v21)</sup> Unto him be glory in the church by Christ Jesus throughout all ages, world without end. Amen.

    “That Christ may dwell in your hearts by faith; that ye, being rooted and grounded in love, May be able to comprehend with all saints what is the breadth, and length, and depth, and height”. The breadth, meaning the width: it is going into what the Lord has prepared for those of us that love him. Once you fully understand that, it gives you hope, it exhorts you and comforts you to continue in this walk and not fall out and go back in the world.

**[1 Corinthians 2:13](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v13)</sup> Which things also we speak, not in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.

- “Which things also we speak, not in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth.” Man’s wisdom teaches you that economics is the way to salvation, that economics is what black people need for salvation. That’s a lie; we’ve heard that crap before. I’m not saying money is not important; the Bible says money answers all things, so I’m not knocking that. But when you put money over Christ there’s a problem.
- “But which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.” In our teaching we must compare spiritual things with spiritual, meaning precept upon precept, here a little and there a little, line upon line. That’s what he’s saying.
- “Which things also we speak, not in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth, but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.” We don’t teach in man’s wisdom of words. Comparing spiritual things with spiritual means precept upon precept, here a little and there a little.
- “Which things also we speak, not in the words which man’s wisdom teacheth”: we speak as the oracles of God, “but which the Holy Ghost teacheth; comparing spiritual things with spiritual.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Job 11:6](/bible/job/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/job/11#v6)</sup> And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom, that they are double to that which is! Know therefore that God exacteth of thee less than thine iniquity deserveth.

    “And that he would shew thee the secrets of wisdom”: we want the Lord to show us the secrets of wisdom. “That they are double to that which is”: the Bible has dual meanings, double meanings. That’s why it’s a spiritual book, and that’s why it says comparing spiritual things with spiritual.
  - **[Ephesians 4:11-12](/bible/ephesians/4#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/4#v11)</sup> And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers;
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/4#v12)</sup> For the perfecting of the saints, for the work of the ministry, for the edifying of the body of Christ:

    “And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers”. Some of us in here are apostles, some prophets, some evangelists, some pastors, some teachers, and some brothers have more gifts than others.
  - **[1 Corinthians 14:1-3](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v1)</sup> Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts, but rather that ye may prophesy.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v2)</sup> For he that speaketh in an unknown tongue speaketh not unto men, but unto God: for no man understandeth him; howbeit in the spirit he speaketh mysteries.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v3)</sup> But he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort.

    “Follow after charity, and desire spiritual gifts, but rather that ye may prophesy.” Paul says, I would rather you prophesy, because “he that prophesieth speaketh unto men to edification, and exhortation, and comfort.”

**[1 Corinthians 2:14](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v14)</sup> But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God: for they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.

- “But the natural man”: the natural man is the sinful man. He “receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God”: they cannot understand the Bible. “For they are foolishness unto him.” Brother, you mean to tell me that all we got to do is return as Israelites, keep the commandments and believe in Christ our King? That’s it? Yeah, that’s it. Brother, I don’t believe that; I think we need to protest in the streets, organize, put our money together, go against the white man and get our guns. That is the natural man: it’s foolishness unto him. “Neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.” You got to have the spirit of God to be able to understand his Bible.
- “But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God”: the natural man is the sinful man, the so-called Christian man, the so-called Israelite man, even those not keeping the Commandments. “For they are foolishness unto him: neither can he know them, because they are spiritually discerned.”
- You must have the Holy Ghost on you to understand precepts in the Bible. Without it you’ll say, I don’t understand it, I cannot see it. That’s why a Christian can’t comprehend the slave ships, getting off the slave ship and sold to our enemies: no, that’s not what it means. What does it mean, then? I don’t know. Or Christ’s woolly hair and feet like they burned in a furnace: it means judgment, it don’t mean color.
- “But the natural man receiveth not the things of the Spirit of God.” We all are the natural man, but we’re also the spiritual man; there is a duality in us. That’s how we live and move: we’re in these fleshly bodies, and this is why we have to be in control. It’s like jumping into one of those exoskeletons they show in movies and letting it control things. It’s not supposed to be that way. You steer this; you run this.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 13:10-11](/bible/matthew/13#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/13#v10)</sup> And the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/13#v11)</sup> He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.

    This verse is about Israelites, our people. “Why speakest thou unto them in parables?” Why do you speak to the people in dark sayings, in illustrated stories? “Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.”
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 2:22](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/2#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/2#v22)</sup> As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not: neither hoped they for the wages of righteousness, nor discerned a reward for blameless souls.

    Now Esau. Surely the white man knows: he reads manuscripts and textbooks, archaeological digs and ancient texts. “As for the mysteries of God, they knew them not.” The ungodly, the white man, knew them not.

**[1 Corinthians 2:15](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v15)</sup> But he that is spiritual judgeth all things, yet he himself is judged of no man.

- “But he that is spiritual judgeth all things, yet he himself is judged of no man”: meaning if you’re keeping the commandments, no man can judge you, meaning condemn you.
- “But he that is spiritual judgeth all things, yet he himself is judged of no man.” You’re not going to be judged of any man, because you’re going to judge those things in your mind before they become words, actions and habits. You’re getting it right in your head, comparing spiritual to spiritual, so nobody’s going to have to tell you, damn, you messed up and you should have known better. You kill it in your head.
- “But he that is spiritual judgeth all things, yet he himself is judged of no man.”

**[1 Corinthians 2:16](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v16)</sup> For who hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him? But we have the mind of Christ.

- “For who hath known the mind of the Lord, that he may instruct him?” Nobody has known the mind of the Lord that he may instruct him, but he instructed one. “But we have the mind of Christ”: that crown of glory that’s on our head.
