Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 6](/bible/1-chronicles/6)

This session covers [1 Chronicles 6](/bible/1-chronicles/6) through 9, a heavy stretch of genealogy that the teacher urged the family not to take lightly. These names are our forefathers, and the histories in Chronicles unlock much that is otherwise confusing in the New Testament. Chapter 6 gives the sons of Levi, the line of Aaron that held the high priest's office, the singers David set over the service of song, and the cities appointed to the Levites. Read these as the record of the greatest men and women of the mighty tribe of Levi, chosen for the service of the Most High.

# 1 Chronicles 7: The Warrior Tribes

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 7](/bible/1-chronicles/7)

This chapter numbers the sons of Issachar, Benjamin, Naphtali, Manasseh, Ephraim, and Asher, and reckons them again and again as mighty men of valor and soldiers fit for war. Do not let the world dwindle our forefathers down to servants only. These were warlike men of strength and power, warriors reckoned by their genealogies for the battle.

This chapter gives the genealogy of Benjamin in fuller detail, the heads of the fathers who dwelt in Geba, Ono, Lod, Jerusalem, and Gibeon, and closes with the line of Saul the king down through Jonathan and his sons. # 1 Chronicles 9: The Returned Inhabitants and Their Offices

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 9](/bible/1-chronicles/9)

This chapter records those who first dwelt again in their possessions after the return from Babylon, the Israelites, priests, Levites, and Nethinims, and the offices they held in the house of the Most High. It names the porters who kept the gates, the singers, and those set over the vessels, the shewbread, the fine flour, wine, oil, frankincense, and spices, then closes by repeating the line of Saul.
