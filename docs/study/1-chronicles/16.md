**[1 Chronicles 16:1-3](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v1)</sup> So they brought the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it: and they offered burnt sacrifices and peace offerings before God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v2)</sup> And when David had made an end of offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v3)</sup> And he dealt to every one of Israel, both man and woman, to every one a loaf of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.

- The ark was set in the midst of the tent David pitched, and burnt and peace offerings were made before the Most High.
- David blessed the people in the name of the Most High and dealt to every man and woman a loaf of bread, a piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.
  - **[2 Samuel 6:19](/bible/2-samuel/6#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/2-samuel/6#v19)</sup> And he dealt among all the people, even among the whole multitude of Israel, as well to the women as men, to every one a cake of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine. So all the people departed every one to his house.

    The shorter account records the same gifts of bread, flesh, and wine to all the people.

**[1 Chronicles 16:4-6](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v4)</sup> And he appointed certain of the Levites to minister before the ark of the Lord, and to record, and to thank and praise the Lord God of Israel:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v5)</sup> Asaph the chief, and next to him Zechariah, Jeiel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah, and Obed–edom: and Jeiel with psalteries and with harps; but Asaph made a sound with cymbals;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v6)</sup> Benaiah also and Jahaziel the priests with trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God.

- David appointed certain Levites to minister before the ark, to record, thank, and praise the Most High as a daily work.
- Asaph was made chief, with named Levites playing psalteries, harps, and cymbals, and priests sounding trumpets continually. No one could take another's job or the whole order would fail.

**[1 Chronicles 16:7-13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v7)</sup> Then on that day David delivered first this psalm to thank the Lord into the hand of Asaph and his brethren.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v8)</sup> Give thanks unto the Lord, call upon his name, make known his deeds among the people.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v9)</sup> Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him, talk ye of all his wondrous works.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v10)</sup> Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v11)</sup> Seek the Lord and his strength, seek his face continually.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v12)</sup> Remember his marvellous works that he hath done, his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v13)</sup> O ye seed of Israel his servant, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

- David delivered this psalm into the hand of Asaph to thank the Most High, a prayer for the congregation much like a set brother who sends up the prayers.
- The song calls Israel to give thanks, sing, glory in his holy name, and seek his strength and face continually.
- It names them plainly as the seed of Israel his servant, the children of Jacob his chosen ones, showing these are the chosen people and it has never changed.

**[1 Chronicles 16:14-18](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v14)</sup> He is the Lord our God; his judgments are in all the earth.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v15)</sup> Be ye mindful always of his covenant; the word which he commanded to a thousand generations;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v16)</sup> Even of the covenant which he made with Abraham, and of his oath unto Isaac;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v17)</sup> And hath confirmed the same to Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v18)</sup> Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance;

- The Most High is mindful always of his covenant, the word commanded to a thousand generations, which is to say forever.
- The covenant was made with Abraham, sworn to Isaac, and confirmed to Jacob as an everlasting covenant, giving Israel the land of Canaan as their inheritance.
  - **[Psalm 105:8-10](/bible/psalms/105#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/105#v8)</sup> He hath remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/105#v9)</sup> Which covenant he made with Abraham, and his oath unto Isaac;
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/105#v10)</sup> And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant:

    The same everlasting covenant to a thousand generations, made with Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
  - **[Genesis 12:2-3](/bible/genesis/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/12#v2)</sup> And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/12#v3)</sup> And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.

    The Most High makes of Abraham a great nation, and in him all families of the earth are blessed.

**[1 Chronicles 16:19-22](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v19)</sup> When ye were but few, even a few, and strangers in it.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v20)</sup> And when they went from nation to nation, and from one kingdom to another people;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v21)</sup> He suffered no man to do them wrong: yea, he reproved kings for their sakes,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v22)</sup> Saying, Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm.

- When Israel were few and strangers going from nation to nation, the Most High suffered no man to do them wrong and reproved kings for their sakes.
- He warned kings to touch not his anointed and do his prophets no harm, recalling deliverances such as when Abraham went down to Egypt.

**[1 Chronicles 16:23-30](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v23)</sup> Sing unto the Lord, all the earth; shew forth from day to day his salvation.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v24)</sup> Declare his glory among the heathen; his marvellous works among all nations.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v25)</sup> For great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised: he also is to be feared above all gods.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v26)</sup> For all the gods of the people are idols: but the Lord made the heavens.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v27)</sup> Glory and honour are in his presence; strength and gladness are in his place.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v28)</sup> Give unto the Lord, ye kindreds of the people, give unto the Lord glory and strength.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v29)</sup> Give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name: bring an offering, and come before him: worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v30)</sup> Fear before him, all the earth: the world also shall be stable, that it be not moved.

- The song calls all the earth to show forth his salvation and declare his glory among the heathen, for great is the Most High and greatly to be praised.
- All the gods of the people are idols, but the Most High made the heavens; strength and gladness are in his place, and all the earth is to fear before him.

**[1 Chronicles 16:31-36](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v31)**
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v31)</sup> Let the heavens be glad, and let the earth rejoice: and let men say among the nations, The Lord reigneth.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v32)</sup> Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof: let the fields rejoice, and all that is therein.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v33)</sup> Then shall the trees of the wood sing out at the presence of the Lord, because he cometh to judge the earth.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v34)</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v35)</sup> And say ye, Save us, O God of our salvation, and gather us together, and deliver us from the heathen, that we may give thanks to thy holy name, and glory in thy praise.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v36)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel for ever and ever. And all the people said, Amen, and praised the Lord.

- Let the heavens be glad, the earth rejoice, and the nations say the Most High reigns, for he cometh to judge the earth.
- The song ends with the plea to save, gather, and deliver Israel from the heathen, and all the people said Amen and praised the Most High. This is a good prayer to meditate on.

**[1 Chronicles 16:37-43](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v37)**
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v37)</sup> So he left there before the ark of the covenant of the Lord Asaph and his brethren, to minister before the ark continually, as every day’s work required:
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v38)</sup> And Obed–edom with their brethren, threescore and eight; Obed–edom also the son of Jeduthun and Hosah to be porters:
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v39)</sup> And Zadok the priest, and his brethren the priests, before the tabernacle of the Lord in the high place that was at Gibeon,
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v40)</sup> To offer burnt offerings unto the Lord upon the altar of the burnt offering continually morning and evening, and to do according to all that is written in the law of the Lord, which he commanded Israel;
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v41)</sup> And with them Heman and Jeduthun, and the rest that were chosen, who were expressed by name, to give thanks to the Lord, because his mercy endureth for ever;
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v42)</sup> And with them Heman and Jeduthun with trumpets and cymbals for those that should make a sound, and with musical instruments of God. And the sons of Jeduthun were porters.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v43)</sup> And all the people departed every man to his house: and David returned to bless his house.

- David left Asaph and his brethren before the ark to minister continually, as every day's work required, with Obededom and doorkeepers appointed.
- Zadok the priest and his brethren were left before the tabernacle at Gibeon to offer burnt offerings morning and evening according to all written in the law.
- Heman and Jeduthun with trumpets and instruments gave thanks because his mercy endureth forever, and all the people departed, and David returned to bless his house.
