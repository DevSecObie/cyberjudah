Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 4](/bible/1-chronicles/4)

This chapter gathers more of the families of Judah, including the prayer of Jabez, and then turns to the sons of Simeon, their dwellings, and their conquests. The teaching drew out that the blessing rested heavily on Judah, who multiplied above his brethren, and that the tribe of Simeon took land that had belonged to the sons of Ham.

**[1 Chronicles 4:1-8](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v1)</sup> The sons of Judah; Pharez, Hezron, and Carmi, and Hur, and Shobal.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v2)</sup> And Reaiah the son of Shobal begat Jahath; and Jahath begat Ahumai, and Lahad. These are the families of the Zorathites.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v3)</sup> And these were of the father of Etam; Jezreel, and Ishma, and Idbash: and the name of their sister was Hazelelponi:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v4)</sup> And Penuel the father of Gedor, and Ezer the father of Hushah. These are the sons of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah, the father of Beth–lehem.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v5)</sup> And Ashur the father of Tekoa had two wives, Helah and Naarah.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v6)</sup> And Naarah bare him Ahuzam, and Hepher, and Temeni, and Haahashtari. These were the sons of Naarah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v7)</sup> And the sons of Helah were, Zereth, and Jezoar, and Ethnan.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v8)</sup> And Coz begat Anub, and Zobebah, and the families of Aharhel the son of Harum.

- The sons of Judah and the families that came from them are set down, including the line of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah, the father of Bethlehem.
- The names here carry meaning, and the record keeps the families in order down to the sons of Koz.

**[1 Chronicles 4:9-10](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v9)</sup> And Jabez was more honourable than his brethren: and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, Because I bare him with sorrow.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v10)</sup> And Jabez called on the God of Israel, saying, Oh that thou wouldest bless me indeed, and enlarge my coast, and that thine hand might be with me, and that thou wouldest keep me from evil, that it may not grieve me! And God granted him that which he requested.

- Jabez was more honorable than his brethren, and his mother called his name Jabez because she bare him with sorrow, the meaning of the name matching the scripture itself.
- Jabez called on the God of Israel and asked that He would bless him indeed, enlarge his coast, keep His hand with him, and keep him from evil, and the Most High granted him what he requested.

**[1 Chronicles 4:11-20](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v11)</sup> And Chelub the brother of Shuah begat Mehir, which was the father of Eshton.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v12)</sup> And Eshton begat Beth–rapha, and Paseah, and Tehinnah the father of Ir–nahash. These are the men of Rechah.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v13)</sup> And the sons of Kenaz; Othniel, and Seraiah: and the sons of Othniel; Hathath.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v14)</sup> And Meonothai begat Ophrah: and Seraiah begat Joab, the father of the valley of Charashim; for they were craftsmen.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v15)</sup> And the sons of Caleb the son of Jephunneh; Iru, Elah, and Naam: and the sons of Elah, even Kenaz.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v16)</sup> And the sons of Jehaleleel; Ziph, and Ziphah, Tiria, and Asareel.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v17)</sup> And the sons of Ezra were, Jether, and Mered, and Epher, and Jalon: and she bare Miriam, and Shammai, and Ishbah the father of Eshtemoa.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v18)</sup> And his wife Jehudijah bare Jered the father of Gedor, and Heber the father of Socho, and Jekuthiel the father of Zanoah. And these are the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh, which Mered took.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v19)</sup> And the sons of his wife Hodiah the sister of Naham, the father of Keilah the Garmite, and Eshtemoa the Maachathite.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v20)</sup> And the sons of Shimon were, Amnon, and Rinnah, Ben–hanan, and Tilon. And the sons of Ishi were, Zoheth, and Ben–zoheth.

- The families of Judah are traced further through Chelub, Kenaz, Othniel, and the sons of Ezra and their households.
- The record names the sons of Bithiah the daughter of Pharaoh and other households, and the names in this stretch carry meaning, as Jekuthiel was noted to point to the hope of the congregation of God.

**[1 Chronicles 4:21-23](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v21)</sup> The sons of Shelah the son of Judah were, Er the father of Lecah, and Laadah the father of Mareshah, and the families of the house of them that wrought fine linen, of the house of Ashbea,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v22)</sup> And Jokim, and the men of Chozeba, and Joash, and Saraph, who had the dominion in Moab, and Jashubi–lehem. And these are ancient things.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v23)</sup> These were the potters, and those that dwelt among plants and hedges: there they dwelt with the king for his work.

- The sons of Shelah the son of Judah are named, with the families that wrought fine linen and the potters that dwelt with the king for his work.
- These are called the ancient things, the old record of the trades and dwellings of these families.

**[1 Chronicles 4:24-33](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v24)</sup> The sons of Simeon were, Nemuel, and Jamin, Jarib, Zerah, and Shaul:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v25)</sup> Shallum his son, Mibsam his son, Mishma his son.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v26)</sup> And the sons of Mishma; Hamuel his son, Zacchur his son, Shimei his son.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v27)</sup> And Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters; but his brethren had not many children, neither did all their family multiply, like to the children of Judah.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v28)</sup> And they dwelt at Beer–sheba, and Moladah, and Hazar–shual,
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v29)</sup> And at Bilhah, and at Ezem, and at Tolad,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v30)</sup> And at Bethuel, and at Hormah, and at Ziklag,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v31)</sup> And at Beth–marcaboth, and Hazar–susim, and at Beth–birei, and at Shaaraim. These were their cities unto the reign of David.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v32)</sup> And their villages were, Etam, and Ain, Rimmon, and Tochen, and Ashan, five cities:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v33)</sup> And all their villages that were round about the same cities, unto Baal. These were their habitations, and their genealogy.

- The sons of Simeon are set down, and the record notes that Shimei had sixteen sons and six daughters, but his brethren had not many children.
- Simeon did not multiply like the children of Judah, showing that the blessing of increase rested on Judah.
- Their dwellings at Beersheba and the surrounding cities are named, the same inheritance given to Simeon in Joshua.
  - **[Joshua 19:2](/bible/joshua/19#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/19#v2)</sup> And they had in their inheritance Beer–sheba, Sheba, and Moladah,

    Simeon's inheritance is named, Beersheba, and Sheba, and Moladah, and the cities round about.

**[1 Chronicles 4:34-43](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v34)</sup> And Meshobab, and Jamlech, and Joshah the son of Amaziah,
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v35)</sup> And Joel, and Jehu the son of Josibiah, the son of Seraiah, the son of Asiel,
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v36)</sup> And Elioenai, and Jaakobah, and Jeshohaiah, and Asaiah, and Adiel, and Jesimiel, and Benaiah,
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v37)</sup> And Ziza the son of Shiphi, the son of Allon, the son of Jedaiah, the son of Shimri, the son of Shemaiah;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v38)</sup> These mentioned by their names were princes in their families: and the house of their fathers increased greatly.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v39)</sup> And they went to the entrance of Gedor, even unto the east side of the valley, to seek pasture for their flocks.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v40)</sup> And they found fat pasture and good, and the land was wide, and quiet, and peaceable; for they of Ham had dwelt there of old.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v41)</sup> And these written by name came in the days of Hezekiah king of Judah, and smote their tents, and the habitations that were found there, and destroyed them utterly unto this day, and dwelt in their rooms: because there was pasture there for their flocks.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v42)</sup> And some of them, even of the sons of Simeon, five hundred men, went to mount Seir, having for their captains Pelatiah, and Neariah, and Rephaiah, and Uzziel, the sons of Ishi.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v43)</sup> And they smote the rest of the Amalekites that were escaped, and dwelt there unto this day.

- The princes of Simeon are named by their families, whose houses increased greatly, and they went to seek pasture for their flocks.
- They found fat and quiet pasture in a land where the sons of Ham had dwelt of old, and in the days of Hezekiah they smote those habitations and dwelt in their place.
- Five hundred of the sons of Simeon went to mount Seir, smote the remnant of the Amalekites that had escaped, and dwelt there, showing the Most High's blessing on them in war.
