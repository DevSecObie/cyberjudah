Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 8](/bible/1-chronicles/8)

This chapter gives the genealogy of Benjamin in fuller detail, the heads of the fathers who dwelt in Geba, Ono, Lod, Jerusalem, and Gibeon, and closes with the line of Saul the king down through Jonathan and his sons. It sets the stage for the record of those who returned to dwell in Jerusalem in the next chapter.

**[1 Chronicles 8:1-7](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v1)</sup> Now Benjamin begat Bela his firstborn, Ashbel the second, and Aharah the third,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v2)</sup> Nohah the fourth, and Rapha the fifth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v3)</sup> And the sons of Bela were, Addar, and Gera, and Abihud,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v4)</sup> And Abishua, and Naaman, and Ahoah,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v5)</sup> And Gera, and Shephuphan, and Huram.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v6)</sup> And these are the sons of Ehud: these are the heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Geba, and they removed them to Manahath:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v7)</sup> And Naaman, and Ahiah, and Gera, he removed them, and begat Uzza, and Ahihud.

- Benjamin's sons are named in order, Bela his firstborn, Ashbel, Aharah, Nohah, and Rapha, with the sons of Bela and of Ehud set down after.
- These are the heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Geba who were removed to Manahath.

**[1 Chronicles 8:8-13](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v8)</sup> And Shaharaim begat children in the country of Moab, after he had sent them away; Hushim and Baara were his wives.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v9)</sup> And he begat of Hodesh his wife, Jobab, and Zibia, and Mesha, and Malcham,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v10)</sup> And Jeuz, and Shachia, and Mirma. These were his sons, heads of the fathers.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v11)</sup> And of Hushim he begat Abitub, and Elpaal.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v12)</sup> The sons of Elpaal; Eber, and Misham, and Shamed, who built Ono, and Lod, with the towns thereof:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v13)</sup> Beriah also, and Shema, who were heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Aijalon, who drove away the inhabitants of Gath:

- Shaharaim begat children in the country of Moab after he sent away his wives, and his sons are named as heads of the fathers.
- The sons of Elpaal, Eber, Misham, and Shamed, built Ono and Lod with their towns, and Beriah and Shema were heads of the fathers of the inhabitants of Aijalon.

**[1 Chronicles 8:29-40](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v29)</sup> And at Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon; whose wife’s name was Maachah:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v30)</sup> And his firstborn son Abdon, and Zur, and Kish, and Baal, and Nadab,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v31)</sup> And Gedor, and Ahio, and Zacher.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v32)</sup> And Mikloth begat Shimeah. And these also dwelt with their brethren in Jerusalem, over against them.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v33)</sup> And Ner begat Kish, and Kish begat Saul, and Saul begat Jonathan, and Malchi–shua, and Abinadab, and Esh–baal.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v34)</sup> And the son of Jonathan was Merib–baal; and Merib–baal begat Micah.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v35)</sup> And the sons of Micah were, Pithon, and Melech, and Tarea, and Ahaz.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v36)</sup> And Ahaz begat Jehoadah; and Jehoadah begat Alemeth, and Azmaveth, and Zimri; and Zimri begat Moza,
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v37)</sup> And Moza begat Binea: Rapha was his son, Eleasah his son, Azel his son:
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v38)</sup> And Azel had six sons, whose names are these, Azrikam, Bocheru, and Ishmael, and Sheariah, and Obadiah, and Hanan. All these were the sons of Azel.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v39)</sup> And the sons of Eshek his brother were, Ulam his firstborn, Jehush the second, and Eliphelet the third.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-chronicles/8#v40)</sup> And the sons of Ulam were mighty men of valour, archers, and had many sons, and sons’ sons, an hundred and fifty. All these are of the sons of Benjamin.

- At Gibeon dwelt the father of Gibeon, and his sons are named down to the line of Ner, Kish, and Saul.
- The line of Saul the king is traced through Jonathan, Merib-baal, Micah, and on to Azel, whose six sons close the chapter, and the sons of Ulam who were mighty men of valor, archers, with many sons.
