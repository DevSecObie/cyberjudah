Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 3](/bible/1-chronicles/3)

This chapter narrows the record of Judah to the house of David: the sons born to him in Hebron, the sons born to him in Jerusalem, and then the kings that came from Solomon down through the captivity and the sons of Jeconiah after it. This is the line of our kings, kept by name.

**[1 Chronicles 3:1-4](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v1)</sup> Now these were the sons of David, which were born unto him in Hebron; the firstborn Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; the second Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v2)</sup> The third, Absalom the son of Maachah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur: the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v3)</sup> The fifth, Shephatiah of Abital: the sixth, Ithream by Eglah his wife.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v4)</sup> These six were born unto him in Hebron; and there he reigned seven years and six months: and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty and three years.

- The sons born to David in Hebron are named, from Amnon the firstborn to the sixth, with their mothers.
- Amnon was the son who defiled his sister Tamar, and Absalom, named here among the younger sons, later avenged her and paid for it with his own life.
- David reigned in Hebron seven years and six months before he reigned in Jerusalem.

**[1 Chronicles 3:5-9](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v5)</sup> And these were born unto him in Jerusalem; Shimea, and Shobab, and Nathan, and Solomon, four, of Bath–shua the daughter of Ammiel:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v6)</sup> Ibhar also, and Elishama, and Eliphelet,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v7)</sup> And Nogah, and Nepheg, and Japhia,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v8)</sup> And Elishama, and Eliada, and Eliphelet, nine.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v9)</sup> These were all the sons of David, beside the sons of the concubines, and Tamar their sister.

- The sons born to David in Jerusalem are named, including Solomon, born of Bathshua the daughter of Ammiel.
- Nine sons are set down here besides the sons of the concubines, and their sister Tamar is named among them.

**[1 Chronicles 3:10-16](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v10)</sup> And Solomon’s son was Rehoboam, Abia his son, Asa his son, Jehoshaphat his son,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v11)</sup> Joram his son, Ahaziah his son, Joash his son,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v12)</sup> Amaziah his son, Azariah his son, Jotham his son,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v13)</sup> Ahaz his son, Hezekiah his son, Manasseh his son,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v14)</sup> Amon his son, Josiah his son.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v15)</sup> And the sons of Josiah were, the firstborn Johanan, the second Jehoiakim, the third Zedekiah, the fourth Shallum.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v16)</sup> And the sons of Jehoiakim: Jeconiah his son, Zedekiah his son.

- The kings of Judah are set down in order from Solomon: Rehoboam, Abijah, Asa, Jehoshaphat, and on through the line.
- The record runs through Hezekiah, Manasseh, Amon, and Josiah, keeping the royal succession by name.
- Josiah means the Most High has healed, a name still carried among us today.

**[1 Chronicles 3:17-24](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v17)</sup> And the sons of Jeconiah; Assir, Salathiel his son,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v18)</sup> Malchiram also, and Pedaiah, and Shenazar, Jecamiah, Hoshama, and Nedabiah.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v19)</sup> And the sons of Pedaiah were, Zerubbabel, and Shimei: and the sons of Zerubbabel; Meshullam, and Hananiah, and Shelomith their sister:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v20)</sup> And Hashubah, and Ohel, and Berechiah, and Hasadiah, Jushab–hesed, five.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v21)</sup> And the sons of Hananiah; Pelatiah, and Jesaiah: the sons of Rephaiah, the sons of Arnan, the sons of Obadiah, the sons of Shechaniah.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v22)</sup> And the sons of Shechaniah; Shemaiah: and the sons of Shemaiah; Hattush, and Igeal, and Bariah, and Neariah, and Shaphat, six.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v23)</sup> And the sons of Neariah; Elioenai, and Hezekiah, and Azrikam, three.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v24)</sup> And the sons of Elioenai were, Hodaiah, and Eliashib, and Pelaiah, and Akkub, and Johanan, and Dalaiah, and Anani, seven.

- The sons of Jeconiah are named, and the line is carried through Salathiel, Pedaiah, and Zerubbabel after the captivity.
- The record runs on through the sons of Zerubbabel and their descendants, and these too are our forefathers, kept by name so we can search out our fathers.
- Many of these names carry meaning; among them Shecaniah was found to mean one intimate with God and Elioenai to mean I look to God.
