Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 14](/bible/1-chronicles/14)

This session covers [1 Chronicles 14](/bible/1-chronicles/14) through 17: David confirmed as king, his two battles with the Philistines fought under the Most High's instruction, the ordered bringing up of the ark of the covenant into the city of David by the Levites, the song of thanksgiving delivered into the hand of Asaph, and the promise of an everlasting throne through David's seed. Throughout these chapters the pattern is the same: the righteous forefathers went to the Most High before they acted, and everything concerning the ark and the priesthood was done according to a set order that no man could break.

**[1 Chronicles 14:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v1)</sup> Now Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and timber of cedars, with masons and carpenters, to build him an house.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v2)</sup> And David perceived that the Lord had confirmed him king over Israel, for his kingdom was lifted up on high, because of his people Israel.

- Hiram king of Tyre sent workmen and cedars to build David a house, and David recognized that the Most High had confirmed him king over Israel.
- His kingdom was lifted up on high for the sake of the Most High's people Israel, not for David's own sake.

**[1 Chronicles 14:8-10](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v8)</sup> And when the Philistines heard that David was anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David. And David heard of it, and went out against them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v9)</sup> And the Philistines came and spread themselves in the valley of Rephaim.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v10)</sup> And David enquired of God, saying, Shall I go up against the Philistines? and wilt thou deliver them into mine hand? And the Lord said unto him, Go up; for I will deliver them into thine hand.

- When the Philistines heard David was anointed king over all Israel they went up to seek him, and David went out against them.
- David inquired of the Most High before the battle, asking whether he should go up and whether victory would be given. This is the mark of the righteous forefathers: they took their battles to the Most High first.
- The Most High answered him plainly, promising to deliver the Philistines into his hand.

**[1 Chronicles 14:11-12](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v11)</sup> So they came up to Baal–perazim; and David smote them there. Then David said, God hath broken in upon mine enemies by mine hand like the breaking forth of waters: therefore they called the name of that place Baal–perazim.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v12)</sup> And when they had left their gods there, David gave a commandment, and they were burned with fire.

- David defeated them at Baalperazim and named the place for how the Most High had broken through his enemies like a bursting of waters.
- The Philistine gods left there were burned with fire.
  - **[Deuteronomy 7:25](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v25)</sup> The graven images of their gods shall ye burn with fire: thou shalt not desire the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it unto thee, lest thou be snared therein: for it is an abomination to the Lord thy God.

    Their graven images are to be burned so they do not become a snare unto us.

**[1 Chronicles 14:13-16](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v13)</sup> And the Philistines yet again spread themselves abroad in the valley.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v14)</sup> Therefore David enquired again of God; and God said unto him, Go not up after them; turn away from them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v15)</sup> And it shall be, when thou shalt hear a sound of going in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt go out to battle: for God is gone forth before thee to smite the host of the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v16)</sup> David therefore did as God commanded him: and they smote the host of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gazer.

- The Philistines came up a second time, and David inquired again rather than presuming on the first victory.
- This time the Most High said not to go straight up but to circle behind them by the mulberry trees, and to move only when he heard a sound of going in the treetops, for the Most High had gone before to smite the host.
- David did exactly as commanded and smote them from Gibeon to Gazer. The lesson is precise obedience: if he says do not go up, do not go up; if he says go this way, do not go that way.
  - **[Exodus 15:3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)</sup> The Lord is a man of war: the Lord is his name.

    The Most High is a man of war and knows exactly how to handle the battle.
  - **[1 Samuel 15:22-23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)</sup> And Samuel said, Hath the Lord as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the Lord? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)</sup> For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

    Saul was told to overcome nations and did not, and was told not to go to his last battle yet went and died there; obedience is better than sacrifice.

**[1 Chronicles 14:17](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v17)</sup> And the fame of David went out into all lands; and the Lord brought the fear of him upon all nations.

- The fame of David went out into all lands and the Most High brought the fear of him upon all nations.
