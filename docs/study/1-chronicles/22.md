## Introduction

This session covers 1 Chronicles chapters 22 through 25. It is a brief but important reading, heavy with names, offices, and order. David, now old and full of days, cannot build the temple himself because he has shed much blood in war, so he prepares the materials in abundance and charges his son Solomon to build the house unto the name of the Most High. David then sets the Levites in order, dividing them for the work of the house, numbers the priests into their courses by lot, and appoints the singers and musicians for the service of the sanctuary. The running thread is order and organization: keeping the commandments to prosper, and everyone in Israel knowing his place and his service before the Most High.

**[1 Chronicles 22:1-4](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v1)</sup> Then David said, This is the house of the Lord God, and this is the altar of the burnt offering for Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v2)</sup> And David commanded to gather together the strangers that were in the land of Israel; and he set masons to hew wrought stones to build the house of God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v3)</sup> And David prepared iron in abundance for the nails for the doors of the gates, and for the joinings; and brass in abundance without weight;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v4)</sup> Also cedar trees in abundance: for the Zidonians and they of Tyre brought much cedar wood to David.

- David said, This is the house of the Lord God, and this is the altar of the burnt offering for Israel. He commanded to gather together the strangers that were in the land of Israel and set masons to hew wrought stones to build the house of God. These strangers were brought in to help with the building of the temple.
- David prepared iron in abundance for the nails and for the joinings, brass in abundance without weight, and cedar trees in abundance. The Zidonians and they of Tyre brought much cedar wood to David.

    Hiram king of Tyre was ever a lover of David, and he sent his servants unto Solomon, helping to supply the timber for the building of the temple.

**[1 Chronicles 22:5-6](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v5)</sup> And David said, Solomon my son is young and tender, and the house that is to be builded for the Lord must be exceeding magnifical, of fame and of glory throughout all countries: I will therefore now make preparation for it. So David prepared abundantly before his death.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v6)</sup> Then he called for Solomon his son, and charged him to build an house for the Lord God of Israel.

- David said that Solomon his son was young and tender, and the house to be built for the Lord must be exceeding magnifical, of fame and glory throughout all countries, so he prepared abundantly before his death.
- He called for Solomon his son and charged him to build a house for the Lord God of Israel.

**[1 Chronicles 22:7-10](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v7)</sup> And David said to Solomon, My son, as for me, it was in my mind to build an house unto the name of the Lord my God:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v8)</sup> But the word of the Lord came to me, saying, Thou hast shed blood abundantly, and hast made great wars: thou shalt not build an house unto my name, because thou hast shed much blood upon the earth in my sight.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v9)</sup> Behold, a son shall be born to thee, who shall be a man of rest; and I will give him rest from all his enemies round about: for his name shall be Solomon, and I will give peace and quietness unto Israel in his days.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v10)</sup> He shall build an house for my name; and he shall be my son, and I will be his father; and I will establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel for ever.

- David told Solomon that it was in his mind to build a house unto the name of the Lord, but the word of the Lord came to him saying he should not build it because he had shed blood abundantly and made great wars. Because of all the wars and fighting David had done, the Most High did not allow him to build the temple.
- The Lord promised David a son who would be a man of rest, to whom he would give rest from all his enemies round about, whose name would be Solomon, and in whose days he would give peace and quietness unto Israel. This son would build the house for the Most High's name, and the Most High would establish the throne of his kingdom over Israel forever.

**[1 Chronicles 22:11-13](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v11)</sup> Now, my son, the Lord be with thee; and prosper thou, and build the house of the Lord thy God, as he hath said of thee.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v12)</sup> Only the Lord give thee wisdom and understanding, and give thee charge concerning Israel, that thou mayest keep the law of the Lord thy God.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v13)</sup> Then shalt thou prosper, if thou takest heed to fulfil the statutes and judgments which the Lord charged Moses with concerning Israel: be strong, and of good courage; dread not, nor be dismayed.

- David charged Solomon that the Lord be with him and prosper him to build the house of the Lord his God, and asked that the Lord give him wisdom and understanding and charge concerning Israel that he might keep the law of the Lord.
- He said Solomon would prosper if he took heed to fulfill the statutes and judgments which the Lord charged Moses with concerning Israel. This is the same message the Most High gives us today: we will prosper as long as we obey the commandments. Be strong and of good courage; dread not, nor be dismayed.

**[1 Chronicles 22:14-16](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v14)</sup> Now, behold, in my trouble I have prepared for the house of the Lord an hundred thousand talents of gold, and a thousand thousand talents of silver; and of brass and iron without weight; for it is in abundance: timber also and stone have I prepared; and thou mayest add thereto.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v15)</sup> Moreover there are workmen with thee in abundance, hewers and workers of stone and timber, and all manner of cunning men for every manner of work.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v16)</sup> Of the gold, the silver, and the brass, and the iron, there is no number. Arise therefore, and be doing, and the Lord be with thee.

- In his trouble David prepared for the house of the Lord a hundred thousand talents of gold and a thousand thousand talents of silver, and brass and iron without weight, and timber and stone in abundance, telling Solomon he might add thereto. Without weight means they gave in such abundance they were not counting it.
- There were workmen with him in abundance, hewers and workers of stone and timber and all manner of cunning men for every kind of work. David told Solomon to arise and be doing, and the Lord be with him.

**[1 Chronicles 22:17-19](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v17)</sup> David also commanded all the princes of Israel to help Solomon his son, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v18)</sup> Is not the Lord your God with you? and hath he not given you rest on every side? for he hath given the inhabitants of the land into mine hand; and the land is subdued before the Lord, and before his people.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/22#v19)</sup> Now set your heart and your soul to seek the Lord your God; arise therefore, and build ye the sanctuary of the Lord God, to bring the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and the holy vessels of God, into the house that is to be built to the name of the Lord.

- David commanded all the princes of Israel to help Solomon his son, asking whether the Lord their God was not with them and had not given them rest on every side, for he had given the inhabitants of the land into David's hand and the land was subdued before the Lord. This is what David was there to do: he subdued all the nations by the power of the Most High.
- He charged them to set their heart and soul to seek the Lord their God, to arise and build the sanctuary of the Lord God, and to bring the ark of the covenant and the holy vessels of God into the house that was to be built. Earlier David had moved the ark from place to place, and that is when he desired to build a house for it before the Lord.
