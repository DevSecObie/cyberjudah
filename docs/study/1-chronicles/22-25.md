## Introduction

This session covers 1 Chronicles chapters 22 through 25. It is a brief but important reading, heavy with names, offices, and order. David, now old and full of days, cannot build the temple himself because he has shed much blood in war, so he prepares the materials in abundance and charges his son Solomon to build the house unto the name of the Most High. David then sets the Levites in order, dividing them for the work of the house, numbers the priests into their courses by lot, and appoints the singers and musicians for the service of the sanctuary. The running thread is order and organization: keeping the commandments to prosper, and everyone in Israel knowing his place and his service before the Most High.

# 1 Chronicles 23: The Numbering and Ordering of the Levites

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 23](/bible/1-chronicles/23)

## Introduction

David, old and full of days, makes Solomon king over Israel and gathers the princes, the priests, and the Levites. The Levites are numbered and divided for the different tasks of the house of the Lord. This chapter shows the order and organization of the Levites, the genealogies of their families, and the duties assigned to them for the service of the sanctuary.

# 1 Chronicles 25: The Singers and Musicians of the Sanctuary

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 25](/bible/1-chronicles/25)

## Introduction

This chapter appoints the singers and musicians for the service of the house of the Lord, the sons of Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun, who prophesied with harps, psalteries, and cymbals. It teaches that music is a form of prophecy, and it shows the same order and organization seen throughout these chapters, the musicians divided by lot into twenty and four courses.

**[1 Chronicles 25:1-4](/bible/1-chronicles/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/25#v1)</sup> Moreover David and the captains of the host separated to the service of the sons of Asaph, and of Heman, and of Jeduthun, who should prophesy with harps, with psalteries, and with cymbals: and the number of the workmen according to their service was:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/25#v2)</sup> Of the sons of Asaph; Zaccur, and Joseph, and Nethaniah, and Asarelah, the sons of Asaph under the hands of Asaph, which prophesied according to the order of the king.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/25#v3)</sup> Of Jeduthun: the sons of Jeduthun; Gedaliah, and Zeri, and Jeshaiah, Hashabiah, and Mattithiah, six, under the hands of their father Jeduthun, who prophesied with a harp, to give thanks and to praise the Lord.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/25#v4)</sup> Of Heman: the sons of Heman; Bukkiah, Mattaniah, Uzziel, Shebuel, and Jerimoth, Hananiah, Hanani, Eliathah, Giddalti, and Romamti–ezer, Joshbekashah, Mallothi, Hothir, and Mahazioth:

- The sons of Asaph prophesied according to the order of the king, the sons of Jeduthun prophesied with a harp to give thanks and to praise the Lord, and the sons of Heman were given by God to lift up the horn, for God gave Heman fourteen sons and three daughters.

    Miriam the prophetess took a timbrel in her hand, and all the women went out after her with timbrels and with dances, showing that music is a form of prophecy.
