Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 10](/bible/1-chronicles/10)

Chronicles is written from the standpoint of the southern kingdom, whose registry was kept in Jerusalem. After the name chapters, First Chronicles becomes a recap of First Samuel, retelling the life, troubles, and rise of David. The name chapters are not tedious filler; they were written for a reason. When Israel returned from captivity a registry was kept so that it could be known who was a priest, what tribe a man came from, and where he could be, because each tribe had its own alignment and lane. Read everything and do not skip. This session covers the death of Saul, the anointing of David, his mighty men, the gathering of the tribes, and the moving of the ark. First Chronicles 10 parallels First Samuel 31 directly.

**[1 Chronicles 10:1-7](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v1)</sup> Now the Philistines fought against Israel; and the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines, and fell down slain in mount Gilboa.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v2)</sup> And the Philistines followed hard after Saul, and after his sons; and the Philistines slew Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchi–shua, the sons of Saul.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v3)</sup> And the battle went sore against Saul, and the archers hit him, and he was wounded of the archers.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v4)</sup> Then said Saul to his armourbearer, Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith; lest these uncircumcised come and abuse me. But his armourbearer would not; for he was sore afraid. So Saul took a sword, and fell upon it.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v5)</sup> And when his armourbearer saw that Saul was dead, he fell likewise on the sword, and died.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v6)</sup> So Saul died, and his three sons, and all his house died together.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v7)</sup> And when all the men of Israel that were in the valley saw that they fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, then they forsook their cities, and fled: and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.

- The Philistines fought Israel, the men of Israel fled and fell slain in mount Gilboa, and the Philistines killed Jonathan, Abinadab, and Malchishua, the sons of Saul.
- The battle went hard against Saul, the archers wounded him, and he asked his armourbearer to run him through so the uncircumcised would not abuse him. The armourbearer was too afraid, so Saul fell on his own sword and died.
- Saul died, his three sons, and all his house together, and when Israel saw it they forsook their cities and fled, and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.

**[1 Chronicles 10:8-10](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v8)</sup> And it came to pass on the morrow, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, that they found Saul and his sons fallen in mount Gilboa.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v9)</sup> And when they had stripped him, they took his head, and his armour, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to carry tidings unto their idols, and to the people.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v10)</sup> And they put his armour in the house of their gods, and fastened his head in the temple of Dagon.

- The next day the Philistines came to strip the slain, found Saul and his sons fallen in Gilboa, took his head and his armour, and sent word throughout their land to carry tidings to their idols and to the people.
- They put his armour in the house of their gods and fastened his head in the temple of Dagon. Dagon was the fish god, and its likeness is carried over into false religion in the fish-shaped mitre and the fish symbol.

**[1 Chronicles 10:11-12](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v11)</sup> And when all Jabesh–gilead heard all that the Philistines had done to Saul,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v12)</sup> They arose, all the valiant men, and took away the body of Saul, and the bodies of his sons, and brought them to Jabesh, and buried their bones under the oak in Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

- When Jabesh-gilead heard what the Philistines had done to Saul, all their valiant men arose, took the bodies of Saul and his sons, brought them to Jabesh, buried their bones under the oak, and fasted seven days.
- It has always been in Israel to mourn our dead. No matter what a person has done, it does not sit right with us to see our brothers put to death. This is why we pray for all Israel to recover themselves.

**[1 Chronicles 10:13-14](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v13)</sup> So Saul died for his transgression which he committed against the Lord, even against the word of the Lord, which he kept not, and also for asking counsel of one that had a familiar spirit, to enquire of it;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/10#v14)</sup> And enquired not of the Lord: therefore he slew him, and turned the kingdom unto David the son of Jesse.

- Saul died for his transgression against the Most High, for not keeping His word, and for asking counsel of one that had a familiar spirit rather than inquiring of the Most High. Therefore the Most High slew him and turned the kingdom to David the son of Jesse.
- Saul had gone to a woman with a familiar spirit to bring up Samuel. The left hand of the Most High is real. People who meddle with that get entangled and get spirits put on them. It is not our custom.
- The last straw for Saul was seeking that familiar spirit. Through Samuel the Most High told him he would join him the next day. The difference is that Samuel died righteously.
