Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 2](/bible/1-chronicles/2)

The reading continues in 1 Chronicles, the book of our records. Chronicles is our history, the record of our forefathers set down by name. This chapter names the twelve sons of Israel and then follows the line of Judah down through Perez, on through Hezron, Caleb, and Jerahmeel, and toward the house of David. The reading is heavy with names, and the teaching held that every name here has significance and belongs to our forefathers. Where a stretch of names was read without added exposition, the main entries below follow the verse order and hold the record as it stands. The framing set at the start is that it is an honor and a privilege to be known as the sons of God, for we are the descendants of these men, and we are told to inquire of the former age and prepare ourselves for the search of our fathers.

>

- The twelve sons of Israel are set down: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.
- These are the same sons of Jacob that came into Egypt, and we carry their name, their lineage, and their descent from the twelve tribes of Israel.
- The nations call us a minority and divide us by many labels so they can deal wisely with us, but we are not a minority, and the Most High promised to multiply our seed like the stars of heaven and the sand of the sea.

    The children of Israel that came into Egypt with Jacob are named, the same sons listed here.

    It is a great love and an honor that we should be called the sons of God, though the world knows us not.
  - **[Job 8:8](/bible/job/8#v8)**

**[1 Chronicles 2:3-4](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v3)</sup> The sons of Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah: which three were born unto him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the sight of the Lord; and he slew him.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v4)</sup> And Tamar his daughter in law bare him Pharez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five.

- The sons of Judah are named: Er, Onan, and Shelah, born to him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess, and then Perez and Zerah, born to him of Tamar his daughter-in-law.
- Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the sight of the Most High, and he slew him, just as Cain was evil and is passed over in the genealogy of the sons of God.
- This is why the counsel is to desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, for an evil son is not counted in the record.

- Boaz means strength, and Solomon named one of the pillars of the temple Boaz after this man.

# 1 Chronicles 3: The Sons of David and the Kings of Judah

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 3](/bible/1-chronicles/3)

This chapter narrows the record of Judah to the house of David: the sons born to him in Hebron, the sons born to him in Jerusalem, and then the kings that came from Solomon down through the captivity and the sons of Jeconiah after it. This is the line of our kings, kept by name.

**[1 Chronicles 3:1-4](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v1)</sup> Now these were the sons of David, which were born unto him in Hebron; the firstborn Amnon, of Ahinoam the Jezreelitess; the second Daniel, of Abigail the Carmelitess:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v2)</sup> The third, Absalom the son of Maachah the daughter of Talmai king of Geshur: the fourth, Adonijah the son of Haggith:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v3)</sup> The fifth, Shephatiah of Abital: the sixth, Ithream by Eglah his wife.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/3#v4)</sup> These six were born unto him in Hebron; and there he reigned seven years and six months: and in Jerusalem he reigned thirty and three years.

- The sons born to David in Hebron are named, from Amnon the firstborn to the sixth, with their mothers.
- Amnon was the son who defiled his sister Tamar, and Absalom, named here among the younger sons, later avenged her and paid for it with his own life.
- David reigned in Hebron seven years and six months before he reigned in Jerusalem.

# 1 Chronicles 4: The Families of Judah and Simeon

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 4](/bible/1-chronicles/4)

This chapter gathers more of the families of Judah, including the prayer of Jabez, and then turns to the sons of Simeon, their dwellings, and their conquests. The teaching drew out that the blessing rested heavily on Judah, who multiplied above his brethren, and that the tribe of Simeon took land that had belonged to the sons of Ham.

- The sons of Judah and the families that came from them are set down, including the line of Hur, the firstborn of Ephratah, the father of Bethlehem.
- The names here carry meaning, and the record keeps the families in order down to the sons of Koz.

**[1 Chronicles 4:9-10](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/4#v9)</sup> And Jabez was more honourable than his brethren: and his mother called his name Jabez, saying, Because I bare him with sorrow.

- Jabez was more honorable than his brethren, and his mother called his name Jabez because she bare him with sorrow, the meaning of the name matching the scripture itself.
- Jabez called on the God of Israel and asked that He would bless him indeed, enlarge his coast, keep His hand with him, and keep him from evil, and the Most High granted him what he requested.

# 1 Chronicles 5: The Tribes East of Jordan and Their Captivity

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 5](/bible/1-chronicles/5)

This chapter records the eastern tribes: Reuben, Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh. It sets down why Reuben lost the birthright, names the families and their dwellings, tells of their victory over the Hagarites because they trusted in the Most High, and closes with their captivity because they went a-whoring after the gods of the land.

**[1 Chronicles 5:1-10](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)</sup> Now the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel, (for he was the firstborn; but, forasmuch as he defiled his father’s bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph the son of Israel: and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v2)</sup> For Judah prevailed above his brethren, and of him came the chief ruler; but the birthright was Joseph’s:)
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v3)</sup> The sons, I say, of Reuben the firstborn of Israel were, Hanoch, and Pallu, Hezron, and Carmi.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v4)</sup> The sons of Joel; Shemaiah his son, Gog his son, Shimei his son,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v5)</sup> Micah his son, Reaia his son, Baal his son,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v6)</sup> Beerah his son, whom Tilgath–pilneser king of Assyria carried away captive: he was prince of the Reubenites.
>

>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v8)</sup> And Bela the son of Azaz, the son of Shema, the son of Joel, who dwelt in Aroer, even unto Nebo and Baal–meon:

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v9)</sup> And eastward he inhabited unto the entering in of the wilderness from the river Euphrates: because their cattle were multiplied in the land of Gilead.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v10)</sup> And in the days of Saul they made war with the Hagarites, who fell by their hand: and they dwelt in their tents throughout all the east land of Gilead.

- Reuben was the firstborn of Israel, but because he defiled his father's bed his birthright was given to the sons of Joseph, and the genealogy is not reckoned after the birthright.
- Judah prevailed above his brethren, and of him came the chief ruler, though the birthright was Joseph's, much as Esau came out first but did not keep the place of the heir.
- The sons and dwellings of Reuben are named down to Beerah, whom Tilgath-pilneser king of Assyria carried away captive.

- They took away much cattle and a hundred thousand captives, for the war was of the Most High; this is the curse of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) falling on the enemy instead of on us.
