**[1 Chronicles 17:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass, as David sat in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, Lo, I dwell in an house of cedars, but the ark of the covenant of the Lord remaineth under curtains.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v2)</sup> Then Nathan said unto David, Do all that is in thine heart; for God is with thee.

- David told Nathan the prophet that he dwelt in a house of cedars while the ark remained under curtains.
- Nathan first answered that David should do all in his heart, for the Most High was with him.

**[1 Chronicles 17:3-6](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v3)</sup> And it came to pass the same night, that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v4)</sup> Go and tell David my servant, Thus saith the Lord, Thou shalt not build me an house to dwell in:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v5)</sup> For I have not dwelt in an house since the day that I brought up Israel unto this day; but have gone from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v6)</sup> Wheresoever I have walked with all Israel, spake I a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why have ye not built me an house of cedars?

- That same night the word of the Most High came to Nathan, saying David should not build him a house to dwell in.
- The Most High had gone from tent to tent since bringing Israel up, and had never asked any judge why they had not built him a house of cedar.

**[1 Chronicles 17:7-10](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v7)</sup> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, even from following the sheep, that thou shouldest be ruler over my people Israel:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v8)</sup> And I have been with thee whithersoever thou hast walked, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee, and have made thee a name like the name of the great men that are in the earth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v9)</sup> Also I will ordain a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell in their place, and shall be moved no more; neither shall the children of wickedness waste them any more, as at the beginning,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v10)</sup> And since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. Moreover I will subdue all thine enemies. Furthermore I tell thee that the Lord will build thee an house.

- The Most High took David from following the sheep to be ruler over Israel, was with him wherever he went, and cut off his enemies.
- He promised to make David a name like the great men of the earth, to plant Israel in their own place, and to subdue all David's enemies.

**[1 Chronicles 17:11-14](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v11)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when thy days be expired that thou must go to be with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed after thee, which shall be of thy sons; and I will establish his kingdom.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v12)</sup> He shall build me an house, and I will stablish his throne for ever.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v13)</sup> I will be his father, and he shall be my son: and I will not take my mercy away from him, as I took it from him that was before thee:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v14)</sup> But I will settle him in mine house and in my kingdom for ever: and his throne shall be established for evermore.

- Instead of David building him a house, the Most High declared he would build David a house through his seed after him.
- That son, Solomon, would build the house, and his throne would be established forever; the Most High would not take his mercy from him as he took it from Saul.

**[1 Chronicles 17:15-19](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v15)</sup> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v16)</sup> And David the king came and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord God, and what is mine house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v17)</sup> And yet this was a small thing in thine eyes, O God; for thou hast also spoken of thy servant’s house for a great while to come, and hast regarded me according to the estate of a man of high degree, O Lord God.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v18)</sup> What can David speak more to thee for the honour of thy servant? for thou knowest thy servant.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v19)</sup> O Lord, for thy servant’s sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all this greatness, in making known all these great things.

- Nathan spoke all these words to David, who sat before the Most High and asked who he was that he should be brought this far.
- David marveled that the Most High regarded him as a man of high degree and did such greatness for his servant's sake.

**[1 Chronicles 17:20-22](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v20)</sup> O Lord, there is none like thee, neither is there any God beside thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v21)</sup> And what one nation in the earth is like thy people Israel, whom God went to redeem to be his own people, to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness, by driving out nations from before thy people, whom thou hast redeemed out of Egypt?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v22)</sup> For thy people Israel didst thou make thine own people for ever; and thou, Lord, becamest their God.

- David confessed there is none like the Most High and no god beside him, and that no nation is like Israel, whom he went to redeem to be his own people forever.
- He recalled the redemption out of Egypt where the Most High made his name great and terrible by driving out nations; Israel is his own people forever and he became their God.

**[1 Chronicles 17:23-27](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v23)</sup> Therefore now, Lord, let the thing that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant and concerning his house be established for ever, and do as thou hast said.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v24)</sup> Let it even be established, that thy name may be magnified for ever, saying, The Lord of hosts is the God of Israel, even a God to Israel: and let the house of David thy servant be established before thee.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v25)</sup> For thou, O my God, hast told thy servant that thou wilt build him an house: therefore thy servant hath found in his heart to pray before thee.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v26)</sup> And now, Lord, thou art God, and hast promised this goodness unto thy servant:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v27)</sup> Now therefore let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may be before thee for ever: for thou blessest, O Lord, and it shall be blessed for ever.

- David prayed that the thing spoken concerning his house be established forever, that the Most High's name be magnified.
- He asked that the house of his servant be blessed to continue before the Most High forever, for what the Most High blesses shall be blessed forever.
