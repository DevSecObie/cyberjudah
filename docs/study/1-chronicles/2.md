Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 2](/bible/1-chronicles/2)

The reading continues in 1 Chronicles, the book of our records. Chronicles is our history, the record of our forefathers set down by name. This chapter names the twelve sons of Israel and then follows the line of Judah down through Perez, on through Hezron, Caleb, and Jerahmeel, and toward the house of David. The reading is heavy with names, and the teaching held that every name here has significance and belongs to our forefathers. Where a stretch of names was read without added exposition, the main entries below follow the verse order and hold the record as it stands. The framing set at the start is that it is an honor and a privilege to be known as the sons of God, for we are the descendants of these men, and we are told to inquire of the former age and prepare ourselves for the search of our fathers.

**[1 Chronicles 2:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v1)</sup> These are the sons of Israel; Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v2)</sup> Dan, Joseph, and Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.

- The twelve sons of Israel are set down: Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Dan, Joseph, Benjamin, Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.
- These are the same sons of Jacob that came into Egypt, and we carry their name, their lineage, and their descent from the twelve tribes of Israel.
- The nations call us a minority and divide us by many labels so they can deal wisely with us, but we are not a minority, and the Most High promised to multiply our seed like the stars of heaven and the sand of the sea.
  - **[Exodus 1:1](/bible/exodus/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/1#v1)</sup> Now these are the names of the children of Israel, which came into Egypt; every man and his household came with Jacob.

    The children of Israel that came into Egypt with Jacob are named, the same sons listed here.
  - **[1 John 3:1](/bible/1-john/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/3#v1)</sup> Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not.

    It is a great love and an honor that we should be called the sons of God, though the world knows us not.
  - **[Job 8:8](/bible/job/8#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/job/8#v8)</sup> For enquire, I pray thee, of the former age, and prepare thyself to the search of their fathers:

    Inquire of the former age and prepare thyself to the search of thy fathers, for it matters whose son you are.

**[1 Chronicles 2:3-4](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v3)</sup> The sons of Judah; Er, and Onan, and Shelah: which three were born unto him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess. And Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the sight of the Lord; and he slew him.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v4)</sup> And Tamar his daughter in law bare him Pharez and Zerah. All the sons of Judah were five.

- The sons of Judah are named: Er, Onan, and Shelah, born to him of the daughter of Shua the Canaanitess, and then Perez and Zerah, born to him of Tamar his daughter-in-law.
- Er, the firstborn of Judah, was evil in the sight of the Most High, and he slew him, just as Cain was evil and is passed over in the genealogy of the sons of God.
- This is why the counsel is to desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, for an evil son is not counted in the record.
  - **[Genesis 38:2](/bible/genesis/38#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/38#v2)</sup> And Judah saw there a daughter of a certain Canaanite, whose name was Shuah; and he took her, and went in unto her.

    Judah took the daughter of Shua the Canaanite, who bare him Er, Onan, and Shelah.

**[1 Chronicles 2:5-8](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v5)</sup> The sons of Pharez; Hezron, and Hamul.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v6)</sup> And the sons of Zerah; Zimri, and Ethan, and Heman, and Calcol, and Dara: five of them in all.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v7)</sup> And the sons of Carmi; Achar, the troubler of Israel, who transgressed in the thing accursed.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v8)</sup> And the sons of Ethan; Azariah.

- The line runs from Perez through his sons Hezron and Hamul, and the sons of Zerah are named, five in all.
- Achar, called the troubler of Israel, is set in the record as the one who transgressed in the accursed thing, a warning kept alongside the names.

**[1 Chronicles 2:9-12](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v9)</sup> The sons also of Hezron, that were born unto him; Jerahmeel, and Ram, and Chelubai.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v10)</sup> And Ram begat Amminadab; and Amminadab begat Nahshon, prince of the children of Judah;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v11)</sup> And Nahshon begat Salma, and Salma begat Boaz,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v12)</sup> And Boaz begat Obed, and Obed begat Jesse,

- The sons of Hezron are named, and the line is carried through Ram down to Amminadab, Nahshon, Salmon, Boaz, Obed, and Jesse.
- The names in this stretch carry meaning and are worth studying, for a name should be lived up to; a man named Abraham who lives as Judas has not honored the name he chose.
- Boaz means strength, and Solomon named one of the pillars of the temple Boaz after this man.

**[1 Chronicles 2:13-17](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v13)</sup> And Jesse begat his firstborn Eliab, and Abinadab the second, and Shimma the third,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v14)</sup> Nethaneel the fourth, Raddai the fifth,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v15)</sup> Ozem the sixth, David the seventh:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v16)</sup> Whose sisters were Zeruiah, and Abigail. And the sons of Zeruiah; Abishai, and Joab, and Asahel, three.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v17)</sup> And Abigail bare Amasa: and the father of Amasa was Jether the Ishmeelite.

- Jesse's sons are set down in order, with David named the seventh, and his sisters Zeruiah and Abigail.
- The sons of Zeruiah, Abishai, Joab, and Asahel, are named, and Amasa the son of Abigail, whose father was Jether the Ishmaelite.
- This is the record of David's own house, our king and his kinsmen kept by name.

**[1 Chronicles 2:18-24](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v18)</sup> And Caleb the son of Hezron begat children of Azubah his wife, and of Jerioth: her sons are these; Jesher, and Shobab, and Ardon.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v19)</sup> And when Azubah was dead, Caleb took unto him Ephrath, which bare him Hur.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v20)</sup> And Hur begat Uri, and Uri begat Bezaleel.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v21)</sup> And afterward Hezron went in to the daughter of Machir the father of Gilead, whom he married when he was threescore years old; and she bare him Segub.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v22)</sup> And Segub begat Jair, who had three and twenty cities in the land of Gilead.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v23)</sup> And he took Geshur, and Aram, with the towns of Jair, from them, with Kenath, and the towns thereof, even threescore cities. All these belonged to the sons of Machir the father of Gilead.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v24)</sup> And after that Hezron was dead in Caleb–ephratah, then Abiah Hezron’s wife bare him Ashur the father of Tekoa.

- The line of Caleb the son of Hezron is set down through his wives and sons, including Hur, Uri, and Bezaleel.
- The record follows Hezron into Gilead and names Segub and Jair, who had three and twenty cities in the land of Gilead.

**[1 Chronicles 2:25-33](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v25)</sup> And the sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron were, Ram the firstborn, and Bunah, and Oren, and Ozem, and Ahijah.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v26)</sup> Jerahmeel had also another wife, whose name was Atarah; she was the mother of Onam.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v27)</sup> And the sons of Ram the firstborn of Jerahmeel were, Maaz, and Jamin, and Eker.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v28)</sup> And the sons of Onam were, Shammai, and Jada. And the sons of Shammai; Nadab, and Abishur.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v29)</sup> And the name of the wife of Abishur was Abihail, and she bare him Ahban, and Molid.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v30)</sup> And the sons of Nadab; Seled, and Appaim: but Seled died without children.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v31)</sup> And the sons of Appaim; Ishi. And the sons of Ishi; Sheshan. And the children of Sheshan; Ahlai.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v32)</sup> And the sons of Jada the brother of Shammai; Jether, and Jonathan: and Jether died without children.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v33)</sup> And the sons of Jonathan; Peleth, and Zaza. These were the sons of Jerahmeel.

- The sons of Jerahmeel the firstborn of Hezron are named through his wives, including Ram, Onam, Shammai, and Jada.
- The record notes that Seled died without children and Jether died without children, keeping account of the branches that did not continue.
- Names in this line carry meaning, as when the Most High named the children of Hosea to declare His judgment on the house of Israel.
  - **[Hosea 1:4](/bible/hosea/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/1#v4)</sup> And the Lord said unto him, Call his name Jezreel; for yet a little while, and I will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu, and will cause to cease the kingdom of the house of Israel.

    The Most High names Jezreel to declare He will avenge the blood of Jezreel upon the house of Jehu.
  - **[Hosea 1:6](/bible/hosea/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/1#v6)</sup> And she conceived again, and bare a daughter. And God said unto him, Call her name Lo–ruhamah: for I will no more have mercy upon the house of Israel; but I will utterly take them away.

    The Most High names Lo-ruhamah, for He will no more have mercy upon the house of Israel.

> <sup>[37](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v37)</sup> And Zabad begat Ephlal, and Ephlal begat Obed,

>
> <sup>[54](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v54)</sup> The sons of Salma; Beth–lehem, and the Netophathites, Ataroth, the house of Joab, and half of the Manahethites, the Zorites.
>
> <sup>[55](/bible/1-chronicles/2#v55)</sup> And the families of the scribes which dwelt at Jabez; the Tirathites, the Shimeathites, and Suchathites. These are the Kenites that came of Hemath, the father of the house of Rechab.

- The sons of Caleb the brother of Jerahmeel are set down, with the families that came from them and the towns they fathered.
- The firstborn is named as the heir, the one who inherits and receives the blessing, and the record closes with the families of the scribes that dwelt at Jabez and the Kenites of the house of Rechab.
- Every one of these names carries significance; two picked at random were found to mean father of salvation and hearing and obeying, and much of this line appears in the lineage of Christ.
