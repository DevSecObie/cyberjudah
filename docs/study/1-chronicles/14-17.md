Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 14](/bible/1-chronicles/14)

This session covers [1 Chronicles 14](/bible/1-chronicles/14) through 17: David confirmed as king, his two battles with the Philistines fought under the Most High's instruction, the ordered bringing up of the ark of the covenant into the city of David by the Levites, the song of thanksgiving delivered into the hand of Asaph, and the promise of an everlasting throne through David's seed. Throughout these chapters the pattern is the same: the righteous forefathers went to the Most High before they acted, and everything concerning the ark and the priesthood was done according to a set order that no man could break.

**[1 Chronicles 14:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v1)</sup> Now Hiram king of Tyre sent messengers to David, and timber of cedars, with masons and carpenters, to build him an house.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v2)</sup> And David perceived that the Lord had confirmed him king over Israel, for his kingdom was lifted up on high, because of his people Israel.

- Hiram king of Tyre sent workmen and cedars to build David a house, and David recognized that the Most High had confirmed him king over Israel.
- His kingdom was lifted up on high for the sake of the Most High's people Israel, not for David's own sake.

**[1 Chronicles 14:8-10](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v8)</sup> And when the Philistines heard that David was anointed king over all Israel, all the Philistines went up to seek David. And David heard of it, and went out against them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v9)</sup> And the Philistines came and spread themselves in the valley of Rephaim.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v10)</sup> And David enquired of God, saying, Shall I go up against the Philistines? and wilt thou deliver them into mine hand? And the Lord said unto him, Go up; for I will deliver them into thine hand.

- When the Philistines heard David was anointed king over all Israel they went up to seek him, and David went out against them.
- David inquired of the Most High before the battle, asking whether he should go up and whether victory would be given. This is the mark of the righteous forefathers: they took their battles to the Most High first.
- The Most High answered him plainly, promising to deliver the Philistines into his hand.

**[1 Chronicles 14:11-12](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v11)</sup> So they came up to Baal–perazim; and David smote them there. Then David said, God hath broken in upon mine enemies by mine hand like the breaking forth of waters: therefore they called the name of that place Baal–perazim.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v12)</sup> And when they had left their gods there, David gave a commandment, and they were burned with fire.

- David defeated them at Baalperazim and named the place for how the Most High had broken through his enemies like a bursting of waters.
- The Philistine gods left there were burned with fire.
  - **[Deuteronomy 7:25](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v25)</sup> The graven images of their gods shall ye burn with fire: thou shalt not desire the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it unto thee, lest thou be snared therein: for it is an abomination to the Lord thy God.

    Their graven images are to be burned so they do not become a snare unto us.

**[1 Chronicles 14:13-16](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v13)</sup> And the Philistines yet again spread themselves abroad in the valley.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v14)</sup> Therefore David enquired again of God; and God said unto him, Go not up after them; turn away from them, and come upon them over against the mulberry trees.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v15)</sup> And it shall be, when thou shalt hear a sound of going in the tops of the mulberry trees, that then thou shalt go out to battle: for God is gone forth before thee to smite the host of the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v16)</sup> David therefore did as God commanded him: and they smote the host of the Philistines from Gibeon even to Gazer.

- The Philistines came up a second time, and David inquired again rather than presuming on the first victory.
- This time the Most High said not to go straight up but to circle behind them by the mulberry trees, and to move only when he heard a sound of going in the treetops, for the Most High had gone before to smite the host.
- David did exactly as commanded and smote them from Gibeon to Gazer. The lesson is precise obedience: if he says do not go up, do not go up; if he says go this way, do not go that way.
  - **[Exodus 15:3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)</sup> The Lord is a man of war: the Lord is his name.

    The Most High is a man of war and knows exactly how to handle the battle.
  - **[1 Samuel 15:22-23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/1-samuel/15#v22)</sup> And Samuel said, Hath the Lord as great delight in burnt offerings and sacrifices, as in obeying the voice of the Lord? Behold, to obey is better than sacrifice, and to hearken than the fat of rams.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)</sup> For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

    Saul was told to overcome nations and did not, and was told not to go to his last battle yet went and died there; obedience is better than sacrifice.

**[1 Chronicles 14:17](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/14#v17)</sup> And the fame of David went out into all lands; and the Lord brought the fear of him upon all nations.

- The fame of David went out into all lands and the Most High brought the fear of him upon all nations.

# 1 Chronicles 15: None Ought to Carry the Ark but the Levites

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 15](/bible/1-chronicles/15)

**[1 Chronicles 15:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v1)</sup> And David made him houses in the city of David, and prepared a place for the ark of God, and pitched for it a tent.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v2)</sup> Then David said, None ought to carry the ark of God but the Levites: for them hath the Lord chosen to carry the ark of God, and to minister unto him for ever.

- David prepared a place and pitched a tent for the ark in the city of David.
- He declared that none ought to carry the ark but the Levites, for the Most High had chosen them to carry it and to minister unto him forever.
  - **[Numbers 8:19](/bible/numbers/8#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/8#v19)</sup> And I have given the Levites as a gift to Aaron and to his sons from among the children of Israel, to do the service of the children of Israel in the tabernacle of the congregation, and to make an atonement for the children of Israel: that there be no plague among the children of Israel, when the children of Israel come nigh unto the sanctuary.

    The Levites were given to Aaron to do the service of the tabernacle and were set apart to this office.
  - **[1 Chronicles 13:9-10](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v9)</sup> And when they came unto the threshingfloor of Chidon, Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark; for the oxen stumbled.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v10)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was kindled against Uzza, and he smote him, because he put his hand to the ark: and there he died before God.

    Uzza handled the ark when he was not to and died for it, showing why this order matters.

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v8)</sup> Of the sons of Elizaphan; Shemaiah the chief, and his brethren two hundred:

- David assembled all Israel and the chief Levites by their families, naming the sons of Kohath, Merari, Gershom, Elizaphan, Hebron, and Uzziel with their chiefs and numbers.
- He called for the priests Zadok and Abiathar and for the Levites, showing the Most High had set a clear structure among the priesthood.

**[1 Chronicles 15:12-15](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v12)</sup> And said unto them, Ye are the chief of the fathers of the Levites: sanctify yourselves, both ye and your brethren, that ye may bring up the ark of the Lord God of Israel unto the place that I have prepared for it.

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v13)</sup> For because ye did it not at the first, the Lord our God made a breach upon us, for that we sought him not after the due order.

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v15)</sup> And the children of the Levites bare the ark of God upon their shoulders with the staves thereon, as Moses commanded according to the word of the Lord.

- David charged the chief fathers of the Levites to sanctify themselves before bringing up the ark, because the first attempt failed when they did not seek him after the due order.
- The Levites bore the ark upon their shoulders with the staves, as Moses commanded according to the word of the Most High.
  - **[Exodus 25:14-15](/bible/exodus/25#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/25#v14)</sup> And thou shalt put the staves into the rings by the sides of the ark, that the ark may be borne with them.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/25#v15)</sup> The staves shall be in the rings of the ark: they shall not be taken from it.

    The staves are set in the rings so the ark is borne, and the staves are not to be taken out.
  - **[Leviticus 15:31](/bible/leviticus/15#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/leviticus/15#v31)</sup> Thus shall ye separate the children of Israel from their uncleanness; that they die not in their uncleanness, when they defile my tabernacle that is among them.

    The priests could not deal with holy things while unclean, so they had to be clean before serving.

- David appointed Levites as singers with psalteries, harps, and cymbals, to lift up the voice with joy.
- Heman, Asaph, and Ethan were appointed to sound cymbals of brass; others played harps and psalteries; the priests blew the trumpets before the ark, and doorkeepers were set. Every rank had its assigned place.

**[1 Chronicles 15:25-27](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v25)</sup> So David, and the elders of Israel, and the captains over thousands, went to bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord out of the house of Obed–edom with joy.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v26)</sup> And it came to pass, when God helped the Levites that bare the ark of the covenant of the Lord, that they offered seven bullocks and seven rams.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v27)</sup> And David was clothed with a robe of fine linen, and all the Levites that bare the ark, and the singers, and Chenaniah the master of the song with the singers: David also had upon him an ephod of linen.

- David and the elders and captains went to bring up the ark from the house of Obededom with joy.
- When the Most High helped the Levites bearing the ark, they offered seven bullocks and seven rams.
- David was clothed in a robe of fine linen along with the Levites and singers, and wore an ephod of linen. The people were dressed well, and the same custom carried down through Solomon.

**[1 Chronicles 15:28-29](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v28)</sup> Thus all Israel brought up the ark of the covenant of the Lord with shouting, and with sound of the cornet, and with trumpets, and with cymbals, making a noise with psalteries and harps.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/15#v29)</sup> And it came to pass, as the ark of the covenant of the Lord came to the city of David, that Michal the daughter of Saul looking out at a window saw king David dancing and playing: and she despised him in her heart.

- All Israel brought up the ark with shouting, cornet, trumpets, cymbals, psalteries, and harps.
- Michal the daughter of Saul looked out the window and despised David in her heart as he danced and played. In the midst of pure righteousness she was out of the spirit, hating him instead of rejoicing.

# 1 Chronicles 16: The Song Delivered into the Hand of Asaph

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 16](/bible/1-chronicles/16)

**[1 Chronicles 16:1-3](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v1)</sup> So they brought the ark of God, and set it in the midst of the tent that David had pitched for it: and they offered burnt sacrifices and peace offerings before God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v2)</sup> And when David had made an end of offering the burnt offerings and the peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v3)</sup> And he dealt to every one of Israel, both man and woman, to every one a loaf of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.

- The ark was set in the midst of the tent David pitched, and burnt and peace offerings were made before the Most High.
- David blessed the people in the name of the Most High and dealt to every man and woman a loaf of bread, a piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine.
  - **[2 Samuel 6:19](/bible/2-samuel/6#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/2-samuel/6#v19)</sup> And he dealt among all the people, even among the whole multitude of Israel, as well to the women as men, to every one a cake of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine. So all the people departed every one to his house.

    The shorter account records the same gifts of bread, flesh, and wine to all the people.

**[1 Chronicles 16:4-6](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v4)</sup> And he appointed certain of the Levites to minister before the ark of the Lord, and to record, and to thank and praise the Lord God of Israel:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v5)</sup> Asaph the chief, and next to him Zechariah, Jeiel, and Shemiramoth, and Jehiel, and Mattithiah, and Eliab, and Benaiah, and Obed–edom: and Jeiel with psalteries and with harps; but Asaph made a sound with cymbals;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v6)</sup> Benaiah also and Jahaziel the priests with trumpets continually before the ark of the covenant of God.

- David appointed certain Levites to minister before the ark, to record, thank, and praise the Most High as a daily work.
- Asaph was made chief, with named Levites playing psalteries, harps, and cymbals, and priests sounding trumpets continually. No one could take another's job or the whole order would fail.

**[1 Chronicles 16:7-13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v7)</sup> Then on that day David delivered first this psalm to thank the Lord into the hand of Asaph and his brethren.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v8)</sup> Give thanks unto the Lord, call upon his name, make known his deeds among the people.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v9)</sup> Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him, talk ye of all his wondrous works.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v10)</sup> Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v11)</sup> Seek the Lord and his strength, seek his face continually.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v12)</sup> Remember his marvellous works that he hath done, his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v13)</sup> O ye seed of Israel his servant, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

- David delivered this psalm into the hand of Asaph to thank the Most High, a prayer for the congregation much like a set brother who sends up the prayers.
- The song calls Israel to give thanks, sing, glory in his holy name, and seek his strength and face continually.
- It names them plainly as the seed of Israel his servant, the children of Jacob his chosen ones, showing these are the chosen people and it has never changed.

- The covenant was made with Abraham, sworn to Isaac, and confirmed to Jacob as an everlasting covenant, giving Israel the land of Canaan as their inheritance.

    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/105#v10)</sup> And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant:

  - **[Genesis 12:2-3](/bible/genesis/12#v2)**

**[1 Chronicles 16:19-22](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v19)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v21)</sup> He suffered no man to do them wrong: yea, he reproved kings for their sakes,

> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v22)</sup> Saying, Touch not mine anointed, and do my prophets no harm.

- When Israel were few and strangers going from nation to nation, the Most High suffered no man to do them wrong and reproved kings for their sakes.
- He warned kings to touch not his anointed and do his prophets no harm, recalling deliverances such as when Abraham went down to Egypt.

- All the gods of the people are idols, but the Most High made the heavens; strength and gladness are in his place, and all the earth is to fear before him.

- The song ends with the plea to save, gather, and deliver Israel from the heathen, and all the people said Amen and praised the Most High. This is a good prayer to meditate on.

**[1 Chronicles 16:37-43](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v37)**

# 1 Chronicles 17: The Most High Will Build David a House

Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 17](/bible/1-chronicles/17)

**[1 Chronicles 17:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass, as David sat in his house, that David said to Nathan the prophet, Lo, I dwell in an house of cedars, but the ark of the covenant of the Lord remaineth under curtains.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v2)</sup> Then Nathan said unto David, Do all that is in thine heart; for God is with thee.

- David told Nathan the prophet that he dwelt in a house of cedars while the ark remained under curtains.
- Nathan first answered that David should do all in his heart, for the Most High was with him.

**[1 Chronicles 17:3-6](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v3)</sup> And it came to pass the same night, that the word of God came to Nathan, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v4)</sup> Go and tell David my servant, Thus saith the Lord, Thou shalt not build me an house to dwell in:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v5)</sup> For I have not dwelt in an house since the day that I brought up Israel unto this day; but have gone from tent to tent, and from one tabernacle to another.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v6)</sup> Wheresoever I have walked with all Israel, spake I a word to any of the judges of Israel, whom I commanded to feed my people, saying, Why have ye not built me an house of cedars?

- That same night the word of the Most High came to Nathan, saying David should not build him a house to dwell in.
- The Most High had gone from tent to tent since bringing Israel up, and had never asked any judge why they had not built him a house of cedar.

**[1 Chronicles 17:7-10](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v7)</sup> Now therefore thus shalt thou say unto my servant David, Thus saith the Lord of hosts, I took thee from the sheepcote, even from following the sheep, that thou shouldest be ruler over my people Israel:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v8)</sup> And I have been with thee whithersoever thou hast walked, and have cut off all thine enemies from before thee, and have made thee a name like the name of the great men that are in the earth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v9)</sup> Also I will ordain a place for my people Israel, and will plant them, and they shall dwell in their place, and shall be moved no more; neither shall the children of wickedness waste them any more, as at the beginning,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v10)</sup> And since the time that I commanded judges to be over my people Israel. Moreover I will subdue all thine enemies. Furthermore I tell thee that the Lord will build thee an house.

- The Most High took David from following the sheep to be ruler over Israel, was with him wherever he went, and cut off his enemies.
- He promised to make David a name like the great men of the earth, to plant Israel in their own place, and to subdue all David's enemies.

**[1 Chronicles 17:11-14](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v11)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when thy days be expired that thou must go to be with thy fathers, that I will raise up thy seed after thee, which shall be of thy sons; and I will establish his kingdom.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v12)</sup> He shall build me an house, and I will stablish his throne for ever.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v13)</sup> I will be his father, and he shall be my son: and I will not take my mercy away from him, as I took it from him that was before thee:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v14)</sup> But I will settle him in mine house and in my kingdom for ever: and his throne shall be established for evermore.

- Instead of David building him a house, the Most High declared he would build David a house through his seed after him.
- That son, Solomon, would build the house, and his throne would be established forever; the Most High would not take his mercy from him as he took it from Saul.

**[1 Chronicles 17:15-19](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v15)</sup> According to all these words, and according to all this vision, so did Nathan speak unto David.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v16)</sup> And David the king came and sat before the Lord, and said, Who am I, O Lord God, and what is mine house, that thou hast brought me hitherto?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v17)</sup> And yet this was a small thing in thine eyes, O God; for thou hast also spoken of thy servant’s house for a great while to come, and hast regarded me according to the estate of a man of high degree, O Lord God.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v18)</sup> What can David speak more to thee for the honour of thy servant? for thou knowest thy servant.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v19)</sup> O Lord, for thy servant’s sake, and according to thine own heart, hast thou done all this greatness, in making known all these great things.

- Nathan spoke all these words to David, who sat before the Most High and asked who he was that he should be brought this far.
- David marveled that the Most High regarded him as a man of high degree and did such greatness for his servant's sake.

**[1 Chronicles 17:20-22](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v20)</sup> O Lord, there is none like thee, neither is there any God beside thee, according to all that we have heard with our ears.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v21)</sup> And what one nation in the earth is like thy people Israel, whom God went to redeem to be his own people, to make thee a name of greatness and terribleness, by driving out nations from before thy people, whom thou hast redeemed out of Egypt?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v22)</sup> For thy people Israel didst thou make thine own people for ever; and thou, Lord, becamest their God.

- David confessed there is none like the Most High and no god beside him, and that no nation is like Israel, whom he went to redeem to be his own people forever.
- He recalled the redemption out of Egypt where the Most High made his name great and terrible by driving out nations; Israel is his own people forever and he became their God.

**[1 Chronicles 17:23-27](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v23)</sup> Therefore now, Lord, let the thing that thou hast spoken concerning thy servant and concerning his house be established for ever, and do as thou hast said.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v24)</sup> Let it even be established, that thy name may be magnified for ever, saying, The Lord of hosts is the God of Israel, even a God to Israel: and let the house of David thy servant be established before thee.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v25)</sup> For thou, O my God, hast told thy servant that thou wilt build him an house: therefore thy servant hath found in his heart to pray before thee.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v26)</sup> And now, Lord, thou art God, and hast promised this goodness unto thy servant:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/17#v27)</sup> Now therefore let it please thee to bless the house of thy servant, that it may be before thee for ever: for thou blessest, O Lord, and it shall be blessed for ever.

- David prayed that the thing spoken concerning his house be established forever, that the Most High's name be magnified.
- He asked that the house of his servant be blessed to continue before the Most High forever, for what the Most High blesses shall be blessed forever.
