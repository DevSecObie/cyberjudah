Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 13](/bible/1-chronicles/13)

David consulted the leaders and gathered all Israel to bring the ark of the Most High to himself so the priests could inquire of Him, for it had been neglected in the days of Saul. The chapter also records the death of Uzza, a warning that the Most High does not play when His word is broken.

**[1 Chronicles 13:1-4](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v1)</sup> And David consulted with the captains of thousands and hundreds, and with every leader.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v2)</sup> And David said unto all the congregation of Israel, If it seem good unto you, and that it be of the Lord our God, let us send abroad unto our brethren every where, that are left in all the land of Israel, and with them also to the priests and Levites which are in their cities and suburbs, that they may gather themselves unto us:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v3)</sup> And let us bring again the ark of our God to us: for we enquired not at it in the days of Saul.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v4)</sup> And all the congregation said that they would do so: for the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.

- David consulted with the captains of thousands and hundreds and every leader, and proposed to all the congregation that they send abroad to their brethren everywhere, with the priests and Levites, to gather to them.
- His purpose was to bring again the ark of the Most High, for they had not inquired at it in the days of Saul. All the congregation agreed, for the thing was right in the eyes of all the people.

**[1 Chronicles 13:5-8](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v5)</sup> So David gathered all Israel together, from Shihor of Egypt even unto the entering of Hemath, to bring the ark of God from Kirjath–jearim.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v6)</sup> And David went up, and all Israel, to Baalah, that is, to Kirjath–jearim, which belonged to Judah, to bring up thence the ark of God the Lord, that dwelleth between the cherubims, whose name is called on it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v7)</sup> And they carried the ark of God in a new cart out of the house of Abinadab: and Uzza and Ahio drave the cart.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v8)</sup> And David and all Israel played before God with all their might, and with singing, and with harps, and with psalteries, and with timbrels, and with cymbals, and with trumpets.

- David gathered all Israel from Shihor of Egypt to the entering of Hemath to bring the ark from Kirjath-jearim, and went up to Baalah, which is Kirjath-jearim, belonging to Judah.
- They brought up the ark of the Most High, who dwelleth between the cherubims, carrying it in a new cart out of the house of Abinadab.
- David and all Israel played before the Most High with all their might, with singing, harps, psalteries, timbrels, cymbals, and trumpets, as they moved the ark to where David was so the priests could inquire.
- Do not let anyone claim the ark is in Ethiopia. The prophet Jeremiah hid the tabernacle, the ark, and the altar of incense in a cave, and it will not be found until the Most High gathers His people again.
  - **[2 Maccabees 2:5-7](/bible/2-maccabees/2#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-maccabees/2#v5)</sup> And when Jeremy came thither, he found an hollow cave, wherein he laid the tabernacle, and the ark, and the altar of incense, and so stopped the door.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-maccabees/2#v6)</sup> And some of those that followed him came to mark the way, but they could not find it.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-maccabees/2#v7)</sup> Which when Jeremy perceived, he blamed them, saying, As for that place, it shall be unknown until the time that God gather his people again together, and receive them unto mercy.

    The ark is hidden until Christ returns.

**[1 Chronicles 13:9-10](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v9)</sup> And when they came unto the threshingfloor of Chidon, Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark; for the oxen stumbled.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v10)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was kindled against Uzza, and he smote him, because he put his hand to the ark: and there he died before God.

- When they came to the threshingfloor of Chidon, the oxen stumbled and Uzza put forth his hand to hold the ark.
- The anger of the Most High was kindled against Uzza, and He smote him because he put his hand to the ark, and he died there before the Most High. The ark was only to be touched by the Levites; the Most High does not play.
  - **[Numbers 4:15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)</sup> And when Aaron and his sons have made an end of covering the sanctuary, and all the vessels of the sanctuary, as the camp is to set forward; after that, the sons of Kohath shall come to bear it: but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die. These things are the burden of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the congregation.

    The sons of Kohath were forbidden to touch the holy things lest they die; only the Levites bore the ark.

**[1 Chronicles 13:11-14](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v11)</sup> And David was displeased, because the Lord had made a breach upon Uzza: wherefore that place is called Perez–uzza to this day.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v12)</sup> And David was afraid of God that day, saying, How shall I bring the ark of God home to me?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v13)</sup> So David brought not the ark home to himself to the city of David, but carried it aside into the house of Obed–edom the Gittite.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/13#v14)</sup> And the ark of God remained with the family of Obed–edom in his house three months. And the Lord blessed the house of Obed–edom, and all that he had.

- David was displeased that the Most High had made a breach upon Uzza, and that place was called Perez-uzza. David was afraid that day and asked how he could bring the ark home.
- David carried the ark aside into the house of Obed-edom the Gittite, where it remained three months, and the Most High blessed the house of Obed-edom and all that he had.
- The ark blesses when it is with Israel and afflicts those who wrongly hold it, as when the Philistines were struck with emerods for taking it. If it falls, let it fall, and get a Levite to lift it.
  - **[1 Samuel 5:6-9](/bible/1-samuel/5#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/1-samuel/5#v6)</sup> But the hand of the Lord was heavy upon them of Ashdod, and he destroyed them, and smote them with emerods, even Ashdod and the coasts thereof.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/5#v7)</sup> And when the men of Ashdod saw that it was so, they said, The ark of the God of Israel shall not abide with us: for his hand is sore upon us, and upon Dagon our god.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-samuel/5#v8)</sup> They sent therefore and gathered all the lords of the Philistines unto them, and said, What shall we do with the ark of the God of Israel? And they answered, Let the ark of the God of Israel be carried about unto Gath. And they carried the ark of the God of Israel about thither.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/5#v9)</sup> And it was so, that, after they had carried it about, the hand of the Lord was against the city with a very great destruction: and he smote the men of the city, both small and great, and they had emerods in their secret parts.

    The Philistines were smitten with emerods for holding the ark and begged Israel to take it away.
