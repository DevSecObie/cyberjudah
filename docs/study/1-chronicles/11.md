Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 11](/bible/1-chronicles/11)

First Chronicles 11 parallels Second Samuel chapter 5. The chapter records all Israel gathering to make David king, the taking of Jerusalem, and the roll of David's mighty men. As they were mighty in the physical war, we are to be that much more mighty scripturally and spiritually.

**[1 Chronicles 11:1-3](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v1)</sup> Then all Israel gathered themselves to David unto Hebron, saying, Behold, we are thy bone and thy flesh.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v2)</sup> And moreover in time past, even when Saul was king, thou wast he that leddest out and broughtest in Israel: and the Lord thy God said unto thee, Thou shalt feed my people Israel, and thou shalt be ruler over my people Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v3)</sup> Therefore came all the elders of Israel to the king to Hebron; and David made a covenant with them in Hebron before the Lord; and they anointed David king over Israel, according to the word of the Lord by Samuel.

- All Israel gathered to David at Hebron, confessing they were his bone and flesh, and remembering that even when Saul was king David led Israel out and brought them in.
- The Most High had said to David, "Thou shalt feed my people Israel, and thou shalt be ruler over my people Israel." The phrase "my people Israel" is repeated, and the Bible does not change.
- The elders came to Hebron, David made a covenant with them before the Most High, and they anointed him king according to the word of the Most High by Samuel.

**[1 Chronicles 11:4-9](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v4)</sup> And David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus; where the Jebusites were, the inhabitants of the land.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v5)</sup> And the inhabitants of Jebus said to David, Thou shalt not come hither. Nevertheless David took the castle of Zion, which is the city of David.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v6)</sup> And David said, Whosoever smiteth the Jebusites first shall be chief and captain. So Joab the son of Zeruiah went first up, and was chief.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v7)</sup> And David dwelt in the castle; therefore they called it the city of David.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v8)</sup> And he built the city round about, even from Millo round about: and Joab repaired the rest of the city.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v9)</sup> So David waxed greater and greater: for the Lord of hosts was with him.

- David and all Israel went to Jerusalem, which is Jebus, and the Jebusites told him he would not come in. Nevertheless David took the castle of Zion, the city of David.
- David said whoever smote the Jebusites first would be chief and captain. Joab the son of Zeruiah went up first and became chief.
- David dwelt in the castle and built the city round about from Millo, Joab repaired the rest, and David grew greater and greater because the Most High of hosts was with him.

**[1 Chronicles 11:10-11](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v10)</sup> These also are the chief of the mighty men whom David had, who strengthened themselves with him in his kingdom, and with all Israel, to make him king, according to the word of the Lord concerning Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v11)</sup> And this is the number of the mighty men whom David had; Jashobeam, an Hachmonite, the chief of the captains: he lifted up his spear against three hundred slain by him at one time.

- These are the chief of David's mighty men who strengthened themselves with him to make him king according to the word of the Most High concerning Israel.
- Jashobeam, chief of the captains, lifted his spear against three hundred slain by him at one time. Our forefathers did for real what fighting films only imitate.

**[1 Chronicles 11:12-14](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v12)</sup> And after him was Eleazar the son of Dodo, the Ahohite, who was one of the three mighties.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v13)</sup> He was with David at Pas–dammim, and there the Philistines were gathered together to battle, where was a parcel of ground full of barley; and the people fled from before the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v14)</sup> And they set themselves in the midst of that parcel, and delivered it, and slew the Philistines; and the Lord saved them by a great deliverance.

- Eleazar the son of Dodo, one of the three mighties, stood with David at Pas-dammim where the Philistines gathered to battle in a parcel of ground full of barley.
- When the people fled, they set themselves in the midst of the parcel, delivered it, and slew the Philistines, and the Most High saved them by a great deliverance.
- The Most High was working with these brothers to make them mighty. When we go into the street it is not a physical war, but we are to stand against three hundred with the word. This is why we must study, and make our calling and election sure.
  - **[2 Peter 1:10](/bible/2-peter/1#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-peter/1#v10)</sup> Wherefore the rather, brethren, give diligence to make your calling and election sure: for if ye do these things, ye shall never fall:

    Give diligence to make your calling and election sure, for the word must be gone over constantly.

**[1 Chronicles 11:15-19](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v15)</sup> Now three of the thirty captains went down to the rock to David, into the cave of Adullam; and the host of the Philistines encamped in the valley of Rephaim.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v16)</sup> And David was then in the hold, and the Philistines’ garrison was then at Beth–lehem.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v17)</sup> And David longed, and said, Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Beth–lehem, that is at the gate!
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v18)</sup> And the three brake through the host of the Philistines, and drew water out of the well of Beth–lehem, that was by the gate, and took it, and brought it to David: but David would not drink of it, but poured it out to the Lord,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v19)</sup> And said, My God forbid it me, that I should do this thing: shall I drink the blood of these men that have put their lives in jeopardy? for with the jeopardy of their lives they brought it. Therefore he would not drink it. These things did these three mightiest.

- Three of the thirty captains went down to the cave of Adullam while the Philistines were encamped in the valley of Rephaim and their garrison was in Bethlehem.
- David longed and said, "Oh that one would give me drink of the water of the well of Bethlehem, that is at the gate." A stronghold is a solid place to gather troops and plan, near enough to strike but not so close as to be found out.
- The three broke through the Philistine host, drew the water, and brought it to David, but he would not drink it and poured it out to the Most High, saying he would not drink the blood of men who had put their lives in jeopardy. That was the heart of David.

**[1 Chronicles 11:20-21](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v20)</sup> And Abishai the brother of Joab, he was chief of the three: for lifting up his spear against three hundred, he slew them, and had a name among the three.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v21)</sup> Of the three, he was more honourable than the two; for he was their captain: howbeit he attained not to the first three.

- Abishai the brother of Joab was chief of the second three, and lifted his spear against three hundred and slew them, gaining a name among the three.
- He was more honourable than the other two and was their captain, but he did not attain to the first three. There is order, structure, and rank, just as the Most High has angels and archangels.

**[1 Chronicles 11:22-25](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v22)</sup> Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, the son of a valiant man of Kabzeel, who had done many acts; he slew two lionlike men of Moab: also he went down and slew a lion in a pit in a snowy day.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v23)</sup> And he slew an Egyptian, a man of great stature, five cubits high; and in the Egyptian’s hand was a spear like a weaver’s beam; and he went down to him with a staff, and plucked the spear out of the Egyptian’s hand, and slew him with his own spear.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v24)</sup> These things did Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, and had the name among the three mighties.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/11#v25)</sup> Behold, he was honourable among the thirty, but attained not to the first three: and David set him over his guard.

- Benaiah the son of Jehoiada, a valiant man of many acts, slew two lionlike men of Moab, went down and slew a lion in a pit on a snowy day, and killed an Egyptian five cubits high.
- He plucked the spear out of the Egyptian's hand, a spear like a weaver's beam, and slew him with his own spear.
- These acts gave Benaiah a name among the three mighties. He was honourable among the thirty but did not attain to the first three, and David set him over his guard.

- The roll of the valiant men of the armies follows, including Asahel the brother of Joab, Elhanan of Bethlehem, and many more named men from across the tribes.
- Among them is Uriah the Hittite, one of David's valiant men, the same one David later had killed. Men called by the land they lived in, such as the Hittite or one of Moab, were Israelites dwelling in those lands.
- There is much purpose behind these names. Those seeking to change their names can study these forefathers and take a name after the characteristics seen in themselves, for actions create a name, as with Abram to Abraham and Jacob to Israel.
