Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 12](/bible/1-chronicles/12)

First Chronicles 12 records the mighty men who came to David at Ziklag and at Hebron, tribe by tribe, to turn the kingdom to him. When the Most High is working with you, He sends the help. The theme is the unity of the brethren: if we unify, there is nothing we cannot do.

**[1 Chronicles 12:1-2](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v1)</sup> Now these are they that came to David to Ziklag, while he yet kept himself close because of Saul the son of Kish: and they were among the mighty men, helpers of the war.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v2)</sup> They were armed with bows, and could use both the right hand and the left in hurling stones and shooting arrows out of a bow, even of Saul’s brethren of Benjamin.

- These came to David at Ziklag while he kept himself close because of Saul. They were mighty helpers of the war, armed with bows, able to use both the right hand and the left in hurling stones and shooting arrows.
- They were of Saul's own brethren of Benjamin. Benjamin always had the reputation of being ambidextrous, able to hurl a stone with either hand and hit a hair's breadth.

>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v9)</sup> Ezer the first, Obadiah the second, Eliab the third,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v10)</sup> Mishmannah the fourth, Jeremiah the fifth,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v11)</sup> Attai the sixth, Eliel the seventh,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v12)</sup> Johanan the eighth, Elzabad the ninth,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v13)</sup> Jeremiah the tenth, Machbanai the eleventh.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v14)</sup> These were of the sons of Gad, captains of the host: one of the least was over an hundred, and the greatest over a thousand.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v15)</sup> These are they that went over Jordan in the first month, when it had overflown all his banks; and they put to flight all them of the valleys, both toward the east, and toward the west.

- The Gadites separated themselves to David into the hold in the wilderness, men of might and men of war fit for battle, able to handle shield and buckler, whose faces were like the faces of lions and were as swift as roes upon the mountains.
- Their captains ranged from the least being over a hundred to the greatest over a thousand. These went over Jordan in the first month when it had overflowed all its banks and put to flight all who were in the valleys.
- Gad stood on David's side. Historically Gad fought against Esau and helped Judah escape the plantations, especially in New Orleans, which is why many Creoles descend from a mixture of Judah, Benjamin, Levi, and Gad.

**[1 Chronicles 12:16-18](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v16)</sup> And there came of the children of Benjamin and Judah to the hold unto David.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v17)</sup> And David went out to meet them, and answered and said unto them, If ye be come peaceably unto me to help me, mine heart shall be knit unto you: but if ye be come to betray me to mine enemies, seeing there is no wrong in mine hands, the God of our fathers look thereon, and rebuke it.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v18)</sup> Then the spirit came upon Amasai, who was chief of the captains, and he said, Thine are we, David, and on thy side, thou son of Jesse: peace, peace be unto thee, and peace be to thine helpers; for thy God helpeth thee. Then David received them, and made them captains of the band.

- The children of Benjamin and Judah came to the hold, and David went to meet them, warning that if they came peaceably his heart would be knit to them, but if to betray him the Most High would rebuke it.
- David was leery of Benjamin because of the beef between him and Saul, knowing Benjamin tended to stick with Benjamin, as seen when Benjamin fought all the tribes in the book of Judges.
- The spirit came upon Amasai, chief of the captains, who said, "Thine are we, David, and on thy side, thou son of Jesse: peace be unto thee, for thy God helpeth thee." David received them and made them captains.

**[1 Chronicles 12:19-22](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v19)</sup> And there fell some of Manasseh to David, when he came with the Philistines against Saul to battle: but they helped them not: for the lords of the Philistines upon advisement sent him away, saying, He will fall to his master Saul to the jeopardy of our heads.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v20)</sup> As he went to Ziklag, there fell to him of Manasseh, Adnah, and Jozabad, and Jediael, and Michael, and Jozabad, and Elihu, and Zilthai, captains of the thousands that were of Manasseh.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v21)</sup> And they helped David against the band of the rovers: for they were all mighty men of valour, and were captains in the host.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v22)</sup> For at that time day by day there came to David to help him, until it was a great host, like the host of God.

- Some of Manasseh fell to David; they had come with the Philistines against Saul but did not help them, for the Philistine lords sent them away, fearing he would return to Saul at the jeopardy of their heads.
- At Ziklag captains of thousands of Manasseh helped David against the band of the rovers, being mighty men of valour.
- Day by day men came to David until it was a great host like the host of God. When the Most High works with you He sends help, as Christ said to pray for more labourers while continuing the work.

>

>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v37)</sup> And on the other side of Jordan, of the Reubenites, and the Gadites, and of the half tribe of Manasseh, with all manner of instruments of war for the battle, an hundred and twenty thousand.

- These are the numbers of the armed bands that came to David at Hebron to turn Saul's kingdom to him according to the word of the Most High, tribe by tribe.
- Among them were Judah, Simeon, Levi with Jehoiada leader of the Aaronites, Benjamin the kindred of Saul, Ephraim, and the half tribe of Manasseh, along with the tribes beyond Jordan.
- The Levites are named here, for Jehoiada led the Aaronites, the sons of Aaron, and Zadok also was a Levite.

**[1 Chronicles 12:32](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v32)**
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v32)</sup> And of the children of Issachar, which were men that had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do; the heads of them were two hundred; and all their brethren were at their commandment.

- The children of Issachar were men that had understanding of the times, to know what Israel ought to do, and their two hundred heads had all their brethren at their commandment.
- Issachar's knowledge of the times is why they knew how to sail from the eastern hemisphere to the western, a feat that is no easy thing. When you meet Issachar in the street and speak of the Aztecs and Mayas, bring up their calendars and this verse.

**[1 Chronicles 12:33](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v33)**
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v33)</sup> Of Zebulun, such as went forth to battle, expert in war, with all instruments of war, fifty thousand, which could keep rank: they were not of double heart.

- Of Zebulun, fifty thousand went forth to battle, expert in war with all instruments of war, which could keep rank, and were not of a double heart.
- To keep rank is to stay in proper order and play your role without jockeying for position. They were not double minded but listened to orders and structure. When we battle the wickedness of this world we must keep our rank; it is imperative.

**[1 Chronicles 12:38-40](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v38)**
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v38)</sup> All these men of war, that could keep rank, came with a perfect heart to Hebron, to make David king over all Israel: and all the rest also of Israel were of one heart to make David king.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v39)</sup> And there they were with David three days, eating and drinking: for their brethren had prepared for them.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-chronicles/12#v40)</sup> Moreover they that were nigh them, even unto Issachar and Zebulun and Naphtali, brought bread on asses, and on camels, and on mules, and on oxen, and meat, meal, cakes of figs, and bunches of raisins, and wine, and oil, and oxen, and sheep abundantly: for there was joy in Israel.

- All these men of war that could keep rank came with a perfect heart to Hebron to make David king, and all the rest of Israel were of one heart to do the same.
- The tribes beyond Jordan were of one accord with the rest, which is why we must be of one accord, for Christ is going to sit on the throne of David his father.
- They were with David three days eating and drinking, and their brethren brought bread, figs, raisins, wine, oil, and cattle abundantly, for there was joy in Israel. The joy came from being of one mind, which is why the feast days are to be kept with gladness.
