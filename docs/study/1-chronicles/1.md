Read the chapter → [1 Chronicles 1](/bible/1-chronicles/1)

After moving through the fall of Judah and the carrying away to Babylon in 2 Kings, the reading turns into [1 Chronicles 1](/bible/1-chronicles/1). This chapter is genealogy: it traces the line from Adam down through Noah and his sons, the nations that came from Japheth, Ham, and Shem, the line of Abraham through Isaac and Ishmael, and the sons of Esau and the dukes of Edom. The chapter was read as lineage with little added exposition, so the main entries below follow the verse order and hold the record of the generations as it stands.

**[1 Chronicles 1:1-4](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v1)</sup> Adam, Sheth, Enosh,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v2)</sup> Kenan, Mahalaleel, Jered,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v3)</sup> Henoch, Methuselah, Lamech,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v4)</sup> Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

- The line from the first man is set down in order: Adam, Seth, Enos, Cainan, Mahalaleel, Jered, Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech, Noah, and the three sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth.
- This is the record of the generations from Adam to Noah, the same line preserved through the flood.

**[1 Chronicles 1:5-7](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v5)</sup> The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v6)</sup> And the sons of Gomer; Ashchenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v7)</sup> And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.

- The sons of Japheth are named: Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.
- The sons of Gomer and the sons of Javan are set down after him, tracing the nations that came out of Japheth.

**[1 Chronicles 1:8-16](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v8)</sup> The sons of Ham; Cush, and Mizraim, Put, and Canaan.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v9)</sup> And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabta, and Raamah, and Sabtecha. And the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v10)</sup> And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be mighty upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v11)</sup> And Mizraim begat Ludim, and Anamim, and Lehabim, and Naphtuhim,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v12)</sup> And Pathrusim, and Casluhim, (of whom came the Philistines,) and Caphthorim.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v13)</sup> And Canaan begat Zidon his firstborn, and Heth,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v14)</sup> The Jebusite also, and the Amorite, and the Girgashite,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v15)</sup> And the Hivite, and the Arkite, and the Sinite,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v16)</sup> And the Arvadite, and the Zemarite, and the Hamathite.

- The sons of Ham are named: Cush, Mizraim, Put, and Canaan, and the sons that came from them.
- The line of Cush is traced through to Nimrod, and the sons of Mizraim and of Canaan are listed with the peoples that came from them.

**[1 Chronicles 1:17-23](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v17)</sup> The sons of Shem; Elam, and Asshur, and Arphaxad, and Lud, and Aram, and Uz, and Hul, and Gether, and Meshech.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v18)</sup> And Arphaxad begat Shelah, and Shelah begat Eber.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v19)</sup> And unto Eber were born two sons: the name of the one was Peleg; because in his days the earth was divided: and his brother’s name was Joktan.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v20)</sup> And Joktan begat Almodad, and Sheleph, and Hazarmaveth, and Jerah,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v21)</sup> Hadoram also, and Uzal, and Diklah,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v22)</sup> And Ebal, and Abimael, and Sheba,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v23)</sup> And Ophir, and Havilah, and Jobab. All these were the sons of Joktan.

- The sons of Shem are named: Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram, and the sons that came after them.
- The line runs through Arphaxad, Shelah, and Eber, in whose days the earth was divided, and on through Joktan and his sons.

**[1 Chronicles 1:24-27](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v24)</sup> Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah,
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v25)</sup> Eber, Peleg, Reu,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v26)</sup> Serug, Nahor, Terah,
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v27)</sup> Abram; the same is Abraham.

- The chosen line is set down plainly: Shem, Arphaxad, Shelah, Eber, Peleg, Reu, Serug, Nahor, Terah, and Abram, who is Abraham.
- This narrows the whole record from Shem down to Abraham, the father of the promise.

**[1 Chronicles 1:28-31](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v28)</sup> The sons of Abraham; Isaac, and Ishmael.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v29)</sup> These are their generations: The firstborn of Ishmael, Nebaioth; then Kedar, and Adbeel, and Mibsam,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v30)</sup> Mishma, and Dumah, Massa, Hadad, and Tema,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v31)</sup> Jetur, Naphish, and Kedemah. These are the sons of Ishmael.

- The sons of Abraham are named, Isaac and Ishmael, and then the twelve sons of Ishmael are set down in order.
- The generations of Ishmael are listed before the record returns to the promised seed through Isaac.

**[1 Chronicles 1:32-33](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v32)</sup> Now the sons of Keturah, Abraham’s concubine: she bare Zimran, and Jokshan, and Medan, and Midian, and Ishbak, and Shuah. And the sons of Jokshan; Sheba, and Dedan.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v33)</sup> And the sons of Midian; Ephah, and Epher, and Henoch, and Abida, and Eldaah. All these are the sons of Keturah.

- The sons of Keturah, Abraham's concubine, are named, and the sons and grandsons that came from them.
- These are the other seed of Abraham, set apart from the line of promise.

**[1 Chronicles 1:34-37](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v34)</sup> And Abraham begat Isaac. The sons of Isaac; Esau and Israel.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v35)</sup> The sons of Esau; Eliphaz, Reuel, and Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v36)</sup> The sons of Eliphaz; Teman, and Omar, Zephi, and Gatam, Kenaz, and Timna, and Amalek.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v37)</sup> The sons of Reuel; Nahath, Zerah, Shammah, and Mizzah.

- Abraham begat Isaac, and the sons of Isaac are named, Esau and Israel.
- The sons of Esau and the sons that came from them are set down before the record of the dukes of Edom.

**[1 Chronicles 1:38-42](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v38)</sup> And the sons of Seir; Lotan, and Shobal, and Zibeon, and Anah, and Dishon, and Ezer, and Dishan.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v39)</sup> And the sons of Lotan; Hori, and Homam: and Timna was Lotan’s sister.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v40)</sup> The sons of Shobal; Alian, and Manahath, and Ebal, Shephi, and Onam. And the sons of Zibeon; Aiah, and Anah.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v41)</sup> The sons of Anah; Dishon. And the sons of Dishon; Amram, and Eshban, and Ithran, and Cheran.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v42)</sup> The sons of Ezer; Bilhan, and Zavan, and Jakan. The sons of Dishan; Uz, and Aran.

- The sons of Seir and their descendants are listed, the people who dwelt in the land before Edom.
- The line is kept in order alongside the sons of Esau.

**[1 Chronicles 1:43-54](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v43)</sup> Now these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over the children of Israel; Bela the son of Beor: and the name of his city was Dinhabah.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v44)</sup> And when Bela was dead, Jobab the son of Zerah of Bozrah reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v45)</sup> And when Jobab was dead, Husham of the land of the Temanites reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v46)</sup> And when Husham was dead, Hadad the son of Bedad, which smote Midian in the field of Moab, reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Avith.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v47)</sup> And when Hadad was dead, Samlah of Masrekah reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v48)</sup> And when Samlah was dead, Shaul of Rehoboth by the river reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v49)</sup> And when Shaul was dead, Baal–hanan the son of Achbor reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v50)</sup> And when Baal–hanan was dead, Hadad reigned in his stead: and the name of his city was Pai; and his wife’s name was Mehetabel, the daughter of Matred, the daughter of Mezahab.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v51)</sup> Hadad died also. And the dukes of Edom were; duke Timnah, duke Aliah, duke Jetheth,
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v52)</sup> Duke Aholibamah, duke Elah, duke Pinon,
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v53)</sup> Duke Kenaz, duke Teman, duke Mibzar,
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/1-chronicles/1#v54)</sup> Duke Magdiel, duke Iram. These are the dukes of Edom.

- The kings that reigned in the land of Edom before any king reigned over Israel are named in order.
- The dukes of Edom are set down to close the chapter, completing the record of the generations from Adam to Edom.
