## Introduction

This chapter sets in order the divisions of the sons of Aaron, the priests, dividing them into their courses by lot for their service in the house of the Lord. It shows the ranking and order of the priests and, at the end, the casting of lots of the rest of the Levites over against their brethren.

**[1 Chronicles 24:1-6](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v1)</sup> Now these are the divisions of the sons of Aaron. The sons of Aaron; Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v2)</sup> But Nadab and Abihu died before their father, and had no children: therefore Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest’s office.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v3)</sup> And David distributed them, both Zadok of the sons of Eleazar, and Ahimelech of the sons of Ithamar, according to their offices in their service.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v4)</sup> And there were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar than of the sons of Ithamar; and thus were they divided. Among the sons of Eleazar there were sixteen chief men of the house of their fathers, and eight among the sons of Ithamar according to the house of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v5)</sup> Thus were they divided by lot, one sort with another; for the governors of the sanctuary, and governors of the house of God, were of the sons of Eleazar, and of the sons of Ithamar.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v6)</sup> And Shemaiah the son of Nethaneel the scribe, one of the Levites, wrote them before the king, and the princes, and Zadok the priest, and Ahimelech the son of Abiathar, and before the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites: one principal household being taken for Eleazar, and one taken for Ithamar.

- These are the divisions of the sons of Aaron: Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar. Nadab and Abihu died before their father and had no children, so Eleazar and Ithamar executed the priest's office. David distributed them according to their offices in their service.
- There were more chief men found of the sons of Eleazar, sixteen of the house of their fathers, and eight among the sons of Ithamar. They were divided by lot, one sort with another, and Shemaiah the scribe wrote them before the king and the princes, taking record for each.

- They had an order and directions on how they were to come into the house of the Lord, according to their manner under Aaron their father, as the Lord God of Israel had commanded them.

**[1 Chronicles 24:20-31](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v20)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v21)</sup> Concerning Rehabiah: of the sons of Rehabiah, the first was Isshiah.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v22)</sup> Of the Izharites; Shelomoth: of the sons of Shelomoth; Jahath.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v23)</sup> And the sons of Hebron; Jeriah the first, Amariah the second, Jahaziel the third, Jekameam the fourth.

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v25)</sup> The brother of Michah was Isshiah: of the sons of Isshiah; Zechariah.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v26)</sup> The sons of Merari were Mahli and Mushi: the sons of Jaaziah; Beno.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v27)</sup> The sons of Merari by Jaaziah; Beno, and Shoham, and Zaccur, and Ibri.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v28)</sup> Of Mahli came Eleazar, who had no sons.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v29)</sup> Concerning Kish: the son of Kish was Jerahmeel.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v30)</sup> The sons also of Mushi; Mahli, and Eder, and Jerimoth. These were the sons of the Levites after the house of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-chronicles/24#v31)</sup> These likewise cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron in the presence of David the king, and Zadok, and Ahimelech, and the chief of the fathers of the priests and Levites, even the principal fathers over against their younger brethren.

- The rest of the sons of Levi are named after the house of their fathers, family by family, down the line. These likewise cast lots over against their brethren the sons of Aaron in the presence of David the king, and the priests and Levites, even the principal fathers over against their younger brethren.
- All these families come from particular bloodlines and particular families that were set over particular things. Just as Aaron's family was already chosen to do the priest's duties of the holy things, each house received its own service as an inheritance, kept in the family.
