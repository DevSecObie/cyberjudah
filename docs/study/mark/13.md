**[Mark 13:1-4](/bible/mark/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/mark/13#v1)</sup> And as he went out of the temple, one of his disciples saith unto him, Master, see what manner of stones and what buildings are here!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/mark/13#v2)</sup> And Jesus answering said unto him, Seest thou these great buildings? there shall not be left one stone upon another, that shall not be thrown down.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/mark/13#v3)</sup> And as he sat upon the mount of Olives over against the temple, Peter and James and John and Andrew asked him privately,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/mark/13#v4)</sup> Tell us, when shall these things be? and what shall be the sign when all these things shall be fulfilled?

- Of the great buildings he says there shall not be left one stone upon another that shall not be thrown down, and privately on the mount of Olives they ask when.

**[Mark 13:5-8](/bible/mark/13#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/mark/13#v5)</sup> And Jesus answering them began to say, Take heed lest any man deceive you:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/mark/13#v6)</sup> For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/mark/13#v7)</sup> And when ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars, be ye not troubled: for such things must needs be; but the end shall not be yet.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/mark/13#v8)</sup> For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be earthquakes in divers places, and there shall be famines and troubles: these are the beginnings of sorrows.

- Many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many. The deceiving has been done on a scale of billions, and the figure held up is not the man of the scriptures.
- Wars and rumours of wars are not the end. Nation shall rise against nation, and there shall be earthquakes, famines and troubles: these are the beginnings of sorrows.

**[Mark 13:9-13](/bible/mark/13#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/mark/13#v9)</sup> But take heed to yourselves: for they shall deliver you up to councils; and in the synagogues ye shall be beaten: and ye shall be brought before rulers and kings for my sake, for a testimony against them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/mark/13#v10)</sup> And the gospel must first be published among all nations.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/mark/13#v11)</sup> But when they shall lead you, and deliver you up, take no thought beforehand what ye shall speak, neither do ye premeditate: but whatsoever shall be given you in that hour, that speak ye: for it is not ye that speak, but the Holy Ghost.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/mark/13#v12)</sup> Now the brother shall betray the brother to death, and the father the son; and children shall rise up against their parents, and shall cause them to be put to death.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/mark/13#v13)</sup> And ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake: but he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved.

- Ye shall be delivered up to councils, beaten in the synagogues, and brought before rulers and kings for a testimony against them. That happened to the disciples and it will happen again.
- Take no thought beforehand what ye shall speak, for it is not ye that speak, but the Holy Ghost.
- He that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved. Read that against verse 8: this is the beginning of sorrows, and the end is not yet, so no man is finished yet.

**[Mark 13:14-18](/bible/mark/13#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/mark/13#v14)</sup> But when ye shall see the abomination of desolation, spoken of by Daniel the prophet, standing where it ought not, (let him that readeth understand,) then let them that be in Judea flee to the mountains:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/mark/13#v15)</sup> And let him that is on the housetop not go down into the house, neither enter therein, to take any thing out of his house:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/mark/13#v16)</sup> And let him that is in the field not turn back again for to take up his garment.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/mark/13#v17)</sup> But woe to them that are with child, and to them that give suck in those days!
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/mark/13#v18)</sup> And pray ye that your flight be not in the winter.

- When ye shall see the abomination of desolation standing where it ought not, then let them that be in Judaea flee to the mountains.
- Woe to them that are with child and to them that give suck in those days, and pray that your flight be not in the winter. What that siege was is written in the law.

**[Mark 13:28-32](/bible/mark/13#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/mark/13#v28)</sup> Now learn a parable of the fig tree; When her branch is yet tender, and putteth forth leaves, ye know that summer is near:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/mark/13#v29)</sup> So ye in like manner, when ye shall see these things come to pass, know that it is nigh, even at the doors.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/mark/13#v30)</sup> Verily I say unto you, that this generation shall not pass, till all these things be done.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/mark/13#v31)</sup> Heaven and earth shall pass away: but my words shall not pass away.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/mark/13#v32)</sup> But of that day and that hour knoweth no man, no, not the angels which are in heaven, neither the Son, but the Father.

- Learn a parable of the fig tree: when her branch is yet tender and putteth forth leaves, ye know that summer is near.
- Heaven and earth shall pass away, but my words shall not pass away. Of that day and that hour knoweth no man, neither the Son, but the Father.

**[Mark 13:33-37](/bible/mark/13#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/mark/13#v33)</sup> Take ye heed, watch and pray: for ye know not when the time is.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/mark/13#v34)</sup> For the Son of man is as a man taking a far journey, who left his house, and gave authority to his servants, and to every man his work, and commanded the porter to watch.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/mark/13#v35)</sup> Watch ye therefore: for ye know not when the master of the house cometh, at even, or at midnight, or at the cockcrowing, or in the morning:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/mark/13#v36)</sup> Lest coming suddenly he find you sleeping.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/mark/13#v37)</sup> And what I say unto you I say unto all, Watch.

- Take ye heed, watch and pray: for ye know not when the time is. The porter is commanded to watch, and every man has his work.
- Watching means keeping an eye on what is happening in the world and filtering it through the scriptures, not living blind to it.
- It also means being found doing right whenever he comes. A man does not want to be found in the middle of the thing he has not repented of. What I say unto you I say unto all, Watch.
