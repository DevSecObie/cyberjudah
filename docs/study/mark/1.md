## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Mark 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 16-17, 19-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Mark 1:1-3](/bible/mark/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/mark/1#v1)</sup> The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/mark/1#v2)</sup> As it is written in the prophets, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/mark/1#v3)</sup> The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.

- “The beginning of the gospel of Jesus Christ, the Son of God; As it is written in the prophets, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee. The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.” God said, I'm going to send my messenger before me, which was John, and he is going to teach you, to get you right, to prepare for me. That is the job of the prophet, the apostle, the teacher: to show you.

**[Mark 1:16-17](/bible/mark/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/mark/1#v16)</sup> Now as he walked by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother casting a net into the sea: for they were fishers.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/mark/1#v17)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, Come ye after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men.

- “Now as he walked by the sea of Galilee, he saw Simon and Andrew his brother casting a net into the sea: for they were fishers. And Jesus said unto them, Come ye after me, and I will make you to become fishers of men.” After John, Christ is bringing these men in. They are coming to get right.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 22:31-34](/bible/luke/22#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/luke/22#v31)</sup> And the Lord said, Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat:
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/luke/22#v32)</sup> But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/luke/22#v33)</sup> And he said unto him, Lord, I am ready to go with thee, both into prison, and to death.
    >
    > <sup>[34](/bible/luke/22#v34)</sup> And he said, I tell thee, Peter, the cock shall not crow this day, before that thou shalt thrice deny that thou knowest me.

    Christ had to expose to Peter what was in him. “Simon, Simon, behold, Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat: But I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not: and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.” I prayed that your faith won't fail, and when you are converted, go and strengthen your brethren. When you get right, then go teach the people. He is showing him that he still has things to work on: you want to walk with me, I'm going to show you what your problems are.

**[Mark 1:19-20](/bible/mark/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/mark/1#v19)</sup> And when he had gone a little further thence, he saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the ship mending their nets.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/mark/1#v20)</sup> And straightway he called them: and they left their father Zebedee in the ship with the hired servants, and went after him.

- He “saw James the son of Zebedee, and John his brother, who also were in the ship mending their nets. And straightway he called them: and they left their father Zebedee in the ship with the hired servants, and went after him.” Do you think when he was walking with them he wasn't showing them all their errors?
