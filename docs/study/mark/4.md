## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Mark 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 9-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Mark 4:9](/bible/mark/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/mark/4#v9)</sup> And he said unto them, He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.

- “He that hath ears to hear, let him hear.” We are living in the last days, and the Most High's word is going out faster than you believe, and Bible prophecies are being fulfilled faster than we understand. God said you are going to see your teachers; they will not be hid in the corner anymore. You are going to see who is telling the truth and who is not, who is serious about this truth, and who is walking right according to the word.

**[Mark 4:10-11](/bible/mark/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/mark/4#v10)</sup> And when he was alone, they that were about him with the twelve asked of him the parable.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/mark/4#v11)</sup> And he said unto them, Unto you it is given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God: but unto them that are without, all these things are done in parables:

- “Unto you it is given to know the mystery of the kingdom of God”. It was made known to the twelve, and it is given to us, the Israelites, to understand the mystery of God.
- “But unto them that are without, all these things are done in parables”: them that are not keeping the law and commandments, not walking right in the faith of Christ, not understanding who they are according to the Bible. To them it is done in parables, a similitude, a dark sentence, an allegory. One of those men brought up that Genesis is an allegory, and he did not understand it; he did not even have a Bible in his hand to explain what he was talking about, while saying he had read the whole Bible.

**[Mark 4:12](/bible/mark/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/mark/4#v12)</sup> That seeing they may see, and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand; lest at any time they should be converted, and their sins should be forgiven them.

- “That seeing they may see, and not perceive; and hearing they may hear, and not understand”. They were reading it with the camp and not perceiving what God was telling them. They heard the word coming out plainly, slowly, clearly, and still did not understand.
- “Lest at any time they should be converted, and their sins should be forgiven them.” Unless God puts his spirit on them and they change, because some do change: Nicodemus, Joseph of Arimathaea, Gamaliel, those who came to him by night. Fred Price seemed like he was starting to understand: that Christ, the Father and the Holy Ghost are not one person, that Christ did have a father, that we do have to keep the law and commandments. That is between him and the Lord.
- They kept throwing out the whole law. The whole law went back to the temple, the Levitical priests, the sons of Aaron making the sacrifice, sprinkling the blood, the ordinances that made the animal sacrifice complete. We have no temple now, no Levitical priests, no animal sacrifice. Now we go through Christ: I am the way, the truth and the life (John 14:6). We cannot get to the Father but through him, because he is the sacrificial lamb.
- Their sins will be forgiven if they repent, catch themselves, come back and shake that dead spirit off.

**[Mark 4:13](/bible/mark/4#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/mark/4#v13)</sup> And he said unto them, Know ye not this parable? and how then will ye know all parables?

- “Know ye not this parable? and how then will ye know all parables?” Every last one of us falls under the four categories of Israelites in this parable. You have to figure out which one you fall into.

**[Mark 4:14](/bible/mark/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/mark/4#v14)</sup> The sower soweth the word.

- “The sower soweth the word.” We often talk about the four types of Israelites, and this is the time to discuss it again.

**[Mark 4:15](/bible/mark/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/mark/4#v15)</sup> And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown; but when they have heard, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.

- The first type: “And these are they by the way side, where the word is sown; but when they have heard, Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.” That is when you are teaching on the street, and the person you are teaching starts to get it, and a friend or a neighbour comes by and draws that person away. That is Satan, taking them away from hearing the truth.
- The first type hears the word “by the way side”. That is the brother or sister on the street: they hear the brothers teaching and say, that makes sense. Then “Satan cometh immediately, and taketh away the word that was sown in their hearts.” He takes away that little seed that was planted.

**[Mark 4:16-17](/bible/mark/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/mark/4#v16)</sup> And these are they likewise which are sown on stony ground; who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with gladness;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/mark/4#v17)</sup> And have no root in themselves, and so endure but for a time: afterward, when affliction or persecution ariseth for the word’s sake, immediately they are offended.

- The second type: “And these are they likewise which are sown on stony ground; who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with gladness”. That is some of you in here: you receive the word with gladness as soon as you hear the truth.
- “These are they likewise which are sown on stony ground; who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with gladness.”
- “And have no root in themselves”: you brothers and sisters that hear this truth gladly, some of you have no root, and that means you don't study the things we are teaching. You are not taking it seriously. Brothers put the class on and say, I watch class every day. A part of study is not just watching class: it is taking notes, then going over those notes when class is finished, rehearsing them: what does this verse mean, why did the Bishop or the deacons or the captains say this, let me go back to the video. That is building yourself up, applying what you have been taught; not cutting the class on while you walk around cooking, cleaning and mopping. You are not playing us; you are playing yourself. It is like people on fake diets, keto this and keto that, who won't go to the gym and order a Diet Coke with no ice with their five hamburgers. Then at the table or in the war room: I never heard that. We went over that last week. Check your notes.
- “And so endure but for a time”: brothers and sisters like that will only endure for a time. It does not say how long; it is kept vague, a year, two, three, maybe five. “Afterward, when affliction or persecution ariseth for the word’s sake”: it will surely come. Remember: when you come to serve the Lord, prepare your soul. “Immediately they are offended”: you get offended and you leave, especially you precept brothers who don't apply nothing. As soon as a little affliction comes your way you are out the door, or you find an excuse: he looked at me wrong, she didn't say shalom to me, y'all don't have the spirit of Christ, I'm out. But it was really the affliction you were getting, and you don't want to say that, so you blame somebody else.

  Precepts:
  - **[Colossians 2:6-7](/bible/colossians/2#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/colossians/2#v6)</sup> As ye have therefore received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk ye in him:
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/colossians/2#v7)</sup> Rooted and built up in him, and stablished in the faith, as ye have been taught, abounding therein with thanksgiving.

    What does it mean to have no root? “As ye have therefore received Christ Jesus the Lord, so walk ye in him: Rooted and built up in him, and stablished in the faith, as ye have been taught”. As ye have been taught: brothers and sisters, what we have been teaching you, for some of you years and for some months, you better hold on to it.
  - **[1 Peter 4:14](/bible/1-peter/4#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/1-peter/4#v14)</sup> If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye; for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you: on their part he is evil spoken of, but on your part he is glorified.

    The affliction and persecution that arise for the word's sake: “If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye; for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you”. You are being reproached for the name of Christ, and that is a part of your affliction.
  - **[Matthew 10:23](/bible/matthew/10#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/matthew/10#v23)</sup> But when they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another: for verily I say unto you, Ye shall not have gone over the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be come.

    “But when they persecute you in this city, flee ye into another: for verily I say unto you, Ye shall not have gone over the cities of Israel, till the Son of man be come.” That is the men out there in the highways and byways teaching: persecution arises for the word's sake.

**[Mark 4:18-19](/bible/mark/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/mark/4#v18)</sup> And these are they which are sown among thorns; such as hear the word,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/mark/4#v19)</sup> And the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becometh unfruitful.

- The third group: “And these are they which are sown among thorns; such as hear the word, And the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches”.
- “And the lusts of other things entering in”: that is so vague it can be anything. It is left vague because everybody in here is struggling with a different demon. We all battle with thirteen, as Christ said, and one of them is stronger than the others, and only you know which one it is, you and the Lord.
- The third type is sown among thorns: “the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becometh unfruitful.” The lust of other things goes even into sex. Some hear the word and receive it with joy, and they are amongst us one year, two years, three, four years, and they are horny. The lust comes in and chokes the word, and they go back into the world to fulfill the lust of the flesh.
- Others have one foot in the truth and one foot in the world, trying to become rich, loving this world, and the world ends up choking the word of God. You know if you are being choked: you are not studying, you have no joy to come and congregate, it feels like, I got to go to class. You can feel it in your spirit. If it is happening to you, fast and pray and examine yourself.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 6:9-10](/bible/1-timothy/6#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/6#v9)</sup> But they that will be rich fall into temptation and a snare, and into many foolish and hurtful lusts, which drown men in destruction and perdition.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-timothy/6#v10)</sup> For the love of money is the root of all evil: which while some coveted after, they have erred from the faith, and pierced themselves through with many sorrows.

    The deceitfulness of riches. Some of you want to be rich, like the sister who wanted to go to England and become rich, or the one in Houston who was on America's Next Top Model and got hooked into a lesbian affair: all to be rich and famous. Where is she now? Nowhere, probably homeless.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 10:9](/bible/sirach/10#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/10#v9)</sup> Why is earth and ashes proud? There is not a more wicked thing than a covetous man: for such an one setteth his own soul to sale; because while he liveth he casteth away his bowels.

    “Why is earth and ashes proud?” Man is earth and ashes. “There is not a more wicked thing than a covetous man: for such an one setteth his own soul to sale”: there is nothing worse than a covetous man; he will sell his own soul. “Because while he liveth he casteth away his bowels”: while he lives he casts away his bowels, his rectum.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 18:30-31](/bible/sirach/18#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/18#v30)</sup> Go not after thy lusts, but refrain thyself from thine appetites.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/18#v31)</sup> If thou givest thy soul the desires that please her, she will make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies that malign thee.

    “Go not after thy lusts”: brothers, sisters, do not go after your lust. Examine yourself right now and think about what your lust is. “But refrain thyself from thine appetites”: refrain means discipline that spirit within you. Notice it puts lust and food together: some of you lust after chicken and hamburgers and grits, some of you lust after sex.

**[Mark 4:20](/bible/mark/4#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/mark/4#v20)</sup> And these are they which are sown on good ground; such as hear the word, and receive it, and bring forth fruit, some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some an hundred.

- The fourth are “sown on good ground”: studying, praying, applying, with roots in this truth, learning to overcome lust and temptations. They “bring forth fruit, some thirtyfold, some sixty, and some an hundred.”
- You bring forth fruit by going through things. A sister offends you, you go to her and apply Matthew 18, she says she is sorry, and you forgive her. That is bringing forth fruit. If somebody offends you, the Bible tells you how to deal with it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 22:14](/bible/matthew/22#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/22#v14)</sup> For many are called, but few are chosen.

    “For many are called, but few are chosen.” Many brothers and sisters will be called to this truth, but few of us will be chosen. The chosen are those of us that learn to love each other, that overcome the trials and tribulations, that are rooted in this truth, that don’t let the cares of this world and other things come in and choke the word.
  - **[Philippians 2:12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.

    “As ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.” This is the scripture for all of us. It is up to the leaders to guide you and show you what to apply to your life to attain salvation, but every one of us has to work it out.
