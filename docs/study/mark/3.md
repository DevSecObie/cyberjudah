## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Mark 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2, 6, 27-29, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Mark 3:2](/bible/mark/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/mark/3#v2)</sup> And they watched him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse him.

- “And they watched him, whether he would heal him on the sabbath day; that they might accuse him.” The they that watched him are the Pharisees. They were always looking for accusations against Christ, following him to see whether he would slip up. Remember what Christ said: if they do it unto you, surely they have done it unto me. They follow you block to block, state to state, camp corner to camp corner; surely they did it unto Christ.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 6:7](/bible/luke/6#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/luke/6#v7)</sup> And the scribes and Pharisees watched him, whether he would heal on the sabbath day; that they might find an accusation against him.

    “And the scribes and Pharisees watched him, whether he would heal on the sabbath day; that they might find an accusation against him.” The same watching: the scribes and Pharisees waiting for an accusation.
  - **[Luke 11:53-54](/bible/luke/11#v53)**
    > <sup>[53](/bible/luke/11#v53)</sup> And as he said these things unto them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to urge him vehemently, and to provoke him to speak of many things:
    >
    > <sup>[54](/bible/luke/11#v54)</sup> Laying wait for him, and seeking to catch something out of his mouth, that they might accuse him.

    Christ had just finished condemning them: woe unto you lawyers, for you build the sepulchres of the prophets and your fathers killed them. “And as he said these things unto them, the scribes and the Pharisees began to urge him vehemently, and to provoke him to speak of many things”. They were trying to bring Christ out of the spirit, to get him to speak vehemently and say things he should not say. But Christ kept it spiritual, according to the Bible, the way we should.
  - **[Luke 20:20](/bible/luke/20#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/luke/20#v20)</sup> And they watched him, and sent forth spies, which should feign themselves just men, that they might take hold of his words, that so they might deliver him unto the power and authority of the governor.

    “And they watched him, and sent forth spies, which should feign themselves just men, that they might take hold of his words”. We might have a few of them up in here, in our Atlanta, New York and Mississippi congregations. We know you are there, biding your time. “That so they might deliver him unto the power and authority of the governor.”

**[Mark 3:6](/bible/mark/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/mark/3#v6)</sup> And the Pharisees went forth, and straightway took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.

- “And the Pharisees went forth, and straightway took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.” The Herodians were supporters of Herod, black men supporting Rome. All the well-off Negroes, commentators and content creators named in the class are Herodians: supporters of America, supporters of Rome, supporters of money.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 12:13](/bible/mark/12#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/mark/12#v13)</sup> And they send unto him certain of the Pharisees and of the Herodians, to catch him in his words.

    “And they send unto him certain of the Pharisees and of the Herodians, to catch him in his words.” That is what these men do: have us on their shows and podcasts to catch us in our words, make fools of us and embarrass us to make content. Herodians.

**[Mark 3:27](/bible/mark/3#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/mark/3#v27)</sup> No man can enter into a strong man’s house, and spoil his goods, except he will first bind the strong man; and then he will spoil his house.

- Porn will also destroy your marriage. “No man can enter into a strong man’s house, and spoil his goods, except he will first bind the strong man.” You are the strong man of your house, the leader of that house. When you watch porn, it does not just affect you, though Satan says it is just porn. It affects your wife, it affects your kids, and it affects you, and you do not even see it. That is from experience.
- Satan makes you downplay it: you are not really committing adultery, you are not sleeping with another woman, it is just something you are watching. But really you are inviting demons into your house. All of a sudden your kids act different and weird and have demons on them, because you opened that door. Your wife opens your phone and sees that thing, and asks why you watch it and whether you find her attractive, and now she deals with low self-esteem. It is not worth it. When you open that door you let Satan into your house.
- And you sisters who watch porn and tell your husband to choke you: if he does a thing one time and you both like it, that is between the two of you. But for her to tell you she wants that done to her, brother, check the Carfax.

**[Mark 3:28](/bible/mark/3#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/mark/3#v28)</sup> Verily I say unto you, All sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and blasphemies wherewith soever they shall blaspheme:

- “All sins shall be forgiven unto the sons of men, and blasphemies wherewith soever they shall blaspheme”: thank the Most High God for our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, because every last one of us has blasphemed. You women wearing pants, you are blaspheming. You committing adultery would get the death penalty if it wasn’t for Christ sacrificing himself so that you have grace; you would automatically be put to death. Homosexuality, you can repent from that. Back then, you got caught with somebody else’s wife or husband, you were to be put to death, both of you. You got caught with some sheep, ram or animal, you were put to death and they had to kill the animal too. Thank God for Jesus the Christ.

**[Mark 3:29](/bible/mark/3#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/mark/3#v29)</sup> But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost hath never forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal damnation:

- But if you don’t correct yourself, if you don’t fix this fornication going on in the nation of Israel and all the likeness of sin, if you continue on against the word of God, against the laws of God, you are going to keep blaspheming. “But he that shall blaspheme against the Holy Ghost hath never forgiveness, but is in danger of eternal damnation.” You are in danger. We are letting you know it is danger, like the old warning, danger Will Robinson.
- Damnation is condemnation to eternal punishment in hell. Ain’t we had enough of this hell we are in now? Haven’t we seen enough happen to our people in this captivity? This is hell. But there is a second death coming. You don’t just die here. That second death is the eternal damnation we are talking about.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 2:10-11](/bible/revelation/2#v10)**

    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/2#v11)</sup> He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; He that overcometh shall not be hurt of the second death.

    “Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.” That is why we have to go through that narrow path. They don’t want us to go down that path; they want us to take the broad way. There is going to be persecution and suffering for that, but it is worth it, rather than continue to live like we are living and end up at the very end with eternal damnation.
