## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Mark 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Mark 16:15](/bible/mark/16#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/mark/16#v15)</sup> And he said unto them, Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.

- The Christians run here too: “Go ye into all the world, and preach the gospel to every creature.” Every creature, brother, see, everybody; you’re misled, you have hate in your heart.
- So when he said every creature, there are many creatures today: animals and all of that. Did he come to save the fish in the sea and the rocks? Did he come to save the water bottles, the cups, the phones, the goats, the icicles? Every creature does not mean every living thing on the earth, and “all the world” does not mean every nation. The scriptures define both: all the world is wherever the Jews were scattered, and his creature is his people.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 24:5](/bible/acts/24#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/acts/24#v5)</sup> For we have found this man a pestilent fellow, and a mover of sedition among all the Jews throughout the world, and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes:

    They accused Paul: “we have found this man a pestilent fellow, and a mover of sedition among all the Jews throughout the world”. Among the Jews where? Throughout the world. All the Jews throughout the world: the scattered ones the apostles were sent to. That is “all the world” in Mark 16:15.
  - **[Jeremiah 31:35-36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The Lord of hosts is his name:
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v36)</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the Lord, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.

    The Most High does not go by how you look; he goes by the father. David’s grandmother was a Moabite, but was David a mix? No, he was Israel, because his father was Judah. If your father is a white man, like the previous mayor of New York, de Blasio, a white man, an Edomite, whose kids are dark with afros, wide noses and big lips and don’t look like him, are his kids descendants of Israel? No. It’s not about how you look or complexion; it is the seed of Israel, and the seed of Israel has been scattered throughout the four corners of the earth. The nation of Israel in general are people of color, but there are exceptions to the rule: remnants of us scattered abroad who are high yellow or pale. It’s not a color thing, it’s a nation thing. But concerning Christ it is a color thing, because the scripture told us exactly: Christ is a very dark-skinned brother.
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v27)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations, and ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.

    Why did he say go into all nations? “And the Lord shall scatter you among the nations”. Because the Lord scattered Israel among all nations, he will redeem them from among the nations, the same as Revelation 5:9. “And ye shall be left few in number among the heathen, whither the Lord shall lead you.” Among the heathen, twice: Israel left few, among the heathen nations, wherever the Lord led them. That is all the world the disciples were sent into.
