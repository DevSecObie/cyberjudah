## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-11, 14-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 5:1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v1)</sup> Then shall the righteous man stand in great boldness before the face of such as have afflicted him, and made no account of his labours.

- “Then shall the righteous man stand in great boldness before the face of such as have afflicted him”: that is what we are doing now. When we are fishing for brothers and sisters, we are standing in great boldness against the man who has afflicted us. This so-called white man is the oppressor, he’s the afflicter, and it is great boldness to say the things we say on the street. We teach brothers and sisters, this is the man that has afflicted us, enslaved us, oppressed us and colonized us, and here comes a Christian with John 3:16 and Galatians 3:28. It never fails.
- They say it’s hate speech. Just saying the white man is an oppressor is translated as hate speech; just saying God is black, that Jesus is black, is hate speech. They took a post down off Instagram calling it hate: Christians today, ‘hi, I’m Galatians 3:28, my name is John 3:16’. That is modern day Christianity, how our people are coons and vagabonds. Anytime we try to speak and let our people know the truth, everything we put up is hate speech. These oppressors think they can put fear upon us, but the Bible says we’re going to speak with great boldness. So lift up your voice in the street knowing the angels are around you. You can speak now; speak like a man. We’ve been commanded to speak boldly.
- “And made no account of his labours”: 400 years of free slave labor, no account. Reparations? Please. Y’all ain’t getting nothing.
- This is what is going to be even more spectacular, what will make everybody forget about ancient Egypt. “Then shall the righteous man stand in great boldness”. That is what we are being taught now: to be bold among our enemies, who are literally praying for us to remain slaves and do nothing for ourselves, so they can keep exploiting and dominating us. In the middle of that psychological tyranny and physical tyranny, the Most High says we are still going to stand in great boldness.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 5:2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v2)</sup> When they see it, they shall be troubled with terrible fear, and shall be amazed at the strangeness of his salvation, so far beyond all that they looked for.

- “When they see it, they shall be troubled with terrible fear”: believe it or not, a lot of our people join with the oppressor. These are the negroes and the Latinos that join with the white man. They are going to see it, because they disregarded everything we’ve been teaching them. “And shall be amazed at the strangeness of his salvation, so far beyond all that they looked for”: they are going to be amazed at the strangeness of our salvation. How so? Revelation 11 shows it.
- “When they see it, they shall be troubled with terrible fear”. When our enemies see us stand up in great boldness they shall be shaken, because they know who they have in captivity, who they have in slavery. The point is for you not to know who you are, but they know who you are. As long as you don’t know who you are, you will subject yourself to inferior behaviour and to mediocrity in your walk, your aspirations and your endeavours; you will shortchange yourself, thinking no more of yourself than they taught you to think.
- So the terrible fear is you breaking out of that sickness and recognizing who you truly are. When you do that, the world is going to catch hell, because they say it means the God of these people is soon to come. That is what they are praying against today, celebrating to make sure we stay dominated. Our rise tells them we expect a deliverer: we expect Christ to come back, the deliverance and saving from the hand of our enemies and all that hate us. Once we start looking for that, they can’t buy us off; we know our God is coming to kick their ass. That is why they are acting up the way they are: they are having a fit that the Israelites are waking up.
- “and shall be amazed at the strangeness of his salvation”. Amazed means your mind is blown. The strangeness of the Lord’s salvation is not only the people he is going to save but how he is going to do it. It is written in the Psalms read at the opening: a thousand shall fall at thy side and ten thousand at thy right hand, but it shall not come nigh thee; only with thine eyes shalt thou behold it. Being delivered and seeing the destruction of your enemies with your own eyes: that is the strangeness of his salvation.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 11:11-12](/bible/revelation/11#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)</sup> And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/11#v12)</sup> And they heard a great voice from heaven saying unto them, Come up hither. And they ascended up to heaven in a cloud; and their enemies beheld them.

    Revelation 11 was read earlier, but not the main point. “And after three days and an half”: 350 years. “The Spirit of life from God entered into them”: the Holy Spirit entered into us; we realized we’re the Israelites and must keep the commandments. “And they stood upon their feet”: we stood upon our feet as the 12 tribes of Israel. “And great fear fell upon them which saw them”: what the nations see is fear, because they know what is going to happen.
  - **[Matthew 24:30-31](/bible/matthew/24#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/24#v30)</sup> And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)</sup> And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.

    Matthew 24:30 says the same thing: “And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn”, all the tribes going to cry, “and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven”: we are going to see the King of kings and Lord of lords, “with power and great glory.”
  - **[Ezekiel 1:12](/bible/ezekiel/1#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/1#v12)</sup> And they went every one straight forward: whither the spirit was to go, they went; and they turned not when they went.

    The strangeness of his salvation is the power of how these angels move: they move with thought. “And they went every one straight forward: whither the spirit was to go, they went”. That is the chariots. Everywhere the spirit went, the chariots were there.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 5:3-5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v3)</sup> And they repenting and groaning for anguish of spirit shall say within themselves, This was he, whom we had sometimes in derision, and a proverb of reproach:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v4)</sup> We fools accounted his life madness, and his end to be without honour:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v5)</sup> How is he numbered among the children of God, and his lot is among the saints!

- “And they repenting and groaning for anguish of spirit shall say within themselves, This was he, whom we had sometimes in derision, and a proverb of reproach: We fools accounted his life madness, and his end to be without honour: How is he numbered among the children of God, and his lot is among the saints!”
- Our people that join with the white man, and a lot of these Christian pastors too, are going to be saying that same thing, because they so trusted their oppressor, loved him so much. They thought the Israelites were crazy.
- “And they repenting and groaning for anguish of spirit shall say within themselves.” When that destruction takes place and they see the righteous man delivered, they will be sorry within themselves: we were wrong, everything those Israelites were teaching was the truth.
- “This was he, whom we had sometimes in derision.” Derision is treating someone with contempt, scorn or mocking laughter. They laughed at us, scorned and mocked us: that is a hate group, they are worthless, they are stupid. They will say, isn’t that the man we killed and said was part of a hate group, the man we hanged and said was in a cult, the sister we said was in a cult? “and a proverb of reproach”: they call us terrorists and a hate group and put us on their lists.

Are you setting things up for that now?

> <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/5#v15)</sup> But the righteous live for evermore; their reward also is with the Lord, and the care of them is with the most High.

- “But the righteous live for evermore”: the righteous are going to live for evermore. “Their reward also is with the Lord, and the care of them is with the most High.”
