## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 20-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 16:20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v20)</sup> Instead whereof thou feddest thine own people with angels’ food, and didst send them from heaven bread prepared without their labour, able to content every man’s delight, and agreeing to every taste.

- Back to the manna. “Instead whereof thou feddest thine own people with angels’ food”: that is the manna, “and didst send them from heaven bread prepared without their labour, able to content every man’s delight, and agreeing to every taste.” One man loves cheesecake and another doesn't, but the manna tasted however it needed to taste for you: sweeter for the one who likes it sweet, subtly sweet for the one who likes it subtle. Every man to his taste, and that is spiritual.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 16:26](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v26)</sup> That thy children, O Lord, whom thou lovest, might know, that it is not the growing of fruits that nourisheth man: but that it is thy word, which preserveth them that put their trust in thee.

    Why did he do this? “That thy children, O Lord, whom thou lovest, might know, that it is not the growing of fruits that nourisheth man”: it is not physical food, “but that it is thy word, which preserveth them that put their trust in thee.” That is how we are preserved, and the manna represents that. It goes back to Deuteronomy 8: it was never about feeding their stomachs. It was teaching what truly preserves life, lining us up to accept and receive Christ.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 16:21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v21)</sup> For thy sustenance declared thy sweetness unto thy children, and serving to the appetite of the eater, tempered itself to every man’s liking.

- “For thy sustenance declared thy sweetness unto thy children, and serving to the appetite of the eater, tempered itself to every man’s liking.” So don't just read. Some are averse to reading the verses that deal with what they are dealing with. Feed yourself with what you need, and you can't do that if you are not in there looking every day. Some study everything except what is eating at them, because they don't want to fix it. The manna pleased everyone, some took more and some less, and it filled them and nourished them with what they needed.
