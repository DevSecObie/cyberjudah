## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7-10, 13-30, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 7:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v7)</sup> Wherefore I prayed, and understanding was given me: I called upon God, and the spirit of wisdom came to me.

- “Wherefore I prayed, and understanding was given me: I called upon God, and the spirit of wisdom came to me.” If you believe you just pray, God give me understanding so I can break down Revelation, and it comes, that is not how it works. You pray to God, and God will tell you to study to show thyself approved, rightly dividing the word. You have to choose this.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 7:8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v8)</sup> I preferred her before sceptres and thrones, and esteemed riches nothing in comparison of her.

- “I preferred her before sceptres and thrones, and esteemed riches nothing in comparison of her.” Sometimes your job, or whatever you have, becomes more important, and then you wonder why you can’t grow. Our chief love is the word of God. Jobs and careers are just to get by in this captivity; they should not be the focal point of your life. Seek the kingdom first, and everything else shall be added.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 7:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v9)</sup> Neither compared I unto her any precious stone, because all gold in respect of her is as a little sand, and silver shall be counted as clay before her.

- “Neither compared I unto her any precious stone, because all gold in respect of her is as a little sand, and silver shall be counted as clay before her.” All gold is as a little sand next to wisdom.
- Why is wisdom better than gold or silver? Those things are corruptible and won’t last into the kingdom to come; they can be stolen or destroyed. And what do people do with gold? They sell it. If you had gold right now you could sell some and make some money, but in time it runs out. Wisdom never runs out, because with wisdom you get the understanding to make more.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 7:10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v10)</sup> I loved her above health and beauty, and chose to have her instead of light: for the light that cometh from her never goeth out.

- “I loved her above health and beauty, and chose to have her instead of light: for the light that cometh from her never goeth out.” The light that comes from the word is a light that will never go out. Choose wisdom above all things; prefer the Lord above all things. If you want understanding, call upon God and he will give it to you, but we have to make him first.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 7:14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v14)</sup> For she is a treasure unto men that never faileth: which they that use become the friends of God, being commended for the gifts that come from learning.

- “For she is a treasure unto men that never faileth: which they that use become the friends of God.” This is a treasure that never fails, and those that use it, that apply it in their lives, become friends of God, “being commended for the gifts that come from learning.”
- Some of those gifts are the senses this class is about, a certain level of understanding. Those who have been studying for a while have run across a scripture they never heard before and known at once that it lines up perfectly with what they already studied, without calling anybody for confirmation. That doesn’t mean you don’t get counsel. It means that when the Holy Spirit takes over you, God guides you and makes it clear, because all the precepts line up. It is euphoric; it is almost the greatest feeling you can get.

>
> <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v17)</sup> For he hath given me certain knowledge of the things that are, namely, to know how the world was made, and the operation of the elements:

For he hath given me certain knowledge of the things that are, namely, to know how the world was made, and the operation of the elements”. God gave Solomon to understand how this world was made and how the elements operate.
- The operation of the elements is what we call the periodic table today. The elements in soil are the same ones in man: oxygen (46% of the soil, 65% of a human), iron, calcium, sodium, potassium, magnesium. When God made us from the dust of the ground, he took from all these elements and gave them different percentages to make man. That is the formula he showed Solomon. What we call God's creation, or his miracles, is that he understands how to work these elements to form man, to form land, to form sea.
- This is what Esau is trying to do with his so-called science. He is Frankenstein, trying to become God and do what God has done.
- “For in his hand are both we and our words; all wisdom also, and knowledge of workmanship. For he hath given me certain knowledge of the things that are, namely, to know how the world was made, and the operation of the elements.”
