## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 19 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 19:22](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/19#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/19#v22)</sup> For in all things, O Lord, thou didst magnify thy people, and glorify them, neither didst thou lightly regard them: but didst assist them in every time and place.

- “For in all things, O Lord, thou didst magnify thy people, and glorify them.” In all things the Lord magnified his people, the twelve tribes of Israel. “Neither didst thou lightly regard them: but didst assist them in every time and place.”
- God assisted us in every time and wherever we were. The Lord was always there fighting for us, and even in these last days the Lord will help us.
