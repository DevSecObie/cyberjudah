## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 12 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 9-11, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 12:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/12#v9)</sup> Not that thou wast unable to bring the ungodly under the hand of the righteous in battle, or to destroy them at once with cruel beasts, or with one rough word:

- “Not that thou wast unable to bring the ungodly under the hand of the righteous in battle”, as in the book of Maccabees, “or to destroy them at once with cruel beasts”, like the newly made beasts in Egypt that made men’s hearts faint, “or with one rough word”, as he will do in the end.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 12:10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/12#v10)</sup> But executing thy judgments upon them by little and little, thou gavest them place of repentance, not being ignorant that they were a naughty generation, and that their malice was bred in them, and that their cogitation would never be changed.

- “But executing thy judgments upon them by little and little.” The Lord is taking his time: a flood here, a fire there, an earthquake here, a war there. “Thou gavest them place of repentance, not being ignorant that they were a naughty generation, and that their malice was bred in them.” Their hatred for us is bred in them: a woman in Springfield, Illinois called the police for help and was killed in her own house.
- The Lord created them, so he knows what he created; he knows their pedigree. “And that their cogitation would never be changed”: their way of thinking, the hatred they have for us, cannot change. That is why the cop could shoot a man and say, I don’t even know why I shot you. It is in them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Habakkuk 2:4](/bible/habakkuk/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/habakkuk/2#v4)</sup> Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith.

    Read again to set up Wisdom 12: “Behold, his soul which is lifted up is not upright in him: but the just shall live by his faith.” Remember, his soul is not right in him. Wisdom 12 shows why: his malice was bred in him and his cogitation will never be changed.
  - **[Ezekiel 35:5](/bible/ezekiel/35#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/35#v5)</sup> Because thou hast had a perpetual hatred, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time that their iniquity had an end:

    “Because thou hast had a perpetual hatred, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time that their iniquity had an end.” Innocent blood is shed again: while we are here struggling and suffering, they are still killing us. A perpetual hatred is the malice bred in them, and so he executes judgment on them little by little.
  - **[Genesis 27:41](/bible/genesis/27#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/genesis/27#v41)</sup> And Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him: and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning for my father are at hand; then will I slay my brother Jacob.

    “And Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him: and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning for my father are at hand; then will I slay my brother Jacob.” That is the malice bred in him and the cogitation that can never be changed, right there in Genesis. He made up his mind from young to kill Jacob, and that thought has continued generation to generation. It started with Cain and Abel, because Esau is Cain regenerated.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 12:11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/12#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/12#v11)</sup> For it was a cursed seed from the beginning; neither didst thou for fear of any man give them pardon for those things wherein they sinned.

- “For it was a cursed seed from the beginning.” These people are cursed by the almighty God; from creation God made them to be the evil of this planet. “Neither didst thou for fear of any man give them pardon for those things wherein they sinned.” There is no pardon, no grace, nothing for them. Their neck is on the chopping block; they can jump, yell and shout, but they will get the business the Lord set up for them.
