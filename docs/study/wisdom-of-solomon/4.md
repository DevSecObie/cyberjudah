## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, 8-9, 12, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v1)</sup> Better it is to have no children, and to have virtue: for the memorial thereof is immortal: because it is known with God, and with men.

- Some women have no children and no husband, and people make them feel like they are nothing. This is what the Bible says: “Better it is to have no children, and to have virtue.” Remember, who can find a virtuous woman? You can be a woman with no kids and no husband and still have your virtue, meaning you are right in the eyes of God. Some women rush into a relationship, or see others in relationships and want to jump into one; some are single for a reason.
- “For the memorial thereof is immortal”: nobody has anything bad to say about you, because you were not sleeping around and did not become a baby mom, and it is going to get you the kingdom. “Because it is known with God, and with men”: men are not saying you are promiscuous, and God sees that you are keeping yourself for him. You are following God’s plan, not having a baby just to have a baby, like the strong independent black woman who says, I don’t need no man for a kid. That does not make her strong; everything she is doing is dead wrong.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v2)</sup> When it is present, men take example at it; and when it is gone, they desire it: it weareth a crown, and triumpheth for ever, having gotten the victory, striving for undefiled rewards.

- “When it is present, men take example at it”: they see you are not sleeping around; they see a woman of God. “And when it is gone, they desire it”: once you let the wrong man defile you, then you want it back. “It weareth a crown, and triumpheth for ever”: you are going to get eternal life when you do things right. “Having gotten the victory”: it is not bad to realize, I have not found the right person, so I am going to stay celibate and keep my virtue. “Striving for undefiled rewards”: the reward is the kingdom.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v3)</sup> But the multiplying brood of the ungodly shall not thrive, nor take deep rooting from bastard slips, nor lay any fast foundation.

- “But the multiplying brood of the ungodly shall not thrive”: the women who say, I don’t need no man, with a whole bunch of kids and no man. Nothing good is going to happen for those kids. “Nor take deep rooting from bastard slips”, because they have no daddy, “nor lay any fast foundation”. They have no sure foundation in this world, because the mother wanted to be a strong independent woman and have kids with no father around, to show everybody, I can be the mommy and the daddy.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v4)</sup> For though they flourish in branches for a time; yet standing not fast, they shall be shaken with the wind, and through the force of winds they shall be rooted out.

- “They shall be shaken with the wind, and through the force of winds they shall be rooted out.” When life catches up to them, she will have to deal with their problems, because she decided to be a strong black woman and have kids out of wedlock out of hating men. The winds are life: when trials come, those kids are going to fall, because a sure foundation is a loving husband and a loving wife.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v5)</sup> The imperfect branches shall be broken off, their fruit unprofitable, not ripe to eat, yea, meet for nothing.

- “The imperfect branches shall be broken off.” Why imperfect? Because the strong independent woman said, I don’t need no man, I can do it by myself. “Their fruit unprofitable, not ripe to eat, yea, meet for nothing”: the children brought forth that way, God says, are nothing.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v6)</sup> For children begotten of unlawful beds are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial.

- “For children begotten of unlawful beds.” Why unlawful? Because she said, I don’t need no man, I’ll get pregnant and have the baby. Even in vitro fertilization is not of God; it is unlawful, taking strange semen, DNA from another man, without even caring where it came from, like the couple who took a white man’s DNA to make a baby and are now on the magazines as the happy family. That child goes against God; they believe the white man is their savior. Going to a sperm bank to create a child is not according to God, and such children are of no value in God’s eyes. They “are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial”: the mother is wicked.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v8)</sup> For honourable age is not that which standeth in length of time, nor that is measured by number of years.

- “For honourable age is not that which standeth in length of time, nor that is measured by number of years.” Honourable age is not about how old you are. Some say they are too old for these young men to tell them what to do. God does not care how old you are; you can be old and an old dummy that nobody wants to listen to.
- An older woman with gray hair, married three times, who you would think should have wisdom to guide the young sisters, tells her daughter to have sex before marriage and to live with a man before marriage. That is an evil woman, and why the black community is the way it is.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v9)**

**[Wisdom of Solomon 4:12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v12)</sup> For the bewitching of naughtiness doth obscure things that are honest; and the wandering of concupiscence doth undermine the simple mind.

- Remember Cain’s seed, the foundational Caucasian Antichrist. “For the bewitching of naughtiness doth obscure things that are honest.” Bewitching, and who is the father of witchcraft? Cain.
- “And the wandering of concupiscence doth undermine the simple mind.” Concupiscence is lust. That is the other law in your members telling you to go here and there, listen to this, watch this, and it undermines the simple mind.
- To undermine is to make someone weaker and less effective, gradually, secretly or indirectly; to erode confidence, authority and trust in a group. That is why the BBC and the SPLC are used: to erode your confidence in where you congregate. In its physical sense, to undermine is to dig beneath a structure, or to wash away the material underneath until it collapses. What do you wash with? Water. And what is the serpent’s voice? A flood of waters. The media is the water.
- When water flows continuously against a rock, it slowly erodes the softer layers beneath; as the foundation is hollowed out, the rock loses its support and collapses. That is undermining through bewitching, and that is Satan’s end goal for every one of us. From the beginning they dealt in music through witchcraft, as mediums of Satan; he will surely weaponize it to destroy our minds in Christ.
