## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7-11, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 8:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v7)</sup> And if a man love righteousness, her labours are virtues: for she teacheth temperance and prudence, justice and fortitude: which are such things, as men can have nothing more profitable in their life.

- “And if a man love righteousness, her labours are virtues: for she teacheth temperance”: to maintain your calm, your cool. “And prudence”: wisdom. “Justice”: the Bible teaches real justice. Black folks are the most forgiving people on earth: they gun us down and hang us, and we forgive because Jesus is white. When Botham Jean was killed in his own apartment by Amber Guyger, his brother gave her a hug, the judge gave her a hug and a Bible, and the court officer combed her hair. No wisdom. Justice is God's vengeance: he that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity, he that lives by the sword must die by the sword. Outside of that is feelings and emotions, Barney and Sesame Street, not Bible.
- “And fortitude.” Fortitude is courage in pain or adversity: bravery, strength of mind and of character, moral strength, toughness of spirit, firmness of purpose, resilience, backbone, spine, mettle, nerve, fearlessness, valor, stoutheartedness, endurance, steadfastness, patience, longsuffering, tenacity, perseverance, resolve, determination, guts, grit. From the Latin, strength, force, firmness, manliness. Wisdom gives you all those attributes. Some of these Israelites and pastors lack testicular fortitude on the highest level.
- It is emphasized because, as was said weeks ago, a time will come when the Israelites out here get scared at the acts of the prophets and become podcast prophets: no more street teaching, nations can be saved, we love everybody. “Which are such things, as men can have nothing more profitable in their life”: there is nothing more profitable in your life than being courageous, wise and patient.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 8:8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v8)</sup> If a man desire much experience, she knoweth things of old, and conjectureth aright what is to come: she knoweth the subtilties of speeches, and can expound dark sentences: she foreseeth signs and wonders, and the events of seasons and times.

- “If a man desire much experience, she knoweth things of old, and conjectureth aright what is to come”: she can figure out what is to come, the prophecies. “She knoweth the subtilties of speeches, and can expound dark sentences”: she can go into the Bible and bring out prophecies that people don't see though they are right there in front of them. “She foreseeth signs and wonders, and the events of seasons and times.”
- Iran, the Arabs, the Israelis, the war: the bishops, the deacons and the captains bring it out in class all the time. We are foreseeing seasons and times. There is no time to sit around justifying multiple girlfriends; arguing about multiple vaginas is a child's game. We have grown folks' business to deal with.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 8:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v9)</sup> Therefore I purposed to take her to me to live with me, knowing that she would be a counsellor of good things, and a comfort in cares and grief.

- “Therefore I purposed to take her to me to live with me, knowing that she would be a counsellor of good things, and a comfort in cares and grief.” That is why wisdom is called she: she is a companion like a wife, your comfort. And what did Christ call it? A comforter.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 8:10-11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v10)</sup> For her sake I shall have estimation among the multitude, and honour with the elders, though I be young.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v11)</sup> I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgment, and shall be admired in the sight of great men.

- “For her sake I shall have estimation among the multitude, and honour with the elders, though I be young.” Solomon became king young, but he had honor because of the wisdom God gave him, and estimation among the elders who were before him and walked with David. “I shall be found of a quick conceit in judgment, and shall be admired in the sight of great men.” Over time people will begin to admire you because of the words of wisdom you teach out of this book.
