## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 18 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7-8, 13-16, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)</sup> So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies.

- Watch this part, for you John 3:16 people out there. “So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous”: yeah, they accept that part of the Bible. “And destruction of the enemies”: I don’t know about that part right there, John 3:16 says. What about John 3:16, my brother? The Bible says you have to accept both, the salvation of the righteous and the destruction of the enemies. That is what it done said. You get people who say they don’t accept it; then you ain’t gonna make it. Goodbye.
- “So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies.” We must change and accept this: you have to accept the salvation of the righteous, and you have to accept the destruction of the enemies. There is no salvation for the righteous without the destruction of our enemies.
- And who are our enemies? Esau, Edom, the Caucasian, the Caucasus mountain man, the caveman. They must fall. That is the war between black and white, and that is why the heathen rage.
- There will be no revolution without bloodshed, but we are not the ones picking up guns to fight. Nobody upstages the King: a king always goes to war first, on the front line, and he sheds the first blood.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 43:1-2](/bible/isaiah/43#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/43#v1)</sup> But now thus saith the Lord that created thee, O Jacob, and he that formed thee, O Israel, Fear not: for I have redeemed thee, I have called thee by thy name; thou art mine.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/43#v2)</sup> When thou passest through the waters, I will be with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee: when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned; neither shall the flame kindle upon thee.

    Go to ancient Egypt, the ransom. Israel couldn’t say, oh, we don’t want Egypt destroyed. Those Israelites that thought like that got put to death.
  - **[2 Thessalonians 1:6](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-thessalonians/1#v6)</sup> Seeing it is a righteous thing with God to recompense tribulation to them that trouble you;

    “It is a righteous thing with God to recompense tribulation to them that trouble you”. Those who trouble us, our God will intervene against and recompense with tribulation. They are going to catch hell. He is going to destroy them.
  - **[Luke 1:68-75](/bible/luke/1#v68)**
    > <sup>[68](/bible/luke/1#v68)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people,
    >
    > <sup>[69](/bible/luke/1#v69)</sup> And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David;
    >
    > <sup>[70](/bible/luke/1#v70)</sup> As he spake by the mouth of his holy prophets, which have been since the world began:
    >
    > <sup>[71](/bible/luke/1#v71)</sup> That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us;
    >
    > <sup>[72](/bible/luke/1#v72)</sup> To perform the mercy promised to our fathers, and to remember his holy covenant;
    >
    > <sup>[73](/bible/luke/1#v73)</sup> The oath which he sware to our father Abraham,
    >
    > <sup>[74](/bible/luke/1#v74)</sup> That he would grant unto us, that we being delivered out of the hand of our enemies might serve him without fear,
    >
    > <sup>[75](/bible/luke/1#v75)</sup> In holiness and righteousness before him, all the days of our life.

    “Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people.” His people is the Israelites; it is possessive. “That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us.”

**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v8)</sup> For wherewith thou didst punish our adversaries, by the same thou didst glorify us, whom thou hadst called.

- “For wherewith thou didst punish our adversaries, by the same thou didst glorify us, whom thou hadst called.” This was a revolution.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:13](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v13)</sup> For whereas they would not believe any thing by reason of the enchantments; upon the destruction of the firstborn, they acknowledged this people to be the sons of God.

- It was always Christ, brothers and sisters. It was always the word of God, always him: that’s who we dealt with right through.
- “For whereas they would not believe any thing by reason of the enchantments; upon the destruction of the firstborn”: this is what happened in Egypt when we came out of Egypt, when God was destroying all the firstborns in Egypt. Now who is the God that was doing this? Keep reading. “They acknowledged this people to be the sons of God”: they acknowledged, these people are the sons of God.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v14)</sup> For while all things were in quiet silence, and that night was in the midst of her swift course,

- “For while all things were in quiet silence”: while everybody was sleeping, sleeping on the Passover in the night, “and that night was in the midst of her swift course”.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v15)</sup> Thine Almighty word leaped down from heaven out of thy royal throne, as a fierce man of war into the midst of a land of destruction,

- “Thine Almighty word leaped down from heaven out of thy royal throne, as a fierce man of war into the midst of a land of destruction”. The Almighty Word is Christ.
- “Thine Almighty word”: the Almighty word is the word of God; this is talking about the Lord. While the Egyptians were sleeping, the Almighty word leaped down “from heaven out of thy royal throne”: the royal throne that Isaiah saw him in. He came out of that throne. He leaped out of that throne saying, I’m about to get dirty right now, I’m about to get bloody right now, “as a fierce man of war into the midst of a land of destruction”. He destroyed things when he came; when he jumped down he started killing them Egyptians.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 1:1](/bible/john/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/john/1#v1)</sup> In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.

    “In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.” The Almighty Word that leaped down from heaven out of the royal throne is Christ.
  - **[John 1:14](/bible/john/1#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/john/1#v14)</sup> And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.

    “And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us”. The Word that leaped down from heaven came down and dwelt among us: Christ, the Word made flesh, is the fierce man of war who comes down into the midst of a land of destruction.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 18:16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v16)</sup> And brought thine unfeigned commandment as a sharp sword, and standing up filled all things with death; and it touched the heaven, but it stood upon the earth.

- “And brought thine unfeigned commandment as a sharp sword, and standing up filled all things with death; and it touched the heaven, but it stood upon the earth.” He fills this whole earth with death.
- He brought the commandment “as a sharp sword”, and standing up he filled all things with death: he killed all the firstborn in Egypt with death. Who did that? The Almighty word, the Lord, leaping off from his throne.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 4:12](/bible/hebrews/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/4#v12)</sup> For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

    “For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.” That is the unfeigned commandment brought as a sharp sword.
