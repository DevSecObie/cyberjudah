## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 17 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 12, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 17:12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/17#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/17#v12)</sup> For fear is nothing else but a betraying of the succours which reason offereth.

- Man deals with reality. When we can’t see a future for our families or ourselves, and have no faith to advance beyond where we are, we choose the easier way out rather than challenge what calls for discipline; and it is a betraying comfort to do nothing. Everything IUIC has built, the departments, the missions overseas, the diaspora outreach, the pantries for the famine to come, did not exist at the beginning. It took vision where nothing was yet seen. That is what men do: they prepare. It is no surprise when we do it, because we used the medicine in this Bible and we know the God behind us.
- “For fear is nothing else but a betraying of the succours which reason offereth.” Succours means comfort. The fear is the fear of challenging what we don’t want to face. Challenge the weight instead of making excuses for it; challenge your addiction to alcohol instead of finding a scripture to abuse and saying the Bible told me I could. Fear lets you lie that you don’t need real effort, and hands you a betraying comfort to do something else.
- It addresses the danger of abandoning logic, and logic is handling the situation in real time. In moments of panic, instead of doing what we are supposed to do, we abandon the logical solution for a fantasy, a betraying lie that says we can get the same result. The answer is no: you have to go through the strait gate to get the real results. Sound judgment is a real effort to correct alcoholic behavior, the discipline to curb the fat you’re building. Running to alcohol, dope, fornication or a video-game woman, instead of getting your finances and yourself together to acquire a real wife, is a betraying comfort, and it ends in destruction.
- Reason is what the enemy fears. King Leopold II told the missionaries he sent into the Congo, mercenaries really, to teach the students to read and not to reason: to preach that the poor are happy, to separate the children from their parents, and to make the people despise everything that gives them courage to confront the colonizers. Reasoning is the cognitive process of using existing knowledge and facts to draw conclusions and solve problems; it bridges raw information and rational action. The reasonable solutions are available right around us. That is what the stewardship program was: canvassing the congregation for its doctors, lawyers, nurses, engineers and electricians and organizing them, so that Hope and Healing and the doula program meet real problems with real help. That is not selling out; that is thinking like men.
