## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 6-21, 23-24, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 2:1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/2#v1)</sup> For the ungodly said, reasoning with themselves, but not aright, Our life is short and tedious, and in the death of a man there is no remedy: neither was there any man known to have returned from the grave.

- “For the ungodly said, reasoning with themselves, but not aright.” That was the 250 princes with Moses: the ungodly get together and reason, and they think they are using the Bible, and they use it all wrong. Look at their fruits: zero. They come up shrivelled up and dead.
- “Our life is short and tedious, and in the death of a man there is no remedy.” Many people live for this present time, as if this life were the end. So they say, let’s live it up right now. They are not reasoning aright.
- “For the ungodly said, reasoning with themselves, but not aright, Our life is short and tedious”, “neither was there any man known to have returned from the grave.” The ungodly starts with the white man, followed by the other nations, and our people who follow them. They do not believe in Christ or the resurrection.
- Empires last about 250 years and go through six ages: pioneers, conquest, commerce, affluence, intellect and decadence. The age of conquest is Christopher Columbus discovering America when millions of people were already living here. The age of commerce is when they enslaved the natives, sold them, made them work the land and sold the goods. Then comes affluence, the golden ages, then intellect, and then decadence, because good times bring weak men.
