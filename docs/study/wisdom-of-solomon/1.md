## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-6, 8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v3)</sup> For froward thoughts separate from God: and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise.

- “For froward thoughts separate from God.” This is where we fall off the horse: you have your own mind. A froward thought is your worldly thought, and it separates you from God because you are not on the same playing field the Lord has you on. You want to do what you want to do, not what the Lord is telling you to do.
- You think you know better than the Lord. You think you know better than the men that are over you, the men that rule over you. That means your mind is not on the Lord at all; you have separated yourself by your own thoughts, because you are doing your own damn thing. And that means, ultimately, you lack character. You lack integrity.
- Froward thoughts separate from God, “and his power, when it is tried, reproveth the unwise.”
- This is a fix-your-spirit class, and we all have to fix our spirit, because Satan is coming. When we thought we were good, we still had a long way to go. People are going to be put in compromising positions where they have to use integrity and good character to get out of it, so we have to tighten up our spirit and keep building it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v4)</sup> For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter; nor dwell in the body that is subject unto sin.

- “For into a malicious soul wisdom shall not enter.” Wisdom cannot enter somebody with a malicious soul: a person that cannot be corrected, a person without good moral character, whose mind is not on what the Lord’s mind is on. Wisdom cannot stay there.
- That is why some brothers are perceptive cons. They know all the scriptures, but they cannot keep rank and they are always having issues. You know all these scriptures; why are you not applying them? Because of bad judgment and bad moral character.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)</sup> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

- Earlier in Baruch 4:13 it said the paths of discipline are his commandments. Now: “For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit”. The holy spirit of what? Discipline. Another word for spirit is ghost; holy ghost and holy spirit are synonymous. So the Holy Ghost is the ghost of discipline, and it will “remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.”
- That is why Christ had twelve disciples: they were under the discipline of the commandments. The Holy Spirit is the spirit of discipline.
- Pray and fast that the Lord keep you disciplined, change your appetite and strip away the cravings for sugar and processed carbs.
- “For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit”. If you are deceitful, lying to yourself and others about what you eat, the holy spirit of discipline will not live with you.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v6)</sup> For wisdom is a loving spirit; and will not acquit a blasphemer of his words: for God is witness of his reins, and a true beholder of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.

- “For wisdom is a loving spirit”. Wisdom, God’s commandments, is a loving spirit; the Holy Spirit is wisdom. A lot of you Christian pastors, modern Christianity cult leaders, do not have the Holy Ghost. The spirit is not in you. You’re running around having seizures, looking crazy; you got some kind of disease going on. That’s not the Holy Spirit. You’re making that up. It’s an act, a show, a performance.
- “And will not acquit a blasphemer of his words”. Blasphemy is a man that says he has done greater works than Christ. No you haven’t … no you have not. No one has, because the Father through his Son made the worlds. Have you made worlds before? Have you made angels, made planets? The hell you talking about? This is your leader, this is your pastor. “For God is witness of his reins, and a true beholder of his heart, and a hearer of his tongue.”

**[Wisdom of Solomon 1:8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v8)</sup> Therefore he that speaketh unrighteous things cannot be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punisheth, pass by him.

- “Therefore he that speaketh unrighteous things cannot be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punisheth, pass by him.” You speak unrighteousness, and eventually it is going to come out. When you lack character and integrity and do something you know is wrong, those things are going to come out. There is no getting around it.
- Sometimes things come out and we do not even know how the Lord brought them out, but they came out when they were supposed to. That brother was not going to say anything. That sister was going to maintain her innocence the whole time, while she was out doing wickedness on the job, in the parking lot, where she thought nobody in the congregation could see her. You did not think we would find out? These things happen in Israel, brothers and sisters doing wild things, and how they get found out is wild: somebody sees them in a store or in a car with somebody, and it all comes out. The Lord will reveal it.
