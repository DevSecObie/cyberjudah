## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 9 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 14-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 9:14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v14)</sup> For the thoughts of mortal men are miserable, and our devices are but uncertain.

- “For the thoughts of mortal men are miserable, and our devices are but uncertain”, because we are in this veil.
- Back to what the Deacon was bringing forth. Brothers and sisters online, you have to come out for your own understanding. That’s why uploading God’s laws, the 15 minutes with the captains, the clubhouse, is very important for brothers and sisters to attend.
- “For the thoughts of mortal men are miserable.” God already got it detailed: your carnal mind, your Americanized Babylonian mind, is miserable. You think you know it all, you think you got the game plan, the ultimate plan, but God says your way of thinking is carnal. God told you his thoughts are not your thoughts. This is why he has things in place, why the classes are in place, why class today is important. But a lot of brothers are in bed asleep, still not paying attention.
- “And our devices are but uncertain”: our ways, our schemes, our plans and goals are uncertain. I want to do it my way, I don’t want to do it the way God said. I don’t have time for the wife, I don’t have time for the kids, I don’t have time for the truth; all I have is five minutes and I’m going to sleep.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 9:15-16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)</sup> For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v16)</sup> And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth, and with labour do we find the things that are before us: but the things that are in heaven who hath searched out?

- “For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things. And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth, and with labour do we find the things that are before us”. Read it again: hardly do we guess aright about the things that are upon the earth. “But the things that are in heaven who hath searched out?”
- “For the corruptible body presseth down the soul”: this is the body, corruptible, the thing that makes us drift. It presses down the soul, and it goes back to Galatians 5:17.
- “And the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.” You are going to meditate on something. Your mind is never void and blank; you are always meditating on something. That is why you have to direct your paths, and meditate on the scriptures.
- “And hardly do we guess aright at things that are upon earth.” Our understanding of the scriptures comes through great labor and through revelation; God has to give it to you, and God gives the increase. And the veil of the flesh makes it difficult. We are often wrong when we are not in the spirit and not looking through the lens of the scriptures, and how can you look through it if you are not studying? You won't even know what verse to go to for your nourishment.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 73:26](/bible/psalms/73#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/73#v26)</sup> My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.

    “My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.” The flesh and the heart are going to fail, but God is the strength of the heart and our portion for ever.
  - **[Psalms 143:5](/bible/psalms/143#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/143#v5)</sup> I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the work of thy hands.

    “I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the work of thy hands.” Listen to the word David used: I muse. He would think about why life was the way it is, why we suffer certain conditions; deep, quiet contemplation of God’s works, acts and commandments. Muse and meditate are used interchangeably. Ezra did the same: it was not his mind to know the deep things, he only wanted to know why Israel was suffering what it was going through.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 9:17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v17)</sup> And thy counsel who hath known, except thou give wisdom, and send thy Holy Spirit from above?

- “And thy counsel who hath known”: who knows what God’s counsel is? The only way you’re going to know God’s counsel is “except thou give wisdom, and send thy Holy Spirit from above”. Unless God give wisdom. God sends his Holy Spirit from above to come and dwell in you; that’s how he gives you wisdom. So if God’s spirit, the Holy Spirit, is not dwelling in you, that means you don’t got wisdom.
- God has to send wisdom from above, the Holy Spirit from above. That’s how he makes you know the things he makes known, his counsel, the things in heaven: he’s got to send his spirit and show it to you.
- “And thy counsel who hath known, except thou give wisdom, and send thy Holy Spirit from above?” This is how God gives the wisdom. He sends the Holy Spirit from above just as the manna was sent from above, the spiritual meat.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 14:15-19](/bible/john/14#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/john/14#v16)</sup> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever;
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/john/14#v17)</sup> Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/john/14#v18)</sup> I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/john/14#v19)</sup> Yet a little while, and the world seeth me no more; but ye see me: because I live, ye shall live also.

    How you get the Holy Spirit. Christ said, “If ye love me, keep my commandments.” Everybody say they love Christ, but they don’t want to keep the commandments.
  - **[John 14:26](/bible/john/14#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/john/14#v26)</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

    “But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name”. We read in Wisdom of Solomon that wisdom is going to come from above; that’s how you’re going to understand the things in the heavens. The Most High has got to send wisdom from above.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 9:18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v18)</sup> For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed, and men were taught the things that are pleasing unto thee, and were saved through wisdom.

- “For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed”: that’s how we’re going to be reformed, meaning changed. God has got to send his Holy Spirit upon us. Brothers, do y’all got the Holy Spirit? That’s what I want to hear.
- There are levels to the Holy Spirit: law, understanding, spirit and power. Once you all are in here you got the Holy Spirit on you; now whether or not it’s going to stay with you, that’s dependent on you.
- “For so the ways of them which lived on the earth were reformed”: that is how the souls of those who live on the earth are reformed. “And men were taught the things that are pleasing unto thee”: we know what is right for God and what is wrong. “And were saved through wisdom.”
- This explains why understanding needs continual renewal. When you start drifting, verses like this are what should come to your head to anchor you. It starts with acknowledgement: think of the verses on the flesh, on drifting, on consuming the word every day, and it gives you a hard stop. Then you can assess and get precise about which scriptures you need to meditate on for what you are dealing with. Until you acknowledge that you are going off, you can't prescribe what you need. That is what leads people to drift to the point that they walk away: they did not want to retain all this in their knowledge.
