## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, 9-12, 15-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:1-3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v1)</sup> Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v2)</sup> Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v3)</sup> For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels.

- “Hear therefore, O ye kings, and understand; learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth.” All those kings that ruled are judges of the earth at their appointed times, ruling all nations in their times: “Give ear, ye that rule the people, and glory in the multitude of nations.” “For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels.” All that power and rulership is given to them by God, and the same with us: it was given to us by God.
- The kings and judges are Israelites put in authority, called to learn how to govern. “learn, ye that be judges of the ends of the earth.” The responsibility reaches beyond managing a congregation now: the saints are being prepared to judge the world. Present judgments among our people must be measured in light of that calling.
- “For power is given you of the Lord, and sovereignty from the Highest, who shall try your works, and search out your counsels.” Leadership is power entrusted by God, and he examines both the works and the advice given under that power. A title does not entitle a man to advise on everything. Wrong counsel can condemn the person following it and bring an even greater account upon the man who gave it.
- Measure your words against your actual understanding. When a matter exceeds your strength, have the humility to say that you need to reflect and seek counsel through the next level of leadership. Knowledge can puff a man up into speaking beyond his measure. Study, application and experience are necessary before he can reliably discern good and evil.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 6:2-3](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v2)</sup> Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world? and if the world shall be judged by you, are ye unworthy to judge the smallest matters?
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v3)</sup> Know ye not that we shall judge angels? how much more things that pertain to this life?

    “Do ye not know that the saints shall judge the world?” The world here is the whole world, not only the world of Israel. The promise includes authority over its present rulers. “Know ye not that we shall judge angels?” These angels are the leaders who now rule the earth.
  - **[Hebrews 5:12-14](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)</sup> For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/5#v13)</sup> For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)</sup> But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.

    The Hebrews already knew the law, yet when they ought to have been teachers they needed “the first principles of the oracles of God” again. Before giving counsel, return to those foundations. A man relying on milk remains unskilful in the word of righteousness; strong meat belongs to maturity.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:4-6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v4)</sup> Because, being ministers of his kingdom, ye have not judged aright, nor kept the law, nor walked after the counsel of God;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v5)</sup> Horribly and speedily shall he come upon you: for a sharp judgment shall be to them that be in high places.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v6)</sup> For mercy will soon pardon the meanest: but mighty men shall be mightily tormented.

- “Because, being ministers of his kingdom, ye have not judged aright, nor kept the law, nor walked after the counsel of God; Horribly and speedily shall he come upon you: for a sharp judgment shall be to them that be in high places. For mercy will soon pardon the meanest”, that is, the lowest, “but mighty men shall be mightily tormented.”
- You shall be tormented when you are a king in pride, when you rule in pride, because your counsels, the things you do and your rulership will be judged by God. If it is not according to his ways, he brings you down. That is for every single king he has set up, including all of us, the kings of the earth of Israel.
- “being ministers of his kingdom” applies to the soldier, officer, captain and deacon. These are ministers of God's kingdom, not owners of the people. Their danger is judging wrongly, failing to keep the law themselves and replacing God's counsel with their own.
- Leadership is stewardship of God's heritage. The responsibility is to turn the whole nation in the right direction while providing counsel, empathy, judgment and accountability to the individuals who look for help. Every decision is weighed in heaven by the Most High, Christ and the host with them. A good report before men does not remove the continual trial before God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Job 32:7-8](/bible/job/32#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/job/32#v7)</sup> I said, Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/job/32#v8)</sup> But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.

    “Days should speak, and multitude of years should teach wisdom.” Days points to God, the Ancient of days: let God's counsel speak. Yet there is also a spirit in man, so the leader must distinguish whether the counsel is God's or merely his own.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:9-11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v9)</sup> Unto you therefore, O kings, do I speak, that ye may learn wisdom, and not fall away.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v10)</sup> For they that keep holiness holily shall be judged holy: and they that have learned such things shall find what to answer.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v11)</sup> Wherefore set your affection upon my words; desire them, and ye shall be instructed.

- How can you be sure the Lord is pleased with you serving him? “Unto you therefore, O kings, do I speak, that ye may learn wisdom, and not fall away. For they that keep holiness holily shall be judged holy: and they that have learned such things shall find what to answer.” When you keep that which is holy and remain holy, you learn what to answer: your understanding starts to increase, you increase in this truth, you become more enlightened. “Wherefore set your affection upon my words; desire them, and ye shall be instructed.”
- “Unto you therefore, O kings, do I speak, that ye may learn wisdom, and not fall away. For they that keep holiness holily shall be judged holy: and they that have learned such things shall find what to answer.” “Wherefore set your affection upon my words; desire them, and ye shall be instructed.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:17-18](/bible/sirach/33#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)</sup> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/33#v18)</sup> Hear me, O ye great men of the people, and hearken with your ears, ye rulers of the congregation.

    “Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.” Once you increase in understanding, you begin to acquire the understanding that you are not only learning for yourself but for them that seek learning. You grow and flourish; you learn to teach your people. Things become easy to you and become more clear through repetition and experience.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 32:9](/bible/sirach/32#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/32#v9)</sup> If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.

    “If thou be among great men, make not thyself equal with them; and when ancient men are in place, use not many words.” You begin to learn discretion, to conduct yourself properly among the people, and you teach the people discretion. Discretion is growth. You grow over time, and that’s how you start to realize the Most High is dealing with you.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v12)</sup> Wisdom is glorious, and never fadeth away: yea, she is easily seen of them that love her, and found of such as seek her.

- “Wisdom is glorious, and never fadeth away”, but only if you retain it. Wisdom lets you know to do what you need to do to retain it.
- “Yea, she is easily seen of them that love her”: it becomes clear. The veil is not so obscure, not so dark, but only if you love her. “And found of such as seek her”: only of those who seek her. Your desire has to be that you want it, that you need it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:15-18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v15)</sup> To think therefore upon her is perfection of wisdom: and whoso watcheth for her shall quickly be without care.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v16)</sup> For she goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her, sheweth herself favourably unto them in the ways, and meeteth them in every thought.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v17)</sup> For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v18)</sup> And love is the keeping of her laws; and the giving heed unto her laws is the assurance of incorruption;

- “To think therefore upon her is perfection of wisdom: and whoso watcheth for her shall quickly be without care. For she goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her”. “The very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love; And love is the keeping of her laws; and the giving heed unto her laws is the assurance of incorruption”. One of the main things you’re going to notice about yourself is that you increase in understanding.
- It won’t be like, oh wow, God did this. It takes time before you start noticing those things. People are going to rely on you; you’ll be allowed into circles you weren’t allowed in before, among the great men, the leadership, the captains. When your reputation precedes you, when you are sought after among your superiors and they say that’s a good brother right there, put him over this office, that’s a clear sign the Most High is starting to bless you and deal with you.
- Wisdom “goeth about seeking such as are worthy of her, sheweth herself favourably unto them in the ways, and meeteth them in every thought.” Wisdom has to meet you in every thought. When a thought comes to your mind, measure it: what is the outcome of that thought? Even with good intentions, is there an evil intention hidden in it?
- In an argument, when your husband says something, the first thought may be: say this, it will trigger him, make him feel the way I feel. Those are thoughts being fed to you. What you say can bring a positive or a negative reaction, so use wisdom, filter your thoughts, and make sure they carry godly intentions.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 13:10](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v10)</sup> Therefore I write these things being absent, lest being present I should use sharpness, according to the power which the Lord hath given me to edification, and not to destruction.

    “According to the power which the Lord hath given me to edification, and not to destruction.” The power God gave us is for edification. Every thought that comes to your mind, ask: am I motivating and exhorting this person, or can the way I say it destroy them? Even a good exhortation can land negatively.
  - **[Isaiah 33:6](/bible/isaiah/33#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/33#v6)</sup> And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.

    In these times the only thing that is going to keep us is this Bible. “And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.”

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:19](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v19)</sup> And incorruption maketh us near unto God:

- “And incorruption maketh us near unto God”. So we cannot go back to what our forefathers were doing, being that degenerate plant, that wild grape. We have to be incorruptible, meaning we have to be keeping the laws of God.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 6:20-21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v20)</sup> Therefore the desire of wisdom bringeth to a kingdom.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v21)</sup> If your delight be then in thrones and sceptres, O ye kings of the people, honour wisdom, that ye may reign for evermore.

- “Therefore the desire of wisdom bringeth to a kingdom.” You cannot rule a kingdom without wisdom, and you have to have humility to gain God’s wisdom. “If your delight be then in thrones and sceptres”, if we want to be kings as we were created to be, “O ye kings of the people, honour wisdom, that ye may reign for evermore.” If you walk in pride, you are done for. The only way our kingdom will last forever is when we walk in wisdom and humility toward God. Outside of that the kingdom comes down immediately, as it did with all the nations before us, and today.
