## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 13 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-9, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v1)</sup> Surely vain are all men by nature, who are ignorant of God, and could not out of the good things that are seen know him that is: neither by considering the works did they acknowledge the workmaster;

- “Surely vain are all men by nature, who are ignorant of God, and could not out of the good things that are seen know him that is: neither by considering the works did they acknowledge the workmaster.” They started to forget the Creator of those things.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v2)</sup> But deemed either fire, or wind, or the swift air, or the circle of the stars, or the violent water, or the lights of heaven, to be the gods which govern the world.

- They deemed “fire, or wind, or the swift air, or the circle of the stars”, or “the lights of heaven”, the sun, the moon and the stars, “to be the gods which govern the world.” But they were not.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:3-4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v3)</sup> With whose beauty if they being delighted took them to be gods; let them know how much better the Lord of them is: for the first author of beauty hath created them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v4)</sup> But if they were astonished at their power and virtue, let them understand by them, how much mightier he is that made them.

- “With whose beauty if they being delighted took them to be gods”. They're worshipping other gods. “let them know how much better the Lord of them is: for the first author of beauty hath created them.” He created you after his image. Take this mindset to other young people. It's revolutionary, totally against society. Black people understanding this in the masses will cause a revolt in the world. Young people may receive it more easily from another young person than an old man with a Bible.
- I tell my son he has more wisdom: finesse those teachers. When other kids act up, be nice. Good morning, how was your weekend? That D will turn to a B. My son told a child in front of the teacher, why are you giving him a hard time? He's a good guy. Now the teacher likes him, that little racist crap. Use it as a tool. Play with them, juggle them, toss them around. God gave you wisdom above these nations; tap into it.
- “With whose beauty if they being delighted took them to be gods; let them know how much better the Lord of them is: for the first author of beauty hath created them.” And if they were astonished at their power, let them understand “how much mightier he is that made them.”
- “For the first author of beauty hath created them.” That real beauty is not the damn Statue of Liberty: she is no virgin, and only virgins are really beautiful. The first author of beauty is our God, and he looked at us as beautiful. That's why he refers to us as a woman.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 6:2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)</sup> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.

    “I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.” Comely means beautiful, handsome. That is how the Lord looks at his people.
  - **[Lamentations 2:1](/bible/lamentations/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/lamentations/2#v1)</sup> How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger, and cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel, and remembered not his footstool in the day of his anger!

    “How hath the Lord covered the daughter of Zion with a cloud in his anger, and cast down from heaven unto the earth the beauty of Israel”: cast down from heavenly rulership to earthly servitude. Why would he do that? Because we went off, away from the author who created us.
  - **[Psalms 45:7-11](/bible/psalms/45#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/45#v7)</sup> Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/45#v8)</sup> All thy garments smell of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia, out of the ivory palaces, whereby they have made thee glad.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/45#v9)</sup> Kings’ daughters were among thy honourable women: upon thy right hand did stand the queen in gold of Ophir.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/45#v10)</sup> Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear; forget also thine own people, and thy father’s house;
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/45#v11)</sup> So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty: for he is thy Lord; and worship thou him.

    “Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness”: we have to love righteousness and hate iniquity.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v5)</sup> For by the greatness and beauty of the creatures proportionably the maker of them is seen.

- “For by the greatness and beauty of the creatures”, the greatness and beauty of the sun and moon we are talking about tonight, “proportionably”, a little bit, “the maker of them is seen.”
- What is Christ called? The bridegroom. What is the sun called? The bridegroom. You see a little of him, of his glory, in those things. They represent him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 50:2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)</sup> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined.

    “Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined.” God hath shined: going back to that sun, back to that moon. The maker is seen in the beauty of what he made, and out of Zion he has shined.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:6-8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v6)</sup> But yet for this they are the less to be blamed: for they peradventure err, seeking God, and desirous to find him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v7)</sup> For being conversant in his works they search him diligently, and believe their sight: because the things are beautiful that are seen.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v8)</sup> Howbeit neither are they to be pardoned.

- “But yet for this they are the less to be blamed”: they were seeking God and desirous to find him, and being conversant in his works they searched him diligently and believed their sight, “because the things are beautiful that are seen. Howbeit neither are they to be pardoned.”
- They are not pardoned, because they worship the sun and moon as if those are gods themselves, rather than the God that made them. They do not get off on that; the Most High is going to judge them for it regardless.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 13:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/13#v9)</sup> For if they were able to know so much, that they could aim at the world; how did they not sooner find out the Lord thereof?

- “For if they were able to know so much, that they could aim at the world; how did they not sooner find out the Lord thereof?” If you were so amazed by the creation, why not go back and honor the Creator?
- We learned these things early on, and then we started to worship them. Everybody in this earth searches out what they want to be: to be a doctor you go deep, you search it all out. But when it comes to God, they are confused, ‘I don’t know where God at.’
