## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 5, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 10:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/10#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/10#v5)</sup> Moreover, the nations in their wicked conspiracy being confounded, she found out the righteous, and preserved him blameless unto God, and kept him strong against his tender compassion toward his son.

- “Moreover, the nations in their wicked conspiracy being confounded.” You always hear about conspiracy theories. Esau always puts that out there: any time anybody speaks truth, they throw out, Oh, you’re a conspiracy theorist, so the public won’t believe you.
- But the scriptures say the nations have wicked conspiracies. They have wicked conspiracies against us; they are doing things secretly to destroy our people. There are conspiracies set up on this earth against us. That is the secret hand behind the xenophobia in South Africa: everything going on there, the white man, the CIA and Mossad are behind it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 29:15-16](/bible/isaiah/29#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/29#v15)</sup> Woe unto them that seek deep to hide their counsel from the Lord, and their works are in the dark, and they say, Who seeth us? and who knoweth us?
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/29#v16)</sup> Surely your turning of things upside down shall be esteemed as the potter’s clay: for shall the work say of him that made it, He made me not? or shall the thing framed say of him that framed it, He had no understanding?

    “Woe unto them that seek deep to hide their counsel from the Lord.” There are people seeking to hide the evil they are doing. “And their works are in the dark”: like how these operatives, the CIA and Mossad, go into a country and murder leaders and cause civil unrest, and think nobody sees what they are doing. Doing it in the dark means hiding it.
  - **[Psalms 64:2-6](/bible/psalms/64#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/64#v2)</sup> Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/64#v3)</sup> Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/64#v4)</sup> That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/64#v5)</sup> They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/64#v6)</sup> They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.

    “Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked.” The wicked have secret councils, and we want God to hide us from them. “From the insurrection of the workers of iniquity”: the rising up of the workers of iniquity.
  - **[Habakkuk 3:13-14](/bible/habakkuk/3#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/habakkuk/3#v13)</sup> Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, even for salvation with thine anointed; thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, by discovering the foundation unto the neck. Selah.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/habakkuk/3#v14)</sup> Thou didst strike through with his staves the head of his villages: they came out as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly.

    “Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, even for salvation with thine anointed”: the Lord is coming forth for the salvation of his people, the 12 tribes of Israel. “Thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked”: the head of the house of the wicked is America, and Christ is going to destroy America when he returns. “By discovering the foundation unto the neck”: he is going to cut their head off, and nothing is going to remain.
