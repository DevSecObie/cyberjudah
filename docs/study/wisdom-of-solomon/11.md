## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Wisdom of Solomon 11 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 15-19, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 11:15-16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v15)</sup> But for the foolish devices of their wickedness, wherewith being deceived they worshipped serpents void of reason, and vile beasts, thou didst send a multitude of unreasonable beasts upon them for vengeance;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v16)</sup> That they might know, that wherewithal a man sinneth, by the same also shall he be punished.

- The Egyptians were deceived into worshipping serpents void of reason and vile beasts. God sent unreasonable beasts against them for vengeance, so that they would know a man can be punished by the very thing with which he sins. The creatures they honoured became part of their punishment.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 11:17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v17)</sup> For thy Almighty hand, that made the world of matter without form, wanted not means to send among them a multitude of bears, or fierce lions,

- The Almighty hand made the world from matter without form. God did not lack the power to send multitudes of bears or fierce lions. His choice of creatures for a judgment is not limited by his ability.

**[Wisdom of Solomon 11:18-19](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v18)</sup> Or unknown wild beasts, full of rage, newly created, breathing out either a fiery vapour, or filthy scents of scattered smoke, or shooting horrible sparkles out of their eyes:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/11#v19)</sup> Whereof not only the harm might dispatch them at once, but also the terrible sight utterly destroy them.

- “Or unknown wild beasts, full of rage, newly created”. Esau thinks he has seen everything, but Solomon, the wisest man, speaks of unknown creatures. They are full of rage; you cannot pacify them or tell them to roll over and play dead. They breathe fiery vapour or filthy scents of scattered smoke and shoot horrible sparkles from their eyes. The ideas behind creatures such as Godzilla did not come from nowhere; men have been reading these scriptures.
- Their harm can destroy people, and even the terrible sight can do so. That is why seeing the heavenly beasts could make a person's spirit leave him. God has strange creations in heaven and earth we have not beheld. Think of the angler fish deep in the sea, with great teeth and a light on an antenna: that is only a glimpse of what is below, not proof that we have found it all.
