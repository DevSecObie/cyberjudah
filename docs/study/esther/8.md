**[Esther 8:1-2](/bible/esther/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/esther/8#v1)</sup> On that day did the king Ahasuerus give the house of Haman the Jews’ enemy unto Esther the queen. And Mordecai came before the king; for Esther had told what he was unto her.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/esther/8#v2)</sup> And the king took off his ring, which he had taken from Haman, and gave it unto Mordecai. And Esther set Mordecai over the house of Haman.

- That day the king gave the house of Haman, the Jews' enemy, to Esther, and Mordecai came before the king, for Esther had told what he was to her.
- The king took off the ring he had taken from Haman and gave it to Mordecai, and Esther set Mordecai over the house of Haman. Everything Haman had was given to Mordecai.

**[Esther 8:3-6](/bible/esther/8#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/esther/8#v3)</sup> And Esther spake yet again before the king, and fell down at his feet, and besought him with tears to put away the mischief of Haman the Agagite, and his device that he had devised against the Jews.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/esther/8#v4)</sup> Then the king held out the golden sceptre toward Esther. So Esther arose, and stood before the king,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/esther/8#v5)</sup> And said, If it please the king, and if I have found favour in his sight, and the thing seem right before the king, and I be pleasing in his eyes, let it be written to reverse the letters devised by Haman the son of Hammedatha the Agagite, which he wrote to destroy the Jews which are in all the king’s provinces:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/esther/8#v6)</sup> For how can I endure to see the evil that shall come unto my people? or how can I endure to see the destruction of my kindred?

- Esther fell down at the king's feet and besought him with tears to put away the mischief of Haman the Agagite and the device he had devised against the Jews.
- The king held out the golden scepter, and Esther rose and asked that letters be written to reverse the letters Haman devised to destroy the Jews in all the provinces.
- She asked how she could endure to see the evil that would come upon her people and the destruction of her kindred.

**[Esther 8:7-8](/bible/esther/8#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/esther/8#v7)</sup> Then the king Ahasuerus said unto Esther the queen and to Mordecai the Jew, Behold, I have given Esther the house of Haman, and him they have hanged upon the gallows, because he laid his hand upon the Jews.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/esther/8#v8)</sup> Write ye also for the Jews, as it liketh you, in the king’s name, and seal it with the king’s ring: for the writing which is written in the king’s name, and sealed with the king’s ring, may no man reverse.

- The king told Esther and Mordecai that he had given Esther the house of Haman and had hanged Haman for laying hand upon the Jews.
- He granted them to write for the Jews in the king's name and seal it with the king's ring, for a writing sealed with the king's ring could no man reverse.
- Esther and Mordecai were given the king's authority, so the reputation and word of the king stood behind whatever they wrote.

**[Esther 8:9-10](/bible/esther/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/esther/8#v9)</sup> Then were the king’s scribes called at that time in the third month, that is, the month Sivan, on the three and twentieth day thereof; and it was written according to all that Mordecai commanded unto the Jews, and to the lieutenants, and the deputies and rulers of the provinces which are from India unto Ethiopia, an hundred twenty and seven provinces, unto every province according to the writing thereof, and unto every people after their language, and to the Jews according to their writing, and according to their language.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/esther/8#v10)</sup> And he wrote in the king Ahasuerus’ name, and sealed it with the king’s ring, and sent letters by posts on horseback, and riders on mules, camels, and young dromedaries:

- The king's scribes were called in the third month, Sivan, on the twenty-third day, and it was written to the Jews, lieutenants, deputies, and rulers of the provinces from India to Ethiopia, a hundred twenty and seven provinces, each in its own writing and language.
- This is the span of the Persian empire, and Israel was scattered all through it, from India to Ethiopia, not concentrated in one place but spread out during the captivity, which shows we have history in these lands.

  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    The Most High foretold Israel would be scattered among all people from one end of the earth unto the other.

**[Esther 8:11-14](/bible/esther/8#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/esther/8#v11)</sup> Wherein the king granted the Jews which were in every city to gather themselves together, and to stand for their life, to destroy, to slay, and to cause to perish, all the power of the people and province that would assault them, both little ones and women, and to take the spoil of them for a prey,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/esther/8#v12)</sup> Upon one day in all the provinces of king Ahasuerus, namely, upon the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, which is the month Adar.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/esther/8#v13)</sup> The copy of the writing for a commandment to be given in every province was published unto all people, and that the Jews should be ready against that day to avenge themselves on their enemies.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/esther/8#v14)</sup> So the posts that rode upon mules and camels went out, being hastened and pressed on by the king’s commandment. And the decree was given at Shushan the palace.

- The king granted the Jews in every city to gather, stand for their life, and destroy any power that would assault them, both little ones and women, and to take the spoil.
- Everyone who had listened to Haman's proclamation to kill the Jews was now subject to the Jews' revenge upon their enemies.
- The decree was to be ready upon the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, Adar, the day the Jews would avenge themselves, for nothing pacifies the Most High but vengeance on the enemies of His people.
- The posts rode out hastened on mules, camels, and dromedaries by the king's commandment.

**[Esther 8:15-17](/bible/esther/8#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/esther/8#v15)</sup> And Mordecai went out from the presence of the king in royal apparel of blue and white, and with a great crown of gold, and with a garment of fine linen and purple: and the city of Shushan rejoiced and was glad.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/esther/8#v16)</sup> The Jews had light, and gladness, and joy, and honour.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/esther/8#v17)</sup> And in every province, and in every city, whithersoever the king’s commandment and his decree came, the Jews had joy and gladness, a feast and a good day. And many of the people of the land became Jews; for the fear of the Jews fell upon them.

- Mordecai went out from the king in royal apparel of blue and white, a great crown of gold, and a garment of fine linen and purple, and the city of Shushan rejoiced and was glad.
- The Jews had light, gladness, joy, and honor, and in every province and city where the decree came they had a feast and a good day.
- Many of the people of the land became Jews, for the fear of the Jews fell upon them, a picture of the future when the fear of Israel will be on all nations and they will serve the Most High.
