**[Esther 3:1-4](/bible/esther/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/esther/3#v1)</sup> After these things did king Ahasuerus promote Haman the son of Hammedatha the Agagite, and advanced him, and set his seat above all the princes that were with him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/esther/3#v2)</sup> And all the king’s servants, that were in the king’s gate, bowed, and reverenced Haman: for the king had so commanded concerning him. But Mordecai bowed not, nor did him reverence.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/esther/3#v3)</sup> Then the king’s servants, which were in the king’s gate, said unto Mordecai, Why transgressest thou the king’s commandment?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/esther/3#v4)</sup> Now it came to pass, when they spake daily unto him, and he hearkened not unto them, that they told Haman, to see whether Mordecai’s matters would stand: for he had told them that he was a Jew.

- After these things the king promoted Haman the son of Hammedatha the Agagite and set his seat above all the princes, and all the king's servants in the king's gate bowed and reverenced him, for the king had so commanded, but Mordecai bowed not nor did him reverence.
- The servants asked daily why Mordecai transgressed the king's commandment, and he hearkened not, but told them he was a Jew, and they told Haman to see whether Mordecai's matter would stand. We do not bow, the same as the three Hebrew children did not bow to the image in Babylon, for we pledge allegiance to none but the Most High.

  - **[1 Samuel 15:20](/bible/1-samuel/15#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/1-samuel/15#v20)</sup> And Saul said unto Samuel, Yea, I have obeyed the voice of the Lord, and have gone the way which the Lord sent me, and have brought Agag the king of Amalek, and have utterly destroyed the Amalekites.

    Saul spared Agag king of the Amalekites when he was commanded to utterly destroy them, so the Agagite line lived on and Haman descends from Amalek, which explains his deep disdain for Israel.
  - **[Additions to Esther 13:12-14](/bible/esther-greek/13#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/esther-greek/13#v12)</sup> Thou knowest all things, and thou knowest, Lord, that it was neither in contempt nor pride, nor for any desire of glory, that I did not bow down to proud Aman.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/esther-greek/13#v13)</sup> For I could have been content with good will for the salvation of Israel to kiss the soles of his feet.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/esther-greek/13#v14)</sup> But I did this, that I might not prefer the glory of man above the glory of God: neither will I worship any but thee, O God, neither will I do it in pride.

    Mordecai's own prayer shows he refused to bow not from contempt but so as not to set the glory of man above the glory of the Most High.

**[Esther 3:5-6](/bible/esther/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/esther/3#v5)</sup> And when Haman saw that Mordecai bowed not, nor did him reverence, then was Haman full of wrath.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/esther/3#v6)</sup> And he thought scorn to lay hands on Mordecai alone; for they had shewed him the people of Mordecai: wherefore Haman sought to destroy all the Jews that were throughout the whole kingdom of Ahasuerus, even the people of Mordecai.

- When Haman saw that Mordecai bowed not nor did him reverence, he was full of wrath, and thought scorn to lay hands on Mordecai alone once they had shown him the people of Mordecai.
- Haman sought to destroy all the Jews throughout the whole kingdom of Ahasuerus, the people of Mordecai, not one man only. Because Mordecai would not bow, Edom's hatred sought a death sentence for the entire nation, seeking to erase us out of history.

**[Esther 3:7-11](/bible/esther/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/esther/3#v7)</sup> In the first month, that is, the month Nisan, in the twelfth year of king Ahasuerus, they cast Pur, that is, the lot, before Haman from day to day, and from month to month, to the twelfth month, that is, the month Adar.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/esther/3#v8)</sup> And Haman said unto king Ahasuerus, There is a certain people scattered abroad and dispersed among the people in all the provinces of thy kingdom; and their laws are diverse from all people; neither keep they the king’s laws: therefore it is not for the king’s profit to suffer them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/esther/3#v9)</sup> If it please the king, let it be written that they may be destroyed: and I will pay ten thousand talents of silver to the hands of those that have the charge of the business, to bring it into the king’s treasuries.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/esther/3#v10)</sup> And the king took his ring from his hand, and gave it unto Haman the son of Hammedatha the Agagite, the Jews’ enemy.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/esther/3#v11)</sup> And the king said unto Haman, The silver is given to thee, the people also, to do with them as it seemeth good to thee.

- In the first month, Nisan, in the twelfth year of the king, they cast Pur, that is the lot, before Haman from day to day and month to month, to the twelfth month, Adar.
- Haman told the king there was a certain people scattered abroad whose laws were diverse from all people and who kept not the king's laws, and it was not for the king's profit to suffer them, offering ten thousand talents of silver to have them destroyed. The king took his ring and gave it to Haman the Jews' enemy, granting him the silver and the people to do as he pleased. This is putting a hit out on the entire nation of Israel.

**[Esther 3:12-15](/bible/esther/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/esther/3#v12)</sup> Then were the king’s scribes called on the thirteenth day of the first month, and there was written according to all that Haman had commanded unto the king’s lieutenants, and to the governors that were over every province, and to the rulers of every people of every province according to the writing thereof, and to every people after their language; in the name of king Ahasuerus was it written, and sealed with the king’s ring.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/esther/3#v13)</sup> And the letters were sent by posts into all the king’s provinces, to destroy, to kill, and to cause to perish, all Jews, both young and old, little children and women, in one day, even upon the thirteenth day of the twelfth month, which is the month Adar, and to take the spoil of them for a prey.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/esther/3#v14)</sup> The copy of the writing for a commandment to be given in every province was published unto all people, that they should be ready against that day.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/esther/3#v15)</sup> The posts went out, being hastened by the king’s commandment, and the decree was given in Shushan the palace. And the king and Haman sat down to drink; but the city Shushan was perplexed.

- On the thirteenth day of the first month the king's scribes wrote according to all Haman commanded, to the king's lieutenants, governors and rulers of every province in the king's name and sealed with the king's ring.
- Letters were sent by posts to destroy, kill and cause to perish all Jews, young and old, little children and women, in one day, the thirteenth of the twelfth month Adar, and to take the spoil for prey. The copy was published in every province so they should be ready, and the king and Haman sat down to drink while the city Shushan was perplexed.
