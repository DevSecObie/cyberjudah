**[Esther 9:1-4](/bible/esther/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/esther/9#v1)</sup> Now in the twelfth month, that is, the month Adar, on the thirteenth day of the same, when the king’s commandment and his decree drew near to be put in execution, in the day that the enemies of the Jews hoped to have power over them, (though it was turned to the contrary, that the Jews had rule over them that hated them;)
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/esther/9#v2)</sup> The Jews gathered themselves together in their cities throughout all the provinces of the king Ahasuerus, to lay hand on such as sought their hurt: and no man could withstand them; for the fear of them fell upon all people.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/esther/9#v3)</sup> And all the rulers of the provinces, and the lieutenants, and the deputies, and officers of the king, helped the Jews; because the fear of Mordecai fell upon them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/esther/9#v4)</sup> For Mordecai was great in the king’s house, and his fame went out throughout all the provinces: for this man Mordecai waxed greater and greater.

- In the twelfth month Adar, on the thirteenth day, when the enemies of the Jews hoped to have power over them, it was turned to the contrary and the Jews had rule over those that hated them.
- The Jews gathered in their cities, and no man could withstand them, for the fear of them fell upon all people, and the rulers, lieutenants, deputies, and officers helped the Jews because the fear of Mordecai fell upon them.
- Mordecai was great in the king's house and his fame went throughout all the provinces, and this man waxed greater and greater. Though Esther was queen, she was still subject to Mordecai, the man of Israel who wrote the edict.

**[Esther 9:5-10](/bible/esther/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/esther/9#v5)</sup> Thus the Jews smote all their enemies with the stroke of the sword, and slaughter, and destruction, and did what they would unto those that hated them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/esther/9#v6)</sup> And in Shushan the palace the Jews slew and destroyed five hundred men.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/esther/9#v7)</sup> And Parshandatha, and Dalphon, and Aspatha,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/esther/9#v8)</sup> And Poratha, and Adalia, and Aridatha,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/esther/9#v9)</sup> And Parmashta, and Arisai, and Aridai, and Vajezatha,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/esther/9#v10)</sup> The ten sons of Haman the son of Hammedatha, the enemy of the Jews, slew they; but on the spoil laid they not their hand.

- The Jews smote all their enemies with the sword, slaughter, and destruction, and did what they would to those that hated them.
- In Shushan the palace they slew and destroyed five hundred men, and they slew the ten sons of Haman the son of Hammedatha, the enemy of the Jews.
- On the spoil they laid not their hands.

**[Esther 9:11-15](/bible/esther/9#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/esther/9#v11)</sup> On that day the number of those that were slain in Shushan the palace was brought before the king.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/esther/9#v12)</sup> And the king said unto Esther the queen, The Jews have slain and destroyed five hundred men in Shushan the palace, and the ten sons of Haman; what have they done in the rest of the king’s provinces? now what is thy petition? and it shall be granted thee: or what is thy request further? and it shall be done.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/esther/9#v13)</sup> Then said Esther, If it please the king, let it be granted to the Jews which are in Shushan to do to morrow also according unto this day’s decree, and let Haman’s ten sons be hanged upon the gallows.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/esther/9#v14)</sup> And the king commanded it so to be done: and the decree was given at Shushan; and they hanged Haman’s ten sons.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/esther/9#v15)</sup> For the Jews that were in Shushan gathered themselves together on the fourteenth day also of the month Adar, and slew three hundred men at Shushan; but on the prey they laid not their hand.

- The number slain in Shushan was brought before the king, who told Esther the Jews had slain five hundred men and the ten sons of Haman, and asked what more she desired.
- Esther asked that the Jews in Shushan be granted the next day also according to the decree, and that Haman's ten sons be hanged on the gallows.
- The king commanded it done, and the Jews in Shushan gathered on the fourteenth day and slew three hundred more, but on the spoil they laid not their hands.

**[Esther 9:16-19](/bible/esther/9#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/esther/9#v16)</sup> But the other Jews that were in the king’s provinces gathered themselves together, and stood for their lives, and had rest from their enemies, and slew of their foes seventy and five thousand, but they laid not their hands on the prey,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/esther/9#v17)</sup> On the thirteenth day of the month Adar; and on the fourteenth day of the same rested they, and made it a day of feasting and gladness.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/esther/9#v18)</sup> But the Jews that were at Shushan assembled together on the thirteenth day thereof, and on the fourteenth thereof; and on the fifteenth day of the same they rested, and made it a day of feasting and gladness.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/esther/9#v19)</sup> Therefore the Jews of the villages, that dwelt in the unwalled towns, made the fourteenth day of the month Adar a day of gladness and feasting, and a good day, and of sending portions one to another.

- The other Jews in the king's provinces gathered, stood for their lives, had rest from their enemies, and slew of their foes seventy and five thousand, laying not their hands on the prey.
- This was on the thirteenth day of Adar, and on the fourteenth they rested and made a day of feasting and gladness.
- The Jews of Shushan rested on the fifteenth day, so the Jews of the unwalled towns made the fourteenth a day of gladness, feasting, and sending portions one to another. This is where the gift giving of Purim comes from.

**[Esther 9:20-22](/bible/esther/9#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/esther/9#v20)</sup> And Mordecai wrote these things, and sent letters unto all the Jews that were in all the provinces of the king Ahasuerus, both nigh and far,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/esther/9#v21)</sup> To stablish this among them, that they should keep the fourteenth day of the month Adar, and the fifteenth day of the same, yearly,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/esther/9#v22)</sup> As the days wherein the Jews rested from their enemies, and the month which was turned unto them from sorrow to joy, and from mourning into a good day: that they should make them days of feasting and joy, and of sending portions one to another, and gifts to the poor.

- Mordecai wrote these things and sent letters to all the Jews near and far to establish that they keep the fourteenth and fifteenth days of Adar yearly.
- These were the days the Jews rested from their enemies, the month turned from sorrow to joy and from mourning to a good day.
- They were to make them days of feasting, joy, sending portions one to another, and gifts to the poor. This is where the feast of Purim comes from, a feast that commemorates the destruction of Israel's enemies.

**[Esther 9:23-25](/bible/esther/9#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/esther/9#v23)</sup> And the Jews undertook to do as they had begun, and as Mordecai had written unto them;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/esther/9#v24)</sup> Because Haman the son of Hammedatha, the Agagite, the enemy of all the Jews, had devised against the Jews to destroy them, and had cast Pur, that is, the lot, to consume them, and to destroy them;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/esther/9#v25)</sup> But when Esther came before the king, he commanded by letters that his wicked device, which he devised against the Jews, should return upon his own head, and that he and his sons should be hanged on the gallows.

- The Jews undertook to keep what Mordecai wrote, because Haman the son of Hammedatha the Agagite had devised against the Jews to destroy them and had cast Pur, that is the lot, to consume them.
- Pur was the Persian custom of casting lots, much like the lot cast for Jonah to see whose fault the storm was.
- When Esther came before the king, he commanded by letters that Haman's wicked device return upon his own head, and that he and his sons be hanged on the gallows.

  - **[Jonah 1:7](/bible/jonah/1#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/jonah/1#v7)</sup> And they said every one to his fellow, Come, and let us cast lots, that we may know for whose cause this evil is upon us. So they cast lots, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

    The mariners cast lots to know for whose cause the evil was upon them, and the lot fell upon Jonah.

**[Esther 9:26-28](/bible/esther/9#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/esther/9#v26)</sup> Wherefore they called these days Purim after the name of Pur. Therefore for all the words of this letter, and of that which they had seen concerning this matter, and which had come unto them,
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/esther/9#v27)</sup> The Jews ordained, and took upon them, and upon their seed, and upon all such as joined themselves unto them, so as it should not fail, that they would keep these two days according to their writing, and according to their appointed time every year;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/esther/9#v28)</sup> And that these days should be remembered and kept throughout every generation, every family, every province, and every city; and that these days of Purim should not fail from among the Jews, nor the memorial of them perish from their seed.

- These days were called Purim after the name Pur, and the Jews ordained and took upon themselves and their seed to keep these two days every year.
- The days were to be remembered and kept throughout every generation, family, province, and city.
- These days of Purim should not fail from among the Jews nor the memorial of them perish from their seed, which shows Israel still celebrates Purim today.

**[Esther 9:29-32](/bible/esther/9#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/esther/9#v29)</sup> Then Esther the queen, the daughter of Abihail, and Mordecai the Jew, wrote with all authority, to confirm this second letter of Purim.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/esther/9#v30)</sup> And he sent the letters unto all the Jews, to the hundred twenty and seven provinces of the kingdom of Ahasuerus, with words of peace and truth,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/esther/9#v31)</sup> To confirm these days of Purim in their times appointed, according as Mordecai the Jew and Esther the queen had enjoined them, and as they had decreed for themselves and for their seed, the matters of the fastings and their cry.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/esther/9#v32)</sup> And the decree of Esther confirmed these matters of Purim; and it was written in the book.

- Esther the queen, the daughter of Abihail, and Mordecai the Jew wrote with all authority to confirm this second letter of Purim.
- Letters were sent to all the Jews in the hundred twenty and seven provinces with words of peace and truth, to confirm the days of Purim in their appointed times.
- The decree of Esther confirmed these matters of Purim, and it was written in the book, which is Israel's own book of history.
