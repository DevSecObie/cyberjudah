## Introduction

This chapter opens the book of Esther in the days of Ahasuerus, the Persian king who reigned from India even unto Ethiopia over a hundred and twenty seven provinces. It sets the stage for the whole book with a feast, a queen who refuses the king's command, and a decree sent into every province. The thread the teacher draws out is the biblical order of the house: the Most High set the man to bear rule in his own house, and the earth's societies keep reversing that order, putting the woman over the man and even the children over the parents. Vashti's refusal becomes the occasion for a law written among the Persians and Medes that every man should bear rule in his own house. Read this canonical chapter as the record of a kingdom's excess and a decree that, by the providence of the Most High, opens the door for Esther.

**[Esther 1:1-3](/bible/esther/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/esther/1#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass in the days of Ahasuerus, (this is Ahasuerus which reigned, from India even unto Ethiopia, over an hundred and seven and twenty provinces:)
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/esther/1#v2)</sup> That in those days, when the king Ahasuerus sat on the throne of his kingdom, which was in Shushan the palace,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/esther/1#v3)</sup> In the third year of his reign, he made a feast unto all his princes and his servants; the power of Persia and Media, the nobles and princes of the provinces, being before him:

- In the days of Ahasuerus, who reigned from India even unto Ethiopia over a hundred and twenty seven provinces, the king sat on the throne of his kingdom in Shushan the palace.
- In the third year of his reign he made a feast unto all his princes and servants, the power of Persia and Media, the nobles and princes of the provinces being before him.

**[Esther 1:4-8](/bible/esther/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/esther/1#v4)</sup> When he shewed the riches of his glorious kingdom and the honour of his excellent majesty many days, even an hundred and fourscore days.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/esther/1#v5)</sup> And when these days were expired, the king made a feast unto all the people that were present in Shushan the palace, both unto great and small, seven days, in the court of the garden of the king’s palace;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/esther/1#v6)</sup> Where were white, green, and blue, hangings, fastened with cords of fine linen and purple to silver rings and pillars of marble: the beds were of gold and silver, upon a pavement of red, and blue, and white, and black, marble.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/esther/1#v7)</sup> And they gave them drink in vessels of gold, (the vessels being diverse one from another,) and royal wine in abundance, according to the state of the king.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/esther/1#v8)</sup> And the drinking was according to the law; none did compel: for so the king had appointed to all the officers of his house, that they should do according to every man’s pleasure.

- The king showed the riches of his glorious kingdom and the honour of his excellent majesty for many days, even a hundred and fourscore days, showing out for a full hundred and eighty days.
- When those days were expired he made a feast of seven days for all the people present in Shushan, with white, green, and blue hangings, beds of gold and silver, and royal wine in abundance, and the drinking was according to the law, none did compel, every man doing according to his pleasure.

**[Esther 1:9-12](/bible/esther/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/esther/1#v9)</sup> Also Vashti the queen made a feast for the women in the royal house which belonged to king Ahasuerus.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/esther/1#v10)</sup> On the seventh day, when the heart of the king was merry with wine, he commanded Mehuman, Biztha, Harbona, Bigtha, and Abagtha, Zethar, and Carcas, the seven chamberlains that served in the presence of Ahasuerus the king,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/esther/1#v11)</sup> To bring Vashti the queen before the king with the crown royal, to shew the people and the princes her beauty: for she was fair to look on.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/esther/1#v12)</sup> But the queen Vashti refused to come at the king’s commandment by his chamberlains: therefore was the king very wroth, and his anger burned in him.

- Vashti the queen also made a feast for the women in the royal house belonging to the king. On the seventh day, when the heart of the king was merry with wine, he commanded his seven chamberlains to bring Vashti before him with the crown royal to show the people and princes her beauty, for she was fair to look on.
- But the queen Vashti refused to come at the king's commandment by his chamberlains, and the king was very wroth and his anger burned in him. She disrespected the king before everyone, and a judgment was now set to come.

  - **[Jeremiah 31:22](/bible/jeremiah/31#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/jeremiah/31#v22)</sup> How long wilt thou go about, O thou backsliding daughter? for the Lord hath created a new thing in the earth, A woman shall compass a man.

    The Most High created a new thing in the earth, a woman shall compass a man; Vashti compassing the king is the very thing the wise men move to stop before it spreads.

**[Esther 1:13-15](/bible/esther/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/esther/1#v13)</sup> Then the king said to the wise men, which knew the times, (for so was the king’s manner toward all that knew law and judgment:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/esther/1#v14)</sup> And the next unto him was Carshena, Shethar, Admatha, Tarshish, Meres, Marsena, and Memucan, the seven princes of Persia and Media, which saw the king’s face, and which sat the first in the kingdom;)
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/esther/1#v15)</sup> What shall we do unto the queen Vashti according to law, because she hath not performed the commandment of the king Ahasuerus by the chamberlains?

- The king turned to the wise men who knew the times, for so was the king's manner toward all that knew law and judgment, and the seven princes of Persia and Media who saw the king's face and sat first in the kingdom were next to him.
- He asked what should be done unto the queen Vashti according to law, because she had not performed the commandment of the king by the chamberlains. A judgment had to be rendered because she disrespected him in front of everybody.

**[Esther 1:16-18](/bible/esther/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/esther/1#v16)</sup> And Memucan answered before the king and the princes, Vashti the queen hath not done wrong to the king only, but also to all the princes, and to all the people that are in all the provinces of the king Ahasuerus.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/esther/1#v17)</sup> For this deed of the queen shall come abroad unto all women, so that they shall despise their husbands in their eyes, when it shall be reported, The king Ahasuerus commanded Vashti the queen to be brought in before him, but she came not.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/esther/1#v18)</sup> Likewise shall the ladies of Persia and Media say this day unto all the king’s princes, which have heard of the deed of the queen. Thus shall there arise too much contempt and wrath.

- Memucan answered before the king and the princes that Vashti had not done wrong to the king only, but also to all the princes and all the people in all the provinces of the king.
- The deed of the queen would come abroad unto all women, so that they would despise their husbands in their eyes, saying the king commanded Vashti and she came not; likewise the ladies of Persia and Media would say the same that day, and there would arise too much contempt and wrath.

**[Esther 1:19-20](/bible/esther/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/esther/1#v19)</sup> If it please the king, let there go a royal commandment from him, and let it be written among the laws of the Persians and the Medes, that it be not altered, That Vashti come no more before king Ahasuerus; and let the king give her royal estate unto another that is better than she.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/esther/1#v20)</sup> And when the king’s decree which he shall make shall be published throughout all his empire, (for it is great,) all the wives shall give to their husbands honour, both to great and small.

- Memucan advised the king to let a royal commandment go from him, written among the laws of the Persians and Medes that it be not altered, that Vashti come no more before the king, and that he give her royal estate unto another better than she.
- When the king's decree was published throughout all his empire, for it was great, all the wives would give their husbands honour, both to great and small. Vashti loses her estate so that the order of the house would be upheld everywhere.

**[Esther 1:21-22](/bible/esther/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/esther/1#v21)</sup> And the saying pleased the king and the princes; and the king did according to the word of Memucan:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/esther/1#v22)</sup> For he sent letters into all the king’s provinces, into every province according to the writing thereof, and to every people after their language, that every man should bear rule in his own house, and that it should be published according to the language of every people.

- The saying pleased the king and the princes, and the king did according to the word of Memucan, sending letters into all his provinces, to every province and people after their language, that every man should bear rule in his own house.
- This order, that every man should bear rule in his own house, is set up in the Bible for the man, and it is the very order the Most High established that the societies of the earth keep reversing.

  - **[Titus 1:5](/bible/titus/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/titus/1#v5)</sup> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

    Things left wanting are to be set in order, which is the work of restoring the house to its right order that this chapter puts into a decree.
  - **[Isaiah 3:12](/bible/isaiah/3#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/3#v12)</sup> As for my people, children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. O my people, they which lead thee cause thee to err, and destroy the way of thy paths.

    Where the order is out of course, children become the people's oppressors and women rule over them, the reversal of the house the Most High warns against.
