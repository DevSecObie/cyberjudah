**[Exodus 40:12-15](/bible/exodus/40#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/40#v12)</sup> And thou shalt bring Aaron and his sons unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and wash them with water.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/40#v13)</sup> And thou shalt put upon Aaron the holy garments, and anoint him, and sanctify him; that he may minister unto me in the priest’s office.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/40#v14)</sup> And thou shalt bring his sons, and clothe them with coats:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/40#v15)</sup> And thou shalt anoint them, as thou didst anoint their father, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office: for their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations.

- Aaron and his sons were brought to the door of the tabernacle and washed with water; before they entered they were bathed, spotless. Then they were clothed and anointed to minister in the priest's office, for their anointing shall surely be an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations. The garments, the mitre, the sanctification, and the everlasting priesthood were opened at length in the [Exodus 26](/bible/exodus/26) through 29 session.

**[Exodus 40:34-38](/bible/exodus/40#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/40#v34)</sup> Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/40#v35)</sup> And Moses was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/40#v36)</sup> And when the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel went onward in all their journeys:
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/exodus/40#v37)</sup> But if the cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/40#v38)</sup> For the cloud of the Lord was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.

- Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle. Our forefathers were blessed to witness these things firsthand; it should be very exciting to read of the pillar and the glory that our ancestors saw in the wilderness.
- Israel did not have to rely on maps; the cloud of the Lord upon the tabernacle by day and the fire by night were the navigation system. When the cloud was taken up they journeyed, and if it was not taken up they journeyed not until the day it was taken up.
- The cloud led them in the day and the pillar of fire gave them light in the way by night; the Most High in His manifold mercies forsook them not in the wilderness. They had no need to create their own lamps; the Lord gave them light freely.
- The cloud and the fire speak of spirits the Most High created, His angels: He maketh the clouds His chariot, and He maketh His angels spirits, His ministers a flaming fire. Fire is a spirit, created for vengeance, poured out in the time of destruction to appease the wrath of Him that made them.

    In thy manifold mercies thou forsookest them not in the wilderness; the pillar of cloud and the pillar of fire departed not from them.

    He maketh the clouds His chariot, and His angels spirits, His ministers a flaming fire; the cloud and fire were His created spirits.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 39:28-29](/bible/sirach/39#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/39#v28)</sup> There be spirits that are created for vengeance, which in their fury lay on sore strokes; in the time of destruction they pour out their force, and appease the wrath of him that made them.
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/39#v29)</sup> Fire, and hail, and famine, and death, all these were created for vengeance;

    Fire is one of the spirits created for vengeance; fire is a spirit.
  - **[Psalms 34:7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)</sup> The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

    The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear Him, and delivereth them; Israelites that fear the Most High are guarded by His angels.

- The Most High sent an angel before Israel to keep them in the way and to bring them into the place He had prepared, warning them to obey his voice and provoke him not, for he will not pardon your transgressions, for my name is in him. That shows why the first generation did not make it to the promised land: they provoked the angel, transgressed, complained, worshipped idols, and wanted to go back into Egypt.
- If Israel obeyed the angel's voice, the Lord would be an enemy unto their enemies and cut off the nations of the land before them. The scripture makes a distinction: the enemy whose ox or ass you must bring back and help is your brother that hates you, as the parallel law shows by using the word brother; the enemies of the nations are those the Most High said He would cut off. So love thine enemies does not mean the nation Israel was sold to; it speaks of your brother that you may be at odds with.
