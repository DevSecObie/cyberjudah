## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 30](/bible/exodus/30) through 33: the altar of incense, the ransom of souls, the laver of brass, the holy anointing oil and perfume, Bezaleel and Aholiab filled with wisdom to build, the sabbath as a sign between the Most High and Israel, the golden calf at Horeb, and Moses pleading for the presence of the Lord and seeing his glory pass by. This was a reading session with teaching along the way; the questions answered at the end are merged under the verses they belong to.

**[Exodus 30:1-5](/bible/exodus/30#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/30#v1)</sup> And thou shalt make an altar to burn incense upon: of shittim wood shalt thou make it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/30#v2)</sup> A cubit shall be the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof; foursquare shall it be: and two cubits shall be the height thereof: the horns thereof shall be of the same.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/30#v3)</sup> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, the top thereof, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns thereof; and thou shalt make unto it a crown of gold round about.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/30#v4)</sup> And two golden rings shalt thou make to it under the crown of it, by the two corners thereof, upon the two sides of it shalt thou make it; and they shall be for places for the staves to bear it withal.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/30#v5)</sup> And thou shalt make the staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with gold.

- The altar to burn incense upon was made of shittim wood, a cubit in length and a cubit in breadth, foursquare, and two cubits high, with horns of the same. A cubit has been read to be about seventeen inches, so this stood about waist high.
- It was overlaid with pure gold, the top, the sides round about, and the horns, with a crown of gold round about, two golden rings under the crown, and staves of shittim wood overlaid with gold. The staves through the rings were how the priests bore it as Israel moved through the wilderness.

**[Exodus 30:6-9](/bible/exodus/30#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/30#v6)</sup> And thou shalt put it before the vail that is by the ark of the testimony, before the mercy seat that is over the testimony, where I will meet with thee.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/30#v7)</sup> And Aaron shall burn thereon sweet incense every morning: when he dresseth the lamps, he shall burn incense upon it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/30#v8)</sup> And when Aaron lighteth the lamps at even, he shall burn incense upon it, a perpetual incense before the Lord throughout your generations.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/30#v9)</sup> Ye shall offer no strange incense thereon, nor burnt sacrifice, nor meat offering; neither shall ye pour drink offering thereon.

- The altar was put before the vail that is by the ark of the testimony, before the mercy seat where the Most High would meet with Moses.
- Aaron burned sweet incense on it every morning when he dressed the lamps, and again at even when he lighted the lamps, a perpetual incense before the Lord throughout the generations; it burned continually, morning and evening.
- No strange incense went on it, and no burnt sacrifice, meat offering, or drink offering; this altar was for one specific incense only. The service had to be exactly as prescribed, and deviation carried death, which is a reason to thank the Lord for Christ.

**[Exodus 30:10](/bible/exodus/30#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/30#v10)</sup> And Aaron shall make an atonement upon the horns of it once in a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonements: once in the year shall he make atonement upon it throughout your generations: it is most holy unto the Lord.

- Aaron made an atonement upon the horns of it once in a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonements; it is most holy unto the Lord. This altar served the day of atonement.
- On the tenth day of the seventh month Israel afflicted their souls and did no work at all, and the priest made an atonement for the sanctuary, the tabernacle, the altar, the priests, and all the people of the congregation, to cleanse them from all their sins before the Lord; an everlasting statute, once a year.
- The priests went always into the first tabernacle accomplishing the service of God, but into the second went the high priest alone once every year, not without blood. Christ offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, so the day of atonement is still kept by afflicting the soul and doing no work, but without the animal sacrifice, because Christ took the place of the animal.

**[Exodus 30:11-16](/bible/exodus/30#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/30#v11)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>

>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/30#v13)</sup> This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are numbered, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary: (a shekel is twenty gerahs:) an half shekel shall be the offering of the Lord.

>

- Numbering the children of Israel was not done lightly; when they were counted, every man gave a ransom for his soul unto the Lord, that there be no plague among them when they were numbered.
- The ransom was half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary, from every one that passed among them that were numbered, from twenty years old and above; the rich gave no more and the poor gave no less, to make an atonement for their souls.
- The atonement money was appointed for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation. The gold, the wood, the curtains, and the fabrics all had to be bought and kept up, and the tabernacle moved with the nation through the wilderness; that is the same reason offerings are gathered in the congregation today, because the house of the Most High cannot be built or kept without it.

Our forefathers carried out that tedious, exact work, yet we complain about the smallest things set in place for order, the uniform, the new moon, and not cooking on the sabbath; the spirit has to get right.

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/31#v12)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/31#v14)</sup> Ye shall keep the sabbath therefore; for it is holy unto you: every one that defileth it shall surely be put to death: for whosoever doeth any work therein, that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/31#v15)</sup> Six days may work be done; but in the seventh is the sabbath of rest, holy to the Lord: whosoever doeth any work in the sabbath day, he shall surely be put to death.

- Verily my sabbaths ye shall keep, for it is a sign between me and you throughout your generations, that ye may know that I am the LORD that doth sanctify you. Keeping the weekly sabbath and the feast days shows that Israel are his children and he is their God; the nations do not have this sabbath, and adopted Sunday worship is not the true sabbath given to the children of Israel.
- Every one that defileth the sabbath shall surely be put to death. The stoning was done away in Christ, but the judgment for breaking the sabbath still stands, and people still die on the sabbath day, Friday sundown to Saturday sundown; the Most High is justified, because it is his sign between him and his people.
- Six days may work be done, but in the seventh is the sabbath of rest, holy to the LORD. It is more than a day of rest and congregating; it is the sign of who Israel's God is, which is why saying the sabbath is done away takes away the very sign between Israel and God.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:7-9](/bible/sirach/33#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/33#v7)</sup> Why doth one day excel another, when as all the light of every day in the year is of the sun?

    Every day has the same sun, but by the knowledge of the Lord some days were made high days and hallowed; the sabbath is distinguished by God, not by man.

- From the questions: eating is allowed on the sabbath, but not cooking. What is baked and seethed is prepared the day before and laid up, so the sabbath meal is leftovers or simple food; no microwaving, no coffee pots, no kindling the cooking fire.
- There is no buying or selling on the sabbath or on the holy day, so gas and food are bought before sundown; no fishing either, for what could be called work opens the door to every man excusing his trade on the sabbath.
- The Lord gives no man license to sin, but Christ is Lord of the sabbath and brought grace for necessities out of our control, a blown tire or an empty tank on the way to keep the sabbath, or medicine when the body is in danger; it is lawful to do well on the sabbath days.

- The LORD spake unto Moses face to face, as a man speaketh unto his friend. That is not Moses looking eye to eye at the Lord; it means there was no mediator between Moses and the Lord, for Moses himself was the mediator between the Lord and the people, and the Lord spoke to him directly.
- His servant Joshua, the son of Nun, a young man, departed not out of the tabernacle. Joshua was a true disciple, a learner, eager to stay by Moses' side and learn; the Lord knew his spirit.

**[Exodus 33:12-17](/bible/exodus/33#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/33#v12)</sup> And Moses said unto the Lord, See, thou sayest unto me, Bring up this people: and thou hast not let me know whom thou wilt send with me. Yet thou hast said, I know thee by name, and thou hast also found grace in my sight.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/33#v13)</sup> Now therefore, I pray thee, if I have found grace in thy sight, shew me now thy way, that I may know thee, that I may find grace in thy sight: and consider that this nation is thy people.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/33#v14)</sup> And he said, My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/33#v15)</sup> And he said unto him, If thy presence go not with me, carry us not up hence.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/33#v16)</sup> For wherein shall it be known here that I and thy people have found grace in thy sight? is it not in that thou goest with us? so shall we be separated, I and thy people, from all the people that are upon the face of the earth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/33#v17)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, I will do this thing also that thou hast spoken: for thou hast found grace in my sight, and I know thee by name.

- Moses pled with the Lord: thou hast said, I know thee by name, and thou hast also found grace in my sight; now therefore shew me now thy way, that I may know thee, that I may find grace in thy sight; and consider that this nation is thy people.
- The Lord answered, My presence shall go with thee, and I will give thee rest. And Moses said, If thy presence go not with me, carry us not up hence; for wherein shall it be known here that I and thy people have found grace in thy sight? is it not in that thou goest with us? so shall we be separated, I and thy people, from all the people that are upon the face of the earth.
- The presence of the Most High in the midst is how Israel is known to be separated from all the nations, and it ties back to the sabbath sign: when Israel keeps the sabbath and gathers in his name, the Lord and Christ are in the midst.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 18:20](/bible/matthew/18#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/18#v20)</sup> For where two or three are gathered together in my name, there am I in the midst of them.

    Where two or three are gathered together in his name, there he is in the midst of them; the presence in the midst is the separation from the nations.

**[Exodus 33:18-23](/bible/exodus/33#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/33#v18)</sup> And he said, I beseech thee, shew me thy glory.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/33#v19)</sup> And he said, I will make all my goodness pass before thee, and I will proclaim the name of the Lord before thee; and will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and will shew mercy on whom I will shew mercy.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/33#v20)</sup> And he said, Thou canst not see my face: for there shall no man see me, and live.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/33#v21)</sup> And the Lord said, Behold, there is a place by me, and thou shalt stand upon a rock:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/33#v22)</sup> And it shall come to pass, while my glory passeth by, that I will put thee in a clift of the rock, and will cover thee with my hand while I pass by:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/33#v23)</sup> And I will take away mine hand, and thou shalt see my back parts: but my face shall not be seen.

- Moses said, I beseech thee, shew me thy glory. And he said, I will make all my goodness pass before thee, and I will proclaim the name of the LORD before thee, and will be gracious to whom I will be gracious, and will shew mercy on whom I will shew mercy, then and in the future as well.
- Thou canst not see my face: for there shall no man see me, and live. In verse 11 Christ spoke to Moses on the Lord's behalf, with Moses as the mediator to the people; here it is the Father of heaven and earth himself, and no man can see his face and live.
- Behold, there is a place by me, and thou shalt stand upon a rock: I will put thee in a clift of the rock, and will cover thee with my hand while I pass by; and I will take away mine hand, and thou shalt see my back parts, but my face shall not be seen. Moses beheld the similitude of the LORD, the back parts of his glory, never his face.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 12:8](/bible/numbers/12#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/12#v8)</sup> With him will I speak mouth to mouth, even apparently, and not in dark speeches; and the similitude of the Lord shall he behold: wherefore then were ye not afraid to speak against my servant Moses?

    With Moses the Lord spake mouth to mouth, even apparently, and not in dark speeches, and the similitude of the LORD shall he behold; Moses saw the back parts, not the face.
