**[Exodus 9:1-7](/bible/exodus/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/9#v1)</sup> Then the Lord said unto Moses, Go in unto Pharaoh, and tell him, Thus saith the Lord God of the Hebrews, Let my people go, that they may serve me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/9#v2)</sup> For if thou refuse to let them go, and wilt hold them still,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/9#v3)</sup> Behold, the hand of the Lord is upon thy cattle which is in the field, upon the horses, upon the asses, upon the camels, upon the oxen, and upon the sheep: there shall be a very grievous murrain.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/9#v4)</sup> And the Lord shall sever between the cattle of Israel and the cattle of Egypt: and there shall nothing die of all that is the children’s of Israel.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/9#v5)</sup> And the Lord appointed a set time, saying, To morrow the Lord shall do this thing in the land.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/9#v6)</sup> And the Lord did that thing on the morrow, and all the cattle of Egypt died: but of the cattle of the children of Israel died not one.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/9#v7)</sup> And Pharaoh sent, and, behold, there was not one of the cattle of the Israelites dead. And the heart of Pharaoh was hardened, and he did not let the people go.

- A grievous murrain fell upon the cattle of Egypt, upon the horses, asses, camels, oxen, and sheep; and with this fifth plague the Most High was mocking Hathor, the Egyptian idol goddess of cattle.
- The Lord severed between the cattle of Israel and the cattle of Egypt: all the cattle of Egypt died, but of the cattle of the children of Israel died not one. Pharaoh sent, and behold, there was not one of the cattle of the Israelites dead; and his heart was hardened.
- Picture what was taking place in the land: flies all over Egypt, but where Israel dwelt there were no flies; the water was blood, but where Israel dwelt the water was good; the cattle of Egypt died, but Israel's cattle lived. The plagues touched Pharaoh, his servants, and the Egyptians only; they did not touch the Israelites at all, because this was the Lord redeeming the Israelites.
- The Egyptians could not pray to their idols to help them, because the Lord was mocking them, showing them that Hathor does not exist, Sobek does not exist, Heqet does not exist, and Beelzebub does not exist.

**[Exodus 9:8-12](/bible/exodus/9#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/9#v8)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses and unto Aaron, Take to you handfuls of ashes of the furnace, and let Moses sprinkle it toward the heaven in the sight of Pharaoh.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/9#v9)</sup> And it shall become small dust in all the land of Egypt, and shall be a boil breaking forth with blains upon man, and upon beast, throughout all the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/9#v10)</sup> And they took ashes of the furnace, and stood before Pharaoh; and Moses sprinkled it up toward heaven; and it became a boil breaking forth with blains upon man, and upon beast.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/9#v11)</sup> And the magicians could not stand before Moses because of the boils; for the boil was upon the magicians, and upon all the Egyptians.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/9#v12)</sup> And the Lord hardened the heart of Pharaoh, and he hearkened not unto them; as the Lord had spoken unto Moses.

- Moses sprinkled ashes of the furnace toward heaven, and it became a boil breaking forth with blains upon man and upon beast throughout all the land of Egypt.
- The magicians could not stand before Moses because of the boils, for the boils were upon the magicians themselves and upon all the Egyptians. With this sixth plague the Most High was mocking Sekhmet, the Egyptian idol goddess of health and beauty.

**[Exodus 9:13-17](/bible/exodus/9#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/9#v13)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Rise up early in the morning, and stand before Pharaoh, and say unto him, Thus saith the Lord God of the Hebrews, Let my people go, that they may serve me.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/9#v14)</sup> For I will at this time send all my plagues upon thine heart, and upon thy servants, and upon thy people; that thou mayest know that there is none like me in all the earth.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/9#v15)</sup> For now I will stretch out my hand, that I may smite thee and thy people with pestilence; and thou shalt be cut off from the earth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/9#v16)</sup> And in very deed for this cause have I raised thee up, for to shew in thee my power; and that my name may be declared throughout all the earth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/9#v17)</sup> As yet exaltest thou thyself against my people, that thou wilt not let them go?

- Some mock and ask, What type of God says let my people go and then keeps hardening Pharaoh's heart so he will not let them go? That is exactly the God we serve, and the Lord tells you Himself why He did it: for this cause have I raised thee up, for to shew in thee my power, and that my name may be declared throughout all the earth.
- The same thing is happening now, right here in this captivity. The powers of Babylon know what is being taught, yet the Lord hardens their hearts so that they keep passing laws and pressing against His people, so that the Most High can show His power again, just as He did in Egypt.

**[Exodus 9:18-21](/bible/exodus/9#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/9#v18)</sup> Behold, to morrow about this time I will cause it to rain a very grievous hail, such as hath not been in Egypt since the foundation thereof even until now.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/9#v19)</sup> Send therefore now, and gather thy cattle, and all that thou hast in the field; for upon every man and beast which shall be found in the field, and shall not be brought home, the hail shall come down upon them, and they shall die.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/9#v20)</sup> He that feared the word of the Lord among the servants of Pharaoh made his servants and his cattle flee into the houses:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/9#v21)</sup> And he that regarded not the word of the Lord left his servants and his cattle in the field.

- Before the hail the Lord gave warning: gather thy cattle and all that thou hast in the field, for upon every man and beast found in the field the hail shall come down, and they shall die.
- He that feared the word of the Lord among the servants of Pharaoh made his servants and his cattle flee into the houses; they began to recognize the God of heaven and earth, the God of the Israelites.
- He that regarded not the word of the Lord left his servants and his cattle in the field. Many in Egypt held certain idols in more favor than others, and these trusted that Nut, the idol goddess of the sky, would keep them safe.

**[Exodus 9:22-26](/bible/exodus/9#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/9#v22)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Stretch forth thine hand toward heaven, that there may be hail in all the land of Egypt, upon man, and upon beast, and upon every herb of the field, throughout the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/9#v23)</sup> And Moses stretched forth his rod toward heaven: and the Lord sent thunder and hail, and the fire ran along upon the ground; and the Lord rained hail upon the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/9#v24)</sup> So there was hail, and fire mingled with the hail, very grievous, such as there was none like it in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/9#v25)</sup> And the hail smote throughout all the land of Egypt all that was in the field, both man and beast; and the hail smote every herb of the field, and brake every tree of the field.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/9#v26)</sup> Only in the land of Goshen, where the children of Israel were, was there no hail.

- This was no regular hail. There was hail, and fire mingled with the hail, very grievous, such as there was none like it in all the land of Egypt since it became a nation; the fire ran along upon the ground.
- With this seventh plague the Most High was mocking Nut and Geb, the Egyptian idol gods of the sky and the earth, striking from the sky and burning along the ground they were supposed to rule.
- Only in the land of Goshen, where the children of Israel were, was there no hail. The Lord does not play; our God is a terrible God.

**[Exodus 9:27-30](/bible/exodus/9#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/9#v27)</sup> And Pharaoh sent, and called for Moses and Aaron, and said unto them, I have sinned this time: the Lord is righteous, and I and my people are wicked.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/9#v28)</sup> Intreat the Lord (for it is enough) that there be no more mighty thunderings and hail; and I will let you go, and ye shall stay no longer.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/9#v29)</sup> And Moses said unto him, As soon as I am gone out of the city, I will spread abroad my hands unto the Lord; and the thunder shall cease, neither shall there be any more hail; that thou mayest know how that the earth is the Lord’s.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/9#v30)</sup> But as for thee and thy servants, I know that ye will not yet fear the Lord God.

- Pharaoh said, I have sinned this time: the Lord is righteous, and I and my people are wicked. This time? So all the other times, none of that was sin? Only now, under the thunder and hail, does he see it.
- Moses answered that the thunder and hail would cease, that thou mayest know how that the earth is the Lord's; but as for thee and thy servants, I know that ye will not yet fear the Lord God.

**[Exodus 9:31-32](/bible/exodus/9#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/9#v31)</sup> And the flax and the barley was smitten: for the barley was in the ear, and the flax was bolled.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/9#v32)</sup> But the wheat and the rie were not smitten: for they were not grown up.

- The flax and the barley were smitten, for the barley was in the ear and the flax was bolled; but the wheat and the rie were not smitten, for they were not grown up.

**[Exodus 9:34-35](/bible/exodus/9#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/9#v34)</sup> And when Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunders were ceased, he sinned yet more, and hardened his heart, he and his servants.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/9#v35)</sup> And the heart of Pharaoh was hardened, neither would he let the children of Israel go; as the Lord had spoken by Moses.

- When Pharaoh saw that the rain and the hail and the thunders were ceased, he sinned yet more and hardened his heart, he and his servants. He still had the wheat and the rie in the ground, because they were not grown up yet, and that is part of why he went right back to hardening himself.
