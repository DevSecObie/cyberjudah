## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 10](/bible/exodus/10) through 13: the plagues of locusts and darkness, the death of the firstborn announced and executed, the institution of the Lord's Passover and the feast of unleavened bread, Israel thrust out of Egypt after 430 years, the sanctifying of the firstborn, and the pillar of cloud and fire leading the people. The session opens with a precursor from [Proverbs 21](/bible/proverbs/21) and Romans 9: the things that were written aforetime were written for our learning, and the Most High is in absolute control of the kings of the earth.

**[Exodus 10:1](/bible/exodus/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/10#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Go in unto Pharaoh: for I have hardened his heart, and the heart of his servants, that I might shew these my signs before him:

- The Most High hardened the heart of Pharaoh and of his servants so that he might show his signs and exemplify his power in the earth. After he brought down Egypt and delivered the people, every nation feared Israel and the God of Israel.
- The king's heart is in the hand of the Lord; he turns it whithersoever he will. He is in control of the mind of the kings of the world, so there is no need to worry over who becomes president or who gets put in office; either way the Most High is in control.
- The scripture said to Pharaoh, Even for this same purpose have I raised thee up, that I might show my power in thee, and that my name might be declared throughout all the earth. He has mercy on whom he will have mercy, and whom he will he hardeneth.
- The potter has power over the clay, of the same lump to make one vessel unto honour and another unto dishonour. Some nations are honourable, which are the Israelites, and some are dishonourable; Jacob have I loved, but Esau have I hated. The vessels of wrath were fitted to destruction, endured with much longsuffering so the Most High could show how powerful he is; that is why Esau had to be set on such a high level, so that when he brings these people down all will know God is truly all powerful.
- The same holds in spiritual Egypt today: the Most High will harden the hearts of the rulers of this kingdom so they will not let Israel go and will continue to oppress, because in the affliction the people will seek him early. It does not matter who is voted into office; whoever sits in the seat, the Most High turns him about like the rivers of water.
- The things that were written aforetime were written for our learning, and the thing that hath been is that which shall be. That is why so many similarities appear between the captivities: this captivity carries the marks of Egypt, of Babylon, and of the Greeks, because these things must come to pass.

- The Most High already knew what was going down: yet will I bring one plague more upon Pharaoh and upon Egypt. This last plague would end it, and when Pharaoh let the people go he would thrust them out hence altogether, with the quickness.

**[Exodus 11:2-3](/bible/exodus/11#v2)**

Israel in spiritual Egypt must deal the same way: have the respect of the people, and if people despise you, let it be because they hate God and the word you teach, not because you are in wickedness, shady dealings, deceit, or disrespect.

# Exodus 12: The Lord's Passover

Read the chapter → [Exodus 12](/bible/exodus/12)

**[Exodus 12:1-2](/bible/exodus/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/12#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and Aaron in the land of Egypt, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/12#v2)</sup> This month shall be unto you the beginning of months: it shall be the first month of the year to you.

- This month shall be unto you the beginning of months, the first month of the year. The month is Abib, the month in which Israel came out of Egypt.
- Abib in the Bible dictionary is the pre-exilic name for the first month of the year; after the exile the name was changed to Nisan, and it falls between March and April. So the new year begins in the springtime, around March and April, not on January the first in the dead of winter; that is another lie taught in Babylon.

- The lamb without blemish and without spot points to Christ, who was sinless, verily foreordained before the foundation of the world but manifest in these last times for the nation of Israel.

> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/13#v15)</sup> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh would hardly let us go, that the Lord slew all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, both the firstborn of man, and the firstborn of beast: therefore I sacrifice to the Lord all that openeth the matrix, being males; but all the firstborn of my children I redeem.

- When thy son asketh thee in time to come, saying, What is this? thou shalt say, By strength of hand the Lord brought us out from Egypt, from the house of bondage: when Pharaoh would hardly let us go, the Lord slew all the firstborn in the land of Egypt, therefore the firstborn are sacrificed or redeemed.
- The children must be taught the righteousness of why the feast is kept. Ask the children of this land why they keep Christmas and they answer that Santa Claus is coming down the chimney; Israel's children are to be taught the works of the Most High instead.
- It is for a token upon thine hand and for frontlets between thine eyes, for by strength of hand the Lord brought us forth out of Egypt.

**[Exodus 13:17-18](/bible/exodus/13#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/13#v17)</sup> And it came to pass, when Pharaoh had let the people go, that God led them not through the way of the land of the Philistines, although that was near; for God said, Lest peradventure the people repent when they see war, and they return to Egypt:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/13#v18)</sup> But God led the people about, through the way of the wilderness of the Red sea: and the children of Israel went up harnessed out of the land of Egypt.

- When Pharaoh let the people go, God led them not through the way of the land of the Philistines, although that was near, lest peradventure the people repent when they see war and return to Egypt. Israel would have been fearful at the sight of the Philistine army and turned back.
- The people felt a false security in Egypt: in the wilderness they murmured for the fish, the leeks, the onions, and the garlick of Egypt and loathed the light bread; and when the Assyrians came, Israel again ran down to Egypt for help. The same spirit holds the people in America today: a little job, government assistance, a car, food from the store, and they never want this kingdom to come down. But the Most High has something much greater in store: the world, the earth, and many mansions.
- So God led the people about, through the way of the wilderness of the Red Sea, and the children of Israel went up harnessed out of the land of Egypt.
