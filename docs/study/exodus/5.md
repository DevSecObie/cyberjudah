**[Exodus 5:1-2](/bible/exodus/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/5#v1)</sup> And afterward Moses and Aaron went in, and told Pharaoh, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, Let my people go, that they may hold a feast unto me in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/5#v2)</sup> And Pharaoh said, Who is the Lord, that I should obey his voice to let Israel go? I know not the Lord, neither will I let Israel go.

- Moses and Aaron went in and told Pharaoh, Thus saith the LORD God of Israel, Let my people go, that they may hold a feast unto me in the wilderness. That feast was the Passover.
- Pharaoh said, Who is the LORD, that I should obey his voice to let Israel go? I know not the LORD, neither will I let Israel go. The Lord had already said He would harden Pharaoh's heart.

**[Exodus 5:3](/bible/exodus/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/5#v3)</sup> And they said, The God of the Hebrews hath met with us: let us go, we pray thee, three days’ journey into the desert, and sacrifice unto the Lord our God; lest he fall upon us with pestilence, or with the sword.

- They said, The God of the Hebrews hath met with us: let us go, we pray thee, three days' journey into the desert, and sacrifice unto the LORD our God; lest he fall upon us with pestilence, or with the sword.

**[Exodus 5:4-5](/bible/exodus/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/5#v4)</sup> And the king of Egypt said unto them, Wherefore do ye, Moses and Aaron, let the people from their works? get you unto your burdens.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/5#v5)</sup> And Pharaoh said, Behold, the people of the land now are many, and ye make them rest from their burdens.

- The king of Egypt said, Wherefore do ye, Moses and Aaron, let the people from their works? get you unto your burdens. That is the standing complaint of the oppressor: the prophets are pulling the people from their labor and getting their hopes up that their God will deliver them.

**[Exodus 5:6-9](/bible/exodus/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/5#v6)</sup> And Pharaoh commanded the same day the taskmasters of the people, and their officers, saying,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/5#v7)</sup> Ye shall no more give the people straw to make brick, as heretofore: let them go and gather straw for themselves.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/5#v8)</sup> And the tale of the bricks, which they did make heretofore, ye shall lay upon them; ye shall not diminish ought thereof: for they be idle; therefore they cry, saying, Let us go and sacrifice to our God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/5#v9)</sup> Let there more work be laid upon the men, that they may labour therein; and let them not regard vain words.

- Pharaoh commanded the taskmasters the same day: ye shall no more give the people straw to make brick; let them go and gather straw for themselves; and the tale of the bricks ye shall lay upon them, ye shall not diminish ought thereof. For they be idle; therefore they cry, saying, Let us go and sacrifice to our God.
- Let there more work be laid upon the men, that they may labour therein; and let them not regard vain words. The same tactic runs in this captivity: burdens are increased and extra wages dangled for working the Sabbath and the feast days, to wear the people down so they will not regard the words of the Most High.
- The work of the Lord is the true calling over any occupation held in Babylon: consider the Apostle and High Priest of our profession, Christ Jesus; fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.

**[Exodus 5:10-14](/bible/exodus/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/5#v10)</sup> And the taskmasters of the people went out, and their officers, and they spake to the people, saying, Thus saith Pharaoh, I will not give you straw.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/5#v11)</sup> Go ye, get you straw where ye can find it: yet not ought of your work shall be diminished.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/5#v12)</sup> So the people were scattered abroad throughout all the land of Egypt to gather stubble instead of straw.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/5#v13)</sup> And the taskmasters hasted them, saying, Fulfil your works, your daily tasks, as when there was straw.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/5#v14)</sup> And the officers of the children of Israel, which Pharaoh’s taskmasters had set over them, were beaten, and demanded, Wherefore have ye not fulfilled your task in making brick both yesterday and to day, as heretofore?

- The people were scattered abroad throughout all the land of Egypt to gather stubble instead of straw, and the taskmasters hasted them, saying, Fulfil your works, your daily tasks, as when there was straw. Fewer resources, the same production and the same deadline: the pattern of oppression has not changed in this captivity.
- The officers of the children of Israel, which Pharaoh's taskmasters had set over them, were beaten. Israelites were set as officers over their own people, so that the anger of the oppressed lands on their own brother, the supervisor they can see, instead of on the power above him; it has always been an agenda, and it is the same one today.
- This place is the same house of bondage in the spirit: the great city is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt, and the afflictions Egypt laid on Israel are the same tactics laid on Israel here.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 11:8](/bible/revelation/11#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/11#v8)</sup> And their dead bodies shall lie in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.

    The great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.

**[Exodus 5:15-19](/bible/exodus/5#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/5#v15)</sup> Then the officers of the children of Israel came and cried unto Pharaoh, saying, Wherefore dealest thou thus with thy servants?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/5#v16)</sup> There is no straw given unto thy servants, and they say to us, Make brick: and, behold, thy servants are beaten; but the fault is in thine own people.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/5#v17)</sup> But he said, Ye are idle, ye are idle: therefore ye say, Let us go and do sacrifice to the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/5#v18)</sup> Go therefore now, and work; for there shall no straw be given you, yet shall ye deliver the tale of bricks.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/5#v19)</sup> And the officers of the children of Israel did see that they were in evil case, after it was said, Ye shall not minish ought from your bricks of your daily task.

- The officers of the children of Israel came and cried unto Pharaoh, saying, Wherefore dealest thou thus with thy servants? There is no straw given unto thy servants, and they say to us, Make brick: and, behold, thy servants are beaten; but the fault is in thine own people.
- Pharaoh answered, Ye are idle, ye are idle: therefore ye say, Let us go and do sacrifice to the LORD. The charge of idleness is the standing answer to any who would go serve God, whether it is the three days' journey or keeping the Sabbath and the new moons now.
- The officers did see that they were in evil case. That is when they realized no appeal to Pharaoh would deliver them; deliverance was not coming from Egypt.

  Precepts:

    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/john/11#v48)</sup> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation.

    The chief priests and Pharisees said, If we let him thus alone, the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation; men set over the people fear losing their place more than they love the deliverer.

**[Exodus 5:20-21](/bible/exodus/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/5#v20)</sup> And they met Moses and Aaron, who stood in the way, as they came forth from Pharaoh:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/5#v21)</sup> And they said unto them, The Lord look upon you, and judge; because ye have made our savour to be abhorred in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants, to put a sword in their hand to slay us.

- They met Moses and Aaron and said, The LORD look upon you, and judge; because ye have made our savour to be abhorred in the eyes of Pharaoh, and in the eyes of his servants, to put a sword in their hand to slay us.
- The people blamed the deliverers for the increase of affliction. It is the same spirit that meets the truth today, preferring quiet assimilation to deliverance; the enemy turns up the pressure precisely because the truth is being spoken.

**[Exodus 5:22-23](/bible/exodus/5#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/5#v22)</sup> And Moses returned unto the Lord, and said, Lord, wherefore hast thou so evil entreated this people? why is it that thou hast sent me?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/5#v23)</sup> For since I came to Pharaoh to speak in thy name, he hath done evil to this people; neither hast thou delivered thy people at all.

- Moses returned unto the LORD, and said, Lord, wherefore hast thou so evil entreated this people? why is it that thou hast sent me? For since I came to Pharaoh to speak in thy name, he hath done evil to this people; neither hast thou delivered thy people at all.
- Affliction can increase when the truth first goes forth, and the deliverer himself can be shaken by it. Walk in wisdom under that pressure and hold the tongue where wisdom requires, without letting go of the work.
- Have the faith to see ahead. The work of the Lord grows from a handful over many years because He sees the faith of those who endure, though at the time they cannot yet see what it will become.
