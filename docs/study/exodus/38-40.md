## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 38](/bible/exodus/38) through 40, with [Leviticus 1](/bible/leviticus/1) taken in the same session. These are the chapters men call the boring chapters, but skip past any page and you can miss a jewel; reading them teaches attention to detail. Here Moses follows through on the instructions of the earlier chapters: the tabernacle, the court, and the altar are built, the holy garments are made, and the tabernacle is reared up and filled with the glory of the Lord.

**[Exodus 38:1-8](/bible/exodus/38#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/38#v1)</sup> And he made the altar of burnt offering of shittim wood: five cubits was the length thereof, and five cubits the breadth thereof; it was foursquare; and three cubits the height thereof.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/38#v2)</sup> And he made the horns thereof on the four corners of it; the horns thereof were of the same: and he overlaid it with brass.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/38#v3)</sup> And he made all the vessels of the altar, the pots, and the shovels, and the basons, and the fleshhooks, and the firepans: all the vessels thereof made he of brass.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/38#v4)</sup> And he made for the altar a brasen grate of network under the compass thereof beneath unto the midst of it.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/38#v5)</sup> And he cast four rings for the four ends of the grate of brass, to be places for the staves.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/38#v6)</sup> And he made the staves of shittim wood, and overlaid them with brass.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/38#v7)</sup> And he put the staves into the rings on the sides of the altar, to bear it withal; he made the altar hollow with boards.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/38#v8)</sup> And he made the laver of brass, and the foot of it of brass, of the lookingglasses of the women assembling, which assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

- This chapter is the application of the instructions already given for the tabernacle: the altar of burnt offering of shittim wood overlaid with brass, its vessels, and its staves were made exactly as commanded.
- The laver of brass was made of the looking glasses of the women assembling at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation; even the women's own possessions went into the work.

**[Exodus 38:21](/bible/exodus/38#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/38#v21)</sup> This is the sum of the tabernacle, even of the tabernacle of testimony, as it was counted, according to the commandment of Moses, for the service of the Levites, by the hand of Ithamar, son to Aaron the priest.

- This is the sum of the tabernacle: a collection was taken in order to fund the building of the tabernacle and the service of building it, counted according to the commandment of Moses by the hand of Ithamar son to Aaron the priest.

**[Exodus 38:24-26](/bible/exodus/38#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/38#v24)</sup> All the gold that was occupied for the work in all the work of the holy place, even the gold of the offering, was twenty and nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/38#v25)</sup> And the silver of them that were numbered of the congregation was an hundred talents, and a thousand seven hundred and threescore and fifteen shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/38#v26)</sup> A bekah for every man, that is, half a shekel, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for every one that went to be numbered, from twenty years old and upward, for six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty men.

- All the gold, silver, and brass occupied for the work was counted. The silver came a bekah for every man, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary, from every one numbered from twenty years old and upward, six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty men. Thousands of men contributed to the cause of the sanctuary being built.
- Israel in the wilderness was delivered, not in captivity; today we are waking up in our captivity, and the buildings we get for sanctuaries require work, wood, service, and labor. Yet many do not have the spirit to give. If you have trouble coming up out of your pocket to help the cause of the Lord, examine yourself whether ye be in the faith, whether you really believe and really study.
- After Babylon invaded Jerusalem our fathers were captives, yet they still made a collection for the house of the Lord, not a required amount but according to every man's power. Likewise today the congregations do not push a ten percent; you give according to your power, and the Lord knows how much power you have to give. Sadly some give more than those who have more power to give than they.
- He which soweth sparingly shall reap sparingly, and he which soweth bountifully shall reap bountifully; that does not mean the Most High blesses you with a three story house, for you may be storing up treasures in heaven. Give as you purpose in your heart, not grudgingly or of necessity, for God loveth a cheerful giver; giving with an attitude is worse than keeping the money.

    In the Babylonian captivity our fathers still made a collection, according to every man's power, not a set amount.
  - **[2 Corinthians 9:6-7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v6)</sup> But this I say, He which soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v7)</sup> Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver.

    Sow bountifully and reap bountifully; give as you purpose in your heart, not grudgingly or of necessity, for God loveth a cheerful giver.
  - **[Matthew 6:20](/bible/matthew/6#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/6#v20)</sup> But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal:

    The bountiful reaping may be treasures laid up in heaven rather than riches in this life.

**[Exodus 38:29-31](/bible/exodus/38#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/38#v29)</sup> And the brass of the offering was seventy talents, and two thousand and four hundred shekels.
>

>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/38#v31)</sup> And the sockets of the court round about, and the sockets of the court gate, and all the pins of the tabernacle, and all the pins of the court round about.

Every material was accounted for; not everybody had the spirit to contribute physically, but everybody put their brick in and gave something for the cause.

# Exodus 39: The Holy Garments and the Names of the Children of Israel

Read the chapter → [Exodus 39](/bible/exodus/39)

**[Exodus 39:1](/bible/exodus/39#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/39#v1)</sup> And of the blue, and purple, and scarlet, they made cloths of service, to do service in the holy place, and made the holy garments for Aaron; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Chapter 39 is the application of the instructions of chapter 28: the holy garments for Aaron the priest, the clothes of service to do service in the holy place, made of the blue, purple, and scarlet as the Lord commanded Moses.

**[Exodus 39:8-14](/bible/exodus/39#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/39#v8)</sup> And he made the breastplate of cunning work, like the work of the ephod; of gold, blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/39#v9)</sup> It was foursquare; they made the breastplate double: a span was the length thereof, and a span the breadth thereof, being doubled.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/39#v10)</sup> And they set in it four rows of stones: the first row was a sardius, a topaz, and a carbuncle: this was the first row.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/39#v11)</sup> And the second row, an emerald, a sapphire, and a diamond.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/39#v12)</sup> And the third row, a ligure, an agate, and an amethyst.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/39#v13)</sup> And the fourth row, a beryl, an onyx, and a jasper: they were inclosed in ouches of gold in their inclosings.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/39#v14)</sup> And the stones were according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve, according to their names, like the engravings of a signet, every one with his name, according to the twelve tribes.

- The breastplate carried four rows of stones, and the stones were according to the names of the children of Israel, twelve according to their names, like the engravings of a signet, every one with his name according to the twelve tribes; the shoulders of the ephod carried onyx stones graven the same way, stones for a memorial to the children of Israel.
- The Most High had us representing: when the priest stepped on the scene you already knew what nationality he was. Nationality is very important to the Lord, and he took it very seriously.

**[Exodus 39:24-26](/bible/exodus/39#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/39#v24)</sup> And they made upon the hems of the robe pomegranates of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and twined linen.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/39#v25)</sup> And they made bells of pure gold, and put the bells between the pomegranates upon the hem of the robe, round about between the pomegranates;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/39#v26)</sup> A bell and a pomegranate, a bell and a pomegranate, round about the hem of the robe to minister in; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The pomegranates and the bells of gold went upon the hem of the robe round about; the word keeps saying round about, because the fringes go all throughout the garment, round about, not gathered only at four corners. Old depictions show Israelites with the fringes all the way around, and it would not hurt to put them all throughout, out of the fear of God.

**[Exodus 39:30-31](/bible/exodus/39#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/39#v30)</sup> And they made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold, and wrote upon it a writing, like to the engravings of a signet, HOLINESS TO THE LORD.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/39#v31)</sup> And they tied unto it a lace of blue, to fasten it on high upon the mitre; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- They made the plate of the holy crown of pure gold, written like the engravings of a signet, HOLINESS TO THE LORD, fastened on high upon the mitre with a lace of blue.
- Black man, this is your heritage: our forefathers the priests wore crowns of real pure gold. It is a blessing to come back to this knowledge; they will not teach you this anywhere else.

**[Exodus 40:34-38](/bible/exodus/40#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/40#v34)</sup> Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/40#v35)</sup> And Moses was not able to enter into the tent of the congregation, because the cloud abode thereon, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/40#v36)</sup> And when the cloud was taken up from over the tabernacle, the children of Israel went onward in all their journeys:
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/exodus/40#v37)</sup> But if the cloud were not taken up, then they journeyed not till the day that it was taken up.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/40#v38)</sup> For the cloud of the Lord was upon the tabernacle by day, and fire was on it by night, in the sight of all the house of Israel, throughout all their journeys.

- Then a cloud covered the tent of the congregation, and the glory of the Lord filled the tabernacle. Our forefathers were blessed to witness these things firsthand; it should be very exciting to read of the pillar and the glory that our ancestors saw in the wilderness.
- Israel did not have to rely on maps; the cloud of the Lord upon the tabernacle by day and the fire by night were the navigation system. When the cloud was taken up they journeyed, and if it was not taken up they journeyed not until the day it was taken up.
- The cloud led them in the day and the pillar of fire gave them light in the way by night; the Most High in His manifold mercies forsook them not in the wilderness. They had no need to create their own lamps; the Lord gave them light freely.
- The cloud and the fire speak of spirits the Most High created, His angels: He maketh the clouds His chariot, and He maketh His angels spirits, His ministers a flaming fire. Fire is a spirit, created for vengeance, poured out in the time of destruction to appease the wrath of Him that made them.

    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/39#v29)</sup> Fire, and hail, and famine, and death, all these were created for vengeance;

    Fire is one of the spirits created for vengeance; fire is a spirit.
  - **[Psalms 34:7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)</sup> The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

    The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear Him, and delivereth them; Israelites that fear the Most High are guarded by His angels.

- The Most High sent an angel before Israel to keep them in the way and to bring them into the place He had prepared, warning them to obey his voice and provoke him not, for he will not pardon your transgressions, for my name is in him. That shows why the first generation did not make it to the promised land: they provoked the angel, transgressed, complained, worshipped idols, and wanted to go back into Egypt.
- If Israel obeyed the angel's voice, the Lord would be an enemy unto their enemies and cut off the nations of the land before them. The scripture makes a distinction: the enemy whose ox or ass you must bring back and help is your brother that hates you, as the parallel law shows by using the word brother; the enemies of the nations are those the Most High said He would cut off. So love thine enemies does not mean the nation Israel was sold to; it speaks of your brother that you may be at odds with.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 23:20-23](/bible/exodus/23#v20)**

  - **[Matthew 5:44](/bible/matthew/5#v44)**
