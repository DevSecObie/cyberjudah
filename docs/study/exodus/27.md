**[Exodus 27:1-8](/bible/exodus/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/27#v1)</sup> And thou shalt make an altar of shittim wood, five cubits long, and five cubits broad; the altar shall be foursquare: and the height thereof shall be three cubits.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/27#v2)</sup> And thou shalt make the horns of it upon the four corners thereof: his horns shall be of the same: and thou shalt overlay it with brass.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/27#v3)</sup> And thou shalt make his pans to receive his ashes, and his shovels, and his basons, and his fleshhooks, and his firepans: all the vessels thereof thou shalt make of brass.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/27#v4)</sup> And thou shalt make for it a grate of network of brass; and upon the net shalt thou make four brasen rings in the four corners thereof.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/27#v5)</sup> And thou shalt put it under the compass of the altar beneath, that the net may be even to the midst of the altar.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/27#v6)</sup> And thou shalt make staves for the altar, staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with brass.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/27#v7)</sup> And the staves shall be put into the rings, and the staves shall be upon the two sides of the altar, to bear it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/27#v8)</sup> Hollow with boards shalt thou make it: as it was shewed thee in the mount, so shall they make it.

- Verses one through eight go into the altar in which the sacrifices were done in the tabernacle: the altar of shittim wood foursquare, its horns of the same, overlaid with brass, with its pans, shovels, basons, fleshhooks, and firepans, the grate of network of brass, and the staves to bear it.
- As it was shewed thee in the mount, so shall they make it; the altar too follows the pattern.

>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/27#v10)</sup> And the twenty pillars thereof and their twenty sockets shall be of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets shall be of silver.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/27#v11)</sup> And likewise for the north side in length there shall be hangings of an hundred cubits long, and his twenty pillars and their twenty sockets of brass; the hooks of the pillars and their fillets of silver.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/27#v12)</sup> And for the breadth of the court on the west side shall be hangings of fifty cubits: their pillars ten, and their sockets ten.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/27#v13)</sup> And the breadth of the court on the east side eastward shall be fifty cubits.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/27#v14)</sup> The hangings of one side of the gate shall be fifteen cubits: their pillars three, and their sockets three.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/27#v15)</sup> And on the other side shall be hangings fifteen cubits: their pillars three, and their sockets three.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/27#v16)</sup> And for the gate of the court shall be an hanging of twenty cubits, of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, wrought with needlework: and their pillars shall be four, and their sockets four.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/27#v17)</sup> All the pillars round about the court shall be filleted with silver; their hooks shall be of silver, and their sockets of brass.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/27#v18)</sup> The length of the court shall be an hundred cubits, and the breadth fifty every where, and the height five cubits of fine twined linen, and their sockets of brass.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/27#v19)</sup> All the vessels of the tabernacle in all the service thereof, and all the pins thereof, and all the pins of the court, shall be of brass.

- The court of the tabernacle: hangings of fine twined linen an hundred cubits on the south and north sides, fifty on the west and east, with pillars filleted with silver in sockets of brass, and the gate of the court a hanging of blue, purple, and scarlet wrought with needlework. The length of the court a hundred cubits, the breadth fifty every where, the height five cubits, and all the pins of brass.

**[Exodus 27:20-21](/bible/exodus/27#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/27#v20)</sup> And thou shalt command the children of Israel, that they bring thee pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamp to burn always.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/27#v21)</sup> In the tabernacle of the congregation without the vail, which is before the testimony, Aaron and his sons shall order it from evening to morning before the Lord: it shall be a statute for ever unto their generations on the behalf of the children of Israel.

- The Most High tells Moses to command the children of Israel to bring pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamp to burn always; Aaron and his sons order it from evening to morning before the Lord, a statute for ever unto their generations.
- Though Israel is in captivity now without a temple, without lamps to bring oil for, and no longer under the Levitical priesthood, think about it spiritually: you have to keep your lamp lit and keep the oil in your life. Brotherhood and unity, and correction, which is an excellent oil that shall not break the head, are that oil; if you are not around a body being built up and corrected by teachers and peers, that is how people run out of oil, their lamp goes out, and they fall out of this truth and do not endure to the end. The ten virgins with their lamps and oil come to mind here, offered as a meditation.
