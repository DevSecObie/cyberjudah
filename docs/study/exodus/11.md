**[Exodus 11:1](/bible/exodus/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/11#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Yet will I bring one plague more upon Pharaoh, and upon Egypt; afterwards he will let you go hence: when he shall let you go, he shall surely thrust you out hence altogether.

- The Most High already knew what was going down: yet will I bring one plague more upon Pharaoh and upon Egypt. This last plague would end it, and when Pharaoh let the people go he would thrust them out hence altogether, with the quickness.

**[Exodus 11:2-3](/bible/exodus/11#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/11#v2)</sup> Speak now in the ears of the people, and let every man borrow of his neighbour, and every woman of her neighbour, jewels of silver, and jewels of gold.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/11#v3)</sup> And the Lord gave the people favour in the sight of the Egyptians. Moreover the man Moses was very great in the land of Egypt, in the sight of Pharaoh’s servants, and in the sight of the people.

- Every man and woman was to borrow of their neighbours jewels of silver and jewels of gold, and the Lord gave the people favour in the sight of the Egyptians.
- Moses was very great in the land of Egypt, in the sight of Pharaoh's servants and in the sight of the people; he had a good report. Israel in spiritual Egypt must deal the same way: have the respect of the people, and if people despise you, let it be because they hate God and the word you teach, not because you are in wickedness, shady dealings, deceit, or disrespect.

**[Exodus 11:4-6](/bible/exodus/11#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/11#v4)</sup> And Moses said, Thus saith the Lord, About midnight will I go out into the midst of Egypt:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/11#v5)</sup> And all the firstborn in the land of Egypt shall die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh that sitteth upon his throne, even unto the firstborn of the maidservant that is behind the mill; and all the firstborn of beasts.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/11#v6)</sup> And there shall be a great cry throughout all the land of Egypt, such as there was none like it, nor shall be like it any more.

- About midnight the Lord would go out into the midst of Egypt, and all the firstborn in the land would die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh on his throne to the firstborn of the maidservant behind the mill, and all the firstborn of beasts.
- There would be a great cry throughout all the land of Egypt, such as there was none like it, nor shall be like it any more.

**[Exodus 11:7](/bible/exodus/11#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/11#v7)</sup> But against any of the children of Israel shall not a dog move his tongue, against man or beast: that ye may know how that the Lord doth put a difference between the Egyptians and Israel.

- Against the children of Israel not a dog would move his tongue, that ye may know how that the Lord doth put a difference between the Egyptians and Israel.
- A difference had to be shown because Israel and the Egyptians looked alike. Moses was mistaken for an Egyptian, Paul was mistaken for an Egyptian, Joseph was mistaken for an Egyptian by his own brothers, and at Jacob's funeral the mourners were called Egyptians because no one could tell the difference.
- The difference was made plain in the plague itself: the houses whose firstborn died were shown not to be his people.

- Pharaoh would not hearken, that the wonders of the Most High might be multiplied in the land of Egypt. He is in control of the leaders of every country, presidents, governors, senators, congressmen, and mayors alike; whoever gets in the seat, he twists and turns them about like the river.
