**[Exodus 35:1-3](/bible/exodus/35#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/35#v1)</sup> And Moses gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel together, and said unto them, These are the words which the Lord hath commanded, that ye should do them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/35#v2)</sup> Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you an holy day, a sabbath of rest to the Lord: whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/35#v3)</sup> Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day.

- Moses gathered all the congregation of the children of Israel: six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you an holy day, a sabbath of rest to the Lord; whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.
- Ye shall kindle no fire throughout your habitations upon the sabbath day: kindling fire goes to cooking. Bake that which ye will bake today, and seethe that ye will seethe, and lay up what remains to be kept unto the morning; you could not cook on the sabbath, so the day before is the prep day, and what you prepare that day is what you eat.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 16:23](/bible/exodus/16#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/16#v23)</sup> And he said unto them, This is that which the Lord hath said, To morrow is the rest of the holy sabbath unto the Lord: bake that which ye will bake to day, and seethe that ye will seethe; and that which remaineth over lay up for you to be kept until the morning.

    Bake that which ye will bake today, and lay up what remaineth to be kept until the morning; kindling fire on the sabbath goes to cooking, so the cooking was done on the preparation day.

**[Exodus 35:4-9](/bible/exodus/35#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/35#v4)</sup> And Moses spake unto all the congregation of the children of Israel, saying, This is the thing which the Lord commanded, saying,

> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/35#v6)</sup> And blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goats’ hair,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/35#v7)</sup> And rams’ skins dyed red, and badgers’ skins, and shittim wood,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/35#v8)</sup> And oil for the light, and spices for anointing oil, and for the sweet incense,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/35#v9)</sup> And onyx stones, and stones to be set for the ephod, and for the breastplate.

- Whosoever is of a willing heart, let him bring the Lord's offering: gold, silver, and brass; blue, purple, scarlet, fine linen, and goats' hair; rams' skins dyed red and badgers' skins; shittim wood; oil for the light and spices for the anointing oil and the sweet incense; and onyx stones and stones to be set for the ephod and the breastplate. These are the offerings the people would bring unto the Lord.

- The congregation departed from the presence of Moses, and they came, every one whose heart stirred him up and every one whom his spirit made willing, and brought the Lord's offering for the work of the tabernacle; the people brought the gold and silver and all the things listed willingly.
- Both men and women, as many as were willing hearted, brought bracelets, and earrings, and rings, and tablets, all jewels of gold; and every man with whom was found blue, purple, scarlet, fine linen, goats' hair, red skins of rams, badgers' skins, silver, brass, or shittim wood brought it.
- All the women that were wise hearted did spin with their hands; the women knew how to sew, and brought that which they had spun of blue, purple, scarlet, and fine linen, and the women whose heart stirred them up in wisdom spun goats' hair. The rulers brought onyx stones, stones for the ephod and breastplate, spice, and oil.

**[Exodus 35:30-35](/bible/exodus/35#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/35#v30)</sup> And Moses said unto the children of Israel, See, the Lord hath called by name Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/35#v31)</sup> And he hath filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship;
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/35#v32)</sup> And to devise curious works, to work in gold, and in silver, and in brass,
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/35#v33)</sup> And in the cutting of stones, to set them, and in carving of wood, to make any manner of cunning work.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/35#v34)</sup> And he hath put in his heart that he may teach, both he, and Aholiab, the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/35#v35)</sup> Them hath he filled with wisdom of heart, to work all manner of work, of the engraver, and of the cunning workman, and of the embroiderer, in blue, and in purple, in scarlet, and in fine linen, and of the weaver, even of them that do any work, and of those that devise cunning work.

- The Lord called by name Bezaleel the son of Uri, the son of Hur, of the tribe of Judah, and filled him with the spirit of God, in wisdom, in understanding, and in knowledge, and in all manner of workmanship; the Most High put His spirit on him to be gifted in what he did, to devise curious works in gold, silver, and brass, in cutting of stones, and in carving of wood.
- He hath put in his heart that he may teach, both he and Aholiab the son of Ahisamach, of the tribe of Dan; the two brothers the Most High was dealing with in workmanship trained and taught their fellow brethren how to do it.
- It is the same today: the Most High puts His spirit on brothers and sisters with different talents, and they put their talents to use and teach others in the congregation; Israel was putting their talents together and building, showing each other how to do these works.
