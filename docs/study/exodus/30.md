## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 30](/bible/exodus/30) through 33: the altar of incense, the ransom of souls, the laver of brass, the holy anointing oil and perfume, Bezaleel and Aholiab filled with wisdom to build, the sabbath as a sign between the Most High and Israel, the golden calf at Horeb, and Moses pleading for the presence of the Lord and seeing his glory pass by. This was a reading session with teaching along the way; the questions answered at the end are merged under the verses they belong to.

**[Exodus 30:1-5](/bible/exodus/30#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/30#v1)</sup> And thou shalt make an altar to burn incense upon: of shittim wood shalt thou make it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/30#v2)</sup> A cubit shall be the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof; foursquare shall it be: and two cubits shall be the height thereof: the horns thereof shall be of the same.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/30#v3)</sup> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, the top thereof, and the sides thereof round about, and the horns thereof; and thou shalt make unto it a crown of gold round about.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/30#v4)</sup> And two golden rings shalt thou make to it under the crown of it, by the two corners thereof, upon the two sides of it shalt thou make it; and they shall be for places for the staves to bear it withal.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/30#v5)</sup> And thou shalt make the staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with gold.

- The altar to burn incense upon was made of shittim wood, a cubit in length and a cubit in breadth, foursquare, and two cubits high, with horns of the same. A cubit has been read to be about seventeen inches, so this stood about waist high.
- It was overlaid with pure gold, the top, the sides round about, and the horns, with a crown of gold round about, two golden rings under the crown, and staves of shittim wood overlaid with gold. The staves through the rings were how the priests bore it as Israel moved through the wilderness.

**[Exodus 30:6-9](/bible/exodus/30#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/30#v6)</sup> And thou shalt put it before the vail that is by the ark of the testimony, before the mercy seat that is over the testimony, where I will meet with thee.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/30#v7)</sup> And Aaron shall burn thereon sweet incense every morning: when he dresseth the lamps, he shall burn incense upon it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/30#v8)</sup> And when Aaron lighteth the lamps at even, he shall burn incense upon it, a perpetual incense before the Lord throughout your generations.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/30#v9)</sup> Ye shall offer no strange incense thereon, nor burnt sacrifice, nor meat offering; neither shall ye pour drink offering thereon.

- The altar was put before the vail that is by the ark of the testimony, before the mercy seat where the Most High would meet with Moses.
- Aaron burned sweet incense on it every morning when he dressed the lamps, and again at even when he lighted the lamps, a perpetual incense before the Lord throughout the generations; it burned continually, morning and evening.
- No strange incense went on it, and no burnt sacrifice, meat offering, or drink offering; this altar was for one specific incense only. The service had to be exactly as prescribed, and deviation carried death, which is a reason to thank the Lord for Christ.

**[Exodus 30:10](/bible/exodus/30#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/30#v10)</sup> And Aaron shall make an atonement upon the horns of it once in a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonements: once in the year shall he make atonement upon it throughout your generations: it is most holy unto the Lord.

- Aaron made an atonement upon the horns of it once in a year with the blood of the sin offering of atonements; it is most holy unto the Lord. This altar served the day of atonement.
- On the tenth day of the seventh month Israel afflicted their souls and did no work at all, and the priest made an atonement for the sanctuary, the tabernacle, the altar, the priests, and all the people of the congregation, to cleanse them from all their sins before the Lord; an everlasting statute, once a year.
- The priests went always into the first tabernacle accomplishing the service of God, but into the second went the high priest alone once every year, not without blood. Christ offered one sacrifice for sins for ever, so the day of atonement is still kept by afflicting the soul and doing no work, but without the animal sacrifice, because Christ took the place of the animal.

**[Exodus 30:11-16](/bible/exodus/30#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/30#v11)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/30#v12)</sup> When thou takest the sum of the children of Israel after their number, then shall they give every man a ransom for his soul unto the Lord, when thou numberest them; that there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/30#v13)</sup> This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are numbered, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary: (a shekel is twenty gerahs:) an half shekel shall be the offering of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/30#v14)</sup> Every one that passeth among them that are numbered, from twenty years old and above, shall give an offering unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/30#v15)</sup> The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than half a shekel, when they give an offering unto the Lord, to make an atonement for your souls.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/30#v16)</sup> And thou shalt take the atonement money of the children of Israel, and shalt appoint it for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation; that it may be a memorial unto the children of Israel before the Lord, to make an atonement for your souls.

- Numbering the children of Israel was not done lightly; when they were counted, every man gave a ransom for his soul unto the Lord, that there be no plague among them when they were numbered.
- The ransom was half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary, from every one that passed among them that were numbered, from twenty years old and above; the rich gave no more and the poor gave no less, to make an atonement for their souls.
- The atonement money was appointed for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation. The gold, the wood, the curtains, and the fabrics all had to be bought and kept up, and the tabernacle moved with the nation through the wilderness; that is the same reason offerings are gathered in the congregation today, because the house of the Most High cannot be built or kept without it.

>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/30#v19)</sup> For Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet thereat:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/30#v20)</sup> When they go into the tabernacle of the congregation, they shall wash with water, that they die not; or when they come near to the altar to minister, to burn offering made by fire unto the Lord:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/30#v21)</sup> So they shall wash their hands and their feet, that they die not: and it shall be a statute for ever to them, even to him and to his seed throughout their generations.

- The laver of brass, with his foot also of brass, stood between the tabernacle of the congregation and the altar, with water in it; a great brass bowl set on a brass base.
- Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet thereat when they went into the tabernacle of the congregation and when they came near to the altar to minister, that they die not; the priests could not go in unclean.
- It was a statute for ever to Aaron and to his seed throughout their generations.

**[Exodus 30:22-33](/bible/exodus/30#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/30#v22)</sup> Moreover the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/30#v23)</sup> Take thou also unto thee principal spices, of pure myrrh five hundred shekels, and of sweet cinnamon half so much, even two hundred and fifty shekels, and of sweet calamus two hundred and fifty shekels,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/30#v24)</sup> And of cassia five hundred shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, and of oil olive an hin:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/30#v25)</sup> And thou shalt make it an oil of holy ointment, an ointment compound after the art of the apothecary: it shall be an holy anointing oil.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/30#v26)</sup> And thou shalt anoint the tabernacle of the congregation therewith, and the ark of the testimony,
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/30#v27)</sup> And the table and all his vessels, and the candlestick and his vessels, and the altar of incense,
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/30#v28)</sup> And the altar of burnt offering with all his vessels, and the laver and his foot.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/30#v29)</sup> And thou shalt sanctify them, that they may be most holy: whatsoever toucheth them shall be holy.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/30#v30)</sup> And thou shalt anoint Aaron and his sons, and consecrate them, that they may minister unto me in the priest’s office.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/30#v31)</sup> And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, This shall be an holy anointing oil unto me throughout your generations.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/30#v32)</sup> Upon man’s flesh shall it not be poured, neither shall ye make any other like it, after the composition of it: it is holy, and it shall be holy unto you.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/30#v33)</sup> Whosoever compoundeth any like it, or whosoever putteth any of it upon a stranger, shall even be cut off from his people.

- The holy anointing oil was compounded of principal spices, pure myrrh five hundred shekels, sweet cinnamon two hundred and fifty, sweet calamus two hundred and fifty, cassia five hundred after the shekel of the sanctuary, and oil olive an hin, an ointment compound after the art of the apothecary, which is a maker of perfume.
- With it were anointed the tabernacle of the congregation, the ark of the testimony, the table and all his vessels, the candlestick and his vessels, the altar of incense, the altar of burnt offering with all his vessels, and the laver and his foot, that they be most holy; whatsoever toucheth them shall be holy. Aaron and his sons were anointed and consecrated with it to minister in the priest's office.
- It was not poured upon man's flesh, and none was to be made like it after the composition of it; whosoever compounded any like it, or put any of it upon a stranger, was cut off from his people.
- The Most High gave specific orders down to the spices and the weights, and none could deviate from them or they would die. Our forefathers carried out that tedious, exact work, yet we complain about the smallest things set in place for order, the uniform, the new moon, and not cooking on the sabbath; the spirit has to get right.

**[Exodus 30:34-38](/bible/exodus/30#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/30#v34)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Take unto thee sweet spices, stacte, and onycha, and galbanum; these sweet spices with pure frankincense: of each shall there be a like weight:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/30#v35)</sup> And thou shalt make it a perfume, a confection after the art of the apothecary, tempered together, pure and holy:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/30#v36)</sup> And thou shalt beat some of it very small, and put of it before the testimony in the tabernacle of the congregation, where I will meet with thee: it shall be unto you most holy.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/exodus/30#v37)</sup> And as for the perfume which thou shalt make, ye shall not make to yourselves according to the composition thereof: it shall be unto thee holy for the Lord.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/30#v38)</sup> Whosoever shall make like unto that, to smell thereto, shall even be cut off from his people.

- The perfume was made of sweet spices, stacte, onycha, and galbanum, with pure frankincense, of each a like weight, tempered together, pure and holy, a confection after the art of the apothecary.
- Part of it was beaten very small and put before the testimony in the tabernacle of the congregation; unto you it shall be most holy. The Most High wanted the tabernacle anointed and smelling sweet, everything done one specific way.
- It was for the Lord alone; ye shall not make to yourselves according to the composition thereof, and whosoever made like unto it, to smell thereto, was cut off from his people. There were no knockoffs of the holy things; a copy carried death.
