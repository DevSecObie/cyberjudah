**[Exodus 36:1-3](/bible/exodus/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/36#v1)</sup> Then wrought Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise hearted man, in whom the Lord put wisdom and understanding to know how to work all manner of work for the service of the sanctuary, according to all that the Lord had commanded.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/36#v2)</sup> And Moses called Bezaleel and Aholiab, and every wise hearted man, in whose heart the Lord had put wisdom, even every one whose heart stirred him up to come unto the work to do it:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/36#v3)</sup> And they received of Moses all the offering, which the children of Israel had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary, to make it withal. And they brought yet unto him free offerings every morning.

- Then wrought Bezaleel and Aholiab and every wise hearted man in whom the Lord put wisdom and understanding, according to all that the Lord had commanded; they were over the cunning work of the building and were leading the men among them in how the work was done.
- They received of Moses all the offering which the children of Israel had brought for the work of the service of the sanctuary; and the people brought yet unto him free offerings every morning.

**[Exodus 36:4-7](/bible/exodus/36#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/36#v4)</sup> And all the wise men, that wrought all the work of the sanctuary, came every man from his work which they made;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/36#v5)</sup> And they spake unto Moses, saying, The people bring much more than enough for the service of the work, which the Lord commanded to make.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/36#v6)</sup> And Moses gave commandment, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp, saying, Let neither man nor woman make any more work for the offering of the sanctuary. So the people were restrained from bringing.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/36#v7)</sup> For the stuff they had was sufficient for all the work to make it, and too much.

- The wise men that wrought the work came and spake unto Moses: the people bring much more than enough for the service of the work. That shows how willing they were; it was too much.
- Moses gave commandment, and they caused it to be proclaimed throughout the camp that neither man nor woman make any more work for the offering of the sanctuary; the people were restrained from bringing, for the stuff they had was sufficient for all the work, and too much.

**[Exodus 36:8-13](/bible/exodus/36#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/36#v8)</sup> And every wise hearted man among them that wrought the work of the tabernacle made ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet: with cherubims of cunning work made he them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/36#v9)</sup> The length of one curtain was twenty and eight cubits, and the breadth of one curtain four cubits: the curtains were all of one size.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/36#v10)</sup> And he coupled the five curtains one unto another: and the other five curtains he coupled one unto another.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/36#v11)</sup> And he made loops of blue on the edge of one curtain from the selvedge in the coupling: likewise he made in the uttermost side of another curtain, in the coupling of the second.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/36#v12)</sup> Fifty loops made he in one curtain, and fifty loops made he in the edge of the curtain which was in the coupling of the second: the loops held one curtain to another.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/36#v13)</sup> And he made fifty taches of gold, and coupled the curtains one unto another with the taches: so it became one tabernacle.

- Every wise hearted man among them made ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet, with cherubims of cunning work; the cherubims were wrought into the curtains by the skilled craftsmen.
- A cubit is eighteen inches: the length of one curtain, twenty and eight cubits, is forty two feet, and the breadth of one curtain, four cubits, is seventy two inches; the curtains were all of one size.
- He coupled five curtains one unto another, and the other five likewise, with fifty loops on the edge of each coupling and fifty taches of gold to couple the curtains one unto another; so it became one tabernacle.

**[Exodus 36:14-19](/bible/exodus/36#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/36#v14)</sup> And he made curtains of goats’ hair for the tent over the tabernacle: eleven curtains he made them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/36#v15)</sup> The length of one curtain was thirty cubits, and four cubits was the breadth of one curtain: the eleven curtains were of one size.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/36#v16)</sup> And he coupled five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/36#v17)</sup> And he made fifty loops upon the uttermost edge of the curtain in the coupling, and fifty loops made he upon the edge of the curtain which coupleth the second.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/36#v18)</sup> And he made fifty taches of brass to couple the tent together, that it might be one.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/36#v19)</sup> And he made a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering of badgers’ skins above that.

- He made eleven curtains of goats' hair for the tent over the tabernacle, coupled five by themselves and six by themselves, with fifty loops and fifty taches of brass to couple the tent together, that it might be one.
- He made a covering for the tent of rams' skins dyed red, and a covering of badgers' skins above that; these are the materials used to make the covering of the tent for the tabernacle, all commanded by the Most High God to Moses.

**[Exodus 36:20-34](/bible/exodus/36#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/36#v20)</sup> And he made boards for the tabernacle of shittim wood, standing up.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/36#v21)</sup> The length of a board was ten cubits, and the breadth of a board one cubit and a half.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/36#v22)</sup> One board had two tenons, equally distant one from another: thus did he make for all the boards of the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/36#v23)</sup> And he made boards for the tabernacle; twenty boards for the south side southward:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/36#v24)</sup> And forty sockets of silver he made under the twenty boards; two sockets under one board for his two tenons, and two sockets under another board for his two tenons.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/36#v25)</sup> And for the other side of the tabernacle, which is toward the north corner, he made twenty boards,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/36#v26)</sup> And their forty sockets of silver; two sockets under one board, and two sockets under another board.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/36#v27)</sup> And for the sides of the tabernacle westward he made six boards.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/36#v28)</sup> And two boards made he for the corners of the tabernacle in the two sides.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/36#v29)</sup> And they were coupled beneath, and coupled together at the head thereof, to one ring: thus he did to both of them in both the corners.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/36#v30)</sup> And there were eight boards; and their sockets were sixteen sockets of silver, under every board two sockets.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/36#v31)</sup> And he made bars of shittim wood; five for the boards of the one side of the tabernacle,
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/36#v32)</sup> And five bars for the boards of the other side of the tabernacle, and five bars for the boards of the tabernacle for the sides westward.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/36#v33)</sup> And he made the middle bar to shoot through the boards from the one end to the other.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/36#v34)</sup> And he overlaid the boards with gold, and made their rings of gold to be places for the bars, and overlaid the bars with gold.

- He made boards for the tabernacle of shittim wood standing up: this is the framework of the tabernacle that holds it up. The length of a board was ten cubits, which is one hundred and eighty inches, and the breadth of a board a cubit and a half, which is twenty seven inches.
- Twenty boards for the south side and twenty for the north, with forty sockets of silver under each twenty, two sockets under every board for his two tenons; six boards westward and two for the corners; and bars of shittim wood, five for the boards of each side, with the middle bar shooting through the boards from the one end to the other. He overlaid the boards and the bars with gold, and made their rings of gold for the places of the bars.
- The whole tent was built to be carried. Any time Israel moved through the wilderness, the men appointed as carriers would break everything down and travel with it toward the land of Canaan; wherever the Most High moved, Israel followed, and where the Most High stopped, they set up again.

**[Exodus 36:35-38](/bible/exodus/36#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/36#v35)</sup> And he made a vail of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen: with cherubims made he it of cunning work.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/36#v36)</sup> And he made thereunto four pillars of shittim wood, and overlaid them with gold: their hooks were of gold; and he cast for them four sockets of silver.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/exodus/36#v37)</sup> And he made an hanging for the tabernacle door of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, of needlework;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/36#v38)</sup> And the five pillars of it with their hooks: and he overlaid their chapiters and their fillets with gold: but their five sockets were of brass.

- He made a vail of blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine twined linen, with cherubims of cunning work, upon four pillars of shittim wood overlaid with gold, their hooks of gold, upon four sockets of silver.
- He made an hanging for the tabernacle door of needlework, with five pillars, their chapiters and their fillets overlaid with gold, and their five sockets of brass.
