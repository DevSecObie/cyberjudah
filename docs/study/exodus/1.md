## Introduction

This chapter comes from the same reading session as [Genesis 48](/bible/genesis/48) through 50, marked (READ). The reading covered the children of Israel multiplying after Joseph, the new kingdom that arose and oppressed them, the godliness of the midwives in saving the men children alive, and Pharaoh's charge to cast the sons into the river.

**[Exodus 1:1-7](/bible/exodus/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/1#v1)</sup> Now these are the names of the children of Israel, which came into Egypt; every man and his household came with Jacob.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/1#v2)</sup> Reuben, Simeon, Levi, and Judah,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/1#v3)</sup> Issachar, Zebulun, and Benjamin,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/1#v4)</sup> Dan, and Naphtali, Gad, and Asher.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/1#v5)</sup> And all the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were seventy souls: for Joseph was in Egypt already.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/1#v6)</sup> And Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/1#v7)</sup> And the children of Israel were fruitful, and increased abundantly, and multiplied, and waxed exceeding mighty; and the land was filled with them.

- All the souls that came out of the loins of Jacob were seventy souls, for Joseph was in Egypt already; and Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation.
- The children of Israel were fruitful, and increased abundantly, and multiplied, and waxed exceeding mighty, and the land was filled with them. Israel had those babies; the nation did not cast away its children, and the increase was the blessing.

**[Exodus 1:8-14](/bible/exodus/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/1#v8)</sup> Now there arose up a new king over Egypt, which knew not Joseph.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/1#v9)</sup> And he said unto his people, Behold, the people of the children of Israel are more and mightier than we:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/1#v10)</sup> Come on, let us deal wisely with them; lest they multiply, and it come to pass, that, when there falleth out any war, they join also unto our enemies, and fight against us, and so get them up out of the land.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/1#v11)</sup> Therefore they did set over them taskmasters to afflict them with their burdens. And they built for Pharaoh treasure cities, Pithom and Raamses.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/1#v12)</sup> But the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and grew. And they were grieved because of the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/1#v13)</sup> And the Egyptians made the children of Israel to serve with rigour:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/1#v14)</sup> And they made their lives bitter with hard bondage, in morter, and in brick, and in all manner of service in the field: all their service, wherein they made them serve, was with rigour.

- Now there arose up a new king over Egypt, which knew not Joseph: this is not merely one king but a new kingdom arising, the eighteenth dynasty.
- Come on, let us deal wisely with them, lest they multiply and join our enemies: the same wise dealing works on Israel in America today, through movements set up to redirect the people, through Planned Parenthood, and through the prison industrial complex; but the enemy did not count on Christ returning, restoring the nation, and bringing us back to the commandments of God.
- They set over them taskmasters to afflict them with their burdens, and they built for Pharaoh treasure cities, Pithom and Raamses; but the more they afflicted them, the more they multiplied and grew.
- The Egyptians made the children of Israel to serve with rigour, and made their lives bitter with hard bondage, in morter and in brick and in all manner of service in the field.

**[Exodus 1:15-21](/bible/exodus/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/1#v15)</sup> And the king of Egypt spake to the Hebrew midwives, of which the name of the one was Shiphrah, and the name of the other Puah:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/1#v16)</sup> And he said, When ye do the office of a midwife to the Hebrew women, and see them upon the stools; if it be a son, then ye shall kill him: but if it be a daughter, then she shall live.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/1#v17)</sup> But the midwives feared God, and did not as the king of Egypt commanded them, but saved the men children alive.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/1#v18)</sup> And the king of Egypt called for the midwives, and said unto them, Why have ye done this thing, and have saved the men children alive?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/1#v19)</sup> And the midwives said unto Pharaoh, Because the Hebrew women are not as the Egyptian women; for they are lively, and are delivered ere the midwives come in unto them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/1#v20)</sup> Therefore God dealt well with the midwives: and the people multiplied, and waxed very mighty.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/1#v21)</sup> And it came to pass, because the midwives feared God, that he made them houses.

- The king of Egypt commanded the Hebrew midwives, Shiphrah and Puah, to kill every son born upon the stools and save the daughters alive; but the midwives feared God, and did not as the king of Egypt commanded them, but saved the men children alive.
- The midwives answered Pharaoh that the Hebrew women are not as the Egyptian women, for they are lively, and are delivered ere the midwives come in unto them; the daughters of Israel are not as the women of the nations.
- Because the midwives feared God, God dealt well with them and made them houses; the Most High blessed the women who saved the children of Israel alive, and the people multiplied and waxed very mighty.

**[Exodus 1:22](/bible/exodus/1#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/1#v22)</sup> And Pharaoh charged all his people, saying, Every son that is born ye shall cast into the river, and every daughter ye shall save alive.

- Pharaoh then charged all his people, saying, Every son that is born ye shall cast into the river, and every daughter ye shall save alive.
