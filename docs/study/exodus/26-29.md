## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 26](/bible/exodus/26) through 29: the curtains, boards, and veil of the tabernacle; the brasen altar, the court, and the oil for the lamp; the holy garments of Aaron the high priest; and the consecration of Aaron and his sons in the priest's office. This is a reading session; the chapters are read straight through with teaching at the key points, and the first two chapters move quickly since the tabernacle furniture was covered in the previous session on chapter 25. The recording begins in the middle of [Exodus 26](/bible/exodus/26), at the comment on the cubit in verse 2, so the opening of the session is not on the tape.

**[Exodus 26:1-6](/bible/exodus/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/26#v1)</sup> Moreover thou shalt make the tabernacle with ten curtains of fine twined linen, and blue, and purple, and scarlet: with cherubims of cunning work shalt thou make them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/26#v2)</sup> The length of one curtain shall be eight and twenty cubits, and the breadth of one curtain four cubits: and every one of the curtains shall have one measure.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/26#v3)</sup> The five curtains shall be coupled together one to another; and other five curtains shall be coupled one to another.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/26#v4)</sup> And thou shalt make loops of blue upon the edge of the one curtain from the selvedge in the coupling; and likewise shalt thou make in the uttermost edge of another curtain, in the coupling of the second.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/26#v5)</sup> Fifty loops shalt thou make in the one curtain, and fifty loops shalt thou make in the edge of the curtain that is in the coupling of the second; that the loops may take hold one of another.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/26#v6)</sup> And thou shalt make fifty taches of gold, and couple the curtains together with the taches: and it shall be one tabernacle.

- The ten curtains of the tabernacle are measured in cubits. A cubit is approximately eighteen inches when you look it up today, and it was probably bigger, because the men were of larger stature back then.
- The curtains of fine twined linen with blue, purple, and scarlet are coupled with loops and taches of gold, that it may be one tabernacle.

**[Exodus 26:7-14](/bible/exodus/26#v7)**

>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/26#v8)</sup> The length of one curtain shall be thirty cubits, and the breadth of one curtain four cubits: and the eleven curtains shall be all of one measure.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/26#v9)</sup> And thou shalt couple five curtains by themselves, and six curtains by themselves, and shalt double the sixth curtain in the forefront of the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/26#v10)</sup> And thou shalt make fifty loops on the edge of the one curtain that is outmost in the coupling, and fifty loops in the edge of the curtain which coupleth the second.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/26#v11)</sup> And thou shalt make fifty taches of brass, and put the taches into the loops, and couple the tent together, that it may be one.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/26#v12)</sup> And the remnant that remaineth of the curtains of the tent, the half curtain that remaineth, shall hang over the backside of the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/26#v13)</sup> And a cubit on the one side, and a cubit on the other side of that which remaineth in the length of the curtains of the tent, it shall hang over the sides of the tabernacle on this side and on that side, to cover it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/26#v14)</sup> And thou shalt make a covering for the tent of rams’ skins dyed red, and a covering above of badgers’ skins.

- Eleven curtains of goats' hair cover the tabernacle, with a covering of rams' skins dyed red above, and a covering above that of badgers' skins.
- The badgers' skins settle a doctrine question. [Leviticus 11](/bible/leviticus/11) says not to touch the carcase of the unclean beast, and the badger goes on paws, yet the tabernacle had to be built with badger skins by hand. The law goes into sacrificing the unclean thing and eating it, touching it with your mouth; it is not saying a dead animal in your path cannot be moved out of the way with your hands.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 11:8](/bible/leviticus/11#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/11#v8)</sup> Of their flesh shall ye not eat, and their carcase shall ye not touch; they are unclean to you.

    The carcase not to be touched is spoken of the beasts as food; the command sits in the dietary law.
  - **[Leviticus 11:27](/bible/leviticus/11#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/11#v27)</sup> And whatsoever goeth upon his paws, among all manner of beasts that go on all four, those are unclean unto you: whoso toucheth their carcase shall be unclean until the even.

    Whatsoever goeth upon his paws is unclean, which is what makes the badger an unclean beast.

- The boards of shittim wood, the sockets of silver, and the bars overlaid with gold are the physical structure of the tabernacle, how it would be put together and what it would consist of.
- Rear up the tabernacle according to the fashion thereof which was shewed thee in the mount. The Most High showed Moses all of this in mount Sinai; the forty days and forty nights were not the ten commandments alone, Moses came down with a whole architectural layout based on what the Most High showed him.
- What was built in the wilderness was a shadow of what is in the heavens. The Most High is the architect, and He showed Moses the pattern of a heavenly tabernacle to be made for Israel on earth; thy kingdom come, thy will be done in earth as it is in heaven, is an example of that same order.

# Exodus 28: Holy Garments for Glory and for Beauty

Read the chapter → [Exodus 28](/bible/exodus/28)

**[Exodus 28:1-2](/bible/exodus/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/28#v1)</sup> And take thou unto thee Aaron thy brother, and his sons with him, from among the children of Israel, that he may minister unto me in the priest’s office, even Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron’s sons.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/28#v2)</sup> And thou shalt make holy garments for Aaron thy brother for glory and for beauty.

- Aaron and his sons Nadab, Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar are taken from among the children of Israel to minister unto the Most High in the priest's office.
- The Most High commanded holy garments to be made for Aaron for glory and for beauty. Garments were ordained for the men of Israel; when the world hears [Deuteronomy 22:5](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v5) and asks why the men wear garments, the answer is here: the most holy and regarded men of the nation, the priests, were arrayed in long garments down to the foot.
- The sealed of the Lord receive the same. The hundred forty and four thousand of [Revelation 7](/bible/revelation/7) that must be sealed before destruction comes are read of again in 2 Esdras: they departed from the shadow of the world, leaving spiritual Egypt and the ways Babylon taught behind, and have received glorious garments of the Lord. In the kingdom of heaven the Most High will clothe His men in glorious garments as in ancient times, on another level than anything worn today.

- Urim and Thummim means lights and perfections: objects not specifically described in the scriptures, perhaps stones since they went in the breastplate, by which the high priest ascertained the will of the Most High in important matters affecting the nation. The better theory: they served as a symbol of the high priest's authority to seek counsel of the Most High, His will being revealed to him through inner illumination.
- The significance shows in Nehemiah's day: priests whose register was not found among those reckoned by genealogy were put from the priesthood as polluted, and the Tirshatha, who is Nehemiah the governor, said they should not eat of the most holy things till there stood up a priest with Urim and with Thummim; determining that lineage was outside human ability, so it waited on the priest with the divine discerner.

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/29#v17)</sup> And thou shalt cut the ram in pieces, and wash the inwards of him, and his legs, and put them unto his pieces, and unto his head.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/29#v18)</sup> And thou shalt burn the whole ram upon the altar: it is a burnt offering unto the Lord: it is a sweet savour, an offering made by fire unto the Lord.

- The first ram is slain, its blood sprinkled round about upon the altar, and the whole ram burnt upon the altar: it is a burnt offering unto the Lord, a sweet savour, an offering made by fire. There is the second category, the burnt offering.

**[Exodus 29:19-28](/bible/exodus/29#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/29#v19)</sup> And thou shalt take the other ram; and Aaron and his sons shall put their hands upon the head of the ram.

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/29#v22)</sup> Also thou shalt take of the ram the fat and the rump, and the fat that covereth the inwards, and the caul above the liver, and the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, and the right shoulder; for it is a ram of consecration:

> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/29#v27)</sup> And thou shalt sanctify the breast of the wave offering, and the shoulder of the heave offering, which is waved, and which is heaved up, of the ram of the consecration, even of that which is for Aaron, and of that which is for his sons:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/29#v28)</sup> And it shall be Aaron’s and his sons’ by a statute for ever from the children of Israel: for it is an heave offering: and it shall be an heave offering from the children of Israel of the sacrifice of their peace offerings, even their heave offering unto the Lord.

- The second ram is the ram of consecration: its blood is put upon the tip of the right ear of Aaron and of his sons, upon the thumb of their right hand and the great toe of their right foot, and sprinkled upon the altar round about; the blood and the anointing oil are sprinkled upon Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments, and he shall be hallowed.
- The bread and the parts of the ram are put in the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved for a wave offering before the Lord, then burnt upon the altar; and the breast of the wave offering and the shoulder of the heave offering are sanctified for Aaron and his sons by a statute for ever. There go the wave offering and the heave offering, which you will read about throughout the first five books, Leviticus predominantly.
- These ordinances belonged to the Levitical priesthood, and the doctrines that would put men back under pieces of that office today do not even keep what they claim; they sound right because they use scriptures strongly and confidently, but they are in error. Be not tossed to and fro with every wind of doctrine, nor carried about with strange doctrines; do not ever fall away from the Messiah.

- Aaron and his sons eat the flesh of the ram and the bread at the door of the tabernacle, the things wherewith the atonement was made; a stranger shall not eat thereof, because they are holy. And whatever of the flesh or the bread remains unto the morning is burnt with fire and not eaten: no eating the leftovers of the offering, because it is holy.

**[Exodus 29:43-46](/bible/exodus/29#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/exodus/29#v43)</sup> And there I will meet with the children of Israel, and the tabernacle shall be sanctified by my glory.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/exodus/29#v44)</sup> And I will sanctify the tabernacle of the congregation, and the altar: I will sanctify also both Aaron and his sons, to minister to me in the priest’s office.

> <sup>[45](/bible/exodus/29#v45)</sup> And I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/exodus/29#v46)</sup> And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, that brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, that I may dwell among them: I am the Lord their God.

- The tabernacle shall be sanctified by His glory, and Aaron and his sons sanctified to minister in the priest's office; for the Most High to enter and deal with Israel, everything had to be sanctified. That was serious.
- I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God. He was in no other nation's tabernacle, temple, or synagogue, and though there is no tabernacle today it is still the same case: He is in the midst of Israel and none else, which excludes everybody else from the benefits of having the Most High as their God. There is nothing to be ashamed of in the fact that He chose Israel only, and nothing changed across testaments: blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for he hath visited and redeemed his people, and that is the purpose of Christ, to redeem His people.
- The other nations knew it. When the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin heard the temple was being rebuilt they came saying, Let us build with you, for we seek your God; and Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the chief of the fathers answered, Ye have nothing to do with us to build an house unto our God, but we ourselves together will build unto the Lord God of Israel. That is the mindset for these last days: Israel does not need the other nations' assistance, but rebuilds one another spiritually as a nation while awaiting Christ's return to restore the kingdom.
- He brought them forth out of the land of Egypt that He may dwell among them: I am the Lord their God. Israel should walk confident, special, and chosen above all nations of people, for the Most High is faithful and belongs to no one else; repent from spiritual fornication and return to the Most High.
