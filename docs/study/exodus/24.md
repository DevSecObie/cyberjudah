**[Exodus 24:1-3](/bible/exodus/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/24#v1)</sup> And he said unto Moses, Come up unto the Lord, thou, and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel; and worship ye afar off.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/24#v2)</sup> And Moses alone shall come near the Lord: but they shall not come nigh; neither shall the people go up with him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/24#v3)</sup> And Moses came and told the people all the words of the Lord, and all the judgments: and all the people answered with one voice, and said, All the words which the Lord hath said will we do.

- Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel were called up to worship afar off, and Moses alone came near the Lord.
- Moses told the people all the words of the Lord and all the judgments, and all the people answered with one voice: All the words which the Lord hath said will we do. That was us making a verbal agreement with the Lord; we are our ancestors back on the earth again.
- All the hell we catch today, we have no excuse for: it is our fault, because we agreed to do all the words the Lord had spoken. The Lord hates for us to go through it, but where he said all of this would come back on us, God is not a man that he should lie.

**[Exodus 24:4-8](/bible/exodus/24#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/24#v4)</sup> And Moses wrote all the words of the Lord, and rose up early in the morning, and builded an altar under the hill, and twelve pillars, according to the twelve tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/24#v5)</sup> And he sent young men of the children of Israel, which offered burnt offerings, and sacrificed peace offerings of oxen unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/24#v6)</sup> And Moses took half of the blood, and put it in basons; and half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/24#v7)</sup> And he took the book of the covenant, and read in the audience of the people: and they said, All that the Lord hath said will we do, and be obedient.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/24#v8)</sup> And Moses took the blood, and sprinkled it on the people, and said, Behold the blood of the covenant, which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words.

- Moses wrote all the words of the Lord, rose up early, and builded an altar under the hill with twelve pillars according to the twelve tribes of Israel; young men offered burnt offerings and peace offerings of oxen.
- Moses took half of the blood in basons and half he sprinkled on the altar; then he took the book of the covenant and read in the audience of the people, and they said, All that the Lord hath said will we do, and be obedient. And he sprinkled the blood on the people: Behold the blood of the covenant, which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words. This sprinkling ties in later to the book of Hebrews and its teaching on sacrifice.
- Notice verse seven: Moses had a book of the covenant written and read it before he ever went up into the mount for the forty days. It was more than ten commandments; a whole book of what was to be done was already broken down before he went up.
- Because we swore the oath, all we can do now is pray the Lord have mercy on us and return to redeem us from those that hate us.

**[Exodus 24:9-11](/bible/exodus/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/24#v9)</sup> Then went up Moses, and Aaron, Nadab, and Abihu, and seventy of the elders of Israel:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/24#v10)</sup> And they saw the God of Israel: and there was under his feet as it were a paved work of a sapphire stone, and as it were the body of heaven in his clearness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/24#v11)</sup> And upon the nobles of the children of Israel he laid not his hand: also they saw God, and did eat and drink.

- Moses, Aaron, Nadab, Abihu, and the seventy elders went up, and they saw the God of Israel: under his feet as it were a paved work of a sapphire stone, and as it were the body of heaven in his clearness. Sapphire is blue, and the blue flame is the hottest part of a flame.
- Upon the nobles of the children of Israel he laid not his hand: also they saw God, and did eat and drink. Since no man hath seen God at any time, the one they saw and ate before was Christ, who is called the mighty God and the everlasting Father.
- Paul confirms it: all our fathers were under the cloud and passed through the sea, and they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them, and that Rock was Christ. The Corinthians Paul wrote were Israelites, for Greece was not even a power on the earth in Moses' day. It was Christ dealing with Israel in the wilderness on behalf of the Most High.

**[Exodus 24:12](/bible/exodus/24#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/24#v12)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Come up to me into the mount, and be there: and I will give thee tables of stone, and a law, and commandments which I have written; that thou mayest teach them.

- The Lord said, Come up to me into the mount, and I will give thee tables of stone, and a law, and commandments which I have written, that thou mayest teach them. This lets you know the law and the commandments are one and the same; Christianity tries to separate the law from the commandments, but the commandment is a lamp and the law is light, lamp and light, one and the same. These are the things you build your faith on, tearing down imaginations in the minds of our people with the word of God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

    The commandment is a lamp, and the law is light; commandments and law are one and the same.

**[Exodus 24:13-18](/bible/exodus/24#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/24#v13)</sup> And Moses rose up, and his minister Joshua: and Moses went up into the mount of God.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/24#v14)</sup> And he said unto the elders, Tarry ye here for us, until we come again unto you: and, behold, Aaron and Hur are with you: if any man have any matters to do, let him come unto them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/24#v15)</sup> And Moses went up into the mount, and a cloud covered the mount.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/24#v16)</sup> And the glory of the Lord abode upon mount Sinai, and the cloud covered it six days: and the seventh day he called unto Moses out of the midst of the cloud.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/24#v17)</sup> And the sight of the glory of the Lord was like devouring fire on the top of the mount in the eyes of the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/24#v18)</sup> And Moses went into the midst of the cloud, and gat him up into the mount: and Moses was in the mount forty days and forty nights.

- Moses rose up with his minister Joshua and went up into the mount of God, leaving Aaron and Hur with the elders for any matters. A cloud covered the mount, the glory of the Lord abode upon Sinai six days, and on the seventh day he called unto Moses out of the midst of the cloud.
- The sight of the glory of the Lord was like devouring fire on the top of the mount in the eyes of the children of Israel; and Moses was in the mount forty days and forty nights.
- If you have been actively listening: Moses already had the book of the covenant written before he went up. In the forty days and forty nights he came down with even more; it was never just the ten commandments.
