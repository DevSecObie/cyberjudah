## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 34](/bible/exodus/34) through 37: the second tables of stone and the covenant renewed, Moses' shining face, the sabbath and the willing hearted offering for the tabernacle, the wise hearted building the tabernacle, and Bezaleel making the ark, the mercy seat, and the holy furniture.

**[Exodus 34:1-4](/bible/exodus/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/34#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Hew thee two tables of stone like unto the first: and I will write upon these tables the words that were in the first tables, which thou brakest.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/34#v2)</sup> And be ready in the morning, and come up in the morning unto mount Sinai, and present thyself there to me in the top of the mount.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/34#v3)</sup> And no man shall come up with thee, neither let any man be seen throughout all the mount; neither let the flocks nor herds feed before that mount.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/34#v4)</sup> And he hewed two tables of stone like unto the first; and Moses rose up early in the morning, and went up unto mount Sinai, as the Lord had commanded him, and took in his hand the two tables of stone.

- The Most High commanded Moses to hew, that is carve out or chisel, two tables of stone like unto the first, because Moses brake the first tables; the words were written in stone, and the Lord would write upon these tables the words that were in the first.
- No man was to come up with Moses, neither was any man to be seen throughout all the mount, neither flocks nor herds feed before it; the Most High did not want man nor beast near mount Sinai.

**[Exodus 34:5-7](/bible/exodus/34#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/34#v5)</sup> And the Lord descended in the cloud, and stood with him there, and proclaimed the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/34#v6)</sup> And the Lord passed by before him, and proclaimed, The Lord, The Lord God, merciful and gracious, longsuffering, and abundant in goodness and truth,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/34#v7)</sup> Keeping mercy for thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin, and that will by no means clear the guilty; visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children, and upon the children’s children, unto the third and to the fourth generation.

- The Lord descended in the cloud and stood with him there. The clouds are His chariot, His vehicle, His way of traveling; the Most High, Christ, and the angels travel in these vehicles.
- He proclaimed the name of the Lord, as He had promised Moses in the mount. No man can see the face of the Most High and live, so the Lord set Moses upon a rock, put him in a clift of the rock, and covered him with His hand while His glory passed by; Moses saw only His back parts.
- Keeping mercy for thousands, forgiving iniquity and transgression and sin: this is spoken to Israel. Moses is speaking only to the children of Israel at this time; sin is the transgression of the law, and the word and the statutes were given to Israel only.
- And that will by no means clear the guilty: He will forgive you, but you will still be judged; He still visits the iniquity of the fathers upon the children and upon the children's children, unto the third and to the fourth generation.

**[Exodus 34:8-9](/bible/exodus/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/34#v8)</sup> And Moses made haste, and bowed his head toward the earth, and worshipped.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/34#v9)</sup> And he said, If now I have found grace in thy sight, O Lord, let my Lord, I pray thee, go among us; for it is a stiffnecked people; and pardon our iniquity and our sin, and take us for thine inheritance.

- Moses made haste, bowed his head toward the earth, and worshipped: let my Lord, I pray thee, go among us, for it is a stiffnecked people; and pardon our iniquity, meaning forgive our iniquity, and our sin, and take us for thine inheritance.

**[Exodus 34:10](/bible/exodus/34#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/34#v10)</sup> And he said, Behold, I make a covenant: before all thy people I will do marvels, such as have not been done in all the earth, nor in any nation: and all the people among which thou art shall see the work of the Lord: for it is a terrible thing that I will do with thee.

- The Most High made a covenant to do marvels such as have not been done in all the earth, nor in any nation, and all the people among which Israel dwells shall see the work of the Lord. One of those marvels is laid out in the Revelation.
- Israel lay as dead bodies in the street of the great city which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt: a decayed state, a spiritually dead people. The people and kindreds and tongues and nations saw them in that dead state through the years of captivity, when they were slaves, and their bodies were not put in graves because the death is spiritual, not physical; the two witnesses go to the northern and southern kingdoms.
- They that dwell upon the earth rejoiced over them, made merry, and sent gifts one to another, because these had tormented them. Then the Spirit of life from God entered into them: the people that were spiritually dead in captivity, that would not look their slave masters in the face, stood upon their feet, and great fear fell upon them which saw them; that same fear falls on the slave masters.

    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)</sup> And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.

    The dead bodies in the street of the great city spiritually called Sodom and Egypt are Israel spiritually dead in captivity; the Spirit of life enters them, they stand upon their feet, and great fear falls on them which see them. This is the marvel the Most High spoke of.

**[Exodus 34:11-16](/bible/exodus/34#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/34#v11)</sup> Observe thou that which I command thee this day: behold, I drive out before thee the Amorite, and the Canaanite, and the Hittite, and the Perizzite, and the Hivite, and the Jebusite.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/34#v12)</sup> Take heed to thyself, lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land whither thou goest, lest it be for a snare in the midst of thee:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/34#v13)</sup> But ye shall destroy their altars, break their images, and cut down their groves:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/34#v14)</sup> For thou shalt worship no other god: for the Lord, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/34#v15)</sup> Lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, and they go a whoring after their gods, and do sacrifice unto their gods, and one call thee, and thou eat of his sacrifice;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/34#v16)</sup> And thou take of their daughters unto thy sons, and their daughters go a whoring after their gods, and make thy sons go a whoring after their gods.

- Observe thou that which I command thee this day: behold, the Most High drives out before Israel the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Hittites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites.
- Take heed to thyself, lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, lest it be for a snare, a trap, in the midst of thee. Ye shall destroy their altars, break their images, and cut down their groves, just as the law states it again in Deuteronomy; for the Lord, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God.
- The snare is spelled out: they go a whoring after their gods, they call thee, thou eat of their sacrifice and partake in serving their gods; thou take of their daughters unto thy sons, and their daughters go a whoring after their gods and make thy sons go a whoring after their gods.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 7:5](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v5)</sup> But thus shall ye deal with them; ye shall destroy their altars, and break down their images, and cut down their groves, and burn their graven images with fire.

    Destroy their altars, break down their images, and cut down their groves; the command of this chapter is written again in the law.

**[Exodus 34:17](/bible/exodus/34#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/34#v17)</sup> Thou shalt make thee no molten gods.

- Thou shalt make thee no molten gods. It had to be reiterated here, because in the chapter before Israel made a molten calf; that shall not be done.

**[Exodus 34:18-20](/bible/exodus/34#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/34#v18)</sup> The feast of unleavened bread shalt thou keep. Seven days thou shalt eat unleavened bread, as I commanded thee, in the time of the month Abib: for in the month Abib thou camest out from Egypt.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/34#v19)</sup> All that openeth the matrix is mine; and every firstling among thy cattle, whether ox or sheep, that is male.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/34#v20)</sup> But the firstling of an ass thou shalt redeem with a lamb: and if thou redeem him not, then shalt thou break his neck. All the firstborn of thy sons thou shalt redeem. And none shall appear before me empty.

- The feast of unleavened bread shalt thou keep seven days, in the time of the month Abib, for in the month Abib thou camest out from Egypt; this goes to the Passover season.
- All that openeth the matrix is the Lord's, every firstling among thy cattle that is male, and the firstling of an ass redeemed with a lamb; all the firstborn of thy sons thou shalt redeem. This is symbolic of the Most High bringing Israel out of Egypt: He killed the firstborn of the Egyptians and kept His people alive.

**[Exodus 34:21](/bible/exodus/34#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/34#v21)</sup> Six days thou shalt work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest: in earing time and in harvest thou shalt rest.

- Six days thou shalt work, but on the seventh day thou shalt rest: in earing time and in harvest thou shalt rest. Even when it was the time of harvest and gathering, Israel still rested on the seventh day.

**[Exodus 34:22-24](/bible/exodus/34#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/34#v22)</sup> And thou shalt observe the feast of weeks, of the firstfruits of wheat harvest, and the feast of ingathering at the year’s end.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/34#v23)</sup> Thrice in the year shall all your men children appear before the Lord God, the God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/34#v24)</sup> For I will cast out the nations before thee, and enlarge thy borders: neither shall any man desire thy land, when thou shalt go up to appear before the Lord thy God thrice in the year.

- The feast of weeks goes to Pentecost, and the feast of ingathering at the year's end goes to the feast of tabernacles.
- Thrice in the year shall all your men children appear before the Lord GOD, the God of Israel. The law is written again in Deuteronomy: the feast of unleavened bread, which is the Passover season, the feast of weeks, which is Pentecost, and the feast of tabernacles; and they shall not appear before the Lord empty, for each had to bring his sacrifice.
- Neither shall any man desire thy land when thou shalt go up to appear before the Lord thrice in the year: when the men left their land and went up to keep the three high holy days, the Most High promised no man would desire their land.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 16:16](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v16)</sup> Three times in a year shall all thy males appear before the Lord thy God in the place which he shall choose; in the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tabernacles: and they shall not appear before the Lord empty:

    Three times in a year shall all thy males appear before the LORD in the place which he shall choose, at the three feasts, and they shall not appear before the LORD empty.

**[Exodus 34:25-26](/bible/exodus/34#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/34#v25)</sup> Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leaven; neither shall the sacrifice of the feast of the passover be left unto the morning.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/34#v26)</sup> The first of the firstfruits of thy land thou shalt bring unto the house of the Lord thy God. Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

- Thou shalt not offer the blood of my sacrifice with leaven; neither shall the sacrifice of the feast of the passover be left unto the morning: it had to be eaten up or burned.
- The first of the firstfruits of thy land thou shalt bring unto the house of the LORD thy God. And thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother's milk: a young one still in his mother's milk, as veal is taken for instance, is not ready to be eaten; it is unlawful.

**[Exodus 34:27-28](/bible/exodus/34#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/34#v27)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Write thou these words: for after the tenor of these words I have made a covenant with thee and with Israel.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/34#v28)</sup> And he was there with the Lord forty days and forty nights; he did neither eat bread, nor drink water. And he wrote upon the tables the words of the covenant, the ten commandments.

- Write thou these words: for after the tenor of these words I have made a covenant with thee and with Israel. The words are the laws the Lord had just given.
- Moses was there with the Lord forty days and forty nights; he did neither eat bread, nor drink water. And he wrote upon the tables the words of the covenant, the ten commandments; the words of the covenant take in the directions of the sacrifices, which animals were to be offered and how it was to be done, together with the commandments.

**[Exodus 34:29-35](/bible/exodus/34#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/34#v29)</sup> And it came to pass, when Moses came down from mount Sinai with the two tables of testimony in Moses’ hand, when he came down from the mount, that Moses wist not that the skin of his face shone while he talked with him.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/34#v30)</sup> And when Aaron and all the children of Israel saw Moses, behold, the skin of his face shone; and they were afraid to come nigh him.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/34#v31)</sup> And Moses called unto them; and Aaron and all the rulers of the congregation returned unto him: and Moses talked with them.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/34#v32)</sup> And afterward all the children of Israel came nigh: and he gave them in commandment all that the Lord had spoken with him in mount Sinai.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/34#v33)</sup> And till Moses had done speaking with them, he put a vail on his face.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/34#v34)</sup> But when Moses went in before the Lord to speak with him, he took the vail off, until he came out. And he came out, and spake unto the children of Israel that which he was commanded.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/34#v35)</sup> And the children of Israel saw the face of Moses, that the skin of Moses’ face shone: and Moses put the vail upon his face again, until he went in to speak with him.

- When Moses came down from mount Sinai with the two tables of testimony in his hand, the skin of his face shone while he talked with him, and Moses wist it not; and when Aaron and all the children of Israel saw it, they were afraid to come nigh him.
- A man's wisdom maketh his face to shine. This was no ordinary wisdom Moses was getting; he was getting the utmost wisdom. In the mount the Most High held him by Him a long season, told him many wondrous things, and shewed him the secrets of the times and the end; he saw the beginning of the creation, the middle, and the end of these times, and that is why his face shone.
- Some of that wisdom Moses was commanded to hide and not declare; there is wisdom the Most High gave him that he was not to publish, that we do not even have right now.
- Moses put a vail on his face until he had done speaking with the people; but when he went in before the Lord to speak with Him he took the vail off, to show respect to the Most High.
