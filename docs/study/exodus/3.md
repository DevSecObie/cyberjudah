**[Exodus 3:1-6](/bible/exodus/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/3#v1)</sup> Now Moses kept the flock of Jethro his father in law, the priest of Midian: and he led the flock to the backside of the desert, and came to the mountain of God, even to Horeb.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/3#v2)</sup> And the angel of the Lord appeared unto him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush: and he looked, and, behold, the bush burned with fire, and the bush was not consumed.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/3#v3)</sup> And Moses said, I will now turn aside, and see this great sight, why the bush is not burnt.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/3#v4)</sup> And when the Lord saw that he turned aside to see, God called unto him out of the midst of the bush, and said, Moses, Moses. And he said, Here am I.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/3#v5)</sup> And he said, Draw not nigh hither: put off thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/3#v6)</sup> Moreover he said, I am the God of thy father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob. And Moses hid his face; for he was afraid to look upon God.

- Moses kept the flock of Jethro his father in law and led it to the backside of the desert, to Horeb the mountain of God; and the angel of the LORD appeared unto him in a flame of fire out of the midst of a bush, and the bush was not consumed.
- The voice out of the bush said, Draw not nigh hither: put off thy shoes from off thy feet, for the place whereon thou standest is holy ground; I am the God of thy father, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob.
- The precepts identify that angel. Stephen recounts that the angel which appeared in the bush sent Moses to be a ruler and a deliverer; Joshua met the captain of the host of the LORD, who gave him the same command, Loose thy shoe from off thy foot; and the captain of their salvation, made perfect through sufferings, is Jesus Christ. Precept upon precept, the angel in the burning bush was Christ.
- The same angel went before Israel out of Egypt: Behold, I send an Angel before thee; beware of him, and obey his voice, provoke him not, for my name is in him.

>

- Moses said unto God, Who am I, that I should go unto Pharaoh? This shows the humility of the forefathers: none of them sought the position they were given. The Lord chose Moses because He saw the spirit that was in him.

> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/3#v19)</sup> And I am sure that the king of Egypt will not let you go, no, not by a mighty hand.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/3#v20)</sup> And I will stretch out my hand, and smite Egypt with all my wonders which I will do in the midst thereof: and after that he will let you go.

- I am sure that the king of Egypt will not let you go, no, not by a mighty hand; and I will stretch out my hand, and smite Egypt with all my wonders. The Lord hardened Pharaoh's heart so that the deliverance would come with wonders, destroying the idols of Egypt in the sight of Israel and of Egypt; the Most High is not one to be played with.
