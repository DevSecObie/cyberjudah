**[Exodus 8:5-8](/bible/exodus/8#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/8#v5)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch forth thine hand with thy rod over the streams, over the rivers, and over the ponds, and cause frogs to come up upon the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/8#v6)</sup> And Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters of Egypt; and the frogs came up, and covered the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/8#v7)</sup> And the magicians did so with their enchantments, and brought up frogs upon the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/8#v8)</sup> Then Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron, and said, Intreat the Lord, that he may take away the frogs from me, and from my people; and I will let the people go, that they may do sacrifice unto the Lord.

- Aaron stretched out his hand over the waters, and the frogs came up and covered the land of Egypt. With this second plague the Most High was mocking Heqet, the Egyptian idol god of the frogs.
- The magicians did so with their enchantments and brought up frogs, but just as with the water they could only add to the plague; they could not take the frogs away.
- So Pharaoh called for Moses and Aaron and said, Entreat the Lord that he may take away the frogs. Why did Pharaoh not call on Heqet, the god of frogs? He had to go to the Lord.

**[Exodus 8:16-19](/bible/exodus/8#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/8#v16)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch out thy rod, and smite the dust of the land, that it may become lice throughout all the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/8#v17)</sup> And they did so; for Aaron stretched out his hand with his rod, and smote the dust of the earth, and it became lice in man, and in beast; all the dust of the land became lice throughout all the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/8#v18)</sup> And the magicians did so with their enchantments to bring forth lice, but they could not: so there were lice upon man, and upon beast.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/8#v19)</sup> Then the magicians said unto Pharaoh, This is the finger of God: and Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he hearkened not unto them; as the Lord had said.

- All the dust of the land became lice in man and in beast throughout all the land of Egypt. Imagine that: the dust of the whole land of Egypt turned to lice.
- The magicians did so with their enchantments to bring forth lice, but this time they could not; and the magicians said unto Pharaoh, This is the finger of God. Yet Pharaoh's heart was hardened.
- With the third and fourth plagues, the lice and the swarms of flies, the Most High was mocking Beelzebub, the idol lord of flies and uncleanness.

**[Exodus 8:21-24](/bible/exodus/8#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/8#v21)</sup> Else, if thou wilt not let my people go, behold, I will send swarms of flies upon thee, and upon thy servants, and upon thy people, and into thy houses: and the houses of the Egyptians shall be full of swarms of flies, and also the ground whereon they are.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/8#v22)</sup> And I will sever in that day the land of Goshen, in which my people dwell, that no swarms of flies shall be there; to the end thou mayest know that I am the Lord in the midst of the earth.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/8#v23)</sup> And I will put a division between my people and thy people: to morrow shall this sign be.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/8#v24)</sup> And the Lord did so; and there came a grievous swarm of flies into the house of Pharaoh, and into his servants’ houses, and into all the land of Egypt: the land was corrupted by reason of the swarm of flies.

- These were no ordinary flies. They were biting and killing men and animals; the scripture records that the bitings of flies killed and no remedy was found.
- The Lord severed the land of Goshen, where His people dwelt, so that no swarms of flies were there, and put a division between His people and Pharaoh's people, to the end Pharaoh may know that the Lord is in the midst of the earth.
- Goshen was Israel's own quarter in Egypt, where we had our own houses and our own cattle; it was given to us in the days of Joseph, as we read in the book of Genesis.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 16:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v9)</sup> For them the bitings of grasshoppers and flies killed, neither was there found any remedy for their life: for they were worthy to be punished by such.

    The bitings of flies killed and no remedy was found; these were no ordinary flies.
  - **[Genesis 47:6](/bible/genesis/47#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/47#v6)</sup> The land of Egypt is before thee; in the best of the land make thy father and brethren to dwell; in the land of Goshen let them dwell: and if thou knowest any men of activity among them, then make them rulers over my cattle.

    Goshen, the best of the land, was given to Israel in Joseph's day; that is where the children of Israel dwelt apart from Egypt.

**[Exodus 8:25-32](/bible/exodus/8#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/8#v25)</sup> And Pharaoh called for Moses and for Aaron, and said, Go ye, sacrifice to your God in the land.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/8#v26)</sup> And Moses said, It is not meet so to do; for we shall sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians to the Lord our God: lo, shall we sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians before their eyes, and will they not stone us?
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/8#v27)</sup> We will go three days’ journey into the wilderness, and sacrifice to the Lord our God, as he shall command us.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/8#v28)</sup> And Pharaoh said, I will let you go, that ye may sacrifice to the Lord your God in the wilderness; only ye shall not go very far away: intreat for me.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/8#v29)</sup> And Moses said, Behold, I go out from thee, and I will intreat the Lord that the swarms of flies may depart from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people, to morrow: but let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more in not letting the people go to sacrifice to the Lord.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/8#v30)</sup> And Moses went out from Pharaoh, and intreated the Lord.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/8#v31)</sup> And the Lord did according to the word of Moses; and he removed the swarms of flies from Pharaoh, from his servants, and from his people; there remained not one.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/8#v32)</sup> And Pharaoh hardened his heart at this time also, neither would he let the people go.

- Pharaoh said, Go sacrifice to your God in the land; but Moses answered, It is not meet, for we should sacrifice the abomination of the Egyptians before their eyes, and will they not stone us? Israel's sacrifices were the very animals Egypt held sacred, so Israel must go three days' journey into the wilderness.
- Moses warned Pharaoh, Let not Pharaoh deal deceitfully any more. The Lord removed the swarms so that there remained not one; and Pharaoh hardened his heart at this time also, and would not let the people go.
