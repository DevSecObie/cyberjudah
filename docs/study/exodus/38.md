## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 38](/bible/exodus/38) through 40, with [Leviticus 1](/bible/leviticus/1) taken in the same session. These are the chapters men call the boring chapters, but skip past any page and you can miss a jewel; reading them teaches attention to detail. Here Moses follows through on the instructions of the earlier chapters: the tabernacle, the court, and the altar are built, the holy garments are made, and the tabernacle is reared up and filled with the glory of the Lord.

**[Exodus 38:1-8](/bible/exodus/38#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/38#v1)</sup> And he made the altar of burnt offering of shittim wood: five cubits was the length thereof, and five cubits the breadth thereof; it was foursquare; and three cubits the height thereof.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/38#v2)</sup> And he made the horns thereof on the four corners of it; the horns thereof were of the same: and he overlaid it with brass.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/38#v3)</sup> And he made all the vessels of the altar, the pots, and the shovels, and the basons, and the fleshhooks, and the firepans: all the vessels thereof made he of brass.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/38#v4)</sup> And he made for the altar a brasen grate of network under the compass thereof beneath unto the midst of it.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/38#v5)</sup> And he cast four rings for the four ends of the grate of brass, to be places for the staves.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/38#v6)</sup> And he made the staves of shittim wood, and overlaid them with brass.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/38#v7)</sup> And he put the staves into the rings on the sides of the altar, to bear it withal; he made the altar hollow with boards.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/38#v8)</sup> And he made the laver of brass, and the foot of it of brass, of the lookingglasses of the women assembling, which assembled at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

- This chapter is the application of the instructions already given for the tabernacle: the altar of burnt offering of shittim wood overlaid with brass, its vessels, and its staves were made exactly as commanded.
- The laver of brass was made of the looking glasses of the women assembling at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation; even the women's own possessions went into the work.

**[Exodus 38:21](/bible/exodus/38#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/38#v21)</sup> This is the sum of the tabernacle, even of the tabernacle of testimony, as it was counted, according to the commandment of Moses, for the service of the Levites, by the hand of Ithamar, son to Aaron the priest.

- This is the sum of the tabernacle: a collection was taken in order to fund the building of the tabernacle and the service of building it, counted according to the commandment of Moses by the hand of Ithamar son to Aaron the priest.

**[Exodus 38:24-26](/bible/exodus/38#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/38#v24)</sup> All the gold that was occupied for the work in all the work of the holy place, even the gold of the offering, was twenty and nine talents, and seven hundred and thirty shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/38#v25)</sup> And the silver of them that were numbered of the congregation was an hundred talents, and a thousand seven hundred and threescore and fifteen shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/38#v26)</sup> A bekah for every man, that is, half a shekel, after the shekel of the sanctuary, for every one that went to be numbered, from twenty years old and upward, for six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty men.

- All the gold, silver, and brass occupied for the work was counted. The silver came a bekah for every man, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary, from every one numbered from twenty years old and upward, six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty men. Thousands of men contributed to the cause of the sanctuary being built.
- Israel in the wilderness was delivered, not in captivity; today we are waking up in our captivity, and the buildings we get for sanctuaries require work, wood, service, and labor. Yet many do not have the spirit to give. If you have trouble coming up out of your pocket to help the cause of the Lord, examine yourself whether ye be in the faith, whether you really believe and really study.
- After Babylon invaded Jerusalem our fathers were captives, yet they still made a collection for the house of the Lord, not a required amount but according to every man's power. Likewise today the congregations do not push a ten percent; you give according to your power, and the Lord knows how much power you have to give. Sadly some give more than those who have more power to give than they.
- He which soweth sparingly shall reap sparingly, and he which soweth bountifully shall reap bountifully; that does not mean the Most High blesses you with a three story house, for you may be storing up treasures in heaven. Give as you purpose in your heart, not grudgingly or of necessity, for God loveth a cheerful giver; giving with an attitude is worse than keeping the money.

**[Exodus 38:29-31](/bible/exodus/38#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/38#v29)</sup> And the brass of the offering was seventy talents, and two thousand and four hundred shekels.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/38#v30)</sup> And therewith he made the sockets to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and the brasen altar, and the brasen grate for it, and all the vessels of the altar,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/38#v31)</sup> And the sockets of the court round about, and the sockets of the court gate, and all the pins of the tabernacle, and all the pins of the court round about.

- The brass of the offering, seventy talents and two thousand and four hundred shekels, made the sockets of the door, the brasen altar and its grate and vessels, the sockets of the court, and all the pins of the tabernacle and the court. Every material was accounted for; not everybody had the spirit to contribute physically, but everybody put their brick in and gave something for the cause.
