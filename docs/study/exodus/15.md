**[Exodus 15:1-3](/bible/exodus/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/15#v1)</sup> Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song unto the Lord, and spake, saying, I will sing unto the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously: the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/15#v2)</sup> The Lord is my strength and song, and he is become my salvation: he is my God, and I will prepare him an habitation; my father’s God, and I will exalt him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/15#v3)</sup> The Lord is a man of war: the Lord is his name.

- Then sang Moses and the children of Israel this song unto the Lord. The whole chapter is a song and a prophecy, speaking not only of the coming out of Egypt but in the future tense as well.
- The Lord is a man of war: the Lord is His name. He loves war against His enemies; our carnal minds cannot fathom it, but the word says what it says. He mustereth the host of the battle: He brought the Babylonians, the Persians and Medes, the Greeks, and the Romans against Israel, and He has stood in the midst of the wars of the nations ever since.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 13:4](/bible/isaiah/13#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/13#v4)</sup> The noise of a multitude in the mountains, like as of a great people; a tumultuous noise of the kingdoms of nations gathered together: the Lord of hosts mustereth the host of the battle.

    The Lord of hosts mustereth the host of the battle; He is a man of war.

**[Exodus 15:14-16](/bible/exodus/15#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/15#v14)</sup> The people shall hear, and be afraid: sorrow shall take hold on the inhabitants of Palestina.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/15#v15)</sup> Then the dukes of Edom shall be amazed; the mighty men of Moab, trembling shall take hold upon them; all the inhabitants of Canaan shall melt away.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/15#v16)</sup> Fear and dread shall fall upon them; by the greatness of thine arm they shall be as still as a stone; till thy people pass over, O Lord, till the people pass over, which thou hast purchased.

- The people shall hear and be afraid; the dukes of Edom shall be amazed, trembling shall take hold of the mighty men of Moab, and the inhabitants of Canaan shall melt away. This goes into the future, when the nations would hear what the Lord had done for Israel.
- Fear and dread fall upon them by the greatness of His arm till the people pass over which the Lord purchased.

**[Exodus 15:20-21](/bible/exodus/15#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/15#v20)</sup> And Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took a timbrel in her hand; and all the women went out after her with timbrels and with dances.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/15#v21)</sup> And Miriam answered them, Sing ye to the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously; the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.

- Miriam the prophetess, the sister of Aaron, took a timbrel, and all the women went out after her with timbrels and with dances, answering the song: sing ye to the Lord, for he hath triumphed gloriously.
- The prophetesses guided the women, teaching the young women to be sober and to love their husbands, and Miriam had her part in bringing Israel out of Egypt by keeping the sisters in order; that is the charge on the older sisters today toward the younger.

**[Exodus 15:23-26](/bible/exodus/15#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/15#v23)</sup> And when they came to Marah, they could not drink of the waters of Marah, for they were bitter: therefore the name of it was called Marah.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/15#v24)</sup> And the people murmured against Moses, saying, What shall we drink?
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/15#v25)</sup> And he cried unto the Lord; and the Lord shewed him a tree, which when he had cast into the waters, the waters were made sweet: there he made for them a statute and an ordinance, and there he proved them,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/15#v26)</sup> And said, If thou wilt diligently hearken to the voice of the Lord thy God, and wilt do that which is right in his sight, and wilt give ear to his commandments, and keep all his statutes, I will put none of these diseases upon thee, which I have brought upon the Egyptians: for I am the Lord that healeth thee.

- At Marah the waters were bitter, and the people murmured; the Lord shewed Moses a tree, which when he had cast into the waters, the waters were made sweet.
- There He made a statute and an ordinance, and there He proved them: hearken diligently to the voice of the Lord thy God, do that which is right in His sight, and keep all His statutes, and none of the diseases of Egypt will be put on thee: for I am the Lord that healeth thee.
