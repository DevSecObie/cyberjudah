## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 6](/bible/exodus/6) through 9: the Most High renews His promise to bring Israel out of Egypt, the genealogy of Reuben, Simeon, and Levi of whom came Moses and Aaron, Aaron's rod turned serpent, and the first seven plagues upon Egypt. The thread of the session is that through each plague the Most High was mocking an idol god of Egypt: Sobek the god of the rivers, Heqet the god of the frogs, Beelzebub the lord of flies and uncleanness, Hathor the goddess of cattle, Sekhmet the goddess of health and beauty, and Nut and Geb the gods of the sky and the earth.

**[Exodus 6:1](/bible/exodus/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/6#v1)</sup> Then the Lord said unto Moses, Now shalt thou see what I will do to Pharaoh: for with a strong hand shall he let them go, and with a strong hand shall he drive them out of his land.

- The Lord renews His promise: Pharaoh will not merely let Israel go, but with a strong hand he shall drive them out of his land.

**[Exodus 6:2-3](/bible/exodus/6#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/6#v2)</sup> And God spake unto Moses, and said unto him, I am the Lord:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/6#v3)</sup> And I appeared unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, by the name of God Almighty, but by my name Jehovah was I not known to them.

- The Most High appeared unto Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob by the name of God Almighty, but by His name JEHOVAH was He not known to them. Some say Jehovah and some say Yahawah; you can say whichever name you want to say.

**[Exodus 6:4-5](/bible/exodus/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/6#v4)</sup> And I have also established my covenant with them, to give them the land of Canaan, the land of their pilgrimage, wherein they were strangers.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/6#v5)</sup> And I have also heard the groaning of the children of Israel, whom the Egyptians keep in bondage; and I have remembered my covenant.

- The covenant stands to give Israel the land of Canaan, the land of their pilgrimage wherein they were strangers; and the Most High heard the groaning of the children of Israel whom the Egyptians kept in bondage.
- The land of Canaan is in the land of Ham. So when people say we are Africans, the answer is that we come from Northeast Africa, from that continent, but we are not Africans; our land sits on the continent, and we are the Israelites.

**[Exodus 6:9-12](/bible/exodus/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/6#v9)</sup> And Moses spake so unto the children of Israel: but they hearkened not unto Moses for anguish of spirit, and for cruel bondage.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/6#v10)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/6#v11)</sup> Go in, speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, that he let the children of Israel go out of his land.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/6#v12)</sup> And Moses spake before the Lord, saying, Behold, the children of Israel have not hearkened unto me; how then shall Pharaoh hear me, who am of uncircumcised lips?

- The children of Israel hearkened not unto Moses for anguish of spirit and for cruel bondage.
- Moses answered, Behold, the children of Israel have not hearkened unto me; how then shall Pharaoh hear me, who am of uncircumcised lips? This is why Aaron was given him for a mouthpiece in the next chapter.

**[Exodus 6:16-20](/bible/exodus/6#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/6#v16)</sup> And these are the names of the sons of Levi according to their generations; Gershon, and Kohath, and Merari: and the years of the life of Levi were an hundred thirty and seven years.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/6#v17)</sup> The sons of Gershon; Libni, and Shimi, according to their families.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/6#v18)</sup> And the sons of Kohath; Amram, and Izhar, and Hebron, and Uzziel: and the years of the life of Kohath were an hundred thirty and three years.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/6#v19)</sup> And the sons of Merari; Mahali and Mushi: these are the families of Levi according to their generations.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/6#v20)</sup> And Amram took him Jochebed his father’s sister to wife; and she bare him Aaron and Moses: and the years of the life of Amram were an hundred and thirty and seven years.

- Chapter 6 is dealing with the genealogy: the families of Reuben, Simeon, and Levi, and out of Levi came Moses and Aaron. Levi begat Kohath, Kohath begat Amram, and Amram took Jochebed to wife, and she bare him Aaron and Moses.

**[Exodus 6:26-27](/bible/exodus/6#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/6#v26)</sup> These are that Aaron and Moses, to whom the Lord said, Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt according to their armies.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/6#v27)</sup> These are they which spake to Pharaoh king of Egypt, to bring out the children of Israel from Egypt: these are that Moses and Aaron.

- These are that Aaron and Moses to whom the Lord said, Bring out the children of Israel from the land of Egypt; these are they which spake to Pharaoh king of Egypt.
