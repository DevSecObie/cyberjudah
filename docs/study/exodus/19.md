**[Exodus 19:1-2](/bible/exodus/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/19#v1)</sup> In the third month, when the children of Israel were gone forth out of the land of Egypt, the same day came they into the wilderness of Sinai.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/19#v2)</sup> For they were departed from Rephidim, and were come to the desert of Sinai, and had pitched in the wilderness; and there Israel camped before the mount.

- In the third month after going forth out of Egypt, Israel departed from Rephidim, came into the wilderness of Sinai, and camped before the mount. The journeys are recorded in Numbers 33: Israel departed from Rameses on the fifteenth day of the first month, on the morrow after the Passover, and later pitched in the wilderness of Sinai.
- Israel went forth out of Egypt with their armies. The tribes are armies, for the Lord is a man of war; Israel has always been at war and is at war today, a spiritual war.
- Moses wrote their goings out by the commandment of the Lord. The Lord gave the word, and great was the company of those that published it; so the claim that another people wrote the scriptures falls, for Moses wrote what God commanded him to write.

**[Exodus 19:5-8](/bible/exodus/19#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/19#v5)</sup> Now therefore, if ye will obey my voice indeed, and keep my covenant, then ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people: for all the earth is mine:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/19#v6)</sup> And ye shall be unto me a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation. These are the words which thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/19#v7)</sup> And Moses came and called for the elders of the people, and laid before their faces all these words which the Lord commanded him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/19#v8)</sup> And all the people answered together, and said, All that the Lord hath spoken we will do. And Moses returned the words of the people unto the Lord.

- If ye will obey my voice indeed, and keep my covenant, then ye shall be a peculiar treasure unto me above all people, a kingdom of priests, and an holy nation.
- Moses laid all these words before the elders, and all the people answered together, all that the Lord hath spoken we will do. Right there Israel made a covenant, an agreement, with the Most High.

**[Exodus 19:12-13](/bible/exodus/19#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/19#v12)</sup> And thou shalt set bounds unto the people round about, saying, Take heed to yourselves, that ye go not up into the mount, or touch the border of it: whosoever toucheth the mount shall be surely put to death:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/19#v13)</sup> There shall not an hand touch it, but he shall surely be stoned, or shot through; whether it be beast or man, it shall not live: when the trumpet soundeth long, they shall come up to the mount.

- Bounds were set round about the mount: whosoever touched it, whether beast or man, was to be stoned or shot through, and no hand was to touch it.
- The Most High is serious about that charge. When the oxen shook the cart, Uzzah put forth his hand to the ark of God and took hold of it, and the anger of the Lord was kindled and he smote him there, for the stranger that came nigh the priests' office was to be put to death. Every family of the priesthood had its own responsibility in the tabernacle service, and none could transgress against another's charge; what the world later called unions came from that order.
- When the trumpet sounded long they were to come up to the mount. The trumpets were made for the calling of the assembly and for the journeys of the camps, and trumpets still gather the congregation at the new moons and at the start of classes.
- That trumpet in the mount was not blown by Moses, for Moses was down among the people sanctifying them; the trumpet came from heaven.
