**[Exodus 29:1-4](/bible/exodus/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/29#v1)</sup> And this is the thing that thou shalt do unto them to hallow them, to minister unto me in the priest’s office: Take one young bullock, and two rams without blemish,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/29#v2)</sup> And unleavened bread, and cakes unleavened tempered with oil, and wafers unleavened anointed with oil: of wheaten flour shalt thou make them.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/29#v3)</sup> And thou shalt put them into one basket, and bring them in the basket, with the bullock and the two rams.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/29#v4)</sup> And Aaron and his sons thou shalt bring unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and shalt wash them with water.

- To hallow Aaron and his sons to minister in the priest's office: one young bullock and two rams without blemish, with unleavened bread, cakes tempered with oil, and wafers anointed with oil, brought in one basket to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
- And shalt wash them with water: cleanliness and spotlessness were very important to the Most High in everything dealing with the temple, and He is real specific and detailed about how clean and sanctified a man must be before he enters the holy place. This thread runs all through the first five books, Leviticus especially.

**[Exodus 29:5-9](/bible/exodus/29#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/29#v5)</sup> And thou shalt take the garments, and put upon Aaron the coat, and the robe of the ephod, and the ephod, and the breastplate, and gird him with the curious girdle of the ephod:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/29#v6)</sup> And thou shalt put the mitre upon his head, and put the holy crown upon the mitre.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/29#v7)</sup> Then shalt thou take the anointing oil, and pour it upon his head, and anoint him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/29#v8)</sup> And thou shalt bring his sons, and put coats upon them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/29#v9)</sup> And thou shalt gird them with girdles, Aaron and his sons, and put the bonnets on them: and the priest’s office shall be theirs for a perpetual statute: and thou shalt consecrate Aaron and his sons.

- The garments are put upon Aaron, the coat, the robe of the ephod, the ephod, and the breastplate, with the curious girdle; the mitre upon his head and the holy crown upon the mitre; the anointing oil poured upon his head; and his sons in coats, girdles, and bonnets. The priest's office shall be theirs for a perpetual statute: thou shalt consecrate Aaron and his sons.
- Ecclesiasticus records the same consecration: Moses anointed him with holy oil, an everlasting covenant to Aaron and to his seed so long as the heavens should remain, to minister unto Him, execute the office of the priesthood, offer the sacrifices, and teach Jacob the testimonies and inform Israel in His laws. The priest's job was the offerings and teaching the nation the law, statutes, and commandments.
- What happened to that covenant is answered in Malachi, where the commandment is spoken to the priests: the covenant with Levi was of life and peace, the priest's lips should keep knowledge and they should seek the law at his mouth, for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts; but ye are departed out of the way, ye have caused many to stumble at the law, ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi. The priests were finessing the offerings, keeping the best animals for themselves and sacrificing the blind and the broken, and they perverted the teachings of the law for their own benefit.
- That is why leadership today is not walking around requiring Levite lineage of Aaron. Christ is the high priest now, and the nation is a whole nation of kings and priests; Malachi and Hebrews are two good books to study on the changing of the priesthood, against the doctrine that the Aaronic covenant could never change.

**[Exodus 29:10-14](/bible/exodus/29#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/29#v10)</sup> And thou shalt cause a bullock to be brought before the tabernacle of the congregation: and Aaron and his sons shall put their hands upon the head of the bullock.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/29#v11)</sup> And thou shalt kill the bullock before the Lord, by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/29#v12)</sup> And thou shalt take of the blood of the bullock, and put it upon the horns of the altar with thy finger, and pour all the blood beside the bottom of the altar.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/29#v13)</sup> And thou shalt take all the fat that covereth the inwards, and the caul that is above the liver, and the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, and burn them upon the altar.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/29#v14)</sup> But the flesh of the bullock, and his skin, and his dung, shalt thou burn with fire without the camp: it is a sin offering.

- The bullock is killed before the Lord by the door of the tabernacle, its blood put upon the horns of the altar with the finger, the fat burnt upon the altar, and the flesh, skin, and dung burnt with fire without the camp: it is a sin offering. This is the introduction to the categories of offerings; there is the sin offering.

**[Exodus 29:15-18](/bible/exodus/29#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/29#v15)</sup> Thou shalt also take one ram; and Aaron and his sons shall put their hands upon the head of the ram.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/29#v16)</sup> And thou shalt slay the ram, and thou shalt take his blood, and sprinkle it round about upon the altar.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/29#v17)</sup> And thou shalt cut the ram in pieces, and wash the inwards of him, and his legs, and put them unto his pieces, and unto his head.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/29#v18)</sup> And thou shalt burn the whole ram upon the altar: it is a burnt offering unto the Lord: it is a sweet savour, an offering made by fire unto the Lord.

- The first ram is slain, its blood sprinkled round about upon the altar, and the whole ram burnt upon the altar: it is a burnt offering unto the Lord, a sweet savour, an offering made by fire. There is the second category, the burnt offering.

**[Exodus 29:19-28](/bible/exodus/29#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/29#v19)</sup> And thou shalt take the other ram; and Aaron and his sons shall put their hands upon the head of the ram.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/29#v20)</sup> Then shalt thou kill the ram, and take of his blood, and put it upon the tip of the right ear of Aaron, and upon the tip of the right ear of his sons, and upon the thumb of their right hand, and upon the great toe of their right foot, and sprinkle the blood upon the altar round about.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/29#v21)</sup> And thou shalt take of the blood that is upon the altar, and of the anointing oil, and sprinkle it upon Aaron, and upon his garments, and upon his sons, and upon the garments of his sons with him: and he shall be hallowed, and his garments, and his sons, and his sons’ garments with him.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/29#v22)</sup> Also thou shalt take of the ram the fat and the rump, and the fat that covereth the inwards, and the caul above the liver, and the two kidneys, and the fat that is upon them, and the right shoulder; for it is a ram of consecration:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/29#v23)</sup> And one loaf of bread, and one cake of oiled bread, and one wafer out of the basket of the unleavened bread that is before the Lord:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/29#v24)</sup> And thou shalt put all in the hands of Aaron, and in the hands of his sons; and shalt wave them for a wave offering before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/29#v25)</sup> And thou shalt receive them of their hands, and burn them upon the altar for a burnt offering, for a sweet savour before the Lord: it is an offering made by fire unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/29#v26)</sup> And thou shalt take the breast of the ram of Aaron’s consecration, and wave it for a wave offering before the Lord: and it shall be thy part.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/29#v27)</sup> And thou shalt sanctify the breast of the wave offering, and the shoulder of the heave offering, which is waved, and which is heaved up, of the ram of the consecration, even of that which is for Aaron, and of that which is for his sons:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/29#v28)</sup> And it shall be Aaron’s and his sons’ by a statute for ever from the children of Israel: for it is an heave offering: and it shall be an heave offering from the children of Israel of the sacrifice of their peace offerings, even their heave offering unto the Lord.

- The second ram is the ram of consecration: its blood is put upon the tip of the right ear of Aaron and of his sons, upon the thumb of their right hand and the great toe of their right foot, and sprinkled upon the altar round about; the blood and the anointing oil are sprinkled upon Aaron, his garments, his sons, and their garments, and he shall be hallowed.
- The bread and the parts of the ram are put in the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved for a wave offering before the Lord, then burnt upon the altar; and the breast of the wave offering and the shoulder of the heave offering are sanctified for Aaron and his sons by a statute for ever. There go the wave offering and the heave offering, which you will read about throughout the first five books, Leviticus predominantly.
- These ordinances belonged to the Levitical priesthood, and the doctrines that would put men back under pieces of that office today do not even keep what they claim; they sound right because they use scriptures strongly and confidently, but they are in error. Be not tossed to and fro with every wind of doctrine, nor carried about with strange doctrines; do not ever fall away from the Messiah.

    Be no more children tossed to and fro and carried about with every wind of doctrine, by the sleight of men.

**[Exodus 29:29-34](/bible/exodus/29#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/29#v29)</sup> And the holy garments of Aaron shall be his sons’ after him, to be anointed therein, and to be consecrated in them.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/29#v30)</sup> And that son that is priest in his stead shall put them on seven days, when he cometh into the tabernacle of the congregation to minister in the holy place.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/29#v31)</sup> And thou shalt take the ram of the consecration, and seethe his flesh in the holy place.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/29#v32)</sup> And Aaron and his sons shall eat the flesh of the ram, and the bread that is in the basket, by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/29#v33)</sup> And they shall eat those things wherewith the atonement was made, to consecrate and to sanctify them: but a stranger shall not eat thereof, because they are holy.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/29#v34)</sup> And if ought of the flesh of the consecrations, or of the bread, remain unto the morning, then thou shalt burn the remainder with fire: it shall not be eaten, because it is holy.

- The holy garments of Aaron pass to his sons after him, to be anointed and consecrated in them; the son that is priest in his stead shall put them on seven days when he cometh into the tabernacle to minister.
- Aaron and his sons eat the flesh of the ram and the bread at the door of the tabernacle, the things wherewith the atonement was made; a stranger shall not eat thereof, because they are holy. And whatever of the flesh or the bread remains unto the morning is burnt with fire and not eaten: no eating the leftovers of the offering, because it is holy.

**[Exodus 29:35-42](/bible/exodus/29#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/29#v35)</sup> And thus shalt thou do unto Aaron, and to his sons, according to all things which I have commanded thee: seven days shalt thou consecrate them.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/29#v36)</sup> And thou shalt offer every day a bullock for a sin offering for atonement: and thou shalt cleanse the altar, when thou hast made an atonement for it, and thou shalt anoint it, to sanctify it.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/exodus/29#v37)</sup> Seven days thou shalt make an atonement for the altar, and sanctify it; and it shall be an altar most holy: whatsoever toucheth the altar shall be holy.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/29#v38)</sup> Now this is that which thou shalt offer upon the altar; two lambs of the first year day by day continually.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/exodus/29#v39)</sup> The one lamb thou shalt offer in the morning; and the other lamb thou shalt offer at even:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/exodus/29#v40)</sup> And with the one lamb a tenth deal of flour mingled with the fourth part of an hin of beaten oil; and the fourth part of an hin of wine for a drink offering.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/exodus/29#v41)</sup> And the other lamb thou shalt offer at even, and shalt do thereto according to the meat offering of the morning, and according to the drink offering thereof, for a sweet savour, an offering made by fire unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/exodus/29#v42)</sup> This shall be a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation before the Lord: where I will meet you, to speak there unto thee.

- Seven days shalt thou consecrate them, offering every day a bullock for a sin offering for atonement, cleansing the altar and anointing it to sanctify it, that it be an altar most holy.
- Then the continual offering: two lambs of the first year day by day continually, one lamb in the morning and the other at even, with the tenth deal of flour mingled with beaten oil and the drink offering of wine; a continual burnt offering throughout your generations at the door of the tabernacle, where the Lord meets and speaks with Israel.

**[Exodus 29:43-46](/bible/exodus/29#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/exodus/29#v43)</sup> And there I will meet with the children of Israel, and the tabernacle shall be sanctified by my glory.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/exodus/29#v44)</sup> And I will sanctify the tabernacle of the congregation, and the altar: I will sanctify also both Aaron and his sons, to minister to me in the priest’s office.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/exodus/29#v45)</sup> And I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/exodus/29#v46)</sup> And they shall know that I am the Lord their God, that brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, that I may dwell among them: I am the Lord their God.

- The tabernacle shall be sanctified by His glory, and Aaron and his sons sanctified to minister in the priest's office; for the Most High to enter and deal with Israel, everything had to be sanctified. That was serious.
- I will dwell among the children of Israel, and will be their God. He was in no other nation's tabernacle, temple, or synagogue, and though there is no tabernacle today it is still the same case: He is in the midst of Israel and none else, which excludes everybody else from the benefits of having the Most High as their God. There is nothing to be ashamed of in the fact that He chose Israel only, and nothing changed across testaments: blessed be the Lord God of Israel, for he hath visited and redeemed his people, and that is the purpose of Christ, to redeem His people.
- The other nations knew it. When the adversaries of Judah and Benjamin heard the temple was being rebuilt they came saying, Let us build with you, for we seek your God; and Zerubbabel, Jeshua, and the chief of the fathers answered, Ye have nothing to do with us to build an house unto our God, but we ourselves together will build unto the Lord God of Israel. That is the mindset for these last days: Israel does not need the other nations' assistance, but rebuilds one another spiritually as a nation while awaiting Christ's return to restore the kingdom.
- He brought them forth out of the land of Egypt that He may dwell among them: I am the Lord their God. Israel should walk confident, special, and chosen above all nations of people, for the Most High is faithful and belongs to no one else; repent from spiritual fornication and return to the Most High.
