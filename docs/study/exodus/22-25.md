## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 22](/bible/exodus/22) through 25: the civil laws of restitution, the laws of righteous judgment and the three feasts in the year, the blood of the covenant sworn at Sinai, and the pattern of the tabernacle with the ark, the table, and the candlestick. The session opens on [Matthew 5:17](/bible/matthew/5#v17), that Christ came not to destroy the law but to fulfil, so the mind is right going into the reading: these laws still apply, though some are in a different form or in suspension because of the captivity and because of what Christ took away.

**[Exodus 22:1](/bible/exodus/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/22#v1)</sup> If a man shall steal an ox, or a sheep, and kill it, or sell it; he shall restore five oxen for an ox, and four sheep for a sheep.

- If a man steal an ox or a sheep and kill it or sell it, he restores five oxen for an ox and four sheep for a sheep. We are in a different situation where we do not keep oxen and sheep, but the principle of restitution still applies among brothers and sisters in the body: if something is stolen, the law written here governs the payback. Do not be stealing.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:17-18](/bible/matthew/5#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)</sup> Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/5#v18)</sup> For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.

    Christ came not to destroy the law or the prophets but to fulfil; not one jot or tittle passes from the law till all be fulfilled.

**[Exodus 22:2-4](/bible/exodus/22#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/22#v2)</sup> If a thief be found breaking up, and be smitten that he die, there shall no blood be shed for him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/22#v3)</sup> If the sun be risen upon him, there shall be blood shed for him; for he should make full restitution; if he have nothing, then he shall be sold for his theft.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/22#v4)</sup> If the theft be certainly found in his hand alive, whether it be ox, or ass, or sheep; he shall restore double.

- If a thief be found breaking up means breaking in. He entered your home and you defended yourself and he died: that is what is called self-defense today, and no blood is shed for him, meaning there is no judgment. The judgment was already passed in the breaking in.
- If the sun be risen upon him, the thief got away, and there shall be blood shed for him means there shall be a judgment; then the verse gives the judgment. He makes full restitution, and if he have nothing he is sold for his theft: not sold into slavery, but into work servitude until he pays off the debt of what he stole.
- If the theft be found in his hand alive, he restores double. Verse three covers the thief who already got rid of what he stole; verse four covers the one still holding it.

**[Exodus 22:5-6](/bible/exodus/22#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/22#v5)</sup> If a man shall cause a field or vineyard to be eaten, and shall put in his beast, and shall feed in another man’s field; of the best of his own field, and of the best of his own vineyard, shall he make restitution.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/22#v6)</sup> If fire break out, and catch in thorns, so that the stacks of corn, or the standing corn, or the field, be consumed therewith; he that kindled the fire shall surely make restitution.

- These are self-explanatory: if a man lets his beast feed in another man's field or vineyard, he makes restitution of the best of his own field and vineyard; and he that kindles a fire that consumes another man's corn surely makes restitution.

**[Exodus 22:7-9](/bible/exodus/22#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/22#v7)</sup> If a man shall deliver unto his neighbour money or stuff to keep, and it be stolen out of the man’s house; if the thief be found, let him pay double.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/22#v8)</sup> If the thief be not found, then the master of the house shall be brought unto the judges, to see whether he have put his hand unto his neighbour’s goods.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/22#v9)</sup> For all manner of trespass, whether it be for ox, for ass, for sheep, for raiment, or for any manner of lost thing, which another challengeth to be his, the cause of both parties shall come before the judges; and whom the judges shall condemn, he shall pay double unto his neighbour.

- If a man deliver money or stuff to his neighbor to keep and it be stolen, the thief found pays double. If the thief be not found, the master of the house is brought unto the judges, to see whether he put his hand unto his neighbor's goods, whether he was part of a scheme to deceive the owner out of his goods.
- For all manner of trespass, whether for ox, ass, sheep, raiment, or any lost thing, both parties come before the judges, and whom the judges condemn pays double unto his neighbor.

**[Exodus 22:10-11](/bible/exodus/22#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/22#v10)</sup> If a man deliver unto his neighbour an ass, or an ox, or a sheep, or any beast, to keep; and it die, or be hurt, or driven away, no man seeing it:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/22#v11)</sup> Then shall an oath of the Lord be between them both, that he hath not put his hand unto his neighbour’s goods; and the owner of it shall accept thereof, and he shall not make it good.

- If a beast delivered to a neighbor to keep die or be hurt or driven away, no man seeing it, an oath of the Lord is between them both. In layman's terms he swears: I did not take the stuff, I did not set you up, I do not know where it is. The owner accepts the oath and the matter ends there; it is not to be spoken of again.
- Paul uses this civil law to show how the Most High is unchangeable. An oath for confirmation is the end of all strife; God confirmed his counsel by an oath, and by two immutable things, that it is impossible for God to lie and that he will not take back what went out of his mouth, we have strong consolation. The same way, once the oath of this law is applied, the matter is over.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 6:16-18](/bible/hebrews/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/hebrews/6#v16)</sup> For men verily swear by the greater: and an oath for confirmation is to them an end of all strife.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/6#v17)</sup> Wherein God, willing more abundantly to shew unto the heirs of promise the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath:
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/6#v18)</sup> That by two immutable things, in which it was impossible for God to lie, we might have a strong consolation, who have fled for refuge to lay hold upon the hope set before us:

    An oath for confirmation is to men an end of all strife; God, willing to shew unto the heirs of promise the immutability of his counsel, confirmed it by an oath.

**[Exodus 22:12-15](/bible/exodus/22#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/22#v12)</sup> And if it be stolen from him, he shall make restitution unto the owner thereof.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/22#v13)</sup> If it be torn in pieces, then let him bring it for witness, and he shall not make good that which was torn.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/22#v14)</sup> And if a man borrow ought of his neighbour, and it be hurt, or die, the owner thereof being not with it, he shall surely make it good.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/22#v15)</sup> But if the owner thereof be with it, he shall not make it good: if it be an hired thing, it came for his hire.

- If the thing kept be stolen from him, he makes restitution; if it be torn in pieces, he brings it for witness and does not make it good.
- If a man borrow ought of his neighbor, ought meaning anything, and it be hurt or die with the owner not present, he surely makes it good. You borrowed it, it got messed up, you owe the man.
- But if the owner be with it, or if it be an hired thing, it came for its hire. If you came to plow a man's field for ten shekels with your own donkey and your donkey breaks his leg, you cannot expect the owner to pay for your own faulty equipment; the same as a business loan today, the property owner is not liable when your own equipment breaks down.

**[Exodus 22:16-17](/bible/exodus/22#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/22#v16)</sup> And if a man entice a maid that is not betrothed, and lie with her, he shall surely endow her to be his wife.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/22#v17)</sup> If her father utterly refuse to give her unto him, he shall pay money according to the dowry of virgins.

- If a man entice a maid that is not betrothed and lie with her, he shall surely endow her to be his wife; he has to sign the papers. He is at fault for not going through the proper channels of marriage, and she is at fault for being enticed and laying down.
- The proper channel was that the father married off the daughter. To marry thy daughter is to perform a weighty matter, for the father gives her over to that man for the rest of her life and is out of all watching over her; so she is to be given to a man of understanding, one keeping the commandments as a wise man. In our own land the daughter went from her father's house to a husband's house with the blessing of the father.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 7:25](/bible/sirach/7#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/7#v25)</sup> Marry thy daughter, and so shalt thou have performed a weighty matter: but give her to a man of understanding.

    Marrying off the daughter was the father's weighty matter; she was given to a man of understanding.

- If her father utterly refuse to give her unto him, he does not agree with the backdoor marriage, the man still pays money according to the dowry of virgins. [Genesis 34](/bible/genesis/34) gives the example with Dinah: Shechem and his father Hamor begged Jacob for her and offered never so much dowry, for Shechem understood the union was not supposed to take place, the Hebrews with the Hamites.
- The dowry of virgins was fifty shekels of silver, paid by the man who laid hold on a damsel not betrothed, and she became his wife whom he could not put away all his days. And if a husband afterward brought up an evil name upon a virgin of Israel, saying he found her not a maid, the tokens of her virginity, the cloth from the bedchamber her father kept, were spread before the elders; the man was chastised and amerced in an hundred shekels of silver for the false report.

**[Exodus 22:18-20](/bible/exodus/22#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/22#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not suffer a witch to live.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/22#v19)</sup> Whosoever lieth with a beast shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/22#v20)</sup> He that sacrificeth unto any god, save unto the Lord only, he shall be utterly destroyed.

- Thou shalt not suffer a witch to live: witchcraft goes into horoscopes, palm readings, and all things of that nature.
- Whosoever lieth with a beast, bestiality, shall surely be put to death.
- He that sacrificeth unto any god save unto the Lord only shall be utterly destroyed.

**[Exodus 22:21](/bible/exodus/22#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/22#v21)</sup> Thou shalt neither vex a stranger, nor oppress him: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.

- This is twofold, and first and foremost the stranger is your brother from a different area, our own people. [Leviticus 25](/bible/leviticus/25) shows it: if thy brother be waxen poor, though he be a stranger or a sojourner, sojourn meaning travel, thou shalt relieve him that he may live with thee. A brother of another tribe who falls on hard times and travels down to live with you is that stranger; neither vex him nor oppress him, for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 25:35-37](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)**

    If thy brother be waxen poor, though he be a stranger or a sojourner, relieve him; take no usury of him, and lend him not thy victuals for increase.

**[Exodus 22:22-24](/bible/exodus/22#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/22#v22)</sup> Ye shall not afflict any widow, or fatherless child.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/22#v23)</sup> If thou afflict them in any wise, and they cry at all unto me, I will surely hear their cry;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/22#v24)</sup> And my wrath shall wax hot, and I will kill you with the sword; and your wives shall be widows, and your children fatherless.

- Ye shall not afflict any widow or fatherless child. A wife without a husband already has a hard life, and a son or daughter without a father the same; do not make their life harder than it already is.
- If thou afflict them in any wise and they cry unto the Most High, he will surely hear their cry, and his wrath shall wax hot: he will kill you with the sword, and your own wives shall be widows and your children fatherless. That is the Lord's plain warning against mistreating those less fortunate.

**[Exodus 22:25](/bible/exodus/22#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/22#v25)</sup> If thou lend money to any of my people that is poor by thee, thou shalt not be to him as an usurer, neither shalt thou lay upon him usury.

- Usury means interest. You cannot charge your brother or sister interest: if you lend twenty dollars, you get twenty dollars back, nothing more, nothing less; no little bit more off the top. This is the same law read in [Leviticus 25:36-37](/bible/leviticus/25#v36).

**[Exodus 22:26-27](/bible/exodus/22#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/22#v26)</sup> If thou at all take thy neighbour’s raiment to pledge, thou shalt deliver it unto him by that the sun goeth down:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/22#v27)</sup> For that is his covering only, it is his raiment for his skin: wherein shall he sleep? and it shall come to pass, when he crieth unto me, that I will hear; for I am gracious.

- If thou take thy neighbor's raiment to pledge, deliver it unto him by that the sun goeth down, for that is his covering only; wherein shall he sleep? The tunics and overcoats worn then were also sleeping garments to keep a man warm at night, especially the poor.
- A well-off man does not have to work for you; this speaks of the less fortunate who extend their services for money and give what is most valuable to them, their raiment, as the pledge. If he cries unto the Most High, he will hear, for he is gracious.

**[Exodus 22:28](/bible/exodus/22#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/22#v28)</sup> Thou shalt not revile the gods, nor curse the ruler of thy people.

- Thou shalt not revile the gods means do not hate or speak evil of your leaders. The word gods here is the leaders over you: the Most High says in the psalm, I have said, Ye are gods, and all of you are children of the Most High, speaking of us.
- [Acts 23](/bible/acts/23) is the example: when Paul spoke against the high priest, they that stood by said, Revilest thou God's high priest? And Paul answered, I wist not, brethren, that he was the high priest: for it is written, Thou shalt not speak evil of the ruler of thy people, quoting this very verse.

**[Exodus 22:29-30](/bible/exodus/22#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/22#v29)</sup> Thou shalt not delay to offer the first of thy ripe fruits, and of thy liquors: the firstborn of thy sons shalt thou give unto me.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/22#v30)</sup> Likewise shalt thou do with thine oxen, and with thy sheep: seven days it shall be with his dam; on the eighth day thou shalt give it me.

- Thou shalt not delay to offer the first of thy ripe fruits and of thy liquors, and the firstborn of thy sons shalt thou give unto me. The firstborn sons that opened the matrix were consecrated unto the Lord, and the father redeemed them back from the priest with shekels.
- Likewise with the oxen and the sheep: seven days the firstling stayed with his dam, his mother, and on the eighth day it was given. The Lord did not want it too young, taken as soon as it popped out; it had to be its appointed time with the mother, then brought to the priest.

**[Exodus 22:31](/bible/exodus/22#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/22#v31)</sup> And ye shall be holy men unto me: neither shall ye eat any flesh that is torn of beasts in the field; ye shall cast it to the dogs.

- Ye shall be holy men unto me: neither shall ye eat any flesh that is torn of beasts in the field; ye shall cast it to the dogs. This is twofold, and the dogs are the strangers of other nations, the aliens and foreigners.
- Always get the context of the law, because sometimes the stranger is your brother and sometimes it is literally the other nations. [Deuteronomy 14:21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21) doubles this law: that which dieth of itself thou shalt give unto the stranger that is in thy gates, or sell it unto an alien, for thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God. Cast the torn flesh to the other nations; ye shall not eat it, because ye are holy people.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 14:21](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v21)**

Thou shalt not seethe a kid in his mother’s milk.

    That which dieth of itself is given to the stranger in thy gates or sold to an alien; thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God.

# Exodus 23: Righteous Judgment, the Three Feasts, and the Angel of the Covenant

**[Exodus 23:1-2](/bible/exodus/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/23#v1)</sup> Thou shalt not raise a false report: put not thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/23#v2)</sup> Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil; neither shalt thou speak in a cause to decline after many to wrest judgment:

- Thou shalt not raise a false report, nor put thine hand with the wicked to be an unrighteous witness: basically, thou shalt not lie. If you did not see it, do not make up a story like you were there; keep your mouth shut.
- Thou shalt not follow a multitude to do evil. Enter not into the path of the wicked; sinners entice, saying come with us, cast in thy lot among us, but walk not thou in the way with them, for their feet run to evil and make haste to shed blood. Many of our young men's lives end short because they follow a multitude to do evil. To be in the midst of the singing, like the whole school hollering fight, fight, fight, is to be in the multitude the Most High warns of; a soul without knowledge of the laws of God hastes with his feet and sins.

**[Exodus 23:3](/bible/exodus/23#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/23#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou countenance a poor man in his cause.

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/24#v17)</sup> And the sight of the glory of the Lord was like devouring fire on the top of the mount in the eyes of the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/24#v18)</sup> And Moses went into the midst of the cloud, and gat him up into the mount: and Moses was in the mount forty days and forty nights.

- Moses rose up with his minister Joshua and went up into the mount of God, leaving Aaron and Hur with the elders for any matters. A cloud covered the mount, the glory of the Lord abode upon Sinai six days, and on the seventh day he called unto Moses out of the midst of the cloud.
- The sight of the glory of the Lord was like devouring fire on the top of the mount in the eyes of the children of Israel; and Moses was in the mount forty days and forty nights.
- If you have been actively listening: Moses already had the book of the covenant written before he went up. In the forty days and forty nights he came down with even more; it was never just the ten commandments.

# Exodus 25: The Pattern of the Tabernacle

Read the chapter → [Exodus 25](/bible/exodus/25)

**[Exodus 25:1-2](/bible/exodus/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/25#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/25#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, that they bring me an offering: of every man that giveth it willingly with his heart ye shall take my offering.

- Speak unto the children of Israel, that they bring me an offering: of every man that giveth it willingly with his heart ye shall take my offering. The Lord wants a willing heart in giving, not grudgingly or of necessity or only because the law says so, but because you know your giving benefits someone in need or the house of God, whatever it needs, the lights, the roof, the floor, the tables, the leadership's travel.

**[Exodus 25:3-7](/bible/exodus/25#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/25#v3)</sup> And this is the offering which ye shall take of them; gold, and silver, and brass,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/25#v4)</sup> And blue, and purple, and scarlet, and fine linen, and goats’ hair,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/25#v5)</sup> And rams’ skins dyed red, and badgers’ skins, and shittim wood,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/25#v6)</sup> Oil for the light, spices for anointing oil, and for sweet incense,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/25#v7)</sup> Onyx stones, and stones to be set in the ephod, and in the breastplate.

- The offering was gold, silver, brass, blue, purple, and scarlet, fine linen, goats' hair, rams' skins dyed red, badgers' skins, shittim wood, oil for the light, spices, and onyx stones for the ephod and the breastplate. Thank the Most High he requires no gold and silver now, for half of us would have nothing to give; under the new covenant the offering is the body presented a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable.
- The badgers' skins settle a confusion: the Lord commanded Israel to handle animal skins and hair to build his own tabernacle. So the laws about not touching that which is defiled, the carcass of the unclean beast, go into the matter of sacrifice and coming into the temple, not into everyday handling. A boar-bristle brush is not sin; brush your hair, brothers.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

    Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God; that is the offering of him that has no gold nor silver.

**[Exodus 25:8-9](/bible/exodus/25#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/25#v8)</sup> And let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/25#v9)</sup> According to all that I shew thee, after the pattern of the tabernacle, and the pattern of all the instruments thereof, even so shall ye make it.

- Let them make me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them. According to all that I shew thee, after the pattern of the tabernacle and the pattern of all the instruments thereof, even so shall ye make it. Shew is the old English; everything was built after the pattern the Lord shewed.

**[Exodus 25:10-16](/bible/exodus/25#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/25#v10)</sup> And they shall make an ark of shittim wood: two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/25#v11)</sup> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, within and without shalt thou overlay it, and shalt make upon it a crown of gold round about.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/25#v12)</sup> And thou shalt cast four rings of gold for it, and put them in the four corners thereof; and two rings shall be in the one side of it, and two rings in the other side of it.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/25#v13)</sup> And thou shalt make staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with gold.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/25#v14)</sup> And thou shalt put the staves into the rings by the sides of the ark, that the ark may be borne with them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/25#v15)</sup> The staves shall be in the rings of the ark: they shall not be taken from it.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/25#v16)</sup> And thou shalt put into the ark the testimony which I shall give thee.

- The ark of shittim wood: two cubits and a half the length, a cubit and a half the breadth and the height. A cubit as we know it is about eighteen inches, and it differed with the size of men in each time; when men were bigger the cubit was bigger.
- It was overlaid with pure gold within and without, with a crown of gold round about, four rings of gold in the corners, and staves of shittim wood overlaid with gold to bear it; the staves were not to be taken from it. And into the ark was put the testimony which the Lord gave.

**[Exodus 25:17-22](/bible/exodus/25#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/25#v17)</sup> And thou shalt make a mercy seat of pure gold: two cubits and a half shall be the length thereof, and a cubit and a half the breadth thereof.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/25#v18)</sup> And thou shalt make two cherubims of gold, of beaten work shalt thou make them, in the two ends of the mercy seat.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/25#v19)</sup> And make one cherub on the one end, and the other cherub on the other end: even of the mercy seat shall ye make the cherubims on the two ends thereof.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/25#v20)</sup> And the cherubims shall stretch forth their wings on high, covering the mercy seat with their wings, and their faces shall look one to another; toward the mercy seat shall the faces of the cherubims be.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/25#v21)</sup> And thou shalt put the mercy seat above upon the ark; and in the ark thou shalt put the testimony that I shall give thee.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/exodus/25#v22)</sup> And there I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubims which are upon the ark of the testimony, of all things which I will give thee in commandment unto the children of Israel.

- The mercy seat of pure gold sat above upon the ark, with two cherubims of gold of beaten work on the two ends, stretching forth their wings on high, covering the mercy seat, their faces one to another looking toward the mercy seat.
- There I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubims which are upon the ark of the testimony, of all things which I will give thee in commandment unto the children of Israel.

**[Exodus 25:23-30](/bible/exodus/25#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/25#v23)</sup> Thou shalt also make a table of shittim wood: two cubits shall be the length thereof, and a cubit the breadth thereof, and a cubit and a half the height thereof.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/25#v24)</sup> And thou shalt overlay it with pure gold, and make thereto a crown of gold round about.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/25#v25)</sup> And thou shalt make unto it a border of an hand breadth round about, and thou shalt make a golden crown to the border thereof round about.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/25#v26)</sup> And thou shalt make for it four rings of gold, and put the rings in the four corners that are on the four feet thereof.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/25#v27)</sup> Over against the border shall the rings be for places of the staves to bear the table.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/25#v28)</sup> And thou shalt make the staves of shittim wood, and overlay them with gold, that the table may be borne with them.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/25#v29)</sup> And thou shalt make the dishes thereof, and spoons thereof, and covers thereof, and bowls thereof, to cover withal: of pure gold shalt thou make them.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/25#v30)</sup> And thou shalt set upon the table shewbread before me alway.

- The table of shittim wood was overlaid with pure gold, with a crown of gold round about: the crown is the molding going around the table, like the crown molding some have in their houses today. It bore the dishes, spoons, covers, and bowls, all of pure gold, and the shewbread was set upon it before the Lord alway.
- Understand how we lived: in our kingdom everything was overlaid with pure gold, clean and royal, with the order of kings, priests, counsellors, and the royal garments. Some nations do a good job in their films of bringing their old royal culture to remembrance for their people; that order and gold is how Israel used to live, and we are going back to it.

**[Exodus 25:31-40](/bible/exodus/25#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/25#v31)</sup> And thou shalt make a candlestick of pure gold: of beaten work shall the candlestick be made: his shaft, and his branches, his bowls, his knops, and his flowers, shall be of the same.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/25#v32)</sup> And six branches shall come out of the sides of it; three branches of the candlestick out of the one side, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/25#v33)</sup> Three bowls made like unto almonds, with a knop and a flower in one branch; and three bowls made like almonds in the other branch, with a knop and a flower: so in the six branches that come out of the candlestick.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/25#v34)</sup> And in the candlestick shall be four bowls made like unto almonds, with their knops and their flowers.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/25#v35)</sup> And there shall be a knop under two branches of the same, and a knop under two branches of the same, and a knop under two branches of the same, according to the six branches that proceed out of the candlestick.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/25#v36)</sup> Their knops and their branches shall be of the same: all it shall be one beaten work of pure gold.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/exodus/25#v37)</sup> And thou shalt make the seven lamps thereof: and they shall light the lamps thereof, that they may give light over against it.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/25#v38)</sup> And the tongs thereof, and the snuffdishes thereof, shall be of pure gold.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/exodus/25#v39)</sup> Of a talent of pure gold shall he make it, with all these vessels.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/exodus/25#v40)</sup> And look that thou make them after their pattern, which was shewed thee in the mount.

- The candlestick of pure gold is the menorah, one beaten work: his shaft, his branches, his bowls, his knops, and his flowers of the same. Six branches come out of the sides of it, three out of the one side and three out of the other, with the shaft in the middle: seven lamps in all.
- A ten-candle candlestick is not the pattern given of God; the scripture gives seven, one in the middle and three coming out on each side, with bowls made like unto almonds with a knop and a flower.
- The seven lamps were lit that they may give light over against it: it was not small, for it had to light up a whole section of the tabernacle. The tongs and the snuffdishes were of pure gold, of a talent of pure gold, and all these vessels were made after the pattern shewed in the mount.
