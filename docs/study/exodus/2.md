## Introduction

This reading covers [Exodus 2](/bible/exodus/2) through 5: the birth of Moses and his flight to Midian, the angel in the burning bush, the three signs given to Moses and the circumcision of his son, and the first appearance before Pharaoh with the answer, Let my people go. The session opened with prayer out of [Psalms 56](/bible/psalms/56), be merciful unto me, O God, for man would swallow me up; in God I have put my trust, I will not fear what flesh can do unto me; and with thanksgiving for the reawakening prophesied in [Ezekiel 37](/bible/ezekiel/37), that the people of the Most High will return unto Him and stand upon their feet, an exceeding great army.

**[Exodus 2:1](/bible/exodus/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/2#v1)</sup> And there went a man of the house of Levi, and took to wife a daughter of Levi.

- A man of the house of Levi took to wife a daughter of Levi. These are Amram and Jochebed, the father and mother of Moses, Aaron, and Miriam.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 6:20](/bible/exodus/6#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/6#v20)</sup> And Amram took him Jochebed his father’s sister to wife; and she bare him Aaron and Moses: and the years of the life of Amram were an hundred and thirty and seven years.

    Amram took him Jochebed to wife, and she bare him Aaron and Moses; the scripture names the parents of Moses.

**[Exodus 2:2](/bible/exodus/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/2#v2)</sup> And the woman conceived, and bare a son: and when she saw him that he was a goodly child, she hid him three months.

- The woman conceived and bare a son, and when she saw him that he was a goodly child, she hid him three months. Goodly means the child was exceeding fair; there was something special about this child from his birth.

  - **[Acts 7:20](/bible/acts/7#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/acts/7#v20)</sup> In which time Moses was born, and was exceeding fair, and nourished up in his father’s house three months:

    In which time Moses was born, and was exceeding fair, and nourished up in his father's house three months.

    By faith his parents hid him, because they saw he was a proper child, and they were not afraid of the king's commandment.

**[Exodus 2:3-4](/bible/exodus/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/2#v3)</sup> And when she could not longer hide him, she took for him an ark of bulrushes, and daubed it with slime and with pitch, and put the child therein; and she laid it in the flags by the river’s brink.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/2#v4)</sup> And his sister stood afar off, to wit what would be done to him.

- When she could no longer hide him, she took for him an ark of bulrushes, daubed it with slime and with pitch, and laid it in the flags by the river's brink. Bulrushes are the river reeds known as cattails.
- His sister Miriam stood afar off, to wit what would be done to him. The movie tellings of this account do not give the full details; to get a full understanding of what happened in the days of Moses you have to read the scriptures.

**[Exodus 2:5-6](/bible/exodus/2#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/2#v5)</sup> And the daughter of Pharaoh came down to wash herself at the river; and her maidens walked along by the river’s side; and when she saw the ark among the flags, she sent her maid to fetch it.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/2#v6)</sup> And when she had opened it, she saw the child: and, behold, the babe wept. And she had compassion on him, and said, This is one of the Hebrews’ children.

- The daughter of Pharaoh sent her maid to fetch the ark, and when she had opened it she saw the child, and the babe wept. She had compassion on him and said, This is one of the Hebrews' children.
- She knew the child was a Hebrew and no Egyptian because the Most High put a difference between the Egyptians and Israel; though both peoples were dark of skin, this was a child of the chosen nation.

  - **[Exodus 11:7](/bible/exodus/11#v7)**

  - **[Psalms 50:2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)</sup> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined.

    Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined; she saw this was of the chosen vessel of the Lord.

**[Exodus 2:7-9](/bible/exodus/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/2#v7)</sup> Then said his sister to Pharaoh’s daughter, Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for thee?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/2#v8)</sup> And Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, Go. And the maid went and called the child’s mother.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/2#v9)</sup> And Pharaoh’s daughter said unto her, Take this child away, and nurse it for me, and I will give thee thy wages. And the woman took the child, and nursed it.

- Miriam was crafty. She offered to call a nurse of the Hebrew women, and Pharaoh's daughter sent the maid, who called the child's own mother, and wages were paid her to nurse him.
- So Moses was raised in his first years by his actual mother; the Most High allowed it, all praises to Him.

**[Exodus 2:10](/bible/exodus/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/2#v10)</sup> And the child grew, and she brought him unto Pharaoh’s daughter, and he became her son. And she called his name Moses: and she said, Because I drew him out of the water.

- The child grew, and he became the son of Pharaoh's daughter. She called his name Moses, which means drawn out, because she drew him out of the water.
- So Moses grew up as an Egyptian, although he was a Hebrew.

**[Exodus 2:11-12](/bible/exodus/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/2#v11)</sup> And it came to pass in those days, when Moses was grown, that he went out unto his brethren, and looked on their burdens: and he spied an Egyptian smiting an Hebrew, one of his brethren.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/2#v12)</sup> And he looked this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no man, he slew the Egyptian, and hid him in the sand.

- When Moses was grown he went out unto his brethren the Israelites and looked on their burdens, and he spied an Egyptian smiting an Hebrew, one of his brethren. Moses knew the Israelites were his brothers; he knew he was not of the Egyptian lineage and that there was something different about him.
- He looked this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no man, he slew the Egyptian and hid him in the sand. He did not just marvel at the oppression; he used his position for his people, unlike many who hold a position of power today and will not risk it for their people. Moses is the ultimate revolutionary, as Christ is.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiastes 5:8](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v8)**

    If thou seest the oppression of the poor, and violent perverting of judgment, marvel not at the matter: for he that is higher than the highest regardeth; the Most High sees the oppression.

**[Exodus 2:13-14](/bible/exodus/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/2#v13)</sup> And when he went out the second day, behold, two men of the Hebrews strove together: and he said to him that did the wrong, Wherefore smitest thou thy fellow?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/2#v14)</sup> And he said, Who made thee a prince and a judge over us? intendest thou to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? And Moses feared, and said, Surely this thing is known.

- The second day two men of the Hebrews strove together, and he that did the wrong answered, Who made thee a prince and a judge over us? intendest thou to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? When a people is oppressed they take the oppression out on one another, and brothers become their own enemies; the same spirit answers correction among Israel today, pointing the finger at the one who corrects.
- Moses feared, and said, Surely this thing is known.

**[Exodus 2:15](/bible/exodus/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/2#v15)</sup> Now when Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to slay Moses. But Moses fled from the face of Pharaoh, and dwelt in the land of Midian: and he sat down by a well.

- When Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to slay Moses; but Moses fled from the face of Pharaoh and dwelt in the land of Midian.
- Moses gave up his whole upbringing and position. By faith he refused to be called the son of Pharaoh's daughter, choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season. In Egypt his head was shaved, his beard cut, his dress and customs Egyptian; he laid all of it down to be what he was called to be, an Israelite.
- The same claim stands now: the remnant does not want the names of the captivity but the natural heritage of Israel, esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures of this land.

- The priest of Midian had seven daughters, and the shepherds drove them from the troughs; but Moses stood up and helped them, and watered their flock. Moses was a man of war, not a weak man.
- The daughters told their father, An Egyptian delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds. They took Moses for an Egyptian by his dress, by how he carried himself, and by his skin: Moses was a very dark-skinned man, the same color as the Egyptians. Israel was always being mistaken for Egyptians.
- Reuel the priest of Midian is the same man called Jethro, the father in law of Moses.

**[Exodus 2:23-25](/bible/exodus/2#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/2#v23)</sup> And it came to pass in process of time, that the king of Egypt died: and the children of Israel sighed by reason of the bondage, and they cried, and their cry came up unto God by reason of the bondage.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/2#v24)</sup> And God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/2#v25)</sup> And God looked upon the children of Israel, and God had respect unto them.

- In process of time the king of Egypt died, and the children of Israel sighed by reason of the bondage, and they cried, and their cry came up unto God, as Israel cries in this captivity today.
- God heard their groaning, and God remembered his covenant with Abraham, with Isaac, and with Jacob. The bondage itself was prophesied to Abraham: his seed would be a stranger in a land not theirs and be afflicted four hundred years, and afterward come out with great substance. That prophecy is about Egypt, not a date of release in this present day.
- God had respect unto them. The Most High has respect unto one nation, Israel: He is in the midst of Israel, and His people shall never be ashamed.
- Respect unto His chosen is not respect of persons. Within Israel there is no respect of persons: the Most High renders to every man according to his deeds, tribulation and anguish upon every soul that doeth evil, of the Jew first and also of the Gentile, the southern kingdom and the northern kingdom that was Hellenized; but glory, honour, and peace to every man that worketh good. The nation is chosen above the nations, and all Israel shall be saved through the Deliverer out of Sion.
