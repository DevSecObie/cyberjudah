## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on History of Susanna 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 4-5, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[History of Susanna 1:1](/bible/susanna/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/susanna/1#v1)</sup> There dwelt a man in Babylon, called Joacim:

- “There dwelt a man in Babylon, called Joacim”.

**[History of Susanna 1:4](/bible/susanna/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/susanna/1#v4)</sup> Now Joacim was a great rich man, and had a fair garden joining unto his house: and to him resorted the Jews; because he was more honourable than all others.

- “Now Joacim was a great rich man, and had a fair garden joining unto his house: and to him resorted the Jews; because he was more honourable than all others.” He was an elder, a leader, and they came to his house to sit with him.

**[History of Susanna 1:5](/bible/susanna/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/susanna/1#v5)</sup> The same year were appointed two of the ancients of the people to be judges, such as the Lord spake of, that wickedness came from Babylon from ancient judges, who seemed to govern the people.

- “The same year were appointed two of the ancients of the people to be judges, such as the Lord spake of, that wickedness came from Babylon from ancient judges, who seemed to govern the people.” We had ancient judges who came out of Babylon and brought Babylonian doctrine and ideals with them. They seemed to govern the people, but they did not: they were evil and wicked as hell.
- These same elders came out of Babylon and made their way into the Roman captivity, and started the Talmud: the Babylonian Talmud. When Christ blasted them for their traditions, they got those from Babylon. These are their fathers here.

    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/29#v23)</sup> Because they have committed villany in Israel, and have committed adultery with their neighbours’ wives, and have spoken lying words in my name, which I have not commanded them; even I know, and am a witness, saith the Lord.

    This is where the Lord spoke of it, through Jeremiah: “Hear ye therefore the word of the Lord, all ye of the captivity, whom I have sent from Jerusalem to Babylon”, concerning “Ahab the son of Kolaiah, and of Zedekiah the son of Maaseiah, which prophesy a lie unto you in my name”. The king of Babylon would slay them, and the curse would be, “The Lord make thee like Zedekiah and like Ahab, whom the king of Babylon roasted in the fire”. They cooked them to death.
