## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 8-16, 22-27, 30-36, 41-42, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 10:8](/bible/john/10#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/john/10#v8)</sup> All that ever came before me are thieves and robbers: but the sheep did not hear them.

- “All that ever came before me are thieves and robbers: but the sheep did not hear them.”

**[John 10:9](/bible/john/10#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/john/10#v9)</sup> I am the door: by me if any man enter in, he shall be saved, and shall go in and out, and find pasture.

- “I am the door”: by Christ a man shall enter and “find pasture”, and that pasture is the kingdom.

**[John 10:10-11](/bible/john/10#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/john/10#v10)</sup> The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/john/10#v11)</sup> I am the good shepherd: the good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.

- Who is the thief? Rome. “The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy.” Rome stole, Rome did a whole lot of killing, and Rome destroyed us. Christ came “that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.”
- “The thief cometh not, but for to steal, and to kill, and to destroy: I am come that they might have life.” “The good shepherd giveth his life for the sheep.”
- “The thief cometh not, but for to steal”: the thief is Esau. He does not come with humanitarian aid; he comes with tanks, bombs and fire, to steal the precious resources, “and to kill”, the indigenous people, the Afro-descendants, the Israelites, “and to destroy.” We have seen it in all these videos. So change the algorithm: stop watching funny videos and sports, and watch what is happening to our people in Latin America, in Africa and in the four corners of the earth under the same curses. Watch as well as pray, and pray for deliverance and destruction upon our enemies.
- “I am come that they might have life, and that they might have it more abundantly.” Christ came that we might have real life, because the life we live in captivity is not life at all. Christ died for our sins so that this devil can stop ruling over us, and we have to see him for who he is.

**[John 10:12-13](/bible/john/10#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/john/10#v12)</sup> But he that is an hireling, and not the shepherd, whose own the sheep are not, seeth the wolf coming, and leaveth the sheep, and fleeth: and the wolf catcheth them, and scattereth the sheep.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/john/10#v13)</sup> The hireling fleeth, because he is an hireling, and careth not for the sheep.

- The hireling “seeth the wolf coming”: he sees danger coming, and he “leaveth the sheep, and fleeth.” He runs for his life, and the wolf catcheth them and scattereth the sheep.
- “The hireling fleeth, because he is an hireling, and careth not for the sheep.” A brother that is a hireling does not care for the sheep.
- A hireling is a worker employed purely for monetary gain. He is only there to get what he can get from the body. He lacks loyalty to the cause; a hireling has no loyalty, and 2018 showed us that. There are still hirelings among us. He is willing to do unpleasant or unethical work just for a paycheck. He is a mercenary, he cares only about the cold hard cash, and he has no passion for the work. He is the one a boss uses for dirty work.
- That is where the dead spirits come from. The brothers with dead spirits are hirelings. They have no passion for this truth; they could care less. This is the Buena Vista Social Club for them, a hangout: I ain’t got nothing else to do, they got some pretty women over there.

  Precepts:
  - **[Job 7:1-2](/bible/job/7#v1)**

**[John 10:14-15](/bible/john/10#v14)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/10#v15)</sup> As the Father knoweth me, even so know I the Father: and I lay down my life for the sheep.

**[John 10:16](/bible/john/10#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/10#v16)</sup> And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold: them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd.

- “And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold”. The fold Christ was speaking to were the Jews in Jerusalem, primarily Judah, Benjamin and Levi. The other sheep are the rest of Israel. “Them also I must bring”, that there may be “one fold, and one shepherd”: the two nations gathered together, the southern and the northern kingdom, under one shepherd, Jesus Christ.
- Everything the prophets and our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ spoke about was referred to in what is called today the Old Testament, so every point can be substantiated from it.
- “They shall hear my voice.” For all of us there was a time we heard this voice. We heard the word of God, and it brought us to repentance, to change. There was something innate in us that made us acknowledge the word of God by hearing his voice and changing our life.
- “And other sheep I have, which are not of this fold”. This fold is the southern kingdom, the Jews, whom he dealt with first. The other sheep are the Gentiles, the northern kingdom. “Them also I must bring, and they shall hear my voice; and there shall be one fold, and one shepherd.” One fold is the whole of Israel, both kingdoms united, and the one shepherd is Christ, who will be the king.

**[John 10:22](/bible/john/10#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/john/10#v22)</sup> And it was at Jerusalem the feast of the dedication, and it was winter.

- We’re going to deal with the holiday the Lord gave us, called the Feast of Dedication; in Hebrew it is called Hanukkah, which means feast of dedication. “And it was at Jerusalem the feast of the dedication, and it was winter”: it’s a winter holiday.
- “And it was at Jerusalem the feast of the dedication, and it was winter.” Christ was in Jerusalem at the feast of dedication in winter. Dedication remembers defeating the Greeks, cleansing the profaned temple and rededicating it to the Most High. Christ's presence is proof that he kept the high holy days.

**[John 10:23](/bible/john/10#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/john/10#v23)</sup> And Jesus walked in the temple in Solomon’s porch.

- “And Jesus walked in the temple in Solomon’s porch.” Jesus observed it: he went into the temple because they were celebrating the Feast of Dedication.
- You can read about the Feast of Dedication in brief in Daniel chapter 11, but in fullness you can read about it in First Maccabees chapter 4. That’s why, when Christians say the Apocrypha is not canon, disregard everything they say.
- “And Jesus walked in the temple in Solomon’s porch.” Solomon's porch was a public place at the temple where people gathered to discuss the word of God and where miracles occurred. Christ taught there, and the apostles later worked in the same setting. The temple was open and people assembled around the scriptures.

    “And by the hands of the apostles were many signs and wonders wrought among the people; (and they were all with one accord in Solomon’s porch.” The apostles worked signs and wonders among the people and gathered with one accord in Solomon's porch. This identifies the same temple setting in which Christ walked. The porch was a place of public teaching, miracles and unity around the word of God.

**[John 10:24-25](/bible/john/10#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/john/10#v24)</sup> Then came the Jews round about him, and said unto him, How long dost thou make us to doubt? If thou be the Christ, tell us plainly.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/john/10#v25)</sup> Jesus answered them, I told you, and ye believed not: the works that I do in my Father’s name, they bear witness of me.

- Some Jews asked Christ to tell them plainly whether he was the Christ. He had already told them, and the works done in his Father's name bore witness of him. Their doubt is not evidence that every Israelite rejected him. The passage concerns the people addressing him at that moment.

**[John 10:26-27](/bible/john/10#v26)**

The sheep hear his voice, are known by him and follow him. Being part of Israel does not mean that every individual responds faithfully; the distinction is made within the people rather than by claiming that none of them believed.

**[John 10:34-36](/bible/john/10#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/john/10#v34)</sup> Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/john/10#v35)</sup> If he called them gods, unto whom the word of God came, and the scripture cannot be broken;
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/john/10#v36)</sup> Say ye of him, whom the Father hath sanctified, and sent into the world, Thou blasphemest; because I said, I am the Son of God?

- “Jesus answered them, Is it not written in your law, I said, Ye are gods?” Christ answered from the law. Those to whom God's word came were called gods, and the scripture could not be broken. He used that written authority to answer the charge against the one whom the Father sanctified and sent into the world.
- Following Christ means returning to the scriptures when challenged, rather than placing man's argument over God's word. His answer about being the Son of God comes from what is already written. Let God be true instead of accepting a claim simply because religious opponents repeat it.

**[John 10:41-42](/bible/john/10#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/john/10#v41)</sup> And many resorted unto him, and said, John did no miracle: but all things that John spake of this man were true.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/john/10#v42)</sup> And many believed on him there.

- “And many resorted unto him, and said, John did no miracle: but all things that John spake of this man were true. And many believed on him there.” Many came to Christ and acknowledged that what John had said about him was true, although John did no miracle. Many believed there. This explicitly defeats the assertion that all the Jews rejected Christ. Some did not believe, but others heard his voice and followed him; the unbelief of the former did not cancel God's truth.
