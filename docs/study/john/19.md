## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 19 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 14-15, 38, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 19:1-3](/bible/john/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/john/19#v1)</sup> Then Pilate therefore took Jesus, and scourged him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/john/19#v2)</sup> And the soldiers platted a crown of thorns, and put it on his head, and they put on him a purple robe,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/john/19#v3)</sup> And said, Hail, King of the Jews! and they smote him with their hands.

- The thorns on Christ’s head were mockery. “Then Pilate therefore took Jesus, and scourged him”: he got beat, real bad.
- “And the soldiers platted a crown of thorns, and put it on his head”: Roman soldiers. They said he was the king, so they gave him a fake crown to mock him, “And said, Hail, King of the Jews! and they smote him with their hands.” A mock crown, to mock him.
- Christ was scourged, crowned with thorns, dressed in purple and struck while being mocked as King of the Jews. That crown gives a picture of leadership's cost: real crowns bleed. A leader suffers with the people and moves with them rather than seeking a crown of jewels built on corporate ideas of status.
- Leadership is sacrifice. It is not applause, visibility or a ladder to personal importance. The weight becomes clearer when the responsibility stops with you. An apology cannot undo every failure, so learn what must be known and ask experienced leaders where you lack understanding. The desire for the crown must include willingness to carry its accountability.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 3:6](/bible/mark/3#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/mark/3#v6)</sup> And the Pharisees went forth, and straightway took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.

    “And the Pharisees went forth, and straightway took counsel with the Herodians against him, how they might destroy him.” The Herodians were a Jewish political faction who supported the dynasty of Herod and aligned with Roman rule for political stability. Herod was an Idumean, the same Idumeans the Maccabees fought. The Herodians were set up as Edomite kings over us, and these Jews were in alliance with the Herodians and the Romans ruling over us.

**[John 19:38](/bible/john/19#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/john/19#v38)</sup> And after this Joseph of Arimathea, being a disciple of Jesus, but secretly for fear of the Jews, besought Pilate that he might take away the body of Jesus: and Pilate gave him leave. He came therefore, and took the body of Jesus.
