## Introduction

This session covers John 2 through 5. The chapters in John run long, so the precepts are pulled where they settle a point. The reading carries the first miracles, the first clashes with the Pharisees, Nicodemus coming by night, the woman at the well in Samaria, and the healing at the pool on the sabbath. Two threads run through it: who the world is that he was sent to, and the unbelief that kept his own from seeing what was standing in front of them.

**[John 2:1-11](/bible/john/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/john/2#v1)</sup> And the third day there was a marriage in Cana of Galilee; and the mother of Jesus was there:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/john/2#v2)</sup> And both Jesus was called, and his disciples, to the marriage.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/john/2#v3)</sup> And when they wanted wine, the mother of Jesus saith unto him, They have no wine.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/john/2#v4)</sup> Jesus saith unto her, Woman, what have I to do with thee? mine hour is not yet come.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/john/2#v5)</sup> His mother saith unto the servants, Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/john/2#v6)</sup> And there were set there six waterpots of stone, after the manner of the purifying of the Jews, containing two or three firkins apiece.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/john/2#v7)</sup> Jesus saith unto them, Fill the waterpots with water. And they filled them up to the brim.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/john/2#v8)</sup> And he saith unto them, Draw out now, and bear unto the governor of the feast. And they bare it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/john/2#v9)</sup> When the ruler of the feast had tasted the water that was made wine, and knew not whence it was: (but the servants which drew the water knew;) the governor of the feast called the bridegroom,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/john/2#v10)</sup> And saith unto him, Every man at the beginning doth set forth good wine; and when men have well drunk, then that which is worse: but thou hast kept the good wine until now.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/john/2#v11)</sup> This beginning of miracles did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested forth his glory; and his disciples believed on him.

- At the marriage in Cana his mother tells him they have no wine, and though he answers, Mine hour is not yet come, she tells the servants, Whatsoever he saith unto you, do it. She knew what he could do.
- The governor of the feast says every man sets forth good wine first, and the worse afterward, but thou hast kept the good wine until now. What was made was better than what had been served.
- This beginning of miracles did Jesus in Cana of Galilee, and manifested forth his glory; and his disciples believed on him.

> <sup>[12](/bible/john/2#v12)</sup> After this he went down to Capernaum, he, and his mother, and his brethren, and his disciples: and they continued there not many days.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/john/2#v13)</sup> And the Jews’ passover was at hand, and Jesus went up to Jerusalem,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/john/2#v14)</sup> And found in the temple those that sold oxen and sheep and doves, and the changers of money sitting:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/john/2#v15)</sup> And when he had made a scourge of small cords, he drove them all out of the temple, and the sheep, and the oxen; and poured out the changers’ money, and overthrew the tables;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/john/2#v16)</sup> And said unto them that sold doves, Take these things hence; make not my Father’s house an house of merchandise.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/john/2#v17)</sup> And his disciples remembered that it was written, The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up.

- He made a scourge of small cords and drove them all out of the temple, with the sheep and the oxen, poured out the changers' money and overthrew the tables. He drove grown men out with a whip.
- Take these things hence; make not my Father's house an house of merchandise. No man had business trading in there at the passover.
- His disciples remembered that it was written, The zeal of thine house hath eaten me up. He was fulfilling what David had written.

**[John 2:18-22](/bible/john/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/john/2#v18)</sup> Then answered the Jews and said unto him, What sign shewest thou unto us, seeing that thou doest these things?

>
> <sup>[21](/bible/john/2#v21)</sup> But he spake of the temple of his body.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/john/2#v22)</sup> When therefore he was risen from the dead, his disciples remembered that he had said this unto them; and they believed the scripture, and the word which Jesus had said.

- They ask what sign he shews. Asking after signs was their habit, and the epistle names it.
- Destroy this temple, and in three days I will raise it up. It went over their heads, and they answered with the forty and six years of building.
- He spake of the temple of his body. The Most High does not dwell in temples made with hands, and the body itself is called a temple in the scriptures.
- The saying was kept and used against him later by false witnesses at his trial.
