## Introduction

This session covers Luke 22 through 24 together with John 1; these notes cover John 1, which closes the reading. The chapter opens with who the Word is, moves to John the Baptist sent to bear witness, and ends with Nathanael naming him: thou art the Son of God, thou art the King of Israel.

**[John 1:1-2](/bible/john/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/john/1#v1)</sup> In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/john/1#v2)</sup> The same was in the beginning with God.

- In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God.
- The Word is Christ, and he was there in the beginning. The Word was with God, and the Word was of God: his wisdom and his power, which is why the same sentence can say both.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 19:12-13](/bible/revelation/19#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/19#v12)</sup> His eyes were as a flame of fire, and on his head were many crowns; and he had a name written, that no man knew, but he himself.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/19#v13)</sup> And he was clothed with a vesture dipped in blood: and his name is called The Word of God.

    He was clothed with a vesture dipped in blood: and his name is called The Word of God.
  - **[1 Corinthians 1:24](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v24)</sup> But unto them which are called, both Jews and Greeks, Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.

    Christ the power of God, and the wisdom of God.

**[John 1:3](/bible/john/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/john/1#v3)</sup> All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.

- All things were made by him; and without him was not any thing made that was made.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 33:6](/bible/psalms/33#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/33#v6)</sup> By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.

    By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.

**[John 1:4-5](/bible/john/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/john/1#v4)</sup> In him was life; and the life was the light of men.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/john/1#v5)</sup> And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.

- In him was life; and the life was the light of men. And the light shineth in darkness; and the darkness comprehended it not.
- What gives life is the word itself. The flesh profits nothing; the words are spirit and they are life.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 6:63](/bible/john/6#v63)**
    > <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

    It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.
  - **[John 8:12](/bible/john/8#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/john/8#v12)</sup> Then spake Jesus again unto them, saying, I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life.

    I am the light of the world: he that followeth me shall not walk in darkness.

**[John 1:6-9](/bible/john/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/1#v6)</sup> There was a man sent from God, whose name was John.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/john/1#v7)</sup> The same came for a witness, to bear witness of the Light, that all men through him might believe.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/john/1#v8)</sup> He was not that Light, but was sent to bear witness of that Light.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/john/1#v9)</sup> That was the true Light, which lighteth every man that cometh into the world.

- There was a man sent from God, whose name was John, sent to bear witness of the Light, that all men through him might believe.
- He was not that Light, but was sent to bear witness of it. He prepared the way.
- The blessing that came down on all men is traced in the scripture to one head, and rested on Jacob and was divided among the twelve tribes.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 44:22](/bible/sirach/44#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/44#v22)</sup> With Isaac did he establish likewise for Abraham his father’s sake the blessing of all men, and the covenant, And made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; among the twelve tribes did he part them.

    The blessing of all men and the covenant made to rest upon the head of Jacob, and his inheritance divided among the twelve tribes.

**[John 1:10-11](/bible/john/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/john/1#v10)</sup> He was in the world, and the world was made by him, and the world knew him not.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/john/1#v11)</sup> He came unto his own, and his own received him not.

- He was in the world, and the world was made by him, and the world knew him not. He came unto his own, and his own received him not.
- His own are named all through the scriptures: the people the law was appointed to, and sin is the breaking of that law.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 3:4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)</sup> Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.

    Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.
  - **[Psalms 78:5](/bible/psalms/78#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/78#v5)</sup> For he established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children:

    He established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel.

**[John 1:12-13](/bible/john/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/john/1#v12)</sup> But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/john/1#v13)</sup> Which were born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of God.

- But as many as received him, to them gave he power to become the sons of God, even to them that believe on his name.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 3:1-2](/bible/1-john/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/3#v1)</sup> Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-john/3#v2)</sup> Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.

    Beloved, now are we the sons of God: and when he shall appear, we shall be like him.
  - **[Baruch 3:35-36](/bible/baruch/3#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/baruch/3#v35)</sup> This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/baruch/3#v36)</sup> He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.

    He hath given the way of knowledge unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved. That names who the beloved are.

**[John 1:14](/bible/john/1#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/john/1#v14)</sup> And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.

- And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father, full of grace and truth.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 3:16](/bible/john/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/john/3#v16)</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

    He gave his only begotten Son. A father and a son are two, which is what the verse says on its face.

**[John 1:15-17](/bible/john/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/1#v15)</sup> John bare witness of him, and cried, saying, This was he of whom I spake, He that cometh after me is preferred before me: for he was before me.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/john/1#v16)</sup> And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/john/1#v17)</sup> For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.

- The law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.
- Grace and mercy are said in the scriptures to be with his saints, and the truth is named in the psalm as the law itself.
- Moses gave the first covenant; grace and truth are the new one. Where the law of Moses required a man's own blood for certain things, there is now a mediator.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 3:9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/3#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/3#v9)</sup> They that put their trust in him shall understand the truth: and such as be faithful in love shall abide with him: for grace and mercy is to his saints, and he hath care for his elect.

    They that put their trust in him shall understand the truth, for grace and mercy is to his saints.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 4:15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v15)</sup> This the people saw, and understood it not, neither laid they up this in their minds, That his grace and mercy is with his saints, and that he hath respect unto his chosen.

    His grace and mercy is with his saints, and that he hath respect unto his chosen.
  - **[Psalms 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

**[John 1:18-28](/bible/john/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/john/1#v18)</sup> No man hath seen God at any time; the only begotten Son, which is in the bosom of the Father, he hath declared him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/john/1#v19)</sup> And this is the record of John, when the Jews sent priests and Levites from Jerusalem to ask him, Who art thou?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/john/1#v20)</sup> And he confessed, and denied not; but confessed, I am not the Christ.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/john/1#v21)</sup> And they asked him, What then? Art thou Elias? And he saith, I am not. Art thou that prophet? And he answered, No.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/john/1#v22)</sup> Then said they unto him, Who art thou? that we may give an answer to them that sent us. What sayest thou of thyself?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/john/1#v23)</sup> He said, I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as said the prophet Esaias.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/john/1#v24)</sup> And they which were sent were of the Pharisees.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/john/1#v25)</sup> And they asked him, and said unto him, Why baptizest thou then, if thou be not that Christ, nor Elias, neither that prophet?
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/john/1#v26)</sup> John answered them, saying, I baptize with water: but there standeth one among you, whom ye know not;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/john/1#v27)</sup> He it is, who coming after me is preferred before me, whose shoe’s latchet I am not worthy to unloose.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/john/1#v28)</sup> These things were done in Bethabara beyond Jordan, where John was baptizing.

- John confessed, and denied not, but confessed, I am not the Christ, nor Elias, nor that prophet.
- I am the voice of one crying in the wilderness, Make straight the way of the Lord, as said the prophet Esaias.
- I baptize with water: but there standeth one among you, whom ye know not. Men came to that baptism confessing their sins, and a man has to be taught the law to know what he is confessing.

**[John 1:29-34](/bible/john/1#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/john/1#v29)</sup> The next day John seeth Jesus coming unto him, and saith, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/john/1#v30)</sup> This is he of whom I said, After me cometh a man which is preferred before me: for he was before me.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/john/1#v31)</sup> And I knew him not: but that he should be made manifest to Israel, therefore am I come baptizing with water.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/john/1#v32)</sup> And John bare record, saying, I saw the Spirit descending from heaven like a dove, and it abode upon him.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/john/1#v33)</sup> And I knew him not: but he that sent me to baptize with water, the same said unto me, Upon whom thou shalt see the Spirit descending, and remaining on him, the same is he which baptizeth with the Holy Ghost.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/john/1#v34)</sup> And I saw, and bare record that this is the Son of God.

- Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.
- I knew him not: but that he should be made manifest to Israel, therefore am I come baptizing with water. John says himself who this was made known to.
- Upon whom thou shalt see the Spirit descending, and remaining on him, the same is he which baptizeth with the Holy Ghost.

**[John 1:35-42](/bible/john/1#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/john/1#v35)</sup> Again the next day after John stood, and two of his disciples;
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/john/1#v36)</sup> And looking upon Jesus as he walked, he saith, Behold the Lamb of God!
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/john/1#v37)</sup> And the two disciples heard him speak, and they followed Jesus.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/john/1#v38)</sup> Then Jesus turned, and saw them following, and saith unto them, What seek ye? They said unto him, Rabbi, (which is to say, being interpreted, Master,) where dwellest thou?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/john/1#v39)</sup> He saith unto them, Come and see. They came and saw where he dwelt, and abode with him that day: for it was about the tenth hour.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/john/1#v40)</sup> One of the two which heard John speak, and followed him, was Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/john/1#v41)</sup> He first findeth his own brother Simon, and saith unto him, We have found the Messias, which is, being interpreted, the Christ.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/john/1#v42)</sup> And he brought him to Jesus. And when Jesus beheld him, he said, Thou art Simon the son of Jona: thou shalt be called Cephas, which is by interpretation, A stone.

- Two of John's disciples follow him and ask where he dwelt. He said, Come and see, and they abode with him that day. He had a place to stay.
- Andrew first findeth his own brother Simon, and saith unto him, We have found the Messias, and brought him to Jesus, who said, Thou shalt be called Cephas.

**[John 1:43-51](/bible/john/1#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/john/1#v43)</sup> The day following Jesus would go forth into Galilee, and findeth Philip, and saith unto him, Follow me.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/john/1#v44)</sup> Now Philip was of Bethsaida, the city of Andrew and Peter.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/john/1#v45)</sup> Philip findeth Nathanael, and saith unto him, We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write, Jesus of Nazareth, the son of Joseph.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/john/1#v46)</sup> And Nathanael said unto him, Can there any good thing come out of Nazareth? Philip saith unto him, Come and see.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/john/1#v47)</sup> Jesus saw Nathanael coming to him, and saith of him, Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile!
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/john/1#v48)</sup> Nathanael saith unto him, Whence knowest thou me? Jesus answered and said unto him, Before that Philip called thee, when thou wast under the fig tree, I saw thee.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/john/1#v49)</sup> Nathanael answered and saith unto him, Rabbi, thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/john/1#v50)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto him, Because I said unto thee, I saw thee under the fig tree, believest thou? thou shalt see greater things than these.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/john/1#v51)</sup> And he saith unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man.

- We have found him, of whom Moses in the law, and the prophets, did write. What was written of him was written in the law and the prophets before ever he came.
- Behold an Israelite indeed, in whom is no guile.
- Nathanael answered, Rabbi, thou art the Son of God; thou art the King of Israel. That is the title given him here, and it names one people.
