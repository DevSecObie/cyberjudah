## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 15-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 14:15-20](/bible/john/14#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/john/14#v16)</sup> And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter, that he may abide with you for ever;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/john/14#v17)</sup> Even the Spirit of truth; whom the world cannot receive, because it seeth him not, neither knoweth him: but ye know him; for he dwelleth with you, and shall be in you.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/john/14#v18)</sup> I will not leave you comfortless: I will come to you.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/john/14#v19)</sup> Yet a little while, and the world seeth me no more; but ye see me: because I live, ye shall live also.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/john/14#v20)</sup> At that day ye shall know that I am in my Father, and ye in me, and I in you.

- “If ye love me, keep my commandments. And I will pray the Father, and he shall give you another Comforter”. That’s the same thing we just read in Proverbs: turn at God’s reproof and he pours out his spirit. So there is a stipulation to getting the Holy Spirit: you must keep the commandments. Why is that so difficult, Israel? People read this and can’t get it; they don’t see it, they can’t hear it.
- The church women really annoy me; I grew up around them. They always had some Holy Spirit, and they sleeping with the pastor, they sleeping with the deacons. When you got people talking about they got the Holy Spirit and they don’t keep commandment one, they is a liar. Somebody tell the black Christian woman she’s a liar.
- The question is how do you get the Holy Spirit, how do you get your spiritual eyes open, how do you get your understanding open to understand the scriptures. “If ye love me, keep my commandments.” That is the stipulation to get the Holy Spirit: in order to get that Spirit of truth, brothers and sisters, you must be keeping the commandments.
- Growing up in church there was a class where you could learn to get the Holy Spirit, and the man would have everybody say see my tie, say it faster, say it again, and put it together. That’s stupid black Christian stuff, stupid and evil: ninety-nine percent of them in there are black and Latin women, and the rest are black effeminate men and Latin men doing that garbage.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 2:3-4](/bible/1-john/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/2#v3)</sup> And hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/2#v4)</sup> He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar, and the truth is not in him.

    “And hereby we do know that we know him, if we keep his commandments. He that saith, I know him, and keepeth not his commandments, is a liar”. You don’t hear most men talk like they got the Holy Spirit; it’s always the effeminate men and all the women in the church that say that. Your mama’s a liar, all them Christian women are liars, the effeminate men are liars, every last bunch of them. Oh, I got a personal relationship with Jesus; no you don’t, you big liar. Without keeping the commandments there is no Holy Spirit.
  - **[Proverbs 1:23](/bible/proverbs/1#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/1#v23)</sup> Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you, I will make known my words unto you.

    Proverbs says the same thing. “Turn you at my reproof”: reproof means keep the commandments, God’s reproof with his commandments. “Behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you”: that’s how you get the Holy Spirit. It was true in the past the same way, the same thing Christ said; there’s no other way.
  - **[John 14:26](/bible/john/14#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/john/14#v26)</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

    “But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.” The Comforter brings all things to your remembrance.

**[John 14:21](/bible/john/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/john/14#v21)</sup> He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.

- “He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me”: so if you keep the commandments, you’re the one that loves Christ. “And he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father”: if you love Christ you’re going to be loved of his Father, “and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.”
- “He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me: and he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father.” You must keep his commandments to show that you love him. Out there they were saying, I have a personal relationship with God. If you love God, you are keeping his commandments; a relationship with God is keeping his commandments. That is what he said in red letters.
- “He that hath my commandments, and keepeth them, he it is that loveth me.” The commandments we are supposed to have are the Father’s commandments, all of them. No picking and choosing, cherry-picking, hopping and skipping around like the Christians. Everything the Father told us to do, those are the commandments, and the man or woman that keeps them is the one that loves Christ, that loves the Lord.
- “And he that loveth me shall be loved of my Father, and I will love him, and will manifest myself to him.” Here he is explaining the manifestation, how you receive the Holy Spirit. In order to have the Holy Spirit, you must be one with the Most High God and his Son.

**[John 14:22-24](/bible/john/14#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/john/14#v22)</sup> Judas saith unto him, not Iscariot, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself unto us, and not unto the world?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/john/14#v23)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto him, If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/john/14#v24)</sup> He that loveth me not keepeth not my sayings: and the word which ye hear is not mine, but the Father’s which sent me.

- “Judas saith unto him, not Iscariot, Lord, how is it that thou wilt manifest thyself unto us, and not unto the world? Jesus answered and said unto him, If a man love me, he will keep my words: and my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him.” “He that loveth me not keepeth not my sayings: and the word which ye hear is not mine”: Christ said I didn’t speak my own words, “but the Father’s which sent me.”
- “If a man love me, he will keep my words.” He says it numerous times: a man who loves him or wants a relationship with him and his Father will keep his words, not be a forgetful hearer but a doer. It is the same thing James said. “And my Father will love him, and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him.” Their spirit will abide in you. The Holy Spirit is the spirit of the Father and his Son in you.
- “He that loveth me not keepeth not my sayings.” If you do not love God or have a relationship with him, you will not keep his commandments. The words Christ taught were not his own: they were Moses' words, which his Father taught him, and he taught them unto us.

**[John 14:25-26](/bible/john/14#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/john/14#v25)</sup> These things have I spoken unto you, being yet present with you.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/john/14#v26)</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

- “But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things”: he’s going to teach you your history, your past, your present, your future. “And bring all things to your remembrance”: you’re not Negroes or Latinos, you’re the Israelites, and you went into slavery because you broke God’s commandments. That’s what the comforter, the Holy Ghost, would do. So when these people run around saying they got the Holy Ghost, they a liar: the comforter is going to bring everything to our remembrance that Christianity is telling us to forget.
- “These things have I spoken unto you, being yet present with you”: while I am still here alive with you, because eventually I will be gone, but my spirit will remain in you if you keep my commandments, along with my Father's.
- “But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost”: Christ himself is the Holy Ghost, the spirit of his Father and him abiding in you. When you keep the commandments they dwell in you; when you do not, they don't. “He shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.” Because we were going to go into captivity and forget who we were until that spirit entered into us again, and here we are today. He told us these things before, when we were here before.
- “But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost”: it gives you another term for the Comforter. The Spirit of truth was called the Comforter, the Holy Spirit, the Holy Ghost. “He shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.” The Holy Ghost would bring all things to our remembrance, whatsoever Christ said to us, and Christ said a lot of things to the children of Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 21:24](/bible/luke/21#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/luke/21#v24)</sup> And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.

    One of the main things Christ said, that the churches don’t read and don’t want to talk about. “And they shall fall by the edge of the sword”: the Israelites would fall by the sword, the Roman sword, “and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.” Christ said the Israelites would all go into slavery and the other nations would live in Jerusalem. The churches that claim they got the Holy Spirit: the Holy Spirit never showed them that. You mean to tell me the Holy Spirit never told them that?
  - **[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

    “The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments”. One of the first things to get a good understanding of the Bible: you must do the commandments. A lot of these discombobulated Christians don’t do the commandments, so they don’t understand the Bible, but they put on good talk like they do. When you examine them precept upon precept, they don’t know what the hell they’re talking about.
  - **[John 14:15](/bible/john/14#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/john/14#v15)</sup> If ye love me, keep my commandments.

    Christ also said: “If ye love me, keep my commandments.” That is one of the things the Holy Spirit brings back to our remembrance.

**[John 14:27](/bible/john/14#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/john/14#v27)</sup> Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.

- “Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you.” Christ is not talking about peace on the earth. He is talking about spiritual peace, peace of mind, and there is no price for peace of mind. Stop bothering yourself with everything and focus on what you need to focus on.
- There is nothing wrong with getting up and going to work to pay your bills, spending time with your wife and your children. But at the end of the day your mind is supposed to be on the work of Messiah. That comes first: not your job, not your wife, not your children, but the kingdom of heaven and the work for Messiah. Messiah first; everything else is second.
