## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 12 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4-6, 9-11, 18-31, 36-43, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 12:4-5](/bible/john/12#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/john/12#v4)</sup> Then saith one of his disciples, Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, which should betray him,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/john/12#v5)</sup> Why was not this ointment sold for three hundred pence, and given to the poor?

- Now the terrible examples, of not upholding good character and integrity. “Then saith one of his disciples, Judas Iscariot, Simon’s son, which should betray him, Why was not this ointment sold for three hundred pence, and given to the poor?” This is what was happening in Matthew 26.

**[John 12:6](/bible/john/12#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/12#v6)</sup> This he said, not that he cared for the poor; but because he was a thief, and had the bag, and bare what was put therein.

- “This he said, not that he cared for the poor; but because he was a thief.” He was a thief. This man walked with Christ and saw many miracles, and still betrayed the saviour, and betrayed the disciples and everybody else around him. People like that are around, but not really around; they are just taking up space. That is not the type of character you need. This is a person that lacks integrity.
- Money was the root and the god of Judas. Greed: he wanted more and more. They were casting out demons and doing miracles. What more do you want?
- This is the kind of person the other nations expect you to be: greedy, lacking integrity and morals. This is how they infiltrate organizations, through money. Organizations fail because of money: they come in, offer money, and the organization gives itself up, because it lacked integrity and let money dictate, maneuver and control the movement. That is not happening here. It happened once, with One West. It is not happening again.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:6](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v6)</sup> Keep therefore and do them; for this is your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the nations, which shall hear all these statutes, and say, Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.

    The other nations expect us to be that greedy individual, the one that lacks integrity. At work, your coworkers wonder about you: this brother never murmurs with us, he never works the Sabbath with us.

**[John 12:9-10](/bible/john/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/john/12#v9)</sup> Much people of the Jews therefore knew that he was there: and they came not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/john/12#v10)</sup> But the chief priests consulted that they might put Lazarus also to death;

- Remember: Jews only. “Much people of the Jews therefore knew that he was there: and they came not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.” “But the chief priests consulted that they might put Lazarus also to death”: Christ raised Lazarus from the dead and they want to kill him again. That’s how evil they were.

**[John 12:11](/bible/john/12#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/john/12#v11)</sup> Because that by reason of him many of the Jews went away, and believed on Jesus.

- “Because that by reason of him many of the Jews went away, and believed on Jesus”: by reason of Christ raising him from the dead, many of the Jews left them and started following Christ for his miracle.

**[John 12:18-19](/bible/john/12#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/john/12#v18)</sup> For this cause the people also met him, for that they heard that he had done this miracle.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/john/12#v19)</sup> The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, Perceive ye how ye prevail nothing? behold, the world is gone after him.

- “For this cause the people also met him, for that they heard that he had done this miracle.” The Pharisees were losing their following: “behold, the world is gone after him.” The world there is the Jews who went after Christ because of Lazarus.
- “For this cause the people also met him, for that they heard that he had done this miracle”: they heard he had raised Lazarus from the dead.
- “The Pharisees therefore said among themselves, Perceive ye how ye prevail nothing?” We’re losing our followers. “Behold, the world is gone after him.” What world went after him in this chapter? The Jews. The Jews are the world; the Pharisees called their own people the world, based upon Isaiah 45:17. That’s the world he so loved.

**[John 12:20](/bible/john/12#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/john/12#v20)</sup> And there were certain Greeks among them that came up to worship at the feast:

- “And there were certain Greeks among them that came up to worship at the feast”. Why are there Greeks among the Jews, worshipping at the feast? Who are these Greeks? The margin of the King James sends you to John 7:35, where the Greeks are the dispersed.
- In the Greek the word is Hellen, number 1672. A Greek by nationality, but in a wider sense all nations, not Jews, who made the language, customs and learning of the Greeks their own, and the primary reference is to a difference of religion and worship. So a Greek was not only one born Greek: whoever took up the religion and worship of the Greeks became a Greek.
- These Greeks were our own people, scattered abroad and Hellenized, who had come back to keep the customs of their forefathers, called proselytes. You cannot tell me the Greeks of John 12:20 are actual Gentiles.
- “And there were certain Greeks among them that came up to worship at the feast”: came up to worship, during Passover. The ministry was to Jews only at this time; these were not white guys. They came up to worship God just like the Ethiopian in Acts 8 came to worship, because they were Israelites also.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 7:35](/bible/john/7#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/john/7#v35)</sup> Then said the Jews among themselves, Whither will he go, that we shall not find him? will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?

    “Then said the Jews among themselves, Whither will he go, that we shall not find him?” They followed Christ everywhere, like people do us, harassing. “Will he go unto the dispersed among the Gentiles, and teach the Gentiles?”
  - **[John 11:47-52](/bible/john/11#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/john/11#v47)</sup> Then gathered the chief priests and the Pharisees a council, and said, What do we? for this man doeth many miracles.
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/john/11#v48)</sup> If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him: and the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation.
    >
    > <sup>[49](/bible/john/11#v49)</sup> And one of them, named Caiaphas, being the high priest that same year, said unto them, Ye know nothing at all,
    >
    > <sup>[50](/bible/john/11#v50)</sup> Nor consider that it is expedient for us, that one man should die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.
    >
    > <sup>[51](/bible/john/11#v51)</sup> And this spake he not of himself: but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for that nation;
    >
    > <sup>[52](/bible/john/11#v52)</sup> And not for that nation only, but that also he should gather together in one the children of God that were scattered abroad.

    “If we let him thus alone, all men will believe on him”, and all men there is not all nations. “And the Romans shall come and take away both our place and nation.” Then Caiaphas: “it is expedient for us, that one man should die for the people, and that the whole nation perish not.” One man dying for the people, by the prophecy of Daniel 9 and Isaiah 53.
  - **[Acts 14:1-2](/bible/acts/14#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/acts/14#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in Iconium, that they went both together into the synagogue of the Jews, and so spake, that a great multitude both of the Jews and also of the Greeks believed.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/acts/14#v2)</sup> But the unbelieving Jews stirred up the Gentiles, and made their minds evil affected against the brethren.

    “They went both together into the synagogue of the Jews, and so spake, that a great multitude both of the Jews and also of the Greeks believed. But the unbelieving Jews stirred up the Gentiles”.

**[John 12:21-22](/bible/john/12#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/john/12#v21)</sup> The same came therefore to Philip, which was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and desired him, saying, Sir, we would see Jesus.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/john/12#v22)</sup> Philip cometh and telleth Andrew: and again Andrew and Philip tell Jesus.

- “The same came therefore to Philip, which was of Bethsaida of Galilee, and desired him, saying, Sir, we would see Jesus.” The same Greeks said, listen Philip, can we see Christ, can we meet him? “Philip cometh and telleth Andrew: and again Andrew and Philip tell Jesus”: the Greeks want to talk to you.

**[John 12:23-26](/bible/john/12#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/john/12#v23)</sup> And Jesus answered them, saying, The hour is come, that the Son of man should be glorified.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/john/12#v24)</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, Except a corn of wheat fall into the ground and die, it abideth alone: but if it die, it bringeth forth much fruit.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/john/12#v25)</sup> He that loveth his life shall lose it; and he that hateth his life in this world shall keep it unto life eternal.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/john/12#v26)</sup> If any man serve me, let him follow me; and where I am, there shall also my servant be: if any man serve me, him will my Father honour.

- “And Jesus answered them, saying, The hour is come, that the Son of man should be glorified.” “He that loveth his life shall lose it; and he that hateth his life in this world shall keep it unto life eternal.” “If any man serve me, let him follow me”: he’s talking to the Greeks here. “And where I am, there shall also my servant be: if any man serve me, him will my Father honour.”

**[John 12:27-30](/bible/john/12#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/john/12#v27)</sup> Now is my soul troubled; and what shall I say? Father, save me from this hour: but for this cause came I unto this hour.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/john/12#v28)</sup> Father, glorify thy name. Then came there a voice from heaven, saying, I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/john/12#v29)</sup> The people therefore, that stood by, and heard it, said that it thundered: others said, An angel spake to him.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/john/12#v30)</sup> Jesus answered and said, This voice came not because of me, but for your sakes.

- “Father, glorify thy name. Then came there a voice from heaven, saying, I have both glorified it, and will glorify it again.” They heard a voice coming from heaven. A lot of times people read this and answer that it was God speaking. No: nobody ever heard God’s voice.
- Do you understand what a messenger is? The word angel means messenger. Angels speak on the behalf of God.
- “Others said, An angel spake to him.” The angel is speaking, but the angel is speaking on the behalf of the Father: I have glorified it, and will keep glorifying it. No one has ever heard the voice of the Father; it was always Christ, or an angel speaking on the behalf of the Father.
- “Father, glorify thy name. Then came there a voice from heaven, saying, I have both glorified it”: the Father spoke. “The people therefore, that stood by”, the Greeks that came to talk to him, “said that it thundered: others said, An angel spake to him.” “Jesus answered and said, This voice came not because of me, but for your sakes.” Why would the voice of God come for heathens’ sakes? It didn’t. These Greeks are not heathens; these were Jews that were worshipping, and they heard the Father’s voice. The voice you heard came for your sakes, that you may believe in who I am.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 1:1](/bible/revelation/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/1#v1)</sup> The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass; and he sent and signified it by his angel unto his servant John:

    “The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him”: God gave the revelation to Christ, “to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass.” That is why in the scriptures God showed Christ certain things, and Christ comes to us telling us thus saith the Lord.
  - **[Revelation 1:8](/bible/revelation/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/1#v8)</sup> I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending, saith the Lord, which is, and which was, and which is to come, the Almighty.

    “I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the ending.” This is the angel saying this, speaking on the behalf of Christ. When you read in Revelation, that is an angel speaking, but he is speaking on the behalf of Christ.

**[John 12:31](/bible/john/12#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/john/12#v31)</sup> Now is the judgment of this world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out.

- “Now is the judgment of this world: now shall the prince of this world be cast out”: they’re being cast out, they’re gonna fall. He ain’t talking to those white Greeks; he’s talking to his own people who are called Greeks.

**[John 12:36-37](/bible/john/12#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/john/12#v36)</sup> While ye have light, believe in the light, that ye may be the children of light. These things spake Jesus, and departed, and did hide himself from them.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/john/12#v37)</sup> But though he had done so many miracles before them, yet they believed not on him:

- “While ye have light, believe in the light, that ye may be the children of light. These things spake Jesus, and departed, and did hide himself from them.” Though he had done so many miracles before them, they did not believe Christ.

**[John 12:38](/bible/john/12#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/john/12#v38)</sup> That the saying of Esaias the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spake, Lord, who hath believed our report? and to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?

- Pay attention to what he is saying: “Lord, who hath believed our report?” They don’t want to believe us. “And to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?”

**[John 12:39-40](/bible/john/12#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/john/12#v39)</sup> Therefore they could not believe, because that Esaias said again,
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/john/12#v40)</sup> He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart; that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.

- “Therefore they could not believe”, because of what Isaiah said again: “He hath blinded their eyes”, and hardened their hearts, “that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.” This is what the Lord did: he blinded our people’s eyes and their hearts, so that they can’t see and understand. The I that should heal them is Christ.

**[John 12:41](/bible/john/12#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/john/12#v41)</sup> These things said Esaias, when he saw his glory, and spake of him.

- “These things said” Isaiah, “when he saw his glory, and spake of him.” He saw Christ’s glory, and he spoke of Christ. These scriptures, brothers, are on another level. Isaiah saw Christ and saw his glory; he saw the Lord from heaven. They keep playing with us like we don’t know what we are talking about, but right through the Bible it is Christ; right through the Bible it is him.

**[John 12:42](/bible/john/12#v42)**

> <sup>[42](/bible/john/12#v42)</sup> Nevertheless among the chief rulers also many believed on him; but because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, lest they should be put out of the synagogue:

- “Among the chief rulers also many believed on him; but because of the Pharisees they did not confess him, lest they should be put out of the synagogue”. We know this: brothers and sisters in some of these congregations see the evil going on, the evil spoken of our leadership. Some want to leave and speak on it, but because of fear they do not want to be put out.

**[John 12:43](/bible/john/12#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/john/12#v43)</sup> For they loved the praise of men more than the praise of God.

- “For they loved the praise of men more than the praise of God.” That is the point: men’s admiration, with advantage in mind, instead of the praise of God.
