## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4-13, 31-38, 47-51, 60-64, 66, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 6:4](/bible/john/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/john/6#v4)</sup> And the passover, a feast of the Jews, was nigh.

- “And the passover, a feast of the Jews, was nigh”: the passover was coming close.

**[John 6:5](/bible/john/6#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/john/6#v5)</sup> When Jesus then lifted up his eyes, and saw a great company come unto him, he saith unto Philip, Whence shall we buy bread, that these may eat?

- “Whence shall we buy bread, that these may eat?” He was testing Philip with that question.

**[John 6:6](/bible/john/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/6#v6)</sup> And this he said to prove him: for he himself knew what he would do.

- “And this he said to prove him: for he himself knew what he would do.” Christ knew what he was going to do, but he wanted to see what Philip thought. He was testing his disciples, just to see where their minds were. He was proving them.

**[John 6:7-9](/bible/john/6#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/john/6#v7)</sup> Philip answered him, Two hundred pennyworth of bread is not sufficient for them, that every one of them may take a little.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/john/6#v8)</sup> One of his disciples, Andrew, Simon Peter’s brother, saith unto him,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/john/6#v9)</sup> There is a lad here, which hath five barley loaves, and two small fishes: but what are they among so many?

- Philip answered that “Two hundred pennyworth of bread is not sufficient for them”, and Andrew found a lad with “five barley loaves, and two small fishes: but what are they among so many?” So he was simply proving them, to see what their thoughts were on how to feed the people.

**[John 6:10-13](/bible/john/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/john/6#v10)</sup> And Jesus said, Make the men sit down. Now there was much grass in the place. So the men sat down, in number about five thousand.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/john/6#v11)</sup> And Jesus took the loaves; and when he had given thanks, he distributed to the disciples, and the disciples to them that were set down; and likewise of the fishes as much as they would.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/john/6#v12)</sup> When they were filled, he said unto his disciples, Gather up the fragments that remain, that nothing be lost.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/john/6#v13)</sup> Therefore they gathered them together, and filled twelve baskets with the fragments of the five barley loaves, which remained over and above unto them that had eaten.

- He made the men sit down, “about five thousand”, distributed the loaves and the fishes “as much as they would”, and they “filled twelve baskets with the fragments of the five barley loaves”. That was the miracle that was performed, but the point of the question was proving the disciples, to see what their mind was on how to feed the people.

**[John 6:31-33](/bible/john/6#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/john/6#v31)</sup> Our fathers did eat manna in the desert; as it is written, He gave them bread from heaven to eat.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/john/6#v32)</sup> Then Jesus said unto them, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Moses gave you not that bread from heaven; but my Father giveth you the true bread from heaven.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/john/6#v33)</sup> For the bread of God is he which cometh down from heaven, and giveth life unto the world.

- Our fathers ate manna in the desert, bread from heaven. Christ explains that Moses did not give the true bread; the Father gives the true bread from heaven, which comes down and gives life to the world. The world here is Israel. The manna in the wilderness was a figure pointing to Christ.
- Now we know the flesh presses us down, that we drift, that we forget, and that we need daily renewal and daily bread. “Our fathers did eat manna in the desert; as it is written, He gave them bread from heaven to eat.” Christ is about to open you up.
- “Moses gave you not that bread from heaven; but my Father giveth you the true bread from heaven.” Moses did not give it to you; the Father gives the true bread from heaven.
- “For the bread of God is he which cometh down from heaven, and giveth life unto the world.” He is talking about himself.

**[John 6:34-36](/bible/john/6#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/john/6#v34)</sup> Then said they unto him, Lord, evermore give us this bread.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/john/6#v35)</sup> And Jesus said unto them, I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/john/6#v36)</sup> But I said unto you, That ye also have seen me, and believe not.

- They ask for that bread always, and Christ answers that he is the bread of life. Coming to him and believing in him satisfies hunger and thirst. They had seen him and still did not believe. The hidden manna is Christ and his doctrine, not a promise that ordinary bread will make the flesh immortal.
- “Lord, evermore give us this bread.” “I am the bread of life: he that cometh to me shall never hunger; and he that believeth on me shall never thirst.” What happened with the manna was always symbolic of Christ. When it says it was a shadow, a foreshadowing, that is everything: from beginning to end, Christ is in it, and it was all for us to know and see and understand.
- So boo-hoo on Christians who say the New Testament is greater than the Old, because the Old established everything else. That is why you need here a little, there a little; that is how you unlock the scriptures.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 14:6](/bible/john/14#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/john/14#v6)</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

    This goes into the bread of life: “I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.” He is the way, the truth and the life, and by him we know the Father.
  - **[1 John 2:6](/bible/1-john/2#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/1-john/2#v6)</sup> He that saith he abideth in him ought himself also so to walk, even as he walked.

    He is the way, and we have to walk as he walked: “He that saith he abideth in him ought himself also so to walk, even as he walked.”
  - **[Psalms 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    He is also the truth. He fulfilled, and will fulfil, all prophecies: Lo, I come in the volume of the book. “Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.” This is speaking of Christ, the embodiment of all these things.

**[John 6:37-38](/bible/john/6#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/john/6#v37)</sup> All that the Father giveth me shall come to me; and him that cometh to me I will in no wise cast out.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/john/6#v38)</sup> For I came down from heaven, not to do mine own will, but the will of him that sent me.

- All whom the Father gives Christ will come to him, and he will not cast out the one who comes. The Father chooses who receives this understanding. Christ came down from heaven to do the will of the one who sent him, not his own will.

**[John 6:47-51](/bible/john/6#v47)**

> <sup>[47](/bible/john/6#v47)</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto you, He that believeth on me hath everlasting life.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/john/6#v48)</sup> I am that bread of life.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/john/6#v49)</sup> Your fathers did eat manna in the wilderness, and are dead.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/john/6#v50)</sup> This is the bread which cometh down from heaven, that a man may eat thereof, and not die.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/john/6#v51)</sup> I am the living bread which came down from heaven: if any man eat of this bread, he shall live for ever: and the bread that I will give is my flesh, which I will give for the life of the world.

- The one who believes on Christ has everlasting life. The fathers ate manna and are dead; that physical bread did not prevent death. Christ is the living bread from heaven, and the one who eats it lives for ever. His flesh is given for the life of the world, Israel. Breaking bread points to receiving him and learning his doctrine. That explains the hidden manna promised to the overcomer in Revelation.
- “I am that bread of life.” How many times does he have to say it?
- “Your fathers did eat manna in the wilderness, and are dead.” He is talking about what the manna represented. Christ is that manna.
- “This is the bread which cometh down from heaven, that a man may eat thereof, and not die.”

  Precepts:
  - **[John 6:57](/bible/john/6#v57)**
    > <sup>[57](/bible/john/6#v57)</sup> As the living Father hath sent me, and I live by the Father: so he that eateth me, even he shall live by me.

    “As the living Father hath sent me, and I live by the Father: so he that eateth me, even he shall live by me.” What does it mean to eat of him? Consume his word every day.

**[John 6:60-64](/bible/john/6#v60)**

> <sup>[60](/bible/john/6#v60)</sup> Many therefore of his disciples, when they had heard this, said, This is an hard saying; who can hear it?
>
> <sup>[61](/bible/john/6#v61)</sup> When Jesus knew in himself that his disciples murmured at it, he said unto them, Doth this offend you?
>
> <sup>[62](/bible/john/6#v62)</sup> What and if ye shall see the Son of man ascend up where he was before?
>
> <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/john/6#v64)</sup> But there are some of you that believe not. For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were that believed not, and who should betray him.

- The Christian apologist Vocab Malone says the mockery shows our increased prominence and visibility, and that they need to take us seriously. He knows that if they leave us alone, everybody will believe on us. They said in Acts 5 to leave them alone and let them fall by themselves, but they won’t do that. Once they create the narrative they want, they are going to go through all those steps to create a negative image of who we are.
- Once the persecution starts to come, what is going to happen? “This is an hard saying; who can hear it?” “But there are some of you that believe not.” Some of you don’t believe, and we are just waiting for the thing to happen where you bounce. “For Jesus knew from the beginning who they were that believed not, and who should betray him.”
- “It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.” That is the spirit and the life Christ meant: the true bread from heaven, his words. That should give us a more profound understanding.

**[John 6:66](/bible/john/6#v66)**

> <sup>[66](/bible/john/6#v66)</sup> From that time many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him.

- “From that time many of his disciples went back, and walked no more with him.” The disciples left. When it got real, they bounced, and a lot of you will do the same. These scriptures are shown so you are ready, so you aren’t surprised when things happen.
