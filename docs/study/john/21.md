## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 21 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 25, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 21:25](/bible/john/21#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/john/21#v25)</sup> And there are also many other things which Jesus did, the which, if they should be written every one, I suppose that even the world itself could not contain the books that should be written. Amen.

- “And there are also many other things which Jesus did”. Christ looked out for our people, died for our people, healed our people, and fed our people. “I suppose that even the world itself could not contain the books that should be written.” The books couldn't contain all the wonderful works Christ did for his nation.
- Ninety percent of the time those good intentions end up being bad results, bad judgment, bad decisions. A lot of times they're a cover for evil work, for not completing the task, not doing what's needed, or keeping an escape route ready when you fail. Be mindful when a brother says he has good intentions.
