## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-4, 6, 33, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 16:2-3](/bible/john/16#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/john/16#v2)</sup> They shall put you out of the synagogues: yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/john/16#v3)</sup> And these things will they do unto you, because they have not known the Father, nor me.

- Christ warned us of this, which is why we have no fear: we are turned into another man, and it is Christ dwelling in us doing his work. “They shall put you out of the synagogues: yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service.” “And these things will they do unto you, because they have not known the Father, nor me.”
- “They shall put you out of the synagogues”. Out of the churches. “yea, the time cometh, that whosoever killeth you will think that he doeth God service.” It happened back then, and it's going to happen in these days. These Christians are going to get mad because they can't defend their gospel anymore. Everything they come out with, God is cornering them off. What comes next? Violence. Let's put them to death. They're lying. They're false prophets. They're going to kill us because they can't defend their gospel anymore, thinking they're doing a good thing, thinking they have good intentions.
- “because they have not known the Father, nor me.” They never knew God, Christ, or this Bible. They're mad, the spirit of Satan is on them, and they'll put us to death thinking they're doing right because they can't back up what they're saying anymore.

**[John 16:4](/bible/john/16#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/john/16#v4)</sup> But these things have I told you, that when the time shall come, ye may remember that I told you of them. And these things I said not unto you at the beginning, because I was with you.

- “But these things have I told you”. We have a true and living God who warns us before these things happen. “that when the time shall come, ye may remember that I told you” Remember he warned us they'd kill us thinking they were doing the right thing, having good intentions.
- “But these things have I told you”: Christ pre-warned us, so it is not going to be shocking to those in the spirit. It is the professional students, who do not study day and night, who are shaken when their number is called. “That when the time shall come, ye may remember that I told you of them.”

**[John 16:6](/bible/john/16#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/16#v6)</sup> But because I have said these things unto you, sorrow hath filled your heart.

- “sorrow hath filled your heart.” He warned the disciples, the men around him, what was going to happen. Sorrow filled their hearts because they knew these people would put them to death thinking they were doing right.

**[John 16:33](/bible/john/16#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/john/16#v33)</sup> These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.

- Nothing can separate us from the love of Christ. “These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation”: we shall have tribulation. “But be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.” Don’t worry about it: if you believe in me, I have overcome this world.
