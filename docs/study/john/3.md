**[John 3:1-3](/bible/john/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/john/3#v1)</sup> There was a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/john/3#v2)</sup> The same came to Jesus by night, and said unto him, Rabbi, we know that thou art a teacher come from God: for no man can do these miracles that thou doest, except God be with him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/john/3#v3)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.

- Nicodemus, a Pharisee and a ruler of the Jews, comes by night. Those men were the teachers of the synagogues, and it would have cost him standing to come openly.
- Except a man be born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God.
- This is spoken to a man who believed himself already right. Sinners and publicans received Christ more readily because they knew they needed a saviour; a ruler of the Jews had to start over.

    If so be that ye will subdue your own understanding, and reform your hearts, ye shall be kept alive, and after death ye shall obtain mercy.

**[John 3:4-5](/bible/john/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/john/3#v4)</sup> Nicodemus saith unto him, How can a man be born when he is old? can he enter the second time into his mother’s womb, and be born?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/john/3#v5)</sup> Jesus answered, Verily, verily, I say unto thee, Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God.

- Nicodemus takes it literally and asks whether a man can enter the second time into his mother's womb.
- Except a man be born of water and of the Spirit, he cannot enter into the kingdom of God. Both are named plainly elsewhere as the word.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 1:23](/bible/1-peter/1#v23)**

    Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.

**[John 3:6-8](/bible/john/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/john/3#v6)</sup> That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/john/3#v7)</sup> Marvel not that I said unto thee, Ye must be born again.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/john/3#v8)</sup> The wind bloweth where it listeth, and thou hearest the sound thereof, but canst not tell whence it cometh, and whither it goeth: so is every one that is born of the Spirit.

- That which is born of the flesh is flesh; and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit.
- A man left in the flesh will do the works of the flesh, and those works are named in the epistles.

> <sup>[9](/bible/john/3#v9)</sup> Nicodemus answered and said unto him, How can these things be?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/john/3#v10)</sup> Jesus answered and said unto him, Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/john/3#v11)</sup> Verily, verily, I say unto thee, We speak that we do know, and testify that we have seen; and ye receive not our witness.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/john/3#v12)</sup> If I have told you earthly things, and ye believe not, how shall ye believe, if I tell you of heavenly things?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/john/3#v13)</sup> And no man hath ascended up to heaven, but he that came down from heaven, even the Son of man which is in heaven.

- Art thou a master of Israel, and knowest not these things? He was a chief teacher, and men depended on him for the understanding of the law.
- If I have told you earthly things, and ye believe not, how shall ye believe, if I tell you of heavenly things?

**[John 3:14-15](/bible/john/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/john/3#v14)</sup> And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/john/3#v15)</sup> That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

- As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up.
- The history behind that is plain, and so is who it was for. It was Israel in the wilderness that spake against the Most High and against Moses, and Israel that was bitten, and Israel that looked and lived.
- The serpent of brass was a sign of salvation, and it came with remembrance of the commandment of the law. Looking on it was not the whole of it; there was repentance with it.

**[John 3:16-18](/bible/john/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/3#v16)</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/john/3#v17)</sup> For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/john/3#v18)</sup> He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God.

- For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son. This follows straight on from the serpent, and it is the same people in view.
- John's own gospel names who the world is: the one he was made manifest to, the one the Pharisees said was gone after him, and the one he taught openly in the synagogue and the temple.

**[John 3:19-21](/bible/john/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/john/3#v19)</sup> And this is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/john/3#v20)</sup> For every one that doeth evil hateth the light, neither cometh to the light, lest his deeds should be reproved.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/john/3#v21)</sup> But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be made manifest, that they are wrought in God.

- This is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil.
- A man can be comfortable and even do well in the darkness he was raised in. Coming into the light requires change, and refusing that change is what condemns him.
- The Pharisees and the rulers were not condemned by Christ; they condemned themselves by not humbling down to repent.
- But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be made manifest, that they are wrought in God.

>

**[John 3:31-36](/bible/john/3#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/john/3#v31)</sup> He that cometh from above is above all: he that is of the earth is earthly, and speaketh of the earth: he that cometh from heaven is above all.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/john/3#v32)</sup> And what he hath seen and heard, that he testifieth; and no man receiveth his testimony.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/john/3#v33)</sup> He that hath received his testimony hath set to his seal that God is true.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/john/3#v34)</sup> For he whom God hath sent speaketh the words of God: for God giveth not the Spirit by measure unto him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/john/3#v35)</sup> The Father loveth the Son, and hath given all things into his hand.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/john/3#v36)</sup> He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him.

- He that hath received his testimony hath set to his seal that God is true. Not to receive it is to call the record God gave of his son a lie.
- He that believeth on the Son hath everlasting life: and he that believeth not the Son shall not see life; but the wrath of God abideth on him.
