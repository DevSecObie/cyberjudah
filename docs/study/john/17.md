## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 17 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 8, 11, 14-24, 26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 17:8](/bible/john/17#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/john/17#v8)</sup> For I have given unto them the words which thou gavest me; and they have received them, and have known surely that I came out from thee, and they have believed that thou didst send me.

- “For I have given unto them the words which thou gavest me; and they have received them, and have known surely that I came out from thee.” Christ came out of the Father.

**[John 17:11](/bible/john/17#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/john/17#v11)</sup> And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.

- “Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me.” There is an elect group of Israelites who are Christ's children, and the rest are evil negroes that are going to be destroyed. “That they may be one, as we are.” So what happened to Christ also applies to his children, because the Father and the Son are one, and we are one with our father, Christ.

**[John 17:14](/bible/john/17#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/john/17#v14)</sup> I have given them thy word; and the world hath hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.

- Christ himself said something a lot of us read every day and don't understand. “I have given them thy word; and the world hath hated them.” Automatic: you have the word of God, you better be ready for Satan. The world is going to hit you, brother; your family is going to hate you. A lot of people talk about stock and how good the world is to them. We try it and see how far we get, because we are not of this world. What is set up in this world is not for us; we just have to survive in it.
- “Because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.” You are not of the world, brothers and sisters, so you have to shake that dust.

**[John 17:15](/bible/john/17#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/john/17#v15)</sup> I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.

- “I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world.” Did not Christ know that by giving us his word the world was going to hit us? Then why did he not take us out? Because he knew there is a path, and we have to go through it. Christ understood the world would hit us for the gospel, and that is why he says, I pray for them, “that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.”
- We are going to face evil. Look at America today, look at the world today: it is evil. Christ prayed that we not be taken out of the world of evil because we have a path, and our job is to stand for what's right. From Adam all the way to now we have been fighting good against evil. That is what the battle is, and that is what the world is all about.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 94:16](/bible/psalms/94#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/94#v16)</sup> Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?

    Christ prayed for us and left us in the world, because the world is evil and there are things we have to fight within it to get back where we came from. We are not of this world, so we must suffer the evil that comes upon us in this world, to overcome it and go back to the paradise we came from. This is not our home and not our rulership; it is an evil rulership upon this earth. That is why, when we stand for what's right, they put us to death.

**[John 17:16](/bible/john/17#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/john/17#v16)</sup> They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.

- “They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.” We are not of the world, even as Christ is not of the world.

**[John 17:17](/bible/john/17#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/john/17#v17)</sup> Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.

- “Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.” This is how we cleanse: through the word of God. Every man must take this word seriously, because this is how you are cleansed from the world that is evil, the world that is going to hate you. You have to become a beam of light in that world that hates you for standing for what's right.
- So how are you going to give your wink up? Where are you standing, for what's right or for what's evil? Your people are in captivity, and you talk about giving your wink up. Why are you getting weaker every day? That is what the Sabbath and class are for: to build. If you give that up, who is going to build you up? Satan.

**[John 17:18](/bible/john/17#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/john/17#v18)</sup> As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I also sent them into the world.

- “As thou hast sent me into the world, even so have I also sent them into the world.” God the Father sent his Son into the world, and the world was evil when Christ was here. Christ acknowledged how evil the world was, and he said, I pray for them.

**[John 17:19](/bible/john/17#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/john/17#v19)</sup> And for their sakes I sanctify myself, that they also might be sanctified through the truth.

- “And for their sakes I sanctify myself, that they also might be sanctified through the truth”: the truth, which is the word. That is how we are going to be sanctified.

**[John 17:20-23](/bible/john/17#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/john/17#v20)</sup> Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/john/17#v21)</sup> That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us: that the world may believe that thou hast sent me.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/john/17#v22)</sup> And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them; that they may be one, even as we are one:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/john/17#v23)</sup> I in them, and thou in me, that they may be made perfect in one; and that the world may know that thou hast sent me, and hast loved them, as thou hast loved me.

- “Neither pray I for these alone, but for them also which shall believe on me through their word.” We are in a time of evil, and Christ prayed for us, because he understood the time we would be in. Likewise we, as leaders, pray for you, and you pray for us, so that we stay focused. It is the same thing.
- “That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee, that they also may be one in us.” That is what the whole thing is about: we are sent into a world of evil.
- “That they all may be one; as thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee.” “And the glory which thou gavest me I have given them.” Christ was put to death and received into glory, and so will his children be, because of provocation. “That they may be made perfect in one”: knit as one man, no schism, no division.
- “As thou, Father, art in me, and I in thee”: this is the tabernacle. The Father dwells in the Son. “That they also may be one in us”: we are all in one accord.

**[John 17:24](/bible/john/17#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/john/17#v24)</sup> Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am; that they may behold my glory, which thou hast given me: for thou lovedst me before the foundation of the world.

- “Father, I will that they also, whom thou hast given me, be with me where I am; that they may behold my glory.”

**[John 17:26](/bible/john/17#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/john/17#v26)</sup> And I have declared unto them thy name, and will declare it: that the love wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them, and I in them.

- “That the love wherewith thou hast loved me may be in them, and I in them.” I in them, once again: the tabernacle. Christ dwells in his people.
