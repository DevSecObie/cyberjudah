## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 18 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2, 4-6, 8-11, 20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 18:2](/bible/john/18#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/john/18#v2)</sup> And Judas also, which betrayed him, knew the place: for Jesus ofttimes resorted thither with his disciples.

- “And Judas also, which betrayed him, knew the place”. We have to be mindful. Judas knew their secret hideout, where they used to go with the disciples.

**[John 18:4-6](/bible/john/18#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/john/18#v4)</sup> Jesus therefore, knowing all things that should come upon him, went forth, and said unto them, Whom seek ye?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/john/18#v5)</sup> They answered him, Jesus of Nazareth. Jesus saith unto them, I am he. And Judas also, which betrayed him, stood with them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/john/18#v6)</sup> As soon then as he had said unto them, I am he, they went backward, and fell to the ground.

- “Whom seek ye?” Who are you looking for? “Jesus of Nazareth.” “I am he.” “they went backward, and fell to the ground.” All of them went backwards.

**[John 18:8-9](/bible/john/18#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/john/18#v8)</sup> Jesus answered, I have told you that I am he: if therefore ye seek me, let these go their way:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/john/18#v9)</sup> That the saying might be fulfilled, which he spake, Of them which thou gavest me have I lost none.

- “I have told you that I am he”. He was flexing on them. I told you I'm him. “if therefore ye seek me, let these go their way”. “Of them which thou gavest me have I lost none.” Christ stood up for his people. None of them got lost; he made sure none of the disciples got taken away.

**[John 18:10-11](/bible/john/18#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/john/18#v10)</sup> Then Simon Peter having a sword drew it, and smote the high priest’s servant, and cut off his right ear. The servant’s name was Malchus.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/john/18#v11)</sup> Then said Jesus unto Peter, Put up thy sword into the sheath: the cup which my Father hath given me, shall I not drink it?

- “Then Simon Peter having a sword drew it, and smote the high priest’s servant, and cut off his right ear.” Peter wanted to do a good deed, pulled out the knife, and cut his ear off. “The servant’s name was Malchus.” “Put up thy sword into the sheath: the cup which my Father hath given me, shall I not drink it?”
- Christ did a good deed making sure none of the disciples got taken away. Peter came around trying to do a heroic act and cut the dude's ear off. Don't you understand I have to drink this cup? Peter didn't understand. He was trying to stop the mission through his good deed, and that good deed turned out to be a bad result.
