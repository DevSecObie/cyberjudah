These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on John 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 26, 28-36, 44, 51-53, 56-59, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[John 8:26](/bible/john/8#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/john/8#v26)</sup> I have many things to say and to judge of you: but he that sent me is true; and I speak to the world those things which I have heard of him.

- “I speak to the world those things which I have heard of him.” Christ is not speaking to the entire planet. That world is the nation of Israel.

**[John 8:28](/bible/john/8#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/john/8#v28)</sup> Then said Jesus unto them, When ye have lifted up the Son of man, then shall ye know that I am he, and that I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things.

- “When ye have lifted up the Son of man”: the Son of man is Christ, speaking about himself. “I do nothing of myself; but as my Father hath taught me, I speak these things.” That cuts the unholy trinity doctrine.

**[John 8:29](/bible/john/8#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/john/8#v29)</sup> And he that sent me is with me: the Father hath not left me alone; for I do always those things that please him.

- “He that sent me is with me”: the Father. Christ is our God, and Christ says nothing different than what the heavenly Father says. Christ said he will never leave you nor forsake you, and in like manner the Father told Christ the same. They are not against each other; they are of the same spirit, in agreement. That again cuts the unholy trinity, which is a new thing upon the earth.
- “The Father hath not left me alone; for I do always those things that please him.” Our brothers and sisters don’t want to do anything that pleases the heavenly Father, not one law. But they will shuck and jive to please their boss and please the other nations. When it comes to the laws of God, they want nothing to do with them.
