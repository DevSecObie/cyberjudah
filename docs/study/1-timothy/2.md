## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Timothy 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 11-15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Timothy 2:11-12](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)</sup> Let the woman learn in silence with all subjection.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/2#v12)</sup> But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man, but to be in silence.

- A woman is to learn in silence with all subjection. Paul does not suffer a woman to teach or usurp authority over the man. Shut the hell up; do not bring that spirit up in here. He is undoing the order of Diana worship among converts in Ephesus. A woman learning the Bible is right; a woman using it to take authority over the man is the problem.
- But women rule, the black woman is the goddess of the universe: Paul said I suffer not a woman to teach because they wanted to bring those same characteristics into Israel. Feminism.
- The Bible calls idols devils because they give out a deceiving spirit of role reversal. Diana of the Ephesians today is called Wonder Woman, put throughout the commercials, the cartoons, the movies, the woman be whooping the hell out of men left and right; they’re changing all the male superheroes into female superheroes. Iron Man’s going to be a girl, Wolverine a girl, Thor a girl; they made the black woman the queen of Asgard. America is a compilation of every captivity we were under, Babylon, Greece, Persia, Rome, and it all goes back to feminism, worshipping the females.
- “But I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man, but to be in silence.” That was the problem right there. Satan told Eve that the day she listened to him her eyes would be opened and she would be just like her husband: Adam was already a god, and she could be a god like him. The temptation was her being able to be over her husband. Under Adam, Eve thought she was Adam’s equal, and he had to correct that BS.

**[1 Timothy 2:13-14](/bible/1-timothy/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-timothy/2#v13)</sup> For Adam was first formed, then Eve.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-timothy/2#v14)</sup> And Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived was in the transgression.

- Adam was formed first, then Eve. Adam was not deceived, but the woman was deceived and was in the transgression. Paul gives the reason from the beginning; it is not simply a local custom with no spiritual basis. The order of creation and the fall explain why the woman must not usurp the man.
- “For Adam was first formed, then Eve.” It goes right back to Adam: she came out of Adam.
- “And Adam was not deceived.” Satan could not come to Adam, because he was already a god, made lord of all the creatures, as the Apocrypha says. Eve was supposed to be his comfort, his aid, his charger. “But the woman being deceived was in the transgression”: the transgression was that she listened to Satan to overthrow her husband. Rome is doing the same thing today, using our women against us for their benefit, as Eve was used for Satan’s benefit.
- “For Adam was first formed, then Eve.” He took it all the way back to the beginning of the Bible: the reason for the woman to be silent is that there is order with God.

**[1 Timothy 2:15](/bible/1-timothy/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-timothy/2#v15)</sup> Notwithstanding she shall be saved in childbearing, if they continue in faith and charity and holiness with sobriety.

- It does not mean, sisters, just pop out a baby and you’re going to be saved. Half of you are single mothers; you ain’t going to be saved just on the fact that you can pop out a baby. The conditions follow: continue in faith, charity, holiness and sobriety. Holiness, meaning the commandments; with sobriety: wisdom. The pain of childbirth recalls Eve's transgression and the order God pronounced afterward. A woman must keep the faith and God's order, not merely give birth.
- “Notwithstanding she shall be saved in childbearing, if they continue in faith and charity and holiness with sobriety.” If they continue in the faith of obeying her husband, because that is the way God set it up. With sobriety: don’t be crazy, listening to some foolish Dr. Buzzard stuff telling you to be against your husband. That is out of order. The Most High gave the woman labor pains as a remembrance of her place under her husband.
- And to ask why we have to be under him: no other woman does this. When a thief comes to break into the house, do you want to be the first one to deal with him, or do you want the man to come to the door with the stuff? It would be horrible to see a man jump behind his woman and push her in front of him. So sisters, be under that man in righteousness.
- “Notwithstanding she shall be saved in childbearing, if they continue in faith and charity and holiness with sobriety.” The woman will be saved in childbearing. If she continue in faith, meaning if she believe, and if she deal right with the congregation, deal right with her fellow sisters, deal right with her brothers.
