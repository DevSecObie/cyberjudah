## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Timothy 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-7, 11-12, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Timothy 3:1](/bible/1-timothy/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/3#v1)</sup> This is a true saying, If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work.

- “If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work.” The qualification to lead the church of God, to lead the nation of Israel, is that your house has to be in order before anything else. You are no good as a leader if your house is in disruption and out of order.
- This is why marriage is put first. From the beginning Satan attacked the marriage, the union of Adam and Eve, and from that came everything else: once Eve rebelled against her husband Adam, all hell broke loose. Every other sin is pyramiding from that pinnacle. To redeem the nation we have to go to the root, the union between husband and wife, or we will never get away from immorality and never reach civility. A strong marriage is a strong foundation for the building of a nation, and the camps out there in disarray, always on this doctrine of multiple hoes and all this madness, never teach about marriage.
- Now that we’ve awakened, now that we’ve come into the little sanctuaries, it’s time to report for duty. “If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work.”
- Listen up, brothers. “This is a true saying, If a man desire the office of a bishop, he desireth a good work.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 37:4-5](/bible/psalms/37#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/37#v4)</sup> Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/37#v5)</sup> Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.

    “Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.” This is not a promise that every trivial personal wish becomes the measure of pleasing God. Delight in the Lord must align the desires themselves with what he wants. In desiring a bishop's work, desire the qualities and responsibilities that make a man useful to his people and fit for the kingdom.

**[1 Timothy 3:2-6](/bible/1-timothy/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/3#v2)</sup> A bishop then must be blameless, the husband of one wife, vigilant, sober, of good behaviour, given to hospitality, apt to teach;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/3#v3)</sup> Not given to wine, no striker, not greedy of filthy lucre; but patient, not a brawler, not covetous;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/3#v4)</sup> One that ruleth well his own house, having his children in subjection with all gravity;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/3#v5)</sup> (For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?)
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-timothy/3#v6)</sup> Not a novice, lest being lifted up with pride he fall into the condemnation of the devil.

- “One that ruleth well his own house”: this is how leaders have to be, and leaders means men, period. People try to escape the role by saying, I’m not a bishop, I’m not a deacon, I’m just a regular man. If you are a man, you are made to lead. How can people cut out parts of this and say it is not talking about them? What are you, a homo? A man is supposed to want to fulfil everything a real man does: rule, in righteousness of course, but rule.
- “Having his children in subjection with all gravity”: you have to be over your household, brothers, and allow no disrespect or disobedience in keeping God’s commandments. Don’t let anyone, family members or anybody else, come inside your home and defile your house and your family. That is why the man is the one coming to the door with the tool: you have to protect what is behind you. All men are charged with this.
- “For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?” If you can’t rule your own house, you can’t rule anything. You can’t deal with the church of God, and you won’t be in the kingdom. Wimps and men who don’t want to do what the Most High instructs men to do are not going to be in the kingdom; that is all going to be burnt up. The only men in the kingdom will be real men who stand up for God’s commandments, so we had better get used to it now.
- “One that ruleth well his own house.” “For if a man know not how to rule his own house, how shall he take care of the church of God?” All leadership, and all the civility we are trying to bring out, has to be built on that foundation.

**[1 Timothy 3:7](/bible/1-timothy/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-timothy/3#v7)</sup> Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without; lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.

- “Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without”: those that are not in the truth, “lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.”
- “Moreover he must have a good report of them which are without”. Nobody in the world should have anything evil to say about you. Many in the world will lie on us, but nothing they say should they be able to back up. So if you do business in the world you must be twice as honorable, and if you are, you get more jobs, because nobody likes a thief and everybody wants to save a dollar. A developer will throw jobs at an honest man left and right. Penny wise, pound foolish: you make an extra penny on this job and lose the whole pound, and people stop calling you without saying why, because they saw what you tried to pull.
- That is no way to live: ducking people, no longer going down a certain street, circling the block three times when you come home looking at every parked car, afraid somebody will hop out asking where's his money. Some dudes will pistol whip you or stab you up for twenty dollars, and it's about the principle.
- “Lest he fall into reproach and the snare of the devil.” The devil traps you: he convinced you it was okay to lie, okay to steal, okay to be covetous, and at the end he removes himself, and the people you owe show up. After the beating you still have to give back the money, so why go through that? Deal righteously.
