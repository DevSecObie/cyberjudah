## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Timothy 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, 8-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Timothy 5:1](/bible/1-timothy/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/5#v1)</sup> Rebuke not an elder, but intreat him as a father; and the younger men as brethren;

- This shows how broken men and women coming in are to be built, and during the Men’s Conference it will be gone into in great detail. “Rebuke not an elder, but intreat him as a father”: you are to entreat us as your fathers. “And the younger men as brethren”: see the young men as your brothers.
- “Rebuke not an elder, but intreat him as a father”. An elder began at 50. “And the younger men as brethren”.

**[1 Timothy 5:2](/bible/1-timothy/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/5#v2)</sup> The elder women as mothers; the younger as sisters, with all purity.

- “The elder women as mothers”: see the elder women as mothers, “the younger as sisters”: and the young women as sisters.
- All this goes back to the fifth commandment, honour your father and mother. That goes literal and it also goes spiritual.
- “The elder women as mothers; the younger as sisters, with all purity.”

**[1 Timothy 5:3](/bible/1-timothy/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/5#v3)</sup> Honour widows that are widows indeed.

- What is a widow? You may say a sister whose husband died. But “Honour widows that are widows indeed.” She has to be a widow indeed, and Paul breaks down what that means.

**[1 Timothy 5:4](/bible/1-timothy/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/5#v4)</sup> But if any widow have children or nephews, let them learn first to shew piety at home, and to requite their parents: for that is good and acceptable before God.

- If a widow has children or nephews, they are to “shew piety at home, and to requite their parents”. Her own family comes first.

**[1 Timothy 5:5](/bible/1-timothy/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/5#v5)</sup> Now she that is a widow indeed, and desolate, trusteth in God, and continueth in supplications and prayers night and day.

- “Now she that is a widow indeed, and desolate, trusteth in God, and continueth in supplications and prayers night and day.” A widow had a duty. She trusted in God and continued in supplications and prayers night and day.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 2:36-37](/bible/luke/2#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/luke/2#v36)</sup> And there was one Anna, a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Aser: she was of a great age, and had lived with an husband seven years from her virginity;
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/luke/2#v37)</sup> And she was a widow of about fourscore and four years, which departed not from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day.

    An example of a widow indeed: Anna, “a prophetess, the daughter of Phanuel, of the tribe of Aser”. She “had lived with an husband seven years from her virginity; And she was a widow of about fourscore and four years, which departed not from the temple, but served God with fastings and prayers night and day.”

**[1 Timothy 5:6](/bible/1-timothy/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-timothy/5#v6)</sup> But she that liveth in pleasure is dead while she liveth.

- “But she that liveth in pleasure is dead while she liveth.” The widows made a kind of vow to dedicate themselves to the Lord and pray night and day for the benefit of Israel. She that lives in pleasure has reneged on that.

**[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

- The woman in the clip made the argument that the father was never around, never came to her school, never paid child support. If he did not pay child support there are only a few reasons for it, and the main one is that the sister does not know who the baby daddy is. If she knew who the baby daddy is, she would take the child support immediately, like the average woman does. So why did she not go after him for any of it?
- But we do not advocate men not taking care of their kids. “But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.” That is what we teach, that is what we preach, that is what we apply. We advocate for that thing right there.
- And some sisters have decided they do not want to be subject to their husband. They have ridiculed him, called him names, given his children to other people and taken pictures of them to mock their husband. We have seen it all.
- “But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.” If a widow has family, her family is to look after her first and foremost: her kin, her relatives.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 29:21-22](/bible/sirach/29#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/29#v21)</sup> The chief thing for life is water, and bread, and clothing, and an house to cover shame.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/29#v22)</sup> Better is the life of a poor man in a mean cottage, than delicate fare in another man’s house.

    This is your purpose: to be an example, to be blameless, to rule your own house, to teach your wife, to teach your children and guide them. “The chief thing for life is water, and bread, and clothing, and an house to cover shame.” You’re supposed to be providing the chief things in life for your family.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 25:22](/bible/sirach/25#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/25#v22)</sup> A woman, if she maintain her husband, is full of anger, impudence, and much reproach.

    “A woman, if she maintain her husband, is full of anger, impudence, and much reproach.” The application is wider than a deadbeat who will not work. A wife can be left maintaining the man's role in direction, judgment, stability and responsibility even while he earns money. She may try to fill the vacuum, but that does not remove what he was supposed to supply.

**[1 Timothy 5:9-10](/bible/1-timothy/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/5#v9)</sup> Let not a widow be taken into the number under threescore years old, having been the wife of one man,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-timothy/5#v10)</sup> Well reported of for good works; if she have brought up children, if she have lodged strangers, if she have washed the saints’ feet, if she have relieved the afflicted, if she have diligently followed every good work.

- “Let not a widow be taken into the number”: there was a widowhood of sisters whose job was to pray for Israel, fasting and praying night and day. She did not just sit around saying, my husband died, I’m a widow. She had a duty, an obligation.
- To be taken into the number she had to be a widow indeed, not “under threescore years old”, and “the wife of one man”, married to only one man in her life. She had to be “well reported of for good works”: she raised children; she “lodged strangers”, the brothers who came to the city to visit and stayed at her house; she “washed the saints’ feet”, “relieved the afflicted”, and “diligently followed every good work.”

**[1 Timothy 5:11-12](/bible/1-timothy/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-timothy/5#v11)</sup> But the younger widows refuse: for when they have begun to wax wanton against Christ, they will marry;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/5#v12)</sup> Having damnation, because they have cast off their first faith.

- “But the younger widows refuse”: do not take care of them, do not provide for them. The ones under sixty, not them. “For when they have begun to wax wanton against Christ, they will marry”. Wanton means lustful, sexually active, against Christ. She gave up the vow to dedicate her life to praying and fasting night and day; she got that itch and wants a man.
- “Having damnation, because they have cast off their first faith.” The widowhood was nothing they took lightly. When she dedicated her life to God with prayers and fastings night and day, that was a serious thing; she could not just say, I don’t want to do this no more.

**[1 Timothy 5:13](/bible/1-timothy/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-timothy/5#v13)</sup> And withal they learn to be idle, wandering about from house to house; and not only idle, but tattlers also and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not.

- This is what the young widows will be doing: “wandering about from house to house; and not only idle, but tattlers also and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not.” Tattlers are gossipers.

**[1 Timothy 5:14-15](/bible/1-timothy/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-timothy/5#v14)</sup> I will therefore that the younger women marry, bear children, guide the house, give none occasion to the adversary to speak reproachfully.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-timothy/5#v15)</sup> For some are already turned aside after Satan.

- “I will therefore that the younger women marry, bear children, guide the house”. Get a husband, sister, get married. “For some are already turned aside after Satan.”

**[1 Timothy 5:16](/bible/1-timothy/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-timothy/5#v16)</sup> If any man or woman that believeth have widows, let them relieve them, and let not the church be charged; that it may relieve them that are widows indeed.

- “If any man or woman that believeth have widows, let them relieve them, and let not the church be charged”. If any believer in Christ has widows in the family, you take care of her: her son, her daughter, her uncle, her brother. Don’t burden the church, “that it may relieve them that are widows indeed”, the widows who have no family to help them. The church will take care of them.

**[1 Timothy 5:17-18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-timothy/5#v17)</sup> Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in the word and doctrine.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)</sup> For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn. And, The labourer is worthy of his reward.

- “Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in the word and doctrine.” Elders are to be honoured, and the elder begins at 50. “For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn. And, The labourer is worthy of his reward.”
- “Let the elders that rule well be counted worthy of double honour, especially they who labour in the word and doctrine.” To rule well is to be an example to the men, to the women, and especially to the young men. The Bishop has been in this for over twenty years, putting his bricks in, longer than some of you have been alive.
- Those who labour, who put works into the word and doctrine, are counted worthy of double honour. We come from a society where it is fine to disrespect the black man, and that cannot be allowed here. Whoever comes in to disrespect our brothers, especially our elders, will get a warning, maybe two, and then has to go. You might as well go back to that Christian church.
- The fourth goal was to discredit the groups and individuals, groups, not only individuals, to three segments: to the responsible negro community, the brothers and sisters with the degrees and good jobs who have influence and could help; to the white community and the liberals, and we don’t care what the white man thinks or need his money, because we have the Bible and made it work ourselves as in Acts 2 and Acts 4; and to the so-called radicals. They have it down to a T how to put us in a negative light.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 19:32](/bible/leviticus/19#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/leviticus/19#v32)</sup> Thou shalt rise up before the hoary head, and honour the face of the old man, and fear thy God: I am the Lord.

    Double honour for the elders. Brothers ask why they have to stand up when the older men come into the room. Because black people don’t know honor and don’t know respect. It used to be that when the older people spoke, you shut up or got smacked in the mouth, and all that is gone today.
  - **[Isaiah 3:5](/bible/isaiah/3#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/3#v5)</sup> And the people shall be oppressed, every one by another, and every one by his neighbour: the child shall behave himself proudly against the ancient, and the base against the honourable.

    “And the people shall be oppressed, every one by another, and every one by his neighbour: the child shall behave himself proudly against the ancient”, meaning the old, “and the base against the honourable”: the base are the low kids. What was read of back then is happening today: no respect for the elders in the black and brown community.

**[1 Timothy 5:19](/bible/1-timothy/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-timothy/5#v19)</sup> Against an elder receive not an accusation, but before two or three witnesses.

- “Against an elder receive not an accusation, but before two or three witnesses.” The same law and the same rules pertain to all of us; it is no different with an elder. If there are two or three witnesses, bring it out.

**[1 Timothy 5:20-21](/bible/1-timothy/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-timothy/5#v20)</sup> Them that sin rebuke before all, that others also may fear.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-timothy/5#v21)</sup> I charge thee before God, and the Lord Jesus Christ, and the elect angels, that thou observe these things without preferring one before another, doing nothing by partiality.

- “Them that sin rebuke before all, that others also may fear.” “Without preferring one before another, doing nothing by partiality.”
