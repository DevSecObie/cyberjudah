## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Timothy 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-5, 8, 13-16, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Timothy 4:1-3](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)</sup> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/4#v2)</sup> Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/4#v3)</sup> Forbidding to marry, and commanding to abstain from meats, which God hath created to be received with thanksgiving of them which believe and know the truth.

- How do we know the idols are Christianity and not Islam? This is the prophecy of the world’s last religion that they are going to force on us, and some of you are going to give yourselves to it.
- “Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times”: this is the latter time we are living in. “Some shall depart from the faith”: some of you men, some of you women, shall depart from this faith. That is being fed with things offered unto idols. “Giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils.”
- The religion of the false prophet starts with Christianity: number one, Christianity. “Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times”: we are in the latter times now. “Some shall depart from the faith”: some men and women listening will depart from this faith, from the truth of Christ. Some will see signs and wonders and depart; some will be persecuted and depart. It is already written.
- “Giving heed to seducing spirits”: that goes back to the false Christs and false prophets of Matthew 24:24. “And doctrines of devils.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 24:10-11](/bible/matthew/24#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)</sup> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/24#v11)</sup> And many false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many.

    “And then shall many be offended”: many of you shall be offended. What are you going to do? “And shall betray one another”: you are going to betray the believing brothers and sisters. “And shall hate one another”: you shall hate us.
  - **[Mark 13:22](/bible/mark/13#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/mark/13#v22)</sup> For false Christs and false prophets shall rise, and shall shew signs and wonders, to seduce, if it were possible, even the elect.

    “For false Christs and false prophets shall rise”: TikTok, Instagram. “And shall shew signs and wonders.” These Christians under fake Israel’s banner get millions of dollars, and America works her science for Israel. Remember that Revelation called the fire from heaven wonders on the earth.
  - **[Matthew 24:24-25](/bible/matthew/24#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/24#v24)</sup> For there shall arise false Christs, and false prophets, and shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/24#v25)</sup> Behold, I have told you before.

    The seducing spirits go back to what was read earlier: “For there shall arise false Christs, and false prophets, and shall shew great signs and wonders; insomuch that, if it were possible, they shall deceive the very elect. Behold, I have told you before.” Meaning, I warned you. Some will see those signs and wonders and depart from the faith.

**[1 Timothy 4:4](/bible/1-timothy/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/4#v4)</sup> For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving:

- “For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving”. Somebody is still struggling with that verse. People who believe Christ is black and believe they are Israelites still say, everything is good, I can eat whatever I want, just pray on it. Can I pray over a human foot and eat it?
- Every creature is good for the work it was made to do. Pork carries the worm called trichinella, which gets into the muscles and even the brain; a man from Argentina had part of his brain missing from it. The Most High can release the worms on you and expose you for eating pork on the low, the same way brothers get caught in every other sin.
- Shrimp, lobsters and all the shellfish clean the ocean. Anyone who has kept a fish tank knows what the filter looks like when you change it; cook that and you are eating shrimp. Bottom feeders like catfish, snails and shrimp are natural tank cleaners. Put crabs or any bottom feeder in murky, dirty water and in a few hours it will be crystal clear, because that is their job; that is why shrimp are called the roaches of the sea. A pleco in a fish tank is never fed, yet it grows bigger than the other fish, because it eats the algae and the poop of the other fish. Their key purpose is waste management. How can you eat something whose job is to get rid of waste and expect it to do you good? That is where the gout, the high cholesterol and the hypertension come from, and brothers lose fingers and feet to it. City Island seafood, Massachusetts clam chowder: don’t do that thing. If you eat something that eats garbage, it is going to make you sick. But we are too greedy, and even in the truth, some who no longer eat that garbage still love food too much.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 1:25](/bible/genesis/1#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/1#v25)</sup> And God made the beast of the earth after his kind, and cattle after their kind, and every thing that creepeth upon the earth after his kind: and God saw that it was good.

    “And God made the beast of the earth after his kind, and cattle after their kind, and every thing that creepeth upon the earth after his kind: and God saw that it was good.” This is what makes every creature good: each is good to serve its purpose, not good to eat.

**[1 Timothy 4:5](/bible/1-timothy/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/4#v5)</sup> For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.

- “For it is sanctified by the word of God and prayer.” The Christians never read on to this verse. A creature is not made clean by praying over it. It is sanctified by the word of God, so you have to go to the word and see which animals were sanctified to be eaten and which were not.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 11:44-47](/bible/leviticus/11#v44)**
    > <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/11#v44)</sup> For I am the Lord your God: ye shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/11#v45)</sup> For I am the Lord that bringeth you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.
    >
    > <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/11#v46)</sup> This is the law of the beasts, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/11#v47)</sup> To make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.

    Here is where the animals were sanctified by the word of God. “Ye shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.” He says it again in verse 45: “ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.”
  - **[Romans 7:12](/bible/romans/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/romans/7#v12)</sup> Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.

    How do you become holy, as Leviticus commands? “Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy”.

**[1 Timothy 4:8](/bible/1-timothy/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/4#v8)</sup> For bodily exercise profiteth little: but godliness is profitable unto all things, having promise of the life that now is, and of that which is to come.

- Choose your hard: be disciplined to control your eating and exercise. Many people read “For bodily exercise profiteth little” and conclude there is no value in it. But a man may make a little money that profits him a good amount. That little is a lot. Control your eating and do a little exercise, and it will go a very long way.
- You need a strong constitution to keep going; your bones have to be strong enough to carry you for a long time. If you never eat what strengthens your bones and never work out your muscles, you will end up on crutches, in a wheelchair, in a hospital bed, deteriorating slowly because you won’t listen to the laws of God and won’t control your mouth. You can’t be on a seafood diet, seeing food and eating it everywhere you go.
- Yes, you need to exercise, but the main thing is your diet. One chicken sandwich, large fries, a milkshake and a Coke would take about 40,000 steps, around 20 miles of walking, to burn off, and that is not the only thing you ate that day. Working out keeps your constitution and your organs strong; you lose weight by eating less. Snacking is the biggest problem. And no soda, not even diet: it decalcifies the bones, and you want strong bones to live longer.

**[1 Timothy 4:14](/bible/1-timothy/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-timothy/4#v14)</sup> Neglect not the gift that is in thee, which was given thee by prophecy, with the laying on of the hands of the presbytery.

- “Neglect not the gift that is in thee.” That neglect is what we all do. Some don’t yet know what gift is in them. We all have at least the gift of receiving this word; many of our people will fight to the death to say they are Gentiles and not Israelites, and that alone should move you with compassion and frustration, not at them but at where their minds are. Beyond that there are diversities of gifts. Don’t you want to know your role and purpose?
- “With the laying on of the hands of the presbytery.” The presbytery is the elders of the church. Laying on of hands is not only healing; it is the elders setting men in positions and praying over them, as when captains and up are promoted and anointed, called into something greater by God through those men.

**[1 Timothy 4:15](/bible/1-timothy/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-timothy/4#v15)</sup> Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them; that thy profiting may appear to all.

- “Meditate upon these things; give thyself wholly to them.” This is the part we all miss: one foot in and one foot out. If the fire is foreign to you, you have not given yourself wholly. In the stocks is when Jeremiah gave himself wholly; he stopped resisting.
- In basic training the TI told the recruits they sucked because they were resisting what they were trying to make them, and until they stopped resisting they would keep sucking and some would not make it. Once you embrace it and give yourself wholly, it is still demanding, but you are no longer fighting it. Those still holding on to all their BS trivial things have not given themselves wholly. Take a moral inventory of your desires and ask whether you have given yourself wholly to God and to this mission.
- “That thy profiting may appear to all”: not for selfish reasons, but for all.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 12:7](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v7)</sup> But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.

    “But the manifestation of the Spirit is given to every man to profit withal.” When you give yourself wholly, that manifestation of the spirit is given to each of us. What we read in Jeremiah 20 and Luke 24 was that manifestation for those men in that moment.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 38:24-25](/bible/sirach/38#v24)**

**[1 Timothy 4:16](/bible/1-timothy/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-timothy/4#v16)</sup> Take heed unto thyself, and unto the doctrine; continue in them: for in doing this thou shalt both save thyself, and them that hear thee.

- “Take heed unto thyself, and unto the doctrine; continue in them: for in doing this thou shalt both save thyself, and them that hear thee.” That is the fire inside. When you commit this way you not only save yourself, you become profitable for your people. It is two things we must focus on.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)</sup> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.

    “Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.” It is the same as “thou shalt both save thyself, and them that hear thee.” Many think they are here only for their own salvation. Yes, you stand alone and seek your own, but you also seek for others. That is the duality: all of us putting ourselves on the line for each other. That is fire, intensity, zeal and passion, and it will take us to the next level.
