## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Baruch 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-9, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Baruch 5:1-2](/bible/baruch/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/baruch/5#v1)</sup> Put off, O Jerusalem, the garment of thy mourning and affliction, and put on the comeliness of the glory that cometh from God for ever.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/baruch/5#v2)</sup> Cast about thee a double garment of the righteousness which cometh from God; and set a diadem on thine head of the glory of the Everlasting.

- Baruch is a very heavy book: few chapters, but a lot in it, and it is basically Jeremiah’s words that Baruch wrote down.
- “Put off, O Jerusalem, the garment of thy mourning and affliction, and put on the comeliness of the glory that cometh from God for ever”: put off, O Jews, the garment of mourning and affliction. “Cast about thee a double garment of the righteousness which cometh from God; and set a diadem on thine head of the glory of the Everlasting.”

**[Baruch 5:3](/bible/baruch/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/baruch/5#v3)</sup> For God will shew thy brightness unto every country under heaven.

- “For God will shew thy brightness unto every country under heaven.” God is going to put Israel on top of the planet as a ruling nation of people on the earth, and then we will teach the nations. That is the time to do it, when we are ruling; not while we are captive, refugees, scattered and dispersed. That is what Baruch said in 4:3: don’t do that.
- When Baruch wrote this, Babylon had overthrown us, and Jeremiah was travelling to our brothers and sisters who were refugees in Egypt, upper and lower Egypt, hiding from and running from the Babylonians. When you are captive and brought low, it is not the time to teach these nations. Our goal is to rule, and once we are ruling, then we teach the nations.

**[Baruch 5:4](/bible/baruch/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/baruch/5#v4)</sup> For thy name shall be called of God for ever, The peace of righteousness, and The glory of God’s worship.

- “For thy name shall be called of God for ever, The peace of righteousness, and The glory of God’s worship.”

**[Baruch 5:5](/bible/baruch/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/baruch/5#v5)</sup> Arise, O Jerusalem, and stand on high, and look about toward the east, and behold thy children gathered from the west unto the east by the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the remembrance of God.

- “Arise, O Jerusalem”: that is the context. “And stand on high”: where is that? Back in verse 3, unto every country under heaven. We are going to stand on high.
- “And look about toward the east, and behold thy children gathered from the west unto the east”: from this side, back to our homeland again, from the west to the east, “by the word of the Holy One, rejoicing in the remembrance of God.”

**[Baruch 5:6](/bible/baruch/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/baruch/5#v6)</sup> For they departed from thee on foot, and were led away of their enemies: but God bringeth them unto thee exalted with glory, as children of the kingdom.

- “For they departed from thee on foot, and were led away of their enemies: but God bringeth them unto thee exalted with glory, as children of the kingdom.” Keep in mind the children gathered from the west unto the east; it comes up again. The children of the kingdom gathered and exalted with glory are God’s sons and daughters. That is not all nations; all nations are not gathered from the west to the east.

**[Baruch 5:7](/bible/baruch/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/baruch/5#v7)</sup> For God hath appointed that every high hill, and banks of long continuance, should be cast down, and valleys filled up, to make even the ground, that Israel may go safely in the glory of God.

- “For God hath appointed that every high hill, and banks of long continuance, should be cast down, and valleys filled up, to make even the ground, that Israel may go safely in the glory of God.”
- Every high hill means the governing powers, the nations. The banks of long continuance are the rivers, the streams: that is the nations. They will be cast down, and Daniel 7:9 shows the same thing: the thrones cast down.
- Back to Baruch 5: “For God hath appointed that every high hill, and banks of long continuance, should be cast down, and valleys filled up, to make even the ground, that Israel may go safely in the glory of God”. These high hills and banks are the thrones of the nations that Daniel saw cast down.

**[Baruch 5:8-9](/bible/baruch/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/baruch/5#v8)</sup> Moreover even the woods and every sweetsmelling tree shall overshadow Israel by the commandment of God.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/baruch/5#v9)</sup> For God shall lead Israel with joy in the light of his glory with the mercy and righteousness that cometh from him.

- “Moreover even the woods and every sweetsmelling tree shall overshadow Israel by the commandment of God. For God shall lead Israel with joy in the light of his glory with the mercy and righteousness that cometh from him.” With mercy and righteousness that comes from him, God will lead Israel into glory.
- You do not see Gentiles anywhere in these verses. Hold on to that mercy and righteousness; Zacharias speaks of the same tender mercy of our God in Luke 1.
