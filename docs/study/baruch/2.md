## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Baruch 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 5-10, 30, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Baruch 2:5](/bible/baruch/2#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/baruch/2#v5)</sup> Thus we were cast down, and not exalted, because we have sinned against the Lord our God, and have not been obedient unto his voice.

- “Thus we were cast down, and not exalted”: God threw us down and did not exalt us, “because we have sinned against the Lord our God, and have not been obedient unto his voice.”

**[Baruch 2:6](/bible/baruch/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/baruch/2#v6)</sup> To the Lord our God appertaineth righteousness: but unto us and to our fathers open shame, as appeareth this day.

- “To the Lord our God appertaineth righteousness: but unto us and to our fathers open shame, as appeareth this day.” Here in America, Israel, we have open shame because we departed from our God and refuse to come back and repent.
- Not all of us, but a lot, even a lot in purple and gold, refuse to keep the Lord's statutes and commandments like they are supposed to and refuse to walk right in the sight of God. The Lord is not going to play. Tempt ye not the Lord, brothers and sisters. Get yourself together, come out of that foolishness, and get yourself right before God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Lamentations 2:15-16](/bible/lamentations/2#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/lamentations/2#v15)</sup> All that pass by clap their hands at thee; they hiss and wag their head at the daughter of Jerusalem, saying, Is this the city that men call The perfection of beauty, The joy of the whole earth?
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/lamentations/2#v16)</sup> All thine enemies have opened their mouth against thee: they hiss and gnash the teeth: they say, We have swallowed her up: certainly this is the day that we looked for; we have found, we have seen it.

    This is the open shame. “All that pass by clap their hands at thee”: all the other nations pass by and clap their hands at us. They are happy that we are thrown down, not exalted, at the bottom.

**[Baruch 2:7-8](/bible/baruch/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/baruch/2#v7)</sup> For all these plagues are come upon us, which the Lord hath pronounced against us.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/baruch/2#v8)</sup> Yet have we not prayed before the Lord, that we might turn every one from the imaginations of his wicked heart.

- “For all these plagues are come upon us, which the Lord hath pronounced against us”. “Yet have we not prayed before the Lord, that we might turn every one from the imaginations of his wicked heart.” Even though we have been thrown down, afflicted and oppressed, we have not prayed to our God to get us out of this condition, or that the chosen and righteous of our people would wake up faster. Our people are getting gunned down in the streets and still go back and celebrate Christmas, the Fourth of July, Thanksgiving, Halloween and Easter, even though God has tried to wake them up. We have not prayed in our affliction.
- That is what the camp is trying to do: turn everybody away from the imagination of their wicked heart, from adultery, fornication, lying, stealing and killing.

**[Baruch 2:9](/bible/baruch/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/baruch/2#v9)</sup> Wherefore the Lord watched over us for evil, and the Lord hath brought it upon us: for the Lord is righteous in all his works which he hath commanded us.

- “Wherefore the Lord watched over us for evil, and the Lord hath brought it upon us: for the Lord is righteous in all his works which he hath commanded us.” His judgment is righteous. Him putting us through all this is righteous, because we turned our back and departed from him.

**[Baruch 2:10](/bible/baruch/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/baruch/2#v10)</sup> Yet we have not hearkened unto his voice, to walk in the commandments of the Lord, that he hath set before us.

- “Yet we have not hearkened unto his voice, to walk in the commandments of the Lord, that he hath set before us.” We cry out to our people day and night on Clubhouse, Daily Bread, A to Z, Giving the Sense, all these platforms and radio shows, and our people still will not wake up, like those brothers the camp sat down with, who are still fighting against the understanding of the heavenly Father to this day.

**[Baruch 2:30](/bible/baruch/2#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/baruch/2#v30)</sup> For I knew that they would not hear me, because it is a stiffnecked people: but in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves,

- What we have to do is get back to the Bible and apply it in our lives; that will change the trajectory of our nation and build it back up.
- “For I knew that they would not hear me, because it is a stiffnecked people”. We have been hard-headed since the day God brought us out of the land of Egypt.
- “But in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves.” That is why there is so much opposition to what we teach on the streets and on every platform: the powers that be are trying to keep us from remembering ourselves. Once we remember who we are in this Bible, we will get back to applying its principles, and our situation will change in a generation: more marriages, sons growing up with fathers, daughters with mothers who teach them how to conduct themselves.
- Do not let anybody tell you your history and your past mean nothing. If that were so, the other nations would teach their kids to forget theirs. They are built on the principles of their ancestors and told to become better, everyone except us, because they know who we are and how much better it would do us as a nation. To this day they still teach that George Washington chopped down a cherry tree.
