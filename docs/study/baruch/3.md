## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Baruch 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-11, 24-25, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Baruch 3:1-3](/bible/baruch/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/baruch/3#v1)</sup> O Lord Almighty, God of Israel, the soul in anguish, the troubled spirit, crieth unto thee.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/baruch/3#v2)</sup> Hear, O Lord, and have mercy; for thou art merciful: and have pity upon us, because we have sinned before thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/baruch/3#v3)</sup> For thou endurest for ever, and we perish utterly.

- These nations are watching us. They understand that we are the prophets and the only ones who can interpret prophecy; this is the only book that is alive, and no one else can read it and understand it.
- “The soul in anguish, the troubled spirit, crieth unto thee. Hear, O Lord, and have mercy.” “Have pity upon us, because we have sinned before thee.” That is the mindset we have to have in these last days: Lord, we have sinned against you.

**[Baruch 3:4](/bible/baruch/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/baruch/3#v4)</sup> O Lord Almighty, thou God of Israel, hear now the prayers of the dead Israelites, and of their children, which have sinned before thee, and not hearkened unto the voice of thee their God: for the which cause these plagues cleave unto us.

- “Hear now the prayers of the dead Israelites, and of their children, which have sinned before thee.” That is the sins of our forefathers who died, and also the real martyrs, the real prophets of the Most High sealed over periods of tribulation and torture meant to make us deny our Lord, and the ones to come in Revelation 6. “For the which cause these plagues cleave unto us.”

**[Baruch 3:5](/bible/baruch/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/baruch/3#v5)</sup> Remember not the iniquities of our forefathers: but think upon thy power and thy name now at this time.

- “Remember not the iniquities of our forefathers: but think upon thy power and thy name now at this time.” Think upon your power and your name, Father, and forget the iniquities we committed back then that brought us into the low state we are in now, breaking your law and commandments.

**[Baruch 3:6-7](/bible/baruch/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/baruch/3#v6)</sup> For thou art the Lord our God, and thee, O Lord, will we praise.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/baruch/3#v7)</sup> And for this cause thou hast put thy fear in our hearts, to the intent that we should call upon thy name, and praise thee in our captivity: for we have called to mind all the iniquity of our forefathers, that sinned before thee.

- “For thou art the Lord our God, and thee, O Lord, will we praise. And for this cause thou hast put thy fear in our hearts”. He put his fear in our hearts because we know judgment comes every time we break his law.
- “To the intent that we should call upon thy name, and praise thee in our captivity”. We have to call upon the name of the Lord, make our prayers and supplications like we are supposed to, and return back to the Father in righteousness.
- “For we have called to mind all the iniquity of our forefathers, that sinned before thee.” We call to mind the iniquities our forefathers did, because we are the forefathers.
- “Thou hast put thy fear in our hearts, to the intent that we should call upon thy name, and praise thee in our captivity: for we have called to mind all the iniquity of our forefathers.” That is why we are here now. We have acknowledged our iniquities. Heavenly Father, we acknowledge our sins; we are repenting. Remember the promise you made to our forefathers Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, and see our cause.

**[Baruch 3:8](/bible/baruch/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/baruch/3#v8)</sup> Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity, where thou hast scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to payments, according to all the iniquities of our fathers, which departed from the Lord our God.

- “Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity.” These fake Jews are not in captivity; we are. “Where thou hast scattered us”: the Lord put us here, “for a reproach and a curse.” A reproach is coming off a slave ship butt naked, being separated from your children and family, an embarrassment, at the bottom of society, assimilating. “And to be subject to payments”: the other nations’ faces on the money and we not even in our own land, when they are supposed to be paying tribute to us.
- Our forefathers understood they were in a day of captivity. Today the unrepentant two-thirds of Israel, who just want to party and play games, have no understanding of what is going on around them: that today is your day of captivity. But out of that captivity, if you keep God’s laws, there comes deliverance. We are scattered for a reproach because we refused to humble down and keep God’s commandments. You cannot straddle the fence, stay in sin and pleasure today and expect the kingdom tomorrow.
- Subject to payments: that is what ninety, ninety-five percent of our people complain about all day, the funds, the money, men and women alike hustling, robbing, stealing or whoremongering for it.
- “Behold, we are yet this day in our captivity, where thou hast scattered us, for a reproach and a curse, and to be subject to payments, according to all the iniquities of our fathers, which departed from the Lord our God.” We are yet this day in our captivity. That is why we are subject to payments: you get paid, taxed; you buy something, taxed; you cash your check, more tax, tax on top of tax. They are taxing us crazy here because we broke God's law and commandments.

**[Baruch 3:9-11](/bible/baruch/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/baruch/3#v9)</sup> Hear, Israel, the commandments of life: give ear to understand wisdom.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/baruch/3#v10)</sup> How happeneth it, Israel, that thou art in thine enemies’ land, that thou art waxen old in a strange country, that thou art defiled with the dead,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/baruch/3#v11)</sup> That thou art counted with them that go down into the grave?

- “Hear, Israel, the commandments of life”: we have to start taking heed to the commandments of life. “How happeneth it, Israel, that thou art in thine enemies’ land, that thou art waxen old in a strange country, that thou art defiled with the dead, That thou art counted with them that go down into the grave?” We are counted as the walking dead.
- We are counted as dead and coming back into repentance. Death is on the doorstep of the other nations, and the Lord is doing it as a ransom. So what are you going to do? Play games with your iniquity, with your salvation, delay when it is time to gather together?
- The Lord is asking Israel a question: “How happeneth it, Israel, that thou art in thine enemies’ land”? How did we get into the position we are in, desolate at the bottom, on drugs, baby daddies, baby mamas, barely getting by? We are in the United States of America, the land of our enemies, dealing with lynchings now, our brothers hanging from trees, and these things are going to continue to happen. The Lord is asking, how is this happening to you, Israel?
- “That thou art waxen old in a strange country”: you are not in your own land; you have waxed old in a strange country.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 21:16](/bible/proverbs/21#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/21#v16)</sup> The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

    How did it happen that we are “defiled with the dead”? “The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.” If you wander out of the way of the laws of God, you remain in the congregation of the dead.
  - **[Jeremiah 17:4-5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/17#v4)</sup> And thou, even thyself, shalt discontinue from thine heritage that I gave thee; and I will cause thee to serve thine enemies in the land which thou knowest not: for ye have kindled a fire in mine anger, which shall burn for ever.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.

    Now that we are in a strange country, what else has happened to us? “And thou, even thyself, shalt discontinue from thine heritage that I gave thee”. That happened to all of us collectively. God gave us a heritage, and we lost it. “And I will cause thee to serve thine enemies in the land which thou knowest not”: we serve our enemies, the other nations. “For ye have kindled a fire in mine anger, which shall burn for ever.” God said this would happen.
  - **[Proverbs 13:12](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)</sup> Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life.

    “Hope deferred maketh the heart sick”. At the bottom of society, desolate, you come outside your door and there is a brother who looks just like you on the corner selling crack, another drunk at nine in the morning, a sister who looks just like you scantily clad, maybe selling herself. What do I have to look forward to here? If you come outside and see no opportunity for growth, your hope is deferred, and you say to hell with it, I am going to do what I need to do to survive.

**[Baruch 3:24](/bible/baruch/3#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/baruch/3#v24)</sup> O Israel, how great is the house of God! and how large is the place of his possession!

- “O Israel, how great is the house of God! and how large is the place of his possession!” We are the house of God; we are his possession.

**[Baruch 3:25](/bible/baruch/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/baruch/3#v25)</sup> Great, and hath none end; high, and unmeasurable.

- “Great, and hath none end; high, and unmeasurable.” The house of God, Israel, is great and has no end. That sounds familiar. The class starts with the old first, because the same terminologies are used in the new: when the disciples were teaching the gospel there were no letters, no Matthew, Mark, Luke or John written yet.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 45:17](/bible/isaiah/45#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/45#v17)</sup> But Israel shall be saved in the Lord with an everlasting salvation: ye shall not be ashamed nor confounded world without end.

    “But Israel shall be saved in the Lord with an everlasting salvation: ye shall not be ashamed nor confounded world without end.” Israel is the house that is great and has none end: Israel is a world without end.
