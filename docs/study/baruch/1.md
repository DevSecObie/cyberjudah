## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Baruch 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 17-22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Baruch 1:17-18](/bible/baruch/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/baruch/1#v17)</sup> For we have sinned before the Lord,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/baruch/1#v18)</sup> And disobeyed him, and have not hearkened unto the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in the commandments that he gave us openly:

- “For we have sinned before the Lord”: we have to realize that we have sinned before our Lord. “And disobeyed him, and have not hearkened unto the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in the commandments that he gave us openly”. He gave us the commandments openly; he told us what to do and how to do it, and we have not done it. The churches refuse to keep the law and commandments. Everything those men were talking about was a smoke screen to keep from keeping the Lord's statutes and commandments and bringing their people back to the Lord in righteousness.

**[Baruch 1:19](/bible/baruch/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/baruch/1#v19)</sup> Since the day that the Lord brought our forefathers out of the land of Egypt, unto this present day, we have been disobedient unto the Lord our God, and we have been negligent in not hearing his voice.

- “Since the day that the Lord brought our forefathers out of the land of Egypt, unto this present day, we have been disobedient unto the Lord our God”. We have to realize it and admit it: we have been disobedient as children of the Lord.
- “And we have been negligent in not hearing his voice.” Negligent means failing to take proper care of someone or something, being overly careless, or ignoring a duty. Our duty is to serve the Lord in righteousness and keep the Lord's statutes and commandments as it is written. We are prisoners of the Lord: when you repent and come in to serve the Lord, you are a prisoner of the Lord. This is not a game. We were negligent, not taking care of the duty he gave us.

**[Baruch 1:20](/bible/baruch/1#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/baruch/1#v20)</sup> Wherefore the evils cleaved unto us, and the curse, which the Lord appointed by Moses his servant at the time that he brought our fathers out of the land of Egypt, to give us a land that floweth with milk and honey, like as it is to see this day.

- “Wherefore the evils cleaved unto us, and the curse, which the Lord appointed by Moses his servant”. Those are the curses we just read in Deuteronomy 28:45. Those plagues and curses cleave unto us, “like as it is to see this day.”

**[Baruch 1:21](/bible/baruch/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/baruch/1#v21)</sup> Nevertheless we have not hearkened unto the voice of the Lord our God, according unto all the words of the prophets, whom he sent unto us:

- “Nevertheless we have not hearkened unto the voice of the Lord our God, according unto all the words of the prophets, whom he sent unto us”. He keeps sending us back to hit these streets, saying the same thing to our people, and a lot of our people refuse to come back, refuse to get themselves together, refuse to hear the word of the Lord. The only way the kingdom gets brought forth is by us going out preaching the word, teaching the word, and showing our people who they are.

**[Baruch 1:22](/bible/baruch/1#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/baruch/1#v22)</sup> But every man followed the imagination of his own wicked heart, to serve strange gods, and to do evil in the sight of the Lord our God.

- “But every man followed the imagination of his own wicked heart”. We go out marching, hitting the streets, on all these platforms to wake our people up, and he has a chosen few he is calling in to be part of his army. You can have a thousand people out there and maybe twenty stop through the whole day, and those twenty are the ones he wants to hear. He does not want everybody. We want the chosen, the righteous, the elect to wake up. It is pulling a needle out of a haystack.
- “To serve strange gods”: they want to keep Thanksgiving, Christmas, the Fourth of July, Presidents' Day, their birthdays. “And to do evil in the sight of the Lord our God”: Valentine's Day, New Year's, all this evil in the sight of our God, and they refuse to change their ways.
