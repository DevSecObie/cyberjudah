## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 9 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 9:8](/bible/sirach/9#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/9#v8)</sup> Turn away thine eye from a beautiful woman, and look not upon another’s beauty; for many have been deceived by the beauty of a woman; for herewith love is kindled as a fire.

- “Turn away thine eye from a beautiful woman, and look not upon another’s beauty.” Don't continue to stare at it. If you know that is your device, you need to turn away. Where does it begin? With your eyes. Your eyes are directly correlated with your mind; they work in sync. What you see will make your mind react a certain way, so the best thing to do is turn away.
- Turning your head away and not looking shows that you have self-control, that you have control of your members and can move them about the way you want to. But if you can't turn away, Christ ain't the captain of this ship. Satan is running the show. If you ever saw a woman and did not turn your head, Satan was driving, and you knew consciously, I'm not supposed to be looking.
- “For many have been deceived by the beauty of a woman”: what is happening at that moment is that you are being deceived. Your eyes and your heart, your mind, are directly connected, and your mind is desperately wicked, so the best thing to do is turn away. The sisters have their own things too. You see something inappropriate online, and not even porn, which is obvious, just something that ain't right, and you find yourself clicking and looking. You are not in control. You are down that rabbit hole looking for that feel good, because sin feels good. It is enjoyable.
- “For herewith love is kindled as a fire.” Because you have not turned away, love is kindled. To kindle something is to blow on it. It starts as a little flame, a little ember, but you keep nurturing it by blowing on the flame, and then it becomes inflamed. That is how the two elders of Susanna's history were: at that man's house, lusting after his wife day in and day out, entertaining those thoughts, never turning their heads away, until each of them doubled back to creep and peep, and they busted each other. Their plan was, let’s see if we both can sleep with her. Wicked. It is the same lust inflaming a brother who, as the bread was about to be broken on the Sabbath, leaned over and asked another brother to go to the strip club when they were done. That is bold.

    Moses chose “rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season.” Because you enjoy that pleasure of sin for a season, you are going to pay the price for it. There is a price that is going to be paid.
