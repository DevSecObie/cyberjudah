## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 26-28, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 7:26](/bible/sirach/7#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/7#v26)</sup> Hast thou a wife after thy mind? forsake her not: but give not thyself over to a light woman.

- “Hast thou a wife after thy mind? forsake her not”. Very few understand that this is not their show. It is about the house of the man. A wife after your mind is a woman who understands that she is in subjection to you, that you are the head of the home, and that what you want is what she wants. She makes your desires her desires, your aspirations her aspirations; how you want things to be is how she wants them to be, because she understands her desire is unto her husband, not unto herself. If her husband wants the household a certain way, she makes it that way. That is her job as the manager of the house: to make sure the CEO is happy. The world told you, no, it's yours, sis, do what you want to do.
- The last sign she does not respect the Christ in you is that she doesn't follow your lead spiritually and picks and chooses what to follow in this truth. And a bonus sign: she disrespects other men. If other men complain about your wife's demeanor, you are in denial; she has normalized disrespecting you, so she has no problem disrespecting other men. A woman's demeanor is supposed to change when she speaks to a man. She openly disagrees with leadership and order, when if her father had but spit in her face she would know it is not her place to speak on what leadership has said. She steps out of her role when she thinks she is right, and pride has her on tens. There is never a time when it is permissible for a woman to step out of the place of a woman.
- The female's role is to support the male. The man's job is to build; the woman's job is to nest and to teach values to their offspring, and she needs a husband for that first.

**[Ecclesiasticus 7:27](/bible/sirach/7#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/7#v27)</sup> Honour thy father with thy whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of thy mother.

- Stepfathers get no respect, like the stepdaughter in the video who told her stepfather he was just the guy who married her mother; not everyone is like that, but there have been horror cases. “Honour thy father with thy whole heart, and forget not the sorrows of thy mother.” Don’t forget the labor pains, how she cried trying to push her baby out.

**[Ecclesiasticus 7:28](/bible/sirach/7#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/7#v28)</sup> Remember that thou wast begotten of them; and how canst thou recompense them the things that they have done for thee?

- “Remember that thou wast begotten of them; and how canst thou recompense them the things that they have done for thee?” It is impossible to pay your parents back for what they have done.
