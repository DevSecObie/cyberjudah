## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 45 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-7, 13, 15, 17-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 45:1-2](/bible/sirach/45#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/45#v1)</sup> And he brought out of him a merciful man, which found favour in the sight of all flesh, even Moses, beloved of God and men, whose memorial is blessed.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/45#v2)</sup> He made him like to the glorious saints, and magnified him, so that his enemies stood in fear of him.

- “Even Moses, beloved of God and men, whose memorial is blessed.” “He made him like to the glorious saints, and magnified him, so that his enemies stood in fear of him.” We want to be named among these, to have this legacy and be right with God.
- Moses had his faults. He didn’t circumcise, and Zipporah had to do it; he spoke unadvisedly and didn’t see the promised land. But God still chose him and his legacy lived on.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 44:23](/bible/sirach/44#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/44#v23)</sup> And made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; among the twelve tribes did he part them.

    Read for the complete thought: the Lord “made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; among the twelve tribes did he part them.” Out of Jacob, out of the twelve tribes, came Moses.

**[Ecclesiasticus 45:3](/bible/sirach/45#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/45#v3)</sup> By his words he caused the wonders to cease, and he made him glorious in the sight of kings, and gave him a commandment for his people, and shewed him part of his glory.

- “By his words he caused the wonders to cease”: by his word, by God’s word. “And he made him glorious in the sight of kings”: he made Moses glorious in the sight of kings. “And gave him a commandment for his people”: he gave Moses the commandments to teach us.
- “And shewed him part of his glory.” Who showed Moses part of his glory? Christ is the one, his word: that is who showed Moses part of his glory. People say, see, that was God’s glory, Moses saw God the Father, Christ’s father’s glory. It was not.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 4:6-7](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v6)</sup> For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness, hath shined in our hearts, to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/4#v7)</sup> But we have this treasure in earthen vessels, that the excellency of the power may be of God, and not of us.

    “For God, who commanded the light to shine out of darkness”: the light that shined out of darkness is Christ. God commanded Christ to shine out of darkness, meaning he created Christ.

- “He exalted Aaron, an holy man like unto him, even his brother, of the tribe of Levi.” Aaron was the brother of Moses. For his lineage it is the same as Moses: Amram and Jochebed. His estimated birth is around the same, about three years before Moses, his older brother.

    Phinehas led Israel in battle against the Midianites. “Avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites”: a thousand of every tribe, “twelve thousand armed for war.” “And Moses sent them to the war, a thousand of every tribe, them and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, to the war, with the holy instruments, and the trumpets to blow in his hand.” They slew all the males and the “five kings of Midian.” Phinehas was there in that battle.

**[Ecclesiasticus 45:25](/bible/sirach/45#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/45#v25)</sup> According to the covenant made with David son of Jesse, of the tribe of Juda, that the inheritance of the king should be to his posterity alone: so the inheritance of Aaron should also be unto his seed.

- “So the inheritance of Aaron should also be unto his seed.” Notice how the Levites always stuck with the southern kingdom, and to this day we rock heavy with Levi.

**[Ecclesiasticus 45:26](/bible/sirach/45#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/45#v26)</sup> God give you wisdom in your heart to judge his people in righteousness, that their good things be not abolished, and that their glory may endure for ever.

- “God give you wisdom in your heart to judge his people in righteousness, that their good things be not abolished, and that their glory may endure for ever.” You can do this for twenty or thirty years, then give up, and everything you did goes away. Then you won’t be the famous man; you will be the example of what not to do.
