## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 47 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 7-8, 11, 22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 47:1-4](/bible/sirach/47#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/47#v1)</sup> And after him rose up Nathan to prophesy in the time of David.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/47#v2)</sup> As is the fat taken away from the peace offering, so was David chosen out of the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/47#v3)</sup> He played with lions as with kids, and with bears as with lambs.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/47#v4)</sup> Slew he not a giant, when he was yet but young? and did he not take away reproach from the people, when he lifted up his hand with the stone in the sling, and beat down the boasting of Goliath?

- “As is the fat taken away from the peace offering, so was David chosen out of the children of Israel.” “He played with lions as with kids, and with bears as with lambs. Slew he not a giant, when he was yet but young?”

**[Ecclesiasticus 47:7-8](/bible/sirach/47#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/47#v7)</sup> For he destroyed the enemies on every side, and brought to nought the Philistines his adversaries, and brake their horn in sunder unto this day.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/47#v8)</sup> In all his works he praised the Holy One most high with words of glory; with his whole heart he sung songs, and loved him that made him.

- “For he destroyed the enemies on every side”. He did many great works. “With his whole heart he sung songs, and loved him that made him.” But didn’t David sin against God?

**[Ecclesiasticus 47:11](/bible/sirach/47#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/47#v11)</sup> The Lord took away his sins, and exalted his horn for ever: he gave him a covenant of kings, and a throne of glory in Israel.

- “The Lord took away his sins, and exalted his horn for ever: he gave him a covenant of kings, and a throne of glory in Israel.” David sinned against God, but he repented, and God took away his sins. This is a famous man, and an example to all of us: some of us err in this truth, but you can recover yourself and still earn a name. You just have to do the work and be sincere.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 51:3-4, 7, 13](/bible/psalms/51#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/51#v3)</sup> For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/51#v4)</sup> Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight: that thou mightest be justified when thou speakest, and be clear when thou judgest.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/51#v7)</sup> Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/51#v13)</sup> Then will I teach transgressors thy ways; and sinners shall be converted unto thee.

    After David confessed his sin, what did he do? “For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me. Against thee, thee only, have I sinned, and done this evil in thy sight”. “Purge me with hyssop, and I shall be clean: wash me, and I shall be whiter than snow.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 47:22](/bible/sirach/47#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/47#v22)</sup> But the Lord will never leave off his mercy, neither shall any of his works perish, neither will he abolish the posterity of his elect, and the seed of him that loveth him he will not take away: wherefore he gave a remnant unto Jacob, and out of him a root unto David.

- “But the Lord will never leave off his mercy”, “neither will he abolish the posterity of his elect, and the seed of him that loveth him he will not take away: wherefore he gave a remnant unto Jacob, and out of him a root unto David.”
