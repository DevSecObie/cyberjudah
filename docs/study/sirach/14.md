## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 17-19, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 14:17](/bible/sirach/14#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/14#v17)</sup> All flesh waxeth old as a garment: for the covenant from the beginning is, Thou shalt die the death.

- “All flesh waxeth old as a garment: for the covenant from the beginning is, Thou shalt die the death.” That is what God says. We just read David saying seventy, maybe eighty years: that is the covenant, thou shalt die the death.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 6:23](/bible/romans/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/6#v23)</sup> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

    “For the wages of sin is death”. That is the reason the covenant has passed upon all of us. When Adam listened to his wife Eve and consented to the idolatry she brought to him, sin came into the world, and when sin came, death came as well. We break it down for those new to the faith, to put the puzzle pieces together of why we die.

**[Ecclesiasticus 14:18](/bible/sirach/14#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/14#v18)</sup> As of the green leaves on a thick tree, some fall, and some grow; so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born.

- “As of the green leaves on a thick tree, some fall, and some grow; so is the generation of flesh and blood, one cometh to an end, and another is born.” That is why we have children. You watch them grow up and you realize you are getting old, and that the covenant God has pronounced upon all, death, is about to come to you too.

**[Ecclesiasticus 14:19](/bible/sirach/14#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/14#v19)</sup> Every work rotteth and consumeth away, and the worker thereof shall go withal.

- “Every work rotteth and consumeth away, and the worker thereof shall go withal.” That is the covenant: thou shalt die the death, thus saith the Lord.
