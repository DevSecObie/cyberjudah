## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 34 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 8-14, 16-17, 23-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:1-2](/bible/sirach/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/34#v1)</sup> The hopes of a man void of understanding are vain and false: and dreams lift up fools.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/34#v2)</sup> Whoso regardeth dreams is like him that catcheth at a shadow, and followeth after the wind.

- “The hopes of a man void of understanding are vain and false”. Without understanding and the fear of God, those hopes and desires won't come to pass. “and dreams lift up fools.” You really think you'll make a career playing PlayStation. “Whoso regardeth dreams is like him that catcheth at a shadow”. Those hopes and desires are like catching air, not real, but you're young and think you know.
- Does God still speak to us in dreams, and how do we translate them? There’s no scripture that says how to translate them; it tells you not even to. “The hopes of a man void of understanding are vain and false: and dreams lift up fools.” If you pay too much attention to your dreams, it’ll make you into a fool.
- “Whoso regardeth dreams”: if you’re putting a lot of regard to your dreams, it’s “like him that catcheth at a shadow”: you cannot catch a shadow. “And followeth after the wind”: you cannot follow the wind. So the Bible tells us not to pay attention to our dreams to that extent.
- I have bad dreams every single night; I don’t know what it’s like to have a good dream. Those dreams could be sent from God to keep me the way that I am, because the Lord can interfere with your dreams, but I don’t let them alter my understanding.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:3](/bible/sirach/34#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/34#v3)</sup> The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, even as the likeness of a face to a face.

- “The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another”: your dream cannot always mean what you think it is. “Even as the likeness of a face to a face”: you see a face and it looks like somebody else, but it’s not.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:4](/bible/sirach/34#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/34#v4)</sup> Of an unclean thing what can be cleansed? and from that thing which is false what truth can come?

- “Of an unclean thing what can be cleansed? and from that thing which is false what truth can come?” Some of your dreams are false; there’s no truth to them. So the Bible doesn’t tell us how to interpret dreams, but dreams can be sent to us from God to alter our understanding to the way he wants it.
- My mom used to go to sleep and wake up, I dreamed about fish, let me get my dream book and see what number I should play for fishes. Mom, you was probably hungry. She’d spend hundreds of dollars with that stupid ass dream book, out in her house slippers and her damn robe trying to get the number in before a certain time. When I read this scripture and tried to show it to her, she wouldn’t listen: I dreamed the dog was licking my foot, let me see what this says in my dream book.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:8](/bible/sirach/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/34#v8)</sup> The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.

- “The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.” This man has the law within his mind.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:9](/bible/sirach/34#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/34#v9)</sup> A man that hath travelled knoweth many things; and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom.

- “A man that hath travelled knoweth many things; and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom.” Listen first to your father and mother and experienced elder men and women in the truth. You haven't experienced enough to know how evil this world is. Satan plays at a level where even we can tap into things without realizing.
- I can't stand my children leaving with a do-rag on. You're waiting for police to pull you over and crack your head. I pray God preserves them, but part of me wants them pulled over and cracked once—not killed—so I can say I told you. Officer Canai was reckless with that; Noah too. I'm glad Canai didn't get hit, but I wanted him to understand. I used to do it myself. At sixteen a policeman split my head with a nightstick, and I had one on. You think I'm joking or don't know? That happened to me.
- I want my children to think, not be lobotomized. But sometimes just listen. There isn't time, or I can't express everything, but I'm trying to save and help you.
- “A man that hath travelled knoweth many things; and he that hath much experience will declare wisdom.” You have to be travelled in this truth and have experience. Some men have never been out of their own city.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:10-12](/bible/sirach/34#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/34#v10)</sup> He that hath no experience knoweth little: but he that hath travelled is full of prudence.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/34#v11)</sup> When I travelled, I saw many things; and I understand more than I can express.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/34#v12)</sup> I was ofttimes in danger of death: yet I was delivered because of these things.

- “He that hath no experience knoweth little: but he that hath travelled is full of prudence. When I travelled, I saw many things; and I understand more than I can express.” When he travelled, the law he already understood let him understand what he saw. He travelled teaching, labouring for the Lord and for our people to wake up, for all them that seek learning.
- Military men go to Syria and never see Dura-Europos; they go to Iraq and never see the walls of Cyrus. They travel, but they are not studied to know what to look for. But in travelling while teaching is where you gain experience, and it is part of how you go from milk to meat, because now you can share that experience.
- When the brothers first went to Cuba and Captain Abio was teaching, the police came, then a paddy wagon full of cops with nightsticks. Teaching without a licence there carried two years in prison without trial, and the police chief said, based on what you are teaching, we don’t need another revolution. Asked about their rights, he said, You have no rights here. F your rights. Because that was experienced, it is understood there that a different approach is needed. Not that we won’t teach there, but we find another way. That is why it is good to get around learned men who have travelled: there is more than can be expressed in one conversation, and if you stay around and learn, we can tell you, this won’t work here, this is how you approach it.
- “He that hath no experience knoweth little”. You only know up to the life you've lived. A four-year-old knows ABCs and numbers, not paying bills or trigonometry. You haven't yet known marriage, children, being in love, working to pay bills for people depending on you. You'll get there.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/33#v17)</sup> Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.

    The man that travelled was labouring, and for whom? “Consider that I laboured not for myself only, but for all them that seek learning.” That is the point: you travel the world to teach people that want to learn. But first you have to keep the commandments, learn and study, and then you go out and teach.
  - **[Judith 8:24](/bible/judith/8#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/judith/8#v24)</sup> Now therefore, O brethren, let us shew an example to our brethren, because their hearts depend upon us, and the sanctuary, and the house, and the altar, rest upon us.

    “Now therefore, O brethren, let us shew an example to our brethren, because their hearts depend upon us, and the sanctuary, and the house, and the altar, rest upon us.” The people are depending upon us. So it is our job to study, learn and get right, so we can travel and share the word with our people.
  - **[Hebrews 12:4](/bible/hebrews/12#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/12#v4)</sup> Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin.

    “Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin.” We know it’s going to get worse, but right now the Most High God is holding back. There are things they could have done to us that the Most High God held back.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:13-14](/bible/sirach/34#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/34#v13)</sup> The spirit of those that fear the Lord shall live; for their hope is in him that saveth them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/34#v14)</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord shall not fear nor be afraid; for he is his hope.

- “The spirit of those that fear the Lord shall live; for their hope is in him that saveth them. Whoso feareth the Lord shall not fear nor be afraid; for he is his hope.”
- You learn that when you travel with certain men. If you are sincere in this truth and really keeping his commandments in your personal life, that spirit jumps on you so that you will not fear. In Trinidad the brothers were told they couldn’t go up into Laventille, that the gangs left only one way down. They went up there and taught, and it put a spirit on those men; now they go up there.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:16](/bible/sirach/34#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/34#v16)</sup> For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, a preservation from stumbling, and an help from falling.

- “For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him”: the Lord is watching us. “He is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat, and a cover from the sun at noon, a preservation from stumbling, and an help from falling.” The Lord is a help from falling.
- But pay close attention to what it says: them that love him. If you love me, keep my commandments. Some people get sick, and when they get a little better they don't come to the school; they get Sabbath sick. Sunday through Friday you were good, but here is the Sabbath. Didn't God just heal your black behind? And this is how you repay the Lord? Some of you got delivered from a bad court situation, and now that you are delivered we don't see you no more. You stupid, dumb as hell: you don't know that something else bad can happen. There was a brother who couldn't stand us and showed up once every three moons, until he got caught up in a court situation: can y'all pray for me, can you anoint me? He got delivered, and where is he now? Our people don't learn.
- “For the eyes of the Lord are upon them that love him, he is their mighty protection and strong stay, a defence from heat”: if we love him, he is our mighty protection. What type of heat? Nuclear heat. “And a cover from the sun at noon”, because the destruction comes at the noon day.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 5:14](/bible/john/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/john/5#v14)</sup> Afterward Jesus findeth him in the temple, and said unto him, Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee.

    Christ said the same about being healed and being warned: “Behold, thou art made whole: sin no more, lest a worse thing come unto thee.” A lot of y'all only come here when something is wrong. We pray over you, we anoint you, and as soon as you get better you go back to your wickedness, and you don't understand that you made your situation worse. Christ said a worse thing is going to happen to you than what you came to us for help for.
  - **[Psalms 91:6](/bible/psalms/91#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/91#v6)</sup> Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.

    “Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.” All we have to do is be faithful and fear him, and he'll protect us from the destruction to come.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 14:27](/bible/sirach/14#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/14#v27)</sup> By her he shall be covered from heat, and in her glory shall he dwell.

    “By her he shall be covered from heat, and in her glory shall he dwell.” By wisdom we shall be covered from heat.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:17](/bible/sirach/34#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/34#v17)</sup> He raiseth up the soul, and lighteneth the eyes: he giveth health, life, and blessing.

- “He raiseth up the soul, and lighteneth the eyes”: to lighten the eyes is to enlighten your spiritual eye. “He giveth health, life, and blessing.” The Lord is the one that gives health, life and blessing.

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:23-24](/bible/sirach/34#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/34#v23)</sup> When one buildeth, and another pulleth down, what profit have they then but labour?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/34#v24)</sup> When one prayeth, and another curseth, whose voice will the Lord hear?

- “When one buildeth, and another pulleth down, what profit have they then but labour? When one prayeth, and another curseth, whose voice will the Lord hear?” If one is praying for you and one brother is cursing, whose voice will the Lord hear?

**[Ecclesiasticus 34:25-26](/bible/sirach/34#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/34#v25)</sup> He that washeth himself after the touching of a dead body, if he touch it again, what availeth his washing?
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/34#v26)</sup> So is it with a man that fasteth for his sins, and goeth again, and doeth the same: who will hear his prayer? or what doth his humbling profit him?

- “He that washeth himself after the touching of a dead body, if he touch it again, what availeth his washing? So is it with a man that fasteth for his sins, and goeth again, and doeth the same”: he fasts for his sins, or she fasts for hers, and still has hatred in the heart, still bitterness.
- “Who will hear his prayer? or what doth his humbling profit him?” Nothing. If you are out here fasting with bitterness in your heart, hatred, anger, malevolence, you are wasting your day and starving yourself for no reason. With that festering, fermenting, fragmenting spirit, just eat: have a graham cracker, have some grape juice, some Kool-Aid, some purple drink.
