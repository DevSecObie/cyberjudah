## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 13 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 13:8](/bible/sirach/13#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/13#v8)</sup> Beware that thou be not deceived, and brought down in thy jollity.

- “Beware that thou be not deceived, and brought down in thy jollity.” Sometimes you’re drinking, laughing, having a good time. Ain’t nothing wrong with that, but it will make you forget the scriptures at times, like ‘avoid all appearance of evil’ in 1 Thessalonians 5. It will make us forget that, so we always have to take inventory and check ourselves.
