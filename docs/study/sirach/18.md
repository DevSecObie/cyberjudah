## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 18 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 11-14, 25, 27, 30-32, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 18:11](/bible/sirach/18#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/18#v11)</sup> Therefore is God patient with them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them.

- “Therefore is God patient with them, and poureth forth his mercy upon them”: he poured it on us.

**[Ecclesiasticus 18:12](/bible/sirach/18#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/18#v12)</sup> He saw and perceived their end to be evil; therefore he multiplied his compassion.

- “He saw and perceived their end to be evil; therefore he multiplied his compassion.” He saw it, because we stay in this flesh. We still have that evil mind and those evil thoughts, and that is why he multiplies his compassion.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 3:20-22](/bible/2-esdras/3#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/2-esdras/3#v20)</sup> And yet tookest thou not away from them a wicked heart, that thy law might bring forth fruit in them.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/2-esdras/3#v21)</sup> For the first Adam bearing a wicked heart transgressed, and was overcome; and so be all they that are born of him.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/2-esdras/3#v22)</sup> Thus infirmity was made permanent; and the law (also) in the heart of the people with the malignity of the root; so that the good departed away, and the evil abode still.

    “And yet tookest thou not away from them a wicked heart, that thy law might bring forth fruit in them.” In the beginning he didn’t take the wicked heart all the way away, so that the law might bring forth fruit, works, in us. “For the first Adam bearing a wicked heart transgressed, and was overcome; and so be all they that are born”: we are all born after Adam with that wicked heart, that wicked mind. “Thus infirmity was made permanent”, “so that the good departed away, and the evil abode still.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 18:13](/bible/sirach/18#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/18#v13)</sup> The mercy of man is toward his neighbour; but the mercy of the Lord is upon all flesh: he reproveth, and nurtureth, and teacheth, and bringeth again, as a shepherd his flock.

- “The mercy of man is toward his neighbour; but the mercy of the Lord is upon all flesh: he reproveth, and nurtureth, and teacheth, and bringeth again, as a shepherd his flock”: as the shepherd takes care of his sheep, the Lord takes care of us the same way.

**[Ecclesiasticus 18:14](/bible/sirach/18#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/18#v14)</sup> He hath mercy on them that receive discipline, and that diligently seek after his judgments.

- “He hath mercy on them that receive discipline.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 18:25](/bible/sirach/18#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/18#v25)</sup> When thou hast enough, remember the time of hunger: and when thou art rich, think upon poverty and need.

- “When thou hast enough, remember the time of hunger”. When the Lord blesses you with enough, a good job, a nice savings, remember when you had nothing. Some grew up poor, got a little something, and forgot where they came from. Those who had nothing appreciate what they get; those who were given everything never knew want and don’t appreciate it. So we must learn to live below our means and within our means.
- “And when thou art rich, think upon poverty and need.” Why think on poverty when you are rich? Because you could lose everything, and some will. Christ said we must be willing to lose everything for the kingdom and his name’s sake.
- A financial collapse is about to take place, and it is engineered by the high-ups in society; Trump is not that stupid. It is being forced to bring in the one world order and one digital currency, because the US dollar is losing its power and dominance and they must replace it. The money in your bank account is not even real riches. It is fiat money: by decree, worth what the government says, backed by no gold or silver, enforced by force. It has been so since after World War II, first on the gold standard and then fiat.
- The Great Depression of 1929, with the banks collapsed and a quarter unemployed, will happen again on a much higher level, and because every nation is pegged to the US dollar, when it falls every economy falls. The 40-hour week was set up in the 1920s for control: they say they pay you for your time, but your time is your life, your life force, and we have become wage slaves, a new sharecropping, with every check going right back into bills by design. The 401k, the IRA, retirement funds, stocks and paper gold were set up to feed Wall Street, a Ponzi scheme; when it goes belly up those will be gone. Even the paper gold the banks sell you online is not real: the same gold is sold to millions, a Ponzi scheme, and China has shut that trading down so that only physical gold can be bought. There is nothing wrong with buying a little real gold and putting it to the side instead of leaving all your money in the bank, but don’t let anybody know, or you will get robbed.

**[Ecclesiasticus 18:27](/bible/sirach/18#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/18#v27)</sup> A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the day of sinning he will beware of offence: but a fool will not observe time.

- This is a major hang-up that keeps fuelling the enemy inside. “A wise man will fear in every thing”: the Bible is written in the masculine, but it is the wise brother or sister who applies the commandments. That wise man fears the judgment of the heavenly Father.
- “And in the day of sinning he will beware of offence”: he will not transgress the laws of God, and he will beware of letting that old man dwell and have dominion over him. There is a conscience here. In the world we had none: steal that, sleep with her and her friend, take somebody’s car, with no thought of AIDS or jail behind it. Now in this truth you understand the judgments of the Lord and that he is not playing.
- Yet many still tempt the Father, thinking he will let it slide, until he gives you the monster, until you get AIDS, until you get locked up. Many hard-headed brothers and sisters have been given counsel, and it is not our counsel but the Lord’s, and they went against it. Then the Father takes off his belt: a slow death or a fast one, incarceration, or a warrant waiting for you on the side of the road. The Lord is the king of terrors.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 8:13](/bible/proverbs/8#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/8#v13)</sup> The fear of the Lord is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.

    Why does the wise man fear? “The fear of the Lord is to hate evil”: not to indulge in it, but to despise and loathe evil and everything that comes with the package of evil, and to purge it from inside you.

> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/18#v30)</sup> Go not after thy lusts, but refrain thyself from thine appetites.

- “Go not after thy lusts, but refrain thyself from thine appetites.” When you can refrain yourself, you have self-control. You rule this body.

A father went to clean his son's room and found a blow-up doll packed under the bed. Latex, plastic. It ain't real. Go not after thy lusts.

- To refrain means to control. Coming into the truth, you have to control the appetite and the lust that is in you; you cannot give in to it. Some like little girls, some like teenagers, some like men; some brothers like wearing their wife’s panties and women’s bras, some watch evil, nasty porn. If you are dealing with that, get rid of that evil, filthy spirit. A wife who wants to peg her husband defiles the bed: that is an appetite too.

    “Whoso taketh pleasure in wickedness shall be condemned: but he that resisteth pleasures crowneth his life.” If you can resist these carnal pleasures that are sin, you will receive a crown. Pleasure now is regret later.
  - **[Galatians 5:16-17](/bible/galatians/5#v16)**

    > <sup>[17](/bible/galatians/5#v17)</sup> For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh: and these are contrary the one to the other: so that ye cannot do the things that ye would.

    “Walk in the Spirit, and ye shall not fulfil the lust of the flesh. For the flesh lusteth against the Spirit, and the Spirit against the flesh.” There is a war going on inside of you: your flesh against your spirit, your spirit against your flesh.
