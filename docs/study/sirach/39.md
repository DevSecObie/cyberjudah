## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 39 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-9, 11-13, 27-31, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:1](/bible/sirach/39#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/39#v1)</sup> But he that giveth his mind to the law of the most High, and is occupied in the meditation thereof, will seek out the wisdom of all the ancient, and be occupied in prophecies.

- Here is the flip side: the brother “that giveth his mind to the law of the most High, and is occupied in the meditation thereof, will seek out the wisdom of all the ancient, and be occupied in prophecies.”
- “But he that giveth his mind to the law of the most High, and is occupied in the meditation thereof, will seek out the wisdom of all the ancient, and be occupied in prophecies.” The ancient are Isaiah, Jeremiah, John the Revelator.
- In your leisure time, when you are off work, you are not brain rotting on YouTube shorts or porn. Porn is destroying the minds of the men, and of whoever is doing it. It is a sickness and a disease; you will be no good for the Lord or for anything else, and eventually you act out that behaviour, and some will end up in jail behind it, and we are not bailing you out, little nasty.
- The way you seek out the wisdom of the ancient is to get around leadership. Fifty brothers stand at the podium and a brother stands in the back. Drop everything; you have the opportunity to learn, so learn. Nobody’s arm is being twisted. When class is over and there is a chance to get around leadership, take advantage of it, and do it with your local leadership too: whoever in your school has understanding, get close to him and learn.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:2-3](/bible/sirach/39#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/39#v2)</sup> He will keep the sayings of the renowned men: and where subtil parables are, he will be there also.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/39#v3)</sup> He will seek out the secrets of grave sentences, and be conversant in dark parables.

- “He will keep the sayings of the renowned men: and where subtil parables are, he will be there also. He will seek out the secrets of grave sentences, and be conversant in dark parables.” Seeking out dark sentences, being around the ancients.
- “He will keep the sayings of the renowned men”: renowned means famous. “And where subtil parables are, he will be there also”: where those parables are, you will see that brother right there, wanting to learn.
- “He will seek out the secrets of grave sentences, and be conversant in dark parables.” A secret of a grave sentence is like when Christ asked the Pharisees about David: The Lord said unto my Lord, sit thou on my right hand until I make thy enemies thy footstool. If he is David's son, why does David call him my Lord? Most people can't even explain that.
- Just before this, a sister was shown who had seen the march at the 2819 church: she heard us, saw us walking with authority and presence, was given a pamphlet, listened, and came to class that very morning. The word does not go out void. “He will keep the sayings of the renowned men: and where subtil parables are, he will be there also. He will seek out the secrets of grave sentences, and be conversant in dark parables.”

If we are sitting down with vice presidents, mayors and governors, why should we waste our time arguing with you? They had the nerve to ask the brothers why Israel United in Christ was there, and they followed the brothers all the way to where they were meeting the former president. We are meeting people of high status, sharing the gospel, and people are believing.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:5](/bible/sirach/39#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/39#v5)</sup> He will give his heart to resort early to the Lord that made him, and will pray before the most High, and will open his mouth in prayer, and make supplication for his sins.

- “He will give his heart to resort early to the Lord that made him, and will pray before the most High, and will open his mouth in prayer, and make supplication for his sins.” That is why we send out the morning prayer every morning at sunrise.
- “He will give his heart to resort early to the Lord that made him, and will pray before the most High, and will open his mouth in prayer, and make supplication for his sins.” He is going to repent.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:6](/bible/sirach/39#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/39#v6)</sup> When the great Lord will, he shall be filled with the spirit of understanding: he shall pour out wise sentences, and give thanks unto the Lord in his prayer.

- “When the great Lord will, he shall be filled with the spirit of understanding.” So you can't rush this. You cannot rush your understanding. “He shall pour out wise sentences, and give thanks unto the Lord in his prayer.”
- “When the great Lord will, he shall be filled with the spirit of understanding.” When the Lord has seen that you have been making a conscious change, mortifying and disciplining yourself, then he begins to fill you with the spirit of understanding. First you get around great men to learn, repent and get your mind right; then the Lord can use you.
- Before that, the strong meat is not for you. You have to learn the simple things: no whoremonger shall enter the kingdom, wear your fringes, remember the sabbath day to keep it holy, know the tribes. Years ago a brother taking the officer test, more than ten years ago and almost impossible to fail, could only fill in ten of the twelve tribes of Israel.
- “He shall pour out wise sentences, and give thanks unto the Lord in his prayer.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:7-8](/bible/sirach/39#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/39#v7)</sup> He shall direct his counsel and knowledge, and in his secrets shall he meditate.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/39#v8)</sup> He shall shew forth that which he hath learned, and shall glory in the law of the covenant of the Lord.

- “He shall direct his counsel and knowledge, and in his secrets shall he meditate. He shall shew forth that which he hath learned, and shall glory in the law of the covenant of the Lord.”
- “He shall direct his counsel and knowledge, and in his secrets shall he meditate.” The secrets are in the scriptures, and the Lord is going to reveal them; he will meditate upon these things. This is the transition from milk to meat.
- “He shall shew forth that which he hath learned, and shall glory in the law of the covenant of the Lord.” Out of his mouth he shows forth all the wisdom he has accumulated over years of studying, applying, getting around renowned men and learning the scriptures.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:9](/bible/sirach/39#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/39#v9)</sup> Many shall commend his understanding; and so long as the world endureth, it shall not be blotted out; his memorial shall not depart away, and his name shall live from generation to generation.

- “Many shall commend his understanding; and so long as the world endureth, it shall not be blotted out; his memorial shall not depart away, and his name shall live from generation to generation.” That is what you want for your name: when you leave this earth, your name still rings bells and is still part of the conversation, for how God moved you, worked with you and used you as his disciple.
- For that you have to level up. A teacher’s classes can’t keep talking about the same things, regurgitating the same class over and over. You have to grow in understanding and application to teach the people.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:11](/bible/sirach/39#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/39#v11)</sup> If he die, he shall leave a greater name than a thousand: and if he live, he shall increase it.

- “If he die, he shall leave a greater name than a thousand: and if he live, he shall increase it.” If you live, your name keeps growing. That is a man ever evolving, always getting better, until we measure up to the full stature of Christ.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:27](/bible/sirach/39#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/39#v27)</sup> All these things are for good to the godly: so to the sinners they are turned into evil.

**[Ecclesiasticus 39:28](/bible/sirach/39#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/39#v28)</sup> There be spirits that are created for vengeance, which in their fury lay on sore strokes; in the time of destruction they pour out their force, and appease the wrath of him that made them.

There are spirits all around about us made for vengeance on those that torment this earth and torment the children of Israel, “which in their fury lay on sore strokes; in the time of destruction they pour out their force, and appease the wrath of him that made them.” That is God: God created those forces to destroy those that have tormented this earth.
