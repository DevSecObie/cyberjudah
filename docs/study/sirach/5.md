## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-7, 12, 15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:2](/bible/sirach/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/5#v2)</sup> Follow not thine own mind and thy strength, to walk in the ways of thy heart:

- “Follow not thine own mind and thy strength, to walk in the ways of thy heart.” When you find yourself veering off into your sins, you start to follow your own mind, to do what you want to do, to walk in your own opinions, thoughts and emotions.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:3](/bible/sirach/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/5#v3)</sup> And say not, Who shall controul me for my works? for the Lord will surely revenge thy pride.

- “And say not, Who shall controul me for my works?” Don’t say, who will tell me what to do, I’m not listening to any leaders, I’ll eat what I want, sleep with who I want, when I want. A brother walked in the other day and said he wasn’t coming back, it’s too hard, he’s tired. Tired after seven or eight years? There are men here who have been in this truth almost thirty years. Satan says to you, only you are tired, they don’t understand you, you’ve been alone eight or nine years with no woman and no warmth at home. I understand you. Come, let’s go out in the world where things are easier; you can have who you want, how many you want. America is going to be destroyed and we all die anyway, so go out with a bang. Take that purple off. They call you lazy and immature; I love you, no one loves you more than me. Look at her now: she can kill babies when she wants to; look what I gave her.
- “For the Lord will surely revenge thy pride.” You are going to get it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:4](/bible/sirach/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/5#v4)</sup> Say not, I have sinned, and what harm hath happened unto me? for the Lord is longsuffering, he will in no wise let thee go.

- “Say not, I have sinned, and what harm hath happened unto me?” That is Satan in your head: you fornicated, you had two women last week, and you’re still here, your heart is still beating. That Bible is up to interpretation, they are a cult, I’m God, I’m talking to you now, you’re spiritual.
- “For the Lord is longsuffering, he will in no wise let thee go.” You are not dead because God is patient with his chosen. He says, Satan, you can have him a little while, but I want him back. Some go out like the prodigal son and come back, but they don’t come back unscathed: they come back with child support, with a disease, from jail. And some God does not want back at all. You don’t want that.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:5](/bible/sirach/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/5#v5)</sup> Concerning propitiation, be not without fear to add sin unto sin:

- “Concerning propitiation”: that is reconciliation, returning to God. “Be not without fear to add sin unto sin.” Don’t think that because you got away with it you can do it again and again. We play with sin the way a child keeps touching what his father told him to leave alone. After a while the Most High visits you: he lets Satan send his messengers, makes you see things, lets anger hop in you so you hit your wife or lose your job. Short-term decisions lead to long-term consequences, because you kept poking the bear.
- “Concerning propitiation, be not without fear to add sin unto sin.” You see how the thing escalated: from lying that it was her money, to her sister lying for her, to I was going to bring it to the counter. You get caught up in one sin, and now you have to lie to cover it up. Once that money is tucked in, it is going to the house.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Thessalonians 4:12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)</sup> That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without, and that ye may have lack of nothing.

    “That ye may walk honestly.” A so-called follower of Christ has to walk honestly. Now the church mother is caught on camera, it goes through the whole community, and she walks into church on Sunday with them knowing she was the one rolling on the floor and yelling at the little baby, now in the line for the Easter eggs.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:6](/bible/sirach/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/5#v6)</sup> And say not, His mercy is great; he will be pacified for the multitude of my sins: for mercy and wrath come from him, and his indignation resteth upon sinners.

- “And say not, His mercy is great; he will be pacified for the multitude of my sins.” That is Christianity: grace, grace, I’m delivered, I’m saved, I can do what I want. “For mercy and wrath come from him, and his indignation resteth upon sinners.” He is coming for you.
- “And say not, His mercy is great; he will be pacified for the multitude of my sins.” That is what a Christian would say: no matter how much sin I keep committing, he will give me mercy. But without repenting, how would you receive mercy? You have to confess, then repent.
- “For mercy and wrath come from him.” Christians say he is merciful but forget the wrath part. He is a God of love, but he is also a God of judgment, and the Lord loves judgment. You want to be found as one of the judges, not on the opposite end of it.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

    “Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth”: no more I’m covered by the blood, I’m a Christian. “For the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:7](/bible/sirach/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/5#v7)</sup> Make no tarrying to turn to the Lord, and put not off from day to day: for suddenly shall the wrath of the Lord come forth, and in thy security thou shalt be destroyed, and perish in the day of vengeance.

- “Make no tarrying to turn to the Lord, and put not off from day to day.” If you fell away, return, and sooner rather than later. Some are sitting at home playing in their sin so they won’t come to the school, saying I’m spiritual by myself in front of my computer or the television. That is not biblical; that is Satan. You are watching the news, seeing what Trump is doing and the chariots they call drones mobilizing, all that has been taught these past years coming to pass, and you are still dragging your feet.
- “For suddenly shall the wrath of the Lord come forth, and in thy security thou shalt be destroyed, and perish in the day of vengeance.” Your security is your comfort and complacency. It will be too late.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:12](/bible/sirach/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/5#v12)</sup> If thou hast understanding, answer thy neighbour; if not, lay thy hand upon thy mouth.

- “If thou hast understanding, answer thy neighbour; if not, lay thy hand upon thy mouth.” When you are asked a question and you understand, and you know where in the Bible it speaks of it, give answer. If you are asked and you don't know, shut up. That is what laying your hand upon your mouth means: if you don't know, it is best to be quiet.
- You don't get to freestyle or go off the top of your head; you have to speak truth to your neighbour, and that is the law. A man asked about Luke 3:23, as was supposed, and instead of pulling the scripture he made up that Jacob and Heli were brothers and that Heli was Joseph's father by levirate marriage. Somebody was supposed to say, shut your lip and pull the script: show me in Luke 3 and Matthew 1 that Jacob and Heli were brothers. You don't read that anywhere in the Bible.
- I don't care how composed you look or how unbothered you make yourself appear. If you don't know what you are talking about, shut up. Some of our people are so prideful they want to look like the gurus with all the answers. If there is something you don't know, go find out; pick up the phone and ask. There is no shame in that.

**[Ecclesiasticus 5:15](/bible/sirach/5#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/5#v15)</sup> Be not ignorant of any thing in a great matter or a small.

- Keep this in mind all through the class. “Be not ignorant of any thing in a great matter or a small.” This is especially for the men. It does not mean be a professional in everything; it means know a little bit about everything. When you know a little about everything, it is hard for somebody to lie to you, and a lot of you are going to get fooled by what comes on social media, because you don't study and you don't pay attention to what is going on.
- A man is supposed to know what is going on, because the men are the leaders. You check the weather for tomorrow so you know how to go to work; know the world the same way. You don't have to watch hours of news. Ten or fifteen minutes is enough, and watch what has just come out, not what is days old, because the regular news lies. When somebody starts talking about something, know what they are talking about so you don't look clueless. The war has been going for almost three weeks and some of you still have no clue, living in la-la land, thinking nothing has ever happened here.
