## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 26-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 1:26](/bible/sirach/1#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/1#v26)</sup> If thou desire wisdom, keep the commandments, and the Lord shall give her unto thee.

- “If thou desire wisdom, keep the commandments, and the Lord shall give her unto thee.” Keeping the commandments is an action. It is our foundation, how we live, the integrity we roll by.
- When you are keeping the commandments, willfully and really keeping them, you have no choice but to have good integrity, and a man with good integrity has good character. You cannot keep the commandments with all your heart and all your soul and have horrible character. It makes no sense.

**[Ecclesiasticus 1:27](/bible/sirach/1#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/1#v27)</sup> For the fear of the Lord is wisdom and instruction: and faith and meekness are his delight.

- “For the fear of the Lord is wisdom and instruction”. Our integrity and our moral character are directly tied to our fear toward God; they are one and the same.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 1:18-21](/bible/sirach/1#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/1#v18)</sup> The fear of the Lord is a crown of wisdom, making peace and perfect health to flourish; both which are the gifts of God: and it enlargeth their rejoicing that love him.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/1#v19)</sup> Wisdom raineth down skill and knowledge of understanding, and exalteth them to honour that hold her fast.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/1#v20)</sup> The root of wisdom is to fear the Lord, and the branches thereof are long life.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/1#v21)</sup> The fear of the Lord driveth away sins: and where it is present, it turneth away wrath.

    “The fear of the Lord is a crown of wisdom, making peace and perfect health to flourish; both which are the gifts of God: and it enlargeth their rejoicing that love him. Wisdom raineth down skill and knowledge of understanding, and exalteth them to honour that hold her fast. The root of wisdom is to fear the Lord, and the branches thereof are long life.”
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 18:27](/bible/sirach/18#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/18#v27)</sup> A wise man will fear in every thing, and in the day of sinning he will beware of offence: but a fool will not observe time.

    “A wise man will fear in every thing”. That is integrity guiding you in every step: asking, is this the right move, is this in alignment with the laws of God?
  - **[Proverbs 11:3](/bible/proverbs/11#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/11#v3)</sup> The integrity of the upright shall guide them: but the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them.

    “The integrity of the upright shall guide them”. It goes with what Ecclesiasticus 18 just said: the wise man fears in everything and is wary of offence in the day of sinning. Your integrity guides you into doing the right thing and puts you in position.
