## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 44 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-17, 19, 22-23, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:1-3](/bible/sirach/44#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/44#v1)</sup> Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers that begat us.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/44#v2)</sup> The Lord hath wrought great glory by them through his great power from the beginning.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/44#v3)</sup> Such as did bear rule in their kingdoms, men renowned for their power, giving counsel by their understanding, and declaring prophecies:

- “Let us now praise famous men”, “Such as did bear rule in their kingdoms, men renowned for their power, giving counsel by their understanding, and declaring prophecies”. Men of renown, tied to God, using wisdom to give understanding, and understanding prophecy. The prophecy now is that the time is short: it is coming to an end, so get ready and don’t get caught slipping.
- “Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers that begat us.” We are going to put some respect on our fathers today.
- “Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers that begat us.” These were “men renowned for their power, giving counsel by their understanding, and declaring prophecies.”
- “Let us now praise famous men, and our fathers that begat us.” Believe it or not, we are amongst our fathers right now: the spirit of our forefathers is with the bishops and the deacons.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:4-5](/bible/sirach/44#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/44#v4)</sup> Leaders of the people by their counsels, and by their knowledge of learning meet for the people, wise and eloquent are their instructions:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/44#v5)</sup> Such as found out musical tunes, and recited verses in writing:

- “Leaders of the people by their counsels, and by their knowledge of learning meet for the people, wise and eloquent are their instructions”. “Such as found out musical tunes, and recited verses in writing”: spiritual songs and psalms.
- How do you identify a leader? You can tell who a man is by his speech. “Leaders of the people by their counsels”: you should be able to tell by their godly counsel, coming from the Bible, whether or not they are leaders of the people. Everything the leaders of Israel United in Christ say, they can prove with the scriptures. Why are you sitting under a man who talks about baby oil and cannot break down Romans 3?
- “and by their knowledge of learning meet for the people”: Bishop Kani showed the works from 2 Chronicles and the deeds from Nehemiah 13. “wise and eloquent are their instructions”. The Bible is the rubric that lets us know who the guys are and who are not.
- “Leaders of the people”: not one leader, not two, leaders. We are a nation of leaders. That is why it says to the law and to the prophets: there were many prophets and many holy women throughout the scriptures who were leaders. That is the nation you come from. Their knowledge of learning was “meet for the people”, just right for what the people needed in that time, stage and area.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:6](/bible/sirach/44#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/44#v6)</sup> Rich men furnished with ability, living peaceably in their habitations:

- “Rich men furnished with ability”. When you read rich you think money. No: these are rich in the word of God, as in Colossians, the word dwelling richly. Furnished with the ability to give counsel and understanding.
- “Rich men furnished with ability, living peaceably in their habitations.” Brothers with ability, with leaders under them in every office. They were able to take their wisdom and calm the area they were in, living peaceably with their neighbors, like David, who had all nations in subjection, so that Solomon lived peaceably during his reign.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:7-10](/bible/sirach/44#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/44#v7)</sup> All these were honoured in their generations, and were the glory of their times.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/44#v8)</sup> There be of them, that have left a name behind them, that their praises might be reported.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/44#v9)</sup> And some there be, which have no memorial; who are perished, as though they had never been; and are become as though they had never been born; and their children after them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/44#v10)</sup> But these were merciful men, whose righteousness hath not been forgotten.

- “All these were honoured in their generations, and were the glory of their times.” We want to be the glory of this time, that when our name is spoken they say, that was a man of understanding. The men named were not living in hypocrisy; some of them fell off, but they got right with God.
- “There be of them, that have left a name behind them”, and some “have no memorial”. That doesn’t mean they didn’t do mighty works; you couldn’t name them all, just as in this time there will be a hundred and forty-four thousand and a multitude no man can number. “But these were merciful men, whose righteousness hath not been forgotten.” Not forgotten by God.
- “But these were merciful men, whose righteousness hath not been forgotten.” The Lord has not forgotten them.
- “All these were honoured in their generations, and were the glory of their times. There be of them, that have left a name behind them, that their praises might be reported.” Their leadership was so astounding that we read about it in the Bible and try to extract how they did it. The Bible is the number one selling book every year, and almost every great book quotes it; it is the greatest book with the greatest knowledge ever.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:11-14](/bible/sirach/44#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/44#v11)</sup> With their seed shall continually remain a good inheritance, and their children are within the covenant.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/44#v12)</sup> Their seed standeth fast, and their children for their sakes.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/44#v13)</sup> Their seed shall remain for ever, and their glory shall not be blotted out.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/44#v14)</sup> Their bodies are buried in peace; but their name liveth for evermore.

- “With their seed shall continually remain a good inheritance, and their children are within the covenant.” For us as men and fathers, our legacy is our children, who continue this mission when we are gone, so you can see who that man was through his child and grandchild. In many of our lives we are the first generation in this truth. We don’t want to come into the truth, die, and have our kids go back to the rodeo, our granddaughter out there twerking in booty shorts. We break that generational curse.
- “Their seed standeth fast”, “Their bodies are buried in peace; but their name liveth for evermore.” That is what we want, our name to live for evermore with God.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:15](/bible/sirach/44#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/44#v15)</sup> The people will tell of their wisdom, and the congregation will shew forth their praise.

- “The people will tell of their wisdom, and the congregation will shew forth their praise.” Because they instructed the people when the people came in, and didn’t move in hypocrisy and deceit.
- “The people will tell of their wisdom, and the congregation will shew forth their praise.” Just like today, we still tell of our fathers’ wisdom: when we speak about anything, we as Israelites go back to our history. Some credit Michael Jordan and Kobe or their favorite rappers for their wisdom and understanding, talking like Pac. Our wisdom is our fathers’ wisdom.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:16-17](/bible/sirach/44#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/44#v16)</sup> Enoch pleased the Lord, and was translated, being an example of repentance to all generations.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/44#v17)</sup> Noah was found perfect and righteous; in the time of wrath he was taken in exchange for the world; therefore was he left as a remnant unto the earth, when the flood came.

- “Enoch pleased the Lord, and was translated, being an example of repentance to all generations.” “Noah was found perfect and righteous”. These are the famous men named.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:19](/bible/sirach/44#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/44#v19)</sup> Abraham was a great father of many people: in glory was there none like unto him;

- “Abraham was a great father of many people”.

**[Ecclesiasticus 44:22-23](/bible/sirach/44#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/44#v22)</sup> With Isaac did he establish likewise for Abraham his father’s sake the blessing of all men, and the covenant,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/44#v23)</sup> And made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; among the twelve tribes did he part them.

- The covenant was established with Isaac, “And made it rest upon the head of Jacob. He acknowledged him in his blessing”.
