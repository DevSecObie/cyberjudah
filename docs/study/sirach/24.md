## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 24 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-18, 32-34, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:1-3](/bible/sirach/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/24#v1)</sup> Wisdom shall praise herself, and shall glory in the midst of her people.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/24#v2)</sup> In the congregation of the most High shall she open her mouth, and triumph before his power.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/24#v3)</sup> I came out of the mouth of the most High, and covered the earth as a cloud.

- “Wisdom shall praise herself, and shall glory in the midst of her people. In the congregation of the most High shall she open her mouth”. Wisdom is Christ. “I came out of the mouth of the most High, and covered the earth as a cloud”: Christ came out of the mouth of the Most High.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:4-5](/bible/sirach/24#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/24#v4)</sup> I dwelt in high places, and my throne is in a cloudy pillar.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/24#v5)</sup> I alone compassed the circuit of heaven, and walked in the bottom of the deep.

- “I dwelt in high places, and my throne is in a cloudy pillar.” He walked “in the bottom of the deep”. When you read Genesis 1, when you read Proverbs the 8th chapter, this is what it’s referring to.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:6-7](/bible/sirach/24#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/24#v6)</sup> In the waves of the sea, and in all the earth, and in every people and nation, I got a possession.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/24#v7)</sup> With all these I sought rest: and in whose inheritance shall I abide?

- “In the waves of the sea, and in all the earth, and in every people and nation, I got a possession.” In every people, every nation, kindred and tongue, he has a possession. But watch this: “With all these I sought rest: and in whose inheritance shall I abide?” He came out of the mouth of the Most High, and he asks the question, in whose inheritance shall I abide.

  Precepts:
  - **[Judith 9:12](/bible/judith/9#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/judith/9#v12)</sup> I pray thee, I pray thee, O God of my father, and God of the inheritance of Israel, Lord of the heavens and earth, Creator of the waters, King of every creature, hear thou my prayer:

    He came out of the mouth of the Most High and asked, in whose inheritance shall I abide? “O God of my father, and God of the inheritance of Israel”: the inheritance of who? Of Israel. He came out of the mouth of the Most High, so guess where he’s going to reside: the same place, Israel.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:8](/bible/sirach/24#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/24#v8)</sup> So the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and he that made me caused my tabernacle to rest, and said, Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thine inheritance in Israel.

- The Christians and the apologetics say wisdom is a she, so that is not Christ. “So the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and he that made me caused my tabernacle to rest.” Where did his body rest the first time? In Adam. “And said, Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thine inheritance in Israel”, because out of Adam came Israel.
- “So the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and he that made me caused my tabernacle to rest, and said, Let thy dwelling be in Jacob, and thine inheritance in Israel.”
- We got to take a step back. The Creator of all things, the Most High God of the whole earth, created every nation, kindred and tongue, but he chose us, and he told his Son the same thing: wisdom dwells with us. But remember what we read earlier: the kingdom of heaven suffered violence. How did that come about? Sin.
- “So the Creator of all things gave me a commandment, and he that made me caused my tabernacle to rest”: he that made me is the Father talking to his Son, because Christ is made.

  Precepts:
  - **[Baruch 3:35-37](/bible/baruch/3#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/baruch/3#v35)</sup> This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/baruch/3#v36)</sup> He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/baruch/3#v37)</sup> Afterward did he shew himself upon earth, and conversed with men.

    “This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him.” No one compares to our God, because only he exists.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:9](/bible/sirach/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/24#v9)</sup> He created me from the beginning before the world, and I shall never fail.

- “He created me from the beginning before the world, and I shall never fail.” He created me, from the beginning, before the world; and then I made the world. Wisdom is Christ there.
- Read with verse 1, “Wisdom shall praise herself, and shall glory in the midst of her people”: wisdom has a people, an ethnicity. It is referring to someone, not something.
- “He created me from the beginning before the world, and I shall never fail.” Who’s it speaking of? Jesus the Christ, the Messiah. Once again we read about him in the Old Testament and the Apocrypha. Christ was always there.
- “He created me from the beginning before the world, and I shall never fail.” Christ was created from the beginning, before the world.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 13:10-11](/bible/matthew/13#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/13#v10)</sup> And the disciples came, and said unto him, Why speakest thou unto them in parables?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/13#v11)</sup> He answered and said unto them, Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.

    Who was wisdom? “Why speakest thou unto them in parables?” “Because it is given unto you to know the mysteries of the kingdom of heaven, but to them it is not given.” Christ spoke in parables because he was dealing with a selective audience: he spoke in parables, but revealed it to his children.
  - **[John 16:25](/bible/john/16#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/john/16#v25)</sup> These things have I spoken unto you in proverbs: but the time cometh, when I shall no more speak unto you in proverbs, but I shall shew you plainly of the Father.

    Remember Solomon wrote Proverbs. “These things have I spoken unto you in proverbs”, and the margin gives parables. So a proverb is a parable, and Solomon wrote Proverbs. That would explain why wisdom is called a she.
  - **[Psalms 78:1-3](/bible/psalms/78#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/78#v1)</sup> Give ear, O my people, to my law: incline your ears to the words of my mouth.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/78#v2)</sup> I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/78#v3)</sup> Which we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us.

    So there are parables, proverbs, and the dark sentences read in the prologue of Ecclesiasticus. “Give ear, O my people, to my law”: “I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old.” Asaph says he will utter dark sayings of old: things written by Moses and the prophets before him.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:10](/bible/sirach/24#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/24#v10)</sup> In the holy tabernacle I served before him; and so was I established in Sion.

- “In the holy tabernacle I served before him.” Past tense. Where is that? In Adam, but before that, upstairs: in the heavens where the Father is, the upper room. He served before him up there. “And so was I established in Sion”: when he served him again in Zion.
- “In the holy tabernacle I served before him”: that tabernacle is the one in heaven. “And so was I established in Sion” the same way.
- Who dwells in the holy tabernacle? The high priest. So what was Christ in the holy tabernacle in heaven? The high priest. The same position he had in the heavens, he came down and held on the earth: the same exact thing. The heavenly tabernacle and the earthly tabernacle match.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 8:30](/bible/proverbs/8#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/8#v30)</sup> Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him;

    “Then I was by him, as one brought up with him: and I was daily his delight, rejoicing always before him.” That is how he served before him in the holy tabernacle.
  - **[Hebrews 8:1-2](/bible/hebrews/8#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/8#v1)</sup> Now of the things which we have spoken this is the sum: We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/8#v2)</sup> A minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.

    “We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens”: sitting down. “A minister of the sanctuary”: he is doing again what he did before. “And of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:11](/bible/sirach/24#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/24#v11)</sup> Likewise in the beloved city he gave me rest, and in Jerusalem was my power.

- “Likewise in the beloved city he gave me rest”: there it is again. The beloved city is Jerusalem; now it is fast-forwarding. “And in Jerusalem was my power.” Who had power in Jerusalem and on earth at this time? Solomon.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 6:9](/bible/matthew/6#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/6#v9)</sup> After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.

    Someone will say, if Christ was Solomon, who did Solomon pray to? These gotcha moments are sad. “After this manner therefore pray ye: Our Father which art in heaven, Hallowed be thy name.” When we pray, we pray to the Father. Christ is the way to the Father, so we have faith in Christ and pray through Christ to the Father. Solomon prays to the Father too.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:12](/bible/sirach/24#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/24#v12)</sup> And I took root in an honourable people, even in the portion of the Lord’s inheritance.

- “And I took root in an honourable people, even in the portion of the Lord’s inheritance.” That goes into Solomon, into David, into that line. Wisdom took root in that lineage.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 49:10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)</sup> The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.

    “The sceptre shall not depart from Judah”: rulership. “Nor a lawgiver from between his feet”: this goes back into Adam. It is heavy, layers to the scriptures: there were tribes in the beginning.
  - **[Isaiah 48:1](/bible/isaiah/48#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/48#v1)</sup> Hear ye this, O house of Jacob, which are called by the name of Israel, and are come forth out of the waters of Judah, which swear by the name of the Lord, and make mention of the God of Israel, but not in truth, nor in righteousness.

    “Hear ye this, O house of Jacob, which are called by the name of Israel, and are come forth out of the waters of Judah.” Out of the waters of Judah: that is what Adam was before. That tribe has always been around. The scripture says restore thy judges as at the first; the city coming down from God in Revelation, already prepared, has twelve gates with the twelve tribes on them, showing the twelve tribes were there in the beginning. Moses was told to hide some things, so in Genesis 6 instead of the twelve tribes he wrote the sons of God. Abraham was Judah.
  - **[Jeremiah 10:16](/bible/jeremiah/10#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/10#v16)</sup> The portion of Jacob is not like them: for he is the former of all things; and Israel is the rod of his inheritance: The Lord of hosts is his name.

    “The portion of Jacob is not like them: for he is the former of all things; and Israel is the rod of his inheritance: The Lord of hosts is his name.” The portion of Jacob is Christ, the former of all things. Israel has been around since the very, very beginning.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:13-16](/bible/sirach/24#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/24#v13)</sup> I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree upon the mountains of Hermon.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/24#v14)</sup> I was exalted like a palm tree in En-gaddi, and as a rose plant in Jericho, as a fair olive tree in a pleasant field, and grew up as a plane tree by the water.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/24#v15)</sup> I gave a sweet smell like cinnamon and aspalathus, and I yielded a pleasant odour like the best myrrh, as galbanum, and onyx, and sweet storax, and as the fume of frankincense in the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/24#v16)</sup> As the turpentine tree I stretched out my branches, and my branches are the branches of honour and grace.

- “I was exalted like a cedar in Libanus, and as a cypress tree upon the mountains of Hermon.” He was exalted like a tree: a palm tree, a rose plant, a fair olive tree, a plane tree by the water, giving a sweet smell like cinnamon and myrrh and the fume of frankincense in the tabernacle. “As the turpentine tree I stretched out my branches, and my branches are the branches of honour and grace.” My branches: keep that in mind.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 31:1-9](/bible/ezekiel/31#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ezekiel/31#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in the eleventh year, in the third month, in the first day of the month, that the word of the Lord came unto me, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/31#v2)</sup> Son of man, speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, and to his multitude; Whom art thou like in thy greatness?
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/31#v3)</sup> Behold, the Assyrian was a cedar in Lebanon with fair branches, and with a shadowing shroud, and of an high stature; and his top was among the thick boughs.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/31#v4)</sup> The waters made him great, the deep set him up on high with her rivers running round about his plants, and sent out her little rivers unto all the trees of the field.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/31#v5)</sup> Therefore his height was exalted above all the trees of the field, and his boughs were multiplied, and his branches became long because of the multitude of waters, when he shot forth.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/31#v6)</sup> All the fowls of heaven made their nests in his boughs, and under his branches did all the beasts of the field bring forth their young, and under his shadow dwelt all great nations.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/31#v7)</sup> Thus was he fair in his greatness, in the length of his branches: for his root was by great waters.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/31#v8)</sup> The cedars in the garden of God could not hide him: the fir trees were not like his boughs, and the chesnut trees were not like his branches; nor any tree in the garden of God was like unto him in his beauty.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/31#v9)</sup> I have made him fair by the multitude of his branches: so that all the trees of Eden, that were in the garden of God, envied him.

    “Son of man”: God called Ezekiel son of man, and Christ son of man. What did Christ have that Ezekiel had? A daddy. “Speak unto Pharaoh king of Egypt, and to his multitude; Whom art thou like in thy greatness?” The Lord is showing Ezekiel who Assyria and Egypt are like in their glory.
  - **[Psalms 1:1-3](/bible/psalms/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/1#v1)</sup> Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly, nor standeth in the way of sinners, nor sitteth in the seat of the scornful.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/1#v2)</sup> But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and in his law doth he meditate day and night.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/1#v3)</sup> And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season; his leaf also shall not wither; and whatsoever he doeth shall prosper.

    An example of what the tree and the waters mean: “Blessed is the man that walketh not in the counsel of the ungodly”, “But his delight is in the law of the Lord; and in his law doth he meditate day and night. And he shall be like a tree planted by the rivers of water, that bringeth forth his fruit in his season”: righteousness. When he meditates on God’s laws and commandments, he is like a tree planted by water. So the water is God’s commandments, his laws, his wisdom.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:17](/bible/sirach/24#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/24#v17)</sup> As the vine brought I forth pleasant savour, and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches.

- “As the vine brought I forth pleasant savour, and my flowers are the fruit of honour and riches.” Who is this vine?

  Precepts:
  - **[John 15:1](/bible/john/15#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/john/15#v1)</sup> I am the true vine, and my Father is the husbandman.

    “I am the true vine”: he is the root of us all, from Adam all the way down to now. “And my Father is the husbandman”: the Father planted him in the earth.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:18](/bible/sirach/24#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/24#v18)</sup> I am the mother of fair love, and fear, and knowledge, and holy hope: I therefore, being eternal, am given to all my children which are named of him.

- “I am the mother of fair love, and fear, and knowledge, and holy hope: I therefore, being eternal”: Christ is eternal, but he is not the Ancient of days. “Am given to all my children which are named of him.”
- Given to all my children which are named of whom? Of him: the Father named Christ’s children. The Father handpicked which ones are Christ’s children and which are not. Those are the twelve and those who hear the twelve. The other nations are not his children.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 15:5](/bible/john/15#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/john/15#v5)</sup> I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.

    “I am the vine, ye are the branches: He that abideth in me, and I in him, the same bringeth forth much fruit: for without me ye can do nothing.” Christ said I am the vine: that goes back to Ecclesiasticus 24:17, where wisdom is the vine. Wisdom is divine; that is Christ there.
  - **[John 17:3](/bible/john/17#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/john/17#v3)</sup> And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.

    For the trinitarians: “And this is life eternal, that they might know thee the only true God, and Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.” The only true God is his Father. And Jesus Christ, whom thou hast sent.
  - **[1 John 5:20](/bible/1-john/5#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/1-john/5#v20)</sup> And we know that the Son of God is come, and hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true, and we are in him that is true, even in his Son Jesus Christ. This is the true God, and eternal life.

    The verse they twist and corrupt, read slowly: “And we know that the Son of God is come”: he came, died and resurrected. “And hath given us an understanding, that we may know him that is true”: focus on the him. “And we are in him that is true, even in his Son Jesus Christ.” Who is the him whose Son is Jesus Christ? The Father.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:32](/bible/sirach/24#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/sirach/24#v32)</sup> I will yet make doctrine to shine as the morning, and will send forth her light afar off.

- These scriptures are the Bible speaking about prophecy: what does the scripture say about prophecy? “I will yet make doctrine to shine as the morning, and will send forth her light afar off.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:33](/bible/sirach/24#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/24#v33)</sup> I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and leave it to all ages for ever.

- “I will yet pour out doctrine as prophecy, and leave it to all ages for ever.” The doctrine is poured out as prophecy, and it is left for all ages, ours included.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 1:3](/bible/revelation/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/1#v3)</sup> Blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein: for the time is at hand.

    “Blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy, and keep those things which are written therein.”
  - **[2 Peter 1:19-21](/bible/2-peter/1#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/2-peter/1#v19)</sup> We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/2-peter/1#v20)</sup> Knowing this first, that no prophecy of the scripture is of any private interpretation.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/2-peter/1#v21)</sup> For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.

    “We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed.” Take heed to do what the scriptures say. The Most High God called us into this truth, so we have to take heed of that.

**[Ecclesiasticus 24:34](/bible/sirach/24#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/sirach/24#v34)</sup> Behold that I have not laboured for myself only, but for all them that seek wisdom.

- “Behold that I have not laboured for myself”: we don’t do this work, this labour, for ourselves, the Bible says. It is “for all them that seek wisdom.”
