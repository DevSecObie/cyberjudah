## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 46 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 6-7, 9-11, 13-18, 20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 46:1](/bible/sirach/46#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/46#v1)</sup> Jesus the son of Nave was valiant in the wars, and was the successor of Moses in prophecies, who according to his name was made great for the saving of the elect of God, and taking vengeance of the enemies that rose up against them, that he might set Israel in their inheritance.

- “Jesus the son of Nave was valiant in the wars, and was the successor of Moses in prophecies, who according to his name was made great for the saving of the elect of God”. He was made a great man for saving the elect of God, “and taking vengeance of the enemies that rose up against them, that he might set Israel in their inheritance.”
- This is Joshua. It says Jesus because it is the same name, and it has the same meaning: Yeshua, Yehoshua, Jesus, all the same thing.
- He “was valiant in the wars, and was the successor of Moses in prophecies, who according to his name was made great for the saving of the elect of God, and taking vengeance of the enemies that rose up against them, that he might set Israel in their inheritance.” Made great for the saving of the elect of God, and he was the one who was going to set Israel in their inheritance.
- Joshua was from the tribe of Ephraim, and he was likely born around 1485 BC, in Egypt. These are approximates.

  - **[Numbers 13:8](/bible/numbers/13#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/13#v8)</sup> Of the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea the son of Nun.

    “Of the tribe of Ephraim, Oshea the son of Nun.” That settles the tribe. Joshua was from the tribe of Ephraim, and Oshea the son of Nun is the same name as Joshua.

    “These are the names of the men which Moses sent to spy out the land. And Moses called Oshea the son of Nun Jehoshua.” Moses himself changed it, and Jehoshua is the same name again.
  - **[Numbers 14:30-32](/bible/numbers/14#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/14#v30)</sup> Doubtless ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I sware to make you dwell therein, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.
    >

    >

    Out of that whole generation, who made it out of Egypt? All those that were twenty and under, and Joshua and Caleb. “Doubtless ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I sware to make you dwell therein, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 46:6](/bible/sirach/46#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/46#v6)</sup> And with hailstones of mighty power he made the battle to fall violently upon the nations, and in the descent of Beth-horon he destroyed them that resisted, that the nations might know all their strength, because he fought in the sight of the Lord, and he followed the Mighty One.

- Joshua was with the business. “And with hailstones of mighty power he made the battle to fall violently upon the nations, and in the descent of Beth-horon he destroyed them that resisted.” He was in many wars; he fought many battles.
- “that the nations might know all their strength”, because he fought in the side of the Lord, and “he followed the Mighty One.” He followed the Mighty One, and that is why the nations came to know their strength.

**[Ecclesiasticus 46:7](/bible/sirach/46#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/46#v7)</sup> In the time of Moses also he did a work of mercy, he and Caleb the son of Jephunne, in that they withstood the congregation, and withheld the people from sin, and appeased the wicked murmuring.

- “He and Caleb the son of Jephunne, in that they withstood the congregation, and withheld the people from sin, and appeased the wicked murmuring.” The people were unruly, and Joshua and Caleb withstood them and stood firm in the laws of God. This is how you gain your legacy and your name. Much is given, much is required: you are going to go up against the masses with this word, and that is what we are doing now, as the brothers did in Atlanta, standing firm in the word of God.

**[Ecclesiasticus 46:9](/bible/sirach/46#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/46#v9)</sup> The Lord gave strength also unto Caleb, which remained with him unto his old age: so that he entered upon the high places of the land, and his seed obtained it for an heritage:

- “The Lord gave strength also unto Caleb, which remained with him unto his old age”. He was old, but still fervent in this word and still stood for the laws of God. There is no expiration and no retirement; retirement is death or the kingdom. Until our dying breath we strive for this truth, in a wheelchair or in your cabin.
- “The Lord gave strength also unto Caleb, which remained with him unto his old age: so that he entered upon the high places of the land, and his seed obtained it for an heritage.” Caleb was an example, and it is going to be very similar with Joshua, because it was around the same time.
- Caleb was from the tribe of Judah, he was born in Egypt, and we allot him the same birth year as Joshua, around 1485 BC, because it doesn't specifically state when.
- “The Lord gave strength also unto Caleb, which remained with him unto his old age.” That is why he was that way. The Lord gave him that strength. Everybody don't get that.
