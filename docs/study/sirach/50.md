## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 50 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 25-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 50:1](/bible/sirach/50#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/50#v1)</sup> Simon the high priest, the son of Onias, who in his life repaired the house again, and in his days fortified the temple:

- “Simon the high priest, the son of Onias, who in his life repaired the house again, and in his days fortified the temple”. These renowned men earned their name by building back, walking in integrity and moving right. Go back through these chapters; there is a lot more in them.

**[Ecclesiasticus 50:25](/bible/sirach/50#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/50#v25)</sup> There be two manner of nations which my heart abhorreth, and the third is no nation:

- Remember “not a people”: it’s in Deuteronomy 32:21, it’s in Isaiah 7 and 8, broken that it be not a people. “There be two manner of nations which my heart abhorreth”: the writer says there are two nations my heart cannot stand, I hate them. “And the third is no nation”: no nation, which is not a people. Two of them he hates, and the third is not a people.

**[Ecclesiasticus 50:26](/bible/sirach/50#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/50#v26)</sup> They that sit upon the mountain of Samaria, and they that dwell among the Philistines, and that foolish people that dwell in Sichem.

- “They that sit upon the mountain of Samaria”: the Greeks, because the Greeks took over Samaria. Write that down: those that sit upon Samaria, put a parentheses and put Greeks. “And they that dwell among the Philistines”: Hamites. He despised those two nations; they were always our mortal enemies.
- “And that foolish people that dwell in Sichem.” Sichem is a Latinized version of the word; it is Shechem, the capital of Ephraim. That foolish people is the northern kingdom.
- In verse 25 he says they’re no nation, not a people. Why? Because they would be broken, based upon Isaiah 7:8.
- Ecclesiasticus was written by Jesus the son of Sirach, a wise man, around 200 to 175 BC, in the time of the Greeks, to fight against the Greek influence of his day.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:21](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v21)</sup> They have moved me to jealousy with that which is not God; they have provoked me to anger with their vanities: and I will move them to jealousy with those which are not a people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation.

    “They have moved me to jealousy with that which is not God; they have provoked me to anger with their vanities: and I will move them to jealousy with those which are not a people; I will provoke them to anger with a foolish nation.” Those which are not a people, a foolish nation: the same words as Ecclesiasticus 50, the foolish people that dwell in Shechem. That is talking about the northern kingdom.
  - **[2 Maccabees 6:1-2](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v1)</sup> Not long after this the king sent an old man of Athens to compel the Jews to depart from the laws of their fathers, and not to live after the laws of God:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-maccabees/6#v2)</sup> And to pollute also the temple in Jerusalem, and to call it the temple of Jupiter Olympius; and that in Garizim, of Jupiter the Defender of strangers, as they did desire that dwelt in the place.

    “The king sent an old man of Athens to compel the Jews to depart from the laws of their fathers, and not to live after the laws of God.” The Greek king commanded Judah to leave the laws of our God and live by Greek laws and fashions. That is assimilation, integration, Hellenization: we became Greek citizens, the same as African Americans today.
  - **[1 Maccabees 1:1](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-maccabees/1#v1)</sup> And it happened, after that Alexander son of Philip, the Macedonian, who came out of the land of Chettiim, had smitten Darius king of the Persians and Medes, that he reigned in his stead, the first over Greece,

    “Alexander son of Philip, the Macedonian, who came out of the land of Chettiim, had smitten Darius king of the Persians and Medes.” Alexander the Great’s father was Philip the Macedonian. Chettiim is Cyprus. Alexander killed Darius the Third of the Persians and Medes; the Greeks began to conquer the dark nations at this time.
