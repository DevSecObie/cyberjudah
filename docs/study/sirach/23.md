## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 23 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2, 5-6, 11, 22-23, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 23:2](/bible/sirach/23#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/23#v2)</sup> Who will set scourges over my thoughts, and the discipline of wisdom over mine heart? that they spare me not for mine ignorances, and it pass not by my sins:

- We have to learn discipline. “Who will set scourges over my thoughts, and the discipline of wisdom over mine heart? that they spare me not for mine ignorances, and it pass not by my sins”. We learn discipline so we don't end up looking crazy out here.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 6:17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v17)</sup> For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love;

    “For the very true beginning of her is the desire of discipline; and the care of discipline is love”. The very true beginning of following after wisdom is the desire of discipline. So we have to fight temptation, fight off the devil, and overcome the beast in order to learn that discipline.
  - **[Ephesians 6:11](/bible/ephesians/6#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/6#v11)</sup> Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.

    “Put on the whole armour of God, that ye may be able to stand against the wiles of the devil.” We have to put on all of the armour. If we're not studying, we're not battling; we're just falling victim.
  - **[2 Esdras 16:50](/bible/2-esdras/16#v50)**
    > <sup>[50](/bible/2-esdras/16#v50)</sup> So shall righteousness hate iniquity, when she decketh herself, and shall accuse her to her face, when he cometh that shall defend him that diligently searcheth out every sin upon earth.

    “So shall righteousness hate iniquity”: we have to discipline our minds to hate iniquity. “When he cometh that shall defend him that diligently searcheth out every sin upon earth.” If we want the Lord to defend us, we have to learn to hate iniquity, to hate sin.

**[Ecclesiasticus 23:5](/bible/sirach/23#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/23#v5)</sup> Turn away from me vain hopes and concupiscence, and thou shalt hold him up that is desirous always to serve thee.

- “Turn away from me vain hopes and concupiscence.” Concupiscence is strong sexual desire, lust: lustfulness, sexual appetite, sexual longing, sexual passion, libido, sex drive, the biological urge, lechery, excessive or offensive sexual desire. It goes right back to wine and women making men of understanding fall away, and to the men that dabble at harlots: they tend to be concupiscent.
- It goes to the porn. A lot of brothers deal with that spirit of porn, and brothers and sisters young and old have fallen victim to it so excessively that the spirit of concupiscence comes upon them and they start to lose their minds. It affects their behavior, their mental state and their marriage. Some brothers in here are battling porn so much they begin to despise their spouse, because they are looking at the woman on the screen and saying my wife looks nothing like that, and they push away their loved ones.
- When you watch porn, porn watches you too. The eyes are the windows of the soul, and when you open your eyes to these things, they open themselves to you. The demons and spirits on those people in the video jump on you. And it evolves: it starts as one thing and it grows like a drug, like the weed that is not strong enough anymore so you upgrade. The dopamine is a drug. Porn, food, alcohol, sex, money: they are copes for low self-esteem, anger and personal issues a man refuses to bring forward.
- Married or single, porn is found among us. That concupiscent spirit has been with many of us a long time; many were exposed to sex very early, and being exposed to such mature content so young puts a stronger sexual urge upon you down the line. This is not an attack. It is us reasoning in the scriptures. The prayer is for God to turn concupiscence away, “and thou shalt hold him up that is desirous always to serve thee.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 23:6](/bible/sirach/23#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/23#v6)</sup> Let not the greediness of the belly nor lust of the flesh take hold of me; and give not over me thy servant into an impudent mind.

- “Let not the greediness of the belly nor lust of the flesh take hold of me.” They go hand in hand. The greediness of the belly is food and drink; the lust of the flesh is the sexual desire. Lust and greed are synonymous: it is all a habit, and you keep feeding it and it gets bigger and bigger. When it takes hold of you, you are so consumed in it you cannot stop yourself: so consumed in food and liquor you are morbidly obese and cannot walk half a mile, so consumed in porn that you cannot stay up. A virgin who has never had a woman and does that excessively conditions his body to itself, at its own speed and pace, not to a spouse; the mind knows the difference between a woman and a hand, and it ends in letting off immediately, in erectile dysfunction.
- Don’t let it take hold of you to the point where you say, I can’t fight it, I keep falling, I’m not coming to the school anymore, I’m no good. That is what Satan wants you to believe. You better hop your ass in the shower, rinse yourself off, and come to the school. Let it take hold and you begin to despise yourself and your loved ones, and it evolves until you are in cuffs, from the heterosexual to the rape one, the incest one, the child one, because they will find you. Or it leads you to want the actual thing, out in the streets looking for harlots.
- “And give not over me thy servant into an impudent mind.” See what it leads to: the impudent mind, the same as with wine and women and harlots. You grow disrespectful. You are cursing your wife out, telling her you don’t want her anymore, put me on child support, ready to destroy your whole house for what you watch. When you are told, brother, you’re fat, sister, you’re big, you get mad, and Satan tells you you are just big boned. Nobody ever found a fat skeleton. Big boned is a lie with reason.
- That is why he says give not thy servant into an impudent mind: you begin to reject those who tell you that you are battling lust, of the belly or of the flesh. Satan already has you convinced: I’m good, I got it under control. Usually who you are is the answer to all your problems. You know yourself better than anybody: you know if you are big, if you are lustful, if you are battling porn. When you deny the problem is there, that is when the problem starts.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 3:20](/bible/revelation/3#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/revelation/3#v20)</sup> Behold, I stand at the door, and knock: if any man hear my voice, and open the door, I will come in to him, and will sup with him, and he with me.

    This is the spiritual side of it: the mind is a door, and the eyes are the windows to the soul. “Behold, I stand at the door, and knock.” Christ is not talking about the door of your house. The door is your spirit, your mind. That is the righteous side.

**[Ecclesiasticus 23:11](/bible/sirach/23#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/23#v11)</sup> A man that useth much swearing shall be filled with iniquity, and the plague shall never depart from his house: if he shall offend, his sin shall be upon him: and if he acknowledge not his sin, he maketh a double offence: and if he swear in vain, he shall not be innocent, but his house shall be full of calamities.

- “A man that useth much swearing shall be filled with iniquity, and the plague shall never depart from his house.” He doesn’t keep his promises and his vows. “If he shall offend, his sin shall be upon him: and if he acknowledge not his sin, he maketh a double offence.” When you don’t acknowledge your problem, you make it worse. When you pretend you have it all together and you don’t, you add a double offence. That is why we read, be not ashamed when it concerneth thy soul. Any brother who says he didn’t go through anything in here, online or offline, is a liar: a double offence.
- “And if he swear in vain, he shall not be innocent, but his house shall be full of calamities.” Make false promises and false vows you don’t keep, and you will find yourself in trouble.
