## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 49 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, 10-11, 13, 15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:1](/bible/sirach/49#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/49#v1)</sup> The remembrance of Josias is like the composition of the perfume that is made by the art of the apothecary: it is sweet as honey in all mouths, and as musick at a banquet of wine.

- “The remembrance of Josias is like the composition of the perfume that is made by the art of the apothecary”. When the apothecary mixed the oils, it was set apart, holy, consecrated, not like anything else. Josias was like that; and if you are not like anything else, you are special.

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:2](/bible/sirach/49#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/49#v2)</sup> He behaved himself uprightly in the conversion of the people, and took away the abominations of iniquity.

- “He behaved himself uprightly in the conversion of the people, and took away the abominations of iniquity.” As the people were converting back to God, he wasn’t using his position to take advantage of them, handling the word of God deceitfully, or trying to mack the sisters on the side under cover of counsel. He moved uprightly.
- Compare the church: Eddie Long, the pastor in the speedos; what did he convert them into? And the people still held him up in that chair afterward. The church is crazy.

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:3](/bible/sirach/49#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/49#v3)</sup> He directed his heart unto the Lord, and in the time of the ungodly he established the worship of God.

- “He directed his heart unto the Lord, and in the time of the ungodly he established the worship of God.” In this time of the ungodly we are going to establish the worship of God. That is how you become the famous man: in the midst of all this debauchery we don’t walk in hypocrisy, covetousness or murmuring. We direct the people to God and show them how to live godly, and the nations will say, these are those guys we heard about who were going to come back on the earth.

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:4-6](/bible/sirach/49#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/49#v4)</sup> All, except David and Ezekias and Josias, were defective: for they forsook the law of the most High, even the kings of Juda failed.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/49#v5)</sup> Therefore he gave their power unto others, and their glory to a strange nation.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/49#v6)</sup> They burnt the chosen city of the sanctuary, and made the streets desolate, according to the prophecy of Jeremias.

- “All, except David and Ezekias and Josias, were defective: for they forsook the law of the most High, even the kings of Juda failed.” All these men failed. “Therefore he gave their power unto others, and their glory to a strange nation.” That is where the northern kingdom came in. “They burnt the chosen city of the sanctuary, and made the streets desolate, according to the prophecy of Jeremias.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:10](/bible/sirach/49#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/49#v10)</sup> And of the twelve prophets let the memorial be blessed, and let their bones flourish again out of their place: for they comforted Jacob, and delivered them by assured hope.

- “And of the twelve prophets let the memorial be blessed, and let their bones flourish again out of their place: for they comforted Jacob, and delivered them by assured hope.”
- “And of the twelve prophets let the memorial be blessed, and let their bones flourish again out of their place.” These are the twelve, and they come back in the last days. Every time they come back, their job is the same: “for they comforted Jacob.” To comfort Jacob is to comfort us, the children of Israel, in this life, because we are in the last days and going into some hard times, and Satan is not playing with any of us.
- “And delivered them by assured hope.” That is what this class is about: the power of hope. The prophets deliver Jacob by an assured hope, and that hope is for the salvation to come.
- Look at the movement in place right now: brothers going out to every city in the Northeast on the migrant blitz, reaching the scattered of Israel throughout the four corners of the earth, the northern kingdom and the southern kingdom, as Isaiah 11 speaks of. It is a serious mission, and you are part of a great thing in this earth. That is why you have to shake the boy spirit and become men.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    “For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.” This is how the prophets comfort Jacob: through the scriptures. That is where our hope is going to be, brothers.

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:11](/bible/sirach/49#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/49#v11)</sup> How shall we magnify Zorobabel? even he was as a signet on the right hand:

- “How shall we magnify Zorobabel? even he was as a signet on the right hand”.

**[Ecclesiasticus 49:13](/bible/sirach/49#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/49#v13)</sup> And among the elect was Neemias, whose renown is great, who raised up for us the walls that were fallen, and set up the gates and the bars, and raised up our ruins again.

- “And among the elect was Neemias, whose renown is great, who raised up for us the walls that were fallen, and set up the gates and the bars, and raised up our ruins again.” Along with raising up the walls of Jerusalem, he established the laws: you are not going to get our people to shop on the sabbath. These were the men of renown.

- “Neither was there a man born like unto Joseph, a governor of his brethren, a stay of the people, whose bones were regarded of the Lord.”
