## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 10-11, 17-21, 30, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:10](/bible/sirach/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/4#v10)</sup> Be as a father unto the fatherless, and instead of an husband unto their mother: so shalt thou be as the son of the most High, and he shall love thee more than thy mother doth.

- “Be as a father unto the fatherless, and instead of an husband unto their mother”. Older men, some young men have no father in this truth. Be their father, their mentor, give direction and spark this in them. You're not trying to bag the mom. Make sure the next generation has these scriptures. Without a father these young men will be led astray. Keep guiding and molding them until they reach that age. Young people, I'm proud of you. Stay faithful, stay godly, and let's take this kingdom.
- Some of the sisters are single mothers, not by choice, or because they did not know the truth. This is not tearing them down; it was the strong independent black woman who says, I don’t need no man, I can raise these kids by myself, who was torn down. Now that you are in this truth, these are the instructions for a single mother who does not want her child to be a statistic.
- “Be as a father unto the fatherless.” The men you meet in this truth are to be role models to your children who have no father of God, no man of God, in their life. That is how righteous men think. “And instead of an husband unto their mother”: they are not trying to get in your drawers because they see you with a child. “So shalt thou be as the son of the most High”: they care about the child as an Israelite child and are trying to set the example for him.
- So the single mothers who come around here are to let the men groom their children to be successful serving God, so they will not be a statistic. Do not just think, I need to find a baby daddy; if it happens, fine, but that should not be the first thing on your mind. And the men are not to take advantage of the women who are single mothers.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:11](/bible/sirach/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/4#v11)</sup> Wisdom exalteth her children, and layeth hold of them that seek her.

- “Wisdom exalteth her children, and layeth hold of them that seek her.” Wisdom is Christ, and wisdom lays hold of those who seek her. So how do we seek the Lord?

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:17](/bible/sirach/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/4#v17)</sup> For at the first she will walk with him by crooked ways, and bring fear and dread upon him, and torment him with her discipline, until she may trust his soul, and try him by her laws.

- “For at the first she will walk with him by crooked ways.” In this truth you have to learn to wean yourself off. When you first came through the doors you were still battling what you were battling, and wisdom walked with you in your crooked ways. As you learn, study and apply, you begin to change: “and bring fear and dread upon him.” Fear and dread enter you: I can’t do that anymore. You change your friends, how you dress, how you behave, how you speak.
- “And torment him with her discipline.” What you got away with in the world you can’t do anymore, because now the Most High is holding you accountable. Discipline is torment: it is structure and order, pressure applied to the flesh. In the world you did what the flesh wanted; with God’s laws it is a battle: I can’t watch that, I can’t see her anymore, I can’t be around them. The Bishop has said, I’m not always motivated, but I always stay disciplined. Discipline is not fun and not easy. “Until she may trust his soul, and try him by her laws”: until she can trust you to do right by her and teach her to the rest of the people, and she puts you through a trial.
- This is the secret sauce, the number one thing that helps you overcome that spirit. “For at the first she will walk with him by crooked ways.” At the first, when you just come into this truth. The she is wisdom, the Holy Spirit. Crooked ways like lust and watching porn: wisdom still walks with you even though you are not fully right. That is the Holy Spirit you all have, the thing that keeps telling you, what are you doing, the Lord is going to judge you.
- “And bring fear and dread upon him.” That is the secret sauce: fear. Bad things happen to you, and you know why: you were watching porn, and the Lord jacked you up. The reason many will not stop is that they have no fear; it is pleasurable. But when you understand God is going to judge you and bad things are going to happen, you will not mess with it, the same way a kid does not go against your order because he knows you will whoop him. Live by the two W’s: when anything happens, ask yourself why it happened, and what God is trying to teach you. The Lord is going to start jacking some brothers up, and they will know why, and that is when they will stop.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 10:31](/bible/hebrews/10#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/hebrews/10#v31)</sup> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.

    “It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.” Do you want to fall into the hands of God? Then stop watching the porn.
  - **[Philippians 2:12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.

    “Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence.” You can come in here acting holy, saying shalom, Most High Christ bless, while dealing with a lot of demons.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:18](/bible/sirach/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/4#v18)</sup> Then will she return the straight way unto him, and comfort him, and shew him her secrets.

- “Then will she return the straight way unto him.” Before you walked in crooked ways; now you walk the straight path. Crooked is easy, straight is harder, but better, because it leads to life. “And comfort him, and shew him her secrets”: the deep, dark sayings. Adam and Eve, America in the Bible, nuclear wars, Trump’s policies, the slave ship, America’s destruction and World War III in the Bible; understanding of Genesis, of Revelation, of the law. In the midst of the torment of discipline you are comforted by learning and applying new things, growing and evolving.
- “Then will she return the straight way unto him, and comfort him, and shew him her secrets.” When she can trust your soul you will be comforted, and that is when you will start understanding the secrets of the Bible.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:19](/bible/sirach/4#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/4#v19)</sup> But if he go wrong, she will forsake him, and give him over to his own ruin.

- “But if he go wrong, she will forsake him.” Here is the stipulation: if you turn your back on it and give up the fight, she forsakes you. You leave, but the spirit stays here. If you came in as Tyrone and became Azariah and decide to leave, you leave as Tyrone again; Azariah stays here. “And give him over to his own ruin”: your own ruin, not ours. As they said at an old job, we don’t fire you, you fire you.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:20](/bible/sirach/4#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/4#v20)</sup> Observe the opportunity, and beware of evil; and be not ashamed when it concerneth thy soul.

- “Observe the opportunity, and beware of evil.” Observe the opportunity you have here while you still have it. “And be not ashamed when it concerneth thy soul.” When it comes to what you are battling, make yourself open to communicate. Speak to someone, a counsel, about it. Don’t hold it inside, or wisdom will leave you and it will take hold of you and you will be gone.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:21](/bible/sirach/4#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/4#v21)</sup> For there is a shame that bringeth sin; and there is a shame which is glory and grace.

- “For there is a shame that bringeth sin.” That is your secret sins: your porn, the evil you are involved in, your lust, whatever it is. You know what it is, and you are ashamed of it.
- “And there is a shame which is glory and grace.” That is the shame of confessing it. When you acknowledge it, speak on it, see it as a problem, get counsel for it and fight it, whether homosexuality, violence or drug abuse, it brings shame and you are embarrassed. But because you acknowledged it and spoke to your counselor, it brings forth glory, the kingdom, and grace, mercy, as opposed to the double offence.

**[Ecclesiasticus 4:30](/bible/sirach/4#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/4#v30)</sup> Be not as a lion in thy house, nor frantick among thy servants.

- “Be not as a lion in thy house, nor frantick among thy servants.” This forbids the tyrant-like spirit in the house. Roaring at home breaks a wife's spirit, provokes children as they watch their mother treated badly and creates fear instead of order. The family should see stable leadership and a kingly presence rather than constant intimidation.
- Headship still has to be exercised; a man cannot be only a figurehead. Sometimes firmness includes raising the voice, but cursing and tantrums must not become the default. A woman's silence may mean she no longer wants to deal with the outburst, not that she has been wisely led. Leaders build. When a wrong spirit or barrier must be broken down, building must follow.

  - **[Jeremiah 1:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/1#v9)</sup> Then the Lord put forth his hand, and touched my mouth. And the Lord said unto me, Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/1#v10)</sup> See, I have this day set thee over the nations and over the kingdoms, to root out, and to pull down, and to destroy, and to throw down, to build, and to plant.

    “Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth.” The words used to exhort and convince must be God's words, in the home as well as among the people. Jeremiah is set over nations and kingdoms to root out, pull down, destroy and throw down, but also “to build, and to plant.”
