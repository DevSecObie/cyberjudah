## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 36 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-8, 15-16, 22-24, 26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:1](/bible/sirach/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/36#v1)</sup> Have mercy upon us, O Lord God of all, and behold us:

- Today's class is entitled A Star Called Wormwood, and it continues the understanding of the book of Revelation, due to the latest events occurring between Iran and the United States of America, the impeachment of Donald Trump, and so forth. Things are at work in the spirit and we need desperately to prepare ourselves. Like Christ said in Matthew 24 regarding the last days, there shall be wars and rumours of wars; rumours are things in the talk, in the ear, in the air, that have not occurred as yet but may and will come to pass.
- “Have mercy upon us, O Lord God of all, and behold us:” This is what we want: we want mercy. Like it says in Isaiah 14, the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel. It says yet because we messed up; mentally and spiritually we are still messed up as a whole, as a people.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:2-3](/bible/sirach/36#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/36#v2)</sup> And send thy fear upon all the nations that seek not after thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/36#v3)</sup> Lift up thy hand against the strange nations, and let them see thy power.

- “And send thy fear upon all the nations that seek not after thee. Lift up thy hand against the strange nations, and let them see thy power.” Sirach is asking the Lord to lift up his hand against the strange nations; these are the non-Israelite nations.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:4-5](/bible/sirach/36#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/36#v4)</sup> As thou wast sanctified in us before them: so be thou magnified among them before us.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/36#v5)</sup> And let them know thee, as we have known thee, that there is no God but only thou, O God.

- “As thou wast sanctified in us before them: so be thou magnified among them before us.” Because he is going to smite these down, every last one of them, like it says in Daniel 7:9. “And let them know thee, as we have known thee, that there is no God but only thou, O God.” The whole world is going to know that there is only one God, that's it, and we are going to make all nations bow the knee.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:6-7](/bible/sirach/36#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/36#v6)</sup> Shew new signs, and make other strange wonders: glorify thy hand and thy right arm, that they may set forth thy wondrous works.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/36#v7)</sup> Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath: take away the adversary, and destroy the enemy.

- “Shew new signs, and make other strange wonders: glorify thy hand and thy right arm, that they may set forth thy wondrous works.” You see all the fires in Australia, things happening all across the earth: a billion animals got burnt up in Australia. What is happening on the earth? What is going on? “Raise up indignation, and pour out wrath:” That is what we are seeing happen, but because we have spiritual blindness on we don't correlate what we see on the news to the Bible prophecies. “take away the adversary, and destroy the enemy.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:8](/bible/sirach/36#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/36#v8)</sup> Make the time short, remember the covenant, and let them declare thy wonderful works.

- Make the time short: that is what we want right there. We often ask how long we are going to be in this captivity; Sirach said in the spirit, make the time short. Christ said the same thing in Matthew 24:22: except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved, but for the elect's sake, Israel, those days shall be shortened. And Paul said the time is short: they that have wives, be as though they had none, meaning put God first (1 Corinthians 7:29). Which would you rather have when the Lord returns: you out doing the work, or you huddled under your woman's skirt?
- Some of us know and believe the end is coming; some of us are just hoping the end is coming; and some are just here in unbelief, abiding for a job or a spouse, whichever comes first, and then we will never see you again, and God won't see you any more either. Christ cut them sharp in John 6:26: ye seek me, not because ye saw the miracles, but because ye did eat of the loaves, and were filled. Some of us listening, in here or online, are only following because there is some benefit you are looking for, food, a job or a spouse. Some people will only love you as much as they can use you; their loyalty ends where the benefits stop. Paul said for yet a little while, and he that shall come will come, and will not tarry; the just shall live by faith, but if any man draw back, my soul shall have no pleasure in him (Hebrews 10:37-39). You leave this truth, you have drawn back unto perdition, hell.
- “remember the covenant, and let them declare thy wonderful works.” That is what we are doing: we are declaring the covenants and the wonderful works of the Most High God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 24:22](/bible/matthew/24#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/24#v22)</sup> And except those days should be shortened, there should no flesh be saved: but for the elect’s sake those days shall be shortened.

    Make the time short. Christ said the same thing: “And except those days should be shortened,” He is going to shorten the time. “there should no flesh be saved:” Because if you don't shorten the time, everybody would die, all of Israel; that is what he is talking about. “but for the elect’s sake” That is the proof that it is Israel: we are the elect. “those days shall be shortened.” The days shall be shortened.
  - **[1 Corinthians 7:29](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v29)</sup> But this I say, brethren, the time is short: it remaineth, that both they that have wives be as though they had none;

    “But this I say, brethren, the time is short:” You were wondering why we went here, ain't that talking about women? The time is short, brothers. “it remaineth, that both they that have wives” That's you married men. “be as though they had none;” Meaning what? Put God first. That is all it is saying. It doesn't mean drop your wife like a bad habit; it means put God first, and that is something black men have a hard time doing, with all them damn Keith Sweat songs. Millennials, who is the latest crying Negro out there crying for women? All this crying for women has to stop; the Most High is going to make this thing stop. You brothers are going to stop running around kissing a woman's behind. That is what Paul is saying. Which would you rather have when the Lord returns: you out doing the work, or you huddled under your woman's skirt, oh baby, I love you? Here comes the Lord and you ain't doing his work, you are doing her work. What is my servant doing?
  - **[John 6:24-27](/bible/john/6#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/john/6#v24)</sup> When the people therefore saw that Jesus was not there, neither his disciples, they also took shipping, and came to Capernaum, seeking for Jesus.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/john/6#v25)</sup> And when they had found him on the other side of the sea, they said unto him, Rabbi, when camest thou hither?
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/john/6#v26)</sup> Jesus answered them and said, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Ye seek me, not because ye saw the miracles, but because ye did eat of the loaves, and were filled.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/john/6#v27)</sup> Labour not for the meat which perisheth, but for that meat which endureth unto everlasting life, which the Son of man shall give unto you: for him hath God the Father sealed.

    Some of us know and believe the end is coming. Some of us are just hoping the end is coming. And some are just here in unbelief abiding: abiding time for a job, or a spouse, whichever comes first, and then we will never see you again, and God won't see you any more either. The ones that are only here for a spouse are the ones that can't wait for the proving process, six months to a year; they do the back-door marriage. Oh, that IUIC camp has some pretty women in there; oh, they have some handsome dudes in there; then bang bang, and they are out in the world again. Some of them used to sit right here; now they went to another camp and got another man, measuring tape and all.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:15](/bible/sirach/36#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/36#v15)</sup> Give testimony unto those that thou hast possessed from the beginning, and raise up prophets that have been in thy name.

- “Give testimony unto those that thou hast possessed from the beginning, and raise up prophets that have been in thy name.” Raise up prophets, not weak men: prophets taught in his name. These brothers are going to keep it pushing; they will take the work seriously and constantly endure. That is why we are not going to forget the joy.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:16](/bible/sirach/36#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/36#v16)</sup> Reward them that wait for thee, and let thy prophets be found faithful.

- “Reward them that wait for thee”. God is going to reward them that wait upon him. We are not telling you to take vengeance on the enemy, because vengeance is of the Lord. We leave it in the hands of God, but one thing we know: judgment is coming for this place.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 28:1](/bible/sirach/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/28#v1)</sup> He that revengeth shall find vengeance from the Lord, and he will surely keep his sins in remembrance.

    “He that revengeth shall find vengeance from the Lord”. We are supposed to understand that among our own people, and so with the enemy, because the Lord said vengeance belongs to him. That is why we wait for him, and he rewards them that wait.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:22](/bible/sirach/36#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/36#v22)</sup> The beauty of a woman cheereth the countenance, and a man loveth nothing better.

- A husband needs a wife as a helper and a stay: stability. If he has a wife who is not spiritually mature and stable, his own stability as a man will be weak in times of trouble. Her mind has to be right so she can be a proper aid to the husband who wants to do the right thing. And she must be teachable: even with her mind right, there will be moments when you have to teach her, and she has to be willing to sit down and listen to you, her husband, not the captains. She has to be teachable so you can put wisdom into her mind, until her mind fully becomes your mind. That is why she is called woman: she is of man.
- “The beauty of a woman cheereth the countenance”: men come home from war, from all kinds of crap out there, and the woman builds him up, so he is ready to go to war again and take care of her. A woman who tears him down makes him not even want to go home. His countenance needs cheering because he caught hell out there, and her beauty does it, physical and spiritual.
- Beauty goes much further than what you see. A woman on a show was asked where she wanted a man to take her on a date, and she said, You make that decision, I’m here to follow you. When she spoke all the other women faded to black, because her spirit was so different from theirs; the brother could not even get his words out.
- “And a man loveth nothing better”: he wants that because he needs it. Intimacy even helps his health; some men live in such turmoil in the house that it tears them down physically. The two need each other.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:23](/bible/sirach/36#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/36#v23)</sup> If there be kindness, meekness, and comfort, in her tongue, then is not her husband like other men.

- “If there be kindness, meekness, and comfort, in her tongue, then is not her husband like other men.” The usual answer is that he has set his house in order, but that is learned response: everyone answering the same way because we only look at it in one direction.
- Describe her husband. The wife who does these things turns him into a super god, a superhero; he would be unstoppable. Look what men will do in wickedness: they will sell dope and risk going to jail, five months in and locked up, for a woman who isn’t dealing with them right. What would he do for a woman dealing with him right, in righteousness? She is his comfort, his aid, his cheerleader, on his team, a help meet right for him, and he would have the confidence and fortitude to do anything. He would not be like any of the men we currently see. We can organize this and build that; with that woman in your corner, there is no stopping us.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:24](/bible/sirach/36#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/36#v24)</sup> He that getteth a wife beginneth a possession, a help like unto himself, and a pillar of rest.

- “He that getteth a wife beginneth a possession”. If you get a wife, you get a possession. So your wife is your possession. That's why, back in Genesis, the Lord gave her to Adam; he brought her to Adam and he gave her to Adam.
- The reason why it says you beginneth a possession is because just getting a woman alone, that ain't it. The possession you getting, you might got to do some rehab work. You going to have to teach her, you're going to have to guide her, you're going to have to correct her, you're going to have to protect her. The same thing you do with your possession. You got a house: you got cameras all around the house, you got grills to stop anybody from breaking in, you put up a fence around your house to prevent animals and things from coming in, and you make sure your house is clean. A possession, you take care of it.
- So the same thing with a wife. You get a wife, you got to teach her how to be godly. When she go off, you got to correct her. You got to build her up, to be just like you. Because she's a help; her job is to help you, her job is to serve you.

**[Ecclesiasticus 36:26](/bible/sirach/36#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/36#v26)</sup> Who will trust a thief well appointed, that skippeth from city to city? so who will believe a man that hath no house, and lodgeth wheresoever the night taketh him?

- Read for the trauma of unstable housing, homelessness and poverty, which follows troubled children into adulthood. “Who will trust a thief well appointed, that skippeth from city to city? so who will believe a man that hath no house, and lodgeth wheresoever the night taketh him?” He lives wherever the night takes him, and nobody can trust that.
- A lot of sisters go through this. Brothers are brought to them to provide a stable house, a place to live and call home, and the answer is ‘he’s not my type’, after three kids by three different baby daddies who were all her type and all left her. Keep that unstable life, then; we can’t help you.
