## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 32 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 12-17, 24, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:12](/bible/sirach/32#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/32#v12)</sup> There take thy pastime, and do what thou wilt: but sin not by proud speech.

- “There take thy pastime, and do what thou wilt”: you can have your fun and do what you want while you are on this earth. “But sin not by proud speech.” God listens to your speech, and some of you speak things that are sinful.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:13](/bible/sirach/32#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/32#v13)</sup> And for these things bless him that made thee, and hath replenished thee with his good things.

- “And for these things bless him that made thee”: always remember that you have a creator. Don’t turn into a hater; remember your creator. “And hath replenished thee with his good things.” God replenished us with the things we have on this earth. We owe it all to God.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:14](/bible/sirach/32#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/32#v14)</sup> Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favour.

- “Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline; and they that seek him early shall find favour.” Seek him early: make no tarrying, seek him fast, and you will find favour.
- “Whoso feareth the Lord will receive his discipline”. God is correcting us right now, and some of you in your pride don’t want to receive the correction. Donald Trump is in the position to make our lives miserable because we are being corrected by God. So it is important that we look at the pride of Donald Trump, even though it affects us and the whole world, and see that this is discipline from God. People will say, if there is a God, why do you live like you live, why don’t you have this or that, and use it as justification not to follow our God or listen when we speak.
- “And they that seek him early shall find favour.” That is what we are doing. We are seeking God early. We are not waiting until we are broken and destroyed and about to die. Don’t wait until the whole world is falling apart; seek him early so you can get the favor from God.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:15](/bible/sirach/32#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/32#v15)</sup> He that seeketh the law shall be filled therewith: but the hypocrite will be offended thereat.

- “He that seeketh the law shall be filled therewith: but the hypocrite will be offended thereat.” The hypocrite is angry and offended when he is corrected, even at this class.
- “He that seeketh the law shall be filled therewith”. That is why we teach our people to follow God’s laws. Other denominations call us legalists. No, this is why we follow the laws: we went wrong with God by doing our own thing.
- “But the hypocrite will be offended thereat.” That is what Donald Trump is: a hypocrite, offended at the service of our God.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:16](/bible/sirach/32#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/32#v16)</sup> They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.

- “They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.” They will be able to judge matters.
- “They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.” We cannot bring forth justice on our own. Donald Trump makes it clear: we have the greatest military, we can bomb anyone we want at any time, look at our navy, we dropped big bombs on Iran and have bigger bombs. You hear him talking and think, this guy is an idiot. You better shut your white mouth. What comes out of his mouth is just pride and arrogance.
- But God says to us, they that fear the Lord shall find judgment. We don’t need no bombs. Your arms are too short to box with God, Mr. white man. We don’t need no military and we don’t need no money. All we need is to be filled with the law, which is what we are teaching our people, and you call it evil. We put fringes on, tell our women to dress in skirts and dresses and cover their heads, tell the men to grow the beard and honor the Sabbath and keep it holy. That bothers people, though we are doing nothing evil.
- We are that light shining now. When we bring forth God’s judgment, God brings forth justice. He says, how can I let these holy people be destroyed by these evil people? They are coming forth into my light, so it is time to set things right. That is why we keep God’s law: things are going to get better for the people who keep God’s law and walk in his light.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/32#v17)</sup> A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.

- “A sinful man will not be reproved, but findeth an excuse according to his will.” The sinful man or woman will not be corrected, but finds an excuse according to what they want to do.
- “A sinful man will not be reproved”. There is nothing you can tell these people to correct them. Even in the congregation you speak to people over and over and they don’t get it; they always say it is you: you keep coming for me, you just don’t like me, why are you attacking me, I did nothing wrong. In their heart they believe they can sin and win, so they will not listen to reproof. Keep talking to the same brother over and over? Dismiss him after a while; you are wasting your time. Don’t chase them, replace them.
- “But findeth an excuse according to his will.” With Donald Trump, all that comes out of his mouth is excuses. He will never admit he did anything wrong; it is always somebody else picking on him. That is why he disrespected the Pope: he could not consider for a minute that maybe he made a bad decision. He puts on the red MAGA hat and explains, let me tell you why I did it, it’s not what you think, it can never be me. That is what happens when you deal with a sinful man. On Clubhouse the men were told, stop reading scriptures to them. They already showed what type of person they are; throw them out of the room. You showed them every scripture and they gave you every excuse.
- Even the people in his office tell him, this is not how we fight wars, not how we mediate with countries, not how we handle our finances. There is no money for Meals on Wheels, for government schools and for the elderly, but he is rebuilding the White House with the grandest ballroom you ever saw and talking about Melania’s dress, while the world is at war and gas prices are going up. It is a slap in the face, with the funding cut to schools, transportation and health care and millions losing their health care. He did the longest government shutdown in presidential history, and TSA workers and officers were not getting paid. He didn’t care. The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 6:4](/bible/sirach/6#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/6#v4)</sup> A wicked soul shall destroy him that hath it, and shall make him to be laughed to scorn of his enemies.

    “A wicked soul shall destroy him that hath it, and shall make him to be laughed to scorn of his enemies.” The excuse-making, sin-filled soul destroys the very one who has it.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 27:3](/bible/sirach/27#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/27#v3)</sup> Unless a man hold himself diligently in the fear of the Lord, his house shall soon be overthrown.

    “Unless a man hold himself diligently in the fear of the Lord, his house shall soon be overthrown.” We have to have fear and we have to be diligent, family, if we want to have a fighting chance.

**[Ecclesiasticus 32:24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)</sup> He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse.

- This is what belief means. “He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment.” That is what belief does: it makes you take heed to God’s commandments. The black church hasn’t figured that one out yet, and doesn’t even know about the Apocrypha.
- The black Christian church is confused. Sit down with a pastor and he says works and deeds can’t save him, that to inherit eternal life is only to believe on the one God sent, and that when he believes there is a nature change in his spirit man, but he doesn’t have to live it out. They lack understanding. They lack the learning to articulate exactly what the scriptures are talking about. Your spirit man changes how, like Shazam, a lightning bolt? No. Believing in the Lord shows itself in taking heed to his commandment.
- And that is why Los Angeles is full of crime in the black and brown community: people are taught they are still going to be saved and all they have to do is believe. They have no works toward Christ, no charity or love toward their neighbor; they don’t even know what love is.
- Now there are Christians who believe Christ is black, and some who say we’re Israelites, but they incorporate all the tenets and lies of Christianity: Christmas, Easter, Thanksgiving, and a Christ who looks like Morgan Freeman with a perm, loving everyone, all saved in Jesus if they believe. They know what we’re saying is proper, but they don’t want to upset Esau; they’re trying to stay in his good graces.

I don’t know what the hell they mean when they talk about love, because it ain’t what the Bible says.     “And this is love, that we walk after his commandments.” That’s love. “This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.”
  - **[Romans 7:5-7](/bible/romans/7#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/7#v5)</sup> For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members to bring forth fruit unto death.
    >

    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/romans/7#v7)</sup> What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.

    “For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members to bring forth fruit unto death.” When we were in the flesh our members worked in sin. We did things by nature, natural sinful things like adultery, lying and stealing, and it brought forth fruit unto death.
