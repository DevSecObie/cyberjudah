## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 40 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-8, 12, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:1](/bible/sirach/40#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/40#v1)</sup> Great travail is created for every man, and an heavy yoke is upon the sons of Adam, from the day that they go out of their mother’s womb, till the day that they return to the mother of all things.

- “Great travail is created for every man, and an heavy yoke is upon the sons of Adam”. Because of Adam’s sins we all carry that heavy yoke, “from the day that they go out of their mother’s womb, till the day that they return to the mother of all things”: from the day you are born to the day you die. This is what man goes through.

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:2](/bible/sirach/40#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/40#v2)</sup> Their imagination of things to come, and the day of death, trouble their thoughts, and cause fear of heart;

- “Their imagination of things to come, and the day of death, trouble their thoughts, and cause fear of heart”. Your mind is troubled; you fear death, and you have fear of heart.

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:3-4](/bible/sirach/40#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/40#v3)</sup> From him that sitteth on a throne of glory, unto him that is humbled in earth and ashes;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/40#v4)</sup> From him that weareth purple and a crown, unto him that is clothed with a linen frock.

- “From him that sitteth on a throne of glory, unto him that is humbled in earth and ashes”. It does not matter if you are rich or if you are poor: every man and woman has the same issues. Money does not heal trauma. That is why so many athletes and entertainers lose their minds when they retire, when they get old, when they are away from the drugs and alcohol. When they have to actually be themselves, they go crazy.

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:5](/bible/sirach/40#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/40#v5)</sup> Wrath, and envy, trouble, and unquietness, fear of death, and anger, and strife, and in the time of rest upon his bed his night sleep, do change his knowledge.

- “Wrath, and envy, trouble, and unquietness, fear of death, and anger, and strife, and in the time of rest upon his bed his night sleep, do change his knowledge.” You overthink at night when you are sleeping. Some wake up out of dreams: evil dreams, dreams of death, of losing a loved one, of losing a job, even dreams of committing adultery, and you wake up and thank God you didn’t do it, and say, Lord, take this from me. Why am I having that dream?

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:6-7](/bible/sirach/40#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/40#v6)</sup> A little or nothing is his rest, and afterward he is in his sleep, as in a day of keeping watch, troubled in the vision of his heart, as if he were escaped out of a battle.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/40#v7)</sup> When all is safe, he awaketh, and marvelleth that the fear was nothing.

- “A little or nothing is his rest”: you can’t sleep. “And afterward he is in his sleep, as in a day of keeping watch, troubled in the vision of his heart, as if he were escaped out of a battle.” You are troubled by the dream you just had, and you wake up as if you escaped out of a battle. “When all is safe, he awaketh, and marvelleth that the fear was nothing.” Thank God it was just a dream. All of us have gone through it, every man and woman, because Adam fell and we have all fallen.

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:8](/bible/sirach/40#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/40#v8)</sup> Such things happen unto all flesh, both man and beast, and that is sevenfold more upon sinners.

- “Such things happen unto all flesh, both man and beast”. You have seen a dog jump out of its dream. Even the beast goes through it. “And that is sevenfold more upon sinners”: it is seven times worse if you are in sin.
- Here is what that means in the body. The nervous system has the central nervous system, the brain and spinal cord, the command centre, and the peripheral nervous system, the nerves that run to every part of the body. Put your hand on a hot stove and the brain sends the signal to pull the finger away and feel the pain. The nervous system is built to protect you. But it can be used for good or for evil; that is why Paul said, when I would do good, evil is present with me. The autonomic system runs what you don’t think about: breathing, heart rate, digestion. The sympathetic side is fight or flight: someone rolls up on you and you put your hands up or you run. The parasympathetic side is rest and digest: calm, conserving energy and healing. For many of us that part is broken. We can’t get calm, barely sleep, don’t heal, and some have bodies that attack themselves.
- Under stress and trauma the amygdala, the threat detector, fires all the time: don’t trust my wife, my children, my co-workers, nobody. So you stay to yourself, smoke your weed, play video games, and don’t bother anybody until you want sex or food. Many of us lived that life. Under extreme trauma the body can freeze, going numb and dissociated, or fawn: you put yourself to work because you can never calm down, cleaning and moving all the time. Trauma changes the brain: the amygdala, the hippocampus that holds memory and context, and the prefrontal cortex that makes decisions. That is why some keep making horrible decisions and can’t understand why; why some are always on edge, irritable, anxious, panicky; why some are numb, exhausted and disconnected, missing assignments, late, absent from the Telegram. The first thing we say is he is out of the spirit, he has the devil on him; that may be true, but we should ask why. Sisters with postpartum depression know that numbness: the baby is crying and you don’t hear it.
- The vagus nerve brings the body back to calm, and under prolonged trauma it becomes less efficient, which leads to prolonged anxiety and depression. Your depression is rooted in your body, in what you put into it, and in sin. That is why it is sevenfold more upon sinners. But there is hope, and the word of God is how we get over it. Ongoing traumatic stress brings high blood pressure, digestive problems, chronic pain and weak immunity. Sisters are hormonal, the weaker vessel, and a sister must reset her nervous system by getting her mind right according to God’s word to have the motivation to push through the numbness into the gym. That is where husbands and leaders come in: we set the example. There should be no fat men among us. The men lead the nation; nobody is coming to lead it for us. We are the firstfruits of Christ and we must set the nation in order.

**[Ecclesiasticus 40:12](/bible/sirach/40#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/40#v12)</sup> All bribery and injustice shall be blotted out: but true dealing shall endure for ever.

- What Israel gives out are bribes, and God calls it bribery. “All bribery and injustice shall be blotted out: but true dealing shall endure for ever.” When you become a politician or a religious leader, countries like Israel bribe you so that you cannot speak against Israel or against white supremacy, and you condemn the Israelites. Pastor Philip Anthony Mitchell went to Israel, allegedly got paid big money, and after the trip his whole message became speaking against the Israelites.
- “All bribery and injustice shall be blotted out.” All the injustice, all the bribery, all the evil you see taking place on the earth is going to be blotted out. Every last injustice that was done to us, the Lord is going to deal with them concerning it.
- Western leaders and politicians were named in the Epstein files, molesting, murdering and killing babies, and they have not been judged. The victims are crying to the court system to release the files, and people accuse the president of this country of being involved, but this is an unjust system. Even though there is no justice from this unjust system, the Most High God is going to bring justice on them.
- So don’t seek justice in an unjust system. There is no justice we are going to get here. Our justice we will get when Christ returns.
