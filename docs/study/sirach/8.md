## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4, 8-9, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 8:4](/bible/sirach/8#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/8#v4)</sup> Jest not with a rude man, lest thy ancestors be disgraced.

- That shouldn’t be in the Bible either, because somehow we’re not a race. “Jest not with a rude man”: don’t play around with a rude man, “lest thy ancestors be disgraced.” You bring a bad name to yourself, and you disgrace your ancestors. Ancestry, in the Bible, once again.

**[Ecclesiasticus 8:8](/bible/sirach/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/8#v8)</sup> Despise not the discourse of the wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs: for of them thou shalt learn instruction, and how to serve great men with ease.

- “Despise not the discourse of the wise, but acquaint thyself with their proverbs.” God says do not despise the conversation of the wise. “For of them thou shalt learn instruction, and how to serve great men with ease.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 8:9](/bible/sirach/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/8#v9)</sup> Miss not the discourse of the elders: for they also learned of their fathers, and of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give answer as need requireth.

- “Miss not the discourse of the elders”: don’t miss the discourse of our elders, “for they also learned of their fathers, and of them thou shalt learn understanding, and to give answer as need requireth.” That is how the word becomes fitted in your lips. We have to acquaint ourselves with their proverbs: you walk around still quoting wicked lyrics, racky blicky, when you are supposed to be quoting the Bishops’ sound bites. That is supposed to be second nature to you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 9:15-16](/bible/sirach/9#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)</sup> Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/9#v16)</sup> And let just men eat and drink with thee; and let thy glorying be in the fear of the Lord.

    “Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.” Your communication has to be in accordance with the laws of God. Period. That is what the Bible says.
