## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 15 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 11, 13-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 15:11](/bible/sirach/15#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/15#v11)</sup> Say not thou, It is through the Lord that I fell away: for thou oughtest not to do the things that he hateth.

- “Say not thou, It is through the Lord that I fell away.” Don’t say that because you messed up it is God’s fault, the truth’s fault: when I was in that Bible I had it easier, it was their interpretation that made me fall away, my testimony of being in that cult. We have heard this before from those who departed.
- “For thou oughtest not to do the things that he hateth.” They leave out of here worse than they came in. If we are wicked and evil, why are you celebrating Halloween, Christmas and New Year again, why are the sisters back in pants and the brothers in dresses? You were good before you left; now you are bugged out, so the problem is not us. When you leave, the spirit you were in stays here. It is not a going-away prize. You leave, you stay gone, and you die gone.

**[Ecclesiasticus 15:13](/bible/sirach/15#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/15#v13)</sup> The Lord hateth all abomination; and they that fear God love it not.

- “The Lord hateth all abomination; and they that fear God love it not.” Women in pants, men in dresses, homosexuality, incest, pedophilia, bestiality: we hate that, and we are not to ally ourselves with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 15:14-15](/bible/sirach/15#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/15#v14)</sup> He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/15#v15)</sup> If thou wilt, to keep the commandments, and to perform acceptable faithfulness.

- “He himself made man from the beginning, and left him in the hand of his counsel.” He left Adam in the hand of his counsel: “If thou wilt, to keep the commandments, and to perform acceptable faithfulness.” To perform your faithfulness, you must keep the commandments. This proves faith comes with works. Faith is a performance, an action; your faith is proven by your actions, not your feelings.

**[Ecclesiasticus 15:16-17](/bible/sirach/15#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/15#v16)</sup> He hath set fire and water before thee: stretch forth thy hand unto whether thou wilt.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/15#v17)</sup> Before man is life and death; and whether him liketh shall be given him.

- “He hath set fire and water before thee: stretch forth thy hand unto whether thou wilt.” He set a decision before you: the fire, the death, or the water, the understanding. That is the free will of the Bible: do what I say or don’t, and I’ll bless you or I’ll kill you. “Before man is life and death; and whether him liketh shall be given him.”
- But the decision you make, he knows already. It is predetermined, but it is not for you to know. God is the writer of the script and we are living it out; the prophets already saw the end and we are catching up to what they saw. You choose to be a nigga till you die, you are going to die. You choose that and then come back and the Lord receives you, or you stay righteous the whole time: we all reach the same destination, deliverance or death, fire or water.

**[Ecclesiasticus 15:18-19](/bible/sirach/15#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/15#v18)</sup> For the wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholdeth all things:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/15#v19)</sup> And his eyes are upon them that fear him, and he knoweth every work of man.

- “For the wisdom of the Lord is great, and he is mighty in power, and beholdeth all things.” He sees everything. “And his eyes are upon them that fear him, and he knoweth every work of man.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 15:20](/bible/sirach/15#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/15#v20)</sup> He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin.

- “He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin.” He gave no man or woman a commandment to do evil, and no permission to break his laws. Modern Christians say Christ died so we can do what we want, Christ kept all the commandments so we don’t have to. That is Christianity; it is not in the Bible.
