## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 6-11, 17-18, 22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:1](/bible/sirach/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/16#v1)</sup> Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.

- Numbers don’t equal success; numbers equal progress, as Deacon Eythan said. If numbers were success, the Roman Catholic Church would be God’s success: they have billions. That’s not the success the Bible is talking about.
- “Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children”: what good is it if we have thousands upon thousands of lazy, do-nothing brothers and sisters? It’s a waste of your time and our time, but most of all God’s time. “Neither delight in ungodly sons.”
- “Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.” Another scripture in Psalms says it is a good thing to have a lot of kids, but the key word here is unprofitable: these are wicked kids.
- Having a lot of children was taught earlier, but there is a downside to that as well. “Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.” Don’t delight in unprofitable children. All them boys, them girls you got, you know whether they are good or not. If they are unprofitable, don’t rejoice in them.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:2](/bible/sirach/16#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/16#v2)</sup> Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.

- “Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.” Though your children multiply, though the congregation multiplies, don’t rejoice in that. We rejoice when we see brothers and sisters that love the Lord and fear God: then we’re on the right track.
- In your personal homes, don’t rejoice in your kids if they’re ungodly, only if they fear the Lord. Likewise as a congregation, we don’t relish the big numbers unless the fear of God is on you.
- “Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.”
- “Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.” That is the only time to rejoice in a bunch of kids. If you have a lot of children and they fear the Lord, then rejoice. If they don’t fear God, don’t rejoice.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:3](/bible/sirach/16#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/16#v3)</sup> Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly.

- “Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand.” Don’t respect the numbers. One member that is just is better than a thousand. That’s some heavy stuff.
- “For one that is just is better than a thousand”: it is better to have one good child than a thousand wicked niglets. “And better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly”: it is better to have no kids at all.
- “Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude”: don’t trust in their life, and don’t respect their numbers. “for one that is just is better than a thousand”: one just child, one child that keeps the commandments, is better than a thousand unprofitable kids. “and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly”: it is better to die without ever having children than to have a bunch of ungodly kids, because they are going to bring shame to you. You will be all on the news.
- That is what a group of unprofitable children looks like: grown women fighting in the middle of a street in Philadelphia, more jumping in, the fight pouring into the house past a screen door with the children watching, the police coming, as that way of settling things is passed from one generation to the next. Some of your kids are just like that, especially the girls who like to fight when they don’t get their way. Unprofitable kids. Do not rejoice in children like that.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:6](/bible/sirach/16#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/16#v6)</sup> In the congregation of the ungodly shall a fire be kindled; and in a rebellious nation wrath is set on fire.

- “In the congregation of the ungodly shall a fire be kindled”: God is going to burn you up. In the beginning of the Bible, when there was a congregation of the ungodly, he drowned them; here it is fire.
- “And in a rebellious nation wrath is set on fire.” That is the only thing you can do with people who are rebellious: burn them. They are not going to listen.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:7](/bible/sirach/16#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/16#v7)</sup> He was not pacified toward the old giants, who fell away in the strength of their foolishness.

- “He was not pacified toward the old giants”: the giants were before us, in the elder world, the leaders, and God destroyed them. “Who fell away in the strength of their foolishness.” They were giants, but they were foolish. People feel strong in their foolishness. The one who wants to commit crime and do everything evil feels emboldened and strong in his wickedness; the person with the big mouth who wants to argue and fight with everybody feels strong with that big mouth.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:8](/bible/sirach/16#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/16#v8)</sup> Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for their pride.

- “Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned, but abhorred them for their pride.” God will hate you when you are filled with pride. Tell prideful people this: there is a God, your creator, and your creator can become your hater, because God hates pride. Eventually he will look at all the people you are pissing off and say, I’m sick of you.
- They think the Lord is happy with them. “Neither spared he the place where Lot sojourned”: Sodom and Gomorrah, which the Lord did not spare, “but abhorred them for their pride.” Abhorred means strongly despised, strongly hated. The pride of Sodom and Gomorrah: they worshipped their booty more than they worshipped God.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:9](/bible/sirach/16#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/16#v9)</sup> He pitied not the people of perdition, who were taken away in their sins:

- “He pitied not the people of perdition, who were taken away in their sins”. He is not going to have pity on you, though we like to have pity. People tell you their story, he had a rough life, she had a rough life; how long are you going to keep telling that story? A couple came for counsel: the spouse would not listen to any of the scriptures, and the one who asked for help said, you need to understand, his mother wasn’t there for him, he grew up in a foster home, his aunt was mean to him. Sir, don’t call this number no more. You called to have him corrected with the scripture; he doesn’t want the scripture, and now you are making excuses for the abuser who doesn’t want to change. Stay away.
- “He pitied not the people of perdition.” Who are the people of perdition? Esau: the vessels of wrath fitted to destruction, the son of perdition, the man of sin that has to be revealed. They want to hide their history, so today we have to reveal it and show that their true history is sin, and for that they will pay.
- They say happy pride. Did the Lord not destroy Sodom and Gomorrah for their pride? Spiritual Sodom is back, spiritual Gomorrah is back, and they are happy doing it, so happy they sent a gay flag into the sky. At Pride Tel Aviv they call themselves a beacon of hope for the Middle East and the only democracy where you can be yourself. That is the true definition of democracy: wherever democracy goes is gay. That is America in a nutshell; that is Israel.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:10](/bible/sirach/16#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/16#v10)</sup> Nor the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts.

- “Nor the six hundred thousand footmen, who were gathered together in the hardness of their hearts.” Six hundred thousand people gathered together in the hardness of their hearts, and God pitied them not.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:11](/bible/sirach/16#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/16#v11)</sup> And if there be one stiffnecked among the people, it is marvel if he escape unpunished: for mercy and wrath are with him; he is mighty to forgive, and to pour out displeasure.

- “And if there be one stiffnecked among the people, it is marvel if he escape unpunished”. If you have one wicked person around you, it is a marvel if God doesn’t touch him. The wicked think, I can keep doing it and nothing is going to happen, everybody is stupid, God doesn’t see me, you are the problem, not me. One stiffnecked person in this congregation: I’ll be amazed if God lets you go. That is how much God hates pride.
- “For mercy and wrath are with him; he is mighty to forgive, and to pour out displeasure.” God can be merciful or he can be vengeful against you: he will forgive, and he will pour out his displeasure. A pastor sat down with us and was told, no, God is not good all the time. Did not God drown everybody on the face of the earth? Did he not let the earth swallow people up in the days of Moses, tell Moses to kill the thousands who were giving him a hard time, and leave a generation in the wilderness for forty years and deal with the next generation? Yet they read the Bible and say, turn to your neighbor and say God is good all the time. You are a damn fool. That is not the same God that drowned everybody on the earth; the last book is the bloodiest, and he is going to burn up billions of people with fire.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:17](/bible/sirach/16#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/16#v17)</sup> Say not thou, I will hide myself from the Lord: shall any remember me from above? I shall not be remembered among so many people: for what is my soul among such an infinite number of creatures?

- “Say not thou, I will hide myself from the Lord.” Don’t say you will do things in secret and it won’t be exposed. Over time, when it is evil enough and the Most High wants it known, it will be made known.
- “Shall any remember me from above? I shall not be remembered among so many people.” You think that because millions out there sin and do worse, God will exempt you: I’m just battling porn, people out here murder, steal, lie and sell drugs. God says don’t think like that, because I expect more from you than from them. I hold you to a higher standard. I can call them out of that, but I already called you: you are already on the payroll, and you are being insubordinate. “For what is my soul among such an infinite number of creatures?” Who am I, we all sin, why are my sins so important? That is the evil thought.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:18](/bible/sirach/16#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/16#v18)</sup> Behold, the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, and all that therein is, shall be moved when he shall visit.

- “Behold, the heaven, and the heaven of heavens, the deep, and the earth, and all that therein is, shall be moved when he shall visit.” When he shows up, everybody will know he is here, and no matter where you hide, he will find you.

**[Ecclesiasticus 16:22](/bible/sirach/16#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/16#v22)</sup> Who can declare the works of his justice? or who can endure them? for his covenant is afar off, and the trial of all things is in the end.

- “Who can declare the works of his justice? or who can endure them?” When God brings his justice on this earth, who can declare it, and who can endure it? Many of our people say they want justice, but black people don’t understand what justice is. You think because Thomas Jefferson and all these murderous, evil people died, that was it for them. No: God’s justice is bringing them back from the dead and judging them.
- “For his covenant is afar off, and the trial of all things is in the end.” In the end God judges everybody for what they have done on this earth. All the racist, evil, genocidal people that hate black people, that hate God’s chosen people, the Israelites, because that is who we are, are going to pay for it. The Lord is going to bring judgment and justice on them.
