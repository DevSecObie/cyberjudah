## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 22 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-5, 14-16, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 22:3](/bible/sirach/22#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/22#v3)</sup> An evil-nurtured son is the dishonour of his father that begat him: and a foolish daughter is born to his loss.

- A precept for Ephesians 6:4, about an evil nurtured child. “An evil-nurtured son is the dishonour of his father that begat him: and a foolish daughter is born to his loss.” The kids fighting in the street in the videos are evil nurtured, but raising a child up as a Christian is equally evil nurtured, and may be worse. You never taught them the fundamentals of the Bible and what to beware of; you taught them everybody is the same in Jesus, and now your child is dead because a Christian parent mistaught him.
- Christianity is worse because one who comes up in it will reject scripture. People in the world, even messed up, have a better chance of getting this gospel and cleaning their lives up than a deep fried, hard baked so-called Christian who can’t see anything but white Jesus.

**[Ecclesiasticus 22:4](/bible/sirach/22#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/22#v4)</sup> A wise daughter shall bring an inheritance to her husband: but she that liveth dishonestly is her father’s heaviness.

- “A wise daughter shall bring an inheritance to her husband: but she that liveth dishonestly is her father’s heaviness.” That is the precept: the wise daughter brings an inheritance into her marriage.

    Read last week, and it shows the inheritance a wise daughter brings. Raguel “had said unto him by an oath, that he should not depart till the fourteen days of the marriage were expired; And then he should take the half of his goods, and go in safety to his father”.

**[Ecclesiasticus 22:5](/bible/sirach/22#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/22#v5)</sup> She that is bold dishonoureth both her father and her husband, but they both shall despise her.

- “She that is bold dishonoureth both her father and her husband, but they both shall despise her.” That bold woman says what she wants to say, goes where she wants to go and does what she wants to do, and both father and husband despise her.

**[Ecclesiasticus 22:14](/bible/sirach/22#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/22#v14)</sup> What is heavier than lead? and what is the name thereof, but a fool?

- Today's class is about proving and picking a spouse: asking the right questions and paying attention to all the red flags and all the signs. If you don't, because you're out here love drunk, there will be hell to pay.
- “What is heavier than lead? and what is the name thereof, but a fool?” To be a fool is like carrying heavy lead on your back. It is burdensome. Everything in your life is harder. You know that person who always wants to do things the hard way, who always has to bump his head and mess up to get it, instead of taking counsel? Somebody tells him there's something down that road, so go this way. Oh no, he wants to go down there and find out.

**[Ecclesiasticus 22:15](/bible/sirach/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/22#v15)</sup> Sand, and salt, and a mass of iron, is easier to bear, than a man without understanding.

- “Sand, and salt, and a mass of iron, is easier to bear, than a man without understanding.” Why make something so difficult? Because you don't want to listen. Because you think you know what you're talking about.
- There is the one who always thinks he has the answers: nobody's telling me what to do, I've got to figure it out. And there is a worse one, the one who acts like he wants counsel. He asks all the questions, takes notes, says hold on Deacon, say that again slow, acts like he's hanging on every word. We went from the alpha to the omega, sat up there for hours, covered every step. And then he turns around and does the exact opposite. That is a man without understanding, and he is heavier to bear than sand, salt and iron.

**[Ecclesiasticus 22:16](/bible/sirach/22#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/22#v16)</sup> As timber girt and bound together in a building cannot be loosed with shaking: so the heart that is stablished by advised counsel shall fear at no time.

- Counsel is when a man weighs options and determines what should be done. “As timber girt and bound together in a building cannot be loosed with shaking: so the heart that is stablished by advised counsel shall fear at no time.”
- Always bounce what you think off of men of understanding, and ask questions. Most of the time, when a person does not ask for counsel, it is because they don’t want to be told that what they are doing is wrong, and most of the time they already know it is wrong.
- In his early years in the truth, Bishop Kani told the Bishop he wanted to move to Florida and was told to sit down, that he didn’t know anything. He reasoned with himself: is the Bishop trying to teach me to sin against God? No. Why go to Florida? Because his mother would put down money on a house. So he stayed. Ten years later he asked again and was told to go and build. Had he not listened the first time, he would not be in the truth today. Back then he also brought leadership a precept he thought would let them smoke weed, on mandrakes, and the Bishop laughed, said, Nigga, shut up, and kicked him out of the office. Had he left with that understanding, he would be a weed head today. Thank the Lord.
