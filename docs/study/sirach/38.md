## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ecclesiasticus 38 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 4, 18-20, 22, 24-25, 31-34, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:1](/bible/sirach/38#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/38#v1)</sup> Honour a physician with the honour due unto him for the uses which ye may have of him: for the Lord hath created him.

- “Honour a physician with the honour due unto him for the uses which ye may have of him: for the Lord hath created him.” God created the physician.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:2](/bible/sirach/38#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/38#v2)</sup> For of the most High cometh healing, and he shall receive honour of the king.

- “For of the most High cometh healing.” That is where healing first and foremost comes from: the Most High God. “and he shall receive honour of the king.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:4](/bible/sirach/38#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/38#v4)</sup> The Lord hath created medicines out of the earth; and he that is wise will not abhor them.

- “The Lord hath created medicines out of the earth.” Where did God create medicine? Out of the earth. Did it say from petroleum? Out of the earth. So what the hell are these white people talking about, herbal medicine is quackery? Medicine comes from the earth, from the plants, from the food we eat.
- “and he that is wise will not abhor them.” If you are wise you will not hate them. But the white man said to hell with the medicines from the earth, we will make our pharmaceuticals from petroleum and condemn anyone who uses the medicine from the earth. That is how the whole medical system is set up. He did a job on us and destroyed our doctors that really knew how to do these things, so we had no other choice but to depend on him.
- That does not mean throw your medication away: if your head is killing you, go take your Tylenol. Just know it is a chemical, and taking it all the time affects your kidneys, and the headache is because something else is wrong.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:18](/bible/sirach/38#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/38#v18)</sup> For of heaviness cometh death, and the heaviness of the heart breaketh strength.

- This is still letting bad situations destroy you. “For of heaviness cometh death”: that heaviness is depression. Of depression comes death; death is coming. “And the heaviness of the heart breaketh strength”: the heaviness of the heart is the depression of the mind, and it breaks your spiritual strength, which leads to your physical breaking. Sometimes we go through health issues, and that man or that woman refuses to try to increase their physical strength and their spiritual strength.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:19](/bible/sirach/38#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/38#v19)</sup> In affliction also sorrow remaineth: and the life of the poor is the curse of the heart.

- “In affliction also sorrow remaineth”: when you are afflicted, sorrow remains, like the brother and the sister we saw in California; their sorrow is going to remain for quite a while, till their situation is fixed. “And the life of the poor is the curse of the heart”: being poor will make you rob, steal and kill.
- As we go through these scriptures, apply them to yourself. Don't think this class isn't pertaining to you. This class is for you.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:20](/bible/sirach/38#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/38#v20)</sup> Take no heaviness to heart: drive it away, and remember the last end.

- “Take no heaviness to heart”: these bad situations happen; do not take them to heart. “Drive it away”: when you have that spirit of depression and sorrow, God says drive it away. “and remember the last end”: remember the end, because sorrow will break bones. It will break you.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:22](/bible/sirach/38#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/38#v22)</sup> Remember my judgment: for thine also shall be so; yesterday for me, and to day for thee.

- Be mindful how you speak of the men who came before you. One who now calls the founder of his camp a false prophet is now looked at the same way himself. “Remember my judgment: for thine also shall be so; yesterday for me, and to day for thee.” When men fall and make mistakes, through coercion or old age as their wisdom leaves them, someone’s yesterday can be your tomorrow.
- Do not speak evil of dignities, because the law will come and find you. Say Moses went off, the Baptist was a fake Israelite camp, James went off, Paul taught idolatry: the law will find you. Stay in your place. We learned from our elders to respect our elders; be careful to do the same, because yesterday for them, today for you.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:24](/bible/sirach/38#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/38#v24)</sup> The wisdom of a learned man cometh by opportunity of leisure: and he that hath little business shall become wise.

- “The wisdom of a learned man cometh by opportunity of leisure: and he that hath little business shall become wise.” Leisure is the time you have off work, your free time. What you do with it decides whether you become wise.
- Read on into the next chapter and it shows what the man does with that leisure: he gives his mind to the law of the Most High. That is why 38:24 is precepted with 39:1.
- “The wisdom of a learned man cometh by opportunity of leisure.” If you want to get wisdom and strengthen yourself in wisdom, you have to make the time to have that leisure, because we all have responsibilities, and on top of that there are the masturbatory substitutes that prevent you from increasing in wisdom. “And he that hath little business shall become wise.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/38#v25)</sup> How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough, and that glorieth in the goad, that driveth oxen, and is occupied in their labours, and whose talk is of bullocks?

- “How can he get wisdom that holdeth the plough”, “that driveth oxen, and is occupied in their labours”? It says it in a right way for that time, because they didn’t have video games and movies and all this other stuff to distract you back then. But you could throw that in the mix now too, because that’s the bane of a lot of brothers in increasing in growth and understanding.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:31](/bible/sirach/38#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/38#v31)</sup> All these trust to their hands: and every one is wise in his work.

- “All these trust to their hands: and every one is wise in his work.” This whole chapter is talking about brothers who love their worldly job, their secular job, and that is all they occupy themselves with. They don't occupy themselves with studying, praying and applying the scriptures. Your job in the world is all you really care about.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:32](/bible/sirach/38#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/sirach/38#v32)</sup> Without these cannot a city be inhabited: and they shall not dwell where they will, nor go up and down:

- “Without these cannot a city be inhabited”: without your work the city couldn't be inhabited. “And they shall not dwell where they will, nor go up and down”: you can't dwell where you want to, nor go up and down.

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/sirach/38#v33)</sup> They shall not be sought for in publick counsel, nor sit high in the congregation: they shall not sit on the judges’ seat, nor understand the sentence of judgment: they cannot declare justice and judgment; and they shall not be found where parables are spoken.

- “They shall not be sought for in publick counsel”: because you are so occupied in your work, you will never be sought for in public counsel. When things are going down, we don't want to hear your opinion, because your opinion is your job. You don't know the scriptures. “Nor sit high in the congregation”: you will have no rank. If you want to be a member, be a member; there is nothing wrong with that.
- “They shall not sit on the judges’ seat, nor understand the sentence of judgment.” You are the brother who will always be confused. Deacon Abiel and Officer Laab had a skit on today's show about the brother who says, I don't agree with that judgment. You don't agree because you don't know the scriptures. All you know is your job.
- “They cannot declare justice and judgment; and they shall not be found where parables are spoken.” When brothers are going over parables, you are nowhere around, because you could care less. If the shoe fits, brother, wear it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 28:5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)</sup> Evil men understand not judgment: but they that seek the Lord understand all things.

    Why don't you understand when judgments are passed? “Evil men understand not judgment: but they that seek the Lord understand all things.”

**[Ecclesiasticus 38:34](/bible/sirach/38#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/sirach/38#v34)</sup> But they will maintain the state of the world, and all their desire is in the work of their craft.

- “But they will maintain the state of the world, and all their desire is in the work of their craft.” You will maintain the state of the world, Babylon the great. That is where your occupation is, and that is all you care about.
