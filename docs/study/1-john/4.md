## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 John 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 John 4:1](/bible/1-john/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/4#v1)</sup> Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God: because many false prophets are gone out into the world.

- “Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God”. We often read this about the Christian church, but it speaks of anyone who fits the description: anyone who says he believes in Christ, believes the Bible, or says he is an Israelite. You try the spirit with the Bible, and many are not doing that, especially those who cosign these men. Every spirit is not of Christ, and Christian pastors say the same things as some Israelite leaders.
- “because many false prophets are gone out into the world.” That is what you see: false prophets and false teachers saying all kinds of nonsense.
- “Beloved, believe not every spirit, but try the spirits whether they are of God”. Don't believe every spirit; try them to see if they are of God, “because many false prophets are gone out into the world.” There are many false prophets in this world.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jude 1:4](/bible/jude/1#v4)**

    “For there are certain men crept in unawares, who were before of old ordained to this condemnation”. **[1 John 4:2](/bible/1-john/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-john/4#v2)</sup> Hereby know ye the Spirit of God: Every spirit that confesseth that Jesus Christ is come in the flesh is of God:

Flesh represents nationality: you can tell a Chinese man, a white man, a black man when you see him in the flesh. Christ came from the so-called Negroes, and his skin was black.
- Does it matter what color Malcolm X was, or Martin Luther King? Yes, it matters, because they came fighting for the struggle of a race of people. It is the same with Christ. Christ came to redeem his people from their sins, so the question is, who are Christ's people? If you can change Christ's people, you can change Christ's teachings.
