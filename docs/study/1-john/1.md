## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 John 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4-10, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 John 1:4](/bible/1-john/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/1#v4)</sup> And these things write we unto you, that your joy may be full.

- “And these things write we unto you, that your joy may be full.” Your joy: the joy spoken of earlier in Romans 5:11, the joy we have in God through Christ.

**[1 John 1:5](/bible/1-john/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-john/1#v5)</sup> This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.

- “This then is the message which we have heard of him, and declare unto you, that God is light, and in him is no darkness at all.”

**[1 John 1:6](/bible/1-john/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-john/1#v6)</sup> If we say that we have fellowship with him, and walk in darkness, we lie, and do not the truth:

- “If we say that we have fellowship with him”: convocation, assembling ourselves together with him, “and walk in darkness”: with hatred and bitterness in our hearts and minds, “we lie”: we are lying to ourselves, nobody else, “and do not the truth”: not doing God’s commandments.

**[1 John 1:7](/bible/1-john/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-john/1#v7)</sup> But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship one with another, and the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin.

- “But if we walk in the light, as he is in the light, we have fellowship one with another”. Then there is no fragmentation, no fermentation, no festering. “And the blood of Jesus Christ his Son cleanseth us from all sin”: that is the atonement.

**[1 John 1:8](/bible/1-john/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-john/1#v8)</sup> If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.

- “If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.” We all have sin in us; we all have battles. You are saying, I’m good, I’m fine, I’m great, but you know that hatred for that brother or that sister is there. You are deceiving yourself.

**[1 John 1:9](/bible/1-john/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-john/1#v9)</sup> If we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.

- “If we confess our sins”: confess our faults, which is Proverbs 28:13. “He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins, and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness.”

**[1 John 1:10](/bible/1-john/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-john/1#v10)</sup> If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar, and his word is not in us.

- “If we say that we have not sinned, we make him a liar”: I don’t have to keep the law, I’m perfect, I’m righteous and holy, holier than thou. That makes God a liar. If you are perfect, with no sin at all, Christ died for no reason at all. “And his word is not in us.”
