## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 John 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 7-8, 10-12, 14-15, 19-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 John 3:1](/bible/1-john/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/3#v1)</sup> Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God: therefore the world knoweth us not, because it knew him not.

- “Behold, what manner of love the Father hath bestowed upon us, that we should be called the sons of God.” Out of all that love, you turn around and learn to hate your brother?

**[1 John 3:2](/bible/1-john/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-john/3#v2)</sup> Beloved, now are we the sons of God, and it doth not yet appear what we shall be: but we know that, when he shall appear, we shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.

- “We shall be like him; for we shall see him as he is.” We are going to be like the one that created everything. There is power in that.

**[1 John 3:3](/bible/1-john/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/3#v3)</sup> And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure.

- “And every man that hath this hope in him purifieth himself, even as he is pure.” If you have the hope that you will be like him, made in his express image, the sons of God, you purify yourself. If you don’t get the hate, envy and jealousy out of your spirit, you never really believed. You were here under pretence, lying in wait, feeding on demonic forces, until you became a full-grown demon.

**[1 John 3:4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)</sup> Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.

- What is sin? Ask a so-called Christian and he will wiggle: missing the mark, falling short, not doing good. It is a broad answer because they don't want any responsibility. To sin means you are doing something wrong, and to know what you are doing wrong you have to go somewhere and read it. They don't want that. One sister at the 2018 Blitz said if she isn't convicted in her heart it isn't law, and God has to speak to her himself: he spoke to her about adultery but not about wearing pants. That is selective hearing. Nobody came and told her to pay that ten percent, but she does it. Don't let them run; hold them to what the scripture says.
- “Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.” That is the purest definition, and it is why they hate the Israelites: we give biblical definitions, and the word of God defines itself. To transgress is to infringe, to go beyond the bounds, to break, disobey, defy, breach, violate. To sin is to break God's laws.

**[1 John 3:7](/bible/1-john/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-john/3#v7)</sup> Little children, let no man deceive you: he that doeth righteousness is righteous, even as he is righteous.

- “Little children, let no man deceive you: he that doeth righteousness is righteous.” Don’t get caught by the fair speeches, the look, the grey beard. You don’t have to figure it out: look in the Bible at what is righteous, then look at what he does. His actions are the fruit. Mark them that cause division, and avoid them. Two hundred and fifty princes, men of renown, got caught up and were put to death.

**[1 John 3:8](/bible/1-john/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-john/3#v8)</sup> He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil.

- “For the devil sinneth from the beginning.” Through envy of the devil came death. From the beginning Satan’s game was to divide us: put Eve against Adam, and get Adam to move away from the true living God. Don’t bring a man tales about a brother; he will call that brother and let both of you answer. You can’t program a man of God except with the word.
- “That he might destroy the works of the devil.” The works of the devil are to keep us in sin. Christ was manifested to give us the understanding of the laws and to gather the 12 tribes of Israel. Satan’s job is to teach you to sin and keep the 12 tribes scattered. So if you are not part of gathering the 12 tribes, you are a betrayer of your nation, a secret admirer sitting amongst us looking to destroy, and God is going to kill you.

**[1 John 3:10](/bible/1-john/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-john/3#v10)</sup> In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil: whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, neither he that loveth not his brother.

- “Whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, neither he that loveth not his brother.” That is it. Who would want to destroy their brother or their sister?
- “In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil.” The devil has kids. That is Cain’s children back then, and Esau’s children today.
- “Whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God”: that is Cain and Esau. “Neither he that loveth not his brother”: did Cain hate his brother? Yes. Does Esau hate his brother? Yes.

**[1 John 3:11-12](/bible/1-john/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-john/3#v11)</sup> For this is the message that ye heard from the beginning, that we should love one another.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-john/3#v12)</sup> Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one, and slew his brother. And wherefore slew he him? Because his own works were evil, and his brother’s righteous.

- “That we should love one another. Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one”: he was of Satan. “And slew his brother”: Cain killed his brother because he hated him. “Because his own works were evil, and his brother’s righteous.”
- “Not as Cain, who was of that wicked one, and slew his brother.” He slew him “because his own works were evil, and his brother’s righteous.” He hated because he was not like him, but he could have been, because he was made after God’s express image. He chose to be evil, and then wanted to destroy that which is righteous.
