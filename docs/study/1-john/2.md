## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 John 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 7-11, 14-19, 25-29, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 John 2:1](/bible/1-john/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/2#v1)</sup> My little children, these things write I unto you, that ye sin not. And if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous:

- This is their favorite scripture. “And if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous”. Christ is our advocate with his Father; we never go to the Father directly, there is always a mediator.
- And the apologists who run to the Septuagint should not run there at all, because it helps us more. The disciples used the Apocrypha too; they quoted Sirach and others many times.

**[1 John 2:2](/bible/1-john/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-john/2#v2)</sup> And he is the propitiation for our sins: and not for ours only, but also for the sins of the whole world.

- “And he is the propitiation for our sins”. Propitiation means to conciliate, to win or regain the favor of a god or person by doing something that pleases them. Why regain? Because we have to regain our favor with God again, as reconcile means to bring back together.
- “And not for ours only, but also for the sins of the whole world.” See, brother, the whole world, you are shooting yourself in the foot. But we have been reading all day who the world is. Not for ours only is not for that nation only, as Caiaphas said: and the whole world is the whole family of Israel, all twelve tribes.
