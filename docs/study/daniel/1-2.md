This session covers [Daniel 1](/bible/daniel/1) and 2. Daniel opens with the captivity of Judah into Babylon and the vessels of the house of God carried into the land of Shinar, then shows how the king took the choicest and most educated of Israel to serve in his kingdom. Like many of the kings, Jehoiakim had been warned by prophecy that Judah would be carried into Babylon.

**[Daniel 1:1-2](/bible/daniel/1#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/1#v1)</sup> In the third year of the reign of Jehoiakim king of Judah came Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon unto Jerusalem, and besieged it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/1#v2)</sup> And the Lord gave Jehoiakim king of Judah into his hand, with part of the vessels of the house of God: which he carried into the land of Shinar to the house of his god; and he brought the vessels into the treasure house of his god.

- In the third year of Jehoiakim king of Judah, Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon came to Jerusalem and besieged it, and the Most High gave Jehoiakim into his hand with part of the vessels of the house of God.
- Nebuchadnezzar carried those vessels into the land of Shinar to the house of his god and put them in his treasure house. This came to pass exactly as the prophets had warned.

**[Daniel 1:3-5](/bible/daniel/1#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/1#v3)</sup> And the king spake unto Ashpenaz the master of his eunuchs, that he should bring certain of the children of Israel, and of the king’s seed, and of the princes;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/1#v4)</sup> Children in whom was no blemish, but well favoured, and skilful in all wisdom, and cunning in knowledge, and understanding science, and such as had ability in them to stand in the king’s palace, and whom they might teach the learning and the tongue of the Chaldeans.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/1#v5)</sup> And the king appointed them a daily provision of the king’s meat, and of the wine which he drank: so nourishing them three years, that at the end thereof they might stand before the king.

- The king commanded to bring certain of the children of Israel, of the king's seed and of the princes: young men without blemish, well favoured, skilful in all wisdom, cunning in knowledge, understanding science, and able to stand in the king's palace.
- They took the best and the most educated among us, taught them the learning and the tongue of the Chaldeans, and set them to build up their kingdom. It is the same thing today, where our people serve in the governments and the congresses and are used to build up a system that is not our own.
- Often those who are chosen are not loyal to their own people; they are assimilated to believe they are a part of the system and accepted by that society, and they are well rewarded for it.
- When our people were carried over in the dark ages they were a civilized, educated people, engineers and men of every profession, and they were used to serve and build up Babylon. Israel is the greatest nation the earth has known, and Babylon benefited off our wisdom and our knowledge.
- The king also appointed them a daily provision of the king's meat and of the wine which he drank.

**[Daniel 1:6-7](/bible/daniel/1#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/1#v6)</sup> Now among these were of the children of Judah, Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/1#v7)</sup> Unto whom the prince of the eunuchs gave names: for he gave unto Daniel the name of Belteshazzar; and to Hananiah, of Shadrach; and to Mishael, of Meshach; and to Azariah, of Abed–nego.

- Among those taken were of the children of Judah: Daniel, Hananiah, Mishael, and Azariah.
- The prince of the eunuchs gave them new names, Belteshazzar, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, part of assimilating them into the king's service.
