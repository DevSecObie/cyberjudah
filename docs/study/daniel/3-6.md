Read the chapter → [Daniel 3](/bible/daniel/3)

This session covers [Daniel 3](/bible/daniel/3) through 6, read four chapters at a time by the Orlando camp. It moves through the three Hebrews in the burning fiery furnace, Nebuchadnezzar's dream of the great tree and his abasement until he knew the Most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, the handwriting on the wall at Belshazzar's feast, and Daniel delivered out of the den of lions. Throughout, the heathen kings are forced to acknowledge that Israel serves the one true and living God.

**[Daniel 3:1-3](/bible/daniel/3#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/3#v1)</sup> Nebuchadnezzar the king made an image of gold, whose height was threescore cubits, and the breadth thereof six cubits: he set it up in the plain of Dura, in the province of Babylon.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/3#v2)</sup> Then Nebuchadnezzar the king sent to gather together the princes, the governors, and the captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, to come to the dedication of the image which Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/3#v3)</sup> Then the princes, the governors, and captains, the judges, the treasurers, the counsellors, the sheriffs, and all the rulers of the provinces, were gathered together unto the dedication of the image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up; and they stood before the image that Nebuchadnezzar had set up.

- Nebuchadnezzar made an image of gold, threescore cubits high and six cubits broad, and set it up in the plain of Dura, then gathered the princes, governors, captains, judges, treasurers, counsellors, sheriffs, and rulers to its dedication.
- We have seen things like this before. When they build a monument or a new federal building they hold a dedication and a ribbon-cutting ceremony. This is very similar.

**[Daniel 3:4-5](/bible/daniel/3#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/3#v4)</sup> Then an herald cried aloud, To you it is commanded, O people, nations, and languages,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/3#v5)</sup> That at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, ye fall down and worship the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king hath set up:

- A herald cried that when the people heard the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, dulcimer, and all kinds of music they were to fall down and worship the golden image.
- We see the same thing today when they play the national anthem at ball games and ceremonies. You are supposed to take your hat off and put your hand over your heart. That is a form of worshipping the image of the eagle today.

**[Daniel 3:6-7](/bible/daniel/3#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/3#v6)</sup> And whoso falleth not down and worshippeth shall the same hour be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/3#v7)</sup> Therefore at that time, when all the people heard the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and all kinds of musick, all the people, the nations, and the languages, fell down and worshipped the golden image that Nebuchadnezzar the king had set up.

- Whoever did not fall down and worship would the same hour be cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace, so when the music sounded all the people, nations, and languages fell down and worshipped the golden image.

**[Daniel 3:8-12](/bible/daniel/3#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/3#v8)</sup> Wherefore at that time certain Chaldeans came near, and accused the Jews.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/3#v9)</sup> They spake and said to the king Nebuchadnezzar, O king, live for ever.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/3#v10)</sup> Thou, O king, hast made a decree, that every man that shall hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, shall fall down and worship the golden image:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/3#v11)</sup> And whoso falleth not down and worshippeth, that he should be cast into the midst of a burning fiery furnace.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/3#v12)</sup> There are certain Jews whom thou hast set over the affairs of the province of Babylon, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego; these men, O king, have not regarded thee: they serve not thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.

- Certain Chaldeans came near and accused the Jews, telling the king that Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, whom he had set over the affairs of the province, did not regard him, serve his gods, nor worship the golden image.
- The reason we did not bow was according to the law.
  - **[Exodus 20:3-5](/bible/exodus/20#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)</sup> Thou shalt have no other gods before me.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/20#v4)</sup> Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/20#v5)</sup> Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me;

    Thou shalt have no other gods and make no graven image, nor bow down to them, for the Most High is a jealous God; we are not to bow and serve gods made by hands.

**[Daniel 3:13-15](/bible/daniel/3#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/3#v13)</sup> Then Nebuchadnezzar in his rage and fury commanded to bring Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego. Then they brought these men before the king.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/3#v14)</sup> Nebuchadnezzar spake and said unto them, Is it true, O Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, do not ye serve my gods, nor worship the golden image which I have set up?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/daniel/3#v15)</sup> Now if ye be ready that at what time ye hear the sound of the cornet, flute, harp, sackbut, psaltery, and dulcimer, and all kinds of musick, ye fall down and worship the image which I have made; well: but if ye worship not, ye shall be cast the same hour into the midst of a burning fiery furnace; and who is that God that shall deliver you out of my hands?

- In his rage Nebuchadnezzar brought the three men and demanded whether they would fall down and worship, threatening the furnace and asking, who is that God that shall deliver you out of my hands.
- He is talking tough, talking big, challenging our God when he asks who is going to deliver you out of his hands.

**[Daniel 3:16-18](/bible/daniel/3#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/daniel/3#v16)</sup> Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, answered and said to the king, O Nebuchadnezzar, we are not careful to answer thee in this matter.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/3#v17)</sup> If it be so, our God whom we serve is able to deliver us from the burning fiery furnace, and he will deliver us out of thine hand, O king.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/daniel/3#v18)</sup> But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that we will not serve thy gods, nor worship the golden image which thou hast set up.

- The three answered that they were not careful to answer him in this matter: their God whom they serve is able to deliver them from the burning fiery furnace and out of the king's hand.
- But if not, be it known unto thee, O king, that they would not serve his gods nor worship the golden image. This is exactly what was read in [Exodus 20](/bible/exodus/20).

**[Daniel 3:19-22](/bible/daniel/3#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/daniel/3#v19)</sup> Then was Nebuchadnezzar full of fury, and the form of his visage was changed against Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego: therefore he spake, and commanded that they should heat the furnace one seven times more than it was wont to be heated.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/daniel/3#v20)</sup> And he commanded the most mighty men that were in his army to bind Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, and to cast them into the burning fiery furnace.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/daniel/3#v21)</sup> Then these men were bound in their coats, their hosen, and their hats, and their other garments, and were cast into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/daniel/3#v22)</sup> Therefore because the king’s commandment was urgent, and the furnace exceeding hot, the flame of the fire slew those men that took up Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego.

- Full of fury, Nebuchadnezzar commanded the furnace heated seven times more than usual and had his mighty men bind the three in their coats and garments and cast them in.
- The furnace was so hot that the flame slew the men who carried Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego up to it.
- For more of this history the Song of the Three Holy Children can be read; for the sake of time it was only referenced here.

**[Daniel 3:23-25](/bible/daniel/3#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/daniel/3#v23)</sup> And these three men, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, fell down bound into the midst of the burning fiery furnace.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/daniel/3#v24)</sup> Then Nebuchadnezzar the king was astonied, and rose up in haste, and spake, and said unto his counsellors, Did not we cast three men bound into the midst of the fire? They answered and said unto the king, True, O king.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/daniel/3#v25)</sup> He answered and said, Lo, I see four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, and they have no hurt; and the form of the fourth is like the Son of God.

- The three men fell down bound into the furnace, but Nebuchadnezzar rose up astonished and said he saw four men loose, walking in the midst of the fire, unhurt, and the form of the fourth was like the Son of God.

**[Daniel 3:26-27](/bible/daniel/3#v26)**
> <sup>[26](/bible/daniel/3#v26)</sup> Then Nebuchadnezzar came near to the mouth of the burning fiery furnace, and spake, and said, Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, ye servants of the most high God, come forth, and come hither. Then Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, came forth of the midst of the fire.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/daniel/3#v27)</sup> And the princes, governors, and captains, and the king’s counsellors, being gathered together, saw these men, upon whose bodies the fire had no power, nor was an hair of their head singed, neither were their coats changed, nor the smell of fire had passed on them.

- Nebuchadnezzar called them servants of the Most High God to come forth, and the fire had no power on their bodies: not a hair singed, their coats unchanged, nor the smell of fire on them.
- It was as if they had never fallen into the fire; not even the smell of smoke was on these brothers.

**[Daniel 3:28-30](/bible/daniel/3#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/daniel/3#v28)</sup> Then Nebuchadnezzar spake, and said, Blessed be the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, who hath sent his angel, and delivered his servants that trusted in him, and have changed the king’s word, and yielded their bodies, that they might not serve nor worship any god, except their own God.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/daniel/3#v29)</sup> Therefore I make a decree, That every people, nation, and language, which speak any thing amiss against the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, shall be cut in pieces, and their houses shall be made a dunghill: because there is no other God that can deliver after this sort.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/daniel/3#v30)</sup> Then the king promoted Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed–nego, in the province of Babylon.

- Nebuchadnezzar blessed the God of Shadrach, Meshach, and Abednego, who sent his angel to deliver the servants that trusted in him and would not serve nor worship any god except their own God.
- He understood at that time that we had our own God. Why do people not understand that today.
- He decreed that any people, nation, or language that spake amiss against their God would be cut in pieces, because there is no other God that can deliver after this sort, and he promoted the three in the province of Babylon.
- When the nations get jacked up they come to know who has the real, true, living God; other heathen rulers came to the same acknowledgement, such as Antiochus and Naaman the Syrian.
  - **[Joel 2:27](/bible/joel/2#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/joel/2#v27)</sup> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.

    The Most High is in the midst of Israel and none else, and his people shall never be ashamed.

# Daniel 4: Nebuchadnezzar Abased Until He Knew the Most High Rules

Read the chapter → [Daniel 4](/bible/daniel/4)

> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/4#v1)</sup> Nebuchadnezzar the king, unto all people, nations, and languages, that dwell in all the earth; Peace be multiplied unto you.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/4#v2)</sup> I thought it good to shew the signs and wonders that the high God hath wrought toward me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/4#v3)</sup> How great are his signs! and how mighty are his wonders! his kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and his dominion is from generation to generation.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/4#v4)</sup> I Nebuchadnezzar was at rest in mine house, and flourishing in my palace:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/4#v5)</sup> I saw a dream which made me afraid, and the thoughts upon my bed and the visions of my head troubled me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/4#v6)</sup> Therefore made I a decree to bring in all the wise men of Babylon before me, that they might make known unto me the interpretation of the dream.

- Nebuchadnezzar proclaimed to all people, nations, and languages the signs and wonders the high God worked toward him, whose kingdom is everlasting, then told how a dream made him afraid and he decreed to bring in all the wise men of Babylon to interpret it.
- Remember from the day before that he had already brought in all his wise men once and they could not recall or interpret his dream; when the captain went out to slay the wise men he came upon Daniel, who was granted favour, consulted the Most High, and was given the vision.

**[Daniel 4:7-9](/bible/daniel/4#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/4#v7)</sup> Then came in the magicians, the astrologers, the Chaldeans, and the soothsayers: and I told the dream before them; but they did not make known unto me the interpretation thereof.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/4#v8)</sup> But at the last Daniel came in before me, whose name was Belteshazzar, according to the name of my god, and in whom is the spirit of the holy gods: and before him I told the dream, saying,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/4#v9)</sup> O Belteshazzar, master of the magicians, because I know that the spirit of the holy gods is in thee, and no secret troubleth thee, tell me the visions of my dream that I have seen, and the interpretation thereof.

- The magicians, astrologers, Chaldeans, and soothsayers could not make known the interpretation, but at last Daniel came in, named Belteshazzar after the king's god, in whom is the spirit of the holy gods.
- Daniel was named after one of the king's gods. It is the same thing as Hadassah, whose name was changed to Esther; this is not new for us.

**[Daniel 4:10-17](/bible/daniel/4#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/4#v10)</sup> Thus were the visions of mine head in my bed; I saw, and behold a tree in the midst of the earth, and the height thereof was great.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/4#v11)</sup> The tree grew, and was strong, and the height thereof reached unto heaven, and the sight thereof to the end of all the earth:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/4#v12)</sup> The leaves thereof were fair, and the fruit thereof much, and in it was meat for all: the beasts of the field had shadow under it, and the fowls of the heaven dwelt in the boughs thereof, and all flesh was fed of it.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/4#v13)</sup> I saw in the visions of my head upon my bed, and, behold, a watcher and an holy one came down from heaven;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/4#v14)</sup> He cried aloud, and said thus, Hew down the tree, and cut off his branches, shake off his leaves, and scatter his fruit: let the beasts get away from under it, and the fowls from his branches:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/daniel/4#v15)</sup> Nevertheless leave the stump of his roots in the earth, even with a band of iron and brass, in the tender grass of the field; and let it be wet with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the beasts in the grass of the earth:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/daniel/4#v16)</sup> Let his heart be changed from man’s, and let a beast’s heart be given unto him; and let seven times pass over him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/4#v17)</sup> This matter is by the decree of the watchers, and the demand by the word of the holy ones: to the intent that the living may know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will, and setteth up over it the basest of men.

- Nebuchadnezzar saw a great tree reaching to heaven, feeding and sheltering all flesh, then a watcher and a holy one came down and commanded it hewn down, its stump left bound with iron and brass, its heart changed to a beast's, till seven times pass over him.
- The matter is by the decree of the watchers, to the intent that the living may know that the Most High ruleth in the kingdom of men and giveth it to whomsoever he will and setteth over it the basest of men. That is what we see today, the basest of men ruling the earth.
  - **[Romans 8:19](/bible/romans/8#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/romans/8#v19)</sup> For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.

    The whole creation waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God; the nations are waiting for us to wake up, shake off our sins, and take our place as the kings and priests of the earth.

Now thou, O Belteshazzar, declare the interpretation thereof, forasmuch as all the wise men of my kingdom are not able to make known unto me the interpretation: but thou art able; for the spirit of the holy gods is in thee.

Belteshazzar answered and said, My lord, the dream be to them that hate thee, and the interpretation thereof to thine enemies.

>
> <sup>[21](/bible/daniel/4#v21)</sup> Whose leaves were fair, and the fruit thereof much, and in it was meat for all; under which the beasts of the field dwelt, and upon whose branches the fowls of the heaven had their habitation:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/daniel/4#v22)</sup> It is thou, O king, that art grown and become strong: for thy greatness is grown, and reacheth unto heaven, and thy dominion to the end of the earth.

> <sup>[23](/bible/daniel/4#v23)</sup> And whereas the king saw a watcher and an holy one coming down from heaven, and saying, Hew the tree down, and destroy it; yet leave the stump of the roots thereof in the earth, even with a band of iron and brass, in the tender grass of the field; and let it be wet with the dew of heaven, and let his portion be with the beasts of the field, till seven times pass over him;
>

>
> <sup>[25](/bible/daniel/4#v25)</sup> That they shall drive thee from men, and thy dwelling shall be with the beasts of the field, and they shall make thee to eat grass as oxen, and they shall wet thee with the dew of heaven, and seven times shall pass over thee, till thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men, and giveth it to whomsoever he will.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/daniel/4#v26)</sup> And whereas they commanded to leave the stump of the tree roots; thy kingdom shall be sure unto thee, after that thou shalt have known that the heavens do rule.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/daniel/4#v27)</sup> Wherefore, O king, let my counsel be acceptable unto thee, and break off thy sins by righteousness, and thine iniquities by shewing mercy to the poor; if it may be a lengthening of thy tranquillity.

- Daniel, astonished for an hour, told the king the dream was for his enemies: the tree is the king himself, grown strong to the ends of the earth.
- The decree of the Most High is that he shall be driven from men to dwell with the beasts and eat grass as oxen, wet with the dew of heaven, till seven times pass and he knows the Most High ruleth; the stump left means his kingdom is kept for him once he knows the heavens do rule.
- Daniel counselled him to break off his sins by righteousness and his iniquities by shewing mercy to the poor, that his tranquillity might be lengthened. You need to walk in righteousness and show mercy to the poor.

- While the word was in his mouth a voice from heaven pronounced the kingdom departed, and the same hour he was driven from men, ate grass as oxen, his hairs grown like eagles' feathers and his nails like birds' claws.

  - **[Isaiah 40:17](/bible/isaiah/40#v17)**

# Daniel 5: The Handwriting on the Wall

Read the chapter → [Daniel 5](/bible/daniel/5)

**[Daniel 5:1-4](/bible/daniel/5#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/5#v1)</sup> Belshazzar the king made a great feast to a thousand of his lords, and drank wine before the thousand.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/5#v2)</sup> Belshazzar, whiles he tasted the wine, commanded to bring the golden and silver vessels which his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken out of the temple which was in Jerusalem; that the king, and his princes, his wives, and his concubines, might drink therein.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/5#v3)</sup> Then they brought the golden vessels that were taken out of the temple of the house of God which was at Jerusalem; and the king, and his princes, his wives, and his concubines, drank in them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/5#v4)</sup> They drank wine, and praised the gods of gold, and of silver, of brass, of iron, of wood, and of stone.

- Belshazzar the king made a great feast to a thousand of his lords and, while tasting the wine, commanded the golden and silver vessels his father Nebuchadnezzar had taken from the temple in Jerusalem be brought so he, his princes, wives, and concubines might drink in them.

**[Daniel 6:1-3](/bible/daniel/6#v1)**

>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/6#v3)</sup> Then this Daniel was preferred above the presidents and princes, because an excellent spirit was in him; and the king thought to set him over the whole realm.

- It pleased Darius to set a hundred and twenty princes over the kingdom, with three presidents over them, of whom Daniel was first, so the king should have no damage.
- Daniel was preferred above the presidents and princes because an excellent spirit was in him, and the king thought to set him over the whole realm. He was set in a very high position in government.

**[Daniel 6:4-5](/bible/daniel/6#v4)**

- The presidents and princes sought occasion against Daniel concerning the kingdom but could find none, because he was faithful and no error or fault was found in him. He was blameless.
- This is how our forefathers rolled, and the same kind of spirit we should be rolling in. They concluded they could only find something against him concerning the law of his God.
