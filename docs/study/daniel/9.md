**[Daniel 9:1-2](/bible/daniel/9#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/9#v1)</sup> In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, which was made king over the realm of the Chaldeans;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/9#v2)</sup> In the first year of his reign I Daniel understood by books the number of the years, whereof the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.

- In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, made king over the realm of the Chaldeans, Daniel understood by the books the seventy years of the desolations of Jerusalem spoken to Jeremiah.
- This is the same Darius dealt with when the Persians and Medes were shown in chapters 7 and 8.

**[Daniel 9:3-6](/bible/daniel/9#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/9#v3)</sup> And I set my face unto the Lord God, to seek by prayer and supplications, with fasting, and sackcloth, and ashes:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/9#v4)</sup> And I prayed unto the Lord my God, and made my confession, and said, O Lord, the great and dreadful God, keeping the covenant and mercy to them that love him, and to them that keep his commandments;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/9#v5)</sup> We have sinned, and have committed iniquity, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled, even by departing from thy precepts and from thy judgments:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/9#v6)</sup> Neither have we hearkened unto thy servants the prophets, which spake in thy name to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

- Daniel set his face unto the Most High to seek him by prayer and supplications, with fasting, sackcloth, and ashes, confessing that the people had sinned, committed iniquity, done wickedly, and rebelled, departing from his precepts and judgments.
- They had not hearkened unto the prophets who spake in his name to their kings, princes, fathers, and all the people of the land.

**[Daniel 9:7-10](/bible/daniel/9#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/9#v7)</sup> O Lord, righteousness belongeth unto thee, but unto us confusion of faces, as at this day; to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and unto all Israel, that are near, and that are far off, through all the countries whither thou hast driven them, because of their trespass that they have trespassed against thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/9#v8)</sup> O Lord, to us belongeth confusion of face, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, because we have sinned against thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/9#v9)</sup> To the Lord our God belong mercies and forgivenesses, though we have rebelled against him;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/9#v10)</sup> Neither have we obeyed the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in his laws, which he set before us by his servants the prophets.

- Righteousness belongs to the Most High, but confusion of faces to the people of Judah, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and all Israel, near and far off, driven through all the countries because of their trespasses.
- Our people were scattered everywhere because of our transgressions, exactly as the law warned.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    The Most High shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other.
- To the Most High belong mercies and forgiveness, though the people had rebelled and had not obeyed his voice to walk in his laws set before them by the prophets.

**[Daniel 9:11-14](/bible/daniel/9#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/9#v11)</sup> Yea, all Israel have transgressed thy law, even by departing, that they might not obey thy voice; therefore the curse is poured upon us, and the oath that is written in the law of Moses the servant of God, because we have sinned against him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/9#v12)</sup> And he hath confirmed his words, which he spake against us, and against our judges that judged us, by bringing upon us a great evil: for under the whole heaven hath not been done as hath been done upon Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/9#v13)</sup> As it is written in the law of Moses, all this evil is come upon us: yet made we not our prayer before the Lord our God, that we might turn from our iniquities, and understand thy truth.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/9#v14)</sup> Therefore hath the Lord watched upon the evil, and brought it upon us: for the Lord our God is righteous in all his works which he doeth: for we obeyed not his voice.

- All Israel transgressed the law by departing, so the curse was poured upon them and the oath written in the law of Moses, because they had sinned against him.
- The oath that was written is [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), verses 1 through 68.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

    If thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Most High to do his commandments, all these curses shall come upon thee and overtake thee.
- He confirmed his words by bringing upon them a great evil, for under the whole heaven none was done as was done upon Jerusalem, yet even so they had not made their prayer to turn from their iniquities. The Most High is righteous in all his works.

**[Daniel 9:15-19](/bible/daniel/9#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/daniel/9#v15)</sup> And now, O Lord our God, that hast brought thy people forth out of the land of Egypt with a mighty hand, and hast gotten thee renown, as at this day; we have sinned, we have done wickedly.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/daniel/9#v16)</sup> O Lord, according to all thy righteousness, I beseech thee, let thine anger and thy fury be turned away from thy city Jerusalem, thy holy mountain: because for our sins, and for the iniquities of our fathers, Jerusalem and thy people are become a reproach to all that are about us.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/9#v17)</sup> Now therefore, O our God, hear the prayer of thy servant, and his supplications, and cause thy face to shine upon thy sanctuary that is desolate, for the Lord’s sake.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/daniel/9#v18)</sup> O my God, incline thine ear, and hear; open thine eyes, and behold our desolations, and the city which is called by thy name: for we do not present our supplications before thee for our righteousnesses, but for thy great mercies.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/daniel/9#v19)</sup> O Lord, hear; O Lord, forgive; O Lord, hearken and do; defer not, for thine own sake, O my God: for thy city and thy people are called by thy name.

- Daniel appealed to the Most High who brought his people out of Egypt with a mighty hand, confessing they had sinned and done wickedly, and asking that his anger and fury be turned away from his city Jerusalem, his holy mountain.
- He pleaded for the Most High to hear, forgive, hearken, and do, not for their righteousness but for his great mercies, since the city and the people are called by his name.

**[Daniel 9:20-23](/bible/daniel/9#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/daniel/9#v20)</sup> And whiles I was speaking, and praying, and confessing my sin and the sin of my people Israel, and presenting my supplication before the Lord my God for the holy mountain of my God;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/daniel/9#v21)</sup> Yea, whiles I was speaking in prayer, even the man Gabriel, whom I had seen in the vision at the beginning, being caused to fly swiftly, touched me about the time of the evening oblation.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/daniel/9#v22)</sup> And he informed me, and talked with me, and said, O Daniel, I am now come forth to give thee skill and understanding.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/daniel/9#v23)</sup> At the beginning of thy supplications the commandment came forth, and I am come to shew thee; for thou art greatly beloved: therefore understand the matter, and consider the vision.

- While Daniel was speaking, praying, and confessing his sin and the sin of his people Israel, the man Gabriel, whom he had seen in the vision, was caused to fly swiftly and touched him about the time of the evening oblation.
- Gabriel came to give him skill and understanding, for at the beginning of his supplications the commandment came forth, and Daniel was greatly beloved.

**[Daniel 9:24-27](/bible/daniel/9#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/daniel/9#v24)</sup> Seventy weeks are determined upon thy people and upon thy holy city, to finish the transgression, and to make an end of sins, and to make reconciliation for iniquity, and to bring in everlasting righteousness, and to seal up the vision and prophecy, and to anoint the most Holy.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/daniel/9#v25)</sup> Know therefore and understand, that from the going forth of the commandment to restore and to build Jerusalem unto the Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks, and threescore and two weeks: the street shall be built again, and the wall, even in troublous times.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/daniel/9#v26)</sup> And after threescore and two weeks shall Messiah be cut off, but not for himself: and the people of the prince that shall come shall destroy the city and the sanctuary; and the end thereof shall be with a flood, and unto the end of the war desolations are determined.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/daniel/9#v27)</sup> And he shall confirm the covenant with many for one week: and in the midst of the week he shall cause the sacrifice and the oblation to cease, and for the overspreading of abominations he shall make it desolate, even until the consummation, and that determined shall be poured upon the desolate.

- Seventy weeks are determined upon the people and the holy city to finish the transgression, make an end of sins, make reconciliation for iniquity, bring in everlasting righteousness, seal up the vision, and anoint the most Holy.
- From the going forth of the commandment to restore and build Jerusalem unto Messiah the Prince shall be seven weeks and threescore and two weeks, the street built again even in troublous times.
- After threescore and two weeks Messiah is cut off, but not for himself, and the people of the prince that shall come destroy the city and the sanctuary. He confirms the covenant with many for one week, and in the midst of the week causes the sacrifice and oblation to cease.
- Daniel was given the four beasts first, then this further vision of the seventy weeks by Gabriel in the midst of his prayer and supplications. The bishop's class on the seventy weeks opens this up in full.
