Read the chapter → [Daniel 7](/bible/daniel/7)

This session covers [Daniel 7](/bible/daniel/7) through 10. These four chapters go into the same subject from different angles: the vision of the four beasts, which are four kingdoms of men. Daniel sees them first as beasts coming up out of the sea, then again as a ram and a goat, and he is given the seventy weeks and a final vision by the river. The kingdoms are Babylon, Persia and the Medes, Greece, and Rome, with Rome's extension reaching into Spain, France, Great Britain, and America. Daniel is given to understand these dark sayings because he was greatly beloved of the Most High.

**[Daniel 7:1](/bible/daniel/7#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/7#v1)</sup> In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon Daniel had a dream and visions of his head upon his bed: then he wrote the dream, and told the sum of the matters.

- In the first year of Belshazzar king of Babylon, Daniel had a dream and visions of his head upon his bed, and he wrote the dream and told the sum of the matters.
- This dream is the same subject Daniel returns to in chapter 8 and again in the later chapters. He tells the vision more than once.

**[Daniel 7:2-4](/bible/daniel/7#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/7#v2)</sup> Daniel spake and said, I saw in my vision by night, and, behold, the four winds of the heaven strove upon the great sea.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/7#v3)</sup> And four great beasts came up from the sea, diverse one from another.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/7#v4)</sup> The first was like a lion, and had eagle’s wings: I beheld till the wings thereof were plucked, and it was lifted up from the earth, and made stand upon the feet as a man, and a man’s heart was given to it.

- Daniel saw the four winds of heaven strive upon the great sea, and four great beasts came up from the sea, diverse one from another.
- These beasts are not animals; they are men. When the Most High speaks of beasts here, he is dealing with four kingdoms of men.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 3:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)</sup> I said in mine heart concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts.

    Concerning the estate of the sons of men, the Most High might manifest them that they themselves are beasts.
- The first beast was like a lion with eagle's wings, lifted up and made to stand upon its feet as a man. This first kingdom is Babylon.

**[Daniel 7:5](/bible/daniel/7#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/7#v5)</sup> And behold another beast, a second, like to a bear, and it raised up itself on one side, and it had three ribs in the mouth of it between the teeth of it: and they said thus unto it, Arise, devour much flesh.

- The second beast was like a bear, raised up on one side, with three ribs in its mouth, told to arise and devour much flesh.
- This second beast is the Persians and the Medes. It raised itself higher on one side because one of those two kingdoms was stronger than the other.

**[Daniel 7:6](/bible/daniel/7#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/7#v6)</sup> After this I beheld, and lo another, like a leopard, which had upon the back of it four wings of a fowl; the beast had also four heads; and dominion was given to it.

- The third beast was like a leopard with four wings of a fowl and four heads, and dominion was given to it.
- This third beast is the Greeks. The details and breakdowns are covered in the classes on these chapters.

**[Daniel 7:7](/bible/daniel/7#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/7#v7)</sup> After this I saw in the night visions, and behold a fourth beast, dreadful and terrible, and strong exceedingly; and it had great iron teeth: it devoured and brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with the feet of it: and it was diverse from all the beasts that were before it; and it had ten horns.

- The fourth beast was dreadful and terrible and strong exceedingly, with great iron teeth, devouring and breaking in pieces and stamping the residue with its feet, diverse from all the beasts before it.
- This fourth beast is the Romans. Out of Rome comes the extension: Rome, then Spain, France, Great Britain, and then America.

**[Daniel 7:8](/bible/daniel/7#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/7#v8)</sup> I considered the horns, and, behold, there came up among them another little horn, before whom there were three of the first horns plucked up by the roots: and, behold, in this horn were eyes like the eyes of man, and a mouth speaking great things.

- Daniel considered the horns, and behold, a little horn came up among them, before whom three of the first horns were plucked up by the roots, and in this horn were eyes like the eyes of a man and a mouth speaking great things.
- This little horn is the same horn that makes war with the saints later in the chapter.

**[Daniel 7:9-14](/bible/daniel/7#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/7#v9)</sup> I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne was like the fiery flame, and his wheels as burning fire.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/7#v10)</sup> A fiery stream issued and came forth from before him: thousand thousands ministered unto him, and ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him: the judgment was set, and the books were opened.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/7#v11)</sup> I beheld then because of the voice of the great words which the horn spake: I beheld even till the beast was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the burning flame.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/7#v12)</sup> As concerning the rest of the beasts, they had their dominion taken away: yet their lives were prolonged for a season and time.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/7#v13)</sup> I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/7#v14)</sup> And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.

- Daniel saw the thrones cast down and the Ancient of days sit, his garment white as snow and the hair of his head like pure wool, his throne like the fiery flame, a fiery stream issuing before him, thousand thousands ministering unto him, the judgment set and the books opened.
- The fourth beast is slain and its body given to the burning flame, while the rest of the beasts have their dominion taken away but their lives prolonged for a season.
- One like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven to the Ancient of days, and there was given him dominion and glory and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages should serve him, an everlasting dominion that shall not pass away.
- His coming with the clouds is his coming with his chariots, the same coming [Matthew 24](/bible/matthew/24) speaks of.
  - **[Matthew 24:30](/bible/matthew/24#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/24#v30)</sup> And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.

    The Son of man comes in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.

**[Daniel 7:15-16](/bible/daniel/7#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/daniel/7#v15)</sup> I Daniel was grieved in my spirit in the midst of my body, and the visions of my head troubled me.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/daniel/7#v16)</sup> I came near unto one of them that stood by, and asked him the truth of all this. So he told me, and made me know the interpretation of the things.

- Daniel was grieved in his spirit and the visions of his head troubled him, so he came near unto one that stood by and asked the truth of all this, and was made to know the interpretation.
- The interpretation of the vision described in verses 1 through 14 now begins.

**[Daniel 7:17-18](/bible/daniel/7#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/7#v17)</sup> These great beasts, which are four, are four kings, which shall arise out of the earth.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/daniel/7#v18)</sup> But the saints of the most High shall take the kingdom, and possess the kingdom for ever, even for ever and ever.

- The four great beasts are four kings that shall arise out of the earth.
- But the saints of the Most High shall take the kingdom and possess it forever, even for ever and ever.

**[Daniel 7:19-22](/bible/daniel/7#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/daniel/7#v19)</sup> Then I would know the truth of the fourth beast, which was diverse from all the others, exceeding dreadful, whose teeth were of iron, and his nails of brass; which devoured, brake in pieces, and stamped the residue with his feet;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/daniel/7#v20)</sup> And of the ten horns that were in his head, and of the other which came up, and before whom three fell; even of that horn that had eyes, and a mouth that spake very great things, whose look was more stout than his fellows.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/daniel/7#v21)</sup> I beheld, and the same horn made war with the saints, and prevailed against them;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/daniel/7#v22)</sup> Until the Ancient of days came, and judgment was given to the saints of the most High; and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.

- Daniel wanted the truth of the fourth beast, diverse from all the others, exceeding dreadful, with teeth of iron and nails of brass, and of the ten horns and the little horn with eyes and a mouth speaking great things.
- The ten horns are the ten common markets, as the bishop's class lays out. The little horn made war with the saints and prevailed against them.
- This continues until the Ancient of days came and judgment was given to the saints of the Most High, and the time came that the saints possessed the kingdom.
  - **[2 Esdras 12:11-12](/bible/2-esdras/12#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/12#v11)</sup> The eagle, whom thou sawest come up from the sea, is the kingdom which was seen in the vision of thy brother Daniel.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-esdras/12#v12)</sup> But it was not expounded unto him, therefore now I declare it unto thee.

    Second Esdras opens up that fourth beast, the eagle kingdom, which is an evil, tying it directly back to Daniel's vision.

**[Daniel 7:23-28](/bible/daniel/7#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/daniel/7#v23)</sup> Thus he said, The fourth beast shall be the fourth kingdom upon earth, which shall be diverse from all kingdoms, and shall devour the whole earth, and shall tread it down, and break it in pieces.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/daniel/7#v24)</sup> And the ten horns out of this kingdom are ten kings that shall arise: and another shall rise after them; and he shall be diverse from the first, and he shall subdue three kings.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/daniel/7#v25)</sup> And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/daniel/7#v26)</sup> But the judgment shall sit, and they shall take away his dominion, to consume and to destroy it unto the end.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/daniel/7#v27)</sup> And the kingdom and dominion, and the greatness of the kingdom under the whole heaven, shall be given to the people of the saints of the most High, whose kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and all dominions shall serve and obey him.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/daniel/7#v28)</sup> Hitherto is the end of the matter. As for me Daniel, my cogitations much troubled me, and my countenance changed in me: but I kept the matter in my heart.

- The fourth beast is the fourth kingdom upon the earth, diverse from all kingdoms, devouring the whole earth, treading it down and breaking it in pieces. The ten horns are ten kings, and another rises after them, subduing three.
- This last horn speaks great words against the Most High, wears out the saints, and thinks to change times and laws, and they are given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of times.
- Then the judgment sits, his dominion is taken away and destroyed unto the end, and the kingdom and dominion under the whole heaven is given to the people of the saints of the Most High, whose kingdom is everlasting.
- Daniel kept the matter in his heart, his cogitations much troubling him and his countenance changed.

# Daniel 8: The Ram and the Goat

Read the chapter → [Daniel 8](/bible/daniel/8)

**[Daniel 8:1-2](/bible/daniel/8#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/8#v1)</sup> In the third year of the reign of king Belshazzar a vision appeared unto me, even unto me Daniel, after that which appeared unto me at the first.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/8#v2)</sup> And I saw in a vision; and it came to pass, when I saw, that I was at Shushan in the palace, which is in the province of Elam; and I saw in a vision, and I was by the river of Ulai.

- In the third year of Belshazzar's reign, a vision appeared to Daniel after the first, and he saw himself at Shushan in the palace in the province of Elam, by the river of Ulai.
- This is the same vision as chapter 7, but now the kingdoms are described with different animals.

**[Daniel 8:3-4](/bible/daniel/8#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/8#v3)</sup> Then I lifted up mine eyes, and saw, and, behold, there stood before the river a ram which had two horns: and the two horns were high; but one was higher than the other, and the higher came up last.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/8#v4)</sup> I saw the ram pushing westward, and northward, and southward; so that no beasts might stand before him, neither was there any that could deliver out of his hand; but he did according to his will, and became great.

- Daniel saw a ram with two horns, one higher than the other, the higher coming up last, pushing westward, northward, and southward so that no beast could stand before him.
- This ram is the Persians and the Medes again. Before they were a bear; now they are a ram, with the two horns being the two kingdoms.

**[Daniel 8:5-8](/bible/daniel/8#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/8#v5)</sup> And as I was considering, behold, an he goat came from the west on the face of the whole earth, and touched not the ground: and the goat had a notable horn between his eyes.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/8#v6)</sup> And he came to the ram that had two horns, which I had seen standing before the river, and ran unto him in the fury of his power.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/8#v7)</sup> And I saw him come close unto the ram, and he was moved with choler against him, and smote the ram, and brake his two horns: and there was no power in the ram to stand before him, but he cast him down to the ground, and stamped upon him: and there was none that could deliver the ram out of his hand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/8#v8)</sup> Therefore the he goat waxed very great: and when he was strong, the great horn was broken; and for it came up four notable ones toward the four winds of heaven.

- A he goat came from the west over the face of the whole earth without touching the ground, with a notable horn between his eyes, and ran unto the ram in the fury of his power.
- This goat is Greece, Alexander the Greek. Moved with hatred, he smote the ram, broke his two horns, cast him down, and stamped upon him, and none could deliver the ram.
- When the goat was strong, the great horn was broken, and four notable horns came up toward the four winds of heaven.

**[Daniel 8:9-12](/bible/daniel/8#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/8#v9)</sup> And out of one of them came forth a little horn, which waxed exceeding great, toward the south, and toward the east, and toward the pleasant land.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/8#v10)</sup> And it waxed great, even to the host of heaven; and it cast down some of the host and of the stars to the ground, and stamped upon them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/8#v11)</sup> Yea, he magnified himself even to the prince of the host, and by him the daily sacrifice was taken away, and the place of his sanctuary was cast down.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/8#v12)</sup> And an host was given him against the daily sacrifice by reason of transgression, and it cast down the truth to the ground; and it practised, and prospered.

- Out of one of them came a little horn which waxed exceeding great toward the south, the east, and the pleasant land, magnifying itself even to the host of heaven and casting down some of the host and the stars.
- It magnified itself against the prince of the host, took away the daily sacrifice, cast down the place of the sanctuary, and cast down the truth to the ground, practicing and prospering.

**[Daniel 8:13-14](/bible/daniel/8#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/8#v13)</sup> Then I heard one saint speaking, and another saint said unto that certain saint which spake, How long shall be the vision concerning the daily sacrifice, and the transgression of desolation, to give both the sanctuary and the host to be trodden under foot?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/8#v14)</sup> And he said unto me, Unto two thousand and three hundred days; then shall the sanctuary be cleansed.

- A saint asked how long the vision concerning the daily sacrifice and the transgression of desolation should last, and the answer came: unto two thousand and three hundred days, then shall the sanctuary be cleansed.
- The cleansing of the sanctuary goes into Hanukkah, the feast of dedication. That history is recorded in First Maccabees.
  - **[1 Maccabees 4:52-54](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v52)**
    > <sup>[52](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v52)</sup> Now on the five and twentieth day of the ninth month, which is called the month Casleu, in the hundred forty and eighth year, they rose up betimes in the morning,
    >
    > <sup>[53](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v53)</sup> And offered sacrifice according to the law upon the new altar of burnt offerings, which they had made.
    >
    > <sup>[54](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v54)</sup> Look, at what time and what day the heathen had profaned it, even in that was it dedicated with songs, and citherns, and harps, and cymbals.

    The sanctuary is dedicated again on the very day the heathen had profaned it, the dedication Daniel's cleansing points to.

**[Daniel 8:15-19](/bible/daniel/8#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/daniel/8#v15)</sup> And it came to pass, when I, even I Daniel, had seen the vision, and sought for the meaning, then, behold, there stood before me as the appearance of a man.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/daniel/8#v16)</sup> And I heard a man’s voice between the banks of Ulai, which called, and said, Gabriel, make this man to understand the vision.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/8#v17)</sup> So he came near where I stood: and when he came, I was afraid, and fell upon my face: but he said unto me, Understand, O son of man: for at the time of the end shall be the vision.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/daniel/8#v18)</sup> Now as he was speaking with me, I was in a deep sleep on my face toward the ground: but he touched me, and set me upright.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/daniel/8#v19)</sup> And he said, Behold, I will make thee know what shall be in the last end of the indignation: for at the time appointed the end shall be.

- As Daniel sought the meaning, one with the appearance of a man stood before him, and a voice called Gabriel to make him understand the vision.
- Gabriel came near, and Daniel was afraid and fell on his face, but was told to understand, for at the time of the end shall be the vision. Gabriel set him upright to know what shall be in the last end of the indignation.

**[Daniel 8:20-22](/bible/daniel/8#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/daniel/8#v20)</sup> The ram which thou sawest having two horns are the kings of Media and Persia.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/daniel/8#v21)</sup> And the rough goat is the king of Grecia: and the great horn that is between his eyes is the first king.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/daniel/8#v22)</sup> Now that being broken, whereas four stood up for it, four kingdoms shall stand up out of the nation, but not in his power.

- The ram with two horns are the kings of Media and Persia. The rough goat is the king of Grecia, and the great horn between his eyes is the first king, Alexander the Greek.
- The great horn being broken and four standing up in its place are four kingdoms that stand up out of the nation but not in his power. These four were Alexander's four generals.

**[Daniel 8:23-25](/bible/daniel/8#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/daniel/8#v23)</sup> And in the latter time of their kingdom, when the transgressors are come to the full, a king of fierce countenance, and understanding dark sentences, shall stand up.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/daniel/8#v24)</sup> And his power shall be mighty, but not by his own power: and he shall destroy wonderfully, and shall prosper, and practise, and shall destroy the mighty and the holy people.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/daniel/8#v25)</sup> And through his policy also he shall cause craft to prosper in his hand; and he shall magnify himself in his heart, and by peace shall destroy many: he shall also stand up against the Prince of princes; but he shall be broken without hand.

- In the latter time of their kingdom, when the transgressors are come to the full, a king of fierce countenance understanding dark sentences shall stand up, mighty but not by his own power, destroying wonderfully and prospering, and destroying the mighty and the holy people.
- Through his policy he causes craft to prosper in his hand, magnifies himself, and by peace destroys many, standing up against the Prince of princes, but he shall be broken without hand.
- The nation that stands against the Prince of princes is the United States, Babylon the Great. This is how we know Greece, Rome, and America are an extension of the same beast.

**[Daniel 8:26-27](/bible/daniel/8#v26)**
> <sup>[26](/bible/daniel/8#v26)</sup> And the vision of the evening and the morning which was told is true: wherefore shut thou up the vision; for it shall be for many days.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/daniel/8#v27)</sup> And I Daniel fainted, and was sick certain days; afterward I rose up, and did the king’s business; and I was astonished at the vision, but none understood it.

- The vision of the evening and the morning is true, but Daniel was to shut it up, for it is for many days.
- Daniel fainted and was sick certain days, then rose and did the king's business, astonished at the vision, but none understood it. The Most High gives his prophets to understand dark sayings and visions.

# Daniel 9: The Seventy Weeks

Read the chapter → [Daniel 9](/bible/daniel/9)

**[Daniel 9:1-2](/bible/daniel/9#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/9#v1)</sup> In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, which was made king over the realm of the Chaldeans;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/9#v2)</sup> In the first year of his reign I Daniel understood by books the number of the years, whereof the word of the Lord came to Jeremiah the prophet, that he would accomplish seventy years in the desolations of Jerusalem.

- In the first year of Darius the son of Ahasuerus, of the seed of the Medes, made king over the realm of the Chaldeans, Daniel understood by the books the seventy years of the desolations of Jerusalem spoken to Jeremiah.
- This is the same Darius dealt with when the Persians and Medes were shown in chapters 7 and 8.

**[Daniel 9:3-6](/bible/daniel/9#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/9#v3)</sup> And I set my face unto the Lord God, to seek by prayer and supplications, with fasting, and sackcloth, and ashes:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/9#v4)</sup> And I prayed unto the Lord my God, and made my confession, and said, O Lord, the great and dreadful God, keeping the covenant and mercy to them that love him, and to them that keep his commandments;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/9#v5)</sup> We have sinned, and have committed iniquity, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled, even by departing from thy precepts and from thy judgments:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/9#v6)</sup> Neither have we hearkened unto thy servants the prophets, which spake in thy name to our kings, our princes, and our fathers, and to all the people of the land.

- Daniel set his face unto the Most High to seek him by prayer and supplications, with fasting, sackcloth, and ashes, confessing that the people had sinned, committed iniquity, done wickedly, and rebelled, departing from his precepts and judgments.
- They had not hearkened unto the prophets who spake in his name to their kings, princes, fathers, and all the people of the land.

**[Daniel 9:7-10](/bible/daniel/9#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/9#v7)</sup> O Lord, righteousness belongeth unto thee, but unto us confusion of faces, as at this day; to the men of Judah, and to the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and unto all Israel, that are near, and that are far off, through all the countries whither thou hast driven them, because of their trespass that they have trespassed against thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/9#v8)</sup> O Lord, to us belongeth confusion of face, to our kings, to our princes, and to our fathers, because we have sinned against thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/9#v9)</sup> To the Lord our God belong mercies and forgivenesses, though we have rebelled against him;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/9#v10)</sup> Neither have we obeyed the voice of the Lord our God, to walk in his laws, which he set before us by his servants the prophets.

- Righteousness belongs to the Most High, but confusion of faces to the people of Judah, the inhabitants of Jerusalem, and all Israel, near and far off, driven through all the countries because of their trespasses.
- Our people were scattered everywhere because of our transgressions, exactly as the law warned.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    The Most High shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other.
- To the Most High belong mercies and forgiveness, though the people had rebelled and had not obeyed his voice to walk in his laws set before them by the prophets.

**[Daniel 9:11-14](/bible/daniel/9#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/9#v11)</sup> Yea, all Israel have transgressed thy law, even by departing, that they might not obey thy voice; therefore the curse is poured upon us, and the oath that is written in the law of Moses the servant of God, because we have sinned against him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/9#v12)</sup> And he hath confirmed his words, which he spake against us, and against our judges that judged us, by bringing upon us a great evil: for under the whole heaven hath not been done as hath been done upon Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/9#v13)</sup> As it is written in the law of Moses, all this evil is come upon us: yet made we not our prayer before the Lord our God, that we might turn from our iniquities, and understand thy truth.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/9#v14)</sup> Therefore hath the Lord watched upon the evil, and brought it upon us: for the Lord our God is righteous in all his works which he doeth: for we obeyed not his voice.

- All Israel transgressed the law by departing, so the curse was poured upon them and the oath written in the law of Moses, because they had sinned against him.
- The oath that was written is [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), verses 1 through 68.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

    If thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Most High to do his commandments, all these curses shall come upon thee and overtake thee.
- He confirmed his words by bringing upon them a great evil, for under the whole heaven none was done as was done upon Jerusalem, yet even so they had not made their prayer to turn from their iniquities. The Most High is righteous in all his works.

> <sup>[19](/bible/daniel/9#v19)</sup> O Lord, hear; O Lord, forgive; O Lord, hearken and do; defer not, for thine own sake, O my God: for thy city and thy people are called by thy name.

- He pleaded for the Most High to hear, forgive, hearken, and do, not for their righteousness but for his great mercies, since the city and the people are called by his name.

# Daniel 10: A Man Clothed in Linen

**[Daniel 10:1-3](/bible/daniel/10#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/10#v1)</sup> In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia a thing was revealed unto Daniel, whose name was called Belteshazzar; and the thing was true, but the time appointed was long: and he understood the thing, and had understanding of the vision.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/10#v2)</sup> In those days I Daniel was mourning three full weeks.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/10#v3)</sup> I ate no pleasant bread, neither came flesh nor wine in my mouth, neither did I anoint myself at all, till three whole weeks were fulfilled.

- In the third year of Cyrus king of Persia, a thing was revealed unto Daniel, whose name was called Belteshazzar, and the thing was true but the time appointed was long, and he understood the vision.
- Daniel was mourning three full weeks, eating no pleasant bread, no flesh or wine, and did not anoint himself until the three weeks were fulfilled.

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/10#v6)</sup> His body also was like the beryl, and his face as the appearance of lightning, and his eyes as lamps of fire, and his arms and his feet like in colour to polished brass, and the voice of his words like the voice of a multitude.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/10#v7)</sup> And I Daniel alone saw the vision: for the men that were with me saw not the vision; but a great quaking fell upon them, so that they fled to hide themselves.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/10#v8)</sup> Therefore I was left alone, and saw this great vision, and there remained no strength in me: for my comeliness was turned in me into corruption, and I retained no strength.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/10#v9)</sup> Yet heard I the voice of his words: and when I heard the voice of his words, then was I in a deep sleep on my face, and my face toward the ground.

- By the side of the great river Hiddekel, Daniel lifted his eyes and saw a certain man clothed in linen, his loins girded with fine gold of Uphaz, his body like the beryl, his face as lightning, his eyes as lamps of fire, his arms and feet like polished brass, and his voice like the voice of a multitude.
- Daniel alone saw the vision; the men with him did not see it but a great quaking fell on them and they fled. Daniel was left alone, his strength turned to corruption, and he fell into a deep sleep on his face toward the ground.

**[Daniel 10:10-14](/bible/daniel/10#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/10#v10)</sup> And, behold, an hand touched me, which set me upon my knees and upon the palms of my hands.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/10#v11)</sup> And he said unto me, O Daniel, a man greatly beloved, understand the words that I speak unto thee, and stand upright: for unto thee am I now sent. And when he had spoken this word unto me, I stood trembling.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/10#v12)</sup> Then said he unto me, Fear not, Daniel: for from the first day that thou didst set thine heart to understand, and to chasten thyself before thy God, thy words were heard, and I am come for thy words.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/10#v13)</sup> But the prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood me one and twenty days: but, lo, Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help me; and I remained there with the kings of Persia.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/10#v14)</sup> Now I am come to make thee understand what shall befall thy people in the latter days: for yet the vision is for many days.

- A hand touched Daniel and set him upon his knees and palms, and he was told he is a man greatly beloved, to understand the words spoken and stand upright, for he was now sent to him.
- The one speaking told Daniel that from the first day he set his heart to understand and to chasten himself before the Most High, his words were heard, and he came for his words. The Most High heard Daniel's supplications and prayer because he was beloved, and gave him these visions for the same reason. It is the same with Israel, the beloved of the Most High.
  - **[1 Kings 8:33-34](/bible/1-kings/8#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/1-kings/8#v33)</sup> When thy people Israel be smitten down before the enemy, because they have sinned against thee, and shall turn again to thee, and confess thy name, and pray, and make supplication unto thee in this house:

- The prince of the kingdom of Persia withstood him twenty-one days, until Michael, one of the chief princes, came to help him. He came to make Daniel understand what shall befall his people in the latter days, for the vision is for many days.
- Michael and Gabriel contending with the prince of Persia and the prince of Grecia shows that there are angels set over cities and even governments. The Most High has his angels everywhere, as with the angels that came and dealt with Sodom and Gomorrah.
