Read the chapter → [Daniel 11](/bible/daniel/11)

This session covers [Daniel 11](/bible/daniel/11) and 12. [Daniel 11](/bible/daniel/11) lays out the prophecy given after the fall of Persia and the rise of Greece, following the long war between the king of the south and the king of the north, the Ptolemies and the Seleucids, down to Antiochus who came up against the holy covenant. [Daniel 12](/bible/daniel/12) closes the book with Michael standing up in the time of trouble, the resurrection, and the sealed words that are now being opened in the last days. The books of the Maccabees fill in the history of this same period and are read alongside the prophecy.

**[Daniel 11:1](/bible/daniel/11#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/11#v1)</sup> Also I in the first year of Darius the Mede, even I, stood to confirm and to strengthen him.

- In the first year of Darius the Mede, the messenger stood to confirm and to strengthen him.

**[Daniel 11:2](/bible/daniel/11#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/11#v2)</sup> And now will I shew thee the truth. Behold, there shall stand up yet three kings in Persia; and the fourth shall be far richer than they all: and by his strength through his riches he shall stir up all against the realm of Grecia.

- Three more kings would stand up in Persia: Cyrus, Cambyses, and Darius.
- The fourth would be far richer than they all. This is Xerxes, who profited from all that the kingdom had already gained, and by his strength through his riches he stirred up all against the realm of Grecia.
- This is where they gathered all the heathen together and formed the Greeks. They conquered the original Greeks and named themselves the Greeks, and all the nations, even some of my own tribes, came together and were called the Greeks.

**[Daniel 11:3-4](/bible/daniel/11#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/11#v3)</sup> And a mighty king shall stand up, that shall rule with great dominion, and do according to his will.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/11#v4)</sup> And when he shall stand up, his kingdom shall be broken, and shall be divided toward the four winds of heaven; and not to his posterity, nor according to his dominion which he ruled: for his kingdom shall be plucked up, even for others beside those.

- The mighty king that stands up and rules with great dominion, doing according to his will, is Alexander the Greek. When a nation is a superpower it does what it will and nobody can stop it, the same as America does today.
- When he stood up his kingdom was broken, because he eventually died and left no children, so it was not passed to his posterity.
- His kingdom was divided toward the four winds of heaven, to his four generals, Cassander, Lysimachus, Seleucus, and Ptolemy, not according to his own dominion.

**[Daniel 11:5](/bible/daniel/11#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/11#v5)</sup> And the king of the south shall be strong, and one of his princes; and he shall be strong above him, and have dominion; his dominion shall be a great dominion.

- The king of the south that grows strong is Ptolemy, who was over Egypt.
- One of his princes grows strong above him with a great dominion. Out of Ptolemy came Antiochus.

**[Daniel 11:6](/bible/daniel/11#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/11#v6)</sup> And in the end of years they shall join themselves together; for the king’s daughter of the south shall come to the king of the north to make an agreement: but she shall not retain the power of the arm; neither shall he stand, nor his arm: but she shall be given up, and they that brought her, and he that begat her, and he that strengthened her in these times.

- In the end of years they join themselves together, and the king's daughter of the south comes to the king of the north to make an agreement.
- She does not retain the power of the arm, neither does he stand nor his arm. The power of the arm is Antiochus' strength.

**[Daniel 11:7-9](/bible/daniel/11#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/11#v7)</sup> But out of a branch of her roots shall one stand up in his estate, which shall come with an army, and shall enter into the fortress of the king of the north, and shall deal against them, and shall prevail:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/11#v8)</sup> And shall also carry captives into Egypt their gods, with their princes, and with their precious vessels of silver and of gold; and he shall continue more years than the king of the north.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/11#v9)</sup> So the king of the south shall come into his kingdom, and shall return into his own land.

- Out of a branch of her roots one stands up in her estate, comes with an army, enters the fortress of the king of the north, deals against them and prevails. It was actual war over the kingdoms.
- He carries captives into Egypt, their gods with their princes and their precious vessels of silver and gold, for Ptolemy was over Egypt, and he continues more years than the king of the north.

**[Daniel 11:10-12](/bible/daniel/11#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/11#v10)</sup> But his sons shall be stirred up, and shall assemble a multitude of great forces: and one shall certainly come, and overflow, and pass through: then shall he return, and be stirred up, even to his fortress.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/11#v11)</sup> And the king of the south shall be moved with choler, and shall come forth and fight with him, even with the king of the north: and he shall set forth a great multitude; but the multitude shall be given into his hand.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/11#v12)</sup> And when he hath taken away the multitude, his heart shall be lifted up; and he shall cast down many ten thousands: but he shall not be strengthened by it.

- His sons are stirred up, assemble a multitude of great forces, and one comes and overflows and passes through, even to his fortress.
- The king of the south is moved with anger, comes forth, and fights the king of the north, who sets forth a great multitude, but the multitude is given into the hand of the south.
- When he has taken away the multitude his heart is lifted up and he becomes prideful, casting down many ten thousands, but he is not strengthened by it.

**[Daniel 11:13](/bible/daniel/11#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/11#v13)</sup> For the king of the north shall return, and shall set forth a multitude greater than the former, and shall certainly come after certain years with a great army and with much riches.

- The king of the north returns and sets forth a multitude greater than the former, coming after certain years with a great army and much riches.

**[Daniel 11:14](/bible/daniel/11#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/11#v14)</sup> And in those times there shall many stand up against the king of the south: also the robbers of thy people shall exalt themselves to establish the vision; but they shall fall.

- In those times many stand up against the king of the south, meaning Ptolemy.
- The robbers of thy people, the heathen, the Greeks who robbed Israel, exalt themselves to establish the vision, but they shall fall.

**[Daniel 11:15-16](/bible/daniel/11#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/daniel/11#v15)</sup> So the king of the north shall come, and cast up a mount, and take the most fenced cities: and the arms of the south shall not withstand, neither his chosen people, neither shall there be any strength to withstand.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/daniel/11#v16)</sup> But he that cometh against him shall do according to his own will, and none shall stand before him: and he shall stand in the glorious land, which by his hand shall be consumed.

- The king of the north comes up, casts up a mount, and takes the most fenced cities, and the arms of the south cannot withstand, neither his chosen people, meaning Israel, nor any strength to withstand.
- He that comes against him does according to his own will, and none stands before him, because he is the power at this time, and he stands in the glorious land which is consumed by his hand.

**[Daniel 11:17-19](/bible/daniel/11#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/daniel/11#v17)</sup> He shall also set his face to enter with the strength of his whole kingdom, and upright ones with him; thus shall he do: and he shall give him the daughter of women, corrupting her: but she shall not stand on his side, neither be for him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/daniel/11#v18)</sup> After this shall he turn his face unto the isles, and shall take many: but a prince for his own behalf shall cause the reproach offered by him to cease; without his own reproach he shall cause it to turn upon him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/daniel/11#v19)</sup> Then he shall turn his face toward the fort of his own land: but he shall stumble and fall, and not be found.

- He sets his face to enter with the strength of his whole kingdom, and gives him the daughter of women, corrupting her, but she does not stand on his side.
- He turns his face unto the isles of the coastline and takes many, but a prince causes the reproach he offered to cease and turns it back upon him. Someone causes him to stop conquering land, so he is conquered and falls, and this goes to Ptolemy.
- Then he turns his face toward the fort of his own land, but he stumbles and falls and is not found.

# Daniel 12: The Time of the End

Read the chapter → [Daniel 12](/bible/daniel/12)

**[Daniel 12:1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)</sup> And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.

- At that time Michael stands up, the great prince which stands for the children of thy people, and there is a time of trouble such as never was since there was a nation.
- At that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that is found written in the book. This goes into the prophecy of the last days.

**[Daniel 12:2-3](/bible/daniel/12#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/12#v2)</sup> And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/12#v3)</sup> And they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament; and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars for ever and ever.

- Many that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life and some to shame and everlasting contempt.
- They that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament, and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars forever and ever.

**[Daniel 12:4](/bible/daniel/12#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/daniel/12#v4)</sup> But thou, O Daniel, shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end: many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased.

- Daniel is told to shut up the words and seal the book even to the time of the end, and many shall run to and fro, and knowledge shall be increased.
- This is how we know we are in the last days: the book was sealed until the time of the end, and now understanding is coming out and it is opened.

**[Daniel 12:5-7](/bible/daniel/12#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/12#v5)</sup> Then I Daniel looked, and, behold, there stood other two, the one on this side of the bank of the river, and the other on that side of the bank of the river.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/daniel/12#v6)</sup> And one said to the man clothed in linen, which was upon the waters of the river, How long shall it be to the end of these wonders?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/daniel/12#v7)</sup> And I heard the man clothed in linen, which was upon the waters of the river, when he held up his right hand and his left hand unto heaven, and sware by him that liveth for ever that it shall be for a time, times, and an half; and when he shall have accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people, all these things shall be finished.

- Two others stand on the two banks of the river, and one asks the man clothed in linen how long it shall be to the end of these wonders.
- The man clothed in linen holds up both hands to heaven and swears by him that liveth forever that it shall be for a time, times, and a half, and when he has accomplished to scatter the power of the holy people, all these things shall be finished.

**[Daniel 12:8-10](/bible/daniel/12#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/daniel/12#v8)</sup> And I heard, but I understood not: then said I, O my Lord, what shall be the end of these things?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/12#v9)</sup> And he said, Go thy way, Daniel: for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/daniel/12#v10)</sup> Many shall be purified, and made white, and tried; but the wicked shall do wickedly: and none of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand.

- Daniel hears but understands not, and asks what shall be the end of these things, and he is told to go his way, for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end, which is now being opened.
- Many shall be purified and made white and tried, but the wicked shall do wickedly. Some people are going to repent, but the wicked will continue in their wickedness.
- None of the wicked shall understand, but the wise shall understand, and they shall know that it is mandatory to repent.

**[Daniel 12:11-13](/bible/daniel/12#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/12#v11)</sup> And from the time that the daily sacrifice shall be taken away, and the abomination that maketh desolate set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/12#v12)</sup> Blessed is he that waiteth, and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/12#v13)</sup> But go thou thy way till the end be: for thou shalt rest, and stand in thy lot at the end of the days.

- From the time that the daily sacrifice is taken away and the abomination that maketh desolate is set up, there shall be a thousand two hundred and ninety days.
- Blessed is he that waiteth and cometh to the thousand three hundred and five and thirty days.
- Daniel is told to go his way till the end, for he shall rest, meaning he shall die, and stand in his lot at the end of the days. This is the Most High sending these leaders back to us.
