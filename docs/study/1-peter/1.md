## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Peter 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 6-9, 15-16, 23-25, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Peter 1:1](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-peter/1#v1)</sup> Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia,

- Their original plan was to keep the commandments of God, and then they strayed right back into idolatry. “To the strangers scattered throughout”: remember the northern kingdom was scattered. They became no people and we had no dealing with them, but what did God call them?
- “Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia”. Galatia is where the book of Galatians was written to. Who are the strangers scattered? Verse 2 says it: the elect.
- “Peter, an apostle of Jesus Christ, to the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia”. Remember Isaiah 7 and Deuteronomy 32: not a people. These strangers are the same strangers of Ephraim and Manasseh who came to Judah when Judah was on point, the strangers who came up to Jerusalem in Chronicles.
- “To the strangers scattered throughout Pontus, Galatia, Cappadocia, Asia, and Bithynia”. They are called strangers here: the grafted in strangers, not a people, not my people, Lo-ammi, I am not your God. It is all the same, and it refers to the northern kingdom.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 2:7-10](/bible/1-peter/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/2#v7)</sup> Unto you therefore which believe he is precious: but unto them which be disobedient, the stone which the builders disallowed, the same is made the head of the corner,
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/2#v8)</sup> And a stone of stumbling, and a rock of offence, even to them which stumble at the word, being disobedient: whereunto also they were appointed.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/2#v9)</sup> But ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people; that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-peter/2#v10)</sup> Which in time past were not a people, but are now the people of God: which had not obtained mercy, but now have obtained mercy.

    “But unto them which be disobedient, the stone which the builders disallowed”: the disobedient were the southern kingdom, the Pharisees who rejected him; the builders are the disobedient who rejected Christ and went against that stone of stumbling of Isaiah 8. “The same is made the head of the corner”: that same Rock that made us became our elder, our leader, the high priest.

**[1 Peter 1:2](/bible/1-peter/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-peter/1#v2)</sup> Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father, through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ: Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied.

- “Elect”: they are still elect of God. “Unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ”: the New Covenant. “Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied”: the New Covenant provides grace under Christ, especially for those scattered who were not making sacrifices, going to the temple or coming back to Jerusalem three times a year for the feasts.
- “Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father”. Foreknowledge means the knowledge of God before: who is God’s elect from before? Israel. So the strangers in verse 1 are Israel. “Grace unto you, and peace, be multiplied.”
- “Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father”. The strangers of verse 1 are the elect. Who is that? Israel. The Jews and Gentiles of Romans 9 were called according to Hosea and according to Isaiah: these strangers are Israel. “Through sanctification of the Spirit, unto obedience and sprinkling of the blood of Jesus Christ”.
- “Elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father”. Peter calls the strangers scattered in verse 1 the elect. Who is that? Israelites.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 45:4](/bible/isaiah/45#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/45#v4)</sup> For Jacob my servant’s sake, and Israel mine elect, I have even called thee by thy name: I have surnamed thee, though thou hast not known me.

    Who is the elect, for the new people online? “For Jacob my servant’s sake, and Israel mine elect”. Get your head out of the white man’s ass: this Bible is not all-inclusive; it is exclusive, only for the Israelites. The elect are the Israelites, the saints are the Israelites, the all people are the Israelites, and the majority of the time, especially from Acts onward and in Galatians, the Gentiles and Greeks are the Israelites. We cannot include heathens in our constitution; absolutely unacceptable.

**[1 Peter 1:6](/bible/1-peter/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-peter/1#v6)</sup> Wherein ye greatly rejoice, though now for a season, if need be, ye are in heaviness through manifold temptations:

- “Wherein ye greatly rejoice, though now for a season, if need be, ye are in heaviness through manifold temptations”: sometimes we are sad and depressed through manifold temptations. There is a lot of trouble coming into our lives.

**[1 Peter 1:7](/bible/1-peter/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/1#v7)</sup> That the trial of your faith, being much more precious than of gold that perisheth, though it be tried with fire, might be found unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ:

- “That the trial of your faith”: Christ is letting us know through Peter that it is the trying of your faith, “being much more precious than of gold that perisheth, though it be tried with fire, might be found unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ”. There is a purpose behind the manifold temptations, the manifold struggles.
- “That the trial of your faith, being much more precious than of gold that perisheth, though it be tried with fire”: with your trials. Your trials purify you and prove you. They make sure you're right, make sure you're not fool's gold, make sure you're pure. There is no other way. It's not a cakewalk, and it's not supposed to be.
- The whole time we've been talking, it says the trial of your faith is valued more than gold, and not just more: much more. People die looking for gold, and what you are going to be is more than gold. “Might be found unto praise and honour and glory at the appearing of Jesus Christ”. That's what we are looking for: Christ coming and saying, job well done, my faithful servant, and sealing us worthy of a kingdom. That is what we fight day and night for. I know you're tired, we all get tired, but don't give up. Once you step out, who's to say you can step back in? Once you get off the offramp, who's to say you got enough gas to get back on? Don't play games with God. Don't roll the dice with God.
- If you want to hear God laugh, tell him your plan. Your plan is not God's plan. What God has planned for you is keep his commandments and live; everything else happens because of that. Nothing he put in your path is to destroy you. Everything adverse that happens to you is because you did it: your family is out of order because you're out of order, your wife is out of order because you're out of order, your kids are out of order because you and your wife are out of order. A backdoor marriage, and you are always going to have a problem, because you started out of order. You want your life to be easy? Keep God's commandments. There is no secret recipe to this thing.

**[1 Peter 1:8](/bible/1-peter/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/1#v8)</sup> Whom having not seen, ye love; in whom, though now ye see him not, yet believing, ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory:

- “Whom having not seen, ye love”: we haven't seen Christ, but we love him, “yet believing, ye rejoice with joy unspeakable and full of glory”.

**[1 Peter 1:9](/bible/1-peter/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-peter/1#v9)</sup> Receiving the end of your faith, even the salvation of your souls.

- “Receiving the end of your faith, even the salvation of your souls.” That is what we want; that is what we are waiting on.

**[1 Peter 1:15](/bible/1-peter/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-peter/1#v15)</sup> But as he which hath called you is holy, so be ye holy in all manner of conversation;

- We have to be set apart. “But as he which hath called you is holy”: the Most High who called us is holy, and he called us to be holy. Holy means separate. So if you say you are separate, when mama calls about Thanksgiving, you tell her you cannot celebrate it with her.
- Young men who live with their parents: be wise. Do not go back and forth with your parents about these wicked holidays, or they will throw you out. Something simple: No, Mom, I’m not going to be here, I’m going to be with the brothers, don’t wait for me. You do not have to give all the details. And when you are with the brothers, turn off your phone, because Thanksgiving and Christmas are when the family pressures you most, especially the young men just coming in. Unless you have another place to live, leave them alone.
- “So be ye holy in all manner of conversation.” The way you talk should change. It is not just putting on your fringes and reading the Bible; your conversation, at your job and everywhere, should change. Family should look at you and say, I see some change in that man, brother; sister, I see some change in you, what are you into? They should want to join you, not you join them. You are supposed to be that light, that example.

**[1 Peter 1:16](/bible/1-peter/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-peter/1#v16)</sup> Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy.

- “Because it is written, Be ye holy; for I am holy.” We are to be holy, separate, because God is holy.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 11:44](/bible/leviticus/11#v44)**
    > <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/11#v44)</sup> For I am the Lord your God: ye shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy: neither shall ye defile yourselves with any manner of creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.

    Here is where it is written: “For I am the Lord your God: ye shall therefore sanctify yourselves, and ye shall be holy; for I am holy.” Sanctify means to set apart, or to declare holy. It is the same thing: set apart.

**[1 Peter 1:23](/bible/1-peter/1#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-peter/1#v23)</sup> Being born again, not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God, which liveth and abideth for ever.

- “Being born again”: there are some things you did before coming into the truth, but now, being born again, it shouldn’t be so. Some think a lie is not a big deal, it’s just a white lie. I don’t know why you would even compare it to white. A lie is a lie, and Paul had to deal with it because it causes schisms, division in the body.
- “Not of corruptible seed, but of incorruptible, by the word of God”. The word we more commonly use for being born again is repenting. Before repenting we all were a corruptible seed; after repenting we are now incorruptible seed. And how did that happen? By the word of God: the word God loads us with daily, the benefit Christ taught in the temple daily, which causes us to die daily in order to follow him. The word of God is what makes that transition.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 2:1-3](/bible/1-peter/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-peter/2#v1)</sup> Wherefore laying aside all malice, and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil speakings,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-peter/2#v2)</sup> As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-peter/2#v3)</sup> If so be ye have tasted that the Lord is gracious.

    “Wherefore laying aside all malice, and all guile, and hypocrisies, and envies, and all evil speakings, As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word”. As newborn babes: in chapter one it was called being born again, and Peter touches on it again here. As newborn babes, as those who are born again, desire the sincere milk of the word, “that ye may grow thereby”.

**[1 Peter 1:24](/bible/1-peter/1#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-peter/1#v24)</sup> For all flesh is as grass, and all the glory of man as the flower of grass. The grass withereth, and the flower thereof falleth away:

- “For all flesh is as grass”. Our life is as simple as a blade of grass that can be plucked up and blown away in the wind. We can be leaving here, coming here, going to work, going about our day, and in an instant our life can be snatched up just like that.

**[1 Peter 1:25](/bible/1-peter/1#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-peter/1#v25)</sup> But the word of the Lord endureth for ever. And this is the word which by the gospel is preached unto you.

- “But the word of the Lord endureth for ever. And this is the word which by the gospel is preached unto you.” What endures forever is the word of the Lord, and it is preached to you, available to you, daily. The word of the Lord is there to be a benefit to you every single day. If you don’t use those benefits, shame on you.
