## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Peter 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7-8, 10-19, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Peter 4:7](/bible/1-peter/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/4#v7)</sup> But the end of all things is at hand: be ye therefore sober, and watch unto prayer.

- “But the end of all things is at hand.” Some do not believe it, but the Apostle Peter said it, and it is what even the white man on the news is now saying: America is done. It is not coming back. Trump cannot save it, and the talk of making America great again is propaganda. Great kingdoms last around 250 years, and this July is America’s 250th. We are in it. All we have to do is endure to the end; the kingdom is ours.
- “Be ye therefore sober”: be sober in your mind. “And watch unto prayer”: watch what is going on, and pray. If you are not paying attention, the prophecy is going to skip right over your head while you watch sports. Men, try to know a little bit about everything, the law, the constitution, politics, how government works, religion, so that when someone talks to you, you do not look lost. As a leader you do not want to look stupid.

**[1 Peter 4:8](/bible/1-peter/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/4#v8)</sup> And above all things have fervent charity among yourselves: for charity shall cover the multitude of sins.

- “And above all things have fervent charity among yourselves.” Above all the prophecy that is coming to pass, have fervent charity: that is the love of the brotherhood. No matter what, do not turn your back on the brotherhood. There are things coming that you cannot go through alone; that is the purpose of the brother and the sister sitting next to you. Forsake the love of the brotherhood and you are not going to make it.
- If the school was shut for two weeks and you felt nothing, you are kidding yourself; you are not part of the brotherhood. The Sabbath, being around the brotherhood, is the day you should look forward to, not your job, and not staying home under the woman and the children.

**[1 Peter 4:10](/bible/1-peter/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-peter/4#v10)</sup> As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another, as good stewards of the manifold grace of God.

- “As every man hath received the gift, even so minister the same one to another.” Shame on you if you come in here and sit on your hands, sit on the talent God has given you, and don’t put forth or show effort. If you have a craft or something to offer and you don’t come in here trying to show it and help, shame on you. You have been given that for a reason.
- “As good stewards of the manifold grace of God.” Your job is to utilize that gift and benefit your nation.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 18:16](/bible/proverbs/18#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/18#v16)</sup> A man’s gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.

    “A man’s gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.” When you have a talent, when you are able to do something for the nation, at least come in and say, I’m able to do this. It may be lined up for later, or it may be needed right now.
  - **[Matthew 25:24-28](/bible/matthew/25#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/25#v24)</sup> Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed:
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/matthew/25#v25)</sup> And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth: lo, there thou hast that is thine.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/25#v26)</sup> His lord answered and said unto him, Thou wicked and slothful servant, thou knewest that I reap where I sowed not, and gather where I have not strawed:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/25#v27)</sup> Thou oughtest therefore to have put my money to the exchangers, and then at my coming I should have received mine own with usury.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/25#v28)</sup> Take therefore the talent from him, and give it unto him which hath ten talents.

    The parable of the talents: the talent is money, the understanding God bestows on you. “Then he which had received the one talent came and said, Lord, I knew thee that thou art an hard man, reaping where thou hast not sown, and gathering where thou hast not strawed: And I was afraid, and went and hid thy talent in the earth.” He didn’t do nothing with the understanding, the wisdom, the gift the Lord bestowed on him. He hid it.

**[1 Peter 4:11](/bible/1-peter/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-peter/4#v11)</sup> If any man speak, let him speak as the oracles of God; if any man minister, let him do it as of the ability which God giveth: that God in all things may be glorified through Jesus Christ, to whom be praise and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

- “If any man speak, let him speak as the oracles of God.” If any man is to teach this Bible, he has to use the Bible. Countless times the so-called elders and leaders outside of here will sit on an interview or on YouTube two or three hours straight with no Bible, no scripture, no verses, no chapters, just talk, just ranting. That is not what the Bible says to do.
- You must speak the words in the Bible, not your own opinion or feelings or your own insults and ad hominems. You're a Caucasian, you're the devil; you're an Arab, you're a heathen: that's nice, but Bible. Whenever the Bishop, who is also a dynamic teacher, does an interview, he sits with a Bible in hand or in his lap and a reader on standby. The deacons were trained the same way by their elders: always a Bible in front of you or a reader with a Bible. We are not orphans here; we have elders that guide us.
- “If any man minister, let him do it as of the ability which God giveth: that God in all things may be glorified through Jesus Christ.” When you are just talking and the Bible is not being used, there is no glory to God in that. Anything outside of that is confusion and misguidance.
- “If any man speak, let him speak as the oracles of God.” Unlike the Christian church, Israel can talk about anything: war, slavery, marriage, health, sports, whatever it is. But whatever is talked about, it goes back to the scriptures. You must use the Bible to back up what you are saying and what you are producing.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 17:11](/bible/acts/17#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/acts/17#v11)</sup> These were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so.

    “These were more noble than those in Thessalonica, in that they received the word with all readiness of mind, and searched the scriptures daily, whether those things were so.” They received the word of the apostles with all readiness of mind, paying attention, and then searched the scriptures.
  - **[Isaiah 8:20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

    “To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.” If they are not speaking according to what is written in the Bible, there is no light in them.

**[1 Peter 4:12](/bible/1-peter/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-peter/4#v12)</sup> Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened unto you:

- We’re talking about complaining for your sins. “Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened unto you.” The fiery trial deals with a lot of things. Ultimately, write this down, it is talking about tribulation, but there are things that happen when you come into this truth to build your spirit up.
- It is to try you. You will be tried in this truth, and ultimately the trial will be tribulation. Some of us will lose our homes, our houses, our lives, our spouses; some of us will be incarcerated. So the point is, like we just read in Lamentations, what are you complaining for? You act like this is something strange, like it’s unheard of.
- “Beloved, think it not strange concerning the fiery trial which is to try you, as though some strange thing happened unto you”. Why am I going through this, why are bad things happening to me? Because God is trying to build you. He is trying to make you into a soldier, to build you mentally and spiritually, to teach you patience, experience and hope. That is what the spiritual man and woman have to see; don't look at it as something strange.
- Memorization and application are two separate things. A lot of brothers have good memories, but when it comes to applying, as soon as some trial or tribulation comes they are out the door. These are the weak men and weak women who can quote and find scriptures like firecrackers, the precepticons, but one little thing happens to them and they are gone. Most of them have not been through nothing; their life has been relatively smooth. The leadership sits back: he's all right with them precepts, but let's watch what he does when something bad happens to him. We are not wishing it, but according to the scriptures we know it is going to happen.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.

    “My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.” When you come in this truth, prepare your soul for temptation. Your life might have been good, but when you come in this truth the Lord is going to show you that what you thought was good, you just accepted as normal. It was never good.

**[1 Peter 4:13](/bible/1-peter/4#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-peter/4#v13)</sup> But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s sufferings; that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.

- “But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s sufferings.” The Bible says rejoice in your sufferings, because you are partakers of Christ’s sufferings. That’s the tribulation. “That, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy”: when Christ’s glory is revealed, we’re going to be just like him. That’s power.
- “But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s sufferings”: when you go through bad things, rejoice, because you are a partaker of Christ's sufferings, “that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.”
- “But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s sufferings”. Why is the government coming after my husband? Why is the government labeling us a hate group, when we don’t hate nobody? It is because we are partakers in Christ’s sufferings, “that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.” The only way you will be glad then is if you take part in that suffering now.
- “But rejoice, inasmuch as ye are partakers of Christ’s sufferings”. That’s hard, because the spirit and the flesh are battling each other, but the Bible says rejoice that you are partakers of the sufferings, “that, when his glory shall be revealed, ye may be glad also with exceeding joy.”

**[1 Peter 4:14](/bible/1-peter/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-peter/4#v14)</sup> If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye; for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you: on their part he is evil spoken of, but on your part he is glorified.

- Here’s the point right there. “If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye”: if you brothers and sisters are reproached for the name of Christ, you should be happy, “for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you.” Being reproached for the name of Christ proves the holy spirit is on you. The brothers that almost got ran over in Colorado: the spirit of Christ was upon them.
- “On their part he is evil spoken of”: on the world’s part Christ is evil spoken of, “but on your part he is glorified.”
- “If ye be reproached for the name of Christ, happy are ye”: if somebody is lying on you, slandering you, hating you because you are an Israelite, because the name of Christ is on you, happy are ye, “for the spirit of glory and of God resteth upon you”. When they attack us, as a whole or individually, we know it is the Holy Spirit on us. Oh, you said Christ, that's Greek; all right, Yahawashi; I still hate you.
- “On their part he is evil spoken of, but on your part he is glorified”: on our part we are glorifying the spirit of God, the spirit of the Lord.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Thessalonians 4:8](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v8)</sup> He therefore that despiseth, despiseth not man, but God, who hath also given unto us his holy Spirit.

    “He therefore that despiseth, despiseth not man, but God, who hath also given unto us his holy Spirit.” That is the mentality we all have to have when people are reproaching us for the name of Christ. It is not you that they hate; it is the spirit of God they see within you. That man and that woman have the Holy Spirit, and I can't stand it because I ain't got it. Oh, it's not hatred, it's correction: I wish you'd die. That's what they do.

**[1 Peter 4:15-16](/bible/1-peter/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-peter/4#v15)</sup> But let none of you suffer as a murderer, or as a thief, or as an evildoer, or as a busybody in other men’s matters.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-peter/4#v16)</sup> Yet if any man suffer as a Christian, let him not be ashamed; but let him glorify God on this behalf.

- “But let none of you suffer as a murderer.” Don’t let none of you brothers or sisters suffer as a murderer, and don’t say you’re being persecuted for Christ if you’re a murderer. We are supposed to suffer for Christ’s name, not for murder because you were trying to rob somebody. You murdering your people for a dollar fifteen, and now you in jail talking about I’m being persecuted for righteousness’ sake? That’s a lie. God’s law says thou shalt not kill.
- “Or as a thief, or as an evildoer.” The man in the first video was a thief and a murderer; the man in the second was a thief and an evildoer, and evildoer can cover a lot of things. He stood in front of the judge saying he was in a better place and not committing crimes now, and then he was over that bench. Sisters, these are questions a lot of you don’t ask men: some men are suffering a mental health crisis and are on meds or need to be on meds. Women keep finding the same guy attractive, knowing he was no good to the last woman, and have a baby by him anyway because he’s cute, he’s six-two, broad shoulders, she can change him. And it don’t matter how good-looking a woman is, she could be a thief too.
- “Or as a busybody in other men’s matters.” These are the gossip shows and busybody videos, the ones that run up millions of views in two days. Brothers, we might laugh at this stuff, but we cannot be caught up in it, which is gossip. We are the prophets of the Lord and ambassadors of Christ, and we must act like it.
- One lie can destroy a lot of people. A fifteen-year-old sister in 1987 said she had been raped by white men, police among them, and was found in a trash bag with racial slurs written on her body; it was almost a race riot, black people and black leaders stood behind her, her advisers took it to national prominence, and then a grand jury found it was a lie. The prosecutor she accused sued her and her advisers for defamation and won, and one of the lawyers lost his license to practice law. One lie destroyed a lot of people’s careers. That’s why, brothers, when I say don’t get involved in gossip, don’t do it: if somebody decides to sue for what you said, all hell is going to break loose, and they’re not going to sue Joe Schmo.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Timothy 2:4](/bible/2-timothy/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-timothy/2#v4)</sup> No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life; that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.

    “No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life.” Brothers, we are at war; this is war. So if we are at war we cannot entangle ourselves with the affairs of this life, and all that gossip and who is a power bottom is the affairs of this life. Some of it might be funny and amusing, but you men are the prophets of the Lord. We’ve got to leave that stuff alone. Don’t do videos on it, because you might suffer for it, and that is suffering as a busybody in other men’s matters, not for the name of Christ.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 28:13-18](/bible/sirach/28#v13)**

    Y’all see what one little lie can do: it can destroy. “Curse the whisperer and doubletongued: for such have destroyed many that were at peace.” “A backbiting tongue hath disquieted many, and driven them from nation to nation: strong cities hath it pulled down, and overthrown the houses of great men.” That is what a backbiting tongue, gossip, a lie, can do.
  - **[Luke 16:15](/bible/luke/16#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/luke/16#v15)</sup> And he said unto them, Ye are they which justify yourselves before men; but God knoweth your hearts: for that which is highly esteemed among men is abomination in the sight of God.

    Christ said unto the Pharisees, “Ye are they which justify yourselves before men; but God knoweth your hearts: for that which is highly esteemed among men is abomination in the sight of God.”

**[1 Peter 4:17-18](/bible/1-peter/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-peter/4#v17)</sup> For the time is come that judgment must begin at the house of God: and if it first begin at us, what shall the end be of them that obey not the gospel of God?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-peter/4#v18)</sup> And if the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?

- “For the time is come that judgment must begin at the house of God”. Judgment always starts with the house of God; it always starts with us. In ancient time God raised up Nebuchadnezzar and sent him to judge the nations, and he started with Israel, with Judah. He destroyed us, and then he poured out on Egypt and on all nations. That was God’s judgment.
- “And if it first begin at us, what shall the end be of them that obey not the gospel of God?” That is why this matters. Look at what is happening in the streets of America to blacks, Hispanics and North American Indians. The ICE raids are in the black and Hispanic communities, not in the European communities. Some ask why Trump is doing these things. A man’s goings are of the Lord: God put the spirit on Trump to do what he is doing, and the Bible has the answer why.
- It is not because the Lord just doesn’t like his people. Judgment starts at the house of God for two reasons: first, for us to turn back to our God and seek him; and second, to prove us. As you turn to him, bad things will still happen to you, to prove whether you believe and trust in him.
- “And if the righteous scarcely be saved, where shall the ungodly and the sinner appear?” If the righteous scarcely be saved, a lot of righteous brothers and sisters will die when that judgment comes. Don’t think, Oh, I’m righteous, nothing will happen to me. Judgment starts at the house of God, and righteous men will die also.

  Precepts:
  - **[Micah 2:10](/bible/micah/2#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/micah/2#v10)</sup> Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your rest: because it is polluted, it shall destroy you, even with a sore destruction.

    This is why God’s judgment starts at the house of God first. “Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your rest: because it is polluted, it shall destroy you”. God says this place is polluted, and this place will destroy us.
  - **[Job 36:15](/bible/job/36#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/job/36#v15)</sup> He delivereth the poor in his affliction, and openeth their ears in oppression.

    “He delivereth the poor in his affliction, and openeth their ears in oppression.” When our people are being oppressed, God opens their ears.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    “I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.” In our affliction, when we are being afflicted, we will seek God’s face.

**[1 Peter 4:19](/bible/1-peter/4#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-peter/4#v19)</sup> Wherefore let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing, as unto a faithful Creator.

- “Wherefore let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing”. It is the will of God for some of us to suffer and go through certain things. So you must be in well doing: keep the commandments, do what is right, and get rid of the adultery, the fornication and all these things.
- “Wherefore let them that suffer according to the will of God commit the keeping of their souls to him in well doing”: keep the commitment of your souls even through those fiery trials. Sometimes we slip out of the spirit; slide back in there real quick and catch yourself. Do it “as unto a faithful Creator.”
- He has been faithful unto us. Through history he has been faithful to the 12 tribes of the children of Israel, even when they cried unto him in the land of Egypt in bondage under Pharaoh, and he is faithful to this very day; that’s how you got where you are now.
- We always want to mimic and pattern ourselves after the other nations. Kendrick Lamar, dissing Dre, should have been dissing all the other nations when he said they not like us. They are not like us, because they don’t have the stipulations on them that we have. They don’t have a covenant with the Most High God; we are in covenant with the Most High God. We didn’t know that at one time, because they lied to us and didn’t want us to find it out, but too late.
