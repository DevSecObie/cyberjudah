## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-16, 18-20, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 14:1](/bible/revelation/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/14#v1)</sup> And I looked, and, lo, a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand, having his Father’s name written in their foreheads.

- Satan’s agenda is to cause you men to forget the mission and be distracted; we must not stop teaching and raising up our people because of foolishness, evil speech, gossip and slander. This is John the Revelator, exiled on the island of Patmos in a salt mine, seeing things. When you read the Book of Revelation, understand that the explanation of the visions and similitudes was not given to John; it is given to us now in these last days. “And, lo, a Lamb stood on the mount Sion”: the Lamb represents Christ. John the Baptist said, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world. “And with him an hundred forty and four thousand”: there is a doctrine going around that we don’t know definitively who the tribes of Israel are. “Having his Father’s name written in their foreheads”: why? Because they overcame their trials and tribulations.
- “And I looked, and, lo, a Lamb stood on the mount Sion, and with him an hundred forty and four thousand”: this continues from last week, when Revelation 7 was gone through. Mount Sion is Israel.
- “Having his Father’s name written in their foreheads”: every week an email comes in with a new name of God. Everybody is arguing with everybody, condemning you if you don’t say it the way their camp says it; two camps in Texas almost got into a fist fight over the name of the Most High. Israelites are turning into Bloods and Crips over a name. But the scripture says the Father’s name is written on them when they overcome: he is going to give us the pure language, that we may all call upon the Lord with one consent, when he allures us into the wilderness. So in IUIC we don’t bug out on the name. If a brother says Yahawah or Yahawashi or Yahweh, there is nothing wrong with that; don’t bug out on it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 12:10](/bible/hosea/12#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/12#v10)</sup> I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.

    This shows how the prophets received visions and how they explain things. “I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.”
  - **[Revelation 7:1-4](/bible/revelation/7#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/7#v1)</sup> And after these things I saw four angels standing on the four corners of the earth, holding the four winds of the earth, that the wind should not blow on the earth, nor on the sea, nor on any tree.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/7#v2)</sup> And I saw another angel ascending from the east, having the seal of the living God: and he cried with a loud voice to the four angels, to whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea,
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/7#v3)</sup> Saying, Hurt not the earth, neither the sea, nor the trees, till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/7#v4)</sup> And I heard the number of them which were sealed: and there were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel.

    “Holding the four winds of the earth”: the four winds represent the militaries of the world. When we step outside the wind is blowing, so this wind is not literal wind; it is representative of destruction. “Nor on the sea”: there are militaries on the sea, navies. “To whom it was given to hurt the earth and the sea”: that is the proof it is talking about destruction. “Till we have sealed the servants of our God in their foreheads”: we have to be sealed with the laws of God and the testimony. All Israelites say I’m sealed with the law until test time comes; then the Most High separates the men from the boys and the women from the girls. We shall through much tribulation enter into the kingdom. Nobody is sneaking into the kingdom; you got to earn this by righteousness. “There were sealed an hundred and forty and four thousand of all the tribes of the children of Israel”: these are the leaders.

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/14#v2)</sup> And I heard a voice from heaven, as the voice of many waters, and as the voice of a great thunder: and I heard the voice of harpers harping with their harps:

- “And I heard a voice from heaven, as the voice of many waters”: many waters means loud. “And I heard the voice of harpers harping with their harps”: don’t ask me who the harpers are; that is in heaven, above my pay grade.

**[Revelation 14:3](/bible/revelation/14#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/14#v3)</sup> And they sung as it were a new song before the throne, and before the four beasts, and the elders: and no man could learn that song but the hundred and forty and four thousand, which were redeemed from the earth.

- “And they sung as it were a new song before the throne”: we went over the song of the Lamb and the song of Moses. “And before the four beasts, and the elders”: don’t ask me who the four beasts or the elders in heaven are either. “And no man could learn that song but the hundred and forty and four thousand”: these are teachers; these are the ones that can learn the song and take you precept upon precept, line upon line, here a little and there a little. This is why we tell you men to study. “Which were redeemed from the earth.”
- “And they sung as it were a new song before the throne”: the elect sing the song of Moses and the song of the Lamb. It is none of these gospel songs you hear today, Kirk Franklin or James Cleveland; that is not the new song. It gives you goosebumps, but it ain’t the word of God; it just makes you feel good.
- “And no man could learn that song but the hundred and forty and four thousand”: the 144,000 will be teachers. They are the leaders, able to teach precept upon precept, line upon line, here a little and there a little, like nobody else. Only the 144 will be able to learn that song. “Which were redeemed from the earth.”

**[Revelation 14:4-5](/bible/revelation/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/14#v4)</sup> These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins. These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth. These were redeemed from among men, being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/14#v5)</sup> And in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God.

- “These are they which were not defiled with women”: it don’t mean what you are thinking. The Lord deals with women, everything in his proper order: he dealt with our foremothers Sarah and Rebekah, and with Huldah, who knew the scriptures very well, but they were not setting themselves up as leaders over the men. “For they are virgins”: it does not mean they never had sex; it means undefiled with philosophies and sin. “These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth”: whatever the scriptures say to do, the sheep, the elect, are going to do it, no excuses, no matter how hard. That’s why there must be tests: we all say we love the Lord, and your trial proves whether you love the Lord or not. “These were redeemed from among men”: the 144,000 of men. “Being the firstfruits unto God and to the Lamb.”
- “These are they which were not defiled with women; for they are virgins”: if you are under your woman’s skirt, you are not of the elect; God can’t use you. We are the virgins because all our sins have been wiped away clean; we are the female, the her of Hosea 2 that the Lord allures.
- “These are they which follow the Lamb whithersoever he goeth”: everybody says I follow the Lamb wherever he goes; you are a liar. Christ is not on earth walking with us, so how do we follow him? Whatever the Bible says, we are going to do. Wherever the Lamb takes us we will obey, even if it hurts, even if we love a brother or a sister so much: because the King said it, I must let you go. That is what the elect will say and apply.

No. It is saying the same thing Revelation 7:9 said: the gospel is preached to the Israelites scattered among every nation. When you read all nations, your mind goes to democracy, everybody; you have already omitted the prophecies and the histories of God, and gone to the white man. That is where your mind automatically goes, in Satan. This is why the Old Testament and the New Testament go hand in hand; once you separate the old from the new, you are lost in confusion.

- First understand the word gospel. Write this down: it means good news, good tidings. Tidings means news. Good news.

Look at all the Christian commentaries on Revelation: none of them will admit that the United States of America is written in the Bible as Babylon. When God raised up Egypt as the superpower they were mentioned, ancient Babylon was mentioned, Persia and Media were mentioned, the Greeks and the Romans were mentioned; but in these last days America, the mightiest nation on earth, is not mentioned? Babylon the great, that mighty city, is the United States of America.

She made all nations drink of the lies of the wrath of her spiritual fornication. What does America do to deceive all nations? She has political and economic policies set up to keep all nations in check.
