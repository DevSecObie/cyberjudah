## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 20 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 20:1-2](/bible/revelation/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/20#v1)</sup> And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/20#v2)</sup> And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and bound him a thousand years,

- “And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand”: this great chain that binds Satan is a twofold scripture. Not all scriptures have a twofold meaning; some do, some do not, some have more than two. The first understanding is the spiritual one: the spirits are bound captive for an allotted time. Then there is the literal one: the children of the devil on this earth are bound. “And he laid hold on the dragon, that old serpent, which is the Devil, and Satan, and bound him a thousand years”: the spiritual demons are bound, and the literal demons on this earth are bound for a thousand years.
- This bottomless pit is Europe. Gog and Magog are the areas of Russia and Scythia around the Caucasus mountains. They came from those mountains, and a lot of them are going right back there again. That is the bottomless pit.
- What we read was based upon the law, and Revelation 20 goes into it in detail. “And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand.”
- “And I saw an angel come down from heaven, having the key of the bottomless pit and a great chain in his hand.” This great chain represents captivity, slavery.

- This is when the Father comes. “And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away”: when the Father comes, nobody got nothing to say. When it says the earth and the heaven fled away, all creation stops and backs up. “And there was found no place for them.”
- Pay close attention. In verse 4 we reigned for a thousand years. After the thousand years, who is released and who comes up against us? The ones just read in Ezekiel 38 and 39. Now verse 11: “I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away.” The one on that throne is Christ, the one who destroys death (1 Corinthians 15).
- “And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away”: so when Christ comes on the scene in his power and majesty, nobody got nothing to say. Everything shuts down. “And there was found no place for them.”

**[Revelation 20:12-13](/bible/revelation/20#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)</sup> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/20#v13)</sup> And the sea gave up the dead which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.

- “I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened”. This is the rest of the dead, who lived not again until the thousand years were finished, and everybody else. “They were judged every man according to their works.” What are Christians talking about, just have the love of Jesus in your heart and you’re good? Everybody is judged according to their works.
- “And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God”: this is the final judgment, from the smallest that were born on earth to the greatest people ever known. “And the books were opened”: notice, books, plural. There will be several books. We will be judged on what we have studied and learned according to scripture, and that will be compared with the book on you: on this date you did this, here is what the scripture says about that. It says books because that is how we see things, but this is the spirit world; your life will be opened, and there will be no arguing.
- The difference between verse 6 and verse 12: those in verse 6, who make the first resurrection, are keeping the laws. Verse 12 is everybody on the planet, and they are not keeping the laws.
- “And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God”: this is everybody that ever existed, going to stand before the Father. “And the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life”. There’s books on you and me, there’s books on everybody: God’s laws, what you did and did not do. “And the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books”: see, plural, books, “according to their works.”

Every one of us has an angel who records what we do and reports to the Father. Esau calls it a guardian angel, and they got that from the Bible. That is rank, order, structure.

**[Revelation 20:14-15](/bible/revelation/20#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/20#v14)</sup> And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/20#v15)</sup> And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

- “And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.” So all is going to end. “And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.”
- “Death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death. And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.” Whose names are in the book of life?
- “And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire”: this is when death ends. “This is the second death.” Those that make the first resurrection will not have to go through verse 11 down; on such the second death hath no power. You are exempt from this final judgment. The second death is when everyone that ever lived is judged, except those that made the first resurrection, and after that judgment death and hell are finished.
- Is death ended right away? For the elect who make the first resurrection, yes. For the rest of the people born into the earth, there will still be death. As soon as Christ returns, the whole earth does not submit; the only ones that will submit are the elect. The nations must be subdued, and that takes time, just like when we had to take down the land of Canaan. Moses told Gad and Reuben they could have their inheritance first, but not to inhabit it till they helped their brothers take down theirs. It will be the same way again: ambassadors will be sent to nations with an ultimatum to submit to the King of kings, and men will be sent to take them down.
