## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 5:1](/bible/revelation/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/5#v1)</sup> And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals.

- Today’s class is sealing the 144,000. “And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals”: we went over this before. This is in the heavens; the Father has a book with seven seals on it, and no one can open the seals except Christ.
- This is John the Revelator on the island of Patmos, written around 96 AD; Patmos was a salt mine, a prison camp where Rome put some Israelites. “And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals.” So there was a book that the Lord, the Father, held in his hand. As you read down it tells you that no man was worthy to break the seals off the book to bring forth judgment and wisdom, but then the Lamb came, which was Christ, and he began to break the seals off this particular book, and this particular book brought forth judgment upon the earth and understanding upon the earth.
- “And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals.” There were seven seals on this book. Most of the seals have been done, so today we complete the seals, but that ain't the end of the series. Before Revelation 6, it is prefaced with the precept chapter, Zechariah 6.
