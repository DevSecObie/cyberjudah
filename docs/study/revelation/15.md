## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 15 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-8, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 15:1](/bible/revelation/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/15#v1)</sup> And I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvellous, seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God.

- “Seven angels having the seven last plagues; for in them is filled up the wrath of God”: as we discussed last week, these are seven big black terrible angels. Those of you who don’t fear the Most High read about the wrath of God and it’s like church to you; you don’t believe. When I read stuff about the wrath of God, that puts me on notice; it makes me focus on what is being said.
- “And I saw another sign in heaven, great and marvellous, seven angels having the seven last plagues”: destruction. “For in them is filled up the wrath of God”, and the wrath of God is destruction.

**[Revelation 15:2](/bible/revelation/15#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/15#v2)</sup> And I saw as it were a sea of glass mingled with fire: and them that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name, stand on the sea of glass, having the harps of God.

- “A sea of glass mingled with fire”: that’s like a lake of fire, like the Lord’s throne in Daniel 7. “And them that had gotten the victory over the beast, and over his image, and over his mark, and over the number of his name”: all that represents the world. In 1 John, John makes it simple and says the world; here in Revelation he goes into more detail. This world is run by a particular race that is called the beast, and this beast has an economic system set up, a monetary buying and trade system. We must get the victory over that. Their whole banking system was founded on the backs of slaves; that is why they call it old money. Before we can deal with Esau, understand that Esau already knows there is a large majority of blacks and Latinos under their spell. The only thing that can break the spell over us is the Bible: God’s laws are the spell breaker.
- “And I saw as it were a sea of glass mingled with fire”: this sea of glass mingled with fire is destruction.
- “And them that had gotten the victory over the beast”: that beast is the white man. The seven-headed beast is Greece, Rome, Spain, France, Germany, Russia and Great Britain, and out of the eighth, America came out of Britain. The Lord is saying we got the victory over them.
- “And over his image”: the image he portrays throughout the earth. The original of the bust they call Christ was found in a church that is now destroyed, and it is ‘an open secret’ that the son of Pope Alexander posed for it. What’s always funny is that phrase, an open secret. Our people didn’t know about it. So who was it open to? White folks. They always knew that that was not Jesus Christ. Its name is the word for savior; that’s the one your mothers and fathers pray to, that they do the sign of the cross to when they pass, and this is the image some of them will kill you for if you disrespect it. Leonardo da Vinci made sketches of that same man as Christ. It was an open secret, and now it’s open to us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Daniel 7:9](/bible/daniel/7#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/7#v9)</sup> I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne was like the fiery flame, and his wheels as burning fire.

    “I beheld till the thrones were cast down”: Daniel beheld all the kingdoms on earth being destroyed. Stuff like that makes me fear when I see what is happening with so-called President Trump, the UK and the various nations, and what is going on in Haiti; it lets me know the time is near. “The Ancient of days”: because he has no beginning or end of days. “Whose garment was white as snow”: a beautiful garment. “And the hair of his head like the pure wool”: meaning God is a so-called black man. Get mad if you want; that’s what it’s saying. “His throne was like the fiery flame”: fire all around; you’re not approaching to him.
  - **[1 John 5:4](/bible/1-john/5#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/5#v4)</sup> For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world: and this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.

    Regarding the victory. In church there was a song we used to sing, Victory is mine, and we were wicked as hell singing that song; we didn’t get no victory over nothing. “For whatsoever is born of God overcometh the world”: to be born of God means to be born again. Write that down. “And this is the victory that overcometh the world, even our faith.” Notice in 1 John he says victory over the world, and Revelation 15:2 is talking about the same thing. The world at that time was Rome; during these last days it is Babylon the great with all its intricate systems.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 3:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)</sup> I said in mine heart concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts.

    Before we get to the beast, men are called beasts. “That they might see that they themselves are beasts.” This is why a lot of people leave the Book of Revelation alone: it is written as a metaphor. You think the beast is some dangerous monster; no, it’s man.

**[Revelation 15:3](/bible/revelation/15#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/15#v3)</sup> And they sing the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb, saying, Great and marvellous are thy works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are thy ways, thou King of saints.

- “And they sing the song of Moses the servant of God”: what do most churches say? That the Old Testament is done away with. Then why are the saints, the 144,000 and the great multitude, singing the song of Moses? Because it plays a pivotal part historically. So now we understand what the song of Moses is about: the Lord delivers us out of Egypt, we became fat under Solomon, we rejected the Lord of heaven and earth, we went into slavery and captivity, death and destruction, and then at the end the Lord brings judgment on the nations for what they did to us. That’s it in a nutshell. “And the song of the Lamb”: Christ dying on the cross for us, that messenger who did not judge based upon his sight or his ears; he magnified the law and made it honourable. Both songs are about us being redeemed and put back on top as the top nation.
- “And they sing the song of Moses the servant of God, and the song of the Lamb”. This is what we wanted to get to. What are they going to say? “Saying, Great and marvellous are thy works, Lord God Almighty; just and true are thy ways, thou King of saints.”
- So what is the song of Moses? Revelation 15 only gives a brief description, a glory hallelujah of a song. The detail of the song is in Deuteronomy 31.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 31:19-22](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v19)</sup> Now therefore write ye this song for you, and teach it the children of Israel: put it in their mouths, that this song may be a witness for me against the children of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v20)</sup> For when I shall have brought them into the land which I sware unto their fathers, that floweth with milk and honey; and they shall have eaten and filled themselves, and waxen fat; then will they turn unto other gods, and serve them, and provoke me, and break my covenant.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v21)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when many evils and troubles are befallen them, that this song shall testify against them as a witness; for it shall not be forgotten out of the mouths of their seed: for I know their imagination which they go about, even now, before I have brought them into the land which I sware.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v22)</sup> Moses therefore wrote this song the same day, and taught it the children of Israel.

    “Write ye this song for you, and teach it the children of Israel”: we are the children of Israel; we got to learn this song. “That this song may be a witness for me against the children of Israel.” “That floweth with milk and honey”: to have milk you need cows, to feed the cows you need grass, and honey comes from bees that get it from flowers. This is talking about a rich vegetative land; that’s what the promised land was and will be again. “And waxen fat”: it does not mean we became fat slobs; it means rich. When the Israelites get rich they are going to turn against him. “For it shall not be forgotten out of the mouths of their seed”: he doesn’t want the children of the Israelites to forget this song.
  - **[Deuteronomy 31:27-30](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v27)</sup> For I know thy rebellion, and thy stiff neck: behold, while I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against the Lord; and how much more after my death?
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v28)</sup> Gather unto me all the elders of your tribes, and your officers, that I may speak these words in their ears, and call heaven and earth to record against them.
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v29)</sup> For I know that after my death ye will utterly corrupt yourselves, and turn aside from the way which I have commanded you; and evil will befall you in the latter days; because ye will do evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger through the work of your hands.
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/deuteronomy/31#v30)</sup> And Moses spake in the ears of all the congregation of Israel the words of this song, until they were ended.

    “While I am yet alive with you this day, ye have been rebellious against the Lord; and how much more after my death?” “And evil will befall you in the latter days”: notice Moses is talking about even the latter days.
  - **[2 Esdras 14:3-5](/bible/2-esdras/14#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-esdras/14#v3)</sup> Then said he unto me, In the bush I did manifestly reveal myself unto Moses, and talked with him, when my people served in Egypt:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-esdras/14#v4)</sup> And I sent him, and led my people out of Egypt, and brought him up to the mount of Sinai, where I held him by me a long season,
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/14#v5)</sup> And told him many wondrous things, and shewed him the secrets of the times, and the end; and commanded him, saying,

    Ezra tells us what Moses saw, in brief. “Where I held him by me a long season”: that long season was 40 days and 40 nights. “And shewed him the secrets of the times, and the end”: Christians say God gave Moses Ten Commandments in 40 days and 40 nights; does it take 40 days to give Ten Commandments? Moses saw from Genesis all the way to the second coming of Christ. That’s why when God told Moses he was not getting into the land, Moses didn’t bug out: he saw his resurrection; he saw it all. So Moses is going to tell us everything right now in this song.

**[Revelation 15:4](/bible/revelation/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/15#v4)</sup> Who shall not fear thee, O Lord, and glorify thy name? for thou only art holy: for all nations shall come and worship before thee; for thy judgments are made manifest.

- “For all nations shall come and worship before thee”: that goes back to Zechariah 14:16; the nation that will not serve the King shall die. Not only are we going to be the top nation, we got a job to do: to force the nations to worship the one true God, because they are not going to do it willingly. “For thy judgments are made manifest”: this is what the world doesn’t want; they don’t want God’s judgments to be made manifest. We do, because God is a God of justice, and the black man has never seen justice in the earth.

    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/60#v11)</sup> Therefore thy gates shall be open continually; they shall not be shut day nor night; that men may bring unto thee the forces of the Gentiles, and that their kings may be brought.

Yes, because there is such a thing as regeneration; you are your fathers come back. “Thou shalt also suck the milk of the Gentiles”: everything the Gentiles got belongs to us. “For brass I will bring gold, and for iron I will bring silver.” “I will also make thy officers peace, and thine exactors righteousness”: thine exactors, your tax collectors. When our kingdom is set up the nations are going to bring taxes to us; as long as we got to pay tax to another race we ain’t free.
  - **[Titus 1:5](/bible/titus/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/titus/1#v5)</sup> For this cause left I thee in Crete, that thou shouldest set in order the things that are wanting, and ordain elders in every city, as I had appointed thee:

    Somebody said there is not supposed to be no rank in Israel. “And ordain elders in every city”: there must be rank; somebody has to play the role of elders. It is always leadership; people never just do whatever they want. What Bible are they reading?
  - **[Revelation 16:9](/bible/revelation/16#v9)**

    Some of y’all think when Christ comes back everybody is going to be happy and praise him; that’s a lie. “And they repented not to give him glory”: the nations are not going to submit.

**[Revelation 15:5-6](/bible/revelation/15#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/15#v5)</sup> And after that I looked, and, behold, the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/15#v6)</sup> And the seven angels came out of the temple, having the seven plagues, clothed in pure and white linen, and having their breasts girded with golden girdles.

- “The temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened”: the tabernacle of the Lord was opened. “And the seven angels came out of the temple, having the seven plagues, clothed in pure and white linen, and having their breasts girded with golden girdles.” These are the seven archangels, and they came out with seven plagues.
- “And after that I looked, and, behold, the temple of the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven was opened”: John the Revelator, on the isle of Patmos, sees visions and writes down what he is seeing. He sees the temple and the tabernacle of the testimony in heaven, so what he witnesses is something occurring in heaven.
- “And the seven angels came out of the temple”: these are not seven blond-haired, blue-eyed little boys. The angels of God are terrible. Imagine a terrible-looking black man with big black arms and fists and feet: you are not going to hug him.
- “Clothed in pure and white linen”: the media has already filled minds with a toga, a sheet. No: the garments of the Lord are awesome, terrible and beautiful too; Wisdom of Solomon calls God the author of beauty. Think of a bad garment, all white, made of linen: that is how the angels are dressed. “And having their breasts girded with golden girdles”: gold from the waist up to the chest.

Read through Paul’s letters, there is a deeper understanding: what is going to be established on earth is a complete replica of exactly what is going on in the heavens. It is a prayer, but looked at spiritually there is more in that thing.

You better believe it.

The temple, the sanctuary, the ark of the covenant, what Moses set up and what King Solomon built, are a shadow of the heavenly. “As Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.”

**[Revelation 15:7](/bible/revelation/15#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/revelation/15#v7)</sup> And one of the four beasts gave unto the seven angels seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who liveth for ever and ever.

- “And one of the four beasts gave unto the seven angels seven golden vials full of the wrath of God”: the four beasts are the four archangels. “Who liveth for ever and ever”: this is the Ancient of Days you read about in Daniel 7.
- “And one of the four beasts gave unto the seven angels seven golden vials full of the wrath of God”: the four beasts are the terrible archangels, more terrible than these seven.
- They were given “seven golden vials full of the wrath of God, who liveth for ever and ever.” Those plagues are the wrath of God.

**[Revelation 15:8](/bible/revelation/15#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/15#v8)</sup> And the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God, and from his power; and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled.

- “And the temple was filled with smoke from the glory of God, and from his power; and no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled.” There is a temple in heaven, and Christ gave us a secret about it in the prayer: what is set up on earth is patterned after what is in heaven.
- “Till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled”: these seven plagues are the judgments of war coming upon the earth, which lead up to the great river Euphrates being dried up, World War III commencing and Christ’s return.
- “And no man was able to enter into the temple, till the seven plagues of the seven angels were fulfilled.” The seven plagues, the wrath of God, have to be fulfilled. The Lord said, I will smite Egypt with plagues as before: these seven plagues are the plagues the Lord is bringing on America. And Revelation 18 says, come out of her, my people, that ye receive not of her plagues, and in one hour her plagues come: death, destruction, famine.
