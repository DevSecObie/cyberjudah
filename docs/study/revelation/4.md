## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-11, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 4:1](/bible/revelation/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/4#v1)</sup> After this I looked, and, behold, a door was opened in heaven: and the first voice which I heard was as it were of a trumpet talking with me; which said, Come up hither, and I will shew thee things which must be hereafter.

- Revelation 4 is a vision of the kingdom. Some of what John witnesses in heaven is beyond what we can explain here. This is John the Apostle, the disciple who was always on Christ's bosom, one of the chief three apostles, not John the Baptist. He was banished to Patmos and recorded this around AD 96, years after Jerusalem's destruction.
- The door opens in heaven and the voice sounds like a trumpet, calling him up to see what must be hereafter. John was not the only apostle given revelations. Paul also saw visions, although the Corinthians disrespected him as a little man with a big mouth. Paul was not behind the chiefest apostles in what the Lord had shown him.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 11:4-5, 10-12](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v4)</sup> For if he that cometh preacheth another Jesus, whom we have not preached, or if ye receive another spirit, which ye have not received, or another gospel, which ye have not accepted, ye might well bear with him.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v5)</sup> For I suppose I was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v10)</sup> As the truth of Christ is in me, no man shall stop me of this boasting in the regions of Achaia.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v11)</sup> Wherefore? because I love you not? God knoweth.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v12)</sup> But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off occasion from them which desire occasion; that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.

    Paul was going through the regions of Achaia teaching the gospel, and no man was going to stop that boasting. The Corinthians were disrespecting him, so he had to remind them that he was not a whit behind the very chiefest apostles.
  - **[2 Corinthians 12:1-5](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v1)</sup> It is not expedient for me doubtless to glory. I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v2)</sup> I knew a man in Christ above fourteen years ago, (whether in the body, I cannot tell; or whether out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;) such an one caught up to the third heaven.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v3)</sup> And I knew such a man, (whether in the body, or out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;)
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v4)</sup> How that he was caught up into paradise, and heard unspeakable words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v5)</sup> Of such an one will I glory: yet of myself I will not glory, but in mine infirmities.

    Paul says it is not expedient to glory in himself, then comes to visions and revelations of the Lord. The man in Christ above fourteen years ago is Paul himself, speaking of the spiritual man without boasting in his flesh. He cannot tell whether he was in the body or out of it; God knows.

**[Revelation 4:2](/bible/revelation/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/4#v2)</sup> And immediately I was in the spirit: and, behold, a throne was set in heaven, and one sat on the throne.

- John is immediately in the spirit and sees a throne set in heaven with one sitting on it. The one on the throne is the Father, the Most High. Daniel also saw him as the Ancient of days. He has a body, a garment and hair; he is not a puff of smoke floating in the atmosphere.

  Precepts:
  - **[Daniel 7:9](/bible/daniel/7#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/daniel/7#v9)</sup> I beheld till the thrones were cast down, and the Ancient of days did sit, whose garment was white as snow, and the hair of his head like the pure wool: his throne was like the fiery flame, and his wheels as burning fire.

    Daniel beheld the thrones cast down: the kingdoms of the earth destroyed. The Ancient of days sat. He is called Ancient of days because he existed before days, before the light and before the evening and morning of the first day.

**[Revelation 4:3](/bible/revelation/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/4#v3)</sup> And he that sat was to look upon like a jasper and a sardine stone: and there was a rainbow round about the throne, in sight like unto an emerald.

- He appears like jasper and a sardine stone, sardius. Those stones have a similar colour; the emerald is greenish. Looking at the stones helps us understand what John describes, just as the beryl stone helps with the description of Christ's body elsewhere.
- There is a rainbow around the throne, in sight like an emerald. People say there is no colour in the kingdom of heaven, but a rainbow has colour. This rainbow is not the LGBT community. It was already in heaven before God established it in the earth as the sign of his covenant. Many things in heaven are established on earth at different times.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 9:13-16](/bible/genesis/9#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/9#v13)</sup> I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/9#v14)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when I bring a cloud over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/9#v15)</sup> And I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/9#v16)</sup> And the bow shall be in the cloud; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is upon the earth.

    God sets his bow in the cloud as the token of a covenant between himself and the earth. When clouds gather, the bow appears and he remembers his covenant with every living creature: the waters will not again become a flood destroying all flesh.

**[Revelation 4:4](/bible/revelation/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/4#v4)</sup> And round about the throne were four and twenty seats: and upon the seats I saw four and twenty elders sitting, clothed in white raiment; and they had on their heads crowns of gold.

- The twenty-four elders sit around the throne clothed in white raiment with crowns of gold. They are elders over every hour of the day, twenty-four hours. The fuller explanation of those elders belongs to another lesson.

**[Revelation 4:5](/bible/revelation/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/4#v5)</sup> And out of the throne proceeded lightnings and thunderings and voices: and there were seven lamps of fire burning before the throne, which are the seven Spirits of God.

- Lightnings, thunderings and voices proceed from the throne. Seven lamps of fire burn before it, the seven Spirits of God. This is the seven-branched menorah in heaven. The angels are burning lamps of fire before the Lord in that shape.
- The menorah Moses established on earth came from the heavenly pattern. It was not a little ornament sitting on a table. It stood large, made of pure gold, and was to remain lit continually. The Roman relief and the painting of Jerusalem's fall show several men carrying it away. Rome stole it in AD 70. The kingdom coming and God's will being done in earth as in heaven has levels: the rainbow and the menorah are examples of heavenly things established here.
- The seven lamps burn before the throne and are the seven Spirits of God, seven angels burning with fire. Even a glimpse of that heavenly sight is beyond what our minds are used to. The prophets describe being overwhelmed when they see such things.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 25:31-32, 40](/bible/exodus/25#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/25#v31)</sup> And thou shalt make a candlestick of pure gold: of beaten work shall the candlestick be made: his shaft, and his branches, his bowls, his knops, and his flowers, shall be of the same.
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/25#v32)</sup> And six branches shall come out of the sides of it; three branches of the candlestick out of the one side, and three branches of the candlestick out of the other side:
    >
    > <sup>[40](/bible/exodus/25#v40)</sup> And look that thou make them after their pattern, which was shewed thee in the mount.

    The candlestick is the menorah, made of pure gold and beaten work, with its shaft, branches, bowls, knops and flowers of the same material. The gold was beautifully decorated. Six branches came out of the central shaft, three on either side. That makes seven with the centre, not eight as Amalek has it.
  - **[Hebrews 8:5](/bible/hebrews/8#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/8#v5)</sup> Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.

    “Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things”. Moses was admonished when he was about to make the tabernacle to make all things according to the pattern shown in the mount. The tabernacle came before the temple. Its furnishings were the example and shadow of things in heaven, explaining why John's vision contains what God commanded Moses to make on earth.

**[Revelation 4:6](/bible/revelation/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/4#v6)</sup> And before the throne there was a sea of glass like unto crystal: and in the midst of the throne, and round about the throne, were four beasts full of eyes before and behind.

- The sea of glass like crystal corresponds to the water in the laver and the molten sea established for the service of God. The water surrounding that great vessel gives the earthly example of what is before the heavenly throne.
- The four beasts in the midst of and around the throne are four archangels, male angels, cherubs. They are full of eyes before and behind. Imagine actually seeing those eyes everywhere; it would be a terrifying sight, not the little picture people ordinarily have of an angel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 30:18-21](/bible/exodus/30#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/30#v18)</sup> Thou shalt also make a laver of brass, and his foot also of brass, to wash withal: and thou shalt put it between the tabernacle of the congregation and the altar, and thou shalt put water therein.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/30#v19)</sup> For Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet thereat:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/30#v20)</sup> When they go into the tabernacle of the congregation, they shall wash with water, that they die not; or when they come near to the altar to minister, to burn offering made by fire unto the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/exodus/30#v21)</sup> So they shall wash their hands and their feet, that they die not: and it shall be a statute for ever to them, even to him and to his seed throughout their generations.

    A laver of brass, with a brass foot, is set between the tabernacle of the congregation and the altar and filled with water. Aaron and his sons wash their hands and feet before entering or approaching the altar to minister. “they shall wash with water, that they die not”. Washing for this service is a statute through their generations.
  - **[1 Kings 7:23-26](/bible/1-kings/7#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/1-kings/7#v23)</sup> And he made a molten sea, ten cubits from the one brim to the other: it was round all about, and his height was five cubits: and a line of thirty cubits did compass it round about.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/1-kings/7#v24)</sup> And under the brim of it round about there were knops compassing it, ten in a cubit, compassing the sea round about: the knops were cast in two rows, when it was cast.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/1-kings/7#v25)</sup> It stood upon twelve oxen, three looking toward the north, and three looking toward the west, and three looking toward the south, and three looking toward the east: and the sea was set above upon them, and all their hinder parts were inward.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/1-kings/7#v26)</sup> And it was an hand breadth thick, and the brim thereof was wrought like the brim of a cup, with flowers of lilies: it contained two thousand baths.

    The molten sea is round, ten cubits across, five cubits high, about ten feet, with a line of thirty cubits around it. Knops encircle it in two cast rows. It stands on twelve oxen, three facing each direction, north, west, south and east, with their hinder parts inward.

**[Revelation 4:7-8](/bible/revelation/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/revelation/4#v7)</sup> And the first beast was like a lion, and the second beast like a calf, and the third beast had a face as a man, and the fourth beast was like a flying eagle.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/4#v8)</sup> And the four beasts had each of them six wings about him; and they were full of eyes within: and they rest not day and night, saying, Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty, which was, and is, and is to come.

- One beast is like a lion, another a calf, another has a man's face and another is like a flying eagle. Ezekiel saw the same four beasts and described their four faces together. John describes the face dominating the front of each one: lion, ox, man or eagle. The differing descriptions concern how those faces are presented, not different kinds of creatures.
- Each has six wings, full of eyes within. The illustration shows the eyes, the hands beneath, the calf-like feet and the wheel within a wheel, helping us picture how strange the sight is. They do not rest day or night but continually proclaim God's holiness, the Almighty who was, is and is to come. These are not the only creatures God has made that we have never seen; even the depths of the sea contain unfamiliar creatures.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 1:10-11](/bible/ezekiel/1#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/1#v10)</sup> As for the likeness of their faces, they four had the face of a man, and the face of a lion, on the right side: and they four had the face of an ox on the left side; they four also had the face of an eagle.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/1#v11)</sup> Thus were their faces: and their wings were stretched upward; two wings of every one were joined one to another, and two covered their bodies.

    All four have the face of a man, a lion on the right, an ox on the left and an eagle. Their wings stretch upward, two joined to one another and two covering their bodies. Ezekiel is describing the same beasts John sees.

**[Revelation 4:9](/bible/revelation/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/4#v9)</sup> And when those beasts give glory and honour and thanks to him that sat on the throne, who liveth for ever and ever,

- The beasts give glory, honour and thanks to the one on the throne, who lives for ever. Think of their continual singing. Judah can sing, though that spirit was on Levi in the scriptures, but these creatures are beyond even our best singers. Imagine multiple vocal cords in one beast, able to sound every note and octaves we have never heard, in perfect harmony. God does not have bad singers praising him. Do not compare that heavenly ability to somebody merely believing she can sing along with Whitney Houston.

**[Revelation 4:10](/bible/revelation/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/4#v10)</sup> The four and twenty elders fall down before him that sat on the throne, and worship him that liveth for ever and ever, and cast their crowns before the throne, saying,

- The twenty-four elders fall down, worship the one who lives for ever and cast their crowns before the throne. Their authority does not remove the need to give him honour and thanks.

**[Revelation 4:11](/bible/revelation/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/4#v11)</sup> Thou art worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honour and power: for thou hast created all things, and for thy pleasure they are and were created.

- The Lord is worthy of glory, honour and power because he created all things for his pleasure. The next part of the vision concerns the scroll sealed with seven seals. A seal belongs to one in authority; a sealed writing cannot simply be opened by anyone. No man in heaven, earth or the grave could open that scroll. Christ alone is worthy to break its seals and reveal it.
- The first seal reveals conquest of nations under the guise of righteousness. The second identifies the red, warlike race that conquers and makes war worldwide with the great sword. The third reveals that same race controlling the world's economy. The fourth shows death, hell, pestilence and disease following its conquests.
- The fifth reveals the souls of righteous Israelites asking vengeance against that same race for what it has done. The sixth ushers in World War III, with the kings of the earth hiding at the Lord's return. The seventh brings the destruction of Babylon the great, the United States of America. These are the things Christ makes known by opening the seals. The fourth seal's pestilence also raises the question of why people are so afraid of the coronavirus.
- The Father created all things, beginning with Christ, for his pleasure. We are in the greatest movie of all time, for lack of a better word. A movie needs a script, actors and actresses, good characters and bad characters. The Bible is the greatest story, and Israel is its main people.
