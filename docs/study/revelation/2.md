## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-29, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 2:1](/bible/revelation/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/2#v1)</sup> Unto the angel of the church of Ephesus write; These things saith he that holdeth the seven stars in his right hand, who walketh in the midst of the seven golden candlesticks;

- “Unto the angel of the church of Ephesus write”. The angel is the leader of the church, a man entrusted with the congregation. This is not a letter addressed to Michael, Gabriel or another heavenly angel. Christ holds the seven stars and walks among the seven golden candlesticks: the stars are the leaders and the candlesticks are the seven churches, like the seven-branched menorah.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 29:9](/bible/1-samuel/29#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/29#v9)</sup> And Achish answered and said to David, I know that thou art good in my sight, as an angel of God: notwithstanding the princes of the Philistines have said, He shall not go up with us to the battle.

    “thou art good in my sight, as an angel of God”. Achish is speaking to David, a man. Angel can mean a human messenger. That is how the angel addressed in Ephesus is the man leading that church, not one of the heavenly angels.

**[Revelation 2:2](/bible/revelation/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/2#v2)</sup> I know thy works, and thy labour, and thy patience, and how thou canst not bear them which are evil: and thou hast tried them which say they are apostles, and are not, and hast found them liars:

- Christ knows their works, labour and patience. They cannot bear them which are evil, and they have tried them which say they are apostles and are not, and found them liars. In the church at Ephesus men were trying to set themselves up as apostles: phonies, more than likely younger men who came in and desired to sit in a seat of authority with no experience, understanding or knowledge to sit in that seat.
- It is the old expression: don’t compare your chapter 2 to our chapter 30. I’m on chapter 2; well, we’re on chapter 30. Shut the hell up, sit down and just listen.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 11:12-16](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v12)</sup> But what I do, that I will do, that I may cut off occasion from them which desire occasion; that wherein they glory, they may be found even as we.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v13)</sup> For such are false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v14)</sup> And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v15)</sup> Therefore it is no great thing if his ministers also be transformed as the ministers of righteousness; whose end shall be according to their works.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v16)</sup> I say again, Let no man think me a fool; if otherwise, yet as a fool receive me, that I may boast myself a little.

    Paul cuts off the occasion sought by men who want to glory as though they have the same standing as the apostles. “For such are false apostles, deceitful workers, transforming themselves into the apostles of Christ.” They were pretending to be men Christ had taught, pretending to have walked with him. The false apostles were inside the church, not merely enemies outside it.

**[Revelation 2:3](/bible/revelation/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/2#v3)</sup> And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.

- “And hast borne, and hast patience, and for my name’s sake hast laboured, and hast not fainted.” They carried this truth, endured and kept working for Christ without giving up. These are good works, and Christ acknowledges them before correcting what is wrong.

**[Revelation 2:4](/bible/revelation/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/2#v4)</sup> Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.

- “because thou hast left thy first love”. The first love is Christ. A congregation can have patience, labour and the ability to expose false teachers and still need this correction. Loving Christ cannot be separated from loving the brothers he has put before you. God first showed his love by sending his Son; that obligates us to love one another.
- “Nevertheless I have somewhat against thee, because thou hast left thy first love.” We have left our first love: keeping the law and commandments and the faith of Christ, glorifying the Lord in our bodies, doing the work day in and day out.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 4:9-11, 19-20](/bible/1-john/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-john/4#v9)</sup> In this was manifested the love of God toward us, because that God sent his only begotten Son into the world, that we might live through him.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-john/4#v10)</sup> Herein is love, not that we loved God, but that he loved us, and sent his Son to be the propitiation for our sins.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-john/4#v11)</sup> Beloved, if God so loved us, we ought also to love one another.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/1-john/4#v19)</sup> We love him, because he first loved us.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/1-john/4#v20)</sup> If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar: for he that loveth not his brother whom he hath seen, how can he love God whom he hath not seen?

    God manifested his love by sending his only begotten Son so that we might live through him. Christ is the first creation, the Alpha and Omega, and the propitiation, the mediator for our sins. We did not love God first; he loved us and sent his Son. If God so loved us, we ought to love one another.

**[Revelation 2:5](/bible/revelation/2#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/2#v5)</sup> Remember therefore from whence thou art fallen, and repent, and do the first works; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will remove thy candlestick out of his place, except thou repent.

- Remember where you fell, repent and do the first works. Go back to the zeal you had when you first heard the truth, when you wanted to study and do the work. Examine yourself instead of making everybody else prove what is wrong with you. Your works speak. Many a time we stand up and say, this is a good brother, and that brother turns around and does something dumb as hell, like giving out answers on a test. Not that brother, we love that brother; then lo and behold he did it, and now we have egg on our face.
- Worldly cares, money and lust can choke the word. Brothers in Austin let money problems overthrow them and would not repent. Being removed and then saying that you are still in the truth, without correcting the sin, is not repentance. Get back to your first works; if opening the Bible now only makes you fall asleep, recognize that change.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 13:5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)</sup> Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?

    “Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves.” Find where you went wrong and correct it. Do not make this everybody else's responsibility. Christ is in you unless you are reprobates, void of judgment. Look at your works and examine what only you and the Lord know, not just the appearance you have before the brothers.

**[Revelation 2:6](/bible/revelation/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/2#v6)</sup> But this thou hast, that thou hatest the deeds of the Nicolaitans, which I also hate.

- The Nicolaitans were Israelites worshipping the Greek god Nike, which meant god of victory. Christ hates those deeds. He does not accept every form of worship simply because an Israelite practices it. Ephesus was right to hate what he hates.

**[Revelation 2:7](/bible/revelation/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/revelation/2#v7)</sup> He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the tree of life, which is in the midst of the paradise of God.

- Hear what the Spirit says to the churches. The promise to the one who overcomes is to eat of the tree of life in the paradise of God. Eating is learning the doctrine of life, the knowledge of God's commandments. The fruit is immortality, living and ruling for ever. Do not reduce the tree to Eve eating an apple; understand what that eating represents.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 19:19](/bible/sirach/19#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/19#v19)</sup> The knowledge of the commandments of the Lord is the doctrine of life: and they that do things that please him shall receive the fruit of the tree of immortality.

    “The knowledge of the commandments of the Lord is the doctrine of life: and they that do things that please him shall receive the fruit of the tree of immortality.”. Knowledge of the commandments is the doctrine of life. The people who do what pleases God receive the fruit of that tree, immortality. Eating the tree of life means receiving and doing this doctrine, not eating a literal apple.

**[Revelation 2:8](/bible/revelation/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/2#v8)</sup> And unto the angel of the church in Smyrna write; These things saith the first and the last, which was dead, and is alive;

- The angel of Smyrna is its leader, just as the angel of Ephesus is a man. The first and the last, who was dead and is alive, is Christ. Smyrna, Ephesus and Pergamos competed for the standing of chief city in Asia Minor. Smyrna became a bishopric, a church under a bishop, growing from the Jewish colony there. Keep that Israelite history in mind.

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 4:14](/bible/galatians/4#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/galatians/4#v14)</sup> And my temptation which was in my flesh ye despised not, nor rejected; but received me as an angel of God, even as Christ Jesus.

    “but received me as an angel of God”. Paul is a man, and the Galatians received him as God's messenger. That is another example of angel describing a human leader, explaining the angel of the church in Smyrna.

**[Revelation 2:9](/bible/revelation/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/2#v9)</sup> I know thy works, and tribulation, and poverty, (but thou art rich) and I know the blasphemy of them which say they are Jews, and are not, but are the synagogue of Satan.

- Christ knows the works of the Israelites in Smyrna. They did a lot of works because they were a colony of Rome, they caught a lot of tribulation, and they were deeply impoverished. But thou art rich, because all the promises in the Bible pertain to the Israelites, and the Church of Smyrna was a part of them.
- “I know the blasphemy of them which say they are Jews, and are not, but are the synagogue of Satan.” Christian commentaries say this verse is speaking of the scribes and Pharisees and nothing else. That is not true. Christ himself told the Pharisees they were Abraham’s seed: they were Jews, following their father Rome. The ones who say they are Jews and are not are Idumea, Edom: the robbers of our people who exalt themselves as the Jews, the Edomite Herods set up as kings of Judea, and the white man who took the land in 1948 and established himself as the Jewish people.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 8:31, 33, 37, 44](/bible/john/8#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/john/8#v31)</sup> Then said Jesus to those Jews which believed on him, If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed;
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/john/8#v33)</sup> They answered him, We be Abraham’s seed, and were never in bondage to any man: how sayest thou, Ye shall be made free?
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/john/8#v37)</sup> I know that ye are Abraham’s seed; but ye seek to kill me, because my word hath no place in you.
    >
    > <sup>[44](/bible/john/8#v44)</sup> Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

    Christ was talking to the Jews: “Then said Jesus to those Jews which believed on him”. Some believed and some did not. The ones who did not answered, “We be Abraham’s seed, and were never in bondage to any man: how sayest thou, Ye shall be made free?”

**[Revelation 2:10](/bible/revelation/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/2#v10)</sup> Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer: behold, the devil shall cast some of you into prison, that ye may be tried; and ye shall have tribulation ten days: be thou faithful unto death, and I will give thee a crown of life.

- Fear none of the things you will suffer. The devil casts some into prison so they may be tried, just as Herod imprisoned the apostles under Rome. Ten days is the period of tribulation appointed to them. Christ commands faithfulness unto death and promises a crown of life. Some of us may have to die for the truth; that death is precious to the Lord.
- “Fear none of those things which thou shalt suffer”: now the Lord tells them, don’t worry about what’s going to happen. We need you brothers and your wives to get your minds right.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 12:1-4](/bible/acts/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/acts/12#v1)</sup> Now about that time Herod the king stretched forth his hands to vex certain of the church.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/acts/12#v2)</sup> And he killed James the brother of John with the sword.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/acts/12#v3)</sup> And because he saw it pleased the Jews, he proceeded further to take Peter also. (Then were the days of unleavened bread.)
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/acts/12#v4)</sup> And when he had apprehended him, he put him in prison, and delivered him to four quaternions of soldiers to keep him; intending after Easter to bring him forth to the people.

    Herod stretched out his hands against the church, killed James the brother of John with the sword, and then took Peter because killing James pleased the Jews. Peter was apprehended and put in prison. Herod is the Edomite ruler under Rome. That history shows the devil casting believers into prison and trying them; the warning to Smyrna is something the church had already experienced.

**[Revelation 2:11](/bible/revelation/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/2#v11)</sup> He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; He that overcometh shall not be hurt of the second death.

- The one who overcomes will not be hurt by the second death. That is the lake of fire, not ordinary death. The first resurrection is at Christ's coming; the elect have already been judged and the second death has no power over them. The rest of the dead come afterward, when the thousand years are finished.
- Do not assume that being your mother or father makes somebody exempt. Family members who died without believing will face that judgment. Burning a body or scattering ashes in the sea does not prevent resurrection. God brings the dead back from wherever they are and judges every man according to his works.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 20:11-14](/bible/revelation/20#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/20#v11)</sup> And I saw a great white throne, and him that sat on it, from whose face the earth and the heaven fled away; and there was found no place for them.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)</sup> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/20#v13)</sup> And the sea gave up the dead which were in it; and death and hell delivered up the dead which were in them: and they were judged every man according to their works.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/20#v14)</sup> And death and hell were cast into the lake of fire. This is the second death.

    John sees the great white throne and the one before whose face earth and heaven flee. It is a dreadful judgment. The dead, small and great, stand before God; the books are opened, along with the book of life, and the dead are judged according to their works. The books include the Bible by which the works are judged and the record of those works; the book of life identifies those who receive life.

**[Revelation 2:12-13](/bible/revelation/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/2#v12)</sup> And to the angel of the church in Pergamos write; These things saith he which hath the sharp sword with two edges;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/2#v13)</sup> I know thy works, and where thou dwellest, even where Satan’s seat is: and thou holdest fast my name, and hast not denied my faith, even in those days wherein Antipas was my faithful martyr, who was slain among you, where Satan dwelleth.

- Christ has the sharp sword with two edges. He addresses the leader of Pergamos and knows where they live, even where Satan's seat is. Pergamos was a large city that dealt with a lot of demonic activity; Satan’s seat was there. Yet they hold fast his name and have not denied the faith, even when Antipas, his faithful martyr, was killed among them. Do not be ashamed of the words of the Bible or surrender them because somebody threatens you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Nahum 3:4](/bible/nahum/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/nahum/3#v4)</sup> Because of the multitude of the whoredoms of the wellfavoured harlot, the mistress of witchcrafts, that selleth nations through her whoredoms, and families through her witchcrafts.

    This is talking about Nineveh in the first, literal sense, but it is also a metaphor for America. “Because of the multitude of the whoredoms of the wellfavoured harlot”: the wellfavoured harlot is the United States of America, the mother of harlots of Revelation 17:5. Everybody loves that hoe called America.

**[Revelation 2:14](/bible/revelation/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/2#v14)</sup> But I have a few things against thee, because thou hast there them that hold the doctrine of Balaam, who taught Balac to cast a stumblingblock before the children of Israel, to eat things sacrificed unto idols, and to commit fornication.

- There are still a few things against this church. Some hold the doctrine of Balaam, who taught Balac to put a stumbling block before Israel: food sacrificed to idols and fornication. Balaam loved the wages of unrighteousness. Covetousness and women became the means of turning Israel from God.
- Balaam’s counsel was: we can’t beat the men, send women among them. In the turbulent 60s, with the black radical groups, what did the white man do? Send the white woman. They made a joke of it in Undercover Brother with Eddie Griffin: we can’t take them down, send in the black man’s Kryptonite, and the white woman comes walking in, in slow motion, in a white tight leather outfit, Becky with the good hair. After Loving v. Virginia, black and Latino men went crazy: no other race of men has done more interracial marriage than black and Hispanic men.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Peter 2:15](/bible/2-peter/2#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-peter/2#v15)</sup> Which have forsaken the right way, and are gone astray, following the way of Balaam the son of Bosor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness;

    “Which have forsaken the right way, and are gone astray, following the way of Balaam the son of Bosor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness”. Balaam was covetous; all he was about was the money. Most of the group that ran up out of here loved the wages of unrighteousness.

**[Revelation 2:15-16](/bible/revelation/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/2#v15)</sup> So hast thou also them that hold the doctrine of the Nicolaitans, which thing I hate.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/revelation/2#v16)</sup> Repent; or else I will come unto thee quickly, and will fight against them with the sword of my mouth.

- Pergamos also has those holding the doctrine of the Nicolaitans, the worship of the Greek god Nike, the Greek god of victory, which Christ hates. His command is repent. Otherwise he comes quickly and fights against them with the sword of his mouth. That group, I’mma fight against them with the word of God, I’m going to kill them: that’s what he’s warning them in Pergamos.

**[Revelation 2:17](/bible/revelation/2#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/revelation/2#v17)</sup> He that hath an ear, let him hear what the Spirit saith unto the churches; To him that overcometh will I give to eat of the hidden manna, and will give him a white stone, and in the stone a new name written, which no man knoweth saving he that receiveth it.

- The overcomer receives the hidden manna. Christ is that bread from heaven; it is not merely the physical bread the fathers ate in the wilderness. Receiving his doctrine gives life. The white stone and the new name are further promises to the one who overcomes.
- The overcomer receives a white stone with a new name written in it, known only to the person who receives it. That new name is the true name, the one you had before the world, not another name for men to argue over now. We do not remember those former things in this life.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 9:18](/bible/2-esdras/9#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/2-esdras/9#v18)</sup> And now when I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live, no man spake against me.

    “And now when I prepared the world, which was not yet made, even for them to dwell in that now live, no man spake against me.” This is the Lord speaking, talking about the Israelites. We were all with him in the very beginning, before he prepared the world. That is why no man spake against him: we were there, and we were all in agreement to what God’s plan was.

**[Revelation 2:18](/bible/revelation/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/revelation/2#v18)</sup> And unto the angel of the church in Thyatira write; These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass;

- The message goes to the leader in Thyatira, a city whose name means daughter. On the map it is close to Ephesus and Pergamos; these congregations lived among the same Greek influences. The Son of God has eyes like a flame of fire and feet like fine brass. He sees the works and the problems of the church.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 16:14](/bible/acts/16#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/acts/16#v14)</sup> And a certain woman named Lydia, a seller of purple, of the city of Thyatira, which worshipped God, heard us: whose heart the Lord opened, that she attended unto the things which were spoken of Paul.

    Lydia was a seller of purple from Thyatira, a woman who worshipped God and heard Paul. The Lord opened her heart so that she attended to what Paul spoke. She heard Paul teaching in Thyatira. Thyatira is Greek for daughter, and on the map it sits under the church of Pergamos.

**[Revelation 2:19](/bible/revelation/2#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/revelation/2#v19)</sup> I know thy works, and charity, and service, and faith, and thy patience, and thy works; and the last to be more than the first.

- Christ knows their works, charity, service, faith and patience. Charity is a Greek word for love: the love you have for neighbours, for brothers and sisters. And servants: that’s Leviticus 25:55. Their last works are more than the first. This is a working congregation with real good in it, yet it must still correct what follows.

This Jezebel is not the Jezebel of the book of Kings; two different time periods. But spiritually, if your wife is a Jezebel, you are an Ahab. Jezebel is sitting in here; Jezebel is listening online.

It hates the prophets of God and feels it is better than you. It is a very aggressive spirit, becoming a man and dominating men: a maneater. That is the woman minister jumping in the pulpit, the one in the video: do y’all see the cleavage on this ho today?, and now even an Israelite sister who knows she is an Israelite teaching on the street with a microphone.

  Precepts:

And he laid him down upon his bed, and turned away his face, and would eat no bread.

He jumped when she said jump, and she portrayed herself as holy.

    Y’all know this one: she repented not. “For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry.”

**[Revelation 2:22-23](/bible/revelation/2#v22)**
