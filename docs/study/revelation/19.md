## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 19 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 6-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 19:1](/bible/revelation/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/19#v1)</sup> And after these things I heard a great voice of much people in heaven, saying, Alleluia; Salvation, and glory, and honour, and power, unto the Lord our God:

- The topic is entitled Supper, War and Marriage. “Alleluia”: how many of you know what the word hallelujah means? Literally it’s praise to Yahawah. The yah at the end is abbreviated for the Most High’s name, and halal is praises. This chapter is still continuing on from chapters 17 and 18. John is continuing with the vision he is seeing, in chronological order, and he is going to jump back in the middle of the chapter.

**[Revelation 19:2](/bible/revelation/19#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/19#v2)</sup> For true and righteous are his judgments: for he hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication, and hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand.

- “For he hath judged the great whore”: who is the great whore? Babylon, which is America. I want us all to get in the habit of speaking plain, because many Christian commentaries will tell you Babylon the great whore is Vatican City. Remember in Revelation 18 it said come out of her, my people, letting you know the people of God would be in Babylon. We’re not in Vatican City; how many Negroes are in Vatican City anyway? You might have three. “Which did corrupt the earth with her fornication”: her fornication is spiritual fornication, idolatry: her holidays like Christmas, her democracies, and the main idol, the image of the beast, Caesar Borgia, as students in the class answered. And all nations were confederate to destroy us and keep us in darkness and lies, so that we would not remember that we are Israel. “And hath avenged the blood of his servants at her hand”: all the servants of the Most High are accumulated here with the destruction of Babylon. That includes the servants from the time of Rome, our forefathers Peter, James, John and Paul, who were put to death. They didn’t pay for it yet; they are going to pay for it when Babylon is destroyed. Even the ones that put the Savior to death, like it says in Revelation 1, the Most High is going to make sure they all come back and get judged for that.
- “For true and righteous are his judgments: for he hath judged the great whore, which did corrupt the earth with her fornication.” For true and righteous are his judgments.
