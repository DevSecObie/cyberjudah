## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 16:1](/bible/revelation/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/16#v1)</sup> And I heard a great voice out of the temple saying to the seven angels, Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth.

- “And I heard a great voice out of the temple saying to the seven angels, Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth”: these seven angels are going to wreak havoc upon the earth. The seven vials represent war. Christ said there shall be wars and rumors of wars, and in this chapter we see both: wars, and the rumor of a war to come.
- “Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth.” What is being poured out on the earth is the wrath of God: the seven angels have the seven last plagues, full of the wrath of God.
- “Go your ways, and pour out the vials of the wrath of God upon the earth.” Here are the same plagues God brought on ancient Egypt, and he is going to bring them on America in one hour.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 19:1-2](/bible/isaiah/19#v1)**

    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/19#v2)</sup> And I will set the Egyptians against the Egyptians: and they shall fight every one against his brother, and every one against his neighbour; city against city, and kingdom against kingdom.

    “The burden of Egypt. Behold, the Lord rideth upon a swift cloud, and shall come into Egypt”. The Egypt he is talking about is America, and he is coming to deliver us. “And the idols of Egypt shall be moved at his presence, and the heart of Egypt shall melt in the midst of it.”

**[Revelation 16:2](/bible/revelation/16#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/16#v2)</sup> And the first went, and poured out his vial upon the earth; and there fell a noisome and grievous sore upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image.

- “And there fell a noisome and grievous sore”. Noisome means foul smelling and harmful, extremely offensive to the senses, particularly the smell: a real nasty sore. What could make a man like that? A nuclear bomb. It causes severe radiation burns, blistering, open sores and deep, long-lasting ulcers. When a nuclear bomb goes off, if the blast does not kill you, you are going to be rotting, your skin falling off, and it will be noisome.
- “Upon the men which had the mark of the beast, and upon them which worshipped his image.” How do we know this is that one hour of that one day, the nuclear bomb falling on America? The sore falls only on those who have the mark of the beast and worship his image. So where are we? We are delivered. It is not going to fall upon us, because the Lord will deliver us. This is talking about what happens after a nuclear bomb’s fallout.

This is Mystery Babylon, Babylon the great, being destroyed: the Lord sending on America the same plagues he sent on Egypt, all in one hour. “And every living soul died in the sea”: this is war on the sea, nuclear bombs going off in the ocean, nuclear war.
- “And the third angel poured out his vial upon the rivers and fountains of waters; and they became blood.” When it says they became blood, it means they became poisonous, radioactive. Nobody could drink it.
