## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 22 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 22:1-2](/bible/revelation/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/22#v1)</sup> And he shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/22#v2)</sup> In the midst of the street of it, and on either side of the river, was there the tree of life, which bare twelve manner of fruits, and yielded her fruit every month: and the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.

- “He shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb.” Then “the leaves of the tree were for the healing of the nations.” So the nations are here now. That means it is going back to chapter 20, back to Isaiah 60, back to Zechariah 14.
- The tree of life “yielded her fruit every month”: yielding understanding every month. The leaves are for the healing of the nations, and that is the only way for the nations: they got to come up, like during the Feast of Tabernacles. You must come up, you must obey.
- “And he shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb”: John is seeing a vision; it is written as a similitude, a metaphor, and we need the proper precepts to explain it. This is why the Old Testament is very important; the Christians who say don’t read it, it’s done away with, are agents of Satan. The water symbolizes the word of God. Do you see words coming in the air? No; it is a symbol.
- Now the question may come: when will the curse end? “And he shewed me a pure river of water of life, clear as crystal, proceeding out of the throne of God and of the Lamb.” What did he see? The law of God. The clear water as crystal proceeding from the throne is the law.

    The precept for the pure river: “And, behold, waters issued out from under the threshold of the house eastward.”
  - **[Ephesians 5:26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/ephesians/5#v26)</sup> That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word,

    “That he might sanctify and cleanse it with the washing of water by the word”: when John and Ezekiel see water gushing out from the temple, it all symbolizes the word of God.
  - **[Isaiah 2:1-3](/bible/isaiah/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/2#v1)</sup> The word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem.
    >

    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/2#v3)</sup> And many people shall go and say, Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, to the house of the God of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for out of Zion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.

    Isaiah shows what the river of Revelation 22 is symbolic of. “The word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem. And it shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the Lord’s house shall be established in the top of the mountains”: that is the Kingdom on earth. “And shall be exalted above the hills”: above all governments. “And all nations shall flow unto it”: everybody’s going to be subject to us.

**[Revelation 22:3-5](/bible/revelation/22#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/22#v3)</sup> And there shall be no more curse: but the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him:

- “And there shall be no more curse”: we are not going to be destroyed or go into captivity ever again. Israel shall be all righteous, so that curse will be done away with. “But the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him.”
- “And there shall be no more curse”. So the curse will end when the kingdom is established.
- “And there shall be no more curse”. When this is established, the third temple, there shall be no more curse on the Israelites. When Christ returns, the curse is over. “But the throne of God and of the Lamb shall be in it; and his servants shall serve him”.

- “I fell down to worship before the feet of the angel”: “See thou do it not: for I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren the prophets”: that is a heavy saying, for another class. A hint: in the book of Tobit the angel told Tobit he was of the tribe of such and such, of his brethren; the angel didn’t lie, we just don’t understand yet. “And of them which keep the sayings of this book”: first and foremost the book of Revelation, but it includes the entire Bible.
- John fell down to worship before the feet of the angel. “Then saith he unto me, See thou do it not: for I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren the prophets.” Did anybody pick anything up in that? The angel said, I am your fellow servant, and I am of your brothers the prophets. Now that is some stuff right there. The angel is saying, don’t worship me, I am of your brothers the prophets; “worship God.”
