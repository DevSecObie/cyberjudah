These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 12 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 12:1](/bible/revelation/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/12#v1)</sup> And there appeared a great wonder in heaven; a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars:

- This is a continuation of the Book of Revelation; the captains should know how to break down Revelation 12. John the Revelator was in a prison camp on the island of Patmos, a salt mine, writing around the year 96 AD; Jerusalem had already been destroyed. About 85% of the Book of Revelation is written in parable form, metaphor and allegory, and to understand it you must know the Old Testament precepts. “A woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet”: this woman is not so much Mary; it goes beyond Mary, and her name wasn’t Mary, which is Greek; her name was Miriam. The woman represents the nation of Israel, and she is clothed with the sun and the moon because they represent God’s law. “And upon her head a crown of twelve stars”: the twelve stars represent the twelve tribes of Israel, so this woman is the nation of the twelve tribes.
- We didn’t forget the topic. “And there appeared a great wonder in heaven; a woman”: this woman is Israel, used as a metaphor in a feminine context. Now we know who this woman is.
