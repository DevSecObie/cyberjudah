## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 18 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-24, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 18:1-2](/bible/revelation/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/18#v1)</sup> And after these things I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power; and the earth was lightened with his glory.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/18#v2)</sup> And he cried mightily with a strong voice, saying, Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit, and a cage of every unclean and hateful bird.

- Chapter 17 continues into 18. “Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen”: why is he saying it? Because in chapter 17 he sees her burned with fire. She “is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit”: there are going to be foul spirits here.
- This comes after chapter 17, where John sees Babylon. “Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen”: it goes back to chapter 17. Why does it say it twice? Because Israel is little Babylon. “And is become the habitation of devils, and the hold of every foul spirit”.
- Back to Revelation 18: “After these things I saw another angel come down from heaven, having great power”, and he cried mightily with a strong voice, “saying, Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen, and is become the habitation of devils”. Babylon the great is falling; America is falling. After Babylon has fallen, it becomes the habitation of devils. All devils are going to be here in America; this is where they are all going to be.
- Who is going to be tormented and inhabit here in America? Devils, demons. That’s the Lake of Fire right there, man. They are all going to be in America, and they are going to be cast in the Lake of Fire by Christ and the angels.

**[Revelation 18:3](/bible/revelation/18#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/18#v3)</sup> For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication, and the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her, and the merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies.

- “For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication”: the wine is lies, falsehood; the wrath is anger; and her fornication is spiritual fornication. When it speaks of wine and fornication it is speaking metaphorically, a similitude: God is comparing one thing to another. Babylon is not literally going around committing sexual sin; this is spiritual. “And the kings of the earth have committed fornication with her”: the kings of the earth joined with Babylon in her lies, in her spiritual fornication. “And the merchants of the earth are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies.”
- The main wine the nations drank, the one that brings the wrath of God upon them, is the confederacy to destroy God’s people. We always go to America’s political program, which is true, but the beginning of it was all the nations agreeing together against the Israelites, taking an oath to be in union with Esau against us. That is what Psalms 83 shows: all nations were in cahoots with Edom.
- “For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication”: the same thing read in chapter 17. “The merchants of the earth” are the corporations, and they “are waxed rich”: all nations get rich dealing with the United States of America. There’s no such thing as, oh, our deficit, we don’t have enough money. America prints money: we’re ten billion short? Go in the back and print some.
- “For all nations have drunk of the wine of the wrath of her fornication”: that is their policies, what we read in Daniel 8, that through his policy he shall cause craft to prosper in his hand. All nations follow their policies. “And the kings of the earth”, the nations, “have committed fornication with her”: they got in bed with her. “And the merchants of the earth”, the corporations and companies, “are waxed rich through the abundance of her delicacies.”
