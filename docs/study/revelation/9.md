- All of this is in the Most High's hands. The Lord is angry now and is getting ready to bring serious devastation to deliver his people out of Egypt, out of bondage, with a mighty hand. I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and I have created the waster to destroy (Isaiah 54:16): God caused the smith to create the nuclear bomb, and the Most High is going to cause them to use those weapons on each other while he delivers us. No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper (Isaiah 54:17): that goes back to Psalms 91, a thousand shall fall at thy side and ten thousand at thy right hand, but it shall not come nigh thee; only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked. The Most High means business, so we have nothing to worry about. The Lord is our power, the Lord is our shepherd, and we ain't going to lack nothing. Keep your minds up.

    Ezra saw the same missiles John saw: fire, and hail, and flying swords, and many waters, and they shall break down the cities and walls, mountains and hills, trees of the wood, and grass of the meadows, and their corn. The flying swords is making reference to these missiles that Revelation 9 is making reference to, their tails like unto serpents, and with their heads they do hurt.
  - **[Isaiah 54:16-17](/bible/isaiah/54#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/54#v16)</sup> Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and that bringeth forth an instrument for his work; and I have created the waster to destroy.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/54#v17)</sup> No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord, and their righteousness is of me, saith the Lord.

    Everything we are looking at is all in the Most High's hands. The Lord is angry now, and he is getting ready to bring serious devastation to deliver his people out of Egypt, out of the bondage, with a mighty hand. “Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire,” The smith is the blacksmith, a man that made weapons; the Lord said, I created that thing. Blowing the coals in the fire is to make the steel hot so you can bend it and shape it and make swords, shields, bucklers, battle axes, weapons of war. “and that bringeth forth an instrument for his work;” He is going to cause this smith to bring forth an instrument for his work, and his work is to deliver the Israelites. “and I have created the waster to destroy.” The waster is the instrument he caused these smiths to make: that is what you were just looking at on the screen, the nuclear bomb. God caused the smith to create that, and they are not going to be able to stop it. The Most High is going to cause them to use those weapons on each other while he delivers us. That is what Revelation is talking about: he is going to deliver us up while the bombs are coming; ten thousand on one side, you are going to see people dying, like Psalms 91: only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked. He created the waster to destroy the enemies.
