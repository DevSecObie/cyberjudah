## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Revelation 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-11, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Revelation 10:1](/bible/revelation/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/10#v1)</sup> And I saw another mighty angel come down from heaven, clothed with a cloud: and a rainbow was upon his head, and his face was as it were the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire:

- The class is entitled the little book, sweet and bitter. “And I saw another mighty angel come down from heaven, clothed with a cloud”: it is not talking about a literal cloud of dust around him. “And a rainbow was upon his head”: the rainbow represents the covenant God made with Noah that he would never flood the earth again. It ain’t talking about same-sex marriages. Many of us see rainbows on posts and think of the LGBT; Esau takes the covenant God established and makes it something perverse. “And his face was as it were the sun, and his feet as pillars of fire.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 104:3](/bible/psalms/104#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/104#v3)</sup> Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind:

    This is what the precept says about clouds. “Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters”: not the Atlantic Ocean; it is the waters above the firmament, the third heaven. “Who maketh the clouds his chariot”: what the white man today calls unidentified flying objects.
  - **[Genesis 1:6-7](/bible/genesis/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/1#v6)</sup> And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/1#v7)</sup> And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.

    “Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters”: the firmament is the ozone layer. “The waters which were under the firmament”: our atmosphere. “From the waters which were above the firmament”: what we call space.
  - **[Genesis 9:11-17](/bible/genesis/9#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/9#v11)</sup> And I will establish my covenant with you; neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood; neither shall there any more be a flood to destroy the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/9#v12)</sup> And God said, This is the token of the covenant which I make between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for perpetual generations:
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/9#v13)</sup> I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/9#v14)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when I bring a cloud over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/9#v15)</sup> And I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/9#v16)</sup> And the bow shall be in the cloud; and I will look upon it, that I may remember the everlasting covenant between God and every living creature of all flesh that is upon the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/9#v17)</sup> And God said unto Noah, This is the token of the covenant, which I have established between me and all flesh that is upon the earth.

    “Neither shall there any more be a flood to destroy the earth.” “I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth”: that is the rainbow. This is what our sons and daughters must learn.

**[Revelation 10:2](/bible/revelation/10#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/revelation/10#v2)</sup> And he had in his hand a little book open: and he set his right foot upon the sea, and his left foot on the earth,

- “And he had in his hand a little book open”: the little book makes reference to the Bible. “And he set his right foot upon the sea, and his left foot on the earth.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 17:14](/bible/exodus/17#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/17#v14)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Write this for a memorial in a book, and rehearse it in the ears of Joshua: for I will utterly put out the remembrance of Amalek from under heaven.

    “Write this for a memorial in a book”: a lot of times we think Moses only had two tables of stone. That’s not true; he was writing what we call today, by the Latin word biblos, the Bible.
  - **[Isaiah 30:8](/bible/isaiah/30#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/30#v8)</sup> Now go, write it before them in a table, and note it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever:

    “And note it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever”: Isaiah continued to write in the book.
  - **[Jeremiah 36:2](/bible/jeremiah/36#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/36#v2)</sup> Take thee a roll of a book, and write therein all the words that I have spoken unto thee against Israel, and against Judah, and against all the nations, from the day I spake unto thee, from the days of Josiah, even unto this day.

    “Take thee a roll of a book, and write therein all the words that I have spoken unto thee against Israel, and against Judah”: the prophet Jeremiah continued to write the book.

**[Revelation 10:3](/bible/revelation/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/revelation/10#v3)</sup> And cried with a loud voice, as when a lion roareth: and when he had cried, seven thunders uttered their voices.

- “And cried with a loud voice, as when a lion roareth”: angels didn’t talk soft either. When they spoke they had voices like lions, and this is what we got to pattern ourselves after; the Bible is meant to change us. “And when he had cried, seven thunders uttered their voices.”

**[Revelation 10:4](/bible/revelation/10#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/10#v4)</sup> And when the seven thunders had uttered their voices, I was about to write: and I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not.

- “I was about to write: and I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Seal up those things which the seven thunders uttered, and write them not”: we hear thunder all the time, but in the spirit John understood the language the thunder uttered. He was going to write it, and Christ said don’t write that down. The same thing was said to Paul.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 12:1-4](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v1)</sup> It is not expedient for me doubtless to glory. I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v2)</sup> I knew a man in Christ above fourteen years ago, (whether in the body, I cannot tell; or whether out of the body, I cannot tell: God knoweth;) such an one caught up to the third heaven.
    >

    >

    “I will come to visions and revelations of the Lord.” “I knew a man in Christ above fourteen years ago”: he is talking about himself. Paul got stoned in the book of Acts, the disciples prayed round him, and he got back up and went back into the city to teach; that’s the level of faith. “Such an one caught up to the third heaven”: our sky is the first heaven, space where the moon is is the second, and the third heaven is where the Most High and the angels dwell. “How that he was caught up into paradise”: the proof that the third heaven is where the Most High dwells. “And heard unspeakable words, which it is not lawful for a man to utter”: he was told it is unlawful to write that down. The Lord doesn’t want to make it so easy, because this walk is based upon faith. The scribes and Pharisees always said show us a sign; our forefathers in the wilderness with Moses saw everything, and they turned on Moses.
  - **[Hebrews 11:6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)</sup> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

    “But without faith it is impossible to please him”: without faith you ain’t pleasing the Lord. “And that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.”
  - **[Romans 10:17](/bible/romans/10#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/romans/10#v17)</sup> So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

    Paul gave us the secret to increase our faith: “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” If you spend all your time listening to Biggie Smalls or Drake, your faith is going to be very weak; if you spend your time listening to scriptures and classes, that will increase your faith, because you are feeding your spirit with the word of the Lord.

**[Revelation 10:5](/bible/revelation/10#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/10#v5)</sup> And the angel which I saw stand upon the sea and upon the earth lifted up his hand to heaven,

- “And the angel which I saw stand upon the sea and upon the earth lifted up his hand to heaven”: the angel swore by the God of heaven and earth. That is something we are forbidden to do; angels can do it, but not you and I.

    > <sup>[12](/bible/james/5#v12)</sup> But above all things, my brethren, swear not, neither by heaven, neither by the earth, neither by any other oath: but let your yea be yea; and your nay, nay; lest ye fall into condemnation.

    “But above all things, my brethren, swear not”: the apostle James repeated what Christ taught us. That’s why in court you can affirm instead; let your yea be yea and your nay be nay. Our forefather Jephthah swore that the first thing that came out of the door of his house he would sacrifice, and it was his little daughter.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 5:1-6](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v1)</sup> Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God, and be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools: for they consider not that they do evil.
    >

    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v3)</sup> For a dream cometh through the multitude of business; and a fool’s voice is known by multitude of words.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v4)</sup> When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it; for he hath no pleasure in fools: pay that which thou hast vowed.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v5)</sup> Better is it that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not pay.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v6)</sup> Suffer not thy mouth to cause thy flesh to sin; neither say thou before the angel, that it was an error: wherefore should God be angry at thy voice, and destroy the work of thine hands?

    “Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God”: the house of God is the congregation. The Lord told Moses to take the shoes off his feet; he didn’t want Egyptian doctrine tracked up the holy mountain. Don’t track the philosophies you learned into the house of God. “And be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools”: you can’t learn if your mouth is always open. “Therefore let thy words be few.” “When thou vowest a vow unto God, defer not to pay it.” “Better is it that thou shouldest not vow, than that thou shouldest vow and not pay.” “Neither say thou before the angel, that it was an error”: this all goes back to why Christ told us not to make vows. Angels can, because they fulfil their word.

**[Revelation 10:8-9](/bible/revelation/10#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/10#v8)</sup> And the voice which I heard from heaven spake unto me again, and said, Go and take the little book which is open in the hand of the angel which standeth upon the sea and upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/10#v9)</sup> And I went unto the angel, and said unto him, Give me the little book. And he said unto me, Take it, and eat it up; and it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey.

- John is told, “Go and take the little book”, and the angel says, “Take it, and eat it up; and it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey.” The little book is the scriptures, the understanding.
- The sweet and the bitter is the severity of this truth. When you first learn the truth, it is sweet to know that you are Israel, chosen by God. Later you learn that you are going to be persecuted, that you are going to lose family members, jobs, things of that nature.
- “Go and take the little book which is open in the hand of the angel which standeth upon the sea and upon the earth.”
- “Give me the little book”: give me that Bible. “Take it, and eat it up”: take it, study it and learn it. “And it shall make thy belly bitter, but it shall be in thy mouth sweet as honey”: the Lord said the same thing to Ezekiel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 2:7-10](/bible/ezekiel/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/2#v7)</sup> And thou shalt speak my words unto them, whether they will hear, or whether they will forbear: for they are most rebellious.
    >

Malcolm X admitted his mistake of saying the Honourable Elijah Muhammad teaches us; that gives glory to man. God’s words have power. Calling Esau a name has no power; they laugh. But when you tell them from the Bible that they are Edomites, the nation of Esau, that cuts them.
  - **[Isaiah 34:16](/bible/isaiah/34#v16)**

    “Seek ye out of the book of the Lord, and read”: not out of the Quran. “No one of these shall fail”: no one of these prophecies shall fail. Until you prove to me that slavery never happened to our people, according to Deuteronomy 28, 1 Kings 8, Leviticus 26 and Jeremiah 17, the Bible is a true book; our own condition proves it. “None shall want her mate”: you can’t mate the Bible with the Quran, the Gilgamesh epic or the Egyptian Book of the Dead. “For my mouth it hath commanded, and his spirit it hath gathered them.” This little book is enough for us. King James had 47 of the best Hebrew and Greek scholars examine the writings, and they left out the book of Enoch and the book of Jasher.

**[Revelation 10:10](/bible/revelation/10#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/10#v10)</sup> And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up; and it was in my mouth sweet as honey: and as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter.

- “And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up”: learned it. “And it was in my mouth sweet as honey”: the sweet of the scriptures is knowing we are Israel, that Christ looks like us and is our King, that we will be saved from our enemies, that the kingdom is for us, that the world is ours, and that we will rule our oppressors. That is what draws us in. “And as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter”: the bitter of the scriptures is the results of application and knowledge, for self, friends, family and people in general. All of us are going to be tried as individuals, among friends, and in our families and marriages. It may be easy to cut off a friend or a distant family member, but it is not so easy to cut off a spouse or a child; that’s a true test of your faith. Then there is knowing that two out of every three here in America are going to be put to death.
- “And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up”: this is John the Revelator; he ate up the book, he learned it. “And it was in my mouth sweet as honey: and as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter.” His belly was bitter because John the Revelator saw everything that was going to come to pass, and said damn.
- “And I took the little book out of the angel’s hand, and ate it up”: he got the understanding of the scriptures; he understood Deuteronomy 28. “And it was in my mouth sweet as honey”, because all the promises are for you, as Romans 9 says, all the prophecies of the kingdom of heaven: the dominion, the power and the kingdom.
- “And as soon as I had eaten it, my belly was bitter.” If we are to be joint heirs with Christ, we have to go through what Christ went through. John the Baptist had his head chopped off and put on a platter, for correcting a man by the scriptures, and Christ never did anything wrong. You think you are going to sit beside them, be one of the 144,000, sitting in an Israelite camp, sharing no videos, going to no camp, nobody knowing you’re an Israelite, and never go through anything? You got to be kidding. That is the bitter part.

**[Revelation 10:11](/bible/revelation/10#v11)**

- “Thou must prophesy again”: when he ate the roll, the Lord told him, everything that you ate and learned, you gonna learn it again and you got to go teach it again to your people. Remember John was not in a good place. He was in prison, exiled on that island, up there in age, and the Lord said you got to eat this roll again and teach it to your people. Imagine John saying I got to go back to this people again, you crazy man. But he got to do what he got to do.
- So to eat the roll, or to eat the book, is talking about learning it.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 4:41](/bible/2-esdras/4#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/2-esdras/4#v41)</sup> Then said I, No, Lord, that can she not. And he said unto me, In the grave the chambers of souls are like the womb of a woman:

    “In the grave the chambers of souls are like the womb of a woman”: what does the womb of a woman bring forth? Life. It is telling you from the grave the soul goes back through the woman again. We will go over that in more detail in another class.
  - **[Ezekiel 3:4-5](/bible/ezekiel/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/3#v4)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, go, get thee unto the house of Israel, and speak with my words unto them.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/3#v5)</sup> For thou art not sent to a people of a strange speech and of an hard language, but to the house of Israel;

    “Son of man, go, get thee unto the house of Israel”: this is what you’re gonna have to do after you eat the roll. You learn the Bible, you learn the sweetness of it and you learn the bitterness of it. The bitterness is that you will be hated; the sweetness is the reward you will get when you do what was commanded you to do.
