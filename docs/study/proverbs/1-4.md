Read the chapter → [Proverbs 1](/bible/proverbs/1)

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session moves through [Proverbs 1](/bible/proverbs/1) to 4. Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel, gives understanding in these proverbs, and the whole thread is the fear of the Most High and the keeping of His wisdom. He warns against being enticed by sinners and against greediness of gain, and he holds up wisdom and the commandments as our life, far exceeding silver, gold, and rubies. Chapter 1 opens the book with the purpose of the proverbs and the first warning: consent not when sinners entice you.

**[Proverbs 1:1-6](/bible/proverbs/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/1#v1)</sup> The proverbs of Solomon the son of David, king of Israel;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/1#v2)</sup> To know wisdom and instruction; to perceive the words of understanding;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/1#v3)</sup> To receive the instruction of wisdom, justice, and judgment, and equity;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/1#v4)</sup> To give subtilty to the simple, to the young man knowledge and discretion.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/1#v5)</sup> A wise man will hear, and will increase learning; and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/1#v6)</sup> To understand a proverb, and the interpretation; the words of the wise, and their dark sayings.

- The proverbs of Solomon, the son of David, king of Israel. Solomon is giving understanding in this proverb, and from the very top he is basically talking about the fear of the Most High and understanding.
- These verses lay out why the proverbs are given: to know wisdom and instruction, to perceive the words of understanding, to receive the instruction of wisdom, justice, judgment, and equity, to give subtlety to the simple, and to the young man knowledge and discretion.
- A wise man will hear and increase learning, and a man of understanding shall attain unto wise counsels, to understand the proverb and the interpretation, the words of the wise and their dark sayings.

**[Proverbs 1:7-9](/bible/proverbs/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/1#v7)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/1#v8)</sup> My son, hear the instruction of thy father, and forsake not the law of thy mother:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/1#v9)</sup> For they shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head, and chains about thy neck.

- The fear of the Most High is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction. This is the key that runs through all four chapters.
- My son, hear the instruction of thy father and forsake not the law of thy mother, for they shall be an ornament of grace unto thy head and chains about thy neck.

**[Proverbs 1:10-19](/bible/proverbs/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/1#v10)</sup> My son, if sinners entice thee, consent thou not.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/1#v11)</sup> If they say, Come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/1#v12)</sup> Let us swallow them up alive as the grave; and whole, as those that go down into the pit:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/1#v13)</sup> We shall find all precious substance, we shall fill our houses with spoil:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/1#v14)</sup> Cast in thy lot among us; let us all have one purse:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/1#v15)</sup> My son, walk not thou in the way with them; refrain thy foot from their path:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16)</sup> For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/1#v17)</sup> Surely in vain the net is spread in the sight of any bird.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/1#v18)</sup> And they lay wait for their own blood; they lurk privily for their own lives.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/1#v19)</sup> So are the ways of every one that is greedy of gain; which taketh away the life of the owners thereof.

- A key point is verse 10: my son, if sinners entice thee, consent thou not. If somebody who is committing sin entices you or tries to get you to go with them, do not go with them.
- To bring some life to it, look at something like a robbery. If they say come with us, let us lay wait for blood, let us lurk privily for the innocent without cause, that means you are doing evil to the innocent for no reason.
- Let us swallow them up alive as the grave, and we shall find all precious substance and fill our houses with spoil. Whatever you take from this person, you are going to fill your own pockets with their goods.
- Cast in thy lot among us, let us all have one purse. The sinner is enticing you, saying that if you consent, then they will share the spoils of what they get.
- My son, walk not thou in the way with them, refrain thy foot from their path. Solomon is giving wisdom: do not fall into the hands of sinners, do not be enticed by the things they want to do, and do not be enticed by their greediness.
- So are the ways of everyone that is greedy of gain, which taketh away the life of the owners thereof. If you are greedy of gain, you will be going with them, you will be enticed, and you will consent to them.

**[Proverbs 1:20-27](/bible/proverbs/1#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/1#v20)</sup> Wisdom crieth without; she uttereth her voice in the streets:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/1#v21)</sup> She crieth in the chief place of concourse, in the openings of the gates: in the city she uttereth her words, saying,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/1#v22)</sup> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity? and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/1#v23)</sup> Turn you at my reproof: behold, I will pour out my spirit unto you, I will make known my words unto you.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/1#v24)</sup> Because I have called, and ye refused; I have stretched out my hand, and no man regarded;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/1#v25)</sup> But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/1#v26)</sup> I also will laugh at your calamity; I will mock when your fear cometh;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/1#v27)</sup> When your fear cometh as desolation, and your destruction cometh as a whirlwind; when distress and anguish cometh upon you.

- Wisdom cries without and utters her voice in the streets, in the chief place of concourse and in the openings of the gates, calling out to the simple ones who love simplicity, the scorners who delight in their scorning, and the fools who hate knowledge.
- Turn ye at my reproof, behold I will pour out my spirit unto you and make known my words unto you. The reproof is the correction of the Most High, that is, His commandments.
- Because I have called and you refused, I have stretched out my hand and no man regarded, but ye have set at nought all my counsel and would none of my reproof, I also will laugh at your calamity and mock when your fear cometh.

**[Proverbs 1:28-33](/bible/proverbs/1#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/1#v28)</sup> Then shall they call upon me, but I will not answer; they shall seek me early, but they shall not find me:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/1#v29)</sup> For that they hated knowledge, and did not choose the fear of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/1#v30)</sup> They would none of my counsel: they despised all my reproof.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/1#v31)</sup> Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way, and be filled with their own devices.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/1#v32)</sup> For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/1#v33)</sup> But whoso hearkeneth unto me shall dwell safely, and shall be quiet from fear of evil.

- Then shall they call upon me, but I will not answer; they shall seek me early, but they shall not find me, because they hated knowledge and did not choose the fear of the Most High.
- They were none of my counsel and despised all my reproof, all His commandments. Therefore shall they eat of the fruit of their own way and be filled with their own devices. The Most High is going to give you up to whatever you are choosing to do; whatever you put over Him is what He will allow you to do.
- For the turning away of the simple shall slay them, and the prosperity of fools shall destroy them, but whoso hearkeneth unto the Most High shall dwell safely and be quiet from fear of evil.

# Proverbs 2: The Benefits of Keeping the Wisdom of the Lord

Read the chapter → [Proverbs 2](/bible/proverbs/2)

**[Proverbs 2:1-5](/bible/proverbs/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou wilt receive my words, and hide my commandments with thee;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/2#v2)</sup> So that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom, and apply thine heart to understanding;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/2#v3)</sup> Yea, if thou criest after knowledge, and liftest up thy voice for understanding;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/2#v4)</sup> If thou seekest her as silver, and searchest for her as for hid treasures;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/2#v5)</sup> Then shalt thou understand the fear of the Lord, and find the knowledge of God.

- My son, if thou receive my words and hide my commandments with thee, so that thou incline thine ear unto wisdom and apply thine heart to understanding.
- Verses 2 through 4 explain what that means: crying after knowledge, lifting up thy voice for understanding, and seeking her as silver and searching for her as for hid treasures.
- The reward of hiding the commandments with thee is in verse 5: then shalt thou understand the fear of the Most High and find the knowledge of God.

**[Proverbs 2:6-11](/bible/proverbs/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/2#v6)</sup> For the Lord giveth wisdom: out of his mouth cometh knowledge and understanding.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/2#v7)</sup> He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous: he is a buckler to them that walk uprightly.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/2#v8)</sup> He keepeth the paths of judgment, and preserveth the way of his saints.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/2#v9)</sup> Then shalt thou understand righteousness, and judgment, and equity; yea, every good path.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/2#v10)</sup> When wisdom entereth into thine heart, and knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/2#v11)</sup> Discretion shall preserve thee, understanding shall keep thee:

- For the Most High giveth wisdom; out of His mouth cometh knowledge and understanding. He layeth up sound wisdom for the righteous and is a buckler to them that walk uprightly.
- He keepeth the paths of judgment and preserveth the way of His saints. The saints are the Israelites, as we read yesterday.
- Then shalt thou understand righteousness, judgment, and equity, yea every good path, when wisdom enters into thine heart and knowledge is pleasant unto thy soul; discretion shall preserve thee and understanding shall keep thee.

**[Proverbs 2:12-15](/bible/proverbs/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/2#v12)</sup> To deliver thee from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh froward things;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/2#v13)</sup> Who leave the paths of uprightness, to walk in the ways of darkness;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/2#v14)</sup> Who rejoice to do evil, and delight in the frowardness of the wicked;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/2#v15)</sup> Whose ways are crooked, and they froward in their paths:

- Understanding delivers thee from the way of the evil man, from the man that speaketh forward things. Forward means things that are against the Most High, things that go contrary to the commandments.
- These evil men leave the paths of uprightness to walk in the ways of darkness, who rejoice to do evil and delight in the forwardness of the wicked, whose ways are crooked and who are forward in their paths.

**[Proverbs 2:16-19](/bible/proverbs/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/2#v16)</sup> To deliver thee from the strange woman, even from the stranger which flattereth with her words;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/2#v17)</sup> Which forsaketh the guide of her youth, and forgetteth the covenant of her God.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/2#v18)</sup> For her house inclineth unto death, and her paths unto the dead.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/2#v19)</sup> None that go unto her return again, neither take they hold of the paths of life.

- Understanding also delivers thee from the strange woman, from the stranger which flattereth with her words, which forsaketh the guide of her youth and forgetteth the covenant of her God.
- Her house inclineth unto death and her paths unto the dead; none that go unto her return again, neither take they hold of the paths of life. This goes for men as well, and it is vice versa, so it is something to meditate on.

**[Proverbs 2:20-22](/bible/proverbs/2#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/2#v20)</sup> That thou mayest walk in the way of good men, and keep the paths of the righteous.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/2#v21)</sup> For the upright shall dwell in the land, and the perfect shall remain in it.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/2#v22)</sup> But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth, and the transgressors shall be rooted out of it.

- That thou mayest walk in the way of good men and keep the paths of the righteous. For the upright shall dwell in the land and the perfect shall remain in it.
- But the wicked shall be cut off from the earth and the transgressors shall be rooted out of it. The whole chapter is basically showing the benefits of keeping the wisdom of the Most High.

- She is more precious than rubies, and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her. Nothing can be compared to the wisdom of the Most High.
- Length of days is in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and honor. Her ways are ways of pleasantness and all her paths are peace. She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her, and happy is everyone that retaineth her.

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/4#v18)</sup> But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day.

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/4#v19)</sup> The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble.

- Enter not into the path of the wicked and go not in the way of evil men; avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away. This is the same thing we read in [Proverbs 1:16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16).
- For they sleep not except they have done mischief, and their sleep is taken away unless they cause some to fall, for they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence.
- The path of the just is as the shining light that shineth more and more unto the perfect day, but the way of the wicked is as darkness; they know not at what they stumble.
  - **[Proverbs 1:16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16)</sup> For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood.

    For their feet run to evil and make haste to shed blood, the same wicked path Solomon says to avoid and turn from.

**[Proverbs 4:20-24](/bible/proverbs/4#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/4#v20)</sup> My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/4#v21)</sup> Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thine heart.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/4#v22)</sup> For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/4#v23)</sup> Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/4#v24)</sup> Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee.

- My son, attend to my words and incline thine ear unto my sayings; let them not depart from thine eyes, keep them in the midst of thine heart, for they are life unto those that find them and health to their flesh.
- Keep thy heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. Put away from thee a forward mouth and perverse lips put far from thee. A forward mouth is a mouth that speaks things opposite of what the word of the Most High says.

**[Proverbs 4:25-27](/bible/proverbs/4#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/4#v25)</sup> Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/4#v26)</sup> Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/4#v27)</sup> Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.

- Let thine eyes look right on and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet and let all thy ways be established.
- Turn not to the right hand nor to the left; remove thy foot from evil. Across all four chapters Solomon says the same thing over and over, the same thing he says in his psalms: depart not from the commandments, keep them close to you, and depart from evil, all of it praising the Most High.
- On the question of the law and the commandments, they are exactly the same; the words are just used interchangeably. The commandments He gave are the laws, as in [Exodus 20](/bible/exodus/20) where the commandments are given, thou shalt not kill, thou shalt not steal, the ten commandments plus many more that came with them.
