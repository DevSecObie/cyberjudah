**[Proverbs 7:1-5](/bible/proverbs/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/7#v1)</sup> My son, keep my words, and lay up my commandments with thee.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/7#v3)</sup> Bind them upon thy fingers, write them upon the table of thine heart.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/7#v4)</sup> Say unto wisdom, Thou art my sister; and call understanding thy kinswoman:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/7#v5)</sup> That they may keep thee from the strange woman, from the stranger which flattereth with her words.

- Keep my words and lay up my commandments; keep my commandments and live, and my law as the apple of your eye. Bind them upon your fingers and write them on your heart.
- Call wisdom your sister and understanding your kinswoman, that they may keep you from the strange woman and from the stranger who flatters with her words. The scriptures keep repeating this theme of sexual immorality and adultery through this section.

**[Proverbs 7:6-9](/bible/proverbs/7#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/7#v6)</sup> For at the window of my house I looked through my casement,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/7#v7)</sup> And beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, a young man void of understanding,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/7#v8)</sup> Passing through the street near her corner; and he went the way to her house,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/7#v9)</sup> In the twilight, in the evening, in the black and dark night:

- Looking through the window, the teacher sees among the simple ones a young man void of understanding passing through the street near her corner, going toward her house in the twilight, in the evening, in the black and dark night.

**[Proverbs 7:10-13](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)</sup> And, behold, there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/7#v11)</sup> (She is loud and stubborn; her feet abide not in her house:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/7#v12)</sup> Now is she without, now in the streets, and lieth in wait at every corner.)
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/7#v13)</sup> So she caught him, and kissed him, and with an impudent face said unto him,

- There met him a woman with the attire of an harlot and subtle of heart. She does not go out with her head covered in a modest dress; she dresses in the clothing of a harlot. She is loud and stubborn, her feet do not abide in her house, and she lies in wait at every corner.
- So she caught him and kissed him, and with an impudent face said she had peace offerings and had paid her vows. This is the opposite of the modest apparel the sisters are taught.
  - **[1 Timothy 2:9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)</sup> In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety; not with broided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly array;

    Women are to adorn themselves in modest apparel with shamefacedness and sobriety, the inward beauty and not the outward attire of the harlot.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 41:17](/bible/sirach/41#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/41#v17)</sup> Be ashamed of whoredom before father and mother: and of a lie before a prince and a mighty man;

    Be ashamed of whoredom is the point set against the attire of the harlot.

**[Proverbs 7:14-20](/bible/proverbs/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/7#v14)</sup> I have peace offerings with me; this day have I payed my vows.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/7#v15)</sup> Therefore came I forth to meet thee, diligently to seek thy face, and I have found thee.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/7#v16)</sup> I have decked my bed with coverings of tapestry, with carved works, with fine linen of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/7#v17)</sup> I have perfumed my bed with myrrh, aloes, and cinnamon.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/7#v18)</sup> Come, let us take our fill of love until the morning: let us solace ourselves with loves.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/7#v19)</sup> For the goodman is not at home, he is gone a long journey:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/7#v20)</sup> He hath taken a bag of money with him, and will come home at the day appointed.

- She tells him she came out diligently to seek his face and has found him, the very hunting for the precious soul described in chapter 6. She has decked her bed with fine linen of Egypt and perfumed it with myrrh, aloes and cinnamon, inviting him to take their fill of love until the morning.
- She reassures him that the good man is not at home but has gone on a long journey with a bag of money and will not return until the day appointed. What draws a man is a beautiful woman, good food, and sex, and she offers all three.

**[Proverbs 7:21-27](/bible/proverbs/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/7#v21)</sup> With her much fair speech she caused him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she forced him.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/7#v22)</sup> He goeth after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as a fool to the correction of the stocks;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/7#v23)</sup> Till a dart strike through his liver; as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for his life.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/7#v24)</sup> Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children, and attend to the words of my mouth.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/7#v25)</sup> Let not thine heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/7#v26)</sup> For she hath cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/7#v27)</sup> Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death.

- With much fair speech and the flattering of her lips she persuaded him strongly. He goes after her straightway as an ox to the slaughter and as a fool to the correction of the stocks, until a dart strikes through his liver, not knowing it is for his life.
- Do not let your heart decline to her ways nor go astray in her paths, for she has cast down many wounded, and many strong men have been slain by her. King Solomon himself was drawn by strange women into serving their gods. Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death.
