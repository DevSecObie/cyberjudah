**[Proverbs 15:1-17](/bible/proverbs/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/15#v1)</sup> A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/15#v2)</sup> The tongue of the wise useth knowledge aright: but the mouth of fools poureth out foolishness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/15#v3)</sup> The eyes of the Lord are in every place, beholding the evil and the good.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/15#v4)</sup> A wholesome tongue is a tree of life: but perverseness therein is a breach in the spirit.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/15#v5)</sup> A fool despiseth his father’s instruction: but he that regardeth reproof is prudent.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/15#v6)</sup> In the house of the righteous is much treasure: but in the revenues of the wicked is trouble.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/15#v7)</sup> The lips of the wise disperse knowledge: but the heart of the foolish doeth not so.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/15#v8)</sup> The sacrifice of the wicked is an abomination to the Lord: but the prayer of the upright is his delight.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/15#v9)</sup> The way of the wicked is an abomination unto the Lord: but he loveth him that followeth after righteousness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)</sup> Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/15#v11)</sup> Hell and destruction are before the Lord: how much more then the hearts of the children of men?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/15#v12)</sup> A scorner loveth not one that reproveth him: neither will he go unto the wise.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/15#v13)</sup> A merry heart maketh a cheerful countenance: but by sorrow of the heart the spirit is broken.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/15#v14)</sup> The heart of him that hath understanding seeketh knowledge: but the mouth of fools feedeth on foolishness.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/15#v15)</sup> All the days of the afflicted are evil: but he that is of a merry heart hath a continual feast.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/15#v16)</sup> Better is little with the fear of the Lord than great treasure and trouble therewith.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/15#v17)</sup> Better is a dinner of herbs where love is, than a stalled ox and hatred therewith.

**[Proverbs 15:18-33](/bible/proverbs/15#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18)</sup> A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/15#v19)</sup> The way of the slothful man is as an hedge of thorns: but the way of the righteous is made plain.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/15#v20)</sup> A wise son maketh a glad father: but a foolish man despiseth his mother.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/15#v21)</sup> Folly is joy to him that is destitute of wisdom: but a man of understanding walketh uprightly.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/15#v22)</sup> Without counsel purposes are disappointed: but in the multitude of counsellors they are established.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/15#v23)</sup> A man hath joy by the answer of his mouth: and a word spoken in due season, how good is it!
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/15#v24)</sup> The way of life is above to the wise, that he may depart from hell beneath.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/15#v25)</sup> The Lord will destroy the house of the proud: but he will establish the border of the widow.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/15#v26)</sup> The thoughts of the wicked are an abomination to the Lord: but the words of the pure are pleasant words.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/15#v27)</sup> He that is greedy of gain troubleth his own house; but he that hateth gifts shall live.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/15#v28)</sup> The heart of the righteous studieth to answer: but the mouth of the wicked poureth out evil things.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/15#v29)</sup> The Lord is far from the wicked: but he heareth the prayer of the righteous.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/15#v30)</sup> The light of the eyes rejoiceth the heart: and a good report maketh the bones fat.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/15#v31)</sup> The ear that heareth the reproof of life abideth among the wise.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/15#v32)</sup> He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul: but he that heareth reproof getteth understanding.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/15#v33)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the instruction of wisdom; and before honour is humility.
