**[Proverbs 23:1-3](/bible/proverbs/23#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/23#v1)</sup> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before thee:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/23#v2)</sup> And put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/23#v3)</sup> Be not desirous of his dainties: for they are deceitful meat.

- When you sit to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before you and put a knife to your throat if you are a man given to appetite. This is about being greedy.
- If you are invited to dinner and devour everything, then ask if there are seconds, that is offensive. Put a knife to your appetite so you do not embarrass yourself being insatiable, greedy, and gluttonous.

**[Proverbs 23:4-8](/bible/proverbs/23#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/23#v4)</sup> Labour not to be rich: cease from thine own wisdom.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/23#v5)</sup> Wilt thou set thine eyes upon that which is not? for riches certainly make themselves wings; they fly away as an eagle toward heaven.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/23#v6)</sup> Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/23#v7)</sup> For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/23#v8)</sup> The morsel which thou hast eaten shalt thou vomit up, and lose thy sweet words.

- Labor not to be rich; cease from your own understanding. Riches make themselves wings and fly away as an eagle toward heaven. Money comes and money goes.
- Eat not the bread of him that has an evil eye, for as he thinks in his heart, so is he. He says eat and drink, but his heart is not with you; the morsel you ate you will vomit up.

**[Proverbs 23:9-11](/bible/proverbs/23#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/23#v9)</sup> Speak not in the ears of a fool: for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/23#v10)</sup> Remove not the old landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/23#v11)</sup> For their redeemer is mighty; he shall plead their cause with thee.

- Speak not in the ears of a fool, for he will despise the wisdom of your words. This is Christ saying do not cast your pearls before swine.
- Certain people are not worthy of you trying to deal with them, because they will rebel, disrespect you, and talk crazy. It is a waste of your time.
- Remove not the old landmark and enter not the fields of the fatherless, for their Redeemer is mighty and will plead their cause with you.

**[Proverbs 23:12-16](/bible/proverbs/23#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/23#v12)</sup> Apply thine heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/23#v13)</sup> Withhold not correction from the child: for if thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/23#v14)</sup> Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from hell.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/23#v15)</sup> My son, if thine heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/23#v16)</sup> Yea, my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips speak right things.

- Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to the words of knowledge.
- Withhold not correction from the child, for if you beat him with the rod he will not die. Do not feel bad; you are teaching him a valuable lesson.
- He has levels of discipline: the talking phase, the lecture phase explaining why the behavior is unacceptable, taking away things like the game, Xbox, or TV, then spanking. The punishment must fit the crime, no cruel and unusual punishments.
- You beat him with the rod and deliver his soul from hell, preventing him from making major mistakes later in life.
- My son, if your heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice; my reins rejoice when your lips speak right things.

**[Proverbs 23:17-21](/bible/proverbs/23#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/23#v17)</sup> Let not thine heart envy sinners: but be thou in the fear of the Lord all the day long.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/23#v18)</sup> For surely there is an end; and thine expectation shall not be cut off.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/23#v19)</sup> Hear thou, my son, and be wise, and guide thine heart in the way.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/23#v20)</sup> Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/23#v21)</sup> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags.

- Let not your heart envy sinners, but be in the fear of the Most High all day long. Envy of sinners is like watching 106 and Park wanting the rims, the women, the money, the clothes, the shoes.
- These dudes are wicked, disrespecting our sisters, smoking weed, and rapping about killing another brother. They push that rap music heavily on our people to promote sin among us.
- Surely there is an end and your expectation will not be cut off.
- Be not among winebibbers or riotous eaters of flesh, for the drunkard and glutton will come to poverty, and drowsiness clothes a man with rags.
- These are people who cannot control their drink and have to drink every day. A brother we called to read was drunk at eleven in the morning. Such people meet poverty; you hear of people drinking themselves into poverty.

**[Proverbs 23:22-25](/bible/proverbs/23#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/23#v22)</sup> Hearken unto thy father that begat thee, and despise not thy mother when she is old.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/23#v23)</sup> Buy the truth, and sell it not; also wisdom, and instruction, and understanding.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/23#v24)</sup> The father of the righteous shall greatly rejoice: and he that begetteth a wise child shall have joy of him.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/23#v25)</sup> Thy father and thy mother shall be glad, and she that bare thee shall rejoice.

- Hearken to your father that begot you, and despise not your mother when she is old.
- Buy the truth and sell it not; also wisdom, instruction, and understanding.
- The father of the righteous greatly rejoices, and he that begets a wise child has joy of him. Bad kids are a headache: truancy court because he skips school, picking him up from the police station for stealing, bad grades. A wise child brings joy.

**[Proverbs 23:26-28](/bible/proverbs/23#v26)**
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/23#v26)</sup> My son, give me thine heart, and let thine eyes observe my ways.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/23#v27)</sup> For a whore is a deep ditch; and a strange woman is a narrow pit.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/23#v28)</sup> She also lieth in wait as for a prey, and increaseth the transgressors among men.

- My son, give me your heart and let your eyes observe my ways.
- A whore is a deep ditch and a strange woman a narrow pit. She lies in wait as for a prey and increases the transgressors among men.
  - **[Proverbs 22:14](/bible/proverbs/22#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/22#v14)</sup> The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: he that is abhorred of the Lord shall fall therein.

    The mouth of a strange woman is a deep pit for the man abhorred of the Most High.

**[Proverbs 23:29-35](/bible/proverbs/23#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/23#v29)</sup> Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/23#v30)</sup> They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/23#v31)</sup> Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, when it giveth his colour in the cup, when it moveth itself aright.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/23#v32)</sup> At the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/23#v33)</sup> Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thine heart shall utter perverse things.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/23#v34)</sup> Yea, thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, or as he that lieth upon the top of a mast.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/proverbs/23#v35)</sup> They have stricken me, shalt thou say, and I was not sick; they have beaten me, and I felt it not: when shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.

- Who has woe, sorrow, contentions, babbling, and wounds without cause? They that tarry long at the wine and go to seek mixed wine.
- Look not on the wine when it is red, for at the last it bites like a serpent and stings like an adder. When you get drunk your eyes behold strange women and your heart utters perverse things.
- When you are inebriated you start dealing with women and lose all sense of order, commandments, and understanding.
- They have stricken you and you were not sick; they have beaten you and you felt it not. When shall I awake, I will seek it yet again, showing the pull of drunkenness.
