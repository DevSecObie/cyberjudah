**[Proverbs 6:1-3](/bible/proverbs/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/6#v1)</sup> My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/6#v2)</sup> Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/6#v3)</sup> Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend.

- If you are surety for your friend or have struck your hand with a stranger, you are snared by the words of your mouth. Deliver yourself, go humble yourself, and make sure your friend.
- A surety in today's terms is like co-signing for someone, vouching that they are good and guaranteeing they will be taken care of.
  - **[Genesis 43:8-9](/bible/genesis/43#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/43#v8)</sup> And Judah said unto Israel his father, Send the lad with me, and we will arise and go; that we may live, and not die, both we, and thou, and also our little ones.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/43#v9)</sup> I will be surety for him; of my hand shalt thou require him: if I bring him not unto thee, and set him before thee, then let me bear the blame for ever:

    Judah becomes surety for Benjamin, guaranteeing his safe return and willing to bear the blame forever.

**[Proverbs 6:4-11](/bible/proverbs/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/6#v4)</sup> Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/6#v5)</sup> Deliver thyself as a roe from the hand of the hunter, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/6#v6)</sup> Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/6#v7)</sup> Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/6#v8)</sup> Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/6#v9)</sup> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/6#v10)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/6#v11)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man.

- Give no sleep to your eyes; deliver yourself as a roe from the hand of the hunter. Go to the ant, consider her ways and be wise; having no guide, overseer, or ruler, she provides her meat in the summer and gathers her food in the harvest.
- A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands, and poverty comes as one that travels and want as an armed man. This is why in the proving stage the sisters and leadership ask a brother whether he works, has his own place, and can provide for a woman and offspring. Leadership keeps a report on these men so there are no back-door marriages.
  - **[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

    The man who provides not for his own house has denied the faith and is worse than an infidel.

**[Proverbs 6:12-15](/bible/proverbs/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/6#v12)</sup> A naughty person, a wicked man, walketh with a froward mouth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/6#v13)</sup> He winketh with his eyes, he speaketh with his feet, he teacheth with his fingers;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/6#v14)</sup> Frowardness is in his heart, he deviseth mischief continually; he soweth discord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/6#v15)</sup> Therefore shall his calamity come suddenly; suddenly shall he be broken without remedy.

- A naughty, wicked man walks with a froward mouth, winks with his eyes, speaks with his feet, teaches with his fingers, and devises mischief continually, sowing discord. Therefore his calamity comes suddenly and he is broken without remedy.

**[Proverbs 6:16-19](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/6#v17)</sup> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/6#v18)</sup> An heart that deviseth wicked imaginations, feet that be swift in running to mischief,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/6#v19)</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

- Six things the Most High hates, seven are an abomination to Him. He cannot be all love if He hates certain things.
- A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood. Because He hates hands that shed innocent blood, the murderers among Esau and the nations that took us captive and shed our blood have no place ruling over us in the kingdom, yet our people keep trying to get them in.
- A heart that devises wicked imaginations such as sex changes, homosexuality and lesbianism; feet swift running to mischief such as gangs and gang initiation; a false witness that speaks lies; and he that sows discord among brethren through gossip and heresies.
  - **[Isaiah 3:16](/bible/isaiah/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/3#v16)</sup> Moreover the Lord saith, Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and walk with stretched forth necks and wanton eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet:

    The daughters of Zion are haughty with stretched forth necks and wanton eyes, the proud look the Most High hates.
  - **[Matthew 11:12](/bible/matthew/11#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/11#v12)</sup> And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.

    The kingdom of heaven suffers violence and the violent take it by force; the nations have taken it by the sword.
  - **[Genesis 27:40](/bible/genesis/27#v40)**
    > <sup>[40](/bible/genesis/27#v40)</sup> And by thy sword shalt thou live, and shalt serve thy brother; and it shall come to pass when thou shalt have the dominion, that thou shalt break his yoke from off thy neck.

    By the sword shalt thou live is Esau's blessing; he lives by the sword to kill and take what he wants.

**[Proverbs 6:20-24](/bible/proverbs/6#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/6#v20)</sup> My son, keep thy father’s commandment, and forsake not the law of thy mother:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/6#v21)</sup> Bind them continually upon thine heart, and tie them about thy neck.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/6#v22)</sup> When thou goest, it shall lead thee; when thou sleepest, it shall keep thee; and when thou awakest, it shall talk with thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/6#v24)</sup> To keep thee from the evil woman, from the flattery of the tongue of a strange woman.

- Keep your father's commandment and forsake not the law of your mother; bind them upon your heart and tie them about your neck. When you go it leads you, when you sleep it keeps you, and when you wake it talks with you.
- The commandment is a lamp and the law is light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life, to keep you from the evil woman and the flattery of the strange woman already described.

**[Proverbs 6:25-29](/bible/proverbs/6#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/6#v25)</sup> Lust not after her beauty in thine heart; neither let her take thee with her eyelids.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/6#v26)</sup> For by means of a whorish woman a man is brought to a piece of bread: and the adulteress will hunt for the precious life.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/6#v27)</sup> Can a man take fire in his bosom, and his clothes not be burned?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/6#v28)</sup> Can one go upon hot coals, and his feet not be burned?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/6#v29)</sup> So he that goeth in to his neighbour’s wife; whosoever toucheth her shall not be innocent.

- Do not lust after her beauty in your heart, which is your mind, nor let her take you with her eyelids. By means of a whorish woman a man is brought to a piece of bread, and the adulteress will hunt for the precious life.
- A man cannot take fire in his bosom and not be burned, nor go on hot coals and not be burned; so he that goes in to his neighbour's wife and touches her shall not be innocent.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 9:5](/bible/sirach/9#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/9#v5)</sup> Gaze not on a maid, that thou fall not by those things that are precious in her.

    Do not gaze on a maid, because looking is looking to lust.

**[Proverbs 6:30-35](/bible/proverbs/6#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/6#v30)</sup> Men do not despise a thief, if he steal to satisfy his soul when he is hungry;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/6#v31)</sup> But if he be found, he shall restore sevenfold; he shall give all the substance of his house.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/6#v32)</sup> But whoso committeth adultery with a woman lacketh understanding: he that doeth it destroyeth his own soul.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/6#v33)</sup> A wound and dishonour shall he get; and his reproach shall not be wiped away.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/6#v34)</sup> For jealousy is the rage of a man: therefore he will not spare in the day of vengeance.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/proverbs/6#v35)</sup> He will not regard any ransom; neither will he rest content, though thou givest many gifts.

- Men do not despise a thief who steals to satisfy his hunger, yet he must restore sevenfold. But whoever commits adultery destroys his own soul; a wound and dishonour he gets, and his reproach is not wiped away.
- Jealousy is the rage of a man; in the day of vengeance he will spare nothing and regard no ransom. Let a husband find out his wife has been cheating and inquire the matter, and he may well put that brother to death once he catches up to him.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 23:17-21](/bible/sirach/23#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/23#v17)</sup> All bread is sweet to a whoremonger, he will not leave off till he die.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/23#v18)</sup> A man that breaketh wedlock, saying thus in his heart, Who seeth me? I am compassed about with darkness, the walls cover me, and no body seeth me; what need I to fear? the most High will not remember my sins:
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/23#v19)</sup> Such a man only feareth the eyes of men, and knoweth not that the eyes of the Lord are ten thousand times brighter than the sun, beholding all the ways of men, and considering the most secret parts.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/23#v20)</sup> He knew all things ere ever they were created; so also after they were perfected he looked upon them all.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/23#v21)</sup> This man shall be punished in the streets of the city, and where he suspecteth not he shall be taken.

    The adulterer thinks no one sees, but the eyes of the Most High are ten thousand times brighter than the sun and he shall be punished where he suspected not.
