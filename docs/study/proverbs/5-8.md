Read the chapter → [Proverbs 5](/bible/proverbs/5)

This session covers [Proverbs 5](/bible/proverbs/5) through 8. The majority of these chapters deal with fornication, adultery, and sexual immorality, warning the sons of Israel away from the strange woman. Chapter 6 also turns to the sluggard and the ant, and to the six things the Most High hates. Chapter 8 opens with wisdom crying in the streets and closes with a picture of Christ set up from everlasting. Read along daily and go back and review for yourself; there is no substitute for reading the book of the Most High.

**[Proverbs 5:1-2](/bible/proverbs/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/5#v1)</sup> My son, attend unto my wisdom, and bow thine ear to my understanding:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/5#v2)</sup> That thou mayest regard discretion, and that thy lips may keep knowledge.

- Attend to wisdom and bow your ear to understanding so that you may regard knowledge. The knowledge spoken of here is the commandments of the Most High.
  - **[Malachi 2:7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)</sup> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.

    The priest's lips keep knowledge, so knowledge is tied to the law and the commandments.

**[Proverbs 5:3-6](/bible/proverbs/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/5#v3)</sup> For the lips of a strange woman drop as an honeycomb, and her mouth is smoother than oil:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/5#v4)</sup> But her end is bitter as wormwood, sharp as a twoedged sword.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/5#v5)</sup> Her feet go down to death; her steps take hold on hell.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/5#v6)</sup> Lest thou shouldest ponder the path of life, her ways are moveable, that thou canst not know them.

- The lips of a strange woman drop like honeycomb and her mouth is smoother than oil, but her end is bitter as wormwood and sharp as a two-edged sword. Her feet go down to death and her steps take hold on hell.
- Her ways are so unstable that you cannot know them. Brothers especially are warned not to turn aside after her.

**[Proverbs 5:7-9](/bible/proverbs/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/5#v7)</sup> Hear me now therefore, O ye children, and depart not from the words of my mouth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/5#v8)</sup> Remove thy way far from her, and come not nigh the door of her house:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/5#v9)</sup> Lest thou give thine honour unto others, and thy years unto the cruel:

- Do not depart from these words, remove your way far from her, and do not come near the door of her house, lest you give your honour to others and your years to the cruel.
- Giving your honour to others is like giving what is holy to strangers. Too often our people care more about the other nations repenting and getting into the kingdom than about our own repentance, and want the other nations ruling with us in the kingdom.
  - **[Matthew 7:6](/bible/matthew/7#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/7#v6)</sup> Give not that which is holy unto the dogs, neither cast ye your pearls before swine, lest they trample them under their feet, and turn again and rend you.

    Do not give what is holy to the dogs nor cast your pearls before swine; the pearls are the word of the Most High, not to be handed to strangers.

**[Proverbs 5:10-11](/bible/proverbs/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/5#v10)</sup> Lest strangers be filled with thy wealth; and thy labours be in the house of a stranger;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/5#v11)</sup> And thou mourn at the last, when thy flesh and thy body are consumed,

- Lest strangers be filled with your wealth and your labours be in the house of a stranger, and you mourn at the last when your flesh and body are consumed. Your labours are the riches that come from your work ending up in the house of strangers.
- We are living this now. Every time a foreign king came up to invade Israel he stripped the silver and gold and pleasant things out of the temple and exacted tribute. In this captivity Esau still profits off our labour and our inventions, the light filament, the plasma and open heart surgery, the cotton gin, computers and cell phones, yet makes no account of who produced them.
  - **[Joel 3:4-5](/bible/joel/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/joel/3#v4)</sup> Yea, and what have ye to do with me, O Tyre, and Zidon, and all the coasts of Palestine? will ye render me a recompence? and if ye recompense me, swiftly and speedily will I return your recompence upon your own head;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/joel/3#v5)</sup> Because ye have taken my silver and my gold, and have carried into your temples my goodly pleasant things:

    Tyre and Zidon and the coasts of Palestine took the Most High's silver and gold and carried His goodly pleasant things into their temples.
  - **[Obadiah 1:12-13](/bible/obadiah/1#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/obadiah/1#v12)</sup> But thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have rejoiced over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly in the day of distress.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/obadiah/1#v13)</sup> Thou shouldest not have entered into the gate of my people in the day of their calamity; yea, thou shouldest not have looked on their affliction in the day of their calamity, nor have laid hands on their substance in the day of their calamity;

    Esau is condemned for entering the gate of the Most High's people in their calamity and laying hands on their substance.
  - **[Genesis 30:27](/bible/genesis/30#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/30#v27)</sup> And Laban said unto him, I pray thee, if I have found favour in thine eyes, tarry: for I have learned by experience that the Lord hath blessed me for thy sake.

    Laban confesses he has learned by experience that the Most High blessed him for Jacob's sake, as the nations are blessed for Israel's sake today.

**[Proverbs 5:12-15](/bible/proverbs/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/5#v12)</sup> And say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/5#v13)</sup> And have not obeyed the voice of my teachers, nor inclined mine ear to them that instructed me!
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/5#v14)</sup> I was almost in all evil in the midst of the congregation and assembly.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/5#v15)</sup> Drink waters out of thine own cistern, and running waters out of thine own well.

- The man who despised instruction mourns at the last, saying how he hated instruction and his heart despised reproof, and did not obey the voice of his teachers. The commandments and the prophets are the teachers meant here.
- Drink waters out of your own cistern and running waters out of your own well. This is a call to stay with your own people and your own nationality.
  - **[Proverbs 1:25](/bible/proverbs/1#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/1#v25)</sup> But ye have set at nought all my counsel, and would none of my reproof:

    You have set at nought all counsel and would have none of reproof, matching the man who hated instruction.

**[Proverbs 5:16-19](/bible/proverbs/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/5#v16)</sup> Let thy fountains be dispersed abroad, and rivers of waters in the streets.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/5#v17)</sup> Let them be only thine own, and not strangers’ with thee.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/5#v18)</sup> Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/5#v19)</sup> Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe; let her breasts satisfy thee at all times; and be thou ravished always with her love.

- Let your fountains be your own and not shared with strangers. Let your fountain be blessed and rejoice with the wife of your youth; let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe and let her breast satisfy you at all times.
- Be ravished always with her love, not with another woman. Married love within your own nation is what is blessed here.
  - **[Genesis 26:8](/bible/genesis/26#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/26#v8)</sup> And it came to pass, when he had been there a long time, that Abimelech king of the Philistines looked out at a window, and saw, and, behold, Isaac was sporting with Rebekah his wife.

    Isaac was seen sporting with Rebekah his wife, the picture of a husband playing lovingly with his own wife.

**[Proverbs 5:20-23](/bible/proverbs/5#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/5#v20)</sup> And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger?
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/5#v21)</sup> For the ways of man are before the eyes of the Lord, and he pondereth all his goings.

>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/5#v23)</sup> He shall die without instruction; and in the greatness of his folly he shall go astray.

- Why be ravished with a strange woman and embrace the bosom of a stranger? The strange woman carries a double meaning: a woman of another nation, and a woman outside of your own wife.
- The ways of a man are before the eyes of the Most High and He ponders all his goings. The wicked man's own iniquities take him and he is held with the cords of his sins, dying without instruction in the greatness of his folly.
  - **[Nehemiah 13:23-26](/bible/nehemiah/13#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/nehemiah/13#v23)</sup> In those days also saw I Jews that had married wives of Ashdod, of Ammon, and of Moab:
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/nehemiah/13#v24)</sup> And their children spake half in the speech of Ashdod, and could not speak in the Jews’ language, but according to the language of each people.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/nehemiah/13#v25)</sup> And I contended with them, and cursed them, and smote certain of them, and plucked off their hair, and made them swear by God, saying, Ye shall not give your daughters unto their sons, nor take their daughters unto your sons, or for yourselves.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/nehemiah/13#v26)</sup> Did not Solomon king of Israel sin by these things? yet among many nations was there no king like him, who was beloved of his God, and God made him king over all Israel: nevertheless even him did outlandish women cause to sin.

    The jews had married wives of Ashdod, Ammon and Moab, and even Solomon was caused to sin by outlandish women; strange wives are women of the other nations.
  - **[1 Esdras 8:92](/bible/1-esdras/8#v92)**

    The sons of Israel confess marrying strange wives of the nations, so the strange woman is a woman of the other nations.
  - **[Judges 11:1-2](/bible/judges/11#v1)**

    > <sup>[2](/bible/judges/11#v2)</sup> And Gilead’s wife bare him sons; and his wife’s sons grew up, and they thrust out Jephthah, and said unto him, Thou shalt not inherit in our father’s house; for thou art the son of a strange woman.

    Jephthah is thrust out as the son of a strange woman, that strange woman being likened to an harlot.

# Proverbs 6: The Surety, the Sluggard, and the Six Things the Most High Hates

**[Proverbs 6:1-3](/bible/proverbs/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/6#v1)</sup> My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/6#v2)</sup> Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/6#v3)</sup> Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend.

- If you are surety for your friend or have struck your hand with a stranger, you are snared by the words of your mouth. Deliver yourself, go humble yourself, and make sure your friend.
- A surety in today's terms is like co-signing for someone, vouching that they are good and guaranteeing they will be taken care of.
  - **[Genesis 43:8-9](/bible/genesis/43#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/43#v8)</sup> And Judah said unto Israel his father, Send the lad with me, and we will arise and go; that we may live, and not die, both we, and thou, and also our little ones.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/43#v9)</sup> I will be surety for him; of my hand shalt thou require him: if I bring him not unto thee, and set him before thee, then let me bear the blame for ever:

    Judah becomes surety for Benjamin, guaranteeing his safe return and willing to bear the blame forever.

**[Proverbs 6:4-11](/bible/proverbs/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/6#v4)</sup> Give not sleep to thine eyes, nor slumber to thine eyelids.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/6#v5)</sup> Deliver thyself as a roe from the hand of the hunter, and as a bird from the hand of the fowler.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/6#v6)</sup> Go to the ant, thou sluggard; consider her ways, and be wise:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/6#v7)</sup> Which having no guide, overseer, or ruler,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/6#v8)</sup> Provideth her meat in the summer, and gathereth her food in the harvest.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/6#v9)</sup> How long wilt thou sleep, O sluggard? when wilt thou arise out of thy sleep?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/6#v10)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/6#v11)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth, and thy want as an armed man.

- Give no sleep to your eyes; deliver yourself as a roe from the hand of the hunter. Go to the ant, consider her ways and be wise; having no guide, overseer, or ruler, she provides her meat in the summer and gathers her food in the harvest.
- A little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands, and poverty comes as one that travels and want as an armed man. This is why in the proving stage the sisters and leadership ask a brother whether he works, has his own place, and can provide for a woman and offspring. Leadership keeps a report on these men so there are no back-door marriages.
  - **[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**

    The man who provides not for his own house has denied the faith and is worse than an infidel.

- A heart that devises wicked imaginations such as sex changes, homosexuality and lesbianism; feet swift running to mischief such as gangs and gang initiation; a false witness that speaks lies; and he that sows discord among brethren through gossip and heresies.

# Proverbs 7: The Adulteress Hunts for the Precious Soul

- Call wisdom your sister and understanding your kinswoman, that they may keep you from the strange woman and from the stranger who flatters with her words. The scriptures keep repeating this theme of sexual immorality and adultery through this section.

**[Proverbs 7:10-13](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)</sup> And, behold, there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart.

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/7#v11)</sup> (She is loud and stubborn; her feet abide not in her house:

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/7#v12)</sup> Now is she without, now in the streets, and lieth in wait at every corner.)

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/7#v13)</sup> So she caught him, and kissed him, and with an impudent face said unto him,

- There met him a woman with the attire of an harlot and subtle of heart. She does not go out with her head covered in a modest dress; she dresses in the clothing of a harlot. She is loud and stubborn, her feet do not abide in her house, and she lies in wait at every corner.
- So she caught him and kissed him, and with an impudent face said she had peace offerings and had paid her vows. This is the opposite of the modest apparel the sisters are taught.
  - **[1 Timothy 2:9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-timothy/2#v9)</sup> In like manner also, that women adorn themselves in modest apparel, with shamefacedness and sobriety; not with broided hair, or gold, or pearls, or costly array;

    Women are to adorn themselves in modest apparel with shamefacedness and sobriety, the inward beauty and not the outward attire of the harlot.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 41:17](/bible/sirach/41#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/41#v17)</sup> Be ashamed of whoredom before father and mother: and of a lie before a prince and a mighty man;

    Be ashamed of whoredom is the point set against the attire of the harlot.

- She reassures him that the good man is not at home but has gone on a long journey with a bag of money and will not return until the day appointed. What draws a man is a beautiful woman, good food, and sex, and she offers all three.

- Do not let your heart decline to her ways nor go astray in her paths, for she has cast down many wounded, and many strong men have been slain by her. King Solomon himself was drawn by strange women into serving their gods. Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death.

# Proverbs 8: Wisdom Crieth in the Streets and Christ Set Up from Everlasting

Read the chapter → [Proverbs 8](/bible/proverbs/8)

**[Proverbs 8:1-5](/bible/proverbs/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/8#v1)</sup> Doth not wisdom cry? and understanding put forth her voice?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/8#v2)</sup> She standeth in the top of high places, by the way in the places of the paths.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/8#v3)</sup> She crieth at the gates, at the entry of the city, at the coming in at the doors.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/8#v4)</sup> Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/8#v5)</sup> O ye simple, understand wisdom: and, ye fools, be ye of an understanding heart.

- Wisdom cries and understanding puts forth her voice, standing on the high places, by the way, at the gates and the entry of the city. In our forefathers' days this was the gates; today it is the government buildings and busy concourses where many people pass.
- She calls to men and to the sons of man, telling the simple to understand wisdom and the fools to be of an understanding heart.

**[Proverbs 8:6-9](/bible/proverbs/8#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/8#v6)</sup> Hear; for I will speak of excellent things; and the opening of my lips shall be right things.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/8#v7)</sup> For my mouth shall speak truth; and wickedness is an abomination to my lips.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/8#v8)</sup> All the words of my mouth are in righteousness; there is nothing froward or perverse in them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/8#v9)</sup> They are all plain to him that understandeth, and right to them that find knowledge.

- Wisdom speaks excellent and right things; her mouth speaks truth and her lips hate wickedness. All her words are in righteousness with nothing froward or perverse in them; they are all plain to him that understands and right to them that find knowledge.
- Like Christ saying he that has ears let him hear, everyone has ears, but only the one with understanding truly hears the wisdom coming out.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 6:12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v12)</sup> Wisdom is glorious, and never fadeth away: yea, she is easily seen of them that love her, and found of such as seek her.

    Wisdom is plain and easily seen by those that love and seek her.
  - **[Titus 1:15](/bible/titus/1#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/titus/1#v15)</sup> Unto the pure all things are pure: but unto them that are defiled and unbelieving is nothing pure; but even their mind and conscience is defiled.

    Unto the pure all things are pure, but unto the defiled nothing is pure, even their mind and conscience being defiled.

**[Proverbs 8:10-21](/bible/proverbs/8#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/8#v10)</sup> Receive my instruction, and not silver; and knowledge rather than choice gold.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/8#v11)</sup> For wisdom is better than rubies; and all the things that may be desired are not to be compared to it.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/8#v12)</sup> I wisdom dwell with prudence, and find out knowledge of witty inventions.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/8#v13)</sup> The fear of the Lord is to hate evil: pride, and arrogancy, and the evil way, and the froward mouth, do I hate.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/8#v14)</sup> Counsel is mine, and sound wisdom: I am understanding; I have strength.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/8#v15)</sup> By me kings reign, and princes decree justice.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/8#v16)</sup> By me princes rule, and nobles, even all the judges of the earth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/8#v17)</sup> I love them that love me; and those that seek me early shall find me.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/8#v18)</sup> Riches and honour are with me; yea, durable riches and righteousness.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/8#v19)</sup> My fruit is better than gold, yea, than fine gold; and my revenue than choice silver.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/8#v20)</sup> I lead in the way of righteousness, in the midst of the paths of judgment:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/8#v21)</sup> That I may cause those that love me to inherit substance; and I will fill their treasures.

- Receive instruction rather than silver and knowledge rather than choice gold, for wisdom is better than rubies. The fear of the Most High is to hate evil, pride, arrogancy, the evil way, and the froward mouth.
- By wisdom kings reign and princes decree justice; she loves those that love her, and those that seek her early shall find her. With her are durable riches and righteousness, better than gold and choice silver.
- Durable riches are not money, since lottery winners, entertainers and athletes end up broke. Durable riches are the treasures laid up in heaven, charity and helping your brother and sister in need.
  - **[Matthew 6:19-20](/bible/matthew/6#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/6#v19)</sup> Lay not up for yourselves treasures upon earth, where moth and rust doth corrupt, and where thieves break through and steal:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/matthew/6#v20)</sup> But lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt, and where thieves do not break through nor steal:

    Lay not up treasures on earth where moth and rust corrupt, but treasures in heaven that cannot be corrupted or stolen.

**[Proverbs 8:32-36](/bible/proverbs/8#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/8#v32)</sup> Now therefore hearken unto me, O ye children: for blessed are they that keep my ways.

- Blessed are they that keep wisdom's ways; hear instruction and be wise. Blessed is the man that hears her, watching daily at her gates and waiting at the posts of her doors.
- Whoever finds wisdom finds life and obtains favour of the Most High, but he that sins against her wrongs his own soul, and all they that hate her love death. From verse 22 onward this points to Christ, our commander and king in the Old Testament.
