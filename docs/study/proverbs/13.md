Read the chapter → [Proverbs 13](/bible/proverbs/13)

This session is a "four chapters a day" full-chapter reading covering [Proverbs 13](/bible/proverbs/13) through 16. The teacher reads straight through the chapters and stops to press home the recurring wisdom themes: guarding your mouth, seeking counsel, keeping the company of wise men, disciplining your children, and leaving an inheritance to your children's children. He frames the whole reading as staying meditated in the word a little at a time so you retain it.

**[Proverbs 13:1-3](/bible/proverbs/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/13#v1)</sup> A wise son heareth his father’s instruction: but a scorner heareth not rebuke.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/13#v2)</sup> A man shall eat good by the fruit of his mouth: but the soul of the transgressors shall eat violence.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/13#v3)</sup> He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction.

- A wise son takes his father's instruction, but a scorner will not even hear rebuke. What you do with correction marks whether you are wise or a scorner.
- The scriptures constantly tell you to watch your mouth. Everything you say you will give account for, so he that guards his mouth keeps his life while the one who opens his lips wide brings destruction.

**[Proverbs 13:4-6](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)</sup> The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/13#v5)</sup> A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/13#v6)</sup> Righteousness keepeth him that is upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.

- The lazy soul desires and has nothing, but the diligent soul is made fat. Desire without labor stays empty.
- The righteous man hates lying, but the wicked is loathsome and comes to shame. Righteousness keeps the upright, while wickedness overthrows the sinner.

**[Proverbs 13:7-8](/bible/proverbs/13#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/13#v7)</sup> There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/13#v8)</sup> The ransom of a man’s life are his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke.

- Some make themselves rich yet have nothing, and some make themselves poor yet have great riches. This is the man who forsakes things when he comes into the truth, giving up sponsorships and possessions, yet he still has great riches because the kingdom of heaven is coming to him.
- A man's riches may be the ransom of his life, but the poor does not even hear rebuke.

**[Proverbs 13:9-11](/bible/proverbs/13#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/13#v9)</sup> The light of the righteous rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)</sup> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/13#v11)</sup> Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase.

- The light of the righteous rejoices, but the lamp of the wicked is put out. Only by pride comes contention, but with the well-advised is wisdom, which points to seeking counsel at all times.
- Wealth gotten by vanity is diminished, but he that gathers by labor increases. Those chasing the fast dollar and fast money will see all those riches diminish.

**[Proverbs 13:12-14](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)</sup> Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/13#v13)</sup> Whoso despiseth the word shall be destroyed: but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/13#v14)</sup> The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.

- Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but when the desire comes it is a tree of life.
- Whoever despises the word is destroyed, but he that fears the commandment is rewarded. The law of the wise is a fountain of life to depart from the snares of death.

**[Proverbs 13:15-17](/bible/proverbs/13#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/13#v15)</sup> Good understanding giveth favour: but the way of transgressors is hard.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/13#v16)</sup> Every prudent man dealeth with knowledge: but a fool layeth open his folly.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/13#v17)</sup> A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health.

- Good understanding gives favor, but the way of transgressors is hard. Every prudent man deals with knowledge, but a fool lays his folly open for all to see.
- A wicked messenger falls into mischief, but a faithful ambassador is health.

**[Proverbs 13:18-20](/bible/proverbs/13#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/13#v18)</sup> Poverty and shame shall be to him that refuseth instruction: but he that regardeth reproof shall be honoured.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/13#v19)</sup> The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but it is abomination to fools to depart from evil.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/13#v20)</sup> He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed.

- Poverty and shame come to the one who refuses instruction, but he that regards reproof is honored.
- He that walks with wise men shall be wise, but a companion of fools is destroyed. Every beast cleaves to its like: around fools you become a fool, around wise men you become wise.

**[Proverbs 13:21-23](/bible/proverbs/13#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/13#v21)</sup> Evil pursueth sinners: but to the righteous good shall be repayed.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/13#v22)</sup> A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children’s children: and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/13#v23)</sup> Much food is in the tillage of the poor: but there is that is destroyed for want of judgment.

- Evil pursues sinners, but the righteous are repaid with good. A good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children, and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.
- This is powerful for us as a nation. A lot of black people do not like to talk about death, but get your life insurance and leave an inheritance to your children's children. That is how far ahead we have to think.

**[Proverbs 13:24-25](/bible/proverbs/13#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/13#v24)</sup> He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/13#v25)</sup> The righteous eateth to the satisfying of his soul: but the belly of the wicked shall want.

- He that spares his rod hates his son, but he that loves him disciplines him early. Discipline your children. Esau has set it up so no one wants anyone to discipline our kids, but the discipline is a bullet.
- The righteous eats to the satisfying of his soul, but the belly of the wicked shall want.
