Read the chapter → [Proverbs 28](/bible/proverbs/28)

This closing chapter contrasts the wicked and the righteous, the lawkeeper and the lawforsaker, the honest poor and the man of unjust gain. The teacher shows that boldness comes from being with Christ and speaking the words of the Most High, that keeping the commandments prolongs the throne of Israel, and that mercy comes to the one who confesses and forsakes his sins.

**[Proverbs 28:1-2](/bible/proverbs/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/28#v1)</sup> The wicked flee when no man pursueth: but the righteous are bold as a lion.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/28#v2)</sup> For the transgression of a land many are the princes thereof: but by a man of understanding and knowledge the state thereof shall be prolonged.

- The wicked flee when no man pursues, but the righteous are bold as a lion. Peter and John were perceived as unlearned men, yet their boldness made men take knowledge that they had been with Christ, who taught as one having authority. We can be bold this same way by using the words of the Most High, which no one can gainsay.
- For the transgression of a land many are its princes, but by a man of understanding and knowledge its state shall be prolonged. That understanding and knowledge is the Most High's commandments; David told Solomon that keeping the commandments keeps a man on the throne of Israel.
  - **[Acts 4:13](/bible/acts/4#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/acts/4#v13)</sup> Now when they saw the boldness of Peter and John, and perceived that they were unlearned and ignorant men, they marvelled; and they took knowledge of them, that they had been with Jesus.

    Seeing the boldness of Peter and John, unlearned men, they took knowledge that they had been with Jesus.
  - **[1 Kings 2:2-4](/bible/1-kings/2#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-kings/2#v2)</sup> I go the way of all the earth: be thou strong therefore, and shew thyself a man;
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-kings/2#v3)</sup> And keep the charge of the Lord thy God, to walk in his ways, to keep his statutes, and his commandments, and his judgments, and his testimonies, as it is written in the law of Moses, that thou mayest prosper in all that thou doest, and whithersoever thou turnest thyself:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-kings/2#v4)</sup> That the Lord may continue his word which he spake concerning me, saying, If thy children take heed to their way, to walk before me in truth with all their heart and with all their soul, there shall not fail thee (said he) a man on the throne of Israel.

    Keep the charge of the Most High and walk in his ways, and if your children take heed there shall not fail you a man on the throne of Israel.

**[Proverbs 28:3-6](/bible/proverbs/28#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/28#v3)</sup> A poor man that oppresseth the poor is like a sweeping rain which leaveth no food.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/28#v4)</sup> They that forsake the law praise the wicked: but such as keep the law contend with them.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/28#v5)</sup> Evil men understand not judgment: but they that seek the Lord understand all things.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/28#v6)</sup> Better is the poor that walketh in his uprightness, than he that is perverse in his ways, though he be rich.

- A poor man that oppresses the poor is like a sweeping rain that leaves no food. They that forsake the law praise the wicked, but such as keep the law contend with them, as we do daily in these scriptures.
- Evil men do not understand judgment, but they that seek the Most High understand all things. Better is the poor who walks uprightly than the perverse rich man.

**[Proverbs 28:7-9](/bible/proverbs/28#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/28#v7)</sup> Whoso keepeth the law is a wise son: but he that is a companion of riotous men shameth his father.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8)</sup> He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/28#v9)</sup> He that turneth away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be abomination.

- Whoever keeps the law is a wise son, but a companion of riotous men shames his father. He that increases his substance by usury and unjust gain gathers it for one who will pity the poor.
- He that turns away his ear from hearing the law, even his prayer shall be an abomination, as [John 9](/bible/john/9) says the Most High does not hear sinners.
  - **[John 9:31](/bible/john/9#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/john/9#v31)</sup> Now we know that God heareth not sinners: but if any man be a worshipper of God, and doeth his will, him he heareth.

    The Most High hears not sinners, but the worshipper that does his will.

**[Proverbs 28:10-14](/bible/proverbs/28#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/28#v10)</sup> Whoso causeth the righteous to go astray in an evil way, he shall fall himself into his own pit: but the upright shall have good things in possession.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/28#v11)</sup> The rich man is wise in his own conceit; but the poor that hath understanding searcheth him out.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/28#v12)</sup> When righteous men do rejoice, there is great glory: but when the wicked rise, a man is hidden.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/28#v13)</sup> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/28#v14)</sup> Happy is the man that feareth alway: but he that hardeneth his heart shall fall into mischief.

- Whoever causes the righteous to go astray in an evil way shall fall into his own pit, but the upright shall have good things. The rich man is wise in his own conceit, but the poor that has understanding searches him out.
- He that covers his sins shall not prosper, but whoever confesses and forsakes them shall have mercy. The Most High has mercy on those that forsake their sins; that is why we must repent, Israel. Happy is the man that fears always, but he that hardens his heart falls into mischief.

**[Proverbs 28:15-18](/bible/proverbs/28#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/28#v15)</sup> As a roaring lion, and a ranging bear; so is a wicked ruler over the poor people.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/28#v16)</sup> The prince that wanteth understanding is also a great oppressor: but he that hateth covetousness shall prolong his days.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/28#v17)</sup> A man that doeth violence to the blood of any person shall flee to the pit; let no man stay him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/28#v18)</sup> Whoso walketh uprightly shall be saved: but he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once.

- As a roaring lion and a raging bear, so is a wicked ruler over the people. The prince that lacks understanding is a great oppressor, but he that hates covetousness shall prolong his days.
- A man that does violence to the blood of any person shall flee to the pit; let no man stay him. Whoever walks uprightly shall be saved, but he that is perverse in his ways shall fall at once. Our people cannot say "I'm saved," for he that endures to the end shall be saved; [Ezekiel 18](/bible/ezekiel/18) shows that if your last act is evil, the Most High will not remember the good you did before.

**[Proverbs 28:19-22](/bible/proverbs/28#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/28#v19)</sup> He that tilleth his land shall have plenty of bread: but he that followeth after vain persons shall have poverty enough.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/28#v20)</sup> A faithful man shall abound with blessings: but he that maketh haste to be rich shall not be innocent.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/28#v21)</sup> To have respect of persons is not good: for for a piece of bread that man will transgress.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/28#v22)</sup> He that hasteth to be rich hath an evil eye, and considereth not that poverty shall come upon him.

- He that tills his land shall have plenty of bread, but he that follows vain persons shall have poverty enough. Tilling the land is hard work, and the Most High says hate not laborious work; chasing vain persons means always being on some get-rich-quick scheme that does not work.
- A faithful man shall abound with blessings, but he that makes haste to be rich shall not be innocent. To have respect of persons is not good, for a piece of bread that man will transgress. He that hastens to be rich has an evil eye and does not consider that poverty will come; if you chase quick riches you care nothing for your victims.

**[Proverbs 28:23-25](/bible/proverbs/28#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/28#v23)</sup> He that rebuketh a man afterwards shall find more favour than he that flattereth with the tongue.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/28#v24)</sup> Whoso robbeth his father or his mother, and saith, It is no transgression; the same is the companion of a destroyer.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/28#v25)</sup> He that is of a proud heart stirreth up strife: but he that putteth his trust in the Lord shall be made fat.

- He that rebukes a man shall afterward find more favor than he that flatters with the tongue.
- Whoever robs his father or mother and says it is no transgression is a companion of a destroyer. Think of the times, even as grown people, some went into a mother's purse or stole from a father; we must repent from these things, Israel.
- He that is of a proud heart stirs up strife, but he that puts his trust in the Most High shall be made fat.

**[Proverbs 28:26-28](/bible/proverbs/28#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/28#v26)</sup> He that trusteth in his own heart is a fool: but whoso walketh wisely, he shall be delivered.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/28#v27)</sup> He that giveth unto the poor shall not lack: but he that hideth his eyes shall have many a curse.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/28#v28)</sup> When the wicked rise, men hide themselves: but when they perish, the righteous increase.

- He that trusts in his own heart is a fool, but whoever walks wisely shall be delivered.
- He that gives to the poor shall not lack, but he that hides his eyes shall have many a curse. Ecclesiasticus and the law say the same: do not turn your eye from the needy, and open your hand wide to your poor brother, for if he curses you in the bitterness of his soul his prayer is heard. [Sirach 12](/bible/sirach/12) says if you do good, know to whom you do it, and the law commands us to help our brothers who keep the commandments.
- When the wicked rise, men hide themselves, but when they perish the righteous increase.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 4:4-5](/bible/sirach/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/4#v4)</sup> Reject not the supplication of the afflicted; neither turn away thy face from a poor man.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/4#v5)</sup> Turn not away thine eye from the needy, and give him none occasion to curse thee:

    Do not turn your face or eye from the afflicted and needy, lest they curse you and be heard.
  - **[Deuteronomy 15:7-8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v7)</sup> If there be among you a poor man of one of thy brethren within any of thy gates in thy land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, thou shalt not harden thine heart, nor shut thine hand from thy poor brother:
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v8)</sup> But thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need, in that which he wanteth.

    Do not harden your heart or shut your hand from your poor brother, but open your hand wide and lend him sufficient for his need.
