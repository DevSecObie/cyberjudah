Read the chapter → [Proverbs 13](/bible/proverbs/13)

This session is a "four chapters a day" full-chapter reading covering [Proverbs 13](/bible/proverbs/13) through 16. The teacher reads straight through the chapters and stops to press home the recurring wisdom themes: guarding your mouth, seeking counsel, keeping the company of wise men, disciplining your children, and leaving an inheritance to your children's children. He frames the whole reading as staying meditated in the word a little at a time so you retain it.

**[Proverbs 13:1-3](/bible/proverbs/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/13#v1)</sup> A wise son heareth his father’s instruction: but a scorner heareth not rebuke.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/13#v2)</sup> A man shall eat good by the fruit of his mouth: but the soul of the transgressors shall eat violence.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/13#v3)</sup> He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction.

- A wise son takes his father's instruction, but a scorner will not even hear rebuke. What you do with correction marks whether you are wise or a scorner.
- The scriptures constantly tell you to watch your mouth. Everything you say you will give account for, so he that guards his mouth keeps his life while the one who opens his lips wide brings destruction.

**[Proverbs 13:4-6](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/13#v4)</sup> The soul of the sluggard desireth, and hath nothing: but the soul of the diligent shall be made fat.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/13#v5)</sup> A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/13#v6)</sup> Righteousness keepeth him that is upright in the way: but wickedness overthroweth the sinner.

- The lazy soul desires and has nothing, but the diligent soul is made fat. Desire without labor stays empty.
- The righteous man hates lying, but the wicked is loathsome and comes to shame. Righteousness keeps the upright, while wickedness overthrows the sinner.

**[Proverbs 13:7-8](/bible/proverbs/13#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/13#v7)</sup> There is that maketh himself rich, yet hath nothing: there is that maketh himself poor, yet hath great riches.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/13#v8)</sup> The ransom of a man’s life are his riches: but the poor heareth not rebuke.

- Some make themselves rich yet have nothing, and some make themselves poor yet have great riches. This is the man who forsakes things when he comes into the truth, giving up sponsorships and possessions, yet he still has great riches because the kingdom of heaven is coming to him.
- A man's riches may be the ransom of his life, but the poor does not even hear rebuke.

**[Proverbs 13:9-11](/bible/proverbs/13#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/13#v9)</sup> The light of the righteous rejoiceth: but the lamp of the wicked shall be put out.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)</sup> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/13#v11)</sup> Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase.

- The light of the righteous rejoices, but the lamp of the wicked is put out. Only by pride comes contention, but with the well-advised is wisdom, which points to seeking counsel at all times.
- Wealth gotten by vanity is diminished, but he that gathers by labor increases. Those chasing the fast dollar and fast money will see all those riches diminish.

**[Proverbs 13:12-14](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/13#v12)</sup> Hope deferred maketh the heart sick: but when the desire cometh, it is a tree of life.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/13#v13)</sup> Whoso despiseth the word shall be destroyed: but he that feareth the commandment shall be rewarded.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/13#v14)</sup> The law of the wise is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.

- Hope deferred makes the heart sick, but when the desire comes it is a tree of life.
- Whoever despises the word is destroyed, but he that fears the commandment is rewarded. The law of the wise is a fountain of life to depart from the snares of death.

**[Proverbs 13:15-17](/bible/proverbs/13#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/13#v15)</sup> Good understanding giveth favour: but the way of transgressors is hard.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/13#v16)</sup> Every prudent man dealeth with knowledge: but a fool layeth open his folly.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/13#v17)</sup> A wicked messenger falleth into mischief: but a faithful ambassador is health.

- Good understanding gives favor, but the way of transgressors is hard. Every prudent man deals with knowledge, but a fool lays his folly open for all to see.
- A wicked messenger falls into mischief, but a faithful ambassador is health.

**[Proverbs 13:18-20](/bible/proverbs/13#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/13#v18)</sup> Poverty and shame shall be to him that refuseth instruction: but he that regardeth reproof shall be honoured.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/13#v19)</sup> The desire accomplished is sweet to the soul: but it is abomination to fools to depart from evil.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/13#v20)</sup> He that walketh with wise men shall be wise: but a companion of fools shall be destroyed.

- Poverty and shame come to the one who refuses instruction, but he that regards reproof is honored.
- He that walks with wise men shall be wise, but a companion of fools is destroyed. Every beast cleaves to its like: around fools you become a fool, around wise men you become wise.

**[Proverbs 13:21-23](/bible/proverbs/13#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/13#v21)</sup> Evil pursueth sinners: but to the righteous good shall be repayed.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/13#v22)</sup> A good man leaveth an inheritance to his children’s children: and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/13#v23)</sup> Much food is in the tillage of the poor: but there is that is destroyed for want of judgment.

- Evil pursues sinners, but the righteous are repaid with good. A good man leaves an inheritance to his children's children, and the wealth of the sinner is laid up for the just.
- This is powerful for us as a nation. A lot of black people do not like to talk about death, but get your life insurance and leave an inheritance to your children's children. That is how far ahead we have to think.

**[Proverbs 13:24-25](/bible/proverbs/13#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/13#v24)</sup> He that spareth his rod hateth his son: but he that loveth him chasteneth him betimes.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/13#v25)</sup> The righteous eateth to the satisfying of his soul: but the belly of the wicked shall want.

- He that spares his rod hates his son, but he that loves him disciplines him early. Discipline your children. Esau has set it up so no one wants anyone to discipline our kids, but the discipline is a bullet.
- The righteous eats to the satisfying of his soul, but the belly of the wicked shall want.

# Proverbs 14: Righteousness Exalts a Nation

Read the chapter → [Proverbs 14](/bible/proverbs/14)

**[Proverbs 14:1-3](/bible/proverbs/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/14#v1)</sup> Every wise woman buildeth her house: but the foolish plucketh it down with her hands.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/14#v2)</sup> He that walketh in his uprightness feareth the Lord: but he that is perverse in his ways despiseth him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/14#v3)</sup> In the mouth of the foolish is a rod of pride: but the lips of the wise shall preserve them.

- Every wise woman builds her house, but the foolish plucks it down with her own hands.
- He that walks in his uprightness fears the Most High, but he that is perverse in his ways despises him. If you are not keeping the commandments, you hate God.

**[Proverbs 14:4-6](/bible/proverbs/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/14#v4)</sup> Where no oxen are, the crib is clean: but much increase is by the strength of the ox.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/14#v5)</sup> A faithful witness will not lie: but a false witness will utter lies.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/14#v6)</sup> A scorner seeketh wisdom, and findeth it not: but knowledge is easy unto him that understandeth.

- Where no oxen are the crib is clean, but much increase comes by the strength of the ox.
- A faithful witness will not lie, but a false witness utters lies. A scorner seeks wisdom and cannot find it, but knowledge is easy to the one who understands. To get the understanding you fear the Most High and keep the commandments.

  - **[Psalm 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

    The fear of the Most High is the beginning of wisdom.

**[Proverbs 14:7-9](/bible/proverbs/14#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/14#v7)</sup> Go from the presence of a foolish man, when thou perceivest not in him the lips of knowledge.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/14#v8)</sup> The wisdom of the prudent is to understand his way: but the folly of fools is deceit.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/14#v9)</sup> Fools make a mock at sin: but among the righteous there is favour.

- Go from the presence of a foolish man when you perceive there are no lips of knowledge in him. This is why the scripture says to observe the time when you are among the indiscreet.
- The wisdom of the prudent is to understand his way, but the folly of fools is deceit. Fools make a mock at sin, but among the righteous there is favor.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 27:12](/bible/sirach/27#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/27#v12)</sup> If thou be among the indiscreet, observe the time; but be continually among men of understanding.

    Observe the time when you are among the indiscreet.

**[Proverbs 14:10-12](/bible/proverbs/14#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/14#v10)</sup> The heart knoweth his own bitterness; and a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/14#v11)</sup> The house of the wicked shall be overthrown: but the tabernacle of the upright shall flourish.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/14#v12)</sup> There is a way which seemeth right unto a man, but the end thereof are the ways of death.

- The heart knows its own bitterness, and a stranger does not share in its joy.
- The house of the wicked is overthrown, but the tabernacle of the upright flourishes. There is a way that seems right to a man, but the end of it is the way of death.

**[Proverbs 14:13-15](/bible/proverbs/14#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/14#v13)</sup> Even in laughter the heart is sorrowful; and the end of that mirth is heaviness.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/14#v14)</sup> The backslider in heart shall be filled with his own ways: and a good man shall be satisfied from himself.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/14#v15)</sup> The simple believeth every word: but the prudent man looketh well to his going.

- Even in laughter the heart can be sorrowful, and the end of that mirth is heaviness.
- The simple believes every word, but the prudent man looks well to his going.

**[Proverbs 14:16-18](/bible/proverbs/14#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/14#v16)</sup> A wise man feareth, and departeth from evil: but the fool rageth, and is confident.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/14#v17)</sup> He that is soon angry dealeth foolishly: and a man of wicked devices is hated.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/14#v18)</sup> The simple inherit folly: but the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

- A wise man fears and departs from evil, but the fool rages and is confident. He that is soon angry deals foolishly.
- The simple inherit folly, but the prudent are crowned with knowledge.

**[Proverbs 14:19-20](/bible/proverbs/14#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/14#v19)</sup> The evil bow before the good; and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/14#v20)</sup> The poor is hated even of his own neighbour: but the rich hath many friends.

- The evil bow before the good, and the wicked at the gates of the righteous.
- The poor is hated even by his own neighbor, but the rich have many friends. Ecclesiasticus says the same: when the rich man speaks everybody holds their tongue, but when the poor man speaks they ask who is this.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 13:23](/bible/sirach/13#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/13#v23)</sup> When a rich man speaketh, every man holdeth his tongue, and, look, what he saith, they extol it to the clouds: but if the poor man speak, they say, What fellow is this? and if he stumble, they will help to overthrow him.

    When a rich man speaks all listen, but the poor man is dismissed.

**[Proverbs 14:21-23](/bible/proverbs/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/14#v21)</sup> He that despiseth his neighbour sinneth: but he that hath mercy on the poor, happy is he.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/14#v22)</sup> Do they not err that devise evil? but mercy and truth shall be to them that devise good.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)</sup> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

- He that despises his neighbor sins, but he that has mercy on the poor is happy.
- Those who devise evil go astray, but mercy and truth belong to those who devise good. In all labor there is profit, but mere talk of the lips tends only to poverty.

**[Proverbs 14:24-26](/bible/proverbs/14#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/14#v24)</sup> The crown of the wise is their riches: but the foolishness of fools is folly.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/14#v25)</sup> A true witness delivereth souls: but a deceitful witness speaketh lies.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/14#v26)</sup> In the fear of the Lord is strong confidence: and his children shall have a place of refuge.

- The crown of the wise is their riches, but the foolishness of fools is folly. A true witness delivers souls, but a deceitful witness speaks lies.
- In the fear of the Most High is strong confidence, and his children shall have a place of refuge.

**[Proverbs 14:27-29](/bible/proverbs/14#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/14#v27)</sup> The fear of the Lord is a fountain of life, to depart from the snares of death.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/14#v28)</sup> In the multitude of people is the king’s honour: but in the want of people is the destruction of the prince.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/14#v29)</sup> He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.

- The fear of the Most High is a fountain of life to depart from the snares of death.
- In the multitude of people is the king's honor, but in the lack of people is the destruction of the prince. He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding, but the hasty of spirit exalts folly.

**[Proverbs 14:30-32](/bible/proverbs/14#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/14#v30)</sup> A sound heart is the life of the flesh: but envy the rottenness of the bones.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/14#v31)</sup> He that oppresseth the poor reproacheth his Maker: but he that honoureth him hath mercy on the poor.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/14#v32)</sup> The wicked is driven away in his wickedness: but the righteous hath hope in his death.

- A sound heart is the life of the flesh, but envy is the rottenness of the bones.
- He that oppresses the poor reproaches his Maker, but he that honors him has mercy on the poor. The wicked is driven away in his wickedness, but the righteous has hope in his death.

**[Proverbs 14:33-35](/bible/proverbs/14#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/14#v33)</sup> Wisdom resteth in the heart of him that hath understanding: but that which is in the midst of fools is made known.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/14#v34)</sup> Righteousness exalteth a nation: but sin is a reproach to any people.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/proverbs/14#v35)</sup> The king’s favour is toward a wise servant: but his wrath is against him that causeth shame.

- Wisdom rests in the heart of the one who has understanding, but what is in the midst of fools is made known.
- Righteousness exalts a nation, but sin is a reproach to any people. God's laws will exalt us as a nation. Why do we go through slavery and sit at the bottom of the barrel? Sin. Sin is a reproach to any people.
- The king's favor is toward the wise servant, but his wrath is against the one who causes shame.
