**[Proverbs 19:1-4](/bible/proverbs/19#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/19#v1)</sup> Better is the poor that walketh in his integrity, than he that is perverse in his lips, and is a fool.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/19#v2)</sup> Also, that the soul be without knowledge, it is not good; and he that hasteth with his feet sinneth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/19#v3)</sup> The foolishness of man perverteth his way: and his heart fretteth against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/19#v4)</sup> Wealth maketh many friends; but the poor is separated from his neighbour.

- Better is the poor that walks in his integrity than he that is perverse in his lips and is a fool.
- That the soul be without knowledge is not good, and he that hastes with his feet sins; the foolish man perverts his way then his heart frets against the Lord.
- Wealth makes many friends, but the poor is separated from his neighbor. When goods increase, so do those that eat them.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 5:11](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v11)</sup> When goods increase, they are increased that eat them: and what good is there to the owners thereof, saving the beholding of them with their eyes?

    When goods increase, they are increased that eat them; the owner only beholds them with his eyes.

**[Proverbs 19:5-9](/bible/proverbs/19#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/19#v5)</sup> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall not escape.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/19#v6)</sup> Many will intreat the favour of the prince: and every man is a friend to him that giveth gifts.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/19#v7)</sup> All the brethren of the poor do hate him: how much more do his friends go far from him? he pursueth them with words, yet they are wanting to him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/19#v8)</sup> He that getteth wisdom loveth his own soul: he that keepeth understanding shall find good.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/19#v9)</sup> A false witness shall not be unpunished, and he that speaketh lies shall perish.

- A false witness shall not be unpunished and he that speaks lies shall not escape.
- Every man is a friend to him that gives gifts, but all the brethren of the poor hate him and his friends go far from him.
- He that gets wisdom loves his own soul, and he that keeps understanding shall find good.

**[Proverbs 19:10-14](/bible/proverbs/19#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/19#v10)</sup> Delight is not seemly for a fool; much less for a servant to have rule over princes.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/19#v11)</sup> The discretion of a man deferreth his anger; and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/19#v12)</sup> The king’s wrath is as the roaring of a lion; but his favour is as dew upon the grass.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/19#v13)</sup> A foolish son is the calamity of his father: and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/19#v14)</sup> House and riches are the inheritance of fathers: and a prudent wife is from the Lord.

- Delight is not seemly for a fool, much less for a servant to have rule over princes; and right now our servants are having rule over us.
- The discretion of a man defers his anger, and it is his glory to pass over a transgression.
- A foolish son is the calamity of his father, and the contentions of a wife are a continual dropping; Solomon knew this, and his own son Rehoboam was a foolish young man who lacked his father's wisdom.
- House and riches are the inheritance of fathers, but a prudent wife is from the Lord, called a gift.

**[Proverbs 19:15-18](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)</sup> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)</sup> He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/19#v17)</sup> He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the Lord; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/19#v18)</sup> Chasten thy son while there is hope, and let not thy soul spare for his crying.

- Slothfulness casts into a deep sleep and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.
- He that keeps the commandments keeps his own soul, but he that despises his ways shall die.
- He that has pity on the poor lends unto the Lord, and the Lord will repay him.
- Chasten thy son while there is hope and let not thy soul spare for his crying. Esau has continually tried to take away our ability to rightfully punish our children and bring them up in the nurture and admonition of the Lord.
- Discipline must start in the youth. If you wait until twelve or thirteen to begin, it is over, or you will have a very difficult time.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 30:12](/bible/sirach/30#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/30#v12)</sup> Bow down his neck while he is young, and beat him on the sides while he is a child, lest he wax stubborn, and be disobedient unto thee, and so bring sorrow to thine heart.

    Discipline must start in the youth.

**[Proverbs 19:19-24](/bible/proverbs/19#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/19#v19)</sup> A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment: for if thou deliver him, yet thou must do it again.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/19#v20)</sup> Hear counsel, and receive instruction, that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/19#v21)</sup> There are many devices in a man’s heart; nevertheless the counsel of the Lord, that shall stand.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/19#v22)</sup> The desire of a man is his kindness: and a poor man is better than a liar.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/19#v23)</sup> The fear of the Lord tendeth to life: and he that hath it shall abide satisfied; he shall not be visited with evil.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/19#v24)</sup> A slothful man hideth his hand in his bosom, and will not so much as bring it to his mouth again.

- A man of great wrath shall suffer punishment; hear counsel and receive instruction that thou mayest be wise in thy latter end.
- There are many devices in a man's heart, but the counsel of the Lord shall stand. This is why the heart is called deceitful above all things.
- The fear of the Lord tends to life, and he that has it shall abide satisfied and not be visited with evil.
  - **[Jeremiah 17:9](/bible/jeremiah/17#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/17#v9)</sup> The heart is deceitful above all things, and desperately wicked: who can know it?

    The heart is deceitful above all things and desperately wicked; who can know it?

**[Proverbs 19:25-29](/bible/proverbs/19#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/19#v25)</sup> Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding, and he will understand knowledge.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/19#v26)</sup> He that wasteth his father, and chaseth away his mother, is a son that causeth shame, and bringeth reproach.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/19#v27)</sup> Cease, my son, to hear the instruction that causeth to err from the words of knowledge.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/19#v28)</sup> An ungodly witness scorneth judgment: and the mouth of the wicked devoureth iniquity.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/19#v29)</sup> Judgments are prepared for scorners, and stripes for the back of fools.

- Smite a scorner and the simple will beware; reprove one that has understanding and he will understand knowledge.
- He that wastes his father and chases away his mother is a son that causes shame and brings reproach.
- Judgments are prepared for scorners and stripes for the backs of fools. The Most High is preparing us for the kingdom; he will beat that foolishness out of us.
- Bread, correction, and work are for a servant. We have been brought down to the serving class because of sin, because of disobedience to God's commandments.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:24](/bible/sirach/33#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/33#v24)</sup> Fodder, a wand, and burdens, are for the ass; and bread, correction, and work, for a servant. .

    We were brought down to the serving class because of sin.
  - **[Jeremiah 2:14](/bible/jeremiah/2#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/2#v14)</sup> Is Israel a servant? is he a homeborn slave? why is he spoiled?

    Is Israel a servant? Is he a homeborn slave? Why is he spoiled? Because of disobedience.
