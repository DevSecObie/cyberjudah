**[Proverbs 3:1-4](/bible/proverbs/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/3#v1)</sup> My son, forget not my law; but let thine heart keep my commandments:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/3#v2)</sup> For length of days, and long life, and peace, shall they add to thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/3#v3)</sup> Let not mercy and truth forsake thee: bind them about thy neck; write them upon the table of thine heart:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/3#v4)</sup> So shalt thou find favour and good understanding in the sight of God and man.

- My son, forget not my law, and let thine heart keep my commandments, for length of days, long life, and peace shall they add to thee.
- Let not mercy and truth forsake thee; bind them about thy neck and write them upon the table of thine heart, so shalt thou find favor and good understanding in the sight of the Most High and man.

**[Proverbs 3:5-8](/bible/proverbs/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/3#v5)</sup> Trust in the Lord with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/3#v6)</sup> In all thy ways acknowledge him, and he shall direct thy paths.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/3#v7)</sup> Be not wise in thine own eyes: fear the Lord, and depart from evil.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/3#v8)</sup> It shall be health to thy navel, and marrow to thy bones.

- Trust in the Most High with all thine heart and lean not unto thine own understanding. In all thy ways acknowledge Him and He shall direct thy paths.
- Be not wise in thine own eyes; fear the Most High and depart from evil. It shall be health to thy navel and marrow to thy bones.

**[Proverbs 3:9-12](/bible/proverbs/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/3#v9)</sup> Honour the Lord with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/3#v10)</sup> So shall thy barns be filled with plenty, and thy presses shall burst out with new wine.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/3#v11)</sup> My son, despise not the chastening of the Lord; neither be weary of his correction:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/3#v12)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he correcteth; even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

- Honor the Most High with thy substance and with the firstfruits of all thine increase, so shall thy barns be filled with plenty and thy presses shall burst out with new wine.
- My son, despise not the chastening of the Most High, neither be weary of His correction, for whom the Most High loveth He correcteth, even as a father the son in whom he delighteth.

**[Proverbs 3:13-18](/bible/proverbs/3#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/3#v13)</sup> Happy is the man that findeth wisdom, and the man that getteth understanding.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/3#v14)</sup> For the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver, and the gain thereof than fine gold.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/3#v15)</sup> She is more precious than rubies: and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/3#v16)</sup> Length of days is in her right hand; and in her left hand riches and honour.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/3#v17)</sup> Her ways are ways of pleasantness, and all her paths are peace.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/3#v18)</sup> She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her: and happy is every one that retaineth her.

- Happy is the man that findeth wisdom and the man that getteth understanding, for the merchandise of it is better than the merchandise of silver and the gain thereof than fine gold. Wisdom far, far exceeds riches, because we read in verse 1 of chapter 1 about those that are greedy of gain; the only gain you should be greedy of is keeping the commandments and growing in your spirit.
- She is more precious than rubies, and all the things thou canst desire are not to be compared unto her. Nothing can be compared to the wisdom of the Most High.
- Length of days is in her right hand, and in her left hand riches and honor. Her ways are ways of pleasantness and all her paths are peace. She is a tree of life to them that lay hold upon her, and happy is everyone that retaineth her.

**[Proverbs 3:19-24](/bible/proverbs/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/3#v19)</sup> The Lord by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding hath he established the heavens.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/3#v20)</sup> By his knowledge the depths are broken up, and the clouds drop down the dew.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/3#v21)</sup> My son, let not them depart from thine eyes: keep sound wisdom and discretion:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/3#v22)</sup> So shall they be life unto thy soul, and grace to thy neck.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/3#v23)</sup> Then shalt thou walk in thy way safely, and thy foot shall not stumble.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/3#v24)</sup> When thou liest down, thou shalt not be afraid: yea, thou shalt lie down, and thy sleep shall be sweet.

- The Most High by wisdom hath founded the earth; by understanding He hath established the heavens; by His knowledge the depths are broken up and the clouds drop down the dew.
- My son, let not them depart from thine eyes; keep sound wisdom and discretion, so shall they be life unto thy soul and grace to thy neck.
- Then shalt thou walk in thy way safely and thy foot shall not stumble; when thou liest down thou shalt not be afraid, and thy sleep shall be sweet.

**[Proverbs 3:25-30](/bible/proverbs/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/3#v25)</sup> Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked, when it cometh.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/3#v26)</sup> For the Lord shall be thy confidence, and shall keep thy foot from being taken.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/3#v27)</sup> Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/3#v28)</sup> Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again, and to morrow I will give; when thou hast it by thee.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/3#v29)</sup> Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/3#v30)</sup> Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm.

- Be not afraid of sudden fear, neither of the desolation of the wicked when it cometh, for the Most High shall be thy confidence and shall keep thy foot from being taken.
- Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it. Say not unto thy neighbor go and come again and tomorrow I will give, when thou hast it by thee.
- Devise not evil against thy neighbor, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee, and strive not with a man without cause if he have done thee no harm.

**[Proverbs 3:31-35](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/3#v32)</sup> For the froward is abomination to the Lord: but his secret is with the righteous.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/3#v33)</sup> The curse of the Lord is in the house of the wicked: but he blesseth the habitation of the just.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/3#v34)</sup> Surely he scorneth the scorners: but he giveth grace unto the lowly.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/proverbs/3#v35)</sup> The wise shall inherit glory: but shame shall be the promotion of fools.

- Envy thou not the oppressor and choose none of his ways, for the forward is abomination to the Most High, but His secret is with the righteous.
- The curse of the Most High is in the house of the wicked, but He blesseth the habitation of the just. Surely He scorneth the scorners, but He giveth grace unto the lowly.
- The wise shall inherit glory, but shame shall be the promotion of fools.
