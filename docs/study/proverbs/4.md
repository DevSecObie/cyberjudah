**[Proverbs 4:1-4](/bible/proverbs/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/4#v1)</sup> Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/4#v2)</sup> For I give you good doctrine, forsake ye not my law.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/4#v3)</sup> For I was my father’s son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/4#v4)</sup> He taught me also, and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words: keep my commandments, and live.

- Hear, ye children, the instruction of a father, and attend to know understanding, for I give you good doctrine; forsake ye not my law.
- For I was my father's son, tender and only beloved in the sight of my mother; he taught me also and said unto me, Let thine heart retain my words, keep my commandments and live.

**[Proverbs 4:5-9](/bible/proverbs/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/4#v5)</sup> Get wisdom, get understanding: forget it not; neither decline from the words of my mouth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/4#v6)</sup> Forsake her not, and she shall preserve thee: love her, and she shall keep thee.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/4#v7)</sup> Wisdom is the principal thing; therefore get wisdom: and with all thy getting get understanding.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/4#v8)</sup> Exalt her, and she shall promote thee: she shall bring thee to honour, when thou dost embrace her.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/4#v9)</sup> She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace: a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

- Get wisdom, get understanding; forget it not, neither decline from the words of my mouth. Forsake her not and she shall preserve thee; love her and she shall keep thee.
- Wisdom is the principal thing, therefore get wisdom, and with all thy getting get understanding. Exalt her and she shall promote thee; she shall bring thee to honor when thou dost embrace her.
- She shall give to thine head an ornament of grace and a crown of glory shall she deliver to thee.

**[Proverbs 4:10-13](/bible/proverbs/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/4#v10)</sup> Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings; and the years of thy life shall be many.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/4#v11)</sup> I have taught thee in the way of wisdom; I have led thee in right paths.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/4#v12)</sup> When thou goest, thy steps shall not be straitened; and when thou runnest, thou shalt not stumble.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/4#v13)</sup> Take fast hold of instruction; let her not go: keep her; for she is thy life.

- Hear, O my son, and receive my sayings, and the years of thy life shall be many. I have taught thee in the way of wisdom and led thee in right paths.
- When thou goest thy steps shall not be straitened, and when thou runnest thou shalt not stumble. Take fast hold of instruction, let her not go, keep her, for she is thy life. The commandments are our life.

**[Proverbs 4:14-19](/bible/proverbs/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/4#v14)</sup> Enter not into the path of the wicked, and go not in the way of evil men.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/4#v15)</sup> Avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/4#v16)</sup> For they sleep not, except they have done mischief; and their sleep is taken away, unless they cause some to fall.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/4#v17)</sup> For they eat the bread of wickedness, and drink the wine of violence.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/4#v18)</sup> But the path of the just is as the shining light, that shineth more and more unto the perfect day.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/4#v19)</sup> The way of the wicked is as darkness: they know not at what they stumble.

- Enter not into the path of the wicked and go not in the way of evil men; avoid it, pass not by it, turn from it, and pass away. This is the same thing we read in [Proverbs 1:16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16).
- For they sleep not except they have done mischief, and their sleep is taken away unless they cause some to fall, for they eat the bread of wickedness and drink the wine of violence.
- The path of the just is as the shining light that shineth more and more unto the perfect day, but the way of the wicked is as darkness; they know not at what they stumble.
  - **[Proverbs 1:16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/1#v16)</sup> For their feet run to evil, and make haste to shed blood.

    For their feet run to evil and make haste to shed blood, the same wicked path Solomon says to avoid and turn from.

**[Proverbs 4:20-24](/bible/proverbs/4#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/4#v20)</sup> My son, attend to my words; incline thine ear unto my sayings.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/4#v21)</sup> Let them not depart from thine eyes; keep them in the midst of thine heart.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/4#v22)</sup> For they are life unto those that find them, and health to all their flesh.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/4#v23)</sup> Keep thy heart with all diligence; for out of it are the issues of life.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/4#v24)</sup> Put away from thee a froward mouth, and perverse lips put far from thee.

- My son, attend to my words and incline thine ear unto my sayings; let them not depart from thine eyes, keep them in the midst of thine heart, for they are life unto those that find them and health to their flesh.
- Keep thy heart with all diligence, for out of it are the issues of life. Put away from thee a forward mouth and perverse lips put far from thee. A forward mouth is a mouth that speaks things opposite of what the word of the Most High says.

**[Proverbs 4:25-27](/bible/proverbs/4#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/4#v25)</sup> Let thine eyes look right on, and let thine eyelids look straight before thee.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/4#v26)</sup> Ponder the path of thy feet, and let all thy ways be established.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/4#v27)</sup> Turn not to the right hand nor to the left: remove thy foot from evil.

- Let thine eyes look right on and let thine eyelids look straight before thee. Ponder the path of thy feet and let all thy ways be established.
- Turn not to the right hand nor to the left; remove thy foot from evil. Across all four chapters Solomon says the same thing over and over, the same thing he says in his psalms: depart not from the commandments, keep them close to you, and depart from evil, all of it praising the Most High.
- On the question of the law and the commandments, they are exactly the same; the words are just used interchangeably. The commandments He gave are the laws, as in [Exodus 20](/bible/exodus/20) where the commandments are given, thou shalt not kill, thou shalt not steal, the ten commandments plus many more that came with them.
