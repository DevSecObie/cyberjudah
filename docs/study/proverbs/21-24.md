Read the chapter → [Proverbs 21](/bible/proverbs/21)

This session covers [Proverbs 21](/bible/proverbs/21) through 24, a stretch of Solomon's wisdom that builds character. Proverbs is a character-building book, and you will be hard-pressed to read the book of Proverbs as a righteous brother or sister and not find something that cuts you or lends wisdom to a situation you are dealing with. These chapters deal with wisdom, raising children, avoiding sin, laziness and slothfulness, the tongue, greed, drunkenness, and how to walk uprightly among wicked people. These are books that should be read often.

**[Proverbs 21:1-2](/bible/proverbs/21#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/21#v1)</sup> The king’s heart is in the hand of the Lord, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/21#v2)</sup> Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the Lord pondereth the hearts.

- The king's heart being in the hand of the Most High means the mind of the king is controlled by him. Just as Pharaoh's heart was hardened against Moses and Israel, the Most High can harden the hearts of kings and soften them.
- He used heathen kings to deal in our favor, like Cyrus in the time of Ezra, who did things in favor of our people.
- Man's goings are of the Most High. A lot of us were guided into the truth and do not understand it. You did not come in just because a friend shared a flyer or you found a random YouTube video. It was not random.
  - **[Jeremiah 10:23](/bible/jeremiah/10#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/10#v23)</sup> O Lord, I know that the way of man is not in himself: it is not in man that walketh to direct his steps.

    A man's way is not in himself; his steps are directed by the Most High.
- Every way of a man is right in his own eyes, but the Most High weighs the heart.

**[Proverbs 21:3-4](/bible/proverbs/21#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/21#v3)</sup> To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the Lord than sacrifice.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/21#v4)</sup> An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin.

- To do justice means to do the right thing and be obedient to the laws of the Most High. He always required obedience before sacrifice.
- Justice is seeing your brother drop his wallet and telling him, instead of putting your foot on the money that fell out and sliding it away. That is not justice, that is wickedness.

**[Proverbs 21:5-8](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)</sup> The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/21#v6)</sup> The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/21#v7)</sup> The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/21#v8)</sup> The way of man is froward and strange: but as for the pure, his work is right.

- The thoughts of the diligent tend to plenteousness, but the hasty man comes only to want.
- Getting treasures by a lying tongue is vanity. We see this in America, where a lot of business owners and big corporations have faulty, shaky practices. Even misleading someone is a lie.
- Fast food commercials show a big stacked burger, and when you get there the burger is small. That is misleading. People cut corners, lie, and scam to get money.
- Some people deserve to be scammed because it is the dumbest thing. "Send me a thousand dollars, I'll send you ten thousand back." Your last five hundred becomes a five-hundred-dollar lesson you had to learn.
- The robbery of the wicked will destroy them. A lot of brothers are in jail right now for armed robbery, aggravated robbery, thievery, because they refused to do judgment.

**[Proverbs 21:9-11](/bible/proverbs/21#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/21#v9)</sup> It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/21#v10)</sup> The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/21#v11)</sup> When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

- It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, up in an attic where you can barely stand and have to crouch down, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.
- These brawling sisters have a big mouth and do not know how to be quiet. They poke at you, put a finger in your face, chest bump you, spit at you, trying to provoke you to hit them.
- If you are with a brawling woman, eventually you go to jail, because she will hit you, you react, she calls the police, and you end up locked up. It is better to get away from a woman like that.
- When the scorner is punished the simple is made wise. When judgment comes on people, those watching who did not know better can learn from other people's mistakes. A wise man learns from the mistakes of others.

**[Proverbs 21:12-14](/bible/proverbs/21#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/21#v12)</sup> The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/21#v13)</sup> Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/21#v14)</sup> A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath.

- Whoever stops his ears at the cry of the poor will himself cry and not be heard. This is not the homeless Edomite on the corner with a sign, it is the poor of your own people.
- Christ said the poor will always be with you. There will always be need among us. The abundance we are blessed with is not to lay up riches in this kingdom, but to help those in need when we are able.
- When you are stingy and selfish you get a judgment behind that. When you need help nobody helps you, because you turned a blind eye to brothers and sisters who needed you.
- A gift in secret pacifies anger and a reward in the bosom pacifies strong wrath.

**[Proverbs 21:15-16](/bible/proverbs/21#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/21#v15)</sup> It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/21#v16)</sup> The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

- It is a joy to the just to do judgment. Doing righteous judgment feels good to brothers and sisters who are right. But to the workers of iniquity it is destruction, because their spirit lusts for evil.
- The man who wanders out of the way of understanding will remain in the congregation of the dead. The way of understanding is the understanding of the Most High's commandments and laws.
- Sisters will come in and put on dresses, then wander out of the way of understanding, reject the commandments, and go back to pants and short skirts in the club making nasty videos. They remain among the congregation of the dead.
- The congregation of the dead does not mean literally dead, it means spiritually dead. This is the masses of our people who do not understand the Most High's laws. The word of the Most High and his law bring you life; without the law you are dead.

**[Proverbs 21:17-19](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)</sup> He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/21#v18)</sup> The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/21#v19)</sup> It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman.

- He that loves pleasure will be a poor man, because he spends money to fulfill that pleasure or lust. This is not always sex, though some men love dealing with prostitutes.
- Sometimes food is the pleasure. You spend so much eating out at restaurants that you go broke. Look at your finances at month's end and you could have had an extra two or three hundred dollars if you could control your lust to eat.
- Since planning and meal-prepping his meals for the week, buying vegetables, proteins, and carbs together and staying away from restaurants, he has saved hundreds of dollars. Discipline your diet.
- Alcohol is another pleasure. Some brothers go broke because they drink too much.
- It is better to dwell in the wilderness than with a contentious and angry woman, because such a woman is annoying and the only peace you find is hiding in the wilderness.

**[Proverbs 21:20-22](/bible/proverbs/21#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/21#v20)</sup> There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/21#v21)</sup> He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/21#v22)</sup> A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

- There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man spends it up. You are supposed to reserve and store up wisdom, knowledge, and understanding.
- A lot of people do not regard wisdom; they cast it from them. You try to teach your brother and he says he is not trying to hear that. He does not care about the oil.
- He that follows after righteousness and mercy finds life, righteousness, and honor.
- A wise man scales the city of the mighty and casts down the strength its confidence rested in.

**[Proverbs 21:23-24](/bible/proverbs/21#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/21#v23)</sup> Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/21#v24)</sup> Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

- Whoever keeps his mouth and his tongue keeps his soul from trouble. Often it is your mouth that gets you into trouble.
- When we get annoyed we want to slap somebody, and it is biblical. But we control our spirit and do not put hands on anybody.
- People get knocked out on World Star because they did not know how to shut up. They disrespected the wrong one and ran their mouth. All they had to do was say it was not worth fighting over.
- A proud and haughty scorner is his name, who deals in proud wrath.
  - **[Proverbs 18:6](/bible/proverbs/18#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/18#v6)</sup> A fool’s lips enter into contention, and his mouth calleth for strokes.

    A fool's lips enter into contention and his mouth calls for strokes because he says disrespectful things.

**[Proverbs 21:25-27](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)</sup> The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/21#v26)</sup> He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/21#v27)</sup> The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?

- The desire of the slothful kills him, for his hands refuse to labor. Tell a lazy brother to get a job and he puts in one application. You can put in ten applications in thirty minutes on your phone alone.
- It would have been easier just to do it than to deal with the ramifications of not doing it. Now you have all these issues because you did not do what you needed to when it would have been easier.
- The slothful covets greedily all day long, but the righteous gives and spares not.
- The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination, how much more when he brings it with a wicked mind. When you come before the Most High you have to cleanse your spirit and mind.

**[Proverbs 21:28-31](/bible/proverbs/21#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/21#v28)</sup> A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/21#v29)</sup> A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/21#v30)</sup> There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/21#v31)</sup> The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the Lord.

- A false witness will perish, but the man that hears speaks constantly.
- There is no wisdom, understanding, nor counsel against the Most High. No knowledge supersedes the word of the Most High.
- A man came on Clubhouse talking metaphysics, quantum realities, splitting atoms, and dualities of energy while we were talking about the Bible. Smart-dumb dudes watch a YouTube video and then have to show everybody how much they know. None of it goes against the word.
- The highest form of wisdom and understanding is the Most High's laws. When his laws are preached and enforced throughout the earth, that is when the earth will be at peace and true joy and love will be experienced.
- The horse is prepared against the day of battle, but safety is of the Most High. He is where you get your true safety.

# Proverbs 22: A Good Name and Training Up a Child

Read the chapter → [Proverbs 22](/bible/proverbs/22)

**[Proverbs 22:1-2](/bible/proverbs/22#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/22#v1)</sup> A good name is rather to be chosen than great riches, and loving favour rather than silver and gold.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/22#v2)</sup> The rich and poor meet together: the Lord is the maker of them all.

- A good name goes into your reputation, and your reputation is more important than how much money you have. You can have a lot of money and be a dirtbag, the scum of the earth, and nobody respects you.
- It is about how you treated people and how you impacted their lives. A rich man everybody dislikes will be forgotten when he dies. It is not about money.
- The rich and poor meet together; the Most High is the maker of them all.

**[Proverbs 22:3-4](/bible/proverbs/22#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/22#v3)</sup> A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself: but the simple pass on, and are punished.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/22#v4)</sup> By humility and the fear of the Lord are riches, and honour, and life.

- A prudent man foresees the evil and hides himself. In marriage, when your spouse has a frustrated spirit, a prudent person deals with it wisely and uses choice words to de-escalate instead of coming in guns blazing.
- Out in the world you observe the time. At the basketball court two dudes started trash talking, then it got personal, and foreseeing the evil he grabbed his stuff and left. Afterward he learned one pulled a knife and chased the other around the court.
- A brother said his coworker wanted to learn the Bible and he would go teach her alone. Not a good idea. One thing led to another, the Bible ended up under the bed, and they committed the deed. A righteous man foresees the evil and does not sit alone in a house with an attractive woman.
- The flesh takes over and the spirit leaves, so be mindful of the situations you put yourself in.
- By humility and the fear of the Most High are riches, honor, and life.

**[Proverbs 22:5-6](/bible/proverbs/22#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/22#v5)</sup> Thorns and snares are in the way of the froward: he that doth keep his soul shall be far from them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/22#v6)</sup> Train up a child in the way he should go: and when he is old, he will not depart from it.

- Thorns and snares are in the way of the forward; he that keeps his soul is far from them. The simple do not foresee the evil, destruction, or judgment coming, so they carry on and end up caught up in child support, domestic violence charges, or crossfire.
- Train up a child in the way he should go. Children are like a blank canvas; whatever you put into them and whatever they witness growing up is the type of person they become, unless some intervention happens.
- He trains his ten-year-old son and eight-year-old daughter in the commandments, honesty, against lying, and against laziness. They have chores after school: homework, clean the room, dishes, laundry. It builds character so they are not lazy.
- At first you have to repeat yourself, but it becomes automatic. Then they have the rest of the evening to play or enjoy themselves. You must hold your children accountable.
- Kids with too much liberty get pregnant young or get involved in gang violence and jail, because they were not trained up properly.
- When he is old he will not depart from it, because you instilled these qualities while he was young.

**[Proverbs 22:7-9](/bible/proverbs/22#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/22#v7)</sup> The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/22#v8)</sup> He that soweth iniquity shall reap vanity: and the rod of his anger shall fail.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/22#v9)</sup> He that hath a bountiful eye shall be blessed; for he giveth of his bread to the poor.

- The rich rule over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.
- He that sows iniquity will reap vanity, and the rod of his anger will fail.
- He that has a bountiful eye will be blessed, for he gives of his bread to the poor. This is the brother who is not selfish and is very giving.

**[Proverbs 22:10-12](/bible/proverbs/22#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/22#v10)</sup> Cast out the scorner, and contention shall go out; yea, strife and reproach shall cease.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/22#v11)</sup> He that loveth pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king shall be his friend.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/22#v12)</sup> The eyes of the Lord preserve knowledge, and he overthroweth the words of the transgressor.

- Cast out the scorner and contention goes out. A scorner always has a problem with everything: "why are we eating chicken, why not beef; why water, why no juice." Nothing is ever good enough. Get rid of that person and you have peace.
- He that loves pureness of heart, for the grace of his lips the king will be his friend.
- The eyes of the Most High preserve knowledge, and he overthrows the words of the transgressor.

**[Proverbs 22:13-16](/bible/proverbs/22#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/22#v13)</sup> The slothful man saith, There is a lion without, I shall be slain in the streets.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/22#v14)</sup> The mouth of strange women is a deep pit: he that is abhorred of the Lord shall fall therein.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/22#v15)</sup> Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child; but the rod of correction shall drive it far from him.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/22#v16)</sup> He that oppresseth the poor to increase his riches, and he that giveth to the rich, shall surely come to want.

- The slothful man says there is a lion outside, I will be slain in the streets. There is no lion; it is an excuse not to do something because of the possibility something might go wrong.
- Scared money does not make money. This type of brother sits on his hands: "I might get in a car accident, it is too cold, I might get frostbite, it is too hot, I might get sunburned." One brother would not take off his cap for the Bible because he might get pneumonia.
- The mouth of a strange woman is a deep pit; he that is abhorred of the Most High falls in.
- Foolishness is bound in the heart of a child, but the rod of correction drives it far from him. You have to discipline and correct your child, including spankings, not beating the hell out of him.
- He that oppresses the poor to increase his riches will come to want. This is exploiting the poor, like setting up nail shops and corner stores in our communities with the glass you have to slide your money through.

**[Proverbs 22:17-21](/bible/proverbs/22#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/22#v17)</sup> Bow down thine ear, and hear the words of the wise, and apply thine heart unto my knowledge.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/22#v18)</sup> For it is a pleasant thing if thou keep them within thee; they shall withal be fitted in thy lips.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/22#v19)</sup> That thy trust may be in the Lord, I have made known to thee this day, even to thee.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/22#v20)</sup> Have not I written to thee excellent things in counsels and knowledge,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/22#v21)</sup> That I might make thee know the certainty of the words of truth; that thou mightest answer the words of truth to them that send unto thee?

- Bow down your ear and hear the words of the wise, and apply your heart to knowledge. It is pleasant if you keep them within you.
- These excellent things and counsels are written to make you know the certainty of the words of truth, so you may answer the words of truth to them that send to you.

**[Proverbs 22:22-25](/bible/proverbs/22#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/22#v22)</sup> Rob not the poor, because he is poor: neither oppress the afflicted in the gate:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/22#v23)</sup> For the Lord will plead their cause, and spoil the soul of those that spoiled them.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/22#v24)</sup> Make no friendship with an angry man; and with a furious man thou shalt not go:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/22#v25)</sup> Lest thou learn his ways, and get a snare to thy soul.

- Rob not the poor because he is poor, neither oppress the afflicted, for the Most High will plead their cause and spoil the soul of those that spoil them.
- Make no friendship with an angry man, and with a furious man do not go, because he will turn and fight you, disrespect you, and get you in trouble. He starts mess and you have to fight to defend him.
- Do not befriend a furious man lest you learn his ways and get a snare to your soul. When you hang around certain people their spirit rubs off. Brothers start to talk like each other; a woman not into something picks it up from sisters who are. Be mindful of who you hang around.

**[Proverbs 22:26-29](/bible/proverbs/22#v26)**
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/22#v26)</sup> Be not thou one of them that strike hands, or of them that are sureties for debts.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/22#v27)</sup> If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/22#v28)</sup> Remove not the ancient landmark, which thy fathers have set.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/22#v29)</sup> Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.

- Be not one that strikes hands or is surety for debts. If you have nothing to pay, why should your bed be taken from under you.
- Remove not the ancient landmark which your fathers have set.
- See a man diligent in business; he will stand before kings, not before mean men. To deal among the upper echelon you have to be diligent.
  - **[Proverbs 21:5](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)</sup> The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.

    The diligent tend to plenteousness while the lazy come to want.

# Proverbs 23: Warnings Against Appetite, Wine, and the Strange Woman

Read the chapter → [Proverbs 23](/bible/proverbs/23)

**[Proverbs 23:1-3](/bible/proverbs/23#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/23#v1)</sup> When thou sittest to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before thee:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/23#v2)</sup> And put a knife to thy throat, if thou be a man given to appetite.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/23#v3)</sup> Be not desirous of his dainties: for they are deceitful meat.

- When you sit to eat with a ruler, consider diligently what is before you and put a knife to your throat if you are a man given to appetite. This is about being greedy.
- If you are invited to dinner and devour everything, then ask if there are seconds, that is offensive. Put a knife to your appetite so you do not embarrass yourself being insatiable, greedy, and gluttonous.

**[Proverbs 23:4-8](/bible/proverbs/23#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/23#v4)</sup> Labour not to be rich: cease from thine own wisdom.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/23#v5)</sup> Wilt thou set thine eyes upon that which is not? for riches certainly make themselves wings; they fly away as an eagle toward heaven.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/23#v6)</sup> Eat thou not the bread of him that hath an evil eye, neither desire thou his dainty meats:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/23#v7)</sup> For as he thinketh in his heart, so is he: Eat and drink, saith he to thee; but his heart is not with thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/23#v8)</sup> The morsel which thou hast eaten shalt thou vomit up, and lose thy sweet words.

- Labor not to be rich; cease from your own understanding. Riches make themselves wings and fly away as an eagle toward heaven. Money comes and money goes.
- Eat not the bread of him that has an evil eye, for as he thinks in his heart, so is he. He says eat and drink, but his heart is not with you; the morsel you ate you will vomit up.

**[Proverbs 23:9-11](/bible/proverbs/23#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/23#v9)</sup> Speak not in the ears of a fool: for he will despise the wisdom of thy words.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/23#v10)</sup> Remove not the old landmark; and enter not into the fields of the fatherless:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/23#v11)</sup> For their redeemer is mighty; he shall plead their cause with thee.

- Speak not in the ears of a fool, for he will despise the wisdom of your words. This is Christ saying do not cast your pearls before swine.
- Certain people are not worthy of you trying to deal with them, because they will rebel, disrespect you, and talk crazy. It is a waste of your time.
- Remove not the old landmark and enter not the fields of the fatherless, for their Redeemer is mighty and will plead their cause with you.

**[Proverbs 23:12-16](/bible/proverbs/23#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/23#v12)</sup> Apply thine heart unto instruction, and thine ears to the words of knowledge.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/23#v13)</sup> Withhold not correction from the child: for if thou beatest him with the rod, he shall not die.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/23#v14)</sup> Thou shalt beat him with the rod, and shalt deliver his soul from hell.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/23#v15)</sup> My son, if thine heart be wise, my heart shall rejoice, even mine.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/23#v16)</sup> Yea, my reins shall rejoice, when thy lips speak right things.

- Apply your heart to instruction and your ears to the words of knowledge.
- Withhold not correction from the child, for if you beat him with the rod he will not die. Do not feel bad; you are teaching him a valuable lesson.
- He has levels of discipline: the talking phase, the lecture phase explaining why the behavior is unacceptable, taking away things like the game, Xbox, or TV, then spanking. The punishment must fit the crime, no cruel and unusual punishments.
- You beat him with the rod and deliver his soul from hell, preventing him from making major mistakes later in life.
