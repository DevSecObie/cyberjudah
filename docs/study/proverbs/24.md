**[Proverbs 24:1-4](/bible/proverbs/24#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/24#v1)</sup> Be not thou envious against evil men, neither desire to be with them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/24#v2)</sup> For their heart studieth destruction, and their lips talk of mischief.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/24#v3)</sup> Through wisdom is an house builded; and by understanding it is established:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/24#v4)</sup> And by knowledge shall the chambers be filled with all precious and pleasant riches.

- Be not envious against evil men, neither desire to be with them, for their heart studies destruction and their lips talk of mischief.
- Through wisdom a house is built, by understanding it is established, and by knowledge the chambers are filled with precious and pleasant riches.

**[Proverbs 24:5-7](/bible/proverbs/24#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/24#v5)</sup> A wise man is strong; yea, a man of knowledge increaseth strength.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/24#v6)</sup> For by wise counsel thou shalt make thy war: and in multitude of counsellors there is safety.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/24#v7)</sup> Wisdom is too high for a fool: he openeth not his mouth in the gate.

- A wise man is strong; a man of knowledge increases strength. By wise counsel you make your war, and in the multitude of counselors there is safety.
- Something other nations use that black people have not figured out yet starts with an "m": mentorship. People freestyle without a plan and it does not work out. Other nations have mentors who already accomplished what they are trying to do and get counsel, which is advice, on how to reach that level.
- Some black people are too prideful to ask for help. Wisdom is too high for a fool; he opens not his mouth in the gate.

**[Proverbs 24:8-10](/bible/proverbs/24#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/24#v8)</sup> He that deviseth to do evil shall be called a mischievous person.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/24#v9)</sup> The thought of foolishness is sin: and the scorner is an abomination to men.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/24#v10)</sup> If thou faint in the day of adversity, thy strength is small.

- He that devises to do evil will be called a mischievous person. The thought of foolishness is sin, and the scorner is an abomination to men.
- The thought of foolishness is sin. We have thoughts we cannot control that pop up, and we have to rebuke them and say that is the devil. All of us have some perverse thoughts, and you have to rebuke them.
- If you faint in the day of adversity your strength is small.

**[Proverbs 24:11-14](/bible/proverbs/24#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/24#v11)</sup> If thou forbear to deliver them that are drawn unto death, and those that are ready to be slain;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/24#v12)</sup> If thou sayest, Behold, we knew it not; doth not he that pondereth the heart consider it? and he that keepeth thy soul, doth not he know it? and shall not he render to every man according to his works?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/24#v13)</sup> My son, eat thou honey, because it is good; and the honeycomb, which is sweet to thy taste:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/24#v14)</sup> So shall the knowledge of wisdom be unto thy soul: when thou hast found it, then there shall be a reward, and thy expectation shall not be cut off.

- If you forbear to deliver them that are drawn to death and ready to be slain, and say you knew it not, does not he that ponders the heart consider it and render to every man according to his works?
- Eat honey because it is good, and the honeycomb which is sweet; so is the knowledge of wisdom to your soul. When you find it there is a reward and your expectation will not be cut off.
- Cherish knowledge, wisdom, and understanding. Always be in a position to learn and see the lessons in life. Often a regular situation holds a life lesson and you miss it because your antennas are not up.

**[Proverbs 24:15-18](/bible/proverbs/24#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/24#v15)</sup> Lay not wait, O wicked man, against the dwelling of the righteous; spoil not his resting place:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/24#v16)</sup> For a just man falleth seven times, and riseth up again: but the wicked shall fall into mischief.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/24#v17)</sup> Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth, and let not thine heart be glad when he stumbleth:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/24#v18)</sup> Lest the Lord see it, and it displease him, and he turn away his wrath from him.

- Lay not wait against the dwelling of the righteous, for a just man falls seven times and rises up again, but the wicked fall into mischief.
- Certain brothers come into the truth and make mistakes, but each mistake they learn from and rise up again. He respects brothers who make mistakes and get back in the fight, unlike those too embarrassed to admit they were wrong who run like a little girl.
- Rejoice not when your enemy falls, and let not your heart be glad when he stumbles, lest the Most High see it and turn away his wrath from him.

**[Proverbs 24:19-22](/bible/proverbs/24#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/24#v19)</sup> Fret not thyself because of evil men, neither be thou envious at the wicked;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/24#v20)</sup> For there shall be no reward to the evil man; the candle of the wicked shall be put out.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/24#v21)</sup> My son, fear thou the Lord and the king: and meddle not with them that are given to change:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/24#v22)</sup> For their calamity shall rise suddenly; and who knoweth the ruin of them both?

- Fret not yourself because of evil men, neither be envious of the wicked, for there is no reward to the evil man and the candle of the wicked will be put out.
- Whatever the wicked have going on, or riches gotten by wickedness, do not stress over it. The Most High is going to judge them and deal with them, so keep it moving.
- Fear the Most High and the king, and meddle not with them given to change, for their calamity rises suddenly.

**[Proverbs 24:23-26](/bible/proverbs/24#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/24#v23)</sup> These things also belong to the wise. It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/24#v24)</sup> He that saith unto the wicked, Thou art righteous; him shall the people curse, nations shall abhor him:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/24#v25)</sup> But to them that rebuke him shall be delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/24#v26)</sup> Every man shall kiss his lips that giveth a right answer.

- It is not good to have respect of persons in judgment. He that says to the wicked, you are righteous, the people will curse and nations abhor him, but to them that rebuke him will be delight and a good blessing.
- Every man will kiss his lips that gives a right answer.

**[Proverbs 24:27-29](/bible/proverbs/24#v27)**
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/24#v27)</sup> Prepare thy work without, and make it fit for thyself in the field; and afterwards build thine house.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/24#v28)</sup> Be not a witness against thy neighbour without cause; and deceive not with thy lips.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/24#v29)</sup> Say not, I will do so to him as he hath done to me: I will render to the man according to his work.

- Prepare your work outside and make it fit in the field, and afterward build your house.
- Be not a witness against your neighbor without cause, and deceive not with your lips.
- Say not, I will do to him as he has done to me. This is where turning the other cheek comes from; you have to take the high road instead of getting vengeance.
- His neighbor drunkenly scratched his car while parking her sister's car, and it took everything in him not to get revenge. He let it go and took the high road.

**[Proverbs 24:30-34](/bible/proverbs/24#v30)**
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/24#v30)</sup> I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/24#v31)</sup> And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/24#v32)</sup> Then I saw, and considered it well: I looked upon it, and received instruction.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/24#v33)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/24#v34)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man.

- He went by the field of the slothful and the vineyard of the man void of understanding, and it was all grown over with thorns and nettles, its stone wall broken down.
- He considered it well and received instruction: yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep, so your poverty comes as one that travels and your want as an armed man.
- These chapters deal with wisdom, raising children, avoiding sin, and laziness. These are things we need to put into our character. Proverbs is a very good character-building book that should be read often.
