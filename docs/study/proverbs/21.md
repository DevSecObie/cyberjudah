Read the chapter → [Proverbs 21](/bible/proverbs/21)

This session covers [Proverbs 21](/bible/proverbs/21) through 24, a stretch of Solomon's wisdom that builds character. Proverbs is a character-building book, and you will be hard-pressed to read the book of Proverbs as a righteous brother or sister and not find something that cuts you or lends wisdom to a situation you are dealing with. These chapters deal with wisdom, raising children, avoiding sin, laziness and slothfulness, the tongue, greed, drunkenness, and how to walk uprightly among wicked people. These are books that should be read often.

**[Proverbs 21:1-2](/bible/proverbs/21#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/21#v1)</sup> The king’s heart is in the hand of the Lord, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/21#v2)</sup> Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the Lord pondereth the hearts.

- The king's heart being in the hand of the Most High means the mind of the king is controlled by him. Just as Pharaoh's heart was hardened against Moses and Israel, the Most High can harden the hearts of kings and soften them.
- He used heathen kings to deal in our favor, like Cyrus in the time of Ezra, who did things in favor of our people.
- Man's goings are of the Most High. A lot of us were guided into the truth and do not understand it. You did not come in just because a friend shared a flyer or you found a random YouTube video. It was not random.
  - **[Jeremiah 10:23](/bible/jeremiah/10#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/10#v23)</sup> O Lord, I know that the way of man is not in himself: it is not in man that walketh to direct his steps.

    A man's way is not in himself; his steps are directed by the Most High.
- Every way of a man is right in his own eyes, but the Most High weighs the heart.

**[Proverbs 21:3-4](/bible/proverbs/21#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/21#v3)</sup> To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the Lord than sacrifice.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/21#v4)</sup> An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin.

- To do justice means to do the right thing and be obedient to the laws of the Most High. He always required obedience before sacrifice.
- Justice is seeing your brother drop his wallet and telling him, instead of putting your foot on the money that fell out and sliding it away. That is not justice, that is wickedness.

**[Proverbs 21:5-8](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/21#v5)</sup> The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/21#v6)</sup> The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/21#v7)</sup> The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/21#v8)</sup> The way of man is froward and strange: but as for the pure, his work is right.

- The thoughts of the diligent tend to plenteousness, but the hasty man comes only to want.
- Getting treasures by a lying tongue is vanity. We see this in America, where a lot of business owners and big corporations have faulty, shaky practices. Even misleading someone is a lie.
- Fast food commercials show a big stacked burger, and when you get there the burger is small. That is misleading. People cut corners, lie, and scam to get money.
- Some people deserve to be scammed because it is the dumbest thing. "Send me a thousand dollars, I'll send you ten thousand back." Your last five hundred becomes a five-hundred-dollar lesson you had to learn.
- The robbery of the wicked will destroy them. A lot of brothers are in jail right now for armed robbery, aggravated robbery, thievery, because they refused to do judgment.

**[Proverbs 21:9-11](/bible/proverbs/21#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/21#v9)</sup> It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/21#v10)</sup> The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/21#v11)</sup> When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

- It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, up in an attic where you can barely stand and have to crouch down, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.
- These brawling sisters have a big mouth and do not know how to be quiet. They poke at you, put a finger in your face, chest bump you, spit at you, trying to provoke you to hit them.
- If you are with a brawling woman, eventually you go to jail, because she will hit you, you react, she calls the police, and you end up locked up. It is better to get away from a woman like that.
- When the scorner is punished the simple is made wise. When judgment comes on people, those watching who did not know better can learn from other people's mistakes. A wise man learns from the mistakes of others.

**[Proverbs 21:12-14](/bible/proverbs/21#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/21#v12)</sup> The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/21#v13)</sup> Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/21#v14)</sup> A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath.

- Whoever stops his ears at the cry of the poor will himself cry and not be heard. This is not the homeless Edomite on the corner with a sign, it is the poor of your own people.
- Christ said the poor will always be with you. There will always be need among us. The abundance we are blessed with is not to lay up riches in this kingdom, but to help those in need when we are able.
- When you are stingy and selfish you get a judgment behind that. When you need help nobody helps you, because you turned a blind eye to brothers and sisters who needed you.
- A gift in secret pacifies anger and a reward in the bosom pacifies strong wrath.

**[Proverbs 21:15-16](/bible/proverbs/21#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/21#v15)</sup> It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/21#v16)</sup> The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

- It is a joy to the just to do judgment. Doing righteous judgment feels good to brothers and sisters who are right. But to the workers of iniquity it is destruction, because their spirit lusts for evil.
- The man who wanders out of the way of understanding will remain in the congregation of the dead. The way of understanding is the understanding of the Most High's commandments and laws.
- Sisters will come in and put on dresses, then wander out of the way of understanding, reject the commandments, and go back to pants and short skirts in the club making nasty videos. They remain among the congregation of the dead.
- The congregation of the dead does not mean literally dead, it means spiritually dead. This is the masses of our people who do not understand the Most High's laws. The word of the Most High and his law bring you life; without the law you are dead.

**[Proverbs 21:17-19](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)</sup> He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/21#v18)</sup> The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/21#v19)</sup> It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman.

- He that loves pleasure will be a poor man, because he spends money to fulfill that pleasure or lust. This is not always sex, though some men love dealing with prostitutes.
- Sometimes food is the pleasure. You spend so much eating out at restaurants that you go broke. Look at your finances at month's end and you could have had an extra two or three hundred dollars if you could control your lust to eat.
- Since planning and meal-prepping his meals for the week, buying vegetables, proteins, and carbs together and staying away from restaurants, he has saved hundreds of dollars. Discipline your diet.
- Alcohol is another pleasure. Some brothers go broke because they drink too much.
- It is better to dwell in the wilderness than with a contentious and angry woman, because such a woman is annoying and the only peace you find is hiding in the wilderness.

**[Proverbs 21:20-22](/bible/proverbs/21#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/21#v20)</sup> There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/21#v21)</sup> He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/21#v22)</sup> A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

- There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man spends it up. You are supposed to reserve and store up wisdom, knowledge, and understanding.
- A lot of people do not regard wisdom; they cast it from them. You try to teach your brother and he says he is not trying to hear that. He does not care about the oil.
- He that follows after righteousness and mercy finds life, righteousness, and honor.
- A wise man scales the city of the mighty and casts down the strength its confidence rested in.

**[Proverbs 21:23-24](/bible/proverbs/21#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/21#v23)</sup> Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/21#v24)</sup> Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

- Whoever keeps his mouth and his tongue keeps his soul from trouble. Often it is your mouth that gets you into trouble.
- When we get annoyed we want to slap somebody, and it is biblical. But we control our spirit and do not put hands on anybody.
- People get knocked out on World Star because they did not know how to shut up. They disrespected the wrong one and ran their mouth. All they had to do was say it was not worth fighting over.
- A proud and haughty scorner is his name, who deals in proud wrath.
  - **[Proverbs 18:6](/bible/proverbs/18#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/18#v6)</sup> A fool’s lips enter into contention, and his mouth calleth for strokes.

    A fool's lips enter into contention and his mouth calls for strokes because he says disrespectful things.

**[Proverbs 21:25-27](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/21#v25)</sup> The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/21#v26)</sup> He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/21#v27)</sup> The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?

- The desire of the slothful kills him, for his hands refuse to labor. Tell a lazy brother to get a job and he puts in one application. You can put in ten applications in thirty minutes on your phone alone.
- It would have been easier just to do it than to deal with the ramifications of not doing it. Now you have all these issues because you did not do what you needed to when it would have been easier.
- The slothful covets greedily all day long, but the righteous gives and spares not.
- The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination, how much more when he brings it with a wicked mind. When you come before the Most High you have to cleanse your spirit and mind.

**[Proverbs 21:28-31](/bible/proverbs/21#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/21#v28)</sup> A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/21#v29)</sup> A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/21#v30)</sup> There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/21#v31)</sup> The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the Lord.

- A false witness will perish, but the man that hears speaks constantly.
- There is no wisdom, understanding, nor counsel against the Most High. No knowledge supersedes the word of the Most High.
- A man came on Clubhouse talking metaphysics, quantum realities, splitting atoms, and dualities of energy while we were talking about the Bible. Smart-dumb dudes watch a YouTube video and then have to show everybody how much they know. None of it goes against the word.
- The highest form of wisdom and understanding is the Most High's laws. When his laws are preached and enforced throughout the earth, that is when the earth will be at peace and true joy and love will be experienced.
- The horse is prepared against the day of battle, but safety is of the Most High. He is where you get your true safety.
