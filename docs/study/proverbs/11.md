**[Proverbs 11:1-15](/bible/proverbs/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/11#v1)</sup> A false balance is abomination to the Lord: but a just weight is his delight.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/11#v2)</sup> When pride cometh, then cometh shame: but with the lowly is wisdom.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/11#v3)</sup> The integrity of the upright shall guide them: but the perverseness of transgressors shall destroy them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/11#v4)</sup> Riches profit not in the day of wrath: but righteousness delivereth from death.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/11#v5)</sup> The righteousness of the perfect shall direct his way: but the wicked shall fall by his own wickedness.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/11#v6)</sup> The righteousness of the upright shall deliver them: but transgressors shall be taken in their own naughtiness.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/11#v7)</sup> When a wicked man dieth, his expectation shall perish: and the hope of unjust men perisheth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/11#v8)</sup> The righteous is delivered out of trouble, and the wicked cometh in his stead.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/11#v9)</sup> An hypocrite with his mouth destroyeth his neighbour: but through knowledge shall the just be delivered.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/11#v10)</sup> When it goeth well with the righteous, the city rejoiceth: and when the wicked perish, there is shouting.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/11#v11)</sup> By the blessing of the upright the city is exalted: but it is overthrown by the mouth of the wicked.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/11#v12)</sup> He that is void of wisdom despiseth his neighbour: but a man of understanding holdeth his peace.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/11#v13)</sup> A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/11#v15)</sup> He that is surety for a stranger shall smart for it: and he that hateth suretiship is sure.

**[Proverbs 11:16-31](/bible/proverbs/11#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/11#v16)</sup> A gracious woman retaineth honour: and strong men retain riches.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/11#v17)</sup> The merciful man doeth good to his own soul: but he that is cruel troubleth his own flesh.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/11#v18)</sup> The wicked worketh a deceitful work: but to him that soweth righteousness shall be a sure reward.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/11#v19)</sup> As righteousness tendeth to life: so he that pursueth evil pursueth it to his own death.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/11#v20)</sup> They that are of a froward heart are abomination to the Lord: but such as are upright in their way are his delight.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/11#v21)</sup> Though hand join in hand, the wicked shall not be unpunished: but the seed of the righteous shall be delivered.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/11#v22)</sup> As a jewel of gold in a swine’s snout, so is a fair woman which is without discretion.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/11#v23)</sup> The desire of the righteous is only good: but the expectation of the wicked is wrath.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/11#v24)</sup> There is that scattereth, and yet increaseth; and there is that withholdeth more than is meet, but it tendeth to poverty.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/11#v25)</sup> The liberal soul shall be made fat: and he that watereth shall be watered also himself.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/11#v26)</sup> He that withholdeth corn, the people shall curse him: but blessing shall be upon the head of him that selleth it.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/11#v27)</sup> He that diligently seeketh good procureth favour: but he that seeketh mischief, it shall come unto him.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/11#v28)</sup> He that trusteth in his riches shall fall: but the righteous shall flourish as a branch.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/11#v29)</sup> He that troubleth his own house shall inherit the wind: and the fool shall be servant to the wise of heart.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/11#v30)</sup> The fruit of the righteous is a tree of life; and he that winneth souls is wise.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/11#v31)</sup> Behold, the righteous shall be recompensed in the earth: much more the wicked and the sinner.
