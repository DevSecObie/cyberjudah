**[2 Samuel 4:1-3](/bible/2-samuel/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-samuel/4#v1)</sup> And when Saul’s son heard that Abner was dead in Hebron, his hands were feeble, and all the Israelites were troubled.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-samuel/4#v2)</sup> And Saul’s son had two men that were captains of bands: the name of the one was Baanah, and the name of the other Rechab, the sons of Rimmon a Beerothite, of the children of Benjamin: (for Beeroth also was reckoned to Benjamin:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-samuel/4#v3)</sup> And the Beerothites fled to Gittaim, and were sojourners there until this day.)

- When Saul's son Ishbosheth heard that Abner was dead in Hebron, his hands were feeble and all the Israelites were troubled.
- Ishbosheth had two captains of bands, Baanah and Rechab, sons of Rimmon the Beerothite of the children of Benjamin, for Beeroth also was reckoned to Benjamin; and the Beerothites had fled to Gittaim.

**[2 Samuel 4:4](/bible/2-samuel/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-samuel/4#v4)</sup> And Jonathan, Saul’s son, had a son that was lame of his feet. He was five years old when the tidings came of Saul and Jonathan out of Jezreel, and his nurse took him up, and fled: and it came to pass, as she made haste to flee, that he fell, and became lame. And his name was Mephibosheth.

- Jonathan's son Mephibosheth was lame of his feet. When the tidings came of Saul and Jonathan out of Jezreel, he was five years old, and his nurse took him up and fled, and in her haste he fell and became lame.
- She fled because a new king taking over would customarily kill all the descendants of the former king to make sure none could overthrow him. That is why the nurse ran with the child.

**[2 Samuel 4:5-8](/bible/2-samuel/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-samuel/4#v5)</sup> And the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, Rechab and Baanah, went, and came about the heat of the day to the house of Ish–bosheth, who lay on a bed at noon.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-samuel/4#v6)</sup> And they came thither into the midst of the house, as though they would have fetched wheat; and they smote him under the fifth rib: and Rechab and Baanah his brother escaped.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-samuel/4#v7)</sup> For when they came into the house, he lay on his bed in his bedchamber, and they smote him, and slew him, and beheaded him, and took his head, and gat them away through the plain all night.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-samuel/4#v8)</sup> And they brought the head of Ish–bosheth unto David to Hebron, and said to the king, Behold the head of Ish–bosheth the son of Saul thine enemy, which sought thy life; and the Lord hath avenged my lord the king this day of Saul, and of his seed.

- The sons of Rimmon came about the heat of the day to the house of Ishbosheth, who lay on a bed at noon in the hottest part of the day, and came into the midst of the house as though they would fetch wheat, and smote him under the fifth rib.
- Rechab and Baanah escaped, having smitten and slain him on his bed; and they beheaded him and took his head and got away through the plain all night, and brought the head to David at Hebron, saying, Behold the head of Ishbosheth thine enemy, and claiming the Lord had avenged the king this day of Saul and his seed.
- They did too much; they went overboard. Israel being out of order took the matter upon themselves. David had no quarrel with Saul's sons; it was Saul who sought his life.

**[2 Samuel 4:9-12](/bible/2-samuel/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-samuel/4#v9)</sup> And David answered Rechab and Baanah his brother, the sons of Rimmon the Beerothite, and said unto them, As the Lord liveth, who hath redeemed my soul out of all adversity,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-samuel/4#v10)</sup> When one told me, saying, Behold, Saul is dead, thinking to have brought good tidings, I took hold of him, and slew him in Ziklag, who thought that I would have given him a reward for his tidings:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-samuel/4#v11)</sup> How much more, when wicked men have slain a righteous person in his own house upon his bed? shall I not therefore now require his blood of your hand, and take you away from the earth?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/4#v12)</sup> And David commanded his young men, and they slew them, and cut off their hands and their feet, and hanged them up over the pool in Hebron. But they took the head of Ish–bosheth, and buried it in the sepulchre of Abner in Hebron.

- David answered Rechab and Baanah that as the Lord liveth, who had redeemed his soul out of all adversity, the man who told him Saul was dead, thinking to bring good tidings, he had taken hold of and slain in Ziklag; that was the reward he gave for such tidings.
- How much more, he said, when wicked men had slain a righteous person in his own house upon his bed, should he not require his blood of their hand and take them away from the earth. The man was at rest, asleep, not even a man-to-man contest for the kingdom; it was not how the Most High brings His purpose to pass.
- David commanded his young men, and they slew Rechab and Baanah, cut off their hands and feet, and hanged them up over the pool in Hebron, but took the head of Ishbosheth and buried it in the sepulchre of Abner. The Most High has His own way of doing things; His people are not to put the cart before the horse.
- This is why David is called a man after the Most High's own heart. He did not accept wickedness done in his name or let the murderers slide; he called them out and they received judgment for taking a man's life not ordained of the Lord nor commanded by the king. Let all things be done decently and in order.
