## Introduction

Chapter 10 turns to war. David seeks to show kindness to Hanun the son of Nahash, king of Ammon, as his father had shown kindness to David, but the princes of Ammon poison the king's mind, humiliate David's servants, and hire the Syrians against Israel. Joab and Abishai divide the host and put the enemy to flight, and when the Syrians regroup David gathers all Israel and smites them so thoroughly that the Syrians fear to help Ammon anymore. When Israel keeps the commandments and the Most High is with them, they do not lose.

**[2 Samuel 10:1-2](/bible/2-samuel/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-samuel/10#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after this, that the king of the children of Ammon died, and Hanun his son reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-samuel/10#v2)</sup> Then said David, I will shew kindness unto Hanun the son of Nahash, as his father shewed kindness unto me. And David sent to comfort him by the hand of his servants for his father. And David’s servants came into the land of the children of Ammon.

- The king of the children of Ammon died and Hanun his son reigned in his stead. David said he would show kindness unto Hanun as his father Nahash had shown kindness unto David, and sent his servants to comfort him for his father.
- Remember that the children of Ammon had been warring with Israel; they got smashed and put under tribute back in the days of Saul.

    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-samuel/11#v2)</sup> And Nahash the Ammonite answered them, On this condition will I make a covenant with you, that I may thrust out all your right eyes, and lay it for a reproach upon all Israel.

    Nahash the Ammonite besieged Jabesh-gilead and would make a covenant only on condition of thrusting out all their right eyes, a reproach upon all Israel.

**[2 Samuel 10:3](/bible/2-samuel/10#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-samuel/10#v3)</sup> And the princes of the children of Ammon said unto Hanun their lord, Thinkest thou that David doth honour thy father, that he hath sent comforters unto thee? hath not David rather sent his servants unto thee, to search the city, and to spy it out, and to overthrow it?

- The princes of Ammon told their lord Hanun that David had not sent comforters to honor his father, but had rather sent his servants to search the city, to spy it out, and to overthrow it.
- Through one generation to another they still had this beef with Israel; they kept it in their mind that these people were enemies, not meant to be at peace with them. So they put it in the king's mind that David was really coming to spy the land out and overthrow them.

**[2 Samuel 10:4-5](/bible/2-samuel/10#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-samuel/10#v4)</sup> Wherefore Hanun took David’s servants, and shaved off the one half of their beards, and cut off their garments in the middle, even to their buttocks, and sent them away.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-samuel/10#v5)</sup> When they told it unto David, he sent to meet them, because the men were greatly ashamed: and the king said, Tarry at Jericho until your beards be grown, and then return.

- Hanun took David's servants, shaved off one half of their beards, cut off their garments in the middle even unto their buttocks, and sent them away. When it was told David, he sent to meet them because the men were greatly ashamed, telling them to tarry at Jericho until their beards be grown, then return.
- One of the first things we learn coming into this truth is that a man has to have a beard and men have to pull up their pants. The beard is a badge of manly dignity, a law given by the Most High, and it is a shame for a man to shave it. These men were ashamed to come into the city with half a beard and their garments cut so their behind was showing; David did not even want them in the city until their beards grew back. That is what Israel has to change as a people; today men walk around with their behind showing as a fashion statement, but these men were greatly ashamed.

**[2 Samuel 10:6](/bible/2-samuel/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-samuel/10#v6)</sup> And when the children of Ammon saw that they stank before David, the children of Ammon sent and hired the Syrians of Beth–rehob, and the Syrians of Zoba, twenty thousand footmen, and of king Maacah a thousand men, and of Ish–tob twelve thousand men.

- When the children of Ammon saw that they stank before David, they hired the Syrians of Beth-rehob and Zoba, twenty thousand footmen, and of king Maacah a thousand men, and of Ish-tob twelve thousand men.
- Once these men saw David did not like what happened, they knew David was a man of war and Israel a people of war, so they sought help. But David had already subdued these same nations; they simply tried to get the men who had always been beefing with Israel to come against Israel yet again.

    >

    David had already dedicated unto the Most High the silver and gold of the nations he subdued, including Syria, Moab, and the children of Ammon.

**[2 Samuel 10:7-11](/bible/2-samuel/10#v7)**

>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-samuel/10#v8)</sup> And the children of Ammon came out, and put the battle in array at the entering in of the gate: and the Syrians of Zoba, and of Rehob, and Ish–tob, and Maacah, were by themselves in the field.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-samuel/10#v9)</sup> When Joab saw that the front of the battle was against him before and behind, he chose of all the choice men of Israel, and put them in array against the Syrians:

>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-samuel/10#v11)</sup> And he said, If the Syrians be too strong for me, then thou shalt help me: but if the children of Ammon be too strong for thee, then I will come and help thee.

- When David heard it he sent Joab and all the host of the mighty men. The children of Ammon put the battle in array at the gate, and the Syrians were by themselves in the field. Seeing the battle set against him before and behind, Joab chose of the choice men of Israel against the Syrians and delivered the rest into the hand of Abishai his brother against the children of Ammon, each ready to help the other if too strong.
- This is how much of a warrior nation Israel is: Joab put his troops in a battle array to fight two enemies at one time. That warrior mentality is already etched inside us; we just have to keep the commandments so it can come back out, as it is going to according to [Joel 2](/bible/joel/2).

**[2 Samuel 10:12-14](/bible/2-samuel/10#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/10#v12)</sup> Be of good courage, and let us play the men for our people, and for the cities of our God: and the Lord do that which seemeth him good.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-samuel/10#v13)</sup> And Joab drew nigh, and the people that were with him, unto the battle against the Syrians: and they fled before him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/10#v14)</sup> And when the children of Ammon saw that the Syrians were fled, then fled they also before Abishai, and entered into the city. So Joab returned from the children of Ammon, and came to Jerusalem.

- Joab charged them to be of good courage and play the men for their people and the cities of their God. When Joab and his men drew nigh, the Syrians fled before him, and when the children of Ammon saw the Syrians flee, they also fled before Abishai and entered into the city, and Joab returned to Jerusalem.
- Once it comes out that Israel starts to fight, and the Most High is with them, they do not lose. In chapter 8 David was collecting tribute from all of them, and now they rise up again just because they thought they had help.

**[2 Samuel 10:15-19](/bible/2-samuel/10#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-samuel/10#v15)</sup> And when the Syrians saw that they were smitten before Israel, they gathered themselves together.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-samuel/10#v16)</sup> And Hadarezer sent, and brought out the Syrians that were beyond the river: and they came to Helam; and Shobach the captain of the host of Hadarezer went before them.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-samuel/10#v17)</sup> And when it was told David, he gathered all Israel together, and passed over Jordan, and came to Helam. And the Syrians set themselves in array against David, and fought with him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-samuel/10#v18)</sup> And the Syrians fled before Israel; and David slew the men of seven hundred chariots of the Syrians, and forty thousand horsemen, and smote Shobach the captain of their host, who died there.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-samuel/10#v19)</sup> And when all the kings that were servants to Hadarezer saw that they were smitten before Israel, they made peace with Israel, and served them. So the Syrians feared to help the children of Ammon any more.

- When the Syrians saw they were smitten before Israel, they gathered together, and Hadarezer sent for the Syrians beyond the river with Shobach the captain of the host. David gathered all Israel, passed over Jordan, and the Syrians fled before Israel. David slew the men of seven hundred chariots and forty thousand horsemen and smote Shobach, who died there.
- When all the kings that were servants to Hadarezer saw they were smitten before Israel, they made peace and served them, and the Syrians feared to help the children of Ammon anymore. This is what happens when you mess with the armies of the living God: they got whooped so badly that the Syrians would not even help the Ammonites anymore.
