**[2 Samuel 6:1-5](/bible/2-samuel/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-samuel/6#v1)</sup> Again, David gathered together all the chosen men of Israel, thirty thousand.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-samuel/6#v2)</sup> And David arose, and went with all the people that were with him from Baale of Judah, to bring up from thence the ark of God, whose name is called by the name of the Lord of hosts that dwelleth between the cherubims.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-samuel/6#v3)</sup> And they set the ark of God upon a new cart, and brought it out of the house of Abinadab that was in Gibeah: and Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, drave the new cart.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-samuel/6#v4)</sup> And they brought it out of the house of Abinadab which was at Gibeah, accompanying the ark of God: and Ahio went before the ark.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-samuel/6#v5)</sup> And David and all the house of Israel played before the Lord on all manner of instruments made of fir wood, even on harps, and on psalteries, and on timbrels, and on cornets, and on cymbals.

- David gathered together thirty thousand chosen men of Israel. Not everyone is a warrior, and not everyone goes to the front lines, but everyone in the body is important in some way; the members that seem more feeble are needful, and on them more honor is bestowed.
- David and all the people went from Baale of Judah to bring up the ark of God, whose name is called by the name of the Lord of hosts that dwelleth between the cherubims.
- The ark had been taken from Israel by the Philistines in the days of the judges because of sin, and it had been left in the house of Abinadab. Through the wilderness there had been no house for the Lord, so the ark was moved from place to place.
- They set the ark upon a new cart, and Uzzah and Ahio, the sons of Abinadab, drove it, with Ahio going before the ark, while David and all the house of Israel played before the Lord on harps, psalteries, timbrels, cornets, and cymbals.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 12:22](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v22)</sup> Nay, much more those members of the body, which seem to be more feeble, are necessary:

    The members of the body which seem to be more feeble are necessary; every part in the body has honor.

**[2 Samuel 6:6-11](/bible/2-samuel/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-samuel/6#v6)</sup> And when they came to Nachon’s threshingfloor, Uzzah put forth his hand to the ark of God, and took hold of it; for the oxen shook it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-samuel/6#v7)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was kindled against Uzzah; and God smote him there for his error; and there he died by the ark of God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-samuel/6#v8)</sup> And David was displeased, because the Lord had made a breach upon Uzzah: and he called the name of the place Perez–uzzah to this day.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-samuel/6#v9)</sup> And David was afraid of the Lord that day, and said, How shall the ark of the Lord come to me?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-samuel/6#v10)</sup> So David would not remove the ark of the Lord unto him into the city of David: but David carried it aside into the house of Obed–edom the Gittite.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-samuel/6#v11)</sup> And the ark of the Lord continued in the house of Obed–edom the Gittite three months: and the Lord blessed Obed–edom, and all his household.

- When they came to Nachon's threshingfloor, the oxen shook the cart, and Uzzah put forth his hand and took hold of the ark. The anger of the Lord was kindled, and God smote him for his error, and he died there by the ark.
- Only chosen men could come near the ark. The Lord had warned Aaron not to come at all times into the holy place before the mercy seat that he die not, and had set many stipulations upon it, even after the two sons of Aaron died for offering strange fire. The Most High is holy and not to be handled carelessly.
- Grabbing the ark was a natural reaction, but the Most High had already declared it holy and untouchable. Everything in that day had to be done to a T; we owe thanks that there is mercy through Christ, for the Lord was not playing about His commandments.
- David was displeased that the Lord had made a breach upon Uzzah, and he was afraid of the Lord that day, saying, How shall the ark of the Lord come to me? David had seen the power of the Lord, and his flesh trembled for fear of Him and His judgments.
- David would not remove the ark to the city of David but carried it aside to the house of Obededom the Gittite, where it remained three months; and the Lord blessed Obededom and all his household because of the ark.

  Precepts:

    The LORD said Aaron must not come at all times into the holy place before the mercy seat, that he die not.

    Aaron must put on the holy linen coat and garments and wash his flesh before coming into the holy place.
  - **[Psalm 119:120](/bible/psalms/119#v120)**
    > <sup>[120](/bible/psalms/119#v120)</sup> My flesh trembleth for fear of thee; and I am afraid of thy judgments.

    My flesh trembleth for fear of thee, and I am afraid of thy judgments; so David feared after Uzzah.

**[2 Samuel 6:12-19](/bible/2-samuel/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/6#v12)</sup> And it was told king David, saying, The Lord hath blessed the house of Obed–edom, and all that pertaineth unto him, because of the ark of God. So David went and brought up the ark of God from the house of Obed–edom into the city of David with gladness.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-samuel/6#v13)</sup> And it was so, that when they that bare the ark of the Lord had gone six paces, he sacrificed oxen and fatlings.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/6#v14)</sup> And David danced before the Lord with all his might; and David was girded with a linen ephod.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-samuel/6#v15)</sup> So David and all the house of Israel brought up the ark of the Lord with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-samuel/6#v16)</sup> And as the ark of the Lord came into the city of David, Michal Saul’s daughter looked through a window, and saw king David leaping and dancing before the Lord; and she despised him in her heart.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-samuel/6#v17)</sup> And they brought in the ark of the Lord, and set it in his place, in the midst of the tabernacle that David had pitched for it: and David offered burnt offerings and peace offerings before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-samuel/6#v18)</sup> And as soon as David had made an end of offering burnt offerings and peace offerings, he blessed the people in the name of the Lord of hosts.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-samuel/6#v19)</sup> And he dealt among all the people, even among the whole multitude of Israel, as well to the women as men, to every one a cake of bread, and a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine. So all the people departed every one to his house.

- When David heard the Lord had blessed the house of Obededom because of the ark, he went and brought up the ark to the city of David with gladness. When those that bore it had gone six paces, he sacrificed oxen and fatlings.
- David danced before the Lord with all his might, girded with a linen ephod. He was happy and in the spirit, for the Lord had chosen him to bring the ark back among the people. Israel brought up the ark with shouting and the sound of the trumpet.
- Michal, Saul's daughter, looked through a window and saw David leaping and dancing before the Lord, and she despised him in her heart. This is a spirit to be careful of: coming out of the spirit on the Lord's day and murmuring against a brother who is rejoicing in the Most High.
- David set the ark in the tabernacle he had pitched for it, offered burnt offerings and peace offerings, and blessed the people in the name of the Lord of hosts. He dealt to every one, both men and women, a cake of bread, a good piece of flesh, and a flagon of wine, and the people departed to their houses.

**[2 Samuel 6:20-23](/bible/2-samuel/6#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-samuel/6#v20)</sup> Then David returned to bless his household. And Michal the daughter of Saul came out to meet David, and said, How glorious was the king of Israel to day, who uncovered himself to day in the eyes of the handmaids of his servants, as one of the vain fellows shamelessly uncovereth himself!
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-samuel/6#v21)</sup> And David said unto Michal, It was before the Lord, which chose me before thy father, and before all his house, to appoint me ruler over the people of the Lord, over Israel: therefore will I play before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-samuel/6#v22)</sup> And I will yet be more vile than thus, and will be base in mine own sight: and of the maidservants which thou hast spoken of, of them shall I be had in honour.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-samuel/6#v23)</sup> Therefore Michal the daughter of Saul had no child unto the day of her death.

- David returned to bless his own household, still in a good mood. Michal came out to meet him and said sarcastically, How glorious was the king of Israel today, who uncovered himself in the eyes of the handmaids as one of the vain fellows shamelessly uncovereth himself.
- She had watched from a distance and could think only of the king dancing with his clothes falling, when the Most High had just brought the ark of God back among Israel. She forgot the greatness of the day and let a murmuring spirit take her.
- David answered that he danced before the Lord who chose him before her father to be ruler over Israel, therefore he would play before the Lord, and would yet be more vile and base in his own sight, and of the maidservants he would be had in honor.
- Michal, the daughter of Saul, had no child unto the day of her death; the Lord shut her womb. Do not play with the Most High.
