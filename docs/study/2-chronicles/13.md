Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 13](/bible/2-chronicles/13)

This session covers [2 Chronicles 13](/bible/2-chronicles/13) through 16, which links directly to the account in [1 Kings 15](/bible/1-kings/15). Reading the Kings and the Chronicles together fills in the detail: what Kings gives briefly, the Chronicles often expands. These chapters follow the civil war between the southern kingdom of Judah and the northern kingdom of Israel, the reforms of Asa, and the lesson of trusting the Most High rather than leaning on men. The precepts here keep the two books linked and open up the understanding.

**[2 Chronicles 13:1-3](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v1)</sup> Now in the eighteenth year of king Jeroboam began Abijah to reign over Judah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v2)</sup> He reigned three years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Michaiah the daughter of Uriel of Gibeah. And there was war between Abijah and Jeroboam.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v3)</sup> And Abijah set the battle in array with an army of valiant men of war, even four hundred thousand chosen men: Jeroboam also set the battle in array against him with eight hundred thousand chosen men, being mighty men of valour.

- Abijah begins to reign over Judah in the eighteenth year of Jeroboam the first. War breaks out between Abijah and Jeroboam.
- Abijah sets the battle with 400,000 chosen men; Jeroboam answers with 800,000. Judah is outnumbered two to one, yet Abijah stands on the covenant of the Most High.

**[2 Chronicles 13:4-6](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v4)</sup> And Abijah stood up upon mount Zemaraim, which is in mount Ephraim, and said, Hear me, thou Jeroboam, and all Israel;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v5)</sup> Ought ye not to know that the Lord God of Israel gave the kingdom over Israel to David for ever, even to him and to his sons by a covenant of salt?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v6)</sup> Yet Jeroboam the son of Nebat, the servant of Solomon the son of David, is risen up, and hath rebelled against his lord.

- Standing on mount Zemaraim in mount Ephraim, Abijah declares that the Most High gave the kingdom to David forever by a covenant of salt.
- He names Jeroboam as the servant of Solomon who rose up and rebelled against his lord. To understand that rebellion, the account goes back to [1 Kings 11](/bible/1-kings/11).
  - **[1 Kings 11:26-31](/bible/1-kings/11#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/1-kings/11#v26)</sup> And Jeroboam the son of Nebat, an Ephrathite of Zereda, Solomon’s servant, whose mother’s name was Zeruah, a widow woman, even he lifted up his hand against the king.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/1-kings/11#v27)</sup> And this was the cause that he lifted up his hand against the king: Solomon built Millo, and repaired the breaches of the city of David his father.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/1-kings/11#v28)</sup> And the man Jeroboam was a mighty man of valour: and Solomon seeing the young man that he was industrious, he made him ruler over all the charge of the house of Joseph.
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/1-kings/11#v29)</sup> And it came to pass at that time when Jeroboam went out of Jerusalem, that the prophet Ahijah the Shilonite found him in the way; and he had clad himself with a new garment; and they two were alone in the field:
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/1-kings/11#v30)</sup> And Ahijah caught the new garment that was on him, and rent it in twelve pieces:
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/1-kings/11#v31)</sup> And he said to Jeroboam, Take thee ten pieces: for thus saith the Lord, the God of Israel, Behold, I will rend the kingdom out of the hand of Solomon, and will give ten tribes to thee:

    Jeroboam, Solomon's industrious servant, lifts his hand against the king, and the prophet Ahijah tears his garment in twelve pieces and gives him ten tribes.

**[2 Chronicles 13:7](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v7)</sup> And there are gathered unto him vain men, the children of Belial, and have strengthened themselves against Rehoboam the son of Solomon, when Rehoboam was young and tenderhearted, and could not withstand them.

- Vain men, the children of Belial, gathered to Jeroboam and strengthened themselves against Rehoboam when he was young and tenderhearted and could not withstand them.
- Young and tenderhearted does not mean a small child. Rehoboam was forty and one years old when he began to reign; he simply knew nothing of war, for the land had rest in Solomon's days.
  - **[2 Chronicles 12:13](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v13)</sup> So king Rehoboam strengthened himself in Jerusalem, and reigned: for Rehoboam was one and forty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city which the Lord had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, to put his name there. And his mother’s name was Naamah an Ammonitess.

    Rehoboam was one and forty years old when he began to reign in Jerusalem.

**[2 Chronicles 13:8-9](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v8)</sup> And now ye think to withstand the kingdom of the Lord in the hand of the sons of David; and ye be a great multitude, and there are with you golden calves, which Jeroboam made you for gods.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v9)</sup> Have ye not cast out the priests of the Lord, the sons of Aaron, and the Levites, and have made you priests after the manner of the nations of other lands? so that whosoever cometh to consecrate himself with a young bullock and seven rams, the same may be a priest of them that are no gods.

- Abijah warns the great multitude that trusts in the golden calves Jeroboam made and in the priests he ordained after the manner of the nations.
- Jeroboam cast out the priests of the Most High, the sons of Aaron and the Levites, and made priests of any man who came to consecrate himself, priests of them that are no gods.
  - **[2 Chronicles 11:14](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v14)</sup> For the Levites left their suburbs and their possession, and came to Judah and Jerusalem: for Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off from executing the priest’s office unto the Lord:

    The Levites left their suburbs and came to Judah, for Jeroboam cast them off from the priest's office and ordained his own priests for the high places and the devils.

**[2 Chronicles 13:10-11](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v10)</sup> But as for us, the Lord is our God, and we have not forsaken him; and the priests, which minister unto the Lord, are the sons of Aaron, and the Levites wait upon their business:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v11)</sup> And they burn unto the Lord every morning and every evening burnt sacrifices and sweet incense: the shewbread also set they in order upon the pure table; and the candlestick of gold with the lamps thereof, to burn every evening: for we keep the charge of the Lord our God; but ye have forsaken him.

- Judah answers that the Most High is their God and they have not forsaken him; the sons of Aaron minister and the Levites wait upon their business.
- They burn the daily burnt sacrifices morning and evening, set the shewbread in order, and keep the charge of the Most High that Jeroboam forsook.

    >

    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/3#v7)</sup> And they shall keep his charge, and the charge of the whole congregation before the tabernacle of the congregation, to do the service of the tabernacle.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/3#v8)</sup> And they shall keep all the instruments of the tabernacle of the congregation, and the charge of the children of Israel, to do the service of the tabernacle.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/3#v9)</sup> And thou shalt give the Levites unto Aaron and to his sons: they are wholly given unto him out of the children of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/3#v10)</sup> And thou shalt appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall wait on their priest’s office: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

    The tribe of Levi is given to Aaron and his sons to wait on the priest's office; the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

    The one lamb offered in the morning and the other at evening set the pattern of the daily sacrifice.

**[2 Chronicles 13:12-16](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v12)</sup> And, behold, God himself is with us for our captain, and his priests with sounding trumpets to cry alarm against you. O children of Israel, fight ye not against the Lord God of your fathers; for ye shall not prosper.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v13)</sup> But Jeroboam caused an ambushment to come about behind them: so they were before Judah, and the ambushment was behind them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v14)</sup> And when Judah looked back, behold, the battle was before and behind: and they cried unto the Lord, and the priests sounded with the trumpets.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v15)</sup> Then the men of Judah gave a shout: and as the men of Judah shouted, it came to pass, that God smote Jeroboam and all Israel before Abijah and Judah.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v16)</sup> And the children of Israel fled before Judah: and God delivered them into their hand.

- The Most High himself is Judah's captain, with his priests sounding the trumpets of alarm. Abijah warns Israel not to fight against the God of their fathers.
- Jeroboam sets an ambush behind Judah, so the battle is before and behind. Judah cries unto the Most High, the priests sound the trumpets, the men shout, and the Most High smites Jeroboam and all Israel.

**[2 Chronicles 13:17-20](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v17)</sup> And Abijah and his people slew them with a great slaughter: so there fell down slain of Israel five hundred thousand chosen men.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v18)</sup> Thus the children of Israel were brought under at that time, and the children of Judah prevailed, because they relied upon the Lord God of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v19)</sup> And Abijah pursued after Jeroboam, and took cities from him, Beth–el with the towns thereof, and Jeshanah with the towns thereof, and Ephrain with the towns thereof.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v20)</sup> Neither did Jeroboam recover strength again in the days of Abijah: and the Lord struck him, and he died.

- Five hundred thousand chosen men of Israel fall in this civil war, northern kingdom against southern kingdom. Judah prevails because they relied upon the Most High God of their fathers.
- Abijah pursues Jeroboam and takes cities from him. Jeroboam recovers no strength; the Most High strikes him and he dies.

**[2 Chronicles 13:21-22](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v21)</sup> But Abijah waxed mighty, and married fourteen wives, and begat twenty and two sons, and sixteen daughters.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/13#v22)</sup> And the rest of the acts of Abijah, and his ways, and his sayings, are written in the story of the prophet Iddo.

- Abijah waxes mighty, marries fourteen wives, and begets twenty and two sons and sixteen daughters. His acts are written in the story of the prophet Iddo.
- The great sin the account underlines is Jeroboam casting off the Levites, a law the Most High was very specific about.

    Thou shalt not forsake the Levite within thy gates, for he has no part nor inheritance with thee.
