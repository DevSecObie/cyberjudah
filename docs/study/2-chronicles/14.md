**[2 Chronicles 14:1-5](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v1)</sup> So Abijah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David: and Asa his son reigned in his stead. In his days the land was quiet ten years.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v2)</sup> And Asa did that which was good and right in the eyes of the Lord his God:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v3)</sup> For he took away the altars of the strange gods, and the high places, and brake down the images, and cut down the groves:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v4)</sup> And commanded Judah to seek the Lord God of their fathers, and to do the law and the commandment.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v5)</sup> Also he took away out of all the cities of Judah the high places and the images: and the kingdom was quiet before him.

- Abijah sleeps with his fathers and Asa his son reigns; the land is quiet ten years. Asa does that which is good and right, taking away the altars of the strange gods, the high places, the images, and the groves.
- Asa commands Judah to seek the Most High and to do the law and the commandment. The pattern of Rehoboam warns what happens when a strong king forsakes that law.
  - **[2 Chronicles 11:17](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v17)</sup> So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah, and made Rehoboam the son of Solomon strong, three years: for three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon.

    For three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon and strengthened the kingdom of Judah.
  - **[2 Chronicles 12:1](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when Rehoboam had established the kingdom, and had strengthened himself, he forsook the law of the Lord, and all Israel with him.

    When Rehoboam had established the kingdom and strengthened himself, he forsook the law of the Lord, and all Israel with him.

**[2 Chronicles 14:6-8](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v6)</sup> And he built fenced cities in Judah: for the land had rest, and he had no war in those years; because the Lord had given him rest.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v7)</sup> Therefore he said unto Judah, Let us build these cities, and make about them walls, and towers, gates, and bars, while the land is yet before us; because we have sought the Lord our God, we have sought him, and he hath given us rest on every side. So they built and prospered.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v8)</sup> And Asa had an army of men that bare targets and spears, out of Judah three hundred thousand; and out of Benjamin, that bare shields and drew bows, two hundred and fourscore thousand: all these were mighty men of valour.

- Asa builds fenced cities because the land has rest and no war, for the Most High has given him rest. Rest is not idleness; while the land is before them they build walls, towers, gates, and bars.
- Asa has an army of 300,000 of Judah and 280,000 of Benjamin, all mighty men of valor.
  - **[Numbers 35:4](/bible/numbers/35#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/35#v4)</sup> And the suburbs of the cities, which ye shall give unto the Levites, shall reach from the wall of the city and outward a thousand cubits round about.

    The suburbs of the Levites' cities reach from the wall outward a thousand cubits round about, for the cities have walls.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:52](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v52)**
    > <sup>[52](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v52)</sup> And he shall besiege thee in all thy gates, until thy high and fenced walls come down, wherein thou trustedst, throughout all thy land: and he shall besiege thee in all thy gates throughout all thy land, which the Lord thy God hath given thee.

    A curse on the seed is the enemy besieging the gates until the high and fenced walls come down wherein they trusted.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 10:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/10#v18)</sup> By much slothfulness the building decayeth; and through idleness of the hands the house droppeth through.

    By much slothfulness the building decayeth, so the rest from war was spent building up, not idling.

**[2 Chronicles 14:9-13](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v9)</sup> And there came out against them Zerah the Ethiopian with an host of a thousand thousand, and three hundred chariots; and came unto Mareshah.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v10)</sup> Then Asa went out against him, and they set the battle in array in the valley of Zephathah at Mareshah.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v11)</sup> And Asa cried unto the Lord his God, and said, Lord, it is nothing with thee to help, whether with many, or with them that have no power: help us, O Lord our God; for we rest on thee, and in thy name we go against this multitude. O Lord, thou art our God; let not man prevail against thee.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v12)</sup> So the Lord smote the Ethiopians before Asa, and before Judah; and the Ethiopians fled.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/14#v13)</sup> And Asa and the people that were with him pursued them unto Gerar: and the Ethiopians were overthrown, that they could not recover themselves; for they were destroyed before the Lord, and before his host; and they carried away very much spoil.

- Zerah the Ethiopian, a Cushite, comes out with a host of a thousand thousand and three hundred chariots. Asa cries unto the Most High that it is nothing for him to help with many or with them that have no power.
- The Most High smites the Ethiopians before Asa; they flee and are destroyed, and Judah carries away very much spoil. Faith in the Most High, not the numbers of the enemy, wins the battle.
