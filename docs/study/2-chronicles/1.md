## Introduction

This reading covers [2 Chronicles 1](/bible/2-chronicles/1) through 4. It is a part of the Bible where the history repeats what was written earlier, in the books of Samuel and Kings, sometimes with a different part of the account and sometimes in greater detail. To line up what is read here, go back and read First Kings chapter 3 through chapter 7, which cover the same events concerning Solomon and the building of the house of the Lord. These chapters move from Solomon being strengthened in his kingdom and asking the Most High for wisdom, to his league with Huram king of Tyre, to the beginning of the temple, and to the vessels of brass and gold that Huram made for the house of God. We rehearse the righteous acts of the Most High in the land of our captivity, referencing precept upon precept so that the questions we write down while reading are answered.

**[2 Chronicles 1:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v1)</sup> And Solomon the son of David was strengthened in his kingdom, and the Lord his God was with him, and magnified him exceedingly.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v2)</sup> Then Solomon spake unto all Israel, to the captains of thousands and of hundreds, and to the judges, and to every governor in all Israel, the chief of the fathers.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v3)</sup> So Solomon, and all the congregation with him, went to the high place that was at Gibeon; for there was the tabernacle of the congregation of God, which Moses the servant of the Lord had made in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v4)</sup> But the ark of God had David brought up from Kirjath–jearim to the place which David had prepared for it: for he had pitched a tent for it at Jerusalem.

- Solomon the son of David was strengthened in his kingdom, and the Lord his God was with him and magnified him exceedingly. The Most High established Solomon before he ever built the house.
- Solomon spake to all Israel, to the captains of thousands and of hundreds, to the judges and to every governor, the chief of the fathers, and went with all the congregation to the high place at Gibeon, where the tabernacle of the congregation that Moses had made in the wilderness was.
- The ark of God had already been brought up by David from Kirjathjearim to the place he had prepared for it, for he had pitched a tent for it at Jerusalem. To read that history, go back to Second Samuel 6, and to First Chronicles 15.

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v6)</sup> And Solomon went up thither to the brasen altar before the Lord, which was at the tabernacle of the congregation, and offered a thousand burnt offerings upon it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v7)</sup> In that night did God appear unto Solomon, and said unto him, Ask what I shall give thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v8)</sup> And Solomon said unto God, Thou hast shewed great mercy unto David my father, and hast made me to reign in his stead.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v9)</sup> Now, O Lord God, let thy promise unto David my father be established: for thou hast made me king over a people like the dust of the earth in multitude.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v10)</sup> Give me now wisdom and knowledge, that I may go out and come in before this people: for who can judge this thy people, that is so great?

- The brasen altar that Bezaleel the son of Uri had made was set before the tabernacle of the Lord, and Solomon and the congregation sought unto it. Solomon went up to that brasen altar before the Lord and offered a thousand burnt offerings upon it.
- In that night the Most High appeared unto Solomon and said, Ask what I shall give thee. This same account is told in First Kings chapter 3, where the Lord appeared to Solomon in a dream by night; reading it there gives more detail of what was said.
- Solomon answered that the Lord had shewed great mercy unto David his father and made him to reign in his stead, and asked that the promise unto David be established, for the Lord had made him king over a people like the dust of the earth in multitude.
- Solomon asked for wisdom and knowledge that he might go out and come in before the people, for who could judge so great a people. When you read between Kings and Chronicles and line up the history, you get the full detail of what went on with Solomon and David.

**[2 Chronicles 1:11-17](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v11)</sup> And God said to Solomon, Because this was in thine heart, and thou hast not asked riches, wealth, or honour, nor the life of thine enemies, neither yet hast asked long life; but hast asked wisdom and knowledge for thyself, that thou mayest judge my people, over whom I have made thee king:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v12)</sup> Wisdom and knowledge is granted unto thee; and I will give thee riches, and wealth, and honour, such as none of the kings have had that have been before thee, neither shall there any after thee have the like.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v13)</sup> Then Solomon came from his journey to the high place that was at Gibeon to Jerusalem, from before the tabernacle of the congregation, and reigned over Israel.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v14)</sup> And Solomon gathered chariots and horsemen: and he had a thousand and four hundred chariots, and twelve thousand horsemen, which he placed in the chariot cities, and with the king at Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v15)</sup> And the king made silver and gold at Jerusalem as plenteous as stones, and cedar trees made he as the sycomore trees that are in the vale for abundance.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v16)</sup> And Solomon had horses brought out of Egypt, and linen yarn: the king’s merchants received the linen yarn at a price.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/1#v17)</sup> And they fetched up, and brought forth out of Egypt a chariot for six hundred shekels of silver, and an horse for an hundred and fifty: and so brought they out horses for all the kings of the Hittites, and for the kings of Syria, by their means.

- The Most High said to Solomon that because this was in his heart, and he had not asked riches, wealth, honor, the life of his enemies, nor long life, but wisdom and knowledge to judge the people, wisdom and knowledge were granted unto him.
- The Lord also gave Solomon riches, wealth, and honor such as no king before him had, and none after him would have the like. When a man seeks the things of the Most High first, the other things are added unto him.
- Solomon returned from the high place at Gibeon to Jerusalem and reigned over Israel. He gathered chariots and horsemen, having a thousand and four hundred chariots and twelve thousand horsemen, which he placed in the chariot cities and with the king at Jerusalem.
- The king made silver and gold at Jerusalem as plenteous as stones, and cedars as the sycomore trees in the valley for abundance. Horses were brought out of Egypt, and the merchants received the linen yarn at a price and fetched up horses and chariots for all the kings of the Hittites and of Syria.
