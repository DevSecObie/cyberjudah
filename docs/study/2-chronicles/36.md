**[2 Chronicles 36:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v1)</sup> Then the people of the land took Jehoahaz the son of Josiah, and made him king in his father’s stead in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v2)</sup> Jehoahaz was twenty and three years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v3)</sup> And the king of Egypt put him down at Jerusalem, and condemned the land in an hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v4)</sup> And the king of Egypt made Eliakim his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem, and turned his name to Jehoiakim. And Necho took Jehoahaz his brother, and carried him to Egypt.

- The people of the land take Jehoahaz the son of Josiah and make him king in Jerusalem; he is twenty three years old and reigns three months.
- The king of Egypt puts him down, condemns the land in a hundred talents of silver and a talent of gold, and makes Eliakim his brother king, turning his name to Jehoiakim, and carries Jehoahaz to Egypt.

**[2 Chronicles 36:5-8](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v5)</sup> Jehoiakim was twenty and five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eleven years in Jerusalem: and he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord his God.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v6)</sup> Against him came up Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, and bound him in fetters, to carry him to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v7)</sup> Nebuchadnezzar also carried of the vessels of the house of the Lord to Babylon, and put them in his temple at Babylon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v8)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Jehoiakim, and his abominations which he did, and that which was found in him, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah: and Jehoiachin his son reigned in his stead.

- Jehoiakim is twenty five years old when he begins to reign and reigns eleven years in Jerusalem, doing evil in the sight of the Lord his God, so Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon comes up against him and binds him in fetters to carry him to Babylon.
- Nebuchadnezzar also carries off the vessels of the house of the Lord to Babylon and puts them in his temple. The Most High set a wicked king over the land because his own people would not do what he said.
- The power of the earth is in the hand of the Most High, and in due time he sets over it one that is profitable. When Israel will not rule in righteousness, he raises a Nebuchadnezzar; Israel's purpose was never to be wicked and rule at the same time.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 10:1-4](/bible/sirach/10#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/10#v1)</sup> A wise judge will instruct his people; and the government of a prudent man is well ordered.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/10#v2)</sup> As the judge of the people is himself, so are his officers; and what manner of man the ruler of the city is, such are all they that dwell therein.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/10#v3)</sup> An unwise king destroyeth his people; but through the prudence of them which are in authority the city shall be inhabited.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/10#v4)</sup> The power of the earth is in the hand of the Lord, and in due time he will set over it one that is profitable.

    As the ruler is, so are his people, and the power of the earth is in the hand of the Most High, who sets over it one that is profitable in due time.

**[2 Chronicles 36:9-10](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v9)</sup> Jehoiachin was eight years old when he began to reign, and he reigned three months and ten days in Jerusalem: and he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v10)</sup> And when the year was expired, king Nebuchadnezzar sent, and brought him to Babylon, with the goodly vessels of the house of the Lord, and made Zedekiah his brother king over Judah and Jerusalem.

- Jehoiachin is eight years old when he begins to reign and reigns three months and ten days in Jerusalem, and he does that which is evil in the sight of the Lord.
- When the year is expired, Nebuchadnezzar sends and brings him to Babylon with the goodly vessels of the house of the Lord, and makes Zedekiah his brother king over Judah.

**[2 Chronicles 36:11-16](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v11)</sup> Zedekiah was one and twenty years old when he began to reign, and reigned eleven years in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v12)</sup> And he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord his God, and humbled not himself before Jeremiah the prophet speaking from the mouth of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v13)</sup> And he also rebelled against king Nebuchadnezzar, who had made him swear by God: but he stiffened his neck, and hardened his heart from turning unto the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v14)</sup> Moreover all the chief of the priests, and the people, transgressed very much after all the abominations of the heathen; and polluted the house of the Lord which he had hallowed in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v15)</sup> And the Lord God of their fathers sent to them by his messengers, rising up betimes, and sending; because he had compassion on his people, and on his dwelling place:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v16)</sup> But they mocked the messengers of God, and despised his words, and misused his prophets, until the wrath of the Lord arose against his people, till there was no remedy.

- Zedekiah is twenty one years old when he begins to reign and reigns eleven years, and he does evil in the sight of the Lord his God and humbles not himself before Jeremiah the prophet speaking from the mouth of the Lord.
- He rebels against Nebuchadnezzar who had made him swear by God, and stiffens his neck and hardens his heart from turning to the Lord God of Israel. The chief priests and people transgress very much after all the abominations of the heathen and pollute the house of the Lord.
- The Most High sends to them by his messengers, rising up early and sending because he had compassion, but they mock the messengers, despise his words, and misuse his prophets until the wrath of the Lord arises and there is no remedy.

**[2 Chronicles 36:17-19](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v17)</sup> Therefore he brought upon them the king of the Chaldees, who slew their young men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary, and had no compassion upon young man or maiden, old man, or him that stooped for age: he gave them all into his hand.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v18)</sup> And all the vessels of the house of God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the king, and of his princes; all these he brought to Babylon.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v19)</sup> And they burnt the house of God, and brake down the wall of Jerusalem, and burnt all the palaces thereof with fire, and destroyed all the goodly vessels thereof.

- The Most High brings upon them the king of the Chaldees, who slays their young men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary and has no compassion upon young man or maiden, old man or him that stooped for age.
- He gives them all into Nebuchadnezzar's hand, and all the vessels of the house of God, great and small, and the treasures of the house of the Lord and of the king and his princes are brought to Babylon.
- They burn the house of God, break down the walls of Jerusalem, burn its palaces, and destroy all the goodly vessels, exactly the fierce nation the curses warned of.

**[2 Chronicles 36:20-21](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v20)</sup> And them that had escaped from the sword carried he away to Babylon; where they were servants to him and his sons until the reign of the kingdom of Persia:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v21)</sup> To fulfil the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremiah, until the land had enjoyed her sabbaths: for as long as she lay desolate she kept sabbath, to fulfil threescore and ten years.

- Those who escape the sword are carried away to Babylon, where they are servants to Nebuchadnezzar and his sons until the reign of the kingdom of Persia.
- This fulfills the word of the Lord by the mouth of Jeremiah, that the land would enjoy her sabbaths and lie desolate to fulfill threescore and ten years.

**[2 Chronicles 36:22-23](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v22)</sup> Now in the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, that the word of the Lord spoken by the mouth of Jeremiah might be accomplished, the Lord stirred up the spirit of Cyrus king of Persia, that he made a proclamation throughout all his kingdom, and put it also in writing, saying,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/36#v23)</sup> Thus saith Cyrus king of Persia, All the kingdoms of the earth hath the Lord God of heaven given me; and he hath charged me to build him an house in Jerusalem, which is in Judah. Who is there among you of all his people? The Lord his God be with him, and let him go up.

- In the first year of Cyrus king of Persia, that the word of the Lord by Jeremiah might be accomplished, the Most High stirs up the spirit of Cyrus to make a proclamation throughout all his kingdom.
- Cyrus declares that the Lord God of heaven has given him all the kingdoms of the earth and charged him to build him a house in Jerusalem, and calls any of the Lord's people to go up, showing again that the Most High sets over the earth one that is profitable for his purpose.
