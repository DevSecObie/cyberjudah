**[2 Chronicles 18:1-3](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v1)</sup> Now Jehoshaphat had riches and honour in abundance, and joined affinity with Ahab.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v2)</sup> And after certain years he went down to Ahab to Samaria. And Ahab killed sheep and oxen for him in abundance, and for the people that he had with him, and persuaded him to go up with him to Ramoth–gilead.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v3)</sup> And Ahab king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat king of Judah, Wilt thou go with me to Ramoth–gilead? And he answered him, I am as thou art, and my people as thy people; and we will be with thee in the war.

- Jehoshaphat had riches and honor and abundance, and joined affinity with Ahab through marriage.
- Ahab feasted him and persuaded him to go up to Ramoth Gilead, and Jehoshaphat answered, I am as thou art, my people as thy people, we will be with thee in the war.
- His bond with Ahab was a joining together, like the spirit that witnesses with the spirit: what is mine is yours.

**[2 Chronicles 18:4-5](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v4)</sup> And Jehoshaphat said unto the king of Israel, Enquire, I pray thee, at the word of the Lord to day.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v5)</sup> Therefore the king of Israel gathered together of prophets four hundred men, and said unto them, Shall we go to Ramoth–gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And they said, Go up; for God will deliver it into the king’s hand.

- Jehoshaphat asked to inquire at the word of the Lord before going to battle.
- The king of Israel gathered four hundred prophets who all said go up and prosper, telling the king only what he wanted to hear.

**[2 Chronicles 18:6-7](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v6)</sup> But Jehoshaphat said, Is there not here a prophet of the Lord besides, that we might enquire of him?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v7)</sup> And the king of Israel said unto Jehoshaphat, There is yet one man, by whom we may enquire of the Lord: but I hate him; for he never prophesied good unto me, but always evil: the same is Micaiah the son of Imla. And Jehoshaphat said, Let not the king say so.

- Jehoshaphat asked if there was not still a prophet of the Lord to inquire of.
- Ahab said there was one man, Micaiah, but he hated him because he never prophesied good, only evil, meaning he told the truth.
  - **[Isaiah 30:9-11](/bible/isaiah/30#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/30#v9)</sup> That this is a rebellious people, lying children, children that will not hear the law of the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/30#v10)</sup> Which say to the seers, See not; and to the prophets, Prophesy not unto us right things, speak unto us smooth things, prophesy deceits:
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/30#v11)</sup> Get you out of the way, turn aside out of the path, cause the Holy One of Israel to cease from before us.

    A rebellious people who say to the seers, prophesy smooth things, cause the Holy One to cease.
  - **[2 Timothy 4:2-4](/bible/2-timothy/4#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-timothy/4#v2)</sup> Preach the word; be instant in season, out of season; reprove, rebuke, exhort with all longsuffering and doctrine.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-timothy/4#v3)</sup> For the time will come when they will not endure sound doctrine; but after their own lusts shall they heap to themselves teachers, having itching ears;
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-timothy/4#v4)</sup> And they shall turn away their ears from the truth, and shall be turned unto fables.

    Preach the word in season and out; the time comes when men will not endure sound doctrine.

**[2 Chronicles 18:8-11](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v8)</sup> And the king of Israel called for one of his officers, and said, Fetch quickly Micaiah the son of Imla.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v9)</sup> And the king of Israel and Jehoshaphat king of Judah sat either of them on his throne, clothed in their robes, and they sat in a void place at the entering in of the gate of Samaria; and all the prophets prophesied before them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v10)</sup> And Zedekiah the son of Chenaanah had made him horns of iron, and said, Thus saith the Lord, With these thou shalt push Syria until they be consumed.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v11)</sup> And all the prophets prophesied so, saying, Go up to Ramoth–gilead, and prosper: for the Lord shall deliver it into the hand of the king.

- The king called for Micaiah while the two kings sat in their robes at the gate of Samaria.
- Zedekiah made horns of iron and all the prophets prophesied victory, making a tangible sign to push their false word.

**[2 Chronicles 18:12-13](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v12)</sup> And the messenger that went to call Micaiah spake to him, saying, Behold, the words of the prophets declare good to the king with one assent; let thy word therefore, I pray thee, be like one of theirs, and speak thou good.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v13)</sup> And Micaiah said, As the Lord liveth, even what my God saith, that will I speak.

- The messenger urged Micaiah to speak good like the other prophets, with one assent.
- Micaiah answered, as the Lord liveth, even what my God saith, that will I speak.
  - **[Isaiah 8:20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

    If they speak not according to this word it is because there is no light in them.

**[2 Chronicles 18:14-16](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v14)</sup> And when he was come to the king, the king said unto him, Micaiah, shall we go to Ramoth–gilead to battle, or shall I forbear? And he said, Go ye up, and prosper, and they shall be delivered into your hand.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v15)</sup> And the king said to him, How many times shall I adjure thee that thou say nothing but the truth to me in the name of the Lord?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/18#v16)</sup> Then he said, I did see all Israel scattered upon the mountains, as sheep that have no shepherd: and the Lord said, These have no master; let them return therefore every man to his house in peace.

- Micaiah first answered sarcastically, go up and prosper, until the king adjured him to speak nothing but the truth in the name of the Lord.
- Then he told the real word: he saw all Israel scattered upon the mountains as sheep with no shepherd, and the Lord said these have no master, let them return to their houses in peace.
