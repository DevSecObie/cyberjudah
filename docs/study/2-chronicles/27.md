**[2 Chronicles 27:1-9](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v1)</sup> Jotham was twenty and five years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Jerushah, the daughter of Zadok.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v2)</sup> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Uzziah did: howbeit he entered not into the temple of the Lord. And the people did yet corruptly.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v3)</sup> He built the high gate of the house of the Lord, and on the wall of Ophel he built much.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v4)</sup> Moreover he built cities in the mountains of Judah, and in the forests he built castles and towers.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v5)</sup> He fought also with the king of the Ammonites, and prevailed against them. And the children of Ammon gave him the same year an hundred talents of silver, and ten thousand measures of wheat, and ten thousand of barley. So much did the children of Ammon pay unto him, both the second year, and the third.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v6)</sup> So Jotham became mighty, because he prepared his ways before the Lord his God.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v7)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Jotham, and all his wars, and his ways, lo, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel and Judah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v8)</sup> He was five and twenty years old when he began to reign, and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/27#v9)</sup> And Jotham slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city of David: and Ahaz his son reigned in his stead.

- Jotham was twenty and five years old when he began to reign and reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem. He did that which was right in the sight of the Most High, according to all that his father Uzziah did, only he did not enter into the temple of the Most High. He would not go into the temple, and the people still did corruptly.
- He built the high gate of the house of the Most High and built much on the wall of Ophel, and cities in the mountains, and castles and towers. He fought against the Ammonites and prevailed, and they paid him a hundred talents of silver and ten thousand measures of wheat and ten thousand of barley, both that year and the second and the third.
- Jotham became mighty because he prepared his ways before the Most High. It is a short chapter, but the point is plain: the Most High was with our forefathers when they followed the commandments, and Jotham ordered his ways before God.
