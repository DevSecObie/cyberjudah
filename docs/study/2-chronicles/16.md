**[2 Chronicles 16:1](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v1)</sup> In the six and thirtieth year of the reign of Asa Baasha king of Israel came up against Judah, and built Ramah, to the intent that he might let none go out or come in to Asa king of Judah.

- In the six and thirtieth year of Asa, Baasha king of Israel comes up against Judah and builds Ramah to keep any from going out or coming in to Asa.
- Baasha is trying to stop the northern kingdom from coming down to Judah, because tribes out of Israel had been turning their hearts back to the Most High and joining Asa.

**[2 Chronicles 16:2-6](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v2)</sup> Then Asa brought out silver and gold out of the treasures of the house of the Lord and of the king’s house, and sent to Ben–hadad king of Syria, that dwelt at Damascus, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v3)</sup> There is a league between me and thee, as there was between my father and thy father: behold, I have sent thee silver and gold; go, break thy league with Baasha king of Israel, that he may depart from me.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v4)</sup> And Ben–hadad hearkened unto king Asa, and sent the captains of his armies against the cities of Israel; and they smote Ijon, and Dan, and Abel–maim, and all the store cities of Naphtali.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v5)</sup> And it came to pass, when Baasha heard it, that he left off building of Ramah, and let his work cease.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v6)</sup> Then Asa the king took all Judah; and they carried away the stones of Ramah, and the timber thereof, wherewith Baasha was building; and he built therewith Geba and Mizpah.

- Instead of crying to the Most High as he did against Zerah, Asa takes silver and gold out of the house of the Lord and the king's house and hires Benhadad king of Syria to break his league with Baasha.
- Benhadad smites the cities of Israel, so Baasha leaves off building Ramah. Asa carries away the stones and timber of Ramah and builds Geba and Mizpah with them.

**[2 Chronicles 16:7-10](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v7)</sup> And at that time Hanani the seer came to Asa king of Judah, and said unto him, Because thou hast relied on the king of Syria, and not relied on the Lord thy God, therefore is the host of the king of Syria escaped out of thine hand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v8)</sup> Were not the Ethiopians and the Lubims a huge host, with very many chariots and horsemen? yet, because thou didst rely on the Lord, he delivered them into thine hand.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v9)</sup> For the eyes of the Lord run to and fro throughout the whole earth, to shew himself strong in the behalf of them whose heart is perfect toward him. Herein thou hast done foolishly: therefore from henceforth thou shalt have wars.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v10)</sup> Then Asa was wroth with the seer, and put him in a prison house; for he was in a rage with him because of this thing. And Asa oppressed some of the people the same time.

- Hanani the seer comes to Asa and rebukes him for relying on the king of Syria and not on the Most High, so the host of Syria has escaped his hand. Had he relied on the Most High, he would have delivered them as he did the Ethiopians.
- The eyes of the Most High run to and fro throughout the whole earth to show himself strong for them whose heart is perfect. Asa is wroth, puts the seer in prison, and oppresses some of the people.

**[2 Chronicles 16:11-14](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v11)</sup> And, behold, the acts of Asa, first and last, lo, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v12)</sup> And Asa in the thirty and ninth year of his reign was diseased in his feet, until his disease was exceeding great: yet in his disease he sought not to the Lord, but to the physicians.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v13)</sup> And Asa slept with his fathers, and died in the one and fortieth year of his reign.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/16#v14)</sup> And they buried him in his own sepulchres, which he had made for himself in the city of David, and laid him in the bed which was filled with sweet odours and divers kinds of spices prepared by the apothecaries’ art: and they made a very great burning for him.

- In the thirty and ninth year of his reign Asa is diseased in his feet, exceeding great, yet he seeks not to the Most High but to the physicians. The rebuke is not against physicians but against seeking them in place of the Most High, from whom healing cometh.
- Asa dies in the one and fortieth year of his reign and is buried in his own sepulchre in the city of David, on a bed of sweet odours and spices, with a very great burning.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 38:1-2](/bible/sirach/38#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/38#v1)</sup> Honour a physician with the honour due unto him for the uses which ye may have of him: for the Lord hath created him.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/38#v2)</sup> For of the most High cometh healing, and he shall receive honour of the king.

    Honour the physician, but healing itself comes from the Most High.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 38:9-10](/bible/sirach/38#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/38#v9)</sup> My son, in thy sickness be not negligent: but pray unto the Lord, and he will make thee whole.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/38#v10)</sup> Leave off from sin, and order thine hands aright, and cleanse thy heart from all wickedness.

    In sickness pray to the Most High and leave off from sin, then seek the physician.
