**[2 Chronicles 3:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v1)</sup> Then Solomon began to build the house of the Lord at Jerusalem in mount Moriah, where the Lord appeared unto David his father, in the place that David had prepared in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v2)</sup> And he began to build in the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v3)</sup> Now these are the things wherein Solomon was instructed for the building of the house of God. The length by cubits after the first measure was threescore cubits, and the breadth twenty cubits.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v4)</sup> And the porch that was in the front of the house, the length of it was according to the breadth of the house, twenty cubits, and the height was an hundred and twenty: and he overlaid it within with pure gold.

- Solomon began to build the house of the Lord at Jerusalem in mount Moriah, where the Lord appeared unto David his father, in the place David had prepared in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite. This threshingfloor was read of a few days ago in First Chronicles 21.
- He began to build in the second day of the second month, in the fourth year of his reign. There is a mix here of First Kings 6 and 7, but Kings goes into much more detail on the building.
- These are the things wherein Solomon was instructed for the building of the house of God: the length was threescore cubits, the breadth twenty cubits, and the porch in front was twenty cubits long according to the breadth of the house, and the height was a hundred and twenty. He overlaid it within with pure gold.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Chronicles 21:18](/bible/1-chronicles/21#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/1-chronicles/21#v18)</sup> Then the angel of the Lord commanded Gad to say to David, that David should go up, and set up an altar unto the Lord in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite.

    The angel commanded David to set up an altar unto the Lord in the threshingfloor of Ornan the Jebusite.

**[2 Chronicles 3:5-9](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v5)</sup> And the greater house he cieled with fir tree, which he overlaid with fine gold, and set thereon palm trees and chains.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v6)</sup> And he garnished the house with precious stones for beauty: and the gold was gold of Parvaim.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v7)</sup> He overlaid also the house, the beams, the posts, and the walls thereof, and the doors thereof, with gold; and graved cherubims on the walls.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v8)</sup> And he made the most holy house, the length whereof was according to the breadth of the house, twenty cubits, and the breadth thereof twenty cubits: and he overlaid it with fine gold, amounting to six hundred talents.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v9)</sup> And the weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. And he overlaid the upper chambers with gold.

- The greater house Solomon cieled with fir tree, overlaid with fine gold, and set thereon palm trees and chains. He garnished the house with precious stones for beauty, and the gold was gold of Parvaim. He overlaid the house, the beams, the posts, the walls, and the doors with gold, even real gold through and through, not the counterfeit metal of today.
- He graved cherubims on the walls. Our unlearned Christian brothers and sisters, and some in other doctrines, say a man cannot have images at all, but that is not what the Bible says. The command in [Exodus 20](/bible/exodus/20) forbids making a graven image to bow down to and serve, not the mere carving of a figure.
- The curse in [Deuteronomy 27](/bible/deuteronomy/27) falls on the man that maketh a graven or molten image and putteth it in a secret place. A wall in a man's house is not a secret place, for it is seen plainly when a man enters the home; it is not hidden.
- [Ezekiel 8](/bible/ezekiel/8) shows what a secret place is: the Lord had the prophet dig through a wall into a hidden chamber where the ancients of Israel practiced idolatry in the dark, saying the Lord seeth us not. That is the abomination, worshipping images in secret, not the goodly images made openly for the house of the Lord.
- Solomon was not bowing down to the cherubims and images he made. [Hosea 10](/bible/hosea/10) speaks of goodly images, and these goodly images are the very ones read of here in the building of the house of the Lord.
- He made the most holy house twenty cubits long and twenty broad, overlaid with fine gold to the amount of six hundred talents, and the weight of the nails was fifty shekels of gold. He overlaid the upper chambers also with gold.

**[2 Chronicles 3:10-17](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v10)</sup> And in the most holy house he made two cherubims of image work, and overlaid them with gold.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v11)</sup> And the wings of the cherubims were twenty cubits long: one wing of the one cherub was five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house: and the other wing was likewise five cubits, reaching to the wing of the other cherub.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v12)</sup> And one wing of the other cherub was five cubits, reaching to the wall of the house: and the other wing was five cubits also, joining to the wing of the other cherub.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v13)</sup> The wings of these cherubims spread themselves forth twenty cubits: and they stood on their feet, and their faces were inward.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v14)</sup> And he made the vail of blue, and purple, and crimson, and fine linen, and wrought cherubims thereon.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v15)</sup> Also he made before the house two pillars of thirty and five cubits high, and the chapiter that was on the top of each of them was five cubits.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v16)</sup> And he made chains, as in the oracle, and put them on the heads of the pillars; and made an hundred pomegranates, and put them on the chains.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/3#v17)</sup> And he reared up the pillars before the temple, one on the right hand, and the other on the left; and called the name of that on the right hand Jachin, and the name of that on the left Boaz.

- In the most holy house Solomon made two cherubims of image work and overlaid them with gold. The wings of the cherubims were twenty cubits long altogether, each wing five cubits, one wing reaching to the wall and the other joining to the wing of the other cherub, and they stood on their feet with their faces inward.
- He made the vail of blue, purple, crimson, and fine linen, and wrought cherubims thereon. Even the vail bore the figures of the cherubims, made openly for the service of the Most High.
- He made before the house two pillars thirty and five cubits high, and the chapiter on the top of each was five cubits. He made chains as in the oracle and put them on the heads of the pillars, and made a hundred pomegranates and put them on the chains.
- He reared up the pillars before the temple, one on the right hand and the other on the left, and called the name of the one on the right Jachin and the name of the one on the left Boaz. This same detail is read in First Kings chapter 7.
