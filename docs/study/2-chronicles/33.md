## Introduction

This session covers the last four chapters of 2 Chronicles, the closing of the kingdom of Judah down to the captivity and the first year of Cyrus. It moves through four young kings: Manasseh who began to reign at twelve, Amon at twenty two, and Josiah at eight, and then the last kings under whom Judah went into Babylon. The thread running through it is the difference between a heart that humbles itself before the Most High and a heart that stiffens its neck. Manasseh does worse than the heathen, is carried to Babylon in chains, and is only restored when he humbles himself and prays. Josiah, a wise child, does that which is right, purges the land, and keeps a Passover like no other, yet the sins of the fathers still bring the curses written in the law. Read these chapters as the record of why the Most High sent his people up into captivity, and of the mercy given to any who humble themselves.

**[2 Chronicles 33:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v1)</sup> Manasseh was twelve years old when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty and five years in Jerusalem:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v2)</sup> But did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, like unto the abominations of the heathen, whom the Lord had cast out before the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v3)</sup> For he built again the high places which Hezekiah his father had broken down, and he reared up altars for Baalim, and made groves, and worshipped all the host of heaven, and served them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v4)</sup> Also he built altars in the house of the Lord, whereof the Lord had said, In Jerusalem shall my name be for ever.

- Manasseh begins to reign at twelve years old and reigns fifty five years in Jerusalem, but he does that which is evil in the sight of the Lord, after the abominations of the heathen the Most High had cast out before the children of Israel.
- He rebuilds the high places his father Hezekiah had broken down, rears up altars for Baal, makes groves, and worships all the host of heaven and serves them.
- He builds altars even in the house of the Lord, the very place of which the Most High had said, In Jerusalem shall my name be for ever.

**[2 Chronicles 33:5-9](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v5)</sup> And he built altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v6)</sup> And he caused his children to pass through the fire in the valley of the son of Hinnom: also he observed times, and used enchantments, and used witchcraft, and dealt with a familiar spirit, and with wizards: he wrought much evil in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v7)</sup> And he set a carved image, the idol which he had made, in the house of God, of which God had said to David and to Solomon his son, In this house, and in Jerusalem, which I have chosen before all the tribes of Israel, will I put my name for ever:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v8)</sup> Neither will I any more remove the foot of Israel from out of the land which I have appointed for your fathers; so that they will take heed to do all that I have commanded them, according to the whole law and the statutes and the ordinances by the hand of Moses.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v9)</sup> So Manasseh made Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to err, and to do worse than the heathen, whom the Lord had destroyed before the children of Israel.

- He builds altars for all the host of heaven in the two courts of the house of the Lord, and causes his children to pass through the fire in the valley of the son of Hinnom, which is child sacrifice, the same spirit as abortion today.
- He observes times, uses enchantments, uses witchcraft, and deals with a familiar spirit and with wizards, working much evil to provoke the Most High to anger. Every one of these things is a commandment he broke.
- He sets a carved idol in the house of God, the very house of which the Most High said to David and Solomon, In this house and in Jerusalem will I put my name for ever, and Manasseh makes Judah do worse than the heathen the Lord destroyed before them.

**[2 Chronicles 33:10-11](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v10)</sup> And the Lord spake to Manasseh, and to his people: but they would not hearken.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v11)</sup> Wherefore the Lord brought upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria, which took Manasseh among the thorns, and bound him with fetters, and carried him to Babylon.

- The Most High speaks to Manasseh and his people, but they will not hearken, so he brings upon them the captains of the host of the king of Assyria.
- They take Manasseh among the thorns, bind him with chains, and carry him to Babylon. The kings of Israel that were once kings are brought down into captivity, exactly as the curses warned.

  - **[Deuteronomy 28:36](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v36)</sup> The Lord shall bring thee, and thy king which thou shalt set over thee, unto a nation which neither thou nor thy fathers have known; and there shalt thou serve other gods, wood and stone.

    The curse said the Most High would bring the king they set over themselves into a nation they had not known, to serve other gods of wood and stone.
  - **[1 Esdras 8:77](/bible/1-esdras/8#v77)**
    > <sup>[77](/bible/1-esdras/8#v77)</sup> And for our sins and our fathers’ we with our brethren and our kings and our priests were given up unto the kings of the earth, to the sword, and to captivity, and for a prey with shame, unto this day.

    For our sins and our fathers, we with our brethren and our kings and our priests were given up into the hands of the kings of the earth.

**[2 Chronicles 33:12-13](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v12)</sup> And when he was in affliction, he besought the Lord his God, and humbled himself greatly before the God of his fathers,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v13)</sup> And prayed unto him: and he was intreated of him, and heard his supplication, and brought him again to Jerusalem into his kingdom. Then Manasseh knew that the Lord he was God.

- When he is in affliction, Manasseh beseeches the Lord his God and humbles himself greatly before the God of his fathers, and prays unto him.
- The Most High is entreated of him, hears his supplication, and brings him again to Jerusalem into his kingdom, and then Manasseh knows that the Lord he was God. This is the pattern of [2 Chronicles 7:14](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v14), that a people called by his name who humble themselves will be heard.

**[2 Chronicles 33:14-17](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v14)</sup> Now after this he built a wall without the city of David, on the west side of Gihon, in the valley, even to the entering in at the fish gate, and compassed about Ophel, and raised it up a very great height, and put captains of war in all the fenced cities of Judah.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v15)</sup> And he took away the strange gods, and the idol out of the house of the Lord, and all the altars that he had built in the mount of the house of the Lord, and in Jerusalem, and cast them out of the city.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v16)</sup> And he repaired the altar of the Lord, and sacrificed thereon peace offerings and thank offerings, and commanded Judah to serve the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v17)</sup> Nevertheless the people did sacrifice still in the high places, yet unto the Lord their God only.

- After this he builds a wall for the city of David, sets captains of war in the fenced cities of Judah, and takes away the strange gods and the idol out of the house of the Lord, casting them out of the city.
- He repairs the altar of the Lord, sacrifices peace offerings and thank offerings on it, and commands Judah to serve the Lord God of Israel, though the people still sacrifice in the high places, only unto the Lord their God.

  - **[Deuteronomy 7:25](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v25)</sup> The graven images of their gods shall ye burn with fire: thou shalt not desire the silver or gold that is on them, nor take it unto thee, lest thou be snared therein: for it is an abomination to the Lord thy God.

    The graven images of the nations were to be burned with fire and not desired, for they are an abomination to the Most High, the example Manasseh follows in casting down the idols.

**[2 Chronicles 33:18-20](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v18)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Manasseh, and his prayer unto his God, and the words of the seers that spake to him in the name of the Lord God of Israel, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Israel.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v19)</sup> His prayer also, and how God was intreated of him, and all his sin, and his trespass, and the places wherein he built high places, and set up groves and graven images, before he was humbled: behold, they are written among the sayings of the seers.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v20)</sup> So Manasseh slept with his fathers, and they buried him in his own house: and Amon his son reigned in his stead.

- The rest of the acts of Manasseh, and his prayer unto his God, are written in the book of the kings of Israel, and his prayer and how the Most High was entreated of him are written among the sayings of the seers.
- This is the prayer of Manasseh, a writing of the Apocrypha found before the Maccabees, which is part of the Bible. Manasseh sleeps with his fathers and Amon his son reigns in his stead.

  - **[Prayer of Manasses 1:1](/bible/prayer-of-manasseh/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/prayer-of-manasseh/1#v1)</sup> O Lord, Almighty God of our fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and of their righteous seed;

    The prayer of Manasseh king of Judah, found before the books of Maccabees, is his cry to the God of his fathers when he was held in Babylon.

**[2 Chronicles 33:21-25](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v21)</sup> Amon was two and twenty years old when he began to reign, and reigned two years in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v22)</sup> But he did that which was evil in the sight of the Lord, as did Manasseh his father: for Amon sacrificed unto all the carved images which Manasseh his father had made, and served them;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v23)</sup> And humbled not himself before the Lord, as Manasseh his father had humbled himself; but Amon trespassed more and more.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v24)</sup> And his servants conspired against him, and slew him in his own house.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-chronicles/33#v25)</sup> But the people of the land slew all them that had conspired against king Amon; and the people of the land made Josiah his son king in his stead.

- Amon is twenty two years old when he begins to reign and reigns two years in Jerusalem, doing evil in the sight of the Lord as Manasseh his father, sacrificing to all the carved images Manasseh had made.
- But Amon does not humble himself before the Lord as his father had humbled himself; instead he trespasses more and more, and his own servants conspire against him and slay him in his house.
- The people of the land slay the conspirators and make Josiah his son king in his stead. A sentence not executed speedily set his heart to do more evil, and his sins abounded to his own destruction.

  - **[Ecclesiasticus 23:1-2](/bible/sirach/23#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/23#v1)</sup> O Lord, Father and Governor of all my whole life, leave me not to their counsels, and let me not fall by them.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/23#v2)</sup> Who will set scourges over my thoughts, and the discipline of wisdom over mine heart? that they spare me not for mine ignorances, and it pass not by my sins:

    A humble man asks the Most High to scourge his thoughts and discipline his heart when he errs, so his ignorance does not increase and his sins abound to his destruction.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 8:11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)</sup> Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

    Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, the heart of men is fully set in them to do evil, which is why Amon's evil increased.
