## Introduction

This session covers the reign of Hezekiah king of Judah, one of the great reformers among the children of Israel. Where his fathers had turned their backs on the Most High, Hezekiah reopens the temple, sanctifies the Levites, keeps the Passover, restores the tithe, and stands against the king of Assyria. The thread running through all four chapters is a man who did that which was right in the sight of the Lord and set the service of the house of God back in order, doing everything with all his heart so that he prospered. Read these chapters as the pattern for how a people returns to the Most High after captivity and forsaking.

**[2 Chronicles 29:1-7](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v1)</sup> Hezekiah began to reign when he was five and twenty years old, and he reigned nine and twenty years in Jerusalem. And his mother’s name was Abijah, the daughter of Zechariah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v2)</sup> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v3)</sup> He in the first year of his reign, in the first month, opened the doors of the house of the Lord, and repaired them.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v4)</sup> And he brought in the priests and the Levites, and gathered them together into the east street,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v5)</sup> And said unto them, Hear me, ye Levites, sanctify now yourselves, and sanctify the house of the Lord God of your fathers, and carry forth the filthiness out of the holy place.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v6)</sup> For our fathers have trespassed, and done that which was evil in the eyes of the Lord our God, and have forsaken him, and have turned away their faces from the habitation of the Lord, and turned their backs.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v7)</sup> Also they have shut up the doors of the porch, and put out the lamps, and have not burned incense nor offered burnt offerings in the holy place unto the God of Israel.

- Hezekiah begins to reign at twenty five years old and does that which is right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that David his father had done.
- In the first month of his first year he opens the doors of the house of the Lord and repairs them, then brings in the priests and Levites and charges them to sanctify themselves and carry the filthiness out of the holy place.
- He names the sin plainly: the fathers had trespassed, forsaken the Most High, turned their backs on his habitation, shut the doors, put out the lamps, and stopped burning incense and offering burnt offerings.

**[2 Chronicles 29:8-9](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v8)</sup> Wherefore the wrath of the Lord was upon Judah and Jerusalem, and he hath delivered them to trouble, to astonishment, and to hissing, as ye see with your eyes.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v9)</sup> For, lo, our fathers have fallen by the sword, and our sons and our daughters and our wives are in captivity for this.

- Because of this forsaking, the wrath of the Lord was upon Judah and Jerusalem, and he delivered them to trouble, to astonishment, and to hissing, so that their fathers fell by the sword and their sons, daughters, and wives went into captivity.
- The same warning was spoken over Solomon: turning from the commandments would make Israel a proverb and a byword, an astonishment to all who passed by. That is exactly how the nations look at scattered Israel today.

>

- Hezekiah purposes in his heart to make a covenant with the Lord God of Israel so his fierce wrath may turn away, and charges the Levites not to be negligent since the Most High chose them to minister and burn incense.
- The Levites are named and sanctify themselves, cleansing the house of the Lord and carrying every unclean thing out to the brook Kidron over sixteen days.
- They report to the king that they have cleansed all the house of the Lord, the altar of burnt offering, the showbread table, and even the vessels that king Ahaz had cast away in his transgression.

**[2 Chronicles 29:20-24](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v20)</sup> Then Hezekiah the king rose early, and gathered the rulers of the city, and went up to the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v21)</sup> And they brought seven bullocks, and seven rams, and seven lambs, and seven he goats, for a sin offering for the kingdom, and for the sanctuary, and for Judah. And he commanded the priests the sons of Aaron to offer them on the altar of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v22)</sup> So they killed the bullocks, and the priests received the blood, and sprinkled it on the altar: likewise, when they had killed the rams, they sprinkled the blood upon the altar: they killed also the lambs, and they sprinkled the blood upon the altar.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v23)</sup> And they brought forth the he goats for the sin offering before the king and the congregation; and they laid their hands upon them:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v24)</sup> And the priests killed them, and they made reconciliation with their blood upon the altar, to make an atonement for all Israel: for the king commanded that the burnt offering and the sin offering should be made for all Israel.

- Hezekiah rises early, gathers the rulers, and brings seven bullocks, seven rams, seven lambs, and seven he goats for a sin offering for the kingdom, the sanctuary, and Judah.
- The priests kill the animals and sprinkle the blood on the altar, making reconciliation with the blood to make an atonement for all Israel.
- Under the law, atonement required animal sacrifice, but the reconciliation the Most High provides now is through the death of his son. You cannot be reconciled to someone you were never joined to, so the reconciliation is for the house of Israel his people.

> <sup>[35](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v35)</sup> And also the burnt offerings were in abundance, with the fat of the peace offerings, and the drink offerings for every burnt offering. So the service of the house of the Lord was set in order.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/2-chronicles/29#v36)</sup> And Hezekiah rejoiced, and all the people, that God had prepared the people: for the thing was done suddenly.

- Once the people are consecrated they bring so many sacrifices and thank offerings that the sanctified priests are too few to flay all the burnt offerings, so the rest of the Levites help until the work is ended.
- The Levites were more upright in heart to sanctify themselves than the priests, and the burnt offerings, fat, and drink offerings are in abundance.
- The service of the house of the Lord is set in order, and Hezekiah and the people rejoice that the thing was done suddenly, done decently and in order.
