**[2 Chronicles 24:1-3](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v1)</sup> Joash was seven years old when he began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Zibiah of Beer–sheba.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v2)</sup> And Joash did that which was right in the sight of the Lord all the days of Jehoiada the priest.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v3)</sup> And Jehoiada took for him two wives; and he begat sons and daughters.

- Joash was seven years old when he began to reign and reigned forty years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.
- Joash did that which was right in the sight of the Most High all the days of Jehoiada the priest, because that is what he was taught.
- He had seen the wicked men of Israel try to kill him, so he followed the man who showed him the ropes; Jehoiada taught him how to be a righteous king and keep the commandments.
- A king had a job to do and could not simply sit back; he had a kingdom to watch over, and he had the priests there to help him do it.

**[2 Chronicles 24:4-7](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v4)</sup> And it came to pass after this, that Joash was minded to repair the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v5)</sup> And he gathered together the priests and the Levites, and said to them, Go out unto the cities of Judah, and gather of all Israel money to repair the house of your God from year to year, and see that ye hasten the matter. Howbeit the Levites hastened it not.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v6)</sup> And the king called for Jehoiada the chief, and said unto him, Why hast thou not required of the Levites to bring in out of Judah and out of Jerusalem the collection, according to the commandment of Moses the servant of the Lord, and of the congregation of Israel, for the tabernacle of witness?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v7)</sup> For the sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken up the house of God; and also all the dedicated things of the house of the Lord did they bestow upon Baalim.

- Joash was minded to repair the house of the Most High and gathered the priests and Levites, telling them to go to the cities of Judah and gather money of all Israel to repair the house from year to year, and to hasten the matter.
- The Levites did not hasten it, so the king called Jehoiada the chief and asked why he had not required of the Levites the collection appointed by Moses for the tabernacle of witness.
- Money was needed because Israel did not let the workmen labor for nothing; when men did the jobs they were paid, for the workman is worthy of his hire, and we dealt righteously with our people.
- The sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken up the house of God and bestowed all its dedicated things upon Baalim, which is why so much repair was needed.

**[2 Chronicles 24:8-14](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v8)</sup> And at the king’s commandment they made a chest, and set it without at the gate of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v9)</sup> And they made a proclamation through Judah and Jerusalem, to bring in to the Lord the collection that Moses the servant of God laid upon Israel in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v10)</sup> And all the princes and all the people rejoiced, and brought in, and cast into the chest, until they had made an end.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v11)</sup> Now it came to pass, that at what time the chest was brought unto the king’s office by the hand of the Levites, and when they saw that there was much money, the king’s scribe and the high priest’s officer came and emptied the chest, and took it, and carried it to his place again. Thus they did day by day, and gathered money in abundance.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v12)</sup> And the king and Jehoiada gave it to such as did the work of the service of the house of the Lord, and hired masons and carpenters to repair the house of the Lord, and also such as wrought iron and brass to mend the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v13)</sup> So the workmen wrought, and the work was perfected by them, and they set the house of God in his state, and strengthened it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v14)</sup> And when they had finished it, they brought the rest of the money before the king and Jehoiada, whereof were made vessels for the house of the Lord, even vessels to minister, and to offer withal, and spoons, and vessels of gold and silver. And they offered burnt offerings in the house of the Lord continually all the days of Jehoiada.

- At the king's commandment they made a chest and set it at the gate of the house of the Most High, and made a proclamation to bring in the collection Moses laid upon Israel in the wilderness, and the princes and people rejoiced and cast in until they made an end.
- Day by day the king's scribe and the high priest's officer emptied the chest and gathered money in abundance, and the king and Jehoiada gave it to those who did the work, hiring masons, carpenters, and workers of iron and brass.
- Money was a defense here because it paid the skilled brothers who got the temple work done; we must deal righteously with our people today, treating a brother's business as a place to help the family and the body.
- Not everyone was given the same task; masons, carpenters, and metalworkers each had their charge, and every man had to listen to a manager, for business requires order and submission to authority.
- The workmen perfected the work and set the house of God in its state, then brought the rest of the money to make vessels of gold and silver to minister and offer with, and they offered burnt offerings continually all the days of Jehoiada.
  - **[Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

    When a man keeps and meditates on the law and does it, he makes his way prosperous and has good success, which is why the willing work on the temple succeeded.

**[2 Chronicles 24:15-16](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v15)</sup> But Jehoiada waxed old, and was full of days when he died; an hundred and thirty years old was he when he died.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v16)</sup> And they buried him in the city of David among the kings, because he had done good in Israel, both toward God, and toward his house.

- Jehoiada waxed old and was full of days and died, being a hundred and thirty years old at his death.
- They buried him in the city of David among the kings, because he had done good in Israel both toward the Most High and toward his house.
- That a priest was buried among the kings shows how much respect and diligence this man had earned; his faithfulness set him alongside the rulers of the land.

**[2 Chronicles 24:17-19](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v17)</sup> Now after the death of Jehoiada came the princes of Judah, and made obeisance to the king. Then the king hearkened unto them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v18)</sup> And they left the house of the Lord God of their fathers, and served groves and idols: and wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem for this their trespass.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v19)</sup> Yet he sent prophets to them, to bring them again unto the Lord; and they testified against them: but they would not give ear.

- After the death of Jehoiada the princes of Judah came and made obeisance to the king, and the king hearkened to them.
- They left the house of the Most High and served groves and idols, and wrath came upon Judah and Jerusalem for their trespass.
- The Most High sent prophets to bring them again unto Him, and they testified against them, but the people would not give ear.
- As soon as Jehoiada died, Israel went off again, because the king was weak and started listening to those who served other gods; this teaches the lesson that lust drives Israel to idolatry, and that the Most High will always send prophets to warn them.

**[2 Chronicles 24:20-22](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v20)</sup> And the Spirit of God came upon Zechariah the son of Jehoiada the priest, which stood above the people, and said unto them, Thus saith God, Why transgress ye the commandments of the Lord, that ye cannot prosper? because ye have forsaken the Lord, he hath also forsaken you.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v21)</sup> And they conspired against him, and stoned him with stones at the commandment of the king in the court of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v22)</sup> Thus Joash the king remembered not the kindness which Jehoiada his father had done to him, but slew his son. And when he died, he said, The Lord look upon it, and require it.

- The spirit of God came upon Zechariah the son of Jehoiada the priest, who stood above the people and asked why they transgressed the commandments so that they could not prosper, telling them that because they had forsaken the Most High, He had also forsaken them.
- They conspired against Zechariah and stoned him with stones at the commandment of the king, in the court of the house of the Most High.
- When the prophet came to correct the king, the king had him killed for telling him the truth; this is the same spirit our people show when a laborer brings the medicine and is met with violence.
- As Zechariah died he said, The Most High look upon it and require it. He prayed for vengeance on his murderer, and this is scripture; it is right to pray for the downfall of enemies who do much wickedness, even while we pray for the recovery and repentance of our own people.
  - **[Malachi 4:6](/bible/malachi/4#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/malachi/4#v6)</sup> And he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers, lest I come and smite the earth with a curse.

    The Most High sends the prophet to turn the hearts of the fathers and children, lest He come and smite the earth with a curse; our people are destroyed for lack of knowledge.
  - **[2 Chronicles 15:2](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v2)</sup> And he went out to meet Asa, and said unto him, Hear ye me, Asa, and all Judah and Benjamin; The Lord is with you, while ye be with him; and if ye seek him, he will be found of you; but if ye forsake him, he will forsake you.

    If we seek the Most High He will be found, but if we forsake Him He will forsake us, the very charge Zechariah brought against Judah.

**[2 Chronicles 24:23-27](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v23)</sup> And it came to pass at the end of the year, that the host of Syria came up against him: and they came to Judah and Jerusalem, and destroyed all the princes of the people from among the people, and sent all the spoil of them unto the king of Damascus.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v24)</sup> For the army of the Syrians came with a small company of men, and the Lord delivered a very great host into their hand, because they had forsaken the Lord God of their fathers. So they executed judgment against Joash.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v25)</sup> And when they were departed from him, (for they left him in great diseases,) his own servants conspired against him for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada the priest, and slew him on his bed, and he died: and they buried him in the city of David, but they buried him not in the sepulchres of the kings.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v26)</sup> And these are they that conspired against him; Zabad the son of Shimeath an Ammonitess, and Jehozabad the son of Shimrith a Moabitess.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v27)</sup> Now concerning his sons, and the greatness of the burdens laid upon him, and the repairing of the house of God, behold, they are written in the story of the book of the kings. And Amaziah his son reigned in his stead.

- At the end of the year the host of Syria came up against Joash, destroyed the princes of the people, and sent the spoil to the king of Damascus.
- The Syrians came with a small company, yet the Most High delivered a very great host of Judah into their hand, because Judah had forsaken the Most High God of their fathers; so judgment was executed on Joash for killing Zechariah.
- They left Joash in great diseases, and his own servants conspired against him for the blood of the sons of Jehoiada and slew him on his bed, and he was buried in the city of David but not in the sepulchres of the kings.
- Joash began by doing right under Jehoiada's guidance, repairing the house and clearing out the idols, but none of that was remembered in the end because of the wickedness he turned to after being warned.
- Amaziah his son reigned in his stead; the fuller record of Joash is written in the story of the book of the kings.
  - **[Ezekiel 3:20](/bible/ezekiel/3#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ezekiel/3#v20)</sup> Again, When a righteous man doth turn from his righteousness, and commit iniquity, and I lay a stumblingblock before him, he shall die: because thou hast not given him warning, he shall die in his sin, and his righteousness which he hath done shall not be remembered; but his blood will I require at thine hand.

    When a righteous man turns from his righteousness and commits iniquity, his righteousness shall not be remembered, and he shall die in his sin, exactly as it went for Joash.
