Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 5](/bible/2-chronicles/5)

This session moves through the dedication of the temple that Solomon built for the Most High. It covers the bringing up of the ark into the most holy place, Solomon's long dedication prayer, the fire that came down from heaven, and the covenant terms the Most High set for the kingdom. The account here is the same history told in [1 Kings 5](/bible/1-kings/5) through 8, and the same ground was covered in that earlier reading. What Solomon built was the crown of Jerusalem, the center of worship, and the place where the presence of the Most High would dwell among His people Israel.

**[2 Chronicles 5:1](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v1)</sup> Thus all the work that Solomon made for the house of the Lord was finished: and Solomon brought in all the things that David his father had dedicated; and the silver, and the gold, and all the instruments, put he among the treasures of the house of God.

- The work of the house of the Most High was finished, and Solomon brought in all that David his father had dedicated, the silver, the gold, and the instruments, and laid them among the treasures of the house.
- The temple did not begin from nothing. What the father set apart, the son carried into its place. The dedicated things of one generation are stored up for the work of the next.

**[2 Chronicles 5:2-3](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v2)</sup> Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel, and all the heads of the tribes, the chief of the fathers of the children of Israel, unto Jerusalem, to bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord out of the city of David, which is Zion.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v3)</sup> Wherefore all the men of Israel assembled themselves unto the king in the feast which was in the seventh month.

- Solomon assembled the elders of Israel, the heads of the tribes, and the chiefs of the fathers to bring up the ark of the covenant out of the city of David, which is Zion.
- All the men of Israel gathered at the feast in the seventh month. The dedication was tied to the appointed time, not to a date of convenience.

**[2 Chronicles 5:4-6](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v4)</sup> And all the elders of Israel came; and the Levites took up the ark.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v5)</sup> And they brought up the ark, and the tabernacle of the congregation, and all the holy vessels that were in the tabernacle, these did the priests and the Levites bring up.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v6)</sup> Also king Solomon, and all the congregation of Israel that were assembled unto him before the ark, sacrificed sheep and oxen, which could not be told nor numbered for multitude.

- The Levites took up the ark, the tabernacle of the congregation, and all the holy vessels, and the priests and Levites brought them up.
- Solomon and all the congregation before the ark sacrificed sheep and oxen that could not be numbered for multitude. This was a celebration, a gathered nation of Israel in order, in beautiful garments, keeping the appointed feast together.

**[2 Chronicles 5:7-9](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v7)</sup> And the priests brought in the ark of the covenant of the Lord unto his place, to the oracle of the house, into the most holy place, even under the wings of the cherubims:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v8)</sup> For the cherubims spread forth their wings over the place of the ark, and the cherubims covered the ark and the staves thereof above.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v9)</sup> And they drew out the staves of the ark, that the ends of the staves were seen from the ark before the oracle; but they were not seen without. And there it is unto this day.

- The priests brought the ark into the oracle of the house, into the most holy place, under the wings of the cherubims, whose wings covered the ark and the staves above it.
- The staves were drawn out so their ends could be seen from before the oracle but not from without. The video shown in class pictured this same scene, the two great cherubims flanking the ark with wings stretched from wall to wall.

**[2 Chronicles 5:10](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v10)</sup> There was nothing in the ark save the two tables which Moses put therein at Horeb, when the Lord made a covenant with the children of Israel, when they came out of Egypt.

- There was nothing in the ark except the two tables Moses put there at Horeb, where the Most High made a covenant with the children of Israel when they came out of Egypt.
- The ark held the covenant, the written law. The center of the most holy place pointed back to the commandments given at the exodus.

**[2 Chronicles 5:11-13](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, when the priests were come out of the holy place: (for all the priests that were present were sanctified, and did not then wait by course:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v12)</sup> Also the Levites which were the singers, all of them of Asaph, of Heman, of Jeduthun, with their sons and their brethren, being arrayed in white linen, having cymbals and psalteries and harps, stood at the east end of the altar, and with them an hundred and twenty priests sounding with trumpets:)
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v13)</sup> It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the Lord; and when they lifted up their voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of musick, and praised the Lord, saying, For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever: that then the house was filled with a cloud, even the house of the Lord;

- When the priests came out of the holy place, the Levite singers, Asaph, Heman, and Jeduthun with their sons and brethren, stood arrayed in white linen with cymbals, psalteries, and harps, and a hundred and twenty priests sounded with trumpets.
- The trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one sound in praising and thanking the Most High, saying He is good, for His mercy endureth for ever. This was order, everything set in unison, the whole assembly on one accord.

**[2 Chronicles 5:13-14](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v13)</sup> It came even to pass, as the trumpeters and singers were as one, to make one sound to be heard in praising and thanking the Lord; and when they lifted up their voice with the trumpets and cymbals and instruments of musick, and praised the Lord, saying, For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever: that then the house was filled with a cloud, even the house of the Lord;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/5#v14)</sup> So that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud: for the glory of the Lord had filled the house of God.

- When the one sound of praise went up, the house was filled with a cloud, so that the priests could not stand to minister by reason of the cloud.
- The glory of the Most High had filled the house. The cloud was His glory settling upon the temple, His answer to a people gathered in order and in praise.

# 2 Chronicles 6: Solomon's Prayer of Dedication

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 6](/bible/2-chronicles/6)

**[2 Chronicles 6:1-3](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v1)</sup> Then said Solomon, The Lord hath said that he would dwell in the thick darkness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v2)</sup> But I have built an house of habitation for thee, and a place for thy dwelling for ever.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v3)</sup> And the king turned his face, and blessed the whole congregation of Israel: and all the congregation of Israel stood.

- Solomon declared that the Most High had said He would dwell in the thick darkness, and that Solomon had built Him a house of habitation, a place for His dwelling for ever.
- Solomon turned and blessed the whole congregation of Israel as they stood. The king led the nation in blessing before he led them in prayer.

**[2 Chronicles 6:4-6](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v4)</sup> And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who hath with his hands fulfilled that which he spake with his mouth to my father David, saying,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v5)</sup> Since the day that I brought forth my people out of the land of Egypt I chose no city among all the tribes of Israel to build an house in, that my name might be there; neither chose I any man to be a ruler over my people Israel:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v6)</sup> But I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there; and have chosen David to be over my people Israel.

- Solomon blessed the Most High, who fulfilled with His hand what He spake with His mouth to David, that since bringing Israel out of Egypt He chose no city to build a house for His name, nor any man to rule, until He chose Jerusalem and David.
- When Israel came out of Egypt the Most High did not fix His house in a city. He chose Jerusalem, the city of David, for the temple, and He chose David to be over His people Israel.

  - **[Genesis 49:10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)</sup> The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.

    The scepter and lawgiver stay with Judah, the line David came from.

**[2 Chronicles 6:7-9](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v7)</sup> Now it was in the heart of David my father to build an house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v8)</sup> But the Lord said to David my father, Forasmuch as it was in thine heart to build an house for my name, thou didst well in that it was in thine heart:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v9)</sup> Notwithstanding thou shalt not build the house; but thy son which shall come forth out of thy loins, he shall build the house for my name.

- It was in David's heart to build the house, and the Most High said he did well that it was in his heart, but David would not build it. His son who came forth of his loins would build the house for the name of the Most High.
- A right desire toward the Most High is counted good even when the task is given to another. David's heart was accepted though the building was assigned to Solomon.

**[2 Chronicles 6:10-11](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v10)</sup> The Lord therefore hath performed his word that he hath spoken: for I am risen up in the room of David my father, and am set on the throne of Israel, as the Lord promised, and have built the house for the name of the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v11)</sup> And in it have I put the ark, wherein is the covenant of the Lord, that he made with the children of Israel.

- The Most High performed His word, raising Solomon up in the room of David to sit on the throne of Israel, and Solomon built the house and put the ark there, in which is the covenant made with the children of Israel.
- The promise ran from David to Solomon exactly as spoken. The ark and its covenant were set in the house that fulfilled the word.

**[2 Chronicles 6:12-13](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v12)</sup> And he stood before the altar of the Lord in the presence of all the congregation of Israel, and spread forth his hands:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v13)</sup> For Solomon had made a brasen scaffold, of five cubits long, and five cubits broad, and three cubits high, and had set it in the midst of the court: and upon it he stood, and kneeled down upon his knees before all the congregation of Israel, and spread forth his hands toward heaven,

- Solomon stood before the altar in the presence of all the congregation and spread forth his hands, having made a brazen scaffold five cubits long, five broad, and three high, on which he kneeled before all Israel.
- The king knelt in public before his people and spread his hands toward heaven, the same posture used in reading and prayer. Leadership bowed first.

**[2 Chronicles 6:14-15](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v14)</sup> And said, O Lord God of Israel, there is no God like thee in the heaven, nor in the earth; which keepest covenant, and shewest mercy unto thy servants, that walk before thee with all their hearts:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v15)</sup> Thou which hast kept with thy servant David my father that which thou hast promised him; and spakest with thy mouth, and hast fulfilled it with thine hand, as it is this day.

- Solomon prayed that there is no god like the Most High in heaven or earth, who keepeth covenant and sheweth mercy to servants that walk before Him with all their heart.
- He acknowledged that the Most High had kept His promise to David and fulfilled it with His hand, as it was that day.

**[2 Chronicles 6:16-17](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v16)</sup> Now therefore, O Lord God of Israel, keep with thy servant David my father that which thou hast promised him, saying, There shall not fail thee a man in my sight to sit upon the throne of Israel; yet so that thy children take heed to their way to walk in my law, as thou hast walked before me.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v17)</sup> Now then, O Lord God of Israel, let thy word be verified, which thou hast spoken unto thy servant David.

- Solomon asked the Most High to keep the promise that David would not fail to have a man to sit on the throne of Israel, so long as his children take heed to walk in the law.
- The throne stood on a condition. The line would continue if the children walked in the law as David walked. This is the same word later given to Solomon himself.

**[2 Chronicles 6:18-19](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v18)</sup> But will God in very deed dwell with men on the earth? behold, heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain thee; how much less this house which I have built!
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v19)</sup> Have respect therefore to the prayer of thy servant, and to his supplication, O Lord my God, to hearken unto the cry and the prayer which thy servant prayeth before thee:

- Solomon asked whether the Most High would in very deed dwell with man on earth, since heaven and the heaven of heavens cannot contain Him, how much less the house he had built.
- Even with the house built, Solomon understood that the Most High could not be contained in it. He asked that the Most High have respect to the prayer of His servant.
- Before we pray we ask the Most High to hear and honor the prayer, for He is not bound to hear when our own doings have shut His ear.

**[2 Chronicles 6:20-21](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v20)</sup> That thine eyes may be open upon this house day and night, upon the place whereof thou hast said that thou wouldest put thy name there; to hearken unto the prayer which thy servant prayeth toward this place.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v21)</sup> Hearken therefore unto the supplications of thy servant, and of thy people Israel, which they shall make toward this place: hear thou from thy dwelling place, even from heaven; and when thou hearest, forgive.

- Solomon asked that the eyes of the Most High be open toward this house day and night, to hearken to the prayer His servant made toward the place where He said He would put His name.
- He asked that the Most High hear from His dwelling place in heaven, and when He heard, forgive.

**[2 Chronicles 6:22-23](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v22)</sup> If a man sin against his neighbour, and an oath be laid upon him to make him swear, and the oath come before thine altar in this house;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v23)</sup> Then hear thou from heaven, and do, and judge thy servants, by requiting the wicked, by recompensing his way upon his own head; and by justifying the righteous, by giving him according to his righteousness.

- If a man sinned against his neighbor and an oath were laid on him to come before the altar in this house, Solomon asked the Most High to hear from heaven and judge, requiting the wicked and justifying the righteous.
- He prayed for righteous judgment to go forth. Those who deal with people unrighteously will not go unchecked; where a man cannot bring right judgment, the Most High will.

**[2 Chronicles 6:24-25](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v24)</sup> And if thy people Israel be put to the worse before the enemy, because they have sinned against thee; and shall return and confess thy name, and pray and make supplication before thee in this house;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v25)</sup> Then hear thou from the heavens, and forgive the sin of thy people Israel, and bring them again unto the land which thou gavest to them and to their fathers.

- If Israel were put to the worst before the enemy because they sinned, then turned, confessed His name, and prayed in this house, Solomon asked the Most High to forgive and bring them again to the land.
- This pattern repeated through the history of Israel. The Most High raised saviors when the people prayed in captivity, forgave them, and returned them to the land.

**[2 Chronicles 6:26-27](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v26)</sup> When the heaven is shut up, and there is no rain, because they have sinned against thee; yet if they pray toward this place, and confess thy name, and turn from their sin, when thou dost afflict them;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v27)</sup> Then hear thou from heaven, and forgive the sin of thy servants, and of thy people Israel, when thou hast taught them the good way, wherein they should walk; and send rain upon thy land, which thou hast given unto thy people for an inheritance.

- If heaven were shut and there was no rain because of sin, and the people prayed toward this place, confessed His name, and turned from sin, Solomon asked the Most High to hear, forgive, and teach them the good way and send rain.
- No other nation has this. The Most High promised Israel that in a drought, if they repented and returned to Him, He would honor their prayers and send rain in due season.

  - **[Joel 2:27](/bible/joel/2#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/joel/2#v27)</sup> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.

    Israel shall know the Most High is in their midst, their God and none else, and shall never be ashamed.

**[2 Chronicles 6:28-31](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v28)</sup> If there be dearth in the land, if there be pestilence, if there be blasting, or mildew, locusts, or caterpillers; if their enemies besiege them in the cities of their land; whatsoever sore or whatsoever sickness there be:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v29)</sup> Then what prayer or what supplication soever shall be made of any man, or of all thy people Israel, when every one shall know his own sore and his own grief, and shall spread forth his hands in this house:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v30)</sup> Then hear thou from heaven thy dwelling place, and forgive, and render unto every man according unto all his ways, whose heart thou knowest; (for thou only knowest the hearts of the children of men:)
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v31)</sup> That they may fear thee, to walk in thy ways, so long as they live in the land which thou gavest unto our fathers.

- Whatever the plague, dearth, pestilence, blasting, mildew, locust, caterpiller, or siege, whatever prayer any man of Israel made knowing his own sore and grief, Solomon asked the Most High to hear and render to every man according to his ways.
- The Most High alone knoweth the heart of the children of men. He answers according to what He sees in the heart, that the people may fear Him and walk in His ways in the land.

**[2 Chronicles 6:32-33](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v32)</sup> Moreover concerning the stranger, which is not of thy people Israel, but is come from a far country for thy great name’s sake, and thy mighty hand, and thy stretched out arm; if they come and pray in this house;
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v33)</sup> Then hear thou from the heavens, even from thy dwelling place, and do according to all that the stranger calleth to thee for; that all people of the earth may know thy name, and fear thee, as doth thy people Israel, and may know that this house which I have built is called by thy name.

- Concerning the stranger not of Israel who comes from a far country for His great name and prays toward this house, Solomon asked the Most High to hear, so that all people of the earth may know His name and fear Him as Israel does.
- The Most High hears the stranger who submits and fears Him, so all nations know the name attached to this house. He does not honor a prayer prayed against Israel or for dominion over His people.

  - **[Matthew 15:26-28](/bible/matthew/15#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/15#v26)</sup> But he answered and said, It is not meet to take the children’s bread, and to cast it to dogs.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/15#v27)</sup> And she said, Truth, Lord: yet the dogs eat of the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/15#v28)</sup> Then Jesus answered and said unto her, O woman, great is thy faith: be it unto thee even as thou wilt. And her daughter was made whole from that very hour.

    The Canaanite woman was healed only when she owned herself a dog and submitted under Israel; that faith moved Him.

**[2 Chronicles 6:34-35](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v34)</sup> If thy people go out to war against their enemies by the way that thou shalt send them, and they pray unto thee toward this city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name;
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v35)</sup> Then hear thou from the heavens their prayer and their supplication, and maintain their cause.

- If Israel went to war against their enemies in the way He sent them and prayed toward the city and house, Solomon asked the Most High to hear their prayer and maintain their cause.
- The Most High helps Israel in the way He sends them. An enemy praying to harm Israel does not get in the city, much less the temple; common sense reads the promise rightly.

**[2 Chronicles 6:36-39](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v36)</sup> If they sin against thee, (for there is no man which sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them over before their enemies, and they carry them away captives unto a land far off or near;
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v37)</sup> Yet if they bethink themselves in the land whither they are carried captive, and turn and pray unto thee in the land of their captivity, saying, We have sinned, we have done amiss, and have dealt wickedly;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v38)</sup> If they return to thee with all their heart and with all their soul in the land of their captivity, whither they have carried them captives, and pray toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, and toward the city which thou hast chosen, and toward the house which I have built for thy name:
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v39)</sup> Then hear thou from the heavens, even from thy dwelling place, their prayer and their supplications, and maintain their cause, and forgive thy people which have sinned against thee.

- Since there is no man that sinneth not, if Israel sinned and were carried captive far off or near, then bethought themselves and turned and prayed in the land of their captivity, confessing they had done wickedly, Solomon asked the Most High to hear and maintain their cause and forgive.
- Read this as Solomon himself, standing before the whole nation, praying that even in captivity a repentant Israel would be heard. To maintain their cause is to uphold their right, the covenant that He would hear them when they turned back.

**[2 Chronicles 6:40-42](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v40)**

> <sup>[40](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v40)</sup> Now, my God, let, I beseech thee, thine eyes be open, and let thine ears be attent unto the prayer that is made in this place.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v41)</sup> Now therefore arise, O Lord God, into thy resting place, thou, and the ark of thy strength: let thy priests, O Lord God, be clothed with salvation, and let thy saints rejoice in goodness.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v42)</sup> O Lord God, turn not away the face of thine anointed: remember the mercies of David thy servant.

- Solomon asked that the eyes and ears of the Most High be attentive to the prayer made in this place, that He arise to His resting place with the ark of His strength, that His priests be clothed with salvation and His saints rejoice in goodness.
- He closed asking the Most High not to turn away the face of His anointed, but to remember the mercies of David His servant.

# 2 Chronicles 7: Fire From Heaven and the Terms of the Covenant

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 7](/bible/2-chronicles/7)

**[2 Chronicles 7:1-3](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v1)</sup> Now when Solomon had made an end of praying, the fire came down from heaven, and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices; and the glory of the Lord filled the house.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v2)</sup> And the priests could not enter into the house of the Lord, because the glory of the Lord had filled the Lord’s house.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v3)</sup> And when all the children of Israel saw how the fire came down, and the glory of the Lord upon the house, they bowed themselves with their faces to the ground upon the pavement, and worshipped, and praised the Lord, saying, For he is good; for his mercy endureth for ever.

- When Solomon finished praying, fire came down from heaven and consumed the burnt offering and the sacrifices, and the glory of the Most High filled the house so the priests could not enter.
- When Israel saw the fire and the glory upon the house, they bowed with their faces to the pavement and worshipped, saying He is good, for His mercy endureth for ever. The Most High can send fire straight from the sky; the day of His sacrifice speaks of such fire.

**[2 Chronicles 7:4-6](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v4)</sup> Then the king and all the people offered sacrifices before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v5)</sup> And king Solomon offered a sacrifice of twenty and two thousand oxen, and an hundred and twenty thousand sheep: so the king and all the people dedicated the house of God.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v6)</sup> And the priests waited on their offices: the Levites also with instruments of musick of the Lord, which David the king had made to praise the Lord, because his mercy endureth for ever, when David praised by their ministry; and the priests sounded trumpets before them, and all Israel stood.

- The king and all the people offered sacrifice, Solomon offering twenty and two thousand oxen and a hundred and twenty thousand sheep, and so dedicated the house of the Most High.
- The priests waited on their offices and the Levites sounded instruments of music that David had made to praise the Most High, because His mercy endureth for ever, and all Israel stood.

**[2 Chronicles 7:7-10](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v7)</sup> Moreover Solomon hallowed the middle of the court that was before the house of the Lord: for there he offered burnt offerings, and the fat of the peace offerings, because the brasen altar which Solomon had made was not able to receive the burnt offerings, and the meat offerings, and the fat.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v8)</sup> Also at the same time Solomon kept the feast seven days, and all Israel with him, a very great congregation, from the entering in of Hamath unto the river of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v9)</sup> And in the eighth day they made a solemn assembly: for they kept the dedication of the altar seven days, and the feast seven days.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v10)</sup> And on the three and twentieth day of the seventh month he sent the people away into their tents, glad and merry in heart for the goodness that the Lord had shewed unto David, and to Solomon, and to Israel his people.

- Solomon hallowed the middle of the court for offerings because the brazen altar could not receive them all, kept the feast seven days with all Israel from Hamath to the river of Egypt, and on the eighth day held a solemn assembly.
- They kept the dedication of the altar seven days and the feast seven days. On the twenty-third day of the seventh month the people went home glad and merry in heart for the goodness the Most High had shown to David, Solomon, and Israel.

**[2 Chronicles 7:11-12](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v11)</sup> Thus Solomon finished the house of the Lord, and the king’s house: and all that came into Solomon’s heart to make in the house of the Lord, and in his own house, he prosperously effected.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v12)</sup> And the Lord appeared to Solomon by night, and said unto him, I have heard thy prayer, and have chosen this place to myself for an house of sacrifice.

- Solomon finished the house of the Most High and his own house, and all that came into his heart to make he prosperously effected, and the Most High appeared to him by night.
- The Most High told Solomon He had heard his prayer and chosen this place for a house of sacrifice. To be told the prayer was heard is itself an honor.

**[2 Chronicles 7:13-14](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v13)</sup> If I shut up heaven that there be no rain, or if I command the locusts to devour the land, or if I send pestilence among my people;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v14)</sup> If my people, which are called by my name, shall humble themselves, and pray, and seek my face, and turn from their wicked ways; then will I hear from heaven, and will forgive their sin, and will heal their land.

- If the Most High shut heaven that there be no rain, commanded the locust to devour the land, or sent pestilence among His people, then if His people who are called by His name humble themselves, pray, seek His face, and turn from their wicked ways, He would hear from heaven, forgive their sin, and heal their land.
- The Most High controls the rain, the insects, and the pestilence. The people called by His name are Israel, the same people read through these chapters, and healing follows their humbling and turning, not a general love for all nations.

    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/63#v16)</sup> Doubtless thou art our father, though Abraham be ignorant of us, and Israel acknowledge us not: thou, O Lord, art our father, our redeemer; thy name is from everlasting.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/63#v17)</sup> O Lord, why hast thou made us to err from thy ways, and hardened our heart from thy fear? Return for thy servants’ sake, the tribes of thine inheritance.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/63#v18)</sup> The people of thy holiness have possessed it but a little while: our adversaries have trodden down thy sanctuary.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/63#v19)</sup> We are thine: thou never barest rule over them; they were not called by thy name.

    He is Father and Redeemer to Israel; other nations were never called by His name.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 36:12](/bible/sirach/36#v12)**  O Lord, have mercy upon the people that is called by thy name, and upon Israel, whom thou hast named thy firstborn.

**[2 Chronicles 7:15-16](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v15)</sup> Now mine eyes shall be open, and mine ears attent unto the prayer that is made in this place.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v16)</sup> For now have I chosen and sanctified this house, that my name may be there for ever: and mine eyes and mine heart shall be there perpetually.

- The eyes of the Most High would be open and His ears attentive to the prayer made in this place, for He had chosen and sanctified the house that His name might be there for ever.
- His eyes and heart are on Jerusalem perpetually. He sees the evil done in the land by those who take it, building up the case against them.

**[2 Chronicles 7:17-18](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v17)</sup> And as for thee, if thou wilt walk before me, as David thy father walked, and do according to all that I have commanded thee, and shalt observe my statutes and my judgments;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v18)</sup> Then will I stablish the throne of thy kingdom, according as I have covenanted with David thy father, saying, There shall not fail thee a man to be ruler in Israel.

- If Solomon walked before the Most High as David walked, keeping His statutes and judgments, then the Most High would establish the throne of his kingdom as covenanted with David, that he should not fail to have a man to rule in Israel.
- The promise means every king of Israel would come through David's lineage. The kingdom continues so long as the house keeps the covenant.

  - **[2 Chronicles 6:16](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v16)</sup> Now therefore, O Lord God of Israel, keep with thy servant David my father that which thou hast promised him, saying, There shall not fail thee a man in my sight to sit upon the throne of Israel; yet so that thy children take heed to their way to walk in my law, as thou hast walked before me.

    The same word to David: he shall not fail a man to sit on the throne if his children keep the way.

**[2 Chronicles 7:19-22](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v19)</sup> But if ye turn away, and forsake my statutes and my commandments, which I have set before you, and shall go and serve other gods, and worship them;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v20)</sup> Then will I pluck them up by the roots out of my land which I have given them; and this house, which I have sanctified for my name, will I cast out of my sight, and will make it to be a proverb and a byword among all nations.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v21)</sup> And this house, which is high, shall be an astonishment to every one that passeth by it; so that he shall say, Why hath the Lord done thus unto this land, and unto this house?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/7#v22)</sup> And it shall be answered, Because they forsook the Lord God of their fathers, which brought them forth out of the land of Egypt, and laid hold on other gods, and worshipped them, and served them: therefore hath he brought all this evil upon them.

- But if they turned away, forsook His statutes and commandments, and went to serve other gods, He would pluck them out of the land, cast the house out of His sight, and make it a proverb and a byword among all nations.
- Passers-by would ask why the Most High did this to the land and house, and the answer would be that Israel forsook Him and laid hold on other gods; therefore He brought all this evil upon them. This is exactly what happened to Israel.

# 2 Chronicles 8: Solomon's Cities, Service, and Dominion

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 8](/bible/2-chronicles/8)

**[2 Chronicles 8:1-2](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v1)</sup> And it came to pass at the end of twenty years, wherein Solomon had built the house of the Lord, and his own house,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v2)</sup> That the cities which Huram had restored to Solomon, Solomon built them, and caused the children of Israel to dwell there.

- At the end of twenty years in which Solomon built the house of the Most High and his own house, he built the cities Hiram had restored to him and caused the children of Israel to dwell there.
- The twenty years of building were continuous, with workers rotating in courses so the labor never stopped and the work was finished in time.

**[2 Chronicles 8:3-6](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v3)</sup> And Solomon went to Hamath–zobah, and prevailed against it.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v4)</sup> And he built Tadmor in the wilderness, and all the store cities, which he built in Hamath.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v5)</sup> Also he built Beth–horon the upper, and Beth–horon the nether, fenced cities, with walls, gates, and bars;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v6)</sup> And Baalath, and all the store cities that Solomon had, and all the chariot cities, and the cities of the horsemen, and all that Solomon desired to build in Jerusalem, and in Lebanon, and throughout all the land of his dominion.

- Solomon went to Hamathzobah and prevailed against it, built Tadmor in the wilderness and store cities in Hamath, built Bethhoron the upper and nether as fenced cities with walls, gates, and bars, and all his store, chariot, and horsemen cities.
- Israel was royalty, building whole cities across the land of Solomon's dominion, in Jerusalem, in Lebanon, and wherever he desired to build.

**[2 Chronicles 8:7-10](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v7)</sup> As for all the people that were left of the Hittites, and the Amorites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, which were not of Israel,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v8)</sup> But of their children, who were left after them in the land, whom the children of Israel consumed not, them did Solomon make to pay tribute until this day.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v9)</sup> But of the children of Israel did Solomon make no servants for his work; but they were men of war, and chief of his captains, and captains of his chariots and horsemen.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v10)</sup> And these were the chief of king Solomon’s officers, even two hundred and fifty, that bare rule over the people.

- The people left of the Hittites, Amorites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, who were not of Israel, Solomon made to pay tribute, while of the children of Israel he made no bondmen but men of war, chiefs, captains, and rulers.
- These nations were sons of Canaan, not Israel, and their descendants left in the land paid tribute, that is taxes, to Israel. The kingdom was so strong that other nations paid Israel, and Solomon had two hundred and fifty officers ruling over the people.

**[2 Chronicles 8:11](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v11)</sup> And Solomon brought up the daughter of Pharaoh out of the city of David unto the house that he had built for her: for he said, My wife shall not dwell in the house of David king of Israel, because the places are holy, whereunto the ark of the Lord hath come.

- Solomon brought Pharaoh's daughter out of the city of David to the house he had built for her, saying his wife should not dwell in David's house because the places were holy where the ark had come.
- The presence of the ark set the city of David apart as holy. Solomon would not let his wife dwell where that holiness rested.

**[2 Chronicles 8:12-15](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v12)</sup> Then Solomon offered burnt offerings unto the Lord on the altar of the Lord, which he had built before the porch,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v13)</sup> Even after a certain rate every day, offering according to the commandment of Moses, on the sabbaths, and on the new moons, and on the solemn feasts, three times in the year, even in the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tabernacles.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v14)</sup> And he appointed, according to the order of David his father, the courses of the priests to their service, and the Levites to their charges, to praise and minister before the priests, as the duty of every day required: the porters also by their courses at every gate: for so had David the man of God commanded.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/8#v15)</sup> And they departed not from the commandment of the king unto the priests and Levites concerning any matter, or concerning the treasures.

- The porters kept every gate by their courses as David the man of the Most High had commanded, and they departed not from the commandment concerning any matter or treasure.
