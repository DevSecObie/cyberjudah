Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 9](/bible/2-chronicles/9)

This session moves through [2 Chronicles 9](/bible/2-chronicles/9) to 12, a compressed history that gathers together events told at length in 1 and 2 Samuel and 1 and 2 Kings. It covers the visit of the Queen of Sheba to Solomon, the wealth, fame, and reign of Solomon, and then the death of Solomon and the reign of his son Rehoboam. Under Rehoboam the nation splits, Judah and the northern tribes divide, and the Most High brings correction on those who forsake his law. Read the parallel chapters in 1 and 2 Kings to see the full order of events.

**[2 Chronicles 9:1-2](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v1)</sup> And when the queen of Sheba heard of the fame of Solomon, she came to prove Solomon with hard questions at Jerusalem, with a very great company, and camels that bare spices, and gold in abundance, and precious stones: and when she was come to Solomon, she communed with him of all that was in her heart.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v2)</sup> And Solomon told her all her questions: and there was nothing hid from Solomon which he told her not.

- The Queen of Sheba hears of the fame of Solomon in her own land and travels to Jerusalem to prove him, meaning to test him, with hard questions from what she has heard.
- She comes with a great company, camels bearing spices, gold in abundance, and precious stones, and she opens all that is in her heart to him.
- Solomon answers every question she brings; nothing is hidden from him that he cannot explain to her.

**[2 Chronicles 9:3-4](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v3)</sup> And when the queen of Sheba had seen the wisdom of Solomon, and the house that he had built,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v4)</sup> And the meat of his table, and the sitting of his servants, and the attendance of his ministers, and their apparel; his cupbearers also, and their apparel; and his ascent by which he went up into the house of the Lord; there was no more spirit in her.

- When she sees the wisdom of Solomon, the house he built, the food on his table, the order of his servants and their apparel, and his ascent into the house of the Lord, there is no more spirit left in her.
- She is overcome by the beauty and order of the kingdom that Solomon has established in Jerusalem.

**[2 Chronicles 9:5-6](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v5)</sup> And she said to the king, It was a true report which I heard in mine own land of thine acts, and of thy wisdom:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v6)</sup> Howbeit I believed not their words, until I came, and mine eyes had seen it: and, behold, the one half of the greatness of thy wisdom was not told me: for thou exceedest the fame that I heard.

- She confesses that the report she heard in her own land of his acts and wisdom was true.
- She admits she did not believe the words until she came and saw it with her own eyes, and now she declares that not even half the greatness of his wisdom was told to her; he exceeds the fame that reached her.

**[2 Chronicles 9:7-8](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v7)</sup> Happy are thy men, and happy are these thy servants, which stand continually before thee, and hear thy wisdom.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v8)</sup> Blessed be the Lord thy God, which delighted in thee to set thee on his throne, to be king for the Lord thy God: because thy God loved Israel, to establish them for ever, therefore made he thee king over them, to do judgment and justice.

- She calls his men and servants happy because they stand continually before him and hear his wisdom.
- She blesses the Lord who delighted in Solomon and set him on the throne, and she states plainly that it is because the Most High loved Israel and would establish them forever that he made Solomon king to do judgment and justice.
- The wisdom and glory of the king trace back to the God of Israel, not to Solomon alone.

**[2 Chronicles 9:9-12](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v9)</sup> And she gave the king an hundred and twenty talents of gold, and of spices great abundance, and precious stones: neither was there any such spice as the queen of Sheba gave king Solomon.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v10)</sup> And the servants also of Huram, and the servants of Solomon, which brought gold from Ophir, brought algum trees and precious stones.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v11)</sup> And the king made of the algum trees terraces to the house of the Lord, and to the king’s palace, and harps and psalteries for singers: and there were none such seen before in the land of Judah.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v12)</sup> And king Solomon gave to the queen of Sheba all her desire, whatsoever she asked, beside that which she had brought unto the king. So she turned, and went away to her own land, she and her servants.

- The queen gives Solomon 120 talents of gold, spices in great abundance, and precious stones; there was never such spice as she brought.
- The servants of Huram and Solomon bring gold from Ophir along with algum trees and precious stones, and the king makes terraces for the house of the Lord and the palace, and harps and psalteries for singers.
- Solomon gives the queen all she desires beyond what she brought, and she returns to her own land.

- The weight of gold coming to Solomon in one year is six hundred sixty and six talents, beyond what merchants and the kings of Arabia and the governors bring.
- Solomon makes two hundred targets and three hundred shields of beaten gold and puts them in the house of the forest of Lebanon.

- His ships go to Tarshish with the servants of Huram and return every three years bringing gold, silver, ivory, apes, and peacocks; consider the amount gathered by a crew laboring three years for one voyage.

**[2 Chronicles 9:22-24](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v22)</sup> And king Solomon passed all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/9#v23)</sup> And all the kings of the earth sought the presence of Solomon, to hear his wisdom, that God had put in his heart.
>

- Solomon surpasses all the kings of the earth in riches and wisdom, and all of them seek his presence to hear the wisdom the Most High put in his heart.
- This is a testimony of what the Most High does for his people when they walk according to his law, statutes, and commandments.
- Every man brings his present year by year, vessels of silver and gold, garments, armor, spices, horses, and mules.

- He reigns over all the kings from the river to the land of the Philistines and to the border of Egypt, and he makes silver as plentiful as stones in Jerusalem.
- Horses are brought to him out of Egypt and out of all lands.

- Solomon reigns in Jerusalem over all Israel forty years, then sleeps with his fathers and is buried in the city of David, and his son Rehoboam reigns in his place.
- After all these great works Solomon dies, and the account turns to his son who is raised up next.

# 2 Chronicles 10: Rehoboam Rejects the Elders and the Kingdom Divides

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 10](/bible/2-chronicles/10)

- Rehoboam goes to Shechem where all Israel has gathered to make him king, and Jeroboam the son of Nebat returns out of Egypt where he had fled from Solomon.
- The people, led by Jeroboam, ask Rehoboam to ease the grievous yoke and heavy service his father laid on them, promising then to serve him.
- Rehoboam tells them to come again after three days.

**[2 Chronicles 10:5-7](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v5)</sup> And he said unto them, Come again unto me after three days. And the people departed.

>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v7)</sup> And they spake unto him, saying, If thou be kind to this people, and please them, and speak good words to them, they will be thy servants for ever.

- Rehoboam first takes counsel with the old men who stood before Solomon while his father lived, asking what answer to return.
- They advise him that if he is kind to the people, pleases them, and speaks good words, they will be his servants forever.
- This is the wise counsel he should have followed, coming from the elders who had experience in place before him.

**[2 Chronicles 10:8-11](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v8)</sup> But he forsook the counsel which the old men gave him, and took counsel with the young men that were brought up with him, that stood before him.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v9)</sup> And he said unto them, What advice give ye that we may return answer to this people, which have spoken to me, saying, Ease somewhat the yoke that thy father did put upon us?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v10)</sup> And the young men that were brought up with him spake unto him, saying, Thus shalt thou answer the people that spake unto thee, saying, Thy father made our yoke heavy, but make thou it somewhat lighter for us; thus shalt thou say unto them, My little finger shall be thicker than my father’s loins.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v11)</sup> For whereas my father put a heavy yoke upon you, I will put more to your yoke: my father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.

- Rehoboam forsakes the counsel of the old men and turns to the young men brought up with him, including them as if they were rulers with him over the nation.
- This is the same error seen constantly among the people: one comes to the truth and seeks counsel from a new brother or sister rather than from the elder men and women already established in the congregation.
- The young men advise him to answer roughly, saying his little finger will be thicker than his father's loins, that he will add to their yoke, and chastise them with scorpions instead of whips.

# 2 Chronicles 12: Rehoboam Forsakes the Law and Is Humbled

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 12](/bible/2-chronicles/12)

**[2 Chronicles 12:1](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when Rehoboam had established the kingdom, and had strengthened himself, he forsook the law of the Lord, and all Israel with him.

- When Rehoboam establishes the kingdom and strengthens himself, he forsakes the law of the Lord, and all Israel with him.
- One who leads goes astray and carries many others with him; this is why the people must be examples to one another in righteousness, of one mind and one spirit in the rebuilding of the kingdom.

**[2 Chronicles 12:2-4](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, that in the fifth year of king Rehoboam Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem, because they had transgressed against the Lord,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v3)</sup> With twelve hundred chariots, and threescore thousand horsemen: and the people were without number that came with him out of Egypt; the Lubims, the Sukkiims, and the Ethiopians.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v4)</sup> And he took the fenced cities which pertained to Judah, and came to Jerusalem.

- In the fifth year of Rehoboam, Shishak king of Egypt comes up against Jerusalem because Rehoboam transgressed against the Lord.
- Shishak comes with twelve hundred chariots, threescore thousand horsemen, and people without number out of Egypt, the Lubims, the Sukkiims, and the Ethiopians.
- He takes the fenced cities of Judah and comes to Jerusalem; the invasion is the direct result of forsaking the law.

**[2 Chronicles 12:5-6](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v5)</sup> Then came Shemaiah the prophet to Rehoboam, and to the princes of Judah, that were gathered together to Jerusalem because of Shishak, and said unto them, Thus saith the Lord, Ye have forsaken me, and therefore have I also left you in the hand of Shishak.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v6)</sup> Whereupon the princes of Israel and the king humbled themselves; and they said, The Lord is righteous.

- Shemaiah the prophet comes to Rehoboam and the princes of Judah gathered at Jerusalem and declares the word of the Lord: because they have forsaken him, he has left them in the hand of Shishak.
- The princes and the king humble themselves and say the Lord is righteous.
- When correction comes, the right response is to confess that the Most High is righteous in what he does, acknowledging the punishment as just.

**[2 Chronicles 12:7-8](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v7)</sup> And when the Lord saw that they humbled themselves, the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah, saying, They have humbled themselves; therefore I will not destroy them, but I will grant them some deliverance; and my wrath shall not be poured out upon Jerusalem by the hand of Shishak.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v8)</sup> Nevertheless they shall be his servants; that they may know my service, and the service of the kingdoms of the countries.

- When the Lord sees that they humble themselves, his word comes to Shemaiah saying he will not destroy them but grant them some deliverance, and his wrath will not be poured on Jerusalem by the hand of Shishak.
- Nevertheless they will be Shishak's servants, that they may know the difference between serving the Most High and serving the kingdoms of the countries.
- The people today must likewise humble themselves, relearn to obey the commandments, and accept their condition as righteous judgment from the hand of the Most High.

    > <sup>[40](/bible/leviticus/26#v40)</sup> If they shall confess their iniquity, and the iniquity of their fathers, with their trespass which they trespassed against me, and that also they have walked contrary unto me;
    >
    > <sup>[41](/bible/leviticus/26#v41)</sup> And that I also have walked contrary unto them, and have brought them into the land of their enemies; if then their uncircumcised hearts be humbled, and they then accept of the punishment of their iniquity:

    If they confess their iniquity and their uncircumcised hearts are humbled and they accept the punishment, then the Most High remembers his covenant; humbling brings deliverance.

**[2 Chronicles 12:9-11](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v9)</sup> So Shishak king of Egypt came up against Jerusalem, and took away the treasures of the house of the Lord, and the treasures of the king’s house; he took all: he carried away also the shields of gold which Solomon had made.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v10)</sup> Instead of which king Rehoboam made shields of brass, and committed them to the hands of the chief of the guard, that kept the entrance of the king’s house.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v11)</sup> And when the king entered into the house of the Lord, the guard came and fetched them, and brought them again into the guard chamber.

- Shishak takes away the treasures of the house of the Lord and of the king's house, carrying off all the shields of gold Solomon had made.
- Rehoboam makes shields of brass in their place and commits them to the guard that keeps the entrance of the king's house.
- Whenever the king enters the house of the Lord the guard fetches the shields and returns them to the guard chamber.

**[2 Chronicles 12:12](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v12)</sup> And when he humbled himself, the wrath of the Lord turned from him, that he would not destroy him altogether: and also in Judah things went well.

- Because Rehoboam humbles himself, the wrath of the Lord turns from him so that he is not utterly destroyed, and in Judah things go well.
- Deliverance and the destruction of enemies come to the people when they humble themselves before the Most High.

**[2 Chronicles 12:13-14](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v13)</sup> So king Rehoboam strengthened himself in Jerusalem, and reigned: for Rehoboam was one and forty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city which the Lord had chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, to put his name there. And his mother’s name was Naamah an Ammonitess.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v14)</sup> And he did evil, because he prepared not his heart to seek the Lord.

- Rehoboam strengthens himself in Jerusalem and reigns; he is forty and one years old when he begins to reign and reigns seventeen years in Jerusalem, the city the Lord chose to put his name there.
- His mother's name is Naamah an Ammonitess.
- He does evil because he prepares not his heart to seek the Lord; the failure is a heart never set on the Most High.

**[2 Chronicles 12:15-16](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v15)</sup> Now the acts of Rehoboam, first and last, are they not written in the book of Shemaiah the prophet, and of Iddo the seer concerning genealogies? And there were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/12#v16)</sup> And Rehoboam slept with his fathers, and was buried in the city of David: and Abijah his son reigned in his stead.

- The acts of Rehoboam are written in the book of Shemaiah the prophet and of Iddo the seer concerning genealogies, and there were wars between Rehoboam and Jeroboam continually.
- Rehoboam sleeps with his fathers, is buried in the city of David, and his son Abijah reigns in his place.
- The account then turns to Abijah's rule over Judah in the next chapter, continuing this compressed history that is told more fully in 1 and 2 Kings.
