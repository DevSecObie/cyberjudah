**[2 Chronicles 11:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v1)</sup> And when Rehoboam was come to Jerusalem, he gathered of the house of Judah and Benjamin an hundred and fourscore thousand chosen men, which were warriors, to fight against Israel, that he might bring the kingdom again to Rehoboam.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v2)</sup> But the word of the Lord came to Shemaiah the man of God, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v3)</sup> Speak unto Rehoboam the son of Solomon, king of Judah, and to all Israel in Judah and Benjamin, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v4)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Ye shall not go up, nor fight against your brethren: return every man to his house: for this thing is done of me. And they obeyed the words of the Lord, and returned from going against Jeroboam.

- When Rehoboam comes to Jerusalem he gathers a hundred and eighty thousand chosen warriors of Judah and Benjamin to fight against Israel and bring the kingdom back to himself.
- The word of the Lord comes to Shemaiah the man of God, telling Rehoboam and all Judah and Benjamin not to go up or fight against their brethren, for this thing is done of the Most High.
- They obey the word of the Lord and return from going against Jeroboam, so the war between north and south does not happen.

**[2 Chronicles 11:5-12](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v5)</sup> And Rehoboam dwelt in Jerusalem, and built cities for defence in Judah.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v6)</sup> He built even Beth–lehem, and Etam, and Tekoa,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v7)</sup> And Beth–zur, and Shoco, and Adullam,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v8)</sup> And Gath, and Mareshah, and Ziph,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v9)</sup> And Adoraim, and Lachish, and Azekah,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v10)</sup> And Zorah, and Aijalon, and Hebron, which are in Judah and in Benjamin fenced cities.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v11)</sup> And he fortified the strong holds, and put captains in them, and store of victual, and of oil and wine.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v12)</sup> And in every several city he put shields and spears, and made them exceeding strong, having Judah and Benjamin on his side.

- Rehoboam dwells in Jerusalem and builds cities for defense in Judah, including Bethlehem, Etam, Tekoa, Bethzur, Hebron, and others.
- He fortifies the strongholds, sets captains in them, and stores food, oil, and wine behind the walls he has built.
- He puts shields and spears in every city and makes them exceeding strong, having Judah and Benjamin on his side.

**[2 Chronicles 11:13-17](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v13)</sup> And the priests and the Levites that were in all Israel resorted to him out of all their coasts.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v14)</sup> For the Levites left their suburbs and their possession, and came to Judah and Jerusalem: for Jeroboam and his sons had cast them off from executing the priest’s office unto the Lord:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v15)</sup> And he ordained him priests for the high places, and for the devils, and for the calves which he had made.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v16)</sup> And after them out of all the tribes of Israel such as set their hearts to seek the Lord God of Israel came to Jerusalem, to sacrifice unto the Lord God of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v17)</sup> So they strengthened the kingdom of Judah, and made Rehoboam the son of Solomon strong, three years: for three years they walked in the way of David and Solomon.

- The priests and Levites out of all Israel resort to Rehoboam, leaving their suburbs and possessions to come to Judah and Jerusalem, because Jeroboam cast them off from the priest's office.
- Jeroboam had ordained his own priests for the high places, the devils, and the calves he made, so Levi had to return where they could serve.
- Those out of all the tribes who set their hearts to seek the Lord God of Israel come to Jerusalem to sacrifice, and for three years they strengthen the kingdom of Judah and walk in the way of David and Solomon.

**[2 Chronicles 11:18-23](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v18)</sup> And Rehoboam took him Mahalath the daughter of Jerimoth the son of David to wife, and Abihail the daughter of Eliab the son of Jesse;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v19)</sup> Which bare him children; Jeush, and Shamariah, and Zaham.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v20)</sup> And after her he took Maachah the daughter of Absalom; which bare him Abijah, and Attai, and Ziza, and Shelomith.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v21)</sup> And Rehoboam loved Maachah the daughter of Absalom above all his wives and his concubines: (for he took eighteen wives, and threescore concubines; and begat twenty and eight sons, and threescore daughters.)
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v22)</sup> And Rehoboam made Abijah the son of Maachah the chief, to be ruler among his brethren: for he thought to make him king.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/11#v23)</sup> And he dealt wisely, and dispersed of all his children throughout all the countries of Judah and Benjamin, unto every fenced city: and he gave them victual in abundance. And he desired many wives.

- Rehoboam takes Mahalath and Abihail as wives who bear him children, then takes Maachah the daughter of Absalom, whom he loves above all his wives and concubines.
- He takes eighteen wives and threescore concubines and begets twenty and eight sons and threescore daughters.
- He makes Abijah the son of Maachah chief and ruler among his brethren, intending to make him king, and he deals wisely by dispersing his children through the countries of Judah and Benjamin with abundant provision.
