**[2 Chronicles 28:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v1)</sup> Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign, and he reigned sixteen years in Jerusalem: but he did not that which was right in the sight of the Lord, like David his father:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v2)</sup> For he walked in the ways of the kings of Israel, and made also molten images for Baalim.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v3)</sup> Moreover he burnt incense in the valley of the son of Hinnom, and burnt his children in the fire, after the abominations of the heathen whom the Lord had cast out before the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v4)</sup> He sacrificed also and burnt incense in the high places, and on the hills, and under every green tree.

- Ahaz was twenty years old when he began to reign and reigned sixteen years, but he did not do that which was right in the sight of the Most High like his father David. He walked in the ways of the kings of Israel and made molten images for Baalim.
- He burned incense in the valley of the son of Hinnom and burned his children in the fire, after the abominations of the heathen that the Most High cast out before the children of Israel. This was witchcraft and child sacrifice, the practice called Tophet, in the high places under every green tree.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 7:30-31](/bible/jeremiah/7#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/jeremiah/7#v30)</sup> For the children of Judah have done evil in my sight, saith the Lord: they have set their abominations in the house which is called by my name, to pollute it.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/jeremiah/7#v31)</sup> And they have built the high places of Tophet, which is in the valley of the son of Hinnom, to burn their sons and their daughters in the fire; which I commanded them not, neither came it into my heart.

    They built the high places of Tophet in the valley of Hinnom to burn their sons and daughters in the fire, a thing the Most High commanded not, neither came it into His heart.

**[2 Chronicles 28:5-8](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v5)</sup> Wherefore the Lord his God delivered him into the hand of the king of Syria; and they smote him, and carried away a great multitude of them captives, and brought them to Damascus. And he was also delivered into the hand of the king of Israel, who smote him with a great slaughter.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v6)</sup> For Pekah the son of Remaliah slew in Judah an hundred and twenty thousand in one day, which were all valiant men; because they had forsaken the Lord God of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v7)</sup> And Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, slew Maaseiah the king’s son, and Azrikam the governor of the house, and Elkanah that was next to the king.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v8)</sup> And the children of Israel carried away captive of their brethren two hundred thousand, women, sons, and daughters, and took also away much spoil from them, and brought the spoil to Samaria.

- Therefore the Most High delivered Ahaz into the hand of the king of Syria, who carried away a great multitude of captives to Damascus, and into the hand of the king of Israel, who smote him with a great slaughter. Pekah slew in Judah a hundred and twenty thousand in one day, all valiant men, because they had forsaken the Most High God of their fathers.
- Zichri, a mighty man of Ephraim, slew the king's son, the governor of the house, and the man next to the king. The children of Israel carried away captive of their brethren two hundred thousand women, sons, and daughters, and much spoil, and brought it to Samaria. This was brother against brother, one nation of Israel captiving the other.

**[2 Chronicles 28:9-15](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v9)</sup> But a prophet of the Lord was there, whose name was Oded: and he went out before the host that came to Samaria, and said unto them, Behold, because the Lord God of your fathers was wroth with Judah, he hath delivered them into your hand, and ye have slain them in a rage that reacheth up unto heaven.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v10)</sup> And now ye purpose to keep under the children of Judah and Jerusalem for bondmen and bondwomen unto you: but are there not with you, even with you, sins against the Lord your God?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v11)</sup> Now hear me therefore, and deliver the captives again, which ye have taken captive of your brethren: for the fierce wrath of the Lord is upon you.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v12)</sup> Then certain of the heads of the children of Ephraim, Azariah the son of Johanan, Berechiah the son of Meshillemoth, and Jehizkiah the son of Shallum, and Amasa the son of Hadlai, stood up against them that came from the war,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v13)</sup> And said unto them, Ye shall not bring in the captives hither: for whereas we have offended against the Lord already, ye intend to add more to our sins and to our trespass: for our trespass is great, and there is fierce wrath against Israel.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v14)</sup> So the armed men left the captives and the spoil before the princes and all the congregation.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v15)</sup> And the men which were expressed by name rose up, and took the captives, and with the spoil clothed all that were naked among them, and arrayed them, and shod them, and gave them to eat and to drink, and anointed them, and carried all the feeble of them upon asses, and brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their brethren: then they returned to Samaria.

- A prophet of the Most High named Oded went out before the host that came to Samaria and told them the Most High had delivered Judah into their hand because of His wrath, but they had slain them in a rage that reached up to heaven, and now purposed to keep the children of Judah as bondmen, which is against the law, adding to their own sins.
- Certain heads of the children of Ephraim also stood up against the men that came from the war, saying they had offended against the Most High already and would not add more to their sins. So the armed men left the captives and the spoil before the princes and the congregation.
- The men named by name took the captives, clothed all that were naked among them, shod them, gave them to eat and drink, anointed them, carried the feeble on asses, and brought them to Jericho, the city of palm trees, to their brethren, then returned to Samaria. Even in the middle of the division, these men feared the Most High and would not treat their brethren as slaves.

    If your brother waxes poor and is sold to you, you shall not make him serve as a bondservant, but as a hired servant, and he shall go free in the year of jubilee.

**[2 Chronicles 28:16-21](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v16)</sup> At that time did king Ahaz send unto the kings of Assyria to help him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v17)</sup> For again the Edomites had come and smitten Judah, and carried away captives.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v18)</sup> The Philistines also had invaded the cities of the low country, and of the south of Judah, and had taken Beth–shemesh, and Ajalon, and Gederoth, and Shocho with the villages thereof, and Timnah with the villages thereof, Gimzo also and the villages thereof: and they dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v19)</sup> For the Lord brought Judah low because of Ahaz king of Israel; for he made Judah naked, and transgressed sore against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v20)</sup> And Tilgath–pilneser king of Assyria came unto him, and distressed him, but strengthened him not.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v21)</sup> For Ahaz took away a portion out of the house of the Lord, and out of the house of the king, and of the princes, and gave it unto the king of Assyria: but he helped him not.

- At that time Ahaz sent to the kings of Assyria to help him, for the Edomites had come and smitten Judah and carried away captives, and the Philistines had invaded the cities of the low country and the south of Judah and taken them. The Most High brought Judah low because of Ahaz, for he made Judah naked and transgressed sore against the Most High.
- Tiglath-pileser king of Assyria came to him and distressed him but strengthened him not. Ahaz took a portion out of the house of the Most High, the king's house, and the princes, and gave it to the king of Assyria, but it did not help him. Running to another nation for deliverance from the curses of God never works, then or now.

**[2 Chronicles 28:22-27](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v22)</sup> And in the time of his distress did he trespass yet more against the Lord: this is that king Ahaz.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v23)</sup> For he sacrificed unto the gods of Damascus, which smote him: and he said, Because the gods of the kings of Syria help them, therefore will I sacrifice to them, that they may help me. But they were the ruin of him, and of all Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v24)</sup> And Ahaz gathered together the vessels of the house of God, and cut in pieces the vessels of the house of God, and shut up the doors of the house of the Lord, and he made him altars in every corner of Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v25)</sup> And in every several city of Judah he made high places to burn incense unto other gods, and provoked to anger the Lord God of his fathers.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v26)</sup> Now the rest of his acts and of all his ways, first and last, behold, they are written in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-chronicles/28#v27)</sup> And Ahaz slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the city, even in Jerusalem: but they brought him not into the sepulchres of the kings of Israel: and Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead.

- In the time of his distress Ahaz trespassed yet more against the Most High. He sacrificed to the gods of Damascus that smote him, saying, Because the gods of the kings of Syria help them, I will sacrifice to them that they may help me. But they were the ruin of him and of all Israel.
- Ahaz gathered up the vessels of the house of God, cut them in pieces, shut up the doors of the house of the Most High, and made altars in every corner of Jerusalem. This is the pattern of running to a nation that cannot save, the same spirit that trusts in the systems of the world instead of the Most High.
- Ahaz slept with his fathers and they buried him in the city, in Jerusalem, but not in the sepulchres of the kings of Israel, and Hezekiah his son reigned in his stead. The Most High is the only one who can bring the people out of their condition, then and now.

    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/11#v13)</sup> The envy also of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off: Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim.

    The Most High will assemble the outcasts of Israel and gather the dispersed of Judah; then Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim.
