**[2 Chronicles 30:1-5](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v1)</sup> And Hezekiah sent to all Israel and Judah, and wrote letters also to Ephraim and Manasseh, that they should come to the house of the Lord at Jerusalem, to keep the passover unto the Lord God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v2)</sup> For the king had taken counsel, and his princes, and all the congregation in Jerusalem, to keep the passover in the second month.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v3)</sup> For they could not keep it at that time, because the priests had not sanctified themselves sufficiently, neither had the people gathered themselves together to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v4)</sup> And the thing pleased the king and all the congregation.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v5)</sup> So they established a decree to make proclamation throughout all Israel, from Beer–sheba even to Dan, that they should come to keep the passover unto the Lord God of Israel at Jerusalem: for they had not done it of a long time in such sort as it was written.

- Now that the temple is cleansed and the priests sanctified, Hezekiah sends letters to all Israel and Judah, even to Ephraim and Manasseh, calling them to keep the Passover at Jerusalem.
- The king and congregation agree to keep it in the second month, because the priests had not sanctified themselves sufficiently and the people had not yet gathered in the first month.
- They make a proclamation from Beersheba to Dan to come keep the Passover, for they had not done it for a long time in the way it was written.

**[2 Chronicles 30:6-9](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v6)</sup> So the posts went with the letters from the king and his princes throughout all Israel and Judah, and according to the commandment of the king, saying, Ye children of Israel, turn again unto the Lord God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, and he will return to the remnant of you, that are escaped out of the hand of the kings of Assyria.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v7)</sup> And be not ye like your fathers, and like your brethren, which trespassed against the Lord God of their fathers, who therefore gave them up to desolation, as ye see.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v8)</sup> Now be ye not stiffnecked, as your fathers were, but yield yourselves unto the Lord, and enter into his sanctuary, which he hath sanctified for ever: and serve the Lord your God, that the fierceness of his wrath may turn away from you.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v9)</sup> For if ye turn again unto the Lord, your brethren and your children shall find compassion before them that lead them captive, so that they shall come again into this land: for the Lord your God is gracious and merciful, and will not turn away his face from you, if ye return unto him.

- The posts carry the letters through all Israel and Judah with the king's charge: turn again unto the Lord God of Abraham, Isaac, and Israel, and he will return to the remnant escaped from the kings of Assyria.
- Do not be like your fathers who trespassed and were given up to desolation, and do not be stiffnecked, but yield to the Lord and enter his sanctuary so his fierce wrath may turn away.
- If they turn again, their captive brethren and children will find compassion and return to the land, for the Lord is gracious and merciful and will not turn his face away from those who return to him.

**[2 Chronicles 30:10-14](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v10)</sup> So the posts passed from city to city through the country of Ephraim and Manasseh even unto Zebulun: but they laughed them to scorn, and mocked them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v11)</sup> Nevertheless divers of Asher and Manasseh and of Zebulun humbled themselves, and came to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v12)</sup> Also in Judah the hand of God was to give them one heart to do the commandment of the king and of the princes, by the word of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v13)</sup> And there assembled at Jerusalem much people to keep the feast of unleavened bread in the second month, a very great congregation.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v14)</sup> And they arose and took away the altars that were in Jerusalem, and all the altars for incense took they away, and cast them into the brook Kidron.

- The posts pass from city to city through Ephraim and Manasseh to Zebulun, but the people laugh them to scorn and mock them, the same mockery Israel meets today when the truth is preached.
- Nevertheless some of Asher, Manasseh, and Zebulun humble themselves and come to Jerusalem, and in Judah the hand of God gives them one heart to do the commandment of the king.
- The great congregation removes the altars and incense altars in Jerusalem and casts them into the brook Kidron.

    Speak the same thing, be of one mind and one judgment with no divisions, the same unity that gave Israel one heart to serve the Most High.

**[2 Chronicles 30:15-20](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v15)</sup> Then they killed the passover on the fourteenth day of the second month: and the priests and the Levites were ashamed, and sanctified themselves, and brought in the burnt offerings into the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v16)</sup> And they stood in their place after their manner, according to the law of Moses the man of God: the priests sprinkled the blood, which they received of the hand of the Levites.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v17)</sup> For there were many in the congregation that were not sanctified: therefore the Levites had the charge of the killing of the passovers for every one that was not clean, to sanctify them unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v18)</sup> For a multitude of the people, even many of Ephraim, and Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun, had not cleansed themselves, yet did they eat the passover otherwise than it was written. But Hezekiah prayed for them, saying, The good Lord pardon every one
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v19)</sup> That prepareth his heart to seek God, the Lord God of his fathers, though he be not cleansed according to the purification of the sanctuary.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v20)</sup> And the Lord hearkened to Hezekiah, and healed the people.

- They kill the Passover on the fourteenth day of the second month, and the priests and Levites, ashamed, sanctify themselves and bring the burnt offerings into the house of the Lord.
- Because many in the congregation were not sanctified, the Levites take charge of killing the Passover for everyone not clean.
- Many of Ephraim, Manasseh, Issachar, and Zebulun eat the Passover otherwise than it was written, but Hezekiah prays that the good Lord pardon everyone who prepares his heart to seek God, and the Lord hearkens and heals the people. Grace and mercy are present here in the Old Testament.

**[2 Chronicles 30:21-27](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v21)</sup> And the children of Israel that were present at Jerusalem kept the feast of unleavened bread seven days with great gladness: and the Levites and the priests praised the Lord day by day, singing with loud instruments unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v22)</sup> And Hezekiah spake comfortably unto all the Levites that taught the good knowledge of the Lord: and they did eat throughout the feast seven days, offering peace offerings, and making confession to the Lord God of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v23)</sup> And the whole assembly took counsel to keep other seven days: and they kept other seven days with gladness.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v24)</sup> For Hezekiah king of Judah did give to the congregation a thousand bullocks and seven thousand sheep; and the princes gave to the congregation a thousand bullocks and ten thousand sheep: and a great number of priests sanctified themselves.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v25)</sup> And all the congregation of Judah, with the priests and the Levites, and all the congregation that came out of Israel, and the strangers that came out of the land of Israel, and that dwelt in Judah, rejoiced.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v26)</sup> So there was great joy in Jerusalem: for since the time of Solomon the son of David king of Israel there was not the like in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-chronicles/30#v27)</sup> Then the priests the Levites arose and blessed the people: and their voice was heard, and their prayer came up to his holy dwelling place, even unto heaven.

- The children of Israel keep the feast of unleavened bread seven days with great gladness, then take counsel and keep another seven days, fourteen days in all, all in one mind and one heart.
- Hezekiah and the princes give thousands of bullocks and sheep, and all the congregation, the priests, the Levites, and the strangers out of Israel rejoice together.
- There is great joy in Jerusalem, for since the days of Solomon there had not been the like, and the priests and Levites bless the people and their prayer reaches heaven, his holy dwelling place.

    Solomon and all Israel kept the feast seven days and seven days, even fourteen days, the earlier pattern Hezekiah's congregation repeated.
