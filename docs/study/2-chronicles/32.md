**[2 Chronicles 32:1-8](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v1)</sup> After these things, and the establishment thereof, Sennacherib king of Assyria came, and entered into Judah, and encamped against the fenced cities, and thought to win them for himself.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v2)</sup> And when Hezekiah saw that Sennacherib was come, and that he was purposed to fight against Jerusalem,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v3)</sup> He took counsel with his princes and his mighty men to stop the waters of the fountains which were without the city: and they did help him.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v4)</sup> So there was gathered much people together, who stopped all the fountains, and the brook that ran through the midst of the land, saying, Why should the kings of Assyria come, and find much water?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v5)</sup> Also he strengthened himself, and built up all the wall that was broken, and raised it up to the towers, and another wall without, and repaired Millo in the city of David, and made darts and shields in abundance.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v6)</sup> And he set captains of war over the people, and gathered them together to him in the street of the gate of the city, and spake comfortably to them, saying,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v7)</sup> Be strong and courageous, be not afraid nor dismayed for the king of Assyria, nor for all the multitude that is with him: for there be more with us than with him:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v8)</sup> With him is an arm of flesh; but with us is the Lord our God to help us, and to fight our battles. And the people rested themselves upon the words of Hezekiah king of Judah.

- After these acts of faithfulness Sennacherib king of Assyria enters Judah and encamps against the fenced cities, thinking to win them for himself.
- Hezekiah takes counsel to stop the waters of the fountains outside the city, repairs the broken wall, raises towers, builds another wall, and makes darts and shields in abundance. Our people are resourceful and skilled, doing the work themselves.
- He sets captains over the people and comforts them: be strong and courageous, for there be more with us than with him; with him is an arm of flesh, but with us is the Lord our God to help us and fight our battles.

  - **[2 Kings 20:20](/bible/2-kings/20#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/2-kings/20#v20)</sup> And the rest of the acts of Hezekiah, and all his might, and how he made a pool, and a conduit, and brought water into the city, are they not written in the book of the chronicles of the kings of Judah?

    Hezekiah made a pool and a conduit and brought water into the city, the water system he now used against the siege.
  - **[Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.

    Cursed be the man that trusteth in man and maketh flesh his arm, the opposite of Hezekiah's trust in the Most High.

**[2 Chronicles 32:9-15](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v9)</sup> After this did Sennacherib king of Assyria send his servants to Jerusalem, (but he himself laid siege against Lachish, and all his power with him,) unto Hezekiah king of Judah, and unto all Judah that were at Jerusalem, saying,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v10)</sup> Thus saith Sennacherib king of Assyria, Whereon do ye trust, that ye abide in the siege in Jerusalem?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v11)</sup> Doth not Hezekiah persuade you to give over yourselves to die by famine and by thirst, saying, The Lord our God shall deliver us out of the hand of the king of Assyria?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v12)</sup> Hath not the same Hezekiah taken away his high places and his altars, and commanded Judah and Jerusalem, saying, Ye shall worship before one altar, and burn incense upon it?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v13)</sup> Know ye not what I and my fathers have done unto all the people of other lands? were the gods of the nations of those lands any ways able to deliver their lands out of mine hand?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v14)</sup> Who was there among all the gods of those nations that my fathers utterly destroyed, that could deliver his people out of mine hand, that your God should be able to deliver you out of mine hand?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v15)</sup> Now therefore let not Hezekiah deceive you, nor persuade you on this manner, neither yet believe him: for no god of any nation or kingdom was able to deliver his people out of mine hand, and out of the hand of my fathers: how much less shall your God deliver you out of mine hand?

- Sennacherib sends servants to Jerusalem asking on what the people trust that they abide in the siege, and claims Hezekiah is persuading them to die by famine and thirst.
- He mocks that Hezekiah took away the high places and altars and commanded worship at one altar, twisting obedience to the commandment into a weakness.
- He argues that no god of any nation delivered its people from his hand, so neither will the God of Hezekiah. This is the same seed the world plants to make Israel weary and turn a people off one accord.

  - **[Colossians 2:8](/bible/colossians/2#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/2#v8)</sup> Beware lest any man spoil you through philosophy and vain deceit, after the tradition of men, after the rudiments of the world, and not after Christ.

    Beware lest any man spoil you through vain deceit, for the enemy still seeks to deceive Israel out of the truth.

**[2 Chronicles 32:16-19](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v16)</sup> And his servants spake yet more against the Lord God, and against his servant Hezekiah.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v17)</sup> He wrote also letters to rail on the Lord God of Israel, and to speak against him, saying, As the gods of the nations of other lands have not delivered their people out of mine hand, so shall not the God of Hezekiah deliver his people out of mine hand.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v18)</sup> Then they cried with a loud voice in the Jews’ speech unto the people of Jerusalem that were on the wall, to affright them, and to trouble them; that they might take the city.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v19)</sup> And they spake against the God of Jerusalem, as against the gods of the people of the earth, which were the work of the hands of man.

- Sennacherib's servants speak yet more against the Lord God and against his servant Hezekiah, and he writes letters to rail on the Lord God of Israel.
- They cry with a loud voice in the Jews' speech to the people on the wall to frighten and trouble them so they might take the city.
- They speak against the God of Jerusalem as against the gods of the peoples of the earth, which were only the work of men's hands. The one true God is the God of Israel, not an idol like the false gods of the nations.

**[2 Chronicles 32:20-23](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v20)</sup> And for this cause Hezekiah the king, and the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz, prayed and cried to heaven.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v21)</sup> And the Lord sent an angel, which cut off all the mighty men of valour, and the leaders and captains in the camp of the king of Assyria. So he returned with shame of face to his own land. And when he was come into the house of his god, they that came forth of his own bowels slew him there with the sword.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v22)</sup> Thus the Lord saved Hezekiah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem from the hand of Sennacherib the king of Assyria, and from the hand of all other, and guided them on every side.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v23)</sup> And many brought gifts unto the Lord to Jerusalem, and presents to Hezekiah king of Judah: so that he was magnified in the sight of all nations from thenceforth.

- Hezekiah the king and Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amos, pray and cry to heaven over this railing.
- The Lord sends an angel that cuts off all the mighty men of valour, leaders, and captains in the camp of Assyria, so Sennacherib returns with shame to his own land, and his own sons slay him with the sword in the house of his god.
- Thus the Lord saves Hezekiah and Jerusalem and guides them on every side, and many bring gifts to the Lord and presents to Hezekiah, so he is magnified in the sight of all nations, a picture of the gifts the nations will bring back to Israel in the kingdom.

**[2 Chronicles 32:24-26](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v24)</sup> In those days Hezekiah was sick to the death, and prayed unto the Lord: and he spake unto him, and he gave him a sign.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v25)</sup> But Hezekiah rendered not again according to the benefit done unto him; for his heart was lifted up: therefore there was wrath upon him, and upon Judah and Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v26)</sup> Notwithstanding Hezekiah humbled himself for the pride of his heart, both he and the inhabitants of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of the Lord came not upon them in the days of Hezekiah.

- In those days Hezekiah is sick to death, prays to the Lord, and is given a sign.
- But Hezekiah renders not according to the benefit done to him, for his heart is lifted up in pride, and wrath comes upon him and upon Judah and Jerusalem. When things go well, do not get puffed up and make it about yourself.
- Then Hezekiah humbles himself for the pride of his heart, he and the people of Jerusalem, so that the wrath of the Lord does not come upon them in his days.

  - **[2 Kings 20:1-6](/bible/2-kings/20#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-kings/20#v1)</sup> In those days was Hezekiah sick unto death. And the prophet Isaiah the son of Amoz came to him, and said unto him, Thus saith the Lord, Set thine house in order; for thou shalt die, and not live.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-kings/20#v2)</sup> Then he turned his face to the wall, and prayed unto the Lord, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-kings/20#v3)</sup> I beseech thee, O Lord, remember now how I have walked before thee in truth and with a perfect heart, and have done that which is good in thy sight. And Hezekiah wept sore.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-kings/20#v4)</sup> And it came to pass, afore Isaiah was gone out into the middle court, that the word of the Lord came to him, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-kings/20#v5)</sup> Turn again, and tell Hezekiah the captain of my people, Thus saith the Lord, the God of David thy father, I have heard thy prayer, I have seen thy tears: behold, I will heal thee: on the third day thou shalt go up unto the house of the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-kings/20#v6)</sup> And I will add unto thy days fifteen years; and I will deliver thee and this city out of the hand of the king of Assyria; and I will defend this city for mine own sake, and for my servant David’s sake.

    Isaiah tells Hezekiah to set his house in order for he shall die, and after Hezekiah prays the Lord adds fifteen years to his life and defends the city.
  - **[2 Kings 20:8-11](/bible/2-kings/20#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-kings/20#v8)</sup> And Hezekiah said unto Isaiah, What shall be the sign that the Lord will heal me, and that I shall go up into the house of the Lord the third day?
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/20#v9)</sup> And Isaiah said, This sign shalt thou have of the Lord, that the Lord will do the thing that he hath spoken: shall the shadow go forward ten degrees, or go back ten degrees?
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/20#v10)</sup> And Hezekiah answered, It is a light thing for the shadow to go down ten degrees: nay, but let the shadow return backward ten degrees.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/20#v11)</sup> And Isaiah the prophet cried unto the Lord: and he brought the shadow ten degrees backward, by which it had gone down in the dial of Ahaz.

    The sign is the shadow returning ten degrees backward on the sundial of Ahaz, a thing only the Most High could do.

**[2 Chronicles 32:27-33](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v27)</sup> And Hezekiah had exceeding much riches and honour: and he made himself treasuries for silver, and for gold, and for precious stones, and for spices, and for shields, and for all manner of pleasant jewels;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v28)</sup> Storehouses also for the increase of corn, and wine, and oil; and stalls for all manner of beasts, and cotes for flocks.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v29)</sup> Moreover he provided him cities, and possessions of flocks and herds in abundance: for God had given him substance very much.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v30)</sup> This same Hezekiah also stopped the upper watercourse of Gihon, and brought it straight down to the west side of the city of David. And Hezekiah prospered in all his works.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v31)</sup> Howbeit in the business of the ambassadors of the princes of Babylon, who sent unto him to enquire of the wonder that was done in the land, God left him, to try him, that he might know all that was in his heart.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v32)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Hezekiah, and his goodness, behold, they are written in the vision of Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, and in the book of the kings of Judah and Israel.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/2-chronicles/32#v33)</sup> And Hezekiah slept with his fathers, and they buried him in the chiefest of the sepulchres of the sons of David: and all Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem did him honour at his death. And Manasseh his son reigned in his stead.

- Hezekiah has exceeding riches and honour, making treasuries for silver, gold, precious stones, and spices, and storehouses for corn, wine, oil, and cattle, for God gives him very much substance.
- He stops the upper watercourse of Gihon and brings it straight down to the west side of the city of David, and prospers in all his works, the plumbing he built for Jerusalem.
- When the ambassadors of Babylon come to inquire of the wonder, God leaves him to try him and know his heart. Hezekiah then sleeps with his fathers, is buried in honour among the sons of David, and Manasseh his son reigns in his stead.
