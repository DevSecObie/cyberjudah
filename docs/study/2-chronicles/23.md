**[2 Chronicles 23:1-7](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v1)</sup> And in the seventh year Jehoiada strengthened himself, and took the captains of hundreds, Azariah the son of Jeroham, and Ishmael the son of Jehohanan, and Azariah the son of Obed, and Maaseiah the son of Adaiah, and Elishaphat the son of Zichri, into covenant with him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v2)</sup> And they went about in Judah, and gathered the Levites out of all the cities of Judah, and the chief of the fathers of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v3)</sup> And all the congregation made a covenant with the king in the house of God. And he said unto them, Behold, the king’s son shall reign, as the Lord hath said of the sons of David.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v4)</sup> This is the thing that ye shall do; A third part of you entering on the sabbath, of the priests and of the Levites, shall be porters of the doors;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v5)</sup> And a third part shall be at the king’s house; and a third part at the gate of the foundation: and all the people shall be in the courts of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v6)</sup> But let none come into the house of the Lord, save the priests, and they that minister of the Levites; they shall go in, for they are holy: but all the people shall keep the watch of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v7)</sup> And the Levites shall compass the king round about, every man with his weapons in his hand; and whosoever else cometh into the house, he shall be put to death: but be ye with the king when he cometh in, and when he goeth out.

- In the seventh year Jehoiada strengthened himself and took the captains of hundreds into covenant with him, and they gathered the Levites and the chief of the fathers out of all the cities of Judah to Jerusalem.
- The congregation made a covenant with the king in the house of God, and Jehoiada declared that the king's son should reign, as the Most High had said of the sons of David.
- He set his plan: a third part of the priests and Levites to keep the doors, a third at the king's house, and a third at the gate of the foundation, with all the people in the courts.
- None but the priests and ministering Levites were to enter the house of the Most High, and the Levites were to compass the king round about, every man with his weapon, and put to death any who forced their way in.

**[2 Chronicles 23:8-11](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v8)</sup> So the Levites and all Judah did according to all things that Jehoiada the priest had commanded, and took every man his men that were to come in on the sabbath, with them that were to go out on the sabbath: for Jehoiada the priest dismissed not the courses.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v9)</sup> Moreover Jehoiada the priest delivered to the captains of hundreds spears, and bucklers, and shields, that had been king David’s, which were in the house of God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v10)</sup> And he set all the people, every man having his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the temple to the left side of the temple, along by the altar and the temple, by the king round about.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v11)</sup> Then they brought out the king’s son, and put upon him the crown, and gave him the testimony, and made him king. And Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and said, God save the king.

- The Levites and all Judah did according to all Jehoiada commanded, each man keeping his company, for Jehoiada did not dismiss the courses of the Levites but kept them at their work with security added for the king.
- Jehoiada delivered to the captains the spears and shields that had been king David's, kept in the house of God, and set every man with his weapon from one side of the temple to the other, round about the king.
- They brought out the king's son, put the crown upon him, gave him the testimony, and made him king, and Jehoiada and his sons anointed him and said, God save the king.
- This was done by the proper custom: a priest anoints the king, as Samuel the priest anointed Saul and then David; so Jehoiada the priest anointed Joash.

**[2 Chronicles 23:12-15](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v12)</sup> Now when Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came to the people into the house of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v13)</sup> And she looked, and, behold, the king stood at his pillar at the entering in, and the princes and the trumpets by the king: and all the people of the land rejoiced, and sounded with trumpets, also the singers with instruments of musick, and such as taught to sing praise. Then Athaliah rent her clothes, and said, Treason, Treason.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v14)</sup> Then Jehoiada the priest brought out the captains of hundreds that were set over the host, and said unto them, Have her forth of the ranges: and whoso followeth her, let him be slain with the sword. For the priest said, Slay her not in the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v15)</sup> So they laid hands on her; and when she was come to the entering of the horse gate by the king’s house, they slew her there.

- When Athaliah heard the noise of the people running and praising the king, she came into the house of the Most High and saw the king standing at his pillar with the princes, trumpets, and singers rejoicing.
- She rent her clothes and cried, Treason, treason, but her cry came too late, for the covenant was already made and the true king was crowned.
- Jehoiada commanded the captains to have her forth from the ranges and to slay any that followed her, but not to slay her in the house of the Most High.
- They laid hands on her, brought her to the entering of the horse gate by the king's house, and slew her there, ending the reign of the woman who had killed the seed royal.

**[2 Chronicles 23:16-21](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v16)</sup> And Jehoiada made a covenant between him, and between all the people, and between the king, that they should be the Lord’s people.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v17)</sup> Then all the people went to the house of Baal, and brake it down, and brake his altars and his images in pieces, and slew Mattan the priest of Baal before the altars.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v18)</sup> Also Jehoiada appointed the offices of the house of the Lord by the hand of the priests the Levites, whom David had distributed in the house of the Lord, to offer the burnt offerings of the Lord, as it is written in the law of Moses, with rejoicing and with singing, as it was ordained by David.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v19)</sup> And he set the porters at the gates of the house of the Lord, that none which was unclean in any thing should enter in.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v20)</sup> And he took the captains of hundreds, and the nobles, and the governors of the people, and all the people of the land, and brought down the king from the house of the Lord: and they came through the high gate into the king’s house, and set the king upon the throne of the kingdom.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v21)</sup> And all the people of the land rejoiced: and the city was quiet, after that they had slain Athaliah with the sword.

- Jehoiada made a covenant between himself, all the people, and the king, that they should be the Most High's people, and the people went to the house of Baal, broke it down, brake its altars and images, and slew Mattan the priest of Baal.
- Jehoiada appointed the officers of the house of the Most High by the hand of the priests and Levites whom David had distributed, to offer burnt offerings with rejoicing and singing as ordained by David.
- He set the porters at the gates so that none unclean in anything should enter, then brought the king down and set him upon the throne of the kingdom, and the people rejoiced and the city was quiet.
- Before a king is set in his position he is under tutors and governors; young Joash was king in rank, yet he was still a learner under Jehoiada, for even kings had to be taught. Athaliah had no place ruling Israel; being the king's mother did not make her the ruler.
