Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 21](/bible/2-chronicles/21)

These four chapters cover a dark stretch in the kingdom of Judah. Jehoshaphat was one of the righteous kings the Most High raised up, a man who did that which was right and was given a long reign of peace. As often happens in the history of Israel, right after a good king comes one who tears everything down. Jehoram takes the throne and turns to the ways of the wicked kings, and the account runs through his son Ahaziah, the usurping queen Athaliah, and the boy king Joash. Second Chronicles 21 correlates with Second Kings 8:17 and following; read the two accounts together, for this is the same history told once again, the way the four gospels retell one story.

**[2 Chronicles 21:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v1)</sup> Now Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers, and was buried with his fathers in the city of David. And Jehoram his son reigned in his stead.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v2)</sup> And he had brethren the sons of Jehoshaphat, Azariah, and Jehiel, and Zechariah, and Azariah, and Michael, and Shephatiah: all these were the sons of Jehoshaphat king of Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v3)</sup> And their father gave them great gifts of silver, and of gold, and of precious things, with fenced cities in Judah: but the kingdom gave he to Jehoram; because he was the firstborn.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v4)</sup> Now when Jehoram was risen up to the kingdom of his father, he strengthened himself, and slew all his brethren with the sword, and divers also of the princes of Israel.

- Jehoshaphat slept with his fathers and was buried in the city of David, and Jehoram his son reigned in his stead.
- Jehoshaphat had given his sons great gifts of silver, gold, and precious things with fenced cities, but he gave the kingdom to Jehoram because he was the firstborn.
- As soon as Jehoram was risen up and strengthened himself, he slew all his brethren with the sword, along with divers of the princes of Israel.
- This is a pattern to watch: as soon as some among Israel get power, they turn and hurt everyone around them; the moment he got the throne he killed his own brothers.

**[2 Chronicles 21:5-7](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v5)</sup> Jehoram was thirty and two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v6)</sup> And he walked in the way of the kings of Israel, like as did the house of Ahab: for he had the daughter of Ahab to wife: and he wrought that which was evil in the eyes of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v7)</sup> Howbeit the Lord would not destroy the house of David, because of the covenant that he had made with David, and as he promised to give a light to him and to his sons for ever.

- Jehoram was thirty and two years old when he began to reign, and he reigned eight years in Jerusalem.
- He walked in the way of the kings of Israel, as did the house of Ahab, meaning he was wicked; Israel's kings were all wicked, and he had the daughter of Ahab to wife.
- Yet the Most High would not destroy the house of David, because of the covenant He had made with David, promising to give a light to him and to his sons forever.
- The Most High keeps His promises. Just as He did not break His word to David, He did not break His promise to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; those promises were made to the Israelites and stand to this day, and we have plenty of proof of it.

**[2 Chronicles 21:8-11](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v8)</sup> In his days the Edomites revolted from under the dominion of Judah, and made themselves a king.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v9)</sup> Then Jehoram went forth with his princes, and all his chariots with him: and he rose up by night, and smote the Edomites which compassed him in, and the captains of the chariots.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v10)</sup> So the Edomites revolted from under the hand of Judah unto this day. The same time also did Libnah revolt from under his hand; because he had forsaken the Lord God of his fathers.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v11)</sup> Moreover he made high places in the mountains of Judah, and caused the inhabitants of Jerusalem to commit fornication, and compelled Judah thereto.

- In Jehoram's days Edom revolted from under the dominion of Judah and made themselves a king, and Libnah revolted at the same time because he had forsaken the Most High.
- When we obey the Most High the nations are subject to us; when we come out from under the commandments, that is when the other nations rise up and revolt against us and keep us in sin.
- Jehoram made high places in the mountains of Judah and caused the inhabitants of Jerusalem to commit fornication and compelled Judah thereto.
- Dealing with other gods is spiritual fornication, but physical fornication is built into these false worships; the pagan holidays passed down to our people, whatever the season, are built on fornication, and that is what draws the people to follow them.
  - **[Genesis 27:40](/bible/genesis/27#v40)**
    > <sup>[40](/bible/genesis/27#v40)</sup> And by thy sword shalt thou live, and shalt serve thy brother; and it shall come to pass when thou shalt have the dominion, that thou shalt break his yoke from off thy neck.

    Isaac's blessing to Esau, that he would serve his brother but break his yoke off his neck when he had the dominion, fulfilled here when Edom revolted.

**[2 Chronicles 21:12-15](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v12)</sup> And there came a writing to him from Elijah the prophet, saying, Thus saith the Lord God of David thy father, Because thou hast not walked in the ways of Jehoshaphat thy father, nor in the ways of Asa king of Judah,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v13)</sup> But hast walked in the way of the kings of Israel, and hast made Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem to go a whoring, like to the whoredoms of the house of Ahab, and also hast slain thy brethren of thy father’s house, which were better than thyself:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v14)</sup> Behold, with a great plague will the Lord smite thy people, and thy children, and thy wives, and all thy goods:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v15)</sup> And thou shalt have great sickness by disease of thy bowels, until thy bowels fall out by reason of the sickness day by day.

- There came a writing to Jehoram from Elijah the prophet, telling him he had not walked in the ways of Jehoshaphat his father nor in the ways of Asa king of Judah, but in the way of the kings of Israel.
- Elijah charged him with making Judah and Jerusalem go a whoring like the whoredoms of the house of Ahab, and with slaying his brethren who were better than himself.
- The Most High declared He would smite his people, his children, his wives, and his goods with a great plague.
- His own body would be struck: he would have great sickness by disease of his bowels, until his bowels fell out by reason of the sickness day by day. This is what it looks like to mess with the Most High.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:61](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v61)**
    > <sup>[61](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v61)</sup> Also every sickness, and every plague, which is not written in the book of this law, them will the Lord bring upon thee, until thou be destroyed.

    Every sickness not written in the book of the law the Most High would bring upon a disobedient people until they be destroyed.
  - **[1 Corinthians 11:30](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v30)</sup> For this cause many are weak and sickly among you, and many sleep.

    Because of disobedience many are weak and sickly, and many sleep, showing that sickness follows coming out from under the commandments.

**[2 Chronicles 21:16-20](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v16)</sup> Moreover the Lord stirred up against Jehoram the spirit of the Philistines, and of the Arabians, that were near the Ethiopians:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v17)</sup> And they came up into Judah, and brake into it, and carried away all the substance that was found in the king’s house, and his sons also, and his wives; so that there was never a son left him, save Jehoahaz, the youngest of his sons.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v18)</sup> And after all this the Lord smote him in his bowels with an incurable disease.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v19)</sup> And it came to pass, that in process of time, after the end of two years, his bowels fell out by reason of his sickness: so he died of sore diseases. And his people made no burning for him, like the burning of his fathers.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/21#v20)</sup> Thirty and two years old was he when he began to reign, and he reigned in Jerusalem eight years, and departed without being desired. Howbeit they buried him in the city of David, but not in the sepulchres of the kings.

- The Most High stirred up against Jehoram the spirit of the Philistines and the Arabians that were near the Ethiopians, and they came up into Judah and carried away all his substance, his sons, and his wives, leaving only Jehoahaz his youngest.
- Because of his disobedience his whole family was carried into captivity by the Philistines, the Arabs, and the Ethiopians, the same peoples who later held our people in the trans-Saharan slave trade.
- After all this the Most High smote him in his bowels with an incurable disease, and at the end of two years his bowels fell out and he died of sore diseases.
- He reigned eight years and departed without being desired; many were glad to see him go. They made no burning for him and buried him in the city of David but not in the sepulchres of the kings.
- A good name is better than precious ointment; a man cannot rule wickedly and shame the people and then expect them to stand by him when disease comes. It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the Most High.

# 2 Chronicles 22: Ahaziah and the Rise of Athaliah

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 22](/bible/2-chronicles/22)

**[2 Chronicles 22:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v1)</sup> And the inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah his youngest son king in his stead: for the band of men that came with the Arabians to the camp had slain all the eldest. So Ahaziah the son of Jehoram king of Judah reigned.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v2)</sup> Forty and two years old was Ahaziah when he began to reign, and he reigned one year in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Athaliah the daughter of Omri.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v3)</sup> He also walked in the ways of the house of Ahab: for his mother was his counsellor to do wickedly.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v4)</sup> Wherefore he did evil in the sight of the Lord like the house of Ahab: for they were his counsellors after the death of his father to his destruction.

- The inhabitants of Jerusalem made Ahaziah, Jehoram's youngest son, king in his stead, for the band of Arabians had slain all the elder sons.
- Ahaziah was forty and two years old when he began to reign and reigned one year in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Athaliah the daughter of Omri.
- He walked in the ways of the house of Ahab, for his mother was his counsellor to do wickedly, and the house of Ahab were his counsellors after his father died, to his destruction.
- This is a warning against being ruled by the wrong counsel: instead of going to the priests and the judges, he went to his mother, and she counselled him into ruin. A woman with a grudge can rule a kingdom through her son, and that is what Athaliah did.

**[2 Chronicles 22:5-9](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v5)</sup> He walked also after their counsel, and went with Jehoram the son of Ahab king of Israel to war against Hazael king of Syria at Ramoth–gilead: and the Syrians smote Joram.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v6)</sup> And he returned to be healed in Jezreel because of the wounds which were given him at Ramah, when he fought with Hazael king of Syria. And Azariah the son of Jehoram king of Judah went down to see Jehoram the son of Ahab at Jezreel, because he was sick.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v7)</sup> And the destruction of Ahaziah was of God by coming to Joram: for when he was come, he went out with Jehoram against Jehu the son of Nimshi, whom the Lord had anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v8)</sup> And it came to pass, that, when Jehu was executing judgment upon the house of Ahab, and found the princes of Judah, and the sons of the brethren of Ahaziah, that ministered to Ahaziah, he slew them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v9)</sup> And he sought Ahaziah: and they caught him, (for he was hid in Samaria,) and brought him to Jehu: and when they had slain him, they buried him: Because, said they, he is the son of Jehoshaphat, who sought the Lord with all his heart. So the house of Ahaziah had no power to keep still the kingdom.

- Ahaziah walked after their counsel and went with Jehoram son of Ahab to war against Hazael king of Syria at Ramoth-gilead, where Jehoram was wounded.
- He went down to see the wounded Jehoram at Jezreel, and the destruction of Ahaziah was of the Most High, for by going he came into the path of Jehu, whom the Most High had anointed to cut off the house of Ahab.
- That same Jehu who killed the seventy sons of Ahab found the princes of Judah and the sons of Ahaziah's brethren and slew them, then sought and caught Ahaziah, who was hid in Samaria.
- They buried Ahaziah for the sake of Jehoshaphat his forefather who sought the Most High with all his heart; but the house of Ahaziah had no power to keep the kingdom, so the wicked king was cut off by the same hand the Most High anointed to avenge Israel.

**[2 Chronicles 22:10-12](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v10)</sup> But when Athaliah the mother of Ahaziah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the seed royal of the house of Judah.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v11)</sup> But Jehoshabeath, the daughter of the king, took Joash the son of Ahaziah, and stole him from among the king’s sons that were slain, and put him and his nurse in a bedchamber. So Jehoshabeath, the daughter of king Jehoram, the wife of Jehoiada the priest, (for she was the sister of Ahaziah,) hid him from Athaliah, so that she slew him not.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/22#v12)</sup> And he was with them hid in the house of God six years: and Athaliah reigned over the land.

- When Athaliah saw that her son was dead, she arose and destroyed all the seed royal of the house of Judah, killing the king's sons.
- But Jehoshabeath the daughter of king Jehoram, wife of Jehoiada the priest and sister of Ahaziah, took Joash the son of Ahaziah and stole him from among the king's sons that were slain, hiding him and his nurse in a bedchamber.
- She hid the child in the house of God six years while Athaliah reigned over the land, showing that Athaliah was ruling in the position of the son she had lost.
- Athaliah did not carry out this slaughter alone; she had weak men helping her run over everything, which is how she seized and held the throne.

# 2 Chronicles 23: Jehoiada Anoints Joash and Athaliah Is Slain

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 23](/bible/2-chronicles/23)

>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v2)</sup> And they went about in Judah, and gathered the Levites out of all the cities of Judah, and the chief of the fathers of Israel, and they came to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v3)</sup> And all the congregation made a covenant with the king in the house of God. And he said unto them, Behold, the king’s son shall reign, as the Lord hath said of the sons of David.
>

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v5)</sup> And a third part shall be at the king’s house; and a third part at the gate of the foundation: and all the people shall be in the courts of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v6)</sup> But let none come into the house of the Lord, save the priests, and they that minister of the Levites; they shall go in, for they are holy: but all the people shall keep the watch of the Lord.

- In the seventh year Jehoiada strengthened himself and took the captains of hundreds into covenant with him, and they gathered the Levites and the chief of the fathers out of all the cities of Judah to Jerusalem.
- The congregation made a covenant with the king in the house of God, and Jehoiada declared that the king's son should reign, as the Most High had said of the sons of David.
- He set his plan: a third part of the priests and Levites to keep the doors, a third at the king's house, and a third at the gate of the foundation, with all the people in the courts.
- None but the priests and ministering Levites were to enter the house of the Most High, and the Levites were to compass the king round about, every man with his weapon, and put to death any who forced their way in.

**[2 Chronicles 23:8-11](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v8)</sup> So the Levites and all Judah did according to all things that Jehoiada the priest had commanded, and took every man his men that were to come in on the sabbath, with them that were to go out on the sabbath: for Jehoiada the priest dismissed not the courses.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v9)</sup> Moreover Jehoiada the priest delivered to the captains of hundreds spears, and bucklers, and shields, that had been king David’s, which were in the house of God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v10)</sup> And he set all the people, every man having his weapon in his hand, from the right side of the temple to the left side of the temple, along by the altar and the temple, by the king round about.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v11)</sup> Then they brought out the king’s son, and put upon him the crown, and gave him the testimony, and made him king. And Jehoiada and his sons anointed him, and said, God save the king.

- The Levites and all Judah did according to all Jehoiada commanded, each man keeping his company, for Jehoiada did not dismiss the courses of the Levites but kept them at their work with security added for the king.
- Jehoiada delivered to the captains the spears and shields that had been king David's, kept in the house of God, and set every man with his weapon from one side of the temple to the other, round about the king.
- They brought out the king's son, put the crown upon him, gave him the testimony, and made him king, and Jehoiada and his sons anointed him and said, God save the king.
- This was done by the proper custom: a priest anoints the king, as Samuel the priest anointed Saul and then David; so Jehoiada the priest anointed Joash.

**[2 Chronicles 23:12-15](/bible/2-chronicles/23#v12)**

- She rent her clothes and cried, Treason, treason, but her cry came too late, for the covenant was already made and the true king was crowned.
- Jehoiada commanded the captains to have her forth from the ranges and to slay any that followed her, but not to slay her in the house of the Most High.
- They laid hands on her, brought her to the entering of the horse gate by the king's house, and slew her there, ending the reign of the woman who had killed the seed royal.

# 2 Chronicles 24: Joash Repairs the House and Then Falls

Read the chapter → [2 Chronicles 24](/bible/2-chronicles/24)

**[2 Chronicles 24:1-3](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v1)</sup> Joash was seven years old when he began to reign, and he reigned forty years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Zibiah of Beer–sheba.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v2)</sup> And Joash did that which was right in the sight of the Lord all the days of Jehoiada the priest.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v3)</sup> And Jehoiada took for him two wives; and he begat sons and daughters.

- Joash was seven years old when he began to reign and reigned forty years in Jerusalem, and his mother's name was Zibiah of Beersheba.
- Joash did that which was right in the sight of the Most High all the days of Jehoiada the priest, because that is what he was taught.
- He had seen the wicked men of Israel try to kill him, so he followed the man who showed him the ropes; Jehoiada taught him how to be a righteous king and keep the commandments.
- A king had a job to do and could not simply sit back; he had a kingdom to watch over, and he had the priests there to help him do it.

**[2 Chronicles 24:4-7](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v4)</sup> And it came to pass after this, that Joash was minded to repair the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v5)</sup> And he gathered together the priests and the Levites, and said to them, Go out unto the cities of Judah, and gather of all Israel money to repair the house of your God from year to year, and see that ye hasten the matter. Howbeit the Levites hastened it not.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v6)</sup> And the king called for Jehoiada the chief, and said unto him, Why hast thou not required of the Levites to bring in out of Judah and out of Jerusalem the collection, according to the commandment of Moses the servant of the Lord, and of the congregation of Israel, for the tabernacle of witness?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v7)</sup> For the sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken up the house of God; and also all the dedicated things of the house of the Lord did they bestow upon Baalim.

- Joash was minded to repair the house of the Most High and gathered the priests and Levites, telling them to go to the cities of Judah and gather money of all Israel to repair the house from year to year, and to hasten the matter.
- The Levites did not hasten it, so the king called Jehoiada the chief and asked why he had not required of the Levites the collection appointed by Moses for the tabernacle of witness.
- Money was needed because Israel did not let the workmen labor for nothing; when men did the jobs they were paid, for the workman is worthy of his hire, and we dealt righteously with our people.
- The sons of Athaliah, that wicked woman, had broken up the house of God and bestowed all its dedicated things upon Baalim, which is why so much repair was needed.
