**[2 Chronicles 10:1-4](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v1)</sup> And Rehoboam went to Shechem: for to Shechem were all Israel come to make him king.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, when Jeroboam the son of Nebat, who was in Egypt, whither he had fled from the presence of Solomon the king, heard it, that Jeroboam returned out of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v3)</sup> And they sent and called him. So Jeroboam and all Israel came and spake to Rehoboam, saying,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v4)</sup> Thy father made our yoke grievous: now therefore ease thou somewhat the grievous servitude of thy father, and his heavy yoke that he put upon us, and we will serve thee.

- Rehoboam goes to Shechem where all Israel has gathered to make him king, and Jeroboam the son of Nebat returns out of Egypt where he had fled from Solomon.
- The people, led by Jeroboam, ask Rehoboam to ease the grievous yoke and heavy service his father laid on them, promising then to serve him.
- Rehoboam tells them to come again after three days.

**[2 Chronicles 10:5-7](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v5)</sup> And he said unto them, Come again unto me after three days. And the people departed.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v6)</sup> And king Rehoboam took counsel with the old men that had stood before Solomon his father while he yet lived, saying, What counsel give ye me to return answer to this people?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v7)</sup> And they spake unto him, saying, If thou be kind to this people, and please them, and speak good words to them, they will be thy servants for ever.

- Rehoboam first takes counsel with the old men who stood before Solomon while his father lived, asking what answer to return.
- They advise him that if he is kind to the people, pleases them, and speaks good words, they will be his servants forever.
- This is the wise counsel he should have followed, coming from the elders who had experience in place before him.

**[2 Chronicles 10:8-11](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v8)</sup> But he forsook the counsel which the old men gave him, and took counsel with the young men that were brought up with him, that stood before him.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v9)</sup> And he said unto them, What advice give ye that we may return answer to this people, which have spoken to me, saying, Ease somewhat the yoke that thy father did put upon us?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v10)</sup> And the young men that were brought up with him spake unto him, saying, Thus shalt thou answer the people that spake unto thee, saying, Thy father made our yoke heavy, but make thou it somewhat lighter for us; thus shalt thou say unto them, My little finger shall be thicker than my father’s loins.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v11)</sup> For whereas my father put a heavy yoke upon you, I will put more to your yoke: my father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.

- Rehoboam forsakes the counsel of the old men and turns to the young men brought up with him, including them as if they were rulers with him over the nation.
- This is the same error seen constantly among the people: one comes to the truth and seeks counsel from a new brother or sister rather than from the elder men and women already established in the congregation.
- The young men advise him to answer roughly, saying his little finger will be thicker than his father's loins, that he will add to their yoke, and chastise them with scorpions instead of whips.
  - **[Proverbs 11:14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

    Where no counsel is, the people fall, but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety; Rehoboam rejects the safe counsel of the elders.

**[2 Chronicles 10:12-15](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v12)</sup> So Jeroboam and all the people came to Rehoboam on the third day, as the king bade, saying, Come again to me on the third day.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v13)</sup> And the king answered them roughly; and king Rehoboam forsook the counsel of the old men,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v14)</sup> And answered them after the advice of the young men, saying, My father made your yoke heavy, but I will add thereto: my father chastised you with whips, but I will chastise you with scorpions.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v15)</sup> So the king hearkened not unto the people: for the cause was of God, that the Lord might perform his word, which he spake by the hand of Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam the son of Nebat.

- Jeroboam and the people return on the third day, and the king answers them roughly, forsaking the counsel of the old men and following the young men.
- He tells them he will add to their heavy yoke and chastise them with scorpions.
- The king does not listen to the people, for the cause was of the Most High, that the Lord might fulfill the word spoken by Ahijah the Shilonite to Jeroboam.
  - **[1 Kings 11:9-11](/bible/1-kings/11#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-kings/11#v9)</sup> And the Lord was angry with Solomon, because his heart was turned from the Lord God of Israel, which had appeared unto him twice,
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/11#v10)</sup> And had commanded him concerning this thing, that he should not go after other gods: but he kept not that which the Lord commanded.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-kings/11#v11)</sup> Wherefore the Lord said unto Solomon, Forasmuch as this is done of thee, and thou hast not kept my covenant and my statutes, which I have commanded thee, I will surely rend the kingdom from thee, and will give it to thy servant.

    The Lord, angry that Solomon's heart turned to other gods, declares he will rend the kingdom from him and give it to his servant; this is the prophecy now being fulfilled.

**[2 Chronicles 10:16-19](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v16)</sup> And when all Israel saw that the king would not hearken unto them, the people answered the king, saying, What portion have we in David? and we have none inheritance in the son of Jesse: every man to your tents, O Israel: and now, David, see to thine own house. So all Israel went to their tents.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v17)</sup> But as for the children of Israel that dwelt in the cities of Judah, Rehoboam reigned over them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v18)</sup> Then king Rehoboam sent Hadoram that was over the tribute; and the children of Israel stoned him with stones, that he died. But king Rehoboam made speed to get him up to his chariot, to flee to Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/10#v19)</sup> And Israel rebelled against the house of David unto this day.

- When all Israel sees the king will not listen, they answer that they have no portion in David nor inheritance in the son of Jesse, and every man departs to his tent.
- Rehoboam still reigns over the children of Israel who dwell in the cities of Judah, but when he sends Hadoram over the tribute, Israel stones him to death.
- Rehoboam flees by chariot to Jerusalem, and Israel rebels against the house of David to this day; the same division between the kingdoms persists.
