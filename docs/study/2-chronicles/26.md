**[2 Chronicles 26:1-5](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v1)</sup> Then all the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in the room of his father Amaziah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v2)</sup> He built Eloth, and restored it to Judah, after that the king slept with his fathers.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v3)</sup> Sixteen years old was Uzziah when he began to reign, and he reigned fifty and two years in Jerusalem. His mother’s name also was Jecoliah of Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v4)</sup> And he did that which was right in the sight of the Lord, according to all that his father Amaziah did.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v5)</sup> And he sought God in the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the visions of God: and as long as he sought the Lord, God made him to prosper.

- All the people of Judah took Uzziah, who was sixteen years old, and made him king in place of his father Amaziah. He reigned fifty and two years in Jerusalem and did that which was right in the sight of the Most High.
- He sought the Most High in the days of Zechariah, who had understanding in the visions of God, and as long as he sought the Most High, God made him to prosper. Prosperity comes from the Most High, and it lasts only as long as a man keeps seeking Him.

**[2 Chronicles 26:6-15](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v6)</sup> And he went forth and warred against the Philistines, and brake down the wall of Gath, and the wall of Jabneh, and the wall of Ashdod, and built cities about Ashdod, and among the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v7)</sup> And God helped him against the Philistines, and against the Arabians that dwelt in Gur–baal, and the Mehunims.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v8)</sup> And the Ammonites gave gifts to Uzziah: and his name spread abroad even to the entering in of Egypt; for he strengthened himself exceedingly.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v9)</sup> Moreover Uzziah built towers in Jerusalem at the corner gate, and at the valley gate, and at the turning of the wall, and fortified them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v10)</sup> Also he built towers in the desert, and digged many wells: for he had much cattle, both in the low country, and in the plains: husbandmen also, and vine dressers in the mountains, and in Carmel: for he loved husbandry.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v11)</sup> Moreover Uzziah had an host of fighting men, that went out to war by bands, according to the number of their account by the hand of Jeiel the scribe and Maaseiah the ruler, under the hand of Hananiah, one of the king’s captains.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v12)</sup> The whole number of the chief of the fathers of the mighty men of valour were two thousand and six hundred.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v13)</sup> And under their hand was an army, three hundred thousand and seven thousand and five hundred, that made war with mighty power, to help the king against the enemy.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v14)</sup> And Uzziah prepared for them throughout all the host shields, and spears, and helmets, and habergeons, and bows, and slings to cast stones.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v15)</sup> And he made in Jerusalem engines, invented by cunning men, to be on the towers and upon the bulwarks, to shoot arrows and great stones withal. And his name spread far abroad; for he was marvellously helped, till he was strong.

- Uzziah went to war against the Philistines, broke down walls, and built cities. God helped him against the Philistines, the Arabians, and the Mehunims, and his name spread abroad, for he was marvellously helped till he was strong.
- He built towers in Jerusalem and in the desert, dug many wells, and loved husbandry, keeping much cattle and vine dressers. He had a trained host of fighting men, three hundred thousand and seven thousand five hundred that made war with mighty power, and he prepared for them shields, spears, helmets, and engines of war invented by cunning men to shoot arrows and great stones.
- All of this came because he was helped by the Most High. The strength, the army, the wealth, and even the ingenuity of his engineers were a gift.

**[2 Chronicles 26:16-21](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v16)</sup> But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction: for he transgressed against the Lord his God, and went into the temple of the Lord to burn incense upon the altar of incense.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v17)</sup> And Azariah the priest went in after him, and with him fourscore priests of the Lord, that were valiant men:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v18)</sup> And they withstood Uzziah the king, and said unto him, It appertaineth not unto thee, Uzziah, to burn incense unto the Lord, but to the priests the sons of Aaron, that are consecrated to burn incense: go out of the sanctuary; for thou hast trespassed; neither shall it be for thine honour from the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v19)</sup> Then Uzziah was wroth, and had a censer in his hand to burn incense: and while he was wroth with the priests, the leprosy even rose up in his forehead before the priests in the house of the Lord, from beside the incense altar.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v20)</sup> And Azariah the chief priest, and all the priests, looked upon him, and, behold, he was leprous in his forehead, and they thrust him out from thence; yea, himself hasted also to go out, because the Lord had smitten him.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v21)</sup> And Uzziah the king was a leper unto the day of his death, and dwelt in a several house, being a leper; for he was cut off from the house of the Lord: and Jotham his son was over the king’s house, judging the people of the land.

- But when he was strong, his heart was lifted up to his destruction. He transgressed against the Most High and went into the temple to burn incense on the altar of incense, which was not his office.
- Azariah the priest and fourscore priests went in after him, withstood him, and told him it did not pertain to him to burn incense but to the priests, the sons of Aaron who are consecrated, and told him to go out of the sanctuary for he had trespassed. Uzziah was wroth, and while he was wroth with a censer in his hand, leprosy rose up in his forehead before the priests.
- The lesson to all of us is to stay in the spirit, stay humble, and never forget who put you in the position you are in. When a man becomes lifted up he loses his humility and forgets who set him where he is. Uzziah was a leper to the day of his death and dwelt in a separate house, cut off from the house of the Most High.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 16:18](/bible/proverbs/16#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/16#v18)</sup> Pride goeth before destruction, and an haughty spirit before a fall.

    Pride goeth before destruction; the brother became prideful and high, and it brought him to a fall.
  - **[Deuteronomy 17:18-19](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v18)</sup> And it shall be, when he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom, that he shall write him a copy of this law in a book out of that which is before the priests the Levites:
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v19)</sup> And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life: that he may learn to fear the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them:

    The king was to write himself a copy of the law and read in it all his life, that he learn to fear the Most High and not lift himself above his brethren.
  - **[Numbers 18:7](/bible/numbers/18#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/18#v7)</sup> Therefore thou and thy sons with thee shall keep your priest’s office for every thing of the altar, and within the vail; and ye shall serve: I have given your priest’s office unto you as a service of gift: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

    The priesthood was given to Aaron and his sons as a gift; the stranger that came near was to be put to death, so the office was never the king's to seize.

**[2 Chronicles 26:22-23](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v22)</sup> Now the rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, did Isaiah the prophet, the son of Amoz, write.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-chronicles/26#v23)</sup> So Uzziah slept with his fathers, and they buried him with his fathers in the field of the burial which belonged to the kings; for they said, He is a leper: and Jotham his son reigned in his stead.

- The rest of the acts of Uzziah, first and last, Isaiah the prophet wrote. Uzziah slept with his fathers and they buried him in the field of burial that belonged to the kings, for they said, He is a leper. Jotham his son reigned in his stead.
- When Uzziah stayed with the Most High he prospered, and when he departed and did his own thing, going contrary to the laws of God, the Most High made an example of him. He is a warning against thinking of yourself as more than what you are, for a man who thinks he is something when he is nothing deceives himself.
