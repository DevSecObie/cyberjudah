## Introduction

This session covers the reign of Hezekiah king of Judah, one of the great reformers among the children of Israel. Where his fathers had turned their backs on the Most High, Hezekiah reopens the temple, sanctifies the Levites, keeps the Passover, restores the tithe, and stands against the king of Assyria. The thread running through all four chapters is a man who did that which was right in the sight of the Lord and set the service of the house of God back in order, doing everything with all his heart so that he prospered. Read these chapters as the pattern for how a people returns to the Most High after captivity and forsaking.
