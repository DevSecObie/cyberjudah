**[Song of Solomon 4:1-3](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v1)</sup> Behold, thou art fair, my love; behold, thou art fair; thou hast doves’ eyes within thy locks: thy hair is as a flock of goats, that appear from mount Gilead.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v2)</sup> Thy teeth are like a flock of sheep that are even shorn, which came up from the washing; whereof every one bear twins, and none is barren among them.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v3)</sup> Thy lips are like a thread of scarlet, and thy speech is comely: thy temples are like a piece of a pomegranate within thy locks.

- He praises her as fair, with doves' eyes within her locks, her hair as a flock of goats, her teeth as a flock of sheep even shorn, every one bearing twins.
- Her lips are like a thread of scarlet and her speech comely; her temples are like a piece of a pomegranate within her locks.

**[Song of Solomon 4:4-5](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v4)</sup> Thy neck is like the tower of David builded for an armoury, whereon there hang a thousand bucklers, all shields of mighty men.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v5)</sup> Thy two breasts are like two young roes that are twins, which feed among the lilies.

- Her neck is like the tower of David built for an armory, whereon hang a thousand bucklers, all shields of mighty men.
- Her two breasts are like two young roes that are twins, feeding among the lilies until the day break and the shadows flee away.

**[Song of Solomon 4:6-7](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v6)</sup> Until the day break, and the shadows flee away, I will get me to the mountain of myrrh, and to the hill of frankincense.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v7)</sup> Thou art all fair, my love; there is no spot in thee.

- He will get to the mountain of myrrh and to the hill of frankincense.
- He declares she is all fair, and there is no spot in her.

**[Song of Solomon 4:8-9](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v8)</sup> Come with me from Lebanon, my spouse, with me from Lebanon: look from the top of Amana, from the top of Shenir and Hermon, from the lions’ dens, from the mountains of the leopards.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v9)</sup> Thou hast ravished my heart, my sister, my spouse; thou hast ravished my heart with one of thine eyes, with one chain of thy neck.

- He calls his spouse to come with him from Lebanon, from the top of Amana, Shenir and Hermon, from the lions' dens and the mountains of the leopards.
- She has ravished his heart, his sister, his spouse, with one of her eyes and with one chain of her neck.

**[Song of Solomon 4:10-11](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v10)</sup> How fair is thy love, my sister, my spouse! how much better is thy love than wine! and the smell of thine ointments than all spices!
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v11)</sup> Thy lips, O my spouse, drop as the honeycomb: honey and milk are under thy tongue; and the smell of thy garments is like the smell of Lebanon.

- How fair is her love, better than wine, and the smell of her ointments better than all spices.
- Her lips drop as the honeycomb, honey and milk are under her tongue, and the smell of her garments is like the smell of Lebanon.

**[Song of Solomon 4:12-16](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v12)</sup> A garden inclosed is my sister, my spouse; a spring shut up, a fountain sealed.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v13)</sup> Thy plants are an orchard of pomegranates, with pleasant fruits; camphire, with spikenard,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v14)</sup> Spikenard and saffron; calamus and cinnamon, with all trees of frankincense; myrrh and aloes, with all the chief spices:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v15)</sup> A fountain of gardens, a well of living waters, and streams from Lebanon.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/song-of-solomon/4#v16)</sup> Awake, O north wind; and come, thou south; blow upon my garden, that the spices thereof may flow out. Let my beloved come into his garden, and eat his pleasant fruits.

- She is a garden enclosed, a spring shut up, a fountain sealed; her plants are an orchard of pomegranates with pleasant fruits, camphire, spikenard, saffron, calamus, cinnamon, frankincense, myrrh and aloes.
- She is a fountain of gardens, a well of living waters and streams from Lebanon; she calls the north wind and the south to blow upon her garden and bids her beloved come into his garden and eat his pleasant fruits.
