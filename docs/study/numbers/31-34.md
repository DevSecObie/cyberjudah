## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 31](/bible/numbers/31) through 34: the war of vengeance against Midian and the dividing of the prey, Reuben and Gad receiving their inheritance on this side Jordan, the journeys of Israel from Egypt to the plains of Moab, and the borders and dividers of the land of Canaan. This session is mostly reading with brief exposition; the counsel through the chapters of journeys and borders is to follow along with the maps in the back of the Bible, so the lands and cities being read take on life instead of putting the reader to sleep.

**[Numbers 31:1-2](/bible/numbers/31#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/31#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/31#v2)</sup> Avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites: afterward shalt thou be gathered unto thy people.

- The Most High told Israel to avenge the children of Israel of the Midianites. The reason for this vengeance is the history of [Numbers 25](/bible/numbers/25); go back and read that chapter to see what Midian did.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 25:1-3](/bible/numbers/25#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/25#v1)</sup> And Israel abode in Shittim, and the people began to commit whoredom with the daughters of Moab.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/25#v2)</sup> And they called the people unto the sacrifices of their gods: and the people did eat, and bowed down to their gods.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/25#v3)</sup> And Israel joined himself unto Baal–peor: and the anger of the Lord was kindled against Israel.

    The people began whoredom with the daughters of Moab and were joined unto Baal-peor; that history is the reason the Lord commanded the vengeance.

**[Numbers 31:3-6](/bible/numbers/31#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/31#v3)</sup> And Moses spake unto the people, saying, Arm some of yourselves unto the war, and let them go against the Midianites, and avenge the Lord of Midian.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/31#v4)</sup> Of every tribe a thousand, throughout all the tribes of Israel, shall ye send to the war.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/31#v5)</sup> So there were delivered out of the thousands of Israel, a thousand of every tribe, twelve thousand armed for war.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/31#v6)</sup> And Moses sent them to the war, a thousand of every tribe, them and Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, to the war, with the holy instruments, and the trumpets to blow in his hand.

- A thousand of every tribe, twelve thousand armed for war, were sent with Phinehas the son of Eleazar the priest, with the holy instruments and the trumpets to blow.
- Phinehas is the one who killed the man of Israel that was fornicating with the woman of the other nation, in the history of [Numbers 25](/bible/numbers/25).

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 25:7-8](/bible/numbers/25#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/25#v7)</sup> And when Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose up from among the congregation, and took a javelin in his hand;
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/25#v8)</sup> And he went after the man of Israel into the tent, and thrust both of them through, the man of Israel, and the woman through her belly. So the plague was stayed from the children of Israel.

    Phinehas took a javelin and thrust the man of Israel and the Midianitish woman through, and the plague was stayed.

**[Numbers 31:7-8](/bible/numbers/31#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/31#v7)</sup> And they warred against the Midianites, as the Lord commanded Moses; and they slew all the males.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/31#v8)</sup> And they slew the kings of Midian, beside the rest of them that were slain; namely, Evi, and Rekem, and Zur, and Hur, and Reba, five kings of Midian: Balaam also the son of Beor they slew with the sword.

- They warred against the Midianites as the Lord commanded Moses, slew all the males, and slew the five kings of Midian: Evi, Rekem, Zur, Hur, and Reba.
- Balaam also the son of Beor they slew with the sword. Balaam was a soothsayer, a diviner; he was a witch, so to speak.

  Precepts:
  - **[Joshua 13:22](/bible/joshua/13#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/joshua/13#v22)</sup> Balaam also the son of Beor, the soothsayer, did the children of Israel slay with the sword among them that were slain by them.

    Balaam the son of Beor, the soothsayer, did the children of Israel slay with the sword among them that were slain by them.

**[Numbers 31:9-12](/bible/numbers/31#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/31#v9)</sup> And the children of Israel took all the women of Midian captives, and their little ones, and took the spoil of all their cattle, and all their flocks, and all their goods.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/31#v10)</sup> And they burnt all their cities wherein they dwelt, and all their goodly castles, with fire.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/31#v11)</sup> And they took all the spoil, and all the prey, both of men and of beasts.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/31#v12)</sup> And they brought the captives, and the prey, and the spoil, unto Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and unto the congregation of the children of Israel, unto the camp at the plains of Moab, which are by Jordan near Jericho.

- Israel took the women of Midian captives with their little ones, took the spoil of all their cattle, flocks, and goods, and burnt all their cities and all their goodly castles with fire.
- Midian comes from the lineage of Ishmael, the people known today as the Arab man. This shows Esau was not the one who first came up with castles; the castles seen in the movies were conquered from other nations.
- They took all the spoil and all the prey, both of men and of beasts: the Midianites had captives of their own, and Israel took over both the men and the beasts when they overcame them.

**[Numbers 31:13-18](/bible/numbers/31#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/31#v13)</sup> And Moses, and Eleazar the priest, and all the princes of the congregation, went forth to meet them without the camp.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/31#v14)</sup> And Moses was wroth with the officers of the host, with the captains over thousands, and captains over hundreds, which came from the battle.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/31#v15)</sup> And Moses said unto them, Have ye saved all the women alive?
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/31#v16)</sup> Behold, these caused the children of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to commit trespass against the Lord in the matter of Peor, and there was a plague among the congregation of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/31#v17)</sup> Now therefore kill every male among the little ones, and kill every woman that hath known man by lying with him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/31#v18)</sup> But all the women children, that have not known a man by lying with him, keep alive for yourselves.

- Moses was wroth with the officers of the host, the leaders of the army of Israel, because they saved all the women alive.
- These women caused the children of Israel, through the counsel of Balaam, to commit trespass against the Lord in the matter of Peor. Balaam took it upon himself to send the women of the other nations to Israel, and it caused the people to go into fornication, and the Lord began putting them to death, over twenty and four thousand, in the history of [Numbers 25](/bible/numbers/25).
- Therefore every male among the little ones was to be killed, and every woman that had known man by lying with him; but the women children that had not known a man they kept alive for themselves.
- Moses allowed the men to keep those women so they would not commit sin and go raping the women, being out at war. That does not mean it is done today; it was a time that the Most High allowed it so the men would not be in the midst of sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 25:9](/bible/numbers/25#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/25#v9)</sup> And those that died in the plague were twenty and four thousand.

    Those that died in the plague were twenty and four thousand; that plague in the matter of Peor is why the women who had known man could not be kept.

**[Numbers 31:19-24](/bible/numbers/31#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/31#v19)</sup> And do ye abide without the camp seven days: whosoever hath killed any person, and whosoever hath touched any slain, purify both yourselves and your captives on the third day, and on the seventh day.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/31#v20)</sup> And purify all your raiment, and all that is made of skins, and all work of goats’ hair, and all things made of wood.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/31#v21)</sup> And Eleazar the priest said unto the men of war which went to the battle, This is the ordinance of the law which the Lord commanded Moses;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/31#v22)</sup> Only the gold, and the silver, the brass, the iron, the tin, and the lead,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/31#v23)</sup> Every thing that may abide the fire, ye shall make it go through the fire, and it shall be clean: nevertheless it shall be purified with the water of separation: and all that abideth not the fire ye shall make go through the water.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/31#v24)</sup> And ye shall wash your clothes on the seventh day, and ye shall be clean, and afterward ye shall come into the camp.

- Whoever killed any person or touched any slain had to abide without the camp seven days and purify himself and his captives on the third day and on the seventh day. That is the law going back to [Numbers 5:2](/bible/numbers/5#v2), putting the defiled outside the camp.
- Purifying the raiment, all that is made of skins, all work of goats' hair, and all things made of wood goes back to the law of purification in [Numbers 19](/bible/numbers/19).
- Eleazar the priest gave the ordinance: gold, silver, brass, iron, tin, and lead, everything that may abide the fire, goes through the fire and is purified with the water of separation; whatever abides not the fire goes through the water; and on the seventh day they wash their clothes, are clean, and come into the camp.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 5:2](/bible/numbers/5#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/5#v2)</sup> Command the children of Israel, that they put out of the camp every leper, and every one that hath an issue, and whosoever is defiled by the dead:

    Put out of the camp every one defiled by the dead; the seven days without the camp keep that law.
  - **[Numbers 19:11-12](/bible/numbers/19#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/19#v11)</sup> He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/19#v12)</sup> He shall purify himself with it on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean: but if he purify not himself the third day, then the seventh day he shall not be clean.

    He that touches the dead purifies himself on the third day and on the seventh day; the men of war purified themselves by that law.

**[Numbers 31:25-30](/bible/numbers/31#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/31#v25)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/31#v26)</sup> Take the sum of the prey that was taken, both of man and of beast, thou, and Eleazar the priest, and the chief fathers of the congregation:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/31#v27)</sup> And divide the prey into two parts; between them that took the war upon them, who went out to battle, and between all the congregation:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/31#v28)</sup> And levy a tribute unto the Lord of the men of war which went out to battle: one soul of five hundred, both of the persons, and of the beeves, and of the asses, and of the sheep:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/31#v29)</sup> Take it of their half, and give it unto Eleazar the priest, for an heave offering of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/31#v30)</sup> And of the children of Israel’s half, thou shalt take one portion of fifty, of the persons, of the beeves, of the asses, and of the flocks, of all manner of beasts, and give them unto the Levites, which keep the charge of the tabernacle of the Lord.

- Moses, Eleazar the priest, and the chief fathers of the congregation took the sum of the prey, both of man and of beast, adding up all that was taken from the Midianites.
- The prey was divided into two parts: between the army who went out to battle and took the prey, and all the congregation that did not go to war. The nation looked out for each other.
- A tribute was levied unto the Lord: of the men of war, one soul of five hundred, of persons, beeves, asses, and sheep, given to Eleazar the priest for a heave offering, because he was the priest; and of the children of Israel's half, one portion of fifty, given to the Levites which kept the charge of the tabernacle.

**[Numbers 31:41](/bible/numbers/31#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/numbers/31#v41)</sup> And Moses gave the tribute, which was the Lord’s heave offering, unto Eleazar the priest, as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Moses gave the tribute, the Lord's heave offering, unto Eleazar the priest, as the Lord commanded Moses. The heave offerings belong to the priesthood by statute forever, a covenant of salt, because Aaron and Levi have no inheritance in the land; the Most High is their part and their inheritance.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 18:19](/bible/numbers/18#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/18#v19)</sup> All the heave offerings of the holy things, which the children of Israel offer unto the Lord, have I given thee, and thy sons and thy daughters with thee, by a statute for ever: it is a covenant of salt for ever before the Lord unto thee and to thy seed with thee.

    All the heave offerings of the holy things are given to the priest and his sons by a statute for ever; it is a covenant of salt before the Lord.
  - **[Numbers 18:20](/bible/numbers/18#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/18#v20)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Aaron, Thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither shalt thou have any part among them: I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel.

    Aaron has no inheritance in their land; the Lord is his part and his inheritance among the children of Israel.
  - **[Numbers 18:21](/bible/numbers/18#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/18#v21)</sup> And, behold, I have given the children of Levi all the tenth in Israel for an inheritance, for their service which they serve, even the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.

    The children of Levi are given all the tenth in Israel for an inheritance, for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.

# Numbers 32: The Inheritance of Reuben and Gad on This Side Jordan

- They did not want to go forward any more; they were ready to set up shop before they crossed over Jordan, and they had not even finished the mission.

**[Numbers 32:6-9](/bible/numbers/32#v6)**

- Wherefore discourage ye the heart of the children of Israel from going over into the land which the Lord hath given them: thus did your fathers when they were sent from Kadesh-barnea to see the land. That goes back into the history of the spies in [Numbers 13](/bible/numbers/13); the fathers were not even in the land yet and were already getting comfortable.

# Numbers 33: The Journeys of Israel from Egypt to the Plains of Moab

Read the chapter → [Numbers 33](/bible/numbers/33)

**[Numbers 33:1-4](/bible/numbers/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/33#v1)</sup> These are the journeys of the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt with their armies under the hand of Moses and Aaron.

> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/33#v2)</sup> And Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the Lord: and these are their journeys according to their goings out.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/33#v3)</sup> And they departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the children of Israel went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians.
>

- These are the journeys of the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt, and Moses wrote their goings out by the commandment of the Lord.
- They departed from Rameses on the fifteenth day of the first month, on the morrow after the passover, with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians, while the Egyptians buried all their firstborn, which the Lord had smitten among them: upon their gods also the Lord executed judgments; he put them to death.

**[Numbers 33:8-11](/bible/numbers/33#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/33#v8)</sup> And they departed from before Pi–hahiroth, and passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness, and went three days’ journey in the wilderness of Etham, and pitched in Marah.

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/33#v10)</sup> And they removed from Elim, and encamped by the Red sea.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/33#v11)</sup> And they removed from the Red sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.

- They passed through the midst of the sea into the wilderness, pitched in Marah, came to Elim where there were twelve fountains of water and threescore and ten palm trees, encamped by the Red sea, and encamped in the wilderness of Sin.
- Follow these journeys with the maps in the back of the Bible; most Bibles have them. Seeing the wilderness of Sin, the Red sea, and the route they were taking gives a better understanding of the lands and cities being read.

**[Numbers 33:16-17](/bible/numbers/33#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/33#v16)</sup> And they removed from the desert of Sinai, and pitched at Kibroth–hattaavah.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/33#v17)</sup> And they departed from Kibroth–hattaavah, and encamped at Hazeroth.

- They would walk a little and then set up camp wherever the Lord wanted them to stop. They were led by the cloud by day and the light by night; the light gave them sight at night, and when they saw it stop, they knew to pitch camp right there.

**[Numbers 33:37-39](/bible/numbers/33#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/numbers/33#v37)</sup> And they removed from Kadesh, and pitched in mount Hor, in the edge of the land of Edom.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/33#v38)</sup> And Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of the Lord, and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the first day of the fifth month.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/33#v39)</sup> And Aaron was an hundred and twenty and three years old when he died in mount Hor.

- They pitched in mount Hor, in the edge of the land of Edom; the map shows the land of Edom there.
- Aaron the priest went up into mount Hor at the commandment of the Lord and died there, in the fortieth year after the children of Israel were come out of the land of Egypt, in the first day of the fifth month; and Aaron was an hundred and twenty and three years old when he died. The number is the fortieth year; read it carefully.

**[Numbers 33:44-49](/bible/numbers/33#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/numbers/33#v44)</sup> And they departed from Oboth, and pitched in Ije–abarim, in the border of Moab.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/numbers/33#v45)</sup> And they departed from Iim, and pitched in Dibon–gad.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/numbers/33#v46)</sup> And they removed from Dibon–gad, and encamped in Almon–diblathaim.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/numbers/33#v47)</sup> And they removed from Almon–diblathaim, and pitched in the mountains of Abarim, before Nebo.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/numbers/33#v48)</sup> And they departed from the mountains of Abarim, and pitched in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/numbers/33#v49)</sup> And they pitched by Jordan, from Beth–jesimoth even unto Abel–shittim in the plains of Moab.

- They pitched in Ije-abarim, in the border of Moab; on the map Moab sits right above the land of Edom.
- They departed from the mountains of Abarim and pitched in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho; so they ended in the midst of the land of Moab, where the camp now sits.

**[Numbers 33:50-53](/bible/numbers/33#v50)**

> <sup>[50](/bible/numbers/33#v50)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho, saying,
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/numbers/33#v51)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye are passed over Jordan into the land of Canaan;
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/numbers/33#v52)</sup> Then ye shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, and destroy all their pictures, and destroy all their molten images, and quite pluck down all their high places:
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/numbers/33#v53)</sup> And ye shall dispossess the inhabitants of the land, and dwell therein: for I have given you the land to possess it.

- When ye are passed over Jordan into the land of Canaan, ye shall drive out all the inhabitants of the land from before you, destroy all their pictures and all their molten images, and quite pluck down all their high places: all the idols go.
- Dispossess the inhabitants of the land and dwell therein, for the Most High has given the land to possess it.

**[Numbers 33:54](/bible/numbers/33#v54)**

> <sup>[54](/bible/numbers/33#v54)</sup> And ye shall divide the land by lot for an inheritance among your families: and to the more ye shall give the more inheritance, and to the fewer ye shall give the less inheritance: every man’s inheritance shall be in the place where his lot falleth; according to the tribes of your fathers ye shall inherit.

- The land was to be divided by lot for an inheritance among the families, to the more the more inheritance and to the fewer the less, every man's inheritance in the place where his lot falleth, according to the tribes of the fathers. The map of the tribal lands, Simeon, Judah, Benjamin, Reuben and the rest with their cities, is the product of the tribes following what the Most High said to do.

**[Numbers 33:55-56](/bible/numbers/33#v55)**

> <sup>[55](/bible/numbers/33#v55)</sup> But if ye will not drive out the inhabitants of the land from before you; then it shall come to pass, that those which ye let remain of them shall be pricks in your eyes, and thorns in your sides, and shall vex you in the land wherein ye dwell.
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/numbers/33#v56)</sup> Moreover it shall come to pass, that I shall do unto you, as I thought to do unto them.

- But if ye will not drive out the inhabitants of the land, those which ye let remain shall be pricks in your eyes and thorns in your sides, and shall vex you in the land wherein ye dwell.
- They are going to be problems, because they are not following the Most High as Israel follows; if you allow them to stay in your land they will always cause problems.

  Precepts:
  - **[Joshua 23:13](/bible/joshua/23#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/23#v13)</sup> Know for a certainty that the Lord your God will no more drive out any of these nations from before you; but they shall be snares and traps unto you, and scourges in your sides, and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off this good land which the Lord your God hath given you.

    The nations left remaining become snares and traps, scourges in your sides and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off the good land.

# Numbers 34: The Borders of the Land of Canaan

Read the chapter → [Numbers 34](/bible/numbers/34)

**[Numbers 34:1-5](/bible/numbers/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/34#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/34#v2)</sup> Command the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land of Canaan; (this is the land that shall fall unto you for an inheritance, even the land of Canaan with the coasts thereof:)
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/34#v3)</sup> Then your south quarter shall be from the wilderness of Zin along by the coast of Edom, and your south border shall be the outmost coast of the salt sea eastward:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/34#v4)</sup> And your border shall turn from the south to the ascent of Akrabbim, and pass on to Zin: and the going forth thereof shall be from the south to Kadesh–barnea, and shall go on to Hazar–addar, and pass on to Azmon:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/34#v5)</sup> And the border shall fetch a compass from Azmon unto the river of Egypt, and the goings out of it shall be at the sea.

- This is the land of Canaan that falls to Israel for inheritance, with the coasts thereof; the south quarter runs from the wilderness of Zin along by the coast of Edom, the south border from the outmost coast of the salt sea eastward, fetching a compass unto the river of Egypt and out at the sea.
- This is going into how far the land stretched. When reading these borders, look at the map and visualize how the land is stretched, border by border.

**[Numbers 34:6-12](/bible/numbers/34#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/34#v6)</sup> And as for the western border, ye shall even have the great sea for a border: this shall be your west border.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/34#v7)</sup> And this shall be your north border: from the great sea ye shall point out for you mount Hor:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/34#v8)</sup> From mount Hor ye shall point out your border unto the entrance of Hamath; and the goings forth of the border shall be to Zedad:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/34#v9)</sup> And the border shall go on to Ziphron, and the goings out of it shall be at Hazar–enan: this shall be your north border.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/34#v10)</sup> And ye shall point out your east border from Hazar–enan to Shepham:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/34#v11)</sup> And the coast shall go down from Shepham to Riblah, on the east side of Ain; and the border shall descend, and shall reach unto the side of the sea of Chinnereth eastward:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/34#v12)</sup> And the border shall go down to Jordan, and the goings out of it shall be at the salt sea: this shall be your land with the coasts thereof round about.

- The western border is the great sea; the north border runs from the great sea to mount Hor and the entrance of Hamath out to Hazar-enan; the east border goes down from Shepham to Riblah, reaches unto the side of the sea of Chinnereth eastward, and goes down Jordan out at the salt sea.

**[Numbers 34:13-15](/bible/numbers/34#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/34#v13)</sup> And Moses commanded the children of Israel, saying, This is the land which ye shall inherit by lot, which the Lord commanded to give unto the nine tribes, and to the half tribe:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/34#v14)</sup> For the tribe of the children of Reuben according to the house of their fathers, and the tribe of the children of Gad according to the house of their fathers, have received their inheritance; and half the tribe of Manasseh have received their inheritance:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/34#v15)</sup> The two tribes and the half tribe have received their inheritance on this side Jordan near Jericho eastward, toward the sunrising.

- Moses commanded that this is the land to be inherited by lot, which the Lord commanded to give unto the nine tribes and to the half tribe; for Reuben, Gad, and the half tribe of Manasseh, the son of Joseph, had already received their inheritance on this side Jordan near Jericho eastward.
- Levi did not get an inheritance of land, because Levi was scattered throughout all the other tribes' lands to do the service of the Lord.
