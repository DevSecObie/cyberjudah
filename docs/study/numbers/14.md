**[Numbers 14:1-4](/bible/numbers/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/14#v1)</sup> And all the congregation lifted up their voice, and cried; and the people wept that night.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/14#v2)</sup> And all the children of Israel murmured against Moses and against Aaron: and the whole congregation said unto them, Would God that we had died in the land of Egypt! or would God we had died in this wilderness!
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/14#v3)</sup> And wherefore hath the Lord brought us unto this land, to fall by the sword, that our wives and our children should be a prey? were it not better for us to return into Egypt?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/14#v4)</sup> And they said one to another, Let us make a captain, and let us return into Egypt.

- All the congregation lifted up their voice and cried, and the people wept that night, and murmured against Moses and Aaron, saying, Would God that we had died in the land of Egypt, or in this wilderness.
- Because of the evil report, they said, Wherefore hath the LORD brought us unto this land, to fall by the sword? and they said one to another, Let us make a captain, and let us return into Egypt, forgetting there was nothing to return to, for Pharaoh and his army were destroyed and Egypt laid waste.

**[Numbers 14:5-10](/bible/numbers/14#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/14#v5)</sup> Then Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before all the assembly of the congregation of the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/14#v6)</sup> And Joshua the son of Nun, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, which were of them that searched the land, rent their clothes:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/14#v7)</sup> And they spake unto all the company of the children of Israel, saying, The land, which we passed through to search it, is an exceeding good land.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/14#v8)</sup> If the Lord delight in us, then he will bring us into this land, and give it us; a land which floweth with milk and honey.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/14#v9)</sup> Only rebel not ye against the Lord, neither fear ye the people of the land; for they are bread for us: their defence is departed from them, and the Lord is with us: fear them not.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/14#v10)</sup> But all the congregation bade stone them with stones. And the glory of the Lord appeared in the tabernacle of the congregation before all the children of Israel.

- Moses and Aaron fell on their faces before all the assembly, and Joshua and Caleb rent their clothes and exhorted the people: the land is an exceeding good land; if the Lord delight in us, He will bring us into it; only rebel not against the Lord, neither fear the people of the land, for they are bread for us, and the Lord is with us.
- But all the congregation bade stone them with stones, and the glory of the Lord appeared in the tabernacle. It is the same today: when the truth is brought that the Most High loves the people and has something great for them if they repent, they do not believe it and are ready to kill the messenger, hating him that rebuketh in the gate.

**[Numbers 14:11-19](/bible/numbers/14#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/14#v11)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, How long will this people provoke me? and how long will it be ere they believe me, for all the signs which I have shewed among them?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/14#v12)</sup> I will smite them with the pestilence, and disinherit them, and will make of thee a greater nation and mightier than they.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/14#v13)</sup> And Moses said unto the Lord, Then the Egyptians shall hear it, (for thou broughtest up this people in thy might from among them;)
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/14#v14)</sup> And they will tell it to the inhabitants of this land: for they have heard that thou Lord art among this people, that thou Lord art seen face to face, and that thy cloud standeth over them, and that thou goest before them, by day time in a pillar of a cloud, and in a pillar of fire by night.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/14#v15)</sup> Now if thou shalt kill all this people as one man, then the nations which have heard the fame of thee will speak, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/14#v16)</sup> Because the Lord was not able to bring this people into the land which he sware unto them, therefore he hath slain them in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/14#v17)</sup> And now, I beseech thee, let the power of my Lord be great, according as thou hast spoken, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/14#v18)</sup> The Lord is longsuffering, and of great mercy, forgiving iniquity and transgression, and by no means clearing the guilty, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/14#v19)</sup> Pardon, I beseech thee, the iniquity of this people according unto the greatness of thy mercy, and as thou hast forgiven this people, from Egypt even until now.

- The Lord said, How long will this people provoke me? and how long will it be ere they believe me, for all the signs which I have shewed among them? He would smite them with pestilence, disinherit them, and make of Moses a greater nation.
- Moses interceded: the Egyptians and the nations would hear that the Lord was not able to bring the people into the land and slew them in the wilderness. He pleaded, Let the power of my Lord be great, the Lord is longsuffering and of great mercy, forgiving iniquity; pardon this people according to the greatness of thy mercy, as thou hast forgiven them from Egypt until now. This is a true leader interceding for the people rather than seeing them destroyed.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 18:18](/bible/matthew/18#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/18#v18)</sup> Verily I say unto you, Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven: and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven.

    Whatsoever ye shall bind on earth shall be bound in heaven, and whatsoever ye shall loose on earth shall be loosed in heaven; Moses' intercession loosed the judgment, and the Lord pardoned according to his word.

**[Numbers 14:20-25](/bible/numbers/14#v20)**

>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/14#v21)</sup> But as truly as I live, all the earth shall be filled with the glory of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/14#v22)</sup> Because all those men which have seen my glory, and my miracles, which I did in Egypt and in the wilderness, and have tempted me now these ten times, and have not hearkened to my voice;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/14#v23)</sup> Surely they shall not see the land which I sware unto their fathers, neither shall any of them that provoked me see it:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/14#v24)</sup> But my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully, him will I bring into the land whereinto he went; and his seed shall possess it.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/14#v25)</sup> (Now the Amalekites and the Canaanites dwelt in the valley.) To morrow turn you, and get you into the wilderness by the way of the Red sea.

- The Lord said, I have pardoned according to thy word; but as truly as I live, all the earth shall be filled with the glory of the Lord. The men who had seen His glory and His miracles in Egypt and in the wilderness, and had tempted Him now these ten times, would not see the land which He sware unto their fathers.
- But His servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him and followed the Lord fully, would be brought into the land, and his seed should possess it.

**[Numbers 14:26-38](/bible/numbers/14#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/14#v26)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/14#v27)</sup> How long shall I bear with this evil congregation, which murmur against me? I have heard the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmur against me.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/14#v28)</sup> Say unto them, As truly as I live, saith the Lord, as ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you:
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/14#v29)</sup> Your carcases shall fall in this wilderness; and all that were numbered of you, according to your whole number, from twenty years old and upward, which have murmured against me,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/14#v30)</sup> Doubtless ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I sware to make you dwell therein, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/14#v31)</sup> But your little ones, which ye said should be a prey, them will I bring in, and they shall know the land which ye have despised.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/14#v32)</sup> But as for you, your carcases, they shall fall in this wilderness.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/14#v33)</sup> And your children shall wander in the wilderness forty years, and bear your whoredoms, until your carcases be wasted in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/14#v34)</sup> After the number of the days in which ye searched the land, even forty days, each day for a year, shall ye bear your iniquities, even forty years, and ye shall know my breach of promise.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/numbers/14#v35)</sup> I the Lord have said, I will surely do it unto all this evil congregation, that are gathered together against me: in this wilderness they shall be consumed, and there they shall die.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/numbers/14#v36)</sup> And the men, which Moses sent to search the land, who returned, and made all the congregation to murmur against him, by bringing up a slander upon the land,
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/numbers/14#v37)</sup> Even those men that did bring up the evil report upon the land, died by the plague before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/14#v38)</sup> But Joshua the son of Nun, and Caleb the son of Jephunneh, which were of the men that went to search the land, lived still.

- The Lord asked how long He should bear with this evil congregation which murmured against Him, and declared, As ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you: your carcasses shall fall in this wilderness, all that were numbered from twenty years old and upward that murmured against Him, save Caleb and Joshua.
- The little ones they said would be a prey, those He would bring into the land; but the children would wander in the wilderness forty years, a year for each of the forty days the land was searched, and bear the whoredoms of their fathers until their carcasses were wasted. The very men that brought up the evil report died by the plague before the Lord, but Joshua and Caleb lived still.

**[Numbers 14:39-45](/bible/numbers/14#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/14#v39)</sup> And Moses told these sayings unto all the children of Israel: and the people mourned greatly.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/numbers/14#v40)</sup> And they rose up early in the morning, and gat them up into the top of the mountain, saying, Lo, we be here, and will go up unto the place which the Lord hath promised: for we have sinned.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/numbers/14#v41)</sup> And Moses said, Wherefore now do ye transgress the commandment of the Lord? but it shall not prosper.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/numbers/14#v42)</sup> Go not up, for the Lord is not among you; that ye be not smitten before your enemies.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/numbers/14#v43)</sup> For the Amalekites and the Canaanites are there before you, and ye shall fall by the sword: because ye are turned away from the Lord, therefore the Lord will not be with you.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/numbers/14#v44)</sup> But they presumed to go up unto the hill top: nevertheless the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and Moses, departed not out of the camp.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/numbers/14#v45)</sup> Then the Amalekites came down, and the Canaanites which dwelt in that hill, and smote them, and discomfited them, even unto Hormah.

- When Moses told the people these sayings, they mourned greatly, then rose up early and presumed to go up to the top of the mountain, saying, We have sinned; we will go up unto the place which the Lord hath promised.
- Moses said, Wherefore now do ye transgress the commandment of the LORD? it shall not prosper; go not up, for the LORD is not among you, that ye be not smitten before your enemies. But they presumed to go up, though the ark of the covenant and Moses departed not out of the camp, and the Amalekites and the Canaanites came down and smote them and discomfited them, even unto Hormah.
- All they had to do was follow counsel. Their refusal was written for us, that we would learn to follow the counsel of the men the Most High sets up rather than lean on our own presumption.
