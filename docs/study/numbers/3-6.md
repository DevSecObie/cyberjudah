## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 3](/bible/numbers/3) through 6: the Levites taken for the Most High instead of all the firstborn of Israel, the service and burdens of the sons of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari, the law of putting the defiled out of the camp and the law of jealousies, and the law of the Nazarite with the blessing of the children of Israel. The session picks up at [Numbers 3:11](/bible/numbers/3#v11) after the reading of the earlier verses was interrupted; the recap of the stranger teaching from that portion is kept under [Numbers 3:10](/bible/numbers/3#v10). The word to remember through the whole reading is order: everything in the camp of Israel was set in order, and the Most High required it on pain of death.

**[Numbers 3:10](/bible/numbers/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/3#v10)</sup> And thou shalt appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall wait on their priest’s office: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

- Aaron and his sons were appointed to wait on the priest's office, and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death. Anybody outside the tribe of Levi, when it came to doing the service of the Lord, was considered a stranger.

**[Numbers 3:11-13](/bible/numbers/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/3#v11)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/3#v12)</sup> And I, behold, I have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn that openeth the matrix among the children of Israel: therefore the Levites shall be mine;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/3#v13)</sup> Because all the firstborn are mine; for on the day that I smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt I hallowed unto me all the firstborn in Israel, both man and beast: mine shall they be: I am the Lord.

- The Most High took the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn that openeth the matrix. The matrix is the womb; that is what the Lord calls it.
- All the firstborn are his from the day he smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt. Verse 13 proves the Most High does not love everybody; he killed all the firstborn of Egypt.

**[Numbers 3:14-16](/bible/numbers/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/3#v14)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/3#v15)</sup> Number the children of Levi after the house of their fathers, by their families: every male from a month old and upward shalt thou number them.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/3#v16)</sup> And Moses numbered them according to the word of the Lord, as he was commanded.

- The children of Levi were numbered after the houses of their fathers, by their families. Your lineage is determined by your father, not your mother.
- The claim that identity passes through the mother is nowhere in the Bible; number the children of Levi after the house of their fathers is the standard of scripture.

- Of Gershon were the families of the Libnites and the Shimites, numbered 7,500 males from a month old and upward, and they pitched behind the tabernacle westward. Depending on your family lineage you had a specific side of the tabernacle that you and all your descendants had to be on.
- The charge of the sons of Gershon was the tabernacle and the tent, the covering thereof, the hanging for the door, the hangings of the court, and the cords of it; that was their responsibility.

> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/3#v27)</sup> And of Kohath was the family of the Amramites, and the family of the Izeharites, and the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites: these are the families of the Kohathites.

> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/3#v30)</sup> And the chief of the house of the father of the families of the Kohathites shall be Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.

- The ending "ites" denotes the people of; the Kohathites are the people of Kohath. They numbered 8,600 and pitched on the side of the tabernacle southward.
- Their charge was the ark, the table, the candlestick, the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary. Only they were responsible for the ark; anybody outside of that suffered the judgment of the Most High, as Uzzah did generations later when he put forth his hand to the ark and God smote him on the spot.

# Numbers 4: The Service of the Tabernacle Done in Order

Read the chapter → [Numbers 4](/bible/numbers/4)

**[Numbers 4:1-3](/bible/numbers/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/4#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/4#v2)</sup> Take the sum of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, after their families, by the house of their fathers,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/4#v3)</sup> From thirty years old and upward even until fifty years old, all that enter into the host, to do the work in the tabernacle of the congregation.

- The sons of Kohath were numbered from thirty years old and upward even unto fifty years old, all that enter into the host to do the work in the tabernacle of the congregation. You took on that responsibility at thirty years old.
- Fifty was basically the age of retirement from the service; the law elsewhere shows the elder then sat down to be a judge amongst his brethren.

**[Numbers 4:4-6](/bible/numbers/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/4#v4)</sup> This shall be the service of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the congregation, about the most holy things:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/4#v5)</sup> And when the camp setteth forward, Aaron shall come, and his sons, and they shall take down the covering vail, and cover the ark of testimony with it:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/4#v6)</sup> And shall put thereon the covering of badgers’ skins, and shall spread over it a cloth wholly of blue, and shall put in the staves thereof.

- This is the service of the sons of Kohath about the most holy things. When the camp set forward, Aaron and his sons took down the covering vail and covered the ark of the testimony with it, with the covering of badgers' skins and a cloth wholly of blue.
- The design of the ark and the tabernacle, the pure gold dishes, spoons, and vessels, was laid out back in [Exodus 35](/bible/exodus/35) and 36; review it there.

**[Numbers 4:7-14](/bible/numbers/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/4#v7)</sup> And upon the table of shewbread they shall spread a cloth of blue, and put thereon the dishes, and the spoons, and the bowls, and covers to cover withal: and the continual bread shall be thereon:

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/4#v8)</sup> And they shall spread upon them a cloth of scarlet, and cover the same with a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put in the staves thereof.

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/4#v9)</sup> And they shall take a cloth of blue, and cover the candlestick of the light, and his lamps, and his tongs, and his snuffdishes, and all the oil vessels thereof, wherewith they minister unto it:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/4#v10)</sup> And they shall put it and all the vessels thereof within a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put it upon a bar.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/4#v11)</sup> And upon the golden altar they shall spread a cloth of blue, and cover it with a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put to the staves thereof:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/4#v12)</sup> And they shall take all the instruments of ministry, wherewith they minister in the sanctuary, and put them in a cloth of blue, and cover them with a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put them on a bar:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/4#v13)</sup> And they shall take away the ashes from the altar, and spread a purple cloth thereon:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/4#v14)</sup> And they shall put upon it all the vessels thereof, wherewith they minister about it, even the censers, the fleshhooks, and the shovels, and the basons, all the vessels of the altar; and they shall spread upon it a covering of badgers’ skins, and put to the staves of it.

- The table of shewbread, the candlestick, the golden altar, and all the instruments of ministry were each wrapped in cloths of blue, scarlet, or purple and covered with badgers' skins before they were put on the staves and bars.
- Badger skin is something we would consider unclean today, yet the Lord had his holy things covered in it. Under the law of sacrifice, defilement came by touching unclean things to eat them; touching them to make something for the tabernacle still left you unclean until sundown, unable to sacrifice, but the skins themselves served the sanctuary.

**[Numbers 4:15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)</sup> And when Aaron and his sons have made an end of covering the sanctuary, and all the vessels of the sanctuary, as the camp is to set forward; after that, the sons of Kohath shall come to bear it: but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die. These things are the burden of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the congregation.

- After Aaron and his sons made an end of covering the sanctuary, the sons of Kohath came to bear it, but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die. The Most High is very particular about the most holy things.
- The Lord referred to all the tribes together as the camp. If you are not in a camp you are out of order; people avoid camps because a camp carries a certain level of order and subjection, and they want to do their own thing, which is to sit in the midst of sin.
- All twelve tribes are not gathered together right now, but we are still commanded to rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, and that takes gathering; the body must move in one unison, in one direction, as a camp.

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/4#v17)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,

- It was a heavy responsibility; your life was on the line and you could not be out of order. One slip, one curious peek at the shiny gold before it was covered, and God put you to death on the spot. You had to be serious about your job and follow all instructions.
- That is the God we are dealing with; that is why Christ came, to give us mercy and grace from that thing.

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/6#v18)</sup> And the Nazarite shall shave the head of his separation at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and shall take the hair of the head of his separation, and put it in the fire which is under the sacrifice of the peace offerings.

> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/6#v19)</sup> And the priest shall take the sodden shoulder of the ram, and one unleavened cake out of the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and shall put them upon the hands of the Nazarite, after the hair of his separation is shaven:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/6#v20)</sup> And the priest shall wave them for a wave offering before the Lord: this is holy for the priest, with the wave breast and heave shoulder: and after that the Nazarite may drink wine.

- When the days of his separation were fulfilled, the Nazarite offered a he lamb without blemish for a burnt offering and a ewe lamb without blemish for a sin offering, with unleavened bread and the meat and drink offerings. Without blemish is the same thing going into Christ.
- He shaved the head of his separation at the door of the tabernacle and put the hair in the fire under the sacrifice of the peace offerings; the priest put the sodden shoulder and the cakes on his hands and waved them for a wave offering, which we covered in Exodus: the priest picks it up and waves it before the Lord.
- After that the Nazarite may drink wine; before that he could not. In our understanding today you cannot keep the vow of a Nazarite under Christ: you can let your hair grow as long as you want, but when we break bread and take the cup in remembrance of Christ, renewing that covenant, you have to drink that wine to be in covenant with him.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 11:23-25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v23)</sup> For I have received of the Lord that which also I delivered unto you, That the Lord Jesus the same night in which he was betrayed took bread:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v24)</sup> And when he had given thanks, he brake it, and said, Take, eat: this is my body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v25)</sup> After the same manner also he took the cup, when he had supped, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.

    Take, eat, this is my body; this cup is the new testament in my blood, this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me. The cup of the covenant is why the Nazarite vow cannot be kept under Christ.

**[Numbers 6:22-27](/bible/numbers/6#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/6#v22)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/6#v23)</sup> Speak unto Aaron and unto his sons, saying, On this wise ye shall bless the children of Israel, saying unto them,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/6#v24)</sup> The Lord bless thee, and keep thee:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/6#v25)</sup> The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/6#v26)</sup> The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/6#v27)</sup> And they shall put my name upon the children of Israel; and I will bless them.

- On this wise Aaron and his sons blessed the children of Israel: the Lord bless thee and keep thee, the Lord make his face shine upon thee and be gracious unto thee, the Lord lift up his countenance upon thee and give thee peace; and they shall put my name upon the children of Israel, and I will bless them.
- All of that is a stipulation, a contract; it is not unconditional love with the Most High. Keep my commandments and live.
- When the southern kingdom asked wherefore the Lord pronounced evil against them, the answer was because your fathers forsook me and walked after other gods, and ye have done worse than your fathers. Bring it up to today: Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, every denomination walking after the imagination of its own evil heart, cast out into a land we knew not, serving gods where the Lord shows no favor. That is why our people have no peace and no blessing.
- The Most High has taken that blessing from us, and we have to work to get it back by repentance and enduring to the end. But it will happen again; the Lord is pulling his spirit back out on us, and the remnant is waking up.
