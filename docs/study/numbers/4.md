**[Numbers 4:1-3](/bible/numbers/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/4#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/4#v2)</sup> Take the sum of the sons of Kohath from among the sons of Levi, after their families, by the house of their fathers,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/4#v3)</sup> From thirty years old and upward even until fifty years old, all that enter into the host, to do the work in the tabernacle of the congregation.

- The sons of Kohath were numbered from thirty years old and upward even unto fifty years old, all that enter into the host to do the work in the tabernacle of the congregation. You took on that responsibility at thirty years old.
- Fifty was basically the age of retirement from the service; the law elsewhere shows the elder then sat down to be a judge amongst his brethren.

**[Numbers 4:4-6](/bible/numbers/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/4#v4)</sup> This shall be the service of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the congregation, about the most holy things:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/4#v5)</sup> And when the camp setteth forward, Aaron shall come, and his sons, and they shall take down the covering vail, and cover the ark of testimony with it:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/4#v6)</sup> And shall put thereon the covering of badgers’ skins, and shall spread over it a cloth wholly of blue, and shall put in the staves thereof.

- This is the service of the sons of Kohath about the most holy things. When the camp set forward, Aaron and his sons took down the covering vail and covered the ark of the testimony with it, with the covering of badgers' skins and a cloth wholly of blue.
- The design of the ark and the tabernacle, the pure gold dishes, spoons, and vessels, was laid out back in [Exodus 35](/bible/exodus/35) and 36; review it there.

**[Numbers 4:7-14](/bible/numbers/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/4#v7)</sup> And upon the table of shewbread they shall spread a cloth of blue, and put thereon the dishes, and the spoons, and the bowls, and covers to cover withal: and the continual bread shall be thereon:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/4#v8)</sup> And they shall spread upon them a cloth of scarlet, and cover the same with a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put in the staves thereof.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/4#v9)</sup> And they shall take a cloth of blue, and cover the candlestick of the light, and his lamps, and his tongs, and his snuffdishes, and all the oil vessels thereof, wherewith they minister unto it:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/4#v10)</sup> And they shall put it and all the vessels thereof within a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put it upon a bar.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/4#v11)</sup> And upon the golden altar they shall spread a cloth of blue, and cover it with a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put to the staves thereof:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/4#v12)</sup> And they shall take all the instruments of ministry, wherewith they minister in the sanctuary, and put them in a cloth of blue, and cover them with a covering of badgers’ skins, and shall put them on a bar:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/4#v13)</sup> And they shall take away the ashes from the altar, and spread a purple cloth thereon:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/4#v14)</sup> And they shall put upon it all the vessels thereof, wherewith they minister about it, even the censers, the fleshhooks, and the shovels, and the basons, all the vessels of the altar; and they shall spread upon it a covering of badgers’ skins, and put to the staves of it.

- The table of shewbread, the candlestick, the golden altar, and all the instruments of ministry were each wrapped in cloths of blue, scarlet, or purple and covered with badgers' skins before they were put on the staves and bars.
- Badger skin is something we would consider unclean today, yet the Lord had his holy things covered in it. Under the law of sacrifice, defilement came by touching unclean things to eat them; touching them to make something for the tabernacle still left you unclean until sundown, unable to sacrifice, but the skins themselves served the sanctuary.

**[Numbers 4:15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)</sup> And when Aaron and his sons have made an end of covering the sanctuary, and all the vessels of the sanctuary, as the camp is to set forward; after that, the sons of Kohath shall come to bear it: but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die. These things are the burden of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the congregation.

- After Aaron and his sons made an end of covering the sanctuary, the sons of Kohath came to bear it, but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die. The Most High is very particular about the most holy things.
- The Lord referred to all the tribes together as the camp. If you are not in a camp you are out of order; people avoid camps because a camp carries a certain level of order and subjection, and they want to do their own thing, which is to sit in the midst of sin.
- All twelve tribes are not gathered together right now, but we are still commanded to rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord, and that takes gathering; the body must move in one unison, in one direction, as a camp.

  - **[Judges 5:11](/bible/judges/5#v11)**

    They that are delivered shall rehearse the righteous acts of the Lord in the places of drawing water; that duty stands even before all twelve tribes are gathered.
  - **[1 Corinthians 1:10](/bible/1-corinthians/1#v10)**

    Speak the same thing, no divisions, perfectly joined together in the same mind and the same judgment; that is the camp moving in one direction.

**[Numbers 4:16](/bible/numbers/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/4#v16)</sup> And to the office of Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest pertaineth the oil for the light, and the sweet incense, and the daily meat offering, and the anointing oil, and the oversight of all the tabernacle, and of all that therein is, in the sanctuary, and in the vessels thereof.

- To the office of Eleazar pertained the oil for the light, the sweet incense, the daily meat offering, the anointing oil, and the oversight of all the tabernacle. The burning of the sweet incense stayed in that priestly line: Zacharias, of the course of Abia, descended from the line of the Kohathites, and his lot was to burn incense in the temple of the Lord.
- [Luke 1:6](/bible/luke/1#v6) is the New Testament precept for what it means to be righteous: walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless. Highlight that and commit it to memory.
- The son of Zacharias was John the Baptist, a Levite; and John the Baptist was Elijah back on the earth again, come to do what was prophesied of him. There is no reincarnation, coming back as a bird or such; there is regeneration, and we come back over and over again.

    Zacharias of the course of Abia was righteous, walking in all the commandments and ordinances of the Lord blameless, and his lot was to burn incense in the temple; he served in the Kohathite charge.
  - **[Matthew 17:12-13](/bible/matthew/17#v12)**

**[Numbers 4:17-20](/bible/numbers/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/4#v17)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/4#v18)</sup> Cut ye not off the tribe of the families of the Kohathites from among the Levites:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/4#v19)</sup> But thus do unto them, that they may live, and not die, when they approach unto the most holy things: Aaron and his sons shall go in, and appoint them every one to his service and to his burden:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/4#v20)</sup> But they shall not go in to see when the holy things are covered, lest they die.

- The Most High commanded that the families of the Kohathites not be cut off: Aaron and his sons appointed every one to his service and to his burden, but they shall not go in to see when the holy things are covered, lest they die.
- It was a heavy responsibility; your life was on the line and you could not be out of order. One slip, one curious peek at the shiny gold before it was covered, and God put you to death on the spot. You had to be serious about your job and follow all instructions.
- That is the God we are dealing with; that is why Christ came, to give us mercy and grace from that thing.

**[Numbers 4:21-28](/bible/numbers/4#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/4#v21)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/4#v22)</sup> Take also the sum of the sons of Gershon, throughout the houses of their fathers, by their families;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/4#v23)</sup> From thirty years old and upward until fifty years old shalt thou number them; all that enter in to perform the service, to do the work in the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/4#v24)</sup> This is the service of the families of the Gershonites, to serve, and for burdens:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/4#v25)</sup> And they shall bear the curtains of the tabernacle, and the tabernacle of the congregation, his covering, and the covering of the badgers’ skins that is above upon it, and the hanging for the door of the tabernacle of the congregation,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/4#v26)</sup> And the hangings of the court, and the hanging for the door of the gate of the court, which is by the tabernacle and by the altar round about, and their cords, and all the instruments of their service, and all that is made for them: so shall they serve.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/4#v27)</sup> At the appointment of Aaron and his sons shall be all the service of the sons of the Gershonites, in all their burdens, and in all their service: and ye shall appoint unto them in charge all their burdens.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/4#v28)</sup> This is the service of the families of the sons of Gershon in the tabernacle of the congregation: and their charge shall be under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.

- The sons of Gershon were numbered from thirty to fifty years old for their service: bearing the curtains of the tabernacle, the coverings of badgers' skins, the hangings of the court and the doors, the cords, and all the instruments of their service.
- Their charge was under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest. Ithamar had to make sure the priests had covered all the most holy things before the Gershonites came in to bear them; he was responsible for the lives of all the Gershonites that served, and he had to be in order.

>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/4#v30)</sup> From thirty years old and upward even unto fifty years old shalt thou number them, every one that entereth into the service, to do the work of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/4#v31)</sup> And this is the charge of their burden, according to all their service in the tabernacle of the congregation; the boards of the tabernacle, and the bars thereof, and the pillars thereof, and sockets thereof,
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/4#v32)</sup> And the pillars of the court round about, and their sockets, and their pins, and their cords, with all their instruments, and with all their service: and by name ye shall reckon the instruments of the charge of their burden.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/4#v33)</sup> This is the service of the families of the sons of Merari, according to all their service, in the tabernacle of the congregation, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.

- The sons of Merari were numbered from thirty to fifty years old, charged with the boards, bars, pillars, sockets, pins, and cords of the tabernacle and the court, reckoned by name, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron.
- Only Eleazar and Ithamar were left to oversee, because Nadab and Abihu were killed by the Lord for offering strange fire; there were only two sons of Aaron remaining.

- The numbered for service were 2,750 Kohathites, 2,630 Gershonites, and 3,200 Merarites; the total of those from thirty to fifty years old that entered the service of the tabernacle was 8,580, eight thousand and five hundred and fourscore.
- The Lord constantly repeats himself through these chapters to make sure you stay in order and do not go out of order, because going out of order cost your life. That is why you read the same thing in the Bible over and over again.
