## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 19](/bible/numbers/19) through 21: the water of separation made with the ashes of the red heifer, the strife at the water of Meribah, Edom refusing Israel passage, the death of Aaron, the fiery serpents and the serpent of brass lifted up on a pole, and Israel's journeys toward the border of Moab. The recording opens at [Numbers 19:10](/bible/numbers/19#v10) and cuts off during [Numbers 21:18](/bible/numbers/21#v18), so the earlier verses of chapter 19, the rest of chapter 21, and all of [Numbers 22](/bible/numbers/22) were read without exposition captured in this session; only the taught portions are included here.

**[Numbers 19:10](/bible/numbers/19#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/19#v10)</sup> And he that gathereth the ashes of the heifer shall wash his clothes, and be unclean until the even: and it shall be unto the children of Israel, and unto the stranger that sojourneth among them, for a statute for ever.

- After the priest burns the red heifer and sprinkles her blood, one that is clean gathers up her ashes; even that clean man must wash his clothes and be unclean until the evening.
- This is a statute for ever, unto the children of Israel and unto the stranger that sojourneth among them.

**[Numbers 19:11](/bible/numbers/19#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/19#v11)</sup> He that toucheth the dead body of any man shall be unclean seven days.

- Any man that touches the dead body of any man is unclean seven days. There is history on this law: in [Numbers 9](/bible/numbers/9) certain men were defiled by a dead body around the passover and could not keep it on that day because of this very statute.
- The Most High answered through Moses with a second keeping: the man unclean by reason of a dead body, or far off on a journey, keeps the passover the fourteenth day of the second month at even, eating it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 9:6-7](/bible/numbers/9#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/9#v6)</sup> And there were certain men, who were defiled by the dead body of a man, that they could not keep the passover on that day: and they came before Moses and before Aaron on that day:
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/9#v7)</sup> And those men said unto him, We are defiled by the dead body of a man: wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer an offering of the Lord in his appointed season among the children of Israel?

    Certain men defiled by the dead body of a man could not keep the passover that day; they asked Moses wherefore they were kept back.
  - **[Numbers 9:10-11](/bible/numbers/9#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/9#v10)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If any man of you or of your posterity shall be unclean by reason of a dead body, or be in a journey afar off, yet he shall keep the passover unto the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/9#v11)</sup> The fourteenth day of the second month at even they shall keep it, and eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.

    The unclean by a dead body or far off on a journey keep the passover the fourteenth day of the second month with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.

**[Numbers 19:12-13](/bible/numbers/19#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/19#v12)</sup> He shall purify himself with it on the third day, and on the seventh day he shall be clean: but if he purify not himself the third day, then the seventh day he shall not be clean.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/19#v13)</sup> Whosoever toucheth the dead body of any man that is dead, and purifieth not himself, defileth the tabernacle of the Lord; and that soul shall be cut off from Israel: because the water of separation was not sprinkled upon him, he shall be unclean; his uncleanness is yet upon him.

- The unclean man purifies himself with the water of separation on the third day and on the seventh day, and then he is clean; if he purifies not himself the third day, then the seventh day he shall not be clean.
- Whoever touches the dead and purifies not himself defiles the tabernacle of the Most High, and that soul is cut off from Israel, because the water of separation was not sprinkled upon him; his uncleanness is yet upon him.

**[Numbers 19:14-22](/bible/numbers/19#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/19#v14)</sup> This is the law, when a man dieth in a tent: all that come into the tent, and all that is in the tent, shall be unclean seven days.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/19#v15)</sup> And every open vessel, which hath no covering bound upon it, is unclean.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/19#v16)</sup> And whosoever toucheth one that is slain with a sword in the open fields, or a dead body, or a bone of a man, or a grave, shall be unclean seven days.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/19#v17)</sup> And for an unclean person they shall take of the ashes of the burnt heifer of purification for sin, and running water shall be put thereto in a vessel:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/19#v18)</sup> And a clean person shall take hyssop, and dip it in the water, and sprinkle it upon the tent, and upon all the vessels, and upon the persons that were there, and upon him that touched a bone, or one slain, or one dead, or a grave:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/19#v19)</sup> And the clean person shall sprinkle upon the unclean on the third day, and on the seventh day: and on the seventh day he shall purify himself, and wash his clothes, and bathe himself in water, and shall be clean at even.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/19#v20)</sup> But the man that shall be unclean, and shall not purify himself, that soul shall be cut off from among the congregation, because he hath defiled the sanctuary of the Lord: the water of separation hath not been sprinkled upon him; he is unclean.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/19#v21)</sup> And it shall be a perpetual statute unto them, that he that sprinkleth the water of separation shall wash his clothes; and he that toucheth the water of separation shall be unclean until even.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/19#v22)</sup> And whatsoever the unclean person toucheth shall be unclean; and the soul that toucheth it shall be unclean until even.

- This is the law when a man dies in a tent: all that come into the tent and all that is in the tent are unclean seven days, and every open vessel with no covering bound upon it is unclean.
- For the unclean person they take of the ashes of the burnt heifer of purification for sin, with running water in a vessel; a clean person dips hyssop in the water and sprinkles the tent, the vessels, and the persons on the third day and on the seventh day, and on the seventh day he washes his clothes, bathes in water, and is clean at even.
- The man that stays unclean and will not purify himself is cut off from among the congregation, because he has defiled the sanctuary of the Lord.
- Chapter 19 as a whole gives the water of separation, which is the purification for sin: the first half gives the specifics of the sacrifice and what the priest must do, and the rest gives the examples of how a man becomes unclean, whether by sin, by touching the dead, or by being around the dead.

# Numbers 20: Strife at Meribah and Edom's Refusal

Read the chapter → [Numbers 20](/bible/numbers/20)

**[Numbers 20:1](/bible/numbers/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/20#v1)</sup> Then came the children of Israel, even the whole congregation, into the desert of Zin in the first month: and the people abode in Kadesh; and Miriam died there, and was buried there.

- The whole congregation came into the desert of Zin in the first month and abode in Kadesh, and Miriam died there and was buried there.

**[Numbers 20:2-5](/bible/numbers/20#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/20#v2)</sup> And there was no water for the congregation: and they gathered themselves together against Moses and against Aaron.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/20#v3)</sup> And the people chode with Moses, and spake, saying, Would God that we had died when our brethren died before the Lord!
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/20#v4)</sup> And why have ye brought up the congregation of the Lord into this wilderness, that we and our cattle should die there?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/20#v5)</sup> And wherefore have ye made us to come up out of Egypt, to bring us in unto this evil place? it is no place of seed, or of figs, or of vines, or of pomegranates; neither is there any water to drink.

- There was no water for the congregation, and the people gathered together against Moses and against Aaron; this is another account of Israel coming against them.
- When it says the people chode with Moses, they raised their voices; it was loud, angry, a shouting argument: would God that we had died when our brethren died; why have ye brought us into this evil place with no seed, figs, vines, pomegranates, or water to drink.
- This is us, the blacks and Hispanics. We were taken out of Egypt by the hand of the Most High, headed to the promised land, and we forgot so quickly how we were brought out; anything out of the ordinary, we complained about it. That constant complaining is one of the marks that this book is speaking of this people.
- Moses had to deal with all twelve tribes at once, twelve personalities and twelve attitudes; give that brother all the honor he deserves.

**[Numbers 20:6-11](/bible/numbers/20#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/20#v6)</sup> And Moses and Aaron went from the presence of the assembly unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and they fell upon their faces: and the glory of the Lord appeared unto them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/20#v7)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/20#v8)</sup> Take the rod, and gather thou the assembly together, thou, and Aaron thy brother, and speak ye unto the rock before their eyes; and it shall give forth his water, and thou shalt bring forth to them water out of the rock: so thou shalt give the congregation and their beasts drink.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/20#v9)</sup> And Moses took the rod from before the Lord, as he commanded him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/20#v10)</sup> And Moses and Aaron gathered the congregation together before the rock, and he said unto them, Hear now, ye rebels; must we fetch you water out of this rock?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/20#v11)</sup> And Moses lifted up his hand, and with his rod he smote the rock twice: and the water came out abundantly, and the congregation drank, and their beasts also.

- Moses and Aaron fell upon their faces at the door of the tabernacle, and the glory of the Lord appeared. The command was to take the rod and speak unto the rock before their eyes, and it would give forth his water for the congregation and their beasts.
- At this point Moses got out of the spirit; a stomach can only take so much of a rebellious people. He said, Hear now, ye rebels; must we fetch you water out of this rock? That we is how you know Moses was off: he forgot who was giving the water. It was the Lord giving it, not Moses and not Aaron.
- Moses lifted up his hand and smote the rock twice with his rod, and the water still came out abundantly, and the congregation drank, and their beasts also.

**[Numbers 20:12-13](/bible/numbers/20#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/20#v12)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and Aaron, Because ye believed me not, to sanctify me in the eyes of the children of Israel, therefore ye shall not bring this congregation into the land which I have given them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/20#v13)</sup> This is the water of Meribah; because the children of Israel strove with the Lord, and he was sanctified in them.

- Because ye believed me not, to sanctify me in the eyes of the children of Israel: for this Moses and Aaron would not bring the congregation into the land given them.
- This is the water of Meribah, which means strife, because the children of Israel strove with the Lord, and he was sanctified in them.

**[Numbers 20:14-17](/bible/numbers/20#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/20#v14)</sup> And Moses sent messengers from Kadesh unto the king of Edom, Thus saith thy brother Israel, Thou knowest all the travail that hath befallen us:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/20#v15)</sup> How our fathers went down into Egypt, and we have dwelt in Egypt a long time; and the Egyptians vexed us, and our fathers:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/20#v16)</sup> And when we cried unto the Lord, he heard our voice, and sent an angel, and hath brought us forth out of Egypt: and, behold, we are in Kadesh, a city in the uttermost of thy border:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/20#v17)</sup> Let us pass, I pray thee, through thy country: we will not pass through the fields, or through the vineyards, neither will we drink of the water of the wells: we will go by the king’s high way, we will not turn to the right hand nor to the left, until we have passed thy borders.

- Moses sent messengers to the king of Edom, saying, Thus saith thy brother Israel. Israel rehearsed the travail: our fathers went down into Egypt, the Egyptians vexed us, we cried unto the Lord and he heard our voice and sent an angel and brought us forth.
- Edom is identified in this teaching with the people called the white man today, the Caucasians. This is Israel pleading with the brother nation: not sneaking in, not hiding, not touching the fields, the vineyards, or the water of the wells; only the king's highway, turning neither to the right hand nor to the left, until past the borders, to reach the promised land.

**[Numbers 20:18-21](/bible/numbers/20#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/20#v18)</sup> And Edom said unto him, Thou shalt not pass by me, lest I come out against thee with the sword.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/20#v19)</sup> And the children of Israel said unto him, We will go by the high way: and if I and my cattle drink of thy water, then I will pay for it: I will only, without doing any thing else, go through on my feet.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/20#v20)</sup> And he said, Thou shalt not go through. And Edom came out against him with much people, and with a strong hand.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/20#v21)</sup> Thus Edom refused to give Israel passage through his border: wherefore Israel turned away from him.

- Edom answered, Thou shalt not pass by me, lest I come out against thee with the sword. Israel stayed humble: we will go by the highway, and if I and my cattle drink of thy water, then I will pay for it; I will only go through on my feet.
- Edom still refused and came out against Israel with much people and with a strong hand, and Israel turned away from him.
- Why so harsh against a people asking only to pass through? That hatred stands from something: Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him, and purposed in his heart to slay his brother. [Genesis 25](/bible/genesis/25) through 27 is the start of the two nations, and Israel was given the birthright and the kingdom while Esau was rejected.
- The hatred is perpetual, everlasting, never ending. The forefather's hatred showed in [Numbers 20](/bible/numbers/20) and it shows to this day in the nation of Edom still rejecting Israel; it will not stop until Christ stops it, and Israel's part is to repent and come back to the lost statutes and commandments.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 27:41](/bible/genesis/27#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/genesis/27#v41)</sup> And Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him: and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning for my father are at hand; then will I slay my brother Jacob.

    Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and said in his heart, then will I slay my brother Jacob; the refusal in Numbers 20 goes back to this.
  - **[Ezekiel 35:5](/bible/ezekiel/35#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/35#v5)</sup> Because thou hast had a perpetual hatred, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel by the force of the sword in the time of their calamity, in the time that their iniquity had an end:

    Thou hast had a perpetual hatred, and hast shed the blood of the children of Israel; perpetual means everlasting, never ending.

**[Numbers 20:22-29](/bible/numbers/20#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/20#v22)</sup> And the children of Israel, even the whole congregation, journeyed from Kadesh, and came unto mount Hor.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/20#v23)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and Aaron in mount Hor, by the coast of the land of Edom, saying,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/20#v24)</sup> Aaron shall be gathered unto his people: for he shall not enter into the land which I have given unto the children of Israel, because ye rebelled against my word at the water of Meribah.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/20#v25)</sup> Take Aaron and Eleazar his son, and bring them up unto mount Hor:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/20#v26)</sup> And strip Aaron of his garments, and put them upon Eleazar his son: and Aaron shall be gathered unto his people, and shall die there.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/20#v27)</sup> And Moses did as the Lord commanded: and they went up into mount Hor in the sight of all the congregation.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/20#v28)</sup> And Moses stripped Aaron of his garments, and put them upon Eleazar his son; and Aaron died there in the top of the mount: and Moses and Eleazar came down from the mount.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/20#v29)</sup> And when all the congregation saw that Aaron was dead, they mourned for Aaron thirty days, even all the house of Israel.

- At mount Hor, by the coast of the land of Edom, the Lord said Aaron shall be gathered unto his people; he could not enter the land because of the rebellion at the water of Meribah.
- Moses stripped Aaron of his garments and put them upon Eleazar his son; the priesthood passed to Eleazar, and Aaron died there in the top of the mount.
- When all the congregation saw that Aaron was dead, all the house of Israel mourned for Aaron thirty days.

# Numbers 21: The Fiery Serpents and the Serpent of Brass

Read the chapter → [Numbers 21](/bible/numbers/21)

**[Numbers 21:1-3](/bible/numbers/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/21#v1)</sup> And when king Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt in the south, heard tell that Israel came by the way of the spies; then he fought against Israel, and took some of them prisoners.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/21#v2)</sup> And Israel vowed a vow unto the Lord, and said, If thou wilt indeed deliver this people into my hand, then I will utterly destroy their cities.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/21#v3)</sup> And the Lord hearkened to the voice of Israel, and delivered up the Canaanites; and they utterly destroyed them and their cities: and he called the name of the place Hormah.

- King Arad the Canaanite, which dwelt in the south, fought against Israel and took some of them prisoners. Israel vowed a vow unto the Lord, and the Lord hearkened and delivered up the Canaanites, and Israel utterly destroyed them and their cities.
- The Canaanites are identified in this teaching with the people called Africans today. The place was called Hormah, which means utter destruction, because that is exactly what Israel did to them there.

**[Numbers 21:4-5](/bible/numbers/21#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/21#v4)</sup> And they journeyed from mount Hor by the way of the Red sea, to compass the land of Edom: and the soul of the people was much discouraged because of the way.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/21#v5)</sup> And the people spake against God, and against Moses, Wherefore have ye brought us up out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? for there is no bread, neither is there any water; and our soul loatheth this light bread.

- They journeyed from mount Hor by the way of the Red Sea to compass the land of Edom, and the soul of the people was much discouraged because of the way: since Edom refused passage, Israel had to go around, and the way was longer than they wanted it to be.
- The people spake against God and against Moses: no bread, no water, and our soul loatheth this light bread. The bread they were loathing was angels' bread, and they complained about it anyway.
- No other nation on this earth takes food as seriously as Israel; even the complaints over bread and water are a familiar mark of this people to this day. You cannot put another nation in these scriptures.

**[Numbers 21:6-9](/bible/numbers/21#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/21#v6)</sup> And the Lord sent fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people; and much people of Israel died.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/21#v7)</sup> Therefore the people came to Moses, and said, We have sinned, for we have spoken against the Lord, and against thee; pray unto the Lord, that he take away the serpents from us. And Moses prayed for the people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/21#v8)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Make thee a fiery serpent, and set it upon a pole: and it shall come to pass, that every one that is bitten, when he looketh upon it, shall live.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/21#v9)</sup> And Moses made a serpent of brass, and put it upon a pole, and it came to pass, that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived.

- The Lord sent fiery serpents among the people, and they bit the people, and much people of Israel died. Read it with the nation in view: the people being bitten, the people dying, all of it is Israel.
- Once the serpents started biting and much people died, the people came back into the spirit: we have sinned, for we have spoken against the Lord and against thee; pray unto the Lord that he take away the serpents from us. And Moses prayed for the people.
- Moses made a serpent of brass and set it upon a pole, and it came to pass that if a serpent had bitten any man, when he beheld the serpent of brass, he lived. The symbol on ambulances today, a snake wrapped around a pole, comes from this scripture.
- That brass serpent on the pole was for the Israelites, to heal the Israelites, because it was the Israelites being bitten for rebelling against God and against Moses.
- Christ brought this same passage to Nicodemus. Nicodemus was a Pharisee, a ruler of the Jews, and he came to Christ, who is also a Jew: two Israelites speaking together about the history of Israel.
- As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness to save Israel from death, even so must the Son of man be lifted up: the same way Israel beheld the serpent and did not perish, whosoever believes on Christ shall not perish but have eternal life. And the whosoever is defined in Acts: whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved; ye men of Israel, hear these words. The salvation spoken of is for Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 3:1-2](/bible/john/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/john/3#v1)</sup> There was a man of the Pharisees, named Nicodemus, a ruler of the Jews:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/john/3#v2)</sup> The same came to Jesus by night, and said unto him, Rabbi, we know that thou art a teacher come from God: for no man can do these miracles that thou doest, except God be with him.

    Nicodemus, a Pharisee, a ruler of the Jews, came to Christ by night; an Israelite came to an Israelite.
  - **[John 3:14-15](/bible/john/3#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/john/3#v14)</sup> And as Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/john/3#v15)</sup> That whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have eternal life.

    As Moses lifted up the serpent in the wilderness, even so must the Son of man be lifted up, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish.
  - **[Acts 2:21-22](/bible/acts/2#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/acts/2#v21)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/acts/2#v22)</sup> Ye men of Israel, hear these words; Jesus of Nazareth, a man approved of God among you by miracles and wonders and signs, which God did by him in the midst of you, as ye yourselves also know:

    Whosoever shall call on the name of the Lord shall be saved; ye men of Israel, hear these words. The whosoever is spoken to Israel.

**[Numbers 21:10-15](/bible/numbers/21#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/21#v10)</sup> And the children of Israel set forward, and pitched in Oboth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/21#v11)</sup> And they journeyed from Oboth, and pitched at Ije–abarim, in the wilderness which is before Moab, toward the sunrising.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/21#v12)</sup> From thence they removed, and pitched in the valley of Zared.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/21#v13)</sup> From thence they removed, and pitched on the other side of Arnon, which is in the wilderness that cometh out of the coasts of the Amorites: for Arnon is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/21#v14)</sup> Wherefore it is said in the book of the wars of the Lord, What he did in the Red sea, and in the brooks of Arnon,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/21#v15)</sup> And at the stream of the brooks that goeth down to the dwelling of Ar, and lieth upon the border of Moab.

- The children of Israel set forward and pitched in Oboth, then in Ijeabarim, in the wilderness before Moab toward the sunrising, then in the valley of Zared, then on the other side of Arnon, which is the border of Moab, between Moab and the Amorites.
- Now Israel comes among the Moabites and the Ammonites, identified in this teaching with the nations called the Chinese and the Japanese today.
- It is said in the book of the wars of the Lord what he did in the Red Sea and in the brooks of Arnon.

**[Numbers 21:16-18](/bible/numbers/21#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/21#v16)</sup> And from thence they went to Beer: that is the well whereof the Lord spake unto Moses, Gather the people together, and I will give them water.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/21#v17)</sup> Then Israel sang this song, Spring up, O well; sing ye unto it:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/21#v18)</sup> The princes digged the well, the nobles of the people digged it, by the direction of the lawgiver, with their staves. And from the wilderness they went to Mattanah:

- From thence they went to Beer: that is the well whereof the Lord spake unto Moses, Gather the people together, and I will give them water.
- Then Israel sang this song: Spring up, O well. The princes digged the well, the nobles of the people digged it, by the direction of the lawgiver. The recording ends here, during this passage.
