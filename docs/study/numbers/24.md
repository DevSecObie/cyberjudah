**[Numbers 24:1-2](/bible/numbers/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/24#v1)</sup> And when Balaam saw that it pleased the Lord to bless Israel, he went not, as at other times, to seek for enchantments, but he set his face toward the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/24#v2)</sup> And Balaam lifted up his eyes, and he saw Israel abiding in his tents according to their tribes; and the spirit of God came upon him.

- When Balaam saw that it pleased the Lord to bless Israel, he went not to seek enchantments, and the spirit of God came upon him. Do not be confused at the spirit of God coming on a man of another nation: all the spirits belong to the Most High, and He can do what He wants with any of them.
- That does not make the other nations prophets. Prophets are raised up of the sons of Israel; the Lord used Balaam for one specific purpose, to show the nations there is nothing they can do against Israel when the Lord is on our side, and to have Moses write it down in a book.

  Precepts:

    I raised up of your sons for prophets; the prophets come out of the children of Israel, not the nations.

**[Numbers 24:3-4](/bible/numbers/24#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/24#v3)</sup> And he took up his parable, and said, Balaam the son of Beor hath said, and the man whose eyes are open hath said:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/24#v4)</sup> He hath said, which heard the words of God, which saw the vision of the Almighty, falling into a trance, but having his eyes open:

- Balaam heard the words of God and saw the vision of the Almighty, falling into a trance, but having his eyes open. Picture a man staring off as if daydreaming: he is seeing visions with his eyes open while the Lord gives him what to say to the nations that are trying to do evil to Israel.

The water is the word: Christ sanctifies and cleanses the church with the washing of water by the word, and a young man cleanses his way by taking heed thereto according to the word. That is how you cleanse your spirit.
- His seed shall be in many waters means Israel scattered all over the earth, and his kingdom shall be exalted higher than Agag.

**[Numbers 24:10-11](/bible/numbers/24#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/24#v10)</sup> And Balak’s anger was kindled against Balaam, and he smote his hands together: and Balak said unto Balaam, I called thee to curse mine enemies, and, behold, thou hast altogether blessed them these three times.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/24#v11)</sup> Therefore now flee thou to thy place: I thought to promote thee unto great honour; but, lo, the Lord hath kept thee back from honour.

- Balak's anger was kindled and he smote his hands together: I thought to promote thee unto great honour, but lo, the Lord hath kept thee back from honour. That was Satan speaking through Balak, trying to entice Balaam with honor to curse Israel.
- Satan promotes with honor, riches, prestige, and lust; that is the deceitfulness of riches and the lust of other things that choke the word. When the word is sown, Satan comes immediately to take it away, using whatever device fits where you are.
- He tried the same device on Christ, offering all the power and the glory of the kingdoms in exchange for worship, and Christ answered, Get thee behind me, Satan. Satan is an old spirit using the same tricks from the beginning of time.
- We are not to be ignorant of his devices, lest he get an advantage of us. Many are falling to those devices at home in this quarantine, letting the old man rise back up; resist the devil and he will flee, but give in and he will use you until he destroys you.

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/24#v14)</sup> And now, behold, I go unto my people: come therefore, and I will advertise thee what this people shall do to thy people in the latter days.

- Behold, I go unto my people: Balaam's people were the Midianites, for Israel later slew Balaam with the sword beside the five kings of Midian.
- I will advertise thee what this people shall do to thy people in the latter days. The latter days are the last days, and the last days began when Christ came on the earth, was crucified, and resurrected: God, who spake in time past unto the fathers by the prophets, hath in these last days spoken unto us by His Son.
- A thousand years is as a day to the Lord, and a day as a thousand years; as Hosea says, after two days He will revive us, and in the third day He will raise us up. The full teaching of that timing belongs to another class.

**[Numbers 24:17](/bible/numbers/24#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/24#v17)</sup> I shall see him, but not now: I shall behold him, but not nigh: there shall come a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel, and shall smite the corners of Moab, and destroy all the children of Sheth.

- I shall see him, but not now: I shall behold him, but not nigh. Balaam is saying the Most High will raise him up again in the last days, but as a servant, far off from the King; he will behold him, but he will not be near him.
- Compare that with Job, and the contrast shows Job was an Israelite and not of another nation: Job knew that his redeemer liveth, that in his flesh he shall see God, whom he shall see for himself and his eyes shall behold, and not another. Balaam has no such nearness, because the redeemer is not his.
- There shall come a Star out of Jacob: the Star is Christ, the root and offspring of David, the bright and morning star. And a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel: the sceptre belongs to kings, so the prophecy is that the King shall rise out of Israel.

Moab shall be as Sodom and the children of Ammon as Gomorrah, the breeding of nettles and salt pits; the stinging nettle is a plant that burns, and the fire that fell on Sodom is coming again.

That is why Balaam said he shall behold Him, but not nigh: the nations will worship from their own place and come up only at the appointed times of the year.

**[Numbers 24:18-19](/bible/numbers/24#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/24#v18)</sup> And Edom shall be a possession, Seir also shall be a possession for his enemies; and Israel shall do valiantly.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/24#v19)</sup> Out of Jacob shall come he that shall have dominion, and shall destroy him that remaineth of the city.

- And Edom shall be a possession. Edom is nowhere named in the matter of Balak and Balaam in these chapters, but the Lord made sure Edom was written into the prophecy: Seir also shall be a possession for his enemies, and Israel shall do valiantly. The law of bondmen shows what a possession is: the heathen round about are bought for an inheritance, bondmen for ever, though over our own brethren we do not rule with rigour.
- For Esau that servitude is not for ever, because Esau is the only nation on the earth with an expiration date. The house of Jacob, the twelve tribes of Israel, the blacks, Hispanics, and Native Indians, shall be a fire, the house of Joseph a flame, and the house of Esau for stubble; there shall not be any remaining of the house of Esau, for the Lord hath spoken it. The rest of the nations go into servitude for ever, but the Lord can only bear with Esau so much longer.
- Out of Jacob shall come he that shall have dominion: that is the one who cometh from Edom with dyed garments from Bozrah, glorious in his apparel, traveling in the greatness of his strength, which is the whole host of heaven, thousands upon thousands of angels. The day of vengeance is in his heart, for though Israel serves out her punishment in captivity, Esau went overboard and kept laying on punishment when our iniquity had an end.

**[Numbers 24:23-25](/bible/numbers/24#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/24#v23)</sup> And he took up his parable, and said, Alas, who shall live when God doeth this!

- Alas, who shall live when God doeth this! Ships shall come from the coast of Chittim, and Chittim is Italy; they shall afflict Asshur and afflict Eber, and he also shall perish for ever. Eber here is not speaking of Israel, for other peoples besides us came out of Eber, who is of Shem; the history of prophecy opens this up as the prophets are read.
- And Balaam rose up and returned to his place, and Balak also went his way; they parted.
