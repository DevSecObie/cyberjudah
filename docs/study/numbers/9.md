**[Numbers 9:1-5](/bible/numbers/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/9#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the first month of the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/9#v2)</sup> Let the children of Israel also keep the passover at his appointed season.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/9#v3)</sup> In the fourteenth day of this month, at even, ye shall keep it in his appointed season: according to all the rites of it, and according to all the ceremonies thereof, shall ye keep it.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/9#v4)</sup> And Moses spake unto the children of Israel, that they should keep the passover.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/9#v5)</sup> And they kept the passover on the fourteenth day of the first month at even in the wilderness of Sinai: according to all that the Lord commanded Moses, so did the children of Israel.

- In the first month of the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, the Lord commanded that the children of Israel keep the passover at his appointed season, the fourteenth day of the month at even; and they kept it in the wilderness of Sinai, according to all that the Lord commanded Moses.
- The passover carried its rites and its ceremonies: there were things they had to sacrifice and do according to all the ordinances of it, to keep everything in order for the congregation.

**[Numbers 9:6-8](/bible/numbers/9#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/9#v6)</sup> And there were certain men, who were defiled by the dead body of a man, that they could not keep the passover on that day: and they came before Moses and before Aaron on that day:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/9#v7)</sup> And those men said unto him, We are defiled by the dead body of a man: wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer an offering of the Lord in his appointed season among the children of Israel?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/9#v8)</sup> And Moses said unto them, Stand still, and I will hear what the Lord will command concerning you.

- Certain men were defiled by the dead body of a man, so they could not keep the passover on that day. The law had already put out of the camp whosoever was defiled by the dead, so they could not come in with the congregation to make their offering.
- They asked, wherefore are we kept back, that we may not offer an offering of the Lord in his appointed season? And Moses said, stand still, and I will hear what the Lord will command concerning you.

    >

    Put out of the camp every leper, every one that hath an issue, and whosoever is defiled by the dead; that is why those men could not come in.

**[Numbers 9:9-12](/bible/numbers/9#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/9#v9)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/9#v10)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If any man of you or of your posterity shall be unclean by reason of a dead body, or be in a journey afar off, yet he shall keep the passover unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/9#v11)</sup> The fourteenth day of the second month at even they shall keep it, and eat it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/9#v12)</sup> They shall leave none of it unto the morning, nor break any bone of it: according to all the ordinances of the passover they shall keep it.

- The answer came: if any man be unclean by reason of a dead body, or be in a journey afar off, he shall still keep the passover unto the Lord, on the fourteenth day of the second month at even, eating it with unleavened bread and bitter herbs.
- Defilement by the dead and traveling afar off were the only two reasons given to miss the appointed day, and even then the passover was kept the following month, not neglected.
- The same holds today: many drive trucks and work jobs they cannot get off, and the Most High understands the captivity, but that does not mean you neglect the passover; keep it the second month if it is possible.

**[Numbers 9:13](/bible/numbers/9#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/9#v13)</sup> But the man that is clean, and is not in a journey, and forbeareth to keep the passover, even the same soul shall be cut off from among his people: because he brought not the offering of the Lord in his appointed season, that man shall bear his sin.

- But the man that is clean, and is not in a journey, and forbeareth to keep the passover, shall be cut off from among his people; he brought not the offering of the Lord in his appointed season, and that man shall bear his sin.
- This speaks to the one that likes to make excuses. There were no excuses then without being cut off, and there are none now when the holy days come around; if the reason is not according to the bible, it does not stand.

**[Numbers 9:14](/bible/numbers/9#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/9#v14)</sup> And if a stranger shall sojourn among you, and will keep the passover unto the Lord; according to the ordinance of the passover, and according to the manner thereof, so shall he do: ye shall have one ordinance, both for the stranger, and for him that was born in the land.

- The stranger that sojourns with Israel and will keep the passover keeps it according to the ordinance of the passover and the manner thereof: one ordinance both for the stranger and for him that was born in the land.
- The stranger here is not unbelieving family; it is one of another nation that dwelt among Israel. Even the nations among us had to keep the laws just as we did; they were not getting a pass around us.

**[Numbers 9:15-16](/bible/numbers/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/9#v15)</sup> And on the day that the tabernacle was reared up the cloud covered the tabernacle, namely, the tent of the testimony: and at even there was upon the tabernacle as it were the appearance of fire, until the morning.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/9#v16)</sup> So it was alway: the cloud covered it by day, and the appearance of fire by night.

- On the day that the tabernacle was reared up the cloud covered it, and at even there was upon the tabernacle as it were the appearance of fire until the morning.
- That cloud is the chariot of the Most High: he maketh the clouds his chariot, and that is what sat over the top of the tabernacle.

  Precepts:

    He maketh the clouds his chariot; the cloud over the tabernacle was his chariot.

**[Numbers 9:17-23](/bible/numbers/9#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/9#v17)</sup> And when the cloud was taken up from the tabernacle, then after that the children of Israel journeyed: and in the place where the cloud abode, there the children of Israel pitched their tents.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/9#v18)</sup> At the commandment of the Lord the children of Israel journeyed, and at the commandment of the Lord they pitched: as long as the cloud abode upon the tabernacle they rested in their tents.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/9#v19)</sup> And when the cloud tarried long upon the tabernacle many days, then the children of Israel kept the charge of the Lord, and journeyed not.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/9#v20)</sup> And so it was, when the cloud was a few days upon the tabernacle; according to the commandment of the Lord they abode in their tents, and according to the commandment of the Lord they journeyed.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/9#v21)</sup> And so it was, when the cloud abode from even unto the morning, and that the cloud was taken up in the morning, then they journeyed: whether it was by day or by night that the cloud was taken up, they journeyed.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/9#v22)</sup> Or whether it were two days, or a month, or a year, that the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle, remaining thereon, the children of Israel abode in their tents, and journeyed not: but when it was taken up, they journeyed.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/9#v23)</sup> At the commandment of the Lord they rested in the tents, and at the commandment of the Lord they journeyed: they kept the charge of the Lord, at the commandment of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

- When the cloud was taken up from the tabernacle, the children of Israel journeyed; and in the place where the cloud abode, there they pitched their tents. Wherever the chariot went, Israel got up and followed it, the same way it led them across the Red Sea.
- Whether it were two days, or a month, or a year that the cloud tarried upon the tabernacle, they abode in their tents and journeyed not; and when it was taken up, they journeyed, whether by day or by night. That is how they knew where they needed to stay and when to move.
- At the commandment of the Lord they rested in the tents, and at the commandment of the Lord they journeyed; they kept the charge of the Lord by the hand of Moses.
