## Introduction

This reading session covers [Numbers 11](/bible/numbers/11) through 14: the fire at Taberah, the lusting for flesh, the seventy elders, and the murmuring of the people in the wilderness. These notes were transcribed from the session audio, because the video has no captions. The available transcription ends about thirty minutes in, partway through [Numbers 11](/bible/numbers/11) at verse 25, so these notes cover the portion of the reading that was captured; [Numbers 12](/bible/numbers/12) through 14 were read in the remainder of the session but are not in the transcript.

**[Numbers 11:1](/bible/numbers/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/11#v1)</sup> And when the people complained, it displeased the Lord: and the Lord heard it; and his anger was kindled; and the fire of the Lord burnt among them, and consumed them that were in the uttermost parts of the camp.

- The Most High hates it when His people complain. He had just destroyed Egypt for Israel, its land, its infrastructure, its firstborn, and drowned its army in the sea, all to save the people out; then on the other side of the wilderness, at the first trial or tribulation, the people jumped off His bandwagon, and the same is still being done to this day.
- The Lord heard the complaining, and His anger was kindled, and the fire of the Lord burnt among them. Vengeance, when it punisheth, does not pass by him that speaketh unrighteous things; the murmurer receives the vengeance of the Lord.
- Nothing spoken behind closed doors is hidden. Inquisition is made into the counsels of the ungodly, by the Lord and His angels, the same council seen when the Lord asked who shall persuade Ahab that he may fall at Ramothgilead; the sound of a man's words comes unto the Lord, for the ear of jealousy heareth all things.
- Beware of murmuring and refrain the tongue from backbiting, for there is no word so secret that shall go for nought, and the mouth that belieth, that slandereth a brother, a sister, the leadership, or the Lord, slayeth the soul.
- Many speak evil against leadership because they do not like when judgment comes out; a problem with the leadership the Most High set up is a problem with the Most High Himself.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 1:8-11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v8)</sup> Therefore he that speaketh unrighteous things cannot be hid: neither shall vengeance, when it punisheth, pass by him.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v9)</sup> For inquisition shall be made into the counsels of the ungodly: and the sound of his words shall come unto the Lord for the manifestation of his wicked deeds.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v10)</sup> For the ear of jealousy heareth all things: and the noise of murmurings is not hid.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v11)</sup> Therefore beware of murmuring, which is unprofitable; and refrain your tongue from backbiting: for there is no word so secret, that shall go for nought: and the mouth that belieth slayeth the soul.

    The ear of jealousy heareth all things; beware of murmuring, for the mouth that belieth slayeth the soul.
  - **[1 Kings 22:20](/bible/1-kings/22#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-kings/22#v20)</sup> And the Lord said, Who shall persuade Ahab, that he may go up and fall at Ramoth–gilead? And one said on this manner, and another said on that manner.

    The Lord holds council with His angels; that is the inquisition made into the counsels of the ungodly.

**[Numbers 11:2-3](/bible/numbers/11#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/11#v2)</sup> And the people cried unto Moses; and when Moses prayed unto the Lord, the fire was quenched.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/11#v3)</sup> And he called the name of the place Taberah: because the fire of the Lord burnt among them.

- The people cried unto Moses, and when Moses prayed unto the Lord, the fire was quenched; the Most High quenched the fire because Moses prayed on behalf of the children of Israel.
- The place was called Taberah, because the fire of the Lord burnt among them.

**[Numbers 11:4-6](/bible/numbers/11#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/11#v4)</sup> And the mixt multitude that was among them fell a lusting: and the children of Israel also wept again, and said, Who shall give us flesh to eat?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/11#v5)</sup> We remember the fish, which we did eat in Egypt freely; the cucumbers, and the melons, and the leeks, and the onions, and the garlick:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/11#v6)</sup> But now our soul is dried away: there is nothing at all, beside this manna, before our eyes.

- The mixed multitude that fell a lusting were the other nations sprinkled in among Israel, the mixed multitude that went up with the people out of Egypt; the law in Leviticus tells what they were for, to be the bondmen and bondmaids of Israel.
- It was not Israel complaining at first; it was the nations lusting after the food of Egypt, and the children of Israel, gullible and assimilated as always, followed right after them and murmured against their God. The same spirit is on the people now: whatever the other nations want to do, our people want to be part of it.
- They wept for the fish they ate freely in Egypt, the cucumbers, the melons, the leeks, the onions, and the garlic, right after the Lord destroyed the Egyptians to bring them out of bondage. It is the same today with the entertainments and comforts of this captivity, and some will do the same thing again in the wilderness when the Most High brings the people back out.
- They had just seen the Lord consume the complainers in the uttermost parts of the camp, and they went back and did the same thing. We are the same people today; there is nothing new under the sun, and these things were written aforetime for our learning, so we can read them and refuse to do as they did.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 12:38](/bible/exodus/12#v38)**
> <sup>[38](/bible/exodus/12#v38)</sup> And a mixed multitude went up also with them; and flocks, and herds, even very much cattle.

    A mixed multitude of other nations went up with Israel out of Egypt.
  - **[Leviticus 25:44-45](/bible/leviticus/25#v44)**
> <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/25#v44)</sup> Both thy bondmen, and thy bondmaids, which thou shalt have, shall be of the heathen that are round about you; of them shall ye buy bondmen and bondmaids.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/25#v45)</sup> Moreover of the children of the strangers that do sojourn among you, of them shall ye buy, and of their families that are with you, which they begat in your land: and they shall be your possession.

    The strangers sojourning among Israel were to be their bondmen and bondmaids.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning.

**[Numbers 11:7-9](/bible/numbers/11#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/11#v7)</sup> And the manna was as coriander seed, and the colour thereof as the colour of bdellium.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/11#v8)</sup> And the people went about, and gathered it, and ground it in mills, or beat it in a mortar, and baked it in pans, and made cakes of it: and the taste of it was as the taste of fresh oil.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/11#v9)</sup> And when the dew fell upon the camp in the night, the manna fell upon it.

- The manna was as coriander seed, the colour of bdellium; the people gathered it, ground it, baked it in pans, and the taste of it was as the taste of fresh oil.
- Read precept upon precept to understand what they despised: manna is angels' food, the food the Lord feeds the angels, sent from heaven prepared without their labour; they only had to pick it up.
- The manna was able to content every man's delight and agreed to every taste, tempering itself to every man's liking; whatever the tongue craved that day, the manna tasted like it. To complain about that provision, including the very garlic and onions and melons they wept for, was pure rebellion against the Lord God of heaven and earth.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 16:20-21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v20)</sup> Instead whereof thou feddest thine own people with angels’ food, and didst send them from heaven bread prepared without their labour, able to content every man’s delight, and agreeing to every taste.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v21)</sup> For thy sustenance declared thy sweetness unto thy children, and serving to the appetite of the eater, tempered itself to every man’s liking.

    Manna was angels' food, agreeing to every taste and tempering itself to every man's liking.

**[Numbers 11:10](/bible/numbers/11#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/11#v10)</sup> Then Moses heard the people weep throughout their families, every man in the door of his tent: and the anger of the Lord was kindled greatly; Moses also was displeased.

- Moses heard the people weep throughout their families, every man in the door of his tent; the anger of the Lord was kindled greatly, and Moses also was displeased.

**[Numbers 11:11-15](/bible/numbers/11#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/11#v11)</sup> And Moses said unto the Lord, Wherefore hast thou afflicted thy servant? and wherefore have I not found favour in thy sight, that thou layest the burden of all this people upon me?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/11#v12)</sup> Have I conceived all this people? have I begotten them, that thou shouldest say unto me, Carry them in thy bosom, as a nursing father beareth the sucking child, unto the land which thou swarest unto their fathers?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/11#v13)</sup> Whence should I have flesh to give unto all this people? for they weep unto me, saying, Give us flesh, that we may eat.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/11#v14)</sup> I am not able to bear all this people alone, because it is too heavy for me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/11#v15)</sup> And if thou deal thus with me, kill me, I pray thee, out of hand, if I have found favour in thy sight; and let me not see my wretchedness.

- Moses asked the Most High why He had afflicted His servant and laid the burden of all this people on him: have I conceived all this people, that thou shouldest say, Carry them in thy bosom as a nursing father? He was carrying grown people who acted like children.
- The people Moses bore are the same people today; this teaching identifies the children of Israel with the so-called Blacks, Hispanics, and Native American Indians, and the sheer rebellion and complaining in these chapters is one of the marks of the people.
- Moses said, If thou deal thus with me, kill me, I pray thee, out of hand; he would rather die than bear so rebellious a people alone and look upon his own wretchedness.

**[Numbers 11:16-17](/bible/numbers/11#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/11#v16)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Gather unto me seventy men of the elders of Israel, whom thou knowest to be the elders of the people, and officers over them; and bring them unto the tabernacle of the congregation, that they may stand there with thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/11#v17)</sup> And I will come down and talk with thee there: and I will take of the spirit which is upon thee, and will put it upon them; and they shall bear the burden of the people with thee, that thou bear it not thyself alone.

- The Lord told Moses to gather seventy men of the elders of Israel to the tabernacle, and He would take of the spirit upon Moses and put it upon them, that they bear the burden of the people with him.
- Leadership works the same way today. The spirit on the head, the willingness to put in work, the humility, the patience, is meant to trickle down to the men below him, and the spirit the lead sisters roll in sets the spirit of the sisters in the congregation. That is why the leaders must be right according to the scriptures, because the Most High takes their spirit and lets it come upon the others.

**[Numbers 11:18-20](/bible/numbers/11#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/11#v18)</sup> And say thou unto the people, Sanctify yourselves against to morrow, and ye shall eat flesh: for ye have wept in the ears of the Lord, saying, Who shall give us flesh to eat? for it was well with us in Egypt: therefore the Lord will give you flesh, and ye shall eat.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/11#v19)</sup> Ye shall not eat one day, nor two days, nor five days, neither ten days, nor twenty days;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/11#v20)</sup> But even a whole month, until it come out at your nostrils, and it be loathsome unto you: because that ye have despised the Lord which is among you, and have wept before him, saying, Why came we forth out of Egypt?

- The Lord said, Sanctify yourselves against to morrow, and ye shall eat flesh; ye have wept in the ears of the Lord, saying, It was well with us in Egypt, therefore the Lord will give you flesh.
- Not one day, nor two, nor five, nor ten, nor twenty days, but a whole month, until it come out at your nostrils and it be loathsome unto you. He fed them so much of the flesh they begged for that they would grow sick of it and hate it.
- The reason for the judgment is stated plainly: because ye have despised the Lord which is among you. They spoke evil of His manna and wept to go back among the oppressors He had just destroyed.

**[Numbers 11:21-23](/bible/numbers/11#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/11#v21)</sup> And Moses said, The people, among whom I am, are six hundred thousand footmen; and thou hast said, I will give them flesh, that they may eat a whole month.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/11#v22)</sup> Shall the flocks and the herds be slain for them, to suffice them? or shall all the fish of the sea be gathered together for them, to suffice them?
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/11#v23)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Is the Lord’s hand waxed short? thou shalt see now whether my word shall come to pass unto thee or not.

- Moses inquired how it could be done: the people are six hundred thousand footmen, and that count is not including women and children; there were millions in the wilderness. Shall the flocks and the herds be slain for them, or all the fish of the sea gathered together for them, to suffice them?
- The Lord answered, Is the LORD'S hand waxed short? The One who destroyed Egypt asks whether His hand is short; thou shalt see now whether my word shall come to pass unto thee or not. Be patient and watch the word come to pass.

**[Numbers 11:24-25](/bible/numbers/11#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/11#v24)</sup> And Moses went out, and told the people the words of the Lord, and gathered the seventy men of the elders of the people, and set them round about the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/11#v25)</sup> And the Lord came down in a cloud, and spake unto him, and took of the spirit that was upon him, and gave it unto the seventy elders: and it came to pass, that, when the spirit rested upon them, they prophesied, and did not cease.

- Moses gathered the seventy elders round about the tabernacle, and the Lord came down in a cloud and took of the spirit that was upon Moses and gave it unto the seventy elders; and when the spirit rested upon them, they prophesied, and did not cease.
- The same thing happened with Elijah and Elisha. Elisha, Elijah's right hand and disciple, asked for a double portion of his master's spirit before Elijah was taken up, and when the sons of the prophets saw Elisha smite Jordan with the mantle, they said, The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha.
- It was the same in the time of Christ: the apostles taught like Christ, and men could tell that spirit was on them, just as the elders carried the spirit of Moses.

  - **[2 Kings 2:9](/bible/2-kings/2#v9)**

    Elisha asked for a double portion of Elijah's spirit before his master was taken from him.
  - **[2 Kings 2:15](/bible/2-kings/2#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/2#v15)</sup> And when the sons of the prophets which were to view at Jericho saw him, they said, The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha. And they came to meet him, and bowed themselves to the ground before him.

    The sons of the prophets saw it and said, The spirit of Elijah doth rest on Elisha.
