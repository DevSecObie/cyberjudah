## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 1](/bible/numbers/1) and 2, read in the same session as [Leviticus 26](/bible/leviticus/26) and 27. The book of Numbers opens with the numbering of the armies of the Most High, the armies of the children of Israel: the census of the men of war in the wilderness of Sinai, and then the order of the camps about the tabernacle. Some think this reading is boring, but the Bible is not for them; there is beauty in everything read here, for the Most High was forming the armies of His people, and it is a beautiful thing that He found us fit and chose us to be His people.

**[Numbers 1:1-3](/bible/numbers/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/1#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, in the tabernacle of the congregation, on the first day of the second month, in the second year after they were come out of the land of Egypt, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/1#v2)</sup> Take ye the sum of all the congregation of the children of Israel, after their families, by the house of their fathers, with the number of their names, every male by their polls;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/1#v3)</sup> From twenty years old and upward, all that are able to go forth to war in Israel: thou and Aaron shall number them by their armies.

- The book of Numbers is the numbering of the armies of the Lord, the armies of the children of Israel. Moses and the elders numbered the men from twenty years old and upward, every one able to go forth to war, by their armies.
- Every man went to war unless the law released him: a man that had newly married, or built a house and not dedicated it, did not have to go; and a fearful man did not have to go, because that spirit of fear would jump on the other brothers.
- The Most High chose able men. In the book of Judges He cut the army down from the thousands to three hundred; and when Moses held up his hands over the battle, mighty men had to hold his arms up.

**[Numbers 1:17-19](/bible/numbers/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/1#v17)</sup> And Moses and Aaron took these men which are expressed by their names:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/1#v18)</sup> And they assembled all the congregation together on the first day of the second month, and they declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers, according to the number of the names, from twenty years old and upward, by their polls.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/1#v19)</sup> As the Lord commanded Moses, so he numbered them in the wilderness of Sinai.

- The congregation declared their pedigrees after their families, by the house of their fathers. That is the Bible's rule of lineage: the line runs through the father, not the mother.
- Those called Jewish today reckon you Jewish by your mother, but that is not what the Bible says. The father plants the seed; the woman is the soil, and the seed planted in the soil grows into the child. The Most High established the pedigrees of the children of Israel by the house of their fathers.

- These are those whom Moses and Aaron numbered with the twelve princes of Israel, each one for the house of his fathers: all the men of war from twenty years old and upward came to six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.
- This was the forming of Israel's armies in the wilderness, because the nation was going to have to be prepared to go to war with the nations ahead of them.

> <sup>[48](/bible/numbers/1#v48)</sup> For the Lord had spoken unto Moses, saying,

>
> <sup>[53](/bible/numbers/1#v53)</sup> But the Levites shall pitch round about the tabernacle of testimony, that there be no wrath upon the congregation of the children of Israel: and the Levites shall keep the charge of the tabernacle of testimony.
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/numbers/1#v54)</sup> And the children of Israel did according to all that the Lord commanded Moses, so did they.

- The Levites were not numbered among the men of war, for the Most High did not have Moses, Aaron, and the twelve princes number them; they were set apart to take care of the sanctuary, to minister unto the Lord and to the people.
- The Levites were appointed over the tabernacle of testimony and all its vessels: they bore it, took it down when it set forward, and set it up when it was pitched; and the stranger that came nigh was put to death.
- The Levites pitched round about the tabernacle, that there be no wrath upon the congregation of the children of Israel; and the children of Israel did according to all that the Most High commanded Moses.
