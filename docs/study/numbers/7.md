## Introduction

This reading circles back to catch [Numbers 7](/bible/numbers/7) through 10: the offerings of the twelve princes at the dedication of the altar, the cleansing of the Levites and the years of their service, the passover of the second month and the cloud over the tabernacle, and the two silver trumpets with the order of Israel's journeys. This is a reading session, so the teaching is brief precepts given along the reading, together with the questions answered after it.

**[Numbers 7:1-3](/bible/numbers/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/7#v1)</sup> And it came to pass on the day that Moses had fully set up the tabernacle, and had anointed it, and sanctified it, and all the instruments thereof, both the altar and all the vessels thereof, and had anointed them, and sanctified them;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/7#v2)</sup> That the princes of Israel, heads of the house of their fathers, who were the princes of the tribes, and were over them that were numbered, offered:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/7#v3)</sup> And they brought their offering before the Lord, six covered wagons, and twelve oxen; a wagon for two of the princes, and for each one an ox: and they brought them before the tabernacle.

- On the day that Moses had fully set up the tabernacle and anointed and sanctified it with all the instruments thereof, the princes of Israel, heads of the house of their fathers and over them that were numbered, brought their offering before the Lord: six covered wagons and twelve oxen, a wagon for two of the princes and for each one an ox.

**[Numbers 7:4-9](/bible/numbers/7#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/7#v4)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/7#v5)</sup> Take it of them, that they may be to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation; and thou shalt give them unto the Levites, to every man according to his service.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/7#v6)</sup> And Moses took the wagons and the oxen, and gave them unto the Levites.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/7#v7)</sup> Two wagons and four oxen he gave unto the sons of Gershon, according to their service:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/7#v8)</sup> And four wagons and eight oxen he gave unto the sons of Merari, according unto their service, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/7#v9)</sup> But unto the sons of Kohath he gave none: because the service of the sanctuary belonging unto them was that they should bear upon their shoulders.

- The Lord told Moses to take the wagons and oxen for the service of the tabernacle of the congregation and give them to the Levites, to every man according to his service: two wagons and four oxen to the sons of Gershon, and four wagons and eight oxen to the sons of Merari, under the hand of Ithamar the son of Aaron the priest.
- Unto the sons of Kohath he gave none, because the service of the sanctuary belonging unto them was that they should bear upon their shoulders. [Numbers 4](/bible/numbers/4) shows what that bearing was: Aaron and his sons covered the ark and the vessels first, and after that the sons of Kohath came to bear them, not touching any holy thing lest they die.
- The Kohathites were in charge of carrying the sanctuary to and fro, and the reading further on shows them doing it once the camp moved.

    > <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/4#v6)</sup> And shall put thereon the covering of badgers’ skins, and shall spread over it a cloth wholly of blue, and shall put in the staves thereof.

  - **[Numbers 4:15](/bible/numbers/4#v15)**

These things are the burden of the sons of Kohath in the tabernacle of the congregation.

    When Aaron has made an end of covering the sanctuary, the sons of Kohath come to bear it, but they shall not touch any holy thing, lest they die.

**[Numbers 7:10-11](/bible/numbers/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/7#v10)</sup> And the princes offered for dedicating of the altar in the day that it was anointed, even the princes offered their offering before the altar.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/7#v11)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, They shall offer their offering, each prince on his day, for the dedicating of the altar.

- The princes offered for the dedicating of the altar in the day that it was anointed, and the Lord said unto Moses, they shall offer their offering, each prince on his day.

**[Numbers 7:12-17](/bible/numbers/7#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/7#v12)</sup> And he that offered his offering the first day was Nahshon the son of Amminadab, of the tribe of Judah:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/7#v13)</sup> And his offering was one silver charger, the weight thereof was an hundred and thirty shekels, one silver bowl of seventy shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary; both of them were full of fine flour mingled with oil for a meat offering:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/7#v14)</sup> One spoon of ten shekels of gold, full of incense:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/7#v15)</sup> One young bullock, one ram, one lamb of the first year, for a burnt offering:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/7#v16)</sup> One kid of the goats for a sin offering:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/7#v17)</sup> And for a sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five he goats, five lambs of the first year: this was the offering of Nahshon the son of Amminadab.

- Nahshon the son of Amminadab of the tribe of Judah offered the first day. The charger is simply a plate or a platter, and the bowl and the golden spoon carried the fine flour mingled with oil and the incense.

**[Numbers 7:18-23](/bible/numbers/7#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/7#v18)</sup> On the second day Nethaneel the son of Zuar, prince of Issachar, did offer:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/7#v19)</sup> He offered for his offering one silver charger, the weight whereof was an hundred and thirty shekels, one silver bowl of seventy shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary; both of them full of fine flour mingled with oil for a meat offering:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/7#v20)</sup> One spoon of gold of ten shekels, full of incense:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/7#v21)</sup> One young bullock, one ram, one lamb of the first year, for a burnt offering:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/7#v22)</sup> One kid of the goats for a sin offering:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/7#v23)</sup> And for a sacrifice of peace offerings, two oxen, five rams, five he goats, five lambs of the first year: this was the offering of Nethaneel the son of Zuar.

- On the second day Nethaneel the son of Zuar, prince of Issachar, offered. Each prince on his day brought the very same offering as Nahshon, because this was the order that they had to do.
- The twelve days went in the order of the camps: Judah, Issachar, Zebulun, Reuben, Simeon, Gad, Ephraim, Manasseh, Benjamin, Dan, Asher, and Naphtali, closing with Ahira the son of Enan on the twelfth day.

**[Numbers 7:84-88](/bible/numbers/7#v84)**

> <sup>[84](/bible/numbers/7#v84)</sup> This was the dedication of the altar, in the day when it was anointed, by the princes of Israel: twelve chargers of silver, twelve silver bowls, twelve spoons of gold:
>
> <sup>[85](/bible/numbers/7#v85)</sup> Each charger of silver weighing an hundred and thirty shekels, each bowl seventy: all the silver vessels weighed two thousand and four hundred shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary:
>
> <sup>[86](/bible/numbers/7#v86)</sup> The golden spoons were twelve, full of incense, weighing ten shekels apiece, after the shekel of the sanctuary: all the gold of the spoons was an hundred and twenty shekels.
>
> <sup>[87](/bible/numbers/7#v87)</sup> All the oxen for the burnt offering were twelve bullocks, the rams twelve, the lambs of the first year twelve, with their meat offering: and the kids of the goats for sin offering twelve.
>
> <sup>[88](/bible/numbers/7#v88)</sup> And all the oxen for the sacrifice of the peace offerings were twenty and four bullocks, the rams sixty, the he goats sixty, the lambs of the first year sixty. This was the dedication of the altar, after that it was anointed.

- This was the dedication of the altar in the day when it was anointed by the princes of Israel, and the totals are summed: twelve chargers of silver, twelve silver bowls, twelve spoons of gold, and all the bullocks, rams, lambs, and goats for the burnt offering, the sin offering, and the peace offerings.
- Israel does not do the animal sacrifices anymore. When Christ came into the world he said, sacrifice and offering thou wouldest not; the Most High had no pleasure in them, because the people's minds were not changed when they offered them, and Christ was the ultimate sacrifice who took away the first that he might establish the second.
- There is also no tabernacle today to do them at. The sacrifices had to be done at the tabernacle, and Israel is scattered everywhere in captivity, not able to go to Jerusalem to a temple that is not standing.

**[Numbers 7:89](/bible/numbers/7#v89)**

> <sup>[89](/bible/numbers/7#v89)</sup> And when Moses was gone into the tabernacle of the congregation to speak with him, then he heard the voice of one speaking unto him from off the mercy seat that was upon the ark of testimony, from between the two cherubims: and he spake unto him.

- When Moses was gone into the tabernacle of the congregation to speak with him, that is Moses going in to speak with the Most High. The voice came from off the mercy seat that was upon the ark of testimony, from between the two cherubims, exactly where the Lord said he would meet and commune with him.

    There I will meet with thee, and I will commune with thee from above the mercy seat, from between the two cherubims.
