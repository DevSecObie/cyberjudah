**[Numbers 6:1-4](/bible/numbers/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/6#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/6#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When either man or woman shall separate themselves to vow a vow of a Nazarite, to separate themselves unto the Lord:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/6#v3)</sup> He shall separate himself from wine and strong drink, and shall drink no vinegar of wine, or vinegar of strong drink, neither shall he drink any liquor of grapes, nor eat moist grapes, or dried.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/6#v4)</sup> All the days of his separation shall he eat nothing that is made of the vine tree, from the kernels even to the husk.

- When a man or woman vowed the vow of a Nazarite, to separate themselves unto the Lord, he separated himself from wine and strong drink, drank no vinegar of wine nor liquor of grapes, and ate nothing made of the vine tree, from the kernels even to the husk.
- John the Baptist fell under that Nazarite vow: the angel told Zacharias his son shall drink neither wine nor strong drink, and shall be filled with the Holy Ghost even from his mother's womb.
- John was filled with the Holy Spirit before he was born, and Elizabeth did not sleep with an angel; Zacharias was her husband. The same way Christ was filled with the Holy Spirit before birth; the immaculate conception is a Catholic doctrine, not the scriptures.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 1:13-17](/bible/luke/1#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/luke/1#v13)</sup> But the angel said unto him, Fear not, Zacharias: for thy prayer is heard; and thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/luke/1#v14)</sup> And thou shalt have joy and gladness; and many shall rejoice at his birth.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/luke/1#v15)</sup> For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother’s womb.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/luke/1#v16)</sup> And many of the children of Israel shall he turn to the Lord their God.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/luke/1#v17)</sup> And he shall go before him in the spirit and power of Elias, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children, and the disobedient to the wisdom of the just; to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.

    John shall drink neither wine nor strong drink, filled with the Holy Ghost from the womb, going before the Lord in the spirit and power of Elias to make ready a people prepared for the Lord.

**[Numbers 6:5](/bible/numbers/6#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/6#v5)</sup> All the days of the vow of his separation there shall no razor come upon his head: until the days be fulfilled, in the which he separateth himself unto the Lord, he shall be holy, and shall let the locks of the hair of his head grow.

- All the days of the vow no razor came upon his head; he was holy, and let the locks of the hair of his head grow.
- Samson was a Nazarite also, from the tribe of Dan; he got caught up in wickedness with the daughters of Ham, but he was a Nazarite, and his hair grew in seven locks. That description is not speaking of the people who call themselves Jews today, whatever the movies portray.

  Precepts:
  - **[Judges 16:13](/bible/judges/16#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/judges/16#v13)</sup> And Delilah said unto Samson, Hitherto thou hast mocked me, and told me lies: tell me wherewith thou mightest be bound. And he said unto her, If thou weavest the seven locks of my head with the web.

    Samson spoke of the seven locks of his head; the locks of the Nazarite mark the man the scripture describes.

**[Numbers 6:6-8](/bible/numbers/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/6#v6)</sup> All the days that he separateth himself unto the Lord he shall come at no dead body.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/6#v7)</sup> He shall not make himself unclean for his father, or for his mother, for his brother, or for his sister, when they die: because the consecration of his God is upon his head.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/6#v8)</sup> All the days of his separation he is holy unto the Lord.

- All the days of his separation he came at no dead body, and made not himself unclean even for his father, his mother, his brother, or his sister when they died, because the consecration of his God was upon his head. It was a heavy thing to fall under the Nazarite vow; you had to separate yourself from everybody, even family, so you would not be defiled.

**[Numbers 6:9-12](/bible/numbers/6#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/6#v9)</sup> And if any man die very suddenly by him, and he hath defiled the head of his consecration; then he shall shave his head in the day of his cleansing, on the seventh day shall he shave it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/6#v10)</sup> And on the eighth day he shall bring two turtles, or two young pigeons, to the priest, to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/6#v11)</sup> And the priest shall offer the one for a sin offering, and the other for a burnt offering, and make an atonement for him, for that he sinned by the dead, and shall hallow his head that same day.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/6#v12)</sup> And he shall consecrate unto the Lord the days of his separation, and shall bring a lamb of the first year for a trespass offering: but the days that were before shall be lost, because his separation was defiled.

- If any man died very suddenly by him, the head of his consecration was defiled: he shaved his head on the seventh day of his cleansing, brought two turtles or two young pigeons to the priest on the eighth day, and a lamb of the first year for a trespass offering; the days before were lost.
- Paul partook in that Nazarite purification. Unto the Jews he became a Jew; he brought himself down to their level of understanding to win them over, not by breaking the law but by walking orderly and keeping it.
- The elders at Jerusalem told Paul to purify himself with four men which had a vow on them and be at charges with them, that all may know thou thyself walkest orderly and keepest the law. And note it well: the thousands there that believed were Jews, all zealous of the law, and the Gentiles Paul taught among were Jews scattered among the Gentiles.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 9:20](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v20)</sup> And unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews; to them that are under the law, as under the law, that I might gain them that are under the law;

    Unto the Jews I became as a Jew, that I might gain the Jews; that is why Paul took the charges of the vow.
  - **[Acts 21:20-24](/bible/acts/21#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/acts/21#v20)</sup> And when they heard it, they glorified the Lord, and said unto him, Thou seest, brother, how many thousands of Jews there are which believe; and they are all zealous of the law:
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/acts/21#v21)</sup> And they are informed of thee, that thou teachest all the Jews which are among the Gentiles to forsake Moses, saying that they ought not to circumcise their children, neither to walk after the customs.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/acts/21#v22)</sup> What is it therefore? the multitude must needs come together: for they will hear that thou art come.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/acts/21#v23)</sup> Do therefore this that we say to thee: We have four men which have a vow on them;
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/acts/21#v24)</sup> Them take, and purify thyself with them, and be at charges with them, that they may shave their heads: and all may know that those things, whereof they were informed concerning thee, are nothing; but that thou thyself also walkest orderly, and keepest the law.

    Thousands of Jews which believe, all zealous of the law, and Jews among the Gentiles; Paul purified himself with the four men that had a vow, walking orderly and keeping the law.

**[Numbers 6:13-20](/bible/numbers/6#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/6#v13)</sup> And this is the law of the Nazarite, when the days of his separation are fulfilled: he shall be brought unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/6#v14)</sup> And he shall offer his offering unto the Lord, one he lamb of the first year without blemish for a burnt offering, and one ewe lamb of the first year without blemish for a sin offering, and one ram without blemish for peace offerings,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/6#v15)</sup> And a basket of unleavened bread, cakes of fine flour mingled with oil, and wafers of unleavened bread anointed with oil, and their meat offering, and their drink offerings.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/6#v16)</sup> And the priest shall bring them before the Lord, and shall offer his sin offering, and his burnt offering:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/6#v17)</sup> And he shall offer the ram for a sacrifice of peace offerings unto the Lord, with the basket of unleavened bread: the priest shall offer also his meat offering, and his drink offering.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/6#v18)</sup> And the Nazarite shall shave the head of his separation at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, and shall take the hair of the head of his separation, and put it in the fire which is under the sacrifice of the peace offerings.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/6#v19)</sup> And the priest shall take the sodden shoulder of the ram, and one unleavened cake out of the basket, and one unleavened wafer, and shall put them upon the hands of the Nazarite, after the hair of his separation is shaven:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/6#v20)</sup> And the priest shall wave them for a wave offering before the Lord: this is holy for the priest, with the wave breast and heave shoulder: and after that the Nazarite may drink wine.

- When the days of his separation were fulfilled, the Nazarite offered a he lamb without blemish for a burnt offering and a ewe lamb without blemish for a sin offering, with unleavened bread and the meat and drink offerings. Without blemish is the same thing going into Christ.
- He shaved the head of his separation at the door of the tabernacle and put the hair in the fire under the sacrifice of the peace offerings; the priest put the sodden shoulder and the cakes on his hands and waved them for a wave offering, which we covered in Exodus: the priest picks it up and waves it before the Lord.
- After that the Nazarite may drink wine; before that he could not. In our understanding today you cannot keep the vow of a Nazarite under Christ: you can let your hair grow as long as you want, but when we break bread and take the cup in remembrance of Christ, renewing that covenant, you have to drink that wine to be in covenant with him.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 11:23-25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v23)</sup> For I have received of the Lord that which also I delivered unto you, That the Lord Jesus the same night in which he was betrayed took bread:
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v24)</sup> And when he had given thanks, he brake it, and said, Take, eat: this is my body, which is broken for you: this do in remembrance of me.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v25)</sup> After the same manner also he took the cup, when he had supped, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood: this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me.

    Take, eat, this is my body; this cup is the new testament in my blood, this do ye, as oft as ye drink it, in remembrance of me. The cup of the covenant is why the Nazarite vow cannot be kept under Christ.

**[Numbers 6:22-27](/bible/numbers/6#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/6#v22)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/6#v23)</sup> Speak unto Aaron and unto his sons, saying, On this wise ye shall bless the children of Israel, saying unto them,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/6#v24)</sup> The Lord bless thee, and keep thee:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/6#v25)</sup> The Lord make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/6#v26)</sup> The Lord lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/6#v27)</sup> And they shall put my name upon the children of Israel; and I will bless them.

- On this wise Aaron and his sons blessed the children of Israel: the Lord bless thee and keep thee, the Lord make his face shine upon thee and be gracious unto thee, the Lord lift up his countenance upon thee and give thee peace; and they shall put my name upon the children of Israel, and I will bless them.
- All of that is a stipulation, a contract; it is not unconditional love with the Most High. Keep my commandments and live.
- When the southern kingdom asked wherefore the Lord pronounced evil against them, the answer was because your fathers forsook me and walked after other gods, and ye have done worse than your fathers. Bring it up to today: Christianity, Islam, Buddhism, Hinduism, every denomination walking after the imagination of its own evil heart, cast out into a land we knew not, serving gods where the Lord shows no favor. That is why our people have no peace and no blessing.
- The Most High has taken that blessing from us, and we have to work to get it back by repentance and enduring to the end. But it will happen again; the Lord is pulling his spirit back out on us, and the remnant is waking up.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 16:10-13](/bible/jeremiah/16#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/16#v10)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when thou shalt shew this people all these words, and they shall say unto thee, Wherefore hath the Lord pronounced all this great evil against us? or what is our iniquity? or what is our sin that we have committed against the Lord our God?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/16#v11)</sup> Then shalt thou say unto them, Because your fathers have forsaken me, saith the Lord, and have walked after other gods, and have served them, and have worshipped them, and have forsaken me, and have not kept my law;
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/jeremiah/16#v12)</sup> And ye have done worse than your fathers; for, behold, ye walk every one after the imagination of his evil heart, that they may not hearken unto me:
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/jeremiah/16#v13)</sup> Therefore will I cast you out of this land into a land that ye know not, neither ye nor your fathers; and there shall ye serve other gods day and night; where I will not shew you favour.

    Because your fathers have forsaken me and walked after other gods, and ye have done worse than your fathers, therefore will I cast you out of this land into a land that ye know not, where I will not shew you favour.
