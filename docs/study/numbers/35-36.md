## Introduction

This session covered Numbers 35-36 together with Deuteronomy 1-2, closing the book of Numbers and opening the book of Deuteronomy; the Deuteronomy chapters sit in their own chapter set. [Numbers 35](/bible/numbers/35) gives the cities of the Levites and one of the more interesting subjects in the books of Moses, the cities of refuge, where the Most High taught Israel how to discern murder from manslaughter. [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36) settles the inheritance of the daughters of Zelophehad and the law of marrying within the tribe.

**[Numbers 35:1-2](/bible/numbers/35#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/35#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/35#v2)</sup> Command the children of Israel, that they give unto the Levites of the inheritance of their possession cities to dwell in; and ye shall give also unto the Levites suburbs for the cities round about them.

- Moses commands the tribes to separate cities out of their own inheritance for the Levites to dwell in, with suburbs round about them.
- The Levites needed cities inside every tribe's territory because the Most High took them from among the children of Israel as a gift, to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation. Every tribe needed that service done among them, so the Levites had to be spread out through all the children of Israel.
- The commandment was fulfilled when Israel came into the land: the heads of the fathers of the Levites came to Eleazar the priest and Joshua at Shiloh and claimed the cities the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 18:6](/bible/numbers/18#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/18#v6)</sup> And I, behold, I have taken your brethren the Levites from among the children of Israel: to you they are given as a gift for the Lord, to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.

    The Levites are taken from among the children of Israel as a gift to do the service of the tabernacle; that is why every tribe gave them cities.
  - **[Joshua 21:1-3](/bible/joshua/21#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/joshua/21#v1)</sup> Then came near the heads of the fathers of the Levites unto Eleazar the priest, and unto Joshua the son of Nun, and unto the heads of the fathers of the tribes of the children of Israel;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/joshua/21#v2)</sup> And they spake unto them at Shiloh in the land of Canaan, saying, The Lord commanded by the hand of Moses to give us cities to dwell in, with the suburbs thereof for our cattle.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/joshua/21#v3)</sup> And the children of Israel gave unto the Levites out of their inheritance, at the commandment of the Lord, these cities and their suburbs.

    When Israel reached the land, the Levites claimed the commandment of Numbers 35 and the children of Israel gave them cities and suburbs out of their inheritance.

**[Numbers 35:3-5](/bible/numbers/35#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/35#v3)</sup> And the cities shall they have to dwell in; and the suburbs of them shall be for their cattle, and for their goods, and for all their beasts.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/35#v4)</sup> And the suburbs of the cities, which ye shall give unto the Levites, shall reach from the wall of the city and outward a thousand cubits round about.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/35#v5)</sup> And ye shall measure from without the city on the east side two thousand cubits, and on the south side two thousand cubits, and on the west side two thousand cubits, and on the north side two thousand cubits; and the city shall be in the midst: this shall be to them the suburbs of the cities.

- The cities were for the Levites to dwell in, and the suburbs were for their cattle, their goods, and all their beasts.
- The suburbs were measured out: a thousand cubits from the wall of the city round about, and two thousand cubits on the east, south, west, and north sides, with the city in the midst.

**[Numbers 35:6-8](/bible/numbers/35#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/35#v6)</sup> And among the cities which ye shall give unto the Levites there shall be six cities for refuge, which ye shall appoint for the manslayer, that he may flee thither: and to them ye shall add forty and two cities.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/35#v7)</sup> So all the cities which ye shall give to the Levites shall be forty and eight cities: them shall ye give with their suburbs.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/35#v8)</sup> And the cities which ye shall give shall be of the possession of the children of Israel: from them that have many ye shall give many; but from them that have few ye shall give few: every one shall give of his cities unto the Levites according to his inheritance which he inheriteth.

- Among the Levite cities six were set apart for refuge, appointed for the manslayer to flee there, with forty and two more added: forty and eight cities in all, given from every tribe according to its inheritance, many from them that had many and few from them that had few.
- Why would the Lord permit a manslayer refuge? The Most High gave Moses the understanding of how to discern manslaughter from murder. For murder there was no refuge, but for manslaughter cities of refuge were given.
- The law itself was already instituted in [Exodus 21](/bible/exodus/21); [Numbers 35](/bible/numbers/35) answers where the manslayer goes: the Most High appointed him a place to flee.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 21:12-14](/bible/exodus/21#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/21#v12)</sup> He that smiteth a man, so that he die, shall be surely put to death.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/21#v13)</sup> And if a man lie not in wait, but God deliver him into his hand; then I will appoint thee a place whither he shall flee.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/21#v14)</sup> But if a man come presumptuously upon his neighbour, to slay him with guile; thou shalt take him from mine altar, that he may die.

    He that smiteth a man that he die is put to death; but for him that lay not in wait, God delivering him into his hand, a place was appointed to flee; the presumptuous slayer is taken even from the altar to die.

**[Numbers 35:9-12](/bible/numbers/35#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/35#v9)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/35#v10)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye be come over Jordan into the land of Canaan;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/35#v11)</sup> Then ye shall appoint you cities to be cities of refuge for you; that the slayer may flee thither, which killeth any person at unawares.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/35#v12)</sup> And they shall be unto you cities for refuge from the avenger; that the manslayer die not, until he stand before the congregation in judgment.

- Killeth any person unawares means he did not mean to do it; it was an accident that resulted in a death. Accidental deaths are of the Lord, beyond our control.
- Refuge means a condition of being safe or sheltered from pursuit, danger, or trouble. The cities of refuge exist because the manslayer is being pursued: someone has the right to avenge the death of the one who was killed.
- The city of refuge keeps the manslayer alive until he stands before the congregation in judgment, so the trial can discern between murder and manslaughter before the avenger reaches him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:6](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v6)</sup> Lest the avenger of the blood pursue the slayer, while his heart is hot, and overtake him, because the way is long, and slay him; whereas he was not worthy of death, inasmuch as he hated him not in time past.

    The avenger pursues the slayer while his heart is hot; without refuge he would overtake and slay a man who was not worthy of death.

**[Numbers 35:13-15](/bible/numbers/35#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/35#v13)</sup> And of these cities which ye shall give six cities shall ye have for refuge.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/35#v14)</sup> Ye shall give three cities on this side Jordan, and three cities shall ye give in the land of Canaan, which shall be cities of refuge.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/35#v15)</sup> These six cities shall be a refuge, both for the children of Israel, and for the stranger, and for the sojourner among them: that every one that killeth any person unawares may flee thither.

- Six cities of refuge stood, three on this side Jordan and three in the land of Canaan, and they served the children of Israel, the stranger, and the sojourner among them alike.
- The chapter lays out three categories when someone dies by the hand of another: he meant to hurt him but not kill him, yet he died, and that is murder; he hated him and killed him with premeditation, and that is murder; or he had no quarrel and never meant it, and that is manslaughter, not worthy of death.

**[Numbers 35:16-18](/bible/numbers/35#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/35#v16)</sup> And if he smite him with an instrument of iron, so that he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/35#v17)</sup> And if he smite him with throwing a stone, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/35#v18)</sup> Or if he smite him with an hand weapon of wood, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.

- Smite him with an instrument of iron, with a stone, or with a hand weapon of wood so that he die, and he is a murderer: even if the intent was only to hurt him and not to kill him, if he dies, the judgment is death.
- This is a precept for the street: the music culture glorifies slinging an iron, but the word says if you sling that iron and the brother dies, you are a murderer and your judgment is to die.

**[Numbers 35:19](/bible/numbers/35#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/35#v19)</sup> The revenger of blood himself shall slay the murderer: when he meeteth him, he shall slay him.

- The revenger of blood himself slays the murderer when he meets him; there was no refuge and no mercy for murder.
- This is the law given to Israel at that time, an understanding of those judgments; it is not license for anyone to go about killing today.

**[Numbers 35:20-21](/bible/numbers/35#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/35#v20)</sup> But if he thrust him of hatred, or hurl at him by laying of wait, that he die;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/35#v21)</sup> Or in enmity smite him with his hand, that he die: he that smote him shall surely be put to death; for he is a murderer: the revenger of blood shall slay the murderer, when he meeteth him.

- Laying of wait means he was lurking and stalking, waiting to catch him slipping because of hatred between them; that is premeditated murder.
- Even without a weapon, smiting him in enmity with the hand so that he dies is murder; he that smote him shall surely be put to death.

**[Numbers 35:22-24](/bible/numbers/35#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/35#v22)</sup> But if he thrust him suddenly without enmity, or have cast upon him any thing without laying of wait,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/35#v23)</sup> Or with any stone, wherewith a man may die, seeing him not, and cast it upon him, that he die, and was not his enemy, neither sought his harm:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/35#v24)</sup> Then the congregation shall judge between the slayer and the revenger of blood according to these judgments:

- The law repeats its key words on purpose, the way legal statutes do, because the details decide the case: suddenly, without enmity, without laying of wait, and seeing him not.
- Seeing him not matters because it is nearly impossible to kill a man by accident while looking straight at him; the victim was out of sight when the thing flew and struck him.
- He was not his enemy and sought not his harm: there could be no documented past hatred, and he could not even have been seeking to harm him, since seeking only to harm still makes the death murder. Then the congregation judges between the slayer and the revenger of blood according to these judgments.

**[Numbers 35:25](/bible/numbers/35#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/35#v25)</sup> And the congregation shall deliver the slayer out of the hand of the revenger of blood, and the congregation shall restore him to the city of his refuge, whither he was fled: and he shall abide in it unto the death of the high priest, which was anointed with the holy oil.

- Once the congregation clears him of murder, it delivers the slayer out of the hand of the revenger and restores him to his city of refuge; he still must abide there.
- He stays within the city until the death of the high priest which was anointed with the holy oil, whoever held that office when the incident happened.

**[Numbers 35:26-28](/bible/numbers/35#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/35#v26)</sup> But if the slayer shall at any time come without the border of the city of his refuge, whither he was fled;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/35#v27)</sup> And the revenger of blood find him without the borders of the city of his refuge, and the revenger of blood kill the slayer; he shall not be guilty of blood:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/35#v28)</sup> Because he should have remained in the city of his refuge until the death of the high priest: but after the death of the high priest the slayer shall return into the land of his possession.

- If the slayer steps outside the borders of his city of refuge before that high priest dies and the revenger of blood finds him there and kills him, the revenger is not guilty of blood, because the slayer was instructed to remain within the borders.
- After the death of the high priest the slayer returns to the land of his possession, and the avenger can no longer touch him.

**[Numbers 35:29-30](/bible/numbers/35#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/35#v29)</sup> So these things shall be for a statute of judgment unto you throughout your generations in all your dwellings.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/35#v30)</sup> Whoso killeth any person, the murderer shall be put to death by the mouth of witnesses: but one witness shall not testify against any person to cause him to die.

- The murderer is put to death by the mouth of witnesses, plural: multiple people must testify, and their stories must match in the details for the murder to be established.
- One witness alone cannot cause a man to die, because a single witness could lie in the interest of the avenger and put an innocent man to death.
- The rule stood first for sins worthy of death and then had to become the principle for every matter: two or three witnesses establish any accusation of iniquity or sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 17:6](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6)</sup> At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; but at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.

    At the mouth of two or three witnesses shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; one witness is never enough.
  - **[Deuteronomy 19:15](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/19#v15)</sup> One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity, or for any sin, in any sin that he sinneth: at the mouth of two witnesses, or at the mouth of three witnesses, shall the matter be established.

    One witness shall not rise up against a man for any iniquity or any sin; at the mouth of two or three witnesses is the matter established.

**[Numbers 35:31-32](/bible/numbers/35#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/35#v31)</sup> Moreover ye shall take no satisfaction for the life of a murderer, which is guilty of death: but he shall be surely put to death.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/35#v32)</sup> And ye shall take no satisfaction for him that is fled to the city of his refuge, that he should come again to dwell in the land, until the death of the priest.

- The judges could take no satisfaction for the life of a murderer guilty of death, and no satisfaction for the manslayer to come home before the death of the high priest; a guilty man cannot buy himself off.
- No matter who stands before the judge or what money is offered, if they are guilty of death they are guilty of death; a gift blinds the eyes of the wise and perverts the words of the righteous.
- Israel's judges later fell into exactly this: they became covetous and took bribes to clear the guilty and turn aside the poor in the gate.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 16:18-19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18)</sup> Judges and officers shalt thou make thee in all thy gates, which the Lord thy God giveth thee, throughout thy tribes: and they shall judge the people with just judgment.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift: for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous.

    Judges in all thy gates must judge with just judgment, respect no persons, and take no gift, for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise.
  - **[Amos 5:12](/bible/amos/5#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/amos/5#v12)</sup> For I know your manifold transgressions and your mighty sins: they afflict the just, they take a bribe, and they turn aside the poor in the gate from their right.

    The judges of Israel came to afflict the just and take a bribe, turning aside the poor in the gate from their right.

**[Numbers 35:33-34](/bible/numbers/35#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/35#v33)</sup> So ye shall not pollute the land wherein ye are: for blood it defileth the land: and the land cannot be cleansed of the blood that is shed therein, but by the blood of him that shed it.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/35#v34)</sup> Defile not therefore the land which ye shall inhabit, wherein I dwell: for I the Lord dwell among the children of Israel.

- Blood defileth the land, and the land cannot be cleansed of the blood shed in it but by the blood of him that shed it; the murderer who escapes his judgment leaves the land polluted.
- That judgment was instituted back in the days of Noah, and Moses carried it forward in the statutes given to Israel, for the Lord himself dwells among the children of Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 9:6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)</sup> Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man.

    Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed; the only cleansing of the land is the blood of the shedder.

# Numbers 36: The Inheritance of the Daughters of Zelophehad

Read the chapter → [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36)

**[Numbers 36:1-4](/bible/numbers/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/36#v1)</sup> And the chief fathers of the families of the children of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, of the families of the sons of Joseph, came near, and spake before Moses, and before the princes, the chief fathers of the children of Israel:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/36#v2)</sup> And they said, The Lord commanded my lord to give the land for an inheritance by lot to the children of Israel: and my lord was commanded by the Lord to give the inheritance of Zelophehad our brother unto his daughters.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/36#v3)</sup> And if they be married to any of the sons of the other tribes of the children of Israel, then shall their inheritance be taken from the inheritance of our fathers, and shall be put to the inheritance of the tribe whereunto they are received: so shall it be taken from the lot of our inheritance.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/36#v4)</sup> And when the jubile of the children of Israel shall be, then shall their inheritance be put unto the inheritance of the tribe whereunto they are received: so shall their inheritance be taken away from the inheritance of the tribe of our fathers.

- The chief fathers of the families of Gilead of the tribe of Manasseh bring an issue rising out of what was already ruled in Numbers 27: when Zelophehad died in the wilderness with no sons, the Lord ruled that his inheritance pass to his daughters.
- Their concern is real: if the daughters marry men of another tribe, their father's inheritance passes with them to the husband's tribe, and at the jubilee it is fixed to that tribe, so the tribe of Manasseh loses that land forever.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 27:3-4](/bible/numbers/27#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/27#v3)</sup> Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the company of them that gathered themselves together against the Lord in the company of Korah; but died in his own sin, and had no sons.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/27#v4)</sup> Why should the name of our father be done away from among his family, because he hath no son? Give unto us therefore a possession among the brethren of our father.

    The daughters of Zelophehad pleaded that their father died in his own sin with no son, and asked why his name should be done away from among his family.
  - **[Numbers 27:6-8](/bible/numbers/27#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/27#v6)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/27#v7)</sup> The daughters of Zelophehad speak right: thou shalt surely give them a possession of an inheritance among their father’s brethren; and thou shalt cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/27#v8)</sup> And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a man die, and have no son, then ye shall cause his inheritance to pass unto his daughter.

    The Lord ruled the daughters spoke right: if a man die and have no son, his inheritance passes to his daughter; that ruling set up the question of Numbers 36.

**[Numbers 36:5-7](/bible/numbers/36#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/36#v5)</sup> And Moses commanded the children of Israel according to the word of the Lord, saying, The tribe of the sons of Joseph hath said well.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/36#v6)</sup> This is the thing which the Lord doth command concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, Let them marry to whom they think best; only to the family of the tribe of their father shall they marry.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/36#v7)</sup> So shall not the inheritance of the children of Israel remove from tribe to tribe: for every one of the children of Israel shall keep himself to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers.

- The Most High affirms the tribe of the sons of Joseph: they have said well. The solution: the daughters of Zelophehad may marry whom they think best, only within the family of the tribe of their father.
- The purpose is stated plainly: so shall not the inheritance of the children of Israel remove from tribe to tribe; the marriage boundary here was instituted because of land inheritance.

**[Numbers 36:8-9](/bible/numbers/36#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/36#v8)</sup> And every daughter, that possesseth an inheritance in any tribe of the children of Israel, shall be wife unto one of the family of the tribe of her father, that the children of Israel may enjoy every man the inheritance of his fathers.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/36#v9)</sup> Neither shall the inheritance remove from one tribe to another tribe; but every one of the tribes of the children of Israel shall keep himself to his own inheritance.

- Every daughter that possessed an inheritance had to marry within her father's tribe, so every man of Israel would enjoy the inheritance of his fathers and no inheritance would remove from one tribe to another.
- The tribal restriction belonged to the time when Israel dwelt in the land; it existed because of inheritance. Today Israel has no inheritance in the lands of captivity, so the rule that still applies is marrying within the nation of Israel, not tribe-only marriage.
- No one today knows his exact tribe with certainty; a Judahite could have been dropped off anywhere in the captivity. Without the history of [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36), [Tobit 4:12](/bible/tobit/4#v12) gets twisted into a doctrine that a man may only marry his own tribe, and that is a heresy born of not knowing why the law was given.

  Precepts:
  - **[Tobit 4:12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)</sup> Beware of all whoredom, my son, and chiefly take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, and take not a strange woman to wife, which is not of thy father’s tribe: for we are the children of the prophets, Noe, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: remember, my son, that our fathers from the beginning, even that they all married wives of their own kindred, and were blessed in their children, and their seed shall inherit the land.

    Take a wife of the seed of thy fathers and not a strange woman; the seed-of-thy-fathers command still stands, and the tribe clause belonged to the inheritance law of Numbers 36.

**[Numbers 36:10-13](/bible/numbers/36#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/36#v10)</sup> Even as the Lord commanded Moses, so did the daughters of Zelophehad:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/36#v11)</sup> For Mahlah, Tirzah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, were married unto their father’s brothers’ sons:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/36#v12)</sup> And they were married into the families of the sons of Manasseh the son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of the family of their father.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/36#v13)</sup> These are the commandments and the judgments, which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses unto the children of Israel in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho.

- The daughters of Zelophehad did as the Lord commanded Moses: Mahlah, Tirzah, Hoglah, Milcah, and Noah married their father's brothers' sons, within the families of Manasseh, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of the family of their father.
- These are the commandments and the judgments which the Lord commanded by the hand of Moses in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho; with this verse the book of Numbers is completed.
