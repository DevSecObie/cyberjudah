## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 23](/bible/numbers/23) through 26: Balaam's parables that bless Israel instead of cursing her, the star and sceptre prophecy of the latter days, Israel joined unto Baal-peor and the zeal of Phinehas, and the second census of the families of Israel in the plains of Moab. This is a reading session, with the chapters read straight through and teaching given along the way.

**[Numbers 23:1-2](/bible/numbers/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/23#v1)</sup> And Balaam said unto Balak, Build me here seven altars, and prepare me here seven oxen and seven rams.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/23#v2)</sup> And Balak did as Balaam had spoken; and Balak and Balaam offered on every altar a bullock and a ram.

- Sacrificing animals was not solely a thing pertaining to Israel; sacrificing for sin is what pertained to Israel. All the ancient nations of old sacrificed animals when they worshipped their gods, so Balak and Balaam offering bullocks and rams on altars was nothing new to them.
- Offering animals on altars had been done since the time of Adam. It was commonplace in that time, so do not be confused at why Balaam keeps calling for altars and offerings through this chapter.

**[Numbers 23:7-8](/bible/numbers/23#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/23#v7)</sup> And he took up his parable, and said, Balak the king of Moab hath brought me from Aram, out of the mountains of the east, saying, Come, curse me Jacob, and come, defy Israel.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/23#v8)</sup> How shall I curse, whom God hath not cursed? or how shall I defy, whom the Lord hath not defied?

- Balak the king of Moab brought Balaam from Aram to curse Jacob and defy Israel, and Balaam could not do it: how shall I curse, whom God hath not cursed?
- Understand this: we are a blessed people, but we are living under the curses of God for our disobedience. We are the sons and daughters of God, above all nations, yet on the bottom for our sin, and we remain there until we repent as a nation.

**[Numbers 23:9-10](/bible/numbers/23#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/23#v9)</sup> For from the top of the rocks I see him, and from the hills I behold him: lo, the people shall dwell alone, and shall not be reckoned among the nations.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/23#v10)</sup> Who can count the dust of Jacob, and the number of the fourth part of Israel? Let me die the death of the righteous, and let my last end be like his!

- The people shall dwell alone and shall not be reckoned among the nations; who can count the dust of Jacob? Israel is as the sand of the sea.
- Let me die the death of the righteous, and let my last end be like his. The last end of Israel is a crown of glory in the hand of the Lord: the land married, double for our shame, everlasting joy, and a seed acknowledged among the Gentiles as the seed which the Lord hath blessed. That is why we cannot wait for those days.

**[Numbers 23:11-12](/bible/numbers/23#v11)**

>

- Balak charged Balaam with blessing his enemies altogether, and Balaam answered: must I not take heed to speak that which the Lord hath put in my mouth?
- That is the same charge given to Ezekiel, and it is what every man must do who speaks on behalf of the Most High, especially the brothers: go unto the house of Israel and speak with His words unto them.
- The house of Israel is impudent and hardhearted, so the Lord makes the face and forehead strong, meaning the mind: whatever excuse the people raise, He gives an answer through the scriptures on the spot. The rebellious spirits on our people are only cast down by the word of God, which is why we speak what the Lord puts in our mouth.

**[Numbers 23:19](/bible/numbers/23#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/23#v19)</sup> God is not a man, that he should lie; neither the son of man, that he should repent: hath he said, and shall he not do it? or hath he spoken, and shall he not make it good?

- God is not a man, that he should lie. Whatever the Lord speaks out of His mouth, once the judgment is passed there is no taking it back, whether in this life or the one to come.
- The parable of Lazarus shows it: between us and you there is a great gulf fixed, so that none can pass. The judgment has been set and there is no changing it.
- In the day of doom no man shall be able to save him that is destroyed. This present life is not the end, and asking the righteous to pray for those that outright despise God and His laws is praying for the weak; Esdras tried that with the Most High and was told to concern himself with the righteous.
- That is what we doubted back in Deuteronomy when Moses read the curses over us; we thought the Lord would not make good on His word. But the Lord is longsuffering and patient and will in no wise acquit the wicked; we will not escape our sins.

**[Numbers 23:20-21](/bible/numbers/23#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/23#v20)</sup> Behold, I have received commandment to bless: and he hath blessed; and I cannot reverse it.
>

- He hath received commandment to bless, and it cannot be reversed: he hath not beheld iniquity in Jacob, neither seen perverseness in Israel. Israel had done wickedly since Egypt, and princes rose up to overthrow Moses, but the Lord forgave us over and over again.
- No nation could pull up before God and demand He do away with His people. In His eyes Israel was good compared to the accusers; the shout of a King is among them.

**[Numbers 23:22-23](/bible/numbers/23#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/23#v22)</sup> God brought them out of Egypt; he hath as it were the strength of an unicorn.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/23#v23)</sup> Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel: according to this time it shall be said of Jacob and of Israel, What hath God wrought!

- God brought them out of Egypt; he hath as it were the strength of an unicorn. The unicorn in biblical times is the rhinoceros, the beast with the one great horn on the front.
- Surely there is no enchantment against Jacob, neither is there any divination against Israel. For Levi and all the tribes, that voodoo stuff is nothing: once you repent, there is no hex, divination, or curse of bad luck anyone can put on you. Do not even worry about it.

**[Numbers 23:24](/bible/numbers/23#v24)**

- Behold, the people shall rise up as a great lion, and he shall not lie down until he eat of the prey and drink the blood of the slain: Israel shall destroy his enemies. Deuteronomy carries that same promise, and [Genesis 49](/bible/genesis/49) touches it as well in the blessings of the tribes.

# Numbers 24: A Star out of Jacob and the Latter Days

**[Numbers 24:1-2](/bible/numbers/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/24#v1)</sup> And when Balaam saw that it pleased the Lord to bless Israel, he went not, as at other times, to seek for enchantments, but he set his face toward the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/24#v2)</sup> And Balaam lifted up his eyes, and he saw Israel abiding in his tents according to their tribes; and the spirit of God came upon him.

- When Balaam saw that it pleased the Lord to bless Israel, he went not to seek enchantments, and the spirit of God came upon him. Do not be confused at the spirit of God coming on a man of another nation: all the spirits belong to the Most High, and He can do what He wants with any of them.
- That does not make the other nations prophets. Prophets are raised up of the sons of Israel; the Lord used Balaam for one specific purpose, to show the nations there is nothing they can do against Israel when the Lord is on our side, and to have Moses write it down in a book.

**[Numbers 24:10-11](/bible/numbers/24#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/24#v10)</sup> And Balak’s anger was kindled against Balaam, and he smote his hands together: and Balak said unto Balaam, I called thee to curse mine enemies, and, behold, thou hast altogether blessed them these three times.

- Balak's anger was kindled and he smote his hands together: I thought to promote thee unto great honour, but lo, the Lord hath kept thee back from honour. That was Satan speaking through Balak, trying to entice Balaam with honor to curse Israel.
- Satan promotes with honor, riches, prestige, and lust; that is the deceitfulness of riches and the lust of other things that choke the word. When the word is sown, Satan comes immediately to take it away, using whatever device fits where you are.
- He tried the same device on Christ, offering all the power and the glory of the kingdoms in exchange for worship, and Christ answered, Get thee behind me, Satan. Satan is an old spirit using the same tricks from the beginning of time.
- We are not to be ignorant of his devices, lest he get an advantage of us. Many are falling to those devices at home in this quarantine, letting the old man rise back up; resist the devil and he will flee, but give in and he will use you until he destroys you.

**[Numbers 24:17](/bible/numbers/24#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/24#v17)</sup> I shall see him, but not now: I shall behold him, but not nigh: there shall come a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel, and shall smite the corners of Moab, and destroy all the children of Sheth.

- I shall see him, but not now: I shall behold him, but not nigh. Balaam is saying the Most High will raise him up again in the last days, but as a servant, far off from the King; he will behold him, but he will not be near him.
- Compare that with Job, and the contrast shows Job was an Israelite and not of another nation: Job knew that his redeemer liveth, that in his flesh he shall see God, whom he shall see for himself and his eyes shall behold, and not another. Balaam has no such nearness, because the redeemer is not his.
- There shall come a Star out of Jacob: the Star is Christ, the root and offspring of David, the bright and morning star. And a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel: the sceptre belongs to kings, so the prophecy is that the King shall rise out of Israel.

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/26#v10)</sup> And the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up together with Korah, when that company died, what time the fire devoured two hundred and fifty men: and they became a sign.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/26#v11)</sup> Notwithstanding the children of Korah died not.

- Dathan and Abiram were famous in the congregation, who strove against Moses and against Aaron in the company of Korah, when they strove against the Lord; the account is back in [Numbers 16](/bible/numbers/16). The earth opened her mouth and swallowed them up together with Korah, the fire devoured two hundred and fifty men, and they became a sign.
- Notwithstanding, the children of Korah died not.

**[Numbers 26:19-22](/bible/numbers/26#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/26#v19)</sup> The sons of Judah were Er and Onan: and Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/26#v20)</sup> And the sons of Judah after their families were; of Shelah, the family of the Shelanites: of Pharez, the family of the Pharzites: of Zerah, the family of the Zarhites.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/26#v21)</sup> And the sons of Pharez were; of Hezron, the family of the Hezronites: of Hamul, the family of the Hamulites.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/26#v22)</sup> These are the families of Judah according to those that were numbered of them, threescore and sixteen thousand and five hundred.

- Er and Onan died in the land of Canaan, and the families of Judah continued through Shelah, Pharez, and Zerah, threescore and sixteen thousand and five hundred numbered.
- Do not hear Pharez and think the Pharisees came from the Pharzites; the Pharisees did not come out of that family. The rise of those sects is read of in the books of the Maccabees.

**[Numbers 26:51-56](/bible/numbers/26#v51)**

> <sup>[51](/bible/numbers/26#v51)</sup> These were the numbered of the children of Israel, six hundred thousand and a thousand seven hundred and thirty.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/numbers/26#v52)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[53](/bible/numbers/26#v53)</sup> Unto these the land shall be divided for an inheritance according to the number of names.
>
> <sup>[54](/bible/numbers/26#v54)</sup> To many thou shalt give the more inheritance, and to few thou shalt give the less inheritance: to every one shall his inheritance be given according to those that were numbered of him.
>
> <sup>[55](/bible/numbers/26#v55)</sup> Notwithstanding the land shall be divided by lot: according to the names of the tribes of their fathers they shall inherit.
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/numbers/26#v56)</sup> According to the lot shall the possession thereof be divided between many and few.

- The numbered of the children of Israel were six hundred thousand and a thousand seven hundred and thirty. Unto these the land shall be divided for an inheritance according to the number of names: to many the more inheritance, to few the less, and the land divided by lot according to the tribes of their fathers.
- Land is how wealth is built, and Esau knows how important land is; that is why the land is kept in his generations and why Gad, the Native Indian, was never given one square inch of his land back, only the casinos and the little plots called reservations. Israel is getting the most fertile land back; as for this land here, let it burn.

**[Numbers 26:59-61](/bible/numbers/26#v59)**

> <sup>[59](/bible/numbers/26#v59)</sup> And the name of Amram’s wife was Jochebed, the daughter of Levi, whom her mother bare to Levi in Egypt: and she bare unto Amram Aaron and Moses, and Miriam their sister.
>
> <sup>[60](/bible/numbers/26#v60)</sup> And unto Aaron was born Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.
>
> <sup>[61](/bible/numbers/26#v61)</sup> And Nadab and Abihu died, when they offered strange fire before the Lord.

- Jochebed the daughter of Levi bare unto Amram Aaron and Moses and Miriam their sister; and unto Aaron were born Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar.
- Nadab and Abihu died when they offered strange fire before the Lord; go back and read that account.

> <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/10#v1)</sup> And Nadab and Abihu, the sons of Aaron, took either of them his censer, and put fire therein, and put incense thereon, and offered strange fire before the Lord, which he commanded them not.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/leviticus/10#v2)</sup> And there went out fire from the Lord, and devoured them, and they died before the Lord.

    Nadab and Abihu offered strange fire before the Lord, which he commanded them not, and fire from the Lord devoured them.

**[Numbers 26:63-65](/bible/numbers/26#v63)**

> <sup>[63](/bible/numbers/26#v63)</sup> These are they that were numbered by Moses and Eleazar the priest, who numbered the children of Israel in the plains of Moab by Jordan near Jericho.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/numbers/26#v64)</sup> But among these there was not a man of them whom Moses and Aaron the priest numbered, when they numbered the children of Israel in the wilderness of Sinai.
>
> <sup>[65](/bible/numbers/26#v65)</sup> For the Lord had said of them, They shall surely die in the wilderness. And there was not left a man of them, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.

- Among these numbered by Moses and Eleazar in the plains of Moab there was not a man of them that was numbered in the wilderness of Sinai, for the Lord had said of them, they shall surely die in the wilderness; and there was not left a man of them, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun.
- Why Caleb and Joshua? They had another spirit on them and did not doubt when the Lord sent them to spy out the land. My servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him and hath followed me fully, him will I bring into the land; he let nobody keep him from serving the Most High.
- Christ said the same thing: he that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me. If the Lord finds you lukewarm, choosing your kids, your parents, or your spouse over following God and Christ and keeping the commandments wholly, you will not enter into the land; you must follow fully like Caleb and zealously like Phinehas.
- Let that be a lesson learned, for whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 14:23-24](/bible/numbers/14#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/14#v23)</sup> Surely they shall not see the land which I sware unto their fathers, neither shall any of them that provoked me see it:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/14#v24)</sup> But my servant Caleb, because he had another spirit with him, and hath followed me fully, him will I bring into the land whereinto he went; and his seed shall possess it.

    None that provoked the Lord shall see the land, but Caleb, because he had another spirit with him and followed the Lord fully, shall possess it.
  - **[Matthew 10:37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)**
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/10#v37)</sup> He that loveth father or mother more than me is not worthy of me: and he that loveth son or daughter more than me is not worthy of me.

    He that loveth father or mother more than Christ is not worthy of him; nothing may keep you from following fully.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Things written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.
