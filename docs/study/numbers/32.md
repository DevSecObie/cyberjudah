**[Numbers 32:1-5](/bible/numbers/32#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/32#v1)</sup> Now the children of Reuben and the children of Gad had a very great multitude of cattle: and when they saw the land of Jazer, and the land of Gilead, that, behold, the place was a place for cattle;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/32#v2)</sup> The children of Gad and the children of Reuben came and spake unto Moses, and to Eleazar the priest, and unto the princes of the congregation, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/32#v3)</sup> Ataroth, and Dibon, and Jazer, and Nimrah, and Heshbon, and Elealeh, and Shebam, and Nebo, and Beon,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/32#v4)</sup> Even the country which the Lord smote before the congregation of Israel, is a land for cattle, and thy servants have cattle:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/32#v5)</sup> Wherefore, said they, if we have found grace in thy sight, let this land be given unto thy servants for a possession, and bring us not over Jordan.

- The children of Reuben and Gad had a very great multitude of cattle, and when they saw that Jazer and Gilead was a place for cattle, they asked Moses for that land and said, bring us not over Jordan.
- They did not want to go forward any more; they were ready to set up shop before they crossed over Jordan, and they had not even finished the mission.

**[Numbers 32:6-9](/bible/numbers/32#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/32#v6)</sup> And Moses said unto the children of Gad and to the children of Reuben, Shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/32#v7)</sup> And wherefore discourage ye the heart of the children of Israel from going over into the land which the Lord hath given them?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/32#v8)</sup> Thus did your fathers, when I sent them from Kadesh–barnea to see the land.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/32#v9)</sup> For when they went up unto the valley of Eshcol, and saw the land, they discouraged the heart of the children of Israel, that they should not go into the land which the Lord had given them.

- Moses said unto them, shall your brethren go to war, and shall ye sit here and do nothing?
- Wherefore discourage ye the heart of the children of Israel from going over into the land which the Lord hath given them: thus did your fathers when they were sent from Kadesh-barnea to see the land. That goes back into the history of the spies in [Numbers 13](/bible/numbers/13); the fathers were not even in the land yet and were already getting comfortable.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 13:31-33](/bible/numbers/13#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/13#v31)</sup> But the men that went up with him said, We be not able to go up against the people; for they are stronger than we.
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/13#v32)</sup> And they brought up an evil report of the land which they had searched unto the children of Israel, saying, The land, through which we have gone to search it, is a land that eateth up the inhabitants thereof; and all the people that we saw in it are men of a great stature.
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/13#v33)</sup> And there we saw the giants, the sons of Anak, which come of the giants: and we were in our own sight as grasshoppers, and so we were in their sight.

    The men that went up brought an evil report of the land and discouraged the people; that is the history Moses set before Reuben and Gad.

**[Numbers 32:10-13](/bible/numbers/32#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/32#v10)</sup> And the Lord’s anger was kindled the same time, and he sware, saying,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/32#v11)</sup> Surely none of the men that came up out of Egypt, from twenty years old and upward, shall see the land which I sware unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob; because they have not wholly followed me:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/32#v12)</sup> Save Caleb the son of Jephunneh the Kenezite, and Joshua the son of Nun: for they have wholly followed the Lord.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/32#v13)</sup> And the Lord’s anger was kindled against Israel, and he made them wander in the wilderness forty years, until all the generation, that had done evil in the sight of the Lord, was consumed.

- The Lord's anger was kindled and he sware that none of the men that came up out of Egypt from twenty years old should see the land, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh and Joshua the son of Nun, for they wholly followed the Lord.
- He made Israel wander in the wilderness forty years, until all the generation that had done evil in the sight of the Lord was consumed. Moses is giving Reuben and Gad the history of everything so they see what their request repeats.

**[Numbers 32:14-15](/bible/numbers/32#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/32#v14)</sup> And, behold, ye are risen up in your fathers’ stead, an increase of sinful men, to augment yet the fierce anger of the Lord toward Israel.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/32#v15)</sup> For if ye turn away from after him, he will yet again leave them in the wilderness; and ye shall destroy all this people.

- Behold, ye are risen up in your fathers' stead, an increase of sinful men, to augment yet the fierce anger of the Lord toward Israel; if ye turn away from after him, he will yet again leave them in the wilderness, and ye shall destroy all this people.

**[Numbers 32:16-19](/bible/numbers/32#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/32#v16)</sup> And they came near unto him, and said, We will build sheepfolds here for our cattle, and cities for our little ones:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/32#v17)</sup> But we ourselves will go ready armed before the children of Israel, until we have brought them unto their place: and our little ones shall dwell in the fenced cities because of the inhabitants of the land.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/32#v18)</sup> We will not return unto our houses, until the children of Israel have inherited every man his inheritance.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/32#v19)</sup> For we will not inherit with them on yonder side Jordan, or forward; because our inheritance is fallen to us on this side Jordan eastward.

- They came near and answered: we will build sheepfolds for our cattle and cities for our little ones, but we ourselves will go ready armed before the children of Israel until we have brought them unto their place. Moses got them in the spirit this time.
- They vowed not to return to their houses until the children of Israel had inherited every man his inheritance, since their own inheritance had fallen to them on this side Jordan eastward.

**[Numbers 32:20-24](/bible/numbers/32#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/32#v20)</sup> And Moses said unto them, If ye will do this thing, if ye will go armed before the Lord to war,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/32#v21)</sup> And will go all of you armed over Jordan before the Lord, until he hath driven out his enemies from before him,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/32#v22)</sup> And the land be subdued before the Lord: then afterward ye shall return, and be guiltless before the Lord, and before Israel; and this land shall be your possession before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/32#v23)</sup> But if ye will not do so, behold, ye have sinned against the Lord: and be sure your sin will find you out.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/32#v24)</sup> Build you cities for your little ones, and folds for your sheep; and do that which hath proceeded out of your mouth.

- Moses set the condition: if ye will go all of you armed over Jordan before the Lord until his enemies be driven out, then afterward ye shall return and be guiltless before the Lord and before Israel, and this land shall be your possession.
- But if ye will not do so, behold, ye have sinned against the Lord: and be sure your sin will find you out.
- Build you cities for your little ones and folds for your sheep, and do that which hath proceeded out of your mouth. Do not think only about yourself; look out for your children, the nation coming after you.

- Moses gave unto the children of Gad and of Reuben and unto half the tribe of Manasseh the kingdom of Sihon king of the Amorites and the kingdom of Og king of Bashan, the land with the cities thereof, and they built their cities and folds for sheep.
- The children of Machir the son of Manasseh went to Gilead and took it, and dispossessed the Amorite which was in it: they took them out of the land.
