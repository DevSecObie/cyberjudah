## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 3](/bible/numbers/3) through 6: the Levites taken for the Most High instead of all the firstborn of Israel, the service and burdens of the sons of Kohath, Gershon, and Merari, the law of putting the defiled out of the camp and the law of jealousies, and the law of the Nazarite with the blessing of the children of Israel. The session picks up at [Numbers 3:11](/bible/numbers/3#v11) after the reading of the earlier verses was interrupted; the recap of the stranger teaching from that portion is kept under [Numbers 3:10](/bible/numbers/3#v10). The word to remember through the whole reading is order: everything in the camp of Israel was set in order, and the Most High required it on pain of death.

**[Numbers 3:10](/bible/numbers/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/3#v10)</sup> And thou shalt appoint Aaron and his sons, and they shall wait on their priest’s office: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

- Aaron and his sons were appointed to wait on the priest's office, and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death. Anybody outside the tribe of Levi, when it came to doing the service of the Lord, was considered a stranger.

  - **[Hebrews 7:13-14](/bible/hebrews/7#v13)**

    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/7#v14)</sup> For it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood.

    Our Lord sprang out of Juda, of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood; no man of another tribe gave attendance at the altar.

**[Numbers 3:11-13](/bible/numbers/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/3#v11)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/3#v12)</sup> And I, behold, I have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn that openeth the matrix among the children of Israel: therefore the Levites shall be mine;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/3#v13)</sup> Because all the firstborn are mine; for on the day that I smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt I hallowed unto me all the firstborn in Israel, both man and beast: mine shall they be: I am the Lord.

- The Most High took the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn that openeth the matrix. The matrix is the womb; that is what the Lord calls it.
- All the firstborn are his from the day he smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt. Verse 13 proves the Most High does not love everybody; he killed all the firstborn of Egypt.

**[Numbers 3:14-16](/bible/numbers/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/3#v14)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses in the wilderness of Sinai, saying,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/3#v15)</sup> Number the children of Levi after the house of their fathers, by their families: every male from a month old and upward shalt thou number them.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/3#v16)</sup> And Moses numbered them according to the word of the Lord, as he was commanded.

- The children of Levi were numbered after the houses of their fathers, by their families. Your lineage is determined by your father, not your mother.
- The claim that identity passes through the mother is nowhere in the Bible; number the children of Levi after the house of their fathers is the standard of scripture.

**[Numbers 3:21-26](/bible/numbers/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/3#v21)</sup> Of Gershon was the family of the Libnites, and the family of the Shimites: these are the families of the Gershonites.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/3#v22)</sup> Those that were numbered of them, according to the number of all the males, from a month old and upward, even those that were numbered of them were seven thousand and five hundred.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/3#v23)</sup> The families of the Gershonites shall pitch behind the tabernacle westward.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/3#v24)</sup> And the chief of the house of the father of the Gershonites shall be Eliasaph the son of Lael.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/3#v25)</sup> And the charge of the sons of Gershon in the tabernacle of the congregation shall be the tabernacle, and the tent, the covering thereof, and the hanging for the door of the tabernacle of the congregation,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/3#v26)</sup> And the hangings of the court, and the curtain for the door of the court, which is by the tabernacle, and by the altar round about, and the cords of it for all the service thereof.

- Of Gershon were the families of the Libnites and the Shimites, numbered 7,500 males from a month old and upward, and they pitched behind the tabernacle westward. Depending on your family lineage you had a specific side of the tabernacle that you and all your descendants had to be on.
- The charge of the sons of Gershon was the tabernacle and the tent, the covering thereof, the hanging for the door, the hangings of the court, and the cords of it; that was their responsibility.

**[Numbers 3:27-31](/bible/numbers/3#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/3#v27)</sup> And of Kohath was the family of the Amramites, and the family of the Izeharites, and the family of the Hebronites, and the family of the Uzzielites: these are the families of the Kohathites.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/3#v28)</sup> In the number of all the males, from a month old and upward, were eight thousand and six hundred, keeping the charge of the sanctuary.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/3#v29)</sup> The families of the sons of Kohath shall pitch on the side of the tabernacle southward.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/3#v30)</sup> And the chief of the house of the father of the families of the Kohathites shall be Elizaphan the son of Uzziel.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/3#v31)</sup> And their charge shall be the ark, and the table, and the candlestick, and the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary wherewith they minister, and the hanging, and all the service thereof.

- The ending "ites" denotes the people of; the Kohathites are the people of Kohath. They numbered 8,600 and pitched on the side of the tabernacle southward.
- Their charge was the ark, the table, the candlestick, the altars, and the vessels of the sanctuary. Only they were responsible for the ark; anybody outside of that suffered the judgment of the Most High, as Uzzah did generations later when he put forth his hand to the ark and God smote him on the spot.

**[Numbers 3:32](/bible/numbers/3#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/3#v32)</sup> And Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest shall be chief over the chief of the Levites, and have the oversight of them that keep the charge of the sanctuary.

- Eleazar the son of Aaron was chief over the chief of the Levites, with the oversight of them that keep the charge of the sanctuary. He was the manager of the tabernacle.

**[Numbers 3:38](/bible/numbers/3#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/3#v38)</sup> But those that encamp before the tabernacle toward the east, even before the tabernacle of the congregation eastward, shall be Moses, and Aaron and his sons, keeping the charge of the sanctuary for the charge of the children of Israel; and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

- Moses and Aaron and his sons encamped before the tabernacle toward the east, keeping the charge of the sanctuary; and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death. Anybody outside the tribe of Levi that approached unto the tabernacle was considered a stranger.

**[Numbers 3:39-43](/bible/numbers/3#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/3#v39)</sup> All that were numbered of the Levites, which Moses and Aaron numbered at the commandment of the Lord, throughout their families, all the males from a month old and upward, were twenty and two thousand.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/numbers/3#v40)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Number all the firstborn of the males of the children of Israel from a month old and upward, and take the number of their names.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/numbers/3#v41)</sup> And thou shalt take the Levites for me (I am the Lord) instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel; and the cattle of the Levites instead of all the firstlings among the cattle of the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/numbers/3#v42)</sup> And Moses numbered, as the Lord commanded him, all the firstborn among the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/numbers/3#v43)</sup> And all the firstborn males by the number of names, from a month old and upward, of those that were numbered of them, were twenty and two thousand two hundred and threescore and thirteen.

- All the Levites numbered from a month old and upward were 22,000; the Most High was separating the tribe of Levi unto himself instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel.
- The firstborn males of the children of Israel were twenty and two thousand two hundred and threescore and thirteen. A score is twenty, so threescore and thirteen is 73; the count is 22,273.

**[Numbers 3:44-51](/bible/numbers/3#v44)**

> <sup>[44](/bible/numbers/3#v44)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/numbers/3#v45)</sup> Take the Levites instead of all the firstborn among the children of Israel, and the cattle of the Levites instead of their cattle; and the Levites shall be mine: I am the Lord.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/numbers/3#v46)</sup> And for those that are to be redeemed of the two hundred and threescore and thirteen of the firstborn of the children of Israel, which are more than the Levites;
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/numbers/3#v47)</sup> Thou shalt even take five shekels apiece by the poll, after the shekel of the sanctuary shalt thou take them: (the shekel is twenty gerahs:)
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/numbers/3#v48)</sup> And thou shalt give the money, wherewith the odd number of them is to be redeemed, unto Aaron and to his sons.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/numbers/3#v49)</sup> And Moses took the redemption money of them that were over and above them that were redeemed by the Levites:
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/numbers/3#v50)</sup> Of the firstborn of the children of Israel took he the money; a thousand three hundred and threescore and five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary:
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/numbers/3#v51)</sup> And Moses gave the money of them that were redeemed unto Aaron and to his sons, according to the word of the Lord, as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The firstborn of Israel outnumbered the Levites by 273, and those odd ones were redeemed at five shekels apiece by the poll, after the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs. Moses took the redemption money, 1,365 shekels, and gave it unto Aaron and to his sons.
- The money went to Aaron and the priests because they had to take care of the widows, the fatherless, and the strangers; the strangers are our people that come from different parts of the land down to Jerusalem needing help. That is how the Most High put money into the sanctuary to take care of those that could not take care of themselves.
