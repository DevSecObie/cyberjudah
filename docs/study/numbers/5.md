**[Numbers 5:1-4](/bible/numbers/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/5#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/5#v2)</sup> Command the children of Israel, that they put out of the camp every leper, and every one that hath an issue, and whosoever is defiled by the dead:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/5#v3)</sup> Both male and female shall ye put out, without the camp shall ye put them; that they defile not their camps, in the midst whereof I dwell.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/5#v4)</sup> And the children of Israel did so, and put them out without the camp: as the Lord spake unto Moses, so did the children of Israel.

- Every leper, every one that hath an issue, and whosoever is defiled by the dead, male and female, was put out without the camp, that they defile not their camps in the midst whereof the Most High dwelt. And the children of Israel did so.
- Israel is referred to as a camp all through these chapters. The Lord is gathering his camp back together again to come and deliver us; if you are not in a camp, get in a camp.
- Inside the camp we consider one another to provoke unto love and to good works; the stronger in faith exhort the weaker to keep striving and endure to the end. If you are not gathered you cannot do that, and you do a disservice to the Lord and to your people.
- Separating yourself after receiving the knowledge of the truth is willful sin, and there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins, but a fearful looking for of judgment; that is doing despite unto the spirit of grace that woke us up in these last days.

    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/hebrews/10#v25)</sup> Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, as the manner of some is; but exhorting one another: and so much the more, as ye see the day approaching.

    Not forsaking the assembling of ourselves together, but exhorting one another, and so much the more as ye see the day approaching.
  - **[Hebrews 10:26-29](/bible/hebrews/10#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/hebrews/10#v26)</sup> For if we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins,
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/hebrews/10#v27)</sup> But a certain fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation, which shall devour the adversaries.

    Gather yourselves together, O nation not desired; there is nowhere to obey that except gathered in a camp.

**[Numbers 5:5-8](/bible/numbers/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/5#v5)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/5#v6)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, When a man or woman shall commit any sin that men commit, to do a trespass against the Lord, and that person be guilty;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/5#v7)</sup> Then they shall confess their sin which they have done: and he shall recompense his trespass with the principal thereof, and add unto it the fifth part thereof, and give it unto him against whom he hath trespassed.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/5#v8)</sup> But if the man have no kinsman to recompense the trespass unto, let the trespass be recompensed unto the Lord, even to the priest; beside the ram of the atonement, whereby an atonement shall be made for him.

- When a man or woman commits a trespass against the Lord, they shall confess their sin and recompense the trespass with the principal thereof plus the fifth part, given unto him against whom the trespass was done. [Exodus 22](/bible/exodus/22) is the chapter of those civil matters and repayments; the Lord laid the payment on you so you would learn not to do it again.
- If the man have no kinsman, the trespass was recompensed unto the Lord, even to the priest, beside the ram of the atonement. The atonement now is Christ; we no longer take a ram to a priest, because Christ became the atonement and we ask forgiveness through him.
- Christ is the advocate and the propitiation, the mercy, for our sins; there is no more Levite priest sacrifice, for he is the mediator between us and the Most High now. The whole world in that verse is the whole world of the nation of Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 2:1-2](/bible/1-john/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-john/2#v1)</sup> My little children, these things write I unto you, that ye sin not. And if any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-john/2#v2)</sup> And he is the propitiation for our sins: and not for ours only, but also for the sins of the whole world.

    If any man sin, we have an advocate with the Father, Jesus Christ the righteous, the propitiation for our sins and for the sins of the whole world.
  - **[Isaiah 45:17](/bible/isaiah/45#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/45#v17)</sup> But Israel shall be saved in the Lord with an everlasting salvation: ye shall not be ashamed nor confounded world without end.

    Israel shall be saved in the LORD with an everlasting salvation, world without end; that is the whole world the propitiation covers.

**[Numbers 5:9-10](/bible/numbers/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/5#v9)</sup> And every offering of all the holy things of the children of Israel, which they bring unto the priest, shall be his.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/5#v10)</sup> And every man’s hallowed things shall be his: whatsoever any man giveth the priest, it shall be his.

- Every offering of the holy things brought unto the priest was his, because the Levites had no inheritance of land as the other tribes did. Levi was scattered in every land, in Ephraim, in Dan, in Zebulun, in Judah, in Benjamin, so the people could bring the sacrifice to them; Levi had no plot to cultivate crops on, and was fed by the people of the Lord.

**[Numbers 5:11-15](/bible/numbers/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/5#v11)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/5#v12)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, If any man’s wife go aside, and commit a trespass against him,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/5#v13)</sup> And a man lie with her carnally, and it be hid from the eyes of her husband, and be kept close, and she be defiled, and there be no witness against her, neither she be taken with the manner;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/5#v14)</sup> And the spirit of jealousy come upon him, and he be jealous of his wife, and she be defiled: or if the spirit of jealousy come upon him, and he be jealous of his wife, and she be not defiled:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/5#v15)</sup> Then shall the man bring his wife unto the priest, and he shall bring her offering for her, the tenth part of an ephah of barley meal; he shall pour no oil upon it, nor put frankincense thereon; for it is an offering of jealousy, an offering of memorial, bringing iniquity to remembrance.

- If a man's wife go aside and lie with another carnally, hid from the eyes of her husband with no witness, and the spirit of jealousy come upon him, there is a law for it. The spirit of jealousy is the Lord putting it on the husband that something is not right: the routine changes, and he begins questioning and seeing things differently, whether she be defiled or not defiled.
- The man brought his wife unto the priest with the tenth part of an ephah of barley meal, no oil and no frankincense upon it, for it is an offering of jealousy, an offering of memorial, bringing iniquity to remembrance; the Lord said do not make this offering sweet.

**[Numbers 5:16-18](/bible/numbers/5#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/5#v16)</sup> And the priest shall bring her near, and set her before the Lord:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/5#v17)</sup> And the priest shall take holy water in an earthen vessel; and of the dust that is in the floor of the tabernacle the priest shall take, and put it into the water:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/5#v18)</sup> And the priest shall set the woman before the Lord, and uncover the woman’s head, and put the offering of memorial in her hands, which is the jealousy offering: and the priest shall have in his hand the bitter water that causeth the curse:

- The priest set the woman before the Lord, took holy water in an earthen vessel with the dust of the floor of the tabernacle, and uncovered the woman's head.
- Verse 18 is a precept that our sisters had their heads covered. A wig is not the covering; the covered head is a custom, a tradition, and our heritage.

**[Numbers 5:19-22](/bible/numbers/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/5#v19)</sup> And the priest shall charge her by an oath, and say unto the woman, If no man have lain with thee, and if thou hast not gone aside to uncleanness with another instead of thy husband, be thou free from this bitter water that causeth the curse:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/5#v20)</sup> But if thou hast gone aside to another instead of thy husband, and if thou be defiled, and some man have lain with thee beside thine husband:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/5#v21)</sup> Then the priest shall charge the woman with an oath of cursing, and the priest shall say unto the woman, The Lord make thee a curse and an oath among thy people, when the Lord doth make thy thigh to rot, and thy belly to swell;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/5#v22)</sup> And this water that causeth the curse shall go into thy bowels, to make thy belly to swell, and thy thigh to rot: And the woman shall say, Amen, amen.

- The priest charged her by an oath: if no man have lain with thee, be thou free from this bitter water that causeth the curse; but if thou hast gone aside to another instead of thy husband, the Lord make thy thigh to rot and thy belly to swell. The Most High is hard on his daughters for a reason, and he was not playing with that judgment.
- The woman had to answer Amen, amen. If you would not agree to drink the water, you showed yourself guilty already, and the guilty were stoned. Surely your sin shall find you out; there is nothing you can hide.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 32:23](/bible/numbers/32#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/32#v23)</sup> But if ye will not do so, behold, ye have sinned against the Lord: and be sure your sin will find you out.

    Be sure your sin will find you out; nothing done in the dark stays hid before the Most High.

**[Numbers 5:23-28](/bible/numbers/5#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/5#v23)</sup> And the priest shall write these curses in a book, and he shall blot them out with the bitter water:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/5#v24)</sup> And he shall cause the woman to drink the bitter water that causeth the curse: and the water that causeth the curse shall enter into her, and become bitter.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/5#v25)</sup> Then the priest shall take the jealousy offering out of the woman’s hand, and shall wave the offering before the Lord, and offer it upon the altar:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/5#v26)</sup> And the priest shall take an handful of the offering, even the memorial thereof, and burn it upon the altar, and afterward shall cause the woman to drink the water.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/5#v27)</sup> And when he hath made her to drink the water, then it shall come to pass, that, if she be defiled, and have done trespass against her husband, that the water that causeth the curse shall enter into her, and become bitter, and her belly shall swell, and her thigh shall rot: and the woman shall be a curse among her people.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/5#v28)</sup> And if the woman be not defiled, but be clean; then she shall be free, and shall conceive seed.

- The priest wrote the curses in a book and blotted them out with the bitter water, waved the jealousy offering before the Lord, burnt the memorial upon the altar, and caused the woman to drink.
- If she was defiled, the water became bitter, her belly swelled and her thigh rotted, and the woman became a curse among her people, a plague she could not get rid of. If she was not defiled, she was free and shall conceive seed; the husband was simply tripping, and she was clear.

**[Numbers 5:29-31](/bible/numbers/5#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/5#v29)</sup> This is the law of jealousies, when a wife goeth aside to another instead of her husband, and is defiled;
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/5#v30)</sup> Or when the spirit of jealousy cometh upon him, and he be jealous over his wife, and shall set the woman before the Lord, and the priest shall execute upon her all this law.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/5#v31)</sup> Then shall the man be guiltless from iniquity, and this woman shall bear her iniquity.

- This is the law of jealousies, when a wife goeth aside or when the spirit of jealousy cometh upon a husband: the priest executed upon her all this law, the man was guiltless from iniquity, and the woman, if guilty, bore her iniquity.
