## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 15](/bible/numbers/15) through 18: the offerings to be made in the land and the one law for Israel and the stranger, the difference between sin of ignorance and presumptuous sin with the man that gathered sticks on the sabbath, the rebellion of Korah, Aaron's rod that budded, and the charge and portion of the priests and the Levites. This was a reading session with brief comment on the verses; no questions were raised at the end.

**[Numbers 15:1-3](/bible/numbers/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/15#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/15#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye be come into the land of your habitations, which I give unto you,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/15#v3)</sup> And will make an offering by fire unto the Lord, a burnt offering, or a sacrifice in performing a vow, or in a freewill offering, or in your solemn feasts, to make a sweet savour unto the Lord, of the herd, or of the flock:

- When Israel came into the land of their habitations, they were to make an offering by fire unto the Lord: a burnt offering, a sacrifice in performing a vow, a freewill offering, or an offering in the solemn feasts, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
- These are all the accounts on which you would sacrifice to the Lord, taken of the herd or of the flock.

**[Numbers 15:4-10](/bible/numbers/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/15#v4)</sup> Then shall he that offereth his offering unto the Lord bring a meat offering of a tenth deal of flour mingled with the fourth part of an hin of oil.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/15#v5)</sup> And the fourth part of an hin of wine for a drink offering shalt thou prepare with the burnt offering or sacrifice, for one lamb.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/15#v6)</sup> Or for a ram, thou shalt prepare for a meat offering two tenth deals of flour mingled with the third part of an hin of oil.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/15#v7)</sup> And for a drink offering thou shalt offer the third part of an hin of wine, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/15#v8)</sup> And when thou preparest a bullock for a burnt offering, or for a sacrifice in performing a vow, or peace offerings unto the Lord:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/15#v9)</sup> Then shall he bring with a bullock a meat offering of three tenth deals of flour mingled with half an hin of oil.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/15#v10)</sup> And thou shalt bring for a drink offering half an hin of wine, for an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

- Every sacrifice came with its measured meat offering of flour mingled with oil and its drink offering of wine, and the measures scaled up with the animal: a lamb, a ram, and then a bullock.
- An hin is roughly a gallon to a gallon and a half, so the bullock, the greatest offering, carried half an hin of oil and half an hin of wine.

**[Numbers 15:11-13](/bible/numbers/15#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/15#v11)</sup> Thus shall it be done for one bullock, or for one ram, or for a lamb, or a kid.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/15#v12)</sup> According to the number that ye shall prepare, so shall ye do to every one according to their number.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/15#v13)</sup> All that are born of the country shall do these things after this manner, in offering an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

- Thus it was done for one bullock, one ram, one lamb, or a kid; the kid is the goat. Everyone did according to the number they prepared.
- All that are born of the country did these things after this manner when offering an offering made by fire of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

**[Numbers 15:14-16](/bible/numbers/15#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/15#v14)</sup> And if a stranger sojourn with you, or whosoever be among you in your generations, and will offer an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord; as ye do, so he shall do.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/15#v15)</sup> One ordinance shall be both for you of the congregation, and also for the stranger that sojourneth with you, an ordinance for ever in your generations: as ye are, so shall the stranger be before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/15#v16)</sup> One law and one manner shall be for you, and for the stranger that sojourneth with you.

- The scripture separates the two: the children of Israel and the stranger that sojourns with them. If a stranger joined himself to Israel, he had to keep the law to journey with them in the land.
- One ordinance and one law stood both for the congregation and for the stranger that was joined with them, an ordinance for ever in their generations: as ye are, so shall the stranger be before the Lord.

**[Numbers 15:17-21](/bible/numbers/15#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/15#v17)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/15#v18)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land whither I bring you,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/15#v19)</sup> Then it shall be, that, when ye eat of the bread of the land, ye shall offer up an heave offering unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/15#v20)</sup> Ye shall offer up a cake of the first of your dough for an heave offering: as ye do the heave offering of the threshingfloor, so shall ye heave it.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/15#v21)</sup> Of the first of your dough ye shall give unto the Lord an heave offering in your generations.

- When Israel ate of the bread of the land, they offered up a cake of the first of their dough as an heave offering unto the Lord, as they did the heave offering of the threshingfloor.
- The heave offering was a particular motion in presenting it: the offering was held up to the Most High and brought back down; that is why it was called the heave offering.

**[Numbers 15:22-26](/bible/numbers/15#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/15#v22)</sup> And if ye have erred, and not observed all these commandments, which the Lord hath spoken unto Moses,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/15#v23)</sup> Even all that the Lord hath commanded you by the hand of Moses, from the day that the Lord commanded Moses, and henceforward among your generations;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/15#v24)</sup> Then it shall be, if ought be committed by ignorance without the knowledge of the congregation, that all the congregation shall offer one young bullock for a burnt offering, for a sweet savour unto the Lord, with his meat offering, and his drink offering, according to the manner, and one kid of the goats for a sin offering.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/15#v25)</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them; for it is ignorance: and they shall bring their offering, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord, and their sin offering before the Lord, for their ignorance:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/15#v26)</sup> And it shall be forgiven all the congregation of the children of Israel, and the stranger that sojourneth among them; seeing all the people were in ignorance.

- If the whole congregation sinned through ignorance, without the knowledge of what they were doing, and the priest came behind them and corrected them on it, then all the congregation offered a young bullock for a burnt offering and a kid of the goats for a sin offering.
- The priest made an atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it was forgiven them, for it was ignorance; the stranger among them was included in that forgiveness.

**[Numbers 15:27-29](/bible/numbers/15#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/15#v27)</sup> And if any soul sin through ignorance, then he shall bring a she goat of the first year for a sin offering.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/15#v28)</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for the soul that sinneth ignorantly, when he sinneth by ignorance before the Lord, to make an atonement for him; and it shall be forgiven him.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/15#v29)</sup> Ye shall have one law for him that sinneth through ignorance, both for him that is born among the children of Israel, and for the stranger that sojourneth among them.

- If any single soul sinned through ignorance, he brought a she goat of the first year for a sin offering, and the priest made an atonement for him, and it was forgiven him.
- One law covered him that sinned through ignorance, both for him born among the children of Israel and for the stranger that sojourned among them.

**[Numbers 15:30-31](/bible/numbers/15#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/15#v30)</sup> But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously, whether he be born in the land, or a stranger, the same reproacheth the Lord; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/15#v31)</sup> Because he hath despised the word of the Lord, and hath broken his commandment, that soul shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him.

- But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously sinned willfully; he thought about what he was doing before he did it. Whether born in the land or a stranger, that soul reproached the Lord and was cut off from among his people.
- Because he despised the word of the Lord and broke his commandment, that soul was utterly cut off, meaning put to death, and his iniquity was upon him.

**[Numbers 15:32-36](/bible/numbers/15#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/15#v32)</sup> And while the children of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man that gathered sticks upon the sabbath day.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/15#v33)</sup> And they that found him gathering sticks brought him unto Moses and Aaron, and unto all the congregation.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/15#v34)</sup> And they put him in ward, because it was not declared what should be done to him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/numbers/15#v35)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, The man shall be surely put to death: all the congregation shall stone him with stones without the camp.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/numbers/15#v36)</sup> And all the congregation brought him without the camp, and stoned him with stones, and he died; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The man found gathering sticks on the sabbath day is the example of the presumptuous sin just described: someone who thought about the sin before he did it. He was not picking up sticks just to pick up sticks; he was gathering them to kindle a fire and cook on the sabbath.
- They put him in ward, meaning they locked him up, because it was not declared what should be done to him; then the Lord commanded Moses that the man be surely put to death, and all the congregation stoned him with stones without the camp.
- He did not even get to the act itself; he had the intention, and for that willful intent he was put to death.

**[Numbers 15:37-41](/bible/numbers/15#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/numbers/15#v37)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/15#v38)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and bid them that they make them fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and that they put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue:
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/15#v39)</sup> And it shall be unto you for a fringe, that ye may look upon it, and remember all the commandments of the Lord, and do them; and that ye seek not after your own heart and your own eyes, after which ye use to go a whoring:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/numbers/15#v40)</sup> That ye may remember, and do all my commandments, and be holy unto your God.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/numbers/15#v41)</sup> I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: I am the Lord your God.

- Because of that act, the Most High gave Israel the commandment to make fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and to put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue.
- The fringe is to look upon and remember all the commandments of the Lord and do them, and not to seek after your own heart and your own eyes; so that when we think of sin, we look at the fringes and remember the laws of the Most High instead of going and picking up sticks.
- I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God.

# Numbers 16: The Rebellion of Korah

Read the chapter → [Numbers 16](/bible/numbers/16)

**[Numbers 16:1-3](/bible/numbers/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/16#v1)</sup> Now Korah, the son of Izhar, the son of Kohath, the son of Levi, and Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab, and On, the son of Peleth, sons of Reuben, took men:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/16#v2)</sup> And they rose up before Moses, with certain of the children of Israel, two hundred and fifty princes of the assembly, famous in the congregation, men of renown:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/16#v3)</sup> And they gathered themselves together against Moses and against Aaron, and said unto them, Ye take too much upon you, seeing all the congregation are holy, every one of them, and the Lord is among them: wherefore then lift ye up yourselves above the congregation of the Lord?

- Korah was of Levi, and Dathan, Abiram, and On were of Reuben. With them rose up two hundred and fifty princes of the assembly, famous in the congregation, men of renown; that is two hundred and fifty three in all against Moses and Aaron.
- These were known and powerful men among the congregation, and their influence, because of who they were, is how they were able to persuade people.
- They charged Moses and Aaron with taking too much upon themselves and lifting themselves above the congregation. They thought Moses and Aaron carried themselves as better than the rest, but the Most High is the one who chose them as leaders and was dealing with them; the accusers were jealous of them.

**[Numbers 16:4-7](/bible/numbers/16#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/16#v4)</sup> And when Moses heard it, he fell upon his face:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/16#v5)</sup> And he spake unto Korah and unto all his company, saying, Even to morrow the Lord will shew who are his, and who is holy; and will cause him to come near unto him: even him whom he hath chosen will he cause to come near unto him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/16#v6)</sup> This do; Take you censers, Korah, and all his company;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/16#v7)</sup> And put fire therein, and put incense in them before the Lord to morrow: and it shall be that the man whom the Lord doth choose, he shall be holy: ye take too much upon you, ye sons of Levi.

- When Moses heard it, he fell upon his face, and told Korah and all his company: even to morrow the Lord will shew who are his, and who is holy; the Most High himself would show whom he chose and whom he was dealing with.
- The test was censers: take you censers, Korah and all his company, put fire therein and incense before the Lord, and the man whom the Lord doth choose, he shall be holy. Ye take too much upon you, ye sons of Levi.

**[Numbers 16:8-11](/bible/numbers/16#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/16#v8)</sup> And Moses said unto Korah, Hear, I pray you, ye sons of Levi:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/16#v9)</sup> Seemeth it but a small thing unto you, that the God of Israel hath separated you from the congregation of Israel, to bring you near to himself to do the service of the tabernacle of the Lord, and to stand before the congregation to minister unto them?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/16#v10)</sup> And he hath brought thee near to him, and all thy brethren the sons of Levi with thee: and seek ye the priesthood also?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/16#v11)</sup> For which cause both thou and all thy company are gathered together against the Lord: and what is Aaron, that ye murmur against him?

- Moses exhorted Korah: seemeth it but a small thing that the God of Israel hath separated you from the congregation to bring you near to himself, to do the service of the tabernacle? Only Levi could do the service of the tabernacle, and that was a heavy job in itself.
- You already stand before the congregation and minister unto them; the Most High is still dealing with you. But they sought the priesthood also, and for that cause Korah and all his company were gathered together against the Lord: and what is Aaron, that ye murmur against him?

**[Numbers 16:12-14](/bible/numbers/16#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/16#v12)</sup> And Moses sent to call Dathan and Abiram, the sons of Eliab: which said, We will not come up:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/16#v13)</sup> Is it a small thing that thou hast brought us up out of a land that floweth with milk and honey, to kill us in the wilderness, except thou make thyself altogether a prince over us?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/16#v14)</sup> Moreover thou hast not brought us into a land that floweth with milk and honey, or given us inheritance of fields and vineyards: wilt thou put out the eyes of these men? we will not come up.

- Dathan and Abiram would not come up when Moses called them. They were being rebellious against Moses because they wanted his spot, accusing him of bringing them out of a land flowing with milk and honey to kill them in the wilderness and of making himself a prince over them.
- Those same spirits are around today; the same thing that went on in the old time goes on now.

**[Numbers 16:15-19](/bible/numbers/16#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/16#v15)</sup> And Moses was very wroth, and said unto the Lord, Respect not thou their offering: I have not taken one ass from them, neither have I hurt one of them.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/16#v16)</sup> And Moses said unto Korah, Be thou and all thy company before the Lord, thou, and they, and Aaron, to morrow:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/16#v17)</sup> And take every man his censer, and put incense in them, and bring ye before the Lord every man his censer, two hundred and fifty censers; thou also, and Aaron, each of you his censer.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/16#v18)</sup> And they took every man his censer, and put fire in them, and laid incense thereon, and stood in the door of the tabernacle of the congregation with Moses and Aaron.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/16#v19)</sup> And Korah gathered all the congregation against them unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation: and the glory of the Lord appeared unto all the congregation.

- Moses was very wroth and said unto the Lord, respect not thou their offering: I have not taken one ass from them, neither have I hurt one of them.
- Every man took his censer, two hundred and fifty censers, with Aaron and Korah also, put fire and incense in them, and stood in the door of the tabernacle of the congregation; and Korah gathered all the congregation against Moses and Aaron, and the glory of the Lord appeared unto all the congregation.

**[Numbers 16:20-22](/bible/numbers/16#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/16#v20)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/16#v21)</sup> Separate yourselves from among this congregation, that I may consume them in a moment.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/16#v22)</sup> And they fell upon their faces, and said, O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh, shall one man sin, and wilt thou be wroth with all the congregation?

- The Lord told Moses and Aaron to separate themselves from among the congregation, that he might consume them in a moment: get away from them, because judgment is about to come.
- Moses and Aaron fell upon their faces and pleaded: O God, the God of the spirits of all flesh, shall one man sin, and wilt thou be wroth with all the congregation? They still had that love for their people and pleaded with the Lord not to deal with the whole congregation over one man's sin.

**[Numbers 16:23-27](/bible/numbers/16#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/16#v23)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/16#v24)</sup> Speak unto the congregation, saying, Get you up from about the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/16#v25)</sup> And Moses rose up and went unto Dathan and Abiram; and the elders of Israel followed him.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/16#v26)</sup> And he spake unto the congregation, saying, Depart, I pray you, from the tents of these wicked men, and touch nothing of theirs, lest ye be consumed in all their sins.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/16#v27)</sup> So they gat up from the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram, on every side: and Dathan and Abiram came out, and stood in the door of their tents, and their wives, and their sons, and their little children.

- The Lord commanded the congregation to get up from about the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram; Moses warned them to depart from the tents of these wicked men and touch nothing of theirs, lest they be consumed in all their sins and put to death in all that wickedness.
- The congregation got up from the tabernacle of Korah, Dathan, and Abiram on every side; and Dathan and Abiram came out and stood in the door of their tents with their wives, their sons, and their little children.

**[Numbers 16:28-30](/bible/numbers/16#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/16#v28)</sup> And Moses said, Hereby ye shall know that the Lord hath sent me to do all these works; for I have not done them of mine own mind.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/16#v29)</sup> If these men die the common death of all men, or if they be visited after the visitation of all men; then the Lord hath not sent me.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/16#v30)</sup> But if the Lord make a new thing, and the earth open her mouth, and swallow them up, with all that appertain unto them, and they go down quick into the pit; then ye shall understand that these men have provoked the Lord.

- Moses put the proof on the Most High: hereby ye shall know that the Lord hath sent me to do all these works, for I have not done them of mine own mind. If these men die the common death of all men, then the Lord hath not sent me; if the Most High does not do something out of the ordinary to put them to death, then the Lord had not sent him.
- But if the Lord make a new thing, and the earth open her mouth and swallow them up, and they go down quick into the pit, then ye shall understand that these men have provoked the Lord.

**[Numbers 16:31-34](/bible/numbers/16#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/16#v31)</sup> And it came to pass, as he had made an end of speaking all these words, that the ground clave asunder that was under them:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/16#v32)</sup> And the earth opened her mouth, and swallowed them up, and their houses, and all the men that appertained unto Korah, and all their goods.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/16#v33)</sup> They, and all that appertained to them, went down alive into the pit, and the earth closed upon them: and they perished from among the congregation.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/16#v34)</sup> And all Israel that were round about them fled at the cry of them: for they said, Lest the earth swallow us up also.

- As he made an end of speaking, the ground clave asunder under them, and the earth opened her mouth and swallowed them up, their houses, all the men that appertained unto Korah, and all their goods; anybody that had something to do with them went down, with all they owned.
- The earth just opened and closed upon them, and they perished from among the congregation; all Israel round about fled at the cry of them, for you could hear them crying as they were swallowed up, and they said, lest the earth swallow us up also.

**[Numbers 16:35](/bible/numbers/16#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/numbers/16#v35)</sup> And there came out a fire from the Lord, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that offered incense.

- The other two hundred and fifty men that followed them and offered incense did not escape either: there came out a fire from the Lord and consumed them.

**[Numbers 16:36-40](/bible/numbers/16#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/numbers/16#v36)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/numbers/16#v37)</sup> Speak unto Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, that he take up the censers out of the burning, and scatter thou the fire yonder; for they are hallowed.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/16#v38)</sup> The censers of these sinners against their own souls, let them make them broad plates for a covering of the altar: for they offered them before the Lord, therefore they are hallowed: and they shall be a sign unto the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/16#v39)</sup> And Eleazar the priest took the brasen censers, wherewith they that were burnt had offered; and they were made broad plates for a covering of the altar:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/numbers/16#v40)</sup> To be a memorial unto the children of Israel, that no stranger, which is not of the seed of Aaron, come near to offer incense before the Lord; that he be not as Korah, and as his company: as the Lord said to him by the hand of Moses.

- The Lord told Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest to take up the censers out of the burning, for they are hallowed, and scatter the fire yonder; the censers of these sinners against their own souls were made broad plates for a covering of the altar, because they were offered before the Lord.
- The plates were a memorial and a sign unto the children of Israel, that no stranger which is not of the seed of Aaron come near to offer incense before the Lord, that he be not as Korah and as his company.

**[Numbers 16:41-45](/bible/numbers/16#v41)**

> <sup>[41](/bible/numbers/16#v41)</sup> But on the morrow all the congregation of the children of Israel murmured against Moses and against Aaron, saying, Ye have killed the people of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/numbers/16#v42)</sup> And it came to pass, when the congregation was gathered against Moses and against Aaron, that they looked toward the tabernacle of the congregation: and, behold, the cloud covered it, and the glory of the Lord appeared.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/numbers/16#v43)</sup> And Moses and Aaron came before the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/numbers/16#v44)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/numbers/16#v45)</sup> Get you up from among this congregation, that I may consume them as in a moment. And they fell upon their faces.

- On the morrow, the very next day, all the congregation murmured against Moses and Aaron, saying, ye have killed the people of the Lord. After they had just seen the earth open up and swallow men, they still would not let it go and gathered against Moses again; that is just how rebellious we are.
- The Most High saw what was going on, and the cloud covered the tabernacle and the glory of the Lord appeared; Moses and Aaron came before the tabernacle because they knew the Most High was ready to deal with them.
- The Lord came down ready to put them to death again, saying get you up from among this congregation, that I may consume them as in a moment; and Moses and Aaron fell upon their faces, still pleading for their people because they loved them so much.

**[Numbers 16:46-48](/bible/numbers/16#v46)**

> <sup>[46](/bible/numbers/16#v46)</sup> And Moses said unto Aaron, Take a censer, and put fire therein from off the altar, and put on incense, and go quickly unto the congregation, and make an atonement for them: for there is wrath gone out from the Lord; the plague is begun.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/numbers/16#v47)</sup> And Aaron took as Moses commanded, and ran into the midst of the congregation; and, behold, the plague was begun among the people: and he put on incense, and made an atonement for the people.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/numbers/16#v48)</sup> And he stood between the dead and the living; and the plague was stayed.

- Moses told Aaron to take a censer with fire from off the altar and incense, and go quickly unto the congregation and make an atonement for them, for wrath had gone out from the Lord and the plague was begun; the Most High was already putting them to death.
- Aaron ran into the midst of the congregation, put on incense, made an atonement for the people, and stood between the dead and the living; and the plague was stayed.

**[Numbers 16:49-50](/bible/numbers/16#v49)**

> <sup>[49](/bible/numbers/16#v49)</sup> Now they that died in the plague were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, beside them that died about the matter of Korah.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/numbers/16#v50)</sup> And Aaron returned unto Moses unto the door of the tabernacle of the congregation: and the plague was stayed.

- They that died in the plague were fourteen thousand and seven hundred, beside them that died about the matter of Korah; so beside the two hundred and fifty three that died with Korah, fourteen thousand seven hundred more were put to death.

# Numbers 17: Aaron's Rod That Budded

Read the chapter → [Numbers 17](/bible/numbers/17)

**[Numbers 17:1-5](/bible/numbers/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/17#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/17#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and take of every one of them a rod according to the house of their fathers, of all their princes according to the house of their fathers twelve rods: write thou every man’s name upon his rod.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/17#v3)</sup> And thou shalt write Aaron’s name upon the rod of Levi: for one rod shall be for the head of the house of their fathers.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/17#v4)</sup> And thou shalt lay them up in the tabernacle of the congregation before the testimony, where I will meet with you.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/17#v5)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that the man’s rod, whom I shall choose, shall blossom: and I will make to cease from me the murmurings of the children of Israel, whereby they murmur against you.

- Every tribe took a rod, a stick or staff, according to the house of their fathers, twelve rods, and the prince of every tribe wrote his name upon his rod; Aaron's name was written upon the rod of Levi.
- The rods were laid up in the tabernacle of the congregation before the testimony, the ark of the covenant, where the Lord met with them; the man's rod whom the Lord chose would blossom and grow.
- By this the Most High would make the murmurings of the children of Israel to cease from him, showing them that he was dealing with Moses and Aaron and them alone.

**[Numbers 17:6-9](/bible/numbers/17#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/17#v6)</sup> And Moses spake unto the children of Israel, and every one of their princes gave him a rod apiece, for each prince one, according to their fathers’ houses, even twelve rods: and the rod of Aaron was among their rods.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/17#v7)</sup> And Moses laid up the rods before the Lord in the tabernacle of witness.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/17#v8)</sup> And it came to pass, that on the morrow Moses went into the tabernacle of witness; and, behold, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi was budded, and brought forth buds, and bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/17#v9)</sup> And Moses brought out all the rods from before the Lord unto all the children of Israel: and they looked, and took every man his rod.

- Every prince gave Moses a rod, one for each according to their fathers' houses, twelve rods, and the rod of Aaron was among them, and Moses laid them up before the Lord in the tabernacle of witness.
- On the morrow, the rod of Aaron for the house of Levi was budded, and brought forth buds, and bloomed blossoms, and yielded almonds; and every man took back his own rod.

**[Numbers 17:10-11](/bible/numbers/17#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/17#v10)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Bring Aaron’s rod again before the testimony, to be kept for a token against the rebels; and thou shalt quite take away their murmurings from me, that they die not.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/17#v11)</sup> And Moses did so: as the Lord commanded him, so did he.

- The Lord commanded Aaron's rod to be brought again before the testimony, to be kept for a token against the rebels, to take away their murmurings from the Lord, that they die not; so he would not have to put them to death again for murmuring.
- And Moses did as the Lord commanded him.

**[Numbers 17:12-13](/bible/numbers/17#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/17#v12)</sup> And the children of Israel spake unto Moses, saying, Behold, we die, we perish, we all perish.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/17#v13)</sup> Whosoever cometh any thing near unto the tabernacle of the Lord shall die: shall we be consumed with dying?

- The children of Israel cried, behold, we die, we perish, we all perish: whosoever cometh any thing near unto the tabernacle of the Lord shall die.
- You had to be of Levi to go near the tabernacle; that had to be your office. The next chapter explains who was to minister there.

# Numbers 18: The Charge and Portion of the Priests and Levites

Read the chapter → [Numbers 18](/bible/numbers/18)

**[Numbers 18:1-7](/bible/numbers/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/18#v1)</sup> And the Lord said unto Aaron, Thou and thy sons and thy father’s house with thee shall bear the iniquity of the sanctuary: and thou and thy sons with thee shall bear the iniquity of your priesthood.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/18#v2)</sup> And thy brethren also of the tribe of Levi, the tribe of thy father, bring thou with thee, that they may be joined unto thee, and minister unto thee: but thou and thy sons with thee shall minister before the tabernacle of witness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/18#v3)</sup> And they shall keep thy charge, and the charge of all the tabernacle: only they shall not come nigh the vessels of the sanctuary and the altar, that neither they, nor ye also, die.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/18#v4)</sup> And they shall be joined unto thee, and keep the charge of the tabernacle of the congregation, for all the service of the tabernacle: and a stranger shall not come nigh unto you.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/18#v5)</sup> And ye shall keep the charge of the sanctuary, and the charge of the altar: that there be no wrath any more upon the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/18#v6)</sup> And I, behold, I have taken your brethren the Levites from among the children of Israel: to you they are given as a gift for the Lord, to do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/18#v7)</sup> Therefore thou and thy sons with thee shall keep your priest’s office for every thing of the altar, and within the vail; and ye shall serve: I have given your priest’s office unto you as a service of gift: and the stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death.

- The Lord told Aaron that he and his sons and his father's house would bear the iniquity of the sanctuary and of their priesthood; that was their job. This explains who should minister in the tabernacle after Israel's cry in the last chapter.
- The tribe of Levi was joined unto Aaron to minister unto him and keep the charge of the tabernacle, their jobs; only they were not to come nigh the vessels of the sanctuary and the altar, the ark of the covenant and the different vessels, that neither they nor the priests die.
- The Levites were given to Aaron as a gift for the Lord, to do the service of the tabernacle; Aaron and his sons kept the priest's office for every thing of the altar and within the vail. The stranger that cometh nigh shall be put to death: an Israelite not of Levi could not come near the tabernacle.

**[Numbers 18:8-11](/bible/numbers/18#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/18#v8)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Aaron, Behold, I also have given thee the charge of mine heave offerings of all the hallowed things of the children of Israel; unto thee have I given them by reason of the anointing, and to thy sons, by an ordinance for ever.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/18#v9)</sup> This shall be thine of the most holy things, reserved from the fire: every oblation of theirs, every meat offering of theirs, and every sin offering of theirs, and every trespass offering of theirs, which they shall render unto me, shall be most holy for thee and for thy sons.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/18#v10)</sup> In the most holy place shalt thou eat it; every male shall eat it: it shall be holy unto thee.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/18#v11)</sup> And this is thine; the heave offering of their gift, with all the wave offerings of the children of Israel: I have given them unto thee, and to thy sons and to thy daughters with thee, by a statute for ever: every one that is clean in thy house shall eat of it.

- The Lord gave Aaron the charge of the heave offerings of all the hallowed things of the children of Israel, by reason of the anointing, to him and his sons by an ordinance for ever.
- Of the most holy things reserved from the fire, every oblation, meat offering, sin offering, and trespass offering was most holy for Aaron and his sons; the priests kept a portion of it and ate it in the most holy place.
- The heave offering of their gift, with all the wave offerings of the children of Israel, was given to Aaron and his sons and his daughters by a statute for ever; every one that is clean in the house could eat of it. This is what the children of Israel gave for the Levites.

**[Numbers 18:12-14](/bible/numbers/18#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/18#v12)</sup> All the best of the oil, and all the best of the wine, and of the wheat, the firstfruits of them which they shall offer unto the Lord, them have I given thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/18#v13)</sup> And whatsoever is first ripe in the land, which they shall bring unto the Lord, shall be thine; every one that is clean in thine house shall eat of it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/18#v14)</sup> Every thing devoted in Israel shall be thine.

- All the best of the oil, the best of the wine, and the wheat, the firstfruits which they offered unto the Lord, were given to the priests; whatsoever is first ripe in the land was theirs, and every one that was clean in the house could eat of it.
- Every thing devoted in Israel was theirs.

**[Numbers 18:15-17](/bible/numbers/18#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/18#v15)</sup> Every thing that openeth the matrix in all flesh, which they bring unto the Lord, whether it be of men or beasts, shall be thine: nevertheless the firstborn of man shalt thou surely redeem, and the firstling of unclean beasts shalt thou redeem.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/18#v16)</sup> And those that are to be redeemed from a month old shalt thou redeem, according to thine estimation, for the money of five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/18#v17)</sup> But the firstling of a cow, or the firstling of a sheep, or the firstling of a goat, thou shalt not redeem; they are holy: thou shalt sprinkle their blood upon the altar, and shalt burn their fat for an offering made by fire, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.

- Every thing that openeth the matrix in all flesh, whether of men or beasts, was the Lord's; the firstborn was to be offered. Nevertheless the firstborn of man was surely redeemed, because you would not sacrifice the firstborn of our people, and the firstling of unclean beasts was redeemed also.
- Verse 16 explains how you redeem them: those to be redeemed from a month old were redeemed for the money of five shekels, after the shekel of the sanctuary, which is twenty gerahs; twenty gerahs equal one shekel.
- But the firstling of a cow, a sheep, or a goat was not redeemed, because those you could sacrifice: they are holy; their blood was sprinkled upon the altar and their fat burnt for an offering made by fire, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.

**[Numbers 18:18-19](/bible/numbers/18#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/18#v18)</sup> And the flesh of them shall be thine, as the wave breast and as the right shoulder are thine.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/18#v19)</sup> All the heave offerings of the holy things, which the children of Israel offer unto the Lord, have I given thee, and thy sons and thy daughters with thee, by a statute for ever: it is a covenant of salt for ever before the Lord unto thee and to thy seed with thee.

- The flesh of them was the priest's, as the wave breast and as the right shoulder; all the heave offerings of the holy things which the children of Israel offered unto the Lord were given to Aaron and his sons and his daughters by a statute for ever, to the lineage of the Levites.
- [Exodus 29](/bible/exodus/29) shows how the wave offering and the heave offering were done. The wave offering was put in the hands of Aaron and his sons and waved, meaning shaken, before the Lord; then it was received of their hands and burnt upon the altar. Each had a different way it was waved or lifted up to the Most High before it was actually burnt and offered to the Lord, and the breast of the ram of consecration was waved and became the priest's part.
- It is a covenant of salt for ever before the Lord, unto Aaron and to his seed with him, the children that come after them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 29:24-28](/bible/exodus/29#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/exodus/29#v24)</sup> And thou shalt put all in the hands of Aaron, and in the hands of his sons; and shalt wave them for a wave offering before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/29#v25)</sup> And thou shalt receive them of their hands, and burn them upon the altar for a burnt offering, for a sweet savour before the Lord: it is an offering made by fire unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/29#v26)</sup> And thou shalt take the breast of the ram of Aaron’s consecration, and wave it for a wave offering before the Lord: and it shall be thy part.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/exodus/29#v27)</sup> And thou shalt sanctify the breast of the wave offering, and the shoulder of the heave offering, which is waved, and which is heaved up, of the ram of the consecration, even of that which is for Aaron, and of that which is for his sons:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/29#v28)</sup> And it shall be Aaron’s and his sons’ by a statute for ever from the children of Israel: for it is an heave offering: and it shall be an heave offering from the children of Israel of the sacrifice of their peace offerings, even their heave offering unto the Lord.

    The wave offering was waved in the hands of Aaron and his sons before the Lord and then burnt on the altar, and the wave breast and heave shoulder were the priests' part from the children of Israel for ever.

**[Numbers 18:20-24](/bible/numbers/18#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/18#v20)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Aaron, Thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither shalt thou have any part among them: I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/18#v21)</sup> And, behold, I have given the children of Levi all the tenth in Israel for an inheritance, for their service which they serve, even the service of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/18#v22)</sup> Neither must the children of Israel henceforth come nigh the tabernacle of the congregation, lest they bear sin, and die.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/18#v23)</sup> But the Levites shall do the service of the tabernacle of the congregation, and they shall bear their iniquity: it shall be a statute for ever throughout your generations, that among the children of Israel they have no inheritance.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/18#v24)</sup> But the tithes of the children of Israel, which they offer as an heave offering unto the Lord, I have given to the Levites to inherit: therefore I have said unto them, Among the children of Israel they shall have no inheritance.

- The Lord told Aaron: thou shalt have no inheritance in their land, neither any part among them; I am thy part and thine inheritance among the children of Israel. The Levites had no inheritance of land to pass down; the Most High said, I will be taking care of you.
- The Lord gave the children of Levi all the tenth in Israel for an inheritance. This is the tithe: the tenth part was given to the Levites for their service which they served, the service of the tabernacle of the congregation, their sacrificing and teaching of the laws.
- That tenth went to the children of Levi, and it was done away with when Christ came and died and took that priesthood; we no longer give that tenth to Levi.
- The children of Israel were not to come nigh the tabernacle of the congregation, lest they bear sin and die; it was a sin for the other tribes of Israel to come near the tabernacle, on pain of death. All the work to be done in the tabernacle was committed unto the Levites, and they bore their iniquity, a statute for ever.

**[Numbers 18:25-32](/bible/numbers/18#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/18#v25)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/18#v26)</sup> Thus speak unto the Levites, and say unto them, When ye take of the children of Israel the tithes which I have given you from them for your inheritance, then ye shall offer up an heave offering of it for the Lord, even a tenth part of the tithe.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/18#v27)</sup> And this your heave offering shall be reckoned unto you, as though it were the corn of the threshingfloor, and as the fulness of the winepress.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/18#v28)</sup> Thus ye also shall offer an heave offering unto the Lord of all your tithes, which ye receive of the children of Israel; and ye shall give thereof the Lord’s heave offering to Aaron the priest.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/18#v29)</sup> Out of all your gifts ye shall offer every heave offering of the Lord, of all the best thereof, even the hallowed part thereof out of it.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/18#v30)</sup> Therefore thou shalt say unto them, When ye have heaved the best thereof from it, then it shall be counted unto the Levites as the increase of the threshingfloor, and as the increase of the winepress.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/18#v31)</sup> And ye shall eat it in every place, ye and your households: for it is your reward for your service in the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/18#v32)</sup> And ye shall bear no sin by reason of it, when ye have heaved from it the best of it: neither shall ye pollute the holy things of the children of Israel, lest ye die.

- When the Levites took of the children of Israel the tithes given them for their inheritance, they offered up of it an heave offering for the Lord, even a tenth part of the tithe: after the Levites got their tenth, the Most High wanted his tenth part of that, which they had to offer up.
- Their heave offering was reckoned unto them as though it were the corn of the threshingfloor and the fulness of the winepress, and the Lord's heave offering out of all their tithes was given to Aaron the priest; out of all their gifts they offered every heave offering of the Lord, of all the best thereof, even the hallowed part.
- When they had heaved the best thereof from it, they could eat it in every place, they and their households, for it was their reward for their service in the tabernacle of the congregation; and they bore no sin by reason of it when they heaved the best of it, neither polluted the holy things of the children of Israel, lest they die.
