## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 27](/bible/numbers/27) through 30: the inheritance won by the daughters of Zelophehad, the ordination of Joshua to lead after Moses, the continual burnt offering and the offerings of every sabbath, new moon, and feast, the high holy days of the seventh month, and the law of vows. To answer the inheritance question the whole of [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36) was also read, precept upon precept, and the Q&A after the reading is folded into the verses it belongs to.

**[Numbers 27:1-4](/bible/numbers/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/27#v1)</sup> Then came the daughters of Zelophehad, the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, of the families of Manasseh the son of Joseph: and these are the names of his daughters; Mahlah, Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/27#v2)</sup> And they stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the princes and all the congregation, by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/27#v3)</sup> Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the company of them that gathered themselves together against the Lord in the company of Korah; but died in his own sin, and had no sons.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/27#v4)</sup> Why should the name of our father be done away from among his family, because he hath no son? Give unto us therefore a possession among the brethren of our father.

- The daughters of Zelophehad, of the tribe of Manasseh, stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the princes, and all the congregation at the door of the tabernacle.
- Their father died in the wilderness in his own sin; he was not in the company of Korah that gathered together against the Lord. He had no sons, only these five daughters.
- Their question was the question of the lineage: since the man puts the seed in the woman, if a man had only daughters, why should his name be done away from among his family? So they asked for a possession among the brethren of their father.

**[Numbers 27:5-7](/bible/numbers/27#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/27#v5)</sup> And Moses brought their cause before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/27#v6)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/27#v7)</sup> The daughters of Zelophehad speak right: thou shalt surely give them a possession of an inheritance among their father’s brethren; and thou shalt cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.

- Moses brought their cause before the Lord. He did not do his own thing; he sought counsel and asked the Most High what to do in this instance.
- The Lord said the daughters of Zelophehad speak right: give them a possession of an inheritance among their father's brethren, and cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.

**[Numbers 27:8-11](/bible/numbers/27#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/27#v8)</sup> And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a man die, and have no son, then ye shall cause his inheritance to pass unto his daughter.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/27#v9)</sup> And if he have no daughter, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his brethren.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/27#v10)</sup> And if he have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his father’s brethren.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/27#v11)</sup> And if his father have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his kinsman that is next to him of his family, and he shall possess it: and it shall be unto the children of Israel a statute of judgment, as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Here the Lord set the order of inheritance for all Israel: if a man die and have no son, it passes to his daughter; if no daughter, to his brethren; if no brethren, to his father's brethren; and if his father had no brethren, to the kinsman that is next to him of his family. Every angle was covered so the inheritance stayed within the tribe.
- Israel always had questions, just as we do when we read, and the Lord put these statutes in place to answer them before they were asked.
- But what if a daughter who received the inheritance married into another tribe? You have to go precept upon precept: [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36) takes up that very case with these same daughters. The chief fathers of Gilead brought it to Moses, and the word came back that the daughters of Zelophehad may marry to whom they think best, only to the family of the tribe of their father shall they marry.

- A sister helping to build up other sisters new in the congregation is right, and the sisters bless the nation with real talents, raising the children in the commandments, and the aged women teaching the young women. But teaching and leading the nation is not the place the Most High set for her.

# Numbers 28: The Continual Burnt Offering and the Offerings of the Feasts

>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/28#v5)</sup> And a tenth part of an ephah of flour for a meat offering, mingled with the fourth part of an hin of beaten oil.

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/28#v6)</sup> It is a continual burnt offering, which was ordained in mount Sinai for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord.

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/28#v8)</sup> And the other lamb shalt thou offer at even: as the meat offering of the morning, and as the drink offering thereof, thou shalt offer it, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

- The Lord commanded the offerings made by fire to be observed in their due season: two lambs of the first year without spot, day by day, one lamb in the morning and the other lamb at even, a continual burnt offering which was ordained in mount Sinai.
- With it went a meat offering of a tenth deal of flour mingled with the fourth part of an hin of beaten oil, and a drink offering of the fourth part of an hin; in the holy place the strong wine was poured unto the Lord.
- The fire and the sacrifice continued morning and evening; it burned through the day and through the night, every single day.

**[Numbers 28:9-10](/bible/numbers/28#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/28#v9)</sup> And on the sabbath day two lambs of the first year without spot, and two tenth deals of flour for a meat offering, mingled with oil, and the drink offering thereof:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/28#v10)</sup> This is the burnt offering of every sabbath, beside the continual burnt offering, and his drink offering.

- On the sabbath day two more lambs were offered, with their meat and drink offerings, the burnt offering of every sabbath, beside the continual burnt offering. That is four lambs every sabbath, with the drink offerings attached.
- Precept upon precept this answers what Christ told the Pharisees: the priests in the temple profane the sabbath and are blameless. Israel did not kindle fire for themselves on the sabbath, but this altar work was the ordinance of the Lord for every sabbath day.
- This is also why Christ had to come. In captivity we have no lambs, no farms, no temple, no most holy place, no veil, no high priest; the handwriting of ordinances that was against us was taken out of the way and nailed to his cross. Those who deny Christ and claim the Old Testament only are not keeping these sacrifices, and without Christ the whole law, drink offering to meat offering, still stands over them.

- To afflict your soul is to fast. The fast the Lord has chosen is a day for a man to afflict his soul, to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, to let the oppressed go free, and to break every yoke; that is why we still fast on this day once a year, and why we go out and teach on the day of atonement.
- We fast without the sacrifices, because the atonement is now Christ. He was brought as a lamb to the slaughter, wounded for our transgressions, and the Lord laid on him the iniquity of us all, just as the priest laid the sins of the nation on the sacrifice of [Leviticus 16](/bible/leviticus/16); that is why the veil rent in two when he died, and why John said, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.

- If a man vow a vow unto the Lord, or swear an oath to bind his soul with a bond, he shall not break his word; he shall do according to all that proceedeth out of his mouth.
- A vow is promising the Lord you will do a thing. Say a man vows, Lord, if you bring this to pass I will never eat swine again; he must keep that vow, and he cannot go back on it and simply ask forgiveness afterward.
- If thou shalt forbear to vow, it shall be no sin in thee; but that which is gone out of thy lips thou shalt keep and perform. Do not suffer thy mouth to cause thy flesh to sin, and do not say before the angel that it was an error.

That shows the power the Most High put in the man of the house: a father can hear his daughter bind herself and say no, and the Lord releases her.

**[Numbers 30:6-8](/bible/numbers/30#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/30#v6)</sup> And if she had at all an husband, when she vowed, or uttered ought out of her lips, wherewith she bound her soul;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/30#v7)</sup> And her husband heard it, and held his peace at her in the day that he heard it: then her vows shall stand, and her bonds wherewith she bound her soul shall stand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/30#v8)</sup> But if her husband disallowed her on the day that he heard it; then he shall make her vow which she vowed, and that which she uttered with her lips, wherewith she bound her soul, of none effect: and the Lord shall forgive her.

- The same covering passes to the husband. If a woman with a husband vows, or utters ought out of her lips wherewith she binds her soul, and her husband hears it and holds his peace in the day that he hears it, her vows stand.
- But if her husband disallow her on the day that he heard it, he makes her vow of none effect, and the Lord shall forgive her. A wife could be binding herself to something she cannot perform, and her husband can step in and say, Lord, do not hear that vow, and the Most High releases her because of her husband.
- That is the power of the head of the house: the husband keeping the commandments stands between his wife and a bond that would jack her up. That is a reason to love your hedge, not to despise it.

**[Numbers 30:9](/bible/numbers/30#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/30#v9)</sup> But every vow of a widow, and of her that is divorced, wherewith they have bound their souls, shall stand against her.

- Every vow of a widow, and of her that is divorced, wherewith they have bound their souls, shall stand against her. She has no father or husband to disallow it, so what she vows she must perform.
- The husband is a hedge of protection; where no hedge is, the possession is spoiled. The married woman has a covering that the widow and the divorced woman do not have.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 36:25](/bible/sirach/36#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/36#v25)</sup> Where no hedge is, there the possession is spoiled: and he that hath no wife will wander up and down mourning.

    Where no hedge is, the possession is spoiled; the covering of the house is the hedge.

**[Numbers 30:10-15](/bible/numbers/30#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/30#v10)</sup> And if she vowed in her husband’s house, or bound her soul by a bond with an oath;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/30#v11)</sup> And her husband heard it, and held his peace at her, and disallowed her not: then all her vows shall stand, and every bond wherewith she bound her soul shall stand.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/30#v12)</sup> But if her husband hath utterly made them void on the day he heard them; then whatsoever proceeded out of her lips concerning her vows, or concerning the bond of her soul, shall not stand: her husband hath made them void; and the Lord shall forgive her.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/30#v13)</sup> Every vow, and every binding oath to afflict the soul, her husband may establish it, or her husband may make it void.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/30#v14)</sup> But if her husband altogether hold his peace at her from day to day; then he establisheth all her vows, or all her bonds, which are upon her: he confirmeth them, because he held his peace at her in the day that he heard them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/30#v15)</sup> But if he shall any ways make them void after that he hath heard them; then he shall bear her iniquity.

- If a wife vowed in her husband's house and her husband heard it and held his peace and disallowed her not, then all her vows stand. Her husband may establish every vow and every binding oath to afflict the soul, or her husband may make it void.
- If he altogether holds his peace at her from day to day, then he establishes all her vows and confirms all her bonds, because he held his peace in the day that he heard them. Holding your peace is confirming it.
- But if he shall any ways make them void after that he hath heard them, then he shall bear her iniquity. He cannot hear her vow, hold his peace day after day, and then come later to void it; he had the power to stop it and did not, so the iniquity falls to him. And this runs one way: a wife cannot disallow or nullify her husband's vow.
- This is the order of the house: the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church, and he is the saviour of the body; so let the wives be subject to their own husbands in every thing, even in what is vowed in prayer.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 5:22-24](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/ephesians/5#v24)</sup> Therefore as the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing.

    The husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church; therefore let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing.

**[Numbers 30:16](/bible/numbers/30#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/30#v16)</sup> These are the statutes, which the Lord commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, between the father and his daughter, being yet in her youth in her father’s house.

- These are the statutes which the Lord commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, and between the father and his daughter, being yet in her youth in her father's house. The whole chapter is the government of vows inside the house of Israel.
