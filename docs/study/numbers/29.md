**[Numbers 29:1-6](/bible/numbers/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/29#v1)</sup> And in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work: it is a day of blowing the trumpets unto you.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/29#v2)</sup> And ye shall offer a burnt offering for a sweet savour unto the Lord; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year without blemish:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/29#v3)</sup> And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/29#v4)</sup> And one tenth deal for one lamb, throughout the seven lambs:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/29#v5)</sup> And one kid of the goats for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/29#v6)</sup> Beside the burnt offering of the month, and his meat offering, and the daily burnt offering, and his meat offering, and their drink offerings, according unto their manner, for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord.

- The first day of the seventh month is an holy convocation, a day of blowing the trumpets, the memorial of blowing of trumpets: no servile work, and a burnt offering of one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year without blemish, with their meat offerings and a kid of the goats for a sin offering.
- That day is also the new moon, so the offering of the trumpets came beside the burnt offering of the month, the daily burnt offering, and their meat and drink offerings. In a span of fifteen days this month holds the memorial of blowing of trumpets coupled with the new moon, the day of atonement, and the feast of tabernacles.

**[Numbers 29:7-11](/bible/numbers/29#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/29#v7)</sup> And ye shall have on the tenth day of this seventh month an holy convocation; and ye shall afflict your souls: ye shall not do any work therein:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/29#v8)</sup> But ye shall offer a burnt offering unto the Lord for a sweet savour; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year; they shall be unto you without blemish:

>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/29#v11)</sup> One kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the sin offering of atonement, and the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering of it, and their drink offerings.

- The tenth day of the seventh month is an holy convocation, a large gathering, wherein ye shall afflict your souls and do no work at all: the day of atonement, instituted as a statute for ever, when the priest made an atonement to cleanse the people from all their sins before the Lord.
- To afflict your soul is to fast. The fast the Lord has chosen is a day for a man to afflict his soul, to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, to let the oppressed go free, and to break every yoke; that is why we still fast on this day once a year, and why we go out and teach on the day of atonement.
- We fast without the sacrifices, because the atonement is now Christ. He was brought as a lamb to the slaughter, wounded for our transgressions, and the Lord laid on him the iniquity of us all, just as the priest laid the sins of the nation on the sacrifice of [Leviticus 16](/bible/leviticus/16); that is why the veil rent in two when he died, and why John said, Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world.
- [Isaiah 53](/bible/isaiah/53) cannot be speaking of David, who was already dead in Isaiah's day; it speaks of Jesus Christ, cut off out of the land of the living for the transgression of the people, the ultimate sacrifice.

**[Numbers 29:12-16](/bible/numbers/29#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/29#v12)</sup> And on the fifteenth day of the seventh month ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work, and ye shall keep a feast unto the Lord seven days:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/29#v13)</sup> And ye shall offer a burnt offering, a sacrifice made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord; thirteen young bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year; they shall be without blemish:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/29#v14)</sup> And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals unto every bullock of the thirteen bullocks, two tenth deals to each ram of the two rams,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/29#v15)</sup> And a several tenth deal to each lamb of the fourteen lambs:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/29#v16)</sup> And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, his meat offering, and his drink offering.

- The fifteenth day of the seventh month is an holy convocation, the feast of tabernacles: no servile work, and a feast kept unto the Lord seven days.
- The first day's offering was thirteen young bullocks, two rams, and fourteen lambs of the first year without blemish, with their meat offerings by number and a kid of the goats for a sin offering, beside the continual burnt offering.
- [Leviticus 23](/bible/leviticus/23) shows what this feast is: all that are Israelites born shall dwell in booths seven days, that your generations may know that the Most High made the children of Israel to dwell in booths when he brought them out of the land of Egypt. That is why once a year, around October, you will see us out in the wilderness in tents, eight days counting the solemn assembly, those of us able to get the time off; all our feast days are memorials of what the Lord did for us.

**[Numbers 29:17-19](/bible/numbers/29#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/29#v17)</sup> And on the second day ye shall offer twelve young bullocks, two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without spot:
>

>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/29#v19)</sup> And one kid of the goats for a sin offering; beside the continual burnt offering, and the meat offering thereof, and their drink offerings.

- On the second day the count came down to twelve young bullocks, with the same two rams, fourteen lambs of the first year without spot, their meat and drink offerings by number, and a goat for a sin offering, beside the continual burnt offering.
- The pattern descends a bullock each day: eleven on the third day, ten on the fourth, nine on the fifth, eight on the sixth, and seven on the seventh day, every day with two rams, fourteen lambs, the meat and drink offerings after the manner, and the goat for a sin offering, all beside the continual burnt offering that never stopped.

**[Numbers 29:39-40](/bible/numbers/29#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/29#v39)</sup> These things ye shall do unto the Lord in your set feasts, beside your vows, and your freewill offerings, for your burnt offerings, and for your meat offerings, and for your drink offerings, and for your peace offerings.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/numbers/29#v40)</sup> And Moses told the children of Israel according to all that the Lord commanded Moses.

- All these sacrifices were for the set feasts of the Lord, and they came beside your vows and your freewill offerings, which carried sacrifices of their own. This is why Paul wrote, let no man judge you in meat or in drink; the feast days of the Lord were wrapped in offerings from the sabbath to the solemn assemblies.
- What is left for us is better: present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. Keeping the feast days, telling your employer months ahead that you need the day of atonement or the tabernacles off, and helping supply the feasts is presenting the body a living sacrifice.
- He that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough; to depart from wickedness pleases the Lord, and to forsake unrighteousness is a propitiation. The prophets showed before that Christ should suffer, and he hath so fulfilled it, doing away with the law of animal sacrifice.
- If ye love him, keep his commandments, and he will send the Comforter, the Spirit of truth, which the world cannot receive; the Holy Ghost teaches all things and brings all things to remembrance, and that spirit is how this truth is understood in these last days.
