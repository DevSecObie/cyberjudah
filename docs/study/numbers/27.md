## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 27](/bible/numbers/27) through 30: the inheritance won by the daughters of Zelophehad, the ordination of Joshua to lead after Moses, the continual burnt offering and the offerings of every sabbath, new moon, and feast, the high holy days of the seventh month, and the law of vows. To answer the inheritance question the whole of [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36) was also read, precept upon precept, and the Q&A after the reading is folded into the verses it belongs to.

**[Numbers 27:1-4](/bible/numbers/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/27#v1)</sup> Then came the daughters of Zelophehad, the son of Hepher, the son of Gilead, the son of Machir, the son of Manasseh, of the families of Manasseh the son of Joseph: and these are the names of his daughters; Mahlah, Noah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Tirzah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/27#v2)</sup> And they stood before Moses, and before Eleazar the priest, and before the princes and all the congregation, by the door of the tabernacle of the congregation, saying,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/27#v3)</sup> Our father died in the wilderness, and he was not in the company of them that gathered themselves together against the Lord in the company of Korah; but died in his own sin, and had no sons.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/27#v4)</sup> Why should the name of our father be done away from among his family, because he hath no son? Give unto us therefore a possession among the brethren of our father.

- The daughters of Zelophehad, of the tribe of Manasseh, stood before Moses, Eleazar the priest, the princes, and all the congregation at the door of the tabernacle.
- Their father died in the wilderness in his own sin; he was not in the company of Korah that gathered together against the Lord. He had no sons, only these five daughters.
- Their question was the question of the lineage: since the man puts the seed in the woman, if a man had only daughters, why should his name be done away from among his family? So they asked for a possession among the brethren of their father.

**[Numbers 27:5-7](/bible/numbers/27#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/27#v5)</sup> And Moses brought their cause before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/27#v6)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/27#v7)</sup> The daughters of Zelophehad speak right: thou shalt surely give them a possession of an inheritance among their father’s brethren; and thou shalt cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.

- Moses brought their cause before the Lord. He did not do his own thing; he sought counsel and asked the Most High what to do in this instance.
- The Lord said the daughters of Zelophehad speak right: give them a possession of an inheritance among their father's brethren, and cause the inheritance of their father to pass unto them.

**[Numbers 27:8-11](/bible/numbers/27#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/27#v8)</sup> And thou shalt speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a man die, and have no son, then ye shall cause his inheritance to pass unto his daughter.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/27#v9)</sup> And if he have no daughter, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his brethren.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/27#v10)</sup> And if he have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his father’s brethren.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/27#v11)</sup> And if his father have no brethren, then ye shall give his inheritance unto his kinsman that is next to him of his family, and he shall possess it: and it shall be unto the children of Israel a statute of judgment, as the Lord commanded Moses.

- Here the Lord set the order of inheritance for all Israel: if a man die and have no son, it passes to his daughter; if no daughter, to his brethren; if no brethren, to his father's brethren; and if his father had no brethren, to the kinsman that is next to him of his family. Every angle was covered so the inheritance stayed within the tribe.
- Israel always had questions, just as we do when we read, and the Lord put these statutes in place to answer them before they were asked.
- But what if a daughter who received the inheritance married into another tribe? You have to go precept upon precept: [Numbers 36](/bible/numbers/36) takes up that very case with these same daughters. The chief fathers of Gilead brought it to Moses, and the word came back that the daughters of Zelophehad may marry to whom they think best, only to the family of the tribe of their father shall they marry.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 36:6-7](/bible/numbers/36#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/36#v6)</sup> This is the thing which the Lord doth command concerning the daughters of Zelophehad, saying, Let them marry to whom they think best; only to the family of the tribe of their father shall they marry.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/36#v7)</sup> So shall not the inheritance of the children of Israel remove from tribe to tribe: for every one of the children of Israel shall keep himself to the inheritance of the tribe of his fathers.

    Let them marry to whom they think best, only within the tribe of their father, so the inheritance does not remove from tribe to tribe.
  - **[Numbers 36:8-9](/bible/numbers/36#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/36#v8)</sup> And every daughter, that possesseth an inheritance in any tribe of the children of Israel, shall be wife unto one of the family of the tribe of her father, that the children of Israel may enjoy every man the inheritance of his fathers.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/36#v9)</sup> Neither shall the inheritance remove from one tribe to another tribe; but every one of the tribes of the children of Israel shall keep himself to his own inheritance.

    Every daughter that possesses an inheritance shall be wife unto one of the family of the tribe of her father, that every man enjoy the inheritance of his fathers.
  - **[Numbers 36:10-12](/bible/numbers/36#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/36#v10)</sup> Even as the Lord commanded Moses, so did the daughters of Zelophehad:
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/36#v11)</sup> For Mahlah, Tirzah, and Hoglah, and Milcah, and Noah, the daughters of Zelophehad, were married unto their father’s brothers’ sons:
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/36#v12)</sup> And they were married into the families of the sons of Manasseh the son of Joseph, and their inheritance remained in the tribe of the family of their father.

    The daughters of Zelophehad were married unto their father's brothers' sons, and the inheritance remained in the tribe of the family of their father.

- This is not saying no one ever married from Judah to Benjamin or Levi to Ephraim; it is showing that where an inheritance rode on the marriage, the woman married within her father's tribe.
- It also goes into marriage in the nation as a whole: there was never a case where an inheritance in Israel passed to an Edomite, an Ammonite, or a Moabite. The fathers from the beginning took wives of their own kindred, and interracial marriage is not of the scriptures.

  Precepts:
  - **[Tobit 4:12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)</sup> Beware of all whoredom, my son, and chiefly take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, and take not a strange woman to wife, which is not of thy father’s tribe: for we are the children of the prophets, Noe, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: remember, my son, that our fathers from the beginning, even that they all married wives of their own kindred, and were blessed in their children, and their seed shall inherit the land.

    Chiefly take a wife of the seed of thy fathers; our fathers from the beginning all married wives of their own kindred, and were blessed in their children.

**[Numbers 27:12-14](/bible/numbers/27#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/27#v12)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Get thee up into this mount Abarim, and see the land which I have given unto the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/27#v13)</sup> And when thou hast seen it, thou also shalt be gathered unto thy people, as Aaron thy brother was gathered.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/27#v14)</sup> For ye rebelled against my commandment in the desert of Zin, in the strife of the congregation, to sanctify me at the water before their eyes: that is the water of Meribah in Kadesh in the wilderness of Zin.

- The Lord told Moses to get up into mount Abarim and see the land given to the children of Israel; he would see it but not enter it, and then be gathered unto his people as Aaron his brother was gathered.
- The reason is written: Moses rebelled against the commandment of the Most High in the desert of Zin at the water of Meribah. The Lord gave a specific commandment to speak unto the rock before their eyes, and Moses instead smote the rock twice; the water still came out abundantly, but Moses and Aaron did not sanctify the Lord in the eyes of the children of Israel, and for that neither of them brought the congregation into the land.

  Precepts:
  - **[Numbers 20:7-12](/bible/numbers/20#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/20#v7)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/20#v8)</sup> Take the rod, and gather thou the assembly together, thou, and Aaron thy brother, and speak ye unto the rock before their eyes; and it shall give forth his water, and thou shalt bring forth to them water out of the rock: so thou shalt give the congregation and their beasts drink.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/20#v9)</sup> And Moses took the rod from before the Lord, as he commanded him.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/20#v10)</sup> And Moses and Aaron gathered the congregation together before the rock, and he said unto them, Hear now, ye rebels; must we fetch you water out of this rock?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/20#v11)</sup> And Moses lifted up his hand, and with his rod he smote the rock twice: and the water came out abundantly, and the congregation drank, and their beasts also.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/20#v12)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and Aaron, Because ye believed me not, to sanctify me in the eyes of the children of Israel, therefore ye shall not bring this congregation into the land which I have given them.

    The commandment was to speak unto the rock; Moses smote it twice, and because they believed not, to sanctify the Lord in the eyes of Israel, they did not bring the congregation into the land.

**[Numbers 27:15-17](/bible/numbers/27#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/27#v15)</sup> And Moses spake unto the Lord, saying,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/27#v16)</sup> Let the Lord, the God of the spirits of all flesh, set a man over the congregation,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/27#v17)</sup> Which may go out before them, and which may go in before them, and which may lead them out, and which may bring them in; that the congregation of the Lord be not as sheep which have no shepherd.

- Before his death Moses asked the Lord to set a man over the congregation, to go out before them and come in before them, to lead them out and bring them in, that the congregation of the Lord be not as sheep which have no shepherd.
- Moses asked for a man because the leadership of the nation belongs to men. The woman was made to be an help meet unto the man, not to rule over him; she is the weaker vessel, and the congregation is not set up for her to lead it.
- A sister helping to build up other sisters new in the congregation is right, and the sisters bless the nation with real talents, raising the children in the commandments, and the aged women teaching the young women. But teaching and leading the nation is not the place the Most High set for her.

    Children are their oppressors, and women rule over them; that condition is a mark of a people gone astray, not the order of the Most High.
  - **[1 Timothy 2:11-14](/bible/1-timothy/2#v11)**

    > <sup>[13](/bible/1-timothy/2#v13)</sup> For Adam was first formed, then Eve.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/1-timothy/2#v14)</sup> And Adam was not deceived, but the woman being deceived was in the transgression.

    I suffer not a woman to teach, nor to usurp authority over the man; for Adam was first formed, then Eve, and the woman being deceived was in the transgression.
  - **[Ezekiel 34:31](/bible/ezekiel/34#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/ezekiel/34#v31)</sup> And ye my flock, the flock of my pasture, are men, and I am your God, saith the Lord God.

    The flock of my pasture are men; the shepherds and saviors of the nation are men.
  - **[Proverbs 8:4](/bible/proverbs/8#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/8#v4)</sup> Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.

    Unto you, O men, I call; and my voice is to the sons of man.
  - **[Titus 2:3-5](/bible/titus/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/titus/2#v3)</sup> The aged women likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/titus/2#v4)</sup> That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/titus/2#v5)</sup> To be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

    The aged women are to be teachers of good things, that they may teach the young women to love their husbands and children, to be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, obedient to their own husbands.

**[Numbers 27:18-21](/bible/numbers/27#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/27#v18)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and lay thine hand upon him;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/27#v19)</sup> And set him before Eleazar the priest, and before all the congregation; and give him a charge in their sight.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/27#v20)</sup> And thou shalt put some of thine honour upon him, that all the congregation of the children of Israel may be obedient.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/27#v21)</sup> And he shall stand before Eleazar the priest, who shall ask counsel for him after the judgment of Urim before the Lord: at his word shall they go out, and at his word they shall come in, both he, and all the children of Israel with him, even all the congregation.

- The Lord answered: take thee Joshua the son of Nun, a man in whom is the spirit, and lay thine hand upon him.
- Joshua was set before Eleazar the priest and before all the congregation and given his charge in their sight, and some of Moses' honour was put upon him, so all Israel could see with their own eyes the man the Most High ordained to lead the nation after the death of Moses.
- Joshua was to stand before Eleazar the priest, who would ask counsel for him after the judgment of Urim before the Lord; at his word they would go out and come in, he and all the children of Israel with him.
