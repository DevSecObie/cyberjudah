**[Numbers 10:1-4](/bible/numbers/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/10#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/10#v2)</sup> Make thee two trumpets of silver; of a whole piece shalt thou make them: that thou mayest use them for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying of the camps.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/10#v3)</sup> And when they shall blow with them, all the assembly shall assemble themselves to thee at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/10#v4)</sup> And if they blow but with one trumpet, then the princes, which are heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather themselves unto thee.

- Two trumpets of silver, of a whole piece, were made for the calling of the assembly and for the journeying of the camps. When they blew with both, all the assembly gathered to the door of the tabernacle of the congregation; when they blew but with one, the princes, the heads of the thousands of Israel, gathered.
- There were different ways the Levites had to blow the trumpets: one way to call the whole congregation, another for just the leaders, and another to let everybody know it was war.

**[Numbers 10:5-6](/bible/numbers/10#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/10#v5)</sup> When ye blow an alarm, then the camps that lie on the east parts shall go forward.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/10#v6)</sup> When ye blow an alarm the second time, then the camps that lie on the south side shall take their journey: they shall blow an alarm for their journeys.

- When ye blow an alarm, the camps that lie on the east parts go forward first: that was Judah, Issachar, and Zebulun, under the standard of the camp of Judah.
- When the alarm is blown the second time, the camps that lie on the south side take their journey: that was Reuben, Simeon, and Gad. The alarm for the journeys was short bursts.

**[Numbers 10:7-9](/bible/numbers/10#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/10#v7)</sup> But when the congregation is to be gathered together, ye shall blow, but ye shall not sound an alarm.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/10#v8)</sup> And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow with the trumpets; and they shall be to you for an ordinance for ever throughout your generations.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/10#v9)</sup> And if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets; and ye shall be remembered before the Lord your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.

- When the congregation is to be gathered together, ye shall blow, but ye shall not sound an alarm: a longer sound, a different tone from the short alarm bursts, letting everybody know this is the gathering.
- If the trumpet give an uncertain sound, who shall prepare himself to the battle? The sound had to be certain, or people would sit still or try to gather when it was time to go to war.
- The sons of Aaron the priests blew the trumpets, an ordinance for ever throughout your generations. In war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, blow an alarm, and ye shall be remembered before the Lord your God and saved from your enemies. As Israel marched through the wilderness against the nations, the Most High remembered them and destroyed the enemy, as long as they were keeping the commandments.

**[Numbers 10:10](/bible/numbers/10#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/10#v10)</sup> Also in the day of your gladness, and in your solemn days, and in the beginnings of your months, ye shall blow with the trumpets over your burnt offerings, and over the sacrifices of your peace offerings; that they may be to you for a memorial before your God: I am the Lord your God.

- The trumpets were also blown in the day of your gladness, in your solemn days, and in the beginnings of your months, over the burnt offerings and the sacrifices of the peace offerings, for a memorial before your God.
- The feast days must be a glad moment even to this day. Coming to congregate with your brothers and sisters grudgingly is one of the reasons Israel went into captivity: we were not glad to do what the Most High said.
- The beginnings of the months are marked by the moon: the Most High made the moon to serve in her season for a declaration of times, a light that decreaseth in her perfection and then increaseth in her changing, and the month is called after her name. When the light is full again, that is the new moon.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 43:6-8](/bible/sirach/43#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/43#v6)</sup> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/43#v7)</sup> From the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/43#v8)</sup> The month is called after her name, increasing wonderfully in her changing, being an instrument of the armies above, shining in the firmament of heaven;

    The moon declares the times and seasons and is the sign of feasts; her cycle marks the beginnings of the months.

**[Numbers 10:11-13](/bible/numbers/10#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/10#v11)</sup> And it came to pass on the twentieth day of the second month, in the second year, that the cloud was taken up from off the tabernacle of the testimony.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/10#v12)</sup> And the children of Israel took their journeys out of the wilderness of Sinai; and the cloud rested in the wilderness of Paran.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/10#v13)</sup> And they first took their journey according to the commandment of the Lord by the hand of Moses.

- On the twentieth day of the second month in the second year the cloud was taken up from off the tabernacle of the testimony, and the children of Israel took their journeys out of the wilderness of Sinai; and the cloud rested in the wilderness of Paran.
- The testimony is the commandments of the Most High. If the children keep his covenant and his testimony, their children shall sit upon the throne for evermore; and it is his testimony, not our own testimony, that is righteous and very faithful and that changes the people.

**[Numbers 10:14-28](/bible/numbers/10#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/10#v14)</sup> In the first place went the standard of the camp of the children of Judah according to their armies: and over his host was Nahshon the son of Amminadab.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/10#v15)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Issachar was Nethaneel the son of Zuar.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/10#v16)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Zebulun was Eliab the son of Helon.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/10#v17)</sup> And the tabernacle was taken down; and the sons of Gershon and the sons of Merari set forward, bearing the tabernacle.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/10#v18)</sup> And the standard of the camp of Reuben set forward according to their armies: and over his host was Elizur the son of Shedeur.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/10#v19)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Simeon was Shelumiel the son of Zurishaddai.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/10#v20)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Gad was Eliasaph the son of Deuel.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/10#v21)</sup> And the Kohathites set forward, bearing the sanctuary: and the other did set up the tabernacle against they came.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/10#v22)</sup> And the standard of the camp of the children of Ephraim set forward according to their armies: and over his host was Elishama the son of Ammihud.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/10#v23)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Manasseh was Gamaliel the son of Pedahzur.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/10#v24)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Benjamin was Abidan the son of Gideoni.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/10#v25)</sup> And the standard of the camp of the children of Dan set forward, which was the rereward of all the camps throughout their hosts: and over his host was Ahiezer the son of Ammishaddai.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/10#v26)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Asher was Pagiel the son of Ocran.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/10#v27)</sup> And over the host of the tribe of the children of Naphtali was Ahira the son of Enan.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/10#v28)</sup> Thus were the journeyings of the children of Israel according to their armies, when they set forward.

- In the first place went the standard of the camp of the children of Judah. The standard is the banner, like the flags of the different tribes held up in the schools today.
- The order of the march is given camp by camp: Judah with Issachar and Zebulun first; the tabernacle taken down and borne by the sons of Gershon and Merari; then Reuben with Simeon and Gad; then the Kohathites bearing the sanctuary, and the other did set up the tabernacle against they came; then Ephraim with Manasseh and Benjamin; and last Dan with Asher and Naphtali, the rereward of all the camps.
- The tabernacle went ahead so that it would be set up before the whole congregation of the twelve tribes arrived where they were going. This was the order of the armies whenever they got up and moved.

**[Numbers 10:29-32](/bible/numbers/10#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/10#v29)</sup> And Moses said unto Hobab, the son of Raguel the Midianite, Moses’ father in law, We are journeying unto the place of which the Lord said, I will give it you: come thou with us, and we will do thee good: for the Lord hath spoken good concerning Israel.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/10#v30)</sup> And he said unto him, I will not go; but I will depart to mine own land, and to my kindred.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/10#v31)</sup> And he said, Leave us not, I pray thee; forasmuch as thou knowest how we are to encamp in the wilderness, and thou mayest be to us instead of eyes.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/10#v32)</sup> And it shall be, if thou go with us, yea, it shall be, that what goodness the Lord shall do unto us, the same will we do unto thee.

- Moses said unto Hobab the son of Raguel the Midianite, his kinsman by marriage, we are journeying unto the place of which the Lord said, I will give it you: come thou with us, and we will do thee good. Hobab answered that he would depart to his own land and to his kindred, to go back to his people.
- Leave us not, for thou mayest be to us instead of eyes: Hobab knew the land and how they ought to encamp in the wilderness, so Moses asked him to stay and be their eyes.
- What goodness the Lord shall do unto us, the same will we do unto thee: Moses treated him as one born among Israel, as a brother; whatever we get, you are going to get. That is the law of the stranger, and it is why Moses dealt so with his kinsman.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 19:34](/bible/leviticus/19#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/leviticus/19#v34)</sup> But the stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

    The stranger that dwelleth with you shall be unto you as one born among you, and thou shalt love him as thyself; that is why Moses offered Hobab the same goodness.

**[Numbers 10:33-36](/bible/numbers/10#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/10#v33)</sup> And they departed from the mount of the Lord three days’ journey: and the ark of the covenant of the Lord went before them in the three days’ journey, to search out a resting place for them.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/10#v34)</sup> And the cloud of the Lord was upon them by day, when they went out of the camp.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/numbers/10#v35)</sup> And it came to pass, when the ark set forward, that Moses said, Rise up, Lord, and let thine enemies be scattered; and let them that hate thee flee before thee.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/numbers/10#v36)</sup> And when it rested, he said, Return, O Lord, unto the many thousands of Israel.

- They departed from the mount of the Lord three days' journey, and the ark of the covenant of the Lord went before them in the three days' journey, to search out a resting place for them; and the cloud of the Lord was upon them by day.
- Every time the ark set forward Moses said, rise up, Lord, and let thine enemies be scattered, and let them that hate thee flee before thee; and when it rested he said, return, O Lord, unto the many thousands of Israel.
- The Most High was fighting Israel's battles and winning the wars. It was not because Israel was a great nation of itself that the other nations were defeated; it was because God was with them, the God that brought them out of Egypt and multiplied them into a great nation.

  Precepts:

    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/68#v2)</sup> As smoke is driven away, so drive them away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.

    Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered; as smoke is driven away, as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.
