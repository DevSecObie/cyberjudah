## Introduction

This reading covers [Numbers 15](/bible/numbers/15) through 18: the offerings to be made in the land and the one law for Israel and the stranger, the difference between sin of ignorance and presumptuous sin with the man that gathered sticks on the sabbath, the rebellion of Korah, Aaron's rod that budded, and the charge and portion of the priests and the Levites. This was a reading session with brief comment on the verses; no questions were raised at the end.

**[Numbers 15:1-3](/bible/numbers/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/15#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/15#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye be come into the land of your habitations, which I give unto you,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/15#v3)</sup> And will make an offering by fire unto the Lord, a burnt offering, or a sacrifice in performing a vow, or in a freewill offering, or in your solemn feasts, to make a sweet savour unto the Lord, of the herd, or of the flock:

- When Israel came into the land of their habitations, they were to make an offering by fire unto the Lord: a burnt offering, a sacrifice in performing a vow, a freewill offering, or an offering in the solemn feasts, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
- These are all the accounts on which you would sacrifice to the Lord, taken of the herd or of the flock.

**[Numbers 15:4-10](/bible/numbers/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/15#v4)</sup> Then shall he that offereth his offering unto the Lord bring a meat offering of a tenth deal of flour mingled with the fourth part of an hin of oil.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/15#v5)</sup> And the fourth part of an hin of wine for a drink offering shalt thou prepare with the burnt offering or sacrifice, for one lamb.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/15#v6)</sup> Or for a ram, thou shalt prepare for a meat offering two tenth deals of flour mingled with the third part of an hin of oil.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/15#v7)</sup> And for a drink offering thou shalt offer the third part of an hin of wine, for a sweet savour unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/15#v8)</sup> And when thou preparest a bullock for a burnt offering, or for a sacrifice in performing a vow, or peace offerings unto the Lord:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/15#v9)</sup> Then shall he bring with a bullock a meat offering of three tenth deals of flour mingled with half an hin of oil.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/15#v10)</sup> And thou shalt bring for a drink offering half an hin of wine, for an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

- Every sacrifice came with its measured meat offering of flour mingled with oil and its drink offering of wine, and the measures scaled up with the animal: a lamb, a ram, and then a bullock.
- An hin is roughly a gallon to a gallon and a half, so the bullock, the greatest offering, carried half an hin of oil and half an hin of wine.

**[Numbers 15:11-13](/bible/numbers/15#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/15#v11)</sup> Thus shall it be done for one bullock, or for one ram, or for a lamb, or a kid.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/15#v12)</sup> According to the number that ye shall prepare, so shall ye do to every one according to their number.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/15#v13)</sup> All that are born of the country shall do these things after this manner, in offering an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

- Thus it was done for one bullock, one ram, one lamb, or a kid; the kid is the goat. Everyone did according to the number they prepared.
- All that are born of the country did these things after this manner when offering an offering made by fire of a sweet savour unto the Lord.

**[Numbers 15:14-16](/bible/numbers/15#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/numbers/15#v14)</sup> And if a stranger sojourn with you, or whosoever be among you in your generations, and will offer an offering made by fire, of a sweet savour unto the Lord; as ye do, so he shall do.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/numbers/15#v15)</sup> One ordinance shall be both for you of the congregation, and also for the stranger that sojourneth with you, an ordinance for ever in your generations: as ye are, so shall the stranger be before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/15#v16)</sup> One law and one manner shall be for you, and for the stranger that sojourneth with you.

- The scripture separates the two: the children of Israel and the stranger that sojourns with them. If a stranger joined himself to Israel, he had to keep the law to journey with them in the land.
- One ordinance and one law stood both for the congregation and for the stranger that was joined with them, an ordinance for ever in their generations: as ye are, so shall the stranger be before the Lord.

**[Numbers 15:17-21](/bible/numbers/15#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/15#v17)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/15#v18)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When ye come into the land whither I bring you,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/15#v19)</sup> Then it shall be, that, when ye eat of the bread of the land, ye shall offer up an heave offering unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/numbers/15#v20)</sup> Ye shall offer up a cake of the first of your dough for an heave offering: as ye do the heave offering of the threshingfloor, so shall ye heave it.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/15#v21)</sup> Of the first of your dough ye shall give unto the Lord an heave offering in your generations.

- When Israel ate of the bread of the land, they offered up a cake of the first of their dough as an heave offering unto the Lord, as they did the heave offering of the threshingfloor.
- The heave offering was a particular motion in presenting it: the offering was held up to the Most High and brought back down; that is why it was called the heave offering.

**[Numbers 15:22-26](/bible/numbers/15#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/numbers/15#v22)</sup> And if ye have erred, and not observed all these commandments, which the Lord hath spoken unto Moses,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/numbers/15#v23)</sup> Even all that the Lord hath commanded you by the hand of Moses, from the day that the Lord commanded Moses, and henceforward among your generations;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/numbers/15#v24)</sup> Then it shall be, if ought be committed by ignorance without the knowledge of the congregation, that all the congregation shall offer one young bullock for a burnt offering, for a sweet savour unto the Lord, with his meat offering, and his drink offering, according to the manner, and one kid of the goats for a sin offering.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/numbers/15#v25)</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it shall be forgiven them; for it is ignorance: and they shall bring their offering, a sacrifice made by fire unto the Lord, and their sin offering before the Lord, for their ignorance:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/numbers/15#v26)</sup> And it shall be forgiven all the congregation of the children of Israel, and the stranger that sojourneth among them; seeing all the people were in ignorance.

- If the whole congregation sinned through ignorance, without the knowledge of what they were doing, and the priest came behind them and corrected them on it, then all the congregation offered a young bullock for a burnt offering and a kid of the goats for a sin offering.
- The priest made an atonement for all the congregation of the children of Israel, and it was forgiven them, for it was ignorance; the stranger among them was included in that forgiveness.

**[Numbers 15:27-29](/bible/numbers/15#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/numbers/15#v27)</sup> And if any soul sin through ignorance, then he shall bring a she goat of the first year for a sin offering.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/15#v28)</sup> And the priest shall make an atonement for the soul that sinneth ignorantly, when he sinneth by ignorance before the Lord, to make an atonement for him; and it shall be forgiven him.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/15#v29)</sup> Ye shall have one law for him that sinneth through ignorance, both for him that is born among the children of Israel, and for the stranger that sojourneth among them.

- If any single soul sinned through ignorance, he brought a she goat of the first year for a sin offering, and the priest made an atonement for him, and it was forgiven him.
- One law covered him that sinned through ignorance, both for him born among the children of Israel and for the stranger that sojourned among them.

**[Numbers 15:30-31](/bible/numbers/15#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/15#v30)</sup> But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously, whether he be born in the land, or a stranger, the same reproacheth the Lord; and that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/15#v31)</sup> Because he hath despised the word of the Lord, and hath broken his commandment, that soul shall utterly be cut off; his iniquity shall be upon him.

- But the soul that doeth ought presumptuously sinned willfully; he thought about what he was doing before he did it. Whether born in the land or a stranger, that soul reproached the Lord and was cut off from among his people.
- Because he despised the word of the Lord and broke his commandment, that soul was utterly cut off, meaning put to death, and his iniquity was upon him.

**[Numbers 15:32-36](/bible/numbers/15#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/15#v32)</sup> And while the children of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man that gathered sticks upon the sabbath day.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/15#v33)</sup> And they that found him gathering sticks brought him unto Moses and Aaron, and unto all the congregation.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/15#v34)</sup> And they put him in ward, because it was not declared what should be done to him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/numbers/15#v35)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, The man shall be surely put to death: all the congregation shall stone him with stones without the camp.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/numbers/15#v36)</sup> And all the congregation brought him without the camp, and stoned him with stones, and he died; as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The man found gathering sticks on the sabbath day is the example of the presumptuous sin just described: someone who thought about the sin before he did it. He was not picking up sticks just to pick up sticks; he was gathering them to kindle a fire and cook on the sabbath.
- They put him in ward, meaning they locked him up, because it was not declared what should be done to him; then the Lord commanded Moses that the man be surely put to death, and all the congregation stoned him with stones without the camp.
- He did not even get to the act itself; he had the intention, and for that willful intent he was put to death.

**[Numbers 15:37-41](/bible/numbers/15#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/numbers/15#v37)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/numbers/15#v38)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and bid them that they make them fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and that they put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue:
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/numbers/15#v39)</sup> And it shall be unto you for a fringe, that ye may look upon it, and remember all the commandments of the Lord, and do them; and that ye seek not after your own heart and your own eyes, after which ye use to go a whoring:
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/numbers/15#v40)</sup> That ye may remember, and do all my commandments, and be holy unto your God.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/numbers/15#v41)</sup> I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: I am the Lord your God.

- Because of that act, the Most High gave Israel the commandment to make fringes in the borders of their garments throughout their generations, and to put upon the fringe of the borders a ribband of blue.
- The fringe is to look upon and remember all the commandments of the Lord and do them, and not to seek after your own heart and your own eyes; so that when we think of sin, we look at the fringes and remember the laws of the Most High instead of going and picking up sticks.
- I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, to be your God.
