**[Numbers 2:1-2](/bible/numbers/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/2#v1)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses and unto Aaron, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/2#v2)</sup> Every man of the children of Israel shall pitch by his own standard, with the ensign of their father’s house: far off about the tabernacle of the congregation shall they pitch.

- Every man of the children of Israel pitched by his own standard, with the ensign of his father's house, far off about the tabernacle of the congregation.
- This is the picture of the tribes set in their sections around the tabernacle, with the tabernacle in the middle and the pillar of fire coming down by night: the setting of the Lord's army in formation. That is how we are supposed to be set in formation; that is heavy.

**[Numbers 2:3-9](/bible/numbers/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/2#v3)</sup> And on the east side toward the rising of the sun shall they of the standard of the camp of Judah pitch throughout their armies: and Nahshon the son of Amminadab shall be captain of the children of Judah.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/2#v4)</sup> And his host, and those that were numbered of them, were threescore and fourteen thousand and six hundred.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/2#v5)</sup> And those that do pitch next unto him shall be the tribe of Issachar: and Nethaneel the son of Zuar shall be captain of the children of Issachar.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/2#v6)</sup> And his host, and those that were numbered thereof, were fifty and four thousand and four hundred.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/2#v7)</sup> Then the tribe of Zebulun: and Eliab the son of Helon shall be captain of the children of Zebulun.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/2#v8)</sup> And his host, and those that were numbered thereof, were fifty and seven thousand and four hundred.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/2#v9)</sup> All that were numbered in the camp of Judah were an hundred thousand and fourscore thousand and six thousand and four hundred, throughout their armies. These shall first set forth.

- On the east side toward the rising of the sun pitched the standard of the camp of Judah, with Nahshon the son of Amminadab as captain, and next to him Issachar under Nethaneel the son of Zuar and Zebulun under Eliab the son of Helon.
- All that were numbered in the camp of Judah were an hundred thousand and fourscore thousand and six thousand and four hundred, which is 186,400 throughout their armies; and these set forth first.

>

>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/2#v13)</sup> And his host, and those that were numbered of them, were fifty and nine thousand and three hundred.
>

>

>
> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/2#v16)</sup> All that were numbered in the camp of Reuben were an hundred thousand and fifty and one thousand and four hundred and fifty, throughout their armies. And they shall set forth in the second rank.

- All that were numbered in the camp of Reuben were an hundred thousand and fifty and one thousand and four hundred and fifty, which is 151,450 throughout their armies; and they set forth in the second rank.

**[Numbers 2:17](/bible/numbers/2#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/2#v17)</sup> Then the tabernacle of the congregation shall set forward with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camp: as they encamp, so shall they set forward, every man in his place by their standards.

- Then the tabernacle of the congregation set forward with the camp of the Levites in the midst of the camp: as they encamped, so they set forward, every man in his place by their standards.
- The tabernacle stayed in the middle of the formation whether Israel was camped or marching; the order of the camp was the order of the march.

>

**[Numbers 2:32-34](/bible/numbers/2#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/2#v32)</sup> These are those which were numbered of the children of Israel by the house of their fathers: all those that were numbered of the camps throughout their hosts were six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/2#v33)</sup> But the Levites were not numbered among the children of Israel; as the Lord commanded Moses.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/2#v34)</sup> And the children of Israel did according to all that the Lord commanded Moses: so they pitched by their standards, and so they set forward, every one after their families, according to the house of their fathers.

- All those that were numbered of the camps throughout their hosts were six hundred thousand and three thousand and five hundred and fifty; but the Levites were not numbered among the children of Israel, as the Most High commanded Moses.
- The children of Israel did according to all that the Most High commanded Moses: so they pitched by their standards, and so they set forward, every one after their families, according to the house of their fathers.
