**[Numbers 25:1-3](/bible/numbers/25#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/numbers/25#v1)</sup> And Israel abode in Shittim, and the people began to commit whoredom with the daughters of Moab.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/25#v2)</sup> And they called the people unto the sacrifices of their gods: and the people did eat, and bowed down to their gods.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/25#v3)</sup> And Israel joined himself unto Baal–peor: and the anger of the Lord was kindled against Israel.

- Israel abode in Shittim, and the people began to commit whoredom with the daughters of Moab. Why? Balaam could not curse us, because God said you cannot curse what is blessed; so his counsel was the best the enemy can ever do: throw another god in front of Israel and send the women, and let Israel bring the trespass on himself. That counsel is why the Lord put Balaam to death.
- They called the people unto the sacrifices of their gods, and the people did eat and bowed down; Israel joined himself unto Baal-peor, and the anger of the Lord was kindled. The worship of Baal-peor went into whoredom, that orgy worship: we started worshipping another god and messing with the women together.

**[Numbers 25:4-5](/bible/numbers/25#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/numbers/25#v4)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, Take all the heads of the people, and hang them up before the Lord against the sun, that the fierce anger of the Lord may be turned away from Israel.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/25#v5)</sup> And Moses said unto the judges of Israel, Slay ye every one his men that were joined unto Baal–peor.

- The Lord said, take all the heads of the people and hang them up before the Lord against the sun, that His fierce anger be turned away; and Moses said unto the judges, slay ye every one his man that were joined unto Baal-peor. Every one his man means every judge by his rank: the officer over ten took ten, the officer over fifty took fifty, and so on. With the Most High it is two options only, life and death.
- Under the old covenant there was no winking at ignorance; judgment was instant. The only reason we are not judged that way now for the gods we served is the grace and mercy given through Christ: the times of this ignorance God winked at, the Christianity with the white Jesus, the Islam, the Buddhism, the Hinduism, the Egyptology, the atheism; but now He commandeth all men every where to repent, because He hath appointed a day in which He will judge the world in righteousness by that man whom He hath ordained, Jesus the Christ.
- Do not take that mercy lightly, and do not frustrate the grace of God until He is finished with you. He can cut a man's days down whenever He wants; do not press Him to it.

**[Numbers 25:6-9](/bible/numbers/25#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/25#v6)</sup> And, behold, one of the children of Israel came and brought unto his brethren a Midianitish woman in the sight of Moses, and in the sight of all the congregation of the children of Israel, who were weeping before the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/numbers/25#v7)</sup> And when Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose up from among the congregation, and took a javelin in his hand;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/numbers/25#v8)</sup> And he went after the man of Israel into the tent, and thrust both of them through, the man of Israel, and the woman through her belly. So the plague was stayed from the children of Israel.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/numbers/25#v9)</sup> And those that died in the plague were twenty and four thousand.

- While the whole congregation wept before the door of the tabernacle, one of the children of Israel brought a Midianitish woman unto his brethren in the sight of Moses and all the congregation: a man that did not care about his people while everyone around him mourned.
- When Phinehas the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, saw it, he rose up from among the congregation, took a javelin in his hand, went after the man of Israel into the tent, and thrust both of them through; so the plague was stayed from the children of Israel, and those that died in the plague were twenty and four thousand.
- Let us now praise famous men: Phinehas stands third in glory, because he had zeal in the fear of the Lord and stood up with good courage of heart when the people were turned back, and made reconciliation for Israel; therefore a covenant of peace was made with him, that he and his posterity should have the dignity of the priesthood for ever.
- These things were our examples, that we should not lust after evil things as they lusted. The people sat down to eat and drink and rose up to play, and that play means sex; that was the golden calf. Neither commit fornication as they did, when three and twenty thousand fell in one day; neither tempt Christ, for it was Christ with us in the wilderness. These things are written for our admonition, that we be warned: idolatry leads to whoredom, spiritual whoredom against the Most High and physical whoredom with it.

**[Numbers 25:10-13](/bible/numbers/25#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/numbers/25#v10)</sup> And the Lord spake unto Moses, saying,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/numbers/25#v11)</sup> Phinehas, the son of Eleazar, the son of Aaron the priest, hath turned my wrath away from the children of Israel, while he was zealous for my sake among them, that I consumed not the children of Israel in my jealousy.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/25#v12)</sup> Wherefore say, Behold, I give unto him my covenant of peace:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/numbers/25#v13)</sup> And he shall have it, and his seed after him, even the covenant of an everlasting priesthood; because he was zealous for his God, and made an atonement for the children of Israel.

- Phinehas turned the wrath of the Most High away from the children of Israel, because he was zealous for his God's sake among them; wherefore the Lord gave him His covenant of peace, and to his seed after him the covenant of an everlasting priesthood, because he made an atonement for the children of Israel.
- That covenant of peace is what all of us are striving for, rest from this servitude of bondage; but the wisdom in reading the acts of Phinehas is that you must be zealous for the law of the Most High to obtain it. Be ye zealous for the law, and give your lives for the covenant of your fathers; Phinehas our father, in being zealous and fervent, obtained the covenant of an everlasting priesthood.
- Being zealous today is confessing Christ before men, and you confess him mainly through your actions. Christ suffered for us, leaving us an example that we should follow his steps, and his steps were keeping the commandments of God; the first way you deny Christ is by rejecting the commandments.
- Christ came not to send peace on earth but a sword, setting a man at variance against his own household; he that loveth father or mother more than him is not worthy of him. We stay zealous by letting nothing separate us from following Christ in his steps: keeping the laws of God, teaching the gospel, and spreading the word with wisdom.
