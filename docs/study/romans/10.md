## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 10 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 13-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 10:1](/bible/romans/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/10#v1)</sup> Brethren, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for Israel is, that they might be saved.

- Now to the title of the class: true unity will only be found amongst the Israelites. “Brethren, my heart’s desire and prayer to God for Israel is, that they might be saved.” Paul’s focus is Israel. Don’t let anybody confuse you: the Gentiles he went to are Israelites, the northern kingdom of Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Nehemiah 2:10](/bible/nehemiah/2#v10)**

    The heathen hate it. “When Sanballat the Horonite, and Tobiah the servant, the Ammonite, heard of it, it grieved them exceedingly that there was come a man to seek the welfare of the children of Israel.” The nations hate that: you are trying to stop the oppression machine. That is why the FBI could not leave these movements alone.

**[Romans 10:2](/bible/romans/10#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/10#v2)</sup> For I bear them record that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge.

- “For I bear them record that they have a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge.” Our brothers had a zeal of God, but not according to knowledge. That is the missing element, the missing formula for our deliverance. We must be unified under the banner of our King and his commandments. The commandments are our constitution, and they will be used to unify Israel, not any other nation or group.
- The Zealots were a Jewish patriotic party in the time of Cyrenius that resisted Roman aggression and, in their hatred of the Romans, resorted to violence and assassination. Rome was oppressing Israel, so they formed groups to attack Rome, thinking deliverance would come by might. But that time was done, because we went into captivity for our disobedience to the laws of God.
- The same pattern is in every organization our people have formed against oppression. The Black Panthers, infiltrated and divided, their members killed and imprisoned. The Brown Berets, who fed and defended their people, until the feminist movement and the forerunner of the LGBT movement crept in, the women wanting to take the roles of the men, and the conflicts weakened their unity. The American Indian Movement, worn down by investigations and arrests. The armed coalition whose leader was sent to federal prison. A great zeal, but not according to knowledge. They have the skin, but they don’t have the spirit, the breath of God, in them yet.
- In Haiti, they were asked about 1804: you got your victory, but who did you give the honor and glory to? They said it was due to witchcraft. Immediately France came knocking for its money.

  - **[Acts 5:36-37](/bible/acts/5#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/acts/5#v36)</sup> For before these days rose up Theudas, boasting himself to be somebody; to whom a number of men, about four hundred, joined themselves: who was slain; and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered, and brought to nought.

    There were zealots in the Bible who thought they could do the same thing. “For before these days rose up Theudas, boasting himself to be somebody”: not trusting in the Lord. “To whom a number of men, about four hundred, joined themselves: who was slain; and all, as many as obeyed him, were scattered, and brought to nought.”

    Israel’s problem was a moral issue, a spiritual problem. We were not keeping the covenant of our King, and when we broke it, the judgment and the curses fell upon us. “This is the word of the Lord unto Zerubbabel, saying, Not by might”: not by the sword, “nor by power, but by my spirit, saith the Lord of hosts.” Our victory comes through the spirit, through our obedience to the commandments of God. Then our victory is secured.

**[Romans 10:13](/bible/romans/10#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/10#v13)</sup> For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.

- “For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.” With the calling comes application and learning. It makes no sense that you just call something out and receive it. You call out your order at a pizza shop and don’t pay, he takes the pizza back. You can’t stand in a car dealership, say Honda Civic, and drive off the lot; there is paperwork, license, registration. We understand it in carnal things, but in spiritual things we get stupid.

**[Romans 10:14-15](/bible/romans/10#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/10#v14)</sup> How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed? and how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard? and how shall they hear without a preacher?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/10#v15)</sup> And how shall they preach, except they be sent? as it is written, How beautiful are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things!

- “And how shall they hear without a preacher?” A preacher means a prophet, because to preach means to foretell, and that is what a prophet does. So you brothers are very important in your learning, and you sisters are very important in teaching the children.
- “How then shall they call on him in whom they have not believed?” How could they call on the almighty God that only loves them, when they have not believed in that God? Our people believed in a strange God, a Eurocentric God. We are the only race that has a god that does not look like us.
- “And how shall they believe in him of whom they have not heard?” Because we have been following a strange God all our lives. Why is that God strange? He doesn't look like you, he has no laws, statutes or commandments, and he whipped you in slavery. Yet we run on Sunday morning and pray faithfully to the same white man that shot us down in the streets and raped our mothers and fathers.
- “And how shall they hear without a preacher?” Preacher means prophet. To preach means to foretell, like in the beginning. That is a prophet.

**[Romans 10:16-17](/bible/romans/10#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/romans/10#v16)</sup> But they have not all obeyed the gospel. For Esaias saith, Lord, who hath believed our report?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/10#v17)</sup> So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

- The eyes and the hearing are physical senses we have. “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” In hearing we are able to process what we heard, learn from it and apply it to our lives. Faith increases by hearing the word of God.
- “But they have not all obeyed the gospel.” Calling is tied to obeying. What is written in the New Testament is also the commandments of the Lord, as Paul says, and the point is obedience. “Lord, who hath believed our report?” Belief is demonstrated by action.
- “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” We must keep the fire in us to labor for God and our people, so that they can hear.
- “But they have not all obeyed the gospel.” “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.”

**[Romans 10:19](/bible/romans/10#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/romans/10#v19)</sup> But I say, Did not Israel know? First Moses saith, I will provoke you to jealousy by them that are no people, and by a foolish nation I will anger you.

- Romans 11 continues from the bottom of chapter 10, which gives its context. “Did not Israel know? First Moses saith, I will provoke you to jealousy by them that are no people, and by a foolish nation I will anger you.” That is Deuteronomy 32. The context is what Israel knows. The you is the southern kingdom; the no people and the foolish nation are the northern kingdom. He would provoke them to anger among themselves. The Lord can be petty sometimes: he is getting at Judah with the northern kingdom, giving them the same blessing. The Lord is a mastermind.
- “I will provoke you to jealousy by them that are no people, and by a foolish nation I will anger you”: the northern kingdom.
