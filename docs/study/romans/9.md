## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 9 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-33, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 9:1-4](/bible/romans/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/9#v1)</sup> I say the truth in Christ, I lie not, my conscience also bearing me witness in the Holy Ghost,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/9#v2)</sup> That I have great heaviness and continual sorrow in my heart.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/9#v3)</sup> For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/9#v4)</sup> Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises;

- “I say the truth in Christ, I lie not”: Paul says I’m not lying at all; the Holy Ghost is on me, that’s who’s speaking through me. “For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh”: I wish I could take all the curses of Deuteronomy 28 on me so that my brothers, my kinsmen, wouldn’t have to go through this. Christians like to run from that word the flesh, but he’s going to break it down for us.
- “Who are Israelites”: the Israelites are Paul’s kinsmen according to the flesh, not all nations on the planet. “To whom pertaineth” means to whom belongeth: the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the giving of the law, the service of God and the promises. You know the churches hate Romans chapter 9; it gives you God’s plan perfectly, just like Daniel 7.

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 4:4-5](/bible/galatians/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/galatians/4#v4)</sup> But when the fulness of the time was come, God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law,
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/galatians/4#v5)</sup> To redeem them that were under the law, that we might receive the adoption of sons.

    What is the adoption? “God sent forth his Son, made of a woman, made under the law, To redeem them that were under the law”. That’s not all nations on the planet Earth. All nations were not under the law of God; he gave the law to Moses, who gave it to the 12 tribes of Israel, “that we might receive the adoption of sons.” So that only pertains to the 12 tribes of Israel.

**[Romans 9:5](/bible/romans/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/romans/9#v5)</sup> Whose are the fathers, and of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came, who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.

- “Whose are the fathers”: the fathers of this record called the Bible. “And of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came”. When you’re on the street breaking this down, don’t let the dumb Christian run past that, because they go spiritual Israel. You got to stress it: as concerning the flesh Christ came.
- “Whose are the fathers”: Abraham. Abraham had many children, but who received the promise after him? Isaac. Isaac had two boys, and the promise went to Jacob. Jacob had 12 boys who received the promise, and we are the children of Jacob today: Jacob and Sons, never to be diminished, only replenished when Christ returns.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 34:16-17](/bible/isaiah/34#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/34#v16)</sup> Seek ye out of the book of the Lord, and read: no one of these shall fail, none shall want her mate: for my mouth it hath commanded, and his spirit it hath gathered them.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/34#v17)</sup> And he hath cast the lot for them, and his hand hath divided it unto them by line: they shall possess it for ever, from generation to generation shall they dwell therein.

    Whose are the fathers of this record called the Bible? “Seek ye out of the book of the Lord, and read: no one of these shall fail”: none of the promises in the Bible shall fail. “None shall want her mate”: you can’t mate the Bible with no other book. “For my mouth it hath commanded”: God’s mouth commanded the writings of this record. “And his spirit it hath gathered them”: God’s spirit collected these writings together. “And he hath cast the lot for them, and his hand hath divided it unto them by line”: Jeremiah got a lot, Ezekiel got a lot, Obadiah got one chapter, Jude got one chapter. “They shall possess it for ever”: we’re going to possess the book and the land forever.

**[Romans 9:6-7](/bible/romans/9#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/9#v6)</sup> Not as though the word of God hath taken none effect. For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/9#v7)</sup> Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children: but, In Isaac shall thy seed be called.

- “Not as though the word of God hath taken none effect”, because the word of God is taking effect. Did we go into slavery? Were our families destroyed and decimated? Are there earthquakes in diverse places, pestilence, wars and rumors of wars? The Bible is taking effect. “For they are not all Israel, which are of Israel”: all Israelites are not going to repent.
- “Neither, because they are the seed of Abraham, are they all children”: just because you come from Abraham’s lineage does not make you children of God. Abraham had Ishmael by Hagar and six sons by Keturah. “But, In Isaac shall thy seed be called”: the Lord is saying, I’m not calling all of them; I’m calling the one Sarah had. That’s the boy I’m dealing with.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Maccabees 11:21](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/1-maccabees/11#v21)</sup> Then certain ungodly persons, who hated their own people, went unto the king, and told him that Jonathan besieged the tower.

    “Then certain ungodly persons, who hated their own people”. Those are your Uncle Ruckus, people like Stephen in Django, and believe me they are around today. Some of them are your church ministers, some of them your congressmen, your assemblymen.

**[Romans 9:8](/bible/romans/9#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/9#v8)</sup> That is, They which are the children of the flesh, these are not the children of God: but the children of the promise are counted for the seed.

- “They which are the children of the flesh”: now he’s expanding the thought. The children of the flesh are all the other nations: “these are not the children of God”. Christians say everybody are the children of God, but these are not the children of God. “But the children of the promise are counted for the seed”: the children that came from Isaac.
- “That is, They which are the children of the flesh”. All those other kids of Abraham are the children of the flesh. It’s worded like this because they want you to understand what Paul is talking about when he says in the flesh. “These are not the children of God”: they’re not the children of God, although they are of Abraham’s flesh. “But the children of the promise are counted for the seed.”

Nothing. “Behold, he taketh up the isles as a very little thing”. “And Lebanon is not sufficient to burn”: if they wanted to sacrifice to be God’s people, it’s not sufficient. “All nations before him are as nothing; and they are counted to him less than nothing, and vanity”: vanity means a waste of time.

These were not identical twins, they were fraternal twins. “And the first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name Esau”: the blood showed through his skin. It gave Esau’s color but did not give Jacob’s, because Jacob looked just like Isaac and Rebecca, like all the people from Genesis 2 on up; everybody was melanated until this character Esau came on the scene. “Esau was a cunning hunter, a man of the field; and Jacob was a plain man, dwelling in tents.” “Feed me, I pray thee, with that same red pottage”: that same red, because it looked like him. “Therefore was his name called Edom”: Edom means red, so you’ll read his name as Esau or Edom. A Nigerian couple had one black boy and one albino-looking boy; they come from us, we don’t come from Caucasians.

God made his decision; he’s God and you’re not. Paul is giving you a brief synopsis of God’s plan from Genesis; that’s what this whole chapter is about.

**[Romans 9:15-16](/bible/romans/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/9#v15)</sup> For he saith to Moses, I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/romans/9#v16)</sup> So then it is not of him that willeth, nor of him that runneth, but of God that sheweth mercy.

- “I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy, and I will have compassion on whom I will have compassion.” As it was true back then, when the Lord had mercy on Israel in the captivity under Egypt, Paul is bringing it up to his time. “So then it is not of him that willeth”: Esau can’t will himself to be the people of God, can’t will himself to be the Israelites. “Nor of him that runneth”: you can run around the earth, church after church, synagogue after synagogue, and say I’m a Christian, I’m the people of God. “But of God that sheweth mercy”: it’s God’s decision who he’s going to have mercy on.
- Now he gives the history, that he did it before with the Egyptians. “I will have mercy on whom I will have mercy”: don’t talk to me, shut up, I will do what I want to do because I am the man making the parts, I am the potter. I will have mercy on whom I choose to have mercy on, and compassion on whom I decide. The Bible says the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, so with that understanding there is no need to talk to me about nothing.

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/9#v17)</sup> For the scripture saith unto Pharaoh, Even for this same purpose have I raised thee up, that I might shew my power in thee, and that my name might be declared throughout all the earth.

Before he destroyed Pharaoh he hardened his heart and he built him up; Pharaoh was feared as a god. The same thing happened in the time of Christ, and the same thing is happening right now.

That is why the people in the church are shook and scared. They don’t realize this man has a time limit like Pharaoh had a time limit.
