## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7, 18-32, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 1:7](/bible/romans/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/1#v7)</sup> To all that be in Rome, beloved of God, called to be saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.

- “To all that be in Rome, beloved of God, called to be saints”. Paul is writing to the saints at Rome. Some Christians say he is writing to Israelites and other nations at the same time. No: the letter is addressed to the saints at Rome.
- Is he addressing both Jews and Gentiles in the church? Yes. But they are all saints. Other nations are never referred to as saints, so Paul is not writing to other nations at all.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    “He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him.” The people near unto him are the saints. The children of Israel are the saints: we are the saints.
  - **[Romans 2:17](/bible/romans/2#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/romans/2#v17)</sup> Behold, thou art called a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast of God,

    “Behold, thou art called a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast of God”. Paul is writing to the Jews. There is obviously northern kingdom in the congregations there as well, but he is writing to Judah, to the Jews. That settles who the letter is addressed to.
  - **[Romans 7:1](/bible/romans/7#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/7#v1)</sup> Know ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,) how that the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth?

    “Know ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,)”. He speaks to them that know the law because he is writing to Jews, “how that the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth”.

**[Romans 1:18](/bible/romans/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/1#v18)</sup> For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who hold the truth in unrighteousness;

- “For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men, who hold the truth in unrighteousness.”
- Jude 1:16 is speaking of murmuring, busybodies, gossiping: folks that just can't mind their own damn business. There is so much peace in not knowing things that don't pertain to you, so much tranquility in not knowing all the messed up stuff in people's lives. But no, you want to know everything with your nosy ass. That is why the class starts way back here.
- “For the wrath of God is revealed from heaven against all ungodliness and unrighteousness of men”: the judgment when he returns. You will not be able to say you didn't know your behavior was wrong. When you keep doing it after we go over this, you deserve whatever happens afterwards.
- The ones in the midst of murmuring, gossiping, complaining, being busybodies and talebearing never come to the understanding during a class like this that it is about them. They sit wondering who Deacon is talking about, what happened, what set him off, and they are going to find out. Completely missing the point. A lot of us want to know things that are not for us to know. You are not in administration or leadership, you are not dealing with it, you are not fixing nothing. What you need to know for? You just want to be in the know.

**[Romans 1:19-20](/bible/romans/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/romans/1#v19)</sup> Because that which may be known of God is manifest in them; for God hath shewed it unto them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/romans/1#v20)</sup> For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen, being understood by the things that are made, even his eternal power and Godhead; so that they are without excuse:

- “The invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen”: you see these things plain as day. Anybody who doesn't believe in God is an idiot, however smart they are thought to be, Neil deGrasse Tyson included. If you have not come to the conclusion that we were created and need to do what the Creator told us to do, you are far behind. “So that they are without excuse.”
- “Even his eternal power and Godhead”: that is the name of the topic today, the eternal power and Godhead. The power of God, the power of Christ: Christ created everything, and Christ is the one God put over everything. It is all him. Right through the Bible we are reading about the Lord of hosts, the Lord God of Israel, and that was always Christ.
- Because he came and died, now we get access to his Father. Now we say our Father; now we are adopted to the Father. That is what the adoption is about: us having access to the Father.
- “For the invisible things of him from the creation of the world are clearly seen.” Christ created all things, seen and unseen. You can’t see the air you breathe, but if the air supply is cut off, we all drop dead. Think how all the ecosystems work perfectly together. Evolution can’t do that. Big bang theories can’t do that. They are trying to figure out how God is God, so they give you all these theories to throw you off, just like they give you all the Christian churches to throw you off. It is all the same thing.

**[Romans 1:21-22](/bible/romans/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/1#v21)</sup> Because that, when they knew God, they glorified him not as God, neither were thankful; but became vain in their imaginations, and their foolish heart was darkened.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/1#v22)</sup> Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools,

- “When they knew God, they glorified him not as God, neither were thankful; but became vain in their imaginations.” In their imaginations they started to do whatever they wanted. That is what people do: forget the book, I'mma do it my way. The Lord said forsake not the assembling of yourselves together, and to wear out the steps of the elders, and they say, I'mma do it my way.
- “Professing themselves to be wise, they became fools”: you think you know something, you think you're smart, you think your way is the better way.
- “Because that, when they knew God, they glorified him not”. We knew God, and we did not glorify him. That goes right back to obedience. We knew what God required of us and what his instructions are, and we don’t glorify the Lord when we start doing our own thing, being self-willed, making up our own ways.
- “Neither were thankful”: we were not thankful for all the Lord provided. “But became vain in their imaginations”: our thought processes became unprofitable. “And their foolish heart was darkened”: our minds were darkened.

**[Romans 1:23-25](/bible/romans/1#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/1#v23)</sup> And changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/1#v24)</sup> Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves:
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/1#v25)</sup> Who changed the truth of God into a lie, and worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.

- They “changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man”, and started to worship other things: themselves, some made-up god, mother nature, science. You gave the credit of creation to a molten image. “Wherefore God also gave them up to uncleanness through the lusts of their own hearts, to dishonour their own bodies between themselves”: that is why folks are bugging and tripping. They “worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator”, the things created more than the one that made them. How dumb is that?
- “And changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like to corruptible man, and to birds, and fourfooted beasts, and creeping things.” We didn’t glorify the Lord, and we thought ourselves so wise that we changed the glory of the uncorruptible God into an image made like corruptible man.
- “Who changed the truth of God into a lie.” Someone, somewhere, changed the truth of God into a lie. Paul is dealing with the Israelites in Rome, explaining that the Lord put things in place but they have been changed. And who were the powers that be at that time? Rome.
- “And worshipped and served the creature more than the Creator, who is blessed for ever. Amen.” The truth of God was changed so much that the creature is served more than Christ himself, the Creator. Christ is blessed for ever.

    “But that which is made with hands is cursed, as well it, as he that made it: he, because he made it; and it, because, being corruptible, it was called god.”

**[Romans 1:26-27](/bible/romans/1#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/romans/1#v26)</sup> For this cause God gave them up unto vile affections: for even their women did change the natural use into that which is against nature:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/romans/1#v27)</sup> And likewise also the men, leaving the natural use of the woman, burned in their lust one toward another; men with men working that which is unseemly, and receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet.

- This is the type of people we are dealing with: idol worshipers, people in their own imaginations, and these same type of people be gay. Homosexuals. They have their own way of doing things. “Men with men working that which is unseemly”: the Bible calls it unseemly, and you call it love, love is love, in your own imagination. “And receiving in themselves that recompence of their error which was meet”: that is why they have those problems and those issues.

**[Romans 1:28](/bible/romans/1#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/romans/1#v28)</sup> And even as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind, to do those things which are not convenient;

- It all goes back to convenience. Those who don’t like keeping the Sabbath will leave and join them, so they don’t have to stress on a Friday and run to prepare; and when the day they say is the Sabbath falls on a Tuesday or a Wednesday, they still ain’t going to keep it. Everybody will heap to themselves teachers having itching ears, teachers who teach what is convenient to them: one teaching it’s okay to eat pork, one making up a day, novices teaching foolishness concerning the New Covenant, and brothers will follow after it. Our job is not to go back and forth and argue; our job is to go into the scriptures and bring out the proper understanding and keep it moving. Those whose mind is right will understand, and for those who don’t, remember: the deceived and the deceiver belong to the Lord.
- “And even as they did not like to retain God in their knowledge, God gave them over to a reprobate mind”. These brothers and sisters, God gives them their reprobate mind; they don’t want to retain God in their knowledge. That doctrine was pushed so they could hit the club: the doctrine is for the club, mixing around with the wicked.
- “To do those things which are not convenient”: see that. Doctrines of convenience.
- “God gave them over to a reprobate mind.” Reprobates: the person who can't get right. They are seared with a hot iron, they think they know it all, they can't change. And we are talking about people sitting up in here.

**[Romans 1:29-31](/bible/romans/1#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/romans/1#v29)</sup> Being filled with all unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness; full of envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity; whisperers,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/romans/1#v30)</sup> Backbiters, haters of God, despiteful, proud, boasters, inventors of evil things, disobedient to parents,
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/romans/1#v31)</sup> Without understanding, covenantbreakers, without natural affection, implacable, unmerciful:

- Now the particular things these folks do, all of it together: fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness, envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity, and then “whisperers.” A whisperer is murmuring and gossiping behind the scenes, with certain individuals you call your friends, the brother or sister you are close to.
- “Backbiters, haters of God.” You mean to tell me the backbiters are in the same category with men with men, women with women, murderers, covetousness and fornication? You are the equivalent of a hater of God, all because you can't control your mouth.

**[Romans 1:32](/bible/romans/1#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/romans/1#v32)</sup> Who knowing the judgment of God, that they which commit such things are worthy of death, not only do the same, but have pleasure in them that do them.

- “That they which commit such things are worthy of death.” Worthy of death, for whispering and backbiting. It is a character flaw, and the ones who most need to hear it are not even focused on it. You know the individuals in the congregation that can't keep their mouths closed, always complaining, always gossiping, always involved in some mess, because they can't control their mouth.
