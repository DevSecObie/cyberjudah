## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 8-9, 12-13, 17, 20-24, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 2:8](/bible/romans/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/2#v8)</sup> But unto them that are contentious, and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, indignation and wrath,

- “But unto them that are contentious, and do not obey the truth, but obey unrighteousness, indignation and wrath”: those who go against the truth.

**[Romans 2:9](/bible/romans/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/2#v9)</sup> Tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that doeth evil, of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile;

- “Tribulation and anguish, upon every soul of man that doeth evil, of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile”. The Jew first and also the Gentile. Why does Paul emphasize the Jew first? Who is he getting that from? He is getting it from the prophets, as he always did: the Lord saves Judah first, and Judah must rise first to teach the other tribes. That is why the gospel went to the Jews first and then to the Gentiles, as the book of Acts shows.

  Precepts:
  - **[Zechariah 12:7](/bible/zechariah/12#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/12#v7)</sup> The Lord also shall save the tents of Judah first, that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem do not magnify themselves against Judah.

    “The Lord also shall save the tents of Judah first”: the Jew first. “That the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem do not magnify themselves against Judah”: that’s the rest of the tribes. Judah must rise first. This is where Paul gets “of the Jew first, and also of the Gentile.”
  - **[Deuteronomy 33:7](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v7)</sup> And this is the blessing of Judah: and he said, Hear, Lord, the voice of Judah, and bring him unto his people: let his hands be sufficient for him; and be thou an help to him from his enemies.

    “And this is the blessing of Judah: and he said, Hear, Lord, the voice of Judah, and bring him unto his people”. Bring him unto his people: bring him into Christ, and also bring him into the tribes. Judah must rise first to teach the other tribes. That is why Paul said in Romans 2:9, to the Jew first, then to the Gentile.

**[Romans 2:12](/bible/romans/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/2#v12)</sup> For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law;

- “For as many as have sinned without law shall also perish without law: and as many as have sinned in the law shall be judged by the law”. Whether you die in your ignorance or willfully, you still get death if the Lord so chooses.

**[Romans 2:13](/bible/romans/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/2#v13)</sup> (For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified.

- “For not the hearers of the law are just before God, but the doers of the law shall be justified.” Maybe Paul was schizophrenic, writing the letters high on fentanyl? In Galatians he says no man is justified by the works of the law, and here he says the doers of the law shall be justified. So which law justifies you here, and which law in Galatians does not? Here it is the commandments. In Galatians it is the animal sacrifice explained in Hebrews 7:18-19, Hebrews 9 and Hebrews 10, which we are no longer justified by, because Christ did away with it.
- You are justified when you keep his commandments. The doers of the law shall be justified: they shall live by their faith, Habakkuk 2:4. Faith had to come first, then the works follow.

  Precepts:
  - **[James 1:21-25](/bible/james/1#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/james/1#v21)</sup> Wherefore lay apart all filthiness and superfluity of naughtiness, and receive with meekness the engrafted word, which is able to save your souls.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/james/1#v22)</sup> But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/james/1#v23)</sup> For if any be a hearer of the word, and not a doer, he is like unto a man beholding his natural face in a glass:
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/james/1#v24)</sup> For he beholdeth himself, and goeth his way, and straightway forgetteth what manner of man he was.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/james/1#v25)</sup> But whoso looketh into the perfect law of liberty, and continueth therein, he being not a forgetful hearer, but a doer of the work, this man shall be blessed in his deed.

    James is Christ’s brother. “Wherefore lay apart all filthiness and superfluity of naughtiness”: lay aside all sin. “And receive with meekness the engrafted word”: the word of God, the commandments, the law and the testimony, “which is able to save your souls.” Some say in church that the law can’t save you, only Christ can save you. It is true that Christ saves you, and the law is in Christ: the word of God, keeping God’s commandments, can save your soul.
  - **[Isaiah 8:20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

    What is the engrafted word? “To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.” The law and the testimony are the word.

**[Romans 2:17](/bible/romans/2#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/2#v17)</sup> Behold, thou art called a Jew, and restest in the law, and makest thy boast of God,

- “Behold, thou art called a Jew”: now we know who Paul’s writing to in Rome. That’s what was said earlier about “O man” that judges another: he was writing to the elders, the Jews of the synagogue in Rome, based upon Acts 28. Behold you Romans, you’re a Jew, “and restest in the law”, because they were elders of the law, “and makest thy boast of God”, keeping God’s laws. But they don’t have faith in Christ; that was what was missing.

**[Romans 2:20](/bible/romans/2#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/romans/2#v20)</sup> An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, which hast the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law.

- “An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes”: these are the elders and rulers with “the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law.”

**[Romans 2:21-22](/bible/romans/2#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/2#v21)</sup> Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself? thou that preachest a man should not steal, dost thou steal?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/2#v22)</sup> Thou that sayest a man should not commit adultery, dost thou commit adultery? thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?

- “Thou therefore which teachest another, teachest thou not thyself?” You preach a man should not steal; you should not be stealing, especially from your brothers in the body. You say a man should not commit adultery: is that what you are doing? “Thou that abhorrest idols, dost thou commit sacrilege?” Do you sacrifice to other gods?

**[Romans 2:23](/bible/romans/2#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/2#v23)</sup> Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God?

- “Thou that makest thy boast of the law, through breaking the law dishonourest thou God?” You boast that you keep the law; do you honor God by breaking it?

**[Romans 2:24](/bible/romans/2#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/2#v24)</sup> For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you, as it is written.

- “For the name of God is blasphemed among the Gentiles through you”. The leaders Paul was writing to were teaching and not doing. It is the same as the Lord telling David he gave the enemies occasion to blaspheme. Your actions can be blasphemy too, not only saying the name. Blasphemy has levels.
