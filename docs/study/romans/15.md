## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 15 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4, 13, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

- We want that godhood, but will we receive opposition? Yes, we will. “For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.”
- The four horns we read earlier are Babylon, Media-Persia, the Greeks and Rome. The things written aforetime show that our people received opposition under every one of them while trying to keep the commandments: in Babylon, in Media-Persia, in Greece and in the early stages of Rome. Esau has always done that, and it is written for our learning, because we will receive it today.
- Whatever is written in the Bible was written for us. Paul reveals it: “whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.” The histories, the prophecies, the empires in the scriptures were put there for us to learn from, and it says the same thing as Ecclesiastes 1:9, read the week before.
- So it behooves us to read, read, and read more. The hope comes “through patience and comfort of the scriptures.”

You think it could never happen now in these last days; you have no idea.

**[Romans 15:13](/bible/romans/15#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/15#v13)</sup> Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.

- The first step of recovery is to admit it: you are an alcoholic, you need help, you are sick. Even the white man knows that. A brother says he drank a whole fifth in one night but he is not an alcoholic, and he has not watched the classes on alcohol the Bishops and Deacon Yosua have been going over, because he is running from his addiction. You cannot run away from that thing.
- “Now the God of hope fill you with all joy.” The God of hope: hope and healing. He fills you with joy, knowing that you can get through this battle and put down the bottle. You don't have to be a defender of the bottle.
- “And peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.” Christ is that hope. A lot of brothers lack hope because they lack faith in Christ. Without faith in Christ there is no hope, no ability to see yourself without your addiction. A brother says he can't put it down, it's a part of him. That is what hopelessness looks like, and many brothers and sisters hide those thoughts and tell everybody they are good.
