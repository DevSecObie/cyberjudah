**[Romans 3:1](/bible/romans/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/3#v1)</sup> What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?

- This is the advantage the southern kingdom had. “What advantage then hath the Jew?” What is the advantage of being a Jew? “Or what profit is there of circumcision?” What profit is there in being of the circumcision, being circumcised?
- Some read Romans 3 verse 1 and 2, not verse 3, then skip to verse 9 like those two work together: deceptive as hell. “What advantage then hath the Jew?” If the Jew has an advantage, then if you’re not a Jew you’re at a disadvantage; it’s just that clear. That’s Paul asking the question. “Or what profit is there of circumcision?” What profit is there of being circumcised?
- Another history book says the black Jews had an advantage over the African tribes. Before going any further, that is what the scripture asks: “What advantage then hath the Jew? or what profit is there of circumcision?”
- It is only our own people who come against us, harder than anybody else. Black apologists say they can’t beat the Israelites and tell people to run from us; black pastors say we are saved by race, not by grace, and that our genealogy doesn’t matter. They know what we teach is true, but their masters will not allow them to leave us alone, because if they did, all men would believe on us. They get mad when we bring up identity, yet Christ said who he was and where he was from, and Paul, the one they love most, said he was of the tribe of Benjamin, an Israelite circumcised the eighth day.

**[Romans 3:2](/bible/romans/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/3#v2)</sup> Much every way: chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God.

- “Much every way”: every way you could think, it is profitable to be of the circumcision. “Chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God”: mainly because unto them were committed the oracles of God, the laws.
- Committed: the word to use is give. Unto them were given the oracles of God. The laws were given to Judah, and what did God call Judah? The lawgiver. A lot of times we say wrongly that Levi was the lawgiver; Judah was the lawgiver.
- “Much every way”: that part was skipped. There is every way an advantage to being a Jew and being of the circumcision. “Chiefly, because that unto them were committed the oracles of God”: unto the Israelites. Oracles means the words; unto us were committed the words of God, to keep them. So if we break God’s laws, or decide to break that commitment, we are held accountable for those actions.
- Unto the Jews and Gentiles were committed the oracles of God, and they broke the laws: the Jews and Gentiles as Judah and Israel.
