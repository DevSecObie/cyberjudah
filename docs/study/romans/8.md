## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 5-8, 10, 13-39, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 8:1](/bible/romans/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/8#v1)</sup> There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.

- “There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.”
- “There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus”. The condemnation goes back to the whole nation that should have perished, and to cursed be he that continueth not. Almost all things were purged with blood, not all; if you fell under the almost, you got condemnation, death: stoned to death, set on fire, or hung from a tree. Now there is no condemnation, no death, no cursing to them that have faith in Christ’s death, “who walk not after the flesh”, not in sin, “but after the Spirit.”
- The bottle says you are a failure, you ain't nothing. The Bible says, “There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus.” You can repent. Repent and become a new man.

**[Romans 8:2](/bible/romans/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/8#v2)</sup> For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.

- “For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free”: free is the same as liberty. “From the law of sin and death”: the old law of sacrifice. Under it, if you sinned certain sins against the Lord, you got immediate death. There was no I’m sorry, I repent, I didn’t mean to. There is sin, and there is death.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 10:28](/bible/hebrews/10#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/hebrews/10#v28)</sup> He that despised Moses’ law died without mercy under two or three witnesses:

    This shows the law of sin and death. “He that despised Moses’ law died without mercy”. He hated the law we all agreed to keep, and he died without mercy, without grace. There is no grace under Moses: you sin, you get put to death, in most cases.

**[Romans 8:3](/bible/romans/8#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/8#v3)</sup> For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh:

- “For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh.” Under the law, the sin of adultery was not covered; that was death. Homosexuality was not covered; that was death. But under Christ, under Messiah, we can repent. “God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh.”
- “For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh”: the law here is animal sacrifice. Weak through the flesh means our flesh, and it involves the animals as well.
- “God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh”, a man, “and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh”: he did away with death in the flesh, ending our condemnation by his own death on the cross. That is why Caiaphas said it was expedient that one man should die for the people, that the whole nation perish not. He ended the condemnation of all twelve tribes.
- “For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh”: the law that could not do it is the law of sin and death in verse 2, the same law that was weak in Hebrews 7. It was weak because the people who were offering it were weak and sinful. That is why verse 3 was read first, then back up to verses 1 and 2: it is the same law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 7:18-19](/bible/hebrews/7#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/7#v18)</sup> For there is verily a disannulling of the commandment going before for the weakness and unprofitableness thereof.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/hebrews/7#v19)</sup> For the law made nothing perfect, but the bringing in of a better hope did; by the which we draw nigh unto God.

    “For there is verily a disannulling of the commandment going before for the weakness and unprofitableness thereof.” The weakness is the same as Romans 8:3, “in that it was weak”. The commandment that was annulled is sacrifice: for the weakness and the unprofitableness of it, it was done away in Christ.

**[Romans 8:5-6](/bible/romans/8#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/romans/8#v5)</sup> For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/8#v6)</sup> For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace.

- “For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit. For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace.” If you do the things after the spirit, you are going to get the kingdom.
- “For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.” Where your treasure is, there will your heart be. If you are after the flesh, you will mind the things of the flesh.
- “For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh.” That is all they know; they cannot see past this flesh to anything spiritual. That is why they are blind and groping. “But they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.”
- “For to be carnally minded is death.” If you stay in that carnal mind, that is death. “But to be spiritually minded is life and peace.” Notice: not just life, but peace. Some still have so much drama in their lives because they are not applying this; they have no peace because they are seeking peace in things that are not God. The peace of God passes all things and overcomes whatever you are dealing with. Those who say it doesn't work for them are still not applying what they need to apply.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 6:21](/bible/romans/6#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/romans/6#v21)</sup> What fruit had ye then in those things whereof ye are now ashamed? for the end of those things is death.

    Read with “to be carnally minded is death”: “What fruit had ye then in those things whereof ye are now ashamed? for the end of those things is death.” The fruit of the things you now realize were sinful behavior, the things you should be ashamed of, is death. He is dealing with carnality.
  - **[Isaiah 26:3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/26#v3)</sup> Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.

    On that peace, and on how we are pulled away from God when we are not feeding ourselves: “Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.” It is heavy, because it says trust in him. Saying it doesn't work for you shows that you don't trust God. It is unbelievable how much goes back to your faith. If you are weak in spirit, your faith is weak too. If you can't see the value in what we are doing and trying to put forth, or can't find and trust in God, you are weak in faith. If your faith is only strong when things are going your way, that is not faith at all. If it is only when things benefit you that you feel confident trusting God, then you don't trust in God.

**[Romans 8:7-8](/bible/romans/8#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/8#v7)</sup> Because the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/8#v8)</sup> So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.

- “Because the carnal mind is enmity against God”: the sinful man is against God, and the sinful man cannot please God, “for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.”
- “So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.” Christianity cannot please God. It does not matter how many times you go to church, even from seven in the morning to eleven at night: you cannot please God.
- “The carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.” If you are in this flesh you are against the Heavenly Father, against this Bible and the laws, statutes and commandments he gave you.
- “So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.” Walking in this flesh we cannot please God, and flesh and blood cannot enter heaven. We have to be transformed into a different person, changed to righteousness: put off this old tabernacle and put on the righteous one, get the old man off us.

**[Romans 8:10](/bible/romans/8#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/8#v10)</sup> And if Christ be in you, the body is dead because of sin; but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.

- “And if Christ be in you, the body is dead because of sin; but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.” The body is dead because of sin because we are trying to be like Christ; the goal is to be like Christ. If Christ could do it, we can do it, because Christ did it in the flesh. It was not a puff of smoke that came down; it was flesh.

**[Romans 8:13](/bible/romans/8#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/8#v13)</sup> For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.

- “For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die”: we are going to die. “But if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.”
- When the Most High raises up prophets, a new servant or a clearer revelation of truth, corrupt leaders often recognize that their influence is fading. Instead of humbling themselves and submitting to God, they become envious and defensive, and that envy and defense often leads to violence. They try to preserve their position by persecuting the very people God has chosen, verbally and sometimes physically, thinking the persecution may delay God’s purpose. At the end of the day they cannot stop it. They cannot stop the movement.
- The Most High put a special spirit on our bishops to be the pioneers of this movement in today’s time. That is something you will just have to accept, and play a role, because we are all looking for the same goal, the kingdom. Nowhere in the scriptures is there an award for getting there first.
- So where does the envy and hatred come from? “For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die.” The things of the flesh: jealousy, a major one; lust for money; lust for power. Many of our forefathers battled that lust for power. Some were able to overcome that demon and some succumbed to it. These things happened thousands of years ago, but they were written aforetime for our learning, left as an example for us of what not to be.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 8:44](/bible/john/8#v44)**
    > <sup>[44](/bible/john/8#v44)</sup> Ye are of your father the devil, and the lusts of your father ye will do. He was a murderer from the beginning, and abode not in the truth, because there is no truth in him. When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

    A good example of living after the flesh were the Pharisees. “Ye are of your father the devil”: that is what you don’t want to be, walking after your father the devil, Satan himself. “And the lusts of your father ye will do”: the lust, the envy, the hatred, the malice, the guile, all the works of the flesh the devil puts on display.

**[Romans 8:14](/bible/romans/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/8#v14)</sup> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.

- “For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.” That is us: we are the sons of God.
- “For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God”: both southern and northern kingdom. The prophecy in Ezekiel 37 is being fulfilled, not just the circumcision but the uncircumcision as well.
- “For as many as are led by the Spirit of God”: that is the charitable good works, the fruits of the spirit. “They are the sons of God.” The brothers are the sons of God, but we have to walk in the spirit and run from the flesh.
- A lot of these camps have not been walking in the spirit. They have been walking in the flesh and running from the spirit. So when our light, which is the keeping of the commandments and our good works, shines on their darkness, which is the walking in the flesh, envy and hatred come. That is the agony of defeat: coming to the realization that you are a no good nothing and the Most High is going to put out your candle.

**[Romans 8:15-17](/bible/romans/8#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/8#v15)</sup> For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/romans/8#v16)</sup> The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/8#v17)</sup> And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.

- “But ye have received the Spirit of adoption”: we read that earlier in Ephesians 1:5, how we were chosen before the foundation of the world and predestined for this adoption. “Whereby we cry, Abba, Father. The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God”.
- “Ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.” We have received the spirit of adoption.
- “The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God: And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ.” We are adopted, and we became joint-heirs with Christ: joint-heirs to the Father with Christ.
- People think the adoption was us being adopted to Christ. No: we are adopted to the Father. Christ was the God we were dealing with right through; now we are able to see our grandfather. Christ has got to clean us up and purify us first before he takes us to his Father: you all too dirty, I got to clean you all up before I take you all to my Father. And not all of you are going to be taken.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 1:5](/bible/ephesians/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/1#v5)</sup> Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,

    “Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ”: God predestinated us to be adopted as his children by Jesus Christ. That adoption was predestined.
  - **[Romans 7:1](/bible/romans/7#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/7#v1)</sup> Know ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,) how that the law hath dominion over a man as long as he liveth?

    See who he’s talking to. “Know ye not, brethren, (for I speak to them that know the law,)”. Stop. For I speak to them that know the law. Who is he talking to? And when he comes back to it: so he’s talking to Jews. I speak to them that know the law, so he’s speaking to the Jews that resided in Rome.
  - **[Romans 1:7](/bible/romans/1#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/romans/1#v7)</sup> To all that be in Rome, beloved of God, called to be saints: Grace to you and peace from God our Father, and the Lord Jesus Christ.

    Going to the very beginning of the book: “To all that be in Rome”. Stop. Remember it’s a letter to the Romans, right? So he’s talking to Edom, right? Let’s see: “beloved of God, called to be saints”. Called to be what? Called to be saints.

**[Romans 8:18](/bible/romans/8#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/8#v18)</sup> For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us.

- “For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us.” We read that like it’s words on paper, but none of us can fully understand it. We don’t see it like that because we don’t see heavenly things, we don’t see how the Most High sees it. That’s why we have to read the scriptures, meditate on it, and pray for the Most High to build up our spirits, so when it does come we can go through with it.

**[Romans 8:19-20](/bible/romans/8#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/romans/8#v19)</sup> For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/romans/8#v20)</sup> For the creature was made subject to vanity, not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope,

- “For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.” That’s going into the creation. “For the creature was made subject to vanity”: how was that? When we went into idolatry. “Not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope”.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 8:45](/bible/2-esdras/8#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/2-esdras/8#v45)</sup> Be not wroth with us, but spare thy people, and have mercy upon thine own inheritance: for thou art merciful unto thy creature.

    The creature was made subject to vanity when we went into idolatry. “Be not wroth with us, but spare thy people, and have mercy upon thine own inheritance: for thou art merciful unto thy creature.” Thou art merciful unto thy creature: the creature subjected to vanity is subjected in hope of that mercy.

**[Romans 8:21-22](/bible/romans/8#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/8#v21)</sup> Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/8#v22)</sup> For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.

- “Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God. For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.” The trees, the birds, the animals, the whole creation groaneth, because they hate the way that they’re being treated. There are destroyers of the earth, like when you read Revelation 11:18. The ruling power on the earth right now is not treating and governing the earth correctly, so the creation is groaning. They can’t wait for the rightful possessors of this world to come back into rulership.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 20:19](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v19)</sup> When thou shalt besiege a city a long time, in making war against it to take it, thou shalt not destroy the trees thereof by forcing an axe against them: for thou mayest eat of them, and thou shalt not cut them down (for the tree of the field is man’s life) to employ them in the siege:

    This shows why the creation can’t wait: because of the destroyers of the earth. “When thou shalt besiege a city a long time, in making war against it to take it, thou shalt not destroy the trees thereof by forcing an axe against them: for thou mayest eat of them, and thou shalt not cut them down (for the tree of the field is man’s life)”. Does Esau listen to that? No. All he does is destroy, destroy, destroy. That’s why the creation is groaning: they want us to come back, because we would apply that law.

**[Romans 8:23](/bible/romans/8#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/8#v23)</sup> And not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.

- “And not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit”: that’s our rightful, original state. We were made to be holy. That’s why oppression maketh a wise man mad: he’s the way that God created him to be, holy. “Even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.”

**[Romans 8:24-25](/bible/romans/8#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/8#v24)</sup> For we are saved by hope: but hope that is seen is not hope: for what a man seeth, why doth he yet hope for?
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/8#v25)</sup> But if we hope for that we see not, then do we with patience wait for it.

- “For we are saved by hope: but hope that is seen is not hope”. “But if we hope for that we see not, then do we with patience wait for it.” What is that hope? That faith: Jesus Christ. That’s the only hope in the world that we got.

**[Romans 8:26-27](/bible/romans/8#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/romans/8#v26)</sup> Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/romans/8#v27)</sup> And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.

- “Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.” That’s the holy spirit bringing all things to our memories. When we pray, Christ is the intercessor, the in-between: he takes the right things up to the Most High. A lot of times you say a lot of nonsense and he’s like, nope, not taking that; I’ma take what needs to be done to the Most High. That’s why the Most High allowed this to happen: because we need Christ, because we ain’t right.
- “And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.” So when we say it wrong, he lines it up according to the will of God so it can be answered.

**[Romans 8:28-29](/bible/romans/8#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/romans/8#v28)</sup> And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/romans/8#v29)</sup> For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.

- “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose”: according to his purpose that was predestined for this. “For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son”. It ain’t for everybody; it ain’t for all Israel. We already established that a lot of our people ain’t going to receive it. “That he might be the firstborn among many brethren.”
- “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God”. What is good about what we have just been watching and listening to? All of it, because the Lord has been warning our people, our twelve tribes, for centuries in the Bible, and we have not been listening. We have not repented or returned to him. We went into strange religions and worshipped a strange god that you think is Jesus Christ. All things work together for good, and that includes the things we think are bad for us: there is a divine purpose behind it. “To them who are the called according to his purpose”: we are the called according to his purpose, our people.
- “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose”: we are called according to God’s purpose, and God has a purpose for every last one of us: the brothers on IT, on the audio, editing the videos, making the thumbnails, the brothers and sisters on security. You don’t have to have rank to have a purpose. You don’t have to be a soldier, or in the army, to do the work of God. We have programs set up: we need some of you on the medical team, some in the stewardship program. It is a nation we are building.
- Rank is for order. Rank is important; it is for structure and order. But some let it get to their heads: rank is not for you to be lord over anybody. You still have to do the work, and there are other things in the nation that we need to work together to get done.

  Precepts:
  - **[Daniel 12:1-3](/bible/daniel/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/daniel/12#v1)</sup> And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people: and there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/daniel/12#v2)</sup> And many of them that sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake, some to everlasting life, and some to shame and everlasting contempt.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/daniel/12#v3)</sup> And they that be wise shall shine as the brightness of the firmament; and they that turn many to righteousness as the stars for ever and ever.

    “And at that time shall Michael stand up, the great prince which standeth for the children of thy people”: he stands for the children of God’s people, the Israelites. “And there shall be a time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation even to that same time: and at that time thy people shall be delivered, every one that shall be found written in the book.” If your name is written in the book, brothers, that means you fulfilled your purpose. That’s why your name is in the book: you fulfilled what was predestinated for you.

    “For many are called, but few are chosen.” What will tell whether you are chosen or not is your actions. If you stay in the midst of adultery and keep doing all types of wickedness, carrying grudges and hatred toward your brothers and sisters, being deceitful, still dealing with hoes, buying prostitutes, God is not going to choose you. He called you, but you are not going to be chosen.
  - **[Ephesians 1:11](/bible/ephesians/1#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/1#v11)</sup> In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will:

    “In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will.” We were predestinated according to his purpose: the same calling “according to his purpose” of Romans 8:28.

**[Romans 8:30](/bible/romans/8#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/romans/8#v30)</sup> Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.

- “Moreover whom he did predestinate”: chosen from the beginning of time; this is my people forever. “them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified”: it is not everybody who is called and justified. The only ones called and justified are Israel.
- “Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified”: Paul is not saying everybody on the planet is going to be justified. “And whom he justified, them he also glorified”: glorified in his Son.
- “Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called”: both Jews and Greeks, whom he foreknew: Israelites. “And whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.” Precepts, please, not a concordance.
- “Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called”: moreover, there is more than verse 29. Whom he foreknew he predestinated, and them he called to be the elect, as Peter wrote: elect according to the foreknowledge of God the Father. “and whom he called, them he also justified”: justified through the blood of Christ, as in Romans 5. “and whom he justified, them he also glorified.”

  Precepts:

**[Romans 8:31-34](/bible/romans/8#v31)**

We’re not worthy of his Son, but he delivered him up for us. “How shall he not with him also freely give us all things? Who shall lay any thing to the charge of God’s elect? It is God that justifieth. Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us.”
- “If God be for us, who can be against us?” Meaning, who can conquer us? Who can destroy us? Nobody on this planet, no matter what comes our way.
- “What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us?” The Most High is trying to make it right for us, giving us ways out of these problems. He will never put you in a situation where you can't call your brother and fix it. Offend your brother joking around, and he has to make the phone call and make it right; if he doesn't, it falls on him.

My fault, we good. Problem solved, right then and there. Carry it for years and it becomes a part of you, and no apology fixes it. That is how black people do, not our righteous people. We have to be a righteous people again. These trials and tribulations are geared toward fixing us. Keep doing it the same way and expect it to change, and that's insanity. We have to change as a people, start moving in the right direction, and let the Lord take the rest.

**[Romans 8:35-39](/bible/romans/8#v35)**

> <sup>[35](/bible/romans/8#v35)</sup> Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/romans/8#v36)</sup> As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long; we are accounted as sheep for the slaughter.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/romans/8#v37)</sup> Nay, in all these things we are more than conquerors through him that loved us.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/romans/8#v38)</sup> For I am persuaded, that neither death, nor life, nor angels, nor principalities, nor powers, nor things present, nor things to come,
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/romans/8#v39)</sup> Nor height, nor depth, nor any other creature, shall be able to separate us from the love of God, which is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

- “Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?” It is a question. “As it is written, For thy sake we are killed all the day long”: the prophets, the servants of God. “We are accounted as sheep for the slaughter.”
- As it is written: Paul got this from Psalms 44. He didn’t just make things up and pull them out of the sky; he based it on the scriptures, which is why he would often tell Timothy to bring the books and the parchments so he could write.
- “Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? shall tribulation”: we think if we are catching hell in this world, that separates us from the love of Christ. It don’t. “Or distress”: being stressed in the present times doesn’t separate us. “Or persecution”: being persecuted doesn’t. “Or famine”: not having food and water doesn’t. “Or nakedness”: not having clothes doesn’t. “Or peril”: consistent peril doesn’t. “Or sword”: war. Our people in war-torn areas on the other side of the world are not separated from the love of Christ.
- This clarifies that mindset of just repenting and then being glorified. “Who shall separate us from the love of Christ?” In these last days, our family members are going to try to separate us from the love of God. When you read this, don’t read it the same no more. People say, oh, I didn’t make the sabbath today, who shall separate us. No, it’s much more than that. “Shall tribulation, or distress, or persecution, or famine, or nakedness, or peril, or sword?” That’s what it’s really talking about: life or death, torture; famine, not eating; nakedness, not having clothes; peril, troublesome times; sword, guns. This is real.
