## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 17-18, 25-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 16:17](/bible/romans/16#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/16#v17)</sup> Now I beseech you, brethren, mark them which cause divisions and offences contrary to the doctrine which ye have learned; and avoid them.

- “Mark them which cause divisions and offences.” Those are the “such” of verse 18. These are key indicating signs for us today. When you are in this body and you see somebody trying to cause division, whispering, murmuring, gossiping, pulling people to their side, you should know what is coming out of that mouth.
- If what you are doing is of God, you don’t have to hide it; you can say it openly, because you are speaking the oracles of God. But I’ll call you later, I’ll text you later? If you fall for that, you are simple. Even renowned brothers who have been around a while get caught, because the one baiting them can see something in them. A brother once asked another brother at the Sabbath to go to a strip club after: he had to have seen something in him to say that. That is contingency talk, clique talk, birds of a feather, and that clique stuff supersedes the love of God.
- If your conversation is not one of bringing solutions, even when there is a real problem in the body, you are part of the problem. God will sort it out; that is his job. Your job is to gather the 12 tribes. God will remove who he wants to remove.
- “And avoid them.” Which part of that don’t we understand?

**[Romans 16:18](/bible/romans/16#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/16#v18)</sup> For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple.

- “For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple.” The 250 renowned men were simple men. They had made bricks in Egypt, seen the sea opened and closed on their enemies, and then followed a man who could only talk. Show me you open the ocean. Christ came back from the dead; beat that one. You know them by their fruits: these talkers have little or no fruit in this body, and the simple fall for it.
- This is who that pastor is. “For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly.” He is not really going out to pull these guys off the streets or help them change their lives; he is feeding his own belly, walking them into another part of hell with Cesare Borgia, the white angels and the Sunday lies. Singing and clapping and feeling good does not change your life; he is not giving them the words of the Bible.
- “And by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple.” He deceives them into worshipping on Sunday, the wrong day, looking at white angels and crosses and idols all over the church. Stained windows: really it is their souls that are stained.
- The gang members feel like a family, but that is not family, because they kill each other: Crips fighting Crips and Bloods fighting Bloods. We have to teach our people how to come out of that.

**[Romans 16:25](/bible/romans/16#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/16#v25)</sup> Now to him that is of power to stablish you according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret since the world began,

- “To stablish you according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ”: we are established to understand that Jesus Christ, the black Messiah, died to save us from our sin. “According to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret since the world began.” People don’t know this: we were the first to tell you Christ died exclusively for the Israelites, and that you must keep the commandments to show you love him. Everything we teach was a secret since the world began, and now the Most High is revealing his plan.
- Everything gone over in this class is not regular. “Now to him that is of power to stablish you according to my gospel, and the preaching of Jesus Christ, according to the revelation of the mystery, which was kept secret since the world began.” What is the mystery kept secret since the world began? Christ. Everything in this lesson: who Christ is, his divinity, that he created everything, that he would come and die for our sin. That is the mystery told to Moses: hide these things; hide who I really am and what I have to come and do, because I don’t want everybody to understand, and be healed.
- Read again at the end: who are the secrets revealed to? Us.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 2:7](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/2#v7)</sup> But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom, which God ordained before the world unto our glory:

    “But we speak the wisdom of God in a mystery, even the hidden wisdom, which God ordained before the world unto our glory.” The class began with Christ as the wisdom of God. That hidden wisdom ordained unto our glory is Christ. He was hidden: the Pharisees, the scribes, our people couldn’t see who he really was in the scriptures.
  - **[Ephesians 3:3-6](/bible/ephesians/3#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/3#v3)</sup> How that by revelation he made known unto me the mystery; (as I wrote afore in few words,
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ephesians/3#v4)</sup> Whereby, when ye read, ye may understand my knowledge in the mystery of Christ)
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/3#v5)</sup> Which in other ages was not made known unto the sons of men, as it is now revealed unto his holy apostles and prophets by the Spirit;
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ephesians/3#v6)</sup> That the Gentiles should be fellowheirs, and of the same body, and partakers of his promise in Christ by the gospel:

    “How that by revelation he made known unto me the mystery; (as I wrote afore in few words, Whereby, when ye read, ye may understand my knowledge in the mystery of Christ)”. The mystery is the Israelites becoming one in Christ, both houses; and Christ himself is that mystery. Do not expect everybody to understand it.
  - **[Deuteronomy 29:29](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v29)</sup> The secret things belong unto the Lord our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.

    “The secret things belong unto the Lord our God: but those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law.” The mysteries, revealed, belong unto us and to our children: it is ancestral, a lineage thing, ours by birthright. You can’t just come in here as a good heathen and learn the secrets of the Bible. That isn’t how this works. And for how long? For ever.

**[Romans 16:26-27](/bible/romans/16#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/romans/16#v26)</sup> But now is made manifest, and by the scriptures of the prophets, according to the commandment of the everlasting God, made known to all nations for the obedience of faith:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/romans/16#v27)</sup> To God only wise, be glory through Jesus Christ for ever. Amen. Written to the Romans from Corinthus, and sent by Phebe servant of the church at Cenchrea.

- “But now is made manifest, and by the scriptures of the prophets”, then “made known to all nations for the obedience of faith.” The Lord put the spirit on us to preach, and we go throughout the world to all nations, telling them of the Israelites, the 12 tribes, doing what Christ said: go find the lost sheep of the house of Israel, and as you go, preach the kingdom. No other ministry is teaching that.
- “But now is made manifest”: to whom? “And by the scriptures of the prophets”: written by Israel. “According to the commandment of the everlasting God, made known to all nations for the obedience of faith.” Who are all nations here? Israel: the Israelites scattered among all nations. It is not talking about every man, as some say from the Greek; precept upon precept, rightly dividing the word of truth, here a little and there a little, it is the Israelites. Stay in the Bible and never leave.
