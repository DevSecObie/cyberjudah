## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 13 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 9-14, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 13:1](/bible/romans/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/13#v1)</sup> Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers. For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.

- This is the point of it all: we are all going to be subject to somebody. It had better be us as parents, because if our children are not subject to us and learning the correct ways from us, somebody else will teach them. It is a fearful thing to end up in the hands of the Lord; he will choose who teaches your kids if you aren't teaching them through these scriptures.
- “Let every soul be subject unto the higher powers.” That higher power is God. “For there is no power but of God: the powers that be are ordained of God.” The fathers and mothers set in place are set in place by God. Outside the household, in this organization, from the bishops down to the deacons, captains, officers, soldiers and brothers, all is set in place by God, structure and order, so you understand the levels of power.

**[Romans 13:2](/bible/romans/13#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/13#v2)</sup> Whosoever therefore resisteth the power, resisteth the ordinance of God: and they that resist shall receive to themselves damnation.

- “Whosoever therefore resisteth the power, resisteth the ordinance of God”. If you resist those instructions, you are resisting God. “And they that resist shall receive to themselves damnation.”

**[Romans 13:3](/bible/romans/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/13#v3)</sup> For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil. Wilt thou then not be afraid of the power? do that which is good, and thou shalt have praise of the same:

- “For rulers are not a terror to good works, but to the evil.” The rulers, the powers that be, are not a terror to good works, meaning to those that keep the commandments of God. Even when we are persecuted for keeping them, it is not a terror to us, because we know what it is about. We are in captivity, but we are doing what God said to do, and the ones who mistreat us are going to have to answer to the Father for that.
- There is a difference between suffering for righteousness’ sake and suffering as a murderer. Some of us are going to be locked up for doing what is right, and that takes a whole different mentality: I am being persecuted for a worthy cause, and the Lord is going to receive me because I did what was right. Suffering as a murderer, you are still going to get jacked up at the end, and you ain’t getting the kingdom.
- “Wilt thou then not be afraid of the power?” We were not afraid of our power, the Most High, before. We broke the commandments. So the Lord said, since you did not want to hearken unto me, I am going to put this virulent beast over you, and when you mess up with him you are going to be judged by him, and he ain’t going to have no pity on you.
- “but to the evil”: the rulers are a terror to those that do evil. The 20-year-old in the courtroom who got life is feeling that terror. He can’t say that the Lord is going to jack them up for what they are doing to him, because the reason he is getting jacked up is that he broke the laws of the Most High, suffering as a murderer. He broke down crying, because he is a wankster, not a gangster. What happened to all the gangsterism then, the What Up videos, the shoot them up, bang bang, make them hop double dutch? Nobody cares about your black ass once you are in the jail; whatever friends didn’t go down with you, your girlfriend, they have moved on.

**[Romans 13:4](/bible/romans/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/13#v4)</sup> For he is the minister of God to thee for good. But if thou do that which is evil, be afraid; for he beareth not the sword in vain: for he is the minister of God, a revenger to execute wrath upon him that doeth evil.

- “For he is the minister of God.” Esau, the powers that be, is the minister of God. In other words, he is God’s helper. That don’t sound good at all, but it is what it means. “For he is the minister of God to thee for good”: to you for your good, for you to straighten up.
- “But if thou do that which is evil”: evil against God’s laws. It ain’t talking about us teaching the Bible or being persecuted for righteousness’ sake. But when you are out here stealing, raping, robbing, committing crimes, a murderer, God ain’t with that. “be afraid”: that is when the terrors are going to come upon you.
- “for he beareth not the sword in vain.” He will use it. The police chase that turned into a shootout shows it: a car on its side, a man climbing out of it with a weapon in his hand shooting at police. That is somebody’s son. All the smash and grab, all the What Up, make them hop double dutch, bragged about on the radio and TV with little kids dancing to it, shaped in our people’s minds that they can’t be stopped, and it all ends up with this. The Lord gave the man with the badge all kinds of weaponry, because he is in God’s hands. Don’t get it wrong: the Lord is going to take him out too, in his time.
- The Lord was serious when he said, you break my commandments, you are going to serve your enemies and get torn up until you repent. God is affecting every thing on this planet. He put Esau there because we did not want to do right; that is the only reason we are in this mess. Our way out is keeping the commandments. If we don’t, we are going to be subjected to things like this and worse.

**[Romans 13:9-10](/bible/romans/13#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/13#v9)</sup> For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if there be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/13#v10)</sup> Love worketh no ill to his neighbour: therefore love is the fulfilling of the law.

- “Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet.” We are going to teach that to the people and change their minds through the words of the record book. Do not desire what somebody else has; work and get your own.
- “Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.” That is what they need in their spirit: not to hate or kill their neighbor, but to love him and build with him while he builds with you. That is how we build a nation.
- “Love worketh no ill to his neighbour: therefore love is the fulfilling of the law.”

**[Romans 13:11-12](/bible/romans/13#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/romans/13#v11)</sup> And that, knowing the time, that now it is high time to awake out of sleep: for now is our salvation nearer than when we believed.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/13#v12)</sup> The night is far spent, the day is at hand: let us therefore cast off the works of darkness, and let us put on the armour of light.

- “And that, knowing the time”: this is what we are supposed to be doing. We are supposed to know what time it is, not playing games. “That now it is high time to awake out of sleep”: you see what’s happening, but we are busy sensationalizing what’s on the news, the strikes on Iran, the new supreme leader, the oil, while we allow our spirits to stay rotten. Scripture says watch as well as pray, so we watch to know what’s going on. But you worrying about the bombs overseas, the damn bomb will come hit you in your damn head because you wicked as hell. Get yourselves right.
- “For now is our salvation nearer than when we believed.” Our salvation is right around the corner, and here we are still playing games, entertaining sin, entertaining evil. It is a few, not many, thank the Lord, but those few should let this pierce their spirit.
- As Deacon Yeshua put it: prophecy should move us to correction; it is not entertainment. We see the prophecy happening and we love the videos, but your spirit has to be disciplined to ward off the death that comes from still playing in your sins. You cannot stop any of the prophecy going on over there. What we had better be doing is acknowledging it, getting ourselves right in spite of it, and making sure we are on the right side of God’s judgment, knowing the Lord is on his way soon.
- “The night is far spent, the day is at hand”: it is high time, meaning high noon, the sun at twelve o’clock in the sky, and you are still sleeping in your sins. You should have been awake.

**[Romans 13:13-14](/bible/romans/13#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/13#v14)</sup> But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make not provision for the flesh, to fulfil the lusts thereof.

- “Not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.” All of this is going on with the BHIs and with certain brothers and sisters in the congregation, because they still think this is a joke, not realizing the Lord is making his move already, and the Most High’s judgment is going to catch up to them.
- “But put ye on the Lord Jesus Christ”: that is first and foremost. Put on the commandments.
- “And make not provision for the flesh”: don’t give in to what your flesh is aching to do, those impulsive temptations. If you have problems in this area or that area, remove yourself from it. You say you can stand it, you can meddle with this person, this porn, these drugs, this alcohol. You meddle with it and it will bite the hell out of you.
- “To fulfil the lusts thereof.” You are paying attention to the lusts while acting like you are all about what’s going on overseas, but in reality you are rotten in your spirit, playing games, and the Most High is going to turn your light on and you will catch hell.
