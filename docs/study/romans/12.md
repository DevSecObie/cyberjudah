## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 12 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-13, 17-19, 21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

- “That ye present your bodies a living sacrifice”: be willing to sacrifice your life for this truth when the time comes for it. “Holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.” Why is it your reasonable service? Because the Son of God died for you. You mean to tell me he’s not worth you giving your life for? And they can only kill the body, brothers; they cannot kill the inner man, the soul.
- “That ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God”: again, give yourself wholly.
- “That ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.” That is your damn service. Some want a cookie because they keep the law, because they mopped the floor, because they went to camp: you guys don’t appreciate me. You are here to do a job; do your damn job. You never tell the white man at your work to appreciate you.
- We must separate ourselves and get that mindset. When you come into the truth, you are not the same person. We might breathe the same air, but we are not the same person. That old negro been long gone.

**[Romans 12:2](/bible/romans/12#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/12#v2)</sup> And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.

- The world belongs to the Most High and his Son, not to no man on this earth. We know some of you come out of those lifestyles, and that’s okay, but in this gospel, in this truth, you’re going to be transformed. You will be changed or you will be put out.
- “And be not conformed to this world.” If you were a killer, a drug user, a drug addict, a pimp, a prostitute, a hoe, you will be transformed. To be conformed to this world means to stay in your sin: stay a drug dealer, drug user, homosexual, liar, thief. We’re not going to allow you to stay conformed to this world.
- “But be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind.” That’s what IUIC is about: you must and you will be transformed by the renewing of your mind.
- “That ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.” The only way for you to prove that this is the truth is when people see your transformation and say: I know him, I know her, she made a whole 180, they changed their life, and if it worked for them I know it could work for me.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Kings 8:47-51](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)</sup> And so return unto thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their enemies, which led them away captive, and pray unto thee toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, the city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name:
    >
    > <sup>[49](/bible/1-kings/8#v49)</sup> Then hear thou their prayer and their supplication in heaven thy dwelling place, and maintain their cause,
    >
    > <sup>[50](/bible/1-kings/8#v50)</sup> And forgive thy people that have sinned against thee, and all their transgressions wherein they have transgressed against thee, and give them compassion before them who carried them captive, that they may have compassion on them:
    >
    > <sup>[51](/bible/1-kings/8#v51)</sup> For they be thy people, and thine inheritance, which thou broughtest forth out of Egypt, from the midst of the furnace of iron:

    “Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives”: right now we’ve been bethinking ourselves. We are remembering who we are; the loss of identity is being taken from us; we’re remembering that we are the children of Israel, carried captive here in America. “And repent”: that’s what every man, woman, boy and girl in here is doing.
  - **[Hebrews 5:14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)</sup> But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.

    “But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.” By reason of use: repeated practice, repeated exposure, repeated exercise. That is how the renewing of the mind works.
  - **[Matthew 5:48](/bible/matthew/5#v48)**
    > <sup>[48](/bible/matthew/5#v48)</sup> Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect.

    “Be ye therefore perfect, even as your Father which is in heaven is perfect.” This is the perfect will of God that we are to prove: to become perfect and acceptable for the Lord.

**[Romans 12:3](/bible/romans/12#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/12#v3)</sup> For I say, through the grace given unto me, to every man that is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.

- “Not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think”: don’t get puffed up. “But to think soberly”: this is my brother, this is my sister; that’s how we gotta think soberly. “According as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.”
- Again, this is not about me, it’s not about you, it’s about our people. “Not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think; but to think soberly, according as God hath dealt to every man the measure of faith.” We all have the measure of faith, every man up in here, everyone listening, so we should not try to exalt ourselves above each other.
- Let me back up on that in case somebody gets the wrong thought: that doesn’t mean there’s not rank, structure and order. To think of yourself more highly is looking down on somebody, belittling, demeaning someone. It’s saying don’t do that.
- God has given us different gifts, different offices, but the same mission. Teacher, organizer, counselor, building with your hands, security: if you have experience in anything that could benefit the nation, you are here for a purpose. “For I say, through the grace given unto me, to every man that is among you, not to think of himself more highly than he ought to think.” Stay humble. We are all expendable; we can all be replaced if we don’t use our talent.

  Precepts:
  - **[Philippians 2:3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)</sup> Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.

    “Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory.” That spirit will unravel things, and people can see it when you move like that. Security brothers, be careful: you are able to tell people move here, move there, and you get ahead of yourself. That is your brother, that is your sister. Deal with one another like we are the same, the way you want to be dealt with. Some are hardheaded and you have to speak a certain way to let them know this needs to happen now, but no member is better than the next. We are all the same, working toward the same goal: we want the kingdom.

**[Romans 12:4-5](/bible/romans/12#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/romans/12#v4)</sup> For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/romans/12#v5)</sup> So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.

- “For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office”: all of us in here don’t have the same office. I’m not an IT guy. Yes, I yell at it, but they are very necessary; they’re important, I need them, we need them. Even the kitchen department: I’m not the best cook, there’s people that are chefs. We need them; they’re very important. “So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.”
- When Moses heard their charge he fell upon his face (Numbers 16:4): that’s what you think of me? They painted Moses as acting better than everybody else, a smear campaign to destroy his name, though Moses never said that. This is what those men struggled with: “all members have not the same office.” They don’t understand that.

**[Romans 12:6](/bible/romans/12#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/romans/12#v6)</sup> Having then gifts differing according to the grace that is given to us, whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith;

- “Whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith”: whatever God has inspired you with, run with that. You are made after his image, so it is all good, but he is not going to give any of us all of it, or you would be God. He makes us all work together, each with a piece. Those men tried to outshine Moses, and that is not what they were called for.
- “Whether prophecy, let us prophesy according to the proportion of faith”. Some of you may only be able to prophesy Deuteronomy 28. That’s fine, that’s good. If that’s all you know, prophesy that, talk about that, and it’s still happening. You say prophecy, that’s history? No: our people are still being sold in Libya, places like that, so it’s still going on. If you can prophesy Daniel 7, prophesy. Everyone has a different measure, but whatever level you’re at, prophesy that.

**[Romans 12:7](/bible/romans/12#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/12#v7)</sup> Or ministry, let us wait on our ministering: or he that teacheth, on teaching;

- “Or ministry, let us wait on our ministering.” Some people cannot wait on their ministry; they are speeding. God sets people in this body where he wants them. Either you roll with it, glorify God and be grateful, or you buck against it and find yourself out in the wilderness doing your own thing. Choose the first one.
- “Or ministry”, let us wait on our ministry. Sometimes you got to wait, be patient; that’s what patience is about. Whether it’s the Bishop, the deacons or the captains, if they say not yet, just wait, brother, not yet. When we started the records, one of the first groups came in young and did a song talking about when Adam and Eve ate the apple. I called the Deacon: what the hell am I hearing? Stop letting these young men do music; let them wait. They good now, but back then in the beginning they gave us hell with them records. Sit down, no more records for y’all.
- “He that teacheth, on teaching”: some of you are good teachers. Sometimes we say wait. But believe it or not, if the Bishops or deacons can’t do a class, a deacon will say, hey bro, can you teach class for me? That’s your opportunity, your time to shine, so when we walk in: who’s that brother teaching right there? He could teach good on the street, but he’s just as good in the classroom. Teaching in the class is not the same as teaching on the street.

**[Romans 12:8](/bible/romans/12#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/12#v8)</sup> Or he that exhorteth, on exhortation: he that giveth, let him do it with simplicity; he that ruleth, with diligence; he that sheweth mercy, with cheerfulness.

- “He that exhorteth, on exhortation”: an exhortation is a motivator. Some brothers have a great gift to motivate people; they make you do something you would never do before. They good motivators.
- “He that giveth, let him do it with simplicity”: some brothers have that good hospitable spirit. “He that ruleth, with diligence”: rule with diligence, meaning be consistent. “He that sheweth mercy, with cheerfulness”: be happy when you show mercy, when you forgive somebody. When a brother or sister says, you know what, I’m sorry, don’t take that spirit and go, you ain’t serious, you’re not sincere.

**[Romans 12:9](/bible/romans/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/12#v9)</sup> Let love be without dissimulation. Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good.

- The third step: learn to channel the sin and the pain we have suffered into righteousness. First, study, pray, apply; then turn from sin; now turn all this evil and mischief into righteousness, from the power of Satan to God, from darkness unto light, for repentance.
- “Let love be without dissimulation. Abhor that which is evil”: what we read before. “Cleave to that which is good.” Come home, family.

**[Romans 12:10](/bible/romans/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/romans/12#v10)</sup> Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another;

- “Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love; in honour preferring one another”: we ain’t been taught that. That’s something we got to write down and meditate, meditate, meditate, because that don’t come easy for Negroes and Latinos. At the beginning of the march today there was a group of Negroes on motorcycles that decided, you know what, F y’all, we going to disrupt the beginning of this. It takes a lot of patience; the thought did cross a brother’s mind, but he said no. Be kindly affectioned one to another: if we teach it and we preach it, we got to live it.
- “Be kindly affectioned one to another with brotherly love”: looking out for each other. “In honour preferring one another.” The Lord wants our spirit right for the kingdom: honour one another in our marriages, our relationships and our nation.

**[Romans 12:11](/bible/romans/12#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/romans/12#v11)</sup> Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord;

- “Not slothful in business”: you ain’t slothful when it comes to the white man’s business, but what about God’s business? That’s what it’s talking about. “Fervent in spirit”: be on fire in your spirit; have Holy Ghost fire when you teach, when you preach, when you put your hands to the plow. “Serving the Lord”: that lets you know it’s talking about God’s business.
- “Not slothful in business.” What is our business? We are called by God and here to magnify the law and increase our faith. “Fervent in spirit; serving the Lord”: by doing the work, helping your people.

**[Romans 12:12-13](/bible/romans/12#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/romans/12#v12)</sup> Rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/12#v13)</sup> Distributing to the necessity of saints; given to hospitality.

- “Rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation”: when we going through hard times, let all of us be patient in tribulation. “Continuing instant in prayer”: pray three times a day, evening, morning and noon. It has to become second nature to us, because we’re used to praying once, or some of us only pray when we eat. Damn.
- “Rejoicing in hope; patient in tribulation; continuing instant in prayer”: these are the steps. “Distributing to the necessity of saints.” What better life to live than to help your people?

**[Romans 12:17-18](/bible/romans/12#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/12#v17)</sup> Recompense to no man evil for evil. Provide things honest in the sight of all men.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/12#v18)</sup> If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.

- The ad department made an ad that said, ‘we are a peaceful people, but the God we serve is not.’ Somebody is saying, doesn’t the Bible call him the Prince of Peace? Yeah, he’s the Prince of Peace to the 12 tribes of Israel that obey.
- “If it be possible, as much as lieth in you, live peaceably with all men.” We are commanded to be a peaceful people: not in rioting, not in gathering weapons to cause civil unrest.
- We are not a hate group, and we do not teach violence against any race, ethnicity or gender; anyone who talks of picking up weapons or doing harm should be reported and put out, because it is a setup: COINTELPRO is still alive and agents are amongst us to monitor and disrupt. They are often the ones who help with everything but don’t know the scriptures and have no joy of the Lord in them. You can memorize scripture, but you cannot fake the spirit of Christ; this walk is spiritual.
- “Recompense to no man evil for evil.” When somebody does evil to us, we do not do evil back. The camp leaders and the brothers teaching on the street must not go back and forth arguing with the camp that follows us around to take our spots. If you get into an altercation, even if we are right, we are still wrong, because what Satan is trying to do is paint Israel in a bad light, as hateful and violent. Say shalom, record it and keep it moving.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 12:14](/bible/hebrews/12#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/12#v14)</sup> Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord:

    “Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord”. See what the Bible says: follow peace with all men. We are a peaceful people; the Bible teaches us to be peaceful. But to be peaceful don’t mean don’t teach. Our job is to teach.
  - **[Matthew 10:22](/bible/matthew/10#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/10#v22)</sup> And ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.

    “And ye shall be hated of all men for my name’s sake: but he that endureth to the end shall be saved.” This is the flip side of living peaceably with all men. Even though we live peaceably with all men, all men are going to hate us.

**[Romans 12:19](/bible/romans/12#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/romans/12#v19)</sup> Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves, but rather give place unto wrath: for it is written, Vengeance is mine; I will repay, saith the Lord.

- “Dearly beloved, avenge not yourselves.” Even back then there were zealots who rose up to fight against Rome; Paul says, do not take vengeance for yourself, “but rather give place unto wrath”, God’s wrath. A Christian reads the top, live peaceably with all men, and says you have to love everyone, and forgets the bottom, where God’s wrath is coming.
- “for it is written, Vengeance is mine”. The Lord says, I am the one who takes vengeance on Esau, the white man, for all the evil done on the earth; that belongs to me first and foremost, and you will get the leftovers. So we are not to take up weapons and go and fight the white man, because vengeance belongs to God, and he will pay them back for the slavery, the stealing and the oppression our people go through. Leave it to God: he is coming with wrath and judgment on everybody that did evil on this earth.

**[Romans 12:21](/bible/romans/12#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/12#v21)</sup> Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.

- It is not a sin to drink, but when drinking is used as an outlet for our problems, that is when we fall into damnation and condemnation. That is not the manner it is to be used in. When we are dealing with issues we rely on the scriptures and build our faith to overcome them.
- “Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good”: and the good is God’s laws.
- “Be not overcome of evil”: do not let this world destroy your faith. “But overcome evil with good”: with the good of these commandments and the faith of Christ, knowing who you are, you overcome that evil with the good of this Bible.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 8:13-14](/bible/romans/8#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/romans/8#v13)</sup> For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/romans/8#v14)</sup> For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.

    “For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die”: when we obey the flesh, making the bottle an outlet, death is at the end of that road. As in Habakkuk 2, there is always an evil road, and at its end you find death.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 31:28-29](/bible/sirach/31#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/31#v28)</sup> Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind:
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/31#v29)</sup> But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind, with brawling and quarrelling.

    “Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind: But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind”. Measurably. Bitterness of the mind is the key: the Lord can’t use you if you don’t have that balance.
