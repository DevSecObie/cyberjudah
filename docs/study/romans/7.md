## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Romans 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 7-8, 14-16, 20-25, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Romans 7:7](/bible/romans/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/romans/7#v7)</sup> What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.

- The first of three biblical facts: the law teaches us what sin is. “Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law.” Paul is saying he did not know what sin was except by the law.
- “For I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.” He did not understand the extent of what lust was until he understood what it meant to covet. So what teaches us what sin is? The law. The Christians treat keeping the commandments of God like that is the sin, when it is the opposite.
- “What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.” The only way you know you are in sin is by going back to God’s laws. You read that he said don’t do this, and you change it. Love me, keep the commandments: so I have to learn to love God’s commandments, fellowship, be around my family, build myself up, fast and pray, stop watching porn, stop smoking weed, stop being argumentative and contentious with my husband. You learn it through God’s laws. That is how you get the brain right.

**[Romans 7:8](/bible/romans/7#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/7#v8)</sup> But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law sin was dead.

- “But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence.” Wrought means it worked in me. Concupiscence is not a word our people know: it is strong desire, especially sexual desire; sex drive, lust, lustfulness, sexual passion, libido.
- “All manner” means different types. Paul never said which kind he was dealing with; he kept it open. The point is that while Paul was doing the work, there were evil thoughts in his head.
- “But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law sin was dead.” Concupiscence is an intense, often sensual longing or desire; in Christian teaching, the disordered human inclination to sin, from selfish, worldly or carnal appetites. That is the sinful nature, the shapen in iniquity: the inclination to sin. And we can change it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 7:21-23](/bible/romans/7#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/romans/7#v21)</sup> I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/romans/7#v22)</sup> For I delight in the law of God after the inward man:
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/7#v23)</sup> But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members.

    “I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me.” Even while Paul was doing good, doing the work, there was always that reminder, that demon in him. “For I delight in the law of God after the inward man”: the inward man is the spirit of Christ.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 23:16-17](/bible/sirach/23#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/23#v16)</sup> Two sorts of men multiply sin, and the third will bring wrath: a hot mind is as a burning fire, it will never be quenched till it be consumed: a fornicator in the body of his flesh will never cease till he hath kindled a fire.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/23#v17)</sup> All bread is sweet to a whoremonger, he will not leave off till he die.

    An example of concupiscence. “Two sorts of men multiply sin, and the third will bring wrath: a hot mind is as a burning fire, it will never be quenched till it be consumed”. A hot mind is a lustful mind. “A fornicator in the body of his flesh will never cease till he hath kindled a fire.” That is the inclination to sin.
  - **[Hebrews 3:13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)</sup> But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.

    “But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.” You get hardened because you have been in sin so long you start to think it is the only way. No: God’s laws are the way.

**[Romans 7:14-15](/bible/romans/7#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/7#v14)</sup> For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/7#v15)</sup> For that which I do I allow not: for what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I.

- “For we know that the law is spiritual: but I am carnal, sold under sin. For that which I do I allow not.” What we want to do, we allow not. When the thought of watching porn comes, you know what you should do, so why do you choose not to do it? That is in each one of us. Good and evil are always in our presence; those two thoughts are always going to come.
- “For what I would, that do I not; but what I hate, that do I.” When do you know you hate it? After you have done it. You know you hate it when you are already caught up in it; some call it post-nut clarity. What we should be doing is keeping the commandments. Why are we not doing it, and why do we call it evil only once we are caught? We are lying to ourselves.
- Christ said you must deny yourself. The you that does the denying is the part God is dealing with; the self is the composite of the conditioning you were made into. You have to check it against how you were made: examine your own thoughts and your own behaviours, and ask yourself, why am I behaving like this? Is this the real me, or is this a response to what I have been conditioned to be?
- “For that which I do I allow not”: what I end up doing because of conditioning, the real me, my spirit, does not allow. “for what I would, that do I not”: what my spirit would want to do, I don’t do, because the conditioned person says don’t follow the righteousness of your real spirit. That is the duality that goes on with us every day.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 7:19-20](/bible/romans/7#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/romans/7#v19)</sup> For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/romans/7#v20)</sup> Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.

    Paul clears up what he said before: “For the good that I would I do not: but the evil which I would not, that I do.” Now it is plain.

**[Romans 7:16](/bible/romans/7#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/romans/7#v16)</sup> If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that it is good.

- “If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that it is good.” That is the fight, brothers and sisters. There is a duality, and the real us has to step forward.
- The conditioned us is the man out in front, the mask we show the world. The real soul of us is buried, hiding, scared. Most of the time, dealing with our people, you are dealing with the mask, the representative, while the real them is still scared down in the bottom of their shoes. We have to encourage that real spirit to come forward and be the image God made, rather than the conditioning you see in the mask.
- “If then I do that which I would not, I consent unto the law that it is good.” That is what God gave us the laws for: to let us know how to do good. The laws help you stay on that path, that course of doing good and being righteous, because we are susceptible to sin, to doing evil.

**[Romans 7:20](/bible/romans/7#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/romans/7#v20)</sup> Now if I do that I would not, it is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.

- “It is no more I that do it, but sin that dwelleth in me.” If you do things you are not supposed to do, it is not you, but sin that lies in you.
- A lot of brothers and sisters went off because of the root of bitterness, because they don’t know how to fight it or how to deal with the evil thoughts that keep coming to their mind. When Satan attacks, he keeps putting thoughts in your mind and you keep listening to him.

**[Romans 7:21](/bible/romans/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/7#v21)</sup> I find then a law, that, when I would do good, evil is present with me.

- “When I would do good, evil is present with me.” Evil is present with all of us all the time. Here you are repenting and trying to do good, and here comes Satan, the roaring lion seeking whom he may devour, causing problems and tempting you in different ways. When you will do good, Satan is always there.

**[Romans 7:22](/bible/romans/7#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/romans/7#v22)</sup> For I delight in the law of God after the inward man:

- “For I delight in the law of God after the inward man.” The way we delight in the law of God is from the inward man, meaning in your mind: something you cannot see, the inner you.
- “For I delight in the law of God after the inward man.” You might think there is only one law that enters in, God’s law.

**[Romans 7:23](/bible/romans/7#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/romans/7#v23)</sup> But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind, and bringing me into captivity to the law of sin which is in my members.

- “But I see another law in my members, warring against the law of my mind.” There is something in you that wars against your mind. You want to do good, you want to do right, but something is telling you evil all the time. That is Satan. The thoughts warring against your mind are Satan.
- Somebody says something to you and it triggers your mind: who the hell that person think they are, who he think he talking to, I could be this dude mother. Some of the officers are disrespectful to the people at times, and when that is found out their rank will be taken. But even if the brother was wrong in how he dealt with you, that is Satan whispering all types of things, until all of a sudden your mind says, I don’t like him. You still cannot entertain those evil thoughts, because they turn into bitterness. Everybody here is trying to become perfect and people are not perfect; people will offend you. Either go to that person and fix it, but you cannot sit there and become bitter and hateful.
- Brothers are not supposed to stop talking to a brother because of a sister. That is weird, that is bitterness. Sisters do it too: two sisters were cool, both liked the same brother, he chose one, and now they stop talking. That is bitterness and that is fake. We cannot rule like that. If that brother is for you, he is for you. Brothers who stop talking to each other because of a woman, I ain’t talking to you because you talking to the sister and I like the sister first, that thing is so fake; that is fake and phony, mark them, and they had better repent.
- There is a war that goes on in every last one of our minds, the war between good and evil: you want to do good and Satan is whispering in your ear to do evil. “And bringing me into captivity to the law of sin”: that is what it is trying to do, bring you into captivity, the captivity of sin, the captivity of bitterness.

**[Romans 7:24-25](/bible/romans/7#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/romans/7#v24)</sup> O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/romans/7#v25)</sup> I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God; but with the flesh the law of sin.

- “O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death? I thank God through Jesus Christ our Lord. So then with the mind I myself serve the law of God.” That is how we serve God and how we fight Satan: with the mind. Everything starts with your mind, and your mind controls your thoughts.
- When evil thoughts come to your mind concerning your brother or your sister, you have to shake it off. The root of bitterness will destroy you. Don’t play with bitterness, don’t play with hatred, don’t play with envy. When these things start creeping up in you, shake it off.
- “O wretched man that I am! who shall deliver me from the body of this death?” This body is a body of death. From the day you are born you are heading towards death, and the only thing on the other side of that is the preservation of your soul, which only God can do. That is why you fear God, who can destroy body and soul, and not men, who can only destroy the body.
- For you to do God’s will on this earth, you’re going to have to have it in your mind. That’s why our people don’t understand: when you say you’re not here to keep any laws, you cannot lead anyone in this earth. You’re going to lead them by your imagination, by your mindset, by your own will. This is the will of God: we keep his commandments.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 7:24](/bible/2-esdras/7#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/2-esdras/7#v24)</sup> But his law have they despised, and denied his covenants; in his statutes have they not been faithful, and have not performed his works.

    “But his law have they despised, and denied his covenants; in his statutes have they not been faithful, and have not performed his works.” We just read that we have to perform his work. That’s why he said we must serve: when you serve somebody, you do what the hell he wants you to do. We must serve the law in our mind.
  - **[Mark 7:21](/bible/mark/7#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/mark/7#v21)</sup> For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts, adulteries, fornications, murders,

    There is a war going on inside that mind. “For from within, out of the heart of men, proceed evil thoughts.” Now we learn something about the mind: evil comes from inside the mind. That’s why they tell you to upload the law into the mind, because otherwise you’re going to go according to your own mind and do whatever you want to do. Upload the law, man: inside the same mind evil thoughts are coming out.
  - **[Genesis 6:5](/bible/genesis/6#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/6#v5)</sup> And God saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.

    Everything begins inside that mind, brothers. Everything you see begins in the mind. “And God saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every imagination of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually.” Every imagination of his thoughts, in his mind, continually to do evil: that’s what the mind possesses. And out of the same mind good comes too; there are going to be some good thoughts. But the Lord wants you to know where the beginnings are: your mind. It’s a war going on.
