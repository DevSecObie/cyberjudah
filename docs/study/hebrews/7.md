## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 7 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 5-6, 9-10, 12-14, 17-19, 21-28, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 7:1-2](/bible/hebrews/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/7#v1)</sup> For this Melchisedec, king of Salem, priest of the most high God, who met Abraham returning from the slaughter of the kings, and blessed him;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/7#v2)</sup> To whom also Abraham gave a tenth part of all; first being by interpretation King of righteousness, and after that also King of Salem, which is, King of peace;

- “King of righteousness”, “King of peace”: you can read both titles in Isaiah 9. This Melchisedec is Christ; those are his titles.
- Melchisedec, “King of righteousness, and after that also King of Salem”: the king of peace, acknowledged as high priest by Abraham, who “gave a tenth part of all.”

**[Hebrews 7:3](/bible/hebrews/7#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/7#v3)</sup> Without father, without mother, without descent, having neither beginning of days, nor end of life; but made like unto the Son of God; abideth a priest continually.

- “Having neither beginning of days, nor end of life”: because he’s the Alpha and Omega.
- “Without father, without mother, without descent.” This is no proof of a virgin birth. It speaks of a spiritual being in the heavens: no record of his lineage anywhere, no earthly father or mother, yet acknowledged as high priest by Abraham. Christ in the heavens had no earthly father or mother, but he had to be born into this world and become a son of man, made a little lower than the angels.
- And the Christians cannot use it either: it says without father and without mother, and by their own doctrine Christ had a mother. This is a spiritual being, and Christ did not take on the nature of angels.

**[Hebrews 7:5-6](/bible/hebrews/7#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/7#v5)</sup> And verily they that are of the sons of Levi, who receive the office of the priesthood, have a commandment to take tithes of the people according to the law, that is, of their brethren, though they come out of the loins of Abraham:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/7#v6)</sup> But he whose descent is not counted from them received tithes of Abraham, and blessed him that had the promises.

- “They that are of the sons of Levi, who receive the office of the priesthood”: those that come out of the seed of Aaron; not all the Levites were priests. “But he whose descent is not counted from them received tithes of Abraham”: Melchisedec did not have a written lineage; that’s why Paul mentioned it above.

**[Hebrews 7:9-10](/bible/hebrews/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/7#v9)</sup> And as I may so say, Levi also, who receiveth tithes, payed tithes in Abraham.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/7#v10)</sup> For he was yet in the loins of his father, when Melchisedec met him.

- “Levi also, who receiveth tithes, payed tithes in Abraham. For he was yet in the loins of his father”: Levi was swimming in Abraham’s testicles.

**[Hebrews 7:12](/bible/hebrews/7#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/7#v12)</sup> For the priesthood being changed, there is made of necessity a change also of the law.

- “For the priesthood being changed, there is made of necessity a change also of the law”: one of those changes is uncover your head when you pray and prophesy, where the priests were wearing mitres. Another is that the priesthood would now come from Judah; it always was, Genesis the 49th chapter, the 12th verse, we read about that.

**[Hebrews 7:13-14](/bible/hebrews/7#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/7#v13)</sup> For he of whom these things are spoken pertaineth to another tribe, of which no man gave attendance at the altar.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/7#v14)</sup> For it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda; of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood.

- “Of which no man gave attendance at the altar”: another one, one wife. Brothers try to be slick and denounce titles, I don’t want to be a bishop or deacon no more, because deacons got to keep one wife. Very wise to do evil, Israel. “For it is evident that our Lord sprang out of Juda”: he’s talking about Christ. “Of which tribe Moses spake nothing concerning priesthood”: or did he? In Genesis 49 we read about the law and the sceptre: the sceptre shall not depart from Judah. The Most High held Moses a long season, showed him mysteries, and said reveal these plainly and make these hard to be understood. That’s why the Pharisees and scribes were stumbling over Christ; they had the Torah and the words of the prophets, not the New Testament.

**[Hebrews 7:17](/bible/hebrews/7#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/7#v17)</sup> For he testifieth, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchisedec.

- The class read these precepts with it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 14:18](/bible/genesis/14#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/14#v18)</sup> And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God.

    A brother added this. “And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God”: Melchisedec was a priest long before there was a Levitical priesthood, which goes back to “he whose descent is not counted from them”. Now Christ is that priest, revealed to our understanding; Paul was revealing that Melchisedec was Christ.

**[Hebrews 7:18-19](/bible/hebrews/7#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/7#v18)</sup> For there is verily a disannulling of the commandment going before for the weakness and unprofitableness thereof.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/hebrews/7#v19)</sup> For the law made nothing perfect, but the bringing in of a better hope did; by the which we draw nigh unto God.

- “For there is verily a disannulling of the commandment going before for the weakness and unprofitableness thereof”: that’s the law of animal sacrifice. “For the law made nothing perfect”: because we would go back into the sin; remember Jeremiah said you’re a homeborn slave, and in Isaiah the 1st chapter the Most High tells you he wants obedience more than burnt offerings. “But the bringing in of a better hope did”: hope in Christ, the New Covenant.

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 4:9](/bible/galatians/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/galatians/4#v9)</sup> But now, after that ye have known God, or rather are known of God, how turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements, whereunto ye desire again to be in bondage?

    Where did we read about the weakness and unprofitableness earlier? A brother behind the camera, without his Bible open, answered Galatians; he’s been on point all night, and that makes my teeth white, like Bishop says. “How turn ye again to the weak and beggarly elements”: the weak and beggarly elements are the same weakness and unprofitableness here in Hebrews 7:18, the law of animal sacrifice.

**[Hebrews 7:22](/bible/hebrews/7#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/hebrews/7#v22)</sup> By so much was Jesus made a surety of a better testament.

- “By so much was Jesus made a surety of a better testament”: that better testament is the New Covenant.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 8:6](/bible/hebrews/8#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/8#v6)</sup> But now hath he obtained a more excellent ministry, by how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant, which was established upon better promises.

    “By how much also he is the mediator of a better covenant”: who was the mediator of the old covenant? Moses. Who is the mediator of the New Covenant? Christ. Very simple to understand.

**[Hebrews 7:23-25](/bible/hebrews/7#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/hebrews/7#v23)</sup> And they truly were many priests, because they were not suffered to continue by reason of death:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/hebrews/7#v24)</sup> But this man, because he continueth ever, hath an unchangeable priesthood.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/hebrews/7#v25)</sup> Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them.

- “Because they were not suffered to continue by reason of death”: they didn’t have everlasting life like Jesus Christ has. “But this man, because he continueth ever, hath an unchangeable priesthood”: here we are in 2024 still in captivity, no temple, the old covenant done away with, so we have a New Covenant with a priest that cannot die.
- The Catholic Church in Central and South America has taken in elements of the indigenous peoples of the Americas, the ones we read about in 2 Esdras, and of the enslaved Africans, so long as they are remade to conform to a Christian worldview. The image of Our Lady of Guadalupe is one of them. Their prayer to her is said in the name of the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit (the Trinity) and asks her to make intercession for the church. That would make her the mediator.
- But Christ is the one: “But this man, because he continueth ever, hath an unchangeable priesthood. Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him, seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them.” Christ makes intercession for us, not a woman. That is woman worship, the same thing Paul had to address in the church of Corinth, the same spirit of Diana coming back up.
- “Wherefore he is able also to save them to the uttermost that come unto God by him”: when Christ died, where was the northern kingdom and the outcast remnants of Judah? On this side of the world. After 70 AD we were all scattered; when Paul was teaching we were scattered among the nations, and they came back to the land for Pentecost in Acts 2. “Seeing he ever liveth to make intercession for them”: he’s in the heavens making intercession for us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Nehemiah 1:9](/bible/nehemiah/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/nehemiah/1#v9)</sup> But if ye turn unto me, and keep my commandments, and do them; though there were of you cast out unto the uttermost part of the heaven, yet will I gather them from thence, and will bring them unto the place that I have chosen to set my name there.

    To save those at the uttermost. “Though there were of you cast out unto the uttermost part of the heaven”: just because the northern kingdom took it upon themselves to go to a place where never mankind dwelt doesn’t mean that’s not being cast out, because our land is Jerusalem; the fourth part of the world was given to the heathens, to Esau. Papua New Guinea is the uttermost part of the world; they ain’t sacrificing over there; there ain’t no temple over there. “Yet will I gather them from thence, and will bring them unto the place that I have chosen to set my name there”: that hasn’t happened yet; it’s another stage of the New Covenant. Novices, write it in your Bible like we do in Israel United in Christ, to end the cobwebs of confusion.

**[Hebrews 7:26](/bible/hebrews/7#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/hebrews/7#v26)</sup> For such an high priest became us, who is holy, harmless, undefiled, separate from sinners, and made higher than the heavens;

- “For such an high priest became us”: Christ was made a little lower, in the image of man. “Who is holy, harmless, undefiled”: not like us, defiled with pornography, adultery, fornication, idolatry, religion, lies. “And made higher than the heavens”: because he made the heavens.

**[Hebrews 7:27-28](/bible/hebrews/7#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/hebrews/7#v27)</sup> Who needeth not daily, as those high priests, to offer up sacrifice, first for his own sins, and then for the people’s: for this he did once, when he offered up himself.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/hebrews/7#v28)</sup> For the law maketh men high priests which have infirmity; but the word of the oath, which was since the law, maketh the Son, who is consecrated for evermore.

- “Who needeth not daily, as those high priests, to offer up sacrifice, first for his own sins, and then for the people’s”: the priests had to sacrifice for themselves and then the people. “For this he did once, when he offered up himself”: Christ got on the cross once, and he’s not going to do it again. “Maketh the Son, who is consecrated for evermore.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 55:3-4](/bible/isaiah/55#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/55#v3)</sup> Incline your ear, and come unto me: hear, and your soul shall live; and I will make an everlasting covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/55#v4)</sup> Behold, I have given him for a witness to the people, a leader and commander to the people.

    “Incline your ear, and come unto me”. “And I will make an everlasting covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David”: because Christ came out of the lineage of David; the Most High promised David he would raise a saviour out of his seed to establish his throne forever. Mercy comes through Christ, who is from David. “A leader and commander to the people.”
  - **[Acts 13:34-35](/bible/acts/13#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/acts/13#v34)</sup> And as concerning that he raised him up from the dead, now no more to return to corruption, he said on this wise, I will give you the sure mercies of David.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/acts/13#v35)</sup> Wherefore he saith also in another psalm, Thou shalt not suffer thine Holy One to see corruption.

    Where our forefathers mention the same thing. “I will give you the sure mercies of David.” “Thou shalt not suffer thine Holy One to see corruption”: Christ rose on the third day; his spirit wasn’t corrupted with sin, nor did his body see corruption in the ground.
