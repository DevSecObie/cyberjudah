## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-6, 10, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 6:1](/bible/hebrews/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/6#v1)</sup> Therefore leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ, let us go on unto perfection; not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works, and of faith toward God,

- “Therefore leaving the principles of the doctrine of Christ”: the principles of the doctrine of Christ are the animal sacrifices. Principle means the beginning, and in the beginning we got forgiveness of sin through animal sacrifice. We are to leave it: “let us go on unto perfection”, because there was no perfection in animal sacrifice.
- “Not laying again the foundation of repentance from dead works”: repentance with animal sacrifice. “And of faith toward God,” ends with a comma, so he is continuing the list of what we have to leave. Stress the word leaving.

**[Hebrews 6:2](/bible/hebrews/6#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/6#v2)</sup> Of the doctrine of baptisms, and of laying on of hands, and of resurrection of the dead, and of eternal judgment.

- “Of the doctrine of baptisms”: we must leave the doctrine of baptisms, and that includes the water baptism of John the Baptist. John did not make it up; it came from the priests’ washings, and Christ never used water, because it was always symbolic and was to end. That is the blasphemy Rome put on us when it taught that you must be water baptized to be saved.
- “And of laying on of hands”: what Aaron did over the goat. “And of resurrection of the dead”: the sin offering made for the dead. “And of eternal judgment.” Christ is the new high priest, and all the things the priests did, we have to leave.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 9:10](/bible/hebrews/9#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/9#v10)</sup> Which stood only in meats and drinks, and divers washings, and carnal ordinances, imposed on them until the time of reformation.

    The old ordinances “stood only in meats and drinks, and divers washings, and carnal ordinances, imposed on them until the time of reformation.” The divers washings are the baptisms, and they were imposed only until the time of reformation. That is why Hebrews 6 says to leave the doctrine of baptisms.
  - **[Exodus 30:19](/bible/exodus/30#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/30#v19)</sup> For Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet thereat:

    Where did the washing come from? God gave it to the priests: “For Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet thereat”. This is where John the Baptist got it from.
  - **[Isaiah 1:16](/bible/isaiah/1#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/1#v16)</sup> Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil;

    What the washing symbolized: “Wash you, make you clean; put away the evil of your doings from before mine eyes; cease to do evil”. The water was a sign of putting away the evil of your doings.

**[Hebrews 6:3](/bible/hebrews/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/6#v3)</sup> And this will we do, if God permit.

- “And this will we do, if God permit.” When you read Paul’s letters, they were still doing these things. It finally came to an end, with no ifs, ands or buts, in 70 AD, when Esau destroyed the temple, put the priests in slavery, and many of us fled into Africa. That ended the argument.

**[Hebrews 6:4-6](/bible/hebrews/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/6#v4)</sup> For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened, and have tasted of the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/6#v5)</sup> And have tasted the good word of God, and the powers of the world to come,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/6#v6)</sup> If they shall fall away, to renew them again unto repentance; seeing they crucify to themselves the Son of God afresh, and put him to an open shame.

- “For it is impossible for those who were once enlightened”, enlightened with Christ and the understanding, “and have tasted of the heavenly gift, and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost, and have tasted the good word of God, and the powers of the world to come”.
- “If they shall fall away”: that was the fight. Some Israelites said we have to go back to animal sacrifice and water baptism. If you leave Christ and go back to that, it is impossible “to renew them again unto repentance; seeing they crucify to themselves the Son of God afresh, and put him to an open shame.” They never believed. When brothers and sisters leave this truth and go back to white Jesus and water baptism, we cannot help them.
- “It is impossible for those who were once enlightened”: those who once learned the truth, “and have tasted of the heavenly gift”, the Holy Spirit that healed people in Matthew 12 and Mark 3, “and were made partakers of the Holy Ghost”, the very Holy Ghost Christ said not to blaspheme. “And have tasted the good word of God, and the powers of the world to come”: they learned of Christ our king, the black messiah, arriving to deliver us out of this captivity, out of Babylon called America.
- “If they shall fall away”, if you reject it, it is impossible “to renew them again unto repentance; seeing they crucify to themselves the Son of God afresh, and put him to an open shame.” They kill Christ all over again: blasphemy. You put him to an open shame when you come out and say, that’s a cult, it don’t matter what color Christ is, I used to be a Hebrew Israelite, why I left IUIC.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 10:26-31](/bible/hebrews/10#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/hebrews/10#v26)</sup> For if we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins,
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/hebrews/10#v27)</sup> But a certain fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation, which shall devour the adversaries.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/hebrews/10#v28)</sup> He that despised Moses’ law died without mercy under two or three witnesses:
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/hebrews/10#v29)</sup> Of how much sorer punishment, suppose ye, shall he be thought worthy, who hath trodden under foot the Son of God, and hath counted the blood of the covenant, wherewith he was sanctified, an unholy thing, and hath done despite unto the Spirit of grace?
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/hebrews/10#v30)</sup> For we know him that hath said, Vengeance belongeth unto me, I will recompense, saith the Lord. And again, The Lord shall judge his people.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/hebrews/10#v31)</sup> It is a fearful thing to fall into the hands of the living God.

    “If we sin wilfully after that we have received the knowledge of the truth, there remaineth no more sacrifice for sins, But a certain fearful looking for of judgment and fiery indignation, which shall devour the adversaries.” That is the hellfire waiting.

**[Hebrews 6:10](/bible/hebrews/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/6#v10)</sup> For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister.

- “For God is not unrighteous to forget your work and labour of love, which ye have shewed toward his name, in that ye have ministered to the saints, and do minister.” The Most High God is not going to forget the brothers. As Paul said, there is a reward: for the work, the bricks, the blood and sweat put into this truth.
- And the sisters: if you make a sandwich for a brother, for a prophet, you have ministered to the saints, and God is not going to forget that. There is a reward for that. Just as we need the men, we need the sisters too; we need each other to build this kingdom.
