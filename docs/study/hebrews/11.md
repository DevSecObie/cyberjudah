## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 11 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 6, 8-11, 24-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 11:1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)</sup> Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

- A mind is a terrible thing to waste. The mind of the Israelites, men and women, is a precious thing: the Lord called you out of darkness into his marvelous light because you have a mind to do great things. Why give up on yourself and go back to your own vomit? Many came in with good gifts, grew weak hearted, went back into the world, and are doing nothing at all.
- Think about our forefathers in chattel slavery, who had nothing near the opportunities we have. They endured years of lynchings, rapes, being tarred and feathered and ripped apart by horses. A husband had to walk out of the slave quarters while the master’s visitors soiled his wife, then come back in with his manhood stepped on; and the wife, after all that, still had to console her husband and make him feel like a man. He did not grab bottles of alcohol and waste away, the way brothers are doing today over their problems. They persevered with no relief in sight, not knowing when emancipation was coming.
- “Now faith is the substance of things hoped for”: our people in slavery were hoping for freedom, and faith helped them persevere through tunnels of darkness with no light in sight. Faith is hoping for something that is not in your immediate grasp, not even in your reachable grasp.
- “the evidence of things not seen”: the faith in their minds drove them toward something not even in existence yet, and pulled them through all those years without knowing when the end was. Like Joseph in captivity: for nearly 13 to 14 years he did not know he would be brought out, but he knew there is a God, and he stayed until the Lord decided to deal with him.

**[Hebrews 11:2](/bible/hebrews/11#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/11#v2)</sup> For by it the elders obtained a good report.

- “For by it the elders obtained a good report.” A report is action, a reputation of doing something. It was not just that Abraham, Sarah, Job, David and Aaron believed and so got through. There were actions behind the faith they had.

**[Hebrews 11:3](/bible/hebrews/11#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/11#v3)</sup> Through faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God, so that things which are seen were not made of things which do appear.

- “Through faith we understand that the worlds were framed by the word of God”. Everything happens because the Lord allows it. There is a power behind what your eyes see; what you see is not always what it is. Remember the servant whose eyes had to be opened to see the army with the prophet. In these latter days, do not lean on your own understanding, speak foolishly, or lose faith because you cannot see what God is doing. Put your trust in God and stay upon this faith. The Lord can be doing something greater than the thing you are looking at.

**[Hebrews 11:6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)</sup> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.

- There are necessary steps all of us got to take to be counted worthy in God’s good graces in this coming Kingdom, and here is one of the steps we must have: “But without faith it is impossible to please him.” If you don’t have faith that these words are true, you cannot please God. Faith is paramount; that is what the Lord is requiring of us.
- The longer we doubt, the longer we have fear, the longer we are not pleasing in the sight of God: “But without faith it is impossible to please him.” So we have to let go of that. We have to let go of the weak nature, and the weak nature is going into addictions and distractions, things like social media, lust, whatever it may be. We have to let go of these things.
- “But without faith it is impossible to please him.” If you have faith, you cannot be bashful.
- What is on the mind of a quiet, shy, timid brother? His mind is always on what other people are thinking, and you don’t know what he is thinking. When you are at war with brothers that are shy, timid and bashful, how are you going to trust that he won’t run on you, that he will defend you when a gun or a knife or an unruly mob comes out? You don’t know. It is a deceitful spirit: they hide in the cut. If you have faith you won’t be shy or timid, and brothers will know where you stand.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 9:7](/bible/2-esdras/9#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-esdras/9#v7)</sup> And every one that shall be saved, and shall be able to escape by his works, and by faith, whereby ye have believed,

    “And every one that shall be saved”: and everybody wants to be saved when this coming destruction comes. “And shall be able to escape by his works”: the works is keeping the commandments. “And by faith”: for without faith it is impossible to please God. Works and faith, “whereby ye have believed.” To escape, it takes both.
  - **[2 Corinthians 5:6-9](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v6)</sup> Therefore we are always confident, knowing that, whilst we are at home in the body, we are absent from the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v7)</sup> (For we walk by faith, not by sight:)
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v8)</sup> We are confident, I say, and willing rather to be absent from the body, and to be present with the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v9)</sup> Wherefore we labour, that, whether present or absent, we may be accepted of him.

    “Therefore we are always confident, knowing that, whilst we are at home in the body, we are absent from the Lord.” That is another understanding of death: while we are home in this body we are absent from the Lord.
  - **[Luke 17:5](/bible/luke/17#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/luke/17#v5)</sup> And the apostles said unto the Lord, Increase our faith.

    Christ told the apostles to forgive, even seventy times seven, and they answered: “Increase our faith.” If we are going to let go of all that, we need the Lord to increase our faith. That should be our prayer: that the Lord increase our faith to get us past these roadblocks.

**[Hebrews 11:8](/bible/hebrews/11#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/11#v8)</sup> By faith Abraham, when he was called to go out into a place which he should after receive for an inheritance, obeyed; and he went out, not knowing whither he went.

- “By faith Abraham, when he was called to go out into a place”: Abraham was called, requested of God to go out. He was a sojourner, in a sense homeless, until he came to a land where he could facilitate living. He had wealth, camels and everything, but no roof over his head.
- “Obeyed”: obedience is an action. “And he went out, not knowing whither he went”: he trusted the Lord, who told him, get thee out of the land of your nativity, and go.

**[Hebrews 11:9](/bible/hebrews/11#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/11#v9)</sup> By faith he sojourned in the land of promise, as in a strange country, dwelling in tabernacles with Isaac and Jacob, the heirs with him of the same promise:

- “Dwelling in tabernacles with Isaac and Jacob”: tents, as was common in our culture. If we miss one night in our house, we begin to lose faith; Abraham our father showed integrity through the hard times. We are going to have to get back to dwelling in booths, tabernacles or caves with our children, our wives and our families. Who else did it but us? We are them; they are us.

**[Hebrews 11:10](/bible/hebrews/11#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/11#v10)</sup> For he looked for a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker is God.

- “For he looked for a city which hath foundations, whose builder and maker is God.” The things written aforetime are written for our learning. We need to prepare to be able to sojourn, not in our comfort: bug-in and bug-out bags, a plan for when things pop off, having these conversations with your children and building them up now, not at some certain age, and building up your wife, so that without amazement she is not confused or afraid.

**[Hebrews 11:11](/bible/hebrews/11#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/11#v11)</sup> Through faith also Sara herself received strength to conceive seed, and was delivered of a child when she was past age, because she judged him faithful who had promised.

- “Through faith also Sara herself received strength to conceive seed.” Sarah is brought out with Abraham because, after dealing with the individual, Job, in tribulation, the faith of the marriage, man and woman together, must be built up for hard times.

**[Hebrews 11:24-25](/bible/hebrews/11#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/hebrews/11#v24)</sup> By faith Moses, when he was come to years, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/hebrews/11#v25)</sup> Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season;

- Battle fatigue is a term often used in class, and some use it out of context. Battle fatigue is when you are tired of obeying God, tired of keeping God's commandments. You'd rather be a ho, a drug addict or a drug dealer, or a homo, than keep yourself disciplined day after day and night after night. You are willing to lose salvation and destroy your family over battle fatigue, and we have seen it in here: I just want to be a ho, if only for a night.
- “By faith Moses, when he was come to years, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter; Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season”. That is battle fatigue: when you want to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season, when you want to stop being obedient just for a little while and go into your sin.
- Sisters say, oh, I'm suffering battle fatigue. Sister, do you know what battle fatigue is? You are in a fight, you are losing, and you are giving up. In this spiritual fight you are giving up on God, on his laws, his statutes, his commandments, his precepts.
- “By faith Moses, when he was come to years, refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter; Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God.” Moses refused to be called the son of Pharaoh’s daughter, and chose rather to suffer affliction with the people of God.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 3:4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)</sup> Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.

    The sin of battle fatigue is plain: “Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.” The pleasures of sin are breaking God's laws.
  - **[2 Thessalonians 2:12-13](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v12)</sup> That they all might be damned who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v13)</sup> But we are bound to give thanks alway to God for you, brethren beloved of the Lord, because God hath from the beginning chosen you to salvation through sanctification of the Spirit and belief of the truth:

    “That they all might be damned who believed not the truth, but had pleasure in unrighteousness.” Some of you suffer battle fatigue: you had pleasure in unrighteousness, in sin.
  - **[Exodus 5:14](/bible/exodus/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/5#v14)</sup> And the officers of the children of Israel, which Pharaoh’s taskmasters had set over them, were beaten, and demanded, Wherefore have ye not fulfilled your task in making brick both yesterday and to day, as heretofore?

    “And the officers of the children of Israel, which Pharaoh’s taskmasters had set over them, were beaten.” There were officers of the children of Israel set over us by the taskmasters. In America they are called Uncle Tom Negroes, house Negroes; in ancient Egypt they were the officers the taskmasters set up over us. And they started getting beaten too.

**[Hebrews 11:26](/bible/hebrews/11#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/hebrews/11#v26)</sup> Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt: for he had respect unto the recompence of the reward.

- “Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt: for he had respect unto the recompence of the reward.” Christ is the greater riches, so we got to bear our foundation on Jesus the Christ. The sin is just for a season; sin equals death, but the gift of God is eternal life. So we got to put our faith on Jesus Christ.
- “Esteeming the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures in Egypt”: so Moses had faith in Christ; he was knowledgeable of Christ. In Hebrews, what we call the Hall of Faith speaks about Noah, Abraham, Moses, the mighty prophets of God; we need to read that and build our faith. “For he had respect unto the recompence of the reward.”

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 3:11](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v11)</sup> For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.

    “For other foundation can no man lay than that is laid, which is Jesus Christ.” That’s the only foundation. The Most High God knew that, so in order for us to be delivered, in order for our sins to be wiped away, we had to be groomed and prepared to receive Christ.
