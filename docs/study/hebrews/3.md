## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 12-19, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 3:12](/bible/hebrews/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/3#v12)</sup> Take heed, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living God.

- Be careful that you aren't the one who can't be told anything. “Take heed, brethren, lest there be in any of you an evil heart of unbelief, in departing from the living God.” Does an evil heart of unbelief come in one day? No, it is a process. You can sit here a long, long time, at all the high holy days smiling in everybody's face, and be hardened as we speak.

**[Hebrews 3:13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)</sup> But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.

- “But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.” I had to find a way to bring change into this class; this is my segue right here. Sin is very deceitful. Some of us get hardened through the deceitfulness of sin; we love sin, and we harden because we say God ain’t gonna do nothing to me.
- This happened in Guyana this week: a married brother’s unrepentant daughter introduced him to a woman from her job who wanted a relationship. Y’all keep playing with your rebellious kids and entertaining them. She left them alone in the home and they tongue kissed, and he did not confess it until he developed an outbreak on his mouth, lips, tongue and throat. The infection kept him from swallowing and eating solid food, and the brother died in the hospital. We met the brother when we was in Guyana. You can’t make this stuff up; that was quick judgment. So brothers, keep playing, and you sisters too, banging your ex-boyfriends in the world then sneaking up in shalom. I bring these things up to put fear in you; that’s what it’s supposed to do.
- “But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.” The hardening comes through the deceitfulness of sin.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 3:4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-john/3#v4)</sup> Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.

    What is sin? “Whosoever committeth sin transgresseth also the law: for sin is the transgression of the law.”
  - **[Ecclesiastes 8:11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/8#v11)</sup> Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily, therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.

    God spared David, God will surely spare me? Bro, you might not be David; in fact I know you not David. “Because sentence against an evil work is not executed speedily”: I commit a sin and nothing happened to me, “therefore the heart of the sons of men is fully set in them to do evil.” Because we didn’t get judged right away, our heart is fully set in us to do evil.
  - **[Hebrews 11:25](/bible/hebrews/11#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/hebrews/11#v25)</sup> Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season;

    This is talking about Moses: “Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season”. Whoever said sinning ain’t pleasurable is a liar; sin is very pleasurable. The problem is it’s only temporary, for a season. Once you get post-nut clarity you realize what you got. Now you got monkeypox; was it worth it, bruh? Was it worth it, sis? You got genital warts now, and you come back into Israel talking about I’m working on myself.

**[Hebrews 3:14-19](/bible/hebrews/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/3#v14)</sup> For we are made partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence stedfast unto the end;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/hebrews/3#v15)</sup> While it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts, as in the provocation.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/hebrews/3#v16)</sup> For some, when they had heard, did provoke: howbeit not all that came out of Egypt by Moses.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/3#v17)</sup> But with whom was he grieved forty years? was it not with them that had sinned, whose carcases fell in the wilderness?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/3#v18)</sup> And to whom sware he that they should not enter into his rest, but to them that believed not?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/hebrews/3#v19)</sup> So we see that they could not enter in because of unbelief.

- “For we are made partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence stedfast unto the end”. “Harden not your hearts, as in the provocation”: don’t harden your hearts like your forefathers did in the wilderness. Those rebellious Israelites did not enter the promised land: “So we see that they could not enter in because of unbelief.”
- He also spoke about having confidence, and we always ground these things in scripture. That influencer is a so-called Christian, so he’ll think of certain scriptures, but it’s obviously not on the level of what we have and the understanding we use.
- “For we are made partakers of Christ, if we hold the beginning of our confidence stedfast unto the end”. He is the beginning and the end, so the beginning of our confidence is that we were anointed by him and we abide in him. That’s why it says no man got to teach you anything after you’ve gotten that, because all of it is there for you.
- So some of you, what else do you want? We gave you all the scriptures; we’re showing you what needs to happen for you to apply it and fight against these things. What more can I tell you? I’ve done that with married couples: you don’t want to counsel us no more? You’re damn right I don’t, because it’s all in vain; I’m just wasting my time. I love my people, I’ll make the time for you, but when I see you’re not putting the effort forth, my time is too valuable. I respect myself enough that I’m not going to waste my time in those situations anymore. Maybe if I see something change; the spirit will have to move me to enter into that situation again.
