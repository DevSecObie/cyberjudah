## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-15, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 4:1](/bible/hebrews/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/4#v1)</sup> Let us therefore fear, lest, a promise being left us of entering into his rest, any of you should seem to come short of it.

- “Let us therefore fear, lest, a promise being left us of entering into his rest, any of you should seem to come short of it.”

**[Hebrews 4:2](/bible/hebrews/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/4#v2)</sup> For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them: but the word preached did not profit them, not being mixed with faith in them that heard it.

- “For unto us was the gospel preached, as well as unto them.” Christ was taught back then, not just in the book of Matthew, but to our forefathers. “But the word preached did not profit them”, because they disobeyed Christ: “not being mixed with faith in them that heard it.” They were moved with unbelief.

**[Hebrews 4:3](/bible/hebrews/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/4#v3)</sup> For we which have believed do enter into rest, as he said, As I have sworn in my wrath, if they shall enter into my rest: although the works were finished from the foundation of the world.

- “For we which have believed do enter into rest.” The rest of the seventh-day Sabbath is going into when Christ comes back and we reign with him. That is the true rest. “Although the works were finished from the foundation of the world”: paradise was already here.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 8:52](/bible/2-esdras/8#v52)**
    > <sup>[52](/bible/2-esdras/8#v52)</sup> For unto you is paradise opened, the tree of life is planted, the time to come is prepared, plenteousness is made ready, a city is builded, and rest is allowed, yea, perfect goodness and wisdom.

    “For unto you is paradise opened, the tree of life is planted, the time to come is prepared, plenteousness is made ready, a city is builded, and rest is allowed, yea, perfect goodness and wisdom.” That paradise is for the Israelites; it is not talking about all nations. And there it is: rest is allowed, the rest that we which have believed enter into.
  - **[2 Esdras 3:5-6](/bible/2-esdras/3#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/3#v5)</sup> And gavest a body unto Adam without soul, which was the workmanship of thine hands, and didst breathe into him the breath of life, and he was made living before thee.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-esdras/3#v6)</sup> And thou leddest him into paradise, which thy right hand had planted, before ever the earth came forward.

    “The works were finished from the foundation of the world.” Before Christ said, the evening and the morning were the first day, paradise was already here.

**[Hebrews 4:4-5](/bible/hebrews/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/4#v4)</sup> For he spake in a certain place of the seventh day on this wise, And God did rest the seventh day from all his works.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/4#v5)</sup> And in this place again, If they shall enter into my rest.

- “For he spake in a certain place of the seventh day on this wise, And God did rest the seventh day from all his works.” Notice that number keeps popping up: seven.
- “If they shall enter into my rest.” In the wilderness the Most High also used the word rest: I'm going to bring you into the land, into rest. But did we truly rest in that land? There were enemies on all sides, the Amalekites and the damn Hamites we were fighting to get out of the land. The true rest is the eternal reign with Christ when he comes back: rulership over our enemies.

**[Hebrews 4:6-7](/bible/hebrews/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/4#v6)</sup> Seeing therefore it remaineth that some must enter therein, and they to whom it was first preached entered not in because of unbelief:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/4#v7)</sup> Again, he limiteth a certain day, saying in David, To day, after so long a time; as it is said, To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.

- “They to whom it was first preached entered not in because of unbelief.” “To day if ye will hear his voice, harden not your hearts.”

**[Hebrews 4:8](/bible/hebrews/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/4#v8)</sup> For if Jesus had given them rest, then would he not afterward have spoken of another day.

- “For if Jesus had given them rest”: that Jesus is Joshua. Although Joshua was mighty and was smiting the Hamites, we didn't get true rest. The true rest is not going into the land; it is the kingdom of God on earth.

**[Hebrews 4:9](/bible/hebrews/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/4#v9)</sup> There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.

- “There remaineth therefore a rest to the people of God.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 14:1-3](/bible/isaiah/14#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/14#v1)</sup> For the Lord will have mercy on Jacob, and will yet choose Israel, and set them in their own land: and the strangers shall be joined with them, and they shall cleave to the house of Jacob.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/14#v2)</sup> And the people shall take them, and bring them to their place: and the house of Israel shall possess them in the land of the Lord for servants and handmaids: and they shall take them captives, whose captives they were; and they shall rule over their oppressors.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/14#v3)</sup> And it shall come to pass in the day that the Lord shall give thee rest from thy sorrow, and from thy fear, and from the hard bondage wherein thou wast made to serve,

    To expound that rest: the seventh-day Sabbath, resting from our servile work, is a similitude the Most High uses for our resting in the kingdom when it is established. That is when we will really rest from servile work. After the Sabbath some of us have to go to work on Sunday, then Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, slaving for Esau.
  - **[2 Peter 3:7-8](/bible/2-peter/3#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-peter/3#v7)</sup> But the heavens and the earth, which are now, by the same word are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-peter/3#v8)</sup> But, beloved, be not ignorant of this one thing, that one day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.

    “But the heavens and the earth, which are now, by the same word are kept in store, reserved unto fire against the day of judgment and perdition of ungodly men.” “One day is with the Lord as a thousand years, and a thousand years as one day.” One day with the Lord, brothers, is a thousand years, and the true rest is the thousand years of rulership under Christ.
  - **[Revelation 20:4](/bible/revelation/20#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/revelation/20#v4)</sup> And I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them: and I saw the souls of them that were beheaded for the witness of Jesus, and for the word of God, and which had not worshipped the beast, neither his image, neither had received his mark upon their foreheads, or in their hands; and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years.

    “I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them”, the souls “which had not worshipped the beast, neither his image”, “and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years.” That is the rest. The seventh-day Sabbath represents us resting with Christ.

**[Hebrews 4:10](/bible/hebrews/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/4#v10)</sup> For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.

- “For he that is entered into his rest, he also hath ceased from his own works, as God did from his.” Paul is making a comparison of our works to God's.

  Precepts:
  - **[Micah 4:10](/bible/micah/4#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/micah/4#v10)</sup> Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail: for now shalt thou go forth out of the city, and thou shalt dwell in the field, and thou shalt go even to Babylon; there shalt thou be delivered; there the Lord shall redeem thee from the hand of thine enemies.

    “Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail: for now shalt thou go forth out of the city.” No matter what we are going through, brothers and sisters, we have to be in pain and do this work: be in pain to bring forth the kingdom of heaven on earth. Labour in pain and bring forth your works, to enter into that rest.
  - **[Revelation 14:13](/bible/revelation/14#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/revelation/14#v13)</sup> And I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me, Write, Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them.

    “Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord from henceforth: Yea, saith the Spirit, that they may rest from their labours; and their works do follow them.” Your works are what will allow you to enter in through those gates.

**[Hebrews 4:11](/bible/hebrews/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/4#v11)</sup> Let us labour therefore to enter into that rest, lest any man fall after the same example of unbelief.

- “Let us labour therefore to enter into that rest.” Labour, in pain; bring forth your works to enter into that rest, the kingdom. “Lest any man fall after the same example of unbelief”, the example of what we read in the wilderness.

**[Hebrews 4:12](/bible/hebrews/4#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/4#v12)</sup> For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow, and is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart.

- Saddam Hussein, to the Arab leaders: what’s the meaning of Israel killing Arabs every day on TV, and the harshest statements that came out were denunciations? Draw your sword out. Look how many Arab titles there are, presidents and kings, and five lunatics kill our people in Palestine. It’s embarrassing for someone to say he is the leader of his people when he can’t carry a sword against oppression. I’m not saying I support Saddam Hussein’s policies, but you got to be able to discern right from wrong and see what pertains to us. What does that got to do with us? Everything. We got soldiers, officers, captains, deacons and Bishops, and it’s a shame if you cannot lift a sword against oppression. Our sword is the Bible.
- “For the word of God is quick, and powerful, and sharper than any twoedged sword”: that’s our sword, and our sword must be lifted against oppression. We see oppression, we got to pull the sword, pull the scriptures. “Piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit”: sometimes you got to hurt somebody’s feelings. “And of the joints and marrow”: that comes later. “And is a discerner of the thoughts and intents of the heart”: the Bible will reveal the thoughts and intents of somebody’s heart. Just bring the scriptures out and let the word of God do what it do.
- That’s why, brothers, we travel to these different places, countries and lands: because our people are suffering oppression. Our job is to go to these places with the word of God, the sword, and lift the sword against oppression. There are good days and there are bad days when things don’t go the way you want, but that’s what we signed up for.
- “For the word of God is quick”: no matter how fast you run or where you hide, headphones and all, it is going to find you. “And powerful”: Power Rangers, right? “And sharper than any twoedged sword, piercing even to the dividing asunder of soul and spirit, and of the joints and marrow”: it hurts you physically and spiritually.

**[Hebrews 4:13](/bible/hebrews/4#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/4#v13)</sup> Neither is there any creature that is not manifest in his sight: but all things are naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do.

- “Neither is there any creature that is not manifest in his sight: but all things are naked and opened unto the eyes of him with whom we have to do.” Nothing is hidden from him.

**[Hebrews 4:14](/bible/hebrews/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/4#v14)</sup> Seeing then that we have a great high priest, that is passed into the heavens, Jesus the Son of God, let us hold fast our profession.

- “Seeing then that we have a great high priest”: that is Christ, “Jesus the Son of God.” “Let us hold fast our profession.” Our profession is not our job; some hear profession and think nurse or doctor. We are prophets, and a prophet’s profession is to teach, so all of us are supposed to be teachers. Our first work is supposed to be in this truth.
- What good is a prophet who does nothing with it? Some never travel and have never left the state of Georgia, yet say they are prophets. All Israel is not in Georgia; Israel is scattered to the four corners of the earth. Get a passport, brothers. We have much work to do.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 6:12](/bible/1-timothy/6#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/6#v12)</sup> Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.

    Our profession is this truth. “Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called.” You cannot lay hold on eternal life if you do not hold fast to your profession, and you cannot fight the good fight of faith without holding to it.

**[Hebrews 4:15](/bible/hebrews/4#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/hebrews/4#v15)</sup> For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities; but was in all points tempted like as we are, yet without sin.

- “For we have not an high priest which cannot be touched with the feeling of our infirmities.” Christ was put through the same things we are going through and worse. There is nothing you can say to him that he does not understand: the fight, the battle we face every day, the mind. None of us has an excuse. Take a break from the brotherhood because they do not understand you, and you become worse; you stay away from the one place that can help you.
- Sisters, there is no such thing as a perfect sister. This place is a hospital; all of us are sick, and we come here to get healing. Christ came for those who know they are sick, not for those who say nothing is wrong with them. Stop complaining without a solution. Be patient; it takes time to get it right, and if we do not get it right we will not get the kingdom.
- “But was in all points tempted”: whatever point you can think of, he was tempted, but he did not give in; “yet without sin.” Seeing your ex at the store does not mean you have to talk to them. If you say you are a follower of Christ, you are going to be tempted in all things too. Paul was tempted and did not give in. The men and women in the scriptures were just like us.
- Do not let sin so overwhelm you that you lose the kingdom. We all make mistakes; when you fall, get back up. Is it so bad that you would go back to the Christian church and white Jesus, the thing you hate? And when a brother falls, it is not the time to kick him or to take revenge because he is no longer an officer over you. That is an evil mindset that will destroy you. Build him up and encourage him: you can come back from this.
