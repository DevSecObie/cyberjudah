## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 9, 11-14, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 5:9](/bible/hebrews/5#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/5#v9)</sup> And being made perfect, he became the author of eternal salvation unto all them that obey him;

- “And being made perfect”: when he died. “He became the author of eternal salvation unto all them that obey him.” He is the author: from him is the beginning of our story, of our captivity in this Bible, and in him it comes to an end. He is the author of everything.
- How do we get salvation today? We learn the Bible, and an author is the one who writes it. Lo, I come in the volume of the book, it is written of me: he is the author, the one written of all through the book.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 12:2](/bible/hebrews/12#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/12#v2)</sup> Looking unto Jesus the author and finisher of our faith; who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is set down at the right hand of the throne of God.

    “Looking unto Jesus the author and finisher of our faith; who for the joy that was set before him endured the cross, despising the shame, and is set down at the right hand of the throne of God.” Set down, not standing.
  - **[Revelation 1:1](/bible/revelation/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/1#v1)</sup> The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him, to shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass; and he sent and signified it by his angel unto his servant John:

    Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end: the same thing. “The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave unto him”: God gave Christ the revelation of what is going to happen. “To shew unto his servants things which must shortly come to pass.”

**[Hebrews 5:11](/bible/hebrews/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/5#v11)</sup> Of whom we have many things to say, and hard to be uttered, seeing ye are dull of hearing.

- “Of whom we have many things to say, and hard to be uttered, seeing ye are dull of hearing.” Some people are dull of hearing; they can’t understand or perceive.

**[Hebrews 5:12](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)</sup> For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.

- “For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.” The milk is the laws, the basics. The strong meat is the prophecies, and exercising your senses so you can discern between right and wrong.
- “For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God.” The oracles of God are the word of God, the laws of God, the lively oracles.
- “And are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.” When you first come into this truth you are born again: you must learn to walk again, talk again, see, think, speak and learn again, as a babe all over again. So you must start off nourished with milk. You cannot feed a newborn child meat; they will choke and die on it. The meat comes when you are grown, have your teeth and have learned to chew. The meat is the deep understanding of the Bible: the deep sayings, the prophecies, the commandments and laws, and knowing how to apply them.
- “For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.” Contextually this is talking about the ex-Pharisees, those leaders that were set up: you may think you have an understanding, but now you got to be taught again; you got to be taught Christ. And we can use it this way: you may be called, you may think you know something, but now you got to be taught all over again.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 2:2](/bible/1-peter/2#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-peter/2#v2)</sup> As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby:

    “As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby”. It has to be a constant desire. You can’t let your fire or your lamp burn out. It has to be constant, because you may jump up here, but you’ll start to decline if you don’t properly nourish yourself.
  - **[Isaiah 28:7-9](/bible/isaiah/28#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/28#v7)</sup> But they also have erred through wine, and through strong drink are out of the way; the priest and the prophet have erred through strong drink, they are swallowed up of wine, they are out of the way through strong drink; they err in vision, they stumble in judgment.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/28#v8)</sup> For all tables are full of vomit and filthiness, so that there is no place clean.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/28#v9)</sup> Whom shall he teach knowledge? and whom shall he make to understand doctrine? them that are weaned from the milk, and drawn from the breasts.

    We were all born with the five senses, but the sixth one, you have to be chosen for that thing.

**[Hebrews 5:13](/bible/hebrews/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/5#v13)</sup> For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.

- “For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.” If you are still trying to figure out whether to wear fringes or not eat pork, you are not ready for the deep stuff yet.
- “For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.” Whoever is still on milk is still learning and is not ready to teach. You have to be nourished with the milk first before going out and teaching; otherwise you are unskilful in the word of righteousness. You can't put an infant on the ground and tell him to walk; it don't make no damn sense. He will lay there and cry. A babe will talk and not use a Bible. That is a babe, and a babe cannot teach. Sit down, learn and watch class.
- Read again: “For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.” When you have not yet learned how to apply the laws and commandments of God, you are still a babe with no experience, still crawling, swaddled in a blanket. You cannot teach like that; you have to grow first, by trial and error, rising and falling like an infant does.
- Children are teachers too. They have no malice and they forgive: a child says you're mean, daddy, and is back in your arms within seconds. That is why Christ said to be converted and become as a little child. A lot of our people are vengeful, hateful, jealous, egotistical, arrogant and high-minded, or fearful, weak and cowardly, and you cannot be a dynamic teacher with those attributes. The Lord can't use you.

**[Hebrews 5:14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/5#v14)</sup> But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.

- “But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age, even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.” The reason of use is being exercised in the milk: the deep basics. Understanding the small things, like keeping the commandments, is the foundation of growing in understanding.
- Brothers want to bypass that and go into prophecy, and they don’t know how to deal with one another: how to apply Matthew 18 when there is an issue, how to let go of strife and beef, how to endeavour to keep the unity, keep a marriage together and discipline children.
- A grown man with gray hair, put fourth in line in a city where a school is being planned, said he didn’t agree that the captain and officers should be over him. He was put out. He did not believe the simple thing, obey them that have rule over you, which is 101. He answers to the white man at his job, so why is he mad? A body can’t grow if men can’t follow structure. He could not discern how his actions would affect his wife, his children and the greater body of Israel. David said he would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of God, and this man is jockeying for position.
- “But strong meat belongeth to them that are of full age”: the grown, those who have learned, gained experience, and acquired wisdom through growth and application. “Even those who by reason of use have their senses exercised to discern both good and evil.” By reason of constant use: experience, time, trial and error. You get up, you fall; you make your mistakes, but you rise again and become better. That is how the senses are exercised.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiastes 12:12-14](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v12)</sup> And further, by these, my son, be admonished: of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)</sup> Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v14)</sup> For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil.

    This is where Hebrews gets senses exercised to discern both good and evil. “And further, by these, my son, be admonished”: by these, the commandments. “Of making many books there is no end; and much study is a weariness of the flesh.” Don't be focused on a whole bunch of books. The Bible always is, should be and shall be the primary source.
