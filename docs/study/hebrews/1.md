## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4-14, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 1:4](/bible/hebrews/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/1#v4)</sup> Being made so much better than the angels, as he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they.

- “Being made so much better than the angels”: being made. Creation again. “As he hath by inheritance obtained a more excellent name than they.” Why more excellent? Because he was first, the first formed.
- When a father has a son, who is the son named after? The son takes on the father’s name, as on earth so in heaven. For the most part the Son retains the same titles the Father has. So when you read the capital LORD you can’t say that is always the Father.

**[Hebrews 1:5](/bible/hebrews/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/1#v5)</sup> For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee? And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?

- “For unto which of the angels said he at any time, Thou art my Son, this day have I begotten thee?” That is Psalms 2. None of them, because the first thing the Father formed was Christ.
- “And again, I will be to him a Father, and he shall be to me a Son?” Now Paul is pulling precepts: from the Psalms he runs to 2 Samuel. That is line upon line, rightly dividing; no Strong’s, no concordance, no commentary.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Samuel 7:12-14](/bible/2-samuel/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/7#v12)</sup> And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-samuel/7#v13)</sup> He shall build an house for my name, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/7#v14)</sup> I will be his father, and he shall be my son. If he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men:

    Who is the Lord talking about? “When thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers”: David. “I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels”: Solomon, out of his loins. “He shall build an house for my name”: Solomon did. “And I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever”: Solomon didn’t last forever.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 7:21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v21)</sup> And all such things as are either secret or manifest, them I know.

    “And all such things as are either secret or manifest, them I know.” Solomon knew certain things, secret and manifest. Keep it in mind for his next words.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 8:19-20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v19)</sup> For I was a witty child, and had a good spirit.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/8#v20)</sup> Yea rather, being good, I came into a body undefiled.

    “For I was a witty child, and had a good spirit. Yea rather, being good, I came into a body undefiled.” Was Solomon’s body defiled? Yes, it was. Was Christ’s body defiled? No. Solomon knew who he was: he came back again. Things secret, I know, and things manifest, I know.

**[Hebrews 1:6](/bible/hebrews/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/1#v6)</sup> And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world, he saith, And let all the angels of God worship him.

- “And again, when he bringeth in the firstbegotten into the world”: when Christ came into the world and died for us. “He saith, And let all the angels of God worship him.” When he died, resurrected, and returned to the heavens in Acts 1, the angels of God went up there and worshipped him.

And immediately I was in the spirit: and, behold, a throne was set in heaven, and one sat on the throne. And he that sat was to look upon like a jasper and a sardine stone”: a big brown man in heaven. “And there was a rainbow round about the throne, in sight like unto an emerald”: a green throne, and color in heaven; it isn’t black and white like an old cartoon.

    “And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals.” The Father sitting with a book in his right hand. “And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, Who is worthy to open the book”: the angel looked strong, but he couldn’t open it. “And no man in heaven, nor in earth, neither under the earth, was able to open the book”: no angel, none of the spirits who had passed on. “And I wept much.”
  - **[Revelation 11:16](/bible/revelation/11#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/revelation/11#v16)</sup> And the four and twenty elders, which sat before God on their seats, fell upon their faces, and worshipped God,

    Back to let all the angels worship him. “And the four and twenty elders, which sat before God on their seats.” They had seats, thrones, not benches. If they have thrones, who else has a throne? If there are two thrones, how many gods are there?

**[Hebrews 1:7](/bible/hebrews/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/1#v7)</sup> And of the angels he saith, Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire.

- “Who maketh his angels spirits, and his ministers a flame of fire.” The angels are ministers that serve the Lord.

**[Hebrews 1:8-9](/bible/hebrews/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/1#v8)</sup> But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: a sceptre of righteousness is the sceptre of thy kingdom.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/1#v9)</sup> Thou hast loved righteousness, and hated iniquity; therefore God, even thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.

- “But unto the Son he saith, Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever.” He is telling you who it is: the Son, O God. “Therefore God, even thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.” Fellows means the angels above and us as well: he is the head of the corner, the headstone.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 45:6-7](/bible/psalms/45#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/45#v6)</sup> Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a right sceptre.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/45#v7)</sup> Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.

    “Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever”: was David’s throne forever? No. “The sceptre of thy kingdom is a right sceptre”: remember Genesis 49, the sceptre shall not depart from Judah.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 51:10](/bible/sirach/51#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/51#v10)</sup> I called upon the Lord, the Father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the days of my trouble, and in the time of the proud, when there was no help.

    Therefore God, thy God: “I called upon the Lord, the Father of my Lord, that he would not leave me in the days of my trouble.” The Father of my Lord. That is who Solomon called upon too: the Father. Who is the thy God? Your Father.

**[Hebrews 1:10-12](/bible/hebrews/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/1#v10)</sup> And, Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/1#v11)</sup> They shall perish; but thou remainest; and they all shall wax old as doth a garment;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/1#v12)</sup> And as a vesture shalt thou fold them up, and they shall be changed: but thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail.

- “And, Thou, Lord, in the beginning hast laid the foundation of the earth; and the heavens are the works of thine hands.” In the beginning, thou Lord: that is Christ. “But thou art the same, and thy years shall not fail.” Wisdom said, I shall never fail.

**[Hebrews 1:13](/bible/hebrews/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/1#v13)</sup> But to which of the angels said he at any time, Sit on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool?

- “But to which of the angels said he at any time, Sit on my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool?” None, because none of them is called his son.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 3:21](/bible/revelation/3#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/revelation/3#v21)</sup> To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame, and am set down with my Father in his throne.

    “To him that overcometh will I grant to sit with me in my throne, even as I also overcame”: he was slain and overcame, “and am set down with my Father in his throne.” He is sat down with his Father in his throne, along with the twenty-four elders round about the Father. And they say he is just standing, on punishment, the whole time. Where do these doctrines come from?
  - **[Hebrews 1:3](/bible/hebrews/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/1#v3)</sup> Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person, and upholding all things by the word of his power, when he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high;

    “Who being the brightness of his glory, and the express image of his person”: he looks like his Father. “When he had by himself purged our sins, sat down on the right hand of the Majesty on high.” Sat down, on the throne. The Majesty on high is the Father; he sat down on the right hand, next to his Father. If dad gets a throne, son gets a throne.
  - **[Psalms 110:1](/bible/psalms/110#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/110#v1)</sup> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.

    Read slow: “The Lord said unto my Lord.” Remember Ephesians 1:17, Jesus Christ our Lord. Why keep emphasizing that Christ is our Lord? Where was Eve before she was made? In Adam. And what is the man to the woman? The head. What did women call their men back then? Lord. Who is David’s Lord here?

**[Hebrews 1:14](/bible/hebrews/1#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/1#v14)</sup> Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?

- “Are they not all ministering spirits, sent forth to minister for them who shall be heirs of salvation?” Who are the heirs of salvation? We are. The angels are sent forth to minister unto us.
