## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 13 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-7, 11-14, 17, 23, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 13:1-2](/bible/hebrews/13#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/13#v1)</sup> Let brotherly love continue.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/13#v2)</sup> Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares.

- “Let brotherly love continue”: in these last days we must let brotherly love continue. “Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for thereby some have entertained angels unawares.”
- As we see the prophecies come to pass, we know tribulation is coming. Set up brothers and sisters around you. Start small: have the guest room in your household ready to receive a guest. If you cannot afford an extra room, instead of the leather couch get a pullout bed couch; turn a little space into a room with a bed. If a prophet is on the run and you can give shelter for two days, a meal and shelter for a prophet, that is a blessing for you. That is hospitality and long-suffering one to another.
- “Let brotherly love continue”: that is what this whole thing is all about. If we walk in that spirit of Christ we’re going to be all right; there can never be division among us. We have to speak the same thing.
- The leader that is over you is to watch over you and make sure that you are exalted, and make sure they are doing what they are supposed to be doing. Bring fire to the islands; wherever you are at, follow the same thing. We have to become greater leaders, better than what we think we are.

**[Hebrews 13:3](/bible/hebrews/13#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/13#v3)</sup> Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.

- “Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them”: those put in bonds for things pertaining to this ministry, not a thief or someone who did evil. “And them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.” That is the epitome of loving your neighbour as yourself: when a commandment keeper goes through trial for this ministry, do whatever it takes to support them. Be there in the time of affliction, and watch how the Lord provides for you.

**[Hebrews 13:4-5](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)</sup> Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/13#v5)</sup> Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.

- “Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.” That is why we have to strengthen the marriages. “Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.” The Lord will never leave us, but do not forsake the Lord.
- Pam told the people that singleness is not a curse and marriage is not the goal, with the hands moving, the lights on him and the camera angles, theatrics frying people’s brains. The Bible says “Marriage is honourable in all”. A straight contradiction. So what kind of portal is that? A portal to complete destruction.
- Why does it say in all? Because marriage is what started off everything. When a man and a woman get married, that is the potential to produce righteousness on the earth. Every human being comes out of the coming together of man and woman, and God says that is supposed to be done under the covenant, the agreement of marriage, and he has laws on it. There is not supposed to be a single woman anywhere without a man responsible for her: if it ain’t her husband, it’s her father. What he is really saying is, you women don’t need to marry real men; be married to white Jesus.
- “And the bed undefiled”: the bed is undefiled when it is a married man and a married woman, married to each other by the laws of God. Paperwork. Not some ketubah, a piece of paper with something written on it; you have legal papers for your car and your house.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 5:14](/bible/1-timothy/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/1-timothy/5#v14)</sup> I will therefore that the younger women marry, bear children, guide the house, give none occasion to the adversary to speak reproachfully.

    “I will therefore that the younger women marry, bear children, guide the house”. So what is this preacher saying, that women aren’t supposed to marry but are going to have babies anyway? That is fornication. She is supposed to learn all of that from her mother and her father.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 4:6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/4#v6)</sup> For children begotten of unlawful beds are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial.

    The Bible means what it says about the unlawful bed: “For children begotten of unlawful beds are witnesses of wickedness against their parents in their trial.” An unlawful bed means the man and woman in the bed are not married. That is fornication, and that is sin.
  - **[Judges 14:10](/bible/judges/14#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/judges/14#v10)</sup> So his father went down unto the woman: and Samson made there a feast; for so used the young men to do.

    “So his father went down unto the woman: and Samson made there a feast; for so used the young men to do.” This is Samson, who was in love with a Hamite woman, and his father and mother said, stop marrying these damn Hamites. When we think of the Bible we generally think it is European, but it would be more Afrocentric than European, especially during this time. Not all Ethiopians are Israelites; some of them are Hamites, and they look good.

**[Hebrews 13:6](/bible/hebrews/13#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/13#v6)</sup> So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me.

- “So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper”: the Lord has my back. “And I will not fear what man shall do unto me.” You can kill this body; I do not care what men can do to me.

**[Hebrews 13:7](/bible/hebrews/13#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/13#v7)</sup> Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation.

- A true leader transfers his belief onto others. “Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow.” A true leader imparts his faith to the next man. Think of the level of faith the Bishop had to have for this truth to be where it is in IUIC. The impact of the Bishop and Deacons reaches well beyond the purple and gold: people outside IUIC donate to the booster club; they may not join or fully understand, but they respect it, and the little camps teaching at noon or three all tune in at six on Saturday night.
- “Considering the end of their conversation.” Good leaders make you believe in them; great leaders make you believe in yourself. That is how the Bishop raises up other bishops, deacons, captains, and soldiers by the thousands.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 16:17-18](/bible/matthew/16#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/16#v17)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Simon Bar–jona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/16#v18)</sup> And I say also unto thee, That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.

    “Blessed art thou, Simon Bar–jona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.” Then: “That thou art Peter, and upon this rock I will build my church; and the gates of hell shall not prevail against it.” How do you think that made Peter feel, Christ telling him he would build his church upon him? That is what great leaders do: they empower and strengthen others and make you believe in yourself, not in them.
  - **[Deuteronomy 3:25-28](/bible/deuteronomy/3#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/3#v25)</sup> I pray thee, let me go over, and see the good land that is beyond Jordan, that goodly mountain, and Lebanon.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/3#v26)</sup> But the Lord was wroth with me for your sakes, and would not hear me: and the Lord said unto me, Let it suffice thee; speak no more unto me of this matter.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/3#v27)</sup> Get thee up into the top of Pisgah, and lift up thine eyes westward, and northward, and southward, and eastward, and behold it with thine eyes: for thou shalt not go over this Jordan.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/3#v28)</sup> But charge Joshua, and encourage him, and strengthen him: for he shall go over before this people, and he shall cause them to inherit the land which thou shalt see.

    Moses prayed, “let me go over, and see the good land that is beyond Jordan”, but the Lord said, “Let it suffice thee”, and showed him the land from the top of Pisgah: “thou shalt not go over this Jordan.”
  - **[Job 4:3](/bible/job/4#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/job/4#v3)</sup> Behold, thou hast instructed many, and thou hast strengthened the weak hands.

    “Behold, thou hast instructed many”: your job as a leader is to instruct many. “And thou hast strengthened the weak hands”: it is your job to build up the brothers and the sisters in the spirit.

**[Hebrews 13:11](/bible/hebrews/13#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/13#v11)</sup> For the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sin, are burned without the camp.

- “For the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sin, are burned without the camp.” The bodies of the animals brought for sin were taken outside the camp, outside the congregation, and burnt there.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 4:12](/bible/leviticus/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/4#v12)</sup> Even the whole bullock shall he carry forth without the camp unto a clean place, where the ashes are poured out, and burn him on the wood with fire: where the ashes are poured out shall he be burnt.

    This is where the scripture says it. “If a soul shall sin through ignorance against any of the commandments of the Lord”: if Israel commits sin. Then: “Even the whole bullock shall he carry forth without the camp unto a clean place, where the ashes are poured out, and burn him on the wood with fire”.

**[Hebrews 13:12](/bible/hebrews/13#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/13#v12)</sup> Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate.

- “Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate.” The bullock or the lamb was burnt outside the camp to sanctify the people with the blood, and so Jesus Christ suffered outside the city of Jerusalem. Golgotha, the place of a skull, was a place of execution outside the city. Paul was heavy; he understood these things.

**[Hebrews 13:13](/bible/hebrews/13#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/13#v13)</sup> Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach.

- “Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach.” Let us go forth and serve Christ without the rest of Israel. Bearing his reproach is bearing the hatred that comes on us for following Christ. The world is going to hate us. If you think this is a love fest, no: you are going to be hated.

**[Hebrews 13:14](/bible/hebrews/13#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/13#v14)</sup> For here have we no continuing city, but we seek one to come.

- “For here have we no continuing city, but we seek one to come.” He said we have no continuing city because Jerusalem was about to be destroyed by the Romans, and Paul understood that.

**[Hebrews 13:17](/bible/hebrews/13#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/13#v17)</sup> Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account, that they may do it with joy, and not with grief: for that is unprofitable for you.

- “Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls, as they that must give account”. Brothers mistake this as giving account to the Lord. It is the officers over you giving the leadership a report on you: how is that brother, his life, his work, his marriage? If the report is that he committed adultery last week, judgment has to come down on that brother.
- “That they may do it with joy”: we want to hear good things, this brother is in the spirit, getting married next week, his finances are good. “And not with grief”, because you did some sin, “for that is unprofitable for you”: unprofitable because you may have to leave the body for an amount of time.
- “Obey them that have the rule over you, and submit yourselves: for they watch for your souls”. The leadership's counsel is intended to protect you. Some grew up without a father; now there are men looking out for them, but they reject those men when the counsel interferes with the relationship they want. If we tell you to stay away from a violent man, listen.
- Sister Bina was a quiet, good sister with children. Her husband was removed three times for beating her, and she was told to leave him alone. She left IUIC about a year ago so she could be with him. He killed her this week, shooting her in front of her son. The corrected account is that he stabbed his first girlfriend and shot Sister Bina. Pray for her children and family in this difficult time.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 13:7](/bible/hebrews/13#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/13#v7)</sup> Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation.

    “Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation.” Read with verse 17: those who rule over you are the ones who speak the word of God to you, and they watch for your souls.
  - **[Proverbs 27:23](/bible/proverbs/27#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/27#v23)</sup> Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds.

    “Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds.” The herd is the people, the nation of Israel. It is the leaders’ job to know the state of the flock, to know what is going on in the body.
  - **[Proverbs 11:14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/proverbs/11#v14)</sup> Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.

    “Where no counsel is, the people fall: but in the multitude of counsellors there is safety.” If you don't follow counsel, you will fall. A congregation without counsel will fall; there is no way around it. That is why there are those set over you who watch for your souls.

**[Hebrews 13:23](/bible/hebrews/13#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/hebrews/13#v23)</sup> Know ye that our brother Timothy is set at liberty; with whom, if he come shortly, I will see you.

- “Know ye that our brother Timothy is set at liberty.” Timothy got arrested, and at this time he was set free. “With whom, if he come shortly, I will see you.” Paul rejoiced that Timothy was set free from prison.
