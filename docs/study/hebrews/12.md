## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 12 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 4, 6-7, 9-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 12:1](/bible/hebrews/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/12#v1)</sup> Wherefore seeing we also are compassed about with so great a cloud of witnesses, let us lay aside every weight, and the sin which doth so easily beset us, and let us run with patience the race that is set before us,

- If you feel that Satan is rising up his head against you, if you feel that old man coming back, you have to do any and everything to deaden that spirit. Now ain't the time for that. “Let us lay aside every weight”: everything that is trying to keep us from serving our God, “and the sin which doth so easily beset us, and let us run with patience the race that is set before us”.
- We're not worried about that war over there; God already has that. We need to worry about the war of the day-to-day. We don't have a lot of time left, so now is high time to wake up out of sleep and fight this battle right here.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 9:15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v15)</sup> For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.

    That weight is exactly what sin does: it presses you down from trying to please the Lord. “For the corruptible body presseth down the soul, and the earthy tabernacle weigheth down the mind that museth upon many things.” The earthy tabernacle is our flesh. We were born in sin and shapen in iniquity, so we have to understand the condition of the battle: we're going to fight against our flesh until we die or until Christ returns.
  - **[1 Corinthians 7:31](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v31)</sup> And they that use this world, as not abusing it: for the fashion of this world passeth away.

    This kingdom will go down; it's going to pass away just like everything else. “And they that use this world, as not abusing it”: you can use the things of this world, but don't get caught up, don't hit the snooze button, don't lean to the things of this world over Christ. “For the fashion of this world passeth away.”
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 1:19](/bible/sirach/1#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/1#v19)</sup> Wisdom raineth down skill and knowledge of understanding, and exalteth them to honour that hold her fast.

    When you start walking in this truth, you have to check yourself: you should become skilful in the word of God. “Wisdom raineth down skill and knowledge of understanding, and exalteth them to honour that hold her fast.”

**[Hebrews 12:4](/bible/hebrews/12#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/12#v4)</sup> Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin.

- There’s no revolution, brothers and sisters, without bloodshed. That might scare some of you, but this is war. You think this white man murdered millions of our people and is going to stand by and let a group of what he calls Negroes jump up and overthrow everything? He’s not going to let that happen.
- “Ye have not yet resisted unto blood, striving against sin.” Paul was getting on the Hebrews: y’all ain’t doing the work. Remember Paul was stoned, whipped, beaten; these are the things that occur when you go out and teach. Sisters, you take it for granted when your man goes out and teaches: he might not come home. Oh, you just teaching the Bible? You don’t understand: violence comes with teaching the Bible.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

    “I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.” When you come into this truth, like you men and women in here, you got to be willing to sacrifice for this truth. You think it just means I give up my job? It ain’t about your job. It means exactly what it says: in your life, be willing to become a living sacrifice. That’s your reasonable service.

**[Hebrews 12:6-7](/bible/hebrews/12#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/12#v6)</sup> For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth, and scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/12#v7)</sup> If ye endure chastening, God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?

- “For whom the Lord loveth he chasteneth”. Why would he correct us, rebuke us and take us through this if he didn't love us? The correction comes because of the love, not the opposite. Otherwise he would cut us off: look at Esau, he cut him off because he hates him.
- Some of you say, man, I'm going through it. You're supposed to go through it. Three flat tires? So what: you got one more than you deserve. We all go through it, but we keep pushing, because at the end of the day a whole kingdom is waiting on us. The world being turned right side up is waiting on us; even the animals are waiting on us. The whole world is waiting for us to stand up, and we have to do it manfully, the right way. We can't skate by. We do it together or we don't do it at all. “And scourgeth every son whom he receiveth.”
- “If ye endure chastening”: it says if. “God dealeth with you as with sons; for what son is he whom the father chasteneth not?” As a father you have to correct your son; if you don't correct him, you don't love him. A father wants to see his son do better than him, and if you don't want that, you don't love him as a son. In the truth, the leaders are fathers over the congregation, heads of the school, and want to see every man in here do better than them. Their leadership are fathers over them, and they get chastised as much as you do, if not more. That is out of love. But you turn your back to that and want to take your ball and go home. The hell with you then.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 27:5](/bible/sirach/27#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/27#v5)</sup> The furnace proveth the potter’s vessels; so the trial of man is in his reasoning.

    Going back to the fire: that correction, that chastisement, is the Lord trying your heart, seeing what your heart is made of. “The furnace proveth the potter’s vessels; so the trial of man is in his reasoning.” The furnace proves the potter's vessel, and what the Lord proves is man's reasoning. When the Lord turns up the heat on your behind, he tries your heart.

**[Hebrews 12:9](/bible/hebrews/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/12#v9)</sup> Furthermore we have had fathers of our flesh which corrected us, and we gave them reverence: shall we not much rather be in subjection unto the Father of spirits, and live?

- “Furthermore we have had fathers of our flesh which corrected us, and we gave them reverence”. We all had fathers in the flesh, most of whom beat the hell out of us, and we still reverenced our fathers. “Shall we not much rather be in subjection unto the Father of spirits, and live?” Christ is saying through Paul: isn't it better to submit yourself to the Father of spirits and live? The chastisement the Lord puts on us is to give us that kind of life: eternal life.

**[Hebrews 12:10](/bible/hebrews/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/12#v10)</sup> For they verily for a few days chastened us after their own pleasure; but he for our profit, that we might be partakers of his holiness.

- “For they verily for a few days chastened us after their own pleasure”: your parents chastened you for a few days. They beat you and put you on punishment, timeout: no TV for a week, no video games, you ain't going outside with your friends.
- “But he for our profit”: God for our spiritual profit, our benefit, “that we might be partakers of his holiness.” There is a divine purpose behind it.

**[Hebrews 12:11](/bible/hebrews/12#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/12#v11)</sup> Now no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous: nevertheless afterward it yieldeth the peaceable fruit of righteousness unto them which are exercised thereby.

- “Now no chastening for the present seemeth to be joyous, but grievous”: you are crying like hell when things go wrong. Some brothers have lost a loved one, and that hurts; some of your spouses bounced on you, and that hurts for a moment. “Nevertheless afterward it yieldeth the peaceable fruit of righteousness unto them which are exercised thereby.”

**[Hebrews 12:12](/bible/hebrews/12#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/12#v12)</sup> Wherefore lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees;

- “Wherefore lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees”. Christ is saying: get yourself together. Shake that spirit of depression. Your hands are down and your knees are bent, and he says straighten your back, lift up your hands, send up those prayers. Strengthen those feeble knees, instead of, oh, I can't do it.
- Here we come back to how this crown of pride can affect you. “Wherefore lift up the hands which hang down, and the feeble knees”: don’t let yourself fall into the way you saw in the example of our forefathers, with a crown of pride. Lift up the hands and the feeble knees and keep pressing forward.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 35:3-4](/bible/isaiah/35#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/35#v3)</sup> Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/35#v4)</sup> Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence; he will come and save you.

    “Strengthen ye the weak hands”: that is the depressed brother, the depressed spirit, the sister who can't get over nothing. “And confirm the feeble knees.” “Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not”: we have to tell each other that, be strong. “Behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence; he will come and save you.”

**[Hebrews 12:13](/bible/hebrews/12#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/12#v13)</sup> And make straight paths for your feet, lest that which is lame be turned out of the way; but let it rather be healed.

- “And make straight paths for your feet, lest that which is lame be turned out of the way; but let it rather be healed.” It uses the term healed here, and the scripture talks about the healing of the nations. But when it comes to men: what is a man in the process of healing? Usually laying down, out of commission, on the sidelines. As men in this fight, we don’t have time for healing.
- There is a certain measure of understanding for those who are just coming in and the sick state they are coming out of. But men got to be strong; men got to strengthen themselves. Put some spit on that and keep going; bind it up, throw some dirt on it, that’s it, and keep fighting.
- Proverbs 4 is saying the same thing as Hebrews 12: set yourself and keep moving, don’t let yourself go from one side to the other, lift it up and keep running the race, and move the way you need to move.

**[Hebrews 12:14](/bible/hebrews/12#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/12#v14)</sup> Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord:

- “Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord.” Some are not peaceful. If you do not follow peace with all men, you are not holy, and you are not going to see the kingdom. That is a heavy, powerful scripture.
- “Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord”. You are not getting any kingdom if you are bearing grudges and bitterness.

**[Hebrews 12:15](/bible/hebrews/12#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/hebrews/12#v15)</sup> Looking diligently lest any man fail of the grace of God; lest any root of bitterness springing up trouble you, and thereby many be defiled;

- Any time you hear somebody talk about someone who left IUIC like that, that is how you know they have bitterness in them, and how you will know whether you have it or not. There is always somebody who did them wrong, somebody who did them dirty. But they cannot find no scripture that goes against Matthew 18 or Leviticus 19:17, because they despise God’s laws, they hate God, yet they will tell you, I’m about the Lord’s business: if I left IUIC, God isn’t dealing with me? Ask yourself that question. So read the scripture about bitterness first, to be clear what the spirit of bitterness really means.
- “Looking diligently lest any man fail of the grace of God.” Grace is what teaches us how to live godly. If you fall from there, you are gone. “Lest any root of bitterness”: that is how you fail of it. If the root of bitterness comes out of you, there is nothing grace can do for you. If God helps you through that, it is not many people that come out of it. So when you catch yourself, humble yourself. You have to acknowledge your sin and confess it.
- The same three videos of wickedness you put on YouTube, you have to put three videos up to conquer them, acknowledging how wicked and how sinful you were. Don’t call us to the side, don’t text us; when you were doing your videos you did not call us. It is not an aside thing. Go on the same media and put the videos up. That is what is going to make you a man; until then you are just fake, like a fake dollar bill. Those that follow you are fools. But God is going to have mercy on some of them: they will catch themselves and say, I was a fool to follow these dumb negroes, and repent. If you are of God, if you are of the elect, you are going to catch yourself. If you are not, you will never catch yourself, and you will sit in the grave until the grave takes you over.
- “Springing up trouble you.” What kind of trouble is it going to trouble you with? Brother, I was missed, they didn’t judge the matter the right way, I’m angry with the brother. Why? I don’t think the judgment was right. I’m just five months old in this truth, but I don’t think it was right. Do you see yourself as a baby? No, and that is why. Stay in the spirit, so you can catch yourself out of stupidity. A lot of people who just came into this truth are always looking for problems: let me see if the brother has fringes on, let me see if the sister has her fringes, let me see if she covers her head; one, two years in the body, looking for problems, while they themselves are full of problems. That is why Peter said in 1 Peter 1 you have to be born again: fix yourself first, then you can see clearly what is going on. That is what Matthew is talking about: before you worry about the beam in your brother’s eye, worry about the mote in your own eye. But a lot of brothers and sisters point the finger.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 12:14](/bible/hebrews/12#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/12#v14)</sup> Follow peace with all men, and holiness, without which no man shall see the Lord:

    There is no way to live a life of bitterness and grow in this truth, become a better spouse, or retain the precepts that help you gain the kingdom of heaven while that bitterness is in you.
  - **[Ephesians 4:31-32](/bible/ephesians/4#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/ephesians/4#v31)</sup> Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice:
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32)</sup> And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.

    “Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice”. God says put all of these away from you.
  - **[1 Peter 5:8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)</sup> Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:

    Satan wants to devour all of us. Christ told Peter that Satan desired to have him, to sift him as wheat. “Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour”. We have to be in that mindset and know what's going on, family.

**[Hebrews 12:16-17](/bible/hebrews/12#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/hebrews/12#v16)</sup> Lest there be any fornicator, or profane person, as Esau, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/12#v17)</sup> For ye know how that afterward, when he would have inherited the blessing, he was rejected: for he found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears.

- “Lest there be any fornicator, or profane person, as Esau, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright.” Esau is set out as the profane person: he sold his birthright for one morsel of meat.
- “For ye know how that afterward, when he would have inherited the blessing, he was rejected”. The one word that strikes you in it is rejected. That is why it hurts him so much: he sought it “carefully with tears”, he cried, and after all that crying and emotion his father still says no. No cry me a river.
- And then the blessing turns to Jacob, the supplanter: that is what the prophecy was, that the elder would serve. We have the greatest comeback story ever; there is no greater comeback than what we just read.
- “Lest there be any fornicator, or profane person, as Esau, who for one morsel of meat sold his birthright.” Paul is speaking of being unforgiving, and he compares you to Esau. You have an Edomite spirit when you cannot forgive your brother or sister. “For ye know how that afterward, when he would have inherited the blessing, he was rejected: for he found no place of repentance, though he sought it carefully with tears.” If you are unforgiving, there is no kingdom for you; you are just like Esau.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 109:6-9](/bible/psalms/109#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/109#v6)</sup> Set thou a wicked man over him: and let Satan stand at his right hand.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/109#v7)</sup> When he shall be judged, let him be condemned: and let his prayer become sin.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/109#v8)</sup> Let his days be few; and let another take his office.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/109#v9)</sup> Let his children be fatherless, and his wife a widow.

    This is how we end up esteeming the brothers and sisters that are without the body: they are choked and offended. Paul compares them to Edom, who was profane, with “any fornicator, or profane person, as Esau”. He compares the wicked of our people to Esau.
  - **[1 John 3:8-10](/bible/1-john/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-john/3#v8)</sup> He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil sinneth from the beginning. For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-john/3#v9)</sup> Whosoever is born of God doth not commit sin; for his seed remaineth in him: and he cannot sin, because he is born of God.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-john/3#v10)</sup> In this the children of God are manifest, and the children of the devil: whosoever doeth not righteousness is not of God, neither he that loveth not his brother.

    The parallel being drawn is the spirit that jumps on you when you move in this fashion. “He that committeth sin is of the devil; for the devil sinneth from the beginning”: that is the history being shown, from Cain onward. “For this purpose the Son of God was manifested, that he might destroy the works of the devil.” So why move in the spirit of the devil and think it is the spirit of Christ?
  - **[Romans 8:6-7](/bible/romans/8#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/romans/8#v6)</sup> For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/romans/8#v7)</sup> Because the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.

    The man of the field, the profane man: what is that called? “For to be carnally minded is death”: Edomite minded, the profane mind. That is why he is called the son of perdition. “But to be spiritually minded is life and peace.” Spiritually minded, you want to gain your brother. That does not mean always being buddy-buddy, but the scripture says instead of a friend become not an enemy. Brothers hold on to a rivalry and turn a brother into an enemy so they can get back at him. That is the carnal mind.
