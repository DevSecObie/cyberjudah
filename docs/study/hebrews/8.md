## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Hebrews 8 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 4-5, 8-11, 13, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Hebrews 8:1-2](/bible/hebrews/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/8#v1)</sup> Now of the things which we have spoken this is the sum: We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/8#v2)</sup> A minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.

- Paul discussed the true temple, the fourth temple, but in allegory. “We have such an high priest, who is set on the right hand of the throne of the Majesty in the heavens; a minister of the sanctuary, and of the true tabernacle, which the Lord pitched, and not man.” The true tabernacle, which God made and not man.

  Precepts:
  - **[Daniel 7:13](/bible/daniel/7#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/7#v13)</sup> I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.

    “I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.” They brought Christ near before the Father: he went into the true holy of holies. That is the high priest set on the right hand of the throne.

**[Hebrews 8:4-5](/bible/hebrews/8#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/8#v4)</sup> For if he were on earth, he should not be a priest, seeing that there are priests that offer gifts according to the law:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/8#v5)</sup> Who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things, as Moses was admonished of God when he was about to make the tabernacle: for, See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.

- “There are priests that offer gifts according to the law”: the Levites, “who serve unto the example and shadow of heavenly things”. Everything done when the tabernacle and sanctuaries were set up is symbolic. “See, saith he, that thou make all things according to the pattern shewed to thee in the mount.” Everything Moses made was made according to the pattern God showed him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 9:1-5, 23-24](/bible/hebrews/9#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/9#v1)</sup> Then verily the first covenant had also ordinances of divine service, and a worldly sanctuary.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/hebrews/9#v2)</sup> For there was a tabernacle made; the first, wherein was the candlestick, and the table, and the shewbread; which is called the sanctuary.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/9#v3)</sup> And after the second veil, the tabernacle which is called the Holiest of all;
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/9#v4)</sup> Which had the golden censer, and the ark of the covenant overlaid round about with gold, wherein was the golden pot that had manna, and Aaron’s rod that budded, and the tables of the covenant;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/hebrews/9#v5)</sup> And over it the cherubims of glory shadowing the mercyseat; of which we cannot now speak particularly.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/hebrews/9#v23)</sup> It was therefore necessary that the patterns of things in the heavens should be purified with these; but the heavenly things themselves with better sacrifices than these.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/hebrews/9#v24)</sup> For Christ is not entered into the holy places made with hands, which are the figures of the true; but into heaven itself, now to appear in the presence of God for us:

    The worldly sanctuary again: the candlestick, the table and the shewbread; the Holiest of all with “the ark of the covenant overlaid round about with gold”, the manna, Aaron’s rod and the tables of the covenant, and “the cherubims of glory shadowing the mercyseat”.

**[Hebrews 8:8-9](/bible/hebrews/8#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/hebrews/8#v8)</sup> For finding fault with them, he saith, Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, when I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/hebrews/8#v9)</sup> Not according to the covenant that I made with their fathers in the day when I took them by the hand to lead them out of the land of Egypt; because they continued not in my covenant, and I regarded them not, saith the Lord.

- “For finding fault with them”: he found fault with the Israelites under the old covenant of law of sacrifice. “When I will make a new covenant with the house of Israel and with the house of Judah”. “Because they continued not in my covenant, and I regarded them not”: because we were rebellious.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 26:26-28](/bible/matthew/26#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/26#v26)</sup> And as they were eating, Jesus took bread, and blessed it, and brake it, and gave it to the disciples, and said, Take, eat; this is my body.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/26#v27)</sup> And he took the cup, and gave thanks, and gave it to them, saying, Drink ye all of it;
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/26#v28)</sup> For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins.

    It’s redundant; it says the same thing. “For this is my blood of the new testament, which is shed for many for the remission of sins”: the many that were scattered, not only Judah, Benjamin and Levi that he came to, but the northern kingdom and the remnants of Judah, Benjamin and Levi scattered abroad, who were not sacrificing, and who would have a chance to repent once they found out about Jesus Christ.
  - **[Romans 11:26-27](/bible/romans/11#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/romans/11#v26)</sup> And so all Israel shall be saved: as it is written, There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer, and shall turn away ungodliness from Jacob:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/romans/11#v27)</sup> For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins.

    “And so all Israel shall be saved”. “There shall come out of Sion the Deliverer”: that’s Christ. “For this is my covenant unto them, when I shall take away their sins”: my New Covenant. Christ is that mediator; his body took away our sins.

**[Hebrews 8:10-11](/bible/hebrews/8#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/hebrews/8#v10)</sup> For this is the covenant that I will make with the house of Israel after those days, saith the Lord; I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts: and I will be to them a God, and they shall be to me a people:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/hebrews/8#v11)</sup> And they shall not teach every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the Lord: for all shall know me, from the least to the greatest.

- “I will put my laws into their mind, and write them in their hearts”: this is where the novices get confused and fall off the horse. Everything is happening in stages. Right now we’re rehearsing the righteous acts under grace and mercy; when we enter into the kingdom the laws are going to be written in our minds and hearts, and we’re going to be all righteous. “And they shall not teach every man his neighbour”.

**[Hebrews 8:13](/bible/hebrews/8#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/8#v13)</sup> In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old. Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away.

- “In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old. Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away.”
- Obsolescence describes something that is passing out of use because something greater, newer or more effective has replaced it: the state of being old-fashioned and no longer useful, even if it still be in good working order. We are all headed toward the kingdom, and the Most High is going to use you. But it is up to you what you want to be used for, good or evil.
- “In that he saith, A new covenant, he hath made the first old”: the first, old covenant of animal sacrifice, which became obsolete. “Now that which decayeth and waxeth old is ready to vanish away”: ready upon Christ’s crucifixion. He did away with the old covenant because he was the lamb of God, the ultimate sacrifice for the nation of Israel, bringing in the new covenant. So the old covenant became obsolete.
- The problem is not merely that something old is being replaced. The problem comes when people refuse to accept that God is moving forward. That is when the agony of defeat comes in: you can’t understand or accept what God is doing, or that God is using a particular person, people or organization for a greater purpose because they are more effective. God doesn’t want the old. He wants to use the new, and you won’t accept that.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 5:17](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v17)</sup> Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature: old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.

    Why is it worded like that? “Therefore if any man be in Christ, he is a new creature”: in Christ, in the New Covenant. “Old things are passed away; behold, all things are become new.”
  - **[2 Corinthians 3:6](/bible/2-corinthians/3#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/3#v6)</sup> Who also hath made us able ministers of the new testament; not of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life.

    “Who also hath made us able ministers of the new testament”: that’s what we are, ministering this word to our brothers and sisters who are without. “Not of the letter”: the letter is the law of sacrifice. “But of the spirit”: the keeping of the commandments. “For the letter killeth”: because we could not keep the laws to perfection; there were certain things you could not sacrifice for. “But the spirit giveth life”: we have grace and mercy through Christ to repent of our sins in these last days.
  - **[Romans 7:5-6](/bible/romans/7#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/7#v5)</sup> For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members to bring forth fruit unto death.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/romans/7#v6)</sup> But now we are delivered from the law, that being dead wherein we were held; that we should serve in newness of spirit, and not in the oldness of the letter.

    “For when we were in the flesh, the motions of sins, which were by the law, did work in our members”. “But now we are delivered from the law”: the law of animal sacrifice. “That we should serve in newness of spirit”: when one comes to Christ old things pass away and all things become new. When this was written, the northern kingdom was over here in all sorts of foolishness and idolatry. They weren’t jumping back on the boat, Ephraim by ten thousands and Manasseh by thousands, to go back to Jerusalem to sacrifice, and after 70 AD there was nothing they could do. Those stranded in Peru, Bolivia, Argentina and Brazil, that means they’re completely done? This doctrine makes absolutely no sense. “And not in the oldness of the letter.”
