Read the chapter → [Nehemiah 3](/bible/nehemiah/3)

This session moves through Nehemiah chapters 3 through 6, following the rebuilding of the wall of Jerusalem after the return from captivity. Nehemiah, having received permission from the king, organizes the people family by family to repair the gates and the wall. As the work goes forward, the heathen rulers around Jerusalem mock, conspire, and plot to stop it, yet the Most High keeps the builders of one mind until the wall is finished. The physical rebuilding of the temple in that day is a pattern for the spiritual rebuilding of the minds of the people now.

**[Nehemiah 3:1](/bible/nehemiah/3#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/3#v1)</sup> Then Eliashib the high priest rose up with his brethren the priests, and they builded the sheep gate; they sanctified it, and set up the doors of it; even unto the tower of Meah they sanctified it, unto the tower of Hananeel.

- The high priest and his brethren the priests begin the work by building the sheep gate, sanctifying it, and setting up its doors as far as the tower of Meah and the tower of Hananeel.
- The rebuilding starts with the priests, showing that the sanctified work of the Most High is set in order first before the rest of the wall is raised.

**[Nehemiah 3:3](/bible/nehemiah/3#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/nehemiah/3#v3)</sup> But the fish gate did the sons of Hassenaah build, who also laid the beams thereof, and set up the doors thereof, the locks thereof, and the bars thereof.

- The sons of Hassenaah build the fish gate, laying its beams and setting up its doors, locks, and bars.
- Each gate is named and each family assigned, so the record shows a people organized and accountable for their portion of the work.

**[Nehemiah 3:5](/bible/nehemiah/3#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/nehemiah/3#v5)</sup> And next unto them the Tekoites repaired; but their nobles put not their necks to the work of their Lord.

- The Tekoites repair their section, but their nobles will not put their necks to the work of their Lord.
- Not everyone among the people is willing to labor. The nobles who hold back are marked out, showing that the work of the Most High separates those who serve from those who spare themselves.

**[Nehemiah 3:12](/bible/nehemiah/3#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/nehemiah/3#v12)</sup> And next unto him repaired Shallum the son of Halohesh, the ruler of the half part of Jerusalem, he and his daughters.

- Shallum the ruler of the half part of Jerusalem repairs his portion, he and his daughters.
- The daughters are named as builders alongside the men, showing the whole household given to the work.

**[Nehemiah 3:32](/bible/nehemiah/3#v32)**
> <sup>[32](/bible/nehemiah/3#v32)</sup> And between the going up of the corner unto the sheep gate repaired the goldsmiths and the merchants.

- Between the going up of the corner and the sheep gate the goldsmiths and the merchants make their repairs.
- The chapter closes the circuit of the wall with a long roll of the houses and trades that each took a piece, every man over against his own house.

# Nehemiah 4: Building With a Weapon in Hand

Read the chapter → [Nehemiah 4](/bible/nehemiah/4)

**[Nehemiah 4:1](/bible/nehemiah/4#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/4#v1)</sup> But it came to pass, that when Sanballat heard that we builded the wall, he was wroth, and took great indignation, and mocked the Jews.

- When Sanballat hears that the wall is being built he is wroth, takes great indignation, and mocks the Jews.
- One of the heathen rulers rises up against the work, treating the rebuilding of Jerusalem as something to be scorned.

**[Nehemiah 4:2](/bible/nehemiah/4#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/nehemiah/4#v2)</sup> And he spake before his brethren and the army of Samaria, and said, What do these feeble Jews? will they fortify themselves? will they sacrifice? will they make an end in a day? will they revive the stones out of the heaps of the rubbish which are burned?

- Before his brethren and the army of Samaria, Sanballat mocks the feeble Jews, asking if they will fortify themselves, sacrifice, finish in a day, and revive the burned stones out of the heaps of rubbish.
- He makes his mockery in front of his army, calling the people weak, the same way the heathen today mock the men who go out in one mind to do the work of the Most High.

**[Nehemiah 4:3](/bible/nehemiah/4#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/nehemiah/4#v3)</sup> Now Tobiah the Ammonite was by him, and he said, Even that which they build, if a fox go up, he shall even break down their stone wall.

- Tobiah the Ammonite stands by him and says that even what they build, if a fox go up on it, would break down their stone wall.
- The enemy despises the work as flimsy, but the builders answer by praying that their reproach be turned upon their own head.

**[Nehemiah 4:6](/bible/nehemiah/4#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/nehemiah/4#v6)</sup> So built we the wall; and all the wall was joined together unto the half thereof: for the people had a mind to work.

- The wall is joined together to half its height, for the people had a mind to work.
- This is the pattern for Israel United in Christ today, a people with a mind to work who understand the condition of their people and the mission to rebuild their minds.
  - **[Nehemiah 4:4](/bible/nehemiah/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/nehemiah/4#v4)</sup> Hear, O our God; for we are despised: and turn their reproach upon their own head, and give them for a prey in the land of captivity:

    Nehemiah answers the mockery with prayer, asking the Most High to turn the enemies' reproach upon their own heads.

**[Nehemiah 4:7](/bible/nehemiah/4#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/nehemiah/4#v7)</sup> But it came to pass, that when Sanballat, and Tobiah, and the Arabians, and the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites, heard that the walls of Jerusalem were made up, and that the breaches began to be stopped, then they were very wroth,

- When Sanballat, Tobiah, the Arabians, the Ammonites, and the Ashdodites hear that the walls are going up and the breaches are being stopped, they are very wroth.
- The breaches are the weaker places the enemies expected would fail. When they hear those gaps are fortified, their anger rises because their expectation of failure is broken.

**[Nehemiah 4:8](/bible/nehemiah/4#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/nehemiah/4#v8)</sup> And conspired all of them together to come and to fight against Jerusalem, and to hinder it.

- They conspire together to come and fight against Jerusalem and to hinder the work.
- Their whole plan is to cause the work to cease and keep the city from being rebuilt.
  - **[Nehemiah 4:9](/bible/nehemiah/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/nehemiah/4#v9)</sup> Nevertheless we made our prayer unto our God, and set a watch against them day and night, because of them.

    The builders make prayer to the Most High and set a watch against the enemies day and night.

**[Nehemiah 4:10](/bible/nehemiah/4#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/nehemiah/4#v10)</sup> And Judah said, The strength of the bearers of burdens is decayed, and there is much rubbish; so that we are not able to build the wall.

- Judah says the strength of the bearers of burdens is decayed, there is much rubbish, and they are not able to build the wall.
- Even among the workers the burden grows heavy and discouragement sets in as the enemy pressure mounts.

**[Nehemiah 4:11](/bible/nehemiah/4#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/nehemiah/4#v11)</sup> And our adversaries said, They shall not know, neither see, till we come in the midst among them, and slay them, and cause the work to cease.

- The adversaries say they will not be known or seen until they come into the midst and slay them and cause the work to cease.
- The enemies plot a surprise attack, aiming to kill the builders and stop the work entirely.

**[Nehemiah 4:13](/bible/nehemiah/4#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/nehemiah/4#v13)</sup> Therefore set I in the lower places behind the wall, and on the higher places, I even set the people after their families with their swords, their spears, and their bows.

- Nehemiah sets the people in the lower and higher places behind the wall after their families with their swords, spears, and bows.
- He arms the people by family and posts them at the exposed places, ready to defend the work.

**[Nehemiah 4:14](/bible/nehemiah/4#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/nehemiah/4#v14)</sup> And I looked, and rose up, and said unto the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, Be not ye afraid of them: remember the Lord, which is great and terrible, and fight for your brethren, your sons, and your daughters, your wives, and your houses.

- Nehemiah tells the nobles, rulers, and people not to be afraid but to remember the Lord, who is great and terrible, and to fight for their brethren, sons, daughters, wives, and houses.
- The answer to fear is to remember the greatness of the Most High and to fight for the family and the people.

**[Nehemiah 4:16](/bible/nehemiah/4#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/nehemiah/4#v16)</sup> And it came to pass from that time forth, that the half of my servants wrought in the work, and the other half of them held both the spears, the shields, and the bows, and the habergeons; and the rulers were behind all the house of Judah.

- From that time half of the servants work while the other half hold the spears, shields, and bows, and the rulers stand behind all of Judah.
- The people labor and guard at the same time, knowing the enemies are plotting against the rebuilding.

**[Nehemiah 4:17](/bible/nehemiah/4#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/nehemiah/4#v17)</sup> They which builded on the wall, and they that bare burdens, with those that laded, every one with one of his hands wrought in the work, and with the other hand held a weapon.

- Those who bear burdens work with one hand and hold a weapon with the other.
- Every builder keeps a weapon ready even while laying the wall, watchful against attack.

**[Nehemiah 4:18](/bible/nehemiah/4#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/nehemiah/4#v18)</sup> For the builders, every one had his sword girded by his side, and so builded. And he that sounded the trumpet was by me.

- Every builder has his sword girded by his side as he builds, and the one who sounds the trumpet stays by Nehemiah.
- The trumpet becomes the signal to gather, keeping the scattered workers ready to unite at the point of attack.

**[Nehemiah 4:19](/bible/nehemiah/4#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/nehemiah/4#v19)</sup> And I said unto the nobles, and to the rulers, and to the rest of the people, The work is great and large, and we are separated upon the wall, one far from another.

- Nehemiah tells the people that the work is great and large and they are separated far from one another along the wall.
- Because the builders are spread out, they need a plan to come together quickly if the enemy strikes.

**[Nehemiah 4:20](/bible/nehemiah/4#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/nehemiah/4#v20)</sup> In what place therefore ye hear the sound of the trumpet, resort ye thither unto us: our God shall fight for us.

- Wherever they hear the sound of the trumpet, they are to resort there, for the Most High shall fight for them.
- The confidence of the builders rests on the truth that their God fights for them.

**[Nehemiah 4:21](/bible/nehemiah/4#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/nehemiah/4#v21)</sup> So we laboured in the work: and half of them held the spears from the rising of the morning till the stars appeared.

- They labor in the work, half of them holding the spears from the rising of the morning until the stars appear.
- The people work from dawn to nightfall, giving the full day to the task.

**[Nehemiah 4:23](/bible/nehemiah/4#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/nehemiah/4#v23)</sup> So neither I, nor my brethren, nor my servants, nor the men of the guard which followed me, none of us put off our clothes, saving that every one put them off for washing.

- Neither Nehemiah, his brethren, his servants, nor the men of the guard put off their clothes, saving only for washing.
- They are so serious about the work and so watchful that they stay dressed and ready at all times, of one mind to finish the wall, the same way the people are to be of one mind and one spirit today in the resurrection of the people.

# Nehemiah 5: Nehemiah Rebukes Usury Among the Brethren

Read the chapter → [Nehemiah 5](/bible/nehemiah/5)

**[Nehemiah 5:1](/bible/nehemiah/5#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/5#v1)</sup> And there was a great cry of the people and of their wives against their brethren the Jews.

- There is a great cry of the people and their wives against their brethren the Jews.
- Even in the middle of the outside threat, a wrong rises up inside the camp among the people themselves.

**[Nehemiah 5:2](/bible/nehemiah/5#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/nehemiah/5#v2)</sup> For there were that said, We, our sons, and our daughters, are many: therefore we take up corn for them, that we may eat, and live.

- Some say their sons and daughters are many, and they take up corn for them that they may eat and live.
- The people are burdened, having to trade for grain simply to keep their large households alive.

**[Nehemiah 5:3](/bible/nehemiah/5#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/nehemiah/5#v3)</sup> Some also there were that said, We have mortgaged our lands, vineyards, and houses, that we might buy corn, because of the dearth.

- Others say they have mortgaged their lands, vineyards, and houses to buy corn because of the dearth.
- The famine forces the people to put up their property just to feed their families.

**[Nehemiah 5:4](/bible/nehemiah/5#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/nehemiah/5#v4)</sup> There were also that said, We have borrowed money for the king’s tribute, and that upon our lands and vineyards.

- Still others say they have borrowed money for the king's tribute upon their lands and vineyards.
- The heavy tax drives the people into debt against the very land they depend on.

**[Nehemiah 5:5](/bible/nehemiah/5#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/nehemiah/5#v5)</sup> Yet now our flesh is as the flesh of our brethren, our children as their children: and, lo, we bring into bondage our sons and our daughters to be servants, and some of our daughters are brought unto bondage already: neither is it in our power to redeem them; for other men have our lands and vineyards.

- Their flesh is as the flesh of their brethren, yet they are bringing their sons and daughters into bondage, and some daughters are already brought to bondage with no power to redeem them.
- Their own brethren are enslaving them over debt though they are one flesh, one people just come out of captivity.

**[Nehemiah 5:6](/bible/nehemiah/5#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/nehemiah/5#v6)</sup> And I was very angry when I heard their cry and these words.

- When Nehemiah hears their cry and these words he is very angry.
- The oppression of the people by their own brethren stirs his anger.

**[Nehemiah 5:7](/bible/nehemiah/5#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/nehemiah/5#v7)</sup> Then I consulted with myself, and I rebuked the nobles, and the rulers, and said unto them, Ye exact usury, every one of his brother. And I set a great assembly against them.

- Nehemiah consults with himself, rebukes the nobles and rulers for exacting usury each from his brother, and sets a great assembly against them.
- He brings a great multitude before the nobles to make the point plain that lending against their brethren for gain is usury and wrong.

**[Nehemiah 5:8](/bible/nehemiah/5#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/nehemiah/5#v8)</sup> And I said unto them, We after our ability have redeemed our brethren the Jews, which were sold unto the heathen; and will ye even sell your brethren? or shall they be sold unto us? Then held they their peace, and found nothing to answer.

- Nehemiah reminds them that he and his have redeemed their brethren the Jews from the heathen, and asks if they will now sell their own brethren, so that they hold their peace and find nothing to answer.
- The nobles are silenced because they have no answer for enslaving the same brothers others labored to buy back from the heathen.

**[Nehemiah 5:9](/bible/nehemiah/5#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/nehemiah/5#v9)</sup> Also I said, It is not good that ye do: ought ye not to walk in the fear of our God because of the reproach of the heathen our enemies?

- Nehemiah says it is not good what they do, and asks if they ought not to walk in the fear of God because of the reproach of the heathen their enemies.
- Their conduct toward their brethren should be governed by the fear of the Most High, especially with the enemies watching.

**[Nehemiah 5:10](/bible/nehemiah/5#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/nehemiah/5#v10)</sup> I likewise, and my brethren, and my servants, might exact of them money and corn: I pray you, let us leave off this usury.

- Nehemiah says he, his brethren, and his servants might also exact money and corn, but pleads that they leave off this usury.
- Even those with the right to lend are called to give up usury against their own people.

**[Nehemiah 5:11](/bible/nehemiah/5#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/nehemiah/5#v11)</sup> Restore, I pray you, to them, even this day, their lands, their vineyards, their oliveyards, and their houses, also the hundredth part of the money, and of the corn, the wine, and the oil, that ye exact of them.

- Nehemiah asks them to restore that same day the lands, vineyards, oliveyards, and houses, and the hundredth part of the money, corn, wine, and oil they had exacted.
- He calls for full and immediate restoration of what was taken from the brethren.

**[Nehemiah 5:12](/bible/nehemiah/5#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/nehemiah/5#v12)</sup> Then said they, We will restore them, and will require nothing of them; so will we do as thou sayest. Then I called the priests, and took an oath of them, that they should do according to this promise.

- They agree to restore everything and require nothing, so Nehemiah calls the priests and takes an oath of them to do according to this promise.
- The promise is bound by oath before the priests so it cannot be quietly abandoned.

**[Nehemiah 5:13](/bible/nehemiah/5#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/nehemiah/5#v13)</sup> Also I shook my lap, and said, So God shake out every man from his house, and from his labour, that performeth not this promise, even thus be he shaken out, and emptied. And all the congregation said, Amen, and praised the Lord. And the people did according to this promise.

- Nehemiah shakes his lap and says so may the Most High shake out every man from his house and labor who does not perform this promise, and all the congregation says Amen and praises the Lord, and the people do according to the promise.
- A visible sign seals the oath, and the whole congregation agrees, and the people keep the promise.

**[Nehemiah 5:14](/bible/nehemiah/5#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/nehemiah/5#v14)</sup> Moreover from the time that I was appointed to be their governor in the land of Judah, from the twentieth year even unto the two and thirtieth year of Artaxerxes the king, that is, twelve years, I and my brethren have not eaten the bread of the governor.

- From the twentieth year to the thirty-second year of Artaxerxes, twelve years as governor in Judah, Nehemiah and his brethren do not eat the bread of the governor.
- Nehemiah refuses the provision owed to his office, governing for twelve years without taking from the people.

**[Nehemiah 5:15](/bible/nehemiah/5#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/nehemiah/5#v15)</sup> But the former governors that had been before me were chargeable unto the people, and had taken of them bread and wine, beside forty shekels of silver; yea, even their servants bare rule over the people: but so did not I, because of the fear of God.

- The former governors were chargeable to the people, taking bread, wine, and forty shekels of silver, and their servants bore rule over the people, but Nehemiah does not do so because of the fear of God.
- The fear of the Most High keeps Nehemiah from the burden and rule that earlier governors laid on the people.

**[Nehemiah 5:16](/bible/nehemiah/5#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/nehemiah/5#v16)</sup> Yea, also I continued in the work of this wall, neither bought we any land: and all my servants were gathered thither unto the work.

- Nehemiah continues in the work of the wall, buys no land, and gathers all his servants to the work.
- He gives himself and his household to the labor rather than to gaining property.

**[Nehemiah 5:18](/bible/nehemiah/5#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/nehemiah/5#v18)</sup> Now that which was prepared for me daily was one ox and six choice sheep; also fowls were prepared for me, and once in ten days store of all sorts of wine: yet for all this required not I the bread of the governor, because the bondage was heavy upon this people.

- Daily one ox, six choice sheep, and fowls are prepared for him, with store of wine every ten days, yet he does not require the bread of the governor because the bondage was heavy upon the people.
- Though he provides a table, he refuses to charge the people for it, sparing them because their burden is already heavy.

**[Nehemiah 5:19](/bible/nehemiah/5#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/nehemiah/5#v19)</sup> Think upon me, my God, for good, according to all that I have done for this people.

- Nehemiah asks the Most High to think upon him for good according to all he has done for the people.
- Nehemiah was about the work and for the people, not the usery of the people, and he commits his labor to the remembrance of the Most High.

# Nehemiah 6: The Wall Finished Against the Enemies' Plots

Read the chapter → [Nehemiah 6](/bible/nehemiah/6)

**[Nehemiah 6:1](/bible/nehemiah/6#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/6#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass, when Sanballat, and Tobiah, and Geshem the Arabian, and the rest of our enemies, heard that I had builded the wall, and that there was no breach left therein; (though at that time I had not set up the doors upon the gates;)

- When Sanballat, Tobiah, Geshem the Arabian, and the rest of the enemies hear that the wall is built with no breach left, though the doors are not yet set on the gates, they turn to another plan.
- With the wall standing, the enemies shift from open threat to schemes against Nehemiah himself.

**[Nehemiah 6:2](/bible/nehemiah/6#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/nehemiah/6#v2)</sup> That Sanballat and Geshem sent unto me, saying, Come, let us meet together in some one of the villages in the plain of Ono. But they thought to do me mischief.

- Sanballat and Geshem send for Nehemiah to meet together in one of the villages in the plain of Ono, but they think to do him mischief.
- The invitation is a trap to draw Nehemiah away and harm him.

**[Nehemiah 6:3](/bible/nehemiah/6#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/nehemiah/6#v3)</sup> And I sent messengers unto them, saying, I am doing a great work, so that I cannot come down: why should the work cease, whilst I leave it, and come down to you?

- Nehemiah sends messengers saying he is doing a great work and cannot come down, asking why the work should cease while he leaves it to come to them.
- Nehemiah uses wisdom, refusing to leave the work for a meeting he knows is a plot to put him to death.

**[Nehemiah 6:4](/bible/nehemiah/6#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/nehemiah/6#v4)</sup> Yet they sent unto me four times after this sort; and I answered them after the same manner.

- They send to him four times after the same manner, and he answers them the same way each time.
- The enemies keep pressing, and Nehemiah keeps giving the same steady refusal.

**[Nehemiah 6:5](/bible/nehemiah/6#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/nehemiah/6#v5)</sup> Then sent Sanballat his servant unto me in like manner the fifth time with an open letter in his hand;

- The fifth time Sanballat sends his servant with an open letter in his hand.
- The open letter is meant to be read by others, a public pressure tactic against Nehemiah.

**[Nehemiah 6:6](/bible/nehemiah/6#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/nehemiah/6#v6)</sup> Wherein was written, It is reported among the heathen, and Gashmu saith it, that thou and the Jews think to rebel: for which cause thou buildest the wall, that thou mayest be their king, according to these words.

- The letter reports among the heathen, and Geshem says it, that Nehemiah and the Jews think to rebel and that this is why he builds the wall, that he may be their king.
- The enemies invent a charge of rebellion to frighten Nehemiah and give the king cause against him.

**[Nehemiah 6:7](/bible/nehemiah/6#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/nehemiah/6#v7)</sup> And thou hast also appointed prophets to preach of thee at Jerusalem, saying, There is a king in Judah: and now shall it be reported to the king according to these words. Come now therefore, and let us take counsel together.

- The letter claims Nehemiah has appointed prophets to proclaim him king in Judah and threatens to report it to the king, inviting him to take counsel together.
- The false report is designed to force Nehemiah into their meeting under fear.

**[Nehemiah 6:8](/bible/nehemiah/6#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/nehemiah/6#v8)</sup> Then I sent unto him, saying, There are no such things done as thou sayest, but thou feignest them out of thine own heart.

- Nehemiah answers that there are no such things done as they say, but that they feign them out of their own heart.
- Nehemiah plainly names the accusation as an invention of their own minds.

**[Nehemiah 6:9](/bible/nehemiah/6#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/nehemiah/6#v9)</sup> For they all made us afraid, saying, Their hands shall be weakened from the work, that it be not done. Now therefore, O God, strengthen my hands.

- They all try to make the builders afraid, saying their hands shall be weakened so the work is not done, and Nehemiah prays for the Most High to strengthen his hands.
- The whole aim is to weaken the hands and stop the work, so Nehemiah turns to the Most High for strength.

**[Nehemiah 6:10](/bible/nehemiah/6#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/nehemiah/6#v10)</sup> Afterward I came unto the house of Shemaiah the son of Delaiah the son of Mehetabeel, who was shut up; and he said, Let us meet together in the house of God, within the temple, and let us shut the doors of the temple: for they will come to slay thee; yea, in the night will they come to slay thee.

- Shemaiah, shut up in his house, tells Nehemiah to meet within the temple and shut the doors because men will come to slay him in the night.
- A hired man tries to lure Nehemiah into hiding in the temple, framing it as safety.

**[Nehemiah 6:11](/bible/nehemiah/6#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/nehemiah/6#v11)</sup> And I said, Should such a man as I flee? and who is there, that, being as I am, would go into the temple to save his life? I will not go in.

- Nehemiah answers, should such a man as I flee, and who, being as I am, would go into the temple to save his life, and says he will not go in.
- Nehemiah refuses to run or to misuse the temple to save himself, standing firm in his place.

**[Nehemiah 6:12](/bible/nehemiah/6#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/nehemiah/6#v12)</sup> And, lo, I perceived that God had not sent him; but that he pronounced this prophecy against me: for Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him.

- Nehemiah perceives that the Most High had not sent Shemaiah, but that Tobiah and Sanballat had hired him to pronounce this prophecy.
- Nehemiah discerns the false prophecy and sees the hand of the enemies who paid for it.

**[Nehemiah 6:13](/bible/nehemiah/6#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/nehemiah/6#v13)</sup> Therefore was he hired, that I should be afraid, and do so, and sin, and that they might have matter for an evil report, that they might reproach me.

- Shemaiah was hired so that Nehemiah would be afraid, do so, and sin, giving them matter for an evil report to reproach him.
- The scheme is to trap Nehemiah into a fearful sin they could use to shame him.

**[Nehemiah 6:14](/bible/nehemiah/6#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/nehemiah/6#v14)</sup> My God, think thou upon Tobiah and Sanballat according to these their works, and on the prophetess Noadiah, and the rest of the prophets, that would have put me in fear.

- Nehemiah asks the Most High to think upon Tobiah, Sanballat, the prophetess Noadiah, and the rest of the prophets who would have put him in fear according to their works.
- He commits the hired accusers and false prophets to the judgment of the Most High.

**[Nehemiah 6:15](/bible/nehemiah/6#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/nehemiah/6#v15)</sup> So the wall was finished in the twenty and fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty and two days.

- The wall is finished on the twenty-fifth day of the month Elul, in fifty-two days.
- Despite all the plots, the whole wall is completed in fifty-two days.

**[Nehemiah 6:16](/bible/nehemiah/6#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/nehemiah/6#v16)</sup> And it came to pass, that when all our enemies heard thereof, and all the heathen that were about us saw these things, they were much cast down in their own eyes: for they perceived that this work was wrought of our God.

- When the enemies and all the heathen about them hear of it, they are much cast down in their own eyes, for they perceive that the work was wrought of the Most High.
- The completed wall shames the enemies, who recognize that this was the work and pleasure of the Most High.

**[Nehemiah 6:17](/bible/nehemiah/6#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/nehemiah/6#v17)</sup> Moreover in those days the nobles of Judah sent many letters unto Tobiah, and the letters of Tobiah came unto them.

- In those days the nobles of Judah send many letters to Tobiah, and his letters come to them.
- Even inside Judah some remain tied to Tobiah, keeping a line of communication with the enemy.

**[Nehemiah 6:18](/bible/nehemiah/6#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/nehemiah/6#v18)</sup> For there were many in Judah sworn unto him, because he was the son in law of Shechaniah the son of Arah; and his son Johanan had taken the daughter of Meshullam the son of Berechiah.

- Many in Judah are sworn to Tobiah because he is the son-in-law of Shechaniah and his son married the daughter of Meshullam.
- Marriage alliances bind some of Judah to the enemy, dividing loyalty within the people.

**[Nehemiah 6:19](/bible/nehemiah/6#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/nehemiah/6#v19)</sup> Also they reported his good deeds before me, and uttered my words to him. And Tobiah sent letters to put me in fear.

- They report Tobiah's good deeds before Nehemiah and carry Nehemiah's words to Tobiah, and Tobiah sends letters to put him in fear.
- To the end the enemy uses those inside to pressure and frighten Nehemiah, the same way anything that can be done to stop the work of the Most High is still done today, and the people must keep their spiritual eyes open to see it.
