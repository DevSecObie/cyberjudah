Read the chapter → [Nehemiah 7](/bible/nehemiah/7)

This session covers [Nehemiah 7](/bible/nehemiah/7) through 11, the setting of Jerusalem back in order after the wall was finished. The chapter opens with a long register of the families, priests, Levites, singers, porters, and Nethinims who first came up out of captivity, the same account recorded in [Ezra 2](/bible/ezra/2). From there the reading moves to Ezra opening the book of the law before all the people at the water gate, the keeping of the Feast of Booths, the Levites recounting the whole history of the Most High's mercy toward a stubborn people, and the sure covenant the princes, Levites, and priests sealed to keep the law. The opening precepts mark up how the Most High deals with a people He gathers, corrects, and restores.

**[Nehemiah 7:1-4](/bible/nehemiah/7#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/7#v1)</sup> Now it came to pass, when the wall was built, and I had set up the doors, and the porters and the singers and the Levites were appointed,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/nehemiah/7#v2)</sup> That I gave my brother Hanani, and Hananiah the ruler of the palace, charge over Jerusalem: for he was a faithful man, and feared God above many.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/nehemiah/7#v3)</sup> And I said unto them, Let not the gates of Jerusalem be opened until the sun be hot; and while they stand by, let them shut the doors, and bar them: and appoint watches of the inhabitants of Jerusalem, every one in his watch, and every one to be over against his house.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/nehemiah/7#v4)</sup> Now the city was large and great: but the people were few therein, and the houses were not builded.

- When the wall was built and the doors set up, Nehemiah appoints the porters, the singers, and the Levites, and sets his brother Hanani and Hananiah the ruler of the palace over Jerusalem.
- The city was large and great, but the people within it were few and the houses not yet built.

**[Nehemiah 7:5-7](/bible/nehemiah/7#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/nehemiah/7#v5)</sup> And my God put into mine heart to gather together the nobles, and the rulers, and the people, that they might be reckoned by genealogy. And I found a register of the genealogy of them which came up at the first, and found written therein,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/nehemiah/7#v6)</sup> These are the children of the province, that went up out of the captivity, of those that had been carried away, whom Nebuchadnezzar the king of Babylon had carried away, and came again to Jerusalem and to Judah, every one unto his city;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/nehemiah/7#v7)</sup> Who came with Zerubbabel, Jeshua, Nehemiah, Azariah, Raamiah, Nahamani, Mordecai, Bilshan, Mispereth, Bigvai, Nehum, Baanah. The number, I say, of the men of the people of Israel was this;

- The Most High puts it into Nehemiah's heart to gather the nobles, rulers, and people to be reckoned by genealogy, and he finds the register of those who came up first.
- This register matches the one recorded in [Ezra 2](/bible/ezra/2). Every name means something, and Israel kept a careful record of exactly who returned to the land.

> <sup>[61](/bible/nehemiah/7#v61)</sup> And these were they which went up also from Tel–melah, Tel–haresha, Cherub, Addon, and Immer: but they could not shew their father’s house, nor their seed, whether they were of Israel.

>
> <sup>[63](/bible/nehemiah/7#v63)</sup> And of the priests: the children of Habaiah, the children of Koz, the children of Barzillai, which took one of the daughters of Barzillai the Gileadite to wife, and was called after their name.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/nehemiah/7#v64)</sup> These sought their register among those that were reckoned by genealogy, but it was not found: therefore were they, as polluted, put from the priesthood.
>
> <sup>[65](/bible/nehemiah/7#v65)</sup> And the Tirshatha said unto them, that they should not eat of the most holy things, till there stood up a priest with Urim and Thummim.

- Some came up from Telmelah, Telharsha, Cherub, Addon, and Immer but could not show their father's house or their seed, whether they were of Israel.
- The priests who could not find their register by genealogy were put from the priesthood as polluted. The Tirshatha forbade them the most holy things until a priest stood up with Urim and Thummim, because a man had to prove he came from the line of Aaron.

>
> <sup>[68](/bible/nehemiah/7#v68)</sup> Their horses, seven hundred thirty and six: their mules, two hundred forty and five:
>
> <sup>[69](/bible/nehemiah/7#v69)</sup> Their camels, four hundred thirty and five: six thousand seven hundred and twenty asses.

- The whole congregation together numbered forty and two thousand three hundred and threescore, beside their menservants and maidservants.
- Among them were singing men and singing women, and their horses, mules, camels, and asses are all counted. There is nothing wrong with a brother singing, and if the Most High gives the gift, it should be used.

> <sup>[73](/bible/nehemiah/7#v73)</sup> So the priests, and the Levites, and the porters, and the singers, and some of the people, and the Nethinims, and all Israel, dwelt in their cities; and when the seventh month came, the children of Israel were in their cities.

- Some of the chief of the fathers give to the work: gold, silver, priests' garments, and offerings to the treasure.
- So the priests, the Levites, the porters, the singers, the Nethinims, and all Israel dwell in their cities, setting back in order the things that had been lacking.
