## Introduction

This session covers the last three chapters of Nehemiah and then opens the book of Esther. [Nehemiah 11](/bible/nehemiah/11) and 12 are largely registers of names, the record of who came back and settled Jerusalem, who served as priests and Levites, and how they dedicated the wall with thanksgiving and singing. These are our ancestors, and this is the book of records; a man's name has to be found written in the book to have a part in the inheritance. [Nehemiah 13](/bible/nehemiah/13) turns to reform, cutting off the mixed multitude, guarding the Sabbath from buying and selling, and putting away strange wives that had turned the people's hearts and their children's speech away from the law. The thread through it all is order restored according to the covenant given by Moses. Read these chapters as the record of a people applying their laws again once they came back into their land.

**[Nehemiah 11:1-2](/bible/nehemiah/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/nehemiah/11#v1)</sup> And the rulers of the people dwelt at Jerusalem: the rest of the people also cast lots, to bring one of ten to dwell in Jerusalem the holy city, and nine parts to dwell in other cities.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/nehemiah/11#v2)</sup> And the people blessed all the men, that willingly offered themselves to dwell at Jerusalem.

- The rulers of the people dwelt at Jerusalem, and the rest of the people cast lots to bring one of ten to dwell in the holy city, while nine parts remained in the other cities.
- This flows out of the covenant made in chapter 10, where the people entered into a curse and an oath to walk in the law of Moses, keep the Sabbath, and not marry the people of the land. Coming back into the land, they began to apply the things they had always done.

  - **[Nehemiah 10:29](/bible/nehemiah/10#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/nehemiah/10#v29)</sup> They clave to their brethren, their nobles, and entered into a curse, and into an oath, to walk in God’s law, which was given by Moses the servant of God, and to observe and do all the commandments of the Lord our Lord, and his judgments and his statutes;

    The people clave to their nobles and entered into a curse and an oath to walk in God's law given by Moses and to do all his commandments.
  - **[Nehemiah 10:30](/bible/nehemiah/10#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/nehemiah/10#v30)</sup> And that we would not give our daughters unto the people of the land, nor take their daughters for our sons:

    Part of that oath was that they would not give their daughters to the people of the land nor take their daughters for their sons.
  - **[Nehemiah 10:31](/bible/nehemiah/10#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/nehemiah/10#v31)</sup> And if the people of the land bring ware or any victuals on the sabbath day to sell, that we would not buy it of them on the sabbath, or on the holy day: and that we would leave the seventh year, and the exaction of every debt.

    They also swore not to buy victuals from the people of the land on the Sabbath day, the same Sabbath commerce Nehemiah later has to enforce in chapter 13.
  - **[Isaiah 3:16-17](/bible/isaiah/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/3#v16)</sup> Moreover the Lord saith, Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and walk with stretched forth necks and wanton eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet:
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/3#v17)</sup> Therefore the Lord will smite with a scab the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and the Lord will discover their secret parts.

    As the holy city is filled again, the haughtiness of the daughters of Zion is a warning against pride and outward vanity in the restored people.

# Nehemiah 13: Cleansing the People, the Sabbath, and the Strange Wives

- When the people heard the law, they separated from Israel all the mixed multitude. The reading of the law produced immediate obedience, cutting off what the law said to cut off.

  - **[Numbers 22:5-6](/bible/numbers/22#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/22#v5)</sup> He sent messengers therefore unto Balaam the son of Beor to Pethor, which is by the river of the land of the children of his people, to call him, saying, Behold, there is a people come out from Egypt: behold, they cover the face of the earth, and they abide over against me:
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/numbers/22#v6)</sup> Come now therefore, I pray thee, curse me this people; for they are too mighty for me: peradventure I shall prevail, that we may smite them, and that I may drive them out of the land: for I wot that he whom thou blessest is blessed, and he whom thou cursest is cursed.

    Balak hired Balaam to curse Israel, the history behind why the Ammonite and Moabite were shut out of the congregation.
  - **[Deuteronomy 23:3-4](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v3)</sup> An Ammonite or Moabite shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord; even to their tenth generation shall they not enter into the congregation of the Lord for ever:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v4)</sup> Because they met you not with bread and with water in the way, when ye came forth out of Egypt; and because they hired against thee Balaam the son of Beor of Pethor of Mesopotamia, to curse thee.

    The law itself: an Ammonite or Moabite shall not enter the congregation of the Lord for ever, because they met not Israel with bread and water and hired Balaam against them.

>

>
> <sup>[17](/bible/nehemiah/13#v17)</sup> Then I contended with the nobles of Judah, and said unto them, What evil thing is this that ye do, and profane the sabbath day?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/nehemiah/13#v18)</sup> Did not your fathers thus, and did not our God bring all this evil upon us, and upon this city? yet ye bring more wrath upon Israel by profaning the sabbath.

- Nehemiah saw men in Judah treading wine presses on the Sabbath, bringing in sheaves, lading asses, and bringing wine, grapes, and figs into Jerusalem on the Sabbath day, and men of Tyre selling fish and all manner of ware on the Sabbath.
- He contended with the nobles of Judah and asked what evil thing this was to profane the Sabbath day, reminding them that their fathers did the same and the Most High brought this evil upon the city, and now they brought more wrath upon Israel. Buying and selling on the Sabbath is breaking the commandment.

>

> <sup>[21](/bible/nehemiah/13#v21)</sup> Then I testified against them, and said unto them, Why lodge ye about the wall? if ye do so again, I will lay hands on you. From that time forth came they no more on the sabbath.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/nehemiah/13#v22)</sup> And I commanded the Levites that they should cleanse themselves, and that they should come and keep the gates, to sanctify the sabbath day. Remember me, O my God, concerning this also, and spare me according to the greatness of thy mercy.

- When the gates of Jerusalem began to be dark before the Sabbath, Nehemiah commanded them shut and set his servants at the gates so no burden would be brought in on the Sabbath, and the merchants lodged outside Jerusalem once or twice.

This laying on of hands is not a blessing but a serious warning, and the merchants knew he was not joking. He then commanded the Levites to cleanse themselves and keep the gates to sanctify the Sabbath day.
