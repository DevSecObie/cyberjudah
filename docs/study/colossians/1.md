## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Colossians 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 4, 6-8, 10, 12-21, 24-29, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Colossians 1:1-2](/bible/colossians/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/colossians/1#v1)</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, and Timotheus our brother,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/colossians/1#v2)</sup> To the saints and faithful brethren in Christ which are at Colosse: Grace be unto you, and peace, from God our Father and the Lord Jesus Christ.

- Ephesus was a great city: the big theater, and Diana of the Ephesians.
- “To the saints and faithful brethren in Christ which are at Colosse.” Just like Ephesians is written to the saints at Ephesus, this is the same concept: the saints in Colosse. These are Israelites in Asia.

**[Colossians 1:4](/bible/colossians/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/colossians/1#v4)</sup> Since we heard of your faith in Christ Jesus, and of the love which ye have to all the saints,

- “Since we heard of your faith in Christ Jesus, and of the love which ye have to all the saints.” Paul is saying he never went to Colosse. The spirit was so heavy in Ephesus that it spread all throughout Asia, and Paul ended up hearing there was another church over in Colosse, fervent in spirit too, so he wrote to them.
- On the map you have Ephesus in the middle, Colosse to the right of it, and above it Laodicea, which you read about in Revelation, and Hierapolis. Colosse was known as one of the wealthy cities of that region, and the gospel spread through all those cities.

  Precepts:
  - **[Colossians 2:1](/bible/colossians/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/colossians/2#v1)</sup> For I would that ye knew what great conflict I have for you, and for them at Laodicea, and for as many as have not seen my face in the flesh;

    Paul said he heard of their faith, though he did not visit them. “For I would that ye knew what great conflict I have for you, and for them at Laodicea, and for as many as have not seen my face in the flesh.” Why had they not seen Paul’s face? Because he never went to Laodicea and he never went to Colosse. The word reached them through others.

**[Colossians 1:6](/bible/colossians/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/colossians/1#v6)</sup> Which is come unto you, as it is in all the world; and bringeth forth fruit, as it doth also in you, since the day ye heard of it, and knew the grace of God in truth:

- “Which is come unto you, as it is in all the world; and bringeth forth fruit, as it doth also in you, since the day ye heard of it, and knew the grace of God in truth.” This is the spread of the gospel throughout Asia, and specifically to Colosse. The word came unto them as it came in all the world, and brought forth fruit in them too.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 19:26-27](/bible/acts/19#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/acts/19#v26)</sup> Moreover ye see and hear, that not alone at Ephesus, but almost throughout all Asia, this Paul hath persuaded and turned away much people, saying that they be no gods, which are made with hands:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/acts/19#v27)</sup> So that not only this our craft is in danger to be set at nought; but also that the temple of the great goddess Diana should be despised, and her magnificence should be destroyed, whom all Asia and the world worshippeth.

    The gospel came unto them as in all the world. In Ephesus they said, “not alone at Ephesus, but almost throughout all Asia, this Paul hath persuaded and turned away much people, saying that they be no gods, which are made with hands.” Turned away from idolatry, to serving the one true God and believing on Jesus the Christ.

**[Colossians 1:7](/bible/colossians/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/colossians/1#v7)</sup> As ye also learned of Epaphras our dear fellowservant, who is for you a faithful minister of Christ;

- “As ye also learned of Epaphras our dear fellowservant, who is for you a faithful minister of Christ.” How did the church at Colosse hear and learn? Through Epaphras. That is how they woke up: this brother heard it and said, I’m going to teach all of you.
- That is just like today, when somebody sees IUIC on YouTube and says, I have to print out these flyers and start hitting the city, and then this school pops up, then that school pops up. We are the continuation of the acts of the apostles. When we read these scriptures we have to put ourselves in them and envision how the forefathers were, because we are doing the exact same thing.
- Epaphras was a self-starter. He did not need someone right there to get the ground running. He said, that’s true, I believe it, I’m going to go teach that. A faithful minister of Christ.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 18:8](/bible/acts/18#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/acts/18#v8)</sup> And Crispus, the chief ruler of the synagogue, believed on the Lord with all his house; and many of the Corinthians hearing believed, and were baptized.

    An example of the same concept. “After these things Paul departed from Athens, and came to Corinth”: the church at Corinth. Then: “And Crispus, the chief ruler of the synagogue, believed on the Lord with all his house; and many of the Corinthians hearing believed, and were baptized.”
  - **[Colossians 4:12-13](/bible/colossians/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/colossians/4#v12)</sup> Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ, saluteth you, always labouring fervently for you in prayers, that ye may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/colossians/4#v13)</sup> For I bear him record, that he hath a great zeal for you, and them that are in Laodicea, and them in Hierapolis.

    More on Epaphras. “Epaphras, who is one of you, a servant of Christ, saluteth you, always labouring fervently for you in prayers, that ye may stand perfect and complete in all the will of God.” He is one of them, a Colossian.
  - **[Philemon 1:23-25](/bible/philemon/1#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/philemon/1#v23)</sup> There salute thee Epaphras, my fellowprisoner in Christ Jesus;
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/philemon/1#v24)</sup> Marcus, Aristarchus, Demas, Lucas, my fellowlabourers.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/philemon/1#v25)</sup> The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with your spirit. Amen. Written from Rome to Philemon, by Onesimus a servant.

    “There salute thee Epaphras, my fellowprisoner in Christ Jesus.” Paul calls Epaphras his fellow prisoner. Why is he mentioned in the epistle to Philemon? Because Philemon was also from Colosse.

**[Colossians 1:8](/bible/colossians/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/1#v8)</sup> Who also declared unto us your love in the Spirit.

- “Who also declared unto us your love in the Spirit.” Epaphras brought word back to Paul of the Colossians.

**[Colossians 1:10](/bible/colossians/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/colossians/1#v10)</sup> That ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work, and increasing in the knowledge of God;

- “That ye might walk worthy of the Lord unto all pleasing, being fruitful in every good work”: like we read earlier, that’s why he created us. “And increasing in the knowledge of God.” Because if you’re not increasing, by default you’re getting worse. So once you tap into that full potential, you have to sustain the full potential, and grow upon it and build upon it.

**[Colossians 1:12](/bible/colossians/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/colossians/1#v12)</sup> Giving thanks unto the Father, which hath made us meet to be partakers of the inheritance of the saints in light:

- The angels gave thanks to the one on the throne; Paul likewise teaches the brothers and sisters in Colosse to give thanks to the Father. He has made us fit to share the inheritance of the saints in light. Giving him thanks is the response to what he has prepared for us.

**[Colossians 1:13](/bible/colossians/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/colossians/1#v13)</sup> Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son:

- The power of darkness is Satan's kingdom, Babylon, Rome, the realm in which the white man rules. Darkness is sin. The Father delivers us from that power and translates us into the kingdom of his dear Son. Leaving that darkness means learning and following the light Christ brought.
- “Who hath delivered us from the power of darkness, and hath translated us into the kingdom of his dear Son”. We were doomed. We were caught up in our lust, our idols, whatever it may be, but God saw fit to enlighten us, to open up our eyes.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 16:10](/bible/revelation/16#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/16#v10)</sup> And the fifth angel poured out his vial upon the seat of the beast; and his kingdom was full of darkness; and they gnawed their tongues for pain,

    The fifth angel pours his vial on the seat of the beast, and its kingdom is full of darkness while they gnaw their tongues for pain. The beast is the so-called white man. The place where he rules is the kingdom of darkness, Satan's realm, from which the Father delivers his people. The darkness translates to sin.

**[Colossians 1:14](/bible/colossians/1#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/colossians/1#v14)</sup> In whom we have redemption through his blood, even the forgiveness of sins:

- Redemption and forgiveness come through Christ's blood. Without his sacrifice we can do nothing. Evil is present even when we want to do good; that nature is in every man and woman from the womb. Watch a child take candy when nobody is looking, or strike another child who receives the toy he wanted. The evil nature does not have to be taught, but the light, God's laws, must be taught.
- “Even the forgiveness of sins.” Now is the time, brothers and sisters.

**[Colossians 1:15](/bible/colossians/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/colossians/1#v15)</sup> Who is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of every creature:

- Christ is the image of the invisible God and the firstborn of every creature. Firstborn means exactly that: he is the first of the Father's creations. That is why he is the only begotten Son. He is not merely another prophet. Proverbs explains his existence with the Father before the earth and the rest of creation.
- “Who is the image of the invisible God”: Christ is the image of the invisible God. The Father is called the invisible God because no one ever saw him. The word of God, his Son Christ, is the only one that ever saw him; that is why he is called the invisible God. It doesn’t mean he has an invisible body. He is the image, meaning Christ looks like him.
- “The firstborn of every creature”: Christ was the first thing created, the first thing in day one. A lot of things were created on that day one, things on a celestial level: Satan, good, evil, the angels. Everything that was in the heavens was created on day one. That is why it says all the host in heaven was created and all the host on earth was created.
- “The firstborn of every creature”: Christ was the first thing created. He was created before the earth, before the heavens, before the angels. He is “the image of the invisible God”.

**[Colossians 1:16-17](/bible/colossians/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/colossians/1#v16)</sup> For by him were all things created, that are in heaven, and that are in earth, visible and invisible, whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/colossians/1#v17)</sup> And he is before all things, and by him all things consist.

- All things in heaven and earth, visible and invisible, thrones, dominions, principalities and powers, were created by Christ and for him. He is before all things and by him they consist. Proverbs 8 and John's opening already explained it. Christ is much more than a regular prophet.
- “For by him were all things created”: the him there is Christ. Christ created the angels, and guess who he also created: Satan. By him were all things created, meaning Satan was created by him and the angels were created by Christ.
- The things “that are in heaven, and that are in earth” were created by Christ, by the word of God. That is why brothers don’t fully understand the eternity and the power of the one who came and died for us. He created everything; he is a God.
- “Visible and invisible”: the things you can see were created by him, and the things you cannot see were created by him, “whether they be thrones, or dominions, or principalities, or powers: all things were created by him, and for him.” All things were created by the word of God, and all things were created for him: the Father said, create them things, and they are for you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 4:7-8](/bible/luke/4#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/luke/4#v7)</sup> If thou therefore wilt worship me, all shall be thine.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/luke/4#v8)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto him, Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.

    Christ created Satan, and here Satan is tempting him: “If thou therefore wilt worship me, all shall be thine.” This is what Jesus answered Satan while he was tempting him: “Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.”
  - **[Luke 4:9-12](/bible/luke/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/luke/4#v9)</sup> And he brought him to Jerusalem, and set him on a pinnacle of the temple, and said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, cast thyself down from hence:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/luke/4#v10)</sup> For it is written, He shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee:
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/luke/4#v11)</sup> And in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/luke/4#v12)</sup> And Jesus answering said unto him, It is said, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.

    The point is in verse 12, read from verse 9. Satan “brought him to Jerusalem, and set him on a pinnacle of the temple, and said unto him, If thou be the Son of God”. Who is the Son of God? Christ is the Son of God, and he knows he is. The Son of God is also the word of God, and he created everything: all things were created by him and for him.
  - **[Psalms 33:6](/bible/psalms/33#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/33#v6)</sup> By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.

    “By the word of the Lord were the heavens made”: by the word of the Lord, which is Christ, were the heavens made, “and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.” The same way the Lord breathed breath into things, Christ breathed breath into things too.

**[Colossians 1:18-19](/bible/colossians/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)</sup> And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/colossians/1#v19)</sup> For it pleased the Father that in him should all fulness dwell;

- Christ is the head of the body, the church, the beginning and the firstborn from the dead. He has the preeminence in all things, both as the first creation and the firstborn from the dead. The Father was pleased that all fullness should dwell in him.
- The church is not the Roman Catholic Church. Church means the general assembly, Israel, the people who were with Moses in the wilderness and received the lively oracles. Christ is the head of that assembly.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 12:23](/bible/hebrews/12#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/hebrews/12#v23)</sup> To the general assembly and church of the firstborn, which are written in heaven, and to God the Judge of all, and to the spirits of just men made perfect,

    The general assembly is the church of the firstborn, written in heaven, with God the judge of all and the spirits of just men made perfect. Christ is the firstborn and head of that church. General assembly explains the word church; it does not identify the Roman Catholic institution as Christ's body.
  - **[Acts 7:37-38](/bible/acts/7#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/acts/7#v37)</sup> This is that Moses, which said unto the children of Israel, A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear.
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/7#v38)</sup> This is he, that was in the church in the wilderness with the angel which spake to him in the mount Sina, and with our fathers: who received the lively oracles to give unto us:

    Moses told the children of Israel that God would raise a prophet from their brethren, whom they must hear. That prophet is Christ, though he is more than a regular prophet. Moses was in the church in the wilderness with the angel speaking on Sinai and the fathers who received the lively oracles to give to us.

**[Colossians 1:20](/bible/colossians/1#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/colossians/1#v20)</sup> And, having made peace through the blood of his cross, by him to reconcile all things unto himself; by him, I say, whether they be things in earth, or things in heaven.

- Peace is made through the blood of Christ's cross to reconcile all things to himself. Do not run past the word reconcile. It means restore to friendship or harmony: a relationship existed, was lost, and is brought back. It does not mean starting an entirely new relationship with people who never had one.
- The reconciliation is of the children of Israel. Under Ezekiel's order, sacrifices reconciled the house of Israel; now Christ's sacrifice does it. If someone claims this means every race, ask when the Jebusites, Philistines, Moabites or Ammonites were in that friendship with the Lord. There is no chapter establishing that former covenant with them. The prior relationship belongs to Israel.
- “Having made peace through the blood of his cross, by him to reconcile all things unto himself”: all twelve tribes.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 7:10-11](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v10)</sup> And unto the married I command, yet not I, but the Lord, Let not the wife depart from her husband:
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v11)</sup> But and if she depart, let her remain unmarried, or be reconciled to her husband: and let not the husband put away his wife.

    The command to the married is that the wife not depart from her husband; if she departs, she remains unmarried or is reconciled to him. Reconciled means restored to the friendship or harmony they had before the separation. It is not a new marriage to a stranger.
  - **[Ezekiel 45:17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/45#v17)</sup> And it shall be the prince’s part to give burnt offerings, and meat offerings, and drink offerings, in the feasts, and in the new moons, and in the sabbaths, in all solemnities of the house of Israel: he shall prepare the sin offering, and the meat offering, and the burnt offering, and the peace offerings, to make reconciliation for the house of Israel.

    The prince here is the priest. His part is to provide the burnt, meat and drink offerings in the feasts, new moons, sabbaths and solemnities of the house of Israel. The sin, meat, burnt and peace offerings make reconciliation for the house of Israel.
  - **[Colossians 1:25-27](/bible/colossians/1#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/colossians/1#v25)</sup> Whereof I am made a minister, according to the dispensation of God which is given to me for you, to fulfil the word of God;
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/colossians/1#v26)</sup> Even the mystery which hath been hid from ages and from generations, but now is made manifest to his saints:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/colossians/1#v27)</sup> To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles; which is Christ in you, the hope of glory:

    “Whereof I am made a minister, according to the dispensation of God which is given to me for you, to fulfil the word of God”. What word is he going to fulfil? “Even the mystery which hath been hid from ages and from generations, but now is made manifest to his saints”.

**[Colossians 1:21](/bible/colossians/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/colossians/1#v21)</sup> And you, that were sometime alienated and enemies in your mind by wicked works, yet now hath he reconciled

- “And you, that were sometime alienated and enemies in your mind by wicked works, yet now hath he reconciled”. He’s writing to the Colossians: you were once alienated, now you are reconciled. We read the same thing in Ephesians.
- This is why reading is so important. Our people out in the world ain’t reading this Bible to understand; you have to read between the lines. Who’s the you? Those people who were sometimes called uncircumcised, the Gentiles. To be alienated means at one point you had to have been together. Reconciled means he brought them back. If you are reconciled, at one point in time you had to have been together, then went off, and then were brought back. This Bible from front to back is about us.

**[Colossians 1:24](/bible/colossians/1#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/colossians/1#v24)</sup> Who now rejoice in my sufferings for you, and fill up that which is behind of the afflictions of Christ in my flesh for his body’s sake, which is the church:

- Paul caught a lot of afflictions for getting this gospel out, filling up “that which is behind of the afflictions of Christ in my flesh for his body’s sake, which is the church.” Now he is going to explain what he is talking about.

**[Colossians 1:25-27](/bible/colossians/1#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/colossians/1#v25)</sup> Whereof I am made a minister, according to the dispensation of God which is given to me for you, to fulfil the word of God;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/colossians/1#v26)</sup> Even the mystery which hath been hid from ages and from generations, but now is made manifest to his saints:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/colossians/1#v27)</sup> To whom God would make known what is the riches of the glory of this mystery among the Gentiles; which is Christ in you, the hope of glory:

- Paul was made a minister by God's dispensation to fulfill the word and reveal the mystery hidden through ages and generations. It is now manifested to the saints, the Israelites. The Gentiles here are the northern kingdom, who must also know the riches of this mystery: Christ in you, the hope of glory.
- Christ is the Father's first creation, promised from Genesis, the king of kings and Lord of lords coming through Israel. He is not every race and colour, a Rainbow Coalition; he is of Judah and comes for the nation of Israel. We will rule under him as joint-heirs. The world rejects that mystery. Faith in Christ is necessary to receive the spiritual understanding of the scriptures.
- “According to the dispensation of God which is given to me for you”: for you, the Colossians.
- This is the mystery of Colossians 2:2: “the mystery which hath been hid from ages and from generations, but now is made manifest to his saints.” When you pull up key words like saints, it shuts Christians down, because the saints are the children of Israel.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    Who are his saints? “He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel.”
  - **[Psalms 50:4-5](/bible/psalms/50#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/50#v4)</sup> He shall call to the heavens from above, and to the earth, that he may judge his people.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

    “He shall call to the heavens from above”: that is the angels. “And to the earth”: that is the prophets. What is the message to the angels and the prophets? “That he may judge his people,” and “Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.”
  - **[Matthew 4:15](/bible/matthew/4#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/4#v15)</sup> The land of Zabulon, and the land of Nephthalim, by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles;

    “The land of Zabulon, and the land of Nephthalim, by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, Galilee of the Gentiles.” That is what they called Zebulun and Naphtali: Gentiles.

**[Colossians 1:28](/bible/colossians/1#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/colossians/1#v28)</sup> Whom we preach, warning every man, and teaching every man in all wisdom; that we may present every man perfect in Christ Jesus:

- “Whom we preach, warning every man”: every man of the children of Israel. “And teaching every man in all wisdom”: of the children of Israel. “That we may present every man perfect in Christ Jesus”: every man of the children of Israel.
- “Whom we preach, warning every man, and teaching every man in all wisdom; that we may present every man perfect in Christ Jesus”. That is the work: we warn every man and every woman so that they may be presented perfect in the sight of Christ. Brother, it is time to stop; it is time to build in. Every one of these classes is a warning to our people, because destruction is coming.
- That warning is what we do in the streets, in the classroom, on the radio show and on the TV show. We are in the business of warning people, for the destruction to come and for the fire that is coming to hit this place.
- A soul that has no purpose is going to be destroyed; it is going to be gone. So you must have a purpose in this truth. Ask yourself every morning why the Lord chose you out of thousands. He knows where you were, and you know where you are now, and how the Lord could have removed you. The Lord has everybody numbered: he knows the number that is going to be sealed, the 144, the leading men, and the women, men and children who come in behind them. This earth is huge, there are too many places to go and too much work to be done, and there is no time to argue with foolishness. Some brothers have never been off their block, never been on a plane or a boat.

  Precepts:
  - **[Malachi 4:1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)</sup> For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.

    This is how deep the warning is: “For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven”. That is why we warn every man and every woman in this truth, and why nobody here is playing with any of you. Back then it was water, but the Lord promised that this time it is going to be fire, and that fire is able to burn your soul. You are able to feel it while you are in it. So get out of that foolishness, the whole burden you create inside your head about why people don't like you. A lot of people didn't like Christ; did he let that stop his mission? A lot of people didn't like the prophets; did the prophets stop preaching?
  - **[Jeremiah 26:3-5](/bible/jeremiah/26#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/26#v3)</sup> If so be they will hearken, and turn every man from his evil way, that I may repent me of the evil, which I purpose to do unto them because of the evil of their doings.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/26#v4)</sup> And thou shalt say unto them, Thus saith the Lord; If ye will not hearken to me, to walk in my law, which I have set before you,
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/26#v5)</sup> To hearken to the words of my servants the prophets, whom I sent unto you, both rising up early, and sending them, but ye have not hearkened;

    Why we warn: “If so be they will hearken, and turn every man from his evil way, that I may repent me of the evil, which I purpose to do unto them because of the evil of their doings.” That is what God wants us to do: take heed and turn from your evil ways, so that he may repent himself of the evil he is about to hit us with because of the evil of our doings.
  - **[Isaiah 1:17-20](/bible/isaiah/1#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/1#v17)</sup> Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/1#v18)</sup> Come now, and let us reason together, saith the Lord: though your sins be as scarlet, they shall be as white as snow; though they be red like crimson, they shall be as wool.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/1#v19)</sup> If ye be willing and obedient, ye shall eat the good of the land:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/1#v20)</sup> But if ye refuse and rebel, ye shall be devoured with the sword: for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.

    “Learn to do well; seek judgment, relieve the oppressed, judge the fatherless, plead for the widow.” When you have evil thoughts, you have to fight them and learn to do well. Constantly examine yourself; as Paul said, prove yourself, see if you are of that faith. If you believe in brotherly love, if you believe this is a family, keep checking your spirit on that.

**[Colossians 1:29](/bible/colossians/1#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/colossians/1#v29)</sup> Whereunto I also labour, striving according to his working, which worketh in me mightily.

- “Whereunto I also labour.” That is what Paul was labouring for, and that is why he was afflicted for it.
