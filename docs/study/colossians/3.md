## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Colossians 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 5-14, 16-22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Colossians 3:1](/bible/colossians/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/colossians/3#v1)</sup> If ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ sitteth on the right hand of God.

- The thought continues from Colossians 2:23. “If ye then be risen with Christ”: if you are a new creature with Christ, “seek those things which are above”: seek heavenly things, godly things, “where Christ sitteth on the right hand of God.”
- “If ye then be risen with Christ, seek those things which are above, where Christ sitteth on the right hand of God.” If you be risen with Christ means if you decide to repent and now follow Christ: then this is what you do. If you don’t want to accept Christ, it is not for you. All Israel is not Israel, just as Romans 9:6 says.

  Precepts:

    Those who are risen with Christ come through the Christ seed. “Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made.” Unto Abraham and his seed, Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, from which came the 12 tribes of Israel. “He saith not, And to seeds, as of many”: he does not say to every nation, only that chosen lineage. “But as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.” Through that lineage came Jesus the Christ.

Really? After reading all of that in context? No.
- Greek: the Encyclopedia of the Jewish Diaspora says it is common knowledge that the Jews of the western diaspora knew only Greek. They knew neither Hebrew nor Aramaic, and their prayer and the reading of the Torah were in Greek. The sages recognized there were whole diaspora communities that spoke only Greek, and the places named in Acts 2, in the journeys of Paul and in the Pauline epistles give the picture of the western Jewish Greek-speaking diaspora. So some of our people were called Greeks and some were called Jews, because of a language difference, but we are still the same people.
- Circumcision is the Jews, the southern kingdom; uncircumcision is the northern kingdom, the Gentiles.
- Barbarian is from the Greek barbaros, used among the early Greeks to describe all foreigners, including the Romans. If you were not a Greek, they called you a barbarian, and we were scattered throughout the four corners of the earth.
