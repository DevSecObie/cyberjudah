## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Colossians 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 5, 7, 9-10, 16-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Colossians 4:1](/bible/colossians/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/colossians/4#v1)</sup> Masters, give unto your servants that which is just and equal; knowing that ye also have a Master in heaven.

- “Masters, give unto your servants that which is just and equal.” They said they were the masters. Did they do that? No. They lived in the big house and we lived in the outhouse, and when we got emancipated they never gave us the 40 acres and a mule. They are showing that they are hypocrites and impostors, the damn devil that the Bible speaks of.
- “Knowing that ye also have a Master in heaven.” Colossians, Ephesians and Philemon were all written at about the same time, and they say the same thing about servants and masters.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 6:5-6](/bible/ephesians/6#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/6#v5)</sup> Servants, be obedient to them that are your masters according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as unto Christ;
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ephesians/6#v6)</sup> Not with eyeservice, as menpleasers; but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart;

    “Servants, be obedient to them that are your masters according to the flesh, with fear and trembling, in singleness of your heart, as unto Christ; Not with eyeservice, as menpleasers; but as the servants of Christ, doing the will of God from the heart.” The same teaching as Colossians, written in the same time to the same region: doing the will of God from the heart.
  - **[Jeremiah 34:9](/bible/jeremiah/34#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/34#v9)</sup> That every man should let his manservant, and every man his maidservant, being an Hebrew or an Hebrewess, go free; that none should serve himself of them, to wit, of a Jew his brother.

    More on these servants: “That every man should let his manservant, and every man his maidservant, being an Hebrew or an Hebrewess, go free; that none should serve himself of them, to wit, of a Jew his brother.” These servants are Israelites, a Jew his brother.
  - **[1 Timothy 6:1-2](/bible/1-timothy/6#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/6#v1)</sup> Let as many servants as are under the yoke count their own masters worthy of all honour, that the name of God and his doctrine be not blasphemed.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/6#v2)</sup> And they that have believing masters, let them not despise them, because they are brethren; but rather do them service, because they are faithful and beloved, partakers of the benefit. These things teach and exhort.

    “Let as many servants as are under the yoke count their own masters worthy of all honour, that the name of God and his doctrine be not blasphemed.” Under the yoke of bondage. If you have a master who still believes in the law of sacrifice, but you believe in Christ, you still honor him and treat him with respect.

**[Colossians 4:5](/bible/colossians/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/4#v5)</sup> Walk in wisdom toward them that are without, redeeming the time.

- “Walk in wisdom toward them that are without”: toward the ones who are not here. You must be an example not only here but outside. The people at your job know you are an Israelite; you have your fringes on and you talk about the Bible. Then your wicked coworker invites you to the bar down the street after work, and you go, and you come back telling the Deacon there were hoes in there. What did you expect to be in a bar?
- Because you are stupid. You are simple. Some have been coming for years and are still simple, talking about sitting at the bar when they should have been preparing for the new moon. Some are too old for this: you have a wife and kids at home, and you are sitting in a bar destroying your family. When are you going to change?

**[Colossians 4:7](/bible/colossians/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/colossians/4#v7)</sup> All my state shall Tychicus declare unto you, who is a beloved brother, and a faithful minister and fellowservant in the Lord:

- “All my state shall Tychicus declare unto you, who is a beloved brother, and a faithful minister and fellowservant in the Lord.” Tychicus is one of the two who carried the letter to Colosse.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Timothy 4:12](/bible/2-timothy/4#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-timothy/4#v12)</sup> And Tychicus have I sent to Ephesus.

    Ephesians, Colossians, Philemon, Philippians, 1 Timothy, Titus and 2 Timothy were all written in the same period. “And Tychicus have I sent to Ephesus.” Ephesus is right there next to Colosse. The same Tychicus Paul sent to Ephesus he sent unto the Colossians: “whom I have sent unto you for the same purpose, that he might know your estate, and comfort your hearts; With Onesimus, a faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you.” Onesimus is one of them, whom we read about in Philemon.

**[Colossians 4:9](/bible/colossians/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/colossians/4#v9)</sup> With Onesimus, a faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They shall make known unto you all things which are done here.

- “With Onesimus, a faithful and beloved brother, who is one of you. They shall make known unto you all things which are done here.” Onesimus, the other one who carried the letter, is one of them, a Colossian.

  Precepts:
  - **[Philemon 1:10](/bible/philemon/1#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/philemon/1#v10)</sup> I beseech thee for my son Onesimus, whom I have begotten in my bonds:

    “I beseech thee for my son Onesimus, whom I have begotten in my bonds.” Why would Paul beseech Philemon on behalf of Onesimus? Because they had to be in the same place, Colosse. Onesimus is one of you, and so is Philemon. That proves who the Colossians are: these are Israelites.

**[Colossians 4:10](/bible/colossians/4#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/colossians/4#v10)</sup> Aristarchus my fellowprisoner saluteth you, and Marcus, sister’s son to Barnabas, (touching whom ye received commandments: if he come unto you, receive him;)

- “Aristarchus my fellowprisoner saluteth you, and Marcus, sister’s son to Barnabas.” If Marcus is Barnabas’ sister’s son, that makes Marcus his nephew. That is John Mark, and he is the one Paul and Barnabas had their dispute over at the end of Acts 15.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 15:37-41](/bible/acts/15#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/acts/15#v37)</sup> And Barnabas determined to take with them John, whose surname was Mark.
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/15#v38)</sup> But Paul thought not good to take him with them, who departed from them from Pamphylia, and went not with them to the work.
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/15#v39)</sup> And the contention was so sharp between them, that they departed asunder one from the other: and so Barnabas took Mark, and sailed unto Cyprus;
    >
    > <sup>[40](/bible/acts/15#v40)</sup> And Paul chose Silas, and departed, being recommended by the brethren unto the grace of God.
    >
    > <sup>[41](/bible/acts/15#v41)</sup> And he went through Syria and Cilicia, confirming the churches.

    “And Barnabas determined to take with them John, whose surname was Mark. But Paul thought not good to take him with them, who departed from them from Pamphylia, and went not with them to the work.” Not just anybody could travel. Paul gave him a chance, and when it came to the work he was nowhere to be found; he came up missing.

**[Colossians 4:16](/bible/colossians/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/colossians/4#v16)</sup> And when this epistle is read among you, cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans; and that ye likewise read the epistle from Laodicea.

- “And when this epistle is read among you, cause that it be read also in the church of the Laodiceans; and that ye likewise read the epistle from Laodicea.” Laodicea was right there next to Colosse. It may not be mentioned in the book of Acts, but put the map up with the churches of Asia, Philadelphia, Sardis, all of them right there by Colosse, and it connects: you get a better understanding of Asia.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 1:11](/bible/revelation/1#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/1#v11)</sup> Saying, I am Alpha and Omega, the first and the last: and, What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches which are in Asia; unto Ephesus, and unto Smyrna, and unto Pergamos, and unto Thyatira, and unto Sardis, and unto Philadelphia, and unto Laodicea.

    “What thou seest, write in a book, and send it unto the seven churches which are in Asia.” The seven churches in Asia are Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamos, Thyatira, Sardis, Philadelphia and Laodicea, and Laodicea was right there next to Colosse.

**[Colossians 4:17](/bible/colossians/4#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/colossians/4#v17)</sup> And say to Archippus, Take heed to the ministry which thou hast received in the Lord, that thou fulfil it.

- “Take heed to the ministry which thou hast received in the Lord”: take heed to your ministry. Your ministry is your calling, your purpose, and we all have one. “that thou fulfil it”: we all have to make sure we fulfil our calling, our ministry. Eventually every last one of us will figure it out and see why God called us.

**[Colossians 4:18](/bible/colossians/4#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/4#v18)</sup> The salutation by the hand of me Paul. Remember my bonds. Grace be with you. Amen. Written from Rome to the Colossians by Tychicus and Onesimus.

- “The salutation by the hand of me Paul. Remember my bonds. Grace be with you. Amen. Written from Rome to the Colossians by Tychicus and Onesimus.” Not every Bible has that last line, so it is put on the screen for everyone. The epistle was written from Rome, where Paul was in bonds, to the church at Colosse, and carried by Tychicus and Onesimus.
