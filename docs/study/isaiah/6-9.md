Read the chapter → [Isaiah 6](/bible/isaiah/6)

This session covers [Isaiah 6](/bible/isaiah/6) through 9. Chapter six gives Isaiah's vision of the Most High high and lifted up, his cleansing, and his commission to a people whose hearts are made fat so that they hear but understand not. Chapters seven and eight turn to the days of Ahaz, the confederacy of Syria and Ephraim against Judah, and the signs given through Isaiah's own children, who are set for signs and wonders in Israel. Chapter nine carries the prophecy of the great light, the child born whose government has no end, and the burning judgment that comes because the people turn not to him that smites them. Much of the deeper background was opened in Captain OC's class, The Purpose of Christ Told by the Israelites, and Deacon Nadan's Sabbath class, History of the King Part One.

**[Isaiah 6:1](/bible/isaiah/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/6#v1)</sup> In the year that king Uzziah died I saw also the Lord sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up, and his train filled the temple.

- In the year that King Uzziah died, Isaiah saw the Most High sitting upon a throne, high and lifted up, and his train filled the temple. The train is his cape, so long that it flowed out behind him and filled the whole temple.

**[Isaiah 6:2-4](/bible/isaiah/6#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/6#v2)</sup> Above it stood the seraphims: each one had six wings; with twain he covered his face, and with twain he covered his feet, and with twain he did fly.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/6#v3)</sup> And one cried unto another, and said, Holy, holy, holy, is the Lord of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/6#v4)</sup> And the posts of the door moved at the voice of him that cried, and the house was filled with smoke.

- Above the throne stood the seraphims, each one having six wings. With two he covered his face, with two he covered his feet, and with two he did fly; twain means two.
- One cried to another, "Holy, holy, holy, is the LORD of hosts: the whole earth is full of his glory." The posts of the door moved at the voice of him that cried, and the house was filled with smoke.

**[Isaiah 6:5](/bible/isaiah/6#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/6#v5)</sup> Then said I, Woe is me! for I am undone; because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for mine eyes have seen the King, the Lord of hosts.

- Isaiah says, "Woe is me! for I am undone; because I am a man of unclean lips, and I dwell in the midst of a people of unclean lips: for mine eyes have seen the King, the LORD of hosts."
- When men see the Most High or the day of the Most High, terror comes upon them and they cry, "Woe is me." Another prophet said he hoped he would not be living when that time came.

**[Isaiah 6:6-7](/bible/isaiah/6#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/6#v6)</sup> Then flew one of the seraphims unto me, having a live coal in his hand, which he had taken with the tongs from off the altar:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/6#v7)</sup> And he laid it upon my mouth, and said, Lo, this hath touched thy lips; and thine iniquity is taken away, and thy sin purged.

- Then flew one of the seraphims with a live coal in his hand, taken with the tongs from off the altar, and laid it upon Isaiah's mouth, saying his iniquity is taken away and his sin purged.

**[Isaiah 6:8-9](/bible/isaiah/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/6#v8)</sup> Also I heard the voice of the Lord, saying, Whom shall I send, and who will go for us? Then said I, Here am I; send me.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/6#v9)</sup> And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.

- Isaiah heard the voice of the Most High saying, "Whom shall I send, and who will go for us?" and answered, "Here am I; send me."
- He was sent to tell this people, "Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not."

**[Isaiah 6:10](/bible/isaiah/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/6#v10)</sup> Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes; lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed.

- The Most High says to make the heart of this people fat, their ears heavy, and shut their eyes, lest they see, hear, understand with their heart, convert, and be healed.
- Christ spoke in parables because it is not meant for everybody to get the understanding of the bible. Captain OC's class, The Purpose of Christ Told by the Israelites, opens this in detail.
  - **[Matthew 13:13-14](/bible/matthew/13#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/13#v13)</sup> Therefore speak I to them in parables: because they seeing see not; and hearing they hear not, neither do they understand.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/13#v14)</sup> And in them is fulfilled the prophecy of Esaias, which saith, By hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and shall not perceive:

    Christ spoke in parables so that seeing they see not and hearing they hear not, fulfilling the prophecy of Isaiah.

**[Isaiah 6:11-12](/bible/isaiah/6#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/6#v11)</sup> Then said I, Lord, how long? And he answered, Until the cities be wasted without inhabitant, and the houses without man, and the land be utterly desolate,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/6#v12)</sup> And the Lord have removed men far away, and there be a great forsaking in the midst of the land.

- Isaiah asks, "Lord, how long?" and is answered, "Until the cities be wasted without inhabitant, and the houses without man, and the land be utterly desolate."
- The Most High will remove men far away, and there will be a great forsaking in the midst of the land.

**[Isaiah 6:13](/bible/isaiah/6#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/6#v13)</sup> But yet in it shall be a tenth, and it shall return, and shall be eaten: as a teil tree, and as an oak, whose substance is in them, when they cast their leaves: so the holy seed shall be the substance thereof.

- Yet a tenth shall return and be eaten, as a teil tree and as an oak whose substance is in them when they cast their leaves; the holy seed shall be the substance thereof.

# Isaiah 7: Ahaz, the Confederacy, and the Sign of Immanuel

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 7](/bible/isaiah/7)

**[Isaiah 7:1-2](/bible/isaiah/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/7#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in the days of Ahaz the son of Jotham, the son of Uzziah, king of Judah, that Rezin the king of Syria, and Pekah the son of Remaliah, king of Israel, went up toward Jerusalem to war against it, but could not prevail against it.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/7#v2)</sup> And it was told the house of David, saying, Syria is confederate with Ephraim. And his heart was moved, and the heart of his people, as the trees of the wood are moved with the wind.

- In the days of Ahaz, son of Jotham, son of Uzziah, king of Judah, Rezin king of Syria and Pekah son of Remaliah king of Israel went up toward Jerusalem to war, but could not prevail against it.
- It was told the house of David that Syria was confederate with Ephraim, and the heart of Ahaz and his people was moved as the trees of the wood are moved with the wind.

**[Isaiah 7:3-4](/bible/isaiah/7#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/7#v3)</sup> Then said the Lord unto Isaiah, Go forth now to meet Ahaz, thou, and Shear–jashub thy son, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool in the highway of the fuller’s field;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/7#v4)</sup> And say unto him, Take heed, and be quiet; fear not, neither be fainthearted for the two tails of these smoking firebrands, for the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria, and of the son of Remaliah.

- The Most High told Isaiah to go meet Ahaz, he and his son Shearjashub, at the end of the conduit of the upper pool, in the highway of the fuller's field.
- Isaiah was to say, "Take heed, and be quiet; fear not, neither be fainthearted," for the two tails of these smoking firebrands, the fierce anger of Rezin with Syria and of Remaliah's son.

**[Isaiah 7:5-7](/bible/isaiah/7#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/7#v5)</sup> Because Syria, Ephraim, and the son of Remaliah, have taken evil counsel against thee, saying,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/7#v6)</sup> Let us go up against Judah, and vex it, and let us make a breach therein for us, and set a king in the midst of it, even the son of Tabeal:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/7#v7)</sup> Thus saith the Lord God, It shall not stand, neither shall it come to pass.

- Syria, Ephraim, and Remaliah's son took evil counsel, saying, "Let us go up against Judah, and vex it, and set a king in the midst of it, even the son of Tabeal."
- The Most High says it shall not stand nor come to pass, for the head of Syria is Damascus and the head of Damascus is Rezin, meaning the capital.
- Deacon Nadan taught that the sign here is Isaiah's children, and Captain OC taught on the Samaritan woman being from Ephraim.

**[Isaiah 7:8-9](/bible/isaiah/7#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/7#v8)</sup> For the head of Syria is Damascus, and the head of Damascus is Rezin; and within threescore and five years shall Ephraim be broken, that it be not a people.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/7#v9)</sup> And the head of Ephraim is Samaria, and the head of Samaria is Remaliah’s son. If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established.

- Within threescore and five years Ephraim shall be broken, that it be not a people; the head of Ephraim is Samaria and the head of Samaria is Remaliah's son.
- "If ye will not believe, surely ye shall not be established."

**[Isaiah 7:10-12](/bible/isaiah/7#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/7#v10)</sup> Moreover the Lord spake again unto Ahaz, saying,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/7#v11)</sup> Ask thee a sign of the Lord thy God; ask it either in the depth, or in the height above.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/7#v12)</sup> But Ahaz said, I will not ask, neither will I tempt the Lord.

- The Most High spoke again to Ahaz, telling him to ask a sign, whether in the depth or in the height above.
- Ahaz said, "I will not ask, neither will I tempt the LORD." Isaiah answered, "Hear ye now, O house of David; Is it a small thing for you to weary men, but will ye weary my God also?"

**[Isaiah 7:13-14](/bible/isaiah/7#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/7#v13)</sup> And he said, Hear ye now, O house of David; Is it a small thing for you to weary men, but will ye weary my God also?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/7#v14)</sup> Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel.

- "Therefore the Lord himself shall give you a sign; Behold, a virgin shall conceive, and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel."
- The sign is the child, not the virgin herself. Many think the virgin was the sign, and that leads into all types of false doctrine; the virgin is simply a woman of age to be married. Captain OC opened how christianity stumbles over the word seed.
  - **[Isaiah 8:18](/bible/isaiah/8#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/8#v18)</sup> Behold, I and the children whom the Lord hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the Lord of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion.

    Isaiah and the children the Most High gave him are set for signs and wonders in Israel, dwelling in mount Zion.

**[Isaiah 7:15-16](/bible/isaiah/7#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/7#v15)</sup> Butter and honey shall he eat, that he may know to refuse the evil, and choose the good.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/7#v16)</sup> For before the child shall know to refuse the evil, and choose the good, the land that thou abhorrest shall be forsaken of both her kings.

- "Butter and honey shall he eat, that he may know to refuse the evil, and choose the good."
- Before the child shall know to refuse evil and choose good, the land that Ahaz abhorred shall be forsaken of both her kings.

**[Isaiah 7:17](/bible/isaiah/7#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/7#v17)</sup> The Lord shall bring upon thee, and upon thy people, and upon thy father’s house, days that have not come, from the day that Ephraim departed from Judah; even the king of Assyria.

- The Most High shall bring upon Ahaz, his people, and his father's house days that have not come since Ephraim departed from Judah, even the king of Assyria.

**[Isaiah 7:18-20](/bible/isaiah/7#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/7#v18)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall hiss for the fly that is in the uttermost part of the rivers of Egypt, and for the bee that is in the land of Assyria.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/7#v19)</sup> And they shall come, and shall rest all of them in the desolate valleys, and in the holes of the rocks, and upon all thorns, and upon all bushes.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/7#v20)</sup> In the same day shall the Lord shave with a razor that is hired, namely, by them beyond the river, by the king of Assyria, the head, and the hair of the feet: and it shall also consume the beard.

- In that day the Most High shall hiss for the fly in the rivers of Egypt and the bee in the land of Assyria, and they shall come and rest in the desolate valleys, in the holes of the rocks, upon all thorns and bushes.
- In the same day the Most High shall shave with a hired razor, the king of Assyria, the head and the hair of the feet, and consume the beard.

**[Isaiah 7:21-22](/bible/isaiah/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/7#v21)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that a man shall nourish a young cow, and two sheep;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/7#v22)</sup> And it shall come to pass, for the abundance of milk that they shall give he shall eat butter: for butter and honey shall every one eat that is left in the land.

- In that day a man shall nourish a young cow and two sheep, and for the abundance of milk they give he shall eat butter; butter and honey shall everyone eat that is left in the land.

**[Isaiah 7:23-25](/bible/isaiah/7#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/7#v23)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that every place shall be, where there were a thousand vines at a thousand silverlings, it shall even be for briers and thorns.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/7#v24)</sup> With arrows and with bows shall men come thither; because all the land shall become briers and thorns.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/7#v25)</sup> And on all hills that shall be digged with the mattock, there shall not come thither the fear of briers and thorns: but it shall be for the sending forth of oxen, and for the treading of lesser cattle.

- The hills digged with the mattock shall not be entered for fear of briers and thorns, but shall be for the sending forth of oxen and the treading of lesser cattle.

# Isaiah 8: Maher-shalal-hash-baz, the Snare, and the Law and the Testimony

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 8](/bible/isaiah/8)

**[Isaiah 8:1](/bible/isaiah/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/8#v1)</sup> Moreover the Lord said unto me, Take thee a great roll, and write in it with a man’s pen concerning Maher–shalal–hash–baz.

- The Most High told Isaiah to take a great roll and write in it with a man's pen concerning Maher-shalal-hash-baz, meaning quick to the plunder, swift to the spoil.

**[Isaiah 8:2-4](/bible/isaiah/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/8#v2)</sup> And I took unto me faithful witnesses to record, Uriah the priest, and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/8#v3)</sup> And I went unto the prophetess; and she conceived, and bare a son. Then said the Lord to me, Call his name Maher–shalal–hash–baz.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/8#v4)</sup> For before the child shall have knowledge to cry, My father, and my mother, the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.

- Isaiah took faithful witnesses to record, Uriah the priest and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah, then went to the prophetess, who conceived and bare a son.
- The Most High said to call his name Maher-shalal-hash-baz, for before the child shall have knowledge to cry, "My father, and my mother," the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.
- This ties to [Isaiah 7:16](/bible/isaiah/7#v16), "before the child shall know to refuse evil and choose the good"; both point to the fall of the lands of Rezin and Pekah.

**[Isaiah 8:5-8](/bible/isaiah/8#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/8#v5)</sup> The Lord spake also unto me again, saying,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/8#v6)</sup> Forasmuch as this people refuseth the waters of Shiloah that go softly, and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah’s son;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/8#v7)</sup> Now therefore, behold, the Lord bringeth up upon them the waters of the river, strong and many, even the king of Assyria, and all his glory: and he shall come up over all his channels, and go over all his banks:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/8#v8)</sup> And he shall pass through Judah; he shall overflow and go over, he shall reach even to the neck; and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel.

- Because the people refuse the waters of Shiloah that go softly and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah's son, the Most High brings upon them the waters of the river strong and many, the king of Assyria and all his glory, rising over all his channels and banks.
- He shall pass through Judah and overflow, reaching even to the neck, and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of the land, O Immanuel, which means God with us.

**[Isaiah 8:9-10](/bible/isaiah/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/8#v9)</sup> Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces; and give ear, all ye of far countries: gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces; gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/8#v10)</sup> Take counsel together, and it shall come to nought; speak the word, and it shall not stand: for God is with us.

- "Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces." Though the far countries gird themselves, they shall be broken; take counsel together and it shall come to nought, speak the word and it shall not stand, for God is with us.
- If the counsel is from man it will fall, but if it is from the Most High it cannot be overthrown, and men would only end up fighting against God.
  - **[Acts 5:38-39](/bible/acts/5#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/5#v38)</sup> And now I say unto you, Refrain from these men, and let them alone: for if this counsel or this work be of men, it will come to nought:
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/5#v39)</sup> But if it be of God, ye cannot overthrow it; lest haply ye be found even to fight against God.

    If this counsel be of men it will come to nought, but if it be of God it cannot be overthrown, lest men be found fighting against God.

**[Isaiah 8:11-13](/bible/isaiah/8#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/8#v11)</sup> For the Lord spake thus to me with a strong hand, and instructed me that I should not walk in the way of this people, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/8#v12)</sup> Say ye not, A confederacy, to all them to whom this people shall say, A confederacy; neither fear ye their fear, nor be afraid.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/8#v13)</sup> Sanctify the Lord of hosts himself; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread.

- The Most High spoke with a strong hand, instructing Isaiah not to walk in the way of this people, and not to say "a confederacy" to all them to whom this people says a confederacy, nor to fear their fear.
- "Sanctify the LORD of hosts himself; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread."

**[Isaiah 8:14](/bible/isaiah/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/8#v14)</sup> And he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

- He shall be for a sanctuary, but also a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, and a gin and snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
- He is a sanctuary for his people, a little sanctuary in the places where they are scattered, but a stone of stumbling to those who reject him.
- The word became a snare upon the leadership because the understanding is given precept upon precept, in another tongue, so that those without it stumble and fall.
  - **[Ezekiel 11:16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)</sup> Therefore say, Thus saith the Lord God; Although I have cast them far off among the heathen, and although I have scattered them among the countries, yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they shall come.

    The Most High is a little sanctuary to his people in the countries where they are scattered.
  - **[Isaiah 28:10](/bible/isaiah/28#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/28#v10)</sup> For precept must be upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little:

    Precept must be upon precept, line upon line, for the understanding comes with stammering lips and another tongue.
  - **[Isaiah 28:13](/bible/isaiah/28#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/28#v13)</sup> But the word of the Lord was unto them precept upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little; that they might go, and fall backward, and be broken, and snared, and taken.

    The word of the Most High was made precept upon precept so that they go and fall backward, and be broken, snared, and taken.
  - **[Romans 11:9](/bible/romans/11#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/romans/11#v9)</sup> And David saith, Let their table be made a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them:

    When Christ came teaching, the word was a snare and a trap to the leadership of the southern kingdom.
  - **[Psalm 69:22](/bible/psalms/69#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/69#v22)</sup> Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap.

    David prophesied that their table would become a snare to the enemies.

**[Isaiah 8:15](/bible/isaiah/8#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/8#v15)</sup> And many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken.

- Many among them shall stumble, fall, be broken, snared, and taken.

**[Isaiah 8:16](/bible/isaiah/8#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/8#v16)</sup> Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples.

- "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." Knowing the law is knowing what the mark truly is, instead of thinking of a microchip and being snared and taken by every wind, like the trees moved with the wind.

**[Isaiah 8:17-18](/bible/isaiah/8#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/8#v17)</sup> And I will wait upon the Lord, that hideth his face from the house of Jacob, and I will look for him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/8#v18)</sup> Behold, I and the children whom the Lord hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the Lord of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion.

- "I will wait upon the LORD, that hideth his face from the house of Jacob, and I will look for him."
- Behold, Isaiah and the children the Most High gave him are for signs and wonders in Israel, from the Lord of hosts which dwelleth in mount Zion.

**[Isaiah 8:19-20](/bible/isaiah/8#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/8#v19)</sup> And when they shall say unto you, Seek unto them that have familiar spirits, and unto wizards that peep, and that mutter: should not a people seek unto their God? for the living to the dead?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

- When they say to seek unto them that have familiar spirits, and wizards that peep and mutter, the answer is: should not a people seek unto their God? For the living to the dead?
- "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them." Christians say Jesus is the word and go to [John 17:17](/bible/john/17#v17), "thy word is truth," but the word spoken of is the law; no law means no light, which is why there is no light in christianity.
  - **[John 17:17](/bible/john/17#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/john/17#v17)</sup> Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.

    Christians cite this to call Christ the word, but the word that sanctifies is the truth, which is the law.

**[Isaiah 8:21-22](/bible/isaiah/8#v21)**

# Isaiah 9: For Unto Us a Child Is Born

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 9](/bible/isaiah/9)

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/9#v1)</sup> Nevertheless the dimness shall not be such as was in her vexation, when at the first he lightly afflicted the land of Zebulun and the land of Naphtali, and afterward did more grievously afflict her by the way of the sea, beyond Jordan, in Galilee of the nations.

**[Isaiah 9:5](/bible/isaiah/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/9#v5)</sup> For every battle of the warrior is with confused noise, and garments rolled in blood; but this shall be with burning and fuel of fire.

- "For every battle of the warrior is with confused noise, and garments rolled in blood; but this shall be with burning and fuel of fire."
- The confused noise is swords clashing, guns firing, and people hollering, with garments rolled in blood; but this final battle is with burning and fuel of fire, a prophecy of the nuclear bomb.
