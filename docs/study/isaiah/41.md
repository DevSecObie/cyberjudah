**[Isaiah 41:1-7](/bible/isaiah/41#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/41#v1)</sup> Keep silence before me, O islands; and let the people renew their strength: let them come near; then let them speak: let us come near together to judgment.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/41#v2)</sup> Who raised up the righteous man from the east, called him to his foot, gave the nations before him, and made him rule over kings? he gave them as the dust to his sword, and as driven stubble to his bow.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/41#v3)</sup> He pursued them, and passed safely; even by the way that he had not gone with his feet.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/41#v4)</sup> Who hath wrought and done it, calling the generations from the beginning? I the Lord, the first, and with the last; I am he.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/41#v5)</sup> The isles saw it, and feared; the ends of the earth were afraid, drew near, and came.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/41#v6)</sup> They helped every one his neighbour; and every one said to his brother, Be of good courage.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/41#v7)</sup> So the carpenter encouraged the goldsmith, and he that smootheth with the hammer him that smote the anvil, saying, It is ready for the sodering: and he fastened it with nails, that it should not be moved.

- Keep silence before me, O islands, and let the people renew their strength and come near to judgment. Who raised up the righteous man from the east, called him to his foot, gave nations before him and made him rule over kings, giving them as dust to his sword and driven stubble to his bow.
- The Lord who wrought and did it, calling the generations from the beginning, is the first and with the last: I am he. This is talking about Christ, the first and the last, whom the isles saw and feared as the ends of the earth drew near.
- The carpenter encouraged the goldsmith, and he that smooths with the hammer encouraged him that smote the anvil, saying it is ready for the soldering, and he fastened the idol with nails so it should not be moved.

**[Isaiah 41:8-13](/bible/isaiah/41#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/41#v8)</sup> But thou, Israel, art my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/41#v9)</sup> Thou whom I have taken from the ends of the earth, and called thee from the chief men thereof, and said unto thee, Thou art my servant; I have chosen thee, and not cast thee away.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/41#v10)</sup> Fear thou not; for I am with thee: be not dismayed; for I am thy God: I will strengthen thee; yea, I will help thee; yea, I will uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/41#v11)</sup> Behold, all they that were incensed against thee shall be ashamed and confounded: they shall be as nothing; and they that strive with thee shall perish.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/41#v12)</sup> Thou shalt seek them, and shalt not find them, even them that contended with thee: they that war against thee shall be as nothing, and as a thing of nought.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/41#v13)</sup> For I the Lord thy God will hold thy right hand, saying unto thee, Fear not; I will help thee.

- But thou, Israel, art my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend. When the New Testament speaks of the chosen and the servants, it is talking about Israel; the servants of God are Israelites and the chosen of God are Israelites, all twelve tribes, not everybody.
- The Most High took Israel from the ends of the earth, called him from the chief men, and said, Thou art my servant, I have chosen thee and not cast thee away.
- Fear thou not, for I am with thee; I will strengthen thee and uphold thee with the right hand of my righteousness. All that were incensed against Israel shall be ashamed and confounded, be as nothing, and perish; those that war against Israel shall be as a thing of naught.
  - **[Romans 11:1](/bible/romans/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/11#v1)</sup> I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.

    The precept that God has not cast away his people whom he foreknew, the seed of Abraham.

**[Isaiah 41:14-20](/bible/isaiah/41#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/41#v14)</sup> Fear not, thou worm Jacob, and ye men of Israel; I will help thee, saith the Lord, and thy redeemer, the Holy One of Israel.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/41#v15)</sup> Behold, I will make thee a new sharp threshing instrument having teeth: thou shalt thresh the mountains, and beat them small, and shalt make the hills as chaff.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/41#v16)</sup> Thou shalt fan them, and the wind shall carry them away, and the whirlwind shall scatter them: and thou shalt rejoice in the Lord, and shalt glory in the Holy One of Israel.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/41#v17)</sup> When the poor and needy seek water, and there is none, and their tongue faileth for thirst, I the Lord will hear them, I the God of Israel will not forsake them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/41#v18)</sup> I will open rivers in high places, and fountains in the midst of the valleys: I will make the wilderness a pool of water, and the dry land springs of water.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/41#v19)</sup> I will plant in the wilderness the cedar, the shittah tree, and the myrtle, and the oil tree; I will set in the desert the fir tree, and the pine, and the box tree together:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/41#v20)</sup> That they may see, and know, and consider, and understand together, that the hand of the Lord hath done this, and the Holy One of Israel hath created it.

- Fear not, thou worm Jacob, and ye men of Israel; I will help thee, saith the Lord thy Redeemer, the Holy One of Israel. He will make Israel a new sharp threshing instrument with teeth to thresh the mountains, beat them small, and make the hills as chaff to be scattered by the whirlwind, that Israel may rejoice and glory in the Holy One of Israel.
- When the poor and needy seek water and their tongue fails for thirst, the Lord will hear them and the God of Israel will not forsake them; he will open rivers in high places and make the wilderness a pool of water.
- He will plant in the wilderness the cedar, the shittah tree, the myrtle, and the oil tree, and set in the desert the fir, the pine, and the box tree together, that all may see and know that the hand of the Lord has done it and the Holy One of Israel created it.

**[Isaiah 41:21-24](/bible/isaiah/41#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/41#v21)</sup> Produce your cause, saith the Lord; bring forth your strong reasons, saith the King of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/41#v22)</sup> Let them bring them forth, and shew us what shall happen: let them shew the former things, what they be, that we may consider them, and know the latter end of them; or declare us things for to come.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/41#v23)</sup> Shew the things that are to come hereafter, that we may know that ye are gods: yea, do good, or do evil, that we may be dismayed, and behold it together.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/41#v24)</sup> Behold, ye are of nothing, and your work of nought: an abomination is he that chooseth you.

- Produce your cause, saith the Lord; bring forth your strong reasons, saith the King of Jacob. Let the idols show the former things and declare the things to come, that men may know whether they are gods, and do good or evil that all may be dismayed together.
- The idols are of nothing and their work of naught; an abomination is he that chooses them. This goes to the false gods: nowhere in Christianity or any other religion on earth can they tell us why we are in the position we are in, what happened in the past, what is going on now, and what is to come. That is how you know this book is the most powerful book on the face of the earth.

**[Isaiah 41:25-29](/bible/isaiah/41#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/41#v25)</sup> I have raised up one from the north, and he shall come: from the rising of the sun shall he call upon my name: and he shall come upon princes as upon morter, and as the potter treadeth clay.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/isaiah/41#v26)</sup> Who hath declared from the beginning, that we may know? and beforetime, that we may say, He is righteous? yea, there is none that sheweth, yea, there is none that declareth, yea, there is none that heareth your words.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/isaiah/41#v27)</sup> The first shall say to Zion, Behold, behold them: and I will give to Jerusalem one that bringeth good tidings.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/isaiah/41#v28)</sup> For I beheld, and there was no man; even among them, and there was no counsellor, that, when I asked of them, could answer a word.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/isaiah/41#v29)</sup> Behold, they are all vanity; their works are nothing: their molten images are wind and confusion.

- The Most High raised one up from the earth who comes with the rising of the sun and calls upon his name, coming upon the princes as upon mortar and as the potter treads clay. None of the idols declared it from the beginning or foretold it so men could call them righteous.
- The first shall say to Zion, Behold them, and give to Jerusalem one that brings good tidings, meaning the gospel, the good news. Among the idols there was no man, no counselor that could answer a word; they are all vanity, their works nothing, their molten images wind and confusion.
- This is a heavy chapter on idolatry and what is to come for Israel. Israel is the chosen, the seed of the Lord, the servants of God, with the kingdom coming to them, while destruction is coming on the idols and the nations that stand against the prophets.
