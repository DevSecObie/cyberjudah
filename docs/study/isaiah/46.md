**[Isaiah 46:1-7](/bible/isaiah/46#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/46#v1)</sup> Bel boweth down, Nebo stoopeth, their idols were upon the beasts, and upon the cattle: your carriages were heavy loaden; they are a burden to the weary beast.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/46#v2)</sup> They stoop, they bow down together; they could not deliver the burden, but themselves are gone into captivity.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/46#v3)</sup> Hearken unto me, O house of Jacob, and all the remnant of the house of Israel, which are borne by me from the belly, which are carried from the womb:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/46#v4)</sup> And even to your old age I am he; and even to hoar hairs will I carry you: I have made, and I will bear; even I will carry, and will deliver you.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/46#v5)</sup> To whom will ye liken me, and make me equal, and compare me, that we may be like?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/46#v6)</sup> They lavish gold out of the bag, and weigh silver in the balance, and hire a goldsmith; and he maketh it a god: they fall down, yea, they worship.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/46#v7)</sup> They bear him upon the shoulder, they carry him, and set him in his place, and he standeth; from his place shall he not remove: yea, one shall cry unto him, yet can he not answer, nor save him out of his trouble.

**[Isaiah 46:8-13](/bible/isaiah/46#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/46#v8)</sup> Remember this, and shew yourselves men: bring it again to mind, O ye transgressors.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/46#v9)</sup> Remember the former things of old: for I am God, and there is none else; I am God, and there is none like me,
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/46#v10)</sup> Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/46#v11)</sup> Calling a ravenous bird from the east, the man that executeth my counsel from a far country: yea, I have spoken it, I will also bring it to pass; I have purposed it, I will also do it.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/46#v12)</sup> Hearken unto me, ye stouthearted, that are far from righteousness:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/46#v13)</sup> I bring near my righteousness; it shall not be far off, and my salvation shall not tarry: and I will place salvation in Zion for Israel my glory.
