Read the chapter → [Isaiah 18](/bible/isaiah/18)

This session moves through [Isaiah 18](/bible/isaiah/18) to 21, four burdens laid on the nations around Israel. Most of these chapters go into history, prophecies of what would happen to Babylon, Assyria, Egypt, Judah, and Israel before the time of the Persians and the Medes. Some of it carries a double meaning that the Most High may open more fully one day. Chapter eighteen opens with a woe upon the land shadowing with wings beyond the rivers of Ethiopia, a reference to the land of Assyria, and moves toward the present being brought to the Most High of hosts at mount Zion.

**[Isaiah 18:1-3](/bible/isaiah/18#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/18#v1)</sup> Woe to the land shadowing with wings, which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/18#v2)</sup> That sendeth ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of bulrushes upon the waters, saying, Go, ye swift messengers, to a nation scattered and peeled, to a people terrible from their beginning hitherto; a nation meted out and trodden down, whose land the rivers have spoiled!
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/18#v3)</sup> All ye inhabitants of the world, and dwellers on the earth, see ye, when he lifteth up an ensign on the mountains; and when he bloweth a trumpet, hear ye.

- "Woe to the land shadowing with wings which is beyond the rivers of Ethiopia." This is the reference to the land of Assyria, that sends ambassadors by the sea, even in vessels of bulrushes upon the water.
- The message goes to a nation scattered and peeled, a people terrible from their beginning, a nation meted out and trodden down whose land the rivers have spoiled.
- All the inhabitants of the world and dwellers on the earth are called to see when the ensign is lifted up on the mountains and to hear when the trumpet is blown.

**[Isaiah 18:4-7](/bible/isaiah/18#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/18#v4)</sup> For so the Lord said unto me, I will take my rest, and I will consider in my dwelling place like a clear heat upon herbs, and like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/18#v5)</sup> For afore the harvest, when the bud is perfect, and the sour grape is ripening in the flower, he shall both cut off the sprigs with pruning hooks, and take away and cut down the branches.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/18#v6)</sup> They shall be left together unto the fowls of the mountains, and to the beasts of the earth: and the fowls shall summer upon them, and all the beasts of the earth shall winter upon them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/18#v7)</sup> In that time shall the present be brought unto the Lord of hosts of a people scattered and peeled, and from a people terrible from their beginning hitherto; a nation meted out and trodden under foot, whose land the rivers have spoiled, to the place of the name of the Lord of hosts, the mount Zion.

- The Most High said He would take His rest and consider upon His dwelling place, like a clear heat upon herbs and like a cloud of dew in the heat of harvest.
- Before the harvest He cuts off the sprigs with pruning hooks and takes away and cuts down the branches, left together to the fowls of the mountains and the beasts of the earth to summer and winter upon.
- In that time the present shall be brought to the Most High of hosts, of a people scattered and peeled, to the place of the name of the Most High of hosts, the mount Zion.
