**[Isaiah 32:1-2](/bible/isaiah/32#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/32#v1)</sup> Behold, a king shall reign in righteousness, and princes shall rule in judgment.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/32#v2)</sup> And a man shall be as an hiding place from the wind, and a covert from the tempest; as rivers of water in a dry place, as the shadow of a great rock in a weary land.

- A king shall reign in righteousness and princes shall rule in judgment. That man is a hiding place from the wind, a covert from the tempest, rivers of water in a dry place, the shadow of a great rock in a weary land.

**[Isaiah 32:3-4](/bible/isaiah/32#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/32#v3)</sup> And the eyes of them that see shall not be dim, and the ears of them that hear shall hearken.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/32#v4)</sup> The heart also of the rash shall understand knowledge, and the tongue of the stammerers shall be ready to speak plainly.

- The eyes of them that see shall not be dim and the ears of them that hear shall hearken; you will begin to see spiritually and hear with understanding. The heart of the rash shall understand knowledge and the stammering tongue shall speak plainly.
  - **[Isaiah 6:9-10](/bible/isaiah/6#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/6#v9)</sup> And he said, Go, and tell this people, Hear ye indeed, but understand not; and see ye indeed, but perceive not.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/6#v10)</sup> Make the heart of this people fat, and make their ears heavy, and shut their eyes; lest they see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and convert, and be healed.

    Hear ye indeed but understand not; their eyes are shut lest they convert and be healed.
  - **[John 12:37-40](/bible/john/12#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/john/12#v37)</sup> But though he had done so many miracles before them, yet they believed not on him:
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/john/12#v38)</sup> That the saying of Esaias the prophet might be fulfilled, which he spake, Lord, who hath believed our report? and to whom hath the arm of the Lord been revealed?
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/john/12#v39)</sup> Therefore they could not believe, because that Esaias said again,
    >
    > <sup>[40](/bible/john/12#v40)</sup> He hath blinded their eyes, and hardened their heart; that they should not see with their eyes, nor understand with their heart, and be converted, and I should heal them.

    Christ quoted Isaiah: they could not believe because the Most High blinded their eyes and hardened their hearts.

**[Isaiah 32:5-8](/bible/isaiah/32#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/32#v5)</sup> The vile person shall be no more called liberal, nor the churl said to be bountiful.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/32#v6)</sup> For the vile person will speak villany, and his heart will work iniquity, to practise hypocrisy, and to utter error against the Lord, to make empty the soul of the hungry, and he will cause the drink of the thirsty to fail.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/32#v7)</sup> The instruments also of the churl are evil: he deviseth wicked devices to destroy the poor with lying words, even when the needy speaketh right.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/32#v8)</sup> But the liberal deviseth liberal things; and by liberal things shall he stand.

- In that day the vile person is no longer called liberal, nor the churl bountiful, for the vile person speaks villainy, works iniquity, and utters error against the Most High to empty the hungry soul.
- The churl's instruments are evil, devising wicked devices to destroy the poor with lying words, but the liberal devises liberal things and by them shall he stand.

**[Isaiah 32:9-14](/bible/isaiah/32#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/32#v9)</sup> Rise up, ye women that are at ease; hear my voice, ye careless daughters; give ear unto my speech.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/32#v10)</sup> Many days and years shall ye be troubled, ye careless women: for the vintage shall fail, the gathering shall not come.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/32#v11)</sup> Tremble, ye women that are at ease; be troubled, ye careless ones: strip you, and make you bare, and gird sackcloth upon your loins.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/32#v12)</sup> They shall lament for the teats, for the pleasant fields, for the fruitful vine.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/32#v13)</sup> Upon the land of my people shall come up thorns and briers; yea, upon all the houses of joy in the joyous city:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/32#v14)</sup> Because the palaces shall be forsaken; the multitude of the city shall be left; the forts and towers shall be for dens for ever, a joy of wild asses, a pasture of flocks;

- The women at ease and the careless daughters are called to hear, for after days and years they will be troubled when the vintage and gathering fail. They must strip, gird sackcloth, and lament for the pleasant fields and fruitful vine.
- Thorns and briers come up on the land, the palaces are forsaken, and the forts and towers become dens forever, a joy of wild asses and a pasture of flocks.

**[Isaiah 32:15-17](/bible/isaiah/32#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/32#v15)</sup> Until the spirit be poured upon us from on high, and the wilderness be a fruitful field, and the fruitful field be counted for a forest.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/32#v16)</sup> Then judgment shall dwell in the wilderness, and righteousness remain in the fruitful field.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/32#v17)</sup> And the work of righteousness shall be peace; and the effect of righteousness quietness and assurance for ever.

- This lasts until the spirit is poured upon us from on high and the wilderness becomes a fruitful field. Then judgment dwells in the wilderness, righteousness remains in the fruitful field, and the work of righteousness is peace, quietness, and assurance forever.
- When we apply righteousness we will be at peace among each other; this is the peace Christ and Paul preached among the brethren. Even our forefathers understood in captivity that walking in the way of God brings peace.
  - **[Baruch 3:10-13](/bible/baruch/3#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/baruch/3#v10)</sup> How happeneth it Israel, that thou art in thine enemies’ land, that thou art waxen old in a strange country, that thou art defiled with the dead,
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/baruch/3#v11)</sup> That thou art counted with them that go down into the grave?
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/baruch/3#v12)</sup> Thou hast forsaken the fountain of wisdom.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/baruch/3#v13)</sup> For if thou hadst walked in the way of God, thou shouldest have dwelled in peace for ever.

    We forsook the fountain of wisdom; had we walked in his way we would have dwelt in peace forever.

**[Isaiah 32:18-20](/bible/isaiah/32#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/32#v18)</sup> And my people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, and in sure dwellings, and in quiet resting places;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/32#v19)</sup> When it shall hail, coming down on the forest; and the city shall be low in a low place.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/32#v20)</sup> Blessed are ye that sow beside all waters, that send forth thither the feet of the ox and the ass.

- The Most High's people shall dwell in a peaceable habitation, in sure dwellings and quiet resting places, though hail comes down on the forest and the city is brought low. We are not dwelling peaceably now, so we wait on that peace to return.
- Blessed are they that sow beside all waters and send out the feet of the ox and the ass.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:16](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v16)</sup> Cursed shalt thou be in the city, and cursed shalt thou be in the field.

    Cursed in the city and cursed in the field, because of the high crime and black-on-black violence among us.
