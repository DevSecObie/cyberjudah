**[Isaiah 44:1-5](/bible/isaiah/44#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/44#v1)</sup> Yet now hear, O Jacob my servant; and Israel, whom I have chosen:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/44#v2)</sup> Thus saith the Lord that made thee, and formed thee from the womb, which will help thee; Fear not, O Jacob, my servant; and thou, Jesurun, whom I have chosen.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/44#v3)</sup> For I will pour water upon him that is thirsty, and floods upon the dry ground: I will pour my spirit upon thy seed, and my blessing upon thine offspring:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/44#v4)</sup> And they shall spring up as among the grass, as willows by the water courses.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/44#v5)</sup> One shall say, I am the Lord’s; and another shall call himself by the name of Jacob; and another shall subscribe with his hand unto the Lord, and surname himself by the name of Israel.

- Yet now hear, O Jacob my servant, and Israel whom I have chosen; thus saith the Lord that made thee and formed thee from the womb and will help thee. Fear not, for I will pour water upon him that is thirsty and floods upon the dry ground, and pour my spirit upon thy seed and my blessing upon thine offspring.
- They shall spring up as among the grass, as willows by the watercourses. One shall say I am the Lord's, another shall call himself by the name of Jacob, and another shall subscribe with his hand unto the Lord and surname himself by the name of Israel.
- This is prophecy of our name being changed back: on the sabbath classes a member is announced no longer a Corinthian or an Ephesian, because they take a pen and legalize their name change, subscribing with the hand as the scripture said.

    Ye shall leave your name for a curse unto my chosen, for the Lord shall slay thee and call his servants by another name; our names were left for a curse in slavery.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:37](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v37)**

    Thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword among all nations; our nationality and name were changed, called African American, one of the curses driven upon us.

>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/44#v7)</sup> And who, as I, shall call, and shall declare it, and set it in order for me, since I appointed the ancient people? and the things that are coming, and shall come, let them shew unto them.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/44#v8)</sup> Fear ye not, neither be afraid: have not I told thee from that time, and have declared it? ye are even my witnesses. Is there a God beside me? yea, there is no God; I know not any.

- Thus saith the Lord the King of Israel and his redeemer the Lord of hosts: I am the first and I am the last, and beside me there is no God. Who as I shall call and declare it and set it in order, since I appointed the ancient people?
- Fear ye not, neither be afraid; have not I told thee from that time and declared it? Ye are even my witnesses. Is there a God beside me? Yea, there is no God, I know not any. Our God stays bragging through the bible that he knows no other God.
  - **[Psalms 96:5](/bible/psalms/96#v5)**

    For all the gods of the nations are idols, but the Lord made the heavens; the nations' gods are nothing but stuff our God made, which he gave them to worship.

**[Isaiah 44:9-17](/bible/isaiah/44#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/44#v9)</sup> They that make a graven image are all of them vanity; and their delectable things shall not profit; and they are their own witnesses; they see not, nor know; that they may be ashamed.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/44#v10)</sup> Who hath formed a god, or molten a graven image that is profitable for nothing?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/44#v11)</sup> Behold, all his fellows shall be ashamed: and the workmen, they are of men: let them all be gathered together, let them stand up; yet they shall fear, and they shall be ashamed together.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/44#v12)</sup> The smith with the tongs both worketh in the coals, and fashioneth it with hammers, and worketh it with the strength of his arms: yea, he is hungry, and his strength faileth: he drinketh no water, and is faint.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/44#v13)</sup> The carpenter stretcheth out his rule; he marketh it out with a line; he fitteth it with planes, and he marketh it out with the compass, and maketh it after the figure of a man, according to the beauty of a man; that it may remain in the house.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/44#v14)</sup> He heweth him down cedars, and taketh the cypress and the oak, which he strengtheneth for himself among the trees of the forest: he planteth an ash, and the rain doth nourish it.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/44#v15)</sup> Then shall it be for a man to burn: for he will take thereof, and warm himself; yea, he kindleth it, and baketh bread; yea, he maketh a god, and worshippeth it; he maketh it a graven image, and falleth down thereto.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/44#v16)</sup> He burneth part thereof in the fire; with part thereof he eateth flesh; he roasteth roast, and is satisfied: yea, he warmeth himself, and saith, Aha, I am warm, I have seen the fire:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/44#v17)</sup> And the residue thereof he maketh a god, even his graven image: he falleth down unto it, and worshippeth it, and prayeth unto it, and saith, Deliver me; for thou art my god.

- They that make a graven image are all of them vanity, their delectable things shall not profit, and they are their own witnesses; they see not nor know, that they may be ashamed. Who has formed a god or molten a graven image profitable for nothing?
- The smith works it in the coals and fashions it with hammers, working with the strength of his arms until he is hungry and his strength fails; the carpenter stretches out his rule, marks it with a compass, and makes it after the figure of a man. Same as Jeremiah said, they fasten idols with nails so they move not and must be carried, so how can it have power?
- He heweth down cedars, cypress, and oak, which grow among the trees of the forest; part of the same tree he burns to warm himself and bake bread, and the residue he maketh a god and worshippeth it, falling down and praying deliver me, for thou art my god. He uses the same wood to warm himself yet thinks the rest is a god.

>

>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/44#v20)</sup> He feedeth on ashes: a deceived heart hath turned him aside, that he cannot deliver his soul, nor say, Is there not a lie in my right hand?

- They have not known nor understood, for he hath shut their eyes that they cannot see and their hearts that they cannot understand. None considereth in his heart to say I have burned part in the fire and roasted flesh, so shall I make the residue an abomination and fall down to the stock of a tree?
- This is the strong delusion: our people are convinced color does not matter, so it is easy for a white jesus to sit in the temple and show himself to be god, when Christ is shown black from the scripture. He feedeth on ashes, a deceived heart hath turned him aside, and he cannot deliver his soul nor say, is there not a lie in my right hand?

**[Isaiah 44:21-23](/bible/isaiah/44#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/44#v21)</sup> Remember these, O Jacob and Israel; for thou art my servant: I have formed thee; thou art my servant: O Israel, thou shalt not be forgotten of me.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/44#v22)</sup> I have blotted out, as a thick cloud, thy transgressions, and, as a cloud, thy sins: return unto me; for I have redeemed thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/44#v23)</sup> Sing, O ye heavens; for the Lord hath done it: shout, ye lower parts of the earth: break forth into singing, ye mountains, O forest, and every tree therein: for the Lord hath redeemed Jacob, and glorified himself in Israel.

- Remember these, O Jacob and Israel, for thou art my servant; I have formed thee, thou shalt not be forgotten of me. I have blotted out as a thick cloud thy transgressions and as a cloud thy sins; return unto me, for I have redeemed thee.
- Repentance is for Israel; when he blots out the sins he is speaking of Jacob, the servants that sinned whom God is calling back. Sing O ye heavens, shout ye lower parts of the earth, break forth into singing, for the Lord hath redeemed Jacob and glorified himself in Israel.
  - **[2 Corinthians 7:9-10](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v9)**

    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-corinthians/7#v10)</sup> For godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of: but the sorrow of the world worketh death.

    Answering whether we must be angry with ourselves for breaking God's laws: godly sorrow worketh repentance to salvation not to be repented of, but the sorrow of the world worketh death, so be angry enough to repent and change, not to beat yourself down.

**[Isaiah 44:24-28](/bible/isaiah/44#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/44#v24)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, thy redeemer, and he that formed thee from the womb, I am the Lord that maketh all things; that stretcheth forth the heavens alone; that spreadeth abroad the earth by myself;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/44#v25)</sup> That frustrateth the tokens of the liars, and maketh diviners mad; that turneth wise men backward, and maketh their knowledge foolish;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/isaiah/44#v26)</sup> That confirmeth the word of his servant, and performeth the counsel of his messengers; that saith to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be inhabited; and to the cities of Judah, Ye shall be built, and I will raise up the decayed places thereof:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/isaiah/44#v27)</sup> That saith to the deep, Be dry, and I will dry up thy rivers:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/isaiah/44#v28)</sup> That saith of Cyrus, He is my shepherd, and shall perform all my pleasure: even saying to Jerusalem, Thou shalt be built; and to the temple, Thy foundation shall be laid.

- Thus saith the Lord thy redeemer that formed thee from the womb: I am the Lord that maketh all things, that stretcheth forth the heavens alone and spreadeth abroad the earth by myself. There is no God beside our God.
- He frustrateth the tokens of the liars and maketh diviners mad, turning wise men backward and making their knowledge foolish, for the wisdom of the world is foolishness to God. He confirmeth the word of his servant and performeth the counsel of his messengers.
- That saith to Jerusalem, thou shalt be inhabited, and to the cities of Judah, ye shall be built, raising up the decayed places. That saith of Cyrus, he is my shepherd and shall perform all my pleasure, saying to Jerusalem thou shalt be built and to the temple thy foundation shall be laid. Jerusalem will be the place where Christ's feet touch down and Israel rules the world, not the gentiles trodding it down now.
