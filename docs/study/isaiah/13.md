Read the chapter → [Isaiah 13](/bible/isaiah/13)

[Isaiah 13](/bible/isaiah/13) carries the burden of Babylon. It goes into the destruction of Edom and the rest of the nations in the day of the Most High. The remnant that studies understands the prophecies and what is to come; those who should be afraid are the ones who do not know what is going to come to pass.

**[Isaiah 13:1-5](/bible/isaiah/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/13#v1)</sup> The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/13#v2)</sup> Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/13#v3)</sup> I have commanded my sanctified ones, I have also called my mighty ones for mine anger, even them that rejoice in my highness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/13#v4)</sup> The noise of a multitude in the mountains, like as of a great people; a tumultuous noise of the kingdoms of nations gathered together: the Lord of hosts mustereth the host of the battle.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/13#v5)</sup> They come from a far country, from the end of heaven, even the Lord, and the weapons of his indignation, to destroy the whole land.

- The burden of Babylon which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see: lift up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice, shake the hand that they may go into the gates of the nobles. The banner is the Bible.
- The Most High has commanded His sanctified ones and called His mighty ones for His anger. There is the noise of a multitude in the mountains, kingdoms of nations gathered together; the Most High of hosts musters the host of the battle.
- They come from a far country, from the end of heaven, the Most High and the weapons of His indignation, to destroy the whole land. Those nukes are not just sitting there for nothing; they will be used.

**[Isaiah 13:6-11](/bible/isaiah/13#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/13#v6)</sup> Howl ye; for the day of the Lord is at hand; it shall come as a destruction from the Almighty.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/13#v7)</sup> Therefore shall all hands be faint, and every man’s heart shall melt:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/13#v8)</sup> And they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces shall be as flames.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/13#v9)</sup> Behold, the day of the Lord cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/13#v10)</sup> For the stars of heaven and the constellations thereof shall not give their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not cause her light to shine.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/13#v11)</sup> And I will punish the world for their evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible.

- Howl, for the day of the Most High is at hand; it comes as a destruction from the Almighty. Every hand faints, every heart melts, and pain and sorrow take hold of them.
- If you study this Bible, you understand the prophecies and what is to come, so you prepare your mind. The ones who should be afraid are those who do not know what is coming to pass.
- They shall be amazed one at another, their faces as flames. Esau's face is going to be as flames, meaning it is going to be red. The day of the Most High comes cruel with wrath and fierce anger to lay the land desolate and destroy the sinners out of it, those not keeping His laws, which is why the remnant keeping His law escapes and is recovered the second time.

**[Isaiah 13:12-16](/bible/isaiah/13#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/13#v12)</sup> I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/13#v13)</sup> Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth shall remove out of her place, in the wrath of the Lord of hosts, and in the day of his fierce anger.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/13#v14)</sup> And it shall be as the chased roe, and as a sheep that no man taketh up: they shall every man turn to his own people, and flee every one into his own land.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/13#v15)</sup> Every one that is found shall be thrust through; and every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/13#v16)</sup> Their children also shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses shall be spoiled, and their wives ravished.

- The stars of heaven and the constellations, the satellites, shall not give their light; the sun is darkened and the moon does not shine. The Most High will punish the world for their evil and the wicked for their iniquity, and cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease. The arrogant one is Esau, a very proud nation.
- The Most High will make a man more precious than fine gold, even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir. This is the Israelite man made precious as fine gold. He shakes the heavens, and the earth removes out of her place off its axis, and what removes it is nuclear destruction, in the day of His fierce anger.
- Every man turns to his own people and flees into his own land; that is a word against interracial marriage. Everyone found with the other nations, especially Edom, shall be thrust through, everyone joined to them shall fall by the sword, their children dashed to pieces before their eyes, their houses spoiled and their wives ravished. This is not talking about us; it is about these other nations.

**[Isaiah 13:17-22](/bible/isaiah/13#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/13#v17)</sup> Behold, I will stir up the Medes against them, which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/13#v18)</sup> Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces; and they shall have no pity on the fruit of the womb; their eye shall not spare children.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/13#v19)</sup> And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees’ excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/13#v20)</sup> It shall never be inhabited, neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation: neither shall the Arabian pitch tent there; neither shall the shepherds make their fold there.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/13#v21)</sup> But wild beasts of the desert shall lie there; and their houses shall be full of doleful creatures; and owls shall dwell there, and satyrs shall dance there.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/13#v22)</sup> And the wild beasts of the islands shall cry in their desolate houses, and dragons in their pleasant palaces: and her time is near to come, and her days shall not be prolonged.

- The Most High stirs up the Medes against them, who do not regard silver nor delight in gold. Their bows dash the young men to pieces; they have no pity on the fruit of the womb and their eye does not spare children.
- Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees' excellency, shall be as when the Most High overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah. That is how the United States of Babylon is going to be.
- It shall never be inhabited from generation to generation; no Arabian pitches a tent there, no shepherd folds there, but wild beasts, owls, satyrs, and dragons dwell in the desolate houses and pleasant palaces. Her time is near to come and her days shall not be prolonged. We do not have that much time, Israel; we have to gather ourselves together, a nation that is not desired.
