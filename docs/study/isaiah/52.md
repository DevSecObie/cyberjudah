**[Isaiah 52:1-6](/bible/isaiah/52#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/52#v1)</sup> Awake, awake; put on thy strength, O Zion; put on thy beautiful garments, O Jerusalem, the holy city: for henceforth there shall no more come into thee the uncircumcised and the unclean.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/52#v2)</sup> Shake thyself from the dust; arise, and sit down, O Jerusalem: loose thyself from the bands of thy neck, O captive daughter of Zion.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/52#v3)</sup> For thus saith the Lord, Ye have sold yourselves for nought; and ye shall be redeemed without money.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/52#v4)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God, My people went down aforetime into Egypt to sojourn there; and the Assyrian oppressed them without cause.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/52#v5)</sup> Now therefore, what have I here, saith the Lord, that my people is taken away for nought? they that rule over them make them to howl, saith the Lord; and my name continually every day is blasphemed.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/52#v6)</sup> Therefore my people shall know my name: therefore they shall know in that day that I am he that doth speak: behold, it is I.

- Zion is called to awake and put on strength, and Jerusalem the holy city to put on her beautiful garments, for henceforth the uncircumcised and the unclean shall no more come into her. She is told to shake off the dust, arise, and loose the bands of her neck.
- They sold themselves for nothing and shall be redeemed without money. His people went down into Egypt to sojourn, and the Assyrian oppressed them without cause.
- Because those that rule over them make them howl and his name is blasphemed continually every day, his people shall therefore know his name, and know in that day that he is the one who speaks.

**[Isaiah 52:7-12](/bible/isaiah/52#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/52#v7)</sup> How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that bringeth good tidings, that publisheth peace; that bringeth good tidings of good, that publisheth salvation; that saith unto Zion, Thy God reigneth!
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/52#v8)</sup> Thy watchmen shall lift up the voice; with the voice together shall they sing: for they shall see eye to eye, when the Lord shall bring again Zion.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/52#v9)</sup> Break forth into joy, sing together, ye waste places of Jerusalem: for the Lord hath comforted his people, he hath redeemed Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/52#v10)</sup> The Lord hath made bare his holy arm in the eyes of all the nations; and all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/52#v11)</sup> Depart ye, depart ye, go ye out from thence, touch no unclean thing; go ye out of the midst of her; be ye clean, that bear the vessels of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/52#v12)</sup> For ye shall not go out with haste, nor go by flight: for the Lord will go before you; and the God of Israel will be your rereward.

- Beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him that brings good tidings, that publishes peace and salvation and says to Zion, Thy God reigneth. The watchmen shall lift up the voice and sing together when they see eye to eye and the Lord brings again Zion.
- The waste places of Jerusalem are told to break forth into joy, for the Lord has comforted his people and redeemed Jerusalem, making bare his holy arm in the eyes of all the nations, so all the ends of the earth shall see the salvation of our God.
- They are told to depart and go out from thence, touching no unclean thing and being clean that bear the vessels of the Lord; they shall not go out in haste or by flight, for the Lord will go before them and be their rearward.

**[Isaiah 52:13-15](/bible/isaiah/52#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/52#v13)</sup> Behold, my servant shall deal prudently, he shall be exalted and extolled, and be very high.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/52#v14)</sup> As many were astonied at thee; his visage was so marred more than any man, and his form more than the sons of men:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/52#v15)</sup> So shall he sprinkle many nations; the kings shall shut their mouths at him: for that which had not been told them shall they see; and that which they had not heard shall they consider.

- The Servant shall deal prudently and be exalted, extolled, and made very high. Many were astonished at him because his visage was marred more than any man and his form more than the sons of men.
- So shall he sprinkle many nations, and kings shall shut their mouths at him, for what had not been told them they shall see, and what they had not heard they shall consider.
