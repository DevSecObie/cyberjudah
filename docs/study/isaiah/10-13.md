Read the chapter → [Isaiah 10](/bible/isaiah/10)

This session covers Isaiah chapters 10 through 13. [Isaiah 10](/bible/isaiah/10) moves into the judgments that come against Israel for disobedience, and the judgment that comes upon the nation the Most High used to punish us. The Most High uses nations to chastise His people. In that day it was the Assyrians. Today it is the so-called white man, Esau, the nation of Edom, and truly the rest of the nations as well, though mainly Edom. This is a twofold prophecy: it reaches from the ancient captivity all the way to our condition now. Chapter 11 is a prophecy of Christ and of Israel being gathered a second time from the four corners of the earth. Chapter 12 is a chapter of salvation and joy. Chapter 13 carries the burden of Babylon and the day of the Most High against Edom and the nations.

**[Isaiah 10:1-4](/bible/isaiah/10#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/10#v1)</sup> Woe unto them that decree unrighteous decrees, and that write grievousness which they have prescribed;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/10#v2)</sup> To turn aside the needy from judgment, and to take away the right from the poor of my people, that widows may be their prey, and that they may rob the fatherless!
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/10#v3)</sup> And what will ye do in the day of visitation, and in the desolation which shall come from far? to whom will ye flee for help? and where will ye leave your glory?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/10#v4)</sup> Without me they shall bow down under the prisoners, and they shall fall under the slain. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.

- Woe is pronounced on those who decree unrighteous decrees and write grievous laws to turn aside the needy from judgment and take away the right from the poor. That is us: we are the needy, we are the poor.
- Back then this was the Assyrians doing it to us because of our disobedience. Today it is your friendly neighborhood white man writing unrighteous laws and prescribing them for us to keep. It is a twofold prophecy.
- The unrighteous decrees rob the widows and the fatherless. The question is put to the oppressor: what will you do in the day of visitation and desolation, to whom will you flee for help, and where will you leave your glory. Without the Most High they bow down under the prisoners and fall under the slain, for His anger is not turned away and His hand is stretched out still.

**[Isaiah 10:5-11](/bible/isaiah/10#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/10#v5)</sup> O Assyrian, the rod of mine anger, and the staff in their hand is mine indignation.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/10#v6)</sup> I will send him against an hypocritical nation, and against the people of my wrath will I give him a charge, to take the spoil, and to take the prey, and to tread them down like the mire of the streets.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/10#v7)</sup> Howbeit he meaneth not so, neither doth his heart think so; but it is in his heart to destroy and cut off nations not a few.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/10#v8)</sup> For he saith, Are not my princes altogether kings?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/10#v9)</sup> Is not Calno as Carchemish? is not Hamath as Arpad? is not Samaria as Damascus?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/10#v10)</sup> As my hand hath found the kingdoms of the idols, and whose graven images did excel them of Jerusalem and of Samaria;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/10#v11)</sup> Shall I not, as I have done unto Samaria and her idols, so do to Jerusalem and her idols?

- The Most High calls the Assyrian the rod of His anger, the staff in their hand His indignation. He used nations to punish us. He sends the Assyrian against a hypocritical nation, the people of His wrath, to take the spoil and tread them down like the mire of the streets.
- The Most High calls us hypocrites because we love to say we love the Most High, but we do not want to do what the Bible says.
- The Assyrian did not mean it that way; it was in his heart to destroy and cut off nations. He boasted of the kingdoms he conquered and their idols, not realizing the Most High was using him to punish us and the rest of the nations. We had graven images throughout our land, breaking the Most High's laws.

**[Isaiah 10:12-19](/bible/isaiah/10#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/10#v12)</sup> Wherefore it shall come to pass, that when the Lord hath performed his whole work upon mount Zion and on Jerusalem, I will punish the fruit of the stout heart of the king of Assyria, and the glory of his high looks.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/10#v13)</sup> For he saith, By the strength of my hand I have done it, and by my wisdom; for I am prudent: and I have removed the bounds of the people, and have robbed their treasures, and I have put down the inhabitants like a valiant man:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/10#v14)</sup> And my hand hath found as a nest the riches of the people: and as one gathereth eggs that are left, have I gathered all the earth; and there was none that moved the wing, or opened the mouth, or peeped.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/10#v15)</sup> Shall the axe boast itself against him that heweth therewith? or shall the saw magnify itself against him that shaketh it? as if the rod should shake itself against them that lift it up, or as if the staff should lift up itself, as if it were no wood.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/10#v16)</sup> Therefore shall the Lord, the Lord of hosts, send among his fat ones leanness; and under his glory he shall kindle a burning like the burning of a fire.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/10#v17)</sup> And the light of Israel shall be for a fire, and his Holy One for a flame: and it shall burn and devour his thorns and his briers in one day;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/10#v18)</sup> And shall consume the glory of his forest, and of his fruitful field, both soul and body: and they shall be as when a standardbearer fainteth.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/10#v19)</sup> And the rest of the trees of his forest shall be few, that a child may write them.

- When the Most High has finished His work on Zion and Jerusalem, He will punish the fruit of the stout heart of the king of Assyria and the glory of his high looks. The Assyrian said, "By the strength of my hand I have done it, and by my wisdom, for I am prudent," boasting that he removed the treasures and inhabitants of the people on his own.
- The Most High asks whether the axe boasts against the one who hews with it, or the saw magnifies itself against the one who shakes it. The Assyrian was only the axe; the Most High used him to cut us down from rulership to captivity.
- Therefore the Most High sends leanness among the fat ones and kindles a burning fire that devours the thorns and briers in one day and consumes the glory of the forest, both soul and body, until the trees are so few a child may write them.

**[Isaiah 10:20-23](/bible/isaiah/10#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/10#v20)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the remnant of Israel, and such as are escaped of the house of Jacob, shall no more again stay upon him that smote them; but shall stay upon the Lord, the Holy One of Israel, in truth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/10#v21)</sup> The remnant shall return, even the remnant of Jacob, unto the mighty God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/10#v22)</sup> For though thy people Israel be as the sand of the sea, yet a remnant of them shall return: the consumption decreed shall overflow with righteousness.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/10#v23)</sup> For the Lord God of hosts shall make a consumption, even determined, in the midst of all the land.

- In that day the remnant of Israel and those who escaped of the house of Jacob shall no more stay upon him that smote them, but stay upon the Most High, the Holy One of Israel, in truth. This is a future prophecy.
- "Stay" means to rely upon. Just like back then, we still rely upon the ones who smote us, even today under the nation of Edom, trusting in oppression, relying on the very ones still smiting us in the streets. In that day the remnant, those keeping the Most High's commandments in their right mind, will escape and stop leaning on the oppressor.
- Though Israel be as the sand of the sea, only a remnant shall return. The Most High will make a consumption decreed and overflowing with righteousness in the midst of all the land.
  - **[Revelation 12:17](/bible/revelation/12#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/revelation/12#v17)</sup> And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ.

    The dragon went to make war with the remnant of the woman's seed, those keeping the commandments of the Most High and holding the testimony of Jesus Christ; that remnant is the Israelites.

> <sup>[27](/bible/isaiah/10#v27)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that his burden shall be taken away from off thy shoulder, and his yoke from off thy neck, and the yoke shall be destroyed because of the anointing.

> <sup>[34](/bible/isaiah/10#v34)</sup> And he shall cut down the thickets of the forest with iron, and Lebanon shall fall by a mighty one.

- The Most High tells His people who dwell in Zion not to be afraid of the Assyrian who smites with a rod and lifts up his staff after the manner of Egypt, for in a very little while the indignation will cease and His anger will turn to their destruction.
- The Most High will stir up a scourge for the Assyrian, and his yoke shall be taken off Israel's neck and destroyed because of the anointing.
- The chapter traces the invader's march through the towns until he shakes his hand against the mount of the daughter of Zion. Then the Most High of hosts lops the bough with terror, the high ones are hewn down, and Lebanon falls by a mighty one.

# Isaiah 11: The Rod of Jesse and the Second Gathering

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 11](/bible/isaiah/11)

[Isaiah 11](/bible/isaiah/11) is a prophecy of Christ returning and of Israel being gathered from the four corners of the earth. It is the remnant that is gathered, not all Israel. Two-thirds of our people are not going to repent; only the remnant will be recovered the second time.

**[Isaiah 11:1-5](/bible/isaiah/11#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/11#v1)</sup> And there shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a Branch shall grow out of his roots:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/11#v2)</sup> And the spirit of the Lord shall rest upon him, the spirit of wisdom and understanding, the spirit of counsel and might, the spirit of knowledge and of the fear of the Lord;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/11#v3)</sup> And shall make him of quick understanding in the fear of the Lord: and he shall not judge after the sight of his eyes, neither reprove after the hearing of his ears:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/11#v4)</sup> But with righteousness shall he judge the poor, and reprove with equity for the meek of the earth: and he shall smite the earth with the rod of his mouth, and with the breath of his lips shall he slay the wicked.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/11#v5)</sup> And righteousness shall be the girdle of his loins, and faithfulness the girdle of his reins.

- There shall come forth a rod out of the stem of Jesse, and a branch shall grow out of his roots. This is a prophecy of Christ. The Spirit of the Most High rests upon Him: wisdom, understanding, counsel, might, knowledge, and the fear of the Most High.
- He does not judge after the sight of His eyes or reprove after the hearing of His ears. The Most High does not look at the stature; He judges us according to the law.
- With righteousness He judges the poor and reproves with equity for the meek of the earth, smiting the earth with the rod of His mouth and slaying the wicked with the breath of His lips. Righteousness and faithfulness are the girdle of His loins.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)</sup> And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he hath commanded us.

    It shall be our righteousness if we observe to do all these commandments before the Most High; He judges the earth in righteousness according to the law.

**[Isaiah 11:6-9](/bible/isaiah/11#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/11#v6)</sup> The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid; and the calf and the young lion and the fatling together; and a little child shall lead them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/11#v7)</sup> And the cow and the bear shall feed; their young ones shall lie down together: and the lion shall eat straw like the ox.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/11#v8)</sup> And the sucking child shall play on the hole of the asp, and the weaned child shall put his hand on the cockatrice’ den.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/11#v9)</sup> They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain: for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea.

- The wolf shall dwell with the lamb, the leopard lie down with the kid, the calf and the young lion and the fatling together, and a little child shall lead them.
- The cow and the bear shall feed, their young lie down together, and the lion shall eat straw like the ox. The nursing child shall play on the hole of the asp and the weaned child put his hand on the cockatrice den.
- They shall not hurt nor destroy in all the Most High's holy mountain, for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Most High as the waters cover the sea.

**[Isaiah 11:10-12](/bible/isaiah/11#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/11#v10)</sup> And in that day there shall be a root of Jesse, which shall stand for an ensign of the people; to it shall the Gentiles seek: and his rest shall be glorious.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/11#v11)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria, and from Egypt, and from Pathros, and from Cush, and from Elam, and from Shinar, and from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/11#v12)</sup> And he shall set up an ensign for the nations, and shall assemble the outcasts of Israel, and gather together the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth.

- In that day there shall be a root of Jesse standing for an ensign of the people; Jesse refers to Christ, and to Him the Gentiles shall seek, and His rest shall be glorious.
- This is the prophecy of the second time the Most High sets His hand to recover the remnant of His people. We already read what remnant means: those keeping His commandments. Though Israel be as the sand of the sea, only a remnant returns.
- He recovers us from the places we are scattered: Assyria, Egypt, Pathros, Cush, Elam, Shinar, Hamath, and the islands of the sea. He sets up an ensign for the nations, assembles the outcasts of Israel, the northern kingdom, and gathers the dispersed of Judah from the four corners of the earth. All twelve tribes are scattered to the four corners of the earth.

**[Isaiah 11:13-16](/bible/isaiah/11#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/11#v13)</sup> The envy also of Ephraim shall depart, and the adversaries of Judah shall be cut off: Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/11#v14)</sup> But they shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines toward the west; they shall spoil them of the east together: they shall lay their hand upon Edom and Moab; and the children of Ammon shall obey them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/11#v15)</sup> And the Lord shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea; and with his mighty wind shall he shake his hand over the river, and shall smite it in the seven streams, and make men go over dryshod.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/11#v16)</sup> And there shall be an highway for the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria; like as it was to Israel in the day that he came up out of the land of Egypt.

- The envy of Ephraim shall depart and the adversaries of Judah be cut off; Ephraim shall not envy Judah, and Judah shall not vex Ephraim. This prophecy goes all the way back to Genesis and reaches up to how the northern and southern kingdoms treat one another today.
- They shall fly upon the shoulders of the Philistines toward the west and spoil the children of the east together. They shall lay their hand upon Edom and Moab, and the children of Ammon shall obey them.
- The Most High shall utterly destroy the tongue of the Egyptian sea and smite the river into seven streams so men go over dry-shod. There shall be a highway for the remnant left from Assyria, like the day Israel came up out of Egypt.

# Isaiah 12: Salvation, Joy, and the Holy One of Israel

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 12](/bible/isaiah/12)

[Isaiah 12](/bible/isaiah/12) is a short chapter about salvation and joy. It also settles a point on the name of the Most High, showing that Jehovah and the Holy One of Israel are the same God.

**[Isaiah 12:1-3](/bible/isaiah/12#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/12#v1)</sup> And in that day thou shalt say, O Lord, I will praise thee: though thou wast angry with me, thine anger is turned away, and thou comfortedst me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/12#v2)</sup> Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and not be afraid: for the Lord Jehovah is my strength and my song; he also is become my salvation.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/12#v3)</sup> Therefore with joy shall ye draw water out of the wells of salvation.

- In that day you shall say, "O Lord, I will praise Thee; though Thou wast angry with me, Thine anger is turned away, and Thou comfortedst me."
- "Behold, God is my salvation; I will trust, and not be afraid, for the Lord Jehovah is my strength and my song; He also is become my salvation." With joy shall you draw water out of the wells of salvation.

**[Isaiah 12:4-6](/bible/isaiah/12#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/12#v4)</sup> And in that day shall ye say, Praise the Lord, call upon his name, declare his doings among the people, make mention that his name is exalted.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/12#v5)</sup> Sing unto the Lord; for he hath done excellent things: this is known in all the earth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/12#v6)</sup> Cry out and shout, thou inhabitant of Zion: for great is the Holy One of Israel in the midst of thee.

- In that day you shall praise the Most High, call upon His name, declare His doings among the people, and make mention that His name is exalted. Sing unto the Most High, for He has done excellent things known in all the earth.
- Cry out and shout, inhabitant of Zion, for great is the Holy One of Israel in the midst of thee. Verse 2 calls Him Jehovah, and verse 6 calls Him the Holy One of Israel; in context they are the same God.
- Jehovah's witnesses say "say the name, say Jehovah." Right here in the scriptures the Holy One of Israel and Jehovah are shown to be the same God. This is how to keep it in context with the scriptures.

# Isaiah 13: The Burden of Babylon and the Day of the Most High

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 13](/bible/isaiah/13)

[Isaiah 13](/bible/isaiah/13) carries the burden of Babylon. It goes into the destruction of Edom and the rest of the nations in the day of the Most High. The remnant that studies understands the prophecies and what is to come; those who should be afraid are the ones who do not know what is going to come to pass.

**[Isaiah 13:1-5](/bible/isaiah/13#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/13#v1)</sup> The burden of Babylon, which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/13#v2)</sup> Lift ye up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice unto them, shake the hand, that they may go into the gates of the nobles.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/13#v3)</sup> I have commanded my sanctified ones, I have also called my mighty ones for mine anger, even them that rejoice in my highness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/13#v4)</sup> The noise of a multitude in the mountains, like as of a great people; a tumultuous noise of the kingdoms of nations gathered together: the Lord of hosts mustereth the host of the battle.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/13#v5)</sup> They come from a far country, from the end of heaven, even the Lord, and the weapons of his indignation, to destroy the whole land.

- The burden of Babylon which Isaiah the son of Amoz did see: lift up a banner upon the high mountain, exalt the voice, shake the hand that they may go into the gates of the nobles. The banner is the Bible.
- The Most High has commanded His sanctified ones and called His mighty ones for His anger. There is the noise of a multitude in the mountains, kingdoms of nations gathered together; the Most High of hosts musters the host of the battle.
- They come from a far country, from the end of heaven, the Most High and the weapons of His indignation, to destroy the whole land. Those nukes are not just sitting there for nothing; they will be used.

**[Isaiah 13:6-11](/bible/isaiah/13#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/13#v6)</sup> Howl ye; for the day of the Lord is at hand; it shall come as a destruction from the Almighty.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/13#v7)</sup> Therefore shall all hands be faint, and every man’s heart shall melt:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/13#v8)</sup> And they shall be afraid: pangs and sorrows shall take hold of them; they shall be in pain as a woman that travaileth: they shall be amazed one at another; their faces shall be as flames.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/13#v9)</sup> Behold, the day of the Lord cometh, cruel both with wrath and fierce anger, to lay the land desolate: and he shall destroy the sinners thereof out of it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/13#v10)</sup> For the stars of heaven and the constellations thereof shall not give their light: the sun shall be darkened in his going forth, and the moon shall not cause her light to shine.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/13#v11)</sup> And I will punish the world for their evil, and the wicked for their iniquity; and I will cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease, and will lay low the haughtiness of the terrible.

- Howl, for the day of the Most High is at hand; it comes as a destruction from the Almighty. Every hand faints, every heart melts, and pain and sorrow take hold of them.
- If you study this Bible, you understand the prophecies and what is to come, so you prepare your mind. The ones who should be afraid are those who do not know what is coming to pass.
- They shall be amazed one at another, their faces as flames. Esau's face is going to be as flames, meaning it is going to be red. The day of the Most High comes cruel with wrath and fierce anger to lay the land desolate and destroy the sinners out of it, those not keeping His laws, which is why the remnant keeping His law escapes and is recovered the second time.

**[Isaiah 13:12-16](/bible/isaiah/13#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/13#v12)</sup> I will make a man more precious than fine gold; even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/13#v13)</sup> Therefore I will shake the heavens, and the earth shall remove out of her place, in the wrath of the Lord of hosts, and in the day of his fierce anger.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/13#v14)</sup> And it shall be as the chased roe, and as a sheep that no man taketh up: they shall every man turn to his own people, and flee every one into his own land.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/13#v15)</sup> Every one that is found shall be thrust through; and every one that is joined unto them shall fall by the sword.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/13#v16)</sup> Their children also shall be dashed to pieces before their eyes; their houses shall be spoiled, and their wives ravished.

- The stars of heaven and the constellations, the satellites, shall not give their light; the sun is darkened and the moon does not shine. The Most High will punish the world for their evil and the wicked for their iniquity, and cause the arrogancy of the proud to cease. The arrogant one is Esau, a very proud nation.
- The Most High will make a man more precious than fine gold, even a man than the golden wedge of Ophir. This is the Israelite man made precious as fine gold. He shakes the heavens, and the earth removes out of her place off its axis, and what removes it is nuclear destruction, in the day of His fierce anger.
- Every man turns to his own people and flees into his own land; that is a word against interracial marriage. Everyone found with the other nations, especially Edom, shall be thrust through, everyone joined to them shall fall by the sword, their children dashed to pieces before their eyes, their houses spoiled and their wives ravished. This is not talking about us; it is about these other nations.

**[Isaiah 13:17-22](/bible/isaiah/13#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/13#v17)</sup> Behold, I will stir up the Medes against them, which shall not regard silver; and as for gold, they shall not delight in it.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/13#v18)</sup> Their bows also shall dash the young men to pieces; and they shall have no pity on the fruit of the womb; their eye shall not spare children.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/13#v19)</sup> And Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees’ excellency, shall be as when God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/13#v20)</sup> It shall never be inhabited, neither shall it be dwelt in from generation to generation: neither shall the Arabian pitch tent there; neither shall the shepherds make their fold there.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/13#v21)</sup> But wild beasts of the desert shall lie there; and their houses shall be full of doleful creatures; and owls shall dwell there, and satyrs shall dance there.

> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/13#v22)</sup> And the wild beasts of the islands shall cry in their desolate houses, and dragons in their pleasant palaces: and her time is near to come, and her days shall not be prolonged.

- The Most High stirs up the Medes against them, who do not regard silver nor delight in gold. Their bows dash the young men to pieces; they have no pity on the fruit of the womb and their eye does not spare children.
- Babylon, the glory of kingdoms, the beauty of the Chaldees' excellency, shall be as when the Most High overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah. That is how the United States of Babylon is going to be.
- It shall never be inhabited from generation to generation; no Arabian pitches a tent there, no shepherd folds there, but wild beasts, owls, satyrs, and dragons dwell in the desolate houses and pleasant palaces. Her time is near to come and her days shall not be prolonged. We do not have that much time, Israel; we have to gather ourselves together, a nation that is not desired.
