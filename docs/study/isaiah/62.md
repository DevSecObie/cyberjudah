**[Isaiah 62:1-12](/bible/isaiah/62#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/62#v1)</sup> For Zion’s sake will I not hold my peace, and for Jerusalem’s sake I will not rest, until the righteousness thereof go forth as brightness, and the salvation thereof as a lamp that burneth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/62#v2)</sup> And the Gentiles shall see thy righteousness, and all kings thy glory: and thou shalt be called by a new name, which the mouth of the Lord shall name.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/62#v3)</sup> Thou shalt also be a crown of glory in the hand of the Lord, and a royal diadem in the hand of thy God.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/62#v4)</sup> Thou shalt no more be termed Forsaken; neither shall thy land any more be termed Desolate: but thou shalt be called Hephzi–bah, and thy land Beulah: for the Lord delighteth in thee, and thy land shall be married.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/62#v5)</sup> For as a young man marrieth a virgin, so shall thy sons marry thee: and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/62#v6)</sup> I have set watchmen upon thy walls, O Jerusalem, which shall never hold their peace day nor night: ye that make mention of the Lord, keep not silence,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/62#v7)</sup> And give him no rest, till he establish, and till he make Jerusalem a praise in the earth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/62#v8)</sup> The Lord hath sworn by his right hand, and by the arm of his strength, Surely I will no more give thy corn to be meat for thine enemies; and the sons of the stranger shall not drink thy wine, for the which thou hast laboured:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/62#v9)</sup> But they that have gathered it shall eat it, and praise the Lord; and they that have brought it together shall drink it in the courts of my holiness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/62#v10)</sup> Go through, go through the gates; prepare ye the way of the people; cast up, cast up the highway; gather out the stones; lift up a standard for the people.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/62#v11)</sup> Behold, the Lord hath proclaimed unto the end of the world, Say ye to the daughter of Zion, Behold, thy salvation cometh; behold, his reward is with him, and his work before him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/62#v12)</sup> And they shall call them, The holy people, The redeemed of the Lord: and thou shalt be called, Sought out, A city not forsaken.
