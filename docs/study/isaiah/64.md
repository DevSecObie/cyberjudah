**[Isaiah 64:1-12](/bible/isaiah/64#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/64#v1)</sup> Oh that thou wouldest rend the heavens, that thou wouldest come down, that the mountains might flow down at thy presence,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/64#v2)</sup> As when the melting fire burneth, the fire causeth the waters to boil, to make thy name known to thine adversaries, that the nations may tremble at thy presence!
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/64#v3)</sup> When thou didst terrible things which we looked not for, thou camest down, the mountains flowed down at thy presence.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/64#v4)</sup> For since the beginning of the world men have not heard, nor perceived by the ear, neither hath the eye seen, O God, beside thee, what he hath prepared for him that waiteth for him.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/64#v5)</sup> Thou meetest him that rejoiceth and worketh righteousness, those that remember thee in thy ways: behold, thou art wroth; for we have sinned: in those is continuance, and we shall be saved.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/64#v6)</sup> But we are all as an unclean thing, and all our righteousnesses are as filthy rags; and we all do fade as a leaf; and our iniquities, like the wind, have taken us away.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/64#v7)</sup> And there is none that calleth upon thy name, that stirreth up himself to take hold of thee: for thou hast hid thy face from us, and hast consumed us, because of our iniquities.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/64#v8)</sup> But now, O Lord, thou art our father; we are the clay, and thou our potter; and we all are the work of thy hand.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/64#v9)</sup> Be not wroth very sore, O Lord, neither remember iniquity for ever: behold, see, we beseech thee, we are all thy people.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/64#v10)</sup> Thy holy cities are a wilderness, Zion is a wilderness, Jerusalem a desolation.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/64#v11)</sup> Our holy and our beautiful house, where our fathers praised thee, is burned up with fire: and all our pleasant things are laid waste.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/64#v12)</sup> Wilt thou refrain thyself for these things, O Lord? wilt thou hold thy peace, and afflict us very sore?
