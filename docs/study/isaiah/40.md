**[Isaiah 40:1-8](/bible/isaiah/40#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/40#v1)</sup> Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/40#v2)</sup> Speak ye comfortably to Jerusalem, and cry unto her, that her warfare is accomplished, that her iniquity is pardoned: for she hath received of the Lord’s hand double for all her sins.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/40#v3)</sup> The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make straight in the desert a highway for our God.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/40#v4)</sup> Every valley shall be exalted, and every mountain and hill shall be made low: and the crooked shall be made straight, and the rough places plain:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/40#v5)</sup> And the glory of the Lord shall be revealed, and all flesh shall see it together: for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/40#v6)</sup> The voice said, Cry. And he said, What shall I cry? All flesh is grass, and all the goodliness thereof is as the flower of the field:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/40#v7)</sup> The grass withereth, the flower fadeth: because the spirit of the Lord bloweth upon it: surely the people is grass.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/40#v8)</sup> The grass withereth, the flower fadeth: but the word of our God shall stand for ever.

- Comfort ye, comfort ye my people, saith your God. The word is to speak comfortably to Jerusalem and cry that her warfare is accomplished and her iniquity pardoned, for she has received of the Lord's hand double for all her sins.
- The voice crying in the wilderness to prepare the way of the Lord goes into the prophecy of John, the spirit of Elijah, with every valley exalted, every mountain and hill made low, and the crooked made straight so the glory of the Lord shall be revealed and all flesh shall see it together.
- All flesh is grass and its goodliness as the flower of the field; the grass withers and the flower fades, but the word of our God stands forever. It compares our lives to the flowers of the field: we are born, we grow, we die, and go back into the ground, but the word of the Most High is forever.
  - **[Malachi 4:5](/bible/malachi/4#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/malachi/4#v5)</sup> Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord:

    The Most High promised to send Elijah before the great day, the spirit that comes in John.
  - **[Matthew 3:3](/bible/matthew/3#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/3#v3)</sup> For this is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.

    John is the voice of one crying in the wilderness, prepare ye the way of the Lord.

**[Isaiah 40:9-11](/bible/isaiah/40#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/40#v9)</sup> O Zion, that bringest good tidings, get thee up into the high mountain; O Jerusalem, that bringest good tidings, lift up thy voice with strength; lift it up, be not afraid; say unto the cities of Judah, Behold your God!
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/40#v10)</sup> Behold, the Lord God will come with strong hand, and his arm shall rule for him: behold, his reward is with him, and his work before him.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/40#v11)</sup> He shall feed his flock like a shepherd: he shall gather the lambs with his arm, and carry them in his bosom, and shall gently lead those that are with young.

- Zion is told to get up to the high mountain and lift up her voice with strength, not afraid, to say to the cities of Judah, Behold your God. The Lord God will come with a strong hand, his arm ruling for him, his reward with him and his work before him.
- He shall feed his flock like a shepherd, gather the lambs with his arm and carry them in his bosom, and gently lead those that are with young.

**[Isaiah 40:12-17](/bible/isaiah/40#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/40#v12)</sup> Who hath measured the waters in the hollow of his hand, and meted out heaven with the span, and comprehended the dust of the earth in a measure, and weighed the mountains in scales, and the hills in a balance?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/40#v13)</sup> Who hath directed the Spirit of the Lord, or being his counsellor hath taught him?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/40#v14)</sup> With whom took he counsel, and who instructed him, and taught him in the path of judgment, and taught him knowledge, and shewed to him the way of understanding?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/40#v15)</sup> Behold, the nations are as a drop of a bucket, and are counted as the small dust of the balance: behold, he taketh up the isles as a very little thing.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/40#v16)</sup> And Lebanon is not sufficient to burn, nor the beasts thereof sufficient for a burnt offering.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/40#v17)</sup> All nations before him are as nothing; and they are counted to him less than nothing, and vanity.

- The Most High measured the waters in the hollow of his hand, meted out heaven with a span, and weighed the mountains in scales. No one directed his Spirit or taught him counsel, judgment, knowledge, or the way of understanding.
- All nations are as a drop of a bucket and counted as the small dust of the balance. Lebanon is not sufficient to burn nor its beasts sufficient for the offering; all nations before him are as nothing, counted less than nothing and vanity.
  - **[2 Esdras 6:56](/bible/2-esdras/6#v56)**
    > <sup>[56](/bible/2-esdras/6#v56)</sup> As for the other people, which also come of Adam, thou hast said that they are nothing, but be like unto spittle: and hast likened the abundance of them unto a drop that falleth from a vessel.

    A strong precept that all the nations are nothing to the Most High, likened to a drop from a vessel.

**[Isaiah 40:18-20](/bible/isaiah/40#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/40#v18)</sup> To whom then will ye liken God? or what likeness will ye compare unto him?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/40#v19)</sup> The workman melteth a graven image, and the goldsmith spreadeth it over with gold, and casteth silver chains.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/40#v20)</sup> He that is so impoverished that he hath no oblation chooseth a tree that will not rot; he seeketh unto him a cunning workman to prepare a graven image, that shall not be moved.

- To whom then will you liken God, or what likeness compare to him? The workman melts a graven image, the goldsmith spreads it with gold and casts silver chains.
- He that is too poor for such an offering chooses a tree that will not rot and seeks a skilled workman to prepare a graven image that will not be moved. Seeking a tree is the spirit of Nimrod that our people worshipped back then; instead of the tree itself, he made a graven image of the tree.

**[Isaiah 40:21-26](/bible/isaiah/40#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/40#v21)</sup> Have ye not known? have ye not heard? hath it not been told you from the beginning? have ye not understood from the foundations of the earth?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/40#v22)</sup> It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers; that stretcheth out the heavens as a curtain, and spreadeth them out as a tent to dwell in:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/40#v23)</sup> That bringeth the princes to nothing; he maketh the judges of the earth as vanity.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/40#v24)</sup> Yea, they shall not be planted; yea, they shall not be sown: yea, their stock shall not take root in the earth: and he shall also blow upon them, and they shall wither, and the whirlwind shall take them away as stubble.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/40#v25)</sup> To whom then will ye liken me, or shall I be equal? saith the Holy One.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/isaiah/40#v26)</sup> Lift up your eyes on high, and behold who hath created these things, that bringeth out their host by number: he calleth them all by names by the greatness of his might, for that he is strong in power; not one faileth.

- Have you not known, have you not heard from the beginning? It is he that sits upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants are as grasshoppers; he stretches out the heavens as a curtain and spreads them as a tent to dwell in. The circle of the earth is the two-dimensional shape of a sphere.
- He brings the princes to nothing and makes the judges of the earth as vanity; they are not planted or sown before he blows upon them and the whirlwind takes them away as stubble.
- Lift up your eyes on high and behold who created these things, bringing out their host by number and calling them all by name; by the greatness of his might, not one fails.

**[Isaiah 40:27-31](/bible/isaiah/40#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/isaiah/40#v27)</sup> Why sayest thou, O Jacob, and speakest, O Israel, My way is hid from the Lord, and my judgment is passed over from my God?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/isaiah/40#v28)</sup> Hast thou not known? hast thou not heard, that the everlasting God, the Lord, the Creator of the ends of the earth, fainteth not, neither is weary? there is no searching of his understanding.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/isaiah/40#v29)</sup> He giveth power to the faint; and to them that have no might he increaseth strength.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/isaiah/40#v30)</sup> Even the youths shall faint and be weary, and the young men shall utterly fall:
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/isaiah/40#v31)</sup> But they that wait upon the Lord shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as eagles; they shall run, and not be weary; and they shall walk, and not faint.

- Why does Jacob say his way is hid from the Lord and his judgment passed over from his God? The everlasting God, the Creator of the ends of the earth, faints not, is not weary, and there is no searching of his understanding.
- He gives power to the faint and increases strength to them that have no might. Even the youths faint and the young men utterly fall, but they that wait upon the Lord renew their strength.
- This goes into Israel receiving the kingdom and the power of the Lord. Everybody may get weary and faint, but the Lord does not, and those that trust in him mount up with wings as eagles, run and are not weary, walk and do not faint.
