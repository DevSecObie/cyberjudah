Read the chapter → [Isaiah 42](/bible/isaiah/42)

This session reads the four chapters [Isaiah 42](/bible/isaiah/42) through 45. The thread that ties them together is the servant: the chapter opens on the Most High's elect servant in whom his soul delights, moving into Christ, and then declares plainly that the servant, the witness, and the chosen is Israel. Along the way the teaching drives home who the "gentiles" are, that Israel was robbed and spoiled and hidden in prison houses, that the idols of the nations are nothing but wood and stone, that our name was changed in slavery as prophesied, and that the Most High alone forms light and creates darkness, makes peace and creates evil.

**[Isaiah 42:1-4](/bible/isaiah/42#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/42#v1)</sup> Behold my servant, whom I uphold; mine elect, in whom my soul delighteth; I have put my spirit upon him: he shall bring forth judgment to the Gentiles.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/42#v2)</sup> He shall not cry, nor lift up, nor cause his voice to be heard in the street.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/42#v3)</sup> A bruised reed shall he not break, and the smoking flax shall he not quench: he shall bring forth judgment unto truth.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/42#v4)</sup> He shall not fail nor be discouraged, till he have set judgment in the earth: and the isles shall wait for his law.

- Behold my servant whom I uphold, my elect in whom my soul delighteth; the Most High put his spirit upon him and he shall bring forth judgment to the gentiles. This goes into Christ, who shall not cry nor lift up nor cause his voice to be heard in the street.
- A bruised reed he shall not break and the smoking flax he shall not quench; he shall bring forth judgment unto truth. He shall not fail nor be discouraged till he has set judgment in the earth, and the isles shall wait for his law.

    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/4#v16)</sup> The people which sat in darkness saw great light; and to them which sat in the region and shadow of death light is sprung up.

    Zebulun and Naphtali, Israelite tribes, are called the land and the region of the gentiles, showing the gentiles here are the children of Israel.
  - **[Matthew 4:17](/bible/matthew/4#v17)**

    From that time Jesus began to preach repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand, preaching to those same tribes called gentiles.

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/42#v5)</sup> Thus saith God the Lord, he that created the heavens, and stretched them out; he that spread forth the earth, and that which cometh out of it; he that giveth breath unto the people upon it, and spirit to them that walk therein:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/42#v6)</sup> I the Lord have called thee in righteousness, and will hold thine hand, and will keep thee, and give thee for a covenant of the people, for a light of the Gentiles;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/42#v7)</sup> To open the blind eyes, to bring out the prisoners from the prison, and them that sit in darkness out of the prison house.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/42#v8)</sup> I am the Lord: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/42#v9)</sup> Behold, the former things are come to pass, and new things do I declare: before they spring forth I tell you of them.

- Thus saith God the Lord, he that created the heavens and stretched them out, that spread forth the earth, that giveth breath unto the people upon it and spirit to them that walk therein.
- I the Lord have called thee in righteousness and will hold thine hand and keep thee, and give thee for a covenant of the people, for a light of the gentiles, to open the blind eyes, to bring out the prisoners from the prison and them that sit in darkness out of the prison house.
- I am the Lord, that is my name, and my glory will I not give to another nor my praise to graven images. He declares the end from the beginning: before the new things spring forth he tells them to you.

>

Let the wilderness and the cities, the villages of Kedar, and the inhabitants of the rock lift up their voice and shout from the top of the mountains, giving glory unto the Lord.
- The Lord shall go forth as a mighty man and stir up jealousy like a man of war; he shall cry, yea roar, and prevail against his enemies.

**[Isaiah 42:14-17](/bible/isaiah/42#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/42#v14)</sup> I have long time holden my peace; I have been still, and refrained myself: now will I cry like a travailing woman; I will destroy and devour at once.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/42#v15)</sup> I will make waste mountains and hills, and dry up all their herbs; and I will make the rivers islands, and I will dry up the pools.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/42#v16)</sup> And I will bring the blind by a way that they knew not; I will lead them in paths that they have not known: I will make darkness light before them, and crooked things straight. These things will I do unto them, and not forsake them.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/42#v17)</sup> They shall be turned back, they shall be greatly ashamed, that trust in graven images, that say to the molten images, Ye are our gods.

- I have long time held my peace, been still and refrained myself, but now will I cry like a travailing woman and destroy and devour at once. The Most High holds back because all the prophecies in this bible must be fulfilled, and once that happens it is going down.
- I will make waste mountains and hills, dry up the herbs, and make the rivers islands. I will bring the blind by a way they knew not, make darkness light before them and crooked things straight, and not forsake them.
- They shall be turned back and greatly ashamed that trust in graven images, that say to the molten images, ye are our gods.

    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/20#v2)</sup> I am the Lord thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)</sup> Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

    > <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/20#v5)</sup> Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me;

    Make no graven image to bow down and serve it, for the Lord thy God is a jealous God; it is not that images cannot be made, since angels stood in the temple, but that they must not be made to worship them.

**[Isaiah 42:18-21](/bible/isaiah/42#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/42#v18)</sup> Hear, ye deaf; and look, ye blind, that ye may see.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/42#v19)</sup> Who is blind, but my servant? or deaf, as my messenger that I sent? who is blind as he that is perfect, and blind as the Lord’s servant?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/42#v20)</sup> Seeing many things, but thou observest not; opening the ears, but he heareth not.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/42#v21)</sup> The Lord is well pleased for his righteousness’ sake; he will magnify the law, and make it honourable.

- Hear ye deaf and look ye blind that ye may see; who is blind but my servant, or deaf as my messenger that I sent? The Lord is well pleased for his righteousness' sake and will magnify the law and make it honorable.
- This is why Christ came magnifying the law: ye heard it said thou shalt not commit adultery, but whoever looks on a woman to lust after her has committed adultery already; and when they questioned him on divorce he said in the beginning it was not so.

**[Isaiah 42:22-25](/bible/isaiah/42#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/42#v22)</sup> But this is a people robbed and spoiled; they are all of them snared in holes, and they are hid in prison houses: they are for a prey, and none delivereth; for a spoil, and none saith, Restore.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/42#v23)</sup> Who among you will give ear to this? who will hearken and hear for the time to come?
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/42#v24)</sup> Who gave Jacob for a spoil, and Israel to the robbers? did not the Lord, he against whom we have sinned? for they would not walk in his ways, neither were they obedient unto his law.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/42#v25)</sup> Therefore he hath poured upon him the fury of his anger, and the strength of battle: and it hath set him on fire round about, yet he knew not; and it burned him, yet he laid it not to heart.

- But this is a people robbed and spoiled, all of them snared in holes and hidden in prison houses; they are for a prey and none delivereth, a spoil and none saith restore. This is talking about us the Israelites: the other nations come and set up in our communities, sell their goods, and take the money back, preying on us and spoiling us like the spoils of war.
- Who among you will give ear to this? Who gave Jacob for a spoil and Israel to the robbers? Did not the Lord, he against whom we have sinned, for they would not walk in his ways nor be obedient to his law.
- Therefore he poured upon him the fury of his anger and the strength of battle, and it set him on fire round about, yet he knew not, and it burned him, yet he laid it not to heart. We were consumed for our sins on the slave ships but laid it not to heart, not considering that the Most High punished us and brought us over to turn us back to our maker.
