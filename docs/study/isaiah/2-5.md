Read the chapter → [Isaiah 2](/bible/isaiah/2)

This session covers [Isaiah 2](/bible/isaiah/2) through 5. The prophet opens with the last days, when the government of the Most High will be established above every other government and all nations flow to it to learn His law. From that height the teaching falls to the charge against the nation: witchcraft, idolatry, and interracial marriages have filled the land with idols, so the day of the Lord comes to humble every proud thing and utterly abolish the idols. Chapters 3 and 4 show the nation turned upside down, its women and children out of order, and its beauty stripped away, before a remnant is washed and preserved. Chapter 5 closes with the parable of the vineyard, the house of Israel that brought forth wild grapes and was given up to captivity.

**[Isaiah 2:1-4](/bible/isaiah/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/2#v1)</sup> The word that Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/2#v2)</sup> And it shall come to pass in the last days, that the mountain of the Lord’s house shall be established in the top of the mountains, and shall be exalted above the hills; and all nations shall flow unto it.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/2#v3)</sup> And many people shall go and say, Come ye, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, to the house of the God of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for out of Zion shall go forth the law, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/2#v4)</sup> And he shall judge among the nations, and shall rebuke many people: and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.

- The word Isaiah the son of Amoz saw concerns Judah and Jerusalem: in the last days the mountain of the Lord's house shall be established in the top of the mountains. Mountains here means government; the government of the Most High will be on top of every other government, and all nations shall flow to it.
- Many people shall say, come, let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, and He will teach us of His ways. Out of Zion shall go forth the law, because everybody on the face of the planet will bow the knee and obey the laws, statutes, and commandments of the Most High.
- He shall judge among the nations and rebuke many people, and they shall beat their swords into plowshares and their spears into pruninghooks. The nations will not be warring like they are now; Christ is going to come, set their behind straight, and that will be a wrap.

**[Isaiah 2:5-9](/bible/isaiah/2#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/2#v5)</sup> O house of Jacob, come ye, and let us walk in the light of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/2#v6)</sup> Therefore thou hast forsaken thy people the house of Jacob, because they be replenished from the east, and are soothsayers like the Philistines, and they please themselves in the children of strangers.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/2#v7)</sup> Their land also is full of silver and gold, neither is there any end of their treasures; their land is also full of horses, neither is there any end of their chariots:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/2#v8)</sup> Their land also is full of idols; they worship the work of their own hands, that which their own fingers have made:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/2#v9)</sup> And the mean man boweth down, and the great man humbleth himself: therefore forgive them not.

- "O house of Jacob, come ye, and let us walk in the light of the LORD." This is the call to the nation to return to the ways of the Most High.
- He forsook His people because they are replenished from the east, are soothsayers like the Philistines, and please themselves in the children of strangers. This means the nation was involved in witchcraft, idolatry, and interracial marriages.
- Their land is full of silver, gold, horses, chariots, and idols, and they worship the work of their own hands, that which their own fingers have made. Because of that idolatry a whole lot of bad things happened to us, and He says forgive them not.

**[Isaiah 2:10-18](/bible/isaiah/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/2#v10)</sup> Enter into the rock, and hide thee in the dust, for fear of the Lord, and for the glory of his majesty.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/2#v11)</sup> The lofty looks of man shall be humbled, and the haughtiness of men shall be bowed down, and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/2#v12)</sup> For the day of the Lord of hosts shall be upon every one that is proud and lofty, and upon every one that is lifted up; and he shall be brought low:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/2#v13)</sup> And upon all the cedars of Lebanon, that are high and lifted up, and upon all the oaks of Bashan,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/2#v14)</sup> And upon all the high mountains, and upon all the hills that are lifted up,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/2#v15)</sup> And upon every high tower, and upon every fenced wall,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/2#v16)</sup> And upon all the ships of Tarshish, and upon all pleasant pictures.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/2#v17)</sup> And the loftiness of man shall be bowed down, and the haughtiness of men shall be made low: and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/2#v18)</sup> And the idols he shall utterly abolish.

- "Enter into the rock, and hide thee in the dust, for fear of the LORD, and for the glory of his majesty." The lofty looks of man shall be humbled and the haughtiness of men bowed down, and the Lord alone shall be exalted in that day.
- The day of the Lord of hosts is upon everyone proud and lofty, upon the cedars of Lebanon, the oaks of Bashan, the high mountains, every high tower, every fenced wall, the ships of Tarshish, and all pleasant pictures. When the Lord comes back, destruction is coming to everything and everyone.
- We are going to rule over the nations with a rod of iron, and everybody that does not submit will be dealt with severely. The idols He shall utterly abolish.

**[Isaiah 2:19](/bible/isaiah/2#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/2#v19)</sup> And they shall go into the holes of the rocks, and into the caves of the earth, for fear of the Lord, and for the glory of his majesty, when he ariseth to shake terribly the earth.

- They shall go into the holes of the rocks and the caves of the earth for the fear of the Lord when He arises to shake terribly the earth. This is Esau trying to hide in the doomsday bunkers he is building for that day, but it is not going to work.

**[Isaiah 2:20-22](/bible/isaiah/2#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/2#v20)</sup> In that day a man shall cast his idols of silver, and his idols of gold, which they made each one for himself to worship, to the moles and to the bats;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/2#v21)</sup> To go into the clefts of the rocks, and into the tops of the ragged rocks, for fear of the Lord, and for the glory of his majesty, when he ariseth to shake terribly the earth.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/2#v22)</sup> Cease ye from man, whose breath is in his nostrils: for wherein is he to be accounted of?

- In that day a man shall cast his idols of silver and gold, which he made to worship, to the moles and to the bats, and flee into the clefts of the rocks for fear of the Lord when He arises to shake terribly the earth.
- When the Lord comes, people will cast away all the idols they love, the Jesus pieces and the crosses and all of that, because it is going to be shown to have no profit. Cease from man, whose breath is in his nostrils, for wherein is he to be accounted of.

# Isaiah 3: The Nation Turned Upside Down

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 3](/bible/isaiah/3)

**[Isaiah 3:1-4](/bible/isaiah/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/3#v1)</sup> For, behold, the Lord, the Lord of hosts, doth take away from Jerusalem and from Judah the stay and the staff, the whole stay of bread, and the whole stay of water,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/3#v2)</sup> The mighty man, and the man of war, the judge, and the prophet, and the prudent, and the ancient,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/3#v3)</sup> The captain of fifty, and the honourable man, and the counsellor, and the cunning artificer, and the eloquent orator.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/3#v4)</sup> And I will give children to be their princes, and babes shall rule over them.

- The Lord of hosts takes away from Jerusalem and Judah the stay and the staff, the whole stay of bread and water, the mighty man, the man of war, the judge, the prophet, the prudent, the ancient, the captain of fifty, the honourable man, the counsellor, the cunning artificer, and the eloquent orator. He turned our nation upside down.
- "I will give children to be their princes, and babes shall rule over them." This is exactly what we have today, and it is why our nation is so out of order.

**[Isaiah 3:5-9](/bible/isaiah/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/3#v5)</sup> And the people shall be oppressed, every one by another, and every one by his neighbour: the child shall behave himself proudly against the ancient, and the base against the honourable.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/3#v6)</sup> When a man shall take hold of his brother of the house of his father, saying, Thou hast clothing, be thou our ruler, and let this ruin be under thy hand:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/3#v7)</sup> In that day shall he swear, saying, I will not be an healer; for in my house is neither bread nor clothing: make me not a ruler of the people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/3#v8)</sup> For Jerusalem is ruined, and Judah is fallen: because their tongue and their doings are against the Lord, to provoke the eyes of his glory.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/3#v9)</sup> The shew of their countenance doth witness against them; and they declare their sin as Sodom, they hide it not. Woe unto their soul! for they have rewarded evil unto themselves.

- The people shall be oppressed, every one by another and every one by his neighbour, and the child shall behave himself proudly against the ancient and the base against the honourable. This comes from our idolatry, witchcraft, and interracial marriages, which give us disrespectful children and houses out of order.
- A man takes hold of his brother to make him ruler, but the brother refuses, saying he will not be a healer, for in his house is neither bread nor clothing. Jerusalem is ruined and Judah is fallen because their tongue and their doings are against the Lord; there is always a payment for sin.
- The show of their countenance witnesses against them, and they declare their sin as Sodom and hide it not. You can know a sodomite when you see him, whatever he wants to call himself, because his whole countenance, the way he walks, acts, and talks, shows it; they are flamboyant.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 19:29](/bible/sirach/19#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/19#v29)</sup> A man may be known by his look, and one that hath understanding by his countenance, when thou meetest him.

    A man is known by his look and countenance, so the sin declares itself.

**[Isaiah 3:10-12](/bible/isaiah/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/3#v10)</sup> Say ye to the righteous, that it shall be well with him: for they shall eat the fruit of their doings.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/3#v11)</sup> Woe unto the wicked! it shall be ill with him: for the reward of his hands shall be given him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/3#v12)</sup> As for my people, children are their oppressors, and women rule over them. O my people, they which lead thee cause thee to err, and destroy the way of thy paths.

- Say to the righteous that it shall be well with him, for they shall eat the fruit of their doings; woe to the wicked, for the reward of his hands shall be given him.
- As for my people, children are their oppressors and women rule over them. Children are the ones gang-banging, stealing cars, and murdering people, and the women are leading the households with no father in sight; they which lead the people cause them to err.

**[Isaiah 3:13-15](/bible/isaiah/3#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/3#v13)</sup> The Lord standeth up to plead, and standeth to judge the people.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/3#v14)</sup> The Lord will enter into judgment with the ancients of his people, and the princes thereof: for ye have eaten up the vineyard; the spoil of the poor is in your houses.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/3#v15)</sup> What mean ye that ye beat my people to pieces, and grind the faces of the poor? saith the Lord God of hosts.

- The Lord stands up to plead and stands to judge the people, and He will enter into judgment with the ancients of His people and the princes thereof, for they have eaten up the vineyard and the spoil of the poor is in their houses.
- "What mean ye that ye beat my people to pieces, and grind the faces of the poor?" saith the Lord of hosts.

**[Isaiah 3:16-17](/bible/isaiah/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/3#v16)</sup> Moreover the Lord saith, Because the daughters of Zion are haughty, and walk with stretched forth necks and wanton eyes, walking and mincing as they go, and making a tinkling with their feet:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/3#v17)</sup> Therefore the Lord will smite with a scab the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion, and the Lord will discover their secret parts.

- The daughters of Zion are haughty and walk with stretched-forth necks and wanton eyes, mincing as they go and making a tinkling with their feet. That is our sisters walking with straight-up attitude.
- Therefore the Lord will smite with a scab the crown of the head of the daughters of Zion and discover their secret parts. This is the plagues of the women; that is why a lot of our sisters' hair does not grow, because the Lord smote them with a scab on the top of the head.

**[Isaiah 3:18-24](/bible/isaiah/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/3#v18)</sup> In that day the Lord will take away the bravery of their tinkling ornaments about their feet, and their cauls, and their round tires like the moon,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/3#v19)</sup> The chains, and the bracelets, and the mufflers,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/3#v20)</sup> The bonnets, and the ornaments of the legs, and the headbands, and the tablets, and the earrings,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/3#v21)</sup> The rings, and nose jewels,
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/3#v22)</sup> The changeable suits of apparel, and the mantles, and the wimples, and the crisping pins,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/3#v23)</sup> The glasses, and the fine linen, and the hoods, and the vails.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/3#v24)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that instead of sweet smell there shall be stink; and instead of a girdle a rent; and instead of well set hair baldness; and instead of a stomacher a girding of sackcloth; and burning instead of beauty.

- In that day the Lord will take away the bravery of their ornaments: the tinkling ornaments about the feet, the cauls, the round tires like the moon that are the big head wraps, the chains, bracelets, mufflers, bonnets, leg ornaments, headbands, tablets, earrings, rings, nose jewels, changeable suits of apparel, mantles, wimples, crisping pins, glasses, fine linen, hoods, and veils.
- Instead of sweet smell there shall be stink, instead of well-set hair baldness, and burning instead of beauty. That is why our sisters go through the perms, the straightening and ironing, the chemical burns, and the body odor and other issues, because of the sins of our people.
- Everything He describes taking away is the beauty and the renown our sisters once held.

**[Isaiah 3:25-26](/bible/isaiah/3#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/3#v25)</sup> Thy men shall fall by the sword, and thy mighty in the war.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/isaiah/3#v26)</sup> And her gates shall lament and mourn; and she being desolate shall sit upon the ground.

- Thy men shall fall by the sword and thy mighty men in the war, and her gates shall lament and mourn, and she being desolate shall sit upon the ground. It is a horrible thing, and when we come back as the Israelites we are supposed to be, we have to appreciate the beauty and renown the Lord gave us.

# Isaiah 4: The Branch of the Lord and the Cleansed Remnant

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 4](/bible/isaiah/4)

**[Isaiah 4:1](/bible/isaiah/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/4#v1)</sup> And in that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, We will eat our own bread, and wear our own apparel: only let us be called by thy name, to take away our reproach.

- In that day seven women shall take hold of one man, saying, we will eat our own bread and wear our own apparel, only let us be called by thy name to take away our reproach. There will be no independent women in that day.

**[Isaiah 4:2-4](/bible/isaiah/4#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/4#v2)</sup> In that day shall the branch of the Lord be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth shall be excellent and comely for them that are escaped of Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/4#v3)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that he that is left in Zion, and he that remaineth in Jerusalem, shall be called holy, even every one that is written among the living in Jerusalem:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/4#v4)</sup> When the Lord shall have washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion, and shall have purged the blood of Jerusalem from the midst thereof by the spirit of judgment, and by the spirit of burning.

- In that day the branch of the Lord shall be beautiful and glorious, and the fruit of the earth excellent and comely for them that are escaped of Israel.
- He that is left in Zion and remaineth in Jerusalem shall be called holy, everyone written among the living, when the Lord has washed away the filth of the daughters of Zion and purged the blood of Jerusalem by the spirit of judgment and the spirit of burning. This is being saved from the destruction.

**[Isaiah 4:5-6](/bible/isaiah/4#v5)**

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/4#v6)</sup> And there shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the daytime from the heat, and for a place of refuge, and for a covert from storm and from rain.

- There shall be a tabernacle for a shadow in the daytime from the heat, and for a place of refuge and a covert from storm and from rain.

# Isaiah 5: The Song of the Vineyard

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 5](/bible/isaiah/5)

**[Isaiah 5:1-2](/bible/isaiah/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/5#v1)</sup> Now will I sing to my wellbeloved a song of my beloved touching his vineyard. My wellbeloved hath a vineyard in a very fruitful hill:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/5#v2)</sup> And he fenced it, and gathered out the stones thereof, and planted it with the choicest vine, and built a tower in the midst of it, and also made a winepress therein: and he looked that it should bring forth grapes, and it brought forth wild grapes.

- The prophet sings a song of his well-beloved touching His vineyard in a very fruitful hill. He fenced it, gathered out the stones, planted it with the choicest vine, built a tower in the midst, and made a winepress in it, and He looked that it should bring forth grapes, but it brought forth wild grapes.

**[Isaiah 5:3-7](/bible/isaiah/5#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/5#v3)</sup> And now, O inhabitants of Jerusalem, and men of Judah, judge, I pray you, betwixt me and my vineyard.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/5#v4)</sup> What could have been done more to my vineyard, that I have not done in it? wherefore, when I looked that it should bring forth grapes, brought it forth wild grapes?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/5#v5)</sup> And now go to; I will tell you what I will do to my vineyard: I will take away the hedge thereof, and it shall be eaten up; and break down the wall thereof, and it shall be trodden down:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/5#v6)</sup> And I will lay it waste: it shall not be pruned, nor digged; but there shall come up briers and thorns: I will also command the clouds that they rain no rain upon it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/5#v7)</sup> For the vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah his pleasant plant: and he looked for judgment, but behold oppression; for righteousness, but behold a cry.

- The Lord asks the inhabitants of Jerusalem and the men of Judah to judge between Him and His vineyard: what more could He have done that He has not done in it, and why, when He looked for grapes, did it bring forth wild grapes.
- Therefore He will take away the hedge and it shall be eaten up, break down the wall and it shall be trodden down, lay it waste unpruned and undug so that briars and thorns come up, and command the clouds to rain no rain upon it. The vineyard of the Lord of hosts is the house of Israel, and the men of Judah His pleasant plant; He looked for judgment but behold oppression, for righteousness but behold a cry.
- The vineyard is the nation of Israel. Because of our sins, the wild grapes, we became the degenerate fruit of a strange plant; and like a vineyard left unkempt with the fences taken down, everybody came in and picked it apart. That is what happened to Israel: given up to the nations, trodden down, invaded numerous times, brought into captivity and brought very low.

**[Isaiah 5:8-10](/bible/isaiah/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/5#v8)</sup> Woe unto them that join house to house, that lay field to field, till there be no place, that they may be placed alone in the midst of the earth!
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/5#v9)</sup> In mine ears said the Lord of hosts, Of a truth many houses shall be desolate, even great and fair, without inhabitant.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/5#v10)</sup> Yea, ten acres of vineyard shall yield one bath, and the seed of an homer shall yield an ephah.

- Woe to them that join house to house and lay field to field, till there is no place that they may be placed alone in the midst of the earth. Many houses shall be desolate, even great and fair, without inhabitant.
- Ten acres of vineyard shall yield one bath and the seed of a homer shall yield an ephah, meaning you will not get as many things from your crops as you used to.

**[Isaiah 5:11-12](/bible/isaiah/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/5#v11)</sup> Woe unto them that rise up early in the morning, that they may follow strong drink; that continue until night, till wine inflame them!
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/5#v12)</sup> And the harp, and the viol, the tabret, and pipe, and wine, are in their feasts: but they regard not the work of the Lord, neither consider the operation of his hands.

- Woe to them that rise up early to follow strong drink and continue until night till wine inflames them. They had winos back then, the same people you can find in front of any corner store in the hood getting drunk and laying in the middle of the street.
- The harp, the viol, the tabret, the pipe, and wine are in their feasts, but they regard not the work of the Lord nor consider the operation of His hands.

**[Isaiah 5:13-14](/bible/isaiah/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/5#v13)</sup> Therefore my people are gone into captivity, because they have no knowledge: and their honourable men are famished, and their multitude dried up with thirst.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/5#v14)</sup> Therefore hell hath enlarged herself, and opened her mouth without measure: and their glory, and their multitude, and their pomp, and he that rejoiceth, shall descend into it.

- Therefore my people are gone into captivity because they have no knowledge, and their honourable men are famished and their multitude dried up with thirst.
- Therefore hell hath enlarged herself and opened her mouth without measure. In this context hell is captivity, which is why the verse before it says the people are gone into captivity because they have no knowledge.

**[Isaiah 5:15-17](/bible/isaiah/5#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/5#v15)</sup> And the mean man shall be brought down, and the mighty man shall be humbled, and the eyes of the lofty shall be humbled:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/5#v16)</sup> But the Lord of hosts shall be exalted in judgment, and God that is holy shall be sanctified in righteousness.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/5#v17)</sup> Then shall the lambs feed after their manner, and the waste places of the fat ones shall strangers eat.

- The mean man shall be brought down and the mighty man humbled, and the eyes of the lofty shall be humbled, but the Lord of hosts shall be exalted in judgment and the holy God sanctified in righteousness.
- Then shall the lambs feed after their manner, and the waste places of the fat ones shall strangers eat.

**[Isaiah 5:18-23](/bible/isaiah/5#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/5#v18)</sup> Woe unto them that draw iniquity with cords of vanity, and sin as it were with a cart rope:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/5#v19)</sup> That say, Let him make speed, and hasten his work, that we may see it: and let the counsel of the Holy One of Israel draw nigh and come, that we may know it!
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/5#v20)</sup> Woe unto them that call evil good, and good evil; that put darkness for light, and light for darkness; that put bitter for sweet, and sweet for bitter!
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/5#v21)</sup> Woe unto them that are wise in their own eyes, and prudent in their own sight!
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/5#v22)</sup> Woe unto them that are mighty to drink wine, and men of strength to mingle strong drink:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/5#v23)</sup> Which justify the wicked for reward, and take away the righteousness of the righteous from him!

- Woe to them that draw iniquity with cords of vanity and sin as it were with a cart rope, that say let Him make speed and hasten His work that we may see it.
- Woe to them that call evil good and good evil, that put darkness for light and bitter for sweet, that are wise in their own eyes, mighty to drink wine, and which justify the wicked for reward and take away the righteousness of the righteous. That is Esau, and it is hard to see how anyone can fight for Esau, who took away our history, our Bible, and our heritage.

**[Isaiah 5:24-25](/bible/isaiah/5#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/5#v24)</sup> Therefore as the fire devoureth the stubble, and the flame consumeth the chaff, so their root shall be as rottenness, and their blossom shall go up as dust: because they have cast away the law of the Lord of hosts, and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/isaiah/5#v25)</sup> Therefore is the anger of the Lord kindled against his people, and he hath stretched forth his hand against them, and hath smitten them: and the hills did tremble, and their carcases were torn in the midst of the streets. For all this his anger is not turned away, but his hand is stretched out still.

- As the fire devours the stubble and the flame consumes the chaff, so their root shall be as rottenness and their blossom go up as dust, because they cast away the law of the Lord of hosts and despised the word of the Holy One of Israel.
- Therefore the anger of the Lord is kindled against His people, and He has stretched forth His hand and smitten them, and the hills tremble and their carcasses are torn in the midst of the streets. For all this His anger is not turned away, but His hand is stretched out still, which means there is still an opportunity for repentance.

>
> <sup>[27](/bible/isaiah/5#v27)</sup> None shall be weary nor stumble among them; none shall slumber nor sleep; neither shall the girdle of their loins be loosed, nor the latchet of their shoes be broken:

> <sup>[28](/bible/isaiah/5#v28)</sup> Whose arrows are sharp, and all their bows bent, their horses’ hoofs shall be counted like flint, and their wheels like a whirlwind:

> <sup>[29](/bible/isaiah/5#v29)</sup> Their roaring shall be like a lion, they shall roar like young lions: yea, they shall roar, and lay hold of the prey, and shall carry it away safe, and none shall deliver it.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/isaiah/5#v30)</sup> And in that day they shall roar against them like the roaring of the sea: and if one look unto the land, behold darkness and sorrow, and the light is darkened in the heavens thereof.

- He will lift up an ensign to the nations from afar and hiss them from the end of the earth, and they shall come with speed swiftly, none weary nor stumbling, none slumbering nor sleeping, their loins girded and their shoes unbroken.
- Their arrows are sharp and their bows bent, their horses' hooves counted like flint and their wheels like a whirlwind, their roaring like a lion; they lay hold of the prey and carry it away safe, and none shall deliver it. In that day they shall roar like the roaring of the sea, and if one look unto the land, behold darkness and sorrow, and the light is darkened in the heavens thereof.
