Read the chapter → [Isaiah 58](/bible/isaiah/58)

This session reads the four chapters [Isaiah 58](/bible/isaiah/58) through 61. The teacher opens by saying he is not here to teach this time, only to read the scriptures straight through, because these are fairly short chapters. The reading moves from the true fast the Most High has chosen in chapter 58, to the iniquities that separate Israel from their God in chapter 59, to the call to arise and shine in the coming kingdom in chapter 60, and closes with the anointing to preach good tidings in chapter 61. The little exposition given is kept here under the verses it belongs to.

**[Isaiah 58:1-3](/bible/isaiah/58#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/58#v1)</sup> Cry aloud, spare not, lift up thy voice like a trumpet, and shew my people their transgression, and the house of Jacob their sins.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/58#v2)</sup> Yet they seek me daily, and delight to know my ways, as a nation that did righteousness, and forsook not the ordinance of their God: they ask of me the ordinances of justice; they take delight in approaching to God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/58#v3)</sup> Wherefore have we fasted, say they, and thou seest not? wherefore have we afflicted our soul, and thou takest no knowledge? Behold, in the day of your fast ye find pleasure, and exact all your labours.

- The Most High tells the prophet to cry aloud and spare not, to lift up his voice like a trumpet and show the people their transgression and the house of Jacob their sins.
- The people seek him daily and delight to know his ways as a nation that did righteousness, and they ask why they have fasted and afflicted their soul and he has taken no knowledge of it.

**[Isaiah 58:4-9](/bible/isaiah/58#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/58#v4)</sup> Behold, ye fast for strife and debate, and to smite with the fist of wickedness: ye shall not fast as ye do this day, to make your voice to be heard on high.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/58#v5)</sup> Is it such a fast that I have chosen? a day for a man to afflict his soul? is it to bow down his head as a bulrush, and to spread sackcloth and ashes under him? wilt thou call this a fast, and an acceptable day to the Lord?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/58#v6)</sup> Is not this the fast that I have chosen? to loose the bands of wickedness, to undo the heavy burdens, and to let the oppressed go free, and that ye break every yoke?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/58#v7)</sup> Is it not to deal thy bread to the hungry, and that thou bring the poor that are cast out to thy house? when thou seest the naked, that thou cover him; and that thou hide not thyself from thine own flesh?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/58#v8)</sup> Then shall thy light break forth as the morning, and thine health shall spring forth speedily: and thy righteousness shall go before thee; the glory of the Lord shall be thy rereward.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/58#v9)</sup> Then shalt thou call, and the Lord shall answer; thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Here I am. If thou take away from the midst of thee the yoke, the putting forth of the finger, and speaking vanity;

- Their fasting is for strife and debate and to smite with the fist of wickedness, and such a fast will not make their voice to be heard on high. The Most High is not looking for a man to bow his head like a bulrush and spread sackcloth and ashes.
- The fast he has chosen is to loose the bands of wickedness, undo the heavy burdens, let the oppressed go free, break every yoke, deal thy bread to the hungry, bring the poor that are cast out to thy house, and cover the naked.
- The reward is that thy light breaks forth as the morning, thy health springs forth speedily, thy righteousness goes before thee, and the glory of the Lord is thy reward; then thou shalt call and the Lord shall answer.
- "The putting forth of the finger" is when you blame other people. Somebody comes and says a brother did this or that, and instead of taking it, the brother says, "no, but what about her, look what she did." You have to take accountability for your own actions; this is about taking accountability.

**[Isaiah 58:10-12](/bible/isaiah/58#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/58#v10)</sup> And if thou draw out thy soul to the hungry, and satisfy the afflicted soul; then shall thy light rise in obscurity, and thy darkness be as the noonday:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/58#v11)</sup> And the Lord shall guide thee continually, and satisfy thy soul in drought, and make fat thy bones: and thou shalt be like a watered garden, and like a spring of water, whose waters fail not.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/58#v12)</sup> And they that shall be of thee shall build the old waste places: thou shalt raise up the foundations of many generations; and thou shalt be called, The repairer of the breach, The restorer of paths to dwell in.

- If thou draw out thy soul to the hungry and satisfy the afflicted soul, thy light shall rise in obscurity and thy darkness be as the noonday, and the Lord shall guide thee continually and make fat thy bones.
- Thou shalt be like a watered garden and a spring of water whose waters fail not, and they that come of thee shall build the old waste places and be called the repairer of the breach, the restorer of paths to dwell in.

**[Isaiah 58:13-14](/bible/isaiah/58#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/58#v13)</sup> If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy pleasure on my holy day; and call the sabbath a delight, the holy of the Lord, honourable; and shalt honour him, not doing thine own ways, nor finding thine own pleasure, nor speaking thine own words:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/58#v14)</sup> Then shalt thou delight thyself in the Lord; and I will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth, and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father: for the mouth of the Lord hath spoken it.

- If thou turn away thy foot from the sabbath, from doing thy own pleasure on his holy day, and call the sabbath a delight and honor him, then thou shalt delight thyself in the Lord.
- He will cause thee to ride upon the high places of the earth and feed thee with the heritage of Jacob thy father, for the mouth of the Lord has spoken it.

# Isaiah 59: Your Iniquities Have Separated You

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 59](/bible/isaiah/59)

**[Isaiah 59:1-8](/bible/isaiah/59#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/59#v1)</sup> Behold, the Lord’s hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/59#v2)</sup> But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/59#v3)</sup> For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, your tongue hath muttered perverseness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/59#v4)</sup> None calleth for justice, nor any pleadeth for truth: they trust in vanity, and speak lies; they conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/59#v5)</sup> They hatch cockatrice’ eggs, and weave the spider’s web: he that eateth of their eggs dieth, and that which is crushed breaketh out into a viper.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/59#v6)</sup> Their webs shall not become garments, neither shall they cover themselves with their works: their works are works of iniquity, and the act of violence is in their hands.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/59#v7)</sup> Their feet run to evil, and they make haste to shed innocent blood: their thoughts are thoughts of iniquity; wasting and destruction are in their paths.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/59#v8)</sup> The way of peace they know not; and there is no judgment in their goings: they have made them crooked paths: whosoever goeth therein shall not know peace.

- The Lord's hand is not shortened that it cannot save, neither his ear heavy that it cannot hear, but their iniquities have separated between them and their God, and their sins have hid his face from them.
- Their hands are defiled with blood, their lips speak lies, their tongue mutters perverseness, and none calls for justice nor pleads for truth; they trust in vanity and speak lies.
- They hatch cockatrice eggs and weave the spider's web, their feet run to evil and make haste to shed innocent blood, and the way of peace they know not.

**[Isaiah 59:9-15](/bible/isaiah/59#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/59#v9)</sup> Therefore is judgment far from us, neither doth justice overtake us: we wait for light, but behold obscurity; for brightness, but we walk in darkness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/59#v10)</sup> We grope for the wall like the blind, and we grope as if we had no eyes: we stumble at noonday as in the night; we are in desolate places as dead men.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/59#v11)</sup> We roar all like bears, and mourn sore like doves: we look for judgment, but there is none; for salvation, but it is far off from us.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/59#v12)</sup> For our transgressions are multiplied before thee, and our sins testify against us: for our transgressions are with us; and as for our iniquities, we know them;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/59#v13)</sup> In transgressing and lying against the Lord, and departing away from our God, speaking oppression and revolt, conceiving and uttering from the heart words of falsehood.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/59#v14)</sup> And judgment is turned away backward, and justice standeth afar off: for truth is fallen in the street, and equity cannot enter.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/59#v15)</sup> Yea, truth faileth; and he that departeth from evil maketh himself a prey: and the Lord saw it, and it displeased him that there was no judgment.

- Because of this, judgment is far from them and justice does not overtake them; they wait for light but behold obscurity, and grope for the wall like the blind.
- Their transgressions are multiplied before the Most High and their sins testify against them, for in transgressing and lying against the Lord and departing away from their God, judgment is turned away backward and justice stands afar off.
- Truth fails, and he that departs from evil makes himself a prey; the Lord saw it and it displeased him that there was no judgment.

**[Isaiah 59:16-21](/bible/isaiah/59#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/59#v16)</sup> And he saw that there was no man, and wondered that there was no intercessor: therefore his arm brought salvation unto him; and his righteousness, it sustained him.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/59#v17)</sup> For he put on righteousness as a breastplate, and an helmet of salvation upon his head; and he put on the garments of vengeance for clothing, and was clad with zeal as a cloke.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/59#v18)</sup> According to their deeds, accordingly he will repay, fury to his adversaries, recompence to his enemies; to the islands he will repay recompence.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/59#v19)</sup> So shall they fear the name of the Lord from the west, and his glory from the rising of the sun. When the enemy shall come in like a flood, the Spirit of the Lord shall lift up a standard against him.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/59#v20)</sup> And the Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/59#v21)</sup> As for me, this is my covenant with them, saith the Lord; My spirit that is upon thee, and my words which I have put in thy mouth, shall not depart out of thy mouth, nor out of the mouth of thy seed, nor out of the mouth of thy seed’s seed, saith the Lord, from henceforth and for ever.

- The Most High wondered that there was no intercessor, so his own arm brought salvation, and he put on righteousness as a breastplate and a helmet of salvation, and the garment of vengeance for clothing.
- According to their deeds he will repay fury to his adversaries; so shall they fear the name of the Lord from the west, and when the enemy comes in like a flood the Spirit of the Lord shall lift up a standard against him.
- The Redeemer shall come to Zion, and to them that turn from transgression in Jacob; his covenant is that his Spirit and his words shall not depart out of their mouth, nor the mouth of their seed, from henceforth and forever.

# Isaiah 60: Arise and Shine

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 60](/bible/isaiah/60)

**[Isaiah 60:1-5](/bible/isaiah/60#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/60#v1)</sup> Arise, shine; for thy light is come, and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/60#v2)</sup> For, behold, the darkness shall cover the earth, and gross darkness the people: but the Lord shall arise upon thee, and his glory shall be seen upon thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/60#v3)</sup> And the Gentiles shall come to thy light, and kings to the brightness of thy rising.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/60#v4)</sup> Lift up thine eyes round about, and see: all they gather themselves together, they come to thee: thy sons shall come from far, and thy daughters shall be nursed at thy side.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/60#v5)</sup> Then thou shalt see, and flow together, and thine heart shall fear, and be enlarged; because the abundance of the sea shall be converted unto thee, the forces of the Gentiles shall come unto thee.

- Arise and shine, for thy light is come and the glory of the Lord is risen upon thee. Though darkness shall cover the earth and gross darkness the people, the Lord shall arise upon thee and his glory be seen upon thee.
- The Gentiles shall come to the light and kings to the brightness of thy rising; thy sons shall come from far and thy daughters be nursed at thy side, and thy heart shall fear and be enlarged.

**[Isaiah 60:6-9](/bible/isaiah/60#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/60#v6)</sup> The multitude of camels shall cover thee, the dromedaries of Midian and Ephah; all they from Sheba shall come: they shall bring gold and incense; and they shall shew forth the praises of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/60#v7)</sup> All the flocks of Kedar shall be gathered together unto thee, the rams of Nebaioth shall minister unto thee: they shall come up with acceptance on mine altar, and I will glorify the house of my glory.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/60#v8)</sup> Who are these that fly as a cloud, and as the doves to their windows?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/60#v9)</sup> Surely the isles shall wait for me, and the ships of Tarshish first, to bring thy sons from far, their silver and their gold with them, unto the name of the Lord thy God, and to the Holy One of Israel, because he hath glorified thee.

- The multitude of camels shall cover thee, and they from Sheba shall come bringing gold and incense and showing forth the praises of the Lord; the flocks of Kedar and the rams of Nebaioth shall minister and come up with acceptance on the altar.
- The isles shall wait for the Most High, and the ships of Tarshish shall bring thy sons from far, their silver and gold with them, unto the name of the Lord thy God and to the Holy One of Israel.

**[Isaiah 60:10-14](/bible/isaiah/60#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/60#v10)</sup> And the sons of strangers shall build up thy walls, and their kings shall minister unto thee: for in my wrath I smote thee, but in my favour have I had mercy on thee.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/60#v11)</sup> Therefore thy gates shall be open continually; they shall not be shut day nor night; that men may bring unto thee the forces of the Gentiles, and that their kings may be brought.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/60#v12)</sup> For the nation and kingdom that will not serve thee shall perish; yea, those nations shall be utterly wasted.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/60#v13)</sup> The glory of Lebanon shall come unto thee, the fir tree, the pine tree, and the box together, to beautify the place of my sanctuary; and I will make the place of my feet glorious.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/60#v14)</sup> The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee; and all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet; and they shall call thee, The city of the Lord, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

- The sons of strangers shall build up thy walls and their kings shall minister unto thee; thy gates shall be open continually to bring in the forces of the Gentiles, for the nation and kingdom that will not serve thee shall perish.
- The glory of Lebanon shall come to beautify the place of his sanctuary, and the sons of them that afflicted thee shall come bending, and they that despised thee shall bow at the soles of thy feet and call thee the city of the Lord, the Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

**[Isaiah 60:15-22](/bible/isaiah/60#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/60#v15)</sup> Whereas thou hast been forsaken and hated, so that no man went through thee, I will make thee an eternal excellency, a joy of many generations.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/60#v16)</sup> Thou shalt also suck the milk of the Gentiles, and shalt suck the breast of kings: and thou shalt know that I the Lord am thy Saviour and thy Redeemer, the mighty One of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/60#v17)</sup> For brass I will bring gold, and for iron I will bring silver, and for wood brass, and for stones iron: I will also make thy officers peace, and thine exactors righteousness.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/60#v18)</sup> Violence shall no more be heard in thy land, wasting nor destruction within thy borders; but thou shalt call thy walls Salvation, and thy gates Praise.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/60#v19)</sup> The sun shall be no more thy light by day; neither for brightness shall the moon give light unto thee: but the Lord shall be unto thee an everlasting light, and thy God thy glory.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/60#v20)</sup> Thy sun shall no more go down; neither shall thy moon withdraw itself: for the Lord shall be thine everlasting light, and the days of thy mourning shall be ended.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/60#v21)</sup> Thy people also shall be all righteous: they shall inherit the land for ever, the branch of my planting, the work of my hands, that I may be glorified.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/60#v22)</sup> A little one shall become a thousand, and a small one a strong nation: I the Lord will hasten it in his time.

- For brass he will bring gold, for iron silver, for wood brass and for stones iron; violence shall no more be heard in thy land, and thy walls shall be called Salvation and thy gates Praise.
- The sun and moon shall be no more thy light, for the Lord shall be thy everlasting light and thy God thy glory, and the days of thy mourning shall be ended.
- Thy people shall be all righteous and inherit the land forever; a little one shall become a thousand and a small one a strong nation, and the Lord will hasten it in his time.
- This chapter goes into future prophecy dealing with when we get the kingdom, how the Most High is going to bless us with all of these beautiful things. He is going to give us the heathen to build up our kingdom.

# Isaiah 61: The Spirit of the Lord God Is Upon Me

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 61](/bible/isaiah/61)

**[Isaiah 61:1-3](/bible/isaiah/61#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/61#v1)</sup> The Spirit of the Lord God is upon me; because the Lord hath anointed me to preach good tidings unto the meek; he hath sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives, and the opening of the prison to them that are bound;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/61#v2)</sup> To proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord, and the day of vengeance of our God; to comfort all that mourn;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/61#v3)</sup> To appoint unto them that mourn in Zion, to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness; that they might be called trees of righteousness, the planting of the Lord, that he might be glorified.

- The Spirit of the Lord God is upon the anointed one, sent to preach good tidings unto the meek, to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim liberty to the captives and the opening of the prison to them that are bound.
- He is sent to proclaim the acceptable year of the Lord and the day of vengeance of our God, to comfort all that mourn, and to give them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning, and the garment of praise for the spirit of heaviness, that they might be called trees of righteousness.
  - **[Luke 4:18](/bible/luke/4#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/luke/4#v18)</sup> The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,

    Christ quoted this passage when he read in the synagogue, applying the anointing to preach the gospel to himself.

**[Isaiah 61:4-7](/bible/isaiah/61#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/61#v4)</sup> And they shall build the old wastes, they shall raise up the former desolations, and they shall repair the waste cities, the desolations of many generations.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/61#v5)</sup> And strangers shall stand and feed your flocks, and the sons of the alien shall be your plowmen and your vinedressers.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/61#v6)</sup> But ye shall be named the Priests of the Lord: men shall call you the Ministers of our God: ye shall eat the riches of the Gentiles, and in their glory shall ye boast yourselves.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/61#v7)</sup> For your shame ye shall have double; and for confusion they shall rejoice in their portion: therefore in their land they shall possess the double: everlasting joy shall be unto them.

- They shall build the old wastes and raise up the former desolations, repairing the waste cities the desolations of many generations, and strangers shall stand and feed their flocks and be their plowmen and vinedressers.
- They shall be named the priests of the Lord and called the ministers of God, and they shall eat the riches of the Gentiles and boast in their glory; for their shame they shall have double, and everlasting joy shall be unto them.

**[Isaiah 61:8-11](/bible/isaiah/61#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/61#v8)</sup> For I the Lord love judgment, I hate robbery for burnt offering; and I will direct their work in truth, and I will make an everlasting covenant with them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/61#v9)</sup> And their seed shall be known among the Gentiles, and their offspring among the people: all that see them shall acknowledge them, that they are the seed which the Lord hath blessed.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/61#v10)</sup> I will greatly rejoice in the Lord, my soul shall be joyful in my God; for he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness, as a bridegroom decketh himself with ornaments, and as a bride adorneth herself with her jewels.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/61#v11)</sup> For as the earth bringeth forth her bud, and as the garden causeth the things that are sown in it to spring forth; so the Lord God will cause righteousness and praise to spring forth before all the nations.

- For the Lord loves judgment and hates robbery for burnt offerings; he will direct their work in truth and make an everlasting covenant with them, and their seed shall be known among the Gentiles as the seed which the Lord hath blessed.
- The prophet greatly rejoices in the Lord, for he has clothed him with the garments of salvation and covered him with the robe of righteousness; as the earth brings forth her bud, so the Lord will cause righteousness and praise to spring forth before all nations.
