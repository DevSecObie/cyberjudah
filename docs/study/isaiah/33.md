**[Isaiah 33:1-2](/bible/isaiah/33#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/33#v1)</sup> Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; and when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/33#v2)</sup> O Lord, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee: be thou their arm every morning, our salvation also in the time of trouble.

- Woe to the spoiler who was not spoiled and the treacherous who was not dealt with treacherously; when he ceases, he himself will be spoiled and dealt with treacherously.
- "O Lord, be gracious unto us; we have waited for thee" is again the cry of patience. As Passover approaches spirits can get shaky, so the word is brought to heal you and remind you that when you go through trials your faith is being tested, the way [Ecclesiasticus 2](/bible/sirach/2) must be applied.
  - **[Psalms 131:1-3](/bible/psalms/131#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/131#v1)</sup> Lord, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/131#v2)</sup> Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/131#v3)</sup> Let Israel hope in the Lord from henceforth and for ever.

    King David quiets himself as a weaned child and says, "Let Israel hope in the Lord from henceforth and for ever."

**[Isaiah 33:3-4](/bible/isaiah/33#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/33#v3)</sup> At the noise of the tumult the people fled; at the lifting up of thyself the nations were scattered.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/33#v4)</sup> And your spoil shall be gathered like the gathering of the caterpiller: as the running to and fro of locusts shall he run upon them.

- At the noise of the tumult the peoples fled, and at the lifting up of the Most High the nations were scattered. The spoil is gathered like the gathering of the caterpillar and the running of locusts.

**[Isaiah 33:5-6](/bible/isaiah/33#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/33#v5)</sup> The Lord is exalted; for he dwelleth on high: he hath filled Zion with judgment and righteousness.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/33#v6)</sup> And wisdom and knowledge shall be the stability of thy times, and strength of salvation: the fear of the Lord is his treasure.

- The Most High is exalted, dwelling on high, filling Zion with judgment and righteousness. Wisdom and knowledge, which are the commandments, shall be the stability of thy times, and the fear of the Most High is his treasure.
- We are the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth, and we must learn to behave ourselves and possess our vessels, constrained from sin.
  - **[2 Timothy 3:14-15](/bible/2-timothy/3#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-timothy/3#v14)</sup> But continue thou in the things which thou hast learned and hast been assured of, knowing of whom thou hast learned them;
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-timothy/3#v15)</sup> And that from a child thou hast known the holy scriptures, which are able to make thee wise unto salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus.

    Know how to behave in the house of God, the church of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth.
  - **[2 Corinthians 5:14](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/5#v14)</sup> For the love of Christ constraineth us; because we thus judge, that if one died for all, then were all dead:

    The love of Christ constraineth us from sin.
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:5](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v5)</sup> Behold, I have taught you statutes and judgments, even as the Lord my God commanded me, that ye should do so in the land whither ye go to possess it.

    Wisdom is the keeping of the statutes and judgments the Most High commanded.
  - **[Malachi 2:7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)</sup> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.

    The priest's lips should keep knowledge, and the people seek the law at his mouth.

**[Isaiah 33:7-12](/bible/isaiah/33#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/33#v7)</sup> Behold, their valiant ones shall cry without: the ambassadors of peace shall weep bitterly.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/33#v8)</sup> The highways lie waste, the wayfaring man ceaseth: he hath broken the covenant, he hath despised the cities, he regardeth no man.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/33#v9)</sup> The earth mourneth and languisheth: Lebanon is ashamed and hewn down: Sharon is like a wilderness; and Bashan and Carmel shake off their fruits.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/33#v10)</sup> Now will I rise, saith the Lord; now will I be exalted; now will I lift up myself.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/33#v11)</sup> Ye shall conceive chaff, ye shall bring forth stubble: your breath, as fire, shall devour you.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/33#v12)</sup> And the people shall be as the burnings of lime: as thorns cut up shall they be burned in the fire.

- The valiant ones cry outside and the ambassadors of peace weep bitterly; the highways lie waste, the covenant is broken, and the earth mourns while Lebanon, Sharon, Bashan, and Carmel are ashamed. Now the Most High arises, exalts himself, and lifts himself up.
- The people conceive chaff and bring forth stubble, and their own breath as fire devours them; they burn like lime and cut thorns in the fire.

**[Isaiah 33:13-16](/bible/isaiah/33#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/33#v13)</sup> Hear, ye that are far off, what I have done; and, ye that are near, acknowledge my might.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/33#v14)</sup> The sinners in Zion are afraid; fearfulness hath surprised the hypocrites. Who among us shall dwell with the devouring fire? who among us shall dwell with everlasting burnings?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/33#v15)</sup> He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/33#v16)</sup> He shall dwell on high: his place of defence shall be the munitions of rocks: bread shall be given him; his waters shall be sure.

- Those far off and near are told to acknowledge the Most High's might; the sinners in Zion are afraid and fearfulness surprises the hypocrites. That is the breaking that comes in an instant, so prepare yourself.
- Who shall dwell with the devouring fire and everlasting burnings? He that walks righteously, speaks uprightly, despises oppression's gain, shakes his hands from bribes, and stops his ears from blood. He shall dwell on high, his bread and water sure.

**[Isaiah 33:17-19](/bible/isaiah/33#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/33#v17)</sup> Thine eyes shall see the king in his beauty: they shall behold the land that is very far off.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/33#v18)</sup> Thine heart shall meditate terror. Where is the scribe? where is the receiver? where is he that counted the towers?
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/33#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not see a fierce people, a people of a deeper speech than thou canst perceive; of a stammering tongue, that thou canst not understand.

- Thine eyes shall see the King in his beauty and behold the land that is very far off, our homeland, far off because we are scattered through the four corners of the earth in Babylon. The heart shall meditate on the terror that is past, asking where is the scribe and the receiver.
- Thou shalt not see the fierce people of a deeper, stammering speech thou canst not understand.

**[Isaiah 33:20-24](/bible/isaiah/33#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/33#v20)</sup> Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities: thine eyes shall see Jerusalem a quiet habitation, a tabernacle that shall not be taken down; not one of the stakes thereof shall ever be removed, neither shall any of the cords thereof be broken.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/33#v21)</sup> But there the glorious Lord will be unto us a place of broad rivers and streams; wherein shall go no galley with oars, neither shall gallant ship pass thereby.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/33#v22)</sup> For the Lord is our judge, the Lord is our lawgiver, the Lord is our king; he will save us.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/33#v23)</sup> Thy tacklings are loosed; they could not well strengthen their mast, they could not spread the sail: then is the prey of a great spoil divided; the lame take the prey.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/isaiah/33#v24)</sup> And the inhabitant shall not say, I am sick: the people that dwell therein shall be forgiven their iniquity.

- Look upon Zion, the city of our solemnities; Jerusalem shall be a quiet habitation, a tabernacle not taken down, its stakes never removed and its cords never broken. There the glorious Most High is a place of broad rivers where no hostile galley or gallant ship passes.
- The Most High is our judge, lawgiver, and king, and he will save us; the enemy's tacklings hang loose and cannot spread the sail, so even the lame take the prey, as the feeble among us will be as David in that day. The inhabitant shall not say "I am sick," and the people are forgiven their iniquity, which tells you those in Jerusalem today are not the real Jews.
