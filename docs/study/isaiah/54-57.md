Read the chapter → [Isaiah 54](/bible/isaiah/54)

This session is a four-chapters-a-day reading, picking up at [Isaiah 54](/bible/isaiah/54) and running straight through [Isaiah 57](/bible/isaiah/57). The four chapters carry one thread: the Most High comforting and restoring the wife He had forsaken for a moment, calling His people back, and setting a plain line between the righteous and the wicked. This is a reading session, so the teaching is light; the class is encouraged to reread these chapters carefully, write down questions, and take them to the camp leader, because chapters like these confuse Christians and anyone not yet learned, but for Israel it is understood that all of it is the Most High speaking to Israel.

**[Isaiah 54:1-5](/bible/isaiah/54#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/54#v1)</sup> Sing, O barren, thou that didst not bear; break forth into singing, and cry aloud, thou that didst not travail with child: for more are the children of the desolate than the children of the married wife, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/54#v2)</sup> Enlarge the place of thy tent, and let them stretch forth the curtains of thine habitations: spare not, lengthen thy cords, and strengthen thy stakes;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/54#v3)</sup> For thou shalt break forth on the right hand and on the left; and thy seed shall inherit the Gentiles, and make the desolate cities to be inhabited.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/54#v4)</sup> Fear not; for thou shalt not be ashamed: neither be thou confounded; for thou shalt not be put to shame: for thou shalt forget the shame of thy youth, and shalt not remember the reproach of thy widowhood any more.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/54#v5)</sup> For thy Maker is thine husband; the Lord of hosts is his name; and thy Redeemer the Holy One of Israel; The God of the whole earth shall he be called.

- The barren woman who did not bear is told to break forth into singing, for the children of the desolate are more than the children of the married wife. This is the promise of increase to a people who had been left desolate.
- She is told to enlarge the place of her tent, stretch forth the curtains, and strengthen her stakes, because she will break forth on the right hand and on the left, and her seed will inherit the Gentiles and make the desolate cities inhabited.
- She is told to fear not and not be ashamed, for she will forget the shame of her youth and the reproach of her widowhood.
- The names given here are titles of the Most High: the Lord of hosts, thy redeemer, the Holy One of Israel, the God of the whole earth. When the Scripture reads a different name it is still the same God under a different title.

**[Isaiah 54:6-10](/bible/isaiah/54#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/54#v6)</sup> For the Lord hath called thee as a woman forsaken and grieved in spirit, and a wife of youth, when thou wast refused, saith thy God.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/54#v7)</sup> For a small moment have I forsaken thee; but with great mercies will I gather thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/54#v8)</sup> In a little wrath I hid my face from thee for a moment; but with everlasting kindness will I have mercy on thee, saith the Lord thy Redeemer.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/54#v9)</sup> For this is as the waters of Noah unto me: for as I have sworn that the waters of Noah should no more go over the earth; so have I sworn that I would not be wroth with thee, nor rebuke thee.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/54#v10)</sup> For the mountains shall depart, and the hills be removed; but my kindness shall not depart from thee, neither shall the covenant of my peace be removed, saith the Lord that hath mercy on thee.

- The Most High called her as a woman forsaken and grieved in spirit, a wife of youth who was refused. For a small moment He forsook her, but with great mercies He will gather her.
- In a little wrath He hid His face for a moment, but with everlasting kindness He will have mercy on her. He compares this to the waters of Noah: as He swore the waters would no more cover the earth, so He has sworn He would not be wroth with her.
- The mountains may depart and the hills be removed, but His kindness will not depart, and the covenant of His peace will not be removed.

**[Isaiah 54:11-14](/bible/isaiah/54#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/54#v11)</sup> O thou afflicted, tossed with tempest, and not comforted, behold, I will lay thy stones with fair colours, and lay thy foundations with sapphires.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/54#v12)</sup> And I will make thy windows of agates, and thy gates of carbuncles, and all thy borders of pleasant stones.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/54#v13)</sup> And all thy children shall be taught of the Lord; and great shall be the peace of thy children.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/54#v14)</sup> In righteousness shalt thou be established: thou shalt be far from oppression; for thou shalt not fear: and from terror; for it shall not come near thee.

- To the afflicted one, tossed with tempest and not comforted, He promises to lay her stones with fair colours and her foundations with sapphires, her windows of agates, her gates of carbuncles, and all her borders of pleasant stones.
- All her children will be taught of the Most High, and great will be the peace of her children.
- In righteousness she will be established; she will be far from oppression and will not fear, and terror will not come near her.

**[Isaiah 54:15-17](/bible/isaiah/54#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/54#v15)</sup> Behold, they shall surely gather together, but not by me: whosoever shall gather together against thee shall fall for thy sake.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/54#v16)</sup> Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and that bringeth forth an instrument for his work; and I have created the waster to destroy.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/54#v17)</sup> No weapon that is formed against thee shall prosper; and every tongue that shall rise against thee in judgment thou shalt condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the Lord, and their righteousness is of me, saith the Lord.

- Any who gather together against her do not do so by Him, and whoever gathers against her will fall for her sake.
- He created the smith that blows the coals and brings forth an instrument for his work, and He created the waster to destroy. Both maker and destroyer are His.
- No weapon formed against her will prosper, and every tongue that rises against her in judgment she will condemn. This is the heritage of the servants of the Most High, and their righteousness is of Him.

# Isaiah 55: A Free Invitation

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 55](/bible/isaiah/55)

**[Isaiah 55:1-5](/bible/isaiah/55#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/55#v1)</sup> Ho, every one that thirsteth, come ye to the waters, and he that hath no money; come ye, buy, and eat; yea, come, buy wine and milk without money and without price.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/55#v2)</sup> Wherefore do ye spend money for that which is not bread? and your labour for that which satisfieth not? hearken diligently unto me, and eat ye that which is good, and let your soul delight itself in fatness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/55#v3)</sup> Incline your ear, and come unto me: hear, and your soul shall live; and I will make an everlasting covenant with you, even the sure mercies of David.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/55#v4)</sup> Behold, I have given him for a witness to the people, a leader and commander to the people.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/55#v5)</sup> Behold, thou shalt call a nation that thou knowest not, and nations that knew not thee shall run unto thee because of the Lord thy God, and for the Holy One of Israel; for he hath glorified thee.

- Everyone that thirsts is called to the waters, and he that has no money is told to come, buy, and eat wine and milk without money and without price. The invitation is free.
- He asks why they spend money for that which is not bread and their labour for that which does not satisfy. They are told to hearken and eat that which is good, and let the soul delight itself in fatness.
- If they incline their ear and come, their soul will live, and He will make an everlasting covenant with them, even the sure mercies of David.
- David is given for a witness to the people, a leader and a commander. A nation not known will be called, and nations that did not know her will run to her because of the Most High, who has glorified her.

**[Isaiah 55:6-9](/bible/isaiah/55#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/55#v6)</sup> Seek ye the Lord while he may be found, call ye upon him while he is near:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/55#v7)</sup> Let the wicked forsake his way, and the unrighteous man his thoughts: and let him return unto the Lord, and he will have mercy upon him; and to our God, for he will abundantly pardon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/55#v8)</sup> For my thoughts are not your thoughts, neither are your ways my ways, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/55#v9)</sup> For as the heavens are higher than the earth, so are my ways higher than your ways, and my thoughts than your thoughts.

- Seek the Most High while He may be found, call upon Him while He is near. The wicked is to forsake his way and the unrighteous man his thoughts, and return to the Most High, who will have mercy and abundantly pardon.
- His thoughts are not our thoughts, and His ways are not our ways. As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are His ways and His thoughts higher than ours.

**[Isaiah 55:10-13](/bible/isaiah/55#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/55#v10)</sup> For as the rain cometh down, and the snow from heaven, and returneth not thither, but watereth the earth, and maketh it bring forth and bud, that it may give seed to the sower, and bread to the eater:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/55#v11)</sup> So shall my word be that goeth forth out of my mouth: it shall not return unto me void, but it shall accomplish that which I please, and it shall prosper in the thing whereto I sent it.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/55#v12)</sup> For ye shall go out with joy, and be led forth with peace: the mountains and the hills shall break forth before you into singing, and all the trees of the field shall clap their hands.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/55#v13)</sup> Instead of the thorn shall come up the fir tree, and instead of the brier shall come up the myrtle tree: and it shall be to the Lord for a name, for an everlasting sign that shall not be cut off.

- As the rain and snow come down from heaven and do not return without watering the earth, causing it to bring forth seed and bread, so His word does not return to Him void but accomplishes what He pleases.
- His people will go out with joy and be led forth with peace; the mountains and hills will break forth into singing and the trees of the field will clap their hands.
- Instead of the thorn will come up the fir tree, and instead of the brier the myrtle tree, and this will be to the Most High for a name and an everlasting sign that will not be cut off.

# Isaiah 56: A House of Prayer for All Israel

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 56](/bible/isaiah/56)

**[Isaiah 56:1-2](/bible/isaiah/56#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/56#v1)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Keep ye judgment, and do justice: for my salvation is near to come, and my righteousness to be revealed.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/56#v2)</sup> Blessed is the man that doeth this, and the son of man that layeth hold on it; that keepeth the sabbath from polluting it, and keepeth his hand from doing any evil.

- Keep judgment and do justice, for His salvation is near to come and His righteousness to be revealed.
- Blessed is the man that does this and lays hold on it, that keeps the Sabbath from polluting it and keeps his hand from doing any evil.

**[Isaiah 56:3-8](/bible/isaiah/56#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/56#v3)</sup> Neither let the son of the stranger, that hath joined himself to the Lord, speak, saying, The Lord hath utterly separated me from his people: neither let the eunuch say, Behold, I am a dry tree.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/56#v4)</sup> For thus saith the Lord unto the eunuchs that keep my sabbaths, and choose the things that please me, and take hold of my covenant;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/56#v5)</sup> Even unto them will I give in mine house and within my walls a place and a name better than of sons and of daughters: I will give them an everlasting name, that shall not be cut off.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/56#v6)</sup> Also the sons of the stranger, that join themselves to the Lord, to serve him, and to love the name of the Lord, to be his servants, every one that keepeth the sabbath from polluting it, and taketh hold of my covenant;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/56#v7)</sup> Even them will I bring to my holy mountain, and make them joyful in my house of prayer: their burnt offerings and their sacrifices shall be accepted upon mine altar; for mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/56#v8)</sup> The Lord God which gathereth the outcasts of Israel saith, Yet will I gather others to him, beside those that are gathered unto him.

- The son of the stranger that has joined himself to the Most High is not to say the Lord has utterly separated him from His people, and the eunuch is not to say he is a dry tree. These strangers spoken of are Israel; the whole passage is the Most High speaking to Israel.
- To the eunuchs that keep His Sabbaths and take hold of His covenant, He gives a place and a name in His house better than of sons and daughters, an everlasting name that will not be cut off.
- The sons of the stranger that join themselves to Him, keep the Sabbath, and take hold of His covenant, He will bring to His holy mountain and make joyful in His house of prayer; their offerings will be accepted, for His house will be called a house of prayer for all people.
- The Lord God who gathers the outcasts of Israel says He will yet gather others to Him besides those already gathered.

**[Isaiah 56:9-12](/bible/isaiah/56#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/56#v9)</sup> All ye beasts of the field, come to devour, yea, all ye beasts in the forest.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/56#v10)</sup> His watchmen are blind: they are all ignorant, they are all dumb dogs, they cannot bark; sleeping, lying down, loving to slumber.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/56#v11)</sup> Yea, they are greedy dogs which can never have enough, and they are shepherds that cannot understand: they all look to their own way, every one for his gain, from his quarter.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/56#v12)</sup> Come ye, say they, I will fetch wine, and we will fill ourselves with strong drink; and to morrow shall be as this day, and much more abundant.

- All the beasts of the field and forest are called to devour. Judgment is turned on the failed leadership.
- The watchmen are blind and ignorant, dumb dogs that cannot bark, sleeping and loving to slumber. They are greedy dogs that never have enough, shepherds that cannot understand.
- They all look to their own way, every one for his gain, saying they will fetch wine and fill themselves with strong drink, and tomorrow will be as this day and much more abundant.

# Isaiah 57: No Peace to the Wicked

Read the chapter → [Isaiah 57](/bible/isaiah/57)

**[Isaiah 57:1-4](/bible/isaiah/57#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/57#v1)</sup> The righteous perisheth, and no man layeth it to heart: and merciful men are taken away, none considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/57#v2)</sup> He shall enter into peace: they shall rest in their beds, each one walking in his uprightness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/57#v3)</sup> But draw near hither, ye sons of the sorceress, the seed of the adulterer and the whore.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/57#v4)</sup> Against whom do ye sport yourselves? against whom make ye a wide mouth, and draw out the tongue? are ye not children of transgression, a seed of falsehood,

- The righteous perishes and no man lays it to heart; merciful men are taken away, none considering that the righteous is taken away from the evil to come. He enters into peace and rests in his bed, walking in his uprightness.
- The sons of the sorceress, the seed of the adulterer, are called to draw near. They sport themselves and draw out the tongue.
- They are named as children of transgression, a seed of falsehood.

**[Isaiah 57:5-10](/bible/isaiah/57#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/57#v5)</sup> Enflaming yourselves with idols under every green tree, slaying the children in the valleys under the clifts of the rocks?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/57#v6)</sup> Among the smooth stones of the stream is thy portion; they, they are thy lot: even to them hast thou poured a drink offering, thou hast offered a meat offering. Should I receive comfort in these?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/57#v7)</sup> Upon a lofty and high mountain hast thou set thy bed: even thither wentest thou up to offer sacrifice.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/57#v8)</sup> Behind the doors also and the posts hast thou set up thy remembrance: for thou hast discovered thyself to another than me, and art gone up; thou hast enlarged thy bed, and made thee a covenant with them; thou lovedst their bed where thou sawest it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/57#v9)</sup> And thou wentest to the king with ointment, and didst increase thy perfumes, and didst send thy messengers far off, and didst debase thyself even unto hell.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/57#v10)</sup> Thou art wearied in the greatness of thy way; yet saidst thou not, There is no hope: thou hast found the life of thine hand; therefore thou wast not grieved.

- They inflame themselves with idols under every green tree, slaying the children in the valleys under the clefts of the rocks. Among the smooth stones of the stream is their portion, and to them they poured a drink offering and offered a meat offering.
- Upon a lofty and high mountain they set their bed and went up to offer sacrifice; behind the doors and the posts they set up their remembrance, having discovered themselves to another than the Most High.
- They enlarged their bed and made a covenant with the idols, loving their bed. They went to the king with ointment and increased their perfumes, and sent their messengers far off, debasing themselves even unto hell.
- They wearied themselves in the greatness of their way, yet did not say there is no hope; they found the life of their hand, therefore they were not grieved.

**[Isaiah 57:11-14](/bible/isaiah/57#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/57#v11)</sup> And of whom hast thou been afraid or feared, that thou hast lied, and hast not remembered me, nor laid it to thy heart? have not I held my peace even of old, and thou fearest me not?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/57#v12)</sup> I will declare thy righteousness, and thy works; for they shall not profit thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/57#v13)</sup> When thou criest, let thy companies deliver thee; but the wind shall carry them all away; vanity shall take them: but he that putteth his trust in me shall possess the land, and shall inherit my holy mountain;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/57#v14)</sup> And shall say, Cast ye up, cast ye up, prepare the way, take up the stumblingblock out of the way of my people.

- He asks of whom they were afraid, that they lied and did not remember Him nor lay it to their heart. He held His peace of old, and so they did not fear Him.
- He will declare their righteousness and their works, which will not profit them; when they cry, their companies will not deliver them, but the wind will carry them away.
- But he that puts his trust in Him will possess the land and inherit His holy mountain. The way is to be prepared and the stumbling block taken out of the way of His people.

**[Isaiah 57:15-21](/bible/isaiah/57#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/57#v15)</sup> For thus saith the high and lofty One that inhabiteth eternity, whose name is Holy; I dwell in the high and holy place, with him also that is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble, and to revive the heart of the contrite ones.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/57#v16)</sup> For I will not contend for ever, neither will I be always wroth: for the spirit should fail before me, and the souls which I have made.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/57#v17)</sup> For the iniquity of his covetousness was I wroth, and smote him: I hid me, and was wroth, and he went on frowardly in the way of his heart.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/57#v18)</sup> I have seen his ways, and will heal him: I will lead him also, and restore comforts unto him and to his mourners.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/57#v19)</sup> I create the fruit of the lips; Peace, peace to him that is far off, and to him that is near, saith the Lord; and I will heal him.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/57#v20)</sup> But the wicked are like the troubled sea, when it cannot rest, whose waters cast up mire and dirt.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/57#v21)</sup> There is no peace, saith my God, to the wicked.

- The high and lofty One that inhabits eternity, whose name is Holy, dwells in the high and holy place, and also with him that is of a contrite and humble spirit, to revive the spirit of the humble and the heart of the contrite ones.
- He will not contend forever nor be always wroth, for the spirit would fail before Him. For the iniquity of covetousness He was wroth and smote him, and hid Himself, and the man went on frowardly in the way of his heart.
- He has seen his ways and will heal him, leading him and restoring comforts to him and his mourners, creating peace for him that is far off and him that is near.
- But the wicked are like the troubled sea that cannot rest, whose waters cast up mire and dirt. There is no peace to the wicked, says the Most High.
- This is a good four chapters and a good read; the class is encouraged to read them again carefully, take notes, and bring questions to the camp leader, because the whole of it is the Most High speaking to Israel.
