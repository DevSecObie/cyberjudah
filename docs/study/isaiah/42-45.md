Read the chapter → [Isaiah 42](/bible/isaiah/42)

This session reads the four chapters [Isaiah 42](/bible/isaiah/42) through 45. The thread that ties them together is the servant: the chapter opens on the Most High's elect servant in whom his soul delights, moving into Christ, and then declares plainly that the servant, the witness, and the chosen is Israel. Along the way the teaching drives home who the "gentiles" are, that Israel was robbed and spoiled and hidden in prison houses, that the idols of the nations are nothing but wood and stone, that our name was changed in slavery as prophesied, and that the Most High alone forms light and creates darkness, makes peace and creates evil.

This is talking about us the Israelites: the other nations come and set up in our communities, sell their goods, and take the money back, preying on us and spoiling us like the spoils of war.
- Who among you will give ear to this? Who gave Jacob for a spoil and Israel to the robbers? Did not the Lord, he against whom we have sinned, for they would not walk in his ways nor be obedient to his law.
- Therefore he poured upon him the fury of his anger and the strength of battle, and it set him on fire round about, yet he knew not, and it burned him, yet he laid it not to heart. We were consumed for our sins on the slave ships but laid it not to heart, not considering that the Most High punished us and brought us over to turn us back to our maker.
