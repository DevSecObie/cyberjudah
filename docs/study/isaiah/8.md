**[Isaiah 8:1](/bible/isaiah/8#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/8#v1)</sup> Moreover the Lord said unto me, Take thee a great roll, and write in it with a man’s pen concerning Maher–shalal–hash–baz.

- The Most High told Isaiah to take a great roll and write in it with a man's pen concerning Maher-shalal-hash-baz, meaning quick to the plunder, swift to the spoil.

**[Isaiah 8:2-4](/bible/isaiah/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/8#v2)</sup> And I took unto me faithful witnesses to record, Uriah the priest, and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/8#v3)</sup> And I went unto the prophetess; and she conceived, and bare a son. Then said the Lord to me, Call his name Maher–shalal–hash–baz.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/8#v4)</sup> For before the child shall have knowledge to cry, My father, and my mother, the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.

- Isaiah took faithful witnesses to record, Uriah the priest and Zechariah the son of Jeberechiah, then went to the prophetess, who conceived and bare a son.
- The Most High said to call his name Maher-shalal-hash-baz, for before the child shall have knowledge to cry, "My father, and my mother," the riches of Damascus and the spoil of Samaria shall be taken away before the king of Assyria.
- This ties to [Isaiah 7:16](/bible/isaiah/7#v16), "before the child shall know to refuse evil and choose the good"; both point to the fall of the lands of Rezin and Pekah.

**[Isaiah 8:5-8](/bible/isaiah/8#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/8#v5)</sup> The Lord spake also unto me again, saying,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/8#v6)</sup> Forasmuch as this people refuseth the waters of Shiloah that go softly, and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah’s son;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/8#v7)</sup> Now therefore, behold, the Lord bringeth up upon them the waters of the river, strong and many, even the king of Assyria, and all his glory: and he shall come up over all his channels, and go over all his banks:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/8#v8)</sup> And he shall pass through Judah; he shall overflow and go over, he shall reach even to the neck; and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of thy land, O Immanuel.

- Because the people refuse the waters of Shiloah that go softly and rejoice in Rezin and Remaliah's son, the Most High brings upon them the waters of the river strong and many, the king of Assyria and all his glory, rising over all his channels and banks.
- He shall pass through Judah and overflow, reaching even to the neck, and the stretching out of his wings shall fill the breadth of the land, O Immanuel, which means God with us.

**[Isaiah 8:9-10](/bible/isaiah/8#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/8#v9)</sup> Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces; and give ear, all ye of far countries: gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces; gird yourselves, and ye shall be broken in pieces.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/8#v10)</sup> Take counsel together, and it shall come to nought; speak the word, and it shall not stand: for God is with us.

- "Associate yourselves, O ye people, and ye shall be broken in pieces." Though the far countries gird themselves, they shall be broken; take counsel together and it shall come to nought, speak the word and it shall not stand, for God is with us.
- If the counsel is from man it will fall, but if it is from the Most High it cannot be overthrown, and men would only end up fighting against God.
  - **[Acts 5:38-39](/bible/acts/5#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/5#v38)</sup> And now I say unto you, Refrain from these men, and let them alone: for if this counsel or this work be of men, it will come to nought:
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/5#v39)</sup> But if it be of God, ye cannot overthrow it; lest haply ye be found even to fight against God.

    If this counsel be of men it will come to nought, but if it be of God it cannot be overthrown, lest men be found fighting against God.

**[Isaiah 8:11-13](/bible/isaiah/8#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/8#v11)</sup> For the Lord spake thus to me with a strong hand, and instructed me that I should not walk in the way of this people, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/8#v12)</sup> Say ye not, A confederacy, to all them to whom this people shall say, A confederacy; neither fear ye their fear, nor be afraid.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/8#v13)</sup> Sanctify the Lord of hosts himself; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread.

- The Most High spoke with a strong hand, instructing Isaiah not to walk in the way of this people, and not to say "a confederacy" to all them to whom this people says a confederacy, nor to fear their fear.
- "Sanctify the LORD of hosts himself; and let him be your fear, and let him be your dread."

**[Isaiah 8:14](/bible/isaiah/8#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/8#v14)</sup> And he shall be for a sanctuary; but for a stone of stumbling and for a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, for a gin and for a snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.

- He shall be for a sanctuary, but also a stone of stumbling and a rock of offence to both the houses of Israel, and a gin and snare to the inhabitants of Jerusalem.
- He is a sanctuary for his people, a little sanctuary in the places where they are scattered, but a stone of stumbling to those who reject him.
- The word became a snare upon the leadership because the understanding is given precept upon precept, in another tongue, so that those without it stumble and fall.
  - **[Ezekiel 11:16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)</sup> Therefore say, Thus saith the Lord God; Although I have cast them far off among the heathen, and although I have scattered them among the countries, yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they shall come.

    The Most High is a little sanctuary to his people in the countries where they are scattered.
  - **[Isaiah 28:10](/bible/isaiah/28#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/28#v10)</sup> For precept must be upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little:

    Precept must be upon precept, line upon line, for the understanding comes with stammering lips and another tongue.
  - **[Isaiah 28:13](/bible/isaiah/28#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/28#v13)</sup> But the word of the Lord was unto them precept upon precept, precept upon precept; line upon line, line upon line; here a little, and there a little; that they might go, and fall backward, and be broken, and snared, and taken.

    The word of the Most High was made precept upon precept so that they go and fall backward, and be broken, snared, and taken.
  - **[Romans 11:9](/bible/romans/11#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/romans/11#v9)</sup> And David saith, Let their table be made a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them:

    When Christ came teaching, the word was a snare and a trap to the leadership of the southern kingdom.
  - **[Psalm 69:22](/bible/psalms/69#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/69#v22)</sup> Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap.

    David prophesied that their table would become a snare to the enemies.

**[Isaiah 8:15](/bible/isaiah/8#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/8#v15)</sup> And many among them shall stumble, and fall, and be broken, and be snared, and be taken.

- Many among them shall stumble, fall, be broken, snared, and taken.

**[Isaiah 8:16](/bible/isaiah/8#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/8#v16)</sup> Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples.

- "Bind up the testimony, seal the law among my disciples." Knowing the law is knowing what the mark truly is, instead of thinking of a microchip and being snared and taken by every wind, like the trees moved with the wind.

**[Isaiah 8:17-18](/bible/isaiah/8#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/8#v17)</sup> And I will wait upon the Lord, that hideth his face from the house of Jacob, and I will look for him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/8#v18)</sup> Behold, I and the children whom the Lord hath given me are for signs and for wonders in Israel from the Lord of hosts, which dwelleth in mount Zion.

- "I will wait upon the LORD, that hideth his face from the house of Jacob, and I will look for him."
- Behold, Isaiah and the children the Most High gave him are for signs and wonders in Israel, from the Lord of hosts which dwelleth in mount Zion.

**[Isaiah 8:19-20](/bible/isaiah/8#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/8#v19)</sup> And when they shall say unto you, Seek unto them that have familiar spirits, and unto wizards that peep, and that mutter: should not a people seek unto their God? for the living to the dead?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

- When they say to seek unto them that have familiar spirits, and wizards that peep and mutter, the answer is: should not a people seek unto their God? For the living to the dead?
- "To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them." Christians say Jesus is the word and go to [John 17:17](/bible/john/17#v17), "thy word is truth," but the word spoken of is the law; no law means no light, which is why there is no light in christianity.
  - **[John 17:17](/bible/john/17#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/john/17#v17)</sup> Sanctify them through thy truth: thy word is truth.

    Christians cite this to call Christ the word, but the word that sanctifies is the truth, which is the law.

**[Isaiah 8:21-22](/bible/isaiah/8#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/8#v21)</sup> And they shall pass through it, hardly bestead and hungry: and it shall come to pass, that when they shall be hungry, they shall fret themselves, and curse their king and their God, and look upward.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/8#v22)</sup> And they shall look unto the earth; and behold trouble and darkness, dimness of anguish; and they shall be driven to darkness.

- They shall pass through it hardly bestead and hungry, fret themselves, and curse their king and their God, looking upward and to the earth.
- They shall behold trouble and darkness, dimness of anguish, and be driven to darkness.
