Read the chapter → [Isaiah 35](/bible/isaiah/35)

This chapter is a prophecy of Christ's return and of Israel's return. The wilderness and the desert become glad and blossom, the weak and fearful are strengthened, the Most High comes with vengeance to save, and a highway called the way of holiness is opened for the redeemed alone to walk on.

**[Isaiah 35:1-2](/bible/isaiah/35#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/35#v1)</sup> The wilderness and the solitary place shall be glad for them; and the desert shall rejoice, and blossom as the rose.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/35#v2)</sup> It shall blossom abundantly, and rejoice even with joy and singing: the glory of Lebanon shall be given unto it, the excellency of Carmel and Sharon, they shall see the glory of the Lord, and the excellency of our God.

- The wilderness and the solitary place are glad, and the desert rejoices and blossoms as the rose. The barren places become fruitful when the Most High moves.
- The glory of Lebanon and the excellency of Carmel and Sharon are given to it, and they shall see the glory of the Most High. The prophecy points to the restoration that comes with His return.

**[Isaiah 35:3-4](/bible/isaiah/35#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/35#v3)</sup> Strengthen ye the weak hands, and confirm the feeble knees.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/35#v4)</sup> Say to them that are of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not: behold, your God will come with vengeance, even God with a recompence; he will come and save you.

- Strengthen the weak hands and confirm the feeble knees, and say to those of a fearful heart, Be strong, fear not. The message to the people is one of encouragement in the day of trouble.
- Behold, your God will come with vengeance, even the Most High with a recompense; He will come and save you. His coming is both vengeance on the wicked and salvation for His people.

**[Isaiah 35:5-7](/bible/isaiah/35#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/35#v5)</sup> Then the eyes of the blind shall be opened, and the ears of the deaf shall be unstopped.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/35#v6)</sup> Then shall the lame man leap as an hart, and the tongue of the dumb sing: for in the wilderness shall waters break out, and streams in the desert.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/35#v7)</sup> And the parched ground shall become a pool, and the thirsty land springs of water: in the habitation of dragons, where each lay, shall be grass with reeds and rushes.

- Then the eyes of the blind are opened and the ears of the deaf unstopped, the lame man leaps as a hart, and the tongue of the dumb sings. The Most High's coming brings healing and restoration.
- In the wilderness waters break out and streams in the desert, the parched ground becomes a pool, and the thirsty land springs of water. The dry places are made alive again.

**[Isaiah 35:8-10](/bible/isaiah/35#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/35#v8)</sup> And an highway shall be there, and a way, and it shall be called The way of holiness; the unclean shall not pass over it; but it shall be for those: the wayfaring men, though fools, shall not err therein.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/35#v9)</sup> No lion shall be there, nor any ravenous beast shall go up thereon, it shall not be found there; but the redeemed shall walk there:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/35#v10)</sup> And the ransomed of the Lord shall return, and come to Zion with songs and everlasting joy upon their heads: they shall obtain joy and gladness, and sorrow and sighing shall flee away.

- A highway is there called the way of holiness, and the unclean shall not pass over it. Wayfaring means a traveling man, and though fools travel through many places, they shall not err on this way, because the unclean cannot walk it.
- No lion or ravenous beast goes up on it; it is not found there, but the redeemed walk there. The wayfaring man, the fool, is somebody outside of these people, and only the redeemed are those who can walk on this way.
- The ransomed of the Most High return and come to Zion with songs and everlasting joy upon their heads; they obtain joy and gladness, and sorrow and sighing flee away. This is the return of the people to Zion when the Most High saves them.
