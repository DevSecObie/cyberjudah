## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Titus 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-5, 9-11, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Titus 3:1-2](/bible/titus/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/titus/3#v1)</sup> Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates, to be ready to every good work,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/3#v2)</sup> To speak evil of no man, to be no brawlers, but gentle, shewing all meekness unto all men.

- “Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates, to be ready to every good work, To speak evil of no man, to be no brawlers”: we can’t speak evil of any man or be brawlers. “But gentle”: be humble, “shewing all meekness unto all men”, righteous toward all men.
- “Put them in mind to be subject to principalities and powers, to obey magistrates, to be ready to every good work”. Some won’t like this verse. It is like Romans 13, obey the powers that be: obey the laws of the land, as long as they don’t contradict God’s laws. If we don’t bring that out, the kids end up like the young boy cursing out the cops after he stole the brandy, then getting knocked upside the head, and the parents want to know why the child was acting up. We never say all police are good; some are no damn good.
- “To speak evil of no man, to be no brawlers”. Speak evil of no man: that is gossip.

**[Titus 3:3](/bible/titus/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/titus/3#v3)</sup> For we ourselves also were sometimes foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.

- “For we ourselves also were sometimes foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving divers lusts and pleasures”: we were foolish once, partying, having a good time, “living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.”

**[Titus 3:4-5](/bible/titus/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/titus/3#v4)</sup> But after that the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward man appeared,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/titus/3#v5)</sup> Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to his mercy he saved us, by the washing of regeneration, and renewing of the Holy Ghost;

- “But after that the kindness and love of God our Saviour toward man appeared”: after all that, after we changed, after we repented. “Not by works of righteousness which we have done”: nothing we did. We are not such righteous brothers that he said, I’m going to gather them because they are so perfect and holy. Not for our righteousness: “but according to his mercy he saved us.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 43:3-7](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/43#v3)</sup> For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour: I gave Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/43#v4)</sup> Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/43#v5)</sup> Fear not: for I am with thee: I will bring thy seed from the east, and gather thee from the west;
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/43#v6)</sup> I will say to the north, Give up; and to the south, Keep not back: bring my sons from far, and my daughters from the ends of the earth;
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/43#v7)</sup> Even every one that is called by my name: for I have created him for my glory, I have formed him; yea, I have made him.

    Not by our righteousness: “For I am the Lord thy God, the Holy One of Israel, thy Saviour: I gave Egypt for thy ransom, Ethiopia and Seba for thee.” He gave Egypt, Ethiopia and Seba for us, “Since thou wast precious in my sight, thou hast been honourable, and I have loved thee: therefore will I give men for thee, and people for thy life.”

**[Titus 3:9](/bible/titus/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/titus/3#v9)</sup> But avoid foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law; for they are unprofitable and vain.

- “But avoid foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law; for they are unprofitable and vain.” When we teach, we are not there to convince anybody of anything, to argue or to debate. That will not win souls. We are there to teach and plant the seed; whether they accept it is up to the Messiah.
- It is nothing personal. Everybody is not going to agree with you; some will curse you out and call you names. You are there to do a job: do the job and leave. Teach the scriptures and stay in the scriptures. Keep your emotion out of the Bible and your personal opinion out of the word of God. Nobody wants to hear your opinion, and God does not care about it.

**[Titus 3:10](/bible/titus/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/titus/3#v10)</sup> A man that is an heretick after the first and second admonition reject;

- “A man that is an heretick after the first and second admonition reject.” This is how to judge matters, for the new officers. Do not argue back and forth. Reject. Once you put the law down and they do not want to hear it, bye. That is it.

**[Titus 3:11](/bible/titus/3#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/titus/3#v11)</sup> Knowing that he that is such is subverted, and sinneth, being condemned of himself.

- “Knowing that he that is such is subverted, and sinneth, being condemned of himself.” Being condemned of himself.
