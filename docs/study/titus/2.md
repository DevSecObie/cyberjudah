## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Titus 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-6, 9-12, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Titus 2:2](/bible/titus/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/2#v2)</sup> That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience.

- These are the morals of God. “That the aged men be sober, grave, temperate, sound in faith, in charity, in patience.” Notice it starts with the man. That is the order, the structure of a family: it starts with the father in the house and then trickles down.

**[Titus 2:3-5](/bible/titus/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/titus/2#v3)</sup> The aged women likewise, that they be in behaviour as becometh holiness, not false accusers, not given to much wine, teachers of good things;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/titus/2#v4)</sup> That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands, to love their children,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/titus/2#v5)</sup> To be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

- “That they may teach the young women to be sober, to love their husbands”. Why do the young women need to be taught to love their husbands? Because it is not in them unless you teach them. That is why the older women need to teach their young daughters how to love their husbands. It is a never-ending cycle.
- Do you understand what happens to a man when he feels disrespected? Society has blinded all our minds, and you don't realize the realm you are stepping into. That is why there is domestic violence and broken families among us. Yet even men: if you are not answering disrespect with the scriptures, with the Bible, brother, you are not in the truth either. Somebody has to be spiritual in the situation.
- The moment a woman stops respecting her man, his attraction to her dies. A man builds a house the right way, lays the foundation, gets the plumbing right, brings his family in, and nothing will harm that house worse than a disrespectful woman. Adultery will ruin a household; but the word no to a man who is head of the house is a deal breaker. If he does not have full subjection over you, he can't protect you; you are now under some other man, and you are not his.
- At the same time, brothers, sisters speak out of emotion; so do you. She might say no, but watch what she does, then respond.

  - **[Proverbs 31:10-11](/bible/proverbs/31#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/31#v10)</sup> Who can find a virtuous woman? for her price is far above rubies.
    >

When we say don’t listen to black women, we talking about the evil sisters out here, the evil sisters that prophesy out of their own heart. But these righteous women, these women getting themselves right with God, we listening to them. If Abigail was here and in the spirit, we would listen to her; John the Baptist’s mama, Dorcas, Priscilla, these righteous women, we listen to them. This righteous, virtuous woman is worth more than rubies.

**[Titus 2:6](/bible/titus/2#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/titus/2#v6)</sup> Young men likewise exhort to be sober minded.

- “Young men likewise exhort to be sober minded.” We play our position as disciplined soldiers, soldiers with a biblical education; we cannot get caught up. Sober minded means don’t smoke weed and don’t be drunk, absolutely, but it also means keeping a clear head in every situation and possessing your soul.
- Do we feel emotion? Yes, we proved that: the prophets always had a heart and compassion for their people. But as servants of God we have to move in the spirit of Christ, who was an austere man. Soldiers cannot be emotional.

**[Titus 2:9](/bible/titus/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/titus/2#v9)</sup> Exhort servants to be obedient unto their own masters, and to please them well in all things; not answering again;

- “Exhort servants to be obedient unto their own masters, and to please them well in all things; not answering again.” Esau perverted this. Not answering again means not talking back.

**[Titus 2:10](/bible/titus/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/titus/2#v10)</sup> Not purloining, but shewing all good fidelity; that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things.

- “Not purloining.” Purloin means to steal something: sticky fingers, a kleptomaniac. That is what we just read in Exodus 22. Paul is dealing with the brothers who were stealing and got caught and had to work it off. “But shewing all good fidelity; that they may adorn the doctrine of God our Saviour in all things.”

**[Titus 2:11](/bible/titus/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/titus/2#v11)</sup> For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men,

- Spot the signs when he is led by lust. Some of you think you're just gorgeous and have that effect on people. Maybe he's just nasty. “For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men.” Does he understand this?

**[Titus 2:12](/bible/titus/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/titus/2#v12)</sup> Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;

- “Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world.” Denying is an action: to deny ungodliness he has to be working to make sure he is not in the midst of sin.
- How does he show a lust issue? Porn, social media. It's eleven at night and he lets her know, I'm naked right now. He put that thought in her mind. You have to see through the innuendos: he's setting the mood, lining you up. What you got on right now? Let's go live real quick. The devil upped the game.
- When you see that, sister, get the hell off the phone: holler at me when you've got clothes on. Put him in his place, and he will know she's not one of them, I probably got a strike. Sometimes that is a complete deal breaker. And when it gets out that she ended it because she wouldn't let him drag her into sin, brothers will hear she is about the Lord. But if it comes out that y'all were having phone sex, the next one will hear that too and you'll attract a brother just like that again. Break the cycle.
