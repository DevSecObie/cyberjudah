## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Titus 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2, 9-11, 14-16, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Titus 1:2](/bible/titus/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/1#v2)</sup> In hope of eternal life, which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began;

- “In hope of eternal life”. Our hope, brothers and sisters, is to have eternal life, “which God, that cannot lie, promised before the world began”. With all the oppression and all the affliction, God promised it before the world began, and God cannot turn around and say he will not give it: he is God, and he can never lie. He promised us before the world began eternal life, the new body. We must control the mind.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 119:133](/bible/psalms/119#v133)**
    > <sup>[133](/bible/psalms/119#v133)</sup> Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.

    “Order my steps in thy word: and let not any iniquity have dominion over me.” That is what we have to pray for. That is why we have to take full control of our mind: that is where our sin comes from, and where its dominion comes from, holding us down when we are captive to our sin. We pray that the Lord guides our steps in his word; that is the only way we are going to be protected and keep hold of the hope of eternal life.

**[Titus 1:9](/bible/titus/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/titus/1#v9)</sup> Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught, that he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers.

- Our leadership begins by bringing the scriptures to clamp the evil mouths of the simple. We have to shut down simplicity and evil whenever it comes up, and that is what this verse is about: shutting down evil against the Most High’s program.
- “Holding fast the faithful word as he hath been taught”: we’re not going to hold our tongue. Holding fast means holding tight to the faithful word as we have been taught. We’ve been taught how to deliver the most hard words, how to deliver the message to the people to heal them, to resurrect them, to rejuvenate them, and we have to teach that in all faithfulness.
- “That he may be able by sound doctrine both to exhort and to convince the gainsayers”: sound doctrine is the doctrine of the Most High, the doctrine from God. Weak doctrine ain’t gonna be able to set the tone; sound doctrine is how we exhort and how we move in this earth. Our job is to convince the gainsayers, to convince everyone. There is no turning down this gospel. It has got to go out through the whole planet earth; it has to bend minds, bend spirits, break knees, because this is the rock that’s going to smash everything down. So we can’t be soft with it; we got to bring it out as thus saith the Lord.
- In other words, shut everybody down: you bend to the will of this Bible or you’ll be dead. That’s what the Most High is basically saying. There’s no ifs, ands or buts about it; the Most High’s program is going to flow regardless of how anybody feels about it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 30:5-6](/bible/proverbs/30#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/30#v5)</sup> Every word of God is pure: he is a shield unto them that put their trust in him.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/30#v6)</sup> Add thou not unto his words, lest he reprove thee, and thou be found a liar.

    This is the sound doctrine. “Every word of God is pure”: this is the pure doctrine on the planet earth, and it can’t be gainsaid. “He is a shield unto them that put their trust in him”: the words are the Most High’s words and they represent him. He is a shield, a protector, to those who put their trust in the words of God.
