## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Timothy 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-13, 15-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Timothy 3:1-2](/bible/2-timothy/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-timothy/3#v1)</sup> This know also, that in the last days perilous times shall come.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/2-timothy/3#v2)</sup> For men shall be lovers of their own selves, covetous, boasters, proud, blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful, unholy,

- We were warned: “This know also, that in the last days perilous times shall come.”
- “Covetous, boasters, proud.” That prideful spirit won't allow you to take any correction, won't allow you to accept what is being said, and will have you thinking it is everybody but you.
- “For men shall be lovers of their own selves”: that is why such a man will not hold himself accountable, because he loves himself. Look at my Prada, my Gucci, my gator shoes, a leader on IG arguing about whether Drake or Kendrick is the best rapper. These are elders? No, children.
- “Blasphemers, disobedient to parents, unthankful”: unthankful to those who came before them. They fell, but they built the foundation you walk on today, and you spit in their face. Disrespectful, shiftless, grimy.

  Precepts:
  - **[Daniel 2:40-42](/bible/daniel/2#v40)**

    >
    > <sup>[41](/bible/daniel/2#v41)</sup> And whereas thou sawest the feet and toes, part of potters’ clay, and part of iron, the kingdom shall be divided; but there shall be in it of the strength of the iron, forasmuch as thou sawest the iron mixed with miry clay.
    >

What are you talking about? Did you forget how you arrived? It wasn’t on a yacht. It wasn’t first class, negro.
  - **[Philippians 2:3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)**

**[2 Timothy 3:3-4](/bible/2-timothy/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-timothy/3#v3)</sup> Without natural affection, trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/2-timothy/3#v4)</sup> Traitors, heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God;

- They are “trucebreakers, false accusers, incontinent, fierce, despisers of those that are good, Traitors”. There it is: traitors. I used to be a Hebrew Israelite, why I left. Why announce your departure? Just go. Nobody held you hostage or put a gun to your head. Wanting to be heard is emotional, feminine. But you are a traitor, going to the white man to say, I was there and I’ll tell you all the evil they were doing, when you saw all the good, and now all the good is evil.
- Some who left are atheists now and come into the rooms trying to make others stop believing. If you no longer believe, don’t believe by yourself; why do we have to unbelieve too? Like a woman who comes in where women are working on themselves and says all men are bad: let them figure it out for themselves. Misery loves company. They want people to leave with them and die with them in the world. And when they leave they go right back to Christianity, the streets, Christmas, New Year’s and birthdays, and they get worse, never better. Outside of us, what are you in the Bible? Your family says welcome back, now we can all die together.
- “False accusers”: how many run their mouths and gossip without understanding, without the full knowledge of what is going on, yet have so many opinions about what so-and-so is doing and shouldn't be doing.
- “Despisers of those that are good.” Some people just hate that you are doing what you are supposed to do: that you are above them in rank, that you have more time to put in for the truth, that you are active in the body doing things they can't do. So they hate everything you put out and have a problem with every order you give. Every time you turn around they have a suggestion. Nobody asked you. And you don't say it to them; you tell everybody underneath them, all your peers. These are the ones you never know for exhorting anybody, only for bringing problems about everything they see. If they ever did exhort, it would be strange.

**[2 Timothy 3:5](/bible/2-timothy/3#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-timothy/3#v5)</sup> Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away.

- Saddam said, we don’t care about appearances, we care about results. That hit the bomb. To hell with appearances; we want to see results, brothers and sisters, and God wants to see results. “Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away.” God says I don’t care about your appearances: you got a form of godliness, but when it comes down to the nitty-gritty you ain’t in the battle, you ain’t really in this fight, you ain’t really obedient. What about the results? What are you doing? What he was saying to them, the Bible is saying to us. Let that marinate, meditate on that.
- “Having a form of godliness”. A lot of them have a form of godliness, “but denying the power thereof”. They deny the power God gives all of us. Their actions, not their words, show that they hate the Bible, hate us, hate God and hate God's people.
- “from such turn away.” You Christians in those churches better get away from these demons. May the Most High bless those who came out of that garbage. You're leaving that foolishness, coming into this truth, and learning what you need to do before Christ comes or before you leave this earth.
- “Having a form of godliness, but denying the power thereof: from such turn away.” That form of godliness is the double-mindedness. You sit down, brothers surround you, you teach for one, two, three hours, go to Corinthians, go to Titus, go to Genesis, let me give you the breakdown, and your wife is dumb as a doornail when it comes to the scriptures. She doesn’t know the colour scriptures, no basic scriptures, nothing, because you don’t teach her, and when she asks a question you get mad.

The same demons are back on this earth, resisting this truth, fighting the word of God and fighting change. Our people want change, but they don't want to change.
