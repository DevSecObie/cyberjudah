## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Timothy 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 6-18, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Timothy 1:6](/bible/2-timothy/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-timothy/1#v6)</sup> Wherefore I put thee in remembrance that thou stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands.

- That is what Paul was saying to Timothy in the first letter, and he continues in the second. “Stir up the gift of God, which is in thee by the putting on of my hands”: Timothy, you have the Holy Spirit on you; stir that gift up, don’t let it lie. Not to stir it up is to bury your talent in the ground, to put it in a napkin like the parables Christ gave, and do nothing with it.

**[2 Timothy 1:7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)</sup> For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

- “For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.” Paul is telling Timothy, you have nothing to be afraid of. Do the work. Stir up the gift of the Holy Spirit within you. Why does he mention fear? Because of the afflictions that were going on.
- “For God hath not given us the spirit of fear.” None of us should have the spirit of fear. How do you not have it? By expecting the unexpected: reading, reading, reading, getting familiar with the scriptures and the prophecies, so that when it happens you already knew it was going to happen. If you have already set your mind that they are going to kill you one day for this, then when Esau shows up, fear will not jump on you. It will not be easy, but prepare your mind.
- Prepare your mind so that fear does not get on you so badly that you betray the brother or the sister who sits next to you. Some of you are going to betray based on fear. Esau says, I'm going to tell the congregation you were watching gay porn, and you are afraid, so you betray your brothers because you don't want that to come out and the men to look at you different. You were not prepared; your mind was never in the scriptures, you were on porn, and that is why you got caught up out there. Prepare yourself.
- With AI today they are going to set a lot of us up. Esau is so evil; it is already coming. They could show a video of the deacon effing a goat, and some of our own people are going to believe it with no proof: oh yeah, I know the man, that's not AI. They will believe it because they did not build their minds in the scriptures.

**[2 Timothy 1:8](/bible/2-timothy/1#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/2-timothy/1#v8)</sup> Be not thou therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but be thou partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God;

- “Be not thou therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner”: don’t be ashamed of Christ, and don’t be ashamed of me. “But be thou partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God.” Afflictions were on the rise for the believers, and that is why Paul was telling Timothy not to be afraid.
- In closing: it’s time for us to ramp up and tap into this full potential. Of course we’re going to watch and pray; we have to know what’s going on, but don’t let that put fear in you, family.
- “Be not thou therefore ashamed of the testimony of our Lord, nor of me his prisoner: but be thou partaker of the afflictions of the gospel according to the power of God”. Like it says in Matthew 24:14, us going through these afflictions is making a witness to the power of God, because Christ already told us this would happen. So by us going through this boldly, with fortitude, trusting and believing and carrying his will out, that’s a witness against the nations.

**[2 Timothy 1:9](/bible/2-timothy/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-timothy/1#v9)</sup> Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began,

- “Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling, not according to our works.” We all have that holy calling, and we are not called into this truth because of some good works we did, something so magnanimous that God said, I’ll choose this person. “But according to his own purpose and grace”: God saw something in you that you didn’t know was there. I’ll call that brother and that sister; I see greatness in them.
- “Which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began.” We were with the Lord in the beginning, all of us, and we were given a gift. What Paul says to Timothy he is saying to us. We used to say, I ain’t nobody, I ain’t got nothing in me. No: you have more than you think. We were given understanding, a gift, before the world began.
- “Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling”: a holy calling. “Not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began”. We have to figure out God’s purpose, and ultimately we have to be worthy of the calling.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 1:4](/bible/ephesians/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/ephesians/1#v4)</sup> According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love:

    Paul said the same thing to the brothers at Ephesus: “According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world.”

**[2 Timothy 1:10](/bible/2-timothy/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-timothy/1#v10)</sup> But is now made manifest by the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who hath abolished death, and hath brought life and immortality to light through the gospel:

- “But is now made manifest by the appearing of our Saviour Jesus Christ, who hath abolished death.” When Christ rose from the dead, he abolished death: by believing in me, I abolished death; I’m the King, I’m the Lord; come unto me.
- “And hath brought life and immortality to light through the gospel”: life and immortality come to light only through the gospel, the good news.

**[2 Timothy 1:11](/bible/2-timothy/1#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-timothy/1#v11)</sup> Whereunto I am appointed a preacher, and an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.

- “Whereunto I am appointed a preacher”: preacher means prophet. “And an apostle, and a teacher of the Gentiles.” Paul had a multitude of gifts.

**[2 Timothy 1:12](/bible/2-timothy/1#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-timothy/1#v12)</sup> For the which cause I also suffer these things: nevertheless I am not ashamed: for I know whom I have believed, and am persuaded that he is able to keep that which I have committed unto him against that day.

- “For the which cause I also suffer these things”: because he is appointed a preacher, an apostle and a teacher, he suffers the afflictions. “Nevertheless I am not ashamed”: I might be in and out of jail for preaching this gospel, but I’m not ashamed and I’m not going to stop.
- “For I know whom I have believed, and am persuaded that he is able to keep that which I have committed unto him against that day.” Paul committed his soul, his life, to Christ. And that day is the second coming.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 121:7](/bible/psalms/121#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/121#v7)</sup> The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.

    “The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil: he shall preserve thy soul.”
  - **[2 Thessalonians 2:1-3](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v1)</sup> Now we beseech you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and by our gathering together unto him,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v2)</sup> That ye be not soon shaken in mind, or be troubled, neither by spirit, nor by word, nor by letter as from us, as that the day of Christ is at hand.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v3)</sup> Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition;

    This explains the day Paul is talking about. “Now we beseech you, brethren, by the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ, and by our gathering together unto him”: all twelve tribes can only gather unto Christ.
  - **[Luke 21:24](/bible/luke/21#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/luke/21#v24)</sup> And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.

    What is the falling away? “And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations”: they shall fall, meaning all twelve tribes shall fall.

**[2 Timothy 1:13](/bible/2-timothy/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-timothy/1#v13)</sup> Hold fast the form of sound words, which thou hast heard of me, in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.

- “Hold fast the form of sound words”: the doctrine we are teaching, the gospel, is sound words. Hold fast to that; don’t let it go. If we hold fast to these sound words, it will enrich us personally, in our marriages, in our families, as a community and as a nation.
- “Hold fast the form of sound words, which thou hast heard of me”: the words leadership has taught you, “in faith and love which is in Christ Jesus.” Those sound words are also the words you should be studying to show yourself approved unto God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 10:17](/bible/romans/10#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/romans/10#v17)</sup> So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

    “So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” Faith, belief, comes by hearing, and the word of God builds your faith unto salvation.
  - **[Ephesians 6:16](/bible/ephesians/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/ephesians/6#v16)</sup> Above all, taking the shield of faith, wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked.

    “Above all, taking the shield of faith”, and faith comes by hearing the word of God. What do you do with a shield? You are on guard; you are protecting. “Wherewith ye shall be able to quench all the fiery darts of the wicked”: but only if you build up your faith with the word of God. Otherwise the fiery darts of the wicked penetrate and take you out.

**[2 Timothy 1:14](/bible/2-timothy/1#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-timothy/1#v14)</sup> That good thing which was committed unto thee keep by the Holy Ghost which dwelleth in us.

- “That good thing which was committed unto thee keep by the Holy Ghost which dwelleth in us.” That is why he said, stir up the gift that is within you.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 14:26](/bible/john/14#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/john/14#v26)</sup> But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you.

    “But the Comforter, which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my name, he shall teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance”: your history, back to your remembrance.

**[2 Timothy 1:15](/bible/2-timothy/1#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-timothy/1#v15)</sup> This thou knowest, that all they which are in Asia be turned away from me; of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.

- “All they which are in Asia be turned away from me; of whom are Phygellus and Hermogenes.” Men that stood strong in this gospel, men that warred with Paul, left. They left the faith.
- This is what we have warned you brothers and sisters about. We have seen a lot come and a lot go, so don’t get too attached. You’re strong today, but we’ll see about tomorrow, when affliction, tribulation and persecution come. That is why Paul told Timothy, stir up the gift, and God has not given us the spirit of fear: because of the afflictions.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Timothy 4:10](/bible/2-timothy/4#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-timothy/4#v10)</sup> For Demas hath forsaken me, having loved this present world, and is departed unto Thessalonica; Crescens to Galatia, Titus unto Dalmatia.

    “For Demas hath forsaken me, having loved this present world, and is departed unto Thessalonica.” Demas travelled with Paul and warred with Paul, and he forsook him. Why? He loved this present world. Can I get a piece of ass? He loved the things he saw, the looseness of the women: Paul, I got to go, I can’t roll with you no more, I got needs. And he let that overcome him.
  - **[Mark 4:16-19](/bible/mark/4#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/mark/4#v16)</sup> And these are they likewise which are sown on stony ground; who, when they have heard the word, immediately receive it with gladness;
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/mark/4#v17)</sup> And have no root in themselves, and so endure but for a time: afterward, when affliction or persecution ariseth for the word’s sake, immediately they are offended.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/mark/4#v18)</sup> And these are they which are sown among thorns; such as hear the word,
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/mark/4#v19)</sup> And the cares of this world, and the deceitfulness of riches, and the lusts of other things entering in, choke the word, and it becometh unfruitful.

    This is what happened to Paul’s companions, who were strong in the Lord and the power of his might. Some of you are those guys.
  - **[2 Timothy 4:14-16](/bible/2-timothy/4#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-timothy/4#v14)</sup> Alexander the coppersmith did me much evil: the Lord reward him according to his works:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-timothy/4#v15)</sup> Of whom be thou ware also; for he hath greatly withstood our words.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/2-timothy/4#v16)</sup> At my first answer no man stood with me, but all men forsook me: I pray God that it may not be laid to their charge.

    “Alexander the coppersmith did me much evil: the Lord reward him according to his works.” Here is another that was with Paul and turned on him; he disrespected Paul when that great Diana affair was going on.

**[2 Timothy 1:16-17](/bible/2-timothy/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/2-timothy/1#v16)</sup> The Lord give mercy unto the house of Onesiphorus; for he oft refreshed me, and was not ashamed of my chain:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-timothy/1#v17)</sup> But, when he was in Rome, he sought me out very diligently, and found me.

- “The Lord give mercy unto the house of Onesiphorus; for he oft refreshed me, and was not ashamed of my chain.” Paul prays God bless this brother: he was not ashamed that Paul was locked up in prison. Paul was telling Timothy not to be ashamed of the gospel because he saw that spirit coming on Timothy.
- “But, when he was in Rome, he sought me out very diligently, and found me.” There were no cell phones then; he had to go on foot and find Paul.
- This is for the people that love us and are not going to turn their back on us. They are going to be rewarded for holding us down. When the ADL and the Southern Poverty Law Center are trying to shame us, they’re going to say, these are the men of the Lord, and go out of their way to help them.
- “The Lord give mercy unto the house of Onesiphorus; for he oft refreshed me, and was not ashamed of my chain.” When Paul went to prison, people were ashamed of him. But this man said, this is a man of God; they locked him up for doing the Lord’s work. He was not ashamed to back him.

**[2 Timothy 1:18](/bible/2-timothy/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/2-timothy/1#v18)</sup> The Lord grant unto him that he may find mercy of the Lord in that day: and in how many things he ministered unto me at Ephesus, thou knowest very well.

- “The Lord grant unto him that he may find mercy of the Lord in that day”: that same day we have been reading about, the day of the Lord. “And in how many things he ministered unto me at Ephesus, thou knowest very well”: this brother even helped Paul in Ephesus, and Timothy knew it.
- “The Lord grant unto him that he may find mercy of the Lord in that day”: in that day, when judgment comes, his house is spared. Why? Because he cared. So the people that hold us down when we are going through the trials, the ones who say, if something happens to him, bring his whole family here, tell his wife she don’t have to worry, we’re going to cover everything, they will find mercy in that day.
- “And in how many things he ministered unto me at Ephesus, thou knowest very well.” Y’all think the Most High is not watching. Some of you are here because the Most High knows that you care. The other ones that are moving fake, get out of here.
