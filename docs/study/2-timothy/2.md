## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 2 Timothy 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-5, 15-16, 19-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[2 Timothy 2:2](/bible/2-timothy/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-timothy/2#v2)</sup> And the things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.

- “The things that thou hast heard of me among many witnesses, the same commit thou to faithful men, who shall be able to teach others also.” What is heard is committed to faithful men, so that they may be able to teach others also.

**[2 Timothy 2:3](/bible/2-timothy/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-timothy/2#v3)</sup> Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.

- “Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.” All of us are good soldiers of Jesus Christ, from Bishops to soldiers, every last one of us. Paul was a soldier, Peter was a soldier, James was a soldier, John was a soldier. We’re all soldiers.
- So we got to understand that hardness will come. I don’t get excited a lot like some brothers, because I see the picture of what’s going to happen, and I know that certain brothers will not endure hardness when it comes. That hardness is persecution, it’s tribulation, even what we went over today. How many people are offended by us just reading all the names of our brothers and sisters that were killed and asking the Lord for vengeance? That sent spiritual shock waves through the internet. People get pissed off by that, black and white, mad at that: these guys no good, you hear what they’re reading the Bible. Persecution will come. Don’t sleep.
- We watch these things, like the ICE agent who shot a woman three times when all he had to do was take her license plate, and we get mad and emotional. You can feel rage coming in. That is the might makes right mentality: it causes them to dehumanize everybody, including their own, because that was a white woman. But we have to reflect back on this scripture.
- “Thou therefore endure hardness, as a good soldier of Jesus Christ.” Christ called us into this ministry to be soldiers. Every one of us in here is a soldier.

When Israel fell away, he used those enemies to make war against Israel and bring the people into slavery. His being a man of war does not mean he protects disobedience from its consequences.

**[2 Timothy 2:4](/bible/2-timothy/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-timothy/2#v4)</sup> No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life; that he may please him who hath chosen him to be a soldier.

- So now people say, why don’t you stand up for the Palestinians? No, we on God’s side.
- “No man that warreth”: brothers and sisters, we are at war, and it’s a spiritual war that we’re in. “Entangleth himself with the affairs of this life.” Why? Because prophecies are going to come. Stay out of God’s work. When he’s allowing these nations to devastate and create catastrophes, just move out of God’s way. Remember King Josiah interfered in God’s work and got put to death for it. So we not going out there protesting; we ain’t picking sides.
- “That he may please him”: our job is to please him, “who hath chosen him to be a soldier.” The Lord called us to be soldiers. Our job is to please the Lord, and that’s why we got to remove our emotions out of it. You see this devastation going on with the Palestinians and you want to say or do something, but the Lord said, mind your business, gather the Israelites, that’s what I called you to do.
- “No man that warreth entangleth himself with the affairs of this life”: don’t get caught up in it. That’s why even when we do certain things we got to make sure we’re not standing up for drug dealers, pimps, prostitutes. Pooky killed half the block, now he get killed: we’re not marching for that. Pooky was a murderer. Pooky murdered half the block. We’ll stand for brothers like Kendrick Johnson; we’re going to check that story out.

Any Israelite camp that is busy slandering and speaking much evil about other camps, to draw people away, is not of God; that camp ain’t getting the kingdom, so it is not getting a crown. People say why don’t you retaliate? We’re not going to roll like that. Those are our brothers; we are one people and we must come together as one. If that group don’t see that, avoid them like the plague.
- “And if a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, except he strive lawfully.” You have to strive lawfully.
- “And if a man also strive for masteries, yet is he not crowned, except he strive lawfully.” If you aspire to be a soldier, an officer, a captain, a deacon or a bishop, you will not be crowned unless you strive lawfully: not undercutting, undermining, slandering or lying. Leaving and crying and complaining will not get you crowned in that day when Christ returns.

**[2 Timothy 2:15](/bible/2-timothy/2#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/2-timothy/2#v15)</sup> Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.

- “Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.” This is one of the first scriptures we learned when we came into this truth, and it is elementary. An elder has men under him looking to him for wisdom, knowledge and understanding. This brother embarrassed himself and his organization, and because he represents the Israelite community, the world labels all of us by him. Never trust a man wearing dark sunglasses inside: he can hide his embarrassment and his eyes. Brother, repent and change your ways.
- We have to take our learning seriously in these last days. If you think Satan is just going to step back and say ‘come on down this narrow road and walk right into the kingdom,’ no: he is going to make it difficult. It is going to be an all-out war against the truth.
- “Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.” You have to study to show yourself approved to God, so that God gives you the spirit of discernment. You have to be able to study, discern and divide up the word: what is the context here, what does this mean here, what does that mean there. Otherwise you just take scriptures like John 3:16 and equate them to everybody, and that is what has happened.
- What else do we have to do? “Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.” Rightly dividing the word of truth lets you know there are a lot of people out there that don’t live up to that standard: a lot of people are dividing the word of God incorrectly. Their breakdowns, their understanding, the hermeneutics, the exegesis, is all incorrect.

**[2 Timothy 2:20](/bible/2-timothy/2#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-timothy/2#v20)</sup> But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth; and some to honour, and some to dishonour.

- But brother, I’m tired? Go sit down, catch a wind, then come back. We are engaged in active combat. Battle fatigue, combat stress: we all go through that, but don’t give up. Never give up, never give in. Keep pushing.
- “But in a great house”: the nation of Israel, even amongst us right here. “There are not only vessels of gold”: amongst all of you men and women there are vessels of gold; some of you are gold. “And of silver”: some of you are vessels of silver in here. “But also of wood”: some of you is no damn good. Some of you are vessels of wood: those of you still hiding your sins, in the midst of adultery, lying, pedophiles on the side, liars and thieves. That’s the wood. “And of earth”: that’s you still no damn good. “And some to honour, and some to dishonour”: some of you are to honour and some of you are to dishonour in this truth.
- I don’t look at things with rose colored glasses and say oh everybody’s perfect. Through years of experience I know some of you come into this truth no damn good; I read that in the Bible, so I know that. But some of you I know are gold and silver, and some of you are trying your best to be that gold and silver. You will struggle, you will strive, like we all do.
- This is catharsis in the Bible: purgative, purging, cleansing. “But in a great house there are not only vessels of gold and of silver, but also of wood and of earth.” In that great house, some vessels will burn and some are pure and cannot burn. The house will burn down if you have tinder in it, the wood and the earth.

You men, you women, through conversation you’re going to know who’s gold, who’s silver, who’s wood and who’s earth. Leave them to themselves; let the Lord deal with them, let them fight and struggle. If you decide that’s your friend, when they go down you go down too.

You see it on a grand scale in us as a people, and you see it in brothers and sisters individually who have bitterness in their hearts; those things eat you up inside and burn you up.
- “He shall be a vessel unto honour, sanctified”, which means cleansed, “and meet for the master’s use”: then you are ready to serve the Lord, and you cannot be while you hold those things in. “And prepared unto every good work.” That is part of the transforming process that repentance does for you when you purge it out of you. We have to be able to tell our story and get those things off our chest.
