## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Luke 19 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-3, 7-27, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Luke 19:2](/bible/luke/19#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/luke/19#v2)</sup> And, behold, there was a man named Zaccheus, which was the chief among the publicans, and he was rich.

- Zaccheus was another bourgeois, a wealthy individual. “The chief among the publicans, and he was rich.” The publicans were the tax collectors for Rome.

**[Luke 19:3](/bible/luke/19#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/luke/19#v3)</sup> And he sought to see Jesus who he was; and could not for the press, because he was little of stature.

- “Because he was little of stature”: he might have been five foot, five foot two. He was short.

**[Luke 19:24-26](/bible/luke/19#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/luke/19#v24)</sup> And he said unto them that stood by, Take from him the pound, and give it to him that hath ten pounds.

- “And he said unto them that stood by, Take from him the pound, and give it to him that hath ten pounds. (And they said unto him, Lord, he hath ten pounds.) For I say unto you, That unto every one which hath shall be given; and from him that hath not, even that he hath shall be taken away from him.” That's what we just read in Matthew 13:12, saying the same thing.
- Now watch this. The brother whose pound gained ten pounds became lord of how many cities? Ten. The one whose pound gained five pounds had five cities. So the one that had one pound and didn't do anything was supposed to have authority over one city, and the Lord said, take that city and give it to the one that has ten cities.
- “And he said unto them that stood by”: these are the soldiers, the men around the throne. Imagine a king giving orders to the men next to the throne, like in Game of Thrones. It is not going to be a crowd; it is pure order.
- “From him that hath not, even that he hath shall be taken away from him.” His one pound was supposed to make him lord over one city, and it is taken from him. A brother in the class added: we have seen brothers come among us with certain skills who did not expand on them, so the Most High, in the musical chairs, moved them out of the way. Other brothers came in, took that office and ran with it, and you hear nothing of the men who had the ability.
