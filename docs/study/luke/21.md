    > <sup>[15](/bible/job/33#v15)</sup> In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, in slumberings upon the bed;
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/job/33#v16)</sup> Then he openeth the ears of men, and sealeth their instruction,
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/job/33#v17)</sup> That he may withdraw man from his purpose, and hide pride from man.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/job/33#v18)</sup> He keepeth back his soul from the pit, and his life from perishing by the sword.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/job/33#v19)</sup> He is chastened also with pain upon his bed, and the multitude of his bones with strong pain:
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/job/33#v20)</sup> So that his life abhorreth bread, and his soul dainty meat.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/job/33#v21)</sup> His flesh is consumed away, that it cannot be seen; and his bones that were not seen stick out.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/job/33#v22)</sup> Yea, his soul draweth near unto the grave, and his life to the destroyers.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/job/33#v23)</sup> If there be a messenger with him, an interpreter, one among a thousand, to shew unto man his uprightness:
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/job/33#v24)</sup> Then he is gracious unto him, and saith, Deliver him from going down to the pit: I have found a ransom.

    “In a dream, in a vision of the night, when deep sleep falleth upon men, in slumberings upon the bed; Then he openeth the ears of men, and sealeth their instruction”. When we are asleep, that is when God opens our minds to his instructions and seals them.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 7:27-28](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v27)</sup> Behold, this have I found, saith the preacher, counting one by one, to find out the account:
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v28)</sup> Which yet my soul seeketh, but I find not: one man among a thousand have I found; but a woman among all those have I not found.

    “Behold, this have I found, saith the preacher, counting one by one, to find out the account: Which yet my soul seeketh, but I find not: one man among a thousand have I found”. Solomon found one man among a thousand that sought after God. “But a woman among all those have I not found.” Solomon had a thousand women, and out of all of them not one sought after the Lord.
  - **[2 Esdras 16:70-73](/bible/2-esdras/16#v70)**
    > <sup>[70](/bible/2-esdras/16#v70)</sup> For there shall be in every place, and in the next cities, a great insurrection upon those that fear the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[71](/bible/2-esdras/16#v71)</sup> They shall be like mad men, sparing none, but still spoiling and destroying those that fear the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[72](/bible/2-esdras/16#v72)</sup> For they shall waste and take away their goods, and cast them out of their houses.
    >
    > <sup>[73](/bible/2-esdras/16#v73)</sup> Then shall they be known, who are my chosen; and they shall be tried as the gold in the fire.

    “For there shall be in every place, and in the next cities, a great insurrection upon those that fear the Lord.” What we saw with the brother and the sister who lost their homes, we pray the Most High be merciful to them, but things are going to escalate towards our people.
