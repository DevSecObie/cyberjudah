## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Luke 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 15-23, 26-33, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Luke 14:15-16](/bible/luke/14#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/luke/14#v15)</sup> And when one of them that sat at meat with him heard these things, he said unto him, Blessed is he that shall eat bread in the kingdom of God.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/luke/14#v16)</sup> Then said he unto him, A certain man made a great supper, and bade many:

- “Blessed is he that shall eat bread in the kingdom of God.” Now he is going to start the parable. “A certain man made a great supper, and bade many”: a certain man made a big supper and invited many to come.

**[Luke 14:17](/bible/luke/14#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/luke/14#v17)</sup> And sent his servant at supper time to say to them that were bidden, Come; for all things are now ready.

- “Come; for all things are now ready”: come, for all things are now ready for this dinner.

**[Luke 14:18-20](/bible/luke/14#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/14#v18)</sup> And they all with one consent began to make excuse. The first said unto him, I have bought a piece of ground, and I must needs go and see it: I pray thee have me excused.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/luke/14#v19)</sup> And another said, I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I go to prove them: I pray thee have me excused.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/14#v20)</sup> And another said, I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come.

- “And they all with one consent began to make excuse.” The Pharisees, the scribes, all of them are in here; that’s these religious leaders today, all making excuses for not doing the work. A piece of ground, five yoke of oxen, “I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come.” Nobody wants to do the work.

**[Luke 14:21-22](/bible/luke/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/luke/14#v21)</sup> So that servant came, and shewed his lord these things. Then the master of the house being angry said to his servant, Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in hither the poor, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blind.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/luke/14#v22)</sup> And the servant said, Lord, it is done as thou hast commanded, and yet there is room.

- “Then the master of the house being angry said to his servant, Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in hither the poor, and the maimed, and the halt, and the blind”: bring these people to the supper. “And yet there is room”: there is still room left.
- What is our job as Israelites? “Go out quickly into the streets and lanes of the city, and bring in hither the poor.” Bring in. Do you know the honor it is to be used to bring somebody in? That is what we want to be used for. Our job is to bring people in. You sisters are being used too: when you hand a flyer, that flyer could change a life, because you are bringing them in.

  - **[Isaiah 42:22](/bible/isaiah/42#v22)**

    Here is another place we have to go to bring them in. “But this is a people robbed and spoiled; they are all of them snared in holes, and they are hid in prison houses.” We are robbed and spoiled, snared in the traps of this society, and we fill the majority of the prisons. “They are for a prey, and none delivereth; for a spoil, and none saith, Restore.” Somebody has to go and bring them in: the brothers in the streets, in the prisons, in the jails, wherever they are.

**[Luke 14:23](/bible/luke/14#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/luke/14#v23)</sup> And the lord said unto the servant, Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.

- “Go out into the highways and hedges, and compel them to come in, that my house may be filled.” Compel our people. Brothers, sisters, that’s our job, and it starts first with the men. Every sanctuary has to be filled, and there’s never enough people, because once we fill up that house we get a new one. This is all a parable of going into the kingdom, but look at it also with these sanctuaries: we are going out and compelling our people to come in.
- On the fly mission, giving out flyers, a brother says, ‘Nah, I’m good.’ We don’t accept no, unless you get that one demon that just straight up wants to fight, or some white people that just say no. A lot of our people are saying no just because they’re clueless: ‘Nah brother, I’m good.’ Nah brother, you ain’t good, come back over here. That’s how we have to deal, because whenever we give out these flyers our people are going to say no.
- Compel them means we are not giving our people an option: you must come in. This is the truth without a shadow of a doubt; that’s what we’re showing our people. But you have to make your calling and election sure; that’s your job. So why are some brothers still confused? What are you to be confused about? Our people are being oppressed, and the Most High called you to help bring them in. ‘Is God dealing with me? I don’t know’: brother, get the hell out the way. The reason you’re in here is because the Most High called you, so what are you doubting for?

**[Luke 14:27](/bible/luke/14#v27)**

- “And whosoever doth not bear his cross, and come after me, cannot be my disciple”: Christ says you can’t be his disciple if you aren’t willing to carry your cross, your burdens, and follow after him.

**[Luke 14:28-30](/bible/luke/14#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/luke/14#v28)</sup> For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/luke/14#v29)</sup> Lest haply, after he hath laid the foundation, and is not able to finish it, all that behold it begin to mock him,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/14#v30)</sup> Saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish.

- “which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost”: you had better sit down and count the cost. “all that behold it begin to mock him, Saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish.” People will mock you if you start in this truth and give up. Brothers and sisters, you talk all that good talk to your family, telling them they are wicked, and then you go back: they are going to laugh at you.
- “For which of you, intending to build a tower, sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?” That tower is your walk with the Lord: whether or not you are going to be able to finish your path to the kingdom. Can you continue in the spirit of Christ keeping the commandments till you die or the sky cracks?
- “Sitteth not down first, and counteth the cost, whether he have sufficient to finish it?” He did not count the cost; neither did Nick Cannon, shamed by Candace Owens. And the money: hundreds of millions, the hooks they have in it, and standing to lose it all.
- “Lest haply, after he hath laid the foundation, and is not able to finish it, all that behold it begin to mock him, Saying, This man began to build, and was not able to finish.” All of us in this truth are building. We are putting in bricks.

Do not be dismayed by them; stay focused on the mission, harden your face and your spirit constantly, or you will be confounded by the wicked.

**[Luke 14:33](/bible/luke/14#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/luke/14#v33)</sup> So likewise, whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple.

- “So likewise, whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple”: this is the point of hating father, mother and children. If you are not willing to forsake all you have and make sacrifices, you cannot be Christ’s disciple, and that includes your own life. You have to be willing to die for what you believe in.
- “So likewise, whosoever he be of you that forsaketh not all that he hath, he cannot be my disciple.”
- So what is Christ doing with this parable? He is giving us examples of the pain, the struggles and the obstacles we are going to face, to make us mentally prepared before whatever happens, so that we don’t get deceived thinking this truth is a cakewalk. It is not. It is war. That is why our leadership in these last days keeps telling us: stock up, prepare for famine, study, pray, apply, endure, hold the line. There will be things that come against you.
