## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Luke 17 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 20-21, 26-32, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Luke 17:20-21](/bible/luke/17#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/17#v20)</sup> And when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come, he answered them and said, The kingdom of God cometh not with observation:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/luke/17#v21)</sup> Neither shall they say, Lo here! or, lo there! for, behold, the kingdom of God is within you.

- A lot of you have the spirit of the Pharisees: you don't believe, and all they wanted was things for gain and positions that benefit only them. Watch what Christ had to tell them. “And when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come,” You don't understand that what we are doing now is building the kingdom of God, and the Pharisees were in opposition to Christ establishing the kingdom of God. Some of you are just watching online to see when this kingdom is coming; you don't care about building it, about putting your brick in. If class doesn't go on, where they at? You don't care, you ain't doing nothing to make sure it happens from one week to the next, but you are right there demanding answers: break this down, break that down. “he answered them and said, The kingdom of God cometh not with observation:” That is what a lot of you people are doing: just observing. You don't want to do nothing, you don't want to help; the kingdom of God doesn't come by your observation. I don't care if you watch class; when you call and say I watch all your classes, well, what are you doing? Don't call here and waste my ears; we are at war.
- Everybody want to know when should the kingdom of God come. “The kingdom of God cometh not with observation”: it doesn’t come with eye service. Brothers and sisters, I’m just going to sit here and look, that’s going to make the kingdom come? No, it’s not.
- “And when he was demanded of the Pharisees, when the kingdom of God should come”: that is what they were pressing Christ for. “The kingdom of God cometh not with observation”: you can’t just sit back and squeeze your eyes, oh, it’s coming, it’s going to land here. The Most High wants work that demonstrates you deserve it. Who will commit to your trust the true riches? You have to demonstrate that you can handle yourself with righteous behaviour in the kingdom. The Most High doesn’t want you messing his kingdom up with your wickedness.
- The best place to practise getting it out is right here, right now. That is why the wilderness and the tabernacles matter: that is the place to iron it out. Before we go into the literal kingdom, we are going through a major tabernacle, recorded in Ezekiel, where he makes us pass under the rod, and the ones that don’t make it will die right there.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 1:1-4](/bible/genesis/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/1#v1)</sup> In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/1#v2)</sup> And the earth was without form and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/1#v3)</sup> And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/1#v4)</sup> And God saw the light, that it was good: and God divided the light from the darkness.

    “In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth. And the earth was without form and void.” The earth was not even in physical existence yet, but it still existed in God’s mind: if you are going to call it earth, it existed, and where did it exist? In God’s mind. “And darkness was upon the face of the deep”: the deep is the elements God used to fashion the earth. It had to exist in his mind first, and he manipulated the elements to bring them into the form of what was in his mind. “And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters”: upon the elements.
  - **[1 Chronicles 28:5](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v5)</sup> And of all my sons, (for the Lord hath given me many sons,) he hath chosen Solomon my son to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.

    Some think the kingdom of heaven means living up in the sky. David says the Lord “hath chosen Solomon my son to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.” The kingdom of God, the kingdom of heaven, was Solomon ruling over Israel. It is about rulership of this earth, about land, and our being put back in our land.
  - **[Acts 1:6](/bible/acts/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/acts/1#v6)</sup> When they therefore were come together, they asked of him, saying, Lord, wilt thou at this time restore again the kingdom to Israel?

    “Lord, wilt thou at this time restore again the kingdom to Israel?” The Romans were ruling Israel then, with Herod set up under them, so the apostles were asking Christ: are you going to start the revolution now, kill the Romans and give us back our land? A revolution is always about land. There cannot be a revolution without land.

**[Luke 17:26-27](/bible/luke/17#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/luke/17#v26)</sup> And as it was in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/luke/17#v27)</sup> They did eat, they drank, they married wives, they were given in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark, and the flood came, and destroyed them all.

- Now to bring it to modern time. “As it was in the days of Noe, so shall it be also in the days of the Son of man.” In Noah’s time the wicked ruled over the people and our people followed them, the same as today. “They did eat, they drank, they married wives”: we dwell carelessly in Babylon, until the flood came.

**[Luke 17:28-30](/bible/luke/17#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/luke/17#v28)</sup> Likewise also as it was in the days of Lot; they did eat, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded;
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/luke/17#v29)</sup> But the same day that Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/17#v30)</sup> Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed.

- “Likewise also as it was in the days of Lot”: they bought, sold, planted and builded, life as usual, until fire and brimstone. Even so in the day the Son of man is revealed: everyone unsuspecting, living carelessly.

**[Luke 17:31-32](/bible/luke/17#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/luke/17#v31)</sup> In that day, he which shall be upon the housetop, and his stuff in the house, let him not come down to take it away: and he that is in the field, let him likewise not return back.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/luke/17#v32)</sup> Remember Lot’s wife.

- He on the housetop, let him not come down; he in the field, let him not return back: don’t look back when this place is destroyed, don’t miss it. “Remember Lot’s wife.” This place has an end. If you miss it, want to stay, think America will last forever, want to bring Babylon with you into the kingdom, that is your Lot’s wife: you are looking back because you are comfortable here. Noah and Lot were not comfortable.
- Those who turn their backs on this truth are the same people who ignored Noah when the flood came and ignored Lot when the fire and brimstone came, and they will ignore us when Christ comes. It is the same mentality.

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 9:59-62](/bible/luke/9#v59)**
    > <sup>[59](/bible/luke/9#v59)</sup> And he said unto another, Follow me. But he said, Lord, suffer me first to go and bury my father.
    >
    > <sup>[60](/bible/luke/9#v60)</sup> Jesus said unto him, Let the dead bury their dead: but go thou and preach the kingdom of God.
    >
    > <sup>[61](/bible/luke/9#v61)</sup> And another also said, Lord, I will follow thee; but let me first go bid them farewell, which are at home at my house.
    >
    > <sup>[62](/bible/luke/9#v62)</sup> And Jesus said unto him, No man, having put his hand to the plough, and looking back, is fit for the kingdom of God.

    “Lord, suffer me first to go and bury my father.” “Let the dead bury their dead: but go thou and preach the kingdom of God.” That is cold: the hell with your family, they are all in the world. Some say that is a cult. No, Christ said, you follow me and do what I say: go preach the kingdom.
