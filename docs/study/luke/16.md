## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Luke 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 9, 13-15, 18-31, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Luke 16:9](/bible/luke/16#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/luke/16#v9)</sup> And I say unto you, Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness; that, when ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.

- This goes with cutting off the family members or friends that cause you to sin. “Make to yourselves friends of the mammon of unrighteousness”: it seems like a contradiction, Christ telling us to make friends of the mammon of unrighteousness. Mammon is wealth regarded as an evil influence or false object of worship; in the Bible it is the term for riches, often used for the debasing influence of material wealth. The mammon of unrighteousness is the people in the world that don’t believe the truth.
- Verses 1 through 8 are the parable of an unjust steward that dealt unwisely and got fired from his job, and he had to deal right with people so they would help him. So Christ says make friends of the mammon of unrighteousness, “that, when ye fail, they may receive you into everlasting habitations.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 3:31](/bible/sirach/3#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/3#v31)</sup> And he that requiteth good turns is mindful of that which may come hereafter; and when he falleth, he shall find a stay.

    Here is the precept for understanding why: “And he that requiteth good turns is mindful of that which may come hereafter; and when he falleth, he shall find a stay.” When you fall you shall find help.

And they had Moses and the prophets right there in their day, and they were still not going to listen to them.
- “And he said, Nay, father Abraham: but if one went unto them from the dead, they will repent. And he said unto him, If they hear not Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded, though one rose from the dead.” If they don’t listen to Moses and the prophets, they won’t even believe if somebody rises from the dead.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 5:46-47](/bible/john/5#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/john/5#v46)</sup> For had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me.
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/john/5#v47)</sup> But if ye believe not his writings, how shall ye believe my words?

    Remember what Abraham told the rich man: they got Moses and the prophets, and if they won’t hear them, they won’t hear one risen from the dead. Christ says the same: “For had ye believed Moses, ye would have believed me: for he wrote of me.” That’s just for all you Christians out there: Moses wrote of Christ. “But if ye believe not his writings, how shall ye believe my words?” And that goes for some of you Israelites that say, I don’t believe in no JC. There’s no magic scripture; some of y’all we cannot help.
  - **[John 12:9-10](/bible/john/12#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/john/12#v9)</sup> Much people of the Jews therefore knew that he was there: and they came not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/john/12#v10)</sup> But the chief priests consulted that they might put Lazarus also to death;

    He said if one went unto them from the dead they will repent. “Much people of the Jews therefore knew that he was there: and they came not for Jesus’ sake only, but that they might see Lazarus also, whom he had raised from the dead.” Christ had just raised Lazarus from the dead. “But the chief priests consulted that they might put Lazarus also to death”. You see how wicked these are? These are wicked as hell. One came back from the dead, and instead of repenting they wanted to kill him again.
  - **[John 12:1](/bible/john/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/john/12#v1)</sup> Then Jesus six days before the passover came to Bethany, where Lazarus was which had been dead, whom he raised from the dead.

    Here’s the evidence that they won’t be persuaded though one rose from the dead. “Then Jesus six days before the passover came to Bethany, where Lazarus was which had been dead, whom he raised from the dead.” Remember in history, Christ raised Lazarus from the dead.
