## Introduction

This session covers Luke 2 through 5. It is a reading session, with precepts brought in where they settle a point rather than a full breakdown. The chapters carry the birth of Christ under the taxing of Caesar, his circumcision and presentation according to the law, the preaching of John, the genealogy back to Adam, the temptation in the wilderness, the reading in the synagogue at Nazareth, and the calling of the first disciples. The reason for reading straight through is given in the epistle: give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 4:13](/bible/1-timothy/4#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/1-timothy/4#v13)</sup> Till I come, give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.

    Till I come, give attendance to reading, to exhortation, to doctrine.

# Luke 3: The Preaching of John and the Generations Back to Adam

**[Luke 3:3-6](/bible/luke/3#v3)**

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/luke/3#v6)</sup> And all flesh shall see the salvation of God.

- John preaches the baptism of repentance for the remission of sins, as it is written in the book of the words of Esaias: the voice of one crying in the wilderness.

**[Luke 3:7-9](/bible/luke/3#v7)**

>
> <sup>[9](/bible/luke/3#v9)</sup> And now also the axe is laid unto the root of the trees: every tree therefore which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.

- O generation of vipers, bring forth therefore fruits worthy of repentance, and begin not to say within yourselves, We have Abraham to our father.
- Being dipped in water without works that go with it is not what is written here. The fruits worthy of repentance are the works.

# Luke 4: The Temptation and the Reading at Nazareth

Read the chapter → [Luke 4](/bible/luke/4)

**[Luke 4:1-4](/bible/luke/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/luke/4#v1)</sup> And Jesus being full of the Holy Ghost returned from Jordan, and was led by the Spirit into the wilderness,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/luke/4#v2)</sup> Being forty days tempted of the devil. And in those days he did eat nothing: and when they were ended, he afterward hungered.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/luke/4#v3)</sup> And the devil said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, command this stone that it be made bread.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/luke/4#v4)</sup> And Jesus answered him, saying, It is written, That man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.

- Being full of the Holy Ghost he is led by the Spirit into the wilderness, forty days tempted of the devil, and in those days he did eat nothing.
- He answers with the law: man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word of God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 8:3](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/8#v3)</sup> And he humbled thee, and suffered thee to hunger, and fed thee with manna, which thou knewest not, neither did thy fathers know; that he might make thee know that man doth not live by bread only, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the Lord doth man live.

    The verse he went back to, written to Israel in the wilderness.

**[Luke 4:5-8](/bible/luke/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/luke/4#v5)</sup> And the devil, taking him up into an high mountain, shewed unto him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/luke/4#v6)</sup> And the devil said unto him, All this power will I give thee, and the glory of them: for that is delivered unto me; and to whomsoever I will I give it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/luke/4#v7)</sup> If thou therefore wilt worship me, all shall be thine.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/luke/4#v8)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto him, Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve.

- The devil shows him all the kingdoms of the world in a moment of time. No man can do that, which settles who was standing there.
- All this power will I give thee, for that is delivered unto me, and to whomsoever I will I give it. The offer is real, and the price is worship.
- Get thee behind me, Satan: for it is written, Thou shalt worship the Lord thy God, and him only shalt thou serve. Again the answer is a commandment.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:13](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v13)</sup> Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and serve him, and shalt swear by his name.

    Thou shalt fear the Lord thy God, and serve him, and shalt swear by his name.

**[Luke 4:9-13](/bible/luke/4#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/luke/4#v9)</sup> And he brought him to Jerusalem, and set him on a pinnacle of the temple, and said unto him, If thou be the Son of God, cast thyself down from hence:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/luke/4#v10)</sup> For it is written, He shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/luke/4#v11)</sup> And in their hands they shall bear thee up, lest at any time thou dash thy foot against a stone.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/luke/4#v12)</sup> And Jesus answering said unto him, It is said, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/4#v13)</sup> And when the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season.

- Set on a pinnacle of the temple and answered with scripture, he replies, It is said, Thou shalt not tempt the Lord thy God.
- When the devil had ended all the temptation, he departed from him for a season. The whole battle was fought with the word, and that is the only way it is won.

**[Luke 4:14-21](/bible/luke/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/4#v14)</sup> And Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit into Galilee: and there went out a fame of him through all the region round about.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/luke/4#v15)</sup> And he taught in their synagogues, being glorified of all.

> <sup>[16](/bible/luke/4#v16)</sup> And he came to Nazareth, where he had been brought up: and, as his custom was, he went into the synagogue on the sabbath day, and stood up for to read.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/luke/4#v17)</sup> And there was delivered unto him the book of the prophet Esaias. And when he had opened the book, he found the place where it was written,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/4#v18)</sup> The Spirit of the Lord is upon me, because he hath anointed me to preach the gospel to the poor; he hath sent me to heal the brokenhearted, to preach deliverance to the captives, and recovering of sight to the blind, to set at liberty them that are bruised,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/luke/4#v19)</sup> To preach the acceptable year of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/4#v20)</sup> And he closed the book, and he gave it again to the minister, and sat down. And the eyes of all them that were in the synagogue were fastened on him.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/luke/4#v21)</sup> And he began to say unto them, This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears.

- This day is this scripture fulfilled in your ears.

# Luke 5: The Draught of Fishes and the Calling of Sinners

Read the chapter → [Luke 5](/bible/luke/5)

**[Luke 5:1-7](/bible/luke/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/luke/5#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, that, as the people pressed upon him to hear the word of God, he stood by the lake of Gennesaret,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/luke/5#v2)</sup> And saw two ships standing by the lake: but the fishermen were gone out of them, and were washing their nets.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/luke/5#v3)</sup> And he entered into one of the ships, which was Simon’s, and prayed him that he would thrust out a little from the land. And he sat down, and taught the people out of the ship.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/luke/5#v4)</sup> Now when he had left speaking, he said unto Simon, Launch out into the deep, and let down your nets for a draught.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/luke/5#v5)</sup> And Simon answering said unto him, Master, we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing: nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/luke/5#v6)</sup> And when they had this done, they inclosed a great multitude of fishes: and their net brake.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/luke/5#v7)</sup> And they beckoned unto their partners, which were in the other ship, that they should come and help them. And they came, and filled both the ships, so that they began to sink.

- He teaches the people out of Simon's ship, then tells him to launch out into the deep and let down the nets for a draught.
- Master, we have toiled all the night, and have taken nothing: nevertheless at thy word I will let down the net. The nets brake and both ships began to sink.

**[Luke 5:8-11](/bible/luke/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/luke/5#v8)</sup> When Simon Peter saw it, he fell down at Jesus’ knees, saying, Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/luke/5#v9)</sup> For he was astonished, and all that were with him, at the draught of the fishes which they had taken:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/luke/5#v10)</sup> And so was also James, and John, the sons of Zebedee, which were partners with Simon. And Jesus said unto Simon, Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/luke/5#v11)</sup> And when they had brought their ships to land, they forsook all, and followed him.

- Peter falls down at his knees saying, Depart from me; for I am a sinful man, O Lord.
- Confessing sin is not the end of it. What changes a man is bringing forth fruits of repentance and not going back to the thing.
- Fear not; from henceforth thou shalt catch men. And they forsook all, and followed him.

> <sup>[12](/bible/luke/5#v12)</sup> And it came to pass, when he was in a certain city, behold a man full of leprosy: who seeing Jesus fell on his face, and besought him, saying, Lord, if thou wilt, thou canst make me clean.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/5#v13)</sup> And he put forth his hand, and touched him, saying, I will: be thou clean. And immediately the leprosy departed from him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/5#v14)</sup> And he charged him to tell no man: but go, and shew thyself to the priest, and offer for thy cleansing, according as Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them.

>
> <sup>[16](/bible/luke/5#v16)</sup> And he withdrew himself into the wilderness, and prayed.

- The man full of leprosy is made clean, and is charged to go and shew himself to the priest and offer for his cleansing according as Moses commanded, for a testimony unto them.

**[Luke 5:17-26](/bible/luke/5#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/luke/5#v17)</sup> And it came to pass on a certain day, as he was teaching, that there were Pharisees and doctors of the law sitting by, which were come out of every town of Galilee, and Judea, and Jerusalem: and the power of the Lord was present to heal them.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/5#v18)</sup> And, behold, men brought in a bed a man which was taken with a palsy: and they sought means to bring him in, and to lay him before him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/luke/5#v19)</sup> And when they could not find by what way they might bring him in because of the multitude, they went upon the housetop, and let him down through the tiling with his couch into the midst before Jesus.

> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/5#v20)</sup> And when he saw their faith, he said unto him, Man, thy sins are forgiven thee.

- When he saw their faith he said, Man, thy sins are forgiven thee, and the scribes and Pharisees began to reason that this was blasphemy.
