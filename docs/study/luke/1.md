**[Luke 1:13](/bible/luke/1#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/1#v13)</sup> But the angel said unto him, Fear not, Zacharias: for thy prayer is heard; and thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.

- “Thy wife Elisabeth shall bear thee a son, and thou shalt call his name John.” Future tense. The angel named John before he was conceived.

**[Luke 1:18-20](/bible/luke/1#v18)**

**[Luke 1:78](/bible/luke/1#v78)**

> <sup>[78](/bible/luke/1#v78)</sup> Through the tender mercy of our God; whereby the dayspring from on high hath visited us,

- “Through the tender mercy of our God”: remember earlier, Baruch 5:9 said he will lead his people Israel with mercy and righteousness. “Whereby the dayspring from on high hath visited us”. The dayspring is Christ: he is the dayspring from on high, the light from on high, that visited us.

**[Luke 1:79](/bible/luke/1#v79)**

> <sup>[79](/bible/luke/1#v79)</sup> To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, to guide our feet into the way of peace.

- “To give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death”: Christ came to give light to them that sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, “to guide our feet into the way of peace.” The way of peace is out of the shadow of death, out of darkness. That’s Israel he’s referring to.
