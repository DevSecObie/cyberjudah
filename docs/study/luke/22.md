## Introduction

This session covers Luke 22 through 24 and John 1. These notes cover the three chapters in Luke: the last passover, the betrayal in the garden, the trial and the crucifixion, and the resurrection with the walk to Emmaus. The reading keeps returning to two things, who the covenant belongs to and what the prophets had already written concerning him.

**[Luke 22:1-2](/bible/luke/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/luke/22#v1)</sup> Now the feast of unleavened bread drew nigh, which is called the Passover.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/luke/22#v2)</sup> And the chief priests and scribes sought how they might kill him; for they feared the people.

- The feast of unleavened bread drew nigh, which is called the Passover. The feast of unleavened bread and the passover are spoken of here as one and the same season.

**[Luke 22:3-6](/bible/luke/22#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/luke/22#v3)</sup> Then entered Satan into Judas surnamed Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/luke/22#v4)</sup> And he went his way, and communed with the chief priests and captains, how he might betray him unto them.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/luke/22#v5)</sup> And they were glad, and covenanted to give him money.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/luke/22#v6)</sup> And he promised, and sought opportunity to betray him unto them in the absence of the multitude.

- Then entered Satan into Judas surnamed Iscariot, being of the number of the twelve.
- The chief priests and captains were glad, and covenanted to give him money. Men are not in their right mind when they can be glad over a thing like that.

> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/22#v20)</sup> Likewise also the cup after supper, saying, This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you.

- With desire I have desired to eat this passover with you before I suffer.
- This cup is the new testament in my blood, which is shed for you. Testament and covenant are the same word, and the scripture names who that covenant was made with.

>
> <sup>[23](/bible/luke/22#v23)</sup> And they began to enquire among themselves, which of them it was that should do this thing.

- Behold, the hand of him that betrayeth me is with me on the table. He told the rest, while sitting with them, that one of them would do it.

**[Luke 22:24-27](/bible/luke/22#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/luke/22#v24)</sup> And there was also a strife among them, which of them should be accounted the greatest.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/luke/22#v25)</sup> And he said unto them, The kings of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them; and they that exercise authority upon them are called benefactors.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/luke/22#v26)</sup> But ye shall not be so: but he that is greatest among you, let him be as the younger; and he that is chief, as he that doth serve.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/luke/22#v27)</sup> For whether is greater, he that sitteth at meat, or he that serveth? is not he that sitteth at meat? but I am among you as he that serveth.

- The kings of the Gentiles exercise lordship over them, and they that exercise authority upon them are called benefactors: men who take a benefit for ruling over others.
- But ye shall not be so. He that is greatest among you, let him be as the younger; and he that is chief, as he that doth serve.
- As a man is raised in knowledge and understanding he is made more of a servant, not less. I am among you as he that serveth, and he was the greatest of them.

**[Luke 22:28-30](/bible/luke/22#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/luke/22#v28)</sup> Ye are they which have continued with me in my temptations.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/luke/22#v29)</sup> And I appoint unto you a kingdom, as my Father hath appointed unto me;
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/22#v30)</sup> That ye may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel.

- Ye may eat and drink at my table in my kingdom, and sit on thrones judging the twelve tribes of Israel. The apostles are set to judge the twelve tribes, which is who this word is for.

**[Luke 22:31-32](/bible/luke/22#v31)**

>

- Satan hath desired to have you, that he may sift you as wheat; but I have prayed for thee, that thy faith fail not; and when thou art converted, strengthen thy brethren.
- Peter walked with him and was not yet converted. What converts a man is named in the psalm, and what he strengthens his brethren with is the same thing.

- Being in an agony he prayed more earnestly, and his sweat was as it were great drops of blood falling down to the ground.
- He found them sleeping for sorrow. Grief drains a man and puts him to sleep, and that is what was on them knowing what was coming.
