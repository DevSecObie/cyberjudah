## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Tobit 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-2, 4-13, 16-22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Tobit 1:1-2](/bible/tobit/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/tobit/1#v1)</sup> The book of the words of Tobit, son of Tobiel, the son of Ananiel, the son of Aduel, the son of Gabael, of the seed of Asael, of the tribe of Nephthali;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/tobit/1#v2)</sup> Who in the time of Enemessar king of the Assyrians was led captive out of Thisbe, which is at the right hand of that city, which is called properly Nephthali in Galilee above Aser.

- Tobit, of the tribe of Nephthali, was led captive by the king of Assyria out of Thisbe in Galilee.

**[Tobit 1:4-8](/bible/tobit/1#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/tobit/1#v4)</sup> And when I was in mine own country, in the land of Israel, being but young, all the tribe of Nephthali my father fell from the house of Jerusalem, which was chosen out of all the tribes of Israel, that all the tribes should sacrifice there, where the temple of the habitation of the most High was consecrated and built for all ages.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/tobit/1#v5)</sup> Now all the tribes which together revolted, and the house of my father Nephthali, sacrificed unto the heifer Baal.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/tobit/1#v6)</sup> But I alone went often to Jerusalem at the feasts, as it was ordained unto all the people of Israel by an everlasting decree, having the firstfruits and tenths of increase, with that which was first shorn; and them gave I at the altar to the priests the children of Aaron.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/tobit/1#v7)</sup> The first tenth part of all increase I gave to the sons of Aaron, who ministered at Jerusalem: another tenth part I sold away, and went, and spent it every year at Jerusalem:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/tobit/1#v8)</sup> And the third I gave unto them to whom it was meet, as Debora my father’s mother had commanded me, because I was left an orphan by my father.

- When he was young, all the tribe of Nephthali and his father's house fell into idolatry and “sacrificed unto the heifer Baal.” “But I alone went often to Jerusalem at the feasts”, giving his tenths to the sons of Aaron. He was left an orphan by his father, who went into idolatry and abandoned him, and was raised by his grandmother. Some of the men became wicked and abandoned their children.
- “as Debora my father’s mother had commanded me, because I was left an orphan by my father.” Tobit was an orphan. His grandmother told him how to give alms and take care of his business.

**[Tobit 1:9](/bible/tobit/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/tobit/1#v9)</sup> Furthermore, when I was come to the age of a man, I married Anna of mine own kindred, and of her I begat Tobias.

- “I married Anna of mine own kindred, and of her I begat Tobias.”

**[Tobit 1:10-13](/bible/tobit/1#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/tobit/1#v10)</sup> And when we were carried away captives to Nineve, all my brethren and those that were of my kindred did eat of the bread of the Gentiles.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/tobit/1#v11)</sup> But I kept myself from eating;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/1#v12)</sup> Because I remembered God with all my heart.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/tobit/1#v13)</sup> And the most High gave me grace and favour before Enemessar, so that I was his purveyor.

- In captivity in Nineve his brethren ate “of the bread of the Gentiles”, things offered to idols. “But I kept myself from eating; Because I remembered God with all my heart.” And the most High gave him favour, so that he became the king's purveyor, over the king's accounts.

**[Tobit 1:16-18](/bible/tobit/1#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/tobit/1#v16)</sup> And in the time of Enemessar I gave many alms to my brethren, and gave my bread to the hungry,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/tobit/1#v17)</sup> And my clothes to the naked: and if I saw any of my nation dead, or cast about the walls of Nineve, I buried him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/tobit/1#v18)</sup> And if the king Sennacherib had slain any, when he was come, and fled from Judea, I buried them privily; (for in his wrath he killed many;) but the bodies were not found, when they were sought for of the king.

- While he was well off he looked out for his brethren, fed the hungry, clothed the naked, and buried any of his nation he found cast about the walls of Nineve. When Sennacherib came back from Judea, where an angel had killed his men, he took it out on Israel and killed many in his wrath, and Tobit buried them “privily”, secretly, though he worked for that king.
- “if I saw any of my nation dead, or cast about the walls of Nineve, I buried him.” He walked around burying the dead, looking out for his people. “And if the king Sennacherib had slain any, when he was come, and fled from Judea, I buried them privily”. He saw the bodies, picked them up, and made sure he put them in the ground.

**[Tobit 1:19-20](/bible/tobit/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/tobit/1#v19)</sup> And when one of the Ninevites went and complained of me to the king, that I buried them, and hid myself; understanding that I was sought for to be put to death, I withdrew myself for fear.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/tobit/1#v20)</sup> Then all my goods were forcibly taken away, neither was there any thing left me, beside my wife Anna and my son Tobias.

- He was ratted out, became a fugitive, and “all my goods were forcibly taken away”, even his wife and son. That is an example of what is coming: when you go against this system and teach against it and refuse to support the beast, everything you have will be taken from you.
- “Then all my goods were forcibly taken away”. All his goods were taken for doing right, for not thinking about himself. “neither was there any thing left me, beside my wife Anna and my son Tobias.” All he had left was his wife and son, for running around doing great works, burying Israel instead of letting their bodies lie in the streets.

**[Tobit 1:21-22](/bible/tobit/1#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/tobit/1#v21)</sup> And there passed not five and fifty days, before two of his sons killed him, and they fled into the mountains of Ararath; and Sarchedonus his son reigned in his stead; who appointed over his father’s accounts, and over all his affairs, Achiacharus my brother Anael’s son.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/tobit/1#v22)</sup> And Achiacharus intreating for me, I returned to Nineve. Now Achiacharus was cupbearer, and keeper of the signet, and steward, and overseer of the accounts: and Sarchedonus appointed him next unto him: and he was my brother’s son.

- Within fifty-five days the king's two sons killed him, and Sarchedonus reigned, and set over all his accounts “Achiacharus my brother Anael's son.” His nephew had status next unto the king, and he interceded for Tobit, and Tobit returned to Nineve. Tobit was not alone in being righteous in captivity: his nephew looked out for him the way he had looked out for his own people, and the Lord looked out for him.
