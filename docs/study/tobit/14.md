## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Tobit 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 4, 12-13, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Tobit 14:4](/bible/tobit/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/tobit/14#v4)</sup> Go into Media, my son, for I surely believe those things which Jonas the prophet spake of Nineve, that it shall be overthrown; and that for a time peace shall rather be in Media; and that our brethren shall lie scattered in the earth from that good land: and Jerusalem shall be desolate, and the house of God in it shall be burned, and shall be desolate for a time;

- Tobit foretold that Nineve would be overthrown, “and Jerusalem shall be desolate, and the house of God in it shall be burned”: by Babylon.

**[Tobit 14:12-13](/bible/tobit/14#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/14#v12)</sup> And when Anna his mother was dead, he buried her with his father. But Tobias departed with his wife and children to Ecbatane to Raguel his father in law,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/tobit/14#v13)</sup> Where he became old with honour, and he buried his father and mother in law honourably, and he inherited their substance, and his father Tobit’s.

- When Tobit and Anna died, Tobias went to Ecbatane to his father-in-law, buried him and his wife, “and he inherited their substance, and his father Tobit's.” He received half when he left, and the rest when they died. He inherited both lands, his own father's and his parents-in-law's, because they were of the same tribe and Raguel had no son. Keep that in mind.
