## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Tobit 6 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 10-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Tobit 6:10-11](/bible/tobit/6#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/tobit/6#v10)</sup> The angel said to the young man, Brother, to day we shall lodge with Raguel, who is thy cousin; he also hath one only daughter, named Sara; I will speak for her, that she may be given thee for a wife.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/tobit/6#v11)</sup> For to thee doth the right of her appertain, seeing thou only art of her kindred.

- Raguel is Tobias' cousin and “hath one only daughter, named Sara.” “For to thee doth the right of her appertain, seeing thou only art of her kindred.” She is the only heir left of Raguel.

**[Tobit 6:12](/bible/tobit/6#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/6#v12)</sup> And the maid is fair and wise: now therefore hear me, and I will speak to her father; and when we return from Rages we will celebrate the marriage: for I know that Raguel cannot marry her to another according to the law of Moses, but he shall be guilty of death, because the right of inheritance doth rather appertain to thee than to any other.

- “Raguel cannot marry her to another according to the law of Moses, but he shall be guilty of death, because the right of inheritance doth rather appertain to thee than to any other.” Giving your land to someone it does not belong to is worthy of death. Land is a heavy thing; you cannot give it away to anybody.

**[Tobit 6:13-14](/bible/tobit/6#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/tobit/6#v13)</sup> Then the young man answered the angel, I have heard, brother Azarias, that this maid hath been given to seven men, who all died in the marriage chamber.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/tobit/6#v14)</sup> And now I am the only son of my father, and I am afraid, lest, if I go in unto her, I die, as the other before: for a wicked spirit loveth her, which hurteth no body, but those which come unto her: wherefore I also fear lest I die, and bring my father’s and my mother’s life, because of me, to the grave with sorrow: for they have no other son to bury them.

- The seven men “all died in the marriage chamber”, when they came together, before they could consummate. Tobias was afraid: “I am the only son of my father.” He is the only son of his father and she is the only daughter of hers.

**[Tobit 6:15-17](/bible/tobit/6#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/tobit/6#v15)</sup> Then the angel said unto him, Dost thou not remember the precepts which thy father gave thee, that thou shouldest marry a wife of thine own kindred? wherefore hear me, O my brother; for she shall be given thee to wife; and make thou no reckoning of the evil spirit; for this same night shall she be given thee in marriage.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/tobit/6#v16)</sup> And when thou shalt come into the marriage chamber, thou shalt take the ashes of perfume, and shalt lay upon them some of the heart and liver of the fish, and shalt make a smoke with it:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/tobit/6#v17)</sup> And the devil shall smell it, and flee away, and never come again any more: but when thou shalt come to her, rise up both of you, and pray to God which is merciful, who will have pity on you, and save you: fear not, for she is appointed unto thee from the beginning; and thou shalt preserve her, and she shall go with thee. Moreover I suppose that she shall bear thee children. Now when Tobias had heard these things, he loved her, and his heart was effectually joined to her.

- The angel reminded him of “the precepts which thy father gave thee, that thou shouldest marry a wife of thine own kindred,” and called him “O my brother.” “She is appointed unto thee from the beginning.” She was meant to be his from the beginning.
- Some sisters pull this verse and ask a question about it. “And the devil shall smell it, and flee away, and never come again any more.” This is talking about Tobias, who was going to marry this sister. She had a devil around her, and the Lord showed Raphael the angel what to do to get the devil out.
- “But when thou shalt come to her, rise up both of you, and pray to God which is merciful, who will have pity on you, and save you: fear not, for she is appointed unto thee from the beginning.” See that part right there: she is appointed unto thee from the beginning. “And thou shalt preserve her, and she shall go with thee. Moreover I suppose that she shall bear thee children. Now when Tobias had heard these things, he loved her, and his heart was effectually joined to her.”
- Sisters use this verse and Genesis 24 to ask, was I appointed for this man? Here is the answer: both men were virgins and both women were virgins.

The servant stood by the well: “Behold, I stand here by the well of water; and the daughters of the men of the city come out to draw water.” He asked for a sign: “let it come to pass, that the damsel to whom I shall say, Let down thy pitcher, I pray thee, that I may drink; and she shall say, Drink, and I will give thy camels drink also: let the same be she that thou hast appointed for thy servant Isaac.”
