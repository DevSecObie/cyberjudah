## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Tobit 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 6-10, 15-17, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Tobit 3:6](/bible/tobit/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/tobit/3#v6)</sup> Now therefore deal with me as seemeth best unto thee, and command my spirit to be taken from me, that I may be dissolved, and become earth: for it is profitable for me to die rather than to live, because I have heard false reproaches, and have much sorrow: command therefore that I may now be delivered out of this distress, and go into the everlasting place: turn not thy face away from me.

- Tobit asked God to take his life: “it is profitable for me to die rather than to live.”

**[Tobit 3:7-10](/bible/tobit/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/tobit/3#v7)</sup> It came to pass the same day, that in Ecbatane a city of Media Sara the daughter of Raguel was also reproached by her father’s maids;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/tobit/3#v8)</sup> Because that she had been married to seven husbands, whom Asmodeus the evil spirit had killed, before they had lain with her. Dost thou not know, said they, that thou hast strangled thine husbands? thou hast had already seven husbands, neither wast thou named after any of them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/tobit/3#v9)</sup> Wherefore dost thou beat us for them? if they be dead, go thy ways after them, let us never see of thee either son or daughter.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/tobit/3#v10)</sup> When she heard these things, she was very sorrowful, so that she thought to have strangled herself; and she said, I am the only daughter of my father, and if I do this, it shall be a reproach unto him, and I shall bring his old age with sorrow unto the grave.

- The same day in Ecbatane, Sara the daughter of Raguel had “been married to seven husbands, whom Asmodeus the evil spirit had killed, before they had lain with her.” They were her husbands before they lay with her, because she was promised to them: the espousal is the marriage. Her father's maids mocked her and she grew so sorrowful she thought to strangle herself. “I am the only daughter of my father”: Raguel had no son, only one daughter.

**[Tobit 3:15](/bible/tobit/3#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/tobit/3#v15)</sup> And that I never polluted my name, nor the name of my father, in the land of my captivity: I am the only daughter of my father, neither hath he any child to be his heir, neither any near kinsman, nor any son of his alive, to whom I may keep myself for a wife: my seven husbands are already dead; and why should I live? but if it please not thee that I should die, command some regard to be had of me, and pity taken of me, that I hear no more reproach.

- “Neither hath he any child to be his heir, neither any near kinsman, nor any son of his alive, to whom I may keep myself for a wife.” Remember the importance of inheritance: she has nobody to marry who can keep her father's land.

**[Tobit 3:16-17](/bible/tobit/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/tobit/3#v16)</sup> So the prayers of them both were heard before the majesty of the great God.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/tobit/3#v17)</sup> And Raphael was sent to heal them both, that is, to scale away the whiteness of Tobit’s eyes, and to give Sara the daughter of Raguel for a wife to Tobias the son of Tobit; and to bind Asmodeus the evil spirit; because she belonged to Tobias by right of inheritance. The selfsame time came Tobit home, and entered into his house, and Sara the daughter of Raguel came down from her upper chamber.

- The prayers of both were heard, and Raphael was sent to heal Tobit's eyes and “to give Sara the daughter of Raguel for a wife to Tobias”, “because she belonged to Tobias by right of inheritance.”
