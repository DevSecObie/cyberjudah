**[Deuteronomy 12:1](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v1)</sup> These are the statutes and judgments, which ye shall observe to do in the land, which the Lord God of thy fathers giveth thee to possess it, all the days that ye live upon the earth.

- These are the statutes and judgments which ye shall observe to do in the land: the Lord gave the fathers a land other nations were living in, and Israel went to war with those nations to take it.
- The whole book of Deuteronomy is the Lord speaking unto his people Israel; he showed his statutes and his judgments to no other nation.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 147:19-20](/bible/psalms/147#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/147#v19)</sup> He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/147#v20)</sup> He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the Lord.

    He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel; he hath not dealt so with any nation. Keep this precept under your belt, it is brought out almost every camp.

**[Deuteronomy 12:2](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v2)</sup> Ye shall utterly destroy all the places, wherein the nations which ye shall possess served their gods, upon the high mountains, and upon the hills, and under every green tree:

- Utterly destroy all the places wherein the nations served their gods, upon the high mountains, upon the hills, and under every green tree.
- Even after being warned, Israel was still idolatrous: the children of Israel secretly built high places in all their cities, set up images and groves in every high hill and under every green tree, and burnt incense as did the heathen, the very thing the Lord said ye shall not do.
- Because Israel served the gods of the nations, the Most High gave those nations the rule over Israel; that is how they became the oppressor, and the word says envy not the oppressor and choose none of his ways. Unlearned and lost brothers and sisters today are still serving other gods, whatever the religion calls itself.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Kings 17:9-12](/bible/2-kings/17#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/17#v9)</sup> And the children of Israel did secretly those things that were not right against the Lord their God, and they built them high places in all their cities, from the tower of the watchmen to the fenced city.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/17#v10)</sup> And they set them up images and groves in every high hill, and under every green tree:
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/17#v11)</sup> And there they burnt incense in all the high places, as did the heathen whom the Lord carried away before them; and wrought wicked things to provoke the Lord to anger:
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/17#v12)</sup> For they served idols, whereof the Lord had said unto them, Ye shall not do this thing.

    Israel secretly built high places, set up images and groves under every green tree, and served idols, whereof the Lord had said, ye shall not do this thing.
  - **[Proverbs 3:31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

    Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

**[Deuteronomy 12:5](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v5)</sup> But unto the place which the Lord your God shall choose out of all your tribes to put his name there, even unto his habitation shall ye seek, and thither thou shalt come:

- Unto the place which the Lord your God shall choose out of all your tribes to put his name there shall ye seek: out of all the tribes he chose Jerusalem as the place for his name and the temple.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Chronicles 6:6](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-chronicles/6#v6)</sup> But I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there; and have chosen David to be over my people Israel.

    I have chosen Jerusalem, that my name might be there, and have chosen David to be over my people.

**[Deuteronomy 12:9-10](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v9)</sup> For ye are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance, which the Lord your God giveth you.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v10)</sup> But when ye go over Jordan, and dwell in the land which the Lord your God giveth you to inherit, and when he giveth you rest from all your enemies round about, so that ye dwell in safety;

- Ye are not as yet come to the rest and to the inheritance: the rest is when the Lord gives rest from all your enemies round about, so that ye dwell in safety.
- This is why the scripture is pulled out on the street, this is not our rest: Israel today dwells among its enemies and serves them for food, shelter, and clothing, as [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) declares.
- Arise ye and depart does not mean to leave physically; it means come up out of the ways of this place, because it is polluted. Israel waits for the time when the third part has repented, the hundred forty and four thousand are sealed, and Christ returns to take his people out of this place and set them back in their own land.

  Precepts:
  - **[Micah 2:10](/bible/micah/2#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/micah/2#v10)</sup> Arise ye, and depart; for this is not your rest: because it is polluted, it shall destroy you, even with a sore destruction.

    Arise ye, and depart, for this is not your rest; it is polluted, it shall destroy you with a sore destruction.

**[Deuteronomy 12:15](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v15)</sup> Notwithstanding thou mayest kill and eat flesh in all thy gates, whatsoever thy soul lusteth after, according to the blessing of the Lord thy God which he hath given thee: the unclean and the clean may eat thereof, as of the roebuck, and as of the hart.

- Whatsoever thy soul lusteth after is not license to eat anything: it stays within the guidelines of the clean foods, as of the roebuck and as of the hart, the beasts listed in chapter 14, for the law says thou shalt not eat any abominable thing.
- The unclean and the clean that may eat thereof are unclean and clean persons, not unclean meats; Israel had servants then and will have servants again.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 15:22](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v22)</sup> Thou shalt eat it within thy gates: the unclean and the clean person shall eat it alike, as the roebuck, and as the hart.

    The unclean and the clean person shall eat it alike, as the roebuck and as the hart; the unclean and clean here are persons.
  - **[Deuteronomy 14:3](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v3)</sup> Thou shalt not eat any abominable thing.

    Thou shalt not eat any abominable thing; the lusting of the soul sits inside the dietary law.

**[Deuteronomy 12:30](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v30)</sup> Take heed to thyself that thou be not snared by following them, after that they be destroyed from before thee; and that thou enquire not after their gods, saying, How did these nations serve their gods? even so will I do likewise.

- Take heed that thou be not snared by inquiring after the gods of the nations after they are destroyed, saying, how did these nations serve their gods, even so will I do likewise; Israel has a history of following what the other nations do.
- The snare fulfilled is written in the psalm: Israel did not destroy the nations, but was mingled among the heathen, learned their works, served their idols, and sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto devils, the very abomination the Lord warned of here.
- The wrath of the Lord was kindled insomuch that he abhorred his own inheritance; that snare is why Israel is where it is now, scattered among the heathen and serving them.
