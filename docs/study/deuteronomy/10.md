**[Deuteronomy 10:1-5](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v1)</sup> At that time the Lord said unto me, Hew thee two tables of stone like unto the first, and come up unto me into the mount, and make thee an ark of wood.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v2)</sup> And I will write on the tables the words that were in the first tables which thou brakest, and thou shalt put them in the ark.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v3)</sup> And I made an ark of shittim wood, and hewed two tables of stone like unto the first, and went up into the mount, having the two tables in mine hand.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v4)</sup> And he wrote on the tables, according to the first writing, the ten commandments, which the Lord spake unto you in the mount out of the midst of the fire in the day of the assembly: and the Lord gave them unto me.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v5)</sup> And I turned myself and came down from the mount, and put the tables in the ark which I had made; and there they be, as the Lord commanded me.

- Hew thee two tables of stone like unto the first, and make thee an ark of wood; Moses made the ark of shittim wood and went up with the two tables in his hand.
- He wrote on the tables, according to the first writing, the ten commandments: twice the record says God wrote them, in [Deuteronomy 9:10](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v10) written with the finger of God and here again. That is why the law is so important; these are not merely the commandments of Moses, they are the laws of God, and breaking them brings judgment if you do not repent.
- We have to repent, keep the commandments, and be found blameless before the Lord when Christ returns.
- Moses put the tables in the ark which he had made, and there they be, as the Lord commanded.

**[Deuteronomy 10:6-9](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v6)</sup> And the children of Israel took their journey from Beeroth of the children of Jaakan to Mosera: there Aaron died, and there he was buried; and Eleazar his son ministered in the priest’s office in his stead.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v7)</sup> From thence they journeyed unto Gudgodah; and from Gudgodah to Jotbath, a land of rivers of waters.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v8)</sup> At that time the Lord separated the tribe of Levi, to bear the ark of the covenant of the Lord, to stand before the Lord to minister unto him, and to bless in his name, unto this day.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v9)</sup> Wherefore Levi hath no part nor inheritance with his brethren; the Lord is his inheritance, according as the Lord thy God promised him.

- Aaron died and was buried, and Eleazar his son ministered in the priest's office in his stead.
- At that time the Lord separated the tribe of Levi, to bear the ark of the covenant, to stand before the Lord to minister unto him, and to bless in his name; wherefore Levi hath no part nor inheritance with his brethren, the Lord is his inheritance.
- Levi ate well through the sacrifices the Lord set up: the Levites dwelt in the best areas, the suburbs, and lived in castles, because the Lord was their provider. All they had to do was teach Israel the commandments of God.
- But they corrupted the covenant of Levi, left off teaching the law, and were partial in it, so the Lord gave them a swift and mighty judgment. Now you understand why Levi, identified in the land of Haiti, has been the poorest in the western hemisphere and catches hell to this day: their provider was the Lord, and they departed out of the way.

    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/malachi/2#v7)</sup> For the priest’s lips should keep knowledge, and they should seek the law at his mouth: for he is the messenger of the Lord of hosts.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/malachi/2#v8)</sup> But ye are departed out of the way; ye have caused many to stumble at the law; ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi, saith the Lord of hosts.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/malachi/2#v9)</sup> Therefore have I also made you contemptible and base before all the people, according as ye have not kept my ways, but have been partial in the law.

    The law of truth was in his mouth, and the priest's lips should keep knowledge, but ye are departed out of the way, ye have caused many to stumble at the law, ye have corrupted the covenant of Levi; therefore have I made you contemptible and base before all the people.

**[Deuteronomy 10:12-13](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v12)</sup> And now, Israel, what doth the Lord thy God require of thee, but to fear the Lord thy God, to walk in all his ways, and to love him, and to serve the Lord thy God with all thy heart and with all thy soul,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v13)</sup> To keep the commandments of the Lord, and his statutes, which I command thee this day for thy good?

- And now, Israel, what doth the Lord thy God require of thee: here is your purpose in life, the stipulations for the body the Most High gave you, and what you must do to please him as the children of Israel.
- Fear the Lord thy God, walk in all his ways, love him, and serve him with all thy heart and with all thy soul: it all boils down to keeping the commandments of the Lord and his statutes, which he commands thee for thy good. That is how you love and serve God until the day we return to the dust or he sends his son to redeem us from the land of our captivity.
- Do not just say you love God, put action behind it. Sisters, come out of the pants and mini skirts and put on a modest dress, and be in subjection to your own husbands; brothers, put on your fringes, stop dyeing your hair, and marry the mother of your children instead of leaving her a baby mama. When you will not do what he told you to do, no words out of your mouth can make you love him; your actions show you actually hate him.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

    Talk no more so exceeding proudly, let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed; saying God knows my heart while breaking his laws is speaking proudly.
  - **[2 John 1:6](/bible/2-john/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-john/1#v6)</sup> And this is love, that we walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.

    And this is love, that we walk after his commandments; it never changed from old covenant to new.

**[Deuteronomy 10:14-15](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v14)</sup> Behold, the heaven and the heaven of heavens is the Lord’s thy God, the earth also, with all that therein is.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v15)</sup> Only the Lord had a delight in thy fathers to love them, and he chose their seed after them, even you above all people, as it is this day.

- Behold, the heaven and the heaven of heavens is the Lord's thy God, the earth also; yet only the Lord had a delight in thy fathers to love them, and he chose their seed after them, even you above all people, as it is this day.
- No other nation can lay claim to this: our forefather Abraham was the friend of God, and the Most High took Israel from the chief men of the earth, for Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob were the best men walking the face of the earth when he made us a nation. Heavy scriptures right there.

    But thou, Israel, art my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend; I have taken thee from the ends of the earth, and called thee from the chief men thereof, and said, Thou art my servant; I have chosen thee, and not cast thee away.

**[Deuteronomy 10:16-17](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v16)</sup> Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be no more stiffnecked.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v17)</sup> For the Lord your God is God of gods, and Lord of lords, a great God, a mighty, and a terrible, which regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward:

- Circumcise therefore the foreskin of your heart, and be no more stiffnecked.
- For the Lord your God is God of gods, and Lord of lords, a great God, a mighty, and a terrible, which regardeth not persons, nor taketh reward: that is what needs to be taught in the church, who we are really dealing with.
- Christ described his father the same way and forewarned us plainly: the Most High can destroy both the flesh and the spirit, disintegrate the spirit with no second chance at life, so make sure you are walking correct and keeping these laws before you come before him.

  - **[Luke 12:4-5](/bible/luke/12#v4)**

    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/luke/12#v5)</sup> But I will forewarn you whom ye shall fear: Fear him, which after he hath killed hath power to cast into hell; yea, I say unto you, Fear him.

    Be not afraid of them that kill the body; but I will forewarn you whom ye shall fear: fear him, which after he hath killed hath power to cast into hell; yea, I say unto you, Fear him.

**[Deuteronomy 10:18-19](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v18)</sup> He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow, and loveth the stranger, in giving him food and raiment.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/10#v19)</sup> Love ye therefore the stranger: for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.

- He doth execute the judgment of the fatherless and widow, and loveth the stranger, in giving him food and raiment; love ye therefore the stranger, for ye were strangers in the land of Egypt.
- Which stranger is Moses telling Israel to love? He is prophesying of the day Israel's own brother would become a stranger: when the northern kingdom was cut off, the brother became the stranger who would come back. Love them, because they are coming back into the covenant.
- The stranger of this commandment is thy brother fallen into decay, whom thou shalt relieve, not the nations Israel was commanded to drive out.

  Precepts:
  - **[Obadiah 1:12](/bible/obadiah/1#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/obadiah/1#v12)</sup> But thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; neither shouldest thou have rejoiced over the children of Judah in the day of their destruction; neither shouldest thou have spoken proudly in the day of distress.

    Thou shouldest not have looked on the day of thy brother in the day that he became a stranger; the brother becoming a stranger is the key to who Moses means.
  - **[Leviticus 25:35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/25#v35)</sup> And if thy brother be waxen poor, and fallen in decay with thee; then thou shalt relieve him: yea, though he be a stranger, or a sojourner; that he may live with thee.

    If thy brother be waxen poor, then thou shalt relieve him, yea, though he be a stranger or a sojourner, that he may live with thee.
