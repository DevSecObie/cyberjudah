**[Deuteronomy 17:1](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v1)</sup> Thou shalt not sacrifice unto the Lord thy God any bullock, or sheep, wherein is blemish, or any evilfavouredness: for that is an abomination unto the Lord thy God.

- Thou shalt not sacrifice unto the Most High any bullock or sheep wherein is blemish, or any evilfavouredness, for that is an abomination unto the Lord thy God.
- Back then you could be put to death for sacrificing a bullock or sheep with a blemish, because doing an abominable thing carried death.

**[Deuteronomy 17:2-5](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v2)</sup> If there be found among you, within any of thy gates which the Lord thy God giveth thee, man or woman, that hath wrought wickedness in the sight of the Lord thy God, in transgressing his covenant,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v3)</sup> And hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the sun, or moon, or any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v4)</sup> And it be told thee, and thou hast heard of it, and enquired diligently, and, behold, it be true, and the thing certain, that such abomination is wrought in Israel:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v5)</sup> Then shalt thou bring forth that man or that woman, which have committed that wicked thing, unto thy gates, even that man or that woman, and shalt stone them with stones, till they die.

- If there be found among you man or woman that hath wrought wickedness, transgressed the covenant, and gone and served other gods, the sun or moon or any of the host of heaven, which the Most High commanded not, the matter had to be established first.
- A lot of people do not know how to apply [Leviticus 5:1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1); the precept for [Leviticus 5:1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1) is [Deuteronomy 17:4](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v4). Whether it be told thee or thou hast heard of it, thou shalt enquire diligently, and behold, if it be true and the thing certain that such abomination is wrought in Israel, then judgment proceeds.
- Make sure you are certain of a thing before you bring somebody to judgment; only after diligent enquiry was the man or woman brought to the gates and stoned with stones till they die.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 5:1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1)</sup> And if a soul sin, and hear the voice of swearing, and is a witness, whether he hath seen or known of it; if he do not utter it, then he shall bear his iniquity.

    If a soul hear the voice of swearing, and is a witness, and do not utter it, he shall bear his iniquity; the witness law is applied through the diligent enquiry of Deuteronomy 17:4.

**[Deuteronomy 17:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v6)</sup> At the mouth of two witnesses, or three witnesses, shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; but at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v7)</sup> The hands of the witnesses shall be first upon him to put him to death, and afterward the hands of all the people. So thou shalt put the evil away from among you.

- At the mouth of two witnesses or three witnesses shall he that is worthy of death be put to death; at the mouth of one witness he shall not be put to death.
- The hands of the witnesses shall be first upon him to put him to death, and afterward the hands of all the people. Wait till we go back to this: the ones who witnessed against you were the first to lay hands on you, so a false witness could not hide behind the crowd.
- So thou shalt put the evil away from among you.

**[Deuteronomy 17:8-13](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v8)</sup> If there arise a matter too hard for thee in judgment, between blood and blood, between plea and plea, and between stroke and stroke, being matters of controversy within thy gates: then shalt thou arise, and get thee up into the place which the Lord thy God shall choose;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v9)</sup> And thou shalt come unto the priests the Levites, and unto the judge that shall be in those days, and enquire; and they shall shew thee the sentence of judgment:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v10)</sup> And thou shalt do according to the sentence, which they of that place which the Lord shall choose shall shew thee; and thou shalt observe to do according to all that they inform thee:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v11)</sup> According to the sentence of the law which they shall teach thee, and according to the judgment which they shall tell thee, thou shalt do: thou shalt not decline from the sentence which they shall shew thee, to the right hand, nor to the left.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v12)</sup> And the man that will do presumptuously, and will not hearken unto the priest that standeth to minister there before the Lord thy God, or unto the judge, even that man shall die: and thou shalt put away the evil from Israel.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v13)</sup> And all the people shall hear, and fear, and do no more presumptuously.

- If there arise a matter too hard for thee in judgment, between blood and blood, between plea and plea, and between stroke and stroke, matters of controversy within thy gates, then thou shalt get thee up into the place which the Lord shall choose.
- Come unto the priests the Levites and unto the judge that shall be in those days, and they shall shew thee the sentence of judgment; thou shalt do according to all that they inform thee, according to the sentence of the law which they shall teach thee, declining neither to the right hand nor to the left.
- The man that will do presumptuously and will not hearken unto the priest that standeth to minister before the Most High, or unto the judge, even that man shall die; and all the people shall hear, and fear, and do no more presumptuously.

**[Deuteronomy 17:14-17](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v14)</sup> When thou art come unto the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, and shalt possess it, and shalt dwell therein, and shalt say, I will set a king over me, like as all the nations that are about me;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v15)</sup> Thou shalt in any wise set him king over thee, whom the Lord thy God shall choose: one from among thy brethren shalt thou set king over thee: thou mayest not set a stranger over thee, which is not thy brother.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v16)</sup> But he shall not multiply horses to himself, nor cause the people to return to Egypt, to the end that he should multiply horses: forasmuch as the Lord hath said unto you, Ye shall henceforth return no more that way.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v17)</sup> Neither shall he multiply wives to himself, that his heart turn not away: neither shall he greatly multiply to himself silver and gold.

- When thou art come unto the land and shalt say, I will set a king over me like as all the nations that are about me, thou shalt in any wise set him king whom the Lord thy God shall choose: one from among thy brethren; thou mayest not set a stranger over thee, which is not thy brother.
- These are the laws for a king that is to rule over Israel. He shall not multiply horses to himself, nor cause the people to return to Egypt to multiply horses, for the Lord hath said, Ye shall henceforth return no more that way.
- Neither shall he multiply wives to himself, that his heart turn not away. You hear a lot of these dudes today saying king this and king that, and many of the same men pushing for multiple wives; the law of the king says do not multiply wives to thyself.
- Neither shall he greatly multiply to himself silver and gold.

**[Deuteronomy 17:18-20](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v18)</sup> And it shall be, when he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom, that he shall write him a copy of this law in a book out of that which is before the priests the Levites:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v19)</sup> And it shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life: that he may learn to fear the Lord his God, to keep all the words of this law and these statutes, to do them:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v20)</sup> That his heart be not lifted up above his brethren, and that he turn not aside from the commandment, to the right hand, or to the left: to the end that he may prolong his days in his kingdom, he, and his children, in the midst of Israel.

- When he sitteth upon the throne of his kingdom, he shall write him a copy of this law in a book out of that which is before the priests the Levites.
- It shall be with him, and he shall read therein all the days of his life, that he may learn to fear the Most High and keep all the words of this law and these statutes to do them.
- The purpose: that his heart be not lifted up above his brethren, that he turn not aside from the commandment to the right hand or to the left, and that he prolong his days in his kingdom, he and his children, in the midst of Israel.
