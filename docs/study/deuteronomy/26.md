**[Deuteronomy 26:5](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v5)</sup> And thou shalt speak and say before the Lord thy God, A Syrian ready to perish was my father, and he went down into Egypt, and sojourned there with a few, and became there a nation, great, mighty, and populous:

- The firstfruits confession spoken before the Lord begins: a Syrian ready to perish was my father, and he went down into Egypt and sojourned there with a few, and became there a nation, great, mighty, and populous.
- The father is Jacob: Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep, as we read in Genesis.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 12:12](/bible/hosea/12#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/hosea/12#v12)</sup> And Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.

    Jacob fled into the country of Syria, and Israel served for a wife, and for a wife he kept sheep.

**[Deuteronomy 26:6](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v6)</sup> And the Egyptians evil entreated us, and afflicted us, and laid upon us hard bondage:

- The Egyptians evil entreated us, and afflicted us, and laid upon us hard bondage.
- There arose up a new king over Egypt which knew not Joseph; fearing that the children of Israel were more and mightier than they, Egypt set over them taskmasters to afflict them with their burdens, and they built for Pharaoh the treasure cities, Pithom and Raamses.

  Precepts:

**[Deuteronomy 26:16-17](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v16)</sup> This day the Lord thy God hath commanded thee to do these statutes and judgments: thou shalt therefore keep and do them with all thine heart, and with all thy soul.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v17)</sup> Thou hast avouched the Lord this day to be thy God, and to walk in his ways, and to keep his statutes, and his commandments, and his judgments, and to hearken unto his voice:

- This day the Lord thy God hath commanded thee to do these statutes and judgments: thou shalt keep and do them with all thine heart and with all thy soul.
- Thou hast avouched the Lord this day to be thy God: avouch means affirm or confirm. We confirm, thou art our God and we are going to be thy people, walking in his ways and keeping his statutes, his commandments, and his judgments, and hearkening unto his voice.

**[Deuteronomy 26:18](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v18)</sup> And the Lord hath avouched thee this day to be his peculiar people, as he hath promised thee, and that thou shouldest keep all his commandments;

- The Lord hath avouched thee this day to be his peculiar people, as he hath promised thee: the Lord hath chosen Jacob unto himself, and Israel for his peculiar treasure.
- Ye are a chosen generation, a royal priesthood, an holy nation, a peculiar people, that ye should shew forth the praises of him who hath called you out of darkness into his marvellous light: we were called out of our sins into the light, and the light is the commandments and the law.
- Which in time past were not a people, but are now the people of God: we were called by other names, African American, Dominican, Negro, but now we are coming into the understanding that we are actually the children of Israel.

**[Deuteronomy 26:19](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/26#v19)</sup> And to make thee high above all nations which he hath made, in praise, and in name, and in honour; and that thou mayest be an holy people unto the Lord thy God, as he hath spoken.

- And to make thee high above all nations which he hath made, in praise, and in name, and in honour, and that thou mayest be an holy people unto the Lord thy God, as he hath spoken.
- This is the same word given earlier in Deuteronomy, among other scriptures: thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God, chosen to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth.
