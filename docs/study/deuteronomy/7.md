## Introduction

This session covered Deuteronomy 7-10, precepting through as the chapters were read. The class opened sounding the alarm in the street, lifting up the voice like a trumpet, with [Luke 13:3](/bible/luke/13#v3) and 5: except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish. Classroom order was set by [1 Corinthians 14:33](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v33) and 40, for God is not the author of confusion, and all things are to be done decently and in order. Chapter 7 lays out the destruction of the seven nations, the ban on marrying them, and Israel's election as a holy people; chapter 8 ties life to the commandments and warns against forgetting the Most High in prosperity; chapter 9 strips away self-righteousness and retells the molten calf; chapter 10 gives the second tables, the separation of Levi, and what the Most High requires of Israel.

**[Deuteronomy 7:1-2](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v1)</sup> When the Lord thy God shall bring thee into the land whither thou goest to possess it, and hath cast out many nations before thee, the Hittites, and the Girgashites, and the Amorites, and the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, and the Hivites, and the Jebusites, seven nations greater and mightier than thou;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v2)</sup> And when the Lord thy God shall deliver them before thee; thou shalt smite them, and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them:

- The seven nations cast out before Israel, the Hittites, Girgashites, Amorites, Canaanites, Perizzites, Hivites, and Jebusites, greater and mightier than Israel, are Hamitic nations.
- Thou shalt smite them and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them. Since the days of old the Most High has never been about the love-of-the-world gospel; throw that doctrine in the trash.
- This is why they do not want you reading the old covenant: verses like this correct the miseducation received through Christianity, and the mind begins to correct itself as you read.

**[Deuteronomy 7:3-4](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them; thy daughter thou shalt not give unto his son, nor his daughter shalt thou take unto thy son.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v4)</sup> For they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve other gods: so will the anger of the Lord be kindled against you, and destroy thee suddenly.

- Neither shalt thou make marriages with them: the Most High forbids marrying outside the nation. The seed of the fathers today is found among the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and Native Indians, who make up the twelve tribes of Israel; a spouse may come from any of the twelve tribes, but from within the tribes only.
- Going outside your nation for a husband or wife is despising your brethren in your heart. Whom you marry is the greatest commitment you make in this life; if you truly loved your people, you would choose one of your own people to become aged together with.
- They will turn away thy son from following me: the nations are strangers to the Most High and cannot come into this covenant, so they draw the Israelite spouse off to religions that accept everything. Christianity accepts that union, and that is how they turn you away from your God.

**[Deuteronomy 7:6](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v6)</sup> For thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God: the Lord thy God hath chosen thee to be a special people unto himself, above all people that are upon the face of the earth.

- For thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God: this is one of the most uplifting scriptures in the book, and it should be etched into your children's minds from the moment they begin to read, until it is implanted into their spirit.
- The mind controls the body: what goes into the mind comes out through the body, so a people that knows it is holy and special above all people walks and carries itself differently.
- Being chosen above all people keeps you out of the affairs of this world, their fashions, their hairstyles, their trends and challenges, and keeping up with the Joneses; you cannot flip-flop with every new wave and still carry the speciality the Most High put on you.

  Precepts:

    As a man thinketh in his heart, so is he; the heart is the mind, and what is planted there comes out in how you carry yourself.

**[Deuteronomy 7:7-8](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v7)</sup> The Lord did not set his love upon you, nor choose you, because ye were more in number than any people; for ye were the fewest of all people:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v8)</sup> But because the Lord loved you, and because he would keep the oath which he had sworn unto your fathers, hath the Lord brought you out with a mighty hand, and redeemed you out of the house of bondmen, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt.

- The Lord did not choose Israel for their number, for ye were the fewest of all people: only threescore and ten souls came out of the loins of Jacob when Israel first went down, as Genesis records, some places reading seventy and some counting seventy-five.
- But because the Lord loved you: the Most High needs no other reason, he simply loved us, and we should never apologize for that.
- He kept the oath sworn unto the fathers and redeemed Israel out of the house of bondmen, from the hand of Pharaoh king of Egypt; Israel came out from under Rameses.
