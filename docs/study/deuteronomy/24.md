**[Deuteronomy 24:1](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v1)</sup> When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her: then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.

- When a man hath taken a wife and she find no favour in his eyes because he hath found some uncleanness in her, then let him write her a bill of divorcement, give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.
- Christ magnified this law: when the Pharisees asked why Moses then commanded to give a writing of divorcement, he said, Moses because of the hardness of your hearts suffered you to put away your wives, but from the beginning it was not so.
- Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery; and whoso marrieth her which is put away doth commit adultery.

**[Deuteronomy 24:3-4](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v3)</sup> And if the latter husband hate her, and write her a bill of divorcement, and giveth it in her hand, and sendeth her out of his house; or if the latter husband die, which took her to be his wife;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v4)</sup> Her former husband, which sent her away, may not take her again to be his wife, after that she is defiled; for that is abomination before the Lord: and thou shalt not cause the land to sin, which the Lord thy God giveth thee for an inheritance.

- If the latter husband hate her and write her a bill of divorcement, or if the latter husband die, her former husband which sent her away may not take her again to be his wife after that she is defiled, for that is abomination before the Lord.
- The question is whether a woman who leaves her husband and becomes another man's wife can be joined to the first again; the prophet asks the same thing: shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted?

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 3:1](/bible/jeremiah/3#v1)**

    If a man put away his wife and she become another man's, shall he return unto her again? shall not that land be greatly polluted?

**[Deuteronomy 24:5](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v5)</sup> When a man hath taken a new wife, he shall not go out to war, neither shall he be charged with any business: but he shall be free at home one year, and shall cheer up his wife which he hath taken.

- When a man hath taken a new wife, he shall not go out to war, neither shall he be charged with any business; he shall be free at home one year and shall cheer up his wife which he hath taken. Make it plain: the first year, stay at home and make a baby.
- Husband and wife render due benevolence to one another; benevolence means good, it is a good thing.
- The war laws give the same understanding: the man that had betrothed a wife and not taken her was sent back to his house, lest he die in the battle and another man take her, for the Lord is the one that goeth with the host to fight against the enemies.

**[Deuteronomy 24:6](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v6)</sup> No man shall take the nether or the upper millstone to pledge: for he taketh a man’s life to pledge.

- No man shall take the nether or the upper millstone to pledge, for he taketh a man's life to pledge. The millstone is a tool for grinding meal: the manna was gathered and ground in mills or beaten in a mortar and baked into cakes.
- Taking a man's tool takes away his living, like somebody stealing your tools so you cannot make a living. It is better that they steal your money, because with your tools you can go back and make more money.

**[Deuteronomy 24:9](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v9)</sup> Remember what the Lord thy God did unto Miriam by the way, after that ye were come forth out of Egypt.

- Remember what the Lord thy God did unto Miriam by the way, after that ye were come forth out of Egypt: Miriam and Aaron spake against Moses because of the Ethiopian woman whom he had married, and Miriam was not supposed to be speaking against Moses.
- The anger of the Lord was kindled against them, the cloud departed from off the tabernacle, and behold, Miriam became leprous, white as snow.
- The Lord said, if her father had but spit in her face, should she not be ashamed seven days? Had her father chastised her and rebuked her sharply, she would not have had that tongue. That is for a lot of our sisters today who do not have that understanding.

**[Deuteronomy 24:10-13](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v10)</sup> When thou dost lend thy brother any thing, thou shalt not go into his house to fetch his pledge.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v11)</sup> Thou shalt stand abroad, and the man to whom thou dost lend shall bring out the pledge abroad unto thee.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v12)</sup> And if the man be poor, thou shalt not sleep with his pledge:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v13)</sup> In any case thou shalt deliver him the pledge again when the sun goeth down, that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee: and it shall be righteousness unto thee before the Lord thy God.

- When thou dost lend thy brother any thing, thou shalt not go into his house to fetch his pledge; stand abroad, and let the man bring the pledge out unto thee.
- If the man be poor, thou shalt not sleep with his pledge; deliver him the pledge again when the sun goeth down, that he may sleep in his own raiment and bless thee, and it shall be righteousness unto thee before the Lord thy God.
- The law was already given: if thou at all take thy neighbour's raiment to pledge, deliver it by that the sun goeth down, for that is his covering only, and when he crieth unto the Most High, he will hear, for he is gracious.
- The history shows how wicked we were to each other: Israel laid themselves down upon clothes laid to pledge by every altar, stacking up people's raiment like pallets and drinking the wine of the condemned in the house of their god, instead of returning the pledge before the sun went down as righteousness. That is why the Lord was judging us.

**[Deuteronomy 24:16](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v16)</sup> The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: every man shall be put to death for his own sin.

- The fathers shall not be put to death for the children, neither shall the children be put to death for the fathers: every man shall be put to death for his own sin.
- The example: as soon as the kingdom was confirmed in Amaziah's hand, he slew his servants which had slain the king his father, but the children of the murderers he slew not, according unto that which is written in the book of the law of Moses.
- The soul that sinneth, it shall die: the son shall not bear the iniquity of the father, neither shall the father bear the iniquity of the son.

  Precepts:
