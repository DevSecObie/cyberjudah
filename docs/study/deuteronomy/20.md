**[Deuteronomy 20:1](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v1)</sup> When thou goest out to battle against thine enemies, and seest horses, and chariots, and a people more than thou, be not afraid of them: for the Lord thy God is with thee, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.

- When thou goest out to battle and seest horses and chariots and a people more than thou, be not afraid, for the Lord thy God is with thee, which brought thee up out of the land of Egypt.
- That goes for us now, because we are still in a battle. We are not physically out battling the nations as our forefathers were when they took the land; we are in a spiritual battle, for the nations are confederate against Israel being a nation any more and against our remembrance of Israel and our laws. This whole world is set up for our people to go into sin and iniquity.
- Fear makes people trim their ways: afraid of family at the pagan holiday table, tucking their fringes in certain places, scared to be seen in the truth. Stand boldly; the Lord has not given us the spirit of fear, for that spirit came from Satan himself.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 83:4](/bible/psalms/83#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/83#v4)</sup> They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.

    The nations have said, come, and let us cut them off from being a nation, that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance; this is the battle Israel is in now.
  - **[2 Corinthians 10:3-4](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v3)</sup> For though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-corinthians/10#v4)</sup> (For the weapons of our warfare are not carnal, but mighty through God to the pulling down of strong holds;)

    Though we walk in the flesh, we do not war after the flesh; the weapons of our warfare are not carnal but mighty through God to the pulling down of strongholds, the doctrines, lusts, and wicked thoughts in the minds of our people, fought with the scriptures, the sword of truth.
  - **[2 Timothy 1:7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-timothy/1#v7)</sup> For God hath not given us the spirit of fear; but of power, and of love, and of a sound mind.

    God hath not given us the spirit of fear; do not be afraid in this battle.

**[Deuteronomy 20:2-4](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v2)</sup> And it shall be, when ye are come nigh unto the battle, that the priest shall approach and speak unto the people,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v3)</sup> And shall say unto them, Hear, O Israel, ye approach this day unto battle against your enemies: let not your hearts faint, fear not, and do not tremble, neither be ye terrified because of them;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v4)</sup> For the Lord your God is he that goeth with you, to fight for you against your enemies, to save you.

- When Israel came nigh unto the battle, the priest approached and spoke: let not your hearts faint, fear not, do not tremble, neither be ye terrified, for the Lord your God is he that goeth with you to fight for you against your enemies to save you.
- A lot of times you do not see that the Lord is fighting for you in the spirit; that is where faith comes in, for the Bible is a faith-based book. You have to have faith that the Most High is fighting for you, with angels encamped round about the righteous, as David told us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 34:7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)</sup> The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

    The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

**[Deuteronomy 20:5-7](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v5)</sup> And the officers shall speak unto the people, saying, What man is there that hath built a new house, and hath not dedicated it? let him go and return to his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man dedicate it.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v6)</sup> And what man is he that hath planted a vineyard, and hath not yet eaten of it? let him also go and return unto his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man eat of it.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v7)</sup> And what man is there that hath betrothed a wife, and hath not taken her? let him go and return unto his house, lest he die in the battle, and another man take her.

- The officers sent home the man that had built a new house and not dedicated it, the man that had planted a vineyard and not eaten of it, and the man that had betrothed a wife and not taken her, lest he die in the battle and another man take his portion.
- You could very well die fighting for your people, so go enjoy these things first: dedicate your new house unto the Lord, eat the fruits of your labour from your vineyard, take your wife, then come to war for your people.

**[Deuteronomy 20:8](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v8)</sup> And the officers shall speak further unto the people, and they shall say, What man is there that is fearful and fainthearted? let him go and return unto his house, lest his brethren’s heart faint as well as his heart.

- The fearful and fainthearted man was also sent home, lest his brethren's heart faint as well as his heart. The spirit of fear spreads: one man's fear makes the next man wonder what he knows, and soon the whole company questions the battle.

**[Deuteronomy 20:9](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v9)</sup> And it shall be, when the officers have made an end of speaking unto the people, that they shall make captains of the armies to lead the people.

- When the officers finished speaking, they made captains of the armies to lead the people, because you cannot have order without a ranking structure. Everybody cannot be calling the shots; there is one shot caller and the people follow, and that same order set in Moses' time is why a body under rank is proficient in everything it does.

**[Deuteronomy 20:10-11](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v10)</sup> When thou comest nigh unto a city to fight against it, then proclaim peace unto it.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v11)</sup> And it shall be, if it make thee answer of peace, and open unto thee, then it shall be, that all the people that is found therein shall be tributaries unto thee, and they shall serve thee.

- When thou comest nigh unto a city to fight against it, first proclaim peace unto it. If it answered peace and opened, all the people found in it became tributaries unto Israel and served them.
- In this time Israel had the power to make nations tributaries. Right now we are not even a nation, just a people with Bibles and nothing established; but wait until Christ comes, for Israel will be established as a nation again and rule the earth, and the nations will be tributaries.

**[Deuteronomy 20:12-14](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v12)</sup> And if it will make no peace with thee, but will make war against thee, then thou shalt besiege it:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v13)</sup> And when the Lord thy God hath delivered it into thine hands, thou shalt smite every male thereof with the edge of the sword:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v14)</sup> But the women, and the little ones, and the cattle, and all that is in the city, even all the spoil thereof, shalt thou take unto thyself; and thou shalt eat the spoil of thine enemies, which the Lord thy God hath given thee.

- Israel came to a land with terms: agree, pay the tribute, and there is peace. If the city would make no peace but made war, Israel besieged it, and when the Lord delivered it into their hands, every male was smitten with the edge of the sword.
- The women, the little ones, the cattle, and all the spoil of the city Israel took unto themselves and ate the spoil of their enemies which the Lord their God had given them.

**[Deuteronomy 20:15-18](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v15)</sup> Thus shalt thou do unto all the cities which are very far off from thee, which are not of the cities of these nations.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v16)</sup> But of the cities of these people, which the Lord thy God doth give thee for an inheritance, thou shalt save alive nothing that breatheth:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v17)</sup> But thou shalt utterly destroy them; namely, the Hittites, and the Amorites, the Canaanites, and the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites; as the Lord thy God hath commanded thee:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v18)</sup> That they teach you not to do after all their abominations, which they have done unto their gods; so should ye sin against the Lord your God.

- That was the rule for the cities very far off. But of the cities of the nations given Israel for an inheritance, thou shalt save alive nothing that breatheth; everything had to die, from humans to animals.
- The Hittites, the Amorites, the Canaanites, the Perizzites, the Hivites, and the Jebusites were to be utterly destroyed for a stated purpose: that they teach you not to do after all their abominations which they have done unto their gods, their whoredoms, their idolatry, and their wicked ways of living, so that Israel sin not against the Lord.

**[Deuteronomy 20:19-20](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v19)</sup> When thou shalt besiege a city a long time, in making war against it to take it, thou shalt not destroy the trees thereof by forcing an axe against them: for thou mayest eat of them, and thou shalt not cut them down (for the tree of the field is man’s life) to employ them in the siege:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/20#v20)</sup> Only the trees which thou knowest that they be not trees for meat, thou shalt destroy and cut them down; and thou shalt build bulwarks against the city that maketh war with thee, until it be subdued.

- In a long siege the trees for meat were not to be destroyed by forcing an axe against them, for thou mayest eat of them; the tree of the field is man's life.
- Only the trees known not to be trees for meat could be cut down to build bulwarks against the city that maketh war with thee, until it be subdued.
