**[Deuteronomy 34:1-4](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v1)</sup> And Moses went up from the plains of Moab unto the mountain of Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, that is over against Jericho. And the Lord shewed him all the land of Gilead, unto Dan,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v2)</sup> And all Naphtali, and the land of Ephraim, and Manasseh, and all the land of Judah, unto the utmost sea,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v3)</sup> And the south, and the plain of the valley of Jericho, the city of palm trees, unto Zoar.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v4)</sup> And the Lord said unto him, This is the land which I sware unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, saying, I will give it unto thy seed: I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not go over thither.

- Moses went up from the plains of Moab unto the mountain of Nebo, to the top of Pisgah, over against Jericho, and the Most High shewed him all the land: Gilead unto Dan, Naphtali, Ephraim and Manasseh, Judah unto the utmost sea, the south, and the plain of the valley of Jericho unto Zoar.
- This is the land which I sware unto Abraham, unto Isaac, and unto Jacob, saying, I will give it unto thy seed: I have caused thee to see it with thine eyes, but thou shalt not go over thither. Moses could see it, but he could not go into it.

**[Deuteronomy 34:5-7](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v5)</sup> So Moses the servant of the Lord died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v6)</sup> And he buried him in a valley in the land of Moab, over against Beth–peor: but no man knoweth of his sepulchre unto this day.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v7)</sup> And Moses was an hundred and twenty years old when he died: his eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated.

- So Moses the servant of the Lord died there in the land of Moab, according to the word of the Lord, and he was buried in a valley in the land of Moab, over against Bethpeor: but no man knoweth of his sepulchre unto this day. So do not go trying to find it.
- Moses was an hundred and twenty years old when he died: his eye was not dim, nor his natural force abated. He was not weak, old, and sickly like a man about to drop dead; he was in full strength, and still he had to go up and die because he could not enter the land.

**[Deuteronomy 34:8-9](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v8)</sup> And the children of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days: so the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were ended.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v9)</sup> And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom; for Moses had laid his hands upon him: and the children of Israel hearkened unto him, and did as the Lord commanded Moses.

- The children of Israel wept for Moses in the plains of Moab thirty days, until the days of weeping and mourning for Moses were ended.
- Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom; for Moses had laid his hands upon him. Joshua was Moses' protege, and he carried the spirit Moses had, a great leader compassionate for the people; and the children of Israel hearkened unto him, and did as the Lord commanded Moses.

**[Deuteronomy 34:10-12](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v10)</sup> And there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the Lord knew face to face,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v11)</sup> In all the signs and the wonders, which the Lord sent him to do in the land of Egypt to Pharaoh, and to all his servants, and to all his land,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v12)</sup> And in all that mighty hand, and in all the great terror which Moses shewed in the sight of all Israel.

- And there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the Lord knew face to face. This verse is heavy because of a doctrine: some say Joshua was the prophet of [Deuteronomy 18:18](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v18), the prophet raised up like unto Moses, and therefore there is no Christ.
- But the scripture that acknowledges Joshua rising up with the spirit upon him says in the same breath that there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses. Joshua was a mighty prophet, but he did not fit that word; Christ did.
- None matched all the signs and the wonders which the Lord sent Moses to do in the land of Egypt, and all that mighty hand, and all the great terror which Moses shewed in the sight of all Israel. With this the Torah, the books of Moses, is finished.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 18:18](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v18)</sup> I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee, and will put my words in his mouth; and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him.

    I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee: Christ, not Joshua, fulfilled this word.
