**[Deuteronomy 9:1-3](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v1)</sup> Hear, O Israel: Thou art to pass over Jordan this day, to go in to possess nations greater and mightier than thyself, cities great and fenced up to heaven,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v2)</sup> A people great and tall, the children of the Anakims, whom thou knowest, and of whom thou hast heard say, Who can stand before the children of Anak!
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v3)</sup> Understand therefore this day, that the Lord thy God is he which goeth over before thee; as a consuming fire he shall destroy them, and he shall bring them down before thy face: so shalt thou drive them out, and destroy them quickly, as the Lord hath said unto thee.

- Hear, O Israel: thou art to pass over Jordan this day, to possess nations greater and mightier than thyself, a people great and tall, the children of the Anakims, of whom it was said, who can stand before the children of Anak.
- The Anakims are Hamites, scattered now in the land of Ham; do not ask which nation they are today, for the Most High has not told us.
- The Lord thy God goeth over before thee as a consuming fire; he shall destroy them and bring them down before thy face, so shalt thou drive them out and destroy them quickly.

**[Deuteronomy 9:4-6](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v4)</sup> Speak not thou in thine heart, after that the Lord thy God hath cast them out from before thee, saying, For my righteousness the Lord hath brought me in to possess this land: but for the wickedness of these nations the Lord doth drive them out from before thee.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v5)</sup> Not for thy righteousness, or for the uprightness of thine heart, dost thou go to possess their land: but for the wickedness of these nations the Lord thy God doth drive them out from before thee, and that he may perform the word which the Lord sware unto thy fathers, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v6)</sup> Understand therefore, that the Lord thy God giveth thee not this good land to possess it for thy righteousness; for thou art a stiffnecked people.

- Speak not thou in thine heart, saying, for my righteousness the Lord hath brought me in to possess this land: here is more of the Christianity spirit corrected. Israel entered for the wickedness of those nations and to perform the word sworn unto Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, not for their own uprightness.
- Understand that the only reason any of us have a roof over our head and food in our stomach right now is the mercy and righteousness of the Most High, for thou art a stiffnecked people; it is because of him that we survive every single day.

**[Deuteronomy 9:7-9](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v7)</sup> Remember, and forget not, how thou provokedst the Lord thy God to wrath in the wilderness: from the day that thou didst depart out of the land of Egypt, until ye came unto this place, ye have been rebellious against the Lord.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v8)</sup> Also in Horeb ye provoked the Lord to wrath, so that the Lord was angry with you to have destroyed you.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v9)</sup> When I was gone up into the mount to receive the tables of stone, even the tables of the covenant which the Lord made with you, then I abode in the mount forty days and forty nights, I neither did eat bread nor drink water:

- Remember, and forget not, how thou provokedst the Lord thy God to wrath in the wilderness: from the day of leaving Egypt unto this place, ye have been rebellious against the Lord.
- Moses abode in the mount forty days and forty nights, neither eating bread nor drinking water, when he went up to receive the tables of the covenant.
- What was he doing those forty days? He was receiving the understanding of the beginning of times, the midst of times, and the end of times; that is why some things Moses wrote, like [Genesis 49](/bible/genesis/49) and [Deuteronomy 32](/bible/deuteronomy/32) and 33, are hard to understand, hidden in plain view, while keep the commandments is written easy.

    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-esdras/14#v2)</sup> And I said, Here am I, Lord And I stood up upon my feet.

**[Deuteronomy 9:10-11](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v10)</sup> And the Lord delivered unto me two tables of stone written with the finger of God; and on them was written according to all the words, which the Lord spake with you in the mount out of the midst of the fire in the day of the assembly.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v11)</sup> And it came to pass at the end of forty days and forty nights, that the Lord gave me the two tables of stone, even the tables of the covenant.

- The Lord delivered unto me two tables of stone written with the finger of God: God took his time and wrote the ten commandments down himself, according to all the words which the Lord spake with you in the mount out of the midst of the fire.

**[Deuteronomy 9:12-14](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v12)</sup> And the Lord said unto me, Arise, get thee down quickly from hence; for thy people which thou hast brought forth out of Egypt have corrupted themselves; they are quickly turned aside out of the way which I commanded them; they have made them a molten image.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v13)</sup> Furthermore the Lord spake unto me, saying, I have seen this people, and, behold, it is a stiffnecked people:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v14)</sup> Let me alone, that I may destroy them, and blot out their name from under heaven: and I will make of thee a nation mightier and greater than they.

- Arise, get thee down quickly; thy people have corrupted themselves and made them a molten image. That molten image was the calf, the Egyptian goddess Hathor; you can look that up yourself.
- Let me alone, that I may destroy them, and blot out their name from under heaven, and I will make of thee a nation mightier and greater than they. The Most High was ready to go back to the days of the flood and start over with one man; time means nothing to the Ancient of days.
- Religion has tried to remove the fierceness, the wrath, and the severity of the Most High and push an effeminate white Jesus in front of us that loves everybody, forgives everything at the last breath with no commandments kept, and can do no wrong. We are suffering in all our cities, in Africa, in Europe, and in the Caribbean from that lack of fear of God before our eyes.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 3:18](/bible/romans/3#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/romans/3#v18)</sup> There is no fear of God before their eyes.

    There is no fear of God before their eyes; religion removed the fear and austerity of the Most High from before us, and this is the result.

**[Deuteronomy 9:15-17](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v15)</sup> So I turned and came down from the mount, and the mount burned with fire: and the two tables of the covenant were in my two hands.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v16)</sup> And I looked, and, behold, ye had sinned against the Lord your God, and had made you a molten calf: ye had turned aside quickly out of the way which the Lord had commanded you.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v17)</sup> And I took the two tables, and cast them out of my two hands, and brake them before your eyes.

- Moses came down from the mount, the mount burning with fire, the two tables of the covenant in his two hands, and behold, ye had sinned against the Lord your God and made you a molten calf.
- Ye had turned aside quickly out of the way which the Lord had commanded you: the Most High had been proving them through hard times, and they quickly turned back, just as many today go right back to their old ways the moment they are left alone. Brothers and sisters, check yourselves; we must endure to the end and rehearse the righteous acts now.
- Moses took the two tables, cast them out of his two hands, and brake them before your eyes.

**[Deuteronomy 9:18-21](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v18)</sup> And I fell down before the Lord, as at the first, forty days and forty nights: I did neither eat bread, nor drink water, because of all your sins which ye sinned, in doing wickedly in the sight of the Lord, to provoke him to anger.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v19)</sup> For I was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure, wherewith the Lord was wroth against you to destroy you. But the Lord hearkened unto me at that time also.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v20)</sup> And the Lord was very angry with Aaron to have destroyed him: and I prayed for Aaron also the same time.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v21)</sup> And I took your sin, the calf which ye had made, and burnt it with fire, and stamped it, and ground it very small, even until it was as small as dust: and I cast the dust thereof into the brook that descended out of the mount.

- Moses fell down before the Lord, as at the first, forty days and forty nights, neither eating bread nor drinking water, because of all your sins; that is two forty-day fasts, for he was afraid of the anger and hot displeasure wherewith the Lord was wroth against the people.
- The Lord was very angry with Aaron to have destroyed him, and Moses prayed for Aaron also at the same time. Moses prayed, and the Lord hearkened unto him; there arose no prophet like Moses, a mighty great man in the scriptures.
- He took your sin, the calf which ye had made, burnt it with fire, stamped it, ground it very small until it was as small as dust, and cast the dust into the brook that descended out of the mount.

**[Deuteronomy 9:22-24](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v22)</sup> And at Taberah, and at Massah, and at Kibroth–hattaavah, ye provoked the Lord to wrath.

> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v23)</sup> Likewise when the Lord sent you from Kadesh–barnea, saying, Go up and possess the land which I have given you; then ye rebelled against the commandment of the Lord your God, and ye believed him not, nor hearkened to his voice.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v24)</sup> Ye have been rebellious against the Lord from the day that I knew you.

- Ye have been rebellious against the Lord from the day that I knew you: Moses called Israel out plainly, and it is real talk still.

**[Deuteronomy 9:25-29](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v25)</sup> Thus I fell down before the Lord forty days and forty nights, as I fell down at the first; because the Lord had said he would destroy you.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v26)</sup> I prayed therefore unto the Lord, and said, O Lord God, destroy not thy people and thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed through thy greatness, which thou hast brought forth out of Egypt with a mighty hand.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v27)</sup> Remember thy servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; look not unto the stubbornness of this people, nor to their wickedness, nor to their sin:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v28)</sup> Lest the land whence thou broughtest us out say, Because the Lord was not able to bring them into the land which he promised them, and because he hated them, he hath brought them out to slay them in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/9#v29)</sup> Yet they are thy people and thine inheritance, which thou broughtest out by thy mighty power and by thy stretched out arm.

- Moses fell down before the Lord forty days and forty nights because the Lord had said he would destroy the people, and prayed: O Lord God, destroy not thy people and thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed through thy greatness.
- Remember thy servants, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob; look not unto the stubbornness of this people, nor to their wickedness, lest the land whence thou broughtest us out say the Lord was not able to bring them in, and because he hated them he brought them out to slay them.
- Esdras prayed the same prayer as Moses for the children of Israel, so precept these two prayers together: pay no attention to the fakers, for they remove themselves, but be merciful to us who have no works of righteousness, for by that mercy thou shalt be called merciful. When we are in the midst of sin we live worse than beasts, for a beast runs on instinct, but we consciously choose to go against the laws of the Most High.
