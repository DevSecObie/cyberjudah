## Introduction

This session covers [Deuteronomy 27](/bible/deuteronomy/27) through 30, the heart of the covenant. Chapter 27 commands Israel to write the law very plainly upon great stones and sets half the tribes on mount Gerizim to bless and half on mount Ebal to curse, with the people answering Amen to every curse. Chapter 28 lays out the blessings for obedience and the curses for disobedience, the curses that identify who the true Israelites are to this day. Chapter 29 is the covenant made in the land of Moab, made not only with those who stood there that day but with us who were not there. Chapter 30 is the goodness of the Most High: the way back out of the curses, to call them to mind in captivity, return, keep the commandments, and choose life.

**[Deuteronomy 27:2-3](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v2)</sup> And it shall be on the day when ye shall pass over Jordan unto the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, that thou shalt set thee up great stones, and plaister them with plaister:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v3)</sup> And thou shalt write upon them all the words of this law, when thou art passed over, that thou mayest go in unto the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee, a land that floweth with milk and honey; as the Lord God of thy fathers hath promised thee.

- On the day Israel passed over Jordan into the land, they were to set up great stones, plaster them with plaster, and write upon them all the words of this law.
- Here is the difference between the truth and Islam and every other religion. The stone is not to be worshipped or thought to be a god; you set it up and put the laws on it so you can remember the laws.

**[Deuteronomy 27:4-8](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v4)</sup> Therefore it shall be when ye be gone over Jordan, that ye shall set up these stones, which I command you this day, in mount Ebal, and thou shalt plaister them with plaister.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v5)</sup> And there shalt thou build an altar unto the Lord thy God, an altar of stones: thou shalt not lift up any iron tool upon them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v6)</sup> Thou shalt build the altar of the Lord thy God of whole stones: and thou shalt offer burnt offerings thereon unto the Lord thy God:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v7)</sup> And thou shalt offer peace offerings, and shalt eat there, and rejoice before the Lord thy God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v8)</sup> And thou shalt write upon the stones all the words of this law very plainly.

- The stones were to be set up in mount Ebal, and an altar of whole stones built there unto the Most High; no iron tool was to be lifted upon them.
- Burnt offerings and peace offerings were offered there, and the people were to eat and rejoice before the Most High.
- Thou shalt write upon the stones all the words of this law very plainly. That is what the Most High wants remembered most: not the stone itself, but the law written on it.

**[Deuteronomy 27:9-10](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v9)</sup> And Moses and the priests the Levites spake unto all Israel, saying, Take heed, and hearken, O Israel; this day thou art become the people of the Lord thy God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v10)</sup> Thou shalt therefore obey the voice of the Lord thy God, and do his commandments and his statutes, which I command thee this day.

- Moses and the priests the Levites told all Israel: this day thou art become the people of the Lord thy God; therefore obey his voice and do his commandments and statutes.
- Even the other nations' own books witness to it. The Martyrdom of Man says that "God made all men equal" is a fine sounding phrase but not a scientific fact, and that those who outlive hardships and sufferings which fall on all alike owe their existence to some superiority not only of body but of mind.
- There is no way we could have lasted this long through what we have been through if we were not the people of the Most High: thousands of years run out of the homeland, living among our enemies, put on ships and transported all over the world, and still surviving from 1492 on up here in Babylon the great.

**[Deuteronomy 27:11-13](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v11)</sup> And Moses charged the people the same day, saying,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v12)</sup> These shall stand upon mount Gerizim to bless the people, when ye are come over Jordan; Simeon, and Levi, and Judah, and Issachar, and Joseph, and Benjamin:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v13)</sup> And these shall stand upon mount Ebal to curse; Reuben, Gad, and Asher, and Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali.

- Moses charged the people that day: Simeon, Levi, Judah, Issachar, Joseph (which is Ephraim and Manasseh), and Benjamin were to stand upon mount Gerizim to bless the people.
- Reuben, Gad, Asher, Zebulun, Dan, and Naphtali were to stand upon mount Ebal to curse. Half the tribes stood on one side and half on the other, one saying one thing and the other answering.

**[Deuteronomy 27:14-15](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v14)</sup> And the Levites shall speak, and say unto all the men of Israel with a loud voice,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v15)</sup> Cursed be the man that maketh any graven or molten image, an abomination unto the Lord, the work of the hands of the craftsman, and putteth it in a secret place. And all the people shall answer and say, Amen.

- The Levites were to speak with a loud voice unto all the men of Israel: cursed be the man that maketh any graven or molten image, an abomination unto the Most High, the work of the hands of the craftsman, and putteth it in a secret place.
- And all the people shall answer and say, Amen. Every curse that follows carried that same answer from the whole congregation.

**[Deuteronomy 27:16](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v16)</sup> Cursed be he that setteth light by his father or his mother. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that setteth light by his father or his mother. Setting light by them means disrespecting your parents and not obeying them.

**[Deuteronomy 27:17](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v17)</sup> Cursed be he that removeth his neighbour’s landmark. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that removeth his neighbour's landmark. Every man in Israel was allotted a certain plot of land for his inheritance individually.
- If you moved that landmark trying to make your own land bigger, the Most High says cursed be upon you. The allotments are recorded in Joshua and in [Numbers 27](/bible/numbers/27), where the inheritance laws are given.

**[Deuteronomy 27:18](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v18)</sup> Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that maketh the blind to wander out of the way. Do not mistreat those we call the disabled.

**[Deuteronomy 27:19](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v19)</sup> Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that perverteth the judgment of the stranger, fatherless, and widow.
- This goes back to Deuteronomy chapter 1, where the judges were charged to judge righteously between every man and his brother and the stranger with him, and to respect no persons in judgment.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 1:16-17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v16)</sup> And I charged your judges at that time, saying, Hear the causes between your brethren, and judge righteously between every man and his brother, and the stranger that is with him.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/1#v17)</sup> Ye shall not respect persons in judgment; but ye shall hear the small as well as the great; ye shall not be afraid of the face of man; for the judgment is God’s: and the cause that is too hard for you, bring it unto me, and I will hear it.

    Judge righteously between every man and his brother and the stranger; ye shall not respect persons in judgment.

**[Deuteronomy 27:20](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v20)</sup> Cursed be he that lieth with his father’s wife; because he uncovereth his father’s skirt. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that lieth with his father's wife, because he uncovereth his father's skirt. That is nasty stuff, and the curse of the whole congregation stood against it.

**[Deuteronomy 27:21](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v21)</sup> Cursed be he that lieth with any manner of beast. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that lieth with any manner of beast. Bestiality is the practice of Esau, and Israel was cursed for touching it.

**[Deuteronomy 27:22](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v22)</sup> Cursed be he that lieth with his sister, the daughter of his father, or the daughter of his mother. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that lieth with his sister, the daughter of his father or the daughter of his mother.
- This goes back to Leviticus chapter 18, where the nakedness laws were first given.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 18:9](/bible/leviticus/18#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/18#v9)</sup> The nakedness of thy sister, the daughter of thy father, or daughter of thy mother, whether she be born at home, or born abroad, even their nakedness thou shalt not uncover.

    The nakedness of thy sister thou shalt not uncover; the curse repeats the law already given.

**[Deuteronomy 27:23-24](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v23)</sup> Cursed be he that lieth with his mother in law. And all the people shall say, Amen.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v24)</sup> Cursed be he that smiteth his neighbour secretly. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that lieth with his mother in law, and all the people shall say, Amen.
- Cursed be he that smiteth his neighbour secretly, and all the people shall say, Amen.

**[Deuteronomy 27:25](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v25)</sup> Cursed be he that taketh reward to slay an innocent person. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that taketh reward to slay an innocent person. That is your hitman right there in verse 25: taking payment to kill the innocent puts you under the curse.

**[Deuteronomy 27:26](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v26)</sup> Cursed be he that confirmeth not all the words of this law to do them. And all the people shall say, Amen.

- Cursed be he that confirmeth not all the words of this law to do them. At that time this encompassed everything, including the law of sacrifice.
- It also applies to us today, because sacrifice is alive and well; it just transformed. We still sacrifice, only it is no longer animals but our bodies.
- Our bodies must be holy and without blemish before the Most High, because the law says you cannot bring anything with blemishes into the house of the Lord. If your sacrifice is not holy and acceptable, the Son is not taking you to present you before the Father. Christ magnified the law, as Isaiah says.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

    Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God; the animal no longer suffices, the sacrifice is your body.
  - **[Isaiah 42:21](/bible/isaiah/42#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/42#v21)</sup> The Lord is well pleased for his righteousness’ sake; he will magnify the law, and make it honourable.

    He will magnify the law and make it honourable; Christ magnified the law, he did not do away with the sacrifice principle.

# Deuteronomy 28: The Blessings and the Curses That Identify Israel

Read the chapter → [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28)

**[Deuteronomy 28:1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:

- If thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God to observe and to do all his commandments, the Most High will set thee on high above all nations of the earth.
- Diligent means characterized by steady, earnest, and energetic effort. You have to be on fire for the laws of the Most High, standing fast.
- You cannot be one foot in and one foot out, lukewarm, yawning through the classes. You cannot be tired of the word of the Lord; be excited to hear it.

**[Deuteronomy 28:2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v2)</sup> And all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God.

- All these blessings shall come on thee and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God. Hearken means to listen.

**[Deuteronomy 28:3-6](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v3)</sup> Blessed shalt thou be in the city, and blessed shalt thou be in the field.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v4)</sup> Blessed shall be the fruit of thy body, and the fruit of thy ground, and the fruit of thy cattle, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v5)</sup> Blessed shall be thy basket and thy store.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v6)</sup> Blessed shalt thou be when thou comest in, and blessed shalt thou be when thou goest out.

- Blessed in the city and blessed in the field; blessed shall be the fruit of thy body, which is your children, and the fruit of thy ground and cattle.
- Blessed shall be thy basket and thy store: that is going into your surplus.
- Blessed when thou comest in and blessed when thou goest out: it is twofold, but it is going into coming in and out of Jerusalem.

**[Deuteronomy 28:7-14](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v7)</sup> The Lord shall cause thine enemies that rise up against thee to be smitten before thy face: they shall come out against thee one way, and flee before thee seven ways.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v8)</sup> The Lord shall command the blessing upon thee in thy storehouses, and in all that thou settest thine hand unto; and he shall bless thee in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v9)</sup> The Lord shall establish thee an holy people unto himself, as he hath sworn unto thee, if thou shalt keep the commandments of the Lord thy God, and walk in his ways.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v10)</sup> And all people of the earth shall see that thou art called by the name of the Lord; and they shall be afraid of thee.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v11)</sup> And the Lord shall make thee plenteous in goods, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy ground, in the land which the Lord sware unto thy fathers to give thee.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v12)</sup> The Lord shall open unto thee his good treasure, the heaven to give the rain unto thy land in his season, and to bless all the work of thine hand: and thou shalt lend unto many nations, and thou shalt not borrow.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v13)</sup> And the Lord shall make thee the head, and not the tail; and thou shalt be above only, and thou shalt not be beneath; if that thou hearken unto the commandments of the Lord thy God, which I command thee this day, to observe and to do them:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v14)</sup> And thou shalt not go aside from any of the words which I command thee this day, to the right hand, or to the left, to go after other gods to serve them.

- The enemies that rise up against thee shall flee before thee seven ways; the blessing is commanded on thy storehouses and on all thou settest thine hand unto.
- The Most High shall establish thee an holy people unto himself, and all the people of the earth shall see that thou art called by the name of the Lord and be afraid of thee.
- Thou shalt lend unto many nations and not borrow; the Lord shall make thee the head and not the tail, above only and not beneath, if thou hearken to the commandments and turn not aside after other gods.
- Isaiah sums up [Deuteronomy 28:1-14](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1): we possessed it but a little while. Israel had the blessings to a point when they went into the land, but only about eighty years at the full level, under David and Solomon. How Solomon abounded in gold, ships, and plenty of food you can read in 1 Kings.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 63:18](/bible/isaiah/63#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/63#v18)</sup> The people of thy holiness have possessed it but a little while: our adversaries have trodden down thy sanctuary.

    The people of thy holiness have possessed it but a little while; our adversaries have trodden down thy sanctuary.

**[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

- But if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes, all these curses shall come upon thee and overtake thee. Now we do some precepting, word by word.
- The voice of the Lord is his commandments; to hearken unto his voice means to keep his commandments.
- Observe to DO all his commandments: do is the action word. The Most High is a God of doing, and by him actions are weighed. Do not talk proudly and arrogantly, saying God loves me no matter what and God knows my heart; he had a personal relationship with a few men like Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Samuel, and David, and talking like you are one of them is arrogancy coming out of your mouth.
- Curses means evil: the Lord said, I will bring evil upon this people because they have not hearkened unto my words. Overtake thee means you shall not be able to escape them, and though they cry unto him, he will not hearken.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 103:20](/bible/psalms/103#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/103#v20)</sup> Bless the Lord, ye his angels, that excel in strength, that do his commandments, hearkening unto the voice of his word.

    His angels do his commandments, hearkening unto the voice of his word; the voice of the Most High is his commandments.
  - **[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

    Talk no more so exceeding proudly; the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.
  - **[James 2:20](/bible/james/2#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/james/2#v20)</sup> But wilt thou know, O vain man, that faith without works is dead?

    Faith without works is dead; the works are the action of doing the commandments.
  - **[James 2:26](/bible/james/2#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/james/2#v26)</sup> For as the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.

    As the body without the spirit is dead, so faith without works is dead also.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:19](/bible/jeremiah/6#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/jeremiah/6#v19)</sup> Hear, O earth: behold, I will bring evil upon this people, even the fruit of their thoughts, because they have not hearkened unto my words, nor to my law, but rejected it.

    I will bring evil upon this people because they have not hearkened unto my words nor to my law; evil is another way of saying curses.
  - **[Jeremiah 11:11](/bible/jeremiah/11#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/jeremiah/11#v11)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord, Behold, I will bring evil upon them, which they shall not be able to escape; and though they shall cry unto me, I will not hearken unto them.

    I will bring evil upon them which they shall not be able to escape; the curses shall overtake thee, meaning you will not escape them.

**[Deuteronomy 28:16](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v16)</sup> Cursed shalt thou be in the city, and cursed shalt thou be in the field.

- Cursed shalt thou be in the city: as when police fired on a car of siblings as young as nine years old in Georgia after the children panicked when a cruiser followed their vehicle. That is cursed in the city.
- Cursed shalt thou be in the field: we came off the slave ship and went right to the field, picking cotton from sunup to sundown. We know that one.

**[Deuteronomy 28:17](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v17)</sup> Cursed shall be thy basket and thy store.

- Cursed shall be thy basket and thy store: that goes into your businesses and your holdings. It is hard enough for a black man or woman to start a business; we are left with side hustles while the major trades, the ships, the metals, and the timber belong to Esau.
- These curses are on us as a nation. It is not about us individually; keep that in mind.

**[Deuteronomy 28:18](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v18)</sup> Cursed shall be the fruit of thy body, and the fruit of thy land, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep.

- Cursed shall be the fruit of thy body: your children. The same children panicking in front of police, and the dozens of children killed by youth gun violence in Chicago in a matter of months.
- Cursed shall be the fruit of thy land, thy kine, and thy sheep: the food we eat is cursed. Fast food fills our neighborhoods, and where there is no fast food there is a corner store selling unclean meat that causes diseases in us.
- Ezekiel says it plainly: the children of Israel eat their defiled bread among the Gentiles. Others may eat that food, but it is foul for us because we are supposed to eat the best food on the planet, for we are an holy people as [Deuteronomy 14](/bible/deuteronomy/14) says.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 4:13](/bible/ezekiel/4#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/4#v13)</sup> And the Lord said, Even thus shall the children of Israel eat their defiled bread among the Gentiles, whither I will drive them.

    Even thus shall the children of Israel eat their defiled bread among the Gentiles, whither I will drive them.
  - **[Deuteronomy 14:2](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/14#v2)</sup> For thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God, and the Lord hath chosen thee to be a peculiar people unto himself, above all the nations that are upon the earth.

    Thou art an holy people unto the Lord thy God, chosen to be a peculiar people above all the nations upon the earth.

**[Deuteronomy 28:19](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v19)</sup> Cursed shalt thou be when thou comest in, and cursed shalt thou be when thou goest out.

- Cursed shalt thou be when thou comest in and cursed when thou goest out. He is talking about Jerusalem, but it is twofold: it is coming in and going out of life itself.
- Ecclesiasticus, for you newer people in the Apocrypha, spells it out: we are born under the curses and we die still living under them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 41:8-9](/bible/sirach/41#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/41#v8)</sup> Woe be unto you, ungodly men, which have forsaken the law of the most high God! for if ye increase, it shall be to your destruction:
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/41#v9)</sup> And if ye be born, ye shall be born to a curse: and if ye die, a curse shall be your portion.

    Born to a curse and dying with a curse for a portion; and the more we have increased as a people, the worse it has become.

**[Deuteronomy 28:20](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v20)</sup> The Lord shall send upon thee cursing, vexation, and rebuke, in all that thou settest thine hand unto for to do, until thou be destroyed, and until thou perish quickly; because of the wickedness of thy doings, whereby thou hast forsaken me.

- The Lord shall send upon thee cursing, vexation, and rebuke in all that thou settest thine hand unto, until thou be destroyed.
- Rebuke means sharp disapproval or criticism because of your behavior: no matter what you do, nobody will see the silver lining in it; even what you mean for good turns out bad.
- Vexation means the state of being annoyed or a thing that causes annoyance: among these nations they do not love you, they tolerate you. Your marching for equal rights annoys them; they ask why you will not just accept what you were given.

**[Deuteronomy 28:21-22](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v21)</sup> The Lord shall make the pestilence cleave unto thee, until he have consumed thee from off the land, whither thou goest to possess it.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v22)</sup> The Lord shall smite thee with a consumption, and with a fever, and with an inflammation, and with an extreme burning, and with the sword, and with blasting, and with mildew; and they shall pursue thee until thou perish.

- The Lord shall make the pestilence cleave unto thee: diseases. He shall smite thee with a consumption, a fever, an inflammation, an extreme burning, the sword, blasting, and mildew.
- Amos shows what the blasting and mildew go into: famine, pestilence after the manner of Egypt, young men slain with the sword, death and destruction.

  Precepts:
  - **[Amos 4:9](/bible/amos/4#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/amos/4#v9)</sup> I have smitten you with blasting and mildew: when your gardens and your vineyards and your fig trees and your olive trees increased, the palmerworm devoured them: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.

    I have smitten you with blasting and mildew; your gardens, vineyards, fig trees, and olive trees the palmerworm devoured: famine.
  - **[Amos 4:10](/bible/amos/4#v10)**

    > <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v29)</sup> And thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways: and thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save thee.

    Thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save thee; the quick precept for no man shall buy you.

# Deuteronomy 29: The Covenant Made with Us Who Were Not There

Read the chapter → [Deuteronomy 29](/bible/deuteronomy/29)

**[Deuteronomy 29:1](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v1)</sup> These are the words of the covenant, which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, beside the covenant which he made with them in Horeb.

- These are the words of the covenant which the Lord commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, beside the covenant which he made with them in Horeb.

**[Deuteronomy 29:2-4](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v2)</sup> And Moses called unto all Israel, and said unto them, Ye have seen all that the Lord did before your eyes in the land of Egypt unto Pharaoh, and unto all his servants, and unto all his land;

>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v4)</sup> Yet the Lord hath not given you an heart to perceive, and eyes to see, and ears to hear, unto this day.

- Yet the Lord hath not given you an heart to perceive, and eyes to see, and ears to hear, unto this day. Moses is telling them: you do not truly understand what [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) means; you do not know how bad it is really going to get.

**[Deuteronomy 29:5-9](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v5)**

>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v6)</sup> Ye have not eaten bread, neither have ye drunk wine or strong drink: that ye might know that I am the Lord your God.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v7)</sup> And when ye came unto this place, Sihon the king of Heshbon, and Og the king of Bashan, came out against us unto battle, and we smote them:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v8)</sup> And we took their land, and gave it for an inheritance unto the Reubenites, and to the Gadites, and to the half tribe of Manasseh.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v9)</sup> Keep therefore the words of this covenant, and do them, that ye may prosper in all that ye do.

- The Most High led Israel forty years in the wilderness; their clothes waxed not old upon them, nor their shoes upon their feet, that they might know that he is the Lord their God.
- Sihon king of Heshbon and Og king of Bashan came out against Israel, and Israel smote them and gave their land for an inheritance to the Reubenites, the Gadites, and the half tribe of Manasseh.
- Keep therefore the words of this covenant and do them, that ye may prosper in all that ye do.

**[Deuteronomy 29:10-13](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v10)</sup> Ye stand this day all of you before the Lord your God; your captains of your tribes, your elders, and your officers, with all the men of Israel,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v11)</sup> Your little ones, your wives, and thy stranger that is in thy camp, from the hewer of thy wood unto the drawer of thy water:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v12)</sup> That thou shouldest enter into covenant with the Lord thy God, and into his oath, which the Lord thy God maketh with thee this day:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v13)</sup> That he may establish thee to day for a people unto himself, and that he may be unto thee a God, as he hath said unto thee, and as he hath sworn unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.

- All Israel stood that day before the Lord: captains, elders, officers, little ones, wives, and the stranger in the camp, from the hewer of wood unto the drawer of water. Even the servants among us heard this covenant.
- But though those strangers were among us, [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) did not apply to them. That is why no other nation on earth suffers the curses of the Most High the way we do; they live here in Babylon under the same judicial system, but they are not suffering from it like us.
- The purpose: that he may establish thee to day for a people unto himself, as he sware unto thy fathers, to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob. That is who we come from.

**[Deuteronomy 29:14-15](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v14)</sup> Neither with you only do I make this covenant and this oath;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v15)</sup> But with him that standeth here with us this day before the Lord our God, and also with him that is not here with us this day:

- Neither with you only do I make this covenant and this oath, but also with him that is not here with us this day. That is us: the Lord made this covenant with the generations which were to come.
- This is how you know the covenant and the curses apply to our forefathers and to us to this day. If we do not hearken to his voice and obey his commandments diligently, all the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) fall upon us; that is what is happening to us right now.

**[Deuteronomy 29:16-19](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v16)</sup> (For ye know how we have dwelt in the land of Egypt; and how we came through the nations which ye passed by;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v17)</sup> And ye have seen their abominations, and their idols, wood and stone, silver and gold, which were among them:)
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v18)</sup> Lest there should be among you man, or woman, or family, or tribe, whose heart turneth away this day from the Lord our God, to go and serve the gods of these nations; lest there should be among you a root that beareth gall and wormwood;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v19)</sup> And it come to pass, when he heareth the words of this curse, that he bless himself in his heart, saying, I shall have peace, though I walk in the imagination of mine heart, to add drunkenness to thirst:

- Ye have seen the abominations and idols of the nations, wood and stone, silver and gold; lest there should be among you man, woman, family, or tribe whose heart turneth away from the Lord to serve the gods of these nations.
- Lest there should be among you a root that beareth gall and wormwood: destruction.
- Verse 19 is Christianity 101: when he heareth the words of this curse, he blesseth himself in his heart, saying, I shall have peace, though I walk in the imagination of mine heart. He hears the whole of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), the ships, the children given away, the no rest and no ease, and says in his mind, I am an American, I live under the blessings of God, while he catches the bus, pays rent, and drinks dirty water in Flint.
- To add drunkenness to thirst: the Most High says you are drunk with that dumb understanding, lying to yourself, and thirstier for it.

**[Deuteronomy 29:20-21](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v20)</sup> The Lord will not spare him, but then the anger of the Lord and his jealousy shall smoke against that man, and all the curses that are written in this book shall lie upon him, and the Lord shall blot out his name from under heaven.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v21)</sup> And the Lord shall separate him unto evil out of all the tribes of Israel, according to all the curses of the covenant that are written in this book of the law:

- The Lord will not spare him; the anger of the Lord and his jealousy shall smoke against that man, and all the curses written in this book shall lie upon him, and the Lord shall blot out his name from under heaven: death.
- Once you hear [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), you cannot go back to living how you used to live. You heard the curses, even ten minutes of it on the street; you are marked, and if you go back, the Most High says, I am coming for you.

**[Deuteronomy 29:22-28](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v22)</sup> So that the generation to come of your children that shall rise up after you, and the stranger that shall come from a far land, shall say, when they see the plagues of that land, and the sicknesses which the Lord hath laid upon it;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v23)</sup> And that the whole land thereof is brimstone, and salt, and burning, that it is not sown, nor beareth, nor any grass groweth therein, like the overthrow of Sodom, and Gomorrah, Admah, and Zeboim, which the Lord overthrew in his anger, and in his wrath:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v24)</sup> Even all nations shall say, Wherefore hath the Lord done thus unto this land? what meaneth the heat of this great anger?
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v25)</sup> Then men shall say, Because they have forsaken the covenant of the Lord God of their fathers, which he made with them when he brought them forth out of the land of Egypt:

>
> <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v27)</sup> And the anger of the Lord was kindled against this land, to bring upon it all the curses that are written in this book:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/29#v28)</sup> And the Lord rooted them out of their land in anger, and in wrath, and in great indignation, and cast them into another land, as it is this day.

- The generation to come and the stranger from a far land shall see the plagues of the land, the whole land brimstone, salt, and burning, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah, Admah and Zeboim.
- All nations shall say, Wherefore hath the Lord done thus unto this land? And men shall answer, Because they have forsaken the covenant of the Lord God of their fathers.
- That is what they say when they look at our people: the ones who know we are the real Israelites say, they forsook God and now they are being punished; but we are not going to tell them who they are, because we are living good while they suffer.
- The Lord rooted them out of their land in anger and cast them into another land, as it is this day. Welcome to Babylon the great.

- But those things which are revealed belong unto us and to our children for ever, that we may do all the words of this law. The revealed things, the milk, are the laws that are easy to be understood, and they belong to us; focus on those.

# Deuteronomy 30: Call the Curses to Mind and Choose Life

Read the chapter → [Deuteronomy 30](/bible/deuteronomy/30)

**[Deuteronomy 30:1-2](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when all these things are come upon thee, the blessing and the curse, which I have set before thee, and thou shalt call them to mind among all the nations, whither the Lord thy God hath driven thee,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v2)</sup> And shalt return unto the Lord thy God, and shalt obey his voice according to all that I command thee this day, thou and thy children, with all thine heart, and with all thy soul;

- With all that being said, here is the goodness of the Most High: he said, I am going to punish you, but he gave us the solution to get up out of the curses.
- When all these things are come upon thee, the blessing and the curse, thou shalt call them to mind among all the nations whither the Lord thy God hath driven thee. Baruch tells you what calling them to mind is: in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves.
- We are remembering ourselves now: we know we got here on ships, we know we did not speak the white man's language when he came to the west coast of Africa, we know we rise when the alarm clock goes off and work for food, water, and drink, and we know Christianity is a slave religion forced on our ancestors. That is calling the curses to mind.
- And shalt return unto the Lord thy God, and shalt obey his voice according to all that I command thee, thou and thy children, with all thine heart and with all thy soul. The stipulation of returning is keeping the commandments; you cannot just say I accept the Lord in my heart. That does not work. And your children must be in order under your household too; they cannot fake it either.

  Precepts:
  - **[Baruch 2:30](/bible/baruch/2#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/baruch/2#v30)</sup> For I knew that they would not hear me, because it is a stiffnecked people: but in the land of their captivities they shall remember themselves.

    Calling the blessing and the curse to mind is Israel remembering themselves in the land of their captivities.

**[Deuteronomy 30:3-5](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v3)</sup> That then the Lord thy God will turn thy captivity, and have compassion upon thee, and will return and gather thee from all the nations, whither the Lord thy God hath scattered thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v4)</sup> If any of thine be driven out unto the outmost parts of heaven, from thence will the Lord thy God gather thee, and from thence will he fetch thee:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v5)</sup> And the Lord thy God will bring thee into the land which thy fathers possessed, and thou shalt possess it; and he will do thee good, and multiply thee above thy fathers.

- Then the Lord thy God will turn thy captivity and have compassion upon thee, and will return and gather thee from all the nations whither the Lord thy God hath scattered thee.
- It did not say the League of Nations, NATO, or an airline jet will gather us. The Most High is sending Christ to come and get us and take us back to the land, the same way he delivered us out of Egypt himself, with the angel leading, and no Amorite, Greek, or Roman helping.
- There is nothing new under the sun; that is how we know the people over in the land now are not the people of the book, because Israel returns by the hand of the Most High, not by planes.
- If any of thine be driven out unto the outmost parts of heaven, from thence will he fetch thee: no matter where we are in the four corners of the earth, if we repent and endure to the end, Christ said, I am coming to get you.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 24:31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)</sup> And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.

    He shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds; Christ gathers Israel, nobody else.

**[Deuteronomy 30:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v6)</sup> And the Lord thy God will circumcise thine heart, and the heart of thy seed, to love the Lord thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul, that thou mayest live.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v7)</sup> And the Lord thy God will put all these curses upon thine enemies, and on them that hate thee, which persecuted thee.

- The Lord thy God will circumcise thine heart and the heart of thy seed, to love the Lord thy God with all thine heart and with all thy soul, that thou mayest live.
- And the Lord thy God will put all these curses upon thine enemies and on them that hate thee, which persecuted thee. When the number of the 144,000 is fulfilled, every curse we just read in [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) goes on every other nation on the face of the planet, double for what we received. And what we got was terrible; endure to those days.

**[Deuteronomy 30:8-10](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v8)</sup> And thou shalt return and obey the voice of the Lord, and do all his commandments which I command thee this day.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v9)</sup> And the Lord thy God will make thee plenteous in every work of thine hand, in the fruit of thy body, and in the fruit of thy cattle, and in the fruit of thy land, for good: for the Lord will again rejoice over thee for good, as he rejoiced over thy fathers:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v10)</sup> If thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to keep his commandments and his statutes which are written in this book of the law, and if thou turn unto the Lord thy God with all thine heart, and with all thy soul.

- Thou shalt return and obey the voice of the Lord, and do all his commandments; and the Lord will again rejoice over thee for good, as he rejoiced over thy fathers, making thee plenteous in every work of thine hand.
- If thou hearken unto his voice, to keep his commandments and his statutes which are written in this book of the law: before it was called the Holy Bible, it was called the book of the law. That is what the Most High governs by: law.

**[Deuteronomy 30:11-14](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v11)</sup> For this commandment which I command thee this day, it is not hidden from thee, neither is it far off.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v12)</sup> It is not in heaven, that thou shouldest say, Who shall go up for us to heaven, and bring it unto us, that we may hear it, and do it?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v13)</sup> Neither is it beyond the sea, that thou shouldest say, Who shall go over the sea for us, and bring it unto us, that we may hear it, and do it?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v14)</sup> But the word is very nigh unto thee, in thy mouth, and in thy heart, that thou mayest do it.

- This commandment is not hidden from thee, neither is it far off; it is not in heaven and not beyond the sea, that thou shouldest say, Who shall fetch it for us?
- The word is very nigh unto thee, in thy mouth and in thy heart, that thou mayest do it. Look how close the word is now: the Most High put it on the spirit of even our oppressor to make Bible apps, so the judgments, the curses, the blessings, and what he requires sit in everybody's pocket.
- There are no excuses on the day of judgment. None.

**[Deuteronomy 30:15-16](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v15)</sup> See, I have set before thee this day life and good, and death and evil;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v16)</sup> In that I command thee this day to love the Lord thy God, to walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments and his statutes and his judgments, that thou mayest live and multiply: and the Lord thy God shall bless thee in the land whither thou goest to possess it.

- See, I have set before thee this day life and good, and death and evil.
- How do you love the Most High? To walk in his ways, and to keep his commandments and his statutes and his judgments, that thou mayest live and multiply. That did not change from Old Testament to New: the only way we love God is by keeping his commandments.

**[Deuteronomy 30:17-18](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v17)</sup> But if thine heart turn away, so that thou wilt not hear, but shalt be drawn away, and worship other gods, and serve them;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v18)</sup> I denounce unto you this day, that ye shall surely perish, and that ye shall not prolong your days upon the land, whither thou passest over Jordan to go to possess it.

- But if thine heart turn away, and thou be drawn away to worship other gods and serve them: you want Christianity and whatever denomination feels good to you, or Islam with four and five wives worshipping a stone, or Buddhism, or Scientology?
- I denounce unto you this day, that ye shall surely perish, and that ye shall not prolong your days upon the land, whither thou passest over Jordan to possess it.

**[Deuteronomy 30:19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/30#v19)</sup> I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live:

- Highlight verse 19 if you do not have it highlighted: I call heaven and earth to record this day against you, that I have set before you life and death, blessing and cursing: therefore choose life, that both thou and thy seed may live.
- He already told you what is up. The life he is telling you to choose is in Proverbs: keep my commandments and live. The commandments give you life; they are your spiritual force field out on the streets in the land of wickedness.

    Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye; that is the life the Most High tells you to choose.
