**[Deuteronomy 16:1-8](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v1)</sup> Observe the month of Abib, and keep the passover unto the Lord thy God: for in the month of Abib the Lord thy God brought thee forth out of Egypt by night.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v2)</sup> Thou shalt therefore sacrifice the passover unto the Lord thy God, of the flock and the herd, in the place which the Lord shall choose to place his name there.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v3)</sup> Thou shalt eat no leavened bread with it; seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread therewith, even the bread of affliction; for thou camest forth out of the land of Egypt in haste: that thou mayest remember the day when thou camest forth out of the land of Egypt all the days of thy life.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v4)</sup> And there shall be no leavened bread seen with thee in all thy coast seven days; neither shall there any thing of the flesh, which thou sacrificedst the first day at even, remain all night until the morning.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v5)</sup> Thou mayest not sacrifice the passover within any of thy gates, which the Lord thy God giveth thee:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v6)</sup> But at the place which the Lord thy God shall choose to place his name in, there thou shalt sacrifice the passover at even, at the going down of the sun, at the season that thou camest forth out of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v7)</sup> And thou shalt roast and eat it in the place which the Lord thy God shall choose: and thou shalt turn in the morning, and go unto thy tents.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v8)</sup> Six days thou shalt eat unleavened bread: and on the seventh day shall be a solemn assembly to the Lord thy God: thou shalt do no work therein.

- Observe the month of Abib and keep the passover unto the Most High, for in the month of Abib the Lord thy God brought thee forth out of Egypt by night.
- Thou shalt eat no leavened bread with it; seven days shalt thou eat unleavened bread, even the bread of affliction, because thou camest forth out of the land of Egypt in haste, that thou mayest remember the day all the days of thy life.
- Nothing of the flesh sacrificed the first day at even, the lamb, may remain all night until the morning. The passover may not be sacrificed within any of thy gates, but only at the place which the Lord thy God shall choose to place his name there, at even, at the going down of the sun.
- Six days thou shalt eat unleavened bread, and on the seventh day is a solemn assembly and no work: the first day of the passover is a sabbath, and the seventh day of the passover is a sabbath.

**[Deuteronomy 16:9-12](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v9)</sup> Seven weeks shalt thou number unto thee: begin to number the seven weeks from such time as thou beginnest to put the sickle to the corn.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v10)</sup> And thou shalt keep the feast of weeks unto the Lord thy God with a tribute of a freewill offering of thine hand, which thou shalt give unto the Lord thy God, according as the Lord thy God hath blessed thee:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v11)</sup> And thou shalt rejoice before the Lord thy God, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy manservant, and thy maidservant, and the Levite that is within thy gates, and the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, that are among you, in the place which the Lord thy God hath chosen to place his name there.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v12)</sup> And thou shalt remember that thou wast a bondman in Egypt: and thou shalt observe and do these statutes.

- Seven weeks shalt thou number unto thee: begin to number the seven weeks from such time as thou beginnest to put the sickle to the corn, and keep the feast of weeks unto the Most High with a tribute of a freewill offering according as the Lord thy God hath blessed thee.
- Another name for the feast of weeks is the feast of firstfruits: you count seven weeks and then a day, fifty days, which is Pentecost.
- Rejoice before the Most High, thou and thy son and thy daughter, thy manservant and thy maidservant, the Levite, the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow; and remember that thou wast a bondman in Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 23:15](/bible/leviticus/23#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/23#v15)</sup> And ye shall count unto you from the morrow after the sabbath, from the day that ye brought the sheaf of the wave offering; seven sabbaths shall be complete:

    Count from the morrow after the sabbath, from the day the wave sheaf is brought; seven sabbaths shall be complete; the counting law behind the feast of weeks.

**[Deuteronomy 16:13-15](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v13)</sup> Thou shalt observe the feast of tabernacles seven days, after that thou hast gathered in thy corn and thy wine:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v14)</sup> And thou shalt rejoice in thy feast, thou, and thy son, and thy daughter, and thy manservant, and thy maidservant, and the Levite, the stranger, and the fatherless, and the widow, that are within thy gates.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v15)</sup> Seven days shalt thou keep a solemn feast unto the Lord thy God in the place which the Lord shall choose: because the Lord thy God shall bless thee in all thine increase, and in all the works of thine hands, therefore thou shalt surely rejoice.

- Thou shalt observe the feast of tabernacles seven days, after that thou hast gathered in thy corn and thy wine.
- Rejoice in thy feast, thou and thy son and thy daughter, thy manservant and thy maidservant, the Levite, the stranger, the fatherless, and the widow within thy gates.
- Seven days shalt thou keep a solemn feast in the place which the Lord shall choose, because the Most High shall bless thee in all thine increase and in all the works of thine hands; therefore thou shalt surely rejoice.

**[Deuteronomy 16:16-17](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v16)</sup> Three times in a year shall all thy males appear before the Lord thy God in the place which he shall choose; in the feast of unleavened bread, and in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tabernacles: and they shall not appear before the Lord empty:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v17)</sup> Every man shall give as he is able, according to the blessing of the Lord thy God which he hath given thee.

- Three times in a year shall all thy males appear before the Most High in the place which he shall choose: in the feast of unleavened bread, in the feast of weeks, and in the feast of tabernacles.
- They shall not appear before the Lord empty, meaning they have to come with their sacrifices: every man shall give as he is able, according to the blessing of the Lord thy God which he hath given thee.

**[Deuteronomy 16:18-20](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v18)</sup> Judges and officers shalt thou make thee in all thy gates, which the Lord thy God giveth thee, throughout thy tribes: and they shall judge the people with just judgment.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not wrest judgment; thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift: for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise, and pervert the words of the righteous.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v20)</sup> That which is altogether just shalt thou follow, that thou mayest live, and inherit the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

- Judges and officers shalt thou make thee in all thy gates, throughout thy tribes, and they shall judge the people with just judgment.
- There is nothing new under the sun: when you see judges and officers among us today, know that we are following the things that are written within this book.
- Thou shalt not wrest judgment, thou shalt not respect persons, neither take a gift, for a gift doth blind the eyes of the wise and pervert the words of the righteous. That which is altogether just shalt thou follow, that thou mayest live and inherit the land.

**[Deuteronomy 16:21-22](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v21)</sup> Thou shalt not plant thee a grove of any trees near unto the altar of the Lord thy God, which thou shalt make thee.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v22)</sup> Neither shalt thou set thee up any image; which the Lord thy God hateth.

- Thou shalt not plant thee a grove of any trees near unto the altar of the Lord thy God; yet these pastors set up Christmas trees inside the church, directly against this law.
- Neither shalt thou set thee up any image, which the Lord thy God hateth.
