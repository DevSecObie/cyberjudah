## Introduction

This session covers [Deuteronomy 23](/bible/deuteronomy/23) through 26. Many of these laws should sound familiar, because we have already been through Exodus, Leviticus, and Numbers, and some of these laws are simply being repeated in Deuteronomy. Chapter 23 sets who may enter the congregation of the Lord and requires the camp to be holy because the Most High walks in the midst of it; chapter 24 covers the bill of divorcement, pledges, and mercy toward the poor; chapter 25 covers just judgment, the duty toward a dead brother's wife, and the memorial against Amalek; chapter 26 covers the firstfruits confession and the Most High avouching Israel to be his peculiar people.

**[Deuteronomy 23:1](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v1)</sup> He that is wounded in the stones, or hath his privy member cut off, shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord.

- He that is wounded in the stones or hath his privy member cut off shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord.
- This law repeats what was already given: the priests' law in Leviticus lists the same blemishes that barred a man from approaching to offer the bread of his God, including him that hath his stones broken.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 21:17-20](/bible/leviticus/21#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/21#v17)</sup> Speak unto Aaron, saying, Whosoever he be of thy seed in their generations that hath any blemish, let him not approach to offer the bread of his God.
>

    Whosoever of Aaron's seed hath any blemish, including him that hath his stones broken, shall not approach to offer the bread of his God.

**[Deuteronomy 23:2](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v2)</sup> A bastard shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord; even to his tenth generation shall he not enter into the congregation of the Lord.

- A bastard shall not enter into the congregation of the Lord, even to his tenth generation: the children begotten of forbidden unions with the nations were shut out of the congregation.
- The prophets tie the bastard to Ashdod: a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod. Ashdod was one of the cities given to the tribe of Judah in the inheritance, but Israel did not drive out all the inhabitants and lost it.
