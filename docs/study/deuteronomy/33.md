**[Deuteronomy 33:1-5](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v1)</sup> And this is the blessing, wherewith Moses the man of God blessed the children of Israel before his death.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v2)</sup> And he said, The Lord came from Sinai, and rose up from Seir unto them; he shined forth from mount Paran, and he came with ten thousands of saints: from his right hand went a fiery law for them.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v3)</sup> Yea, he loved the people; all his saints are in thy hand: and they sat down at thy feet; every one shall receive of thy words.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v4)</sup> Moses commanded us a law, even the inheritance of the congregation of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v5)</sup> And he was king in Jeshurun, when the heads of the people and the tribes of Israel were gathered together.

- This is the blessing wherewith Moses the man of God blessed the children of Israel before his death. This chapter is prophetic concerning who the tribes would be in the last days: the blessings point to where you can examine history and identify each tribe.
- The Lord came from Sinai and shined forth from mount Paran, and came with ten thousands of saints; from his right hand went a fiery law for them.
- Moses commanded us a law, even the inheritance of the congregation of Jacob: the laws of the Most High are our inheritance. And he was king in Jeshurun, when the heads of the people and the tribes of Israel were gathered together.

**[Deuteronomy 33:6](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v6)</sup> Let Reuben live, and not die; and let not his men be few.

- Let Reuben live, and not die; and let not his men be few. Reuben is the Seminole Indians: under Andrew Jackson the Indian removal campaign set out to exterminate them, and many were slaughtered, but they were not able to kill them all.
- They tried it, but they were not fully successful; that is why the blessing says let Reuben live, and not die.

**[Deuteronomy 33:7](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v7)</sup> And this is the blessing of Judah: and he said, Hear, Lord, the voice of Judah, and bring him unto his people: let his hands be sufficient for him; and be thou an help to him from his enemies.

- Hear, Lord, the voice of Judah, and bring him unto his people: let his hands be sufficient for him; and be thou an help to him from his enemies.
- The Most High said we would be scattered into the four corners and the nations would conspire that we not remember who we are; but he wakes Judah first. Judah, the so-called blacks in America, are the first to remember that they are the Israelites, and once Judah learns who they are, they go and teach the other tribes scattered throughout the four corners; that is bring him unto his people.
- That is why Judah is at the forefront of this work. The gates of hell will try to prevail against the quest to wake up the twelve tribes, but the Most High is a help to Judah from his enemies to fulfill that mission.

**[Deuteronomy 33:8-9](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v8)</sup> And of Levi he said, Let thy Thummim and thy Urim be with thy holy one, whom thou didst prove at Massah, and with whom thou didst strive at the waters of Meribah;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v9)</sup> Who said unto his father and to his mother, I have not seen him; neither did he acknowledge his brethren, nor knew his own children: for they have observed thy word, and kept thy covenant.

- And of Levi he said, Let thy Thummim and thy Urim be with thy holy one. The Urim and the Thummim were worn in the garment of the high priest, as [Exodus 28](/bible/exodus/28) lays out; that pertains to the Levites, the lineage of Aaron, whom thou didst prove at Massah, and with whom thou didst strive at the waters of Meribah, in [Exodus 17](/bible/exodus/17).
- Who said unto his father and to his mother, I have not seen him; neither did he acknowledge his brethren, nor knew his own children: for they have observed thy word, and kept thy covenant. This is the zeal Levi had for the Most High.
- At the golden calf, when Moses stood in the gate of the camp and said, Who is on the Lord's side? let him come unto me, only the sons of Levi gathered themselves unto him. They took every man his sword and did according to the word of Moses without hesitating, and there fell of the people that day about three thousand men. That is why the blessing says they saw not father or mother nor acknowledged their brethren: that is the devotion they had for the covenant.

**[Deuteronomy 33:10](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v10)</sup> They shall teach Jacob thy judgments, and Israel thy law: they shall put incense before thee, and whole burnt sacrifice upon thine altar.

- They shall teach Jacob thy judgments, and Israel thy law: they shall put incense before thee, and whole burnt sacrifice upon thine altar. This is the office the Most High gave Levi, and in particular the high priest.
- Aaron was chosen out of all men living to offer sacrifices, incense, and a sweet savour, to make reconciliation for the people, and that office pertained to his lineage: to teach Jacob the testimonies and inform Israel in his laws.
- That is why Levi had no land of their own but dwelt among all the tribes of Israel: they were responsible for making the sacrifices and teaching the laws to the people.

**[Deuteronomy 33:11](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v11)</sup> Bless, Lord, his substance, and accept the work of his hands: smite through the loins of them that rise against him, and of them that hate him, that they rise not again.

- Bless, Lord, his substance, and accept the work of his hands. His substance goes into the tithes that Levi would receive, and the work of his hands goes into the freewill offerings brought forth to the Lord.
- Smite through the loins of them that rise against him, and of them that hate him, that they rise not again. In history you see it in the Haitian revolution under Toussaint Louverture: they smote through the loins of them that hated them. Levi was about that action to a magnitude the other tribes never matched.
- That they rise not again is ultimately fulfilled when Christ returns.

**[Deuteronomy 33:12](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v12)</sup> And of Benjamin he said, The beloved of the Lord shall dwell in safety by him; and the Lord shall cover him all the day long, and he shall dwell between his shoulders.

- And of Benjamin he said, The beloved of the Lord shall dwell in safety by him. Dwelling in safety means Benjamin would not be killed off in slavery.
- The Lord shall cover him all the day long, and he shall dwell between his shoulders: between the shoulders of the Lord represents the skies and the earth, going into the good climate and the good land they would have.

**[Deuteronomy 33:13-16](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v13)</sup> And of Joseph he said, Blessed of the Lord be his land, for the precious things of heaven, for the dew, and for the deep that coucheth beneath,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v14)</sup> And for the precious fruits brought forth by the sun, and for the precious things put forth by the moon,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v15)</sup> And for the chief things of the ancient mountains, and for the precious things of the lasting hills,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v16)</sup> And for the precious things of the earth and fulness thereof, and for the good will of him that dwelt in the bush: let the blessing come upon the head of Joseph, and upon the top of the head of him that was separated from his brethren.

- And of Joseph he said, Blessed of the Lord be his land. Joseph is Ephraim and Manasseh: Joseph was one of the twelve sons of Jacob, but Jacob put his name on Joseph's two sons, so his grandsons make up two tribes of Israel; when we read of Joseph we are reading of Ephraim and Manasseh.
- The precious things of heaven refer to the good weather on their land, and the dew and the deep that coucheth beneath go into the seas whose benefits they reap.
- The precious fruits brought forth by the sun are the crops their land grows: mango, papaya, avocado, pineapple, coconut, banana, sugar cane, fruits you cannot grow in north Florida. The precious things put forth by the moon go into the coqui, a frog species that exists only in Puerto Rico and comes out at night with its distinct call.
- The chief things of the ancient mountains are the diamonds, gold, and silver in their land, and the precious things of the lasting hills include the coffee beans that come out of their hills. Let the blessing come upon the head of Joseph, him that was separated from his brethren.
- Ephraim said, Yet I am become rich: hence the very name Puerto Rico, the port of riches. But like we read earlier, they waxed fat and became prideful as well.

**[Deuteronomy 33:17](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v17)</sup> His glory is like the firstling of his bullock, and his horns are like the horns of unicorns: with them he shall push the people together to the ends of the earth: and they are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and they are the thousands of Manasseh.

- His glory is like the firstling of his bullock: as in the law that sanctified the firstborn unto the Most High, that is the glory of the birthright given to Joseph.
- His horns are like the horns of unicorns: two horns, Ephraim and Manasseh. With them he shall push the people together to the ends of the earth: the Most High used Ephraim and Manasseh as the heads to push the ten tribes over to this side of the world, as the account of the tribes taking counsel to go forth into a further country shows.
- And they are the ten thousands of Ephraim, and they are the thousands of Manasseh: the blessing on Jacob's grandsons was to grow into a multitude in the midst of the earth. The Most High gets geographic with it: they went from the east to the west and multiplied in the midst of the hemisphere, in Central America.

**[Deuteronomy 33:18-19](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v18)</sup> And of Zebulun he said, Rejoice, Zebulun, in thy going out; and, Issachar, in thy tents.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v19)</sup> They shall call the people unto the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness: for they shall suck of the abundance of the seas, and of treasures hid in the sand.

- And of Zebulun he said, Rejoice, Zebulun, in thy going out; and, Issachar, in thy tents. Notice the grouping: Zebulun and Issachar are blessed together because they would dwell very close, with linked cities at their borders.
- They shall call the people unto the mountain; there they shall offer sacrifices of righteousness. In the promised land they linked up to make sacrifices of righteousness; but when they linked up on this side of the world as the Aztecs and the Mayans, they erected pyramids and eventually went off and sacrificed to other gods, even human sacrifice, as Hosea testifies of their sacrificing.
- For they shall suck of the abundance of the seas, and of treasures hid in the sand: the gold and silver discovered in their land, which is why the conquistadors were so glad to stumble upon it.

  Precepts:

**[Deuteronomy 33:20-21](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v20)</sup> And of Gad he said, Blessed be he that enlargeth Gad: he dwelleth as a lion, and teareth the arm with the crown of the head.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v21)</sup> And he provided the first part for himself, because there, in a portion of the lawgiver, was he seated; and he came with the heads of the people, he executed the justice of the Lord, and his judgments with Israel.

- And of Gad he said, Blessed be he that enlargeth Gad. Gad alone holds over three hundred Indian tribes within itself; it is a very large tribe.
- He dwelleth as a lion, and teareth the arm with the crown of the head: Gad was a warlike, predator people, and the tearing of the arm goes into the blood brother pact they were known for making, the chief with the crown of feathers having a man tear his arm to be accepted into the tribe.
- And he provided the first part for himself: that is North America, the mainland, the largest and best portion. Because there, in a portion of the lawgiver, was he seated: the tribe of Gad served as the lawgivers for the northern kingdom.
- And he came with the heads of the people: the travel to this side of the earth was made with Ephraim and Manasseh, the horns, at the head, dealing with the so-called Hispanics and Indians together. He executed the justice of the Lord, and his judgments with Israel, before they went off.

**[Deuteronomy 33:22](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v22)</sup> And of Dan he said, Dan is a lion’s whelp: he shall leap from Bashan.

- And of Dan he said, Dan is a lion's whelp: he shall leap from Bashan. Dan is a warlike people; the Carib Indians did not play.
- He shall leap from Bashan says a lot in a short verse: Bashan was in the most northern part of Israel, so leaping from Bashan lands Dan in the middle of all Israel. That is why Dan today is not their own tribe but is absorbed into all the other tribes, primarily Benjamin, dwelling in the Caribbean.
- Amos says they shall fall, and never rise up again: Dan fell and never rose again as a tribe, which is why Dan is not mentioned among the tribes of the 144,000 in [Revelation 7](/bible/revelation/7). And as [Genesis 49](/bible/genesis/49) says, Dan is an adder in the path: look how the islands are shaped geographically, like a serpent.

**[Deuteronomy 33:23](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v23)</sup> And of Naphtali he said, O Naphtali, satisfied with favour, and full with the blessing of the Lord: possess thou the west and the south.

- And of Naphtali he said, O Naphtali, satisfied with favour: Naphtali is Argentina and Chile today. Satisfied with favour goes into the grapes they are able to produce in their land for wine, and coffee as well.
- And full with the blessing of the Lord: to this day their land is rich with agriculture and cattle.
- Possess thou the west and the south: the Most High gets geographic again. Look at a map of the world: what land sits in the southwestern region of the earth? Argentina and Chile. Stop playing with the prophets; we know who we are.

**[Deuteronomy 33:24-25](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v24)</sup> And of Asher he said, Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v25)</sup> Thy shoes shall be iron and brass; and as thy days, so shall thy strength be.

- And of Asher he said, Let Asher be blessed with children. Asher is also a large tribe: the countries that make up Asher today include Brazil, Peru, Colombia, and Uruguay, and Brazil has the largest population of children in the world.
- Let him be acceptable to his brethren: for a period of time they became so wild in their ways that they were not acceptable in the sight of their brethren.
- And let him dip his foot in oil: one of the largest outputs of crude oil resources is Colombia.
- Thy shoes shall be iron and brass: the Hebrew word for iron is barzel; say it fast and you hear Brazil. It goes into the mineral resources in that land, iron, copper, zinc. And as thy days, so shall thy strength be.

**[Deuteronomy 33:26-29](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v26)</sup> There is none like unto the God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven in thy help, and in his excellency on the sky.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v27)</sup> The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: and he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee; and shall say, Destroy them.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v28)</sup> Israel then shall dwell in safety alone: the fountain of Jacob shall be upon a land of corn and wine; also his heavens shall drop down dew.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v29)</sup> Happy art thou, O Israel: who is like unto thee, O people saved by the Lord, the shield of thy help, and who is the sword of thy excellency! and thine enemies shall be found liars unto thee; and thou shalt tread upon their high places.

- There is none like unto the God of Jeshurun, who rideth upon the heaven in thy help. The eternal God is thy refuge, and underneath are the everlasting arms: he shall thrust out the enemy from before thee.
- Notice how the song and blessing have evolved: from Israel provoking the Most High and being punished, the teeth of beasts sent upon us, now it goes into our salvation, how the Most High will be our refuge. Israel then shall dwell in safety alone.
- Happy art thou, O Israel: who is like unto thee, O people saved by the Lord! And thine enemies shall be found liars unto thee: after all these years of the crafty counsel, being told you are anything but Israel and that everybody else holds your heritage, the Most High said your enemies shall be found liars, and thou shalt tread upon their high places.
- Never trust your enemy: your enemy will do everything in his power to keep you oppressed and subject to him, including lying to you about your history. As in the days of the Maccabees, the leaders show the people the falsehood of the heathen and the breach of their oaths, the covenants and treaties they broke with our people; after so long you learn that your enemies have been liars this whole time.
