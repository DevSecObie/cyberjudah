**[Deuteronomy 4:1](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v1)</sup> Now therefore hearken, O Israel, unto the statutes and unto the judgments, which I teach you, for to do them, that ye may live, and go in and possess the land which the Lord God of your fathers giveth you.

- Hearken, O Israel: hearken means listen, pay attention, and there is a difference between hearing and listening.
- The statutes and judgments are taught for to do them; knowing you are an Israelite is only half the battle. The main thing is applying the scriptures, doing what the Most High is telling you to do, that ye may live.
- The promise is to go in and possess the land, and possessing land is true wealth: your milk and honey, your cattle, your agriculture, where you cultivate and take care of your family. It is not the diamonds, the cars, the sneakers, or the newest phone; a big house with no land is not wealth, and that is why the heathen boxes our people into apartments.

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v2)</sup> Ye shall not add unto the word which I command you, neither shall ye diminish ought from it, that ye may keep the commandments of the Lord your God which I command you.

This was spoken around 1500 BC, and on the last page of the Bible the same charge closes the book: the prophets across millenniums and captivities all speak the same thing, Moses, Isaiah, Jeremiah, Christ, and John, and that consistency proves the Bible is a true book, because twenty people cannot keep one story straight for five minutes.
- The watered down versions such as the NIV and ESV show what taking away looks like: many of them remove [Matthew 18:11](/bible/matthew/18#v11) and other scriptures, and they have taken out the Apocrypha.

- Which shall hear all these statutes and say, surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people: they may not say it out loud today, but when they see Israel united in Christ and in order, that is what rises in their minds.

- Egypt is where feminism was already incorporated: Hathor the cattle goddess and Qetesh the goddess of beauty were among the idols learned there, so the Most High had to say plainly, no deity male or female. There is no goddess in the scriptures.

- When thou art in tribulation, and all these things are come upon thee, even in the latter days: the latter days began when Christ came, for God who spake in time past by the prophets hath in these last days spoken by his Son.
- And shalt be obedient unto his voice: the voice of the Most High tells you to keep his commandments. If someone claims God spoke to him in a dream and told him everything but obedience, that is not the voice of the Most High.
- For the Lord thy God is a merciful God; he will not forsake thee, nor destroy thee, nor forget the covenant of thy fathers.

**[Deuteronomy 4:32-35](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v32)</sup> For ask now of the days that are past, which were before thee, since the day that God created man upon the earth, and ask from the one side of heaven unto the other, whether there hath been any such thing as this great thing is, or hath been heard like it?
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v33)</sup> Did ever people hear the voice of God speaking out of the midst of the fire, as thou hast heard, and live?
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v34)</sup> Or hath God assayed to go and take him a nation from the midst of another nation, by temptations, by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a mighty hand, and by a stretched out arm, and by great terrors, according to all that the Lord your God did for you in Egypt before your eyes?
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v35)</sup> Unto thee it was shewed, that thou mightest know that the Lord he is God; there is none else beside him.

- Ask now of the days that are past, since the day that God created man upon the earth: you are supposed to search out your forefathers and look up your history, and not slavery only, but the reign under David and Solomon and the empires where Israel ruled, and slavery too, because failing to keep the laws is why the people went into it.
- Did ever people hear the voice of God speaking out of the midst of the fire and live? Hath God assayed to take him a nation from the midst of another nation, by temptations, by signs, and by wonders, and by war, and by a mighty hand? There is no such history anywhere else; the Most High did this for Israel alone.
- Unto thee it was shewed, that thou mightest know that the Lord he is God; there is none else beside him.

**[Deuteronomy 4:36-39](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v36)</sup> Out of heaven he made thee to hear his voice, that he might instruct thee: and upon earth he shewed thee his great fire; and thou heardest his words out of the midst of the fire.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v37)</sup> And because he loved thy fathers, therefore he chose their seed after them, and brought thee out in his sight with his mighty power out of Egypt;
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v38)</sup> To drive out nations from before thee greater and mightier than thou art, to bring thee in, to give thee their land for an inheritance, as it is this day.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v39)</sup> Know therefore this day, and consider it in thine heart, that the Lord he is God in heaven above, and upon the earth beneath: there is none else.

- Out of heaven he made thee to hear his voice, that he might instruct thee; and because he loved thy fathers, therefore he chose their seed after them, and brought thee out of Egypt in his sight with his mighty power.
- To drive out nations from before thee greater and mightier than thou art: they had more economic might and more military power, but the Most High delivered Israel out of their hands, and to this day the twelve tribes are still here.
- Know therefore this day, and consider it in thine heart, that the Lord he is God in heaven above and upon the earth beneath; there is none else. Consider is the key word: really appreciate all of it, for Israel could have been born of any nation, but the Most High ordained this people, and the way to cash in is simply keeping the commandments.

**[Deuteronomy 4:40](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v40)**

> <sup>[40](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v40)</sup> Thou shalt keep therefore his statutes, and his commandments, which I command thee this day, that it may go well with thee, and with thy children after thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days upon the earth, which the Lord thy God giveth thee, for ever.
