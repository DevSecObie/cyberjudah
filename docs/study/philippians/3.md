## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Philippians 3 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 2-3, 10-21, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Philippians 3:2](/bible/philippians/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/philippians/3#v2)</sup> Beware of dogs, beware of evil workers, beware of the concision.

- “Beware of dogs, beware of evil workers, beware of the concision.” The concision means the precise cut: those who are supposed to sit in Moses’ seat, the doctors of the law, those who are supposed to know something. Beware of them, because they are dogs.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 12:38-40](/bible/mark/12#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/mark/12#v38)</sup> And he said unto them in his doctrine, Beware of the scribes, which love to go in long clothing, and love salutations in the marketplaces,
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/mark/12#v39)</sup> And the chief seats in the synagogues, and the uppermost rooms at feasts:
    >
    > <sup>[40](/bible/mark/12#v40)</sup> Which devour widows’ houses, and for a pretence make long prayers: these shall receive greater damnation.

    “And he said unto them in his doctrine, Beware of the scribes, which love to go in long clothing, and love salutations in the marketplaces”. Paul said beware of dogs; Christ tells you who to beware of: the scribes and Pharisees. All we can do is warn you. That was around the same time we began putting Mark Riser on the screen and shaming him, the devil he is, and the man who left did not beware: as soon as he left he went right to Mark Riser. Whether brothers and sisters take heed is up to them.

**[Philippians 3:3](/bible/philippians/3#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/3#v3)</sup> For we are the circumcision, which worship God in the spirit, and rejoice in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh.

- “For we are the circumcision, which worship God in the spirit, and rejoice in Christ Jesus, and have no confidence in the flesh.” We are the worshippers of Christ, but others try to diminish the fullness of Christ, make a mockery of it and lie on it, and then strain at a gnat. In that sit-down they talked about the virgin birth and the Trinity, while our people are being killed: a brother goes to a music festival and ends up hung. You don’t see any of that, but you want to talk about the Trinity. You stupid. Have no confidence in the flesh whatsoever.

**[Philippians 3:10-11](/bible/philippians/3#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/philippians/3#v10)</sup> That I may know him, and the power of his resurrection, and the fellowship of his sufferings, being made conformable unto his death;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/philippians/3#v11)</sup> If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead.

- “If by any means I might attain unto the resurrection of the dead”: by any means, that I might get the kingdom.

**[Philippians 3:12](/bible/philippians/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/philippians/3#v12)</sup> Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect: but I follow after, if that I may apprehend that for which also I am apprehended of Christ Jesus.

- “Not as though I had already attained, either were already perfect”. Paul could not say he had made it. Some say, I’m the elect, I’m the 144,000. You don’t know that. Paul said he did not know. “If that I may apprehend”: that I may obtain the kingdom.

**[Philippians 3:13-14](/bible/philippians/3#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/philippians/3#v13)</sup> Brethren, I count not myself to have apprehended: but this one thing I do, forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/philippians/3#v14)</sup> I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.

- “I count not myself to have apprehended”: I have not obtained the kingdom yet; I am still here with you. “Forgetting those things which are behind”, putting the past behind me, “and reaching forth unto those things which are before”, working on myself now. “I press toward the mark for the prize of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.”
- Discipline, purpose and vision are the bridges to the fire, built on the foundation already laid. “Forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before.” This is Paul, and we know how he dealt with his people before. Had he kept beating himself up over it, he could never have been on fire enough to say he would gladly spend and be spent. He put it behind him and used it as fuel to seek the Lord ten times more. That is singleness of mind, not double-mindedness, and how he endured all the afflictions he boasted of. It takes a magnanimous spirit.
- “Forgetting those things which are behind, and reaching forth unto those things which are before.” Forget the past and let it go; look to what is before you. Some will be destroyed by the past because they refuse to let go of what so-and-so did twenty years ago.
- Twenty years ago is twenty atonements past. You went to Messiah at the atonement crying for him to forgive you, yet you still hold a grudge against your brothers and sisters. While you will not forgive that brother or sister, those sins of yours stay. Forget the past and look forward.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiastes 7:10](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v10)</sup> Say not thou, What is the cause that the former days were better than these? for thou dost not enquire wisely concerning this.

    “Say not thou, What is the cause that the former days were better than these?” Never sit back and think, when I was with her everything was good, before you my life had no problems; my ex had the bathwater ready, the food always ready. Then you should have married her or stayed with her. But she didn’t believe in God and didn’t want to keep the commandments, so you left and found a woman who believes she is an Israelite, keeps the commandments and has the faith of Jesus Christ, and now you compare her to a wicked woman. You can’t build on that foundation. That’s wicked, and it makes a woman jealous over another woman.

**[Philippians 3:15](/bible/philippians/3#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/philippians/3#v15)</sup> Let us therefore, as many as be perfect, be thus minded: and if in any thing ye be otherwise minded, God shall reveal even this unto you.

- “Let us therefore, as many as be perfect”: as many as walk in the spirit, “be thus minded: and if in any thing ye be otherwise minded, God shall reveal even this unto you.”

**[Philippians 3:16](/bible/philippians/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/philippians/3#v16)</sup> Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained, let us walk by the same rule, let us mind the same thing.

- “Nevertheless, whereto we have already attained”: we got this knowledge, this understanding. “Let us walk by the same rule”: let all of us, brothers and sisters, walk by the same rule; that’s God’s laws. “Let us mind the same thing.”

**[Philippians 3:17](/bible/philippians/3#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/philippians/3#v17)</sup> Brethren, be followers together of me, and mark them which walk so as ye have us for an ensample.

- “Brethren, be followers together of me, and mark them which walk so”: he said mark those, as you have us for an example. Watch those who are keeping the commandments, take good note of them: those men, those women following the example.

**[Philippians 3:18](/bible/philippians/3#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/philippians/3#v18)</sup> (For many walk, of whom I have told you often, and now tell you even weeping, that they are the enemies of the cross of Christ:

- “For many walk, of whom I have told you often, and now tell you even weeping, that they are the enemies of the cross of Christ.” Those are your adulterers, your liars, your thieves, your drug users, drug abusers, your haters, your child molesters. And some of them are sitting in here among us; some of them are online.
- Food and sex look like two separate things, but they are closely related when it comes to our body: one is the procreation of life and the other the nourishing of that life. Paul speaks of “the enemies of the cross of Christ”, those in opposition to the gospel: the scribes, the Pharisees and the wicked Israelites. They knew Christ. They could not resist his wisdom, and they saw miracles no man could do. But they loved the position Rome placed them in, the accolades, the greetings in the marketplace, the authority. And what comes with that position? Food and sex.

**[Philippians 3:19](/bible/philippians/3#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/philippians/3#v19)</sup> Whose end is destruction, whose God is their belly, and whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things.)

- “Whose end is destruction”, like we read earlier. During this time he’s referring to the scribes and Pharisees and them, ’cause them was wicked as hell. “Whose God is their belly”: it’s always give me, what can I get from this, give me, give me, all about me. “And whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things”: those men, those women mind earthly things.
- “Whose God is their belly”. There are people who make their belly their god: nourishing their flesh, their food consumption, the excess. The Pharisees were committing adultery through the time of Babylon, Greece and Rome, creeping into women’s houses as they did in Susanna and exploiting the women. Eli’s sons sat at the temple and, because of their position and status, exploited the women, because position, status and power get money, power and respect, and they used it to their advantage. Their god went into their food, into their excess, into their sex.
- “Whose glory is in their shame, who mind earthly things.” An earthly person cares about the things of the earth: when am I going to eat, the things that please the eyes and the flesh. When we overindulge outside of the guidelines God gave us, we step out of the realm of God and of discipline into the realm of Satan. That is why God gave us a law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 1:5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/1#v5)</sup> For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding, and will not abide when unrighteousness cometh in.

    “For the holy spirit of discipline will flee deceit, and remove from thoughts that are without understanding”. Discipline is a holy spirit. There is a cap and a limitation. The person who overindulges in food, who cannot stop eating, is worshipping himself, because God says to eat in moderation and to eat what is nourishing; don’t eat just to eat. People followed Christ just for the food, because their god was their belly, while Christ’s meat was to do the will of the Father.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:29](/bible/sirach/33#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/33#v29)</sup> But be not excessive toward any; and without discretion do nothing.

    “Be not excessive toward any; and without discretion do nothing.” Don’t be excessive in anything. You may say, but I’m hungry. Ask yourself why you are eating. Is it for nourishment and the proper nutrients, or because it looks good, smells good, tastes good, sounds good? Are outside entities programming you, catering to the lust of the flesh through the tunnels of your eyes and your ears? Did you eat five minutes ago and now you need a snack?
  - **[Romans 16:18](/bible/romans/16#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/romans/16#v18)</sup> For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple.

    “For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly”. This is the wicked of our people. They do not serve Christ; they serve their fleshly pleasures and lust. Let me get over on this person, defraud this person, steal from this person, so I can fill my own belly. Like Biggie with the Ten Crack Commandments: I’m only selling crack to feed my family. He did not serve the Lord Jesus Christ; he served his own belly, and his stomach became his god.

**[Philippians 3:20](/bible/philippians/3#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/philippians/3#v20)</sup> For our conversation is in heaven; from whence also we look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ:

- “For our conversation is in heaven”: our talk, our conversation, should be in heaven; our conversation should be in this Bible. Now I know it ain’t easy to always talk about the scriptures, but guess what, we got to train ourselves for that, just like you have trained yourself to talk about sports stats. Some of y’all know every stat, and I’m like, what the hell are you talking about? He scored this, he scored that, he was born on this: what the hell, who gives a damn about that?
- “We look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ”: that’s what we look for.
- “For our conversation is in heaven; from whence also we look for the Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ”. Our conversation is the way we talk to each other, and the way we talk to people in the world. Your conversation should be about this Bible, not sports.
- On the job some of them know you are an Israelite, yet one out of every three words is a curse word. That is not godly conversation: it is about sport, or look at this big butt sister over there. You want that brother or sister at your job to repent, but your conversation does not match what you teach. As leaders and ambassadors, understand that they are always watching you. We look for the Saviour, so there are certain things you have to watch that you say.

**[Philippians 3:21](/bible/philippians/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/philippians/3#v21)</sup> Who shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body, according to the working whereby he is able even to subdue all things unto himself.

- “Who shall change our vile body”: all our bodies are vile. These bodies we got are broken and sickly, filled with pestilence, disease, sickness, and destined to die to corruption. “That it may be fashioned like unto his glorious body”: that celestial, that God body, is what we want. “According to the working whereby he is able even to subdue all things unto himself”: Christ is going to subdue all things unto himself.
