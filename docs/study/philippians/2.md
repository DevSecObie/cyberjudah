## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Philippians 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-12, 14, 25-27, 29-30, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Philippians 2:3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)</sup> Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.

- Leadership is not about doing everything yourself; that’s called micromanaging, and that’s a bad quality. The best leaders know they can’t meet their goals alone; they need the right people doing the right things. Leadership means delegating to the right people to get the job done right. That’s why you got officers of 10, over 10 men; officers of 20, over 20 men; officers of 50. Delegate, delegate, delegate. A micromanager says everybody’s a dumb ass, I got to do it myself. No, that’s bad. I refuse to believe that everybody in here is a dumb ass. There’s some good men with good qualities, smarter than us; why else would the Lord bring them in? Some brothers have carpentry skills, some brothers have electrical skills; whatever it is, there’s a right brother for the right job.
- “Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory”: vainglory is vanity, me, me, me, me. “In lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves”. That’s why, when men are teaching, I was telling them: let’s stop interrupting the young men that’s teaching; let’s observe them, let’s watch them. One day a deacon was teaching and a brother asked him a question, and he went into deep waters and got lost. He went so deep he looked back at me to pull him out of the waters. I looked the other way: you got this.
- We got to learn to esteem one another. We got to see the good qualities in one another. It’s there; with some it’s deep down in there, and you got to learn to pull that thing out.
- This is the way Jesus the Christ thought, and Christ was no weak, effeminate black man. He was a strong, powerful man, and an upright man.

**[Philippians 2:4-6](/bible/philippians/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/philippians/2#v4)</sup> Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/philippians/2#v5)</sup> Let this mind be in you, which was also in Christ Jesus:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/philippians/2#v6)</sup> Who, being in the form of God, thought it not robbery to be equal with God:

- “Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.” That does not mean just looking at a brother and saying, yep, he’s struggling like hell. Look means action, damn it.
- “Look not every man on his own things, but every man also on the things of others.” The husband may feel he carries the financial responsibility and want his wife to understand it; the wife may feel overwhelmed with the children and the household. Don't look only at what you are carrying.
- Look at what she's been going through: with the kids all day, every day, from sun up to sun down, cleaning. That can break her, and these things happen; there was the Edomite demon that killed her three kids. You come home and her eyes are bloodshot. Kids will drive you crazy; try it one day and everybody ends up in a headlock and sent to their rooms. It is psychologically traumatizing if you don't give her the space to stabilize, think, and meditate on the scripture. Give her peace and rest so she can regroup and be her best for you. People can't run like machines continually, and then you say she doesn't reverence me or care for me, when you never gave her the opportunity.
- Sisters, think on your husband's things too. He's tired, financially tired: work, bills, the things he deals with in the world. Men don't talk much about their feelings or what they go through psychologically, the burden of a man in society to provide and keep the household running. Understand that, approach him and treat him as the head, and be kindly affectionate one to another.

At the center of our walk on the highway of the upright is the nuclear family. From the strong father in the house comes the right way to eat, which destroys obesity, and the teaching a young man needs to deal with what he will face on his job and in society. Women can’t teach our men that. And the sister in the house balances the man: the son learns strength, structure and law from his father, and empathy from his mother. It is not a woman bash by any means.
  - **[John 5:16-19](/bible/john/5#v16)**

    >

**[Philippians 2:7](/bible/philippians/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/philippians/2#v7)</sup> But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men:

- “But made himself of no reputation, and took upon him the form of a servant”: meaning he came in the flesh. “And was made in the likeness of men”: he came as a man. That goes back to John 1:14, the Word was made flesh.
- Verse 6 ends with a colon, so verse 7 explains it. “But made himself of no reputation”: that goes back to not robbery to be equal with God. “And took upon him the form of a servant, and was made in the likeness of men”. In the Greek, “made himself of no reputation” is said to mean he emptied himself. Not like a tea kettle: it means he humbled himself and took on the status of a servant, even though he was a king and the son of God on the earth. He made himself void of the title of being above everybody else. That is why he washed their feet.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 1:14](/bible/john/1#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/john/1#v14)</sup> And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,) full of grace and truth.

    Read it again: “And the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us, (and we beheld his glory, the glory as of the only begotten of the Father,)”. Christ is the only begotten of the Father.

**[Philippians 2:8](/bible/philippians/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/philippians/2#v8)</sup> And being found in fashion as a man, he humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.

- “And being found in fashion as a man”. Who did that? Our God, Christ, the Word of God, the only begotten Son of the Father. “He humbled himself, and became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.”
- Verse 7 ends with a colon too, so verse 8 explains it. “And being found in fashion as a man”: back to the likeness of men, made like everybody else, of mother and father. “He humbled himself”: back to the form of a servant and no reputation, the same thing. “And became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.”

    “He that spared not his own Son, but delivered him up for us all, how shall he not with him also freely give us all things?”
  - **[Hebrews 9:14-15](/bible/hebrews/9#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/hebrews/9#v14)</sup> How much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without spot to God, purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?

    > <sup>[15](/bible/hebrews/9#v15)</sup> And for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance.

    “How much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit”: that Spirit that was in that flesh, that spirit in that body, is called Christ. Christ is that eternal Spirit.

**[Philippians 2:9-11](/bible/philippians/2#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/philippians/2#v9)</sup> Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/philippians/2#v10)</sup> That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow, of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/philippians/2#v11)</sup> And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.

- “Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him, and given him a name which is above every name”. If they are the same, what name could he possibly be given that is higher? It makes no sense. “That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow”, “And that every tongue should confess that Jesus Christ is Lord, to the glory of God the Father.” A distinction: God exalted him, to the glory of God the Father.
- “Wherefore God also hath highly exalted him”: the Most High God has highly exalted him, “and given him a name which is above every name.” He has got a name that is above every name.
- Christians love this verse: “That at the name of Jesus every knee should bow”. We know that won’t be his name in the kingdom, because he has a new name. At the name of Jesus means at the word of God. “Of things in heaven, and things in earth, and things under the earth”.
- This is the all dominions serving and obeying him of Daniel 7:27. But we read in Revelation that the nations are not going to repent. So how are these nations going to bow the knee? It ain’t gonna bow willingly. We are going to force them, with the power we shall be given, to bow the knee. They have two options: nations, bow the knee or die.

    > <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/16#v14)</sup> For they are the spirits of devils, working miracles, which go forth unto the kings of the earth and of the whole world, to gather them to the battle of that great day of God Almighty.

    “And men were scorched with great heat, and blasphemed the name of God”. This is the white man and all the nations. They “repented not to give him glory.” You think the nations are going to repent and give the Lord glory? They are not. It says they repented not.
  - **[Revelation 3:9](/bible/revelation/3#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/revelation/3#v9)</sup> Behold, I will make them of the synagogue of Satan, which say they are Jews, and are not, but do lie; behold, I will make them to come and worship before thy feet, and to know that I have loved thee.

    How will the nations bow the knee if they won’t repent? “Behold, I will make them of the synagogue of Satan, which say they are Jews, and are not, but do lie”: a bunch of liars. “Behold, I will make them to come and worship before thy feet, and to know that I have loved thee.” Christ is going to make them bow and worship. That is what every knee shall bow means.

    > <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/49#v23)</sup> And kings shall be thy nursing fathers, and their queens thy nursing mothers: they shall bow down to thee with their face toward the earth, and lick up the dust of thy feet; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord: for they shall not be ashamed that wait for me.

    Every knee shall bow. “And kings shall be thy nursing fathers, and their queens thy nursing mothers”: the kings of the other nations and the queens of the other nations. “They shall bow down to thee with their face toward the earth”: that is every knee shall bow. “And lick up the dust of thy feet”: they are going to lick the dust of our feet.

**[Philippians 2:12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.

- Paul had this problem with certain brothers and sisters. “Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only.” Some people just show themselves to be that guy in front of everybody. You got them sisters: a brother has a wife, and he knows she ain’t right. She is over there talking about shalom, and she is a damn demon.
- Not in my presence only, “but now much more in my absence.” You need to be the same in the absence of everybody. This is the perfect biblical example of integrity: obey in my absence, and “work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.”

**[Philippians 2:14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)</sup> Do all things without murmurings and disputings:

- Murmuring and complaining. If you don’t have a solution, shut the hell up. How is your complaining going to help me, or the men and women in this room? We want solutions. If you don’t have one, sit down, brother. And God does not want to hear you complain either.
- “Do all things without murmurings and disputings”.
- The chaos you grew up in becomes your default setting, so you gravitate toward people and places that produce the same chaos. That is why some cannot accept a wife or husband being nice to them, or brothers and sisters dancing and having a good time at the new moon: you are used to the club, people screaming and cursing each other. Your mind is not used to peace, so you work to turn a peaceful sister into a chaos sister, a peaceful brother into a chaos brother.
- The old man goes to the back page of the book when you come into this truth, but he never left. If you hold on to him you will reach that back page and the old man will come out: Deacon, I just snapped.

  - **[Numbers 14:27-30](/bible/numbers/14#v27)**

    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/numbers/14#v28)</sup> Say unto them, As truly as I live, saith the Lord, as ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you:

    > <sup>[29](/bible/numbers/14#v29)</sup> Your carcases shall fall in this wilderness; and all that were numbered of you, according to your whole number, from twenty years old and upward, which have murmured against me,

    > <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/14#v30)</sup> Doubtless ye shall not come into the land, concerning which I sware to make you dwell therein, save Caleb the son of Jephunneh, and Joshua the son of Nun.

    The Most High hates murmuring. “How long shall I bear with this evil congregation, which murmur against me? I have heard the murmurings of the children of Israel, which they murmur against me.” “As truly as I live, saith the Lord, as ye have spoken in mine ears, so will I do to you: Your carcases shall fall in this wilderness; and all that were numbered of you”, “from twenty years old and upward, which have murmured against me, Doubtless ye shall not come into the land”. He killed every single one of those who murmured.
  - **[1 Samuel 15:23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/15#v23)</sup> For rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft, and stubbornness is as iniquity and idolatry. Because thou hast rejected the word of the Lord, he hath also rejected thee from being king.

    Murmuring weakens morale, divides the people, discourages leadership and spreads negativity, and “rebellion is as the sin of witchcraft.”

**[Philippians 2:25](/bible/philippians/2#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/philippians/2#v25)</sup> Yet I supposed it necessary to send to you Epaphroditus, my brother, and companion in labour, and fellowsoldier, but your messenger, and he that ministered to my wants.

- “Epaphroditus, my brother, and companion in labour, and fellowsoldier”. “and he that ministered to my wants.” Epaphroditus helped Paul out and ministered to his wants.

**[Philippians 2:26-27](/bible/philippians/2#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/philippians/2#v26)</sup> For he longed after you all, and was full of heaviness, because that ye had heard that he had been sick.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/philippians/2#v27)</sup> For indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him; and not on him only, but on me also, lest I should have sorrow upon sorrow.

- “For he longed after you all, and was full of heaviness”. He loved you all. “because that ye had heard that he had been sick.” He got really sick. Like when we go on these trips: sometimes the bishop, deacons, captains, or officers get sick, but we're out there doing the work. “For indeed he was sick nigh unto death: but God had mercy on him”. God had mercy because he was doing the work.

**[Philippians 2:29-30](/bible/philippians/2#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/philippians/2#v29)</sup> Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness; and hold such in reputation:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/philippians/2#v30)</sup> Because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death, not regarding his life, to supply your lack of service toward me.

- “Receive him therefore in the Lord with all gladness; and hold such in reputation”. He put his life on the line to serve them, to help Paul get to these people and build them up. He almost died from sickness doing the work. “Because for the work of Christ he was nigh unto death”. All of us in the nation of Israel should have that mindset to do Christ's work, even when it comes down to sickness. We still have to get the work done.
- “not regarding his life, to supply your lack of service toward me.” He didn't regard his own life in doing what Paul asked. To hell with it, I'm still going. I'm not feeling a hundred; I'm still going.
