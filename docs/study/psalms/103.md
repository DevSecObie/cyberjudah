Read the chapter → [Psalms 103](/bible/psalms/103)

This session moves through [Psalms 103](/bible/psalms/103) through 106, four psalms of remembrance and praise. It opens with David blessing the Most High for His mercy toward Israel, moves through the Most High's care over creation in [Psalm 104](/bible/psalms/104), and then recounts the nation's history from Abraham through the wilderness in [Psalms 105](/bible/psalms/105) and 106. The thread throughout is that the mercy, grace, and covenant of the Most High belong to Israel, and that this mercy is not a license to sin but a call to keep His commandments. Christianity is a curse ([Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)); the grace of the Most High was given so that when the Holy Ghost reminds us of His commandments, we apply them to our lives.

**[Psalms 103:1-6](/bible/psalms/103#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/103#v1)</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/103#v2)</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/103#v3)</sup> Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/103#v4)</sup> Who redeemeth thy life from destruction; who crowneth thee with lovingkindness and tender mercies;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/103#v5)</sup> Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things; so that thy youth is renewed like the eagle’s.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/103#v6)</sup> The Lord executeth righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed.

- David charges his own soul and all that is within him to bless the holy name of the Most High and to forget not His benefits.
- Those benefits are named: He forgives all iniquities, heals diseases, redeems life from destruction, crowns with lovingkindness and tender mercies, and renews the youth like the eagle's.
- The Most High executes righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed. Hear that clearly: it does not say for all people, it says for all that are oppressed.

**[Psalms 103:7](/bible/psalms/103#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/103#v7)</sup> He made known his ways unto Moses, his acts unto the children of Israel.

- He made known His ways unto Moses, His acts unto the children of Israel. Look at that: His acts unto the children of Israel, not to every nation.
- When Israel fled Egypt there were oppressed nations who came out with us; the Bible calls them strangers. But the ways of the Most High were made known to Moses and Israel.
  - **[Psalms 147:19-20](/bible/psalms/147#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/147#v19)</sup> He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/147#v20)</sup> He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the Lord.

    He shows His word and statutes to Jacob and Israel; He has not dealt so with any other nation.

**[Psalms 103:8-14](/bible/psalms/103#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/103#v8)</sup> The Lord is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and plenteous in mercy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/103#v9)</sup> He will not always chide: neither will he keep his anger for ever.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/103#v10)</sup> He hath not dealt with us after our sins; nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/103#v11)</sup> For as the heaven is high above the earth, so great is his mercy toward them that fear him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/103#v12)</sup> As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/103#v13)</sup> Like as a father pitieth his children, so the Lord pitieth them that fear him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/103#v14)</sup> For he knoweth our frame; he remembereth that we are dust.

- The Most High is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, plenteous in mercy. He has not dealt with us after our sins nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.
- This is heavy because Christ came to forgive Israel of their sins. That mercy and grace is to Israel, and it is not so that we could continue in sin.
- As far as the east is from the west, so far He removes our transgressions; He pities them that fear Him, for He knows we are dust.
  - **[Matthew 1:21](/bible/matthew/1#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/1#v21)</sup> And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.

    He shall save His people from their sins, and His people are the Israelites.
  - **[Acts 15:15](/bible/acts/15#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/acts/15#v15)</sup> And to this agree the words of the prophets; as it is written,

    To this agree the words of the prophets; David the prophet and Ezra the prophet say the same thing.
  - **[Ezra 9:13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)</sup> And after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for our great trespass, seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this;

    Ezra says the Most High punished us less than our iniquities deserve, agreeing with David.
  - **[Acts 13:38-39](/bible/acts/13#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/13#v38)</sup> Be it known unto you therefore, men and brethren, that through this man is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins:
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/13#v39)</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.

    Through Christ is preached forgiveness, justifying believers from things the law of Moses could not.

**[Psalms 103:15-18](/bible/psalms/103#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/103#v15)</sup> As for man, his days are as grass: as a flower of the field, so he flourisheth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/103#v16)</sup> For the wind passeth over it, and it is gone; and the place thereof shall know it no more.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/103#v17)</sup> But the mercy of the Lord is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear him, and his righteousness unto children’s children;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/103#v18)</sup> To such as keep his covenant, and to those that remember his commandments to do them.

- Man's days are as grass and as a flower of the field; the wind passes over it and it is gone.
- But the mercy of the Most High is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear Him, and His righteousness unto children's children.
- This is why we must teach our children in every generation, so they receive that same mercy.
- The covenant is to such as keep His commandments and remember them to do them. To do them is an action word.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v6)</sup> And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart:
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v7)</sup> And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.

    We are commanded to teach our children the words of the Most High diligently.
  - **[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

    The Most High is a God of knowledge, and by Him actions are weighed.
  - **[James 1:22](/bible/james/1#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/james/1#v22)</sup> But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.

    Be doers of the word and not hearers only.

**[Psalms 103:19-22](/bible/psalms/103#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/103#v19)</sup> The Lord hath prepared his throne in the heavens; and his kingdom ruleth over all.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/103#v20)</sup> Bless the Lord, ye his angels, that excel in strength, that do his commandments, hearkening unto the voice of his word.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/103#v21)</sup> Bless ye the Lord, all ye his hosts; ye ministers of his, that do his pleasure.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/103#v22)</sup> Bless the Lord, all his works in all places of his dominion: bless the Lord, O my soul.

- The Most High has prepared His throne in the heavens and His kingdom rules over all.
- The angels that excel in strength bless Him by doing His commandments and hearkening to the voice of His word.
- All His hosts and works in every place of His dominion bless Him; and David returns to charge his own soul: bless the Lord.
