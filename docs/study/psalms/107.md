Read the chapter → [Psalms 107](/bible/psalms/107)

This session covers [Psalms 107](/bible/psalms/107) through 110, four chapters a day for the nation. [Psalm 107](/bible/psalms/107) opens with thanks to the Most High because His mercy endures forever, and the teaching shows that His mercy is to those that keep His commandments. The chapter runs the whole cycle of Israel: the redeemed gathered out of the four corners of the earth, the affliction that came because we rebelled against the words of God, and the deliverance that comes when we cry unto the Lord and acknowledge our offenses.

**[Psalms 107:1](/bible/psalms/107#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/107#v1)</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.

- Give thanks unto the Lord for He is good, for His mercy endureth forever. Throughout Psalms and throughout the Bible it says His mercy endures forever, and the teaching stops to show exactly what that mercy is.
- The mercy is to those that keep His commandments, so His commandments endure forever and they are not going away. This is the same as the grace spoken of in [Ephesians 2:8](/bible/ephesians/2#v8).
  - **[Exodus 20:6](/bible/exodus/20#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/20#v6)</sup> And shewing mercy unto thousands of them that love me, and keep my commandments.

    The mercy is shown unto thousands of them that love the Most High and keep His commandments.
  - **[Ephesians 2:8](/bible/ephesians/2#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/ephesians/2#v8)</sup> For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God:

    This grace, this mercy, is the same grace through faith spoken of here.

**[Psalms 107:2](/bible/psalms/107#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/107#v2)</sup> Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom he hath redeemed from the hand of the enemy;

- Let the redeemed of the Lord say so, whom He has redeemed from the hand of the enemy. The redeemed are the Israelites, Zion, and they are the ones that need to say these things.
- Zion shall be redeemed with judgment, and to be converted you have to be keeping God's laws, His righteousness. When you forsake the Lord's laws you are consumed together with the sinners.
  - **[Isaiah 1:27-28](/bible/isaiah/1#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/isaiah/1#v27)</sup> Zion shall be redeemed with judgment, and her converts with righteousness.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/isaiah/1#v28)</sup> And the destruction of the transgressors and of the sinners shall be together, and they that forsake the Lord shall be consumed.

    Zion, the Israelites, shall be redeemed with judgment and her converts with righteousness, but the transgressors and sinners that forsake the Lord shall be consumed together.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)</sup> And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he hath commanded us.

    To be converted you must be keeping God's righteousness, His commandments.
  - **[1 Kings 8:1](/bible/1-kings/8#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/8#v1)</sup> Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel, and all the heads of the tribes, the chief of the fathers of the children of Israel, unto king Solomon in Jerusalem, that they might bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord out of the city of David, which is Zion.

    Zion is Israel.
  - **[Isaiah 59:20](/bible/isaiah/59#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/59#v20)</sup> And the Redeemer shall come to Zion, and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob, saith the Lord.

    The Redeemer, referring to Christ, shall come to Zion and unto them that turn from transgression in Jacob.

**[Psalms 107:3](/bible/psalms/107#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/107#v3)</sup> And gathered them out of the lands, from the east, and from the west, from the north, and from the south.

- He gathers the redeemed out of the lands from the east and from the west, from the north and from the south. The ones being gathered are the redeemed, those that are keeping God's commandments.

**[Psalms 107:4-7](/bible/psalms/107#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/107#v4)</sup> They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way; they found no city to dwell in.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/107#v5)</sup> Hungry and thirsty, their soul fainted in them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/107#v6)</sup> Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he delivered them out of their distresses.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/107#v7)</sup> And he led them forth by the right way, that they might go to a city of habitation.

- They wandered in the wilderness in a solitary way and found no city to dwell in, hungry and thirsty, their soul fainting in them. Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble and He delivered them out of their distresses.
- He led them forth by the right way that they might go to a city of habitation. This is the pattern: crying to the Most High in trouble brings deliverance.

**[Psalms 107:8-9](/bible/psalms/107#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/107#v8)</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/107#v9)</sup> For he satisfieth the longing soul, and filleth the hungry soul with goodness.

- Men would praise the Lord for His goodness and for His wonderful works to the children of men, for He satisfieth the longing soul and filleth the hungry soul with goodness. The goodness is the word of God, the Bible.
- The Bible is the image of His goodness to His people, the Israelites. We are to live by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of the Most High, and eating the word means learning it.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 7:26](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/7#v26)</sup> For she is the brightness of the everlasting light, the unspotted mirror of the power of God, and the image of his goodness.

    Wisdom, the unspotted mirror of the power of God, is this Bible, the image of His goodness.
  - **[Matthew 4:4](/bible/matthew/4#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/4#v4)</sup> But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.

    Man shall not live by bread alone but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God, so we must live by this Bible.
  - **[Jeremiah 15:16](/bible/jeremiah/15#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/15#v16)</sup> Thy words were found, and I did eat them; and thy word was unto me the joy and rejoicing of mine heart: for I am called by thy name, O Lord God of hosts.

    The words were found and eaten, becoming the joy and rejoicing of the heart, so this Bible should be our chief joy.

**[Psalms 107:10-12](/bible/psalms/107#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/107#v10)</sup> Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/107#v11)</sup> Because they rebelled against the words of God, and contemned the counsel of the most High:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/107#v12)</sup> Therefore he brought down their heart with labour; they fell down, and there was none to help.

- Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron, because they rebelled against the words of God and condemned the counsel of the Most High. We are still bound in affliction and iron today, in captivity, even as we are raising back up and learning the words of God.
- Because we rebelled and hated this Bible, He brought down our heart with labor, He brought down our minds with slavery. We fell down and there was none to help.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15)</sup> But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:

    If you will not hearken unto the voice of the Lord to observe and do all His commandments, all these curses shall come upon you and overtake you, and this is why the curses came upon us as a people.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:46-48](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v46)</sup> And they shall be upon thee for a sign and for a wonder, and upon thy seed for ever.
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v47)</sup> Because thou servedst not the Lord thy God with joyfulness, and with gladness of heart, for the abundance of all things;
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v48)</sup> Therefore shalt thou serve thine enemies which the Lord shall send against thee, in hunger, and in thirst, and in nakedness, and in want of all things: and he shall put a yoke of iron upon thy neck, until he have destroyed thee.

    These curses are a sign and a wonder upon us and our seed forever, generational curses, because we served not the Lord with joyfulness and gladness of heart, so we serve our enemies in hunger, thirst, and nakedness with a yoke of iron.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:68](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v68)**
    > <sup>[68](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v68)</sup> And the Lord shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships, by the way whereof I spake unto thee, Thou shalt see it no more again: and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you.

    The Lord brings us into Egypt again with ships, sold unto our enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man buys us, identifying the children of Israel as the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and native Indians who went on the slave ships.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:29](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v29)</sup> And thou shalt grope at noonday, as the blind gropeth in darkness, and thou shalt not prosper in thy ways: and thou shalt be only oppressed and spoiled evermore, and no man shall save thee.

    We grope at noonday as the blind grope in darkness, seeing the truth right in front of us but walking right on by, only oppressed and spoiled evermore with no man to save us.

**[Psalms 107:13-16](/bible/psalms/107#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/107#v13)</sup> Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble, and he saved them out of their distresses.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/107#v14)</sup> He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death, and brake their bands in sunder.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/107#v15)</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/107#v16)</sup> For he hath broken the gates of brass, and cut the bars of iron in sunder.

- Then they cried unto the Lord in their trouble and He saved them out of their distresses. This is when we acknowledge our offenses, the wrong we did in breaking God's laws, and that is when He begins to save us.
- He brought them out of darkness and the shadow of death and brake their bands asunder. He broke the gates of brass and cut the bars of iron in sunder, taking away our captivity.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    The Most High returns to His place until we acknowledge our offense and seek His face; in our affliction we will seek Him early, so sometimes affliction is good for our people that we may seek the Lord.

**[Psalms 107:17-20](/bible/psalms/107#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/107#v17)</sup> Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/107#v18)</sup> Their soul abhorreth all manner of meat; and they draw near unto the gates of death.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/107#v19)</sup> Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble, and he saveth them out of their distresses.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/107#v20)</sup> He sent his word, and healed them, and delivered them from their destructions.

- Fools, because of their transgression and because of their iniquities, are afflicted. When we break God's laws we get afflicted, but the Bible calls us fools, and our soul abhorreth all manner of meat, showing how stiffnecked we were even in the wilderness.
- They draw near unto the gates of death, then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble and He saveth them. He sent His word and healed them, so we get healed today by God's word.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 16:20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/16#v20)</sup> Instead whereof thou feddest thine own people with angels’ food, and didst send them from heaven bread prepared without their labour, able to content every man’s delight, and agreeing to every taste.

    In the wilderness the angels' food agreed to every taste, whatever your delight was it tasted like, yet we still complained as a people.

**[Psalms 107:21-32](/bible/psalms/107#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/107#v21)</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/107#v22)</sup> And let them sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving, and declare his works with rejoicing.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/107#v23)</sup> They that go down to the sea in ships, that do business in great waters;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/107#v24)</sup> These see the works of the Lord, and his wonders in the deep.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/107#v25)</sup> For he commandeth, and raiseth the stormy wind, which lifteth up the waves thereof.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/107#v26)</sup> They mount up to the heaven, they go down again to the depths: their soul is melted because of trouble.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/107#v27)</sup> They reel to and fro, and stagger like a drunken man, and are at their wits’ end.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/107#v28)</sup> Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble, and he bringeth them out of their distresses.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/107#v29)</sup> He maketh the storm a calm, so that the waves thereof are still.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/107#v30)</sup> Then are they glad because they be quiet; so he bringeth them unto their desired haven.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/107#v31)</sup> Oh that men would praise the Lord for his goodness, and for his wonderful works to the children of men!
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/107#v32)</sup> Let them exalt him also in the congregation of the people, and praise him in the assembly of the elders.

- Let them sacrifice the sacrifices of thanksgiving and declare His works with rejoicing. They that go down to the sea in ships and do business in great waters see the works of the Lord and His wonders in the deep.
- He commandeth and raiseth the stormy wind which lifteth up the waves, referring to hurricanes that mount up to heaven and go down again to the depths, and the sailors' souls are melted because of trouble as they reel and stagger like a drunken man at their wits' end.
- Then they cry unto the Lord in their trouble and He bringeth them out of their distresses, making the storm a calm so the waves are still. We can do nothing without the Lord and must lean upon the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob to deliver us out of any trouble, exalting Him also in the congregation of the people.

**[Psalms 107:33-38](/bible/psalms/107#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/107#v33)</sup> He turneth rivers into a wilderness, and the watersprings into dry ground;
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/107#v34)</sup> A fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/107#v35)</sup> He turneth the wilderness into a standing water, and dry ground into watersprings.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/107#v36)</sup> And there he maketh the hungry to dwell, that they may prepare a city for habitation;
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/psalms/107#v37)</sup> And sow the fields, and plant vineyards, which may yield fruits of increase.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/psalms/107#v38)</sup> He blesseth them also, so that they are multiplied greatly; and suffereth not their cattle to decrease.

- He turneth rivers into a wilderness and the watersprings into dry ground, a fruitful land into barrenness, for the wickedness of them that dwell therein. He does these things for the sins that we commit.
- He turneth the wilderness into a standing water and dry ground into watersprings, and there He maketh the hungry to dwell that they may prepare a city for habitation, sow fields, and plant vineyards. He blesses them so that they are multiplied greatly, talking about Israel multiplying, and suffereth not their cattle to decrease.

**[Psalms 107:39-43](/bible/psalms/107#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/psalms/107#v39)</sup> Again, they are minished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/psalms/107#v40)</sup> He poureth contempt upon princes, and causeth them to wander in the wilderness, where there is no way.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/psalms/107#v41)</sup> Yet setteth he the poor on high from affliction, and maketh him families like a flock.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/psalms/107#v42)</sup> The righteous shall see it, and rejoice: and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/psalms/107#v43)</sup> Whoso is wise, and will observe these things, even they shall understand the lovingkindness of the Lord.

- Again they are diminished and brought low through oppression, affliction, and sorrow, meaning we go back into slavery. He poureth contempt upon princes and causeth them to wander in the wilderness where there is no way.
- Yet He setteth the poor on high from affliction, the poor in spirit, and maketh him families like a flock. The righteous, those keeping God's commandments, shall see it and rejoice, and all iniquity shall stop her mouth.
- Whoso is wise and will observe these things, even they shall understand the lovingkindness of the Lord. If you have wisdom you will observe these things.
  - **[Matthew 5:3](/bible/matthew/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/5#v3)</sup> Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

    The poor in spirit are blessed, and it is they whom the Most High sets on high from affliction.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 11:14](/bible/sirach/11#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/11#v14)</sup> Prosperity and adversity, life and death, poverty and riches, come of the Lord.

    Prosperity and adversity, life and death, poverty and riches all come of the Lord; you prospered one time and now you are in your season of adversity.
  - **[Psalms 127:3](/bible/psalms/127#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/127#v3)</sup> Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.

    Children are the heritage and gift of the Lord, one of the things we understand come of Him.
