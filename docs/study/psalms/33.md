**[Psalm 33:1-3](/bible/psalms/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/33#v1)</sup> Rejoice in the Lord, O ye righteous: for praise is comely for the upright.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/33#v2)</sup> Praise the Lord with harp: sing unto him with the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/33#v3)</sup> Sing unto him a new song; play skilfully with a loud noise.

- Rejoice in the Lord O ye righteous, for praise is comely for the upright, and comely means beautiful. This is where praises are being given to the Most High.
- We must remember to rejoice, because that is why we came into captivity in the first place, because we did not serve the Lord with joyfulness. Our people do have a spirit to rejoice in the Lord, but they are not rejoicing to Him on Sunday in church, they are rejoicing to Cesare Borgia. The spirit within them to serve is a godly spirit because we are of God, but they are not serving Him in spirit and in truth because they have no idea who the Most High is.
- It is the righteous that must rejoice in the Lord, us that understand who the Most High is, that He is a black man according to the Bible, that His son is black, and that the Israelites are black. This is how we rejoice in the Lord, not to Cesare Borgia. Praise the Lord with harp, sing unto Him with the psaltery and an instrument of ten strings, sing unto Him a new song, play skillfully with a loud noise.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:47](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v47)</sup> Because thou servedst not the Lord thy God with joyfulness, and with gladness of heart, for the abundance of all things;

    Because thou servedst not the Lord thy God with joyfulness and gladness of heart, therefore shalt thou serve thine enemies, which is who our people serve now, even on Sundays when they think they are serving the Most High but are serving the man that put us into captivity and stole the identity of the black Messiah.

**[Psalm 33:4-9](/bible/psalms/33#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/33#v4)</sup> For the word of the Lord is right; and all his works are done in truth.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/33#v5)</sup> He loveth righteousness and judgment: the earth is full of the goodness of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/33#v6)</sup> By the word of the Lord were the heavens made; and all the host of them by the breath of his mouth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/33#v7)</sup> He gathereth the waters of the sea together as an heap: he layeth up the depth in storehouses.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/33#v8)</sup> Let all the earth fear the Lord: let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of him.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/33#v9)</sup> For he spake, and it was done; he commanded, and it stood fast.

- For the word of the Lord is right and all His works are done in truth, He loveth righteousness and judgment, the earth is full of the goodness of the Lord.
- By the word of the Lord were the heavens made and all the host of them by the breath of His mouth, He gathereth the waters of the sea together as a heap and layeth up the deep in storehouses.
- Let all the earth fear the Lord, let all the inhabitants of the world stand in awe of Him, for He spake and it was done, He commanded and it stood fast.

**[Psalm 33:10-12](/bible/psalms/33#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/33#v10)</sup> The Lord bringeth the counsel of the heathen to nought: he maketh the devices of the people of none effect.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/33#v11)</sup> The counsel of the Lord standeth for ever, the thoughts of his heart to all generations.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/33#v12)</sup> Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord; and the people whom he hath chosen for his own inheritance.

- The Lord bringeth the counsel of the heathen to nought and maketh the devices of the people of none effect. We have to take that into consideration in this time of pandemic where the vaccination of information is floating around and there is fake news everywhere.
- Even though they plot against us and set traps for the nation of Israel as we push this truth throughout the four corners of the earth, even though they will cross heaven and earth to stop the rise of the nation of Israel, the Most High brings the counsel of the wicked to nought. We ought to ask the Most High in our prayers to bring their devices to an end. Do not worry about the vaccine, whether you take it or not is up to you, but the counsel of the ungodly will have no effect on us who are keeping the commandments, for we are under His will and under His wings.
- The counsel of the Lord standeth for ever, the thoughts of His heart to all generations. Blessed is the nation whose God is the Lord, and that is not everybody, that is the nation of Israel, because there is only one true God of heaven and earth and He belongs to us. The people whom He hath chosen for His own inheritance, and there are thousands of precepts on who that is.

**[Psalm 33:13-19](/bible/psalms/33#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/33#v13)</sup> The Lord looketh from heaven; he beholdeth all the sons of men.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/33#v14)</sup> From the place of his habitation he looketh upon all the inhabitants of the earth.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/33#v15)</sup> He fashioneth their hearts alike; he considereth all their works.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/33#v16)</sup> There is no king saved by the multitude of an host: a mighty man is not delivered by much strength.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/33#v17)</sup> An horse is a vain thing for safety: neither shall he deliver any by his great strength.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/33#v18)</sup> Behold, the eye of the Lord is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/33#v19)</sup> To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine.

- The Lord looketh from heaven and beholdeth all the sons of men, from the place of His habitation He looketh upon all the inhabitants of the earth, He fashioneth their hearts alike and considereth all their works.
- There is no king saved by the multitude of a host, a mighty man is not delivered by much strength, a horse is a vain thing for safety, neither shall he deliver any by his great strength.
- Behold the eye of the Lord is upon them that fear Him, upon them that hope in His mercy. This is a heavy psalm. Fearing God means you are going to keep the commandments, so if you are keeping the commandments the eyes of the Lord are upon you, and we need to watch what we do and how we move out here in society, because just because we are not with another brother or sister does not mean we can sneak off and do evil and it will not come up. It will come up.
- To deliver their soul from death and to keep them alive in famine. Our soul waited for the Lord, He is our help and our shield, for our heart shall rejoice in Him because we have trusted in His holy name.
  - **[Psalm 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

    The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom, and a good understanding have all they that do His commandments, so if you are not keeping the commandments you have no wisdom or understanding.
