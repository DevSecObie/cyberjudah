**[Psalms 94:1-3](/bible/psalms/94#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/94#v1)</sup> O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth; O God, to whom vengeance belongeth, shew thyself.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/94#v2)</sup> Lift up thyself, thou judge of the earth: render a reward to the proud.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/94#v3)</sup> Lord, how long shall the wicked, how long shall the wicked triumph?

- O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth, show thyself. We really have to understand this in these times when a lot of our brothers and sisters want to take up arms against the oppressor, because vengeance belongeth to the Most High.
- We ask the question, how long shall the wicked triumph.
  - **[Luke 18:2-8](/bible/luke/18#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/luke/18#v2)</sup> Saying, There was in a city a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/luke/18#v3)</sup> And there was a widow in that city; and she came unto him, saying, Avenge me of mine adversary.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/luke/18#v4)</sup> And he would not for a while: but afterward he said within himself, Though I fear not God, nor regard man;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/luke/18#v5)</sup> Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/luke/18#v6)</sup> And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/luke/18#v7)</sup> And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them?
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/luke/18#v8)</sup> I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. Nevertheless when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?

    The unjust judge avenged the widow for her continual coming; how much more shall God avenge His own elect speedily, yet shall He find faith on earth when the Son of man cometh.
  - **[Luke 21:19](/bible/luke/21#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/luke/21#v19)</sup> In your patience possess ye your souls.

    In your patience possess your souls; while we cry out how long, do not lose faith, Israel.

**[Psalms 94:4-7](/bible/psalms/94#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/94#v4)</sup> How long shall they utter and speak hard things? and all the workers of iniquity boast themselves?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/94#v5)</sup> They break in pieces thy people, O Lord, and afflict thine heritage.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/94#v6)</sup> They slay the widow and the stranger, and murder the fatherless.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/94#v7)</sup> Yet they say, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it.

- The workers of iniquity utter and speak hard things and boast themselves, like Trump and the Proud Boys who can scale the walls and get into the White House, while a sister who did a U-turn out there got shot up. Do not think the Most High is negligent or does not see these things.
- They break the Most High's people in pieces and afflict His heritage; they slay the widow and the stranger and murder the fatherless. That is the curses we are up under.
- Yet they say the Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it. That is a heavy point, because it shows the people know who we are; it does not say the God of all people, it says the God of Jacob.
  - **[Jeremiah 51:20](/bible/jeremiah/51#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/51#v20)</sup> Thou art my battle axe and weapons of war: for with thee will I break in pieces the nations, and with thee will I destroy kingdoms;

    We are the Most High's battle axe and weapons of war, so we must prepare ourselves to be part of that army.

**[Psalms 94:8-11](/bible/psalms/94#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/94#v8)</sup> Understand, ye brutish among the people: and ye fools, when will ye be wise?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/94#v9)</sup> He that planted the ear, shall he not hear? he that formed the eye, shall he not see?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/94#v10)</sup> He that chastiseth the heathen, shall not he correct? he that teacheth man knowledge, shall not he know?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/94#v11)</sup> The Lord knoweth the thoughts of man, that they are vanity.

- Understand, ye brutish among the people, and ye fools, when will ye be wise. He that planted the ear shall hear, He that formed the eye shall see, and He that teacheth man knowledge shall know.
- The Most High knoweth the thoughts of man that they are vanity. He knows our thoughts as His heritage, and He knows the thoughts of the heathen because He created them.
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:26-28](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v26)</sup> I said, I would scatter them into corners, I would make the remembrance of them to cease from among men:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v27)</sup> Were it not that I feared the wrath of the enemy, lest their adversaries should behave themselves strangely, and lest they should say, Our hand is high, and the Lord hath not done all this.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v28)</sup> For they are a nation void of counsel, neither is there any understanding in them.

    The Most High scattered us into corners as a curse, but feared the wrath of the enemy lest they should say their hand is high; He knows the heathen are a nation void of counsel.

**[Psalms 94:12-15](/bible/psalms/94#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/94#v12)</sup> Blessed is the man whom thou chastenest, O Lord, and teachest him out of thy law;
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/94#v13)</sup> That thou mayest give him rest from the days of adversity, until the pit be digged for the wicked.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/94#v14)</sup> For the Lord will not cast off his people, neither will he forsake his inheritance.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/94#v15)</sup> But judgment shall return unto righteousness: and all the upright in heart shall follow it.

- Blessed is the man whom the Most High chasteneth and teacheth out of His law. We get chastened and taught out of that law; that is why in Revelation the books are opened and the book of your life is measured against the laws.
- The Most High will not cast off His people nor forsake His inheritance. People say black people are gentiles and nationality is not important, but this tells you nationality is extremely important; you must know who you are.
  - **[Romans 11:1-3](/bible/romans/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/11#v1)</sup> I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/11#v2)</sup> God hath not cast away his people which he foreknew. Wot ye not what the scripture saith of Elias? how he maketh intercession to God against Israel, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/romans/11#v3)</sup> Lord, they have killed thy prophets, and digged down thine altars; and I am left alone, and they seek my life.

    Paul says God hath not cast away His people whom He foreknew, for Paul himself is an Israelite of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.

**[Psalms 94:16-19](/bible/psalms/94#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/94#v16)</sup> Who will rise up for me against the evildoers? or who will stand up for me against the workers of iniquity?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/94#v17)</sup> Unless the Lord had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/94#v18)</sup> When I said, My foot slippeth; thy mercy, O Lord, held me up.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/94#v19)</sup> In the multitude of my thoughts within me thy comforts delight my soul.

- Who will rise up for me against the evildoers and workers of iniquity, iniquity being sin. Unless the Most High had been my help, my soul had almost dwelt in silence.
- When I said my foot slippeth, Thy mercy held me up; in the multitude of my thoughts within me, Thy comforts delight my soul.

**[Psalms 94:20](/bible/psalms/94#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/94#v20)</sup> Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by a law?

- Shall the throne of iniquity have fellowship with thee, which frameth mischief by law. A throne is the image of power, so shall that power have fellowship with the Most High that frames mischief by law.
- The mischief framed by law is men marrying men, women marrying women, setting the new year in the midst of winter to have us worshiping another god, and coexist meaning you can serve any gods you want.
  - **[Habakkuk 1:3-4](/bible/habakkuk/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/habakkuk/1#v3)</sup> Why dost thou shew me iniquity, and cause me to behold grievance? for spoiling and violence are before me: and there are that raise up strife and contention.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/habakkuk/1#v4)</sup> Therefore the law is slacked, and judgment doth never go forth: for the wicked doth compass about the righteous; therefore wrong judgment proceedeth.

    Iniquity, spoiling, and violence are before us; the law is slack and judgment never goes forth, and wrong judgment proceeds so our oppressors are found not guilty.
  - **[2 Corinthians 6:14-15](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v14)</sup> Be ye not unequally yoked together with unbelievers: for what fellowship hath righteousness with unrighteousness? and what communion hath light with darkness?
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v15)</sup> And what concord hath Christ with Belial? or what part hath he that believeth with an infidel?

    Be ye not unequally yoked with unbelievers, for there is no fellowship between righteousness and unrighteousness, nor concord between Christ and Belial.
  - **[1 Timothy 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

    He that provides not for his own house hath denied the faith and is worse than an infidel, so do not hang around a slothful man or woman.
  - **[2 Corinthians 6:16-17](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v16)</sup> And what agreement hath the temple of God with idols? for ye are the temple of the living God; as God hath said, I will dwell in them, and walk in them; and I will be their God, and they shall be my people.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v17)</sup> Wherefore come out from among them, and be ye separate, saith the Lord, and touch not the unclean thing; and I will receive you,

    We are the temple of the living God; He does not say He is the God of all people, so come out from among them, be ye separate, and touch not the unclean thing.

**[Psalms 94:21-23](/bible/psalms/94#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/94#v21)</sup> They gather themselves together against the soul of the righteous, and condemn the innocent blood.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/94#v22)</sup> But the Lord is my defence; and my God is the rock of my refuge.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/94#v23)</sup> And he shall bring upon them their own iniquity, and shall cut them off in their own wickedness; yea, the Lord our God shall cut them off.

- They gather together against the soul of the righteous and condemn the innocent blood; this has been happening to us from the beginning, especially in Babylon.
- But the Most High is my defense and the rock of my refuge, and He shall bring upon them their own iniquity and cut them off in their own wickedness. The first verse says vengeance is His, and the last verse says He shall cut them off; we do not have the power ourselves.
  - **[Romans 9:17](/bible/romans/9#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/romans/9#v17)</sup> For the scripture saith unto Pharaoh, Even for this same purpose have I raised thee up, that I might shew my power in thee, and that my name might be declared throughout all the earth.

    For the same purpose the Most High raised up Pharaoh, that He might show His power in him and that His name might be declared throughout all the earth.
