Read the chapter → [Psalms 15](/bible/psalms/15)

This session moves through [Psalms 15](/bible/psalms/15) through 18 in the "4 Chapters a Day" reading. The opening disclaimer is a reminder that Christ commanded us to watch and pray, and that this is not the time to revolt physically; the Most High has not given us that strength and power as of yet, so we stay vigilant and on guard. [Psalm 15](/bible/psalms/15) sets the standard of the righteous man, [Psalm 16](/bible/psalms/16) preserves the trust of the saints, [Psalm 17](/bible/psalms/17) is a prayer for protection from the wicked, and [Psalm 18](/bible/psalms/18) is prophesied to reach into the last days concerning the elect.

**[Psalms 15:1-2](/bible/psalms/15#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/15#v2)</sup> He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart.

- The Psalm opens with a question: who shall abide in the Most High's tabernacle and dwell in his holy hill. The answer is the man that walketh uprightly and worketh righteousness and speaketh the truth in his heart.
- Righteousness is defined by keeping the commandments.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)</sup> And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he hath commanded us.

    Righteousness is when we observe to do all these commandments.

**[Psalms 15:3-5](/bible/psalms/15#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/15#v3)</sup> He that backbiteth not with his tongue, nor doeth evil to his neighbour, nor taketh up a reproach against his neighbour.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/15#v4)</sup> In whose eyes a vile person is contemned; but he honoureth them that fear the Lord. He that sweareth to his own hurt, and changeth not.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/15#v5)</sup> He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

- The righteous man does no evil to his neighbor, does not take up a reproach against him, condemns the vile person but honors them that fear the Lord, and sweareth to his own hurt and changeth not.
- He puts not out his money to usury nor takes reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.
- These attributes are the same standard laid out in the law and in the description of the just, righteous man.
  - **[Ezekiel 18:5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)</sup> But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right,

    The just man that doeth that which is lawful and right is the picture of this righteous cell.
