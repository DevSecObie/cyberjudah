Read the chapter → [Psalms 111](/bible/psalms/111)

This session covers [Psalms 111](/bible/psalms/111) through 114, four brief chapters returning to the four chapters a day reading. The thread running through all four is the same: praise the Most High, and blessed are those who fear him and keep his commandments. [Psalm 111](/bible/psalms/111) praises the greatness, honor, and righteousness of his works and names the fear of the Most High as the beginning of wisdom. [Psalm 112](/bible/psalms/112) shows the blessing on the man who delights greatly in his commandments. [Psalm 113](/bible/psalms/113) lifts up the Most High as high above all nations, the one who raises the poor out of the dust and lifts up his people Israel. [Psalm 114](/bible/psalms/114) recalls how the earth itself trembled when Israel came out of Egypt.

**[Psalms 111:1](/bible/psalms/111#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/111#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. I will praise the Lord with my whole heart, in the assembly of the upright, and in the congregation.

- Praise you the Most High. David will praise the Most High with his whole heart, in the assembly of the upright and in the congregation.

**[Psalms 111:2](/bible/psalms/111#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/111#v2)</sup> The works of the Lord are great, sought out of all them that have pleasure therein.

- The works of the Most High are great, sought out of all them that have pleasure therein.

**[Psalms 111:3](/bible/psalms/111#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/111#v3)</sup> His work is honourable and glorious: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

- His work is honorable and glorious, and his righteousness endureth forever.

**[Psalms 111:4](/bible/psalms/111#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/111#v4)</sup> He hath made his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is gracious and full of compassion.

- He has made his glorious works to be remembered. The Most High is gracious and full of compassion.

**[Psalms 111:5](/bible/psalms/111#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/111#v5)</sup> He hath given meat unto them that fear him: he will ever be mindful of his covenant.

- He has given meat unto them that fear him. He will ever be mindful of his covenant.

**[Psalms 111:6](/bible/psalms/111#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/111#v6)</sup> He hath shewed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

- He has showed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

**[Psalms 111:7](/bible/psalms/111#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/111#v7)</sup> The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure.

- The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure.

**[Psalms 111:8](/bible/psalms/111#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/111#v8)</sup> They stand fast for ever and ever, and are done in truth and uprightness.

- They stand fast forever and ever, and are done in truth and uprightness.

**[Psalms 111:9](/bible/psalms/111#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/111#v9)</sup> He sent redemption unto his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever: holy and reverend is his name.

- He sent redemption unto his people; he has commanded his covenant forever. Holy and reverend is his name.

**[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

- The fear of the Most High is the beginning of wisdom; a good understanding have all they that do his commandments. His praise endureth forever.
