**[Psalm 120:1-4](/bible/psalms/120#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/120#v1)</sup> In my distress I cried unto the Lord, and he heard me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/120#v2)</sup> Deliver my soul, O Lord, from lying lips, and from a deceitful tongue.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/120#v3)</sup> What shall be given unto thee? or what shall be done unto thee, thou false tongue?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/120#v4)</sup> Sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals of juniper.

- In my distress I cried unto the Most High, and He heard me. Deliver my soul from lying lips, and from a deceitful tongue. What shall be given unto thee, thou false tongue? Sharp arrows of the mighty, with coals of juniper, coals that lay waste.

**[Psalm 120:5-7](/bible/psalms/120#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/120#v5)</sup> Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech, that I dwell in the tents of Kedar!
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/120#v6)</sup> My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/120#v7)</sup> I am for peace: but when I speak, they are for war.

- Woe is me, that I sojourn in Mesech, that I dwell in the tents of Kedar. My soul hath long dwelt with him that hateth peace. The Israelites have long dwelt with Esau, Edom, the daughter of Babylon, who hate peace, hate us, and hate our guts.
- I am for peace, but when I speak, they are for war. With his smooth policies, politics, religion, and lies our people believe him, but war is in his mind, designed to destroy us secretly, the same as was done to the Indians. We are to live peaceably with all men if possible and rely on the law of this Bible.
