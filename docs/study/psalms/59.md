Read the chapter → [Psalms 59](/bible/psalms/59)

This session reads through [Psalms 59](/bible/psalms/59) through 62, four chapters a day for the nation. The teacher notes up front that the chapters are extremely short, so the purpose is mostly to read and give as much explanation as needed without doing too much. [Psalm 59](/bible/psalms/59) is set during the time when Saul sent men to watch David's house to kill him. The teaching holds this up as the mind we should have when we are going through affliction: with people watching us, nations watching us, the heathen raging, and our own people hating us, these are the prayers that need to come out of our spirit. Stay in the spirit.

**[Psalms 59:1-2](/bible/psalms/59#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/59#v1)</sup> Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/59#v2)</sup> Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, and save me from bloody men.

- The superscription places this at the time Saul sent men who watched the house to kill David. Saul and his men were watching David, his ins and outs, to try to kill him, and this is what David was going through.
- David cries for the Most High to deliver him from his enemies and defend him from those that rise up against him, and to save him from the workers of iniquity and the bloody men.

**[Psalms 59:3-5](/bible/psalms/59#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/59#v3)</sup> For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul: the mighty are gathered against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/59#v4)</sup> They run and prepare themselves without my fault: awake to help me, and behold.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/59#v5)</sup> Thou therefore, O Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, awake to visit all the heathen: be not merciful to any wicked transgressors. Selah.

- The mighty are gathered against David, lying in wait for his soul, not for his transgression nor his sin. They run and prepare themselves without any fault of his.
- He calls on the Lord God of hosts, the God of Israel, to awake to visit all the heathen and to be not merciful to any wicked transgressors.

**[Psalms 59:6-8](/bible/psalms/59#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/59#v6)</sup> They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/59#v7)</sup> Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they, doth hear?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/59#v8)</sup> But thou, O Lord, shalt laugh at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision.

- The enemies return at evening, make a noise like a dog, and go around about the city; they belch out with their mouth, and swords are in their lips, thinking no one hears.
- But the Most High laughs at them and holds all the heathen in derision.

**[Psalms 59:9-11](/bible/psalms/59#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/59#v9)</sup> Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defence.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/59#v10)</sup> The God of my mercy shall prevent me: God shall let me see my desire upon mine enemies.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/59#v11)</sup> Slay them not, lest my people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down, O Lord our shield.

- Because of the enemy's strength, David waits upon the Most High, who is his defense and the God of his mercy.
- He asks that they not be slain outright lest his people forget, but scattered by the power of the Most High and brought down.

**[Psalms 59:12-13](/bible/psalms/59#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/59#v12)</sup> For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride: and for cursing and lying which they speak.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/59#v13)</sup> Consume them in wrath, consume them, that they may not be: and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. Selah.

- For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips, let them be taken in their pride, in their cursing and lying.
- The heavy point: let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. That is a heavy point.

**[Psalms 59:14-17](/bible/psalms/59#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/59#v14)</sup> And at evening let them return; and let them make a noise like a dog, and go round about the city.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/59#v15)</sup> Let them wander up and down for meat, and grudge if they be not satisfied.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/59#v16)</sup> But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning: for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/59#v17)</sup> Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing: for God is my defence, and the God of my mercy.

- Again the enemies return at evening making a noise like a dog, wandering up and down for meat, grudging if they are not satisfied.
- But David will sing of the power of the Most High and sing aloud of His mercy in the morning, for He has been his defense and refuge in the day of trouble.
- This is the mind of King David, and the type of mind we should have when going through affliction. With people watching us, nations watching us, the heathen raging, and our own people hating us, these are the prayers that need to come out of our spirit. Stay in the spirit.
