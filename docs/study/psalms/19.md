Read the chapter → [Psalms 19](/bible/psalms/19)

This session covers [Psalms 19](/bible/psalms/19) through 22, the last four chapters of the day's reading. It opens with the gospel going out to all the earth ([Psalm 19](/bible/psalms/19)), digs deep into a basic precept about the law being perfect and converting the soul, moves through cleansing from secret and presumptuous sins, then into the Lord's deliverance and the destruction of the enemy (Psalms 20-21), and closes with [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22), a prophecy fulfilled in Christ on the cross. We picked up out of Ezekiel where we left off before turning back to Psalms.

**[Ezekiel 47:2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v2)</sup> Then brought he me out of the way of the gate northward, and led me about the way without unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward; and, behold, there ran out waters on the right side.

- The man with the line went forth eastward and measured, and the waters that ran out on the right side going out from the temple is the gospel going out, the man with the line measuring your understanding of the scriptures.
  - **[Ezekiel 47:3](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)</sup> And when the man that had the line in his hand went forth eastward, he measured a thousand cubits, and he brought me through the waters; the waters were to the ankles.

    He measured a thousand cubits and brought me through the waters, and the waters were to the ankles; not only is the gospel going out, your understanding is being measured.

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/19#v4)</sup> Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,

- The line is gone out through all the earth, the gospel going out to the end of the world, and in them he set a tabernacle for the sun.
  - **[Isaiah 62:5](/bible/isaiah/62#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/isaiah/62#v5)</sup> For as a young man marrieth a virgin, so shall thy sons marry thee: and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride, so shall thy God rejoice over thee.

    As a young man marrieth a virgin so shall thy sons marry thee, and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride so shall thy God rejoice over thee; the gospel is about us being joined back to Christ.

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/19#v6)</sup> His going forth is from the end of the heaven, and his circuit unto the ends of it: and there is nothing hid from the heat thereof.

**[Psalm 19:7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

- The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul; the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple. We hear this scripture all the time, so we are going to dig into it a little while we do these chapters.
- The law of the Lord is perfect means going into perfection with our lives.
- The law of the Lord converting the soul is what changes us. You do not just up and decide to follow the Bible; it takes conversion.
- The testimony of the Lord is sure because the prophecies in this Bible are sure; we have a more sure word of prophecy. Did we not go into slavery on ships like the Most High declared through Moses in [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28)? We are living proof that the Bible is real.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 34:8](/bible/sirach/34#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/34#v8)</sup> The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.

    The law is perfect, taking us into perfection with our lives.
  - **[Jeremiah 13:23](/bible/jeremiah/13#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/13#v23)</sup> Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil.

    Can the Ethiopian change his skin or the leopard his spots? The Ethiopians are the dark nations of people; change does not just up and happen, so neither can you do good who are accustomed to do evil.
  - **[2 Esdras 4:2](/bible/2-esdras/4#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-esdras/4#v2)</sup> And said, Thy heart hath gone to far in this world, and thinkest thou to comprehend the way of the most High?

    The angel tells Ezra his mind is gone too far in the world, so his thoughts are not our thoughts.
  - **[Psalm 119:9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

    Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed thereto according to thy word; that is how we are converted and changed.
  - **[Matthew 18:3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)</sup> And said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.

    Except ye be converted and become as little children ye shall not enter the kingdom, because a child is teachable and will not argue back when it is time to learn.
  - **[Matthew 18:4](/bible/matthew/18#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/18#v4)</sup> Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven.

    Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven; we must humble ourselves as little children.

- The testimony of the Lord is sure; the testimonies and prophecies in this Bible are sure.
  - **[2 Peter 1:19](/bible/2-peter/1#v19)**

    We have also a more sure word of prophecy; the testimony of the Lord is sure.
  - **[Numbers 23:19](/bible/numbers/23#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/numbers/23#v19)</sup> God is not a man, that he should lie; neither the son of man, that he should repent: hath he said, and shall he not do it? or hath he spoken, and shall he not make it good?

    God is not a man that he should lie; hath he said, and shall he not do it? This is why the testimony is so sure, because he will perform his word.
  - **[Isaiah 46:10](/bible/isaiah/46#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/46#v10)</sup> Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure:

    Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done; that is what prophecy is, and we have a sure word of it.

**[Psalm 19:7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

- Making wise the simple: pay attention to who the simple is, the scorner, the foolish, the youth void of understanding. A lot of times we go out and think the scorner is somebody who does not want to hear, but it also goes into a youth who thinks he knows something but really does not.
  - **[Proverbs 19:25](/bible/proverbs/19#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/19#v25)</sup> Smite a scorner, and the simple will beware: and reprove one that hath understanding, and he will understand knowledge.

    Smite a scorner and the simple will beware; the scorner is called simple, and these words are synonymous.
  - **[Proverbs 21:11](/bible/proverbs/21#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/21#v11)</sup> When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

    When the scorner is punished the simple is made wise; that is making wise the simple.
  - **[Proverbs 1:22](/bible/proverbs/1#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/1#v22)</sup> How long, ye simple ones, will ye love simplicity? and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge?

    How long ye simple ones will ye love simplicity, and the scorners delight in their scorning, and fools hate knowledge; simple, scorner and fool are synonyms.
  - **[Proverbs 1:7](/bible/proverbs/1#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/1#v7)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge: but fools despise wisdom and instruction.

    The fear of the Lord is the beginning of knowledge, but fools despise wisdom and instruction.
  - **[Proverbs 7:6-7](/bible/proverbs/7#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/7#v6)</sup> For at the window of my house I looked through my casement,
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/7#v7)</sup> And beheld among the simple ones, I discerned among the youths, a young man void of understanding,

    He looked through his casement and beheld among the simple ones a young man void of understanding; simple means you are void of understanding, the youth.
  - **[Romans 2:20](/bible/romans/2#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/romans/2#v20)</sup> An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes, which hast the form of knowledge and of the truth in the law.

    An instructor of the foolish, a teacher of babes; there go the synonyms, foolish and babes and youth, so we must come back as a baby knowing we do not know anything.

**[Psalm 19:8](/bible/psalms/19#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/19#v8)</sup> The statutes of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart: the commandment of the Lord is pure, enlightening the eyes.

- The statutes of the Lord are right, rejoicing the heart; the commandment of the Lord is pure, enlightening the eyes. When the commandments enlighten your eyes you know where to walk, you will not stumble, you stay focused on keeping God's laws.
  - **[Matthew 6:22-23](/bible/matthew/6#v22)**

    The light of the body is the eye; if thine eye be single thy whole body is full of light, but if thine eye be evil thy body is full of darkness; keep the commandment as the apple of your eye.
  - **[Proverbs 7:2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

    Keep my commandments and live, and my law as the apple of thine eye.
  - **[Psalm 119:105](/bible/psalms/119#v105)**
    > <sup>[105](/bible/psalms/119#v105)</sup> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

    Thy word is a lamp unto my feet and a light unto my path; the word enlightens your eyes and becomes a light to navigate life.

  - **[Job 32:7-9](/bible/job/32#v7)**

    > <sup>[8](/bible/job/32#v8)</sup> But there is a spirit in man: and the inspiration of the Almighty giveth them understanding.

    Days should speak and multitude of years should teach wisdom, but great men are not always wise, neither do the aged understand judgment; the multitude of years should have taught our people wisdom about the judgments, but they do not have it.

**[Psalm 19:12](/bible/psalms/19#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/19#v12)</sup> Who can understand his errors? cleanse thou me from secret faults.

- Who can understand his errors? Cleanse thou me from secret faults. This should be your reason to be in this walk, not to appear righteous, but because God shall bring every work into judgment; work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.
  - **[Psalm 139:23-24](/bible/psalms/139#v23)**

    > <sup>[12](/bible/philippians/2#v12)</sup> Wherefore, my beloved, as ye have always obeyed, not as in my presence only, but now much more in my absence, work out your own salvation with fear and trembling.

    Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, now much more in his absence; this goes into cleansing you from secret faults.
  - **[Psalm 4:4](/bible/psalms/4#v4)**

  - **[1 Corinthians 16:15](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v15)</sup> I beseech you, brethren, (ye know the house of Stephanas, that it is the firstfruits of Achaia, and that they have addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints,)

    The house of Stephanas addicted themselves to the ministry of the saints; studying in your alone time should become an addiction, a righteous addiction that replaces idleness.

**[Psalm 19:13](/bible/psalms/19#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/19#v13)</sup> Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me: then shall I be upright, and I shall be innocent from the great transgression.

- Keep back thy servant also from presumptuous sins; let them not have dominion over me; then shall I be upright and innocent from the great transgression. A sin addiction has to be replaced with a righteous addiction, because change does not just up and happen when you are accustomed to doing evil.
