**[Psalms 105:1-5](/bible/psalms/105#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/105#v1)</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; call upon his name: make known his deeds among the people.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/105#v2)</sup> Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him: talk ye of all his wondrous works.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/105#v3)</sup> Glory ye in his holy name: let the heart of them rejoice that seek the Lord.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/105#v4)</sup> Seek the Lord, and his strength: seek his face evermore.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/105#v5)</sup> Remember his marvellous works that he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;

- Give thanks unto the Most High, call upon His name, and make known His deeds among the people.
- Sing unto Him, glory in His holy name, and let the heart of them that seek Him rejoice; seek His face forevermore.
- Remember His marvelous works, His wonders, and the judgments of His mouth. The Bible tells us to remember our past.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Whatever was written before was written for our learning, that we might have hope.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.

    Stand in the ways and ask for the old paths and walk therein; but they said we will not.

**[Psalms 105:6-11](/bible/psalms/105#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/105#v6)</sup> O ye seed of Abraham his servant, ye children of Jacob his chosen.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/105#v7)</sup> He is the Lord our God: his judgments are in all the earth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/105#v8)</sup> He hath remembered his covenant for ever, the word which he commanded to a thousand generations.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/105#v9)</sup> Which covenant he made with Abraham, and his oath unto Isaac;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/105#v10)</sup> And confirmed the same unto Jacob for a law, and to Israel for an everlasting covenant:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/105#v11)</sup> Saying, Unto thee will I give the land of Canaan, the lot of your inheritance:

- O seed of Abraham and children of Jacob His chosen: the Most High is our God and His judgments are in all the earth.
- He has remembered His covenant forever, the word commanded to a thousand generations, made with Abraham, sworn to Isaac, confirmed to Jacob for a law and to Israel for an everlasting covenant.
- If Israel was to have an everlasting covenant, then the new covenant cannot suddenly involve all people. You must ask these questions of anyone who does not use the scriptures.

- The king loosed him and made him lord of his house and ruler of his substance, to bind his princes and teach his senators wisdom. Our forefather Joseph taught the Egyptians; those who boast in Egyptology forget our forefathers taught them.

**[Psalms 105:23-36](/bible/psalms/105#v23)**
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/105#v23)</sup> Israel also came into Egypt; and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/105#v24)</sup> And he increased his people greatly; and made them stronger than their enemies.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/105#v25)</sup> He turned their heart to hate his people, to deal subtilly with his servants.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/105#v26)</sup> He sent Moses his servant; and Aaron whom he had chosen.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/105#v27)</sup> They shewed his signs among them, and wonders in the land of Ham.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/105#v28)</sup> He sent darkness, and made it dark; and they rebelled not against his word.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/105#v29)</sup> He turned their waters into blood, and slew their fish.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/105#v30)</sup> Their land brought forth frogs in abundance, in the chambers of their kings.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/105#v31)</sup> He spake, and there came divers sorts of flies, and lice in all their coasts.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/105#v32)</sup> He gave them hail for rain, and flaming fire in their land.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/105#v33)</sup> He smote their vines also and their fig trees; and brake the trees of their coasts.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/105#v34)</sup> He spake, and the locusts came, and caterpillers, and that without number,
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/105#v35)</sup> And did eat up all the herbs in their land, and devoured the fruit of their ground.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/105#v36)</sup> He smote also all the firstborn in their land, the chief of all their strength.

- Israel came into Egypt and Jacob sojourned in the land of Ham; He increased His people and made them stronger than their enemies, then turned the Egyptians' hearts to hate them.
- He sent Moses and Aaron who showed His signs and wonders in the land of Ham; He recounts the plagues of darkness, blood, frogs, flies, lice, hail, fire, and locusts.
- The locusts and caterpillars devoured all the herbs and fruit of the ground; this is the devourer, and the Most High promises to rebuke the devourer for the sake of a righteous nation.
- He smote all the firstborn, the chief of all their strength. The firstborn is the chief of one's strength, as Reuben was Jacob's strength before he lost it.

  - **[Malachi 3:8-11](/bible/malachi/3#v8)**

    Coupled with Malachi, the tithe was tied to the Levitical priesthood; our duty now is alms.

**[Psalms 105:37-45](/bible/psalms/105#v37)**
> <sup>[37](/bible/psalms/105#v37)</sup> He brought them forth also with silver and gold: and there was not one feeble person among their tribes.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/psalms/105#v38)</sup> Egypt was glad when they departed: for the fear of them fell upon them.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/psalms/105#v39)</sup> He spread a cloud for a covering; and fire to give light in the night.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/psalms/105#v40)</sup> The people asked, and he brought quails, and satisfied them with the bread of heaven.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/psalms/105#v41)</sup> He opened the rock, and the waters gushed out; they ran in the dry places like a river.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/psalms/105#v42)</sup> For he remembered his holy promise, and Abraham his servant.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/psalms/105#v43)</sup> And he brought forth his people with joy, and his chosen with gladness:
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/psalms/105#v44)</sup> And gave them the lands of the heathen: and they inherited the labour of the people;
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/psalms/105#v45)</sup> That they might observe his statutes, and keep his laws. Praise ye the Lord.

- He brought Israel out with silver and gold, and there was not one feeble person among the tribes; look at the sicknesses we suffer now under the curses.
- He spread a cloud for a covering and fire for light, brought quails and the bread of heaven, and opened the rock so the waters gushed out in the dry place.
- He gave them the lands of the heathen and the labor of the people, that they might keep His statutes and laws. But we were not thankful, we went into idolatry, and now we are scattered in Babylon in America.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:58-60](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v58)**
    > <sup>[58](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v58)</sup> If thou wilt not observe to do all the words of this law that are written in this book, that thou mayest fear this glorious and fearful name, THE LORD THY GOD;
    >
    > <sup>[59](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v59)</sup> Then the Lord will make thy plagues wonderful, and the plagues of thy seed, even great plagues, and of long continuance, and sore sicknesses, and of long continuance.
    >
    > <sup>[60](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v60)</sup> Moreover he will bring upon thee all the diseases of Egypt, which thou wast afraid of; and they shall cleave unto thee.

    Disobedience brings the diseases of Egypt to cleave unto us, such as we suffer today.
  - **[Joel 3:10](/bible/joel/3#v10); 2:8**  *10 Beat your plowshares into swords, and your pruninghooks into spears: let the weak say, I am strong.*  When Christ returns even the feeble of us will be like David and fall on the sword unhurt.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:47](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v47)</sup> Because thou servedst not the Lord thy God with joyfulness, and with gladness of heart, for the abundance of all things;

    Because we served not the Most High with joyfulness for the good given us, these curses came; remember why we are here.
