Read the chapter → [Psalms 35](/bible/psalms/35)

This session covers [Psalms 35](/bible/psalms/35) through 38. A lot of Psalms was written by David, and a lot of Psalms is prophecy, with Christ speaking to us through many of them. If you know how to navigate the book of Psalms, you can slice up the so-called Christians who carry a Bible with just Psalms, Proverbs, and the New Testament. You can show them the Most High is only for Israel, who the law was given to, and who salvation is for. Learn to wield this sword.

**[Psalms 35:1-8](/bible/psalms/35#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/35#v1)</sup> Plead my cause, O Lord, with them that strive with me: fight against them that fight against me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/35#v2)</sup> Take hold of shield and buckler, and stand up for mine help.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/35#v3)</sup> Draw out also the spear, and stop the way against them that persecute me: say unto my soul, I am thy salvation.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/35#v4)</sup> Let them be confounded and put to shame that seek after my soul: let them be turned back and brought to confusion that devise my hurt.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/35#v5)</sup> Let them be as chaff before the wind: and let the angel of the Lord chase them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/35#v6)</sup> Let their way be dark and slippery: and let the angel of the Lord persecute them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/35#v7)</sup> For without cause have they hid for me their net in a pit, which without cause they have digged for my soul.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/35#v8)</sup> Let destruction come upon him at unawares; and let his net that he hath hid catch himself: into that very destruction let him fall.

- This is the prayer that we all have, especially us as Israel. We want the Most High to fight against those who fight against us. That is a righteous prayer that our forefather David had.
- This is the prayer we have for everybody that comes against us. If you do not feel like that, then you do not believe in the Bible.
- David is praying that the same destruction his enemies want to bring on him falls on them instead. That is what we have to understand: the Most High is not a god of saving everybody, he is a god of destroying our enemies. He has been that way and it is not going to change.

**[Psalms 35:9-10](/bible/psalms/35#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/35#v9)</sup> And my soul shall be joyful in the Lord: it shall rejoice in his salvation.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/35#v10)</sup> All my bones shall say, Lord, who is like unto thee, which deliverest the poor from him that is too strong for him, yea, the poor and the needy from him that spoileth him?

- The salvation David rejoices in is the destruction of his enemies. That is what salvation is. You cannot be saved if you still have to answer to your enemies. You do not save the wolf and the sheep together.
- They just came out with more footage about Ahmaud Arbery saying the brother was still alive when the police came, and the one who shot him admitted it, and the police still let them go home. These are the things we have to pray for. That is the salvation we are looking for. We do not worship a God that saves our enemies alongside us.
- No matter what we think, Esau is too strong for us right now. He owns a military. Black people do not own a military. You may be able to fight one and win, but we do not own a military. We are behind enemy lines. They took over this country and we still have not taken it back. The Most High is the only one who is going to save us from that one who is stronger than us. That is what David prophesied about the nation of Israel.

**[Psalms 35:11-16](/bible/psalms/35#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/35#v11)</sup> False witnesses did rise up; they laid to my charge things that I knew not.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/35#v12)</sup> They rewarded me evil for good to the spoiling of my soul.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/35#v13)</sup> But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth: I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer returned into mine own bosom.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/35#v14)</sup> I behaved myself as though he had been my friend or brother: I bowed down heavily, as one that mourneth for his mother.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/35#v15)</sup> But in mine adversity they rejoiced, and gathered themselves together: yea, the abjects gathered themselves together against me, and I knew it not; they did tear me, and ceased not:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/35#v16)</sup> With hypocritical mockers in feasts, they gnashed upon me with their teeth.

- False witnesses rise up and lay to our charge things we knew not. That happens with us right now. You have our own brothers claiming we are with the Rosicrucians, sold out to the Illuminati, that we wear purple because we had to choose a color. The most racist people in the world are not going to recruit us. We have our own money; we gave out gold coins worth a million dollars at the 2015 or 2016 Passover.
- They rewarded evil for good, but when they were sick, David's clothing was sackcloth and he humbled his soul with fasting. That is praying for your enemies and for those that despitefully use and persecute you. You do not go railing for railing. You defend yourself, but you do not turn around and do something wicked back to them. When they were down, David felt their pain just as they had felt it. That is how we have to move.
- David bowed down heavily as one that mourns for his mother, but in his adversity they rejoiced and gathered themselves together against him.
- When our school got hit by a tornado, people on Facebook and all social media were rejoicing, saying all praises to the Lord that a tornado hit our school. But those same people, when they needed help, we were there. A lot of it we did not say anything about, because this is the prayer we had: let the Most High look on it and see how we move and how they move, and let him judge that.
- Our feast days are full of hypocrites, people who do not have the betterment of their people at heart. They come to the feast days, the new moons, the sabbaths, and sit there wicked, crept in unawares. It always happens. This is what David was talking about when he spoke of hypocritical mockers in feast.

**[Psalms 35:17-21](/bible/psalms/35#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/35#v17)</sup> Lord, how long wilt thou look on? rescue my soul from their destructions, my darling from the lions.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/35#v18)</sup> I will give thee thanks in the great congregation: I will praise thee among much people.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/35#v19)</sup> Let not them that are mine enemies wrongfully rejoice over me: neither let them wink with the eye that hate me without a cause.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/35#v20)</sup> For they speak not peace: but they devise deceitful matters against them that are quiet in the land.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/35#v21)</sup> Yea, they opened their mouth wide against me, and said, Aha, aha, our eye hath seen it.

- David cries out asking how long the Most High will look on, and asks him to rescue his soul from their destructions.
- He asks that his enemies not wrongfully rejoice over him nor wink with the eye that hate him without a cause.
- When we fall or do something, they point and mock, "Aha, aha, I have seen it." No matter what happens, you have to keep the commandments of the Most High and keep your faith in him. When everybody is against you, keep your faith. That is the example David set.

**[Psalms 35:22-28](/bible/psalms/35#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/35#v22)</sup> This thou hast seen, O Lord: keep not silence: O Lord, be not far from me.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/35#v23)</sup> Stir up thyself, and awake to my judgment, even unto my cause, my God and my Lord.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/35#v24)</sup> Judge me, O Lord my God, according to thy righteousness; and let them not rejoice over me.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/35#v25)</sup> Let them not say in their hearts, Ah, so would we have it: let them not say, We have swallowed him up.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/35#v26)</sup> Let them be ashamed and brought to confusion together that rejoice at mine hurt: let them be clothed with shame and dishonour that magnify themselves against me.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/35#v27)</sup> Let them shout for joy, and be glad, that favour my righteous cause: yea, let them say continually, Let the Lord be magnified, which hath pleasure in the prosperity of his servant.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/35#v28)</sup> And my tongue shall speak of thy righteousness and of thy praise all the day long.

- David asks the Most High not to keep silence, to stir up himself and awake to his judgment, and to judge him according to his righteousness.
- He prays that his enemies not say in their hearts, "We would have had it," or "We have swallowed him up," but that they be brought to confusion.
- There are people not in the body who see what we go through and pray the best for us. Brothers from a lot of different backgrounds saw what we went through as a body and said they pray the best for us. That is a righteous thing to have. But the ones who do not, the prayer is to let them be brought to confusion, along with all the stuff they talk all the time.

# Psalm 36: The Transgression of the Wicked

Read the chapter → [Psalms 36](/bible/psalms/36)

**[Psalms 36:1-4](/bible/psalms/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/36#v1)</sup> The transgression of the wicked saith within my heart, that there is no fear of God before his eyes.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/36#v2)</sup> For he flattereth himself in his own eyes, until his iniquity be found to be hateful.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/36#v3)</sup> The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit: he hath left off to be wise, and to do good.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/36#v4)</sup> He deviseth mischief upon his bed; he setteth himself in a way that is not good; he abhorreth not evil.

- The transgression of the wicked says there is no fear of God before his eyes, for he flatters himself in his own eyes. He boasts himself up: nobody could take me down.
  - **[Psalms 10:3](/bible/psalms/10#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/10#v3)</sup> For the wicked boasteth of his heart’s desire, and blesseth the covetous, whom the Lord abhorreth.

    The wicked boasts of his heart's desire and blesses the covetous, whom the Most High abhors, and will not seek after God.
  - **[Malachi 1:4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)</sup> Whereas Edom saith, We are impoverished, but we will return and build the desolate places; thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall build, but I will throw down; and they shall call them, The border of wickedness, and, The people against whom the Lord hath indignation for ever.

    Edom is the border of wickedness, the beginning and ending of all wickedness; a border is where something starts and where it ends, and that border of wickedness is Esau, who is the wicked one doing all this boasting.
- The wicked one devises mischief upon his bed, sets himself in a way that is not good, and abhors not evil. Esau does this all the time; they devise iniquity on their beds.
- The wickedness of this nation gets worse and worse. People who have changed what the Most High made them are now over the educational system, showing children sexual things they are not even supposed to know exist. Yet those same children they say are not fit to choose which parent they want to be with; they put up laws and red tape to keep a child from the parent they want to be around.
  - **[Micah 2:1](/bible/micah/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/micah/2#v1)</sup> Woe to them that devise iniquity, and work evil upon their beds! when the morning is light, they practise it, because it is in the power of their hand.

    Woe to them that devise iniquity and work evil upon their beds, and when the morning is light they practice it, because it is in the power of their hand.
- While we are sleeping, they are up thinking how to take us down, whether it be our people or the wicked Esau of the other nations. While we do the work and study the word, they are typing on Facebook figuring out how to take us down. That is why when Christ said we would be hated of all nations, he meant all nations, including our own nation.

**[Psalms 36:5-9](/bible/psalms/36#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/36#v5)</sup> Thy mercy, O Lord, is in the heavens; and thy faithfulness reacheth unto the clouds.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/36#v6)</sup> Thy righteousness is like the great mountains; thy judgments are a great deep: O Lord, thou preservest man and beast.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/36#v7)</sup> How excellent is thy lovingkindness, O God! therefore the children of men put their trust under the shadow of thy wings.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/36#v8)</sup> They shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of thy house; and thou shalt make them drink of the river of thy pleasures.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/36#v9)</sup> For with thee is the fountain of life: in thy light shall we see light.

- The Most High's mercy is in the heavens and his faithfulness reaches to the clouds; his righteousness is like the great mountains and his judgments a great deep.
- We cannot take anything we do for granted or get high-minded in this truth and think we cannot be touched. Remember this one thing above all.
  - **[Ezra 9:13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)</sup> And after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for our great trespass, seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this;

    After all our evil deeds and great trespass, the Most High punished us less than our iniquity deserved and gave us such deliverance as this; we deserve far worse, but because of his loving kindness he punished us less.
- Because of his loving kindness, they shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of his house and drink of the river of his pleasures, for with him is the fountain of life and in his light we shall see light.
  - **[Revelation 21:6](/bible/revelation/21#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/21#v6)</sup> And he said unto me, It is done. I am Alpha and Omega, the beginning and the end. I will give unto him that is athirst of the fountain of the water of life freely.

    He that is athirst is given of the fountain of the water of life freely, talking about us getting the kingdom and eternal life; he that overcometh shall inherit all things.
- If we keep the commandments, we are going to see that kingdom of heaven and the fatness of his house. That is why Paul says to set your affection on things above.

**[Psalms 36:10-12](/bible/psalms/36#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/36#v10)</sup> O continue thy lovingkindness unto them that know thee; and thy righteousness to the upright in heart.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/36#v11)</sup> Let not the foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of the wicked remove me.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/36#v12)</sup> There are the workers of iniquity fallen: they are cast down, and shall not be able to rise.

- David asks that the Most High continue his loving kindness to them that know him, and that the foot of pride and the hand of the wicked not move him.
- When we keep the commandments, those who devise wickedness against us are going to get cast down, whether it be another nation or one of our own people. If you come against the work of the Most High, you are fighting against him, not against the man you see with your face.
  - **[Acts 5:38-39](/bible/acts/5#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/5#v38)</sup> And now I say unto you, Refrain from these men, and let them alone: for if this counsel or this work be of men, it will come to nought:
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/5#v39)</sup> But if it be of God, ye cannot overthrow it; lest haply ye be found even to fight against God.

    Refrain from these men and let them alone, for if this work be of men it will come to naught, but if it be of God you cannot overthrow it, lest you be found to fight against God.
- When we keep the commandments, like Deacon Yawasap says, the angels get activated, and when the angels get activated our enemies get the destruction coming to them, no matter how much they have.

# Psalm 37: Fret Not Because of Evildoers

Read the chapter → [Psalms 37](/bible/psalms/37)

**[Psalms 37:1-3](/bible/psalms/37#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/37#v1)</sup> Fret not thyself because of evildoers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/37#v2)</sup> For they shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither as the green herb.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/37#v3)</sup> Trust in the Lord, and do good; so shalt thou dwell in the land, and verily thou shalt be fed.

- This chapter hit home. Coming into this truth, David saw where his life was versus where other people's lives were and what they were doing, and it started to mess with his spirit a little. But reading things like this gave the strength to keep going.
- Do not fret because of evildoers nor be envious against the workers of iniquity, for they shall soon be cut down like the grass.
  - **[Psalms 73:3](/bible/psalms/73#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/73#v3)</sup> For I was envious at the foolish, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

    I was envious at the foolish when I saw the prosperity of the wicked, for there are no bands in their death and their strength is firm; pride and violence cover them.
- When I first came into the truth I would look at the wicked of our people and of the other nations, with no God and no laws, and they were prospering with a bunch of material things. That is why the scripture says do not fret or be envious of them.

**[Psalms 37:4-6](/bible/psalms/37#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/37#v4)</sup> Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/37#v5)</sup> Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/37#v6)</sup> And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday.

- Delight thyself in the Most High and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart; commit thy way unto him and he shall bring it to pass.
  - **[1 John 5:14](/bible/1-john/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/1-john/5#v14)</sup> And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us:

    This is the confidence we have in him, that if we ask anything according to his will he hears us; ask according to his will, not to stumble somebody or to boast on social media, but to serve the nation.
  - **[James 4:3](/bible/james/4#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/james/4#v3)</sup> Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts.

    Ye ask and receive not because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts; do not pray for enemies to fall so you can stunt on them, but so you can continue to do his will uninterrupted.
- When you delight yourself in the Most High by keeping the commandments and trusting him, he brings it to pass. If you are always doubting, or asking for stuff without believing in him, it is not going to happen. That is why we have to continually meditate in the word day and night.

**[Psalms 37:7-11](/bible/psalms/37#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/37#v7)</sup> Rest in the Lord, and wait patiently for him: fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/37#v8)</sup> Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself in any wise to do evil.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/37#v9)</sup> For evildoers shall be cut off: but those that wait upon the Lord, they shall inherit the earth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/37#v10)</sup> For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/37#v11)</sup> But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.

- Rest in the Most High and wait patiently for him; fret not because of the man who brings wicked devices to pass. Cease from anger and forsake wrath.
- Everything happens on the Most High's time. You may not get it right now because you are not ready for it. Sisters and brothers may be asking for a husband or wife, but if you are not ready he will make you wait; wait for it and you may come out with a beautiful marriage, but if you are impatient you get a back door marriage because you are not trusting in him. Whenever we do something outside the counsel given by godly men, we are not trusting in the Most High.
- For evildoers shall be cut off, but those that wait upon the Most High shall inherit the earth. We put the Most High through drama to give this earth away, so we have to go through some drama to get it back.
- For yet a little while and the wicked shall not be; you shall diligently consider his place and it shall not be.
  - **[Job 20:5-8](/bible/job/20#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/job/20#v5)</sup> That the triumphing of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment?
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/job/20#v6)</sup> Though his excellency mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds;
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/job/20#v7)</sup> Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung: they which have seen him shall say, Where is he?
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/job/20#v8)</sup> He shall fly away as a dream, and shall not be found: yea, he shall be chased away as a vision of the night.

    The triumphing of the wicked is short and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment; though his excellency mount up to the heavens, he shall perish forever like his own dung and be chased away as a vision of the night.
- These wicked people running this earth are not going to be here forever. It will come a time when they are gone forever, and we will wake up as from a bad dream, hardly believing our slave was ruler over a whole country.

**[Psalms 37:12-15](/bible/psalms/37#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/37#v12)</sup> The wicked plotteth against the just, and gnasheth upon him with his teeth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/37#v13)</sup> The Lord shall laugh at him: for he seeth that his day is coming.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/37#v14)</sup> The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, and to slay such as be of upright conversation.

> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/37#v15)</sup> Their sword shall enter into their own heart, and their bows shall be broken.

Nobody can answer who else we would be or show the historical facts; not Nubian, not Egyptian. There is too much history in Kings and Bishop's work, straight out of history books written not by our people, most of it while we were still in slavery. They knew who we were.
- They say we are a hate group because I denounced the label of African-American and went back to my history. Their sword shall enter their own heart and their bows shall be broken.

**[Psalms 37:16-20](/bible/psalms/37#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/37#v16)</sup> A little that a righteous man hath is better than the riches of many wicked.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/37#v17)</sup> For the arms of the wicked shall be broken: but the Lord upholdeth the righteous.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/37#v18)</sup> The Lord knoweth the days of the upright: and their inheritance shall be for ever.

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/37#v19)</sup> They shall not be ashamed in the evil time: and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.

> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/37#v20)</sup> But the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the Lord shall be as the fat of lambs: they shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away.

- But the wicked shall perish and the enemies of the Most High shall be as the fat of lambs; they shall consume into smoke.
  - **[Zephaniah 1:8](/bible/zephaniah/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/zephaniah/1#v8)</sup> And it shall come to pass in the day of the Lord’s sacrifice, that I will punish the princes, and the king’s children, and all such as are clothed with strange apparel.

    In the day of the Lord's sacrifice he will punish the princes and the king's children and all such as are clothed with strange apparel; strange apparel goes against what the Bible says, like women in pants.

**[Psalms 37:21-26](/bible/psalms/37#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/37#v21)</sup> The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/37#v22)</sup> For such as be blessed of him shall inherit the earth; and they that be cursed of him shall be cut off.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/37#v23)</sup> The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/37#v24)</sup> Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the Lord upholdeth him with his hand.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/37#v25)</sup> I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/37#v26)</sup> He is ever merciful, and lendeth; and his seed is blessed.

- The wicked borrows and pays not again, but the righteous shows mercy and gives. That is those people who borrow and then shut their hand when it comes time to pay. That is not the way we are supposed to be as a people.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 4:31](/bible/sirach/4#v31)**

# Psalm 38: David's Godly Sorrow

**[Psalms 38:1-7](/bible/psalms/38#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/38#v1)</sup> O Lord, rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/38#v2)</sup> For thine arrows stick fast in me, and thy hand presseth me sore.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/38#v3)</sup> There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine anger; neither is there any rest in my bones because of my sin.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/38#v4)</sup> For mine iniquities are gone over mine head: as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/38#v5)</sup> My wounds stink and are corrupt because of my foolishness.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/38#v6)</sup> I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; I go mourning all the day long.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/38#v7)</sup> For my loins are filled with a loathsome disease: and there is no soundness in my flesh.

- This is a chapter where David was going through some things. He asks the Most High not to rebuke him in wrath nor chasten him in hot displeasure, for the arrows stick fast in him and the hand presses him sore.
- There is no soundness in his flesh because of the anger, no rest in his bones because of his sin; his iniquities are gone over his head as a heavy burden.
- His wounds stink and are corrupt because of his foolishness; he is troubled, bowed down greatly, and goes mourning all day long, for his loins are filled with a loathsome disease.
- Given the loins filled with a loathsome disease and the wounds that stink and are corrupt, David had an ancient sexually transmitted disease. It may seem like he did not, but keep reading.

**[Psalms 38:8-14](/bible/psalms/38#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/38#v8)</sup> I am feeble and sore broken: I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/38#v9)</sup> Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning is not hid from thee.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/38#v10)</sup> My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/38#v11)</sup> My lovers and my friends stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/38#v12)</sup> They also that seek after my life lay snares for me: and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, and imagine deceits all the day long.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/38#v13)</sup> But I, as a deaf man, heard not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/38#v14)</sup> Thus I was as a man that heareth not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.

- David is feeble and sore broken and has roared by reason of the disquietness of his heart; all his desire is before the Most High and his groaning is not hid.
- His heart pants, his strength fails, and the light of his eyes is gone from him. His lovers and friends stand aloof from his sore and his kinsmen stand afar off. If the trouble is in his loins and even his kinsmen stand afar off from his sore, that tells you what it is. A lot of brothers and sisters need to read this; there is nothing new under the sun.
- They that seek after his life lay snares and speak mischievous things and imagine deceit all day long. When you get plagued like that and catch diseases because of foolish things you do, people start talking about you.
- But David was as a deaf man that heard not and a dumb man that opened not his mouth, in whose mouth are no reproofs. He could not even say anything back; everybody was talking bad about him but what could he say.

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/38#v19)</sup> But mine enemies are lively, and they are strong: and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.

- David is ready to halt and his sorrow is continually before him; he declares his iniquity and is sorry for his sin. That is David showing godly sorrow.
- His enemies are lively and strong, and they that hate him wrongfully are multiplied; they render evil for good and are his adversaries because he follows the thing that good is.
- He asks the Most High not to forsake him nor be far off, but to make haste to help him, his salvation. Though David caught some disease, he still showed godly sorrow and repentance.
