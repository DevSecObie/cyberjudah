**[Psalm 8:1-2](/bible/psalms/8#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/8#v1)</sup> O Lord our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth! who hast set thy glory above the heavens.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/8#v2)</sup> Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings hast thou ordained strength because of thine enemies, that thou mightest still the enemy and the avenger.

- The Most High's name is excellent in all the earth, and he set his glory above the heavens because he dwells in the third heaven.
- Out of the mouth of babes and sucklings he has ordained strength. This is why you see young men and young women rising up to teach the bible and change their lives. And "young" is relative: our ancestors lived to 900, Abraham had Isaac at 100 and Sarah at 90, so at 60 or 70 you are young, and we are all young in spirit.

**[Psalm 8:3-6](/bible/psalms/8#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/8#v3)</sup> When I consider thy heavens, the work of thy fingers, the moon and the stars, which thou hast ordained;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/8#v4)</sup> What is man, that thou art mindful of him? and the son of man, that thou visitest him?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/8#v5)</sup> For thou hast made him a little lower than the angels, and hast crowned him with glory and honour.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/8#v6)</sup> Thou madest him to have dominion over the works of thy hands; thou hast put all things under his feet:

- David considers the heavens, the moon, and the stars, and asks what man is that God is mindful of him. He is writing about the son of man made a little lower than the angels, crowned with glory and honor, given dominion over the works of God's hands.
- The so-called yaya israelites say the foolish thing that Jesus was an idol not prophesied in the Old Testament. But David is writing about a son of man made lower than the angels; that son of man is Jesus Christ, the black messiah.
- Christ had to be made lower than the angels so that he could die. In his angelic form he would be immortal; he had to come after the seed of Abraham, David, and Joseph, from sperm, so that he could taste death for every Israelite man.

**[Psalm 8:7-9](/bible/psalms/8#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/8#v7)</sup> All sheep and oxen, yea, and the beasts of the field;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/8#v8)</sup> The fowl of the air, and the fish of the sea, and whatsoever passeth through the paths of the seas.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/8#v9)</sup> O Lord our Lord, how excellent is thy name in all the earth!

- All sheep and oxen, the beasts of the field, the fowls of the air, and the fish of the sea are put under him; the Most High's name is excellent in all the earth. When Israelites say Christ is not in the Old Testament, ask when all things were ever given into the hands of David or Solomon; this dominion belongs to Christ.
