Read the chapter → [Psalms 87](/bible/psalms/87)

This session reads through [Psalms 87](/bible/psalms/87) through 90, four chapters a day for the nation. The teaching moves through the psalms mostly by reading, bringing out precepts along the way. [Psalm 87](/bible/psalms/87) speaks of the Most High hallowing Jerusalem, the city of God, whose foundation is in the holy mountains and whose gates the Lord loveth more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

**[Psalms 87:1-4](/bible/psalms/87#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/87#v1)</sup> His foundation is in the holy mountains.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/87#v2)</sup> The Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/87#v3)</sup> Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/87#v4)</sup> I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia; this man was born there.

- The foundation of the city is in the holy mountains, and the Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. This is about how the Most High hallowed Him a city, Jerusalem.
- Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. The Most High makes mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know Him, and of Philistia and Tyre with Ethiopia, saying this man was born there.

**[Psalms 87:5-7](/bible/psalms/87#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/87#v5)</sup> And of Zion it shall be said, This and that man was born in her: and the highest himself shall establish her.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/87#v6)</sup> The Lord shall count, when he writeth up the people, that this man was born there. Selah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/87#v7)</sup> As well the singers as the players on instruments shall be there: all my springs are in thee.

- Of Zion it shall be said that this and that man was born in her, and the Most High Himself shall establish her. The Lord shall count, when He writeth up the people, that this man was born there.
- As well the singers as the players of instruments shall be there; all the springs are in the Most High.

# Psalm 88: A Soul Full of Troubles

Read the chapter → [Psalms 88](/bible/psalms/88)

[Psalm 88](/bible/psalms/88) is a cry of a soul full of troubles, whose life draws near unto the grave. Much of it lines up with what was prophesied in [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22) concerning Christ, when the crucifixion was being foretold. The teaching shows this is prophecy of our forefathers and is relevant to us today.

**[Psalms 88:1-5](/bible/psalms/88#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/88#v1)</sup> O Lord God of my salvation, I have cried day and night before thee:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/88#v2)</sup> Let my prayer come before thee: incline thine ear unto my cry;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/88#v3)</sup> For my soul is full of troubles: and my life draweth nigh unto the grave.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/88#v4)</sup> I am counted with them that go down into the pit: I am as a man that hath no strength:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/88#v5)</sup> Free among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cut off from thy hand.

- The prayer cries day and night before the Most High, asking that his prayer come before Him and that He incline His ear unto his cry, for his soul is full of troubles and his life draws near unto the grave.
- He is counted with them that go down into the pit and is as a man that has no strength. This sounds like some of the same things said in [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22), when Christ was prophesying about what happened to Christ, the cross being prophesied.

**[Psalms 88:6-9](/bible/psalms/88#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/88#v6)</sup> Thou hast laid me in the lowest pit, in darkness, in the deeps.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/88#v7)</sup> Thy wrath lieth hard upon me, and thou hast afflicted me with all thy waves. Selah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/88#v8)</sup> Thou hast put away mine acquaintance far from me; thou hast made me an abomination unto them: I am shut up, and I cannot come forth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/88#v9)</sup> Mine eye mourneth by reason of affliction: Lord, I have called daily upon thee, I have stretched out my hands unto thee.

- The Most High has laid him in the lowest pit, in darkness, in the deep; His wrath lieth hard upon him and He has afflicted him with all His waves. He has put away his acquaintance far from him and made him an abomination unto them; he is shut up and cannot come forth.
- His eye mourneth by reason of affliction; he has called daily upon the Most High and stretched out his hands unto Him. When the Lord turns His face from us, a lot of this stuff is relevant to us today, like the dead bodies laying in the streets of the great city.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    In our afflictions we will seek Him early, so we cry daily to the Lord in repentance until He hear us.
  - **[1 Kings 8:47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;

    We cry daily to the Lord keeping the commandments and the faith in Christ until He hear us.

**[Psalms 88:10-12](/bible/psalms/88#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/88#v10)</sup> Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? shall the dead arise and praise thee? Selah.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/88#v11)</sup> Shall thy lovingkindness be declared in the grave? or thy faithfulness in destruction?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/88#v12)</sup> Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? and thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

- He asks whether the Most High will show wonders to the dead, whether the dead shall arise and praise Him, whether His lovingkindness shall be declared in the grave or His faithfulness in destruction.
- He asks whether His wonders shall be known in the dark and His righteousness in the land of forgetfulness. You can't serve God when you're dead.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 9:10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)</sup> Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.

    There is no work and no devices in the grave; you cannot serve God when you are dead.

**[Psalms 88:13-18](/bible/psalms/88#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/88#v13)</sup> But unto thee have I cried, O Lord; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/88#v14)</sup> Lord, why castest thou off my soul? why hidest thou thy face from me?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/88#v15)</sup> I am afflicted and ready to die from my youth up: while I suffer thy terrors I am distracted.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/88#v16)</sup> Thy fierce wrath goeth over me; thy terrors have cut me off.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/88#v17)</sup> They came round about me daily like water; they compassed me about together.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/88#v18)</sup> Lover and friend hast thou put far from me, and mine acquaintance into darkness.

- He cries unto the Most High, and in the morning his prayer shall prevent Him; he asks why the Lord casts off his soul and hides His face from him. He is afflicted, like Christ said, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani, my God why are you so far from me.
- He is afflicted and ready to die from his youth up, suffering the terrors of the Most High; His fierce wrath goes over him and His terrors have cut him off, like Christ was cut off from the land of the living.
- They came round about him daily like water, dogs have compassed him, like it says in [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22); lover and friend are put far from him and his acquaintance into darkness. These are the prophecies of our forefathers, and this is the real black history, the Most High declaring the end from the beginning to strengthen our faith.
  - **[Psalms 22:16](/bible/psalms/22#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/22#v16)</sup> For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet.

    For dogs have compassed me; they compassed Christ about as prophesied here.
  - **[Psalms 62:11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)</sup> God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God.

    The Lord gave the word; the forefathers wrote down what the Lord said through them.

# Psalm 89: The Covenant Made with David

Read the chapter → [Psalms 89](/bible/psalms/89)

[Psalm 89](/bible/psalms/89) sings of the mercies of the Lord forever and of the covenant the Most High made with David His servant, that his seed would be established forever and his throne built up to all generations. The teaching stresses that Christ is of the seed of David, and that the doctrines of a virgin birth denying this lineage are blasphemy against the Bible.

**[Psalms 89:1-4](/bible/psalms/89#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/89#v1)</sup> I will sing of the mercies of the Lord for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/89#v2)</sup> For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very heavens.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/89#v3)</sup> I have made a covenant with my chosen, I have sworn unto David my servant,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/89#v4)</sup> Thy seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Selah.

- He will sing of the mercies of the Lord forever, making known His faithfulness to all generations, for mercy shall be built up forever and His faithfulness established in the very heavens.
- The Most High has made a covenant with His chosen and sworn unto David His servant that his seed will be established forever and his throne built up to all generations. Because he was the seed of David, the doctrine of the virgin birth denying that lineage is blasphemy against the Bible, vain lies.
  - **[Psalms 132:11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)</sup> The Lord hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.

    The Lord has sworn in truth unto David, that of the fruit of his body He would set upon his throne.

**[Psalms 89:5-8](/bible/psalms/89#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/89#v5)</sup> And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O Lord: thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/89#v6)</sup> For who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord? who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto the Lord?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/89#v7)</sup> God is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence of all them that are about him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/89#v8)</sup> O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee?

- The heavens shall praise His wonders and His faithfulness in the congregation of the saints, for who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord, who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto Him.
- The Most High is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints and to be had in reverence of all them about Him; O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto Him, or to His faithfulness round about Him.

**[Psalms 89:9-13](/bible/psalms/89#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/89#v9)</sup> Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/89#v10)</sup> Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy strong arm.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/89#v11)</sup> The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: as for the world and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them.

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/89#v12)</sup> The north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/89#v13)</sup> Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand.

- The Most High rules the raging of the sea; when the waves arise, He stills them, like when Christ was on the boat and they said Lord you don't care if we perish, and he rebuked the storm, and they said what manner of man is this that even the sea obeys him.
- He has broken Rahab in pieces as one that is slain and scattered His enemies with His strong arm; the heavens are His and the earth also, the world and the fullness thereof He has founded.
- The north and the south He has created; Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in His name; He has a mighty arm, strong is His hand and high is His right hand.

**[Psalms 89:14-18](/bible/psalms/89#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/89#v14)</sup> Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.

> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/89#v15)</sup> Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.

- We go through things, but this should build up your faith; it is why it is so important for things to be done decent and in order, the order that God established.
- Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound; they shall walk in the light of His countenance. We are going to walk in the laws and be before the Lord blameless like Abraham and our forefathers were.

# Psalm 90: Teach Us to Number Our Days

Read the chapter → [Psalms 90](/bible/psalms/90)

[Psalm 90](/bible/psalms/90) is a prayer of Moses, the man of God, on the eternity of the Most High and the shortness of man's life. The Lord has been our dwelling place in all generations; from everlasting to everlasting He is God, while man is turned to destruction. The teaching draws out how the Most High has cut man's days short down to the present day.

**[Psalms 90:1-6](/bible/psalms/90#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/90#v1)</sup> Lord, thou hast been our dwelling place in all generations.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/90#v2)</sup> Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/90#v3)</sup> Thou turnest man to destruction; and sayest, Return, ye children of men.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/90#v4)</sup> For a thousand years in thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/90#v5)</sup> Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep: in the morning they are like grass which groweth up.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/90#v6)</sup> In the morning it flourisheth, and groweth up; in the evening it is cut down, and withereth.

- The Lord has been our dwelling place in all generations; before the mountains were brought forth, or ever He had formed the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting He is God.
- He turns man to destruction and says return, ye children of men; this is the circle of life, He destroys us, puts us through the grave, and regenerates the souls. A thousand years in His sight are but as yesterday when it is past.
- We are like grass which groweth up; in the morning it flourisheth and groweth up, in the evening it is cut down and withereth. Our youth is like a blade of grass, green and healthy, but the glory of it fades away.

**[Psalms 90:7-10](/bible/psalms/90#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/90#v7)</sup> For we are consumed by thine anger, and by thy wrath are we troubled.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/90#v8)</sup> Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/90#v9)</sup> For all our days are passed away in thy wrath: we spend our years as a tale that is told.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/90#v10)</sup> The days of our years are threescore years and ten; and if by reason of strength they be fourscore years, yet is their strength labour and sorrow; for it is soon cut off, and we fly away.

- We are consumed by His anger and troubled by His wrath; He has set our iniquities before Him, our secret sins in the light of His countenance. That is how the Lord can judge you; we don't get away.
- In the regeneration they shall live again, and then the works of the righteous and the works of the wicked shall be declared. All our days are passed away in His wrath; we spend our years as a tale that is told.
- The days of our years are threescore years and ten; a score is twenty, so three score and ten is seventy. At seventy you must give up the ghost, for the Lord's spirit will not always be striving with you.
  - **[Genesis 6:3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)</sup> And the Lord said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

    My spirit shall not always strive with man; his days shall be a hundred and twenty years, as He constantly cuts our days short.

**[Psalms 90:11-13](/bible/psalms/90#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/90#v11)</sup> Who knoweth the power of thine anger? even according to thy fear, so is thy wrath.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/90#v12)</sup> So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/90#v13)</sup> Return, O Lord, how long? and let it repent thee concerning thy servants.

- Who knoweth the power of His anger; even according to His fear, so is His wrath. He asks the Most High to teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
- Now we know we only have about seventy years, so we number our days and consider the end from the beginning, to be effective in our walk and use the time and gifts God gives us, because there are no devices in the grave.
- He asks the Most High to return, and how long, and to let it repent Him concerning His servants.

**[Psalms 90:14-17](/bible/psalms/90#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/90#v14)</sup> O satisfy us early with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/90#v15)</sup> Make us glad according to the days wherein thou hast afflicted us, and the years wherein we have seen evil.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/90#v16)</sup> Let thy work appear unto thy servants, and thy glory unto their children.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/90#v17)</sup> And let the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.

- He asks the Most High to satisfy us early with His mercy, that we may rejoice and be glad all our days, and to make us glad according to the days wherein He has afflicted us and the years wherein we have seen evil.
- He asks that His work appear unto His servants and His glory unto their children, that the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us, and that He establish the work of our hands. The Lord will give us according to the fruit of our doings.
