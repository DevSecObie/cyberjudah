**[Psalms 104:1-4](/bible/psalms/104#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/104#v1)</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honour and majesty.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/104#v2)</sup> Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/104#v3)</sup> Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/104#v4)</sup> Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire:

- David blesses the Most High, who is clothed with honor and majesty and covers Himself with light as with a garment.
- He stretches out the heavens like a curtain, makes the clouds His chariot, and walks upon the wings of the wind.
- He makes His angels spirits and His ministers a flaming fire.
  - **[Genesis 1:14](/bible/genesis/1#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/1#v14)</sup> And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years:

    The lights of heaven are for signs and seasons, days and years.

**[Psalms 104:5-9](/bible/psalms/104#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/104#v5)</sup> Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/104#v6)</sup> Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/104#v7)</sup> At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/104#v8)</sup> They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys unto the place which thou hast founded for them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/104#v9)</sup> Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth.

- He laid the foundations of the earth so it should not be removed forever.
- The waters once stood above the mountains, but at His rebuke they fled and were set within bounds they may not pass.
- Consider that little crystals of sand are set as a bound to hold back the waves of the sea; that is the genius and magnificence of the Most High.

**[Psalms 104:10-12](/bible/psalms/104#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/104#v10)</sup> He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/104#v11)</sup> They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/104#v12)</sup> By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches.

- He sends springs into the valleys to give drink to every beast of the field, and the wild asses quench their thirst.
- The fowls of the heaven have their habitation by them and sing among the branches.
- In the country the owls and birds sing before the sun rises to wake you; that is the joy of the Most High.

**[Psalms 104:13-15](/bible/psalms/104#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/104#v13)</sup> He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/104#v14)</sup> He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/104#v15)</sup> And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man’s heart.

- He waters the hills from His chambers and causes the grass to grow for the cattle and herb for the service of man, to bring forth food out of the earth.
- Note it carefully: herb for the service of man means food, not herb to smoke. Just as at Passover we eat lamb and bitter herbs, we do not smoke them.
- Wine makes glad the heart of man and oil makes his face to shine. This shows we are dark-skinned people, for oil is put on dry skin to make it shine, and the scriptures describe us.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 31:27-29](/bible/sirach/31#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/31#v27)</sup> Wine is as good as life to a man, if it be drunk moderately: what life is then to a man that is without wine? for it was made to make men glad.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/31#v28)</sup> Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind:
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/31#v29)</sup> But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind, with brawling and quarrelling.

    Wine drunk moderately is as good as life and makes men glad, but with excess it brings bitterness.

**[Psalms 104:16-23](/bible/psalms/104#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/104#v16)</sup> The trees of the Lord are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/104#v17)</sup> Where the birds make their nests: as for the stork, the fir trees are her house.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/104#v18)</sup> The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/104#v19)</sup> He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/104#v20)</sup> Thou makest darkness, and it is night: wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/104#v21)</sup> The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their meat from God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/104#v22)</sup> The sun ariseth, they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/104#v23)</sup> Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening.

- The trees of the Most High are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon are where the birds make their nests, and the high hills are a refuge for the wild goats.
- He appointed the moon for seasons and the sun knows its going down.
- He makes darkness and it is night, wherein the beasts of the forest and the young lions seek their meat from God; the night belongs to the beasts, the day belongs to man who goes to his labor until evening.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 43:6-7](/bible/sirach/43#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/43#v6)</sup> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/43#v7)</sup> From the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.

    The moon marks the seasons and the appointed feasts.

**[Psalms 104:24-30](/bible/psalms/104#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/104#v24)</sup> O Lord, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/104#v25)</sup> So is this great and wide sea, wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/104#v26)</sup> There go the ships: there is that leviathan, whom thou hast made to play therein.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/104#v27)</sup> These wait all upon thee; that thou mayest give them their meat in due season.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/104#v28)</sup> That thou givest them they gather: thou openest thine hand, they are filled with good.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/104#v29)</sup> Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled: thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/104#v30)</sup> Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created: and thou renewest the face of the earth.

- How manifold are His works; in wisdom He made them all, and the earth is full of His riches, including the great and wide sea teeming with creatures.
- All creatures wait upon Him for their meat in due season; when He opens His hand they are filled, when He hides His face they are troubled.
- When He takes away their breath they die and return to their dust; when He sends forth His spirit they are created and He renews the face of the earth.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 3:19-20](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v19)</sup> For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other; yea, they have all one breath; so that a man hath no preeminence above a beast: for all is vanity.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v20)</sup> All go unto one place; all are of the dust, and all turn to dust again.

    Man and beast have one departure; the body returns to dust, but our spirit returns to the Most High.

**[Psalms 104:31-35](/bible/psalms/104#v31)**
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/104#v31)</sup> The glory of the Lord shall endure for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/104#v32)</sup> He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth: he toucheth the hills, and they smoke.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/104#v33)</sup> I will sing unto the Lord as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/104#v34)</sup> My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the Lord.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/104#v35)</sup> Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the Lord, O my soul. Praise ye the Lord.

- The glory of the Most High endures forever; He looks on the earth and it trembles, He touches the hills and they smoke.
- David resolves to sing unto the Most High as long as he lives, and his meditation of Him shall be sweet.
- He prays the wicked be consumed from the earth, and again charges his soul to bless the Lord.
  - **[Psalms 137:1-4](/bible/psalms/137#v1)**

    >

  - **[Amos 5:23](/bible/amos/5#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/amos/5#v23)</sup> Take thou away from me the noise of thy songs; for I will not hear the melody of thy viols.

    As Israel repents and returns, righteous songs of praise are rising again in place of the songs of captivity.
