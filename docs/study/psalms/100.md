[Psalm 100](/bible/psalms/100) is a short call to worship: make a joyful noise, serve the Most High with gladness, and come before His presence with singing. The teaching ties His truth enduring to all generations to His laws, and His making us and not we ourselves to His forming us in the belly before we were born.

**[Psalms 100:1-3](/bible/psalms/100#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/100#v1)</sup> Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all ye lands.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/100#v2)</sup> Serve the Lord with gladness: come before his presence with singing.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/100#v3)</sup> Know ye that the Lord he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

- All lands are to make a joyful noise unto the Most High and serve Him with gladness, coming before His presence with singing.
- Know that the Most High is God; it is He that made us and not we ourselves. We are His people and the sheep of His pasture.
- That He made us and not we ourselves goes with the Most High forming us in the belly and knowing us before we were born.
  - **[Jeremiah 1:5](/bible/jeremiah/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/1#v5)</sup> Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations.

    The Most High knew us and formed us in the belly before we came forth.

**[Psalms 100:4-5](/bible/psalms/100#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/100#v4)</sup> Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/100#v5)</sup> For the Lord is good; his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations.

- Enter into His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; be thankful and bless His name.
- The Most High is good, His mercy is everlasting, and His truth endures to all generations. That truth is His laws, which endure to all generations.
  - **[Psalms 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    The righteousness of the Most High is an everlasting righteousness and His law is the truth.
