This session covers [Psalms 127](/bible/psalms/127) through 130, four psalms that teach Israel where true blessing comes from: building on the foundation of the apostles, the prophets, and Christ, keeping the laws of the Most High, and hoping in the Lord for redemption. The teaching moves through the heritage Israel was disconnected from, the raising of children as arrows in the laws of God, the blessings that come to those who fear the Lord, Israel's afflictions and repentance, and the hope of eternal life. Everything labored for without this bible as its foundation is labored for in vain.

**[Psalms 127:1](/bible/psalms/127#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/127#v1)</sup> Except the Lord build the house, they labour in vain that build it: except the Lord keep the city, the watchman waketh but in vain.

- Except the Lord build the house, they labor in vain that build it. This house being referred to is us. We represent God's house, and we have to be built up in these scriptures.
- We labor in vain if we do not build it upon this bible. We have had many groups in the past, the Black Panthers and many other groups of our people, and the only reason they did not succeed is because they did not build the house upon the prophets and the apostles in Christ.
- We have to be built up on the foundation of this bible: our forefathers the prophets, the apostles, and Christ as our leader, the King of kings and Lord of lords, Jesus Christ.
- When the spirit of the Most High comes upon you and you come into this truth, the many different skill sets we have come together to build up the house of the Lord, just like in the days of old. We are one body through the spirit.
  - **[Ephesians 2:19](/bible/ephesians/2#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/ephesians/2#v19)</sup> Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God;

    We are no more strangers and foreigners, no more so-called blacks and Spanish and native Indians, but fellow citizens with the saints, and of the household of God.
  - **[Ephesians 2:20](/bible/ephesians/2#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ephesians/2#v20)</sup> And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;

    Built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief cornerstone.
  - **[Ephesians 2:21](/bible/ephesians/2#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/ephesians/2#v21)</sup> In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord:

    In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto a holy temple in the Lord.
  - **[Ephesians 2:22](/bible/ephesians/2#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/2#v22)</sup> In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.

    In whom we also are builded together for a habitation of God through the Spirit, one body.
  - **[Ephesians 4:3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)</sup> Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

    Endeavoring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

**[Psalms 127:2](/bible/psalms/127#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/127#v2)</sup> It is vain for you to rise up early, to sit up late, to eat the bread of sorrows: for so he giveth his beloved sleep.

- It is vain to rise up early and sit up late to eat the bread of sorrow. It is vain for us to labor and build without the apostles, the prophets, and Christ as our leader.
- For so he giveth his beloved sleep. He gives the Israelites rest, rest from the bread of sorrows.
  - **[Baruch 3:37](/bible/baruch/3#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/baruch/3#v37)</sup> Afterward did he shew himself upon earth, and conversed with men.

    Israel is the beloved; the Most High hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.

**[Psalms 127:3](/bible/psalms/127#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/127#v3)</sup> Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.

- Children are an heritage of the Lord. The children are our inheritance. We have been disconnected and discontinued from our heritage, including our children.
- These are the curses we are under. Our heritage, the heathens, became our masters and our oppressors today. We are still under the curses of God, disconnected from our blessings and from our homeland.
  - **[Jeremiah 17:4](/bible/jeremiah/17#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/jeremiah/17#v4)</sup> And thou, even thyself, shalt discontinue from thine heritage that I gave thee; and I will cause thee to serve thine enemies in the land which thou knowest not: for ye have kindled a fire in mine anger, which shall burn for ever.

    Thou hast discontinued from thine heritage that I gave thee; we have been disconnected from our heritage.
  - **[Psalms 111:6](/bible/psalms/111#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/111#v6)</sup> He hath shewed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

    He gave them the heritage of the heathen; we were disconnected from this, and the heathens became our masters.
  - **[Psalms 119:111](/bible/psalms/119#v111)**
    > <sup>[111](/bible/psalms/119#v111)</sup> Thy testimonies have I taken as an heritage for ever: for they are the rejoicing of my heart.

    Thy testimonies have I taken as an heritage forever; the testimonies of who we are and who Christ came for.
  - **[Psalms 135:12](/bible/psalms/135#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/135#v12)</sup> And gave their land for an heritage, an heritage unto Israel his people.

    He gave their land for an heritage, an heritage unto Israel his people; yet we are not in our homeland today.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 17:11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/17#v11)</sup> Beside this he gave them knowledge, and the law of life for an heritage.

    He gave them knowledge and the law of life for an heritage, to bring us back to life.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 44:23](/bible/sirach/44#v23)**
    For Isaac's sake he made the blessing rest upon the head of Jacob, gave him an heritage, and divided his portions among the twelve tribes.

**[Psalms 127:4](/bible/psalms/127#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/127#v4)</sup> As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth.

- As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man, so are children of the youth. God is comparing our children to weapons, arrows in the hand of a mighty man, right in their youth.
- Why in their youth? Because they are in a heritage to carry on your name and to meet the enemy in the gate. It is very important to raise our children in God's laws so they understand who the enemy is.
  - **[Genesis 27:2](/bible/genesis/27#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/27#v2)</sup> And he said, Behold now, I am old, I know not the day of my death:

    Isaac says, behold now I am old, I know not the day of my death.
  - **[Genesis 27:3](/bible/genesis/27#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/27#v3)</sup> Now therefore take, I pray thee, thy weapons, thy quiver and thy bow, and go out to the field, and take me some venison;

    Take thy weapons, thy quiver and thy bow; a quiver is something that carries arrows.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 30:1](/bible/sirach/30#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/30#v1)</sup> He that loveth his son causeth him oft to feel the rod, that he may have joy of him in the end.

    Many brothers do not do this, but they need to cause their children to feel the rod that they may have joy of them in the end.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 30:2](/bible/sirach/30#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/30#v2)</sup> He that chastiseth his son shall have joy in him, and shall rejoice of him among his acquaintance.

    When you chastise your son of what he is doing wrong, you will have joy in him and rejoice of him among your acquaintance.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 30:3](/bible/sirach/30#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/30#v3)</sup> He that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy: and before his friends he shall rejoice of him.

    He that teacheth his son grieveth the enemy, and before his friends he shall rejoice of him.

**[Psalms 127:4](/bible/psalms/127#v4) (continued)**
*4 As arrows are in the hand of a mighty man; so are children of the youth.*

- When you chastise your son of the things he is doing wrong, you will have joy in him, and you will brag about your son and what he is learning among your acquaintance.
- Raising your son in God's laws leaves an avenger behind you against your enemies, one who is like yourself even after you die.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 30:4](/bible/sirach/30#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/30#v4)</sup> Though his father die, yet he is as though he were not dead: for he hath left one behind him that is like himself.

    He left behind him an avenger against his enemies, and one that shall requite kindness to his friends.

**[Psalms 127:5](/bible/psalms/127#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/127#v5)</sup> Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them: they shall not be ashamed, but they shall speak with the enemies in the gate.

- Happy is the man that hath his quiver full of them, a quiver full of arrows, of children. They shall not be ashamed.
- Why shall they not be ashamed? Because they shall speak with the enemies in the gate. The generation that comes behind us needs to learn God's laws and know who the enemy is.

# Psalm 128: Blessed Is Every One That Feareth the Lord

Read the chapter → [Psalms 128](/bible/psalms/128)

**[Psalms 128:1](/bible/psalms/128#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/128#v1)</sup> Blessed is every one that feareth the Lord; that walketh in his ways.

- Blessed is every one that feareth the Lord, that walketh in his ways, walking in God's commandments. When we walk in the laws of the Lord we are undefiled; when we do not, we are defiled.
- For thou shalt eat the labor of thine hands. There is profit in laboring, and this is not just the nine to five, it is laboring in this truth. We have too many people in IUIC who just talk and do not labor at all, and shame on you. You will escape this world by your works.
  - **[Psalms 119:1](/bible/psalms/119#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/119#v1)</sup> Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.

    Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.
  - **[Proverbs 14:23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)</sup> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

    In all labor there is profit, but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

**[Psalms 128:2](/bible/psalms/128#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/128#v2)</sup> For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.

- Happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee. This blessing follows the man who fears the Lord and walks in his ways, eating the labor of his own hands.

**[Psalms 128:3](/bible/psalms/128#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/128#v3)</sup> Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine by the sides of thine house: thy children like olive plants round about thy table.

- Thy wife shall be as a fruitful vine. Jump back to [Psalms 127:3](/bible/psalms/127#v3), for the wife is that fruitful vine, the fruit of the womb that is his reward. Your wife will be a fruitful possession producing many children.
- Thy children like olive plants round about thy table. Our children will grow up full-grown in this truth; our sons honored in the age of wisdom, our daughters precious cornerstones, polished daughters of Sarah, eloquent as in the days of old.
  - **[Psalms 144:12](/bible/psalms/144#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/144#v12)</sup> That our sons may be as plants grown up in their youth; that our daughters may be as corner stones, polished after the similitude of a palace:

    Our sons as plants grown up in their youth, our daughters as corner stones, polished after the similitude of a palace.
  - **[Psalms 144:13](/bible/psalms/144#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/144#v13)</sup> That our garners may be full, affording all manner of store: that our sheep may bring forth thousands and ten thousands in our streets:

    Our garners full, affording all manner of store; our sheep bringing forth thousands and ten thousands in our streets.
  - **[Psalms 144:14](/bible/psalms/144#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/144#v14)</sup> That our oxen may be strong to labour; that there be no breaking in, nor going out; that there be no complaining in our streets.

    Our oxen strong to labor, no breaking in nor going out; our businesses not taken and no going into slavery.
  - **[Psalms 144:15](/bible/psalms/144#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/144#v15)</sup> Happy is that people, that is in such a case: yea, happy is that people, whose God is the Lord.

    No complaining in our streets; happy is that people whose God is the Lord.

**[Psalms 128:4](/bible/psalms/128#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/128#v4)</sup> Behold, that thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord.

- Behold, thus shall the man be blessed that feareth the Lord. A man will be blessed with blessed children if he fears the Lord.

**[Psalms 128:5](/bible/psalms/128#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/128#v5)</sup> The Lord shall bless thee out of Zion: and thou shalt see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.

- The Lord shall bless thee out of Zion, and thou shalt see the good of Jerusalem all the days of thy life.

**[Psalms 128:6](/bible/psalms/128#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/128#v6)</sup> Yea, thou shalt see thy children’s children, and peace upon Israel.

- Yea, thou shalt see thy children's children, and peace upon Israel. There are plenty of benefits to keeping God's laws and being blessed.
- Job is an example. Job was tried by the Lord in the first chapters, and he was blessed in his latter end, living to see four generations of his children.
  - **[Job 42:12](/bible/job/42#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/job/42#v12)</sup> So the Lord blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning: for he had fourteen thousand sheep, and six thousand camels, and a thousand yoke of oxen, and a thousand she asses.

    The Lord blessed the latter end of Job more than his beginning with sheep, camels, oxen, and she asses.
  - **[Job 42:13](/bible/job/42#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/job/42#v13)</sup> He had also seven sons and three daughters.

    He had also seven sons and three daughters.
  - **[Job 42:15](/bible/job/42#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/job/42#v15)</sup> And in all the land were no women found so fair as the daughters of Job: and their father gave them inheritance among their brethren.

    In all the land were no women found so fair as the daughters of Job; God blessed them.
  - **[Job 42:16](/bible/job/42#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/job/42#v16)</sup> After this lived Job an hundred and forty years, and saw his sons, and his sons’ sons, even four generations.

    After this Job lived a hundred and forty years, and saw his sons and his sons' sons, even four generations.
  - **[Job 42:17](/bible/job/42#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/job/42#v17)</sup> So Job died, being old and full of days.

    So Job died, being old and full of days.

# Psalm 129: Many a Time Have They Afflicted Me

Read the chapter → [Psalms 129](/bible/psalms/129)

**[Psalms 129:1](/bible/psalms/129#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/129#v1)</sup> Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, may Israel now say:

- Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, may Israel now say. Israel has always been afflicted from its youth, yet they have not prevailed against us.

**[Psalms 129:2](/bible/psalms/129#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/129#v2)</sup> Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth: yet they have not prevailed against me.

- Many a time have they afflicted me from my youth, yet they have not prevailed against me. Israel is afflicted because of sin, but the affliction has not prevailed, because we have been in and out of captivity through repentance.
  - **[Psalms 107:17](/bible/psalms/107#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/107#v17)</sup> Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted.

    Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted; when we break God's laws he calls us fools.
  - **[Psalms 65:3](/bible/psalms/65#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/65#v3)</sup> Iniquities prevail against me: as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away.

    Iniquities prevail against me; as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away.

**[Psalms 129:3](/bible/psalms/129#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/129#v3)</sup> The plowers plowed upon my back: they made long their furrows.

- The plowers plowed upon my back; they made long their furrows. This describes the affliction laid upon Israel.

**[Psalms 129:4](/bible/psalms/129#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/129#v4)</sup> The Lord is righteous: he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked.

- The Lord is righteous; he hath cut asunder the cords of the wicked.

**[Psalms 129:5](/bible/psalms/129#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/129#v5)</sup> Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Zion.

- Let them all be confounded and turned back that hate Zion. The them who hate Zion are the wicked; let the wicked be confounded and turned back.

**[Psalms 129:6](/bible/psalms/129#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/129#v6)</sup> Let them be as the grass upon the housetops, which withereth afore it groweth up:

- Let them be as the grass upon the housetops, which withereth afore it groweth up. The wicked and the enemies are like grass on a housetop; it has no deep root, so it never reaches its full potential or full growth.

**[Psalms 129:7](/bible/psalms/129#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/129#v7)</sup> Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand; nor he that bindeth sheaves his bosom.

- Wherewith the mower filleth not his hand, nor he that bindeth sheaves his bosom. There is nothing to gather from the wicked.

**[Psalms 129:8](/bible/psalms/129#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/129#v8)</sup> Neither do they which go by say, The blessing of the Lord be upon you: we bless you in the name of the Lord.

- Neither do they which go by say, The blessing of the Lord be upon you. Zion goes by and does not bless the wicked; we bless in the name of the Lord.

# Psalm 130: Israel, Hope in the Lord

Read the chapter → [Psalms 130](/bible/psalms/130)

**[Psalms 130:1](/bible/psalms/130#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/130#v1)</sup> Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord.

- Out of the depths have I cried unto thee, O Lord. This is a cry to the Lord from the depths.

**[Psalms 130:2](/bible/psalms/130#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/130#v2)</sup> Lord, hear my voice: let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications.

- Lord, hear my voice; let thine ears be attentive to the voice of my supplications.

**[Psalms 130:3](/bible/psalms/130#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/130#v3)</sup> If thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand?

- If thou, Lord, shouldest mark iniquities, O Lord, who shall stand? None could stand if the Most High marked every iniquity.

**[Psalms 130:4](/bible/psalms/130#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/130#v4)</sup> But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared.

- But there is forgiveness with thee, that thou mayest be feared.

**[Psalms 130:5](/bible/psalms/130#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/130#v5)</sup> I wait for the Lord, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope.

- I wait for the Lord, my soul doth wait, and in his word do I hope. We have to wait on the Lord; in your patience possess ye your soul.

**[Psalms 130:6](/bible/psalms/130#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/130#v6)</sup> My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning: I say, more than they that watch for the morning.

- My soul waiteth for the Lord more than they that watch for the morning. The waiting for the Lord is more intense than the watchmen watching for daybreak.

**[Psalms 130:7](/bible/psalms/130#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/130#v7)</sup> Let Israel hope in the Lord: for with the Lord there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption.

- Let Israel hope in the Lord, for with the Lord there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption. Only the people of Israel, the so-called blacks, Spanish, and native Indians, can have this hope of repentance and eternal life.
- This is the same hope Paul taught. Paul's mission was to go teach the twelve tribes of Israel, to show the Jews there is no more animal sacrifice and to show the Gentiles they must come back and keep God's laws for hope's sake. Our hope comes from the patience and comfort of the scriptures.
  - **[Acts 26:4](/bible/acts/26#v4)**

    Paul's manner of life from his youth was known among his own nation at Jerusalem.
  - **[Acts 26:5](/bible/acts/26#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/acts/26#v5)</sup> Which knew me from the beginning, if they would testify, that after the most straitest sect of our religion I lived a Pharisee.

    After the most straitest sect of our religion he lived a Pharisee.
  - **[Acts 26:6](/bible/acts/26#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/acts/26#v6)</sup> And now I stand and am judged for the hope of the promise made of God unto our fathers:

    He stands for the hope of the promise made of God unto the fathers.
  - **[Acts 26:7](/bible/acts/26#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/acts/26#v7)</sup> Unto which promise our twelve tribes, instantly serving God day and night, hope to come. For which hope’s sake, king Agrippa, I am accused of the Jews.

    Unto which promise the twelve tribes, serving God day and night, hope to come.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

**[Psalms 130:8](/bible/psalms/130#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/130#v8)</sup> And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities.

- And he shall redeem Israel from all his iniquities, from all sin. The Most High will redeem Israel from all sin.
