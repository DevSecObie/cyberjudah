Read the chapter → [Psalms 86](/bible/psalms/86)

[Psalm 86](/bible/psalms/86) is a prayer of David, poor and needy, calling on the Most High daily because He is good and ready to forgive and plenteous in mercy. Among the gods there is none like unto Him, and all nations whom He has made shall come and worship before Him.

**[Psalms 86:1-5](/bible/psalms/86#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/86#v1)</sup> Bow down thine ear, O Lord, hear me: for I am poor and needy.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/86#v2)</sup> Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/86#v3)</sup> Be merciful unto me, O Lord: for I cry unto thee daily.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/86#v4)</sup> Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/86#v5)</sup> For thou, Lord, art good, and ready to forgive; and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon thee.

- David asks the Most High to bow down His ear and hear him, for he is poor and needy, and to preserve his soul, for he is holy; to save His servant that trusteth in Him and be merciful, for he cries daily.
- He asks the Most High to rejoice the soul of His servant, for unto Him David lifts up his soul; for the Lord is good and ready to forgive, and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon Him.

**[Psalms 86:6-10](/bible/psalms/86#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/86#v6)</sup> Give ear, O Lord, unto my prayer; and attend to the voice of my supplications.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/86#v7)</sup> In the day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/86#v8)</sup> Among the gods there is none like unto thee, O Lord; neither are there any works like unto thy works.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/86#v9)</sup> All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/86#v10)</sup> For thou art great, and doest wondrous things: thou art God alone.

- David asks the Most High to give ear to his prayer and attend to the voice of his supplications; in the day of his trouble he will call upon Him, for He will answer him.
- Among the gods there is none like unto Him, neither are there any works like His works; all nations whom He has made shall come and worship before Him and glorify His name, for He is great and doeth wondrous things, He is God alone.

**[Psalms 86:11-13](/bible/psalms/86#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/86#v11)</sup> Teach me thy way, O Lord; I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/86#v12)</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with all my heart: and I will glorify thy name for evermore.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/86#v13)</sup> For great is thy mercy toward me: and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell.

- David asks the Most High to teach him His way, that he may walk in His truth, and to unite his heart to fear His name; he will praise Him with all his heart and glorify His name forevermore.
- Great is His mercy toward David, and He has delivered his soul from the lowest hell.

**[Psalms 86:14-17](/bible/psalms/86#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/86#v14)</sup> O God, the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men have sought after my soul; and have not set thee before them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/86#v15)</sup> But thou, O Lord, art a God full of compassion, and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/86#v16)</sup> O turn unto me, and have mercy upon me; give thy strength unto thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/86#v17)</sup> Shew me a token for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed: because thou, Lord, hast holpen me, and comforted me.

- The proud are risen against David and the assemblies of violent men have sought after his soul and have not set the Most High before them; but He is a God full of compassion and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth.
- David asks the Most High to turn unto him and have mercy, to give His strength unto His servant and save the son of His handmaid, and to show him a token for good, that they which hate Him may see it and be ashamed, because the Lord has holpen and comforted him.
