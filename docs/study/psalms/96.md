**[Psalm 96:1](/bible/psalms/96#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/96#v1)</sup> O sing unto the Lord a new song: sing unto the Lord, all the earth.

- O sing unto the Lord a new song, sing unto the Lord all the earth. Singing a new song is joyfulness.

**[Psalm 96:2](/bible/psalms/96#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/96#v2)</sup> Sing unto the Lord, bless his name; shew forth his salvation from day to day.

- Sing unto the Lord, bless His name, show forth His salvation from day to day. Why show forth His salvation from day to day? Because salvation is only for the Israelites, but your mind has to be correct, your mind has to be in the right place.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 18:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)</sup> So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies.

    You have to accept the salvation of the righteous, those keeping God's commandments, and the destruction of the enemy; if you do not, Christianity, religion, and "God loves everybody" is still in your mind.

**[Psalm 96:3](/bible/psalms/96#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/96#v3)</sup> Declare his glory among the heathen, his wonders among all people.

- Declare His glory among the heathen, His wonders among all people. What is God's glory? His glory goes into many things, His mercy and righteousness, and Israel itself.
- Israel is God's glory, so declaring His glory among the heathen means the so-called blacks, native Indians, and Hispanics are coming back to their glory, coming back to God's law, statutes, and commandments. God is putting us back in order among the heathen; we are coming back to our traditions and our heritage and leaving the heritage of the heathen.
  - **[Baruch 5:9](/bible/baruch/5#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/baruch/5#v9)</sup> For God shall lead Israel with joy in the light of his glory with the mercy and righteousness that cometh from him.

    God will lead Israel with joy; the mercy and righteousness that come from Him are His glory.
  - **[Isaiah 46:13](/bible/isaiah/46#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/46#v13)</sup> I bring near my righteousness; it shall not be far off, and my salvation shall not tarry: and I will place salvation in Zion for Israel my glory.

    He will place salvation in Zion for Israel His glory; Israel is God's glory.

**[Psalm 96:4-6](/bible/psalms/96#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/96#v4)</sup> For the Lord is great, and greatly to be praised: he is to be feared above all gods.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/96#v5)</sup> For all the gods of the nations are idols: but the Lord made the heavens.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/96#v6)</sup> Honour and majesty are before him: strength and beauty are in his sanctuary.

- The Lord is great and greatly to be praised, He is to be feared above all gods. All the gods of the nations are idols; the nations are the heathen, and their gods are idolatry, but the Lord made the heavens.
- Honour and majesty are before Him, strength and beauty are in His sanctuary.

**[Psalm 96:7-8](/bible/psalms/96#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/96#v7)</sup> Give unto the Lord, O ye kindreds of the people, give unto the Lord glory and strength.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/96#v8)</sup> Give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name: bring an offering, and come into his courts.

- Give unto the Lord glory and strength, give unto the Lord the glory due unto His name. The glory due unto His name is His honour for the things He has done for Israel: He put us in slavery and now He is bringing us back to His glory, our glory, where we once were as a people before.
- Bring an offering and come into His courts. Do we sacrifice? No, we do not sacrifice anymore, so the offering we bring is keeping the commandments, doing God's commandments.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 35:1](/bible/sirach/35#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/35#v1)</sup> He that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough: he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering.

    When you keep the law you bring offerings enough; keeping the commandment is the peace offering.

**[Psalm 96:9-10](/bible/psalms/96#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/96#v9)</sup> O worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness: fear before him, all the earth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/96#v10)</sup> Say among the heathen that the Lord reigneth: the world also shall be established that it shall not be moved: he shall judge the people righteously.

- O worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness, fear before Him all the earth.
- Say among the heathen that the Lord reigneth: the world also shall be established, that it shall not be moved. That is very heavy. The world referred to is the world of Israel, the world without end that shall not be moved.
- He shall judge the people righteously.
  - **[2 Esdras 6:8-9](/bible/2-esdras/6#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-esdras/6#v8)</sup> And he said unto me, From Abraham unto Isaac, when Jacob and Esau were born of him, Jacob’s hand held first the heel of Esau.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-esdras/6#v9)</sup> For Esau is the end of the world, and Jacob is the beginning of it that followeth.

    Esau is the end of the world and Jacob is the beginning; Jacob is that world that will be established and not moved.

**[Psalm 96:11-12](/bible/psalms/96#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/96#v11)</sup> Let the heavens rejoice, and let the earth be glad; let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/96#v12)</sup> Let the field be joyful, and all that is therein: then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice

- Let the heavens rejoice and let the earth be glad, let the sea roar and the fulness thereof. When the heavens rejoice there will be no more holes in the ozone layer and no more toxic fumes going up to the heavens.
- Why will the earth be glad? No more GMOs, no more digging for oil. Why will the sea roar? No more oil spills.
- Let the field be joyful and all that is therein, then shall all the trees of the wood rejoice. The field being joyful is still about the GMOs in our foods and plants. The trees rejoicing means no more Christmas, because when Esau's world ends that world in verse 10 shall not be moved, so everything is going to rejoice.

**[Psalm 96:13](/bible/psalms/96#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/96#v13)</sup> Before the Lord: for he cometh, for he cometh to judge the earth: he shall judge the world with righteousness, and the people with his truth.

- He cometh to judge the earth: He shall judge the world with righteousness and the people with His truth.
