**[Psalm 149:1-9](/bible/psalms/149#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/149#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Sing unto the Lord a new song, and his praise in the congregation of saints.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/149#v2)</sup> Let Israel rejoice in him that made him: let the children of Zion be joyful in their King.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/149#v3)</sup> Let them praise his name in the dance: let them sing praises unto him with the timbrel and harp.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/149#v4)</sup> For the Lord taketh pleasure in his people: he will beautify the meek with salvation.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/149#v5)</sup> Let the saints be joyful in glory: let them sing aloud upon their beds.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/149#v6)</sup> Let the high praises of God be in their mouth, and a twoedged sword in their hand;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/149#v7)</sup> To execute vengeance upon the heathen, and punishments upon the people;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/149#v8)</sup> To bind their kings with chains, and their nobles with fetters of iron;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/149#v9)</sup> To execute upon them the judgment written: this honour have all his saints. Praise ye the Lord.

- Sing unto the Most High a new song, and His praise in the congregation of saints. Let Israel rejoice in Him that made him; let the children of Zion be joyful in their King.
- Zion is another word for Jerusalem or Israel. Jerusalem or Israel was a people before it was a place, so when scripture refers to Zion it points to the children of Israel.
  - **[1 Kings 8:1](/bible/1-kings/8#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-kings/8#v1)</sup> Then Solomon assembled the elders of Israel, and all the heads of the tribes, the chief of the fathers of the children of Israel, unto king Solomon in Jerusalem, that they might bring up the ark of the covenant of the Lord out of the city of David, which is Zion.

    Solomon assembled the elders of Israel to bring up the ark out of the city of David, which is Zion; Zion is another word for Jerusalem or Israel.
- Let the children of Zion be joyful in their King. That King is the same horn of His people, the horn of salvation, which is Christ.
- Let them praise His name in the dance; let them sing praises unto Him with the timbrel and harp. For the Most High taketh pleasure in His people; He will beautify the meek with salvation.
- The word meek means submissive or obedient. He will beautify the meek, the obedient, with salvation. If you keep the commandments and are obedient to the Most High, as you read in [Psalm 147](/bible/psalms/147), you will have salvation.
- Let the saints be joyful in glory; let them sing aloud upon their beds. Let the high praises of the Most High be in their mouth and a two-edged sword in their hand. This explains what happens once they get their salvation.
- To execute vengeance upon the heathen and punishments upon the people, to bind their kings with chains and their nobles with fetters of iron, to execute upon them the judgment written; this honour have all His saints.
- The honor and glory the saints, the children of Israel, receive when they overcome these people is the privilege of binding their kings with chains and their nobles with fetters of iron. If you do not understand that we are Israelites, you will read this and think it is talking about a time during David or during the kings, but it is talking about the future.
