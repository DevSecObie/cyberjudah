Read the chapter → [Psalms 90](/bible/psalms/90)

[Psalm 90](/bible/psalms/90) is a prayer of Moses, the man of God, on the eternity of the Most High and the shortness of man's life. The Lord has been our dwelling place in all generations; from everlasting to everlasting He is God, while man is turned to destruction. The teaching draws out how the Most High has cut man's days short down to the present day.

**[Psalms 90:1-6](/bible/psalms/90#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/90#v1)</sup> Lord, thou hast been our dwelling place in all generations.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/90#v2)</sup> Before the mountains were brought forth, or ever thou hadst formed the earth and the world, even from everlasting to everlasting, thou art God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/90#v3)</sup> Thou turnest man to destruction; and sayest, Return, ye children of men.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/90#v4)</sup> For a thousand years in thy sight are but as yesterday when it is past, and as a watch in the night.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/90#v5)</sup> Thou carriest them away as with a flood; they are as a sleep: in the morning they are like grass which groweth up.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/90#v6)</sup> In the morning it flourisheth, and groweth up; in the evening it is cut down, and withereth.

- The Lord has been our dwelling place in all generations; before the mountains were brought forth, or ever He had formed the earth and the world, from everlasting to everlasting He is God.
- He turns man to destruction and says return, ye children of men; this is the circle of life, He destroys us, puts us through the grave, and regenerates the souls. A thousand years in His sight are but as yesterday when it is past.
- We are like grass which groweth up; in the morning it flourisheth and groweth up, in the evening it is cut down and withereth. Our youth is like a blade of grass, green and healthy, but the glory of it fades away.

**[Psalms 90:7-10](/bible/psalms/90#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/90#v7)</sup> For we are consumed by thine anger, and by thy wrath are we troubled.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/90#v8)</sup> Thou hast set our iniquities before thee, our secret sins in the light of thy countenance.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/90#v9)</sup> For all our days are passed away in thy wrath: we spend our years as a tale that is told.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/90#v10)</sup> The days of our years are threescore years and ten; and if by reason of strength they be fourscore years, yet is their strength labour and sorrow; for it is soon cut off, and we fly away.

- We are consumed by His anger and troubled by His wrath; He has set our iniquities before Him, our secret sins in the light of His countenance. That is how the Lord can judge you; we don't get away.
- In the regeneration they shall live again, and then the works of the righteous and the works of the wicked shall be declared. All our days are passed away in His wrath; we spend our years as a tale that is told.
- The days of our years are threescore years and ten; a score is twenty, so three score and ten is seventy. At seventy you must give up the ghost, for the Lord's spirit will not always be striving with you.
  - **[Genesis 6:3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)</sup> And the Lord said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

    My spirit shall not always strive with man; his days shall be a hundred and twenty years, as He constantly cuts our days short.

**[Psalms 90:11-13](/bible/psalms/90#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/90#v11)</sup> Who knoweth the power of thine anger? even according to thy fear, so is thy wrath.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/90#v12)</sup> So teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/90#v13)</sup> Return, O Lord, how long? and let it repent thee concerning thy servants.

- Who knoweth the power of His anger; even according to His fear, so is His wrath. He asks the Most High to teach us to number our days, that we may apply our hearts unto wisdom.
- Now we know we only have about seventy years, so we number our days and consider the end from the beginning, to be effective in our walk and use the time and gifts God gives us, because there are no devices in the grave.
- He asks the Most High to return, and how long, and to let it repent Him concerning His servants.

**[Psalms 90:14-17](/bible/psalms/90#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/90#v14)</sup> O satisfy us early with thy mercy; that we may rejoice and be glad all our days.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/90#v15)</sup> Make us glad according to the days wherein thou hast afflicted us, and the years wherein we have seen evil.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/90#v16)</sup> Let thy work appear unto thy servants, and thy glory unto their children.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/90#v17)</sup> And let the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us: and establish thou the work of our hands upon us; yea, the work of our hands establish thou it.

- He asks the Most High to satisfy us early with His mercy, that we may rejoice and be glad all our days, and to make us glad according to the days wherein He has afflicted us and the years wherein we have seen evil.
- He asks that His work appear unto His servants and His glory unto their children, that the beauty of the Lord our God be upon us, and that He establish the work of our hands. The Lord will give us according to the fruit of our doings.

    Make me to hear joy and gladness, that the bones which thou hast broken may rejoice.
  - **[Jeremiah 17:10](/bible/jeremiah/17#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/jeremiah/17#v10)</sup> I the Lord search the heart, I try the reins, even to give every man according to his ways, and according to the fruit of his doings.

    I the Lord search the heart, to give every man according to his ways and the fruit of his doings, so our doings must be the fruit of the spirit.
