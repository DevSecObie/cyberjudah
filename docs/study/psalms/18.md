Read the chapter → [Psalms 18](/bible/psalms/18)

[Psalm 18](/bible/psalms/18) is prophecy: David was speaking of the last days concerning the elect. The elect are Jacob, the Most High's chosen, the Israelites, and that identity has not changed from the Old Testament into the New.

  - **[Isaiah 45:4](/bible/isaiah/45#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/45#v4)</sup> For Jacob my servant’s sake, and Israel mine elect, I have even called thee by thy name: I have surnamed thee, though thou hast not known me.

    For Jacob my servant's sake and Israel mine elect, the Most High names his elect plainly.
  - **[Matthew 24:30-31](/bible/matthew/24#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/24#v30)</sup> And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)</sup> And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.

    The Son of man will send his angels to gather his elect from the four winds; since they are gathered, they were first scattered.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    The Lord shall scatter thee among all people from one end of the earth to the other, where thou shalt serve other gods of wood and stone; this is the scattering the gathering reverses.

**[Psalms 18:1-3](/bible/psalms/18#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/18#v1)</sup> I will love thee, O Lord, my strength.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/18#v2)</sup> The Lord is my rock, and my fortress, and my deliverer; my God, my strength, in whom I will trust; my buckler, and the horn of my salvation, and my high tower.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/18#v3)</sup> I will call upon the Lord, who is worthy to be praised: so shall I be saved from mine enemies.

- David loves the Most High, his strength, his rock, fortress, deliverer, buckler, the horn of his salvation and his high tower.
- He calls upon the Lord who is worthy to be praised, and so is saved from his enemies.

**[Psalms 18:4-6](/bible/psalms/18#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/18#v4)</sup> The sorrows of death compassed me, and the floods of ungodly men made me afraid.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/18#v5)</sup> The sorrows of hell compassed me about: the snares of death prevented me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/18#v6)</sup> In my distress I called upon the Lord, and cried unto my God: he heard my voice out of his temple, and my cry came before him, even into his ears.

- The sorrows of death compassed him and the floods of ungodly men made him afraid; the sorrows of hell compassed him and the snares of death prevented him.
- The sorrows of death describe the curses of the scattering.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:65-66](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)**
    > <sup>[65](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)</sup> And among these nations shalt thou find no ease, neither shall the sole of thy foot have rest: but the Lord shall give thee there a trembling heart, and failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind:
    >
    > <sup>[66](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v66)</sup> And thy life shall hang in doubt before thee; and thou shalt fear day and night, and shalt have none assurance of thy life:

    Among the nations we find no ease but a trembling heart, failing eyes, sorrow of mind, and life hanging in doubt with fear day and night; this includes depression that many of our people suffer, and the constant fear even a law-abiding person feels when police pull up behind them.
- In his distress David called upon the Lord, and the Most High heard his voice out of his temple.

**[Psalms 18:7-15](/bible/psalms/18#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/18#v7)</sup> Then the earth shook and trembled; the foundations also of the hills moved and were shaken, because he was wroth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/18#v8)</sup> There went up a smoke out of his nostrils, and fire out of his mouth devoured: coals were kindled by it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/18#v9)</sup> He bowed the heavens also, and came down: and darkness was under his feet.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/18#v10)</sup> And he rode upon a cherub, and did fly: yea, he did fly upon the wings of the wind.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/18#v11)</sup> He made darkness his secret place; his pavilion round about him were dark waters and thick clouds of the skies.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/18#v12)</sup> At the brightness that was before him his thick clouds passed, hail stones and coals of fire.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/18#v13)</sup> The Lord also thundered in the heavens, and the Highest gave his voice; hail stones and coals of fire.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/18#v14)</sup> Yea, he sent out his arrows, and scattered them; and he shot out lightnings, and discomfited them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/18#v15)</sup> Then the channels of waters were seen, and the foundations of the world were discovered at thy rebuke, O Lord, at the blast of the breath of thy nostrils.

- The earth shook and the foundations of the hills moved and were shaken because the Most High was wroth. Smoke went out of his nostrils and fire out of his mouth devoured.
- He bowed the heavens and came down with darkness under his feet; he rode upon a cherub and did fly upon the wings of the wind.
- He made darkness his secret place, dark waters and thick clouds his pavilion; at his brightness the clouds passed with hailstones and coals of fire.
- The Lord thundered in the heavens, the Most High gave his voice, and sent out his arrows and lightnings to scatter and discomfit his enemies.
- The channels of waters were seen and the foundations of the world were discovered at his rebuke, at the blast of the breath of his nostrils.

**[Psalms 18:16-19](/bible/psalms/18#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/18#v16)</sup> He sent from above, he took me, he drew me out of many waters.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/18#v17)</sup> He delivered me from my strong enemy, and from them which hated me: for they were too strong for me.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/18#v18)</sup> They prevented me in the day of my calamity: but the Lord was my stay.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/18#v19)</sup> He brought me forth also into a large place; he delivered me, because he delighted in me.

- He sent from above and drew David out of many waters, delivering him from his strong enemy and from them which hated him, for they were too strong for him.
- We are still looking for that deliverance from our strong enemy and them that hate us.
  - **[Leviticus 26:17](/bible/leviticus/26#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/26#v17)</sup> And I will set my face against you, and ye shall be slain before your enemies: they that hate you shall reign over you; and ye shall flee when none pursueth you.

    If we disobey, they that hate us shall reign over us and we shall be slain before our enemies; this is why the wicked oppressor rules right now, and why our people run when the police come even when innocent.
- They prevented him in the day of his calamity, but the Lord was his stay and brought him forth into a large place because he delighted in him.
  - **[Ezekiel 20:35](/bible/ezekiel/20#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/ezekiel/20#v35)</sup> And I will bring you into the wilderness of the people, and there will I plead with you face to face.

    The large place ties to being brought into the wilderness of the people, where the Most High delivers again.

**[Psalms 18:20-24](/bible/psalms/18#v20)**
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/18#v20)</sup> The Lord rewarded me according to my righteousness; according to the cleanness of my hands hath he recompensed me.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/18#v21)</sup> For I have kept the ways of the Lord, and have not wickedly departed from my God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/18#v22)</sup> For all his judgments were before me, and I did not put away his statutes from me.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/18#v23)</sup> I was also upright before him, and I kept myself from mine iniquity.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/18#v24)</sup> Therefore hath the Lord recompensed me according to my righteousness, according to the cleanness of my hands in his eyesight.

- The Lord rewarded David according to his righteousness and the cleanness of his hands, because he kept the ways of the Lord and did not wickedly depart from his God.
- We get clean by taking heed to the word and the commandments, not by water baptism.
  - **[Psalms 119:9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

    A young man cleanses his way by taking heed according to the word.
  - **[John 15:3](/bible/john/15#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/john/15#v3)</sup> Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you.

    Ye are clean through the word which Christ has spoken; the cleansing did not change in the New Testament.
  - **[Isaiah 8:20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/8#v20)</sup> To the law and to the testimony: if they speak not according to this word, it is because there is no light in them.

    To the law and to the testimony; if they speak not according to this word there is no light in them, so we learn and apply the law to clean ourselves up.
  - **[Titus 2:11-12](/bible/titus/2#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/titus/2#v11)</sup> For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men,
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/titus/2#v12)</sup> Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;

    The grace of God teaches us to deny ungodliness and worldly lusts and to live soberly and righteously, so grace itself teaches us to keep the commandments.
- David kept the Most High's judgments and statutes before him and kept himself from his iniquity. Iniquity is sin.
  - **[Colossians 3:5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

    Mortify your members, fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness which is idolatry, for which the wrath of God comes on the disobedient.

**[Psalms 18:25-27](/bible/psalms/18#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/18#v25)</sup> With the merciful thou wilt shew thyself merciful; with an upright man thou wilt shew thyself upright;
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/18#v26)</sup> With the pure thou wilt shew thyself pure; and with the froward thou wilt shew thyself froward.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/18#v27)</sup> For thou wilt save the afflicted people; but wilt bring down high looks.

- With the merciful the Most High shows himself merciful, with the upright man upright, with the pure pure, and with the froward he shows himself froward.
- He will save the afflicted people but bring down high looks. Israel is the afflicted people the scripture is talking about.
  - **[Daniel 9:12](/bible/daniel/9#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/9#v12)</sup> And he hath confirmed his words, which he spake against us, and against our judges that judged us, by bringing upon us a great evil: for under the whole heaven hath not been done as hath been done upon Jerusalem.

    He confirmed his words against us and our judges by bringing on us a great evil, not against all people but against us, as was done upon Jerusalem, who are the Israelites.

**[Psalms 18:28-33](/bible/psalms/18#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/18#v28)</sup> For thou wilt light my candle: the Lord my God will enlighten my darkness.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/18#v29)</sup> For by thee I have run through a troop; and by my God have I leaped over a wall.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/18#v30)</sup> As for God, his way is perfect: the word of the Lord is tried: he is a buckler to all those that trust in him.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/18#v31)</sup> For who is God save the Lord? or who is a rock save our God?
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/18#v32)</sup> It is God that girdeth me with strength, and maketh my way perfect.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/18#v33)</sup> He maketh my feet like hinds’ feet, and setteth me upon my high places.

- The Most High lights David's candle and enlightens his darkness; by his God he has run through a troop and leaped over a wall.
- As for God, his way is perfect and the word of the Lord is tried; he is a buckler to all that trust in him.
  - **[Leviticus 26:7-8](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)</sup> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/26#v8)</sup> And five of you shall chase an hundred, and an hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight: and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

    Ye shall chase your enemies and five shall chase a hundred and a hundred put ten thousand to flight; this is the power we are looking to receive.
- There is no God save the Lord and no rock save our God. It is God that girds David with strength, makes his way perfect, and makes his feet like hinds' feet.

**[Psalms 18:34-40](/bible/psalms/18#v34)**
> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/18#v34)</sup> He teacheth my hands to war, so that a bow of steel is broken by mine arms.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/18#v35)</sup> Thou hast also given me the shield of thy salvation: and thy right hand hath holden me up, and thy gentleness hath made me great.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/18#v36)</sup> Thou hast enlarged my steps under me, that my feet did not slip.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/psalms/18#v37)</sup> I have pursued mine enemies, and overtaken them: neither did I turn again till they were consumed.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/psalms/18#v38)</sup> I have wounded them that they were not able to rise: they are fallen under my feet.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/psalms/18#v39)</sup> For thou hast girded me with strength unto the battle: thou hast subdued under me those that rose up against me.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/psalms/18#v40)</sup> Thou hast also given me the necks of mine enemies; that I might destroy them that hate me.

- He teaches David's hands to war so that a bow of steel is broken by his arms; he gives him the shield of his salvation, his right hand holds him up, and his gentleness makes him great.
- He enlarges David's steps under him so his feet do not slip; David pursues his enemies, overtakes them, and does not turn again till they are consumed.
  - **[Micah 5:7-9](/bible/micah/5#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/micah/5#v7)</sup> And the remnant of Jacob shall be in the midst of many people as a dew from the Lord, as the showers upon the grass, that tarrieth not for man, nor waiteth for the sons of men.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/micah/5#v8)</sup> And the remnant of Jacob shall be among the Gentiles in the midst of many people as a lion among the beasts of the forest, as a young lion among the flocks of sheep: who, if he go through, both treadeth down, and teareth in pieces, and none can deliver.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/micah/5#v9)</sup> Thine hand shall be lifted up upon thine adversaries, and all thine enemies shall be cut off.

    The remnant of Jacob shall be among the Gentiles as a lion among the beasts, treading down and tearing in pieces with none to deliver, and every enemy shall be cut off; another proof we were scattered everywhere like the dew and rain, and a picture of the strength the Most High returns to us.
- David wounds his enemies so they cannot rise; they fall under his feet, for the Most High girded him with strength for the battle and subdued under him those that rose up against him.

**[Psalms 18:41-45](/bible/psalms/18#v41)**
> <sup>[41](/bible/psalms/18#v41)</sup> They cried, but there was none to save them: even unto the Lord, but he answered them not.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/psalms/18#v42)</sup> Then did I beat them small as the dust before the wind: I did cast them out as the dirt in the streets.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/psalms/18#v43)</sup> Thou hast delivered me from the strivings of the people; and thou hast made me the head of the heathen: a people whom I have not known shall serve me.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/psalms/18#v44)</sup> As soon as they hear of me, they shall obey me: the strangers shall submit themselves unto me.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/psalms/18#v45)</sup> The strangers shall fade away, and be afraid out of their close places.

- His enemies cry but there is none to save them, even unto the Lord, but he answers them not; David beats them small as the dust before the wind and casts them out as dirt in the streets.
- The Most High delivers him from the strivings of the people and makes him the head of the heathen; a people whom he has not known shall serve him.
  - **[Isaiah 60:14](/bible/isaiah/60#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/60#v14)</sup> The sons also of them that afflicted thee shall come bending unto thee; and all they that despised thee shall bow themselves down at the soles of thy feet; and they shall call thee, The city of the Lord, The Zion of the Holy One of Israel.

    The sons of them that afflicted us shall come bending, showing the strangers submitting themselves.
- As soon as they hear of him the strangers submit and fade away, afraid out of their close places.

**[Psalms 18:46-48](/bible/psalms/18#v46)**
> <sup>[46](/bible/psalms/18#v46)</sup> The Lord liveth; and blessed be my rock; and let the God of my salvation be exalted.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/psalms/18#v47)</sup> It is God that avengeth me, and subdueth the people under me.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/psalms/18#v48)</sup> He delivereth me from mine enemies: yea, thou liftest me up above those that rise up against me: thou hast delivered me from the violent man.

- The Lord liveth, blessed be his rock, and let the God of his salvation be exalted; it is God that avenges David and subdues the people under him.
- He delivers David from his enemies and lifts him above those that rise against him, delivering him from the violent man. The enemies are not just going to and fro; there is a people who serve satan, the so-called white man, to make it plain.
  - **[Matthew 11:12](/bible/matthew/11#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/11#v12)</sup> And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.

    The kingdom of heaven suffereth violence and the violent take it by force; the violent men of that time were Rome fighting against the kingdom, which is Israel.
  - **[1 Chronicles 28:5](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v5)</sup> And of all my sons, (for the Lord hath given me many sons,) he hath chosen Solomon my son to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel.

    The Most High chose Solomon to sit upon the throne of the kingdom of the Lord over Israel, so the kingdom of God is Israel.

**[Psalms 18:49-50](/bible/psalms/18#v49)**
> <sup>[49](/bible/psalms/18#v49)</sup> Therefore will I give thanks unto thee, O Lord, among the heathen, and sing praises unto thy name.
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/psalms/18#v50)</sup> Great deliverance giveth he to his king; and sheweth mercy to his anointed, to David, and to his seed for evermore.

- Therefore David gives thanks among the heathen and sings praises to the Most High's name.
- Great deliverance he gives to his king and shows mercy to his anointed, to David and to his seed forevermore.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 18:7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v7)</sup> So of thy people was accepted both the salvation of the righteous, and destruction of the enemies.

    This great deliverance is the good news, for a remnant of Israel is fighting for our enemies and against the remnant of Jacob, but the righteous will be accepted.
  - **[Jeremiah 16:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/16#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/16#v14)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that it shall no more be said, The Lord liveth, that brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/16#v15)</sup> But, The Lord liveth, that brought up the children of Israel from the land of the north, and from all the lands whither he had driven them: and I will bring them again into their land that I gave unto their fathers.

    The days come that it shall no more be said the Lord liveth that brought Israel out of Egypt, but the Lord liveth that brought the children of Israel from the land of the north, which is North America, and from all the lands whither he had driven them; if the people in the land now were not Israel, why are they still here in New York, Hollywood, and Miami.
