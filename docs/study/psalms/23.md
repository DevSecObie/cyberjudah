Read the chapter → [Psalms 23](/bible/psalms/23)

This session is Scriptures 365, four chapters a day, reading [Psalm 23](/bible/psalms/23) through [Psalm 26](/bible/psalms/26), all Psalms of King David. Sisters cover your heads, brothers uncover, and we face Jerusalem, which from America is east, acknowledging that we are in captivity, that we are from Jerusalem, and that we will return there again to our homeland. [Psalm 23](/bible/psalms/23) is a heavy chapter and gets extra time; the rest are read with the precepts brought out along the way. Everything written is for the Israelites, for our benefit, for us to study and understand.

**[Psalm 23:1](/bible/psalms/23#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/23#v1)</sup> The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want.

- The Lord is my shepherd, I shall not want. That word "want" means lack.

**[Psalm 23:2](/bible/psalms/23#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/23#v2)</sup> He maketh me to lie down in green pastures: he leadeth me beside the still waters.

- He maketh me to lie down in green pastures, He leadeth me beside the still waters. Let us look at what these green pastures are.
- The green pastures are peace and the beauty of the land: the grass, the cattle, the flowers of the field. It is going into the homeland, the glory of all lands, which is Jerusalem, Israel.
  - **[Ezekiel 20:6](/bible/ezekiel/20#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/20#v6)</sup> In the day that I lifted up mine hand unto them, to bring them forth of the land of Egypt into a land that I had espied for them, flowing with milk and honey, which is the glory of all lands:

    A land flowing with milk and honey, the glory of all lands, which is Jerusalem, our homeland; so laying in green pastures is going into peace.

**[Psalm 23:3](/bible/psalms/23#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/23#v3)</sup> He restoreth my soul: he leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for his name’s sake.

- He restoreth my soul and leadeth me in the paths of righteousness for His name's sake.

**[Psalm 23:4](/bible/psalms/23#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/23#v4)</sup> Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me.

- The valley of the shadow of death is talking about captivity. Though I walk through it I will fear no evil, for thou art with me; thy rod and thy staff, they comfort me.
- The shadow of death is those bound in affliction and iron. So the valley of the shadow of death is America, the valley of dry bones, the Israelites waking up in the presence of their enemies.
  - **[Psalm 107:10](/bible/psalms/107#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/107#v10)</sup> Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron;

    Such as sit in darkness and in the shadow of death, being bound in affliction and iron; that is the shadow of death, captivity.

**[Psalm 23:5](/bible/psalms/23#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/23#v5)</sup> Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies: thou anointest my head with oil; my cup runneth over.

- Thou preparest a table before me in the presence of mine enemies. Our enemies are the other nations, but more specifically the Edomite nations whose hands are still in America today.
- The table is the word of the Lord: His laws, statutes and commandments written in a book. It is our correction and our comfort in these last days.
- He prepares that table in the presence of the enemy by setting them in order before our eyes. The other nations do heinous things against us, they kill us, shoot us down, imprison us, put drugs in our neighborhoods and call it a "war on drugs" when they mean war on us, the Israelites.
- They claim to be a Christian nation but do the most killings; the Lord says He will reprove them and set them in order before our eyes, giving us the book, the correction and comfort, to rise up as a great army in the presence of our enemies.
  - **[Isaiah 30:8](/bible/isaiah/30#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/30#v8)</sup> Now go, write it before them in a table, and note it in a book, that it may be for the time to come for ever and ever:

    Now go, write it before them in a table and note it in a book; the "it" is the words of the Lord, His laws and commandments to get us right.
  - **[Psalm 50:21](/bible/psalms/50#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/50#v21)</sup> These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.

    These things hast thou done and I kept silence; but I will reprove thee and set them in order before thine eyes, talking about the other nations.
  - **[Revelation 11:11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/11#v11)</sup> And after three days and an half the Spirit of life from God entered into them, and they stood upon their feet; and great fear fell upon them which saw them.

    After three days and a half the spirit of life from God entered into them and they stood upon their feet, and great fear fell upon them which saw them, in the presence of our enemies.

**[Psalm 23:6](/bible/psalms/23#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/23#v6)</sup> Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life: and I will dwell in the house of the Lord for ever.

- Thou anointest my head with oil, my cup runneth over. Surely goodness and mercy shall follow me all the days of my life, and I will dwell in the house of the Lord forever.
- We wake up through the words of life, not through the comedic community, not Egyptology, not Scientology, or whatever else is out there.
