**[Psalms 17:1-3](/bible/psalms/17#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/17#v1)</sup> Hear the right, O Lord, attend unto my cry, give ear unto my prayer, that goeth not out of feigned lips.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/17#v2)</sup> Let my sentence come forth from thy presence; let thine eyes behold the things that are equal.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/17#v3)</sup> Thou hast proved mine heart; thou hast visited me in the night; thou hast tried me, and shalt find nothing; I am purposed that my mouth shall not transgress.

- David asks the Most High to hear the right, attend to his cry, and receive a prayer that goeth not out of feigned lips. Feigned means fake, so he prays from real and not fake lips.
- Let his sentence come forth from the Most High's presence; let his eyes behold the things that are equal.
- The Most High has proved and visited him and found nothing; David is purposed that his mouth shall not transgress.
  - **[James 3:2](/bible/james/3#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/james/3#v2)</sup> For in many things we offend all. If any man offend not in word, the same is a perfect man, and able also to bridle the whole body.

    He that offends not in word is a perfect man, able to bridle the whole body; read this chapter if you struggle with a foul mouth or talking back.

**[Psalms 17:4-5](/bible/psalms/17#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/17#v4)</sup> Concerning the works of men, by the word of thy lips I have kept me from the paths of the destroyer.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/17#v5)</sup> Hold up my goings in thy paths, that my footsteps slip not.

- Concerning the works of men, by the word of the Most High's lips David has kept himself from the paths of the destroyer.
- He asks the Most High to hold up his goings in his paths so that his footsteps slip not. Our footsteps slip when we come off the path of righteousness, turning to the right hand or the left instead of following the good path that leads to the kingdom.
  - **[Psalms 73:2-3](/bible/psalms/73#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/73#v2)</sup> But as for me, my feet were almost gone; my steps had well nigh slipped.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/73#v3)</sup> For I was envious at the foolish, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

    His feet were almost gone when he was envious at the foolish and saw the prosperity of the wicked.
  - **[Proverbs 3:31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/3#v31)</sup> Envy thou not the oppressor, and choose none of his ways.

    Envy not the oppressor and choose none of his ways; envy is one thing that makes the feet slip, like sticking a Christmas tree in your house and following the ways of the wicked.
  - **[Psalms 73:17](/bible/psalms/73#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/73#v17)</sup> Until I went into the sanctuary of God; then understood I their end.

    Not until he went into the sanctuary of the Most High did he understand their end; get knowledge and understanding built up first.

**[Psalms 17:6-9](/bible/psalms/17#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/17#v6)</sup> I have called upon thee, for thou wilt hear me, O God: incline thine ear unto me, and hear my speech.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/17#v7)</sup> Shew thy marvellous lovingkindness, O thou that savest by thy right hand them which put their trust in thee from those that rise up against them.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/17#v8)</sup> Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/17#v9)</sup> From the wicked that oppress me, from my deadly enemies, who compass me about.

- David calls because he knows the Most High will hear him; he asks him to incline his ear and show his marvellous lovingkindness, saving by his right hand them that put their trust in him.
- Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings. This prayer asks to be kept safe from the wicked that oppress him and from his deadly enemies who compass him about.
  - **[Malachi 1:4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)</sup> Whereas Edom saith, We are impoverished, but we will return and build the desolate places; thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall build, but I will throw down; and they shall call them, The border of wickedness, and, The people against whom the Lord hath indignation for ever.

    Edom, the so-called white man or red man, says they will build the desolate places, but the Most High calls them the border of wickedness, the people against whom he has indignation forever.
  - **[Genesis 25:25](/bible/genesis/25#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/25#v25)</sup> And the first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name Esau.

    Esau came out red all over, which identifies Edom.
  - **[Job 9:24](/bible/job/9#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/job/9#v24)</sup> The earth is given into the hand of the wicked: he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, and who is he?

    The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; the example of that wicked ruler right now is the so-called white man, not the African or the Arab.

**[Psalms 17:10-12](/bible/psalms/17#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/17#v10)</sup> They are inclosed in their own fat: with their mouth they speak proudly.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/17#v11)</sup> They have now compassed us in our steps: they have set their eyes bowing down to the earth;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/17#v12)</sup> Like as a lion that is greedy of his prey, and as it were a young lion lurking in secret places.

- The wicked are enclosed in their own fat, meaning they have wealth, and with their mouth they speak proudly.
- They have compassed David in his steps, set their eyes bowing down to the earth, like a lion greedy of his prey and a young lion lurking in secret places. What happened in DC with the group calling themselves the Proud Boys shows the Bible is a true book, for they speak proudly with their mouth.

**[Psalms 17:13-14](/bible/psalms/17#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/17#v13)</sup> Arise, O Lord, disappoint him, cast him down: deliver my soul from the wicked, which is thy sword:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/17#v14)</sup> From men which are thy hand, O Lord, from men of the world, which have their portion in this life, and whose belly thou fillest with thy hid treasure: they are full of children, and leave the rest of their substance to their babes.

- Arise, O Lord, disappoint him, cast him down, deliver my soul from the wicked which is thy sword. The wicked here is the same deadly enemy and oppressor named in verse 9.
  - **[Leviticus 26:3-7](/bible/leviticus/26#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/leviticus/26#v3)</sup> If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them;
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/leviticus/26#v4)</sup> Then I will give you rain in due season, and the land shall yield her increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/leviticus/26#v5)</sup> And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing time: and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land safely.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/26#v6)</sup> And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will rid evil beasts out of the land, neither shall the sword go through your land.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)</sup> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.

    If we keep his statutes and commandments he will give peace in the land, rid evil beasts, and the sword shall not go through our land; keeping the law is how the sword turns.
- David asks deliverance from men of the world which have their portion in this life, whose belly the Most High fills with hid treasure, who are full of children and leave their substance to their babes.
- This verse ties to the teaching in Bishop's Sabbath class going through [Job 20](/bible/job/20).
