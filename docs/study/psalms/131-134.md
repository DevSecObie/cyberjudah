This session covers [Psalms 131](/bible/psalms/131) through 134. [Psalm 131](/bible/psalms/131) is David humbling himself as a weaned child, teaching that coming into the truth means starting with the milk of the word before the meat. [Psalm 132](/bible/psalms/132) traces the oath the Most High swore to David that his seed would sit on the throne forever, pointing to Christ. [Psalm 133](/bible/psalms/133) shows how good it is for the brethren, the twelve tribes scattered abroad, to dwell together in unity. [Psalm 134](/bible/psalms/134) closes with the servants of the Most High blessing him in the sanctuary, the little sanctuaries set up in captivity.

**[Psalm 131:1](/bible/psalms/131#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/131#v1)</sup> Lord, my heart is not haughty, nor mine eyes lofty: neither do I exercise myself in great matters, or in things too high for me.

- David says he does not think he is better than anyone and is not proud above measure. He does not exercise himself in great matters or things too high for him.
- Many babes fall because instead of consuming the milk they try to go straight for the meat. The teaching is about brothers and sisters who reach for things a little too hard.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 3:21-23](/bible/sirach/3#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/3#v21)</sup> Seek not out things that are too hard for thee, neither search the things that are above thy strength.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/3#v22)</sup> But what is commanded thee, think thereupon with reverence, for it is not needful for thee to see with thine eyes the things that are in secret.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/3#v23)</sup> Be not curious in unnecessary matters: for more things are shewed unto thee than men understand.

    Seek not the things too hard for thee; think on what is commanded with reverence, not curious in unnecessary matters.

**[Psalm 131:2](/bible/psalms/131#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/131#v2)</sup> Surely I have behaved and quieted myself, as a child that is weaned of his mother: my soul is even as a weaned child.

- David behaves and quiets himself, meaning he calms himself as a child weaned from his mother. Coming into the truth we must desire the sincere milk of the word, the basics.
- We have to come back to this Bible as a child. It is not about your age when you come to the truth; that is a carnal thing. A person can be 40 or 50 and sit among people who look younger but are spiritually older because they gained the experience of the scriptures.
  - **[1 Peter 2:2](/bible/1-peter/2#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-peter/2#v2)</sup> As newborn babes, desire the sincere milk of the word, that ye may grow thereby:

    As newborn babes desire the sincere milk of the word that you may grow thereby.
  - **[Hebrews 5:12-13](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/hebrews/5#v12)</sup> For when for the time ye ought to be teachers, ye have need that one teach you again which be the first principles of the oracles of God; and are become such as have need of milk, and not of strong meat.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/5#v13)</sup> For every one that useth milk is unskilful in the word of righteousness: for he is a babe.

    You have need to be taught again the first principles; he that uses milk is unskillful, for he is a babe.
  - **[Matthew 18:3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)</sup> And said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.

    Except you be converted and become as little children you shall not enter the kingdom of heaven.

**[Psalm 131:3](/bible/psalms/131#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/131#v3)</sup> Let Israel hope in the Lord from henceforth and for ever.

- Israel is to hope in the Most High from henceforth and forever. The only people who have hope in the Most High are the Israelites, the so-called blacks, Spanish, and native Indians today.
- Our hope lies in this Bible. We get hope through the patience and comfort of the scriptures, because we have been taught wrong all our lives and must be converted back as a child.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Whatsoever was written aforetime was written for our learning, that through patience and comfort of the scriptures we might have hope.

# Psalm 132: The Oath to David and the Throne Forever

Read the chapter → [Psalms 132](/bible/psalms/132)

**[Psalm 132:1](/bible/psalms/132#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/132#v1)</sup> Lord, remember David, and all his afflictions:

- The affliction refers to David's sins. If you know David's history, he did foolish things, got one of his best men killed and took his wife.
- We get afflicted because of our sins, and the Most High calls us fools. Yet these scriptures were written for our learning so that through patience we might have hope.
  - **[Psalm 107:17](/bible/psalms/107#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/107#v17)</sup> Fools because of their transgression, and because of their iniquities, are afflicted.

    Fools because of their transgression and their iniquities are afflicted.

**[Psalm 132:2-5](/bible/psalms/132#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/132#v2)</sup> How he sware unto the Lord, and vowed unto the mighty God of Jacob;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/132#v3)</sup> Surely I will not come into the tabernacle of my house, nor go up into my bed;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/132#v4)</sup> I will not give sleep to mine eyes, or slumber to mine eyelids,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/132#v5)</sup> Until I find out a place for the Lord, an habitation for the mighty God of Jacob.

- David swore to the mighty God of Jacob, this Bible, that he would not rest until he found a place for the Most High, a habitation for the mighty God of Jacob.

**[Psalm 132:6-8](/bible/psalms/132#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/132#v6)</sup> Lo, we heard of it at Ephratah: we found it in the fields of the wood.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/132#v7)</sup> We will go into his tabernacles: we will worship at his footstool.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/132#v8)</sup> Arise, O Lord, into thy rest; thou, and the ark of thy strength.

- We will go into his tabernacles and worship at his footstool. We come into the house of the Most High to learn his laws, to be holy, to be separate.
- Holy means severed. We were severed from other people, given a set of laws contrary to all nations. Once we mingle with the other nations we become unholy.
  - **[Psalm 99:5](/bible/psalms/99#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/99#v5)</sup> Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship at his footstool; for he is holy.

    Exalt the Most High and worship at his footstool, for he is holy.
  - **[Leviticus 20:26](/bible/leviticus/20#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/leviticus/20#v26)</sup> And ye shall be holy unto me: for I the Lord am holy, and have severed you from other people, that ye should be mine.

    You shall be holy unto me, for I have severed you from other people that you should be mine.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 5:1](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v1)</sup> Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God, and be more ready to hear, than to give the sacrifice of fools: for they consider not that they do evil.

    Keep thy foot when thou goest to the house of God; keep other doctrines away, for the sacrifice of fools is evil.

**[Psalm 132:9-10](/bible/psalms/132#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/132#v9)</sup> Let thy priests be clothed with righteousness; and let thy saints shout for joy.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/132#v10)</sup> For thy servant David’s sake turn not away the face of thine anointed.

- Let the priest be clothed with righteousness and the saints shout for joy. For David's sake, turn not away the face of thine anointed.
- Anointed means the smearing of oil, but this goes deeper, into Christ. David was anointed too, but this points to the Messiah who is called Christ.
  - **[John 4:25-26](/bible/john/4#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/john/4#v25)</sup> The woman saith unto him, I know that Messias cometh, which is called Christ: when he is come, he will tell us all things.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/john/4#v26)</sup> Jesus saith unto her, I that speak unto thee am he.

    The woman says the Messiah which is called Christ is coming; Jesus says, I that speak unto thee am he.
  - **[Acts 10:38](/bible/acts/10#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/10#v38)</sup> How God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power: who went about doing good, and healing all that were oppressed of the devil; for God was with him.

    God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Ghost and with power, for God was with him.

**[Psalm 132:11-12](/bible/psalms/132#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)</sup> The Lord hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/132#v12)</sup> If thy children will keep my covenant and my testimony that I shall teach them, their children shall also sit upon thy throne for evermore.

- The Most High swore in truth to David that of the fruit of his body he would set upon his throne. It has been prophesied that David's seed will sit on the throne forevermore, pointing to Christ.
- Solomon was set up after David and built the house, but the throne established forever refers to Christ. Solomon committed much iniquity and was not chastened with the rod of men; this points to something deeper in Christ.
  - **[2 Samuel 7:12-14](/bible/2-samuel/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/7#v12)</sup> And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-samuel/7#v13)</sup> He shall build an house for my name, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/7#v14)</sup> I will be his father, and he shall be my son. If he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men, and with the stripes of the children of men:

    I will set up thy seed and establish his kingdom; he shall build a house and his throne shall be established forever.

**[Psalm 132:13](/bible/psalms/132#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/132#v13)</sup> For the Lord hath chosen Zion; he hath desired it for his habitation.

- The Most High has chosen Zion for his habitation. Zion is called the city of David, but Zion also refers to a people, the Israelites.
  - **[Isaiah 52:1](/bible/isaiah/52#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/52#v1)</sup> Awake, awake; put on thy strength, O Zion; put on thy beautiful garments, O Jerusalem, the holy city: for henceforth there shall no more come into thee the uncircumcised and the unclean.

    Awake, put on thy strength O Zion, put on thy beautiful garments O Jerusalem the holy city.

**[Psalm 132:14-15](/bible/psalms/132#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/132#v14)</sup> This is my rest for ever: here will I dwell; for I have desired it.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/132#v15)</sup> I will abundantly bless her provision: I will satisfy her poor with bread.

- This is the Most High's rest forever; here he will dwell in the midst of Israel. He will abundantly bless her provisions and satisfy her poor with bread, the poor in spirit, the Israelites.
- His rulership, his throne, will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel forever. He is the Most High our God and none else, the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
  - **[Joel 2:27](/bible/joel/2#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/joel/2#v27)</sup> And ye shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and that I am the Lord your God, and none else: and my people shall never be ashamed.

    You shall know that I am in the midst of Israel, and I am the Most High your God and none else.
  - **[Ezekiel 43:7](/bible/ezekiel/43#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/43#v7)</sup> And he said unto me, Son of man, the place of my throne, and the place of the soles of my feet, where I will dwell in the midst of the children of Israel for ever, and my holy name, shall the house of Israel no more defile, neither they, nor their kings, by their whoredom, nor by the carcases of their kings in their high places.

    The place of my throne where I dwell in the midst of the children of Israel forever.
  - **[Matthew 5:3](/bible/matthew/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/5#v3)</sup> Blessed are the poor in spirit: for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

    Blessed are the poor in spirit, for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.

**[Psalm 132:16](/bible/psalms/132#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/132#v16)</sup> I will also clothe her priests with salvation: and her saints shall shout aloud for joy.

- The Most High will clothe her priests with salvation. The priest will teach our lost people how to get salvation, which comes by doing the righteousness of this book, keeping the Most High's laws.
- Righteousness goes into the commandments. That is how we get salvation, the kingdom of heaven, eternal life.
  - **[Revelation 22:14](/bible/revelation/22#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/revelation/22#v14)</sup> Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life, and may enter in through the gates into the city.

    Blessed are they that do his commandments, that they may have right to the tree of life and enter through the gates into the city.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)</sup> And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he hath commanded us.

    It shall be our righteousness if we observe to do all these commandments.

**[Psalm 132:17](/bible/psalms/132#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/132#v17)</sup> There will I make the horn of David to bud: I have ordained a lamp for mine anointed.

- Here the Most High will make the horn of David to bud, meaning become a leader. The horn refers to Christ, our leader, raised up among the saints, the children of Israel.
- The lamp ordained for the anointed is Christ, the light that came into this world of darkness. The commandment is a lamp to correct us, a reproof and instruction in the way of life to get eternal life.
  - **[Psalm 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    He exalts the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints, even the children of Israel near unto him.
  - **[Luke 1:68-69](/bible/luke/1#v68)**
    > <sup>[68](/bible/luke/1#v68)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people,
    >
    > <sup>[69](/bible/luke/1#v69)</sup> And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David;

    Blessed be the Most High of Israel, for he has raised up a horn of salvation in the house of his servant David.
  - **[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

    The commandment is a lamp and the law is light, and reproofs of instruction are the way of life.

**[Psalm 132:18](/bible/psalms/132#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/132#v18)</sup> His enemies will I clothe with shame: but upon himself shall his crown flourish.

- His enemies will be clothed with shame. This is not only the nations but the wickedness of our own people, the evildoers, who will be clothed in shame when the day of judgment comes.
- The evildoers hate the light and love darkness. But upon Christ his crown, his rulership, will flourish forever, because he has budded as a leader and his second coming will flourish forevermore.
  - **[Job 8:20-22](/bible/job/8#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/job/8#v20)</sup> Behold, God will not cast away a perfect man, neither will he help the evil doers:
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/job/8#v21)</sup> Till he fill thy mouth with laughing, and thy lips with rejoicing.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/job/8#v22)</sup> They that hate thee shall be clothed with shame; and the dwelling place of the wicked shall come to nought.

    God will not cast away a perfect man; they that hate thee shall be clothed with shame and the dwelling place of the wicked come to nought.

# Psalm 133: The Brethren Dwelling Together in Unity

Read the chapter → [Psalms 133](/bible/psalms/133)

**[Psalm 133:1](/bible/psalms/133#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/133#v1)</sup> Behold, how good and how pleasant it is for brethren to dwell together in unity!

- It is good and pleasant for the brethren to dwell together in unity. The brethren are the twelve tribes scattered abroad worldwide.
- It is good for us to dwell among ourselves because that is all we have; everybody else hates our guts, a thing we understand as we grow in the truth. We admonish, edify, comfort, and esteem one another in the scriptures.
  - **[James 1:1](/bible/james/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/james/1#v1)</sup> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.

    James to the twelve tribes scattered abroad, my brethren.
  - **[1 Thessalonians 5:11-14](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v11)</sup> Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v12)</sup> And we beseech you, brethren, to know them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you;
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v13)</sup> And to esteem them very highly in love for their work’s sake. And be at peace among yourselves.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v14)</sup> Now we exhort you, brethren, warn them that are unruly, comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men.

    Comfort and edify one another, know them which labor among you and are over you in the Most High, and esteem them highly in love.

**[Psalm 133:2-3](/bible/psalms/133#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/133#v2)</sup> It is like the precious ointment upon the head, that ran down upon the beard, even Aaron’s beard: that went down to the skirts of his garments;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/133#v3)</sup> As the dew of Hermon, and as the dew that descended upon the mountains of Zion: for there the Lord commanded the blessing, even life for evermore.

- The unity is like the precious ointment upon the head that ran down Aaron's beard to the skirts of his garments, and as the dew of Hermon descending on the mountains of Zion.
- There the Most High commanded the blessing, even life forevermore.

# Psalm 134: The Servants Blessing the Most High in the Sanctuary

Read the chapter → [Psalms 134](/bible/psalms/134)

**[Psalm 134:1](/bible/psalms/134#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/134#v1)</sup> Behold, bless ye the Lord, all ye servants of the Lord, which by night stand in the house of the Lord.

- Bless the Most High, all you servants of the Most High. Not everyone is a servant; the servants are the children of Israel, the so-called blacks, Spanish, and native Indians.
- These servants stand by night in the house of the Most High.
  - **[Leviticus 25:55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)**
    > <sup>[55](/bible/leviticus/25#v55)</sup> For unto me the children of Israel are servants; they are my servants whom I brought forth out of the land of Egypt: I am the Lord your God.

    Unto me the children of Israel are servants, whom I brought out of the land of Egypt; I am the Most High your God.

**[Psalm 134:2](/bible/psalms/134#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/134#v2)</sup> Lift up your hands in the sanctuary, and bless the Lord.

- Lift up your hands in the sanctuary and bless the Most High. The sanctuary is the house of the Most High where you go every Sabbath, the little sanctuaries IUIC has set up.
- The Most High cast us off among the heathen and scattered us among the countries, yet he became to us a little sanctuary in the countries where we come. In these sanctuaries you learn who the ungodly are, who prosper in this world serving Satan, and what their end is.
  - **[Ezekiel 11:16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/11#v16)</sup> Therefore say, Thus saith the Lord God; Although I have cast them far off among the heathen, and although I have scattered them among the countries, yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they shall come.

    Yet will I be to them as a little sanctuary in the countries where they shall come.
  - **[Psalm 73:12-17](/bible/psalms/73#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/73#v12)</sup> Behold, these are the ungodly, who prosper in the world; they increase in riches.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/73#v13)</sup> Verily I have cleansed my heart in vain, and washed my hands in innocency.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/73#v14)</sup> For all the day long have I been plagued, and chastened every morning.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/73#v15)</sup> If I say, I will speak thus; behold, I should offend against the generation of thy children.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/73#v16)</sup> When I thought to know this, it was too painful for me;
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/73#v17)</sup> Until I went into the sanctuary of God; then understood I their end.

    The ungodly prosper, but when I went into the sanctuary of God then understood I their end.

**[Psalm 134:3](/bible/psalms/134#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/134#v3)</sup> The Lord that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion.

- The Most High that made heaven and earth bless thee out of Zion. Bring your Bible, pen, paper, and Apocrypha to the house of the Most High to understand what you must do to get right and to understand the end of the nations.
