**[Psalm 98:1](/bible/psalms/98#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/98#v1)</sup> O sing unto the Lord a new song; for he hath done marvellous things: his right hand, and his holy arm, hath gotten him the victory.

- O sing unto the Lord a new song, for He hath done marvellous things: His right hand and His holy arm have gotten Him the victory.
- This "sing a new song" is repeated in [Psalm 95](/bible/psalms/95), [Psalm 96](/bible/psalms/96), and [Psalm 98](/bible/psalms/98). All our lives we have been hearing the songs of fools, R&B, hip hop, and fake gospel. We never sang about the so-called black, Hispanic, and native Indian Israelites taking back over the earth; the Lord keeps reminding us to sing a new song unto Him.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 7:5](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v5)</sup> It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise, than for a man to hear the song of fools.

    It is better to hear the rebuke of the wise than for a man to hear the song of fools; all our lives we heard the songs of fools.

**[Psalm 98:2-3](/bible/psalms/98#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/98#v2)</sup> The Lord hath made known his salvation: his righteousness hath he openly shewed in the sight of the heathen.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/98#v3)</sup> He hath remembered his mercy and his truth toward the house of Israel: all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God.

- The Lord hath made known His salvation, His righteousness hath He openly showed in the sight of the heathen.
- He hath remembered His mercy and His truth toward the house of Israel; all the ends of the earth have seen the salvation of our God. The line, the gospel, is going out, so the nations understand that salvation is for the Israelites; we are coming back to our rightful position and our rightful name, and they are seeing it unfold in front of their eyes.
  - **[2 Esdras 13:36-39](/bible/2-esdras/13#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/2-esdras/13#v36)</sup> And Sion shall come, and shall be shewed to all men, being prepared and builded, like as thou sawest the hill graven without hands.
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/2-esdras/13#v37)</sup> And this my Son shall rebuke the wicked inventions of those nations, which for their wicked life are fallen into the tempest;
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/2-esdras/13#v38)</sup> And shall lay before them their evil thoughts, and the torments wherewith they shall begin to be tormented, which are like unto a flame: and he shall destroy them without labour by the law which is like unto me.
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/2-esdras/13#v39)</sup> And whereas thou sawest that he gathered another peaceable multitude unto him;

    Christ destroys the nations without labor, light work by the law, and gathers another peaceable multitude, the Israelites coming back to their heritage as upright people.

**[Psalm 98:4-6](/bible/psalms/98#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/98#v4)</sup> Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all the earth: make a loud noise, and rejoice, and sing praise.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/98#v5)</sup> Sing unto the Lord with the harp; with the harp, and the voice of a psalm.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/98#v6)</sup> With trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the Lord, the King.

- Make a joyful noise unto the Lord all the earth; make a loud noise, rejoice, and sing praise. We need to break forth and sing praise, saying man, I am an Israelite, God chose me. We should be very happy about that thing.
- Sing unto the Lord with the harp and the voice of a psalm, with trumpets and sound of cornet make a joyful noise before the Lord the King.

**[Psalm 98:7-8](/bible/psalms/98#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/98#v7)</sup> Let the sea roar, and the fulness thereof; the world, and they that dwell therein.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/98#v8)</sup> Let the floods clap their hands: let the hills be joyful together

- Let the sea roar and the fulness thereof, the world and they that dwell therein.
- Let the floods clap their hands, let the hills be joyful together before the Lord.

**[Psalm 98:9](/bible/psalms/98#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/98#v9)</sup> Before the Lord; for he cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall he judge the world, and the people with equity.

- He cometh to judge the earth: with righteousness shall He judge the world and the people with equity. Righteousness means us keeping God's commandments, observing and doing them, and equity means justice, the practice of giving what is due. By His righteousness He is going to give the people the justice due to them.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)</sup> And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he hath commanded us.

    It shall be our righteousness if we observe to do all these commandments; righteousness is keeping God's commandments.
  - **[Psalm 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness; the commandments are everlasting and are not done away with.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 32:16](/bible/sirach/32#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/32#v16)</sup> They that fear the Lord shall find judgment, and shall kindle justice as a light.

    They that fear the Lord shall find judgment and kindle justice as a light.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 32:24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/32#v24)</sup> He that believeth in the Lord taketh heed to the commandment; and he that trusteth in him shall fare never the worse.

    He that believes in the Lord takes heed to the commandment and shall fare never the worse; that is how you receive the equity, the justice, due to you.
