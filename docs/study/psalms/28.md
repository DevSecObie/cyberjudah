**[Psalms 28:1-2](/bible/psalms/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/28#v1)</sup> Unto thee will I cry, O Lord my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/28#v2)</sup> Hear the voice of my supplications, when I cry unto thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy oracle.

- Unto Thee will I cry; be not silent to me, lest I become like them that go down into the pit.
- When the Most High is silent against us it is the same thing as Hosea: He withdraws and does not listen to our prayers until we seek His face.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    The Most High returns to His place until we acknowledge our offense and seek Him early in our affliction.

**[Psalms 28:3-5](/bible/psalms/28#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/28#v3)</sup> Draw me not away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, which speak peace to their neighbours, but mischief is in their hearts.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/28#v4)</sup> Give them according to their deeds, and according to the wickedness of their endeavours: give them after the work of their hands; render to them their desert.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/28#v5)</sup> Because they regard not the works of the Lord, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them, and not build them up.

- Draw me not away with the wicked, who speak peace to their neighbors but have mischief in their hearts. He is giving clues about the different types of wicked that will be judged.
- Some wickedness is visible and clear, like homosexuality, but there is wickedness you will not pick up on as easily, and men are judged for it.
- Give them according to their deeds, because they regard not the works of the Most High nor the operations of His hand; destroy them and not build them up. That is Esau.
- Esau does not regard the operations of the Most High; they want to be as God, above God, in their sciences and everything their hands touch. But the wicked, whether Esau or our own people, will not be built back up once destroyed. When those bombs hit, they are gone.
  - **[Psalms 55:21](/bible/psalms/55#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/55#v21)</sup> The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.

    His words were smoother than butter but war was in his heart; this wicked is Esau.
  - **[Obadiah 1:3-4](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)</sup> The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/obadiah/1#v4)</sup> Though thou exalt thyself as the eagle, and though thou set thy nest among the stars, thence will I bring thee down, saith the Lord.

    Esau's pride and desire to exalt himself as God brings the Most High's promise to bring him down.

**[Psalms 28:6-9](/bible/psalms/28#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/28#v6)</sup> Blessed be the Lord, because he hath heard the voice of my supplications.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/28#v7)</sup> The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/28#v8)</sup> The Lord is their strength, and he is the saving strength of his anointed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/28#v9)</sup> Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: feed them also, and lift them up for ever.

- Blessed be the Most High, for He heard the voice of supplication; He is strength and shield, the saving strength of His anointed.
- He is being very specific about who is being saved: save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance. Israel is His people, His anointed, His inheritance.
