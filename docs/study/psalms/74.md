Read the chapter → [Psalms 74](/bible/psalms/74)

[Psalm 74](/bible/psalms/74) cries out asking why the Most High has cast off the sheep of His pasture, the congregation He purchased of old and redeemed. The enemy has broken down the sanctuary, cast fire into it, and set up their own signs for signs, replacing the truth with their images, their histories, and their ideologies, just as in slavery when our people were given a white image of God and Christ with no one left to teach who we were. The psalm pleads with the Most High to remember the covenant, to plead His own cause, and not to forget the congregation of the poor forever, tying back to the promise that Israel will be saved with an everlasting salvation.

**[Psalms 74:1-3](/bible/psalms/74#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/74#v1)</sup> O God, why hast thou cast us off for ever? why doth thine anger smoke against the sheep of thy pasture?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/74#v2)</sup> Remember thy congregation, which thou hast purchased of old; the rod of thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed; this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/74#v3)</sup> Lift up thy feet unto the perpetual desolations; even all that the enemy hath done wickedly in the sanctuary.

- The psalmist asks why the Most High has cast us off forever and why His anger smokes against the sheep of His pasture, which is Israel.
- He asks the Most High to remember the congregation He purchased of old, the rod of His inheritance He redeemed, this Mount Zion where He dwelt.
- The Most High purchased us when He instituted animal sacrifice, when Moses sprinkled the blood over the people.
  - **[Exodus 24:6-8](/bible/exodus/24#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/24#v6)</sup> And Moses took half of the blood, and put it in basons; and half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/24#v7)</sup> And he took the book of the covenant, and read in the audience of the people: and they said, All that the Lord hath said will we do, and be obedient.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/24#v8)</sup> And Moses took the blood, and sprinkled it on the people, and said, Behold the blood of the covenant, which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words.

    Moses sprinkled the blood of the covenant on the people, sealing them as the purchased congregation.

**[Psalms 74:4-8](/bible/psalms/74#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/74#v4)</sup> Thine enemies roar in the midst of thy congregations; they set up their ensigns for signs.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/74#v5)</sup> A man was famous according as he had lifted up axes upon the thick trees.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/74#v6)</sup> But now they break down the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/74#v7)</sup> They have cast fire into thy sanctuary, they have defiled by casting down the dwelling place of thy name to the ground.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/74#v8)</sup> They said in their hearts, Let us destroy them together: they have burned up all the synagogues of God in the land.

- The enemies roar in the midst of the congregations and set up their ensigns for signs; a man was famous as he lifted up axes upon the thick trees.
- The ensigns set up for signs are the other nations setting up their images, histories, and laws for us to follow. In school you get American history, French history, all the histories of their people, never ours.
- They break down the carved work with axes and hammers and cast fire into the sanctuary, defiling the dwelling place of the Most High's name to the ground.
- They said in their hearts, let us destroy them together, and burned up all the synagogues of God in the land.
  - **[1 Maccabees 3:48](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v48)**
    > <sup>[48](/bible/1-maccabees/3#v48)</sup> And laid open the book of the law, wherein the heathen had sought to paint the likeness of their images.

    The heathen sought to paint the likeness of their images in the book of the law, setting up their own patterns.

**[Psalms 74:9-11](/bible/psalms/74#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/74#v9)</sup> We see not our signs: there is no more any prophet: neither is there among us any that knoweth how long.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/74#v10)</sup> O God, how long shall the adversary reproach? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/74#v11)</sup> Why withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? pluck it out of thy bosom.

- There is no more any sign, no prophet, and none who knows how long. In slavery there was a time when our image of Christ was replaced with a white image and no one could teach us who we were or how long, because the Most High cast us off.
- Even today the other nations fight to keep this alive: the Christian church ideologies, their politics, their government, everything they set up.
- He asks how long the adversary shall reproach and whether the enemy shall blaspheme the Most High's name forever, and why He withdraws His hand.
- He asks the Most High to pluck His right hand out of His bosom, meaning show forth His righteousness and strength against the other nations.

**[Psalms 74:12-17](/bible/psalms/74#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/74#v12)</sup> For God is my King of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/74#v13)</sup> Thou didst divide the sea by thy strength: thou brakest the heads of the dragons in the waters.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/74#v14)</sup> Thou brakest the heads of leviathan in pieces, and gavest him to be meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/74#v15)</sup> Thou didst cleave the fountain and the flood: thou driedst up mighty rivers.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/74#v16)</sup> The day is thine, the night also is thine: thou hast prepared the light and the sun.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/74#v17)</sup> Thou hast set all the borders of the earth: thou hast made summer and winter.

- God is his King of old, working salvation in the midst of the earth; He divided the sea by His strength and broke the heads of the dragons in the waters.
- He broke the heads of leviathan and gave him as meat to the people inhabiting the wilderness, and He cleaved the fountain and the flood and dried up mighty rivers.
- The day is His and the night also; He prepared the light and the sun, referring back to Genesis chapter one when He created all things on the earth.
- He set all the borders of the earth and made summer and winter.

**[Psalms 74:18-23](/bible/psalms/74#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/74#v18)</sup> Remember this, that the enemy hath reproached, O Lord, and that the foolish people have blasphemed thy name.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/74#v19)</sup> O deliver not the soul of thy turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked: forget not the congregation of thy poor for ever.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/74#v20)</sup> Have respect unto the covenant: for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/74#v21)</sup> O let not the oppressed return ashamed: let the poor and needy praise thy name.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/74#v22)</sup> Arise, O God, plead thine own cause: remember how the foolish man reproacheth thee daily.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/74#v23)</sup> Forget not the voice of thine enemies: the tumult of those that rise up against thee increaseth continually.

- Remember that the enemy has reproached and the foolish people have blasphemed His name; do not deliver the soul of His turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked, and forget not the congregation of the poor forever.
- The congregation of the poor is the Israelites; have respect unto the covenant, for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty. The covenant is that His people would inherit the Kingdom of Heaven, established from Adam and Shem onward.
- Let not the oppressed return ashamed; let the poor and needy praise His name. Our people will not return to the Lord ashamed; the prophecies show Israel will be redeemed with a mighty salvation.
- Arise, O God, plead thine own cause, and remember how the foolish man reproaches daily; the enemy's tumult increases continually, thinking evil upon their beds every day on how to oppress and crush Israel.
- We must keep in mind it is because of our own doing; He put us in captivity for our disobedience. As long as we come with a clean heart and fix what we must fix, there is no issue for those repenting.
  - **[Isaiah 45:17](/bible/isaiah/45#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/isaiah/45#v17)</sup> But Israel shall be saved in the Lord with an everlasting salvation: ye shall not be ashamed nor confounded world without end.

    Israel shall be saved in the Lord with an everlasting salvation and shall not be ashamed nor confounded, world without end.
  - **[Psalms 119:140](/bible/psalms/119#v140)**
    > <sup>[140](/bible/psalms/119#v140)</sup> Thy word is very pure: therefore thy servant loveth it.

    Thy word is very pure, therefore thy servant loveth it, the mark of a pure-hearted reading of the scriptures.
