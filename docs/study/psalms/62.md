Read the chapter → [Psalms 62](/bible/psalms/62)

[Psalm 62](/bible/psalms/62) is the last chapter of the session, a psalm of David to Jeduthun. The soul waits only upon God, who alone is the rock, the salvation, and the defense. The teacher lifts up verse 5 as the heavy point: a lot of us expect things from men and expect people not to let us down, but let your expectations be of God as David's expectations were of God. You cannot change anybody, but God can. Expect great things from the Most High and you do great things yourself.

**[Psalms 62:1-4](/bible/psalms/62#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/62#v1)</sup> Truly my soul waiteth upon God: from him cometh my salvation.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/62#v2)</sup> He only is my rock and my salvation; he is my defence; I shall not be greatly moved.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/62#v3)</sup> How long will ye imagine mischief against a man? ye shall be slain all of you: as a bowing wall shall ye be, and as a tottering fence.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/62#v4)</sup> They only consult to cast him down from his excellency: they delight in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah.

- Truly David's soul waits upon God, from whom comes his salvation; He only is his rock and salvation and defense, so he shall not be greatly moved.
- He rebukes those who imagine mischief against a man, who shall be slain like a bowing wall and a tottering fence; they consult to cast him down from his excellency, delight in lies, and bless with their mouth but curse inwardly.

**[Psalms 62:5-8](/bible/psalms/62#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/62#v5)</sup> My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation is from him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/62#v6)</sup> He only is my rock and my salvation: he is my defence; I shall not be moved.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/62#v7)</sup> In God is my salvation and my glory: the rock of my strength, and my refuge, is in God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/62#v8)</sup> Trust in him at all times; ye people, pour out your heart before him: God is a refuge for us. Selah.

- David tells his own soul to wait only upon God, for his expectation is from Him; He only is his rock, salvation, defense, glory, and refuge.
- A lot of us expect things from men and expect people not to let us down. "I expected more of you," "I expected you to be this way," and that is your problem. Let your expectations be of God, as David's expectations were of God. You cannot change anybody, but God can.
- Trust in Him at all times, pour out your heart before Him, for God is a refuge for us.

**[Psalms 62:9-12](/bible/psalms/62#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/62#v9)</sup> Surely men of low degree are vanity, and men of high degree are a lie: to be laid in the balance, they are altogether lighter than vanity.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/62#v10)</sup> Trust not in oppression, and become not vain in robbery: if riches increase, set not your heart upon them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)</sup> God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/62#v12)</sup> Also unto thee, O Lord, belongeth mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work.

- Men of low degree are vanity and men of high degree are a lie; laid in the balance they are altogether lighter than vanity. Trust not in oppression, become not vain in robbery, and if riches increase, set not your heart upon them.
- God has spoken once, twice have we heard this: that power belongs to God, and to Him belongs mercy, for He renders to every man according to his work.
- Expect great things from the Most High and you do great things yourself.
