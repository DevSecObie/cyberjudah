Read the chapter → [Psalms 67](/bible/psalms/67)

This session moves through [Psalms 67](/bible/psalms/67) through 70, read for the nation four chapters a day. [Psalm 67](/bible/psalms/67) asks the Most High to be merciful and to bless us so that His way is known upon the earth and His saving health among all nations. The teaching ties the blessing of the psalm directly to Deuteronomy 28: the blessings are national, not the individual blessing that people today claim for themselves. Our forefathers understood that when we were blessed as a nation of people, we were the ruling class, and the earth yielding her increase is the very increase promised for keeping the commandments.

**[Psalms 67:1-2](/bible/psalms/67#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/67#v1)</sup> God be merciful unto us, and bless us; and cause his face to shine upon us; Selah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/67#v2)</sup> That thy way may be known upon earth, thy saving health among all nations.

- The plea is for the Most High to be merciful, to bless us, and to cause His face to shine upon us.
- The purpose of the blessing is that His way may be known upon the earth and His saving health among all nations.

**[Psalms 67:3-5](/bible/psalms/67#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/67#v3)</sup> Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/67#v4)</sup> O let the nations be glad and sing for joy: for thou shalt judge the people righteously, and govern the nations upon earth. Selah.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/67#v5)</sup> Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee.

- The call is for all the people to praise the Most High, for the nations to be glad and sing for joy.
- He shall judge the people righteously and govern the nations upon the earth. This is us going into rulership.
- The blessing our forefathers understood was that when we were blessed as a nation of people, we were the ruling class.

**[Psalms 67:6-7](/bible/psalms/67#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/67#v6)</sup> Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/67#v7)</sup> God shall bless us; and all the ends of the earth shall fear him.

- Then the earth shall yield her increase and our own God shall bless us, and all the ends of the earth shall fear Him.
- This is what the psalmist was referring to: when we keep the commandments of God, we as a nation come under the blessings of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28). The ground yielding its increase is exactly that promise.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:1-5](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v2)</sup> And all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v3)</sup> Blessed shalt thou be in the city, and blessed shalt thou be in the field.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v4)</sup> Blessed shall be the fruit of thy body, and the fruit of thy ground, and the fruit of thy cattle, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v5)</sup> Blessed shall be thy basket and thy store.

    Hearken diligently to His commandments and the Most High sets thee on high above all nations; blessed in the city and field, and the fruit of thy body and thy ground and thy cattle.
