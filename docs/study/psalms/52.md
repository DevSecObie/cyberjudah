**[Psalms 52:1](/bible/psalms/52#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/52#v1)</sup> Why boastest thou thyself in mischief, O mighty man? the goodness of God endureth continually.

- To the chief musician, Maschil, a Psalm of David, written when Doeg the Edomite came and told Saul that David had come to the house of Ahimelech. Why boastest thou thyself in mischief, O mighty man?
- The man of sin has to be revealed, and our people still do not want to acknowledge that Esau is the devil, not Christ. The white picture of Jesus is a deception; the Bible says nothing like that.
  - **[Obadiah 1:3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)</sup> The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?

    The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock; this is a proud man.
  - **[Isaiah 14:13](/bible/isaiah/14#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/14#v13)</sup> For thou hast said in thine heart, I will ascend into heaven, I will exalt my throne above the stars of God: I will sit also upon the mount of the congregation, in the sides of the north:

    He said in his heart he would ascend into heaven, exalt his throne above the stars of God, and sit upon the mount of the congregation in the sides of the north.
  - **[1 Chronicles 28:8](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-chronicles/28#v8)</sup> Now therefore in the sight of all Israel the congregation of the Lord, and in the audience of our God, keep and seek for all the commandments of the Lord your God: that ye may possess this good land, and leave it for an inheritance for your children after you for ever.

    The congregation of the Lord in the sight of all Israel; Esau boasts he will rule over the congregation of the Most High in the north, and Israel is the audience of the Bible.
  - **[Isaiah 14:14](/bible/isaiah/14#v14)**

    > <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/2#v6)</sup> Ho, ho, come forth, and flee from the land of the north, saith the Lord: for I have spread you abroad as the four winds of the heaven, saith the Lord.

    "Ho, ho" is what he says coming through; flee from the land of the north where he rules over the Israelites, scattered abroad as the four winds.
  - **[Zechariah 2:7](/bible/zechariah/2#v7)**

    Deliver thyself, O Zion, that dwellest with the daughter of Babylon; this America is mystery Babylon.
  - **[Isaiah 14:15](/bible/isaiah/14#v15)**

    Yet thou shalt be brought down to hell, to the sides of the pit.

    They that see thee shall narrowly look upon thee, saying, Is this the man that made the earth to tremble; Satan is a man, Lucifer is a man.
  - **[Isaiah 14:12](/bible/isaiah/14#v12)**

    How art thou fallen from heaven, O Lucifer; the man of sin is revealed as a man, not a horned figure with a pitchfork.
