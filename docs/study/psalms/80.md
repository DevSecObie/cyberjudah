[Psalm 80](/bible/psalms/80) cries out to the Shepherd of Israel to turn us again and cause His face to shine that we may be saved. The heavy point in this chapter is the vine: the Most High brought a vine out of Egypt, cast out the heathen, and planted it. That vine is Israel. We are that vine that the Lord brought out of Egypt.

**[Psalms 80:1-3](/bible/psalms/80#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/80#v1)</sup> Give ear, O Shepherd of Israel, thou that leadest Joseph like a flock; thou that dwellest between the cherubims, shine forth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/80#v2)</sup> Before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh stir up thy strength, and come and save us.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/80#v3)</sup> Turn us again, O God, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.

- The prayer calls on the Shepherd of Israel that leads Joseph like a flock and dwells between the cherubims to shine forth before Ephraim, Benjamin, and Manasseh.
- It asks Him to stir up His strength and come and save us, to turn us again and cause His face to shine that we shall be saved.

**[Psalms 80:4-7](/bible/psalms/80#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/80#v4)</sup> O Lord God of hosts, how long wilt thou be angry against the prayer of thy people?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/80#v5)</sup> Thou feedest them with the bread of tears; and givest them tears to drink in great measure.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/80#v6)</sup> Thou makest us a strife unto our neighbours: and our enemies laugh among themselves.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/80#v7)</sup> Turn us again, O God of hosts, and cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.

- It asks the Lord God of hosts how long He will be angry against the prayer of His people. He has fed them with the bread of tears and given them tears to drink in great measure.
- The bread of tears goes into affliction, being afflicted because of our oppression. Isaiah speaks of the Lord giving us the bread of adversity and the water of affliction.
- He has made us a strife unto our neighbors, and our enemies laugh among themselves. Again the refrain: turn us again, O God of hosts, and cause Thy face to shine, and we shall be saved.
- Causing His face to shine goes into God having mercy on us, loving us, giving us strength, being on our behalf and fighting for us. It is like when Moses came down from the mountain and his face shone.
  - **[Isaiah 30:20](/bible/isaiah/30#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/30#v20)</sup> And though the Lord give you the bread of adversity, and the water of affliction, yet shall not thy teachers be removed into a corner any more, but thine eyes shall see thy teachers:

    The Lord gives the bread of adversity and the water of affliction.

**[Psalms 80:8-11](/bible/psalms/80#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/80#v8)</sup> Thou hast brought a vine out of Egypt: thou hast cast out the heathen, and planted it.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/80#v9)</sup> Thou preparedst room before it, and didst cause it to take deep root, and it filled the land.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/80#v10)</sup> The hills were covered with the shadow of it, and the boughs thereof were like the goodly cedars.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/80#v11)</sup> She sent out her boughs unto the sea, and her branches unto the river.

- The Most High brought a vine out of Egypt, cast out the heathen, and planted it. This is the israelites; we are that vine the Lord brought out of Egypt. He cast out the canaanites and planted us in Jerusalem and the land of Israel.
- He prepared room before it and caused it to take deep root, and it filled the land. This is how we grew as a nation.
- The hills were covered with the shadow of it, the boughs were like the goodly cedars, and she sent out her boughs unto the sea and her branches unto the river.

**[Psalms 80:12-15](/bible/psalms/80#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/80#v12)</sup> Why hast thou then broken down her hedges, so that all they which pass by the way do pluck her?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/80#v13)</sup> The boar out of the wood doth waste it, and the wild beast of the field doth devour it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/80#v14)</sup> Return, we beseech thee, O God of hosts: look down from heaven, and behold, and visit this vine;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/80#v15)</sup> And the vineyard which thy right hand hath planted, and the branch that thou madest strong for thyself.

- Why then has He broken down her hedges, so that all they which pass by the way do pluck her? The "her" is Israel, that same vine brought out of Egypt.
- The boar out of the wood wastes it, and the wild beast of the field devours it.
- The plea is that the God of hosts return, look down from heaven, behold, and visit this vine and the vineyard which His right hand has planted, and the branch that He made strong for Himself.

**[Psalms 80:16-19](/bible/psalms/80#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/80#v16)</sup> It is burned with fire, it is cut down: they perish at the rebuke of thy countenance.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/80#v17)</sup> Let thy hand be upon the man of thy right hand, upon the son of man whom thou madest strong for thyself.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/80#v18)</sup> So will not we go back from thee: quicken us, and we will call upon thy name.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/80#v19)</sup> Turn us again, O Lord God of hosts, cause thy face to shine; and we shall be saved.

- It is burned with fire and cut down; they perish at the rebuke of His countenance. His countenance is the face of God. When God came down in Exodus twenty, the people were in fear at His presence, not seeing His literal face but in fear because He is an omnipotent power.
- Let His hand be upon the man of His right hand, upon the son of man whom He made strong for Himself; so will we not go back from Him. Quicken us and we will call upon Thy name.
- The closing refrain once more: turn us again, O Lord God of hosts, cause Thy face to shine, and we shall be saved. Causing His face to shine is Him taking us out of captivity and blessing us, the opposite of when He set His face against us.
