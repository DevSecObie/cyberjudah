Read the chapter → [Psalms 88](/bible/psalms/88)

[Psalm 88](/bible/psalms/88) is a cry of a soul full of troubles, whose life draws near unto the grave. Much of it lines up with what was prophesied in [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22) concerning Christ, when the crucifixion was being foretold. The teaching shows this is prophecy of our forefathers and is relevant to us today.

**[Psalms 88:1-5](/bible/psalms/88#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/88#v1)</sup> O Lord God of my salvation, I have cried day and night before thee:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/88#v2)</sup> Let my prayer come before thee: incline thine ear unto my cry;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/88#v3)</sup> For my soul is full of troubles: and my life draweth nigh unto the grave.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/88#v4)</sup> I am counted with them that go down into the pit: I am as a man that hath no strength:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/88#v5)</sup> Free among the dead, like the slain that lie in the grave, whom thou rememberest no more: and they are cut off from thy hand.

- The prayer cries day and night before the Most High, asking that his prayer come before Him and that He incline His ear unto his cry, for his soul is full of troubles and his life draws near unto the grave.
- He is counted with them that go down into the pit and is as a man that has no strength. This sounds like some of the same things said in [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22), when Christ was prophesying about what happened to Christ, the cross being prophesied.

**[Psalms 88:6-9](/bible/psalms/88#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/88#v6)</sup> Thou hast laid me in the lowest pit, in darkness, in the deeps.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/88#v7)</sup> Thy wrath lieth hard upon me, and thou hast afflicted me with all thy waves. Selah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/88#v8)</sup> Thou hast put away mine acquaintance far from me; thou hast made me an abomination unto them: I am shut up, and I cannot come forth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/88#v9)</sup> Mine eye mourneth by reason of affliction: Lord, I have called daily upon thee, I have stretched out my hands unto thee.

- The Most High has laid him in the lowest pit, in darkness, in the deep; His wrath lieth hard upon him and He has afflicted him with all His waves. He has put away his acquaintance far from him and made him an abomination unto them; he is shut up and cannot come forth.
- His eye mourneth by reason of affliction; he has called daily upon the Most High and stretched out his hands unto Him. When the Lord turns His face from us, a lot of this stuff is relevant to us today, like the dead bodies laying in the streets of the great city.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    In our afflictions we will seek Him early, so we cry daily to the Lord in repentance until He hear us.
  - **[1 Kings 8:47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;

    We cry daily to the Lord keeping the commandments and the faith in Christ until He hear us.

**[Psalms 88:10-12](/bible/psalms/88#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/88#v10)</sup> Wilt thou shew wonders to the dead? shall the dead arise and praise thee? Selah.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/88#v11)</sup> Shall thy lovingkindness be declared in the grave? or thy faithfulness in destruction?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/88#v12)</sup> Shall thy wonders be known in the dark? and thy righteousness in the land of forgetfulness?

- He asks whether the Most High will show wonders to the dead, whether the dead shall arise and praise Him, whether His lovingkindness shall be declared in the grave or His faithfulness in destruction.
- He asks whether His wonders shall be known in the dark and His righteousness in the land of forgetfulness. You can't serve God when you're dead.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 9:10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)</sup> Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.

    There is no work and no devices in the grave; you cannot serve God when you are dead.

**[Psalms 88:13-18](/bible/psalms/88#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/88#v13)</sup> But unto thee have I cried, O Lord; and in the morning shall my prayer prevent thee.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/88#v14)</sup> Lord, why castest thou off my soul? why hidest thou thy face from me?
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/88#v15)</sup> I am afflicted and ready to die from my youth up: while I suffer thy terrors I am distracted.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/88#v16)</sup> Thy fierce wrath goeth over me; thy terrors have cut me off.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/88#v17)</sup> They came round about me daily like water; they compassed me about together.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/88#v18)</sup> Lover and friend hast thou put far from me, and mine acquaintance into darkness.

- He cries unto the Most High, and in the morning his prayer shall prevent Him; he asks why the Lord casts off his soul and hides His face from him. He is afflicted, like Christ said, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani, my God why are you so far from me.
- He is afflicted and ready to die from his youth up, suffering the terrors of the Most High; His fierce wrath goes over him and His terrors have cut him off, like Christ was cut off from the land of the living.
- They came round about him daily like water, dogs have compassed him, like it says in [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22); lover and friend are put far from him and his acquaintance into darkness. These are the prophecies of our forefathers, and this is the real black history, the Most High declaring the end from the beginning to strengthen our faith.
  - **[Psalms 22:16](/bible/psalms/22#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/22#v16)</sup> For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet.

    For dogs have compassed me; they compassed Christ about as prophesied here.
  - **[Psalms 62:11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/62#v11)</sup> God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power belongeth unto God.

    The Lord gave the word; the forefathers wrote down what the Lord said through them.
