**[Psalms 106:1-5](/bible/psalms/106#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/106#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/106#v2)</sup> Who can utter the mighty acts of the Lord? who can shew forth all his praise?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/106#v3)</sup> Blessed are they that keep judgment, and he that doeth righteousness at all times.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/106#v4)</sup> Remember me, O Lord, with the favour that thou bearest unto thy people: O visit me with thy salvation;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/106#v5)</sup> That I may see the good of thy chosen, that I may rejoice in the gladness of thy nation, that I may glory with thine inheritance.

- Give thanks unto the Most High, for He is good and His mercy endures forever; who can show forth all His praise.
- Blessed are they that keep judgment and do righteousness at all times. At all times, not only when convenient.
- David asks to be remembered with the favor toward His people, to see the good of His chosen and rejoice in the gladness of His nation.

**[Psalms 106:6-12](/bible/psalms/106#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/106#v6)</sup> We have sinned with our fathers, we have committed iniquity, we have done wickedly.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/106#v7)</sup> Our fathers understood not thy wonders in Egypt; they remembered not the multitude of thy mercies; but provoked him at the sea, even at the Red sea.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/106#v8)</sup> Nevertheless he saved them for his name’s sake, that he might make his mighty power to be known.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/106#v9)</sup> He rebuked the Red sea also, and it was dried up: so he led them through the depths, as through the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/106#v10)</sup> And he saved them from the hand of him that hated them, and redeemed them from the hand of the enemy.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/106#v11)</sup> And the waters covered their enemies: there was not one of them left.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/106#v12)</sup> Then believed they his words; they sang his praise.

- We have sinned with our fathers and done wickedly; our fathers understood not His wonders in Egypt and provoked Him at the Red Sea.
- Nevertheless He saved them for His name's sake, rebuked the Red Sea so it dried up, and led them through the depths as through the wilderness.
- Esau's science says a wind blew it into a tsunami, but the Most High said He rebuked it. The waters covered their enemies, and then they believed His words and sang His praise.

**[Psalms 106:13-23](/bible/psalms/106#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/106#v13)</sup> They soon forgat his works; they waited not for his counsel:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/106#v14)</sup> But lusted exceedingly in the wilderness, and tempted God in the desert.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/106#v15)</sup> And he gave them their request; but sent leanness into their soul.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/106#v16)</sup> They envied Moses also in the camp, and Aaron the saint of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/106#v17)</sup> The earth opened and swallowed up Dathan, and covered the company of Abiram.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/106#v18)</sup> And a fire was kindled in their company; the flame burned up the wicked.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/106#v19)</sup> They made a calf in Horeb, and worshipped the molten image.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/106#v20)</sup> Thus they changed their glory into the similitude of an ox that eateth grass.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/106#v21)</sup> They forgat God their saviour, which had done great things in Egypt;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/106#v22)</sup> Wondrous works in the land of Ham, and terrible things by the Red sea.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/106#v23)</sup> Therefore he said that he would destroy them, had not Moses his chosen stood before him in the breach, to turn away his wrath, lest he should destroy them.

- They soon forgot His works and waited not for His counsel, but lusted exceedingly in the wilderness and tempted God, so He sent leanness into their soul.
- They envied Moses and Aaron the saint of the Lord; the earth swallowed Dathan and the company of Abiram for their insurrection.
- They made a calf and worshipped the molten image, changing their glory into the likeness of an ox that eats grass, forgetting God their savior. He would have destroyed them had not Moses stood in the breach.

>

>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/106#v27)</sup> To overthrow their seed also among the nations, and to scatter them in the lands.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/106#v28)</sup> They joined themselves also unto Baal–peor, and ate the sacrifices of the dead.
>

>

>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/106#v31)</sup> And that was counted unto him for righteousness unto all generations for evermore.

- They despised the pleasant land and believed not His word but brought up a false report and murmured in their tents; therefore He lifted His hand to overthrow them and scatter their seed among the nations.
- They joined themselves to Baal-peor and ate the sacrifices of the dead, provoking Him to anger, and the plague broke in upon them.
- Then Phinehas stood up and executed judgment, and the plague was stayed; it was counted to him for righteousness unto all generations, for he had zeal in the fear of the Most High.

**[Psalms 106:32-39](/bible/psalms/106#v32)**
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/106#v32)</sup> They angered him also at the waters of strife, so that it went ill with Moses for their sakes:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/106#v33)</sup> Because they provoked his spirit, so that he spake unadvisedly with his lips.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/106#v34)</sup> They did not destroy the nations, concerning whom the Lord commanded them:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/106#v35)</sup> But were mingled among the heathen, and learned their works.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/106#v36)</sup> And they served their idols: which were a snare unto them.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/psalms/106#v37)</sup> Yea, they sacrificed their sons and their daughters unto devils,
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/psalms/106#v38)</sup> And shed innocent blood, even the blood of their sons and of their daughters, whom they sacrificed unto the idols of Canaan: and the land was polluted with blood.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/psalms/106#v39)</sup> Thus were they defiled with their own works, and went a whoring with their own inventions.

- They angered Him at the waters of strife, so it went ill with Moses, who spoke unadvisedly with his lips. Moses lost the promised land because he smote the rock and called the people rebels instead of speaking to it and sanctifying the Most High.
- They did not destroy the nations as commanded but were mingled among the heathen and learned their works.
- They served idols, sacrificed their sons and daughters to devils, and shed innocent blood, polluting the land and going a-whoring with their own inventions.

**[Psalms 106:40-48](/bible/psalms/106#v40)**
> <sup>[40](/bible/psalms/106#v40)</sup> Therefore was the wrath of the Lord kindled against his people, insomuch that he abhorred his own inheritance.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/psalms/106#v41)</sup> And he gave them into the hand of the heathen; and they that hated them ruled over them.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/psalms/106#v42)</sup> Their enemies also oppressed them, and they were brought into subjection under their hand.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/psalms/106#v43)</sup> Many times did he deliver them; but they provoked him with their counsel, and were brought low for their iniquity.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/psalms/106#v44)</sup> Nevertheless he regarded their affliction, when he heard their cry:
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/psalms/106#v45)</sup> And he remembered for them his covenant, and repented according to the multitude of his mercies.
>
> <sup>[46](/bible/psalms/106#v46)</sup> He made them also to be pitied of all those that carried them captives.
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/psalms/106#v47)</sup> Save us, O Lord our God, and gather us from among the heathen, to give thanks unto thy holy name, and to triumph in thy praise.
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/psalms/106#v48)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting to everlasting: and let all the people say, Amen. Praise ye the Lord.

- The wrath of the Most High was kindled against His people so that He abhorred His own inheritance and gave them into the hand of the heathen, who ruled over them.
- The heathen ruling in our communities today, the legal system and the government, is the law and the prophets agreeing; He delivered them many times, but they provoked Him and were brought low for their iniquity.
- Nevertheless He regarded their affliction, remembered His covenant, and repented according to His mercies; David prays: save us and gather us from among the heathen. Blessed be the Lord God of Israel from everlasting to everlasting.
