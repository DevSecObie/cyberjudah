**[Psalm 50:1-3](/bible/psalms/50#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/50#v1)</sup> The mighty God, even the Lord, hath spoken, and called the earth from the rising of the sun unto the going down thereof.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/50#v2)</sup> Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, God hath shined.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/50#v3)</sup> Our God shall come, and shall not keep silence: a fire shall devour before him, and it shall be very tempestuous round about him.

- The mighty God, the Most High, has spoken and called the earth from the rising of the sun to its going down, from east to west.
- Out of Zion, the perfection of beauty, the Most High has shined; Zion is the people, the land mass, the nation and the city, though right now it does not look like it.
- When the Most High brings destruction and gives redemption Israel will be beautiful, no more fat, diabetes, heart disease or cancer, but the perfection of beauty.
- The Most High shall come and not keep silence; a fire shall devour before him and it shall be very tempestuous round about him, as Christ comes back with fire.

**[Psalm 50:4-6](/bible/psalms/50#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/50#v4)</sup> He shall call to the heavens from above, and to the earth, that he may judge his people.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/50#v6)</sup> And the heavens shall declare his righteousness: for God is judge himself. Selah.

- The Most High calls the heavens above and the earth that he may judge his people, gathering his saints who made a covenant with him by sacrifice, which is the Israelites.
- He sends his angels with a great sound of a trumpet to gather his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other; this is what the world calls the rapture, and only the children of Israel are gathered.
- While Christ brings destruction on the nations himself, most of the angels are used to gather Israel up; the Most High destroys and the angels gather.
- The heavens declare his righteousness, for the Most High is judge himself.
  - **[Matthew 24:30-31](/bible/matthew/24#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/24#v30)</sup> And then shall appear the sign of the Son of man in heaven: and then shall all the tribes of the earth mourn, and they shall see the Son of man coming in the clouds of heaven with power and great glory.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/24#v31)</sup> And he shall send his angels with a great sound of a trumpet, and they shall gather together his elect from the four winds, from one end of heaven to the other.

    The sign of the Son of man appears, all tribes of the earth mourn, they see him coming in the clouds with power and great glory, and he sends his angels with a great sound of a trumpet to gather his elect from the four winds.

**[Psalm 50:7-15](/bible/psalms/50#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/50#v7)</sup> Hear, O my people, and I will speak; O Israel, and I will testify against thee: I am God, even thy God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/50#v8)</sup> I will not reprove thee for thy sacrifices or thy burnt offerings, to have been continually before me.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/50#v9)</sup> I will take no bullock out of thy house, nor he goats out of thy folds.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/50#v10)</sup> For every beast of the forest is mine, and the cattle upon a thousand hills.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/50#v11)</sup> I know all the fowls of the mountains: and the wild beasts of the field are mine.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/50#v12)</sup> If I were hungry, I would not tell thee: for the world is mine, and the fulness thereof.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/50#v13)</sup> Will I eat the flesh of bulls, or drink the blood of goats?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/50#v14)</sup> Offer unto God thanksgiving; and pay thy vows unto the most High:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/50#v15)</sup> And call upon me in the day of trouble: I will deliver thee, and thou shalt glorify me.

- The Most High testifies against Israel as their God but will not reprove them for their sacrifices or burnt offerings, for he takes no bullock or he goats out of their houses.
- Every beast of the forest and the cattle on a thousand hills are already his; the world and its fullness are his, and if he were hungry he would not tell.
- The Most High will not eat the flesh of bulls or drink the blood of goats; instead offer thanksgiving and pay thy vows unto the Most High, showing he was doing away with animal sacrifice.
- This is the mercy prophesied in the New Testament: the Most High will have mercy and not sacrifice, for the blood of bulls and goats cannot take away sin, which is why Christ had to die.
- Call upon the Most High in the day of trouble and he will deliver thee and thou shalt glorify him.
  - **[Matthew 9:13](/bible/matthew/9#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/9#v13)</sup> But go ye and learn what that meaneth, I will have mercy, and not sacrifice: for I am not come to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.

    Go and learn what this meaneth, I will have mercy and not sacrifice, for I am not come to call the righteous but sinners to repentance.
  - **[Hosea 6:6](/bible/hosea/6#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hosea/6#v6)</sup> For I desired mercy, and not sacrifice; and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.

    The Most High desired mercy and not sacrifice, and the knowledge of God more than burnt offerings.
  - **[Hebrews 10:4](/bible/hebrews/10#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/10#v4)</sup> For it is not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away sins.

    It is not possible that the blood of bulls and of goats should take away sins.

**[Psalm 50:16-18](/bible/psalms/50#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/50#v16)</sup> But unto the wicked God saith, What hast thou to do to declare my statutes, or that thou shouldest take my covenant in thy mouth?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/50#v17)</sup> Seeing thou hatest instruction, and castest my words behind thee.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/50#v18)</sup> When thou sawest a thief, then thou consentedst with him, and hast been partaker with adulterers.

- To the wicked the Most High says, what hast thou to do to declare my statutes or take my covenant in thy mouth? The covenant is not for the nations, and the Edomite on the street preaching a new covenant for all nations is not according to the Most High.
- The wicked is Esau, the so-called white man, the number one culprit on the Most High's list, who says he is impoverished but will rebuild the desolate places, and whom the Most High calls the border of wickedness forever.
- The heathen cannot keep the commandments; they never have and never will, as Ezra testified that Israel by name has kept the precepts but not the heathen. Esau cannot even stop lying or admit that Israel are the children of Israel.
- The wicked hate instruction and cast the words behind them; when they saw a thief they consented, and when Babylon came to raze Jerusalem Esau was there saying raze it, partaking with the thief against his brother Jacob.
  - **[Malachi 1:4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)</sup> Whereas Edom saith, We are impoverished, but we will return and build the desolate places; thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall build, but I will throw down; and they shall call them, The border of wickedness, and, The people against whom the Lord hath indignation for ever.

    Whereas Edom saith they will return and build the desolate places, the Most High of hosts will throw down, and they shall be called the border of wickedness and the people against whom the Most High hath indignation forever.
  - **[2 Esdras 3:36](/bible/2-esdras/3#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/2-esdras/3#v36)</sup> Thou shalt find that Israel by name hath kept thy precepts; but not the heathen.

    Israel by name have kept the Most High's precepts, but not the heathen.

**[Psalm 50:19-21](/bible/psalms/50#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/50#v19)</sup> Thou givest thy mouth to evil, and thy tongue frameth deceit.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/50#v20)</sup> Thou sittest and speakest against thy brother; thou slanderest thine own mother’s son.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/50#v21)</sup> These things hast thou done, and I kept silence; thou thoughtest that I was altogether such an one as thyself: but I will reprove thee, and set them in order before thine eyes.

- The wicked give their mouth to evil and their tongue frames deceit; all they do is lie, and they slander their own mother's son, portraying Israel as thugs and thots in the media.
- Esau accuses Israel day and night, saying they are not Israelites, that Jews are white and live in Israel, that Israel are only African-American, Negroes, Hispanic and Native Indian, and that Christ did away with the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28).
- Because the Most High kept silence while the curses were fulfilled, Esau grew prideful, saying the Most High looks like him and painting images in his own likeness, denying that Christ is black.
- The Most High thought silent, and the wicked thought he was such a one as themselves, but he will reprove them and set their sins in order before their eyes.
  - **[Revelation 12:10](/bible/revelation/12#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/12#v10)</sup> And I heard a loud voice saying in heaven, Now is come salvation, and strength, and the kingdom of our God, and the power of his Christ: for the accuser of our brethren is cast down, which accused them before our God day and night.

    The accuser of the brethren, who accused them before the Most High day and night, is cast down when salvation and the kingdom of the Most High and the power of his Christ are come.

**[Psalm 50:22-23](/bible/psalms/50#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/50#v22)</sup> Now consider this, ye that forget God, lest I tear you in pieces, and there be none to deliver.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/50#v23)</sup> Whoso offereth praise glorifieth me: and to him that ordereth his conversation aright will I shew the salvation of God.

- The Most High is raising up the Israelites today, not only Israel United in Christ but other Israelite camps doing real work, and this exceeding great army is set before the nations to reprove Esau and expose him as the man of sin and a liar.
- Consider this, ye that forget God, lest he tear you in pieces and there be none to deliver; the nations that forget the Most High face this judgment.
- Whoso offereth praise glorifieth the Most High, and to him that ordereth his conversation aright he will show the salvation of God, which goes to Israel; the Israelites will see the salvation of God.
