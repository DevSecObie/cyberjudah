Read the chapter → [Psalms 44](/bible/psalms/44)

[Psalm 44](/bible/psalms/44) recounts how the fathers took the land, not by their own strength but by the right hand of the Most High, and then turns to lament the present captivity. The teaching drives home that the scattering, the shame, the byword status, and the spoiling all trace back to breaking the commandments. This is the difference between coming into the truth and reading and studying, versus staying in Christianity not knowing who your enemy really is.

**[Psalms 44:1-3](/bible/psalms/44#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/44#v1)</sup> We have heard with our ears, O God, our fathers have told us, what work thou didst in their days, in the times of old.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/44#v2)</sup> How thou didst drive out the heathen with thy hand, and plantedst them; how thou didst afflict the people, and cast them out.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/44#v3)</sup> For they got not the land in possession by their own sword, neither did their own arm save them: but thy right hand, and thine arm, and the light of thy countenance, because thou hadst a favour unto them.

- The fathers told what work the Most High did in the times of old, driving out the heathen and planting Israel in the land.
- Israel did not get the land by their own sword or their own arm, but by the right hand and the light of the countenance of the Most High, because He had favor toward them.
- When brothers ask "were you there?" or "how you know what color he was?" the answer is that the fathers heard and witnessed the Red Sea and the wars, and they told the following generations. We are supposed to search the scriptures.

    Remember the days of old; ask thy father and he will show thee, thy elders and they will tell thee.
  - **[Malachi 4:4-5](/bible/malachi/4#v4)**

    Remember the law of Moses for all Israel; Elijah is sent to turn the heart of the fathers to the children before the great and dreadful day.

**[Psalms 44:4-5](/bible/psalms/44#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/44#v4)</sup> Thou art my King, O God: command deliverances for Jacob.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/44#v5)</sup> Through thee will we push down our enemies: through thy name will we tread them under that rise up against us.

- The Most High is called the king who commands deliverance for Jacob, and through Him the enemies are pushed down.
- We cannot go it alone. Those who take up arms on their own without the Most High have examples of failure; men who went to make a name for themselves fell.
- Treading down the enemy through His name is not screaming a name you believe is the name of the Lord. The name means keeping the commandments.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:1,7](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v7)</sup> The Lord shall cause thine enemies that rise up against thee to be smitten before thy face: they shall come out against thee one way, and flee before thee seven ways.

    Hearken diligently to His commandments and He sets thee on high and causes thine enemies to be smitten before thy face, fleeing seven ways.

**[Psalms 44:6-8](/bible/psalms/44#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/44#v6)</sup> For I will not trust in my bow, neither shall my sword save me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/44#v7)</sup> But thou hast saved us from our enemies, and hast put them to shame that hated us.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/44#v8)</sup> In God we boast all the day long, and praise thy name for ever. Selah.

- David will not trust in his bow, nor will his sword save him; the Most High saved them from their enemies and put to shame those that hate them.
- In God they boast all the day long and praise His name forever. We are looking for that same salvation, which will be the destruction of our enemies.
  - **[Luke 1:70-71](/bible/luke/1#v70)**
    > <sup>[70](/bible/luke/1#v70)</sup> As he spake by the mouth of his holy prophets, which have been since the world began:
    >
    > <sup>[71](/bible/luke/1#v71)</sup> That we should be saved from our enemies, and from the hand of all that hate us;

    Salvation is that we should be saved from our enemies and from the hand of all that hate us.

**[Psalms 44:9-12](/bible/psalms/44#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/44#v9)</sup> But thou hast cast off, and put us to shame; and goest not forth with our armies.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/44#v10)</sup> Thou makest us to turn back from the enemy: and they which hate us spoil for themselves.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/44#v11)</sup> Thou hast given us like sheep appointed for meat; and hast scattered us among the heathen.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/44#v12)</sup> Thou sellest thy people for nought, and dost not increase thy wealth by their price.

- Now the Most High has cast off and put them to shame, and goes not out with their armies; He makes them turn back and those that hate them spoil for themselves.
- They are given like sheep appointed for meat and scattered among the heathen. The enemy gained the power to spoil us because the Most High gave us over for our sins.
- He sold His people for nothing and did not increase His wealth by their price.

    The Most High gave Jacob for a spoil because they would not walk in His ways nor obey His law.

    If thou wilt not hearken, the curses come; thou shalt flee seven ways and be removed into all kingdoms of the earth.

**[Psalms 44:13-14](/bible/psalms/44#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/44#v13)</sup> Thou makest us a reproach to our neighbours, a scorn and a derision to them that are round about us.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/44#v14)</sup> Thou makest us a byword among the heathen, a shaking of the head among the people.

- Israel became a reproach, a scorn, a derision, a byword, and a shaking of the head among the heathen.
- The shaking of the head is because we are an astonishment to our enemies. We went into slavery, our names were changed, our culture and lands were taken, and another nation took our name.

    Thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword among all nations whither the Most High leads thee.

**[Psalms 44:15-16](/bible/psalms/44#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/44#v15)</sup> My confusion is continually before me, and the shame of my face hath covered me,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/44#v16)</sup> For the voice of him that reproacheth and blasphemeth; by reason of the enemy and avenger.

- Confusion is continually before them and the shame of their face covers them, because of the voice of him that reproaches and blasphemes.
- When the enemy looks at us now, they no longer see a perfection of beauty. They see men sagging their pants, women dressed immodestly, whoredom in the songs, and they shake their heads.
- They will call us any name we want except Esau, as long as we keep celebrating Christmas, Thanksgiving, New Year's, the 4th of July, and birthdays. If they keep us in sin, the Most High will not fight our battles.

    Righteousness belongs to the Most High but confusion of faces to all Israel scattered in the countries because they trespassed.

**[Psalms 44:17-19](/bible/psalms/44#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/44#v17)</sup> All this is come upon us; yet have we not forgotten thee, neither have we dealt falsely in thy covenant.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/44#v18)</sup> Our heart is not turned back, neither have our steps declined from thy way;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/44#v19)</sup> Though thou hast sore broken us in the place of dragons, and covered us with the shadow of death.

- All this has come, yet they claim they have not forgotten Him nor dealt falsely in His covenant, and their heart is not turned back.
- Yet He has sore broken them in the place of dragons and covered them with the shadow of death. The broken part is that He brought down their heart with labor and there was none to help.
- This place is spiritual Egypt. The nations dealt wisely with us and put us to hard labor and bondage, and we are the essential workers on the front line.

**[Psalms 44:20-22](/bible/psalms/44#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/44#v20)</sup> If we have forgotten the name of our God, or stretched out our hands to a strange god;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/44#v21)</sup> Shall not God search this out? for he knoweth the secrets of the heart.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/44#v22)</sup> Yea, for thy sake are we killed all the day long; we are counted as sheep for the slaughter.

- If they had forgotten the name of their God or stretched out their hands to a strange god, He would search it out, for He knows the secrets of the heart.
- Forgetting the name again is not the literal name; many run around saying "this is the name" and it has not gotten them out of captivity.
- For His sake they are killed all the day long and counted as sheep for the slaughter. We changed our glory in Christ for that which does not profit.

**[Psalms 44:23-26](/bible/psalms/44#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/44#v23)</sup> Awake, why sleepest thou, O Lord? arise, cast us not off for ever.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/44#v24)</sup> Wherefore hidest thou thy face, and forgettest our affliction and our oppression?
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/44#v25)</sup> For our soul is bowed down to the dust: our belly cleaveth unto the earth.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/44#v26)</sup> Arise for our help, and redeem us for thy mercies’ sake.

- The plea is for the Most High to awake, arise, and not cast off forever, and to stop hiding His face and forgetting the affliction and oppression.
- Their soul is bowed down to the dust and their belly cleaves to the earth; they were once at a higher state and were brought low.
- The face is hidden because of iniquity. Sins separate us from the Most High so that He will not hear.
