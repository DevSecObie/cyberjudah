This session covers [Psalms 51](/bible/psalms/51) through 54, four chapters of David. [Psalm 51](/bible/psalms/51) is David's prayer of repentance after Nathan the prophet confronted him over Bathsheba and the killing of Uriah. The teaching drives home that Israel must put itself in these scriptures: acknowledge our transgressions, understand what sin is, seek to be cleansed by the word, and know that the wages of sin is the death and captivity we are living in. [Psalms 52](/bible/psalms/52) through 54 turn to the boasting of the wicked man, the fool who says there is no God, and David's cry to be saved from the strangers and oppressors who rise against him.

**[Psalms 51:1](/bible/psalms/51#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/51#v1)</sup> Have mercy upon me, O God, according to thy lovingkindness: according unto the multitude of thy tender mercies blot out my transgressions.

- To the chief musician, a Psalm of David, written after Nathan the prophet came to him following his sin with Bathsheba, the wife of Uriah one of the soldiers in David's army.
- Nathan got on David about it and corrected him. Now David sends up this prayer, asking the Most High for forgiveness.
- David appeals to the loving kindness and tender mercies of the Most High, asking that his transgressions be blotted out.

**[Psalms 51:2](/bible/psalms/51#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/51#v2)</sup> Wash me throughly from mine iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.

- David asks to be washed thoroughly from his iniquity and cleansed from his sin. The question is: how are we going to be washed?
  - **[Psalms 119:9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

    We cleanse our ways by taking heed to the word, meaning the Commandments.
  - **[Psalms 119:10](/bible/psalms/119#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/119#v10)</sup> With my whole heart have I sought thee: O let me not wander from thy commandments.

    Seek the Most High with the whole heart and ask not to wander from his Commandments; in a world of evil communication we pray that he keep us on the path clean.
  - **[John 15:3](/bible/john/15#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/john/15#v3)</sup> Now ye are clean through the word which I have spoken unto you.

    We are made clean through the word spoken to us; when we ask to be cleansed we must stand in Christ, abiding in the word to stay on the path of righteousness.

**[Psalms 51:3](/bible/psalms/51#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/51#v3)</sup> For I acknowledge my transgressions: and my sin is ever before me.

- David says, "I acknowledge my transgressions, and my sin is ever before me." That is something we must do: acknowledge our transgressions.
- A lot of people do not think we deserve to come over here on slave ships or deserve to be shot down in the streets or deserve the curses put on us. But we sinned against the Most High.
- The pants your grandma loved wearing, the auntie who thinks she can teach in the church, standing before the scriptures with a covered head, men and women with the head wrong; we set light by the Commandments as if these are small things. We sinned against the Most High.
  - **[1 Kings 8:46](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/1-kings/8#v46)</sup> If they sin against thee, (for there is no man that sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captives unto the land of the enemy, far or near;

    There is no man that sinneth not; when Israel sins the Most High delivers them into the hand of the enemy, carried away captive.

    When Israel is smitten before the enemy because they have sinned, they must turn again and confess his name.
  - **[Romans 6:23](/bible/romans/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/romans/6#v23)</sup> For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.

    The wages of sin is death; through sin death came into the world, and our forefathers understood this.
