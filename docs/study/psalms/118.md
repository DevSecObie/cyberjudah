**[Psalms 118:1-6](/bible/psalms/118#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/118#v1)</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: because his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/118#v2)</sup> Let Israel now say, that his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/118#v3)</sup> Let the house of Aaron now say, that his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/118#v4)</sup> Let them now that fear the Lord say, that his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/118#v5)</sup> I called upon the Lord in distress: the Lord answered me, and set me in a large place.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/118#v6)</sup> The Lord is on my side; I will not fear: what can man do unto me?

- O give thanks unto the Lord, for He is good, because His mercy endures forever. Let Israel, the house of Aaron, and them that fear the Lord all say that His mercy endures forever.
- I called upon the Lord in distress and He answered me and set me in a large place. The Lord is on my side; I will not fear what man can do unto me, but this does not mean fear will never come upon you at all.
  - **[Matthew 10:28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)</sup> And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

    Fear not them which kill the body but are not able to kill the soul, but rather fear Him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.
  - **[2 Maccabees 7:11-14](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v11)</sup> And said courageously, These I had from heaven; and for his laws I despise them; and from him I hope to receive them again.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v12)</sup> Insomuch that the king, and they that were with him, marvelled at the young man’s courage, for that he nothing regarded the pains.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v13)</sup> Now when this man was dead also, they tormented and mangled the fourth in like manner.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v14)</sup> So when he was ready to die he said thus, It is good, being put to death by men, to look for hope from God to be raised up again by him: as for thee, thou shalt have no resurrection to life.

    The young men despised the pains and died for the laws of the Most High, showing the very spirit of not fearing them that kill the body.

**[Psalms 118:7-14](/bible/psalms/118#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/118#v7)</sup> The Lord taketh my part with them that help me: therefore shall I see my desire upon them that hate me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/118#v8)</sup> It is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in man.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/118#v9)</sup> It is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in princes.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/118#v10)</sup> All nations compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord will I destroy them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/118#v11)</sup> They compassed me about; yea, they compassed me about: but in the name of the Lord I will destroy them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/118#v12)</sup> They compassed me about like bees; they are quenched as the fire of thorns: for in the name of the Lord I will destroy them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/118#v13)</sup> Thou hast thrust sore at me that I might fall: but the Lord helped me.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/118#v14)</sup> The Lord is my strength and song, and is become my salvation.

- The Lord takes my part with them that help me; it is better to trust in the Lord than to put confidence in man or in princes. All nations may compass me about, but in the name of the Lord will I destroy them; they are quenched as the fire of thorns.
- Thou hast thrust sore at me that I might fall, but the Lord helped me. The Lord is my strength and song, and is become my salvation.
- We learn to reach a level of faith like those young men by prayer, study, and application: pray and apply. When your life is clean, fasting and praying and overcoming your sins, the Most High will put a spirit of boldness upon you and you will start to believe the scriptures written about us. Sin causes fear and sin causes doubt.

**[Psalms 118:15-18](/bible/psalms/118#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/118#v15)</sup> The voice of rejoicing and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous: the right hand of the Lord doeth valiantly.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/118#v16)</sup> The right hand of the Lord is exalted: the right hand of the Lord doeth valiantly.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/118#v17)</sup> I shall not die, but live, and declare the works of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/118#v18)</sup> The Lord hath chastened me sore: but he hath not given me over unto death.

- The voice of rejoicing and salvation is in the tabernacles of the righteous; the right hand of the Lord is exalted and does valiantly. I shall not die but live and declare the works of the Lord.
- The Lord has chastened me sore but has not given me over unto death. When you come to serve the Lord, understand that this walk brings chastising to try us.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:1-5](/bible/sirach/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/2#v2)</sup> Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/2#v3)</sup> Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/2#v4)</sup> Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully, and be patient when thou art changed to a low estate.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/2#v5)</sup> For gold is tried in the fire, and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.

    My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation, set thy heart aright, endure, and take whatever is brought upon thee cheerfully, for gold is tried in the fire and acceptable men in the furnace of adversity.
  - **[2 Corinthians 6:7-9](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v7)</sup> By the word of truth, by the power of God, by the armour of righteousness on the right hand and on the left,
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v8)</sup> By honour and dishonour, by evil report and good report: as deceivers, and yet true;
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-corinthians/6#v9)</sup> As unknown, and yet well known; as dying, and, behold, we live; as chastened, and not killed;

    By the word of truth, by honor and dishonor, as chastened and not killed; the prophets and the apostle Paul agree with what we read in the psalm.

**[Psalms 118:19-23](/bible/psalms/118#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/118#v19)</sup> Open to me the gates of righteousness: I will go into them, and I will praise the Lord:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/118#v20)</sup> This gate of the Lord, into which the righteous shall enter.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/118#v21)</sup> I will praise thee: for thou hast heard me, and art become my salvation.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/118#v22)</sup> The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/118#v23)</sup> This is the Lord’s doing; it is marvellous in our eyes.

- Open to me the gates of righteousness; I will go in and praise the Lord. This is the gate of the Lord into which the righteous shall enter.
- The stone which the builders refused is become the head stone of the corner; this is the Lord's doing and it is marvelous in our eyes. This is prophecy speaking of Christ, showing that Christ is in the Old Testament.
  - **[Mark 12:7-11](/bible/mark/12#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/mark/12#v7)</sup> But those husbandmen said among themselves, This is the heir; come, let us kill him, and the inheritance shall be ours.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/mark/12#v8)</sup> And they took him, and killed him, and cast him out of the vineyard.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/mark/12#v9)</sup> What shall therefore the lord of the vineyard do? he will come and destroy the husbandmen, and will give the vineyard unto others.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/mark/12#v10)</sup> And have ye not read this scripture; The stone which the builders rejected is become the head of the corner:
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/mark/12#v11)</sup> This was the Lord’s doing, and it is marvellous in our eyes?

    The husbandmen killed the heir and cast him out, so the lord of the vineyard will destroy them; the stone which the builders rejected is become the head of the corner.
  - **[Hebrews 10:7](/bible/hebrews/10#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/10#v7)</sup> Then said I, Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of me,) to do thy will, O God.

    Lo, I come, in the volume of the book it is written of Me, showing that Christ is written of in the Old Testament.

**[Psalms 118:24-29](/bible/psalms/118#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/118#v24)</sup> This is the day which the Lord hath made; we will rejoice and be glad in it.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/118#v25)</sup> Save now, I beseech thee, O Lord: O Lord, I beseech thee, send now prosperity.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/118#v26)</sup> Blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord: we have blessed you out of the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/118#v27)</sup> God is the Lord, which hath shewed us light: bind the sacrifice with cords, even unto the horns of the altar.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/118#v28)</sup> Thou art my God, and I will praise thee: thou art my God, I will exalt thee.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/118#v29)</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.

- This is the day which the Lord has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it. Save now, I beseech Thee, O Lord, and send now prosperity; blessed be he that cometh in the name of the Lord.
- God is the Lord which has showed us light. The light is the laws, and we are to bind the sacrifice with cords unto the horns of the altar and give thanks unto the Lord, for He is good and His mercy endures forever unto the children of the promise.
  - **[Matthew 21:9-11](/bible/matthew/21#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/21#v9)</sup> And the multitudes that went before, and that followed, cried, saying, Hosanna to the Son of David: Blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord; Hosanna in the highest.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/21#v10)</sup> And when he was come into Jerusalem, all the city was moved, saying, Who is this?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/21#v11)</sup> And the multitude said, This is Jesus the prophet of Nazareth of Galilee.

    The multitudes cried Hosanna to the son of David, blessed is he that cometh in the name of the Lord, and the multitude said this is Jesus of Nazareth, the Messiah spoken of in the psalm.
  - **[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**
