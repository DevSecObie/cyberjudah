Read the chapter → [Psalms 27](/bible/psalms/27)

This session covers [Psalms 27](/bible/psalms/27) through 30, a set of short chapters with a strong thread: making the Most High your refuge, salvation, and strength in the last days. The teaching moves through fear and confidence in tribulation, the different kinds of wickedness that face judgment, who the Most High's saints truly are, and the stipulations behind His help. These are uplifting words, but they should also be taken with a level of seriousness, forcing us to re-evaluate where we stand and whether we are moving in the right spirit.

**[Psalms 27:1-3](/bible/psalms/27#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/27#v1)</sup> The Lord is my light and my salvation; whom shall I fear? the Lord is the strength of my life; of whom shall I be afraid?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/27#v2)</sup> When the wicked, even mine enemies and my foes, came upon me to eat up my flesh, they stumbled and fell.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/27#v3)</sup> Though an host should encamp against me, my heart shall not fear: though war should rise against me, in this will I be confident.

- David is saying the Most High is his guide and his way to salvation. It is a rhetorical question: if the Most High is my salvation, why should I be afraid of anybody?
- This is very good reading for what is going on in these last days.
- The wicked, enemies, and foes here are the other nations, but David is also referring to his own people. Preferably we are talking about the wicked or the other nations who come against you and stumble and fall.
- Even with everything coming against us today, covid-19, the vaccine, all the different things being pushed, wars going on, these words remind us that in this we can be confident. Whether it is other camps, family, or other nations coming against us, we can still be confident.
  - **[Psalms 119:105](/bible/psalms/119#v105)**
    > <sup>[105](/bible/psalms/119#v105)</sup> Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.

    The word, the Commandments, is the lamp and light David means in verse 1; the Most High gave us the Commandments as instructions to walk through this captivity.
  - **[Psalms 91:1-2](/bible/psalms/91#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/91#v1)</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/91#v2)</sup> I will say of the Lord, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.

    The same thought as Psalm 27: he that dwelleth in the secret place of the Most High is under the shadow of the Almighty, his refuge and fortress.

**[Psalms 27:4-5](/bible/psalms/27#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/27#v4)</sup> One thing have I desired of the Lord, that will I seek after; that I may dwell in the house of the Lord all the days of my life, to behold the beauty of the Lord, and to enquire in his temple.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/27#v5)</sup> For in the time of trouble he shall hide me in his pavilion: in the secret of his tabernacle shall he hide me; he shall set me up upon a rock.

- One thing David desires is to dwell in the house of the Most High all his days, to behold His beauty and inquire in His Temple, in His kingdom.
- In a time of trouble He shall hide you in His Pavilion. Lord willing, much of Israel alive will have to go through this time of trouble, and He says He will hide you.
- This is just one instance; there are many scenarios where the Most High will encamp around you and be your refuge, even when people come against IUIC He is there in the midst.
  - **[Psalms 91:3-9](/bible/psalms/91#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/91#v3)</sup> Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/91#v4)</sup> He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/91#v5)</sup> Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/91#v6)</sup> Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/91#v7)</sup> A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/91#v8)</sup> Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/91#v9)</sup> Because thou hast made the Lord, which is my refuge, even the most High, thy habitation;

    You shall not be afraid; a thousand shall fall at your side and ten thousand at your right hand, but only with your eyes shall you behold the reward of the wicked when Christ returns, because you made the Most High your habitation.

**[Psalms 27:6-8](/bible/psalms/27#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/27#v6)</sup> And now shall mine head be lifted up above mine enemies round about me: therefore will I offer in his tabernacle sacrifices of joy; I will sing, yea, I will sing praises unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/27#v7)</sup> Hear, O Lord, when I cry with my voice: have mercy also upon me, and answer me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/27#v8)</sup> When thou saidst, Seek ye my face; my heart said unto thee, Thy face, Lord, will I seek.

- Now his head is lifted up above his enemies, so he offers sacrifices of joy and sings praises unto the Most High.
- Crying unto the Most High is praying to Him. We are taught to cry unto Him in tribulation, in times of trouble, and even in good times. But He only answers the cries of those that keep His Commandments.
- Verse 8 is an obedient spirit: when the Most High says seek My face, the heart answers, Thy face will I seek. Especially remembering the day when everybody gets judged, this should be the mindset for every single one of us.

**[Psalms 27:9-10](/bible/psalms/27#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/27#v9)</sup> Hide not thy face far from me; put not thy servant away in anger: thou hast been my help; leave me not, neither forsake me, O God of my salvation.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/27#v10)</sup> When my father and my mother forsake me, then the Lord will take me up.

- David pleads that the Most High not hide His face or put away His servant in anger, for He has been his help.
- When father and mother forsake me, then the Most High will take me up.
  - **[Matthew 10:34-36](/bible/matthew/10#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/10#v34)</sup> Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/10#v35)</sup> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/10#v36)</sup> And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household.

    Your enemies will be those of your own household; close family can be the ones that forsake you and come against you in these last days.

**[Psalms 27:11-14](/bible/psalms/27#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/27#v11)</sup> Teach me thy way, O Lord, and lead me in a plain path, because of mine enemies.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/27#v12)</sup> Deliver me not over unto the will of mine enemies: for false witnesses are risen up against me, and such as breathe out cruelty.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/27#v13)</sup> I had fainted, unless I had believed to see the goodness of the Lord in the land of the living.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/27#v14)</sup> Wait on the Lord: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the Lord.

- Teach me Thy way and lead me in a plain path because of my enemies. False witnesses will rise up against the righteous and breathe out cruelty, speaking evil against those keeping the Commandments.
- These are people, whether other camps, others who dislike IUIC or Israelites, or other nations, who rise up against the righteous walking in strife and vain glory.
- Every line reads like a stipulation: David would have fainted unless he had believed to see the goodness of the Most High. Without faith that the kingdom is coming, he would have fainted.
- Wait on the Most High and be of good courage, and He shall strengthen your heart. But the double-minded, half in and half out, He will not defend, and this comes up again in the later chapters.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:6-13](/bible/sirach/2#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/2#v6)</sup> Believe in him, and he will help thee; order thy way aright, and trust in him.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/2#v7)</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, wait for his mercy; and go not aside, lest ye fall.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/2#v8)</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, believe him; and your reward shall not fail.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/2#v9)</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, hope for good, and for everlasting joy and mercy.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/2#v10)</sup> Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/2#v11)</sup> For the Lord is full of compassion and mercy, longsuffering, and very pitiful, and forgiveth sins, and saveth in time of affliction.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/2#v12)</sup> Woe be to fearful hearts, and faint hands, and the sinner that goeth two ways!
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/2#v13)</sup> Woe unto him that is fainthearted! for he believeth not; therefore shall he not be defended.

    Believe in Him and He will help you; stipulations to His help. The fearful heart and the sinner that goeth two ways cannot serve both; the fainthearted who believes not shall not be defended.

# Psalm 28: Be Not Silent To Me

Read the chapter → [Psalms 28](/bible/psalms/28)

**[Psalms 28:1-2](/bible/psalms/28#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/28#v1)</sup> Unto thee will I cry, O Lord my rock; be not silent to me: lest, if thou be silent to me, I become like them that go down into the pit.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/28#v2)</sup> Hear the voice of my supplications, when I cry unto thee, when I lift up my hands toward thy holy oracle.

- Unto Thee will I cry; be not silent to me, lest I become like them that go down into the pit.
- When the Most High is silent against us it is the same thing as Hosea: He withdraws and does not listen to our prayers until we seek His face.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    The Most High returns to His place until we acknowledge our offense and seek Him early in our affliction.

**[Psalms 28:3-5](/bible/psalms/28#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/28#v3)</sup> Draw me not away with the wicked, and with the workers of iniquity, which speak peace to their neighbours, but mischief is in their hearts.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/28#v4)</sup> Give them according to their deeds, and according to the wickedness of their endeavours: give them after the work of their hands; render to them their desert.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/28#v5)</sup> Because they regard not the works of the Lord, nor the operation of his hands, he shall destroy them, and not build them up.

- Draw me not away with the wicked, who speak peace to their neighbors but have mischief in their hearts. He is giving clues about the different types of wicked that will be judged.
- Some wickedness is visible and clear, like homosexuality, but there is wickedness you will not pick up on as easily, and men are judged for it.
- Give them according to their deeds, because they regard not the works of the Most High nor the operations of His hand; destroy them and not build them up. That is Esau.
- Esau does not regard the operations of the Most High; they want to be as God, above God, in their sciences and everything their hands touch. But the wicked, whether Esau or our own people, will not be built back up once destroyed. When those bombs hit, they are gone.
  - **[Psalms 55:21](/bible/psalms/55#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/55#v21)</sup> The words of his mouth were smoother than butter, but war was in his heart: his words were softer than oil, yet were they drawn swords.

    His words were smoother than butter but war was in his heart; this wicked is Esau.
  - **[Obadiah 1:3-4](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)</sup> The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/obadiah/1#v4)</sup> Though thou exalt thyself as the eagle, and though thou set thy nest among the stars, thence will I bring thee down, saith the Lord.

    Esau's pride and desire to exalt himself as God brings the Most High's promise to bring him down.

**[Psalms 28:6-9](/bible/psalms/28#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/28#v6)</sup> Blessed be the Lord, because he hath heard the voice of my supplications.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/28#v7)</sup> The Lord is my strength and my shield; my heart trusted in him, and I am helped: therefore my heart greatly rejoiceth; and with my song will I praise him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/28#v8)</sup> The Lord is their strength, and he is the saving strength of his anointed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/28#v9)</sup> Save thy people, and bless thine inheritance: feed them also, and lift them up for ever.

- Blessed be the Most High, for He heard the voice of supplication; He is strength and shield, the saving strength of His anointed.
- He is being very specific about who is being saved: save Thy people and bless Thine inheritance. Israel is His people, His anointed, His inheritance.

# Psalm 29: The Voice of the Most High

Read the chapter → [Psalms 29](/bible/psalms/29)

**[Psalms 29:1-11](/bible/psalms/29#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/29#v1)</sup> Give unto the Lord, O ye mighty, give unto the Lord glory and strength.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/29#v2)</sup> Give unto the Lord the glory due unto his name; worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/29#v3)</sup> The voice of the Lord is upon the waters: the God of glory thundereth: the Lord is upon many waters.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/29#v4)</sup> The voice of the Lord is powerful; the voice of the Lord is full of majesty.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/29#v5)</sup> The voice of the Lord breaketh the cedars; yea, the Lord breaketh the cedars of Lebanon.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/29#v6)</sup> He maketh them also to skip like a calf; Lebanon and Sirion like a young unicorn.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/29#v7)</sup> The voice of the Lord divideth the flames of fire.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/29#v8)</sup> The voice of the Lord shaketh the wilderness; the Lord shaketh the wilderness of Kadesh.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/29#v9)</sup> The voice of the Lord maketh the hinds to calve, and discovereth the forests: and in his temple doth every one speak of his glory.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/29#v10)</sup> The Lord sitteth upon the flood; yea, the Lord sitteth King for ever.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/29#v11)</sup> The Lord will give strength unto his people; the Lord will bless his people with peace.

- Give unto the Most High glory and strength, and worship Him in the beauty of holiness.
- The voice of the Most High is upon the waters, powerful and full of majesty; it breaks the cedars, divides the flames of fire, and shakes the wilderness.
- The Most High sits King forever. The verse to hold is the last: He will give strength unto His people and bless His people with peace.
- That time of strength and peace comes as long as we follow the directions seen in [Psalms 27](/bible/psalms/27) and 28, making the Most High our refuge and trusting in Him; that is the only way to receive strength as His people and the blessing of peace.

# Psalm 30: Joy Cometh in the Morning

Read the chapter → [Psalms 30](/bible/psalms/30)

**[Psalms 30:1-3](/bible/psalms/30#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/30#v1)</sup> I will extol thee, O Lord; for thou hast lifted me up, and hast not made my foes to rejoice over me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/30#v2)</sup> O Lord my God, I cried unto thee, and thou hast healed me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/30#v3)</sup> O Lord, thou hast brought up my soul from the grave: thou hast kept me alive, that I should not go down to the pit.

- David will extol the Most High for lifting him up and not letting his foes rejoice over him; he cried and was healed, brought up from the grave and kept alive.

**[Psalms 30:4-5](/bible/psalms/30#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/30#v4)</sup> Sing unto the Lord, O ye saints of his, and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/30#v5)</sup> For his anger endureth but a moment; in his favour is life: weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning.

- Sing unto the Most High, all ye saints of His, and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness. He specifies His saints.
- This punishment will not last forever. His anger endures but a moment and His favor is life; weeping may endure for a night, but joy cometh in the morning. What feels like eternity to us is a little time to the Most High.
  - **[Psalms 50:5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

    The saints are those that made a covenant with the Most High by sacrifice, the Israelites who came out of Egypt.

**[Psalms 30:6](/bible/psalms/30#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/30#v6)</sup> And in my prosperity I said, I shall never be moved.

- In my prosperity I said I shall never be moved: the key point of making the Most High your refuge and salvation is staying on the right path regardless, whether prosperity or affliction comes.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 11:25](/bible/sirach/11#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/11#v25)</sup> In the day of prosperity there is a forgetfulness of affliction: and in the day of affliction there is no more remembrance of prosperity.

    In the day of prosperity there is forgetfulness of affliction, and in the day of affliction no more remembrance of prosperity; people forget to save and prepare when things go well, and forget past prosperity when things go wrong.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:1-4](/bible/sirach/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/2#v1)</sup> My son, if thou come to serve the Lord, prepare thy soul for temptation.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/2#v2)</sup> Set thy heart aright, and constantly endure, and make not haste in time of trouble.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/2#v3)</sup> Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/2#v4)</sup> Whatsoever is brought upon thee take cheerfully, and be patient when thou art changed to a low estate.

    If you come to serve the Most High, prepare your soul, set your heart right, endure, and take whatever is brought upon you cheerfully, staying patient when changed to a lower state.

**[Psalms 30:7-8](/bible/psalms/30#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/30#v7)</sup> Lord, by thy favour thou hast made my mountain to stand strong: thou didst hide thy face, and I was troubled.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/30#v8)</sup> I cried to thee, O Lord; and unto the Lord I made supplication.

- By Thy favor Thou hast made my mountain to stand strong; Thou didst hide Thy face and I was troubled. I cried and made supplication.
- To make supplication is to plead, ask forgiveness, and confess specifically what we have done. You cannot just say "Lord I have sinned"; you must name what you are dealing with.
  - **[1 Kings 8:47-49](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)**
    > <sup>[47](/bible/1-kings/8#v47)</sup> Yet if they shall bethink themselves in the land whither they were carried captives, and repent, and make supplication unto thee in the land of them that carried them captives, saying, We have sinned, and have done perversely, we have committed wickedness;
    >
    > <sup>[48](/bible/1-kings/8#v48)</sup> And so return unto thee with all their heart, and with all their soul, in the land of their enemies, which led them away captive, and pray unto thee toward their land, which thou gavest unto their fathers, the city which thou hast chosen, and the house which I have built for thy name:
    >
    > <sup>[49](/bible/1-kings/8#v49)</sup> Then hear thou their prayer and their supplication in heaven thy dwelling place, and maintain their cause,

    In captivity, repent and make supplication saying we have sinned and committed wickedness; then He hears the prayer and maintains our cause. He only hears when we repent.

**[Psalms 30:9-12](/bible/psalms/30#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/30#v9)</sup> What profit is there in my blood, when I go down to the pit? Shall the dust praise thee? shall it declare thy truth?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/30#v10)</sup> Hear, O Lord, and have mercy upon me: Lord, be thou my helper.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/30#v11)</sup> Thou hast turned for me my mourning into dancing: thou hast put off my sackcloth, and girded me with gladness;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/30#v12)</sup> To the end that my glory may sing praise to thee, and not be silent. O Lord my God, I will give thanks unto thee for ever.

- What profit is in my blood when I go down to the pit? Shall the dust praise Thee? Have mercy, be Thou my helper.
- He turned mourning into dancing and put off the sackcloth, that glory may sing praise and not be silent; David will give thanks forever. This is the kingdom, where we will sing thanks to the Most High forever, but only if we do what we are supposed to do.

**[Psalms 30](/bible/psalms/30) Q&A: Confessing Sins Specifically**

- On repenting daily but naming sins specifically: the Most High looks for us to say exactly what we are dealing with. One brother may struggle with fornication, another with cigarettes; you cannot bring the same prayer, so make it clearly known what you are dealing with.
- Make your request known plainly, the way you would tell your mother or father exactly what you need, not just "Lord bless me." As Israelites we seek a country, the kingdom and this world back, and must make that request known.
- Prayer is largely invalid if there is no action behind it. If you ask for a home but take no steps toward the finances, why would you receive it. Faith without works is dead.
  - **[Proverbs 28:13](/bible/proverbs/28#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/28#v13)</sup> He that covereth his sins shall not prosper: but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy.

    He that covereth his sins shall not prosper, but whoso confesseth and forsaketh them shall have mercy; hide your sins and you will be buried by them.
  - **[Psalms 3:8](/bible/psalms/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/3#v8)</sup> Salvation belongeth unto the Lord: thy blessing is upon thy people. Selah.

    Salvation belongeth unto the Most High; affirmed as a precept for Psalms 27:1 on the Most High being salvation.
  - **[Baruch 4:1](/bible/baruch/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/baruch/4#v1)</sup> This is the book of the commandments of God, and the law that endureth for ever: all they that keep it shall come to life; but such as leave it shall die.

    This is the book of the Commandments; the whole book is the law, so there is no single list. Those that keep it come to life, but such as leave it shall die.
