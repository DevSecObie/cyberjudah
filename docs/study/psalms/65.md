Read the chapter → [Psalms 65](/bible/psalms/65)

[Psalm 65](/bible/psalms/65) opens with praise waiting for the Most High in Zion and the vow performed, then moves to the purging of transgressions and the blessedness of the man He chooses to approach Him. It closes with the Most High enriching the earth, watering the ridges and furrows, and crowning the year with His goodness so that the pastures and valleys shout for joy.

**[Psalms 65:1-4](/bible/psalms/65#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/65#v1)</sup> Praise waiteth for thee, O God, in Sion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/65#v2)</sup> O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/65#v3)</sup> Iniquities prevail against me: as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/65#v4)</sup> Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple.

- Praise waits for the Most High in Zion and the vow shall be performed; He hears prayer and unto Him shall all flesh come.
- Iniquities prevail, but He purges away the transgressions; blessed is the man He chooses and causes to approach to dwell in His courts, satisfied with the goodness of His house and holy temple.

**[Psalms 65:5-8](/bible/psalms/65#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/65#v5)</sup> By terrible things in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation; who art the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are afar off upon the sea:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/65#v6)</sup> Which by his strength setteth fast the mountains; being girded with power:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/65#v7)</sup> Which stilleth the noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/65#v8)</sup> They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid at thy tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice.

- By terrible things in righteousness He answers as the God of salvation, the confidence of all the ends of the earth and of them far off upon the sea.
- By His strength He sets the mountains and stills the noise of the seas and the tumult of the people; they that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid of His tokens, and He makes the outgoings of morning and evening rejoice.

**[Psalms 65:9-13](/bible/psalms/65#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/65#v9)</sup> Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou greatly enrichest it with the river of God, which is full of water: thou preparest them corn, when thou hast so provided for it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/65#v10)</sup> Thou waterest the ridges thereof abundantly: thou settlest the furrows thereof: thou makest it soft with showers: thou blessest the springing thereof.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/65#v11)</sup> Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/65#v12)</sup> They drop upon the pastures of the wilderness: and the little hills rejoice on every side.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/65#v13)</sup> The pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing.

- He visits the earth and waters it, greatly enriching it with the river of God which is full of water, and He prepares corn, waters the ridges and settles the furrows.
- He crowns the year with His goodness and His paths drop fatness; the pastures are clothed with flocks, the valleys covered over with corn, and they shout for joy and sing.
