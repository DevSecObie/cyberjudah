**[Psalms 112:1](/bible/psalms/112#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/112#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Blessed is the man that feareth the Lord, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.

- Praise you the Most High. Blessed is the man that feareth the Most High, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.
- A lot of people try to say they are blessed like the Christians do, saying "I'm blessed, I'm highly favored." But the scripture says blessed is the man that feareth the Most High and delighteth greatly in his commandments. To be blessed you must be keeping the commands and be delighted greatly in them, meaning they are not grievous to you. That is the mindset every single person has to have when it comes to keeping these commandments.

**[Psalms 112:2](/bible/psalms/112#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/112#v2)</sup> His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed.

- His seed shall be mighty upon the earth; the generation of the upright shall be blessed. That is the chosen that get to the kingdom.

**[Psalms 112:3](/bible/psalms/112#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/112#v3)</sup> Wealth and riches shall be in his house: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

- Wealth and riches shall be in his house, and his righteousness endureth forever. All the wealth and all the riches will belong to the Israelites when that time comes with Christ to redeem his people.
- He is going to bring the earth back to the Israelites, and the other nations, the heathens, are going to bring all the wealth back to us. They are going to bring the things that are needed to rebuild the kingdom.

**[Psalms 112:4](/bible/psalms/112#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/112#v4)</sup> Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness: he is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

- Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness. He is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

**[Psalms 112:5](/bible/psalms/112#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/112#v5)</sup> A good man sheweth favour, and lendeth: he will guide his affairs with discretion.

- A good man showeth favor and lendeth; he will guide his affairs with discretion.

**[Psalms 112:6](/bible/psalms/112#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/112#v6)</sup> Surely he shall not be moved for ever: the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.

- Surely he shall not be moved forever; the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.
  - **[Malachi 3:16](/bible/malachi/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/malachi/3#v16)</sup> Then they that feared the Lord spake often one to another: and the Lord hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the Lord, and that thought upon his name.

    A book of remembrance was written for those that feared the Most High and thought upon his name, which is the same thing read back in verse one.

**[Psalms 112:7](/bible/psalms/112#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/112#v7)</sup> He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: his heart is fixed, trusting in the Lord.

- He shall not be afraid of evil tidings; his heart is fixed, trusting in the Most High. This is the same thing reiterated all through Psalms, how David was not going to be afraid of the things our enemies do against him.

**[Psalms 112:8](/bible/psalms/112#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/112#v8)</sup> His heart is established, he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies.

- His heart is established; he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies.

**[Psalms 112:9](/bible/psalms/112#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/112#v9)</sup> He hath dispersed, he hath given to the poor; his righteousness endureth for ever; his horn shall be exalted with honour.

- He has dispersed, he has given to the poor; his righteousness endureth forever; his horn shall be exalted with honor.

**[Psalms 112:10](/bible/psalms/112#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/112#v10)</sup> The wicked shall see it, and be grieved; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away: the desire of the wicked shall perish.

- The wicked shall see it and be grieved; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away; the desire of the wicked shall perish.
