Read the chapter → [Psalms 123](/bible/psalms/123)

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session covers [Psalms 123](/bible/psalms/123) through 126, four very short chapters that move quickly. The thread running through all of them is Israel being saved from the hand of their enemies and protected by the Most High. [Psalm 123](/bible/psalms/123) lifts the eyes of the servant to the Most High for mercy. [Psalm 124](/bible/psalms/124) declares that if it had not been for the Most High on Israel's side, the enemy would have swallowed them up. [Psalm 125](/bible/psalms/125) promises that those who trust in the Most High shall be as mount Zion, that Israel will never be cast off, and that peace shall be upon Israel. [Psalm 126](/bible/psalms/126) rejoices in the Most High turning again the captivity of Zion, sowing in tears now but reaping in joy.

**[Psalm 123:1-4](/bible/psalms/123#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/123#v1)</sup> Unto thee lift I up mine eyes, O thou that dwellest in the heavens.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/123#v2)</sup> Behold, as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress; so our eyes wait upon the Lord our God, until that he have mercy upon us.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/123#v3)</sup> Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/123#v4)</sup> Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease, and with the contempt of the proud.

- Unto Thee lift I mine eyes, O Thou that dwellest in the heavens. As the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress, so our eyes wait upon the Most High our God until He have mercy upon us.
- Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us, for we are exceedingly filled with contempt. Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease and with the contempt of the proud.
