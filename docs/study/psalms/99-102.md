This session moves through [Psalms 99](/bible/psalms/99) through 102, read for the nation four chapters a day. [Psalm 99](/bible/psalms/99) declares that the Most High reigns, sitting between the cherubims, high above all people, and calls Israel to worship at His footstool because He is holy. Moses, Aaron, and Samuel are held up as men who called upon His name because they kept His commandments. The session runs through the joyful worship of [Psalm 100](/bible/psalms/100), the perfect way David sets before his eyes in [Psalm 101](/bible/psalms/101), and the affliction and enduring mercy of Zion in [Psalm 102](/bible/psalms/102).

**[Psalms 99:1-3](/bible/psalms/99#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/99#v1)</sup> The Lord reigneth; let the people tremble: he sitteth between the cherubims; let the earth be moved.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/99#v2)</sup> The Lord is great in Zion; and he is high above all the people.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/99#v3)</sup> Let them praise thy great and terrible name; for it is holy.

- The Most High reigns and the people are to tremble; He sits between the cherubims and the earth is moved.
- He is great in Zion and high above all the people, and His great and terrible name is to be praised, for it is holy.

**[Psalms 99:4-5](/bible/psalms/99#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/99#v4)</sup> The king’s strength also loveth judgment; thou dost establish equity, thou executest judgment and righteousness in Jacob.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/99#v5)</sup> Exalt ye the Lord our God, and worship at his footstool; for he is holy.

- The King's strength loves judgment; He establishes equity and executes judgment and righteousness in Jacob.
- Israel is called to exalt the Most High and worship at His footstool, for He is holy.

**[Psalms 99:6-7](/bible/psalms/99#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/99#v6)</sup> Moses and Aaron among his priests, and Samuel among them that call upon his name; they called upon the Lord, and he answered them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/99#v7)</sup> He spake unto them in the cloudy pillar: they kept his testimonies, and the ordinance that he gave them.

- Moses and Aaron among His priests, and Samuel among them that call upon His name, called upon the Most High and He answered them.
- He spoke to them in the cloudy pillar; they kept His testimonies and the ordinance He gave them.
- When Moses, Aaron, and Samuel called upon His name, they were keeping the commandments of the Most High. That is why they were answered.

**[Psalms 99:8-9](/bible/psalms/99#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/99#v8)</sup> Thou answeredst them, O Lord our God: thou wast a God that forgavest them, though thou tookest vengeance of their inventions.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/99#v9)</sup> Exalt the Lord our God, and worship at his holy hill; for the Lord our God is holy.

- The Most High answered them; He was a God that forgave them, though He took vengeance of their inventions.
- Israel is called again to exalt the Most High and worship at His holy hill, for the Most High our God is holy.

# Psalm 100: Make a Joyful Noise Unto the Lord

Read the chapter → [Psalms 100](/bible/psalms/100)

[Psalm 100](/bible/psalms/100) is a short call to worship: make a joyful noise, serve the Most High with gladness, and come before His presence with singing. The teaching ties His truth enduring to all generations to His laws, and His making us and not we ourselves to His forming us in the belly before we were born.

**[Psalms 100:1-3](/bible/psalms/100#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/100#v1)</sup> Make a joyful noise unto the Lord, all ye lands.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/100#v2)</sup> Serve the Lord with gladness: come before his presence with singing.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/100#v3)</sup> Know ye that the Lord he is God: it is he that hath made us, and not we ourselves; we are his people, and the sheep of his pasture.

- All lands are to make a joyful noise unto the Most High and serve Him with gladness, coming before His presence with singing.
- Know that the Most High is God; it is He that made us and not we ourselves. We are His people and the sheep of His pasture.
- That He made us and not we ourselves goes with the Most High forming us in the belly and knowing us before we were born.
  - **[Jeremiah 1:5](/bible/jeremiah/1#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/1#v5)</sup> Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee; and before thou camest forth out of the womb I sanctified thee, and I ordained thee a prophet unto the nations.

    The Most High knew us and formed us in the belly before we came forth.

**[Psalms 100:4-5](/bible/psalms/100#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/100#v4)</sup> Enter into his gates with thanksgiving, and into his courts with praise: be thankful unto him, and bless his name.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/100#v5)</sup> For the Lord is good; his mercy is everlasting; and his truth endureth to all generations.

- Enter into His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; be thankful and bless His name.
- The Most High is good, His mercy is everlasting, and His truth endures to all generations. That truth is His laws, which endure to all generations.
  - **[Psalms 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    The righteousness of the Most High is an everlasting righteousness and His law is the truth.

# Psalm 101: I Will Behave Myself Wisely in a Perfect Way

Read the chapter → [Psalms 101](/bible/psalms/101)

[Psalm 101](/bible/psalms/101) is David setting a standard for his own house and his own eyes: he will sing of mercy and judgment, behave wisely in a perfect way, and set no wicked thing before his eyes. The teaching lands on the closing verse: the Most High does not like the sin nor the sinner, the wickedness nor the wicked. If you are doing wicked, He will cut you off from the city of the Most High.

**[Psalms 101:1-3](/bible/psalms/101#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/101#v1)</sup> I will sing of mercy and judgment: unto thee, O Lord, will I sing.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/101#v2)</sup> I will behave myself wisely in a perfect way. O when wilt thou come unto me? I will walk within my house with a perfect heart.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/101#v3)</sup> I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that turn aside; it shall not cleave to me.

- David will sing of mercy and judgment unto the Most High and behave himself wisely in a perfect way, asking when He will come to him.
- He will walk within his house with a perfect heart and set no wicked thing before his eyes; he hates the work of them that turn aside.

**[Psalms 101:4-5](/bible/psalms/101#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/101#v4)</sup> A froward heart shall depart from me: I will not know a wicked person.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/101#v5)</sup> Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer.

- A froward heart will depart from him; he will not know a wicked person.
- Whoever privily slanders his neighbor, him will he cut off; the high look and proud heart he will not suffer.

**[Psalms 101:6-8](/bible/psalms/101#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/101#v6)</sup> Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walketh in a perfect way, he shall serve me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/101#v7)</sup> He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/101#v8)</sup> I will early destroy all the wicked of the land; that I may cut off all wicked doers from the city of the Lord.

- His eyes will be upon the faithful of the land that they may dwell with him; he that walks in a perfect way shall serve him.
- He that works deceit and he that tells lies shall not dwell in his house nor tarry in his sight.
- He will early destroy all the wicked of the land to cut off all wicked doers from the city of the Most High. This shows He does not like the sin nor the sinner, the wickedness nor the wicked. If you are doing wicked, He will cut you off.

# Psalm 102: Hear My Prayer, O Lord

Read the chapter → [Psalms 102](/bible/psalms/102)

[Psalm 102](/bible/psalms/102) is the prayer of the afflicted when he is overwhelmed and pours out his complaint before the Most High. His days are consumed like smoke and his bones burned as a hearth, yet the psalm turns to the Most High enduring forever and the set time to favor Zion having come. The teaching points to the remembrance to all generations being that He is the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and to the children of His servants being the children of Israel.

**[Psalms 102:1-5](/bible/psalms/102#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/102#v1)</sup> Hear my prayer, O Lord, and let my cry come unto thee.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/102#v2)</sup> Hide not thy face from me in the day when I am in trouble; incline thine ear unto me: in the day when I call answer me speedily.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/102#v3)</sup> For my days are consumed like smoke, and my bones are burned as an hearth.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/102#v4)</sup> My heart is smitten, and withered like grass; so that I forget to eat my bread.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/102#v5)</sup> By reason of the voice of my groaning my bones cleave to my skin.

- The afflicted asks the Most High to hear his prayer and let his cry come to Him, not to hide His face in the day of trouble but to answer speedily.
- His days are consumed like smoke and his bones burned as a hearth; his heart is smitten and withered like grass so that he forgets to eat his bread.

**[Psalms 102:6-9](/bible/psalms/102#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/102#v6)</sup> I am like a pelican of the wilderness: I am like an owl of the desert.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/102#v7)</sup> I watch, and am as a sparrow alone upon the house top.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/102#v8)</sup> Mine enemies reproach me all the day; and they that are mad against me are sworn against me.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/102#v9)</sup> For I have eaten ashes like bread, and mingled my drink with weeping,

- By reason of his groaning his bones cleave to his skin; he is like a pelican of the wilderness and an owl of the desert, a sparrow alone upon the housetop.
- His enemies reproach him all the day and are sworn against him; he has eaten ashes like bread and mingled his drink with weeping.

**[Psalms 102:10-12](/bible/psalms/102#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/102#v10)</sup> Because of thine indignation and thy wrath: for thou hast lifted me up, and cast me down.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/102#v11)</sup> My days are like a shadow that declineth; and I am withered like grass.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/102#v12)</sup> But thou, O Lord, shalt endure for ever; and thy remembrance unto all generations.

- Because of the indignation and wrath of the Most High he was lifted up and cast down; his days are like a declining shadow and he is withered like grass.
- But the Most High shall endure forever and His remembrance unto all generations. That remembrance is that He is the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
  - **[Exodus 3:15](/bible/exodus/3#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/3#v15)</sup> And God said moreover unto Moses, Thus shalt thou say unto the children of Israel, The Lord God of your fathers, the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob, hath sent me unto you: this is my name for ever, and this is my memorial unto all generations.

    This is His name forever and His memorial unto all generations: the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

**[Psalms 102:13-16](/bible/psalms/102#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/102#v13)</sup> Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Zion: for the time to favour her, yea, the set time, is come.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/102#v14)</sup> For thy servants take pleasure in her stones, and favour the dust thereof.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/102#v15)</sup> So the heathen shall fear the name of the Lord, and all the kings of the earth thy glory.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/102#v16)</sup> When the Lord shall build up Zion, he shall appear in his glory.

- The Most High will arise and have mercy upon Zion, for the set time to favor her has come.
- His servants take pleasure in her stones and favor her dust; the heathen shall fear His name and all the kings of the earth His glory when He builds up Zion and appears in His glory.

**[Psalms 102:17-20](/bible/psalms/102#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/102#v17)</sup> He will regard the prayer of the destitute, and not despise their prayer.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/102#v18)</sup> This shall be written for the generation to come: and the people which shall be created shall praise the Lord.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/102#v19)</sup> For he hath looked down from the height of his sanctuary; from heaven did the Lord behold the earth;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/102#v20)</sup> To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death;

- He will regard the prayer of the destitute and not despise their prayer; this shall be written for the generation to come, that the people created shall praise the Most High.
- He has looked down from the height of His sanctuary and from heaven beheld the earth, to hear the groaning of the prisoner and loose those appointed to death.

**[Psalms 102:21-24](/bible/psalms/102#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/102#v21)</sup> To declare the name of the Lord in Zion, and his praise in Jerusalem;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/102#v22)</sup> When the people are gathered together, and the kingdoms, to serve the Lord.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/102#v23)</sup> He weakened my strength in the way; he shortened my days.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/102#v24)</sup> I said, O my God, take me not away in the midst of my days: thy years are throughout all generations.

- The purpose is to declare the name of the Most High in Zion and His praise in Jerusalem, when the people are gathered together to serve Him.
- He weakened his strength in the way and shortened his days; he asks not to be taken away in the midst of his days, for the years of the Most High are throughout all generations.

**[Psalms 102:25-28](/bible/psalms/102#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/102#v25)</sup> Of old hast thou laid the foundation of the earth: and the heavens are the work of thy hands.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/102#v26)</sup> They shall perish, but thou shalt endure: yea, all of them shall wax old like a garment; as a vesture shalt thou change them, and they shall be changed:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/102#v27)</sup> But thou art the same, and thy years shall have no end.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/102#v28)</sup> The children of thy servants shall continue, and their seed shall be established before thee.

- Of old the Most High laid the foundation of the earth, and the heavens are the work of His hands; they shall perish but He shall endure, and they shall be changed but He is the same and His years have no end.
- The children of His servants shall continue and their seed shall be established before Him. The children of His servants are the children of Israel, the servants of the Most High.
