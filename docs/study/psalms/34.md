**[Psalm 34:1-3](/bible/psalms/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/34#v1)</sup> I will bless the Lord at all times: his praise shall continually be in my mouth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/34#v2)</sup> My soul shall make her boast in the Lord: the humble shall hear thereof, and be glad.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/34#v3)</sup> O magnify the Lord with me, and let us exalt his name together.

- I will bless the Lord at all times, His praise shall continually be in my mouth, my soul shall make her boast in the Lord, the humble shall hear thereof and be glad. This is a psalm of David where he is giving praise of deliverance to the Most High.
- O magnify the Lord with me and let us exalt His name together.

**[Psalm 34:4-7](/bible/psalms/34#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/34#v4)</sup> I sought the Lord, and he heard me, and delivered me from all my fears.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/34#v5)</sup> They looked unto him, and were lightened: and their faces were not ashamed.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/34#v6)</sup> This poor man cried, and the Lord heard him, and saved him out of all his troubles.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/34#v7)</sup> The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him, and delivereth them.

- I sought the Lord and He heard me and delivered me from all my fears. They looked upon Him and were lightened and their faces were not ashamed. This poor man cried and the Lord heard him and saved him out of his troubles.
- The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear him and delivereth them. Just as said in the last chapter, when you repent and come into this truth there is an angel recording what you do. To fear the Lord means to keep the commandments, so if you are keeping the commandments there is an angel taking notes of everything you do, and whether you confess it or not the angel has your report.

**[Psalm 34:8-10](/bible/psalms/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/34#v8)</sup> O taste and see that the Lord is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/34#v9)</sup> O fear the Lord, ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/34#v10)</sup> The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger: but they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing.

- O taste and see that the Lord is good, taste the commandments, taste these scriptures we should be reading day in and day out, this is our daily bread, this Bible. Blessed is the man that trusteth in the Lord, that trusts the words of this Bible and understands that it is a true book, our heritage written to the Israelites in these last days.
- O fear the Lord ye His saints, for there is no want to them that fear Him. The young lions do lack and suffer hunger, but they that seek the Lord shall not want any good thing.
  - **[Psalm 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    He also exalteth the horn of His people, the praise of all His saints, even of the children of Israel, showing His saints and His people are the same, the children of Israel near unto Him.

**[Psalm 34:11-16](/bible/psalms/34#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/34#v11)</sup> Come, ye children, hearken unto me: I will teach you the fear of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/34#v12)</sup> What man is he that desireth life, and loveth many days, that he may see good?
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/34#v13)</sup> Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking guile.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/34#v14)</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/34#v15)</sup> The eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous, and his ears are open unto their cry.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/34#v16)</sup> The face of the Lord is against them that do evil, to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth.

- Come ye children, hearken unto me, I will teach you the fear of the Lord. What man is he that desireth life and loveth many days that he may see good? Keep thy tongue from evil and thy lips from speaking guile, depart from evil and do good, seek peace and pursue it.
- The eyes of the Lord are upon the righteous and His ears are open unto their cry, the face of the Lord is against them that do evil to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth. If you are doing evil you are on your way to a speedy death, and just because it has not happened yet does not mean it will not.
- Depart from evil and do good, seek peace and pursue it, that is our job when we come into this truth and read this Bible, to depart from the wickedness we were involved in in the world. But a lot of people are not pursuing peace, they are pursuing confusion and stirring up strife in the body, and when you have those issues in the body it is because that person is not here to pursue peace but to stir up strife, and might be a darn agent.

**[Psalm 34:17-22](/bible/psalms/34#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/34#v17)</sup> The righteous cry, and the Lord heareth, and delivereth them out of all their troubles.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/34#v18)</sup> The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart; and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/34#v19)</sup> Many are the afflictions of the righteous: but the Lord delivereth him out of them all.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/34#v20)</sup> He keepeth all his bones: not one of them is broken.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/34#v21)</sup> Evil shall slay the wicked: and they that hate the righteous shall be desolate.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/34#v22)</sup> The Lord redeemeth the soul of his servants: and none of them that trust in him shall be desolate.

- The righteous cry and the Lord heareth and delivereth them out of all their troubles. The Lord is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit. That is us in this captivity, the brokenhearted, them of a contrite spirit, the last hired and the first fired, the ones they want to use as test dummies for a vaccine they created in under six months. We are the ones that need to be giving the Most High these praises, we are His people.
- Many are the afflictions of the righteous, but the Lord delivereth him out of them all, He keepeth all his bones, not one of them is broken. Evil shall slay the wicked, and they that hate the righteous shall be desolate, so to the haters out there, you are going to be destroyed.
- The Lord redeemeth the soul of His servants, them that are out there doing the work putting the brick in that understanding mission, and none of them that trust in Him shall be desolate. None of us that trust in the Lord shall be destroyed, we are going to be redeemed from this captivity.
