Read the chapter → [Psalms 108](/bible/psalms/108)

[Psalm 108](/bible/psalms/108) is a psalm of singing praise to the Most High among the nations, letting your light shine. It declares that Israel, the beloved, may be delivered and saved with the Lord's right hand, and it names the tribes and the enemies, showing that the Israelites will be saved by keeping God's commandments.

**[Psalms 108:1-5](/bible/psalms/108#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/108#v1)</sup> O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise, even with my glory.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/108#v2)</sup> Awake, psaltery and harp: I myself will awake early.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/108#v3)</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord, among the people: and I will sing praises unto thee among the nations.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/108#v4)</sup> For thy mercy is great above the heavens: and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/108#v5)</sup> Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens: and thy glory above all the earth;

- O God, my heart is fixed; I will sing and give praise even with my glory. Awake, psaltery and harp; I myself will awake early. We are to be singing praise to the Lord all the time amongst the nations, letting your light shine.
- I will praise thee among the people and sing praises unto thee among the nations, for thy mercy is great above the heavens and thy truth reacheth unto the clouds. Be thou exalted, O God, above the heavens, and thy glory above all the earth.

**[Psalms 108:6-9](/bible/psalms/108#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/108#v6)</sup> That thy beloved may be delivered: save with thy right hand, and answer me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/108#v7)</sup> God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem, and mete out the valley of Succoth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/108#v8)</sup> Gilead is mine; Manasseh is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/108#v9)</sup> Moab is my washpot; over Edom will I cast out my shoe; over Philistia will I triumph.

- That thy beloved may be delivered, save with thy right hand and answer me. The beloved is Israel, and the right hand is full of righteousness, so the Israelites will be saved by keeping God's commandments.
- God has spoken in His holiness: Gilead is His, Manasseh is His, Ephraim is the strength of His head, and Judah is His lawgiver. Judah being the lawgiver is a prophecy of Christ, from whom the sceptre and rulership shall not depart.
- Moab is His washpot, the servant, and over Edom He casts out His shoe, treating Edom as a slave to clean His shoes.
  - **[Baruch 3:35-36](/bible/baruch/3#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/baruch/3#v35)</sup> This is our God, and there shall none other be accounted of in comparison of him
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/baruch/3#v36)</sup> He hath found out all the way of knowledge, and hath given it unto Jacob his servant, and to Israel his beloved.

    Israel is the beloved of the Bible, and this is our God, the God of our forefathers.
  - **[Psalms 48:10](/bible/psalms/48#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/48#v10)</sup> According to thy name, O God, so is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of righteousness.

    According to His name, so is His praise unto the ends of the earth; His right hand is full of righteousness.
  - **[Genesis 49:10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)</sup> The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.

    The sceptre shall not depart from Judah nor a lawgiver from between his feet until Shiloh come, the prophecy of Christ, unto whom the gathering of the people shall be.

**[Psalms 108:10-13](/bible/psalms/108#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/108#v10)</sup> Who will bring me into the strong city? who will lead me into Edom?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/108#v11)</sup> Wilt not thou, O God, who hast cast us off? and wilt not thou, O God, go forth with our hosts?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/108#v12)</sup> Give us help from trouble: for vain is the help of man.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/108#v13)</sup> Through God we shall do valiantly: for he it is that shall tread down our enemies.

- Who will bring me into the strong city, who will lead me into Edom? Wilt not thou, O God, who hadst cast us off, and wilt not thou go forth with our hosts? We must call upon the name of the Lord to give us help from trouble, and the trouble is our enemies.
- Give us help from trouble, for vain is the help of man. Through God we shall do valiantly, for it is He that shall tread down our enemies.
