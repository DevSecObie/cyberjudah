This session moves through [Psalms 143](/bible/psalms/143) to 146, four psalms of David. David cries out in affliction, remembers the old paths and the works of the Most High, and prays for deliverance and vengeance upon his oppressors. The teaching then turns to the coming kingdom, the greatness and mercy of the Most High, and His promise to execute judgment for the oppressed and loose the prisoners bound in captivity.

**[Psalms 143:1-2](/bible/psalms/143#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/143#v1)</sup> Hear my prayer, O Lord, give ear to my supplications: in thy faithfulness answer me, and in thy righteousness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/143#v2)</sup> And enter not into judgment with thy servant: for in thy sight shall no man living be justified.

- David opens by pleading for the Most High to hear his prayer and answer him in faithfulness and righteousness.
- He asks the Most High not to enter into judgment with him, because in His sight no man living can be counted righteous.

**[Psalms 143:3-5](/bible/psalms/143#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/143#v3)</sup> For the enemy hath persecuted my soul; he hath smitten my life down to the ground; he hath made me to dwell in darkness, as those that have been long dead.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/143#v4)</sup> Therefore is my spirit overwhelmed within me; my heart within me is desolate.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/143#v5)</sup> I remember the days of old; I meditate on all thy works; I muse on the work of thy hands.

- The enemy has smitten David's life down to the ground and made him dwell in darkness like those long dead, and his spirit is overwhelmed and his heart desolate.
- David remembers the days of old and meditates on the works and the hands of the Most High. The days of old matter for us today, because our oppressor wants us to forget our past while he never forgets his own. They always remember 9/11 and the so-called holocaust; they do not forget their history, but they want us to forget ours.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Whatsoever was written before was written for our learning, that through the scriptures we might have hope.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.

    Stand and ask for the old paths, the good way, and walk in it to find rest for your souls; but they said they would not walk therein.

**[Psalms 143:6-7](/bible/psalms/143#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/143#v6)</sup> I stretch forth my hands unto thee: my soul thirsteth after thee, as a thirsty land. Selah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/143#v7)</sup> Hear me speedily, O Lord: my spirit faileth: hide not thy face from me, lest I be like unto them that go down into the pit.

- David stretches forth his hands and pleads for the Most High to answer speedily and not hide His face, lest he be like those who go down into the pit.
- When the Most High hides His face, that is where we are today. He promised that if we walk in all His ways He would be with us and fight our battles.
  - **[Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/hosea/5#v15)</sup> I will go and return to my place, till they acknowledge their offence, and seek my face: in their affliction they will seek me early.

    The Most High returns to His place until they acknowledge their offense and seek His face; in affliction they will seek Him early. When things are good we do not focus on the Father; only when judgment comes do we consider our God.

**[Psalms 143:8-10](/bible/psalms/143#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/143#v8)</sup> Cause me to hear thy lovingkindness in the morning; for in thee do I trust: cause me to know the way wherein I should walk; for I lift up my soul unto thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/143#v9)</sup> Deliver me, O Lord, from mine enemies: I flee unto thee to hide me.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/143#v10)</sup> Teach me to do thy will; for thou art my God: thy spirit is good; lead me into the land of uprightness.

- David asks to hear the loving kindness of the Most High in the morning, to know the way he should walk, and to be delivered from his enemies.
- He prays, teach me to do thy will, for thou art my God; thy spirit is good. The will of the Most High is to keep His commandments, and His spirit is the word.
  - **[Psalms 40:8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/40#v8)</sup> I delight to do thy will, O my God: yea, thy law is within my heart.

    To do the will of the Most High is to keep His law, which is within the heart.
  - **[John 6:63](/bible/john/6#v63)**
    > <sup>[63](/bible/john/6#v63)</sup> It is the spirit that quickeneth; the flesh profiteth nothing: the words that I speak unto you, they are spirit, and they are life.

    It is the spirit that quickeneth; the words He speaks are spirit and life.
  - **[Ephesians 2:1-2](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)</sup> And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/2#v2)</sup> Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience:

    We were dead in trespasses and sins, walking according to the course of this world; this is what we had to be quickened from.
  - **[Galatians 5:19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,

    The works of the flesh that we were dead in are made manifest.

**[Psalms 143:11-12](/bible/psalms/143#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/143#v11)</sup> Quicken me, O Lord, for thy name’s sake: for thy righteousness’ sake bring my soul out of trouble.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/143#v12)</sup> And of thy mercy cut off mine enemies, and destroy all them that afflict my soul: for I am thy servant.

- David asks to be quickened and led into the land of uprightness for the name and righteousness of the Most High.
- He prays for his soul to be brought out of trouble and for his enemies and all who afflict his soul to be cut off and destroyed, for he is the Most High's servant. We have to learn how to pray for vengeance upon our enemies and oppressors who afflict our souls.

# Psalm 144: War, Deliverance, and the Kingdom to Come

Read the chapter → [Psalms 144](/bible/psalms/144)

**[Psalms 144:1-2](/bible/psalms/144#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/144#v1)</sup> Blessed be the Lord my strength, which teacheth my hands to war, and my fingers to fight:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/144#v2)</sup> My goodness, and my fortress; my high tower, and my deliverer; my shield, and he in whom I trust; who subdueth my people under me.

- David blesses the Most High, his strength, who teaches his hands to war and his fingers to fight.
- The Most High is his goodness, fortress, high tower, deliverer, and shield, who subdues his people under him.

**[Psalms 144:3-4](/bible/psalms/144#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/144#v3)</sup> Lord, what is man, that thou takest knowledge of him! or the son of man, that thou makest account of him!
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/144#v4)</sup> Man is like to vanity: his days are as a shadow that passeth away.

- David marvels that the Most High takes knowledge of man and makes account of the son of man.
- Man is like vanity and his days are as a shadow that passes away.

**[Psalms 144:5-8](/bible/psalms/144#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/144#v5)</sup> Bow thy heavens, O Lord, and come down: touch the mountains, and they shall smoke.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/144#v6)</sup> Cast forth lightning, and scatter them: shoot out thine arrows, and destroy them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/144#v7)</sup> Send thine hand from above; rid me, and deliver me out of great waters, from the hand of strange children;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/144#v8)</sup> Whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of falsehood.

- David calls on the Most High to bow the heavens and come down, to touch the mountains so they smoke, and to cast forth lightning and arrows to scatter and destroy.
- He asks to be rid and delivered out of great waters from the hand of strange children whose mouths speak vanity and whose right hand is a right hand of falsehood.

**[Psalms 144:9-11](/bible/psalms/144#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/144#v9)</sup> I will sing a new song unto thee, O God: upon a psaltery and an instrument of ten strings will I sing praises unto thee.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/144#v10)</sup> It is he that giveth salvation unto kings: who delivereth David his servant from the hurtful sword.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/144#v11)</sup> Rid me, and deliver me from the hand of strange children, whose mouth speaketh vanity, and their right hand is a right hand of falsehood:

- David vows to sing a new song of praise to the Most High, who gives salvation to kings and delivered David His servant from the hurtful sword.
- He repeats the plea to be delivered from the hand of strange children whose mouth speaks vanity and whose right hand is falsehood.

**[Psalms 144:12-14](/bible/psalms/144#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/144#v12)</sup> That our sons may be as plants grown up in their youth; that our daughters may be as corner stones, polished after the similitude of a palace:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/144#v13)</sup> That our garners may be full, affording all manner of store: that our sheep may bring forth thousands and ten thousands in our streets:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/144#v14)</sup> That our oxen may be strong to labour; that there be no breaking in, nor going out; that there be no complaining in our streets.

- David describes sons grown up as strong plants in their youth and daughters as polished cornerstones after the similitude of a palace. This is future prophecy.
- Garners full with all manner of store, sheep bringing forth thousands, oxen strong to labor, no breaking in or going out, and no complaining in the streets.

**[Psalms 144:15](/bible/psalms/144#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/144#v15)</sup> Happy is that people, that is in such a case: yea, happy is that people, whose God is the Lord.

- Happy is the people in such a case; happy is the people whose God is the Most High.
- That day is going to be returned to us, showing that in the kingdom there will be no unrighteousness.

# Psalm 145: The Greatness and Mercy of the Most High

Read the chapter → [Psalms 145](/bible/psalms/145)

**[Psalms 145:1-3](/bible/psalms/145#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/145#v1)</sup> I will extol thee, my God, O king; and I will bless thy name for ever and ever.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/145#v2)</sup> Every day will I bless thee; and I will praise thy name for ever and ever.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/145#v3)</sup> Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised; and his greatness is unsearchable.

- David extols the Most High his King and vows to bless His name forever and ever, every day.
- Great is the Most High and greatly to be praised, and His greatness is unsearchable.

**[Psalms 145:4-6](/bible/psalms/145#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/145#v4)</sup> One generation shall praise thy works to another, and shall declare thy mighty acts.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/145#v5)</sup> I will speak of the glorious honour of thy majesty, and of thy wondrous works.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/145#v6)</sup> And men shall speak of the might of thy terrible acts: and I will declare thy greatness.

- One generation shall praise His works to another and declare His mighty acts. We are to give our children the understanding of how great our Lord and King is and to speak upon His mighty acts.
- David will speak of the glorious honor of His majesty and His terrible acts and declare His greatness. When the kingdom comes we will have something new to talk about, no longer only the deliverance of the past.
  - **[Jeremiah 16:14-15](/bible/jeremiah/16#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/16#v14)</sup> Therefore, behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that it shall no more be said, The Lord liveth, that brought up the children of Israel out of the land of Egypt;
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/16#v15)</sup> But, The Lord liveth, that brought up the children of Israel from the land of the north, and from all the lands whither he had driven them: and I will bring them again into their land that I gave unto their fathers.

    The days come when it shall no more be said the Most High liveth that brought Israel out of Egypt, but that brought them from the land of the north, which is North America.
  - **[Jeremiah 50:33](/bible/jeremiah/50#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/50#v33)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; The children of Israel and the children of Judah were oppressed together: and all that took them captives held them fast; they refused to let them go.

    Judah and Israel were oppressed together, pressed together right here in Babylon, in America.
  - **[Isaiah 11:11](/bible/isaiah/11#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/11#v11)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, that the Lord shall set his hand again the second time to recover the remnant of his people, which shall be left, from Assyria, and from Egypt, and from Pathros, and from Cush, and from Elam, and from Shinar, and from Hamath, and from the islands of the sea.

    The Most High will set His hand again to recover His people from all the places He scattered them.

**[Psalms 145:7-9](/bible/psalms/145#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/145#v7)</sup> They shall abundantly utter the memory of thy great goodness, and shall sing of thy righteousness.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/145#v8)</sup> The Lord is gracious, and full of compassion; slow to anger, and of great mercy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/145#v9)</sup> The Lord is good to all: and his tender mercies are over all his works.

- They shall utter the memory of His great goodness and sing of His righteousness; the Most High is gracious, full of compassion, slow to anger, and of great mercy.
- His compassion and mercy is another word for grace. He is good to all, and His tender mercies are over all His works.
  - **[Acts 13:38-39](/bible/acts/13#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/13#v38)</sup> Be it known unto you therefore, men and brethren, that through this man is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins:
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/13#v39)</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.

    Through Christ the forgiveness of sins is preached, and all who believe are justified from things the law of Moses could not justify, since some sins in that law required our blood.

**[Psalms 145:10-13](/bible/psalms/145#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/145#v10)</sup> All thy works shall praise thee, O Lord; and thy saints shall bless thee.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/145#v11)</sup> They shall speak of the glory of thy kingdom, and talk of thy power;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/145#v12)</sup> To make known to the sons of men his mighty acts, and the glorious majesty of his kingdom.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/145#v13)</sup> Thy kingdom is an everlasting kingdom, and thy dominion endureth throughout all generations.

- All His works shall praise Him and His saints shall bless Him, speaking of the glory of His kingdom and His power.
- His kingdom is an everlasting kingdom and His dominion endures through all generations.

**[Psalms 145:14-16](/bible/psalms/145#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/145#v14)</sup> The Lord upholdeth all that fall, and raiseth up all those that be bowed down.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/145#v15)</sup> The eyes of all wait upon thee; and thou givest them their meat in due season.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/145#v16)</sup> Thou openest thine hand, and satisfiest the desire of every living thing.

- The Most High upholds all that fall and raises up all those that are bowed down.
- The eyes of all wait upon Him, and He opens His hand and satisfies the desire of every living thing.

**[Psalms 145:17-18](/bible/psalms/145#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/145#v17)</sup> The Lord is righteous in all his ways, and holy in all his works.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/145#v18)</sup> The Lord is nigh unto all them that call upon him, to all that call upon him in truth.

- The Most High is righteous in all His ways and holy in all His works.
- He is near to all who call upon Him in truth. Calling upon Him in truth means keeping His commandments.
  - **[John 4:24](/bible/john/4#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/john/4#v24)</sup> God is a Spirit: and they that worship him must worship him in spirit and in truth.

    God is a spirit, and they that worship Him must worship Him in spirit and in truth.
  - **[Psalms 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    His law is the truth, so we must worship Him according to the commandments.

**[Psalms 145:19-21](/bible/psalms/145#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/145#v19)</sup> He will fulfil the desire of them that fear him: he also will hear their cry, and will save them.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/145#v20)</sup> The Lord preserveth all them that love him: but all the wicked will he destroy.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/145#v21)</sup> My mouth shall speak the praise of the Lord: and let all flesh bless his holy name for ever and ever.

- He fulfills the desire of them that fear Him, hears their cry, and saves them.
- The Most High preserves all that love Him but destroys the wicked; loving Him means keeping His commandments.
  - **[1 John 5:3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)</sup> For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.

    This is the love of God, that we keep His commandments.

# Psalm 146: The Most High Looses the Prisoners

Read the chapter → [Psalms 146](/bible/psalms/146)

**[Psalms 146:1-2](/bible/psalms/146#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/146#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise the Lord, O my soul.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/146#v2)</sup> While I live will I praise the Lord: I will sing praises unto my God while I have any being.

- David calls his soul to praise the Most High.
- He will praise the Most High while he lives and has any being.

**[Psalms 146:3-4](/bible/psalms/146#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/146#v3)</sup> Put not your trust in princes, nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/146#v4)</sup> His breath goeth forth, he returneth to his earth; in that very day his thoughts perish.

- Put not your trust in princes nor in the son of man, in whom there is no help.
- His breath goes forth, he returns to his earth, and in that very day his thoughts perish.

**[Psalms 146:5-6](/bible/psalms/146#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/146#v5)</sup> Happy is he that hath the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the Lord his God:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/146#v6)</sup> Which made heaven, and earth, the sea, and all that therein is: which keepeth truth for ever:

- Happy is he that has the God of Jacob for his help, whose hope is in the Most High his God.
- He made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that is in them, and keeps truth forever.

**[Psalms 146:7](/bible/psalms/146#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/146#v7)</sup> Which executeth judgment for the oppressed: which giveth food to the hungry. The Lord looseth the prisoners:

- He executes judgment for the oppressed, gives food to the hungry, and looses the prisoners. We are the prisoners, prisoners of hope bound here in Babylon. If you do not think we are prisoners and want to go back to Africa or to the homeland, which is Jerusalem in northeast Africa, go find a boat or a plane ticket and try to produce a passport; that passport is your permission slip. Just because you do not have chains on your neck, do not be fooled that we are not bound here.
  - **[Jeremiah 50:33](/bible/jeremiah/50#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/jeremiah/50#v33)</sup> Thus saith the Lord of hosts; The children of Israel and the children of Judah were oppressed together: and all that took them captives held them fast; they refused to let them go.

    Israel and Judah were oppressed together, and all that took them captive held them fast and refused to let them go.
  - **[Romans 9:17](/bible/romans/9#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/romans/9#v17)</sup> For the scripture saith unto Pharaoh, Even for this same purpose have I raised thee up, that I might shew my power in thee, and that my name might be declared throughout all the earth.

    The Most High raised up Pharaoh to show His power and declare His name throughout all the earth; the oppressor has that same mindset, that he will not let us go, because he likes us weak and our people destroyed so he remains on top.
  - **[Exodus 21:16](/bible/exodus/21#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/21#v16)</sup> And he that stealeth a man, and selleth him, or if he be found in his hand, he shall surely be put to death.

    He that steals a man and sells him, or in whose hand he is found, shall surely be put to death; our oppressors who stole us and brought us here have not been put to death.
  - **[Revelation 13:10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/13#v10)</sup> He that leadeth into captivity shall go into captivity: he that killeth with the sword must be killed with the sword. Here is the patience and the faith of the saints.

    He that leads into captivity shall go into captivity, and he that kills with the sword must be killed with the sword; here is the patience and faith of the saints, and we are the saints, the Israelites.

**[Psalms 146:8-9](/bible/psalms/146#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/146#v8)</sup> The Lord openeth the eyes of the blind: the Lord raiseth them that are bowed down: the Lord loveth the righteous:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/146#v9)</sup> The Lord preserveth the strangers; he relieveth the fatherless and widow: but the way of the wicked he turneth upside down.

- The Most High opens the eyes of the blind, and those tuned in are witnesses to Him opening their eyes to this truth, that they are the Israelites and not negros, hispanics, or African-Americans, and not the derogatory terms we were raised under.
- He raises them that are bowed down, loves the righteous, which is what we are, preserves the strangers, and relieves the fatherless and widow, but the way of the wicked He turns upside down; that is what we are waiting on.

**[Psalms 146:10](/bible/psalms/146#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/146#v10)</sup> The Lord shall reign for ever, even thy God, O Zion, unto all generations. Praise ye the Lord.

- The Most High shall reign forever, even thy God, O Zion, unto all generations.
- This brings us back into the knowledge of who we are.
