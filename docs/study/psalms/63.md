Read the chapter → [Psalms 63](/bible/psalms/63)

This session reads [Psalms 63](/bible/psalms/63) through 66, four chapters a day, called relatively short chapters with a lot to go into. This is a reading recording with brief exposition: the summary given is that these psalms are David giving praise to the Most High and showing the workings of the wicked, which the bishop touches on all the time. [Psalm 63](/bible/psalms/63) is the thirst of the soul for the Most High in a dry and thirsty land, the praise that is better than life, and the promise that those who seek the soul to destroy it go down into the lower parts of the earth.

**[Psalms 63:1-2](/bible/psalms/63#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/63#v1)</sup> O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/63#v2)</sup> To see thy power and thy glory, so as I have seen thee in the sanctuary.

- David seeks the Most High early; his soul thirsts and his flesh longs for Him in a dry and thirsty land where no water is.
- He longs to see the power and glory of the Most High as he has seen it in the sanctuary.

**[Psalms 63:3-5](/bible/psalms/63#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/63#v3)</sup> Because thy lovingkindness is better than life, my lips shall praise thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/63#v4)</sup> Thus will I bless thee while I live: I will lift up my hands in thy name.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/63#v5)</sup> My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness; and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips:

- Because the loving kindness of the Most High is better than life, David's lips shall praise Him, and he will bless Him while he lives and lift up his hands in His name.
- His soul shall be satisfied with marrow and fatness, and his mouth shall praise Him with joyful lips.

**[Psalms 63:6-8](/bible/psalms/63#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/63#v6)</sup> When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/63#v7)</sup> Because thou hast been my help, therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/63#v8)</sup> My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me.

- When he remembers the Most High upon his bed and meditates on Him in the night watches, he rejoices in the shadow of His wings because He has been his help.
- His soul follows hard after the Most High, and His right hand upholds him.

**[Psalms 63:9-11](/bible/psalms/63#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/63#v9)</sup> But those that seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the lower parts of the earth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/63#v10)</sup> They shall fall by the sword: they shall be a portion for foxes.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/63#v11)</sup> But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by him shall glory: but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.

- Those that seek his soul to destroy it shall go down into the lower parts of the earth; they shall fall by the sword and be a portion for foxes.
- The king shall rejoice in the Most High, and everyone that swears by Him shall glory, but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.
