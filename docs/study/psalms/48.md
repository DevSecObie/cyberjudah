**[Psalm 48:1-3](/bible/psalms/48#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/48#v1)</sup> Great is the Lord, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/48#v2)</sup> Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth, is mount Zion, on the sides of the north, the city of the great King.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/48#v3)</sup> God is known in her palaces for a refuge.

- Great is the Most High and greatly to be praised in Zion, beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth and the most sought after place on the earth.
- This is why Esau always coveted the land and told Babylon to raze it even to the foundation; they covet fields and take them by violence.
- The kingdom of heaven, the Israelites, suffered violence and the violent took it by force; Israel was taken out of the land by violence and Jerusalem is trodden down of the Gentiles until their time is fulfilled.
  - **[Micah 2:2](/bible/micah/2#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/micah/2#v2)</sup> And they covet fields, and take them by violence; and houses, and take them away: so they oppress a man and his house, even a man and his heritage.

    They covet fields and take them by violence, and houses, and take them away, oppressing a man and his heritage.
  - **[Matthew 11:12](/bible/matthew/11#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/11#v12)</sup> And from the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence, and the violent take it by force.

    From the days of John the Baptist until now the kingdom of heaven suffereth violence and the violent take it by force.
  - **[Luke 21:24](/bible/luke/21#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/luke/21#v24)</sup> And they shall fall by the edge of the sword, and shall be led away captive into all nations: and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles, until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.

    Israel shall fall by the edge of the sword and be led away captive into all nations, and Jerusalem shall be trodden down of the Gentiles until the times of the Gentiles be fulfilled.

**[Psalm 48:4-8](/bible/psalms/48#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/48#v4)</sup> For, lo, the kings were assembled, they passed by together.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/48#v5)</sup> They saw it, and so they marvelled; they were troubled, and hasted away.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/48#v6)</sup> Fear took hold upon them there, and pain, as of a woman in travail.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/48#v7)</sup> Thou breakest the ships of Tarshish with an east wind.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/48#v8)</sup> As we have heard, so have we seen in the city of the Lord of hosts, in the city of our God: God will establish it for ever. Selah.

- Israel is the joy of the earth, the salt of the earth; you cannot make America great without the slaves who are the strongest people on earth working for crumbs.
- Israel's savor is the commandments; having lost the identity and the commandments, Israel became good for nothing but to be trodden under foot, only worth more when keeping the commandments and knowing the identity.
- When Israel was in rulership the nations fled and were afraid; they understood they could not come against Israel when the Most High was among them.
- The kings assembled, saw Zion, marveled, were troubled and hasted away; fear and pain took hold of them as of a woman in travail.

    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/5#v14)</sup> Ye are the light of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.

**[Psalm 48:9-11](/bible/psalms/48#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/48#v9)</sup> We have thought of thy lovingkindness, O God, in the midst of thy temple.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/48#v10)</sup> According to thy name, O God, so is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: thy right hand is full of righteousness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/48#v11)</sup> Let mount Zion rejoice, let the daughters of Judah be glad, because of thy judgments.

- The Most High will establish Jerusalem forever and break the ships of Tarshish; his right hand is full of righteousness and his praise reaches the ends of the earth.
- Let Zion rejoice and the daughters of Judah be glad because of the Most High's judgments; Israel is to rejoice when he brings fear upon the nations.
- The nations are perplexed and in distress right now over the coronavirus, hearts failing them for fear, while Israel with understanding is to be rejoicing that redemption draws near.
- While the nations are afraid Israel should gather together, get the pantry and the food and the house and the spirit in order, and rejoice rather than run around in fear.

**[Psalm 48:12-14](/bible/psalms/48#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/48#v12)</sup> Walk about Zion, and go round about her: tell the towers thereof.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/48#v13)</sup> Mark ye well her bulwarks, consider her palaces; that ye may tell it to the generation following.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/48#v14)</sup> For this God is our God for ever and ever: he will be our guide even unto death.

- Walk about Zion, count her towers, mark her bulwarks and consider her palaces, to tell it to the generation following, for this Most High is our God forever.
- He will be Israel's guide even unto death; through Ezra the Most High tells his beloved that in the days of trouble he will deliver them and be their guide, so they should not be afraid or doubt.
- The Most High led the teaching straight to that scripture; the Bible precepts link together because he leads his people to the places they need.

    > <sup>[74](/bible/2-esdras/16#v74)</sup> Hear, O ye my beloved, saith the Lord: behold, the days of trouble are at hand, but I will deliver you from the same.
    >
    > <sup>[75](/bible/2-esdras/16#v75)</sup> Be ye not afraid neither doubt; for God is your guide,
    >
    > <sup>[76](/bible/2-esdras/16#v76)</sup> And the guide of them who keep my commandments and precepts, saith the Lord God: let not your sins weigh you down, and let not your iniquities lift up themselves.

    In the days of trouble the Most High delivers his beloved and guides them that keep his commandments.
