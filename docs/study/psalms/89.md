Read the chapter → [Psalms 89](/bible/psalms/89)

[Psalm 89](/bible/psalms/89) sings of the mercies of the Lord forever and of the covenant the Most High made with David His servant, that his seed would be established forever and his throne built up to all generations. The teaching stresses that Christ is of the seed of David, and that the doctrines of a virgin birth denying this lineage are blasphemy against the Bible.

**[Psalms 89:1-4](/bible/psalms/89#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/89#v1)</sup> I will sing of the mercies of the Lord for ever: with my mouth will I make known thy faithfulness to all generations.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/89#v2)</sup> For I have said, Mercy shall be built up for ever: thy faithfulness shalt thou establish in the very heavens.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/89#v3)</sup> I have made a covenant with my chosen, I have sworn unto David my servant,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/89#v4)</sup> Thy seed will I establish for ever, and build up thy throne to all generations. Selah.

- He will sing of the mercies of the Lord forever, making known His faithfulness to all generations, for mercy shall be built up forever and His faithfulness established in the very heavens.
- The Most High has made a covenant with His chosen and sworn unto David His servant that his seed will be established forever and his throne built up to all generations. Because he was the seed of David, the doctrine of the virgin birth denying that lineage is blasphemy against the Bible, vain lies.
  - **[Psalms 132:11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)</sup> The Lord hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.

    The Lord has sworn in truth unto David, that of the fruit of his body He would set upon his throne.

**[Psalms 89:5-8](/bible/psalms/89#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/89#v5)</sup> And the heavens shall praise thy wonders, O Lord: thy faithfulness also in the congregation of the saints.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/89#v6)</sup> For who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord? who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto the Lord?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/89#v7)</sup> God is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints, and to be had in reverence of all them that are about him.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/89#v8)</sup> O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto thee? or to thy faithfulness round about thee?

- The heavens shall praise His wonders and His faithfulness in the congregation of the saints, for who in the heaven can be compared unto the Lord, who among the sons of the mighty can be likened unto Him.
- The Most High is greatly to be feared in the assembly of the saints and to be had in reverence of all them about Him; O Lord God of hosts, who is a strong Lord like unto Him, or to His faithfulness round about Him.

**[Psalms 89:9-13](/bible/psalms/89#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/89#v9)</sup> Thou rulest the raging of the sea: when the waves thereof arise, thou stillest them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/89#v10)</sup> Thou hast broken Rahab in pieces, as one that is slain; thou hast scattered thine enemies with thy strong arm.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/89#v11)</sup> The heavens are thine, the earth also is thine: as for the world and the fulness thereof, thou hast founded them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/89#v12)</sup> The north and the south thou hast created them: Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in thy name.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/89#v13)</sup> Thou hast a mighty arm: strong is thy hand, and high is thy right hand.

- The Most High rules the raging of the sea; when the waves arise, He stills them, like when Christ was on the boat and they said Lord you don't care if we perish, and he rebuked the storm, and they said what manner of man is this that even the sea obeys him.
- He has broken Rahab in pieces as one that is slain and scattered His enemies with His strong arm; the heavens are His and the earth also, the world and the fullness thereof He has founded.
- The north and the south He has created; Tabor and Hermon shall rejoice in His name; He has a mighty arm, strong is His hand and high is His right hand.

**[Psalms 89:14-18](/bible/psalms/89#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/89#v14)</sup> Justice and judgment are the habitation of thy throne: mercy and truth shall go before thy face.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/89#v15)</sup> Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound: they shall walk, O Lord, in the light of thy countenance.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/89#v16)</sup> In thy name shall they rejoice all the day: and in thy righteousness shall they be exalted.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/89#v17)</sup> For thou art the glory of their strength: and in thy favour our horn shall be exalted.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/89#v18)</sup> For the Lord is our defence; and the Holy One of Israel is our king.

- Justice and judgment are the habitation of His throne; mercy and truth shall go before His face. That is the gospel, and it is a great thing to have a just God who has the final authority.
- We go through things, but this should build up your faith; it is why it is so important for things to be done decent and in order, the order that God established.
- Blessed is the people that know the joyful sound; they shall walk in the light of His countenance. We are going to walk in the laws and be before the Lord blameless like Abraham and our forefathers were.
  - **[Revelation 19:11](/bible/revelation/19#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/revelation/19#v11)</sup> And I saw heaven opened, and behold a white horse; and he that sat upon him was called Faithful and True, and in righteousness he doth judge and make war.

    In righteousness He doth judge and make war, for justice and judgment are His throne.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 5:8](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v8)</sup> If thou seest the oppression of the poor, and violent perverting of judgment and justice in a province, marvel not at the matter: for he that is higher than the highest regardeth; and there be higher than they.

    See the violent perverting of judgment; marvel not, for there is one higher than they, over how they shoot us down in the streets.

**[Psalms 89:19-23](/bible/psalms/89#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/89#v19)</sup> Then thou spakest in vision to thy holy one, and saidst, I have laid help upon one that is mighty; I have exalted one chosen out of the people.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/89#v20)</sup> I have found David my servant; with my holy oil have I anointed him:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/89#v21)</sup> With whom my hand shall be established: mine arm also shall strengthen him.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/89#v22)</sup> The enemy shall not exact upon him; nor the son of wickedness afflict him.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/89#v23)</sup> And I will beat down his foes before his face, and plague them that hate him.

- The more we understand the importance of this truth, the more our walk becomes a tightrope walk, making sure we walk the straight and narrow, like John the Baptist said, let all the crooked ways be made straight and make straight the path of the Lord.
- The Most High spoke in vision to His Holy One, saying He has laid help upon one that is mighty, exalted one chosen out of the people, found David His servant and anointed him with His holy oil, with whom His hand shall be established and His arm shall strengthen him.
- So the Most High made a way for salvation for His people through David, through the chosen lineage of David the son of Jesse. The enemy shall not exact upon him nor the son of wickedness afflict him, and He will beat down his foes before his face and plague them that hate him.

**[Psalms 89:24-29](/bible/psalms/89#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/89#v24)</sup> But my faithfulness and my mercy shall be with him: and in my name shall his horn be exalted.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/89#v25)</sup> I will set his hand also in the sea, and his right hand in the rivers.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/89#v26)</sup> He shall cry unto me, Thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/89#v27)</sup> Also I will make him my firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/89#v28)</sup> My mercy will I keep for him for evermore, and my covenant shall stand fast with him.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/89#v29)</sup> His seed also will I make to endure for ever, and his throne as the days of heaven.

- His faithfulness and His mercy shall be with him, and in His name shall his horn be exalted; He will set his hand in the sea and his right hand in the rivers.
- He shall cry unto the Most High, thou art my father, my God, and the rock of my salvation; and He will make him His firstborn, higher than the kings of the earth. Christ is the firstborn from the dead.
- His mercy the Most High will keep for him forevermore, and His covenant shall stand fast with him; his seed also He will make to endure forever and his throne as the days of heaven. That is why of his kingdom there shall be no end.

**[Psalms 89:30-37](/bible/psalms/89#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/89#v30)</sup> If his children forsake my law, and walk not in my judgments;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/89#v31)</sup> If they break my statutes, and keep not my commandments;
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/89#v32)</sup> Then will I visit their transgression with the rod, and their iniquity with stripes.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/89#v33)</sup> Nevertheless my lovingkindness will I not utterly take from him, nor suffer my faithfulness to fail.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/89#v34)</sup> My covenant will I not break, nor alter the thing that is gone out of my lips.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/89#v35)</sup> Once have I sworn by my holiness that I will not lie unto David.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/89#v36)</sup> His seed shall endure for ever, and his throne as the sun before me.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/psalms/89#v37)</sup> It shall be established for ever as the moon, and as a faithful witness in heaven. Selah.

- If his children forsake His law and walk not in His judgments, if they break His statutes and keep not His commandments, then He will visit their transgression with the rod and their iniquity with stripes; nevertheless His lovingkindness He will not utterly take from him. People say mercy is not in the Old Testament, but it is in there.
- The Most High will not break His covenant nor alter the thing gone out of His lips; once He has sworn by His holiness He will not lie unto David, that his seed shall endure forever and his throne as the sun before Him, established forever as the moon and as a faithful witness in heaven.
- This is the visiting of iniquity in the regeneration; after death comes the judgment, when you reform your mind and subdue your own understanding, and you will be kept alive and obtain mercy.
  - **[Deuteronomy 5:9](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v9)</sup> Thou shalt not bow down thyself unto them, nor serve them: for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me,

    I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me, showing mercy unto thousands of them that love me.
  - **[2 Esdras 14:34](/bible/2-esdras/14#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/2-esdras/14#v34)</sup> Therefore if so be that ye will subdue your own understanding, and reform your hearts, ye shall be kept alive and after death ye shall obtain mercy.

    If you subdue your own understanding and reform your mind, after death you shall obtain mercy and be kept alive.
  - **[Isaiah 66:22](/bible/isaiah/66#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/66#v22)</sup> For as the new heavens and the new earth, which I will make, shall remain before me, saith the Lord, so shall your seed and your name remain.

    As the new heavens and the new earth remain before Him, so shall your seed and your name remain.
  - **[Jeremiah 31:35-36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The Lord of hosts is his name:
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v36)</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the Lord, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.

    Just like the sun, moon, and sky are a witness, the Lord has not forsaken His chosen people.

**[Psalms 89:38-45](/bible/psalms/89#v38)**

> <sup>[38](/bible/psalms/89#v38)</sup> But thou hast cast off and abhorred, thou hast been wroth with thine anointed.
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/psalms/89#v39)</sup> Thou hast made void the covenant of thy servant: thou hast profaned his crown by casting it to the ground.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/psalms/89#v40)</sup> Thou hast broken down all his hedges; thou hast brought his strong holds to ruin.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/psalms/89#v41)</sup> All that pass by the way spoil him: he is a reproach to his neighbours.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/psalms/89#v42)</sup> Thou hast set up the right hand of his adversaries; thou hast made all his enemies to rejoice.
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/psalms/89#v43)</sup> Thou hast also turned the edge of his sword, and hast not made him to stand in the battle.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/psalms/89#v44)</sup> Thou hast made his glory to cease, and cast his throne down to the ground.
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/psalms/89#v45)</sup> The days of his youth hast thou shortened: thou hast covered him with shame. Selah.

- But the Most High has cast off and abhorred, been wroth with His anointed, made void the covenant of His servant, profaned his crown by casting it to the ground, and broken down all his hedges and brought his strongholds to ruin.
- All that pass by the way spoil him; he is a reproach to his neighbor; his adversaries are set up and his enemies made to rejoice. This sounds like it is talking about Saul.
- The Most High has turned the edge of his sword and not made him to stand in battle, made his glory to cease and cast his throne down to the ground, shortened the days of his youth and covered him with shame. It sounds like Saul, who fell on his own sword.

**[Psalms 89:46-52](/bible/psalms/89#v46)**

> <sup>[46](/bible/psalms/89#v46)</sup> How long, Lord? wilt thou hide thyself for ever? shall thy wrath burn like fire?
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/psalms/89#v47)</sup> Remember how short my time is: wherefore hast thou made all men in vain?
>
> <sup>[48](/bible/psalms/89#v48)</sup> What man is he that liveth, and shall not see death? shall he deliver his soul from the hand of the grave? Selah.
>
> <sup>[49](/bible/psalms/89#v49)</sup> Lord, where are thy former lovingkindnesses, which thou swarest unto David in thy truth?
>
> <sup>[50](/bible/psalms/89#v50)</sup> Remember, Lord, the reproach of thy servants; how I do bear in my bosom the reproach of all the mighty people;
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/psalms/89#v51)</sup> Wherewith thine enemies have reproached, O Lord; wherewith they have reproached the footsteps of thine anointed.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/psalms/89#v52)</sup> Blessed be the Lord for evermore. Amen, and Amen.

- He asks how long the Most High will hide Himself, whether His wrath shall burn like fire; he remembers how short his time is, asking wherefore He has made all men in vain. Solomon has a whole chapter about the vanity of this thing we call life and the things we covet.
- What man is he that liveth and shall not see death, that shall deliver his soul from the hand of the grave; he asks where are the former lovingkindnesses which the Most High swore unto David in His truth.
- He asks the Most High to remember the reproach of His servants, how they have reproached the footsteps of His anointed. They hated the very steps of Christ, and the Lord is going to remember that. Blessed be the Lord forevermore, amen and amen.
