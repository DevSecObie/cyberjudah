Read the chapter → [Psalms 55](/bible/psalms/55)

This session moves through [Psalms 55](/bible/psalms/55) through 58, read for the nation four chapters a day out of the Orlando camp. [Psalm 55](/bible/psalms/55) is the cry of a man surrounded by the voice of the enemy and the oppression of the wicked, longing to fly away like a dove and be at rest, yet betrayed by his own acquaintance. The teaching drives home that we have to put ourselves in the scriptures and see our own condition in them: even when we come into keeping the commandments we still suffer the same oppression, the violence and strife in the city, the betrayal that King David and Christ both endured. The answer is not escape but endurance, casting the burden upon the Most High who will sustain the righteous.

**[Psalms 55:1-3](/bible/psalms/55#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/55#v1)</sup> Give ear to my prayer, O God; and hide not thyself from my supplication.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/55#v2)</sup> Attend unto me, and hear me: I mourn in my complaint, and make a noise;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/55#v3)</sup> Because of the voice of the enemy, because of the oppression of the wicked: for they cast iniquity upon me, and in wrath they hate me.

- David asks the Most High to give ear to his prayer and not hide from his supplication. You have to put yourself in the Bible and see yourself in these scriptures, the way Elder Kena, Bishop, and the leadership men teach.
- He mourns and makes a noise because of the voice of the enemy and the oppression of the wicked, who cast iniquity upon him and hate him in wrath.
- The way to get the Most High to hear our prayers is righteousness. His eyes are over the righteous and His ears are open to their prayers, but His face is against them that do evil.
  - **[1 Peter 3:12](/bible/1-peter/3#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-peter/3#v12)</sup> For the eyes of the Lord are over the righteous, and his ears are open unto their prayers: but the face of the Lord is against them that do evil.

    The eyes of the Most High are over the righteous and His ears are open to their prayers.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v25)</sup> And it shall be our righteousness, if we observe to do all these commandments before the Lord our God, as he hath commanded us.

    It shall be our righteousness if we observe to do all these commandments as He commanded.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 35:16-17](/bible/sirach/35#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/35#v16)</sup> He that serveth the Lord shall be accepted with favour, and his prayer shall reach unto the clouds.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/35#v17)</sup> The prayer of the humble pierceth the clouds: and till it come nigh, he will not be comforted; and will not depart, till the most High shall behold to judge righteously, and execute judgment.

    He that serveth the Most High is accepted with favor and his prayer reaches unto the clouds, and the prayer of the humble pierceth the clouds.

**[Psalms 55:4-5](/bible/psalms/55#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/55#v4)</sup> My heart is sore pained within me: and the terrors of death are fallen upon me.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/55#v5)</sup> Fearfulness and trembling are come upon me, and horror hath overwhelmed me.

- His heart is sore pained within him and the terrors of death are fallen upon him. Think about the condition we are in today; even keeping the commandments, we still suffer the same oppression, though the Most High covers those that keep them.
- When you drive, police will still jump behind you and run your plates, so make sure your tags are legal and not expired.
- The fearfulness and trembling and horror are written in the curses, the curses we did not learn in the Christian church. Many of the scriptures come back to these curses.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:65-66](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)**
    > <sup>[65](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v65)</sup> And among these nations shalt thou find no ease, neither shall the sole of thy foot have rest: but the Lord shall give thee there a trembling heart, and failing of eyes, and sorrow of mind:
    >
    > <sup>[66](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v66)</sup> And thy life shall hang in doubt before thee; and thou shalt fear day and night, and shalt have none assurance of thy life:

    Among these nations thou shalt find no ease; the Most High gives a trembling heart, failing eyes, sorrow of mind, and thy life hangs in doubt.

**[Psalms 55:6-8](/bible/psalms/55#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/55#v6)</sup> And I said, Oh that I had wings like a dove! for then would I fly away, and be at rest.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/55#v7)</sup> Lo, then would I wander far off, and remain in the wilderness. Selah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/55#v8)</sup> I would hasten my escape from the windy storm and tempest.

- He wishes for wings like a dove to fly away and be at rest, to wander far off and remain in the wilderness, hastening his escape from the windy storm and tempest.
- When these things come upon us many times you would think, if I could just get away, just leave this place. But the scriptures say we must serve out this punishment and endure much tribulation to enter the kingdom.
- Our eyes have been enlightened to why these things happen. George Floyd and Breonna Taylor got the most news for 2020, but many of our people have suffered getting beat and murdered, like the brother they found in the cornfield.
  - **[Acts 14:22](/bible/acts/14#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/acts/14#v22)</sup> Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

    We must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom, and the four chapters a day is part of that exhortation to continue in the faith.
  - **[Mark 10:29-30](/bible/mark/10#v29)**
    > <sup>[29](/bible/mark/10#v29)</sup> And Jesus answered and said, Verily I say unto you, There is no man that hath left house, or brethren, or sisters, or father, or mother, or wife, or children, or lands, for my sake, and the gospel’s,

    > <sup>[30](/bible/mark/10#v30)</sup> But he shall receive an hundredfold now in this time, houses, and brethren, and sisters, and mothers, and children, and lands, with persecutions; and in the world to come eternal life.

    Whoever leaves house or family for the gospel receives a hundredfold now with persecutions, and in the world to come eternal life.

**[Psalms 55:9-11](/bible/psalms/55#v9)**

- He asks the Most High to destroy and divide their tongues, for he has seen violence and strife in the city. This is us being cursed in the city.
- You want to hasten your escape because you open the doors and go out into the world to work, shop, or school, and there is all this strife among our people. Moses dealt with the same thing when he left Pharaoh's house and found two men fighting.
- Day and night they go about seeking violence; wickedness is in the midst and deceit and guile depart not from her streets.

>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/55#v17)</sup> Evening, and morning, and at noon, will I pray, and cry aloud: and he shall hear my voice.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/55#v18)</sup> He hath delivered my soul in peace from the battle that was against me: for there were many with me.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/55#v19)</sup> God shall hear, and afflict them, even he that abideth of old. Selah. Because they have no changes, therefore they fear not God.

- He asks that death seize the wicked and they go down quick into hell, for wickedness is in their dwellings, while he calls upon the Most High evening, morning, and at noon, and is heard and delivered.
- The Most High hears and afflicts them because they have no changes; there was no change in us, no repentance.
- Even when He pitied us and provided everything, bearing us on His wings, we rebelled and vexed His Holy Spirit, so He turned to be our enemy.

>
