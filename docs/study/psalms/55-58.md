Read the chapter → [Psalms 55](/bible/psalms/55)

This session moves through [Psalms 55](/bible/psalms/55) through 58, read for the nation four chapters a day out of the Orlando camp. [Psalm 55](/bible/psalms/55) is the cry of a man surrounded by the voice of the enemy and the oppression of the wicked, longing to fly away like a dove and be at rest, yet betrayed by his own acquaintance. The teaching drives home that we have to put ourselves in the scriptures and see our own condition in them: even when we come into keeping the commandments we still suffer the same oppression, the violence and strife in the city, the betrayal that King David and Christ both endured. The answer is not escape but endurance, casting the burden upon the Most High who will sustain the righteous.

- He mourns and makes a noise because of the voice of the enemy and the oppression of the wicked, who cast iniquity upon him and hate him in wrath.
- The way to get the Most High to hear our prayers is righteousness. His eyes are over the righteous and His ears are open to their prayers, but His face is against them that do evil.

# Psalm 56: I Will Not Fear What Flesh Can Do Unto Me

Read the chapter → [Psalms 56](/bible/psalms/56)

[Psalm 56](/bible/psalms/56) is the confidence of a man daily swallowed up and fought against by many, who resolves to trust the Most High and not fear what flesh can do. The teaching applies this to Christ, whom the scribes and Pharisees followed daily to catch Him in His words, and to the enemies of the truth today who come against IUIC. The answer is the same: put all your trust in the Most High, hold the line, and be a light to your brothers and sisters.

**[Psalms 56:1-4](/bible/psalms/56#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/56#v1)</sup> Be merciful unto me, O God: for man would swallow me up; he fighting daily oppresseth me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/56#v2)</sup> Mine enemies would daily swallow me up: for they be many that fight against me, O thou most High.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/56#v3)</sup> What time I am afraid, I will trust in thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/56#v4)</sup> In God I will praise his word, in God I have put my trust; I will not fear what flesh can do unto me.

- He asks for mercy because man would swallow him up, fighting daily and oppressing him, for many fight against him. What time he is afraid, he will trust in the Most High.
- In God he praises His word and puts his trust, and will not fear what flesh can do. This does not mean you never experience fear, but you will not put the fear of man over the fear of God or let it stand between you and the mission.
  - **[Matthew 10:28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)**
    > <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/10#v28)</sup> And fear not them which kill the body, but are not able to kill the soul: but rather fear him which is able to destroy both soul and body in hell.

    Fear not them that kill the body but cannot kill the soul; rather fear Him who can destroy both soul and body in hell.

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/56#v7)</sup> Shall they escape by iniquity? in thine anger cast down the people, O God.

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/56#v8)</sup> Thou tellest my wanderings: put thou my tears into thy bottle: are they not in thy book?

# Psalm 57: My Heart Is Fixed

Read the chapter → [Psalms 57](/bible/psalms/57)

[Psalm 57](/bible/psalms/57) makes the shadow of the Most High's wings a refuge until the calamities be overpast. The teaching locates that refuge in meditating on the scriptures day and night, in the secret place of the Most High, and lifts up the deliverance He sends from heaven, illustrated by the angels that scourged Heliodorus in 2 Maccabees. The heart that is fixed is the tree planted by the rivers of water that will not be swayed by doctrines, looks, or money, and no one takes its crown.

# Psalm 58: The Righteous Shall Rejoice When He Seeth the Vengeance

Read the chapter → [Psalms 58](/bible/psalms/58)

[Psalm 58](/bible/psalms/58) calls out the wicked congregation that weighs violence in the earth, estranged from the womb and full of the poison of a serpent, and asks the Most High to break their teeth. The teaching lands on the vengeance to come: the righteous washing his feet in the blood of the wicked, tied to Isaiah's treader of the winepress and to Ezekiel's burial of Gog, when the house of Israel will spend seven months burying the multitude and know that there is a God that judges the earth.

**[Psalms 58:1-5](/bible/psalms/58#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/58#v1)</sup> Do ye indeed speak righteousness, O congregation? do ye judge uprightly, O ye sons of men?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/58#v2)</sup> Yea, in heart ye work wickedness; ye weigh the violence of your hands in the earth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/58#v3)</sup> The wicked are estranged from the womb: they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/58#v4)</sup> Their poison is like the poison of a serpent: they are like the deaf adder that stoppeth her ear;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/58#v5)</sup> Which will not hearken to the voice of charmers, charming never so wisely.

- He challenges the congregation whether they speak righteousness and judge uprightly, when in heart they work wickedness and weigh the violence of their hands in the earth.
- The wicked are estranged from the womb and go astray as soon as they are born, speaking lies; their poison is like a serpent's, like the deaf adder that will not hearken to the charmer.

**[Psalms 58:6-9](/bible/psalms/58#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/58#v6)</sup> Break their teeth, O God, in their mouth: break out the great teeth of the young lions, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/58#v7)</sup> Let them melt away as waters which run continually: when he bendeth his bow to shoot his arrows, let them be as cut in pieces.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/58#v8)</sup> As a snail which melteth, let every one of them pass away: like the untimely birth of a woman, that they may not see the sun.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/58#v9)</sup> Before your pots can feel the thorns, he shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living, and in his wrath.

- He asks the Most High to break their teeth and the great teeth of the young lions, to let them melt away as waters that run continually and pass away like a snail that melts.
- Before the pots can feel the thorns, He shall take them away as with a whirlwind, both living and in His wrath.

**[Psalms 58:10-11](/bible/psalms/58#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/58#v10)</sup> The righteous shall rejoice when he seeth the vengeance: he shall wash his feet in the blood of the wicked.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/58#v11)</sup> So that a man shall say, Verily there is a reward for the righteous: verily he is a God that judgeth in the earth.

- The Most High is going to lay His vengeance upon Edom by the hand of His people Israel, His battle axe, and there will be so much death that everyone has to pitch in and bury them for seven months. That is a beautiful thing.
