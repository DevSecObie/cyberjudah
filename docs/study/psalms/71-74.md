Read the chapter → [Psalms 71](/bible/psalms/71)

This session moves through [Psalms 71](/bible/psalms/71) through 74, read four chapters a day for the nation. [Psalm 71](/bible/psalms/71) is David's prayer for deliverance from the wicked and cruel man, showing by his own example how to handle enemies who speak evil against him. The same trouble David faced runs down to today, where IUIC and Israelites in general are spoken evil of, and the answer is the same: serve the Most High, who is a strong habitation to those who listen to Him. The relationship is a give and give relationship, and David repeats these things over and over so we learn to lean on the Most High in old age and in trouble alike.

**[Psalms 71:1-6](/bible/psalms/71#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/71#v1)</sup> In thee, O Lord, do I put my trust: let me never be put to confusion.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/71#v2)</sup> Deliver me in thy righteousness, and cause me to escape: incline thine ear unto me, and save me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/71#v3)</sup> Be thou my strong habitation, whereunto I may continually resort: thou hast given commandment to save me; for thou art my rock and my fortress.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/71#v4)</sup> Deliver me, O my God, out of the hand of the wicked, out of the hand of the unrighteous and cruel man.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/71#v5)</sup> For thou art my hope, O Lord God: thou art my trust from my youth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/71#v6)</sup> By thee have I been holden up from the womb: thou art he that took me out of my mother’s bowels: my praise shall be continually of thee.

- David puts his trust in the Most High and asks never to be put to confusion, to be delivered in His righteousness and to have his cry heard.
- He asks the Most High to be his strong habitation, a place he can continually resort to, because the Lord is his rock and his fortress.
- He asks deliverance out of the hand of the wicked, the unrighteous and cruel man, calling the Most High his hope and his trust from his youth.
- The Most High has held him up from the womb and took him out of his mother's belly, so his praise is continually of Him.

**[Psalms 71:7-11](/bible/psalms/71#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/71#v7)</sup> I am as a wonder unto many; but thou art my strong refuge.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/71#v8)</sup> Let my mouth be filled with thy praise and with thy honour all the day.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/71#v9)</sup> Cast me not off in the time of old age; forsake me not when my strength faileth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/71#v10)</sup> For mine enemies speak against me; and they that lay wait for my soul take counsel together,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/71#v11)</sup> Saying, God hath forsaken him: persecute and take him; for there is none to deliver him.

- David is a wonder unto many, and the Most High is his strong refuge; he asks that his mouth be filled with praise and honor all the day.
- He pleads not to be cast off in the time of old age nor forsaken when his strength fails, because his enemies speak against him and take counsel together.
- The enemy says God has forsaken him, so persecute and take him, for there is none to deliver him. This is the same thing said against Israel today: persecuted by other nations and by our own people, in captivity for hundreds of years, as if no change is ever coming.
- David is not only recalling his own past, where he did things the Most High could have forsaken him for, but giving Israel understanding and direction on how to deal with enemies who speak evil, which is to go back to serving the Lord.

**[Psalms 71:12-16](/bible/psalms/71#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/71#v12)</sup> O God, be not far from me: O my God, make haste for my help.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/71#v13)</sup> Let them be confounded and consumed that are adversaries to my soul; let them be covered with reproach and dishonour that seek my hurt.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/71#v14)</sup> But I will hope continually, and will yet praise thee more and more.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/71#v15)</sup> My mouth shall shew forth thy righteousness and thy salvation all the day; for I know not the numbers thereof.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/71#v16)</sup> I will go in the strength of the Lord God: I will make mention of thy righteousness, even of thine only.

- David asks the Most High not to be far from him and to make haste for his help, that his adversaries be confounded, consumed, covered with reproach and dishonor.
- Regardless of the enemy, he will hope continually and praise the Most High more and more, showing forth His righteousness and salvation all the day.
- Because he does not know the number of his days or when his end will come, he purposes to stay obedient and follow the Most High until the day he dies.
- He will go in the strength of the Lord God and make mention of His righteousness, even of His only.

**[Psalms 71:17-19](/bible/psalms/71#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/71#v17)</sup> O God, thou hast taught me from my youth: and hitherto have I declared thy wondrous works.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/71#v18)</sup> Now also when I am old and grayheaded, O God, forsake me not; until I have shewed thy strength unto this generation, and thy power to every one that is to come.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/71#v19)</sup> Thy righteousness also, O God, is very high, who hast done great things: O God, who is like unto thee!

- The Most High taught David from his youth, and he has declared His wondrous works, asking that he not be forsaken now that he is old and grayheaded.
- He asks to be kept until he has showed the strength of the Most High to this generation and His power to everyone that is to come.
- This is the work of men and women alike: to show forth the Lord's strength and salvation to this generation and generations to come, through our actions, our works, and our words.
- His righteousness is very high; He has done great things, and there is none like unto Him.

**[Psalms 71:20-24](/bible/psalms/71#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/71#v20)</sup> Thou, which hast shewed me great and sore troubles, shalt quicken me again, and shalt bring me up again from the depths of the earth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/71#v21)</sup> Thou shalt increase my greatness, and comfort me on every side.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/71#v22)</sup> I will also praise thee with the psaltery, even thy truth, O my God: unto thee will I sing with the harp, O thou Holy One of Israel.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/71#v23)</sup> My lips shall greatly rejoice when I sing unto thee; and my soul, which thou hast redeemed.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/71#v24)</sup> My tongue also shall talk of thy righteousness all the day long: for they are confounded, for they are brought unto shame, that seek my hurt.

- The Most High has showed David great and sore troubles and chastised him, but will quicken him and bring him up again from the depths of the earth. To quicken is to make alive.
- He will increase David's greatness and comfort him on every side, and David will praise Him with the psaltery and sing to the Holy One of Israel.
- His lips and his redeemed soul will greatly rejoice, and his tongue will talk of the Most High's righteousness all the day long, for his enemies are brought to shame that sought his hurt.

# Psalm 72: All Nations Shall Serve Him

Read the chapter → [Psalms 72](/bible/psalms/72)

[Psalm 72](/bible/psalms/72) is a prayer that the king's son judge the people with righteousness and the poor with judgment. The poor here is not just anybody; it is the Israelites, the poor of His people. What David is hinting at through the whole psalm is the Kingdom of Heaven and Christ, who will break the oppressors in pieces, hold dominion from sea to sea, and have all nations serve Him and bring their gifts and resources to build up our cities. The psalm closes with the prayers of David the son of Jesse ended.

**[Psalms 72:1-4](/bible/psalms/72#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/72#v1)</sup> Give the king thy judgments, O God, and thy righteousness unto the king’s son.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/72#v2)</sup> He shall judge thy people with righteousness, and thy poor with judgment.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/72#v3)</sup> The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and the little hills, by righteousness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/72#v4)</sup> He shall judge the poor of the people, he shall save the children of the needy, and shall break in pieces the oppressor.

- David asks the Most High to give the king His judgments and the king's son His righteousness, that he judge the people with righteousness and the poor with judgment.
- The poor is the Israelites. The mountains shall bring peace to the people, and by righteousness he shall judge the poor and save the children of the needy.
- He shall break in pieces the oppressor, which is why this is pointing to things to come, the Kingdom of Heaven.
  - **[Isaiah 14:32](/bible/isaiah/14#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/isaiah/14#v32)</sup> What shall one then answer the messengers of the nation? That the Lord hath founded Zion, and the poor of his people shall trust in it.

    The poor of His people is the people of Zion, the Israelites, who shall trust in what the Most High has founded.

**[Psalms 72:5-8](/bible/psalms/72#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/72#v5)</sup> They shall fear thee as long as the sun and moon endure, throughout all generations.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/72#v6)</sup> He shall come down like rain upon the mown grass: as showers that water the earth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/72#v7)</sup> In his days shall the righteous flourish; and abundance of peace so long as the moon endureth.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/72#v8)</sup> He shall have dominion also from sea to sea, and from the river unto the ends of the earth.

- The poor of His people will fear the Most High and keep His commandments throughout all generations, as long as the sun and moon endure.
- The king's son comes down like rain upon the mown grass, and in his days the righteous flourish with abundance of peace as long as the moon endures.
- He shall have dominion from sea to sea and from the river unto the ends of the earth, meaning he will rule the entire planet.
  - **[Jeremiah 31:35-36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The Lord of hosts is his name:
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v36)</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the Lord, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.

    Israel will remain on this earth as long as the sun, moon, stars, and all the ordinances of heaven are still in place.

**[Psalms 72:9-11](/bible/psalms/72#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/72#v9)</sup> They that dwell in the wilderness shall bow before him; and his enemies shall lick the dust.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/72#v10)</sup> The kings of Tarshish and of the isles shall bring presents: the kings of Sheba and Seba shall offer gifts.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/72#v11)</sup> Yea, all kings shall fall down before him: all nations shall serve him.

- They that dwell in the wilderness shall bow before him and his enemies shall lick the dust.
- The kings of Tarshish, the isles, Sheba, and Seba shall bring presents and offer gifts.
- All kings shall fall down before him and all nations shall serve him. This is Christ: the nations bring gifts, they bring the Israelites, and they bring the resources of their land to build up our cities.

**[Psalms 72:12-17](/bible/psalms/72#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/72#v12)</sup> For he shall deliver the needy when he crieth; the poor also, and him that hath no helper.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/72#v13)</sup> He shall spare the poor and needy, and shall save the souls of the needy.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/72#v14)</sup> He shall redeem their soul from deceit and violence: and precious shall their blood be in his sight.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/72#v15)</sup> And he shall live, and to him shall be given of the gold of Sheba: prayer also shall be made for him continually; and daily shall he be praised.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/72#v16)</sup> There shall be an handful of corn in the earth upon the top of the mountains; the fruit thereof shall shake like Lebanon: and they of the city shall flourish like grass of the earth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/72#v17)</sup> His name shall endure for ever: his name shall be continued as long as the sun: and men shall be blessed in him: all nations shall call him blessed.

- He delivers the needy that cries, the poor also, and him that has no helper, redeeming their soul from deceit and violence, and their blood is precious in his sight.
- He shall live, be given of the gold of Sheba, prayer made for him continually, and daily he shall be praised.
- A handful of corn in the earth on the tops of the mountains shall shake like Lebanon, and the city shall flourish like grass of the earth.
- His name shall endure forever and be continued as long as the sun, and men shall be blessed in him and all nations shall call him blessed.

**[Psalms 72:18-20](/bible/psalms/72#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/72#v18)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God, the God of Israel, who only doeth wondrous things.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/72#v19)</sup> And blessed be his glorious name for ever: and let the whole earth be filled with his glory; Amen, and Amen.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/72#v20)</sup> The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.

- Blessed be the Lord God, the God of Israel, who only does wondrous things. It is hard to negate that He is a God of Israel; He gives the prophecy and specifies it is for the Israelites.
- Blessed be His glorious name forever, and let the whole earth be filled with His glory, amen and amen.
- The prayers of David the son of Jesse are ended.

# Psalm 73: God Is Good to Israel

Read the chapter → [Psalms 73](/bible/psalms/73)

[Psalm 73](/bible/psalms/73) opens by declaring the Most High is good to Israel, even to such as are of a clean heart, meaning those who have repented or are repenting from their sins. The psalmist confesses his feet almost slipped because he was envious of the prosperity of the wicked, who live in wickedness yet get the houses, the jobs, and the money while Israel goes through trials. Only when he went into the sanctuary did he understand that the wicked are set in slippery places, and their end is destruction. The turn comes back to the truth that God is the strength of his heart and his portion forever.

**[Psalms 73:1-3](/bible/psalms/73#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/73#v1)</sup> Truly God is good to Israel, even to such as are of a clean heart.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/73#v2)</sup> But as for me, my feet were almost gone; my steps had well nigh slipped.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/73#v3)</sup> For I was envious at the foolish, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

- The Most High is good to Israel, specifically to such as are of a clean heart, those repenting and who know the Israelites.
- As for the psalmist, his feet were almost gone and his steps had well nigh slipped, meaning he was almost in sin.
- He was envious of the foolish when he saw the prosperity of the wicked, our own people and other nations doing wickedness yet prospering while Israel goes through all types of trials and tribulations.

**[Psalms 73:4-9](/bible/psalms/73#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/73#v4)</sup> For there are no bands in their death: but their strength is firm.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/73#v5)</sup> They are not in trouble as other men; neither are they plagued like other men.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/73#v6)</sup> Therefore pride compasseth them about as a chain; violence covereth them as a garment.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/73#v7)</sup> Their eyes stand out with fatness: they have more than heart could wish.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/73#v8)</sup> They are corrupt, and speak wickedly concerning oppression: they speak loftily.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/73#v9)</sup> They set their mouth against the heavens, and their tongue walketh through the earth.

- There are no bands in their death and their strength is firm; they are not in trouble or plagued like other men.
- Pride compasses them about as a chain and violence covers them as a garment; they wear their pride because they think no hurt can come to them. The entertainers, rappers, and people in Congress live luxurious lives and no harm comes to them.
- Their eyes stand out with fatness and they have more than the heart could wish; they have abundance, more than they need.
- They are corrupt and speak wickedly concerning oppression, speaking loftily and proudly. Our people do not believe they are oppressed; some say there is no racism and the world is equal.
- They set their mouth against the heavens and their tongue walks through the earth.
  - **[Proverbs 30:8-9](/bible/proverbs/30#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/30#v8)</sup> Remove far from me vanity and lies: give me neither poverty nor riches; feed me with food convenient for me:
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/30#v9)</sup> Lest I be full, and deny thee, and say, Who is the Lord? or lest I be poor, and steal, and take the name of my God in vain.

    Two types of people: those that are full deny the Most High and say who is the Lord, and those with too little steal and take His name in vain.

**[Psalms 73:10-14](/bible/psalms/73#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/73#v10)</sup> Therefore his people return hither: and waters of a full cup are wrung out to them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/73#v11)</sup> And they say, How doth God know? and is there knowledge in the most High?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/73#v12)</sup> Behold, these are the ungodly, who prosper in the world; they increase in riches.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/73#v13)</sup> Verily I have cleansed my heart in vain, and washed my hands in innocency.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/73#v14)</sup> For all the day long have I been plagued, and chastened every morning.

- His people return there and waters of a full cup are wrung out to them, and they say how does God know, and is there knowledge in the Most High.
- These are the ungodly who prosper in the world and increase in riches; he says plainly what he has been building toward.
- The Most High gives you exactly what you need. Whether it is thirty thousand or seventy thousand a year, that is what you need, and what you hold, whether money, possessions, or a name, must be used righteously.
- The psalmist cleansed his heart in vain and washed his hands in innocency, yet all day long he has been plagued and chastened every morning. That is Israel: chastened every morning while the unrighteous prosper in all things.

**[Psalms 73:15-20](/bible/psalms/73#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/73#v15)</sup> If I say, I will speak thus; behold, I should offend against the generation of thy children.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/73#v16)</sup> When I thought to know this, it was too painful for me;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/73#v17)</sup> Until I went into the sanctuary of God; then understood I their end.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/73#v18)</sup> Surely thou didst set them in slippery places: thou castedst them down into destruction.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/73#v19)</sup> How are they brought into desolation, as in a moment! they are utterly consumed with terrors.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/73#v20)</sup> As a dream when one awaketh; so, O Lord, when thou awakest, thou shalt despise their image.

- If he spoke this way openly he would offend against the generation of the Most High's children; when he thought to know this it was too painful.
- Only when he went into the sanctuary of God did he understand the end of the wicked: destruction, no matter how luxurious or plentiful their life.
- Surely the Most High sets them in slippery places and casts them down into destruction; they are brought into desolation in a moment, utterly consumed with terrors.
- As a dream when one awakes, so when the Lord awakes He shall despise their image.

**[Psalms 73:21-24](/bible/psalms/73#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/73#v21)</sup> Thus my heart was grieved, and I was pricked in my reins.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/73#v22)</sup> So foolish was I, and ignorant: I was as a beast before thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/73#v23)</sup> Nevertheless I am continually with thee: thou hast holden me by my right hand.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/73#v24)</sup> Thou shalt guide me with thy counsel, and afterward receive me to glory.

- His heart was grieved and he was pricked in his reins; he was foolish and ignorant, as a beast before the Most High.
- Nevertheless he is continually with the Most High, who holds him by his right hand. He snapped back to his senses and realized the wicked are set for destruction.
- Those afflicted are being prepared, for in Acts we read that we must go through much afflictions to enter the kingdom, whether financial, spiritual, physical, or mental.
- The Most High shall guide him with His counsel and afterward receive him to glory.
  - **[Acts 14:22](/bible/acts/14#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/acts/14#v22)</sup> Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

    We must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

**[Psalms 73:25-28](/bible/psalms/73#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/73#v25)</sup> Whom have I in heaven but thee? and there is none upon earth that I desire beside thee.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/73#v26)</sup> My flesh and my heart faileth: but God is the strength of my heart, and my portion for ever.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/73#v27)</sup> For, lo, they that are far from thee shall perish: thou hast destroyed all them that go a whoring from thee.

> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/73#v28)</sup> But it is good for me to draw near to God: I have put my trust in the Lord God, that I may declare all thy works.

- They that are far from the Most High shall perish, those who do not keep the commandments; He has destroyed all who go whoring from Him, chasing lust, finances, women, drugs, alcohol, whatever the lust is.
- It is good to draw near to God; he puts his trust in the Lord God that he may declare all His works.
  - **[2 Corinthians 12:7-9](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v7)</sup> And lest I should be exalted above measure through the abundance of the revelations, there was given to me a thorn in the flesh, the messenger of Satan to buffet me, lest I should be exalted above measure.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v8)</sup> For this thing I besought the Lord thrice, that it might depart from me.

    Paul asked three times for his infirmity removed, but the Most High said His grace was sufficient and His strength made perfect in weakness.

# Psalm 74: Have Respect Unto the Covenant

Read the chapter → [Psalms 74](/bible/psalms/74)

[Psalm 74](/bible/psalms/74) cries out asking why the Most High has cast off the sheep of His pasture, the congregation He purchased of old and redeemed. The enemy has broken down the sanctuary, cast fire into it, and set up their own signs for signs, replacing the truth with their images, their histories, and their ideologies, just as in slavery when our people were given a white image of God and Christ with no one left to teach who we were. The psalm pleads with the Most High to remember the covenant, to plead His own cause, and not to forget the congregation of the poor forever, tying back to the promise that Israel will be saved with an everlasting salvation.

**[Psalms 74:1-3](/bible/psalms/74#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/74#v1)</sup> O God, why hast thou cast us off for ever? why doth thine anger smoke against the sheep of thy pasture?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/74#v2)</sup> Remember thy congregation, which thou hast purchased of old; the rod of thine inheritance, which thou hast redeemed; this mount Zion, wherein thou hast dwelt.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/74#v3)</sup> Lift up thy feet unto the perpetual desolations; even all that the enemy hath done wickedly in the sanctuary.

- The psalmist asks why the Most High has cast us off forever and why His anger smokes against the sheep of His pasture, which is Israel.
- He asks the Most High to remember the congregation He purchased of old, the rod of His inheritance He redeemed, this Mount Zion where He dwelt.
- The Most High purchased us when He instituted animal sacrifice, when Moses sprinkled the blood over the people.
  - **[Exodus 24:6-8](/bible/exodus/24#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/24#v6)</sup> And Moses took half of the blood, and put it in basons; and half of the blood he sprinkled on the altar.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/exodus/24#v7)</sup> And he took the book of the covenant, and read in the audience of the people: and they said, All that the Lord hath said will we do, and be obedient.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/24#v8)</sup> And Moses took the blood, and sprinkled it on the people, and said, Behold the blood of the covenant, which the Lord hath made with you concerning all these words.

    Moses sprinkled the blood of the covenant on the people, sealing them as the purchased congregation.

**[Psalms 74:4-8](/bible/psalms/74#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/74#v4)</sup> Thine enemies roar in the midst of thy congregations; they set up their ensigns for signs.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/74#v5)</sup> A man was famous according as he had lifted up axes upon the thick trees.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/74#v6)</sup> But now they break down the carved work thereof at once with axes and hammers.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/74#v7)</sup> They have cast fire into thy sanctuary, they have defiled by casting down the dwelling place of thy name to the ground.

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/74#v8)</sup> They said in their hearts, Let us destroy them together: they have burned up all the synagogues of God in the land.

- The ensigns set up for signs are the other nations setting up their images, histories, and laws for us to follow. In school you get American history, French history, all the histories of their people, never ours.

**[Psalms 74:9-11](/bible/psalms/74#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/74#v9)</sup> We see not our signs: there is no more any prophet: neither is there among us any that knoweth how long.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/74#v10)</sup> O God, how long shall the adversary reproach? shall the enemy blaspheme thy name for ever?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/74#v11)</sup> Why withdrawest thou thy hand, even thy right hand? pluck it out of thy bosom.

- There is no more any sign, no prophet, and none who knows how long. In slavery there was a time when our image of Christ was replaced with a white image and no one could teach us who we were or how long, because the Most High cast us off.
- Even today the other nations fight to keep this alive: the Christian church ideologies, their politics, their government, everything they set up.
- He asks how long the adversary shall reproach and whether the enemy shall blaspheme the Most High's name forever, and why He withdraws His hand.
- He asks the Most High to pluck His right hand out of His bosom, meaning show forth His righteousness and strength against the other nations.

**[Psalms 74:18-23](/bible/psalms/74#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/74#v18)</sup> Remember this, that the enemy hath reproached, O Lord, and that the foolish people have blasphemed thy name.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/74#v19)</sup> O deliver not the soul of thy turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked: forget not the congregation of thy poor for ever.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/74#v20)</sup> Have respect unto the covenant: for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/74#v21)</sup> O let not the oppressed return ashamed: let the poor and needy praise thy name.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/74#v22)</sup> Arise, O God, plead thine own cause: remember how the foolish man reproacheth thee daily.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/74#v23)</sup> Forget not the voice of thine enemies: the tumult of those that rise up against thee increaseth continually.

- Remember that the enemy has reproached and the foolish people have blasphemed His name; do not deliver the soul of His turtledove unto the multitude of the wicked, and forget not the congregation of the poor forever.
- The congregation of the poor is the Israelites; have respect unto the covenant, for the dark places of the earth are full of the habitations of cruelty. The covenant is that His people would inherit the Kingdom of Heaven, established from Adam and Shem onward.
- Let not the oppressed return ashamed; let the poor and needy praise His name. Our people will not return to the Lord ashamed; the prophecies show Israel will be redeemed with a mighty salvation.
- Arise, O God, plead thine own cause, and remember how the foolish man reproaches daily; the enemy's tumult increases continually, thinking evil upon their beds every day on how to oppress and crush Israel.
- We must keep in mind it is because of our own doing; He put us in captivity for our disobedience. As long as we come with a clean heart and fix what we must fix, there is no issue for those repenting.
