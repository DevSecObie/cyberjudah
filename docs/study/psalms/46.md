Read the chapter → [Psalms 46](/bible/psalms/46)

[Psalm 46](/bible/psalms/46) declares the Most High a refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble, unshaken though the earth be removed. The teaching centers on the promise that He makes wars to cease to the ends of the earth, tying it to Christ's prophecy of wars and rumors of wars as the beginning of sorrows, and to the last-days beating of swords into plowshares.

**[Psalms 46:1-3](/bible/psalms/46#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/46#v1)</sup> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/46#v2)</sup> Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/46#v3)</sup> Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Selah.

- The Most High is a refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble, so we will not fear though the earth be removed and the mountains carried into the sea.
- Though the waters roar and be troubled and the mountains shake, the confidence stands.

**[Psalms 46:4-6](/bible/psalms/46#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/46#v4)</sup> There is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God, the holy place of the tabernacles of the most High.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/46#v5)</sup> God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved: God shall help her, and that right early.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/46#v6)</sup> The heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved: he uttered his voice, the earth melted.

- There is a river whose streams make glad the city of God, the tabernacles of the Most High, and God is in the midst of her; He helps her right early.
- The heathen raged and the kingdoms were moved; He uttered His voice and the earth melted.

**[Psalms 46:7-9](/bible/psalms/46#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/46#v7)</sup> The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/46#v8)</sup> Come, behold the works of the Lord, what desolations he hath made in the earth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/46#v9)</sup> He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the earth; he breaketh the bow, and cutteth the spear in sunder; he burneth the chariot in the fire.

- The Lord of hosts is with us and the God of Jacob is our refuge; behold the desolations He has made in the earth.
- He makes wars to cease unto the end of the earth, breaks the bow, cuts the spear, and burns the chariot in the fire.
- Right now the Most High has not stopped the wars, so this is a prophecy not yet come. We are in the midst of wars and rumors of wars, the beginning of sorrows.
  - **[Matthew 24:4-8](/bible/matthew/24#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/24#v4)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Take heed that no man deceive you.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/24#v5)</sup> For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/24#v6)</sup> And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/matthew/24#v7)</sup> For nation shall rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom: and there shall be famines, and pestilences, and earthquakes, in divers places.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/24#v8)</sup> All these are the beginning of sorrows.

    Take heed no man deceive you; wars and rumors of wars, nation against nation, famines and pestilences are the beginning of sorrows.
  - **[Micah 4:1-3](/bible/micah/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/micah/4#v1)</sup> But in the last days it shall come to pass, that the mountain of the house of the Lord shall be established in the top of the mountains, and it shall be exalted above the hills; and people shall flow unto it.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/micah/4#v2)</sup> And many nations shall come, and say, Come, and let us go up to the mountain of the Lord, and to the house of the God of Jacob; and he will teach us of his ways, and we will walk in his paths: for the law shall go forth of Zion, and the word of the Lord from Jerusalem.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/micah/4#v3)</sup> And he shall judge among many people, and rebuke strong nations afar off; and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up a sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.

    In the last days He shall rebuke strong nations afar off and they shall beat their swords into plowshares and learn war no more.
  - **[Isaiah 2:4](/bible/isaiah/2#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/2#v4)</sup> And he shall judge among the nations, and shall rebuke many people: and they shall beat their swords into plowshares, and their spears into pruninghooks: nation shall not lift up sword against nation, neither shall they learn war any more.

    He shall judge among the nations and they shall beat their swords into plowshares; nation shall not lift up sword against nation.

**[Psalms 46:10-11](/bible/psalms/46#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/46#v10)</sup> Be still, and know that I am God: I will be exalted among the heathen, I will be exalted in the earth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/46#v11)</sup> The Lord of hosts is with us; the God of Jacob is our refuge. Selah.

- Be still and know that He is God; He will be exalted among the heathen and exalted in the earth.
- The Lord of hosts is with us and the God of Jacob is our refuge. When Christ comes back He lays war down; everyone else goes into bondage and we rule.

    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/36#v15)</sup> Give testimony unto those that thou hast possessed from the beginning, and raise up prophets that have been in thy name.
    >

    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/36#v17)</sup> O Lord, hear the prayer of thy servants, according to the blessing of Aaron over thy people, that all they which dwell upon the earth may know that thou art the Lord, the eternal God.

    Send thy fear upon the nations, destroy the adversary, and let them perish that oppress the people, that all may know there is no God but only thou.
