Read the chapter → [Psalms 119](/bible/psalms/119)

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session covers [Psalms 119](/bible/psalms/119) through 122. [Psalm 119](/bible/psalms/119) is the longest chapter in the Bible, a marathon of one hundred seventy six verses arranged as an acrostic that magnifies the law, the testimonies, the statutes, the precepts, the commandments, and the judgments of the Most High. The teaching drives home again and again that a precept is a law, a rule, a commandment, and that our whole duty upon this earth is to keep the commandments of the Most High until we die. [Psalms 120](/bible/psalms/120) through 122 turn to the pilgrim songs of ascent, dwelling with them that hate peace in Babylon, the help that comes from the Most High, and the call to pray for the peace of Jerusalem.

**[Psalm 119:1-8](/bible/psalms/119#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/119#v1)</sup> Blessed are the undefiled in the way, who walk in the law of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/119#v2)</sup> Blessed are they that keep his testimonies, and that seek him with the whole heart.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/119#v3)</sup> They also do no iniquity: they walk in his ways.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/119#v4)</sup> Thou hast commanded us to keep thy precepts diligently.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/119#v5)</sup> O that my ways were directed to keep thy statutes!
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/119#v6)</sup> Then shall I not be ashamed, when I have respect unto all thy commandments.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/119#v7)</sup> I will praise thee with uprightness of heart, when I shall have learned thy righteous judgments.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/119#v8)</sup> I will keep thy statutes: O forsake me not utterly.

- Blessed are the undefiled in the way who walk in the law of the Most High. We have to walk in the law, because without it we are defiled. Blessed are they that keep His testimonies and seek Him with the whole heart, the whole mind.
- Thou hast commanded us to keep Thy precepts diligently. When you look at the word precept it means law, it means commandment, it means rule. That is what a precept is, so precept upon precept is talking about the word of the Most High, the scriptures, the law.
- Once we depart from the precepts we are in the midst of sin. The prayer here is that our ways were directed to keep His statutes, so that we shall not be ashamed when we have respect unto all His commandments.
  - **[Daniel 9:5](/bible/daniel/9#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/daniel/9#v5)</sup> We have sinned, and have committed iniquity, and have done wickedly, and have rebelled, even by departing from thy precepts and from thy judgments:

    Departing from the precepts and the judgments of the Most High is sin, as Israel confessed they had done wickedly and rebelled by departing from His precepts.

- Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed thereto according to Thy word. The word of the Most High cleanses our brains, our mind, cleaning the filth that is within our heart, which is actually the mind.
- With my whole heart have I sought Thee, so let me not wander from Thy commandments. Thy word have I hid in my heart, that I might not sin against Thee. The word hidden in the heart is what keeps a man from sinning.
- I will meditate in Thy precepts. We have to meditate on the word of the Most High day and night, the same thing said in [Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8), delighting in His statutes and not forgetting His word.

    This says the same thing as Psalm 119, that the Most High guides them who keep His commandments and precepts, and that we must not let our sins weigh us down.
