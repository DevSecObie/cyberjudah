**[Psalms 117:1-2](/bible/psalms/117#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/117#v1)</sup> O praise the Lord, all ye nations: praise him, all ye people.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/117#v2)</sup> For his merciful kindness is great toward us: and the truth of the Lord endureth for ever. Praise ye the Lord.

- O praise the Lord, all ye nations; praise Him, all ye people. His merciful kindness is great toward us and the truth of the Lord endures forever.
- These are the only two verses in this psalm, a short call to praise Him for His mercy and His enduring truth.
