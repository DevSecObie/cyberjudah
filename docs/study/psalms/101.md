Read the chapter → [Psalms 101](/bible/psalms/101)

[Psalm 101](/bible/psalms/101) is David setting a standard for his own house and his own eyes: he will sing of mercy and judgment, behave wisely in a perfect way, and set no wicked thing before his eyes. The teaching lands on the closing verse: the Most High does not like the sin nor the sinner, the wickedness nor the wicked. If you are doing wicked, He will cut you off from the city of the Most High.

**[Psalms 101:1-3](/bible/psalms/101#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/101#v1)</sup> I will sing of mercy and judgment: unto thee, O Lord, will I sing.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/101#v2)</sup> I will behave myself wisely in a perfect way. O when wilt thou come unto me? I will walk within my house with a perfect heart.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/101#v3)</sup> I will set no wicked thing before mine eyes: I hate the work of them that turn aside; it shall not cleave to me.

- David will sing of mercy and judgment unto the Most High and behave himself wisely in a perfect way, asking when He will come to him.
- He will walk within his house with a perfect heart and set no wicked thing before his eyes; he hates the work of them that turn aside.

**[Psalms 101:4-5](/bible/psalms/101#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/101#v4)</sup> A froward heart shall depart from me: I will not know a wicked person.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/101#v5)</sup> Whoso privily slandereth his neighbour, him will I cut off: him that hath an high look and a proud heart will not I suffer.

- A froward heart will depart from him; he will not know a wicked person.
- Whoever privily slanders his neighbor, him will he cut off; the high look and proud heart he will not suffer.

**[Psalms 101:6-8](/bible/psalms/101#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/101#v6)</sup> Mine eyes shall be upon the faithful of the land, that they may dwell with me: he that walketh in a perfect way, he shall serve me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/101#v7)</sup> He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/101#v8)</sup> I will early destroy all the wicked of the land; that I may cut off all wicked doers from the city of the Lord.

- His eyes will be upon the faithful of the land that they may dwell with him; he that walks in a perfect way shall serve him.
- He that works deceit and he that tells lies shall not dwell in his house nor tarry in his sight.
- He will early destroy all the wicked of the land to cut off all wicked doers from the city of the Most High. This shows He does not like the sin nor the sinner, the wickedness nor the wicked. If you are doing wicked, He will cut you off.
