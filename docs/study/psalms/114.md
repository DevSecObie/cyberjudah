**[Psalms 114:1](/bible/psalms/114#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/114#v1)</sup> When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language;

- When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language.

**[Psalms 114:2](/bible/psalms/114#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/114#v2)</sup> Judah was his sanctuary, and Israel his dominion.

- Judah was his sanctuary, and Israel his dominion.

**[Psalms 114:3](/bible/psalms/114#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/114#v3)</sup> The sea saw it, and fled: Jordan was driven back.

- The sea saw it, and fled; Jordan was driven back.

**[Psalms 114:4](/bible/psalms/114#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/114#v4)</sup> The mountains skipped like rams, and the little hills like lambs.

- The mountains skipped like rams, and the little hills like lambs.

**[Psalms 114:5](/bible/psalms/114#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/114#v5)</sup> What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou wast driven back?

- What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou wast driven back?

**[Psalms 114:6](/bible/psalms/114#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/114#v6)</sup> Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams; and ye little hills, like lambs?

- Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams; and ye little hills, like lambs?

**[Psalms 114:7](/bible/psalms/114#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/114#v7)</sup> Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob;

- Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Most High, at the presence of the God of Jacob.

**[Psalms 114:8](/bible/psalms/114#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/114#v8)</sup> Which turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters.

- Which turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters.
- This is a very brief and quick reading, but overall we see it talking about those that are blessed, those being blessed that keep the commandments. It says who is like the Most High, and then it gives praise to God who delivered Israel. It is still good to read; it is a good refresher, especially [Psalms 111](/bible/psalms/111) and 112, and there is always a takeaway from the readings that we do.
