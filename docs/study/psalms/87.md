Read the chapter → [Psalms 87](/bible/psalms/87)

This session reads through [Psalms 87](/bible/psalms/87) through 90, four chapters a day for the nation. The teaching moves through the psalms mostly by reading, bringing out precepts along the way. [Psalm 87](/bible/psalms/87) speaks of the Most High hallowing Jerusalem, the city of God, whose foundation is in the holy mountains and whose gates the Lord loveth more than all the dwellings of Jacob.

**[Psalms 87:1-4](/bible/psalms/87#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/87#v1)</sup> His foundation is in the holy mountains.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/87#v2)</sup> The Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/87#v3)</sup> Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. Selah.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/87#v4)</sup> I will make mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know me: behold Philistia, and Tyre, with Ethiopia; this man was born there.

- The foundation of the city is in the holy mountains, and the Lord loveth the gates of Zion more than all the dwellings of Jacob. This is about how the Most High hallowed Him a city, Jerusalem.
- Glorious things are spoken of thee, O city of God. The Most High makes mention of Rahab and Babylon to them that know Him, and of Philistia and Tyre with Ethiopia, saying this man was born there.

**[Psalms 87:5-7](/bible/psalms/87#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/87#v5)</sup> And of Zion it shall be said, This and that man was born in her: and the highest himself shall establish her.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/87#v6)</sup> The Lord shall count, when he writeth up the people, that this man was born there. Selah.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/87#v7)</sup> As well the singers as the players on instruments shall be there: all my springs are in thee.

- Of Zion it shall be said that this and that man was born in her, and the Most High Himself shall establish her. The Lord shall count, when He writeth up the people, that this man was born there.
- As well the singers as the players of instruments shall be there; all the springs are in the Most High.
