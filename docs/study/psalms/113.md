**[Psalms 113:1](/bible/psalms/113#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/113#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise, O ye servants of the Lord, praise the name of the Lord.

- Praise you the Most High. Praise, O ye servants of the Most High, praise the name of the Most High.

**[Psalms 113:2](/bible/psalms/113#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/113#v2)</sup> Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and for evermore.

- Blessed be the name of the Most High from this time forth and forevermore.

**[Psalms 113:3](/bible/psalms/113#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/113#v3)</sup> From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same the Lord’s name is to be praised.

- From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same, the name of the Most High is to be praised.

**[Psalms 113:4](/bible/psalms/113#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/113#v4)</sup> The Lord is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens.

- The Most High is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens. He is letting you know that he is the only God, high above all nations.

**[Psalms 113:5](/bible/psalms/113#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/113#v5)</sup> Who is like unto the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high,

- Who is like unto the Most High our God, who dwelleth on high?

**[Psalms 113:6](/bible/psalms/113#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/113#v6)</sup> Who humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven, and in the earth!

- He humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven and in the earth.

**[Psalms 113:7](/bible/psalms/113#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/113#v7)</sup> He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill;

- He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill. That is what is happening right now; our people are being lifted up.

**[Psalms 113:8](/bible/psalms/113#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/113#v8)</sup> That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people.

- That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people, which is Israel.

**[Psalms 113:9](/bible/psalms/113#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/113#v9)</sup> He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise ye the Lord.

- He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise you the Most High.
- At the bottom he lets you know he can bring our people up from a lower state. He showed the princes and then he showed the women, saying, I can bring the princes up and I can bring the women up.
