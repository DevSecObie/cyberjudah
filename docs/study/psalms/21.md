**[Psalm 21:1-7](/bible/psalms/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/21#v1)</sup> The king shall joy in thy strength, O Lord; and in thy salvation how greatly shall he rejoice!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/21#v2)</sup> Thou hast given him his heart’s desire, and hast not withholden the request of his lips. Selah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/21#v3)</sup> For thou preventest him with the blessings of goodness: thou settest a crown of pure gold on his head.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/21#v4)</sup> He asked life of thee, and thou gavest it him, even length of days for ever and ever.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/21#v5)</sup> His glory is great in thy salvation: honour and majesty hast thou laid upon him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/21#v6)</sup> For thou hast made him most blessed for ever: thou hast made him exceeding glad with thy countenance.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/21#v7)</sup> For the king trusteth in the Lord, and through the mercy of the most High he shall not be moved.

- The king shall joy in thy strength, O Lord; thou hast given him his heart's desire, set a crown of pure gold on his head, and made him most blessed for ever; for the king trusteth in the Lord and through the mercy of the Most High he shall not be moved. This goes into Christ; he is our King, and we get the victory with Christ.

**[Psalm 21:9](/bible/psalms/21#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/21#v9)</sup> Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven in the time of thine anger: the Lord shall swallow them up in his wrath, and the fire shall devour them.

- Thou shalt make them as a fiery oven in the time of thine anger; the Lord shall swallow them up in his wrath and the fire shall devour them.
  - **[Malachi 4:1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/malachi/4#v1)</sup> For, behold, the day cometh, that shall burn as an oven; and all the proud, yea, and all that do wickedly, shall be stubble: and the day that cometh shall burn them up, saith the Lord of hosts, that it shall leave them neither root nor branch.

    For behold the day cometh that shall burn as an oven, and all the proud and all that do wickedly shall be stubble; this is the day of the Lord.

**[Psalm 21:10-12](/bible/psalms/21#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/21#v10)</sup> Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth, and their seed from among the children of men.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/21#v11)</sup> For they intended evil against thee: they imagined a mischievous device, which they are not able to perform.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/21#v12)</sup> Therefore shalt thou make them turn their back, when thou shalt make ready thine arrows upon thy strings against the face of them.

- Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth and their seed from among the children of men; Esau is not going to be no more. They imagined a mischievous device which they are not able to perform; the Most High makes their devices of none effect and makes ready his arrows against the face of them.
  - **[2 Esdras 16:3-6](/bible/2-esdras/16#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-esdras/16#v3)</sup> A sword is sent upon you, and who may turn it back?
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-esdras/16#v4)</sup> A fire is sent among you, and who may quench it?
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/16#v5)</sup> Plagues are sent unto you, and what is he that may drive them away?
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-esdras/16#v6)</sup> May any man drive away an hungry lion in the wood? or may any one quench the fire in stubble, when it hath begun to burn?

    The Lord sends his sword, fire, and plagues, and none can turn them back.
  - **[Zechariah 14:12](/bible/zechariah/14#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/14#v12)</sup> And this shall be the plague wherewith the Lord will smite all the people that have fought against Jerusalem; Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth.

    This shall be the plague: their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, their eyes in their holes and their tongues in their mouth; that is a nuclear bomb. We saw it at Hiroshima and Nagasaki, and the weapons have only got more advanced; the Most High is manipulating that weapon so the earth turns into a microwave.
