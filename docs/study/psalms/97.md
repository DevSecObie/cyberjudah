**[Psalm 97:1-2](/bible/psalms/97#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/97#v1)</sup> The Lord reigneth; let the earth rejoice; let the multitude of isles be glad thereof.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/97#v2)</sup> Clouds and darkness are round about him: righteousness and judgment are the habitation of his throne.

- The Lord reigneth, let the earth rejoice, let the multitude of isles be glad thereof.
- Clouds and darkness are round about Him; righteousness and judgment are the habitation of His throne.

**[Psalm 97:3](/bible/psalms/97#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/97#v3)</sup> A fire goeth before him, and burneth up his enemies round about.

- A fire goeth before Him and burneth up His enemies round about. That is Christ coming back to destroy the nations.
  - **[Habakkuk 3:4-6](/bible/habakkuk/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/habakkuk/3#v4)</sup> And his brightness was as the light; he had horns coming out of his hand: and there was the hiding of his power.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/habakkuk/3#v5)</sup> Before him went the pestilence, and burning coals went forth at his feet.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/habakkuk/3#v6)</sup> He stood, and measured the earth: he beheld, and drove asunder the nations; and the everlasting mountains were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: his ways are everlasting.

    His brightness was as the light, He had horns coming out of His hand where His power was hidden, pestilence and burning coals went before Him, and He beheld and drove asunder the nations; His ways are everlasting.

**[Psalm 97:4-6](/bible/psalms/97#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/97#v4)</sup> His lightnings enlightened the world: the earth saw, and trembled.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/97#v5)</sup> The hills melted like wax at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/97#v6)</sup> The heavens declare his righteousness, and all the people see his glory.

- His lightnings enlightened the world; the earth saw and trembled. The hills melted like wax at the presence of the Lord of the whole earth.
- The heavens declare His righteousness and all the people see His glory. How do all the people see His glory? Israel, the so-called blacks, Hispanics, and native Indians, are His glory.
  - **[Psalm 19:1-4](/bible/psalms/19#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/19#v1)</sup> The heavens declare the glory of God; and the firmament sheweth his handywork.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/19#v2)</sup> Day unto day uttereth speech, and night unto night sheweth knowledge.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/19#v3)</sup> There is no speech nor language, where their voice is not heard.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/19#v4)</sup> Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,

    The heavens declare the glory of God; their line is gone out through all the earth, and that line is the gospel, the laws of God going out through the brothers teaching to the four corners of the earth.
  - **[Matthew 24:14](/bible/matthew/24#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/24#v14)</sup> And this gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness unto all nations; and then shall the end come.

    This gospel of the kingdom shall be preached in all the world for a witness, and then the end comes; once the last person is sealed with God's laws, Christ comes back.
- You see Israel coming back to their nationality and seeing His glory. The men are pure gold, the leaders of the earth, the kings and priests of the earth.

**[Psalm 97:7-8](/bible/psalms/97#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/97#v7)</sup> Confounded be all they that serve graven images, that boast themselves of idols: worship him, all ye gods.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/97#v8)</sup> Zion heard, and was glad; and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of thy judgments, O Lord.

- Confounded be all they that serve graven images and boast themselves of idols; worship Him all ye gods.
- Zion heard and was glad, and the daughters of Judah rejoiced because of His judgments. The Israelites, Zion, are glad because they know salvation is for them and they understand why we lost our identity and why we are catching hell upon the earth.

**[Psalm 97:9](/bible/psalms/97#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/97#v9)</sup> For thou, Lord, art high above all the earth: thou art exalted far above all gods.

- For the Lord is high above all the earth; He is exalted far above all gods.

**[Psalm 97:10](/bible/psalms/97#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/97#v10)</sup> Ye that love the Lord, hate evil: he preserveth the souls of his saints; he delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked.

- Ye that love the Lord hate evil. How do we love the Lord? We cannot hug Him, so we love Him by keeping His commandments. If you love God's commandments you hate sin, and if you keep His commandments you abstain from breaking His laws.
- He preserveth the souls of His saints and delivereth them out of the hand of the wicked.
  - **[1 John 5:3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)</sup> For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.

    This is the love of God, that we keep His commandments, and His commandments are not grievous; that is how we love God.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 33:14-15](/bible/sirach/33#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/33#v14)</sup> Good is set against evil, and life against death: so is the godly against the sinner, and the sinner against the godly.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/33#v15)</sup> So look upon all the works of the most High; and there are two and two, one against another.

    Good is set against evil and the godly against the sinner; God's laws are contrary to sin.
  - **[Proverbs 19:16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/19#v16)</sup> He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; but he that despiseth his ways shall die.

    He that keepeth the commandment keepeth his own soul; that is how you preserve your soul and are delivered out of the hands of the wicked. He that despiseth His ways shall die, meaning you love evil and love sin and you will die.

**[Psalm 97:11-12](/bible/psalms/97#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/97#v11)</sup> Light is sown for the righteous, and gladness for the upright in heart.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/97#v12)</sup> Rejoice in the Lord, ye righteous; and give thanks at the remembrance of his holiness.

- Light is sown for the righteous and gladness for the upright in heart. What is the light? The light is the commandments, and it gets sown in your mind by keeping it.
- We will be glad because we love the Lord with all our heart and soul and love our neighbor as ourself, bringing forth fruits of repentance like God wants us to be.
- Rejoice in the Lord ye righteous, and give thanks at the remembrance of His holiness. He did not forget us; He chastened us for a little but He did not forget, and He is going to remember the covenant.
  - **[Proverbs 6:23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/6#v23)</sup> For the commandment is a lamp; and the law is light; and reproofs of instruction are the way of life:

    The commandment is a lamp and the law is light; the commandments are the light.
  - **[2 Esdras 9:30-31](/bible/2-esdras/9#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/2-esdras/9#v30)</sup> And thou spakest saying, Hear me, O Israel; and mark my words, thou seed of Jacob.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/2-esdras/9#v31)</sup> For, behold, I sow my law in you, and it shall bring fruit in you, and ye shall be honoured in it for ever.

    Hear O Israel, I sow My law in you and it shall bring forth fruit; the law has to be sown in our minds, which is why we are told to study, pray, and apply.
