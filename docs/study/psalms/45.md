Read the chapter → [Psalms 45](/bible/psalms/45)

[Psalm 45](/bible/psalms/45) is a king's psalm, fairer than the children of men, girding his sword and riding prosperously because of truth and meekness and righteousness. The teaching applies the throne to Christ, the everlasting scepter of righteousness held by the ruler from Judah, and the daughter who must forget her own people and father's house to please her lord.

**[Psalms 45:1-5](/bible/psalms/45#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/45#v1)</sup> My heart is inditing a good matter: I speak of the things which I have made touching the king: my tongue is the pen of a ready writer.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/45#v2)</sup> Thou art fairer than the children of men: grace is poured into thy lips: therefore God hath blessed thee for ever.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/45#v3)</sup> Gird thy sword upon thy thigh, O most mighty, with thy glory and thy majesty.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/45#v4)</sup> And in thy majesty ride prosperously because of truth and meekness and righteousness; and thy right hand shall teach thee terrible things.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/45#v5)</sup> Thine arrows are sharp in the heart of the king’s enemies; whereby the people fall under thee.

- The heart indites a good matter concerning the king, who is fairer than the children of men with grace poured into his lips.
- He girds his sword upon his thigh and rides prosperously because of truth, meekness, and righteousness, and his arrows are sharp in the heart of the king's enemies.

**[Psalms 45:6](/bible/psalms/45#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/45#v6)</sup> Thy throne, O God, is for ever and ever: the sceptre of thy kingdom is a right sceptre.

- The throne of the Most High is forever and ever, and the scepter of the kingdom is a right scepter.
- That rulership belongs to Christ from the tribe of Judah, and if we keep the commandments we will be heirs with Him in the kingdom.

**[Psalms 45:7-9](/bible/psalms/45#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/45#v7)</sup> Thou lovest righteousness, and hatest wickedness: therefore God, thy God, hath anointed thee with the oil of gladness above thy fellows.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/45#v8)</sup> All thy garments smell of myrrh, and aloes, and cassia, out of the ivory palaces, whereby they have made thee glad.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/45#v9)</sup> Kings’ daughters were among thy honourable women: upon thy right hand did stand the queen in gold of Ophir.

- He loves righteousness and hates wickedness, therefore the Most High anointed him with the oil of gladness above his fellows.
- His garments smell of myrrh and aloes and cassia, kings' daughters are among his honorable women, and the queen stands in gold of Ophir.
- The scepter of righteousness means there will be peace and righteousness in the kingdom, no more of the wickedness and Jacob's trouble we suffer now.

**[Psalms 45:10-11](/bible/psalms/45#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/45#v10)</sup> Hearken, O daughter, and consider, and incline thine ear; forget also thine own people, and thy father’s house;
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/45#v11)</sup> So shall the king greatly desire thy beauty: for he is thy Lord; and worship thou him.

- The daughter is told to hearken, consider, incline her ear, and forget her own people and her father's house, so the king will greatly desire her beauty.
- This is the woman leaving her father's house for her husband's house when we live righteously; she now cares to please her husband.
  - **[1 Corinthians 7:34](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v34)</sup> There is difference also between a wife and a virgin. The unmarried woman careth for the things of the Lord, that she may be holy both in body and in spirit: but she that is married careth for the things of the world, how she may please her husband.

    The married woman cares how she may please her husband, being gone from her father's house.

    > <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/30#v16)</sup> These are the statutes, which the Lord commanded Moses, between a man and his wife, between the father and his daughter, being yet in her youth in her father’s house.

    These are the statutes between a man and his wife, and between the father and his daughter yet in her youth in her father's house.

**[Psalms 45:12-17](/bible/psalms/45#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/45#v12)</sup> And the daughter of Tyre shall be there with a gift; even the rich among the people shall intreat thy favour.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/45#v13)</sup> The king’s daughter is all glorious within: her clothing is of wrought gold.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/45#v14)</sup> She shall be brought unto the king in raiment of needlework: the virgins her companions that follow her shall be brought unto thee.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/45#v15)</sup> With gladness and rejoicing shall they be brought: they shall enter into the king’s palace.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/45#v16)</sup> Instead of thy fathers shall be thy children, whom thou mayest make princes in all the earth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/45#v17)</sup> I will make thy name to be remembered in all generations: therefore shall the people praise thee for ever and ever.

- The daughter of Tyre comes with a gift and the rich among the people entreat her favor; the king's daughter is all glorious within.
- She is brought to the king in raiment of needlework, with her virgin companions following in gladness and joy into the king's palace.
- Instead of her fathers shall be her children, made princes in all the earth, and the king's name will be remembered in all generations.
