**[Psalm 32:1-2](/bible/psalms/32#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/32#v1)</sup> Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven, whose sin is covered.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/32#v2)</sup> Blessed is the man unto whom the Lord imputeth not iniquity, and in whose spirit there is no guile.

- Blessed is he whose transgression is forgiven and whose sin is covered, blessed is the man unto whom the Lord imputeth not iniquity and in whose spirit there is no guile.

**[Psalm 32:3-5](/bible/psalms/32#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/32#v3)</sup> When I kept silence, my bones waxed old through my roaring all the day long.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/32#v4)</sup> For day and night thy hand was heavy upon me: my moisture is turned into the drought of summer. Selah.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/32#v5)</sup> I acknowledged my sin unto thee, and mine iniquity have I not hid. I said, I will confess my transgressions unto the Lord; and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. Selah.

- When I kept silence my bones waxed old through my roaring all the day long, for day and night thy hand was heavy upon me, my moisture is turned into the drought of summer.
- I acknowledge my sin unto thee and my iniquity have I not hid, I said I will confess my transgressions unto the Lord and thou forgavest the iniquity of my sin. That is heavy. A lot of our people say they are not sinning and ask what sin they are doing, but there is none of us that has not sinned, and that is something we have to be aware of on a daily basis.
- You know the things you struggle with on your own, and even when you are around the brethren and they cannot tell what it is, you yourself know, so you have to examine yourself and acknowledge to the Most High that you are in sin. You cannot hide your sin anyway, because the eyes of the Most High are ten times brighter than the sun, and when you repent and come into this truth there is an angel with you every day of the week writing down all the stuff that you do and giving a report on your behalf.
  - **First Kings 8:46**  *46 If they sin against thee, (for there is no man that sinneth not,) and thou be angry with them, and deliver them to the enemy, so that they carry them away captives unto the land of the enemy, far or near;*  There is no man that sinneth not, and when carried captive we must bethink ourselves, repent, and make supplication saying we have sinned and done perversely.

**[Psalm 32:6-11](/bible/psalms/32#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/32#v6)</sup> For this shall every one that is godly pray unto thee in a time when thou mayest be found: surely in the floods of great waters they shall not come nigh unto him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/32#v7)</sup> Thou art my hiding place; thou shalt preserve me from trouble; thou shalt compass me about with songs of deliverance. Selah.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/32#v8)</sup> I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go: I will guide thee with mine eye.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/32#v9)</sup> Be ye not as the horse, or as the mule, which have no understanding: whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle, lest they come near unto thee.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/32#v10)</sup> Many sorrows shall be to the wicked: but he that trusteth in the Lord, mercy shall compass him about.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/32#v11)</sup> Be glad in the Lord, and rejoice, ye righteous: and shout for joy, all ye that are upright in heart.

- For this shall every one that is godly pray unto thee in a time when thou mayest be found, surely in the floods of great waters they shall not come nigh unto him. Thou art my hiding place, thou shalt preserve me from trouble and compass me about with songs of deliverance.
- I will instruct thee and teach thee in the way which thou shalt go, I will guide thee with mine eye. Be ye not as the horse or as the mule which have no understanding, whose mouth must be held in with bit and bridle.
- Many sorrows shall be to the wicked, but he that trusteth in the Lord, mercy shall compass him about. Be glad in the Lord and rejoice ye righteous, and shout for joy all ye that are upright in heart.
