**[Psalm 148:1-14](/bible/psalms/148#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/148#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise ye the Lord from the heavens: praise him in the heights.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/148#v2)</sup> Praise ye him, all his angels: praise ye him, all his hosts.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/148#v3)</sup> Praise ye him, sun and moon: praise him, all ye stars of light.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/148#v4)</sup> Praise him, ye heavens of heavens, and ye waters that be above the heavens.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/148#v5)</sup> Let them praise the name of the Lord: for he commanded, and they were created.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/148#v6)</sup> He hath also stablished them for ever and ever: he hath made a decree which shall not pass.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/148#v7)</sup> Praise the Lord from the earth, ye dragons, and all deeps:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/148#v8)</sup> Fire, and hail; snow, and vapour; stormy wind fulfilling his word:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/148#v9)</sup> Mountains, and all hills; fruitful trees, and all cedars:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/148#v10)</sup> Beasts, and all cattle; creeping things, and flying fowl:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/148#v11)</sup> Kings of the earth, and all people; princes, and all judges of the earth:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/148#v12)</sup> Both young men, and maidens; old men, and children:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/148#v13)</sup> Let them praise the name of the Lord: for his name alone is excellent; his glory is above the earth and heaven.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

- Praise ye the Most High from the heavens; praise Him in the heights. Praise ye Him, all His angels; praise ye Him, all His hosts. Praise Him, sun and moon; praise Him, all ye stars of light.
- Praise Him, ye heavens of heavens, and ye waters that be above the heavens. The waters that be above the heavens go back to Genesis, when He divided the waters.
- Let them praise the name of the Most High, for He commanded and they were created. All these things He created obey Him. He hath also stablished them forever and ever; He hath made a decree which shall not pass.
  - **[Jeremiah 31:35-36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/jeremiah/31#v35)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, which giveth the sun for a light by day, and the ordinances of the moon and of the stars for a light by night, which divideth the sea when the waves thereof roar; The Lord of hosts is his name:
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/jeremiah/31#v36)</sup> If those ordinances depart from before me, saith the Lord, then the seed of Israel also shall cease from being a nation before me for ever.

    The decree that shall not pass: as long as the sun, moon, and stars keep their ordinances, the seed of Israel shall not cease from being a nation before Him forever.
- As long as the nation of Israel is on this earth, these ordinances will stay and do the jobs they were given, serving for a light by night and by day. Even the heavens, the sun, the moon, and the stars are not here for decoration; they are here for a purpose, used for signs and for seasons as we read in [Ecclesiasticus 43](/bible/sirach/43).
- Praise the Most High from the earth, ye dragons and all deeps, fire and hail, snow and vapours, stormy wind fulfilling His word.
- Mountains and all hills, fruitful trees and all cedars, beasts and all cattle, creeping things and flying fowl, kings of the earth and all people, princes and all judges of the earth, both young men and maidens, old men and children.
- Let them praise the name of the Most High, for His name alone is excellent; His glory is above the earth and heaven. He also exalteth the horn of His people, the praise of all His saints, even of the children of Israel, a people near unto Him.
- He lets you know who His people is. There is no other people He says is near unto Him but the children of Israel. The horn He exalteth is the horn of salvation, which is Christ.
  - **[Luke 1:68-69](/bible/luke/1#v68)**
    > <sup>[68](/bible/luke/1#v68)</sup> Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people,
    >
    > <sup>[69](/bible/luke/1#v69)</sup> And hath raised up an horn of salvation for us in the house of his servant David;

    Blessed be the Lord God of Israel, who raised up a horn of salvation for His people in the house of His servant David; that horn is Christ.
  - **[Matthew 1:21](/bible/matthew/1#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/1#v21)</sup> And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.

    She shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call His name Jesus, for He shall save His people from their sins; salvation comes through Christ.
- This verse also lets you know that the children of Israel are the saints mentioned in the Bible. The saints are not Saint Nicholas or the different saints of the Christian church doctrine; they are no other nation but the children of Israel.
