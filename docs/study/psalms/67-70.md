Read the chapter → [Psalms 67](/bible/psalms/67)

This session moves through [Psalms 67](/bible/psalms/67) through 70, read for the nation four chapters a day. [Psalm 67](/bible/psalms/67) asks the Most High to be merciful and to bless us so that His way is known upon the earth and His saving health among all nations. The teaching ties the blessing of the psalm directly to Deuteronomy 28: the blessings are national, not the individual blessing that people today claim for themselves. Our forefathers understood that when we were blessed as a nation of people, we were the ruling class, and the earth yielding her increase is the very increase promised for keeping the commandments.

**[Psalms 67:1-2](/bible/psalms/67#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/67#v1)</sup> God be merciful unto us, and bless us; and cause his face to shine upon us; Selah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/67#v2)</sup> That thy way may be known upon earth, thy saving health among all nations.

- The plea is for the Most High to be merciful, to bless us, and to cause His face to shine upon us.
- The purpose of the blessing is that His way may be known upon the earth and His saving health among all nations.

**[Psalms 67:3-5](/bible/psalms/67#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/67#v3)</sup> Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/67#v4)</sup> O let the nations be glad and sing for joy: for thou shalt judge the people righteously, and govern the nations upon earth. Selah.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/67#v5)</sup> Let the people praise thee, O God; let all the people praise thee.

- The call is for all the people to praise the Most High, for the nations to be glad and sing for joy.
- He shall judge the people righteously and govern the nations upon the earth. This is us going into rulership.
- The blessing our forefathers understood was that when we were blessed as a nation of people, we were the ruling class.

**[Psalms 67:6-7](/bible/psalms/67#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/67#v6)</sup> Then shall the earth yield her increase; and God, even our own God, shall bless us.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/67#v7)</sup> God shall bless us; and all the ends of the earth shall fear him.

- Then the earth shall yield her increase and our own God shall bless us, and all the ends of the earth shall fear Him.
- This is what the psalmist was referring to: when we keep the commandments of God, we as a nation come under the blessings of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28). The ground yielding its increase is exactly that promise.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:1-5](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v2)</sup> And all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v3)</sup> Blessed shalt thou be in the city, and blessed shalt thou be in the field.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v4)</sup> Blessed shall be the fruit of thy body, and the fruit of thy ground, and the fruit of thy cattle, the increase of thy kine, and the flocks of thy sheep.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v5)</sup> Blessed shall be thy basket and thy store.

    Hearken diligently to His commandments and the Most High sets thee on high above all nations; blessed in the city and field, and the fruit of thy body and thy ground and thy cattle.

# Psalm 68: Let God Arise

Read the chapter → [Psalms 68](/bible/psalms/68)

[Psalm 68](/bible/psalms/68) opens with the cry for the Most High to arise and scatter His enemies, and the teaching keeps tying that scattering back to the blessing of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28) and Leviticus 26: our enemies fleeing before us is part of what comes with being a blessed nation. The chapter moves through the Most High going before His people in the wilderness, the Lord giving the word and the great company that published it, His chariots of twenty thousand, and the tribes marching with singers and damsels playing instruments. The Most High is a man of war, and to Him belong the issues of death.

**[Psalms 68:1-2](/bible/psalms/68#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/68#v1)</sup> Let God arise, let his enemies be scattered: let them also that hate him flee before him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/68#v2)</sup> As smoke is driven away, so drive them away: as wax melteth before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.

- The cry is for the Most High to arise so His enemies are scattered and those that hate Him flee before Him.
- As smoke is driven away, so drive them away; as wax melts before the fire, so let the wicked perish at the presence of God.
- This is going into the blessing again. Today people say they are blessed as an individual, but we understood that the blessings of God are in [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), and they refer to our condition as a nation of people. Our enemies fleeing from us is part of that blessing.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:1-2,7](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v1)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if thou shalt hearken diligently unto the voice of the Lord thy God, to observe and to do all his commandments which I command thee this day, that the Lord thy God will set thee on high above all nations of the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v2)</sup> And all these blessings shall come on thee, and overtake thee, if thou shalt hearken unto the voice of the Lord thy God.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v7)</sup> The Lord shall cause thine enemies that rise up against thee to be smitten before thy face: they shall come out against thee one way, and flee before thee seven ways.

    Hearken diligently and the blessings overtake thee as a nation; the Most High causes thine enemies to be smitten before thy face, coming out one way and fleeing seven ways.
  - **[Leviticus 26:7-8](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/26#v7)</sup> And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/26#v8)</sup> And five of you shall chase an hundred, and an hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight: and your enemies shall fall before you by the sword.

    Ye shall chase your enemies and they shall fall before you by the sword; five of you shall chase a hundred, and a hundred of you shall put ten thousand to flight.

**[Psalms 68:3-6](/bible/psalms/68#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/68#v3)</sup> But let the righteous be glad; let them rejoice before God: yea, let them exceedingly rejoice.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/68#v4)</sup> Sing unto God, sing praises to his name: extol him that rideth upon the heavens by his name Jah, and rejoice before him.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/68#v5)</sup> A father of the fatherless, and a judge of the widows, is God in his holy habitation.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/68#v6)</sup> God setteth the solitary in families: he bringeth out those which are bound with chains: but the rebellious dwell in a dry land.

- Let the righteous, those keeping the commandments, be glad and rejoice exceedingly before God. We are going to rejoice on our feast days, not oppressed by the enemy, serving the Lord without fear in the Kingdom.
- Sing praises to His name, extol Him that rideth upon the heavens by His name Yah, and rejoice before Him. The Lord has many titles as you read the scriptures.
- He is a father of the fatherless and a judge of the widows; He sets the solitary in families and brings out those bound with chains, but the rebellious dwell in a dry land.

**[Psalms 68:7-10](/bible/psalms/68#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/68#v7)</sup> O God, when thou wentest forth before thy people, when thou didst march through the wilderness; Selah:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/68#v8)</sup> The earth shook, the heavens also dropped at the presence of God: even Sinai itself was moved at the presence of God, the God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/68#v9)</sup> Thou, O God, didst send a plentiful rain, whereby thou didst confirm thine inheritance, when it was weary.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/68#v10)</sup> Thy congregation hath dwelt therein: thou, O God, hast prepared of thy goodness for the poor.

- When the Most High went forth before His people and marched through the wilderness, the earth shook and the heavens dropped at His presence, and even Sinai itself was moved.
- Just as when we came out of Egypt, the Most High went before us in the wilderness and in the chariot; He always went before us.
- The scriptures talk about how when the Most High comes, the hailstorms come, the mountains tremble, and thunder and lightning appear. We serve the only true God, the God of Israel, the God of everybody.
- He sent a plentiful rain to confirm His inheritance when it was weary, and prepared of His goodness for the poor.

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/68#v14)</sup> When the Almighty scattered kings in it, it was white as snow in Salmon.

- The Lord gave the word, and great was the company of those that published it. That is why you have different books of people that the Lord spoke to: Ezekiel, Jeremiah, and all the prophets in the company of Israel that published what God said.
- Kings of armies did flee, and she that tarried at home divided the spoil. Though they lay among the pots, yet shall they be as the wings of a dove covered with silver.

    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/2-peter/1#v21)</sup> For the prophecy came not in old time by the will of man: but holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.

    We have a more sure word of prophecy; no prophecy of scripture is of any private interpretation, for holy men of God spake as they were moved by the Holy Ghost.

> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/68#v15)</sup> The hill of God is as the hill of Bashan; an high hill as the hill of Bashan.

- That is why Christ said a city on a hill cannot be hidden; that is the city Jerusalem where the Lord dwells.
- The chariots of God are twenty thousand, even thousands of angels, the Lord among them as in Sinai. That is why in [Isaiah 66:15](/bible/isaiah/66#v15) He is coming back with the whirlwind.
- He has ascended on high, led captivity captive, and received gifts for men, even for the rebellious, that the Lord God might dwell among them.

**[Psalms 68:19-21](/bible/psalms/68#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/68#v19)</sup> Blessed be the Lord, who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation. Selah.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/68#v20)</sup> He that is our God is the God of salvation; and unto God the Lord belong the issues from death.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/68#v21)</sup> But God shall wound the head of his enemies, and the hairy scalp of such an one as goeth on still in his trespasses.

- Blessed be the Lord who daily loadeth us with benefits, even the God of our salvation. As it says in [Lamentations 3](/bible/lamentations/3), His mercy is renewed every morning.
- He that is our God is the God of salvation, and unto God the Lord belong the issues of death.
- There is something we must understand: to the Lord belong the issues of death. That is why you see people get shot many times and still live, or go through things people wonder how they survived. When it is not your time you are not going, and when it is your time you are not staying.
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:39](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v39)**

**[Psalms 68:22-23](/bible/psalms/68#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/68#v22)</sup> The Lord said, I will bring again from Bashan, I will bring my people again from the depths of the sea:

# Psalm 69: Save Me, O God

Read the chapter → [Psalms 69](/bible/psalms/69)

[Psalm 69](/bible/psalms/69) is the cry of a man come into deep waters, hated without a cause, bearing reproach for the Most High's sake. The teaching frames the reproach and shame as the very marks of being on the right track, since the world hates what we teach because it loves its evil deeds, and ties the man being a stranger to his brethren to the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28). The chapter turns into imprecation against the persecutors, being blotted out of the book of the living, and closes on the promise that God will save Zion and build the cities of Judah for the seed that loves His name.

**[Psalms 69:1-4](/bible/psalms/69#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/69#v1)</sup> Save me, O God; for the waters are come in unto my soul.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/69#v2)</sup> I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing: I am come into deep waters, where the floods overflow me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/69#v3)</sup> I am weary of my crying: my throat is dried: mine eyes fail while I wait for my God.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/69#v4)</sup> They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of mine head: they that would destroy me, being mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away.

- Save me, O God, for the waters are come in unto my soul; I sink in deep mire where there is no standing, come into deep waters where the floods overflow me.
- I am weary of my crying, my throat is dry, my eyes fail while I wait for my God. They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head.

**[Psalms 69:5](/bible/psalms/69#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/69#v5)</sup> O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee.

- O God, Thou knowest my foolishness, and my sins are not hid from Thee.
- Nothing is hid from God. Whether it be good or evil, He brings every secret thing into judgment.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 12:13-14](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)</sup> Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v14)</sup> For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil.

    Fear God and keep His commandments, for this is the whole duty of man; for God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether good or evil.

**[Psalms 69:6-9](/bible/psalms/69#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/69#v6)</sup> Let not them that wait on thee, O Lord God of hosts, be ashamed for my sake: let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake, O God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/69#v7)</sup> Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/69#v8)</sup> I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother’s children.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/69#v9)</sup> For the zeal of thine house hath eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee are fallen upon me.

- Let not them that wait on Thee be ashamed for my sake, nor those that seek Thee be confounded, O God of Israel.
- For Thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face. I am become a stranger unto my brethren and an alien unto my mother's children, for the zeal of Thine house hath eaten me up.
- The scripture says blessed are you when they hate you, mistreat you, and speak evil of you. We should look at going through these conditions and know that according to the scriptures we are blessed. It may not look like it, but this is one of the ways you know you are on the right track, because the world hates what we teach because they love their evil deeds.

**[Psalms 69:10-12](/bible/psalms/69#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/69#v10)</sup> When I wept, and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach.

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/69#v11)</sup> I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb to them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/69#v12)</sup> They that sit in the gate speak against me; and I was the song of the drunkards.

- When I wept and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach; I made sackcloth my garment and became a proverb to them.
- Just like it says in [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), this is how it feels to be under the curses, to be a proverb and an astonishment and a byword. David was like, Lord, I am a king, this is not supposed to be happening.
- They that sit in the gate speak against me, and I was the song of the drunkards.

**[Psalms 69:13-15](/bible/psalms/69#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/69#v13)</sup> But as for me, my prayer is unto thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation.

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/69#v14)</sup> Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters.

- Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink; let me be delivered from them that hate me and out of the deep waters. Deliver me from the haters, and let not the deep swallow me up.

- Hear me speedily; draw nigh unto my soul and redeem it; deliver me because of mine enemies.

# Psalm 70: Make Haste to Deliver Me

Read the chapter → [Psalms 70](/bible/psalms/70)

[Psalm 70](/bible/psalms/70) is the short, urgent cry for the Most High to make haste to deliver, that the enemies who say aha be turned back in shame while those who love His salvation rejoice. The teaching closes the four chapters on patience: the Lord is long suffering, a day is like a thousand years to Him, and in the last days people will ask where is the sign of His coming, but we are to trust that He will deliver the marked remnant in His acceptable time.

**[Psalms 70:1-3](/bible/psalms/70#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/70#v1)</sup> Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/70#v2)</sup> Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul: let them be turned backward, and put to confusion, that desire my hurt.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/70#v3)</sup> Let them be turned back for a reward of their shame that say, Aha, aha.

- Make haste, O God, to deliver me; make haste to help me, O Lord.
- Let them be ashamed and confounded that seek after my soul; let them be turned backward and put to confusion that desire my hurt; let them be turned back that say aha, aha.

**[Psalms 70:4-5](/bible/psalms/70#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/70#v4)</sup> Let all those that seek thee rejoice and be glad in thee: and let such as love thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/70#v5)</sup> But I am poor and needy: make haste unto me, O God: thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.

- Let all those that seek Thee rejoice and be glad in Thee, and let such as love Thy salvation say continually, Let God be magnified.
- But I am poor and needy; make haste unto me, O God; Thou art my help and my deliverer; O Lord, make no tarrying.
- The scripture says a day is like a thousand years to the Lord, and in the last days people will say where is the sign of His coming, but the Lord is long suffering toward us, not willing that any perish but that all come to repentance, meaning all marked to be saved, the 144,000 and the one third. Be patient and trust that the Lord is going to deliver us.
