**[Psalm 122:1-3](/bible/psalms/122#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/122#v1)</sup> I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/122#v2)</sup> Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/122#v3)</sup> Jerusalem is builded as a city that is compact together:

- I was glad when they said unto me, Let us go into the house of the Most High. Our feet shall stand within thy gates, O Jerusalem. Jerusalem is builded as a city that is compact together.
- Jerusalem builded as a city compacted together is comparing the city to a people. Now that we come into this truth and know we are the Israelites, everyone has a part to play, so if you are sitting back not doing anything, shame on you, no matter how small you think the task is.
  - **[Ephesians 2:19-22](/bible/ephesians/2#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/ephesians/2#v19)</sup> Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God;
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ephesians/2#v20)</sup> And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/ephesians/2#v21)</sup> In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/2#v22)</sup> In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.

    We are no more strangers and foreigners but fellow citizens, built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Christ the chief cornerstone, fitly framed together and growing unto a holy temple, each with different skill sets to build the nation back up.

**[Psalm 122:4-9](/bible/psalms/122#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/122#v4)</sup> Whither the tribes go up, the tribes of the Lord, unto the testimony of Israel, to give thanks unto the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/122#v5)</sup> For there are set thrones of judgment, the thrones of the house of David.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/122#v6)</sup> Pray for the peace of Jerusalem: they shall prosper that love thee.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/122#v7)</sup> Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/122#v8)</sup> For my brethren and companions’ sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/122#v9)</sup> Because of the house of the Lord our God I will seek thy good.

- Whither the tribes go up, the tribes of the Most High, unto the testimony of Israel, to give thanks unto His name. For there are set thrones of judgment, the thrones of the house of David.
- Pray for the peace of Jerusalem; they shall prosper that love thee. Peace be within thy walls, and prosperity within thy palaces. Those who love and meditate on this Bible shall prosper.
- For my brethren and companions' sakes, I will now say, Peace be within thee. My brethren are the twelve tribes. Because of the house of the Most High our God I will seek thy good, and the good is His law, His commandments, which are holy, just, and good for us.
  - **[Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

    Meditate in this book of the law day and night and observe to do it, and then thou shalt make thy way prosperous and have good success.
  - **[James 1:1](/bible/james/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/james/1#v1)</sup> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.

    James, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad; the brethren are the twelve tribes of Israel.
  - **[Romans 7:12](/bible/romans/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/romans/7#v12)</sup> Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.

    Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good; the commandments are good for us.
