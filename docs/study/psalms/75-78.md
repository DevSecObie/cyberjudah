Read the chapter → [Psalms 75](/bible/psalms/75)

This session is four chapters a day, Scripts 365, covering [Psalms 75](/bible/psalms/75) through 78. The reading is relatively short today, though [Psalm 78](/bible/psalms/78) is long. The thread running through these chapters is that nothing that happens is for no reason: the Most High is in control of everything, He sits one man up and sets another down, He judges the nations and spares His people, and He has done wonderful works for the children of Israel throughout their history even while they kept provoking Him. Before beginning, this is a Bible-based movement and not a hate group; it does not advocate or condone acts of violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender.

**[Psalm 75:1](/bible/psalms/75#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/75#v1)</sup> Unto thee, O God, do we give thanks, unto thee do we give thanks: for that thy name is near thy wondrous works declare.

- Give thanks unto God, for His name is near and His wondrous works declare that name.

**[Psalm 75:2-3](/bible/psalms/75#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/75#v2)</sup> When I shall receive the congregation I will judge uprightly.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/75#v3)</sup> The earth and all the inhabitants thereof are dissolved: I bear up the pillars of it. Selah.

- When the Most High receives the congregation He will judge uprightly. The earth and all the inhabitants are dissolved, but He bears up the pillars of it.

**[Psalm 75:4-5](/bible/psalms/75#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/75#v4)</sup> I said unto the fools, Deal not foolishly: and to the wicked, Lift not up the horn:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/75#v5)</sup> Lift not up your horn on high: speak not with a stiff neck.

- He tells the fools not to deal foolishly and the wicked not to lift up the horn. Do not lift up your horn on high or speak with a stiff neck.

**[Psalm 75:6-7](/bible/psalms/75#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/75#v6)</sup> For promotion cometh neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/75#v7)</sup> But God is the judge: he putteth down one, and setteth up another.

- Promotion comes neither from the east, nor from the west, nor from the south, but God is the judge. He puts down one and sets up another.
- Nothing that happens is just for any reason. The Lord is in control of everything; He sits up, He sits down. As one of the soldiers from Tampa put it, the Lord brought you in and He will take you out.

**[Psalm 75:8](/bible/psalms/75#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/75#v8)</sup> For in the hand of the Lord there is a cup, and the wine is red; it is full of mixture; and he poureth out of the same: but the dregs thereof, all the wicked of the earth shall wring them out, and drink them.

- In the hand of the Lord there is a cup, and the wine is red and full of mixture. He pours it out, but the dregs the wicked of the earth wring out and drink.

**[Psalm 75:9-10](/bible/psalms/75#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/75#v9)</sup> But I will declare for ever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/75#v10)</sup> All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off; but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.

- The psalmist declares he will sing praises to the God of Jacob forever. All the horns of the wicked will be cut off, but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.

# Psalm 76: God Is Known in Judah and Terrible to the Kings of the Earth

Read the chapter → [Psalms 76](/bible/psalms/76)

**[Psalm 76:1-3](/bible/psalms/76#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/76#v1)</sup> In Judah is God known: his name is great in Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/76#v2)</sup> In Salem also is his tabernacle, and his dwelling place in Zion.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/76#v3)</sup> There brake he the arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the battle. Selah.

- In Judah is God known, His name is great in Israel. In Salem is His tabernacle and in Zion His dwelling place. There He broke the arrows of the bow, the shield, the sword, and the battle.

**[Psalm 76:4-6](/bible/psalms/76#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/76#v4)</sup> Thou art more glorious and excellent than the mountains of prey.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/76#v5)</sup> The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep: and none of the men of might have found their hands.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/76#v6)</sup> At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob, both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep.

- The Most High is more glorious and excellent than the mountains of prey. The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep, and none of the men of might found their hands. At His rebuke both the chariot and horse are cast into a dead sleep.

**[Psalm 76:7-9](/bible/psalms/76#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/76#v7)</sup> Thou, even thou, art to be feared: and who may stand in thy sight when once thou art angry?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/76#v8)</sup> Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven; the earth feared, and was still,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/76#v9)</sup> When God arose to judgment, to save all the meek of the earth. Selah.

- The Most High alone is to be feared, and none may stand in His sight when He is angry. He caused judgment to be heard from heaven, and the earth feared and was still when God arose to save all the meek of the earth.

**[Psalm 76:10](/bible/psalms/76#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/76#v10)</sup> Surely the wrath of man shall praise thee: the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain.

- When we see the Lord put in His judgment upon the earth, everybody will know that the Most High did it. The wrath of man shall praise Him.
- When it says the remainder of wrath He shall restrain, it means He is not going to destroy everything and everybody like He did in the flood, where even then eight remained. When the Lord touches up on something, He touches up on him.
  - **[Exodus 9:5-6](/bible/exodus/9#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/9#v5)</sup> And the Lord appointed a set time, saying, To morrow the Lord shall do this thing in the land.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/9#v6)</sup> And the Lord did that thing on the morrow, and all the cattle of Egypt died: but of the cattle of the children of Israel died not one.

    The Lord appointed a set time, and on the morrow all the cattle of Egypt died, but of the cattle of the children of Israel not one died. He touches the nations but spares His people.

**[Psalm 76:11-12](/bible/psalms/76#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/76#v11)</sup> Vow, and pay unto the Lord your God: let all that be round about him bring presents unto him that ought to be feared.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/76#v12)</sup> He shall cut off the spirit of princes: he is terrible to the kings of the earth.

- Vow and pay unto the Lord your God; let all that are round about Him bring presents unto Him that ought to be feared. He shall cut off the spirit of princes and is terrible to the kings of the earth.

# Psalm 77: I Will Remember the Works of the Most High

Read the chapter → [Psalms 77](/bible/psalms/77)

**[Psalm 77:1-3](/bible/psalms/77#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/77#v1)</sup> I cried unto God with my voice, even unto God with my voice; and he gave ear unto me.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/77#v2)</sup> In the day of my trouble I sought the Lord: my sore ran in the night, and ceased not: my soul refused to be comforted.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/77#v3)</sup> I remembered God, and was troubled: I complained, and my spirit was overwhelmed. Selah.

- The psalmist cried unto God with his voice and God gave ear. In the day of his trouble he sought the Lord; his sore ran in the night and ceased not, and his soul refused to be comforted. He remembered God and was troubled, and complained that his spirit was overwhelmed.

**[Psalm 77:4-6](/bible/psalms/77#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/77#v4)</sup> Thou holdest mine eyes waking: I am so troubled that I cannot speak.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/77#v5)</sup> I have considered the days of old, the years of ancient times.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/77#v6)</sup> I call to remembrance my song in the night: I commune with mine own heart: and my spirit made diligent search.

- God held his eyes waking, and he was so troubled that he could not speak. He considered the days of old, the years of ancient times, called to remembrance his song in the night, communed with his own heart, and his spirit made diligent search.

**[Psalm 77:7-9](/bible/psalms/77#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/77#v7)</sup> Will the Lord cast off for ever? and will he be favourable no more?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/77#v8)</sup> Is his mercy clean gone for ever? doth his promise fail for evermore?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/77#v9)</sup> Hath God forgotten to be gracious? hath he in anger shut up his tender mercies? Selah.

- He asks whether the Lord will cast off forever and be favorable no more, whether His mercy is clean gone forever, whether His promise fails, whether God has forgotten to be gracious and shut up His tender mercies in anger.

**[Psalm 77:10-12](/bible/psalms/77#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/77#v10)</sup> And I said, This is my infirmity: but I will remember the years of the right hand of the most High.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/77#v11)</sup> I will remember the works of the Lord: surely I will remember thy wonders of old.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/77#v12)</sup> I will meditate also of all thy work, and talk of thy doings.

- He calls this his infirmity, then resolves to remember the years of the right hand of the Most High. He will remember the works of the Lord, His wonders of old, and meditate and talk of His doings.

**[Psalm 77:13-15](/bible/psalms/77#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/77#v13)</sup> Thy way, O God, is in the sanctuary: who is so great a God as our God?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/77#v14)</sup> Thou art the God that doest wonders: thou hast declared thy strength among the people.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/77#v15)</sup> Thou hast with thine arm redeemed thy people, the sons of Jacob and Joseph. Selah.

- The Most High's way is in the sanctuary. There is no god so great as our God, who does wonders and has declared His strength among the people, redeeming with His arm the sons of Jacob and Joseph.

**[Psalm 77:16-18](/bible/psalms/77#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/77#v16)</sup> The waters saw thee, O God, the waters saw thee; they were afraid: the depths also were troubled.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/77#v17)</sup> The clouds poured out water: the skies sent out a sound: thine arrows also went abroad.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/77#v18)</sup> The voice of thy thunder was in the heaven: the lightnings lightened the world: the earth trembled and shook.

- The waters saw the Most High and were afraid, and the depths were troubled. The clouds poured out water, the skies sent out sound, His arrows went abroad, His thunder was in the heaven, and the lightnings lightened the world while the earth trembled and shook.

**[Psalm 77:19-20](/bible/psalms/77#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/77#v19)</sup> Thy way is in the sea, and thy path in the great waters, and thy footsteps are not known.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/77#v20)</sup> Thou leddest thy people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

- His way is in the sea and His path in the great waters, and His footsteps are not known. He led His people like a flock by the hand of Moses and Aaron.

# Psalm 78: The History of Israel's Rebellion and the Choosing of Judah and David

Read the chapter → [Psalms 78](/bible/psalms/78)

**[Psalm 78:1-2](/bible/psalms/78#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/78#v1)</sup> Give ear, O my people, to my law: incline your ears to the words of my mouth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/78#v2)</sup> I will open my mouth in a parable: I will utter dark sayings of old:

- Give ear, O my people, to my law; incline your ears to the words of my mouth. I will open my mouth in a parable and utter dark sayings of old.
- When you look at the tribe of Judah, at Christ and his ministry, this is a prophecy of what Christ would do. The scriptures talk about how the spirit of Christ was in the prophets and he would speak through them.
- When Christ spoke to the apostles and disciples, he would give them the understanding of the parables and dark sayings in a separate setting than what he gave everyone else. This was so that everyone after Christ was gone could come to the disciples or apostles for the understanding, setting up the hierarchy of the church.

**[Psalm 78:3-4](/bible/psalms/78#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/78#v3)</sup> Which we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/78#v4)</sup> We will not hide them from their children, shewing to the generation to come the praises of the Lord, and his strength, and his wonderful works that he hath done.

- What we have heard and known, and our fathers have told us, we will not hide from the children, but show to the generation to come the praises of the Lord and His strength and His wonderful works.
- That was the apostles bringing it to the children of Israel after they were taught by Christ.

**[Psalm 78:5-7](/bible/psalms/78#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/78#v5)</sup> For he established a testimony in Jacob, and appointed a law in Israel, which he commanded our fathers, that they should make them known to their children:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/78#v6)</sup> That the generation to come might know them, even the children which should be born; who should arise and declare them to their children:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/78#v7)</sup> That they might set their hope in God, and not forget the works of God, but keep his commandments:

- He established a testimony in Jacob and appointed a law in Israel, which He commanded our fathers to make known to their children, so the generation to come and the children yet to be born might know them, declare them to their children, set their hope in God, and keep His commandments.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 24:23](/bible/sirach/24#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/24#v23)</sup> All these things are the book of the covenant of the most high God, even the law which Moses commanded for an heritage unto the congregations of Jacob.

    A lot of people, meaning the other nations, think this Bible pertains to them, but it does not; you might find them in the Bible, but they are not of the Bible.

**[Psalm 78:8](/bible/psalms/78#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/78#v8)</sup> And might not be as their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation; a generation that set not their heart aright, and whose spirit was not stedfast with God.

- The purpose was that they not be like their fathers, a stubborn and rebellious generation that set not their heart aright and whose spirit was not steadfast with God.

>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/78#v11)</sup> And forgat his works, and his wonders that he had shewed them.

- The children of Ephraim, being armed and carrying bows, turned back in the day of battle. They kept not the covenant of God, refused to walk in His law, and forgot His works and the wonders He had showed them.

**[Psalm 78:12-14](/bible/psalms/78#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/78#v12)</sup> Marvellous things did he in the sight of their fathers, in the land of Egypt, in the field of Zoan.
>

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/78#v14)</sup> In the daytime also he led them with a cloud, and all the night with a light of fire.

**[Psalm 78:15-16](/bible/psalms/78#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/78#v15)</sup> He clave the rocks in the wilderness, and gave them drink as out of the great depths.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/78#v16)</sup> He brought streams also out of the rock, and caused waters to run down like rivers.

- He clave the rocks in the wilderness and gave them drink as out of the great depths, bringing streams out of the rock and causing waters to run down like rivers.

**[Psalm 78:17-18](/bible/psalms/78#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/78#v17)</sup> And they sinned yet more against him by provoking the most High in the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/78#v18)</sup> And they tempted God in their heart by asking meat for their lust.

- Yet they sinned more against Him, provoking the Most High in the wilderness and tempting God in their heart by asking meat for their lust.
- This is going into their covetousness. They were always looking back to Egypt, wanting to be like Egypt, wanting the leeks, the fish, the cucumbers, all the foods and benefits they got there. They wanted their cake and to eat it too in the wilderness, and that was the children of Israel, us.

>

- They spoke against God, saying, can God furnish a table in the wilderness? He smote the rock so the waters gushed out and streams overflowed, but they asked whether He could give bread and provide flesh for His people.

- You can use this scripture too, along with [Numbers 11](/bible/numbers/11), to answer people who say you have to be a vegan or a vegetarian; this shows the Lord gave us meat in the wilderness and did it Himself.

**[Psalm 78:29-31](/bible/psalms/78#v29)**

He made a way to His anger, spared not their soul from death, gave their life over to the pestilence, and smote all the firstborn in Egypt, the chief of their strength in the tabernacles of Ham.
- That is a heavy thing. The judgments of the lightning bolts, the fire, the locusts and caterpillars going on throughout Egypt, the Lord said these were His messengers, His evil angels sent to judge Egypt. Israel would look on these things and consider that the Lord does double to the nations of what He does to us.
- The pestilence going into the pandemics and things happening at that time as well.
