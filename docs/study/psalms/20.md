**[Psalm 20:1-2](/bible/psalms/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/20#v1)</sup> The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/20#v2)</sup> Send thee help from the sanctuary, and strengthen thee out of Zion;

- The Lord hear thee in the day of trouble; the name of the God of Jacob defend thee; send thee help from the sanctuary and strengthen thee out of Zion. When the time comes Christ is jumping down from heaven like he did in Egypt; we have to visualize this because it is a prophecy that will come true.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 18:14-16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v14)</sup> For while all things were in quiet silence, and that night was in the midst of her swift course,
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v15)</sup> Thine Almighty word leaped down from heaven out of thy royal throne, as a fierce man of war into the midst of a land of destruction,
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/18#v16)</sup> And brought thine unfeigned commandment as a sharp sword, and standing up filled all things with death; and it touched the heaven, but it stood upon the earth.

    He is coming back with vengeance to defend us.

**[Psalm 20:4-5](/bible/psalms/20#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/20#v4)</sup> Grant thee according to thine own heart, and fulfil all thy counsel.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/20#v5)</sup> We will rejoice in thy salvation, and in the name of our God we will set up our banners: the Lord fulfil all thy petitions.

- Grant thee according to thine own heart and fulfil all thy counsel; we will rejoice in thy salvation and set up our banners; this goes into lifting up the banner on this mountain, the Bible.

**[Psalm 20:6](/bible/psalms/20#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/20#v6)</sup> Now know I that the Lord saveth his anointed; he will hear him from his holy heaven with the saving strength of his right hand.

- Now know I that the Lord saveth his anointed; he will hear him from his holy heaven with the saving strength of his right hand. This is why we ask, to whom has the arm of the Lord been revealed; his arm is not short, it can save.
  - **[Isaiah 59:1-2](/bible/isaiah/59#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/59#v1)</sup> Behold, the Lord’s hand is not shortened, that it cannot save; neither his ear heavy, that it cannot hear:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/59#v2)</sup> But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.

    Behold the Lord's hand is not shortened that it cannot save, but your iniquities have separated between you and your God; that is the problem we face, not that his hand is short. Once we get right as a nation he will fight for us and get a name for himself.

**[Psalm 20:7](/bible/psalms/20#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/20#v7)</sup> Some trust in chariots, and some in horses: but we will remember the name of the Lord our God.

- Some trust in chariots and some in horses, but we will remember the name of the Lord our God. Like today we trust in the shadow of Egypt still doing it; but we Israelites do not trust in oppression, we remember our God.
  - **[Isaiah 31:1](/bible/isaiah/31#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/31#v1)</sup> Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help; and stay on horses, and trust in chariots, because they are many; and in horsemen, because they are very strong; but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel, neither seek the Lord!

    Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help and stay on horses and trust in chariots, but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel; you trust and get comfortable in captivity, in America's army, thinking destruction is not coming to this place.
  - **[Isaiah 30:12](/bible/isaiah/30#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/30#v12)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Holy One of Israel, Because ye despise this word, and trust in oppression and perverseness, and stay thereon:

    Because ye despise this word and trust in oppression and perverseness and stay thereon; you trust in the white man's word that a woman can wear pants and be over the man, though the Most High never said it, and you trust in their perverseness.
  - **[Isaiah 31:3](/bible/isaiah/31#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/31#v3)</sup> Now the Egyptians are men, and not God; and their horses flesh, and not spirit. When the Lord shall stretch out his hand, both he that helpeth shall fall, and he that is holpen shall fall down, and they all shall fail together.

    Now the Egyptians are men and not God, and their horses flesh and not spirit; the white man is just a man, he is not God, and their army has nothing on God's army.

**[Psalm 20:8-9](/bible/psalms/20#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/20#v8)</sup> They are brought down and fallen: but we are risen, and stand upright.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/20#v9)</sup> Save, Lord: let the king hear us when we call.

- They are brought down and fallen, but we are risen and stand upright; save, Lord, let the king hear us when we call.
