Read the chapter → [Psalms 69](/bible/psalms/69)

[Psalm 69](/bible/psalms/69) is the cry of a man come into deep waters, hated without a cause, bearing reproach for the Most High's sake. The teaching frames the reproach and shame as the very marks of being on the right track, since the world hates what we teach because it loves its evil deeds, and ties the man being a stranger to his brethren to the curses of [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28). The chapter turns into imprecation against the persecutors, being blotted out of the book of the living, and closes on the promise that God will save Zion and build the cities of Judah for the seed that loves His name.

**[Psalms 69:1-4](/bible/psalms/69#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/69#v1)</sup> Save me, O God; for the waters are come in unto my soul.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/69#v2)</sup> I sink in deep mire, where there is no standing: I am come into deep waters, where the floods overflow me.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/69#v3)</sup> I am weary of my crying: my throat is dried: mine eyes fail while I wait for my God.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/69#v4)</sup> They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of mine head: they that would destroy me, being mine enemies wrongfully, are mighty: then I restored that which I took not away.

- Save me, O God, for the waters are come in unto my soul; I sink in deep mire where there is no standing, come into deep waters where the floods overflow me.
- I am weary of my crying, my throat is dry, my eyes fail while I wait for my God. They that hate me without a cause are more than the hairs of my head.

**[Psalms 69:5](/bible/psalms/69#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/69#v5)</sup> O God, thou knowest my foolishness; and my sins are not hid from thee.

- O God, Thou knowest my foolishness, and my sins are not hid from Thee.
- Nothing is hid from God. Whether it be good or evil, He brings every secret thing into judgment.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 12:13-14](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v13)</sup> Let us hear the conclusion of the whole matter: Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/ecclesiastes/12#v14)</sup> For God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether it be good, or whether it be evil.

    Fear God and keep His commandments, for this is the whole duty of man; for God shall bring every work into judgment, with every secret thing, whether good or evil.

**[Psalms 69:6-9](/bible/psalms/69#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/69#v6)</sup> Let not them that wait on thee, O Lord God of hosts, be ashamed for my sake: let not those that seek thee be confounded for my sake, O God of Israel.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/69#v7)</sup> Because for thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/69#v8)</sup> I am become a stranger unto my brethren, and an alien unto my mother’s children.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/69#v9)</sup> For the zeal of thine house hath eaten me up; and the reproaches of them that reproached thee are fallen upon me.

- Let not them that wait on Thee be ashamed for my sake, nor those that seek Thee be confounded, O God of Israel.
- For Thy sake I have borne reproach; shame hath covered my face. I am become a stranger unto my brethren and an alien unto my mother's children, for the zeal of Thine house hath eaten me up.
- The scripture says blessed are you when they hate you, mistreat you, and speak evil of you. We should look at going through these conditions and know that according to the scriptures we are blessed. It may not look like it, but this is one of the ways you know you are on the right track, because the world hates what we teach because they love their evil deeds.

**[Psalms 69:10-12](/bible/psalms/69#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/69#v10)</sup> When I wept, and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/69#v11)</sup> I made sackcloth also my garment; and I became a proverb to them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/69#v12)</sup> They that sit in the gate speak against me; and I was the song of the drunkards.

- When I wept and chastened my soul with fasting, that was to my reproach; I made sackcloth my garment and became a proverb to them.
- Just like it says in [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28), this is how it feels to be under the curses, to be a proverb and an astonishment and a byword. David was like, Lord, I am a king, this is not supposed to be happening.
- They that sit in the gate speak against me, and I was the song of the drunkards.

**[Psalms 69:13-15](/bible/psalms/69#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/69#v13)</sup> But as for me, my prayer is unto thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time: O God, in the multitude of thy mercy hear me, in the truth of thy salvation.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/69#v14)</sup> Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink: let me be delivered from them that hate me, and out of the deep waters.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/69#v15)</sup> Let not the waterflood overflow me, neither let the deep swallow me up, and let not the pit shut her mouth upon me.

- But my prayer is unto Thee, O Lord, in an acceptable time; in the multitude of Thy mercy hear me, in the truth of Thy salvation.
- We may think the Lord does not hear us. That is why in the fullness of time God sent His son. In the Christian church they say He may not come when you want Him, but He is always on time; the Lord comes in an acceptable time.
- Deliver me out of the mire, and let me not sink; let me be delivered from them that hate me and out of the deep waters. Deliver me from the haters, and let not the deep swallow me up.

**[Psalms 69:16-18](/bible/psalms/69#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/69#v16)</sup> Hear me, O Lord; for thy lovingkindness is good: turn unto me according to the multitude of thy tender mercies.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/69#v17)</sup> And hide not thy face from thy servant; for I am in trouble: hear me speedily.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/69#v18)</sup> Draw nigh unto my soul, and redeem it: deliver me because of mine enemies.

- Hear me, O Lord, for Thy lovingkindness is good; turn unto me according to the multitude of Thy tender mercies, and hide not Thy face from Thy servant, for I am in trouble.
- When we call on God we want Him to hear us. It is not pleasant to call on God and have Him tell you, as in [Hosea 5:15](/bible/hosea/5#v15), that He will return to His place till you acknowledge your offense.
- Hear me speedily; draw nigh unto my soul and redeem it; deliver me because of mine enemies.
  - **[Deuteronomy 4:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v6)</sup> Keep therefore and do them; for this is your wisdom and your understanding in the sight of the nations, which shall hear all these statutes, and say, Surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/4#v7)</sup> For what nation is there so great, who hath God so nigh unto them, as the Lord our God is in all things that we call upon him for?

    Keep and do the statutes, for this is your wisdom in the sight of the nations, who will say surely this great nation is a wise and understanding people; for what nation is so great, who hath God so nigh unto them.

**[Psalms 69:19-21](/bible/psalms/69#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/69#v19)</sup> Thou hast known my reproach, and my shame, and my dishonour: mine adversaries are all before thee.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/69#v20)</sup> Reproach hath broken my heart; and I am full of heaviness: and I looked for some to take pity, but there was none; and for comforters, but I found none.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/69#v21)</sup> They gave me also gall for my meat; and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink.

- Thou hast known my reproach, my shame, and my dishonour; mine adversaries are all before Thee. Reproach hath broken my heart, and I am full of heaviness.
- Do not give yourself to heaviness, because heaviness and sorrow have killed many people. Through faith and comfort of the scriptures you will have hope, because in this walk you look to this person and that person for comfort and find none. We have to deliver our own soul by our own righteousness.
- I looked for some to take pity, but there was none, and for comforters, but I found none. They gave me gall for my meat, and in my thirst they gave me vinegar to drink. This goes into Christ.

**[Psalms 69:22-23](/bible/psalms/69#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/69#v22)</sup> Let their table become a snare before them: and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/69#v23)</sup> Let their eyes be darkened, that they see not; and make their loins continually to shake.

- Let their table become a snare before them, and that which should have been for their welfare, let it become a trap.
- You can read about this in Isaiah 28: the word became precept upon precept and became a snare and a trap unto them, and they went backwards, because we bring out the understanding of the Bible but people do not want to hear it. You think you are a Christian and good with God going to church every Sunday, but it does not work like that.
- Let their eyes be darkened that they see not.

**[Psalms 69:24-28](/bible/psalms/69#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/69#v24)</sup> Pour out thine indignation upon them, and let thy wrathful anger take hold of them.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/69#v25)</sup> Let their habitation be desolate; and let none dwell in their tents.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/69#v26)</sup> For they persecute him whom thou hast smitten; and they talk to the grief of those whom thou hast wounded.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/69#v27)</sup> Add iniquity unto their iniquity: and let them not come into thy righteousness.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/69#v28)</sup> Let them be blotted out of the book of the living, and not be written with the righteous.

- Pour out Thine indignation upon them, and let Thy wrathful anger take hold of them; let their habitation be desolate.
- This is why blindness happened to the southern Kingdom leadership that forsook Christ; their eyes were darkened and their table became a snare. You read about all this in [Romans 11](/bible/romans/11).
- Add iniquity unto their iniquity, and let them not come into Thy righteousness; let them be blotted out of the book of the living and not be written with the righteous. That is a heavy statement.
  - **[Revelation 20:12,15](/bible/revelation/20#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/revelation/20#v12)</sup> And I saw the dead, small and great, stand before God; and the books were opened: and another book was opened, which is the book of life: and the dead were judged out of those things which were written in the books, according to their works.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/revelation/20#v15)</sup> And whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

    The dead were judged out of the books according to their works, and whosoever was not found written in the book of life was cast into the lake of fire.

**[Psalms 69:29-33](/bible/psalms/69#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/69#v29)</sup> But I am poor and sorrowful: let thy salvation, O God, set me up on high.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/69#v30)</sup> I will praise the name of God with a song, and will magnify him with thanksgiving.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/69#v31)</sup> This also shall please the Lord better than an ox or bullock that hath horns and hoofs.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/69#v32)</sup> The humble shall see this, and be glad: and your heart shall live that seek God.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/69#v33)</sup> For the Lord heareth the poor, and despiseth not his prisoners.

- But I am poor and sorrowful; let Thy salvation, O God, set me up on high. I will praise the name of God with a song and magnify Him with thanksgiving.
- This shall please the Lord better than an ox or bullock, because He loves obedience more than sacrifice. The humble shall see this and be glad, for the Lord heareth the poor and despiseth not His prisoners.

**[Psalms 69:34-36](/bible/psalms/69#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/69#v34)</sup> Let the heaven and earth praise him, the seas, and every thing that moveth therein.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/69#v35)</sup> For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Judah: that they may dwell there, and have it in possession.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/69#v36)</sup> The seed also of his servants shall inherit it: and they that love his name shall dwell therein.

- Let the heaven and earth praise Him, the seas and everything that moveth therein. When you read the Three Holy Children, all the creation praises God.
- For God will save Zion and will build the cities of Judah, that they may dwell there and have it in possession. Only the oppressed need to be saved, and that is us.
- The seed of His servants shall inherit it, and they that love His name shall dwell therein. Those that keep the commandments are going to dwell there.
  - **[1 John 5:3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-john/5#v3)</sup> For this is the love of God, that we keep his commandments: and his commandments are not grievous.

    This is the love of God, that we keep His commandments, and His commandments are not grievous.
