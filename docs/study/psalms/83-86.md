Read the chapter → [Psalms 83](/bible/psalms/83)

This session reads through [Psalms 83](/bible/psalms/83) through 86, four chapters a day for the nation. The teaching moves through the psalms mostly by reading, with brief explanation where needed. [Psalm 83](/bible/psalms/83) shows that God has enemies, and the chapter is about to name God's enemies right now. It is a prayer that the Most High keep not silence while the heathen take crafty counsel against His people, the Israelites, His hidden ones, saying come let us cut them off from being a nation.

**[Psalms 83:1-4](/bible/psalms/83#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/83#v1)</sup> Keep not thou silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/83#v2)</sup> For, lo, thine enemies make a tumult: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/83#v3)</sup> They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy hidden ones.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/83#v4)</sup> They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.

- The prayer opens asking the Most High to keep not silence, hold not His peace, and be not still, for His enemies make a tumult and they that hate Him have lifted up the head. This shows that God has enemies, and the chapter is about to name God's enemies right now.
- They have taken crafty counsel against His people, against us the Israelites, and consulted against His hidden ones, saying come let us cut them off from being a nation, that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.
- This is why today, nowadays, we are separated from our history, according to [Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5).
  - **[Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.

    The curse on trusting in man is tied to Israel being cut off and separated from their history.

**[Psalms 83:5-8](/bible/psalms/83#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/83#v5)</sup> For they have consulted together with one consent: they are confederate against thee:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/83#v6)</sup> The tabernacles of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/83#v7)</sup> Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/83#v8)</sup> Assur also is joined with them: they have holpen the children of Lot. Selah.

- Here the enemies of God begin to be named. They have consulted together with one consent and are confederate against the Most High: the tabernacles of Edom and the Ishmaelites, of Moab and the Hagarenes.
- Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek, the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre; Assur also is joined with them, and they have holpen the children of Lot.

**[Psalms 83:9-12](/bible/psalms/83#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/83#v9)</sup> Do unto them as unto the Midianites; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook of Kison:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/83#v10)</sup> Which perished at En–dor: they became as dung for the earth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/83#v11)</sup> Make their nobles like Oreb, and like Zeeb: yea, all their princes as Zebah, and as Zalmunna:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/83#v12)</sup> Who said, Let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.

- David asks the Most High to do unto these enemies as unto the Midianites, as to Sisera and as to Jabin at the brook of Kison, who perished at Endor and became as dung for the earth.
- He asks that their nobles be made like Oreb and like Zeeb, all their princes as Zebah and as Zalmunna, who said let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.

**[Psalms 83:13-18](/bible/psalms/83#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/83#v13)</sup> O my God, make them like a wheel; as the stubble before the wind.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/83#v14)</sup> As the fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/83#v15)</sup> So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/83#v16)</sup> Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek thy name, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/83#v17)</sup> Let them be confounded and troubled for ever; yea, let them be put to shame, and perish:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/83#v18)</sup> That men may know that thou, whose name alone is Jehovah, art the most high over all the earth.

- He asks the Most High to make them like a wheel, as the stubble before the wind; as the fire burneth a wood and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire, so to persecute them with His tempest and make them afraid with His storm.
- The aim of the prayer is that their faces be filled with shame, that they may seek His name; that men may know that He whose name alone is JEHOVAH is the Most High over all the earth.

# Psalm 84: How Amiable Are Thy Tabernacles

Read the chapter → [Psalms 84](/bible/psalms/84)

[Psalm 84](/bible/psalms/84) is a longing for the courts of the Most High. The soul faints for the courts of the Lord, and the heart and flesh cry out for the living God. Blessed are they that dwell in His house and are still praising Him; a day in His courts is better than a thousand elsewhere.

**[Psalms 84:1-4](/bible/psalms/84#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/84#v1)</sup> How amiable are thy tabernacles, O Lord of hosts!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/84#v2)</sup> My soul longeth, yea, even fainteth for the courts of the Lord: my heart and my flesh crieth out for the living God.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/84#v3)</sup> Yea, the sparrow hath found an house, and the swallow a nest for herself, where she may lay her young, even thine altars, O Lord of hosts, my King, and my God.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/84#v4)</sup> Blessed are they that dwell in thy house: they will be still praising thee. Selah.

- How amiable are the tabernacles of the Lord of hosts; the soul longeth, yea even fainteth for the courts of the Lord, and the heart and flesh cry out for the living God.
- Even the sparrow has found a house and the swallow a nest for herself where she may lay her young, even the altars of the Lord of hosts, the King and God; blessed are they that dwell in His house and are still praising Him.

**[Psalms 84:5-8](/bible/psalms/84#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/84#v5)</sup> Blessed is the man whose strength is in thee; in whose heart are the ways of them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/84#v6)</sup> Who passing through the valley of Baca make it a well; the rain also filleth the pools.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/84#v7)</sup> They go from strength to strength, every one of them in Zion appeareth before God.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/84#v8)</sup> O Lord God of hosts, hear my prayer: give ear, O God of Jacob. Selah.

- Blessed is the man whose strength is in the Most High, in whose heart are the ways of them who, passing through the valley of Baca, make it a well, and the rain also filleth the pools.
- They go from strength to strength; every one of them in Zion appeareth before God. David asks the Lord God of hosts to hear his prayer and to give ear, O God of Jacob.

**[Psalms 84:9-12](/bible/psalms/84#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/84#v9)</sup> Behold, O God our shield, and look upon the face of thine anointed.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/84#v10)</sup> For a day in thy courts is better than a thousand. I had rather be a doorkeeper in the house of my God, than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/84#v11)</sup> For the Lord God is a sun and shield: the Lord will give grace and glory: no good thing will he withhold from them that walk uprightly.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/84#v12)</sup> O Lord of hosts, blessed is the man that trusteth in thee.

- David asks the Most High, their shield, to look upon the face of His anointed, for a day in His courts is better than a thousand; he would rather be a doorkeeper in the house of his God than to dwell in the tents of wickedness.
- For the Lord God is a sun and shield; He will give grace and glory, and no good thing will He withhold from them that walk uprightly. Blessed is the man that trusteth in Him.

# Psalm 85: Turn Us, O God of Our Salvation

Read the chapter → [Psalms 85](/bible/psalms/85)

[Psalm 85](/bible/psalms/85) remembers how the Most High has been favorable to His land and brought back the captivity of Jacob, forgiving the iniquity of the people. It asks Him to turn His people again and cause His anger to cease, that mercy and truth may meet together and righteousness and peace kiss each other.

**[Psalms 85:1-4](/bible/psalms/85#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/85#v1)</sup> Lord, thou hast been favourable unto thy land: thou hast brought back the captivity of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/85#v2)</sup> Thou hast forgiven the iniquity of thy people, thou hast covered all their sin. Selah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/85#v3)</sup> Thou hast taken away all thy wrath: thou hast turned thyself from the fierceness of thine anger.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/85#v4)</sup> Turn us, O God of our salvation, and cause thine anger toward us to cease.

- The Most High has been favorable unto His land, has brought back the captivity of Jacob, forgiven the iniquity of the people, and covered all their sin.
- He has taken away all His wrath and turned Himself from the fierceness of His anger; David asks Him to turn His people again, O God of their salvation, and cause His anger toward them to cease.

**[Psalms 85:5-8](/bible/psalms/85#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/85#v5)</sup> Wilt thou be angry with us for ever? wilt thou draw out thine anger to all generations?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/85#v6)</sup> Wilt thou not revive us again: that thy people may rejoice in thee?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/85#v7)</sup> Shew us thy mercy, O Lord, and grant us thy salvation.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/85#v8)</sup> I will hear what God the Lord will speak: for he will speak peace unto his people, and to his saints: but let them not turn again to folly.

- The prayer asks whether the Most High will be angry forever and draw out His anger to all generations, and pleads that He revive His people again that they may rejoice in Him, showing them His mercy and granting His salvation.
- David will hear what God the Lord will speak, for He will speak peace unto His people and to His saints; but let them not turn again to folly.

**[Psalms 85:9-13](/bible/psalms/85#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/85#v9)</sup> Surely his salvation is nigh them that fear him; that glory may dwell in our land.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/85#v10)</sup> Mercy and truth are met together; righteousness and peace have kissed each other.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/85#v11)</sup> Truth shall spring out of the earth; and righteousness shall look down from heaven.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/85#v12)</sup> Yea, the Lord shall give that which is good; and our land shall yield her increase.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/85#v13)</sup> Righteousness shall go before him; and shall set us in the way of his steps.

- Surely His salvation is nigh them that fear Him, that glory may dwell in the land; mercy and truth are met together, righteousness and peace have kissed each other.
- Truth shall spring out of the earth and righteousness shall look down from heaven; the Lord shall give that which is good and the land shall yield her increase, and righteousness shall go before Him and set us in the way of His steps.

# Psalm 86: Bow Down Thine Ear, O Lord

Read the chapter → [Psalms 86](/bible/psalms/86)

[Psalm 86](/bible/psalms/86) is a prayer of David, poor and needy, calling on the Most High daily because He is good and ready to forgive and plenteous in mercy. Among the gods there is none like unto Him, and all nations whom He has made shall come and worship before Him.

**[Psalms 86:1-5](/bible/psalms/86#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/86#v1)</sup> Bow down thine ear, O Lord, hear me: for I am poor and needy.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/86#v2)</sup> Preserve my soul; for I am holy: O thou my God, save thy servant that trusteth in thee.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/86#v3)</sup> Be merciful unto me, O Lord: for I cry unto thee daily.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/86#v4)</sup> Rejoice the soul of thy servant: for unto thee, O Lord, do I lift up my soul.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/86#v5)</sup> For thou, Lord, art good, and ready to forgive; and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon thee.

- David asks the Most High to bow down His ear and hear him, for he is poor and needy, and to preserve his soul, for he is holy; to save His servant that trusteth in Him and be merciful, for he cries daily.
- He asks the Most High to rejoice the soul of His servant, for unto Him David lifts up his soul; for the Lord is good and ready to forgive, and plenteous in mercy unto all them that call upon Him.

**[Psalms 86:6-10](/bible/psalms/86#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/86#v6)</sup> Give ear, O Lord, unto my prayer; and attend to the voice of my supplications.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/86#v7)</sup> In the day of my trouble I will call upon thee: for thou wilt answer me.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/86#v8)</sup> Among the gods there is none like unto thee, O Lord; neither are there any works like unto thy works.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/86#v9)</sup> All nations whom thou hast made shall come and worship before thee, O Lord; and shall glorify thy name.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/86#v10)</sup> For thou art great, and doest wondrous things: thou art God alone.

- David asks the Most High to give ear to his prayer and attend to the voice of his supplications; in the day of his trouble he will call upon Him, for He will answer him.
- Among the gods there is none like unto Him, neither are there any works like His works; all nations whom He has made shall come and worship before Him and glorify His name, for He is great and doeth wondrous things, He is God alone.

**[Psalms 86:11-13](/bible/psalms/86#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/86#v11)</sup> Teach me thy way, O Lord; I will walk in thy truth: unite my heart to fear thy name.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/86#v12)</sup> I will praise thee, O Lord my God, with all my heart: and I will glorify thy name for evermore.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/86#v13)</sup> For great is thy mercy toward me: and thou hast delivered my soul from the lowest hell.

- David asks the Most High to teach him His way, that he may walk in His truth, and to unite his heart to fear His name; he will praise Him with all his heart and glorify His name forevermore.
- Great is His mercy toward David, and He has delivered his soul from the lowest hell.

**[Psalms 86:14-17](/bible/psalms/86#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/86#v14)</sup> O God, the proud are risen against me, and the assemblies of violent men have sought after my soul; and have not set thee before them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/86#v15)</sup> But thou, O Lord, art a God full of compassion, and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/86#v16)</sup> O turn unto me, and have mercy upon me; give thy strength unto thy servant, and save the son of thine handmaid.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/86#v17)</sup> Shew me a token for good; that they which hate me may see it, and be ashamed: because thou, Lord, hast holpen me, and comforted me.

- The proud are risen against David and the assemblies of violent men have sought after his soul and have not set the Most High before them; but He is a God full of compassion and gracious, longsuffering, and plenteous in mercy and truth.
- David asks the Most High to turn unto him and have mercy, to give His strength unto His servant and save the son of His handmaid, and to show him a token for good, that they which hate Him may see it and be ashamed, because the Lord has holpen and comforted him.
