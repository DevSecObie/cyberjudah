This session covers [Psalms 7](/bible/psalms/7) through 10, prayers of King David that read as prophecy for the Israelites in these last days. David was a prophet, so his cries are not only about his own time but about the persecution God's people endure now: mass incarceration, medical apartheid, the coronavirus, and the confederate nations arrayed against Israel. The thread throughout is faith and repentance: pray to the Most High, get your house in order, and trust that God is just, that he will avenge the blood of the saints and destroy the wicked nations while establishing the righteous.

**[Psalm 7:1-2](/bible/psalms/7#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/7#v1)</sup> O Lord my God, in thee do I put my trust: save me from all them that persecute me, and deliver me:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/7#v2)</sup> Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it in pieces, while there is none to deliver.

- David is a mighty warrior, so it sounds strange that he pleads to be saved from those who persecute him. The answer is that David was a prophet. He was not only speaking of his own time; he was prophesying for us in these last days, which means David is on the earth today.
- This is our prayer too: that the Most High will save us from the hand of those who persecute us and want to come against us.
- "Rent my soul in pieces" is how the other nations try to take advantage of us. The coronavirus was set up to rend our souls in pieces, mass incarceration was set up to rend our souls in pieces, medical apartheid was set up to rend our souls in pieces.
- The 1619 project laid out what the so-called blacks (Judah, Benjamin, and Levi) have endured in this country: mass incarceration, inequality in medicine, being used as lab rats and guinea pigs like Henrietta Lacks and the Tuskegee institute, Black Wall Street bombed, Emmett Till's death. A senator, Tom Cotton of Arkansas, wants it banned. Ask yourself why somebody would hate the history of 1619 so much.

**[Psalm 7:3-5](/bible/psalms/7#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/7#v3)</sup> O Lord my God, if I have done this; if there be iniquity in my hands;

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/7#v4)</sup> If I have rewarded evil unto him that was at peace with me; (yea, I have delivered him that without cause is mine enemy:)

If he is breaking the law, then getting arrested is just; if he is selling drugs to his people, then it is just a matter of time before a rival riddles his trap with bullets. If we are wicked, the evil happening to us is just.
- The hidden ones are the key. When you learn your true identity you change your life: less pornography, less marijuana, less going to the doctor, less mass incarceration, no eating pork, no celebrating Christmas or the fourth of July. That repentance is where your power lies, and that is why they work to keep you asleep.

## Psalm 7 (continued): God Angry With the Wicked Every Day
