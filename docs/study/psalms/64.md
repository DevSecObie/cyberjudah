Read the chapter → [Psalms 64](/bible/psalms/64)

[Psalm 64](/bible/psalms/64) is a prayer to be preserved from the fear of the enemy and hidden from the secret counsel of the wicked. It sets out the workings of the wicked who whet their tongue like a sword and shoot bitter words in secret, and it declares that the Most High shall shoot at them with an arrow so that their own tongue falls upon themselves.

**[Psalms 64:1-2](/bible/psalms/64#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/64#v1)</sup> Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/64#v2)</sup> Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:

- David asks the Most High to hear his voice in prayer and preserve his life from fear of the enemy.
- He asks to be hidden from the secret counsel of the wicked and from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity.

**[Psalms 64:3-6](/bible/psalms/64#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/64#v3)</sup> Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/64#v4)</sup> That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/64#v5)</sup> They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/64#v6)</sup> They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.

- The wicked whet their tongue like a sword and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words, that they may shoot in secret at the perfect and suddenly, without fear.
- They encourage themselves in an evil matter, commune of laying snares privily, and say who shall see them; they search out iniquities and accomplish a diligent search, and the inward thought and heart of every one of them is deep.

**[Psalms 64:7-10](/bible/psalms/64#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/64#v7)</sup> But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/64#v8)</sup> So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/64#v9)</sup> And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/64#v10)</sup> The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.

- But the Most High shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they shall be wounded, and their own tongue shall fall upon themselves, so all that see them flee away.
- All men shall declare the work of the Most High and wisely consider His doing; the righteous shall be glad in the Most High, trust in Him, and all the upright in heart shall glory.
