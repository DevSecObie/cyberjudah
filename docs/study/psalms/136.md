**[Psalm 136:1-13](/bible/psalms/136#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/136#v1)</sup> O give thanks unto the Lord; for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/136#v2)</sup> O give thanks unto the God of gods: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/136#v3)</sup> O give thanks to the Lord of lords: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/136#v4)</sup> To him who alone doeth great wonders: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/136#v5)</sup> To him that by wisdom made the heavens: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/136#v6)</sup> To him that stretched out the earth above the waters: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/136#v7)</sup> To him that made great lights: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/136#v8)</sup> The sun to rule by day: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/136#v9)</sup> The moon and stars to rule by night: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/136#v10)</sup> To him that smote Egypt in their firstborn: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/136#v11)</sup> And brought out Israel from among them: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/136#v12)</sup> With a strong hand, and with a stretched out arm: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/136#v13)</sup> To him which divided the Red sea into parts: for his mercy endureth for ever:

**[Psalm 136:14-26](/bible/psalms/136#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/136#v14)</sup> And made Israel to pass through the midst of it: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/136#v15)</sup> But overthrew Pharaoh and his host in the Red sea: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/136#v16)</sup> To him which led his people through the wilderness: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/136#v17)</sup> To him which smote great kings: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/136#v18)</sup> And slew famous kings: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/136#v19)</sup> Sihon king of the Amorites: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/136#v20)</sup> And Og the king of Bashan: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/136#v21)</sup> And gave their land for an heritage: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/136#v22)</sup> Even an heritage unto Israel his servant: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/136#v23)</sup> Who remembered us in our low estate: for his mercy endureth for ever:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/136#v24)</sup> And hath redeemed us from our enemies: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/136#v25)</sup> Who giveth food to all flesh: for his mercy endureth for ever.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/136#v26)</sup> O give thanks unto the God of heaven: for his mercy endureth for ever.
