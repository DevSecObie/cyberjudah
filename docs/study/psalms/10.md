**[Psalm 10:1-2](/bible/psalms/10#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/10#v1)</sup> Why standest thou afar off, O Lord? why hidest thou thyself in times of trouble?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/10#v2)</sup> The wicked in his pride doth persecute the poor: let them be taken in the devices that they have imagined.

- David asks why the Lord stands far off and hides in times of trouble, while the wicked in his pride persecutes the poor. He prays that the wicked be taken in the devices they imagined.
- They tried to use the coronavirus to persecute the poor of our people who lack good health insurance and to kill off our brothers and sisters in Africa, but the Lord turned it against the other nations, and many of them are being put to death by the disease.

**[Psalm 10:3-6](/bible/psalms/10#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/10#v3)</sup> For the wicked boasteth of his heart’s desire, and blesseth the covetous, whom the Lord abhorreth.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/10#v4)</sup> The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/10#v5)</sup> His ways are always grievous; thy judgments are far above out of his sight: as for all his enemies, he puffeth at them.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/10#v6)</sup> He hath said in his heart, I shall not be moved: for I shall never be in adversity.

- The wicked boasts of his heart's desire and blesses the covetous whom the Lord abhors. There are Israelite coons who take money from the so-called white man to come on television and tell you to take fifteen hundred dollars and a vaccine; work out your own salvation with fear and trembling, but know they use entertainers, rappers, and black politicians to influence black people for a reason.
- Through the pride of his countenance the wicked will not seek after God; God is not in his thoughts. He is thinking about money and how to destroy the Israelites and keep his kingdom going, not knowing he is only fulfilling the prophecy God said he would.
- He says in his heart, "I shall never be in adversity." This is Esau saying his kingdom will last forever, naming America and Africa and the European countries after his ancestors.

**[Psalm 10:7-10](/bible/psalms/10#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/10#v7)</sup> His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and vanity.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/10#v8)</sup> He sitteth in the lurking places of the villages: in the secret places doth he murder the innocent: his eyes are privily set against the poor.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/10#v9)</sup> He lieth in wait secretly as a lion in his den: he lieth in wait to catch the poor: he doth catch the poor, when he draweth him into his net.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/10#v10)</sup> He croucheth, and humbleth himself, that the poor may fall by his strong ones.

- His mouth is full of cursing, deceit, and fraud; he sits in the lurking places of the villages to murder the innocent, his eyes privily set against the poor. Some officers sit off in the corner just trying to catch you.

**[Psalm 10:11-14](/bible/psalms/10#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/10#v11)</sup> He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten: he hideth his face; he will never see it.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/10#v12)</sup> Arise, O Lord; O God, lift up thine hand: forget not the humble.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/10#v13)</sup> Wherefore doth the wicked contemn God? he hath said in his heart, Thou wilt not require it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/10#v14)</sup> Thou hast seen it; for thou beholdest mischief and spite, to requite it with thy hand: the poor committeth himself unto thee; thou art the helper of the fatherless.

- The wicked says in his heart that God has forgotten and hidden his face and will never see it, so he thinks God is not remembering what he does to our people. David prays the Lord to arise and lift up his hand, and not forget the humble.
- God has seen the mischief and spite and will requite it with his hand. The poor commits himself to the Lord, who is the help of the fatherless; keep the laws of God and the Lord will redeem the poor, who are the Israelites. Just hold the line and stand strong.
