This session moves through [Psalms 103](/bible/psalms/103) through 106, four psalms of remembrance and praise. It opens with David blessing the Most High for His mercy toward Israel, moves through the Most High's care over creation in [Psalm 104](/bible/psalms/104), and then recounts the nation's history from Abraham through the wilderness in [Psalms 105](/bible/psalms/105) and 106. The thread throughout is that the mercy, grace, and covenant of the Most High belong to Israel, and that this mercy is not a license to sin but a call to keep His commandments. Christianity is a curse ([Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)); the grace of the Most High was given so that when the Holy Ghost reminds us of His commandments, we apply them to our lives.

**[Psalms 103:1-6](/bible/psalms/103#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/103#v1)</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul: and all that is within me, bless his holy name.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/103#v2)</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/103#v3)</sup> Who forgiveth all thine iniquities; who healeth all thy diseases;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/103#v4)</sup> Who redeemeth thy life from destruction; who crowneth thee with lovingkindness and tender mercies;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/103#v5)</sup> Who satisfieth thy mouth with good things; so that thy youth is renewed like the eagle’s.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/103#v6)</sup> The Lord executeth righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed.

- David charges his own soul and all that is within him to bless the holy name of the Most High and to forget not His benefits.
- Those benefits are named: He forgives all iniquities, heals diseases, redeems life from destruction, crowns with lovingkindness and tender mercies, and renews the youth like the eagle's.
- The Most High executes righteousness and judgment for all that are oppressed. Hear that clearly: it does not say for all people, it says for all that are oppressed.

**[Psalms 103:7](/bible/psalms/103#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/103#v7)</sup> He made known his ways unto Moses, his acts unto the children of Israel.

- He made known His ways unto Moses, His acts unto the children of Israel. Look at that: His acts unto the children of Israel, not to every nation.
- When Israel fled Egypt there were oppressed nations who came out with us; the Bible calls them strangers. But the ways of the Most High were made known to Moses and Israel.
  - **[Psalms 147:19-20](/bible/psalms/147#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/147#v19)</sup> He sheweth his word unto Jacob, his statutes and his judgments unto Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/147#v20)</sup> He hath not dealt so with any nation: and as for his judgments, they have not known them. Praise ye the Lord.

    He shows His word and statutes to Jacob and Israel; He has not dealt so with any other nation.

**[Psalms 103:8-14](/bible/psalms/103#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/103#v8)</sup> The Lord is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, and plenteous in mercy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/103#v9)</sup> He will not always chide: neither will he keep his anger for ever.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/103#v10)</sup> He hath not dealt with us after our sins; nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/103#v11)</sup> For as the heaven is high above the earth, so great is his mercy toward them that fear him.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/103#v12)</sup> As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/103#v13)</sup> Like as a father pitieth his children, so the Lord pitieth them that fear him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/103#v14)</sup> For he knoweth our frame; he remembereth that we are dust.

- The Most High is merciful and gracious, slow to anger, plenteous in mercy. He has not dealt with us after our sins nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.
- This is heavy because Christ came to forgive Israel of their sins. That mercy and grace is to Israel, and it is not so that we could continue in sin.
- As far as the east is from the west, so far He removes our transgressions; He pities them that fear Him, for He knows we are dust.

    He shall save His people from their sins, and His people are the Israelites.
  - **[Acts 15:15](/bible/acts/15#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/acts/15#v15)</sup> And to this agree the words of the prophets; as it is written,

    To this agree the words of the prophets; David the prophet and Ezra the prophet say the same thing.

    Ezra says the Most High punished us less than our iniquities deserve, agreeing with David.
  - **[Acts 13:38-39](/bible/acts/13#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/acts/13#v38)</sup> Be it known unto you therefore, men and brethren, that through this man is preached unto you the forgiveness of sins:
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/acts/13#v39)</sup> And by him all that believe are justified from all things, from which ye could not be justified by the law of Moses.

    Through Christ is preached forgiveness, justifying believers from things the law of Moses could not.

**[Psalms 103:15-18](/bible/psalms/103#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/103#v15)</sup> As for man, his days are as grass: as a flower of the field, so he flourisheth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/103#v16)</sup> For the wind passeth over it, and it is gone; and the place thereof shall know it no more.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/103#v17)</sup> But the mercy of the Lord is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear him, and his righteousness unto children’s children;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/103#v18)</sup> To such as keep his covenant, and to those that remember his commandments to do them.

- Man's days are as grass and as a flower of the field; the wind passes over it and it is gone.
- But the mercy of the Most High is from everlasting to everlasting upon them that fear Him, and His righteousness unto children's children.
- This is why we must teach our children in every generation, so they receive that same mercy.
- The covenant is to such as keep His commandments and remember them to do them. To do them is an action word.
  - **[Deuteronomy 6:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v6)</sup> And these words, which I command thee this day, shall be in thine heart:
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v7)</sup> And thou shalt teach them diligently unto thy children, and shalt talk of them when thou sittest in thine house, and when thou walkest by the way, and when thou liest down, and when thou risest up.

    We are commanded to teach our children the words of the Most High diligently.
  - **[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

    The Most High is a God of knowledge, and by Him actions are weighed.
  - **[James 1:22](/bible/james/1#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/james/1#v22)</sup> But be ye doers of the word, and not hearers only, deceiving your own selves.

    Be doers of the word and not hearers only.

**[Psalms 103:19-22](/bible/psalms/103#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/103#v19)</sup> The Lord hath prepared his throne in the heavens; and his kingdom ruleth over all.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/103#v20)</sup> Bless the Lord, ye his angels, that excel in strength, that do his commandments, hearkening unto the voice of his word.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/103#v21)</sup> Bless ye the Lord, all ye his hosts; ye ministers of his, that do his pleasure.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/103#v22)</sup> Bless the Lord, all his works in all places of his dominion: bless the Lord, O my soul.

- The Most High has prepared His throne in the heavens and His kingdom rules over all.
- The angels that excel in strength bless Him by doing His commandments and hearkening to the voice of His word.
- All His hosts and works in every place of His dominion bless Him; and David returns to charge his own soul: bless the Lord.

# Psalm 104: The Most High Clothed with Majesty over Creation

Read the chapter → [Psalms 104](/bible/psalms/104)

**[Psalms 104:1-4](/bible/psalms/104#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/104#v1)</sup> Bless the Lord, O my soul. O Lord my God, thou art very great; thou art clothed with honour and majesty.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/104#v2)</sup> Who coverest thyself with light as with a garment: who stretchest out the heavens like a curtain:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/104#v3)</sup> Who layeth the beams of his chambers in the waters: who maketh the clouds his chariot: who walketh upon the wings of the wind:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/104#v4)</sup> Who maketh his angels spirits; his ministers a flaming fire:

- David blesses the Most High, who is clothed with honor and majesty and covers Himself with light as with a garment.
- He stretches out the heavens like a curtain, makes the clouds His chariot, and walks upon the wings of the wind.
- He makes His angels spirits and His ministers a flaming fire.
  - **[Genesis 1:14](/bible/genesis/1#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/1#v14)</sup> And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years:

    The lights of heaven are for signs and seasons, days and years.

**[Psalms 104:5-9](/bible/psalms/104#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/104#v5)</sup> Who laid the foundations of the earth, that it should not be removed for ever.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/104#v6)</sup> Thou coveredst it with the deep as with a garment: the waters stood above the mountains.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/104#v7)</sup> At thy rebuke they fled; at the voice of thy thunder they hasted away.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/104#v8)</sup> They go up by the mountains; they go down by the valleys unto the place which thou hast founded for them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/104#v9)</sup> Thou hast set a bound that they may not pass over; that they turn not again to cover the earth.

- He laid the foundations of the earth so it should not be removed forever.
- The waters once stood above the mountains, but at His rebuke they fled and were set within bounds they may not pass.
- Consider that little crystals of sand are set as a bound to hold back the waves of the sea; that is the genius and magnificence of the Most High.

**[Psalms 104:10-12](/bible/psalms/104#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/104#v10)</sup> He sendeth the springs into the valleys, which run among the hills.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/104#v11)</sup> They give drink to every beast of the field: the wild asses quench their thirst.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/104#v12)</sup> By them shall the fowls of the heaven have their habitation, which sing among the branches.

- He sends springs into the valleys to give drink to every beast of the field, and the wild asses quench their thirst.
- The fowls of the heaven have their habitation by them and sing among the branches.
- In the country the owls and birds sing before the sun rises to wake you; that is the joy of the Most High.

**[Psalms 104:13-15](/bible/psalms/104#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/104#v13)</sup> He watereth the hills from his chambers: the earth is satisfied with the fruit of thy works.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/104#v14)</sup> He causeth the grass to grow for the cattle, and herb for the service of man: that he may bring forth food out of the earth;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/104#v15)</sup> And wine that maketh glad the heart of man, and oil to make his face to shine, and bread which strengtheneth man’s heart.

- He waters the hills from His chambers and causes the grass to grow for the cattle and herb for the service of man, to bring forth food out of the earth.
- Note it carefully: herb for the service of man means food, not herb to smoke. Just as at Passover we eat lamb and bitter herbs, we do not smoke them.
- Wine makes glad the heart of man and oil makes his face to shine. This shows we are dark-skinned people, for oil is put on dry skin to make it shine, and the scriptures describe us.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 31:27-29](/bible/sirach/31#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/31#v27)</sup> Wine is as good as life to a man, if it be drunk moderately: what life is then to a man that is without wine? for it was made to make men glad.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/31#v28)</sup> Wine measurably drunk and in season bringeth gladness of the heart, and cheerfulness of the mind:
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/31#v29)</sup> But wine drunken with excess maketh bitterness of the mind, with brawling and quarrelling.

    Wine drunk moderately is as good as life and makes men glad, but with excess it brings bitterness.

**[Psalms 104:16-23](/bible/psalms/104#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/104#v16)</sup> The trees of the Lord are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon, which he hath planted;
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/104#v17)</sup> Where the birds make their nests: as for the stork, the fir trees are her house.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/104#v18)</sup> The high hills are a refuge for the wild goats; and the rocks for the conies.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/104#v19)</sup> He appointed the moon for seasons: the sun knoweth his going down.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/104#v20)</sup> Thou makest darkness, and it is night: wherein all the beasts of the forest do creep forth.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/104#v21)</sup> The young lions roar after their prey, and seek their meat from God.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/104#v22)</sup> The sun ariseth, they gather themselves together, and lay them down in their dens.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/104#v23)</sup> Man goeth forth unto his work and to his labour until the evening.

- The trees of the Most High are full of sap; the cedars of Lebanon are where the birds make their nests, and the high hills are a refuge for the wild goats.
- He appointed the moon for seasons and the sun knows its going down.
- He makes darkness and it is night, wherein the beasts of the forest and the young lions seek their meat from God; the night belongs to the beasts, the day belongs to man who goes to his labor until evening.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 43:6-7](/bible/sirach/43#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/43#v6)</sup> He made the moon also to serve in her season for a declaration of times, and a sign of the world.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/43#v7)</sup> From the moon is the sign of feasts, a light that decreaseth in her perfection.

    The moon marks the seasons and the appointed feasts.

**[Psalms 104:24-30](/bible/psalms/104#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/104#v24)</sup> O Lord, how manifold are thy works! in wisdom hast thou made them all: the earth is full of thy riches.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/104#v25)</sup> So is this great and wide sea, wherein are things creeping innumerable, both small and great beasts.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/104#v26)</sup> There go the ships: there is that leviathan, whom thou hast made to play therein.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/104#v27)</sup> These wait all upon thee; that thou mayest give them their meat in due season.
>

>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/104#v29)</sup> Thou hidest thy face, they are troubled: thou takest away their breath, they die, and return to their dust.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/104#v30)</sup> Thou sendest forth thy spirit, they are created: and thou renewest the face of the earth.

- How manifold are His works; in wisdom He made them all, and the earth is full of His riches, including the great and wide sea teeming with creatures.
- All creatures wait upon Him for their meat in due season; when He opens His hand they are filled, when He hides His face they are troubled.
- When He takes away their breath they die and return to their dust; when He sends forth His spirit they are created and He renews the face of the earth.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 3:19-20](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v19)</sup> For that which befalleth the sons of men befalleth beasts; even one thing befalleth them: as the one dieth, so dieth the other; yea, they have all one breath; so that a man hath no preeminence above a beast: for all is vanity.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v20)</sup> All go unto one place; all are of the dust, and all turn to dust again.

    Man and beast have one departure; the body returns to dust, but our spirit returns to the Most High.

**[Psalms 104:31-35](/bible/psalms/104#v31)**
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/104#v31)</sup> The glory of the Lord shall endure for ever: the Lord shall rejoice in his works.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/psalms/104#v32)</sup> He looketh on the earth, and it trembleth: he toucheth the hills, and they smoke.

> <sup>[33](/bible/psalms/104#v33)</sup> I will sing unto the Lord as long as I live: I will sing praise to my God while I have my being.

> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/104#v34)</sup> My meditation of him shall be sweet: I will be glad in the Lord.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/104#v35)</sup> Let the sinners be consumed out of the earth, and let the wicked be no more. Bless thou the Lord, O my soul. Praise ye the Lord.

- David resolves to sing unto the Most High as long as he lives, and his meditation of Him shall be sweet.
- He prays the wicked be consumed from the earth, and again charges his soul to bless the Lord.
  - **[Psalms 137:1-4](/bible/psalms/137#v1)**

    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/137#v3)</sup> For there they that carried us away captive required of us a song; and they that wasted us required of us mirth, saying, Sing us one of the songs of Zion.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/137#v4)</sup> How shall we sing the Lord’s song in a strange land?

    By the rivers of Babylon our captors required a song of Zion, but how shall we sing the Lord's song in a strange land?
  - **[Amos 5:23](/bible/amos/5#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/amos/5#v23)</sup> Take thou away from me the noise of thy songs; for I will not hear the melody of thy viols.

    As Israel repents and returns, righteous songs of praise are rising again in place of the songs of captivity.

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/105#v2)</sup> Sing unto him, sing psalms unto him: talk ye of all his wondrous works.

>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/105#v5)</sup> Remember his marvellous works that he hath done; his wonders, and the judgments of his mouth;

- Give thanks unto the Most High, call upon His name, and make known His deeds among the people.
- Sing unto Him, glory in His holy name, and let the heart of them that seek Him rejoice; seek His face forevermore.
- Remember His marvelous works, His wonders, and the judgments of His mouth. The Bible tells us to remember our past.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

  - **[Jeremiah 6:16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/6#v16)</sup> Thus saith the Lord, Stand ye in the ways, and see, and ask for the old paths, where is the good way, and walk therein, and ye shall find rest for your souls. But they said, We will not walk therein.

    Stand in the ways and ask for the old paths and walk therein; but they said we will not.
