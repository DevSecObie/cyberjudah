Read the chapter → [Psalms 19](/bible/psalms/19)

This session covers [Psalms 19](/bible/psalms/19) through 22, the last four chapters of the day's reading. It opens with the gospel going out to all the earth ([Psalm 19](/bible/psalms/19)), digs deep into a basic precept about the law being perfect and converting the soul, moves through cleansing from secret and presumptuous sins, then into the Lord's deliverance and the destruction of the enemy (Psalms 20-21), and closes with [Psalm 22](/bible/psalms/22), a prophecy fulfilled in Christ on the cross. We picked up out of Ezekiel where we left off before turning back to Psalms.

**[Ezekiel 47:2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/ezekiel/47#v2)</sup> Then brought he me out of the way of the gate northward, and led me about the way without unto the utter gate by the way that looketh eastward; and, behold, there ran out waters on the right side.

- The man with the line went forth eastward and measured, and the waters that ran out on the right side going out from the temple is the gospel going out, the man with the line measuring your understanding of the scriptures.
  - **[Ezekiel 47:3](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/ezekiel/47#v3)</sup> And when the man that had the line in his hand went forth eastward, he measured a thousand cubits, and he brought me through the waters; the waters were to the ankles.

    He measured a thousand cubits and brought me through the waters, and the waters were to the ankles; not only is the gospel going out, your understanding is being measured.

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/19#v4)</sup> Their line is gone out through all the earth, and their words to the end of the world. In them hath he set a tabernacle for the sun,

- The line is gone out through all the earth, the gospel going out to the end of the world, and in them he set a tabernacle for the sun.
  - **[Isaiah 62:5](/bible/isaiah/62#v5)**

    As a young man marrieth a virgin so shall thy sons marry thee, and as the bridegroom rejoiceth over the bride so shall thy God rejoice over thee; the gospel is about us being joined back to Christ.

**[Psalm 19:7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

- The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul; the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple. We hear this scripture all the time, so we are going to dig into it a little while we do these chapters.
- The law of the Lord is perfect means going into perfection with our lives.
- The law of the Lord converting the soul is what changes us. You do not just up and decide to follow the Bible; it takes conversion.
- The testimony of the Lord is sure because the prophecies in this Bible are sure; we have a more sure word of prophecy. Did we not go into slavery on ships like the Most High declared through Moses in [Deuteronomy 28](/bible/deuteronomy/28)? We are living proof that the Bible is real.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 34:8](/bible/sirach/34#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/34#v8)</sup> The law shall be found perfect without lies: and wisdom is perfection to a faithful mouth.

    The law is perfect, taking us into perfection with our lives.
  - **[Jeremiah 13:23](/bible/jeremiah/13#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/13#v23)</sup> Can the Ethiopian change his skin, or the leopard his spots? then may ye also do good, that are accustomed to do evil.

    Can the Ethiopian change his skin or the leopard his spots? The Ethiopians are the dark nations of people; change does not just up and happen, so neither can you do good who are accustomed to do evil.
  - **[2 Esdras 4:2](/bible/2-esdras/4#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-esdras/4#v2)</sup> And said, Thy heart hath gone to far in this world, and thinkest thou to comprehend the way of the most High?

    The angel tells Ezra his mind is gone too far in the world, so his thoughts are not our thoughts.
  - **[Psalm 119:9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/119#v9)</sup> Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? by taking heed thereto according to thy word.

    Wherewithal shall a young man cleanse his way? By taking heed thereto according to thy word; that is how we are converted and changed.
  - **[Matthew 18:3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/18#v3)</sup> And said, Verily I say unto you, Except ye be converted, and become as little children, ye shall not enter into the kingdom of heaven.

    Except ye be converted and become as little children ye shall not enter the kingdom, because a child is teachable and will not argue back when it is time to learn.
  - **[Matthew 18:4](/bible/matthew/18#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/18#v4)</sup> Whosoever therefore shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven.

    Whosoever shall humble himself as this little child, the same is greatest in the kingdom of heaven; we must humble ourselves as little children.

  - **[Isaiah 46:10](/bible/isaiah/46#v10)**

    Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done; that is what prophecy is, and we have a sure word of it.

    Now the Egyptians are men and not God, and their horses flesh and not spirit; the white man is just a man, he is not God, and their army has nothing on God's army.

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/21#v12)</sup> Therefore shalt thou make them turn their back, when thou shalt make ready thine arrows upon thy strings against the face of them.

- Their fruit shalt thou destroy from the earth and their seed from among the children of men; Esau is not going to be no more. They imagined a mischievous device which they are not able to perform; the Most High makes their devices of none effect and makes ready his arrows against the face of them.
  - **[2 Esdras 16:3-6](/bible/2-esdras/16#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-esdras/16#v3)</sup> A sword is sent upon you, and who may turn it back?
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-esdras/16#v4)</sup> A fire is sent among you, and who may quench it?
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/16#v5)</sup> Plagues are sent unto you, and what is he that may drive them away?
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-esdras/16#v6)</sup> May any man drive away an hungry lion in the wood? or may any one quench the fire in stubble, when it hath begun to burn?

    The Lord sends his sword, fire, and plagues, and none can turn them back.
  - **[Zechariah 14:12](/bible/zechariah/14#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/14#v12)</sup> And this shall be the plague wherewith the Lord will smite all the people that have fought against Jerusalem; Their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, and their eyes shall consume away in their holes, and their tongue shall consume away in their mouth.

    This shall be the plague: their flesh shall consume away while they stand upon their feet, their eyes in their holes and their tongues in their mouth; that is a nuclear bomb. We saw it at Hiroshima and Nagasaki, and the weapons have only got more advanced; the Most High is manipulating that weapon so the earth turns into a microwave.

- Be thou exalted, Lord, in thine own strength; so will we sing and praise thy power.

# Psalm 22: The Prophecy of Christ on the Cross

Read the chapter → [Psalms 22](/bible/psalms/22)

This chapter is a prophecy about Christ, familiar to many, so we touch on a few points. It is the last chapter of the four-chapter reading.

**[Psalm 22:1](/bible/psalms/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/22#v1)</sup> My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?

- My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me? Why art thou so far from helping me? This is what Christ said when he was on the cross; Christ did not come to do away with the law but to fulfil it.
  - **[Luke 24:44](/bible/luke/24#v44)**
    > <sup>[44](/bible/luke/24#v44)</sup> And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me.

    All things must be fulfilled which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the Psalms, concerning me; Christ had to fulfil the prophecies written about him.
  - **[Matthew 27:45-46](/bible/matthew/27#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/27#v45)</sup> Now from the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land unto the ninth hour.
    >
    > <sup>[46](/bible/matthew/27#v46)</sup> And about the ninth hour Jesus cried with a loud voice, saying, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani? that is to say, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me?

    From the sixth hour there was darkness over all the land, and about the ninth hour Jesus cried, Eli, Eli, lama sabachthani, that is, My God, my God, why hast thou forsaken me; this was fulfilled on the cross.

**[Psalm 22:2-5](/bible/psalms/22#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/22#v2)</sup> O my God, I cry in the daytime, but thou hearest not; and in the night season, and am not silent.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/22#v3)</sup> But thou art holy, O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/22#v4)</sup> Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted, and thou didst deliver them.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/22#v5)</sup> They cried unto thee, and were delivered: they trusted in thee, and were not confounded.

- I cry in the daytime but thou hearest not, but thou art holy; our fathers trusted in thee and thou didst deliver them; they cried unto thee and were delivered.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:10](/bible/sirach/2#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/2#v10)</sup> Look at the generations of old, and see; did ever any trust in the Lord, and was confounded? or did any abide in his fear, and was forsaken? or whom did he ever despise, that called upon him?

    If you abide in the fear of God you will not be forsaken.

**[Psalm 22:6-8](/bible/psalms/22#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/22#v6)</sup> But I am a worm, and no man; a reproach of men, and despised of the people.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/22#v7)</sup> All they that see me laugh me to scorn: they shoot out the lip, they shake the head, saying,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/22#v8)</sup> He trusted on the Lord that he would deliver him: let him deliver him, seeing he delighted in him.

- But I am a worm and no man, a reproach of men and despised of the people; all they that see me laugh me to scorn, saying, He trusted on the Lord that he would deliver him, let him deliver him. We read that same mockery in Matthew when they said, If he be the Son of God let God deliver him.

**[Psalm 22:9-11](/bible/psalms/22#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/22#v9)</sup> But thou art he that took me out of the womb: thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother’s breasts.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/22#v10)</sup> I was cast upon thee from the womb: thou art my God from my mother’s belly.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/22#v11)</sup> Be not far from me; for trouble is near; for there is none to help.

- But thou art he that took me out of the womb; thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts. Christ was on his mother's breast drinking milk growing up until he was of age, and she taught him the commandments; even filled with the Holy Ghost, [Deuteronomy 6:7](/bible/deuteronomy/6#v7) still applied in their household, which is why at twelve he was in the temple.
  - **[2 Esdras 8:8-12](/bible/2-esdras/8#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-esdras/8#v8)</sup> For when the body is fashioned now in the mother’s womb, and thou givest it members, thy creature is preserved in fire and water, and nine months doth thy workmanship endure thy creature which is created in her.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-esdras/8#v9)</sup> But that which keepeth and is kept shall both be preserved: and when the time cometh, the womb preserved delivereth up the things that grew in it.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-esdras/8#v10)</sup> For thou hast commanded out of the parts of the body, that is to say, out of the breasts, milk to be given, which is the fruit of the breasts,
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/8#v11)</sup> That the thing which is fashioned may be nourished for a time, till thou disposest it to thy mercy.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-esdras/8#v12)</sup> Thou broughtest it up with thy righteousness, and nurturedst it in thy law, and reformedst it with thy judgment.

    You are fed milk until you can talk, then you start learning the commandments.

**[Psalm 22:14-17](/bible/psalms/22#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/22#v14)</sup> I am poured out like water, and all my bones are out of joint: my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/22#v15)</sup> My strength is dried up like a potsherd; and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws; and thou hast brought me into the dust of death.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/22#v16)</sup> For dogs have compassed me: the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me: they pierced my hands and my feet.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/22#v17)</sup> I may tell all my bones: they look and stare upon me.

- I am poured out like water and all my bones are out of joint; my strength is dried up, they pierced my hands and my feet, I may tell all my bones. His soul was made a sin offering; they pierced his hands and feet as they did to Christ, and because he was already dead none of his bones were broken, so the prophecy that he could tell all his bones was fulfilled.
  - **[Isaiah 53:10](/bible/isaiah/53#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/53#v10)</sup> Yet it pleased the Lord to bruise him; he hath put him to grief: when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin, he shall see his seed, he shall prolong his days, and the pleasure of the Lord shall prosper in his hand.

    Yet it pleased the Lord to bruise him, when thou shalt make his soul an offering for sin; his soul was made a sin offering, and he shall see his seed and prolong his days.

**[Psalm 22:18](/bible/psalms/22#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/22#v18)</sup> They part my garments among them, and cast lots upon my vesture.

- They part my garments among them and cast lots upon my vesture; but be not thou far from me, O Lord.

**[Psalm 22:22-24](/bible/psalms/22#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/22#v22)</sup> I will declare thy name unto my brethren: in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/22#v23)</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob, glorify him; and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/22#v24)</sup> For he hath not despised nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted; neither hath he hid his face from him; but when he cried unto him, he heard.

- I will declare thy name unto my brethren; Christ is the one who declared the word of God to us. He did not hold back; he came, preached, and laid down his life. He hath not despised the affliction of the afflicted; when he cried unto him he heard, so the Lord will hear our prayers.

**[Psalm 22:26-28](/bible/psalms/22#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/22#v26)</sup> The meek shall eat and be satisfied: they shall praise the Lord that seek him: your heart shall live for ever.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/22#v27)</sup> All the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the Lord: and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/22#v28)</sup> For the kingdom is the Lord’s: and he is the governor among the nations.

- The meek shall eat and be satisfied; all the ends of the world shall remember and turn unto the Lord, for the kingdom is the Lord's and he is the governor among the nations; that is why the scripture says the government shall be upon his shoulder.

**[Psalm 22:30-31](/bible/psalms/22#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/22#v30)</sup> A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/22#v31)</sup> They shall come, and shall declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this.

- A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation; they shall come and declare his righteousness unto a people that shall be born, that he hath done this. This answers the question Isaiah asked about who shall declare his generation.
  - **[Isaiah 53:1-3](/bible/isaiah/53#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/53#v1)</sup> Who hath believed our report? and to whom is the arm of the Lord revealed?
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/53#v2)</sup> For he shall grow up before him as a tender plant, and as a root out of a dry ground: he hath no form nor comeliness; and when we shall see him, there is no beauty that we should desire him.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/53#v3)</sup> He is despised and rejected of men; a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief: and we hid as it were our faces from him; he was despised, and we esteemed him not.

    Who hath believed our report, and to whom is the arm of the Lord revealed? He is despised and rejected of men, a man of sorrows; the arm of the Lord is Christ, the power and salvation of the Lord.
  - **[Isaiah 53:8](/bible/isaiah/53#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/53#v8)</sup> He was taken from prison and from judgment: and who shall declare his generation? for he was cut off out of the land of the living: for the transgression of my people was he stricken.

    He was taken from prison and from judgment, and who shall declare his generation? For he was cut off out of the land of the living for the transgression of my people; the seed serving him and accounted for a generation answers that question.
