This session covers [Psalms 115](/bible/psalms/115) through 118, moving through the reading with basic precepts along the way. The teaching turns on two threads: the vanity of idols made with men's hands versus the power of our God who does whatsoever He pleases, and the truth that we are not a cursed people but a people under the curses, blessed and remembered by the Most High even when we are cast out among our enemies. As the disclaimer reminds us, Israel United in Christ is a non-violent, Bible-based movement that does not advocate or condone violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender.

**[Psalms 115:1-8](/bible/psalms/115#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/115#v1)</sup> Not unto us, O Lord, not unto us, but unto thy name give glory, for thy mercy, and for thy truth’s sake.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/115#v2)</sup> Wherefore should the heathen say, Where is now their God?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/115#v3)</sup> But our God is in the heavens: he hath done whatsoever he hath pleased.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/115#v4)</sup> Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men’s hands.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/115#v5)</sup> They have mouths, but they speak not: eyes have they, but they see not:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/115#v6)</sup> They have ears, but they hear not: noses have they, but they smell not:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/115#v7)</sup> They have hands, but they handle not: feet have they, but they walk not: neither speak they through their throat.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/115#v8)</sup> They that make them are like unto them; so is every one that trusteth in them.

- Not unto us but unto His name give glory, for His mercy and His truth's sake. The heathen ask where is now their God, but our God is in the heavens and He has done whatsoever He has pleased.
- Their idols are silver and gold, the work of men's hands. They have mouths but speak not, eyes but see not, ears but hear not, and they that make them are like unto them. This is going into idols and idolatry, which is spoken of throughout the scriptures.
  - **[Isaiah 42:8](/bible/isaiah/42#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/42#v8)</sup> I am the Lord: that is my name: and my glory will I not give to another, neither my praise to graven images.

    I am the Lord, that is My name, and My glory will I not give to another; the graven images are the idols.
  - **[Isaiah 40:18-23](/bible/isaiah/40#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/isaiah/40#v18)</sup> To whom then will ye liken God? or what likeness will ye compare unto him?
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/isaiah/40#v19)</sup> The workman melteth a graven image, and the goldsmith spreadeth it over with gold, and casteth silver chains.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/40#v20)</sup> He that is so impoverished that he hath no oblation chooseth a tree that will not rot; he seeketh unto him a cunning workman to prepare a graven image, that shall not be moved.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/40#v21)</sup> Have ye not known? have ye not heard? hath it not been told you from the beginning? have ye not understood from the foundations of the earth?
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/40#v22)</sup> It is he that sitteth upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants thereof are as grasshoppers; that stretcheth out the heavens as a curtain, and spreadeth them out as a tent to dwell in:
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/40#v23)</sup> That bringeth the princes to nothing; he maketh the judges of the earth as vanity.

    To whom will you liken God? The workman melts a graven image, but it is the Most High that sits upon the circle of the earth, and the inhabitants are as grasshoppers; He brings the princes to nothing.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)**
    > <sup>[64](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v64)</sup> And the Lord shall scatter thee among all people, from the one end of the earth even unto the other; and there thou shalt serve other gods, which neither thou nor thy fathers have known, even wood and stone.

    Because of these idols the Most High said He would scatter us among all people and there we would serve other gods of wood and stone.
  - **[Habakkuk 2:18](/bible/habakkuk/2#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/habakkuk/2#v18)</sup> What profiteth the graven image that the maker thereof hath graven it; the molten image, and a teacher of lies, that the maker of his work trusteth therein, to make dumb idols?

    The graven image is a teacher of lies, so the entertainers, athletes, and dope boys flossing with the jesus piece around their neck are carrying a teacher of lies.
  - **[John 3:16](/bible/john/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/john/3#v16)</sup> For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.

    This is one of those lies learned under the false image, telling everybody on the earth that God so loved the whole world.

**[Psalms 115:9-12](/bible/psalms/115#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/115#v9)</sup> O Israel, trust thou in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/115#v10)</sup> O house of Aaron, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/115#v11)</sup> Ye that fear the Lord, trust in the Lord: he is their help and their shield.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/115#v12)</sup> The Lord hath been mindful of us: he will bless us; he will bless the house of Israel; he will bless the house of Aaron.

- O Israel, O house of Aaron, and ye that fear the Lord, trust in the Lord; He is their help and their shield. The Lord has been mindful of us and He will bless us.
- This is so important because we are not a cursed people; we are a people under the curses. Because the Lord has been mindful of us, let us see how He has been mindful.
  - **[Leviticus 26:44](/bible/leviticus/26#v44)**
    > <sup>[44](/bible/leviticus/26#v44)</sup> And yet for all that, when they be in the land of their enemies, I will not cast them away, neither will I abhor them, to destroy them utterly, and to break my covenant with them: for I am the Lord their God.

    When we are in the land of our enemies the Most High will not cast us away nor destroy us utterly, but will remember the covenant of our ancestors.
  - **[Romans 9:4](/bible/romans/9#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/9#v4)</sup> Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises;

    This is one of the promises that belong to us as Israelites, the covenants and the promises.

**[Psalms 115:13-16](/bible/psalms/115#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/115#v13)</sup> He will bless them that fear the Lord, both small and great.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/115#v14)</sup> The Lord shall increase you more and more, you and your children.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/115#v15)</sup> Ye are blessed of the Lord which made heaven and earth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/115#v16)</sup> The heaven, even the heavens, are the Lord’s: but the earth hath he given to the children of men.

- He will bless them that fear the Lord, both small and great, and increase you more and more, you and your children. The scriptures say we are blessed, not cursed, and this speaks of us as a nation, not the brother or sister in the midst of sin claiming to be blessed and highly favored.
- The heavens are the Lord's, but the earth He has given to the children of men. The dead praise not the Lord, but we will bless the Lord from this time forth and forevermore.
  - **[Numbers 22:12](/bible/numbers/22#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/numbers/22#v12)</sup> And God said unto Balaam, Thou shalt not go with them; thou shalt not curse the people: for they are blessed.

    The Most High told Balaam not to curse the people for they are blessed, refusing to let the king of Moab hire a curse upon Israel coming up out of Egypt.
  - **[Isaiah 61:9](/bible/isaiah/61#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/61#v9)</sup> And their seed shall be known among the Gentiles, and their offspring among the people: all that see them shall acknowledge them, that they are the seed which the Lord hath blessed.

    Our seed shall be known among the Gentiles, and all that see them shall acknowledge that they are the seed which the Lord has blessed.

# Psalm 116: I Love the Lord Because He Hath Heard My Voice

**[Psalms 116:1-5](/bible/psalms/116#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/116#v1)</sup> I love the Lord, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/116#v2)</sup> Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, therefore will I call upon him as long as I live.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/116#v3)</sup> The sorrows of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/116#v4)</sup> Then called I upon the name of the Lord; O Lord, I beseech thee, deliver my soul.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/116#v5)</sup> Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful.

- I love the Lord because He has heard my voice and my supplications; therefore will I call upon Him as long as I live. When the sorrows of death and the pains of hell got hold on me, I called upon the name of the Lord to deliver my soul.
- Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful. Our God is merciful, and this is why we are not consumed.
  - **[Lamentations 3:22-23](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)</sup> It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/lamentations/3#v23)</sup> They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

    It is of the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not; they are new every morning, great is Thy faithfulness.

**[Psalms 116:6-12](/bible/psalms/116#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/116#v6)</sup> The Lord preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he helped me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/116#v7)</sup> Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord hath dealt bountifully with thee.

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/116#v8)</sup> For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/116#v10)</sup> I believed, therefore have I spoken: I was greatly afflicted:

>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/116#v12)</sup> What shall I render unto the Lord for all his benefits toward me?

- The Most High preserves the simple; when I was brought low, He helped me. Return unto thy rest, O my soul, for the Lord has dealt bountifully with thee, delivering my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.
- What shall I render unto the Lord for all His benefits toward me? The apostle Paul answered that question.
  - **[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

    Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
  - **[Ezra 9:13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)</sup> And after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for our great trespass, seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this;

    One of His benefits is that the Most High has punished us less than our iniquities deserve and has given us such deliverance.

**[Psalms 116:13-19](/bible/psalms/116#v13)**

>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/116#v14)</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/116#v15)</sup> Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/116#v16)</sup> O Lord, truly I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid: thou hast loosed my bonds.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/116#v17)</sup> I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call upon the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/116#v18)</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/116#v19)</sup> In the courts of the Lord’s house, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord.

- I will take the cup of salvation and call upon the name of the Lord, and pay my vows unto Him now in the presence of all His people. What we render is keeping the seventh-day Sabbath, the Passover, the new moons and the high holy days, observing the dietary laws, and learning to love our brethren as ourselves.
- Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints. The sisters are to come up out of the uncleanness they have been in, marry, and become righteous wives, offering the sacrifice of thanksgiving unto the Lord.
