**[Psalms 116:1-5](/bible/psalms/116#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/116#v1)</sup> I love the Lord, because he hath heard my voice and my supplications.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/116#v2)</sup> Because he hath inclined his ear unto me, therefore will I call upon him as long as I live.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/116#v3)</sup> The sorrows of death compassed me, and the pains of hell gat hold upon me: I found trouble and sorrow.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/116#v4)</sup> Then called I upon the name of the Lord; O Lord, I beseech thee, deliver my soul.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/116#v5)</sup> Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful.

- I love the Lord because He has heard my voice and my supplications; therefore will I call upon Him as long as I live. When the sorrows of death and the pains of hell got hold on me, I called upon the name of the Lord to deliver my soul.
- Gracious is the Lord, and righteous; yea, our God is merciful. Our God is merciful, and this is why we are not consumed.
  - **[Lamentations 3:22-23](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/lamentations/3#v22)</sup> It is of the Lord’s mercies that we are not consumed, because his compassions fail not.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/lamentations/3#v23)</sup> They are new every morning: great is thy faithfulness.

    It is of the Lord's mercies that we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not; they are new every morning, great is Thy faithfulness.

**[Psalms 116:6-12](/bible/psalms/116#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/116#v6)</sup> The Lord preserveth the simple: I was brought low, and he helped me.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/116#v7)</sup> Return unto thy rest, O my soul; for the Lord hath dealt bountifully with thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/116#v8)</sup> For thou hast delivered my soul from death, mine eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/116#v9)</sup> I will walk before the Lord in the land of the living.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/116#v10)</sup> I believed, therefore have I spoken: I was greatly afflicted:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/116#v11)</sup> I said in my haste, All men are liars.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/116#v12)</sup> What shall I render unto the Lord for all his benefits toward me?

- The Most High preserves the simple; when I was brought low, He helped me. Return unto thy rest, O my soul, for the Lord has dealt bountifully with thee, delivering my soul from death, my eyes from tears, and my feet from falling.
- What shall I render unto the Lord for all His benefits toward me? The apostle Paul answered that question.
  - **[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

    Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.
  - **[Ezra 9:13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ezra/9#v13)</sup> And after all that is come upon us for our evil deeds, and for our great trespass, seeing that thou our God hast punished us less than our iniquities deserve, and hast given us such deliverance as this;

    One of His benefits is that the Most High has punished us less than our iniquities deserve and has given us such deliverance.

**[Psalms 116:13-19](/bible/psalms/116#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/116#v13)</sup> I will take the cup of salvation, and call upon the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/116#v14)</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/116#v15)</sup> Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his saints.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/116#v16)</sup> O Lord, truly I am thy servant; I am thy servant, and the son of thine handmaid: thou hast loosed my bonds.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/116#v17)</sup> I will offer to thee the sacrifice of thanksgiving, and will call upon the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/116#v18)</sup> I will pay my vows unto the Lord now in the presence of all his people,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/116#v19)</sup> In the courts of the Lord’s house, in the midst of thee, O Jerusalem. Praise ye the Lord.

- I will take the cup of salvation and call upon the name of the Lord, and pay my vows unto Him now in the presence of all His people. What we render is keeping the seventh-day Sabbath, the Passover, the new moons and the high holy days, observing the dietary laws, and learning to love our brethren as ourselves.
- Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of His saints. The sisters are to come up out of the uncleanness they have been in, marry, and become righteous wives, offering the sacrifice of thanksgiving unto the Lord.
