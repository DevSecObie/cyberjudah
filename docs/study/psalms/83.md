Read the chapter → [Psalms 83](/bible/psalms/83)

This session reads through [Psalms 83](/bible/psalms/83) through 86, four chapters a day for the nation. The teaching moves through the psalms mostly by reading, with brief explanation where needed. [Psalm 83](/bible/psalms/83) shows that God has enemies, and the chapter is about to name God's enemies right now. It is a prayer that the Most High keep not silence while the heathen take crafty counsel against His people, the Israelites, His hidden ones, saying come let us cut them off from being a nation.

**[Psalms 83:1-4](/bible/psalms/83#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/83#v1)</sup> Keep not thou silence, O God: hold not thy peace, and be not still, O God.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/83#v2)</sup> For, lo, thine enemies make a tumult: and they that hate thee have lifted up the head.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/83#v3)</sup> They have taken crafty counsel against thy people, and consulted against thy hidden ones.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/83#v4)</sup> They have said, Come, and let us cut them off from being a nation; that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.

- The prayer opens asking the Most High to keep not silence, hold not His peace, and be not still, for His enemies make a tumult and they that hate Him have lifted up the head. This shows that God has enemies, and the chapter is about to name God's enemies right now.
- They have taken crafty counsel against His people, against us the Israelites, and consulted against His hidden ones, saying come let us cut them off from being a nation, that the name of Israel may be no more in remembrance.
- This is why today, nowadays, we are separated from our history, according to [Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5).
  - **[Jeremiah 17:5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/jeremiah/17#v5)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; Cursed be the man that trusteth in man, and maketh flesh his arm, and whose heart departeth from the Lord.

    The curse on trusting in man is tied to Israel being cut off and separated from their history.

**[Psalms 83:5-8](/bible/psalms/83#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/83#v5)</sup> For they have consulted together with one consent: they are confederate against thee:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/83#v6)</sup> The tabernacles of Edom, and the Ishmaelites; of Moab, and the Hagarenes;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/83#v7)</sup> Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek; the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/83#v8)</sup> Assur also is joined with them: they have holpen the children of Lot. Selah.

- Here the enemies of God begin to be named. They have consulted together with one consent and are confederate against the Most High: the tabernacles of Edom and the Ishmaelites, of Moab and the Hagarenes.
- Gebal, and Ammon, and Amalek, the Philistines with the inhabitants of Tyre; Assur also is joined with them, and they have holpen the children of Lot.

**[Psalms 83:9-12](/bible/psalms/83#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/83#v9)</sup> Do unto them as unto the Midianites; as to Sisera, as to Jabin, at the brook of Kison:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/83#v10)</sup> Which perished at En–dor: they became as dung for the earth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/83#v11)</sup> Make their nobles like Oreb, and like Zeeb: yea, all their princes as Zebah, and as Zalmunna:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/83#v12)</sup> Who said, Let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.

- David asks the Most High to do unto these enemies as unto the Midianites, as to Sisera and as to Jabin at the brook of Kison, who perished at Endor and became as dung for the earth.
- He asks that their nobles be made like Oreb and like Zeeb, all their princes as Zebah and as Zalmunna, who said let us take to ourselves the houses of God in possession.

**[Psalms 83:13-18](/bible/psalms/83#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/83#v13)</sup> O my God, make them like a wheel; as the stubble before the wind.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/83#v14)</sup> As the fire burneth a wood, and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/83#v15)</sup> So persecute them with thy tempest, and make them afraid with thy storm.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/83#v16)</sup> Fill their faces with shame; that they may seek thy name, O Lord.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/83#v17)</sup> Let them be confounded and troubled for ever; yea, let them be put to shame, and perish:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/83#v18)</sup> That men may know that thou, whose name alone is Jehovah, art the most high over all the earth.

- He asks the Most High to make them like a wheel, as the stubble before the wind; as the fire burneth a wood and as the flame setteth the mountains on fire, so to persecute them with His tempest and make them afraid with His storm.
- The aim of the prayer is that their faces be filled with shame, that they may seek His name; that men may know that He whose name alone is JEHOVAH is the Most High over all the earth.
