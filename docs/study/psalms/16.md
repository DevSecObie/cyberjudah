**[Psalms 16:1-3](/bible/psalms/16#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/16#v1)</sup> Preserve me, O God: for in thee do I put my trust.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/16#v2)</sup> O my soul, thou hast said unto the Lord, Thou art my Lord: my goodness extendeth not to thee;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/16#v3)</sup> But to the saints that are in the earth, and to the excellent, in whom is all my delight.

- David asks the Most High to preserve him because he puts his trust in him, confessing that his goodness extends to the saints that are in the earth and to the excellent in whom is all his delight.
- The saints are defined by the scripture itself.
  - **[Psalms 50:5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

    The saints are those that have made a covenant with the Most High by sacrifice.
  - **[Psalms 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    The Most High exalted the horn of his people, his saints, even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him.

**[Psalms 16:4](/bible/psalms/16#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/16#v4)</sup> Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god: their drink offerings of blood will I not offer, nor take up their names into my lips.

- Their sorrows shall be multiplied that hasten after another god. The pronoun "their" points back to his saints, warning that even his own people multiply sorrow when they chase after other gods.
- David refuses to offer their drink offerings of blood or take up their names into his lips.

**[Psalms 16:5-8](/bible/psalms/16#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/16#v5)</sup> The Lord is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup: thou maintainest my lot.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/16#v6)</sup> The lines are fallen unto me in pleasant places; yea, I have a goodly heritage.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/16#v7)</sup> I will bless the Lord, who hath given me counsel: my reins also instruct me in the night seasons.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/16#v8)</sup> I have set the Lord always before me: because he is at my right hand, I shall not be moved.

- The Lord is the portion of his inheritance and of his cup; the lines are fallen unto him in pleasant places and he has a goodly heritage.
- He blesses the Most High who gives him counsel, and his reins instruct him in the night season.
- He sets the Lord always before him; because the Most High is at his right hand he shall not be moved. This is not a bumper-sticker slogan; it is the true standing of a man who keeps the Most High before his face.

**[Psalms 16:9-11](/bible/psalms/16#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/16#v9)</sup> Therefore my heart is glad, and my glory rejoiceth: my flesh also shall rest in hope.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/16#v10)</sup> For thou wilt not leave my soul in hell; neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/16#v11)</sup> Thou wilt shew me the path of life: in thy presence is fulness of joy; at thy right hand there are pleasures for evermore.

- His heart is glad and his flesh shall rest in hope, for the Most High will not leave his soul in hell nor suffer his Holy One to see corruption. This speaks of the resurrection on the third day.
- The Most High will show him the path of life; in his presence is fullness of joy, and at his right hand are pleasures forevermore.
