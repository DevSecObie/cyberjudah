[Psalm 110](/bible/psalms/110) is the prophecy of Christ seated at the right hand of the Father until His enemies are made His footstool. The Most High sends the rod of His strength out of Zion, and Christ, a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek, rules in the midst of His enemies and shall judge among the heathen in the day of His wrath, waiting on Israel to keep God's laws so He can activate.

**[Psalms 110:1](/bible/psalms/110#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/110#v1)</sup> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.

- The Lord said unto my Lord, sit thou at my right hand until I make thine enemies thy footstool. The one sitting at the Father's right hand is Christ.
  - **[Acts 2:34-36](/bible/acts/2#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/acts/2#v34)</sup> For David is not ascended into the heavens: but he saith himself, The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand,
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/acts/2#v35)</sup> Until I make thy foes thy footstool.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/acts/2#v36)</sup> Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ.

    David is not ascended into the heavens, but he himself says the Lord said unto my Lord, sit thou on my right hand; so let all the house of Israel know that God has made that same Jesus, whom they crucified, both Lord and Christ.

**[Psalms 110:2-3](/bible/psalms/110#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/110#v2)</sup> The Lord shall send the rod of thy strength out of Zion: rule thou in the midst of thine enemies.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/110#v3)</sup> Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning: thou hast the dew of thy youth.

- The Lord shall send the rod of thy strength out of Zion; rule thou in the midst of thine enemies. The rod is Christ ruling in the midst of the enemies.
- Thy people shall be willing in the day of thy power, in the beauties of holiness from the womb of the morning; thou hast the dew of thy youth. Israel is patiently waiting to be saved.
  - **[Daniel 7:11-14](/bible/daniel/7#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/daniel/7#v11)</sup> I beheld then because of the voice of the great words which the horn spake: I beheld even till the beast was slain, and his body destroyed, and given to the burning flame.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/daniel/7#v12)</sup> As concerning the rest of the beasts, they had their dominion taken away: yet their lives were prolonged for a season and time.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/daniel/7#v13)</sup> I saw in the night visions, and, behold, one like the Son of man came with the clouds of heaven, and came to the Ancient of days, and they brought him near before him.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/daniel/7#v14)</sup> And there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom, that all people, nations, and languages, should serve him: his dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away, and his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.

    The beast, America, is slain and its body destroyed and given to the burning flame, its allies' lives prolonged for a season for slavery; then one like the Son of man comes with the clouds of heaven to the Ancient of days and is given an everlasting dominion that shall not pass away.
  - **[Luke 1:31-33](/bible/luke/1#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/luke/1#v31)</sup> And, behold, thou shalt conceive in thy womb, and bring forth a son, and shalt call his name JESUS.
    >
    > <sup>[32](/bible/luke/1#v32)</sup> He shall be great, and shall be called the Son of the Highest: and the Lord God shall give unto him the throne of his father David:
    >
    > <sup>[33](/bible/luke/1#v33)</sup> And he shall reign over the house of Jacob for ever; and of his kingdom there shall be no end.

    Jesus shall be called the Son of the Highest, and the Lord God shall give unto Him the throne of His father David; He shall reign over the house of Jacob forever and of His kingdom there shall be no end.

**[Psalms 110:4](/bible/psalms/110#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/110#v4)</sup> The Lord hath sworn, and will not repent, Thou art a priest for ever after the order of Melchizedek.

- The Lord hath sworn and will not repent, thou art a priest forever after the order of Melchizedek. Christ is the priest forever after this order.
  - **[Zechariah 6:12-13](/bible/zechariah/6#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/zechariah/6#v12)</sup> And speak unto him, saying, Thus speaketh the Lord of hosts, saying, Behold the man whose name is The BRANCH; and he shall grow up out of his place, and he shall build the temple of the Lord:
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/zechariah/6#v13)</sup> Even he shall build the temple of the Lord; and he shall bear the glory, and shall sit and rule upon his throne; and he shall be a priest upon his throne: and the counsel of peace shall be between them both.

    Behold the man whose name is The BRANCH, referring to Christ; He shall build the temple of the Lord, bear the glory, sit and rule upon His throne, and be a priest upon His throne.

**[Psalms 110:5-7](/bible/psalms/110#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/110#v5)</sup> The Lord at thy right hand shall strike through kings in the day of his wrath.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/110#v6)</sup> He shall judge among the heathen, he shall fill the places with the dead bodies; he shall wound the heads over many countries.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/110#v7)</sup> He shall drink of the brook in the way: therefore shall he lift up the head.

- The Lord at thy right hand shall strike through kings in the day of his wrath. The right hand is the commandments, and He shall strike through kings.
- He shall judge among the heathen, He shall fill the places with dead bodies, He shall wound the heads over many countries. He is going to destroy the heathen, and it will be very light work, without labor, by the law which is like fire.
- He shall drink of the brook in the way, therefore shall He lift up the head. God is waiting on us, on Israel, to keep His laws so He can activate and do His thing.
  - **[2 Esdras 13:37-38](/bible/2-esdras/13#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/2-esdras/13#v37)</sup> And this my Son shall rebuke the wicked inventions of those nations, which for their wicked life are fallen into the tempest;
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/2-esdras/13#v38)</sup> And shall lay before them their evil thoughts, and the torments wherewith they shall begin to be tormented, which are like unto a flame: and he shall destroy them without labour by the law which is like unto me.

    Christ shall rebuke the wicked inventions of the nations and destroy them without labor by the law, which is like fire.
  - **[Jeremiah 5:14](/bible/jeremiah/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/jeremiah/5#v14)</sup> Wherefore thus saith the Lord God of hosts, Because ye speak this word, behold, I will make my words in thy mouth fire, and this people wood, and it shall devour them.

    Because of these words, the Lord makes His words in the mouth fire and this people wood, and it shall devour them; God's word and His law are like fire.
  - **[Malachi 1:14](/bible/malachi/1#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/malachi/1#v14)</sup> But cursed be the deceiver, which hath in his flock a male, and voweth, and sacrificeth unto the Lord a corrupt thing: for I am a great King, saith the Lord of hosts, and my name is dreadful among the heathen.

    Cursed be the deceiver; for the Most High is a great King and His name is dreadful among the heathen, because when Israel starts keeping God's laws the heathen know they are finished.
