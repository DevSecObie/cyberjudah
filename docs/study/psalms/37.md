**[Psalms 37:1-3](/bible/psalms/37#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/37#v1)</sup> Fret not thyself because of evildoers, neither be thou envious against the workers of iniquity.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/37#v2)</sup> For they shall soon be cut down like the grass, and wither as the green herb.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/37#v3)</sup> Trust in the Lord, and do good; so shalt thou dwell in the land, and verily thou shalt be fed.

- This chapter hit home. Coming into this truth, David saw where his life was versus where other people's lives were and what they were doing, and it started to mess with his spirit a little. But reading things like this gave the strength to keep going.
- Do not fret because of evildoers nor be envious against the workers of iniquity, for they shall soon be cut down like the grass.
  - **[Psalms 73:3](/bible/psalms/73#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/73#v3)</sup> For I was envious at the foolish, when I saw the prosperity of the wicked.

    I was envious at the foolish when I saw the prosperity of the wicked, for there are no bands in their death and their strength is firm; pride and violence cover them.
- When I first came into the truth I would look at the wicked of our people and of the other nations, with no God and no laws, and they were prospering with a bunch of material things. That is why the scripture says do not fret or be envious of them.

**[Psalms 37:4-6](/bible/psalms/37#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/37#v4)</sup> Delight thyself also in the Lord; and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/37#v5)</sup> Commit thy way unto the Lord; trust also in him; and he shall bring it to pass.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/37#v6)</sup> And he shall bring forth thy righteousness as the light, and thy judgment as the noonday.

- Delight thyself in the Most High and he shall give thee the desires of thine heart; commit thy way unto him and he shall bring it to pass.
  - **[1 John 5:14](/bible/1-john/5#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/1-john/5#v14)</sup> And this is the confidence that we have in him, that, if we ask any thing according to his will, he heareth us:

    This is the confidence we have in him, that if we ask anything according to his will he hears us; ask according to his will, not to stumble somebody or to boast on social media, but to serve the nation.
  - **[James 4:3](/bible/james/4#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/james/4#v3)</sup> Ye ask, and receive not, because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts.

    Ye ask and receive not because ye ask amiss, that ye may consume it upon your lusts; do not pray for enemies to fall so you can stunt on them, but so you can continue to do his will uninterrupted.
- When you delight yourself in the Most High by keeping the commandments and trusting him, he brings it to pass. If you are always doubting, or asking for stuff without believing in him, it is not going to happen. That is why we have to continually meditate in the word day and night.

**[Psalms 37:7-11](/bible/psalms/37#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/37#v7)</sup> Rest in the Lord, and wait patiently for him: fret not thyself because of him who prospereth in his way, because of the man who bringeth wicked devices to pass.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/37#v8)</sup> Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself in any wise to do evil.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/37#v9)</sup> For evildoers shall be cut off: but those that wait upon the Lord, they shall inherit the earth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/37#v10)</sup> For yet a little while, and the wicked shall not be: yea, thou shalt diligently consider his place, and it shall not be.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/37#v11)</sup> But the meek shall inherit the earth; and shall delight themselves in the abundance of peace.

- Rest in the Most High and wait patiently for him; fret not because of the man who brings wicked devices to pass. Cease from anger and forsake wrath.
- Everything happens on the Most High's time. You may not get it right now because you are not ready for it. Sisters and brothers may be asking for a husband or wife, but if you are not ready he will make you wait; wait for it and you may come out with a beautiful marriage, but if you are impatient you get a back door marriage because you are not trusting in him. Whenever we do something outside the counsel given by godly men, we are not trusting in the Most High.
- For evildoers shall be cut off, but those that wait upon the Most High shall inherit the earth. We put the Most High through drama to give this earth away, so we have to go through some drama to get it back.
- For yet a little while and the wicked shall not be; you shall diligently consider his place and it shall not be.
  - **[Job 20:5-8](/bible/job/20#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/job/20#v5)</sup> That the triumphing of the wicked is short, and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment?
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/job/20#v6)</sup> Though his excellency mount up to the heavens, and his head reach unto the clouds;
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/job/20#v7)</sup> Yet he shall perish for ever like his own dung: they which have seen him shall say, Where is he?
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/job/20#v8)</sup> He shall fly away as a dream, and shall not be found: yea, he shall be chased away as a vision of the night.

    The triumphing of the wicked is short and the joy of the hypocrite but for a moment; though his excellency mount up to the heavens, he shall perish forever like his own dung and be chased away as a vision of the night.
- These wicked people running this earth are not going to be here forever. It will come a time when they are gone forever, and we will wake up as from a bad dream, hardly believing our slave was ruler over a whole country.

**[Psalms 37:12-15](/bible/psalms/37#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/37#v12)</sup> The wicked plotteth against the just, and gnasheth upon him with his teeth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/37#v13)</sup> The Lord shall laugh at him: for he seeth that his day is coming.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/37#v14)</sup> The wicked have drawn out the sword, and have bent their bow, to cast down the poor and needy, and to slay such as be of upright conversation.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/37#v15)</sup> Their sword shall enter into their own heart, and their bows shall be broken.

- The wicked plots against the just and gnashes upon him with his teeth, but the Most High shall laugh at him, for he sees his day is coming. If the Most High can laugh at them, as a follower of Christ I am going to laugh at my enemies too.
- The wicked draw out the sword to slay the upright, meaning us. They put out that we are not who we are. We are the Israelites. Nobody can answer who else we would be or show the historical facts; not Nubian, not Egyptian. There is too much history in Kings and Bishop's work, straight out of history books written not by our people, most of it while we were still in slavery. They knew who we were.
- They say we are a hate group because I denounced the label of African-American and went back to my history. Their sword shall enter their own heart and their bows shall be broken.

**[Psalms 37:16-20](/bible/psalms/37#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/37#v16)</sup> A little that a righteous man hath is better than the riches of many wicked.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/37#v17)</sup> For the arms of the wicked shall be broken: but the Lord upholdeth the righteous.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/37#v18)</sup> The Lord knoweth the days of the upright: and their inheritance shall be for ever.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/37#v19)</sup> They shall not be ashamed in the evil time: and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/37#v20)</sup> But the wicked shall perish, and the enemies of the Lord shall be as the fat of lambs: they shall consume; into smoke shall they consume away.

- A little that the righteous man has is better than the riches of many wicked, for neither their silver nor their gold is going to save them in that day.
- The arms of the wicked shall be broken, but the Most High upholds the righteous. The righteous shall not be ashamed in the evil time, and in the days of famine they shall be satisfied.
- That is why we have food pantries in our cities. When the days of famine come, the Most High will make sure we are satisfied. All you have to do is get in touch with your leadership in your state, and there is no shame in it.
- But the wicked shall perish and the enemies of the Most High shall be as the fat of lambs; they shall consume into smoke.
  - **[Zephaniah 1:8](/bible/zephaniah/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/zephaniah/1#v8)</sup> And it shall come to pass in the day of the Lord’s sacrifice, that I will punish the princes, and the king’s children, and all such as are clothed with strange apparel.

    In the day of the Lord's sacrifice he will punish the princes and the king's children and all such as are clothed with strange apparel; strange apparel goes against what the Bible says, like women in pants.

**[Psalms 37:21-26](/bible/psalms/37#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/37#v21)</sup> The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/37#v22)</sup> For such as be blessed of him shall inherit the earth; and they that be cursed of him shall be cut off.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/37#v23)</sup> The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord: and he delighteth in his way.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/37#v24)</sup> Though he fall, he shall not be utterly cast down: for the Lord upholdeth him with his hand.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/psalms/37#v25)</sup> I have been young, and now am old; yet have I not seen the righteous forsaken, nor his seed begging bread.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/psalms/37#v26)</sup> He is ever merciful, and lendeth; and his seed is blessed.

- The wicked borrows and pays not again, but the righteous shows mercy and gives. That is those people who borrow and then shut their hand when it comes time to pay. That is not the way we are supposed to be as a people.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 4:31](/bible/sirach/4#v31)**
    > <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/4#v31)</sup> Let not thine hand be stretched out to receive, and shut when thou shouldest repay.

    Do not always be the give-me person who then vanishes when it is time to give back.
- You cannot make it in this captivity alone. Everything we have we have to share with one another. Black Wall Street is here; the little we have, we try to make sure our brothers and sisters are taken care of.
- The steps of a good man are ordered by the Most High, and though he fall he shall not be utterly cast down, for the Most High upholds him with his hand. Good men are men that keep the commandments.
- Somebody may say the Bible says none is good, which is true, but you have to fight to be good. Christ says strive to enter in through the straight gate. Wickedness will come and you may fall, but like Proverbs says, a righteous man falls seven times and gets back up again. If you fall short of the glory and stay short, that is on you; get back up and reach for the full glory.
- I have been young and now am old, yet I have not seen the righteous forsaken nor his seed begging bread. The Most High never saw the righteous begging bread, because we are supposed to have each other; if we have each other we will not be begging bread and will be good in times of famine.

**[Psalms 37:27-31](/bible/psalms/37#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/psalms/37#v27)</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; and dwell for evermore.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/psalms/37#v28)</sup> For the Lord loveth judgment, and forsaketh not his saints; they are preserved for ever: but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/psalms/37#v29)</sup> The righteous shall inherit the land, and dwell therein for ever.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/psalms/37#v30)</sup> The mouth of the righteous speaketh wisdom, and his tongue talketh of judgment.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/psalms/37#v31)</sup> The law of his God is in his heart; none of his steps shall slide.

- Do good and dwell forevermore, for the Most High loves judgment and forsakes not his saints; they are preserved forever, but the seed of the wicked shall be cut off.
- A lot of people, especially the wicked Esau and those Christians, say the Most High has forgotten and hides his face.
  - **[Psalms 10:11](/bible/psalms/10#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/10#v11)</sup> He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten: he hideth his face; he will never see it.

    He hath said in his heart, God hath forgotten, he hideth his face, he will never see it; that is why they say it is all in the past, that you are stuck in the Old Testament.
- The Most High forsakes not his saints. Everything the wicked and their seed have done, they are still going to pay for. Just like even after Christ we went through slavery and all types of wicked punishments at the hand of our enemies, he did not forget that they are wicked and that payback is coming to them.
- The righteous shall inherit the land and dwell therein forever; the law of his God is in his heart, none of his steps shall slide.

  - **[Psalms 11:2](/bible/psalms/11#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/11#v2)</sup> For, lo, the wicked bend their bow, they make ready their arrow upon the string, that they may privily shoot at the upright in heart.

    Lo, the wicked bend their bow and make ready their arrow upon the string, that they may privily shoot at the upright in heart.
  - **[Psalms 64:3-5](/bible/psalms/64#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/64#v3)</sup> Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/64#v4)</sup> That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/64#v5)</sup> They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?

    They whet their tongue like a sword and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words, that they may shoot in secret at the perfect; whet means to sharpen, so they prepare exactly what to say so it can slice just right and catch the attention of the masses.
- The wicked are always watching, which is why when we go out in public we are supposed to be the righteous example and let our light shine. Esau will read your Israel United in Christ shirt, your true Jew or Israelite shirt, and study everything, looking for a weak link in the chain to blame everybody. One person could get caught doing wrong and they will say everybody in there is like that and put it on the news.
- The Most High will not leave the righteous in the wicked's hand or condemn him when he is judged; wait on him and he shall exalt thee to inherit the land, and when the wicked are cut off thou shalt see it.

- Mark the perfect man and behold the upright, for the end of that man is peace. As long as we keep the commandments, it is nothing but peace for us in the kingdom to come, but the transgressors shall be destroyed together.
- The salvation of the righteous is of the Most High; he is their strength in the time of trouble and delivers them from the wicked because they trust in him. That is why salvation is so important: we get saved from the wicked.
  - **[Malachi 1:4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/malachi/1#v4)</sup> Whereas Edom saith, We are impoverished, but we will return and build the desolate places; thus saith the Lord of hosts, They shall build, but I will throw down; and they shall call them, The border of wickedness, and, The people against whom the Lord hath indignation for ever.

    Esau is the border of wickedness, the one we are saved from.
  - **[Job 9:24](/bible/job/9#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/job/9#v24)</sup> The earth is given into the hand of the wicked: he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, and who is he?

    The earth is given into the hand of the wicked; we have to remember Esau runs the earth.
