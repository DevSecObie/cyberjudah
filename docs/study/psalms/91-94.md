Read the chapter → [Psalms 91](/bible/psalms/91)

This session covers [Psalms 91](/bible/psalms/91) through 94, focusing on the reading with basic precepts along the way. The Most High is preparing His people that a time of destruction is coming upon the earth, and we are not to fear it because our trust is in Him. As the disclaimer reminds us, Israel United in Christ is a non-violent, Bible-based movement that does not advocate or condone violence against any race, ethnicity, or gender. With civil unrest jumping off in the world, this is exactly why it is necessary for us to be that light in the dark place and restore order among our people, praying that the 144 and the one third repents soon.

**[Psalms 91:1-4](/bible/psalms/91#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/91#v1)</sup> He that dwelleth in the secret place of the most High shall abide under the shadow of the Almighty.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/91#v2)</sup> I will say of the Lord, He is my refuge and my fortress: my God; in him will I trust.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/91#v3)</sup> Surely he shall deliver thee from the snare of the fowler, and from the noisome pestilence.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/91#v4)</sup> He shall cover thee with his feathers, and under his wings shalt thou trust: his truth shall be thy shield and buckler.

- Whoever dwells in the secret place of the Most High shall abide under His shadow. When we say the Most High is our refuge and fortress and put our trust in Him, He delivers us from the snare of the fowler and the noisome pestilence.
- His truth shall be our shield and buckler. The shield is what you use to protect yourself, so we have to understand what the truth is.
  - **[Psalms 119:142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)**
    > <sup>[142](/bible/psalms/119#v142)</sup> Thy righteousness is an everlasting righteousness, and thy law is the truth.

    God's laws are the truth; His righteousness is an everlasting righteousness.

**[Psalms 91:5-7](/bible/psalms/91#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/91#v5)</sup> Thou shalt not be afraid for the terror by night; nor for the arrow that flieth by day;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/91#v6)</sup> Nor for the pestilence that walketh in darkness; nor for the destruction that wasteth at noonday.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/91#v7)</sup> A thousand shall fall at thy side, and ten thousand at thy right hand; but it shall not come nigh thee.

- We are not to be afraid of the terror by night nor the arrow that flieth by day. The Most High is preparing us that a time of destruction is coming upon the earth, and we are not supposed to fear that time because our trust is in our God.
- Christ warned us about this. A thousand shall fall at thy side and ten thousand at thy right hand, but it shall not come nigh thee. That means we have to gird up our minds, because we are going to see death, more than the little jimmy or pookie getting shot in the streets that we have been seeing.
  - **[Matthew 24:4-6](/bible/matthew/24#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/24#v4)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Take heed that no man deceive you.
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/24#v5)</sup> For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/24#v6)</sup> And ye shall hear of wars and rumours of wars: see that ye be not troubled: for all these things must come to pass, but the end is not yet.

    Take heed no man deceive you; wars and rumors of wars must come, but see that ye be not troubled as long as your trust is in our God.
  - **[Matthew 24:9-11](/bible/matthew/24#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/24#v9)</sup> Then shall they deliver you up to be afflicted, and shall kill you: and ye shall be hated of all nations for my name’s sake.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/24#v10)</sup> And then shall many be offended, and shall betray one another, and shall hate one another.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/24#v11)</sup> And many false prophets shall rise, and shall deceive many.

    They shall deliver you up to be afflicted and killed, and ye shall be hated of all nations for Christ's name's sake.
  - **[Matthew 24:12](/bible/matthew/24#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/24#v12)</sup> And because iniquity shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold.

    Because iniquity, being sin, shall abound, the love of many shall wax cold.
  - **[Matthew 24:13](/bible/matthew/24#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/matthew/24#v13)</sup> But he that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved.

    He that shall endure unto the end, the same shall be saved; this is the point for us.

**[Psalms 91:8-10](/bible/psalms/91#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/91#v8)</sup> Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/91#v9)</sup> Because thou hast made the Lord, which is my refuge, even the most High, thy habitation;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/91#v10)</sup> There shall no evil befall thee, neither shall any plague come nigh thy dwelling.

- Only with thine eyes shalt thou behold and see the reward of the wicked. Because we have made the Most High our habitation, no evil shall befall us and no plague shall come nigh our dwelling.
- This does not mean some of us will not die, but the Most High promises He will protect us. We have to make sure we are grounded in this truth.

**[Psalms 91:11-16](/bible/psalms/91#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/91#v11)</sup> For he shall give his angels charge over thee, to keep thee in all thy ways.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/91#v12)</sup> They shall bear thee up in their hands, lest thou dash thy foot against a stone.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/91#v13)</sup> Thou shalt tread upon the lion and adder: the young lion and the dragon shalt thou trample under feet.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/91#v14)</sup> Because he hath set his love upon me, therefore will I deliver him: I will set him on high, because he hath known my name.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/91#v15)</sup> He shall call upon me, and I will answer him: I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him, and honour him.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/91#v16)</sup> With long life will I satisfy him, and shew him my salvation.

- He shall give His angels charge over thee to keep thee in all thy ways. Because he hath set his love upon the Most High and known His name, the Most High will deliver him and set him on high.
- His name being the commandments, the laws. The Most High promises to answer him, be with him in trouble, deliver and honor him, and satisfy him with long life.
- [Psalm 91](/bible/psalms/91) is about deliverance when we put our faith and trust in the one true living God and in these commandments.

# Psalm 92: Planted in the House of the Lord

**[Psalms 92:1-6](/bible/psalms/92#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/92#v1)</sup> It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Lord, and to sing praises unto thy name, O most High:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/92#v2)</sup> To shew forth thy lovingkindness in the morning, and thy faithfulness every night,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/92#v3)</sup> Upon an instrument of ten strings, and upon the psaltery; upon the harp with a solemn sound.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/92#v4)</sup> For thou, Lord, hast made me glad through thy work: I will triumph in the works of thy hands.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/92#v5)</sup> O Lord, how great are thy works! and thy thoughts are very deep.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/92#v6)</sup> A brutish man knoweth not; neither doth a fool understand this.

- It is a good thing to give thanks unto the Most High and to sing praises unto His name, to show forth His lovingkindness in the morning and His faithfulness every night.
- The Most High's works are great and His thoughts are very deep. A brutish man and a fool do not understand this.

**[Psalms 92:7-11](/bible/psalms/92#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/92#v7)</sup> When the wicked spring as the grass, and when all the workers of iniquity do flourish; it is that they shall be destroyed for ever:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/92#v8)</sup> But thou, Lord, art most high for evermore.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/92#v9)</sup> For, lo, thine enemies, O Lord, for, lo, thine enemies shall perish; all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/92#v10)</sup> But my horn shalt thou exalt like the horn of an unicorn: I shall be anointed with fresh oil.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/92#v11)</sup> Mine eye also shall see my desire on mine enemies, and mine ears shall hear my desire of the wicked that rise up against me.

- When the wicked spring as the grass and the workers of iniquity flourish, it is that they shall be destroyed forever. The Most High's enemies shall perish and all the workers of iniquity shall be scattered.
- My horn shalt thou exalt like the horn of a unicorn; I shall be anointed with fresh oil, and mine eyes shall see my desire on mine enemies.

**[Psalms 92:12-13](/bible/psalms/92#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/92#v12)</sup> The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree: he shall grow like a cedar in Lebanon.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/92#v13)</sup> Those that be planted in the house of the Lord shall flourish in the courts of our God.

- The righteous shall flourish like the palm tree and grow like a cedar in Lebanon. Listen to the terminology: those that be planted take root, meaning you start to gain understanding because you are focused on the word and meditating on it.
- If you are planted in the house of the Lord, that means you are applying these things that keep you grounded in this truth.
  - **[Joshua 1:8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/joshua/1#v8)</sup> This book of the law shall not depart out of thy mouth; but thou shalt meditate therein day and night, that thou mayest observe to do according to all that is written therein: for then thou shalt make thy way prosperous, and then thou shalt have good success.

    Meditate on the book of the law day and night and observe to do all written in it, and thy way shall be prosperous and have good success.

**[Psalms 92:14-15](/bible/psalms/92#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/92#v14)</sup> They shall still bring forth fruit in old age; they shall be fat and flourishing;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/92#v15)</sup> To shew that the Lord is upright: he is my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in him.

- They shall still bring forth fruit in old age and shall be fat and flourishing, to show that the Most High is upright, my rock, and there is no unrighteousness in Him.
- There is no sin in the Most High. Our people may struggle saying you cannot keep all the laws, but the Most High says His laws are not grievous and He did not give any man permission to sin.
  - **[1 John 5:17](/bible/1-john/5#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/1-john/5#v17)</sup> All unrighteousness is sin: and there is a sin not unto death.

    All unrighteousness is sin, and there is a sin not unto death.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 15:20](/bible/sirach/15#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/15#v20)</sup> He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin.

    He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath He given any man license to sin.

**[Psalms 93:1-4](/bible/psalms/93#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/93#v1)</sup> The Lord reigneth, he is clothed with majesty; the Lord is clothed with strength, wherewith he hath girded himself: the world also is stablished, that it cannot be moved.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/93#v2)</sup> Thy throne is established of old: thou art from everlasting.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/93#v3)</sup> The floods have lifted up, O Lord, the floods have lifted up their voice; the floods lift up their waves.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/93#v4)</sup> The Lord on high is mightier than the noise of many waters, yea, than the mighty waves of the sea.

- The Most High reigneth, clothed with majesty and girded with strength. The world is established that it cannot be moved, and His throne is established of old; He is from everlasting.
- The floods have lifted up their voice and their waves, but the Most High on high is mightier than the noise of many waters.

**[Psalms 93:5](/bible/psalms/93#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/93#v5)</sup> Thy testimonies are very sure: holiness becometh thine house, O Lord, for ever.

- His testimonies are very sure; holiness becometh His house forever.
- If each of us took a strong look at ourselves, we are more wise than before we came into the knowledge of the truth, because we should be growing in understanding as we continue to apply the commandments. The scriptures are spiritual; the law is spiritual.
  - **[Psalms 19:7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/19#v7)</sup> The law of the Lord is perfect, converting the soul: the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.

    The law of the Most High is perfect, converting the soul; the testimony of the Lord is sure, making wise the simple.
  - **[Revelation 19:10](/bible/revelation/19#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/revelation/19#v10)</sup> And I fell at his feet to worship him. And he said unto me, See thou do it not: I am thy fellowservant, and of thy brethren that have the testimony of Jesus: worship God: for the testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy.

    The testimony of Jesus is the spirit of prophecy; He comes in the volume of the book.

# Psalm 94: The God of Vengeance and Defense of His People

Read the chapter → [Psalms 94](/bible/psalms/94)

**[Psalms 94:1-3](/bible/psalms/94#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/94#v1)</sup> O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth; O God, to whom vengeance belongeth, shew thyself.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/94#v2)</sup> Lift up thyself, thou judge of the earth: render a reward to the proud.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/94#v3)</sup> Lord, how long shall the wicked, how long shall the wicked triumph?

- O Lord God, to whom vengeance belongeth, show thyself. We really have to understand this in these times when a lot of our brothers and sisters want to take up arms against the oppressor, because vengeance belongeth to the Most High.
- We ask the question, how long shall the wicked triumph.
  - **[Luke 18:2-8](/bible/luke/18#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/luke/18#v2)</sup> Saying, There was in a city a judge, which feared not God, neither regarded man:
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/luke/18#v3)</sup> And there was a widow in that city; and she came unto him, saying, Avenge me of mine adversary.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/luke/18#v4)</sup> And he would not for a while: but afterward he said within himself, Though I fear not God, nor regard man;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/luke/18#v5)</sup> Yet because this widow troubleth me, I will avenge her, lest by her continual coming she weary me.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/luke/18#v6)</sup> And the Lord said, Hear what the unjust judge saith.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/luke/18#v7)</sup> And shall not God avenge his own elect, which cry day and night unto him, though he bear long with them?
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/luke/18#v8)</sup> I tell you that he will avenge them speedily. Nevertheless when the Son of man cometh, shall he find faith on the earth?

    The unjust judge avenged the widow for her continual coming; how much more shall God avenge His own elect speedily, yet shall He find faith on earth when the Son of man cometh.
  - **[Luke 21:19](/bible/luke/21#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/luke/21#v19)</sup> In your patience possess ye your souls.

    In your patience possess your souls; while we cry out how long, do not lose faith, Israel.

**[Psalms 94:4-7](/bible/psalms/94#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/94#v4)</sup> How long shall they utter and speak hard things? and all the workers of iniquity boast themselves?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/94#v5)</sup> They break in pieces thy people, O Lord, and afflict thine heritage.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/94#v6)</sup> They slay the widow and the stranger, and murder the fatherless.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/94#v7)</sup> Yet they say, The Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it.

- The workers of iniquity utter and speak hard things and boast themselves, like Trump and the Proud Boys who can scale the walls and get into the White House, while a sister who did a U-turn out there got shot up. Do not think the Most High is negligent or does not see these things.
- They break the Most High's people in pieces and afflict His heritage; they slay the widow and the stranger and murder the fatherless. That is the curses we are up under.
- Yet they say the Lord shall not see, neither shall the God of Jacob regard it. That is a heavy point, because it shows the people know who we are; it does not say the God of all people, it says the God of Jacob.
  - **[Jeremiah 51:20](/bible/jeremiah/51#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/jeremiah/51#v20)</sup> Thou art my battle axe and weapons of war: for with thee will I break in pieces the nations, and with thee will I destroy kingdoms;

    We are the Most High's battle axe and weapons of war, so we must prepare ourselves to be part of that army.

**[Psalms 94:8-11](/bible/psalms/94#v8)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/94#v10)</sup> He that chastiseth the heathen, shall not he correct? he that teacheth man knowledge, shall not he know?

  - **[Deuteronomy 32:26-28](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v26)**
