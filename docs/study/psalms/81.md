Read the chapter → [Psalms 81](/bible/psalms/81)

[Psalm 81](/bible/psalms/81) calls the nation to sing aloud unto God our strength, remembering that this was a statute for Israel and a law of the God of Jacob. The Most High recounts how He relieved our shoulder from the burden in Egypt, and then grieves that His people would not hearken to His voice. The chapter turns on the promise: open thy mouth wide and I will fill it, and the finest of the wheat and honey out of the rock He would have satisfied us with.

**[Psalms 81:1-4](/bible/psalms/81#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/81#v1)</sup> Sing aloud unto God our strength: make a joyful noise unto the God of Jacob.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/81#v2)</sup> Take a psalm, and bring hither the timbrel, the pleasant harp with the psaltery.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/81#v3)</sup> Blow up the trumpet in the new moon, in the time appointed, on our solemn feast day.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/81#v4)</sup> For this was a statute for Israel, and a law of the God of Jacob.

- Sing aloud unto God our strength; make a joyful noise unto the God of Jacob. Take a psalm, bring the timbrel, the pleasant harp with the psaltery.
- Blow up the trumpet in the new moon, in the time appointed, on our solemn feast day. This was a statute for Israel and a law of the God of Jacob.

**[Psalms 81:5-7](/bible/psalms/81#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/81#v5)</sup> This he ordained in Joseph for a testimony, when he went out through the land of Egypt: where I heard a language that I understood not.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/81#v6)</sup> I removed his shoulder from the burden: his hands were delivered from the pots.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/81#v7)</sup> Thou calledst in trouble, and I delivered thee; I answered thee in the secret place of thunder: I proved thee at the waters of Meribah. Selah.

- This He ordained in Joseph for a testimony when he went out through the land of Egypt, where he heard a language he understood not.
- The Most High removed his shoulder from the burden and his hands were delivered from the pots. He answered in trouble, in the secret place of thunder, and proved him at the waters of Meribah.

**[Psalms 81:8-10](/bible/psalms/81#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/81#v8)</sup> Hear, O my people, and I will testify unto thee: O Israel, if thou wilt hearken unto me;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/81#v9)</sup> There shall no strange god be in thee; neither shalt thou worship any strange god.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/81#v10)</sup> I am the Lord thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt: open thy mouth wide, and I will fill it.

- Hear, O My people, and I will testify unto thee, O Israel, if thou wilt hearken unto Me: there shall no strange god be in thee, neither shalt thou worship any strange god.
- I am the Lord thy God which brought thee out of the land of Egypt; open thy mouth wide and I will fill it.

**[Psalms 81:11-13](/bible/psalms/81#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/81#v11)</sup> But my people would not hearken to my voice; and Israel would none of me.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/81#v12)</sup> So I gave them up unto their own hearts’ lust: and they walked in their own counsels.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/81#v13)</sup> Oh that my people had hearkened unto me, and Israel had walked in my ways!

- But My people would not hearken to My voice, and Israel would none of Me. So I gave them up unto their own hearts' lust, and they walked in their own counsels.
- The grief of it: Oh that My people had hearkened unto Me and Israel had walked in My ways.

**[Psalms 81:14-16](/bible/psalms/81#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/81#v14)</sup> I should soon have subdued their enemies, and turned my hand against their adversaries.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/81#v15)</sup> The haters of the Lord should have submitted themselves unto him: but their time should have endured for ever.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/81#v16)</sup> He should have fed them also with the finest of the wheat: and with honey out of the rock should I have satisfied thee.

- Then He should soon have subdued their enemies and turned His hand against their adversaries. The haters of the Lord should have submitted themselves unto Him, but their time should have endured forever.
- He would have fed them with the finest of the wheat and with honey out of the rock He would have satisfied them. The honey out of the rock goes into salvation and the future rest we were meant to have coming out of Egypt into Canaan, the land that became Israel.

    He made him to suck honey out of the rock and oil out of the flinty rock.
