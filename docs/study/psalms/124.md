**[Psalm 124:1-8](/bible/psalms/124#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/124#v1)</sup> If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, now may Israel say;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/124#v2)</sup> If it had not been the Lord who was on our side, when men rose up against us:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/124#v3)</sup> Then they had swallowed us up quick, when their wrath was kindled against us:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/124#v4)</sup> Then the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/124#v5)</sup> Then the proud waters had gone over our soul.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/124#v6)</sup> Blessed be the Lord, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/124#v7)</sup> Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers: the snare is broken, and we are escaped.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/124#v8)</sup> Our help is in the name of the Lord, who made heaven and earth.

- If it had not been for the Most High who was on our side, now may Israel say, if it had not been for the Most High who was on our side when men rose up against us, then they had swallowed us up quick when their wrath was kindled against us.
- When the waters had overwhelmed us, the stream had gone over our soul, then the proud waters had gone over our soul. Blessed be the Most High, who hath not given us as a prey to their teeth.
- Our soul is escaped as a bird out of the snare of the fowlers, and the snare is broken and we are escaped. Our help is in the name of the Most High, who made heaven and earth.
- When Christians go to say that these psalms are not talking about Israel, these psalms are talking about Israel being saved from their enemies, being protected from our enemies, because our help only comes from the Most High God of Israel.
