Read the chapter → [Psalms 63](/bible/psalms/63)

This session reads [Psalms 63](/bible/psalms/63) through 66, four chapters a day, called relatively short chapters with a lot to go into. This is a reading recording with brief exposition: the summary given is that these psalms are David giving praise to the Most High and showing the workings of the wicked, which the bishop touches on all the time. [Psalm 63](/bible/psalms/63) is the thirst of the soul for the Most High in a dry and thirsty land, the praise that is better than life, and the promise that those who seek the soul to destroy it go down into the lower parts of the earth.

**[Psalms 63:1-2](/bible/psalms/63#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/63#v1)</sup> O God, thou art my God; early will I seek thee: my soul thirsteth for thee, my flesh longeth for thee in a dry and thirsty land, where no water is;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/63#v2)</sup> To see thy power and thy glory, so as I have seen thee in the sanctuary.

- David seeks the Most High early; his soul thirsts and his flesh longs for Him in a dry and thirsty land where no water is.
- He longs to see the power and glory of the Most High as he has seen it in the sanctuary.

**[Psalms 63:3-5](/bible/psalms/63#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/63#v3)</sup> Because thy lovingkindness is better than life, my lips shall praise thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/63#v4)</sup> Thus will I bless thee while I live: I will lift up my hands in thy name.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/63#v5)</sup> My soul shall be satisfied as with marrow and fatness; and my mouth shall praise thee with joyful lips:

- Because the loving kindness of the Most High is better than life, David's lips shall praise Him, and he will bless Him while he lives and lift up his hands in His name.
- His soul shall be satisfied with marrow and fatness, and his mouth shall praise Him with joyful lips.

**[Psalms 63:6-8](/bible/psalms/63#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/63#v6)</sup> When I remember thee upon my bed, and meditate on thee in the night watches.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/63#v7)</sup> Because thou hast been my help, therefore in the shadow of thy wings will I rejoice.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/63#v8)</sup> My soul followeth hard after thee: thy right hand upholdeth me.

- When he remembers the Most High upon his bed and meditates on Him in the night watches, he rejoices in the shadow of His wings because He has been his help.
- His soul follows hard after the Most High, and His right hand upholds him.

**[Psalms 63:9-11](/bible/psalms/63#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/63#v9)</sup> But those that seek my soul, to destroy it, shall go into the lower parts of the earth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/63#v10)</sup> They shall fall by the sword: they shall be a portion for foxes.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/63#v11)</sup> But the king shall rejoice in God; every one that sweareth by him shall glory: but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.

- Those that seek his soul to destroy it shall go down into the lower parts of the earth; they shall fall by the sword and be a portion for foxes.
- The king shall rejoice in the Most High, and everyone that swears by Him shall glory, but the mouth of them that speak lies shall be stopped.

# Psalm 64: Hide Me From the Secret Counsel of the Wicked

Read the chapter → [Psalms 64](/bible/psalms/64)

[Psalm 64](/bible/psalms/64) is a prayer to be preserved from the fear of the enemy and hidden from the secret counsel of the wicked. It sets out the workings of the wicked who whet their tongue like a sword and shoot bitter words in secret, and it declares that the Most High shall shoot at them with an arrow so that their own tongue falls upon themselves.

**[Psalms 64:1-2](/bible/psalms/64#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/64#v1)</sup> Hear my voice, O God, in my prayer: preserve my life from fear of the enemy.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/64#v2)</sup> Hide me from the secret counsel of the wicked; from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity:

- David asks the Most High to hear his voice in prayer and preserve his life from fear of the enemy.
- He asks to be hidden from the secret counsel of the wicked and from the insurrection of the workers of iniquity.

**[Psalms 64:3-6](/bible/psalms/64#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/64#v3)</sup> Who whet their tongue like a sword, and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/64#v4)</sup> That they may shoot in secret at the perfect: suddenly do they shoot at him, and fear not.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/64#v5)</sup> They encourage themselves in an evil matter: they commune of laying snares privily; they say, Who shall see them?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/64#v6)</sup> They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.

- The wicked whet their tongue like a sword and bend their bows to shoot their arrows, even bitter words, that they may shoot in secret at the perfect and suddenly, without fear.
- They encourage themselves in an evil matter, commune of laying snares privily, and say who shall see them; they search out iniquities and accomplish a diligent search, and the inward thought and heart of every one of them is deep.

**[Psalms 64:7-10](/bible/psalms/64#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/64#v7)</sup> But God shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly shall they be wounded.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/64#v8)</sup> So they shall make their own tongue to fall upon themselves: all that see them shall flee away.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/64#v9)</sup> And all men shall fear, and shall declare the work of God; for they shall wisely consider of his doing.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/64#v10)</sup> The righteous shall be glad in the Lord, and shall trust in him; and all the upright in heart shall glory.

- But the Most High shall shoot at them with an arrow; suddenly they shall be wounded, and their own tongue shall fall upon themselves, so all that see them flee away.
- All men shall declare the work of the Most High and wisely consider His doing; the righteous shall be glad in the Most High, trust in Him, and all the upright in heart shall glory.

# Psalm 65: Thou Crownest the Year With Thy Goodness

Read the chapter → [Psalms 65](/bible/psalms/65)

[Psalm 65](/bible/psalms/65) opens with praise waiting for the Most High in Zion and the vow performed, then moves to the purging of transgressions and the blessedness of the man He chooses to approach Him. It closes with the Most High enriching the earth, watering the ridges and furrows, and crowning the year with His goodness so that the pastures and valleys shout for joy.

**[Psalms 65:1-4](/bible/psalms/65#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/65#v1)</sup> Praise waiteth for thee, O God, in Sion: and unto thee shall the vow be performed.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/65#v2)</sup> O thou that hearest prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/65#v3)</sup> Iniquities prevail against me: as for our transgressions, thou shalt purge them away.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/65#v4)</sup> Blessed is the man whom thou choosest, and causest to approach unto thee, that he may dwell in thy courts: we shall be satisfied with the goodness of thy house, even of thy holy temple.

- Praise waits for the Most High in Zion and the vow shall be performed; He hears prayer and unto Him shall all flesh come.
- Iniquities prevail, but He purges away the transgressions; blessed is the man He chooses and causes to approach to dwell in His courts, satisfied with the goodness of His house and holy temple.

**[Psalms 65:5-8](/bible/psalms/65#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/65#v5)</sup> By terrible things in righteousness wilt thou answer us, O God of our salvation; who art the confidence of all the ends of the earth, and of them that are afar off upon the sea:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/65#v6)</sup> Which by his strength setteth fast the mountains; being girded with power:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/65#v7)</sup> Which stilleth the noise of the seas, the noise of their waves, and the tumult of the people.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/65#v8)</sup> They also that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid at thy tokens: thou makest the outgoings of the morning and evening to rejoice.

- By terrible things in righteousness He answers as the God of salvation, the confidence of all the ends of the earth and of them far off upon the sea.
- By His strength He sets the mountains and stills the noise of the seas and the tumult of the people; they that dwell in the uttermost parts are afraid of His tokens, and He makes the outgoings of morning and evening rejoice.

**[Psalms 65:9-13](/bible/psalms/65#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/65#v9)</sup> Thou visitest the earth, and waterest it: thou greatly enrichest it with the river of God, which is full of water: thou preparest them corn, when thou hast so provided for it.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/65#v10)</sup> Thou waterest the ridges thereof abundantly: thou settlest the furrows thereof: thou makest it soft with showers: thou blessest the springing thereof.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/65#v11)</sup> Thou crownest the year with thy goodness; and thy paths drop fatness.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/65#v12)</sup> They drop upon the pastures of the wilderness: and the little hills rejoice on every side.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/65#v13)</sup> The pastures are clothed with flocks; the valleys also are covered over with corn; they shout for joy, they also sing.

- He visits the earth and waters it, greatly enriching it with the river of God which is full of water, and He prepares corn, waters the ridges and settles the furrows.
- He crowns the year with His goodness and His paths drop fatness; the pastures are clothed with flocks, the valleys covered over with corn, and they shout for joy and sing.

# Psalm 66: Come and See the Works of God

Read the chapter → [Psalms 66](/bible/psalms/66)

[Psalm 66](/bible/psalms/66) calls all lands to make a joyful noise unto the Most High and to see His works, terrible in His doing toward the children of men, turning the sea into dry land. It recounts how He proved and tried His people, brought them through fire and water, and brought them out into a wealthy place, closing with the vows and offerings paid to Him and the declaration of what He has done for the soul.

**[Psalms 66:1-4](/bible/psalms/66#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/66#v1)</sup> Make a joyful noise unto God, all ye lands:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/66#v2)</sup> Sing forth the honour of his name: make his praise glorious.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/66#v3)</sup> Say unto God, How terrible art thou in thy works! through the greatness of thy power shall thine enemies submit themselves unto thee.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/66#v4)</sup> All the earth shall worship thee, and shall sing unto thee; they shall sing to thy name. Selah.

- Make a joyful noise unto the Most High, all lands; sing forth the honor of His name and make His praise glorious.
- Say unto the Most High how terrible He is in His works; through the greatness of His power His enemies submit themselves, and all the earth shall worship and sing unto Him.

**[Psalms 66:5-7](/bible/psalms/66#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/66#v5)</sup> Come and see the works of God: he is terrible in his doing toward the children of men.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/66#v6)</sup> He turned the sea into dry land: they went through the flood on foot: there did we rejoice in him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/66#v7)</sup> He ruleth by his power for ever; his eyes behold the nations: let not the rebellious exalt themselves. Selah.

- Come and see the works of the Most High, terrible in His doing toward the children of men, who turned the sea into dry land so they went through the flood on foot.
- He rules by His power forever; His eyes behold the nations, and let not the rebellious exalt themselves.

**[Psalms 66:8-12](/bible/psalms/66#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/66#v8)</sup> O bless our God, ye people, and make the voice of his praise to be heard:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/66#v9)</sup> Which holdeth our soul in life, and suffereth not our feet to be moved.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/66#v10)</sup> For thou, O God, hast proved us: thou hast tried us, as silver is tried.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/66#v11)</sup> Thou broughtest us into the net; thou laidst affliction upon our loins.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/66#v12)</sup> Thou hast caused men to ride over our heads; we went through fire and through water: but thou broughtest us out into a wealthy place.

- Bless the Most High and make the voice of His praise heard, who holds the soul in life and does not suffer the feet to be moved.
- He proved and tried His people as silver is tried, brought them into the net, laid affliction upon their loins, and caused men to ride over their heads, but brought them out through fire and water into a wealthy place.

**[Psalms 66:13-15](/bible/psalms/66#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/66#v13)</sup> I will go into thy house with burnt offerings: I will pay thee my vows,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/66#v14)</sup> Which my lips have uttered, and my mouth hath spoken, when I was in trouble.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/66#v15)</sup> I will offer unto thee burnt sacrifices of fatlings, with the incense of rams; I will offer bullocks with goats. Selah.

- David will go into the house of the Most High with burnt offerings and pay the vows his lips uttered and his mouth spoke when he was in trouble.
- He will offer burnt sacrifices of fatlings with the incense of rams, and offer bullocks with goats.

**[Psalms 66:16-20](/bible/psalms/66#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/66#v16)</sup> Come and hear, all ye that fear God, and I will declare what he hath done for my soul.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/66#v17)</sup> I cried unto him with my mouth, and he was extolled with my tongue.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/66#v18)</sup> If I regard iniquity in my heart, the Lord will not hear me:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/66#v19)</sup> But verily God hath heard me; he hath attended to the voice of my prayer.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/66#v20)</sup> Blessed be God, which hath not turned away my prayer, nor his mercy from me.

- Come and hear, all that fear the Most High, and he will declare what He has done for his soul; he cried unto Him with his mouth and He was extolled with his tongue.
- If he regarded iniquity in his heart the Most High would not hear him, but truly He heard and attended to the voice of his prayer; blessed be the Most High, who has not turned away his prayer nor His mercy from him.
