Read the chapter → [Psalms 95](/bible/psalms/95)

This session covers [Psalms 95](/bible/psalms/95) through 98, four psalms of praise. The whole point of these four chapters is giving praise to the Most High, and the teaching walks through precepts inside them to show why we give Him praise. We should always give praise to the Most High that you are an Israelite and you are the best people upon this earth above all nations.

**[Psalm 95:1](/bible/psalms/95#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/95#v1)</sup> O come, let us sing unto the Lord: let us make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation.

- Come let us sing unto the Lord and make a joyful noise to the rock of our salvation. So we should always be giving praise that you are an Israelite, the best people upon this earth above all nations.
- Who is the rock of our salvation? That rock is Christ.
- Why should we make a joyful noise? Because at one time we thought Christ was white, we thought Christ was for everyone, and we were deceived by the nations. Knowing that Christ looked like us and He is a black man from the tribe of Judah is a joyful thing, and He is coming to save us, the so-called black, Hispanic, and native Indians. The rock, our foundation that we build this church and our faith upon, is Christ.
  - **[1 Corinthians 10:4](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v4)</sup> And did all drink the same spiritual drink: for they drank of that spiritual Rock that followed them: and that Rock was Christ.

    That rock that followed them was Christ; the rock of our salvation is Christ.

**[Psalm 95:2](/bible/psalms/95#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/95#v2)</sup> Let us come before his presence with thanksgiving, and make a joyful noise unto him with psalms.

- Let us come before His presence with thanksgiving and make a joyful noise unto Him with psalms. We need to come with thanksgiving, joyful noise, and psalms.

**[Psalm 95:3](/bible/psalms/95#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/95#v3)</sup> For the Lord is a great God, and a great King above all gods.

- The Lord is a great God and a great King above all gods. Our Lord is a great God above all gods upon the earth. We only have the one true power, the living power, and He chose us out of all the nations, so that is a joyful thing.

**[Psalm 95:4-5](/bible/psalms/95#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/95#v4)</sup> In his hand are the deep places of the earth: the strength of the hills is his also.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/95#v5)</sup> The sea is his, and he made it: and his hands formed the dry land.

- In His hand are the deep places of the earth, and the strength of the hills is His. The strength here refers to the height of the hills.
- The sea is His, He made it, and His hands formed the dry land. Through the Lord's hand He made all things.
  - **[Genesis 1:9-10](/bible/genesis/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/1#v9)</sup> And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry land appear: and it was so.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/1#v10)</sup> And God called the dry land Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that it was good.

    God called the dry land earth; through the Lord's hand He made all things.

**[Psalm 95:6](/bible/psalms/95#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/95#v6)</sup> O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the Lord our maker.

- O come let us worship and bow down, let us kneel before the Lord our maker. Why? Because He made the earth and the seas. Who did He make it for? He made it for the Israelites, the saints. He made it for us to rule this planet earth and the nations, and that is a joyful thing.

**[Psalm 95:7](/bible/psalms/95#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/95#v7)</sup> For he is our God; and we are the people of his pasture, and the sheep of his hand. To day if ye will hear his voice,

- For He is our God and we are the people of His pasture and the sheep of His hand. Today if you will hear His voice, that voice is this Bible.
- We are that flock, the lost sheep that needs to be found by the voice, this Bible. This Bible tells us from [Deuteronomy 28:15](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v15) through 68 who the Israelites are that have been scattered to the four corners of the earth. That is the gospel that needs to go out to give our people praise and uplift them in their spirits.
  - **[Psalm 78:52](/bible/psalms/78#v52)**
    > <sup>[52](/bible/psalms/78#v52)</sup> But made his own people to go forth like sheep, and guided them in the wilderness like a flock.

    He made His own people go forth like sheep and guided them like a flock; we are that flock.
  - **[Luke 12:32](/bible/luke/12#v32)**
    > <sup>[32](/bible/luke/12#v32)</sup> Fear not, little flock; for it is your Father’s good pleasure to give you the kingdom.

    Fear not little flock, it is the Father's good pleasure to give you the kingdom; the kingdom is going to that flock, the Israelites.

**[Psalm 95:8-9](/bible/psalms/95#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/95#v8)</sup> Harden not your heart, as in the provocation, and as in the day of temptation in the wilderness:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/95#v9)</sup> When your fathers tempted me, proved me, and saw my work.

- Harden not your heart as in the provocation. Your heart means your mind, and provocation means to provoke to sin. Do not harden your neck or your mind to sin.
- When your fathers tempted Me, proved Me, and saw My work. Now we are getting some history of our forefathers, but Paul told us the same thing in the New Testament: do not harden your heart, do not be like our forefathers.
  - **[1 Corinthians 10:6-11](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v6)</sup> Now these things were our examples, to the intent we should not lust after evil things, as they also lusted.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v7)</sup> Neither be ye idolaters, as were some of them; as it is written, The people sat down to eat and drink, and rose up to play.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v8)</sup> Neither let us commit fornication, as some of them committed, and fell in one day three and twenty thousand.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v9)</sup> Neither let us tempt Christ, as some of them also tempted, and were destroyed of serpents.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v10)</sup> Neither murmur ye, as some of them also murmured, and were destroyed of the destroyer.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v11)</sup> Now all these things happened unto them for ensamples: and they are written for our admonition, upon whom the ends of the world are come.

    These things were examples so we should not lust, be idolaters, commit fornication, tempt Christ, or murmur as our forefathers did; they are written for our admonition upon whom the ends of the world are come.
  - **[Romans 15:4](/bible/romans/15#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/15#v4)</sup> For whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that we through patience and comfort of the scriptures might have hope.

    Whatsoever things were written aforetime were written for our learning, that through patience and comfort of the scriptures we might have hope.

**[Psalm 95:10-11](/bible/psalms/95#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/95#v10)</sup> Forty years long was I grieved with this generation, and said, It is a people that do err in their heart, and they have not known my ways:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/95#v11)</sup> Unto whom I sware in my wrath that they should not enter into my rest.

- Forty years long was the Lord grieved with that generation, a people that do err in their heart and have not known His ways.
- He swore in His wrath that they should not enter into His rest. Our forefathers did not enter into the rest, meaning the promised land, at that time. It goes the same way today: we want the kingdom of heaven, to enter into that rest. These are examples we need to go by, for our learning, of what our forefathers did wrong.
