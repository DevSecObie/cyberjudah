This session covers [Psalms 111](/bible/psalms/111) through 114, four brief chapters returning to the four chapters a day reading. The thread running through all four is the same: praise the Most High, and blessed are those who fear him and keep his commandments. [Psalm 111](/bible/psalms/111) praises the greatness, honor, and righteousness of his works and names the fear of the Most High as the beginning of wisdom. [Psalm 112](/bible/psalms/112) shows the blessing on the man who delights greatly in his commandments. [Psalm 113](/bible/psalms/113) lifts up the Most High as high above all nations, the one who raises the poor out of the dust and lifts up his people Israel. [Psalm 114](/bible/psalms/114) recalls how the earth itself trembled when Israel came out of Egypt.

**[Psalms 111:1](/bible/psalms/111#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/111#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. I will praise the Lord with my whole heart, in the assembly of the upright, and in the congregation.

- Praise you the Most High. David will praise the Most High with his whole heart, in the assembly of the upright and in the congregation.

**[Psalms 111:2](/bible/psalms/111#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/111#v2)</sup> The works of the Lord are great, sought out of all them that have pleasure therein.

- The works of the Most High are great, sought out of all them that have pleasure therein.

**[Psalms 111:3](/bible/psalms/111#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/111#v3)</sup> His work is honourable and glorious: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

- His work is honorable and glorious, and his righteousness endureth forever.

**[Psalms 111:4](/bible/psalms/111#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/111#v4)</sup> He hath made his wonderful works to be remembered: the Lord is gracious and full of compassion.

- He has made his glorious works to be remembered. The Most High is gracious and full of compassion.

**[Psalms 111:5](/bible/psalms/111#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/111#v5)</sup> He hath given meat unto them that fear him: he will ever be mindful of his covenant.

- He has given meat unto them that fear him. He will ever be mindful of his covenant.

**[Psalms 111:6](/bible/psalms/111#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/111#v6)</sup> He hath shewed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

- He has showed his people the power of his works, that he may give them the heritage of the heathen.

**[Psalms 111:7](/bible/psalms/111#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/111#v7)</sup> The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure.

- The works of his hands are verity and judgment; all his commandments are sure.

**[Psalms 111:8](/bible/psalms/111#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/111#v8)</sup> They stand fast for ever and ever, and are done in truth and uprightness.

- They stand fast forever and ever, and are done in truth and uprightness.

**[Psalms 111:9](/bible/psalms/111#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/111#v9)</sup> He sent redemption unto his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever: holy and reverend is his name.

- He sent redemption unto his people; he has commanded his covenant forever. Holy and reverend is his name.

**[Psalms 111:10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/111#v10)</sup> The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom: a good understanding have all they that do his commandments: his praise endureth for ever.

- The fear of the Most High is the beginning of wisdom; a good understanding have all they that do his commandments. His praise endureth forever.

# Psalm 112: Blessed Is the Man That Feareth the Most High

Read the chapter → [Psalms 112](/bible/psalms/112)

**[Psalms 112:1](/bible/psalms/112#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/112#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Blessed is the man that feareth the Lord, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.

- Praise you the Most High. Blessed is the man that feareth the Most High, that delighteth greatly in his commandments.
- A lot of people try to say they are blessed like the Christians do, saying "I'm blessed, I'm highly favored." But the scripture says blessed is the man that feareth the Most High and delighteth greatly in his commandments. To be blessed you must be keeping the commands and be delighted greatly in them, meaning they are not grievous to you. That is the mindset every single person has to have when it comes to keeping these commandments.

**[Psalms 112:2](/bible/psalms/112#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/112#v2)</sup> His seed shall be mighty upon earth: the generation of the upright shall be blessed.

- His seed shall be mighty upon the earth; the generation of the upright shall be blessed. That is the chosen that get to the kingdom.

**[Psalms 112:3](/bible/psalms/112#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/112#v3)</sup> Wealth and riches shall be in his house: and his righteousness endureth for ever.

- Wealth and riches shall be in his house, and his righteousness endureth forever. All the wealth and all the riches will belong to the Israelites when that time comes with Christ to redeem his people.
- He is going to bring the earth back to the Israelites, and the other nations, the heathens, are going to bring all the wealth back to us. They are going to bring the things that are needed to rebuild the kingdom.

**[Psalms 112:4](/bible/psalms/112#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/112#v4)</sup> Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness: he is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

- Unto the upright there ariseth light in the darkness. He is gracious, and full of compassion, and righteous.

**[Psalms 112:5](/bible/psalms/112#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/112#v5)</sup> A good man sheweth favour, and lendeth: he will guide his affairs with discretion.

- A good man showeth favor and lendeth; he will guide his affairs with discretion.

**[Psalms 112:6](/bible/psalms/112#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/112#v6)</sup> Surely he shall not be moved for ever: the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.

- Surely he shall not be moved forever; the righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance.
  - **[Malachi 3:16](/bible/malachi/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/malachi/3#v16)</sup> Then they that feared the Lord spake often one to another: and the Lord hearkened, and heard it, and a book of remembrance was written before him for them that feared the Lord, and that thought upon his name.

    A book of remembrance was written for those that feared the Most High and thought upon his name, which is the same thing read back in verse one.

**[Psalms 112:7](/bible/psalms/112#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/112#v7)</sup> He shall not be afraid of evil tidings: his heart is fixed, trusting in the Lord.

- He shall not be afraid of evil tidings; his heart is fixed, trusting in the Most High. This is the same thing reiterated all through Psalms, how David was not going to be afraid of the things our enemies do against him.

**[Psalms 112:8](/bible/psalms/112#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/112#v8)</sup> His heart is established, he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies.

- His heart is established; he shall not be afraid, until he see his desire upon his enemies.

**[Psalms 112:9](/bible/psalms/112#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/112#v9)</sup> He hath dispersed, he hath given to the poor; his righteousness endureth for ever; his horn shall be exalted with honour.

- He has dispersed, he has given to the poor; his righteousness endureth forever; his horn shall be exalted with honor.

**[Psalms 112:10](/bible/psalms/112#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/112#v10)</sup> The wicked shall see it, and be grieved; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away: the desire of the wicked shall perish.

- The wicked shall see it and be grieved; he shall gnash with his teeth, and melt away; the desire of the wicked shall perish.

# Psalm 113: The Most High Is High Above All Nations

Read the chapter → [Psalms 113](/bible/psalms/113)

**[Psalms 113:1](/bible/psalms/113#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/113#v1)</sup> Praise ye the Lord. Praise, O ye servants of the Lord, praise the name of the Lord.

- Praise you the Most High. Praise, O ye servants of the Most High, praise the name of the Most High.

**[Psalms 113:2](/bible/psalms/113#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/113#v2)</sup> Blessed be the name of the Lord from this time forth and for evermore.

- Blessed be the name of the Most High from this time forth and forevermore.

**[Psalms 113:3](/bible/psalms/113#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/113#v3)</sup> From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same the Lord’s name is to be praised.

- From the rising of the sun unto the going down of the same, the name of the Most High is to be praised.

**[Psalms 113:4](/bible/psalms/113#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/113#v4)</sup> The Lord is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens.

- The Most High is high above all nations, and his glory above the heavens. He is letting you know that he is the only God, high above all nations.

**[Psalms 113:5](/bible/psalms/113#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/113#v5)</sup> Who is like unto the Lord our God, who dwelleth on high,

- Who is like unto the Most High our God, who dwelleth on high?

**[Psalms 113:6](/bible/psalms/113#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/113#v6)</sup> Who humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven, and in the earth!

- He humbleth himself to behold the things that are in heaven and in the earth.

**[Psalms 113:7](/bible/psalms/113#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/113#v7)</sup> He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill;

- He raiseth up the poor out of the dust, and lifteth the needy out of the dunghill. That is what is happening right now; our people are being lifted up.

**[Psalms 113:8](/bible/psalms/113#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/113#v8)</sup> That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people.

- That he may set him with princes, even with the princes of his people, which is Israel.

**[Psalms 113:9](/bible/psalms/113#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/113#v9)</sup> He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise ye the Lord.

- He maketh the barren woman to keep house, and to be a joyful mother of children. Praise you the Most High.
- At the bottom he lets you know he can bring our people up from a lower state. He showed the princes and then he showed the women, saying, I can bring the princes up and I can bring the women up.

# Psalm 114: The Earth Trembled When Israel Came Out of Egypt

Read the chapter → [Psalms 114](/bible/psalms/114)

**[Psalms 114:1](/bible/psalms/114#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/114#v1)</sup> When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language;

- When Israel went out of Egypt, the house of Jacob from a people of strange language.

**[Psalms 114:2](/bible/psalms/114#v2)**
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/114#v2)</sup> Judah was his sanctuary, and Israel his dominion.

- Judah was his sanctuary, and Israel his dominion.

**[Psalms 114:3](/bible/psalms/114#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/114#v3)</sup> The sea saw it, and fled: Jordan was driven back.

- The sea saw it, and fled; Jordan was driven back.

**[Psalms 114:4](/bible/psalms/114#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/114#v4)</sup> The mountains skipped like rams, and the little hills like lambs.

- The mountains skipped like rams, and the little hills like lambs.

**[Psalms 114:5](/bible/psalms/114#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/114#v5)</sup> What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou wast driven back?

- What ailed thee, O thou sea, that thou fleddest? thou Jordan, that thou wast driven back?

**[Psalms 114:6](/bible/psalms/114#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/114#v6)</sup> Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams; and ye little hills, like lambs?

- Ye mountains, that ye skipped like rams; and ye little hills, like lambs?

**[Psalms 114:7](/bible/psalms/114#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/114#v7)</sup> Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Lord, at the presence of the God of Jacob;

- Tremble, thou earth, at the presence of the Most High, at the presence of the God of Jacob.

**[Psalms 114:8](/bible/psalms/114#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/114#v8)</sup> Which turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters.

- Which turned the rock into a standing water, the flint into a fountain of waters.
- This is a very brief and quick reading, but overall we see it talking about those that are blessed, those being blessed that keep the commandments. It says who is like the Most High, and then it gives praise to God who delivered Israel. It is still good to read; it is a good refresher, especially [Psalms 111](/bible/psalms/111) and 112, and there is always a takeaway from the readings that we do.
