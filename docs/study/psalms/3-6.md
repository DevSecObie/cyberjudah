Read the chapter → [Psalms 3](/bible/psalms/3)

This portion of the "4 Chapters a Day" session covers [Psalms 3](/bible/psalms/3) through 6, four short psalms of David. The teaching frames these chapters around putting yourself in David's shoes so the scriptures better your understanding, because the things written aforetime were written for our learning. [Psalm 3](/bible/psalms/3) is a psalm of David when he fled from Absalom his son, and the session opens by asking why a man would ever have to flee from his own son. The answer is set up with Christ's words that He came not to send peace but a sword, to set a man at variance against his father, showing that the household division David suffered is a trial that may one day come upon any of us.

**[Psalm 3:1](/bible/psalms/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/3#v1)</sup> Lord, how are they increased that trouble me! many are they that rise up against me.

- Read this psalm remembering the heading: it is a psalm of David when he fled from Absalom his son. If you do not know that history, go back to Second Samuel and read it. Try to put yourself in the scriptures to better your understanding, because things written the fourth time were written for our learning, that through the patience and comfort of the scriptures we might have hope.
- Lord, how are they increased that trouble me, many are they that rise up against me. Put yourself in that position: David's own son was out to get him, and the men who once served the king were being turned against him.
  - **[Matthew 10:34-36](/bible/matthew/10#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/10#v34)</sup> Think not that I am come to send peace on earth: I came not to send peace, but a sword.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/10#v35)</sup> For I am come to set a man at variance against his father, and the daughter against her mother, and the daughter in law against her mother in law.
    >
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/10#v36)</sup> And a man’s foes shall be they of his own household.

    Christ came not to send peace but a sword, to set a man at variance against his father, a daughter against her mother, and a man's foes shall be they of his own household. This is exactly what David is facing in Psalm 3, and it may one day be your trial too.
  - **[2 Samuel 15:1-6](/bible/2-samuel/15#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/2-samuel/15#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after this, that Absalom prepared him chariots and horses, and fifty men to run before him.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-samuel/15#v2)</sup> And Absalom rose up early, and stood beside the way of the gate: and it was so, that when any man that had a controversy came to the king for judgment, then Absalom called unto him, and said, Of what city art thou? And he said, Thy servant is of one of the tribes of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-samuel/15#v3)</sup> And Absalom said unto him, See, thy matters are good and right; but there is no man deputed of the king to hear thee.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-samuel/15#v4)</sup> Absalom said moreover, Oh that I were made judge in the land, that every man which hath any suit or cause might come unto me, and I would do him justice!
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-samuel/15#v5)</sup> And it was so, that when any man came nigh to him to do him obeisance, he put forth his hand, and took him, and kissed him.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-samuel/15#v6)</sup> And on this manner did Absalom to all Israel that came to the king for judgment: so Absalom stole the hearts of the men of Israel.

    Absalom rose up early and stood beside the way of the gate, and when any man came to the king for judgment Absalom flattered him and kissed him, so Absalom stole the hearts of the men of Israel.
  - **[2 Samuel 15:12-14](/bible/2-samuel/15#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/15#v12)</sup> And Absalom sent for Ahithophel the Gilonite, David’s counsellor, from his city, even from Giloh, while he offered sacrifices. And the conspiracy was strong; for the people increased continually with Absalom.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-samuel/15#v13)</sup> And there came a messenger to David, saying, The hearts of the men of Israel are after Absalom.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/15#v14)</sup> And David said unto all his servants that were with him at Jerusalem, Arise, and let us flee; for we shall not else escape from Absalom: make speed to depart, lest he overtake us suddenly, and bring evil upon us, and smite the city with the edge of the sword.

    Absalom was making a power play, conspiring to overthrow his father, and the conspiracy was strong for the people increased continually with Absalom, so David told his servants to arise and flee lest Absalom overtake them.

**[Psalm 3:2-3](/bible/psalms/3#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/3#v2)</sup> Many there be which say of my soul, There is no help for him in God. Selah.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/3#v3)</sup> But thou, O Lord, art a shield for me; my glory, and the lifter up of mine head.

- Many there be which say of my soul, there is no help for him in God. Selah. Even with his own son coming against him and the men turning away, David did not agree; he was a man after the Most High's own heart and trusted in Him.
- But thou O Lord art a shield for me, my glory, and the lifter up of mine head. Think about it: your grown son gets big and bad and comes against you, yet David still trusted in the Most High.
  - **[Psalm 33:18-22](/bible/psalms/33#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/33#v18)</sup> Behold, the eye of the Lord is upon them that fear him, upon them that hope in his mercy;
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/psalms/33#v19)</sup> To deliver their soul from death, and to keep them alive in famine.
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/psalms/33#v20)</sup> Our soul waiteth for the Lord: he is our help and our shield.
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/33#v21)</sup> For our heart shall rejoice in him, because we have trusted in his holy name.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/33#v22)</sup> Let thy mercy, O Lord, be upon us, according as we hope in thee.

    Behold, the eye of the Lord is upon them that fear Him, upon them that hope in His mercy; our soul waiteth for the Lord, for He is our help and our shield. The Most High is the help and shield David is leaning on.

**[Psalm 3:4-8](/bible/psalms/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/3#v4)</sup> I cried unto the Lord with my voice, and he heard me out of his holy hill. Selah.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/3#v5)</sup> I laid me down and slept; I awaked; for the Lord sustained me.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/3#v6)</sup> I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people, that have set themselves against me round about.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/3#v7)</sup> Arise, O Lord; save me, O my God: for thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheek bone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/3#v8)</sup> Salvation belongeth unto the Lord: thy blessing is upon thy people. Selah.

- I cried unto the Lord with my voice and He heard me out of His holy hill. I laid me down and slept, I awaked, for the Lord sustained me. Even in the middle of his son's rebellion David could lie down and rest because the Most High held him up.
- I will not be afraid of ten thousands of people that have set themselves against me round about. This is a mindset to feed yourself: when trouble comes will you have a moment and come out of the spirit, or will you trust in the Most High? That is why you must constantly feed yourself these scriptures and stay around godly men and godly women.
- Arise O Lord, save me O my God, for thou hast smitten all mine enemies upon the cheekbone; thou hast broken the teeth of the ungodly. Salvation belongeth unto the Lord, thy blessing is upon thy people. The Most High defends His people, and the blessing rests on them.

# Psalm 4: The Lord Hath Set Apart Him That Is Godly for Himself

Read the chapter → [Psalms 4](/bible/psalms/4)

**[Psalm 4:1-2](/bible/psalms/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/4#v1)</sup> Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness: thou hast enlarged me when I was in distress; have mercy upon me, and hear my prayer.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/4#v2)</sup> O ye sons of men, how long will ye turn my glory into shame? how long will ye love vanity, and seek after leasing? Selah.

- Hear me when I call, O God of my righteousness; thou hast enlarged me when I was in distress; have mercy upon me and hear my prayer. David calls on the Most High as the God of his righteousness.
- O ye sons of men, how long will ye turn my glory into shame, how long will ye love vanity and seek after leasing (lying). Selah. David asks the men set against him how long they will chase after vanity and lies.

**[Psalm 4:3](/bible/psalms/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/4#v3)</sup> But know that the Lord hath set apart him that is godly for himself: the Lord will hear when I call unto him.

- But know that the Lord hath set apart him that is godly for Himself; the Lord will hear when I call unto Him. The Most High sets apart the godly for Himself, and He hears them when they call.
- This ties to the class the bishop taught about being sealed. The Most High knows how to keep those meant for the kingdom and deliver them out of their temptations. None of us can be certain we are meant for the kingdom, so do not lose your crown and do not let anybody lead you or cause you to lose it, because only you can do that.
  - **[2 Peter 2:9](/bible/2-peter/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-peter/2#v9)</sup> The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished:

    The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished. The same Most High who sets apart the godly also knows how to deliver them.

**[Psalm 4:4](/bible/psalms/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/4#v4)</sup> Stand in awe, and sin not: commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still. Selah.

- Stand in awe, and sin not; commune with your own heart upon your bed, and be still. Selah. Be angry but sin not; no matter what you are going through, the Most High does not give you the right to sin.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 15:20](/bible/sirach/15#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/15#v20)</sup> He hath commanded no man to do wickedly, neither hath he given any man licence to sin.

    The Most High has commanded no man to do wickedly and has given no man licence to sin, so whatever you are going through does not give you the right to sin.

**[Psalm 4:5](/bible/psalms/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/4#v5)</sup> Offer the sacrifices of righteousness, and put your trust in the Lord.

- Offer the sacrifices of righteousness, and put your trust in the Lord. The sacrifice of righteousness today is keeping the law and presenting your body a living sacrifice.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 35:1-2](/bible/sirach/35#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/35#v1)</sup> He that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough: he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/35#v2)</sup> He that requiteth a good turn offereth fine flour; and he that giveth alms sacrificeth praise.

    He that keepeth the law bringeth offerings enough, and he that taketh heed to the commandment offereth a peace offering, which was a sacrifice.
  - **[Romans 12:1](/bible/romans/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/12#v1)</sup> I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

    Present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service. This means the dietary laws are not done away, the dress code is not done away, and we still keep the ceremonial laws when we come back into the knowledge of who we are.
  - **[Psalm 103:10-13](/bible/psalms/103#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/103#v10)</sup> He hath not dealt with us after our sins; nor rewarded us according to our iniquities.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/103#v11)</sup> For as the heaven is high above the earth, so great is his mercy toward them that fear him.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/103#v12)</sup> As far as the east is from the west, so far hath he removed our transgressions from us.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/103#v13)</sup> Like as a father pitieth his children, so the Lord pitieth them that fear him.

    He hath not dealt with us after our sins, nor rewarded us according to our iniquities; as far as the east is from the west, so far hath He removed our transgressions from us. Even though believing in white Jesus, Allah, or Buddha or keeping your birthday was idolatry punishable by death, He is forever merciful to them that fear Him by keeping the commandments.

**[Psalm 4:6-8](/bible/psalms/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/4#v6)</sup> There be many that say, Who will shew us any good? Lord, lift thou up the light of thy countenance upon us.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/4#v7)</sup> Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time that their corn and their wine increased.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/4#v8)</sup> I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, Lord, only makest me dwell in safety.

- There be many that say, who will shew us any good? Lord, lift thou up the light of thy countenance upon us. Many are looking for something good, and David asks the Most High to lift up the light of His countenance.
- Thou hast put gladness in my heart, more than in the time that their corn and their wine increased. The gladness the Most High gives is greater than the increase of harvest and wine.
- I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep; for thou, Lord, only makest me to dwell in safety. Safety comes from the Most High, and it comes by keeping His statutes and judgments.
  - **[Leviticus 25:18](/bible/leviticus/25#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/25#v18)</sup> Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety.

    Wherefore ye shall do my statutes, and keep my judgments, and do them; and ye shall dwell in the land in safety. Safety in the land is tied directly to doing the statutes and judgments.
  - **[Baruch 3:12-13](/bible/baruch/3#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/baruch/3#v12)</sup> Thou hast forsaken the fountain of wisdom.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/baruch/3#v13)</sup> For if thou hadst walked in the way of God, thou shouldest have dwelled in peace for ever.

    If our forefathers had walked in the way of the Most High they would have dwelled in peace forever, which shows we are the ones under the curses, because right now we are not living in a time of peace.

# Psalm 5: For Thou Lord Wilt Bless the Righteous

Read the chapter → [Psalms 5](/bible/psalms/5)

**[Psalm 5:1-3](/bible/psalms/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/5#v1)</sup> Give ear to my words, O Lord, consider my meditation.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/5#v2)</sup> Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King, and my God: for unto thee will I pray.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/5#v3)</sup> My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O Lord; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.

- Give ear to my words, O Lord, consider my meditation. Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King and my God, for unto thee will I pray. David directs his cry to the Most High as his King and his God.
- My voice shalt thou hear in the morning, O Lord; in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up. David sets his prayer before the Most High in the morning and looks up in expectation.

**[Psalm 5:4-7](/bible/psalms/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/5#v4)</sup> For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness: neither shall evil dwell with thee.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/5#v5)</sup> The foolish shall not stand in thy sight: thou hatest all workers of iniquity.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/5#v6)</sup> Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing: the Lord will abhor the bloody and deceitful man.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/5#v7)</sup> But as for me, I will come into thy house in the multitude of thy mercy: and in thy fear will I worship toward thy holy temple.

- For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness, neither shall evil dwell with thee. The foolish shall not stand in thy sight; thou hatest all workers of iniquity. The Most High hates all workers of iniquity.
- Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing (lying); the Lord will abhor the bloody and deceitful man. The Most High will destroy the liar and abhors the bloody and deceitful.
- But as for me, I will come into thy house in the multitude of thy mercy, and in thy fear will I worship toward thy holy temple. David sets himself apart from the wicked, coming into the Most High's house in reverence.

**[Psalm 5:8-9](/bible/psalms/5#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/5#v8)</sup> Lead me, O Lord, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/5#v9)</sup> For there is no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness; their throat is an open sepulchre; they flatter with their tongue.

- Lead me, O Lord, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies; make thy way straight before my face. David asks to be led in righteousness because of the enemies watching him.
- For there is no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness; their throat is an open sepulchre; they flatter with their tongue. The enemies are full of wickedness and deceit, flattering with their tongues.
  - **[Romans 3:11-13](/bible/romans/3#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/romans/3#v11)</sup> There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/romans/3#v12)</sup> They are all gone out of the way, they are together become unprofitable; there is none that doeth good, no, not one.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/romans/3#v13)</sup> Their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongues they have used deceit; the poison of asps is under their lips:

    There is none that understandeth, there is none that seeketh after God; their throat is an open sepulchre, with their tongues they have used deceit, the poison of asps is under their lips. This describes the same wicked mouths David speaks of in Psalm 5:9.

**[Psalm 5:10-12](/bible/psalms/5#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/5#v10)</sup> Destroy thou them, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions; for they have rebelled against thee.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/5#v11)</sup> But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice: let them ever shout for joy, because thou defendest them: let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/5#v12)</sup> For thou, Lord, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield.

- Destroy thou them, O God; let them fall by their own counsels; cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions, for they have rebelled against thee. David asks that the wicked fall by their own schemes because they rebelled against the Most High.
- But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice; let them ever shout for joy, because thou defendest them; let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee. The Most High defends those who trust in Him, just as He defended David.
- For thou, Lord, wilt bless the righteous; with favour wilt thou compass him as with a shield. The Most High fights our battles as long as we align ourselves with these scriptures and keep the commandments, including the commandment of brotherly and sisterly love, which Israel sometimes forgets, especially in marriage. Sometimes you have to take an L in this walk.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 47:2-11](/bible/sirach/47#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/47#v2)</sup> As is the fat taken away from the peace offering, so was David chosen out of the children of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/47#v3)</sup> He played with lions as with kids, and with bears as with lambs.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/sirach/47#v4)</sup> Slew he not a giant, when he was yet but young? and did he not take away reproach from the people, when he lifted up his hand with the stone in the sling, and beat down the boasting of Goliath?
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/sirach/47#v5)</sup> For he called upon the most high Lord; and he gave him strength in his right hand to slay that mighty warrior, and set up the horn of his people.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/sirach/47#v6)</sup> So the people honoured him with ten thousands, and praised him in the blessings of the Lord, in that he gave him a crown of glory.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/sirach/47#v7)</sup> For he destroyed the enemies on every side, and brought to nought the Philistines his adversaries, and brake their horn in sunder unto this day.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/sirach/47#v8)</sup> In all his works he praised the Holy One most high with words of glory; with his whole heart he sung songs, and loved him that made him.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/47#v9)</sup> He set singers also before the altar, that by their voices they might make sweet melody, and daily sing praises in their songs.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/47#v10)</sup> He beautified their feasts, and set in order the solemn times until the end, that they might praise his holy name, and that the temple might sound from morning.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/47#v11)</sup> The Lord took away his sins, and exalted his horn for ever: he gave him a covenant of kings, and a throne of glory in Israel.

    Look at how David operated: in all his works he praised the Most High with words of glory and loved Him that made him, and even through all the trials he went through the Lord took away his sins and exalted his horn forever. That is what you want, for the Most High to take away your sins.

# Psalm 6: O Lord, Rebuke Me Not in Thine Anger

Read the chapter → [Psalms 6](/bible/psalms/6)

**[Psalm 6:1-3](/bible/psalms/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/6#v1)</sup> O Lord, rebuke me not in thine anger, neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/6#v2)</sup> Have mercy upon me, O Lord; for I am weak: O Lord, heal me; for my bones are vexed.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/6#v3)</sup> My soul is also sore vexed: but thou, O Lord, how long?

- O Lord, rebuke me not in thine anger, neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure. Pray to the Most High knowing He does not judge you in His anger; think of Moses and how many times the Most High wanted to destroy the people and start over, yet held back.
- Have mercy upon me, O Lord, for I am weak; O Lord, heal me, for my bones are vexed. My soul is also sore vexed; but thou, O Lord, how long? David is weak and vexed in body and soul and cries out how long.

**[Psalm 6:4-7](/bible/psalms/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/6#v4)</sup> Return, O Lord, deliver my soul: oh save me for thy mercies’ sake.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/6#v5)</sup> For in death there is no remembrance of thee: in the grave who shall give thee thanks?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/6#v6)</sup> I am weary with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/6#v7)</sup> Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies.

- Return, O Lord, deliver my soul; oh save me for thy mercies' sake. For in death there is no remembrance of thee; in the grave who shall give thee thanks? David pleads for deliverance for the sake of the Most High's mercy.
- I am weary with my groaning; all the night make I my bed to swim; I water my couch with my tears. Mine eye is consumed because of grief; it waxeth old because of all mine enemies. David is worn down with weeping and grief because of all his enemies.

**[Psalm 6:8-10](/bible/psalms/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/6#v8)</sup> Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/6#v9)</sup> The Lord hath heard my supplication; the Lord will receive my prayer.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/psalms/6#v10)</sup> Let all mine enemies be ashamed and sore vexed: let them return and be ashamed suddenly.

- Depart from me, all ye workers of iniquity; for the Lord hath heard the voice of my weeping. David turns on the workers of iniquity because the Most High has heard his weeping.
- The Lord hath heard my supplication; the Lord will receive my prayer. Let all mine enemies be ashamed and sore vexed; let them return and be ashamed suddenly. The Most High receives David's prayer, and his enemies will be put to shame.
- Go back through these short chapters and read about King David, and again put yourself in his shoes, and it will help you when your own test comes.
