## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 19 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1, 18-34, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 19:1](/bible/acts/19#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/19#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, that, while Apollos was at Corinth, Paul having passed through the upper coasts came to Ephesus: and finding certain disciples,

- “While Apollos was at Corinth, Paul having passed through the upper coasts came to Ephesus”: they swapped. “And finding certain disciples”: when Paul came to Ephesus there were believers in Christ there.
- “Paul having passed through the upper coasts came to Ephesus.” The Apostle Paul came unto Ephesus. That is why the map keeps going up: look how close Ephesus is to Colosse, Ephesus to the left and Colosse to the right.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 19:10](/bible/acts/19#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/acts/19#v10)</sup> And this continued by the space of two years; so that all they which dwelt in Asia heard the word of the Lord Jesus, both Jews and Greeks.

    “And this continued by the space of two years; so that all they which dwelt in Asia heard the word of the Lord Jesus.” All they which dwelt in Asia, so Colosse heard the word that Paul was preaching.

**[Acts 19:18-19](/bible/acts/19#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/acts/19#v18)</sup> And many that believed came, and confessed, and shewed their deeds.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/acts/19#v19)</sup> Many of them also which used curious arts brought their books together, and burned them before all men: and they counted the price of them, and found it fifty thousand pieces of silver.

- “Many of them also which used curious arts brought their books together, and burned them before all men: and they counted the price of them, and found it fifty thousand pieces of silver.” Curious arts are magical arts, the jugglery practised by the Ephesian conjurers. These were books of witchcraft, and it shows what that land was known for.

**[Acts 19:20](/bible/acts/19#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/acts/19#v20)</sup> So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed.

- “So mightily grew the word of God and prevailed.” They repented and burned up their books of witchcraft, which was prevalent in Ephesus.

**[Acts 19:21-22](/bible/acts/19#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/acts/19#v21)</sup> After these things were ended, Paul purposed in the spirit, when he had passed through Macedonia and Achaia, to go to Jerusalem, saying, After I have been there, I must also see Rome.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/19#v22)</sup> So he sent into Macedonia two of them that ministered unto him, Timotheus and Erastus; but he himself stayed in Asia for a season.

- “So he sent into Macedonia two of them that ministered unto him, Timotheus and Erastus”: this Timotheus is the Timothy we read about in 1 and 2 Timothy.

**[Acts 19:23](/bible/acts/19#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/acts/19#v23)</sup> And the same time there arose no small stir about that way.

- The class read these precepts with it.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 14:6](/bible/john/14#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/john/14#v6)</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.

    “And the same time there arose no small stir about that way.” What is that way? “Jesus saith unto him, I am the way, the truth, and the life: no man cometh unto the Father, but by me.” The way they were stirred up and mad about was Christ. They had a problem with it because the whole city was given to witchcraft.

**[Acts 19:24-27](/bible/acts/19#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/acts/19#v24)</sup> For a certain man named Demetrius, a silversmith, which made silver shrines for Diana, brought no small gain unto the craftsmen;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/acts/19#v25)</sup> Whom he called together with the workmen of like occupation, and said, Sirs, ye know that by this craft we have our wealth.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/acts/19#v26)</sup> Moreover ye see and hear, that not alone at Ephesus, but almost throughout all Asia, this Paul hath persuaded and turned away much people, saying that they be no gods, which are made with hands:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/acts/19#v27)</sup> So that not only this our craft is in danger to be set at nought; but also that the temple of the great goddess Diana should be despised, and her magnificence should be destroyed, whom all Asia and the world worshippeth.

- Demetrius made silver shrines for Diana and brought the craftsmen much gain. Paul was persuading people throughout Ephesus and almost all Asia that gods made with hands were no gods. Demetrius was worried about the loss of their livelihood and the loss of Diana's standing. Religion and money were bound together.
- This is the Greek goddess Diana, which was also the Canaanite goddess Ashtoreth, where Easter comes from: the moon goddess, the goddess of fertility. All of those breasts because she represented sexuality, sex, and people committed orgies under her dominance. This was about female power, female dominance.
- It relates to us very much today. The Kemetic community: who do they say is goddess on earth? The black woman. All that black woman worship goes back to this. When Paul went throughout Asia Minor he was preaching against Diana, preaching against woman dominance, teaching them Christ the black Messiah, and everybody was mad as hell at him because he was interfering with their money.
- “A certain man named Demetrius, a silversmith, which made silver shrines for Diana, brought no small gain unto the craftsmen”, and he said, “Sirs, ye know that by this craft we have our wealth.” That is how they made their living.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 1:2-3](/bible/1-timothy/1#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/1#v2)</sup> Unto Timothy, my own son in the faith: Grace, mercy, and peace, from God our Father and Jesus Christ our Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/1#v3)</sup> As I besought thee to abide still at Ephesus, when I went into Macedonia, that thou mightest charge some that they teach no other doctrine,

    Paul calls Timothy his own son in the faith and reminds him that he was left at Ephesus to charge some that they teach no other doctrine. Ephesus is the city of Diana. Timothy has to keep the congregation from bringing its former goddess religion into the truth. That is the setting for the instruction about women learning in subjection.
  - **[1 Corinthians 14:34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)</sup> Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.

    Now we understand why Paul is getting on the woman, with Corinth right there on the map. “Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.” Why? Because he’s a misogynist? No. They were coming from worshipping the goddess Diana, that feminist mother goddess, and they were bringing those principles into Israel. Paul said no, that shall not stand here.
  - **[Revelation 2:18-23](/bible/revelation/2#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/revelation/2#v18)</sup> And unto the angel of the church in Thyatira write; These things saith the Son of God, who hath his eyes like unto a flame of fire, and his feet are like fine brass;

Thyatira is right there in Asia Minor. “Unto the angel of the church in Thyatira write; These things saith the Son of God”. Watch this: “I have a few things against thee, because thou sufferest that woman Jezebel, which calleth herself a prophetess, to teach and to seduce my servants to commit fornication”.

When Alexander was recognized as a Jew, they cried for Diana for about two hours.
