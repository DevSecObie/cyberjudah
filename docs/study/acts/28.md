## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 28 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-6, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 28:3-6](/bible/acts/28#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/28#v3)</sup> And when Paul had gathered a bundle of sticks, and laid them on the fire, there came a viper out of the heat, and fastened on his hand.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/acts/28#v4)</sup> And when the barbarians saw the venomous beast hang on his hand, they said among themselves, No doubt this man is a murderer, whom, though he hath escaped the sea, yet vengeance suffereth not to live.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/acts/28#v5)</sup> And he shook off the beast into the fire, and felt no harm.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/acts/28#v6)</sup> Howbeit they looked when he should have swollen, or fallen down dead suddenly: but after they had looked a great while, and saw no harm come to him, they changed their minds, and said that he was a god.

- A man in Texas calling himself a doctor in his church said Mark 16:18 was being done in his church, healing people, and even raising somebody from the dead. Where was he during Covid? We here in New York and you ain’t never come here and raised nobody up.
- Mark 16:18 was fulfilled with Paul: “there came a viper out of the heat, and fastened on his hand”, and the barbarians said “No doubt this man is a murderer, whom, though he hath escaped the sea, yet vengeance suffereth not to live.” “And he shook off the beast into the fire, and felt no harm.” They looked when he should have swollen or fallen down dead, and when no harm came to him, “they changed their minds, and said that he was a god.”
- Now these churches have healing services where they throw a jacket around and everybody falls back. They should throw some serpents in there too and see what happens. A pastor in some country had a serpent, and it bit him, and he ain’t do too good. Tell him try that.
