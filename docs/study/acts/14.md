## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 14 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-7, 11-15, 19-22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 14:1-2](/bible/acts/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/14#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in Iconium, that they went both together into the synagogue of the Jews, and so spake, that a great multitude both of the Jews and also of the Greeks believed.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/acts/14#v2)</sup> But the unbelieving Jews stirred up the Gentiles, and made their minds evil affected against the brethren.

- It came to pass “in Iconium”, and a great multitude of the Jews and the Greeks believed. Paul was traveling; he wasn’t stationary, he wasn’t local, he was moving around. “But the unbelieving Jews stirred up the Gentiles, and made their minds evil affected against the brethren.”

**[Acts 14:3](/bible/acts/14#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/14#v3)</sup> Long time therefore abode they speaking boldly in the Lord, which gave testimony unto the word of his grace, and granted signs and wonders to be done by their hands.

- “Long time therefore abode they speaking boldly in the Lord.” They weren’t bashful.

**[Acts 14:4-7](/bible/acts/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/acts/14#v4)</sup> But the multitude of the city was divided: and part held with the Jews, and part with the apostles.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/acts/14#v5)</sup> And when there was an assault made both of the Gentiles, and also of the Jews with their rulers, to use them despitefully, and to stone them,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/acts/14#v6)</sup> They were ware of it, and fled unto Lystra and Derbe, cities of Lycaonia, and unto the region that lieth round about:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/acts/14#v7)</sup> And there they preached the gospel.

- When an assault was made to stone them, they “fled unto Lystra and Derbe, cities of Lycaonia, and unto the region that lieth round about: And there they preached the gospel.” They faced persecution in Iconium, left, and went on preaching the gospel. How did they preach it? Boldly.

**[Acts 14:11-13](/bible/acts/14#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/acts/14#v11)</sup> And when the people saw what Paul had done, they lifted up their voices, saying in the speech of Lycaonia, The gods are come down to us in the likeness of men.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/acts/14#v12)</sup> And they called Barnabas, Jupiter; and Paul, Mercurius, because he was the chief speaker.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/acts/14#v13)</sup> Then the priest of Jupiter, which was before their city, brought oxen and garlands unto the gates, and would have done sacrifice with the people.

- “The gods are come down to us in the likeness of men.” They called Barnabas Jupiter and Paul Mercurius “because he was the chief speaker”, and the priest of Jupiter brought oxen and garlands to sacrifice to them. They wanted to worship Paul and Barnabas, looking at them as gods.

**[Acts 14:14-15](/bible/acts/14#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/acts/14#v14)</sup> Which when the apostles, Barnabas and Paul, heard of, they rent their clothes, and ran in among the people, crying out,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/acts/14#v15)</sup> And saying, Sirs, why do ye these things? We also are men of like passions with you, and preach unto you that ye should turn from these vanities unto the living God, which made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and all things that are therein:

- They rent their clothes and said, “Sirs, why do ye these things? We also are men of like passions with you”: we are just like you. Stop this nonsense, “turn from these vanities unto the living God”, keep the commandments.

**[Acts 14:19](/bible/acts/14#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/acts/14#v19)</sup> And there came thither certain Jews from Antioch and Iconium, who persuaded the people, and, having stoned Paul, drew him out of the city, supposing he had been dead.

- “Having stoned Paul, drew him out of the city, supposing he had been dead.” They knocked Paul upside the head with stones, and he went to the third heaven.

**[Acts 14:20-21](/bible/acts/14#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/acts/14#v20)</sup> Howbeit, as the disciples stood round about him, he rose up, and came into the city: and the next day he departed with Barnabas to Derbe.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/acts/14#v21)</sup> And when they had preached the gospel to that city, and had taught many, they returned again to Lystra, and to Iconium, and Antioch,

- “Howbeit, as the disciples stood round about him, he rose up, and came into the city.” They thought Paul was dead and he rose up. And after preaching in Derbe “they returned again to Lystra, and to Iconium, and Antioch”: right back to the place where they tried to kill him. Just like in Kenya: when they stoned us, brothers got hit in the jaw, and we went right back. We didn’t run. We ain’t going nowhere.

**[Acts 14:22](/bible/acts/14#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/14#v22)</sup> Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.

- “Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith, and that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.” We are going to enter the kingdom through much tribulation. It is not going to be no rose garden.
- Why go through much tribulation? To try us, to prove us, to test us, to see if we are worthy. That is the battle fatigue we all go through in this truth, and we have to continue in the faith through it.
- “Confirming the souls of the disciples, and exhorting them to continue in the faith”: that is our job, to exhort you to continue in the faith, to continue in this truth and this walk. Don’t let nothing take you out of this truth.
- “And that we must through much tribulation enter into the kingdom of God.” Through much tribulation we are going to enter into the kingdom. The tribulation has not fully started yet. You see it on a certain scale, but it will intensify. It is going to get worse, and worse, and worse.

  Precepts:
  - **[Micah 4:10](/bible/micah/4#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/micah/4#v10)</sup> Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion, like a woman in travail: for now shalt thou go forth out of the city, and thou shalt dwell in the field, and thou shalt go even to Babylon; there shalt thou be delivered; there the Lord shall redeem thee from the hand of thine enemies.

    “Be in pain, and labour to bring forth, O daughter of Zion”: the Bible says to be in pain. Pain is coming, and it has already begun; pay attention to what Trump and these people are doing. But whatever pain you go through, you still have to labour. You have to be labouring in this truth.
  - **[Revelation 12:17](/bible/revelation/12#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/revelation/12#v17)</sup> And the dragon was wroth with the woman, and went to make war with the remnant of her seed, which keep the commandments of God, and have the testimony of Jesus Christ.

    “And the dragon was wroth with the woman”: the dragon is the beast, the white man, Esau. According to the prophecy, they will be angry at us. “And went to make war with the remnant of her seed”: they will make war with us.
