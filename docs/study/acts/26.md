## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 26 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 5, 18, 22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 26:1](/bible/acts/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/26#v1)</sup> Then Agrippa said unto Paul, Thou art permitted to speak for thyself. Then Paul stretched forth the hand, and answered for himself:

- “Then Agrippa said unto Paul, Thou art permitted to speak for thyself.” Agrippa comes from the family of Herod: the same race of people, the same kinsmen, the same clan.

**[Acts 26:2](/bible/acts/26#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/acts/26#v2)</sup> I think myself happy, king Agrippa, because I shall answer for myself this day before thee touching all the things whereof I am accused of the Jews:

- “I think myself happy, king Agrippa, because I shall answer for myself this day before thee touching all the things whereof I am accused of the Jews”. So you had wicked Israelites accusing Paul.

**[Acts 26:3](/bible/acts/26#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/26#v3)</sup> Especially because I know thee to be expert in all customs and questions which are among the Jews: wherefore I beseech thee to hear me patiently.

- This is the point: “Especially because I know thee to be expert in all customs and questions which are among the Jews”. Why was he an expert? Because he grew up and learned the teachings of Israel. He learned the law, the statutes and the Commandments; he knew the culture, the customs. That’s what they did: to rule us effectively they had to learn us, and to do that they tried to become us.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 22:3](/bible/acts/22#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/acts/22#v3)</sup> I am verily a man which am a Jew, born in Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, yet brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, and taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers, and was zealous toward God, as ye all are this day.

    “I am verily a man which am a Jew, born in Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, yet brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel”. So Paul was educated at the feet of Gamaliel, “and taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers, and was zealous toward God, as ye all are this day.” That was the schooling of Israel.
  - **[Acts 13:1](/bible/acts/13#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/acts/13#v1)</sup> Now there were in the church that was at Antioch certain prophets and teachers; as Barnabas, and Simeon that was called Niger, and Lucius of Cyrene, and Manaen, which had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch, and Saul.

    “Now there were in the church that was at Antioch certain prophets and teachers; as Barnabas, and Simeon that was called” by a name that means black guy, “and Lucius of Cyrene”, which “had been brought up with Herod the tetrarch, and Saul.” Brought up means raised, taught with Herod.

**[Acts 26:5](/bible/acts/26#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/acts/26#v5)</sup> Which knew me from the beginning, if they would testify, that after the most straitest sect of our religion I lived a Pharisee.

- “After the most straitest sect of our religion I lived a Pharisee”: the strictest sect. He was born and raised a Pharisee, under the feet of Gamaliel, and learned the perfect manner of the law. That is Paul’s background.

**[Acts 26:18](/bible/acts/26#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/acts/26#v18)</sup> To open their eyes, and to turn them from darkness to light, and from the power of Satan unto God, that they may receive forgiveness of sins, and inheritance among them which are sanctified by faith that is in me.

- Some of our brothers, like those in 2 Esdras 16, are bound with their sin, and the people in the street are bound with their sin. “To open their eyes”: our job is to open the eyes of our people. “And to turn them from darkness to light”: they don't know where they came from; they come from darkness. Our job is to bring them back to the light, to the commandments.
- “And from the power of Satan”: Satan has power over these people in the world. “Unto God”: turning them to God, knowing who God is. Christ is a black man. Christ only died for the nation of Israel, Christ is going to return for the nation of Israel only, and then the nation of Israel is going to be the superpower that governs the whole planet earth. If you are not from the twelve tribes, welcome to your shackles and chains. Like it or not, that is what God said.
- “That they may receive forgiveness of sins.” These are the people we are fighting for.
- Why are we here? What is our purpose? You have to choose a side: righteousness, your heritage, your nation keeping God’s commandments, gathering together in the laws of the Most High God.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 2:1-2](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)</sup> And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/2#v2)</sup> Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience:

    Our people are in a state of darkness, and our job is to fight Satan to get him off of them. “And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins.” Our people are dead, because they destroyed themselves by breaking God's commandments. They are in a state of death, bound by Satan. When you look at your mother, your uncle, your auntie, inside these religions, they are bound by Satan.

**[Acts 26:22](/bible/acts/26#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/26#v22)</sup> Having therefore obtained help of God, I continue unto this day, witnessing both to small and great, saying none other things than those which the prophets and Moses did say should come:

- This is Paul’s source material. “Having therefore obtained help of God, I continue unto this day, witnessing both to small and great, saying none other things than those which the prophets and Moses did say should come”. Paul was only teaching what Moses and the prophets said.
- That is why Peter mentions the other scriptures. People stumble because they believe Paul was writing things out of thin air. To understand Paul’s letters you must understand the other scriptures, because Paul taught from nothing else than what was written in the law and in the prophets.
- Paul: “Having therefore obtained help of God, I continue unto this day, witnessing both to small and great, saying none other things than those which the prophets and Moses did say should come”. Saying nothing else but what Moses and the prophets said.
- Every single letter Paul wrote was based upon the words of Moses and the prophets. He wasn’t pulling from Matthew, he wasn’t pulling from Mark, Jude or John; he was pulling from the law and the prophets. Christ pulled from the law and the prophets, and Peter pulled from the law and the prophets. When you read Paul’s letters and think he is saying don’t keep the laws, you don’t know what you’re talking about. The law doesn’t say thou shalt not keep what the law says.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 28:23](/bible/acts/28#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/acts/28#v23)</sup> And when they had appointed him a day, there came many to him into his lodging; to whom he expounded and testified the kingdom of God, persuading them concerning Jesus, both out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets, from morning till evening.

    “to whom he expounded and testified the kingdom of God, persuading them concerning Jesus, both out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets, from morning till evening.” Paul was teaching out of the law and the prophets, so his letters are based on the law and the prophets. He was not sitting around making things up.
  - **[Acts 28:23-29](/bible/acts/28#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/acts/28#v23)</sup> And when they had appointed him a day, there came many to him into his lodging; to whom he expounded and testified the kingdom of God, persuading them concerning Jesus, both out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets, from morning till evening.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/acts/28#v24)</sup> And some believed the things which were spoken, and some believed not.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/acts/28#v25)</sup> And when they agreed not among themselves, they departed, after that Paul had spoken one word, Well spake the Holy Ghost by Esaias the prophet unto our fathers,
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/acts/28#v26)</sup> Saying, Go unto this people, and say, Hearing ye shall hear, and shall not understand; and seeing ye shall see, and not perceive:
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/acts/28#v27)</sup> For the heart of this people is waxed gross, and their ears are dull of hearing, and their eyes have they closed; lest they should see with their eyes, and hear with their ears, and understand with their heart, and should be converted, and I should heal them.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/acts/28#v28)</sup> Be it known therefore unto you, that the salvation of God is sent unto the Gentiles, and that they will hear it.
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/acts/28#v29)</sup> And when he had said these words, the Jews departed, and had great reasoning among themselves.

    At this point Paul is in Rome on house arrest, and the elders of the Jews of the synagogues in Rome came to visit him in his lodging, “to whom he expounded and testified the kingdom of God, persuading them concerning Jesus, both out of the law of Moses, and out of the prophets, from morning till evening.” Paul taught Christ out of the law of Moses and the prophets, from morning hours to evening hours.
