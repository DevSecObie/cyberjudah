    > <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/15#v18)</sup> In the same day the Lord made a covenant with Abram, saying, Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates:

    The Most High made a covenant with Abram, "Unto thy seed have I given this land, from the river of Egypt unto the great river, the river Euphrates," so the restitution restores the land of Israel to its proper owners.

**[Acts 3:22-23](/bible/acts/3#v22)**
> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/3#v22)</sup> For Moses truly said unto the fathers, A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear in all things whatsoever he shall say unto you.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/acts/3#v23)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that every soul, which will not hear that prophet, shall be destroyed from among the people.

- "For Moses truly said unto the fathers, A prophet shall the Lord your God raise up unto you of your brethren, like unto me; him shall ye hear in all things," and every soul that will not hear that prophet shall be destroyed from among the people. The prophet is raised up "from among their brethren," meaning from among men, which proves Christ came through the seed of a man, not immaculate conception.
- Peter is quoting Moses, and every scripture in the New Testament is quoted from the Old, so there is no such thing as reading only the New Testament and skipping precept upon precept.
  - **[Deuteronomy 18:18](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/deuteronomy/18#v18)</sup> I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee, and will put my words in his mouth; and he shall speak unto them all that I shall command him.

    "I will raise them up a Prophet from among their brethren, like unto thee, and will put my words in his mouth," the passage Peter quotes.
  - **[Exodus 6:23](/bible/exodus/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/exodus/6#v23)</sup> And Aaron took him Elisheba, daughter of Amminadab, sister of Naashon, to wife; and she bare him Nadab, and Abihu, Eleazar, and Ithamar.

    Aaron married Elisheba and she bore him children, establishing Amminadab in the lineage, because the prophet comes from among the brethren, meaning from a man.
  - **[Matthew 1:1](/bible/matthew/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/1#v1)</sup> The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham.

    "The book of the generation of Jesus Christ, the son of David, the son of Abraham," the genealogy tracing the seed of man.
  - **[Matthew 1:4](/bible/matthew/1#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/1#v4)</sup> And Aram begat Aminadab; and Aminadab begat Naasson; and Naasson begat Salmon;

    Aram begat Amminadab, and Amminadab begat Naashon, showing the generation of Christ runs through the lineage of men, the same Amminadab of Exodus 6.
  - **[Matthew 1:16](/bible/matthew/1#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/1#v16)</sup> And Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom was born Jesus, who is called Christ.

    Jacob begat Joseph the husband of Mary, of whom Jesus was born, because the man carries the seed the way an apple seed determines the tree, not the soil; the man determines the nationality, not the woman.
- "Like unto thee," like unto Moses, means the same similarities: Moses spoke to the Most High directly, mediated between the Most High and Israel, gave the laws, was hidden as a child when the young were sought to be killed, and led Israel toward the promised land.
  - **[Deuteronomy 34:9](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/34#v9)</sup> And Joshua the son of Nun was full of the spirit of wisdom; for Moses had laid his hands upon him: and the children of Israel hearkened unto him, and did as the Lord commanded Moses.

    Joshua was full of the spirit of wisdom, but "there arose not a prophet since in Israel like unto Moses, whom the LORD knew face to face," so the prophet like Moses is not Joshua.
  - **[Matthew 17:5](/bible/matthew/17#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/17#v5)</sup> While he yet spake, behold, a bright cloud overshadowed them: and behold a voice out of the cloud, which said, This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him.

    A voice out of the cloud says, "This is my beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased; hear ye him," showing Christ is the only other prophet the Most High dealt with face to face and put his words in his mouth.
  - **[2 Samuel 7:12-13](/bible/2-samuel/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/2-samuel/7#v12)</sup> And when thy days be fulfilled, and thou shalt sleep with thy fathers, I will set up thy seed after thee, which shall proceed out of thy bowels, and I will establish his kingdom.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/2-samuel/7#v13)</sup> He shall build an house for my name, and I will stablish the throne of his kingdom for ever.

    The Most High would set up David's seed that proceeds out of his bowels and establish the throne of his kingdom forever, which is Christ's everlasting kingdom.

**[Acts 3:24](/bible/acts/3#v24)**
> <sup>[24](/bible/acts/3#v24)</sup> Yea, and all the prophets from Samuel and those that follow after, as many as have spoken, have likewise foretold of these days.

- "Yea, and all the prophets from Samuel and those that follow after, as many as have spoken, have likewise foretold of these days." All the prophets from Samuel onward foretold these days, tying back to the "you" who are the men of Israel established in verse 12.

**[Acts 3:25](/bible/acts/3#v25)**
> <sup>[25](/bible/acts/3#v25)</sup> Ye are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed.

- "Ye are the children of the prophets, and of the covenant which God made with our fathers, saying unto Abraham, And in thy seed shall all the kindreds of the earth be blessed." The "ye" are the children of Israel, and "kindreds" means a lineage or family that descends from a common progenitor, Abraham, not all nations.
  - **[Galatians 3:16](/bible/galatians/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/galatians/3#v16)</sup> Now to Abraham and his seed were the promises made. He saith not, And to seeds, as of many; but as of one, And to thy seed, which is Christ.

    The promises were made "to Abraham and his seed... not... to seeds, as of many; but as of one... which is Christ," so the blessing comes through the one seed, Christ, to the same people.
  - **[Amos 3:1-2](/bible/amos/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/amos/3#v1)</sup> Hear this word that the Lord hath spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/amos/3#v2)</sup> You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.

    The Most High speaks against "the whole family which I brought up," saying, "You only have I known of all the families of the earth," so "all the kindreds" points to the one family, Israel.
  - **[Genesis 28:14](/bible/genesis/28#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/28#v14)</sup> And thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth, and thou shalt spread abroad to the west, and to the east, and to the north, and to the south: and in thee and in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.

    "Thy seed shall be as the dust of the earth... and in thee and in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed," part of the promise to Abraham through Israel.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:25](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v25)**
    > <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v25)</sup> The Lord shall cause thee to be smitten before thine enemies: thou shalt go out one way against them, and flee seven ways before them: and shalt be removed into all the kingdoms of the earth.

    Israel would be smitten before their enemies and "removed into all the kingdoms of the earth," the same scattered families of the earth that are to be blessed.

**[Acts 3:26](/bible/acts/3#v26)**
> <sup>[26](/bible/acts/3#v26)</sup> Unto you first God, having raised up his Son Jesus, sent him to bless you, in turning away every one of you from his iniquities.

- "Unto you first God, having raised up his Son Jesus, sent him to bless you, in turning away every one of you from his iniquities." "Unto you first" means unto Judah, the Jews first, and the "you" being turned from iniquities is Israel, the same audience throughout the chapter.
- There was an order and prophecy for the twelve tribes to be redeemed. The gospel went first to the southern kingdom, Judah, Benjamin, and Levi, the "Jews," and the "Jew and Gentile" and "Jew and Greek" language is the split between the southern kingdom and the northern kingdom of Israel, not Israel and the nations. The Most High did not cast away the northern tribes; the whole twelve tribes are scattered and being saved.
  - **[Zechariah 12:7](/bible/zechariah/12#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/12#v7)</sup> The Lord also shall save the tents of Judah first, that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem do not magnify themselves against Judah.

    "The LORD also shall save the tents of Judah first," showing the order in which the tribes are redeemed.
  - **[Acts 11:19](/bible/acts/11#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/acts/11#v19)</sup> Now they which were scattered abroad upon the persecution that arose about Stephen travelled as far as Phenice, and Cyprus, and Antioch, preaching the word to none but unto the Jews only.

    The scattered disciples preached the word "to none but unto the Jews only," which is the "unto you first."
  - **[James 1:1](/bible/james/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/james/1#v1)</sup> James, a servant of God and of the Lord Jesus Christ, to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad, greeting.

    James writes "to the twelve tribes which are scattered abroad," so all twelve tribes are still in view, not three tribes only.
  - **[Matthew 1:21](/bible/matthew/1#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/1#v21)</sup> And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.

    "Thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins," and his people are Israel, whom he turns from their iniquities.
