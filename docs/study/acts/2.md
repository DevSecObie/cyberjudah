Studying the book of Acts is what makes the writings of Paul and most of the New Testament open up, because the acts of the apostles are literally the acts they carried out on this earth to spread the gospel, and we are living those same acts in these last days. We are defenders of the gospel and defenders of the faith, so no apologist, riserist, or pastor should ever be able to confound us. The whole chapter is built to prove two things: that the law was still in full effect after Christ died, and that the tongues of Pentecost were the languages of the nations where the house of Israel had been scattered, one race of people dispersed to every country on the globe.

**[Acts 2:1](/bible/acts/2#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/2#v1)</sup> And when the day of Pentecost was fully come, they were all with one accord in one place.

- The day of Pentecost being kept confounds the claim that after Christ died the law was done away with. If the ceremonial law was nailed to the cross, why did the disciples keep gathering for Pentecost, and why did Christ never once say to stop keeping Passover, the feasts, or the Sabbath.
- Christ was born around the feasts, killed through the Passover, went up at the feast of Tabernacles, and read in the synagogue every Sabbath as his custom. The law remained in effect after his death, and the disciples continued keeping the feast days written in [Leviticus 23](/bible/leviticus/23).
- Pentecost comes from penta, meaning fifty, the fiftieth day counted from the Sabbath after Passover. It is also called the Feast of Weeks and the first fruits, and Israel kept it through every captivity, Assyrian, Babylonian, and Greek.

>
> <sup>[24](/bible/acts/2#v24)</sup> Whom God hath raised up, having loosed the pains of death: because it was not possible that he should be holden of it.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/acts/2#v25)</sup> For David speaketh concerning him, I foresaw the Lord always before my face, for he is on my right hand, that I should not be moved:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/acts/2#v26)</sup> Therefore did my heart rejoice, and my tongue was glad; moreover also my flesh shall rest in hope:
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/acts/2#v27)</sup> Because thou wilt not leave my soul in hell, neither wilt thou suffer thine Holy One to see corruption.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/acts/2#v28)</sup> Thou hast made known to me the ways of life; thou shalt make me full of joy with thy countenance.

- Christ was delivered by the determinate counsel and foreknowledge of the Most High, taken and crucified by wicked hands, and raised up because death could not hold him. Peter proves the resurrection by quoting King David, showing again that the apostles built everything on the Old Testament rather than inventing new doctrine.

**[Acts 2:29-31](/bible/acts/2#v29)**
> <sup>[29](/bible/acts/2#v29)</sup> Men and brethren, let me freely speak unto you of the patriarch David, that he is both dead and buried, and his sepulchre is with us unto this day.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/acts/2#v30)</sup> Therefore being a prophet, and knowing that God had sworn with an oath to him, that of the fruit of his loins, according to the flesh, he would raise up Christ to sit on his throne;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/acts/2#v31)</sup> He seeing this before spake of the resurrection of Christ, that his soul was not left in hell, neither his flesh did see corruption.

- David is dead and buried and his sepulchre is still with us, so he was speaking as a prophet, not of himself. Knowing the Most High had sworn that of the fruit of his loins, the seed and lineage from his body, a king would be raised to sit on his throne, David foresaw the resurrection of Christ.
  - **[Psalms 132:11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/132#v11)</sup> The Lord hath sworn in truth unto David; he will not turn from it; Of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne.

    The Most High sware in truth unto David, of the fruit of thy body will I set upon thy throne; Christ the son of David is that king.

**[Acts 2:32-35](/bible/acts/2#v32)**
> <sup>[32](/bible/acts/2#v32)</sup> This Jesus hath God raised up, whereof we all are witnesses.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/acts/2#v33)</sup> Therefore being by the right hand of God exalted, and having received of the Father the promise of the Holy Ghost, he hath shed forth this, which ye now see and hear.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/acts/2#v34)</sup> For David is not ascended into the heavens: but he saith himself, The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou on my right hand,
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/acts/2#v35)</sup> Until I make thy foes thy footstool.

- This Jesus the Most High raised up, and the apostles are witnesses; being exalted to the right hand and having received the promise of the Holy Ghost, he shed it forth. Understanding has to be opened for a man to know Christ is the Messiah, which is why not everyone grasped it, while the rest were blinded.
- David himself did not ascend, but said the Most High told his Lord to sit at his right hand until his foes are made his footstool, another prophecy pointing to Christ.
  - **[Matthew 16:13-17](/bible/matthew/16#v13)**

    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/16#v14)</sup> And they said, Some say that thou art John the Baptist: some, Elias; and others, Jeremias, or one of the prophets.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/16#v15)</sup> He saith unto them, But whom say ye that I am?
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/16#v16)</sup> And Simon Peter answered and said, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/16#v17)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto him, Blessed art thou, Simon Bar–jona: for flesh and blood hath not revealed it unto thee, but my Father which is in heaven.

    Peter's confession that Jesus is the Christ was not revealed by flesh and blood but by the Father; understanding of the Messiah must be given.
  - **[Romans 11:8-9](/bible/romans/11#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/romans/11#v8)</sup> (According as it is written, God hath given them the spirit of slumber, eyes that they should not see, and ears that they should not hear;) unto this day.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/romans/11#v9)</sup> And David saith, Let their table be made a snare, and a trap, and a stumblingblock, and a recompence unto them:

    The Most High gave the rest the spirit of slumber and let their table be a snare; not everyone reads and understands the same, because it is spiritual.
  - **[Psalms 110:1](/bible/psalms/110#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/110#v1)</sup> The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.

    The Lord said unto my Lord, Sit thou at my right hand, until I make thine enemies thy footstool.

**[Acts 2:36](/bible/acts/2#v36)**
> <sup>[36](/bible/acts/2#v36)</sup> Therefore let all the house of Israel know assuredly, that God hath made that same Jesus, whom ye have crucified, both Lord and Christ.

- Peter concludes that all the house of Israel should know assuredly that the Most High made that same Jesus whom they crucified both Lord and Christ. He reached this by going through all the Old Testament prophecies, the very method we use to prove the identity of Israel today.

**[Acts 2:37](/bible/acts/2#v37)**
> <sup>[37](/bible/acts/2#v37)</sup> Now when they heard this, they were pricked in their heart, and said unto Peter and to the rest of the apostles, Men and brethren, what shall we do?

- Hearing this they were pricked in their heart and asked what they should do, because Peter convinced them by prophecy that this was the man David and the prophets spoke of. The difference between then and now is that they knew their nationality but not Christ; today people accept Christ worldwide, so our ministry expounds that Christ was black and that salvation is to Israel.

**[Acts 2:38](/bible/acts/2#v38)**
> <sup>[38](/bible/acts/2#v38)</sup> Then Peter said unto them, Repent, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins, and ye shall receive the gift of the Holy Ghost.

- Repent means sin no more, and be baptized every one of you in the name of Jesus Christ for the remission of sins. It is done in the name of Jesus because Christ was not fully known then and the people had followed animal sacrifice, so now they had to believe on his name.
- The gift of the Holy Ghost that follows is understanding being opened. Baptism with the Holy Ghost is the spirit of wisdom and understanding, seen when Christ opened the disciples' understanding to grasp the scriptures concerning him.
  - **[Matthew 3:11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/3#v11)</sup> I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:

    John baptized with water unto repentance, but he that cometh after shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost.
  - **[Luke 24:44-45](/bible/luke/24#v44)**
    > <sup>[44](/bible/luke/24#v44)</sup> And he said unto them, These are the words which I spake unto you, while I was yet with you, that all things must be fulfilled, which were written in the law of Moses, and in the prophets, and in the psalms, concerning me.
    >
    > <sup>[45](/bible/luke/24#v45)</sup> Then opened he their understanding, that they might understand the scriptures,

    Christ expounded all things written in the law of Moses, the prophets, and the psalms concerning him, then opened their understanding to understand the scriptures.

- The promise is unto you and to your children and to all that are afar off, meaning Israel and their seed, not all races and nations. The "afar off" are the scattered ones of verse 5, the Israelites dispersed to the four corners of the earth, as many as the Most High shall call.

    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/9#v5)</sup> Whose are the fathers, and of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came, who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.

    Paul's kinsmen according to the flesh who are Israelites, to whom pertain the adoption, the glory, the covenants, the law, the service, and the promises.
  - **[Acts 26:6-7](/bible/acts/26#v6)**

    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/acts/26#v7)</sup> Unto which promise our twelve tribes, instantly serving God day and night, hope to come. For which hope’s sake, king Agrippa, I am accused of the Jews.

    Paul stands judged for the hope of the promise made unto the fathers, unto which the twelve tribes hope to come; his ministry was to Israel.

**[Acts 2:40-41](/bible/acts/2#v40)**
> <sup>[40](/bible/acts/2#v40)</sup> And with many other words did he testify and exhort, saying, Save yourselves from this untoward generation.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/acts/2#v41)</sup> Then they that gladly received his word were baptized: and the same day there were added unto them about three thousand souls.

- Peter exhorts them to save themselves from this untoward generation, and those who gladly received his word were baptized. About three thousand souls were added that same day, baptized meaning their understanding was opened to believe everything brought out concerning Christ.

**[Acts 2:42](/bible/acts/2#v42)**
> <sup>[42](/bible/acts/2#v42)</sup> And they continued stedfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers.

- They continued stedfastly in the apostles' doctrine, which is Christ's doctrine, in fellowship, breaking of bread, and prayers, and they did it daily in the temple. This is why we open the schools and hold class seven days a week; if you believe, you addict yourself to the gospel and count sleep and everything else as nothing next to teaching it.

**[Acts 2:43-45](/bible/acts/2#v43)**
> <sup>[43](/bible/acts/2#v43)</sup> And fear came upon every soul: and many wonders and signs were done by the apostles.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/acts/2#v44)</sup> And all that believed were together, and had all things common;
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/acts/2#v45)</sup> And sold their possessions and goods, and parted them to all men, as every man had need.

- Fear came on every soul, and all that believed were together and had all things common. Having all things common means the same speech and the same mind, perfectly joined together, with blameless conversation and pleasure in the law, so that when the mind is fixed on the law, materialistic things stop mattering.
  - **[1 Maccabees 4:42](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v42)**
    > <sup>[42](/bible/1-maccabees/4#v42)</sup> So he chose priests of blameless conversation, such as had pleasure in the law:

    The standard for the body is blameless conversation and pleasure in the law of the Most High.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 9:15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/9#v15)</sup> Let thy talk be with the wise, and all thy communication in the law of the most High.

    Let all our communication be in the law of the Most High, having all things in common.

**[Acts 2:46-47](/bible/acts/2#v46)**
> <sup>[46](/bible/acts/2#v46)</sup> And they, continuing daily with one accord in the temple, and breaking bread from house to house, did eat their meat with gladness and singleness of heart,
>
> <sup>[47](/bible/acts/2#v47)</sup> Praising God, and having favour with all the people. And the Lord added to the church daily such as should be saved.

- Continuing daily with one accord in the temple, breaking bread with gladness and singleness of heart, they had favour with all the people, and the Most High added to the congregation daily such as should be saved. It is the Most High who adds, so when a congregation is not growing, something is wrong: the apostles' doctrine is not being followed, the people do not have all things in common, or they lack the fear and the one mind.
- The order of growth follows the leaders getting their minds right. When the bishops and deacons were right, the Most High added; then the captains, the officers, and the people, and he keeps adding more and more as long as we do what is pleasing to him.
