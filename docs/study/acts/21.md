## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 21 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 21, 27-28, 37-40, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 21:21](/bible/acts/21#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/acts/21#v21)</sup> And they are informed of thee, that thou teachest all the Jews which are among the Gentiles to forsake Moses, saying that they ought not to circumcise their children, neither to walk after the customs.

- “And they are informed of thee, that thou teachest all the Jews which are among the Gentiles”. Do you see it? All the Jews, among the Gentiles. Why? Because we were scattered amongst all nations.
- “To forsake Moses, saying that they ought not to circumcise their children, neither to walk after the customs.” That was the lie they were accusing the Apostle Paul of.

**[Acts 21:27](/bible/acts/21#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/acts/21#v27)</sup> And when the seven days were almost ended, the Jews which were of Asia, when they saw him in the temple, stirred up all the people, and laid hands on him,

- “The Jews which were of Asia, when they saw him in the temple, stirred up all the people, and laid hands on him”.

**[Acts 21:28](/bible/acts/21#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/acts/21#v28)</sup> Crying out, Men of Israel, help: This is the man, that teacheth all men every where against the people, and the law, and this place: and further brought Greeks also into the temple, and hath polluted this holy place.

- “Crying out, Men of Israel, help: This is the man, that teacheth all men every where against the people, and the law, and this place”.
- “And further brought Greeks also into the temple, and hath polluted this holy place.” Those Greeks were Hellenized Jews, Greek-speaking Jews. Greeks and Grecians are to be distinguished: Grecians were Greek-speaking Jews.
- So this has been the acts of the apostles and foundation through Christ, so that when you open up the book of Acts, you can actually understand what it’s talking about.

**[Acts 21:37-38](/bible/acts/21#v37)**

> <sup>[37](/bible/acts/21#v37)</sup> And as Paul was to be led into the castle, he said unto the chief captain, May I speak unto thee? Who said, Canst thou speak Greek?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/acts/21#v38)</sup> Art not thou that Egyptian, which before these days madest an uproar, and leddest out into the wilderness four thousand men that were murderers?

- “Art not thou that Egyptian”: the chief captain thought Paul was an Egyptian. The Egyptians are like the Dinka tribe: very tall, thin, lanky, dark skinned. It is telling you what Paul looked like.
- The chief captain asked Paul, “Canst thou speak Greek? Art not thou that Egyptian, which before these days madest an uproar, and leddest out into the wilderness four thousand men that were murderers?” He took him for a particular Egyptian.

**[Acts 21:39-40](/bible/acts/21#v39)**

> <sup>[39](/bible/acts/21#v39)</sup> But Paul said, I am a man which am a Jew of Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city: and, I beseech thee, suffer me to speak unto the people.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/acts/21#v40)</sup> And when he had given him licence, Paul stood on the stairs, and beckoned with the hand unto the people. And when there was made a great silence, he spake unto them in the Hebrew tongue, saying,

- “I am a man which am a Jew of Tarsus, a city in Cilicia”: Cilicia is Turkey. “A citizen of no mean city”: a citizen of no ghetto. Paul was not raised or born in a ghetto.
- “I am a man which am a Jew of Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city”: a good place. And he spake to the people “in the Hebrew tongue.” Paul was a Jew of Cilicia. He was not a Roman or a white man.
- “But Paul said, I am a man which am a Jew of Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, a citizen of no mean city”: I am a Jew, and I am not from a bad place. Paul passed for an Egyptian. Moses passed for an Egyptian. Joseph passed for an Egyptian. And Christ too: his family ran into Africa from white folks. What more do you possibly need?

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 22:3](/bible/acts/22#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/acts/22#v3)</sup> I am verily a man which am a Jew, born in Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, yet brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, and taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers, and was zealous toward God, as ye all are this day.

    “I am verily a man which am a Jew, born in Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, yet brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, and taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers.” Paul came from a very well-educated and financially well-off family. He was born in Turkey and they took him to Jerusalem to learn under Gamaliel. His family had money and they were very well educated. That is how Paul was able to speak all those languages. He had the education and the finances, all under Rome.
  - **[Philippians 3:8](/bible/philippians/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/philippians/3#v8)</sup> Yea doubtless, and I count all things but loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I have suffered the loss of all things, and do count them but dung, that I may win Christ,

    With all the education and the wealth Paul had from his family, watch what he says: “I count all things but loss for the excellency of the knowledge of Christ Jesus my Lord: for whom I have suffered the loss of all things.” Paul gave everything up. “And do count them but dung”: dung means doo-doo; he counted them as nothing, “that I may win Christ.”
