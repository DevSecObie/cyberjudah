## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 16 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 16-18, 23, 25-26, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 16:16](/bible/acts/16#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/acts/16#v16)</sup> And it came to pass, as we went to prayer, a certain damsel possessed with a spirit of divination met us, which brought her masters much gain by soothsaying:

- Muhammad said an angel came and gave him the revelation of the Quran. Remember that Deuteronomy 32 says we sacrificed unto devils.
- “a certain damsel possessed with a spirit of divination met us, which brought her masters much gain by soothsaying”. She was a witch. She had the spirit of divination, the same way Balaam had the spirit of divination and could see things in the future. This woman had a devil on her and could see things. There are evil spirits with these kinds of powers, to certain limits.

  Precepts:
  - **[Job 12:16](/bible/job/12#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/job/12#v16)</sup> With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his.

    “With him is strength and wisdom: the deceived and the deceiver are his.” The deceiver and the deceived both belong to the Lord. The deceiver, Islam, came up on the earth in the seventh century and began deceiving the world, and the Lord allowed it to happen. He allowed that religion to be created because it goes hand in hand with his prophecies and the things that have to take place.

**[Acts 16:17](/bible/acts/16#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/acts/16#v17)</sup> The same followed Paul and us, and cried, saying, These men are the servants of the most high God, which shew unto us the way of salvation.

- “The same followed Paul and us, and cried, saying, These men are the servants of the most high God, which shew unto us the way of salvation.” The demon in her was following Paul and the apostles. It could see who they were.

**[Acts 16:18](/bible/acts/16#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/acts/16#v18)</sup> And this did she many days. But Paul, being grieved, turned and said to the spirit, I command thee in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her. And he came out the same hour.

- “And this did she many days. But Paul, being grieved, turned and said to the spirit, I command thee in the name of Jesus Christ to come out of her. And he came out the same hour.” Paul rebuked the spirit, the devil she was possessed with, and it came out.
- Muhammad claimed to see things and have revelations. It would be no surprise if evil spirits were dealing with him, the spirit of a devil, the same way that soothsaying spirit knew things about the apostles and Balaam saw things that were not God showing him. And it is not only the white man who has the spirit of a devil.
- If an angel or anybody comes and tells you anything different, don’t listen to them. Even if what they tell you comes to pass, don’t follow them: it is not the spirit of God on that person but the spirit of a devil, and it could be the Lord proving you, to see whether you will trust in him or in that devil.

**[Acts 16:23](/bible/acts/16#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/acts/16#v23)</sup> And when they had laid many stripes upon them, they cast them into prison, charging the jailor to keep them safely:

- “And when they had laid many stripes upon them, they cast them into prison.” They beat Paul and Silas and threw them in prison, “and made their feet fast in the stocks.”

**[Acts 16:25](/bible/acts/16#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/acts/16#v25)</sup> And at midnight Paul and Silas prayed, and sang praises unto God: and the prisoners heard them.

- “And at midnight Paul and Silas prayed, and sang praises unto God: and the prisoners heard them.”

**[Acts 16:26](/bible/acts/16#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/acts/16#v26)</sup> And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken: and immediately all the doors were opened, and every one’s bands were loosed.

- “And suddenly there was a great earthquake, so that the foundations of the prison were shaken: and immediately all the doors were opened, and every one’s bands were loosed.” Not a little earthquake: a great earthquake, when they prayed. When I pray, I want to feel that: an earthquake, knowing the Lord hears my prayers.
