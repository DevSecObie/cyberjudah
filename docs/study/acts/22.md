## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 22 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-4, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 22:3](/bible/acts/22#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/22#v3)</sup> I am verily a man which am a Jew, born in Tarsus, a city in Cilicia, yet brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel, and taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers, and was zealous toward God, as ye all are this day.

- “Brought up in this city at the feet of Gamaliel”: Paul came from a high, prestigious family, and he sat and learned under a famous teacher, Gamaliel, a Pharisee. He was “taught according to the perfect manner of the law of the fathers”, a very prestigious background of schooling in the law, born and raised a Pharisee and “zealous toward God”. He was not an idiot who sat down and wrote errors and mistakes into his letters, like some of the idiots say on YouTube.

**[Acts 22:4](/bible/acts/22#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/acts/22#v4)</sup> And I persecuted this way unto the death, binding and delivering into prisons both men and women.

- “And I persecuted this way unto the death”: Paul said, I persecuted this way, meaning them that followed Christ, unto death, “binding and delivering into prisons both men and women.”
- Remember Revelation 2:9-10, read earlier: the devil shall cast some of you into prison; be thou faithful unto death. People read things and think, oh, that’s only for back then. No, it’s true now. We’re waking up. You think they’re going to stand back? They say that kingdom is ending, and now these guys are waking up about a new kingdom that is coming.
- “Both men and women”: your wives will not escape.
- “I persecuted this way unto the death”: this doctrine, this teaching. He was killing people that followed Christ, “binding and delivering into prisons both men and women”, locking them up for following that way.
