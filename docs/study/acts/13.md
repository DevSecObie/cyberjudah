**[Acts 13:43-45](/bible/acts/13#v43)**

> <sup>[43](/bible/acts/13#v43)</sup> Now when the congregation was broken up, many of the Jews and religious proselytes followed Paul and Barnabas: who, speaking to them, persuaded them to continue in the grace of God.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/acts/13#v44)</sup> And the next sabbath day came almost the whole city together to hear the word of God.
>

- Paul and Barnabas “persuaded them to continue in the grace of God.” Paul and Barnabas are on fire, persuading the people. “And the next sabbath day came almost the whole city together to hear the word of God”: the whole city comes out, because Paul and Barnabas were on fire persuading these people, gathering the twelve tribes of Israel together.

Now in Acts 13, “when the Jews saw the multitudes, they were filled with envy, and spake against those things which were spoken by Paul”.

Like today, you’re going to see it: they want to keep that division.

**[Acts 13:46](/bible/acts/13#v46)**

> <sup>[46](/bible/acts/13#v46)</sup> Then Paul and Barnabas waxed bold, and said, It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you: but seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the Gentiles.

- “It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you”: the reader has to stress first. You who? The Jews, the kingdom of Judah. “but seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the Gentiles.”
- “It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you”: to Judah first. “But seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the Gentiles”: then to the northern kingdom.
- “It was necessary that the word of God should first have been spoken to you”. Why was it necessary that the word be spoken first to the Jews? Because the prophets said Judah first; unlike Christians, the apostles followed the Old Testament and understood it.
- “But seeing ye put it from you, and judge yourselves unworthy of everlasting life, lo, we turn to the Gentiles.” When you say these Gentiles are heathens, you are saying Paul and them went to teach the white man, the African, the Arab and the Filipino. No: they went to the northern kingdom scattered abroad, the Gentiles dispersed among the nations, and to the dispersed of Judah.
