## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Acts 24 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 5, 16, 22, 24-25, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Acts 24:5](/bible/acts/24#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/acts/24#v5)</sup> For we have found this man a pestilent fellow, and a mover of sedition among all the Jews throughout the world, and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes:

- “For we have found this man a pestilent fellow, and a mover of sedition among all the Jews throughout the world, and a ringleader of the sect of the Nazarenes”: that is talking about Christ.
- Sect is a group of people with somewhat different religious beliefs, typically regarded as heretical by the larger group to which they belong; and a word similar to it is cult. That’s what they said: oh, they’re a cult, these guys are a cult. Sect and cult are the same thing. That’s what Acts 24 is saying: a ringleader of the sect, or cult, of the Nazarenes.

  Precepts:
  - **[Acts 28:22](/bible/acts/28#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/acts/28#v22)</sup> But we desire to hear of thee what thou thinkest: for as concerning this sect, we know that every where it is spoken against.

    “But we desire to hear of thee what thou thinkest: for as concerning this sect”, that cult, “we know that every where it is spoken against.”

**[Acts 24:16](/bible/acts/24#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/acts/24#v16)</sup> And herein do I exercise myself, to have always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men.

- Our job as prophets, as leaders: Paul helps us here. “And herein do I exercise myself, to have always a conscience void of offence toward God, and toward men.” That is the same thing Christ called the greatest commandment: love your God with all your heart, and love your neighbor as yourself.
- So we have to be constantly fighting and understand the battle we are fighting and what we need to do for this salvation. We have to be constantly aware of what is going on inside our mind, because that is where the war begins. As long as you are in this flesh, when you try your best not to offend God, Satan is in full force.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 7:7-8](/bible/romans/7#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/romans/7#v7)</sup> What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law: for I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/romans/7#v8)</sup> But sin, taking occasion by the commandment, wrought in me all manner of concupiscence. For without the law sin was dead.

    Paul was set up for two things: to show the elect there is hope, and to show the wicked of our people that they ain't going to get it. “What shall we say then? Is the law sin? God forbid. Nay, I had not known sin, but by the law.” The law shows us what sin is. “For I had not known lust, except the law had said, Thou shalt not covet.”

**[Acts 24:22](/bible/acts/24#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/acts/24#v22)</sup> And when Felix heard these things, having more perfect knowledge of that way, he deferred them, and said, When Lysias the chief captain shall come down, I will know the uttermost of your matter.

- If none of this is true, and what we teach is heresy, they wouldn’t go to these great lengths and spend all this money. “And when Felix heard these things, having more perfect knowledge of that way”: like Katt Williams said, the liar knows the lie he told. Felix knew exactly what Paul was teaching.

**[Acts 24:24](/bible/acts/24#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/acts/24#v24)</sup> And after certain days, when Felix came with his wife Drusilla, which was a Jewess, he sent for Paul, and heard him concerning the faith in Christ.

- “He sent for Paul, and heard him concerning the faith in Christ.” He knew about that way, and he wanted to hear it.

**[Acts 24:25](/bible/acts/24#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/acts/24#v25)</sup> And as he reasoned of righteousness, temperance, and judgment to come, Felix trembled, and answered, Go thy way for this time; when I have a convenient season, I will call for thee.

- “And as he reasoned of righteousness, temperance, and judgment to come, Felix trembled”. Paul told him of righteousness, the commandments, of temperance, and of the judgment to come. If the judgment Paul taught were foolishness, like a black man coming out of the sky, Felix would have said, you’re just crazy. Instead he trembled. That is why Esau is crazy over us on the street corners: he hears Revelation 2:9 and it clicks, I have to get payback. As James said, the devils also believe, and tremble.
