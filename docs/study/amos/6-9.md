Read the chapter → [Amos 6](/bible/amos/6)

This session finishes the book of Amos, moving through chapters 6 to 9. The whole stretch is prophesied against the northern kingdom because of their idolatry and the things they invented for themselves. Judgment was coming on them, yet the Most High promises at the end to bring them back.

**[Amos 6:1-3](/bible/amos/6#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/6#v1)</sup> Woe to them that are at ease in Zion, and trust in the mountain of Samaria, which are named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/6#v2)</sup> Pass ye unto Calneh, and see; and from thence go ye to Hamath the great: then go down to Gath of the Philistines: be they better than these kingdoms? or their border greater than your border?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/6#v3)</sup> Ye that put far away the evil day, and cause the seat of violence to come near;

- Woe to those at ease in Zion and trusting in the mountain of Samaria, named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came.
- They are told to pass over to Calneh, to Hamath the great, and down to Gath of the Philistines, to see whether those kingdoms were better or their borders greater than theirs.
- They put far away the evil day and cause the seat of violence to come near.

**[Amos 6:4-7](/bible/amos/6#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/6#v4)</sup> That lie upon beds of ivory, and stretch themselves upon their couches, and eat the lambs out of the flock, and the calves out of the midst of the stall;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/6#v5)</sup> That chant to the sound of the viol, and invent to themselves instruments of musick, like David;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/6#v6)</sup> That drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the chief ointments: but they are not grieved for the affliction of Joseph.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/6#v7)</sup> Therefore now shall they go captive with the first that go captive, and the banquet of them that stretched themselves shall be removed.

- They lie upon beds of ivory, stretch themselves on their couches, and eat the lambs out of the flock and the calves out of the stall.
- They chant to the sound of the viol, invent to themselves instruments of music like David, drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the chief ointments, but they are not grieved for the affliction of Joseph.
- Therefore they shall go captive with the first that go captive, and the banquet of them that stretch themselves shall be removed.

**[Amos 6:8-11](/bible/amos/6#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/6#v8)</sup> The Lord God hath sworn by himself, saith the Lord the God of hosts, I abhor the excellency of Jacob, and hate his palaces: therefore will I deliver up the city with all that is therein.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/6#v9)</sup> And it shall come to pass, if there remain ten men in one house, that they shall die.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/6#v10)</sup> And a man’s uncle shall take him up, and he that burneth him, to bring out the bones out of the house, and shall say unto him that is by the sides of the house, Is there yet any with thee? and he shall say, No. Then shall he say, Hold thy tongue: for we may not make mention of the name of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/6#v11)</sup> For, behold, the Lord commandeth, and he will smite the great house with breaches, and the little house with clefts.

- The Most High swears by himself that he abhors the excellency of Jacob and hates his palaces, and will deliver up the city with all that is in it.
- If ten men remain in one house they shall die, and none may make mention of the name of the Lord.
- He will smite the great house with breaches and the little house with clefts.

**[Amos 6:12-14](/bible/amos/6#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/6#v12)</sup> Shall horses run upon the rock? will one plow there with oxen? for ye have turned judgment into gall, and the fruit of righteousness into hemlock:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/6#v13)</sup> Ye which rejoice in a thing of nought, which say, Have we not taken to us horns by our own strength?
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/6#v14)</sup> But, behold, I will raise up against you a nation, O house of Israel, saith the Lord the God of hosts; and they shall afflict you from the entering in of Hemath unto the river of the wilderness.

- They turned judgment into gall and the fruit of righteousness into hemlock, rejoicing in a thing of naught and boasting that they took horns by their own strength.
- This is prophesied against the northern kingdom because of their idolatry and because of things they invented for themselves, so judgment was coming on them.
- Therefore the Most High raises up a nation against the house of Israel to afflict them from the entering in of Hamath unto the river of the wilderness.

# Amos 7: The Plumb Line and the Prophet's Job

Read the chapter → [Amos 7](/bible/amos/7)

**[Amos 7:1-3](/bible/amos/7#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/7#v1)</sup> Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me; and, behold, he formed grasshoppers in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and, lo, it was the latter growth after the king’s mowings.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/7#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, that when they had made an end of eating the grass of the land, then I said, O Lord God, forgive, I beseech thee: by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/7#v3)</sup> The Lord repented for this: It shall not be, saith the Lord.

- The Most High showed Amos grasshoppers formed in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth, after the king's mowings, and they ate the grass of the land.
- Amos pleaded, asking by whom shall Jacob arise, for he is small, and the Lord repented and said it shall not be.

**[Amos 7:4-6](/bible/amos/7#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/7#v4)</sup> Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me: and, behold, the Lord God called to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep, and did eat up a part.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/7#v5)</sup> Then said I, O Lord God, cease, I beseech thee: by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/7#v6)</sup> The Lord repented for this: This also shall not be, saith the Lord God.

- The Most High showed Amos that he called to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep and ate up a part.
- Amos again beseeched him to cease, asking by whom shall Jacob arise, for he is small, and the Lord repented and said this also shall not be.

**[Amos 7:7-9](/bible/amos/7#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/7#v7)</sup> Thus he shewed me: and, behold, the Lord stood upon a wall made by a plumbline, with a plumbline in his hand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/7#v8)</sup> And the Lord said unto me, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A plumbline. Then said the Lord, Behold, I will set a plumbline in the midst of my people Israel: I will not again pass by them any more:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/7#v9)</sup> And the high places of Isaac shall be desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste; and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.

- The Lord stood upon a wall made by a plumbline with a plumbline in his hand and asked Amos what he saw, and Amos said a plumbline.
- The Most High will set a plumbline in the midst of his people Israel and will not again pass by them anymore.
- The high places of Isaac shall be desolate, the sanctuaries of Israel laid waste, and he will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.

**[Amos 7:10-13](/bible/amos/7#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/7#v10)</sup> Then Amaziah the priest of Beth–el sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, Amos hath conspired against thee in the midst of the house of Israel: the land is not able to bear all his words.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/7#v11)</sup> For thus Amos saith, Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall surely be led away captive out of their own land.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/7#v12)</sup> Also Amaziah said unto Amos, O thou seer, go, flee thee away into the land of Judah, and there eat bread, and prophesy there:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/7#v13)</sup> But prophesy not again any more at Beth–el: for it is the king’s chapel, and it is the king’s court.

- Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent word to Jeroboam that Amos had conspired against him and that the land could not bear his words.
- Amaziah told Amos to flee into the land of Judah, eat bread there, and prophesy no more at Bethel, for it was the king's chapel and court.

**[Amos 7:14-17](/bible/amos/7#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/7#v14)</sup> Then answered Amos, and said to Amaziah, I was no prophet, neither was I a prophet’s son; but I was an herdman, and a gatherer of sycomore fruit:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/7#v15)</sup> And the Lord took me as I followed the flock, and the Lord said unto me, Go, prophesy unto my people Israel.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/amos/7#v16)</sup> Now therefore hear thou the word of the Lord: Thou sayest, Prophesy not against Israel, and drop not thy word against the house of Isaac.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/amos/7#v17)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Thy wife shall be an harlot in the city, and thy sons and thy daughters shall fall by the sword, and thy land shall be divided by line; and thou shalt die in a polluted land: and Israel shall surely go into captivity forth of his land.

- Amos answered that he was no prophet nor a prophet's son, but a herdman and a gatherer of sycamore fruit whom the Lord took as he followed the flock and sent to prophesy unto his people Israel.
- Because Amaziah said prophesy not against Israel, the Most High declared his wife would be a harlot in the city, his sons and daughters fall by the sword, his land divided, and he would die in a polluted land while Israel goes into captivity.
- Amaziah was trying to get the brother to stop prophesying, and judgment came upon him for that.
- The Most High gives his word to the prophets, and the prophet's job is to carry out the word of the Lord, no more, no less. If you do not like it, too bad.

# Amos 8: A Basket of Summer Fruit and a Famine of the Word

Read the chapter → [Amos 8](/bible/amos/8)

**[Amos 8:1-3](/bible/amos/8#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/8#v1)</sup> Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me: and behold a basket of summer fruit.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/8#v2)</sup> And he said, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A basket of summer fruit. Then said the Lord unto me, The end is come upon my people of Israel; I will not again pass by them any more.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/8#v3)</sup> And the songs of the temple shall be howlings in that day, saith the Lord God: there shall be many dead bodies in every place; they shall cast them forth with silence.

- The Most High showed Amos a basket of summer fruit and said the end is come upon his people of Israel, and he will not again pass by them anymore.
- The songs of the temple shall be howlings in that day, and there shall be many dead bodies in every place, cast forth with silence.

**[Amos 8:4-6](/bible/amos/8#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/8#v4)</sup> Hear this, O ye that swallow up the needy, even to make the poor of the land to fail,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/8#v5)</sup> Saying, When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell corn? and the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat, making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/8#v6)</sup> That we may buy the poor for silver, and the needy for a pair of shoes; yea, and sell the refuse of the wheat?

- Hear this, you that swallow up the needy and make the poor of the land to fail, longing for the new moon and the sabbath to be gone so you may sell corn and set forth wheat.
- They make the ephah small and the shekel great, falsify the balances by deceit, and buy the poor for silver and the needy for a pair of shoes, selling the refuse of the wheat.
- This shows the wickedness of our people, more willing to pursue business than to follow the commandments of the Lord, and to deal deceitfully with one another rather than keep the commandments.

**[Amos 8:7-10](/bible/amos/8#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/8#v7)</sup> The Lord hath sworn by the excellency of Jacob, Surely I will never forget any of their works.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/8#v8)</sup> Shall not the land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein? and it shall rise up wholly as a flood; and it shall be cast out and drowned, as by the flood of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/8#v9)</sup> And it shall come to pass in that day, saith the Lord God, that I will cause the sun to go down at noon, and I will darken the earth in the clear day:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/8#v10)</sup> And I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into lamentation; and I will bring up sackcloth upon all loins, and baldness upon every head; and I will make it as the mourning of an only son, and the end thereof as a bitter day.

- The Lord has sworn by the excellency of Jacob that he will never forget any of their works, and the land shall tremble and rise up wholly as a flood.
- He will cause the sun to go down at noon and darken the earth in the clear day.
- He will turn their feasts into mourning and their songs into lamentations, bring sackcloth upon all loins and baldness upon every head, and make it as the mourning of an only son.

**[Amos 8:11-14](/bible/amos/8#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/8#v11)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord God, that I will send a famine in the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/8#v12)</sup> And they shall wander from sea to sea, and from the north even to the east, they shall run to and fro to seek the word of the Lord, and shall not find it.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/8#v13)</sup> In that day shall the fair virgins and young men faint for thirst.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/8#v14)</sup> They that swear by the sin of Samaria, and say, Thy god, O Dan, liveth; and, The manner of Beer–sheba liveth; even they shall fall, and never rise up again.

- The Most High will send a famine in the land, not of bread nor thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the Lord, and men shall wander from sea to sea and run to and fro to seek the word and not find it.
- In that day the fair virgins and young men shall faint for thirst, and they that swear by the sin of Samaria and by the god of Dan and the manner of Beersheba shall fall and never rise up again.
- This last verse shows that the tribe of Dan was going to fall and never rise again. That is why Dan does not have a gate. Dan fell and did not rise up again as a tribe.

# Amos 9: Judgment and the Restoration of the Tabernacle of David

Read the chapter → [Amos 9](/bible/amos/9)

**[Amos 9:1-4](/bible/amos/9#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/9#v1)</sup> I saw the Lord standing upon the altar: and he said, Smite the lintel of the door, that the posts may shake: and cut them in the head, all of them; and I will slay the last of them with the sword: he that fleeth of them shall not flee away, and he that escapeth of them shall not be delivered.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/9#v2)</sup> Though they dig into hell, thence shall mine hand take them; though they climb up to heaven, thence will I bring them down:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/9#v3)</sup> And though they hide themselves in the top of Carmel, I will search and take them out thence; and though they be hid from my sight in the bottom of the sea, thence will I command the serpent, and he shall bite them:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/9#v4)</sup> And though they go into captivity before their enemies, thence will I command the sword, and it shall slay them: and I will set mine eyes upon them for evil, and not for good.

- Amos saw the Lord standing upon the altar, commanding to smite the lintel of the door so the posts shake, and to cut them in the head, and the last of them he will slay with the sword.
- None shall flee away or be delivered; though they dig into hell, climb to heaven, hide in the top of Carmel, or hide in the bottom of the sea, he will bring them down and command the serpent to bite them.
- Though they go into captivity before their enemies, he will command the sword to slay them, and he will set his eyes upon them for evil and not for good.

**[Amos 9:5-6](/bible/amos/9#v5)**
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/9#v5)</sup> And the Lord God of hosts is he that toucheth the land, and it shall melt, and all that dwell therein shall mourn: and it shall rise up wholly like a flood; and shall be drowned, as by the flood of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/9#v6)</sup> It is he that buildeth his stories in the heaven, and hath founded his troop in the earth; he that calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth: The Lord is his name.

- The Lord God of hosts touches the land and it melts, and all that dwell therein mourn, and it rises up wholly like a flood and is drowned as by the flood of Egypt.
- He builds his stories in the heaven, founds his troop in the earth, and calls for the waters of the sea to pour them out upon the face of the earth. The Lord is his name.

**[Amos 9:7-10](/bible/amos/9#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/9#v7)</sup> Are ye not as children of the Ethiopians unto me, O children of Israel? saith the Lord. Have not I brought up Israel out of the land of Egypt? and the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Syrians from Kir?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/9#v8)</sup> Behold, the eyes of the Lord God are upon the sinful kingdom, and I will destroy it from off the face of the earth; saving that I will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/9#v9)</sup> For, lo, I will command, and I will sift the house of Israel among all nations, like as corn is sifted in a sieve, yet shall not the least grain fall upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/9#v10)</sup> All the sinners of my people shall die by the sword, which say, The evil shall not overtake nor prevent us.

- The Most High asks whether Israel is not as the children of the Ethiopians unto him, reminding them that he brought Israel out of Egypt, the Philistines from Caphtor, and the Syrians from Kir.
- His eyes are upon the sinful kingdom to destroy it from the earth, yet he will not utterly destroy the house of Jacob.
- He will sift the house of Israel among all nations like corn sifted in a sieve, yet not the least grain shall fall to the earth, and all the sinners who say the evil shall not overtake them shall die by the sword.

**[Amos 9:11-15](/bible/amos/9#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/9#v11)</sup> In that day will I raise up the tabernacle of David that is fallen, and close up the breaches thereof; and I will raise up his ruins, and I will build it as in the days of old:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/9#v12)</sup> That they may possess the remnant of Edom, and of all the heathen, which are called by my name, saith the Lord that doeth this.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/9#v13)</sup> Behold, the days come, saith the Lord, that the plowman shall overtake the reaper, and the treader of grapes him that soweth seed; and the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and all the hills shall melt.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/9#v14)</sup> And I will bring again the captivity of my people of Israel, and they shall build the waste cities, and inhabit them; and they shall plant vineyards, and drink the wine thereof; they shall also make gardens, and eat the fruit of them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/9#v15)</sup> And I will plant them upon their land, and they shall no more be pulled up out of their land which I have given them, saith the Lord thy God.

- In that day the Most High will raise up the tabernacle of David that is fallen, close up its breaches, raise up its ruins, and build it as in the days of old, that they may possess the remnant of Edom and all the heathen called by his name.
- The days come when the plowman shall overtake the reaper and the treader of grapes him that soweth seed, the mountains shall drop sweet wine, and all the hills shall melt.
- He will bring again the captivity of his people Israel, and they shall build the waste cities, plant vineyards, make gardens, eat the fruit, and no more be pulled out of their land.
- Even though the Most High was going to bring judgment upon the northern kingdom, he was always going to bring them back, because the promise was given to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.
- You cannot have the twelve tribes of the nation of Israel if ten of them are not there.
