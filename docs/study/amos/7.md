**[Amos 7:1-3](/bible/amos/7#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/7#v1)</sup> Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me; and, behold, he formed grasshoppers in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth; and, lo, it was the latter growth after the king’s mowings.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/7#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, that when they had made an end of eating the grass of the land, then I said, O Lord God, forgive, I beseech thee: by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/7#v3)</sup> The Lord repented for this: It shall not be, saith the Lord.

- The Most High showed Amos grasshoppers formed in the beginning of the shooting up of the latter growth, after the king's mowings, and they ate the grass of the land.
- Amos pleaded, asking by whom shall Jacob arise, for he is small, and the Lord repented and said it shall not be.

**[Amos 7:4-6](/bible/amos/7#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/7#v4)</sup> Thus hath the Lord God shewed unto me: and, behold, the Lord God called to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep, and did eat up a part.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/7#v5)</sup> Then said I, O Lord God, cease, I beseech thee: by whom shall Jacob arise? for he is small.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/7#v6)</sup> The Lord repented for this: This also shall not be, saith the Lord God.

- The Most High showed Amos that he called to contend by fire, and it devoured the great deep and ate up a part.
- Amos again beseeched him to cease, asking by whom shall Jacob arise, for he is small, and the Lord repented and said this also shall not be.

**[Amos 7:7-9](/bible/amos/7#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/7#v7)</sup> Thus he shewed me: and, behold, the Lord stood upon a wall made by a plumbline, with a plumbline in his hand.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/7#v8)</sup> And the Lord said unto me, Amos, what seest thou? And I said, A plumbline. Then said the Lord, Behold, I will set a plumbline in the midst of my people Israel: I will not again pass by them any more:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/7#v9)</sup> And the high places of Isaac shall be desolate, and the sanctuaries of Israel shall be laid waste; and I will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.

- The Lord stood upon a wall made by a plumbline with a plumbline in his hand and asked Amos what he saw, and Amos said a plumbline.
- The Most High will set a plumbline in the midst of his people Israel and will not again pass by them anymore.
- The high places of Isaac shall be desolate, the sanctuaries of Israel laid waste, and he will rise against the house of Jeroboam with the sword.

**[Amos 7:10-13](/bible/amos/7#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/7#v10)</sup> Then Amaziah the priest of Beth–el sent to Jeroboam king of Israel, saying, Amos hath conspired against thee in the midst of the house of Israel: the land is not able to bear all his words.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/7#v11)</sup> For thus Amos saith, Jeroboam shall die by the sword, and Israel shall surely be led away captive out of their own land.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/7#v12)</sup> Also Amaziah said unto Amos, O thou seer, go, flee thee away into the land of Judah, and there eat bread, and prophesy there:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/7#v13)</sup> But prophesy not again any more at Beth–el: for it is the king’s chapel, and it is the king’s court.

- Amaziah the priest of Bethel sent word to Jeroboam that Amos had conspired against him and that the land could not bear his words.
- Amaziah told Amos to flee into the land of Judah, eat bread there, and prophesy no more at Bethel, for it was the king's chapel and court.

**[Amos 7:14-17](/bible/amos/7#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/7#v14)</sup> Then answered Amos, and said to Amaziah, I was no prophet, neither was I a prophet’s son; but I was an herdman, and a gatherer of sycomore fruit:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/7#v15)</sup> And the Lord took me as I followed the flock, and the Lord said unto me, Go, prophesy unto my people Israel.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/amos/7#v16)</sup> Now therefore hear thou the word of the Lord: Thou sayest, Prophesy not against Israel, and drop not thy word against the house of Isaac.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/amos/7#v17)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord; Thy wife shall be an harlot in the city, and thy sons and thy daughters shall fall by the sword, and thy land shall be divided by line; and thou shalt die in a polluted land: and Israel shall surely go into captivity forth of his land.

- Amos answered that he was no prophet nor a prophet's son, but a herdman and a gatherer of sycamore fruit whom the Lord took as he followed the flock and sent to prophesy unto his people Israel.
- Because Amaziah said prophesy not against Israel, the Most High declared his wife would be a harlot in the city, his sons and daughters fall by the sword, his land divided, and he would die in a polluted land while Israel goes into captivity.
- Amaziah was trying to get the brother to stop prophesying, and judgment came upon him for that.
- The Most High gives his word to the prophets, and the prophet's job is to carry out the word of the Lord, no more, no less. If you do not like it, too bad.
