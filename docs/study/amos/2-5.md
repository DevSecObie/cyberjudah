Read the chapter → [Amos 2](/bible/amos/2)

This session covers [Amos 2](/bible/amos/2) through 5, continuing the Most High's judgments that began in chapter 1. The Lord pronounces punishment on Moab and Judah, then turns to Israel for selling the righteous for silver and the poor for a pair of shoes, oppressing the poor, the needy, and the just. The reason we are in captivity even in today's time is the same reason our forefathers went into the Assyrian captivity: they were rebellious against the Most High's laws, would not keep the commandments, and wanted to worship idols. The other nations can do whatever they want, but Israel is punished for their iniquities, and these curses are generational, repeating from captivity to captivity.

**[Amos 2:1-3](/bible/amos/2#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/2#v1)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; For three transgressions of Moab, and for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because he burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/2#v2)</sup> But I will send a fire upon Moab, and it shall devour the palaces of Kerioth: and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/2#v3)</sup> And I will cut off the judge from the midst thereof, and will slay all the princes thereof with him, saith the Lord.

- For three transgressions of Moab and for four, the Most High will not turn away the punishment, because he burned the bones of the king of Edom into lime. Moab today is the Chinese.
- He will send a fire upon Moab that shall devour the palaces of Kerioth, and Moab shall die with tumult, with shouting, and with the sound of the trumpet, and he will cut off the judge and slay all the princes with him.

**[Amos 2:4-5](/bible/amos/2#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/2#v4)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; For three transgressions of Judah, and for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because they have despised the law of the Lord, and have not kept his commandments, and their lies caused them to err, after the which their fathers have walked:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/2#v5)</sup> But I will send a fire upon Judah, and it shall devour the palaces of Jerusalem.

- For three transgressions of Judah and for four, the Most High will not turn away the punishment, because they despised the law of the Lord, did not keep his commandments, and their lies caused them to err after which their fathers walked.
- This is the reason we are in captivity even today. Going back to the Assyrian captivity, our forefathers were rebellious against the Most High's laws, did not want to keep the commandments, and wanted to worship idols. He will send a fire upon Judah that shall devour the palaces of Jerusalem.

**[Amos 2:6-8](/bible/amos/2#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/2#v6)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; For three transgressions of Israel, and for four, I will not turn away the punishment thereof; because they sold the righteous for silver, and the poor for a pair of shoes;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/2#v7)</sup> That pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, and turn aside the way of the meek: and a man and his father will go in unto the same maid, to profane my holy name:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/2#v8)</sup> And they lay themselves down upon clothes laid to pledge by every altar, and they drink the wine of the condemned in the house of their god.

- For three transgressions of Israel and for four, the Most High will not turn away the punishment, because they sold the righteous for silver and the poor for a pair of shoes. That goes into oppression, and just like in today's time nobody wants to hear the righteous words of the Most High and keep the commandments; when you bring out the commandments you hear "what, I can't wear pants."
- They pant after the dust of the earth on the head of the poor, turn aside the way of the meek, and a man and his father go in unto the same maid to profane the Most High's holy name. There were men even in that time going in unto their father's wife, and you may find a TV show today promoting that behavior.
- They lay themselves down upon clothes laid to pledge by every altar and drink the wine of the condemned in the house of their god.
  - **[Isaiah 29:20-21](/bible/isaiah/29#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/isaiah/29#v20)</sup> For the terrible one is brought to nought, and the scorner is consumed, and all that watch for iniquity are cut off:
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/29#v21)</sup> That make a man an offender for a word, and lay a snare for him that reproveth in the gate, and turn aside the just for a thing of nought.

    Some of our people watch for iniquity, hating those that reprove in the gate and turning aside the just for a thing of naught; they will sell us just as Amos says.
  - **[Leviticus 18:8](/bible/leviticus/18#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/18#v8)</sup> The nakedness of thy father’s wife shalt thou not uncover: it is thy father’s nakedness.

    The nakedness of thy father's wife thou shalt not uncover; you profane the Most High's holy name when you go against this commandment.

**[Amos 2:9-12](/bible/amos/2#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/2#v9)</sup> Yet destroyed I the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of the cedars, and he was strong as the oaks; yet I destroyed his fruit from above, and his roots from beneath.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/2#v10)</sup> Also I brought you up from the land of Egypt, and led you forty years through the wilderness, to possess the land of the Amorite.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/2#v11)</sup> And I raised up of your sons for prophets, and of your young men for Nazarites. Is it not even thus, O ye children of Israel? saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/2#v12)</sup> But ye gave the Nazarites wine to drink; and commanded the prophets, saying, Prophesy not.

- Yet the Most High destroyed the Amorite before them, whose height was like the height of the cedar and who was strong as the oaks; he destroyed his fruit from above and his roots from beneath. The Amorite is another heathen nation.
- He brought Israel up from the land of Egypt, led them forty years through the wilderness to possess the land of the Amorite, and raised up their sons for prophets and their young men for Nazirites. You can use this to show that the children of Israel are the prophets of the Most High.
- But they gave the Nazirites wine to drink and commanded the prophets saying, prophesy not. That goes into lies and deceit, doing anything to stop the word of the Most High.

**[Amos 2:13-16](/bible/amos/2#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/2#v13)</sup> Behold, I am pressed under you, as a cart is pressed that is full of sheaves.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/2#v14)</sup> Therefore the flight shall perish from the swift, and the strong shall not strengthen his force, neither shall the mighty deliver himself:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/2#v15)</sup> Neither shall he stand that handleth the bow; and he that is swift of foot shall not deliver himself: neither shall he that rideth the horse deliver himself.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/amos/2#v16)</sup> And he that is courageous among the mighty shall flee away naked in that day, saith the Lord.

- The Most High is pressed under them as a cart is pressed that is full of sheaves; therefore the flight shall perish from the swift, the strong shall not strengthen his force, and the mighty shall not deliver himself, because Israel will be in captivity.
- He that is courageous among the mighty shall flee away naked in that day; every possession you had will be stripped from you as you flee, the same way a robber coming to a bank strips everybody down and takes the goods.

# Amos 3: You Only Have I Known

Read the chapter → [Amos 3](/bible/amos/3)

**[Amos 3:1-2](/bible/amos/3#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/3#v1)</sup> Hear this word that the Lord hath spoken against you, O children of Israel, against the whole family which I brought up from the land of Egypt, saying,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/3#v2)</sup> You only have I known of all the families of the earth: therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.

- Hear this word the Lord has spoken against the children of Israel, the whole family brought up from the land of Egypt: you only have I known of all the families of the earth, therefore I will punish you for all your iniquities.
- Anything we do against the Most High's commandments he will punish us for. The other nations can do whatever they want; they can worship their gods, eat unclean, and lie with animals, because those are things they do by nature, but Israel will be punished for applying those things.

**[Amos 3:3-6](/bible/amos/3#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/3#v3)</sup> Can two walk together, except they be agreed?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/3#v4)</sup> Will a lion roar in the forest, when he hath no prey? will a young lion cry out of his den, if he have taken nothing?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/3#v5)</sup> Can a bird fall in a snare upon the earth, where no gin is for him? shall one take up a snare from the earth, and have taken nothing at all?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/3#v6)</sup> Shall a trumpet be blown in the city, and the people not be afraid? shall there be evil in a city, and the Lord hath not done it?

- Can two walk together except they be agreed? Will a lion roar in the forest when he has no prey, or a young lion cry out of his den if he has taken nothing? Can a bird fall in a snare where no gin is laid for him?
- Shall a trumpet be blown in the city and the people not be afraid? Shall there be evil in a city and the Lord has not done it? The Most High is doing all these things; in Christianity we attribute anything bad to the devil, but the Lord says he does it.
  - **[Isaiah 45:7](/bible/isaiah/45#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/45#v7)</sup> I form the light, and create darkness: I make peace, and create evil: I the Lord do all these things.

    The Lord forms the light and creates darkness, makes peace and creates evil; the Lord does all these things.

**[Amos 3:7-8](/bible/amos/3#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/3#v7)</sup> Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/3#v8)</sup> The lion hath roared, who will not fear? the Lord God hath spoken, who can but prophesy?

- Surely the Lord God will do nothing but he reveals his secret unto his servants the prophets. The lion has roared, who will not fear? The Lord God has spoken, who can but prophesy?
  - **[Revelation 5:5](/bible/revelation/5#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/5#v5)</sup> And one of the elders saith unto me, Weep not: behold, the Lion of the tribe of Juda, the Root of David, hath prevailed to open the book, and to loose the seven seals thereof.

    The Lion of the tribe of Judah, the Root of David, has prevailed to open the book and loose the seven seals; this Lion goes into Christ.

**[Amos 3:9-11](/bible/amos/3#v9)**
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/3#v9)</sup> Publish in the palaces at Ashdod, and in the palaces in the land of Egypt, and say, Assemble yourselves upon the mountains of Samaria, and behold the great tumults in the midst thereof, and the oppressed in the midst thereof.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/3#v10)</sup> For they know not to do right, saith the Lord, who store up violence and robbery in their palaces.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/3#v11)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; An adversary there shall be even round about the land; and he shall bring down thy strength from thee, and thy palaces shall be spoiled.

- Publish in the palaces at Ashdod and in the land of Egypt, and say, assemble upon the mountains of Samaria and behold the great tumults and the oppressed in the midst, for they know not to do right, who store up violence and robbery in their palaces.
- Therefore an adversary shall be even round about the land, and he shall bring down thy strength from thee and thy palaces shall be spoiled.

**[Amos 3:12](/bible/amos/3#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/3#v12)</sup> Thus saith the Lord; As the shepherd taketh out of the mouth of the lion two legs, or a piece of an ear; so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria in the corner of a bed, and in Damascus in a couch.

- As the shepherd takes out of the mouth of the lion two legs or a piece of an ear, so shall the children of Israel be taken out that dwell in Samaria in the corner of a bed and in Damascus on a couch.

**[Amos 3:13-15](/bible/amos/3#v13)**
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/3#v13)</sup> Hear ye, and testify in the house of Jacob, saith the Lord God, the God of hosts,
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/3#v14)</sup> That in the day that I shall visit the transgressions of Israel upon him I will also visit the altars of Beth–el: and the horns of the altar shall be cut off, and fall to the ground.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/3#v15)</sup> And I will smite the winter house with the summer house; and the houses of ivory shall perish, and the great houses shall have an end, saith the Lord.

- Hear and testify in the house of Jacob, saith the Lord God of hosts, that in the day he visits the transgressions of Israel upon him, he will also visit the altars of Bethel, a heathen god.
- The horns of the altar shall be cut off and fall to the ground, and he will smite the winter house with the summer house, the houses of ivory shall perish, and the great houses shall have an end.

# Amos 4: Prepare to Meet Thy God

Read the chapter → [Amos 4](/bible/amos/4)

**[Amos 4:1-3](/bible/amos/4#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/4#v1)</sup> Hear this word, ye kine of Bashan, that are in the mountain of Samaria, which oppress the poor, which crush the needy, which say to their masters, Bring, and let us drink.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/4#v2)</sup> The Lord God hath sworn by his holiness, that, lo, the days shall come upon you, that he will take you away with hooks, and your posterity with fishhooks.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/4#v3)</sup> And ye shall go out at the breaches, every cow at that which is before her; and ye shall cast them into the palace, saith the Lord.

- Hear this word, ye kine of Bashan in the mountain of Samaria, who oppress the poor, crush the needy, and say to their masters, bring and let us drink. The common theme here is the oppression of the poor, the needy, and the righteous.
- The Lord God has sworn by his holiness that the day shall come when he will take you away with hooks and your posterity with fishhooks, and you shall go out at the breaches, every cow at that which is before her, and cast them into the palace.

**[Amos 4:4-5](/bible/amos/4#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/4#v4)</sup> Come to Beth–el, and transgress; at Gilgal multiply transgression; and bring your sacrifices every morning, and your tithes after three years:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/4#v5)</sup> And offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with leaven, and proclaim and publish the free offerings: for this liketh you, O ye children of Israel, saith the Lord God.

- Come to Bethel and transgress, at Gilgal multiply transgression, bring your sacrifices every morning and your tithes after three years, and offer a sacrifice of thanksgiving with leaven, for this liketh you, O children of Israel.

**[Amos 4:6-11](/bible/amos/4#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/4#v6)</sup> And I also have given you cleanness of teeth in all your cities, and want of bread in all your places: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/4#v7)</sup> And also I have withholden the rain from you, when there were yet three months to the harvest: and I caused it to rain upon one city, and caused it not to rain upon another city: one piece was rained upon, and the piece whereupon it rained not withered.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/4#v8)</sup> So two or three cities wandered unto one city, to drink water; but they were not satisfied: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/4#v9)</sup> I have smitten you with blasting and mildew: when your gardens and your vineyards and your fig trees and your olive trees increased, the palmerworm devoured them: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/4#v10)</sup> I have sent among you the pestilence after the manner of Egypt: your young men have I slain with the sword, and have taken away your horses; and I have made the stink of your camps to come up unto your nostrils: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/4#v11)</sup> I have overthrown some of you, as God overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, and ye were as a firebrand plucked out of the burning: yet have ye not returned unto me, saith the Lord.

- The Most High gave them cleanness of teeth and want of bread in all their places, withheld the rain three months before harvest, and caused it to rain on one city and not another, so people wandered from city to city to drink and were not satisfied, yet they did not return unto him.
- He smote them with blasting and mildew, the palmerworm devoured their gardens, vineyards, fig trees, and olive trees, he sent pestilence after the manner of Egypt, slew their young men with the sword, and overthrew some of them as he overthrew Sodom and Gomorrah, yet they did not return unto him. The Lord blesses us and withholds and blesses again, and Israel is still not satisfied, forgetting the good times when everything turns bad.

**[Amos 4:12-13](/bible/amos/4#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/4#v12)</sup> Therefore thus will I do unto thee, O Israel: and because I will do this unto thee, prepare to meet thy God, O Israel.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/4#v13)</sup> For, lo, he that formeth the mountains, and createth the wind, and declareth unto man what is his thought, that maketh the morning darkness, and treadeth upon the high places of the earth, The Lord, The God of hosts, is his name.

- Therefore thus will he do unto Israel, and because he will do this, prepare to meet thy God, O Israel.
- He forms the mountains, creates the wind, declares unto man his thought, makes the morning darkness, and treads upon the high places of the earth; the Lord the God of hosts is his name. He treads upon the high places, the other nations and their power, being over all of it. You can use this as a precept when someone wants to play the name game with you, for there are many different titles.

# Amos 5: Seek Ye Me and Ye Shall Live

Read the chapter → [Amos 5](/bible/amos/5)

**[Amos 5:1-2](/bible/amos/5#v1)**
> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/5#v1)</sup> Hear ye this word which I take up against you, even a lamentation, O house of Israel.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/5#v2)</sup> The virgin of Israel is fallen; she shall no more rise: she is forsaken upon her land; there is none to raise her up.

- Hear this word which the Lord takes up against the house of Israel, even a lamentation: the virgin of Israel is fallen, she shall no more rise, she is forsaken upon her land, and there is none to raise her up.
- The virgin of Israel is falling into all types of sin and sacrificing to other gods; we are supposed to be a chaste virgin, but the virgin of Israel played the harlot.
  - **[2 Corinthians 11:2](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v2)</sup> For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.

    Paul is jealous over Israel with godly jealousy, having espoused them to one husband, to present them as a chaste virgin to Christ.

**[Amos 5:3](/bible/amos/5#v3)**
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/5#v3)</sup> For thus saith the Lord God; The city that went out by a thousand shall leave an hundred, and that which went forth by an hundred shall leave ten, to the house of Israel.

- Thus saith the Lord God: the city that went out by a thousand shall leave a hundred, and that which went forth by a hundred shall leave ten to the house of Israel.

**[Amos 5:4-6](/bible/amos/5#v4)**
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/5#v4)</sup> For thus saith the Lord unto the house of Israel, Seek ye me, and ye shall live:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/5#v5)</sup> But seek not Beth–el, nor enter into Gilgal, and pass not to Beer–sheba: for Gilgal shall surely go into captivity, and Beth–el shall come to nought.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/5#v6)</sup> Seek the Lord, and ye shall live; lest he break out like fire in the house of Joseph, and devour it, and there be none to quench it in Beth–el.

- Thus saith the Lord unto the house of Israel, seek ye me and ye shall live, but seek not Bethel, nor enter into Gilgal, and pass not to Beersheba, for Gilgal shall surely go into captivity and Bethel shall come to nought.
- Seek the Lord and ye shall live, lest he break out like fire in the house of Joseph and devour it, and there be none to quench it in Bethel. If you worship these other gods and visit these other cities, the Lord will throw down everything.

**[Amos 5:7-9](/bible/amos/5#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/5#v7)</sup> Ye who turn judgment to wormwood, and leave off righteousness in the earth,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/5#v8)</sup> Seek him that maketh the seven stars and Orion, and turneth the shadow of death into the morning, and maketh the day dark with night: that calleth for the waters of the sea, and poureth them out upon the face of the earth: The Lord is his name:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/amos/5#v9)</sup> That strengtheneth the spoiled against the strong, so that the spoiled shall come against the fortress.

- Ye who turn judgment to wormwood and leave off righteousness in the earth, seek him that makes the seven stars and Orion, turns the shadow of death into the morning, makes the day dark with night, and calls for the waters of the sea and pours them out upon the face of the earth; the Lord is his name.
- He strengthens the spoiled against the strong, so that the spoiled shall come against the fortress.

**[Amos 5:10-11](/bible/amos/5#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/amos/5#v10)</sup> They hate him that rebuketh in the gate, and they abhor him that speaketh uprightly.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/5#v11)</sup> Forasmuch therefore as your treading is upon the poor, and ye take from him burdens of wheat: ye have built houses of hewn stone, but ye shall not dwell in them; ye have planted pleasant vineyards, but ye shall not drink wine of them.

- They hate him that rebukes in the gate and abhor him that speaks uprightly, and they do the same thing today.
- Forasmuch as their treading is upon the poor and they take from him burdens of wheat, they have built houses of hewn stone but shall not dwell in them, and planted pleasant vineyards but shall not drink the wine of them. These curses are generational from each captivity, the same thing repeating over and over.
  - **[Deuteronomy 28:30](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v30)</sup> Thou shalt betroth a wife, and another man shall lie with her: thou shalt build an house, and thou shalt not dwell therein: thou shalt plant a vineyard, and shalt not gather the grapes thereof.

    Thou shalt build a house and not dwell therein, and plant a vineyard and not gather the grapes; the same curse first pronounced in Deuteronomy.

**[Amos 5:12-13](/bible/amos/5#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/5#v12)</sup> For I know your manifold transgressions and your mighty sins: they afflict the just, they take a bribe, and they turn aside the poor in the gate from their right.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/amos/5#v13)</sup> Therefore the prudent shall keep silence in that time; for it is an evil time.

- For the Most High knows their manifold transgressions and mighty sins: they afflict the just, take a bribe, and turn aside the poor in the gate from their right, acting like they do not even see the poor, which is not the spirit of Christ.
- Therefore the prudent shall keep silence in that time, for it is an evil time.
  - **[Romans 7:12](/bible/romans/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/romans/7#v12)</sup> Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.

    The law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good; the just are those seeking righteousness and keeping the commandments.

**[Amos 5:14-15](/bible/amos/5#v14)**
> <sup>[14](/bible/amos/5#v14)</sup> Seek good, and not evil, that ye may live: and so the Lord, the God of hosts, shall be with you, as ye have spoken.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/amos/5#v15)</sup> Hate the evil, and love the good, and establish judgment in the gate: it may be that the Lord God of hosts will be gracious unto the remnant of Joseph.

- Seek good and not evil, that ye may live, and so the Lord the God of hosts shall be with you as ye have spoken.
- Hate the evil and love the good, and establish judgment in the gate; it may be that the Lord God of hosts will be gracious unto the remnant of Joseph.

**[Amos 5:16-17](/bible/amos/5#v16)**
> <sup>[16](/bible/amos/5#v16)</sup> Therefore the Lord, the God of hosts, the Lord, saith thus; Wailing shall be in all streets; and they shall say in all the highways, Alas! alas! and they shall call the husbandman to mourning, and such as are skilful of lamentation to wailing.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/amos/5#v17)</sup> And in all vineyards shall be wailing: for I will pass through thee, saith the Lord.

- Therefore the Lord the God of hosts says wailing shall be in all streets, they shall say in all the highways, alas, alas, and they shall call the husbandman to mourning and such as are skillful of lamentation to wailing.
- In all vineyards shall be wailing, for the Most High will pass through thee bringing judgment.

**[Amos 5:18-20](/bible/amos/5#v18)**
> <sup>[18](/bible/amos/5#v18)</sup> Woe unto you that desire the day of the Lord! to what end is it for you? the day of the Lord is darkness, and not light.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/amos/5#v19)</sup> As if a man did flee from a lion, and a bear met him; or went into the house, and leaned his hand on the wall, and a serpent bit him.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/amos/5#v20)</sup> Shall not the day of the Lord be darkness, and not light? even very dark, and no brightness in it?

- Woe unto you that desire the day of the Lord; to what end is it for you? The day of the Lord is darkness and not light. This is a spirit checker; when you find yourself saying "I can't wait till Christ gets back," you had better examine yourself and get all the impurities out of you before that day, because you do not know what end it will be for you.
- It is as if a man fled from a lion and a bear met him, or went into the house, leaned his hand on the wall, and a serpent bit him. Shall not the day of the Lord be darkness and not light, even very dark with no brightness in it?

**[Amos 5:21-23](/bible/amos/5#v21)**
> <sup>[21](/bible/amos/5#v21)</sup> I hate, I despise your feast days, and I will not smell in your solemn assemblies.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/amos/5#v22)</sup> Though ye offer me burnt offerings and your meat offerings, I will not accept them: neither will I regard the peace offerings of your fat beasts.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/amos/5#v23)</sup> Take thou away from me the noise of thy songs; for I will not hear the melody of thy viols.

- I hate, I despise your feast days, and I will not smell in your solemn assemblies. This is not talking about the feasts of the Lord, but about how Israel wanted to conduct the feast asking "is the Sabbath over yet," with no spirit whatsoever in it.
- Though ye offer burnt offerings and meat offerings, he will not accept them, nor regard the peace offerings of your fat beasts. Take away the noise of thy songs, for he will not hear the melody of thy viols; he does not want to hear anything, because we are not living according to the commandments.
