## Introduction

This session reads 2 Esdras 4 through 7. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The angel Uriel answers Esdras with three things no man can do, and from there the reading runs through the tokens of the end, the questions about why one people was given over to many, the seven days of creation recounted before the Most High, and the narrow entrance that must be passed before the broad city can be inherited.
