## Introduction

This session reads 2 Esdras 12 through 15. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The eagle of the last vision is interpreted as the kingdom Daniel saw, the man rising from the sea is interpreted as the son kept by the Most High, the ten tribes are named as the peaceable multitude gathered to him, and the reading closes with Esdras drinking the cup like fire, dictating the books, and the burden spoken against Babylon and Asia.

**[2 Esdras 12:10-21](/bible/2-esdras/12#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-esdras/12#v10)</sup> And he said unto me, This is the interpretation of the vision:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/12#v11)</sup> The eagle, whom thou sawest come up from the sea, is the kingdom which was seen in the vision of thy brother Daniel.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-esdras/12#v12)</sup> But it was not expounded unto him, therefore now I declare it unto thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-esdras/12#v13)</sup> Behold, the days will come, that there shall rise up a kingdom upon earth, and it shall be feared above all the kingdoms that were before it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-esdras/12#v14)</sup> In the same shall twelve kings reign, one after another:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-esdras/12#v15)</sup> Whereof the second shall begin to reign, and shall have more time than any of the twelve.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/2-esdras/12#v16)</sup> And this do the twelve wings signify, which thou sawest.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/2-esdras/12#v17)</sup> As for the voice which thou heardest speak, and that thou sawest not to go out from the heads but from the midst of the body thereof, this is the interpretation:
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/2-esdras/12#v18)</sup> That after the time of that kingdom there shall arise great strivings, and it shall stand in peril of failing: nevertheless it shall not then fall, but shall be restored again to his beginning.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/2-esdras/12#v19)</sup> And whereas thou sawest the eight small under feathers sticking to her wings, this is the interpretation:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/2-esdras/12#v20)</sup> That in him there shall arise eight kings, whose times shall be but small, and their years swift.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/2-esdras/12#v21)</sup> And two of them shall perish, the middle time approaching: four shall be kept until their end begin to approach: but two shall be kept unto the end.

- The eagle, whom thou sawest come up from the sea, is the kingdom which was seen in the vision of thy brother Daniel; but it was not expounded unto him, therefore now I declare it unto thee.
- Behold, the days will come, that there shall rise up a kingdom upon earth, and it shall be feared above all the kingdoms that were before it.
- In the same shall twelve kings reign, one after another: whereof the second shall begin to reign, and shall have more time than any of the twelve.
- After the time of that kingdom there shall arise great strivings, and it shall stand in peril of falling: nevertheless it shall not then fall, but shall be restored again to his beginning.
- Whereas thou sawest the eight small under feathers sticking to her wings, this is the interpretation: that in him there shall arise eight kings, whose times shall be but small, and their years swift.

**[2 Esdras 12:22-30](/bible/2-esdras/12#v22)**

>
> <sup>[30](/bible/2-esdras/12#v30)</sup> It signifieth that these are they, whom the Highest hath kept unto their end: this is the small kingdom and full of trouble, as thou sawest.
