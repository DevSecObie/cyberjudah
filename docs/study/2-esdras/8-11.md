## Introduction

This session reads 2 Esdras 8 through 11. It is a reading session, so the chapters are read straight through and the notes follow the text. The reading runs from the answer that this world was made for many but the world to come for few, through Esdras pleading for his people and being told to measure the time by the signs, to the mourning woman in the field who turns out to be Zion herself, and closes with the vision of the eagle from the sea and the lion that speaks against her.

- Therefore appear no more, thou eagle, nor thy horrible wings, nor thy wicked feathers, nor thy malicious heads, nor thy hurtful claws, nor all thy vain body: that all the earth may be refreshed, and may return, being delivered from thy violence, and that she may hope for the judgment and mercy of him that made her.
