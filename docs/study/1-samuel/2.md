**[1 Samuel 2:3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/2#v3)</sup> Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed.

- Talk no more so exceeding proudly; let not arrogancy come out of your mouth: for the Lord is a God of knowledge, and by him actions are weighed. What the Lord weighs is actions: if it came out of your mouth, are you going to do it?
- All of us in this walk, keeping the commandments and the faith in Christ, have taken covenant and proclaimed out of our mouths that we are going to endure to the end; we broke the bread and drank the wine and took the covenant with the Lord. Now the Lord is judging you by your actions: are you going to endure to the end, whether to Christ's return or until you leave this earth, if you truly love him?

  Precepts:
  - **[Luke 6:46](/bible/luke/6#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/luke/6#v46)</sup> And why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say?

    Why call ye me, Lord, Lord, and do not the things which I say? You cannot just say you love the Lord, that you believe in the God of Israel, and do nothing of what he says: not loving your neighbour, holding grudges and envy, respecting leadership above your own husband who is Christ in the house.
  - **[Hebrews 10:7](/bible/hebrews/10#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/10#v7)</sup> Then said I, Lo, I come (in the volume of the book it is written of me,) to do thy will, O God.

    Lo, I come, in the volume of the book it is written of me, to do thy will, O God. The letters of Paul, the book of Samuel, the book of Genesis, the laws of Moses: everything we read ultimately leads up to the life of Christ and his return, and all of it is the spirit of Christ telling you to do those things.
  - **[Matthew 12:36-37](/bible/matthew/12#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/12#v36)</sup> But I say unto you, That every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment.
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/12#v37)</sup> For by thy words thou shalt be justified, and by thy words thou shalt be condemned.

    Every idle word that men shall speak, they shall give account thereof in the day of judgment; by thy words thou shalt be justified, and by thy words thou shalt be condemned. Make sure what comes out of your mouth is calculated; that whole "we were arguing and it just slipped" will not fly on judgment day. Repent, and make a door and a bar for thy mouth, as the scriptures say.

- "I have a personal relationship with God; God knows my heart; God loves me no matter what": that is arrogancy coming out of the mouth. The scripture says he loves you if you keep the commandments.

**[1 Samuel 2:11-12](/bible/1-samuel/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-samuel/2#v11)</sup> And Elkanah went to Ramah to his house. And the child did minister unto the Lord before Eli the priest.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-samuel/2#v12)</sup> Now the sons of Eli were sons of Belial; they knew not the Lord.

- Elkanah went to Ramah to his house, and the child did minister unto the Lord before Eli the priest. Now the sons of Eli were sons of Belial; they knew not the Lord. They were the devil.
- They were Levites, priests in the house of the Lord doing the work of the Lord, and yet it says they knew not the Lord. Why? Because they did not keep the commandments true in their spirit; they kept it on the surface where you can see it. They looked the part: they had on the fringes, they had the beard, they showed up on the sabbath day; but their spirit was not right with the Lord, and they were really the sons of the devil. Do not let that be you, Israel.

**[1 Samuel 2:13-14](/bible/1-samuel/2#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-samuel/2#v13)</sup> And the priests’ custom with the people was, that, when any man offered sacrifice, the priest’s servant came, while the flesh was in seething, with a fleshhook of three teeth in his hand;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-samuel/2#v14)</sup> And he struck it into the pan, or kettle, or caldron, or pot; all that the fleshhook brought up the priest took for himself. So they did in Shiloh unto all the Israelites that came thither.

- The priests' custom with the people was that when any man offered sacrifice, the priest's servant came, while the flesh was in seething, with a fleshhook of three teeth in his hand, and struck it into the pan, or kettle, or caldron, or pot; all that the fleshhook brought up the priest took for himself. They would dip it in, and what came up was the priest's portion of meat for that day; all the Levites did that, based on the sacrifices Israel brought in.
- This is how the Lord took care of Levi. He told Levi in the law that Levi would have no inheritance of land among the tribes; the Lord provided for them through the sacrifices that Israel brought, so they had need of nothing.

And if any man said, Let them not fail to burn the fat presently, and then take as much as thy soul desireth, he would answer, Nay; but thou shalt give it me now: and if not, I will take it by force.

- He said unto them, Why do ye such things? for I hear of your evil dealings by all this people; ye make the Lord's people to transgress. The Levites were the leaders, so the people followed their actions: what the priests did, the people did also. They saw the sons of Eli sleeping with the harlots and said, it must be all right, look what they have, look how they live in the temple; let us follow their lead.
- Notwithstanding they hearkened not unto the voice of their father, because the Lord would slay them. And the child Samuel grew on, and was in favour both with the Lord, and also with men.
- Eli might have said something to his children, but he did not remove them and put them out of the priesthood for their evil dealings; he said something, but he did not go further than words, he took no action with it. Watch what happened to his children.

**[1 Samuel 2:27-29](/bible/1-samuel/2#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/1-samuel/2#v27)</sup> And there came a man of God unto Eli, and said unto him, Thus saith the Lord, Did I plainly appear unto the house of thy father, when they were in Egypt in Pharaoh’s house?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/1-samuel/2#v28)</sup> And did I choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be my priest, to offer upon mine altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before me? and did I give unto the house of thy father all the offerings made by fire of the children of Israel?
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/1-samuel/2#v29)</sup> Wherefore kick ye at my sacrifice and at mine offering, which I have commanded in my habitation; and honourest thy sons above me, to make yourselves fat with the chiefest of all the offerings of Israel my people?

- There came a man of God unto Eli, saying, Thus saith the Lord, Did I plainly appear unto the house of thy father, when they were in Egypt in Pharaoh's house? That is talking about Levi, going into Moses. Did I choose him out of all the tribes of Israel to be my priest, to offer upon mine altar, to burn incense, to wear an ephod before me? and did I give unto the house of thy father all the offerings made by fire of the children of Israel? They had need of nothing; they were well taken care of.
- Wherefore kick ye at my sacrifice and at mine offering, which I have commanded in my habitation; and honourest thy sons above me? You are disrespecting me, is what he is saying: I am supposed to be honoured in everything before the children of Israel, and you let your sons disrespect my offerings.
- Take that into account: do you honour your children above what the Lord says to do? Can they do no wrong, even when they are doing real wrong against the Lord, keeping no commandments, never showing up on the sabbath day, and still living under your roof grown? If they are of age, they know right from wrong, and they are under your roof while you keep the commandments, then they had better be keeping the commandments: showing up to every feast day, the blowing of trumpets, the day of atonement, the passover, the same as you. When they are of age they can go their own way; but they should not be living under your roof and breaking the commandments of God. You honour your children above God when you allow that.

    As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord.

**[1 Samuel 2:31-33](/bible/1-samuel/2#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/1-samuel/2#v31)</sup> Behold, the days come, that I will cut off thine arm, and the arm of thy father’s house, that there shall not be an old man in thine house.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-samuel/2#v32)</sup> And thou shalt see an enemy in my habitation, in all the wealth which God shall give Israel: and there shall not be an old man in thine house for ever.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-samuel/2#v33)</sup> And the man of thine, whom I shall not cut off from mine altar, shall be to consume thine eyes, and to grieve thine heart: and all the increase of thine house shall die in the flower of their age.

- Behold, the days come, that I will cut off thine arm, and the arm of thy father's house, that there shall not be an old man in thine house. Cutting off the arm means cutting off thy strength: it was talking about his children. He was going to kill Eli's descendants young, and all the increase of his house would die in the flower of their age, in the full strength and peak of their youth.
- Why would the Lord judge the children of future generations of Eli's house? The thought might be, he is killing them for the sins of their forefather; I thought the sons did not die for the fathers. [Ezekiel 18](/bible/ezekiel/18) shows what it is talking about: the judgment falls on the son that continues in the sins of his father.

    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/18#v11)</sup> And that doeth not any of those duties, but even hath eaten upon the mountains, and defiled his neighbour’s wife,
    >

    If he beget a son that is a robber, a shedder of blood, that lifts up his eyes to the idols and commits abomination, he shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him. The son that continues in the sins of his father dies for his own doings.

    > <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/18#v15)</sup> That hath not eaten upon the mountains, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, hath not defiled his neighbour’s wife,
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/18#v16)</sup> Neither hath oppressed any, hath not withholden the pledge, neither hath spoiled by violence, but hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment,
    >

    But if he beget a son that seeth all his father's sins which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not such like, he shall not die for the iniquity of his father; he shall surely live. Eating upon the mountains is partaking in the heathen's idol worship, the food and the drink that went into it, as when Israel rose up to eat and to play before the calf.

- So why would the Lord cut off all those descendants of Eli in the strength of their youth? Because they were going to follow in the same way of their fathers, the sons of Belial, doing wickedness and evil; because Eli did not reprove his sons, the generations after would follow right in their footsteps.

**[1 Samuel 2:34-35](/bible/1-samuel/2#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-samuel/2#v34)</sup> And this shall be a sign unto thee, that shall come upon thy two sons, on Hophni and Phinehas; in one day they shall die both of them.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-samuel/2#v35)</sup> And I will raise me up a faithful priest, that shall do according to that which is in mine heart and in my mind: and I will build him a sure house; and he shall walk before mine anointed for ever.

- And this shall be a sign unto thee, that shall come upon thy two sons, on Hophni and Phinehas: in one day they shall die both of them. And I will raise me up a faithful priest, that shall do according to that which is in mine heart and in my mind; and I will build him a sure house, and he shall walk before mine anointed for ever.
- That is the same man we have to be: faithful unto the Lord, doing all that is in his heart and in his mind, not in our own. We cannot lean upon our own understanding, and we cannot lean upon our own emotions and how we feel; in whatever situation, at any given moment, it is what did the Lord say. Do not let the old man guide you and lead you; it should be the spirit of Christ which is in you, the hidden man.

**[1 Samuel 2:36](/bible/1-samuel/2#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/1-samuel/2#v36)</sup> And it shall come to pass, that every one that is left in thine house shall come and crouch to him for a piece of silver and a morsel of bread, and shall say, Put me, I pray thee, into one of the priests’ offices, that I may eat a piece of bread.

- It shall come to pass, that every one that is left in thine house shall come and crouch to him for a piece of silver and a morsel of bread. The Lord was going to make it hard on the descendants of Eli: he removed them from the priesthood, and it was written down that the descendants of Eli were not to be allowed into the house of the Lord any more.
- Remember, Levi was given no inheritance of land; so how would they eat, how would they live, how would they survive? Receiving their inheritance and their goods was based on them doing what thus saith the Lord told them to do, teaching the laws. Cut off from the priesthood, Eli's house was left to beg.
