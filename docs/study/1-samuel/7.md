**[1 Samuel 7:1-2](/bible/1-samuel/7#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-samuel/7#v1)</sup> And the men of Kirjath–jearim came, and fetched up the ark of the Lord, and brought it into the house of Abinadab in the hill, and sanctified Eleazar his son to keep the ark of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-samuel/7#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, while the ark abode in Kirjath–jearim, that the time was long; for it was twenty years: and all the house of Israel lamented after the Lord.

- The men of Kirjath-jearim fetched up the ark of the Lord into the house of Abinadab in the hill, and sanctified Eleazar his son to keep it. While the ark abode in Kirjath-jearim the time was long, twenty years, and all the house of Israel lamented after the Lord.

**[1 Samuel 7:3-4](/bible/1-samuel/7#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/7#v3)</sup> And Samuel spake unto all the house of Israel, saying, If ye do return unto the Lord with all your hearts, then put away the strange gods and Ashtaroth from among you, and prepare your hearts unto the Lord, and serve him only: and he will deliver you out of the hand of the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-samuel/7#v4)</sup> Then the children of Israel did put away Baalim and Ashtaroth, and served the Lord only.

- Samuel spake unto all the house of Israel: If ye do return unto the Lord with all your hearts, then put away the strange gods and Ashtaroth from among you, and prepare your hearts unto the Lord, and serve him only, and he will deliver you out of the hand of the Philistines. Go back to how we lost the battle even with the ark of God among us: we had strange gods among us. Deliverance is not just having the ark, it is applying the commandments that are in the ark, thou shalt have no other gods before me.
- Who is Ashtoreth? Astaroth in demonology is a chief figure of hell, named after the Near Eastern goddess Astarte, and Astarte is the Hellenized form of Ashtoreth, a form of Ishtar, the goddess called the queen of heaven, associated with love, beauty, sex, and war. Samuel was exhorting the people to depart from these fake gods and goddesses of the nations, and to this day there are Israelites that have not put away Ashtoreth from among them.
- Easter is at its roots the celebration of Ishtar, the goddess of fertility and sex. When Constantine set out to merge paganism with the faith of the apostles to bring everything under one empire, pagan rituals like Easter and Saturnalia were dressed up as honoring Christ, and that was the birth of Roman Catholicism. Nothing about it mathematically adds up to Christ rising, the apostles never celebrated it or taught it, and a bunny laying eggs has nothing to do with your Lord and Saviour; it is not of Christ.
- Then the children of Israel did put away Baalim and Ashtaroth, and served the Lord only. That is repentance.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 20:3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)</sup> Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

    Thou shalt have no other gods before me; the first commandment in the very ark they had been trusting in.
  - **[Jeremiah 44:15-16](/bible/jeremiah/44#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/jeremiah/44#v15)</sup> Then all the men which knew that their wives had burned incense unto other gods, and all the women that stood by, a great multitude, even all the people that dwelt in the land of Egypt, in Pathros, answered Jeremiah, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/jeremiah/44#v16)</sup> As for the word that thou hast spoken unto us in the name of the Lord, we will not hearken unto thee.

    The men knew that their wives burned incense unto other gods, and the people told Jeremiah plainly, we will not hearken unto thee; the same spirit is on brothers today who know their wives are in the midst of evil and will not check them.
  - **[Jeremiah 44:17-18](/bible/jeremiah/44#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/jeremiah/44#v17)</sup> But we will certainly do whatsoever thing goeth forth out of our own mouth, to burn incense unto the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, as we have done, we, and our fathers, our kings, and our princes, in the cities of Judah, and in the streets of Jerusalem: for then had we plenty of victuals, and were well, and saw no evil.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/jeremiah/44#v18)</sup> But since we left off to burn incense to the queen of heaven, and to pour out drink offerings unto her, we have wanted all things, and have been consumed by the sword and by the famine.

    We will burn incense unto the queen of heaven, for then had we plenty of victuals; since we left off, we have wanted all things. That is Ishtar worship, and it is the same spirit that says, when I was in the world celebrating her holidays I was good, and now in the truth I am catching hell.

**[1 Samuel 7:5-8](/bible/1-samuel/7#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-samuel/7#v5)</sup> And Samuel said, Gather all Israel to Mizpeh, and I will pray for you unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-samuel/7#v6)</sup> And they gathered together to Mizpeh, and drew water, and poured it out before the Lord, and fasted on that day, and said there, We have sinned against the Lord. And Samuel judged the children of Israel in Mizpeh.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/7#v7)</sup> And when the Philistines heard that the children of Israel were gathered together to Mizpeh, the lords of the Philistines went up against Israel. And when the children of Israel heard it, they were afraid of the Philistines.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-samuel/7#v8)</sup> And the children of Israel said to Samuel, Cease not to cry unto the Lord our God for us, that he will save us out of the hand of the Philistines.

- Samuel said, Gather all Israel to Mizpeh, and I will pray for you unto the Lord. They gathered, drew water and poured it out before the Lord, fasted that day, and said, We have sinned against the Lord; and Samuel judged the children of Israel in Mizpeh.
- When the lords of the Philistines heard Israel was gathered at Mizpeh, they came up against Israel again, and Israel was afraid. But this time, instead of running for the ark, the children of Israel charged Samuel, Cease not to cry unto the Lord our God for us, that he will save us out of the hand of the Philistines. They knew the prayer of a righteous man reaches the ears of the Most High.

  Precepts:
  - **[James 5:16](/bible/james/5#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/james/5#v16)</sup> Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

    The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much; that is why they told Samuel, do not stop praying for us.
  - **[1 Samuel 12:23](/bible/1-samuel/12#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/12#v23)</sup> Moreover as for me, God forbid that I should sin against the Lord in ceasing to pray for you: but I will teach you the good and the right way:

    Samuel's mentality as a leader: God forbid that I should sin against the Lord in ceasing to pray for you; for a leader of Israel to stop praying for the nation is a transgression.

**[1 Samuel 7:9-12](/bible/1-samuel/7#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/7#v9)</sup> And Samuel took a sucking lamb, and offered it for a burnt offering wholly unto the Lord: and Samuel cried unto the Lord for Israel; and the Lord heard him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-samuel/7#v10)</sup> And as Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near to battle against Israel: but the Lord thundered with a great thunder on that day upon the Philistines, and discomfited them; and they were smitten before Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-samuel/7#v11)</sup> And the men of Israel went out of Mizpeh, and pursued the Philistines, and smote them, until they came under Beth–car.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-samuel/7#v12)</sup> Then Samuel took a stone, and set it between Mizpeh and Shen, and called the name of it Eben–ezer, saying, Hitherto hath the Lord helped us.

- Samuel took a sucking lamb and offered it for a burnt offering wholly unto the Lord, and Samuel cried unto the Lord for Israel, and the Lord heard him.
- As Samuel was offering up the burnt offering, the Philistines drew near to battle, but the Lord thundered with a great thunder on that day upon the Philistines, and discomfited them, and they were smitten before Israel. That was no regular thunder; it was the Lord entering the battle himself. They had repented, put away the false gods, and the prayer of the righteous man reached his ears, so now the Philistines had to deal with the Most High.
- The Philistines were so terrified they put up no fight; the men of Israel went out of Mizpeh and pursued them, smiting them until they came under Beth-car. Then Samuel took a stone, set it between Mizpeh and Shen, and called the name of it Ebenezer, saying, Hitherto hath the Lord helped us.
- Samuel was one of the most respected prophets in the whole history of the prophets: he established the kingdom and anointed Saul, and by his faithfulness he was found a true prophet. Faithfulness translates loyalty. He came up under Eli, watched Eli's sons go wicked, Eli die, and the nation take losses, but Samuel stayed loyal to the Most High, just as Abraham's faith was tried again and again and he remained faithful.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 46:13-15](/bible/sirach/46#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/sirach/46#v13)</sup> Samuel, the prophet of the Lord, beloved of his Lord, established a kingdom, and anointed princes over his people.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/sirach/46#v14)</sup> By the law of the Lord he judged the congregation, and the Lord had respect unto Jacob.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/46#v15)</sup> By his faithfulness he was found a true prophet, and by his word he was known to be faithful in vision.

    Samuel established the kingdom, judged the congregation by the law, and by his loyalty was found a true prophet.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 46:16-17](/bible/sirach/46#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/sirach/46#v16)</sup> He called upon the mighty Lord, when his enemies pressed upon him on every side, when he offered the sucking lamb.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/46#v17)</sup> And the Lord thundered from heaven, and with a great noise made his voice to be heard.

    The Apocrypha records this very history: the sucking lamb offered, the enemies pressing on every side, and the Lord thundering from heaven.
  - **[Genesis 22:1](/bible/genesis/22#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/22#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after these things, that God did tempt Abraham, and said unto him, Abraham: and he said, Behold, here I am.

    God did tempt Abraham; the Lord tries his servants to see if they will remain faithful, and that tried loyalty is what marked Samuel as a true prophet.
  - **[Isaiah 29:6](/bible/isaiah/29#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/29#v6)</sup> Thou shalt be visited of the Lord of hosts with thunder, and with earthquake, and great noise, with storm and tempest, and the flame of devouring fire.

    Thou shalt be visited of the Lord of hosts with thunder, and with earthquake, and great noise, with storm and tempest; when that thunder hit, the Philistines knew who had entered the battle.
  - **[2 Samuel 22:14-15](/bible/2-samuel/22#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-samuel/22#v14)</sup> The Lord thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/2-samuel/22#v15)</sup> And he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them.

    The Lord thundered from heaven, and the most High uttered his voice; he sent out arrows, and scattered them; lightning, and discomfited them, the same word used of the Philistines at Mizpeh.

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-samuel/7#v13)</sup> So the Philistines were subdued, and they came no more into the coast of Israel: and the hand of the Lord was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-samuel/7#v14)</sup> And the cities which the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored to Israel, from Ekron even unto Gath; and the coasts thereof did Israel deliver out of the hands of the Philistines. And there was peace between Israel and the Amorites.

- So the Philistines were subdued, and they came no more into the coast of Israel: and the hand of the Lord was against the Philistines all the days of Samuel. As long as Samuel lived, the Lord kept them down.
- The cities the Philistines had taken from Israel were restored, from Ekron even unto Gath, and Israel delivered the coasts out of the hands of the Philistines; and there was peace between Israel and the Amorites.

**[1 Samuel 7:15-17](/bible/1-samuel/7#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-samuel/7#v15)</sup> And Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-samuel/7#v16)</sup> And he went from year to year in circuit to Beth–el, and Gilgal, and Mizpeh, and judged Israel in all those places.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-samuel/7#v17)</sup> And his return was to Ramah; for there was his house; and there he judged Israel; and there he built an altar unto the Lord.

- Samuel judged Israel all the days of his life, and went from year to year in circuit to Bethel, and Gilgal, and Mizpeh, and judged Israel in all those places; and his return was to Ramah, for there was his house, and there he built an altar unto the Lord. Ramah was his headquarters.
- That yearly circuit is leadership making its rounds, establishing the people and coming back to check on them, the same example Paul followed with the churches. These chapters close with the ark lost and regained, and Samuel rising up for the nation: prayer is a superpower, and that is how our mighty forefathers rolled, from Moses to Samuel to Judas Maccabeus all the way to Christ. Keep the commandments and send up that prayer, and the Most High will come down in thunder against your enemies.

    Paul said to Barnabas, Let us go again and visit our brethren in every city where we have preached the word of the Lord, and see how they do; the same circuit the leaders of old, like Samuel, kept every year.
