> <sup>[1](/bible/1-samuel/25#v1)</sup> And Samuel died; and all the Israelites were gathered together, and lamented him, and buried him in his house at Ramah. And David arose, and went down to the wilderness of Paran.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-samuel/25#v2)</sup> And there was a man in Maon, whose possessions were in Carmel; and the man was very great, and he had three thousand sheep, and a thousand goats: and he was shearing his sheep in Carmel.
>

- Samuel died, and all the Israelites gathered together and lamented him and buried him in his house at Ramah; and David arose and went down to the wilderness of Paran.
- There was a very great man in Maon whose possessions were in Carmel, three thousand sheep and a thousand goats, shearing his sheep in Carmel. His name was Nabal, and his wife was Abigail, a woman of good understanding and of a beautiful countenance; but the man was churlish and evil in his doings, and he was of the house of Caleb.

- Nabal answered, Who is David? and who is the son of Jesse? there be many servants now a days that break away every man from his master. He was talking much smack, as if he did not even know who David was, and refused to give of his bread, his water, and his flesh to men he knew not.
- When his young men told David, he said, Gird ye on every man his sword; about four hundred men went up after David, and two hundred abode by the stuff.

**[1 Samuel 25:14-17](/bible/1-samuel/25#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-samuel/25#v14)</sup> But one of the young men told Abigail, Nabal’s wife, saying, Behold, David sent messengers out of the wilderness to salute our master; and he railed on them.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-samuel/25#v15)</sup> But the men were very good unto us, and we were not hurt, neither missed we any thing, as long as we were conversant with them, when we were in the fields:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-samuel/25#v16)</sup> They were a wall unto us both by night and day, all the while we were with them keeping the sheep.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-samuel/25#v17)</sup> Now therefore know and consider what thou wilt do; for evil is determined against our master, and against all his household: for he is such a son of Belial, that a man cannot speak to him.

- One of the young men told Abigail: David sent messengers out of the wilderness to salute our master, and he railed on them. But the men were very good unto us; we were not hurt, neither missed we any thing as long as we were conversant with them; they were a wall unto us both by night and day while we were keeping the sheep. David's men had been giving Nabal's shepherds protection from their enemies.
- Evil is determined against our master and against all his household, for he is such a son of Belial that a man cannot speak to him. Some of us know people like that: you cannot talk to them, they will not reason with you. Consider that Nabal was rich, a very wealthy man, and a lot of times what comes with that is an arrogant spirit.

- David had said, Surely in vain have I kept all that this fellow hath in the wilderness; he hath requited me evil for good. He swore to leave Nabal none by the morning light of any that pisseth against the wall; David was coming with intent to destroy the men of that house.

**[1 Samuel 25:23-25](/bible/1-samuel/25#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-samuel/25#v23)</sup> And when Abigail saw David, she hasted, and lighted off the ass, and fell before David on her face, and bowed herself to the ground,
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/1-samuel/25#v24)</sup> And fell at his feet, and said, Upon me, my lord, upon me let this iniquity be: and let thine handmaid, I pray thee, speak in thine audience, and hear the words of thine handmaid.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-samuel/25#v25)</sup> Let not my lord, I pray thee, regard this man of Belial, even Nabal: for as his name is, so is he; Nabal is his name, and folly is with him: but I thine handmaid saw not the young men of my lord, whom thou didst send.

- When Abigail saw David she hasted, lighted off the ass, fell at his feet, and said, Upon me, my lord, upon me let this iniquity be. She was not the one who did anything wrong, but she took that iniquity upon herself; she was being that pillar of her house, using wisdom to protect it, for the scripture says she was a woman of good understanding.
- She did not turn her lord over to be destroyed, as some women today would be ready to do; instead she pleaded, Let not my lord regard this man of Belial, even Nabal: for as his name is, so is he; Nabal is his name, and folly is with him. She had not seen the young men David sent, or she would have stepped in then to protect their house.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 2:18-19](/bible/1-peter/2#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/1-peter/2#v18)</sup> Servants, be subject to your masters with all fear; not only to the good and gentle, but also to the froward.
    >
    > <sup>[19](/bible/1-peter/2#v19)</sup> For this is thankworthy, if a man for conscience toward God endure grief, suffering wrongfully.

    Servants, be subject to your masters with all fear, not only to the good and gentle but also to the froward, to those that are like Nabal; enduring grief wrongfully for conscience toward God is thankworthy.
  - **[1 Peter 3:1-2](/bible/1-peter/3#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-peter/3#v1)</sup> Likewise, ye wives, be in subjection to your own husbands; that, if any obey not the word, they also may without the word be won by the conversation of the wives;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-peter/3#v2)</sup> While they behold your chaste conversation coupled with fear.

    Likewise, ye wives, be in subjection to your own husbands, that if any obey not the word, they may without the word be won by the conversation of the wives, beholding your chaste conversation coupled with fear; this is what Abigail was putting in place.

- Her chaste conversation was her behavior: when she heard David was coming she made that feast and brought it down to meet him, to spare her house. That is the lesson for wives: if your lord is out of the spirit, instead of going railing for railing with him, your behavior should be able to win him over, to make him say, I am tripping, I have a good wife keeping the commandments who stays in a humble spirit. Provoking with the mouth just leaves an unhappy household where people living under the same roof are set against each other.

>

>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-samuel/25#v31)</sup> That this shall be no grief unto thee, nor offence of heart unto my lord, either that thou hast shed blood causeless, or that my lord hath avenged himself: but when the Lord shall have dealt well with my lord, then remember thine handmaid.

- Abigail said, The Lord hath withholden thee from coming to shed blood and from avenging thyself with thine own hand; now let thine enemies, and they that seek evil to my lord, be as Nabal. She asked, Forgive the trespass of thine handmaid; it was Nabal who was out of the spirit, but again that chaste conversation, stepping up for her household.
- The soul of my lord shall be bound in the bundle of life with the Lord thy God, and the souls of thine enemies, them shall he sling out as out of the middle of a sling. When the Lord shall have appointed thee ruler over Israel, this shall be no grief unto thee, that thou hast shed blood causeless or that my lord hath avenged himself; then remember thine handmaid.

**[1 Samuel 25:36-38](/bible/1-samuel/25#v36)**

> <sup>[36](/bible/1-samuel/25#v36)</sup> And Abigail came to Nabal; and, behold, he held a feast in his house, like the feast of a king; and Nabal’s heart was merry within him, for he was very drunken: wherefore she told him nothing, less or more, until the morning light.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/1-samuel/25#v37)</sup> But it came to pass in the morning, when the wine was gone out of Nabal, and his wife had told him these things, that his heart died within him, and he became as a stone.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/1-samuel/25#v38)</sup> And it came to pass about ten days after, that the Lord smote Nabal, that he died.

- Abigail came home and Nabal held a feast in his house like the feast of a king, and his heart was merry within him, for he was very drunken; so she told him nothing, less or more, until the morning light.
- In the morning, when the wine was gone out of Nabal, his wife told him these things, and his heart died within him, and he became as a stone. Remember he had railed, Who is David? but when he sobered up and heard that the coming king had come down to destroy everybody in his house, his heart died within him. About ten days after, the Lord smote Nabal, that he died.

And David sent and communed with Abigail, to take her to him to wife.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/1-samuel/25#v40)</sup> And when the servants of David were come to Abigail to Carmel, they spake unto her, saying, David sent us unto thee, to take thee to him to wife.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/1-samuel/25#v41)</sup> And she arose, and bowed herself on her face to the earth, and said, Behold, let thine handmaid be a servant to wash the feet of the servants of my lord.
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/1-samuel/25#v42)</sup> And Abigail hasted, and arose, and rode upon an ass, with five damsels of hers that went after her; and she went after the messengers of David, and became his wife.

- When David heard that Nabal was dead, he said, Blessed be the Lord, that hath pleaded the cause of my reproach from the hand of Nabal, and hath kept his servant from evil: for the Lord hath returned the wickedness of Nabal upon his own head. Then David sent and communed with Abigail, to take her to him to wife.
- Abigail bowed herself on her face to the earth and said, Behold, let thine handmaid be a servant to wash the feet of the servants of my lord, and she arose and washed the feet of the servants who delivered the good tidings; that is a very humble act.
- She was probably very joyous and glad to go: a woman living with a man who is hard to deal with, who will not listen, will not take advice, arrogant, maybe verbally abusive, was relieved of that thing when Nabal died, and she hasted and arose with five damsels of hers and became David's wife.
