## Introduction

This session covers [1 Samuel 8](/bible/1-samuel/8) through 11: the first king of Israel. Samuel's sons pervert judgment for bribes, and the elders demand a king to judge them like all the nations; the Most High tells Samuel that Israel has not rejected the prophet but rejected him, that he should not reign over them. Samuel warns the people of the manner of the king that shall reign over them, a warning later fulfilled in Solomon and Rehoboam. Then Saul is led to Samuel the seer while seeking his father's asses, is anointed captain over the Lord's inheritance, receives another heart, is chosen at Mizpeh, and after the victory over the Ammonites at Jabeshgilead the kingdom is renewed at Gilgal. The running thread is Israel putting its trust in a man instead of the Most High, the same spirit we walk in today.
