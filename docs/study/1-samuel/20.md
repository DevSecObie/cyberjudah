## Introduction

This session covers 1 Samuel chapters 20 through 23, the history of David fleeing from Saul: Jonathan's covenant with David and the sign of the arrows, David's empty seat at the new moon, the hallowed bread at Nob and the sword of Goliath, David feigning madness before Achish king of Gath, Doeg the Edomite slaying the priests of the Lord, and Saul pursuing David to Keilah and through the wilderness of Ziph and Maon. The history is pretty straightforward reading, so the teaching pulls in only a few precepts: Christ's own recounting of the shewbread, and the witness of scripture on trusting an enemy, shown in Doeg the Edomite.

**[1 Samuel 20:24-27](/bible/1-samuel/20#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/1-samuel/20#v24)</sup> So David hid himself in the field: and when the new moon was come, the king sat him down to eat meat.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/1-samuel/20#v25)</sup> And the king sat upon his seat, as at other times, even upon a seat by the wall: and Jonathan arose, and Abner sat by Saul’s side, and David’s place was empty.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-samuel/20#v26)</sup> Nevertheless Saul spake not any thing that day: for he thought, Something hath befallen him, he is not clean; surely he is not clean.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-samuel/20#v27)</sup> And it came to pass on the morrow, which was the second day of the month, that David’s place was empty: and Saul said unto Jonathan his son, Wherefore cometh not the son of Jesse to meat, neither yesterday, nor to day?

- David hid himself in the field, and when the new moon was come the king sat down to eat. David fled at the first of the month, and at the new moon meal his seat was empty.
- That first day Saul spake not anything, for he thought something had befallen David, that surely he was not clean; but on the second day of the month, with David's place empty again, Saul asked Jonathan, Wherefore cometh not the son of Jesse to meat?

**[1 Samuel 20:30-33](/bible/1-samuel/20#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/1-samuel/20#v30)</sup> Then Saul’s anger was kindled against Jonathan, and he said unto him, Thou son of the perverse rebellious woman, do not I know that thou hast chosen the son of Jesse to thine own confusion, and unto the confusion of thy mother’s nakedness?
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/1-samuel/20#v31)</sup> For as long as the son of Jesse liveth upon the ground, thou shalt not be established, nor thy kingdom. Wherefore now send and fetch him unto me, for he shall surely die.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/1-samuel/20#v32)</sup> And Jonathan answered Saul his father, and said unto him, Wherefore shall he be slain? what hath he done?
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/1-samuel/20#v33)</sup> And Saul cast a javelin at him to smite him: whereby Jonathan knew that it was determined of his father to slay David.

- Saul got wind that his own son was hiding David out. When Jonathan covered for David, Saul's anger was kindled against Jonathan: as long as the son of Jesse liveth upon the ground, thou shalt not be established, nor thy kingdom.
- Saul cast a javelin at Jonathan to smite him, whereby Jonathan knew that it was determined of his father to slay David; from here the history follows Saul pursuing David.
