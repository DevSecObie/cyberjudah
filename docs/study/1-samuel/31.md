**[1 Samuel 31:1-3](/bible/1-samuel/31#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-samuel/31#v1)</sup> Now the Philistines fought against Israel: and the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines, and fell down slain in mount Gilboa.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-samuel/31#v2)</sup> And the Philistines followed hard upon Saul and upon his sons; and the Philistines slew Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchi–shua, Saul’s sons.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/31#v3)</sup> And the battle went sore against Saul, and the archers hit him; and he was sore wounded of the archers.

- Now the Philistines fought against Israel, and the men of Israel fled from before the Philistines and fell down slain in mount Gilboa. The Philistines followed hard upon Saul and upon his sons, and slew Jonathan, and Abinadab, and Malchishua, Saul's sons.
- The battle went sore against Saul, and the archers hit him, and he was sore wounded of the archers.

**[1 Samuel 31:4-6](/bible/1-samuel/31#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-samuel/31#v4)</sup> Then said Saul unto his armourbearer, Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith; lest these uncircumcised come and thrust me through, and abuse me. But his armourbearer would not; for he was sore afraid. Therefore Saul took a sword, and fell upon it.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-samuel/31#v5)</sup> And when his armourbearer saw that Saul was dead, he fell likewise upon his sword, and died with him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-samuel/31#v6)</sup> So Saul died, and his three sons, and his armourbearer, and all his men, that same day together.

- Saul said unto his armourbearer, Draw thy sword, and thrust me through therewith; lest these uncircumcised come and thrust me through, and abuse me. What Saul was fearful of is what you see in war, how men will abuse their captives, killing them and abusing their bodies. He was already wounded and could not get away, so instead of being taken captive he told his armourbearer to kill him.
- His armourbearer would not, for he was sore afraid; therefore Saul took a sword and fell upon it, and when his armourbearer saw that Saul was dead, he fell likewise upon his sword and died with him. So Saul died, and his three sons, and his armourbearer, and all his men, that same day together.
- Remember what we read in chapter 28: when Saul went and conjured up the spirit of Samuel, Samuel said to him, To morrow shalt thou and thy sons be with me. He prophesied unto Saul what was going to happen, and now we read the fulfillment of that prophecy.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Samuel 28:19](/bible/1-samuel/28#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/1-samuel/28#v19)</sup> Moreover the Lord will also deliver Israel with thee into the hand of the Philistines: and to morrow shalt thou and thy sons be with me: the Lord also shall deliver the host of Israel into the hand of the Philistines.

    Samuel's prophecy from the grave, fulfilled to the letter on mount Gilboa: Saul and his sons died the next day, and Israel was delivered into the hand of the Philistines.

**[1 Samuel 31:7](/bible/1-samuel/31#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/31#v7)</sup> And when the men of Israel that were on the other side of the valley, and they that were on the other side Jordan, saw that the men of Israel fled, and that Saul and his sons were dead, they forsook the cities, and fled; and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.

- When the men of Israel that were on the other side of the valley, and they that were on the other side of Jordan, saw that the men of Israel fled and that Saul and his sons were dead, they forsook the cities and fled, and the Philistines came and dwelt in them.

**[1 Samuel 31:8-10](/bible/1-samuel/31#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-samuel/31#v8)</sup> And it came to pass on the morrow, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, that they found Saul and his three sons fallen in mount Gilboa.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-samuel/31#v9)</sup> And they cut off his head, and stripped off his armour, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to publish it in the house of their idols, and among the people.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-samuel/31#v10)</sup> And they put his armour in the house of Ashtaroth: and they fastened his body to the wall of Beth–shan.

- On the morrow, when the Philistines came to strip the slain, they found Saul and his three sons fallen in mount Gilboa. They cut off his head and stripped off his armour, and sent into the land of the Philistines round about, to publish it in the house of their idols and among the people.
- They put his armour in the house of Ashtaroth, and they fastened his body to the wall of Bethshan.

**[1 Samuel 31:11-13](/bible/1-samuel/31#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-samuel/31#v11)</sup> And when the inhabitants of Jabesh–gilead heard of that which the Philistines had done to Saul;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-samuel/31#v12)</sup> All the valiant men arose, and went all night, and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Beth–shan, and came to Jabesh, and burnt them there.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-samuel/31#v13)</sup> And they took their bones, and buried them under a tree at Jabesh, and fasted seven days.

- When the inhabitants of Jabeshgilead heard of that which the Philistines had done to Saul, all the valiant men arose and went all night, and took the body of Saul and the bodies of his sons from the wall of Bethshan, and came to Jabesh and burnt them there. They took their bones and buried them under a tree at Jabesh, and fasted seven days.
- So in chapter 31 we read of Saul and his sons and his armourbearer dying at the hands of the Philistines. This concludes the history of Saul coming to an end; the second book of Samuel takes up the rise and much of the history concerning King David.
