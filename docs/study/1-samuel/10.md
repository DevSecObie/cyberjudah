**[1 Samuel 10:1](/bible/1-samuel/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-samuel/10#v1)</sup> Then Samuel took a vial of oil, and poured it upon his head, and kissed him, and said, Is it not because the Lord hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?

- Samuel took a vial of oil and poured it upon Saul's head, and kissed him, and said, Is it not because the Lord hath anointed thee to be captain over his inheritance?

**[1 Samuel 10:2-8](/bible/1-samuel/10#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-samuel/10#v2)</sup> When thou art departed from me to day, then thou shalt find two men by Rachel’s sepulchre in the border of Benjamin at Zelzah; and they will say unto thee, The asses which thou wentest to seek are found: and, lo, thy father hath left the care of the asses, and sorroweth for you, saying, What shall I do for my son?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-samuel/10#v3)</sup> Then shalt thou go on forward from thence, and thou shalt come to the plain of Tabor, and there shall meet thee three men going up to God to Beth–el, one carrying three kids, and another carrying three loaves of bread, and another carrying a bottle of wine:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-samuel/10#v4)</sup> And they will salute thee, and give thee two loaves of bread; which thou shalt receive of their hands.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-samuel/10#v5)</sup> After that thou shalt come to the hill of God, where is the garrison of the Philistines: and it shall come to pass, when thou art come thither to the city, that thou shalt meet a company of prophets coming down from the high place with a psaltery, and a tabret, and a pipe, and a harp, before them; and they shall prophesy:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-samuel/10#v6)</sup> And the Spirit of the Lord will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-samuel/10#v7)</sup> And let it be, when these signs are come unto thee, that thou do as occasion serve thee; for God is with thee.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-samuel/10#v8)</sup> And thou shalt go down before me to Gilgal; and, behold, I will come down unto thee, to offer burnt offerings, and to sacrifice sacrifices of peace offerings: seven days shalt thou tarry, till I come to thee, and shew thee what thou shalt do.

- Samuel gave Saul the signs that would confirm the word: two men by Rachel's sepulchre saying the asses are found and thy father sorroweth for you; three men at the plain of Tabor going up to God to Bethel, who would give him two loaves of bread; and at the hill of God, where the garrison of the Philistines is, a company of prophets coming down from the high place, and the Spirit of the Lord will come upon thee, and thou shalt prophesy with them, and shalt be turned into another man.
- When these signs are come unto thee, do as occasion serve thee, for God is with thee; and go down before me to Gilgal, and seven days shalt thou tarry, till I come to thee and shew thee what thou shalt do.

>

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-samuel/10#v14)</sup> And Saul’s uncle said unto him and to his servant, Whither went ye? And he said, To seek the asses: and when we saw that they were no where, we came to Samuel.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-samuel/10#v15)</sup> And Saul’s uncle said, Tell me, I pray thee, what Samuel said unto you.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-samuel/10#v16)</sup> And Saul said unto his uncle, He told us plainly that the asses were found. But of the matter of the kingdom, whereof Samuel spake, he told him not.

**[1 Samuel 10:17-19](/bible/1-samuel/10#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-samuel/10#v17)</sup> And Samuel called the people together unto the Lord to Mizpeh;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-samuel/10#v18)</sup> And said unto the children of Israel, Thus saith the Lord God of Israel, I brought up Israel out of Egypt, and delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all kingdoms, and of them that oppressed you:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/1-samuel/10#v19)</sup> And ye have this day rejected your God, who himself saved you out of all your adversities and your tribulations; and ye have said unto him, Nay, but set a king over us. Now therefore present yourselves before the Lord by your tribes, and by your thousands.

- Samuel called the people together unto the Lord to Mizpeh: thus saith the Lord God of Israel, I brought up Israel out of Egypt, and delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians, and out of the hand of all kingdoms, and of them that oppressed you.
- Remember, we just came out of reading Judges, and we were oppressed by many nations there. Every time one of our judges rose up and delivered us from oppression, we would get right during the life of that judge, and when that judge died we went right back into our falling.
- And ye have this day rejected your God, who himself saved you out of all your adversities and your tribulations; and ye have said unto him, Nay, but set a king over us.

They ran and fetched him thence, and when he stood among the people he was higher than any of the people from his shoulders and upward; he was real tall, taller than everybody.
- Samuel said unto all the people, See ye him whom the Lord hath chosen, that there is none like him among all the people; and all the people shouted, and said, God save the king.

**[1 Samuel 10:26-27](/bible/1-samuel/10#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/1-samuel/10#v26)</sup> And Saul also went home to Gibeah; and there went with him a band of men, whose hearts God had touched.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-samuel/10#v27)</sup> But the children of Belial said, How shall this man save us? And they despised him, and brought him no presents. But he held his peace.

- Saul went home to Gibeah, and there went with him a band of men whose hearts God had touched. But the children of Belial said, How shall this man save us? and they despised him, and brought him no presents; but he held his peace.
- That is just like us today. When a man gets raised up, instead of brothers being happy for him, a brother may feel that should have been me, not knowing he has things to work on in his own spirit, and not realizing that with rank comes responsibility; you should already be doing the work. Men are being raised up for order's sake in Israel, to help set the nation in order, and we should bless them.
- That is why the scriptures call the ones who despised him the children of Belial, the children of the devil: the haters.
