## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Thessalonians 5 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-8, 11-23, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:1](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v1)</sup> But of the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that I write unto you.

- Some of us love it when the president talks about peace, stomping our feet because we think nothing is going to happen in this country. “But of the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that I write unto you.” Paul is saying, I don’t have to tell you the times and the seasons of Christ’s return; you should know them. We read the prophecies every week. If you are not paying attention, you are going to get caught off guard.
- The real brothers and the real sisters will not have to be warned. They are aware of what is about to happen.
- “Of the times and the seasons, brethren, ye have no need that I write unto you”: it doesn’t need to be explained to us; we should know this already.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:2-3](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v2)</sup> For yourselves know perfectly that the day of the Lord so cometh as a thief in the night.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v3)</sup> For when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.

- “For when they shall say, Peace and safety; then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child”. These global problems are contractions. A woman has a contraction, then a pause, then another; the contractions get closer together, from five minutes to four, three, two, one, until the baby comes. Likewise trouble comes, slows down, and makes people think everything is all right, then another trouble hits. The Lord makes his entrance at the end of those contractions, like a thief in the night.
- Do not sleep on what is happening. Esau is doing an experiment on us: a social experiment to see how society reacts to an alleged pandemic. Esau is buying up all manner of guns, and the stores have run out of bullets. It is only the beginning. A peace agreement between America and Iran does not mean safety, and China is not the next world ruler. After Esau comes Israel; that is our time to rule. Keep faith through the lulls as well as the trouble.
- “For when they shall say, Peace and safety”: that is what Trump wants, peace and safety. “Then sudden destruction cometh upon them, as travail upon a woman with child; and they shall not escape.”
- A pain will come one month, then things calm down; then another pain comes two or three months later, and it calms down again. The Bible says that is how it is going to be in this world. What we notice with the Israelites is that when things calm down we say, nothing’s going to happen, and go back to business as usual.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 16:38](/bible/2-esdras/16#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/2-esdras/16#v38)</sup> As when a woman with child in the ninth month bringeth forth her son, within two or three hours of her birth great pains compass her womb, which pains, when the child cometh forth, they slack not a moment:

    This shows what “as travail upon a woman with child” means. “As when a woman with child in the ninth month bringeth forth her son, within two or three hours of her birth great pains compass her womb, which pains, when the child cometh forth, they slack not a moment”. In the times of nations, those two or three hours translate to two or three months.
  - **[Isaiah 14:21-22](/bible/isaiah/14#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/14#v21)</sup> Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/14#v22)</sup> For I will rise up against them, saith the Lord of hosts, and cut off from Babylon the name, and remnant, and son, and nephew, saith the Lord.

    “Prepare slaughter for his children for the iniquity of their fathers; that they do not rise, nor possess the land, nor fill the face of the world with cities. For I will rise up against them, saith the Lord of hosts, and cut off from Babylon the name, and remnant, and son, and nephew, saith the Lord.”
  - **[Isaiah 54:16](/bible/isaiah/54#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/isaiah/54#v16)</sup> Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and that bringeth forth an instrument for his work; and I have created the waster to destroy.

    “Behold, I have created the smith that bloweth the coals in the fire, and that bringeth forth an instrument for his work; and I have created the waster to destroy.” The smith in those days is what we call scientists today, the ones who make weapons.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:4](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v4)</sup> But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.

- “But ye, brethren, are not in darkness, that that day should overtake you as a thief.” The day of the Lord is not coming on us as a thief; it is coming on them as a thief. We are watching, reading the signs of the time, seeing prophecies fulfilled, so we know how close it is. It could be 10 or 20 years, but as things happen you will know how close it is. This is the time to prepare, mentally and physically.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:5](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v5)</sup> Ye are all the children of light, and the children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness.

- “Ye are all the children of light, and the children of the day: we are not of the night, nor of darkness.”

**[1 Thessalonians 5:6](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v6)</sup> Therefore let us not sleep, as do others; but let us watch and be sober.

- “Therefore let us not sleep, as do others.” Don't let us sleep. We have to be up, woke, observant, because Satan is out here. He doesn't care about your status. He is trying to take out those who are doing what God said and who are going to be infectious; he attacks the ones who can make the biggest impact. If you wonder why you are getting jacked up, you are a threat. The one not going through anything isn't doing anything, and Satan isn't worried about him. But those who could be influential in raising brothers up, teaching and guiding, Satan is coming for you.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:7-8](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v7)</sup> For they that sleep sleep in the night; and they that be drunken are drunken in the night.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v8)</sup> But let us, who are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.

- “For they that sleep sleep in the night; and they that be drunken are drunken in the night. But let us, who are of the day, be sober.” Of the day means of the light. “Putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.” We have to be armored up, because that is what it takes to fight. Without it you will lose. However much you lift and however fast you are, it means nothing: if you aren't spiritually strong, you are weak.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:11](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v11)</sup> Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.

- “Wherefore comfort yourselves together, and edify one another, even as also ye do.” Knowing all these things, we don’t comfort ourselves with hugs and kisses. We edify one another.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:12](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v12)</sup> And we beseech you, brethren, to know them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you;

- This is to the leaders, dealing with the different offices and administrations: you must know your men. “And we beseech you, brethren, to know them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you.” In a position of leadership you have to know the different talents brothers have, which means paying attention to the spirits in your school. Be able to spot them and utilize those brothers, especially when they show you what they are good at or you observe it.
- You will have a soul in your school with a talent, a gift, and if you as an administrator are not utilizing them, it keeps you all in the same spot. Sometimes all the answers are right there among you, and you think you have to venture out when you have everything you need in the congregation with you.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:13](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v13)</sup> And to esteem them very highly in love for their work’s sake. And be at peace among yourselves.

- “And to esteem them very highly in love for their work’s sake.” Take the spirit of exhortation: you have to understand these spirits and place them in the right places, because they will build up other brothers.
- Because the body came together, IUIC got its own channel on Roku, and the haters came out saying they sold out. They don’t understand the power of God or the spirit of Christ that is on each one who said, we are going to put our money where our faith is and make great things happen.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:14](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v14)</sup> Now we exhort you, brethren, warn them that are unruly, comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men.

- “Warn them that are unruly”: those that don’t want to follow what the Bible says, as many do with marriage. “Comfort the feebleminded, support the weak, be patient toward all men.” In these verses is the whole ministry we must focus on.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:15-18](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v15)</sup> See that none render evil for evil unto any man; but ever follow that which is good, both among yourselves, and to all men.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v16)</sup> Rejoice evermore.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v17)</sup> Pray without ceasing.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v18)</sup> In every thing give thanks: for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.

- “See that none render evil for evil unto any man; but ever follow that which is good.” “For this is the will of God in Christ Jesus concerning you.” It is God’s will that we do these things. As we see these times and these things happening, these are the moments to move forward.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:19-22](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v19)</sup> Quench not the Spirit.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v20)</sup> Despise not prophesyings.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v21)</sup> Prove all things; hold fast that which is good.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v22)</sup> Abstain from all appearance of evil.

- We got a plethora of phone calls and emails about New York City on New Year’s Eve: at midnight there was screaming and partying, get down, boogie, shake it, drop it like it’s hot. The Bible says “Abstain from all appearance of evil.”
- I could say I know y’all wasn’t celebrating New Year’s Eve, but to the world and to new spirits it looks like it. They go, nah, you trying to be slick, y’all are celebrating it.
- “Quench not the Spirit. Despise not prophesyings. Prove all things; hold fast that which is good. Abstain from all appearance of evil.” Prophecy is not meant to entertain us. It is meant to correct us. The more we see the signs, the more urgently we should be repenting, correcting our lives and strengthening our households to prepare for those days. How are you going to do in that day, in the afflictions that are coming, when there are still issues in the marriages and divisions, and you can’t even endure each other? We have to band together in those times.
- If the days truly are getting shorter, and they are, then marriage, family order and the structure of the household can’t be treated casually anymore. Marriage is the foundation of nation building. If the marriages and the households are weak, and your household could be yourself alone, then how are you going to stand in that wicked day? We have to get it right according to scripture, and we have to get it right now.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 41:21-22](/bible/isaiah/41#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/isaiah/41#v21)</sup> Produce your cause, saith the Lord; bring forth your strong reasons, saith the King of Jacob.
    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/isaiah/41#v22)</sup> Let them bring them forth, and shew us what shall happen: let them shew the former things, what they be, that we may consider them, and know the latter end of them; or declare us things for to come.

    “Produce your cause, saith the Lord; bring forth your strong reasons, saith the King of Jacob.” We have to produce our cause when we go to speak. It has to be biblical, not our own words; it has to be the word of God. Why are you believing what you are saying? You have to bring it forth.

**[1 Thessalonians 5:23](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v23)</sup> And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly; and I pray God your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.

- Nothing is more important than getting yourself right. Whatever you are going through, at least you are preparing and building up your soul and getting yourself perfect for the Most High God, for his salvation, for the true Messiah to return.
- “And the very God of peace sanctify you wholly”: the Lord sanctified us when he called us. We are secure; we are comforted.
- “Your whole spirit and soul and body be preserved blameless unto the coming of our Lord Jesus Christ.” Before the return of the Lord we are commanded to preserve our souls blameless, and blameless means perfection: that we maintain our spirit with our faith and endure, so we come to perfection.
