## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on 1 Thessalonians 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 3-6, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[1 Thessalonians 4:3](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v3)</sup> For this is the will of God, even your sanctification, that ye should abstain from fornication:

- “For this is the will of God, even your sanctification”: your holiness, your cleansing. Evil cannot dwell in a defiled temple; the Holy Spirit will flee fornication. “That ye should abstain from fornication”.
- To abstain is to choose not to do or have something; to refrain deliberately, and often with an effort of self-denial, from an action or practice. You have to be deliberate: delete the apps from your phone and the porn sites from your computer, put a lock on it, have a brother create a password so you cannot get in, and use it only for the Lord’s work. It is easy access and free now, so be deliberate. It will not happen on its own and you will not suddenly be super strong; you have to put effort into self-denial of the fleshly lusts and self-gratifications like pornography and masturbation. Pornography leads to masturbation; nobody watches porn for the cinematography. The action is masturbating; the practice is pornography and prostitution, buying women, going on OnlyFans.

  Precepts:
  - **[Galatians 5:19-21](/bible/galatians/5#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,
    >
    > <sup>[20](/bible/galatians/5#v20)</sup> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,
    >
    > <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/5#v21)</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

    “Now the works of the flesh are manifest”. We are vessels where the Holy Spirit is supposed to dwell and sanctify us, but these are the works of the flesh, what we are dealing with in pornography and fornication. “Adultery, fornication, uncleanness”: you are no longer holy or sanctified. “Lasciviousness”: looking at another man’s wife or another woman’s husband, looking at a man the way you look at a woman, looking at a child: wicked, evil sexual desires.
  - **[1 Corinthians 10:13](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/10#v13)</sup> There hath no temptation taken you but such as is common to man: but God is faithful, who will not suffer you to be tempted above that ye are able; but will with the temptation also make a way to escape, that ye may be able to bear it.

    There is an out. You have time to be watching porn because you do not do enough in the body or for yourself: get busy in the work, go to the gym, get a real job. Too much idle time brings sin; idleness is Satan’s playground.
  - **[James 5:16](/bible/james/5#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/james/5#v16)</sup> Confess your faults one to another, and pray one for another, that ye may be healed. The effectual fervent prayer of a righteous man availeth much.

    The key is to be sincere and confess. A lot of us deal with pride and are ashamed of confessing, but we are all sick in here, and we all come to be healed by the word of the Lord and helped by our leadership.

**[1 Thessalonians 4:4](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v4)</sup> That every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour;

- “That every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour”. You have to know how to possess the vessel God gave you, in holiness and honor. It was not given to you for self-gratification, masturbation, pornography and fornication; it was given for a purpose: to go and teach the people, to heal the broken people, to preach deliverance. If you are into pornography, masturbation, prostitution or soliciting, you cannot do the work. You will be a hypocrite, you will feel guilty, and you will start separating yourself: I’m not feeling well, I can’t make it to camp. We have to let the Holy Spirit dwell in us, not the demonic entities pornography brings.

**[1 Thessalonians 4:5](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v5)</sup> Not in the lust of concupiscence, even as the Gentiles which know not God:

- “Not in the lust of concupiscence”. Concupiscence is strong desire, especially sexual desire: the burning desire you get when you want to watch porn and gratify yourself. “Even as the Gentiles which know not God”: the other nations that do not know God. We cannot be like them. You dishonor the body God gave you when you deal in pornography, fornication and masturbation. These are the scriptures we have to read to bring us out of the abyss, out of the depressed spirit and the evil concupiscence that comes over us when we are alone, without the unity and strength of the body.

**[1 Thessalonians 4:6](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v6)</sup> That no man go beyond and defraud his brother in any matter: because that the Lord is the avenger of all such, as we also have forewarned you and testified.

- “As we also have forewarned you and testified.” This is a forewarning and a testimony: stop dealing in pornography, fornication and masturbation. It only leads to depression and to worse sins; there are levels to it. A homosexual spirit, a lesbian spirit, bestiality, looking at children as sexual objects: all these things come when you deal in pornography.
