## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ephesians 2 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-3, 10-22, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ephesians 2:1](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/2#v1)</sup> And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins;

- This shows Romans 11 easier. Paul writes to the saints which are at Ephesus, and the saints are Israelites. So who is the you? “And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins”: the saints at Ephesus.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 1:1-2](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus:
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/1#v2)</sup> Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.

    “Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus”. Who is Paul writing to? The saints, and the saints are Israelites. “Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.” Now we know who Paul is writing to, with no confusion, and every ye and you in chapter 2 is them.

**[Ephesians 2:2](/bible/ephesians/2#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/2#v2)</sup> Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience:

- “Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air”: the devil.
- “Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air.” He is called the prince of the power of the air because he controls the airwaves: the media, the communication, broadcasting. That is why the book of Revelation speaks of the mouth of the dragon and the mouth of the beast, his communication through his media.
- This man controls the media not only in America but globally. Everything we do as a people is shaped by public opinion, shaped by the persuasion of Esau, the so-called white man, because he controls social media and the news, and his thoughts become our thoughts. That is why it is called networking and broadcasting: broadcasting his opinions. And his opinions, which are lies, now say the chariots are evil and demonic, to persuade the minds of the people to fight against Christ.
- “Wherein in time past ye walked according to the course of this world, according to the prince of the power of the air, the spirit that now worketh in the children of disobedience”. The class is stay in the spirit, so the question is which spirit you are staying in. First Peter showed sanctification of the spirit unto obedience. If you are obedient, you are moving in the Lord’s spirit. If you are disobedient, whose spirit are you moving in? Being disobedient is a spirit as well, and that spirit of disobedience is at work in some of us.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Peter 5:8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)</sup> Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:

    “Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour”. The devil counts you as a disgruntled employee. You were once fervent for your former employer, in all manner of lust, evil and concupiscence, lying and slandering. We have all worked for Satan at some point.
  - **[James 4:7](/bible/james/4#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/james/4#v7)</sup> Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.

    “Submit yourselves therefore to God. Resist the devil, and he will flee from you.” Satan will tempt you to keep it quiet, keep it under wraps, nobody is going to know. If you do that, you are not resisting the devil; you are doing what he told you to do. Confessing is part of resisting.
  - **[Matthew 4:1-4](/bible/matthew/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/4#v1)</sup> Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/4#v2)</sup> And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/4#v3)</sup> And when the tempter came to him, he said, If thou be the Son of God, command that these stones be made bread.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/4#v4)</sup> But he answered and said, It is written, Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceedeth out of the mouth of God.

    “Then was Jesus led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil. And when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred. And when the tempter came to him”. Another name for the devil is the tempter. All he has to do is present a certain temptation for you to take in, lock in, and go right back to your former boss. And you ain’t got to say nothing, they’ll never know: that is one of Satan’s greatest devices, and it is untrue, because your spirit says more than your mouth ever will. You cannot mask it. Trying to hide that spirit that is weighing you down is like Shaq hiding behind a tree. It is obvious.

**[Ephesians 2:3](/bible/ephesians/2#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/2#v3)</sup> Among whom also we all had our conversation in times past in the lusts of our flesh, fulfilling the desires of the flesh and of the mind; and were by nature the children of wrath, even as others.

- “Among whom also we all had our conversation in times past in the lusts of our flesh”. If your conversation now is in the lust of your flesh, who are you obedient to? That includes porn; that includes inappropriate conversation with an ex. Now we can determine if we are in the spirit of the Lord or the spirit of Satan. It is that simple. That is why things are kept secret: you know they are wrong and not of the Lord.
- “we all”. Some did not want to answer, but the Bible says we all, some more than others, some more often than others. “fulfilling the desires of the flesh and of the mind; and were by nature the children of wrath, even as others.” When children are disobedient to their parents, who are they following? Satan. Speaking reproachfully, rebellion, which is as the sin of witchcraft, stubbornness: we have all given into it. We have to see what spirit we are moving in.

**[Ephesians 2:10](/bible/ephesians/2#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/ephesians/2#v10)</sup> For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.

- “And you hath he quickened, who were dead in trespasses and sins”: all of us were dead in trespasses and sins, and Christ is the one that quickened us. “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works”.
- “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.” Whom did God before ordain to walk in his commandments? Israelites. That is who Paul is talking to.
- “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus unto good works, which God hath before ordained that we should walk in them.” God before ordained that we should walk in the commandments, and he is writing to the Israelites, the saints.
- “For we are his workmanship, created in Christ Jesus”: we are his creation. “Unto good works”: that is what we are supposed to do. Once we hear ‘This is the way, walk ye in it’, now we have to perform those good works. Don’t sit around. Don’t waste time. Put your hand to the plow and do the work.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 2:13](/bible/ephesians/2#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/ephesians/2#v13)</sup> But now in Christ Jesus ye who sometimes were far off are made nigh by the blood of Christ.

    The class reads on in the same chapter and jumps down to verse 13: “But now in Christ Jesus ye who sometimes were far off are made nigh by the blood of Christ.” We are created in Christ Jesus unto good works, and in Christ Jesus we who were far off are made nigh.

**[Ephesians 2:11-15](/bible/ephesians/2#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/2#v11)</sup> Wherefore remember, that ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh, who are called Uncircumcision by that which is called the Circumcision in the flesh made by hands;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ephesians/2#v12)</sup> That at that time ye were without Christ, being aliens from the commonwealth of Israel, and strangers from the covenants of promise, having no hope, and without God in the world:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ephesians/2#v13)</sup> But now in Christ Jesus ye who sometimes were far off are made nigh by the blood of Christ.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ephesians/2#v14)</sup> For he is our peace, who hath made both one, and hath broken down the middle wall of partition between us;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ephesians/2#v15)</sup> Having abolished in his flesh the enmity, even the law of commandments contained in ordinances; for to make in himself of twain one new man, so making peace;

- “Who are called Uncircumcision by that which is called the Circumcision”: the circumcision called the northern kingdom and those scattered uncircumcision, because they were not keeping the law of Moses, meaning the sacrificial laws and all the ordinances that came with them.
- “Wherefore remember, that ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh”. If you were born a heathen, how do you change into something else? If you are born a Gentile you are always a Gentile; your blood and your DNA do not change when you follow Christ. So these were Gentiles only in time past.
- “Who are called Uncircumcision by that which is called the Circumcision in the flesh made by hands”. The uncircumcision is the northern kingdom, and the circumcision is the southern kingdom, born and raised in the law. The northern kingdom was called uncircumcision because we took on the nature of the Greeks, the heathens: strangers, Gentiles, uncircumcision, Greeks.
- “Wherefore remember, that ye being in time past Gentiles in the flesh”. The saints, the Israelites, were in time past Gentiles. In the flesh they were literally uncut: not circumcised, raised in pagan customs, following the course of the world, behaving as the heathen in idolatry. “Who are called Uncircumcision”: not a people, Gentiles. “By that which is called the Circumcision in the flesh made by hands”: the southern kingdom, literally cut by hands.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 1:1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus:

    So we know who Paul is addressing: “to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus”. The saints are the Israelites.
  - **[2 Chronicles 15:3](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v3)</sup> Now for a long season Israel hath been without the true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law.

    “Now for a long season Israel hath been without the true God, and without a teaching priest, and without law.” Israel there is the northern kingdom: without the true God, without God in the world, which is what Paul is quoting from in Ephesians 2:12.
  - **[2 Chronicles 15:9](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-chronicles/15#v9)</sup> And he gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the strangers with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon: for they fell to him out of Israel in abundance, when they saw that the Lord his God was with him.

    Let’s see who this is: “And he gathered all Judah and Benjamin, and the strangers with them out of Ephraim and Manasseh, and out of Simeon”. The strangers are out of Ephraim, Manasseh and Simeon: the northern kingdom. The strangers of Ephesians 2 are the northern kingdom.

**[Ephesians 2:16-18](/bible/ephesians/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/ephesians/2#v16)</sup> And that he might reconcile both unto God in one body by the cross, having slain the enmity thereby:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ephesians/2#v17)</sup> And came and preached peace to you which were afar off, and to them that were nigh.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/2#v18)</sup> For through him we both have access by one Spirit unto the Father.

- “And that he might reconcile both unto God in one body by the cross”. You cannot run from that word reconcile: to be reconciled, you had to once be together. At one time we were, then the split happened, the wall of partition was built, and it has not been right since. Now through Christ both have the opportunity to make it to the kingdom.
- “Having slain the enmity thereby: And came and preached peace to you which were afar off, and to them that were nigh.” That is why Christ dealt with the Samaritan woman in John 4 and spoke to others of the northern kingdom. He understood his mission: the ushering in of the Messiah was for the unification of the body, bringing Israel back as one. Your forefathers were cut off, and now you must be brought back to who you are. You are not a Puerto Rican, not a Native American; you are an Israelite.
- “And that he might reconcile both unto God in one body by the cross, having slain the enmity thereby”. What does reconcile mean? To bring back together something that was together before. If you and your wife separate and reconcile, you come back together because you were together before. When was God ever in union with the other nations, that he should be reconciled with them? That one word destroys your doctrine. Reconcile both: Judah and Ephraim, one body, one nation, no more two kingdoms, their hatred slain.
- “And that he might reconcile both unto God in one body by the cross”. Both kingdoms reconciled to God. The purpose of Christ dying was to unite the whole twelve tribes. Look at the Israelites marching together. That's the uprising, the spiritual uprising, the mind changing. What you're looking at has never happened on the earth before. Our weapon is the Bible. That's our weapon.

  Precepts:
  - **[John 11:51-52](/bible/john/11#v51)**
    > <sup>[51](/bible/john/11#v51)</sup> And this spake he not of himself: but being high priest that year, he prophesied that Jesus should die for that nation;
    >
    > <sup>[52](/bible/john/11#v52)</sup> And not for that nation only, but that also he should gather together in one the children of God that were scattered abroad.

    “He prophesied that Jesus should die for that nation; And not for that nation only, but that also he should gather together in one the children of God that were scattered abroad.”

**[Ephesians 2:19-21](/bible/ephesians/2#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/ephesians/2#v19)</sup> Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/ephesians/2#v20)</sup> And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone;
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/ephesians/2#v21)</sup> In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord:

- “Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners”. That is why we were called Gentiles: you were called a stranger and a foreigner because you were among them, living like them, and those who remained said, they are dead, they are done, when the Lord comes back they are finished, and cut you off. Now you are “fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God”.
- “And are built upon the foundation of the apostles and prophets, Jesus Christ himself being the chief corner stone; In whom all the building fitly framed together groweth unto an holy temple in the Lord”. That is what we now are. The Lord brought us back together: in this room the northern and southern kingdoms are together again, and that wall of partition is done.
- “Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners, but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God.” This is not talking about the heathens. It is talking about the Israelites who were scattered, the northern kingdom and the remnants of the Jews, who were scattered and were living like heathens.
- “Now therefore ye are no more strangers and foreigners”. We're no longer strangers or foreigners. “but fellowcitizens with the saints, and of the household of God”. All praises for that.

  Precepts:
  - **[Colossians 1:18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/1#v18)</sup> And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.

    “And he is the head of the body, the church: who is the beginning, the firstborn from the dead; that in all things he might have the preeminence.” Christ is the head of the body, the church. In all things Christ will have the preeminence; that is why he is the Lord of lords and the King of kings. He is the chief corner stone the building rests on.
  - **[1 Kings 6:7](/bible/1-kings/6#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/1-kings/6#v7)</sup> And the house, when it was in building, was built of stone made ready before it was brought thither: so that there was neither hammer nor axe nor any tool of iron heard in the house, while it was in building.

    “And the house, when it was in building, was built of stone made ready before it was brought thither: so that there was neither hammer nor axe nor any tool of iron heard in the house, while it was in building.” When Solomon built the temple it was glorious and beautiful, fitly framed together. It was dovetailing: the stones were cut to fit like a puzzle piece or a Lego, so you did not need a nail to fasten it or anything to constrain it. That is how the wise man King Solomon built the temple.
  - **[Romans 12:4-5](/bible/romans/12#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/12#v4)</sup> For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/12#v5)</sup> So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.

    “For as we have many members in one body, and all members have not the same office: So we, being many, are one body in Christ, and every one members one of another.” We are one body in Christ because we come from Christ.

**[Ephesians 2:22](/bible/ephesians/2#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/2#v22)</sup> In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.

- “In whom ye also are builded together for an habitation of God through the Spirit.” This is talking about the chosen. Builded together means you cannot do it without each other and you cannot do it on your own. We need each other. The pinky needs the thumb; take any limb away and the others have to compensate. So we have to move in the spirit, because we depend on each other.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ephesians 5:30](/bible/ephesians/5#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/ephesians/5#v30)</sup> For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.

    “For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.” Literally: Christ took a piece of himself and made us, as the Bishop brought out. We are literally from Christ, and that is why we are supposed to be Christlike.
  - **[John 17:9-16](/bible/john/17#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/john/17#v9)</sup> I pray for them: I pray not for the world, but for them which thou hast given me; for they are thine.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/john/17#v10)</sup> And all mine are thine, and thine are mine; and I am glorified in them.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/john/17#v11)</sup> And now I am no more in the world, but these are in the world, and I come to thee. Holy Father, keep through thine own name those whom thou hast given me, that they may be one, as we are.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/john/17#v12)</sup> While I was with them in the world, I kept them in thy name: those that thou gavest me I have kept, and none of them is lost, but the son of perdition; that the scripture might be fulfilled.
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/john/17#v13)</sup> And now come I to thee; and these things I speak in the world, that they might have my joy fulfilled in themselves.
    >
    > <sup>[14](/bible/john/17#v14)</sup> I have given them thy word; and the world hath hated them, because they are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/john/17#v15)</sup> I pray not that thou shouldest take them out of the world, but that thou shouldest keep them from the evil.
    >
    > <sup>[16](/bible/john/17#v16)</sup> They are not of the world, even as I am not of the world.

    This further shows what Ephesians 5 says, that we are members of his body. “I pray for them: I pray not for the world, but for them which thou hast given me; for they are thine.” He did not pray for the Moabites or the Ammonites. Christians read this and do not comprehend it.
