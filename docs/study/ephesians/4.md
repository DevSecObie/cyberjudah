## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ephesians 4 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-4, 7-8, 11-13, 15-16, 22-32, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ephesians 4:1-3](/bible/ephesians/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/4#v1)</sup> I therefore, the prisoner of the Lord, beseech you that ye walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/4#v2)</sup> With all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another in love;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/4#v3)</sup> Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.

- This is what you're supposed to be: “I therefore, the prisoner of the Lord, beseech you that ye walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called”. Vocation is purpose, and more: our job, our profession, our career, our occupation. We are supposed to be prisoners to this Bible, prisoners to the Lord's commandments given to us, and walk worthy of that calling. This is our lifestyle. It isn't something you put on today and take off tomorrow; you live this thing day in and day out. He isn't asking you, he's telling you to walk worthy of your calling.
- What have we been called to do? To restore, renew, and bring our people back again to the heavenly Father. Our life career is to go out and teach this word, prophesy to the people, and build their spirit up, because it is in a low place, and only this word can build them back up. It is our job as men of the Lord to go out and feed them this.
- Walking worthy of the vocation, we are to be servants to the people. The sacrifices leaders have to make are for the greater good of building back Christ's kingdom: time, money, and sometimes neglecting your own family for the greater good of the body. If this is about money or status, it will never grow. We can't come into this still lying and stealing; it doesn't work.
- “With all lowliness and meekness, with longsuffering, forbearing one another in love; Endeavouring to keep the unity of the Spirit in the bond of peace.” It's hard to keep the unity of the Spirit and the bond of peace if you're lying and stealing, if your conversation is corrupt, if you're f-bombing brothers. Not hard: impossible to grow. People are not going to buy into it.

**[Ephesians 4:11](/bible/ephesians/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/4#v11)</sup> And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers;

- Watch these titles. “And he gave some, apostles”. In the New Testament an apostle is someone who is sent on a mission as a representative of the one who sent them and has the authority to speak for them. The word comes from the Greek apostolos. Apostle is sometimes confused with disciple, but they are not the same. A disciple is a student or learner devoted to learning from Jesus: everybody in this room, we’re all disciples. An apostle is a messenger, one who is sent, and believe it or not, we’re sending some of you brothers out. Walk worthy of the vocation wherewith you are called.
- “And some, prophets”. Prophets can have different roles. Teaching prophets teach truth and interpret the word of God. Revelatory prophets receive revelations and directions from the Lord for our benefit. Prophets can prophesy about the future, but that is not their primary function. Many times we think a prophet just prophesies about the future and that’s all they do. No, we’re more than that: we teach and we’re revelatory. Prophets were instrumental in guiding the nation of Israel.
- “And some, evangelists”: a preacher who travels to spread the gospel and encourage people to repent.
- “And some, pastors and teachers”. A pastor and a Bible teacher have different roles, but there is significant overlap between the two, just like with the prophets. A pastor is a spiritual leader who guides and influences the congregation, gives support and advice on spiritual matters, oversees staff and does administrative and community outreach. A lot of you brothers who are over schools, camp leaders, the head of a school, fall into that. Pastoring involves more personal interaction, seeing the practical needs and spiritual struggles of others. A Bible teacher creates an environment where people can learn about God, explains the scriptures, upholds doctrine and equips others to teach, and teaching is primarily public.

> <sup>[30](/bible/ephesians/4#v30)</sup> And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.

- “And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.” At the end of it, you are grieving the Holy Spirit when you lie and steal from God's people.
- “And grieve not the holy Spirit of God”: the laws, the spirit of God within you. Let your tongue be seasoned with salt and always bring forth edification; even a simple shalom, how you doing, the Most High and Christ bless you, because you never know what a brother or sister is going through.
- “Whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.” By applying these things you are sealed. As Bishop Kennai says, you have the crown; just don’t let anybody take it. Many cast their crown away because they like this wicked world.
- “And grieve not the holy Spirit of God, whereby ye are sealed unto the day of redemption.” Do not be the bitter brother who grieves the spirit. These are the signs of hidden bitterness.

**[Ephesians 4:31](/bible/ephesians/4#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/ephesians/4#v31)</sup> Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice:

- “Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking”: bitterness comes first, and wrath accompanies it; evil speaking is talking foolish and reckless about the brother, the sister, your wife. “Be put away from you, with all malice”: put it away with extreme hatred.
- How do we purge out the bitter leaven? The core scriptures were Exodus 12:15, putting the leaven out of the house, which is symbolic for your spirit, and 1 Corinthians 5, where Paul told Corinth the same. This brings it down to the practical level. If this spirit going on in the body is left unchecked, it can destroy the congregation.
- “Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice”. That bitterness, that hatred, will become physical at some point. How is there abuse in relationships? A man who assaults his wife, or a wife who assaults her husband, did not wake up that day saying, as soon as I open my eyes I'm going to beat the person sleeping next to me. Things compounded over time and were left unresolved: you never went to your spouse and talked them out, never laboured to gain them, to win your husband or your wife back over so that whatever spirit is on them would flee. That is how it gets to malice and to violence.
- King Saul did not just throw that javelin; he did not wake up saying whoever comes in this room, I'm throwing a javelin at them. Things led up to it that he would not acknowledge and repent of. He was not sorrowful to God, so the Lord did not remove that spirit from him. There are times we have those spirits in our own houses and the Lord does not remove them, because there is a lack of accountability among us: we are still putting forth the finger.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 13:5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v5)</sup> Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves. Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?

    “Examine yourselves, whether ye be in the faith; prove your own selves.” Not peeping at your neighbour: examine yourself. “Know ye not your own selves, how that Jesus Christ is in you, except ye be reprobates?”
  - **[Proverbs 14:10](/bible/proverbs/14#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/14#v10)</sup> The heart knoweth his own bitterness; and a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy.

    “The heart knoweth his own bitterness.” You cannot be confused when somebody asks where that bitterness is coming from. Don’t ask the Bishop to tell you what is going on with you. We can say, brother, you are off, there is sin in there, but you know exactly the specifics of what you are dealing with. “And a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy.”
  - **[Psalms 139:23-24](/bible/psalms/139#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/psalms/139#v23)</sup> Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts:
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/psalms/139#v24)</sup> And see if there be any wicked way in me, and lead me in the way everlasting.

    “Search me, O God, and know my heart: try me, and know my thoughts: And see if there be any wicked way in me.” Ask the Lord to reveal the iniquity in you and not let you be blind to your own folly. And how does he search you? The Lord is not going to come down and open the door to see what is behind it. You have to search the scriptures.

**[Ephesians 4:32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32)</sup> And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.

- “And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.” We read that in the law. You want pardon from the Lord while you have hatred toward your brother; Sirach 28 already answered that.
- “And be ye kind one to another”. We have to learn to be kind one to another.

  Precepts:
  - **[Colossians 3:12-13](/bible/colossians/3#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/colossians/3#v12)</sup> Put on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering;
    >
    > <sup>[13](/bible/colossians/3#v13)</sup> Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.

    “Put on therefore, as the elect of God, holy and beloved, bowels of mercies, kindness, humbleness of mind, meekness, longsuffering; Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.” That is how we have to move: forgive one another.
  - **[Matthew 6:14-15](/bible/matthew/6#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/6#v14)</sup> For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you:
    >
    > <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/6#v15)</sup> But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

    “For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you: But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.” Offences are going to come; a brother or sister will say something and we will get offended. We have to have mercy on one another, because we are all sick. Two men in the same emergency room with the same sickness: one coughs on the other, and the other rebukes him while coughing himself. This is a hospital, family. If a brother coughs on you, forgive him.
