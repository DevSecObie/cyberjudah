## Introduction

These notes gather what the CyberJudah classes taught on Ephesians 1 in their scripture breakdowns: verses 1-7, 9-11, 17-19, each with the class's breakdown and the precepts read with it.

**[Ephesians 1:1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/1#v1)</sup> Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus:

- Before you get to Ephesians 2, where Christians try to hem you up, you have to deal with Ephesians 1:1. “to the saints which are at Ephesus”: who are the saints Paul is addressing? The children of Israel only.
- “Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus”. Paul writes to the saints at Ephesus, in Turkey. Who are the saints? The Psalms say why Paul calls these people saints.
- “Paul, an apostle of Jesus Christ by the will of God, to the saints which are at Ephesus, and to the faithful in Christ Jesus.” An apostle, not a false apostle. Who are the saints at Ephesus? The saints are the children of Israel.
- Paul wrote to the Ephesians, a Greek colony, in the language they understood, and he defined saints from the Hebrew scriptures by precept. So why go to the Greek to define something we read in English? It makes no sense. Stop watching these kids; you will get yourselves confused and destroyed.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 148:14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/148#v14)</sup> He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him. Praise ye the Lord.

    “He also exalteth the horn of his people, the praise of all his saints; even of the children of Israel, a people near unto him.” The only saints are the children of Israel. There are no saints called Catholics, Baptists, Methodists or Presbyterians; that is supersessionism, replacement theology.
  - **[Psalms 50:5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/50#v5)</sup> Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.

    “Gather my saints together unto me; those that have made a covenant with me by sacrifice.” The saints are those who made a covenant with him by sacrifice: Israelites.
  - **[Ezekiel 37:22](/bible/ezekiel/37#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/ezekiel/37#v22)</sup> And I will make them one nation in the land upon the mountains of Israel; and one king shall be king to them all: and they shall be no more two nations, neither shall they be divided into two kingdoms any more at all:

    A sidebar for a question from the audience. “And I will make them one nation in the land upon the mountains of Israel; and one king shall be king to them all: and they shall be no more two nations, neither shall they be divided into two kingdoms any more at all.” That is Judah and Israel.

**[Ephesians 1:2-4](/bible/ephesians/1#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/ephesians/1#v2)</sup> Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ephesians/1#v3)</sup> Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/ephesians/1#v4)</sup> According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love:

- “Grace be to you, and peace, from God our Father, and from the Lord Jesus Christ. Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ.” Our Christ has a God. “Who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ.”
- “Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ: According as he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world, that we should be holy and without blame before him in love”. The class began in the womb; this is before the womb. When you are in the womb, it is the vessel already pre-ordained, predestined, foreknown, coming through as he ordained. A mother is already carrying the prophet or the princess that was already in Christ before the world was made. Some say there is no way he chose me, Cleopus Jenkins Jr., before the world was made. Then you do not believe what God said.
- In Ephesus there were Gentiles and Greeks living by Hellenized means, worshipping other gods. Paul is telling them that before they ever became Hellenized they were already chosen to hear the gospel of Christ and follow after him. That was ordained before the Greek captivity, before the Roman captivity, before Persia and Babylon.
- “who hath blessed us with all spiritual blessings in heavenly places in Christ”: that is heavy, and a whole class could be spent on it, on power, knowledge, wisdom and understanding, but that is for another class.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Chronicles 16:13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/1-chronicles/16#v13)</sup> O ye seed of Israel his servant, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.

    “O ye seed of Israel his servant, ye children of Jacob, his chosen ones.” We are the chosen ones. That is the us of “he hath chosen us in him before the foundation of the world”.
  - **[Romans 7:12](/bible/romans/7#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/romans/7#v12)</sup> Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.

    “Wherefore the law is holy, and the commandment holy, and just, and good.” So for us to be the holy people God destines us to be, “that we should be holy and without blame before him”, we must study the law and apply the law.
  - **[2 Timothy 1:9](/bible/2-timothy/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-timothy/1#v9)</sup> Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling, not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began,

    “Who hath saved us, and called us with an holy calling”: we are the called, and those who were predestinated are whom he called. “Not according to our works, but according to his own purpose and grace, which was given us in Christ Jesus before the world began.” The grace and mercy were given unto us in Christ before the world began, as Ephesians 1:4 says. We were always predestined to receive mercy and grace, before we were formed in the womb. That is why he told Jeremiah, before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee.

**[Ephesians 1:5](/bible/ephesians/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/1#v5)</sup> Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself, according to the good pleasure of his will,

- “Having predestinated us”: there it is again, predestinated before the foundation of the world, “unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself”.
- “Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself”: this is the Father. We become his children, the sons of God, through Christ: that is the adoption, to himself, to the Father. Christ is no longer the only begotten Son; we are all sons of God. That is why Christ teaches us to pray, our Father which art in heaven: he is my Father and he is your Father. For that, Christ had to come and die first.
- Many things in the scriptures were hidden, a veil over everybody’s eyes, so that our people couldn’t see that Christ was the one dealing with us all through the scriptures. When he came and died, everything became clear.
- “Having predestinated us unto the adoption of children by Jesus Christ to himself”. Who is the us? Those whom he foreknew. The adoption goes back to the same words: reconciliation, justification, restoration, atonement. It is all the same thing. We will be the true God’s children because Christ died for us, if we overcome. “according to the good pleasure of his will.”

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 11:1-2](/bible/romans/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/romans/11#v1)</sup> I say then, Hath God cast away his people? God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/romans/11#v2)</sup> God hath not cast away his people which he foreknew. Wot ye not what the scripture saith of Elias? how he maketh intercession to God against Israel, saying,

    On foreknew: “I say then, Hath God cast away his people?” Modern Christians say we were cast away because we denied Christ. “God forbid. For I also am an Israelite, of the seed of Abraham, of the tribe of Benjamin.” “God hath not cast away his people which he foreknew.” Which he foreknew: back to Amos 3 and Romans 8. The people he foreknew, and predestinated unto the adoption, are Israel.
  - **[Romans 9:3-5](/bible/romans/9#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/romans/9#v3)</sup> For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/romans/9#v4)</sup> Who are Israelites; to whom pertaineth the adoption, and the glory, and the covenants, and the giving of the law, and the service of God, and the promises;
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/romans/9#v5)</sup> Whose are the fathers, and of whom as concerning the flesh Christ came, who is over all, God blessed for ever. Amen.

    “For I could wish that myself were accursed from Christ for my brethren, my kinsmen according to the flesh”. Paul says, I wish I could die for my people, my kinsmen: my race, ethnically, according to the flesh. “Who are Israelites”: Paul was of Benjamin.

**[Ephesians 1:6-7](/bible/ephesians/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/ephesians/1#v6)</sup> To the praise of the glory of his grace, wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ephesians/1#v7)</sup> In whom we have redemption through his blood, the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace;

- “wherein he hath made us accepted in the beloved. In whom we have redemption through his blood”: we, the saints, have redemption through the blood of Christ, “the forgiveness of sins, according to the riches of his grace.”

**[Ephesians 1:9-10](/bible/ephesians/1#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/ephesians/1#v9)</sup> Having made known unto us the mystery of his will, according to his good pleasure which he hath purposed in himself:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ephesians/1#v10)</sup> That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him:

- Read as a whole letter, Ephesians focuses heavily on God’s plan to bring all things united under Christ. He made known “the mystery of his will”: “That in the dispensation of the fulness of times he might gather together in one all things in Christ, both which are in heaven, and which are on earth; even in him.” Unity: gathering all things into one.
- In chapters 1 to 3 Paul shows that plan being carried out: the middle wall of partition broken down between the northern and southern kingdom, and now one new man in Christ. In chapters 4 to 6 he applies it: living out the unity of the spirit in the congregation, in personal daily conduct, and especially in the household. So when he reaches Ephesians 5:22 and on, marriage represents the relationship between Christ and the church, the same order as 1 Corinthians 11:3; then he touches the children; and in chapter 6 he tells marriages, households, congregations and individuals to put on armour against whatever would break down that unity of all things under Christ.
- That is what makes his marriage argument so strong. Submission, love, reverence, grace and self-examination are not only for marriage; they apply to every one of us in this walk, and they show what life is going to be in the kingdom of heaven. If you can’t be faithful in those things here on earth, how can you be an heir of them in the kingdom? Biblical marriage is not about men trying to dominate women. It is God’s order, so that all things can be brought under Christ, quite literally.

> <sup>[11](/bible/ephesians/1#v11)</sup> In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will:

- “In whom also we have obtained an inheritance, being predestinated according to the purpose of him who worketh all things after the counsel of his own will.”

**[Ephesians 1:17](/bible/ephesians/1#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/ephesians/1#v17)</sup> That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him:

- Prophecy is a good thing, and we love prophecy. But Paul prays “that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom”: brothers, we all want God to give us the spirit of wisdom. “And revelation”: we want the Lord to bless us with that spirit of revelation, “in the knowledge of him.” That’s what you pray for; if y’all ain’t praying for it, you better pray for it.
- ‘The Bible is the most valuable treasure of knowledge, but it is applying God’s laws that turns that knowledge into wisdom.’
- “That the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him.” The knowledge of God is a revelation: understanding things that must be revealed. People don’t understand the Bible because God has not imparted that revelation to them. We don’t read the Bible like a textbook. This is spiritual; it has to be shown to you, and you have to love him first for him to reveal it.
- God created us for his purpose and for his glory. Our job is to understand just that: not to try to figure it out on our own, but to figure out exactly what God wants us to do. So he prays “that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of glory, may give unto you the spirit of wisdom and revelation in the knowledge of him.”

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Timothy 3:16](/bible/1-timothy/3#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/1-timothy/3#v16)</sup> And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory.

    “And without controversy great is the mystery of godliness: God was manifest in the flesh, justified in the Spirit, seen of angels, preached unto the Gentiles, believed on in the world, received up into glory.” That is the mystery the God of our Lord gives the knowledge of.
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 9:9-11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v9)</sup> And wisdom was with thee: which knoweth thy works, and was present when thou madest the world, and knew what was acceptable in thy sight, and right in thy commandments.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v10)</sup> O send her out of thy holy heavens, and from the throne of thy glory, that being present she may labour with me, that I may know what is pleasing unto thee.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/9#v11)</sup> For she knoweth and understandeth all things, and she shall lead me soberly in my doings, and preserve me in her power.

    “And wisdom was with thee: which knoweth thy works, and was present when thou madest the world.” Wisdom was present when God made the world. That goes back to John 1:1, the Word that was with God, and let us make man in our image. “And knew what was acceptable in thy sight, and right in thy commandments.”

**[Ephesians 1:18](/bible/ephesians/1#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/1#v18)</sup> The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints,

- “The eyes of your understanding being enlightened”: brothers, understand that. “That ye may know what is the hope of his calling”: y’all are called in here for a reason. It’s not just to get a wife or a spouse. It’s to improve our lives and gear ourselves towards getting the kingdom of heaven on earth, to gather the twelve tribes of Israel. “And what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints.”
- “The eyes of your understanding being enlightened; that ye may know what is the hope of his calling, and what the riches of the glory of his inheritance in the saints.”
- “The eyes of your understanding being enlightened.” Keep this in mind. Do not take the gift you have for granted. The Most High did not enlighten everybody to the knowledge of being an Israelite, that the world was created for you, that God is black and Christ is black. God pulled you out of the matrix. We were living in the same world before, but now we see clearly. We see things differently because the Most High saw fit to open our eyes, so we could see the truth and what is really going on in the world.
- “That ye may know what is the hope of his calling.” If you don’t have discernment, if you feel lost, if you don’t have a clue, you need to pray and fast and be amongst the righteous, so you can start developing and learning what you are called for. Some brothers and sisters don’t know, and that is not good. If you don’t know why you keep going to the Sabbath, why you wear fringes and don’t eat pork, that is an issue. You better repent, fast and pray so you can get on board with the Most High’s program.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 119:18](/bible/psalms/119#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/119#v18)</sup> Open thou mine eyes, that I may behold wondrous things out of thy law.

    “Open thou mine eyes”: this is what David said, and this is what we ought to say. It’s talking about our spiritual eyes, not the eyes y’all got in your head; it’s your mental eye, what the world calls your third eye. That is the eyes of your understanding being enlightened.

**[Ephesians 1:19](/bible/ephesians/1#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/ephesians/1#v19)</sup> And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power,

- “And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe, according to the working of his mighty power.” How great a power we have in God, that he has imparted it to us. In this world we are superheroes, larger than life. We may not get respect from these nations, but God sees what we are doing, turning things right side up on these streets.
- We are the only ones making real noise. The church is on lunch break. The Nation of Islam is no more out in the streets. Other Israelite groups argue about multiple wives. We are the only ones out there pleading the cause of all people and bringing them to repentance, raising men from the grassroots: young men and children coming in off the street, where others only have the grandchildren of the people already there.
- A young man in a video had the image of Christ as the wallpaper on his phone and listened to Original Royalty music; he called it God, and had never yet been in a school. He was learning by watching. That is a glimpse of the ripple effect, the next generation being reached, and the spirit of God directing the brothers to stand out there that day and run into him. We have not yet fully seen the power of God exposed; the Lord is going to manifest it soon enough.
- “And what is the exceeding greatness of his power to us-ward who believe.” What we are to see is that this was hidden from us, and it has to be given.
