Read the chapter → [Genesis 2](/bible/genesis/2)

**[Genesis 2:1-3](/bible/genesis/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/2#v1)</sup> Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/2#v2)</sup> And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/2#v3)</sup> And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.

- This is the first Sabbath that you read about in the scriptures. The Most High blessed the seventh day and sanctified it.

**[Genesis 2:4-6](/bible/genesis/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/2#v4)</sup> These are the generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were created, in the day that the Lord God made the earth and the heavens,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/2#v5)</sup> And every plant of the field before it was in the earth, and every herb of the field before it grew: for the Lord God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was not a man to till the ground.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/2#v6)</sup> But there went up a mist from the earth, and watered the whole face of the ground.

- Chapter 2 is the manner of creation. It goes back through what you read in chapter 1 and gives you more detail on certain things.

**[Genesis 2:7](/bible/genesis/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/2#v7)</sup> And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.

- Here is the detail on how man was formed: the Most High formed him of the dust of the ground.
- The breath of life is talking about the commandments.
- This is Adam, the chosen. Out of all the people the Most High created on the earth, He had a favorite from the beginning.

**[Genesis 2:8-14](/bible/genesis/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/2#v8)</sup> And the Lord God planted a garden eastward in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had formed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/2#v9)</sup> And out of the ground made the Lord God to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/2#v10)</sup> And a river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/2#v11)</sup> The name of the first is Pison: that is it which compasseth the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/2#v12)</sup> And the gold of that land is good: there is bdellium and the onyx stone.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/2#v13)</sup> And the name of the second river is Gihon: the same is it that compasseth the whole land of Ethiopia.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/2#v14)</sup> And the name of the third river is Hiddekel: that is it which goeth toward the east of Assyria. And the fourth river is Euphrates.

- The Most High planted the garden eastward in Eden and put the chosen man there.
- The river Gihon is the Blue Nile; it compasses the whole land of Ethiopia.

**[Genesis 2:16-17](/bible/genesis/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/2#v16)</sup> And the Lord God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/2#v17)</sup> But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.

- Some of these trees are literal trees, and some of these trees represent men.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 8:24](/bible/mark/8#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/mark/8#v24)</sup> And he looked up, and said, I see men as trees, walking.

    Scripture uses trees to represent men.

**[Genesis 2:18-20](/bible/genesis/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/2#v18)</sup> And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/2#v19)</sup> And out of the ground the Lord God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air; and brought them unto Adam to see what he would call them: and whatsoever Adam called every living creature, that was the name thereof.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/2#v20)</sup> And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field; but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him.

- The Most High looked at the women on the earth and there was no help meet right for Adam. This again shows there were more people on the earth than Adam alone.

**[Genesis 2:21-23](/bible/genesis/2#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/2#v21)</sup> And the Lord God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept: and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/2#v22)</sup> And the rib, which the Lord God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/2#v23)</sup> And Adam said, This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.

- The Most High created Eve from Adam because there was no woman on the earth right for him at that time.
