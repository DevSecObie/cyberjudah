## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 36](/bible/genesis/36) through 39: the generations of Esau, Joseph the dreamer sold into Egypt, Judah and Tamar, and Joseph in Potiphar's house.

**[Genesis 36:1](/bible/genesis/36#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/36#v1)</sup> Now these are the generations of Esau, who is Edom.

- Now these are the generations of Esau, who is Edom. Esau is Edom, and this teaching identifies Edom with the people called the white man today.
- Esau came out of the womb red, all over like an hairy garment; his blood could be seen through his skin for lack of melanin, and his name Esau goes into wasted away. Jacob was not described, because Jacob looked like everyone from Adam down to his time; the man of melanin needed no description.
- Esau was called Edom, which means red, because he sold his birthright for that same red pottage.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 25:24-26](/bible/genesis/25#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/25#v24)</sup> And when her days to be delivered were fulfilled, behold, there were twins in her womb.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/25#v25)</sup> And the first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; and they called his name Esau.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/25#v26)</sup> And after that came his brother out, and his hand took hold on Esau’s heel; and his name was called Jacob: and Isaac was threescore years old when she bare them.

    The first came out red, all over like an hairy garment; the twins were two nations, polar opposites.
  - **[Genesis 25:30](/bible/genesis/25#v30)**
    > <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/25#v30)</sup> And Esau said to Jacob, Feed me, I pray thee, with that same red pottage; for I am faint: therefore was his name called Edom.

    Feed me with that same red pottage; therefore was his name called Edom, which is red.

**[Genesis 36:2-5](/bible/genesis/36#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/36#v2)</sup> Esau took his wives of the daughters of Canaan; Adah the daughter of Elon the Hittite, and Aholibamah the daughter of Anah the daughter of Zibeon the Hivite;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/36#v3)</sup> And Bashemath Ishmael’s daughter, sister of Nebajoth.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/36#v4)</sup> And Adah bare to Esau Eliphaz; and Bashemath bare Reuel;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/36#v5)</sup> And Aholibamah bare Jeush, and Jaalam, and Korah: these are the sons of Esau, which were born unto him in the land of Canaan.

- Esau took his wives of the daughters of Canaan and of Ishmael. He was the first of the Edomites; there were no women of his nation yet, so his lineage began through those wives. Genesis is Bereshith, the beginning of nations.

**[Genesis 36:6-8](/bible/genesis/36#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/36#v6)</sup> And Esau took his wives, and his sons, and his daughters, and all the persons of his house, and his cattle, and all his beasts, and all his substance, which he had got in the land of Canaan; and went into the country from the face of his brother Jacob.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/36#v7)</sup> For their riches were more than that they might dwell together; and the land wherein they were strangers could not bear them because of their cattle.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/36#v8)</sup> Thus dwelt Esau in mount Seir: Esau is Edom.

- Esau went from the face of his brother because their riches were more than that they might dwell together, the same as with Abram and Lot in [Genesis 13](/bible/genesis/13). Esau was rich at this time, out of the house of Isaac and Abraham.
- Thus dwelt Esau in mount Seir. Mount Seir is the clefts of the rock; the vision of Obadiah speaks to Edom as thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose pride hath deceived him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Obadiah 1:3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/obadiah/1#v3)</sup> The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock, whose habitation is high; that saith in his heart, Who shall bring me down to the ground?

    The pride of thine heart hath deceived thee, thou that dwellest in the clefts of the rock; that is Edom in mount Seir.

**[Genesis 36:9-11](/bible/genesis/36#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/36#v9)</sup> And these are the generations of Esau the father of the Edomites in mount Seir:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/36#v10)</sup> These are the names of Esau’s sons; Eliphaz the son of Adah the wife of Esau, Reuel the son of Bashemath the wife of Esau.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/36#v11)</sup> And the sons of Eliphaz were Teman, Omar, Zepho, and Gatam, and Kenaz.

- Esau is the father of the Edomites in mount Seir, and his grandson Teman, son of Eliphaz, is identified in this teaching with Germany; the reference work Edom and Germany lays that connection out.
- The prophecy over Teman stands: the wise men shall be destroyed out of Edom and understanding out of the mount of Esau, and the mighty men of Teman shall be dismayed. Much of the world's science and weapons technology has come out of Germany, and the word says that wisdom will be destroyed.

  Precepts:
  - **[Obadiah 1:8-9](/bible/obadiah/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/obadiah/1#v8)</sup> Shall I not in that day, saith the Lord, even destroy the wise men out of Edom, and understanding out of the mount of Esau?
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/obadiah/1#v9)</sup> And thy mighty men, O Teman, shall be dismayed, to the end that every one of the mount of Esau may be cut off by slaughter.

    The wise men of Edom and the mighty men of Teman shall be destroyed, that every one of the mount of Esau may be cut off.

**[Genesis 36:12](/bible/genesis/36#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/36#v12)</sup> And Timna was concubine to Eliphaz Esau’s son; and she bare to Eliphaz Amalek: these were the sons of Adah Esau’s wife.

- Timna was concubine to Eliphaz, and she bare Amalek. Amalek came of a concubine, a lesser wife, which is why the word bastard is studied here: in the scriptures it means a child of incest or one born outside the proper marriage.
- The word says a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod, and Ashdod is the area of modern day Tel Aviv; this teaching places the line of Amalek dwelling there today.

  Precepts:
  - **[Zechariah 9:6](/bible/zechariah/9#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/zechariah/9#v6)</sup> And a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod, and I will cut off the pride of the Philistines.

    And a bastard shall dwell in Ashdod; the prophecy names the place where that seed sits.

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/36#v15)</sup> These were dukes of the sons of Esau: the sons of Eliphaz the firstborn son of Esau; duke Teman, duke Omar, duke Zepho, duke Kenaz,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/36#v16)</sup> Duke Korah, duke Gatam, and duke Amalek: these are the dukes that came of Eliphaz in the land of Edom; these were the sons of Adah.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/36#v17)</sup> And these are the sons of Reuel Esau’s son; duke Nahath, duke Zerah, duke Shammah, duke Mizzah: these are the dukes that came of Reuel in the land of Edom; these are the sons of Bashemath Esau’s wife.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/36#v18)</sup> And these are the sons of Aholibamah Esau’s wife; duke Jeush, duke Jaalam, duke Korah: these were the dukes that came of Aholibamah the daughter of Anah, Esau’s wife.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/36#v19)</sup> These are the sons of Esau, who is Edom, and these are their dukes.

- These were the dukes of the sons of Esau: dukes are the chiefs of their tribes, the leaders of the families of Edom.

- The sons of Seir the Horite inhabited the land before Esau; the Horites were the original dwellers of mount Seir.
- The Lord destroyed the Horims from before the children of Esau, and they succeeded them and dwelt in their stead, as Deuteronomy records.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 14:6](/bible/genesis/14#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/14#v6)</sup> And the Horites in their mount Seir, unto El–paran, which is by the wilderness.

    The Horites were in their mount Seir already in Abraham's day.
  - **[Deuteronomy 2:22](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v22)</sup> As he did to the children of Esau, which dwelt in Seir, when he destroyed the Horims from before them; and they succeeded them, and dwelt in their stead even unto this day:

    The Lord destroyed the Horims from before the children of Esau, and they dwelt in their stead.

**[Genesis 36:31](/bible/genesis/36#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/36#v31)</sup> And these are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom, before there reigned any king over the children of Israel.

- These are the kings that reigned in the land of Edom before there reigned any king over the children of Israel. Israel had no king until Saul, whom the Lord sought out when Israel asked for a king like the nations, in [1 Samuel 8](/bible/1-samuel/8) and 9.
