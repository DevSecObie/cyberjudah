## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 1](/bible/genesis/1) through 4 straight through. When you deal with Genesis you are dealing with an illustrated story; the pictures in it stand for something, and every teaching is backed with a precept.

**[Genesis 1:1-2](/bible/genesis/1#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/1#v1)</sup> In the beginning God created the heaven and the earth.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/1#v2)</sup> And the earth was without form and void; and darkness was upon the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God moved upon the face of the waters.

- This darkness is not a symbol for sin; it is real darkness. In the beginning everything was black.

**[Genesis 1:3](/bible/genesis/1#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/1#v3)</sup> And God said, Let there be light: and there was light.

- This light came before the sun, the moon, and the stars, which were not made until the fourth day. It is the light you see in the morning sky before the sun comes up.
- There is light in the sky when the sun is not out; the Most High made that light first. The light is twelve hours and the darkness is twelve hours, and afterward He made the sun as the greater light to rule the day.
- What exactly that first light was is not declared; what is declared is that He commanded a fair light so that His work might appear. [Genesis 1:1](/bible/genesis/1#v1) coincides with [2 Esdras 6](/bible/2-esdras/6).

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 6:38-40](/bible/2-esdras/6#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/2-esdras/6#v38)</sup> And I said, O Lord, thou spakest from the beginning of the creation, even the first day, and saidst thus; Let heaven and earth be made; and thy word was a perfect work.
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/2-esdras/6#v39)</sup> And then was the spirit, and darkness and silence were on every side; the sound of man’s voice was not yet formed.
    >
    > <sup>[40](/bible/2-esdras/6#v40)</sup> Then commandedst thou a fair light to come forth of thy treasures, that thy work might appear.

    The Most High commanded a fair light on the first day so that His work might appear.

**[Genesis 1:5](/bible/genesis/1#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/1#v5)</sup> And God called the light Day, and the darkness he called Night. And the evening and the morning were the first day.

- The day begins in the evening. The day begins when it is dark and it ends when it is dark.

**[Genesis 1:6-8](/bible/genesis/1#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/1#v6)</sup> And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/1#v7)</sup> And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament: and it was so.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/1#v8)</sup> And God called the firmament Heaven. And the evening and the morning were the second day.

- The firmament is the sky. It divides the waters below from the waters above; space is water up there.

**[Genesis 1:14-19](/bible/genesis/1#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/1#v14)</sup> And God said, Let there be lights in the firmament of the heaven to divide the day from the night; and let them be for signs, and for seasons, and for days, and years:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/1#v15)</sup> And let them be for lights in the firmament of the heaven to give light upon the earth: and it was so.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/1#v16)</sup> And God made two great lights; the greater light to rule the day, and the lesser light to rule the night: he made the stars also.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/1#v17)</sup> And God set them in the firmament of the heaven to give light upon the earth,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/1#v18)</sup> And to rule over the day and over the night, and to divide the light from the darkness: and God saw that it was good.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/1#v19)</sup> And the evening and the morning were the fourth day.

- On the fourth day the Most High created the sun, the moon, and the stars to give light upon the earth and make a brighter light upon it.
- The sun is the greater light; it was made brighter than the light created on the first day, which is how it rules over the day.

**[Genesis 1:26-27](/bible/genesis/1#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/1#v26)</sup> And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/1#v27)</sup> So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.

- Notice it says let them have dominion. The Most High did not create just one man; the man spoken of here is mankind.
- The words created he him point to a particular person, who begins to be described in [Genesis 2](/bible/genesis/2). Male and female created he them is mankind.

**[Genesis 1:28](/bible/genesis/1#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/1#v28)</sup> And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.

- The Most High blessed mankind here, just as He blessed the whales, the fowls, and the cattle before them.

**[Genesis 1:29](/bible/genesis/1#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/1#v29)</sup> And God said, Behold, I have given you every herb bearing seed, which is upon the face of all the earth, and every tree, in the which is the fruit of a tree yielding seed; to you it shall be for meat.

- The herb was created for meat, that is, for food.

**[Genesis 1:31](/bible/genesis/1#v31)**

> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/1#v31)</sup> And God saw every thing that he had made, and, behold, it was very good. And the evening and the morning were the sixth day.

- Everything He made was very good according to the Most High; snakes and jellyfish included. He created everything for His own purpose.

# Genesis 2: Adam, the Chosen of the Most High

Read the chapter → [Genesis 2](/bible/genesis/2)

**[Genesis 2:1-3](/bible/genesis/2#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/2#v1)</sup> Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/2#v2)</sup> And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/2#v3)</sup> And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.

- This is the first Sabbath that you read about in the scriptures. The Most High blessed the seventh day and sanctified it.

**[Genesis 2:4-6](/bible/genesis/2#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/2#v4)</sup> These are the generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were created, in the day that the Lord God made the earth and the heavens,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/2#v5)</sup> And every plant of the field before it was in the earth, and every herb of the field before it grew: for the Lord God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was not a man to till the ground.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/2#v6)</sup> But there went up a mist from the earth, and watered the whole face of the ground.

- Chapter 2 is the manner of creation. It goes back through what you read in chapter 1 and gives you more detail on certain things.

**[Genesis 2:7](/bible/genesis/2#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/2#v7)</sup> And the Lord God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living soul.

- Here is the detail on how man was formed: the Most High formed him of the dust of the ground.
- The breath of life is talking about the commandments.
- This is Adam, the chosen. Out of all the people the Most High created on the earth, He had a favorite from the beginning.

**[Genesis 2:8-14](/bible/genesis/2#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/2#v8)</sup> And the Lord God planted a garden eastward in Eden; and there he put the man whom he had formed.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/2#v9)</sup> And out of the ground made the Lord God to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/2#v10)</sup> And a river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/2#v11)</sup> The name of the first is Pison: that is it which compasseth the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/2#v12)</sup> And the gold of that land is good: there is bdellium and the onyx stone.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/2#v13)</sup> And the name of the second river is Gihon: the same is it that compasseth the whole land of Ethiopia.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/2#v14)</sup> And the name of the third river is Hiddekel: that is it which goeth toward the east of Assyria. And the fourth river is Euphrates.

- The Most High planted the garden eastward in Eden and put the chosen man there.
- The river Gihon is the Blue Nile; it compasses the whole land of Ethiopia.

**[Genesis 2:16-17](/bible/genesis/2#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/2#v16)</sup> And the Lord God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/2#v17)</sup> But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.

- Some of these trees are literal trees, and some of these trees represent men.

  Precepts:
  - **[Mark 8:24](/bible/mark/8#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/mark/8#v24)</sup> And he looked up, and said, I see men as trees, walking.

    Scripture uses trees to represent men.

**[Genesis 2:18-20](/bible/genesis/2#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/2#v18)</sup> And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/2#v19)</sup> And out of the ground the Lord God formed every beast of the field, and every fowl of the air; and brought them unto Adam to see what he would call them: and whatsoever Adam called every living creature, that was the name thereof.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/2#v20)</sup> And Adam gave names to all cattle, and to the fowl of the air, and to every beast of the field; but for Adam there was not found an help meet for him.

- The Most High looked at the women on the earth and there was no help meet right for Adam. This again shows there were more people on the earth than Adam alone.

**[Genesis 2:21-23](/bible/genesis/2#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/2#v21)</sup> And the Lord God caused a deep sleep to fall upon Adam, and he slept: and he took one of his ribs, and closed up the flesh instead thereof;
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/2#v22)</sup> And the rib, which the Lord God had taken from man, made he a woman, and brought her unto the man.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/2#v23)</sup> And Adam said, This is now bone of my bones, and flesh of my flesh: she shall be called Woman, because she was taken out of Man.

- The Most High created Eve from Adam because there was no woman on the earth right for him at that time.

# Genesis 3: The Craftiness of Your Enemy

Read the chapter → [Genesis 3](/bible/genesis/3)

**[Genesis 3:1](/bible/genesis/3#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/3#v1)</sup> Now the serpent was more subtil than any beast of the field which the Lord God had made. And he said unto the woman, Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?

- Chapter 3 deals with the craftiness of your enemy.
- This is not a literal snake. Another word for serpent is deceiver; the serpent is Satan. You know it is not a literal serpent because serpents do not speak.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Corinthians 11:14](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/2-corinthians/11#v14)</sup> And no marvel; for Satan himself is transformed into an angel of light.

    Satan transforms himself, which is how the serpent is Satan in disguise.

**[Genesis 3:4-5](/bible/genesis/3#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/3#v4)</sup> And the serpent said unto the woman, Ye shall not surely die:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/3#v5)</sup> For God doth know that in the day ye eat thereof, then your eyes shall be opened, and ye shall be as gods, knowing good and evil.

- Satan taught Eve doctrine. She had been taught that if she broke the commandments of the Most High she would die; the serpent now taught her that she would not die.
- The same doctrine is taught today: that your eyes will be opened and you do not have to keep the commandments and you will not die. If you leave the commandments you are going to die.

**[Genesis 3:6](/bible/genesis/3#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/3#v6)</sup> And when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was pleasant to the eyes, and a tree to be desired to make one wise, she took of the fruit thereof, and did eat, and gave also unto her husband with her; and he did eat.

- Any time scripture speaks of good for food like this, it is talking about good to learn.
- The fruit she ate was the lies. She was enticed to break the commandments of the Most High, then she went and taught her husband the same thing the serpent taught her, and he broke the commandments as well.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 10:13](/bible/hosea/10#v13)**
    > <sup>[13](/bible/hosea/10#v13)</sup> Ye have plowed wickedness, ye have reaped iniquity; ye have eaten the fruit of lies: because thou didst trust in thy way, in the multitude of thy mighty men.

    The fruit of lies is eaten; the fruit Eve ate was the lie she was taught.

**[Genesis 3:7](/bible/genesis/3#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/3#v7)</sup> And the eyes of them both were opened, and they knew that they were naked; and they sewed fig leaves together, and made themselves aprons.

- They were not literally naked; nakedness represents sin. They were in sin.
- Remember this is an illustrated story. The fig leaves and aprons represent them trying to cover their transgressions.

  Precepts:
  - **[Job 31:33](/bible/job/31#v33)**
    > <sup>[33](/bible/job/31#v33)</sup> If I covered my transgressions as Adam, by hiding mine iniquity in my bosom:

    Adam covered his transgressions; the fig leaves are that covering.
  - **[Lamentations 1:8](/bible/lamentations/1#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/lamentations/1#v8)</sup> Jerusalem hath grievously sinned; therefore she is removed: all that honoured her despise her, because they have seen her nakedness: yea, she sigheth, and turneth backward.

    Jerusalem sinned and her nakedness was seen; nakedness is sin.

**[Genesis 3:8](/bible/genesis/3#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/3#v8)</sup> And they heard the voice of the Lord God walking in the garden in the cool of the day: and Adam and his wife hid themselves from the presence of the Lord God amongst the trees of the garden.

- The trees of the garden here are people. Adam and his wife tried to hide their sins among the other people that were in the garden with them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 31:8](/bible/ezekiel/31#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/31#v8)</sup> The cedars in the garden of God could not hide him: the fir trees were not like his boughs, and the chesnut trees were not like his branches; nor any tree in the garden of God was like unto him in his beauty.

    The trees in the garden of God are men among whom a man may seek to hide.

**[Genesis 3:9](/bible/genesis/3#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/3#v9)</sup> And the Lord God called unto Adam, and said unto him, Where art thou?

- The Most High was not looking for Adam as though He could not find him; He was calling Adam to examine himself.
- Adam had started to separate himself from the Most High by covering and hiding his sins, and the Most High already knew what he had done.

**[Genesis 3:12-13](/bible/genesis/3#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/3#v12)</sup> And the man said, The woman whom thou gavest to be with me, she gave me of the tree, and I did eat.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/3#v13)</sup> And the Lord God said unto the woman, What is this that thou hast done? And the woman said, The serpent beguiled me, and I did eat.

- Adam blamed Eve for his sin, and in saying the woman whom thou gavest he blamed the Most High also. Then Eve blamed the serpent; they played the blame game.

**[Genesis 3:14](/bible/genesis/3#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/3#v14)</sup> And the Lord God said unto the serpent, Because thou hast done this, thou art cursed above all cattle, and above every beast of the field; upon thy belly shalt thou go, and dust shalt thou eat all the days of thy life:

- The cattle and the beasts of the field here are the nations.
- Going on the belly and eating dust means the serpent was brought to a very low estate, made like a vagabond upon the earth. Cain became a vagabond upon the earth, and so did Esau.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 49:23](/bible/isaiah/49#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/isaiah/49#v23)</sup> And kings shall be thy nursing fathers, and their queens thy nursing mothers: they shall bow down to thee with their face toward the earth, and lick up the dust of thy feet; and thou shalt know that I am the Lord: for they shall not be ashamed that wait for me.

    The nations lick up the dust, showing the low estate spoken to the serpent.
  - **[Micah 7:16-17](/bible/micah/7#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/micah/7#v16)</sup> The nations shall see and be confounded at all their might: they shall lay their hand upon their mouth, their ears shall be deaf.
    >
    > <sup>[17](/bible/micah/7#v17)</sup> They shall lick the dust like a serpent, they shall move out of their holes like worms of the earth: they shall be afraid of the Lord our God, and shall fear because of thee.

    The nations shall lick the dust like a serpent, proving the cattle and beasts are the nations.
  - **[Ecclesiastes 3:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)**
    > <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v18)</sup> I said in mine heart concerning the estate of the sons of men, that God might manifest them, and that they might see that they themselves are beasts.

    The sons of men are themselves called beasts.

**[Genesis 3:15](/bible/genesis/3#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/3#v15)</sup> And I will put enmity between thee and the woman, and between thy seed and her seed; it shall bruise thy head, and thou shalt bruise his heel.

- This is a prophecy. It is not talking about the serpent and Eve; the woman is the nation of Israel.
- The woman does not plant seed; the man plants seed. The Most High is saying He will put enmity between Satan's seed and Israel's seed, and that enmity remains.
- Christ was to come from the woman. [Revelation 12](/bible/revelation/12) explains this verse.
- Bruising the heel is making Israel stumble. When a serpent strikes at your heel you stumble; Satan makes Israel stumble through evil communication and corrupting good manners.
- It shall bruise thy head is spoken to the serpent: Christ was going to come and destroy the works of Satan.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jeremiah 3:2](/bible/jeremiah/3#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/3#v2)</sup> Lift up thine eyes unto the high places, and see where thou hast not been lien with. In the ways hast thou sat for them, as the Arabian in the wilderness; and thou hast polluted the land with thy whoredoms and with thy wickedness.

    The Most High speaks to Israel as the woman.
  - **[Romans 16:20](/bible/romans/16#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/romans/16#v20)</sup> And the God of peace shall bruise Satan under your feet shortly. The grace of our Lord Jesus Christ be with you. Amen.

    The Most High bruises Satan under the feet of His people; that is the bruising of the head.

**[Genesis 3:16](/bible/genesis/3#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/3#v16)</sup> Unto the woman he said, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception; in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children; and thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee.

- Your desire, your learning, your desire of anything was to be to your husband, and he shall rule over thee.
- This is one of the first commandments given, alongside be fruitful and multiply, the Sabbath, and the commandment not to eat of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil.
- Sisters, if you do not allow your husband to rule over you, it does not matter what else you keep; you are not entering the kingdom without fulfilling this.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 14:34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)</sup> Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.

    The women are commanded to be under obedience, as the law says from the beginning.

**[Genesis 3:17-19](/bible/genesis/3#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/3#v17)</sup> And unto Adam he said, Because thou hast hearkened unto the voice of thy wife, and hast eaten of the tree, of which I commanded thee, saying, Thou shalt not eat of it: cursed is the ground for thy sake; in sorrow shalt thou eat of it all the days of thy life;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/3#v18)</sup> Thorns also and thistles shall it bring forth to thee; and thou shalt eat the herb of the field;
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/3#v19)</sup> In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground; for out of it wast thou taken: for dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return.

- Because Adam hearkened to his wife and broke the commandment, the ground was cursed for his sake; in sorrow and sweat he would eat of it all his days.

**[Genesis 3:21](/bible/genesis/3#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/3#v21)</sup> Unto Adam also and to his wife did the Lord God make coats of skins, and clothed them.

- This is animal sacrifice to cover the sins. This is where the Most High ordained the sacrificial law; this is when they started to sacrifice.
- The first sacrifice is twofold. A literal lamb was slain here to clothe Adam and his wife, and that lamb slain from the foundation of the world represents Christ, slain for our sins.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 61:10](/bible/isaiah/61#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/isaiah/61#v10)</sup> I will greatly rejoice in the Lord, my soul shall be joyful in my God; for he hath clothed me with the garments of salvation, he hath covered me with the robe of righteousness, as a bridegroom decketh himself with ornaments, and as a bride adorneth herself with her jewels.

    The Most High clothes His saints with righteousness, as Adam was clothed to cover sin.
  - **[Leviticus 16:27](/bible/leviticus/16#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/16#v27)</sup> And the bullock for the sin offering, and the goat for the sin offering, whose blood was brought in to make atonement in the holy place, shall one carry forth without the camp; and they shall burn in the fire their skins, and their flesh, and their dung.

    The sin offering yields skins, as the Most High made coats of skins to cover sin.
  - **[Revelation 13:8](/bible/revelation/13#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/13#v8)</sup> And all that dwell upon the earth shall worship him, whose names are not written in the book of life of the Lamb slain from the foundation of the world.

    The Lamb was slain from the foundation of the world; Christ is that Lamb.

**[Genesis 3:22-24](/bible/genesis/3#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/3#v22)</sup> And the Lord God said, Behold, the man is become as one of us, to know good and evil: and now, lest he put forth his hand, and take also of the tree of life, and eat, and live for ever:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/3#v23)</sup> Therefore the Lord God sent him forth from the garden of Eden, to till the ground from whence he was taken.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/3#v24)</sup> So he drove out the man; and he placed at the east of the garden of Eden Cherubims, and a flaming sword which turned every way, to keep the way of the tree of life.

- The man was driven out of the garden to till the ground from whence he was taken, and the way of the tree of life was kept.

# Genesis 4: Cain, Abel, and the Sons of the Wicked

Read the chapter → [Genesis 4](/bible/genesis/4)

**[Genesis 4:1-2](/bible/genesis/4#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/4#v1)</sup> And Adam knew Eve his wife; and she conceived, and bare Cain, and said, I have gotten a man from the Lord.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/4#v2)</sup> And she again bare his brother Abel. And Abel was a keeper of sheep, but Cain was a tiller of the ground.

- Here is the birth of trade and of religion with Cain and Abel: Abel a keeper of sheep, Cain a tiller of the ground.

**[Genesis 4:3-5](/bible/genesis/4#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/4#v3)</sup> And in process of time it came to pass, that Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/4#v4)</sup> And Abel, he also brought of the firstlings of his flock and of the fat thereof. And the Lord had respect unto Abel and to his offering:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/4#v5)</sup> But unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect. And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell.

- The Most High had respect to Abel's offering and not to Cain's, and Cain was wroth.
- Remember the earth was cursed in chapter 3, so it was hard to get fruit out of the ground; that is part of why Cain was angry.

**[Genesis 4:7](/bible/genesis/4#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/4#v7)</sup> If thou doest well, shalt thou not be accepted? and if thou doest not well, sin lieth at the door. And unto thee shall be his desire, and thou shalt rule over him.

- The Most High is calling Cain to repent. Cain knew that the Most High required animal sacrifice, because the sacrificial law was ordained at [Genesis 3:21](/bible/genesis/3#v21).
- If Cain would not repent, sin lay at the door; he would become subject to Satan and Satan would rule over him.

**[Genesis 4:8-9](/bible/genesis/4#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/4#v8)</sup> And Cain talked with Abel his brother: and it came to pass, when they were in the field, that Cain rose up against Abel his brother, and slew him.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/4#v9)</sup> And the Lord said unto Cain, Where is Abel thy brother? And he said, I know not: Am I my brother’s keeper?

- When Cain talked with Abel, Abel was trying to get Cain to repent. Abel was a prophet.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 23:35](/bible/matthew/23#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/23#v35)</sup> That upon you may come all the righteous blood shed upon the earth, from the blood of righteous Abel unto the blood of Zacharias son of Barachias, whom ye slew between the temple and the altar.

    Christ counts righteous Abel with the blood of the prophets, proving Abel was a prophet.

**[Genesis 4:11-12](/bible/genesis/4#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/4#v11)</sup> And now art thou cursed from the earth, which hath opened her mouth to receive thy brother’s blood from thy hand;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/4#v12)</sup> When thou tillest the ground, it shall not henceforth yield unto thee her strength; a fugitive and a vagabond shalt thou be in the earth.

- Cain was cursed from the earth and made a fugitive and a vagabond, the same low estate spoken of the serpent in chapter 3.

**[Genesis 4:14-15](/bible/genesis/4#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/4#v14)</sup> Behold, thou hast driven me out this day from the face of the earth; and from thy face shall I be hid; and I shall be a fugitive and a vagabond in the earth; and it shall come to pass, that every one that findeth me shall slay me.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/4#v15)</sup> And the Lord said unto him, Therefore whosoever slayeth Cain, vengeance shall be taken on him sevenfold. And the Lord set a mark upon Cain, lest any finding him should kill him.

- Every one that findeth me shall slay me is proof in the pudding, like we brought out in [Genesis 1](/bible/genesis/1), that there were more people on the earth than Adam's household.
- The mark set upon Cain changed him; at the beginning Cain looked like everybody else, and the Most High marked him so that any finding him would not kill him.

**[Genesis 4:16-17](/bible/genesis/4#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/4#v16)</sup> And Cain went out from the presence of the Lord, and dwelt in the land of Nod, on the east of Eden.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/4#v17)</sup> And Cain knew his wife; and she conceived, and bare Enoch: and he builded a city, and called the name of the city, after the name of his son, Enoch.

- Cain found his wife in the land of Nod; there were already people on the earth during the time of Adam.
- Cain built a city, and you do not build a city for three people; this is more proof that the earth was peopled.
- The lands in those days were named after men, which is why the land was called Nod.
- Cain called the city after the name of his son, as the wicked call their lands after their own names.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 49:11](/bible/psalms/49#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/psalms/49#v11)</sup> Their inward thought is, that their houses shall continue for ever, and their dwelling places to all generations; they call their lands after their own names.

    The wicked call their lands after their own names, as Cain named the city after Enoch.

**[Genesis 4:19](/bible/genesis/4#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/4#v19)</sup> And Lamech took unto him two wives: the name of the one was Adah, and the name of the other Zillah.

- This is the first time you read about a man taking multiple wives, and it is the sons of the wicked who did it. In the beginning it was one wife.
- When you read about the sons of God in [Genesis 5](/bible/genesis/5) you do not read about them taking multiple wives; only the sons of the wicked.

**[Genesis 4:22](/bible/genesis/4#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/4#v22)</sup> And Zillah, she also bare Tubal–cain, an instructer of every artificer in brass and iron: and the sister of Tubal–cain was Naamah.

- An instructor of every artificer in brass and iron: these men started making weapons. This is the first you read about weapons being created.

**[Genesis 4:23-24](/bible/genesis/4#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/4#v23)</sup> And Lamech said unto his wives, Adah and Zillah, Hear my voice; ye wives of Lamech, hearken unto my speech: for I have slain a man to my wounding, and a young man to my hurt.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/4#v24)</sup> If Cain shall be avenged sevenfold, truly Lamech seventy and sevenfold.

- Lamech was murdering men; Satan was working with the sons of the wicked.
- Lamech knew the commandment thou shalt not kill was in force in his day; that is why he appealed to Cain's sevenfold, saying seventy and sevenfold. They knew the commandments during that time.

**[Genesis 4:25-26](/bible/genesis/4#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/4#v25)</sup> And Adam knew his wife again; and she bare a son, and called his name Seth: For God, said she, hath appointed me another seed instead of Abel, whom Cain slew.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/4#v26)</sup> And to Seth, to him also there was born a son; and he called his name Enos: then began men to call upon the name of the Lord.

- Seth was appointed another seed instead of Abel. Through Seth men began to call upon the name of the Lord; they started to worship the Most High and keep the commandments, while the line of Cain went on in evil and wickedness. That is why the Most High chose the line of Seth.
