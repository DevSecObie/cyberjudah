## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 9](/bible/genesis/9) through 12, read with precepts as the chapters go. The laws you meet in these chapters stand from the beginning, long before Moses.

**[Genesis 9:1](/bible/genesis/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/9#v1)</sup> And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth.

- Noah and his sons were told to replenish the earth after the flood, and the earth is replenished with righteous children; a multitude of ungodly children profits nothing.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 16:1-3](/bible/sirach/16#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/16#v1)</sup> Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/16#v2)</sup> Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/16#v3)</sup> Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly.

    One just child is better than a thousand ungodly; replenish the earth with righteous seed.

**[Genesis 9:2](/bible/genesis/9#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/9#v2)</sup> And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth, and upon every fowl of the air, upon all that moveth upon the earth, and upon all the fishes of the sea; into your hand are they delivered.

- The fear and dread of man upon the beasts is not how it was in the beginning, and not how it will remain; the original relationship between man and the animals will be restored.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 11:6-9](/bible/isaiah/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/11#v6)</sup> The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid; and the calf and the young lion and the fatling together; and a little child shall lead them.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/11#v7)</sup> And the cow and the bear shall feed; their young ones shall lie down together: and the lion shall eat straw like the ox.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/11#v8)</sup> And the sucking child shall play on the hole of the asp, and the weaned child shall put his hand on the cockatrice’ den.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/11#v9)</sup> They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain: for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea.

    The wolf dwells with the lamb and a little child leads them; the animals return to their original state when things are set back in order.

**[Genesis 9:3](/bible/genesis/9#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/9#v3)</sup> Every moving thing that liveth shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things.

- This is the introduction of man eating meat. The Most High did not give man a new stomach; the body was already made for it and it lay dormant until after the flood.
- Having the liberty to eat meat does not mean eating without balance; be not too greedy upon meats, for excess of meats brings sickness.
- The law of what may be eaten was already understood; you could not eat everything. The doctrine that says you can pray over anything and eat it is a seducing spirit and a doctrine of devils.
- Fish is meat; all flesh is not the same flesh, but the flesh of beasts, of fishes, and of birds are all flesh. Every creature of God is good for what it was made for, and it is sanctified by the word of God, and the word that sanctifies what may be eaten is the dietary law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 37:27-31](/bible/sirach/37#v27)**
    > <sup>[27](/bible/sirach/37#v27)</sup> My son, prove thy soul in thy life, and see what is evil for it, and give not that unto it.
    >
    > <sup>[28](/bible/sirach/37#v28)</sup> For all things are not profitable for all men, neither hath every soul pleasure in every thing.
    >
    > <sup>[29](/bible/sirach/37#v29)</sup> Be not unsatiable in any dainty thing, nor too greedy upon meats:
    >
    > <sup>[30](/bible/sirach/37#v30)</sup> For excess of meats bringeth sickness, and surfeiting will turn into choler.
    >
    > <sup>[31](/bible/sirach/37#v31)</sup> By surfeiting have many perished; but he that taketh heed prolongeth his life.

    Be not unsatiable in any dainty thing nor too greedy upon meats; excess of meats bringeth sickness.
  - **[Leviticus 11:46-47](/bible/leviticus/11#v46)**
    > <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/11#v46)</sup> This is the law of the beasts, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/11#v47)</sup> To make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.

    The law of the beasts makes a difference between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.
  - **[1 Timothy 4:1-5](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)</sup> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/4#v2)</sup> Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron;
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/4#v3)</sup> Forbidding to marry, and commanding to abstain from meats, which God hath created to be received with thanksgiving of them which believe and know the truth.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/4#v4)</sup> For every creature of God is good, and nothing to be refused, if it be received with thanksgiving:
    >

  - **[1 Corinthians 15:38-39](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v38)**
    > <sup>[38](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v38)</sup> But God giveth it a body as it hath pleased him, and to every seed his own body.
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v39)</sup> All flesh is not the same flesh: but there is one kind of flesh of men, another flesh of beasts, another of fishes, and another of birds.

    All flesh is not the same flesh; there is flesh of beasts, of fishes, and of birds, so fish is meat.

**[Genesis 9:4](/bible/genesis/9#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/9#v4)</sup> But flesh with the life thereof, which is the blood thereof, shall ye not eat.

- The law against eating flesh with the blood was given to Adam and is still in place with Noah; the word is true from the beginning.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 119:160](/bible/psalms/119#v160)**
    > <sup>[160](/bible/psalms/119#v160)</sup> Thy word is true from the beginning: and every one of thy righteous judgments endureth for ever.

    The word is true from the beginning, and the righteous judgments, the laws, endure for ever.
  - **[2 Esdras 3:4-8](/bible/2-esdras/3#v4)**
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-esdras/3#v4)</sup> O Lord, who bearest rule, thou spakest at the beginning, when thou didst plant the earth, and that thyself alone, and commandedst the people,

    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/3#v5)</sup> And gavest a body unto Adam without soul, which was the workmanship of thine hands, and didst breathe into him the breath of life, and he was made living before thee.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-esdras/3#v6)</sup> And thou leadest him into paradise, which thy right hand had planted, before ever the earth came forward.
    >

**[Genesis 9:5](/bible/genesis/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/9#v5)</sup> And surely your blood of your lives will I require; at the hand of every beast will I require it, and at the hand of man; at the hand of every man’s brother will I require the life of man.

- The Most High requiring blood at the hand of every beast is animal sacrifice, still in place with Noah, passed down generation to generation.
- The judgment for sin has always been death. When Eve sinned and caused Adam to sin, either they had to die, or something had to stand in their place; the blood of the animal stood in for them.
- That is the scapegoat: it takes the wrongdoings, mistakes, and faults of others and places them on the animal. That is why Cain and Abel were bringing offerings, because they knew of the sacrifice from [Genesis 3](/bible/genesis/3).

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 3:21](/bible/genesis/3#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/3#v21)</sup> Unto Adam also and to his wife did the Lord God make coats of skins, and clothed them.

    The coats of skins were the first sacrifice; the beast stood in place of Adam and Eve.
  - **[Genesis 4:3-5](/bible/genesis/4#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/4#v3)</sup> And in process of time it came to pass, that Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the Lord.
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/4#v4)</sup> And Abel, he also brought of the firstlings of his flock and of the fat thereof. And the Lord had respect unto Abel and to his offering:
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/4#v5)</sup> But unto Cain and to his offering he had not respect. And Cain was very wroth, and his countenance fell.

    Cain and Abel brought offerings, proving the sacrifice was known and passed down.
  - **[Leviticus 16:8-10](/bible/leviticus/16#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/16#v8)</sup> And Aaron shall cast lots upon the two goats; one lot for the Lord, and the other lot for the scapegoat.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/16#v9)</sup> And Aaron shall bring the goat upon which the Lord’s lot fell, and offer him for a sin offering.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/16#v10)</sup> But the goat, on which the lot fell to be the scapegoat, shall be presented alive before the Lord, to make an atonement with him, and to let him go for a scapegoat into the wilderness.

    The scapegoat is presented alive to make an atonement, standing in for the faults of others.

**[Genesis 9:6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)</sup> Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man.

- Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed: life for life. This law is way before Moses, so it cannot be dismissed as the law of Moses.
- The laws were never done away with; Christ came not to destroy the law but to fulfil, and not one jot or tittle passes from the law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:17-18](/bible/matthew/5#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/5#v17)</sup> Think not that I am come to destroy the law, or the prophets: I am not come to destroy, but to fulfil.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/5#v18)</sup> For verily I say unto you, Till heaven and earth pass, one jot or one tittle shall in no wise pass from the law, till all be fulfilled.

    Christ came not to destroy the law or the prophets; not one jot or one tittle shall pass from the law.

**[Genesis 9:9-11](/bible/genesis/9#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/9#v9)</sup> And I, behold, I establish my covenant with you, and with your seed after you;
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/9#v10)</sup> And with every living creature that is with you, of the fowl, of the cattle, and of every beast of the earth with you; from all that go out of the ark, to every beast of the earth.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/9#v11)</sup> And I will establish my covenant with you; neither shall all flesh be cut off any more by the waters of a flood; neither shall there any more be a flood to destroy the earth.

- The covenant established here is with Noah, his seed, and every living creature: all flesh shall never again be cut off by the waters of a flood.
- This is a general covenant with all flesh. The covenant with Israel is found in [Jeremiah 31](/bible/jeremiah/31).

**[Genesis 9:12-13](/bible/genesis/9#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/9#v12)</sup> And God said, This is the token of the covenant which I make between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for perpetual generations:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/9#v13)</sup> I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.

- The bow in the cloud, the rainbow, is the token of the covenant between the Most High and the earth; it compasses the heaven about with a glorious circle, and His hands bended it.
- The world has taken that same bow, the token of the covenant, and turned it into the symbol of sodomy. The spiritual eyes will see what that means: the great city is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 43:11-12](/bible/sirach/43#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/43#v11)</sup> Look upon the rainbow, and praise him that made it; very beautiful it is in the brightness thereof.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/sirach/43#v12)</sup> It compasseth the heaven about with a glorious circle, and the hands of the most High have bended it.

    Look upon the rainbow and praise Him that made it; the hands of the Most High bended it.
  - **[Revelation 11:8](/bible/revelation/11#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/revelation/11#v8)</sup> And their dead bodies shall lie in the street of the great city, which spiritually is called Sodom and Egypt, where also our Lord was crucified.

    The great city is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt; the place that flies the bow as a symbol of sodomy identifies itself.

**[Genesis 9:14-15](/bible/genesis/9#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/9#v14)</sup> And it shall come to pass, when I bring a cloud over the earth, that the bow shall be seen in the cloud:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/9#v15)</sup> And I will remember my covenant, which is between me and you and every living creature of all flesh; and the waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh.

- The waters shall no more become a flood to destroy all flesh. The next time the destruction is not water but fire, when the day of the Lord comes and the elements melt with fervent heat.

    The Lord is longsuffering, not willing that any should perish; but the day of the Lord will come, and the elements shall melt with fervent heat.

**[Genesis 9:18-19](/bible/genesis/9#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/9#v18)</sup> And the sons of Noah, that went forth of the ark, were Shem, and Ham, and Japheth: and Ham is the father of Canaan.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/9#v19)</sup> These are the three sons of Noah: and of them was the whole earth overspread.

- Shem, Ham, and Japheth went forth out of the ark and the whole earth was overspread from them. Ham dwelt in what is called Africa; Shem in the land mass toward Arabia; Japheth went into Europe.
- Ham is the father of Canaan, and Canaan dwelt in the land later called the land of Israel. That is how you know the land of Israel is a part of Africa; the maps separate Israel from Africa so that the connection is not made.

**[Genesis 9:20-23](/bible/genesis/9#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/9#v20)</sup> And Noah began to be an husbandman, and he planted a vineyard:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/9#v21)</sup> And he drank of the wine, and was drunken; and he was uncovered within his tent.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/9#v22)</sup> And Ham, the father of Canaan, saw the nakedness of his father, and told his two brethren without.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/9#v23)</sup> And Shem and Japheth took a garment, and laid it upon both their shoulders, and went backward, and covered the nakedness of their father; and their faces were backward, and they saw not their father’s nakedness.

- Noah was still perfect and righteous. A just man falleth seven times and riseth up again; one fall does not undo righteousness.
- Understand Noah's heart: he preached repentance for around a hundred years, and everyone he knew outside his house drowned. Strong drink is for him that is ready to perish and wine for those of heavy hearts; Noah drank too much, but he remained righteous in the eyes of the Most High.
- Ham saw the nakedness of his father and did not cover him; Shem and Japheth went backward and covered their father without looking. The commandments concerning nakedness were being applied; [Leviticus 18](/bible/leviticus/18) opens those laws.

**[Genesis 9:24-27](/bible/genesis/9#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/9#v24)</sup> And Noah awoke from his wine, and knew what his younger son had done unto him.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/9#v25)</sup> And he said, Cursed be Canaan; a servant of servants shall he be unto his brethren.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/9#v26)</sup> And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of Shem; and Canaan shall be his servant.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/9#v27)</sup> God shall enlarge Japheth, and he shall dwell in the tents of Shem; and Canaan shall be his servant.

- Noah was asleep, so how did he know what his younger son had done? The Most High speaks to man in a dream, in a vision of the night, and reveals His secret unto His servants the prophets; Noah was a preacher of righteousness, and the Lord told him.
- Cursed be Canaan: a servant of servants unto his brethren.
- Blessed be the Lord God of Shem: it was revealed that the Messiah and the righteous seed would come through Shem.
