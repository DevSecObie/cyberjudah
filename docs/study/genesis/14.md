**[Genesis 14:1-3](/bible/genesis/14#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/14#v1)</sup> And it came to pass in the days of Amraphel king of Shinar, Arioch king of Ellasar, Chedorlaomer king of Elam, and Tidal king of nations;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/14#v2)</sup> That these made war with Bera king of Sodom, and with Birsha king of Gomorrah, Shinab king of Admah, and Shemeber king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela, which is Zoar.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/14#v3)</sup> All these were joined together in the vale of Siddim, which is the salt sea.

- Four kings, the Mesopotamian kings, made an allegiance against five kings; this was a world war, nations and kingdoms making alliances to war against other nations. Shinar is modern day Iraq.
- The king of Bela is the one king whose name is not given; Bela is the city, which is Zoar. The vale of Siddim is the salt sea, known today as the Dead Sea.

**[Genesis 14:4-11](/bible/genesis/14#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/14#v4)</sup> Twelve years they served Chedorlaomer, and in the thirteenth year they rebelled.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/14#v5)</sup> And in the fourteenth year came Chedorlaomer, and the kings that were with him, and smote the Rephaims in Ashteroth Karnaim, and the Zuzims in Ham, and the Emims in Shaveh Kiriathaim,
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/14#v6)</sup> And the Horites in their mount Seir, unto El–paran, which is by the wilderness.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/14#v7)</sup> And they returned, and came to En–mishpat, which is Kadesh, and smote all the country of the Amalekites, and also the Amorites, that dwelt in Hazezon–tamar.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/14#v8)</sup> And there went out the king of Sodom, and the king of Gomorrah, and the king of Admah, and the king of Zeboiim, and the king of Bela (the same is Zoar;) and they joined battle with them in the vale of Siddim;
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/14#v9)</sup> With Chedorlaomer the king of Elam, and with Tidal king of nations, and Amraphel king of Shinar, and Arioch king of Ellasar; four kings with five.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/14#v10)</sup> And the vale of Siddim was full of slimepits; and the kings of Sodom and Gomorrah fled, and fell there; and they that remained fled to the mountain.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/14#v11)</sup> And they took all the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah, and all their victuals, and went their way.

- The five kings served Chedorlaomer twelve years and rebelled in the thirteenth; the four kings defeated them and took the goods of Sodom and Gomorrah.
- The vale of Siddim was full of slimepits: bitumen, tar, petroleum. The same region is rich in oil to this day.

**[Genesis 14:12-16](/bible/genesis/14#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/14#v12)</sup> And they took Lot, Abram’s brother’s son, who dwelt in Sodom, and his goods, and departed.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/14#v13)</sup> And there came one that had escaped, and told Abram the Hebrew; for he dwelt in the plain of Mamre the Amorite, brother of Eshcol, and brother of Aner: and these were confederate with Abram.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/14#v14)</sup> And when Abram heard that his brother was taken captive, he armed his trained servants, born in his own house, three hundred and eighteen, and pursued them unto Dan.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/14#v15)</sup> And he divided himself against them, he and his servants, by night, and smote them, and pursued them unto Hobah, which is on the left hand of Damascus.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/14#v16)</sup> And he brought back all the goods, and also brought again his brother Lot, and his goods, and the women also, and the people.

- Because Lot dwelt in Sodom he was carried away captive with his goods, and one that escaped told Abram the Hebrew.
- Abram armed his trained servants, three hundred and eighteen, born in his own house. That tells you Abram trained them: he was a warrior fit for battle who knew how to conduct war, nothing like the frail figure in the whitewashed images.
- He divided his men and moved by night; that is military strategy. With three hundred and eighteen men and his three confederates he smote four kingdoms and brought back Lot, the goods, and the people.
- The victory of battle stands not in the multitude of an host; strength comes from heaven. Whether with many or with them that have no power, the battle is not yours but God's; our forefathers proved it, as when Asa cried to the Lord and a million Ethiopians fell before Judah and Benjamin.

**[Genesis 14:18-20](/bible/genesis/14#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/14#v18)</sup> And Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine: and he was the priest of the most high God.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/14#v19)</sup> And he blessed him, and said, Blessed be Abram of the most high God, possessor of heaven and earth:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/14#v20)</sup> And blessed be the most high God, which hath delivered thine enemies into thy hand. And he gave him tithes of all.

- Melchizedek king of Salem brought forth bread and wine; Salem goes into Shalom, peace, so he is the king of peace, and he was the priest of the Most High God.
- Abram paid tithes to Melchizedek. This is way before the Levitical priesthood; the twelve tribes were not yet born. For the order of Melchisedec, study [Hebrews 7](/bible/hebrews/7) and [John 8:56](/bible/john/8#v56).

**[Genesis 14:21-24](/bible/genesis/14#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/14#v21)</sup> And the king of Sodom said unto Abram, Give me the persons, and take the goods to thyself.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/14#v22)</sup> And Abram said to the king of Sodom, I have lift up mine hand unto the Lord, the most high God, the possessor of heaven and earth,
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/14#v23)</sup> That I will not take from a thread even to a shoelatchet, and that I will not take any thing that is thine, lest thou shouldest say, I have made Abram rich:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/14#v24)</sup> Save only that which the young men have eaten, and the portion of the men which went with me, Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre; let them take their portion.

- The king of Sodom asked only for the persons and offered Abram all the goods, and Abram refused from a thread even to a shoelatchet, lest the king of Sodom say, I have made Abram rich. He would not give him that satisfaction.
- The young men's portion was not refused: Aner, Eshcol, and Mamre fought, and they earned their portion.
