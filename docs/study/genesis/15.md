**[Genesis 15:1-4](/bible/genesis/15#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/15#v1)</sup> After these things the word of the Lord came unto Abram in a vision, saying, Fear not, Abram: I am thy shield, and thy exceeding great reward.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/15#v2)</sup> And Abram said, Lord God, what wilt thou give me, seeing I go childless, and the steward of my house is this Eliezer of Damascus?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/15#v3)</sup> And Abram said, Behold, to me thou hast given no seed: and, lo, one born in my house is mine heir.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/15#v4)</sup> And, behold, the word of the Lord came unto him, saying, This shall not be thine heir; but he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir.

- Abram asked what the Lord would give him, seeing he went childless and the steward of his house, Eliezer of Damascus, stood to inherit everything.
- The word came: This shall not be thine heir; he that shall come forth out of thine own bowels shall be thine heir. The Most High prophesied children to Abram out of his own seed.

**[Genesis 15:5-6](/bible/genesis/15#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/15#v5)</sup> And he brought him forth abroad, and said, Look now toward heaven, and tell the stars, if thou be able to number them: and he said unto him, So shall thy seed be.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/15#v6)</sup> And he believed in the Lord; and he counted it to him for righteousness.

- Look now toward heaven and tell the stars: so shall thy seed be. Abram believed the Lord, and it was counted to him for righteousness.
- Righteousness is immortal, and righteousness is defined by the law: it shall be our righteousness if we observe to do all these commandments. Abram's faith showed itself in his works.

**[Genesis 15:9-12](/bible/genesis/15#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/15#v9)</sup> And he said unto him, Take me an heifer of three years old, and a she goat of three years old, and a ram of three years old, and a turtledove, and a young pigeon.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/15#v10)</sup> And he took unto him all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another: but the birds divided he not.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/15#v11)</sup> And when the fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/15#v12)</sup> And when the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, an horror of great darkness fell upon him.

- Abram divided the heifer, the goat, and the ram, and drove the fowls from the carcases; he was offering sacrifice.
- When the sun went down a deep sleep and a horror of great darkness fell on Abram: he was about to receive a vision of future prophecy. The Most High speaks in a dream, in a vision of the night, and reveals His secret to His servants the prophets.

    In a dream, in a vision of the night, He openeth the ears of men and sealeth their instruction.
  - **[Amos 3:7](/bible/amos/3#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/amos/3#v7)</sup> Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.

    The Lord God revealeth His secret unto His servants the prophets.

**[Genesis 15:13-16](/bible/genesis/15#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/15#v13)</sup> And he said unto Abram, Know of a surety that thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them; and they shall afflict them four hundred years;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/15#v14)</sup> And also that nation, whom they shall serve, will I judge: and afterward shall they come out with great substance.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/15#v15)</sup> And thou shalt go to thy fathers in peace; thou shalt be buried in a good old age.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/15#v16)</sup> But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again: for the iniquity of the Amorites is not yet full.

- Know of a surety: thy seed shall be a stranger in a land that is not theirs, shall serve them, and be afflicted four hundred years. This prophecy was fulfilled in Egypt; the sojourning of the children of Israel in Egypt was four hundred and thirty years, and the four hundred was an approximation of that time.
- This verse is not the doctrine about America and 1619. The northern kingdom's captivity on this side began in 1492, and by that counting the years pass four hundred and Israel is still in captivity and still scattered this day; the prophecy of [Genesis 15:13](/bible/genesis/15#v13) was fulfilled at the Exodus.
- They came out with great substance: the children of Israel required jewels of silver and gold and raiment of the Egyptians, and they spoiled Egypt as they left.
- The twelve tribes scattered among the nations today, the people called Blacks, Hispanics, and Native Americans, are the seed this covenant history belongs to.
- Abram himself would go to his fathers in peace and be buried in a good old age; and the seed would return in the fourth generation, for the iniquity of the Amorites was not yet full.
