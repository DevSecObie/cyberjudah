**[Genesis 50:1-3](/bible/genesis/50#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/50#v1)</sup> And Joseph fell upon his father’s face, and wept upon him, and kissed him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/50#v2)</sup> And Joseph commanded his servants the physicians to embalm his father: and the physicians embalmed Israel.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/50#v3)</sup> And forty days were fulfilled for him; for so are fulfilled the days of those which are embalmed: and the Egyptians mourned for him threescore and ten days.

- Joseph fell upon his father's face, and wept upon him, and kissed him, and commanded the physicians to embalm his father; forty days were fulfilled for the embalming, and the Egyptians mourned for Israel threescore and ten days.

**[Genesis 50:4-6](/bible/genesis/50#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/50#v4)</sup> And when the days of his mourning were past, Joseph spake unto the house of Pharaoh, saying, If now I have found grace in your eyes, speak, I pray you, in the ears of Pharaoh, saying,
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/50#v5)</sup> My father made me swear, saying, Lo, I die: in my grave which I have digged for me in the land of Canaan, there shalt thou bury me. Now therefore let me go up, I pray thee, and bury my father, and I will come again.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/50#v6)</sup> And Pharaoh said, Go up, and bury thy father, according as he made thee swear.

- Joseph asked leave of Pharaoh according to the oath his father made him swear, to bury him in the grave he had digged in the land of Canaan; and Pharaoh said, Go up, and bury thy father, according as he made thee swear.

**[Genesis 50:7-11](/bible/genesis/50#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/50#v7)</sup> And Joseph went up to bury his father: and with him went up all the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt,
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/50#v8)</sup> And all the house of Joseph, and his brethren, and his father’s house: only their little ones, and their flocks, and their herds, they left in the land of Goshen.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/50#v9)</sup> And there went up with him both chariots and horsemen: and it was a very great company.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/50#v10)</sup> And they came to the threshingfloor of Atad, which is beyond Jordan, and there they mourned with a great and very sore lamentation: and he made a mourning for his father seven days.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/50#v11)</sup> And when the inhabitants of the land, the Canaanites, saw the mourning in the floor of Atad, they said, This is a grievous mourning to the Egyptians: wherefore the name of it was called Abel–mizraim, which is beyond Jordan.

- All the servants of Pharaoh, the elders of his house, and all the elders of the land of Egypt went up with Joseph and his brethren and his father's house, with chariots and horsemen, a very great company.
- The Canaanites saw the mourning in the floor of Atad and said, This is a grievous mourning to the Egyptians; wherefore the place was called Abelmizraim, which means the mourning of the Egyptians, beyond Jordan.
- The Israelites and the Egyptians were compared as looking like one people, because the Egyptians are black and the Israelites are black. The Bible dictionary defines Ham as the progenitor of the dark races, and Egypt was of Ham; the people sitting in that land today are not the true ancient Egyptians of the scriptures.

**[Genesis 50:12-14](/bible/genesis/50#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/50#v12)</sup> And his sons did unto him according as he commanded them:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/50#v13)</sup> For his sons carried him into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah, which Abraham bought with the field for a possession of a buryingplace of Ephron the Hittite, before Mamre.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/50#v14)</sup> And Joseph returned into Egypt, he, and his brethren, and all that went up with him to bury his father, after he had buried his father.

- His sons did unto him according as he commanded them, and carried him into the land of Canaan, and buried him in the cave of the field of Machpelah before Mamre; and Joseph returned into Egypt, he and his brethren, and all that went up with him.

**[Genesis 50:15-21](/bible/genesis/50#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/50#v15)</sup> And when Joseph’s brethren saw that their father was dead, they said, Joseph will peradventure hate us, and will certainly requite us all the evil which we did unto him.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/50#v16)</sup> And they sent a messenger unto Joseph, saying, Thy father did command before he died, saying,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/50#v17)</sup> So shall ye say unto Joseph, Forgive, I pray thee now, the trespass of thy brethren, and their sin; for they did unto thee evil: and now, we pray thee, forgive the trespass of the servants of the God of thy father. And Joseph wept when they spake unto him.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/50#v18)</sup> And his brethren also went and fell down before his face; and they said, Behold, we be thy servants.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/50#v19)</sup> And Joseph said unto them, Fear not: for am I in the place of God?
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/50#v20)</sup> But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/50#v21)</sup> Now therefore fear ye not: I will nourish you, and your little ones. And he comforted them, and spake kindly unto them.

- When Joseph's brethren saw that their father was dead, they said, Joseph will peradventure hate us, and will certainly requite us all the evil which we did unto him; for they had sold him as a slave into Egypt. They sent a messenger pleading forgiveness and fell down before his face: Behold, we be thy servants.
- Joseph wept and answered, Fear not: for am I in the place of God? But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive. And he comforted them and spake kindly unto them, and nourished them and their little ones.
- Joseph had a real forgiving spirit. The lesson is to always be forgiving: sometimes people do evil against you, but the Most High means it unto good for you, so keep that spirit of forgiveness when people do certain things.

**[Genesis 50:22-26](/bible/genesis/50#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/50#v22)</sup> And Joseph dwelt in Egypt, he, and his father’s house: and Joseph lived an hundred and ten years.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/50#v23)</sup> And Joseph saw Ephraim’s children of the third generation: the children also of Machir the son of Manasseh were brought up upon Joseph’s knees.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/50#v24)</sup> And Joseph said unto his brethren, I die: and God will surely visit you, and bring you out of this land unto the land which he sware to Abraham, to Isaac, and to Jacob.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/50#v25)</sup> And Joseph took an oath of the children of Israel, saying, God will surely visit you, and ye shall carry up my bones from hence.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/50#v26)</sup> So Joseph died, being an hundred and ten years old: and they embalmed him, and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.

- Joseph dwelt in Egypt and lived an hundred and ten years, and saw the children of Ephraim of the third generation; the children of Machir the son of Manasseh were brought up upon Joseph's knees.
- Joseph took an oath of the children of Israel: God will surely visit you, and ye shall carry up my bones from hence. So Joseph died, and they embalmed him, and he was put in a coffin in Egypt.
- That is why no Egyptologist can show you the mummy of Joseph, the tomb of so famous a man: when Israel fled out of the land of Egypt they took the bones of Joseph with them, according to that oath.
