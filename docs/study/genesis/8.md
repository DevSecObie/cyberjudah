**[Genesis 8:2](/bible/genesis/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/8#v2)</sup> The fountains also of the deep and the windows of heaven were stopped, and the rain from heaven was restrained;

- The windows of heaven being stopped and the rain from heaven being restrained shows you that the windows of heaven refer to the rain.

**[Genesis 8:20-22](/bible/genesis/8#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/8#v20)</sup> And Noah builded an altar unto the Lord; and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt offerings on the altar.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/8#v21)</sup> And the Lord smelled a sweet savour; and the Lord said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for man’s sake; for the imagination of man’s heart is evil from his youth; neither will I again smite any more every thing living, as I have done.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/8#v22)</sup> While the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.

- Noah built an altar and offered burnt offerings of every clean beast and every clean fowl, and the Most High smelled a sweet savour and said He would not again curse the ground any more for man's sake.
- An everlasting covenant was made with Noah that all flesh should perish no more by a flood. Before it was water; the next time it is going to be fire, so let us get our minds right.
