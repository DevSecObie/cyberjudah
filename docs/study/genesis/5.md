## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 5](/bible/genesis/5) through 8, read straight through, with the key points opened after the reading. The 365 reading is to give faith through hearing the word of the Most High.

**[Romans 10:17](/bible/romans/10#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/10#v17)</sup> So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

- The daily reading of the scriptures gives faith through hearing the word of the Most High.

**[Genesis 5:1-2](/bible/genesis/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/5#v1)</sup> This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/5#v2)</sup> Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created.

- The words made he him speak of the first Adam. This Adam was made in the likeness of God, a direct descendant of the Most High.
- Male and female created he them, and called their name Adam: they were not that Adam; these were the other people on the earth. When the Most High creates a fish He does not create only a salmon; there is mackerel and tuna and all types of fish. In the same way, He created Adam and He created the other people.
- Chapter 5 is the direct lineage of Adam. That lineage is the sons of God you read of in chapter 6, and the later chapters of Genesis tie the Israelites directly to the lineage of Adam through Shem.
- When Christ said, Ye are gods, He was saying we come from that line of Adam.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 1:26-27](/bible/genesis/1#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/1#v26)</sup> And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/1#v27)</sup> So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.

    The man made in our image, created he him, is the first Adam; male and female created he them is the other people.
  - **[Luke 3:36-38](/bible/luke/3#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/luke/3#v36)</sup> Which was the son of Cainan, which was the son of Arphaxad, which was the son of Sem, which was the son of Noe, which was the son of Lamech,
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/luke/3#v37)</sup> Which was the son of Mathusala, which was the son of Enoch, which was the son of Jared, which was the son of Maleleel, which was the son of Cainan,
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/luke/3#v38)</sup> Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam, which was the son of God.

    Christ's lineage on his mother's side runs back through Seth to Adam, the son of God.

**[Genesis 5:24](/bible/genesis/5#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/5#v24)</sup> And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him.

- Enoch walked with God and was not: the Most High took him up, and Enoch did not taste death, the same way Elijah did not taste death.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Kings 2:9-11](/bible/2-kings/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/2#v9)</sup> And it came to pass, when they were gone over, that Elijah said unto Elisha, Ask what I shall do for thee, before I be taken away from thee. And Elisha said, I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/2#v10)</sup> And he said, Thou hast asked a hard thing: nevertheless, if thou see me when I am taken from thee, it shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/2#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, as they still went on, and talked, that, behold, there appeared a chariot of fire, and horses of fire, and parted them both asunder; and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven.

    Elijah was taken up by a whirlwind into heaven; that is what it means that God took Enoch.
  - **[Acts 1:9](/bible/acts/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/acts/1#v9)</sup> And when he had spoken these things, while they beheld, he was taken up; and a cloud received him out of their sight.

    Christ was taken up and received in a cloud; taken up means received by the Most High.

**[Genesis 5:32](/bible/genesis/5#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/5#v32)</sup> And Noah was five hundred years old: and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

- Noah was five hundred years old here, and he was six hundred when the flood came ([Genesis 7:6](/bible/genesis/7#v6)), so Noah preached for around a hundred years, give or take.
