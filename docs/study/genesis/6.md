**[Genesis 6:1-2](/bible/genesis/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/6#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/6#v2)</sup> That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose.

- The men who began to multiply on the face of the earth were of the other nations; they were not the direct descendants of Adam. The sons of God are the lineage of Adam laid out in chapter 5; that is the distinction between the sons of God and the daughters of men.
- The law against marrying into the other nations began here in Genesis. It was already known, and Moses had to reiterate that same law to the children of Israel when they came over Jordan.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 7:3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them; thy daughter thou shalt not give unto his son, nor his daughter shalt thou take unto thy son.

    Moses reiterated to the sons of God the law against taking wives of the other nations.

**[Genesis 6:3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)</sup> And the Lord said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

- Here the Most High shortened the lifespan of man to a hundred and twenty years. In chapter 5 the forefathers lived seven, eight, and nine hundred years.

**[Genesis 6:4](/bible/genesis/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/6#v4)</sup> There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown.

- The giants in the earth came from the sons of God intermingling with the other nations, and the children born to them became mighty men of renown.
- The angels which kept not their first estate are these same men of [Genesis 6:4](/bible/genesis/6#v4); they came from the lineage of Adam, and their first estate was sons of God. Another word for angel is messenger.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jude 1:6](/bible/jude/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/jude/1#v6)</sup> And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.

    The angels left their first estate as sons of God, and the Most High reserved them unto judgment.

**[Genesis 6:8-9](/bible/genesis/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/6#v8)</sup> But Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/6#v9)</sup> These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.

- Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord; he was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.
- Without Noah none of us would be here; Shem, Ham, and Japheth repopulated the earth after the flood.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 44:17-18](/bible/sirach/44#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/44#v17)</sup> Noah was found perfect and righteous; in the time of wrath he was taken in exchange for the world; therefore was he left as a remnant unto the earth, when the flood came.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/44#v18)</sup> An everlasting covenant was made with him, that all flesh should perish no more by the flood.

    Noah was found perfect and righteous and was left as a remnant unto the earth when the flood came.

**[Genesis 6:14](/bible/genesis/6#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/6#v14)</sup> Make thee an ark of gopher wood; rooms shalt thou make in the ark, and shalt pitch it within and without with pitch.

- The ark was a literal boat, made of gopher wood.

**[Genesis 6:21](/bible/genesis/6#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/6#v21)</sup> And take thou unto thee of all food that is eaten, and thou shalt gather it to thee; and it shall be for food for thee, and for them.

- At this time the animals were vegetarian; that is why the Most High explained to Noah to gather food for himself, for his family, and for the animals, and why the animals did not eat one another on the ark.

**[Genesis 6:22](/bible/genesis/6#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/6#v22)</sup> Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he.

- Noah did all that the Most High commanded him by faith. Up to that time rain had never been seen; it takes faith to build an ark for a thing that has never happened.
- While the ark was preparing, Noah was a preacher of righteousness, telling his people to repent, and the longsuffering of the Most High waited for them to repent.
- Eight souls were saved by water; the ones that were actually in the water died, so this is not talking about getting baptized.
- As Noah prepared the ark to the saving of his house, we must prepare our households for when Christ comes back; that takes the same faith. In the days of Noah they were eating, drinking, and marrying until Noah entered the ark and there was no more chance to repent; so shall the coming of the Son of man be. We have to put on the spirit of Noah.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 11:1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)</sup> Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

    Faith is the evidence of things not seen; with Noah the thing not seen was rain.
  - **[Hebrews 11:6-7](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)</sup> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/11#v7)</sup> By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.

    By faith Noah, warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear and prepared an ark to the saving of his house.
  - **[1 Peter 3:20](/bible/1-peter/3#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/1-peter/3#v20)</sup> Which sometime were disobedient, when once the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing, wherein few, that is, eight souls were saved by water.

    The longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah while the ark was preparing, and eight souls were saved by water.
  - **[2 Peter 2:5](/bible/2-peter/2#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-peter/2#v5)</sup> And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;

    Noah was the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness.
  - **[Matthew 24:37-39](/bible/matthew/24#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/24#v37)</sup> But as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/24#v38)</sup> For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark,
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/24#v39)</sup> And knew not until the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.

    As the days of Noah were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.
