**[Genesis 49:1-2](/bible/genesis/49#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/49#v1)</sup> And Jacob called unto his sons, and said, Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which shall befall you in the last days.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/49#v2)</sup> Gather yourselves together, and hear, ye sons of Jacob; and hearken unto Israel your father.

- Gather yourselves together, that I may tell you that which shall befall you in the last days. These blessings are twofold: they speak to what would come upon each tribe in their day and in the last days, and they help determine who the tribes are and where they would be found today.

**[Genesis 49:3-4](/bible/genesis/49#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/49#v3)</sup> Reuben, thou art my firstborn, my might, and the beginning of my strength, the excellency of dignity, and the excellency of power:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/49#v4)</sup> Unstable as water, thou shalt not excel; because thou wentest up to thy father’s bed; then defiledst thou it: he went up to my couch.

- Reuben is the so-called Seminole Indian today. Reuben, thou art my firstborn, the beginning of my strength: the firstborn is the father's posterity, and the excellency of dignity and the excellency of power belonged to him; the Seminoles were great warriors.
- Unstable as water: the Seminoles were nomads, always moving around, as the scripture said.
- Thou shalt not excel: Reuben would not excel as the firstborn, because he went up to his father's couch. His birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph because he defiled his father's bed, and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Chronicles 5:1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)</sup> Now the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel, (for he was the firstborn; but, forasmuch as he defiled his father’s bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph the son of Israel: and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright.

    Reuben's birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph because he defiled his father's bed.

**[Genesis 49:5-7](/bible/genesis/49#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/49#v5)</sup> Simeon and Levi are brethren; instruments of cruelty are in their habitations.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/49#v6)</sup> O my soul, come not thou into their secret; unto their assembly, mine honour, be not thou united: for in their anger they slew a man, and in their selfwill they digged down a wall.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/49#v7)</sup> Cursed be their anger, for it was fierce; and their wrath, for it was cruel: I will divide them in Jacob, and scatter them in Israel.

- Simeon is the so-called Dominican and Levi the so-called Haitian today. Simeon and Levi are brethren: all the tribes of Israel are brethren, but these two in particular were to dwell together, and they share the same island, Hispaniola.
- Instruments of cruelty are in their habitations: the witchcraft practiced on that island, such as the voodoo of Haiti. O my soul, come not thou into their secret: because of those practices the Most High does not unite them, and the two nations stay divided on the island even though Levi helped free Simeon from slavery.
- For in their anger they slew a man: when their sister was defiled at Shechem, they told all the men of the city to be circumcised, and while the men were sore they slew every male of the city. Simeon and Levi did not play.
- I will divide them in Jacob, and scatter them in Israel: Levi had no whole portion but cities scattered throughout all the tribes; and as Judah was scattered to the four corners of the earth, so was Levi, for Judah, Benjamin, and Levi make up the southern kingdom.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 34:24-25](/bible/genesis/34#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/34#v24)</sup> And unto Hamor and unto Shechem his son hearkened all that went out of the gate of his city; and every male was circumcised, all that went out of the gate of his city.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/34#v25)</sup> And it came to pass on the third day, when they were sore, that two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brethren, took each man his sword, and came upon the city boldly, and slew all the males.

    The men of Shechem were circumcised, and on the third day, when they were sore, Simeon and Levi slew all the males; that is the anger Jacob cursed.

**[Genesis 49:8-12](/bible/genesis/49#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/49#v8)</sup> Judah, thou art he whom thy brethren shall praise: thy hand shall be in the neck of thine enemies; thy father’s children shall bow down before thee.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/49#v9)</sup> Judah is a lion’s whelp: from the prey, my son, thou art gone up: he stooped down, he couched as a lion, and as an old lion; who shall rouse him up?
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/49#v10)</sup> The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come; and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/49#v11)</sup> Binding his foal unto the vine, and his ass’s colt unto the choice vine; he washed his garments in wine, and his clothes in the blood of grapes:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/49#v12)</sup> His eyes shall be red with wine, and his teeth white with milk.

- Judah is the so-called American Black man today. Judah, thou art he whom thy brethren shall praise: Judah was the first to bring out this truth that we are the nation of Israel, and the great majority of the teachers of this truth today are of the kingdom of Judah.
- Thy hand shall be in the neck of thine enemies: Judah sits right in the neck of the enemy's kingdom, right up under Esau here in America, in his politics and his government, always in a key position.
- Thy father's children shall bow down before thee: Judah is the top tribe in the awakening today, for the Lord saves the tents of Judah first.
- Judah is a lion's whelp: from the prey, my son, thou art gone up. The movements that stood up against the enemy in this land, that was Judah rebelling; then he stooped down and couched as an old lion, content, and only the Most High shall rouse him up.
- The sceptre shall not depart from Judah, nor a lawgiver from between his feet, until Shiloh come: Shiloh means the peaceful one, Christ, who comes from the tribe of Judah, and unto him shall the gathering of the people be.
- Binding his foal unto the vine is the prophecy of Christ riding into Jerusalem upon the colt; and he washed his garments in wine and his clothes in the blood of grapes runs ahead to Christ returning from Bozrah with dyed garments, treading the winepress, to destroy Israel's enemies.
- His eyes shall be red with wine: Christ drank wine, so much that men called him a winebibber, but he was never drunken.

  Precepts:
  - **[Zechariah 12:7](/bible/zechariah/12#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/zechariah/12#v7)</sup> The Lord also shall save the tents of Judah first, that the glory of the house of David and the glory of the inhabitants of Jerusalem do not magnify themselves against Judah.

    The Lord shall save the tents of Judah first, that the glory of the house of David do not magnify itself against Judah.
  - **[Zechariah 9:9](/bible/zechariah/9#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/zechariah/9#v9)</sup> Rejoice greatly, O daughter of Zion; shout, O daughter of Jerusalem: behold, thy King cometh unto thee: he is just, and having salvation; lowly, and riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass.

    Thy King cometh riding upon an ass, and upon a colt the foal of an ass; the foal bound unto the vine is that prophecy.
  - **[Isaiah 63:1-3](/bible/isaiah/63#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/63#v1)</sup> Who is this that cometh from Edom, with dyed garments from Bozrah? this that is glorious in his apparel, travelling in the greatness of his strength? I that speak in righteousness, mighty to save.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/63#v2)</sup> Wherefore art thou red in thine apparel, and thy garments like him that treadeth in the winefat?
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/63#v3)</sup> I have trodden the winepress alone; and of the people there was none with me: for I will tread them in mine anger, and trample them in my fury; and their blood shall be sprinkled upon my garments, and I will stain all my raiment.

    He that cometh from Bozrah with dyed garments has trodden the winepress alone; the garments washed in the blood of grapes look ahead to that day.
  - **[Matthew 11:19](/bible/matthew/11#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/11#v19)</sup> The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they say, Behold a man gluttonous, and a winebibber, a friend of publicans and sinners. But wisdom is justified of her children.

    The Son of man came eating and drinking, and they said, Behold a winebibber; yet he never was drunken.

**[Genesis 49:13](/bible/genesis/49#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/49#v13)</sup> Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea; and he shall be for an haven of ships; and his border shall be unto Zidon.

- Zebulun is the so-called Mayan Indians today, from Guatemala down to Panama. Zebulun shall dwell at the haven of the sea, and he shall be for an haven of ships: the Panama Canal sits in his land, built to shorten the trip around the world, and the ships of the earth must come through and dock there.
- His border shall be unto Zidon: as Zebulun dwelt by the sea in ancient Israel, so he dwells by the sea now; it was going to be the same, nothing different.

**[Genesis 49:14-15](/bible/genesis/49#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/49#v14)</sup> Issachar is a strong ass couching down between two burdens:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/49#v15)</sup> And he saw that rest was good, and the land that it was pleasant; and bowed his shoulder to bear, and became a servant unto tribute.

- Issachar is the so-called Aztec Indian, the Mexican of today. Issachar is a strong ass: a hard worker, given over to hard work and known over the earth for labor.
- Couching down between two burdens: the two burdens are North America and Mexico, and the tribe sits pressed between them at the border, under the immigration laws, with even their children detained.
- He saw that rest was good, and the land that it was pleasant: Mexico is a beautiful land.
- He bowed his shoulder to bear, and became a servant unto tribute: hired out for cheap labor, and that labor is used to undercut and compete with Judah in the market.

**[Genesis 49:16-18](/bible/genesis/49#v16)**

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/49#v16)</sup> Dan shall judge his people, as one of the tribes of Israel.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/49#v17)</sup> Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder in the path, that biteth the horse heels, so that his rider shall fall backward.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/49#v18)</sup> I have waited for thy salvation, O Lord.

- Dan shall judge his people, as one of the tribes of Israel. Dan shall be a serpent by the way, an adder in the path, that biteth the horse heels, so that his rider shall fall backward: Dan was like an ambush to the enemies, a powerful tribe that did not play.
- The tribe of Dan was absorbed into the other tribes, and chiefly into Benjamin; that is part of why Benjamin had so many slave revolts, and successful ones.

**[Genesis 49:19](/bible/genesis/49#v19)**

> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/49#v19)</sup> Gad, a troop shall overcome him: but he shall overcome at the last.

- Gad is the so-called North American Indian, the Native American, today. Gad, a troop shall overcome him: the United States cavalry led by Andrew Jackson overcame Gad.
- But he shall overcome at the last: in the last days the tribe of Gad rises back up, as you see it rising now.

**[Genesis 49:20](/bible/genesis/49#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/49#v20)</sup> Out of Asher his bread shall be fat, and he shall yield royal dainties.

- Asher is the so-called Incan Indians of South America, dwelling in the places from Colombia on down. Out of Asher his bread shall be fat, and he shall yield royal dainties: Asher is known for resources, an abundance of natural riches in his land.
- The blessing of Moses says the same of Asher: let him dip his foot in oil.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 33:24](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/33#v24)</sup> And of Asher he said, Let Asher be blessed with children; let him be acceptable to his brethren, and let him dip his foot in oil.

    Let Asher be blessed with children and dip his foot in oil; the fatness of his bread is the abundance of his resources.

**[Genesis 49:21](/bible/genesis/49#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/49#v21)</sup> Naphtali is a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words.

- Naphtali is the so-called Indians of Argentina and Chile today. Naphtali is a hind let loose: he giveth goodly words, and the capital of Argentina carries that mark, for Buenos Aires means good airs, goodly words.

**[Genesis 49:22-26](/bible/genesis/49#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/49#v22)</sup> Joseph is a fruitful bough, even a fruitful bough by a well; whose branches run over the wall:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/49#v23)</sup> The archers have sorely grieved him, and shot at him, and hated him:
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/49#v24)</sup> But his bow abode in strength, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob; (from thence is the shepherd, the stone of Israel:)
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/49#v25)</sup> Even by the God of thy father, who shall help thee; and by the Almighty, who shall bless thee with blessings of heaven above, blessings of the deep that lieth under, blessings of the breasts, and of the womb:
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/49#v26)</sup> The blessings of thy father have prevailed above the blessings of my progenitors unto the utmost bound of the everlasting hills: they shall be on the head of Joseph, and on the crown of the head of him that was separate from his brethren.

- Joseph is a fruitful bough, even a fruitful bough by a well: Joseph, blessed in his sons Ephraim and Manasseh, is the so-called Puerto Ricans and Cubans today, a fruitful people.
- Whose branches run over the wall: the Spaniards built a wall in Puerto Rico to keep themselves separate, but the daughters went over the wall and mixed themselves among the people; interracial marriage ran high, and that is why a great part of them favor Esau today.
- The archers have sorely grieved him, and shot at him, and hated him: the Spanish conquistadors. But his bow abode in strength, and the arms of his hands were made strong by the hands of the mighty God of Jacob.
- The blessings of thy father have prevailed above the blessings of my progenitors: they shall be on the head of Joseph, on the crown of the head of him that was separate from his brethren; these are the blessings Ephraim and Manasseh received in [Genesis 48](/bible/genesis/48).

**[Genesis 49:27](/bible/genesis/49#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/49#v27)</sup> Benjamin shall ravin as a wolf: in the morning he shall devour the prey, and at night he shall divide the spoil.

- Benjamin is the so-called Jamaican today. Benjamin shall ravin as a wolf: loud when he communicates, quick to anger, fierce; in the morning he shall devour the prey, and at night he shall divide the spoil.
- With Dan absorbed into him, Benjamin was dangerous in captivity; his slave revolts were plenty, and they were successful.

**[Genesis 49:28-33](/bible/genesis/49#v28)**

> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/49#v28)</sup> All these are the twelve tribes of Israel: and this is it that their father spake unto them, and blessed them; every one according to his blessing he blessed them.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/49#v29)</sup> And he charged them, and said unto them, I am to be gathered unto my people: bury me with my fathers in the cave that is in the field of Ephron the Hittite,
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/49#v30)</sup> In the cave that is in the field of Machpelah, which is before Mamre, in the land of Canaan, which Abraham bought with the field of Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a buryingplace.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/49#v31)</sup> There they buried Abraham and Sarah his wife; there they buried Isaac and Rebekah his wife; and there I buried Leah.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/49#v32)</sup> The purchase of the field and of the cave that is therein was from the children of Heth.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/genesis/49#v33)</sup> And when Jacob had made an end of commanding his sons, he gathered up his feet into the bed, and yielded up the ghost, and was gathered unto his people.

- All these are the twelve tribes of Israel, and their father blessed them, every one according to his blessing. The blessings tell who the tribes are and where they would be found in the last days.
- Jacob charged them to bury him with his fathers in the cave of the field of Machpelah, which Abraham bought of Ephron the Hittite for a possession of a buryingplace, where Abraham and Sarah, Isaac and Rebekah, and Leah lie buried.
- When Jacob had made an end of commanding his sons, he gathered up his feet into the bed, and yielded up the ghost, and was gathered unto his people.
