**[Genesis 38:1-7](/bible/genesis/38#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/38#v1)</sup> And it came to pass at that time, that Judah went down from his brethren, and turned in to a certain Adullamite, whose name was Hirah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/38#v2)</sup> And Judah saw there a daughter of a certain Canaanite, whose name was Shuah; and he took her, and went in unto her.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/38#v3)</sup> And she conceived, and bare a son; and he called his name Er.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/38#v4)</sup> And she conceived again, and bare a son; and she called his name Onan.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/38#v5)</sup> And she yet again conceived, and bare a son; and called his name Shelah: and he was at Chezib, when she bare him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/38#v6)</sup> And Judah took a wife for Er his firstborn, whose name was Tamar.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/38#v7)</sup> And Er, Judah’s firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the Lord; and the Lord slew him.

- Judah took the daughter of Shuah and had Er, Onan, and Shelah; and Er, Judah's firstborn, was wicked in the sight of the Lord, and the Lord slew him.

**[Genesis 38:8-10](/bible/genesis/38#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/38#v8)</sup> And Judah said unto Onan, Go in unto thy brother’s wife, and marry her, and raise up seed to thy brother.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/38#v9)</sup> And Onan knew that the seed should not be his; and it came to pass, when he went in unto his brother’s wife, that he spilled it on the ground, lest that he should give seed to his brother.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/38#v10)</sup> And the thing which he did displeased the Lord: wherefore he slew him also.

- Onan was told to go in unto his brother's wife and raise up seed to his brother, and he refused, spilling it on the ground, and the Lord slew him also.
- The sin was refusing to raise up seed to his brother, not the act itself; do not build a doctrine on the wrong point. What happened here later became law: the husband's brother raises up the name of the dead, and he that refuses has his shoe loosed and his face spat in, and his house carries the reproach.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 25:5-9](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)</sup> If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no child, the wife of the dead shall not marry without unto a stranger: her husband’s brother shall go in unto her, and take her to him to wife, and perform the duty of an husband’s brother unto her.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v6)</sup> And it shall be, that the firstborn which she beareth shall succeed in the name of his brother which is dead, that his name be not put out of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v7)</sup> And if the man like not to take his brother’s wife, then let his brother’s wife go up to the gate unto the elders, and say, My husband’s brother refuseth to raise up unto his brother a name in Israel, he will not perform the duty of my husband’s brother.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v8)</sup> Then the elders of his city shall call him, and speak unto him: and if he stand to it, and say, I like not to take her;
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v9)</sup> Then shall his brother’s wife come unto him in the presence of the elders, and loose his shoe from off his foot, and spit in his face, and shall answer and say, So shall it be done unto that man that will not build up his brother’s house.

    The brother's duty to raise up a name in Israel became law, and the refuser bore public reproach; Onan refused that duty.

**[Genesis 38:11-19](/bible/genesis/38#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/38#v11)</sup> Then said Judah to Tamar his daughter in law, Remain a widow at thy father’s house, till Shelah my son be grown: for he said, Lest peradventure he die also, as his brethren did. And Tamar went and dwelt in her father’s house.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/38#v12)</sup> And in process of time the daughter of Shuah Judah’s wife died; and Judah was comforted, and went up unto his sheepshearers to Timnath, he and his friend Hirah the Adullamite.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/38#v13)</sup> And it was told Tamar, saying, Behold thy father in law goeth up to Timnath to shear his sheep.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/38#v14)</sup> And she put her widow’s garments off from her, and covered her with a vail, and wrapped herself, and sat in an open place, which is by the way to Timnath; for she saw that Shelah was grown, and she was not given unto him to wife.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/38#v15)</sup> When Judah saw her, he thought her to be an harlot; because she had covered her face.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/38#v16)</sup> And he turned unto her by the way, and said, Go to, I pray thee, let me come in unto thee; (for he knew not that she was his daughter in law.) And she said, What wilt thou give me, that thou mayest come in unto me?
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/38#v17)</sup> And he said, I will send thee a kid from the flock. And she said, Wilt thou give me a pledge, till thou send it?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/38#v18)</sup> And he said, What pledge shall I give thee? And she said, Thy signet, and thy bracelets, and thy staff that is in thine hand. And he gave it her, and came in unto her, and she conceived by him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/38#v19)</sup> And she arose, and went away, and laid by her vail from her, and put on the garments of her widowhood.

- Judah promised Tamar to Shelah and did not perform it. When she saw Shelah was grown and she was not given to him, she put off her widow's garments, covered herself with a vail, and sat where Judah would pass.
- Judah thought her to be an harlot because of the attire; the scriptures show a harlot was always known by her dress, as the woman with the attire of an harlot and subtil of heart.
- She took his signet, bracelets, and staff as a pledge, and conceived by him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 7:10](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/7#v10)</sup> And, behold, there met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart.

    There met him a woman with the attire of an harlot, and subtil of heart; the attire spoke before she did.

**[Genesis 38:24-26](/bible/genesis/38#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/38#v24)</sup> And it came to pass about three months after, that it was told Judah, saying, Tamar thy daughter in law hath played the harlot; and also, behold, she is with child by whoredom. And Judah said, Bring her forth, and let her be burnt.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/38#v25)</sup> When she was brought forth, she sent to her father in law, saying, By the man, whose these are, am I with child: and she said, Discern, I pray thee, whose are these, the signet, and bracelets, and staff.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/38#v26)</sup> And Judah acknowledged them, and said, She hath been more righteous than I; because that I gave her not to Shelah my son. And he knew her again no more.

- When Judah heard Tamar was with child by whoredom he said, Bring her forth, and let her be burnt. A woman promised in the covenant that plays the harlot and brings an heir by another disobeys the law of the Most High, and her reproach is not blotted out; that is why the judgment was so heavy.
- Then she produced the signet, bracelets, and staff, and Judah acknowledged them: She hath been more righteous than I, because that I gave her not to Shelah my son. And he knew her again no more.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 23:22-26](/bible/sirach/23#v22)**
    > <sup>[22](/bible/sirach/23#v22)</sup> Thus shall it go also with the wife that leaveth her husband, and bringeth in an heir by another.
    >
    > <sup>[23](/bible/sirach/23#v23)</sup> For first, she hath disobeyed the law of the most High; and secondly, she hath trespassed against her own husband; and thirdly, she hath played the whore in adultery, and brought children by another man.
    >
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/23#v24)</sup> She shall be brought out into the congregation, and inquisition shall be made of her children.
    >
    > <sup>[25](/bible/sirach/23#v25)</sup> Her children shall not take root, and her branches shall bring forth no fruit.
    >
    > <sup>[26](/bible/sirach/23#v26)</sup> She shall leave her memory to be cursed, and her reproach shall not be blotted out.

    The wife that brings in an heir by another disobeys the law of the Most High and leaves her memory to be cursed; that standard is why Judah called for judgment.

**[Genesis 38:27-30](/bible/genesis/38#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/38#v27)</sup> And it came to pass in the time of her travail, that, behold, twins were in her womb.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/38#v28)</sup> And it came to pass, when she travailed, that the one put out his hand: and the midwife took and bound upon his hand a scarlet thread, saying, This came out first.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/38#v29)</sup> And it came to pass, as he drew back his hand, that, behold, his brother came out: and she said, How hast thou broken forth? this breach be upon thee: therefore his name was called Pharez.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/38#v30)</sup> And afterward came out his brother, that had the scarlet thread upon his hand: and his name was called Zarah.

- Twins were in her womb: the midwife bound a scarlet thread on the hand of the first, but his brother broke forth first and was called Pharez, and after him came Zarah with the scarlet thread.
- Judah's line went down into Egypt in the end, and Judah died there with his brethren; and Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation.

  Precepts:
  - **[Exodus 1:6](/bible/exodus/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/exodus/1#v6)</sup> And Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation.

    And Joseph died, and all his brethren, and all that generation; Judah ended his days in Egypt.
