**[Genesis 47:1-6](/bible/genesis/47#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/47#v1)</sup> Then Joseph came and told Pharaoh, and said, My father and my brethren, and their flocks, and their herds, and all that they have, are come out of the land of Canaan; and, behold, they are in the land of Goshen.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/47#v2)</sup> And he took some of his brethren, even five men, and presented them unto Pharaoh.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/47#v3)</sup> And Pharaoh said unto his brethren, What is your occupation? And they said unto Pharaoh, Thy servants are shepherds, both we, and also our fathers.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/47#v4)</sup> They said moreover unto Pharaoh, For to sojourn in the land are we come; for thy servants have no pasture for their flocks; for the famine is sore in the land of Canaan: now therefore, we pray thee, let thy servants dwell in the land of Goshen.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/47#v5)</sup> And Pharaoh spake unto Joseph, saying, Thy father and thy brethren are come unto thee:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/47#v6)</sup> The land of Egypt is before thee; in the best of the land make thy father and brethren to dwell; in the land of Goshen let them dwell: and if thou knowest any men of activity among them, then make them rulers over my cattle.

- Joseph presented five of his brethren, and they answered as instructed: thy servants are shepherds, both we and also our fathers. Pharaoh gave the best of the land: in the land of Goshen let them dwell, and men of activity among them were made rulers over his cattle.

**[Genesis 47:11-12](/bible/genesis/47#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/47#v11)</sup> And Joseph placed his father and his brethren, and gave them a possession in the land of Egypt, in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/47#v12)</sup> And Joseph nourished his father, and his brethren, and all his father’s household, with bread, according to their families.

- Joseph placed his father and his brethren in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, and nourished them with bread. Goshen and the land of Rameses are the exact same place.
- Rameses was the king at the time Israel left Egypt, and Moses wrote the first five books of the Bible; he wrote the place under the name of his own day. In the bush the Most High manifestly revealed himself to Moses, showed him the secrets of the times and the end, the beginning to the end, and the words were written again with Esdras.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 14:3-6](/bible/2-esdras/14#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-esdras/14#v3)</sup> Then said he unto me, In the bush I did manifestly reveal myself unto Moses, and talked with him, when my people served in Egypt:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-esdras/14#v4)</sup> And I sent him and led my people out of Egypt, and brought him up to the mount of where I held him by me a long season,
    >
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/14#v5)</sup> And told him many wondrous things, and shewed him the secrets of the times, and the end; and commanded him, saying,
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/2-esdras/14#v6)</sup> These words shalt thou declare, and these shalt thou hide.

    Moses was shown the secrets of the times and the end; that is how the land carried the name Rameses in the writing.

**[Genesis 47:13-20](/bible/genesis/47#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/47#v13)</sup> And there was no bread in all the land; for the famine was very sore, so that the land of Egypt and all the land of Canaan fainted by reason of the famine.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/47#v14)</sup> And Joseph gathered up all the money that was found in the land of Egypt, and in the land of Canaan, for the corn which they bought: and Joseph brought the money into Pharaoh’s house.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/47#v15)</sup> And when money failed in the land of Egypt, and in the land of Canaan, all the Egyptians came unto Joseph, and said, Give us bread: for why should we die in thy presence? for the money faileth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/47#v16)</sup> And Joseph said, Give your cattle; and I will give you for your cattle, if money fail.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/47#v17)</sup> And they brought their cattle unto Joseph: and Joseph gave them bread in exchange for horses, and for the flocks, and for the cattle of the herds, and for the asses: and he fed them with bread for all their cattle for that year.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/47#v18)</sup> When that year was ended, they came unto him the second year, and said unto him, We will not hide it from my lord, how that our money is spent; my lord also hath our herds of cattle; there is not ought left in the sight of my lord, but our bodies, and our lands:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/47#v19)</sup> Wherefore shall we die before thine eyes, both we and our land? buy us and our land for bread, and we and our land will be servants unto Pharaoh: and give us seed, that we may live, and not die, that the land be not desolate.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/47#v20)</sup> And Joseph bought all the land of Egypt for Pharaoh; for the Egyptians sold every man his field, because the famine prevailed over them: so the land became Pharaoh’s.

- When money failed in the land, the Egyptians gave their cattle for bread, and the next year their bodies and their lands; so the land became Pharaoh's. Pharaoh got everybody in servitude off the business dealings of Joseph and the favor the Lord had with him.

**[Genesis 47:23-26](/bible/genesis/47#v23)**

> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/47#v23)</sup> Then Joseph said unto the people, Behold, I have bought you this day and your land for Pharaoh: lo, here is seed for you, and ye shall sow the land.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/47#v24)</sup> And it shall come to pass in the increase, that ye shall give the fifth part unto Pharaoh, and four parts shall be your own, for seed of the field, and for your food, and for them of your households, and for food for your little ones.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/47#v25)</sup> And they said, Thou hast saved our lives: let us find grace in the sight of my lord, and we will be Pharaoh’s servants.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/47#v26)</sup> And Joseph made it a law over the land of Egypt unto this day, that Pharaoh should have the fifth part; except the land of the priests only, which became not Pharaoh’s.

- Joseph gave the people seed and made it a law over Egypt: give the fifth part unto Pharaoh, and four parts are your own for seed of the field, for your food, and for your households and little ones.
- Joseph teaches how to save money: pay the fifth part, and lay up for the years of trouble. That wisdom is how Egypt got through the famine, and it is the same perseverance to practice now in times of tribulation.

**[Genesis 47:27-31](/bible/genesis/47#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/47#v27)</sup> And Israel dwelt in the land of Egypt, in the country of Goshen; and they had possessions therein, and grew, and multiplied exceedingly.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/47#v28)</sup> And Jacob lived in the land of Egypt seventeen years: so the whole age of Jacob was an hundred forty and seven years.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/47#v29)</sup> And the time drew nigh that Israel must die: and he called his son Joseph, and said unto him, If now I have found grace in thy sight, put, I pray thee, thy hand under my thigh, and deal kindly and truly with me; bury me not, I pray thee, in Egypt:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/47#v30)</sup> But I will lie with my fathers, and thou shalt carry me out of Egypt, and bury me in their buryingplace. And he said, I will do as thou hast said.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/47#v31)</sup> And he said, Swear unto me. And he sware unto him. And Israel bowed himself upon the bed’s head.

- Israel dwelt in the country of Goshen, had possessions therein, and grew and multiplied exceedingly. Jacob lived in Egypt seventeen years, so the whole age of Jacob was an hundred forty and seven years.
- Jacob made Joseph swear to carry him out of Egypt and bury him in the burying place of his fathers. By faith Jacob, when he was a dying, blessed both the sons of Joseph; and by faith Joseph made mention of the departing of the children of Israel and gave commandment concerning his bones. [Hebrews 11](/bible/hebrews/11) is the hall of faith, the faithful men of the sons of God, a chapter to read.
- Neither was there a man born like unto Joseph, a governor of his brethren, a stay of the people, whose bones were regarded of the Lord. To grow in spirit and be like him, put on the spirit of the forefathers.

    >
    > <sup>[22](/bible/hebrews/11#v22)</sup> By faith Joseph, when he died, made mention of the departing of the children of Israel; and gave commandment concerning his bones.

    By faith Jacob blessed the sons of Joseph, and by faith Joseph gave commandment concerning his bones.
