**[Genesis 41:1-8](/bible/genesis/41#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/41#v1)</sup> And it came to pass at the end of two full years, that Pharaoh dreamed: and, behold, he stood by the river.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/41#v2)</sup> And, behold, there came up out of the river seven well favoured kine and fatfleshed; and they fed in a meadow.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/41#v3)</sup> And, behold, seven other kine came up after them out of the river, ill favoured and leanfleshed; and stood by the other kine upon the brink of the river.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/41#v4)</sup> And the ill favoured and leanfleshed kine did eat up the seven well favoured and fat kine. So Pharaoh awoke.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/41#v5)</sup> And he slept and dreamed the second time: and, behold, seven ears of corn came up upon one stalk, rank and good.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/41#v6)</sup> And, behold, seven thin ears and blasted with the east wind sprung up after them.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/41#v7)</sup> And the seven thin ears devoured the seven rank and full ears. And Pharaoh awoke, and, behold, it was a dream.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/41#v8)</sup> And it came to pass in the morning that his spirit was troubled; and he sent and called for all the magicians of Egypt, and all the wise men thereof: and Pharaoh told them his dream; but there was none that could interpret them unto Pharaoh.

- After two full years Pharaoh dreamed: seven well favoured kine eaten up by seven ill favoured ones, and seven good ears devoured by seven thin ears blasted with the east wind. He saw different variations of the number seven, as the butler and the baker saw variations of three; the dream is a resemblance of one thing to another.
- His spirit was troubled, and none of the magicians and wise men of Egypt could interpret the dreams unto Pharaoh.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 34:3](/bible/sirach/34#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/34#v3)</sup> The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, even as the likeness of a face to a face.

    The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another; the sevens in Pharaoh's dreams resembled seven years.

**[Genesis 41:15-16](/bible/genesis/41#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/41#v15)</sup> And Pharaoh said unto Joseph, I have dreamed a dream, and there is none that can interpret it: and I have heard say of thee, that thou canst understand a dream to interpret it.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/41#v16)</sup> And Joseph answered Pharaoh, saying, It is not in me: God shall give Pharaoh an answer of peace.

- Pharaoh told Joseph he had heard he could understand a dream to interpret it, and Joseph gave all the praise to the Most High: it is not in me; God shall give Pharaoh an answer of peace.
- The Most High reveals his secrets to his servants, and the secret of the Lord is with them that fear him. That is why the interpretation was with Joseph and not with the magicians of Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[Amos 3:7](/bible/amos/3#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/amos/3#v7)</sup> Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.

    The Lord God revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.
  - **[Psalm 25:14](/bible/psalms/25#v14)**
    > <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/25#v14)</sup> The secret of the Lord is with them that fear him; and he will shew them his covenant.

    The secret of the Lord is with them that fear him; the Most High shows those things unto his servants.

**[Genesis 41:32-36](/bible/genesis/41#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/41#v32)</sup> And for that the dream was doubled unto Pharaoh twice; it is because the thing is established by God, and God will shortly bring it to pass.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/genesis/41#v33)</sup> Now therefore let Pharaoh look out a man discreet and wise, and set him over the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/41#v34)</sup> Let Pharaoh do this, and let him appoint officers over the land, and take up the fifth part of the land of Egypt in the seven plenteous years.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/genesis/41#v35)</sup> And let them gather all the food of those good years that come, and lay up corn under the hand of Pharaoh, and let them keep food in the cities.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/genesis/41#v36)</sup> And that food shall be for store to the land against the seven years of famine, which shall be in the land of Egypt; that the land perish not through the famine.

- The dream was doubled unto Pharaoh twice because the thing is established by God, and God will surely bring it to pass.
- Joseph did not stop at the interpretation; he gave the counsel: take up the fifth part of the land in the seven plenteous years and store it up, so Egypt could ration food through the years of famine. That shows what was on Joseph: he was wise, understanding, and diligent.
- A prudent man foresees the evil coming and makes preparation so he is not consumed in it; the simple pass on and are punished. There is treasure and oil in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man spends it up; spending irrationally is a spirit of folly.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 22:3](/bible/proverbs/22#v3)**

    A prudent man foreseeth the evil, and hideth himself; that prudent spirit was on Joseph.
  - **[Proverbs 21:20](/bible/proverbs/21#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/21#v20)</sup> There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up.

    There is treasure to be desired in the dwelling of the wise, but a foolish man spendeth it up.

**[Genesis 41:50-52](/bible/genesis/41#v50)**

> <sup>[50](/bible/genesis/41#v50)</sup> And unto Joseph were born two sons before the years of famine came, which Asenath the daughter of Poti–pherah priest of On bare unto him.
>
> <sup>[51](/bible/genesis/41#v51)</sup> And Joseph called the name of the firstborn Manasseh: For God, said he, hath made me forget all my toil, and all my father’s house.
>
> <sup>[52](/bible/genesis/41#v52)</sup> And the name of the second called he Ephraim: For God hath caused me to be fruitful in the land of my affliction.

- Joseph called the firstborn Manasseh, forgetting, for God had made him forget all his toil and all his father's house; and the second called he Ephraim, fruitful, for God had caused him to be fruitful in the land of his affliction.
- Ephraim was the second born, yet Ephraim possessed the kingship: the first king of the Northern Kingdom, Jeroboam, was of the tribe of Ephraim, not of Manasseh. Ephraim is the head tribe of the Northern Kingdom.
- Jacob set that in place. When Joseph saw his father lay his right hand upon Ephraim's head it displeased him, and he tried to move it to Manasseh's head; but Jacob refused and said, his younger brother shall be greater than he; and he set Ephraim before Manasseh.

**[Genesis 41:53-57](/bible/genesis/41#v53)**

>

>
> <sup>[55](/bible/genesis/41#v55)</sup> And when all the land of Egypt was famished, the people cried to Pharaoh for bread: and Pharaoh said unto all the Egyptians, Go unto Joseph; what he saith to you, do.
>
> <sup>[56](/bible/genesis/41#v56)</sup> And the famine was over all the face of the earth: And Joseph opened all the storehouses, and sold unto the Egyptians; and the famine waxed sore in the land of Egypt.
>
> <sup>[57](/bible/genesis/41#v57)</sup> And all countries came into Egypt to Joseph for to buy corn; because that the famine was so sore in all lands.

- The seven years of dearth began to come, according as Joseph had said. Another word for dearth is famine: no food, no sustenance to nourish the body.
- The dearth was in all lands, but in all the land of Egypt there was bread, because Joseph had stored up the seven years of plenty. All countries came into Egypt to Joseph for to buy corn.
