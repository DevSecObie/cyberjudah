**[Genesis 26:1-5](/bible/genesis/26#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/26#v1)</sup> And there was a famine in the land, beside the first famine that was in the days of Abraham. And Isaac went unto Abimelech king of the Philistines unto Gerar.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/26#v2)</sup> And the Lord appeared unto him, and said, Go not down into Egypt; dwell in the land which I shall tell thee of:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/26#v3)</sup> Sojourn in this land, and I will be with thee, and will bless thee; for unto thee, and unto thy seed, I will give all these countries, and I will perform the oath which I sware unto Abraham thy father;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/26#v4)</sup> And I will make thy seed to multiply as the stars of heaven, and will give unto thy seed all these countries; and in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/26#v5)</sup> Because that Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.

- In the famine Isaac was told, Go not down into Egypt; the oath sworn to Abraham was performed unto Isaac: seed as the stars, all these countries, and in thy seed shall all nations of the earth be blessed.
- In thy seed shall all nations be blessed does not put everybody in the covenant. The seed is Christ, and the children of the prophets and of the covenant are Israel, scattered into every nation; through Christ scattered Israel in all kindreds of the earth receives repentance and comes back to the Father.
- The reason the oath stood: because Abraham obeyed my voice, and kept my charge, my commandments, my statutes, and my laws.

**[Genesis 26:7-11](/bible/genesis/26#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/26#v7)</sup> And the men of the place asked him of his wife; and he said, She is my sister: for he feared to say, She is my wife; lest, said he, the men of the place should kill me for Rebekah; because she was fair to look upon.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/26#v8)</sup> And it came to pass, when he had been there a long time, that Abimelech king of the Philistines looked out at a window, and saw, and, behold, Isaac was sporting with Rebekah his wife.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/26#v9)</sup> And Abimelech called Isaac, and said, Behold, of a surety she is thy wife: and how saidst thou, She is my sister? And Isaac said unto him, Because I said, Lest I die for her.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/26#v10)</sup> And Abimelech said, What is this thou hast done unto us? one of the people might lightly have lien with thy wife, and thou shouldest have brought guiltiness upon us.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/26#v11)</sup> And Abimelech charged all his people, saying, He that toucheth this man or his wife shall surely be put to death.

- Isaac said of Rebekah, She is my sister, fearing the men of the place, and Abimelech saw Isaac sporting with Rebekah his wife and knew.
- Take the lesson from the sporting: love your wife, play with your wife, keep the flirting and the freshness in the marriage. Give her love, attention, and affection all the days.

**[Genesis 26:12-16](/bible/genesis/26#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/26#v12)</sup> Then Isaac sowed in that land, and received in the same year an hundredfold: and the Lord blessed him.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/26#v13)</sup> And the man waxed great, and went forward, and grew until he became very great:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/26#v14)</sup> For he had possession of flocks, and possession of herds, and great store of servants: and the Philistines envied him.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/26#v15)</sup> For all the wells which his father’s servants had digged in the days of Abraham his father, the Philistines had stopped them, and filled them with earth.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/26#v16)</sup> And Abimelech said unto Isaac, Go from us; for thou art much mightier than we.

- Isaac sowed and received in the same year an hundredfold, and the man waxed great until he became very great; and the Philistines envied him and stopped the wells with earth.
- The nations envy the prosperity of Israel. It is not for anything done to them; the blessing itself is what is hated.

**[Genesis 26:17-22](/bible/genesis/26#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/26#v17)</sup> And Isaac departed thence, and pitched his tent in the valley of Gerar, and dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/26#v18)</sup> And Isaac digged again the wells of water, which they had digged in the days of Abraham his father; for the Philistines had stopped them after the death of Abraham: and he called their names after the names by which his father had called them.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/26#v19)</sup> And Isaac’s servants digged in the valley, and found there a well of springing water.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/26#v20)</sup> And the herdmen of Gerar did strive with Isaac’s herdmen, saying, The water is ours: and he called the name of the well Esek; because they strove with him.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/26#v21)</sup> And they digged another well, and strove for that also: and he called the name of it Sitnah.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/26#v22)</sup> And he removed from thence, and digged another well; and for that they strove not: and he called the name of it Rehoboth; and he said, For now the Lord hath made room for us, and we shall be fruitful in the land.

- Isaac digged again the wells of his father and the herdmen strove for Esek and Sitnah; he removed and digged Rehoboth, and they strove not: for now the Lord hath made room for us.

**[Genesis 26:26-31](/bible/genesis/26#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/26#v26)</sup> Then Abimelech went to him from Gerar, and Ahuzzath one of his friends, and Phichol the chief captain of his army.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/26#v27)</sup> And Isaac said unto them, Wherefore come ye to me, seeing ye hate me, and have sent me away from you?
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/26#v28)</sup> And they said, We saw certainly that the Lord was with thee: and we said, Let there be now an oath betwixt us, even betwixt us and thee, and let us make a covenant with thee;
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/26#v29)</sup> That thou wilt do us no hurt, as we have not touched thee, and as we have done unto thee nothing but good, and have sent thee away in peace: thou art now the blessed of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/26#v30)</sup> And he made them a feast, and they did eat and drink.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/26#v31)</sup> And they rose up betimes in the morning, and sware one to another: and Isaac sent them away, and they departed from him in peace.

- Abimelech came saying, We have done unto thee nothing but good, though his people had stopped the wells and sent Isaac away. Isaac answered plainly: Wherefore come ye to me, seeing ye hate me?
- Isaac was not simple; he separated from the people that hated him rather than assimilating among them, and he dealt with their covenant with open eyes. The words can be like butter while war is in the heart.

**[Genesis 26:34-35](/bible/genesis/26#v34)**

> <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/26#v34)</sup> And Esau was forty years old when he took to wife Judith the daughter of Beeri the Hittite, and Bashemath the daughter of Elon the Hittite:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/genesis/26#v35)</sup> Which were a grief of mind unto Isaac and to Rebekah.

- Esau at forty took wives of the Hittites, and they were a grief of mind unto Isaac and to Rebekah. He went straight against the fathers' law of marrying within the kindred; that is the nature of the vessel.
