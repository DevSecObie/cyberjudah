**[Genesis 43:8-10](/bible/genesis/43#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/43#v8)</sup> And Judah said unto Israel his father, Send the lad with me, and we will arise and go; that we may live, and not die, both we, and thou, and also our little ones.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/43#v9)</sup> I will be surety for him; of my hand shalt thou require him: if I bring him not unto thee, and set him before thee, then let me bear the blame for ever:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/43#v10)</sup> For except we had lingered, surely now we had returned this second time.

- Judah stood surety for the lad: of my hand shalt thou require him; if I bring him not unto thee, and set him before thee, then let me bear the blame for ever.
- Except we had lingered, surely now we had returned this second time. Judah reasoned with his father: had Benjamin been sent at the first, they would have been down to Egypt and back already with food.

**[Genesis 43:11-14](/bible/genesis/43#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/43#v11)</sup> And their father Israel said unto them, If it must be so now, do this; take of the best fruits in the land in your vessels, and carry down the man a present, a little balm, and a little honey, spices, and myrrh, nuts, and almonds:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/43#v12)</sup> And take double money in your hand; and the money that was brought again in the mouth of your sacks, carry it again in your hand; peradventure it was an oversight:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/43#v13)</sup> Take also your brother, and arise, go again unto the man:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/43#v14)</sup> And God Almighty give you mercy before the man, that he may send away your other brother, and Benjamin. If I be bereaved of my children, I am bereaved.

- Israel yielded: carry down the man a present of the best fruits in the land, a little balm, a little honey, spices, and myrrh, nuts, and almonds.
- Take double money in your hand, and carry again the money that was brought back in your sacks; peradventure it was an oversight. Jacob would not keep what was not his: return it and be guiltless.
- And God Almighty give you mercy before the man. Jacob committed the outcome to the Most High: if I be bereaved of my children, I am bereaved.

**[Genesis 43:29-31](/bible/genesis/43#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/43#v29)</sup> And he lifted up his eyes, and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother’s son, and said, Is this your younger brother, of whom ye spake unto me? And he said, God be gracious unto thee, my son.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/43#v30)</sup> And Joseph made haste; for his bowels did yearn upon his brother: and he sought where to weep; and he entered into his chamber, and wept there.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/43#v31)</sup> And he washed his face, and went out, and refrained himself, and said, Set on bread.

- Joseph lifted up his eyes and saw his brother Benjamin, his mother's son, and said, God be gracious unto thee, my son. His bowels did yearn upon his brother, and he entered into his chamber and wept there; then he washed his face, and went out, and refrained himself.

**[Genesis 43:32](/bible/genesis/43#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/43#v32)</sup> And they set on for him by himself, and for them by themselves, and for the Egyptians, which did eat with him, by themselves: because the Egyptians might not eat bread with the Hebrews; for that is an abomination unto the Egyptians.

- The Egyptians might not eat bread with the Hebrews, for that is an abomination unto the Egyptians. The same thing showed up back in the 1900s in America: colored only and whites only, no seat at the same table; they fashioned themselves after ancient Egypt.
- Esau patterns himself after the former kingdoms. He learns how to govern and oppress by studying the kingdoms that were before him: ancient Babylon, ancient Greece, ancient Rome, ancient Egypt. They search out iniquities and accomplish a diligent search; nothing they do is accidental, everything follows the blueprint of the kingdoms that came before them.

    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/64#v6)</sup> They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search: both the inward thought of every one of them, and the heart, is deep.

    They search out iniquities; they accomplish a diligent search; the oppressor studies the former kingdoms and follows their blueprint.

**[Genesis 43:33-34](/bible/genesis/43#v33)**

> <sup>[33](/bible/genesis/43#v33)</sup> And they sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright, and the youngest according to his youth: and the men marvelled one at another.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/43#v34)</sup> And he took and sent messes unto them from before him: but Benjamin’s mess was five times so much as any of theirs. And they drank, and were merry with him.

- They sat before him, the firstborn according to his birthright and the youngest according to his youth, and the men marvelled one at another.
- Benjamin's mess was five times so much as any of theirs. Joseph gave all of them provision, but he made sure his baby brother was well taken care of: Benjamin was his mother's son and had nothing to do with him being sold. And they drank, and were merry with him.
