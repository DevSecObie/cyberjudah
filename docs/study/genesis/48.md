## Introduction

This session is a reading session, marked (READ), covering [Genesis 48](/bible/genesis/48) through 50 and Exodus 1: Jacob blessing Ephraim and Manasseh, the prophecies over the twelve tribes for the last days, the burial of Jacob and the death of Joseph, and Israel under a new king in Egypt. The reading moved straight through with brief teaching and no questions, and the full twelve-tribe breakdown of [Genesis 49](/bible/genesis/49) was reserved for its own class; the tribe identifications given in the reading are recorded here. The [Exodus 1](/bible/exodus/1) portion received its own teaching and is written in its own chapter file.

**[Genesis 48:1-2](/bible/genesis/48#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/48#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after these things, that one told Joseph, Behold, thy father is sick: and he took with him his two sons, Manasseh and Ephraim.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/48#v2)</sup> And one told Jacob, and said, Behold, thy son Joseph cometh unto thee: and Israel strengthened himself, and sat upon the bed.

- Word came to Joseph, Behold, thy father is sick, and he took with him his two sons Manasseh and Ephraim to visit Jacob; and Israel strengthened himself and sat upon the bed.

**[Genesis 48:3-6](/bible/genesis/48#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/48#v3)</sup> And Jacob said unto Joseph, God Almighty appeared unto me at Luz in the land of Canaan, and blessed me,
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/48#v4)</sup> And said unto me, Behold, I will make thee fruitful, and multiply thee, and I will make of thee a multitude of people; and will give this land to thy seed after thee for an everlasting possession.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/48#v5)</sup> And now thy two sons, Ephraim and Manasseh, which were born unto thee in the land of Egypt before I came unto thee into Egypt, are mine; as Reuben and Simeon, they shall be mine.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/48#v6)</sup> And thy issue, which thou begettest after them, shall be thine, and shall be called after the name of their brethren in their inheritance.

- Jacob rehearsed to Joseph how God Almighty appeared unto him at Luz in the land of Canaan and blessed him, promising to make him a multitude of people and to give the land to his seed after him for an everlasting possession.
- Thy two sons Ephraim and Manasseh, as Reuben and Simeon, they shall be mine. Jacob took Joseph's sons as his own, so the blessings that came upon Jacob's sons came upon Ephraim and Manasseh also; they became children of Israel, partakers of the same promises as the rest of the sons of Jacob.

**[Genesis 48:7](/bible/genesis/48#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/48#v7)</sup> And as for me, when I came from Padan, Rachel died by me in the land of Canaan in the way, when yet there was but a little way to come unto Ephrath: and I buried her there in the way of Ephrath; the same is Beth–lehem.

- Jacob told Joseph where his mother was buried: Rachel died by him in the land of Canaan in the way of Ephrath, which is Bethlehem, and he buried her there.

**[Genesis 48:8-14](/bible/genesis/48#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/48#v8)</sup> And Israel beheld Joseph’s sons, and said, Who are these?
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/48#v9)</sup> And Joseph said unto his father, They are my sons, whom God hath given me in this place. And he said, Bring them, I pray thee, unto me, and I will bless them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/48#v10)</sup> Now the eyes of Israel were dim for age, so that he could not see. And he brought them near unto him; and he kissed them, and embraced them.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/48#v11)</sup> And Israel said unto Joseph, I had not thought to see thy face: and, lo, God hath shewed me also thy seed.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/48#v12)</sup> And Joseph brought them out from between his knees, and he bowed himself with his face to the earth.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/48#v13)</sup> And Joseph took them both, Ephraim in his right hand toward Israel’s left hand, and Manasseh in his left hand toward Israel’s right hand, and brought them near unto him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/48#v14)</sup> And Israel stretched out his right hand, and laid it upon Ephraim’s head, who was the younger, and his left hand upon Manasseh’s head, guiding his hands wittingly; for Manasseh was the firstborn.

- The eyes of Israel were dim for age, so that he could not see, and he kissed and embraced Joseph's sons: I had not thought to see thy face, and lo, God hath shewed me also thy seed.
- Joseph set Manasseh toward Israel's right hand and Ephraim toward his left, but Israel stretched out his right hand and laid it upon Ephraim the younger, guiding his hands wittingly, for Manasseh was the firstborn.

**[Genesis 48:15-16](/bible/genesis/48#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/48#v15)</sup> And he blessed Joseph, and said, God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac did walk, the God which fed me all my life long unto this day,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/48#v16)</sup> The Angel which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be named on them, and the name of my fathers Abraham and Isaac; and let them grow into a multitude in the midst of the earth.

- Jacob blessed Joseph: God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac did walk, the God which fed me all my life long unto this day, the Angel which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads; and let my name be named on them.
- By that word Joseph's sons carried the name of Israel and grew into a multitude in the midst of the earth.

**[Genesis 48:17-19](/bible/genesis/48#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/48#v17)</sup> And when Joseph saw that his father laid his right hand upon the head of Ephraim, it displeased him: and he held up his father’s hand, to remove it from Ephraim’s head unto Manasseh’s head.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/48#v18)</sup> And Joseph said unto his father, Not so, my father: for this is the firstborn; put thy right hand upon his head.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/48#v19)</sup> And his father refused, and said, I know it, my son, I know it: he also shall become a people, and he also shall be great: but truly his younger brother shall be greater than he, and his seed shall become a multitude of nations.

- It displeased Joseph, and he held up his father's hand to move it to Manasseh the firstborn; but Jacob refused: I know it, my son, I know it. Manasseh also shall become a people and shall be great, but truly his younger brother shall be greater than he, and his seed shall become a multitude of nations.
- Ephraim was set above Manasseh because Ephraim became the head of the Northern Kingdom of Israel.

**[Genesis 48:20-22](/bible/genesis/48#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/48#v20)</sup> And he blessed them that day, saying, In thee shall Israel bless, saying, God make thee as Ephraim and as Manasseh: and he set Ephraim before Manasseh.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/48#v21)</sup> And Israel said unto Joseph, Behold, I die: but God shall be with you, and bring you again unto the land of your fathers.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/48#v22)</sup> Moreover I have given to thee one portion above thy brethren, which I took out of the hand of the Amorite with my sword and with my bow.

- In thee shall Israel bless, saying, God make thee as Ephraim and as Manasseh; and he set Ephraim before Manasseh.
- Behold, I die: but God shall be with you, and bring you again unto the land of your fathers. Jacob prophesied the return of Joseph's seed to the land of Israel, and gave Joseph one portion above his brethren, which he took out of the hand of the Amorite with his sword and with his bow.
