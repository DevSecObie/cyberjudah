**[Genesis 11:1-4](/bible/genesis/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/11#v1)</sup> And the whole earth was of one language, and of one speech.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/11#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/11#v3)</sup> And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them throughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/11#v4)</sup> And they said, Go to, let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven; and let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.

- The whole earth was of one language, and they journeyed from the east to the plain of Shinar. Coming from Jerusalem eastward lands you in Shinar, in Babylon.
- They built the tower so they would not be scattered abroad; the fear of the flood was still on them, and they wanted to make themselves a name.

**[Genesis 11:5-9](/bible/genesis/11#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/11#v5)</sup> And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men builded.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/11#v6)</sup> And the Lord said, Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/11#v7)</sup> Go to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/11#v8)</sup> So the Lord scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/11#v9)</sup> Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the Lord did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the Lord scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.

- The people being one was not going to produce righteousness; nothing would be restrained from what they imagined to do, and it would be evil, so the Most High confounded their language and scattered them.
- There has never been a doctrine of all nations holding hands as one in the scriptures. The Most High divided the nations their inheritance and separated the sons of Adam, setting the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel.
- The man of sin opposes this: he exalts himself above all that is called God, gathers all nations to himself as one, and deifies himself, making God and the Messiah after his own image. That is the same thing Nimrod did in the land of Shinar.
- After Babel the Most High sent every family back to where it was supposed to be: Ham to his place, Shem to his, Japheth to his.

**[Genesis 11:10-13](/bible/genesis/11#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/11#v10)</sup> These are the generations of Shem: Shem was an hundred years old, and begat Arphaxad two years after the flood:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/11#v11)</sup> And Shem lived after he begat Arphaxad five hundred years, and begat sons and daughters.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/11#v12)</sup> And Arphaxad lived five and thirty years, and begat Salah:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/11#v13)</sup> And Arphaxad lived after he begat Salah four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.

- Watch the lifespans in the generations of Shem: five hundred years, then four hundred, then two hundred, then down past one hundred and nineteen with Nahor. The word of [Genesis 6:3](/bible/genesis/6#v3) is taking hold on man's days.
- Each of these generations begat sons and daughters for hundreds of years; the earth is being populated again all through this chapter.

**[Genesis 11:27-32](/bible/genesis/11#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/11#v27)</sup> Now these are the generations of Terah: Terah begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran begat Lot.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/11#v28)</sup> And Haran died before his father Terah in the land of his nativity, in Ur of the Chaldees.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/11#v29)</sup> And Abram and Nahor took them wives: the name of Abram’s wife was Sarai; and the name of Nahor’s wife, Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah, and the father of Iscah.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/11#v30)</sup> But Sarai was barren; she had no child.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/11#v31)</sup> And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran his son’s son, and Sarai his daughter in law, his son Abram’s wife; and they went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go into the land of Canaan; and they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/11#v32)</sup> And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years: and Terah died in Haran.

- Haran begat Lot and died before his father Terah in Ur of the Chaldees. Ur means fire in the etymology, and the Chaldees is Babylon; Abram was born and called out of that area.
- Lot was a just man. To be just means to do that which is lawful and right, a commandment keeper; righteous Lot was vexed from day to day with the unlawful deeds of the wicked.
