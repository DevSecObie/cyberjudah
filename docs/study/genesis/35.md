**[Genesis 35:1-4](/bible/genesis/35#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/35#v1)</sup> And God said unto Jacob, Arise, go up to Beth–el, and dwell there: and make there an altar unto God, that appeared unto thee when thou fleddest from the face of Esau thy brother.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/35#v2)</sup> Then Jacob said unto his household, and to all that were with him, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean, and change your garments:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/35#v3)</sup> And let us arise, and go up to Beth–el; and I will make there an altar unto God, who answered me in the day of my distress, and was with me in the way which I went.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/35#v4)</sup> And they gave unto Jacob all the strange gods which were in their hand, and all their earrings which were in their ears; and Jacob hid them under the oak which was by Shechem.

- Before going up to Bethel, Jacob told his household, Put away the strange gods that are among you, and be clean. The strange gods were there because Rachel had stolen her father's images and hidden them, sitting on them when Laban searched.
- Idolatry hidden in the household must be put away before you can stand at Bethel. Rachel finally gave up the images, and Jacob hid them under the oak by Shechem.
- From Eve to Rachel the scriptures warn how sin enters through the household; of the woman came the beginning of sin. Sisters, stay in the spirit and guard against the serpent's beguiling as the mothers were beguiled.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 31:19](/bible/genesis/31#v19)**
    > <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/31#v19)</sup> And Laban went to shear his sheep: and Rachel had stolen the images that were her father’s.

    Rachel had stolen the images that were her father's; that is where the strange gods in the household came from.
  - **[Genesis 31:34-35](/bible/genesis/31#v34)**
    > <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/31#v34)</sup> Now Rachel had taken the images, and put them in the camel’s furniture, and sat upon them. And Laban searched all the tent, but found them not.
    >
    > <sup>[35](/bible/genesis/31#v35)</sup> And she said to her father, Let it not displease my lord that I cannot rise up before thee; for the custom of women is upon me. And he searched, but found not the images.

    Rachel hid the images in the camel's furniture and sat upon them, and Laban found them not.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 25:24](/bible/sirach/25#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/25#v24)</sup> Of the woman came the beginning of sin, and through her we all die.

    Of the woman came the beginning of sin; the warning runs from Eve through the mothers.

**[Genesis 35:5](/bible/genesis/35#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/35#v5)</sup> And they journeyed: and the terror of God was upon the cities that were round about them, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob.

- The terror of God was upon the cities round about, and they did not pursue after the sons of Jacob. What Jacob feared after Shechem never came, because the angel of the Lord encamps round about them that fear Him and delivers them.

    The angel of the Lord encampeth round about them that fear Him, and delivereth them.

**[Genesis 35:9-11](/bible/genesis/35#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/35#v9)</sup> And God appeared unto Jacob again, when he came out of Padan–aram, and blessed him.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/35#v10)</sup> And God said unto him, Thy name is Jacob: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name: and he called his name Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/35#v11)</sup> And God said unto him, I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins;

- God appeared to Jacob again and confirmed it: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name. Be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins.
- The nation and company of nations of Abraham's covenant is Israel, plain and clear, and kings come out of Israel's loins. Christ washed us from our sins and made us kings and priests unto God; the men of Israel are kings in the sight of the Most High.
- Israel once had mighty kings who ruled over countries beyond the river, and tribute and custom were paid unto them. The kings failed when they forsook the law, and their power was given to a strange nation; that is why the kings of the scriptures are not known as black kings today. When the King of kings returns He restores the crowns, and He sets crowns on the heads of the great multitude on mount Sion.

    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/revelation/1#v6)</sup> And hath made us kings and priests unto God and his Father; to him be glory and dominion for ever and ever. Amen.

    Christ washed us from our sins and hath made us kings and priests unto God.

    All except David, Ezekias, and Josias were defective and forsook the law; therefore their power was given unto others, and their glory to a strange nation.

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/35#v16)</sup> And they journeyed from Beth–el; and there was but a little way to come to Ephrath: and Rachel travailed, and she had hard labour.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/35#v17)</sup> And it came to pass, when she was in hard labour, that the midwife said unto her, Fear not; thou shalt have this son also.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/35#v18)</sup> And it came to pass, as her soul was in departing, (for she died) that she called his name Ben–oni: but his father called him Benjamin.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/35#v19)</sup> And Rachel died, and was buried in the way to Ephrath, which is Beth–lehem.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/35#v20)</sup> And Jacob set a pillar upon her grave: that is the pillar of Rachel’s grave unto this day.

- Rachel travailed in hard labour and died bearing her second son; she called him Benoni, but his father called him Benjamin, the son of the right hand. He was the twelfth son, and Jacob set a pillar upon Rachel's grave in the way to Ephrath, which is Bethlehem.

**[Genesis 35:22](/bible/genesis/35#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/35#v22)</sup> And it came to pass, when Israel dwelt in that land, that Reuben went and lay with Bilhah his father’s concubine: and Israel heard it. Now the sons of Jacob were twelve:

- Reuben went and lay with Bilhah, his father's concubine, and Israel heard it. That is fornication such as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father's wife.
- The judgment followed him: forasmuch as he defiled his father's bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph, and the genealogy is not reckoned after the birthright. The firstborn lost the birthright over it.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Corinthians 5:1](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v1)</sup> It is reported commonly that there is fornication among you, and such fornication as is not so much as named among the Gentiles, that one should have his father’s wife.

    That a man should have his father's wife is fornication not so much as named among the Gentiles.
  - **[1 Chronicles 5:1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-chronicles/5#v1)</sup> Now the sons of Reuben the firstborn of Israel, (for he was the firstborn; but, forasmuch as he defiled his father’s bed, his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph the son of Israel: and the genealogy is not to be reckoned after the birthright.

    Reuben was the firstborn, but because he defiled his father's bed his birthright was given unto the sons of Joseph.
