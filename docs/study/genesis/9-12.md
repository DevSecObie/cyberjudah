## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 9](/bible/genesis/9) through 12, read with precepts as the chapters go. The laws you meet in these chapters stand from the beginning, long before Moses.

**[Genesis 9:1](/bible/genesis/9#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/9#v1)</sup> And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth.

- Noah and his sons were told to replenish the earth after the flood, and the earth is replenished with righteous children; a multitude of ungodly children profits nothing.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 16:1-3](/bible/sirach/16#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/sirach/16#v1)</sup> Desire not a multitude of unprofitable children, neither delight in ungodly sons.
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/sirach/16#v2)</sup> Though they multiply, rejoice not in them, except the fear of the Lord be with them.
    >
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/16#v3)</sup> Trust not thou in their life, neither respect their multitude: for one that is just is better than a thousand; and better it is to die without children, than to have them that are ungodly.

    One just child is better than a thousand ungodly; replenish the earth with righteous seed.

**[Genesis 9:2](/bible/genesis/9#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/9#v2)</sup> And the fear of you and the dread of you shall be upon every beast of the earth, and upon every fowl of the air, upon all that moveth upon the earth, and upon all the fishes of the sea; into your hand are they delivered.

- The fear and dread of man upon the beasts is not how it was in the beginning, and not how it will remain; the original relationship between man and the animals will be restored.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 11:6-9](/bible/isaiah/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/isaiah/11#v6)</sup> The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb, and the leopard shall lie down with the kid; and the calf and the young lion and the fatling together; and a little child shall lead them.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/11#v7)</sup> And the cow and the bear shall feed; their young ones shall lie down together: and the lion shall eat straw like the ox.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/11#v8)</sup> And the sucking child shall play on the hole of the asp, and the weaned child shall put his hand on the cockatrice’ den.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/isaiah/11#v9)</sup> They shall not hurt nor destroy in all my holy mountain: for the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord, as the waters cover the sea.

    The wolf dwells with the lamb and a little child leads them; the animals return to their original state when things are set back in order.

**[Genesis 9:3](/bible/genesis/9#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/9#v3)</sup> Every moving thing that liveth shall be meat for you; even as the green herb have I given you all things.

- This is the introduction of man eating meat. The Most High did not give man a new stomach; the body was already made for it and it lay dormant until after the flood.
- Having the liberty to eat meat does not mean eating without balance; be not too greedy upon meats, for excess of meats brings sickness.
- The law of what may be eaten was already understood; you could not eat everything. The doctrine that says you can pray over anything and eat it is a seducing spirit and a doctrine of devils.
- Fish is meat; all flesh is not the same flesh, but the flesh of beasts, of fishes, and of birds are all flesh. Every creature of God is good for what it was made for, and it is sanctified by the word of God, and the word that sanctifies what may be eaten is the dietary law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 37:27-31](/bible/sirach/37#v27)**

    The law of the beasts makes a difference between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.
  - **[1 Timothy 4:1-5](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)</sup> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;

- The law against eating flesh with the blood was given to Adam and is still in place with Noah; the word is true from the beginning.

**[Genesis 9:5](/bible/genesis/9#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/9#v5)</sup> And surely your blood of your lives will I require; at the hand of every beast will I require it, and at the hand of man; at the hand of every man’s brother will I require the life of man.

- The Most High requiring blood at the hand of every beast is animal sacrifice, still in place with Noah, passed down generation to generation.
- The judgment for sin has always been death. When Eve sinned and caused Adam to sin, either they had to die, or something had to stand in their place; the blood of the animal stood in for them.
- That is the scapegoat: it takes the wrongdoings, mistakes, and faults of others and places them on the animal. That is why Cain and Abel were bringing offerings, because they knew of the sacrifice from [Genesis 3](/bible/genesis/3).

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 3:21](/bible/genesis/3#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/3#v21)</sup> Unto Adam also and to his wife did the Lord God make coats of skins, and clothed them.

    The coats of skins were the first sacrifice; the beast stood in place of Adam and Eve.
  - **[Genesis 4:3-5](/bible/genesis/4#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/4#v3)</sup> And in process of time it came to pass, that Cain brought of the fruit of the ground an offering unto the Lord.

    Cain and Abel brought offerings, proving the sacrifice was known and passed down.
  - **[Leviticus 16:8-10](/bible/leviticus/16#v8)**

    The scapegoat is presented alive to make an atonement, standing in for the faults of others.

**[Genesis 9:6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)</sup> Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man.

- Whoso sheddeth man's blood, by man shall his blood be shed: life for life. This law is way before Moses, so it cannot be dismissed as the law of Moses.
- The laws were never done away with; Christ came not to destroy the law but to fulfil, and not one jot or tittle passes from the law.

  Precepts:
  - **[Matthew 5:17-18](/bible/matthew/5#v17)**

**[Genesis 9:9-11](/bible/genesis/9#v9)**

- The covenant established here is with Noah, his seed, and every living creature: all flesh shall never again be cut off by the waters of a flood.
- This is a general covenant with all flesh. The covenant with Israel is found in [Jeremiah 31](/bible/jeremiah/31).

**[Genesis 9:12-13](/bible/genesis/9#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/9#v12)</sup> And God said, This is the token of the covenant which I make between me and you and every living creature that is with you, for perpetual generations:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/9#v13)</sup> I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.

- The bow in the cloud, the rainbow, is the token of the covenant between the Most High and the earth; it compasses the heaven about with a glorious circle, and His hands bended it.
- The world has taken that same bow, the token of the covenant, and turned it into the symbol of sodomy. The spiritual eyes will see what that means: the great city is spiritually called Sodom and Egypt.

# Genesis 10: Shem, Ham, Japheth, and Nimrod

Read the chapter → [Genesis 10](/bible/genesis/10)

**[Genesis 10:1-5](/bible/genesis/10#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/10#v1)</sup> Now these are the generations of the sons of Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth: and unto them were sons born after the flood.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/10#v2)</sup> The sons of Japheth; Gomer, and Magog, and Madai, and Javan, and Tubal, and Meshech, and Tiras.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/10#v3)</sup> And the sons of Gomer; Ashkenaz, and Riphath, and Togarmah.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/10#v4)</sup> And the sons of Javan; Elishah, and Tarshish, Kittim, and Dodanim.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/10#v5)</sup> By these were the isles of the Gentiles divided in their lands; every one after his tongue, after their families, in their nations.

- The sons of Japheth settled what became Europe. Ashkenaz is a Hebrew name for Germany; Tarshish is Spain; Kittim is Rome; Javan is Greece; you can see Meshech, Tubal, and Magog on the old maps.
- These were the isles of the Gentiles, divided in their lands, every one after his tongue.

**[Genesis 10:6-7](/bible/genesis/10#v6)**

> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/10#v6)</sup> And the sons of Ham; Cush, and Mizraim, and Phut, and Canaan.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/10#v7)</sup> And the sons of Cush; Seba, and Havilah, and Sabtah, and Raamah, and Sabtecha: and the sons of Raamah; Sheba, and Dedan.

- The sons of Ham: Cush is Ethiopia; Mizraim is Egypt; Phut is Libya, to the left of Egypt; and Canaan is the land where Jerusalem sits.
- Ham inhabited what is known as Africa, and Canaan's land is the land of Israel; so the land of Israel is part of Africa.
- The Israelites are of Shem, not of Ham; the children of Israel are Shemites scattered among the children of Ham in Africa.

**[Genesis 10:8-11](/bible/genesis/10#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/10#v8)</sup> And Cush begat Nimrod: he began to be a mighty one in the earth.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/10#v9)</sup> He was a mighty hunter before the Lord: wherefore it is said, Even as Nimrod the mighty hunter before the Lord.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/10#v10)</sup> And the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, and Erech, and Accad, and Calneh, in the land of Shinar.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/10#v11)</sup> Out of that land went forth Asshur, and builded Nineveh, and the city Rehoboth, and Calah,

- Cush begat Nimrod, the mighty hunter, and the beginning of his kingdom was Babel, Erech, Accad, and Calneh in the land of Shinar; Shinar is what is known as Iraq. Out of that land Asshur, the Assyrians, built Nineveh.
- History records that Nimrod was the first king of Nineveh; the name Nineveh means the habitation of Ninus, which was Nimrod. He was the first mortal deified at his death, the father of the gods, and that pagan practice of deifying kings spread from Mesopotamia to Egypt and beyond.
- Nimrod, his queen Semiramis, and her child Tammuz became the first pagan trinity, and their worship spread under different names through every nation: Baal, Bel, Zeus, Jupiter, Ra, Thor; Ishtar, Ashtaroth, Isis, Diana.
- Sunday sacredness, Christmas, the Christmas tree, Easter, the Easter egg, and Good Friday have their origin in that Babylonian sun worship, not in the Old and New Testaments. This is why the scriptures must be studied precept upon precept, so ancient paganism mixed in is not mistaken for the truth.
- The origins of these places, Babel, Erech, Accad, Shinar, still sit on the eastern side of the world, which is why the history reads like a fairy tale to a people carried to the western side with none of it to see.

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/10#v16)</sup> And the Jebusite, and the Amorite, and the Girgasite,

- The border of the Canaanites reached unto Sodom and Gomorrah, and Admah and Zeboim; when Sodom and Gomorrah were destroyed, those cities went into destruction with them. Deuteronomy names the cities together.

**[Genesis 10:21](/bible/genesis/10#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/10#v21)</sup> Unto Shem also, the father of all the children of Eber, the brother of Japheth the elder, even to him were children born.

# Genesis 11: Babel and the Separation of the Nations

Read the chapter → [Genesis 11](/bible/genesis/11)

**[Genesis 11:1-4](/bible/genesis/11#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/11#v1)</sup> And the whole earth was of one language, and of one speech.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/11#v2)</sup> And it came to pass, as they journeyed from the east, that they found a plain in the land of Shinar; and they dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/11#v3)</sup> And they said one to another, Go to, let us make brick, and burn them throughly. And they had brick for stone, and slime had they for morter.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/11#v4)</sup> And they said, Go to, let us build us a city and a tower, whose top may reach unto heaven; and let us make us a name, lest we be scattered abroad upon the face of the whole earth.

- The whole earth was of one language, and they journeyed from the east to the plain of Shinar. Coming from Jerusalem eastward lands you in Shinar, in Babylon.
- They built the tower so they would not be scattered abroad; the fear of the flood was still on them, and they wanted to make themselves a name.

**[Genesis 11:5-9](/bible/genesis/11#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/11#v5)</sup> And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower, which the children of men builded.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/11#v6)</sup> And the Lord said, Behold, the people is one, and they have all one language; and this they begin to do: and now nothing will be restrained from them, which they have imagined to do.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/11#v7)</sup> Go to, let us go down, and there confound their language, that they may not understand one another’s speech.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/11#v8)</sup> So the Lord scattered them abroad from thence upon the face of all the earth: and they left off to build the city.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/11#v9)</sup> Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the Lord did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the Lord scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.

- The people being one was not going to produce righteousness; nothing would be restrained from what they imagined to do, and it would be evil, so the Most High confounded their language and scattered them.
- There has never been a doctrine of all nations holding hands as one in the scriptures. The Most High divided the nations their inheritance and separated the sons of Adam, setting the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel.
- The man of sin opposes this: he exalts himself above all that is called God, gathers all nations to himself as one, and deifies himself, making God and the Messiah after his own image. That is the same thing Nimrod did in the land of Shinar.
- After Babel the Most High sent every family back to where it was supposed to be: Ham to his place, Shem to his, Japheth to his.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 32:7-9](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v7)</sup> Remember the days of old, consider the years of many generations: ask thy father, and he will shew thee; thy elders, and they will tell thee.
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v8)</sup> When the most High divided to the nations their inheritance, when he separated the sons of Adam, he set the bounds of the people according to the number of the children of Israel.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/32#v9)</sup> For the Lord’s portion is his people; Jacob is the lot of his inheritance.

    The Most High divided the nations and separated the sons of Adam by the number of the children of Israel; Jacob is His portion.
  - **[2 Thessalonians 2:3-4](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v3)</sup> Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition;
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v4)</sup> Who opposeth and exalteth himself above all that is called God, or that is worshipped; so that he as God sitteth in the temple of God, shewing himself that he is God.

    The man of sin exalts himself above all that is called God, sitting as God; the spirit of Nimrod all over again.

**[Genesis 11:10-13](/bible/genesis/11#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/11#v10)</sup> These are the generations of Shem: Shem was an hundred years old, and begat Arphaxad two years after the flood:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/11#v11)</sup> And Shem lived after he begat Arphaxad five hundred years, and begat sons and daughters.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/11#v12)</sup> And Arphaxad lived five and thirty years, and begat Salah:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/11#v13)</sup> And Arphaxad lived after he begat Salah four hundred and three years, and begat sons and daughters.

- Watch the lifespans in the generations of Shem: five hundred years, then four hundred, then two hundred, then down past one hundred and nineteen with Nahor. The word of [Genesis 6:3](/bible/genesis/6#v3) is taking hold on man's days.
- Each of these generations begat sons and daughters for hundreds of years; the earth is being populated again all through this chapter.

**[Genesis 11:27-32](/bible/genesis/11#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/11#v27)</sup> Now these are the generations of Terah: Terah begat Abram, Nahor, and Haran; and Haran begat Lot.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/11#v28)</sup> And Haran died before his father Terah in the land of his nativity, in Ur of the Chaldees.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/11#v29)</sup> And Abram and Nahor took them wives: the name of Abram’s wife was Sarai; and the name of Nahor’s wife, Milcah, the daughter of Haran, the father of Milcah, and the father of Iscah.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/11#v30)</sup> But Sarai was barren; she had no child.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/11#v31)</sup> And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran his son’s son, and Sarai his daughter in law, his son Abram’s wife; and they went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go into the land of Canaan; and they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/11#v32)</sup> And the days of Terah were two hundred and five years: and Terah died in Haran.

- Haran begat Lot and died before his father Terah in Ur of the Chaldees. Ur means fire in the etymology, and the Chaldees is Babylon; Abram was born and called out of that area.
- Lot was a just man. To be just means to do that which is lawful and right, a commandment keeper; righteous Lot was vexed from day to day with the unlawful deeds of the wicked.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ezekiel 18:5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)</sup> But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right,

    A just man is one that does that which is lawful and right.
  - **[2 Peter 2:7-9](/bible/2-peter/2#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/2-peter/2#v7)</sup> And delivered just Lot, vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked:
    >
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-peter/2#v8)</sup> (For that righteous man dwelling among them, in seeing and hearing, vexed his righteous soul from day to day with their unlawful deeds;)
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-peter/2#v9)</sup> The Lord knoweth how to deliver the godly out of temptations, and to reserve the unjust unto the day of judgment to be punished:

    Just Lot was a righteous man, vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked.

# Genesis 12: The Calling of Abram

Read the chapter → [Genesis 12](/bible/genesis/12)

**[Genesis 12:1](/bible/genesis/12#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/12#v1)</sup> Now the Lord had said unto Abram, Get thee out of thy country, and from thy kindred, and from thy father’s house, unto a land that I will shew thee:

- The Most High called Abram to get out of his country, from his kindred, and from his father's house. He called Abraham alone, and blessed him, and increased him.
- Some hold back from repenting because of what family or friends may say. Do not let your kindred hold you back from being blessed and increased; when the Most High calls you out, you have to go.
- Confessing Christ before men means something. When Christ asked, Whom say ye that I am, Peter confessed, Thou art the Christ, the Son of the living God; whoever confesses Him before men, He confesses before His Father, and He came to set a man at variance against his own household for the truth's sake.

  - **[Matthew 10:32-37](/bible/matthew/10#v32)**

**[Genesis 12:2-3](/bible/genesis/12#v2)**

The children of the prophets and of the covenant are Israel, and Israel would be scattered into all nations of the earth; through Abraham's seed the kindreds of the earth are blessed because scattered Israel gets the chance to repent and return, circumcised or not.
