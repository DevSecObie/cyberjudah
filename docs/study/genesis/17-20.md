## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 17](/bible/genesis/17) through 20. Abraham is a righteous example all across the board: faith, obedience, hospitality, leadership, and immediate action on the commandments of the Most High.

**[Genesis 17:1](/bible/genesis/17#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/17#v1)</sup> And when Abram was ninety years old and nine, the Lord appeared to Abram, and said unto him, I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect.

- At ninety and nine years old Abram was commanded to walk before the Most High and be perfect. There is no age excuse; Christ said nothing new in [Matthew 5:48](/bible/matthew/5#v48), for the command to be perfect was given thousands of years before.
- Perfect according to the scriptures means to walk in His statutes and keep His commandments; that is how Israel perfects itself.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 Kings 8:61](/bible/1-kings/8#v61)**
> <sup>[61](/bible/1-kings/8#v61)</sup> Let your heart therefore be perfect with the Lord our God, to walk in his statutes, and to keep his commandments, as at this day.

    A perfect heart is one that walks in His statutes and keeps His commandments; that is the meaning of be thou perfect.

**[Genesis 17:4-6](/bible/genesis/17#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/17#v4)</sup> As for me, behold, my covenant is with thee, and thou shalt be a father of many nations.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/17#v5)</sup> Neither shall thy name any more be called Abram, but thy name shall be Abraham; for a father of many nations have I made thee.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/17#v6)</sup> And I will make thee exceeding fruitful, and I will make nations of thee, and kings shall come out of thee.

- When the Most High began dealing with Abram on a covenant level, his name was changed: Abram became Abraham, a father of many nations, and kings would come out of him.
- Father of many nations does not mean everybody is spiritually a child of Abraham. The same promise was passed down the seed line: Jacob's name was changed to Israel and told a nation and a company of nations and kings would come out of his loins.
- The blessing of all men and the covenant were made to rest upon the head of Jacob and divided among the twelve tribes. Kings come out of that seed, including Christ, the King of kings.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 35:10-11](/bible/genesis/35#v10)**
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/35#v10)</sup> And God said unto him, Thy name is Jacob: thy name shall not be called any more Jacob, but Israel shall be thy name: and he called his name Israel.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/35#v11)</sup> And God said unto him, I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins;

    The same covenant words passed to Jacob, named Israel: nations and kings out of his loins.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 44:19-23](/bible/sirach/44#v19)**
> <sup>[19](/bible/sirach/44#v19)</sup> Abraham was a great father of many people: in glory was there none like unto him;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/sirach/44#v20)</sup> Who kept the law of the most High, and was in covenant with him: he established the covenant in his flesh; and when he was proved, he was found faithful.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/sirach/44#v21)</sup> Therefore he assured him by an oath, that he would bless the nations in his seed, and that he would multiply him as the dust of the earth, and exalt his seed as the stars, and cause them to inherit from sea to sea, and from the river unto the utmost part of the land.
>

He acknowledged him in his blessing, and gave him an heritage, and divided his portions; among the twelve tribes did he part them.

    The blessing of all men and the covenant rested on the head of Jacob and was divided among the twelve tribes.

**[Genesis 17:7](/bible/genesis/17#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/17#v7)</sup> And I will establish my covenant between me and thee and thy seed after thee in their generations for an everlasting covenant, to be a God unto thee, and to thy seed after thee.

- The covenant is with Abraham's seed after him, and seed is literal offspring, not a spiritual metaphor. The Most High promised to be a God to Abraham and to his seed after him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Isaiah 41:8](/bible/isaiah/41#v8)**
> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/41#v8)</sup> But thou, Israel, art my servant, Jacob whom I have chosen, the seed of Abraham my friend.

    Israel is the servant, Jacob the chosen, the seed of Abraham; Israel is the nation that carries the seed of Abraham.

**[Genesis 17:10-14](/bible/genesis/17#v10)**

> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/17#v10)</sup> This is my covenant, which ye shall keep, between me and you and thy seed after thee; Every man child among you shall be circumcised.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/17#v11)</sup> And ye shall circumcise the flesh of your foreskin; and it shall be a token of the covenant betwixt me and you.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/17#v12)</sup> And he that is eight days old shall be circumcised among you, every man child in your generations, he that is born in the house, or bought with money of any stranger, which is not of thy seed.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/17#v13)</sup> He that is born in thy house, and he that is bought with thy money, must needs be circumcised: and my covenant shall be in your flesh for an everlasting covenant.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/17#v14)</sup> And the uncircumcised man child whose flesh of his foreskin is not circumcised, that soul shall be cut off from his people; he hath broken my covenant.

- Circumcision is the token of the covenant: every man child circumcised at eight days, and the uncircumcised soul cut off from his people.
- Abraham received circumcision as a seal of the righteousness of the faith he had yet being uncircumcised. He believed and obeyed first, and circumcision sealed it; likewise a brother can come into the truth, get built up in the faith, and then go through with the process.
- It still has to be done. A covenant is a contract with terms; the covenant was confirmed to Jacob for a law, and circumcision is one of the laws within the covenant.

  Precepts:

**[Genesis 17:15-19](/bible/genesis/17#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/17#v15)</sup> And God said unto Abraham, As for Sarai thy wife, thou shalt not call her name Sarai, but Sarah shall her name be.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/17#v16)</sup> And I will bless her, and give thee a son also of her: yea, I will bless her, and she shall be a mother of nations; kings of people shall be of her.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/17#v17)</sup> Then Abraham fell upon his face, and laughed, and said in his heart, Shall a child be born unto him that is an hundred years old? and shall Sarah, that is ninety years old, bear?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/17#v18)</sup> And Abraham said unto God, O that Ishmael might live before thee!
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/17#v19)</sup> And God said, Sarah thy wife shall bear thee a son indeed; and thou shalt call his name Isaac: and I will establish my covenant with him for an everlasting covenant, and with his seed after him.

- Sarai's name was changed to Sarah, and she shall be a mother of nations; those are the same nations of the covenant, and she and Abraham had one child together, Isaac.
- Abraham fell on his face and laughed in his heart: shall a child be born to a hundred year old man, and Sarah bear at ninety? The Most High read his thought and named the child from it: Isaac means he will laugh.

**[Genesis 17:20-21](/bible/genesis/17#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/17#v20)</sup> And as for Ishmael, I have heard thee: Behold, I have blessed him, and will make him fruitful, and will multiply him exceedingly; twelve princes shall he beget, and I will make him a great nation.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/17#v21)</sup> But my covenant will I establish with Isaac, which Sarah shall bear unto thee at this set time in the next year.

- Ishmael received a blessing: fruitful, multiplied exceedingly, twelve princes, a great nation. But the word to Abraham was kings shall come out of thee, and the covenant passes to Isaac: my covenant will I establish with Isaac.
- Not all who are of Abraham's seed are the children; in Isaac shall thy seed be called. Ishmael was born of the flesh, of Sarai's plan, with no word from God ordaining it; the children of the promise are counted for the seed.

  Precepts:
  - **[Romans 9:7-9](/bible/romans/9#v7)**

    In Isaac shall thy seed be called; the children of the flesh are not the children of God, but the children of the promise are counted for the seed.

# Genesis 18: The Promise of Isaac and the Cry of Sodom

**[Genesis 18:9-15](/bible/genesis/18#v9)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/18#v12)</sup> Therefore Sarah laughed within herself, saying, After I am waxed old shall I have pleasure, my lord being old also?

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/18#v13)</sup> And the Lord said unto Abraham, Wherefore did Sarah laugh, saying, Shall I of a surety bear a child, which am old?

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/18#v15)</sup> Then Sarah denied, saying, I laughed not; for she was afraid. And he said, Nay; but thou didst laugh.

- Sarah was barren from the beginning and it had ceased to be with her after the manner of women; she laughed within herself at the promise of a son.
- The Lord answered the thought she never spoke: Wherefore did Sarah laugh? She denied it out of fear, and He said, Nay, but thou didst laugh. The Most High sees what is within the mind.
- Is any thing too hard for the Lord? The barren woman is made a joyful mother of children. Through faith Sarah received strength to conceive when she was past age, and Abraham, as good as dead at a hundred, staggered not at the promise but was strong in faith, fully persuaded that what He had promised He was able to perform.

# Genesis 19: The Destruction of Sodom

**[Genesis 19:4-5](/bible/genesis/19#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/19#v4)</sup> But before they lay down, the men of the city, even the men of Sodom, compassed the house round, both old and young, all the people from every quarter:

- The whole city, both small and great, was given to this wickedness.

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/19#v13)</sup> For we will destroy this place, because the cry of them is waxen great before the face of the Lord; and the Lord hath sent us to destroy it.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/19#v14)</sup> And Lot went out, and spake unto his sons in law, which married his daughters, and said, Up, get you out of this place; for the Lord will destroy this city. But he seemed as one that mocked unto his sons in law.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/19#v15)</sup> And when the morning arose, then the angels hastened Lot, saying, Arise, take thy wife, and thy two daughters, which are here; lest thou be consumed in the iniquity of the city.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/19#v16)</sup> And while he lingered, the men laid hold upon his hand, and upon the hand of his wife, and upon the hand of his two daughters; the Lord being merciful unto him: and they brought him forth, and set him without the city.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/19#v17)</sup> And it came to pass, when they had brought them forth abroad, that he said, Escape for thy life; look not behind thee, neither stay thou in all the plain; escape to the mountain, lest thou be consumed.

- Lot's sons in law took him as one that mocked, and they perished. When the warning comes, treating it as a joke is death.
- While Lot lingered, the angels laid hold on his hand, the Lord being merciful to him. Then the instruction: Escape for thy life; look not behind thee; escape to the mountain.

**[Genesis 19:18-25](/bible/genesis/19#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/19#v18)</sup> And Lot said unto them, Oh, not so, my Lord:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/19#v19)</sup> Behold now, thy servant hath found grace in thy sight, and thou hast magnified thy mercy, which thou hast shewed unto me in saving my life; and I cannot escape to the mountain, lest some evil take me, and I die:
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/19#v20)</sup> Behold now, this city is near to flee unto, and it is a little one: Oh, let me escape thither, (is it not a little one?) and my soul shall live.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/19#v21)</sup> And he said unto him, See, I have accepted thee concerning this thing also, that I will not overthrow this city, for the which thou hast spoken.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/19#v22)</sup> Haste thee, escape thither; for I cannot do any thing till thou be come thither. Therefore the name of the city was called Zoar.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/19#v23)</sup> The sun was risen upon the earth when Lot entered into Zoar.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/19#v24)</sup> Then the Lord rained upon Sodom and upon Gomorrah brimstone and fire from the Lord out of heaven;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/19#v25)</sup> And he overthrew those cities, and all the plain, and all the inhabitants of the cities, and that which grew upon the ground.

- The angel told Lot, Haste thee, for I cannot do any thing till thou be come thither. The destruction waited on the righteous being brought out, the same way the angels in [Revelation 7](/bible/revelation/7) hold the four winds until the servants of God are sealed.
- When Lot entered Zoar, the Lord rained brimstone and fire on Sodom and Gomorrah. Those cities are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire; what was done there on a small scale is done in spiritual Sodom on a greater scale, and the example stands.
- Just Lot was delivered, a righteous man vexed from day to day with the filthy conversation and unlawful deeds of the wicked. A righteous man cannot be at home with that conduct.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jude 1:7](/bible/jude/1#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/jude/1#v7)</sup> Even as Sodom and Gomorrha, and the cities about them in like manner, giving themselves over to fornication, and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.

    Sodom and Gomorrah, giving themselves to fornication and going after strange flesh, are set forth for an example, suffering the vengeance of eternal fire.
  - **[2 Peter 2:6-8](/bible/2-peter/2#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-peter/2#v6)</sup> And turning the cities of Sodom and Gomorrha into ashes condemned them with an overthrow, making them an ensample unto those that after should live ungodly;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-peter/2#v7)</sup> And delivered just Lot, vexed with the filthy conversation of the wicked:
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-peter/2#v8)</sup> (For that righteous man dwelling among them, in seeing and hearing, vexed his righteous soul from day to day with their unlawful deeds;)

    The cities were made an example unto those that after should live ungodly, and just Lot was delivered.

**[Genesis 19:26](/bible/genesis/19#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/19#v26)</sup> But his wife looked back from behind him, and she became a pillar of salt.

- Lot's wife looked back, against the instruction, and became a pillar of salt: a standing monument of an unbelieving soul.
- Remember Lot's wife: three words among the weightiest in the scriptures. As it was in the days of Lot, they ate, drank, bought, sold, planted, and builded until the day the fire fell; so shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed. If you find yourself reminiscing after the old life, remember Lot's wife.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 10:6-7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/10#v6)**
> <sup>[6](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/10#v6)</sup> When the ungodly perished, she delivered the righteous man, who fled from the fire which fell down upon the five cities.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/10#v7)</sup> Of whose wickedness even to this day the waste land that smoketh is a testimony, and plants bearing fruit that never come to ripeness: and a standing pillar of salt is a monument of an unbelieving soul.

    The standing pillar of salt is a monument of an unbelieving soul; the wasteland that smoketh is a testimony to this day.
  - **[Luke 17:28-32](/bible/luke/17#v28)**
> <sup>[28](/bible/luke/17#v28)</sup> Likewise also as it was in the days of Lot; they did eat, they drank, they bought, they sold, they planted, they builded;
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/luke/17#v29)</sup> But the same day that Lot went out of Sodom it rained fire and brimstone from heaven, and destroyed them all.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/17#v30)</sup> Even thus shall it be in the day when the Son of man is revealed.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/luke/17#v31)</sup> In that day, he which shall be upon the housetop, and his stuff in the house, let him not come down to take it away: and he that is in the field, let him likewise not return back.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/luke/17#v32)</sup> Remember Lot’s wife.

    As it was in the days of Lot, so shall it be in the day the Son of man is revealed; remember Lot's wife.

**[Genesis 19:30-38](/bible/genesis/19#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/19#v30)</sup> And Lot went up out of Zoar, and dwelt in the mountain, and his two daughters with him; for he feared to dwell in Zoar: and he dwelt in a cave, he and his two daughters.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/19#v31)</sup> And the firstborn said unto the younger, Our father is old, and there is not a man in the earth to come in unto us after the manner of all the earth:
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/19#v32)</sup> Come, let us make our father drink wine, and we will lie with him, that we may preserve seed of our father.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/genesis/19#v33)</sup> And they made their father drink wine that night: and the firstborn went in, and lay with her father; and he perceived not when she lay down, nor when she arose.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/19#v34)</sup> And it came to pass on the morrow, that the firstborn said unto the younger, Behold, I lay yesternight with my father: let us make him drink wine this night also; and go thou in, and lie with him, that we may preserve seed of our father.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/genesis/19#v35)</sup> And they made their father drink wine that night also: and the younger arose, and lay with him; and he perceived not when she lay down, nor when she arose.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/genesis/19#v36)</sup> Thus were both the daughters of Lot with child by their father.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/genesis/19#v37)</sup> And the firstborn bare a son, and called his name Moab: the same is the father of the Moabites unto this day.
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/genesis/19#v38)</sup> And the younger, she also bare a son, and called his name Ben–ammi: the same is the father of the children of Ammon unto this day.

- Lot's daughters thought Sodom had been the whole world and no man was left on the earth to give them seed, so they made their father drink wine and lay with him; he perceived not when they lay down nor when they arose.
- Do not take any wayward idea from this: uncovering the nakedness of thy father is against the laws of the Most High. It was unlawful then and it is unlawful now.
- The firstborn bare Moab, father of the Moabites, and the younger bare Benammi, father of the Ammonites, unto this day. In this teaching those nations are identified with the peoples known today as the Chinese and the Japanese.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 18:7](/bible/leviticus/18#v7)**
> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/18#v7)</sup> The nakedness of thy father, or the nakedness of thy mother, shalt thou not uncover: she is thy mother; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.

    The nakedness of thy father and mother thou shalt not uncover; incest is against the law of the Most High.

# Genesis 20: Sarah and Abimelech

Read the chapter → [Genesis 20](/bible/genesis/20)

**[Genesis 20:1-2](/bible/genesis/20#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/20#v1)</sup> And Abraham journeyed from thence toward the south country, and dwelled between Kadesh and Shur, and sojourned in Gerar.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/20#v2)</sup> And Abraham said of Sarah his wife, She is my sister: and Abimelech king of Gerar sent, and took Sarah.

- Abraham said again, She is my sister, as he had done entering Egypt, for fear the men of the place would kill him for his wife's sake; Sarah was still that fair to look upon even at ninety.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 12:11-13](/bible/genesis/12#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/12#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, when he was come near to enter into Egypt, that he said unto Sarai his wife, Behold now, I know that thou art a fair woman to look upon:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/12#v12)</sup> Therefore it shall come to pass, when the Egyptians shall see thee, that they shall say, This is his wife: and they will kill me, but they will save thee alive.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/12#v13)</sup> Say, I pray thee, thou art my sister: that it may be well with me for thy sake; and my soul shall live because of thee.

    The saying began at Egypt: they will kill me, but save thee alive; say thou art my sister.

**[Genesis 20:3-7](/bible/genesis/20#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/20#v3)</sup> But God came to Abimelech in a dream by night, and said to him, Behold, thou art but a dead man, for the woman which thou hast taken; for she is a man’s wife.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/20#v4)</sup> But Abimelech had not come near her: and he said, Lord, wilt thou slay also a righteous nation?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/20#v5)</sup> Said he not unto me, She is my sister? and she, even she herself said, He is my brother: in the integrity of my heart and innocency of my hands have I done this.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/20#v6)</sup> And God said unto him in a dream, Yea, I know that thou didst this in the integrity of thy heart; for I also withheld thee from sinning against me: therefore suffered I thee not to touch her.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/20#v7)</sup> Now therefore restore the man his wife; for he is a prophet, and he shall pray for thee, and thou shalt live: and if thou restore her not, know thou that thou shalt surely die, thou, and all that are thine.

- God came to Abimelech in a dream: Behold, thou art but a dead man, for the woman is a man's wife. Adultery was sin in Genesis, way before [Exodus 20:14](/bible/exodus/20#v14); the Most High said, I withheld thee from sinning against me.
- Abimelech had acted in the integrity of his heart, and the Most High withheld him from touching her. Restore the man his wife, for he is a prophet, and he shall pray for thee.

**[Genesis 20:9-13](/bible/genesis/20#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/20#v9)</sup> Then Abimelech called Abraham, and said unto him, What hast thou done unto us? and what have I offended thee, that thou hast brought on me and on my kingdom a great sin? thou hast done deeds unto me that ought not to be done.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/20#v10)</sup> And Abimelech said unto Abraham, What sawest thou, that thou hast done this thing?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/20#v11)</sup> And Abraham said, Because I thought, Surely the fear of God is not in this place; and they will slay me for my wife’s sake.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/20#v12)</sup> And yet indeed she is my sister; she is the daughter of my father, but not the daughter of my mother; and she became my wife.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/20#v13)</sup> And it came to pass, when God caused me to wander from my father’s house, that I said unto her, This is thy kindness which thou shalt shew unto me; at every place whither we shall come, say of me, He is my brother.

- Abimelech called it what it is: a great sin. Adultery is one of the weightier matters; there is no animal sacrifice that atones for it.
- Abraham thought, Surely the fear of God is not in this place. And technically she was his sister: the daughter of his father but not of his mother, who became his wife; the sister saying was their agreement in every strange land.

**[Genesis 20:17-18](/bible/genesis/20#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/20#v17)</sup> So Abraham prayed unto God: and God healed Abimelech, and his wife, and his maidservants; and they bare children.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/20#v18)</sup> For the Lord had fast closed up all the wombs of the house of Abimelech, because of Sarah Abraham’s wife.

- Behind the scenes the Lord had fast closed up all the wombs of the house of Abimelech because of Sarah; when Abraham prayed, God healed them.
- The same happened with Pharaoh before. The Most High plagues a household over the prophet's wife; He does not play about that.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 12:17](/bible/genesis/12#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/12#v17)</sup> And the Lord plagued Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai Abram’s wife.

    The Lord plagued Pharaoh and his house with great plagues because of Sarai; it happened both times she was taken.
