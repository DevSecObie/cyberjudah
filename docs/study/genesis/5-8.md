## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 5](/bible/genesis/5) through 8, read straight through, with the key points opened after the reading. The 365 reading is to give faith through hearing the word of the Most High.

**[Romans 10:17](/bible/romans/10#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/10#v17)</sup> So then faith cometh by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.

- The daily reading of the scriptures gives faith through hearing the word of the Most High.

**[Genesis 5:1-2](/bible/genesis/5#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/5#v1)</sup> This is the book of the generations of Adam. In the day that God created man, in the likeness of God made he him;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/5#v2)</sup> Male and female created he them; and blessed them, and called their name Adam, in the day when they were created.

- The words made he him speak of the first Adam. This Adam was made in the likeness of God, a direct descendant of the Most High.
- Male and female created he them, and called their name Adam: they were not that Adam; these were the other people on the earth. When the Most High creates a fish He does not create only a salmon; there is mackerel and tuna and all types of fish. In the same way, He created Adam and He created the other people.
- Chapter 5 is the direct lineage of Adam. That lineage is the sons of God you read of in chapter 6, and the later chapters of Genesis tie the Israelites directly to the lineage of Adam through Shem.
- When Christ said, Ye are gods, He was saying we come from that line of Adam.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 1:26-27](/bible/genesis/1#v26)**
    > <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/1#v26)</sup> And God said, Let us make man in our image, after our likeness: and let them have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over the cattle, and over all the earth, and over every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth.
    >
    > <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/1#v27)</sup> So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.

    The man made in our image, created he him, is the first Adam; male and female created he them is the other people.
  - **[Luke 3:36-38](/bible/luke/3#v36)**
    > <sup>[36](/bible/luke/3#v36)</sup> Which was the son of Cainan, which was the son of Arphaxad, which was the son of Sem, which was the son of Noe, which was the son of Lamech,
    >
    > <sup>[37](/bible/luke/3#v37)</sup> Which was the son of Mathusala, which was the son of Enoch, which was the son of Jared, which was the son of Maleleel, which was the son of Cainan,
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/luke/3#v38)</sup> Which was the son of Enos, which was the son of Seth, which was the son of Adam, which was the son of God.

    Christ's lineage on his mother's side runs back through Seth to Adam, the son of God.

**[Genesis 5:24](/bible/genesis/5#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/5#v24)</sup> And Enoch walked with God: and he was not; for God took him.

- Enoch walked with God and was not: the Most High took him up, and Enoch did not taste death, the same way Elijah did not taste death.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Kings 2:9-11](/bible/2-kings/2#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-kings/2#v9)</sup> And it came to pass, when they were gone over, that Elijah said unto Elisha, Ask what I shall do for thee, before I be taken away from thee. And Elisha said, I pray thee, let a double portion of thy spirit be upon me.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/2#v10)</sup> And he said, Thou hast asked a hard thing: nevertheless, if thou see me when I am taken from thee, it shall be so unto thee; but if not, it shall not be so.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/2#v11)</sup> And it came to pass, as they still went on, and talked, that, behold, there appeared a chariot of fire, and horses of fire, and parted them both asunder; and Elijah went up by a whirlwind into heaven.

    Elijah was taken up by a whirlwind into heaven; that is what it means that God took Enoch.
  - **[Acts 1:9](/bible/acts/1#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/acts/1#v9)</sup> And when he had spoken these things, while they beheld, he was taken up; and a cloud received him out of their sight.

    Christ was taken up and received in a cloud; taken up means received by the Most High.

**[Genesis 5:32](/bible/genesis/5#v32)**

> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/5#v32)</sup> And Noah was five hundred years old: and Noah begat Shem, Ham, and Japheth.

- Noah was five hundred years old here, and he was six hundred when the flood came ([Genesis 7:6](/bible/genesis/7#v6)), so Noah preached for around a hundred years, give or take.

# Genesis 6: The Sons of God and the Preparing of the Ark

Read the chapter → [Genesis 6](/bible/genesis/6)

**[Genesis 6:1-2](/bible/genesis/6#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/6#v1)</sup> And it came to pass, when men began to multiply on the face of the earth, and daughters were born unto them,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/6#v2)</sup> That the sons of God saw the daughters of men that they were fair; and they took them wives of all which they chose.

- The men who began to multiply on the face of the earth were of the other nations; they were not the direct descendants of Adam. The sons of God are the lineage of Adam laid out in chapter 5; that is the distinction between the sons of God and the daughters of men.
- The law against marrying into the other nations began here in Genesis. It was already known, and Moses had to reiterate that same law to the children of Israel when they came over Jordan.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 7:3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them; thy daughter thou shalt not give unto his son, nor his daughter shalt thou take unto thy son.

    Moses reiterated to the sons of God the law against taking wives of the other nations.

**[Genesis 6:3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/6#v3)</sup> And the Lord said, My spirit shall not always strive with man, for that he also is flesh: yet his days shall be an hundred and twenty years.

- Here the Most High shortened the lifespan of man to a hundred and twenty years. In chapter 5 the forefathers lived seven, eight, and nine hundred years.

**[Genesis 6:4](/bible/genesis/6#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/6#v4)</sup> There were giants in the earth in those days; and also after that, when the sons of God came in unto the daughters of men, and they bare children to them, the same became mighty men which were of old, men of renown.

- The giants in the earth came from the sons of God intermingling with the other nations, and the children born to them became mighty men of renown.
- The angels which kept not their first estate are these same men of [Genesis 6:4](/bible/genesis/6#v4); they came from the lineage of Adam, and their first estate was sons of God. Another word for angel is messenger.

  Precepts:
  - **[Jude 1:6](/bible/jude/1#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/jude/1#v6)</sup> And the angels which kept not their first estate, but left their own habitation, he hath reserved in everlasting chains under darkness unto the judgment of the great day.

    The angels left their first estate as sons of God, and the Most High reserved them unto judgment.

**[Genesis 6:8-9](/bible/genesis/6#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/6#v8)</sup> But Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/6#v9)</sup> These are the generations of Noah: Noah was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.

- Noah found grace in the eyes of the Lord; he was a just man and perfect in his generations, and Noah walked with God.
- Without Noah none of us would be here; Shem, Ham, and Japheth repopulated the earth after the flood.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 44:17-18](/bible/sirach/44#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/sirach/44#v17)</sup> Noah was found perfect and righteous; in the time of wrath he was taken in exchange for the world; therefore was he left as a remnant unto the earth, when the flood came.
    >
    > <sup>[18](/bible/sirach/44#v18)</sup> An everlasting covenant was made with him, that all flesh should perish no more by the flood.

    Noah was found perfect and righteous and was left as a remnant unto the earth when the flood came.

**[Genesis 6:14](/bible/genesis/6#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/6#v14)</sup> Make thee an ark of gopher wood; rooms shalt thou make in the ark, and shalt pitch it within and without with pitch.

- The ark was a literal boat, made of gopher wood.

**[Genesis 6:21](/bible/genesis/6#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/6#v21)</sup> And take thou unto thee of all food that is eaten, and thou shalt gather it to thee; and it shall be for food for thee, and for them.

- At this time the animals were vegetarian; that is why the Most High explained to Noah to gather food for himself, for his family, and for the animals, and why the animals did not eat one another on the ark.

**[Genesis 6:22](/bible/genesis/6#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/6#v22)</sup> Thus did Noah; according to all that God commanded him, so did he.

- Noah did all that the Most High commanded him by faith. Up to that time rain had never been seen; it takes faith to build an ark for a thing that has never happened.
- While the ark was preparing, Noah was a preacher of righteousness, telling his people to repent, and the longsuffering of the Most High waited for them to repent.
- Eight souls were saved by water; the ones that were actually in the water died, so this is not talking about getting baptized.
- As Noah prepared the ark to the saving of his house, we must prepare our households for when Christ comes back; that takes the same faith. In the days of Noah they were eating, drinking, and marrying until Noah entered the ark and there was no more chance to repent; so shall the coming of the Son of man be. We have to put on the spirit of Noah.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hebrews 11:1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/hebrews/11#v1)</sup> Now faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not seen.

    Faith is the evidence of things not seen; with Noah the thing not seen was rain.
  - **[Hebrews 11:6-7](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)**
    > <sup>[6](/bible/hebrews/11#v6)</sup> But without faith it is impossible to please him: for he that cometh to God must believe that he is, and that he is a rewarder of them that diligently seek him.
    >
    > <sup>[7](/bible/hebrews/11#v7)</sup> By faith Noah, being warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear, prepared an ark to the saving of his house; by the which he condemned the world, and became heir of the righteousness which is by faith.

    By faith Noah, warned of God of things not seen as yet, moved with fear and prepared an ark to the saving of his house.
  - **[1 Peter 3:20](/bible/1-peter/3#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/1-peter/3#v20)</sup> Which sometime were disobedient, when once the longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah, while the ark was a preparing, wherein few, that is, eight souls were saved by water.

    The longsuffering of God waited in the days of Noah while the ark was preparing, and eight souls were saved by water.
  - **[2 Peter 2:5](/bible/2-peter/2#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-peter/2#v5)</sup> And spared not the old world, but saved Noah the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness, bringing in the flood upon the world of the ungodly;

    Noah was the eighth person, a preacher of righteousness.
  - **[Matthew 24:37-39](/bible/matthew/24#v37)**
    > <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/24#v37)</sup> But as the days of Noe were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.
    >
    > <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/24#v38)</sup> For as in the days that were before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noe entered into the ark,
    >
    > <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/24#v39)</sup> And knew not until the flood came, and took them all away; so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.

    As the days of Noah were, so shall also the coming of the Son of man be.

# Genesis 7: The First Rain and the Flood

Read the chapter → [Genesis 7](/bible/genesis/7)

**[Genesis 7:2-3](/bible/genesis/7#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/7#v2)</sup> Of every clean beast thou shalt take to thee by sevens, the male and his female: and of beasts that are not clean by two, the male and his female.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/7#v3)</sup> Of fowls also of the air by sevens, the male and the female; to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth.

- The dietary law was not yet instituted, but the Most High had already defined what was a clean beast and what was an unclean beast, all the way back in Genesis; Moses reiterated that law to the children of Israel.
- Today this gets confused with the doctrine that says I can eat all things through Christ who strengthens me, and that is not true; the laws were instituted for us to discern between the unclean and the clean.
- The beasts went in male and female to keep seed alive upon the face of all the earth, so that they could repopulate once the earth dried up; nature itself tells you what we should and should not be doing.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 11:45-47](/bible/leviticus/11#v45)**
    > <sup>[45](/bible/leviticus/11#v45)</sup> For I am the Lord that bringeth you up out of the land of Egypt, to be your God: ye shall therefore be holy, for I am holy.
    >
    > <sup>[46](/bible/leviticus/11#v46)</sup> This is the law of the beasts, and of the fowl, and of every living creature that moveth in the waters, and of every creature that creepeth upon the earth:
    >
    > <sup>[47](/bible/leviticus/11#v47)</sup> To make a difference between the unclean and the clean, and between the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.

    The law of the beasts makes a difference between the unclean and the clean, the beast that may be eaten and the beast that may not be eaten.
  - **[Ezekiel 44:23](/bible/ezekiel/44#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/ezekiel/44#v23)</sup> And they shall teach my people the difference between the holy and profane, and cause them to discern between the unclean and the clean.

    The people are taught to discern between the unclean and the clean; that is what the laws were instituted for.

**[Genesis 7:4](/bible/genesis/7#v4)**

> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/7#v4)</sup> For yet seven days, and I will cause it to rain upon the earth forty days and forty nights; and every living substance that I have made will I destroy from off the face of the earth.

- Up until this point it had never rained on the earth; a mist went up from the earth and watered the ground. Understand the faith Noah had to have, being told to build an ark because it was going to rain, a thing that had never happened.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 2:5-6](/bible/genesis/2#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/2#v5)</sup> And every plant of the field before it was in the earth, and every herb of the field before it grew: for the Lord God had not caused it to rain upon the earth, and there was not a man to till the ground.
    >
    > <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/2#v6)</sup> But there went up a mist from the earth, and watered the whole face of the ground.

    The Most High had not caused it to rain upon the earth; a mist watered the whole face of the ground.

**[Genesis 7:11](/bible/genesis/7#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/7#v11)</sup> In the six hundredth year of Noah’s life, in the second month, the seventeenth day of the month, the same day were all the fountains of the great deep broken up, and the windows of heaven were opened.

- The great deep is the seas, the rivers, and the oceans. The windows of heaven are the sky, the firmament, and their opening is the rain.
- This is the first time it ever rained on the earth, and when the rain came it came with destruction.

**[Genesis 7:21-23](/bible/genesis/7#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/7#v21)</sup> And all flesh died that moved upon the earth, both of fowl, and of cattle, and of beast, and of every creeping thing that creepeth upon the earth, and every man:
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/7#v22)</sup> All in whose nostrils was the breath of life, of all that was in the dry land, died.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/7#v23)</sup> And every living substance was destroyed which was upon the face of the ground, both man, and cattle, and the creeping things, and the fowl of the heaven; and they were destroyed from the earth: and Noah only remained alive, and they that were with him in the ark.

- This was the first time the Most High destroyed the earth for the wickedness that was being done. The flood came because they despised the commandments; destruction came because of sin.
- Of them that dwelt in the world only Noah and his household were left, of whom came all righteous men: through Shem's seed to Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, and through their line the Israelites.
- The breath of life is twofold. One is the commandments, which were given to Adam; the other is the breathing life, the oxygen. Here everything on the dry land that breathed died; the fish were not touched.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 3:8-11](/bible/2-esdras/3#v8)**
    > <sup>[8](/bible/2-esdras/3#v8)</sup> And every people walked after their own will, and did wonderful things before thee, and despised thy commandments.
    >
    > <sup>[9](/bible/2-esdras/3#v9)</sup> And again in process of time thou broughtest the flood upon those that dwelt in the world, and destroyedst them.
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/2-esdras/3#v10)</sup> And it came to pass in every of them, that as death was to Adam, so was the flood to these.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/2-esdras/3#v11)</sup> Nevertheless one of them thou leftest, namely, Noah with his household, of whom came all righteous men.

    The flood came upon them that despised the commandments, and Noah alone was left with his household, of whom came all righteous men.
  - **[2 Esdras 3:5](/bible/2-esdras/3#v5)**
    > <sup>[5](/bible/2-esdras/3#v5)</sup> And gavest a body unto Adam without soul, which was the workmanship of thine hands, and didst breathe into him the breath of life, and he was made living before thee.

    The Most High breathed into Adam the breath of life; that breath is the commandments.
  - **[Proverbs 7:2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/7#v2)</sup> Keep my commandments, and live; and my law as the apple of thine eye.

    Keep my commandments, and live; the commandments are the breath of life.

# Genesis 8: The Earth Dried and the Covenant

Read the chapter → [Genesis 8](/bible/genesis/8)

**[Genesis 8:2](/bible/genesis/8#v2)**

> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/8#v2)</sup> The fountains also of the deep and the windows of heaven were stopped, and the rain from heaven was restrained;

- The windows of heaven being stopped and the rain from heaven being restrained shows you that the windows of heaven refer to the rain.

**[Genesis 8:20-22](/bible/genesis/8#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/8#v20)</sup> And Noah builded an altar unto the Lord; and took of every clean beast, and of every clean fowl, and offered burnt offerings on the altar.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/8#v21)</sup> And the Lord smelled a sweet savour; and the Lord said in his heart, I will not again curse the ground any more for man’s sake; for the imagination of man’s heart is evil from his youth; neither will I again smite any more every thing living, as I have done.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/8#v22)</sup> While the earth remaineth, seedtime and harvest, and cold and heat, and summer and winter, and day and night shall not cease.

- Noah built an altar and offered burnt offerings of every clean beast and every clean fowl, and the Most High smelled a sweet savour and said He would not again curse the ground any more for man's sake.
- An everlasting covenant was made with Noah that all flesh should perish no more by a flood. Before it was water; the next time it is going to be fire, so let us get our minds right.
