**[Genesis 34:1-2](/bible/genesis/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/34#v1)</sup> And Dinah the daughter of Leah, which she bare unto Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/34#v2)</sup> And when Shechem the son of Hamor the Hivite, prince of the country, saw her, he took her, and lay with her, and defiled her.

- Dinah, Jacob's only daughter, went out to see the daughters of the land without supervision, and Shechem the son of Hamor took her, and lay with her, and defiled her. This was stranger rape.
- The lesson stands for every generation: keep watch over the daughters. Have a care of their body; do not give overmuch liberty; the father wakes for the daughter and the care for her takes away sleep. Everything the children do should have supervision, especially in this age.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 7:24](/bible/sirach/7#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/7#v24)</sup> Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.

    Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 26:10](/bible/sirach/26#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/26#v10)</sup> If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.

    If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 42:9-11](/bible/sirach/42#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/42#v9)</sup> The father waketh for the daughter, when no man knoweth; and the care for her taketh away sleep: when she is young, lest she pass away the flower of her age; and being married, lest she should be hated:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/42#v10)</sup> In her virginity, lest she should be defiled and gotten with child in her father’s house; and having an husband, lest she should misbehave herself; and when she is married, lest she should be barren.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/42#v11)</sup> Keep a sure watch over a shameless daughter, lest she make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies, and a byword in the city, and a reproach among the people, and make thee ashamed before the multitude.

    The father waketh for the daughter when no man knoweth; keep a sure watch, lest she be defiled and made a reproach.

**[Genesis 34:3-7](/bible/genesis/34#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/34#v3)</sup> And his soul clave unto Dinah the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the damsel, and spake kindly unto the damsel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/34#v4)</sup> And Shechem spake unto his father Hamor, saying, Get me this damsel to wife.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/34#v5)</sup> And Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter: now his sons were with his cattle in the field: and Jacob held his peace until they were come.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/34#v6)</sup> And Hamor the father of Shechem went out unto Jacob to commune with him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/34#v7)</sup> And the sons of Jacob came out of the field when they heard it: and the men were grieved, and they were very wroth, because he had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob’s daughter; which thing ought not to be done.

- Shechem's soul clave to Dinah after he defiled her; he did it backwards, taking first and loving after. That is the world's pattern, not righteousness.
- When Jacob's sons heard, the men were grieved and very wroth, because he had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob's daughter, which thing ought not to be done. The laws were in place and kept long before they were written in stone at Sinai.

**[Genesis 34:8-10](/bible/genesis/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/34#v8)</sup> And Hamor communed with them, saying, The soul of my son Shechem longeth for your daughter: I pray you give her him to wife.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/34#v9)</sup> And make ye marriages with us, and give your daughters unto us, and take our daughters unto you.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/34#v10)</sup> And ye shall dwell with us: and the land shall be before you; dwell and trade ye therein, and get you possessions therein.

- Hamor proposed the exact opposite of the law: make ye marriages with us, give your daughters unto us, and take our daughters unto you, and dwell with us, and become one people. Assimilation and intermarriage is the offer the nations always make, and it is the offer the law forbids.

**[Genesis 34:13-24](/bible/genesis/34#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/34#v13)</sup> And the sons of Jacob answered Shechem and Hamor his father deceitfully, and said, because he had defiled Dinah their sister:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/34#v14)</sup> And they said unto them, We cannot do this thing, to give our sister to one that is uncircumcised; for that were a reproach unto us:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/34#v15)</sup> But in this will we consent unto you: If ye will be as we be, that every male of you be circumcised;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/34#v16)</sup> Then will we give our daughters unto you, and we will take your daughters to us, and we will dwell with you, and we will become one people.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/34#v17)</sup> But if ye will not hearken unto us, to be circumcised; then will we take our daughter, and we will be gone.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/34#v18)</sup> And their words pleased Hamor, and Shechem Hamor’s son.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/34#v19)</sup> And the young man deferred not to do the thing, because he had delight in Jacob’s daughter: and he was more honourable than all the house of his father.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/34#v20)</sup> And Hamor and Shechem his son came unto the gate of their city, and communed with the men of their city, saying,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/34#v21)</sup> These men are peaceable with us; therefore let them dwell in the land, and trade therein; for the land, behold, it is large enough for them; let us take their daughters to us for wives, and let us give them our daughters.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/34#v22)</sup> Only herein will the men consent unto us for to dwell with us, to be one people, if every male among us be circumcised, as they are circumcised.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/34#v23)</sup> Shall not their cattle and their substance and every beast of theirs be ours? only let us consent unto them, and they will dwell with us.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/34#v24)</sup> And unto Hamor and unto Shechem his son hearkened all that went out of the gate of his city; and every male was circumcised, all that went out of the gate of his city.

- The sons of Jacob answered deceitfully: every male of the city must be circumcised before they would consent. Hamor and Shechem persuaded the whole city, reasoning that the wealth of Jacob's house would become theirs; every male was circumcised.

**[Genesis 34:25-29](/bible/genesis/34#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/34#v25)</sup> And it came to pass on the third day, when they were sore, that two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brethren, took each man his sword, and came upon the city boldly, and slew all the males.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/34#v26)</sup> And they slew Hamor and Shechem his son with the edge of the sword, and took Dinah out of Shechem’s house, and went out.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/34#v27)</sup> The sons of Jacob came upon the slain, and spoiled the city, because they had defiled their sister.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/34#v28)</sup> They took their sheep, and their oxen, and their asses, and that which was in the city, and that which was in the field,
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/34#v29)</sup> And all their wealth, and all their little ones, and their wives took they captive, and spoiled even all that was in the house.

- On the third day, when they were sore, two of the sons, Simeon and Levi, took each man his sword, came upon the city boldly, and slew all the males; they took Dinah out, spoiled the city, and carried away the substance and the households.
- Two men do not take a city alone. The sword of Simeon was given of God to take vengeance of the strangers that defiled the maid; they were moved with the zeal of the Most High, and He was behind the judgment.

    O Lord God of my father Simeon, to whom thou gavest a sword to take vengeance of the strangers who defiled the maid; the vengeance was ordained of God, and they were moved with His zeal.

**[Genesis 34:30-31](/bible/genesis/34#v30)**

> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/34#v30)</sup> And Jacob said to Simeon and Levi, Ye have troubled me to make me to stink among the inhabitants of the land, among the Canaanites and the Perizzites: and I being few in number, they shall gather themselves together against me, and slay me; and I shall be destroyed, I and my house.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/34#v31)</sup> And they said, Should he deal with our sister as with an harlot?

- Jacob was troubled: ye have made me to stink among the inhabitants of the land, and I being few in number. He feared the nations round about gathering against his house.
- The brothers answered, Should he deal with our sister as with an harlot? There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel; the daughters of Israel are not to be handled so, and the sons of Israel did not go for it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Deuteronomy 23:17](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v17)**
    > <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v17)</sup> There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel, nor a sodomite of the sons of Israel.

    There shall be no whore of the daughters of Israel; the answer of Simeon and Levi stood on that law.
