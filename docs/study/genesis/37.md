**[Genesis 37:1-2](/bible/genesis/37#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/37#v1)</sup> And Jacob dwelt in the land wherein his father was a stranger, in the land of Canaan.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/37#v2)</sup> These are the generations of Jacob. Joseph, being seventeen years old, was feeding the flock with his brethren; and the lad was with the sons of Bilhah, and with the sons of Zilpah, his father’s wives: and Joseph brought unto his father their evil report.

- Joseph brought unto his father the evil report of his brethren. That was law, not tale bearing: if a soul hear swearing and be a witness and do not utter it, he shall bear his iniquity. The law was already around, and Joseph kept it.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 5:1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/leviticus/5#v1)</sup> And if a soul sin, and hear the voice of swearing, and is a witness, whether he hath seen or known of it; if he do not utter it, then he shall bear his iniquity.

    A witness that does not utter what he has seen bears his own iniquity; Joseph uttered the evil report according to that law.

**[Genesis 37:3-4](/bible/genesis/37#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/37#v3)</sup> Now Israel loved Joseph more than all his children, because he was the son of his old age: and he made him a coat of many colours.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/37#v4)</sup> And when his brethren saw that their father loved him more than all his brethren, they hated him, and could not speak peaceably unto him.

- Israel loved Joseph more than all his children, the son of his old age, and made him a coat of many colours; and his brethren hated him and could not speak peaceably unto him.
- That is real hatred among brothers, and the same spirit is on Israel today, where a brother cannot even receive a shalom in peace.

**[Genesis 37:5-8](/bible/genesis/37#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/37#v5)</sup> And Joseph dreamed a dream, and he told it his brethren: and they hated him yet the more.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/37#v6)</sup> And he said unto them, Hear, I pray you, this dream which I have dreamed:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/37#v7)</sup> For, behold, we were binding sheaves in the field, and, lo, my sheaf arose, and also stood upright; and, behold, your sheaves stood round about, and made obeisance to my sheaf.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/37#v8)</sup> And his brethren said to him, Shalt thou indeed reign over us? or shalt thou indeed have dominion over us? And they hated him yet the more for his dreams, and for his words.

- Joseph dreamed, and the sheaves of his brethren made obeisance to his sheaf. The Lord speaks by the prophets in visions and similitudes; the vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, as the likeness of a face to a face.
- His brethren understood the dream, that he should reign over them, and hated him yet the more for his dreams and for his words.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 12:10](/bible/hosea/12#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/hosea/12#v10)</sup> I have also spoken by the prophets, and I have multiplied visions, and used similitudes, by the ministry of the prophets.

    The Most High multiplies visions and uses similitudes by the ministry of the prophets.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 34:3](/bible/sirach/34#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/34#v3)</sup> The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another, even as the likeness of a face to a face.

    The vision of dreams is the resemblance of one thing to another; the sheaf stood for Joseph and the sheaves for his brethren.

**[Genesis 37:9-10](/bible/genesis/37#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/37#v9)</sup> And he dreamed yet another dream, and told it his brethren, and said, Behold, I have dreamed a dream more; and, behold, the sun and the moon and the eleven stars made obeisance to me.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/37#v10)</sup> And he told it to his father, and to his brethren: and his father rebuked him, and said unto him, What is this dream that thou hast dreamed? Shall I and thy mother and thy brethren indeed come to bow down ourselves to thee to the earth?

- In the second dream the sun, the moon, and the eleven stars made obeisance to Joseph. Jacob interpreted it at once: shall I and thy mother and thy brethren indeed come to bow down ourselves to thee?
- The same figures mark Israel: the woman clothed with the sun with a crown of twelve stars is the daughter of Zion, the nation of Israel, and the twelve stars are the tribes.
- The dream came true. In the famine the whole house of Jacob came down to Joseph in Egypt, and he placed them in the best of the land and nourished them with bread; they bowed to the one the Most High had set over them.

  Precepts:
  - **[Revelation 12:1](/bible/revelation/12#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/revelation/12#v1)</sup> And there appeared a great wonder in heaven; a woman clothed with the sun, and the moon under her feet, and upon her head a crown of twelve stars:

    The woman clothed with the sun, with the moon under her feet and a crown of twelve stars, figures Israel and her tribes.
  - **[Jeremiah 6:2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)**
    > <sup>[2](/bible/jeremiah/6#v2)</sup> I have likened the daughter of Zion to a comely and delicate woman.

    The daughter of Zion is likened to a comely and delicate woman; the woman of the vision is Israel.
  - **[Genesis 47:11-12](/bible/genesis/47#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/47#v11)</sup> And Joseph placed his father and his brethren, and gave them a possession in the land of Egypt, in the best of the land, in the land of Rameses, as Pharaoh had commanded.
    >
    > <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/47#v12)</sup> And Joseph nourished his father, and his brethren, and all his father’s household, with bread, according to their families.

    Joseph placed his father and his brethren in the best of the land and nourished them; the dream was fulfilled.

**[Genesis 37:11](/bible/genesis/37#v11)**

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/37#v11)</sup> And his brethren envied him; but his father observed the saying.

- His brethren envied him, but his father observed the saying. Envy consumes a man, and such a man shall have no fellowship with wisdom; when envy takes over, wisdom leaves.

  Precepts:
  - **[Wisdom of Solomon 6:23](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v23)**
    > <sup>[23](/bible/wisdom-of-solomon/6#v23)</sup> Neither will I go with consuming envy; for such a man shall have no fellowship with wisdom.

    Consuming envy has no fellowship with wisdom; the brothers' envy put wisdom away from them.

**[Genesis 37:18-20](/bible/genesis/37#v18)**

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/37#v18)</sup> And when they saw him afar off, even before he came near unto them, they conspired against him to slay him.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/37#v19)</sup> And they said one to another, Behold, this dreamer cometh.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/37#v20)</sup> Come now therefore, and let us slay him, and cast him into some pit, and we will say, Some evil beast hath devoured him: and we shall see what will become of his dreams.

- When they saw him afar off, they conspired against him to slay him: Behold, this dreamer cometh. That is the same spirit Cain had, and the same spirit Esau had.
- Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer. Inner hatred, given room, plots death; that is why the law says thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart.

  Precepts:
  - **[1 John 3:15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)**
    > <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)</sup> Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

    Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer, and no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

**[Genesis 37:21-28](/bible/genesis/37#v21)**

> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/37#v21)</sup> And Reuben heard it, and he delivered him out of their hands; and said, Let us not kill him.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/37#v22)</sup> And Reuben said unto them, Shed no blood, but cast him into this pit that is in the wilderness, and lay no hand upon him; that he might rid him out of their hands, to deliver him to his father again.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/37#v23)</sup> And it came to pass, when Joseph was come unto his brethren, that they stript Joseph out of his coat, his coat of many colours that was on him;
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/37#v24)</sup> And they took him, and cast him into a pit: and the pit was empty, there was no water in it.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/37#v25)</sup> And they sat down to eat bread: and they lifted up their eyes and looked, and, behold, a company of Ishmeelites came from Gilead with their camels bearing spicery and balm and myrrh, going to carry it down to Egypt.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/37#v26)</sup> And Judah said unto his brethren, What profit is it if we slay our brother, and conceal his blood?
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/37#v27)</sup> Come, and let us sell him to the Ishmeelites, and let not our hand be upon him; for he is our brother and our flesh. And his brethren were content.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/37#v28)</sup> Then there passed by Midianites merchantmen; and they drew and lifted up Joseph out of the pit, and sold Joseph to the Ishmeelites for twenty pieces of silver: and they brought Joseph into Egypt.

- Reuben delivered him out of their hands: cast him into this pit, but lay no hand upon him; he meant to return him to his father.
- Judah said, What profit is it if we slay our brother and conceal his blood? They sold Joseph to the Ishmaelites, the people known today as the Arab man, for twenty pieces of silver, and they brought him into Egypt.

**[Genesis 37:29-36](/bible/genesis/37#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/37#v29)</sup> And Reuben returned unto the pit; and, behold, Joseph was not in the pit; and he rent his clothes.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/37#v30)</sup> And he returned unto his brethren, and said, The child is not; and I, whither shall I go?
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/37#v31)</sup> And they took Joseph’s coat, and killed a kid of the goats, and dipped the coat in the blood;
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/37#v32)</sup> And they sent the coat of many colours, and they brought it to their father; and said, This have we found: know now whether it be thy son’s coat or no.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/genesis/37#v33)</sup> And he knew it, and said, It is my son’s coat; an evil beast hath devoured him; Joseph is without doubt rent in pieces.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/genesis/37#v34)</sup> And Jacob rent his clothes, and put sackcloth upon his loins, and mourned for his son many days.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/genesis/37#v35)</sup> And all his sons and all his daughters rose up to comfort him; but he refused to be comforted; and he said, For I will go down into the grave unto my son mourning. Thus his father wept for him.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/genesis/37#v36)</sup> And the Midianites sold him into Egypt unto Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh’s, and captain of the guard.

- The brothers dipped the coat in the blood of a kid and let their father believe an evil beast had devoured Joseph. They dealt falsely and lied, against the law: ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.
- Jacob could not see the lie because the thing was of the Lord. As Joseph told them later, ye thought evil against me, but God meant it unto good, to save much people alive; had Joseph never gone down to Egypt, the whole house of Israel would have died in the famine.
- The Midianites sold him in Egypt to Potiphar, an officer of Pharaoh and captain of the guard.

  Precepts:
  - **[Leviticus 19:11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)**
    > <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

    Neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another; the brothers broke that law before their father.
  - **[Genesis 50:20](/bible/genesis/50#v20)**
    > <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/50#v20)</sup> But as for you, ye thought evil against me; but God meant it unto good, to bring to pass, as it is this day, to save much people alive.

    Ye thought evil against me, but God meant it unto good, to save much people alive.
