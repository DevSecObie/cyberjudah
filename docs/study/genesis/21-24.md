## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 21](/bible/genesis/21) through 24: the birth of Isaac, the offering on Moriah, the death of Sarah, and the taking of a wife for Isaac from Abraham's own kindred.

**[Genesis 21:1-6](/bible/genesis/21#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/21#v1)</sup> And the Lord visited Sarah as he had said, and the Lord did unto Sarah as he had spoken.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/21#v2)</sup> For Sarah conceived, and bare Abraham a son in his old age, at the set time of which God had spoken to him.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/21#v3)</sup> And Abraham called the name of his son that was born unto him, whom Sarah bare to him, Isaac.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/21#v4)</sup> And Abraham circumcised his son Isaac being eight days old, as God had commanded him.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/21#v5)</sup> And Abraham was an hundred years old, when his son Isaac was born unto him.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/21#v6)</sup> And Sarah said, God hath made me to laugh, so that all that hear will laugh with me.

- The Lord visited Sarah as He had said, and she bare Isaac at the set time. Abraham was a hundred years old, and he circumcised Isaac at eight days as commanded.
- God hath made me to laugh: Isaac's name means he will laugh, for the story of a hundred year old father and a ninety year old mother is a joyful and comical thing.

**[Genesis 21:8](/bible/genesis/21#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/21#v8)</sup> And the child grew, and was weaned: and Abraham made a great feast the same day that Isaac was weaned.

- The great feast the day Isaac was weaned was not a birthday celebration. Weaned means finished being breastfed, and the mothers gave their children suck about three years; Abraham was celebrating Isaac coming off his mother's breast at around three years old.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Maccabees 7:27](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v27)**
> <sup>[27](/bible/2-maccabees/7#v27)</sup> But she bowing herself toward him, laughing the cruel tyrant to scorn, spake in her country language on this manner; O my son, have pity upon me that bare thee nine months in my womb, and gave thee such three years, and nourished thee, and brought thee up unto this age, and endured the troubles of education.

    The mothers gave their sons suck three years; being weaned was a milestone at about that age, not a birthday.

**[Genesis 21:9-13](/bible/genesis/21#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/21#v9)</sup> And Sarah saw the son of Hagar the Egyptian, which she had born unto Abraham, mocking.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/21#v10)</sup> Wherefore she said unto Abraham, Cast out this bondwoman and her son: for the son of this bondwoman shall not be heir with my son, even with Isaac.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/21#v11)</sup> And the thing was very grievous in Abraham’s sight because of his son.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/21#v12)</sup> And God said unto Abraham, Let it not be grievous in thy sight because of the lad, and because of thy bondwoman; in all that Sarah hath said unto thee, hearken unto her voice; for in Isaac shall thy seed be called.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/21#v13)</sup> And also of the son of the bondwoman will I make a nation, because he is thy seed.

- Sarah saw the son of Hagar mocking and said, Cast out this bondwoman and her son; the son of the bondwoman shall not be heir with Isaac.
- It was grievous to Abraham because he loved his firstborn, but God said, Hearken unto her voice, for in Isaac shall thy seed be called. Sarah was on point; Isaac is the child of the promise.
- Ishmael would still be made a nation, because he was Abraham's seed.

**[Genesis 21:14-19](/bible/genesis/21#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/21#v14)</sup> And Abraham rose up early in the morning, and took bread, and a bottle of water, and gave it unto Hagar, putting it on her shoulder, and the child, and sent her away: and she departed, and wandered in the wilderness of Beer–sheba.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/21#v15)</sup> And the water was spent in the bottle, and she cast the child under one of the shrubs.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/21#v16)</sup> And she went, and sat her down over against him a good way off, as it were a bowshot: for she said, Let me not see the death of the child. And she sat over against him, and lift up her voice, and wept.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/21#v17)</sup> And God heard the voice of the lad; and the angel of God called to Hagar out of heaven, and said unto her, What aileth thee, Hagar? fear not; for God hath heard the voice of the lad where he is.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/21#v18)</sup> Arise, lift up the lad, and hold him in thine hand; for I will make him a great nation.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/21#v19)</sup> And God opened her eyes, and she saw a well of water; and she went, and filled the bottle with water, and gave the lad drink.

- When the water was spent, Hagar cast the child under a shrub and wept, thinking he would die of thirst. God heard the voice of the lad and opened her eyes to a well of water.
- The lad could not die there; the prophecy of chapter 16 stood over him, that he would become a great nation.

**[Genesis 21:20-21](/bible/genesis/21#v20)**

> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/21#v20)</sup> And God was with the lad; and he grew, and dwelt in the wilderness, and became an archer.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/genesis/21#v21)</sup> And he dwelt in the wilderness of Paran: and his mother took him a wife out of the land of Egypt.

- Ishmael dwelt in the wilderness and became an archer; he learned the skills of combat and war, according to his nature.
- The word over his lineage stands: a wild people, his hand against every man and every man's hand against him, dwelling in the presence of all his brethren in the Middle East. His mother took him a wife out of Egypt.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 16:11-12](/bible/genesis/16#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/16#v11)</sup> And the angel of the Lord said unto her, Behold, thou art with child, and shalt bear a son, and shalt call his name Ishmael; because the Lord hath heard thy affliction.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/16#v12)</sup> And he will be a wild man; his hand will be against every man, and every man’s hand against him; and he shall dwell in the presence of all his brethren.

    The prophecy over Ishmael's posterity: a wild man, his hand against every man, dwelling in the presence of all his brethren.

**[Genesis 21:22-32](/bible/genesis/21#v22)**

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/21#v22)</sup> And it came to pass at that time, that Abimelech and Phichol the chief captain of his host spake unto Abraham, saying, God is with thee in all that thou doest:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/21#v23)</sup> Now therefore swear unto me here by God that thou wilt not deal falsely with me, nor with my son, nor with my son’s son: but according to the kindness that I have done unto thee, thou shalt do unto me, and to the land wherein thou hast sojourned.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/21#v24)</sup> And Abraham said, I will swear.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/21#v25)</sup> And Abraham reproved Abimelech because of a well of water, which Abimelech’s servants had violently taken away.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/21#v26)</sup> And Abimelech said, I wot not who hath done this thing: neither didst thou tell me, neither yet heard I of it, but to day.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/21#v27)</sup> And Abraham took sheep and oxen, and gave them unto Abimelech; and both of them made a covenant.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/21#v28)</sup> And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock by themselves.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/21#v29)</sup> And Abimelech said unto Abraham, What mean these seven ewe lambs which thou hast set by themselves?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/21#v30)</sup> And he said, For these seven ewe lambs shalt thou take of my hand, that they may be a witness unto me, that I have digged this well.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/21#v31)</sup> Wherefore he called that place Beer–sheba; because there they sware both of them.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/21#v32)</sup> Thus they made a covenant at Beer–sheba: then Abimelech rose up, and Phichol the chief captain of his host, and they returned into the land of the Philistines.

- Abraham reproved Abimelech over a well his servants had taken violently, and they made a covenant; the seven ewe lambs stood as a witness that Abraham digged the well.
- The place was called Beersheba, which means the well of the oath, because there they sware both of them.

# Genesis 22: The Offering of Isaac

Read the chapter → [Genesis 22](/bible/genesis/22)

**[Genesis 22:1-3](/bible/genesis/22#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/22#v1)</sup> And it came to pass after these things, that God did tempt Abraham, and said unto him, Abraham: and he said, Behold, here I am.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/22#v2)</sup> And he said, Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac, whom thou lovest, and get thee into the land of Moriah; and offer him there for a burnt offering upon one of the mountains which I will tell thee of.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/22#v3)</sup> And Abraham rose up early in the morning, and saddled his ass, and took two of his young men with him, and Isaac his son, and clave the wood for the burnt offering, and rose up, and went unto the place of which God had told him.

- God did tempt Abraham: Take now thy son, thine only son Isaac. Ishmael was alive, but the Most High counted Isaac the only son, because Ishmael was born of the flesh, of a plan the Lord never spoke, while Isaac was the child of the promise.
- Abraham rose up early in the morning and went. No hesitation, no debating with the word; that is obedience.

**[Genesis 22:7-8](/bible/genesis/22#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/22#v7)</sup> And Isaac spake unto Abraham his father, and said, My father: and he said, Here am I, my son. And he said, Behold the fire and the wood: but where is the lamb for a burnt offering?
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/22#v8)</sup> And Abraham said, My son, God will provide himself a lamb for a burnt offering: so they went both of them together.

- Isaac asked, Where is the lamb for a burnt offering? Abraham answered, My son, God will provide himself a lamb. He trusted the Most High with what was missing.

**[Genesis 22:9-12](/bible/genesis/22#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/22#v9)</sup> And they came to the place which God had told him of; and Abraham built an altar there, and laid the wood in order, and bound Isaac his son, and laid him on the altar upon the wood.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/22#v10)</sup> And Abraham stretched forth his hand, and took the knife to slay his son.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/22#v11)</sup> And the angel of the Lord called unto him out of heaven, and said, Abraham, Abraham: and he said, Here am I.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/22#v12)</sup> And he said, Lay not thine hand upon the lad, neither do thou any thing unto him: for now I know that thou fearest God, seeing thou hast not withheld thy son, thine only son from me.

- Abraham bound Isaac, laid him on the altar, and stretched forth his hand with the knife; the angel called out of heaven in the midst of it: Lay not thine hand upon the lad.
- Now I know that thou fearest God. The fear of God is proved in obedience; they that fear the Lord will not disobey His word.
- Abraham's inner faith carried him: he accounted that God was able to raise Isaac up, even from the dead, since the promise stood in Isaac who had no children yet. Faith and the commandments go together; you cannot have one without the other.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:15](/bible/sirach/2#v15)**
> <sup>[15](/bible/sirach/2#v15)</sup> They that fear the Lord will not disobey his Word; and they that love him will keep his ways.

    They that fear the Lord will not disobey His word; Abraham's fear was proved by his obedience.
  - **[Hebrews 11:17-19](/bible/hebrews/11#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/hebrews/11#v17)</sup> By faith Abraham, when he was tried, offered up Isaac: and he that had received the promises offered up his only begotten son,
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/11#v18)</sup> Of whom it was said, That in Isaac shall thy seed be called:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/hebrews/11#v19)</sup> Accounting that God was able to raise him up, even from the dead; from whence also he received him in a figure.

    By faith Abraham offered up Isaac, accounting that God was able to raise him up even from the dead.

**[Genesis 22:13-14](/bible/genesis/22#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/22#v13)</sup> And Abraham lifted up his eyes, and looked, and behold behind him a ram caught in a thicket by his horns: and Abraham went and took the ram, and offered him up for a burnt offering in the stead of his son.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/22#v14)</sup> And Abraham called the name of that place Jehovah–jireh: as it is said to this day, In the mount of the Lord it shall be seen.

- Once the obedience was fulfilled, God provided the sacrifice: a ram caught in a thicket by his horns, offered in the stead of his son, just as Abraham had told Isaac. Abraham called the place Jehovahjireh: in the mount of the Lord it shall be seen.

**[Genesis 22:15-18](/bible/genesis/22#v15)**

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/22#v15)</sup> And the angel of the Lord called unto Abraham out of heaven the second time,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/22#v16)</sup> And said, By myself have I sworn, saith the Lord, for because thou hast done this thing, and hast not withheld thy son, thine only son:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/22#v17)</sup> That in blessing I will bless thee, and in multiplying I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heaven, and as the sand which is upon the sea shore; and thy seed shall possess the gate of his enemies;
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/22#v18)</sup> And in thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed; because thou hast obeyed my voice.

- By myself have I sworn, saith the Lord: in multiplying I will multiply thy seed as the stars of the heaven and as the sand upon the sea shore, and thy seed shall possess the gate of his enemies.
- The seed possessing the gate of his enemies is part of the covenant: the seed of Jacob shall possess the remnant of Edom and of all the heathen, and Edom shall be a possession. That is why all nations cannot be inside this covenant; the covenant has Israel possessing their enemies.
- In thy seed shall all the nations of the earth be blessed: those nations are the nations of Israel, the twelve tribes.

  Precepts:
  - **[Amos 9:11-12](/bible/amos/9#v11)**
> <sup>[11](/bible/amos/9#v11)</sup> In that day will I raise up the tabernacle of David that is fallen, and close up the breaches thereof; and I will raise up his ruins, and I will build it as in the days of old:
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/amos/9#v12)</sup> That they may possess the remnant of Edom, and of all the heathen, which are called by my name, saith the Lord that doeth this.

    The tabernacle of David is raised up, that they may possess the remnant of Edom and of all the heathen.
  - **[Numbers 24:17-18](/bible/numbers/24#v17)**
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/24#v17)</sup> I shall see him, but not now: I shall behold him, but not nigh: there shall come a Star out of Jacob, and a Sceptre shall rise out of Israel, and shall smite the corners of Moab, and destroy all the children of Sheth.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/24#v18)</sup> And Edom shall be a possession, Seir also shall be a possession for his enemies; and Israel shall do valiantly.

    A Star out of Jacob and a Sceptre out of Israel; Edom shall be a possession, and Israel shall do valiantly.

# Genesis 23: The Death of Sarah

Read the chapter → [Genesis 23](/bible/genesis/23)

**[Genesis 23:1-2](/bible/genesis/23#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/23#v1)</sup> And Sarah was an hundred and seven and twenty years old: these were the years of the life of Sarah.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/23#v2)</sup> And Sarah died in Kirjath–arba; the same is Hebron in the land of Canaan: and Abraham came to mourn for Sarah, and to weep for her.

- Sarah lived an hundred and seven and twenty years, and died in Kirjatharba, the same is Hebron, in the land of Canaan; Abraham came to mourn and to weep for her.

**[Genesis 23:14-20](/bible/genesis/23#v14)**

> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/23#v14)</sup> And Ephron answered Abraham, saying unto him,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/23#v15)</sup> My lord, hearken unto me: the land is worth four hundred shekels of silver; what is that betwixt me and thee? bury therefore thy dead.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/23#v16)</sup> And Abraham hearkened unto Ephron; and Abraham weighed to Ephron the silver, which he had named in the audience of the sons of Heth, four hundred shekels of silver, current money with the merchant.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/23#v17)</sup> And the field of Ephron, which was in Machpelah, which was before Mamre, the field, and the cave which was therein, and all the trees that were in the field, that were in all the borders round about, were made sure
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/23#v18)</sup> Unto Abraham for a possession in the presence of the children of Heth, before all that went in at the gate of his city.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/23#v19)</sup> And after this, Abraham buried Sarah his wife in the cave of the field of Machpelah before Mamre: the same is Hebron in the land of Canaan.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/23#v20)</sup> And the field, and the cave that is therein, were made sure unto Abraham for a possession of a buryingplace by the sons of Heth.

- Ephron offered the field freely, but Abraham insisted on paying, and weighed four hundred shekels of silver, current money with the merchant. The field of Machpelah and its cave were made sure unto Abraham for a possession of a buryingplace, and there he buried Sarah.

# Genesis 24: A Wife for Isaac

Read the chapter → [Genesis 24](/bible/genesis/24)

**[Genesis 24:1-4](/bible/genesis/24#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/24#v1)</sup> And Abraham was old, and well stricken in age: and the Lord had blessed Abraham in all things.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/24#v2)</sup> And Abraham said unto his eldest servant of his house, that ruled over all that he had, Put, I pray thee, thy hand under my thigh:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/24#v3)</sup> And I will make thee swear by the Lord, the God of heaven, and the God of the earth, that thou shalt not take a wife unto my son of the daughters of the Canaanites, among whom I dwell:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/24#v4)</sup> But thou shalt go unto my country, and to my kindred, and take a wife unto my son Isaac.

- Abraham made his eldest servant swear that he would not take a wife for Isaac of the daughters of the Canaanites, but go to Abraham's country and kindred. The Most High never condoned marrying into the other nations; the custom was kept from the beginning, way before Moses and [Deuteronomy 7:3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3).

  Precepts:
  - **[Tobit 4:12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)**
> <sup>[12](/bible/tobit/4#v12)</sup> Beware of all whoredom, my son, and chiefly take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, and take not a strange woman to wife, which is not of thy father’s tribe: for we are the children of the prophets, Noe, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob: remember, my son, that our fathers from the beginning, even that they all married wives of their own kindred, and were blessed in their children, and their seed shall inherit the land.

    Take a wife of the seed of thy fathers, as Noah, Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob did from the beginning, and their seed shall inherit the land.

**[Genesis 24:7](/bible/genesis/24#v7)**

> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/24#v7)</sup> The Lord God of heaven, which took me from my father’s house, and from the land of my kindred, and which spake unto me, and that sware unto me, saying, Unto thy seed will I give this land; he shall send his angel before thee, and thou shalt take a wife unto my son from thence.

- Abraham told the servant, The Lord God of heaven shall send His angel before thee. He trusted the oath would be fulfilled because the Most High goes before the errand.

**[Genesis 24:12-16](/bible/genesis/24#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/24#v12)</sup> And he said, O Lord God of my master Abraham, I pray thee, send me good speed this day, and shew kindness unto my master Abraham.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/24#v13)</sup> Behold, I stand here by the well of water; and the daughters of the men of the city come out to draw water:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/24#v14)</sup> And let it come to pass, that the damsel to whom I shall say, Let down thy pitcher, I pray thee, that I may drink; and she shall say, Drink, and I will give thy camels drink also: let the same be she that thou hast appointed for thy servant Isaac; and thereby shall I know that thou hast shewed kindness unto my master.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/24#v15)</sup> And it came to pass, before he had done speaking, that, behold, Rebekah came out, who was born to Bethuel, son of Milcah, the wife of Nahor, Abraham’s brother, with her pitcher upon her shoulder.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/24#v16)</sup> And the damsel was very fair to look upon, a virgin, neither had any man known her: and she went down to the well, and filled her pitcher, and came up.

- The servant prayed for a sign at the well: the damsel who offers to draw for the camels also is the one appointed. Before he had done speaking, Rebekah came out.
- Rebekah was very fair to look upon, a virgin, neither had any man known her: undefiled and beautiful, the kind of wife the forefathers had from the beginning.

**[Genesis 24:26-27](/bible/genesis/24#v26)**

> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/24#v26)</sup> And the man bowed down his head, and worshipped the Lord.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/24#v27)</sup> And he said, Blessed be the Lord God of my master Abraham, who hath not left destitute my master of his mercy and his truth: I being in the way, the Lord led me to the house of my master’s brethren.

- When the sign came to pass exactly, the servant bowed his head and worshipped the Lord: I being in the way, the Lord led me to the house of my master's brethren.

**[Genesis 24:58-60](/bible/genesis/24#v58)**

> <sup>[58](/bible/genesis/24#v58)</sup> And they called Rebekah, and said unto her, Wilt thou go with this man? And she said, I will go.
>
> <sup>[59](/bible/genesis/24#v59)</sup> And they sent away Rebekah their sister, and her nurse, and Abraham’s servant, and his men.
>
> <sup>[60](/bible/genesis/24#v60)</sup> And they blessed Rebekah, and said unto her, Thou art our sister, be thou the mother of thousands of millions, and let thy seed possess the gate of those which hate them.

- Rebekah said, I will go. Her family blessed her: Be thou the mother of thousands of millions, and let thy seed possess the gate of those which hate them.
- The covenant narrows again here: it runs through Rebekah's children, and of her two sons it is Jacob who carries it, for Esau sold his birthright. The covenants in Genesis cannot be broken.

**[Genesis 24:63-65](/bible/genesis/24#v63)**

> <sup>[63](/bible/genesis/24#v63)</sup> And Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide: and he lifted up his eyes, and saw, and, behold, the camels were coming.
>
> <sup>[64](/bible/genesis/24#v64)</sup> And Rebekah lifted up her eyes, and when she saw Isaac, she lighted off the camel.
>
> <sup>[65](/bible/genesis/24#v65)</sup> For she had said unto the servant, What man is this that walketh in the field to meet us? And the servant had said, It is my master: therefore she took a vail, and covered herself.

- Isaac went out to meditate in the field at the eventide; that was a custom of the forefathers, showing the Lord was dealing with them long before the law was written at Sinai.
- When Rebekah saw Isaac she lighted off the camel, and she took a vail and covered herself. The four mothers covered their heads with actual material as a sign of submission; that is the custom [1 Corinthians 11](/bible/1-corinthians/11) goes into.

**[Genesis 24:66-67](/bible/genesis/24#v66)**

> <sup>[66](/bible/genesis/24#v66)</sup> And the servant told Isaac all things that he had done.
>
> <sup>[67](/bible/genesis/24#v67)</sup> And Isaac brought her into his mother Sarah’s tent, and took Rebekah, and she became his wife; and he loved her: and Isaac was comforted after his mother’s death.

- Isaac brought Rebekah into his mother's tent, and she became his wife, and he loved her; and Isaac was comforted after his mother's death, for he received a righteous wife like unto his mother.
