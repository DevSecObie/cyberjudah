## Introduction

This reading covers [Genesis 32](/bible/genesis/32) through 35: Jacob's return, the wrestling at Peniel, the meeting with Esau, the matter of Dinah, and the renewing of the name Israel at Bethel.

**[Genesis 32:1-2](/bible/genesis/32#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/32#v1)</sup> And Jacob went on his way, and the angels of God met him.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/32#v2)</sup> And when Jacob saw them, he said, This is God’s host: and he called the name of that place Mahanaim.

- The angels of God met Jacob as he departed from Laban, and he said, This is God's host, and called the place Mahanaim, which means two hosts or camps.
- When the scriptures speak of the Lord of hosts, the hosts are the armies of angels that stand before Him.

  Precepts:
  - **[2 Esdras 8:21](/bible/2-esdras/8#v21)**
    > <sup>[21](/bible/2-esdras/8#v21)</sup> Whose throne is inestimable; whose glory may not be comprehended; before whom the hosts of angels stand with trembling,

    Before the throne of the Most High the hosts of angels stand with trembling; the host Jacob saw was the angels.

**[Genesis 32:3-7](/bible/genesis/32#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/32#v3)</sup> And Jacob sent messengers before him to Esau his brother unto the land of Seir, the country of Edom.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/32#v4)</sup> And he commanded them, saying, Thus shall ye speak unto my lord Esau; Thy servant Jacob saith thus, I have sojourned with Laban, and stayed there until now:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/32#v5)</sup> And I have oxen, and asses, flocks, and menservants, and womenservants: and I have sent to tell my lord, that I may find grace in thy sight.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/32#v6)</sup> And the messengers returned to Jacob, saying, We came to thy brother Esau, and also he cometh to meet thee, and four hundred men with him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/32#v7)</sup> Then Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed: and he divided the people that was with him, and the flocks, and herds, and the camels, into two bands;

- Jacob sent messengers to Esau, and they returned saying Esau was coming with four hundred men, and Jacob was greatly afraid and distressed.
- His fear was rightful: the last word he had of Esau was that Esau purposed in his heart to slay him once their father's mourning came. The hatred began at the blessing, and the scriptures show in Ezekiel that the hatred never ended.
- The violent man imagines mischiefs in his heart and is continually gathered together for war; that is what Jacob understood was coming toward him.

  Precepts:
  - **[Genesis 27:41-42](/bible/genesis/27#v41)**
    > <sup>[41](/bible/genesis/27#v41)</sup> And Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing wherewith his father blessed him: and Esau said in his heart, The days of mourning for my father are at hand; then will I slay my brother Jacob.
    >
    > <sup>[42](/bible/genesis/27#v42)</sup> And these words of Esau her elder son were told to Rebekah: and she sent and called Jacob her younger son, and said unto him, Behold, thy brother Esau, as touching thee, doth comfort himself, purposing to kill thee.

    Esau hated Jacob because of the blessing and said in his heart, I will slay my brother Jacob; that was the last report Jacob had.
  - **[Psalms 140:1-2](/bible/psalms/140#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/140#v1)</sup> Deliver me, O Lord, from the evil man: preserve me from the violent man;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/140#v2)</sup> Which imagine mischiefs in their heart; continually are they gathered together for war.

    Deliver me from the evil man, preserve me from the violent man, which imagine mischiefs in their heart; Jacob's prayer fit the psalm.

**[Genesis 32:9-12](/bible/genesis/32#v9)**

> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/32#v9)</sup> And Jacob said, O God of my father Abraham, and God of my father Isaac, the Lord which saidst unto me, Return unto thy country, and to thy kindred, and I will deal well with thee:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/32#v10)</sup> I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies, and of all the truth, which thou hast shewed unto thy servant; for with my staff I passed over this Jordan; and now I am become two bands.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/32#v11)</sup> Deliver me, I pray thee, from the hand of my brother, from the hand of Esau: for I fear him, lest he will come and smite me, and the mother with the children.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/32#v12)</sup> And thou saidst, I will surely do thee good, and make thy seed as the sand of the sea, which cannot be numbered for multitude.

- Jacob prayed humbly: I am not worthy of the least of all the mercies; deliver me from the hand of my brother. He was not too proud to confess his fear to the Most High.
- He pleaded the covenant: thou saidst, I will make thy seed as the sand of the sea. That is the pattern for our prayers; plead the promise made to the fathers and ask the Most High to remember His covenant.

**[Genesis 32:13-20](/bible/genesis/32#v13)**

> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/32#v13)</sup> And he lodged there that same night; and took of that which came to his hand a present for Esau his brother;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/32#v14)</sup> Two hundred she goats, and twenty he goats, two hundred ewes, and twenty rams,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/32#v15)</sup> Thirty milch camels with their colts, forty kine, and ten bulls, twenty she asses, and ten foals.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/32#v16)</sup> And he delivered them into the hand of his servants, every drove by themselves; and said unto his servants, Pass over before me, and put a space betwixt drove and drove.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/32#v17)</sup> And he commanded the foremost, saying, When Esau my brother meeteth thee, and asketh thee, saying, Whose art thou? and whither goest thou? and whose are these before thee?
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/32#v18)</sup> Then thou shalt say, They be thy servant Jacob’s; it is a present sent unto my lord Esau: and, behold, also he is behind us.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/32#v19)</sup> And so commanded he the second, and the third, and all that followed the droves, saying, On this manner shall ye speak unto Esau, when ye find him.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/32#v20)</sup> And say ye moreover, Behold, thy servant Jacob is behind us. For he said, I will appease him with the present that goeth before me, and afterward I will see his face; peradventure he will accept of me.

- Jacob sent droves of livestock ahead as a present, spaced apart, each servant instructed what to say. He said, I will appease him with the present that goeth before me. He moved strategically to soften Esau before they met face to face.
- What he did was scriptural: a man's gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 18:16](/bible/proverbs/18#v16)**
    > <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/18#v16)</sup> A man’s gift maketh room for him, and bringeth him before great men.

    A man's gift maketh room for him; the present went before Jacob to make room.

**[Genesis 32:24-26](/bible/genesis/32#v24)**

> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/32#v24)</sup> And Jacob was left alone; and there wrestled a man with him until the breaking of the day.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/32#v25)</sup> And when he saw that he prevailed not against him, he touched the hollow of his thigh; and the hollow of Jacob’s thigh was out of joint, as he wrestled with him.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/32#v26)</sup> And he said, Let me go, for the day breaketh. And he said, I will not let thee go, except thou bless me.

- Jacob was left alone, and there wrestled a man with him until the breaking of the day; the man had to touch the hollow of Jacob's thigh and put it out of joint, because Jacob would not be beaten.
- With his thigh out of joint Jacob still held on: I will not let thee go, except thou bless me. That is faith with works. Cleave unto the Most High and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.
- That is the spirit to carry through hardship: fight the good fight of faith and lay hold on eternal life the way Jacob laid hold and would not let go.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 2:3](/bible/sirach/2#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/sirach/2#v3)</sup> Cleave unto him, and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end.

    Cleave unto Him and depart not away, that thou mayest be increased at thy last end; hold on until the blessing.
  - **[1 Timothy 6:12](/bible/1-timothy/6#v12)**
    > <sup>[12](/bible/1-timothy/6#v12)</sup> Fight the good fight of faith, lay hold on eternal life, whereunto thou art also called, and hast professed a good profession before many witnesses.

    Fight the good fight of faith; lay hold on eternal life as Jacob laid hold until he was blessed.

**[Genesis 32:27-28](/bible/genesis/32#v27)**

> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/32#v27)</sup> And he said unto him, What is thy name? And he said, Jacob.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/32#v28)</sup> And he said, Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with God and with men, and hast prevailed.

- Thy name shall be called no more Jacob, but Israel: for as a prince hast thou power with God and with men, and hast prevailed. Israel means a prince that has power with God.
- The God of Israel is He that giveth strength and power unto His people; He is the power source. When Israel went astray and served other gods, the name and the power were taken, and the nations called the people by every other name. Returning to the Most High is how the power returns, not carnal weapons of warfare.

  Precepts:
  - **[Psalms 68:35](/bible/psalms/68#v35)**
    > <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/68#v35)</sup> O God, thou art terrible out of thy holy places: the God of Israel is he that giveth strength and power unto his people. Blessed be God.

    The God of Israel is He that giveth strength and power unto His people; the power of the name is from Him.

**[Genesis 32:29-30](/bible/genesis/32#v29)**

> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/32#v29)</sup> And Jacob asked him, and said, Tell me, I pray thee, thy name. And he said, Wherefore is it that thou dost ask after my name? And he blessed him there.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/32#v30)</sup> And Jacob called the name of the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved.

- The man would not give his name, but he blessed Jacob there, and Jacob called the place Peniel: for I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved.
- The one Jacob wrestled was an angel: by his strength he had power with God, yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed. The word God, Elohim, is plural, which clears up the passages where men are said to have seen God and yet no man hath seen the Father.

  Precepts:
  - **[Hosea 12:3-4](/bible/hosea/12#v3)**
    > <sup>[3](/bible/hosea/12#v3)</sup> He took his brother by the heel in the womb, and by his strength he had power with God:
    >
    > <sup>[4](/bible/hosea/12#v4)</sup> Yea, he had power over the angel, and prevailed: he wept, and made supplication unto him: he found him in Beth–el, and there he spake with us;

    Jacob had power with God; he had power over the angel, and prevailed; the wrestler was an angel.

# Genesis 33: The Meeting With Esau

Read the chapter → [Genesis 33](/bible/genesis/33)

**[Genesis 33:1-4](/bible/genesis/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/33#v1)</sup> And Jacob lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, behold, Esau came, and with him four hundred men. And he divided the children unto Leah, and unto Rachel, and unto the two handmaids.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/33#v2)</sup> And he put the handmaids and their children foremost, and Leah and her children after, and Rachel and Joseph hindermost.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/33#v3)</sup> And he passed over before them, and bowed himself to the ground seven times, until he came near to his brother.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/33#v4)</sup> And Esau ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell on his neck, and kissed him: and they wept.

- Jacob bowed himself to the ground seven times as he came near, and Esau ran, embraced him, fell on his neck, and kissed him, and they wept.
- That embrace is not proof that the hatred was gone. Jacob had prayed for deliverance, and when a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; the Most High put that peace on Esau in that moment.
- The same prayer belongs to us: pray for all that are in authority, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness, keeping the commandments until Christ returns.

  Precepts:
  - **[Proverbs 16:7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)**
    > <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)</sup> When a man’s ways please the Lord, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him.

    When a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; that is what happened with Esau.
  - **[1 Timothy 2:1-2](/bible/1-timothy/2#v1)**
    > <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/2#v1)</sup> I exhort therefore, that, first of all, supplications, prayers, intercessions, and giving of thanks, be made for all men;
    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/2#v2)</sup> For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

    Pray for all in authority, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

# Genesis 34: Dinah and the Zeal of Simeon and Levi

Read the chapter → [Genesis 34](/bible/genesis/34)

**[Genesis 34:1-2](/bible/genesis/34#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/34#v1)</sup> And Dinah the daughter of Leah, which she bare unto Jacob, went out to see the daughters of the land.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/34#v2)</sup> And when Shechem the son of Hamor the Hivite, prince of the country, saw her, he took her, and lay with her, and defiled her.

- Dinah, Jacob's only daughter, went out to see the daughters of the land without supervision, and Shechem the son of Hamor took her, and lay with her, and defiled her. This was stranger rape.
- The lesson stands for every generation: keep watch over the daughters. Have a care of their body; do not give overmuch liberty; the father wakes for the daughter and the care for her takes away sleep. Everything the children do should have supervision, especially in this age.

  Precepts:
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 7:24](/bible/sirach/7#v24)**
    > <sup>[24](/bible/sirach/7#v24)</sup> Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.

    Hast thou daughters? have a care of their body, and shew not thyself cheerful toward them.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 26:10](/bible/sirach/26#v10)**
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/26#v10)</sup> If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.

    If thy daughter be shameless, keep her in straitly, lest she abuse herself through overmuch liberty.
  - **[Ecclesiasticus 42:9-11](/bible/sirach/42#v9)**
    > <sup>[9](/bible/sirach/42#v9)</sup> The father waketh for the daughter, when no man knoweth; and the care for her taketh away sleep: when she is young, lest she pass away the flower of her age; and being married, lest she should be hated:
    >
    > <sup>[10](/bible/sirach/42#v10)</sup> In her virginity, lest she should be defiled and gotten with child in her father’s house; and having an husband, lest she should misbehave herself; and when she is married, lest she should be barren.
    >
    > <sup>[11](/bible/sirach/42#v11)</sup> Keep a sure watch over a shameless daughter, lest she make thee a laughingstock to thine enemies, and a byword in the city, and a reproach among the people, and make thee ashamed before the multitude.

    The father waketh for the daughter when no man knoweth; keep a sure watch, lest she be defiled and made a reproach.

**[Genesis 34:3-7](/bible/genesis/34#v3)**

> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/34#v3)</sup> And his soul clave unto Dinah the daughter of Jacob, and he loved the damsel, and spake kindly unto the damsel.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/34#v4)</sup> And Shechem spake unto his father Hamor, saying, Get me this damsel to wife.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/34#v5)</sup> And Jacob heard that he had defiled Dinah his daughter: now his sons were with his cattle in the field: and Jacob held his peace until they were come.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/34#v6)</sup> And Hamor the father of Shechem went out unto Jacob to commune with him.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/34#v7)</sup> And the sons of Jacob came out of the field when they heard it: and the men were grieved, and they were very wroth, because he had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob’s daughter; which thing ought not to be done.

- Shechem's soul clave to Dinah after he defiled her; he did it backwards, taking first and loving after. That is the world's pattern, not righteousness.
- When Jacob's sons heard, the men were grieved and very wroth, because he had wrought folly in Israel in lying with Jacob's daughter, which thing ought not to be done. The laws were in place and kept long before they were written in stone at Sinai.

**[Genesis 34:8-10](/bible/genesis/34#v8)**

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/34#v8)</sup> And Hamor communed with them, saying, The soul of my son Shechem longeth for your daughter: I pray you give her him to wife.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/34#v9)</sup> And make ye marriages with us, and give your daughters unto us, and take our daughters unto you.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/34#v10)</sup> And ye shall dwell with us: and the land shall be before you; dwell and trade ye therein, and get you possessions therein.

- Hamor proposed the exact opposite of the law: make ye marriages with us, give your daughters unto us, and take our daughters unto you, and dwell with us, and become one people. Assimilation and intermarriage is the offer the nations always make, and it is the offer the law forbids.

- The sons of Jacob answered deceitfully: every male of the city must be circumcised before they would consent. Hamor and Shechem persuaded the whole city, reasoning that the wealth of Jacob's house would become theirs; every male was circumcised.

**[Genesis 34:25-29](/bible/genesis/34#v25)**

> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/34#v25)</sup> And it came to pass on the third day, when they were sore, that two of the sons of Jacob, Simeon and Levi, Dinah’s brethren, took each man his sword, and came upon the city boldly, and slew all the males.
