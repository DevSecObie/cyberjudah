**[Genesis 33:1-4](/bible/genesis/33#v1)**

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/33#v1)</sup> And Jacob lifted up his eyes, and looked, and, behold, Esau came, and with him four hundred men. And he divided the children unto Leah, and unto Rachel, and unto the two handmaids.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/genesis/33#v2)</sup> And he put the handmaids and their children foremost, and Leah and her children after, and Rachel and Joseph hindermost.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/genesis/33#v3)</sup> And he passed over before them, and bowed himself to the ground seven times, until he came near to his brother.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/genesis/33#v4)</sup> And Esau ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell on his neck, and kissed him: and they wept.

- Jacob bowed himself to the ground seven times as he came near, and Esau ran, embraced him, fell on his neck, and kissed him, and they wept.
- That embrace is not proof that the hatred was gone. Jacob had prayed for deliverance, and when a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; the Most High put that peace on Esau in that moment.
- The same prayer belongs to us: pray for all that are in authority, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness, keeping the commandments until Christ returns.

    > <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/16#v7)</sup> When a man’s ways please the Lord, he maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him.

    When a man's ways please the Lord, He maketh even his enemies to be at peace with him; that is what happened with Esau.

    >
    > <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/2#v2)</sup> For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

    Pray for all in authority, that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

**[Genesis 33:5-11](/bible/genesis/33#v5)**

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/33#v5)</sup> And he lifted up his eyes, and saw the women and the children; and said, Who are those with thee? And he said, The children which God hath graciously given thy servant.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/33#v6)</sup> Then the handmaidens came near, they and their children, and they bowed themselves.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/33#v7)</sup> And Leah also with her children came near, and bowed themselves: and after came Joseph near and Rachel, and they bowed themselves.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/33#v8)</sup> And he said, What meanest thou by all this drove which I met? And he said, These are to find grace in the sight of my lord.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/genesis/33#v9)</sup> And Esau said, I have enough, my brother; keep that thou hast unto thyself.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/genesis/33#v10)</sup> And Jacob said, Nay, I pray thee, if now I have found grace in thy sight, then receive my present at my hand: for therefore I have seen thy face, as though I had seen the face of God, and thou wast pleased with me.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/33#v11)</sup> Take, I pray thee, my blessing that is brought to thee; because God hath dealt graciously with me, and because I have enough. And he urged him, and he took it.

- Jacob presented the children which God graciously gave thy servant, and urged the present on Esau until he took it. Receiving the gift sealed the peace; Jacob wanted the assurance.

**[Genesis 33:12-16](/bible/genesis/33#v12)**

> <sup>[12](/bible/genesis/33#v12)</sup> And he said, Let us take our journey, and let us go, and I will go before thee.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/genesis/33#v13)</sup> And he said unto him, My lord knoweth that the children are tender, and the flocks and herds with young are with me: and if men should overdrive them one day, all the flock will die.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/genesis/33#v14)</sup> Let my lord, I pray thee, pass over before his servant: and I will lead on softly, according as the cattle that goeth before me and the children be able to endure, until I come unto my lord unto Seir.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/33#v15)</sup> And Esau said, Let me now leave with thee some of the folk that are with me. And he said, What needeth it? let me find grace in the sight of my lord.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/33#v16)</sup> So Esau returned that day on his way unto Seir.

- Esau offered to travel together and to leave some of his four hundred men, and Jacob declined both, pleading the tender children and the young flocks: I will lead on softly. He stayed wise and kept his distance, and Esau returned that day to Seir.

**[Genesis 33:17-20](/bible/genesis/33#v17)**

> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/33#v17)</sup> And Jacob journeyed to Succoth, and built him an house, and made booths for his cattle: therefore the name of the place is called Succoth.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/33#v18)</sup> And Jacob came to Shalem, a city of Shechem, which is in the land of Canaan, when he came from Padan–aram; and pitched his tent before the city.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/33#v19)</sup> And he bought a parcel of a field, where he had spread his tent, at the hand of the children of Hamor, Shechem’s father, for an hundred pieces of money.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/33#v20)</sup> And he erected there an altar, and called it El–elohe–Israel.

- Jacob journeyed to Succoth, which means booths, built him an house, and made booths for his cattle; the forefather was industrious.
- At Shalem he bought a parcel of a field for an hundred pieces of money and erected an altar, and called it Elelohe-Israel: God, the God of Israel.
