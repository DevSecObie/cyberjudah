- My son, meddle not with many matters: for if thou meddle much, thou shalt not be innocent; and if thou follow after, thou shalt not obtain, neither shalt thou escape by fleeing.
- Oh no, you're in it now. You wanted to know what the flavor of the Kool-Aid was; now you're making the Kool-Aid. It's all yours. All because you wanted to be in someone else's business. The scripture says, meddle not.

**[Proverbs 20:3](/bible/proverbs/20#v3)**  *[2:04:48]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/20#v3)</sup> It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling.

- It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: that's what you supposed to want. But every fool will be meddling. So wait a minute: it says fools meddle. What does that make a nosy person? Mind your business.
- You going to mess around and get caught up in whatever's going on, and now you're to blame. I have watched the whole situation shift from the people who were involved to the person that was just being nosy. Come to find out: it's your fault, you're the one running around telling everybody about this. You're the reason why they at odds with one another right now, cuz they got to defend themselves from all the stories being told. All cuz you can't keep your mouth shut. Don't let it be named amongst us.

**[Luke 12:13-14](/bible/luke/12#v13)**  *[2:06:12]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/12#v13)</sup> And one of the company said unto him, Master, speak to my brother, that he divide the inheritance with me.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/12#v14)</sup> And he said unto him, Man, who made me a judge or a divider over you?

- Jesus didn't get caught up in that stuff. Here's an example. One of the company said unto him, Master, speak to my brother, that he divide the inheritance with me. What did this brother just try to do? Involve him in some strife that ain't got nothing to do with him.
- And he said unto him, Man, who made me a judge or a divider over you? Christ didn't want none of that business. It wasn't his; he wasn't meddling with it. That's the same way we got to roll in other matters of people's business. It's brought to us: how may I help? Ain't got nothing to do with me. Go to them. Cuz I ain't trying to get bit, cuz now my name is associated with it.
- And we live in a time now where something might get revealed to you, and that person being talked about might be crazy enough that everybody that knows got to go. Now they coming for you. A lot of the stuff that happens in the streets, where you see multiple people getting hurt over a process of time, is somebody that knows something. And it could have been stopped at the door: it ain't none of my business, man, I ain't meddling. That's an example from Christ.

**[James 3:3-5](/bible/james/3#v3)**  *[2:08:03]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/james/3#v3)</sup> Behold, we put bits in the horses’ mouths, that they may obey us; and we turn about their whole body.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/james/3#v4)</sup> Behold also the ships, which though they be so great, and are driven of fierce winds, yet are they turned about with a very small helm, whithersoever the governor listeth.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/james/3#v5)</sup> Even so the tongue is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how great a matter a little fire kindleth!

- All of these things could be alleviated if we just find out how to control our mouths.
- Behold, we put bits in the horses' mouths, that they may obey us; and we turn about their whole body. Y'all know how much a horse weighs? Heavy, all that muscle. And you can control that whole horse by a bit that's in his mouth. That little bitty thing can have so much effect over the whole body. That's the tongue.
- Behold also the ships, which though they be so great, and are driven of fierce winds, yet are they turned about with a very small helm. The little steering wheel controlling that big old ship. Same thing with the tongue.
- Even so the tongue is a little member, and boasteth great things. Behold, how great a matter a little fire kindleth. Your tongue can mess up a whole lot of stuff. It has that much power. Knowing that, you better guard it. Be very careful about what comes out of it, so that you don't tarnish your name, or ruin your situation or somebody else's.

**[Proverbs 18:20-21](/bible/proverbs/18#v20)**  *[2:10:13]*

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/18#v20)</sup> A man’s belly shall be satisfied with the fruit of his mouth; and with the increase of his lips shall he be filled.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/18#v21)</sup> Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof.

- Death and life are in the power of the tongue: and they that love it shall eat the fruit thereof. This tongue can get you killed, or can give life.
- Be mindful, because your mouth can either get you rewarded or get you in a whole lot of trouble.

**[Psalms 34:13-14](/bible/psalms/34#v13)**  *[2:10:58]*

> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/34#v13)</sup> Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking guile.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/34#v14)</sup> Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.

- Keep thy tongue from evil, and thy lips from speaking guile. This takes practice. Ruling over your tongue takes practice, especially for those that don't know they ain't got no control.
- They think it's normal. They think you're supposed to murmur, you're supposed to complain, you're supposed to talk about everybody else's problems and their business. It's been normalized; they grew up watching it. But it's not normal, it's not right, and it's not how we're supposed to move or conduct ourselves.
- Depart from evil, and do good; seek peace, and pursue it.

**[Proverbs 21:23-24](/bible/proverbs/21#v23)**  *[2:11:55]*

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/21#v23)</sup> Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/21#v24)</sup> Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

- Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles. If you learn how to control your mouth, you will keep yourself from certain troubles.
- I know it don't seem like a lot, but some of you all have dodged many bullets, many situations, because you held your tongue, or you didn't speak on something, or you just stayed out of something, and you don't even know the world of hurt you might have caused yourself. It's very hard to look back and see the things you've avoided, because it's everyday life. But trust and know: if you get good at holding your tongue, you'll avoid those things, and you'll never know what it's like to go through the things that a person who can't control their mouth has to go through.

**[Ecclesiastes 5:2](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v2)**  *[2:13:05]*

> <sup>[2](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v2)</sup> Be not rash with thy mouth, and let not thine heart be hasty to utter any thing before God: for God is in heaven, and thou upon earth: therefore let thy words be few.

- Be not rash with thy mouth, and let not thine heart be hasty to utter any thing before God: for God is in heaven, and thou upon earth: therefore let thy words be few.
- Let your words be few. Control your mouth. There's a time and a place for everything. Be mindful of what you say, be mindful of the things you say to people, and the things you take in as well.

**[Proverbs 13:3](/bible/proverbs/13#v3)**  *[2:13:38]*

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/13#v3)</sup> He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life: but he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction.

- He that keepeth his mouth keepeth his life. That man that has the power, the control, to keep his tongue, to watch his mouth: you keep your life, you preserve yourself.
- But he that openeth wide his lips shall have destruction. These are the examples that we've read over and over and over. The Lord put a whole generation of people to death because they was murmuring, complaining, couldn't control a mouth, gossiping and talebearing. God ain't playing with that, so we can't play with it either.
