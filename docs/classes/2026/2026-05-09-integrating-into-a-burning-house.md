## Introduction

Shalom, Israel. The Deacon brings forth the class, following the brothers who taught earlier in the day. There is a lot going on in the world, and the way this class works is to go into what is happening and show it in the scriptures, to prepare you for what is about to take place. Before the topic, a word on something that has been irritating for the last couple of weeks: the xenophobia in South Africa. Then the topic itself: integrating into a burning house. The thumbnail montage set it up, from the I have a dream speech, to the promise of black spending power, to a politician boasting he would have us voting one way for the next two hundred years, to Dr. King's own confession: I fear I have integrated my people into a burning house.

## In The News

- Videos played from South Africa: crowds attacking and looting the stores of fellow Africans, telling Nigerians, Malians, and Ethiopians to get out of the country. They call it xenophobia, prejudice against immigrants. Ask yourself: beating people is assault, so where are the police, where are the politicians, why is nobody doing anything? Because it is a setup. Esau is behind what you see taking place in South Africa.
- Video played of a European visitor being warmly welcomed and celebrated in South Africa amid the xenophobia concerns. The question is why the anger lands on brothers who look just like them while the white man is embraced.
- Definition read in class: afrophobia is a specific form of racism, prejudice, and discrimination targeting black people, people of African descent, and African culture, manifesting as structural violence, hate speech, and dehumanization stemming from colonial era stereotypes. What you see in South Africa is not really xenophobia; it is afrophobia, self hatred, the same black on black hatred you see in the streets of America.
- Reading shown in class: approximately 40 to 50 percent of all the gold ever mined in human history has been found in South Africa, specifically within the Witwatersrand Basin discovered in 1886, considered the richest gold deposit on earth; that gold rush led to the establishment of Johannesburg. So if half the gold ever mined came from South Africa, why are the black people of South Africa not rich? The wealth went to the colonizers. That is who the anger belongs to, not the brother with a little cart selling shoes and handbags. That is also why apartheid lasted so long and why the powers never intervened: money was being made, and they are still extracting gold from that same ground today.

## In Closing

The thing that has been is the thing that shall be. Pharaoh said, let us deal wisely with them, and the same words are being acted out in America now that the count has turned. Our leaders made a covenant with hell and death, the movement was hijacked, and we were assimilated, not integrated: healed slightly, told peace when there is no peace. Dr. King saw the end of it and called it a burning house, and the scriptures call it brimstone, salt, and burning, like the overthrow of Sodom and Gomorrah. Do not trust in oppression; return to the Lord, because our Redeemer is strong, and he shall throughly plead our cause.
