### 3L.1 {#3L.1}

Trespasses are to be first taken to the transgressor for him to remedy his fault.

[Matt 18:15-17](/bible/matthew/18#v15); [Rom 15:14](/bible/romans/15#v14)

<details><summary>Matthew 18:15-17</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/18#v15)</sup> Moreover if thy brother shall trespass against thee, go and tell him his fault between thee and him alone: if he shall hear thee, thou hast gained thy brother.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/matthew/18#v16)</sup> But if he will not hear thee, then take with thee one or two more, that in the mouth of two or three witnesses every word may be established.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/matthew/18#v17)</sup> And if he shall neglect to hear them, tell it unto the church: but if he neglect to hear the church, let him be unto thee as an heathen man and a publican.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 15:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/romans/15#v14)</sup> And I myself also am persuaded of you, my brethren, that ye also are full of goodness, filled with all knowledge, able also to admonish one another.

</details>

### 3L.2 {#3L.2}

[Luke 17:3](/bible/luke/17#v3)

<details><summary>Luke 17:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/luke/17#v3)</sup> Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him.

</details>

### 3L.3 {#3L.3}

We should reprove and admonish one another to prevent sin.

[Lev 19:17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17); [1 Thess 5:12](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v12); [2 Thess 3:14-15](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v14); [Heb 3:13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v12)</sup> And we beseech you, brethren, to know them which labour among you, and are over you in the Lord, and admonish you;

</details>
<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v14)</sup> And if any man obey not our word by this epistle, note that man, and have no company with him, that he may be ashamed.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v15)</sup> Yet count him not as an enemy, but admonish him as a brother.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 3:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/hebrews/3#v13)</sup> But exhort one another daily, while it is called To day; lest any of you be hardened through the deceitfulness of sin.

</details>

<details><summary>Psalms 141:5</summary>

<details><summary>Proverbs 9:7-8</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/9#v7)</sup> He that reproveth a scorner getteth to himself shame: and he that rebuketh a wicked man getteth himself a blot.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/9#v8)</sup> Reprove not a scorner, lest he hate thee: rebuke a wise man, and he will love thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/24#v25)</sup> But to them that rebuke him shall be delight, and a good blessing shall come upon them.

</details>

<details><summary>Matthew 7:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/matthew/7#v1)</sup> Judge not, that ye be not judged.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/7#v2)</sup> For with what judgment ye judge, ye shall be judged: and with what measure ye mete, it shall be measured to you again.

### 3L.7 {#3L.7}

Hatred and rejection of reproof of one's faults leads to error, ignorance, remorse and destruction.

[Prov 5:12](/bible/proverbs/5#v12); [Prov 10:17](/bible/proverbs/10#v17); [Prov 12:1](/bible/proverbs/12#v1); [Prov 15:10,32](/bible/proverbs/15#v10); [Prov 29:1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)

<details><summary>Proverbs 5:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/5#v12)</sup> And say, How have I hated instruction, and my heart despised reproof;

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 10:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/10#v17)</sup> He is in the way of life that keepeth instruction: but he that refuseth reproof erreth.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 12:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/12#v1)</sup> Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof is brutish.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 15:10,32</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/15#v10)</sup> Correction is grievous unto him that forsaketh the way: and he that hateth reproof shall die.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/15#v32)</sup> He that refuseth instruction despiseth his own soul: but he that heareth reproof getteth understanding.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 29:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/29#v1)</sup> He, that being often reproved hardeneth his neck, shall suddenly be destroyed, and that without remedy.

</details>

>

### 3L.9 {#3L.9}

Do not reprove and correct others of their ignorance and blindness when you suffer from the same affliction.

[Matt 7:3-5](/bible/matthew/7#v3); [Luke 6:41-42](/bible/luke/6#v41)

<details><summary>Matthew 7:3-5</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/matthew/7#v3)</sup> And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye, but considerest not the beam that is in thine own eye?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/7#v4)</sup> Or how wilt thou say to thy brother, Let me pull out the mote out of thine eye; and, behold, a beam is in thine own eye?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/7#v5)</sup> Thou hypocrite, first cast out the beam out of thine own eye; and then shalt thou see clearly to cast out the mote out of thy brother’s eye.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 6:41-42</summary>

> <sup>[41](/bible/luke/6#v41)</sup> And why beholdest thou the mote that is in thy brother’s eye, but perceivest not the beam that is in thine own eye?
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/luke/6#v42)</sup> Either how canst thou say to thy brother, Brother, let me pull out the mote that is in thine eye, when thou thyself beholdest not the beam that is in thine own eye? Thou hypocrite, cast out first the beam out of thine own eye, and then shalt thou see clearly to pull out the mote that is in thy brother’s eye.

</details>
