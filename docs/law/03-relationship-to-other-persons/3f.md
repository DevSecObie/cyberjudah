### 3F.1 {#3F.1}

Thou shall not hate your brethren in your heart.

[Lev 19:17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17); [Col 3:8](/bible/colossians/3#v8); [1 John 3:15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/19#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not hate thy brother in thine heart: thou shalt in any wise rebuke thy neighbour, and not suffer sin upon him.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/3#v8)</sup> But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.

</details>
<details><summary>1 John 3:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)</sup> Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

</details>

### 3F.2 {#3F.2}

Wrath is a work of the flesh.

[Gal 5:20](/bible/galatians/5#v20)

<details><summary>Galatians 5:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/galatians/5#v20)</sup> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,

</details>

### 3F.3 {#3F.3}

It is wicked to have hatred cloaked by deceit.

[Prov 10:18](/bible/proverbs/10#v18); [Prov 26:24-26](/bible/proverbs/26#v24)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/10#v18)</sup> He that hideth hatred with lying lips, and he that uttereth a slander, is a fool.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 26:24-26</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/26#v24)</sup> He that hateth dissembleth with his lips, and layeth up deceit within him;
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/26#v25)</sup> When he speaketh fair, believe him not: for there are seven abominations in his heart.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/26#v26)</sup> Whose hatred is covered by deceit, his wickedness shall be shewed before the whole congregation.

</details>

### 3F.4 {#3F.4}

Hatred stirs up strife

[Prov 10:12](/bible/proverbs/10#v12); [Prov 15:18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/proverbs/10#v12)</sup> Hatred stirreth up strifes: but love covereth all sins.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 15:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/15#v18)</sup> A wrathful man stirreth up strife: but he that is slow to anger appeaseth strife.

</details>

### 3F.5 {#3F.5}

Hatred is inconsistent with the ways of God.

[1 John 2:9,11](/bible/1-john/2#v9); [1 John 4:20](/bible/1-john/4#v20)

<details><summary>1 John 2:9,11</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-john/2#v9)</sup> He that saith he is in the light, and hateth his brother, is in darkness even until now.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-john/2#v11)</sup> But he that hateth his brother is in darkness, and walketh in darkness, and knoweth not whither he goeth, because that darkness hath blinded his eyes.

</details>
<details><summary>1 John 4:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-john/4#v20)</sup> If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar: for he that loveth not his brother whom he hath seen, how can he love God whom he hath not seen?

</details>

### 3F.6 {#3F.6}

Blessed are you, when men shall hate you, and ostracized you, insult you, and spurn your name as evil, for the sake of Christ.

[Luke 6:22](/bible/luke/6#v22)

<details><summary>Luke 6:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/luke/6#v22)</sup> Blessed are ye, when men shall hate you, and when they shall separate you from their company, and shall reproach you, and cast out your name as evil, for the Son of man’s sake.

</details>

### 3F.7 {#3F.7}

Let none of you think evil in your heart against your neighbor.

[Zech 8:17](/bible/zechariah/8#v17)

<details><summary>Zechariah 8:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/zechariah/8#v17)</sup> And let none of you imagine evil in your hearts against his neighbour; and love no false oath: for all these are things that I hate, saith the Lord.

</details>

### 3F.8 {#3F.8}

Whosoever is angry with his brother without cause shall be in danger of judgment.

[Matt 5:22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/5#v22)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever is angry with his brother without a cause shall be in danger of the judgment: and whosoever shall say to his brother, Raca, shall be in danger of the council: but whosoever shall say, Thou fool, shall be in danger of hell fire.

</details>

### 3F.9 {#3F.9}

Show no malice nor desire ill will towards your brethren.

[Job 31:29-30](/bible/job/31#v29); [Ps 35:12-14](/bible/psalms/35#v12); [1 Cor 14:20](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v20); [Col 3:8](/bible/colossians/3#v8)

<details><summary>Job 31:29-30</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/job/31#v29)</sup> If I rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, or lifted up myself when evil found him:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/job/31#v30)</sup> Neither have I suffered my mouth to sin by wishing a curse to his soul.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 35:12-14</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/psalms/35#v12)</sup> They rewarded me evil for good to the spoiling of my soul.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/psalms/35#v13)</sup> But as for me, when they were sick, my clothing was sackcloth: I humbled my soul with fasting; and my prayer returned into mine own bosom.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/psalms/35#v14)</sup> I behaved myself as though he had been my friend or brother: I bowed down heavily, as one that mourneth for his mother.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 14:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v20)</sup> Brethren, be not children in understanding: howbeit in malice be ye children, but in understanding be men.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/colossians/3#v8)</sup> But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.

</details>

### 3F.11 {#3F.11}

Malice works as leaven and is incompatible with sincerity and truth.

[1 Cor 5:8](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v8)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 5:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v8)</sup> Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, neither with the leaven of malice and wickedness; but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth.

</details>

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 3:8</summary>

</details>

<details><summary>1 Peter 2:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-peter/2#v16)</sup> As free, and not using your liberty for a cloke of maliciousness, but as the servants of God.

</details>

<details><summary>Psalms 139:21-22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/psalms/139#v22)</sup> I hate them with perfect hatred: I count them mine enemies.

### 3F.15 {#3F.15}

A servant of God is to be gentle to all men.

[1 Thess 2:7](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v7); [2 Tim 2:24](/bible/2-timothy/2#v24); [Titus 3:2](/bible/titus/3#v2); [James 3:17](/bible/james/3#v17)

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 2:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v7)</sup> But we were gentle among you, even as a nurse cherisheth her children:

</details>
<details><summary>2 Timothy 2:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-timothy/2#v24)</sup> And the servant of the Lord must not strive; but be gentle unto all men, apt to teach, patient,

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 3:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/titus/3#v2)</sup> To speak evil of no man, to be no brawlers, but gentle, shewing all meekness unto all men.

</details>
<details><summary>James 3:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/james/3#v17)</sup> But the wisdom that is from above is first pure, then peaceable, gentle, and easy to be intreated, full of mercy and good fruits, without partiality, and without hypocrisy.

</details>
