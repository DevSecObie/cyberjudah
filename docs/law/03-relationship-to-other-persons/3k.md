### 3K.1 {#3K.1}

We are exhorted to sympathize with other Christians.

[Rom 12:15](/bible/romans/12#v15); [1 Cor 12:25-27](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v25); [1 John 3:17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)

<details><summary>Romans 12:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/romans/12#v15)</sup> Rejoice with them that do rejoice, and weep with them that weep.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 12:25-27</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v25)</sup> That there should be no schism in the body; but that the members should have the same care one for another.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v26)</sup> And whether one member suffer, all the members suffer with it; or one member be honoured, all the members rejoice with it.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-corinthians/12#v27)</sup> Now ye are the body of Christ, and members in particular.

</details>
<details><summary>1 John 3:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/1-john/3#v17)</sup> But whoso hath this world’s good, and seeth his brother have need, and shutteth up his bowels of compassion from him, how dwelleth the love of God in him?

</details>

### 3K.2 {#3K.2}

Having compassion can make a difference.

[Jude 1:22](/bible/jude/1#v22)

<details><summary>Jude 1:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/jude/1#v22)</sup> And of some have compassion, making a difference:

</details>

### 3K.3 {#3K.3}

We should exercise compassion and pity towards the afflicted.

[Job 6:14](/bible/job/6#v14); [Matt 18:33](/bible/matthew/18#v33); [2 Cor 2:6-8](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v6); [Heb 13:3](/bible/hebrews/13#v3)

<details><summary>Job 6:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/job/6#v14)</sup> To him that is afflicted pity should be shewed from his friend; but he forsaketh the fear of the Almighty.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 18:33</summary>

> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/18#v33)</sup> Shouldest not thou also have had compassion on thy fellowservant, even as I had pity on thee?

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 2:6-8</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v6)</sup> Sufficient to such a man is this punishment, which was inflicted of many.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v7)</sup> So that contrariwise ye ought rather to forgive him, and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v8)</sup> Wherefore I beseech you that ye would confirm your love toward him.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 13:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/hebrews/13#v3)</sup> Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.

</details>

### 3K.4 {#3K.4}

Have compassion for the poor.

[Prov 19:17](/bible/proverbs/19#v17)

<details><summary>Proverbs 19:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/19#v17)</sup> He that hath pity upon the poor lendeth unto the Lord; and that which he hath given will he pay him again.

</details>

### 3K.5 {#3K.5}

Compassion and aid to be given to one that is found hurt and injured and robbed.

[Luke 10:30-37](/bible/luke/10#v30)

<details><summary>Luke 10:30-37</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/10#v30)</sup> And Jesus answering said, A certain man went down from Jerusalem to Jericho, and fell among thieves, which stripped him of his raiment, and wounded him, and departed, leaving him half dead.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/luke/10#v31)</sup> And by chance there came down a certain priest that way: and when he saw him, he passed by on the other side.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/luke/10#v32)</sup> And likewise a Levite, when he was at the place, came and looked on him, and passed by on the other side.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/luke/10#v33)</sup> But a certain Samaritan, as he journeyed, came where he was: and when he saw him, he had compassion on him,
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/luke/10#v34)</sup> And went to him, and bound up his wounds, pouring in oil and wine, and set him on his own beast, and brought him to an inn, and took care of him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/luke/10#v35)</sup> And on the morrow when he departed, he took out two pence, and gave them to the host, and said unto him, Take care of him; and whatsoever thou spendest more, when I come again, I will repay thee.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/luke/10#v36)</sup> Which now of these three, thinkest thou, was neighbour unto him that fell among the thieves?
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/luke/10#v37)</sup> And he said, He that shewed mercy on him. Then said Jesus unto him, Go, and do thou likewise.

</details>
