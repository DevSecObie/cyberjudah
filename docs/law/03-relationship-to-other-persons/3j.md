### 3J.1 {#3J.1}

We are forbidden to defraud our neighbor or brethren.

[Lev 19:13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13); [Mark 10:19](/bible/mark/10#v19); [1 Cor 6:8](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v8)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/19#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not defraud thy neighbour, neither rob him: the wages of him that is hired shall not abide with thee all night until the morning.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/mark/10#v19)</sup> Thou knowest the commandments, Do not commit adultery, Do not kill, Do not steal, Do not bear false witness, Defraud not, Honour thy father and mother.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 6:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v8)</sup> Nay, ye do wrong, and defraud, and that your brethren.

</details>

### 3J.2 {#3J.2}

No one should defraud his brother regarding the matter of lust and sexual immorality.

[1 Thess 4:3-7](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v3)

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 4:3-7</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v3)</sup> For this is the will of God, even your sanctification, that ye should abstain from fornication:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v4)</sup> That every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v5)</sup> Not in the lust of concupiscence, even as the Gentiles which know not God:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v6)</sup> That no man go beyond and defraud his brother in any matter: because that the Lord is the avenger of all such, as we also have forewarned you and testified.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v7)</sup> For God hath not called us unto uncleanness, but unto holiness.

</details>

### 3J.3 {#3J.3}

Do not let those who work deceit dwell within your house.

[Ps 101:7](/bible/psalms/101#v7)

<details><summary>Psalms 101:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/101#v7)</sup> He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.

</details>

### 3J.4 {#3J.4}

Deceive not with your lips.

[Prov 24:28](/bible/proverbs/24#v28)

<details><summary>Proverbs 24:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/24#v28)</sup> Be not a witness against thy neighbour without cause; and deceive not with thy lips.

</details>

### 3J.5 {#3J.5}

Beware of those that serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, for by their good words and flattering speech they deceive the hearts of the unsuspecting.

[Rom 16:18](/bible/romans/16#v18)

<details><summary>Romans 16:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/romans/16#v18)</sup> For they that are such serve not our Lord Jesus Christ, but their own belly; and by good words and fair speeches deceive the hearts of the simple.

</details>

### 3J.6 {#3J.6}

You shall not deal falsely or treacherously with your brethren.

[Lev 19:11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11); [Prov 3:27-29](/bible/proverbs/3#v27); [Isa 33:1](/bible/isaiah/33#v1); [Mal 2:10](/bible/malachi/2#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 3:27-29</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/3#v27)</sup> Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/3#v28)</sup> Say not unto thy neighbour, Go, and come again, and to morrow I will give; when thou hast it by thee.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/3#v29)</sup> Devise not evil against thy neighbour, seeing he dwelleth securely by thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 33:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/33#v1)</sup> Woe to thee that spoilest, and thou wast not spoiled; and dealest treacherously, and they dealt not treacherously with thee! when thou shalt cease to spoil, thou shalt be spoiled; and when thou shalt make an end to deal treacherously, they shall deal treacherously with thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Malachi 2:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/malachi/2#v10)</sup> Have we not all one father? hath not one God created us? why do we deal treacherously every man against his brother, by profaning the covenant of our fathers?

</details>

### 3J.7 {#3J.7}

You shall not keep back the wages due a hired laborer by fraud.

[James 5:4](/bible/james/5#v4)

<details><summary>James 5:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/james/5#v4)</sup> Behold, the hire of the labourers who have reaped down your fields, which is of you kept back by fraud, crieth: and the cries of them which have reaped are entered into the ears of the Lord of sabaoth.

</details>

<details><summary>Psalms 119:118</summary>

</details>

[Matt 24:4](/bible/matthew/24#v4); [Mark 13:5-6](/bible/mark/13#v5); [Col 2:18](/bible/colossians/2#v18)

<details><summary>Matthew 24:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/24#v4)</sup> And Jesus answered and said unto them, Take heed that no man deceive you.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 13:5-6</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/mark/13#v5)</sup> And Jesus answering them began to say, Take heed lest any man deceive you:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/mark/13#v6)</sup> For many shall come in my name, saying, I am Christ; and shall deceive many.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 2:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/2#v18)</sup> Let no man beguile you of your reward in a voluntary humility and worshipping of angels, intruding into those things which he hath not seen, vainly puffed up by his fleshly mind,

</details>

### 3J.11 {#3J.11}

God abhors a deceitful man.

[Ps 5:6](/bible/psalms/5#v6); [1 Pet 3:10](/bible/1-peter/3#v10)

<details><summary>Psalms 5:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/5#v6)</sup> Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing: the Lord will abhor the bloody and deceitful man.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 3:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-peter/3#v10)</sup> For he that will love life, and see good days, let him refrain his tongue from evil, and his lips that they speak no guile:

</details>

### 3J.12 {#3J.12}

Deceit and fraud a characteristic of the wicked.

[Ps 10:4,7](/bible/psalms/10#v4)

<details><summary>Psalms 10:4,7</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/10#v4)</sup> The wicked, through the pride of his countenance, will not seek after God: God is not in all his thoughts.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/10#v7)</sup> His mouth is full of cursing and deceit and fraud: under his tongue is mischief and vanity.

</details>

### 3J.13 {#3J.13}

The saints are to be free from deceit and avoid such practices.

[Job 31:5](/bible/job/31#v5); [Ps 24:4](/bible/psalms/24#v4); [Zeph 3:13](/bible/zephaniah/3#v13); [Rev 14:5](/bible/revelation/14#v5)

<details><summary>Job 31:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/job/31#v5)</sup> If I have walked with vanity, or if my foot hath hasted to deceit;

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 24:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/24#v4)</sup> He that hath clean hands, and a pure heart; who hath not lifted up his soul unto vanity, nor sworn deceitfully.

</details>
<details><summary>Zephaniah 3:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/zephaniah/3#v13)</sup> The remnant of Israel shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: for they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid.

</details>
<details><summary>Revelation 14:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/revelation/14#v5)</sup> And in their mouth was found no guile: for they are without fault before the throne of God.

</details>

### 3J.14 {#3J.14}

Let no one deceive you by any means that would cause you to depart from the faith.

[2 Thess 2:3](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v3); [1 Tim 4:1-3](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 2:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v3)</sup> Let no man deceive you by any means: for that day shall not come, except there come a falling away first, and that man of sin be revealed, the son of perdition;

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 4:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)</sup> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/4#v2)</sup> Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/1-timothy/4#v3)</sup> Forbidding to marry, and commanding to abstain from meats, which God hath created to be received with thanksgiving of them which believe and know the truth.

</details>
