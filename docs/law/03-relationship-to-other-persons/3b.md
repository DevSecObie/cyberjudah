### 3B.1 {#3B.1}

You are to forgive the trespasses of your brethren.

[Gen 50:17](/bible/genesis/50#v17); [Matt 6:12](/bible/matthew/6#v12); [Matt 18:21-22,35](/bible/matthew/18#v21); [Luke 11:4](/bible/luke/11#v4)

<details><summary>Genesis 50:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/50#v17)</sup> So shall ye say unto Joseph, Forgive, I pray thee now, the trespass of thy brethren, and their sin; for they did unto thee evil: and now, we pray thee, forgive the trespass of the servants of the God of thy father. And Joseph wept when they spake unto him.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 6:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/6#v12)</sup> And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 18:21-22,35</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/matthew/18#v21)</sup> Then came Peter to him, and said, Lord, how oft shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? till seven times?
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/matthew/18#v22)</sup> Jesus saith unto him, I say not unto thee, Until seven times: but, Until seventy times seven.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/18#v35)</sup> So likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 11:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/luke/11#v4)</sup> And forgive us our sins; for we also forgive every one that is indebted to us. And lead us not into temptation; but deliver us from evil.

</details>

### 3B.2 {#3B.2}

Forgive those you have a quarrel against as Christ forgives us.

[Eph 4:32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32); [Col 3:13](/bible/colossians/3#v13)

<details><summary>Ephesians 4:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/4#v32)</sup> And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/colossians/3#v13)</sup> Forbearing one another, and forgiving one another, if any man have a quarrel against any: even as Christ forgave you, so also do ye.

</details>

### 3B.3 {#3B.3}

If your brother trespass against you, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him.

[Luke 17:3](/bible/luke/17#v3)

<details><summary>Luke 17:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/luke/17#v3)</sup> Take heed to yourselves: If thy brother trespass against thee, rebuke him; and if he repent, forgive him.

</details>

### 3B.4 {#3B.4}

If your brother trespass against you seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to you, saying, I repent; you shall forgive him.

[Luke 17:4](/bible/luke/17#v4)

<details><summary>Luke 17:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/luke/17#v4)</sup> And if he trespass against thee seven times in a day, and seven times in a day turn again to thee, saying, I repent; thou shalt forgive him.

</details>

### 3B.5 {#3B.5}

If any has caused sorrow, forgive and comfort him lest he be overwhelmed by the sorrow.

[2 Cor 2:5-7](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v5)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 2:5-7</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v5)</sup> But if any have caused grief, he hath not grieved me, but in part: that I may not overcharge you all.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v6)</sup> Sufficient to such a man is this punishment, which was inflicted of many.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v7)</sup> So that contrariwise ye ought rather to forgive him, and comfort him, lest perhaps such a one should be swallowed up with overmuch sorrow.

</details>

### 3B.6 {#3B.6}

If you do not forgive others God will not forgive your trespasses.

[Matt 6:14-15](/bible/matthew/6#v14); [Matt 18:29-35](/bible/matthew/18#v29); [Mark 11:25-26](/bible/mark/11#v25)

<details><summary>Matthew 6:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/matthew/6#v14)</sup> For if ye forgive men their trespasses, your heavenly Father will also forgive you:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/matthew/6#v15)</sup> But if ye forgive not men their trespasses, neither will your Father forgive your trespasses.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 18:29-35</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/matthew/18#v29)</sup> And his fellowservant fell down at his feet, and besought him, saying, Have patience with me, and I will pay thee all.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/matthew/18#v30)</sup> And he would not: but went and cast him into prison, till he should pay the debt.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/matthew/18#v31)</sup> So when his fellowservants saw what was done, they were very sorry, and came and told unto their lord all that was done.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/18#v32)</sup> Then his lord, after that he had called him, said unto him, O thou wicked servant, I forgave thee all that debt, because thou desiredst me:
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/matthew/18#v33)</sup> Shouldest not thou also have had compassion on thy fellowservant, even as I had pity on thee?
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/matthew/18#v34)</sup> And his lord was wroth, and delivered him to the tormentors, till he should pay all that was due unto him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/18#v35)</sup> So likewise shall my heavenly Father do also unto you, if ye from your hearts forgive not every one his brother their trespasses.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 11:25-26</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/mark/11#v25)</sup> And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/mark/11#v26)</sup> But if ye do not forgive, neither will your Father which is in heaven forgive your trespasses.

</details>

### 3B.7 {#3B.7}

Whenever you stand praying, forgive, if you have anything against anyone.

[Mark 11:25](/bible/mark/11#v25)

<details><summary>Mark 11:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/mark/11#v25)</sup> And when ye stand praying, forgive, if ye have ought against any: that your Father also which is in heaven may forgive you your trespasses.

</details>
