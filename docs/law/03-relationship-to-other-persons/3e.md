### 3E.1 {#3E.1}

You shall not lie to one another.

[Lev 19:11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11); [Zeph 3:13](/bible/zephaniah/3#v13); [Eph 4:25](/bible/ephesians/4#v25); [Col 3:9](/bible/colossians/3#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

</details>
<details><summary>Zephaniah 3:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/zephaniah/3#v13)</sup> The remnant of Israel shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: for they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 4:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/ephesians/4#v25)</sup> Wherefore putting away lying, speak every man truth with his neighbour: for we are members one of another.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/colossians/3#v9)</sup> Lie not one to another, seeing that ye have put off the old man with his deeds;

</details>

### 3E.2 {#3E.2}

Lying is iniquity and an abomination to God.

[Prov 6:16-17,19](/bible/proverbs/6#v16); [Prov 12:22](/bible/proverbs/12#v22); [Isa 59:2-4](/bible/isaiah/59#v2)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:16-17,19</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/6#v17)</sup> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/6#v19)</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 12:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/12#v22)</sup> Lying lips are abomination to the Lord: but they that deal truly are his delight.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 59:2-4</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/isaiah/59#v2)</sup> But your iniquities have separated between you and your God, and your sins have hid his face from you, that he will not hear.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/isaiah/59#v3)</sup> For your hands are defiled with blood, and your fingers with iniquity; your lips have spoken lies, your tongue hath muttered perverseness.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/isaiah/59#v4)</sup> None calleth for justice, nor any pleadeth for truth: they trust in vanity, and speak lies; they conceive mischief, and bring forth iniquity.

</details>

### 3E.3 {#3E.3}

We should pray to be delivered from lying lips that speak contemptuously against the righteous.

[Ps 31:18](/bible/psalms/31#v18); [Ps 120:2](/bible/psalms/120#v2)

<details><summary>Psalms 31:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/psalms/31#v18)</sup> Let the lying lips be put to silence; which speak grievous things proudly and contemptuously against the righteous.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 120:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/120#v2)</sup> Deliver my soul, O Lord, from lying lips, and from a deceitful tongue.

</details>

### 3E.4 {#3E.4}

The righteous should hate and avoid lying.

[Ps 13:5](/bible/psalms/13#v5); [Ps 119:163](/bible/psalms/119#v163); [Prov 13:5](/bible/proverbs/13#v5); [Isa 63:8](/bible/isaiah/63#v8); [Zeph 3:13](/bible/zephaniah/3#v13)

<details><summary>Psalms 13:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/13#v5)</sup> But I have trusted in thy mercy; my heart shall rejoice in thy salvation.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 119:163</summary>

> <sup>[163](/bible/psalms/119#v163)</sup> I hate and abhor lying: but thy law do I love.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 13:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/proverbs/13#v5)</sup> A righteous man hateth lying: but a wicked man is loathsome, and cometh to shame.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 63:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/isaiah/63#v8)</sup> For he said, Surely they are my people, children that will not lie: so he was their Saviour.

</details>
<details><summary>Zephaniah 3:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/zephaniah/3#v13)</sup> The remnant of Israel shall not do iniquity, nor speak lies; neither shall a deceitful tongue be found in their mouth: for they shall feed and lie down, and none shall make them afraid.

</details>

### 3E.5 {#3E.5}

Acquiring treasures by lying is the fleeting fantasy of those who seek death.

[Prov 21:6](/bible/proverbs/21#v6)

<details><summary>Proverbs 21:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/proverbs/21#v6)</sup> The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.

</details>

### 3E.6 {#3E.6}

Should not respect or keep company with those that lie.

[Ps 40:4](/bible/psalms/40#v4); [Ps 101:7](/bible/psalms/101#v7)

<details><summary>Psalms 40:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/40#v4)</sup> Blessed is that man that maketh the Lord his trust, and respecteth not the proud, nor such as turn aside to lies.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 101:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/101#v7)</sup> He that worketh deceit shall not dwell within my house: he that telleth lies shall not tarry in my sight.

</details>

### 3E.7 {#3E.7}

It is better to be a poor man than a liar.

[Prov 19:22](/bible/proverbs/19#v22)

<details><summary>Proverbs 19:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/19#v22)</sup> The desire of a man is his kindness: and a poor man is better than a liar.

</details>

### 3E.8 {#3E.8}

Lying is a sign of apostasy.

[2 Thess 2:9](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v9); [1 Tim 4:1-2](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 2:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/2-thessalonians/2#v9)</sup> Even him, whose coming is after the working of Satan with all power and signs and lying wonders,

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 4:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-timothy/4#v1)</sup> Now the Spirit speaketh expressly, that in the latter times some shall depart from the faith, giving heed to seducing spirits, and doctrines of devils;
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/4#v2)</sup> Speaking lies in hypocrisy; having their conscience seared with a hot iron;

</details>

### 3E.9 {#3E.9}

A lie lasts for but a moment, but truth shall be established forever.

[Prov 12:19](/bible/proverbs/12#v19)

<details><summary>Proverbs 12:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/12#v19)</sup> The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue is but for a moment.

</details>

### 3E.10 {#3E.10}

Lying is an inherent attribute of the wicked.

[Ps 58:3](/bible/psalms/58#v3)

<details><summary>Psalms 58:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/58#v3)</sup> The wicked are estranged from the womb: they go astray as soon as they be born, speaking lies.

</details>

Punishment and judgment for lying.

<details><summary>Jeremiah 50:36</summary>

</details>
