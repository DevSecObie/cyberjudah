### 3I.1 {#3I.1}

Rejoice not at the misfortunes of your adversaries.

[Job 31:29](/bible/job/31#v29); [Prov 24:17](/bible/proverbs/24#v17)

<details><summary>Job 31:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/job/31#v29)</sup> If I rejoiced at the destruction of him that hated me, or lifted up myself when evil found him:

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/24#v17)</sup> Rejoice not when thine enemy falleth, and let not thine heart be glad when he stumbleth:

</details>

### 3I.2 {#3I.2}

We are to love our personal adversaries.

[Matt 5:44](/bible/matthew/5#v44); [Luke 6:35](/bible/luke/6#v35)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:44</summary>

> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/5#v44)</sup> But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you;

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 6:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/luke/6#v35)</sup> But love ye your enemies, and do good, and lend, hoping for nothing again; and your reward shall be great, and ye shall be the children of the Highest: for he is kind unto the unthankful and to the evil.

</details>

### 3I.3 {#3I.3}

Pray for and forgive those that hate and persecute you.

[Matt 5:44](/bible/matthew/5#v44); [Luke 23:34](/bible/luke/23#v34); [Acts 7:60](/bible/acts/7#v60)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:44</summary>

> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/5#v44)</sup> But I say unto you, Love your enemies, bless them that curse you, do good to them that hate you, and pray for them which despitefully use you, and persecute you;

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 23:34</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/luke/23#v34)</sup> Then said Jesus, Father, forgive them; for they know not what they do. And they parted his raiment, and cast lots.

</details>
<details><summary>Acts 7:60</summary>

> <sup>[60](/bible/acts/7#v60)</sup> And he kneeled down, and cried with a loud voice, Lord, lay not this sin to their charge. And when he had said this, he fell asleep.

### 3I.4 {#3I.4}

</details>

### 3I.5 {#3I.5}

<details><summary>Job 31:30</summary>

### 3I.6 {#3I.6}

Do not hastily strive with your neighbor, but discuss your cause with him.

[Prov 25:8-10](/bible/proverbs/25#v8)

<details><summary>Proverbs 25:8-10</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/25#v8)</sup> Go not forth hastily to strive, lest thou know not what to do in the end thereof, when thy neighbour hath put thee to shame.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/25#v9)</sup> Debate thy cause with thy neighbour himself; and discover not a secret to another:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/25#v10)</sup> Lest he that heareth it put thee to shame, and thine infamy turn not away.

</details>

### 3I.7 {#3I.7}

Do not strive with a man without cause.

[Prov 3:30](/bible/proverbs/3#v30)

<details><summary>Proverbs 3:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/3#v30)</sup> Strive not with a man without cause, if he have done thee no harm.

</details>

### 3I.8 {#3I.8}

Christians exhorted to avoid foolish and ignorant disputes over words, fables, unlearned questions, or matters about the law or genealogies that give rise to mere speculation, knowing that they generate strife, envy, abusive language and evil suspicions.

[1 Tim 1:4](/bible/1-timothy/1#v4); [1 Tim 6:4](/bible/1-timothy/6#v4); [2 Tim 2:14,23](/bible/2-timothy/2#v14); [Titus 3:9](/bible/titus/3#v9)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 1:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/1#v4)</sup> Neither give heed to fables and endless genealogies, which minister questions, rather than godly edifying which is in faith: so do.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 6:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-timothy/6#v4)</sup> He is proud, knowing nothing, but doting about questions and strifes of words, whereof cometh envy, strife, railings, evil surmisings,

</details>
<details><summary>2 Timothy 2:14,23</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/2-timothy/2#v14)</sup> Of these things put them in remembrance, charging them before the Lord that they strive not about words to no profit, but to the subverting of the hearers.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-timothy/2#v23)</sup> But foolish and unlearned questions avoid, knowing that they do gender strifes.

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 3:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/titus/3#v9)</sup> But avoid foolish questions, and genealogies, and contentions, and strivings about the law; for they are unprofitable and vain.

</details>

### 3I.9 {#3I.9}

[Prov 6:16,19](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:16,19</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/6#v19)</sup> A false witness that speaketh lies, and he that soweth discord among brethren.

</details>

### 3I.10 {#3I.10}

[1 Tim 6:5](/bible/1-timothy/6#v5)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 6:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/1-timothy/6#v5)</sup> Perverse disputings of men of corrupt minds, and destitute of the truth, supposing that gain is godliness: from such withdraw thyself.

</details>

### 3I.11 {#3I.11}

Strife in your heart is dishonorable.

[2 Cor 12:20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20); [James 3:14](/bible/james/3#v14)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 12:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20)</sup> For I fear, lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would, and that I shall be found unto you such as ye would not: lest there be debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults:

</details>
<details><summary>James 3:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/james/3#v14)</sup> But if ye have bitter envying and strife in your hearts, glory not, and lie not against the truth.

</details>

### 3I.12 {#3I.12}

<details><summary>Proverbs 13:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/13#v10)</sup> Only by pride cometh contention: but with the well advised is wisdom.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 28:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/28#v25)</sup> He that is of a proud heart stirreth up strife: but he that putteth his trust in the Lord shall be made fat.

</details>

### 3I.13 {#3I.13}

Do not grumble and complain against one another, lest you be condemned.

[James 5:9](/bible/james/5#v9)

<details><summary>James 5:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/james/5#v9)</sup> Grudge not one against another, brethren, lest ye be condemned: behold, the judge standeth before the door.

</details>

### 3I.14 {#3I.14}

We should do all that is possible to avoid strife between brethren.

[Gen 13:8](/bible/genesis/13#v8)

<details><summary>Genesis 13:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/genesis/13#v8)</sup> And Abram said unto Lot, Let there be no strife, I pray thee, between me and thee, and between my herdmen and thy herdmen; for we be brethren.

</details>

### 3I.15 {#3I.15}

Christians are not to walk in strife.

[Prov 26:17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17); [Rom 13:13](/bible/romans/13#v13)

<details><summary>Proverbs 26:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17)</sup> He that passeth by, and meddleth with strife belonging not to him, is like one that taketh a dog by the ears.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

</details>

### 3I.16 {#3I.16}

We are to avoid those who cause divisions and offenses contrary to biblical doctrine.

[Rom 16:17](/bible/romans/16#v17)

<details><summary>Romans 16:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/16#v17)</sup> Now I beseech you, brethren, mark them which cause divisions and offences contrary to the doctrine which ye have learned; and avoid them.

</details>

### 3I.17 {#3I.17}

You are not to act out of strife but do all things without disputing.

[Phil 2:3,14](/bible/philippians/2#v3)

<details><summary>Philippians 2:3,14</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/philippians/2#v3)</sup> Let nothing be done through strife or vainglory; but in lowliness of mind let each esteem other better than themselves.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/philippians/2#v14)</sup> Do all things without murmurings and disputings:

</details>

### 3I.18 {#3I.18}

Christians should submit to a petty assault or wrong rather than engage in strife and conflict (should turn the other cheek).

[Matt 5:39-40](/bible/matthew/5#v39); [Luke 6:28-30](/bible/luke/6#v28); [1 Cor 6:7](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v7)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:39-40</summary>

> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/5#v39)</sup> But I say unto you, That ye resist not evil: but whosoever shall smite thee on thy right cheek, turn to him the other also.
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/5#v40)</sup> And if any man will sue thee at the law, and take away thy coat, let him have thy cloke also.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 6:28-30</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/luke/6#v28)</sup> Bless them that curse you, and pray for them which despitefully use you.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/luke/6#v29)</sup> And unto him that smiteth thee on the one cheek offer also the other; and him that taketh away thy cloke forbid not to take thy coat also.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/luke/6#v30)</sup> Give to every man that asketh of thee; and of him that taketh away thy goods ask them not again.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 6:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v7)</sup> Now therefore there is utterly a fault among you, because ye go to law one with another. Why do ye not rather take wrong? why do ye not rather suffer yourselves to be defrauded?

</details>

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:12</summary>

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 20:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/20#v3)</sup> It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling.
