### 3C.1 {#3C.1}

You shall love your neighbor as yourself.

[Lev 19:18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18); [Matt 19:19](/bible/matthew/19#v19); [Matt 22:39](/bible/matthew/22#v39); [Mark 12:31](/bible/mark/12#v31); [Luke 10:27-28](/bible/luke/10#v27); [Rom 13:9](/bible/romans/13#v9); [Gal 5:14](/bible/galatians/5#v14); [James 2:8](/bible/james/2#v8)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/19#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not avenge, nor bear any grudge against the children of thy people, but thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself: I am the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 19:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/matthew/19#v19)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother: and, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 22:39</summary>

> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/22#v39)</sup> And the second is like unto it, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 12:31</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/mark/12#v31)</sup> And the second is like, namely this, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself. There is none other commandment greater than these.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 10:27-28</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/luke/10#v27)</sup> And he answering said, Thou shalt love the Lord thy God with all thy heart, and with all thy soul, and with all thy strength, and with all thy mind; and thy neighbour as thyself.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/luke/10#v28)</sup> And he said unto him, Thou hast answered right: this do, and thou shalt live.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/13#v9)</sup> For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if there be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

</details>
<details><summary>Galatians 5:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/galatians/5#v14)</sup> For all the law is fulfilled in one word, even in this; Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

</details>
<details><summary>James 2:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/james/2#v8)</sup> If ye fulfil the royal law according to the scripture, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself, ye do well:

</details>

<details><summary>1 John 4:20-21</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/1-john/4#v20)</sup> If a man say, I love God, and hateth his brother, he is a liar: for he that loveth not his brother whom he hath seen, how can he love God whom he hath not seen?

</details>

### 3C.7 {#3C.7}

He that loves not his brother abides in death and walks in darkness.

[1 John 2:9-11](/bible/1-john/2#v9); [1 John 3:14-15](/bible/1-john/3#v14)

<details><summary>1 John 2:9-11</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-john/2#v9)</sup> He that saith he is in the light, and hateth his brother, is in darkness even until now.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/1-john/2#v10)</sup> He that loveth his brother abideth in the light, and there is none occasion of stumbling in him.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-john/2#v11)</sup> But he that hateth his brother is in darkness, and walketh in darkness, and knoweth not whither he goeth, because that darkness hath blinded his eyes.

</details>
<details><summary>1 John 3:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-john/3#v14)</sup> We know that we have passed from death unto life, because we love the brethren. He that loveth not his brother abideth in death.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/1-john/3#v15)</sup> Whosoever hateth his brother is a murderer: and ye know that no murderer hath eternal life abiding in him.

</details>

<details><summary>Romans 13:8-10</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/13#v8)</sup> Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.
