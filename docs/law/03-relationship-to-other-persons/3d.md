### 3D.1 {#3D.1}

Honesty is to be provided to everyone and in all things in life you do.

[Rom 12:17](/bible/romans/12#v17); [2 Cor 8:21](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v21); [2 Cor 13:7](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v7); [1 Tim 2:2](/bible/1-timothy/2#v2); [Heb 13:18](/bible/hebrews/13#v18)

<details><summary>Romans 12:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/romans/12#v17)</sup> Recompense to no man evil for evil. Provide things honest in the sight of all men.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 8:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/2-corinthians/8#v21)</sup> Providing for honest things, not only in the sight of the Lord, but also in the sight of men.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 13:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-corinthians/13#v7)</sup> Now I pray to God that ye do no evil; not that we should appear approved, but that ye should do that which is honest, though we be as reprobates.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 2:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/1-timothy/2#v2)</sup> For kings, and for all that are in authority; that we may lead a quiet and peaceable life in all godliness and honesty.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 13:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/hebrews/13#v18)</sup> Pray for us: for we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly.

</details>

### 3D.2 {#3D.2}

We should think on honest things.

[Phil 4:8](/bible/philippians/4#v8)

<details><summary>Philippians 4:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/philippians/4#v8)</sup> Finally, brethren, whatsoever things are true, whatsoever things are honest, whatsoever things are just, whatsoever things are pure, whatsoever things are lovely, whatsoever things are of good report; if there be any virtue, and if there be any praise, think on these things.

</details>

### 3D.4 {#3D.4}

We are to walk honestly.

[Rom 13:13](/bible/romans/13#v13); [1 Thess 4:12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)

<details><summary>Romans 13:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 4:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v12)</sup> That ye may walk honestly toward them that are without, and that ye may have lack of nothing.

</details>

### 3D.5 {#3D.5}

Sincerity characterizes our love to Christ.

[Eph 6:24](/bible/ephesians/6#v24)

<details><summary>Ephesians 6:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/ephesians/6#v24)</sup> Grace be with all them that love our Lord Jesus Christ in sincerity. Amen. Written from Rome unto the Ephesians by Tychicus.

</details>

### 3D.6 {#3D.6}

A godly sincerity should characterize our whole conduct.

[2 Cor 1:12](/bible/2-corinthians/1#v12)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 1:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/2-corinthians/1#v12)</sup> For our rejoicing is this, the testimony of our conscience, that in simplicity and godly sincerity, not with fleshly wisdom, but by the grace of God, we have had our conversation in the world, and more abundantly to you-ward.

</details>

### 3D.7 {#3D.7}

[Ps 15:1-2](/bible/psalms/15#v1)

<details><summary>Psalms 15:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/15#v2)</sup> He that walketh uprightly, and worketh righteousness, and speaketh the truth in his heart.

</details>

### 3D.9 {#3D.9}

The preaching of the gospel to be with sincerity.

[2 Cor 2:17](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v17); [Phil 1:16](/bible/philippians/1#v16); [1 Thess 2:3-5](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v3)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 2:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/2-corinthians/2#v17)</sup> For we are not as many, which corrupt the word of God: but as of sincerity, but as of God, in the sight of God speak we in Christ.

</details>
<details><summary>Philippians 1:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/philippians/1#v16)</sup> The one preach Christ of contention, not sincerely, supposing to add affliction to my bonds:

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 2:3-5</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v3)</sup> For our exhortation was not of deceit, nor of uncleanness, nor in guile:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v4)</sup> But as we were allowed of God to be put in trust with the gospel, even so we speak; not as pleasing men, but God, which trieth our hearts.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v5)</sup> For neither at any time used we flattering words, as ye know, nor a cloke of covetousness; God is witness:

</details>

<details><summary>Luke 8:15</summary>
