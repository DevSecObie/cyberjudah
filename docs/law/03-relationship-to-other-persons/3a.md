### 3A.1 {#3A.1}

To restore the erring.

[Gal 6:1](/bible/galatians/6#v1); [James 5:19-20](/bible/james/5#v19)

<details><summary>Galatians 6:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/galatians/6#v1)</sup> Brethren, if a man be overtaken in a fault, ye which are spiritual, restore such an one in the spirit of meekness; considering thyself, lest thou also be tempted.

</details>
<details><summary>James 5:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/james/5#v19)</sup> Brethren, if any of you do err from the truth, and one convert him;
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/james/5#v20)</sup> Let him know, that he which converteth the sinner from the error of his way shall save a soul from death, and shall hide a multitude of sins.

</details>

### 3A.2 {#3A.2}

To help bear the burdens of one another.

[Rom 15:1](/bible/romans/15#v1); [Gal 6:2](/bible/galatians/6#v2)

<details><summary>Romans 15:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/romans/15#v1)</sup> We then that are strong ought to bear the infirmities of the weak, and not to please ourselves.

</details>
<details><summary>Galatians 6:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/galatians/6#v2)</sup> Bear ye one another’s burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ.

</details>

<details><summary>Romans 14:1</summary>

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:12</summary>

</details>

### 3A.4 {#3A.4}

To pray for brethren.

[Job 42:8-10](/bible/job/42#v8); [2 Cor 1:11](/bible/2-corinthians/1#v11); [1 Thess 5:25](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v25); [2 Thess 3:1](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v1)

<details><summary>Job 42:8-10</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/job/42#v8)</sup> Therefore take unto you now seven bullocks and seven rams, and go to my servant Job, and offer up for yourselves a burnt offering; and my servant Job shall pray for you: for him will I accept: lest I deal with you after your folly, in that ye have not spoken of me the thing which is right, like my servant Job.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/job/42#v9)</sup> So Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite and Zophar the Naamathite went, and did according as the Lord commanded them: the Lord also accepted Job.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/42#v10)</sup> And the Lord turned the captivity of Job, when he prayed for his friends: also the Lord gave Job twice as much as he had before.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Corinthians 1:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-corinthians/1#v11)</sup> Ye also helping together by prayer for us, that for the gift bestowed upon us by the means of many persons thanks may be given by many on our behalf.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v25)</sup> Brethren, pray for us.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v1)</sup> Finally, brethren, pray for us, that the word of the Lord may have free course, and be glorified, even as it is with you:

</details>

### 3A.5 {#3A.5}

That which we do for our brethren we do for Christ.

[Matt 25:35-45](/bible/matthew/25#v35)

<details><summary>Matthew 25:35-45</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/matthew/25#v35)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me drink: I was a stranger, and ye took me in:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/matthew/25#v36)</sup> Naked, and ye clothed me: I was sick, and ye visited me: I was in prison, and ye came unto me.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/matthew/25#v37)</sup> Then shall the righteous answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, and fed thee? or thirsty, and gave thee drink?
>
> <sup>[38](/bible/matthew/25#v38)</sup> When saw we thee a stranger, and took thee in? or naked, and clothed thee?
>
> <sup>[39](/bible/matthew/25#v39)</sup> Or when saw we thee sick, or in prison, and came unto thee?
>
> <sup>[40](/bible/matthew/25#v40)</sup> And the King shall answer and say unto them, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye have done it unto one of the least of these my brethren, ye have done it unto me.
>
> <sup>[41](/bible/matthew/25#v41)</sup> Then shall he say also unto them on the left hand, Depart from me, ye cursed, into everlasting fire, prepared for the devil and his angels:
>
> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/25#v42)</sup> For I was an hungred, and ye gave me no meat: I was thirsty, and ye gave me no drink:
>
> <sup>[43](/bible/matthew/25#v43)</sup> I was a stranger, and ye took me not in: naked, and ye clothed me not: sick, and in prison, and ye visited me not.
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/matthew/25#v44)</sup> Then shall they also answer him, saying, Lord, when saw we thee an hungred, or athirst, or a stranger, or naked, or sick, or in prison, and did not minister unto thee?
>
> <sup>[45](/bible/matthew/25#v45)</sup> Then shall he answer them, saying, Verily I say unto you, Inasmuch as ye did it not to one of the least of these, ye did it not to me.

</details>

### 3A.7 {#3A.7}

He is considered great who is servant to his brethren.

[Matt 20:26-28](/bible/matthew/20#v26); [Matt 23:11](/bible/matthew/23#v11); [Mark 10:43-44](/bible/mark/10#v43); [Luke 22:26](/bible/luke/22#v26)

<details><summary>Matthew 20:26-28</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/matthew/20#v26)</sup> But it shall not be so among you: but whosoever will be great among you, let him be your minister;
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/matthew/20#v27)</sup> And whosoever will be chief among you, let him be your servant:
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/matthew/20#v28)</sup> Even as the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 23:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/matthew/23#v11)</sup> But he that is greatest among you shall be your servant.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:43-44</summary>

> <sup>[43](/bible/mark/10#v43)</sup> But so shall it not be among you: but whosoever will be great among you, shall be your minister:
>
> <sup>[44](/bible/mark/10#v44)</sup> And whosoever of you will be the chiefest, shall be servant of all.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 22:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/luke/22#v26)</sup> But ye shall not be so: but he that is greatest among you, let him be as the younger; and he that is chief, as he that doth serve.

</details>

### 3A.8 {#3A.8}

It is not good to do anything which causes your brother to stumble, or become offended, or be made weak.

[Rom 14:13,21](/bible/romans/14#v13); [1 Cor 8:11-13](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v11)

<details><summary>Romans 14:13,21</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/14#v13)</sup> Let us not therefore judge one another any more: but judge this rather, that no man put a stumblingblock or an occasion to fall in his brother’s way.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/14#v21)</sup> It is good neither to eat flesh, nor to drink wine, nor any thing whereby thy brother stumbleth, or is offended, or is made weak.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 8:11-13</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v11)</sup> And through thy knowledge shall the weak brother perish, for whom Christ died?
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v12)</sup> But when ye sin so against the brethren, and wound their weak conscience, ye sin against Christ.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/8#v13)</sup> Wherefore, if meat make my brother to offend, I will eat no flesh while the world standeth, lest I make my brother to offend.

</details>

### 3A.9 {#3A.9}

We ought to lay down our lives for the brethren whom God laid down his life for.

[1 John 3:16](/bible/1-john/3#v16)

<details><summary>1 John 3:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/1-john/3#v16)</sup> Hereby perceive we the love of God, because he laid down his life for us: and we ought to lay down our lives for the brethren.

</details>

### 3A.10 {#3A.10}

[Matt 7:12](/bible/matthew/7#v12); [Luke 6:31](/bible/luke/6#v31)

<details><summary>Matthew 7:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/matthew/7#v12)</sup> Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 6:31</summary>

> <sup>[31](/bible/luke/6#v31)</sup> And as ye would that men should do to you, do ye also to them likewise.

</details>
