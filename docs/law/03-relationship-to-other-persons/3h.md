### 3H.1 {#3H.1}

Flatter is not godly and is not to be used.

[Job 32:21-22](/bible/job/32#v21); [Ps 5:9](/bible/psalms/5#v9); [Ps 12:2-3](/bible/psalms/12#v2)

<details><summary>Job 32:21-22</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/job/32#v21)</sup> Let me not, I pray you, accept any man’s person, neither let me give flattering titles unto man.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/job/32#v22)</sup> For I know not to give flattering titles; in so doing my maker would soon take me away.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 5:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/5#v9)</sup> For there is no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness; their throat is an open sepulchre; they flatter with their tongue.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 12:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/12#v2)</sup> They speak vanity every one with his neighbour: with flattering lips and with a double heart do they speak.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/12#v3)</sup> The Lord shall cut off all flattering lips, and the tongue that speaketh proud things:

</details>

### 3H.2 {#3H.2}

Do not associate with those that use flattery.

[Prov 7:21-27](/bible/proverbs/7#v21)

<details><summary>Proverbs 7:21-27</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/7#v21)</sup> With her much fair speech she caused him to yield, with the flattering of her lips she forced him.
>
> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/7#v22)</sup> He goeth after her straightway, as an ox goeth to the slaughter, or as a fool to the correction of the stocks;
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/7#v23)</sup> Till a dart strike through his liver; as a bird hasteth to the snare, and knoweth not that it is for his life.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/proverbs/7#v24)</sup> Hearken unto me now therefore, O ye children, and attend to the words of my mouth.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/proverbs/7#v25)</sup> Let not thine heart decline to her ways, go not astray in her paths.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/7#v26)</sup> For she hath cast down many wounded: yea, many strong men have been slain by her.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/7#v27)</sup> Her house is the way to hell, going down to the chambers of death.

</details>

### 3H.3 {#3H.3}

Speak not with flattering words so as to please men but rather please God who tries our heart.

[1 Thess 2:4-5](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v4)

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 2:4-5</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v4)</sup> But as we were allowed of God to be put in trust with the gospel, even so we speak; not as pleasing men, but God, which trieth our hearts.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/1-thessalonians/2#v5)</sup> For neither at any time used we flattering words, as ye know, nor a cloke of covetousness; God is witness:

</details>

### 3H.5 {#3H.5}

Beware of those who speak many words, flattering people to gain advantage.

[Jude 1:16](/bible/jude/1#v16)

<details><summary>Jude 1:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/jude/1#v16)</sup> These are murmurers, complainers, walking after their own lusts; and their mouth speaketh great swelling words, having men’s persons in admiration because of advantage.

</details>

### 3H.7 {#3H.7}

Flattery is a snare to those that use it.

<details><summary>Proverbs 29:5</summary>

</details>

### 3H.8 {#3H.8}

A flattering mouth brings ruin.

<details><summary>Proverbs 26:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/26#v28)</sup> A lying tongue hateth those that are afflicted by it; and a flattering mouth worketh ruin.

</details>
