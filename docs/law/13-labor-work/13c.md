### 13C.1 {#13C.1}

A hard-working farmer ought to be the first to partake of his crops.

[2 Tim 2:6](/bible/2-timothy/2#v6)

<details><summary>2 Timothy 2:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-timothy/2#v6)</sup> The husbandman that laboureth must be first partaker of the fruits.

</details>

### 13C.2 {#13C.2}

He that sows sparingly shall also reap sparingly; and he which sows bountifully shall also reap bountifully.

[2 Cor 9:6](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v6)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 9:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/2-corinthians/9#v6)</sup> But this I say, He which soweth sparingly shall reap also sparingly; and he which soweth bountifully shall reap also bountifully.

</details>

### 13C.3 {#13C.3}

The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing.

[Prov 20:4](/bible/proverbs/20#v4)

<details><summary>Proverbs 20:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/20#v4)</sup> The sluggard will not plow by reason of the cold; therefore shall he beg in harvest, and have nothing.

</details>

### 13C.4 {#13C.4}

He that tills his land shall have plenty of bread; but he that follows after vain persons shall have poverty.

[Prov 28:19](/bible/proverbs/28#v19)

<details><summary>Proverbs 28:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/28#v19)</sup> He that tilleth his land shall have plenty of bread: but he that followeth after vain persons shall have poverty enough.

</details>

### 13C.5 {#13C.5}

You shall eat the labor of your hands, and it shall be well with you.

[Ps 128:2](/bible/psalms/128#v2)

<details><summary>Psalms 128:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/psalms/128#v2)</sup> For thou shalt eat the labour of thine hands: happy shalt thou be, and it shall be well with thee.

</details>

### 13C.6 {#13C.6}

The fields of the slothful will be overgrown with thistles and weeds, his farm broken down, and he will fall into poverty.

[Prov 24:30-34](/bible/proverbs/24#v30)

<details><summary>Proverbs 24:30-34</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/24#v30)</sup> I went by the field of the slothful, and by the vineyard of the man void of understanding;
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/24#v31)</sup> And, lo, it was all grown over with thorns, and nettles had covered the face thereof, and the stone wall thereof was broken down.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/24#v32)</sup> Then I saw, and considered it well: I looked upon it, and received instruction.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/24#v33)</sup> Yet a little sleep, a little slumber, a little folding of the hands to sleep:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/24#v34)</sup> So shall thy poverty come as one that travelleth; and thy want as an armed man.

</details>

### 13C.7 {#13C.7}

Be not overly concerned about the weather when you sow and reap, for you do not know the works of God.

[Eccl 11:4-6](/bible/ecclesiastes/11#v4)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 11:4-6</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/11#v4)</sup> He that observeth the wind shall not sow; and he that regardeth the clouds shall not reap.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ecclesiastes/11#v5)</sup> As thou knowest not what is the way of the spirit, nor how the bones do grow in the womb of her that is with child: even so thou knowest not the works of God who maketh all.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ecclesiastes/11#v6)</sup> In the morning sow thy seed, and in the evening withhold not thine hand: for thou knowest not whether shall prosper, either this or that, or whether they both shall be alike good.

</details>
