### 13A.1 {#13A.1}

Whatever work you put your hands to, do it with all your might.

[Eccl 9:10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 9:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/9#v10)</sup> Whatsoever thy hand findeth to do, do it with thy might; for there is no work, nor device, nor knowledge, nor wisdom, in the grave, whither thou goest.

</details>

### 13A.2 {#13A.2}

Every man should enjoy the good of all his labor.

[Eccl 2:24](/bible/ecclesiastes/2#v24); [Eccl 3:13](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v13); [Eccl 5:18](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v18)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 2:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/ecclesiastes/2#v24)</sup> There is nothing better for a man, than that he should eat and drink, and that he should make his soul enjoy good in his labour. This also I saw, that it was from the hand of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 3:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/ecclesiastes/3#v13)</sup> And also that every man should eat and drink, and enjoy the good of all his labour, it is the gift of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 5:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v18)</sup> Behold that which I have seen: it is good and comely for one to eat and to drink, and to enjoy the good of all his labour that he taketh under the sun all the days of his life, which God giveth him: for it is his portion.

</details>

### 13A.3 {#13A.3}

The laborer is worthy of his hire (wages).

[Matt 10:10](/bible/matthew/10#v10); [Matt 20:8](/bible/matthew/20#v8); [Luke 10:7](/bible/luke/10#v7); [1 Tim 5:18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)

<details><summary>Matthew 10:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/matthew/10#v10)</sup> Nor scrip for your journey, neither two coats, neither shoes, nor yet staves: for the workman is worthy of his meat.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 20:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/matthew/20#v8)</sup> So when even was come, the lord of the vineyard saith unto his steward, Call the labourers, and give them their hire, beginning from the last unto the first.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 10:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/luke/10#v7)</sup> And in the same house remain, eating and drinking such things as they give: for the labourer is worthy of his hire. Go not from house to house.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)</sup> For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn. And, The labourer is worthy of his reward.

</details>

### 13A.4 {#13A.4}

We are exhorted to walk in a manner worthy of the vocation with which we have been called.

[Eph 4:1](/bible/ephesians/4#v1)

<details><summary>Ephesians 4:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/ephesians/4#v1)</sup> I therefore, the prisoner of the Lord, beseech you that ye walk worthy of the vocation wherewith ye are called,

</details>

### 13A.5 {#13A.5}

Every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labor.

[1 Cor 3:8](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v8)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 3:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/3#v8)</sup> Now he that planteth and he that watereth are one: and every man shall receive his own reward according to his own labour.

</details>

### 13A.6 {#13A.6}

If any would not work, neither should he eat.

[2 Thess 3:10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v10)</sup> For even when we were with you, this we commanded you, that if any would not work, neither should he eat.

</details>

### 13A.7 {#13A.7}

Lead a quite life, mind your own business, and work with your own hands.

[1 Thess 4:11](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v11)

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 4:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v11)</sup> And that ye study to be quiet, and to do your own business, and to work with your own hands, as we commanded you;

</details>

### 13A.8 {#13A.8}

Be not slothful in business, but be fervent in spirit.

[Rom 12:11](/bible/romans/12#v11)

<details><summary>Romans 12:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/romans/12#v11)</sup> Not slothful in business; fervent in spirit; serving the Lord;

</details>

### 13A.9 {#13A.9}

Do not engage in work and labor that is vain or futile.

[Jer 10:3](/bible/jeremiah/10#v3)

<details><summary>Jeremiah 10:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/jeremiah/10#v3)</sup> For the customs of the people are vain: for one cutteth a tree out of the forest, the work of the hands of the workman, with the axe.

</details>
