### 13B.1 {#13B.1}

For wealth -In all labor there is profit.

[Prov 14:23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)

<details><summary>Proverbs 14:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/14#v23)</sup> In all labour there is profit: but the talk of the lips tendeth only to penury.

</details>

### 13B.2 {#13B.2}

Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished; but he that gathers by labor shall increase.

[Prov 13:11](/bible/proverbs/13#v11)

<details><summary>Proverbs 13:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/13#v11)</sup> Wealth gotten by vanity shall be diminished: but he that gathereth by labour shall increase.

</details>

### 13B.3 {#13B.3}

To reduce crime - Let him who stole steal no more: but rather let him labor, working with his hands.

[Eph 4:28](/bible/ephesians/4#v28)

<details><summary>Ephesians 4:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/ephesians/4#v28)</sup> Let him that stole steal no more: but rather let him labour, working with his hands the thing which is good, that he may have to give to him that needeth.

</details>

### 13B.4 {#13B.4}

To satisfy hunger - A person who labors, labors for himself, for his hunger urges him on.

[Prov 16:26](/bible/proverbs/16#v26)

<details><summary>Proverbs 16:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/16#v26)</sup> He that laboureth laboureth for himself; for his mouth craveth it of him.

</details>

### 13B.5 {#13B.5}

An idle man shall suffer hunger.

[Prov 19:15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)

<details><summary>Proverbs 19:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/19#v15)</sup> Slothfulness casteth into a deep sleep; and an idle soul shall suffer hunger.

</details>

### 13B.6 {#13B.6}

In the sweat of thy face shall you eat bread.

[Gen 3:19](/bible/genesis/3#v19)

<details><summary>Genesis 3:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/3#v19)</sup> In the sweat of thy face shalt thou eat bread, till thou return unto the ground; for out of it wast thou taken: for dust thou art, and unto dust shalt thou return.

</details>

### 13B.7 {#13B.7}

All the labor of man is for his mouth.

[Eccl 6:7](/bible/ecclesiastes/6#v7)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 6:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/ecclesiastes/6#v7)</sup> All the labour of man is for his mouth, and yet the appetite is not filled.

</details>

### 13B.8 {#13B.8}

To prevent poverty - He becomes poor that deals with a slack hand; but the hand of the diligent makes one rich.

[Prov 10:4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/10#v4)</sup> He becometh poor that dealeth with a slack hand: but the hand of the diligent maketh rich.

</details>

### 13B.9 {#13B.9}

Love not sleep, lest you come to poverty.

[Prov 20:13](/bible/proverbs/20#v13)

<details><summary>Proverbs 20:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/proverbs/20#v13)</sup> Love not sleep, lest thou come to poverty; open thine eyes, and thou shalt be satisfied with bread.

</details>

### 13B.10 {#13B.10}

For life - the labor of the righteous leads to life.

[Prov 10:16](/bible/proverbs/10#v16)

<details><summary>Proverbs 10:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/10#v16)</sup> The labour of the righteous tendeth to life: the fruit of the wicked to sin.

</details>

### 13B.11 {#13B.11}

To cause productivity rather than waste - He who is slack in his work is brother to him who destroys.

[Prov 18:9](/bible/proverbs/18#v9)

<details><summary>Proverbs 18:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/18#v9)</sup> He also that is slothful in his work is brother to him that is a great waster.

</details>

### 13B.12 {#13B.12}

A man who is diligent in his business will stand before kings, and not the lowly.

[Prov 22:29](/bible/proverbs/22#v29)

<details><summary>Proverbs 22:29</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/22#v29)</sup> Seest thou a man diligent in his business? he shall stand before kings; he shall not stand before mean men.

</details>

### 13B.13 {#13B.13}

To have a peaceful life - The sleep of a laboring man is sweet, whether he eat little or much; but the abundance of the rich will not suffer him to sleep.

[Eccl 5:12](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v12)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 5:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/ecclesiastes/5#v12)</sup> The sleep of a labouring man is sweet, whether he eat little or much: but the abundance of the rich will not suffer him to sleep.

</details>
