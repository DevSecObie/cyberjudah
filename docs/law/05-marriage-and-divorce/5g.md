### 5G.1 {#5G.1}

With father or mother.

[Lev 18:7-8](/bible/leviticus/18#v7); [Lev 20:11](/bible/leviticus/20#v11); [Deut 22:30](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v30); [Deut 27:20](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v20)

<details><summary>Leviticus 18:7-8</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/leviticus/18#v7)</sup> The nakedness of thy father, or the nakedness of thy mother, shalt thou not uncover: she is thy mother; thou shalt not uncover her nakedness.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/18#v8)</sup> The nakedness of thy father’s wife shalt thou not uncover: it is thy father’s nakedness.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 20:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/20#v11)</sup> And the man that lieth with his father’s wife hath uncovered his father’s nakedness: both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:30</summary>

> <sup>[30](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v30)</sup> A man shall not take his father’s wife, nor discover his father’s skirt.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 27:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v20)</sup> Cursed be he that lieth with his father’s wife; because he uncovereth his father’s skirt. And all the people shall say, Amen.

</details>
