### 5F.1 {#5F.1}

One should not depart from their spouse.

[1 Cor 7:10-11](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v10)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v10)</sup> And unto the married I command, yet not I, but the Lord, Let not the wife depart from her husband:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v11)</sup> But and if she depart, let her remain unmarried, or be reconciled to her husband: and let not the husband put away his wife.

</details>

### 5F.2 {#5F.2}

We should not separate from our spouse because of their disbelief.

[1 Cor 7:12-14](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v12)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:12-14</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v12)</sup> But to the rest speak I, not the Lord: If any brother hath a wife that believeth not, and she be pleased to dwell with him, let him not put her away.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v13)</sup> And the woman which hath an husband that believeth not, and if he be pleased to dwell with her, let her not leave him.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v14)</sup> For the unbelieving husband is sanctified by the wife, and the unbelieving wife is sanctified by the husband: else were your children unclean; but now are they holy.

</details>

### 5F.3 {#5F.3}

A bill of divorce can be written when some uncleanness is found in a woman recently married.

[Deut 24:1](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v1)</sup> When a man hath taken a wife, and married her, and it come to pass that she find no favour in his eyes, because he hath found some uncleanness in her: then let him write her a bill of divorcement, and give it in her hand, and send her out of his house.

</details>

### 5F.4 {#5F.4}

Divorce of a wife permitted for her act of fornication.

[Matt 5:32](/bible/matthew/5#v32); [Matt 19:9](/bible/matthew/19#v9)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/5#v32)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever shall put away his wife, saving for the cause of fornication, causeth her to commit adultery: and whosoever shall marry her that is divorced committeth adultery.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 19:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/matthew/19#v9)</sup> And I say unto you, Whosoever shall put away his wife, except it be for fornication, and shall marry another, committeth adultery: and whoso marrieth her which is put away doth commit adultery.

</details>

### 5F.5 {#5F.5}

If a man divorces his wife and marries another he commits adultery against her.

[Mark 10:11](/bible/mark/10#v11)

<details><summary>Mark 10:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/mark/10#v11)</sup> And he saith unto them, Whosoever shall put away his wife, and marry another, committeth adultery against her.

</details>

### 5F.6 {#5F.6}

If a woman divorces her husband and marries another she commits adultery.

[Mark 10:12](/bible/mark/10#v12)

<details><summary>Mark 10:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/mark/10#v12)</sup> And if a woman shall put away her husband, and be married to another, she committeth adultery.

</details>

### 5F.7 {#5F.7}

A woman, even if divorced, cannot marry another man as long as her husband is alive.

[Rom 7:2-3](/bible/romans/7#v2)

<details><summary>Romans 7:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/7#v2)</sup> For the woman which hath an husband is bound by the law to her husband so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/7#v3)</sup> So then if, while her husband liveth, she be married to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if her husband be dead, she is free from that law; so that she is no adulteress, though she be married to another man.

</details>

### 5F.8 {#5F.8}

A man or a woman is not bound to an unbelieving mate that departs.

[1 Cor 7:15](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v15)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v15)</sup> But if the unbelieving depart, let him depart. A brother or a sister is not under bondage in such cases: but God hath called us to peace.

</details>
