### 5D.2 {#5D.2}

Whosoever divorces his wife and marries another woman commits adultery.

[Luke 16:18](/bible/luke/16#v18); [Mark 10:11](/bible/mark/10#v11)

<details><summary>Luke 16:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/luke/16#v18)</sup> Whosoever putteth away his wife, and marrieth another, committeth adultery: and whosoever marrieth her that is put away from her husband committeth adultery.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/mark/10#v11)</sup> And he saith unto them, Whosoever shall put away his wife, and marry another, committeth adultery against her.

</details>

### 5D.3 {#5D.3}

[Mark 10:12](/bible/mark/10#v12)

<details><summary>Mark 10:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/mark/10#v12)</sup> And if a woman shall put away her husband, and be married to another, she committeth adultery.

### 5D.7 {#5D.7}

A childless widow is not to marry a stranger, but rather the brother of her dead husband.

[Deut 25:5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5); [Matt 22:24](/bible/matthew/22#v24)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v5)</sup> If brethren dwell together, and one of them die, and have no child, the wife of the dead shall not marry without unto a stranger: her husband’s brother shall go in unto her, and take her to him to wife, and perform the duty of an husband’s brother unto her.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 22:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/matthew/22#v24)</sup> Saying, Master, Moses said, If a man die, having no children, his brother shall marry his wife, and raise up seed unto his brother.

</details>

> <sup>[32](/bible/matthew/5#v32)</sup> But I say unto you, That whosoever shall put away his wife, saving for the cause of fornication, causeth her to commit adultery: and whosoever shall marry her that is divorced committeth adultery.
