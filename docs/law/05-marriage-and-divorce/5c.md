### 5C.1 {#5C.1}

Wives are to obey their husband.

[1 Cor 14:34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34); [Titus 2:5](/bible/titus/2#v5)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 14:34</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)</sup> Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 2:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/titus/2#v5)</sup> To be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

</details>

### 5C.2 {#5C.2}

Wife is to remain with her husband for life.

[Rom 7:2,3](/bible/romans/7#v2)

<details><summary>Romans 7:2,3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/7#v2)</sup> For the woman which hath an husband is bound by the law to her husband so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/7#v3)</sup> So then if, while her husband liveth, she be married to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if her husband be dead, she is free from that law; so that she is no adulteress, though she be married to another man.

</details>

<details><summary>Genesis 3:16</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/ephesians/5#v22)</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as unto the Lord.

> <sup>[24](/bible/ephesians/5#v24)</sup> Therefore as the church is subject unto Christ, so let the wives be to their own husbands in every thing.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/colossians/3#v18)</sup> Wives, submit yourselves unto your own husbands, as it is fit in the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 3:1,5-6</summary>

### 5C.7 {#5C.7}

The wife is not to raise questions in the church but to ask through her husband.

[1 Cor 14:34-35](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 14:34-35</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v34)</sup> Let your women keep silence in the churches: for it is not permitted unto them to speak; but they are commanded to be under obedience, as also saith the law.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/1-corinthians/14#v35)</sup> And if they will learn any thing, let them ask their husbands at home: for it is a shame for women to speak in the church.

</details>
