### 5B.1 {#5B.1}

Husbands are to have authority over their wives.

[Gen 3:16](/bible/genesis/3#v16); [1 Cor 11:3,7-9](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3); [Eph 5:23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)

<details><summary>Genesis 3:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/3#v16)</sup> Unto the woman he said, I will greatly multiply thy sorrow and thy conception; in sorrow thou shalt bring forth children; and thy desire shall be to thy husband, and he shall rule over thee.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 11:3,7-9</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v3)</sup> But I would have you know, that the head of every man is Christ; and the head of the woman is the man; and the head of Christ is God.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v7)</sup> For a man indeed ought not to cover his head, forasmuch as he is the image and glory of God: but the woman is the glory of the man.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v8)</sup> For the man is not of the woman; but the woman of the man.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v9)</sup> Neither was the man created for the woman; but the woman for the man.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 5:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/ephesians/5#v23)</sup> For the husband is the head of the wife, even as Christ is the head of the church: and he is the saviour of the body.

</details>

### 5B.2 {#5B.2}

A husband is to live with his wife in an understanding way, and give honor and respect to her.

[1 Pet 3:7](/bible/1-peter/3#v7)

<details><summary>1 Peter 3:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/3#v7)</sup> Likewise, ye husbands, dwell with them according to knowledge, giving honour unto the wife, as unto the weaker vessel, and as being heirs together of the grace of life; that your prayers be not hindered.

</details>

### 5B.3 {#5B.3}

Husbands are to love their wives and be not bitter against them.

[Eph 5:25,28-33](/bible/ephesians/5#v25); [Col 3:19](/bible/colossians/3#v19)

<details><summary>Ephesians 5:25,28-33</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/ephesians/5#v25)</sup> Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ also loved the church, and gave himself for it;
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/ephesians/5#v28)</sup> So ought men to love their wives as their own bodies. He that loveth his wife loveth himself.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/ephesians/5#v29)</sup> For no man ever yet hated his own flesh; but nourisheth and cherisheth it, even as the Lord the church:
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/ephesians/5#v30)</sup> For we are members of his body, of his flesh, and of his bones.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/ephesians/5#v31)</sup> For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and shall be joined unto his wife, and they two shall be one flesh.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/ephesians/5#v32)</sup> This is a great mystery: but I speak concerning Christ and the church.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/ephesians/5#v33)</sup> Nevertheless let every one of you in particular so love his wife even as himself; and the wife see that she reverence her husband.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/colossians/3#v19)</sup> Husbands, love your wives, and be not bitter against them.

</details>

### 5B.4 {#5B.4}

A husband is to be faithful to his wife.

[Prov 5:18-20](/bible/proverbs/5#v18); [Mal 2:14-15](/bible/malachi/2#v14)

<details><summary>Proverbs 5:18-20</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/5#v18)</sup> Let thy fountain be blessed: and rejoice with the wife of thy youth.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/5#v19)</sup> Let her be as the loving hind and pleasant roe; let her breasts satisfy thee at all times; and be thou ravished always with her love.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/5#v20)</sup> And why wilt thou, my son, be ravished with a strange woman, and embrace the bosom of a stranger?

</details>
<details><summary>Malachi 2:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/malachi/2#v14)</sup> Yet ye say, Wherefore? Because the Lord hath been witness between thee and the wife of thy youth, against whom thou hast dealt treacherously: yet is she thy companion, and the wife of thy covenant.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/malachi/2#v15)</sup> And did not he make one? Yet had he the residue of the spirit. And wherefore one? That he might seek a godly seed. Therefore take heed to your spirit, and let none deal treacherously against the wife of his youth.

</details>

### 5B.6 {#5B.6}

Let the husband render to his wife due benevolence or affection.

[1 Cor 7:3](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v3)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v3)</sup> Let the husband render unto the wife due benevolence: and likewise also the wife unto the husband.

</details>

### 5B.7 {#5B.7}

Husband to provide for the family.

[1 Tim 5:8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-timothy/5#v8)</sup> But if any provide not for his own, and specially for those of his own house, he hath denied the faith, and is worse than an infidel.

</details>
