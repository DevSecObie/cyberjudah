### 5A.1 {#5A.1}

Marriage between man and woman is instituted by God as a good and natural thing.

[Gen 2:18](/bible/genesis/2#v18); [Prov 18:22](/bible/proverbs/18#v22); [Matt 19:4](/bible/matthew/19#v4); [Mark 10:6](/bible/mark/10#v6)

<details><summary>Genesis 2:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/2#v18)</sup> And the Lord God said, It is not good that the man should be alone; I will make him an help meet for him.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 18:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/proverbs/18#v22)</sup> Whoso findeth a wife findeth a good thing, and obtaineth favour of the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 19:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/matthew/19#v4)</sup> And he answered and said unto them, Have ye not read, that he which made them at the beginning made them male and female,

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/mark/10#v6)</sup> But from the beginning of the creation God made them male and female.

</details>

### 5A.2 {#5A.2}

Husband and wife are to remain together for life (marriage vows to be kept).

[Gen 2:24](/bible/genesis/2#v24); [Matt 19:5](/bible/matthew/19#v5); [Mark 10:7](/bible/mark/10#v7); [1 Cor 7:10-11,27](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v10)

<details><summary>Genesis 2:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/2#v24)</sup> Therefore shall a man leave his father and his mother, and shall cleave unto his wife: and they shall be one flesh.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 19:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/19#v5)</sup> And said, For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife: and they twain shall be one flesh?

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/mark/10#v7)</sup> For this cause shall a man leave his father and mother, and cleave to his wife;

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:10-11,27</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v10)</sup> And unto the married I command, yet not I, but the Lord, Let not the wife depart from her husband:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v11)</sup> But and if she depart, let her remain unmarried, or be reconciled to her husband: and let not the husband put away his wife.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v27)</sup> Art thou bound unto a wife? seek not to be loosed. Art thou loosed from a wife? seek not a wife.

</details>

### 5A.3 {#5A.3}

A woman is bound by the law to her husband so long as he lives; but if the husband dies, she is released from the law concerning the husband.

[Rom 7:2-3](/bible/romans/7#v2)

<details><summary>Romans 7:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/romans/7#v2)</sup> For the woman which hath an husband is bound by the law to her husband so long as he liveth; but if the husband be dead, she is loosed from the law of her husband.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/romans/7#v3)</sup> So then if, while her husband liveth, she be married to another man, she shall be called an adulteress: but if her husband be dead, she is free from that law; so that she is no adulteress, though she be married to another man.

</details>

### 5A.4 {#5A.4}

Husband and wife regarded as one flesh. Gen, 2:23-24;

[Matt 19:5](/bible/matthew/19#v5); [Mark 10:8](/bible/mark/10#v8); [Eph 5:31](/bible/ephesians/5#v31)

<details><summary>Matthew 19:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/matthew/19#v5)</sup> And said, For this cause shall a man leave father and mother, and shall cleave to his wife: and they twain shall be one flesh?

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/mark/10#v8)</sup> And they twain shall be one flesh: so then they are no more twain, but one flesh.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 5:31</summary>

</details>

### 5A.5 {#5A.5}

Marriage is honorable among all, and the bed undefiled.

[Heb 13:4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)

<details><summary>Hebrews 13:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)</sup> Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.

</details>

### 5A.6 {#5A.6}

Husband and wife are to have children (be fruitful and multiply).

[Gen 1:28](/bible/genesis/1#v28); [Gen 9:1,7](/bible/genesis/9#v1); [Gen 35:11](/bible/genesis/35#v11); [Ps 127:3](/bible/psalms/127#v3); [Jer 29:6](/bible/jeremiah/29#v6); [1 Tim 5:14](/bible/1-timothy/5#v14)

<details><summary>Genesis 1:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/1#v28)</sup> And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.

</details>
<details><summary>Genesis 9:1,7</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/genesis/9#v1)</sup> And God blessed Noah and his sons, and said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/genesis/9#v7)</sup> And you, be ye fruitful, and multiply; bring forth abundantly in the earth, and multiply therein.

</details>
<details><summary>Genesis 35:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/genesis/35#v11)</sup> And God said unto him, I am God Almighty: be fruitful and multiply; a nation and a company of nations shall be of thee, and kings shall come out of thy loins;

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 127:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/psalms/127#v3)</sup> Lo, children are an heritage of the Lord: and the fruit of the womb is his reward.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 29:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/jeremiah/29#v6)</sup> Take ye wives, and beget sons and daughters; and take wives for your sons, and give your daughters to husbands, that they may bear sons and daughters; that ye may be increased there, and not diminished.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-timothy/5#v14)</sup> I will therefore that the younger women marry, bear children, guide the house, give none occasion to the adversary to speak reproachfully.

</details>

### 5A.7 {#5A.7}

Marriage bonds are of God and not to be put asunder by any man.

[Matt 19:6](/bible/matthew/19#v6); [Mark 10:9](/bible/mark/10#v9)

<details><summary>Matthew 19:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/matthew/19#v6)</sup> Wherefore they are no more twain, but one flesh. What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.

</details>
<details><summary>Mark 10:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/mark/10#v9)</sup> What therefore God hath joined together, let not man put asunder.

</details>

### 5A.8 {#5A.8}

Man is not independent of woman, nor is woman independent of man.

[1 Cor 7:4](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v4); [1 Cor 11:11](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v11)

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 7:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/1-corinthians/7#v4)</sup> The wife hath not power of her own body, but the husband: and likewise also the husband hath not power of his own body, but the wife.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 11:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/11#v11)</sup> Nevertheless neither is the man without the woman, neither the woman without the man, in the Lord.

</details>
