### 1A.1 {#1A.1}

I am the LORD your God, you shall have no other gods besides me.

[Exod 20:2-3](/bible/exodus/20#v2); [Deut 5:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v6)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/20#v2)</sup> I am the Lord thy God, which have brought thee out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/exodus/20#v3)</sup> Thou shalt have no other gods before me.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:6-7</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v6)</sup> I am the Lord thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v7)</sup> Thou shalt have none other gods before me.

</details>

### 1A.2 {#1A.2}

You shall not make for yourself any carved image, or any likeness of anything; you shall not bow down to them nor serve them.

[Exod 20:4-5](/bible/exodus/20#v4); [Deut 5:8-9](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v8)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:4-5</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/20#v4)</sup> Thou shalt not make unto thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the water under the earth:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/20#v5)</sup> Thou shalt not bow down thyself to them, nor serve them: for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me;

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:8-9</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v8)</sup> Thou shalt not make thee any graven image, or any likeness of any thing that is in heaven above, or that is in the earth beneath, or that is in the waters beneath the earth:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v9)</sup> Thou shalt not bow down thyself unto them, nor serve them: for I the Lord thy God am a jealous God, visiting the iniquity of the fathers upon the children unto the third and fourth generation of them that hate me,

</details>

### 1A.4 {#1A.4}

Observe the Sabbath day, to keep it holy.

[Exod 20:8-11](/bible/exodus/20#v8); [Deut 5:12](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v12)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:8-11</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/exodus/20#v8)</sup> Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/exodus/20#v9)</sup> Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work:
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/20#v10)</sup> But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/20#v11)</sup> For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day: wherefore the Lord blessed the sabbath day, and hallowed it.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v12)</sup> Keep the sabbath day to sanctify it, as the Lord thy God hath commanded thee.

</details>

### 1A.5 {#1A.5}

[Exod 20:12](/bible/exodus/20#v12); [Deut 5:16](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v16)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/20#v12)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother: that thy days may be long upon the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v16)</sup> Honour thy father and thy mother, as the Lord thy God hath commanded thee; that thy days may be prolonged, and that it may go well with thee, in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

</details>

### 1A.10 {#1A.10}

You shall not covet anything that is your neighbor's.

[Exod 20:17](/bible/exodus/20#v17); [Deut 5:21](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v21)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/exodus/20#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s house, thou shalt not covet thy neighbour’s wife, nor his manservant, nor his maidservant, nor his ox, nor his ass, nor any thing that is thy neighbour’s.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v21)</sup> Neither shalt thou desire thy neighbour’s wife, neither shalt thou covet thy neighbour’s house, his field, or his manservant, or his maidservant, his ox, or his ass, or any thing that is thy neighbour’s.

</details>
