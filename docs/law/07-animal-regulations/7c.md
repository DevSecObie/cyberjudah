### 7C.1 {#7C.1}

Stolen animals are to be repaid to the owner 4 or 5 times.

[Exod 22:1](/bible/exodus/22#v1)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/exodus/22#v1)</sup> If a man shall steal an ox, or a sheep, and kill it, or sell it; he shall restore five oxen for an ox, and four sheep for a sheep.

</details>

### 7C.2 {#7C.2}

Stray animals belonging to your brethren are be brought back to the owner. If the owner is unknown, then you are to keep the animal until the owner comes looking for it and then return it to him.

[Deut 22:1-3](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v1)</sup> Thou shalt not see thy brother’s ox or his sheep go astray, and hide thyself from them: thou shalt in any case bring them again unto thy brother.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v2)</sup> And if thy brother be not nigh unto thee, or if thou know him not, then thou shalt bring it unto thine own house, and it shall be with thee until thy brother seek after it, and thou shalt restore it to him again.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v3)</sup> In like manner shalt thou do with his ass; and so shalt thou do with his raiment; and with all lost thing of thy brother’s, which he hath lost, and thou hast found, shalt thou do likewise: thou mayest not hide thyself.

</details>

### 7C.3 {#7C.3}

If you encounter an animal of your enemy going astray, you shall bring it back to him.

[Exod 23:4](/bible/exodus/23#v4)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/exodus/23#v4)</sup> If thou meet thine enemy’s ox or his ass going astray, thou shalt surely bring it back to him again.

</details>

### 7C.4 {#7C.4}

If a pit is opened or dug and left uncovered, and an animal falls into it, the owner of the pit shall pay the owner of the animal but he can keep the dead animal.

[Exod 21:33-34](/bible/exodus/21#v33)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:33-34</summary>

> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/21#v33)</sup> And if a man shall open a pit, or if a man shall dig a pit, and not cover it, and an ox or an ass fall therein;
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/exodus/21#v34)</sup> The owner of the pit shall make it good, and give money unto the owner of them; and the dead beast shall be his.

</details>

### 7C.5 {#7C.5}

If you see an animal of one that hates you lying helpless under its load, you shall release the animal.

[Exod 23:5](/bible/exodus/23#v5)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/23#v5)</sup> If thou see the ass of him that hateth thee lying under his burden, and wouldest forbear to help him, thou shalt surely help with him.

</details>

### 7C.6 {#7C.6}

You shall not see your brother's ass or ox fall down by the way, and hide yourself from them: you shall surely help him to lift them up again.

[Deut 22:4](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v4)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v4)</sup> Thou shalt not see thy brother’s ass or his ox fall down by the way, and hide thyself from them: thou shalt surely help him to lift them up again.

</details>

### 7C.7 {#7C.7}

Whoever kills another's animal shall make it good, animal for animal.

[Lev 24:18,21](/bible/leviticus/24#v18)

<details><summary>Leviticus 24:18,21</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/leviticus/24#v18)</sup> And he that killeth a beast shall make it good; beast for beast.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/24#v21)</sup> And he that killeth a beast, he shall restore it: and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.

</details>
