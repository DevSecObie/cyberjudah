### 7D.1 {#7D.1}

If a man gives his neighbor his animal to keep for him and it dies or runs away, then an oath before the LORD shall be made between them, that he has not laid his hands on his neighbor's animal. He is not required to pay for it.

[Exod 22:10-11](/bible/exodus/22#v10)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/22#v10)</sup> If a man deliver unto his neighbour an ass, or an ox, or a sheep, or any beast, to keep; and it die, or be hurt, or driven away, no man seeing it:
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/exodus/22#v11)</sup> Then shall an oath of the Lord be between them both, that he hath not put his hand unto his neighbour’s goods; and the owner of it shall accept thereof, and he shall not make it good.

</details>

### 7D.2 {#7D.2}

If a borrowed animal is stolen, the keeper shall make restitution to the owner of the animal.

[Exod 22:12](/bible/exodus/22#v12)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/22#v12)</sup> And if it be stolen from him, he shall make restitution unto the owner thereof.

</details>

### 7D.3 {#7D.3}

If a borrowed animal is torn in pieces, the remains shall be brought as evidence, and no restitution shall be made.

[Exod 22:13](/bible/exodus/22#v13)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/22#v13)</sup> If it be torn in pieces, then let him bring it for witness, and he shall not make good that which was torn.

</details>

### 7D.4 {#7D.4}

If a man borrows any animal, and it is injured or dies while its owner is not with it, he shall make full restitution to the owner. But if the owner is with it, no restitution is to be made.

[Exod 22:14-15](/bible/exodus/22#v14)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/22#v14)</sup> And if a man borrow ought of his neighbour, and it be hurt, or die, the owner thereof being not with it, he shall surely make it good.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/22#v15)</sup> But if the owner thereof be with it, he shall not make it good: if it be an hired thing, it came for his hire.

</details>
