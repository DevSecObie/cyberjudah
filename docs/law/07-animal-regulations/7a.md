### 7A.1 {#7A.1}

You are not to work animals on the Sabbath.

[Exod 20:10](/bible/exodus/20#v10); [Exod 23:12](/bible/exodus/23#v12); [Deut 5:14](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v14)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/exodus/20#v10)</sup> But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates:

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 23:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/23#v12)</sup> Six days thou shalt do thy work, and on the seventh day thou shalt rest: that thine ox and thine ass may rest, and the son of thy handmaid, and the stranger, may be refreshed.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v14)</sup> But the seventh day is the sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, nor thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thine ox, nor thine ass, nor any of thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates; that thy manservant and thy maidservant may rest as well as thou.

</details>

### 7A.2 {#7A.2}

You are not to have two different kinds of animals work together; You shall not plow with an ox and an ass together.

[Deut 22:10](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v10)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v10)</sup> Thou shalt not plow with an ox and an ass together.

</details>

### 7A.3 {#7A.3}

You shall not muzzle the mouth of the ox that is being used for work.

[Deut 25:4](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v4); [1 Cor 9:9](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v9); [1 Tim 5:18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v4)</sup> Thou shalt not muzzle the ox when he treadeth out the corn.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 9:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/1-corinthians/9#v9)</sup> For it is written in the law of Moses, Thou shalt not muzzle the mouth of the ox that treadeth out the corn. Doth God take care for oxen?

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/1-timothy/5#v18)</sup> For the scripture saith, Thou shalt not muzzle the ox that treadeth out the corn. And, The labourer is worthy of his reward.

</details>
