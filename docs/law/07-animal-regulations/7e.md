### 7E.1 {#7E.1}

You are not to breed cattle with a diverse kind (no hybrid cattle, livestock or pets).

[Lev 19:19](/bible/leviticus/19#v19)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/leviticus/19#v19)</sup> Ye shall keep my statutes. Thou shalt not let thy cattle gender with a diverse kind: thou shalt not sow thy field with mingled seed: neither shall a garment mingled of linen and woollen come upon thee.

</details>

### 7E.2 {#7E.2}

Should have regard for the life of your animals.

[Prov 12:10](/bible/proverbs/12#v10)

<details><summary>Proverbs 12:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/12#v10)</sup> A righteous man regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked are cruel.

</details>

### 7E.3 {#7E.3}

Do not separate a newly born ox or sheep or a goat from its mother for the first seven days.

[Lev 22:27](/bible/leviticus/22#v27)

<details><summary>Leviticus 22:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/22#v27)</sup> When a bullock, or a sheep, or a goat, is brought forth, then it shall be seven days under the dam; and from the eighth day and thenceforth it shall be accepted for an offering made by fire unto the Lord.

</details>

### 7E.4 {#7E.4}

The eggs or young can be taken from a bird's nest but not along with the mother.

[Deut 22:6-7](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v6)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:6-7</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v6)</sup> If a bird’s nest chance to be before thee in the way in any tree, or on the ground, whether they be young ones, or eggs, and the dam sitting upon the young, or upon the eggs, thou shalt not take the dam with the young:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v7)</sup> But thou shalt in any wise let the dam go, and take the young to thee; that it may be well with thee, and that thou mayest prolong thy days.

</details>

### 7E.5 {#7E.5}

Know well the condition of your flocks, and pay attention to your herds.

[Prov 27:23](/bible/proverbs/27#v23)

<details><summary>Proverbs 27:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/27#v23)</sup> Be thou diligent to know the state of thy flocks, and look well to thy herds.

</details>
