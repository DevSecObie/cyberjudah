### 7B.1 {#7B.1}

If an animal kills a person the animal is to be put to death.

[Exod 21:28-29](/bible/exodus/21#v28)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:28-29</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/exodus/21#v28)</sup> If an ox gore a man or a woman, that they die: then the ox shall be surely stoned, and his flesh shall not be eaten; but the owner of the ox shall be quit.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/21#v29)</sup> But if the ox were wont to push with his horn in time past, and it hath been testified to his owner, and he hath not kept him in, but that he hath killed a man or a woman; the ox shall be stoned, and his owner also shall be put to death.

</details>

### 7B.2 {#7B.2}

If an animal is proven to be aggressive, and its owner has been warned, yet he does not confine it, and it kills a man or woman, then both the animal and the owner are to be put to death. If there is a ransom of money demanded of the owner, it can be paid for the redemption of his life.

[Exod 21:29-31](/bible/exodus/21#v29)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:29-31</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/exodus/21#v29)</sup> But if the ox were wont to push with his horn in time past, and it hath been testified to his owner, and he hath not kept him in, but that he hath killed a man or a woman; the ox shall be stoned, and his owner also shall be put to death.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/exodus/21#v30)</sup> If there be laid on him a sum of money, then he shall give for the ransom of his life whatsoever is laid upon him.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/exodus/21#v31)</sup> Whether he have gored a son, or have gored a daughter, according to this judgment shall it be done unto him.

</details>

### 7B.3 {#7B.3}

If an animal shall gore a servant, the owner shall give the servant's master a set amount of money and the animal is to be killed.

[Exod 21:32](/bible/exodus/21#v32)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/21#v32)</sup> If the ox shall push a manservant or a maidservant; he shall give unto their master thirty shekels of silver, and the ox shall be stoned.

</details>

### 7B.4 {#7B.4}

If a man's animal eats the crop of another man's field, he shall make restitution from the best of his own field.

[Exod 22:5](/bible/exodus/22#v5)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/exodus/22#v5)</sup> If a man shall cause a field or vineyard to be eaten, and shall put in his beast, and shall feed in another man’s field; of the best of his own field, and of the best of his own vineyard, shall he make restitution.

</details>

### 7B.5 {#7B.5}

If one man's animal kills another's animal, then the live animal shall be sold and the money (and the dead animal if it be cattle) shall be divided between them.

[Exod 21:35](/bible/exodus/21#v35)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:35</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/exodus/21#v35)</sup> And if one man’s ox hurt another’s, that he die; then they shall sell the live ox, and divide the money of it; and the dead ox also they shall divide.

</details>

### 7B.6 {#7B.6}

If an animal which is known to be aggressive kills another animal yet its owner had not confined him, then he shall pay for the animal killed but can keep the dead animal.

[Exod 21:36](/bible/exodus/21#v36)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:36</summary>

> <sup>[36](/bible/exodus/21#v36)</sup> Or if it be known that the ox hath used to push in time past, and his owner hath not kept him in; he shall surely pay ox for ox; and the dead shall be his own.

</details>
