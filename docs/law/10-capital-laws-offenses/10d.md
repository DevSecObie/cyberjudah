### 10D.1 {#10D.1}

A man or a woman with a familiar spirit or who is a wizard shall be put to death.

[Lev 20:27](/bible/leviticus/20#v27); [2 Kgs 23:24](/bible/2-kings/23#v24)

<details><summary>Leviticus 20:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/leviticus/20#v27)</sup> A man also or woman that hath a familiar spirit, or that is a wizard, shall surely be put to death: they shall stone them with stones: their blood shall be upon them.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Kings 23:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/2-kings/23#v24)</sup> Moreover the workers with familiar spirits, and the wizards, and the images, and the idols, and all the abominations that were spied in the land of Judah and in Jerusalem, did Josiah put away, that he might perform the words of the law which were written in the book that Hilkiah the priest found in the house of the Lord.

</details>

### 10D.2 {#10D.2}

You are not to allow a witch or sorceress to live.

[Exod 22:18](/bible/exodus/22#v18)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/exodus/22#v18)</sup> Thou shalt not suffer a witch to live.

</details>
