### 10F.1 {#10F.1}

Anyone that has served or worshipped false or pagan gods shall be put to death.

[Deut 17:2-5](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v2)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 17:2-5</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v2)</sup> If there be found among you, within any of thy gates which the Lord thy God giveth thee, man or woman, that hath wrought wickedness in the sight of the Lord thy God, in transgressing his covenant,
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v3)</sup> And hath gone and served other gods, and worshipped them, either the sun, or moon, or any of the host of heaven, which I have not commanded;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v4)</sup> And it be told thee, and thou hast heard of it, and enquired diligently, and, behold, it be true, and the thing certain, that such abomination is wrought in Israel:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/17#v5)</sup> Then shalt thou bring forth that man or that woman, which have committed that wicked thing, unto thy gates, even that man or that woman, and shalt stone them with stones, till they die.

</details>

### 10F.2 {#10F.2}

Idolaters are to be destroyed.

[Deut 13:13-15](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v13)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 13:13-15</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v13)</sup> Certain men, the children of Belial, are gone out from among you, and have withdrawn the inhabitants of their city, saying, Let us go and serve other gods, which ye have not known;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v14)</sup> Then shalt thou enquire, and make search, and ask diligently; and, behold, if it be truth, and the thing certain, that such abomination is wrought among you;
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v15)</sup> Thou shalt surely smite the inhabitants of that city with the edge of the sword, destroying it utterly, and all that is therein, and the cattle thereof, with the edge of the sword.

</details>

### 10F.3 {#10F.3}

A preacher or a prophet who leads people to worship false gods is to be put to death, because he has counseled rebel against God.

[Deut 13:1-5](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 13:1-5</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v1)</sup> If there arise among you a prophet, or a dreamer of dreams, and giveth thee a sign or a wonder,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v2)</sup> And the sign or the wonder come to pass, whereof he spake unto thee, saying, Let us go after other gods, which thou hast not known, and let us serve them;
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v3)</sup> Thou shalt not hearken unto the words of that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams: for the Lord your God proveth you, to know whether ye love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v4)</sup> Ye shall walk after the Lord your God, and fear him, and keep his commandments, and obey his voice, and ye shall serve him, and cleave unto him.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v5)</sup> And that prophet, or that dreamer of dreams, shall be put to death; because he hath spoken to turn you away from the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt, and redeemed you out of the house of bondage, to thrust thee out of the way which the Lord thy God commanded thee to walk in. So shalt thou put the evil away from the midst of thee.

</details>

### 10F.4 {#10F.4}

Those who entice others to serve pagan gods and religions are to be put to death.

[Deut 13:6-11](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v6)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 13:6-11</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v6)</sup> If thy brother, the son of thy mother, or thy son, or thy daughter, or the wife of thy bosom, or thy friend, which is as thine own soul, entice thee secretly, saying, Let us go and serve other gods, which thou hast not known, thou, nor thy fathers;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v7)</sup> Namely, of the gods of the people which are round about you, nigh unto thee, or far off from thee, from the one end of the earth even unto the other end of the earth;
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v8)</sup> Thou shalt not consent unto him, nor hearken unto him; neither shall thine eye pity him, neither shalt thou spare, neither shalt thou conceal him:
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v9)</sup> But thou shalt surely kill him; thine hand shall be first upon him to put him to death, and afterwards the hand of all the people.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v10)</sup> And thou shalt stone him with stones, that he die; because he hath sought to thrust thee away from the Lord thy God, which brought thee out of the land of Egypt, from the house of bondage.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/13#v11)</sup> And all Israel shall hear, and fear, and shall do no more any such wickedness as this is among you.

</details>

### 10F.5 {#10F.5}

He that blasphemes the name of God shall be put to death.

[Lev 24:16](/bible/leviticus/24#v16); [1 Kgs 21:10,13](/bible/1-kings/21#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 24:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/24#v16)</sup> And he that blasphemeth the name of the Lord, he shall surely be put to death, and all the congregation shall certainly stone him: as well the stranger, as he that is born in the land, when he blasphemeth the name of the Lord, shall be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Kings 21:10,13</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-kings/21#v10)</sup> And set two men, sons of Belial, before him, to bear witness against him, saying, Thou didst blaspheme God and the king. And then carry him out, and stone him, that he may die.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/1-kings/21#v13)</sup> And there came in two men, children of Belial, and sat before him: and the men of Belial witnessed against him, even against Naboth, in the presence of the people, saying, Naboth did blaspheme God and the king. Then they carried him forth out of the city, and stoned him with stones, that he died.

</details>

### 10F.6 {#10F.6}

He that sacrifices to any god, other than the LORD God alone, shall be utterly destroyed.

[Exod 22:20](/bible/exodus/22#v20)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/exodus/22#v20)</sup> He that sacrificeth unto any god, save unto the Lord only, he shall be utterly destroyed.

</details>
