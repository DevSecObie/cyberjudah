### 10J.1 {#10J.1}

The adulterer and adulteress are to be put to death.

[Lev 20:10](/bible/leviticus/20#v10); [Deut 22:22](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v22); [Ezek 18:11,13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v11)

<details><summary>Leviticus 20:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/leviticus/20#v10)</sup> And the man that committeth adultery with another man’s wife, even he that committeth adultery with his neighbour’s wife, the adulterer and the adulteress shall surely be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v22)</sup> If a man be found lying with a woman married to an husband, then they shall both of them die, both the man that lay with the woman, and the woman: so shalt thou put away evil from Israel.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:11,13</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/18#v11)</sup> And that doeth not any of those duties, but even hath eaten upon the mountains, and defiled his neighbour’s wife,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v13)</sup> Hath given forth upon usury, and hath taken increase: shall he then live? he shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him.

</details>

### 10J.2 {#10J.2}

Sexual relations to affinity of blood that require death.

[Lev 20:11,12,14](/bible/leviticus/20#v11)

<details><summary>Leviticus 20:11,12,14</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/20#v11)</sup> And the man that lieth with his father’s wife hath uncovered his father’s nakedness: both of them shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/leviticus/20#v12)</sup> And if a man lie with his daughter in law, both of them shall surely be put to death: they have wrought confusion; their blood shall be upon them.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/20#v14)</sup> And if a man take a wife and her mother, it is wickedness: they shall be burnt with fire, both he and they; that there be no wickedness among you.

</details>

Those guilty of sodomy (homosexuality) to be put to death.

<details><summary>Leviticus 18:22</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/leviticus/18#v22)</sup> Thou shalt not lie with mankind, as with womankind: it is abomination.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 20:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/leviticus/20#v13)</sup> If a man also lie with mankind, as he lieth with a woman, both of them have committed an abomination: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

</details>

### 10J.6 {#10J.6}

[Exod 22:19](/bible/exodus/22#v19); [Lev 18:23](/bible/leviticus/18#v23); [Lev 20:15-16](/bible/leviticus/20#v15); [Deut 27:21](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v21)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/exodus/22#v19)</sup> Whosoever lieth with a beast shall surely be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 18:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/leviticus/18#v23)</sup> Neither shalt thou lie with any beast to defile thyself therewith: neither shall any woman stand before a beast to lie down thereto: it is confusion.

</details>
<details><summary>Leviticus 20:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/20#v15)</sup> And if a man lie with a beast, he shall surely be put to death: and ye shall slay the beast.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/20#v16)</sup> And if a woman approach unto any beast, and lie down thereto, thou shalt kill the woman, and the beast: they shall surely be put to death; their blood shall be upon them.

</details>

<details><summary>Leviticus 21:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/21#v9)</sup> And the daughter of any priest, if she profane herself by playing the whore, she profaneth her father: she shall be burnt with fire.

</details>

> <sup>[21](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v21)</sup> Then they shall bring out the damsel to the door of her father’s house, and the men of her city shall stone her with stones that she die: because she hath wrought folly in Israel, to play the whore in her father’s house: so shalt thou put evil away from among you.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:17</summary>

</details>

### 10J.8 {#10J.8}

If a virgin betrothed to a husband lie with another man, both the man and woman shall be put to death.

[Deut 22:24](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v24)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v24)</sup> Then ye shall bring them both out unto the gate of that city, and ye shall stone them with stones that they die; the damsel, because she cried not, being in the city; and the man, because he hath humbled his neighbour’s wife: so thou shalt put away evil from among you.

</details>
