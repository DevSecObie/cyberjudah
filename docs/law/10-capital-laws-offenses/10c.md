### 10C.1 {#10C.1}

He that kidnaps a man, whether he sells him or he is found in his possession, he shall be put to death.

[Exod 21:16](/bible/exodus/21#v16); [Deut 24:7](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v7)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/21#v16)</sup> And he that stealeth a man, and selleth him, or if he be found in his hand, he shall surely be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v7)</sup> If a man be found stealing any of his brethren of the children of Israel, and maketh merchandise of him, or selleth him; then that thief shall die; and thou shalt put evil away from among you.

</details>
