### 10B.1 {#10B.1}

Every one that defiles the Sabbath by doing servile work on that day shall be put to death.

[Exod 31:14-15](/bible/exodus/31#v14); [Exod 35:2](/bible/exodus/35#v2); [Num 15:32-36](/bible/numbers/15#v32)

<details><summary>Exodus 31:14-15</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/31#v14)</sup> Ye shall keep the sabbath therefore; for it is holy unto you: every one that defileth it shall surely be put to death: for whosoever doeth any work therein, that soul shall be cut off from among his people.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/31#v15)</sup> Six days may work be done; but in the seventh is the sabbath of rest, holy to the Lord: whosoever doeth any work in the sabbath day, he shall surely be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 35:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/exodus/35#v2)</sup> Six days shall work be done, but on the seventh day there shall be to you an holy day, a sabbath of rest to the Lord: whosoever doeth work therein shall be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 15:32-36</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/numbers/15#v32)</sup> And while the children of Israel were in the wilderness, they found a man that gathered sticks upon the sabbath day.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/15#v33)</sup> And they that found him gathering sticks brought him unto Moses and Aaron, and unto all the congregation.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/numbers/15#v34)</sup> And they put him in ward, because it was not declared what should be done to him.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/numbers/15#v35)</sup> And the Lord said unto Moses, The man shall be surely put to death: all the congregation shall stone him with stones without the camp.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/numbers/15#v36)</sup> And all the congregation brought him without the camp, and stoned him with stones, and he died; as the Lord commanded Moses.

</details>
