### 10A.1 {#10A.1}

Thou shall not murder.

[Exod 20:13](/bible/exodus/20#v13); [Deut 5:17](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v17); [Matt 5:2](/bible/matthew/5#v2); [Matt 19:18](/bible/matthew/19#v18); [Rom 13:9](/bible/romans/13#v9)

<details><summary>Exodus 20:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/20#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not kill.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 5:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/5#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not kill.

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 5:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/matthew/5#v2)</sup> And he opened his mouth, and taught them, saying,

</details>
<details><summary>Matthew 19:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/matthew/19#v18)</sup> He saith unto him, Which? Jesus said, Thou shalt do no murder, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness,

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/romans/13#v9)</sup> For this, Thou shalt not commit adultery, Thou shalt not kill, Thou shalt not steal, Thou shalt not bear false witness, Thou shalt not covet; and if there be any other commandment, it is briefly comprehended in this saying, namely, Thou shalt love thy neighbour as thyself.

</details>

### 10A.2 {#10A.2}

Shedding of innocent blood is an abomination to God.

[Prov 6:16-17](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:16-17</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/proverbs/6#v16)</sup> These six things doth the Lord hate: yea, seven are an abomination unto him:
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/6#v17)</sup> A proud look, a lying tongue, and hands that shed innocent blood,

</details>

### 10A.3 {#10A.3}

God requires the blood of a murderer for it defiles the land.

[Gen 9:5-6](/bible/genesis/9#v5); [Num 35:33](/bible/numbers/35#v33)

<details><summary>Genesis 9:5-6</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/genesis/9#v5)</sup> And surely your blood of your lives will I require; at the hand of every beast will I require it, and at the hand of man; at the hand of every man’s brother will I require the life of man.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/genesis/9#v6)</sup> Whoso sheddeth man’s blood, by man shall his blood be shed: for in the image of God made he man.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 35:33</summary>

> <sup>[33](/bible/numbers/35#v33)</sup> So ye shall not pollute the land wherein ye are: for blood it defileth the land: and the land cannot be cleansed of the blood that is shed therein, but by the blood of him that shed it.

</details>

### 10A.4 {#10A.4}

Those guilty of murder are to be put to death.

[Lev 24:17,21](/bible/leviticus/24#v17); [Num 35:16,21,30-31](/bible/numbers/35#v16)

<details><summary>Leviticus 24:17,21</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/24#v17)</sup> And he that killeth any man shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/leviticus/24#v21)</sup> And he that killeth a beast, he shall restore it: and he that killeth a man, he shall be put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 35:16,21,30-31</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/35#v16)</sup> And if he smite him with an instrument of iron, so that he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/numbers/35#v21)</sup> Or in enmity smite him with his hand, that he die: he that smote him shall surely be put to death; for he is a murderer: the revenger of blood shall slay the murderer, when he meeteth him.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/numbers/35#v30)</sup> Whoso killeth any person, the murderer shall be put to death by the mouth of witnesses: but one witness shall not testify against any person to cause him to die.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/numbers/35#v31)</sup> Moreover ye shall take no satisfaction for the life of a murderer, which is guilty of death: but he shall be surely put to death.

</details>

### 10A.5 {#10A.5}

If a man acts with premeditation against his neighbor, to kill him with guile, he shall die.

[Exod 21:14](/bible/exodus/21#v14)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/21#v14)</sup> But if a man come presumptuously upon his neighbour, to slay him with guile; thou shalt take him from mine altar, that he may die.

</details>

### 10A.6 {#10A.6}

One who strikes a man so that he dies is a murderer and shall be put to death.

[Exod 21:12](/bible/exodus/21#v12); [Num 35:16-18](/bible/numbers/35#v16)

<details><summary>Exodus 21:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/21#v12)</sup> He that smiteth a man, so that he die, shall be surely put to death.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 35:16-18</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/numbers/35#v16)</sup> And if he smite him with an instrument of iron, so that he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/numbers/35#v17)</sup> And if he smite him with throwing a stone, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/numbers/35#v18)</sup> Or if he smite him with an hand weapon of wood, wherewith he may die, and he die, he is a murderer: the murderer shall surely be put to death.

</details>

### 10A.7 {#10A.7}

Killing for hire is condemned (the assassin and the one who hires him are both guilty of murder).

[Deut 27:25](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v25); [Ezek 22:12](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 27:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/deuteronomy/27#v25)</sup> Cursed be he that taketh reward to slay an innocent person. And all the people shall say, Amen.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 22:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)</sup> In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.

</details>

### 10A.8 {#10A.8}

You are not to act against the life of your neighbor.

[Lev 19:16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:16</summary>

> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/19#v16)</sup> Thou shalt not go up and down as a talebearer among thy people: neither shalt thou stand against the blood of thy neighbour: I am the Lord.

</details>

### 10A.9 {#10A.9}

The law concerning unsolved murders.

[Deut 21:1-9](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 21:1-9</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v1)</sup> If one be found slain in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee to possess it, lying in the field, and it be not known who hath slain him:
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v2)</sup> Then thy elders and thy judges shall come forth, and they shall measure unto the cities which are round about him that is slain:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v3)</sup> And it shall be, that the city which is next unto the slain man, even the elders of that city shall take an heifer, which hath not been wrought with, and which hath not drawn in the yoke;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v4)</sup> And the elders of that city shall bring down the heifer unto a rough valley, which is neither eared nor sown, and shall strike off the heifer’s neck there in the valley:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v5)</sup> And the priests the sons of Levi shall come near; for them the Lord thy God hath chosen to minister unto him, and to bless in the name of the Lord; and by their word shall every controversy and every stroke be tried:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v6)</sup> And all the elders of that city, that are next unto the slain man, shall wash their hands over the heifer that is beheaded in the valley:
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v7)</sup> And they shall answer and say, Our hands have not shed this blood, neither have our eyes seen it.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v8)</sup> Be merciful, O Lord, unto thy people Israel, whom thou hast redeemed, and lay not innocent blood unto thy people of Israel’s charge. And the blood shall be forgiven them.
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v9)</sup> So shalt thou put away the guilt of innocent blood from among you, when thou shalt do that which is right in the sight of the Lord.

</details>
