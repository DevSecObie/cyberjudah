### 16D.1 {#16D.1}

You are not to deal dishonestly or falsely with others when you buy and sell.

[Lev 19:11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11); [2 Kgs 22:7](/bible/2-kings/22#v7); [Acts 5:1-6](/bible/acts/5#v1)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/leviticus/19#v11)</sup> Ye shall not steal, neither deal falsely, neither lie one to another.

</details>
<details><summary>2 Kings 22:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/2-kings/22#v7)</sup> Howbeit there was no reckoning made with them of the money that was delivered into their hand, because they dealt faithfully.

</details>
<details><summary>Acts 5:1-6</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/acts/5#v1)</sup> But a certain man named Ananias, with Sapphira his wife, sold a possession,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/acts/5#v2)</sup> And kept back part of the price, his wife also being privy to it, and brought a certain part, and laid it at the apostles’ feet.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/acts/5#v3)</sup> But Peter said, Ananias, why hath Satan filled thine heart to lie to the Holy Ghost, and to keep back part of the price of the land?
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/acts/5#v4)</sup> Whiles it remained, was it not thine own? and after it was sold, was it not in thine own power? why hast thou conceived this thing in thine heart? thou hast not lied unto men, but unto God.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/acts/5#v5)</sup> And Ananias hearing these words fell down, and gave up the ghost: and great fear came on all them that heard these things.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/acts/5#v6)</sup> And the young men arose, wound him up, and carried him out, and buried him.

</details>

### 16D.2 {#16D.2}

When you sell to your neighbor or buy from your neighbor, you shall not oppress one another.

[Lev 25:14-17](/bible/leviticus/25#v14)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:14-17</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/leviticus/25#v14)</sup> And if thou sell ought unto thy neighbour, or buyest ought of thy neighbour’s hand, ye shall not oppress one another:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/leviticus/25#v15)</sup> According to the number of years after the jubile thou shalt buy of thy neighbour, and according unto the number of years of the fruits he shall sell unto thee:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/leviticus/25#v16)</sup> According to the multitude of years thou shalt increase the price thereof, and according to the fewness of years thou shalt diminish the price of it: for according to the number of the years of the fruits doth he sell unto thee.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/leviticus/25#v17)</sup> Ye shall not therefore oppress one another; but thou shalt fear thy God: for I am the Lord your God.

</details>

### 16D.3 {#16D.3}

You shall not be unrighteous or have unjust gain in trading, selling or buying.

[Prov 28:8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8); [Isa 33:15](/bible/isaiah/33#v15); [Ezek 28:4-8,18](/bible/ezekiel/28#v4)

<details><summary>Proverbs 28:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8)</sup> He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor.

</details>
<details><summary>Isaiah 33:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/isaiah/33#v15)</sup> He that walketh righteously, and speaketh uprightly; he that despiseth the gain of oppressions, that shaketh his hands from holding of bribes, that stoppeth his ears from hearing of blood, and shutteth his eyes from seeing evil;

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 28:4-8,18</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/ezekiel/28#v4)</sup> With thy wisdom and with thine understanding thou hast gotten thee riches, and hast gotten gold and silver into thy treasures:
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/28#v5)</sup> By thy great wisdom and by thy traffick hast thou increased thy riches, and thine heart is lifted up because of thy riches:
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/ezekiel/28#v6)</sup> Therefore thus saith the Lord God; Because thou hast set thine heart as the heart of God;
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/28#v7)</sup> Behold, therefore I will bring strangers upon thee, the terrible of the nations: and they shall draw their swords against the beauty of thy wisdom, and they shall defile thy brightness.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/28#v8)</sup> They shall bring thee down to the pit, and thou shalt die the deaths of them that are slain in the midst of the seas.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/28#v18)</sup> Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

</details>
