### 16A.1 {#16A.1}

There shall be a cancellation of debt every seven years - a creditor can only hold a debt for 6 years and must release the obligation on the seventh year.

[Deut 15:1-2](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v1)</sup> At the end of every seven years thou shalt make a release.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v2)</sup> And this is the manner of the release: Every creditor that lendeth ought unto his neighbour shall release it; he shall not exact it of his neighbour, or of his brother; because it is called the Lord’s release.

</details>

### 16A.2 {#16A.2}

Debts of foreigners may be continued beyond seven years.

[Deut 15:3](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v3)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v3)</sup> Of a foreigner thou mayest exact it again: but that which is thine with thy brother thine hand shall release;

</details>

### 16A.3 {#16A.3}

Those who need to borrow from you are not to be turned away.

[Matt 5:42](/bible/matthew/5#v42)

<details><summary>Matthew 5:42</summary>

> <sup>[42](/bible/matthew/5#v42)</sup> Give to him that asketh thee, and from him that would borrow of thee turn not thou away.

</details>

### 16A.4 {#16A.4}

The borrower is servant to the lender.

[Prov 22:7](/bible/proverbs/22#v7)

<details><summary>Proverbs 22:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/22#v7)</sup> The rich ruleth over the poor, and the borrower is servant to the lender.

</details>

### 16A.5 {#16A.5}

You shall not think evil and withhold loaning to your brother in need because the Seventh Year, the year of release, is at hand. You shall surely give to him and the LORD will bless you in all your works.

[Deut 15:9-10](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v9)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:9-10</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v9)</sup> Beware that there be not a thought in thy wicked heart, saying, The seventh year, the year of release, is at hand; and thine eye be evil against thy poor brother, and thou givest him nought; and he cry unto the Lord against thee, and it be sin unto thee.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v10)</sup> Thou shalt surely give him, and thine heart shall not be grieved when thou givest unto him: because that for this thing the Lord thy God shall bless thee in all thy works, and in all that thou puttest thine hand unto.

</details>

### 16A.6 {#16A.6}

It is wicked to borrow and not repay.

[Ps 37:21](/bible/psalms/37#v21); [Prov 3:27](/bible/proverbs/3#v27); [Rom 13:8](/bible/romans/13#v8)

<details><summary>Psalms 37:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/psalms/37#v21)</sup> The wicked borroweth, and payeth not again: but the righteous sheweth mercy, and giveth.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 3:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/3#v27)</sup> Withhold not good from them to whom it is due, when it is in the power of thine hand to do it.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/13#v8)</sup> Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.

</details>

### 16A.7 {#16A.7}

Lend to the poor and needy what is sufficient for his needs.

[Deut 15:8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v8)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v8)</sup> But thou shalt open thine hand wide unto him, and shalt surely lend him sufficient for his need, in that which he wanteth.

</details>

### 16A.8 {#16A.8}

It is best to owe no man any thing.

[Rom 13:8](/bible/romans/13#v8)

<details><summary>Romans 13:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/romans/13#v8)</sup> Owe no man any thing, but to love one another: for he that loveth another hath fulfilled the law.

</details>

### 16A.9 {#16A.9}

Items necessary to sustain a livelihood are not to be given as a pledge (security) for a debt.

[Deut 24:6](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v6); [Job 24:3](/bible/job/24#v3)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v6)</sup> No man shall take the nether or the upper millstone to pledge: for he taketh a man’s life to pledge.

</details>
<details><summary>Job 24:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/job/24#v3)</sup> They drive away the ass of the fatherless, they take the widow’s ox for a pledge.

</details>

### 16A.10 {#16A.10}

You shall not take a widow's garment as a pledge.

[Deut 24:17](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v17)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v17)</sup> Thou shalt not pervert the judgment of the stranger, nor of the fatherless; nor take a widow’s raiment to pledge:

</details>

### 16A.11 {#16A.11}

Warnings against giving sureties for debts of others.

[Prov 6:1-3](/bible/proverbs/6#v1); [Prov 17:18](/bible/proverbs/17#v18); [Prov 22:26-27](/bible/proverbs/22#v26)

<details><summary>Proverbs 6:1-3</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/6#v1)</sup> My son, if thou be surety for thy friend, if thou hast stricken thy hand with a stranger,
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/proverbs/6#v2)</sup> Thou art snared with the words of thy mouth, thou art taken with the words of thy mouth.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/6#v3)</sup> Do this now, my son, and deliver thyself, when thou art come into the hand of thy friend; go, humble thyself, and make sure thy friend.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 17:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/proverbs/17#v18)</sup> A man void of understanding striketh hands, and becometh surety in the presence of his friend.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 22:26-27</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/proverbs/22#v26)</sup> Be not thou one of them that strike hands, or of them that are sureties for debts.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/proverbs/22#v27)</sup> If thou hast nothing to pay, why should he take away thy bed from under thee?

</details>

### 16A.12 {#16A.12}

He who is surety for a stranger will suffer for it, but one who hates being surety is secure.

[Prov 11:15](/bible/proverbs/11#v15)

<details><summary>Proverbs 11:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/proverbs/11#v15)</sup> He that is surety for a stranger shall smart for it: and he that hateth suretiship is sure.

</details>

### 16A.13 {#16A.13}

Pledges given by the poor for debt are not to be retained overnight.

[Exod 22:26](/bible/exodus/22#v26); [Deut 24:12-13](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v12); [Job 24:9-10](/bible/job/24#v9)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:26</summary>

> <sup>[26](/bible/exodus/22#v26)</sup> If thou at all take thy neighbour’s raiment to pledge, thou shalt deliver it unto him by that the sun goeth down:

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v12)</sup> And if the man be poor, thou shalt not sleep with his pledge:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v13)</sup> In any case thou shalt deliver him the pledge again when the sun goeth down, that he may sleep in his own raiment, and bless thee: and it shall be righteousness unto thee before the Lord thy God.

</details>
<details><summary>Job 24:9-10</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/job/24#v9)</sup> They pluck the fatherless from the breast, and take a pledge of the poor.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/job/24#v10)</sup> They cause him to go naked without clothing, and they take away the sheaf from the hungry;

</details>

### 16A.14 {#16A.14}

When you lend your brother anything you shall not go into his house to take his pledge, but rather he shall bring the pledge out to you.

[Deut 24:10-11](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v10)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 24:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v10)</sup> When thou dost lend thy brother any thing, thou shalt not go into his house to fetch his pledge.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/deuteronomy/24#v11)</sup> Thou shalt stand abroad, and the man to whom thou dost lend shall bring out the pledge abroad unto thee.

</details>

### 16A.15 {#16A.15}

The righteous man restores to the debtor his pledge, but the unrighteous does not restore a pledge.

[Ezek 18:5,7,12](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:5,7,12</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/ezekiel/18#v5)</sup> But if a man be just, and do that which is lawful and right,
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/ezekiel/18#v7)</sup> And hath not oppressed any, but hath restored to the debtor his pledge, hath spoiled none by violence, hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment;
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/18#v12)</sup> Hath oppressed the poor and needy, hath spoiled by violence, hath not restored the pledge, and hath lifted up his eyes to the idols, hath committed abomination,

</details>
