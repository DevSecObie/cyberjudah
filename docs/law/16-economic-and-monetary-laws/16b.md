### 16B.1 {#16B.1}

Produce, commodities, and money are to follow just weights and measures in their use and exchange.

[Lev 19:35-36](/bible/leviticus/19#v35); [Ezek 45:10-14](/bible/ezekiel/45#v10)

<details><summary>Leviticus 19:35-36</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/leviticus/19#v35)</sup> Ye shall do no unrighteousness in judgment, in meteyard, in weight, or in measure.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/19#v36)</sup> Just balances, just weights, a just ephah, and a just hin, shall ye have: I am the Lord your God, which brought you out of the land of Egypt.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 45:10-14</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/45#v10)</sup> Ye shall have just balances, and a just ephah, and a just bath.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/45#v11)</sup> The ephah and the bath shall be of one measure, that the bath may contain the tenth part of an homer, and the ephah the tenth part of an homer: the measure thereof shall be after the homer.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/45#v12)</sup> And the shekel shall be twenty gerahs: twenty shekels, five and twenty shekels, fifteen shekels, shall be your maneh.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/45#v13)</sup> This is the oblation that ye shall offer; the sixth part of an ephah of an homer of wheat, and ye shall give the sixth part of an ephah of an homer of barley:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/45#v14)</sup> Concerning the ordinance of oil, the bath of oil, ye shall offer the tenth part of a bath out of the cor, which is an homer of ten baths; for ten baths are an homer:

</details>

### 16B.2 {#16B.2}

A just weight and balance are of God's work.

[Prov 16:11](/bible/proverbs/16#v11)

<details><summary>Proverbs 16:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/proverbs/16#v11)</sup> A just weight and balance are the Lord’s: all the weights of the bag are his work.

</details>

### 16B.3 {#16B.3}

Those who use unjust and deceitful weights are wicked.

[Mic 6:10-11](/bible/micah/6#v10)

<details><summary>Micah 6:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/micah/6#v10)</sup> Are there yet the treasures of wickedness in the house of the wicked, and the scant measure that is abominable?
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/micah/6#v11)</sup> Shall I count them pure with the wicked balances, and with the bag of deceitful weights?

</details>

### 16B.4 {#16B.4}

You shall not have in your bag differing weights, a heavy and light. You shall not have in your house differing measures, large and a small. You shall have a perfect and just weight and measure.

[Deut 25:13-15](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v13)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 25:13-15</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v13)</sup> Thou shalt not have in thy bag divers weights, a great and a small.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v14)</sup> Thou shalt not have in thine house divers measures, a great and a small.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/deuteronomy/25#v15)</sup> But thou shalt have a perfect and just weight, a perfect and just measure shalt thou have: that thy days may be lengthened in the land which the Lord thy God giveth thee.

</details>

### 16B.5 {#16B.5}

A false balance and diverse weights and measures are an abomination to the LORD; but a just weight is His delight.

[Prov 11:1](/bible/proverbs/11#v1); [Prov 20:10,23](/bible/proverbs/20#v10)

<details><summary>Proverbs 11:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/11#v1)</sup> A false balance is abomination to the Lord: but a just weight is his delight.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 20:10,23</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/proverbs/20#v10)</sup> Divers weights, and divers measures, both of them are alike abomination to the Lord.
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/proverbs/20#v23)</sup> Divers weights are an abomination unto the Lord; and a false balance is not good.

</details>

### 16B.6 {#16B.6}

Punishments for not using just weights and measures.

[Amos 8:5-8](/bible/amos/8#v5)

<details><summary>Amos 8:5-8</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/8#v5)</sup> Saying, When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell corn? and the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat, making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit?
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/8#v6)</sup> That we may buy the poor for silver, and the needy for a pair of shoes; yea, and sell the refuse of the wheat?
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/8#v7)</sup> The Lord hath sworn by the excellency of Jacob, Surely I will never forget any of their works.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/amos/8#v8)</sup> Shall not the land tremble for this, and every one mourn that dwelleth therein? and it shall rise up wholly as a flood; and it shall be cast out and drowned, as by the flood of Egypt.

</details>
