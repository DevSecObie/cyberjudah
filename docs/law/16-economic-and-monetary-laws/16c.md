### 16C.1 {#16C.1}

If you lend to any of your brethren in need, you are not to take or lay usury upon them.

[Exod 22:25](/bible/exodus/22#v25)

<details><summary>Exodus 22:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/exodus/22#v25)</sup> If thou lend money to any of my people that is poor by thee, thou shalt not be to him as an usurer, neither shalt thou lay upon him usury.

</details>

### 16C.2 {#16C.2}

No substance loaned or borrowed is to be repaid with increase or interest-no usury of money, or food, or of anything that is borrowed.

[Lev 25:37](/bible/leviticus/25#v37); [Deut 23:19](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v19)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:37</summary>

> <sup>[37](/bible/leviticus/25#v37)</sup> Thou shalt not give him thy money upon usury, nor lend him thy victuals for increase.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v19)</sup> Thou shalt not lend upon usury to thy brother; usury of money, usury of victuals, usury of any thing that is lent upon usury:

</details>

### 16C.3 {#16C.3}

You may charge usury to a foreigner.

[Deut 23:20](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v20)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 23:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/23#v20)</sup> Unto a stranger thou mayest lend upon usury; but unto thy brother thou shalt not lend upon usury: that the Lord thy God may bless thee in all that thou settest thine hand to in the land whither thou goest to possess it.

</details>

### 16C.4 {#16C.4}

Usury and the usurer are condemned.

[Neh 5:7,10](/bible/nehemiah/5#v7); [Ps 15:1,5](/bible/psalms/15#v1); [Prov 28:8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8)

<details><summary>Nehemiah 5:7,10</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/nehemiah/5#v7)</sup> Then I consulted with myself, and I rebuked the nobles, and the rulers, and said unto them, Ye exact usury, every one of his brother. And I set a great assembly against them.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/nehemiah/5#v10)</sup> I likewise, and my brethren, and my servants, might exact of them money and corn: I pray you, let us leave off this usury.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 15:1,5</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/15#v5)</sup> He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 28:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8)</sup> He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor.

</details>

### 16C.5 {#16C.5}

If one of your brethren becomes poor, you shall exact no usury or interest from him; you shall not lend him your money for usury, nor lend him your food at a profit.

[Lev 25:36](/bible/leviticus/25#v36)

<details><summary>Leviticus 25:36</summary>

> <sup>[36](/bible/leviticus/25#v36)</sup> Take thou no usury of him, or increase: but fear thy God; that thy brother may live with thee.

</details>

### 16C.6 {#16C.6}

Usury is regarded as dishonest profit.

[Prov 28:8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8); [Ezek 22:12-13](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)

<details><summary>Proverbs 28:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/proverbs/28#v8)</sup> He that by usury and unjust gain increaseth his substance, he shall gather it for him that will pity the poor.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezekiel 22:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/22#v12)</sup> In thee have they taken gifts to shed blood; thou hast taken usury and increase, and thou hast greedily gained of thy neighbours by extortion, and hast forgotten me, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/22#v13)</sup> Behold, therefore I have smitten mine hand at thy dishonest gain which thou hast made, and at thy blood which hath been in the midst of thee.

</details>

### 16C.7 {#16C.7}

Usury is an iniquity, for the righteous man does not lend money on interest or take increase.

[Ezek 18:8-13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v8)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:8-13</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/ezekiel/18#v8)</sup> He that hath not given forth upon usury, neither hath taken any increase, that hath withdrawn his hand from iniquity, hath executed true judgment between man and man,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/ezekiel/18#v9)</sup> Hath walked in my statutes, and hath kept my judgments, to deal truly; he is just, he shall surely live, saith the Lord God.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ezekiel/18#v10)</sup> If he beget a son that is a robber, a shedder of blood, and that doeth the like to any one of these things,
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ezekiel/18#v11)</sup> And that doeth not any of those duties, but even hath eaten upon the mountains, and defiled his neighbour’s wife,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/ezekiel/18#v12)</sup> Hath oppressed the poor and needy, hath spoiled by violence, hath not restored the pledge, and hath lifted up his eyes to the idols, hath committed abomination,
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/ezekiel/18#v13)</sup> Hath given forth upon usury, and hath taken increase: shall he then live? he shall not live: he hath done all these abominations; he shall surely die; his blood shall be upon him.

</details>

### 16C.8 {#16C.8}

He who does not put out his money at usury may dwell in the LORD's holy nation.

[Ps 15:1,5](/bible/psalms/15#v1)

<details><summary>Psalms 15:1,5</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/15#v1)</sup> Lord, who shall abide in thy tabernacle? who shall dwell in thy holy hill?
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/psalms/15#v5)</sup> He that putteth not out his money to usury, nor taketh reward against the innocent. He that doeth these things shall never be moved.

</details>

### 16C.9 {#16C.9}

Sons are not to suffer punishment for usury exacted by their fathers.

[Ezek 18:14-17](/bible/ezekiel/18#v14)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 18:14-17</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/ezekiel/18#v14)</sup> Now, lo, if he beget a son, that seeth all his father’s sins which he hath done, and considereth, and doeth not such like,
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/ezekiel/18#v15)</sup> That hath not eaten upon the mountains, neither hath lifted up his eyes to the idols of the house of Israel, hath not defiled his neighbour’s wife,
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/ezekiel/18#v16)</sup> Neither hath oppressed any, hath not withholden the pledge, neither hath spoiled by violence, but hath given his bread to the hungry, and hath covered the naked with a garment,
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/ezekiel/18#v17)</sup> That hath taken off his hand from the poor, that hath not received usury nor increase, hath executed my judgments, hath walked in my statutes; he shall not die for the iniquity of his father, he shall surely live.

</details>
