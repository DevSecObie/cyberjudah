### 16E.1 {#16E.1}

Taxes are paid for the support of godly governments.

<details><summary>Romans 13:6</summary>

### 16E.2 {#16E.2}

A poll or head tax can be levied (which is to be of a small amount) according to a census taken on persons of a certain age.

[Exod 30:12-15](/bible/exodus/30#v12); [Lev 27:3-8](/bible/leviticus/27#v3); [Num 3:40-51](/bible/numbers/3#v40)

<details><summary>Exodus 30:12-15</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/30#v12)</sup> When thou takest the sum of the children of Israel after their number, then shall they give every man a ransom for his soul unto the Lord, when thou numberest them; that there be no plague among them, when thou numberest them.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/30#v13)</sup> This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are numbered, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary: (a shekel is twenty gerahs:) an half shekel shall be the offering of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/30#v14)</sup> Every one that passeth among them that are numbered, from twenty years old and above, shall give an offering unto the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/30#v15)</sup> The rich shall not give more, and the poor shall not give less than half a shekel, when they give an offering unto the Lord, to make an atonement for your souls.

</details>

> <sup>[6](/bible/leviticus/27#v6)</sup> And if it be from a month old even unto five years old, then thy estimation shall be of the male five shekels of silver, and for the female thy estimation shall be three shekels of silver.

>
> <sup>[8](/bible/leviticus/27#v8)</sup> But if he be poorer than thy estimation, then he shall present himself before the priest, and the priest shall value him; according to his ability that vowed shall the priest value him.

### 16E.4 {#16E.4}

Every man shall give as he is able, according to the blessings God has given him (advalorern tax).

[Deut 16:17](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v17); [1 Cor 16:1-2](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v1)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 16:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/deuteronomy/16#v17)</sup> Every man shall give as he is able, according to the blessing of the Lord thy God which he hath given thee.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 16:1-2</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v1)</sup> Now concerning the collection for the saints, as I have given order to the churches of Galatia, even so do ye.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/1-corinthians/16#v2)</sup> Upon the first day of the week let every one of you lay by him in store, as God hath prospered him, that there be no gatherings when I come.

</details>

>
> <sup>[33](/bible/nehemiah/9#v33)</sup> Howbeit thou art just in all that is brought upon us; for thou hast done right, but we have done wickedly:
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/nehemiah/9#v34)</sup> Neither have our kings, our princes, our priests, nor our fathers, kept thy law, nor hearkened unto thy commandments and thy testimonies, wherewith thou didst testify against them.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/nehemiah/9#v35)</sup> For they have not served thee in their kingdom, and in thy great goodness that thou gavest them, and in the large and fat land which thou gavest before them, neither turned they from their wicked works.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/nehemiah/9#v36)</sup> Behold, we are servants this day, and for the land that thou gavest unto our fathers to eat the fruit thereof and the good thereof, behold, we are servants in it:
>

</details>

Tithes and offerings collected as taxes are to be put into the treasury.

[Neh 10:38](/bible/nehemiah/10#v38); [Neh 13:12-13](/bible/nehemiah/13#v12)

<details><summary>Nehemiah 10:38</summary>

> <sup>[38](/bible/nehemiah/10#v38)</sup> And the priest the son of Aaron shall be with the Levites, when the Levites take tithes: and the Levites shall bring up the tithe of the tithes unto the house of our God, to the chambers, into the treasure house.

</details>
<details><summary>Nehemiah 13:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/nehemiah/13#v12)</sup> Then brought all Judah the tithe of the corn and the new wine and the oil unto the treasuries.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/nehemiah/13#v13)</sup> And I made treasurers over the treasuries, Shelemiah the priest, and Zadok the scribe, and of the Levites, Pedaiah: and next to them was Hanan the son of Zaccur, the son of Mattaniah: for they were counted faithful, and their office was to distribute unto their brethren.

</details>

### 16E.9 {#16E.9}

It is unlawful to impose a toll, tribute, or custom tax upon any priest or ministerial aide.

[Ezra 7:24](/bible/ezra/7#v24)

<details><summary>Ezra 7:24</summary>

> <sup>[24](/bible/ezra/7#v24)</sup> Also we certify you, that touching any of the priests and Levites, singers, porters, Nethinims, or ministers of this house of God, it shall not be lawful to impose toll, tribute, or custom, upon them.

</details>
