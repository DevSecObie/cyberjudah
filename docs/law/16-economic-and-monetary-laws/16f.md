### 16F.1 {#16F.1}

Money is to be of a standard fixed weight of a precious metal (silver, gold, etc.).

[Gen 23:15-16](/bible/genesis/23#v15); [Exod 30:13](/bible/exodus/30#v13); [Deut 22:19](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v19); [1 Kgs 10:14](/bible/1-kings/10#v14); [2 Kgs 5:22-23](/bible/2-kings/5#v22); [2 Kgs 6:25](/bible/2-kings/6#v25); [Neh 5:15](/bible/nehemiah/5#v15); [Jer 32:9-10](/bible/jeremiah/32#v9)

<details><summary>Genesis 23:15-16</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/genesis/23#v15)</sup> My lord, hearken unto me: the land is worth four hundred shekels of silver; what is that betwixt me and thee? bury therefore thy dead.
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/23#v16)</sup> And Abraham hearkened unto Ephron; and Abraham weighed to Ephron the silver, which he had named in the audience of the sons of Heth, four hundred shekels of silver, current money with the merchant.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 30:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/30#v13)</sup> This they shall give, every one that passeth among them that are numbered, half a shekel after the shekel of the sanctuary: (a shekel is twenty gerahs:) an half shekel shall be the offering of the Lord.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 22:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/22#v19)</sup> And they shall amerce him in an hundred shekels of silver, and give them unto the father of the damsel, because he hath brought up an evil name upon a virgin of Israel: and she shall be his wife; he may not put her away all his days.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Kings 10:14</summary>

> <sup>[14](/bible/1-kings/10#v14)</sup> Now the weight of gold that came to Solomon in one year was six hundred threescore and six talents of gold,

</details>

>
> <sup>[23](/bible/2-kings/5#v23)</sup> And Naaman said, Be content, take two talents. And he urged him, and bound two talents of silver in two bags, with two changes of garments, and laid them upon two of his servants; and they bare them before him.

### 16F.3 {#16F.3}

Money is to be paid for work done and labor performed.

[2 Kgs 12:11-15](/bible/2-kings/12#v11); [2 Kgs 22:4-6](/bible/2-kings/22#v4); [2 Chr 24:11-13](/bible/2-chronicles/24#v11); [2 Chr 34:9-11](/bible/2-chronicles/34#v9); [Ezra 3:7](/bible/ezra/3#v7)

<details><summary>2 Kings 12:11-15</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-kings/12#v11)</sup> And they gave the money, being told, into the hands of them that did the work, that had the oversight of the house of the Lord: and they laid it out to the carpenters and builders, that wrought upon the house of the Lord,
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-kings/12#v12)</sup> And to masons, and hewers of stone, and to buy timber and hewed stone to repair the breaches of the house of the Lord, and for all that was laid out for the house to repair it.
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/2-kings/12#v13)</sup> Howbeit there were not made for the house of the Lord bowls of silver, snuffers, basons, trumpets, any vessels of gold, or vessels of silver, of the money that was brought into the house of the Lord:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-kings/12#v14)</sup> But they gave that to the workmen, and repaired therewith the house of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-kings/12#v15)</sup> Moreover they reckoned not with the men, into whose hand they delivered the money to be bestowed on workmen: for they dealt faithfully.

</details>
