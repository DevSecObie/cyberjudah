### 11C.1 {#11C.1}

Alliances and covenants (treaties) with pagan or ungodly nations are forbidden.

[Exod 23:32](/bible/exodus/23#v32); [Exod 34:12-16](/bible/exodus/34#v12); [Deut 7:2-4](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v2); [Judg 2:2-3](/bible/judges/2#v2); [Ezra 9:12](/bible/ezra/9#v12); [Ps 106:34-36](/bible/psalms/106#v34)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/exodus/23#v32)</sup> Thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor with their gods.

</details>
<details><summary>Exodus 34:12-16</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/exodus/34#v12)</sup> Take heed to thyself, lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land whither thou goest, lest it be for a snare in the midst of thee:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/exodus/34#v13)</sup> But ye shall destroy their altars, break their images, and cut down their groves:
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/exodus/34#v14)</sup> For thou shalt worship no other god: for the Lord, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/exodus/34#v15)</sup> Lest thou make a covenant with the inhabitants of the land, and they go a whoring after their gods, and do sacrifice unto their gods, and one call thee, and thou eat of his sacrifice;
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/exodus/34#v16)</sup> And thou take of their daughters unto thy sons, and their daughters go a whoring after their gods, and make thy sons go a whoring after their gods.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 7:2-4</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v2)</sup> And when the Lord thy God shall deliver them before thee; thou shalt smite them, and utterly destroy them; thou shalt make no covenant with them, nor shew mercy unto them:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v3)</sup> Neither shalt thou make marriages with them; thy daughter thou shalt not give unto his son, nor his daughter shalt thou take unto thy son.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/deuteronomy/7#v4)</sup> For they will turn away thy son from following me, that they may serve other gods: so will the anger of the Lord be kindled against you, and destroy thee suddenly.

</details>
<details><summary>Judges 2:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/judges/2#v2)</sup> And ye shall make no league with the inhabitants of this land; ye shall throw down their altars: but ye have not obeyed my voice: why have ye done this?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/2#v3)</sup> Wherefore I also said, I will not drive them out from before you; but they shall be as thorns in your sides, and their gods shall be a snare unto you.

</details>
<details><summary>Ezra 9:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/ezra/9#v12)</sup> Now therefore give not your daughters unto their sons, neither take their daughters unto your sons, nor seek their peace or their wealth for ever: that ye may be strong, and eat the good of the land, and leave it for an inheritance to your children for ever.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 106:34-36</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/psalms/106#v34)</sup> They did not destroy the nations, concerning whom the Lord commanded them:
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/psalms/106#v35)</sup> But were mingled among the heathen, and learned their works.
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/psalms/106#v36)</sup> And they served their idols: which were a snare unto them.

</details>

### 11C.3 {#11C.3}

Do not to follow the ways of other nations.

[Deut 12:29-30](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v29)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 12:29-30</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v29)</sup> When the Lord thy God shall cut off the nations from before thee, whither thou goest to possess them, and thou succeedest them, and dwellest in their land;
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/deuteronomy/12#v30)</sup> Take heed to thyself that thou be not snared by following them, after that they be destroyed from before thee; and that thou enquire not after their gods, saying, How did these nations serve their gods? even so will I do likewise.

</details>

### 11C.4 {#11C.4}

A treaty of peace, agreeing to do no harm to another friendly nation, can be enacted.

[Gen 21:22-32](/bible/genesis/21#v22); [Gen 26:28-31](/bible/genesis/26#v28)

<details><summary>Genesis 21:22-32</summary>

> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/21#v22)</sup> And it came to pass at that time, that Abimelech and Phichol the chief captain of his host spake unto Abraham, saying, God is with thee in all that thou doest:
>
> <sup>[23](/bible/genesis/21#v23)</sup> Now therefore swear unto me here by God that thou wilt not deal falsely with me, nor with my son, nor with my son’s son: but according to the kindness that I have done unto thee, thou shalt do unto me, and to the land wherein thou hast sojourned.
>
> <sup>[24](/bible/genesis/21#v24)</sup> And Abraham said, I will swear.
>
> <sup>[25](/bible/genesis/21#v25)</sup> And Abraham reproved Abimelech because of a well of water, which Abimelech’s servants had violently taken away.
>
> <sup>[26](/bible/genesis/21#v26)</sup> And Abimelech said, I wot not who hath done this thing: neither didst thou tell me, neither yet heard I of it, but to day.
>
> <sup>[27](/bible/genesis/21#v27)</sup> And Abraham took sheep and oxen, and gave them unto Abimelech; and both of them made a covenant.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/21#v28)</sup> And Abraham set seven ewe lambs of the flock by themselves.
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/21#v29)</sup> And Abimelech said unto Abraham, What mean these seven ewe lambs which thou hast set by themselves?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/21#v30)</sup> And he said, For these seven ewe lambs shalt thou take of my hand, that they may be a witness unto me, that I have digged this well.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/21#v31)</sup> Wherefore he called that place Beer–sheba; because there they sware both of them.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/genesis/21#v32)</sup> Thus they made a covenant at Beer–sheba: then Abimelech rose up, and Phichol the chief captain of his host, and they returned into the land of the Philistines.

</details>
<details><summary>Genesis 26:28-31</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/genesis/26#v28)</sup> And they said, We saw certainly that the Lord was with thee: and we said, Let there be now an oath betwixt us, even betwixt us and thee, and let us make a covenant with thee;
>
> <sup>[29](/bible/genesis/26#v29)</sup> That thou wilt do us no hurt, as we have not touched thee, and as we have done unto thee nothing but good, and have sent thee away in peace: thou art now the blessed of the Lord.
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/genesis/26#v30)</sup> And he made them a feast, and they did eat and drink.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/genesis/26#v31)</sup> And they rose up betimes in the morning, and sware one to another: and Isaac sent them away, and they departed from him in peace.

</details>

### 11C.5 {#11C.5}

Alliances made with wicked nations will not be fruitful.

[2 Chr 20:35-37](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v35)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 20:35-37</summary>

> <sup>[35](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v35)</sup> And after this did Jehoshaphat king of Judah join himself with Ahaziah king of Israel, who did very wickedly:
>
> <sup>[36](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v36)</sup> And he joined himself with him to make ships to go to Tarshish: and they made the ships in Ezion–geber.
>
> <sup>[37](/bible/2-chronicles/20#v37)</sup> Then Eliezer the son of Dodavah of Mareshah prophesied against Jehoshaphat, saying, Because thou hast joined thyself with Ahaziah, the Lord hath broken thy works. And the ships were broken, that they were not able to go to Tarshish.

</details>
