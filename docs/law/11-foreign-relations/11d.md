### 11D.1 {#11D.1}

We should make an effort, and do what is reasonably possible, to avoid strife and contention (and possible war) with other nations.

[Gen 26:15-22](/bible/genesis/26#v15)

>
> <sup>[16](/bible/genesis/26#v16)</sup> And Abimelech said unto Isaac, Go from us; for thou art much mightier than we.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/genesis/26#v17)</sup> And Isaac departed thence, and pitched his tent in the valley of Gerar, and dwelt there.
>
> <sup>[18](/bible/genesis/26#v18)</sup> And Isaac digged again the wells of water, which they had digged in the days of Abraham his father; for the Philistines had stopped them after the death of Abraham: and he called their names after the names by which his father had called them.

> <sup>[19](/bible/genesis/26#v19)</sup> And Isaac’s servants digged in the valley, and found there a well of springing water.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/genesis/26#v20)</sup> And the herdmen of Gerar did strive with Isaac’s herdmen, saying, The water is ours: and he called the name of the well Esek; because they strove with him.
>

>
> <sup>[22](/bible/genesis/26#v22)</sup> And he removed from thence, and digged another well; and for that they strove not: and he called the name of it Rehoboth; and he said, For now the Lord hath made room for us, and we shall be fruitful in the land.

### 11D.3 {#11D.3}

Iniquity in trading is condemned.

[Ezek 28:18](/bible/ezekiel/28#v18)

<details><summary>Ezekiel 28:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/ezekiel/28#v18)</sup> Thou hast defiled thy sanctuaries by the multitude of thine iniquities, by the iniquity of thy traffick; therefore will I bring forth a fire from the midst of thee, it shall devour thee, and I will bring thee to ashes upon the earth in the sight of all them that behold thee.

</details>

### 11D.5 {#11D.5}

You shall not allow foreigners that are pagans to emigrate into or dwell in your nation; for they shall be as pricks in your eyes and as thorns in your sides, and they shall trouble you in your land.

[Exod 23:33](/bible/exodus/23#v33); [Num 33:5](/bible/numbers/33#v5); [Josh 23:12-13](/bible/joshua/23#v12)

<details><summary>Exodus 23:33</summary>

> <sup>[33](/bible/exodus/23#v33)</sup> They shall not dwell in thy land, lest they make thee sin against me: for if thou serve their gods, it will surely be a snare unto thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Numbers 33:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/numbers/33#v5)</sup> And the children of Israel removed from Rameses, and pitched in Succoth.

</details>
<details><summary>Joshua 23:12-13</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/joshua/23#v12)</sup> Else if ye do in any wise go back, and cleave unto the remnant of these nations, even these that remain among you, and shall make marriages with them, and go in unto them, and they to you:
>
> <sup>[13](/bible/joshua/23#v13)</sup> Know for a certainty that the Lord your God will no more drive out any of these nations from before you; but they shall be snares and traps unto you, and scourges in your sides, and thorns in your eyes, until ye perish from off this good land which the Lord your God hath given you.

</details>

### 11D.6 {#11D.6}

Do not render evil to a nation at peace with you.

[Ps 7:4](/bible/psalms/7#v4)

<details><summary>Psalms 7:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/psalms/7#v4)</sup> If I have rewarded evil unto him that was at peace with me; (yea, I have delivered him that without cause is mine enemy:)

</details>

### 11D.7 {#11D.7}

When in a foreign nation, you may buy and sell with it but meddle not in its affairs.

[Deut 2:5-6,19,28](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v5)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 2:5-6,19,28</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v5)</sup> Meddle not with them; for I will not give you of their land, no, not so much as a foot breadth; because I have given mount Seir unto Esau for a possession.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v6)</sup> Ye shall buy meat of them for money, that ye may eat; and ye shall also buy water of them for money, that ye may drink.
>
> <sup>[19](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v19)</sup> And when thou comest nigh over against the children of Ammon, distress them not, nor meddle with them: for I will not give thee of the land of the children of Ammon any possession; because I have given it unto the children of Lot for a possession.
>
> <sup>[28](/bible/deuteronomy/2#v28)</sup> Thou shalt sell me meat for money, that I may eat; and give me water for money, that I may drink: only I will pass through on my feet;

</details>
