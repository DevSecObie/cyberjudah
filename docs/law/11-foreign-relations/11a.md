### 11A.1 {#11A.1}

We are to pray for deliverance from our enemies.

[1 Sam 12:10-11](/bible/1-samuel/12#v10); [Ps 17:6-9](/bible/psalms/17#v6); [Ps 35:15-17](/bible/psalms/35#v15); [Ps 59:1](/bible/psalms/59#v1); [Ps 64:1](/bible/psalms/64#v1)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 12:10-11</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-samuel/12#v10)</sup> And they cried unto the Lord, and said, We have sinned, because we have forsaken the Lord, and have served Baalim and Ashtaroth: but now deliver us out of the hand of our enemies, and we will serve thee.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/1-samuel/12#v11)</sup> And the Lord sent Jerubbaal, and Bedan, and Jephthah, and Samuel, and delivered you out of the hand of your enemies on every side, and ye dwelled safe.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 17:6-9</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/psalms/17#v6)</sup> I have called upon thee, for thou wilt hear me, O God: incline thine ear unto me, and hear my speech.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/17#v7)</sup> Shew thy marvellous lovingkindness, O thou that savest by thy right hand them which put their trust in thee from those that rise up against them.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/psalms/17#v8)</sup> Keep me as the apple of the eye, hide me under the shadow of thy wings,
>
> <sup>[9](/bible/psalms/17#v9)</sup> From the wicked that oppress me, from my deadly enemies, who compass me about.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 35:15-17</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/psalms/35#v15)</sup> But in mine adversity they rejoiced, and gathered themselves together: yea, the abjects gathered themselves together against me, and I knew it not; they did tear me, and ceased not:
>
> <sup>[16](/bible/psalms/35#v16)</sup> With hypocritical mockers in feasts, they gnashed upon me with their teeth.
>
> <sup>[17](/bible/psalms/35#v17)</sup> Lord, how long wilt thou look on? rescue my soul from their destructions, my darling from the lions.

</details>
<details><summary>Psalms 59:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/psalms/59#v1)</sup> Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.

</details>

</details>

### 11A.2 {#11A.2}

Overcome enemies by kindness.

[1 Sam 26:21](/bible/1-samuel/26#v21); [Rom 12:20-21](/bible/romans/12#v20)

<details><summary>1 Samuel 26:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/1-samuel/26#v21)</sup> Then said Saul, I have sinned: return, my son David: for I will no more do thee harm, because my soul was precious in thine eyes this day: behold, I have played the fool, and have erred exceedingly.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 12:20-21</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/romans/12#v20)</sup> Therefore if thine enemy hunger, feed him; if he thirst, give him drink: for in so doing thou shalt heap coals of fire on his head.
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/12#v21)</sup> Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with good.

</details>

### 11A.3 {#11A.3}

[Luke 19:27](/bible/luke/19#v27)

<details><summary>Luke 19:27</summary>

> <sup>[27](/bible/luke/19#v27)</sup> But those mine enemies, which would not that I should reign over them, bring hither, and slay them before me.

</details>
