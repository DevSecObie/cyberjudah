### 11B.1 {#11B.1}

We are not to help ungodly and anti-Christ nations or show friendship towards them.

[2 Chr 19:2](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v2)

<details><summary>2 Chronicles 19:2</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/2-chronicles/19#v2)</sup> And Jehu the son of Hanani the seer went out to meet him, and said to king Jehoshaphat, Shouldest thou help the ungodly, and love them that hate the Lord? therefore is wrath upon thee from before the Lord.

</details>

### 11B.2 {#11B.2}

We can lend to other nations but are not to borrow from them.

[Deut 15:6](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v6); [Deut 28:12](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v12)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 15:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/deuteronomy/15#v6)</sup> For the Lord thy God blesseth thee, as he promised thee: and thou shalt lend unto many nations, but thou shalt not borrow; and thou shalt reign over many nations, but they shall not reign over thee.

</details>
<details><summary>Deuteronomy 28:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/deuteronomy/28#v12)</sup> The Lord shall open unto thee his good treasure, the heaven to give the rain unto thy land in his season, and to bless all the work of thine hand: and thou shalt lend unto many nations, and thou shalt not borrow.

</details>

### 11B.3 {#11B.3}

It is vain to rely on other nations for help while ignoring the aid of God.

[Isa 31:1](/bible/isaiah/31#v1); [Jer 3:23](/bible/jeremiah/3#v23)

<details><summary>Isaiah 31:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/isaiah/31#v1)</sup> Woe to them that go down to Egypt for help; and stay on horses, and trust in chariots, because they are many; and in horsemen, because they are very strong; but they look not unto the Holy One of Israel, neither seek the Lord!

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 3:23</summary>

> <sup>[23](/bible/jeremiah/3#v23)</sup> Truly in vain is salvation hoped for from the hills, and from the multitude of mountains: truly in the Lord our God is the salvation of Israel.

</details>
