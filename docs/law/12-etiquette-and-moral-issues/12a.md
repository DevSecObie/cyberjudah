### 12A.1 {#12A.1}

Chastity commanded; and that you not to be fornicators.

[Acts 15:20](/bible/acts/15#v20); [Col 3:5](/bible/colossians/3#v5); [1 Thess 4:3](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v3); [Titus 2:5](/bible/titus/2#v5)

<details><summary>Acts 15:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/acts/15#v20)</sup> But that we write unto them, that they abstain from pollutions of idols, and from fornication, and from things strangled, and from blood.

</details>
<details><summary>Colossians 3:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/colossians/3#v5)</sup> Mortify therefore your members which are upon the earth; fornication, uncleanness, inordinate affection, evil concupiscence, and covetousness, which is idolatry:

</details>
<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 4:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-thessalonians/4#v3)</sup> For this is the will of God, even your sanctification, that ye should abstain from fornication:

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 2:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/titus/2#v5)</sup> To be discreet, chaste, keepers at home, good, obedient to their own husbands, that the word of God be not blasphemed.

</details>

<details><summary>1 Corinthians 6:13,15-19</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v13)</sup> Meats for the belly, and the belly for meats: but God shall destroy both it and them. Now the body is not for fornication, but for the Lord; and the Lord for the body.

>
> <sup>[18](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v18)</sup> Flee fornication. Every sin that a man doeth is without the body; but he that committeth fornication sinneth against his own body.

> <sup>[19](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v19)</sup> What? know ye not that your body is the temple of the Holy Ghost which is in you, which ye have of God, and ye are not your own?

</details>

### 12A.7 {#12A.7}

Immoral and unchaste conduct (works of the flesh) excludes entry into the Kingdom of God.

[Gal 5:19-21](/bible/galatians/5#v19); [Eph 5:5](/bible/ephesians/5#v5); [Heb 13:4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)

<details><summary>Galatians 5:19-21</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/galatians/5#v19)</sup> Now the works of the flesh are manifest, which are these; Adultery, fornication, uncleanness, lasciviousness,
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/galatians/5#v20)</sup> Idolatry, witchcraft, hatred, variance, emulations, wrath, strife, seditions, heresies,
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/5#v21)</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 5:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/ephesians/5#v5)</sup> For this ye know, that no whoremonger, nor unclean person, nor covetous man, who is an idolater, hath any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 13:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/hebrews/13#v4)</sup> Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.

</details>
