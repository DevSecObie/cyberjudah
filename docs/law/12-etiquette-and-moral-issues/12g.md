### 12G.1 {#12G.1}

Sobriety commanded.

[1 Thess 5:6,8](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v6); [1 Pet 1:13](/bible/1-peter/1#v13); [1 Pet 4:7](/bible/1-peter/4#v7); [1 Pet 5:8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)

<details><summary>1 Thessalonians 5:6,8</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v6)</sup> Therefore let us not sleep, as do others; but let us watch and be sober.
>
> <sup>[8](/bible/1-thessalonians/5#v8)</sup> But let us, who are of the day, be sober, putting on the breastplate of faith and love; and for an helmet, the hope of salvation.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 1:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-peter/1#v13)</sup> Wherefore gird up the loins of your mind, be sober, and hope to the end for the grace that is to be brought unto you at the revelation of Jesus Christ;

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 4:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/1-peter/4#v7)</sup> But the end of all things is at hand: be ye therefore sober, and watch unto prayer.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 5:8</summary>

> <sup>[8](/bible/1-peter/5#v8)</sup> Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:

</details>

### 12G.2 {#12G.2}

The gospel teaches we should live soberly.

[Titus 2:12](/bible/titus/2#v12)

<details><summary>Titus 2:12</summary>

> <sup>[12](/bible/titus/2#v12)</sup> Teaching us that, denying ungodliness and worldly lusts, we should live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world;

</details>

### 12G.3 {#12G.3}

The taking of wine or strong drink is forbidden when going into the tabernacle of the congregation.

[Lev 10:9](/bible/leviticus/10#v9)

<details><summary>Leviticus 10:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/leviticus/10#v9)</sup> Do not drink wine nor strong drink, thou, nor thy sons with thee, when ye go into the tabernacle of the congregation, lest ye die: it shall be a statute for ever throughout your generations:

</details>

### 12G.4 {#12G.4}

Those in the service of God are not to drink alcohol.

[Num 6:2-3](/bible/numbers/6#v2)

<details><summary>Numbers 6:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/numbers/6#v2)</sup> Speak unto the children of Israel, and say unto them, When either man or woman shall separate themselves to vow a vow of a Nazarite, to separate themselves unto the Lord:
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/numbers/6#v3)</sup> He shall separate himself from wine and strong drink, and shall drink no vinegar of wine, or vinegar of strong drink, neither shall he drink any liquor of grapes, nor eat moist grapes, or dried.

</details>

### 12G.5 {#12G.5}

Drinking and Drunkenness not approved.

[Deut 21:20](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v20); [Hos 4:11](/bible/hosea/4#v11); [Luke 21:34](/bible/luke/21#v34); [Rom 13:13](/bible/romans/13#v13); [Rom 14:21](/bible/romans/14#v21); [1 Cor 6:10](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v10); [Gal 5:21](/bible/galatians/5#v21); [Eph 5:18](/bible/ephesians/5#v18)

<details><summary>Deuteronomy 21:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/deuteronomy/21#v20)</sup> And they shall say unto the elders of his city, This our son is stubborn and rebellious, he will not obey our voice; he is a glutton, and a drunkard.

</details>
<details><summary>Hosea 4:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/4#v11)</sup> Whoredom and wine and new wine take away the heart.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 21:34</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/luke/21#v34)</sup> And take heed to yourselves, lest at any time your hearts be overcharged with surfeiting, and drunkenness, and cares of this life, and so that day come upon you unawares.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 13:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

</details>
<details><summary>Romans 14:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/romans/14#v21)</sup> It is good neither to eat flesh, nor to drink wine, nor any thing whereby thy brother stumbleth, or is offended, or is made weak.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 6:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/1-corinthians/6#v10)</sup> Nor thieves, nor covetous, nor drunkards, nor revilers, nor extortioners, shall inherit the kingdom of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Galatians 5:21</summary>

> <sup>[21](/bible/galatians/5#v21)</sup> Envyings, murders, drunkenness, revellings, and such like: of the which I tell you before, as I have also told you in time past, that they which do such things shall not inherit the kingdom of God.

</details>
<details><summary>Ephesians 5:18</summary>

> <sup>[18](/bible/ephesians/5#v18)</sup> And be not drunk with wine, wherein is excess; but be filled with the Spirit;

</details>

### 12G.6 {#12G.6}

Drinking and merrymaking prevents the people from seeing the afflictions of the nation.

[Joel 1:4-7](/bible/joel/1#v4); [Amos 6:1-7](/bible/amos/6#v1)

<details><summary>Joel 1:4-7</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/joel/1#v4)</sup> That which the palmerworm hath left hath the locust eaten; and that which the locust hath left hath the cankerworm eaten; and that which the cankerworm hath left hath the caterpiller eaten.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/joel/1#v5)</sup> Awake, ye drunkards, and weep; and howl, all ye drinkers of wine, because of the new wine; for it is cut off from your mouth.
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/joel/1#v6)</sup> For a nation is come up upon my land, strong, and without number, whose teeth are the teeth of a lion, and he hath the cheek teeth of a great lion.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/joel/1#v7)</sup> He hath laid my vine waste, and barked my fig tree: he hath made it clean bare, and cast it away; the branches thereof are made white.

</details>
<details><summary>Amos 6:1-7</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/amos/6#v1)</sup> Woe to them that are at ease in Zion, and trust in the mountain of Samaria, which are named chief of the nations, to whom the house of Israel came!
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/amos/6#v2)</sup> Pass ye unto Calneh, and see; and from thence go ye to Hamath the great: then go down to Gath of the Philistines: be they better than these kingdoms? or their border greater than your border?
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/amos/6#v3)</sup> Ye that put far away the evil day, and cause the seat of violence to come near;
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/amos/6#v4)</sup> That lie upon beds of ivory, and stretch themselves upon their couches, and eat the lambs out of the flock, and the calves out of the midst of the stall;
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/amos/6#v5)</sup> That chant to the sound of the viol, and invent to themselves instruments of musick, like David;
>
> <sup>[6](/bible/amos/6#v6)</sup> That drink wine in bowls, and anoint themselves with the chief ointments: but they are not grieved for the affliction of Joseph.
>
> <sup>[7](/bible/amos/6#v7)</sup> Therefore now shall they go captive with the first that go captive, and the banquet of them that stretched themselves shall be removed.

</details>

### 12G.7 {#12G.7}

Excessive or habitual drinking is admonished against, as it causes a disregard of the judgments and works of God.

[Isa 5:11-12](/bible/isaiah/5#v11)

<details><summary>Isaiah 5:11-12</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/isaiah/5#v11)</sup> Woe unto them that rise up early in the morning, that they may follow strong drink; that continue until night, till wine inflame them!
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/isaiah/5#v12)</sup> And the harp, and the viol, the tabret, and pipe, and wine, are in their feasts: but they regard not the work of the Lord, neither consider the operation of his hands.

</details>

### 12G.8 {#12G.8}

Pregnant women are not to take alcoholic drinks.

[Judg 13:3-4,79](/bible/judges/13#v3); [Luke 1:15](/bible/luke/1#v15)

<details><summary>Judges 13:3-4,79</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/judges/13#v3)</sup> And the angel of the Lord appeared unto the woman, and said unto her, Behold now, thou art barren, and bearest not: but thou shalt conceive, and bear a son.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/judges/13#v4)</sup> Now therefore beware, I pray thee, and drink not wine nor strong drink, and eat not any unclean thing:

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 1:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/luke/1#v15)</sup> For he shall be great in the sight of the Lord, and shall drink neither wine nor strong drink; and he shall be filled with the Holy Ghost, even from his mother’s womb.

</details>

### 12G.9 {#12G.9}

Intoxicating drink causes persons to err and be led astray.

[Isa 28:7](/bible/isaiah/28#v7)

<details><summary>Isaiah 28:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/isaiah/28#v7)</sup> But they also have erred through wine, and through strong drink are out of the way; the priest and the prophet have erred through strong drink, they are swallowed up of wine, they are out of the way through strong drink; they err in vision, they stumble in judgment.

</details>

### 12G.10 {#12G.10}

We are not to associate with those that are drunkards and gluttons.

[Prov 23:20](/bible/proverbs/23#v20); [1 Cor 5:11](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v11)

<details><summary>Proverbs 23:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/23#v20)</sup> Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh:

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 5:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/1-corinthians/5#v11)</sup> But now I have written unto you not to keep company, if any man that is called a brother be a fornicator, or covetous, or an idolater, or a railer, or a drunkard, or an extortioner; with such an one no not to eat.

</details>

### 12G.11 {#12G.11}

Addiction to intoxicating drink brings sorrow, contentions, complaints, wounds without cause, and redness of eyes.

[Prov 23:29-35](/bible/proverbs/23#v29)

<details><summary>Proverbs 23:29-35</summary>

> <sup>[29](/bible/proverbs/23#v29)</sup> Who hath woe? who hath sorrow? who hath contentions? who hath babbling? who hath wounds without cause? who hath redness of eyes?
>
> <sup>[30](/bible/proverbs/23#v30)</sup> They that tarry long at the wine; they that go to seek mixed wine.
>
> <sup>[31](/bible/proverbs/23#v31)</sup> Look not thou upon the wine when it is red, when it giveth his colour in the cup, when it moveth itself aright.
>
> <sup>[32](/bible/proverbs/23#v32)</sup> At the last it biteth like a serpent, and stingeth like an adder.
>
> <sup>[33](/bible/proverbs/23#v33)</sup> Thine eyes shall behold strange women, and thine heart shall utter perverse things.
>
> <sup>[34](/bible/proverbs/23#v34)</sup> Yea, thou shalt be as he that lieth down in the midst of the sea, or as he that lieth upon the top of a mast.
>
> <sup>[35](/bible/proverbs/23#v35)</sup> They have stricken me, shalt thou say, and I was not sick; they have beaten me, and I felt it not: when shall I awake? I will seek it yet again.

</details>

### 12G.12 {#12G.12}

Wine is a mocker, strong drink arouses brawling, and whoever is intoxicated by it is deluded and not wise.

[Prov 20:1](/bible/proverbs/20#v1)

<details><summary>Proverbs 20:1</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/proverbs/20#v1)</sup> Wine is a mocker, strong drink is raging: and whosoever is deceived thereby is not wise.

</details>

### 12G.13 {#12G.13}

You are not to encourage another to drink or attempt to get another drunk.

[Hab 2:15](/bible/habakkuk/2#v15)

<details><summary>Habakkuk 2:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/habakkuk/2#v15)</sup> Woe unto him that giveth his neighbour drink, that puttest thy bottle to him, and makest him drunken also, that thou mayest look on their nakedness!

</details>
