### 12B.1 {#12B.1}

Busybodies exhorted to mind their own business.

[2 Thess 3:11-12](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v11); [Prov 26:17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17); [1 Pet 4:15](/bible/1-peter/4#v15)

<details><summary>2 Thessalonians 3:11-12</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v11)</sup> For we hear that there are some which walk among you disorderly, working not at all, but are busybodies.
>
> <sup>[12](/bible/2-thessalonians/3#v12)</sup> Now them that are such we command and exhort by our Lord Jesus Christ, that with quietness they work, and eat their own bread.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 26:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17)</sup> He that passeth by, and meddleth with strife belonging not to him, is like one that taketh a dog by the ears.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 4:15</summary>

> <sup>[15](/bible/1-peter/4#v15)</sup> But let none of you suffer as a murderer, or as a thief, or as an evildoer, or as a busybody in other men’s matters.

</details>

### 12B.3 {#12B.3}

We are not to wander from house to house as tattlers and busybodies.

[1 Tim 5:13](/bible/1-timothy/5#v13)

<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/1-timothy/5#v13)</sup> And withal they learn to be idle, wandering about from house to house; and not only idle, but tattlers also and busybodies, speaking things which they ought not.

</details>

### 12B.4 {#12B.4}

We should not meddle in other's concerns.

[2 Kgs 14:10](/bible/2-kings/14#v10); [Prov 20:3](/bible/proverbs/20#v3); [Prov 24:21](/bible/proverbs/24#v21); [Prov 26:17](/bible/proverbs/26#v17)

<details><summary>2 Kings 14:10</summary>

> <sup>[10](/bible/2-kings/14#v10)</sup> Thou hast indeed smitten Edom, and thine heart hath lifted thee up: glory of this, and tarry at home: for why shouldest thou meddle to thy hurt, that thou shouldest fall, even thou, and Judah with thee?

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 20:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/20#v3)</sup> It is an honour for a man to cease from strife: but every fool will be meddling.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 24:21</summary>

<details><summary>Romans 1:28-29</summary>

>
> <sup>[29](/bible/romans/1#v29)</sup> Being filled with all unrighteousness, fornication, wickedness, covetousness, maliciousness; full of envy, murder, debate, deceit, malignity; whisperers,

### 12B.7 {#12B.7}

We are admonished not to gossip.

[2 Cor 12:20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20)

<details><summary>2 Corinthians 12:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/2-corinthians/12#v20)</sup> For I fear, lest, when I come, I shall not find you such as I would, and that I shall be found unto you such as ye would not: lest there be debates, envyings, wraths, strifes, backbitings, whisperings, swellings, tumults:

</details>

### 12B.8 {#12B.8}

We should not talk about the errors or transgressions of others.

[Prov 17:9](/bible/proverbs/17#v9)

<details><summary>Proverbs 17:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/17#v9)</sup> He that covereth a transgression seeketh love; but he that repeateth a matter separateth very friends.

</details>

### 12B.9 {#12B.9}

[Ps 41:7](/bible/psalms/41#v7)

<details><summary>Psalms 41:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/psalms/41#v7)</sup> All that hate me whisper together against me: against me do they devise my hurt.

</details>

<details><summary>Proverbs 16:28</summary>

> <sup>[28](/bible/proverbs/16#v28)</sup> A froward man soweth strife: and a whisperer separateth chief friends.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 17:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/proverbs/17#v9)</sup> He that covereth a transgression seeketh love; but he that repeateth a matter separateth very friends.

### 12B.11 {#12B.11}

Do not associate with a gossip.

[Prov 20:19](/bible/proverbs/20#v19)

<details><summary>Proverbs 20:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/proverbs/20#v19)</sup> He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips.

</details>

### 12B.12 {#12B.12}

Where gossiping is eliminated, strife ceases.

[Prov 26:20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)

<details><summary>Proverbs 26:20</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/26#v20)</sup> Where no wood is, there the fire goeth out: so where there is no talebearer, the strife ceaseth.

</details>
