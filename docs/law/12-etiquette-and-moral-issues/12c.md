### 12C.1 {#12C.1}

Seeking pleasures and lavish living is not Godly.

[Rom 13:13](/bible/romans/13#v13); [1 Tim 5:6](/bible/1-timothy/5#v6); [Titus 3:3](/bible/titus/3#v3); [Heb 11:25](/bible/hebrews/11#v25); [1 Pet 4:3-4](/bible/1-peter/4#v3)

<details><summary>Romans 13:13</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/romans/13#v13)</sup> Let us walk honestly, as in the day; not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Timothy 5:6</summary>

> <sup>[6](/bible/1-timothy/5#v6)</sup> But she that liveth in pleasure is dead while she liveth.

</details>
<details><summary>Titus 3:3</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/titus/3#v3)</sup> For we ourselves also were sometimes foolish, disobedient, deceived, serving divers lusts and pleasures, living in malice and envy, hateful, and hating one another.

</details>
<details><summary>Hebrews 11:25</summary>

> <sup>[25](/bible/hebrews/11#v25)</sup> Choosing rather to suffer affliction with the people of God, than to enjoy the pleasures of sin for a season;

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 4:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-peter/4#v3)</sup> For the time past of our life may suffice us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles, when we walked in lasciviousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-peter/4#v4)</sup> Wherein they think it strange that ye run not with them to the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you:

</details>

### 12C.2 {#12C.2}

Lovers of pleasure are brought into poverty.

[Prov 21:17](/bible/proverbs/21#v17); [Prov 23:20-21](/bible/proverbs/23#v20); [Luke 15:13-14](/bible/luke/15#v13)

<details><summary>Proverbs 21:17</summary>

> <sup>[17](/bible/proverbs/21#v17)</sup> He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

</details>
<details><summary>Proverbs 23:20-21</summary>

> <sup>[20](/bible/proverbs/23#v20)</sup> Be not among winebibbers; among riotous eaters of flesh:
>
> <sup>[21](/bible/proverbs/23#v21)</sup> For the drunkard and the glutton shall come to poverty: and drowsiness shall clothe a man with rags.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 15:13-14</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/luke/15#v13)</sup> And not many days after the younger son gathered all together, and took his journey into a far country, and there wasted his substance with riotous living.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/luke/15#v14)</sup> And when he had spent all, there arose a mighty famine in that land; and he began to be in want.

</details>

### 12C.3 {#12C.3}

To seek mirth and pleasure is vanity.

[Eccl 2:1,10-11](/bible/ecclesiastes/2#v1); [Jer 7:34](/bible/jeremiah/7#v34); [Jer 16:9](/bible/jeremiah/16#v9); [Hos 2:11](/bible/hosea/2#v11)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 2:1,10-11</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/ecclesiastes/2#v1)</sup> I said in mine heart, Go to now, I will prove thee with mirth, therefore enjoy pleasure: and, behold, this also is vanity.
>
> <sup>[10](/bible/ecclesiastes/2#v10)</sup> And whatsoever mine eyes desired I kept not from them, I withheld not my heart from any joy; for my heart rejoiced in all my labour: and this was my portion of all my labour.
>
> <sup>[11](/bible/ecclesiastes/2#v11)</sup> Then I looked on all the works that my hands had wrought, and on the labour that I had laboured to do: and, behold, all was vanity and vexation of spirit, and there was no profit under the sun.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 7:34</summary>

> <sup>[34](/bible/jeremiah/7#v34)</sup> Then will I cause to cease from the cities of Judah, and from the streets of Jerusalem, the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride: for the land shall be desolate.

</details>
<details><summary>Jeremiah 16:9</summary>

> <sup>[9](/bible/jeremiah/16#v9)</sup> For thus saith the Lord of hosts, the God of Israel; Behold, I will cause to cease out of this place in your eyes, and in your days, the voice of mirth, and the voice of gladness, the voice of the bridegroom, and the voice of the bride.

</details>
<details><summary>Hosea 2:11</summary>

> <sup>[11](/bible/hosea/2#v11)</sup> I will also cause all her mirth to cease, her feast days, her new moons, and her sabbaths, and all her solemn feasts.

</details>

### 12C.4 {#12C.4}

Beware of those who are lovers of pleasure rather than lovers of God.

[2 Tim 3:4](/bible/2-timothy/3#v4)

<details><summary>2 Timothy 3:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/2-timothy/3#v4)</sup> Traitors, heady, highminded, lovers of pleasures more than lovers of God;

</details>

### 12C.5 {#12C.5}

We should display moderation.

[Phil 4:5](/bible/philippians/4#v5)

<details><summary>Philippians 4:5</summary>

> <sup>[5](/bible/philippians/4#v5)</sup> Let your moderation be known unto all men. The Lord is at hand.

</details>

### 12C.6 {#12C.6}

Gluttony and excessiveness is iniquity and leads to a carnal outlook on life.

[Isa 22:13-14](/bible/isaiah/22#v13); [Luke 12:19-20](/bible/luke/12#v19); [1 Cor 15:32](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v32)

<details><summary>Isaiah 22:13-14</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/isaiah/22#v13)</sup> And behold joy and gladness, slaying oxen, and killing sheep, eating flesh, and drinking wine: let us eat and drink; for to morrow we shall die.
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/isaiah/22#v14)</sup> And it was revealed in mine ears by the Lord of hosts, Surely this iniquity shall not be purged from you till ye die, saith the Lord God of hosts.

</details>
<details><summary>Luke 12:19-20</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/luke/12#v19)</sup> And I will say to my soul, Soul, thou hast much goods laid up for many years; take thine ease, eat, drink, and be merry.
>
> <sup>[20](/bible/luke/12#v20)</sup> But God said unto him, Thou fool, this night thy soul shall be required of thee: then whose shall those things be, which thou hast provided?

</details>
<details><summary>1 Corinthians 15:32</summary>

> <sup>[32](/bible/1-corinthians/15#v32)</sup> If after the manner of men I have fought with beasts at Ephesus, what advantageth it me, if the dead rise not? let us eat and drink; for to morrow we die.

</details>

### 12C.7 {#12C.7}

It is wise to abstain from indulgences.

[Prov 28:7](/bible/proverbs/28#v7); [Eccl 7:2-3](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v2)

<details><summary>Proverbs 28:7</summary>

> <sup>[7](/bible/proverbs/28#v7)</sup> Whoso keepeth the law is a wise son: but he that is a companion of riotous men shameth his father.

</details>
<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 7:2-3</summary>

> <sup>[2](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v2)</sup> It is better to go to the house of mourning, than to go to the house of feasting: for that is the end of all men; and the living will lay it to his heart.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v3)</sup> Sorrow is better than laughter: for by the sadness of the countenance the heart is made better.

</details>

### 12C.8 {#12C.8}

Avoid lasciviousness and wantonness or covetousness.

[Eph 4:19](/bible/ephesians/4#v19); [1 Pet 4:3-4](/bible/1-peter/4#v3); [2 Pet 2:13-15](/bible/2-peter/2#v13)

<details><summary>Ephesians 4:19</summary>

> <sup>[19](/bible/ephesians/4#v19)</sup> Who being past feeling have given themselves over unto lasciviousness, to work all uncleanness with greediness.

</details>
<details><summary>1 Peter 4:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/1-peter/4#v3)</sup> For the time past of our life may suffice us to have wrought the will of the Gentiles, when we walked in lasciviousness, lusts, excess of wine, revellings, banquetings, and abominable idolatries:
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/1-peter/4#v4)</sup> Wherein they think it strange that ye run not with them to the same excess of riot, speaking evil of you:

</details>
<details><summary>2 Peter 2:13-15</summary>

> <sup>[13](/bible/2-peter/2#v13)</sup> And shall receive the reward of unrighteousness, as they that count it pleasure to riot in the day time. Spots they are and blemishes, sporting themselves with their own deceivings while they feast with you;
>
> <sup>[14](/bible/2-peter/2#v14)</sup> Having eyes full of adultery, and that cannot cease from sin; beguiling unstable souls: an heart they have exercised with covetous practices; cursed children:
>
> <sup>[15](/bible/2-peter/2#v15)</sup> Which have forsaken the right way, and are gone astray, following the way of Balaam the son of Bosor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness;

</details>

### 12C.9 {#12C.9}

We are not to desire riches.

[Prov 23:3-4](/bible/proverbs/23#v3); [James 5:1-5](/bible/james/5#v1)

<details><summary>Proverbs 23:3-4</summary>

> <sup>[3](/bible/proverbs/23#v3)</sup> Be not desirous of his dainties: for they are deceitful meat.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/proverbs/23#v4)</sup> Labour not to be rich: cease from thine own wisdom.

</details>
<details><summary>James 5:1-5</summary>

> <sup>[1](/bible/james/5#v1)</sup> Go to now, ye rich men, weep and howl for your miseries that shall come upon you.
>
> <sup>[2](/bible/james/5#v2)</sup> Your riches are corrupted, and your garments are motheaten.
>
> <sup>[3](/bible/james/5#v3)</sup> Your gold and silver is cankered; and the rust of them shall be a witness against you, and shall eat your flesh as it were fire. Ye have heaped treasure together for the last days.
>
> <sup>[4](/bible/james/5#v4)</sup> Behold, the hire of the labourers who have reaped down your fields, which is of you kept back by fraud, crieth: and the cries of them which have reaped are entered into the ears of the Lord of sabaoth.
>
> <sup>[5](/bible/james/5#v5)</sup> Ye have lived in pleasure on the earth, and been wanton; ye have nourished your hearts, as in a day of slaughter.

</details>

### 12C.10 {#12C.10}

Indulgence is a proof of folly.

[Eccl 7:4](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v4)

<details><summary>Ecclesiastes 7:4</summary>

> <sup>[4](/bible/ecclesiastes/7#v4)</sup> The heart of the wise is in the house of mourning; but the heart of fools is in the house of mirth.

</details>
